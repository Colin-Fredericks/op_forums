                                        What I really like about G-KB is the scale of events. The Dragon-Blooded really feel like a big deal. I've never been in a game in which Dragon-Blooded were not just the opposition, this vacationist Dynast is very well written and I can see how he is both disturbing, dangerous, and magnifiscent to the other characters around him.

                                        I've said it before, the aesthetic of G-KB is very interesting, the design choices are very unique and original. The petals around the DB'S sword and the chameleon lunar, are some of the examples of unconventional elements that fit perfectly in the Exalted we know but at the same time we hadn't seen before (or at least not with frequency) in official art.

                                        When the artist knows and loves the setting, he can elaborate over it and bring magic to it. That is why despite being professionals, many of the hired artists for the books end up producing a very average result. Tell them to make a magic sword made of green jade and they'll draw you a gigantic green machete with useless ornaments, they won't imagine "a blade that seems to blossom from the hilt", a much more subtle interpretation of the wood element integrated into a weapon.

                                        That IMO is what makes this fan effort outstanding*.

                                        *also the story is very good of course.
