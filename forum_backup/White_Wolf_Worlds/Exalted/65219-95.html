                        3e thaumaturgy is just like 2e.

                        Becoming a sorcerer grants you power. Metaphysical weight. It makes certain charms and spells less effective against you. It decreases the efficacy of mind altering magics.

                        Sorcery allows you to access thaumaturgy. This is not automatic, but it definitely gives several bonuses. The aforementioned metaphysical weight increases the power of any thaumaturgy the sorcerer casts, the same way a sorcerer can cover an entire field with a permanent ward against pests, while a thaumaturge can only do so one acre at a time, which lasts only a week.

                            I kind of go the opposite end. Thaumaturgy is not really an IC "thing" that exists. It's a mechanical representation of some of the strange, unique or uncanny things people in the world sometimes can do, but ultimately they rarely do more than that. Some might not even know they have a supernatural power. The girl who reads tea leaves accurately might have it be something she does on her own, while working for a fortune teller who's a complete shame but makes bank. It might be contact with some other-wordly entity, being able to change your physical sex at a whim (ala Simmu in Death's Master), let someone see spirits and so on. Being able to do this is kind of just having a connection with the Uncanny that not everyone gets. Kind of like what you see in a lot of fiction or anime where the character can see ghosts all their lives, but live otherwise a normal life until some factor comes in and pushes them to other things.

                            This often results in something also failry Flat Earthy: You have folks who may have a random charm who don't use it for anything useful or it backfires a lot. And you have a lot of so-called sages, holymen, prophets, and great people who are full of shit and don't even know it.

                            Sorcerers are similarly the case. Sorcerers have access to certain secrets of the world, but how they get there has a lot of different journeys. All sorcerers can do Death of Obsidian Butterflies potentially, but why they can is different between them and initiations. Just like I can do arithematic and my computer can as well, but how it does it followers actually a different set of rules than how I do it.
