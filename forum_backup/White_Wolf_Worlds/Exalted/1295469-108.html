                            Originally posted by Nicolas Milioni View Post
                            I'm still a little lost when it comes to Abyssal and Infernal exaltations. They are stolen Solar Exaltations right? But,how were they stolen?
                            They were stolen from the Jade Prison. The precise details of how this was accomplished have not yet been revealed.

                            are they like "stored" somewehre?
                            As in past editions, when an Abyssal Exaltation is not incarnate in an Abyssal, it resides in a Monstrance of Celestial Portion. A similar arrangement exists for Infernal Exaltations.

                            and how are they modified to turn a mortal into an Abyssal or Infernal and not an Solar?
                            By the power of the Deathlords, drawing upon the bleak glories of the Neverborn, and by the power of the Yozis, respectively.
