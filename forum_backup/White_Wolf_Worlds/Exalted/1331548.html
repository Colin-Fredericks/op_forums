    Heaven For Everyone (Exalted in oWoD actual play)

    08-19-2019, 01:16 AM
    We've recently started a new Exalted actual play - Heaven For Everyone. It's a cross between Exalted and V20 / Wraith20 - a group of teens in the 1980s Los Angeles get turned into Exalts. They start taking in the Old World of Darkness as they try figuring out what they are, all the while putting local vampires in their place and keeping a low profile as teenagers in a high school (we wanted the game to have a slower burn, so we didn't go straight to being god-kings ).

    Our plan for the game is to eventually face off against the end-of-the-world metaplot and see how things shape up, but for the first season we're just dealing with local problems.

    Our cast consists of:
    - Brass Dancer, an Infernal
    - Creation Ruling Mandate, a Sidereal Chosen of Secrets
    - Princess Magnificent, a Midnight Caste Abyssal
    - Salt Spray Purity, a Solar Dawn

    You can find our introduction episode here:
    - https://sponsoredbynobody.podbean.co...-introduction/

    And our first actual episode is here:
    - https://sponsoredbynobody.podbean.co...01-episode-01/
