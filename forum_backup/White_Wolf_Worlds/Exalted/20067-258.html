        Originally posted by Ellis View Post
        Two people clash when they attack each other at the same time, but what happens if Alice attacks Bob at the same time Bob attacks Eve? Nobody clashes, right?
        You can flurry a Clash with a Defend Other, but that's where things get complicated. Basically, you're forcing your opponent to choose between attacking over you, which I would argue can be Clashed normally, or with a Clash charm that doesn't specify whether or not the in-coming attack is targeting you, or to attack you, in which case, just about any appropriate Clash charm should work normally. The advantage there is that if he chooses to go over you, he takes a penalty to his roll equal to your Parry, which while significant, does not bother me from a game balance perspective. Just remember to declare the Defend Other as the first action in the Flurry.
