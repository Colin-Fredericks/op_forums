        Originally posted by Erinys View Post
        How big are manses? Are they all the same size, or does it vary by rating? If it varies, about how big are one-dot and five-dot manses?

        I checked the 2E corebook and didn't see anything about this. The 1E corebook only says that they're "enormous" in general and vaguely implies a place the size of a very large mansion or monastery.
        Book of Sorcery III-Oadenol's Codex, page 57 claims that any structure built to cap and channel a demesne's essence is a manse, be it a fortress, a temple, a single room. The manse proper can be part of a building, or the manse may be subdivided into several buildings. A manse can also be landscaping; it's a geomantic construction after all.

        The codex also details manse construction options (such as amenities and protections) and these are suggestive of how they might affect size and appearance; however it doesn't give any exacting measures. The design is pretty much anything goes as long as the form fits the options' descriptions.

        (Eg: an array of mirrors creating a manse of reflected light is an example of three dots of Fragility; meaning every successful attack on the manse causes power failure.)



        tl;dr However big you want them to be, but as more dot manses can by more expensive options (and fortifications) they're likely to be on the larger scale.
