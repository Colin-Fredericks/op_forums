                                                            I was going to ask about CycloneJoker's point... it's not just that Charm, but almost all the social Charms with Protean effects.

                                                            Due to Protean's exemption of the Lunar's natural human form, Feral Smile Tactic, Seductive Moonlit Dream, Honeyed Lips Inquiry, Eye-Catching Appeal, Obsession-Building Prana, Inchoate Horror Embodiment, Panicked Herd Incitement, Pride-Scattering Approach, Beast-King Dictates, Lightning-Calling Challenge, Songbird's Eternal Refrain, and Taboo-Enforcing Beast can all never have their strongest effect while the Lunar is in their natural form, even if the Protean condition would otherwise apply to them.

                                                            This incentivizes Lunars socializing, and even leading nations, in human shapes other than their own, since they miss out on some mechanical bonuses by simply acting as themselves. Was that the intention?
