    In my game, the Shadow of All Things is a crafty sonuvabitch. At the Primordial War, he foresaw that the Primordials would lose. He rewrote casuality in a way that created the Ebon Dragon by putting most of his essence into that being and altering everyone's memories of him ever existing as something outside of that being. The rest of him scattered across the shadows of Creation where he rests in a dormant state. This caused the Shadow of All Things to be the only Yozi to be able to leave Hell at the time of the Reclamation because he already exists in both worlds, and he will use the Scarlet Empress in order to merge the two essences and reform the Shadow of All Things in Creation. He's lied to the other Yozis who are a part of the Reclamation and will basically give them the finger if he's ever freed.

        I'm totally stealing that headcannon.

        'Course, that comboes nicely with another bit of headcannon of mine, which is that the Scarlet Empress is a crafty sumbich who is always two steps ahead of basically everyone. She spent years dropping hints to Mnemon that the stress of 780 years of rulership was getting to her, and egging her desire for the throne on. And also letting her sneak into a private library and learn everything she could about Celestial Sorcery. Then one day the Scarlet Empress had a "case of the vapors" and nearly fainted in public. Mnemon was oh-so-worried about her "dear, aging mother" and insistent that she needed to take a break from all the stress. And it just so happened that Mnemon had discovered a spell that her mother, with all her power, should be able to work - the spell would allow them to switch bodies, so that Her Redness could relax for a while and Mnemon could get some practice ruling the kingdom. Of course she would listen to her most beloved mother's advice and of course it was just a temporary measure that would be undone later. Once Scarlet felt better. Secretly Mnemon had no intention of ever trading back, of course; maybe she'd exile her "daughter" to some distant realm. Anyway, with her mother's mind still a bit foggy from the stress, Mnemon was able to convince her to go with it. The spell was cast, and would take effect at midnight.

        That night, just a couple minutes before midnight, the Scarlet Empress wrote the last words of the Broken-Winged Crane.

        Murcushio: Sure, you avoid all that messy withdrawal, but you still need to stop doing heroin (for the purposes of this discussion your cousin is named Heroin).

Two Battles would sound like the Dawn conversation above, but with a chain of 3~5 causal events preceding each attack, all of them initially set off via innocuous, ostensibly unrelated actions. That leaves two:

                                                "Aha! Don't tell me..." *smooths crease in sleeve* *steeples index and middle fingers*
