                                                    Hmmm... while Supernal is a very potent effect, I suspect Lunars will still end up being closer to Solar power than they were in 2nd ed. Though I think they'll also be a bit less comparable, by virtue of being more different than in 2nd ed (where they had a large number of charms that were just nerfed Solar charms).

                                                    Originally posted by Lashet View Post
                                                    You speak about methods, not the purpose of Lunars. And they should have one.
                                                    What's the purpose of Solars? To defeat the Primordials.
                                                    What's the purpose of Dragonblood? To defeat the Primordials.
                                                    What's the purpose of Sidereals? To defeat the Primordials.*
                                                    What's the purpose of Lunars? To defeat the Primordials.
                                                    They all had the same purpose, only their methods differed.

                                                    You'll notice this has already been done.

                                                    After the Primordial War, the Exalted were given a new purpose of course; to rule humanity (partly as a reward, partly so the Gods didn't have to).
                                                    But that was pretty unspecific, and they weren't designed to rule humanity. They just happened to be fairly skilled at it, because they were superhumans, and things like the super-power of being really good at organising an army can be translated to being really good at an organising a kingdom (though there are issues with that, but I'm not going to go into that now).

                                                    *To be fair, Sidereals were given a specific purpose by their Creators after the end of the Primordial War. But the others were not.

                                                    What's the purpose of Liminals? There isn't one. Exigents? It varies. Alchemicals? In 2nd ed it was just to be prototype Exalted, don't know if that would still be true. Getimians? I don't know, but as they were sealed away and only found and released later to fight Heaven, I'm not sure that is their original purpose, tbh.

                                                    Only Abyssals and Infernals have a single purpose that they actually, generally, fulfilling (revenge), because they're new. They were designed for the Age of Sorrows, the other Exalts weren't, they were designed for the Primordial War. Charms for doing extra damage to Dragonblood would be far more useful to the Solars than Charms for doing extra damage to demons, but they were designed to fight demons, not Dragonblood, so anti-demon charms is what they have.

                                                    So Lunars don't need to have a current purpose. It may be that many of them have a similar purpose (that's why about a lot are in the Silver Pact; their purpose is to destroy the Realm), but that's not something built into them (which is why many are not trying to destroy the Realm, they're doing other things).


                                                    Of course, this is all an in-game explanation.
                                                    Out-of-game? The purpose of Lunars is to let you play certain kinds of character that the other Exalts are not great at. Monstrous anti-heroes, were-creatures, trickster-spirits, shapeshifters, heroes with a totem animal, shamans, bestial berzerkers, semi-human freaks, etc. But that doesn't require a specific in-game purpose, and in fact a specific in-game purpose can be detrimental and sometimes odd.

                                                    For example, in 2nd ed the Lunars' purpose was the Thousand Streams River Project. Which a lot of people thought was ridiculous (I didn't, but I could see that it distracted from the themes of the game), and it did dissuade you from having Lunar Beast-King PCs or NPCs, which was a pity.

                                                    If their purpose was destroy the Realm, that'd be weird if your game was set really deep in the Threshold. If their purpose was to defend Creation from the Raksha (which is something Luna does), that'd be weird if your game was set in, say, the Deshan Slave States, on the coast opposite the Realm.

                                                    So they need to be able to fit into multiple kinds of games, with multiple STs wanting to run varied plots.


                                                    This does, of course, also mean their purpose can't be "Solar's no.2". That wouldn't work if there's no Solars in your game, not many Solars, or they just don't fit together well (I tried using Solar-Lunar mate pairs in 2nd ed, and honestly it didn't work well. Some players are into it, most aren't).
