                                                    I'm wary about suggestions that are too thematic or illustrative of specific concepts that exist in Exalted.

                                                    You don't want the watch list equivalent of "Exalted is like a T-Rex in an F-14" for example, I consider subplots in both Star Wars Rebels and Star Wars Resistance a great way to show that empire is evil* but I'd reserve it for an established Exalted player rather than get a newbie to conflate the Realm with the First Order.

                                                    *
                                                    In each series we see the effort that's been made to cover up the existence of the Death Star and Starkiller Base, the Empire built the Deathstar on Geonosis before wiping out all life on the planet while the First Order culled an entire area of space so they could test Starkiller Base discreetly. There's a cold brutality to those acts that's very distinct from how a Sith Lord might take their anger out on a human settlement. There was no way they didn't know from the start how they were going to keep these gigantic projects secret, genocide was plan A.

                                                    It's a more general problem with trying to do big secret projects as an Empire rather than the Sith being so evil from the Dark Side that they indulge in behaviour that is counterintuative. A Great House might do something very similar on a smaller scale if it's trying to build a tactical advantage against the other Great Houses.
