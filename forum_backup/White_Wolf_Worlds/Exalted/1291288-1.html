        Is this a good place to talk about a disconnect between expressing a concept with Dragonblooded versus Solars?

        With Solars, if you want to be a fireball-throwing wizard, you can grab three dots of Occult, jump right into sorcery and spells, and never buy a single Occult Charm.

        With Terrestrials, you can't be a sorcerer without also being a dedicated exorcist, so if you have absolutely in interest in dealing with supernatural creatures with anything more esoteric than a fireball to the face, you actually want to invest in Lore where all of the fireballs are. All of Terrestrial Lore's Charms does a great job of making a Terrestrial feel like a wizard.

                                Originally posted by SpectacularTentacular View Post
                                Lunars win because "I make myself the ideal winner! I pick an animal with the right talents, or a whole bunch of different ones, and suddenly I'm the perfect tool for the job at hand!"
                                Okay, I want to address this one, less for itself, and more because we finally have good Lunar Charms to actually adequately answer the question*.

                                That and summarising the Lunar approach solely in terms of their shapeshifting has often sold them a bit short, and has often reasonably fed into concerns with their inadequacy.

                                So, with the benefit of previewed Lunar Charms, a more accurate line would be "Lunars win because they can be really good at things in a manner not tied to specific ability, often with an edge of being predatory or tricky or scary or beautiful, unrelenting and unconfinable, and some helpful body mods. Sometimes, they can push how good they are at things a bit further by adopting a complementary animal shape."

                                The problem with phrasing Lunar powers primarily in terms of the benefit of turning into animals is that animals are still mortal, and Lunars are competing at the level of gods and monsters.

                                Lunars don't become mighty by turning into animals; animals become mighty because a Lunar turned into them. Protean effects are about that animal body benefiting from becoming a conduit for the Lunar Essence (which can still often get the job done without it).

                                * I mean, these punchy one-line summaries of how Exalted do things always tend to be broad to the point of inaccurate, it's just that Lunars have often suffered the worst because their powers besides shapeshifting always felt indistinct in comparison.

                                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                        I think that's kind of an illusion. You need four occult charms, and well, there are about four written occult charms at that level, and that's what they do.

                                        If we get more essence 1 and 2 occult charms this issue will probably vanish.
                                        I don't think so, because Occult is by definition the Ability for dealing with supernatural creatures in esoteric ways other than punching them in the face. Solar Occult Charms represent being an exorcist just as much as Terrestrial Occult Charms.

                                        The difference is that the Dragon-Blood actually needs to invest into that archetype to access sorcery, where the Solar can pay lipservice to it; meanwhile the Dragon-Blood doesn't need sorcery at all to be a fireball wizard, where a Solar does.

                                        The difference is enough to change whether I would have a character favor Lore or Occult depending on if they're a Dragon-Blood or a Solar.

                                        I'm not saying that any of this is BAD, mind you. But it certainly is quirky.


                                        Lunars don't have this issue partially because they don't favor Abilities in the first place, but mostly because "any five Mental Attribute Charms" is not nearly as definitive as "four or five Terrestrial Occult Charms". A Lunar could buy up the Intelligence Warfare Charms and be a fireball wizard who is also a general. Or a fireball wizard who is also a crafter, or a healer.

                                        Technically, I'm not sure a Lunar even needs to have any dots in Occult to be a sorcerer.
