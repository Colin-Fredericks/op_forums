                                Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post
                                If an NPC is attempting to instill a belief in me, can I just keep spending Willpower to resist it every time he beats my resolve?
                                I would say something along the lines of yes and no. Based on the "Resisting Influence Rolls" part in explaining social combat, along with the "Retrying Social Actions", it seems that whomever is trying to spin an intimacy on you can continue to retry for as long as they have more evidence and better arguments, and you can continue to resist as long as you have willpower, but depending on how the presented their argument, some arguments shouldn't even be continuable after the first time.

                                To say, if someone were to try and instill a positive tie of admiration for the Scarlet Empress on you, and they started by listing all of her greatest accomplishments, and you resisted it with willpower. At that point, any other mention of her deeds that were not as grand as the initial ones you were told should have no hope of convincing you.

                                However, if said someone was to try and instill the same tie of admiration for Her Redness, and started by listing how she started as a Shogunate general several hundred years ago, and saved creation from the fair folk, that may be one roll, and 1 wp to resist. They could continue to mention other things, such as how she created one of the most effective military powers in creation today, how she was able to start the most effective Anethema-hunting party of dragon-bloods probably ever, how she build the Blessed Isle from the ground up around the Imperial Manse with nothing but her force of will (Not everything will probably will be truthful, but that won't stop people from trying to lie you into the intimacy); each argument would produce another situation to roll for, and another chance to spend willpower to resist.

                                At least this is how I take to interpret the rules, so this so to be taken with a grain of salt.
