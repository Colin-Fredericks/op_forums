            From the names alone, here's how I'd probably do them.

            Presence is the direct short-term stuff, Embassy involves longer negotiations and planning.

            If you're talking to someone who works with the Guild and you're haggling to cut a deal, which may involve doing favors or something, that'd be Presence because the aim itself is short-term (get a good bargain on the goods)

            If you're talking to someone who works with the Guild and you're in negotiations for giving them a cut of the profits if they sell the goods you can secure them for the next year and a day, that'd probably be Embassy because you're working on long-term agreements and deals.

            Time will tell whether Grand Speeches will be Presence or Performance. I can see the logic for moving the Motivation Through Speech into Presence while Dancing, Painting, and Acting go into Performance.

                                            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                            Maybe disguise, pickpocketing and lockpicking... are just in Stealth.
                                            Well, obviously. It's just an odd name for it, akin to naming the Archery-Thrown ability "Archery" instead of "Ranged Combat."

                                            - Power replaces Initiative pools from Ex3, starts at 0 at combat start
                                            - Build Power is an option to build withering damage power, always difficluty 3
                                            - Withering attacks build Power for every threshold success, plus weapon Accuracy successes
                                            - To make a decisive attack, the character must spend Power to overcome a target's Hardness. Damage = threshold successes and additional spent Power rolled + weapon Damage successes
                                            So, if I understand this correctly, the Damage value of a weapon is basically decisive Accuracy, and the Build Power action exists for the low-Accuracy weapons (such as, presumably, grand daiklaves).

                                            I... think I like that? It sounds slightly better than EX3's Accuracy/Damage disparity, at least, assuming the Build Power option does what I think it does.

                                                    For those looking for a more narrative variant, we've always been pretty clear that this is primarily a streamlined version of Ex3 akin to Fate Accelerated to Fate Core. It plays similarly, though if you look under the hood there's definitely some different stuff in there, and it's meant to be a simpler and more narrative expression - that doesn't mean we're discarding the trad heritage in favor of pure story-style mechanics. However, we didn't feature a lot of the heavy story* inspired stuff - the dramatic editing and the Great Curse mechanics, for one, and the Virtues were streamlined a bit.

                                                            If I had to take a guess (I don't, but I want to)...

                                                            Awareness: Seems self-explanatory, but it wouldn't be too strange for it to also cover Investigation.
                                                            Close Combat: Let's not waste time here.
                                                            Craft: It's Craft.
                                                            Embassy: Bureacracy-Socialize-Linguistics, no doubt. The Eclipse Ability.
                                                            Integrity: It's Integrity. They could potentially put some of the more holy man-esque Resistance and Survival Charms here, like Hardship-Surviving Mendicant Spirit or Untiring Earth Meditation.
                                                            Navigate: Sail and Survival, possibly with a side of Ride.
                                                            Performance: Performance is Performance.
                                                            Physique: Athletics, likely Resistance, possibly Dodge.
                                                            Presence: Well.
                                                            Ranged Combat: It's Archery and it's Thrown. Hopefully Elemental Bolt Attack goes here instead of in...
                                                            Sagacity: If Embassy is about being an Eclipse (and it is), this is about being a Twilight-No Moon. It's Lore, it's Occult, it's probably Medicine, and if Investigation isn't folded into Awareness, it's folded into Sagacity.
                                                            Stealth: Sneaky shit; Stealth and Larceny, maybe with the more Guile-focused Socialize effects alongside them.
                                                            War: Huh! What is it good for?
