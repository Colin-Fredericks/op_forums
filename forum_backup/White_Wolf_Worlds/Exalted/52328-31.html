    Okay, I've reached out some feelers to one of the players in my game who's been playing a Performance-centric Zenith for years. I'll see if she has any contributions that escape the incredibly dense black hole of Prayer charms that we seem to have found ourselves in.

    Meanwhile, what John's said has kind of reframed what I'm thinking and made me think a bit more of what I'd like out of Performance.

    The Solar performer should fascinate.

    Crowds should gather as he plays, and he can form a congregation drumming on an empty wooden tub in the poorest streets of Nexus.It won't be a politically, economically, or even particularly physically powerful congregation, but their awe of him would be genuine.

    She should be able to call others to join in her revels. Her ecstatic dance beckons, and her assembly falls into step with her without meaning to. Even gods might follow, for her power is such that they can recognize their greater in her. She does not command their obedience--that may come after, through more blunt application of personality, or more tangible tools--but she has their attention.

    His touch is narcotic.

    Her words will outlive her by a thousand years or more; some will say that they have lost their power, for surely see how they no longer call the gods to order, and see how a child's rough emulation of her song no longer brings women and men to tears as was written. Yet that boy will pass it down to his sons, as they will their sons after them. Perhaps in time the origins will be forgotten completely, or the lyrics will fall away and an achingly poignant melody remains if only as a whistle or a hum. But it does live on, less changed and far longer than it had any right to.

    It is immortality in a notes; some Wyld Hunts have put villages to the sword after taking down the Anathema; it is not to kill the people, but to stop the music.

    Sometimes, it works.

                            Haven't gone through the giant thread as of yet so I maybe reiterating something. Please forgive me.

                            For me, I have little problem with Solar Performance. It works really well though I wish it had more enhancement powers (think super bard) and a little less mind assault (emotional control and memory erase).

                            The thing for me is that I am less concerned with Solar charms. I trust you guys on that utterly. I am more concerned with Solars as a whole thematically. For a long time, I didn't like them. I had always from the 2E core book gotten the idea that they were the generic "awesome" splat. After a conversation about the current game I am running. We noticed something. The main book in 2E is really only relevant up until 50 exp or so. We see Solars fighting minor gods and bandits and that is about it. Fast forward to Dreams and suddenly they at 3000+ exp are all douche bags.

                            Nothing in those books communicates greatness. It does not have the same resonance as a Heracles or Gilgamesh.

                            I think for me, I want the performance charms to reflect what has excited me about Solars as of late. The sheer greatness. The unlimited power that any one of them could be the next superman OR the next doomsday (power level in their relative setting analogy). I want the charms to be something that an effectively cosmological force would have. What does performance look like from the perspective of epic greatness. If it is just neat tricks and powers, that is one thing. But I want the charms to from any ability to excite me about the splat. In this case, communicate the granduer or horror that Solars could be.

                            I know that is abstract. Hope it helps.

                            Hokyo Joshua
