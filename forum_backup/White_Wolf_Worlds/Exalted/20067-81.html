                            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                            Anyone know where the rules for jumping are?
                            I'm not sure, but I think it's just an Athletics check. Dexterity or Strength, depending on context. All the rules for figuring out absolute distances and velocities are gone. The benefit of Monkey Leap is similar to Graceful Crane Stance, now. It just lets you do a thing without a roll, no matter how ridiculous it might be, but the vague approximate distance of a single range band imply that it maxes out about as far as a human could conceivably jump in ideal conditions (the long jump record is like 29 feet). If I were running it, it might wind up with situations like this: a Solar flees over a narrow canyon with Monkey Leap but only 3 successes on the dexterity + dodge disengage roll, but the dragon-blooded chasing him have to score at least 5 successes on the dexterity + athletics roll to oppose, or they fall to their deaths.

                                                    Narrative over mechanics.

                                                    The story required that the Mask of Winter overthrow Thorns by rapidly expanding Shadowlands inside the city limits; thus shocking the world by doing something that never happened before ... or since.                                                     So it was a Plot Device. Perhaps you could say it was one-shot artefact or expensive ritual (or necromantic working) that cannot be easily reproduced.
