    Originally posted by Hexmeister Vic View Post
    Ok, so an enemy declares a Decisive attack against your character and you declare that you will Parry. You then declare that you are going to reflexively Clash said attack via Over and Under Method, paying 7m, 1wp. After your (Dex + Melee + everything but Accuracy) roll beats your opponent's (Dex + Brawl/Martial Arts/Melee + everything but Accuracy) roll, you roll (Dex + Melee + everything including Accuracy) vs. your opponent's Defense, then determine your Raw damage for a Withering attack with that roll, roll it, then, after resolving the withering damage, rather than adding the Initiative drained by the Flashing Edge of Dawn to your own Initiative, you add that much Decisive damage, in addition to the extra level gained from the Clash, to the result of the Initiative roll, assuming the target didn't have enough Hardness to absorb the attack, although, and I imagine this is where the confusion arises, I believe that the bonus damage, both from winning the Clash and from Flashing Edge of Dawn, isn't affected by Hardness, and thus is applied directly to their Health Levels. I also believe that the extra point of Initiative gained by landing Flashing Edge of Dawn would not be granted until after the Initiative reset created by the inciting Decisive Attack.

    My RAI/homebrew version is that it allows you to make a reflexive withering clash, and if you win then immediately make a decisive attack that auto-hits with 0 extra successes.
    This makes it slightly stronger by including accuracy bonus on the clash attack roll and allowing the use of decisive damage enhancers on the second step but seems a lot more convenient and easier to use at an actual table. Any thoughts/suggestions/criticisms on this interpretation?

                        Originally posted by aluminiumtrioxid View Post
                        Are weapons created with Dreaming Pearl Courtesan's Elegant Weapon Repertoire Charm compatible with any other MA, or are they exclusive to Dreaming Pearl Courtesan Style?
                        They could hypothetically be compatible with an MA that listed something similar as a form weapon, but they're not compatible with any of the written MAs.

                        Can you use Demure Carp Feint (fluff text: "The Dreaming Pearl Courtesan dances away from the blows of her enemies without effort") to satisfy the "when dancing" requirement of Battle-Dancer Method?
                        My short answer would be no.

                        My medium answer would be, the "when dancing" requirement of Battle-Dancer Method is ambiguous and might be usable as part of a combat style, but that Demure Carp Feint's flavor text definitely wouldn't be an enabling factor even so.

                        My long answer would be that based on balance considerations and - I think, although I don't have cites handy - developer statements, Battle-Dancer Method's "when dancing" is supposed to imply you are engaged in a Performance action to dance. Performance actions generally take much longer than combat actions (e.g. minutes instead of seconds) and so you would not be able to actually take regular combat actions and/or would take a flurry penalty on each one. (This whole area of the rules is a little under-defined.)

                        An alternate interpretation would be that, basically, if you can describe your fighting style as "dancing" then you can use Battle-Dancer Method all the time. The problem with this is that the Charm then seems a little too good and makes the Performance dip really really good for most combat builds.

                        (The problem with not allowing this is that Silver-Voiced Nightingale really seems like it ought to be able to say "yep might fights are all also dances".)

                                        Originally posted by GrayWalker View Post
                                        Can one hybridize demons?
                                        It's tricky, but it can be done.

                                        Originally posted by GrayWalker View Post
                                        I mean if two demons of different types mate would it be a hybrid?
                                        The general rule has been that demons largely don't sexually reproduce with each other (though producing Demon-Blooded children with mortals and animals is another matter). When they have sex, it's most often not for reproductive purposes; the various species of First Circle Demon tend to have unique ways of reproducing.

                                        A blood-ape can reproduce by impaling itself on a spike and howling in agony in just such a way that the scream of torment becomes another blood-ape.

                                        Sesseljae are driven to consume poisons, and when enough of them die from it, a horde of new ones skitter out from the heap of corpses.

                                        Perroneles just sorta emerge from Lucien, IIRC.

                                        And failing all that, a Neomah can fashion together a baby from semen, menstrual blood, or other assorted fleshes and fluids, casting the resulting creation into a fire to give it life.

                                        How could I create a hybrid otherwise?
                                        Sorcerous working, neomah, the Solar Occult capstone…
