      And if you were in the Kickstarter, you’ve already received the final copy on DT RPG.

      Everspace on the Exalted Discord was kind enough to compile the changes which I faithfully copy here:

      **===== Rough Changelog for Release Version =====**

      Various language updates (like Totemic -> Archetype)

      Glossery:
      - Added Lunar Dominion entry to describe what they are.
      - Moonsilver Tattoos: now specifies "unwanted" magical transformation
      - Moonsilver Artifacts: reshape to accommodate their shapeshifted form __if they wish__

      (charms below)
      Charms:
      (-> = Renamed to)

      - **Totem Beast Embodiment** -> **Sacred Beast Embodiment**
      - **Finding the Needle's Eye**: Protean now rerolls an additional 1 instead of ignoring another point of defense. (This is probably a nerf if you are in a protean form)
      - **Flesh Sculpting Art** : Clarify that it's reroll 1s until they fail to appear.
      - **Silver General Foresight**: Clarify that it's reroll 1s until they fail to appear.
      - **Many-Voiced Mockingbrid**: Clarify that it's reroll 1s until they fail to appear.
      - **Brother-or-Duty Dilemma**: Instead of "lower" it is "equal or lesser" intimacy (better)
      - **Echo-Drinking Awareness**: Protean changed to a noncharm success from db9s. (typically better since db9 is "better sense" for animals typically)
      - **Mountainous Spirit Expression**: adds +3 to all raw withering damage rather than just natural attacks
      - **Deadly Claw Blow**: Removed the following:
      ```
      She always uses her unmodiied Strength to
      attack, and doesn’t add dice from bonus Strength dots
      or double non-10 successes on attack rolls.
      ```
      Allowing dice from bonus strength dots to apply. This has little effect on Lunars as written, but if you have ported this charm elsewhere like me, you should review. (better)

      - **Kraken-Arm Lash**: changed from the specific "Ininite Grasp" to the more general "tentacles". (better)
      - **Foe-Defying Frenzy**: No longer resets inititative with the repurchase. (better)
      - **Shadow-Mind Meditation**: Rewording so that it is not "hollywood-multiple-personality disorder" charm.
      - **Lodestone Reckoning Manner**: You can now use sail! Wow! (better)
      - **The Spider's Trapdoor**: Repurchase changes the gambit to difficulty 6 to combine it with an effect, instead of rasing the difficulty by 1. (worse)

      Martial Arts:
      Centipede Style:
      - **Blurring Scurry Prana** -> **Blurring Scurry Technique**

      Falcon Style:
      - **Doom Plummets Down**: mastery effect specifies "she may reflexively rush the struck enemy __from close range__"

      Swaying Grass:
      - **Jubilant Battle Proposition**: __No longer changes join battle__, instead adds (Performance) to join battle, and (Performance) to the inspire if stunted.
      - **Whirling Rhythm Revolution**: Removed the ability to gain the effects from a rush.
      - **Inescapable Tumbleweed Prana** -> **Inescapable Tumbleweed Pursuit*

      Artifacts:
      Night’s Sweet Whisper:
      - **Moonlit Serenity Paean**: Works with Heard-Reinforcement Stance instead of Taboo-Enforcing Beast

      Animals:
      - **Moose**: from 2 parry to 4 parry. Now no longer has a speed bonus of +0. They are now impossible to ride I'm sorry you had to find out like this (/s).

      Backers:
      - Beauteous Jewels of Ending Wrath's wrath is now Unending (better)
      Last edited by prototype00; 10-06-2020, 03:09 AM.
