                                                To my understanding some Exigents fought during the Primordial War. Further the way that the Great Curse functioned is that the Primordials threw it at the Incarna who had appropriate defences prepared. Unfortunately however, it "trickled down" to affect their Exalted.

                                                It's possible that any Exigents who were around at the time were affected by the Great Curse - if the God of Mount Meru had an Exigent, maybe his Exigent was affected by the Great Curse.

                                                But, what about gods who weren't even around at the time? Would their Exigents suffer from the Great Curse? Consider if Tien-Yu, the goddess of Lookshy made an Exigent. Lookshy didn't exist during the Primordial War. Why would her Exigent suffer from the Great Curse? The Exigent would probably still suffer from some kind of "Limit Break" mechanic, just like Alchemicals have one even though they were never affected by the Great Curse.

                                                Of course, it's also possible that the Great Curse could have corrupted the Divine Fire itself and that every Exigent ever produced after that suffers from the Great Curse. Unfortunately we won't know what the deal is until the Exigent book comes out.
