    JTroy's Exalted Sketchbook

    01-26-2017, 09:55 PM
    I've done a lot of Exalted fan art, character concepts, and commissions over the years that I've been playing it. I love the setting, the characters, and the way that it grabs my imagination. I thought that I would start compiling stuff that I've done in this thread, and I'll begin with my favorite setting, Thorns. The Juggernaut rules.





    It's kinda like the Makeout Point of Thorns, isn't it?







    I did this a while back for Forumites Omicron and Vegetalss4 of their characters Idesa Adara and the Celestial Dragon, slugging it out in the zombie-filled streets as the Juggernaut looms oppressively over the city.





    This is a pretty old sketch I did way back when I first began to run Exalted. I've always loved Juggs.

                                      5 expressions of Jade in it's natural, raw form. One of my players was learning mandarin and han at the time, so me and Google translate got together and made what he assured me was a terrible and nonsensical literary puzzle. I passed these along to his monk IC, and he declared them to be ancient art from a revered proto-Immaculate boddhisattva, and went about the process of translating it. What we have below is his (almost entirely fabricated) translation based on the written characters. It was a very satisfying bit of player/ST collaboration.




                                      *(Ex.1: Sample was observed while on trek in the Far East. We were passing through a great bamboo forest- it was so subtle I almost passed it by! It sprouted up from the ground, giving the area surrounding a gloriously verdant vitality. A truly breathtaking specimen.)

                                      *Translated to Dragon Tongue for the modern reader


                                      *(Ex.2: Sample of black jade was recovered from the Western sea 100 miles offshore due west from Blessed Isle. It is uncertain how long it took for such a marvel to gestate, or even how it was possible to form within a giant clam, for it is far more grand than the most lustrous of pearls!)

                                      *Translated to Dragon tongue for the modern reader


                                      *(Ex.3: Sample from the granite quarries of *******. Surely if they had known there was such a rich vein of jade beneath the base earth they mined for, they would have hollowed the mountain sooner. Glory to ******, and Glory to the Empire!)

                                      *Translated to Dragon Tongue for the modern reader


                                      *(Ex.4: Sample taken from the Black Sand Mountain some 150 miles south of Gem. Locals say that the entire range is in fact one single volcano, ever-ready to explode! Seeing this exposed jade deposit, once so hot that the stone melted away from it, I am ready to believe it!)

                                      *Translated to Dragon Tongue for the modern reader
