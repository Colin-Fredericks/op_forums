                    Eh, rolling Dex+Melee multiple times (and Stealth once, for Blazing Battle Feint) was how I acquired the blue jade I used to make my twin short daiklaives and new hand. That'll teach my enemies to have stuff.

                    I've never seen people acquire magical materials with bureaucracy or investigation though.

                    There's a player in my game who has Solar Sorcery, and she does rule her own kingdom. But she's too busy with plot to send out people to find where magical materials are*, then send more people out to go to war with whoever's there, then others to mine it.

                    *Well, I guess she might know that Harborhead has a famous large jade mine, but she's being invaded by the Realm on one side and the Djala on the other, so can't really project her power enough to conquer Harborhead.


                    Anyway, this is not to say that Solar sorcery should definitely not be able to make magical materials (with a working, not a spell). But, as Isator says, probably not massive amounts, or I could sit there churning out a new daiklaive every few sessions, which is something the developers of 3rd edition specifically wanted to avoid.
                    The key thing with magical materials I think, is that unlike, say, money, they're a resource that even high-level, experienced, powerful Exalts can't just acquire easily. I mean, even the Realm has to go to war to acquire them.

                    It's probably worth mentioning that you can make magical materials with Wyld-Shaping Technique, but the amount produced is pretty small.

                                                    Honestly, demonyms are rather arbitrary in the real world.
                                                    Why Taiwanese but Filipino? Portugese but Spanish? The only ones that are pretty standard are -a becomes -an, and -land becomes -ish.

                                                    I will say that countries ending in -s at the end tend to drop it. Filipino, not Filipineso. Cypriot not Cyprusiot.
                                                    So with Thorns and Great Forks you can probably drop the -s. Personally I'd go for Thornese (because of Japan-ese and Taiwan-ese. Though Iran becomes Iranian, Cameroon is Cameroonian, etc. I like Thornese more than Thornian though).
                                                    Hmmm... what countries end with -/k/... I think Denmark is the only one. Which becomes Danish (because -mark is a suffix anyway).

                                                    Whitewallian, Whitewallese, Whitewalli... Whitewallian sounds best, but still ugly. I kind of feel it should just be based on White.

                                                    Fortitude... literally there's no real world country ending in "d" unless it's for "land".

                                                    I've always wondered, what about the Realm? Realmese? Realmian? Realmese sounds best, but not great (and honestly, I think The Realm is actually quite a silly name. Sorry.)
                                                    I'd suggest that maybe the inhabitants could refer to themselves as the Blessed (because they come from the Blessed Isle).
