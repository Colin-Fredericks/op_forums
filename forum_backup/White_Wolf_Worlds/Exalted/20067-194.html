                Originally posted by Fuzzykuma View Post
                How would your deal with hunting?
                Short answer: Not as combat between the hunter and the prey.

                Longer answer: Set a difficulty based on terrain, chosen game for the hunt, and other circumstantial details. Call for a roll, which attribute + which ability determined by what resolves the most uncertain part of the hunt.

                I.e. if it's small fast game, it's likely the most uncertain part of the hunt is actually hitting the mark, so that'd be Dexterity + Archery (or thrown, if that's your thing).
                If it's larger game that a single shot probably won't take down immediately, the roll would probably be Perception + Survival (so you can follow the blood trail and find the animal where it finally bled out)
                But there could also be certain circumstances of a hunt where it is a matter of stealth, or athletic prowess, or even something else that determines whether you can pull it off.

                If the roll is successful, congratulations hunter, you have acquired food.

                I don't go in for rolling out each little bit of a hunt because asking for a bunch of rolls like that just slows down play and ups the odds of failure dramatically (even just 2 rolls, assuming a 50/50 chance of success on each, means a 25% chance at overall success) and nothing is added to the game by attempting to use it for granular simulation of events.

                Save the combat system for things which fight back.

                Not so noble anymore.
