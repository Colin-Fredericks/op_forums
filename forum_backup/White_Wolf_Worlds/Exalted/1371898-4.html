                                            Originally posted by Cypheriel View Post

                                            the fact that you actually have to make that a rule shows the inefficiencies of the system
                                            Exalted is a game whose conceptions involved trying to represent the kind of stylistic, high concept action scenes you'd find in something like Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon.

                                            The Initiative system is an attempt to realise that conception by giving fights a focus on a shifting visual dynamic of who is winning with varied moment to moment tactical possibilities before suddenly bringing down a dangerous blow, rather than cutting things down to incentivize making every attack as lethal as possible.

                                            Trivial opponents and non-combatants is a contingency to maintain that focus, a reminder that the point is cinematic action in which those people don't belong.

                                            And yes, to preempt people that would not engage in their novel idea for representing that action in good faith and instead focus on finding technicalities to exploit. Even then, it's not actually that difficult to reconcile with the diegesis if one actually tries to picture the thing unfolding like an action scene, in which cutting down a helpless civilian is basically an afterthought and contributes nothing to the dynamic flow of the battle.

                                            What exactly do you want? Do you want the core book to have included the exact attack stats for a pebble and to have made them non-lethal so that you have a citation to assure you what is and is not a viable method of applying deadly force? That's not the combat system being weird, that's the writing team having the least bit of faith in their readers that they shall have an idea of what is and is not dangerous and apply their decisive attacks accordingly.

                                            Alternatively, you're speaking as if there are stats for a pebble, and you find them unaccountably lethal.

                                            I have approximate knowledge of many things.
                                            Write up as I play Xenoblade Chronicles.
