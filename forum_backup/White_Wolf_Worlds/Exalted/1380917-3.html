                                                        That's not an opposed roll, it's a Difficulty.

                                                        If something is Difficulty 1, you need 1 or more. If it's Difficulty 4, you need 4 or more.
                                                        When you attack someone with Parry 8, it's difficulty 8, so you need 8 or more.

                                                        When both of you roll dice, the Defender wins. (Although in practice it's not always clear to me who is the Defender.)
