                There is something else about this I was mulling over,
                Realistically, people tend to have children (when it's intentional) with people they can respect. Not insects they could literally kill with an idle thought. That, shared perspectives, ability to relate to one's partner's life experience...and not being terrified out of your mind all the time that your powerful enemies are going to squish your mortal love interest to strike at you.

                In other words, I actually think that in many cases, both parents often WILL be supernatural. Sure, there will be gobs of cases where that isn't the case, but many Exalts likely try to keep some emotional distance between themselves and mortals they find themselves attracted to. It hurts too much to bury someone after only the tiniest fraction of your own life has gone by. Now, that isn't to say that means these childrens' parents will be powerhouses...but I imagine that -blooded of various types often get together with other Ghost/Fae/Demon/God-Blooded. That Outcaste DBs might find love for a time with a small god (or a more powerful Terrestrial god, even).

                Yes, there are all the cases where the supernatural partner simply finds a way to make their mortal love interest live longer (like Fakharu, the Western Censor). Yet I could definitely see significant numbers of those with a supernatural heritage finding common ground, friendship, and later love with others of similar heritage.

                        Equal matings between the powerful are not going to be the origin of a large percentage of the God-Blooded, because there are simply not enough of the powerful for them to make up a large percentage. There's less than a thousand Celestial Exalts, and Terrestrials are in the tens of thousands. But there are hundreds of millions of mortals. And there are lots and lots of Essence 2 Terrestial gods and elementals in Creation, while mighty ones tend to wind up in Yu Shan. The vast majority of cases of humans conceiving a child with a spirit in-setting are going to be mortal with Essence 2 spirit, because the vast majority of contacts between humans of any kind and spirits of any kind are going to be mortal with Essence 2 spirit.

                            Well yes,
                            Just going by the over-scale of looking at the total population, you're absolutely right Verzio. I was simply saying that OUT OF the various God/Fae/Ghost/Demon-Blooded who exist, all the various Terrestrial Gods, Elementals, and Celestial Gods slumming it (Burning Feather, Goddess of Intoxication, seems to have a bunch of canon God-Blooded descendants, for example)...It won't be uncommon for two individuals with shared supernatural heritage to choose to pair off.

                            Given the numbers, their offspring will of course be vastly outnumbered by a) regular mortals, and b) Single supernatural parent -Blooded. (Except perhaps Demon-Blooded, given the sheer proliferation of 1st-Circle Demons escaping into Creation. The escape-conditions of the various 1st Circle Demons are things common enough to happen fairly often given Creation's size.) I was more saying that the factors I described mean there are compelling reasons for supernaturals to pair with other supernaturals.

                            Especially when you talk about the -Blooded getting with a member of their "source species." While many mortals might fear/hate a Fae-Blooded, a Raksha certainly wouldn't hold the Fae-Blooded's heritage against them. Similarly, a Ghost-Blooded raised in a Shadowland might think nothing of having a Ghostly lover who possesses some comely mortal. Many Demons might find the demonic mutations some Demon-Bloods possess that Demon-Blood's most attractive features given the difference in Demonic aesthetic sense. Same for a Lesser Elemental Dragon looking at a God-Blood who descends from an Elemental of their element.

                            In the scope of Creation's Total Population, such beings will always be a vast minority. It had simply occurred to me there are good reasons why a supernatural looking to start a family might not want to do so with a regular mortal. Particularly if that supernatural has already experienced one or more of the drawbacks of pairing with a normal human being in a prior relationship. A Faeblooded who was happily living with their mortal spouse, until some people from the neighboring village who'd lost loved ones to the Fair Folk learned of the Faeblood who was living in a secluded cabin in the woods, came to kill said Faeblood by burning down the cabin in the middle of the night, and ended up killing their human lover instead.

                            To say nothing of the ambitious. A God who had God-blooded offspring as part of a plan to increase their worship in Creation might think nothing of taking their plan a step further, arrange for a Supernatural who owes them to be willing to get with their God-Blooded son or daughter, and tell said son or daughter "If you're hoping to become a God/Goddess yourself one day, get with the program." For more sinister reasons (or maybe not, depending on the God)...I could DEFINITELY see a 2nd-Circle Demon like Sondok simply demand one of their children procreate with a Demon or pay the price for their defiance.

                            All of these circumstances are only contributing to a small minority of the total population, but there's no reason to believe they don't exist. Tiny minority or not, the individuals who result from such unions can definitely have a massive impact on Creation wildly disproportionate to their numbers. The God-Blooded son of the Volcano God Hamoji is the premier Captain, who'd be the go-to if the Feathered One needed an Admiral during hostilities on behalf of Wavecrest. If the ensuing war with Coral aroused Siakal enough she wanted to get with him, their son or daughter could become the West's non-Exalt champion against the Lintha or Silver Prince.

                                    Too true. Makes me remember those ridiculously arbitrary and bizantine ghoul mechanics for Masquerade. Now that is an utterly useless thing some people just can't leave alone in limbo and forget...

                                    Me, i decided to retooling Inheritance using 3e Artifact & Evocation mechanics, just to see how could it turn out, nothing to fancy.
                                    - Inheritance 1-2, just some minor benefit, like whatt one might get from a mutation, thaumaturgy ritual or equivalent low-level supernatural merit.
                                    - Inheritance 3-5, that opens the room not only to awakening one's Essence, but with it the potential to develop a set of evocation-esque powers reflecting springing from the interplay of divine legacy and a hero's own temperament and budding personal legend.

                                    The results have been quite fun so far - one player even invested in having multiple Inheritance, due to a very, very messy family history of spirit deallings and it has worked pretty well so far, to the point that we have considered taking things a little further by mining other Artifact related mechanics for inane stuff like "refining the divinity" within one's veins, sharing legacies through magical vows and a bbunch of other things.
