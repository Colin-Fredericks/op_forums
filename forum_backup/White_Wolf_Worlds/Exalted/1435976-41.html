    Originally posted by Anteros View Post
    Could one of the Demon City's Demon-Blooded exalt as a Celestial or Abyssal while living in Hell?
    Supernatural blood isn't an impediment to Solar/Lunar Exaltation, but being outside Creation is more ambiguous, which I think is intentional. For *my* money, Hell is under the dominion of its own sun with its own champions and could draw neither the eye nor power of bodies whose light never shines upon it, but if you have a cool idea for a Solar or Lunar who Exalts in Hell, feel free to write off the lack of book precedent as being due to the low mortal population more than any rule of reality.

    Sidereals and Abyssals are a different story. Sidereal Exaltations are destined from birth, and Hell has its own stars; someone born there wouldn't fall under the Loom's purview. And while the question of whether it's strictly possible for an Abyssal to Exalt in Hell is likely the same as whatever you decide for a Solar, such an Exaltation also requires the decision/approval of a Deathlord, who, opposite of the hands-off approach of the Unconquered Sun and Luna, Exalt their Deathknights with the express intent of recruiting, training and directing them. And while the Unconquered Sun and Luna could at least theoretically care about the mortal population in Hell, or want a thorn running around in some demons' sides because they're causing trouble in Creation, it's truly difficult to imagine any Deathlord being interested enough in anything going on there to think a disconnected guerrilla agent throwing around the powers of death is doing enough good(/bad) to be worth sacrificing the rare and special potential of a would-be vassal to it.

                That hardly seems unusual.                 The other facet of that section is that the Deathlords do not sit idle for their knights, since they want them collected as soon as possible to begin indoctrination. It's perfectly possible for them to need to Choose somebody that would be weeks or months away from accessing the Underworld if left to their own devices, and with far greater impediments than even Hell.

                In cases like that, the Deathlords send other servants to go get them. An Abyssal Exalted in Hell could find their departure hastened by the assistance of yet another Abyssal.

                (And again, there are lines of communication. That there are Abyssals at all is a result of how the Deathlords could send entreaties to the Demon Princes.                                             Couple questions about things I don't see discussed explicitly anywhere in rules text, so I'm wondering if we have any precedent in flavor text or the short fictions that I haven't seen.

                                            1) The rules don't seem to worry about this, probably because characters are generally expected to be armoring themselves rather than pilfering enemy gear, but armor (especially heavy armor) irl tends to require pretty strict tailoring to its wearer; is there any indication in the rules that artifact armor adjusts itself to its wearer as part of attunement, or do stolen/hereditary/etc artifact armors need to be reworked when they switch wearers?

                                            2) Can the process of Exaltation itself grant skills? This one can sorta be inferred as a "no" from the mechanical side of things, since there are rules for Exalting in play and they don't give extra Ability dots, but that can also be written off as servicing the 28-dot baseline, itself an abstraction for the benefit of mechanics over setting consistency; after all, it's not like E1 outcastes and E2 Dynasts both starting with 28 Ability dots is supposed to imply that Realm schools don't teach anything. So, with concepts floating around like past life memories and Exaltation coming packaged with supernatural talent in a set of skills, is there any precedent for, to use the archetypal example, your naive-but-boisterous farmboy who's never picked up a sword Exalting as a Fire DB or Dawn Solar and suddenly finding it second nature?
