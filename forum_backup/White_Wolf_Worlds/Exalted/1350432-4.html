    Originally posted by Bastet View Post
    The setting changes Ex3 has brought to the Realm have led to some soft retcons of why my renegade Dynasts left the Realm.

    Back when I made them in Second Edition the arranged marriages represented a really low (this could be the basis a dystopian YA novel) bar for why it actually sucked to be a Dynast with all those servants and money. Ex3 did a lot to downplay the consequences of not participating in the system of arranged marriages, making the whole thing the setting equivalent of first world problems.
    Amata Kinslayer's backstory with her husband serves as a counterpoint to WFHW as a much more traditional portrayal of arranged marriage right down to a controlling and abusive husband.
    Which I appreciate because it's such a common trope in fiction that it shouldn't feel closed off to people playing a Dynast for the first time.

        While I agree with the comments that disagree with the OPs characterization of the situation and the Realm:

        Mnemnan Amal: Earth Aspect who favored Medicine who went with the entourage of a new satrap to the West. Besides designing and building several new immaculate temples and monasteries, he also used (rather too much) of the budget on improving clean water infrastructure, improving farming methods, and establishing hospitals. During some of his downtime, he used his medicine charms on the poor and orphaned (rich people had to make an appropriate donation to the temple). The newly built monasteries had an overabundance of young monks who idolized him and there was a bit of a worry that he was being personally worshiped as a byproduct of "local superstitions".

        His motivation wasn't exactly pure benevolence. He believed that freedom from a lot of the worst problems in life made people free to contemplate the dragons and render spiritually beneficial service to the Realm and the Exalted. Starving children don't chant sutras and a population suffering from disease isn't producing tribute.

                                                Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                                Which they might've had a chance at fighting off or negotiating with themselves, if they had the resources.
                                                That might not necessarily be as easy as it seems at first. If the Realm crumbled away it would leave a lot of room open for other empires to spring up all over with the same problems. Some small nations currently under Realm control wouldn’t be in close enough proximity to care much right away, others would fall to their more powerful neighbors, and others would become the new conquers. Cherak is ripe to form its new conquering empire, for example. Of course the people on the Blessed Isle would also suffer greatly.

                                                The Realm is like any historically large and powerful nation, it’s a symptom of human pride and avarice. If you want to solve the actual problem problem and not just knock down Creations superpower de jour you’re really going to have to fix a deeper problem. Any form of government will lead to some people having a lot of power, and they will all have to refrain from using that power to gather more power and security.

                                                If you actually want to have fun playing Lunar Skywalker battling the Empire though, you should probably just not think about that. I’d also look to flossing over a lot of the factional fighting and crimes that are common to rebel groups the world over. Otherwise it’s not so much a game as your ST sitting there lecturing you on how you’re bad people.
