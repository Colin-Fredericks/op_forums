    Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
    Probably to some extent, but I don't think you can just replicate artifacts by interring them. If you want a nega-Karvara to exist in your game, then feel free to declare that it exists, but the Underworld isn't jam-packed with ghost daiklaves.
    I don't see any reason an epic ruler with an attachment to an epic blade wouldn't carry a copy of it with them into death. In fifteen years of Exalted I've never had a player abuse underworld 'copying' in any way that the Chicanary keyword couldn't deal with, despite the knee-jerk reaction of unfairness that most have when reading the mechanic.

    I feel it's important to keep, too. Here's why.

    Say we are creating an Underworld that's the supposed to correspond to an area where an Exalted version of King Arthur lives.

    In the Underworld, this place creates a few regional 'islands' of dead which tie to periods of historical death and strong cultural identity in the kingdom.

    So you've got the Ancient Arthurians in a memory-hell that more or less resembles the land at that time...

    ... then you've got Arthur's grandson, ruling a Hell of people culturally from that time period...

    ... and then you've got modern Brittania, ruled by his descents in this hypothetical other dimension.

    All three of these places are distinct 'kingdoms' in the Underworld that might trade / raid / interact with each other.

    All three of them have a king who was buried with 'Excalibur', the legendary blade of the monarch.

    So all three of them in fact ought to wield it, in the land of the dead.

    This makes sense to me! But I respect all your decisions, you are so ultimately awesome I know you guys will figure it out.
