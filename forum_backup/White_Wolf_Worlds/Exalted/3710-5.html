            Originally posted by Morangias View Post
            Same thing applies as with other Exalted - while a particular DB can, at the moment at least, be weaker than a god, another can probably kick that god's ass, or the same DB can come back with his Sworn Brotherhood and do just that. Superiority is a complex thing in Exalted, and general statements about it don't always apply to the situation at hand, but overall, the Dragon-Blooded are still gods' superiors.
            How? What makes Exalted "the gods' superiors"? How does that account for how gods vary wildly in power? How is the fact that the Dragon Blooded are the weakest of the Exalted in any way meaningful if you just go "they're still stronger than those pissant gods", especially considering that there are great warriors of the Celestial City? How does any of this play into the fact that Celestial Lions have always been presented as actual relevant security for the gates of Heaven, such that a Solar dueling one of them would be hard put and fighting off a dozen would be extremely difficult to surmount?

            And saying "oh, you can just build up your numbers of Dragon Blooded to overcome the god" is quite the cop out, considering that you can do the same with gods, and there are more of them.

            The idea that gods are patently inferior to the Exalted is not actually present in the written setting, and that the mechanics have generally born it out is a failing of the mechanics. The Incarnae are not supposed to be the only gods who are anything comparable to or above the Exalted (look at descriptions of the divine ranks in Compass: Yu-Shan), whatever shoddy mechanics forced their write-ups to accommodate to actually present their status. This is why we need to get rid of universal god Charms.
