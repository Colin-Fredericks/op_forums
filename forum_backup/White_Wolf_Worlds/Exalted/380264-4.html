    Re: spies, if you do tend to try and impersonate the World's Most Amazing Man and you are not the most amazing man, that will perhaps not end that well.

    But! There is nothing forcing you to do that should you choose to play a spy. You can find a cover story that fits your talents - Lunars have a lot more options than others.

    Spies IRL, are for good reason, are backed by large organisations and specifically trained and coached. They do not assume people's identities whole cloth without being an uncannily close match to that person. Spy agencies manufacture identities and lay a lot of groundwork carefully to exploit and match the agent's actual skillset. They often choose naturally inconspicuous target identities for a reason.

    Having powers to help fool others into thinking the Lunar is a spirit, OK - the amount of additional magic you get to bridge the gap should probably not, by itself, have the effect of actually giving you more real capabilities, particularly not ones which can outgrow their function of helping you impersonate the target in question.

    If we end up with a case of "To impersonate Raidan, God of Lightning, I must lightning..." and this transforms into lightning becoming a primary method of attack or used to solve problems far removed from actually impersonating the lightning god, that can be an unintended consequence which could be a problem. Magic which is to help impersonate should have that function and be limited from use for any other function. It would need a fair amount of thought to do well.

    (Same for any Lunar magic from Social or Mental Attributes for emulating a person's skill.)

    Originally posted by Wise Old Guru View Post
    I'd like it to be an option.
    ...
    If you want to play her as someone who studies the occult, who poisons herself and goes on vision quests, who shapes the bright markings on her skin into sigils of terrible power, a shaman who learns the secret ways of the spirits, who studies the ancient languages of gods and demons...and then hunts them? A dread sorcerer who summons demons and orders them to flee, then pursues them through the jungle and devours their hearts to ritually steal their fell powers? A predator who stalks the lands of the dead and the halls of Heaven, luring stray spirits away from their fellows and consuming them, and adding their strange magicks to her own...and, perhaps, becomes more and more spirit and less and less flesh herself as she does so? That should be valid.
    I'm still not *that* interested in this type, but this is a lot more of an interesting way to spin the idea (I might be more easily persuaded here than I think).
    Specifically because she does draw that link between Lunars as gifted sorcerors and the sacrifice of spirits to gain sorcerous power, which is a generally in theme act for sorcerors (not just those who are Lunars).

    It feels closer to how sorcerors learn sorcery. It feels different from that idea of being guy who doesn't feel notable for his talent and doesn't use his skills, whose sole thing is to defeat a godbeast and eat its heart and impersonate it and leach off its style and identity in lieu of having his own Lunar style, skill, own personality as expressed through his Charms and sorceries and his own way of working.

    Main concern there would be more about that stream ending up having a transhuman feel and lacking human warmth or being an overpowered option.

                            If Flickering Star Infusion should work at all on demons, raksha, or ghosts, it won't work as fully as for humans.

                            I don't think any of them have stars that fall when they die, or that Sidereals can place astrological curses or blessings on them.

                            A Lunar with Flickering Star Infusion should still be able to inherit (or share) ownership and attunement to a demon's, raksha's or ghost's possessions (artifacts, graces, grave goods, etc.*), but that shouldn't allow a Lunar to take a Second or Third Circle Soul's place within a Primordial's soul hierarchy.

                            The Primordial and its soul should reflect each other. The Lunar cannot become a soul to make the Primordial reflect him unless the Primordial can impact him in turn. Establishing that kind of relationship should probably require a permanent and irreversible transformation into a demonic soul which costs the Lunar his humanity and Exaltation.


                            * A victim's familiars and bonds with those familiars probably shouldn't count among their possessions, and neither any sapient servitors. You should need to persuade them to obey you (or fool them) yourself, though Flickering Star Infusion or a following Charm/Knack could probably HELP with that.
