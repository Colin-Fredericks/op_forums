                                                            Hey yall,

                                                            I've got a number of questions. Right now i'm running this game:
                                                            https://brotherhoodofthegoldenlion.obsidianportal.com/

                                                            1. Mechanical Details for Martial Arts Fight About to Happen
                                                            We're about to have a very dramatic confrontation between a pc(Shen) and his brother over differing philosophies of the use of exalted powers. His brother is a DB MA prodigy who inherited the family title and rank skipping over the main pc. They were both raised in the same monestary that taught tiger,crane, and steel devil style martial arts. The PC is mainly oriented towards crane style and his charms reflect that. I'm thinking that as his brother left their school a few years ago, he would 1. know the style his brother uses 2. would have started learning a new style as his patrons wealth and privilege afford him the ability to do so.

                                                            As the two stylists are set to fight in a couple days, what recommendations would you use to help me stat out a db that could fight a solar crane stylist effectively given currently revealed martial arts? Are there any combination of styles that would mix well with crane/tiger/or steel devil to effectively fight a crane stylist? The match is not to the death and the brother will demand as his win condition that the PC be banished from the kingdom they inhabit so i can't make the fight too hard. Any suggestions on that front?

                                                            2. A little writer's block
                                                            After the duel, the PCs will set sail to an island where a goddess (red bird) rules over. She will meet them in human form (and not let them know explicitly of her status) and lead them in the direction of another PCs (Yin) missing mother (again they wont know until they go there and meet with the woman). We'll jump into a story about a plot by forbidden gods stealing exigencies and using them to rebel against heaven with Han Tha as the leader. Another PC (Dew) is still worshipping a goddess from her old village from pre-exaltation. This goddess was unknown by heaven to be above ground and is in fact one of the forbidden gods and is based off of Anansi. I'm trying to think of an exigent that isn't of this Anansi goddess or of Han Tha. This particular exigent is meant to be Yin's brother and will probably appear from time to time as a minor antagonist. Thoughts on fun and interesting forbidden gods?
