                                                      I hope you enjoy The Silence Of Our Ancestors. It's a novella, so it'll probably just take you an afternoon or two.

                                                      Circle of Protection... got a bad review on DriveThru that makes the plot out to be nonsensical. I dunno about that.
                                                      Circle of Protection has a very different tone. Less high fantasy, more YA adventure. It's an extension of the anthology story The Circle Will Be Broken, with help from me to better fit the setting.
