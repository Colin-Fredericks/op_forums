                                      I'm rereading The Wheel of Time. This is from the battle of Dumai's Wells.

                                      For those not familiar with Wheel of Time, there is this group of female magic users called Aes Sedai. Think Sidereals with how they rule behind the scenes as they advise every throne.

                                      Anywho, there are two groups of Aes Sedai at this point. The White Tower and the Rebels. Both sent delegations to the the protagonist, Rand.

                                      Anywho, the main group of Aes Sefai kidnapped Rand. And there was a battle. At the end of the battle some rebel Aes Sedai showed themselves as being party to the rescue. But that they didn't follow directions. To their detriment.

                                      “I forget nothing, Aes Sedai,” Rand said coldly. “I said six could come, but I count nine. I said you would be on an equal footing with the Tower emissaries, and for bringing nine, you will be. They are on their knees, Aes Sedai. Kneel!”
                                      Coldly serene faces stared back at him. He felt Asha’man readying shields of Spirit. Defiance grew on Kiruna’s face, on Bera’s, on others. Two dozen black-coated men made a ring around Rand and the Aes Sedai.
                                      Taim appeared as close to a smile as Rand had ever seen him. “Kneel and swear to the Lord Dragon,” he said softly, “or you will be knelt.”

                                                          Originally posted by Accelerator View Post

                                                          Putting it spoilers for those who haven't read it or isn't there yet.
                                                          The Salidar Aes Sedai did come to help. However, they didn't actually affect the outcome all that much. It was Taim's Asha'man who really affected things. Nalek is also right, Rand essentially fled Caemlyn because he learned that there were now 13 or more Aes Sedai in the city.                                                           Also, overall the scene is important because up until now, Aes Sedai had never laid fealty at another's feet. They've always been top dog in the world. This scene shows the balance of power shifting.
