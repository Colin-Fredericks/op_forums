    Something I came up with today, just on the ground that Solar teachers should be able to very quickly teach people by demonstration.

    Excellent Tutoring Demonstration Technique
    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Lore 3, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Bridge
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: First Knowledge's Grace or any 3 charms from one Ability, at least one of which must require a minimum of 3 in that Ability

    The greatest teachers among Heaven's Lamps know that a demonstration of correct technique by the master can be more effective in instructing an apprentice than hours of theory and explanation. This charm educates a target chosen by the Solar, raising one of their Abilities to the next level, as long as this is legal. Player characters must pay for this increase out of of unspent XP, while Storyteller characters can only benefit from this charm once per story.

    Special activation rules: Excellent Tutoring Demonstration Technique can only be activated for a target after they have witnessed a demonstration of the relevant Ability by the Solar. This must involve either a successful roll that overcame a difficulty equal or higher to the dot rating the target's Ability is being raised to, or the use of a Simple or Supplemental charm with an Ability minimum of the same level. Excellent Tutoring Demonstration Technique must also be activated within a day of the target witnessing the feat. Additionally, if the Solar doesn't have First Knowledge's Grace, she may only use this charm to teach Abilities that she meets the alternate Bridge prerequisites, and in any case, she may never use the charm to raise someone's Ability higher than her own rating in it. She can only use this charm on a target once per Ability per story, but this can be reset by using Flowing Mind Prana to successfully raise their Ability.

    At Lore 5 and Essence 2, this charm may be repurchased, becoming capable of educating several people at once. By paying a surcharge of 5m, and WP equal to the Size of the group to be taught (as a battlegroup), she may activate Excellent Tutoring Demonstration Technique simultaneously for all targets who qualify normally.

        Preternatural Beast Union
        Cost: -; Mins: Wits 4, Essence 3
        Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: Penumbra Witch Mastery, Terrestrial Circle Sorcery

        Perform a Sacred Hunt on a magical animal. After declaring the hunt, you must give the animal a defining positive tie to you. When you conclude this hunt, their soul becomes bonded to you and their physical form disappears in a flash of anima.

        While in this magical animal's Shape, your lose access to all Charms except Excellencies and Charms that would allow you to exit the Shape. Your mote pool is replaced with the animal's if it has one and you have access to all of the animal's Abilities and Merits. The animal's mote pool does not regenerate naturally and only replenishes after a full night's rest.

        Only 1 magical animal may be bonded to you in this way. once per Season you may release your bonded magical animal, returning it to a physical body, in order to bond a new one.

            I really like the idea, but I wonder if this restriction is actually a little too harsh. There are some powerful magical creature shapes, obviously, but "absolutely no charms except for Excellencies" is a pretty big limit. We know that magical creatures can have Essence ratings higher than 1 (see Plentimon's Ladybug, for instance), so that might make a useful guide. Perhaps say that while in the form, your Essence is reduced by (creature's Essence +1), for purposes of which charms you can activate, and if it's effectively reduced to 0, you can only use Excellencies at that point? I think limiting an Essence 5 Lunar who's assumed the form of a Plentimon's Ladybug (Essence 2) to only Essence 2 or lower charms is a pretty good balancing mechanism.

            Also, I know it's not your fault, it's a matter of various things being a bit poorly defined, but there probably needs to be some kind of guideline as to what constitutes a "magical beast" in here. I mean, presumably a fogshark or, as mentioned, Plentimon's Ladybug, qualifies. But what about a dog of the unbroken earth? It's listed in the corebook as an elemental, so I'd guess not. But aughdeighes, from Hundred Devils Night Parade, are magical horse-shaped creatures that aren't defined. Are they elementals? Magical horses with ice and cold powers? Something else? It's weird!

                                    Here's a Lunar charm idea.

                                    Remembrance Enshrining Practice
                                    Cost: 10m; Mins: Intelligence 4, Essence 2
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Memory-Drinking Meditation

                                    The memories of the forms she takes are sacred treasures, the Lunar knows, not to be cast aside lightly. They should be preserved, to be cherished by later generations honoring the brave being that a Chosen of the Moon selected to become. This charm can only be activated while the Lunar currently has an Intimacy gained through Memory-Drinking Meditation. It allows her to create a representation of that Tie in some form. The exact form this takes can vary, but it should be something supported by an Ability the Lunar has at least three dots in. Writing a poem on the subject would require Linguistics, for example, while an interpretative dance would be Performance, and carving a statue would be Craft.

                                    Once this representation is created, the Nightwitch can use it to refresh her memory of the intimacy and thus the shape's memories. If she has eroded the Intimacy the object or activity represents, she can re-assume it (which requires re-activating Memory-Drinking Meditation as well) as part of transforming into the shape it is linked to, once again gaining access to the relevant memories. If the representation is a physical object, she must handle it and contemplate its meaning for at least a minute before transforming into the form. If it is a more transient thing, like a dance or sword kata, she must either perform it herself or watch someone else do so for at least 15 minutes before transforming. And if the representation is a written record, any accurate copy will do, but she must re-read and absorb the meaning of the text for at least an hour before the transformation.

                                    Recording someone's memories in this manner means that, for better or worse, they are no longer completely private (either to the Lunar or the person whose shape was stolen). Someone else who sees or otherwise experiences the representation can interpret it and learn about the form's memories. Basic interpretation calls for an (Intelligence + [Ability the representation was made with]) roll of difficulty 1. If successful, the viewer can then use a Read Intentions action to determine the Intimacy the representation is tied to (using the Lunar's Guile as the target, with the usual -2 penalty for thinking they are unobserved). If the interpretation roll achieves at least 3 successes, the viewer also gains a broad idea of the contents of the memories tied to the representation (for example, "This dance represents the memories of a soldier regarding her commanding officer"). With 5 or more successes, the contents of the memories are revealed in more depth, as if the original memory holder was truthfully describing their own recollections.

                                    If another Lunar gains access to the same shape, and also has Memory-Drinking Meditation, he can use a representation created with Remembrance Enshrining Practice to assume the Intimacy and memories associated with it in the same way the original creator can.

                                    If the Lunar knows the Intelligence 5, Essence 3 repurchase of Memory-Drinking Meditation, she can choose when creating a representation whether it includes only the memories directly associated with the Intimacy, or the shape's complete memories. If she chooses the former, when re-assuming the Intimacy she only gains the more limited set of memories, and does not have the option to pay Memory-Drinking Meditation's surcharge. If she chooses to encode the shape's full memories, she can pay the surcharge later and re-assume all their memories. However, a representation that contains the target's complete memories can be read by others for all their recollections, making it more of a risk to create such a representation.

                                                    Spear-Wall (threshold: 2): The victorious general arranges her forces on the battlefield such that a crucial strategic point is commanded by one of her units, which is braced to block a charge. To implement this stratagem, the commander must have access to a battlegroup armed with spears, great swords, poleaxes, or similar weapons that can be braced effectively. If the spear-wall stratagem is implemented successfully, to win the battle the opposing general must eliminate the affected battlegroup. The battlegroup in spear-wall formation receives a +2 non-charm bonus to its Defense against ranged attacks, and automatically reduces the Magnitude damage taken from successful attacks by 1. Melee attacks against the battlegroup are automatically treated as clashes, without the battlegroup needing to declare a delay or spend initiative, and the battlegroup in spear-wall formation receives +2 non-charm dice on such clashes.

                                                    The benefits of spear-wall formation persist for the affected battlegroup until it loses a melee clash, or until it loses a dot of Size from magnitude loss or fails a rout check for any reason.

                                                            Rise! Riiiiiissseee!

                                                            So a couple of my PCs have approached me with requests for custom homebrew Dragon-Blooded Charms. I didn't really want to make a whole new thread for two Charms, but I really... am not sure about whether these are suitably balanced for Dragon-Blooded PCs. I'd love feedback from folks with more 3E experience than me (and anyone else who care's to comment).

                                                            Charm number 1

                                                            The idea here was that PC 1 wanted a Dragon-Blooded equivilant to Celestial Bliss Trick (Performance Charm, Core Page 368), and they presented the "base" Charm below as an Earth aspected Charm. It didn't feel "Earth" themed to me, so I changed it to Wood and drafted some elemental varients.

                                                            Earthly Pleasure Prana
                                                            Cost: 3m, 1WP; Mins: Performance 4, Essence 2
                                                            Type: Simple
                                                            Keywords: Wood, Balanced, Mute
                                                            Duration: (Essence Weeks)
                                                            Prerequisite Charms: Enticing Paramour Passion

                                                            The Exalt draws on the primal Essence that underpins human sensuality, and channels it upon a lover in an intense act of lovemaking. In the afterglow, the Exalt may automatically Inspire the emotion of their choice in their lover. This inflamed passion lasts until the end of the scene, and the Dragon-Blooded gains (Essence) dice to social influence actions targeting her lover for the rest of the scene.

                                                            Thereafter, whenever their lover becomes aware of the physical presence of the Dragon-Blooded whilst this Charm is active, the Dragon-Blooded may Reflexively roll (Appearance + Performance) with (Essence) automatic successes to Inspire that same emotion again.

                                                            At Essence 3, elemental variants of this Charm may be purchased for 3XP each:

                                                            Air: The Dragon-Blooded need not be physically present to re-Inspire the chosen emotion, instead inflaming passions anew by sending written social influence or by sending a gift to their lover.
                                                            Earth: As the Air variant, save passions are re-inflamed when the lover is in the presence of one of the Dragon-Blooded’s subordinates in a Bureaucratic organization or when inside a building strongly associated with the Dragon-Blooded (such as a manse they are attuned to, or the headquarters of an organization they belong to).
                                                            Fire: Fire’s passions burn hottest of all. The Dragon-Blooded may use the inflamed passion to enable Life-Changing Tasks with Persuade actions (though it otherwise only counts as a Major Intimacy, for example for the purposes of modifying Resolve).
                                                            Water: Few things are as fluid as human emotions. Every time the Dragon-Blooded overcomes their lover’s Resolve to successfully Inspire an emotion, they may spend a point of Willpower to change which emotion this Charm Inspires.

                                                            Charm Number 2

                                                            PC 2 has the Orichalcum Powerbow Summer Thunder. She's a Ride based archer who has been deprived of her enormous dinosaur mount... more than once. The suggestion is simple: Phantom Steed as an Evocation of Summer Thunder. Summon a ghostly echo of Hiparkes, prerequisite charm being Squadron-Inspiring Flare, Charm being identical in all ways to the Solar Ride Charm of the same name (Core Page 383).

                                                            I'm fairly... okay with that? But my question is whether it should allow her to purchase Evoke the Equestrian Echo, and whether folks think I should nerf Evoke the Equestrian Echo because, you know, it's clearly meant for Solars (or whether the fact that Solars get the Resonant bonus but a DB wielder won't is a big enough nerf)?
