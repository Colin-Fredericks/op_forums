                2E Social Combat question (and a basic one at that):

                Dodge MDV is calculated at (Willpower + Integrity + relevant specialty + Essence)/2, rounded down. This represents a dedicated refusal to engage in argument.

                Parry MDV is calculated at (Charisma or Manipulation + Investigate, Performance, or Presence + relevant specialty)/2, rounded up as normal if Exalted. This represents the use of logic, theatrics, or force of personality to counter, dismiss, or invalidate an argument.

                                Originally posted by 39Steps View Post

                                Actually, according to MoEP:Alchemicals, Autochthon developed six initial types of Exaltation, from which the Solar, Lunar, Sidereal and Dragon-Blooded were created. Since he didn't tinker with designing Exaltations after that, we can assume that the Alchemical Exalted was one of those six designs. That leaves one Exalt type that was designed, but not known to Creation. So there's your canon lead in for whatever homebrew type you want to put in your campaign! Unless that spot is to be taken up by Liminals, of course...
                                As was said...the 'Alchemical Prototypes' are not one of the 'initial designs.' They are based on his original prototypes. That's why Orichalcum Alchys are kinda like Solars, Moonsilver Alchys are kinda like Lunars, and so on. An early question about the Alcys was answered by saying that they may have been active in Creation for a very short time prior to Autobot ditching Creation. Of course, it was also mentioned that 'maybe they participated in the Primordial War, but did so in a way that left no records.'

                                The Liminals were not among the prototypes. As was stated in the 3E 'what we know so far' wiki, Liminals didn't turn up until after the fall of the First Age. Dev comments range from 'Late Shogunate' to 'After the Great Contagion.' They are, apparently, frankenstein-esque Exalted. Autobot didn't have anything to do with them, as far as we know. Unless they were a shelved prototype or something that someone later found the blueprints for, and recreated them later.

                                Likewise, the Getimian Exalted are 'something else.' They don't conform to the standard model of Exalted, and were thus probably not part of Auto-bot's designs...They've said that the Getimians are still powered by a god or something else incredibly powerful (like all Exalted), but they aren't fueled by an Incarna. They are possibly related to the power Rakan Thulio sought, which 'even the Demon Princes feared to touch' and was apparently powerful enough to thwart Saturn Herself when she made her sign over him.

                                Obviously, Exigents don't count either. They have explicitly said that Autocthon had nothing to do with the creation of Exigence. It's a divine power handed down by TUS to allow individual gods to create a Champion. (and they have been bluntly said to NOT be connected to Adamant).

                                So, really...for the original template, only four of the 6 MM technically ended up with an Exalted tied to them. Solar, Lunar, Sidereal, Terrestrial. So, the way I see it, there are three options here that may or may not be actually played out:

                                1: There used to be two other types of Exalted that were tied to Soulsteel and Adamant, but they (and their patron) were destroyed in the Primordial War.
                                2: Autocthon tossed his plans for the other two...either because there were either no gods properly associated with those materials, or there simply weren't enough Incarna to power the extra two kinds of Exaltation.
