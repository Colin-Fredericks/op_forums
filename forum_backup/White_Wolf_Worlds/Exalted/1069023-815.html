            Originally posted by Zardoz View Post
            In our last game session there was some ambiguity around the dragon-blooded wood signature occult charm, Eternal Death-Banishing Blossom. The first one was wether or not it was dice, or levels, of damage. I assume in all cases where it is not specified you should err towards dice?
            Yes - both as to Eternal Death-Banishing Blossom, and as a rule of thumb.

            Secondly there was disagreement over whether the occultist should reset to base or not after the charms damage effect activates (some felt that it did not specify, so you did not; others felt not doing so made an already powerful effect too strong).
            Doesn't reset, as you're not making a decisive attack.

            Finally people weren't sure if it counted as an attack, specifically for the purpose of onslaught penalties vs those who survived (but, more generally, for the purpose of perfect defenses).
            It's not an attack, although perfect defenses can still defend against amorphous not-attacks.

            In addition, my character ended up taking 8 damage beyond lethal (11 damage total). I decided to invoke the 1/story crippling injury get out of jail free card at the 1-2 level, losing an eye. Some of the other players in the game felt this wasn't in the spirit of the rules, and I should be compelled to take the highest level (negating 5 damage) - questioning why the higher levels exist if you can just choose the 1-2 level. Is the ability to chose to negate only 1-2 levels, regardless of damage taken, intentional?
            Assuming you mean for purposes of preventing incapacitation, I think it's fine, though I can't speak to intent.

            At the other extreme, in our Solar game my Solar took 1 level of lethal, just enough to kill them. Since the character must take a minimum of 2 lethal after negation to take a crippling injury, and I was unable to do so, I was unsure what happens (once again I just took the 1-2 level injury).
            You can't take the crippling injury and are incapacitated.
