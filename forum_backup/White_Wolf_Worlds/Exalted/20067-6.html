                    Originally posted by Citadel97501 View Post
                    Does being knocked down or prone apply a Defense Value penalty? I am having trouble finding that section? I was thinking about it for a Yeddim mounted character, who uses Iron Raptor to knock targets down then his yeddim comes and tramples you?
                    Sadly no, they only take a penalty to their actions, standing up afterwards do however give the normal action. This is because applying DV isn't a "non-reflexive action"


                    Originally posted by Citadel97501 View Post
                    Oh and what about the errata of Iron Raptor Technique and Graceful Crane Stance? Normally you get a balance check to see if your knocked down from an attack, but Iron Raptor dopesn't seem to have that wording?
                    This depends a bit on ones reading of "Automatic knockdown" which can mean one of 3 things.
                    1) the attack automatically inflicts knockdown even if it doesn't have a raw damage greater than the targets (stamina+resistance).
                    In this case gets the normal roll to resist and GCS automatically protects them from it.
                    2) the target automatically inflicts knockdown even if it doesn't have a raw damage greater than the targets (stamina+resistance) and the target automatically fails the roll to remain standing.
                    In this case GCS overrules IRT since it is a defense and the target remains standing.
                    3) The attack automatically knocks the target prone without any kind of roll, even an automatically failed one.
                    In this case the attack "cheats" its way around the charm conflict rules and the target falls prone.

                    Given that the only area where knockdown is defined is the place that contains all the resistance rules I would say that option 1 or 2 are the most reasonable readings of the charm, and favoring option 2 a bit, so GCS protects the target due to the primacy of defense in exalted.

                    *in quotes because Corral Masks are both fully sapient and in fact smarter than most people.

                                            Originally posted by rkbinder View Post

                                            He used Hybrid body to give himself multiple tentacles (he ate a octopus/squid), Foot trapping to keep them close and Thousand claws to give his natural attacks rate 5, then used a Lunar multi-action charm while in lunar hero form and stunted his tentacles "assaulting" the target, dealing onslaught and co-ordinated attack penalties.

                                            By the book, coordinated attack penalties should not have been applied. Those happen when a character has taken an action to set up a coordinate attack among multiple characters, and then on a later tick, multiple characters attack simultaneously.

                                            Onslaught penalties on the other hand, apply whenever the same attacker attacks the same target in a flurry (magical or otherwise) and so would indeed apply.
