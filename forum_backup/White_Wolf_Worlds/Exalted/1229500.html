    Mapping colors of jade to Autochthon's 6 elements

    06-22-2018, 11:16 PM
    So as some of you know I'm currently embroiled in an attempt to bring alchemicals into 3E using mostly the rules for artifacts and evocations. To further this, I've been focusing on which 2E alchemical charms fit with the paradigms of each magical material as laid out in Arms of the Chosen (with the addition of adamant of course).

    This presents something of a difficulty with jade. The more metaphorical expressions of jade are of course unaffected, but the elemental expressions are quite inappropriate for Autochthonia. Who would expect a champion to have wood growing out of them or to have evocations based on stone? So I came up with the following Autochthonian alternates to the elemental aspects of jade:

    First, since jade is the magical material associated with the element of metal I think that all five colors should be linked with metal in approximately equal strength. This leaves 5 elements to distribute.

    White = Crystal (fairly obvious link here. Crystals are often found underground and this is the element closest to earth/stone)
    Black = Oil (once again, strong links here with the liquid/fluid elements. The color even fits nicely for an association with crude oil, though most oil used as lubricant would be far lighter color)
    Red = smoke (where there's smoke there's fire. The elemental association is quite clear, though this is actually the greatest metaphorical difference of all of them I've gone with)
    Blue = Steam (I'm mostly linking the two based on clouds and such, though the element of steam is noted as often being the hottest of the elements while blue jade is often associated with cold and ice)
    Green = Lightning (this is based on three things and was sort of the odd one out but it kinda works. 1. a tree bears a superficial resemblance to a sheet of lightning 2. Wood and lightning share certain traits of wildness and inability to be restrained fully without careful tending and 3. green jade is associated with life and vitality and I think we all know what you hit an experiment with to give it life! Plus iirc Alchemicals themselves are catalyzed by a bolt of Autochthon's essence which may or may not be lightning in that context)

    So, this is the way that I would arrange them, but can anyone argue for switching them around some? Are there are arrangements that better fit the metaphysical attributes of the elements together? I'm certainly not completely happy with the pairings, but the colors of jade were never written with Autochthonian elements in mind so there's bound to be some compromises.

                          5 types of jade with 6 elements does get messy quick with either double representations or odd man out scenarios.

                          Doing a combo method of two jade types per element gives ten options for six elements:
                          Steam= fire & water=black & red
                          Metal= fire & earth= white & red
                          Lightning= air & (fire or wood)= blue & red/green
                          Oil= water & (earth or wood)= black & white/green
                          Smoke= air & (earth or fire or wood)= blue & white/red/green
                          Crystal= earth & wood= white & green

                          How do you want certain elements symbolized? Is smoke based on things burning, or what was burned or the particulates now in the air. Oil has stuff in it, so it becomes flammable or fire based or is an organic substance and so more woodlike?

                          Answering that stuff may help answer those questions. Checking all of the associations between classic exalts and alchemicals might help as well. Or may confuse it worse to be honest.

                                              So thinking about it, I realized that I was thinking too much like someone from our word or Creation when i was resistant to associating Green Jade with Smoke. Autochthonia doesn’t have plant life for people to associate “green” with natural vibrancy the way we do. Toxic clouds would be among the most natural associations for the color for them, and appropriately enough its green jade that poison-based Evocations typically come from.

                                              So

                                              Blue - Lightning
                                              Black - Oil
                                              Green - Smoke
                                              Red - Steam
                                              White - Metal

                                              Which leaves Crystal the odd element out. But that strikes me as thematically appropriate as it’s the element associated with divinity, the secret Caste of Alchemicals, etc. if you need some crystal-themed effect from Jade, have it require all the colors ala a prism!
