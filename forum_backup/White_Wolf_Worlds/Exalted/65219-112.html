            Originally posted by Chausse View Post

            As I said, my read of the Dragon blooded book and the Realm present all these points as very clear. The extremely clear purpose of the Realm and the lineage system is to produce as much pure blood Dragon-Blooded as possible.

            If your character have Well Bred, and you want this specific theme to be totally core of your personnal story, then talk about it with the ST. Say you want to do a scenario about finding not only a spouse who has Well Bred merit, but a spouse that had the most compatible Well bred merit for you. Talk with the ST if you want this to be an easy and glorifying quest you will actually have all your children be Dragon Blooded, or maybe it will be a tragedy because you absolutely hate your spouse because every second with her makes you unhappy, but you find it necessary. Or maybe everything's fine with her and your 2 first children (!) are not Dragon Blooded, and this is an absolute failure for your character, and something to reflect upon.

            My point, as I already said, is that all these themes are covered by the core book to engage with. Marriage optimization on a personnal and institutionnal level, its consequences, its ramifications, etc ...

            This doesn't make this headcanon bad or anything, but I disagreed with your original reaction and I quoted and explained point by point why I disagreed with it.
            You're entitled to your opinion, but I remain unconvinced. Because canonically, a win isn't possible. You can get married and hate your spouse, but unless the dice love you could you can't have all your children Exalt. That's the crux of my complaint. You can get married, hate your spouse and have your first two children be mortal... but there's no "win" condition. At best, if you do everything right, you fail 20% of the time -- whether that's the culmination of an epic quest, or two PCs each spending 2 Merit points. The only way is down. In 2E, you personally couldn't elevate the Terrestrial Host to Legendary Breeding, but over the course of a dynastic campaign you could elevate your own bloodline. Now you can't with no real explanation. Which brings us back to whether the Dragon's just suck at making Exalted or whether Dragon-Blooded can only get worse and weaker.

            There's a lack of granularity, player agency and room for improvement.

            As a set piece, it makes sense. Empires throughout history IRL have fruitlessly tried eugenics, so the Realm fruitlessly trying eugenics is in theme.
            As something for players to engage with, I find it lacking.

            And it's cool that you disagree. If you're having fun with it then go for it. But I really don't see this the same way you do. *shrug*

            (I mean, you can strive to get your Aunts to take your Cousins from 50% to the 80% plateau you sit on... but how do you even begin to measure that as a PC?)

            Hi, I'm JohnDoe244. My posts represent my opinions, not facts.

And... It sucks. You're basically abused. You're valued for your bloodline, and you, as yourself, am a small part of a giant cog machine. You're not allowed to marry for love. Cause guess what? That same bloodline that gave you power must be preserved at all costs.

                                You're an outcaste drsgonblooded. For years, you were a minor peasant child in the threshold. And then...you exalted as a chosen of the dragons! The might of the elements flow in your veins. Spirits treat you as an equal. And you're invited to the Blessed Isle, the richest and mightiest place on Creation. And you're... Given the choice between the razor or the coin. Eugenics, remember? No matter your character. No matter your accomplishments. No matter your might. If you don't have good breeding, they're not going to marry you. And if they do? The dice gods whack them.

                                The 'super-powered bloodline' of the drsgonblooded uplift and help them, and also humiliates, denigrates, and kicks them down. Drsgonblooded breeding basically ignores character, morals, achievements, in favor of the bloodline and family of the person.

                                Just like in real life eugenics.

                                You also see this in the solars. Yeah, you're the chosen of the gods. The most high gave you the greatest weapon in the universe, patted you on the back, and gave you free reign. And guess what?

                                It ain't all that great. Havesh is a murderer and rapist. Lyta is that character that seeks out to obliterate the evil empire brought to its logical conclusion. And that's not thinking of how ridiculous some exaltations are.

                                What, you prayed for help and the unconquered sun gave you a spring to save your village? What about everyone else? Did those mothers praying their children be spared not pray hard enough? Did those fathers begging the gods to save his sick children not love them enough? What is that, Arianna? You studied so hard that you gain a solar exaltation? Guess that scholar that studied for 50 years didn't count.
