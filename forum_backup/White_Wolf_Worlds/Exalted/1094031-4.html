                Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post

                Just keep in mind that in the end, it's not terribly important how many people are playing Exalted today, as opposed to how many were playing 6 or 7 years ago. Whether there's 10 million people, or 10 thousand, the two things that matter are that that OPP is going to continue to publish Exalted books, and the fact that you like Exalted.
                Yeah, Exalted is doing great, so lots of people are clearly buying books and considering playing. I think it just has yet to hit critical mass in this edition and I strongly suspect the DB book is going to be sort of a moment of rebirth. The old community will never come back but the new community may eclipse it yet. We have our own age to write, no need to pine for the glory of the old!

                I should also note to give full context to the anecdotal lack of Exalted in my area that RPGs in general have completely crashed lately, not JUST Exalted. Our last game store converted into a Warhammer place a while ago and the writing is kinda on the wall that it's becoming a digital age for gamers in my region. So I wouldn't take my inability to recruit as anything close to a death knell for the line.

                            At least in my local vincinity and in my online contact list there is a steady growing of interest. None of these people is active on the forums or other exalted online communities though.

                            We started with 4 guys 2 years ago and now we are actively playing with 9. 2 don't have the time to play at the moment and at least 2 to 4 people I know are at least interested. There is also a completely different group of at least 4 players that play Exalted but not in a way my guys and I would like to play.
                            Only one guy our group tried to convince to play said it was too complex for him.

                            So yeah we went from 4 to around 20 people within 2 years. Probably more if the other group also recruited a few people. I don't think this means the game is dying. Ex3 only added to the interest since everyone got enthusiastic once again.
                            Pretty sweet honestly.

                                    Magic: The Gathering wounded the RPG playerbase, but it was Warhammer that took advantage of the onsought penalty and dealt a decisive blow. Exalted has actually adopted well to the digital shift, but I haven't - I haven't been able to recruit online or play online, really, so the death of the old school recruitment pool has hit my games pretty hard. When I was pulling groups five years ago I had three or four reggies, but these days It's 1-2 player games (which actually Exalted is particularly good for, as crunchy / deep systems really shine when you have fewer people at a table and can bog with a full 5).

                                    I think Exalted 3e is pretty much the best 1 on 1 fantasy game (1 ST, 1 Player) on the market. But I still wish I didn't have to play it that way. If only I could adjust to not playing face to face...
