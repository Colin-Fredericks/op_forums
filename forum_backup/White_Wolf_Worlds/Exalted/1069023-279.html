                        For example: General Sun Tzu rolls twelve successes on his Stratagem roll, and used only General of the All-Seeing Sun and Excellency on his roll.
                        Which of the following is true:
                        A: Sun Tzu's player declares he wants to attempt a Strategic Placement (1 Threshold Succes) with 3 of his successes, a Fortifications (2 Threshold successes) with 4 of his successes and a Demoralized (2 Threshold Successes) with the remaining 5 successes. The result is then compared to the enemy General's roll, and if the opposing general rolled 3 or less successes, Demoralized happens, 2 or less all three happens, and otherwise none happen.

                        B: Sun Tzu's player declares he wants to attempt a Strategic Placement, Fortifications and Demoralized, combined 5 Threshold successes. He compares to his enemy. If the enemy rolled 7 or less successes, all Stratagems happen, otherwise none happen.

                        C: Sun Tzu's player declares he wants to attempt a Strategic Placement, Fortifications and Demoralized, combined 5 Threshold successes. He compares to his enemy. If the enemy rolled 7 or less successes, all Stratagems happen, otherwise the player has the option to have certain Stratagems not happen. So if the enemy rolled 9 successes he could lose the Fortifications but still have the other two happen.

                        D: Sun Tzu's opponent rolls 6 successes. He now chooses to implement an Ambush (3 Threshold Successes), as well as a Demoralized and Strategic Placement.
