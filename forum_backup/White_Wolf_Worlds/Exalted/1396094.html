    Shaping Rituals and You: A Guide To Power

    06-12-2020, 09:45 PM
    There wasn't a good guide to shaping rituals, so I made one. It includes the Core Book, What Fire Has Wrought, and Fangs At The Gate. They are broken down by Initiation and listed with catchy names in the order they are displayed. I didn't cover any of the merits involved, just the Shaping Rituals themselves.

    CAVEAT: There is an assumption with this rating system: the ability to quickly cast your first in-combat Sorcery is much more important than your ability to get off your second or third spell in the scene. This is based on my personal play experience, as well as a deep dive into threads about people's experience with sorcery- namely that many Sorcerers just cast their one big spell with their Shaping Ritual and then proceed to fight with charms. This will skew toward rituals that offer a one-time power boost rather than those that offer you a slow and steady trickle of power. As always, Your Mileage May Vary.

    The Shaping Rituals will follow this format:
    Name of Initiation
    Name of Ritual: Basic Description of Ritual
    Motes: How many motes you get, how you get them, and how long they last.
    Control: is there a bonus for control spells
    Opinion: a general feeling of perceived use or inherent problems.
    Verdict: is it worth taking.

    Author's Note: The "value" of Intimacies and Stunts comes up quite a lot. I will post those here for your ease of reference.
    Intimacies: 2/3/4 minor/major/defining
    Stunts: 1/2/3
    I will not be evaluating Sorcerous Merits in this post.
    CORE

    Bargain With Mara: you boinked a sex demon. Good for you.
    Unrequited love: if you have someone with a tie of Love towards you, that you do not return, you can pull sorcerous motes from their affection. You monster.
    Motes: 4 for a defining, less for weaker ones.
    Restriction: once per person per day. No more than 10 motes per scene.
    Control Bonus: pull from more than one person at a time.
    Opinion: very weak. You need multiple unrequited loves around you to use this, and honestly it means you either worked this out with a player or you have multiple sex-flunkies around and no one wants to play with that guy. Mechanically its a poor effect, you get 4 motes from someone who is defined by their love for you, once a day.
    Verdict: Pass.

    Sex Vampire: You have to sex someone and then eat their soul, literally killing them. How the hell did this pass editing?
    Motes: Spend a bound soul for (Ess+WP) sm. You can keep (Ess+Occult) souls bound at one time.
    Control: Spend a soul in place of paying a willpower to cast a spell.
    Opinion: what the fuck is wrong with you.
    Mechanical Opinion: 6 to 15 motes per scene is pretty powerful, you can keep 4-10 of these on hand, and depending on your pick up game it should be easy to replenish these.
    Verdict: Literally no one wants to play with this at the table. I would hard ban this option and re-evaluate my relationship to anyone who wanted to take it. Mechanically it depends on how often you can bang.

    Cast In Shadow: Night Caste can Cast Too. You get free motes for being in the shadows.
    Motes: 3 for starting a scene in shadow. +1 ever Shape Sorcery for staying in the shadow AND the target is unaware of you. A night with no moon counts as shadow.
    Control: None
    Opinion: Designed for assassins. 3Sm to start, and the +1 is conditional. If the target is unaware of you, it probably doesn't matter how quick you can cast this.
    Verdict: Pass.

    Pact With an Ifrit Lord: Pyromancy with extra steps.

    Fueled by Fire: Pull sm from a fire source. Might extinguish that source.
    Motes: (Essence) from fire in Medium range. Torches: one time use. Bonfires: 2. Burning house: infinite. Works on Fire elementals too, if you beat their resolve.
    Control: Doesn't diminish the fire.
    Opinion: Very thematic, mechanically weak. Terrestrial Circle Sorceries cost 15sm so this is next to useless. It's weird that the size of the fire doesn't give you more motes.
    Verdict: This is cool fodder for stunts and memorable moments. Mechanically, however, it's quite weak.

    Burnt Offerings: throw treasure into a fire to please Flame Daddy. Relies on 3 non-sorcery stats.
    Motes: hope you like math. (Cha+Perf) against Diff(5-Resources burnt). Gain (Ess+Bonus Successes)
    Control: zilch.
    Opinion: this requires Charisma, Performance, Resources, and Downtime. And you lose the Resources. However, this begins the trend that Excellency-able, Storable Shaping Rituals are OP.
    Verdict: its only worth it if you already have a character who fits the stats and wants to splice sorcery. Otherwise, pass.

    Burning Passion: torture a metaphor to get sorcerous motes.
    Motes: if you would get a point of Willpower from upholding an “passionate emotion or fervent belief” intimacy, turn that into 5 sm instead (lasts until sunrise). Also you can downgrade an intimacy to get points, once per day.
    Control: nope
    Opinion: it rewards a certain type of character who always goes out of their way to uphold their intimacies, like a shonen hero. But also it lets you downgrade intimacies for temporary power, so its basically like being a drug addict. I guess you could do something with that contradiction there. This requires a lot of spotlight and rp'ing a hothead character. The motes are temporary so you got to be always working this, and I could see how that requires a lot of ST-Player work and also other players to be onboard with you being a firebrand.
    Verdict: Its a lot of work for 5sm per scene. Pass.

    Scarred By Nightmares: Lovecraft Lite

    Last Night I Dreamt I Went to Manderley Again

    I mean, uh,

    Dark Dreams: When you sleep, have an uneasy time of it and a dark vision.
    Motes: give up the willpower you get from sleeping, and roll (Wits+Occult) to get sm. Once per story, stunt out how you saw this in your dream and get (Ess x [Stunt+1]), yay math. Lasts until you sleep again.
    Control: if you spend ALL of these motes on your control spell, they count for 2x.
    Opinion: This certainly seems OP AF, except that you only carry the SM until you sleep again, which means you need to forgo the willpower from sleeping every night to make sure you have them. And you need WP to do sorcery so...
    Verdict: This ritual is is probably the best balanced one in the whole book, because its easy to carry a massive load of sm around but you also wont get back WP which is a super important resource. Powerful but not OP, thematic as hell.

    Deranged: Its not a phase, mom! This is who I am!
    Motes: verrrry conditional: stunt your derangement/taboo to get the Stunt rating as sm. Get 2 more if its a control spell. Lasts until sleep. Get 10 sm the first time you stunt your shaping roll with the flaw in a way that gives you Solar xp for upholding a flaw, they last until the end of the story. After that, each time you do the flaw thing gain (Ess) sm.
    Control: 2 more sm for the control spell
    Opinion: this has a lot of bookkeeping to it. Mostly though, its Stunt Rating (so 1, 2, or 3) per cast with the one time bonus of 10 motes.
    Verdict: if you want to play a tortured lovecraftian pc, it can be very thematic. Mechanically its too much work for too little bonus.

    Eating Mental Anguish: consume emotions for power.
    Motes: whenever someone else gains or loses an intimacy, you get motes for it. 2/3/4 for Minor/Major/Defining. If anyone gains Limit, you get their (Limit Rank). If someone else is in Limit Break, you can get 10sm. Lasts for the story.
    Control: none
    Opinion: Requires your party/NPCs to be gaining and losing Intimacies at a good rate, so how useful it is depends on your group. Personally this hasn't happened much at my table, but the motes last for a story, so... hard to say.
    Verdict: this depends entirely on your table.

    Soul Perfecting Elixir: will absolutely tell you they are Vegan.

    Protein Shake: Get mana from alchemical Bro Science
    Motes: Drinking a potion lets you roll for (Stam+Occult) motes. that's probably 5 without excellencies, 10 with excellencies.
    Control: you can make a potion that doesn't go bad ever, but only for the control spell.
    Opinion: FIXED. I had originally misread this one. It's fine, Difficulty 2 for a regular potion or Difficulty 4 to get a weeks worth, pretty easy.
    Verdict: Good. Difficulty 4 is an easy Craft roll and you'll almost certainly have this on hand all week, and it lasts for multiple sorceries.

    Power of Prudes: Never have fun, get SM
    Motes: (Stamina) motes every night of sleeping, until the next sleep. You also get 1 more mote per point of Deprivation, but deprivation is just a -3 after “(Stamina) days without food, and final hours (of life) without water”, which seems like it would harm your internal alchemy but what do I know. Once per story, (Ess+Stam) sm, for the whole story.
    Control: none
    Opinion: How the hell does Deprivation even work here, the -3 comes out of your Shape Sorcery roll (and EVERY OTHER ROLL TOO). Otherwise its just a flat 5 motes per day with an RP restriction of being a crossfitter.
    Verdict: Lame. The 5sm (for max Stam) bonus is weak and the Story Length bonus is one time use. You can also loose these if you don't live like an ascetic (which you are begging your ST to challenge). Hard Pass.

    Talisman of 10k Eyes: We saw Doctor Strange too.

    I See You (Bojack): get sm for noticing stuff. No really.
    Motes: 1 for every 2 success on a JB roll, lasts for the scene. Add Occult if there are other Sorcerers or Demons.
    Control: None
    Opinion: Oh hey, free motes for what you are already doing.
    Verdict: Very Strong. This is basically the “free sm” ritual. Ripe for MinMax abuse. If you are a combat character just dabbling in sorcery, take this one.

    Arcane Focus (wait wrong game): get sm for using your shaping ritual.
    Motes: Stunt Rating +2, once a scene
    Control: more than once a scene.
    Opinion: for an RP ritual, this is bland. It's most generic white bread ritual in the game- use your ritual to get a bonus for using your ritual.
    Verdict: Bad. Its gonna get you between 3 and 5 sm, which sucks, but you have a magic doodad so you might as well use it.
    Special: This is one of the rare Shaping Rituals that let you steadily gain power, since you can use it over and over again on your control spell... but that's not really as strong as you were hoping for in the heat of battle.

    Investment of Power: Motes in, Motes out.
    Motes: commit up to 10 motes of essence, and then get (Ess+2) sm's per turn back out.
    Control: trade essence motes for sm's at 1:2 instead of 1:1
    Opinion: the committed motes are a harsh cost, because you never have enough motes in my experience. But trading them 1:2 is pretty good. However, you only get 3~7 per turn, so...
    Verdict: I'd probably pass this one. If you don't use a lot of motes for your build, though, it can be really good.

    Miscellaneous: these were too good to throw away but have no theme.

    Basic Heptagram: Look it up in a book.
    Motes: (Int + Lore), and maybe one or two more if you have a librarian on hand. For the Story.
    Control: No
    Opinion: are you really going to go to the library between every spell casting? No, no you aren't is the DB initiation before WFHW came out.
    Verdict: NPC only, this sucks.

    Storm Born: Powered by Thor.
    Motes: 2/turn in the storm, or (Essence) one time if only a little windy. 5Sm per point of damage taken by stormy environmental effects.
    Control: No
    Opinion: Yeah I saw Stormcaller in AotC too.
    Verdict: Situational at best. Pass unless you are the one making storms.

    Geomancy: Except its actually “Owning a Manse”
    Motes: (Ess+WP) motes, but take 1 Agg damage. Last for 2 weeks. +1 each turn if you are in the manse.
    Control: Seeing a theme here? No.
    Opinion: The Damage heals in a single day if you aren't otherwise wounded, but this is a flat 6-10 sm so neither the benefit or cost are worth much.
    Verdict: Its fine, I guess? It's definitely half baked.

    Just Performance: what if we took all the creativity out of using music to inspire people?
    Motes: (Performance) for a scene, after using Performance to influence people... in a way that matches the spell. Not a (Cha+Perf) roll, a flat bonus, and only if the spell somehow themes into your song?
    Control: Really? Of course not.
    Opinion: Did we just need to pad the word count? Unless you have a song for inspiring pyromania (Brilliant Raptor) or a song about flechetting the enemy (DoOB), what's the actual point of this? You have to move people by Performance in the scene, and find a way to match it to the spell. Its gonna lead to tortured, twisted equivocation at best.
    Verdict: This is just bad.
    Special: I know you don't need to sing. Bite me.


    What Fire Has Wrought

    Heptagram: Exalted Hogwarts AU.

    Book Learnin': I already did this one. Told you it was weak on purpose.

    Mudra Mudra: This is just the Arcane Focus ritual for the Talisman but fluffed differently.

    Actual Geomancy This Time: Battery Hearthstone.
    Motes: +1 per turn in a Manse. Once per story 5sm for emptying a hearthstone, 10 for a greater one. Hearthstones are depowered until the end of the story.
    Control: No
    Opinion: I guess I should evaluate these by the lowered standards of Deebs, but even then, its pretty weak.
    Verdict: pitifully weak. Like Dynasts. (Solars4Lyfe ride or die)

    Wanasaan Exorcist: Something about drowning.

    Paper Tiger: Sacrifice something to yourself to placate your own ghost...?
    Motes: destroy (Resources) effigy to get (Resources) sm for the day.
    Control: No
    Opinion: for this to ever matter you need to be R4 or R5.
    Verdict: No.

    Drown Yourself: Meditate underwater to get motes.
    Motes: (Wits+Occult) after 1 hour of meditation. Lasts for story or until you meditate again. Limit once per day.
    Control: No
    Opinion: Oh hey, easily bankable, excellency-able sm's. The best and most OP kind. This is better than any of the Solar Rituals. The ease of use + the lack of any meaningful restrictions are what makes this very strong. Compare it with Protein Shake from the Alchemy rituals to see what I mean.
    Verdict: Best Ritual in the game.

    (Metal Gear Solid 3) Ghost Eater: fix a ghost's unfinished business.
    Motes: Roll (Stam+Occult+ Ghost's Essence) after you fix a ghost's unfinished business. Maxes out at (I stopped caring) motes. ([Ess+WP]x4)
    Control: I guess we just arent doing this in WFHW
    Opinion: you REALLY have to commit to helping Casper move on for this to matter. The high maximum is offset by how hard it is to build up.
    Verdict: Take this for RP, its almost useless otherwise.

    FANGS AT THE GATE

    Raksi's Tutelage: Notice me, Senpai!

    Side note: so now that it's Book 3 all the shaping rituals have cool names. Way to ruin my fun, devs.

    Balancing on the Edge: The Same, But More
    Motes: 0. Yeah, this ritual actually lets you repeat you shape sorcery roll to do the same sorcery again but tweaked in a different way. Limit once per story
    Control: Get the bennies you got the first time round.
    Opinion: the effects are pretty open ended but you have to make a whole new Shape Sorcery roll, so it aint happening soon. I'm actually not sure if the first spell happens right away or you have to wait until the second roll is done too.
    Verdict: I have no idea. Somebody else can take this one.

    Sigil-Skin Geometry: Power Tattoos!
    Motes: (Int+ Linguistics or Lore) against Diff 2. Extra successes are motes. Can only do one spell at a time. Limit to one spell per day. It doesn't expire though, you keep it until you use it.
    Control: add (Ess or 3) noncharm dice, whichever is higher.
    Opinion: Remember what I said about Excellencies, bankabitily, and OP? Well here you go. The “drawback” is that it only works on one spell at a time, but you only need (and probably only have) one combat spell at a time anyway.
    Verdict: The Second Best Ritual in the Game.

    Terror Relishing Art: Scare people, get motes
    Motes: After you succeed on a roll to Threaten/Inspire Fear, gain (Attribute Used) motes for the scene. If they are a higher Essence than you, gain the difference in your Essence. So this will usually be 5sm, sometime 6 or 7,
    Control: none
    Opinion: I've seen the Lunar Terror build, but this is niche even for that. Will probably make for cool Trex rp stunts though.
    Verdict: This is more of a “cool” Ritual than a “Good” ritual. But if you wanna feel like a badass you could do worse.

    Jasmine Gems of Mishiko: All the nonsense koans you can shake a crystal flower at.

    Remembrance of Mishiko: Sacrifice Resources and use a Perf…. wait a minute
    Opinion: this is literally the “Burnt Offerings” Ritual refluffed.

    Sorcerer's Quest: Follow the plotted line.
    Motes: you get one motes for each piece of candy you find in the woods, up to 10sm, which last for the story. The ST is supposed to put these infront of you like mini plot hooks. Also, if you have an Intimacy that ties into a place where you find the Forbidden Sorcerer Candy, you get (Ess x 2/3/4) sm until you leave that place.
    Control: Nope.
    Opinion: This is the Plot Hook ritual, where you are supposed to hear about the crystal flowers and then go get them like collectibles in a video game. It might get shoehorny real fast, or it might be awesome, heavily ST dependent.
    Verdict: Mechanically weak. I have to admit a bias though- I love flowers, crystals, and adventure so I actually like this one for reason aside from its usefulness.

    Twilight Koan: Riddle Me This, Lunar-Man
    Motes: (Higher of Lore or Int) sm which last for a scene, so basically 5sm.
    Control: No.
    Opinion:does that mean they only last for the scene you get them in? I would think RAI no, but, we'll just say they carry over into the next “real” scene. Get these motes Morning and Evening. Its kinda silly, but at least you get to show off your nonsense koan abilities to everyone.
    Verdict: a free 5sm just for existing isn't bad, but it's not nearly as good as some others.

    Root Lore: Druids in Exalted

    Master Apothecary Enlightenment: Introduce a fact, heal someone, or use craft, to get SM
    Motes: (Int + Extra Successes) on the plant fact or healing roll. That could be very big. (Int+ Silver Points+[Gold Pointsx2]) on the crafting roll. Could be biggish. Story length, can only use one or the other effect, they don't stack
    Control: no
    Opinion: This is rewarding proactive character play with motes, this is how all rituals should be designed. The motes are both Excellenciable and Storable, the best combo of all.
    Verdict: Amazing. This is probably the best designed ritual in the game.

    Sorcerer's Harvest: Tap a Forest for Green Mana
    Motes: (Essence) from a big plant. Hey isnt this....
    Yeah this is the same as Fueled By Fire, but plants this time.

    Thousand Bloom Arts: Unlimited Plant Works.
    Motes: once per scene, you can get highest of (Int, Lore, or Survival), so basically 5sm.
    Control: No
    Opinion: This requires a Diff 2 (Int+Survival) roll to find plants enough for one casting, or Diff 4 to get a whole weeks worth. Does this remind you of Protein Shake? The difference here is that bonus successes do not become SMs. The difficulty is basically non-existant because you are certain to throw like 20 dice at this, so its more like “once a week, remind people you are an herbalist, always have 5sm on hand”
    Verdict: A lot like Twilight Koan, this is basically just giving you SM for existing. You could do worse than a flat 5sm once per scene, so it's probably the most middle-rank, Benchmarkable ritual in the book. Its slightly better than Twilight Koan too, because if you do the Difficulty 4 roll you have enough plants for multiple scenes per day, unlike those zen dweebs who have to wait until nighttime.


    Summary: Far and away, the best Shaping Rituals are those that that you use an Excellency in your downtime (where spent motes essentially have no cost) to store Sorcery Motes.

    Most Powerful Ritual: Wanasaan Exorcist's Drown Self ritual. It's a simple (Wits+Occ) roll that you can full excellency and Charm, there's no difficulty to beat, and the motes last for the story. You just need an hour of downtime and some water. The only drawback is needing to go underwater again to use it. Obviously it becomes weaker the more Sorcery you need to cast in a single day, but the trade off is worth it.
    Runner Up: Sigil Skin Geometry, which is very similar.

    Best Designed Ritual: Root Lore's Master Apothecary Enlightenment. Encourages proactive use of a certain playstyle, while also offering a lot of power.
    Runner Up: Dark Dreams, which has a great tradeoff for power.

    Most OP Ritual: The Talisman's I See You, for giving you free sm on a roll you're heavily incentivized to max out anyway.


    Hey, Overlord, why isn't Most OP the same as Most Powerful?
    Because of the way SM come to you. I See You is the Single Point of Rituals- its a winmore added to mechanics the game already wants you to be disgustingly good at, for no cost because it's a free generated effect. If you are going to play a combat monster this is the Ritual for you.


    Worst Ritual In The Game: Bargain With Mara's Sex Vampire. It gets you 0 motes because I won't play with anyone who has it.

    The Benchmark Rituals: Mishiko's Twilight Koan gives you 5sm at dawn and dusk, no rolls or anything required. Root Lore's Thousand Bloom Arts gives you 5sm every scene as long as you can find plants once per week. These two are basically the “Have 5sm on hand just for existing” rituals. If any ritual is weaker than this, its weak. If it's more powerful than this with no drawback, its strong.

    TL;DR- Excellencies are OP.
