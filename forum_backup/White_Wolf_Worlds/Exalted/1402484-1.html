                                            Others have spoken of the possibility of Kirimu the generous, the god of the mountain & mine, making deals or browbeating earth elementals into working for him and i agree that bringing elementals into the mix could bring a number of hooks and options to the table.

                                            Braninthejar, you have 2e books, no? One thing that might be of inestimable utility and inspiration in devising themes for elementals and their powers would be the "associations" box in page 48 of the Oadenol's codex, that explores the subject of how elemental (and celestial) aspects may express themselves and the points of thematic intercession/overlap that may exist between them.

                                            If you don't have it, the section on the supernatural qualities and resonances of the many types of jade in Arms of Chosen can certainly serve to fill the same thematic inspiration role in coming up with your own weirdly colored alien people, beasts & such, i guess.

                                            With that "dirty mechanics" part out of the way, i would like to speak a bit on their narrative options, fluff & possible roles in the current lore. While not word by word, some bits straight from the 3e corebook:
                                            (These focus more to elementals of "natural" origin - those shaped by a sorcerer's will may have some themes uniquely their own, but that is for another post)

                                            - Elementals are not divine representatives or overseers of places, things, or concepts. No portfolio and cosmic role do they fill and any duties or responsibilities are completely self-imposed, by virtue of either choice or coercion, force & manipulation. But beholden to or defined by such things they are not.

                                            - No spirit-sanctums and temples in (or outside of) Creation await them, except those taken by virtue of either force, guile or luck and even then it is a given/taken abode of a glorified tenant (or squatter).

                                            - The elemental races are old beyond measure. Before men, before beasts, and some say before even the gods, elementals have stalked Creation, birthed into the world from the interplay of the world’s natural energies. Storm fronts, forest fires, earthquakes, mudslides, deep ocean currents, lightning strikes and blizzards, the heat-haze of the desert and the crushing force of the avalanche are their careless unresponsive deadbeat parents of sorts.

                                            The last fragment in particular, the idea of elemental races going side by side with primordial races, playing out as either allies, enemies, rivals and/or surrogates to Dragon Kings, Jadeborn, Lintha and a number of the strange beings inhabiting still in the shores of the Dreaming Sea, is a major change from elemental portrayal in previous editions and a most welcome one, at least imho.
                                            - Being products of the (super)natural cycles of Creation, elementals are beings of the material world, closer to fauna, flora and mortals in perspective and experience than the numinous beings of Yu-shan, Underworld, Malfeas and stranger abodes. Caverns, cities, clouds, deserts, forests, mountains, palaces, plains, ravines, rivers, seas are some of the many other demesnes and places, enchanted or mundane, may they be found on and in some cases decide to call home.

                                            - Plant, beast, person, monster, hero, devil, god: any or all of those things they may be, alternately or at the same time, depending on their interactions with the physical world around them and whatever crosses their path and leaves a mark upon them, for good or for ill. In that they can be peculiarly strange in many ways and yet curiously similar to the recently Exalted trying to find a place in Creation for themselves and their barely understood powers and urges.

                                            In fact, i wouldn't be surprised if the first exposure of many (individual) elementals to the idea of themselves as divine or at least supernatural was through the perspective, first contacts and interaction with mortals, what in itself may bring an interesting twist and angles to their variegated stories.

                                            And now i'm imagining some people dedicated to seeking out freshly spawned elementals of certain sorts to manipulate into becoming the gods, demigods and heroes of their city-state or nation. Or a sorcerer maintaining a "provider of gods" scam through elemental &/or demon summoning and lots of chicanery.
                                            Anyway, my point was that elementals in 3e can be far closer to wyld mutants, non-human races or exalts than gods & demons per se and consequently may have as mundane desires or needs, though with an alien/weird spin. They might have a desire for things they see as exotic, pleasant & valuable and cunning mortals (or a self-serving god guiding them) take advantage of this through commerce or the exchange of favors and services.

                                            Oh, just remembered some earth elementals have teeth made of precious stones...

                                            Ok, little but relevant aside. Not all "natural born" elementals have to be part of inhuman races or exotic beasts/monsters. In fact, some elementals could be very much part of human societies, even be quite popular figures among "their" respective people instead.
