            Originally posted by Volivat View Post
            When using solar counterattack and you kill your opponent do they get to roll damage? (Probably been asked before but couldn't find it)
            Each Counter-attack in Exalted is a unique action custom-created by a specific Charm. There is no "general" rule for counter-attacks, each one creates its own rule when you activate it for how it functions. Furthermore, the rules on many Counterattacks are largely nonexistent, so it's basically going to be entirely up to your ST to adjudicate how each counterattack Charm actually works in play.

            What's important to remember about Solar Counter Attack in particular is that you make your attack after the opponent makes their attack and determines whether it hits or fails, but before they actually move on to rolling damage. If this were Second Edition, it would occur somewhere around Step 7 of Combat Resolution.

            If the enemy attacks you and misses and you kill them with your Counterattack, it's no big deal. But if they do hit you with it, but you kill them before they get around to rolling damage, it'll be up to your ST to decide how that works.

            At my table, if someone dies at a given moment in combat, that's it, they're dead, they do not get to pass Go, and they don't get to collect 200 dollars. The only thing they can do is fall over. So if you were to kill the opponent with your Counterattack, they'd be dead, and therefore incapable of moving on to the next step and rolling out their damage for successfully hitting you. Their attack may have hit you, and perhaps they might have cut your clothing or given you a slight flesh wound, but that attack would not have delt any damage because you managed to kill them just before it otherwise would have.

            But again, I have to stress that other STs may choose to handle things differently at their table and may choose to have the deceased opponent carry through with their attack and roll damage posthumously. There is no "official ruling" on this and any ST's decision is entirely legitimate.

                                    Originally posted by nalak42 View Post
                                    What is a fox carriage? I'm assuming it's not actually a carriage for foxes.
                                    They're described in full on page 141 of Bastions of the North:

                                    FOX CARRIAGE
                                    Description: The fox carriages are an echoing side-effect of the gestation of the Fox Cataphract in the belly of the semi-shaped Swar. When they began, this phenomenon created mere phantoms. However, centuries of echoes and the growing strength of the Fox Cataphract have lent a solidity to these creatures that make them more than mere shaping illusions. Now, they belt up and down the mazy streets of Swar, letting out train-whistle shrieks and snorting blue fire like the proto-raksha that they echo. The fox carriages are seen only in the Heights and the Barrios of Swar, as they cannot fit into the Undercity or the Spires.

                                    The fox carriages are vast fox-headed vehicles of blue flame, appearing somewhat as a cross between a steam locomotive and a chariot, and with room for up to 12 on the rear. At first appearance, they are a form of mass transit system for the city of Swar, and they will often appear to act as one, stopping for visitors and whisking them from location to location or driving them about randomly. When Swar is roused against intruders, and the Fox Cataphract is dispatched to confront intruders, the fox carriages will likewise rouse themselves into combat. They are brutal fighters, attacking by crushing the target with their iron wheels, and dispatching animate fireballs from the top of the fume-stack on the front of the carriage.

                                    Shaping Combat: The fox carriages are some of Swar’s weapons in shaping combat. They are a horror. If for some reason the Fox Cataphract is slain, the fox carriages will become unavailable.
                                    There's even a picture of one there. So basically fox carriages are a kind of living mass transportation device. I am somewhat reminded of the Catbus from My Neighbor Torotoro.
