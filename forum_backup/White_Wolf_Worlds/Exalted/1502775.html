    Shards of the Incrana [Alternative Pantheons and Exalts]

    02-11-2023, 06:13 PM
    Ever since Exigents came out and things like the Apocryphals were introduced, I had that idea buzzing in my head of "how would the Exalted Host look like of the Incrana were different". Basically, something between simply switching the ruling Pantheon with another one while keeping more or less the same overall setting to a completely different Creation, where you have numerous "Incrana" with each ruling over their own regional sphere of influence. While there are numerous ways to tackle this concept, the one that I found the most interesting was using deities from real world mythologies, creating something along the lines of Exalted meeting Scion. At any case, I mostly have just thoughts and ideas rumbling with my head, and I assume that if I were to write them down I may get a more cohesive vision of how it may look like- so I'm making this thread! Suggestions and ideas will be welcomed, obviously

    At any case, I'm planning on going through some major pantheons, focusing mostly on major deities, but I may add some other deities in order to fit some Exalt types that show up in canon but do not have a fitting patron among the main ones. And as it is commonly done, I'm going to start with the well known Greco-Roman pantheon as the first in this "project". I am going to use the Greek names when possible simply because it helps to distinguish them from Exalted's core Incrana. Anyway here we go-

    Zeus- the King of the Gods in Roman and Greek religion, this one clearly needs a new Exalt type to fit for its themes of being a Sky Father and god of thunder. That makes me think of a homebrew I once made called the Aerials, which are a chosen of an Incrana of the Sky, even though that being the King of Olympus it is likely that they should be adjusted to a "Solar level". Their Charms are likely to involve weather patterns, thunder and lightning, as well as something related to oath binding due to his roles about punishing wrongdoers and oathbreakers. Currently, The Aerials have two Castes- a Stormy Sky Caste and Clear Sky Caste, with the first being more warlike and the second about the themes of kingship and diplomacy, with currently a lot of the themes of the Aerials being about being more of Kingmakers and less about Kings themselves (which may be needed to be modified when related to Zeus).

    Hera- Hera is... complicated. She has a very clear portfolio, marriage and womanhood, yet that mantle is not very easy to build a whole Host around. She is the Queen of the Gods, yet she is mostly famous for punishing the lovers of Zeus and their children. Perhaps building on Marriage to something a bit... wider? Rituals, oaths, order, civilization? Or going deep, with her Chosen being connected the bonds between people, social agreements and punishment on those who break the social contract? Definetly WIP in here.

    Aphrodite- I think that we currently have a very easy fit for Aphrodite's Chosen- the Hearteaters. Notice, not the Aurorals- I feel that in this pantheon, the Hearteaters are the original form of this Host, especially as they work well within Aphrodite's themes. After all, we are talking about a love goddess we known for being jealous, destructive, obsessive, maddening and beautiful, starting the whole Try War by giving Helena as a prize after being chosen the most beautiful goddess. So... yeah, the Hearteaters feel very fitting for a goddess who is very much obssessed around the concept of love in a very binding manner.

    Apollo- while being far from the only source of inspiration, the Sun in Exalted has his fair share of inspiration from Apollo, who si the god of variety of skills from music to medicine, from archery to prophecy, all while being the god of the sun. As such, the Solars are very easily related to Apollo as a patron, even though one may argue that they need to have a "toned down" power level. I'm fine with them staying the powerful Solars they are, however.

    Ares- that... is a difficult one. Ares is a God of War after all, moving between violent slaughter in his Greek incarnation to a well disciplined war in his Roman form. At any case, war feels so universal that making a Chosen around a concept is hard, as all Exalts have capabilities in battle, being divine soldiers and champions. While the Chosen of Battles have their own niche about Fate, I feel like it doesn't work for Ares. Maybe something about Chosen of Rage and Bloodshed? Exalts with the ability to enter into blood frenzy? That draw power from the fallen? That feels like it could step a bit into the Lunar territory. Perhaps something about weapons, with the Exaltation itself being a weapon and the Chosen are able to produce similar weaponry? Sounds a bit too much like a god of smithing. Anyway, something to think about.

    Artemis- talking about Lunars, Artemis is clearly the best patron for those Exalts. Again, Luna herself draws a lot from her, being a goddess of the moon, the wild, the hunt, and animals, with some shapeshifting shenanigans (even though that is not very unique for her- the Metamorphoses include most main gods, after all). Anyway, that fitting is good enough, and I think we can just move on.

    Athena- well, this one also looks complicated in first glance, simply because Athena is a goddess of many things, from strategy and war to wisdom and crafts, specifically weaving. However, we do have something that falls alongside those lines- the Sidereals. While she is not a goddess of the stars of fate, her focus of weaving does resonate strongly with them aesthetically, and the concept of strategy and planning ahead and wisdom in general makes her, imo, a fitting patron to those Exalts.

    Demeter- as a goddess of earth, fertility and agriculture, I feel a lot like her Chosen would be something like the Strawmaiden Janset. While she is a unique Exigent in the game, if Demeter were to have her own Host it is likely we would have a bunch of Strawmaidens in the setting.

    Hephaestus- just like the Sun draws from Apollo and Luna from Artemis, Hephaestus has a lot of similarity to Autochthon. After all, both are gods of the craft, being crippled blacksmiths who are laughed upon by their siblings yet who use their inventions to take revenge upon them. As such, making him the patron of the Alchemicals would make a lot of sense within this Shard.

    Hermes- the messenger of the gods, god of cunning, thievery, trade, writing, magic and leading the dead to the afterlife, Hermes is many things. However, paths and ways do seem like his main portfolio. He is also a potential patron for the Sidereals, as it is not unknown for Exalts to have multiple patrons, but in that case we would need 5 of those and it could cause me some headache so we may need to think of another option. It is even not that hard to make Hermes his own Chosen- the Chosen of Paths, and the rest os his functions could be easily justified into this protfolio imo.

    Dionysus- as the god of wine and madness, we could easily see a Chosen which probably fits those themes together with some natural imagery about fertility and primal liberation. Taking it a step further, while we don't know much about the "Originals" (as the first drafts of the Apocryphals are being called from time to time), it was spoiled on discord that those who would have became the Dream Souled were originally tied to the strange bacchanalia of Dajaz (now being demon related instead). Now, while they have long diverged from that concept, those Exalts do seem to work with the whole madness and freedom themes that Dionysus has, and with some aesthetical changes I do believe that he would work fine as their patron in the setting.

    Hestia- the goddess of hearth and home, Hestia in her Roman form was very important for ancient Roman religion, being a guardian of sacred places, the Empire in general, and of purity. As such, Hestia have a very easy to grasp function with a unique niche, and it is easy to think of her Chosen as the Hearthwardens, who hold powers which resonate with territory control, fore, protection and probably some powers against Creatures of Darkness. In an interesting manner, some of her aspects feel related to the Solars to a degree, so a better development may be required to distinguish them from each other. She could also be a patron of the Dragon Blooded, however, yet she does lack the elemental themes that feature them.

    Poseidon- Poseidon is... complicated. I mean, it is very, very tempting to correlate him to Neptune, and with that to the Voice in the Trench and the Spoken. It is also very easy to do so, with the Spoken being the Chosen of an underwater civilization. However, this connection is, well, superficial- not only that it was stated again and again that Neptune was not the god of the sea (he simply escaped to there), the Spoken don't even represent Neptune's actual themes, as he made the Spoken long after he degenerated into the Voice. As such, while the Spoken could work aesthetically, I still wonder if they indeed should be his Chosen, or if they should have a different, more appropriate patron, while Zeus's Chosen should focus more about the sea, the depths, storms and calamity in general (adding Earthquakes into the mix). ​

    Hades- the King of the Underworld has a easy choice for an Exalted Host- the Abyssals. I mean, do I actually need to explain that connection? Not to mention that the whole gothic aesthetic really works for him, imo.

    Persephone- the wife of Hades and the goddess which guards the cycles of life and rebirth, moving between the world of the living to the world of the dead, one which was known as "terrifying" yet lovely and beautiful and that has connections to fertility through her mother, I think that if Greek mythology has a patron for the Liminals, it should be this one.

    Gaia- so here is the thing, while the elemental powers are present in Greek philosophy, they lack some direct connection to the gods as far as I'm aware. The closest we have is with Gaia, which represented the World and those within it, and as such connection to the Dragon Blooded as it is in the canon setting. Still, I am not sure about the themes themselves, especially as even in Exalted, Gaia and her Children do not cover the same concepts, which is why the Dragon Blooded are the Chosen of the Dragons, and not of the primordial. I do not have a better fit, however (except of Hestia potentially), so.. this is what we got?

    Tartarus- a personification of the hellish realm where the titans are held captives, I do think that Tartarus is the most appropriate patron for the Infernals, representing rage and multination. They could also have Cronus, yet I feel like Tartarus fits by being much more alien compared to the fallen King of the Titans, but it is still an option.

    Hecate- the goddess of crossroads and witchcraft, Hecate makes a lot of sense as a liminal goddess who stands between what is and what isn't. As such, if the Getimians have a place in this pantheon, then the ancient titaness (who is was considered among the 12 Gods in some rare instances) is the most likely, imo, to be the one which brings to life dead fates and allows them to cross the impossible boundaries.

    Mnemosyne- talking about titans, Mnemosyne is interesting because that, as the mother of the muses, she may be a replacement patron for the Spoken in case that Poseidon is deemed unfitting. Even if that is not the case, she could easily serve as the patron of the Aurorals, as they themselves serve as muses which inspire great heroes. In any case, it seems that regardless of her function, she could have a place within the setting.

    Janus- a relatively important god of Roman religion, Janus being two faced and ruling over boundaries and gates makes him feel like a very natural choice as a Umbral patron. Shadows are liminal entities, and again it is just hard to ignore the dual personality thing which could be expressed through Janus's themes and aesthetics.

    Plutus- a not well known god in Greek religion which is considered to be related to Pluto and eventually absorbed into Hades (if not always being related to him to a degree), Plutus being a small god could work for him being the patron of the Sovereigns, due to his connection to the treasures of the Earth such as gemstones. However, they themselves may fit better as the Chosen of, potentially, Hestia or even Hermes. Just something to think about if we want to have those Exalts in the setting prominently.

    Tyche- while being the goddess of Fate, Tyche (being a popular goddess) was also correlated to the goddess of community, civilization and, more importantly- cities. Many cities had their own "Tyche", representing the city's providence and fate. That correlation between Tyche and the Tychai works well to represent the Architects in the setting, which emphasis many small, city gods united under one single, powerful entity which allows them to share the burden if Exaltation.

    Prometheus- the fire thief and protector of humanity, Prometheus feels like a good candidate for either the guardian or the vessel of Exigence. While Zeus is the King of Heavens, and as such should be the vessel for the fire, the whole thing about Prometheus stealing the fire from Heaven and sharing it with humanity would make a great setting for how to introduce Exigence into the setting, with Zeus holding it originally and Prometheus deciding to share it, and with it the powers of Exaltation with both humanity and its small gods, something for which he was punished for all eternity. So yeah, I think that this role would make him a very good "patron" of Exigence, if not its embodiment.

    So- this is what I have for the first pantheon! Let me know what do you think and if you have suggestions of your own, and let me know if I missed something :P

      So some follow up thoughts about the Greco-Roman Pantheon-

      The more I think about it, the more I lean towards Hestia being the patron of the Dragon Blooded of her region (if it would end up as "regional Exaltation" setting, still thinking about it). It just fits a lot of her themes of community, the Hearth, fire, empire and so forth. Without any clear alternative, Poseidon will be the patron of the Spoken, leaving Mnemosyne the patron of the Aurorals. Instead of Tartarus being the patron of the Infernals, it is probably better to just make it into the trapped Titans themselves. Still not sure if the Sovereigns should be their own Exalts created by Plutus who decayed under the toil of Exaltation, leaving Hades to absorb his preview, or if Plutus was a minor god who used Exigence to produce them. Still trying to think about clear concept for Ares, Hera and Hermes in terms of which Exalts they were to produce.

      With that in mind, let's give some thought about the potential Exalts of the Egyptian Pantheon-

      Ra- a very easy pick, thankfully. Ra is, again, not merely a Sun God, but also a god with themes of kingship who was dethroned and replaced, with many other gods being merged with him through the ages, include creation gods. Aten, the sun disk, was also considered among Ra's aspects before the whole Akhenaten thing. At any case, the whole thing fits right into the Solars alley, with perhaps a few modifications. Even access to necromancy can be easily justified with Ra's travel through the Underworld.

      Isis- and this is where things start to become complicated. Isis is the goddess of sorcery, healing, motherhood and kingship by being the "mother of the Pharaoh", as well as absorbing many other goddesses and developing a whole mystery cult around her. To a degree, she is a universal goddess, and does share some aspects with Hera for being the Queen of Heavens and centered around motherhood- but the strong focus around magic, even if healing magic, as well as the use of poisons and true names and such... it would require some thought about whether her Chosen and Hera's are the same, or if hers work better as the Chosen of Sorcery to a degree.

      Set- a of chaos who plays a large antagonistic role in mythology, Set is the god of the desert and storms. That territory is very much undefined by Exalted- you could argue somewhat for Infernals, perhaps, which also have a desert themes with Cecelyne and the entrance for Malfeas and the whole chaos show they have... which honestly does work, but it feels like it is not a perfect fit, and that the Chosen of the Desert may be their own thing. We could always revert to Infernals, sure, but I think this demands some more thought.

      Nephthys- Nephthys is... confusing, honestly. People know she was important, but people are not sure what exactly was her role- like her sister, she seems to have been pretty universal, yet overshadowed by Isis. She has funeral aspects and relation to darkness and the night, which makes me wonder if she could serve as the "necromancy" for Isis's "sorcery. In that case, it may be the best if the two goddesses actually share a Host, with one Caste relating to Sorcery and the other to Necromancy. That could also be an interesting way to bring Nocturnals back to the setting (homebrew setting, at least), as their reality wrapping abilities do lean a lot towards Sorcerous workings and the like.

      Osiris- Osiris is very obviously the Liminal patron of that pantheon. Being the king of the dead, torn apart, put back together, and strong connections to fertility makes him a goods candidate for the "Dark Father" which seeds the black earth (which also relates to Egypt's original name, Kemet). At any case, Liminals are undoubtly the easy pick here.

      Horus- a god of sky and kingship, Horus works well under similar themes to those of Zeus in terms of the potential Aerial Exalts. While lacking lighting and thunder and being more focused around the luminaries, overall it seems like a consistent theme which, well, works, and supports a Chosen of the Skies like Exalts.

      Anubis- ahm, Anubis is... complicated. Being the guide of the dead and psychopomp, the instinct is connecting him to one of the already established Underworld Exalts- but Liminals are very Osiris and the Abyssals don't really... fit. We can think about his connection to transitions to Hermes and find common Chosen for them, but Hermes is already well tied with other Egyptian god making it problematic. Perhaps we could use the Dual Monarchy and concepts for their homebrew Exalts as potential fit for Anubis, or eventually revert to Abyssals if not better patron could be found.

      Ptah- god of craftsmen and architects, Ptah have two main options for a Host- either Alchemicals or, potentially, Architects. I personally tend strongly towards the first option, especially as he is considered a Creation God and which relates to Autochthon being a primordial, and it would be easy to justify Architects as simply Exigents and Alchemicals would be more complicated, but it is still an option.

      Sobek- the god of the Nile and the dangers lurking within, Sobek is a wild and fierce deity, one which is so scary that it keeps evil at bay. Lunars could be optional choice as the Host of this god, which means leaving their Lunar themes behind and becoming the Chosen of the River. The Spoken, however, could be another option, with their aquatic themes, even though Sobek does lack any connection to Voices, I think. Something to ponder about.

      Thoth- while Thoth is the god of the Moon, he is first and foremost the god of knowledge, science and art, which is why he was connected to Hermes. As such, he lacks the wild nature which is essential to the Lunars, but may serve as a way to develop the Chosen of Hermes by offering a wider view.

      Bast- goddess of protection, war and, well, cats, Bast does provide a well defined portfolio to work with, but trying to develop the Chosen of Cats in a manner which won't feel too niche or tongue in the cheek would not be a easy task. Some thought about the subject will be required. You could, potentially, stray from the Cat themes and move to the war and protection themes, trying to connect her to the same Chosen as Ares's (whatever they may be), but I feel it loses a lot from her identity... even though it may not be too weird, if we consider Infernals as Chosen of the Desert and moving Lunars from the Moon to the River. Oh, and obviously, she too could also be a Lunars patron- perhaps that would be the best choice, leaving the Spoken for Sobek, potentially.

      Hathor- a goddess of love and healing, she shares many aspects with Isis (which is why Isis eventually took some of her aspects), while her other self, Sekhmet, was subsumed to a degree by Bast. That, however, does give us a very interesting thing to consider- as a goddess with "split personality" serves as great patron for the Umbrals. Both aspects don't really play into the whole angst, however- but Sekhmet is blind rage incarnated, which does work well for the Shadow, making it an interesting choice.

      Neith- being a cosmic goddess of war, hunt, wisdom, fate and weaving which was connected to Athena, making Neith into the patron of the Sidereals would make a lot of sense in this setting, imo, making her into a easy pick as their patron. The only other god which may be argued as a better fit if Thoth, but we are still working comparatively in this project so I do think that she works better in that regard- and she is stated to weave the world on a divine loom so.. yeah, Sidereals it is.

      Khonsu- if neither Sobek or Bast become the patrons of the Lunars, Khonsu is the most likely candidate. Not only he is a lunar deity, he was depicted rather violently as the god invoked to slay other gods and allow the Pharaoh to devour them, which sounds kinda Lunar? Also, his Marvel and Kane Chronicles depictions (which are obviously not "canon" but still) are rather dark, and he is related to be traveler, nomad and defender of paths. Not sure if he even is significant enough to have his own Exalts in the setting, but if Plutus is being considered he too should be brought up.

      Nefertem- while not as significant as other gods, from what I can see he was a part of a triad together with Ptah in Memphis, so he was important at least locally. Him being the "beautiful one" and god of the lotus flowers and their scent, I get some strong vibes of him being a potential Hearteater or Auroral patron. We don't have much one him, Aurorals could work if we go on the inspiring route, while Hearteaters may offer a more... dark twist to his nature. Not sure it fits, however.

      Apep- if Set is not the patron of the Infernals in the setting, Apep, the great of demons who dares to devour the Sun, is very likely to be their Patron. The adversary against the Sun and the legions of demons released in his quest are all fitting for the Infernals, especially with all of that rage and hate which just spawn from him. If Set id decided to be a better patron than Apep, than I assume that Apep could end up as the patron of the Hearteaters, but it should require some work to justify it (Apep is not very charismatic, after all, being a world destroying serpent- but maybe something about his poison? Needs to think about it)

      Ammit- the dread beast which devours the hearts of the unworthy, it is less Ammit herself and more of the Judges of Duat which advise Osiris in the decree, even though we can think about it as a collective process. After all, Ammit is pretty much the Oblivion of her mythology, which puts the Judges as the Deathlords- and all of that leans towards Ammit being the patron of the Abyssals. I personally think it works quite well, but let me know what do you think.

      While that covers the more prominent gods and easy choices, we are still left with a number of Exalts who lack patrons. Specifically, the Dragon Blooded, Getimians, Dream Souled, Exigents in general, maybe Spoken and Aurorals/Hearteaters and maybe some of the example Exigents (Architects, Sovereigns, etc). That give us some things to ponder about, while we also need to consider the next pantheon we should explore in this project.. so much to do, so little time :P
