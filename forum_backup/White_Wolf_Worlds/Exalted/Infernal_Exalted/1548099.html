    Concept for alternative Infernal origins

    01-31-2025, 06:51 PM
    Basically what would Infernals be if not corrupted Solars what would their origin be? So this is an early idea I have for a potential alternative origin and how they work:

    The Yozi's longed for Exalted Servants after the Primordial War but the idea of any major sacrifice to create Exalts digusted them. They were diminished enough and any further sacrifice was not just heresy but insanity. But one day the concept of Exigence was discussed and the Ebon Dragon had an idea, an evil one; get humanity to pay the price. To start it, they would need a small price paid, but it would be the spark of the fire that burned Creation. After wrangling all of Hell, each 3rd and 2nd demon gave a sliver of a sliver of a sliver of power and it was all mixed together to created a circle of Exaltations to start a cycle of damnation.

    The scheme was simple; each Infernal could only be exalted by willing accepting the power as part of a bargain - they would gain an Exaltation to get what they wanted, and when the Yozis deemed the time was right, they would as a favour. And the benefits of this exaltation were beyond power but the favours of demons and special rights in the city of Malfeas; living as princesses at the expense of those pre-human     horrors. But when the time came, the Yozis demanded their wicked price; everything. All relationships and deeds mystical consumed, every mote of power taken and every memory drained until that great Infernal was a hollowed husk restarting their life under new demonic teaching. Some this moment came as they died, being spared death but becoming a radically different person; others as they tried to rebel when they discovered this foul fate; and others when they were drawing too much scuteny from the Heavens or the Solar Deliberative or the Silver Pact or the Realm. But in all cases it was the same; the liquidation of excess power to create new Exaltations and the Exalt become a meek shadow of their former self to restart the cycle.

    Slowly these minions grew in number, originally only a handful they now are 50 or so in number and with the Time of Tumult, the Yozis hope to speed the life cycle of these damned fools. The long term plan is simple; reach a critical point with Infernals and then overwhelm Creation's champions in one war to break old surrend oaths and reclaim the world.

    Tl;dr: Yozis created exalted that gain power for themselves before it is harvested to created more Infernal Exalted.

    While I've only started with this idea but the idea for castes are based on what they originally bargained for and thet would be attribute based not ability based. Probably 6 castes (as the Yozis deliberately break from the 5 numerology found in Creation to re-assert their will), with each caste having 2 Abilities from set and 1 Ability from another (For Example, 2 Social and 1 Physical, etc). Charms are built into trees mimicing specific Yozis that align to that Attribute (like Malfeas is a Charisma tree, Ebon Dragon Manipulation, etc). Rough ideas for some of the castes:
    • Chosen of Health - Wanted better health or some physical improvent, not just to themselves but to others. Tend to become survivors and medics of the Infernals (Int, Str and Sta).
    • Chosen of Greatness - Wanted glory and victory. Warriors, priests, entertainers and showboaters (Cha, App, Str)
    • Chosen of Wealth - wanted material possession or power. Sorcerers, politicians and social climbers. (Int, Cha, Wit).
    • Chosen of Suffering - wanted someone else to suffer; assassins, sabotuers and jealous bitchs (Per, Man, App).
    • Chosen of Life - wanted to live or be free (ironic really). Messengers, artists and labourers (Wit, Per, Dex)
    • Chosen of Loyatly - wanted to serve the Yozis of their own accord - fantatics and crusaders (Dex, Sta, Man).

    Note they can escape the service of the Yozis if they try and ensure they won't be harvested, but it takes a conscious effort to cut free of all Yozi support and puts them at quite a risk (with Hell trying to reclaim their exaltation and heaven trying to kill these Chosen of the Yozi).

    Core themes to their powers would include identity, power, growth, slavery, irony and deceptions.

    When an Infernal starts a new cycle, they lose most of their memories but keep a general outline of their past personality, although occasionally familiaties and memories resurface as they encounter things from previous cycles (like lost loves, places they important to them and situations similar to past events)

    Additionally they can be killed even when in Yozi service, it actually takes a specific costly ritual (costing roughly 50 xp) to bind their heart to Malfeas to get on escape from death; which is only good for one free escape from death (as their body painfully reforms over months around the heart - but the ritual can be repeated) and doesn't work in all occasions (such as when being killed in the presence of a Celestial Exalted at full Anima or while in a spirit's sanctuary).

    In character Creation the PC is given the choice of making an Infernal in their first cycle or one in a latter one; with different options for character generation (more merit dots (with permission for merits pertaining to their human life) vs. more charms).

    Finally their castes roles tend to reflect what the Yozis need and want of them, so a warrior could exalted a Chosen of Wealth but pushed into the role of a conquerer or sorcerer knight by the demonic tutors, espically in later cycles.

    What do people think of this idea?

      I think even with a premise of being able to potentially escape their bargain, the idea of being given power in exchange for a potential to eventually arbitrarily have all of their agency removed from them at best is a bit of a hard sell for making playable.

      As a way to write this as a setting element, I would also see it as having the same problem as the akuma of prior Editions did, than in making these characters fully suborned like that you end up with a lot of figures ostensibly doing important things but with no compelling personality or motivation behind it.

      And I think it's a bit odd to predicate a form of Infernal Exalted on such a hostile relationship with Hell when part of how Exalted distinguishes itself is in the ways it adds nuance to that kind of thing verging on outright subverting it. Like, it's a foundational premise in the game that you can develop the power to summon demons and bind them to tasks, and they will carry those tasks out sincerely and enthusiastically (with many a demon presented in terms of how they will proactively seek to grant certain kinds of service, if on their own terms). The peril of demons isn't that they'll screw you over, it's that how they carry out the task you give them may be disturbing or maybe not quite what you wanted because you didn't understand their mindset.

      The idea of making a demonic bargain that is really just about getting you to sign away everything and be reduced to servitude seems so conventional in comparison.

      (This is something that I think really illustrates why it was not a good idea to first write the Ebon Dragon as a character who will always screw people over in exchanges even when it's counterproductive and then make him basically the most prominent Yozi)

      I have approximate knowledge of many things.

          Well, must admit i have doubts and the more i read, more the doubts seem to strike.

          Ok, those Infernals are not corrupted Solars, but what exactly does that mean in the end?

          Who would their spiritual patron(s) be? What sort of qualities do these exaltations seek in their agents/champions? Where does their power level - as in essence pool, skills in sorcery or necromancy, martial mastery or capacity to resonate with magical materials and develop evocations - stand in relation to other exalted types?

          All these questions and more crossed my mind while reading and answers seem murky to non-existent to say the least.

          Also, pardon me but some of the core themes given also seem kind of bland and lacking - identity, power, growth? Honestly that applies to pretty much every celestial exalt, for whom getting occasional glimpses of memories from previous holders of the power is a base assumption. And even some exalts who are in no way celestial or work in terms of inherited power (Liminals, coff, coff, plus Dragon-Blooded playing with Dynasty-keyword charms, if memory tricks me not) will sometimes get into that bandwagon.

          And slavery, irony & deception seem to be more about "the deal is always bad, you're more boned than a Deathknight under Oblivion if you don't go rogue" than anything closely similar to "what tales shall they tell of their deeds".

          It could be fun to see where this goes, but so far everything feels too raw, kind of formless, only clear image i get is "you made a deal with devils and now...."

          The thing about Infernals growing in number with time, that could be very interesting, if one takes the time to stop, think and maybe consider all kinds of different ways to riff from the many exalt types for different ways to tackle this matter. Just my rambling thoughts so far, hopefully not to salty or antagonistic.

              Okay, firstly sorry for the delay in replying - I was busy with some other things and this fell by the wayside; so I will try to answer your comments and points before commenting on my thoughts about this since then.

              But first, thank you Isator Levi and Baaldam for your input.

              For Isator Levi's comments, you're right, this is all a little too domination and slavery; I should make it more transactional than an abusive deal.
              The idea was the Yozis would be looking for minions not just champions to embody their ideals like the the Incarnae - they need something to control their Exalted so to get their will done, but thinking about it they already have their demons so that's redundant was well.
              Really I need to find some way to make the Infernal Exalted to work with the Yozis willingly without actually forcing them.
              Two posibilities I see are a 'job system', where the Infernal can access a mental job's board where the Yozis and 3rd circles can place missions that will be rewarded to which ever Infernal completes them or special charms that replicate the demon summoning spells but with the cavitate that instead of forcing your will on the demon, you have make a bargain with them and give or do something in exchange.

              For Baaldam's comments, the general power level was Celestial Exalted (so 2nd level sorcery, but with some way to summon 3rd Circle demons) and similar pool of essence.
              For magical material affinity I don't know, but possibly artifacts infused with demonic essence or partially made with a demon instead of a specific magical material.
              As for spiritual patron - technically all of the Yozis, who either split the shards up amongst themselves or have a specific agent (likely a 3rd circle demon) to make the decision.
              As for desired qualities - the only thought I had was whether a person of skill and potential value was open to a making a deal for power in a moment of desperation.
              When I said identity I was more meaning their charms play with identity in various ways; such an apperance charm that lets you swap identities with someone to hide or an strength charm that brands your target with an initimacy towards yourself as their rightful king.
              But you are right that the themes are a bit of a mess with too much focus on the Yozis backstabbing their chosen rather than making interesting character choices.
              Finally I hadn't really thought of how other Exalted types would be affected; I only included it with this version of Infernals as an explaination of why they were so low profile in history (aka not threatening lore), to work around the Law of Diminishment by the Yozis paying as little as possible and also give them a unique flair.
              The only other way I've thought about it is having a choosen of 'plague' for lack of a better word who are able to Exalt mortals by infected them with their exaltation - the survivors exalt and the others just die (except for a few people who are immune or if the plague is too weak).

              As for my ideas since then, I'll divide them into several sections:

              General ideas for Infernal Exalted:
              • Infernals are chosen by the Yozis or their servants to fulfil their will in Creation but are not necessarily bound to eternal service and still have free will to determine their lives.
              • All Infernals should have the ability to open a portal to and from Malfeas (or more specifically the Silver Desert), but without upgrades it can only take themselves
              • The idea of Unwoven Coadjutors returns as demons bound to the Exalt as part of the Exaltation - being the one to offer the Exaltation or find a worthy candidate and serve as a guide to the new Infernal; additionally the Infernal gets some bonuses/unique charms based on the type of demon used.
              • Devil Body Incarnation is a part of their power sets.

              Thoughs on my current idea:
              • Keep the core concept of a few exaltations that are growing in number as they extract energy to be used to create new Exaltations.
              • Celestial level Exalted (roughly Lunar in power level)
              • Infernals willing accept their Exaltation as part of a bargain with the Yozis; currently they are using this fuel the creation of more Exaltations - caste is based on why you accepted an Exaltation (to be stronger, to be free, etc) with castes capable of changing between different incarnations.
              • Infernals are chosen by the Yozis or the proxies based on a combination of their potential skills, alignment to the current stated goals of the Yozis and the potential Exalt's willingness to bargain; a first circle demon is sent to and approaches the potential Exalt and offers them the Exaltation with a description of the terms and conditions; if accepted the demon is absorbed into the new Exalt and becomes a Unwoven Coadjutor.
              • Charm themes based on different Yozis, and Attribute based.
              • Less antagonist relationship over all - Yozis try to tempt and bargain with their Infernal rather than straight subjugation (although strategies vary from Yozi to Yozi, like Malfeas relies on force of personal and social pressure while the Ebon Dragon manipulates and tempts, etc). The most backstabbing thing I might include is Infernals having a shorter life span (~120 to 150 years) to increase the rate of harvesting them for power; in this situation the Yozis can grant years to the Infernals life span in exchange for services rendered as a way to influence their exalts.
              • Not sure for Magical Material, but Soulsteel and/or Moon Silver stick in my mind for some reason.
              • Not sure how the cost to create new exaltations is collected but it is and I'm currently thinking one of the following ways:
              a) A personal sacrifice made at exaltation (like the memories of a one true love, the legal birth right to the throne or ancestral heirloom; something that would justify a major intimacy at least). The Yozis try to control their Exalts by offering alternatives (Here's your new demon waifu, a kingdom in hell or an artefact Daiklave

              b) All social connections, memories, history, etc you have at the point of Exaltation are absorbed and the Infernal is whisked back to the Silver Desert, waking up as a blank slate with only the skills and attributes that marked the Exalt out allowing them to be reshaped as superior servants; in Creation, all evidence of the existence is gone except for the memories of powerful Exalts and spirits and their social net sort of either crumbles or reforms around the hole of the now missing Exalted.

              c) Same as option B, but happens when the Exalt dies, leaving no evidence of their existence and even their followers/servants/vassals having the life drained out of them when they die; this would also give the Infernals an edge as baring the Celestial Bureaucracy and the records of Malfeas, they are complete unknowns to their enemies; but at the cost of being a tragedy that no one remembers them and their legacy is mostly lost.

              d) Infernals are given missions that allow them to claim power or other resources in exchange of gathering energy to help create more Infernal shards.

              e) Infernal Exaltations are offered freely and losely; however the Exaltation process itself is an ordeal and many die without Exalting; their dissolved souls, life essence and even the essence of their flesh is harvest and returns with the Exaltation to the place in Malfeas where it is kep and new Exaltations are made. In this version the castes are based on how the exalt survives the magical torture rather than why they accepted (concepts include sheer willpower, being through worse, distracting themselves, etc)




              Options on the origins of the Infernal Exaltations:
              • As above, the Yozis extracted a sliver from each Third and Second Circle Demon to create a small set of Exaltations.
              • The Yozi forced one of their number (or maybe one volunteered for some reason) was forced to make the sacrifice for the Exaltations but I don't see the Yozis doing that.
              • Corrupted shards of another Exalted type, but I don't like that either as that's basically why I don't like the current Infernals (don't ask me why that it is, it's just a trope I don't like in this situation for some reason; feels a little like it doesn't belong to the Yozis as thus has less weight/realness or something - I don't know.).
              • Yozis stole some of the Fire of Exigence and did some weird experiments on it to get the new Infernal Exaltations.
              • Yozis had an exiled Incarnae or proto-incarnae they recycled for Exaltations. Similar problems with the third option in my opinion, it feels less like the chosen of the Yozis and just their own corrupted copy of someone else's work.
              • Some pre-human race was dying out and sacrificed themselves to create Exaltations to carry on their legacy.
              • The Yozis used some sealed away abandoned concepts for Creation and liquidated them to create the Infernals.
              • Taking an idea from 2nd Edition and have Primordials defined by their charms; The Yozis can grant one charm to a mortal like an Exaltation; while not enslaving the new exalt (pseudo-exalt?) it does cause mental effects similar to the great curse. Would have a limited but incredibly powerful power set with several personal charms growing off the one granted by the Yozis; very thematic or the idea of a demonic bargain for power but might not be suitable for an Exalt origin as it's a little too much like the slave relationship of 2nd edition Akuma, but might also be the origin of a non-Exalted Yozi minion type.

              And finally other vague ideas for what Infernals could be:
              • A three tiered structure of 1st, 2nd and 3rd Circle Infernals of different power levels that form a hierarchical structure with 1st Circles being roughly on par with Dragon Blooded, 2nd Circles with Celestial and 3rd Circle with Solars. Each 3rd Circle has several 2nd Circles bound to them (Positive Defining Intimacy) and 2nd Circles have 1st Circles who are similarly bound - 3rd and 2nd Circles pick mortals to Exalt into 1st Circles and progress is possible through the Circles when ever an opening is made (although candidates need to get a demon of the same rank to sponsor them before the final decision is made).
              • Basical Infernal Exigents - different demons make a sacrifice to either a copy of and stolen part of the Fire of Exigence.
              • Chosen of fallen Incarnae/proto-Incarnae that the Yozis use as their agents but obscuating their infernal allegance.
              • Reflections of the pre-existing Exalted types that are created through something like the mirror fetch soul of Szoreny; each caste would oppose a different type of Exalt in some way an be a messed parody of them.
              • A select collection of these suggestions that the Yozis pit against each other ineffectually to see who will come out on top in any situation using the competetion to keep each group of Exalted in line.
              edit: two ideas for possible origins section/vague ideas section:

              1) Infernals are chosen in batches of candidates who they drag into a mental arena to battle to death in a conflict of wills and cunning - the survivor is exaltation is funded by the demonic dissolution of the losers’ souls and bodies.

              2) Infernals are taken to Malfeas when they make they are offered the Exaltation and modified in horrorific surgeries; if Alchemicals are choice souls remade in bodies of metal and Liminals the resurrected corpse, these are humans remade with ideas the Yozis have conjured up since their imprisonment; a sort of trans-human horror that must retain some humanity to stay an actual Exalt and bot just some grotesque monster. Would likely have ‘Evolutions’, which are the body horror upgrades they have, which only reveal themselves when the Infernal uses charms related to the Evolutions they possess.
