                            One thing that caught my eye was Vance's description of the origin of Abyssal Exaltations:

                            When an Abyssal is Exalted, she inherits the power and legacy of a shining sun-king, but one that has been stolen by thirteen vengeful ghosts of the Usurpation and corrupted by the power of the Neverborn.

                            I consider the above statement to be essentially equivalent to "Abyssal Exaltations are corrupted Solar Exaltations," which they are, but far more useful for describing what the deathknights actually are.

                            Jumping off that, a GSP inherits the power and legacy of a shining sun-king, but one that has been corrupted by the power of the Yozi.

                            They are Lawgivers, but their law is not that of the Sun, or that of death. Their law is that of the creators of the world, those who first shaped things into being. "Hell's Lawgivers?" Perhaps so they may be called, but they know the truth of whence their law comes.

                            And let us be honest, there is something in the Solar legacy that resonates with the Yozi cause, the king betrayed, dethroned, cast down and imprisoned. The king who lost what once was theirs, now returned to make answer for that betrayal, given the chance to make things right as they see fit.

                            (And oh, the Lunar dynamics have the potential to be fun here...)

                            And so, and so. Like their deathknight cousins, the resources and power of Malfeas are at the GSPs' command, should they desire it, should they seek it out. Hell's rock stars, given everything that it is in the Yozi's power to give. And if the Deathlords are patient on those who turn away from them, why should the Yozi be any less such, having waited millennia longer? For all that the Yozi are spiteful and bitter, they are patient, too; they have had no choice.

                                Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                ​I try to find basic, rudimentary ideas about the different takes on the Solar Exalted by looking at them faced with the end of the world (or the onset of chaos, if those are different things); as a Solar, your actions are in service to stopping that (regardless of morality); as Abyssal, you find beauty in it and peace in its apparent inevitability; as Infernal, you revel in it and in the face of it, turn your apparent helplessness into a source of strength, perhaps seek refuge in it beyond the realms of humanity.

                                                    From Infernals in 2nd Edition, I always liked the sense of progression they had. How it felt one power flowed from the other and made you feel each cascade was all one power growing inside the character.

                                                    This is something I think has been sort of "assimilated" by Evocations but that the previous devs tentatively tried to preserve for Infernals by giving them special Mutations and having classic Charms like Mind Hand Manipulation as "Evocations" for them. But I think they ditched that design even before the new devs took over.

                                                    I also enjoyed the sense of being able to learn the powers that made the world. Which is something I hope can be salvaged by having the Infernals learning their Charms as the secrets of the Yozi and their mechanics being simply how they manifest it or something.

                                                    I also, horror or horrors, loved the Reclamation.

                                                    I really liked the idea of having this big, titanic goal that seems unreachable and having to actually build the networks and infrastructure that will begin the process to attempt it. To basically having to build the All-Thing Infernal's power base in Creation.

                                                    Granted, after we got such a horrible "preview" of what the previous devs would give us as "the Reclamation" I've abandoned any hope of having any official support for it.

                                                    But I would really love if 3E Infernals had an endgame that looked a lot like Exalted Modern's setup. To try and turn the God's Chosen against them, cast down the Incarnae and rule Heaven and the Celestial Bureaucracy.

                                                    Something like that would allow for Infernals to integrate in the setting as making an alliance with them would be siding with the Infernals and not the Yozi, which would allow many players and GMs to be able to integrate them without having to deal with all the baggage they bring to the table.

                                                    As for mechanics, I hope the Green Sun Princes are the Sorcerous Exalted.

                                                    To me sorcerous transformations like Incomparable Body Arsenal sound a lot like Shintais and the way Sorcerous Workings already allow for self transformation seem to already step on the toes of what used to be GPS design space. So, it stands to reason that's the direction they are going with it.

                                                    At the very least GPS should be capable of doing awesome things with Evocations from Spells.

                                                    EDIT: oh, and the soul hierarchy charms were awesome too. And I really hope we keep the ability to make Shintais. Those two things were like 90% of the appeal of devil tigers for me.
