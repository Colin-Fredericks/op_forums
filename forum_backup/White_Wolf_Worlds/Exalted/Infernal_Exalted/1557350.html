                                          Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
                                          Your prospective cultists have a lot less motivation to stay in your Holy Land Infliction if there's no water-equivalent available there.
                                          While I think there's a degree to which worshippers might be put in the position of remaining for higher priorities than concern over deprivation, if not where struggling within it for the sake of their devotion is part of the point, I can see the potential synergy between the Infernal being able to desecrate a land and then bestowing sustenance on a select few.

                                          Likewise, in comparing the writing of the Second Edition Charm to the Third and how they represent writing styles across Editions, I must recognise how the latter might have gone for something to get the point of providing general sustenance across without feeling the need for exactly precise language.

                                          In the end, I guess it comes down to how somebody wants to play it, and that it's not ruinous if the locusts suffice to provide for somebody's basic needs. I would still be inclined to an interpretation of it being a food source from which you won't suffer dehydration, but likewise won't feel satisfyingly parched by.
