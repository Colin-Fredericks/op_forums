    11-18-2025, 03:30 PM

      I'm very happy to say it's satisfied many of my doubts-if not all, then more than enough to await the rest of the content with hope rather than dread. I particularly enjoy the Carceral War and it's consequences for demonic society, the elaboration on what warlocks (even non-Infernal ones) actually are, the attempt to distinguish Hell as a freer and more unstable place than Yu-Shan and the defined but distant guidelines for Yozi interaction. I am greatly relieved the entire revolutionary/righteous indignation angle has been utterly de-emphasised in favour of revenge for it's own sake.

      This comes with the caveat that it has satisfied my doubts by being functionally unrecognisable to almost ANY information I could find about Infernals development in the years leading up to this; ironically it most reminds me of the initial preview for Infernals (the one with Basphomy in Verdigris) but less railroad-y (this is a good thing).

      It also comes with the caveat that while I am ALL FOR the very decisive pitch that Infernals are not, in fact, just Green Solars and nothing else. The reveal of what they actually are is such a lawyer-like way of saying "Yes they are the Chosen of the Yozis but not of any SINGLE Yozi" that I wish it had been advertised more strongly, and that in general the slant of Infernal "humanism" wasn't emphasised so much. A lot of my complaints would have been happily discarded if I knew THIS was what they were going for. Above all else I very much appreciate the hints there is more to embodying Hell than simply Charms as RAW. As I said in the other thread, I am more than happy to accept some other kind of inhuman transcendence even if it's not strictly Devil-Tigerhood as long as there IS one. And there seems to be one here.

      Even if nothing is set in stone, from the first draft alone I am much, much, MUCH more willing to give 3e Infernals the benefit of the doubt than I was based on any and all promotional information between this and the very first preview.

                                                  I've only read through Awareness thus far, but I find it very compelling to answer my longstanding question of how complicated the variety of influences should be as "yes, fuck you", and that the cohesive through line of Abilities seems to be "pile in as much weird shit there as even vaguely attaches to the core capability".

                                                  Like, it's a lot, but it works, because it's neat to have such variety of weird manifestations and applications while still feeling grounded in the human actions (and persona for stuff running on Intimacies) at the core of it. You get pinnacles that feel like they express awe-inspiring might or world-building authority, at the same time as there's apparent trade off for being extremely good in a direct way that the Solars have.

                                                  I'm personally not really here for transhumanism as a framing, but I do like that there's transformative stuff like becoming weirdly big or having a freaky appetite.

                                                  And I do think it has threaded the needle of making the Yozis (and other forces) apparent while the dispersal makes it feel less like direct expression of them.

                                                  I also like how the Universal Charms feel like useful and consequential refinement of the core Infernal being while not stepping on the Abilities or feeling strictly necessary. Like, they're useful but not so useful that they demand investment. Devil-Body enhancement stands out especially there; it's nice to have options to increase the powers, add alternate forms for it, and increase the pace of access, but it never fully departs from the point that the transformation is something that happens under duress and doesn't quite sit comfortably on the Exalt. Hence, that limited access makes it feel completely suitable to not really invest in it compared to more direct stuff.

                                                  I actually have this character concept of an Infernal who not only neglects enhancing their Devil-Body, but dislikes it and actively tries to avoid using it. Not because of anything to do with departure from humanity, but regarding it as something undignified and humiliating for the transformation conditions. A manifestation of desperation that withdraws from the self-realisation of the Exalt for a shell of crude Hellish power, far less impressive than their deliberately cultivated and exerted capabilities.

                                                      The main question in my mind is whether any Yozi's themes are at least theoretically valid design space for any Ability. I mean, you wouldn't want to just copy Hegra-themed Archery Charms into Melee or whatever, but plausibly you could have colorful emotion-related sword techniques of some kind?

                                                      Of course, Infernal Charms need to generally have that Hellish twist -- otherwise they'd just be green Solars and we wouldn't need a new book -- but Hell can twist in a lot of different directions.

                                                      There does seem to be a lot of variation into how much different Charms require the Infernal to play into their themes -- some are pretty solid even if you aren't doing anything special, like Hellbound Mariner Saga (which has a key that makes it work to full effect all the time), and some are greatly nerfed if you aren't doing the Hell thing, like Foremost Vengeance Stratagem (whose keys all rely on fulfilling its conditions).

                                                      TBH, it seems like not only the Yozis themselves but also demons can be evoked in some cases -- Sable Revelry Summons is clearly based on Erembour more than the Ebon Dragon himself, and Demon-Legion Emulation evokes various First Circle demons. The possibilities seem nearly limitless.

                                                      There have been a few Charms that don't seem very impressive for Solar-tier Exalted -- Sermon of the Yozis seems painfully overspecialized -- but overall I've really been enjoying this stuff. If only there were a few more Charms for doing sorcerous workings more effectively...
