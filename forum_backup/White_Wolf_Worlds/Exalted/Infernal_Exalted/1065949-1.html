            Small update, I've finally figured out a way of doing feat of strength charms that I liked. Here they are.

            False Idols Cast Down
            Cost: 3m; Mins: Athletics 3, Essence 1
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            Surging with fell might, the Infernal adds her Essence to her Strength score for the purpose of qualifying for a feat of strength, and gains (Essence) non-Charm dice on the roll. If she attempts to destroy an object while one or more other characters who bear positive Ties towards it can witness the destruction she wreaks, the difficulty of the feat of strength is reduced by the intensity of the strongest such Intimacy, reducing the Strength requirement appropriately.

            Tower-Toppling Fist
            Cost: 1wp; Mins: Athletics 4, Essence 2 (The King Still Stands)
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: False Idols Cast Down
            With this Charm the Infernal can perform feats of strength to destroy objects at a greatly accelerated pace. A destructive feat that would take a day is instead completed in an hour, one that would take an hour takes a minute, one that would take a minute takes one action and one that would take an action becomes reflexive. At Essence 3+, the Infernal can reduce the time required to perform a feat by two stages.

            Growing Frenzy Attitude
            Cost: 5m; Mins: Athletics 5, Essence 3 (The King Still Stands)
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: False Idols Cast Down
            Each act of destruction only spurs the Infernal on to another. She gains (Strength) bonus dice and two non-Charm bonus dice on a feat of strength roll, plus two additional non-Charm dice for each time she has successfully used this Charm in the current scene, to a maximum of (Strength + Essence) dice.

            World-Crushing Solace
            Cost: -; Mins: Athletics 5, Essence 3 (The King Still Stands)
            Type: Permanent
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Permanent
            Prerequisite Charms: Growing Frenzy Attitude
            Once per scene when the Infernal succeeds on a feat of strength to destroy something with a difficulty equal to at least (5 + her Essence), she regains one point of Willpower.

            City-Smiting Ruin
            Cost: 12m, 1wp; Mins: Athletics 5, Essence 5 (The King Still Stands)
            Type: Reflexive
            Keywords: None
            Duration: One scene
            Prerequisite Charms: World-Crushing Solace
            The Infernal’s power is endless and untiring. Nothing can stand in the face of her rampage. She doubles 7s on feat of strength rolls, and can attempt any feat regardless of Strength requirement.

            My homebrew: Abyssals, Infernals, Dragon Kings, martial arts.

                                    Minor update to show I haven't totally forgotten about this. I fleshed out presence and thrown a bit, including an Act of Villainy charm for those who were fond of that mechanic. Here's the new stuff:

                                    Dastardly Villain Attitude
                                    Cost: -; Mins: Presence 2, Essence 1
                                    Type: Permanent
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Permanent
                                    Prerequisite Charms: None
                                    An Infernal with this Charm derives no end of satisfaction from acting the role of the worst kind of monster. Upon purchasing Dastardly Villain Attitude, she picks two of the following acts of villainy. When she meets the condition for one of her chosen acts, she gains a point of Willpower and can lose one point of Limit, though she can only perform each given act once per story. Additional acts can be bought for 3xp each.
                                    Infernal Genius Declaration: The Infernal shares the details of her master plan with her enemies, knowing that they will be able to use this information against her and potentially thwart that plan.
                                    Best Enemy Recognition: After developing a positive Tie towards an enemy who poses some threat to her, the Infernal assists him at no benefit to herself or spares him when he is wholly in her power.
                                    Fiendish Deathtrap Compulsion: Rather than personally deal with her enemies, the Infernal leaves them to be slain or held captive by her minions, a deadly environmental feature or some elaborate trap.
                                    Trusted Companion Subversion: Rather than oppose her foe directly, the Infernal deploys some scheme to turn the subject of one of his positive Ties against him.
                                    Chivalrous Fiend Obligation: The Infernal sacrifices some substantial advantage over a foe to give him a fighting chance, such as by ordering her troops to stand down so she can fight him one on one.

                                    Wrapped in Adoration
                                    Cost:                                     5m, 1wp; Mins: Presence 5, Essence 2 (Black Mirror Shintai)
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Cunning Deception Technique
                                    An Infernal with this Charm inspires deep and abiding passion in others – usually trust and devotion, but some delight in making others utterly consumed in hatred for themselves. When confronted with a character bearing a Defining Tie towards herself, the Infernal can roll a special (Manipulation + Presence) instill action to make that Tie inviolable. If she succeeds, any persuasion that would cause her target to act against the affected Tie can and must be treated as unacceptable influence.
                                    If the targeted Intimacy is lowered in strength, this Charm’s effects end. However, any successful attempt made to reduce it must be rerolled, taking the second result instead.

                                    Passion-Exploiting Artistry
                                    Cost: -; Mins: Presence 5, Essence 2 (Black Mirror Shintai)
                                    Type: Permanent
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Permanent
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Wrapped in Adoration
                                    An Infernal with this Charm never needs to roll to convince others to act on a Defining Tie towards herself. Furthermore, if she requests an inconvenient task, the target of her influence cannot attempt to resist in a Decision Point except when another character attempts to overturn the influence.

                                    Armoured in Impropriety Approach
                                    Cost:                                     7m, 1wp; Mins: Presence 5, Essence 3 (Black Mirror Shintai)
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: Mute
                                    Duration: One scene
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Our Little Secret
                                    The Infernal’s dark deeds are too confronting to even witness. Any character who sees her grossly violate social mores or laws will refuse to acknowledge her actions, instead continuing as though nothing had happened. Even after this Charm has ended, such characters will not remember that anything happened, having forced the incident out of their minds. The Infernal’s act must be something unsettling, not merely unacceptable – she could murder a satrap in front of his court and walk out unchallenged, but stealing from his treasury would not be aided by this Charm.
                                    A character whose Major or Defining Intimacy that is threatened by the Infernal can spend a point of Willpower to ignore this Charm’s effects.

                                    Dust Devil Feint
                                    Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Thrown 5, Essence 1
                                    Type: Supplemental
                                    Keywords: Withering-only
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Sandstrike Blast
                                    As she flays her foes alive, the Infernal dissolves into grains of sand herself and reappears elsewhere. After the completion of a withering attack made with Sandstrike Blast, she can transport herself one range band in any direction as long as she doesn’t move through a impermeable barrier.

                                    A Thousand Grains
                                    Cost:                                     1m; Mins: Thrown 4, Essence 2 (Shintai of the Endless Desert)
                                    Type: Supplemental
                                    Keywords: Uniform
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Flesh-Flensing Barrage
                                    Eventually even the most stubborn defence must wear away before the Infernal’s persistent onslaught. An attack enhanced by this Charm gains no benefit itself; however, if the Infernal attacks the same target on her next turn (or before it using reflexive attack Charms) she rerolls all 6s on that attack roll. Each round that she uses this Charm consecutively against the same target she rerolls the next lowest number as well, to a maximum of (Essence) different rerolled numbers, so after three rounds the Infernal will reroll 6s, 5s and 4s. If the Infernal fails to activate this Charm against her target for a full round, all her accumulated rerolls are lost.

                                    Pitiless Choking Wreath
                                    Cost:                                     4m; Mins: Thrown 3, Essence 2 (Shintai of the Endless Desert)
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: Withering-only
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Sandstrike Blast
                                    If the Infernal’s withering attack using Sandstrike Blast is successful, her target is enfolded in her sands, which continue to scrape at his flesh. He suffers an additional point of withering damage at the beginning of each of his turns until he escapes. Escaping the sands requires an action and a successful (Dexterity + Athletics) roll against a difficulty of (Infernal’s Thrown).
                                    If the Infernal’s attack crashes her target, his airways are additionally flooded with sand and he begins to suffocate until he is freed.

                                    Swallowed in Eternity
                                    Cost: 5m; Mins: Thrown 3, Essence 2 (Shintai of the Endless Desert)
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: Decisive-only
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Pitiless Choking Wreath
                                    While she has an enemy trapped in the Pitiless Choking Wreath, the Infernal can make a special decisive gambit using Sandstrike Blast at a difficulty equal to the target’s remaining health levels, which cannot be parried. If it succeeds, the ground beneath her target’s feet dissolves into sand which drags him under, never to be seen again. Whatever fate awaits him is unknown, save that Exalted victims of this Charm may not reincarnate until several years later.

                                    Death Races Onwards
                                    Cost:                                     6m, 1wp; Mins: Thrown 5, Essence 3 (Scarlet Rapture Shintai)
                                    Type: Reflexive
                                    Keywords: Decisive-only
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Carried on the Wind, Hungry Wind Howling
                                    After making a successful close range decisive attack, the Infernal launches herself two or three range bands forward and makes a secondary decisive attack applied against each other enemy she comes within close range of during this move. This attack carries a base damage of the Infernal’s Essence plus any threshold successes on the attack roll, and does not reduce the Infernal’s Initiative on a miss. Using this Charm counts as the Infernal’s movement action for the round.

                                    Scorpion-Tailed Mirage Technique
                                    Cost: 11m, 1wp; Mins: Thrown 5, Essence 3 (Shintai of the Endless Desert)
                                    Type: Reflexive
                                    Keywords: Perilous, Withering-only
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Dust Devil Feint, Dune-Burst Onslaught
                                    As her enemies try to strike her down, the Infernal explodes into a blast of sand that scrapes skin from their bones. She makes a reflexive withering clash against an incoming attack using her Sandstrike Blast, which automatically benefits from Dust Devil Feint.

                                    Updated Charm: World-Grinding Sandstorm Devastation
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Swallowed in Eternity, Scorpion-Tailed Mirage Technique

                                    My homebrew: Abyssals, Infernals, Dragon Kings, martial arts.

                                                            That's a pretty nice homebrew while waiting for the official Infernals to come out.
