    I feel like Demon the Descent has a good way of doing it. Like there are mechanics in play so certain wishes will be granted without the Demon needing to do anything. So person making a pact for strength will start getting stronger once the pact is sealed even if the Demon just leaves and doesn't do anything. However, there's also the option for the Demon to just use their own abilities to grant wishes if they want more control over how its handled or a faster handling on the time table. Example a person making a pact with a demon for a million dollars could just wait for the universe to start mucking around and such until the person has acquired the million or if the Unchained has access to that kind of wealth they can literally just pop down the suitcase full of bills, bullion, gems, or cut them a check. Now granted the Unchained have to list their end of the deal up front, even if part of it was a favor to be named later, but I feel like Infernals doing the wish thing probably would make more sense in the Mephistopheles and Faust roles than the Genie in Disney's Aladdin.

    Plus I like the idea having the option grant wishes my own way. Farmer wishes to be a prince, signs the deal next day finds various demons, elementals and what have you building their manor/keep/castle etc; the infernal hands them a certificate of nobility or what have you and offers to negotiate for an army to defend their territory or acquire more should those be fitting to the new noble's ambitions. (I personally always loved the Aladdin Musical at Disneyland where the Genie responded to Jafar wishing to be Sultan by tapping him pronouncing him Sultan and then snarking for a couple minutes.)

            One of the problems perhaps specific to Exalted where characters have lots of other capabilities and means, would be about introducing a very powerful specific wish power, for use in response to wishes by others (which I think has been mooted upthread), is where the power steps above what a their party can achieve by other means. That seems like it would lend itself to gamified manipulations, where some characters gets an external servant to wish for something which they want. Cute if it's a one off or infrequent, but I think it would feel a bit strange in a longer term, which could feel kind of fake.

            Probably part of my motivation here to argue (tentatively) for Internals to be incentivized to engage in wish granting behaviour, but have no dedicated wish power and use their general capabilities to achieve the end, to avoid that...

                    Originally posted by Ghosthead View Post
                    Those are less strong aspects with Infernals and this particular trick and trope (having Beastmen armies is a core Lunar thing since day one, and this is... less so)
                    Considering the history of Infernal Exalted in the setting, this is an argument that could be used to invalidate basically every possible way of expressing them, so I don't think it's a very strong one.

                    In deciding what capabilities for the Green Sun Princes are iconic enough to return and refine, the only thing we have are the parts of their late Second Edition hardback that people responded strongly to. By what I've seen these last nine odd years, some variation on the wish granting powers is included there.

                    If we focus on what Infernals have had for the same length of time as Lunars, well, there's not much to do around the piece of setting lore of them being employed as honour guard for the Ebon Dragon's nuptials. Besides, social interaction by way of Faustian bargain does not strike me as this huge stretch for how to realise an Exalt that needs to be both demonic and reminiscent of Solars; actually seems kind of basic. Kind of have a stronger basis for that than they do the green fire.

                    In any case, I find even myself to be focusing a bit too much on the side where they have much power to grant things (even if I think that should ultimately be a distinct part of it). That the key iconic side is in the ties of obligations this can create towards the Infernal. The image of them standing there with outstretched arm while the air swirls with green energy, and the impression that binding the agreement with a handshake is maybe not the best idea.

                    Combined with a bit of appropriate showmanship to draw people in.

                    You have a dream you wish to tell.
                    And it's just laughable, but hey kid what the hell.
                    'Cause you're one of a kind, a charming demon belle.
                    Now let's give these burning fools a place to dwell.
                    {take it boys}

                    I must admit that at a moment such as this, I see one of the limitations of mediating powers via Charms. Chronicles of Darkness Changelings get a whole dedicated sub-system to manage their procedures of supernatural deal-making, but Exalted books seem to lack much space for such things (and what Infernals might spare probably needs to be reserved for constructing their shintais).

                    I have approximate knowledge of many things.
                    Write up as I play Xenoblade Chronicles.
