        It also instills an Intimacy. And the key to any Infernal Wish Granter is to keep them coming back for more.

        You want to grant a wish that makes the mark want to wish again. And introduce to to their friends.

        I absolutely think it's fine for an Infernal to get their hooks in someone with a wish, then use other Charms to cement that grip.

        (That's basically how the 3E Social Influence system is structured.)

        You grant a wish, get a favour.
        Turn that favour into a pledge of loyalty.
        Now the mortal has a choice: take the broken arm and be done; live loyal to you; or ask for more wishes, digging themselves deeper.
        You should be leaning on that Intimacy either way. Maybe offer your new friend some rainbow coloured candy.

        Infernals are Mephistopheles handing out rope. They're dealers working to get people hooked. They're Rumplestiltskin. They're not friendly genis handing out favours and cashing in chits. You are definitely supposed to play smart to get the most bang for your buck, and part of that is to combo your charms together, and part of it is using Social Combat, and part of it is to use trickery and guile to force your mark into an impossible position.

                        Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                        Brainstorm. Somewhat connected. Alternate reality has a megacorp that has taken over everything and is basically that horrible megacorporation you see in those sci-fi dystopias. Until one of their victims got an infernal exaltation while awaiting 're-education'.

                        The girl's people were in indentured servitude, and used to be a bunch of natives owning land that the corp wanted. So now they're slaves, with their contracts signed in the name of the corporation.

                        It took the girl a year to turn the corporation into dust and wind. This brainstorming is just for me to write down what she can potentially do to a nation-state that has pissed her off.
                        Infernal is "pissed off".

                        Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                        A fair point, and I was perhaps being too simplistic.
                        You can wish people into power and have them indebted to you. They don't know they're indebted until you come to collect.

                        If there's some kind of parliamentary system, you don't need to call in all your markers at once. You don't call in a hundred chits telling all the senators to vote for the same Bill, you call in one chit telling one senator to make sure the Bill passes by any means neccessary.

                        You can trade a chit in to get people to swear loyalty to you. They now have an Intimacy of terrified awe for you to exploit, and they know that if they ever betray you something bad will happen (but they don't know if that something is 2L or Final Viridescence).

                        Or you just take your one favour. "You are in office because of me, now make me Chancellor with Emergency Powers".

                        It's not total, North Korea, control. But it's pretty much "enough" control. And okay, it's slightly more complex than you made it sound, but not much.

                            “They pissed me off” is not a sufficient answer. What I’d do and the lengths I’d go to do it in a fit of anger are entirely context dependent.

                            Is the Infernal pissed off because of an oppressive militarized police force is unfairly targeting and killing people of her race, with no real checks and balances to reign them in?

                            Is the Infernal pissed off because a government is lying about the availability of tests and PPE during a pandemic and encouraging people to risk their lives for the economy?

                            Is the Infernal pissed because her country’s head of state is demonstrating his lack of understanding of the laws he’s sworn to uphold by promising to sanction a platform for fact-checking him?

                            Is the Infernal pissed off because the so-called “justice” department is engaging in open corruption to protect a criminal while simultaneously dragging its feet regarding classifying white nationalists who shoot up terrified Americans in a Walmart as the terrorists they are?
