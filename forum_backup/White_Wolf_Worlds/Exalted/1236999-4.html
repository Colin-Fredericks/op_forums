    One thing before I continue this discussion...

    Forced marriage is not hilarious. I don't even know why the fact that is gay marriage would have anything to do with it. Are you trying to say that is a grievance from gay people towards heteros?

    The vast majority of people who suffer the shittier end of the realities of arranged marriages are women, many times girls. Many are raped, and some are so young that die as consequence of that rape.

    So saying this is hilarious because it is heteros forced into gay marriage is a shitty, vindictive and thoughtless thing to say.

    But since it was said in disfavor of a non minority makes it alright and nobody says anything.

    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    I'm inclined to assume that sorcery in-character has different considerations and concerns than those out-of-character, in regards to things like how consistent and reliable it can be perceived to be despite game rules necessarily making it so.

    So I could see many trans people in the setting viewing gender reassignment via sorcerous workings with a wary eye.
    This goes back to power and wealth. If you can afford a reliable, great sorcerer with the right materials, facilities and help, you're golden.

    If you have to go to the swamp witch, not so much.

    Its like being able to afford state of the art plastic surgery or having to go the guy that will pump you up with industrial grade silicone.


    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    If it's anything like real surrogacy, I presume that both egg and sperm come from the parents, and the surrogate merely provides the environment for the foetus to develop.
    You're talking extremely high tech modern surrogacy here.

    Mundane surrogacy at pre 20th/21st medicine levels involved getting someone's seed into someone's womb, by mechanical means, usual or otherwise.

    Extracting an egg, inseminating it and placing in back on the uterus is a modern medical miracle, that would surely be Sorcery in Creation, making this just fluff for a Sorcery conception.


    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    Dynastic child rearing being not only about their Essence, but providing the time and money and organisation to the scion's upbringing.

    It's probably not viewed as ideal for a couple to be incapable of conceiving a child, but the contribution of bringing up one that would otherwise present a severe burden to another couple seems to count for something.

    That doesn't really seem to be relevant for dynastic society. Child rearing is very hands off and financially supported by the family, with very little involvement of the parents.

    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    Hmmm... progenitive Essence is supposed to be beyond the reach of all lesser magic, but is still a metaphysical property that can go along with supernatural means of conception, so... I think it can just depend on how the method is phrased. It has to constitute some means of both Exalted parents contributing to forming the child; phrasing it in terms of transferring the Essence to a different person is out.
    I'm talking about a ritual, investing your daughter/son with your heritage, family history, blood and Essence, given mystical weight by Sorcery.
