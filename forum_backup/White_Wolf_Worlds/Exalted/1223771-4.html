                                            re: Behavior Traits
                                            As aforementioned, the Wyld book outright states:
                                            • You don't need derangements - Creation treats them like such shit, many become that shitty
                                            • ....but derangements are a legit possibility - beware the "pretty winged princess" who likes torture
                                            • Debilities will warp your thinking. The "Diet: Blood" or "Diet: Raw Meat" are excellent examples. No human lives like that (exclusively). And human-prey runs/swims much slower than deer or fish. A beastmen with qualms against preying on humans will (a) get over it the day some random bigot attacks them (so that's fair game) or (b) starve and then re-evaluate the slow-moving hairless apes in the village. If humans eat apes, why wouldn't an ape-man eat a human?
                                            • Positive mutations will warp your thinking. If I could "breath underwater" - and was a pirate - do you think I'd risk my life in honest combat against a bunch of (probable) bigots who rather skin me? Nope. I'd burn merchant ships and then peacefully scavenge the wreckage when it sinks. That'd be just as bloody as a pirate with a sword - except the locals are more freaked out because they see it as a "monster who likes cooking people alive and eating the drowned".
                                            Ma-Ha-Suchi is a good reference. So is that Monkey-Queen Sorceress-Thing (the one with the banquets full of human corpses). Any Beastman who emulates Lunar heart-consumption and cannibalism is an example (yes, I hate these types; yes, I know OOC it doesn't "work"; but neither OOC realities changes IC behavior). As aforementioned, the Island of Dr. Moreau comes to mind.

                                            But above all, I never suggested imposing this as a penalty on players. I'm 100% that they're "human" OOC. It's IC where I'd reason "oh yeah, these locals are terrified of your obviously inhuman features" or "those poachers want your tusks" or "the Inexhausitble mutation? after your stunts the last month, the apothecary thinks he can make Exalted-viagra from your skin" (or whatever). Even nice people get accused of being monsters (and expendable).

                                            re: Rhino Beastmen Heroes vs Fair Folk
                                            This is a plausible scenario for a happy ending.
