                            Page 203. Both ambushes and surprise attacks only work if the character has successfully concealed himself via Stealth.

                            For an ambush, the defender must be completely unaware of the attacker's presence AND the attacker must win initiative.

                            For a surprise attack, the attacker must merely be hidden from the defender.

                            Your situation would be resolved with a normal Join Battle roll. If NPC Y wins the Join Battle, it gets to go first.
