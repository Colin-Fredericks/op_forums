                                  deep in the wyld there are horrors and wonders that amaze even the Raksha. Amongst the worlds the Primordials made before Zen-Mu lurks a sphere made entirely of soulsteel. No Primordial claims responsibility for this place, and it is only called the Dead World. Nothing exists here save Soulsteel, and whoever created it abandoned it soon after. Occasionally a Deathlord will send a Abyssal deep into the Wyld in search of this el dorado, none beliefing it anything more than a Faerie tale.                                       Originally posted by Daredevil View Post

                                      The game you played sounds very cool & ambitious. Could you share some details? I'd love to hear how the ST sold the freakish & weird elements.
                                      Okay, so this was a crossover game between 3 different Exalted games, 2 STs.

                                      So we had 3 games, each ran for about a story, each with 6-7 characters drawn from a mix of the 3 games.

                                      My Zenith Alazar and his companion Cid found themselves on a floating island over a great void, where they met a Lunar and two Solars from an alternative Creation, and a Solar from a third world. There were hundreds of floating islands, which slowly drifted up from the frozen void towards a red sun covered in screaming faces with huge hungry mouths. As the islands rose, they defrosted, awakening a variety of talking animals from slumber. These animals sometimes got on, sometimes ate each other. However, as the islands got closer to the sun they'd try to jump down to other islands, because eventually the islands would fall up in the mouths of the sun and burn up.
                                      The sun was inhabited by an ancient smith who sent clockwork angels out to do his will, while much lower down the Queen of Air and Darkness ruled a realm of predators by her pacts with the spirits of the dark void.

                                      My Twilight Shadow of Kings however, escaped his own world (because he was being chased by his clone who wanted to eat him because there can only be one, long story) deliberately, and became chief advisor to the Tree-King. This was on a large island surrounded by an eternal sea. Instead of a sun and stars, it had a huge pillar of light in the centre of the island which rotated. The five peoples of the island (huge blue giants, fish-people who need land to spawn, ent-like tree people, halfling-sized golem-suits of armour, and eusocial lizards) lived in peace under the yellow light. But then a Dawn from another world arrived and incited the fish-people to war. At about the same time, the light of the sun-pillar turned red. The seas began to rise, pushing the five peoples to war over territory to flee the rising waters. Shadow of Kings began producing magitek chips to put inside the peaceful tree-people to help them fight. Then he met some Solars and a Dragonblood from alternative Creations and after some conflict we worked to take down the Dawn and create a new island via sorcery, and arks to take the people there. I learnt that over time the pillar changes between the colours of the Five Maidens, each creating a period associated with that Maiden (a time of trade and migration, a time of war, etc).

                                      The other ST took a slightly different approach.

                                      My Full Moon Dr Soma woke up in a jungle surrounded by Solars from other worlds. They explored and discovered that, again, this was a realm of talking animals who'd overthrown and destroyed humanity at the command of the Sea People. While there were some ancient and mysterious ruins which we explored a bit later, we spent most of our time trying to expel the Pigs, Dogs, Cats and Swans of the Animalist State who'd come across the sea to conquer the local Monkeys and Snakes, or as they saw it, enlighten them by killing the humans and establishing True Animalism, where all animals are equal (but maybe pigs are more equal than others...). Their battle groups with rifles and machine guns were slightly tricky, but our combat-Exalts didn't have too much difficulty, so we soon expelled them from the city and then fought them through the jungle.
                                      But Professor Python and his rebels (who had some kind of vague ideological link to the Meowist State across the mountains) then unleashed a further revolution, with monkey suicide bombers and assassins, and all got quite messy.
                                      In the end, however, a tenuous peace deal was worked out between the various political factions, and Luna took us back to our own world.

                                      After we'd all escaped, we met up in a world beyond the worlds, where we met some other Solars and Lunars, who'd also been in strange worlds. The only one I remember was a giant city where golems fought over a massive library of stone tablets where the secrets of many worlds were written. There were others I think, just can't remember them.

                                      Did he reveal why the Primordials discarded each one?
                                      No. It was 3-5 sessions for each, and the main plot was how to escape, or rather how to pass the Incarnae's tests of righteousness to earn an escape.

                                      STing Bronze Age Exalted
