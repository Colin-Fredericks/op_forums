    Exalted: Essence anima powers

    04-23-2020, 04:08 PM
    So, in Essence, anima powers work kind of differently.

    Everyone has an always-on effect. Then they have, IIRC (can't find the post, if anyone finds it post it), another power that comes on when they flare, and another power that comes on when they flare iconically.

    So... what could these be?

    So for example perhaps a Wood Aspect always gets a bonus against poison and disease, a Defence buff against wooden weapons when flaring, and when they're iconic they poison people nearby. Maybe a No Moon gets the ability to detect magical sites normally, a bonus (rather than penalty) to stealth when flaring, and a bonus to spellcasting and magic when flaring iconically.

    Or some such.

    So what do you think? How closely do you think they'll hew to normal 3rd edition's anima powers?

                  *I think sometimes in 3rd edition different Castes across different splats have different anima powers just for the sake of it.

                  For example, Full Moons have a bonus to, say, being able to lift really heavy objects and running really fast, which fits Creation's greatest predators more than Creation's greatest warriors. Similarly the Dawn's ability to use the most complicated and difficult combat techniques more often fits them better than it does Full Moons.
                  But both have a bonus to resetting initiative. Full Moons have a better, much more expensive initiative booster that's once/battle. Dawns' isn't as good, but it's cheaper and can be used repeatedly.
                  If things are being simplified, why not just give them both an initiative-related bonus that's the same?


                  On an unrelated matter, I think because the idea was to give everyone in 3rd ed 3 anima powers, quite a few Exalt types have some really important, thematic and significant anima powers, and others that are less so. Also, some represent similar things.

                  For example, Dawns get a bonus to intimidation, a power that represents them being really skilled at combat, and another power that represents them being really skilled at combat. The second and third ones could easily be changed to something else that represents being really skilled at combat if necessary (it probably isn't, just making a point). The first one could be swapped for something else (say, a bonus to resisting Fear like Full Moons) and I don't think it'd really be an issue. Not saying it should be, just that it could.
                  Similarly, Air Aspects get to jump well, ignore falling damage, blow away ranged projectiles, and 1/day get a bonus on a social or mental action representing a principle. Any of them could be changed in mechanical implementation, and if the bonus reflecting your principles didn't turn up, I don't think this would destroy the point of Air Aspects (not saying it's bad and should be dropped, it's not, but I mean the writers don't need to force it in if it doesn't work well due to a changed Intimacy system or the way Animas work).

                  (Huh, I just noticed that the Wood Aspects in 3rd ed have lost their bonus against wooden weapns and instead can ignore penalties to movement rolls or Evasion. Didn't realise that, even though I'm playing a Wood Aspect.)



                  Hmmmm... I wonder how, in a system where you have 1 always-on anima power, one at flaring and one at iconic, Night Caste's powers will work? Their power to hide their identity when iconic still works of course. But "don't flare" or "ignore penalties to Stealth" might need to be reworked.

                  I also have wondered if there's no personal and peripheral essence in Essence (it'd simplify things) and Anima works either by just how many motes you've spent, or even you can just choose to bring it up to get the bonus. Because either of those would really alter how Night's work.

                                  Originally posted by Chausse View Post

                                  Not trying to brag but I did a post to propose alternative Anima powers for Solars, I decided to do this after I reached the same conclusion than you that most anima powers are really replaceable or extremely specific
                                  This is sort of true.

                                  Dawns: Be scary, have good combat skill, have good skill. Zeniths: cremate corpses, force spirits to appear, combat bonuses vs demons/undead/etc. Twilights: teleport to a place of power, control spirits, be tough. Nights: hide your Solar-ness, be stealthy, hide your identity under Solarness. Eclipses: Sanctify oaths, learn spirit magic, have diplomatic protection in spirit/ghost/Fae courts.

                                  So some can be done mechanically in different ways (combat bonuses vs Creatures of Darkness, for example). Some I think are awkward with a "you get 1 all the time, 1 with caste mark, 1 when flaring iconically". Some are I think not the best expression of that Caste, or something that was brought in for 3rd edition which lacks the kind of weight of something that's been around for ages in terms of its significance in Exalted... but then, that last one makes no difference to new players.

                                  Anyway. I would quite like to see a different set of Twilight anima powers (as I think the current ones are very, very sorcery-themed) but that's not really about simplifying the game.
