    A thing I've been wondering. Let's say that you created an artificial intelligence through sorcerous workings. Like, say, an A.I. manager that can spin off sub-programs for itself that serves as an assistant to management and control.

    I'm thinking of two versions:

    1. One that serves to cut through red tape and speed up bureaucratic processes, and is used in the upper levels of government.

    2. One that is can connect to everyone in a nation, and can talk to them individually while having no penalties due to having to do multiple things at once.

    So let's say its for a thought experiment. A charm that lets you carry out multiple actions at once (i.e. you're an A.I. and you're managing multiple projects/ talking to multiple people). You can implement and plan new regulations for drugs, reorientate the economic policies for the housing market, plan a propaganda campaign against smoking, write up a project for road development, and then also plan military spending. All at the same time, at high speeds, and with your full dice pool.

    Is this something a 2nd circle level being can have, or is it something reserved for 3rd circles?
