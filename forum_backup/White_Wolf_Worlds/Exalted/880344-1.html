        According to the 2014-2015 OP publishing brochure:

        Ex3 Arms of the Chosen – The definitive tome of artifact weapons and armor for the Exalted.
        Arms expands and develops the Evocation system introduced in the core book, by which
        the Chosen may derive unique Charms from artifacts. Includes a wide selection of weapons,
        armor, and warstriders. 64 pages. PDF/PoD.
        Now, there are ten artifacts with evocations in the core and they take up about 15 pages. Assuming a similar page count to artifact ratio, I'd bet we'll be getting at least twenty more artifacts.

        I'm not sure what "expands and develops" actually means though. It might just explain what Evocations look like for Non-Solars. Or it might be guidelines and advice for building your own. Hard to tell.
