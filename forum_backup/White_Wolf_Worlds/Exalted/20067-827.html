            Originally posted by Warpwind View Post

            and I didn't mean flurry 2 attacks. I meant, +2 bonus as for using 2 similar weapons. you sort of are using 2 similar sets of fists.
            Note that the +2 is only for clash attacks, not all attacks. In any case, though, dual-wielding is pretty clear: unarmed counts as only a single weapon, so you don't get the bonus on clash attacks if you're just using two fists.

            Originally posted by Warpwind
            .Found this gem in water dragon style right now tho.

            "She can flurry a full defense with an
            attack, ignoring the Defense penalty for flurrying."

            seems very complimentary. you get full defense with attack, each at only -1 with extra limbs merit. and if using at least 2 tiger claws, +2 bonus.
            You would only get the +2 bonus if you were clashing, not all attacks. Note that Full Defense doesn't take a penalty anyway, since there's no roll involved in it. So with this charm and Extra Limbs, you could be wielding a pair of tiger claws and do something else with your third limb, and you'd be able to attack one target, at -1, and get a +2 bonus to Defense (for using Full Defense). If you delayed your action so that you were taking it on the same initiative count as your target (or had another charm that allowed a clash), then you'd get the +2 bonus on the attack (though you'd still take the -1 for flurrying, leaving you at a net +1), but you'd still have to pay the initiative cost for delaying your action, and unlike the initiative cost of Full Defense, the one for delaying wouldn't be refunded by the charm. This would only be a strong strategy, though, if you were actually fighting more than one person - the whole point of clash attacks is that they're directly opposed rolls of the attacking skills of the people involved, not a roll of one person's skill vs. the other's Defense. So if you're just fighting one person, it would be best to just clash them, gaining the +2 bonus on the attack, because improving your Defense wouldn't matter, it wouldn't be going up against them anyway.
