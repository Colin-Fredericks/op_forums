    Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post

    Speaking of, there's a situation that the rules have never quite covered that comes up for Lunars most often and I'm hoping a textbox or something could be added to the Lunar's book.

    Many magical disguise charms specify that they're "impossible to see through" and Lunar shapeshifting actually physically turns you into the target of your sacred hunt... but what do you do if someone acts out of character? How do you know when an NPC should get suspicious? Should you give PCs who are using these charms a warning if they're about to do something out of character or should you just let them do it?
    In some cases, it may be evident that an impenetrably disguised character can't be who they appear to be. Maybe an onlooker succeeds on a read intentions or profile character roll that provides information that just seems completely irreconcilable with the impersonated character's personality. Maybe an onlooker was just having lunch with the person the impostor is impersonating. If a character in such a position has reason to know about the possibility of a shapeshifting or other supernatural impostor (good place for an Occult roll, depending on circumstances), they might reasonably conclude that the reason for this is that the impostor is not who they appear to be.

    As a Storytelling tip, I don't recommend using this as a "gotcha" on players, or undermining Flawlessly Impenetrable Disguise et al. with it. It's just a thing that might come up sometimes.

    Actually, how often are people killed because someone thought they were a Lunar in disguise and they weren't? I'd tend to assume that it would happen more often than someone being correct about it, really.
    I'm sure it happens.
