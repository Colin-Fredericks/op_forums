    [Silly] Legendary Pokemon Exalts

    12-21-2023, 06:59 PM
    So I have this idea just rolling in my head for a while so it is better to just get it out to the wild so it will stop living rent free there, ok?

    Basically, many Legendary and Mythical Pokémon are treated as "deity like beings", surrounded by mythologies and related to the principles of the world. Some fit that definition more than others (which is a big point of discussion in the fandom about "what makes a Legendary Pokémon Legendary?"), but in essence it does make me think, as a fan of both Pokémon and Exalted, "If those Pokémon were presented as gods, what if they had Exalts of their own expressing their themes and properties?" in the same manner I think of, let's say, the Exalts of deities in RL mythologies, combinations of Exalted and Fate, or how would the Chosen of Dragonlance deities might look like. Each is a mess on its own, so let's get back to the subject- styling Exigents as the Chosen of different Legendary Pokémon.

    First, let's define the scope of this project- I am not going to write whole Charmsets to each Exigent. Instead, I won't to focus more on the "feel" of the Exigent. To do so, I am going to present them in a manner similar to how potential Exigents and gods were in the introduction chapter of Exigents- some example for their powers, some background where I adapt the Pokémon to the Exalted setting, etc. It should be noted that due to the nature of Pokemon, not all fit the feeling of a "god" and some work better as elementals (or maybe even ghosts and demons), so for now I am going to focus less on being correct to the metaphysics of Exigence and just say "this is how a Chosen of Mewtwo might look like" with "this is what Mewtwo might be in the Exalted setting".

    And with that in mind, let's start from the beginning- Gen 1, the Legendries of Kanto.

    The Legendary Wings
    There are three Legendary Wings (not counting their Galarian Variants, they will be discussed separately at the Galar section)- Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres. While they are among the first Legendries presented in the games, they are also among those who are the most disputed by the fandom for being considered "Legendary" due to how they lack lore within the games. Still, the anime and manga do provide some mythology to work on, such as their connection to Lugia as their master and their connection to natural powers, with an overall theme of storms (as related to their master). Within this in mind, how would this Legendary Trio will be adapted to Exalted?

    Across the West, a trio of islands is found- the Stormbound Isles, where three different tribes resides. Each of the tribes maintains a sacred shrines to a powerful elemental, bound within a temple and meant to be maintained asleep. Long time ago, the three bird-shape elementals chose the nest in each of the Isles, and almost immediately they fell into infighting over dominance and power. As the great spirits harnessed the natural forces in their war, the people of the isles suffered under the changing weather patterns and ruin, desperately praying to someone to save them from the chaos. Answering the call, a great sea god have risen and smitten down the elementals, bringing peace to the Isles. Guided by the wisdom of their shamans, the tribes trapped each of the bird gods within shrines, one on each of the Isles- the Shrine of Ice to the Snowcloak, the Shrine of Thunder to the Thunderstroke, and the Shrine of Fire to the Everburning. In order to keep the spirit calm and appeased, a geomantic culture have taken place across the Isles- a collections of small altars, idols and sacred architecture was constructed, both feeding and harmonizing the natural energies alongside those of the elementals, maintaining them in hibernation.

    For centuries the peace was kept, yet recently House Peleps has started colonizing the Isles, bringing Immaculate missionaries with them. At that point, the legend of the three titans have turned into myth, with the locals simply treating the shrines and their practices as measures to stop a "coming disaster". The Immaculates, from their side, viewed the geomantic faith as almost heretical, especially as they have sensed the local dragonlines spiraling around the shrines and getting drained by them. Believing that even if there is a grain of truth in the myth it is not something they couldn't handle, the monks have started to convert the locals, deny their prayers to the Stormbringers and shatter the alters and idols which kept the dragonlines flowing. The results were disastrous- the three elementals were freed from their boundaries, and went through a mad rampage which brought death upon the tribes and Dynasts as one.

    While they delighted in their freedom, they quickly noticed that their very fabric was tied to that of the Isles, and with the geomantic patterns shattered, they have started to overload with power. Seeing their gods in a mad state, three shamans reached out to the storm, beseeching them to stop the rampage. As an answer, the three elementals turned to those mortals vessels and stroke them down- one was frozen with snow, the other was hit by lightning, and the third was burned in fire. And yet, the three shamans have survived- either by intent or by instinct, the great spirits have reached beyond themselves to invest the mortals with the mad power which ignited their Essence, granting to them the miracle of Exaltation. Soon afterwards, the skies cleared and the three Stormbringers have returned to the shrines, diminished in form and seeking to sleep to regain their power. Their new Chosen have risen to seal the shrines and lead their nations to rebuild the geomantic constructs, allowing the dragonlines to keep the ancients once more in their sleep- and this time, they'll have their Chosen of the Storm to make sure they won't be disturbed.

    While treated as a collective, the Chosen of the Storm are three different Exalts yet with some common relations. Each serves as the guardian of one of the shrines, and after their death their Exaltation would return to the shrine of the corresponding Stormbringer, where it would reside as a crystal orb until a proper vessel with the appropriate elemental affinity and demeanor were to show submission before the natural powers. Unworthy vessels would be destroyed by the elemental forces within the orbs- yet souls who truly recognize the destructive force of nature and the humility to stand before it might be Chosen as new vessels of the Exaltation.

    General Traits:
    Evocations: all Chosen of the Storms are resonant with blue jade, while each is neutral with another color of jade, depending on their patron: white for the Snowcloak, black for the Thunderstroke and red for the Everburning. They are dissonant with all other magical materials.

    Sorcery & Necromancy: all Chosen of the Storm can access First Circle Sorcery and can not learn Necromancy under any circumstances.

    Martial Arts: the Chosen of the Storms are limited by the Terrestrial keyword for Martial Arts.

    Ox Body Mode: Dragon Blooded

    Affinity: Abilities

    Chosen of the Snowcloak
    The wisest and most careful of his kin, Snowcloak was reserved and calculated, never initiating a battle with the other Stormbringers and only responds to their attacks. A presence of ice and frost, Snowcloack is considered to be a beautiful visage, drawn to those lost in its storms to either guide them to safety or to doom them forever. As such, the Chosen of Snowstorms can:

    - invoke freezing winds and falling snow in their presence
    - maintain a calm demeanor against supernatural mental manipulations
    - sense a coming doom to their loved ones
    - blind their enemies with brilliant whiteness
    - learn sorcerous secrets beyond their regular capabilities
    - maintain preciouses treasures in seals made out of ice

    Chosen of Snowstorms: Exalted Advantages

    Cloak of Brilliance: while surrounded by snow, Chosen of Snowstorms can add their Essence to their Parry Defense.

    Doom Sense: commit one mote. While committed, the Chosen of Snowstorms can sense whether a certain individual's destiny leads them towards a coming danger. This advantage can only detect nearby dangers (no more than one day per Essence).

    Elemental Resonance: the Chosen of Snowstorms are resonant with blue and white jade.

    Chosen of the Thunderstroke
    Violent and instinctive, Thunderstroke is always feared for the wrath it may bring. While it is short tempered and destructive, it is not vile in nature- it never keeps grudges, and forgets its anger as quick as it expresses it. Constantly vibrating with energy, it never stays too long in one place, filled with the desire to announce its presence to the world. As such, the Chosen of Thunderstorms can:

    - move as fast as a lightning
    - paralyze their opponents with an electric touch
    - reach levels of evocations beyond their regular limits
    - ignite desires through powerful yells
    - gather a thunderstorm above their enemies
    - enter a instinctive, raging state where pain means nothing

    Chosen of Thunderstorms: Exalted Advantages

    Lightning Rod: when hit by attacks which are either electric or similar enough in nature (such as sparks of pure energy or wild sorcerous power), the Chosen of Thunderstorms does not take damage, and instead gains one mote per such attack absorbed.

    Static Presence: commit one mote. While committed, any trivial character who touched the Chosen of Thunderstorms either directly or through a conducting material is incapacitated until the end of the scene. Nontrivial characters can roll Integrity + Fortitude against Difficulty 3, and can make this roll again once per turn in case they fail. Objects made out of magical materials are always considered to be conducting to the purpose of this ability.

    Elemental Resonance: the Chosen of Thunderstorms are resonant with blue and black jade.

    Chosen of the Everburning
    Engulfed with constant flame, the Everburning is a magnificent sight to behold. While always motivated for war, the Everburning is also a honorable spirit, trying to avoid destruction which it deems unnecessary- even though it often views a certain amount of ruin as "healthy" to replenish the world. When challenged, it never backs down, and seeks to inspire others to follow its footsteps. As such, the Chosen of Firestorms can:

    - envelope their bodies with scorching flame
    - inspire others in battle
    - shoot arrows of flame upon their enemies
    - reach levels of martial arts beyond their regular boundaries
    - ignite a flame that does not burn the innocents but consumes the guilty
    - walk into a molten lava and leave unscathed

    Chosen of Firestorms: Exalted Advantages

    Burning Presence: commit one mote. While committed, the Chosen of Firestorms creates a ongoing fire around themselves. This takes the form of an environmental hazard who causes one damage to anyone in close ranger besides the Chosen of Firestorms once per two rounds. Flammable material is also ignited by this ability.

    Healing Heat: even the most boiling magma will not burn the Chosen of Firestorm- and will even heal them instead. Whenever they are meant to suffer damage from fire or heat source (be it mundane or supernatural), the Exalt does not take any damage and heals one point of damage instead.

    Elemental Resonance: the Chosen of Firestorms are resonant with blue and red jade.

    So while planned on completing Mew and Mewtwo in the same Gen 1 post, it is already late here and I need to prepare to sleep, so I'll make it later. Hope you found it of interest!

      Moving to the next set of Kanto Legendries, let's talk about the-

      Genesis Duo
      Unlike many Legendary Duos, Trios, and so forth, The connection between Mew and Mewtwo is both much more intimate and much more distant- Mewtwo is derived from Mew, but it is not like they have some interaction together in the game (well, generally speaking- the movie did emphasized a sort of rivalry between the two, and the Mewtwo Raid event in SV did relied on it). As such, we can define the two as Mew serving as the "Origin of Species" and Mewtwo being the "man made god" which is derived from it. Pokemon Occultism is a thing, in case you weren't aware, and in the case of this Duo there is a strong resonance between Mew serving as "Adam Kadmon" while Mewtwo is the "homunculus". Regardless, let's focus on how the origin of life in Pokemon and its clone might feature within the Exalted lore...

      When the makers of the world conceived the idea of life independent of their own, it were Gaia and Cytherea who supplied the original basis behind it- a living body made out of matter by the Emerald Mother, and a sparking soul derived from the divine light by the Shining Mystery. As a proof of that concept, the two have first presented before the ancients a being who was made from them yet was independent of them- the Protoplast, Origin of Species. From the Protoplast each of the titans took a bit of its Essence, creating species of their own liking while it remained hidden from sight, bound in sleep in the distant Zen Mu.

      Fast forward to the late days of the First Age, and the Solars too were filled with the desire to make new life, original and unique. While they have modified the flesh of countless people through the years of their rule, it was always a mimicry, nothing original- and how could one describe themselves as suppressing the makers of the world if there weren't able to mimic the very act of creation? Discovering the legends of the Protoplast, the Solars, in their hubris, reached to the forbidder lands and forced the denizens to reveal to them the sleeping location of the Origin of Species. Bringing it back to Creation while still in a sleeping form, they experimented upon the behemoth, draining its Essence to create the greatest and more superior version of it- a new Protoplast in their own form and image, who would usher a new age of terrifying life upon Creation.

      The results were catastrophic. The new Protoplast could not have been controlled- a behemoth of flesh and anger incarnated, it shattered its bonds and caused the manse palace to collapse upon its makers during its escape, leaving the Protoplast buried in the ruins. A few days later, while the Solars still discussed about whether should they capture or slay the beast, the Usurpation took place, all while the entity hid in the ruins of the First Age. Naming itself as Anthroparion the Secondborn, it returned to the facilities from which it was made to find the being from which it was born- yet the Protoplast vanished without traced. Believing that it was destroyed or captured by the Dragon Blooded, Anthroparion instead worked to rebuild the laboratory in secret, evolving its own skills for centuries while capturing beasts, men and eventually Exalts. Through its research, it unlocked how to make new beings from itself. Through its research, it discovered the secrets of Exaltation. And through its research, it plans to finally fulfil its goal of replacing all life with itself, becoming the new Origin of Species- and the coming Time of Tumult might be the greatest opportunity to do so.

      While Anthroparion have worked on its horrifying magnum opus, the Protoplast, who was believed to be destroyed, have started roaming Creation. Finally free from its capturers, it escaped during the fight between the Solars and the Dragon Blooded, allowing itself to be enjoy the prosperity which was born from itself. In its quest, it has seen beasts and humans, gods and demons, living and dead and stranger things still. It learned the truth behind many mysteries and became a mystery be itself- a nomad spirit who might bestow plenty at randomness, filled with childlike wonder and desire for creation. Then came the Great Contagion, and while through its ever adapting Essence the Protoplast was immune as long as there was at least one single living being who could have survived the plague, it could have done little to stop its spread. And then came the Balorian Crusade, and the land itself screamed in pain. And then came the Sword of Creation, and the world was filled with death and ashes. Crying before the ruin, the Protoplast have felt the desire to spread life from itself, repopulate the land which was lost. Invoking the secret he gleaned through his quests, he brought forth new Chosen to guard Creation, while returning to his eternal sleep, from which is might never awaken again.

      A product of pain and scientific ingenuity, the Chosen of the Secondborn are created beings. Formed within the hidden laboratories of Anthroparion, they are formed through the cloning of a human body with the Secondborn's Essence infused into their form, allowing for a soul to be made. The original is often being quickly disposed by Anthroparion, yet a few have managed to escape the experiments, telling strange tales about the torturer deity hidden deep below the ground. While each of the Chosen resembles the original body from which it was made, the Essence of the Secondborn have left its marks upon their forms, and its rage always fills their hearts. Slowly swarming into society, the Homunculi bid their time- even though some have started to enjoy the world that they built for themselves, far from the endless rage of the Secondborn.

      On the contrary, the Chosen of the Protoplast celebrate the life around them. Taking the form of natural plenty such as fruits, nuts and even animals, the Exaltation of the Protoplast is hidden in plain sight all across Creation. Most of those who east those Seeds of Life simply become their bearers, giving them more healthy bodies and resilient souls. The Seeds, in turn, will pass through generations, until eventually finding a heroic soul with caring traits and expressive nature, bestowing upon them the miracle of Exaltation. When the Exalt dies, or if a bearer dies without a heir, their body quickly decomposes into a plentiful of plants of all kinds, and those people and animals who would enjoy their fruits of the Exaltation will become bearers of the Seeds themselves. It is unknown how many of those Seeds of Life the Protoplast have made, for rarely do those Chosen interact with one another, and most are just considered to be Exigents of some sort.

      Chosen of Genesis
      Product of sorcerous experiments, Anthroparion lives in its flesh- corporal entity who seeks to replace the current Origin of Life and bring forth a new age of flesh. As the Solars have only replicated the body of the Protoplast and not its soul, Anthroparion is a wrathful and envious entity, and its Chosen inherit its complex connection to the world, being fleshworks by themselves. As such, Chosen of Genesis can:

      - command living beings which they deem lesser to them
      - enhance their bodies with Essence to shatter through barriers
      - ignore mental compulsions and the needs of their bodies
      - manipulate force vectors who come in touch with them
      - crush their enemies by controlling their organic structure
      - manipulate memories of through eye contact or touch

      General Traits:
      Evocations: Chosen of Genesis are resonant with orichalcum and neutral with all other magical materials.

      Sorcery & Necromancy: Chosen of Genesis can access up to the Third Circle of Sorcery yet only up to Second Circle Necromancy.

      Martial Arts: Chosen of Genesis are not limited by the Terrestrial keyword yet can't access the Mastery keyword or learn Sidereal Martial Arts.

      Ox Body Mode: Solar

      Affinity: Attributes

      Castes: the Chosen of Genesis has two Castes- Soma, who favors physical attributes, and Psyche, who favors mental ones.

      Exalted Advantages

      Disturbing Presence: the Chosen of Genesis are unnatural beings, and even though they can integrate to society most creatures feel their alien nature by instinct. Common animals will try to stay away from those Exalts and will not initiate combat unless forced to, while they gain their Essence in dice to any attempt to intimidate or instill negative emotions in others,

      Mind over Body: the bodies of the Chosen of Genesis have transcended mortal limitations. They do not require sleep, food or water to survive.

      Resonance: the Chosen of Genesis are resonant with orichalcum

      Chosen of Evolution
      A all loving and curious entity, the Protoplast feels kinship towards all living beings. All are its children, and all deserve its love- morality means nothing to the Protoplast, nor are social constructs such as nations, race or or boundaries. All is part of it, and it is part of all. As such, the Chosen of Evolution can:

      - commune with any organic entity regardless of language or origin
      - share emotional state and pain across different individuals
      - inspire positive impressions upon any creature
      - learn techniques from other Exalts
      - adapt their Essence to survive harsh environments, poisons and diseases
      - transform their bodies to other beings while having access to pieces of their body

      General Traits:
      Evocations: the Chosen of Evolution are neutral with all magical materials, yet they can become resonant with one through adaptions

      Sorcery & Necromancy: Chosen of Evolution can access up to Second Circle Sorcery, and could never learn Necromancy.

      Martial Arts: Chosen of Evolution are not limited by the Terrestrial keyword yet can not access the Mastery keyword or learn Sidereal Martial Arts.

      Ox Body: Lunar

      Affinity: Essence

      Adaptions: the Chosen of Evolution hold a special ability to learn Charms for other Exalts by having them serving as their tutors. They must, however, have the needed prerequisites in terms of Essence, Abilities and Attributes. Using certain Charms, they can also adapt more advanced techniques, such as Martial Arts, Sorcery and Evocations.

      Exalted Advantages

      Adaptions: given a tutor, the Chosen of Evolution can learn the Charms and modes of other Exalts, assuming their have the required prerequisites.

      Kinship: the Chosen of Evolution is able to easily forge connections with others, gaining 2 dice bonus to any attempt to instill a positive emotion or intimacy. Additionally, by spending one mote, they can create a empathic sense with someone with which they have a positive Intimacy. They can only maintain one such connection at a time.

      Adaptable Resonance: Chosen of Evolution shares the same resonance as their current Kinship bond.

      Finished Gen 1. Hopefully I'll manage to complete Gen 2 today as well!

          Let's start Gen 2, shall we?

          Legendary Beasts
          The sub legendary Trio of Johto, while sharing some resemblance to to the Legendary Wings those Legendries feels a lot more like "gods" than "elementals"- probably because they have a proper myth in games tying them together, being reborn by Ho Oh from the ruin of a tower where each embody an aspect of the cataclysm- the lightning who stroke the tower, the fire which engulfed it and the rain who put down the flames. As those three are much more related to one another than the Wings, the dilemma about whether making them a single form of Chosen who are simply closely related as I did for the Wings, or a single form of Exaltation where each of the Beasts is a different Aspects. This question is going to rise whenever I will be dealing with a certain group of Exalts, yet in this case I am still tending towards making them separate Exalts. This is because that while they are still very much tied together, Suicune have gained a "special relation" a few times in the series, and while they now all share the same Hidden Ability originally each had its own unique one (which honestly was better). So even though they are more similar to one another than the Wings are, the key differences are enough to make me break them into a trio of different Exalts (even the other choice is also legitimate imo).

          Anyway, with this in mind, here we go-

          Across the Scavenger Lands, a small city state known as Enju sits, well known for its rich theatrical culture. Enju's elites are known as the kimono dancers- traditional storytellers who recreate the city's history and mythology through traditional dancing and acting, drawing many visitors from all across the region to view the artistic expressions which utilize the ruling family's sorcerous arts. The most famous of those stories is that of the Fallen Towers of Enju- for once, the city had two great towers, hosting the guardians gods of the city. However, due to misuse of the natural resources and treating the gods for granted without offering them proper sacrifices and prayers, Silver Wing, the god of tides and storms, called upon an horrifying tempest before leaving Enju to the sea's depths. Whether intentional or not, the storm caused a lightning to strike the home of the second guardian god, Rainbow Wing, the god of harmony and light. The fire quickly took over the tower, and while most have escaped the shrine, three people were trapped inside, consumed by the flames. Eventually, the rain put down the fire, and both shrines were in ruin, leaving the people in tears. Feeling their regret and sorrow, Rainbow Wing returned, flying above the ruins and dying victims. In its sorrow, it invoked the divine right reserved only for it, transforming the burned people into vessels of its will- Lightning Howl to represent the divine strike which caused the ruin, Flame Bellow to represent the fire which consumed the building, and River Cry to represent the rain which brought an end to the disaster. Later, the blessed god has left, leaving the reincarnated divinities to govern its worshippers in its stead- yet the people were afraid of those strange beings, not fully comprehending the miracle that they have witnessed. Acting out of rage and sorrow, they banished the divine beasts, and ever since then the trio have roamed the riverlands, forever warry from humans. However, it is said that sometimes, they come across a person pure of all ill intent, upon which they bestow the fire given to them by the legendary Rainbow Wing- the spark of Exaltation.

          Regardless of whether the story is true or not, the Three Divine Guardians are constantly roaming Creation, being sighted across all eight Directions while resembling lions dogs of some sort, only much more powerful and imbued with elemental energy. The Divine Guardians rarely stay in the same place for more than a few second, being constantly on the move- yet when during their travels they come across a worthy candidate, they will bestow their Exaltation upon them. By doing so, the Guardian would vanish, seemingly gone by their diminishment. However, during the death of their Chosen, the Guardians would rise once more where their Chosen have fallen, starting to roam once more. Whether it is the same lion dog who gets absorbed and released as part of the Exaltation process, that the Guardian always sense when their Chosen falls and are quick to come and collect the Exaltation or that during each Exaltation the former Guardian dies only for a new one to be reborn with the death of their Chosen is unknown.

          General Traits:
          Evocations: all Chosen of the Divine Guardians are neutral with one color of jade (blue for the Chosen of Lightning, red for the Chosen of Flames and black for the Chosen of Rain), and are dissonant with all else.

          Sorcery & Necromancy: the Chosen of the Divine Guardian can learn First Circle of Sorcery. A rare few among their incarnation have shown the ability to learn First Circle Necromancy instead, yet never both.

          Martial Arts: the Chosen of the Divine Guardians are limited by the Terrestrial keyword.

          Ox Body Mode: Liminal

          Affinity: Attributes

          Chosen of Lightning Howl
          Roaming alongside thunderstorms and descending like lightning, Lightning Howl is a fast and powerful being. Its howls shake the land and cause the sky to tremble, its strikes scorch the land with an everlasting mark. Many have tried to hunt down the great god, yet all have either fell behind it or were consumed by the storm clouds it summons. As such, the Chosen of Lightning can:

          - fly upon storm clouds to quickly reach their destination
          - absorb lightning in order to heal their body
          - ignore the pain of wounds as long as their maintain their movement
          - shatter magical barriers with a howl
          - outspeed all of their enemies with quick attacks
          - grow fangs and talons who would electrify their enemies

          Chosen of Lightning: Exalted Advantages

          Constant Movement: the Chosen of Lightning's movement can not be impeded by any means, mundane or otherwise. They still can not pass through barriers or dramatic changes in terrain structure (like moving from land to water), but are unaffected by hazards which should slow their movement or stop it completely, such as slippery floor, sand or thorns.

          Lightning Reflexes: spend one mote. The Chosen of Lightning can take an additional Combat Movement action in their turn.

          Resonance: the Chosen of Lightning are resonant with blue jade

          Chosen of Flame Bellow
          A magnificent sight to behold, Flame Bellow is the incarnation of raw power. Leaving a path of burning flames wherever it goes, it is said that his barks cause volcanoes to erupt and ash to cover the skies, and any who stand in its way will be left burned to crisps. Still, it is of a noble character, and will not initiate battle unless forced to. As such, the Chosen of Flame can:

          - walk through flames unharmed
          - purify all diseases and curses with mystical fire
          - ignore the pain of wounds as long as they maintain their movement
          - shatter magical barriers with a howl
          - cause the land to heat up beneath their feet
          - engulf their fists with molten rock

          Chosen of Flame: Exalted Advantages

          Purifying Flame: the Chosen of Flame can not be infected with diseases or toxins, while targeting them with curses adds their Essence to the Difficulty

          Burning Steps: spend one mote. As long as the Chosen of Flame have moved during that turn, they are surrounded by a molten fire which adds one additional damage to their attacks.

          Resonance: the Chosen of Flame are resonant with red jade

          Chosen of Rain Cry
          Considered to be the wisest of the Divine Guardians, Rain Cry is surrounded by mysteries even compared to the rest of its kin. Many sorcerers claim that they have went through enlightenment simply by seeing it, and that sorcerous words surround Rain Cry as it passes through Creation, forever changing the Essence of the land. As such, the Chosen of Rain Cry can:

          - engulf themselves with a hypnotizing aurora
          - walk freely across water as if it was a solid surface
          - ignore the pain of wounds as long as they maintain their movement
          - shatter magical barriers with a howl​
          - purify polluted water from sickness or poison
          - learn sorcerous secrets beyond their regular limitations

          Chosen of Rain: Exalted Advantages

          Water Step: the Chosen of Water can walk across water as if it was a solid surface.

          Auroral Enlightenment: the Chosen of Rain Cry can access one automatic Terrestrial spell per Essence.

          Resonance: the Chosen of Rain are resonant with black jade

          That's it for the Legendary Beasts- next the Tower Duo and Celebi

              Finally, a bit of free time-

              The Tower Duo
              Composed of the masters for both the Wings and the Beasts, Ho Oh and Lugia represent a form of duality- Ho Oh is the Sun, Day, Fire, the Sky, and the Rainbow that comes after the storm, while Lugia represents the Moon, Night, Water, the Sea and the storm that covers the skies. Serving as the mascots of Gen 2, they are much more powerful compared to their trios, even though they seem to be on a lower level compared to Mewtwo, and are not directly related to any phenomena such as Mega Evolution. While they do have a mythology relating them to each other, the exact nature of the connection is somewhat unclear- both have roosted on the Towers of Ecruteak City, but their origin and exact sphere of influence are somewhat unclear, even though that they are often treated as each other's counterparts. With that taken into account, let's see what we get when we mix them into the setting.

              Considering the trios upon which they hold sway, the Chosen of the Twin Towers are very well integrated into the setting- Rainbow Wing and Silver Wing are dual, avian gods, messengers of the Sun and Luna correspondingly. While initially nomad gods who spread the blessings of their patrons across all of Creation, the people of Enju have gained their favor by constructing great temple towers in their honour, making offers to them and signing prayers through their ceremonial theater performances. Then, the hubris of the people grew, and Silver Wing took offense and brought a storm upon Enju, starting a fire which consumed the temple shrines. And then came Rainbow Wing and brought hope after the fire, turning the charred remain of its the faithful into gods through the divine blessing of its many coloured fire. After the event, Rainbow Wing have left Enju and returned to roam across Creation, bringing hope after cataclysms, while Silver Wing have elft to the West, where it found the struggling elementals of the Stormbound Isles and ended their wrath with a tremendous strike of its will, later teaching the locals the art of geomantic sorcery to keep them bound while it itself went to sleep beneath the waves. However, in the remains of their Towers, feathers which contain the divine power of the dual deities could be found, as well as others which were shed by them during their travels across Creation. Those feathers contain a sliver of their power, and it is said that if a person were to gain one of those sacred relics and present it before the corresponding god, than they would be granted with a miracle if they were to be found worthy- Exaltation, one befitting a Chosen of the fallen Towers.

              Chosen of Rainbows
              As the god of hope and prosperity after cataclysm, Rainbow Wing is associated with healing, purity and protection. It is surrounded by divine light and is said to banish the darkness from one's heart, yet also has a sway over the cycles of life and death, transforming rot into life. As such, the Chosen of Rainbows can:

              - heal any disease regardless of its origin
              - create rainbow barriers that guard against evil
              - draw power from sunlight to fight their enemies
              - accelerate their own healing process
              - create new life out of the souls of the deceased
              - burn down shadowlands with holy fire

              General Traits
              Evocations: the Chosen of Rainbow Wing are resonant with orichalcum and adamant, dissonant with moonsilver and are neutral with all other magical materials.

              Sorcery & Necromancy: the Chosen of Rainbows can access up to Second Circle of both Sorcery and Necromancy.

              Martial Arts: the Chosen of Rainbow Wing are not limited by the Terrestrial Keyword yet can't access the Mastery keyword or learn Sidereal Martial Arts.

              Ox Body: Sidereal

              Affinity: Abilities

              Chosen of Rainbows hold a certain sway towards Creatures of Darkness, yet mostly a passive one and protective in nature, as reflected in their Charms.

              Exalted Advantages

              Regeneration: the Chosen of Rainbows heal twice as fast as normal. By committing one mote, they can grant this ability to another person towards which they hold a positive intimacy, while losing it for themselves.

              Holy Form: commit one mote. Until the end of the scene and as long as the mote is committed, Creatures of Darkness can not directly touch the Chosen of Rainbows. They can, however, attack them indirectly or through weapons.

              Resonance: Chosen of Rainbows are resonant with orichalcum.

              Chosen of Tides
              As the god of waves and storms, Silver Wing is defined by being an untamed power of destruction. A movement from its wings is said to bring a storm that would wipe out a city, while a slight shift of its tail would could maelstroms in the sea and rise horrifying waves which would stroke the shores, demanding from it a great self control. As such, the Chosen of Tides can:

              - create blasts of wind to tear holes through their enemies
              - cause storm to brew above their heads
              - dive into the depths of the ocean without needing to breath
              - surround themselves with a occult barrier to ignore elemental damage
              - control the movements of water streams around themselves to raise tremendous waves
              - smite elementals with their bare hands

              General Traits
              Evocations: the Chosen of Tides are resonant with moonsilver and itzli, dissonant with orichalcum and neutral with all other magical materials.

              Sorcery & Necromancy: Chosen of Silver Wing can access up to Second Circle of Sorcery and First Circle Necromancy.

              Martial Arts: the Chosen of Tides are not limited by the Terrestrial keyword. They main gain access to the Mastery keyword through special cultivation of their Essence, generally by spending Pressure.

              Ox Body: Lunar

              Affinity: Attributes

              Castes: the Chosen of Tides have two Castes- the Silver Caste, which is has great physical endurance, and the Shadow Caste, that has wicked control over the human mind.

              Chosen of Tides have a special ability called Pressure, where by containing stress caused by damage to them they can increase their Pressure (which goes up to 10) to either reinforce their Charms, access Mastery keyword for Martial Arts or expand it all at once to cause horrifying damage around them.

              Exalted Advantages

              Pressure: by refusing to defend against an attack (meaning not applying any of their Defenses), the Chosen of Tides gains an amount of Pressure points equal to the damage their suffer. By spending one point of Pressure, they can add their Essence in dice as automatics successes in any attack involving a Martial Arts Style Charm. If their Pressure reaches 10, however, an explosion forms with the Chosen at the center, forcing anyone in medium range to roll Integrity + Fortitude or suffer from an amount of damage equal to the Chosen's Essence.

              Deep Dive: the Chosen of Tides does not need to breath when found underwater.

              Resonance: the Chosen of Tides are resonant with moonsilver.

              Celebi
              The mythical pokemon of Gen 2, Celebi is the lord of time and a protective forest spirit. Exampling the theme of time in the Gen 2 games, Celebi heavily uses time travel as its main shtick, dying and being reborn through such time shenanigans. As time travel is officially not a thing in Exalted, I do think we can play around with that concept, using the following-

              In the Northwest, a small shrine is found in the middle of the holly forest called Dilation Woods. This shrine, commonly known as the Crystal Shrine, is said to host an ancient god, a calendar deity known as Walker in the Woods. Many have sought the presence of the god, for it is said that Walker could remove any harm both physical and mental, rejuvenating old bodies and regenerating lost organs. Whether true or not, the people of Dilation Woods do follow a special calendar given to them by their god, which describes strange prophecies and warnings about what the future holds, and its prophets often decorate themselves with holly leaves as they divine the future. Dilation Woods are, by themselves, a strange place, for people could roam them for years without noticing the passage of time, often returning unchanged while the world has grown old in their absence. Among the many stories surrounding the forest, one seems to persist- that sometimes, when the prophecies decrees so, a volunteer must enter the timeless woods and make an offering of silver and gold before the shrine. Most of the times, nothing will happen, either due to them missing the exact moment required for the prophecy to work. On others, they are found unworthy, driven by greed and desire for power. Those volunteers are said to wither instantly, as the weight of time false upon them. Yet those who are found worthy would have Walker revealing itself before them, bestowing upon them treasures of gold, silver and crystal and pronouncing them its Chosen. Those people, will be granted the miracle of Exaltation.

              Chosen of Calendar
              A mysterious and auspicious god, Walker in the Woods rules over the passage of time and the sanctity of events. As such, it holds a strange relation with Fate, even though one which is much more physical and expressed directly through its effect upon the world, such as maintaining the cycles of day and night and passage of seasons. As such, the Chosen of Calendar can:

              - manipulate an enemy's perception of time
              - cause the ground sprout plants or quickly wither
              - walk in the memories of others as worlds of their own
              - rewrite a strike so it have never happened, ignoring its damage
              - decree that their opponent must fall, causing the world to turn against them
              - accelerate the growth of seeds to bind their target

              General Traits
              Evocations: the Chosen of Calendar are resonant with starmetal and green jade, and neutral with all other magical materials.

              Sorcery & Necromancy: the Chosen of Calendar can access the First Circle of both Sorcery and Necromancy

              Martial Arts: the Chosen of Calendar are not limited by the Terrestrial keyword, and can access the Mastery keyword by learning all of the Charms in a certain Style while adopting its form. They can also learn Sidereal Martial Arts under a tutor.

              Ox Body: Sidereal

              Affinity: Essence

              Exalted Advantages

              Time Wrap: commit one mote for a scene. For the duration, all damage and effects caused to the Chosen of Calendar are rejected from reality, returning them to their state right before the scene be them beneficial or harmful effects. At the end of the scene, all effects return alongside any additional ones caused in the meantime.

              Omen Reading: once per session, the Chosen of calendar may consult the world around them, reading the omens between the moments as the tune into Fate. They can ask one question per dot of Essence about the process of destiny and its plans. Those questions can not include entities considered as Enemies of Fate.

              Resonance: the Chosen of Calendar are resonant with starmetal and green jade.
