    And the fact that it's a spirit realm means the "laws" of the Underworld don't have to be the same as Creation. Hell, they don't even have to be the same from one area to another. There might be taboos that aren't just social but help feed into keeping the world stable. Taboos that are daily ritual of some kind, not unlike a constant prayer that holds their land together. You can do a lot of very interesting things with that. It could create dangers that aren't some beast, a lack of resources, or harsh environment. And if you are ignoring it, you are endangering lives.

                            Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
                            Every ghost is an essence user and there are potentially a lot more ghosts than there are people in Creation (if 5% of the people since the Usurpation are still ghosts in the Underworld, the number of ghosts in Creation would be tenfold the number of people in Creation). While the vast majority of them probably do not have the initiative or the desire to be a practitioner of supernatural martial arts, even if 0.01% of ghosts are practitioners of supernatural martial arts, that would mean that there would be around 400,000 ghostly practitioners of the supernatural martial arts. In addition, the remainder of the ghosts could be dangerous in their own right.
                            I assume, and would find the setting to be consistent with the idea, that the majority of spirits of all types only have fairly minor and personally useful powers.

                            I do this because I find priorities that amount to "make enormous workforces of spirits" to kind of focus games away from human interests.

                            The idea of somebody having an army of ghosts at their beck and call has its values, but I think it should be distinctly ghostly; something you could direct to haunt and blight and possess. Reducing it down to "they can use Essence, so teach them martial arts and have them make Artifacts" is rather flavourless.
