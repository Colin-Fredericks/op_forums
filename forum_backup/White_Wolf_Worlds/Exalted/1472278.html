    Awakened Anima (Homebrew Sorcerous Initiation + Many, Many Evocations)

    01-11-2022, 07:07 PM
    So I had this basic idea back in 2ed days, but 3ed Sorcerous Initiations helped me flesh it out. The Initiation itself is one thing, an attached Merit grants sorcerer's a weapon, and Exalted Sorcerers can unlock the proposed Evocations from it based on their anima.

    The basic premise of the evocations is that each caste has three sets of Evocations, each based on one of their three anima abilities (Solars have three per set at Ess 1, 2 & 3, Dragonbloods have two since they start at Essence 2 by default). The Ess 4 Evocation is a way of combining these into a power that is relevant to that caste, and the E5 charm is generic, but reflects each Caste's own uniqueness. But these are all just ideas and can be separated from the actual Initiation itself, which I like just fine on its own.

    Note: I am not good especially at Exalted balancing so some of this will be useless and/or broken. But I wanted to get it down in writing.

    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

    Exalted 3rd Edition, p24
    Anima: All living things are surrounded by a colorless, invisible aura called anima. When one of the Exalted uses Essence, the power often spills into her anima, making it visible.
    As a result, an Exalt who expends a great deal of power is usually engulfed by a display of anima that can range from a soft glow to a pillar of heatless ﬂame bright enough to see for miles.


    Awakened Anima (Sorcerous Initiation)

    Every being in Creation, from the lowest slave to the highest Queen is encircled by a permanent aura emanating from their soul. Unseen and unfelt, most will never even know it exists. But in the high first age, powerful mystics carved arcane sigils and tattooed essence-enhancing mudras into their animas, shaping the essence that passes through it and performing miracles. These secrets have survived, passed down from teacher to student across the ages, across both Creation and Heaven.

    Any sorcerer who use these Shaping rituals causes their anima to glow in the same way as an Exalt using peripheral essence. For any Sorcerer with one of these Shaping Rituals, taking a single Shaping (or Distort/Counterspell) action that accrues 5+ successes raises her anima banner one level; from Dim to Glowing to Burning to Bonfire.

    A note on non-Exalted Sorcerers:
    Non-Exalted humans who use these rituals will, by default, raise a silent banner of pale white light. The animas of some Spirits, Godbloods and stranger beings might display other colours and manifestations, though none will have a caste mark, nor an iconic display.


    Shaping Rituals

    Essence Gathering Approach
    A Sorcerer with an Essence pool may spend a scene in meditation to commit ten motes to her anima. Once she begins shaping a spell, she may draw on the power she’s invested, contributing up to (Essence + 2) sorcerous motes towards the spell until the full commitment of motes has been converted, or the spell is cast (or countered). Committed motes are converted to sorcerous motes on a one-to-one basis normally, but are converted into two sorcerous motes when casting the sorcerer’s control spell

    Tearing the Tapestry
    Her anima shining like a divine mirror, the Sorcerer reverses the spells of her enemies. When she makes a Counterspell or Distortion attempt she adds 1 (Glowing), 2 (Burning) or 3 (Bonfire) successes to the attempt based on her current anima level. Once a scene per spell, unless she knows the spell she is opposing.

    Aura of Magic
    Her anima is the manifestation of the Sorcerer’s soul. This energy vibrates sympathetically when she embraces life and rejects death. If the Sorcerer has rolled Join Battle but not caused health or initiative damage to a living being, she adds (Essence) dice to her Shape Sorcery actions. The requirement of this ritual includes Spirits and animals, but not the Dead (who live no longer) nor Fae (which were never truly alive).


    Other Benefits

    Shinmaic Nimbus (Merit ● - ●●●●●): The Sorcerer arranges the symbols and glyphs in her anima just so, revealing their new pattern for all to see as essence spills into it. Whilst her anima is visible, the Sorcerer gains mutation dots equal to her dots in this merit. When her anima increases a level she decides whether to manifest all or none of these mutations, but cannot selectively choose between mutations. These transformations are part of her anima banner and appear in it’s colour and style. Once per story she may undertake occult research (Appearance+Occult, dif 3) and change the mutations that she manifests.

    Weaving of Wonders (Merit ●●●): The arcane sigils scribed into the Sorcerer’s anima function as sorcerous infrastructure, granting +1 Means. Furthermore, by using her instinctive familiarity with her anima as a tool to shape the essence of Creation, she is able to perform sorcerous workings more quickly than usual - though risking her working’s collapse in her expedience:
    Reduce Terminus by 1 - each interval takes 1 day - increase base difficulty by 1
    Reduce Terminus by 2 - each interval takes 12 hours - increase base difficulty by 2
    Reduce Terminus by 3 - each interval takes 1 hour - increase base difficulty by 2
    Reduce Terminus by 4 - each interval takes 30 minutes - increase base difficulty by 2
    Reduce Terminus by 5 - each interval takes 1 minute - increase base difficulty by 3
    Reduce Terminus by 6 - each interval takes 30 seconds - increase base difficulty by 3

    Lance of the Sorcerer-Queen (Merit ●●●●): Channelling her anima outward, the Sorcerer unleashes a flash of concentrated magic that strikes a target with raw force. Rolling (Perception+Occult), this is treated as a mundane archery weapon with the Bashing, Mounted, One-Handed and Special tags. It cannot be disarmed by any means and does not add the user’s Strength to Withering damage. Additionally;
    at Dim or Glowing level, her anima has Light weapon traits and the Archery (Short) tag
    at Burning level, her anima has Medium weapon traits and the Archery (Medium) tag
    at Bonfire level, her anima has Heavy weapon traits and the Archery (Long) tag

    A Sorcerer with an essence pool may commit 5 motes to her anima to attune to it and treat it as an artifact archery weapon instead, using the stats of the appropriate weight category. She may also awaken Evocations in her anima - example evocation trees are below.

    Lance of the Sorcerer-Queen
    Attunement: 5m
    Type: Light / Medium / Heavy
    Tags: Archery (Short / Medium / Long), Bashing, Mounted, Special
    Hearthstone Slots: 0
    Era: Various (first recorded during the Warmage Crusades)
    Evocations of Lance of the Sorcerer-Queen: The Lance’s basic evocations enhance its Sorcerer’s attacks and let her mould her anima. The animas of the Chosen however are shaped by their caste or aspect. They may also awaken evocations which build on their inherent anima powers, eventually blending them to transform the Exalted Sorcerer into a living representation of their innate glory. Upon attunement, the Sorcerer unlocks any Essence 1 evocation for which she qualifies without cost.

    Basic Evocations - Any Sorcerer with an Essence Pool
    Arcane Flash
    Cost: 3m+; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Withering-only
    Duration: Instant
    Pre-requisites: None
    The wise know when flight is preferable to fight. The Sorcerer pays three motes to make a withering attack dazzlingly bright; a target damaged by the attack is blinded for 1 round per 3 motes spent. The Sorcerer does not gain the target's lost initiative but still gains 1i for landing a successful withering attack.

    Wicked Thrust Technique
    Cost: 3m or 6m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Pre-requisites: None
    For 3 motes, the Sorcerer may grant a single decisive attack with her Lance the Lethal and Smashing tags. Alternatively, for 6 motes, she creates a burst of essence which inflicts bashing damage against her target and all those within short range of him, dividing her initiative amongst them equally. She is reset to base initiative as long as at least one target suffers damage. Battlegroups reduce their Defence by 1 when struck by the second version.

    Ephemeral Shield Stance
    Cost: 1m+, 1WP; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Dual
    Duration: Instant
    Pre-requisites: None
    Pouring essence into her anima, the Sorcerer momentarily gives it substance. She gains magical Soak on a 1:1 basis for each mote spent against one withering attack, up to (Essence) motes. Alternatively, she gains 1 Hardness for every two motes spent against one Decisive attack, same maximum. Soak stacks with natural / armour soak but not other magic. Hardness does not stack.

    Cloak of Pearls and Diamonds
    Cost: 3m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Mute, Stackable
    Duration: Indefinite
    Pre-requisites: Ephemeral Shield Stance
    The Sorcerer commits the motes and creates a pocket of tangible essence within her anima. For as long as the motes remain committed, this pocket functions as a hearthstone socket. The Sorcerer may create up to (Essence) such sockets. Socketed hearthstones orbit closely around the sorcerer; a difficulty 4 Unsocket Gambit can shatter a pocket, ending its commitment and causing the hearthstone to fall undamaged to the ground.

    Sword of Creation Emulation
    Cost: 3a, 1WP; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Aggravated, Decisive-Only,
    Duration: One Scene
    Pre-requisites: Arcane Flash, Wicked Thrust Technique
    This Evocation treats the Sorcerer’s lance as cold iron for the scene, allowing her to shred the unearthly bodies of the Wyld-born with ease. All of her lance attacks deal aggravated damage to Fae for the scene.

      Solar Evocations - The Solar’s Lance is treated as an orichalcum weapon for any relevant purposes
      Solar Exalted - Dawn Caste

      At bonfire/iconic anima display, the Dawn adds half her Essence score (round up) to her base Initiative upon resetting to base after a successful decisive attack.

      Golden Within As Without
      Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Decisive-only, (Dawn Caste-only)
      Duration: Instant
      Pre-requisites: None
      Using this Evocation when she resets to base initiative, the Dawn may add (Essence/2, round up) to her base initiative regardless of her anima level. She adds a further +1 when at bonfire level.

      Flawless Warrior’s Stance
      Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 2
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: Dual
      Duration: Permanent
      Pre-requisites: Golden Within As Without
      The Dawn stands unphased in the direst of circumstances. Opponents no longer gain an automatic 1i for landing a successful attack against her.

      Escalating Force Method
      Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 3
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: Decisive-only, Perilous
      Duration: One Scene or until ended
      Pre-requisites: Flawless Warrior’s Stance
      The Dawn activates this charm when rolling Join Battle or resetting to base initiative. She adds an additional +1 to her base initiative each time she subsequently resets to it following a successful decisive attack. These increases stack for the rest of the scene, but if a reset was forced upon her by an opponent or by entering Initiative Crash, the charm ends and her base initiative returns to normal.

      For 10 motes, all of the Dawn’s combat and movement Charms with outstanding reset conditions are automatically reset. This effect can only be used once per day, becoming available again at sunrise.

      Sharing the Sun
      Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: Mute, (Dawn Caste-only)
      Duration: Instant
      Pre-requisites: None
      When the Dawn resets her combat and movement charms with her anima, she may share this benefit with (Essence/2) allies within:
      Short range if her anima is at Dim or Glowing level;
      Medium range if it is at Burning level; or
      Long range if it is at Bonfire level.

      Daybreak Waking Technique
      Cost: 6i; Mins: Essence 2
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: -
      Duration: Instant
      Pre-requisites: Sharing the Sun
      When the Dawn resets her combat and movement charms with her anima, she is treated as having had a full night’s rest. Her fatigue and onslaught penalties drops to zero and she regains a point of temporary willpower, up to her maximum.

      First Light Benediction
      Cost: 4m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 3
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: -
      Duration: Instant
      Pre-requisites: Daybreak Waking Technique
      As shadows recede, the Dawn is reborn. When she resets her combat and movement charms with her anima, she also heals up to (Essence) damaged bashing or lethal health levels and adds up to (Essence+Willpower) motes to her mote pools (peripheral first). This benefit is not shared when using Sharing The Sun.

      The Dawn Caste inspires terror in her foes. She adds half her Essence score (round up) in dice to all intimidating social influence. She may also intimidate targets which do not feel fear, such as automatons, golems, and certain undead.

      Bronze Tiger’s Roar
      Cost: 6m; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: Psyche, (Dawn Caste-only)
      Duration: Instant
      Pre-requisites: None
      A foe’s soul quivers under the Dawn’s gaze. When the Solar makes a Threaten action the target is treated as having a Minor Tie of Submissive Terror towards her. He must enter a decision point and pay the higher of (Solar’s Essence or 2) Willpower to resist this influence.

      Terrifying Brilliance Prana
      Cost: 3m, 3i; Mins: Essence 2
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: Decisive-only
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: Bronze Tiger’s Roar
      So terrifying is the Dawn that opposing forces lose their composure entirely. A battlegroup successfully struck by her reduces their Drill score by one level - from Elite, to Average to Poor for the scene. Units which already have Poor drill must immediately make a Rout check.

      All-Encompassing Commander Stance
      Cost: 9m; Mins: Essence 3
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: -
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: Terrifying Brilliance Prana
      The Dawn is leadership personified. When an opposing battlegroup fails a Rout check, the Solar makes a special Command roll against the Battlegroup’s previous Resolve. On a success, she barks orders to the fleeing force and they are too intimidated to refuse her. They reform at their previous size-1, with Poor Drill and obey the Solar for the remainder of the scene, or until she fails to make a successful Command roll for them.

      Heaven’s Unending Blade Practice
      Cost: 1WP; Mins: Essence 4
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Decisive-only
      Duration: Until ended
      Pre-requisites: All-Encompassing Commander Stance, Escalating Force Method, First Light Benediction
      Nothing stops a Dawn in pursuit of victory. When she makes a successful decisive attack, this evocation allows her to move up to one range band and make a decisive attack against another non-trivial target using the same initiative roll. This repeats until there are no further valid targets in range or she can move no further, at which point she resets to base initiative. She cannot attack the same target more than once with this effect, but battlegroups may be targeted up to (Size) times.

      Avatar of the Ascending Sun
      Cost: 10m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 5
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Dual, Perilous, Uniform
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: Heaven’s Unending Blade Practice
      The Dawn is the embodiment of the Sun's facet her caste represents, her Anima rising to iconic level and unable to subside or be shed until the end of the scene. Whilst this Evocation is active, she awakens the power from the list below that matches her Supernal ability and (Essence-3) others:
      Archery: The Dawn can make archery attacks at extreme range (up to (Essence) range bands). She must still aim to do so beyond Short range.
      Awareness: The Dawn may substitute any sense for another without penalty. She can hear colours and taste conversations.
      Brawl: The Dawn’s unarmed attacks gain the balanced, chopping, lethal, piercing, smashing and two-handed tags. She may parry lethal damage without other magic.
      Dodge: The Dawn may apply her Dodge defence against an undodgeable attack for 1WP.
      Martial Arts: When the Dawn uses a form charm, she may reflexively activate a second form charm she knows and benefit from both simultaneously.
      Melee: The Dawn may apply her Parry defence against an unblockable attack for 1WP.
      Resistance: The Dawn converts half of Aggravated damage to Lethal, and all other Lethal damage to Bashing. Damage still rolls over.
      Thrown: The Dawn can make thrown attacks at extreme range (up to (Essence) range bands). She must still aim to do so beyond Short range.
      War: Battlegroups the Dawn commands possess Might +1 (max 2).

      Additionally, she adds (Essence/2, round down) non-charm dice to any action using her Supernal ability.


      Solar Exalted - Zenith Caste


      For one mote, the Zenith’s anima leaps from her hand to a corpse she’s touching, igniting it in Solar flame. This power incinerates the corpse and ensures the soul of the deceased will not return as a hungry ghost. In addition, the Zenith may perceive the strongest Intimacies of the deceased and may choose to accept them. When she is ready, she may pay one mote, touching a target of those Intimacies to pass on feelings of peace, love, and assurance, shedding them in the process. She may also transfer them to an object, allowing it to convey comfort or a sense of duty to the subject. Conversely, she may touch someone who caused the deceased to suffer, paying one mote and rolling ([A social Attribute] + Presence) with three automatic non-Charm successes against their Resolve to transfer pain suffered by the deceased back on the one who caused it. The Storyteller is free to dictate the length and weight of such attrition.

      Dutiful Shepherd’s Grace
      Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: - (Zenith Caste-only)
      Duration: Permanent
      Pre-requisites: None
      When the Zenith passes on positive feelings to someone or an object after accepting someone’s intimacies, she regains a point of temporary Willpower. When she successfully passes on negative feelings with this anima power, she instead heals (Essence) bashing or lethal health levels.

      Solar Oneiromancy Method
      Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 2
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Stackable
      Duration: One night's sleep
      Pre-requisites: Dutiful Shepherd’s Grace
      In sleep, the Zenith shares her dreams with those whose intimacies she carries in her anima. She may learn things they knew in life related to the intimacies she carries, mechanically represented by asking the ST a question on the relevant topic. She may ask as many questions during a night as she wishes and has motes to pay to activate this Evocation. However, each intimacy may only be questioned once, and if all intimacies are questioned that person’s intimacies are released, meaning they can never be passed on.

      Strength of Lethe Invocation
      Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 3
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: -
      Duration: Permanent (Instant)
      Pre-requisites: Solar Oneiromancy Method
      The emotions the Zenith carries instinctively work to protect her, to ensure she may continue and pass them on. Once per session, when the Zenith would gain Limit by opposing a Major or Defining intimacy, she may instead substitute an appropriate Major or Defining intimacy from someone whose intimacies she has accepted, to negate the Limit roll. The Zenith does not know about the Great Curse, but deep down she feels a swell of gratitude and gains the Minor Principle “I must pass on that person’s feelings” which never degrades until she successfully does so.

      For 10 motes, one Willpower, the Zenith channels her Essence into an aura of divine retribution, surging around an attack. This power supplements a successful decisive attack against a creature of darkness, preventing the Solar’s Initiative from returning to its base value after the attack. When the Zenith’s anima is at bonfire, reduce the cost of this power by five motes and ignore the Willpower. This power may only be used once per day, resetting when the sun is at its zenith.

      Purge the Unclean Prana
      Cost: 5m, 1a; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: Decisive-only, Perilous, (Zenith Caste-only)
      Duration: Instant
      Pre-requisites: None
      This Evocation allows the Zenith to make further decisive attacks against nn-trivial Creatures of Darkness without returning to base initiative. The Zenith may not attack a target already affected by this anima power in the scene, but battlegroups composed mostly of Creatures of Darkness may be targeted up to (Size) times.

      Bulwark Against Sin Stance
      Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 2
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: -
      Duration: Permanent
      Pre-requisites: Purge the Unclean Prana
      When her anima is at bonfire level, Creatures of Darkness within close range of the Zenith are subject to the rules of Anima Flux. She rolls one die of withering damage at the start of each turn, or one die of decisive lethal damage to crashed and trivial opponents.

      Karma Manifesting Method
      Cost: 6i, 1WP; Mins: Essence 3
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Aggravated, Decisive-only
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: Bulwark Against Sin Stance
      Whilst this Evocation is active, the Zenith’s Lance attacks deal aggravated damage to Creatures of Darkness with Essence equal to or less than hers. Those with higher Essence scores instead suffer lethal damage.

      For seven motes, the Zenith may order a dematerialized spirit to manifest using a (Charisma + Presence) persuade action with (Essence) automatic successes. All spirits inherently recognize the Unconquered Sun’s authority in the priest-kings and are compelled as if by a Defining Intimacy, but may still have Defining Ties or Principles which counter this advantage. If successful, the Zenith’s very command draws the spirit into the material world, so that it does not need to pay the cost to materialize.

      Ephemera Shedding Shout
      Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: - (Zenith Caste-only)
      Duration: Permanent
      Pre-requisites: None
      This Evocation allows the Zenith to exercise their authority in both directions. She may now pay the motes and roll to command a spirit to dematerialise as well as ordering them to appear.

      Servant of the Sun Prana
      Cost: 3m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 2
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Psyche
      Duration: (Essence) rounds
      Pre-requisites: Ephemera Shedding Shout
      When the Zenith successfully commands a spirit to materialise, she may use this evocation to have it retain the Defining Intimacy compelling it to obey her for (Essence) rounds. During this time, the Zenith may again leverage her authority manifested in this Intimacy to use social influence on the spirit.

      Mudra of Banishment
      Cost: 6m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 3
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: -
      Duration: Instant
      Pre-requisites: Servant of the Sun Prana
      Drawing the sigil of The Master Key in her anima, the Zenith locks a spirit out of Creation. Using this evocation against a materialised spirit she rolls (Charisma+Performance) against the spirit’s Resolve. If successful, the spirit is banished to its sanctum; if it lacks one it is sent to the most appropriate other realm (Gods to Yu Shan, Demons to Malfeas, Ghosts to the Underworld, Elementals to the sanctum of the local Censor.)

      Bellow of the Golden Bull
      Cost: 6i, 1a; Mins: Essence 4
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Dual
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: Karma Manifesting Technique, Mudra of Banishment, Strength of Lethe Invocation
      Raising her arms high, the Zenith calls out a prayer to Heaven and brings down judgement upon the wicked. Her anima swirls outward, taking the form of a battlegroup of golden warriors. This battlegroup uses the stats of Elite troops with Elite Drill, Might 1 and is (Essence) size. The battlegroup never routs and automatically succeeds such checks. If facing primarily Creatures of Darkness they have Might 2. If opposing the target of a carried Defining negative intimacy, the battlegroup gains +1 Size.

      Avatar of the Resplendent Sun
      Cost: 10m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 5
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Perilous, Psyche, Uniform
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: Bellow of the Golden Bull
      The Zenith is the embodiment of the Sun's facet her caste represents, her Anima rising to iconic level and unable to subside or be shed until the end of the scene. Whilst this Evocation is active, she awakens the power from the list below that matches her Supernal ability and (Essence-3) others:
      Athletics: The Zenith adds her (Essence) to her Strength when determining what Feats of Strength and Destruction she can undertake.
      Integrity: The Zenith is immune to Psyche keyword charms.
      Performance: Those within Long range of the Zenith are inspired by her perfect elegance, gaining (Zenith’s Essence/2) additional motes at the end of the round.
      Lore: The Zenith may introduce a single fact that is not internally consistent, but remains a fact until the end of the scene. Once per story.
      Presence: Any opponent within Long range of the Zenith must move a range band towards her every round unless they roll (Willpower), difficulty (Zenith’s Essence).
      Resistance: The Zenith converts half of Aggravated damage to Lethal, and all other Lethal damage to Bashing. Damage still rolls over.
      Survival: The Zenith is immune to environmental hazards and takes no penalties relating to weather or terrain.
      War: Battlegroups the Zenith commands possess Might +1 (max 2).

      Additionally, she adds (Essence/2, round down) non-charm dice to any action using her Supernal ability.


      Solar Exalted - Twilight Caste


      For five motes, the Twilight projects an aura of pure force, reflexively gaining five Hardness for one turn. At the bonfire/iconic level, this power activates itself automatically at no cost. This effect doesn’t stack with other magic that raises Hardness, but it can be used during Initiative Crash.

      Protecting Heaven’s Lamp
      Cost: 5i; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: - (Twilight Caste-only)
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: None
      For the remainder of the scene the cost to trigger The Twilight’s aura of force is now (5 - Essence) motes. In addition to Hardness it also provides 5 bonus Soak whilst this evocation is active; which stacks with natural soak, but not armour. She may activate this when invoking this anima power or whilst she is at bonfire anima.

      Impenetrable Wall of Adamant
      Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 2
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: -
      Duration: Permanent
      Pre-requisites: Protecting Heaven's Lamp
      Whilst it's pre-requisite is active, the Hardness and Soak of the Twilight's anima increase by (Essence).

      Shield of Copper Flame
      Cost: 8m; Mins: Essence 3
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Perilous, Stackable
      Duration: One Scene
      Pre-requisites: Impenetrable Wall of Adamant
      The Twilight may extend the Hardness of her anima to (Essence) allies within medium range, who then activate it for the same mote cost as her or gain it automatically whilst she is at Bonfire level, all glowing with hues of her anima. Alternatively she may extend this protection to a Battle Group she commands with a Size of no more than her (Essence) within Medium range.

      For 10 motes, one Willpower, the Twilight’s anima flares blue and white and consumes her body over the course of the round. On her next turn, she vanishes into her anima and ceases to exist. If the Exalt moves or suffers knockdown before the action is completed, it fails and the effect is wasted. After using this power, the Twilight will not reappear until the following sunset. When she does, it will be at a place of power within 10 miles of her vanishing point, chosen by the Storyteller. She might appear at a temple, a demesne, a major crossroads, the door of a god’s sanctum, the precise center of a city, etc.

      Sun’s Well-Trodden Path
      Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 1
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: Perilous, (Twilight Caste-only)
