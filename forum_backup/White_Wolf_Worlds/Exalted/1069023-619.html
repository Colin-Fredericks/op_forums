        I had a conversation with my players about lying (in Ex3, not real life) earlier, and it left me with questions. It seems there are at least three ways to pick up on a lie...

        Challenge a Fact lets you shoot down factually-incorrect statements through the power of being a smartypants, but presumably (even with Lore 3+) it shouldn't be able to touch lies based on information a character couldn't know. (Like if you're the first Creation-born person through the Seal of Eight Divinities and the first mad Blightborn you bump into claims to be the King of Gulak, Lore 3 wouldn't let you call bullshit, because who are you to know he isn't?)

        I assume that a successful Read Intentions would let you pick up that someone is lying to you? (Though, unlike Challenge A Fact, it by itself couldn't establish the truth, only that someone is speaking with the intent to mislead you?)

        The Charm Judge's Ear allows lie-detection through Investigation, but none of the mundane uses of Investigation detailed in the book seem to cover lie-detection. As I read it, Profile Character indicates a Sherlock-style cold reading, not an interrogation. Can the Investigation Ability only uncover lies with an appropriate Charm or through the discovery of contradictory evidence, or does it have any mundane application analogous to Judge's Ear's supernatural application?

        The specific question that sparked all this was our social character's player wondering if there was anything they could roll in order to lie well. In investigating that, I noted that all of the mechanics around lies in Ex3 seem to be predicated on actively rolling to detect falsehood against passive difficulties. Challenge A Fact doesn't even seem to leave room for the liar to defend against it. I suppose the act of lying is generally just a stunt as part of whatever social action the lie is meant to further?

        (A couple of Larceny Charms, mostly Clever Bandit's Rook, also incorporate a lying theme, but, analogous to Judge's Ear bringing lie-detection into Investigation, this seems to only be present in Charms, not in baseline mundane application of the Ability.)

Alibaba, Ebay, and Amazon are proof enough that the mercantilism aspects map pretty well, too. Charms like Woe-Capturing Web and Omen-Spawning Beast match nothing so much as counter-hacking operations.
