                              06-02-2020, 12:13 AM
                              So, I won't make a full legend for the map as it stands because early in the process of making it I decided (it seemed like a good idea at the time) to represent the capitals of all countries with full write-ups with symbols of those countries, even if I had to make up symbols for them in order to do so, with the result that there are a lot of one-off symbols floating. I'm planning to go back at some point and change the symbol scheme, but in the meantime the symbols that show up in more than one place are:

                              White castle: human settlement
                              White castle with broken towers: human ruins
                              Blue castle: person-of-the-air settlement
                              Black castle: person-of-the-sea settlement
                              Grey cog: mountainfolk settlement
                              Green butterfly: Fey freehold
                              Bigger butterfly in other colour: Major fey court
                              Orange trapezium: mixed-species dragon-king settlement
                              Trapezium in other colour: single-species dragon-king settlement
                              Flatter, inverted trapezium: Realm navy fleet home port
                              Orange trapezium with broken top: dragon-king ruins
                              Autochthonian sodality symbol: alchemical manse
                              Silver crescent: lunar dominion
                              Fuzzy-edged black circle: shadowland
                              Hard-edged black circle with symbol inside: deathlord's stronghold
                              Purple circle with silver crescent along left edge and animal inside: changing moon dominion
                              Dawn caste symbol in saffron with symbol on lavender centre: dawn's stronghold
                              Great house mon: great house primary centre of power
                              Blue gate with grey frame: gate of Yu-Shan
                              Grey gate flanked by grey pillars: divine sanctum
                              My personal design for the seal of the guild (a light grey circle containing a design consisting of a ship, a wagon, two curved arrows forming circle and a number of small circles representing coins): guild trade post
                              Blue circle with silver border: no-moon dominion
                              Gold gauntlet in red circle: Cult of the Illuminated training camp
                              Grey animal on white circle: full moon dominion
                              Element symbol on the Caul: shrine city
                              Plant: Bureau of Nature directional headquarters
                              Celestial symbol with terrestrial symbol on centre: divine bureau directional headquarters
                              Down-pointing pentagon with circular symbol in centre: Shogunate remnants independent of Realm
                              Pentagram with dragon symbols at points: Immaculate holy site
                              Also, if there's an area where lots of labels have a particular unusual colour it probably means all the places they label are controlled by the same state, stand-alone green text typically denotes Fae states, stand-alone silver text typically denotes lunar dominions.

                              but as I said, there's also lots of inconsistent or one-off symbols that you'll just need to figure out what mean by their labels. I realise I should probably make things more regular.
                              Also, at this point no location has more than one symbol, even though there are plenty of places that have more than one of those features (e.g. Chiaroscuro is, in 2E, a lunar dominion with a directional bureau headquarters). Figuring out a solution for this is a major reason I haven't yet started on a more consistent map.

                                  06-02-2020, 05:28 AM
                                  Yeah, I definitely encourage you to dramatically simplify the symbols. Let me throw a few ideas at you (these are off the top of my head, and in no particular order):
                                  • One symbol for all lunar dominions. Different symbols for Lunar castes needlessly complicates the map, and if it's important in your game, you can look it up.
                                  • The main political divide in Creation is Realm vs. non-Realm; make the castle icon one color for actual Realm cities, a shade of the same color for Threshold (Realm tributary) settlements (dark red and bright red?), and a different color for independent settlements.
                                  • Features like Cult of the Illuminated camps, Guild trading posts, etc. can be indicated by smaller icons next to, above, or below a settlement icon. Dynastic House mons can be represented the same way.

                                  Basically, simplify and standardize your iconography.
