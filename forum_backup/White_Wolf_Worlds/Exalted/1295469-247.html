    Originally posted by autXautY View Post
    Can the cat latent ability, Stalking Shadow Hunter, allow a weapon wielded with Last Warrior's Unity deal full damage to larger enemies?
    Yeah.

    In general, do animal merits ad special attacks that give a bonus to attacks apply when an animal somehow attacks with a weapon other than that animal's innate weapons (Ie. Last Warrior's Unity, Burning Name, Death of Obsidian Butterflies)?
    Does it make sense for what that merit or attack represents? A rhino's big scary horn isn't going to improve Death of Obsidian Butterflies.

    Are there any practical limitations on using larger weapons with smaller forms with Last Warrior's Unity? For the example of a scorpion with a grand daiklave, should I be imagining a 2-inch long scorpion somehow carrying a 6 foot long sword, or a 2-inch scorpion with a 2-inch sword, or a 6-foot scorpion with a 6-ft sword?
    The weapon scales down and reshapes itself to a form that makes sense proportionately for your animal shape.
