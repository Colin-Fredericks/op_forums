            Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
            My Creation is pretty much set in the default setting with a bit more of the blank edges of the map filled in... but sure.

            In my Creation there are secret tunnels built just before the War of the Gods. Some by the gods for their Exalted champions. Some by other beings for other purposes.

            The game is mostly set East of Jiara around a First Age city/ruin called Urza Arcus amidst the broken wonders of the Solars (including a mystic portal to other dimensions -- certainly The Underworld and Malfeas and perhaps others).

            Thunderbirds are still found in the West, but unusually this Eastern city has one.

            Lookshyans, being Shogunate remnants, speak High Realm (stylized with Japanese honorifics and the occasional loan word).

            Uh... there's a manse that lets people pull artifacts out of their soul.

            God-blooded vary tremendously in power. Estoric means exist for mortals to gain power. One of the main antagonists is a god-blood who became a Deathknight after a (well, several) botched attempt(s) to gain power -- whether this means god-blooded can increase their power through estoric ritual (and whether Exalted can) is thus left deliberately ambiguous (I'm leaning "no", And The Gift of Merciful Silence just got (un)lucky).

            High Realm is stylized as English. River Speak is stylized as French. Old Realm is generally stylized as Xhosa, Latin, Greek and any other language I might care to throw in, but demonic speach is stylized as cockney/KSBD English (for 1CDs) or late Elizabthian (2CDs). Might stylize Low Realm as German. This generally means funny accents and the odd loan word when RPing, it's not an in-universe conceit. Although...

            And I think that's about it. Throne still empty, no civil war yet. Haven't killed off any Elemental Dragons or anything like that.
            Also:

            Cynis is the child of the Empress and Tepet.

            This is important because Tepet Igan is from a Cynis bloodline that married back into Tepet, causing some confusion over whether V'neef's husband is a Tepet or "one of the wealthier and less decadent Cynis".

            Also Ragara Bhagwei is the child of Cynis and Ragara, but almost no-one knows that.

            Also the Realm is responsible for the trade war between Gem and Paragon.

            Hi, I'm JohnDoe244. My posts represent my opinions, not facts.

                                                Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post

                                                Cynis was biological daughter of the Empress, and I should be this grossed out, right?
                                                Oh yes.

                                                Cynis's father (and/or second mother) is unstated. I like Tepet for her father.

                                                Ragara's father is Rawar. So she's Ragara's half-sister.

                                                And Bhagwei isn't part of his mother's House. So there's a weird power dynamic there as well as the casual incest.

                                                Plus, Ragara is (according to my headcanon, at least) like 119 years older than Cynis (if Cynis is the daughter of Tepet, then her birth coinciding with the Empress stripping the Shogunate gens of their special privilidges makes sense -- it also makes sense as to why House Cynis isn't a thing until over 116 years after Cynis's adult son graduates the Versino -- because Gens Tepet is still prestigious. So you can tell we're lowballing this age-gap, but it does at least put Cynis at 96 to Ragara's 215 so she's lived an entire mortal lifetime before having her brother's child).

                                                A relationship between Tepet Cynis and Ragara also explains why he doesn't show her the emnity he shows Mnemon -- Tepet was only a consort, and it's not like (to Ragara's mind) Cynis will ever found her own Great House to rival him.

                                                And it kinda explains how Ragara gets the boy, if Cynis has to answer to her Gens leadership (especially as her father is likely yet to go missing before Bhagwei's birth) and Ragara considers his Great House to be closer to the Empress than a Shogunate Gens stripped of its special privileges.

                                                Canon Dates

                                                139 Birth of Ragara (The Realm)
                                                266 Shogunate Gens stripped (The Realm)
                                                369 Birth of Mnemon (The Realm)
                                                385 Destruction of Versino (1E, 2E, Mnemon remains age 16 in 3E timeline)
                                                399 Sesus born (The Realm)
                                                428 Ragara Bhagwei founds the Heptagram (1E Aspect Book Wood, 2E)
                                                501 House Cynis elevated (The Realm)
                                                "Centuries before 768" Cynis dies of heroin and dreamdust overdose (What Fire Hath Wrought)

                                                This gives us anywhere between 139 and 359 for the birth of Cynis (if she remains the mother of Bhagwei). And anywhere between 363 and 371 for the birth of Ragara Bhagwei (assuming he still attended the Versino as a contemporary of Mnemon and founded the Heptagram).

                                                I favor 266 for Cynis and 364 for Bhagwei.

                                                Worth noting that Sesus is now younger than Mnemon, and Ragara's sibling killing spree now ended some time after 399. Making it not unlikely that he had a hand in Cynis's death.
