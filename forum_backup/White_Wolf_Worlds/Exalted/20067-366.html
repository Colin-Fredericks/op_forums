                            11-30-2016, 05:06 AM
                            Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                            Resisting someone trying to disengage is reflexive right?
                            Not exactly. Disengaging is a combat action, that to be successfully made, requires a winning a contested roll against every close opponent who wishes to oppose the move. But yes, the rolls happen automatically if the opponent wishes them to happen at all.
                            Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                            And is there a limit on this? What if like multiple people try to disengage.
                            Technically all of them would be making contested rolls against every opponent that wished to. The storyteller may decide to abbreviate the resolution if I gets tedious; the players might well agree.

                            Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                            Diving for cover doesn't necessarily need you to break engagement right?
                            You don't need to disengage to take cover. You can do so while in close range of hostile opponents. It just requires sufficient bric-a-brac in the scenery; there's got to be something to duck behind after all.
