                                I think a reversal of Mara's initiation would be cool.

                                COVENANT WITH THE SUN
                                The character became a sorceror by meditating on her own Solar Essence, emulating one of the Mice of the Sun, or by being initiated into sorcerous enlightement by a Solar Exalt or the Unconquered Sun himself.

                                Shaping Rituals
                                • The sorcerer draws power from revealing light, channeling it into her spells. Whenever she begins a scene standing directly under rays of bright light, bearing aloft a lit torch or lantern, or ablaze with an anima banner at the burning level, she gains three sorcerous motes, which last until the end of the scene and can be spent towards any spells she casts. Additionally, whenever she takes a shape sorcery action to shape a spell while brightly illuminated and clearly visible, she gains an additional sorcerous mote as long as the spell’s target is paying her his full attention and seeking to engage her in combat or debate.

                                • The sorcerer’s patron offers her power in exchange for burnt offerings, breathing in the smoke of her tribute and rewarding her with glory. The sorcerer may make a prayer roll (Charisma + Performance, difficulty 5 minus the Resources value of any sacrifices offered along with the prayer) seeking power from her patron. Success grants her sorcerous motes equal to (her Essence + extra successes on the roll). These motes last for the duration of the story, and can be spent towards any spell she casts. She cannot pray for power more than once a day, and receiving new motes replaces any granted by past prayers.

                                • The sorcerer has learned to cultivate the light of virtue in her own heart, using it to charge the power of her spells. Whenever she would gain a point of Willpower from upholding an Intimacy that represents a passionate emotion or fervent belief, she may instead claim 5 sorcerous motes. These motes last until the next sunset, and can be spent towards any spell she casts. If upholding that Intimacy causes in-character hardship that would be sufficient to gain solar experience as though from a Flaw, she gains 10 sorcerous motes, which last for the duration of the story. After the first time this bonus is awarded in a story, subsequent awards are lowered to the sorcerer’s (Essence).

                                Other Benefits
                                • Faithful Servant (Merit •): Like one of the Mice of the Sun, the sorceror gains a Defining Tie of loyalty to the Unconquered Sun, which cannot be weakened or altered by any influence rolls or magic.

                                • Fed by Virtue (Merit ••, •••): Like one of the Mice of the Sun, the sorceror may satisfy hunger or thirst by acting compassionately or courageously to aid another. At three dots, her hunger pains may be allayed by witnessing someone else do the same, provided she has a Defining positive Tie to the person performing the virtuous action.

                                • Duke of Burning Light (Merit ••): Light and fire answer readily to the sorcerer’s will. Whenever the sorcerer casts a spell that creates light or fire as its primary effect, or manipulates fire, its cost is lowered by three sorcerous motes. If it’s her control spell, she may also waive a single point of Willpower from its cost once per day.


                                Rejected Merits
                                • Celestial Nobility (Merit •): Gods recognize the sorcerer as one favored by the Unconquered Sun. Minor gods are treated as having a Minor Tie of amazed awe towards her, while major gods have a positive Minor Tie of respect, or some variation suited to their nature. This merit is not available to Celestial Exalted... or rather, would be redundant, as they are already considered favored by a Celestial Incarna by the very fact of their Exaltation.

                                • Distant Solar Patron (Merit ••): The Unconquered Sun appears to the sorceror in visions, feeding her with ambrosia from heaven. Once per story, the sorcerer’s player may declare that she is visited by the Unconquered Sun in a vision while praying or meditating during the day. She does not recover Willpower from that sleep, but receives some significant benefit determined by the Storyteller—tutelage in a new spell in a single night, double 9s on all shape sorcery spells for the next day, the service of a Mouse of the Sun or other servitor as a gift from the Sun, or something similar. She also wakes with a Minor Principle of “I take pleasure in life and virtue,” or strengthens that Intimacy if she already possesses it.

                                    Speaking of the Book of 3 Circles, though they became obsolete fairly quickly as canon marched on, I liked the Lunar and Sidereal initiations. So--


                                    Lunar Initiation:

                                    You have undergone the traditional No Moon initiation into Sorcery, consisting of three trials. Obviously, this is almost unique to Lunars, though it is not impossible for outcaste Dragon-Blooded or Solars to be tutored in this fashion.

                                    --You survived the Full Moon trial, to survive in a punishing wilderness while completing tasks dictated by your master, and being hindered by spirits working for them, and you can draw strength from self-reliance and unbreakable toughness. When you are struck by an attack while using Shape Sorcery, the spell immediately gains 2 sorcerous motes if it was Withering, or 1 sorcerous mote per level of damage you took if it was Decisive. If you succeed at a Resistance or Integrity roll caused by an enemy attack while using Shape Sorcery, you gain sorcerous motes equal to half your successes, to a maximum of 3. Note that you can still only cast a spell during your action, even if the cost is met outside of it.

                                    --You prevailed in the Changing Moon trial, living peacefully amongst animals nominated by your tutor, and overcoming predators that prey on them. When you make a Shape Sorcery action, you gain an additional mote if one or more animals (including your familiar) with a Major or stronger Intimacy towards you are within long range. Additionally, when you would draw Essence or Willpower from a familiar, you may gain 5 sorcerous motes instead of the usual benefit, which must be used on the turn they are drawn. Only one familiar can aid each spell in this manner.

                                    --You overcame the No Moon trial, where you were trapped in an illusion and given a problem with no logical solution. Forced to rely on instinct and intuition instead of your senses, you can still draw power from that side of yourself. When you make a Shape Sorcery roll, you can choose to close your eyes to gain +2 sorcerous motes, or stop your ears to gain +1 sorcerous mote, along with the penalties of blindness or deafness for one round. If you actually have those Flaws, you gain the bonus every turn.

                                                            I've been working on an artifact/initiation for a character concept. It's not quite done yet, still needs a shaping ritual and evocations/merits/secret(optional) history, but I'll post what I've got. I can edit/update when finished if people are interested.

                                                            If anyone is familiar with them, it's loosely based off of "So you want to be a Wizard?", by Diane Duane, and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

                                                            The Great Art (Artifact xx)

                                                            “The first step to finding an answer, is asking a question.”
                                                            -                                                             The Great Art

                                                            In the Age of Sorrows, the Sorcerer is a feared and distrusted figure. Even in the houses of the Realm, sorcerers are shunned and isolated, left alone to perform their strange rituals and research. In the Threshold, it only gets worse; most practitioners hold themselves away in Manses and ancient ruins, seeking power through forbidden lore.

                                                            This is an unavoidable truth for a modern sorcerer, who can only dream of a better time. A time before, when sorcery was respected (if feared), and communities could flourish and share in their art together.

                                                            If legend is to be believed, the Golden Dusk Society was one such community, in a time before. It is said that they were a widely spread group, accepting members from all social strata, from the greatest of the Twilight to a newly initiated Mortal. The only requirements for membership is to find your way into the possession of The Great Art (some say on accident, some say it’s fate, some say it’s a higher intelligence guiding where and when the book appears); the instruction manual for the society. It often first appears as a simple book, smaller then most tomes but larger than a pamphlet. As the sorcerer (or future sorcerer, if The Great Art is initiating them) reads and asks questions, the tome seems to add required information and grow in size, sometimes containing more information than is possible to fit in it’s pages.

                                                            After requesting information for their own experiments and research, the initiate will find several blank pages near the end of the tome. After recording their research and findings, they are finally a full member of the Golden Dusk Society, and can access the research of all other members, or even pass along notes and letters to each other (which fall from the pages of the recipient's book).

                                                            Savants who know of the society don’t believe any members have survived into the Age of Sorrows...or even if they have, the society is no longer recruiting. A copy of The Great Art has not appeared to a prospective initiate since the Usurpation.


                                                            Shaping Rituals

                                                            Continuing research has always been a cornerstone to the Golden Dusk Society, in order to continue the growth of The Great Art. After a sorcerer completes a new, unique sorcerous project and records his research into the blank pages, he gains (Ambition x (2 for Terrestrial, 4 for Celestial, 6 for Solar)) sorcerous motes, which can be used on any spell. These motes last one story, and the sorcerer can only have one such pool of motes at a time.

                                                            Every student quickly finds that The Great Art contains more than sorcerous knowledge. Everything from personal observations of ancient savants to detailed illustrations of the anatomy of demons can be found within it’s pages, and called upon. When a sorcerer uses a stunt to describe how he is using his superior knowledge to gain an upper hand on an opponent, he gains (stunt level + 3) sorcerous motes. The sorcerer can only have one such pool of motes at a time, and the ritual can only be used once per scene. This can be reset by recording a new and unique observation into The Great Art, a 2 dice Lore or Occult stunt.

                                                            Evocations

                                                            While a Sorcerer is attuned to The Great Art, he can consult the tome while using a Shape Sorcery action to gain additional sorcerous motes. The sorcerer gains 1 automatically, and can take additional defence penalties, up to his Essence rating, to gain an equal amount of additional sorcerous motes, as he blocks out the outside world to focus.
