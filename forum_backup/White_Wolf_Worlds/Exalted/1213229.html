      I'd made some initial attempts to write up an orichalcum sword inspired by one interpretation of Excalibur that says the sword would emit a blinding light upon being drawn from its sheath. Probably borrow some mechanics from the Sunflash chakram in Arms for an anima banner. The original idea was to make it a Five-Dot sword as difficult as Stormcaller, since it blinds your own allies as well as your enemies.

      I hunted down the stats on the Scabbard of the Living Weapon, but haven't done much to update it.

      I'd like to get more done on the rocket hammer inspired by Overwatch's Reinhardt and God Eater's Boost Hammer.

      I have some vague plans for an adamant sword based on Cloud Strife's Ultima Weapon.

      I haven't gotten very far with Ruby Rose's Crescent Rose, as we have no canonical Third Edition rules for firearms, yet, and I'm not eager to fully commit to making homebrew rules for such on top of the Evocations.

      Designing warstriders that transform between different forms is tricky.

                          Originally posted by Sandact6 View Post

                          10 characters
                          10 Characters (Artifact 4)

                          It is said that the Endlessly Witty Warrior grew lonesome and bored in the aftermath of the Primordial War, so he journeyed farther into the Wyld than any before him had dared. His m to seek out new forms of conquest. Centuries later, he returned with this weapon, a chain whip of unidentifiable material that is inscribed with shallow, nearly invisible symbols from an unknown language. The wielders of 10 Characters have been many and varied in the ages that followed, though they all shared one universal quality: loquaciousness in combat.

                          No type of Exalted is dissonant with 10 Characters, even those Exalted who are normally dissonant with strange materials. Only Solar Exalted are resonant with 10 Characters, but others who steal, borrow, or emulate the charm Poised Lion Attitude may become resonant after mastering And Another Thing: Some Mysteries are Still To Be Written.

                          Upon attuning to 10 Characters, the wielder may awaken An Uncharacteristically Concise Name: Horizon-Spanning Discourse for free.

                          An Uncharacteristically Concise Name: Horizon-Spanning Discourse
                          Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1;
                          Type: Supplemental
                          Keywords: None
                          Duration: A matter of opinion

                          Through some trickery of the wyld, time twists and stretches with the flick of 10 Character's chains, binding the moment of a strike into an eternity. This evocation does not supplement an attack, but a Social action taken in combat, such as a Presence or Performance roll. It allows the wielder to say much more during their combat action than might ordinarily be possible in the midst of pitched combat, stretching out their dialogue as far as necessary to make their point. While it always lasts at least as long as necessary for the social action to be made, liberal extensions of dialectic spacetime may require repeat activations at the discretion of the Storyteller.

                          A Most Elegant and Obligatory Punishment of Unbecoming Brevity
                          Cost: 5m,1i; Mins: Essence 1
                          Type: Supplemental
                          Keywords: Decisive-Only
                          Duration: Instant
                          Prerequisite Charms: An Uncharacteristically Concise Name: Horizon-Spanning Discourse

                          Those who would ignore 10 Characters do so at their peril. This charm may only be activated on a round immediately following the use of An Uncharacteristically Concise Name: Horizon-Spanning Discourse, against the same opponent who was subject to the previous round's social influence attempt.

                          If the opponent responded with complete silence, not deigning to reply with any words at all, this evocation adds double 10s to the damage roll, and the wielder retains an extra point of initiative upon returning to base initiative.
                          If the opponent responded with a well-reasoned and complete sentence, this evocation only adds the extra point of base initiative.
                          If the opponent is still responding, even while the wielder attacks, the attack automatically fails.
                          If, however, the opponent offered only a succinct reply--no matter how dull or witty, proper or profane--10 Characters finds its mark nigh-unerringly. The attack benefits from an automatic success, ignores hardness, rolls double 9s on the damage roll, and the wielder retains an extra point of initiative upon returning to base initiative.
