                I mean, I imagine that the House as an institution owns all the family's assets, and the leader(s) (the Tepet council, Mnemon herself, Cathak Cainan) decide who is responsible for administering what. Forming a separate household probably yields a measure of autonomy. I took the talk of stipends being used to discourage unwanted behaviour (like marrying mortals) to imply that "The House's money" is distributed the same way as assets & responsibility, no doubt with a fair bit of favouritism involved.

                It seems to me that a Dragon-Blood is an asset worth considerable expense, and even the poorer Great Houses can afford it. I'd expect that if a dynasty reached the point where they felt they had so many Dragon-Blooded children that there were no viable endeavours they could worthily occupy them with (which seems unlikely), it would've been within the Empress' interests to give that house some new source of revenue that would require a lot of work. That way she can ensure the continued steady growth of the Dragon-Blooded host. Unless she wanted to deliberately constrain that house's power, of course. Since her disappearance, the threat of civil war might make some more conservative, wanting to keep funds for legions, or quite the opposite, if they think there's time for a child to be Exalted and educated before open war breaks out. Or if they anticipate that the war will drag on.

                The existence of evocations does seem to undermine the idea that the Great Houses have an arsenal of artifact weapons & armour that they move around as needs/whims dictate. I'd be surprised if that remains in 3e.

                                    Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post

                                    Out of curiosity, where did you get all of that from? According to both First and Second Edition (MoEP: Dragon Blooded pages 21-22), in the Realm Dragon Blood couples are expected to have at least 1 child every 50 years. Once the couple has had two children, the "procreative" requirement on their marriage is considered done, and they don't have to have any more children. So yeah, 2 kids in 100 years.

                                    It seems like you're complaining about something that was never in the gameline.
                                    It is the same source, but a different section. The Realm's familial obligation is two children within 50 years of marriage and no more than fifty years between children thereafter, or the marriage is dissolved. Later the book discusses the biology.
                                    Originally posted by Manual of Exalted Power: The Dragon Blooded, page 22
                                    Among the Terrestrial Exalted, producing children is almost compulsory. The more children a couple produces, the more chance there is that one of the offspring will grow up to be another member of the Dragon-Blooded Host. Some Terrestrial women are known to take particular delight in producing child after child, although physicians have learned that Dragon-Blooded women need at least five years between children. Otherwise, it starts to age them unnaturally quickly. The average number of births is eight per century, or about one every 12 years. A Dragon-Blooded pregnancy lasts a full 15-month year. Given the sturdiness of Terrestrial physiology, a pregnancy only begins to show after five months, and an expectant mother may remain as active as normal for the first 12 months of pregnancy, with no significant danger to herself or the unborn child.​
                                    So, a social minimum rate of one child every fifty years, a biological maximum rate of one every five years, and averaging out to one every twelve years. Per couple.
