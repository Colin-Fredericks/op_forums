                    Originally posted by Kelly Pedersen View Post

                    My ruling would depend on how you're meaning &quot;not immediately obvious&quot;. The Subtlety merit specifies that it makes the attached merit &quot;non-obvious to observers when not in use&quot; (emphasis mine). So, what counts as &quot;in use&quot; for armor? I'd argue that if the armor is capable of protecting you, it's &quot;in use&quot;. Therefore, Subtlety combined with Unusual Hide would mean you could produce armor quickly, but that would take an action. If you want its benefit to apply against attacks you didn't have time to prep for, you'd have to have it visible. In combat, I'd rule that it takes a Draw/Ready Weapon action to &quot;don&quot;, essentially.

                    If you want the Unusual Hide to be constantly there, but only visible once it stops an attack, I'd probably let you slide with a Terrestrial 3 working, on the grounds that this is effectively &quot;a minor supernatural power that augments [your] strongest traits&quot;. I don't think just the equivalent of a mutation is powerful enough to count for the full-blown &quot;Grant a supernatural power&quot; of Celestial 2.
                    So I really like the idea of "sweating" out a rocky armor. But I think I've got a refinement of the idea.

                    Terrestrial 3 - a minor supernatural power that augments [your] strongest trait

                    How about having the Bonfire Earth Anima power kick in at Glowing (and Burning)?

                        A thing I've been wondering.

                        A high speed maneuverable vehicle. One moving as fast as Stormwind Rider or Azure Chariot. Large enough to hold an apocethary office or something and several beds. Like a medivac, without the healing laser.

                        Terrestrial, or celestial? Because apparently creating a millstone that creates grain out of sand is terrestrial. And that really confuses me.
                        As others have said, a vehicle sounds more like an artifact than a working.

                        That said, if you wanted something like a spirit whose main functions are 1) carrying your office and 2) traveling very fast, I might argue that Celestial 3 is suitable. I could see a Second Circle demon doing that if those were its main areas of expertise.

                        It wouldn't be good for much else, though. Moving a lot of stuff very fast is a very useful trick in the Age of Sorrows, and one that few people can duplicate, so your spirit would be very much a specialist.

                        (All that said, I understand why people often come up with working ideas that make more sense as artifacts. I've often been guilty of the same myself. The working rules are way less of a pain than the artifact crafting rules, and definitely require less Charm investment...)

                                        Celestial 2/3

                                        The garden of learning.

                                        A garden the size of a small park, filled with flowers and a unique species of butterfly and bird. No other living creature lives within the garden. A thousand blossoms exist within the garden, all with a unique shape and scent.

                                        The garden is a teacher, a source of knowledge for any known subject in Creation. A sorcerer may discover the secrets of a spell in the movement patterns of the butterflies as they flit past. A warrior may devise a new style as he observes the petals fall to the ground. The scent of the flowers may bring visions of places to explore, and the taste of its fruits may inspire musicians to greater heights.

                                        The garden itself is considered to be a tutor in all known subjects, and halves the time needed to learn any subjects.
