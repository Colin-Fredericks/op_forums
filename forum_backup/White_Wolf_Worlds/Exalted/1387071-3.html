        Lunars have:

        Moon Beckons Tide (adds to the Sail rolls of your allies if they are travelling towards you)
        Lodestone Reckoning Manner (adds successes to Sail rolls to navigate a route if you plan the route in advance)
        Albatross-and-Hurricane Odyssey (bypasses naval hazards)
        Hungry Mouse Mandate (adds a botch to an enemy Sail roll if they are in your territory)

        That's it. No double-9s, reroll 6s, plus Essence successes. That's it. Wits+Sail+Specialty+Excellency(+Stunt+Willpower).

But they can't do much else. To Solars. Otherwise they have a bunch of cool and interesting powers (starting with +1 success and reroll 6s for 2m and working up from there -- a maxed out Essence 5 DB could throw down a ram with 11+Stunt dice, 4 successes, reroll 6s, double-8s, minus 5 dice on the opponent's roll, plus one Hull damage for 18m 3WP 1a if they win a concealment stratagem first...
