                                02-02-2015, 11:41 AM
                                Originally posted by nonamemaddoxx View Post
                                How does the Dragon-Blooded Dodge charm Safety Among Enemies work? The errata says "This Charm may only be activated if the Dragon-Blood successfully dodges an incoming attack as a result of applying Step 5 defenses (do not compare DV and attack pools)". What are Step 5 defenses?
                                IIRC it is:

                                Step 1) Attacker declares attack + all charms they're going to use.
                                Step 2) Defender declares what DV they'll use to defend + all charms.
                                Step 3) Attacker rolls their dice.
                                Step 4) Attacker re-rolls their dice if they have an appropriate charm
                                Step 5) All external penalties (including the defender's DV) are applied to step 4.
                                Step 6) Reroll of above
                                Step 7-10) Damage

                                So my understanding of the charm is now, if the DB successfully blocks the attack using their DVs (which are applied at step 5), they can re-direct it. Otherwise it hits normally. You don't do the weird comparison of DV to attacker dice pool anymore.
