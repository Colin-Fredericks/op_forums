    Exalted - have your PCs ever been defeated?

    06-25-2018, 03:25 AM
    Has your exalted been defeated or lost before?

    I don't mean just a temporary setback. I mean a true defeat. Someone dies. The city you were defending is destroyed. The army you raised was crushed despite all your preparations. The mask of winters rampages all over Creation when you failed to stop him.

    If you did, then how?

      Holy Shit yes, strap yourselves in cuz we are getting explicative'd.

      2e. Playing in the Scavenger lands, Thorns, Celeron general East area. Here's the relevant players: Mako Li, and Eclipse with about 200 xp and 0 combat charms. Not even Dodge or Resistance. Nothing. All Social Fu, egregious use of charmshare and a fully paid up member of the Dangerously Free Thinkers Society. At this point he had Hollow Heart Transcendence, 20 Willpower, and could convince the rain not to fall. He could get seals to club themselves and have everyone thank him for it. The Mask of Winters was impressed at his beautiful cruelty once, and thats saying something.

      Mako is only not an beautiful bloodstain because he's best mates with the Zenith Balasar, the Twin-Tailed Comet. 7 feet of Super heavy plate, hate, and a warhammer that could be confused for a siege weapon. Balasar loves the UCS almost as much as he hates everything else. He has a loyal hound, size of a pony. That comes up.

      Paling around with these to is the mercurial Jason Chance, Dawn caste. If you could fuse Jack Sparrow and Dante into one person and give him the buster sword, you'd basically have Chance. He has all the self preservation of particularly clever brick (a suicidally overconfident brick with no will to live). Chance plays fast and loose with everything only because he hasnt found a way to be even less responsible. He has none of Mako's cunning or Balasar's stubborn refusal to believe actual reality. He likes to go nova with motes early and hard.

      Mako hates the Realm and the Great Houses because he's from one and was seen as a failure for not becoming a Terrestrial. Balasar found out that the Immaculate faith prohibits Sun worship so he decided that they had to be crusaded off the face of Creation and he was going to build that sol-damned crusade if it killed him, except even then he probably wouldn't realize he's dead because he's too stubborn. Chance just fucks around.

      For reasons that dont matter, the group acquired a Manse on a particularly good eldritch leyline, which should have been suspicious but when you are half mad with god-power that word stops having meaning to you. So for a round about way, Mako has put off the Wyld Hunt for months even though people are openly being solicited to renounce their livelihoods and rally to the Keep of the Glorious Sun and take up arms to overthrow the realm (did I mention that Mako is REALLY good at his job?). Well, the Mask of Winters is sick of us helping and then screwing him over by turns so he keikaku's the Wyld Hunt down on us before we are ready- this was part of his long term plan to screw over a gold faction sidereal, but that didn't matter yet.

      So what do you do when you are 4 Anathema and a few thousand unreasonably intense and possible zealotous jihad-crusaders staring down 50k troops and dozens if not hundreds of Terrestrials? Why, make a deal with a Fae Queen that isn't suspicious at all, of course! So she comes to our aid and the Solars and their hated foes the Deadly and Terrible Raksha swoop down on the Heroic and Probably Actually Righteous Wyld Hunt and go through them like wolves through a herd of Imperial Rabbits. The day is saved! Except that the Mask is pissed so his main enforcer/ Mako's technically-wife straight up declares that the Abyssal forces waiting in reserve are called in, reverse -RotK- ghost army style. Turns out that leaving all those thousands of bodies out for a few days on a night with no moon is a pretty good Build-Your-Own-Shadowlands kit (mercilessly squandered lives not included!). So now thats about to happen on, we'll say, wednesday and it's monday night.

      Oh, and that Fae Queen? she wants the ashes of the White Tree of Jewels, aka the geomantic lynchpin for the entire god-damn East. Yeah, cut it down, burn it, and give it to her. On Tuesday. Rock, meet apocalyptic hard place.

      Now, if you were sensible, you'd probably reach out to the remnants of the still-reinforcing Wyld Hunt and try to make a deal to avoid the Apocalypse. Or maybe you'd sell out to the Mask and let him use you to make a few favors and break some Divine kneecaps. Or even Entreat with the Fae Queen.

      So what does our intrepid hero Mako do? Suffers Limit Break from his plans not working out perfectly, of course! So if you had a callous disregard of literally all other beings in the multiverse and owed some ashes to a rainbow tart, well, goddamn, you'd get some ashes. So he did, and just figured if he puts it in a magic bottle that She won't realize the deception. Limit Breaks, kids, not even once. This does not go as planned, but instead of invading she decides, hey, I love Drama as much as the next Feather Boa'd Eldritch Larping Monstrosity, and she drops a truth bomb-

      When I said that Mask called in the Wyld Hunt? I lied. Mako was getting pretty deep in favors to that Abyssal trollop and he fucked up a secret optional objective on a mission, so that got the Wyld Hunt's attention. Ok, Mask was behind it because he's a douche, but still, this one falls onto Mako's shoulders twice. And the dominoes fall.

      The dead start rising, which spurs the Wyld Hunt to come down on us for round 2 with reckless abandon, because they think we are dishonoring their beloved dead and being baby-eater Anathema, and the dead start storm the Castle of the Sun and start killing off the human famrers-turned-warriors because it turns out a lot of buffs from 2e War are temporary. Oops. The Wyld Hunt arrive and their generals (the DBs) start Dynasty Warrior-ing the shit out of the humans and undead, making a strike team tip-of-the-spear move towards the keep as well. It should be noted, however, that this "spear tip" is more like "almost 200 Terrestrial Exalted unified out of shear hate and cashing in all of their artifacts and resources".

      Things, don't, uh, go smooth.

      Balasar's hound, which can talk and is stronger than any 2 bears takes an arrow meant for Balasar and goes down. Balasar The Twin-tailed Comet lives up to his namesake and jumps around bringing the hammer down like a Hulk/Thor fanfiction up to 11. Chance has a great time up until he's backstabbed by that Abyssal scamp. And Mako, hoo boy. Mako.

      Mako, you see, never missed a chance to get a little advantage. So he learned his first and only life-preserving charmas part of the deal with the Fae Queen- Bastion of the Self (Heart). It's a Fae charm- essentially your body becomes dream stuff and obeys cartoon logic: he can't be harmed except by Charms and Magic Materials. He can't even be hurt by gravity. So Mako takes a bunch of arrows and swords into his body and "falls" to his "death" into a river. Go Heroes!

      There was another solar there and some empowered NPCs, but essentially the Solar PC is a sorcerer and she sorcery'd the dead back to dead and then smuggled herself away by boat. She was actually tasked by Balasar to Live and tell our allies about what happend.

      Any way, Mako emerges from the river at dawn and checks to make sure his clothes are fine (spoiler: he still looks picture perfect, the bastard). He finds Balasar clutching his beloved hound, who has been rasping out and trying to hold out for as long as he can. Balasar is inhumanly fucked up- he's only alive because everyone else was sure that he was dead. Hammer abandoned, armor sundered, riddled with spears and arrows. Blood around him so think its made the soggy ground into a proper british dinner. He can't say anything or even think, he's just thousand yard staring at his dog, who breathes out his last breath as the sun reaches them. Balasar is so stoic at this minute that he could have won a staring contest against a statue.

      Now, if you're Mako and your bread and butter is making deals and controlling people, you'd probably forget all that and just be stunned in silence at all the tragedy and failure and death and waste around you. But you are not Mako. So Mako decides that if this is his fault, it would be bad (A), that non-exalted have no intrinsic worth(b), and that, if you crunch the numbers, he's still saved more people up to this point then he got killed (c). He explains this to Balasar, because honestly Mako has no idea how deal with people who don't want things and have no vices.

      Balasar doesn't say anything. Balasar doesn't move. Balasar doesn't even breathe. And then Mako says that, on his life and word as a Solar, he will get Balasar a new dog.

      And Balasar hammers Mako so hard he literally flies into the fucking stratosphere.

      - - - -
      We ended the night on that note, all of us silent and drained. A real life year of work and plot and everything, all cataclysmically mismanaged, everyone beaten or scattered.
      The ST thanks us for the session. We get our coats on. Just as we are about to leave, he says

      "You have 80xp to spend for next week. Let me know what your characters have done in theTwenty Years since the Battle of the Fallen Sun.

                  wow

                  recently in the 2.5 southern alchy game I ST, I had my players raid a realm manse construction in Harborhead territory. There was a legion defending the place, but they did good prep-work, rallying nearby tribes into a coordinated raid on the place via mind-control of the tribal chiefs. (diplomacy via brain-probes)

                  they had their sneaky alchy sneak in and assassinate the legion commander - and got really lucky, since a lot of DB defence charms can't stand up to celestial-tier essence...

                  but later they ran into the local DB immaculate abbot, an essence 4 martial arts NPC.

                  Now, I hadn't statted the guy to floor my players - but the party's two most combat optimized alchies got slapped around like wet noodles. This turned the raid into a running battle where they tried to escape, which they managed to by exploiting the fact that alchies don't really need to breathe - hiding out of sight at the bottom of a muddy river, or in nearby hastily-dug underground tunnels.

                  it completely changed how I had imagined the fight going - but I just went with it, and my players learned an important lesson of just how small-fry they still are as essence 3 alchies in a world of far more seasoned wyld hunters and whatnot

                          For 3e, We recently had such an encounter while wyld-shaping. The golden casket in the center of the hall started shuddering until its lid burst up, and dozens of spindly limbs crawled out, each bearing a smaller coffin. Toxic fumes billowed out of every one of them, and the buzzing sound overwhelming everyone was revealed to come from each coffin: whenever anything got close, the lid opened to reveal thousands of rows of teeth, grinding against each other, snapping at its prey. A behemoth.

                                                          Originally posted by Accelerator View Post

                                                          Dear Lord how do you accidentally kill someone that is perfectly invulnerable?
                                                          There's two answers to that.

                                                          Some character background-
                                                          Stalking Cat is a Chosen of Secrets whose a master thief and heavily involved in organised crime.
                                                          This goes all the way back to her backstory as an orphan in Cherak, she tried to blackmail a crime family called the Chang brothers after stealing one of their letters, they responded by abducting her little sister and the only family she had left. She realises that if she just gives the Changs the letter they'll both die so uses her initiative and gives it to a rival family motivating them to attack the Chang compound while she escapes with her sister.
                                                          Unfortunately by the time this plan goes ahead her sister has already frozen to death and she's told she can't use her newfound power to simply murder the Chang brothers. She eventually puts a hit on them and it fools nobody, almost 20 years later what happened to her sister is still a source of anger and frustration to her.

                                                          Why we don't make deals with bad guys-
                                                          Rakan Thulio approached her a while ago because he knows the little sister is a major point of vulnerability for her. She's expecting an offer of revenge and to tell him to take a hike, instead he offers to bring her sister back or more accurately introduce her to a version who had never died.
                                                          Despite her skepticism she's interested in a way she never thought she could be and Thulio wants one little thing from her, a copy of the spell Unity of the Closed Fist. Easily done for her as she's one of the greatest thieves in existence. She also reasons that if Thulio has 5 Solars as patsies he's already a huge threat what's one spell going to do?

                                                          Our heart was in the right place, Ok?-
                                                          We'd become aware of a group claiming to be the Unconquered Sun's demigod children at around this time, they'd been doing something which was causing older manses to explode and would've gone under our notice if they hadn't put Farhold in danger and then used an ally in Yu-Shan to get some of our group Audited. We decide to let their father deal with them, go to all this trouble to gain an audience with the Incana (this involved befriending a worm-like creature that may have been in Yu-Shan longer than the gods, traveling through a city's worth of mono-wire, fending off a forbidden god and winning a boat race) and tell the Sun his kids are making a mess to try and get his attention.
                                                          His attitude to the whole thing is along the lines of "Shit, not again" even confesses to us that it's not the first time he's been a deadbeat dad. He's willing to meet with them and try to make this thing work. They end up agreeing to meet him at the Pinnacle of Mercy. We even get one of these previous children now elevated to a full goddess along for the ride to help mediate if necessary.
                                                          We smelt a rat too late, Cat realised that Unity of the Closed Fist wasn't for a circle of Solars it was for the five of them. They combined into a being called The Second Sun and The Unconquered Sun, filled with remorse for his inaction and facing an opponent with several of his abilities was outmatched. We don't know if he survived or not but there's reason to believe that The Second Sun is on its way to Yu-Shan.
