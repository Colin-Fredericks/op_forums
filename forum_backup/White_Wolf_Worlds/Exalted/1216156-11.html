            Ok, one more idea. We know that sorcery is grand. Death by obsidian butterflies lets you clear out battalions, and that firey bird lets you outrange Napoleonic artillery. So here's the thing. What about something with a bit more precision? I'm thinking of a spell that turns the air around you rancid and necrotic, swiftly rotting away flesh and equipment. It inflicts damage on the level of, say, a lava or acid bath, and causes no collateral damage. It's pure damage potential is not as large as death by obsidian butterflies, nor is it as wide-ranged as Brilliant Raptor, but trapped in an urban environment, it can strike from corners where no one can see, and kill your enemies in cramped and winding spaces.
