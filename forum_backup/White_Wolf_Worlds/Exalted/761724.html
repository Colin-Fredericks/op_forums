    Unstoppable Force vs. Immovable Object, Dogs and the Solar Athletics Tree

    11-09-2015, 05:42 PM
    Dogs have special attack of Harry (3e p 562) which after some conditions says a character cannot disengage.

    Solars have a Charm in Atheltics called Eagle Wing Style which allow them to create disengage actions. I'm sure other Exalts will also have other ways to create Disengage actions.

    I see a few ways to rule this:

    1.) You're a flying Solar, dogs don't get to hold you back!
    2.) UFvIO, you would have a roll off (probably the same rolls as the Disengage Action) to see if you even get the chance to Disengage. Then you would roll again to see if you are successful.
    3.) Dog's got you down.

    Any opinions? This hasn't come up in play, I'm just trying to sort out rulings in advance and while I'm leaning UFvIO on this one I'd be happy to hear from you knowledgeable forumites.

                              This is solved by looking at the mechanics and asking what they're modeling.

                              Harry represents a dog's ability to bite into an ankle or leg and keep the person from running away from the dog. Eagle Wing Style allows you to run off/jump away in a burst of speed.

                              The question then is: Will jumping really high or running really fast help you disengage from a pit-bull that has clamped down on your leg?

                              The answer here is 'no.' It's biting on your leg. If you want to disengage from it, you need to get if off your leg, not run away.

                                          There's no ambiguity in the rules. Eagle Wing Style allows disengaging in some specific situations where disengages wouldn't be allowed, but having a dog hanging onto your leg isn't one of them. Therefore, you can't disengage with it if a dog has prevented your disengage. You might want to ask your ST to allow a disengage anyway, using your flight powers as a justification for that, but that's a case of changing the rules because it would be cooler/more realistic, not any sort of ambiguity in the rules themselves.
