        Hmmm... I can think of a few main things.

        The first would be the UCS's under-gods. Stuff like his handmaidens, Ryzea, etc, who directly serve him. And gods associated with him like Five Days Darkness.
        And any god-blooded children they believe Ignis had (like Little Beam). And his girlfriends/consorts! For example, Queen Merela, Venus, etc.
        For example, there might be a picture of the 5 Maidens dressed as brides (there was a story in one of the 2nd ed books that Venus arranges marriages between the Maidens and the UCS, but every time Saturn breaks off their engagement).

        Second, would be characters from stories involving him. For example, if the Solar believes in a story of the UCS rescuing someone from monsters, inspiring a village into good deeds, etc, they could feature in the art.
        You mentioned Christian art, there's lots of art featuring Bible stories which just show whoever's in the story, good, bad, and just some regular person. They're not necessarily shown because they're a saint.

        The third is a bit complex, and depends on the socio-cultural context of the Solars who built it.
        And that's pictures of Solars themselves. I'd think that, in the first age religion of the Solars, venerating the UCS, Solars themselves would take the place of saints. Not just any Solars, but famous Solars who were seen as exemplars of the UCS's virtues and examples for other Solars and humans to copy.


        Then of course, we have the ever popular pictures of the people who built the temple, paid for it, or just were regular worshippers who died.
        For example, in my Church, there's a memorial window for an old mayor of our city and his wife.
        http://www.mybrightonandhove.org.uk/...0p115p187p937p
        I don't know who the people are in the picture, but I assume it's the mayor and his wife.

        Actually, in a lot of old British churches, there are pictures that are there because someone donated a lot of money to pay for the Church. They're done in the exact same style as saints and other figures from the Bible.
        And this is hardly unique to Christianity.

        I can well imagine that Solars are egotistical enough to demand that their temple includes pictures of themselves!

                                                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post

                                                Possibly, but with a couple of exceptions, I think a world-body is more a terrain feature than a reality.


                                                Also, I don't think Autocthon is naturally a reality.

                                                What I mean is, generally speaking, you could just walk from inside him to outside him, in a way that you can't with Malfeas. There are magic portals you could walk out of, conceivably, but Malfeas is inherently a seperate reality from Creation.

                                                I don't think Autocthon is. He's more like a planet or moon than a plane of existence, if you see what I mean.

                                                It's just that he used magic to go into a pocket-dimension (which is referred to as the Void) outside Creation, to escape the Solars. But that's not a natural and inherent quality of Autocthon.

                                                But, naturally, he was more like a death star floating around Creation (and at the same time a dude living in a big factory in Yu-Shan), or perhaps a giant golem walking around it (like in this awesome picture: http://cdn.obsidianportal.com/assets...s-EndPages.jpg ).

                                                So more a terrain feature than a reality.

                                                I'm not sure if I've explained this well, but I hope you can understand it.
                                                I think I know what you mean, but I also think you are incorrect. The fact that the seal of eight divinities works is good evidence that there isn't just a bunch of ways in and out of Autobot. Plus Compass Autochthon specifically describes him as an orb that can be death star sized or soccer ball sized without ever changing the volume inside of him. To me that speaks of his orb as being a local extension of his world-body.

                                                As for Malfeas, the Solars specifically designed him as inescapable, but you'll note that you can leave him in any conceivable direction and you'll end up in Cecelyne. Malfeas is inescapable for demons and he'd be a pretty poor prison if he wasn't. It seems to me that it's often easier to leave a world-joten then it is to actually enter them. To get to Cecelyne from creation is a lot more difficult than to go the other way around. So long as the seal remains intact, it's literally impossible to get in to Autochthon whereas with the seal down it makes it slightly less impossible. It's not like dropping the seal bring Autochthon back from Elsewhere for instance.

                                                Anyway though, this probably isn't the place to suss this out. If you wish to converse further, feel free to create a new thread and we'll talk there.
