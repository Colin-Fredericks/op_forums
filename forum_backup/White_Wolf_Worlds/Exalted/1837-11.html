            Tried revisiting my take on Unhesitatingly Loyal Weapon, who while named originally in Time of Tumult ended up being quite different in my take of her from there to what she ended up being in Exalted: the Autochthonians. Big things is that instead of an ancient Orichalchum Caste tactical master, I had her be a fairly young Jade Caste who was originally meant to be flexible and also a big morale-booster after the fuck-up that was Excessively Righteous Blossom. Designed to be in a sense Yugash's attempt of making an Alchemical speically built for a new world. Tough, agile, clever and able to keep troops following her into an exotic world.
