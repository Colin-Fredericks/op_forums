    Setting shattering accomplishments

    08-12-2020, 01:30 PM
    What are some of the things that either your character, your players did, or your ST did in an Exalted game that shattered major setting precepts? Someone that fit well and made your group, but if you try to advocate it being part of the core setting people would look at you as if you were crazy. Major things that shatter the very precepts of the game itself. The intention isn't to shit on others ideas or stories, but rather share them and ask "How the hell did that happen?"

    Here are a few I've heard of and some ways to start off.
    • Morke's example of his circle using Time Travel to head back in time
    • A story of a 2e game where a Twilight managed to unlock the secrets of Exaltations and learn how to make her own champions.
    • A Raksha player who wanted the biggest harem in Creation, to the point where he successfully infiltrated heaven and made all Exaltations seek females only.
    • An ST I saw who declared that 90% of Dragonblooded are women. He gave some interesting points to how such a thing would work out too.
    • An early 2e game a friend had saw them "defeat" the Wyld and beat all the Raksha by using the Imperial Manse to Wyld shape infinity. This made Creation itself into an infinite plane.
    • A Storyteller that gave my Twilight the weapon who belonged to her First Age self after appeasing it's ghost: An Artifact NA daiklave called Horizon Clever. It allowed melee attacks a range, had infinite range, and was even capable of cutting the distance between two points for faster travel. It could even cut reality and allow one to enter other planes of existance (Yu-Shan, Underworld, etc). Thank god we managed to appease the ghost no violently as if the ghost was using that thing then things would've gotten... really ugly.


    Read my shit at my homebrew topic, 2.5e and 3e material!
    Play Alchemical's in 3e now, you're welcome.

      • The PCs discovered that their ancestors had created a country where the Incarnae have no sway. The Zenith had convinced the Celestials (and the Eclipse sanctified the oath) to never exalt anyone inside the area and not to intervene directly in that place.
      • The PCs travelled to a land where Fair Folk lived freely alongside mortals within Creation. These Fair Folk had learned to sustain themselves solely on ambient emotions and therefore could survive without eating people's souls.
      • A campaign's ultimate villain was a Solar who had siphoned his 4 Circlemates' Castes to become a 5 Castes superbeing.
      • A Twilight and their Changing Moon mate changed their Caste by attuning to each other's Caste - The Twilight used the Changing Moon's Essence to become more social, turning into a Zenith, while the Changing Moon used the Twilight's essence to become more scholarly, turning into a No Moon.

              *deep breath*

              The son of a Solar Night and Chosen of Serenity is sought after by Abyssals, Infernals, Sidereals, and a Solar circle (the players, which the Night caste is a part of) as the child is part of a prophecy with multiple interpretations. Eventually they find him and kill his 3rd circle demon keeper, who's death triggers a transformation and the child matures and has strange powers that are demonic, Solar, and Sidereal in nature. The child is convinced their purpose is to go open the door to Heaven for the Infernals (triggering the Lotus Massacre and the Reclamation, in accordance with the prophecy) and the circle had to wrestle with killing the child or letting them go.

              The Zenith sacrifices his Grand Daiklaive (a dangerous weapon sought after by Heaven with the power to permanently sever threads of the Loom of Fate and a plot point from the start of the game) to the Unconquered Sun for advice. UCS tells him that Fate is set but the Exalted make their own fate. Functionally, the circle only had to show the child he could be heroic and in a moment of brilliant argument from the party, the child chooses not to open the door to heaven and exalts as a Solar (cleansing him of all it's other weird mixed powers) and the party stops the Reclamation from ever kicking off.

              That shit was a wild ride and I'm skipping TONS of details but it was the best 2nd Ed story I ever got to see.
