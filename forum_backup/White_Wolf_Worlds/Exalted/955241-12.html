                Throw the trapped Raiton at the Raptor-Cat: 3
                Grab your stuff and run: 3
                Just Run: 1
                Toss the fish to the raptor cat: 1


                Of course, the only option that didn’t lead to Join Battle was “Immediately back slowly and non-threateningly away and leave the predator to its prize,” so…


                Your mind races.

                The raptor cat lets out a deep rumbling growl, warning you away from its delicious find.

                Think, think! The raptor cat is just a cat, it isn’t bright, the amount of money sitting there on the roof is enough to set you up for literal years, the only way to run is backwards off a roof and then climb down the wall, and if the cat comes after you there’s a risk you’ll fall…

                As the raptor cat snarls and crouches, you crouch yourself, preparing to lunge for the raiton so you can grab it and throw it at the cat, hopefully distracting it long enough for you to grab your loot and-

                -and-

                -and it leaps a split second before you do, covering nine yards in a great leap!

                Already prepared to dive for the raiton, you lunge forward and roll, drawing your knife and slashing blindly behind you. You manage to pass under the beast as it lands where you were just crouched, though your back stings with sudden stinging pain as its whirls and leaves four long claw marks right through your nice new shirt and down your back.


                (The Raptor Cat won Initiative, with 7 Initiative to your 6 and the Raiton’s 5. It’s just hit you with a Withering attack which, mercifully, only dealt 2 Withering Damage, raising the Raptor Cat to 10 Initiative and dropping you to 4.)

                You are now crouched with a knife in one hand, a snarling raptor cat in front of you and behind it a long drop. There’s a thrashing raiton slightly behind you and to your left, and just beyond it an eave that you don’t think you can climb before the raptor cat gets you. There’s about four yards of slick roof tiles to your right before a long drop to the cobblestones below. Behind you is about a twelve-yard run before the end of the building-there’s another lower building that way you’re not entirely sure you could jump to from this one-

                There’s a horrible snarl from behind you and you spin your head-another Raptor Cat?!

                The raiton has stopped thrashing and trying to escape. Instead it has opened its toothy beak as wide as it can…and it is making a deep rumbling growl identical to the one the Raptor Cat was just making a few moments ago before it tried to pounce on you. You look back at the real Raptor Cat, and it’s looking around, confused, trying to locate another of its own kind. It’s distracted, sniffing the air and hissing, not paying you its full attention as it looks around for feline competition.

                (The Raiton is…apparently capable of a Distract Gambit? It’s managed to give 4 of its Initiative to you! It has dropped its Initiative to 1 and raised yours to 8! It’s your turn!)

                If you’re going to do something, best do it now!


                We are now in Combat Time! Please be aware that a tied vote here will result in an attempted Flurry.
                ROUND ONE:
                Raptor Cat: 10 Iniative (Acted Already)
                Tavi: 8 Initiative (You! You're the Last Actor in Round 1)
                Raiton: 1 Initiative (Acted Already)

                Distances: You are currently within Close range of the Raptor Cat, and Short range of the Raiton. You are at Short range from the elevated eave on your left, Short range from the edge of the roof on your right, Short range from the edge of the roof ahead of you behind the raptor cat, and at Medium range from the edge of the roof behind you.

                Environmental Factors: Slippery Footing! If you attempt a Combat Move action, you must either stunt in such a way as to avoid traveling across the damp tiles or treat the terrain as Difficult…or you may make a Dex+Athletics roll to treat this as normal terrain. Failure on that roll on a slick and steeply sloped roof is...not recommended.

                Available Combat Resources:
                • Rough twine (1 roll)
                • Your Lucky Pebble
                • Twine Armband
                • Knife (Currently Wielding in your Left Hand)
                • Carrying Bag
                • Two Heavy Clay Jugs (In the Bag)
                • 7 Health Levels
                • 4 Willpower

                Potentially Useful Scenery Scattered About the Roof?
                • Fishhooks (2)
                • A medium sized smoked fish
                • A tangled-up Raiton
                • Three River Dragon Fangs (one big one, four smaller ones-count as Knives)
                • A four-by-four swatch of thick, scaly River Dragon hide
                • A River Dragon Spleen? You think it’s a spleen? Tied in a half-opened bundle. Tied to the Raiton.
                • Assorted River Dragon Bits
                • A Brick Chimney (and a column of thick black smoke above it)
                • A loosely attached drain pipe
                • A Swirling Flock of Croaking Raitons Overhead

                You Have Gained:
                Attributes: (FINALIZED).
                Abilities: (28/28 Allocated)

                Specialties: (2/4 Allocated)
                Merits: (4/7 Allocated)
                Bonus Points: (17/21 Spent)-From now on, every update, we spend at least 1 BP.
                • Dodge 0 to 1: As it turns out, a tumbling roll under a pouncing leopard does in fact teach the basics of Evasion!

                                We've got a tiny advantage in initiative - I say we go for broke.

                                On Turn 1, flail at the raptor cat with your knife (withering attack). We don't need to really hurt it, we just need to hold it at bay and get it to back up just a little. Use Willpower to enhance this attack.
                                On Turn 2, use a Gambit to try to shove the raptor cat off the roof behind it. We only need to push it one range band. Again, use Willpower.

                                Variation 1: Use the satchel as an improvised Smashing weapon instead of shoving on turn 2. Pray we don't break the jars inside.
                                Variation 2: On turn 1, Go grab the drainpipe like Boston123 suggested. Use that as as Smashing weapon on Turn 2. I'm a little leerier of this plan, just because I don't like having to move two range bands on difficult terrain. I can't think of a way to do those moves without crossing the tiles, which means doing risky Dex+Ath checks. Also, our initiative won't be as high when we attempt the Smash, which could leave us at a disadvantage when the raptor cat counterattacks.

                                BTW, are there pictures of raptor cats I can use for reference? I'm having trouble visualizing this creature.
