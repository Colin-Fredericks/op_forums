            Originally posted by SuperG View Post
            *points to the new Sail Charm that means you don't need to favour Athletics to do appropriate Sailor-y athletic stuff*
            The difference is that faith healing is not really a major part of the mythology Exalted draws on. Sure, gods can heal you, but the imagery of a holy man laying his hands on you and declaring you healed in the name of the LORD is heavily associated with two things:

            One, Christian faith-healers in the real world. Exalted doesn't have that faith.

            And two, D&D-style healbot clerics, which Exalted is deliberately trying to get away from.

            On the other hand, in Exalted's setting it is true that a decent sailor is expected to be able to keep his footing on the deck and climb the rigging. Hence, there's a Sail charm to do those things, but there's no Performance-based Faith Healing charm because Faith Healing isn't a major thing in this setting.

            (You could use Presence or Performance to help people with spiritual issues, or psychological ones. Soothing people's souls is part of the purpose of art. But making little Timmy walk again isn't something priests in Exalted's source materials generally do.)

                        I still try to wrap my head about the differences between Presence and Performance.
                        One of which is for me:
                        Performance can be ritualized exchange of words (commoner-priest / priest-god / knight-king / diplomat-diplomat / summoner-demon / actor-audience), where Presence is more spontaneous. A little bit like Melee (you have to prepare a weapon, here it is the ritual) and Brawl (just punch and be good at it).

                        I think Performance does not only to make a character very good at this, but also motivates this behavior in other people, the character talks to (giving them appropriate penalties if they don't oblige).
                        Performance might also help to find the perfect moment to switch from ritual to personal exchange ("Let's talk about this in private"), or make deliberate changes in a ritual (i.e. giving a role in a play the resemblance the king's advisor), which might offset people (feeling honored, or irritated) giving the character appropriate bonuses.

                        Charms could help in the localization of plays and rituals (knowing that you shouldn't dress the hero of a play in blue in this region, as it implies weakness) and in the enforcing or breaking of ritualized behavior.

                        I know, I know, this might overlap with Socialize or even Occult, but somehow everything overlaps slightly.
