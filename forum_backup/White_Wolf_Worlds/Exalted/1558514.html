      Originally posted by Mr.Jaxus View Post
      My question is what book has the best write up for Gem? And any advice on good sources for the particular Death Lords, Fair Folk and Lunars in the area?
      Across the Eight Directions contains this Edition's rendering of Gem. Sworn to the Grave is pretty comprehensive on Deathlords, although I don't think any of them has a very dedicated presence in that part of the South the way the likes of the Bishop and the Lover have in the North, the Dowager and Walker in the East, and Silver Prince in the West. The most South-focused Lunar is actually featured in the Third Edition core (although there might be some reference in Fangs at the Gate I'm not recalling). Unfortunately thus far we don't have a comprehensive overview of the Fair Folk.

      I would note that in this Edition, starmetal has no particular association with dead gods. That's why it's much touted rarity is not compromised by there being vast multitudes of gods.

          If one wants to somewhat keep the concept of departed spirits leaving some sort of remnant or "trophy" in the vein of 1e Scion, i can see some argument for slain elementals of the aerial legion leaving some sort of jade amulets or pseudo-hearthstones, mostly associated with air, but water or other elements should not be out question with some fudging (dust & sandstorms are a thing, lightning sparks fire, etc).

          Also, it sounds to me like Mr.Jaxus is playing a bit with the "Gem is doomed/destroyed" lore/fanon meme from late 1st to 2nd edition. Would that be correct?

          Anyway, if we are speaking of a big, bad, cosmic impromptu battle royale, i'd guess you could have a bit of everything - deities, demons, elementals, fae, ghosts, maybe a Realm legion with a number of dragon-blooded at the helm, a lunar warlord (with or without younger lunar or god-blooded supporters) with its army, a mercenary company led by a circle of Exigents, god-bloods & human-raised elementals, a forbidden god with a host of underbroods, or any of a variety of oddballs one feels like coming up with.

          Gem, as the name implies, is VERY involved with the gemstone international market, perhaps on a "South Africa's mark on diamond trade" level, being also (afaik) the only named source of such clearly supernatural jewels like Yasal crystals (that can imprison immaterial beings, who then must negotiate with the person holding the stone for freedom usually with offers of chamr use) and Dream Opals (that can hold either dreams or the memory of them).

          Based on just this i can see Makarios, the Sigil’s Dreamer, a 2nd circle demon artisan-merchant that works with and from dreams, developing an interest if not outright desire to gain control over the city and its access.
          Same could be said about a variety of fae nobles who might have weird plans to learn more of the peculiar properties of Dream Opal and try to exploit them.
          Beside that, Gem has "rich beyond the dreams of avarice" vibes that would mesh all too well with a "they delved too greedily and too deep" moment drawing the undesirable attention of subterranean elementals with overtones of darkness, volcanic fumes & igneous or obscure forgotten deities worthy of a Dwarven Fortress or Dark Souls' boss.

          Just some rambling thoughts to get the ball rolling.

                  One thing i would be tempted to play with, specially in the context of a Sidereals game, is the idea that there IS some undefined issue/force attempting to bring the city's doom, for unknown reasons and making the discovery of the fact something might be beckoning beings from inside and outside Fate to covet and besiege it, to hopefully unravel the issue spawning such cascading series of unfortunate events.

                  May it relate somehow to Rakan Thulio, his nameless heavenly supporters and the mysterious exalt-like beings known as Getimians?
                  Could the rogue units of the Aerial Legion be somehow connected to them or was their move inspired by a completely unrelated agenda of their own?
                  Or maybe some obscure secret or forgotten truth related to why there's a dead volcano in the deep South with no god or elemental lord to rouse it back to life?


                  But, leaving such minutiae aside, if we are speaking of "every world ending threat was invading Creation and they met up in the area around Gem", i'd say making a threat/invader list of sorts to give you a clearer image of things, with factions, major & minor figures within them, agendas & possible motivations would be of immense help with getting the ball rolling. Just jotting down some types of spirit/entity archetypes of the setting to then expand further, such as my suggestion of Makarios for "demonic overlord" who might have a stake on the Dream Opals of the city and dreams of utmost avarice of the locals for example, can be a start.

                  They may be canon, homebrew, homage-parodies of forces to threaten or help Gem in previous editions - such as a self-conscious soulsteel armor that collects the masses of dead (akin to Asphodel from Arms of Chosen) in mining accidents & hidden assassinations amidst the mines and ore vaults to serve it in an apocalyptic campaign of revenge, for a wink to the First and Forsaken Lion, or a local secret society of artisans that mark their members with gemstones made of yasal that preserve their souls, to them be recovered by their colleagues and added to undying bodies crafted of stone & metals for pseudo-autochtonians/alchemicals and so on. Many, many possibilities there, i'd say.

                      • The Fair Folk are under a complete nutter called 'Colonel Yankee' (note Colonel is his actual name and his title is Dux); who beyond his ice powers seems to believe he is bad stereotype of an American colonel and has somehow made everyone speak Sky Tongue.

                      • There would be two Getimians, both from radicially different versions of Gem and both want to reshape it into their image (which are mutually contradictory).
                      • Several Heaven's Dragons have snuck into Creation and are trying to make contacts with the local Dragon Blooded to improve their gang's resources; unfortunately there is a secret conference of several Realm Imperial Candidates (or more their lackies trying to make deniable back room deals) which is making this situation more complicated.
                      • Three Lunars are trying to figure out what's going on but their all basically kids as their mentor lead the Lunar aligned armies and died fighting.
                      • Several gods from Yu Shan are having a secret meeting to figure out how to work together to get their domains back on track (such as the god of crossbows who wants to take the god of bows down a peg or two to allow more crossbow usage in Creation); someone was supposed to bring some smuggled Flame of Exigence but that's gone missing.
                      • Autochthonians were driven underground and are planning a resurgence with the local Mountain Folk; this would be penultimate challenge of the campaign as they are secretly collecting souls to make into Soulsteel so their Soulsteel Alchemical can go from Colossus to full patropolus/metropolus under the city to serve as a beachhead for the Locust Crusade.
                      • A Circle of Solars is trying to save the day, and by that I mean make a bigger mess but get out on top. Not sure what the details are but some ideas include:
                        • Dawn is "INVINICIBLE SWORD PRINCESS!", the world's most beautiful woman who also comes back to life each morning if slain due to an artifact fused to her bones.
                        • Twilight basically wants to make Pokemon to have them replace all forms of warfare and blood sport.
                        • Night/Eclipse is an unobtusive and unimpressive person who talks successfully people into doing horrible things for fun.
                      • A doomed love triangle between the Night/Ecilpse solar, a Day/Moonshadow abyssal (gothic romantic who is forced by the Deathlords to lure people into doomed romances) and a Nadir/Penumbra Infernal who has a sidereal parent and wants to replace the Immaculate Dragons with the Ebon Dragon in the Immaculate Faith to get back at being forced to be a monk when they didn't want to live in Yu Shan.
                      • If the players allow me to run fan-made exalted types (like Nocturnals) or Apocryphal Exalted (like Dream-Souled, The Spoken, Hearteaters and Umbral Exalted) I might throw a few confused members of them into the mix.
