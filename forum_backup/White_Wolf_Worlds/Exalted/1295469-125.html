                        06-21-2019, 11:56 AM
                        Originally posted by shadowninja View Post
                        I have a few questions about Wyld-Shaping Technique:

                        1) Wyld-Shaping Technique have many options what to do (create Land, Mundane Wealth, Magic Materials, and others), but you can apply all options in same phase? I must specify what option I applying in each phase?
                        Each phase, you can only do one option.

                        2) How I create features in the land, like a castle or city? How I spend success to do it?
                        The "Everything Else" section covers this - you first create the land, as usual, and then use an additional phase to add new features to it, or expand on existing features.

                        3) If I want create Mundane Resources in the form of Gold Mine (Iron Mine, or something like that), how I do that?
                        A mine would be better handled as specific land than "mundane wealth."

                        4) And people, I can spend success to create then? And the "quality"?
                        When you create non-specific land, you have no control over whether or not people arise alongside the land. When you create specific land, you can purposeful create humans to occupy it, although creating especially skilled mortals requires the upgrade Charm Hero-Induction Method.
