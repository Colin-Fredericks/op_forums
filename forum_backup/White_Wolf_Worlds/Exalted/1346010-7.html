    There's a challenge and a challenge.

    I don't run combat-heavy games.
    I like to do a serious fight, but one that the PCs will clearly win (as opposed to say fighting some human soldiers, which I'd just get a Dawn to make a single Attribute+Ability roll to defeat rather than bother running a combat) every 3-4 sessions. That was never much of a problem.

    I like to do a challenging fight (that needs to, in-genre, be really impressive, like fight some mighty heroes or a behemoth) that stretches the PCs and gives them the opportunity to make use of their 25 combat charms, or Death Ray, or whatever, and feel like a total badass because they defeated this extremely difficult and mighty foe, at the end of a story.

    The first type is fine (couple of e3 Dragonblood, battle group of first circle demons, a Solar, etc) for high-level circles.

    The second type I found quite difficult with just the Core Book. The only way to do it was to a)attack 1-2 PCs alone, which is boring for the other players), b)have Ahlat and 100 war-gods attack half the party while the other half were elsewhere, c)use a circle of experienced Solars, d)use the First and Forsaken Lion.*

    *To be fair, he was a social challenge, but one of the PCs thought he was a combat challenge, so grapped the other Lunar and ran away. Sadly. I was looking forward to the Lion's centaur-ghost underling Chiron becoming the Lunar's new sifu. Oh well.


    This isn't a combat-heavy situation; it was mostly a game about politics, changing whole societies and the effects of such, etc. But if there's no awesome fights against mighty enemies, what's even the point of a Dawn? Or the sorceress with Death Ray? Or the Full Moon?

    Nowadays, there are actually rules for the kind of things who can challenge a circle of E5 Solars in a fight besides other Solars; Lunars, Dragonblood, a giant volcano monster. But there's not much that doesn't need a lot of preparation (there's a bit). Making an entire Hearth of E4 Dragonblood to take on your PCs is a lot of work, running it is a lot of work, and it'll probably get slow as you're checking stuff which makes the fight less fun.

    That's why I'm hoping to see more things on the lines of Ahlat and Octavian, or preferably even better, like Third-Circle Demons.


    I'll also say I'm not just talking about challenging the PCs at combat. It's also about challenging them in other arenas. That's a bit easier than for combat, but it was still an issue when there were no rules for, say, high-level Dragonblood and Lunar social charms and world-affecting magic. It's better now.
