    ~ Lunar Fashion Sense in Third Edition ~

    03-29-2014, 10:59 AM

    Behold! A handsome weretiger in a classy red hanbok! Now now, while I know that the writer has stated there will be Lunars who come from the city, I still worry about the majority of artwork will still feature Roid-Rage Monster in Loincloth and Bikini that plagued the MoEP:Lunars book.

    I understand that not every Lunars has moonsilver clothing so it's more logical to wear as little as possible so you don't have to waste money buying new shirt everytime you shapeshift, but seeing a Lunar wearing stereotype barbarian costume just make me roll my eyes and think "Lunar: the Werewolf" every time.

    So I guess that it'll be great if we see more Lunar who dress in Resource 5 cloth in Ex3 and less seashell-bra No Moon or shirtless + fur-skirt combo Full Moon.

    And I'm out before this turn into a DB's imperialism or Lunar theme thread again.

    The no.1 fan of Demetheus. I also draw Exalted things and is looking for commission works ~

      Honestly, I'm pretty happy to have the barbarian aesthetic back, because I like barbarians and think 2nd ed had too much City-States and not enough cool barbarian tribes and cultures. I can see that there might be a worry that 3rd ed Barbarians will be back to bad old "cities bad! Raiding good!" 1st ed Lunars.
      But actually, of all the Lunars I've seen played, I don't remember any that were really much into the barbarian aesthetic, in practice. One No Moon mongol... but he reads books and wears egyptian clothes, so... Others have included an Immaculate Monk, a girl from Nexus, and a committed member of the Cult of the Illuminated. 3 out of 4 of them weren't even cannibals! What sort of Lunars don't even rip out people's still-beating hearts as a sacrifice to their dark Goddess and consume it?

      But saying that: Barbarian doesn't have to mean bikinis and furry jockstraps. Honestly, it shouldn't, few barbarian cultures ever actually wore that kind of thing.
      A wide variety of fur-based or other primitive clothing would be fine with me.
      Actually, I'd rather see Lunars flat-out naked than wearing silly furry bikinis (I imagine their genitals would have to be blocked by strategic object placement, but whatever, I'm looking for realistic costumes, not porn). If you spend a lot of time in animal form, thinking like an animal, and you're worried about breaking clothes by mutating, well for the more barbaric Lunars why even bother with clothes? A lot of barbarian cultures in the real world had a fairly lax attitude towards wearing clothes (actually, it's more that cultures with a lax attitude to clothes tended to be considered to be barbarians, since barbarian is essentially a pejorative label).

      STing Bronze Age Exalted
