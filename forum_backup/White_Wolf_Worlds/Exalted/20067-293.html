            1. The idea is just that the characters in-setting don't have the ability to freely invent any number of new Charms the way you can (generally) do with your regular splat Charms.                                                     Originally posted by AtG View Post

                                                    So, the language for Attunement technically refers to Exalts... and so does Martial Arts. Possibly an oversight, but still... weird?

                                                    Also, I wonder if the mote pool from Prophet-Uplifting Evocation respires, or is it just one-use-and-then-done?
                                                    Well, martial arts charms specifically state that they can be learned by "any of the Exalted (and certain other powerful supernatural beings)". "Certain other" suggests that they're not learnable by just anything that has a mote pool, only those specific supernatural beings that are noted to be able to. The wording regarding attunement isn't so specific. It uses the word "Exalt" and "Exalted user" when discussing attunement, but then, players are assumed to be playing Exalted characters, and the text regarding the mechanical effects of attunement simply state "the wearer". The Nephwrack in the antagonists chapter uses a soulsteel daiklaive, and Mist, the Eternal Revolutionary's sword is at least similar to an artifact in power, if it is not specifically one, so it's unlikely that artifacts are supposed to be restricted to Exalts.

                                                    However, if you find it undesirable for targets of Prophet-Uplifting Evocation to have access to artifacts, I'm sure it's not a big deal to declare them off-limits. Most artifact weapons and armor have an attunement cost higher than the base 4 motes that their pool starts with, so the solar would have to spend the motes to increase their pools to use them, and even then, it leaves them with very little left to use Unhesitating Dedication. Creating custom charms to grant your prophets further powers to spend their motes on seems like it would be valid design space.

                                                    I believe that "permanently empowers" and the fact that it grants a mote pool, rather than a number of motes implies that yes, the prophets regain motes following the usual mote recovery rules.
