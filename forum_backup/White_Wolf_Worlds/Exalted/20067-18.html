                        (Sol in Yu-Shan, I miss the Wiki!)
                        Transformative Clothing and Armor is from Scroll of Fallen Races (page 26). The artifact does what the name suggests; when the wearer spends a mote with a miscellaneous action and a Wits+Craft:Wood roll, it transforms into any garment that the wearer had seen or designed (failure means it looks significantly different to what was imagined), expanding and contracting to anything from a single glove or cap to a complete and elaborate outfit. This transformation includes cleaning and repairs. A one dot artifact gives you transformative clothing, two dots gives you a suit of transformative armor which retains the listed stats regardless of form.

                        ... seriously, only one or two dots for that? Sure, there's better protective gear (and you don't get magical material bonuses), but the sheer utility of having underwear that can become any clothing you need (including backup armor that does not look like armor) when you have to go incognito should not be underestimated.
