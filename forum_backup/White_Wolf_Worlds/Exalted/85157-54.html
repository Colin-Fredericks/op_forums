                        The keys are certainly mysterious. As you say, I don't think possessing one gives Power Level +5 passively, but it certainly does do something, as Allison has proven. I myself wonder if Mottom's become-a-tree spell are due to her using her key, or if they're her own magic that's been empowered by the key (or maybe not even that). The keys certainly has a social implication as well, giving their wielders lots of time and resources to develop their personal talents if nothing else, but that privilege is also in the end bought through power. It seems to be a complex system of causes and effects.

                                                            Yes. If you follow the tumblr for the comic, the author has repeatedly indicated that most of the Demiurges (and Mottom in particular) have a tragic backstory. Of course, the fact that she became exactly the sort of tyrant she hates is part of the tragedy. (She even references it herself when telling Allison what will happen to her.)

                                                            Beyond that, going "haha, jk, it was all a lie" would undermine this last scene with Allison confronting her in particular.
