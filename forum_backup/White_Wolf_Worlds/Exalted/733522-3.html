                Would love to hear some feedback on this working:
                A Guildsman has an extremely vain woman he wants to court. He offers her gems and silver, but she considers those gifts insignificant, mostly because the Guildsman has plenty of money: this was no challenging task for him. So, the Guildsman goes to the PC, a sorcerer, and asks the Twilight for a gift.

                The idea was as follows: a feather-dress made out of feathers of peacocks, with each feather having been bathed in the waters of a Demesne that makes things cleaner and more beautiful.
                The mechanical effects of this dress would allow the wearer to only take a -1 penalty when influencing multiple targets (normally a -3, see page 221), and all non-verbal/body language influence only gives the target a +1 Resolve (normally +2, also page 221). Both of these effects only work if they make sense and the dress is included in the description of the stunt.

                I feel like this is a Sorcerous Working, Terrestrial Ambition 1 or 2. However, I think that this dress should be able to do something a bit cooler, adding perhaps one more power or trick... right now it's rather situational. I'd love ideas. I would like to keep it a Terrestrial Working, however.
                Last edited by wern212; 09-18-2017, 05:07 AM. Reason: Added the line about it remaining a Terrestrial Working.
