    [Sidereals:WFHL] Veiled Arts of Heaven : Sapphire Veil of Passion

    06-08-2016, 04:32 PM
    (with apologies to Holden)


    SIDEREAL MARTIAL ARTS: SAPPHIRE VEIL OF PASSION

    Also known as the Twice Born Style due to having been independently discovered twice, Sapphire Veil of Passion draws inspiration from the fire of lust and passion that burns within all living beings. Many younger Sidereals make jokes (or secretly wonder) at what the training for this Veiled Art of Heaven entails, and ridicule those who devote themselves to its study. This ridicule usually lasts only until they meet with a practitioner; even the ones that don't come away half in love with the mentor that just thrashed them are forced to admit to the style's efficaciousness.

    Weapons and armor: Sapphire Veil of Passion permits the use of the Urumi, or ribbon sword, as well as the artifact equivalent, the floating ribbon. It is incompatible with armor, but does traditionally allow a buckler to be worn as well.
    Urumi: Light melee weapon; Lethal, Martial Arts, Disarming, Flexible, Reaching
    Floating Ribbon: Light artifact melee weapon; Lethal, Martial Arts, Disarming, Flexible, Reaching
    Buckler: Light melee weapon; Melee, Shield

    Satisfying Idle Fantasy
    Cost: 3m, 2i; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Dual
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None

    Passion begins as fantasy, which demands satisfaction. The martial artist kindles and satisfies such a fantasy in a single motion, leaving her victim stunned and confused. Satisfying Idle Fantasy may be used to enhance either a withering attack or a withering savage when in control of a grapple. The martial artist adds her Appearance to her Strength for determining raw damage. Additionally, if this attack's damage exceeds her victim's Resolve, it also instills a tie of lust or fascination towards her in him, or enhances an existing tie of the same. If her victim is crashed by an attack instilling this tie, or Satisfying Idle Fantasy is used to enhance a withering savage, the cost to resist this influence is increased to 3 willpower.

    Sapphire Veil of Passion Form
    Cost: 8m; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Form
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Satisfying Idle Fantasy

    Assuming the Sapphire Veil of Passion Form, the martial artist kindles the blue flame of passion in her, and drawing the attention of all upon the field. Anyone who has a positive tie towards her subtracts successes equal to the rank of the tie when they take any action other than rushing or attempting to grapple her, as well as from their defense against her attacks; additionally, she gains one initiative and two additional motes per turn in which she spends at Close range with an opponent who holds a positive intimacy towards her, drawing on the strength of her enemy's ardor.
    Special activation rules: If the martial artist successfully creates or strengthens a positive tie towards herself in an enemy after joining battle, she may reflexively enter the Sapphire Veil of Passion Form.

    Yellow Dragon Flight
    Cost: (1wp); Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Dual
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Sapphire Veil of Passion Form

    The martial artist draws on the hot, active element of sexuality, thrusting forward with an irresistible attack. Yellow Dragon Flight enhances Satisfying Idle Fantasy; by spending 1 willpower when using Satisfying Idle Fantasy, the attack also becomes unblockable and ignores soak. A successful attack enhanced by Yellow Dragon Flight against an enemy who holds a major or defining positive tie towards the martial artist awards 1 willpower.

    Black Dragon Coils
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Sapphire Veil of Passion Form

    The martial artist draws on the cool, passive element of sexuality, accepting her opponent's attack and smothering the heat of it away. Black Dragon Flight enhances her defense; if she successfully defends herself from the attack, she steals 1 initiative from the attacker; if the attack is successful, then the martial artist may reflexively attempt to instill an intimacy of lust or facination towards her in him. Black Dragon Coil rewards two motes when the martial artist steals initiative from an enemy who holds a major or defining positive tie towards the martial artist.

    Thousand Desires Ribbon
    Cost: (1wp); Mins: Essence 4
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Yellow Dragon Flight, Black Dragon Coils

    The heat and the cool of the martial artist's passion combines to form a ribbon of electric blue light, arcing around them in a reflection of her thousand aching desires. When she enters Sapphire Veil of Passion Form, the martial artist may spend a point of willpower to activate Thousand Desires Ribbon. The ribbon floats and moves around the martial artist, reflecting her desires from moment to moment; each turn, the ribbon acts on the martial artist's initiative, allowing her to take one of the following actions in addition to her normal action:
    * She may set the ribbon to defend herself; until her next action, she gains the benefit of Black Dragon Coils on all defenses.
    * She may make a withering attack an enemy out to Short range; this attack is automatically enhanced with Satisfying Idle Fantasy.
    * She may attempt to perform a disarm, distract or grapple gambit against an enemy out to Short range.

    Charm usage automatically granted by the ribbon do not cost the martial artist any motes

                          Warning- long post is long and goes into brain space detail.

                          As an internally imposed constraint, WFHL SMA styles are (by their nature) shorter than original styles. In compensation, for power scaling, they're intended to be 'pushed' for their Essence minimums; they should be roughly 1.5 Essence ahead of the curve.

                          Here's my notes/summary of the original style's charms:
                          • Into Infinite Depths: Learn a fucking lot about someone
                          • Eternal Infatuation Dance: Perfect dodge & move to close range of attacker, rush enhancer, once per scene
                          • Yellow Dragon Flight: Unblockable, unsoakable attack that ignites victim’s essence
                          • Black Dragon Coils: Perfect parry, restores (virtue channels? willpower?)
                          • Dissolution of Virtue: Until you move, do not suffer penalties to defense
                          • Satisfying Idle Fantasy: Attacks impose or enhance positive intimacies towards you
                          • Sapphire Veil of Passion Form: Gain protection from intimacies others hold towards you, regenerate Essence quickly
                          • Someone Else’s Consequences: Reflexively terminate Psyche, Poison or Disease, transferring them to a victim.
                          • Silken Rope Trick: Red Rule Violation: The Charm
                          • Joy in Ownership: Trap someone with defining intimacy to love and obey, pysche
                          • Sapphire Binding Pattern: Seriously, you win grapples forever
                          • Memories of Intimacy: Make an attack unblockable and undodgable against someone you have beaten, who has an intimacy towards you, or whom you own.
                          • Thousand Desires Ribbon: Supercharge Form; reflexive attacks, out to short range, increase severity of wound penalties


                          In early tinkering with the style, I decided I wanted Sapphire Veil to focus on withering / initiative control, as compared with the more decisive focus that Charcoal March of Spiders had.
                          I also decided that focusing on exploiting intimacies others have towards you would be a sufficiently rich region to mine.
                          I also wanted SVoD to be a more traditionally structured style- having a Form and a capstone (as compared with the all forms PAoC and incomplete CMoS.)
                          (CMoS and SVoD are both probably the most 'normal' SMAs- and as a consequence, the conversation of both comes across as the most down to earth. The big piece of weirdness from CMoS, independent actions, is getting eaten by OSoI)

                          Of the original charms...

                          * Dissolution of Virtue and Into Infinite Depths got cut for being out out of theme.
                          * Silken Rope Trick got cut for being Red Rule Violation: The Charm

                          * Satisfying Idle Fantasy served as an excellent entry into the style.
                          * Yellow Dragon Flight and Black Dragon Coils worked well with the ideas of the style, as well as mirroring one another.

                          That left the Form and a capstone.

                          * Someone Else's Consequence, Eternal Infatuation Dance, and Sapphire Binding Pattern didn't feel like capstones.

                          That left Joy in Ownership, Memories of Intimacy, and Thousand Desires Ribbon as potential capstones.

                          I ruled out Memories of Intimacy as being kind of win-more (unblockable attack against someone you've already beaten) or redundent with Yellow Dragon Flight- that left Joy in Ownership and Thousand Desires Ribbon.

                          When I saw the Form's overdrive mote generation, that pushed me to thinking of SVoD as also being incredibly generative- which echoes on the theme of desire and sexuality.

                          Joy in Ownership wants to be a finishing move- a decisive only gambit you use to trap someone into being enthralled by you. Thousand Desires Ribbon wants to be a form enhancer, accentuating the generative qualities of the style.

                          I decided that, between the two, Joy in Ownership would generate more bad feelings of loss of player agency and trouble with creating an army of lovesick thralls, whereas Thousand Ribbons Desire could be made pure upside.

                          So- thus, selected. From there, it just became a matter of finding the right inspiration for the particulars.

                          Now- all that said -Black Dragon Coils probably got hit too hard with the nerf bat. The original form of letting you freely trade initiative to avoid getting hit was too strong, but I think I overcorrected.

                          Maybe if you hit someone who used BDC, you have to spend a willpower or develop an intimacy?

                                                  After a brief discussion with our fine writer here, I've decided to try and make a few other homebrew charms to 'tack on.' These are meant to make the SVoP style function more autonomously/more similar to it's 2e form, and as such, should be considered carefully before being allowed into your game, or used with other MA abilities, as this can easily shatter your balance harder than a Glorious Solar Homebrew Technique

                                                  That said, I could still use some feedback moving things into 3E style.

                                                  SIDEREAL MARTIAL ARTS: SAPPHIRE VEIL OF PASSION: RUBY RIBBON EXPANSION



                                                  Eternal Infatuation Dance
                                                  Cost: 6m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 3
                                                  Type: Supplemental
                                                  Keywords: Perilous
                                                  Duration: Instant
                                                  Prerequisite Charms: Yellow Dragon Flight

                                                  The martial artist draws on what she has learned from the heat of passion, and of the weaknesses it creates in others. Once per Scene, this Charm may be activated in response to any single attack the martial artist is aware of. The martial artist vanishes in a swirl of collapsing sapphire ribbons, appearing in the close range band of her attacker. In the case of long-range social attacks, this Charm’s maximum range is (Essencex5) Miles. This traditionally avoids all effects of the initial attack, but the movement offers no protection against hazards where she appears. This charm cannot be reset via normal means.

                                                  An Essence 5 repurchase allows this charm to maintain the proximity: As long as the martial artist does not intentionally move away from her target, she may stay in the close range band, regardless of natural or supernatural movement capabilities employed by her target.
                                                  Authors thoughts: I liked the idea of an emergency defense, but there wasn't a single aspect I wasn't okay with turning down, as it technically is a once-per-scene perfect defense, so far as I can tell. Additionally, the unshakeable pursuit option sounded nice, so I moved that to an E5 repurchase.
                                                  Silken Rope trick
                                                  Cost: 10m; 1WP Mins: Essence 4
                                                  Type: Reflexive
                                                  Keywords: Perilous, Psyche
                                                  Duration: One Scene
                                                  Prerequisite Charms: Sapphire Veil of Passion Form, Black Dragon Coils

                                                  While in the Sapphire Veil of Passion Form, the Martial Artist gains additional insight into passion itself, and how it can be manipulated by those with the skill to do so. The martial artist may activate this charm when attempting to make a grapple, or in response to another attempting to enter a grapple with her. She is unable to make Rush or Disengage actions while this Charm is in effect. More beneficially, she adds her Appearance rating in dice to attempts to establish or maintain a grapple. Additionally, anyone who enters a grapple with the martial artist, must immediately pay one willpower, or instils a tie of lust towards her in the attacker, or enhances an existing tie of the same.
                                                  Authors thoughts: Red Rule violation: The Charm. Obviously, this isn't acceptable at some tables, and if so, that's fine. However it exists as another means to instil and strengthen intimacies, but with a fairly high cost and pre-requisite to try and offset the idea.



                                                  Thousand Desires Ribbon ((Minor Addendum))
                                                  Cost: 1wp; Mins: Essence 4
                                                  Type: Permanent
                                                  Keywords: None
                                                  Duration: Permanent
                                                  Prerequisite Charms: Yellow Dragon Flight, Black Dragon Coils

                                                  The heat and the cool of the martial artist's passion combines to form a ribbon of electric blue light, arcing around them in a reflection of her thousand aching desires. When she enters Sapphire Veil of Passion Form, the martial artist may spend a point of willpower to activate Thousand Desires Ribbon. The ribbon floats and moves around the martial artist, reflecting her desires from moment to moment; each turn, the ribbon acts on the martial artist's initiative, allowing her to take one of the following actions in addition to her normal action:

                                                  * She may set the ribbon to defend herself; until her next action, she gains the benefit of Black Dragon Coils on all defenses.
                                                  * She may make a withering attack an enemy out to Short range; this attack is automatically enhanced with Satisfying Idle Fantasy.
                                                  * She may attempt to perform a disarm, distract or grapple gambit against an enemy out to Short range. Grapples made with this attack may be enhanced by Silken Rope Trick as normal, but the user must still activate the charm as normal for the bonus to apply.

                                                  Charms specified by the action taken with the ribbon do not cost the martial artist any motes.
                                                  An Essence 5 repurchase will allow the Martial artist to spend an additional willpower when activating the form, allowing one additional action. This cannot be enhanced further.
                                                  Authors thoughts: Fine as it is, but wanted to clear up no silken rope freebies. I also wanted to add in the ability for an E5 repurchase to make this power capable of more, but ultimately, this isn't what I consider the real strength of the form.
                                                  Joy In Ownership
                                                  Cost: 20m, 2WP; Mins: Essence 5
                                                  Type: Simple
                                                  Keywords: Psyche
                                                  Duration: Instant
                                                  Prerequisite Charms: Thousand Desires Ribbon, Eternal Infatuation Dance, Silken Rope Trick

                                                  The martial artist has learned what is assumed to be every appreciable aspect of passion, and as such, can enforce it's mantra with utmost potency upon the undefended. The martial artist must touch an individual whom is unconscious, or made so by the martial artist. If the subject has a major or defining positive tie towards the martial artist, with a significant effort, she can further reinforce this, creating a new defining intimacy of "Love and Obey." This unnatural mental influence can be resisted only by spending an amount of willpower equal to the target's essence. Otherwise, it must be weakened and removed in a manner similar to other intimacies. While this intimacy is in effect however, other positive intimacies towards the martial artist cannot be weakened or removed short of unnatural mental influence.
                                                  Author Thoughts: Woo, silken rope trick was bad? Controversy out the wazoo in a single charm. That said, I've discussed this with some players, who consider this to be the true 'capstone.' of the style. While I disagree, I can see why it's considered to be an iconic element thereof. I might make this Essence ??? And require a special Essence 6+ teacher to learn. that said, I've bumped up the cost, and allowed it to function a little less like a slavery charm, and more like a knot to hold the other intimacies in place. No memory rewrites, no metaphysical slavery, no multi-scene willpower expenditure.
                                                  Memories of Intimacy
                                                  Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 5
                                                  Type: Supplemental
                                                  Keywords: None
                                                  Duration: Instant
                                                  Prerequisite Charms: Joy In Ownership

                                                  The martial artist is an undisputed master of passion, and any affected by her passing are affected by it, often never able to completely overcome the memories and the effects they have on them. This charm supplements a Martial Arts attack, rendering it both unblockable and undodgeable if any of the following apply:
                                                  • The martial artist has defeated the target in combat at some point in the past (defined as having rendered the target unconscious, or the target having voluntarily admitted defeat or surrendered to the martial artist on the field of battle, even if only in a sparring match).
                                                  • The target carries an Intimacy of any sort for the martial artist.
                                                  • The Martial artist successfully performs a disarm, distract, or unhorse gambit against the target in the same scene.
                                                  If two or more of these criteria apply to the target, the attack is also considered unexpected. Memories of Intimacy may only be used once per scene, but may be reset by increasing the strength of an intimacy held towards you.
                                                  Author thoughts: Here we are. What I consider to be the capstone of the style, is that recurring fights become very dangerous. Aside from a little trimming related to the controversial charms above, I actually felt this charm was too expensive for a single attack booster in Ex3, and shaved three motes off the cost. That said, this is something I'm not quite sure how it affects balance, as unblockable/undodgeable attacks have been nonexistent in my games so far.

                                                  Summary: A lot of these could be particularly powerful. but by splattering it with the psyche and perilous keywords where I thought appropriate, it makes a master of the SVoP style singularly deadly, if not by the traditional means, which is exactly what I think a Sideral Master should be. That said, this absolutely reeks of controversial tones, and has only had the most basic of once-overs for balance, so revisions will definitely be forthcoming.
