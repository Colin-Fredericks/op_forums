                                                    What does the "Grappling" tag do?

                                                    Page 586 says it can be used for Grapple Gambits. But Gambits, as Decisive Attacks, don't use weapon stats. "Unarmed" has both the "Grappling" and "Natural" tags -- meaning you don't need a Draw/Ready action to launch a Grapple Gambit even if you're wielding a two-handed weapon without the Grappling tag.
