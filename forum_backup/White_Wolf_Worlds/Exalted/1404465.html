    Mastery through Artifice

    07-24-2020, 06:38 PM
    In another thread I proposed a possible means for some Exalts to access the Mastery Keyword through the use of a Heathstone (http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...master-s-stone). The more I think about it though, the less convinced I am that it is the right way to go.

    However I do remember that there was an Artifact back in 1st or 2nd Ed that enhanced an Exalt's use of Martial Arts... and I am trying to remember what it was and if it might be possible to rework the idea to serve this purpose. Sadly I don't have access to my 1st and 2nd Ed books at this time and can remember neither the name nor the book it was located in (I would like to say Oadenol's Codex or Wonders of the Lost Age (Books of Sorcery: Volume 1 and 3)... but I am unsure of either. If I remember correctly, they appeared as inscribed bracers that described martial art forms. I want to say they were called Kata Bracers or something along those lines... but I simply don't recall them well enough to be sure.

    If I remember correctly they were often made with traces of Starmetal and enhanced the use of Martial Art Charms in some way. I think this is likely the best way to achieve the affect I am after, namely allowing non-Solar and non-Sidereal martial artists to access the effects of the Mastery Keyword on Charms. That said, I an not entirely sure how powerful an Artifact this originally was or should be for 3rd Edition.

    Given that there are no hard rules as to what a given level of Artifacts can innately achieve (there really is no difference in the mundane applications between a 3 dot Daiklave and a 5 dot Daiklave), I have looked at the kinds of evocations that can be produced by the various levels. 3 dot Artifacts allow one to learn evocations that would be roughly equivalent to what they would normally be able to produce with an equivalent skill level. 4 dot Artifacts however allow for more potent effects than would normally be available to an Exalt of equivalent skill level, allowing for a unique playstyle for the owner. I would see accessing the Mastery Keyword as being soundly within the 4 dot range of ability... as the effects that Mastery produces tends to make a Martial Art charm more potent than what is normally possible for an Exalt and allows a very unique playstyle that would not normally be available to many Exalts.I do not see this as being the same as the almost overwhelming might that a 5 dot Artifact often provides, thus I am fairly certain that a 4 dot Artifact would be sufficient for this effect... powerful, but not overtly game changing (any more so than a Solar or Sidereal having access to these effects naturally is).

    If anyone can tell me the name of the original Artifact I am thinking of and where to locate its write-up (or provide a rough description of it), I might be able to refine this idea better.

      Perfected Kata Bracers are in Oadenol's Codex. I can't recall which, if any, book they were in in 1E.

      ****

      PERFECTED KATA BRACERS - 4 dots

      Perfected kata bracers are prayer strips crafted of a single magical material and curled into bracers.

      The strips’ sutras are starmetal engravings, except in starmetal bracers—they use orichalcum.

      Attunement costs eight motes. When the character activates a Form-type Charm, the sutras inscribed on the bracers glow and lift from the artifact to engulf the character in light for a brief moment. For the rest of the scene, she gains several benefits.

      She adds her Essence to the accuracy, damage and defense of her unarmed attacks and of attacks made with the active Form-type Charm’s particular weapons. She may choose to inflict bashing or lethal damage at will and she may parry lethal attacks unarmed. In addition:

      Orichalcum bracers let Solars inflict aggravated damage on creatures of darkness.

      Moonsilver bracers provide Lunars additional flexibility, so they ignore cover and shields.

      Jade bracers add Essence to Dragon-Blooded’s soak and 2 to the Rate of their attacks.

      Starmetal eases the flow of martial Essence for Sidereals, reducing the cost of activating Charms of the same style by two motes (minimum 1).

      Soulsteel makes an Abyssal’s attack fatal if it inflicts more levels of damage than a mortal victim’s Stamina.

      Perfected kata bracers provide very significant effects. A skilled martial artist wielding them in battle has a significant advantage over her opponents, especially when wielding artifact martial-arts weapons. They suffer the small drawback of being incredibly obvious. If their benefits weren’t limited to the battlefield, the bracers might be Artifact 5.

      ****

      My 3E homebrew suggestion would be something like this:

      Perfected Kata Bracers - 4 dots

      Perfected kata bracers are prayer strips crafted of orichalcum and curled into bracers. Their sutras are engraved in starmetal.

      When the character activates a Form-type Charm, the sutras inscribed on the bracers glow and lift from the artifact to engulf the character in golden light, equivalent to a Burning Anima, for the duration of the Form-type Charm. Whilst so engulfed the character may choose to inflict bashing or lethal damage at will and she may parry lethal and ranged attacks unarmed.

      Characters who are Resonant with either Orichalcum or Starmetal are Resonant with Perfected Kata Bracers (even if they are Dissonant with the other material). Characters who are not Resonant with either material are Neutral, unless they are Dissonant with one or the other material, in which case they are Dissonant. Solars and Sidereals are always Resonant with Perfected Kata Bracers, regardless of other effects.

      Perfected Kata Bracers cost 3m to attune allow the awakening of the following Evocations:

      Tiger-And-Bear Unity
      Preqs: Essence 2, one Form type Charm
      Cost: - (+5m)
      Type: Permenant, Resonant

      On activating a Form-type Charm, the user may spend an additional 5m to ignore the Terrestrial keyword for Charms in that style for as long as the Form remains active.

      Resonant: Ignore this Evocation's minimum Essence requirement.

      Reach-Heaven-Through-Violence
      Preqs: Essence 2, one Form type Charm
      Cost: - (+5m, 1WP)
      Type: Permenant, Resonant

      On activating a Form-type Charm, the user may spend an additional 5m 1WP to activate this Evocation. So long as the user is not limited by the Terrestrial keyword for Charms of this style, they may activate that style's Mastery effects as long as they remain in Form.

      Resonant: Ignore this Evocation's minimum Essence requirement.

      Golden Monkey Enlightenment
      Prereqs: Essence 3, one Evocation this Charm enhances
      Cost: - (+1WP)
      Type: Permenant, Resonant

      When activating Tiger-And-Bear Unity or Reach-Heaven-Through-Violence the user may send an additional +1WP to activate Golden Monkey Enlightenment, those Evocations extend their benefits to all Martial Arts Charms the user knows, not just those of the Form's style.

      Resonant: This Evocation unlocks automatically for free once its prerequisites are met.

      Shining Strike
      Prereqs: Essence 1
      Cost: 3m
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Uniform, Mastery, Dissonant, Resonant

      Whilst engulfed in the bracer's golden light, the user may pay 3m to add a single success to a Martial Arts attack or raise their Martial Arts based Parry by one. If the Martial Arts Attack is placed in a Flurry, reduce the Flurry penalty to -1 (for the Attack only).

      This Evocation unlocks automatically for free the first time the wielder uses a Form-type Charm whilst attuned to the Bracers.

      Mastery: If the user has access to Mastery of the Martial Art being used, add two successes or raise Parry by two instead. The user's Decisive attacks also deal Aggravated damage to Creatures of Darkness and Mortals.

      Dissonant: This Evocation does not automatically unlock and must be purchased as normal.

      Resonant: The wielder always uses the Mastery effects of this Evocation, even if they do not have Mastery of the Martial Art being used.

                  JohnDoe244 Thanks for the information on the 2nd Ed Perfected Kata Bracers.

                  As for your take on them for 3rd Ed... it is a little lack-luster in my opinion.

                  The two Exalts that are normally resonant with this artifact to get the benefit of 3 of the 4 Evocations (in other words don't need to wait until Essence 2+ to learn them) have no need for those Evocations as they naturally have access to the Mastery Keyword and are not subject to the Terrestrial Keyword.

                  As for the last of the 4 Evocations, the one that is automatically learned, again, the Solars and Sidereal have more efficient means of gaining a success or raising their Parry than a 3 mote cost effect. In short... this artifact does not live up to a 4 dot cost for these Exalts... in providing a game-play style change for the character by unlocking effects that they couldn't do on their own. Even the Mastery effect of doubling what the normal Evocation provides and doing Aggravated damage is less than impressive for the costs to get.

                  As for other Exalts, it is rather over-punishing for little real gain. Only one type of Exalt really needs the Evocation that allows one to ignore the effects of the Terrestrial Keyword... Dragon-Bloods. Beyond that, you have rather steep costs for the three Evocations +5 motes and 1 or 2 Willpower, thus a Dragon-Blooded will be spending a significant portion of their Essence Pool (10 motes) and force of will (1 or 2 Willpower) just to access the relatively minor gains that Mastery provides... and only so long as they are able to remain under the effects of a Form Charm. This is in addition to the fact that they need 2+ Essence just to learn those Evocations.

                  Yes, a standard starting Terrestrial will start with Essence 2 (while other Exalts do not), but this means that they will have to give up 2 of their starting charms to learn the basic Evocations... in addition to however many charms it would cost them to learn a Martial Art to the Form Charm (assuming they have the Essence rating to reach the Form Charm). For those who start off at Essence 1 (young Dragon-Bloods and most other Exalts by default)... having to wait until Essence 2 can sometimes be a fair wait. Added to the relative uselessness of the Artifact in the hands of the two Exalts that would have the easiest time using it... and it just seems a little sloppy in design that doesn't live up to its potential. It really puts a lot of hurdles that have to be overcome so that most Exalts have to pay prohibitive amounts to access fairly minor effects (in respect to the cost paid). It honestly looks like it is designed more to punish a player than to reward them with a new style of play.

                  I may be overly harsh in my analysis... but it really does seem to be a lot of effort to pay a significant amount for such a small effect... not what most people likely expect when they purchase a 4 dot artifact. That said, I do see potential in the principles and I understand the reasoning behind the choices... but I find the execution of the design to be a little sloppy and overly punishing for little real gain. It really doesn't measure up to the effects that other 4 dot artifacts grant their owners, even when taking into account they Exalt type using it.

                                  Originally posted by Sorcerous Overlord View Post
                                  Belt of the Serpent

                                  There, that wasn't so hard. It will make a Snake Style DB considerably more powerful, but probably only to the extent that grabbing an Artifact Weapon would to any other DB. You can easily whip one of these up for any other style, and if it turns out to be too powerful, separate the Mastery ability and the Terrestrial ability and put one of them behind a vision quest or something. Fighting the embodiment of Snake Style to prove you are worthy of the belt is sick as fuck.
                                  It is also way too easy to do... in short the benefit is far greater than the cost.

                                  3 dot artifacts don't really do much more than what a character of sufficient skill would be able to do. If we treat the normal effects of Martial Art Charms as the baseline, then a 3 dot artifact shouldn't produce an effect any greater than that... and the Mastery effects, are by definition, greater than what is normally possible by the Charms usual effects. Thus any artifact that would allow for Mastery effects to be possible would need to be at least a 4 dot artifact and such artifacts should at least feel as they are providing a benefit that is somewhat beyond what a character could normally do.

                                  All you have really done is slap on a 3 dot artifact and BOOM! any Exalt can now use Mastery effects... even if you have to create a "unique" for such artifacts depending upon the Martial Art in question. It really comes down to balancing the extraordinary effort that non-Solar/Sidereal Exalts have to go through to achieve this result while not making it so easy that Mastery effectively becomes meaningless.

                                  The artifact should feel like it is a benefit to any Exalt that uses it, regardless of whether or not they can naturally access Mastery effects or are subject to Terrestrial limitations. That this also unlocks the Mastery effects of charms for other Exalts is a secondary effect that, while significant, isn't overly game breaking... especially if there are limitations to when those effects can be used... such as the need to be under the effects of a Form Charm or can only be used with unarmed strikes or such.

                                  Now let's take the Perfected Kata Bracers that JohnDoe244 provided us information on and see if we can't find a way for it to work in 3rd Ed and feel like they are a proper 4 dot artifact. For the base effect of these Bracers, I don't see why we don't stick to the one described in 2nd Eddition... specifically adding Essence to the Accuracy, Damage and Defense of Unarmed and Form Weapons attacks when under the effect of a Form Charm. Yes, this does sound quite potent at first blush, but look at it from a wider perspective... of what it means for starting characters as well as advanced ones. Many starting Exalts will only have a starting Essence of 1 and it will be quite a few sessions before they reach Essence 2 and even longer for higher Essence levels. By the time they get up to Essence 5 or higher, this bonus while still significant is not as spectacular as many of the other effects that most characters will have. This effect is also fairly limited in scope and duration... to just a small selection of weapons (and their artifact equivalents) and unarmed attacks for at most a scene... and while it can change the style of play dramatically, it usually will not have a long lasting effect outside of the current scene. Overall, these bracers don't actually do more than what the character would have been able to do with their own charms (Excellencies can easily make weapons more accurate and increase defenses for example). To limit the abuse that this bonus can cause, we could say that these are counted as dice added by Charms, thus limiting how much more can be added by other Charms.

                                  Now, the 2nd Edition version had additional benefits that were added due to the material they were made from. Magical Materials in 3rd Edition doesn't do this anymore... so this is where we could add the effect that allows a user to nullify the effects of Terrestrial Keywords and/or unlock the Mastery effects of charms. Of course, for it to remain an attractive choice for characters that normally have access to Mastery Effects, there should still be some benefit for them as well... and the Starmetal effect seems like a nice bonus for such characters... a 2 mote reduction in costs (to a minimum of 1 mote) for all style Charms while under the effect of a Form Charm. Still... having both this and the above effect automatically active does seem a little too easy overall. Perhaps a small cost to activate this effect is called for... such as the spending of 1 Willpower to show the level of concentration and mental taxing producing this side effect is... especially if one has to pay it again if they change forms (thus forcing a potentially difficult choice upon characters that know multiple forms as to which Mastery effects they wish to use; or which Martial Arts charms they want to use at a lesser cost for Solars/Sidereal).

                                  Now, lastly, comes the balancing act... specifically any additional drawbacks to using this artifact. The most significant will be the cost to attune to the artifact. Like in 2nd Ed, I feel that 8 motes is fair, as this is the general commitment one pays for most Form Charms (some are higher, some are lower, so this is the rough average). Combined with the costs of the Form Charm (which is committed as well), and this ties up a fair bit of motes from the character's Essence Pools that can not be regained until the commitment is allowed to elapse. The other significant drawback of the 2nd Edition version was the glowing that the bracers produce... a clear display of supernatural power that identified the character as a significant threat. Again, I don't see why this should change... though we do need to take into account that 3rd Edition has effects that can use Anima levels for various effects. Using these bracers will definitely make it quite obvious to all that the user is using a supernatural effect, thus I would say that it causes an Exalt's anima to flare at least to the Burning level... nullifying any stealth effects that the character may have been using. Additionally, should the character's Anima drop bellow Glowing, they are no longer considered to be under the effects of a Form Charm and loose any benefit they may have had as such (including from the Bracers)... at least until such time as they re-establish the use of their Form Charm (basically, if they drop to a Dim Anima, they loose commitment of their Form Charm until the cost to enter it is payed again). In short, to keep the benefits of the Bracers, they must remain fairly obvious to those observing the combat.

                                  Now, when taken as a whole, this feels like a fairly potent artifact. Even for a starting character, they will have some significant bonuses and can unlock some potent effects through Mastery (if they didn't have it) or a cost break (if they naturally have access to Mastery effects). As the character grows more powerful, these bonuses grow with them, though not beyond what one would expect overall. It also encourages a specific style of play... namely staying within a single Form, but doesn't prevent one from doing otherwise (it only limits which Mastery effects one has access to at a given moment, though it is still possible, if costly, to switch if one needs to). It also makes one a rather obvious target to enemies, and any attempts to mitigate this effect could see the loss of the benefits one gains. One is showing off their combat prowess... to hide that would be counter-productive. Add the commitment cost to attune and the Willpower cost to unlock the Mastery effects (and ignore Terrestrial limitations) and I think we have a fairly balanced 4 dot artifact that provides a fair advantage, unique play style, fair costs for the gains received, and reasonable drawbacks that a Storyteller can use keep the player from abusing their new power too much (when done fairly). There is a clear reason why a given player might want this for their martial artist character, but also clear reasons why they might pass it up too (especially for more ninja stealth fighter types who don't want to draw attention to their fights).

In that vein, rather then just making it give you the Mastery keyword or anything, I made specifically synergistic charms that work off of combining the base martial arts.
