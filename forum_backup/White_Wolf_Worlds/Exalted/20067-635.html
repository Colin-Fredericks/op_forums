        First, bear in mind that there are mental three attributes; quick-thinking and common sense are more reflected by Wits. So a total fool may actually have Intelligence 2, but Wits 1.

        Whereas a Wits 2, Int 1 might be okay for a lot of things, but totally stumped by maths, difficult vocabulary, etc.

        I think, like 2 or 3, 1 has a range to represent anything less intelligent than a pretty average. A 1-5 scale can't cover everything perfectly.

        So while a person with serious mental disabilities might be represented with Intelligence 1, so might someone who's just not that bright.

        I'd say, basically, an Intelligence 3 person is an A-grade student, an Intelligence 2 person is a C-grade student, and an Intelligence 1 person is an F-grade student.
        (Assuming they didn't get it by cheating. That'd be Wits!)

        You'd also represent a person with a serious mental disability by giving them Intelligence 1, but that doesn't mean only people with mental disabilities are represented with Intelligence 1.
        (And actually, most people with mental disabilities can have a conversation anyway. Only very severe mental disabilities mean you can't understand what people are saying.)

        So, in short, a PC with Intelligence 1 can still understand what the other PCs are saying, as long as they don't start using jargon.

        (And an Intelligence 1 PC almost certainly has good Wits anyway, because of how the character creation system works.)

            Per the book, Intelligence measures a character's ability to think logically, conduct an analysis of available data, and draw connections between known facts.

            I can personally attest that people who are:
            well spoken,
            bright,
            curious,
            knowledgeable,
            and educated
            are 100% capable of being utterly unintelligent per that definition.

            It's not Lore, Occult, Bureaucracy, Linguisitcs, or Integrity.

            What's more, while I get what y'all are trying to say, "Mental disabilities" covers an enormous range of varied states of mind and social practice. You Could represent a particular person's case with the Intelligence attribute but I really, really, really wouldn't.
