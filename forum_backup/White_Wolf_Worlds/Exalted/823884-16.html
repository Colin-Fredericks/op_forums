    Hello~
    We're starting up a campaign in a week or so and I had a few extra dots left to create artifacts with.

    I've written up two sister artifacts, each at 4 dots: Angelheart Vial and Decree of Iona, one of which is owned by my player character (Vial), while the other is owned by her rival (Decree). Angelheart Vial is a pendant that encourages the wearer to play as a guardian angel, protecting their chosen ward while Decree of Iona is the opposite, a circlet that encourages the wearer to fight the opposition to protect their partner. Together, their theme is "Sacrificing One's Self for the Person the Love", and the pair of artifacts is developed to encourage the theme through their evocations.


    「Angelheart Vial」(Artifact Pendant: ****)

    Attunement: 2m

    A small and elegant pendant crafted of orachalcum, glowing with a soft aura of warmth and safety. From the brooch, hangs a vial, slotted for a single gem. The pendant's wards and magic stem from the love the wearer has towards another, and strengthened if her love is returned.
    When attuned, the wearer is as if she were protected by the heavens. Attacks and blows miss her, as if the threads of fate were twisted, as so the blows would never have hit.

    Soulbond: If the wearer and the target each have a major or greater intimacy of love (Agape, Phileo,Stroge, or Eros), towards the other, upon performing a ritual, the wearer may declare the target to be her Soulbond. The wearer may not be soulbonded with more than one person. If the wearer's intimacy towards her Soulbound is *defining*, her Soulbound is aware of the wearer's every intimacy.

    Passive effect:
    While attuned with the pendant, the wearer receives +1 to parry and evasion, which does not count as dice provided by a charm.

    Evocations of 「Angelheart Vial」:

    「Seraph's Heavenly Ward」 (Cost: 3m; Type: Simple; Duration: Indefinite):
    While within a close vicinity of each other and the wearer is both attuned and committed her motes to the ward, the wearer's Soulbond is protected by the Angelheart, channeled through the pair's bond. The Soulbond adds half the wearer's Parry or Evasion, whichever is greater, rounded down, to his Soak.

    「Angel's Sacrifice」(cost: 5m; Type: Simple; Duration: Indefinite):
    Prerequisite: 「Seraph's Heavenly Ward」, Essence 2
    The wearer links her own body with her Soulbond, taking a part of his suffering as her own.
    If her Soulbond would take damage to their Health Track, they take only half, rounded up. The remaining damage they would have taken is instead dealt to the wearer's Health Track.

    「Grand Aegis of the Seraph」 (Cost:-; Type: Permanent; Duration:Permanent):
    Preqioste: 「Angel's Sacrifice」, Essence 2
    The guardian spirit of the 「Angelheart Vial」 grants the wearer greater defenses. While the wearer is in combat and nearby her Soulbond and her intimacy towards him is defining, the passive defensive bonus granted by 「Angelheart Vial」 is doubled.
    In addition, the wearers intimacy towards her Soulbond may never be lowered by any external influence.

    「In The Dawn, Two Souls Entwined」(Cost: 7m; Type: Simple; Duration: Indefinite):
    Prerequiste: 「Grand Aegis of the Seraph」, Essence 2
    While the wearer keeps her commitment to this evocation and her Soulbond has motes committed to 「In The Dawn, Two Souls Entwined」, the cost of 「Angel's Sacrifice」 & 「Grand Aegis of the Seraph」 is reduced to 0m.
    In addition, the wearer receives the passive effect of 「Decree of Iona」.
    If either of the pair ceases their commitment to either evocation, the other's commitment to their respective evocation is revoked as well.

    「Angel's Grace」(Cost: 7m; Type: Reflexive; Duration: Instant):
    Prerequisite: 「Grand Aegis of the Seraph」, Essence 3
    If the wearer has a defining intimacy towards her Soulbond, and her Soulbond has his last Health Level filled, she may expend the energy of the Hearthstone held within the pendant, performing a single miracle to save her Soulbond.
    In its last moments, the pendant returns to the wearer all of the motes she has committed to the evocations granted by the pendant, but it's forever shattered inert, having exhausted all of its magical power.
    Any non-zero Health Levels are removed from the Soulbond's health track, their Essence Pool is filled to 50%.
    All of the pendant's passives and evocations immediately cease their protection.


    ​「Decree of Iona」 (Artifact Circlet:****)

    Attunement: 2m

    A small and elegant circlet crafted of orachalcum, glowing with a soft aura of warmth and safety. From the brooch, hangs a vial, slotted for a single gem. The circlet's wards and magic stem from the love the wearer has towards another, and strengthened if her love is returned.
    When attuned, the wearer is as if her blade was carrying Iona's Will. The power of the Heavens guide her sword, ensuring it will reach its mark.

    Soulbond: If the wearer and the target each have a major or greater intimacy of love (Agape, Phileo,Stroge, or Eros), towards the other, upon performing a ritual, the wearer may declare the target to be her Soulbond. The wearer may not be soulbonded with more than one person. If the wearer's intimacy towards her Soulbound is *defining*, her Soulbound is aware of the wearer's every intimacy.

    Passive effect:
    While attuned with 「Decree of Iona」, the wearer is granted -1 to the opponent's defense when making a *decisive* attack.

    Evocations of 「The Decree of Iona」:

    「Seraph's Ethereal Shield」 (Cost: 2m, 2i, Type: Reflexive; Duration: Instant):
    The wearer's protection surrounds her Soulbond in a protective halo, reducing the onslaught penalty suffered by the Soulbond by (Intimacy Towards Soulbond/2), rounded down.
    If the Soulbond is a Celestial Exalt, this protection is extended until the Soulbond's next turn.

    「Celestine Rush on Seraphic Wings」 (cost: 3m, 1wp; Type: Supplemental; Duration: Instant):
    Prerequisite: 「Seraph's Ethereal Shield」
    In a flurry of essence and wings, the Soulbond wills his body to reach her. His anima flares for a brief moment, the image of angelic wings behind him, as he rushes at high speed to reach his partner. If the Soulbond is flurrying a defend other action with a misc. or move action to protect the wearer, the flurry penalty he takes is reduced by 1.

    「Grand Blade of the Seraph」 (Cost:–; Type: Permanent; Duration:Permanent):
    Prerequiste:「Celestine Rush on Seraphic Wings」, Essence 2
    「The Decree of Iona」 increases the Heavenly power it grants to the wearer to defeat the foes that would threaten her Soulbond.
    The passive of 「The Decree of Iona」 is enhanced, channeling the essence of the Soulbond through itself and entwining it with the wearer's blade as well.
    While together and nearby in combat and her intimacy towards her Soulbond is defining, his spirit is bound tightly with her own. Together, the Soulbond strikes with the wearer, reducing the target's hardness by his (Essence)/2, minimum 1.
    In addition, the wearers intimacy towards her Soulbond may never be lowered by any external influence.

    「In The Dusk, Two Souls Entwined」 (Cost: 7m; Type: Simple; Duration: Indefinite)
    Prerequiste: 「Grand Blade of the Seraph」, Essence 2
    While the wearer keeps her commitment to this evocation and her Soulbond has motes committed to 「In The Dawn, Two Souls Entwined」, the cost of 「Seraph's Ethereal Shield」 & 「Celestine Rush on Seraphic Wings」 is reduced by 2m.
    In addition, the wearer receives the defensive passive of 「Angelheart Vial」.
    If either of the pair ceases their commitment to either evocation, the other's commitment to their respective evocation is revoked as well.

    「The Heaven's Defied」 (Cost: 7m; Type: Reflexive; Duration: Instant)
    If the wearer has a defining intimacy of love towards her Soulbond, and she would have last Health Level filled, the wearer may expend the energy of the Hearthstone held within the pendant to perform a single miracle to save her life.
    The circlet channels the energy within the Hearthstone, consuming all of it in a single moment, and turns the essence into a lifeline, reviving the wearer in a cloud of white, angelic motes of essence.
    In its last moments, the pendant returns to the wearer all of the motes she has committed to the evocations granted by the pendant, but it's forever shattered, having exhausted all of its magical power.
    Any non-zero health levels are healed from the wearer's health track and their essence pool is restored to 50%.
    「The Decree of Iona」's passives and 「Grand Blade of the Seraph」's protection immediately cease their protection.





    Both artifacts define a new term, a "Soulbond". A Soubond is someone the wearer loves dearly, whether it be romantically, or a strong tie between warriors tested in battle, or the loving towards her family, that the wearer cherishes. The Vial and the Decree are powered by that love, and in turn, make her intimacy a profound strength. The key is that the intimacy must be shared. Just as much the wearer loves her Soulbond, the Soulbond must care equally for her and likewise have an intimacy of love towards the wearer. The Angel and the Decree bind their souls together. Across time and space, they will always be connected through the magic within the artifacts.
    Together, the two artifacts are built to encourage their players and their characters to roleplay couple, and rewards them for doing so, though they can still be used in a non-romantic fashion.

    Angelheart Vial is themed becoming a shield for the person they loves. The pendant's passive is minor, but significant and encourages the wearer to take more defensive actions in combat. The evocations ask the wearer to commit her essence to the Vial, receiving nothing in return, but granting her Soulbond multiple defensive buffs to his Soak and damage prevention to his health track, rather taking a portion of that damage herself. The evocations are designed to encourage the Soulbond to fight at their greatest potential in combat and rewards them with protections for getting dirty in combat. Finally, the penultimate evocation rewards the wearer for her for dedication, increasing its defensive bonus further still, and as long she is near her Soulbond and their intimacy is at its highest possible, it grants a very powerful defense and protects the intimacy she treasures so much from external influence.

    Decree of Iona is themed around becoming oneself a blade to protect the person they loves. The circlet gives a passive that helps the wearer in combat, and Iona's Will empowers her, reducing the opposition's defenses. It's evocations are nearly all actives, unlike the Vial's, which are auras. The first reduces the Soulbond's Onslaught penalty, scaling with their intimacy, while the second supplements the Soulbond's flurried Defend Other actions. Each encourage the Soulbond to play the role of a protector, jumping to the defense of the wearer, and reward him for doing so. Finally, the penultimate evocation rewards the wearer for her for dedication, increasing her ability fight in combat while she is near her Soulbond by helping her pierce through hardness.

    The two artifacts have capstones that are intended to be paired and mirrored. Both capstones actually do nothing, providing no new benefit for either the wearer or her Soulbond. However, when the Soulbond is attuned to the respective sister artifact, the true power of the evocations are unlocked. Angelheart Vial's capstone drastically reduces the cost of maintaining its evocations while granting the wearer the offensive passive of Decree of Iona. Similarly, Decree of Iona's capstone reduces the essence cost of it's two actives, and provides the wearer the defensive passive of Angelheart Vial. By sharing their burden together as equals, they empower the their partner, easing the weight on their shoulders and supporting them as they play their role for the other.

    Each artifact also has one additional evocation that isn't dependent on the rest: a one time use super ability that revives an exalt from near death. The evocation on the Vial lets the wearer restore her Soulbond from near death, while the evocation on the Decree is the mirror, restoring the wearer and giving them one more shot to keep fighting and end the battle. Each is meant to be the ultimate

    All the evocations, combining together with the capstones, are very much intended to resemble wedding vows, with their flavor text and quotations further reinforcing the idea. Unfortunately, those aren't on my pastebin at the moment so I couldn't copy them over here.

    I'd love feedback on the evocations and how well their evocations express what their respective artifact is intended to encourage. Any help or advice on appropriately costing each artifact would be very much appreciated as well!.

                A rare gift offered by the powerful Elemental Dragons of Flame, this Brand is a sign of the favor of an Elemental Dragon, for only they know the secret to render the purest Essence of Flame, culled from the bodies of powerful fire elementals and rendered into the clay-like paste needed to make the tattoos.
                The Brand is not given out lightly, as no mortal has the power to unlock its potential, and many Exalted lack the drive and the passion to do so. Those who have proven worthy of the Elemental Dragon’s favor have been passionate warriors and orators, brilliant artists and blazing pathfinders.
                Those who bear the mark of Elemental Flame go forward with flames that leap to their aid and stoke the fire in their belly. Elementals recognize the mark
                Evocations of the Brand of Elemental Flame

                Attuning to the Brand of Elemental Flame requires 5 motes and grants the first Evocation for the Brand for free.
                Elemental Flame Suffusement

                Cost: -- Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Permanent
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Permanent
                Prerequisites: None
                The Brand of Elemental Flame allows the tattoo-bearer to call upon the mighty Elemental Flame to aid them in battle. This evocation grants access to Elemental Flame, allowing the sorcerer to call forth epic blasts of flames, sending them streaming from her eyes, fingertips, or mouth, to her enemies. While this ability has some mundane uses such as to light a campfire or incinerate ropes that bind her hands, it can also be used as an attack, rolling (Intelligence + Occult) to direct the flames, which are treated as a medium artifact archery weapon with the natural, lethal, flame, thrown (short), and mounted tags.
                Additionally, Elemental Flame Suffusement offers access to the following powers while attuned to the Brand:
                • Sudden Immolation Touch (6m; Simple; Instant; Decisive-only): The sorcerer can perform an immolation gambit with a difficulty of 3, her target becomes immolated by the Elemental Flame with no brand to ground it. They suffer 1L a round until they can take a miscellaneous action to put out the fire, usually a Dexterity + Athletics check with a difficulty of 3.
                • Flame-Shaping Gesture (3m; Reflexive; Instant): Warping and twisting her fire, the sorcerer may solidify and shape their Elemental Flame into a weapon, allowing it to take on the profile of a medium melee artifact weapon if so desired, along with granting them the tag of her choice for the rest of the scene. She might form the flames into a lash of scourging fire to grant them the flexible tag, reshape the flames into a large hammer to grant them the smashing tag, or similar. Only one tag can be granted by this power at a time. This requires that the Sorcerer use Dexterity + Melee to attack their opponents as well, but flame-forged weapons can only be used at close range.
