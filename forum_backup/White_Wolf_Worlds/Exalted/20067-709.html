        Originally posted by Half-Moon Smile View Post
        As someone relatively new to Exalted, I was wondering if I could get some clarification regarding Feats of Strength? Specifically for a Dragonblooded, which my group and I are playing!

        I understand that Athletics is the skill used for Feats, and that the Charms in that 'school' (class? tree?) are the ways to impact it. But in the few charms I've seen that mention FoS, like Grandmother Oak Exertion and Mountain-Toppling Might, the bonuses mentioned seem to only apply to Strength for the purposes of seeing if a roll can be attempted, instead of adding more dice (though Grandmother Oak Exertion provides a success). Am I right in reading that those bonuses don't actually make a Feat of Strength any easier to accomplish, and only provide their benefit to attempting it in the first place? That'd be a bit disappointing, even if Dragonblooded are supposed to be the weak cousins of the Solars.

        Assuming that's a correct reading - would anyone have advice for a Wood Aspect trying to increase his dice on Feats of Strength? Mighty Thew, training Strength, and taking a Specialty all seem straightforward, but I don't have enough bones in the system to know of any other tips or tricks.
        -Strength of Stone gives a non-Charm die from the 1 point of strength.
        -Grandmother Oak gives another non-Charm die.
        -And then Unshakable Mountain Spine gives you double 9s meaning more successes for your dice, doubles your strength (so your strength can go up to 12 with Strength of Stone) and adds 1 extra non-charm dice for each point of strength you have above the requirement (so you get more dice as long as the feat isn't 12 or above).

        -If you wish to go beyond that, the best way to transcend the limitations of a Dragonblooded is to acquire Jade Artifacts, because an Artifact's Resonant Evocations can be stronger than Dragonblooded Charms. Unfortunately I don't think there are any released Artifacts that improve feats of strength, so you may have to devise an artifact with your Storyteller or create new Evocations for an Existing Artifact (In-universe, each owner of an Artifact develops their own unique set of Evocations).

        Edit :

        Assuming a 10+ Feat of Strength :

        Base : 15 dice (5 Strength, 5 Athletics, Excellency)
        Specialty : 2 dice
        Stunt : 2 dice
        Spend Willpower : 1 success

        Mighty Thew : 3 dice
        Strength of Stone : 1 die
        Unshakable Mountain Spine : 2 dice

        Total : 25 dice + 1 success

        Rolling 25 dice with double 9s and 1 success gives you roughly 16 successes on average, which is enough to hurl an elephant or rip a portcullis with your bare hands, and you can still get 1 more success by making a 2 point Stunt. And you still have about a 26% chance of making it to 20 successes, which is not too shabby for a Dragonblooded.

Since Athletics charms that interact with rolls are fair game at your base level your DB would be able to throw Effortlessly Rising Flame into the mix which are automatic successes and a free non-charm die for every 10 that gets rolled in the attempt at the FOS. Strength of Stone Technique also adds 1 to your str for the roll itself as well as the increase to determine if you can make the attempt. Grandmother Oak, adds a bonus success off the top, Unshakeable Mountain Spine provides double 9s and every excess point it raises above what you need to make the attempt is added a non-charm die, Dragon Surmounts the Waterfall adds double 9's to the attempt as well as the increase for rating purposes so long as you're in water.

                                                Tree.

                                                But simply "Atheletics Charms" is the preferred convention.

                                                Originally posted by Half-Moon Smile View Post
                                                But in the few charms I've seen that mention FoS, like Grandmother Oak Exertion and Mountain-Toppling Might, the bonuses mentioned seem to only apply to Strength for the purposes of seeing if a roll can be attempted, instead of adding more dice (though Grandmother Oak Exertion provides a success).                                                 You are mostly correct.

                                                The main Charm that adds dice to your roll (but does not increase which feats you may attempt) is called "Effortlessly Rising Flame" and is on page 168 of What Fire Has Wrought.

                                                Dragon-Blooded (and Solars) do not have innate "super strength". They have a supernatural ability to develop their athleticism to perform feats which would be impossible for normal people (through the use of their native magic). The Feats of Strength are meant to be taken as individual feats, not a benchmark of how strong you are -- how strong you are is decided entirely, and essentially solely, by your Strength score on a scale from 1 to 5.

                                                        Yeah, regarding dragonblooded feats of strength, the excellency will get you up to your dice cap most of the time. (If you have a spare dot cos your ability+speciality is an odd number the bonus die from strength of stone will cover that) So, to get any higher you'll be needing non charm dice.

                                                        There are a couple of ways of getting those. If you're in wood aura Grandmother Oak Exertion can push you over the limit, and at essence 3 you can take Unshakable Mountain Spine, which provides double nines and non-charm dice for each point of surplus strength you have, which essentially converts all those charms that raise your strength for that purpose into charms that provide non charm dice. That's not compatible with the Grandmother Oak non-charm sucess, cos they require different auras, but if you get to essence 4 the boost from adding your stamina to your strength will definitely mean it's not useless.
