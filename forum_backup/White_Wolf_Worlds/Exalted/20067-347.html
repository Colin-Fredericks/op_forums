                Originally posted by PraxisCat View Post
                Can battlegroups use charms? RAW is completely silent on this; the word charm doesn't appear even once in the BG section under systems. There are two camps, AFAIK:
                1. People who think that might completely abstracts this issue away.
                2. People who don't think that that might is a sufficient abstraction, and think charms should still be useable by battlegroups.

                I fall, very very strongly, in camp number 2. What are other people's takes?
                I would ask first: what are you making a battlegroup of, that has homogenous charms?

                If the answer is "Dragon-blooded", then I would say that you should basically never have a battlegroup of DBs. If you do have DBs in a battlegroup, then you must be simulating something at such epic scale that you should just go with what seems right.

                If the answer is "a bunch of identical demons / elements / ghosts" then I'd just let Might 2's benefits cover it. A persistent +2 Accuracy and +1 Defense is nothing to sneeze at.

                And if the battle group is a bunch of differnet spirits, well, how would you even decide what charms it has?

                    Nowhere is it written that when you turn a statblock into a BG statblock that they lose access to the powers of the base statblock.
