                        Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                        What if your anima is a thunderhead with bright silver lining, as if the sun was behind it
                        For real edge cases, which I've only encountered a tiny handful of times, I use the rubric of "if they turned their back and flared their anima, would a reasonably informed savant be confused as to what sort of Exalt type they are?"

                        If the answer is "yes" then I will probably disapprove of it. I know a lot of people in the thread are saying "the Caste mark is most important" and they aren't wrong, but the Caste mark is like the size of a silver dollar and not easily visible in many cases, whereas an anima banner is something you can discern from literally tens of miles away if the circumstances are right. It is true that having a Caste mark at all marks you out as "not a Dragon-Blooded, therefore often problematic" but I genuinely do think the anima banner alone should distinguish your Exalt type readily, especially at bonfire level.

                        That said, this gives you an enormous possibility space to work with, and I'm even willing to bend it all the way to "sure, this looks sort of Fire Aspect-y when I describe it, but in-universe the glory of the sun is readily apparent to all looking at it; someone who sees me flare this from a long way away will not confuse me with someone who has Hesiesh's aspect."

                        Even that has limits, tho.

                        Oh, and as for Coiks story, which is a cool story... I don't think any dev has ever spoken to it one way or the other (could be wrong, the devs have spilled a lot of ink talking about the game) but I've always felt that the entire reason you can't easily hide a Caste mark is precisely to head off clever boots players trying to have their anima banner cake and also eat the cake of "No, really, I'm not a Solar." I've let people try to fake being another Exalt type without using a Resplendent Destiny, but if they want to flare their banner and still get away with it... I've let that happen once, and it involved someone willing to invest a lot of XP into custom Stealth Charms.
