            Short answer is a starting Solar can kill an entire Hearth of Essence 2 DBs. An Essence 5 Solar can take on an entire Essence 3 Hearth.

            But a starting DB can solo a Solar (three successful builds so far -- two Earth, one Water).

            A Solar elder doesn't need HSF to kill upto 6 DBs (can solo 3 DBs without taking a single hit or flaring anima), but 7+ DBs have a good shot at bringing a Solar elder down -- if they ambush and the Solar doesn't have time to prepare.

                                    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                    Thanks.
                                    Because they have to spend too many motes?
                                    Honestly? It's mostly swing.

                                    If a Melee based Solar gets unlucky then they lose 3i or they gain a Wound Penalty that they can DSD away. If they get lucky they kill all their enemies and win.

                                    If a Resistance Solar gets unlucky, their Charms shut down and they die. If they get lucky... nothing different happens to if their luck was average.

                                    Fighting 5 on 1... that's a lot of chances to get unlucky.

                                    But in general, yes: Solars die when they run out of motes. Melee Solars can novastrike their foes into oblivion, spending all their motes but killing all their foes. Resistance Solars get bled dry by the fact that 5 DBs are regening 25m each round to their 5m.

                                    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                    Reading those links, it honestly seems really variable. Sometimes an amazing Solar gets killed dead. Other times a Solar rips through a bunch of DBs with nary a wound.
                                    A large percentage of this is luck of the dice.

                                    But a larger percentage is character creation.

                                    I mathed it out in another thread, but the difference between an optimized and suboptimal PC is something like 150XP at character generation. Most pregen characters (like the Corebook example Solar and the Tomb of Dreams PCs) are built with one or two stats at 5. Combat optimized characters are built with 7+ fives.

                                    There is a massive, massive difference in power between Dex 3 Melee 3 and Dex 5 Melee 5. (Not sure if I posted the percentages here or in the character optimization thread, but allow me to say "massive" one last time.)

                                    I've run a few Solar vs Solar fights, and seen similiar crushing victories.

                                    An optimized character (or Hearth of characters) is going to crush a character made within Exalted's default expectations.

                                    (Other factors like Stunt bonuses also help.)

                                    But this is as it should be. Cynis What's-His-Name killed a Solar boy who was trying to stop the opium trade way back in 1E. Being a Solar shouldn't be an automatic "Win" button. Dragon-Blooded should have the potential to be a meaningful threat. But Solars are the most powerful Exalted for a reason, and the Wyld Hunt is a dangerous duty. I think 3E really nailed the balance point. (And if you really miss the old days of Solars breezing through DBs, run Young Dynasts in a Battlegroup.)
