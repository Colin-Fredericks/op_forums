Once you get into Earth Aura it's better, but Searing Edge can trigger on your first attack, which is when you'll be more likely to be withering anyway. Thankfully, the two work well with their respective styles. Fire can only use light weapons, so with their high accuracy and low damage post-soak damage is good. Earth on the other hand is stuck with a crappy heavy weapon, but Force of the Mountain can help mitigate that by dealing a bunch of extra damage.
