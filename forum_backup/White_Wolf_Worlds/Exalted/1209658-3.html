    I guess to me is that while yeah, Lunars are skin-walkers and can steal resources, a big them to them as I heard it last was for lack of a better term, asymetric and guerilla tactics being a bit thing for them. They're not just about using someone's stuff against them, but also about braking everything around them since the opponents need them and they don't. Sabatoge seems as much in their vein as anything. You see this even to things like their Castes: You don't just use the tools you're given, you break things and use ones no one else has. And when you are taking something from somoene to use against htem, note that there's at least murder involed somewhere in most of the default pwoers. Once the role is broken, there's a hole where you got that heart's blood and you did damage along the way of stealing stuff.

    The Caul having its own special Loom really just...didn't fit this? The original presentation was something Lunars built to yoink the Caul from Fate for Lunars. the issues I think is a fewfold. First, I think it's a matter that it's not really "stealing and breaking" in the way it was presented. Building a bit device and using it to make an anti-Sidereal zone is kind of not fitting, to me at least, in the whole asymetrical and guerilla thing I guess. Maybe it originally had something that made it like that, but at this point I can't think of a way to do that. It was originally sold as this big No Moon collaboration achievement dealie that resulted in this special Lunar zone. But that doesn't fit the Exalt type who's tactics are well, rough, to say the least, be it aggressive anti-Realm culture-jamming or sabotage.

    To some extent, it's also kind of a cheap move? I think it felt to some extent agian, this feel that "To build this Splat Up, let's Tear this Other One Down." It was less "Lunars did something cool to spite Sidereals" and more "Lunars just kind of went at a core Sidereal thing and ripped out a part and said No". It kind of looks like the 2e issues with Lunars versus Solars actually. "No we really invetend sorcery, Solars stole it from us." Or "Anything we do wth the TSR, the Bull did in a decade." Or Infernals and Sidereals a bit, with things like specific anti-Sidereal magic in Infernal stuff or them having powers just out of Sidereal bounds and so negating their abiltiy to interact with them. So having a big achievement of Lunars underming Sidereals being by just plain remvoing Sidereal abiltity to particpate in an area probably felt off. Lunars get plenty of other ways to make Sidereal life hell, like killing them if they get the drop or causing havoc for the Realm in general with or without a specail No Sidereals Allowed treehouse.

    it kind of makes it have this Lunar Blessed Isle factor that 1e and 2e did where games for non-DBs on the Isle were probably pointless to Terrestiral and Sidereal domination. I think 3e is moving away from even that and just introducing an island the size of Greenland that is this but Lunar killzone isn't really adding much. The current version, tied much more to things really beyond Lunar or even Terrestrial control in a way, i think just creates intersting conflict that also happens to do so without having a big "Fuck this other splat" button. I like how it's kind of implicitly "Solars and Sidereals are just not as important here" without having anything like, specifically anti-them.

    So yeah, it's just a thing that while it was a cool idea then, I think as the setting evolved and as Creation is depicted in 3e now, I am not so sure it's something that even fits now to be honest. The Dragon-Blooded manuscirpt shows this a lot too I think. Splats should just sell themselves for themselves, not why they can beat up X other splat that makes thier life hard.

    And stuff.
