                        04-27-2023, 04:52 PM
                        Originally posted by TempleBuilder View Post
                        Am I understanding the Martial Arts rules correctly that one can use both Earth Dragon style and Fire Dragon style with a staff, since it has the Two Handed and potentially also Balanced tags?
                        I think that's how it works in Essence, if I'm reading it right.

                        However, in the mainline games, you can only combine styles that explicitly use the same weapons. So most everything can be combined when used bare-handed (With exceptions like Single Point Shining Into The Void Style), but Earth and Fire Dragon can only combine on unarmed attacks since their style weapons are tetsubos and paired short swords respectively.

                        Staffs, fortunately, are very common weapons. Golden Janissary, Laughing Monster, Wood Dragon, and White Reaper could all be combined on attacks made with a staff, and those are just the ones i can remember off-hand.

                        On a related note, are Artifact staffs a thing?
                        Yep. Normal staffs, spears, seven-section staves, theres lots. One of the Artifacts in Arms of the Chosen is a time-bending staff made of Immortal Peachwood and Starmetal.

                        Is it possible for a Dragon Blooded line that mostly only produces those of a particular aspect? (Like, I know it’s largely genetics, but is that exalting in the first place, or is aspect included?)
                        Yes, most bloodlines tend towards one Aspect. This doesn't mean that their family always Exalts to that element, just that they most commonly do. Ones who don't are typically seen as somewhat mongral, but that's a cultural idiosyncrasy. The Realm Great Houses and the Lookshy Gentes are typically listed with a sobriquet related to their primary element, such as House Ragara's "Earth Slaked On The Blood Of Dragons", Gens Amilar's "Air That Rushes Toward Tomorrow", or Gens Toriki's "Five Dragons Guard The City's Walls".

                        So Soul Steel isn’t very abundant in Creation, does that mean Liminals are mostly out of luck for Artifacts?
                        Not very common in Creation, much more abundant in the Underworld. So they have as good a chance of getting Artifacts as anyone else, as long as they're willing to truck with the dead. Servants of the Neverborn are typically the most skilled at it though.

                        What are the usual heavenly duties of a Chosen of Journeys? Do they mostly stick to messages, and overseeing voyages? Or does it extend to making sure the right artifact ends up in the right place at the right time to be picked up by the right person? Does it involve…corrections when that process goes wrong, or is that more of a Endings area? (Stealing, i’m talking about stealing.)
                        All of the above. There's a lot of overlap between Sidereal duties, and it tends to cover both mundane and esoteric aspects of their theme.

                        I know fire powder is a thing, does that mean fireworks exist?
                        Firedust isn't exactly gunpowder, but fireworks should be OK. Celestial-Circle Sorcerers also have a firework spell.

                        Edit: How do dragon lines work? Is it all about the connection point between different lines, or can a place of power pool into existence from a slow flowing spot in a powerful line?
                        It's... basically ley-lines in more common parlance, Dragon Lines is just a real-life alternative name for the concept. So each line is aspected to something, typically one of the five elements but also Solar, Lunar, Sidereal, and Abyssal aspects. Yozi and Autochthonian elements also had aspects in 2e, and could be shaped into Creation with difficulty. They're a symbiotic existence with the physical area they pass through - a Wood line will be attracted to woodlands, and will encourage their growth, while a fire line would do the same in a desert.

                        Where those lines cross or pool, Demesne form. You can cap those with buildings called Manses, which channel the power into specific effects. The Realm's equivalent of a nuclear arsenal is a massive network of Manses that feed into the Imperial Manse, which is a First Age weapon that can wipe out multiple armies with a single shot. It's... not particularly stable at the best of times though.
