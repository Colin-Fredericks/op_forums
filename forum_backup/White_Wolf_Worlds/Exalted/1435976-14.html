                                        Opening a permanent portal between realms (such as a small shadowland) is a suggested ambition 3 celestial circle working. [Goal Number 35: Intelligence + Occult versus Finesse+2per circle above initiation.]

                                        Alternatively opening a rift that allows communication, possession, and limited interaction(not actual transportation) is suggested as an ambition 3 terrestrial circle working.

                                        Or... you could try the 'natural' way with lots of murder, desecration, and such. Make your own or find one near an existing haunt site.
                                        Originally posted by Loving the Gorgon View Post
                                        Can ghosts automatically "cross over" to the Underworld at any time without a shadowland? If someone wanted to send a bound ghost to "the other side" to check that is.
                                        It's not automatic. Higher soul ghosts typically form in the Underworld and need to travers a shadowland to interact with the living. So if encounter a ghost in creation, it's a clue to the presence of a shadowland of some size.

                                        Of course, there are always atypical events, where a ghost may inexplicably form in creation proper.

                                        Then too you could always allow for a thaumaturgic ritual that summons an ancestor's ghost, or something.

                                            Originally posted by Ashenrogue View Post
                                            A question about the charm Heavenly Guardian Defense. One of its uses allows you to parry normally unparryable attacks. If used against an Ambush attack would it also allow you to apply other charms (such as Excellencies) to increase your base parry rating?
                                            Ambush attacks do not prevent you from using Reflexive actions, including any appropriate charm use. They just completely ignore the target's defence; the target is considered to have Defence of 0. Whether or not this means that ambush attacks are unblockable and undodgeable because they do this is up for interpretation.

                                            But if you do allow that HGD gives normal defence, then that defence can be enhanced reflexively by other charms.

                                                Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                                How many different kinds of Beastfolk are there? I know there'll never be a precise number, but I hope one of the editions has given or at least implied a ballpark.

                                                Just the handful of types mentioned in the books, but no others? A few dozen different types? Hundreds of tribes with hundreds of different animal types? A Beastfolk tribe for every vertebrate and large invertebrate we can think of?

                                                Do Beastfolk exist who are part magical or mutant animal, like unicorn, gryphon, forest mimic, basilisc...?
                                                Most of the first have nothing remotely like a canon answer in 3E to my knowledge, so I'll try to estimate

                                                Lunar-created beast folk with Sharing Luna's Gifts can be literally any animal that Lunar has a form of, so theoretically as many kinds as there are animal species.
                                                In practice there probably are only a few thousand such beast folk max, since there are 400-ish Lunars, and most don't have that charm, and they probably repeat fairly often, and arguably don't count as a "type" since they don't breed true.
                                                Every Lunar who uses Beast-Soul Awakening Crucible creates a hereditary breed of beast folk or 2 based on their spirit shape(s). There're approximately 400 lunars at a time, but not all know that charm, and some Lunar beast folk lineages survive their creator. I'd probably estimate 100 kinds.

                                                Wyld beast folk can theoretically be any animal, I'd guess at least 30 hereditary types, and probably less than 10,000 hereditary types.
                                                Sorcerous beast folk are probably the rarest.
                                                I feel like the setting works better if Lunar-associated beast folk are a large minority among beast folk, such that "that beast folk might be associated with a lunar" is a sign of someone paranoid about Lunars but not totally irrational.
                                                So, maybe 100 large-scale lunar lineages, 400 wyld lineages, 100 sorcery, god blooded, or other lineages. Plus maybe 10,000 beast folk not from a lineage but the result of singular workings

                                                From another point of view, I've seen estimate of the total Creation's population of 500 million. If 1% are beast folk, that's 5,000,000 beast folk. If they're divided into mostly groups of on average 5000, that's 1000 varieties. Groups too much smaller than 5000 will probably have a lot of difficulty lasting long periods of time without marrying out of the lineage. It's hard to get a good sense of the average size since there's probably a lot of tiny groups and a few large ones

                                                I don't think there's any mention of beast folk of a supernatural animal. Some things can result in a beast folk of multiple animals, which could basically be a gryphon beast folk, but those wouldn't be common (and often won't be hereditary).
                                                My impression is that hereditary supernatural powers are very rare, so you're unlikely to have a lineage with some supernatural power besides being animally, but a singular wild-created or sorcerously-created beast folk, or a god-blooded (using god to include Exalts, Demons, Elementals, etc) certainly could have supernatural powers. I'm not sure you'd call them a beast folk, though
