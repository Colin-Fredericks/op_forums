The food arrives on time, without loss, and everyone gets a fair share. Now here's the thing. If a solar can make a project move faster than what should be possible due to his own supernal excellence, can a solar make do with fewer resources than normal people? Can a solar, effectively, feed more people in a famine situation than what should be possible considering the numbers of people and the food supply?
            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
            Does it seem to you that a Solar with a hundred people to feed and eighty "units" of food could effectively divide that food into a hundred units that are maybe less than absolute but where it's better to have everybody partially fed rather than a fifth of them not being fed at all because the rest couldn't figure out how to share?

            Although, I mean, any mortal could do that, so I suppose a better question is whether or not you think something related to Solar organisation could express itself as meaning that people will feel and act as though they're fully fed on only 80% of their total allowance of food rather than 100% of it.
            I'd allow it with the right (custom) Charms. Being so good at sharing out food that you can make sure no-one starves.

            Consider Charms like Contagion Curing Touch or Craftsman Needs No Tools. Food-Gathering Exercise lets you find food where none exists. League of Iron Preparation lets you keep your army marching on empty stomachs.

            I'd say first an Essence 2 Charm with All-Seeing Master Procurer (skill at managing goods) and Bureau-Rectifying Method (skill at managing people) as prereqs to ensure any completed project has literally zero waste. Then an Essence 3 Charm with that (consumate skill at projects) and Infinitely-Efficient Register (getting extra efficiency from your organisation) as prereqs that lets you attempt a project with only 75% of the required resources, with an Essence 4 repurchase to bring that to 50%, then a third purchase at Essence 5 for 25%.

            Though if we're talking one-off famine, then simply using Infinitely-Efficient Register to find out some farm boy called Joseph has been squirrelling away part of the harvest for the last seven years specifically in case of a famine is totally a legitimate use of a canon charm, IMO.

                                                            - I think they have to announce that they are going to Engage. DO they have to specify a unique target to Engage or can they specify whoever they want in range ? If they Engage someone, can they attack &quot;normally&quot; someone else in the same range or is everyone targeted only by Engage ?
                                                            - It's written there is no need to roll initiative to confirm the action, but just the line after it's said it deduces the appropriate initiative cost. What does this means ?

                                                            Yeah they can flurry actions just fine. If you close to melee with a group of archers they can flurry draw/ready their swords with an attack.

                                                            As for engage it works like this:

                                                            Your battlegroup declares they want to attempt an engage roll against one enemy in range. They roll to attack with their grapple pool If they hit, they lose 3 initiative, which for a battlegroup only matters for when they act in the turn. Then they make a control roll with whatever combat ability they normally use. Then they get to savage their target on the same turn, also using whatever combat ability they normally use. I’m not sure if it’s intentional or not that the battlegroup not roll to hit with it’s standard combat pool.

                                                            Also note that Command actions give their bonus to all actions taken that turn, which makes ordering battlegroups to engage incredibly deadly.
