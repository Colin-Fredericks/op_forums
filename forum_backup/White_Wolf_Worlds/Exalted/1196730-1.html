                            Heaven and Earth Gauntlets

                            Poor Sidereals.

                            Love how many of these Artifacts have been made by non-Solars. The classic reference to Greek myth in the text of Legend-Forging Blow made me smile. I also find it clever, how the reset conditions of the capstone Charms of so many of these Artifacts encourage groups toward telling certain stories.

                            Irenio's Bell

                            I fuckin' love this game. If I was playing D&D, and the party defeated a demon prince, and I said, "I want to take his temple bell and make a magic shield out of it, along with the broken pieces of my magic sword," the DM and the other players would probably spend the next twenty minutes trying to come up with twenty ways to persuade me to "stop being ridiculous."

                            Instead we have a game where doing so results in a brassy orichalcum defensive weapon that builds destructive resonance as it is hit in combat, until you can channel its unholy power to call up a demon to fight by your side.

                            Rainwalker

                            Razor Parasol? Razor Parasol. B)

                            The description of this artifact puts a very vivid image in my head. I wonder what the practical difference is between using a raksha's grace to make an artifact, versus using the raksha itself.

                            I doubt that razor parasols are common enough in Creation to call for a martial arts style that has such as a form weapon… that doesn't stop me wanting it, though.

                            Razor Dancer and Wise Steel

                            I like that the heroes trapped in the blades are not gendered and don't have their splat types given; they could conceivably be anything. I also liked contrasting these weapons to Courante and Galliard; this pair of weapons has yet to become very storied, and uses Form-type Evocations.

                                Summer Thunder

                                This is another one of those "Fuck yeah, Exalted" items. A bow, strung with the god of horses' mane, that produces mounted archers from its arrows? I love it; one of my players, he was flipping through the book, and as soon as he saw this item, he wanted to play a character with this item.

                                Sun's Brush

                                Gods in love, jealous paramours, a secret order of librarians, and a magic calligraphy spear? I might have to raise my standards; try as I might to stay on my feet, y'all keep bowling me over.

                                I did have to reread a bit, as I was confused as to the reasoning behind it permitting decisive attacks in the place of foe-scribing gambits; that was until I recalled that some earlier Evocations only worked with foe-scribing. But now you can set those guys on fire. Cool!

                                The Tusk of Galaech-Ma

                                This is a weapon with personality. I wonder how much easier of a time Wander would have had against the Colossi with this bad boy. And again, we see a weapon crafted by a non-Solar and yet still totally badass. I approve.

                                I wonder how its Evocations might be different in the hands of another kind of Exalt, though. Would the Lunar Charmset be compatible enough to not require overmuch structural difference? Intriguing, to be sure.

                                    With non-orichalcum weapons, the writers seem to have stuck with writing Evocations derived from Solar Charms that most likely have approximations in the Charmsets of the associated Exalted.

                                    Solar Exalted have equal, full resonance with all artifacts, but that doesn’t mean that all Solar Charms always combine with every possible Evocation.

                                    Looking to Volcano Cutter, you could easily build something from Magma Burial and Leaping Tiger Attack or Thunderbolt Attack Prana, but there aren’t many Solar Charms that would seem to improve Caldera Reconfiguration Stance or Pyroclastic Holocaust. But Dragon-Blooded have Charms like Dragon Vortex Attack, which might.

                                    But orichalcum Evocations should pretty much always synergize with Solar Charms.

                                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                        That's the big triumph of the book, I think; items that can inspire characters built around them.

                                                        ​Not to knock the ones given in the corebook, mind.
                                                        Yeah, Adorei's certainly no chopped liver; it's just that, I think, no one wants to be the one to cause the poor girl to burn out.

                                                        Asphodel

                                                        In the very first sentence, we get a nice little point about how even during the First Age, a genius Solar artisan still found the insight of a mortal to be something worth preserving. Well, "mortal," anyway, given that the guy was a sorcerer. Still cool. I love that the mace has its own world hidden inside.

                                                        The illustration is fun, too. Perfect Soul seems a bit weirded out; I imagine I would be, too.

                                                        Love the descriptions. I'm assuming that like "Creature of Darkness," "Undead" is one of those non-exhaustive categories where the ST is assumed to have final say over what falls under its umbrella?

                                                        Burning Branch

                                                        This one is also fun; a green jade weapon that unlocks fire-themed Evocations through housing a fiery hearthstone, via the myth-logic of fiery oaths and of it being, well, a burning branch. And it can even damage artifacts! Sounds like I should keep those repair Charms handy, after all.

                                                        Flying Silver Dream

                                                        It's a continuation of the same story from Courante and Galliard, and a reminder that even something as potent as an artifact might see reason to be put aside. Irenio gets a name-drop here as well. I like how the weapon basically becomes a separate combatant; the older editions' FSD didn't seem to convey that the way this one does.
