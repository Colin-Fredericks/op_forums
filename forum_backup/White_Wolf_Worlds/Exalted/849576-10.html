                                                            What breaks if:

                                                            Savaging or throw/slam actions/attacks in a clinch apply onslaught penalties against the victim for the purposes of other character's attacks?

                                                            Savaging and slam/throwing actions are resolved just like attacks, save that they need a clinch and treat the target's Defense as 0. The victim does not treat his Defense as 0 against other opponents (though he takes a Defense penalty for being clinched). It makes sense that being savaged would penalize the victim's Defense against outside attacks, and that the victim would suffer onslaught for being thrown into a hard surface, at least.


                                                            Savaging or throw/slam actions/attacks in a clinch can be treated like attacks for Charms that do not explicitly forbid use within grapples?

                                                            Savaging and slam/throwing actions are resolved just like attacks, save that they need a clinch and treat the target's Defense as 0. The list of non-grappling-specific Brawl Charms that could thus supplement these attacks include:


                                                            Fists of Iron Technique (gets you Lethal grappling damage earlier than Dragon Coil Technique, and even after DCT, still adds damage)

                                                            Ferocious Jab (someone outside of the grapple could attack your victim, and even if their Defense cannot become a negative number, without a penalty-negator, the penalty is still there for Ferocious Jab to exploit)

                                                            Thunderclap Rush Attack (you might need to act outside of your Initiative order to get in one last squeeze before the opponent breaks free, and you can even Crash your opponent before you even apply a savaging or slam/throwing attack!)

                                                            Falling Hammer Strike (if savaging and slam/throwing actions/attacks really do apply onslaught penalties that the Solar grappler's allies might exploit... or the Solar himself with Ferocious Jab)

                                                            Solar Cross-Counter (unless you Restrain a victim, he can still attack you; any Brawl-based attack or counter-attack you make against the victim, including from this Charm, should treat his Defense as 0)

                                                            Ox-Stunning Blow

                                                            Burning Burial Fist

                                                            Force-Rending Strike (again, the victim can still attack you; since their attack is definitely NOT their Defense, your clash would not treat their attack pool as 0)

                                                            Blade-Rebuking Wrath (disarm your victim of that knife he keeps trying to stab you with)

                                                            Hammer On Iron (like Titan-Strengthening Method, but Decisive instead of Withering-only, and you don't expend your rounds of control for it!)

                                                            One With Violence

                                                            Cancel the Apocalypse

                                                            Adamantine Fists of Battle (the victim can still soak, so more Overwhelming damage is useful)

                                                            Intercepting Fury Smite

                                                            Fire-Eating Fist

                                                            Striving Aftershock Method

                                                            Incarnated Fury Attack (the unblockable and undodgeable aspects are wasted, but there's more to this Charm than that)

                                                            Orichalcum Fists of Battle

                                                            Rampage-Berserker Attack (again, like Titan-Straightening Method, but Decisive instead of Withering-only, and you don't expend your rounds of control for it!)

                                                            Apocalypse Flare Attack (the range aspect of this attack is wasted, but you can spend your anima banner to add to the damage you'd inflict with Fire-Eating Fist)

                                                            Heaven Fury Smite (especially nasty with Cancel the Apocalypse, Striving Aftershock Method, Raging Wrath Repeated, Incarnated Fury Attack, and Ferocious Jab; Orichalcum Fists of Battle also increases base Initiative by 1)
