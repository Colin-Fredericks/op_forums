                Wow that was super close. I'm noticing that Mirror on Water Focus requires a kind of ST buy-in to make it very useful. You can't launch Decisive attacks while crashed, which means if the abyssal just went and crashed Ezeri while it was up, the effect would be useless. It's not like crashing is a bad idea either, the only bad thing about crashing a opponent before decisive attacking them is that they can get an initiative shift on you after if they don't die.

                Some other stuff is like that too Aegis of Invincible Might has a perfect counter in that if you never decisive the person who's got it unless they're crashed, they never activate it.

                When it does go off though, I did like the effect. Stripping your opponent of initiative just before they hit you, while dealing damage and potentially giving them a wound penalty to deal with is a pretty badass effect. I feel though that it might be better combined with a Defend Other action. You can't use it if you've attacked that turn anyway, and that way somebody could get crashed, be about to be annihilated, but then you defend them and counterattack on their behalf.
