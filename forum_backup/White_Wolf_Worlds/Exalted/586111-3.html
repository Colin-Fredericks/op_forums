                                        I think the idea of Starmetal could be salvaged as born from and arising in the destined death (and perhaps rebirth in another form) of a god's immortal life... After the god has, in some sense, passed through the signs and waystations of the Maidens (all five?) and received their blessings and favour and thus taken on in part their essence. More of a natural product of a fall that would come anyway, plus repaying, or at least balancing, in a sense, the auspicious blessings of Heaven.

                                        But that would work only if there was the concept of destiny as representing a degree of proper, natural and I might even say dharmic* fulfilment and purpose to events, and to be more than just something the Sidereals rig to get what they want and make the world work out in ways that are suit their preferences.

                                        *Note - I definitely don't actually have hardcore enough philosophy to say if I'm using this properly!

                                            I think it's fairly straightforward to say that a star fell at the time something significant happened in Creation (to the point where you can theme an Artifact made from it around that association) because surely something interesting is happening somewhere in Creation every day. Does the star need to have fallen because the thing happened? Does the distinction matter? Just make your damn Artifact.

                                            Part of the image of starmetal to reflect Sidereals is that it's scarce and it really helps to know where it will turn up by being able to see the future, and you undermine that image when you say "it happens because X thing" because then people wonder why the Sidereals aren't doing X thing all the time, and then the books start actually saying that they're doing X thing all the time, and the whole thing become incoherent and sloppy. Commit to an image!

                                            I, personally, prefer the idea of the scarcity and the significance of prognostication, so I want starmetal to be kept simple.
