            My best interpretation is that if a spell has a duration like "until dismissed" (like Cirrus Skiff or Wood Dragon's Claw) the sorcerer's death might end it, but if there's a clear fixed duration then the spell should last as long as it lasts.

            So the demon remains for the rest of the year-and-a-day, and its Tie of loyalty remains. It will probably keep carrying out the last orders it was given, if any. If it has no remaining orders...I dunno. It's still loyal, so it's likely to be influenced by what it thinks its master would have wanted it to do, but I would not rely on a demon to have an accurate understanding of that sort.

            For some reason, I'm imagining a demon trying to seek out someone to help it get in touch with its master's ghost for further instructions. I don't think this idea would actually occur to most demons, though.

                        Originally posted by Warpwind View Post
                        Sort of made a rough concept. a water aspect archer specialisng in archery and war. Occult and Archery excellency form a bases, use water sphere for moving cover
                        Note that, even with Impervious Sphere of Water as a control spell, this doesn't work. The caster of Impervious Sphere can't take any non-reflexive actions, which attacking certainly counts as. All the control spell effect adds is the ability to move around. I was thinking you were talking about being a Water Aspect who was inside the sphere, but who wasn't the caster. I definitely don't think the caster should ever get to attack out of Impervious Sphere of Water. Full cover is way too big a bonus to allow the caster to both put it up, and ignore it.

                        Originally posted by Warpwind
                        Anyone knows if it's possible to take giant crab mount?
                        This is Exalted, most things are possible if they're sufficiently cool. :-) Crabs aren't provided with a Speed bonus in the Lunars manuscript (and the giant crabs they have stats for are just "not Tiny or Small", not necessarily huge crabs except relative to other crabs), so you'll have to work that out, but that should be all you need to use it as a mount.

                                                    It's kind of because of the way the setting works. A person's soul is made up of two halves, Hun and Po. The Hun being where the more personality related aspects of a person exist, and the Po being home to the more animal and base aspects of a person's being. Severing of those two halves literally splits the person into two beings, usually some kind of ancestor ghost or something and a hungry ghost, if the two do not go on to Lethe.

                                                    Mental illness is just a poisoning or flaw in the Hun soul that can be fixed in a lot of ways. The Occult way is to literally fight the demon.
