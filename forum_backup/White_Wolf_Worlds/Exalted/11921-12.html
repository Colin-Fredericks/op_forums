                    As the Neverborn stir in their deathly slumber, horrible mists gather in the Underworld and seep from Shadowlands into Creation at night. Some who enter those mists end up forever trapped in a bleak dream world populated by all manners of horrors. Those who give in to the malice permeating the place get rewarded with great boons by the Neverborn, at the price of getting stuck in horrible passion plays where they're forever tormented by their past failures until no shred of joy remains in their hearts. Ultimately, each such individual ends up ruling his own domain within the dream world as a nigh-omnipotent being, waging wars against each other through proxies, for the mists don't allow them to leave their domains and do battle in person. The most notable of those twisted lords are the bitter count overseeing his feudal domain from a looming castle, a knight in soulsteel armor commanding vast armies of undead, and an emaciated, horribly maimed sorcerer constantly plotting his escape.

                                    An apprentice thaumaturge, upon slaying a demon sent to assassinate him, Exalted as a Lunar. Now he works as a detective and unofficial protector of his city- a protector it sorely needs, being the battlefield of factions mortal, Exalted and fae. He rarely means to get caught up in all the magical shenanigans ensnaring the city, but somehow, trouble always seems to find him. He survives it with resilience, wit, and a motley collection of allies: a stern Dawn who works in law enforcement, an lustful spirit bound to a skull, an handsome Moonshadow trying to redeem himself, and a young Chosen of Serenity with a talent for disappearing.

                                                In the universe of Heaven's Reach, in a fledgling empire comfortably beyond the reach of His Divine Lunar Presence, there once was a young naval officer. At the beginning of her career, she was idealistic, naive, dedicated, and far more talented than anyone except her closest teachers realized. Now, twenty years, several wars, multiple diplomatic missions, a list of titles as long as her arm, a husband and wife and son, and a Zenith Exaltation later, she is still idealistic and dedicated, has gained wisdom and maturity, and become one of the most feared military names in the explored galaxy. She has won numerous battles, and more than a few allies for her star nation, including an even larger, albeit less advanced, empire that was once their bitterest enemy.

                                                However, even all her skills and willpower may not be enough. For the might of the central empire now sets itself against these backwater upstarts who have dared deny its authority. And they are not even the true threat... behind this move lies a secret network of demonic spies, festering in the heart of the galaxy, slowly advancing a plot by the Yozis and their servants to remake humanity in their own image.

                                                But the transhumanists have made one mistake. They, like so many others before, have underestimated the honor of the Empire of the Sphynx.
