                                                            Originally posted by Epee102 View Post


                                                            Lore is the odd one out--to my understanding, you have a Lore rating, and a 'specialty'(in this case, more like topic knowledge), as opposed to the seperate abillities. This is distinct from Martial arts and Craft.
                                                            So you wouldn't have Lore (history) and Lore(artifacts). You'd have Lore, and then keep track of topics your knowledgable in or sources of knowledge ( So Lore 5, with topics like Realm Education, Artifacts of the THreshold, History of Lookshy, etc).
                                                            Technically you don't need actual specialties to expand your knowledge base. You start play with a 'background' in lore which is not mechanically represented. This background reflect where you learned stuff so a background of 'tutored by the best scholars in the Imperial City' represents a much different knowledge base than 'got a memory dump from a first age artifact.

                                                            In play you can expand your knowledge base through roleplaying. Again, this is not mechanically represented. You don't need to spend xp on it. In the above examples, if you started out tutored in the Imperial City and then got a memory dump from a first age ruin, you'd know everything that the other guy knew plus a lot more. All this does is expand the range of stuff you can introduce facts about, however. It doesn't give you any extra dice unlike an actual specialty.
