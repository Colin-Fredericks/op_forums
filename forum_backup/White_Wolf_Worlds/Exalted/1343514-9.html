                    Alright, fine.

                    Well, I got a pet project. It's not very well done, but its something close to my heart.

                    The first time I ever read an LN was when I picked up a few novels by the name of Campione! from baka-tsuki. Campione is basically a setting where gods from the realm of myth and legend descend onto the mortal world. Bad news. Entering the mortal material realm apparently sends these highly spiritual beings berserk, turning them insane and either apathetic to mortal life at best or just plain malicious. What's worse is that their sheer power means that they cause chaos and destruction wherever they go, war gods causing strife and sun gods causing drought, etc.

                    Mortals in this place can use magic to enhance themselves to the level of first circle demons, and with a lot of effort, be able to launch 2nd-circle leve attacks once or twice. But in the face of literal gods, they fall back. The gods aren't like the bureaucratic field gods in Creation. These gods are approximately on the level of 2nd to 3rd circle demons, with the very strongest capable of wrecking entire cities in a matter of moments. What's worse is the existence of 'tiers'. In game terms, whenever a mortal fires out a magical attack at a divine being, they basically get a massive penalty on the effect, with lower effects simply not working and stronger ones greatly weakened.

                    The only people who can fight off these gods are Campione. People who by luck or by pure insanity managed to kill off a god, and via an old ritual, automatically get a portion of their powers. Problem. These are humans, by all standards, and getting god-like powers isn't helping. And it also gets worse since for a human to try and fight a deity means there is something really 'off' about him. Or at least, abnormal by human standards. Even the most benign and benelovent Campione constantly risks wiping out the human race via temporal paradoxes or destroys one famous world heritage site in every single volume.

                    In other words, humans are fucked, they're helpless against the gods/ campiones, and all the magical organisations can do is just flee, serve them as best as they can, or just try and redirect their attention.

                    The light novel itself isn't very good. Bland protag, standard tropes, harem... its basically a cut above the rest, but there's nothing special about it. At least, not compared to Re zero. But, eh, it was the gateway for Light Novels to me, and I think I should write a story about them.

                    So there are a bunch of characters. The main MC is one, but let's not talk about him, because he's bland as fuck. Instead, let's talk about 2 people! Or at least, 1. Erica Blandelli, basically the one who dragged the MC into the world of magic by pure accident. Child prodigy, who managed to at 16 years of age reach a position of power and skill most grown men don't reach in their 30s. Skilled fighter, highly intelligent, highly determined... and she's also refreshingly direct. No hopping about and stuttering about feelings and shit. She just comes up, wham, bam, and walks through to get what she wants. That includes using superstrength and mind control. She's partnered with her friend, Lilianna. Similarly skilled, but far less direct, more shy, and whatnot. Friends since childhood, and having a bond that's somewhere between enemies and close friends and rivals.

                    Erica's a blonde. Liliana has silver hair. I think you know what's happening.

                    So obviously, we have a chance for a solar-lunar bond thing here. I haven't gotten Fangs at the gate yet (not sure if its worth it) but I'm fairly sure that a solar-lunar combo with a supernal in at least a combat ability lets them fire out bullcrap that, if it cannot outright slay a third circle level being, can at least drive it off or make it think twice before fucking with them. And then there's the training they can carry out... I would like to see a political fic where they decide to rally the men around them, and drive off at least one or two gods and kill them, with the god's death being the equivalent of 'getting lots of xp'.

                    Of course, that can be boring. For instance, another idea would be an infernal exalted appearing. Mortals always fight the gods, the campiones, and the divine beasts they unleash... but being mortals, they just fail. Their spells die, their swords break, and their bodies are broken. The campione or god simply laughs, slays their loved ones, and walks off, content and confident in their power. No one can stop them... unless offered power. I mean, let's be goddamned honest here. Anybody would take the power to just, well, not get beaten down by the divine asshats that roam the earth. At which point, whether Malfean wrath, Cecylenian manipulation, or just plain ass combination of all so they can fight off the gods rampaging across the earth. Lots of people are willing to give up everything, just for a chance to fight the gods. And I'm fiarly sure that Malfeas and his ilk will be willing to help. Not to mention an Abyssal Exaltation works just as well. Someone slain by the casual backhand of a campione or Heretic god, and comes back to haunt them, fighting and hunting them down again and again, until the campione is crippled and weakened and just dies.

                    Then there's another idea. Alchemical exalted.

                    The magic organisations of the world are powerful, mighty, and incredibly influential. They command vast resources, millions of supersoldiers, magic that congress would give up its right eye for, wealth to beggar nations, and even more magical artifacts and mystic lore. The problem is that campiones and gods are so insanely powerful, that all this is useless. Dozens of the world's greatest magi working together might disrupt a campione if he's particularly distracted. Armies of supersoldiers die to greater-scale magics. All the money, all the resources in the world, can't make a dent in the face of divine opposition. And so, the problem is turning that money and power into something that can face the divine head on and win.

                    Enter the Alchemical exalted. Alchemical exalted need many things. Magical materials. Specialised clay. Entire teams of specialists. A heroic soul. And the approval of Autochthon himself. Now since we're writing a story, we can homebrew some shit so that they somehow get it. Maybe a divine revelation from one of their precogs and priestesses. Maybe a discovery of a cache, with it being labelled as a superweapon. But either way, they don't have much to lose. I mean, an entire nation could build an alchemical exalted, and there's nothing saying that the magical organisations can't do the same. Alchemical exalted can be described as an aircraft carrier or an ICBM. A piece of technomagical artistry that is built by a nation or state to serve its needs.

                    The organizations work together, pooling resources and setting up secret meetings. Trawling through the underworld and taking what they can find. Searching old mystic lore, digging up old sites, sacrificing age old relics for the needed materials, devoting the time and energy of thousands of their own experts to build this possible new... weapon.

                    The newcomer is less than stellar, and some lose heart. But others, realize what is happening. There is a spark of the divine here. By this time, all he has is excellencies, but that can soon change. New designs, new ideas, new charms... all there. Something reached through and empowered this... automaton, giving it power and ability when before it should simply be a pile of clay and expensive materials. More is added. The first one is not meant for combat, but instead for thinking. It is meant to build more of its own kind, to marshall resources to build even more charms and alchemicals. Slowly, ever so slowly, the Assembly is built, one by one...

                        I think it would be pretty great if Exaltations showed up in DC comics at the moment. The current major cosmic crisis involves Perpetua, the mother of the Monitor and the Anti-Monitor as well as their brother the World Forger, trying to harness the psychic energy of humans in a philosophy called "Doom" which is essentially social darwinism through the lens of supervillainy to transform the DC multiverse into it's stronger, eternal form with which she can challenge her own race of multiverse-creating cosmic deities. To do this she transformed Lex Luthor into a primordial human from the template she made in the very first version of reality by giving him technology to absorb Martian Manhunter, leaving him looking like a cross between Perfect Cell and Majin Buu. He then formed the Legion of Doom you might recognise from the DC Animated Universe, and went on a quest to give everyone on Earth presents at a time when the Justice League was coming under fire for accidentally cracking a hole in the multiverse while chaining up Barbatos: The cosmic bat-god who had been using nightmare versions of Batman made of dark matter (Shadow Slave Extraction, anyone?) to sink the positive matter portion of the multiverse into the Dark Multiverse beneath it.

                        In the comic the Justice League has been caught flatfooted due to, largely, underestimating the Legion of Doom. So I think Clark becoming a Zenith, Bruce becoming a Night, Diana becoming a Dawn, Barry becoming an Eclipse and Cyborg becoming a Twilight would give them the edge and awareness to respond quicker and fight on more of an even keel instead of being repeatedly outplanned. I see Arthur Curry as more of a Full Moon Lunar, and Kendra (Hawkgirl) currently has an arc where it is discovered that she is supposed to become the current version of reality's cosmic bird of prey to stop Perpetua (a previous one created the Source Wall originally to seal away Perpetua and her army of human-martian hybrids) so I'd say she'd make a good No Moon. J'onn (Martian Manhunter) himself is an interesting case because the current plot has him being Luthor's forgotten childhood friend which Luthor is conflicted about absorbing for power after recovering his memories while also being in a relationship with Hawkgirl, so I also see him as a Changing Moon Lunar.

                        Not all villains have thrown in with Perpetua, and I think it's the more independant ones that would likely earn the other Exaltations. Grail would be a prime Scourge. Mister Thunderbolt could be a Chosen of Secrets since he has the secret of the H-Dial. Black Adam and the Marvel Family are, in many ways, already DC's version of Exigents which is awkward because while a lot of gods would likely want them as their chosen DC deities being Manichean-style self aware ideas whose true forms exist outside the infinite physical dimensions within the Sphere of the Gods mean the Exigence bestower would likely try to sell them on exotic, novel powers since they largely specialise in Superman-like raw power on top of great magical potential. Controller Mu strikes me as a villainous Twilight, and while I'm not sure what Darkseid would be he's probably disqualified from Exaltation by being a god, even in broken aspect.
