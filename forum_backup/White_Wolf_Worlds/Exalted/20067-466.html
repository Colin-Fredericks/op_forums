                                Originally posted by wern212 View Post
                                What ability do you roll when influencing someone as part of a criminal activity?
                                For example, my Solar is in disguise as a Wyld Hunt member and is trying to convince someone that they need to check the inside of a vault for forbidden materials.
                                That it is part of a crime is irrelevant; this trying to claim authority which should be believable for the disguised solar to have (if the disguise worked)... the reasons given just need to be convincing enough. So it is Manipulation + Socialise or Presence; I'd lean towards Presence (Intimidation) for invoking fear of anathema (or fear of crossing a Wyld Hunt), but you could Socialize the heck out of the customs and prestige of the Wyld Hunt. Possibly Performance if you play up the "acting to pull off a con" aspect, which you might stunt for some "with outrageous chutzpa".
                                Originally posted by wern212 View Post
                                Alternatively, when using the charm Clever Bandit's Rook, what ability do I use then?
