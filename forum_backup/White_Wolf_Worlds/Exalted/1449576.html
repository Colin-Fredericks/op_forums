    [What-If] A portal appears to Creation

    05-25-2021, 03:27 PM
    One day while you're setting in your room a portal opens up in your house. Looking through the portal you can see that is a portal leading into a city of Creation. You are not sure where exactly in Creation, but you just somehow know. A voice in your head tells you that you have one hour to prepare. You then have an overwhelming feeling and knowledge that going into this portal is the best thing you can possibly do.

    How do you prepare? What would you bring with you?

    Also in case you ask "Does X work in Creation" or "Do I know X", then the answer is you don't know. Those are things you'll need to account for yourself.

    Read my shit at my homebrew topic, 2.5e and 3e material!
    Play Alchemical's in 3e now, you're welcome.

                                      After thinking about it for a few days, I suppose if I had to jump through a portal into a shithole, I would first start by grabbing my 1750s reenacting gear.

                                      You can't go wrong with wool and linen. Linen shirt, woolen waistcoat, woolen coat, woolen breeches, woolen stockings, woolen leggings, leather moccasins. Add in some hats (slouch-hat for the sun, knit-hat for the cold), some mittens and a matchcoat (a woolen blanket made to wear as a cloak), and I will be good pretty much anywhere in Creation other than the depths of the South. Best yet, everything is capable of being mended or outright replaced with stuff found in Creation, unlike modern gear

                                      Camping equipment? Some more woolen blankets, another linen shirt and pair of woolen stockings, a wooden bowl and spoon. A brass kettle is going to be much lighter than a cast-iron pot. My gourd-canteen is essentially foolproof. A couple knives and 2 axes (a forest axe and a tomahawk) round out tools.

                                      Other equipment? Some flint-and-steel as firestarting equipment. A length of hemp rope. Some whiskey or brandy, I suppose, maybe even some Everclear. An entire modern First Aid Kit dumped into a sack, along with a big ol' bottle of Doxycyline and several tubes of Triple Antibiotic Cream. Most of a medicine-cabinet, ranging from painkillers to stool-softeners to anti-diarrheals and more. Plenty of sugar, salt, tea, maybe some spices if you wanna get frisky. At least 2-3 pounds-worth of flour, or failing that parched nocake (hominy corn). Beans and peas.

                                      In my opinion, if you aren't taking a gun, you are doin' it wrong. Why would I (unless I Exalted, which is far from a guarantee), a soft doughy-ass modern city-dweller, want to fight in melee if I had any other option? Even a modern cartridge-based firearm would be worth its weight in gold, at least until the bullets ran out and the gun becomes dead-weight. Myself?

                                      Im bringin' my musket.

                                      Unlike modern firearms, every part of a musket is capable of being manufactured in Creation. I can get spare parts from effectively any competent Blacksmith in Creation, and the gun itself is stupid simple, just a couple of flat-springs that can be removed using tools I carry in my shooting bag. Just 3 lbs of lead is 60 shots, and I can carry tools to make more of the lead that is presumably well-known in any civilized part of Creation. 1 lb of blackpowder is a little under 100 shots in my gun, and I can carry multiple pounds...... and given a close reading of the "Harbor Cannon" in Arms of the Chosen, Firedust works the same way Blackpowder does, which is amusing. (Black powder burns, it doesn't explode like modern smokeless powder).

                                      The 20 gauge smoothbore musket was used to kill everything on the North American continent, from mice to men to moose, so I am confident it could handle most appropriately-sized things in Creation. I would love to have a pistol to match the musket, as a backup, but I don't own one IRL (looking for one, though!)
