                        *This is what I have seen before about them, posted by ??? (long version) and I don't know anything about "Alex Mercer" so that wasn't a helpful example.
                        Flesh Aspects like Mercer are created by those motivated by vengeance; they're stitching corpses together to create weapons to destroy their enemies. The original Alex Mercer unleashed Blacklight to spite and hurt Blackwatch and humanity in general, and accidentally created his greatest possible weapon against both. Charms of the Flesh Aspect not only allow for more efficient cannibalism, but also for feats of physical, muscular power and athleticism.

                        Blood Aspects are created by those motivated by lust or similarly possessive emotions. Dame Crimson, the signature Blood Aspect, was stitched together from a number of priestesses that served a Forest King. The god murdered them all because they each had some imperfection that displeased him, and he tried to combine them all into a single perfect priestess. Instead, he got Dame Crimson, who murdered him right back. Possibly related, she goes on to seduce another god's priest, which is appropriate to the lusty nature of Blood Aspect Chernozem. Charms of this Aspect can track people through blood, manipulate blood-born diseases, and navigate waters, among other fluid talents.

                        Marrow Aspects are created by those motivated by scientific curiosity; they're more interested in the process and the art than in the specific result. [Presumably like Dr. Frankenstein.] Likewise, Chernozem of this Aspect are talented at delving into secrets. Oh, and they can manipulate bones, giving themselves sharp weapons and hard armor.

                        Breath Aspects are created by those motivated by sheer nihilism -- I guess from a desire to destroy the world or desecrate the dead? Either way, these Chernozem are good at manipulating air and blending in with humans.

                        Soil Aspects are created by those motivated by sorrow and regret -- a lover trying to bring back his best friend rather than his sex-pal, or perhaps a parent (or child) looking to bring back his child (or parent). These Chernozem are best at raising and controlling zombies, as well as reading omens and seeing prophecies.

She tends to try to communicate more through like, atavistic impulses, dreams, and stuff of her Chosen. Why the Shoguante is implied to do maybe with the rise of the Deathlords, but again, all we know is she didn't have Exalts until then.

                                Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                3. Please share, what are the new versions of the Aspects?* What type of origin does each have? What are their animas and aspect markings like?
                                Their Aspects are more or less as in the 3e corebook: Blood, Breath, Flesh, Marrow, Soil. The main thing that results in their Aspect is kind of the emotional resonance of whomever was trying ot create them. Their Aspect markings and anima reflect this as well.
                                • Children of Blood result from desire, greed, and ambition. They tend to become pale, eyes become full with blood and dark water begins to drip from their body. Think a drowning victim I guess.
                                • Children of Breath are from regret or repentence. Aspect markings involve pale skin, foul winds, and their voice seeming quieter. I think the vibes are someone who's suffocated.
                                • Children of Flesh are from rage and vengeance. When they spend too much Essence the patchwork nature of their bodies become more obvious, with stiches becoming more pronounced and ichor oozing between gaps.The vibe I get is the corpse going all-out.
                                • Chidlren of Marrow are from curiousity and obssession. The description of their markings is akin to dessicaiton. Think how hair and nails and teeth become more pronounced as rigor mortus kicks in.
                                • Children of Soil are born from sorrow and yearning. Their bodies become covered in grave dirt and things like worms or other aspects of rot show through.

                                Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                4. What kind of martial arts, sorcery, and necromancy can they access?
                                They are on in the same assumed "area of play" as Dragon-Blooded. So they deal with Terrestrial keyword with Martial Arts, can access Ivory Circle Necromancy, sometimes Terrestrial Circle Sorcery instead. They are as per Arms of the Chosen neutral with soulsteel and Dissonant with other materials, again, as per usal for most Exalts meant for Terrestrial play.

                                Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                ​5. How many Liminals are there in the Time of Tumult?
                                Last it came up a couple years ago from devs around 250, but this is subject to change or never be brought-up.

                                Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                6. Please describe the Castes of Getimians? I know they have been described, but I know nothing about them except names. What kind of "people" are they Chosen from, what are their animas and caste marks?
                                The general thing is the actions that would have made them worthy of Exaltation in their Origins determines their Caste. The Castes are:
                                • Spring - Brought beauty to the world in some way. Typically chairsmatic, inspiring, exemplary.
                                • Summer - Brought change to their world thorugh some form of conquest. War, religious reform, coups, revolutions, rebellions, that sort of thing.
                                • Autumn brought change to their Origins in some way that helped others. Saving the meek, protecting the weak, curing the sick. They sacrificed their time and energy to make other's lives better.
                                • Winter did something to the world thorugh mystic force or reformation of socieites. New academic paradigms, being sorcerer-kings, educating the masses, bringing major cultural reform.
                                Their Castemarks and such are rendered here in order. Spoilered tos ave space.
