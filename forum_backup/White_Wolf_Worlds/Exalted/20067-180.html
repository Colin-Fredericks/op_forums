                                        Originally posted by Korax View Post
                                        I am deeply enamored with the Occult Charm tree, but I have a question about possession.

                                        Burning Exorcism Technique and the charms that follow it are contingent on:

                                        “rolling (Wits + Occult) against the Resolve of the possessing spirit, or the morbidity of a spiritual malaise”.

                                        Yet, I can find no mention of spirits that possess or spiritual malaises anywhere else in the book. The sections on Disease and Disease Gods make no mention of these sorts of problems.
                                        Am I missing something? Did I overlook it? If not, how can I expect to use these charms if my GM has no idea how to handle such things?
                                        Morbidity is the difficulty of rolls against a disease. For example, Puppeteer's Plague (aka SKELETON FEVER) is a magical disease that could be purged with Burning Exorcism Technique, and has a listed Morbidity of 3. For other magic junk that feels like it definitely should fall under the heading of "spiritual malaise" but doesn't have stats, the Storyteller can just assign an analogous 1-5 difficulty.

                                        Possessing spirits are a thing that exist all over in Creation (I can't remember if any are written up in the antagonist section; I think so?). If the Storyteller doesn't want to spend time statting the spirit, they can just go with the Quick Character rules for social actions and picking Resolve on page 495—which basically boils down to, Resolve 1-2 is averageish, Resolve 3-4 is good at sticking to your guns, and Resolve 5-6 is the awesome high end.
