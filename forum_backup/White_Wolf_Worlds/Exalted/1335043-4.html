                                    So long as you realize this is horribly broken. Like massively, horribly broken beyond any hope of saving (unless the speed doesn't stack with other sources and turns off in combat, maybe -- even then).

                                    Cannoicly, an Eclipse-oid (I did it with an Essence 10 Fiend) can reach a max speed of 1920 Yards Per Tick on a Reflexive Flying Dash (that's 3927MPH and a little over Mach 5, or 26 yards/tick walking which shenningans into 106 miles per hour indefinite marching speed, or 548mph indefinite running). So (Post Crisis Wally) Flash speed is possible 2E RAW... but it's hard.

                                    And it's hard to stop backpeddling archers. Increasing speed like this breaks the game. (And god help you if an Eclipse-oid gets their hands on this -- double speed plus five-times speed on Mach 5.16.... *drool*.)

                                    Likewise it's possible to hit Strength+Athletics of 93 for Feats of Strength (which conservatively is at least lift 21 tons without a roll). But it's hard because that's not what Exalted is about. It's magically enhanced skill, not static superpowers.

                                    (You can also reach unbound speeds in a race or when facing a deadline but I don't count those. The Prince of Iron and Fire also had Telescopic, Microscopic, Mass Pentrating (i.e. xray) vision by the end of that campaign. Man that was fun. He also shot lazer beams from his eyes and wore a cape, but that's totally not what Exalted (even 2E) is supposed to be about.)
