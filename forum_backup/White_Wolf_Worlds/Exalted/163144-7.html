    Half-Lidded Thousand-Mile Stare
    Solar Integrity.

    Activated in response to any effect that would affect her mind (such as illusions, mutations, or a simple conversation), the Solar roots himself in the mundanity of the attempt. Her eyelids lower, her eyes look forward as though beyond the initiator of the effect in an expression of such ultimate apathy that not only negates the attempt, but makes the target lose points of willpower equal to the Solar's Integrity + essence as the vile creature feels it's confidence slip away in the face of such an unimpressionable juggernaught of stoicity. Additionally, this charm adds the Solar's Integrity + Essence to any contested roll to resist mental effects.

        Shrew Summons Thunder
        Infernal Larceny

        Though it pains the Infernal to deny the world his full and proper glory, the sweet reveal allows some to weather the burden. When disguised as an individual who is noticed but unremarked upon, such as with "Rainclouds Hide Lightning," the Warlock may cast off this disguise in an obvious and grandiose fashion. Those who had been taken in by his ruse have their senses dashed by the reveal, as though by a roar of thunder after hours of silence, while those unfooled fare little better. The Infernal affects all witnesses in the area with a free and reflexive use of Sense-Stealing Storm, though those who had not been deceived receive a bonus to resist. If the Warlock spent any motes on the action in which he reveals himself, he may reduce the motes spent by 1, to a minimum cost of 1. If the action was casting a Sorcery spell, unveiling a Shintai, or using any effect directly using storms, lightning or thunder, the reduction becomes 3 motes.

                Apocalypse Serpent's Embrace
                Dragon-Blooded Martial Arts. Prereq of As in the Beginning and Smashing Tidal Wave Technique (yes, requires War and Martial Arts)

                The Dragon-Blooded focuses the apocalyptic forces of As in the Beginning into a single person, filling them with fire and fury and destruction. Roll a martial arts attack against a single target. If successes, the primal forces of the ancient world rise up, strike down, and surround the victim. The Dragon-Blooded needs time to draw such potent elemental energies to herself, dependent on the threshold successes of the roll before the effects described below occur.

                With Air, the sky darkens as clouds roll overhead, and lightning strikes a thousand times in a single spot, dealing bashing damage and stunning the victim. With Earth, the land shutters and splits like a gaping maw, swallowing the victim and crushing them, stranding them hundreds of yards beneath the surface. With Fire, molten fire erupts from the ground like a volcano, engulfing the victim and incinerating them with lethal damage. At sea, Water can be used, a tidal wave reaching up to grasp the victim before pulling them down hundreds of yards into an terrible vortex dealing bashing damage and immediately causing the victim to check for drowning. Wood makes life erupt from all surfaces, growing thick and encasing the victim in thorny vines, dealing bashing damage and poisoning the victim with a venom that never runs out as long as the clinch is maintained.

                If the Dragon-Blooded hasn't taken steps to continue the grapple without physically touching the target (such as using Bonds of Unbreakable Ice), then the Dragon-Blooded is subject to the same effects as the victim. If the Dragon-Blooded manages to maintain the clinch the same time as in the beginning, she may activate this Charm again without paying its Willpower cost. This can be reiterated until the clinch is broken or the Dragon-Blooded can no longer pay the mote cost of this Charm.

                            Originally posted by Ellis View Post
                            Dreaming Butterfly Prana
                            Getimian Intelligence
                            "Never assume what you see and feel is real."

                            The Getimian draws a deep breath, focusing intently on a life that may never -- perhaps could never -- have been. This life need not be human, nor even of human-like intelligence. When she exhales, a new pattern spider takes shape in her mind's eye. If she paid for this Charm with motes from her Yang pool, this spider emerges smoothly and painlessly from her forehead. It proceeds at her mental command to the forehead of someone sleeping, where it descends smoothly and painlessly inside to begin its task. If she paid for this Charm with motes from her Yin pool, the spider instead simply begins its task the next time the Getimian sleeps. The spider weaves for the sleeper a powerful dream, a day in the life of the one the Getimian invented.

                            When she wakes, the sleeper is convinced that the dreamed life is her true life, and that her waking life is in fact a dream, albeit one with which she is generally inclined to play along. She receives a number of new Intimacies and Virtues to reflect the dreamed life -- including a Major Bond to her dreamed life being true -- and remains aware of all Intimacies and Virtues of her waking life, but these particular waking traits do not directly affect her. Instead, she gains a Minor Principle to play out her waking life, which is treated as a Major Principle with regard to any of her waking life's Defining Intimacies and any Virtues of 4 dots or more. She receives an extra die on all actions at which the dreamed life would excel, and loses one on all actions at which the dreamed life would be inept.

                            If there is any information her dreamed life would know that her waking life would not or could not (or about which her waking life is mistaken), she becomes certain the answer is something her waking life would find plausible. She does receive a two die bonus to any Lore, Occult, or similar rolls to know things her dreamed life would know, if said rolls are reasonable to make at all. Any Charms or other capabilities of her waking life may be used just as easily as ever, though the dreamed life may have certain compunctions against their use (e.g. a Lunar given the dreamed life of a delicate fop is unlikely to drink any heart's blood). The character may obviously not use any powers or capabilities possessed only by the dreamed life, though this may not stop the character from trying (e.g. a Solar with the dreamed life of a voracious raksha may seek to sup on another's Virtues, and then feel quite silly afterward).

                            The Charm's effects end on their own after a number of days set by the Getimian, not to exceed her Intelligence. The target wakes as though from a dream, in which she was the dreamed self living out all of those events. She rolls Integrity + Willpower to remember them fully, but otherwise remembers no more details than she would from any dream, though relevant sights or accounts may remind her. If the target's Major Bond to her dreamed life is fully degraded by any means, the Charm's effect ends prematurely. The Getimian may also will it to end early at any time (or at any time she can perceive the target, if paid out of her Yang pool).

                                        Grinding Spiral of Eternity
                                        Necromancy spell of the Third Circle

                                        Some speculate that there is an unknown Primordial embodying the concepts of cycles and singularities, who was destroyed or slain and whose only legacy is this spell. Pricking her fingers and ears, the necromancer gestures violently at the ground beneath her feet, which collapses into a sinkhole. This is the beginning of the end.
                                        The sinkhole is a portal straight to Oblivion, with all that that entails. Furthermore, the terrible weight of the Labyrinth sucks at Creation around its wound, pulling it down like a solid whirlpool. Structures in the area take gradual but massive amounts of damage as their frames warp along the lines of a spiral, while living things suffer mutations and insanity without rhyme or reason. The closer anyone approaches to the center of the spiral, the more powerful its effects are. The spell lasts until (unlikely and suitably occult circumstances), at which point the entire area affected by the Grinding Spiral is sucked into the sinkhole and disappears. Yet anyone who sheds Essence-charged blood on the spot where the sinkhole lay may reopen it, and again after it is closed. The spell can only be permanently broken by Third Circle countermagic.

                                            Originally posted by Ellis View Post
                                            First And Final Breath
                                            Lunar Intelligence (Necromancy Charm)

                                            The Moon-Chosen Necromancer sets a second layer of complexity onto the preparations and materials for a Sorcerous working to create life. This second layer may take any fashion that is appropriate (encircling the circle of glowing runes with a second circle of dark ones, carving harsh sigils into the flesh of anointed offerings, surrounding a pyramid with spires of black crystal, etc.). This complete, the Lunar may begin readying the working. This Necromantic working must be complete before the Sorcerous working is, though it takes a fraction of the time and effort. It is permitted to complete this days in advance, and to conceal the marks of its application.

                                            If successful, when the Sorcerous working completes, the entity it meant to create takes form just as intended. However, the entity draws one breath and one breath only before the Necromantic energy floods through it. Seizing on the departing Sorcerous energies used to create it, the black spell draws as horrific or beautiful a cry as the creature can produce and then strikes it dead, refashioning it into a creature of death. The Necromancer may add whatever dread enhancements that are within his power to bestow, drawing on all the life the entity was unable to live. It is bound to the Necromancer as a ghost would be, and its living form added to the Lunar's library of forms.

                                            The Necromancer may affect his own Sorcerous workings with this Charm, but will generally find it more efficient -- and more satisfying -- to taint the efforts of others.

                                                    Dragonblooded Lore

                                                    This charm almost completely severs the tie between the Dragonblood and their ancestors. The Dragonblood can spend XP to purchase up their breeding as though it were favored charms when they first acquire this charm, cutting away their mortal heritage, but the true effect of the charm is to cut them off from their line of descent- from now on, for all magical purposes they have no ancestors, and any effect which would give them ancestors automatically fails, and they become incapable of answering to any surname they had once inherited.                                                         Originally posted by Ellis View Post
                                                        Sign of Impossible Stars
                                                        2nd circle necromancy

                                                        The necromancer spits blood into the sky and forms a blasphemous mudramudra known only to the neverborn. The blood spreads, as though over a glass dome, to form morbid astrological signs and reveal the stars of the underworld. For a month and a day, the area counts as being part of the underworld for the purposes of astrology, and any beings in the area seem to disappear from the loom of fate. For the duration of this spell, the necromancer has access to some effects similar to Sidereal astrology, but that take on a deathly and grim twist and any paradox that would be accrued becomes resonance.


                                                        Edit for PlotVitalNPC's Edit:
                                                        Heart for the Heartless
                                                        Lunar Charm - pre-req: Harmony with Reality Technique

                                                        This subtle charm allows a Lunar to imbue a wyld creature (one that can but doesn't have a Heart Grace) with enough volition to want a Heart grace, and enough individuality and Reality to be able to steal Graces as though a Creation-Born. Alternatively, if the creature does have a Heart Grace in it's possession, the lunar can spend an additional [cost] in order to attempt to transplant the grace into the creature with a successful [intelligence + craft(glamour)] roll at a difficulty of the Heart Grace's rating + 2, giving it a sense of self at the cost of the original owner's self-awareness. Failing the roll means instant calcification for the creature as all its potential bleeds out into the world in a display which many commoner raksha would consider horrific.
                                                        ​
                                                        This charm does not function for transplanting the hearts of Noble Raksha with an Essence Rating higher than the Lunar's.
