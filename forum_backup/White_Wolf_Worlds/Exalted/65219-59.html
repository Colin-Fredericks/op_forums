                                IRL, "totem" refers to a specific thing, usually animal, that a group claims to be descended from. Many fantasy settings have misused the term heavily (especially confusing them with totem poles), but Exalted can match it pretty easily:
                                Each Lunar Exaltation corresponds to a specific animal, distributed across the animal kingdom (more densely among cooler species, of course). That Exaltation will always hold that animal as its spirit shape. In the First Age, Lunars were taught to revere their totem animal as they would Luna herself, and counted themselves literally kin to that animal. The Silver Pact holds no such thing, but it does teach respect for the Lunar's totem animal.

                                                            There was no Solar Deliberative. The Solar Exalted's rule of the world was much more complicated.

                                                            There were multiple parliaments where some Solars sat, but by no means all, nor even many. In some states, Solars were the chieftains of chiefs, patriarchs and matriarchs of large nation-spanning clans, technocratic sorcerer-kings of magocracies, military despots, theocratic priest-lords, and of course, emperors and monarchs aplenty. Some eschewed being the center of power, supporting Lunar, Exigent, Dragon-Blooded, or even the odd Sidereal rulership. Some chose not to rule at all, preferring to travel incognito, enforcing their heroism at the point of the blade or the barrel of a firewand.
