                                    In the Modern Age, popular entertainment is made of watching heroic Dragon-Bloods who, while lacking military discipline, still possess heroic motives and skills. Broadcast live as they stop various villains and criminals, these heroes are part superhero, part marketing extravaganza, and part celebrity. One hero lacks discipline, causing monumental property damage over his career, and when his contract is sold as his sponsor company goes out of business, he ends up partnered with a new, up-and-coming hero with the exact same Charms. The difference between the two? The vet is in this because he wants to help people, while the new guy is in it as a business. He doesn't even use a code name!

                                    Something lurks in the shadows, though, threatening more than the duo's strained relationship. The newcomer hides a grudge for the murder of his parents, and a shadowy organization has to have been behind it. This organization's tendrils have gotten into more than any hero could expect, however, and may reach into the very top levels of their government... or possibly even the entire hero business.

                                            Okay, new one:

                                            A manse discovered near Nexus has become an extremely popular entertainment site, having been converted into a restaurant. It has even been discovered to have working First Age automata to serve and entertain guests! However, the manse's power systems are failing. During the day, when the Daystar can provide it energy to spare, it keeps the automata in pristine working condition... but at night, the essence fields keeping the automata in check collapse, unable to be sustained without proper power. The machines have begun to believe that they are the proper shape of living things, and that humans are simply the bare bones of a mechanical exoskeleton that needs a proper covering, one to match their own razor-sharp-metal-filled suits.

                                            All of this is discovered by a lone security guard, set to watch over the manse at night. With dwindling power, he must keep the automata from reaching him and forcing him into a deathtrap, relying only on the surveillance systems and faulty door locks to keep the murderous machines away from him.
