                04-19-2018, 10:58 AM
                Originally posted by Growls View Post
                1)
                Static Values and Charms that use them as effects. In this case, I'm refering to Doe Eyed Defense (DED).
                I've been told that DED does not use the modified Guile and only the raw Guile. So, if for instance that my Black Claw Stylist with Guile 4 has acquired Guarded Thought Meditation, and commited it, ending your Guile at 7, the value used by the DED would still be 4. Correct?
                Yes. That's how I intended it to work, although I may have conveyed that poorly.

                2)
                Penalties and Battle Groups.
                I've run into a bit of a snag, and I'm using DED again. Battlegroups attack the following way.
                1) melee everyone in contact. This is an attack at everyone, and DED would not apply, as the attack is not targetting her specifically.
                2) Ranged against Black Claw Stylist as Primary Target. You can pop DED and defend your entire group with the penality-stricken Battlegroup.
                3) Ranged against someone within Close of the Black Claw Stylist. The Stylist cries as she gets hammered by the splash Damage.
                So, we generalize this to "Area of Effect attacks vs. defensive magic that penalizes attacks against a single character." There's no standardized rule for handling that, so it's ultimately up to the Storyteller. They could say that the penalty applies for everyone, or, if they think that's too powerful, they could have the attacker roll the penalized pool first, to determine if it hits the character using the defense, and then roll the rest of the dice to determine whether it hits everyone else.

                3)
                Do charms that have a duration "Until Next Turn" mean until turnover or to the USER'S next turn.
                The latter.
