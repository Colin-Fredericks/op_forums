            Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
            I don't see why those two are mutually exclusive. There can be multiple kings with divine rights and things can get ugly.
            in-universe they might not be, but tonally they are. The Solars are chosen by God to rule, you may not question their authority, for their right to dominate lesser beings has been ordained by the highest power, and is a metaphysical truth of Creation. That's the exact reasoning that absolute monarchs and dictators have used for millennia on real Earth. Pharaohs, emperors and kings have killed people for daring to question their rule, because to question their rule is to question god, and that is a sin. A king doesn't need the consent of the people he's ruling, every one of them could despise him and still it would be wrong to replace him because god wills that he rule.

            Which could be fine for a fantasy world, sure, but every other blatant lie used by corrupt powerful people to keep power in the real world isn't true in Exalted. The Realm isn't a shining example of culture that uses its immense power to raise up the unfortunate kingdoms of the Threshold and give them a better life, that's just, basically, a lie told by empires to justify why they should rule you, and you shouldn't rule yourself. In Exalted the rebels fighting their government aren't pure and innocent freedom lovers who never kill anyone that doesn't deserve it, and will make everything better for sure just as soon as they get to be in power.

            But then we get to the divine right of kings as it pertains to the celestial Exalted and suddenly yeah, no, that's totally how it works. If you're a king, and one of your subjects exalts Solar, you should abdicate and put them in charge. Doesn't matter if they're a cruel tyrant, or kind of a dumbass who's exaltation makes them an amazing sailor but kind of a shit negotiator/planner, it is their right to rule and you mustn't question it.

                *raises hand*

                If I may point out, that Exalted doesn't exactly give two shits about the moral power of gods. An interpretation of the Unconquered Sun is that he's basically someone that's coked out, negligient, and basically doesn't give too much of a shit about the world. And another interpretation is that the Exaltations are superweapons set loose onto the world, never to be held down save by great immense effort. The Unconquered Sun is Lucifer, the MorningStar who rose up against his creator, and won. He then bequeathed the Mandate of Heaven upon the Exalted, having wrested it from the Primordials.

                Yes, in Exalted, there is literally divine power floating around, latching around to souls whom the highest and most mighty god of all decide have his favor. Yes, the very laws of metaphysics scream and declare that these people have the right to rule. Yes, their powers are incredible and even the weakest Dragonblooded is an deadly and powerful being. Yes, the gods declare that these have the Mandate.

                But that doesn't mean jack shit. The Mandate was from the Primordials, whom the King of Heaven wrested from their bloodied hands right before fucking off to the X-box and cocaine. The gods are rarely good, at best apathetic, and most of all venal, greedy, and capricious. Being in their favor doesn't mean jack shit, because in the end they were divine bureaucrats who were former slaves, and have little to no moral authority in their own. The Big Good of the setting has my props for not being totally evil, but apathy and overlooking the corruption of Heaven is a sin in and of itself.

                In other words: There is no contradiction to the other things in Exalted (The Realm, Rebels, etc) because yes, they have divine right of kings. But divine right of kings is dependent on the gods being the ultimate moral authority, and when they don't have that, the entire thing crumbles.

                edit: Tl;dr fuck the divine right of kings, murder the gods and topple their thrones

                    That’s true, but what changes it is that it’s not just the gods being dicks. Water flows, stars shine, the things that die are gone forever, and the celestial exalted have the right to rule over all others, each of these is a fact of Creation. With a Dragonblooded in charge they physically can not be content, because a servant being in charge is just wrong, and nothing will ever be truly right until they get back in their place.

                    Which I think just isn’t good for Exalted. It can be fine in other media, but in Creation it just sticks out as desperate from everything else.
