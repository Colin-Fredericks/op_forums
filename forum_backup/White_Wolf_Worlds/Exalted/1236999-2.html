                                                Just dipping my toes in on this one, but I kind of see that there's going to be a particular set of circumstances that leads to this.

                                                1. A large number of them are going to be love matches between two gay DBs which happen to fulfill whatever extra criteria their Houses feel necessary to justify the exception and having to go to adoption/sorcery/other methods to produce the necessary heirs. This isn't a bug in the system, as it provides a major location for adopting orphans in a way that keeps the DBs in the house rather than giving them to the Immaculate Order, or for the sorcery using ones, because it begets favors owed and alliances solidified.

                                                2. As for the other case, it's possible that you might get two heterosexual DBs in an arranged gay marriage because it benefited the Houses involved, but unless it was a one-in-a-million chance ("Hey, if Gary marries John, that means our allied houses control the entire spice trade in Three Bows, and both of them are only kids"), I don't see the Houses who are concerned with their heirs having kids deliberately setting that up in a way that makes that harder. A match like Ledaal Kes and Ragara Szaya, where both parties aren't sexually compatible with each other but their personalities mesh excessively well might be possible, but that's closer to a love match as seen in the first case than an arranged marriage both partners aren't happy with because their Houses say so.

                                                tl;dr: When DB houses want their kids to make kids, they're not going to make it harder by matching up two that can't make them the normal way unless it'd help make them ALL THE MONIES.
