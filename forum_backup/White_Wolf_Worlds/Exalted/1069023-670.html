            I'm still confused about progenitive Essence. Dragon-Blooded Exaltation is hereditary and nothing (except maybe successful Feng Yi pilgrimage) can influence who does and does not Exalt. But you don't Exalt unless your parents progenitive Essence is at its peak? The blood of the Dragon can skip many generations, but mortals do not have progenitive Essence? Leftover children are disdained, but the wording of the preview makes it sound like they CAN Exalt, occasionally, despite their parents profligacy? One of a multiple birth gets the full force of the parents' progenitive Essence, but it's not unheard of for the other children to Exalt? I just can't wrap my head around how this all fits together.

It's a major factor in the odds of Exaltation, but sometimes a child just doesn't Exalt despite their parents do everything that they could have - and conversely, sometimes a completely unexpected Exaltation occurs.

                        Hi, I love what you guys are doing with Exalted! Arms of the Chosen is an utter joy, not just for the new toys and the mechanics but for the delicious, bite-sized plot hooks present in every artifact write-up. That book has sparked so much inspiration for me. And the Dragonblooded previews are giving me many happy and excited feelings. Enthusiastic thumbs up!

                        I just finished reading this whole gargantuan thread over the course of several weeks, and now that I find myself at its end, such is my unquenchable thirst for Exalted knowledge that I must ask you MORE QUESTIONS!

                        I realise that actual mechanics for Yennin are a long way out and probably not nailed down, but do you have any recommendations on how to hack them together as NPCs in the meantime? Are they able to use essence? Or at least, are the mightiest of their number (the ones who can go toe to toe with dragonbloods and sorcerer-princes) essence-users? I find it hard to believe that good stats and supernatural merits could allow a mortal with no command of essence to match a dragonblood for power. Should I go ahead and give them essence pools and supernatural Martial Arts/custom Charms?

                        What about more typical Yennin, the 'everyday folk' of Volivat? Could they be represented with just high stats and some extra merit points? A nation where everyone uses the starting Solar Attribute spread sure sounds superhuman to me.

                        Is it the intention that "Yennin Solar" is a viable character concept, or are they too powerful for that to be balanced without them losing their Yennin traits? I guess I should be asking if they're human enough to be Chosen at all? Will they be their own 'minor splat' that doesn't interact with exaltation, but has its own power-set, like Dragon Kings or ghosts, or a mortal-adjacent minor splat that's still compatible with exaltation, like god-bloods?

                        Lunar question! By way of a detour into Solar territory. We have the story of Panther, who was a right and proper asshole before the Unconquered Sun filled him with new purpose. So there's precedent for the UCS choosing people not because they're already righteous but because they have the potential to be. Luna is a capricious and mischievous witch-goddess. Would she ever choose someone in a similar fashion, someone whose worldview or personality was utterly unsuitable up until that point? Trickster archetypes have a long history of tricking or punishing people to teach them lessons that better them, and crashing a board meeting to turn someone into a shapeshifting god-beast who is hunted and despised by most of civilised society is a hell of a trick.

                        Thanks for being such good sports with all our questions even though you're obviously working really hard. You rock!

                        "Measure of Hope is right about everything." - Wise Old Guru

                        Currently running an Exalted 2.5 Abyssals game in a homebrew modern shard because I value neither my time or my sanity, and I'm loving almost every minute of it.
