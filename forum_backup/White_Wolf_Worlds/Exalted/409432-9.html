                    Well, the impression I've always gotten is that it puts people who think the Unconquered Sun is really horrible at his job at best or a noxious, drug-addicted hypocrite at worst into a bit of a bind re: Abyssals. They can either labor under the Abyssal curse and know that just about everything they do is going to redound to the benefit of the Neverborn in some way... or they can pursue the obvious way to get out from under that curse, which is to get the blessing and approval of someone they hold in contempt who is getting high as a kite in the Jade Pleasure Dome in-between blowjobs from Saturn to lift it.
                    It seems to me this is a profound Character Concept Fail.

                    It's fine to play any character as rejecting their patron and making their own way. A Solar may very well throw up the grand middle finger to the Unconquered Sun and never follow the visions he's given, no matter how insistent they become. An Abyssal might renouce his leige and resolve to do the best he can, shouldering the burden of the dark fate. That's all fine. But an Abyssal seeking redemption is explicitly requesting the mandate of the Unconquered Sun. It's rediculous to suggest that this character should expect to say 'I hold you and everything you are in contempt, but please empower me!'.

                    My question, looking at this would not be 'Why is the Sun the way he is?', but 'why does this player, rejecting basically every long term option for an Abyssal, want to play and Abyssal in the first place?'. Unless the story is that good intentions are always blunted, even after death (which might be made into a good story, for those so inclined!) it seems like it's best for everyone that these players were just not playing Abyssals in the first place.
