But there is a lot of range for disguise in which they would not be, where I think it would not be an intrinsic thing. Exchange one identity where you're fully covered up for another. And again, make up as a more convenient way of disguising skin features than something to cover up big weaponised fangs.

                                        In the event that you have to disguise between different looks in which the tattoos are quite visible... it's plausible enough under most circumstances for a successful role that was penalised to rest in the space of "there's just enough everyday weirdness in Creation that people might only bare take note of mobile tattoos and then move on". Possibly there's a degree to which one goes Orichalcum Rule with it and says some non-magical disguises won't really have much look keeping somebody from at least having a chance to reason you out from a very obvious distinguishing characteristic, especially if they're very familiar with you".

                                                            02-14-2026, 03:31 PM
                                                            Originally posted by Snailslayer View Post
                                                            The Lunar charm Peacock Quill Flourish is for doing calligraphy that makes the reader recall the lunar fondly and stuff. However it doesn't require linguistics... so can a completely illterate lunar make use of it?
                                                            If you're prepared to look your Storyteller in the eye and say "I draw beautiful pictograms," IMO you should indeed be able to use Peacock Quill Flourish that way...but written social actions are always rolled with Linguistics (corebook p. 221), so your dice pool is going to be a bit lacking, and it'd be difficult to convey anything complex or nuanced this way. (One could argue that pictogram influence is basically the written equivalent of making social influence via gestures and body language, and this is gonna be really rough if your ST also gives the target the +2 Resolve for that.)

                                                            But! Peacock Quill Flourish isn't just for written influence, it works on any kind of social influence where the target can't see you (but has seen you before). The Charm explicitly suggests Infallible Messenger as another way to use it. (Interestingly, Infallible Messenger doesn't normally allow the sorcerer to use Charms to enhance influence used through the spell, but since PQF specifically lists it as an example I assume this Charm is an exception.) So it's definitely possible for an illiterate Lunar to use the Charm, even if your ST doesn't buy the pictogram argument.
