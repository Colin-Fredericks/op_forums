    Yeah, without that there's nothing good about Lunar MA. You don't get Mastery, it can't be Favoured, it can't be Supernal, it can't be used with animal forms, and without this you couldn't even combo it with Evasion or Soak-boosting charms (which Solars can).

    Solars have some hefty advantages with MA. Lunars have some different hefty advantages with MA. That seems fair to me, but not the same (same would be boring).

    I'm willing to accept that comboing them may, perhaps, be broken. But if it is, then you'd need to give them some other stuff to make it a viable combat build.

    (I saw 2nd ed Solars take MA a lot, but I only saw it taken once with a Lunar and it wasn't that good. Most Lunar "martial artists" just used regular charms and fists or a staff.)

    But I believe that the devs weren't sure whether to do this or not, and found in playtesting that it was actually okay.

                                                    I just like the ease, lack of hassle, and the concept that Solars are more straight-up powerful with MA while Lunars are more flexible (which is its own strength). So that a Solar Martial Artist and Lunar Martial Artist are both viable but different.
