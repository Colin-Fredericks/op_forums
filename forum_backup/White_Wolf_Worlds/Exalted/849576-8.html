                                                            Originally posted by Anasurimbor View Post
                                                            That's easy to fix. Just give axes a bonus against spears.

                                                            Contrary to "common knowledge", it is actually rather difficult to chop straight through a spear-shaft. They are around 1 1/2 inches thick, of hardwood, and sometimes reinforced with metal. Granted, smacking a spearshaft with a weapon will knock the head out of the way, but it probably won't chop through it.

                                                            I just give axes and other "hooked" weapons a bonus die whenever my characters stunt a disarm or trip Gambit. (clarification: they just get the standard +1 die for stunting) In "real life", it is almost trivially easy to disarm or trip an enemy when you use the heel of a war-axe to hook around a weapon, a shield, or an arm or a leg. The players have to describe using the heel of the axe to do so, however. If they describe the Gambit as "I knock away the weapon with my own", they don't get the bonus.

                                                            I am interested in giving spear-users some Defense bonus against non-spear users (it is really easy to keep someone at bay using a spear), but I would balance it by also giving spears and other Reaching weapons an Accuracy penalty vs adjacent opponents, to represent the difficulty of using a 7-foot-long weapon with 3 feet of the shaft swinging around behind your head, but the arbitrary ranges of 3E make it difficult. In D&D it was easy, as distances in combat were concrete and penalties were easy to add.

                                                            Again, in real life, spears weren't used in close quarters. If an enemy got within the reach of the spearhead, you would usually drop the spear and draw a backup weapon. Of course, you could always choke up on the spear, but doing so made fighting with it difficult.

                                                            Take the above with a grain of salt, as I run mortal campaigns were even a +1/-1 bonus to rolls are important.
