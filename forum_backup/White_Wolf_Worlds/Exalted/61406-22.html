                Has anyone mentioned Animorphs yet? (I haven't read the whole thread.)

                This is a young-adult novella series about young people with shapeshifting "technology" (magic powers) trying to defend Earth from body-snatching aliens.

                It's not something that "screams Exalted," rather I think it's useful as inspiration for Lunars. It has a lot of good examples of what you can do with shapeshifting other than turn into a tyrant lizard and crush people in your jaws. It also depicts of asymmetrical warfare: the aliens are more like Sidereals than the Realm since they don't openly invade with armies and bombs and stuff, but they very much outclass and hugely outnumber the Animorphs, who resort to guerrilla warefare, sabotage, assassination, and terrorism. They're also forced to hide their identities from basically all of humanity, since the body-snatcher aliens could be and often are everywhere. (I know almost zilch about actual asymmetrical warfare, so I can't comment on its plausibility.) For an ST or player who doesn't quite know what to do with Lunar shapeshifting other than direct combat and flying travel, I think it would make a great idea quarry.

                There's also plenty of wordcount devoted to what it's like having your human instincts and senses replaced by an animal's, though I don't know enough about Lunars to know how relevant that is. Just don't pay any attention to the author's concepts of animal anatomy.
