            The roll is Dexterity + Ride +/- Speed. I don't have the book in front of me, will give you the page number as soon as possible.

            EDIT: Page 202:
            Movement: Mounts are generally faster than human warriors, particularly when moving at a charge. As a result, most mounts grant a movement bonus to rush, disengage, and withdraw actions. These bonuses are listed in the stat block of any creature suitable as a mount as a mount’s Speed Bonus, and the most common Speed Bonuses are summarized below. The Ride Ability replaces Athletics and Dodge for any movement rolls made while mounted.
            The Speed Bonus is a bonus, whereas the Ride ability replaces Athletics or Dodge.
            There is no mention of removing Dexterity from the roll.

                            Originally posted by Sakii View Post
                            If i want to craft an artifact, the waiting time is the time before you can start rolling? Like, if i have all the gold points needed, can i roll all the rolls at the same time?

                            The first roll of the project takes from 6 weeks (artifact 2) to two years (artifact 5), depending on the rating. After that, there's no set interval between rolls, but it seems that the more time you spend, the more of a stunt bonus you're going to get from your ST. If you don't care about that, and your ST agrees, there's nothing in the rules stopping you from blowing all your rolls toward the terminus all at once. Ultimately, it's up to the ST to determine what a reasonable interval is going to be, though.

                            EDIT: This is on p. 241, in the Superior Project rules.

                                                            Originally posted by The Unsung Hero View Post
                                                            Old Realm... who/what only speaks it?
                                                            At least in previous editions, gods, Fair Folk, elementals, and the Yozis and their souls all speak it instinctively, but none of those were barred from learning or speaking other languages.

                                                            Originally posted by The Unsung Hero View Post
                                                            Who/what CAN speak it?
                                                            Having a mouth helps, but judging by some of the forms the above can take, it's not strictly necessary.

                                                            Originally posted by The Unsung Hero View Post
                                                            If I'm a sorcerer and I don't know Old Realm, am I screwed in dealing with creatures I summon or encounter?
                                                            Considering that demons and gods alike live until they're killed, they have lots of time and opportunities to pick up other tongues: you've got good odds of finding one that has at least a basic ability to communicate with you.

                                                            As for elementals, they're created on the spot when you summon one, but it's not like they don't know how to do their jobs; a huraka will know how to push clouds around, a tidemare won't spend two weeks learning how to accept people into its bubble.

                                                            Being more than just energy, Essence is also knowledge, and when you gather it from the world until it coalesces into a being, that being will have knowledge as well.

                                                            Which is my way of saying, I'd probably give a summoned elemental a language appropriate to the region in addition to Old Realm.
