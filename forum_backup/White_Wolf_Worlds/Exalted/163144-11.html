    Dreams in Answers Contemplation
    Sidereal Investigation

    The Sidereal prays to the heavens for guidance, and finds that the cosmos will answer him with visions of the past or premonitions of the future. The next time the Sidereal sleeps, he will have a vivid dream that either suggests an answer to a problem currently plagues him, or directly shows him events that happened in the past as though he were present. The Sidereal can use this charm on another character, with or without their knowledge. That character will have visions as described above, which the Sidereal will not be privy to.

        Journey of a Thousand Miles Step
        Ceceline Charm

        With a touch, the infernal releases a fraction of ceceline onto a being, cursing them to feel a lifetime of travel with every step. While this charm doesn't affect it's victims physically, psychologically it wreaks havoc as the target feels the effects of traveling a barren wasteland in complete isolation.

        Every time she moves a yard, the target must roll Willpower contested by the Infernal's own. Every time the target fails, she forgets what she was doing as though a lifetime had passed and loses intimacies, background and ability dots and might change her Motivation (e.g. to "Just survive") as though they simply faded through disuse or avoidance, or gains derangements that could be caused by prolonged isolation and depravation - in reality, the target simply forgets that she once had friends, or was capable of shooting a bow, as the ravages of time make the journey the only persistent memory.

        This charm lasts until the target has taken a number of steps equal to the infernal's essence, or until the infernal chooses to release the committed motes.

        The decision to remain completely motionless staves off this charm's effects, but unless the target dies cowering in the corner, they will have to move some time.

            Penitent Forgiveness Rain.)
            Celestial Circle Sorcery

            Making the sign of Mercury with his left hand and the sign of Venus with his right, the Sorcerer casts a cry for mercy to Heaven so sharp that it tears open the sky, scattering clouds toward all four outer poles, and makes it weep a cleansing, shimmering rain all the same.

            There is no lightning to illuminate the sky, although a distant sussurus of thunder is a near constant.

            For five hours, those caught in the rain treat each scene they partake in as eroding a single negative intimacy. If the intimacy is wholly eroded, they regain willpower as though from a restful night's sleep, and further scenes erode a new intimacy. They may remember the intimacy with effort, but it seems vague and dreamlike, and often silly; any scene spent rebuilding it requires a point of willpower until it is fully re-established.

            Those who avoid the rain entirely merely treat the entire storm as a scene of erosion, while those who remain wholly exposed more than a scene treat every subsequent minute as a new scene of erosion.

                Unthorned Rose Technique
                Liminal Appearance

                The Chernozem breathes a sweet breath upon his body. So long as this Charm is active, the Liminal's body becomes both more attractive and less threatening, extending appropriate bonuses and penalties when perceived. This Charm's effects also aid attempts to conceal scars, sutures, and other signs of his true nature.

                If the Liminal uses this Charm to treat a body part as he attaches it, it permanently bestows these effects upon it without demanding a commitment. These effects apply only if others perceive only body parts the Liminal has treated in this way. For instance, a single treated arm extending a come-hither gesture from an otherwise shrouded Liminal grants these effects, but they will not apply if his untreated other arm is visible.

                        Black Earth Salted Thrice
                        Abyssal War

                        Where the Armies of Oblivion march all things die. An Abyssal with this Charm is not interested in conquering kingdoms, all he cares for is the blissful nothing that can only be found on the other side of the Final War. Whenever the Death Knight partakes in a battle during which both sides lose enough men to count as devastated he may activate this Charm. Blood seems to seep into the earth faster as plants wither and animals flee. Nothing will grow upon this blighted land and now animals will come and forage from it for 1 year and 1 day. Any dead cannot be taken and refuse to burn during this time as well, the bodies seemingly waiting to be called to war once more.

                            Obscured by Starlight
                            Greater Astrology/Sidereal Charm

                            Grabbing at the strands of arcane fate, the Sidereal weaves this destiny around the something in the area. When those present for the scene next role to remember the Sidereal, their roll also applies to their ability to recall the relevance of the things or beings enveloped by the astrological effect to the scene, while obscuring memory of affected objects introduced during the scene. This charm cannot be used by a sidereal with an active resplendent destiny, as to share the Sidereal fate to be forgotten requires that that fate be expressed. If anything would cause the subjects thus concealed to gain paradox, it is instead taken on by the being who has symbolically made the subject a part of their own fate.

                                Auspicious Progeneration Gift
                                Lunar Stamina

                                Giving some of their blood to a creature, the Lunar is able to impart some of its fortean nature.

                                In conjunction with this charm and a sacrifice of 1Lhl, the lunar may activate any charms or knacks that would affect her physical form (such as healing or regeneration charms, mutation charms, knacks such as Twin-faced Hero or Changing Plumage Mastery, etc.) at their normal cost [+surcharge maybe], imbuing the sacrificed blood with their effects. The receiving creature then benefits from the effects of those charms as though she had just activated them, for their normal duration (maximum 1 day).

                                Releasing commitment to this charm ends all gifted effects over the course of 7 ticks.
                                The blood cannot be stored, it must be consumed within a few minutes of this charm's activation, otherwise its effects and cost are wasted.

                                    Abyssal Presence

                                    Subtly stripping her target of every shred of fear, the Abyssal renders her foes gentle lambs for slaughter. While under the effects of this charm, the target can react to clear and present threats to their well-being with perfect clarity of mind, but the Abyssal reduces the target's MDV by her Essence for any attempts the Deathknight makes to gloss over or conceal eerie surroundings, strange noises, an excess of martial or mordbid decor, or other such trappings. She also adds an external penalty of the same amount for any other rolls the target makes to notice the same. For the duration of this charm, the target's Valor is treated as the higher of either its natural value or the Abyssal's Performance solely for the purpose of resisting or overcoming fear.

                                        Three Crowns for Four Kings
                                        Sidereal Larceny

                                        Even the loftiest rewards can fall within the reach of ones united toward a common cause. Alas, it sometimes serves destiny's ends to purloin that unity, and turn its prior owners against one another. When engaging in social influence with a group that shares a common goal, this Charm may supplement a Sidereal's attempt to instill a belief that the rewards of achieving the goal will not be enough to go around. The Charm does not function if it is unclear how this could be so for the goal in question (for instance, if the goal is only to see that a village has food enough to eat and none of the group care for fame, the Sidereal may have his work cut out for him).

                                        If successful, group members who hear his words automatically develop a Minor tie of mistrust toward other group members and a Minor tie of selfish desire for the fruits of the venture's labor. If similar Intimacies already exist, their respective grades increase by one and change their contexts to the ones described. These Intimacies may not be degraded by mundane means without compelling evidence that the reward is enough to go around, such as proof that this is so or by a sufficient reduction in the number who stand to be rewarded (by whatever means this takes).

                                            Hungry Eyes and Empty Hearts
                                            Raksha feeding charm, requiring Banquet of Crumbs and Gaping Virtue Mouth.

                                            A raksha's hunger knows no bounds, not even ownership or shape can stave off it's own gluttony. Using this charm during feeding, a Raksha can imbue an Enchanted creation-born with the Raksha's own hunger. The target gains the ability to feed on virtues as a Raksha would (using a virtue of the Raksha's choosing as though she posessed a Feeding Grace), and feels an endless hunger that compells them to do so. However, such feeding can only be used to create gossamer, and yields no other possible (mechanical) rewards.

                                            Furthermore, if and when the target posesses Gossamer, she is compelled to hoard it in a hiding place of the Raksha's choosing.

                                            Lastly, the Raksha has a general idea of the status of each such creation-born.

                                            This charm can be warded off or dispelled by prolonged contact with Iron (i.e. at least a couple of actions so that attacks don't automatically negate it, but grapples could).

                                                Recursive Shaping Methodology
                                                Pyrian Charm, requires Sorcerous Enlightement of She Who Lives In Her Name.
                                                As she shapes the world, the Infernal does not tie off her sorcery but instead forces the world to remain malleable to her will, turning the end of one spell into the beginning of another.
                                                When the Infernal has cast a spell of any circle of Sorcery, she may immediately begin a new Shape Sorcery action for a new spell of the same or of a lesser circle. There is no limit on the number of spells she may theoretically cast outside of the limits of her Willpower and Essence pools, but she cannot invoke this charm to continue after the invocation of a spell or ability that reduces casting time, such as any Countermagic spell.
