                            Mostly only 3 bits...

                            1: Hearthstones can sometimes form naturally in places of the world, however they are often called Gaia Stones instead as they all seem to form in places of Significant loss for Gaia. The stones all appear to grant a great power at the cost of a Vastly increased sense of emotion when they are used (Grief, Anger, Greed, Love and Apathy).

                            2: The Celestial City Yu-Shan is a Primordial, As the Primordials finished Creation they needed somewhere to Rule from, annoyed with the weakest of their kind constantly disagreeing with what they were doing they Turned on him and crafted him into a great City for them to rule from. His Souls were wrought into new Buildings, Crafted into great Gardens and warped into Silent Monoliths. When the Gods eventually staged their Coup he was expected to deny all access to himself from anyone that wasn't a Primordial.. With Great effort he Made a single Proclamation "Enough" and let his doors be open to all that could find them and open them.
                            Sometimes now in the Silence of Calibration... in the Deepest places of the City one can hear/feel the reverberations of Yu-Shan The Silent, Laughter at the Irony of his Brothers Fate... Malfeas.
                            Expanded upon Here.

                            3: The Yozi's can Warp any of the Magical Metals into Twisted versions of themselves, Jade Becomes Fel Jade, Moonsilver becomes Voidsilver, Orichalium becomes Golden Obsidian Etc.... These do not form Naturally anywhere they must be made/twisted into it.

                            Edit: Changed Black Jade to Fel Jade, After being informed Black Jade Exists already (Brain Fart). Plus i like the Idea that Fel Jade is a Iridescent Black that if you look at it from different Angles you can see Black-Red, Black-Blue Etc.
