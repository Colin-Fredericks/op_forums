                    Our likely-last 2nd Edition campaign, an Exalted Modern game, just ended. Here's the out of context quotes, presented in chronological order from the beginning of the game to the final session:

                    Ray: I assume I can't use the points from the missing limb: hand thing to buy more arms?

                    Mountain: I think "Sun's Out, Guns Out" is our circle name.

                    Sabat: Can I use my chain daiklave - if I'm really fast - as a selfie stick?

                    Sleeping Mountain: I don't actually have any dots of Familiar, but I do have four live animals in my pants.

                    Ray: If I take my shoes off, I can count to 30!

                    Mountain: Tiny, but well-trained martial arts foot, divine testicles.

                    Mountain: I reserve the right to, if imprisoned somewhere for ten years, have a giant shaggy green mane.
                    Abby: I reserve the right to imprison you for ten years.

                    Abby: We are the Scooby Gang with our pet lion.

                    ST: That's Resplendent Jim, he works here on Tuesdays.

                    Mountain: It's kinda like a Raksha strip club, only there's more kung-fu involved.

                    Sabat: Because when I think subtlety, I think Godzilla.

                    ST: This is the most flannel a party of Sidereals has ever worn.

                    Mountain: Perfect parry with a chainsaw.

                    Abby: He was fated to die.
                    Mountain: Was he fated to die without giving us any useful information??
                    Abby:...apparently, yes.

                    Abby: He wasn't naked when I stabbed him!

                    Mountain: Actually, I think we're a pretty good argument FOR the Gold Faction, because honestly, how much worse could the Solars make it?

                    ST: Here, plug this into the bear.

                    ST: Now your nipples are whispering in torment!

                    ST: The sky is, like, on fire or something, we don't know.

                    Mountain: If you give me a ladder big enough...I will flood the East!

                    Hacophonane: I can, in fact, punch a hole through the Imperial Mountain and carry the fucker off. I can Carmen Sandiego the Imperial Mountain.

                    Mountain: I failed my Temperance, I'm putting Soulsteel in the microwave to see what happens.

                    Mountain: I put a bunch of batteries in my pocket on the basis that when I shoot them at people, they'll explode into acid and lightning.

                    Mountain: I mean, shipping may be interfered with on account of apocalypse.
                    Abby: No, they say two day shipping and they mean it! Neither rain nor sleet nor giant zombie.

                    Mountain: I am gathering a social group of pilots who are motivated by sexual tension-
                    Ray: And volleyball?

                    ST: That's odd, what did you do in your past life?
                    Abby: Mostly drugs.

                    Mountain: Look upon my chiseled abdomen and wonder at the glories of heaven!

                    ST: Having a dragonblood who can juggle rocket launchers while shooting lightning out of his ass is better than a regular soldier with one rocket launcher.

                    ST: This is somebody else's gravity now!

                    Mountain: So I have seduced her?
                    ST: She has integrity charms.
                    Abby: And standards.

                    ST: Ray is killing people with math.
                    Abby: Math has certainly made me want to die sometimes.

                    Mountain: On the other hand, we might be able to keep it flying if we parry the ceiling hard enough.

                    Ray: I spent years building up an immunity to implosions.

                    Mountain: I send her a bouquet with a chainsaw in it.

                    ST: This does mean that the internet will be wood-themed, which means it's even more for porn.
                    Sabat: Does it mean it'll be full of lumberjack-themed porn? Because that's what we found in the East. I'm not seeing any downsides.

                    Mountain: What's his name?
                    ST: Ugh. He has a name.
                    Mountain: Hasaname, I have a deal for you...

                    ST: The Infernal Kardashians of Creation.
                    Ray: ...So the Kardashians?

                    Ray: We have, in fact, delivered Kim Kardashian for summary execution.

                    ST: You have killed Kim Kardashian and replaced her with your own.

                    Mountain: *about zombies when Abby wasn't here* Crap. I have no idea if they're supposed to be dead or not!

                    Mountain: Millions dead! Millions dead because you wouldn't take a booze bath.

                    Mountain: Yeah, I think if I had to pick the Sun or Moon, I'd fight Luna.
                    Sabat: But the Sun can't lose!
                    Mountain: But he has mercy.

                    ST: Give me a Stamina + Larceny roll.

                    Mountain: Grow a pair. And I don't mean arms.

                    Ray: If it's alzheimer's Tammuz, he may very well wage a war on pancakes.

                    Sabat: That's why the Usurpation was okay.
                    Hacophonane: There is no proof that any of that ever happened.
                    Sabat: That's why we destroyed the record: to prevent anyone from ever remembering the giant dick fountain.

                    ST: White Lily appears to be a...*huge sneeze*
                    Mountain: Oh, that poor child!

                    ST: Arson and love are very different things!

                    Sabat: Shut up, I'm totally dead, you don't even know, I ate some scones once.

                    Mountain: Well, I don't have a child or any seashells, but what I do have...is chainsaws in my pants!

                    Mountain: I want to find my corpse and see what's in my pants.

                    Mountain and Ray in combination: Craft: Fate is 30% Bureaucracy, 20% other people's signatures, and 50% fear of the Pattern Spiders.

                    Mountain: Wait, you're going to let Abby look at thousands and thousands of people's lives at once? With scissors??

                    Mountain: Iron Siaka is really good at keeping secrets because you ask her a question and then she punches you.

                    Mountain: Who is that stranger who rode into town on the rhino? What stranger, I just remember the rhino!

                    Abby: Please silence your cellphone before invading the enemy fortress.

                    Ray: What we need to do is forge the remains of the Unconquered Sun's corpse into a giant pool-skimmer.

                    Ray: I'm pretty sure the Deathlords [dead Autocthon] creates are Daft Punk.

                    Mountain: Sorry, for some reason I assumed you were talking to Abby's feet. "Surely the feet will give him the book!"
                    (Of note: right after this is when we discovered that Mountain didn't know Abby' real name this whole time.)

                    Ray: Using his way of counting, we have three times more Chejop Kejak than anyone else.

                    Abby: Can one stealthily jump over the front gate?

                    ST: He's Captain Kirk; the hot alien scientist is always who he ends up banging!
                    Sabat: Yeah, sure, it'll be fine, just get in this box.

                    Hacophonane: Killing that many people would be bad for Creation for any number of reasons.
                    ST *as NPC*: Also morally bad?
                    Hacophonane: ...That's what I mean.

                    Mountain: I think ding dong ditch with the Juggernaut is the way to go.

                    Mountain: Supposition: This whole Chejop Kejak thing is a lie and you're just the Green Lady.
                    Sabat: Supposition: Chejop Kejak was the Green Lady all along.
                    Ray: And they're all Mirror Flag.

                    Mountain: ...I mean, I really don't wanna seduce Han-Tha, either.

                    Ray: I'm a very human truck.
                    Mountain: Stop making this weird. I'm inside you, I don't want this to be weird.

                    Mountain: I am going to flare my anima and say "I am Sleeping Mountain, Chosen of Serenity, and I declare that it is time for your rest to come to an end!"
                    ST (as Pasiap): Rrrrrrgh
                    Sabat: Holy shit, that worked?
                    Mountain: Of course it did, I shone a bright light in his eyes.

                    Ray: What's a good siege-scale weapon?
                    Hacophonane: *raises hand*

                    Sabat: Someone needs to Shun the Smiling Trump.

                    Abby: Is your laurel going on your rearview mirror?

                    Sabat: *as Mountain* "Hi, I'm Chejop Kejak and I unleashed the Juggernaut on a civilian populace!"

                    Mountain: If Mars is in the Jacuzzi, it's not the right time to start a war.

                    Sabat: I made you some Solars, but you eated them.

                    Mountain: If no one else has any pressing business, I think I'm gonna sleep with Ma-Ha-Suchi and steal his casino.

                    Sabat: I have a suggestion that none of you will like: the Bureau of Undeathstiny.

                    Ray: What if they're strapping jet engines to birds?
                    Mountain: I thought you didn't have the Great Curse!

                    Mountain: It used to be that I could trust his judgment, but then he started listening to me.

                    Hacophonane: I'm only Essence 5.
                    Ray: I'm a truck.

                    Ray: The car says "Hey asshole, you like what's under the hood?" as the hood pops up and instead of something incredibly inappropriate, a long metal tube pops out.

                    Mountain: Toilet paper is a form weapon, bitches!

                    ST: You knee him in the crotch and his head explodes.
