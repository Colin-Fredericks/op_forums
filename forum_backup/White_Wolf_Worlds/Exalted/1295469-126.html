                            Originally posted by Elkovash View Post
                            for a charm like 'Invincible Beast-Warrior Empowerment' that grant automatic successes for damage dice - how should this interact with Soak?

                            For my last game, I ruled that (in the case of this charm) the first 'x' dice of post-soak damage are converted to successes.

                            eg; a battle group affected by this charm has a raw damage of 28, 6 of which are granted by size/might. Their target has a soak of 23. their post-soak damage is 5 and those 5 are converted to automatic successes of lethal damage. Another target has a soak of only 18, post soak damage is then 10, meaning they roll 4 dice and gain 6 automatic damage successes.

                            How would you have intended this situation to play out?
                            The intent of Invincible Beast-Warrior Empowerment is that it takes things that would normally add dice, and causes them to add successes instead, so it'd bypass soak altogether in the case of damage.

                                                        Not sure if this has been addressed in this or the previous Ask a Dev thread... and trying to search through hundreds of pages will be a needle in a haystack endeavor (and my attempts at using Search have been... abysmal at best) ... so here goes.

                                                        In the Lunar Backers Access Manuscript for one of the Full Moon anima powers, it allows the Lunar, after having their Initiative reset due to landing a decisive strike, they can pay a substantial cost to roll a Join Battle roll to add to their reset Initiative. The problem I am having is that the wording doesn't make this clear if it is a standard Join Battle roll with a bonus... or a special Join Battle with a specific pool as described. The exact wording is as follows:

                                                        roll Join Battle with (highest physical Attribute) non-Charm dice.
                                                        Now to be clear... I see this playing out one of two ways. the Full Moon Lunar rolls their normal Join Battle pool with (highest physical Attribute) non-Charm dice as a bonus... or the Lunar only rolls the (highest physical Attribute) non-Charm dice and applies that result as if it were the Join Battle pool (rather than their normal Join Battle pool). I do wish to know which the Devs/Writers actually intend to be the correct interpretation of how this power is supposed to function.

                                                        Given the cost involved, I am inclined to side with the normal role plus bonus dice, but if I have learned anything with Exalted, it is that quite often, the way something is written is deliberate, and unless something is specifically stated in the description as being a bonus, then it is more often intended to be a specific pool on its own. Hopefully, if this hasn't been brought up before, it can be made more clear before the final draft of LFATG is sent for printing (so as to avoid future confusion on this issue... minor as it may be).
