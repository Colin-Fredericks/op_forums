                                                    Originally posted by rj.au View Post
                                                    A question about the combat positioning system: suppose Maria is in short range of Wei and in medium range of Xiuying.                                                     I'm not totally sure what you're asking. Generally speaking, it depends on how they're arranged in space.                                                         Originally posted by Korax View Post
                                                        I feel stupid admitting this but the mechanics of Breath Seizung Techniqe elude me.

                                                        I know it is a lot to ask but would it be possible to walk through an example?
                                                        Mnemon Alisa uses Breath-Seizing Technique to make a decisive attack against Ragara Bhab (she's trying to renegotiate the terms of her loan; these things happen).

                                                        Alisa makes her decisive attack roll against Bhab and beats his Defense by three successes. Before she makes her decisive damage roll, she makes a special roll of (her successes over Bhab's Defense - Bhab's soak). No matter how high Bhab's soak is, it can't reduce this roll below the Overwhelming rating of her weapon - if she's doing this barehanded, that'd be 1.

                                                        Every success on this special roll reduces Bhab's Initiative by one, but doesn't give Alisa any Initiative. If this crashes Bhab, Alisa gets the normal +5 Initiative bonus for crashing an enemy, which is added to her Initiative before she rolls it for decisive damage.

                                                        Having completed the special roll for Breath-Seizing Technique, Alisa rolls decisive damage normally.
