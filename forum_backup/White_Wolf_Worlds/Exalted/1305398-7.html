                Ledaal Kansi, Flame of Clarity, thought often of her old friend Tunka. He was an outcaste, and had always had a serene bearing that she just couldn't understand. His mastery of the Earth Dragon Style was unparalleled, but he never boasted. Even his lessons were quiet. Musings in tones that one could not discern from glib conversation. Once, when they were walking the lower slopes of Meru, Kansi had seen a great stone at the edge of a cliff above them. It hung dangerously at the edge and she asked, "Fellow Master, what do you think of that Boulder there? Shall we find another route or be ready to strike it from the air?"

                Tunka had frowned, and cocked his head, "Does the Boulder threaten you, by where it sits?". Kansi nodded but had no further answer, and they were silent for a time.

                "I think the Boulder has made a promise." Tunka finally mused, "By its stillness, we can detect no Malice. A day it may have rested there, or a thousand years. Never did it bound up the road, into the temple and splat you as you slept."

                Kansi laughed, "Splat, eh? And you have heard its promise? By your communion with Pasiap?". Tunka shook his head, "The promise of a stone is all it is. Even should it hang so perilously, a stone it remains. You'd know this if you had learned Pasiap's lessons."

                Kansi hadn't, and she nodded in respect. She thought often of old Tunka, and visited where he had gone missing.

                Round 1:
                Kansi takes Join Battle easily. It's expensive, but worth it as she activates Fire Dragon Form with Consuming Might of the Fire Dragon. As we'll see later, she just won the fight.
                Despite this, Boulder takes the first action, coming out with a Thunderclap Rush Attack with Force of the Mountain. With a Smashing attack she knocks Kansi down and actually takes the lead, even surpassing the Initiative effect of CMoFD. As we'll see later, she Almost just won the fight. She also takes a damage from CMoFD.
                In one attack, Boulder has gone Bonfire. These charms ain't cheap.
                In Return, Kansi has to flurry rising from Prone with her own counterattack. Boulder boosts her Parry all the way to 11 but takes -1 for attempting a Smashing attack. Kansi rolls 10 successes on the dot. Boulder Activates Earth Dragon Form along with Perfection of Earth Body. She is now made entirely of Soak. Fortunately, Kansi's form gives her Overwhelming damage, and she rolls 2, which with Searing Edge Attack. That would be bad enough, but Perfection of Earth Body cost Boulder a Whopping 6i to activate. So she Crashes. Goodbye Hardness. We Hardly knew ye.
                Kansi gains Fire Aura, which she will keep for the rest of the fight.
                Round 2:
                Kansi has the advantage now, and tries for an Essence Igniting Nerve Strike. Unfortunaterly 3 Overwhelming is just not very much, So even though she hits with 1 damage, no essence is lost. She did use Searing Edge attack though, so Boulder is now Extremely Crashed. Kansi now enters Bonfire.
                Boulder is now Crashed, so she doesn't have her Perilous charms, and she's not doing great on Essence either. She Has To Hit Kansi so she just boosts her accuracy as high as she can, and gets a solid hit, 7 damage, but its not enough to escape Crash. She also takes 1 damage from CMoFD.
                Round 3:
                Miraculously, Boulder is not hurt by Kansi's Flux.
                Moving first, Kansi hits with a Smoldering Wound attack. 7 Damage and she resets to 4 Initiative.
                Boulder now has 1 HL left before Incapacitated. She's immune to the wound penalties, but every time she hits Kansi she takes a damage die from CMoFD, and if she crashes again she'll get hit by Flux.
                In response, Boulder does manage to fight her way out of Crash, but doesn't get Initiative Shift. She also doesn't take Damage from CMoFD.
                Round 4, 5, etc, : Ok at this point all they have is 5 motes at a time and they can either spend them all on an attack or defense excellency or split them up with something fancier. Kansi uses Flickering Candle Evasion to get a lot more out of her Defense than the excellency could get her, and her Excellency has the reroll 6s effect which gives her two significant edges.
                Boulder's Defensive charms aren't really helpful here, maybe I could've optimized better but honestly she's so mote tapped.
                She soaks any attack down to nothing, but the Overwhelming combined with Searing Edge attack is brutal, and when she counter-attacks she's just giving Kansi another chance to hit her.
                I called the fight there. Theoretically Boulder could save up her Essence over time and make an attempt at getting some Initiative back, but Kansi's dice tricks far outclass her in terms of mote efficiency.

                The girl looked up at Ledaal Kansi, Flame of Clarity. Her golden aura betrayed her sin, the hatred and tyranny engulfing her soul. Kansi's essence beat the air wildly, heating Boulder's skin like stones around a cooking fire.

                That was it. Now, after all these years, Kansi hesitated. The girl fought like Tunka, pebbles and dust rising around her fingers. She had joked like him before challenging an Immaculate Master to a friendly spar, like it was a cup of tea. She had shown her curse-mark brazenly, identified it clearly and wasted no words in argument. She had been willing to die, just to fight Ledaal Kansi, the Flame of Clarity. And why?

                She called herself Hanging Boulder Promise, and her hair fell scruffily around her eyes. Those eyes looked up at Kansi now, still not bowed. Not even squinting.

                They were Tunka's eyes. Without a doubt, and she knew Pasiap's lessons.

                Ledaal Kansi, Flame of Clarity, spared the life of an Anathema, the first in all her years. For the promise of a stone to remain a stone, and to know whatever became of old Tunka.
