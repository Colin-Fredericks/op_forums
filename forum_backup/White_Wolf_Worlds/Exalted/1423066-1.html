                    If I were doing this:

                    I wouldn't get rid of Excellencies completely but change them to 2m to add a single auto success/point of static value. And I'd lose the Parry bonus on Medium weapons. (Except maybe medium shields -- go Clash folks with shields. I'd also lose the Accuracy bonus on shields.)

                    That way there're still some degree of flexibility so you don't get completely chumped (especially important to Lunars) and the DB Excellencies are still worth taking.

                    Do note, this makes foes like Ahlat and Octavian pretty terrifying. If we're preserving the sense of Exalted power, I'd let folks buy native Charms with Splat XP.

                    Artifact difficulties need a long, hard look. Sail (whilst still dull) might actually be viable though.

                    I don't know, just spitballing here... What if Excellencies are usually capped at 1 success for 2m, but under extreme duress (like fighting a Directional War God) the cap goes back to RAW. A Quickening, if you will, that allows Exalted to break through their normal limitations in times of extreme need (i.e. ST fiat).

                        From the perspective of someone that never really played Exalted (I mostly read the books for the flavor and lurk here), Excellency as they are uselessly bloat the dice pools, especially for Solars (Lunars too, but the Lunars have to put at least some amount of effort to get the max benefit from them), with basically no tactical depth aside from the Mote cost.

                        That said, I would not just remove them. They are there to represent the innate ability of the Exalted to transcend mortal limits. Adding some kind of limitation though, to help reduce the dice pools and make non-Exalted (or Dragon-Blooded) more interesting, would be useful.

                        For example, the Solars could have an Excellency that capped at (Ability), unless they're facing an opponent that requires the full might of the Chosen of the Sun (ST discretion, but in general, I'll assume at least something that has a dice pool equal or greater then Attribute+Ability+Excellency), in which case the Excellency rise to (Attribute+Ability), with Free Excellency Charms giving full benefit. But that's just an example.

                        Or go in a similar path of Essence, with Excellenty be a Commit Charm that everybody has, with slightly different effects for everyone, but that grants the same amount of power. Maybe not exactly like Essence, but something on the line.

                            Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
                            Basically excellencies, especially Solar ones, are pretty much good enough to do almost anything anyway, so the charms past it don't feel like they matter as much.
                            But is that a problem with Excellencies or the Charms after them that feel inadequate because they're just more ways of rolling big numbers?

                            A lot of people think Exalted in any edition has too many Charms. Getting rid of anything that's basically redundant with an Excellency seems like a great idea compared to getting rid of Excellencies. That way you get to focus on things that aren't just more dice like being able to jump over mountains, or be able to use your hands as any mundane tool, or being able to grapple things so large you couldn't normally do so, and all that stuff.

                            The 5m option on Lock Opening Touch is a great example of something that's not worth having in the game rather than a case against Excellencies (though the Charm is still worth it since the 1m option is most certainly worth it because it lets you pick magical locks that could easily be immune to mundane rolls no matter how many dice get throw at them).

                            With difficulties capping out at basically 5,..
                            But this aspect swings both ways, and is actually a reason to prefer Excellencies with perhaps a reduction in their maximums, rather than farming out the same effects to a half dozen Charms instead.

                            I don't want to have to buy a +X dice to overcome poisons Charm, and a +X dice to overcome heat exposure Charm, and a +X dice to overcome fatigue Charm, etc. It's boring, it's a massive XP tax, and it makes STing harder because character can't invest in a basic bread and butter Charm to help them past most mundane situations.

                            A non-Resistance or Survival focused character can be tempted into investing in those to help protect them from the dangers of Creation if it's only going to be a small Charm purchase. They're not going to bother if they need to dump a lot of XP in there just to avoid every possible hazard so they don't become a burden on the group.

                            This is all why we've seen what we have out of Essence: not dumping Excellencies, but simplifying them, adjusting the math of the game to match PC abilities better, and making the Charm lists way smaller rather than filling them with edge case Charms.

                            Now, the effect of Lock Opening Touch being only 1m makes it good, but only if you're in a big long adventuring day where you're going to miss those 6 motes you could have spent just destroying the difficulty with your excellency.
                            Again, the fact that it lets you pick magical locks is, by itself, already a massive boost worth the investment in the Charm, and the 1m cost, with the roll just to see if you do it perfectly or get noticed. Which is really the sort of thing non-Excellencies should be able to focus on: cool shit you can't do by just rolling lots of successes because it's beyond mundane skills no matter how mote-charged.

                            It means that you really only see the value of those deep investment charms if you're under a lot of time pressure with a multitude of challenges, which not everyone likes.
                            Your chosen example is a no prerequisite Essence 1 Charm... that's hardly a deep investment.

                            Most of the deep investment Charms, and the one's people tend to enjoy the most, are the ones that let you do things that are beyond what tossing +10 dice with an Excellency could do.

                            You don't just blow through 95% of resistance based challenges because of your free excellency that came bundled with Ox-Body Technique, you actually need to specialize in resisting poison to be that good at resisting it.
                            But why is that any good for the game? Not having the game grind to a halt because you didn't buy every Essence 1 "resist X thing" Charm and now your character is under a status effect that the rest of the group has to deal with... it's not good for anyone. STs are either going to avoid threats because too many PCs don't have all the Charms to deal with them, or players are going to feel gotchaed for upping the wrong resistances.

                            And I know I responded to Dr. M here, but consider it a general response to the topic for any that feel like chiming in.
