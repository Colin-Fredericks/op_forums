                                                    Have you enhanced your defence with charms? Yes? No Unbowed Willow Meditation. It doesn't say "Cannot be used if you have applied charm dice to your defence", so it doesn't care if they add non-charm dice or not. If you use some other effect, whether it adds charm dice or not, it's fine, because it's not a charm. Fortunately the wording is quite clear.

                                                    EDIT: Fun fact, it also doesn't say without using a Dodge Charm, so no raising your Dodge through form charms, but I'd personally say that Smoke and Shadow Cover applies since it's not adding dice itself, but allowing concealment to grant cover which then grants a bonus to your defence.
