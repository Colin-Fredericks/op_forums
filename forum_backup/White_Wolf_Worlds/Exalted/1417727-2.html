                            Originally posted by Sorcerous Overlord View Post
                            This thread is wyld for me as a guy who got into the game at the end of the 2e lifecycle. What I've gleaned so far is that Tamuz is a 1e Lunar who is maybe a fancy dog, or maybe that's another guy, who was bi but now is gay because he hates women because his wife was bad or something but now he's not any of that maybe. He made the Delzhani civilization but now he doesn't like it or maybe he doesn't like all of civilization as a concept in general and wants to end it but he's a pacifist but not really but actually yes.
                            Ok, let me sum up some of the bits i gleaned from the editions and try to organize them:

                            - Tamuz had a fancy dog beastform in 1e and that description that has stayed the same through 2e, though neither edition talked much of it.

                            - There exists another Lunar called Tamas Khan, who is the actual ancestor/patron of the Delzahn people. While not outright eliminating him, 2e relegated him to a position of satelite/one-time proxy of Tamuz, pretty much transfering the connection with the people for the older Lunar. Tamas Khan in 2e was a footnote and you'd be excused for not knowing he ever existed.

                            - Unlike 2e Tamuz, 1e Tamas Khan had a more ambivalent relationship with the directions the Delzahn had taken since the takeover of Chiaroscuro. Beside the Delzahn of the plains, Tamas Khan had a more hands-on association with his dholemen children. It was definitely not his pet project of civilization-building/social engineering.

                            - No real discussion of Tamuz' sexual orientation existed in 1e, that being wholy a product of writers in 2e's creative run, though inspired in what i would peg as a very particular reading of bits on his solar wife from his 1e short write-up.

                            - Tamuz' 1e "gimmick" so to speak, instead of a connection as secret patron to Chiaroscuro and the Delzahn, was being a "compassionate" civilization toppling overlord, dedicated to convert people from many walks in life, barbarian and civilized, to his line of thinking and eventual elimination of all empires, through in as bloodless/merciful a manner as possible. While he does not judge "civilized" people as inherently inferior or degenerate, he does appear to see civilization as something of a corrupting influence that must eventually go. While i compared his "one tribe" with guerilla and terrorist groups, it has also dawned me that other possible paths might also fit under the umbrella of his narrative.

                            I'll also be the first to admit this is my particular (re)reading of his 1e write-up, one that others might or not see eye to eye with. And either way has little to no relation to any direction being taken with Tamuz in 3e afaik, as up to this point i have not yet read Fangs at the Gate.

                            The White Wolf Wiki perhaps? No idea if it also covers Exalted materials or not, though i hope so.
