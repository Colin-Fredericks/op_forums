                            And, again, it's intentional Exalted design.

                            You are playing the beings who cast down the creators of the universe. You are thrust into a broken world that hates you. You are hunted by a world spanning empire and thousand year old martial arts masters.

                            You are supposed to be excellent in all things and unsurpassed in your area of focus (as 1st edition phrased it).

                            A mortal brigand with a rusty blade is not supposed to challenge you.

                            You're supposed to have encounters like DrLoveMonkey described over-leaf. And you're supposed to win.

                            If you want tense, life and death combat against demons then play heroic mortals.
                            If you want to be pushed to the limit by the foes in the Corebook then play Dragon-Blooded.
                            If you want to walk the edges of Creation, testing your limits then that's what Lunars are for.

                            Solars are for playing the returning god kings who change the world in their wake.

                            Complaining that a Solar has Parry 7 is like complaining that The Terminator is too difficult for Sarah Connor to beat and that it should be vulnerable to pepper spray and a tazer.

                            Your bookish Twilight does not have to be a Parry 7 combat monster. Your bookish Twilight can be a Defense 2 non-combatant. But you don't have to be a Dawn Caste to be a capable warrior.

                            The Corebook explicitly tells you that it's ok to take 5s. The sample characters (like Volfer) have 5s. Starting Charms (Essence 1) require 5s.

                            There are three Circles of Demons. Blood Apes are literally the weakest. Blood Apes are the low tier bodyguards summoned ny the very weakest of Exalted sorcerers (because they're just powerful enough to be useful but not oowerful enough to be a threat). Of course your combat focused Exalt can beat them. Forget the Third Circle, go fight Mara with your Immaculate.

                                        Originally posted by Moss Reynholm View Post

                                        So fast... cool complete adventure... how? How? It takes me like a week to do/plan anything in this game...
                                        Well I am a veteran Storyteller at this point. For that adventure though I use cobbled together some ideas I had previously, along with just noodling around the systems chapter a bit. I want combat and social influence to be big parts because they're big parts of the system, environmental damage is always good because it's hard to resist and causes direct damage, same with disease. Also don't be afraid to make up your own stuff like the two headed statue commander. Browse through the corebook and see acid bath and go "sure, there's an acid bath now." Then just keep going adding more and more.

                                        One single challenge will never, ever, actually challenge your PCs, you need to just keep going. Especially out of combat, with no 5m/round drip every full excellency is really felt. I was debating adding another really short scene of a long, wide hallway with a low ceiling and no floor, just a 500 foot drop to spikes or something. Ceiling too low to monkey leap, find some other way across. Alternatively rather than a pit a grid of flamethrower triggering plates that could be destroyed, figured out, or dismantled.

                                        Also remember that absent charms even Solars are basically human. You actually can use DnD style challenges a lot of the time. Like that drop into the pit of webs onto uneven broken stone. You fall 30 feet onto that and you don't get to walk away without a scratch just because you're awesome.

                                        Don't forget about environmental penalties either. Exalted is an epic world with extreme environments and climates. Towering frozen waterfalls and thick weed-choked bogs. Some people will buy charms to ignore those penalties, they didn't buy them to never use them. It's also a good way of leeching motes out of se that don't have them, that helps leeching health levels.
