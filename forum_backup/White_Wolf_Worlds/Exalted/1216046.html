    Wisdom of the Ancient Sages (Homebrew Initiation)

    05-07-2018, 07:47 AM
    Wisdom of the Ancient Sages

    Almost every culture forms rituals of body modification. From marking their skin with ink and piercing their flesh with metal or jewels, to prosthesis and embedded technology. As cultures evolve and thrive, there are always those looking to push new boundaries. In the high first age some of Creation's exalted rulers sought ever more extreme and overtly magical methods of self-expression, until finally a sorcerous cabal inscribed indelible sorcerous tattoos upon the very flame of exaltation within themselves.

    This enabled them to draw on the power of their anima in their spellcasting and weaved sorcerous iconography into their anima banner. But more than that; by branding the shining light that reincarnates to this day with ancient wisdom, the Solars, Lunars and Sidereals ensured that their future host's second breath would simultaneously open their eyes to the world's sorcerous essence. Those Dragonbloods able to survive the feat inscribed their unusual might into their very bloodline, passing it down to each generation and spreading their sorcerous potential across the world in awakening Dynasts and Outcastes alike.

    Though rarer even than exalts with this gift, there have been a handful of mortals with a long forgotten exalted ancestor whose study of the mystic has awakened this ancient wisdom. Lacking the exalted's instinctive ability to wield essence, such sorcerers bleed into their otherwise invisible anima when they draw 5+ sorcerous motes with a single shape sorcery action, raising a banner of pale white light that lacks an iconic display but otherwise acts as a Solar anima banner.

    Shaping Rituals

    The sorcerer may shed their anima to Dim level to fuel a shaping action, rolling (Essence) to gain as many sorcerous motes plus 1 (Glowing), 3 (Burning) or 5 (Bonfire) based on the level she shed. Once per scene unless casting her control spell.

    The sorcerer respires the essence of Creation as easily as her own. After taking a shaping action she may trade up to (Essence) motes regained at the end of her turn for sorcerous motes on a 1:1 basis. Spells are cast immediately if their cost is met in this manner. Mortals without an essence pool cannot learn this shaping ritual.
    Ambition 1 Celestial Working - grant a mortal sorcerer a pool of 3 personal motes. She may reflexively spend up to (Wits) motes on a shaping action, these count as sorcerous motes for the purpose of raising her anima banner but do not actually grant additional sorcerous motes.
    By sacrificing one of the powers granted by their caste or aspect's anima, the sorcerer may roll (Intelligence + Occult + Essence), gaining as many sorcerous motes. The anima power sacrificed is unavailable to the sorcerer for a full day and night, when it returns any unused sorcerous motes are lost. May be stacked by sacrificing her second and third anima powers. A mortal sorcerer may instead surrender the benefits of her Control spell for a full day and night.

    Merits

    Lance of the Sorcerer-King (Merit ●●●●): The sorcerer's anima becomes a light artifact weapon, rolling (Perception + Occult) and unleashing shining rays of energy from her hand, eyes or caste mark (if applicable). The anima has the Archery (Long), Bashing, Mounted, One-Handed and Special tags. The anima is treated as made of the magical material matching the sorcerer's anima (Solar/Orichalcum, Air/Blue Jade, etc), cannot be disarmed and is automatically concealed whilst at dim level.

    An exalt may awaken evocations in her anima, enhancing and merging her anima powers until she becomes an avatar of her caste or aspect. An example tree of Twilight evocations are below.

    Mortals may attune to the Lance by committing 2WP. It is treated as made of steel and may not have Evocations, but she may pay 1WP (or 3m if she has an essence pool) to give it the Lethal tag for (Intelligence) rounds.

    Sensing Reflections of Self (Merit ●): As a miscellaneous action an exalted sorcerer with this merit may sense the direction and approximate distance of the nearest exalt that shares her anima (Lunars sense the nearest Lunar, etc), whereas a mortal instead senses the nearest sorcerer. The target may make a reflexive (Wits + Stealth) check to conceal themselves, in which case the sorcerer identifies the next nearest. By spending 1WP the sorcerer may cause the target to feel their presence, expressed as a warm flicker in their own anima which shares the sorcerer's direction, distance and whether they are a fellow exalt or a fellow sorcerer. This invitation adds one automatic success to any attempt for the sorcerer and target to track one another for a full day and night. Whether Solars and Abyssals can sense one another with this merit is currently unknown.

    Weaving of Wonders (Merit ●●): The sorcerer's anima always offers an additional Means in her sorcerous workings. Furthermore, whilst all sorcerers may gain terminus intervals by increasing their Duration, the sorcerer's ancient insight allows her to sacrifice intervals to accelerate her workings. She may reduce the terminus of her working by one to reduce the duration of each interval from one week to one day. Two reduces the duration to 12 hours, three reduces the duration to 1 hour, four reduces the duration to 30 minutes and five reduces the duration to 1 minute.


    This was mostly just for me to get an idea down out of my head, but I'd be more than happy to accept any feedback anybody had.

      Sorcerous Shield of Twilight - SSoT throughout, a possible evocation tree for Lance of the Sorcerer-King when taken by a Solar Twilight caste.
      Attunement: 5
      Hearthstone Slots: 0
      Era: Return of the Solar Exalted
      Benefit: Upon attuning to Sorcerous Shield of Twilight, the Solar learns Arcane Fire at no cost.

      Evocations
      Arcane Fire (3m+; Essence 1; Supplemental; Withering, Instant; Pre-Reqs: none):
      The wise know when flight is preferable to prolonging a fight. When making a withering attack with SSoT, the Twilight may pay three motes to make the light of his attack dazzlingly bright. A target damaged by the attack is blinded for 1 round per 3 motes spent. The Twilight does not gain the target's lost initiative but still gains 1i for landing a successful attack.

      Beloved Majordomo (3m, Essence 1, Reflexive; Perilous; Instant; Pre-Reqs: none):
      The Twilight values her spirit familiars as more than mere tools or puppets, they are allies, confidants and perhaps even friends. If one of her spirit familiars would be destroyed, she may reflexively recall it into her anima before this happens. Whilst dispersed within her anima it heals one level of damage of any type per day, but may not be summoned again until it is fully healed.

      Protecting Heaven's Lamp (5m; Essence 1, Supplemental; - ; One Scene; Pre-Reqs: none):
      Not combative by nature, the Twilight seeks greater protection from what armor she already has. She activates this charm when invoking her aura of force or upon entering bonfire anima. For the remainder of the scene the cost to trigger her anima's aura of force is now (5 - Essence) motes. In addition to Hardness it also provides 5 bonus Soak whilst this evocation is active; which stacks with natural and magical soak, but not armor.

      Eternal Warlord Stance ( - ; Essence 2; Permanent; - ; Permanent; Pre-Reqs: Beloved Majordomo):
      The Twilight caste are the masters of lesser spirits, the complexities of their essence patterns made simple in their eyes. The Twilight may use his anima to bind a Battle Group comprising all the same type of spirit, in place of single demons or elementals. Only one battle group may be bound at a time, and it takes up a number of 'slots' equal to it's size.

      Impenetrable Wall of Adamant ( - ; Essence 2; Permanent; - ; Permanent; Pre-Reqs: Protecting Heaven's Lamp):
      As the medic crosses the battlefield to tend to the wounded, the Twilight's walks fearless amongst her foes. Whilst it's pre-requisite is active, the Hardness and Soak of the Twilight's anima increase by (Essence).

      Solar Lightning Prana (5i; Essence 2; Supplemental; Decisive; Instant; Pre-Reqs: Arcane Fire):
      The Twilight may grant a decisive attack with SSoT the Lethal and Smashing tags. Alternatively, she may inflict bashing damage against her target and all those (allies and enemies) within short range, dividing her initiative amongst them equally. She is reset to base initiative as long as at least one target suffers damage.

      Arrow of Heaven Technique ((1wp); Occult 5, Essence 3; Supplemental; Aggravated, Dual, Psyche; Instant; Pre-Reqs: Solar Lightning):
      The Twilight recognises the spiritual taint of the Sun's condemnation on her enemies. By expending 1wp, a successful withering attack with SSoT against any creature of darkness may forego claiming the target's initiative, instead instilling a Major Tie of terror towards the Twilight in it's victim. She still gains 1i for landing a withering attack. Any of her subsequent decisive attacks against that target deal aggravated damage, so long as the intimacy remains. The target may pay (Twilight's Essence+1) to resist the Intimacy and render themselves immune for one full day and night.

      Shield of Copper Flame (6m; Essence 3; Simple; - ; One Scene; Pre-Reqs: Impenetrable Wall of Adamant):
      The Solar may extend the Hardness (and Soak if Protecting Heaven's Lamp is active) of her anima to (Essence) allies within medium range, who then activate it for the same mote cost as her or gain it automatically whilst she is at Bonfire level, all glowing with hues of her anima. Alternatively she may extend this protection to a Battle Group she commands with a Size of no more than her (Essence - 2).

      Sovereign Entourage Attitude ( - (1ahl); Essence 3; Permanent; - ; Permanent; Pre-Reqs: Celestial Circle Sorcery, Eternal Warlord Stance):
      The Twilight may make a demon or elemental of any essence rating into a spirit familiar. This causes a level of aggravated damage that cannot heal as long as the familiar bond remains, her anima straining to contain the spirit's immense power. 2nd circle spirits take up two 'slots', 3rd circle spirits take up three 'slots'.

      Guardian Force Assumption (5m, 1wp; Essence 4; Reflexive; - ; Instant; Pre-Reqs: Shield of Copper Flame, Sovereign Entourage Attitude):
      It is the nature of the guard to take an arrow meant for the prince he protects. If the Twilight is hit by a decisive attack, she may select any spirit familiar bound to her anima and manifest their visage around her. The spirit takes the full brunt of the attack for her, increasing the damage one step (bashing to lethal, lethal to aggravated) as it's life force is burned in service of it's mistress. This evocation may only be used once per scene, unless reset by the Twilight inflicting Initiative Crash on a non-trivial opponent.

      Purge the Unclean Soul (10m; Essence 4; Reflexive; Counterattack, Decisive, Perilous; Instant; Pre-Reqs: Impenetrable Wall of Adamant, Arrow of Heaven Technique):
      Should the hardness of the Twilight's anima successfully negate a decisive attack, she may reflexively unleash a decisive counterattack with SSoT, streams of burning gold enveloping her foe. Should this foe be a creature of darkness, then in addition to any damage suffered they are subject to the Plague of the Sun without hope of resisting the holy disease. This does not reset her to base initiative.

      Avatar of the Descending Sun (10m, 1wp; Essence 5; Simple; Perilous; One Scene; Pre-Reqs: two of Dusk Internalising Method, Guardian Force Assumption, Purge the Unclean Soul, Rally the Children of Twilight, Sundown Casts Long Shadows, Untarnished Copper Spider):
      The Solar is the embodiment of the Sun's facet her caste represents, her Anima rising to iconic level and unable to subside or be shed until the end of the scene.
      Integrity: The Twilight is immune to psyche effects and her Minor intimacies are treated as Major when opposing social influence.
      Investigation: The Twilight knows the minds of those she can see, their guile set to zero unless they exceed her essence.
      Linguistics: The Twilight understands, speaks, reads and may write all languages as well as a native speaker. Her graceful tongue increases all positive intimacies towards her by one step.
      Lore: The Twilight may introduce a single fact that is not internally consistent, but remains a fact until the end of the scene. This power may only be used once per story.
      Occult: The Twilight inflicts the Plague of the Sun with any damage-dealing decisive attack with SSoT, her own judgement sufficient to call down the curse in the Sun's name.
