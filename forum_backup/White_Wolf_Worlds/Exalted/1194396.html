    Orichalcum Prayer Beads of Enlightenment - Zenyatta's Orbs

    03-11-2018, 08:09 PM
    Gauging forum interest in this project.

    Light Artifact Throwing weapon. No Hearthstone slot. Primarily orichalcum, with moonsilver and starmetal for flavor.

    Upon attuning to these eight spheres, they levitate around the user's neck, requiring no hands to hold. They can be readied for combat reflexively, but require at a free hand to launch one. Using a Ready Weapon action recalls all fired spheres, which teleport back to the user.

    The first and perhaps only attack Evocation would be Orb of Destruction, which upgrades the use of Cascade of Cutting Terror, perhaps improving that Charm's performance based on how many prayer beads you still have ready, and explicitly letting that Charm reset when you use the Ready Weapon recall action to recall all of the beads.


    The next Evocation is Orb of Harmony, which sends a sphere to another character to orbit around their head. It might provide them with a Passion of Hope, Joy, Reassurance, or similar positive emotion that they might be able to use to boost their Resolve against appropriate social influence or providing an avenue for the monk's Persuade attempts. Leaving a bead floating around someone else means you can't use it to attack, but you can choose to leave it with them when recalling the others.

    From this Evocation would come Solar-specific Evocations that make the orb a channel for Presence Charms.
    • Empowering Shout – reduces the mote cost by 1 if the orb was attached to the subject before the current round.
    • Favor-Conferring Prana, Underling-Promoting Touch – waives the willpower cost, but the effect only lasts as long as the orb is attached
    • Divinity-Conferring Touch – halves the mote cost, but the effect only lasts as long as the orb is attached
    • Shedding Infinite Radiance – can perform the dramatic action within one turn of combat, but the effect only lasts as long as the orb is attached
    Another would allow the Solar to target the orb's subject with the following Lore Charms: Essence-Lending Method, Will-Bolstering Method, and Wound-Accepting Technique.

    Another would allow the Solar to target the orb's subject with Medicine Charms that instantly take effect, such as Touch of Blissful Release and Anointment of Miraculous Health. If the Solar knows Instant Treatment Methodology, medical treatment actions through the Orb of Harmony become possible. Using Instant Treatment Methodology likewise enables the use of Medicine Charms that replace treatment actions.


    "Transcendance" requires the Solar to know the pinnacle Integrity Charm, Inviolable Essence Merging. The Evocation empowers the Orbs to use the Solar's traits -- including her Charms -- to minister to others for as long as she meditates in their presence. This by itself doesn't allow the Solar to float around anywhere, doesn't provide an increase in speed, and certainly doesn't avoid IEM's restriction against attacking or harming others prior to meditation.


    Discord Orb might inflict a Passion of Despair, Doubt, Sadness, or Fear upon the target, penalizing their Resolve against appropriate social influence and providing an avenue for the monk to perform Persuade attempts.

    Evocations would allow the long-range use of Injury-Forcing Technique and Essence-Draining Touch, and assist in the use of Will-Shattering Illusion and the Solar Presence Charms, Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment and Holy Touch.

                                      ORB OF HARMONY
                                      1m; Simple; Indefinite; Decisive-only; Essence 1; No Prerequisites

                                      The monk casts an orb at an ally within long range. Targeting an unwilling subject out of battle requires an opposed roll, and in battle requires a Difficulty 2 gambit. If it succeeds, the sphere orbits around their head and draws out their most positive thoughts and memories. In effect, it provides them with a Passion of Hope, Joy, Reassurance, or similar positive emotion that they might be able to use to boost their Resolve against appropriate social influence. This Passion also provides an avenue for the monk's own social influence attempts, and the bonus is instead applied as a penalty to the subject’s Guile for the monk’s attempts to glean the thoughts or Intimacies which the orb is strengthening.

                                      The monk may bless as many subjects as he has orbs and motes to commit, though he cannot bless an individual with more than one orb. While the orb remains with a subject, the monk cannot use it to attack. The monk need not recall the orb when recalling orbs he has used to attack. If the subject enters extreme range or breaks line of sight for more than one round, the orb reflexively returns on its own.


                                      HARMONIOUS ENCOURAGEMENT
                                      No Cost; Permanent; No Keywords; Essence 2; Orb of Harmony, Empowering Shout

                                      When the Solar is blessing an individual with an Orb of Harmony, he enjoys additional benefits to using the following Charms upon them:

                                      Empowering Shout – reduces the mote cost by 1 if the orb was attached to the subject before the current round.
                                      Favor-Conferring Prana – waives the willpower cost, but the effect only lasts as long as the orb is attached
                                      Underling-Promoting Touch – waives the willpower cost, but the effect only lasts as long as the orb is attached
                                      Divinity-Conferring Touch – halves the mote cost, but the effect only lasts as long as the orb is attached
                                      Shedding Infinite Radiance – can perform the dramatic action within one turn of combat, but the effect only lasts as long as the orb is attached


                                      HARMONIOUS HEALING
                                      No Cost; Permanent; No Keywords; Essence 2; Orb of Harmony, Instant Treatment Methodology or Touch of Blissful Release

                                      When the Solar is blessing an individual with an Orb of Harmony, he may target them from afar with any Medicine Charms which take effect instantly, such as Touch of Blissful Release.

                                      If the Solar knows Flawless Diagnosis Technique, he may perform a diagnosis action from afar, and enjoys +1 success when conventionally diagnosing the patient more closely.

                                      If the Solar knows Instant Treatment Methodology, he may use that Charm to perform medical treatment actions from afar, and use Medicine Charms which take the form of medical treatment actions.


                                      HARMONIOUS EMPOWERMENT
                                      No Cost; Permanent; No Keywords; Essence 2; Orb of Harmony, Essence-Lending Method

                                      When the Solar is blessing an individual with an Orb of Harmony, he may target them with Essence-Lending Method, Will-Bolstering Technique, or Wound-Accepting Technique without needing to touch them.

                                      If the Solar is blessing all allies she wishes to affect with Power-Restoring Invocation, she waives the Charm’s Initiative point cost.

                                      If the Solar knows Surging Essence Flow, her orbs may continue to bless an individual even if they spend an hour outside of her line of sight, though they must remain within long range.

                                          Removing an Orb of Harmony or Discord from one's self or another character requires a Decisive attack which scores (monk's Essence + 2) successes on the damage roll. The Orb's Defense is equal to the monk's Essence rating.


                                          ORB OF DISCORD
                                          2m; Simple; Indefinite; Decisive-only; Essence 2; Orb of Destruction, Orb of Harmony

                                          The monk casts an orb at an individual within long range. If this does not provoke a battle, the action requires an opposed roll. If it does, or the two characters are already rolled into battle, it requires a Difficulty 2 gambit. If it succeeds, the sphere orbits around their head and draws out their most negative thoughts and memories. In effect, it provides them with a Passion of Despair, Sorrow, Regret, Anger, or similar negative emotion that they might be able to use to boost their Resolve against appropriate social influence, or that others might use against them. The penalty of the Passion is applied to the subject’s Guile for the monk’s attempts to glean the thoughts or Intimacies which the orb is strengthening.

                                          The monk may burden as many subjects as he has orbs and motes to commit, though he cannot burden an individual with more than one orb. While the orb remains with a subject, the monk cannot use it to attack. The monk need not recall the orb when recalling orbs he has used to attack. If the subject enters extreme range or breaks line of sight for more than one round, the orb reflexively returns on its own.


                                          PAIN IS AN EXCELLENT TEACHER
                                          No Cost; Permanent; No Keywords; Essence 3; Orb of Harmony, Injury-Forcing Technique or Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment

                                          When the Solar is burdening an individual with an Orb of Discord, he may target them with Injury-Forcing Technique, Essence-Draining Touch, and Holy Touch without needing to touch them. When using Holy Touch, the target is always pushed away from the monk.

                                          The cost of Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment against that subject is reduced by 2 motes.

                                          The penalty of the negative Passion inspired by the Discord Orb applies to their roll to resist Will-Shattering Illusion.

                                                      Type: Light (Damage + 10, Overwhelming 3)
