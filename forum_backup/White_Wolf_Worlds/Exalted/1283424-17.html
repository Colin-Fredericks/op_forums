                                                            Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                                            To be fair, Solars do have something like that. Three, even. One lets the Solar socialite pass himself off as useless, someone to be disregarded; one lets the Solar socialite pass himself off as useful, the key to getting that thing the attempted reader needs; the third lets the Solar socialite pass himself off as a void of infinite depth that leaves his attempted reader hypnotized.

                                                            There's not a Charm that lets you pretend a Read Intentions roll against you succeeded because Read Intentions rolls don't exist in-character. The only people who know a Read Intentions roll succeeded or failed are the players, and if doesn't foster a good gaming environment for a Charm to have you lie to your fellow players.
                                                            Enh. I don't find your argument about the 'in-character / out-character' issue persuasive. All because the mechanics of such a Charm would allow you to invert the expectation of Read Intentions (and I would restrict it to that), that doesn't mean that that is how the in-character narrative plays out.

                                                            I do find your argument about lying to fellow players persuasive, though. I should point out that my players tend to be much more cohesive and not worried about other players knowing their Intimacies, but I can see how other groups that are not so focused would have issue.

                                                            I'll just use an example to help make sure we are on the same page about this. Imagine a character saying/thinking, 'Wow, this guy is really going off on an anti-Immaculate screed. Does he really hate the Immaculates so much?' That is easily an in-story concept that is, mechanically, asking for Read Intentions. The answers you can normally get is either the truth or, 'you can't tell/don't know'. 'You don't know' is a very different animal than believing a lie. And pulling social antagonists into false positions where they try to Persuade you using an Intimacy you don't actually have would be an awesome defensive tool, reserving your precious Willpower for more important issues.
