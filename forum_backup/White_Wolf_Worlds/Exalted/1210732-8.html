                                                    To my eye, there are two basic foundations for getting really hung up on there being this small, very specific collection of styles that lack Mastery and Terrestrial keywords. One is getting extremely hung up on the minutia of the made-up metaphysics of the setting being... I won't even say consistent, because actual physical laws don't have that level of consistency... completely inflexible. The other is getting extremely hung up on how great the Solars are; like, when it's going beyond them being the mightiest of the Exalted and into the heights of their power being beyond comprehension (which... no, I can pretty easily comprehend the things marked out by the Essence 5 Charms) and an actual law of reality.

                                                    I find both things to be ridiculous as a basis for assessing the game. It's getting faaaar too immersed into something, beyond the point where it serves as something useful for telling interesting stories and playing fun games.

                                                    ​I could contrive an explanation for why the Immaculate Styles work as they do within that paradigm, but I hardly see it as being worth the effort, because it's not a premise I accept, and I regard it as a losing proposition anyway.

                                                    ​I find the most important thing to be metatextual, and metatextually, the Immaculate Styles have always held a special place in system and setting in association with the Dragon Blooded. A place that is rather unique in how the game approaches martial arts, and the characteristics given to the Dragon Blooded as reigning Exalted of the setting, both in terms of the forces that they need to fight and the idea of them having an institutionally established monastic order.

                                                    ​So when the martial arts are redesigned from the ground up to flatten Terrestrial and Celestial styles into one plane, with variations for practitioners represented by keywords, I find the weight of that pedigree for how these particular Exalted associate with these particular styles to take precedence, such that it would be undermined by saying "Solars get better use out of them as well". I'm often sceptical of arguments regarding crossover between Solars and other types as constituting a demand for Solars to get everybody else's cool toys, but I would not dispute somebody seeing that there.

                                                    ​I don't find it to be a precedent for how anybody else approaches the martial arts, or any other form of power in the setting, because nothing else has ever had the particular unique relationship that Dragon Blooded have to those five styles.

                                                    ​The only argument that I ever saw that felt like it had any credibility was that lacking Mastery would make an Immaculate Dragon Style an inferior combat choice to other martial arts, or their own Charms, and I find the Charms that have actually been written to sufficiently discredit that. Where else are you getting combat Charms that allow you to make a single attack roll against an indefinite number of opponents and apply a flat Initiative division as damage to all of them? Or various other neat and powerful things that the actual Styles can do? They seem like a reasonable selection compared against the awesome fighting monstrosity that is Black Claw Style.

                                                    ​I'm actually given to wonder if the Immaculate Styles make up for their lack of such keywords by being generally stronger than most styles. That would strike me as the basis for why people that would be constrained by the Terrestrial keyword being unable to learn them at all.
