    Integrity charm tree is up.

    Changes / fixes:
    • Fixed out of order Force-Rending Strike / Blade-Rebuking Wrath in Brawl charm tree
    • Changed Righteous Lion Defense prereq: Stubborn Boar Defense -> Steel Heart Stance (too many things hanging off of Stubborn Boar Defense - also thematically this makes sense)
    • Removed "Steel Heart Defense" (typo) prereq from Invincible Solar Aegis (its remaining dependency now depends on Steel Heart Defense)
    • Removed prereq of Sun King Radiance (Any five Performance, Presence, or Socialize Charms)
    • Removed Destiny-Manifesting Method. As Sanct said long ago (at least I think it was him), you shouldn't need a charm to say "don't turn my character into a frog permanently, ST."
    • Changed Phoenix Renewal Tactic prereq: Destiny-Manifesting Method -> Integrity Protecting Prana.
