                            That said...

                            If you start with Dex 3, Melee 3, Medium Weapon... then you have a Parry of 4 (5 with a Stunt) which you can bump to 7 (8) with Excellent Melee.

                            If you run the wolves as individual characters, in a pack of 4+, with each wolf spending Willpower, you're rolling 9 (11 with a Stunt) dice + 4 automatic successes against that Parry 4-7 (5-8).

                            With Onslaught, you're even likely to land the 6d grapple.

                            That's a pretty decent fight right there.

                            (Wolves also flee after 4HL, making it easy to drive them off for an opening encounter if things start to go wrong, or if the PCs display a dramatic reversal of fortunes -- which is likely to happen after they pull themselves back together after the initial shock-and-awe.)

                            If you can start unlocking Latent abilities on those wolves (Lunar Mistress? Angry Forest King? Wood Outcaste? Wolf-Lord Exigent?) then Hold at Bay and Snarling Guardian Attack/Loyal Guardian gives you the basis of a pretty decent boss fite.
