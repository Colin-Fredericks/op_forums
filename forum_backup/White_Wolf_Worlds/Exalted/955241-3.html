                                Eat Rice Ball: 3
                                Give Rice Away: 2
                                Give Half Rice Ball Away: 2
                                Heist: 1
                                Boat Ride: 2
                                Fishing: Yes

                                You look at the rapidly cooling, increasingly damp rice ball in your hands. You look over at the line. Banked Coals’ words about asking what Sextes Jylis would do echo in your head…you take a step towards the line, which is now breaking into a wave of disappointed and angry people before the gates of the monastery.

                                Then your stomach growls again, and you take a step back. You really, really want to eat this rice ball. You really, really want to do the right thing and give it away. You think about trying to do both-giving away half and keeping half…but it’s not a very big rice ball.

                                You eat it. You feel guilty, and you feel angry for feeling guilty because it’s easy for Banked Coals to talk about doing what Sextes Jylis would do when she’s on the inside of the closed gates with plenty of rice to eat, but dammit, you still feel bad for not being able to feed anyone else. It makes you feel…weak.

                                No, screw this! You are going home, and you are going to get some food of your own on the way. You leave the crowd behind you, shouting as politely as they can at the monks inside the gates in the hopes of more food, and you head for the canal. As you get closer to the water, you can make out the sounds of the canal over the dull pounding of the gray rain. There’s a low horn blast from one of the larger rafts, warning smaller craft of its location to prevent collisions in the fading light and heavy rain. The canal boatmen, without such luxuries, make do with covered lanterns and yelling.

                                You stop a couple blocks short of the canal to see if you can catch some bait. The road here is cobbled stone, but by the side of the road there’s some muddy dirt and grass growing along the edges of the buildings, where the eaves of the building are pouring waterfalls down onto the street. A little bit of searching quickly finds you six fat worms wriggling along the cobblestones to escape drowning. You shove them in your pocket and head for the canal, tying some twine to one of your fishhooks as you go.

                                At the docks, it’s not at all hard to find a ride: the storm has most sensible boatmen moored at the docks. There aren’t too many people out today asking for open air canal rides on an evening like this, so many have called it a night and headed to some warm dry tavern to spend their earnings. There’s a few still out, though: Nexus has plenty of commuters who live in one district and work in another, and pay a boatman to ferry them up and down the canals. Lots of these are workers in the Nighthammer district-the various foundries and ironworks pay well enough, and if your pay is good enough to afford anything else you don’t generally decide to live with Nighthammer’s late night clanging and choking black fogs.

                                There, in the big black wide-rimmed hat with the white koi painted on it! That’s Grinning Eel, and old friend of your father’s. You never knew your father that well, but you have some faint memories of him dropping in from time to time with fish or coin to help Mother out. Once or twice Eel came with him. Grinning Eel is a grumpy old man…but he’s very fond of seafood.

                                “Eel!” You shout as you come down the docks. “Can I get a ride to Nighthammer?”

                                He looks up, rain pouring off the rims of his hat, and squints. “Well I’ll be damned. How’s it going, kid? Haven’t seen you since I gave you that ride home after Calibration! Sure, I think I can make it over to Nighthammer for you. You’ve gotten bigger. You got coin?”

                                “I have six worms and a fishing line, Eel. Figured I could catch you dinner and call it even.”

                                Eel frowns at you, his grey moustache twitching in distaste. “And if you don’t catch anything?”

                                “You’re not getting any paying fares here today anyway. If you give me a ride to Nighthammer, you might at least be able to catch some commuters on the way back who missed their regular boat.”

                                “Yeah…or I could get wrecked halfway there trying to go against the Nighthammer traffic in the dark. In the rain. For no pay.”

                                The old man seems like he wants to help you, but he’s against giving out free rides on principle. What tack should you take here?
                                ----

                                “You’re not going to get wrecked, Eel, you’ve been doing this forever! You know that canal like the back of your hand. Give me a ride, it doesn’t cost you anything and we might both get a free dinner out of it.” (+Charisma, +Larceny, +Survival, Contacts: Nexus Boatmen)

                                “Eel…I don’t like asking for help, but you and Father were friends, which means you’re kind of the closest thing I’ve got to family. If you don’t give me a ride, I gotta take the rooftop roads by night in the rain, and even if I don’t slip and fall I hear there’s a raptor cat on the loose. Can’t you help me out?” (+Charisma, +Socialize, +Survival, Ally: Grinning Eel)

                                “Grinning Eel, I get that you’ve got to make a living, but you’re not doing that sitting here in the rain without a fare anyway. No money and a chance of fish is better than no money and no fish.” (+Manipulation, +Bureaucracy, +Socialize)

                                “Eel…you’re not getting any younger, and like you say wrecks happen in the canal all the time. Wouldn’t you like to be able to tell the Dragons that you helped out a hungry kid just trying to get home on a rainy night? Maybe come back as something better in your next life?” (+Manipulation, +Occult, +Socialize)

                                “You and my Father were friends, old man. Dragons know he wasn’t the most reliable guy, but he paid his debts, and so will I. If I don’t catch anything tonight, I’ll catch something tomorrow, or the next day, and it’s yours.” (+Manipulation, +Presence, +Bureaucracy)

                                “You knew my Father, Eel. He was a deadbeat who gambled away all his money and died in debt. Mother always told me to be better than him, and I mean to do that. If I tell you I’m going to give you a fish, then today or tomorrow or the next day, I’m going to catch a fish and give it to you. I pay my debts.” (+Charisma, +Integrity, +Presence)

                                “Eel. It’s evening. It’s raining. That’s when the fish bite! We’re not gonna catch anything if we stay here yammering, but I bet on a day like today we can catch enough to sell what we can’t eat at the market, if you really want coin so bad. Let’s go!” (+Charisma, +Survival, +Bureaucracy)

                                “Eel…you’re right. I have gotten bigger since last time you saw me. I’m big enough to work now, and you’re getting older. You've got no customers right now, you're not busy. Give me a ride, show me how to steer and pole the boat, and if I’m any good maybe in a few months you can relax in a dry tavern while I take the rainy nights. My Father was a boatman, it's a good trade, and you don't have any kids of your own to give the boat to when you retire, right?” (+Manipulation, +Sail, +Bureaucracy, Ally: Grinning Eel)

                                “Eel…you’re right. I have gotten bigger since last time you saw me. Now shut it and give me a ride, old man, or I take your boat and use you as fish bait.” You pull out your knife and point it threateningly. (+Charisma, +Presence, +Melee)

                                “Eel…you’re right. I have gotten bigger since last time you saw me. Perhaps if I can’t catch any fish there’s…something else I can help you with?” (+Appearance, +Socialize, +Performance)

                                Something Else (+?)

                                • Tattered Rags

                                • Improvised Fishing Line w/hook

                                • Your Lucky Pebble
                                • Pile of Stolen Clothes (mostly rags)
                                • Twine Armband
                                • Steel spearhead (six-inch knife blade with a clumsy handle)
