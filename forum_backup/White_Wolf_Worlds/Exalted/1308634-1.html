    Originally posted by Ancient of days View Post
    OK so after a discussion with my players it appears Aura is a deal breaker. So anyone got any ideas of houserules to get past this?. What breaks if I just ignore this?.

    You can do several things to make it easier on your players:

    1) Ignore Aura-lock at Bonfire, and/or allow Reflexive out-of-turn Charms to create Aura as much as they can disrupt it. This makes several things MUCH more attractive without completely breaking the intended balance for DBs.

    2) As Sandact said, make it so that the PC DBs are ALWAYS treated as being in all five auras, all the time, which kind of breaks them balance-wise but also makes their kit feel so much better.

                                            Okay so let’s do the same thing with a combined Steel Devil Stylist, I’ll even be very generous to your Fire Dragon purist and not have an applicable specialty, or use anima powers, or have a friend who’s species into lore here. Although I appreciate the teamwork.


                                            Start out 15m Naked Fang Draw and Flash Fire, were JB with +2 successes.

                                            So we’re rolling 12 dice since we don’t have an applicable specialty but we can stunt it and it’s not an opposed roll so we can’t just say the effect is canceled by a stunt on the other side. Plus stunting JB is easy with a character this cool. Let’s even say we roll bad and even with two chances we can’t roll better than average so we get 6 becoming 11 initiative.

                                            Now we won JB so we get a free charge from Naked Fang Draw, we can reflexively enter Fire Dragon, and dump another 9 init into charge to max it out.

                                            Now if we’re attacking somebody who has 4 parry, we only need 6 successes for triple attack technique, and it doesn’t even matter if it’s 4 parry because their base is 4, or because it’s 5 and somebody already laid an onslaught penalty on them or what. It could even have been 7 and somebody knocked them to the ground with a smash attack or something. After dumping so much init into our charge it’s likely one of our hearth mates went before us anyway. With a single excellency die on that we’re getting 17 so let’s say we roll under average yet again and get only 7.

                                            So we hit with triple attack and one success over for a total damage bonus, with 5 strength, of +28, and lets say that along with their good parry they had a solid soak of 12 as well, so we’re dealing 16 withering damage.

                                            Rolling that damage and rerolling 6s lets say we get 9, which crashes our target. So we have 15 initiative from that and can pay 5m 1wp to Steel Devil Strike. We won’t need to excellency this more than 1m either, our target has a -4 onslaught penalty on it already. We hit with 5 overflow easy and deal 20 decisive damage, just under what your fire aspect rolled and for 20m 1wp, since we regenerated 5m at the start of the turn.

                                            Doesn’t seem much better eh? But here’s the differences:

                                            It doesn’t work against anyone with a good onslaught penalty negator, which I feel like people reading this will forget I said, even though I’ve put this here.

                                            We crashed the crap out of one enemy

                                            We added a -4 onslaught penalty to that same enemy

                                            We didn’t use our anima power or need an ally species in anything but “has an attack”

                                            We’re sitting with a Steel Devil offhand charge of 10, which means the only difference between the attack combo we launched this turn and the one we’re going to launch next turn is that we won’t be getting that 5 overflow from Flash Fire. It if we want to conserve motes and willpower just launch a triple attack technique and crash another enemy into the ground with a big onslaught penalty and just keep the initiative for 3m.


                                            Is it better in all situations? Hell no! But it’s definitely not crap.
