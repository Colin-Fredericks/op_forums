        Dying Light Rekindled

        DB Dodge.

        Duration: [dodge+specialty] actions?

        The exalts wounds burn with the light of her anima, conferring uncanny reflexes and agility.
        For each lethal or aggravated wound, the Exalt adds one yard to her movement rate and one to her dodge rating for [dodge+specialty] actions, up to normal dice-adder limits. The Exalt can use this extra movement during a dodge attempt, seeming to flicker out of the way of attacks. In an action where she has used her full movement (including the extra), she may apply that many dice as a bonus to her raw damage as her attack bursts with the same light that gives her new-found energy (capped by Dodge). This immediately ends the charm.


        Approaching Glacier Consternation

        DB Presence (water-aspected)

        Type: supplemental
        Duration: [presence + specialty] actions?

        With a well-placed glance, the Dragons' chosen chill the veins of their rivals, plaguing them with the inevitability of a crushing defeat. The dragonblood activates this charm in tandem with an Intimidation attempt (usually Charisma or Appearance + Presence). If successful, the afflicted feels an overwhelming and oppressive urge to back away from the dragonblooded whenever he draws near. If the target cannot back away they suffer a penaly to their actions equal to [distance covered in yards capped by Presence + specialty] as their blood stiffens and their breath draws shorter. The feeling is not unlike a mix of claustrophobia and drowning.

                    Innocent Guilt Declaration

                    Dragonblooded Bureaucracy

                        Twitching Shadows Maneuver - Solar Stealth

                        Instant, 1 action, supplemental

                        The Solar acts with such speed and subtlety that even shadows struggle to keep up.
                        While this charm is active, the solar seems to be doing nothing at all - engaging in pointless banter, keeping on with whatever he was doing (writing, showering) or just standing there. This charm permits the Solar to roll Dexterity + Stealth and to perform a single Action's worth (5 or 6 ticks) of actions under the guise of mundanity. He could, for example, dash to a podium, steal the crown it holds and dash back to his starting position, while seemingly not having moved or done anything at all. The difficulty to notice the Solar's actions is [his stealth successes = his essence]. This charm can be reactivated at the beginning of the next action to extend its effects, for a maximum of [the Solar's Stealth] consecutive activations.

                        If when this charm ends the solar is in a different location than when it started, his image seems to vanish in a puff of dust at the end of that action.
                        [cue Homer Simpson vanishing in a puff of dust at the mention of doughnuts]

                        This charm can explicitly be comboed with charms of other abilities.

                        Tear-Soaked Deathcry Deceit - Abyssal Performance
                        The abyssal makes a great show of mournful heartbreak at the death of a person (at the moment of their death or when the topic is brought up). These crocodile tears are so convincing that as long as the charade is upheld, onlookers are convinced that the Abyssal bears a positive Defining Intimacy towards the deceased. Their attempts to exploit that intimacy however (even if in a positive manner, such as offering condolences) suffer from a penalty equal to the bonus such an exploitation would incur. Every failed attempt feeds The Lover's empty heart with warmth and compassion, robbing the target of a point of willpower and providing the Abyssal with a cumulative +1 Appearance bonus (to a maximum of +Essence) for as long as motes remain committed.

                                Forged from Flesh and Steel - Celestial Circle Sorcery

                                Devised by the Queen of Fangs as a weapon of terror and destruction (when the time arises for such things), she has thoroughly lost control of its distribution; it graces the libraries of many Lunar and Sidereal sorcerers alike. The servant created by this spell's power is a versatile, reliable companion.

                                Using a hammer made of the bones of an earth elemental, the sorcerer beats steel plates into a rough facimile of a skeleton (or a suit of armor) in a ritual that lasts six hours. The light of the full moon is preferred for such an endeavor, for the steel flows like quicksilver under her hammer under Luna's gaze and hardens as the moon sets, but with enough strength and determination it may be done at any time.

                                Her own flesh weaves itself into the construction, spinning into iron-tough joints and ligaments that allow the construct to move with great speed and strike with great strength. Seals of blood are inscribed into it, and a rudimentary intelligence awakens and a baleful red flame glows where its eyes would be. This construct is utterly loyal to the sorcerer, but is not particularly bright and usually extremely literal in its interpretation of commands. (If told not to kill anyone, it may maim a mortal in the way of its objective and explain, "he'll live.")

                                In an additional component of the ritual, traces of blood or shed skin from an enemy may be powdered and incorporated into the blood seals. If this is done, the construct forever knows the direction in which its quarry can be found. When created in this manner, it can't be bargained with. It can't be reasoned with. It doesn't feel pity, or remorse, or fear. And it absolutely will not stop, ever, until they are dead.

                                    Tiny Universes Mantra

                                    Raksha Cup, requiring that one charm that lets you Shape inside people's dreams.
                                    Costly enough to only be used once in a while, because setting it up might be a huge pain for the ST. Like... 10 motes, 5 gossamer and 1 willpower.


                                    Having perfected the art of Shaping the Soul, the Raksha recognises mortal souls for what they truly are: a myriad of worlds, all twisted upon themselves. When inside a Creation-born's dreams, the Raksha spends a scene invoking the Mantra of Tiny Universes to reveal the true splendor of the target's Soul and rolls [Manipulation + Craft (Glamour)], contested by the target's [Willpower + Integrity + Wyld Resistence Bonus]. If successful, the Raksha can Quest against that mortal's soul as though it were an Unshaped - the storyteller makes up Actors and Graces based upon the mortal's virtues, essence and willpower. Effects that protect the target's soul from Shaping permit a Clash of Wills rolls. Directly touching iron protects the target from the effects of this charm.

                                    The Raksha can gain the usual benefits from doing so: gaining or improving Graces, Treasures, Spells, Behemoths and Oaths. Mortals are left permanently deranged by this process - their relevant virtues lose a number of dots equal to the new rating of the Raksha's relevant Artifact (so if it was raised to 3, the victim loses 3 points in the relevant virtue) and that virtue's channels are emptied completely.

                                    This is a Crippling effect that cannot be healed without magic.

                                            Originally posted by Guancyto
                                            Lessons in Scarlet
                                            Fire-Aspect Linguistics.

                                            2m per paragraph, supplemental, indefinite

                                            The Exalt infuses her writing instrument with scarlet essece - the blood of Hesiesh - that marks the page briefly before disappearing. As long as motes remain committed to this charm, the essence-ink remains invisible and undetectable except through effects that can percieve essence (and even then, effects like Measure the Wind don't offer enough precision to read the message, though All Encompassing Sorcerer's Sight would.)

                                            Burning the medium that the message was written on releases the essence (and the commitment), creating luminous fire glyphs that hang in the air for a second before disappearing. Though its appearance is brief, the message lingers in the vision and memory of any onlookers long enough to absorb its full meaning, no matter how long the text was.

                                            Releasing the motes committed to this charm may set the medium ablaze, triggering the described effect, may simply erase the message, or make it appear as normal writing (a mixture of burn and charcoal marks) at the Exalt's discretion.

                                            Edit: on second thought, this might be better as a longer-lasting, reactivate-able sorcery enchantment or something... hmmm


                                            Cloak of Shades and Regrets

                                            1st circle Necromancy.

                                            The Necromancer restrains a ghost and shoves her whole arm down the throat of a ghost while performing the mudra of past regrets. As she pulls her hand out, the ghost is simultaneously turned inside out and transmogrified into a shimmering cowl of pitch-black ectoplasm interwoven with Soulsteel thread.

                                            Wearing the cowl cloaks the Necromancer in spectral shadows. Anyone catching even a glimpse of the caster has their deepest regrets come to the forefront of their minds and feel old wounds anew. This serves to drive attention away from the caster, imposing [some internal penalty due to grief and distraction] on most actions.

                                            Anyone who would gaze directly upon the swirling Shades sees the Necromancer as a black blotch in their vision that absorbs all light - resembling a localized spot of blindness more than a person - and is overcome with grief, increasing the penalty by [a bit].

                                            Additionally, the Necromancer counts as being invisible for enemies wishing to engage in combat.

                                            The cowl persists until it is exposed to light more intense than a bonfire (such as sunlight or a Solar's totemic anima flare), or direct contact with fire (at which point it burns as readily as dry paper. A Fire Dragonblood's anima flux counts.)

                                                    Originally posted by Gaius
                                                    Black Cat Erudition
                                                    Lunar Appearance, scene-long, somewhat-equivalent of Mastery of Small Manners

                                                    The Lunar expresses supernatural assurance and volition in her body language, appearing to be so learned and noble as to be above rudeness and petty slights. Whenever the Lunar's actions would offend a character, that character convinces themselves that the Lunar's offence is meant as something greater - philosophical commentary, artistic expression, light-hearted parody or the like. This charm doesn't give any explicit control over the perceived meaning, though circumstances and context can definitely provide guidance.

                                                    For example, spitting on an expensive tiger-peacock rug may be taken as a commentary on the extreme contrast between the host's exuberance and the poverty of the exploited peasants seen outside.

                                                            Nihilistic Oblation Embargo
                                                            Sidereal Medicine

                                                            The Reckoners understand that the prayer and sacrifice of the living helps to feed the restless dead, and so may choose to sever these threads in several ways.

                                                            By committing 5 motes, the Sidereal may trace an approximation of The Corpse constellation with a circle around it on the ground, at least two feet in diameter. Once complete, a violet flame runs along the constellation while another runs along the circle, until both flicker out. So long as these remain undisturbed, within [Essence] miles, all prayers and sacrifices devoted to ghosts are instead received by Heaven. The Sidereal also gains an Overdrive pool, which fills with 1 mote for each sacrifice valued at Resources 3 or greater, and per effective dot of the Cult Background it captures in prayer. Should she choose, the Sidereal may gain an intuitive understanding of the nature of these prayers and sacrifices (general location of its occurrence, contents and intended recipient, etc.). Ghosts who enter the area may detect the unnatural obstacle to mortal worship with a successful <insert roll> (and then sense its absence), but cannot precisely pinpoint its center or source.

                                                            The Sidereal may instead choose to commit only 1 mote, setting her hand on or offering a prayer before a shrine, altar, or other such place of worship. This act leaves a small tracing of The Corpse on the place of worship -- visible only to Essence detection -- and targets only prayer and sacrifice facilitated through this place of worship. Any ghosts to whom this place of worship was erected or dedicated may detect the interference as above, with a one-die bonus on the roll. Such aware ghosts may detect the small tracing of The Corpse, even if they could not normally see or sense Essence. In all other respects -- including ending the effect by disturbing the tracing -- this barrier to worship functions like the original version.

                                                            Lastly, the Sidereal may commit 3 motes when making contact with the ghost directly, etching its corpus with a tracing of The Corpse -- invisible as the place of worship version -- and capturing all worship it would otherwise receive. The affected spirit receives a three-die bonus on detection, and can remove it only with magic to dispel a curse or by venturing into the Labyrinth. In all other respects, this functions like the previous two versions.
