    Okay, I did have some thoughts on Industrials but they're all over the place so to summarise:
    • I need to rename them, my ultimate goal is a science based one but the name "Industrial" keeps being a creative thorn. "Scientific" Exalted doesn't have the same ring to it. Core concept is they based on mad scientists and even if not intellectually inclined before, their Exaltation pushes them to understand and test reality.
    • Not fully sure if they'd still have Autochthon as their patron, a distant source of power in their creation or not involved at all. Possible other patrons include a Primordial of lack/entropy
    • Main idea is Autochton found a side dimension to the Wyld (basically our universe) and unable to explore it fully gave the local humans a drone/token called the Earthstar which was encoded with a way for them to contact Autochthon when they were advanced enough along with some of his design notes. As their world was dying the finally found out what it was capable of but some bright spark realised it could do more; a lot more. They reverse engineered the processes to create a near infinite energy system; all it required was a massive loss of 'para-mental potential' focused on a 'high para-mental potential' individual who would be cast into Creation/Autochthonia; creating a rift that pours in essence into their world that they capture and use to produce electricity. To mass produce this method they've taken to seeding worlds with human populations, creating artificial histories and when a century or two pass they choose a candidate and then mass harvest the rest of the planet to send people into Creation/Auchthonia as new Exalts.
    • Either that or Industrials are created by their patrons as testers for Creation's laws of (meta)physics, designed to push the boundaries in ways they were not meant to be.
    • Might have a 3/4 level structure based off academic achievement: Zeroth circle (undergrad; person with potential but no access to powers yet), First circle (bachelors ~ terrestrial exalted), Second circle (masters ~ celestial exalted) and finally third circle (doctorate ~ solaroid). This progression would be based on their development of a special power called a 'Magnum Opus', a major redefinition/aberation of the laws of reality they advance over time.

                    My impressions so far:

                    Originally posted by Mr.Jaxus View Post
                    Temporals: Still base idea of a chosen of the Primordial of Time (who alternates between Chronos and Aion for Primordial and Fetch soul); they make one big jump into the past when they Exalt; going back into the past to fix a regret in exchange for some missions either Chronos or Aion. Most of their powers deal with the flow of time, their mission and change; might have some way to make more regular time travel trips but have some limit based on the continuum of history (kill x, but someone else rises up to take over their role in history) or needing to do jobs for Chronos or Aion to gain jumps - overall a espionage theme and theme of not knowing who your allies and enemies really are. I had some ideas they could be specifically survivors of some world ending threat that had a goal of trying to stop that crisis by any means possible, but I dump it due kinda means the world can't really be threatened as a Temporal would be sent back to stop it.
                    I suggest putting the Patron, whatever one decides to name it and true identity/nature is or not, in the backburner....

                    (And then midway through writing, something DOES pop-up - the Eternal Sandglass, keeper of time and its cycles, master-mistress of the neverending story of grains of existence past, present & future trickling into each other, blurring the lines of ends and beginings between countless sand & all-encompassing crystal globes, interlocked. Cecelyne, SWLIHN, whose soul is it, if not both or neither. Buried & erased in the dance of the sands and spheres...)

                    ...to focus instead on your exalts, their flavor, personality, the kind of stories and themes one might explore through them.
                    Look at those short summaries of the many exalt types (that also happen to include at least Liminals & Exigents, if memory tricks me not) in the 3e corebook for examples to ground you on framing your own creations.

                    But speaking of "Temporals" proper:
                    - They interact with time, but how, in what way does it influence their charmset or give them some sort of unique toolset, like Heart's Blood for Lunars or Astrology for Sidereals?

                    - Are future and past fixed or mutable and if the later, what effects may a time paradox or temporal loop have on the exalt or the world around it?

                    - Isn't there potential for issues with causality and if so, might not that attract attention or opposition of Sidereals and/or Getimians (that kind of happen to be alternate timelines Chosen, if memory tricks me not)?

                    Anyway, you need to focus on the Exalt side of things instead of cosmology stuff related to a patron they might not even hear of. I would advise using Exigents, with maybe some Essence mixed in, to make a single prototype to see where things go, as a sprinboard to find out what you want or not from them at least, on the mechanics/playstyle front.

                    Originally posted by Mr.Jaxus View Post
                    Atavistics: Either chosen by Lunar's shadow/blood who was the omen of doom for a people (saving some of the dying species and later using them to create the Atavistic Exalted), a collection of the souls of races slain in the Primordial War who people's ghost mass sacrificed themselves to create Exaltations to get back at the other Exalted, or a Primordial who sacrificed their non-fetch souls to create Exaltations and enter a near-death coma rather than be part of the Neverborn or Yozis. Which ever way, they are bound to various pocket universes called a 'Sidhe', which reflect on the environments of their fallen species. When an Atavistics dies, their Sidhe slow warps into a dungeon called a 'guantlet', any mortal that enters through the portals is both drawn and challenged to the core; the first one to do so will gain the Exaltation. Mortals and creatures in Sidhes will mutate into 'spawn', recreations of their Atavistic's species, which while they don't have direct control over the Atavistic exalted can influence and draw power from. Will have access to Necromancy over Sorcery. I did think of a concept of their being the replacement soul structure for a Primordial with three ranks of Exalted which determine the power of their charms, essence powers, sidhe and level of Necromancy (so a 3rd circle picks 2nd circles who picks 1st circle), but I'm not fully sold on it. Overall feel is a combination a genestealer cults from Warhammer 40k, the movie Evolution, Dungeon Capturers from Magi-The Labyrinth Of Magic and Beast the Primordial from Chronicles of Darkness.
                    The more i think about it, the more your ideas about Atavistic's exaltation process, the more something like the Hedge of Changeling the Lost, a Labyrinth-Jungle of sorts, inhabited by strange ephemeral fauna that mixes the memories of extinct species with the dream-musings of its masters, the animal fathers of beast long gone alongside the dream/shades/embers of hierarchs & hive-queens of lost races of elemental beings straddling the lines between beast, deity or people in the role of Neverborn/Deathlord-like patrons.


                    And on an aside about Scientific/Industrials/Artificers/Experimentalists or whatever other name you prefer, the summary's ideas seem to be as much (or more) about "Earth in Exalted setting somehow" than about a new/unknown Exalt type.

                    So they are mad scientists or builders of strange wonders? How does that play out, how do they mess with the Craft, Occult, Investigation or Lore rules to build or discover new crazy things? What use are the other skills for in their case?

                    Sidereals might make a good example of how to take a more "niche" concept/role, in the form of the seer/vizier/guide and through its lens fleshing out charmsets for all skills.

                        Okay, sorry for the late reply; this has been a busy time with personal matters and running a game but I am currently typing out all for all three but I've only completed Temporals so I'll post that for now:
                        Name Temporal
                        Rough Summary Chosen of time, their powers focus on is manipulating the flow. They are tied to Quests given by their patrons; their lives are shaped by suspicion, trickery and subterfuge not unlike most spy stories as they try to find out who is friend and foe.

                        TLDR: timetravelers who play as knights righting wrongs but actually stuck in a chronological spy thriller.
                        Power Levels and Count Celestial; unknown exact count, but rarely more than 30-50 at any one time except at Gatherings, which can see hundreds being chosen at the same time.

                        Some theorise that their count is even smaller as Chronos and Aion just recycle Exaltations through time as linear time might not be a series concern for them; if this is the case there might only be a handful of Exaltations reused through the past, present and future with Exalts interacting with others with the same shard in a most paradoxical manner.

                        Can live to around a millenium, but each attribute has a charm that grants a form of immortality as it’s pinnacle achievement, however all of these come with a price that must be paid or complications which limit their effectiveness (like having to drain the youth out of others). Only one such charm can be bought per Temporal.
                        Themes Time, Speed, Moments, Countering Specific Targets, Missions, Second Chances, Hope, Confusion, Angelic Inspirations, Sand, the flow of time, change in people, Spy craft, missions, duty; maybe music.
                        Patron The Titan of Time, the Primordial who reigns over time. Unfortunately to produce the glorious flow of time, the Primordial must flip between with their fetch soul; this eternal dance between Chronos and Aion defines the Primordial and shape their goals both long term and short term. Both sides have vast knowledge of the past and future, even to degrees beyond the Maidens, but are also held back by their constant infighting and conflicting goals. They entered the Primordial War on both sides, with Temporal Exalted fight to defend and kill daeva, often in ways and for reasons that seemed contradictory:
                        • Aion is the more distant personality, favouring the rules and laws of the Primordials with a strict top down approach. However they are also more invested in the continued existence of others and are time as a constant a repetitive force on life. Their current goals seem to be finding a way for the Neverborn to reincarnate to the restore either Authothchon or Gaia to the supreme power in Yu Shan.
                        • Chronos is the more involved personality, favouring the rise and fall of challenge, so much as to support the Incarnae in rebellion even before the Primordial War. Chronos is the flow and change innate to time and believes the Exalted over throwing the Yozis to not only be a good thing, but the fundamental nature of reality and seems to be look for some new revolution. Their current goals seem to be trying to overthrow the Realm while also preparing for the next Dragon Blooded uprising against the rule of Celestial Exalted and creating a new species to challenge humanity.
                        The Titan of Time’s other third circle souls are divided into three groups; The Aioni (aligned with Aion), the Chronosi (aligned to Chronos) and the Titani (unaligned with either and more focused on the general good of the Primordials needs rather than one side or the other’s power plays). Most of the time the various Daeva are hidden in personal sanctums across Creation, although one, Hurn Du’ul, pretends to be the God of Sand in Yu Shan, using this position to keep an eye on Temporals and give them a helping hand.

                        Chronos and Aion dwell in Nusquama, the capital city of Outopia, a continent sized island that is one second ahead of Creation that also has vast palatial estates for the other Daeva while replicas of Creation’s greatest people (past, present and future) prance and enjoy themselves while masking the bitter shadow war that is fought every moment in that false paradise.
                        Exaltation Method I had two thoughts on this:
                        • Each Temporal is invited to take an exaltation in order to jump back in time and resolve a singular issue or regret in their life; they can either have their mind sent back to their old body or have a full body. Either way in exchange for this second chance they have to take on a Quest from their patron (Chronos or Aion) which acts a Defining Principle. I prefer this one as it’s more personal to the Exalt and is less world (re)defining.
                        • Each Temporal is literal Exalted as the world is ending and sent back in time (ala Kyle Reese from the Terminator or James Cole from 12 Monkeys) to stop it. While I think it’s cool and adds instant stakes, it also has two major negative points; the first is that it robs world destroying threats of their potential (as someone can always go back in time to stop it) and it give all temporal exalts too much of a narrative weight and can redefine campaigns.
                        Backstory+ History The Titan of Time was always a fickle being at the best of times; unable to choose between their two selves, Chronos and Aion, about who was fetch and who was the primordial. This conflict was temporarily resolved by Autochthon by creating a system where they each take turns being the Primordial and fetch; from this was born time, and the rest is history.

                        Unfortunately this left the Titan of Time with little real presence in Creation, as one version would flicker in and out of reality in tandem with the other, leaving only their main bodies stuck in Yu Shan. Eventually Aion and Chronos were side lined due to their conflicting goals and inability to form long term relationships with the other Primordials. Aion continued to seek their favour while Chronos tried to find rapport with their lesser beings spawned by the Primordials, from the lowest mortal to the highest Incarnae. However they both still found it hard to actual exist and actually shape the affairs of Creation.

                        And then the Exalted came.

                        Temporals were not created for either side of the Primordial War but instead as personal servants of Aion and Chronos; they had initially presented their petition to Autochthon in exchange for vague assurances of helping in the war, but the Titan of Time gave their chosen some little modifications – allowing them to receive direct Quests from the Primordial (or it’s fetch) that would be reward in the course of time, creating a legion of mercenaries that allowed Chronos and Aion to finally shape affairs in Creation; and while Aion did favour the Primordials and lend them aid, Chronos’ support of the Solars and the Incarnae allowed him to butter them up after the war and spare any real repercussions. However they did slowly build a sanctuary, an island one second ahead of all things to hide in for the same reason Autochthon fled Creation; the Exalts were a threat.

                        The Temporals joined the early Deliberative, although some were barred for their service to the Primordials during the Divine Revolution. However the Temporals still had some loyalty to Aion, and their Quests were viewed with suspicion by the other Exalted, to say nothing of their rumoured secret of time travel and jealousy it breed (or annoyance amongst Sidereals). Eventually they were rooted from their positions of power or new (non-temporal) heirs found when one their number fell. Once over a hundred Temporals sat with their Celestial comrades, by the Evening of Peace only one bitter representative remained and no other Temporals were known to exist.

                        And then the First Gathering occurred; at least 319 Temporals all exalted at the same time and started on various Quests, some seeking to kill and remove Solars and Lunars, others seeking to save them. The sudden chaos of war and rebellions was fuelled by the corruption of the Great Curse as mortals and Dragon Blooded saw a chance against actually and perceived tyrannies – and it was so sudden and intense the day before was dubbed the Evening of Peace. The First Gathering was quickly stopped, but the brutality needed to stop it was unimaginable intense and helped water the seeds of the Usurpation centuries later.

                        It was also the start of ‘Gatherings’, when large number of Temporal Exalted were chosen and sent into Creation, often with contradictory Quests, sowing chaos and confusion into the world. Gatherings can see hundreds of Exalts pop up, but most die with the first couple of weeks as the find themselves in misremembered surroundings or targeted by Wyld Hunts; when the Gatherings end, there is rarely more than 50 Temporals surviving and during most of the time there seems to be less than 30 Temporals in Creation (either as survivors from a Gathering or as new Exalts). Gatherings do not happen to any particular time table or regular reason; while the Great Contagion saw one (which broke all records at nearly a 1000 Temporals appeared to fight to defend Creation, and no known survivors are recorded (although some Temporals claim to have been around then and escape by jumping into the future, which is something they are known to be able (and willing) to do)) not all major events see Gathers (like the Usurpation).

                        Most of the time during the period between the First Gathering and the Great Contagion many (if not most) Temporals belonged to an underground movement called the Hourglass Conclave; it taught and guided young Temporals without requiring to go to one of the Souls of the Titan of Time and get caught up in their politics while hiding the Temporal Exalts from the watchful eye of the Deliberative. It also ironed out disputes between Temporals arising from conflicting (or near conflicting) Quests and other issues. The Hourglass helped the fledgling Silver Pact, via intel, shared resources and guidance for escaping Lunars. Both Aion and Chronos were disgruntled with the organisation and tried to destroy the Hourglass Conclave, but it’s doom came from the ruin of the Great Contagion; too much of it’s infrastructure was tied to mortal run safe houses and strong holds at the edge of Creation; the Contagion killed too many people for it to survive and many Temporals that Exalted afterwards were given Quests to dismantle the last of it, not to say of Sidereals using the chaos as an opportunity to make notable Dragon blood heroes by looting it’s artefact stashes and killing senior members while the Silver Pact absorbed those who fled into their open arms.

                        Now-a- days most Temporals are lonely heroes appointed by distant patrons with no connection to their wider history. But the Time of Tumult is quickly becoming a major Gathering and new opportunities await; especially for those to whom both the past and future are open opportunities.
                        Relations with others Temporals biggest enemies are unfortunately the fellow Temporals; their Quests are set by the conflicting Chronos and Aion; and even within the Cells (group of Temporals) of those aligned to the same persona, they can have radically different Quests that contradict with each other. This is made many older Temporals suspicious of their own kind. The reasons for this seems to be due Temperals coming from different points of the future where Aion’s and/or Chronos’ goals have changed with changing times and realities; as new Temporals Quests revealing subtle things about the future to their patrons that further change their plans and shape new Quests given to Temporals over time. The Hourglass Conclave helped alleviate many of these issues in the past but was neither fully successful (as not all Temporals joined it) and is currently non-existent.

                        Fair Folk also have a major bone to pick with the Temporals; their patron is one of the major members of the Primordials who set of the forging of Creation through the creation of time and the subsequent formation of causality wrecking their nice little stories. This has been made worse by ages of Temporals hiding out near freeholds and the edges of Creation leading to longing running conflicts between them.

                        Sidereals, along with the rest of the Bureau of Destiny, are also a major enemy of the Temporals; Even their mere Exaltation causes enough issues with Fate and Destiny that removing them is seen as priority to say nothing of sheer amount of Paradox some of their charms produce (and speaking nothing if the legends of time travel are true). It is for this reason that Sidereals and Yu Shan have one of the highest kill counts of Temporals and maintain the largest via Wyld hunts back by them; Temporals are known Anethema to all Immaculates and the Realm’s looted many of the Hourglass Conclave’s cells to ensure that no Temporals could interfere with the Scarlet Empress’ nascent empire (which considering the rumours of the Temporals martyring themselves to help her take the Imperial Manse might be more than a little hypocritical).

                        Outside of the Bureau of Destiny gods and elementals having varying opinions on Temporals but few as vitriolic as the Sidereals; some even joke the Visiers are only jealous of Temporals due to their broader (if less accurate and ‘blurry’) insights into the future not limited between what is in and out of fate. Some gods and elementals support the Temporals due to support the Hourglass Conclave gave in the past, others are tempted by Hurn Du’ul’s whispers or dreams of a better future pro-Primordial that doesn’t involve turning to the Yozis or neverborn. All in all, Yu Shan is more negative to the Temporals, spirit courts in Creation are more open to them.

                        The Lunars is interested in Temporals, some see recreating the Hourglass conclave to repay old debts or giving them support as proxies against their enemies, but even the most ardent allies of them know the fickleness of Temporals and their patrons. As such the Silver Pact has taken a cautious approach to Temporals but is more than willing to help them if it helps Lunars as well.

                        Getimians are usually willing to find some sort of kinship, as Rakan Thulio holds a level of respect for them and sees them as potential allies against Yu Shan. So far he has been rewarded with some allies and associates, but many Temporals are wary of him and the Getimians and Rakan Thulio is aware at least 2 Temporals have been sent to interfere with his goals.

                        The Yozis range from optimistic to disgusted with Temporals; Aion is more aligned to them than the Incarnae, and Aioni Temporals have helped them in the past, but Chronos is against them. The former rulers of Creation know of the full extent of Temporal’s time powers, and are aware of all timelines they create and destroy by their endeavours, so they are often invaluable sources of knowledge to Temporals, but they will only offer this at a steep price in favours. Infernal Exalted are warned of Temporals and their ways but are allowed to approach them however they wish.

                        The common undead are generally as wary of Temporals as other other Exalts, but don’t treat them differently. Ancient ghosts and Death Lords tend to know more of their powers and seek to limit their Quests in the Underworld. The Neverborn especially want Aioni Temporals kept in line as it is known they seek to reincarnate the Neverborn in one of Aion’s long term plans to remake the Primordials.

                        Autochthon himself is neutral to the Temporals, he insisted on the Quest system rather than being straight servants of the Titan of Time to ensure the Exalted had free will, but he saw them fight on both sides of the war and dislikes the Titan of Time in general (for being a jerk to him in the past) but tries take his chosen as individuals. The average Autochthonian has little to no knowledge of Temporals, and only the Divine ministers (and their servants) have accurate records on them – mostly as Temporals have no place in Autochthonia; however as the Seal of 8 Divinities is broken, Temporals are starting to appear in the Realm of Brass and Shadow.

                        Temporals best chance for allies are Solars, who similarly have a massive disconnect with their history and a lack of pre-build social supports making them kindred groups trying to survive in the Time of Tumult.
                        Castes and Anima Temperals have animas in muted colours, especially beige, but pale blues, pinks and faded greens are also common; their animas glow but do not shine like solars. I envision them as being one of four options:
                        1. Regular animas – regular anima barriers like other exalts that are symbolic representations of their character and ideals.
                        2. Wings – originally for the idea of Temporals as being chosen to stop world ending events and have and angel theme for them; might give it miss because of that but still sounds interesting. Either way it would allow them to fly in addition to other effect and each set of wings is unique to it’s exalt.
                        3. Music – instead of a traditional light based anima their soul manifests it’s power as a musical compliment to the Temporal, which while it’s tempo and tune is depended on what the Temporal is doing, the specific notes are distinct to each Temporal. Might require adding an overall musical theme to Temporals and not instantly as easy to grasp for players are detriments in my eyes.
                        4. Similar to Sidereals, they animas are not obvious, appearing only as a caste mark and several lines flowing across their body in arcane patterns, all of which can be covered with heavy clothing

                        In the most likely case: 3 castes; Past, Present and Future:

                        Past are those who focus on learning from the past and protecting it’s legacy; Their caste mark is an inverted twilight mark. Note sure as to potential powers, but possibly when Burning the Temporal can regain one mote per success whenever they succeed on a roll they use their excellencies on (up to the amount they spent on the excellency). Strength, Charisma and Intelligence.

                        Present are the fast thinks and trouble shooters who live in the here and now. Their caste mark is a ring with a line running vertically down the middle splitting it in two. Again not sure of their anima effects but one possibility is they can spend Initiative instead of essence to power excellencies; others would focus on speed and reactions. Dexterity, Wits and Appearance

                        Future are plotters, planners and survivors. Their cast mark is either a small ring with seven lines spiralling out of it or a small ring with two outstretched lines going upwards. Potential effects are able to transfer extra successes on a roll they used an excellency up to a related follow up roll as a charm dice; also can seal vows like Solar Eclipse castes, although instead of cursing oath breakers they cast them several day/weeks/years/decades/centuries depending on their essence level. Stamina, Perception and Manipulation.

                        Other possible caste solutions are reasons for the mortal having regrets and making the bargain (Love/Prosperity/Revenge/Etc) or over various timescales (moments, days, seasons/ lifetimes/centuries).
                        Material Resonances Starmetal or Adamant. If they had their own material it would be some sort of some sort of glass made of sand from Temporal aligned manses or Outopia.

                        Temporal demesnes and manses are rare and tend to be in sandy places, in silent places or between clusters of other demesnes. Outopia is also dotted with many of them, which are often tied to points in Creation (and one or two in Yu Shan) that serve as anchors to hold that dimension in place.
                        Charm focuses Temporal Charms tend to allow them to achieve things faster and find solutions based on manipulation of the time flow; however they are not as tough as lunars, as glorious as solars or have the subtle strangeness of sidereal charms. Their charms are attribute based and have different themes beyond their initially application. Additionally, each attribute might be topped off with an Immortality charm that can be used to extend their lifespan, but often at some sort of cost; additionally each Temporal can only learn one such charm so their choice has to be good.

                        Strength: Support of the Past – focuses on reclaiming, repeating actions and utilising physical historic strength (or weakness). Charm ideas included attacking without wound penalties, creating a slip stream so allies can add your successes to their dice pool or throw ranged attacks back at their sender. Not sure what the Immortality charm is specifically, but might involve cutting off some of your flesh and putting it in a pot filled with preservatives; when you die of old age the flesh regrows into a new young body.

                        Intelligence: Secrets of the Past – Focuses on giving the user insights into the past and allowing them to know things that have been lost. Charm ideas included psychometry to read the history of an object, turning objects back to their base materials or tapping into past life memories. Not sure for what the Immortality charm is.

                        Charisma: Glory of the Past – Focuses on encourage others, nostalgia and tradition. Charm concepts include giving targets visions of the Primordial’s minds to either drive them insane or influence them (depending on their nature), making others see you as being under one of their intimacies if you would have counted as such in the past, or breaking armies with vision of your past deeds. The Immortality charm makes the user spread stories about themselves, as long as they live up to the legend, their life is extended.

                        Dexterity: Act in the Moment – Focuses on doing as much as possible in the here and now, often giving the user bursts of speed beyond the capabilities of others. Charm concepts include stopping gravity for a few seconds to walk on air, making a flurry of attacks without penalties and move rapidly across the land. The Immortality charm involves constantly moving place to place to literally avoid death and aging.

                        Wits: Study the Moment – Focuses on being aware of the here and now, taking in details faster than other and extending the user’s awareness. Charm concepts include swapping initiative values with a target at the start of combat, instantly picking up all details in a room and scrying nearby targets related to their quest.

                        Appearance: Focuses on fitting in the here and now, it’s is central to the espionage aspects of Temporals, as it allows them to fit into situations via minor shapeshifting, adopting cultural ticks and the like. Charm concepts include changing their apparent age, stopping a target’s time flow with their beauty or holding their image in place for a second so they get around of invisibility while their image is attacked instead. Immortality charm would be sucking the youth out of targets.

                        Stamina : Focuses on living til tomorrow; it’s about survival by any means possible. Charm concepts include jumping into the future to a safer point, committing essence to bank healing time to gain instantaneous healing in combat and manipulating the traits of pregnancies they’re involved with. Immortality charm would give them infinite life and damage no long does health levels, but ages them instead; additionally if I drop Immortality charms, then this is where most of the ways for Temporals to extend their life, as well as those of their allies.

                        Perception: Focuses on perceiving the tomorrow so the Temporal can plan accordingly. Sample charms include picking which path is the best for reaching their Quest, Creating a time loop to test the next essence rounds of potential actions, seeing the future potential in humans. Immortality charm would allow them to see the Destiny of others and slip into it, extending their life as long as they follow the path set down by destiny.

                        Manipulation: Focuses on planning tomorrow; was a key part of the Hourglass Conclave’s apparatus as it allowed them to set up safe houses, followers and supplies in advance and often under the noses of the Deliberative. Charm concepts include convince fallen ghosts/mortals to reincarnate with a positive intimacy to the Temporal when they are slain, training mortals to be sleeper agents and making binding families to debts until the Temporal claims them. Immortality charm would they do something for a person and if they skip on some or all of the payment they get several extra years of life.
                        Extra Powers So the big question is (backwards) time travel possible for Temporals? I imagine jumping to the future to be relatively easy, but whether backwards trips are possible is the question. I see five potentials in order of my preference:
                        1. Only during Exaltation, that is the only time both Aion and Chronos allow time travel in order to facilitate their Exalts, otherwise it’s impossible. Simple with no complications but little extra potential
                        2. Temporals are the key to activating time travel through sorcery workings, artefacts or other powerful effects. Basically any time travel is a campaign defining event and a always a serious affair.
                        3. Temporals would have a charm that allows them to set a sort of saved point of history that they can rewind to; they can only time travel to that point and the further they go from it the longer it will be to recover from the jump. They would be able to take some allies (or captives with them) and recall all that happened in the previous time line. I can see this as being not too hard to implement and give the Storyteller a warning in advanced to any potential time travel shenanigans. Cost would be paid after the jump in temporarily reduced essence levels and willpower.
                        4. Temporals get backwards trips based on completing Quests – the bigger and more complicate a Quest the more points it gives for time travel to be divided between how far you want to travel in time, how far you can physically travel with the jump, number of people you can take and safety precautions when you jump (not being spotted, landing in a safe location, having warning of social complications, etc). Offers a solid result for completing quests in the largest boon of this version.
                        5. Temporals can regularly and easily perform time travel through charms. The distance through, if the jump is temporary or permanent, what they can do, how many people they take, etc are all determined by charm and the amount of essence they pay. When a Temporal time travels in this system they can change the past but face something called ‘Continuum’, a side-effect of fate that tries to force the timeline to remain as close as possible to the Temporal’s timeline and is stronger the further back you go. So for example, if you fight a dragon blood in the evening and jump back in time to kill them before the fight, if you only jump back a day they wouldn’t be replaced and you would not have to fight them later that day, if you kill them a week earlier, a member of their Wyld Hunt would fight you instead, and if you kill them a year earlier and entirely different dragon blood would have replaced them. That being said it also allows the Temporal to do various things if they interact with the past but try to flow with Continuum – kill the Dragon blood so his incompetent cousin takes his place on the Wyld Hunt for example, or use it to investigate the past or set up things that don’t interfere with Continuum (like burying a pit trap under where the Dragon Blood will step). Offers the most freedom but also the most headaches for Storytellers with how much more freely they can toy with the past; however the Continuum does offer a constant pressure on the Temporals as history is drawn to remake their personal histories, creating plot hooks as the world shifts in ways they may not want as it gets closer to the original timeline they tried to flee from.

                        The other major ability is ‘Quests’; each Temporal is aligned to either Chronos or Aion, who assign various goals they want done for some grand scheme. Where either of them have long term plans or just one-ups manship is largely unknown, but they still desire Temporals to be their main agents in Creation; some Quests redefine whole kingdoms while others merely involve getting children to their granny’s house, their scale is variable and purviews wide as it seems the Titan of Time has many interests and sows their schemes in many fields. However many of their charms are keyed into the Quests and all Temporals have an innate sense of direction to where the main portion of their current Quest is. Example charm effects include a Strength charm that deal aggravated damage to enemies associated with a Quest, an Appearance charm that make integrating yourself into a social group easier if it’s directly related to a Quest and a Perception charm that allows you to find the quickest route to your Quest.

                        Whenever a Temporal completes a Quest they are offered a new one (possibly from a choice of many, I’m not sure on that front) in a vision from their particular patron. Fortunately Quests are paid for when completed (Autochthon wouldn’t let the Titan of Time produce Exalts without free will so instead of mindless slaves the Temporals were made to be mercenaries), so I see three potentials for this:
                        1. XP sources; Temporals gain extra XP from completing Quests like how other exalts get it from roleplay.
                        2. Each Quest has a number of dots based on difficulty; when it is completed these dots can be used as a resource to generate temporary merits or add extra dice to a roll, representing Chronos or Aion’s minor influence on the world to pay back their chosen.
                        3. Tied to option 4 of the Time Travel options, the Temporal gets dots to spend on Time Travel every time they complete a Quest; the bigger and more complex the quest, the more points they get.

                        Temporals can learn Celestial Sorcery, Ivory Circle Sorcery and gain Mastery in Martial Arts; however some Temporals are marked with a reversal of the Necromancy and Sorcery alignments if it is the will of Chronos or Aion when they are Exalted. They can also summon the 3rd and 2nd circle souls of the Titan of Time through sorcery like rituals if they have Occult of 5 or 3 respectively, with all the same restrictions of sorcerous demon summoning (except for having to know sorcery); the Deva cannot be bound by this summoning (as they were not bound by the surrender oaths of the Yozis and their demons) but are usually predisposed to helping a Temporal, even one severing the opposite fetch soul, as they sense a kinship to the Temporals and are often wily enough to turn events to their own personal goals.
                        Great Curse and Essence Fever All Temporals suffer the Great Curse, Chronos brought them into the Primordial War on the side of the Incarnae, so they are all doomed even if only half of them were on the winning side. Temporals experience the curse either as unbelievable regret or obsession with their Quests, and would be heavily tied to their castes. It builds up when the Temporal fails on tasks or loses something major based on their own decision.

                        Past Caste: Want to get revenge on enemies, Correct unfixable wrongs, repeat old Quests, cancel out the effects of prior Quests.

                        Present Caste: Indulge in momentary pleasures before they’re gone, run from damage, not only abandon but do as much as possible to avoid a Quest, rush into solving their current Quest as quickly as possible without thought or precaution.

                        Future Caste: Become insanely over protective of loved ones, try to cause disasters to be in ‘control’ (when you’re really not), manically obsessing over the grand plans of Chronos or Aion, attempting to perfect plan how to complete a Quest without interfering with your life.

                        Their Essence Fever is closely tied to their patron, with Aioni Temporals drawn to fight and test the other Exalted while Chronosi seek to undermine the Yozis and the old systems. They often don’t understand it in such grandiose goals, but an Aion aligned Temporal might seek to fight the Realm to strike at the Dragon blooded, while her Chronos aligned ally is seeking to liberate their slaves and commoners. However all Temporals are drawn to each other and any others who travel through time (such as summoned demons), but whether this is for good or ill depends on the Temporal in question.
                        Example Exalts Nellens Jukya; Aioni Past; a scholar who after a life time in sorcerous research realised his arranged marriage to another patrician could have worked out if he had invested time into it. Aion offered him to go back to his pre-civil war youth with a Quest to study and record all the various aspects of romantic love.

                        Sifu Willow, daughter of Sifu Tortoise; Chronosi Past; a daughter of a martial arts sifu in the west, she was denied any training due to tradition; she trained herself in secret but could never quite perfect the secret techniques she knew were trained behind closed doors. She accepted Chronos’ offer of Exaltation and used her years of training to force her way into being accepted into the dojo in exchange for except a Quest to fight a stop a potential student from leaving.

                        Cleric Augu Menta; Aioni Present; a former priest to an ancestor cult, she became disillusioned with the rising power of the dead in her home timeline; Aion sent her back so she could Crusade against the dead and let the living live their lives free from the weight of the fallen. Her current Quest is to kill a particular Abyssal exalt.

                        Jack the lad; Chronosi Present; a party loving nomad whose original life was one of endless duty to family and farm he ended up hating after 80 years. Officially on a quest to turn a Getimian to the Sidereal side, he prefers to indulge and live his life to the full.

                        Eled Windcatcher; Aioni Future; a skill navigator and merchant who careful plans her expeditions and trips across the Scavenger lands. Her Exaltation sent her back only a day as she regretted not taking one precaution that ended up costing her a minor business deal. When not worrying about every little detail and mercantile scheme she is a formidable Temporal completing many quests for Aion, even if they are mostly just smuggling various people (usually other Temporals) or goods to odd locations.

                        Marru Yopa; Chronosi Future; If you ask him, Marru Yopa is a skilled astrologer of the Varang city of Kesil and always has been. In reality he jumped back in his full body rather than take over his past self to correct the disastrously botched horoscope that doomed him to a life of military service rather than being a noted astrologer. When Chronos revealed him the truth, Marru took the Primordial’s offer by the horns and has used a combination of blackmail and appearance charms to find a place in Kesil’s social elite as an outsider astrologer of some noted skill – his manipulation charms are used for personal goals to take over the city and his Quest to set up cults to Chronos.
                        Comments These guys are the ones I have the most on but there are still some issues:
                        • I need a good sobriquet for all Temporals, I can’t keep calling them Temporals. Maybe ‘spies’ or ‘shadows’, but I don’t know.
                        • I need to iron the potential and mechanics for time travel and Quests.
                        • I need to pin down what kind of animas they have and if that effects their charm themes.
                        • I'm aware Chronos being over thrown by Zeus is inspiration for the Primordial War, but I based the assignment of Chronos and Aion based on their namesakes' roles - the Chronos as the God of the cycle of time (so change and the rise of new powers) and Aion as God of time eternal (so eternal systems and immortal beings.
                        • Not sure about the Immortality charms, I might drop the concept and instead give a branch of Stamina to life extension.
                        ​
                        As for Avatastics, yeah the Hedge good example of what a gauntlet is like - a messed up realm that has it's own logic and changes those who go in there.

                        For Industrials I've recently being thinking of doing them as people who have been given immense power to understand the world but it makes them slightly mad; Exaltation is done via the transfer of special charged nanites made by another Industrial - who started the process is unknown and more worrying is where the power comes as it seems to grow and develop as the Exalt learns more and more of the world. This mystery is central to Industrials and their identity, costing many of them their life as they seek for this answer. All they know of their history was a Solar was the first recorded person to use the nanites to Exalt someone after claiming she created them (which many consider to be pure nonsense), and from their they have risen and fallen several times, usually in colleges under a more powerful exalts purview, trying to not only preserve the knowledge of their forbearers but also to expand upon it.

                        The nanites are something all Industrial can innately produce, with the basic substance being what defines the new Exalts caste: Metal, Organics, Energy/Spirit/Essence, Psionic waves and Spacetime. When the Industrial produces the nanites they can either immediately inject them into a candidate or preserve them in some way for later use; either way they possess the ability to choose potential exalts and successors. When a person gets their nanites it pushes them during a 'zeroth' stage and if they can prove themselves after a while by completing the first stage of a Magnum Opus it will ignite and properly Exalt the host.

                        Main special powers are 'Magnum Opus' and 'Artiface'. A Magnum Opus is a singular project that defines an Industrial's life, like the doctoral thesis of a phd student, and is a quest to shape and define the limits of Creation in some way - one might focus on allowing them to eventually learn and master sidereal martial arts, while others might seek to develop an entirely new element; but such wonders are often obscure even to the greatest minds and are hard if not impossible to replicate (but that's what Exalted is about); advancing on the Magnum Opus is necessary for an Industrial to rise between Circles and become stronger - basically they have a singular moment of pushing/testing their theory until they can get more out of it; for example a Zeroth-First circle transformation is making samples of a new element; First-Second is making stable sample on a large enough quantity to be usable by others; Second-third circle is making elementals and manses of your new element; when the Industrial dies their Element is harder to produce and maintain and the secret will probably be lost in the years to come. Artiface is like sorcerous workings but more easily replicated, requiring a commitment of essence and some resources to recreate; these are usually specialist tools the Industrial relies on and defines their fighting style and approach to problem solving, but also seem like mad cap inventions to most sorcerers and scholars (basically, it's mad science inventions). Their anima is also super sentai armour and massively adaptable based on what the Industrial needs or wants. However they do have a major weakness; Industrials have to pick have abilities each time they go up a circle and they can no-long learn charms of that ability any longer and get refunded for charms in those abilities; this represents their minds specialising over time as they focus on greater degrees of knowledge.

                        Also still don't think the name is perfect but at least I have a reason now (it started as derogatory mockery that they where 'Industrially produced chosen' by a First Age Lunar. Although Experimentalists is a cool sobriquet for them, thanks for that Baaldam.
