        I've not actually made a character for the arena fights using social MAs, but based on what I know from say, Black Claw, it kind of really helps to lay some groundwork beforehand.

        Like, if you could get someone to have a Defining Positive intimacy for you ahead of time, and you are doing stealthy tricks to JB, then you could presumably Heart Ripping Claw them to Malfeas and back.

        But barring that, its a bit of a more defensive style (it doesn't have any withering boosters, for example) that helps if you have a bunch of people around to leap in and start roughing the other guy over for bruising the poor delicate flower that you are.

        White Veil has the same kind of thing, you really want to not have the other guy roll JB until the fight is over and your poison has worked its way through his system.

                                            For some inspiration / possibly a dire warning I tried to stat out a character whose combat skill was on the lower end of Exalted characters, based on your and JohnDoe244's remarks. I didn't go all the way down to defense 2, but I kept the combat abilities and physical attributes to at most 3. This character is part of Sail Team 6, my somewhat silly idea of having a circle of Solars on a boat together who are all expert sailors but all take different parts of the Sail charm tree. His job description is Sugar Daddy Captain and he's the one who owns the boat. He's Essence 1 and has only a couple charms that are relevant in combat.

                                            If you find this amusing, you could run a combat with him vs. whatever foe you think he could beat if he finds himself stranded alone and off his ship.

                                            Running though a sample session? Hells yes.

                                            Full disclosure, I'm giving him a Warfan and a Whip. Note these weapons have the same traits as Unarmed attacks, but different tags.

                                            Also, damn that's a weak character (combat wise). I'm going to assume you made this character step by step following the character creation guidelines and you read the Staying Alive sidebar and deliberately chose to make your character this way despite Session 0 expectations. That said, Sugar Daddy Captain is an expert Martial Artist who is excellent at Dodging, so let's have some fun.

                                            ****

                                            Guybrush Sugar Daddy Captain (SDC) stands astride the deck of the “Bay Max” (his blue water merchant ship). Wind whips about his face and the spray of the sea soaks the crew. Dark clouds gather on the horizon:

                                            SDC: “All hands! Position 6! There’s a storm a-brewing!”

                                            Winds lash the Bay Max, a large blue water ship full of rare cargo from the North, and SDC bellows at the helmsman to keep her running “Straight and true!”

                                            Controlling the ship and the crew in the midsts of a storm is the stuff of heroes, and SDC rolls (Wits+Sail+Stunt=11 dice) against Difficulty 1 to keep the vessel on track!

                                            SDC is pleased with the performance of his crew (5 successes) as he confidently steers them through the storm, anticipating every danger with wits like lightning. The crew set their backs to it, brining the ship on course and filling the sails with storm wind.

                                            Syker, the first mate, approaches SDC: “I don’t like this storm cap’n. It squalled up out of no-where!”

                                            SDC turns to reassure his mate, with a throaty laugh he tosses his hair back and bellows: “Aye! There’s something strange on these winds, but we’ll face it down together matey!” (Charisma+Presence+Looking Good+Stunt+Harmonious Presence Meditation-1 dice situational penalty from high wind-3 for multiple targets= 9 dice, 4 successes, SDC Inspires “confidence” in Syker and, indeed, the whole crew (average Resolve 1 as per Militia stats with Sail 2)).

                                            As if from no-where, the Bay Max runs on a reef! SDC surges Essence into the ship with Hull-Preserving Technique (13/11m, 4WP).

                                            Syker: “Where’d THAT come from!”

                                            There’s no time to answer, slinging round a rocky outcropping comes the red flags of the Blood Reavers! This scurvy gang of all female pirates are known to be ferocious man eaters who take no prisoners. Whilst the Bay Max should easily be able to outrun them with SDC at the helm, who knows what damage sailing over the reef may cause! But to stand and fight risks the whole crew being slaughtered by blood thirsty pirates. SDC makes his decision.

                                            SDC: “TO ARMS! Prepare to repel boarders!”

                                            ****

                                            As Followers, SDC’s sailors are normal men of average to competent sailing ability. They shall use the stats of Militia, with Sail 2 and bonus from SDC’s charms if relevant. Fifty of them puts them on the upper end of Size 2.
                                            The Blood Reavers will use the traits of Lintha Reavers (though, of course, they have no connection to the Lintha who are cursed rivals on the seas of creation). A scale will board the Bay Max, also Size 2.

                                            Red Mane, captain of the Blood Reavers, is an elite warrior and so will use the stats of the Champion with Sail 4 added.

                                            Calling on the Resolving Battles and Skip It sidebars, I’m content for the fight between the crew of the Bay Max and the Reavers to play out narratively: it’s going to mirror the fight between SDC and Red Mane. If either party uses a Command action, or specifically attacks the battle-group (they won’t), then I’ll roll them into combat and use them as full combatants.

                                            The ever increasing storm is imposing a -3 penality on all actions. Anyone fighting on a ship they are familiar with can reduce this by 1, as can anyone with a Sail score of 4 or more (keeping in mind that Sail 2 is the level of skilled professionals). Red Mane thus acts at -2 (-1 from the storm, -1 from Ship Claiming Stance) and SDC acts at -1.

                                            SDC rolls (Wits+Stunt-1) 5d for Initiative and comes in at 6i.
                                            Red Mane rolls 8d for 7i.

                                            Round 1

                                            7i: As the Reavers swing aboard the Bay Max, one woman, larger and fiercer than the others singles out SDC. She muscles her way up to SDC’s position and lunges at him with her cutlass (9d).

                                            SDC doesn’t want to get hit, red will ruin his outfit! With Excellent skill he dodges, tucking and rolling his body away from the blow (7/11m, 7 Evasion). Red Mane’s attack goes wide (5 successes vs Evasion 7).

                                            6i: The whip is an elegant weapon. SDC lashes out with Excellency and Elegant Weapon Repertoire (5/8m, 14d). Red Mane twirls her sword to parry, but the whip easily gets past her defences (7 successes vs Parry 5). The whip lashes at Red Mane (13 damage-Soak 10 = 2i), knocking her off-balance. (9i/5i)

                                            SDC: 9i (7/16m, 4WP)
                                            Red Mane: 5i

                                            Narrative: The crew of the Bay Max clash against the red haired pirates in a desperate battle as rain soaks them through.

                                            Round 2

                                            9i: Drawing on his bond with the ship, SDC lashes the boom above Red Mane with his whip and swings in to kick her with both feet (Ship Claiming Stance, Excellent Martial Arts, Elegant Weapon Repertoire, level 2 stunt, 7/12m, 4WP, 17d+2 successes). The blow connects (11 successes vs Parry 4, 7 threshold) crashing into Red Mane (18 damage – Soak 10 = 5i) and sending her sprawling (20i/0i).

                                            0i: Red Mane shakes her head to clear it and barrels into SDC, swinging wildly (9d) as SDC lashes his whip from side to side trying to stop her closing in as he strengthens his clothing with Essence (Pearlescent Filigree Defense, 7/11m). Red Mane ducks under his guard (4 successes vs Parry 3+1), and slashes against his clothing (12 damage-5 soak = 4i). (5i/16i)

                                            SDC: 16i (7/16m, 4WP)
                                            Red Mane: 5i

                                            Narrative: The crew of the Bay Max push the boarders back to the rails of the ship, but with their backs to the sea, the experienced Reavers hold their ground in a determined stand.

                                            Round 3

                                            16i: As Red Mane has stepped past his guard, SDC does the unexpected: instead of backing up, he steps closer still and tries to tackle her (6+6+2-1=13d, 1/16m). Rolling 7 successes vs Parry 5, SDC grapples Red Mane and rolls 16i vs Difficulty 2 to confirm (14i). Red Mane rolls 6 dice to oppose the grapple, and SDC uses a 4m Excellency+stunt to roll 12d, and establishes 3 rounds of control.

                                            Crashing into Red Mane, SDC hoists her up and throws her from the Bay Max, into the side of her own pirate ship for (14i+3 rounds of control= 6B). The badly bruised pirate captain (-4) crashes into the sea and the spray.

                                            SDC: 6/16m 4WP
                                            Red Mane: -4 wound penalty and overboard

                                            Narrative: Seeing their leader so thoroughly defeated, the Reavers turn tail and flee back to their ship before Sugar Daddy Captain can turn on them too. The crew of the Bay Max give a great cheer!

                                            ****

                                            Carefully, the captain sees the crew make their way off the reef without suffering further damage (Difficulty 2, 11d, 4 successes).

                                            Wounds are tended to and look-outs strain their eyes to look for any more red sails.

                                            Once more the Bay Max sets on course, and driving rain and howling wind give way to fog.

                                            Cautious of running into any more reefs, SDC calls to proceed slowly. Lamps are lit.

                                            A piercing scream shrieks out from the mists. SDC runs out on deck to investigate, heroically charging headlong into danger.

                                            “T-T-There! In the mists!” A sailor calls pointing a arm out.

                                            Syker calls out dismissively: “Don’t be so jumpy, there’s nothing there.”

                                            At that moment a giant shark appears, flying in the mists and bites the arm of the terrified sailor clean off!

                                            SDC grabs his war-fan and leaps into the air towards the shark!

                                            ****

                                            The Fog adds a 1d situational penality to SDC and the crew. The Fog Shark ignores this penalty.
                                            The Fog Shark does not take the Ship Claiming Stance penalty, as they are not on the ship: swimming instead through the mists.

                                            The crew won’t act unless ordered to do so by the captain.

                                            SDC: (5d+3) = 5i
                                            Fog Shark: (6d) = 6i

                                            Round 1

                                            6i: The Fog Shark tries to take a bite out of SDC. Biting with 7d vs Parry 3 (Pearlescent Filigree Defense, 6/15m). Fog Shark rolls 4 successes, hitting, but the attack bounces off the Solar’s Hardness – surprisingly SDC’s silk sleeves break the shark’s teeth. (3i/5i)

                                            5i: Elegantly and Excellently (6/6m, 1a), SDC twirls his war-fan and slashes the Fog Shark across the nose (17d vs Evasion 4, 9 successes). The shark’s skin is tough (16 damage vs Soak 8 = 4i), but the blow cuts through the delicate snout, enraging and confusing the beast as the mark of the Eclipse appears on SDC’s forehead. (15i/-3i)

                                            SDC: 15i (6/11m, 1a)
                                            Fog Shark: -3i

                                            Round 2

                                            15i: SDC lands on the deck after his lunge at the shark, dropping into a super-hero landing. Caste mark glowing, the expert martial artist twirls his war-fan with practiced grace and pushes up hard, striking the shark’s underbelly (0/11m, 1a; 13d vs Evasion 3). The blow hits and tears open the shark for 6L, putting the beast in its -2s. (3i/-3i)

                                            -3i: The shark twists and bites at SDC, as he tries to twist out of the way (5d vs Evasion 3). SDC almost avoids the shark’s killer jaws, but is still battered by the bulk of the beast (16 damage – Soak 5 = 5i). The blow knocks SDC off-balance, and dramatically shifts the flow of the battle with an Initiative Shift. (11i/-2i)

                                            SHIFT: The Fog Shark decisively bites down (5d) on the Solar, who spins his war-fan hoping to use it to Excellently wedge the beast’s jaws open (Parry 5, 0/14m). SDC easily catches the kill jaws and turns the blow aside. (8i/-2i)

                                            SDC: -2i (0/16m, 4WP, 1a)
                                            Fog Shark: 8i (6L, -2 wound penalty)

                                            Round 3

                                            8i: The Fog Shark tries to bites down again (5d), SDC braces himself against his war-fan, stopping the jaws from closing on him (Parry 3+1-1). (8i/-2i)

                                            -2i: Reasoning that the shark’s skin is too thick to damage from the outside, SDC uses his vantage point to slash against the inside of the shark’s mouth, climbing up and inside the beast for a better vantage point. Such an audacious ploy counts as a 3-point stunt, and SDC goes all in offensively (0/5m, 2a, 5WP; 13d+3 vs Evasion 2; 8 threshold successes; 8i) and tears and slashes the shark’s soft mouth.

                                            SDC gains 1i for hitting (-1i), and deals 8i in damage (7i). Fog Shark recovered from Crash last round, so no Initiative break bonus. For Crashing the foe who Crashed him, SDC trips Initiative Shift (12i).

                                            SHIFT: SDC gives one last, heroic push into the shark’s guts, disappearing entirely from view (6d+2-1 vs Evasion 1). The blow hits the soft parts of the beast, gutting it from the inside (12i, 4L).

                                            The dead fog shark flops to the deck of the Bay Max with a thump, and SDC pulls himself free of the beast to the triumphant cheers of his crew as burning Solar essence lights up the fog like a shining beacon of hope on an otherwise gloomy sea.

                                            SDC: “Someone draw me a bath!”

                                            SDC: 0/10m, 5WP, 2a

                                            ****

                                            SDC scrapes the shark guts from his body and luxuriates in a bath, fresh clothes waiting for him.

                                            The next hour passes blissfully without incident as he relaxes (4/16m).

                                            But the peace is not to last, Syker barrels in on the captain and insist he come to the deck immeidately. SDC doesn’t have time to dress, but he grabs a bath robe to cover his modesty and heads up on deck.

                                            When they were attacked by the Reavers, a stow-away snuck aboard the Bay Max: this blonde haired woman was clearly a captive of the pirates, and is no threat to the crew.

                                            But a blonde haired woman is bad luck.

                                            If SDC had found her first, he could easily have swayed the crew into accepting her as a valued human being, but SDC didn’t find her first. A Storm Mother did.

                                            The hag-goddess has stopped the ship and is demanding the sailors drown the woman to appease her.

                                            SDC, an Eclipse well used to dealing with gods, strides out on deck wearing only his robe, tied at the waist with his whip, to bargain for the woman’s life.

                                            Storm Mother: THROW THE HARLOT INTO THE SEA IN SACRIFICE AND I SHALL PUT MY BLESSING ON YOUR SHIP.

                                            SDC reads the Storm Mother’s intentions, rolling 6 dice against Guile 2. The Storm Mother seems to be sincere as she’ll gain more power from the sacrifice than she’d spend on calming the weather.

                                            Not looking like a million bucks, SDC is going to take a penalty on his rolls of -1 (this might not be a formal situation, but it’s rarely a good idea to negotiate in a robe).

                                            SDC: “Wise and mighty Storm Mother. Well a being of your power,” [he guesses, knowing nothing about Storm Mothers and having no training in Occult matters] “must know that it is ill-luck to cast good fortune into the sea. We mean you no disrespect, of course, and we offer you our humble prayers in return for your favor. We are both beings of the sea, you and I, surely we can pass in mutual respect.”

                                            SDC rolls Manipulation+Presence+3 Harmonious+2 stunt-1=12d robe against the Storm Mother’s Resolve of 4 as a Bribe. The Storm Mother has a Major Intimacy against Beautiful Women, which raises her Resolve to 7, but also a Defining Intimacy towards being worshipped, which brings her back to 4. SDC rolls 4 successes, and the Storm Mother agrees to provide safe passage, calm seas and fair winds, in exchange for prays from the crew of the Bay Max.

                                            Seizing his chance, SDC sanctifies the pact with his Anima (4/6m, 4WP, 2a), blazing brightly with the light of the Sun.

                                            Outraged by being tricked by one of the Sun’s Chosen, the Storm Mother agrees she’ll honor their pact: the Bay Max will sail safely.

                                            With a swish of her arm, a great wave crashes over the ship. Miraculously the water bends around the crew, not touching them with so much as a drop of water, but SDC is swept out to sea!

                                            ****

                                            SDC washes up on a beach. He’s reassured that the Storm Mother is bound by her oath not to harm the ship and the crew, and clearly the oath was strong enough to see that he was merely marooned instead of drowned. He doesn’t know how long he has been at sea, but he feels strangely rested and his anima is dim (4/16m).

                                            SDC looks around to get his bearings. He sees some ruined ships: not having Craft, he cannot technically Craft a new boat. But as I’m a nice ST, I let him make a Sail roll to cobble together a delicate raft.

                                            The body of a lion, the head of a man, and the wicked barbed tail of a scorpion. A being of myth and legend. The dreaded Manticore!

                                            Sugar Daddy Captain must roll 10 successes on a Strength + Athletics roll to push his raft off the beach and into the sea. Each round, he gains one automatic success as the tide comes in. The manticore is sure to wreck the delicate raft is SDC runs away, so the Captain can’t simply run away for 10 rounds. The manticore starts at long range.

                                            SDC: 7i (4/16m, 4WP)
                                            Manticore: 8i, Long Range

                                            Round 1

                                            8i: The Manticore closes to Medium range.

                                            7i: SDC grabs his raft and starts pushing it to sea, bracing the raft at all the weak points and pushing with his legs (4d, 1 success = 9 remaining).

                                            Manticore: 8i, Medium Range
                                            SDC: 7i (9/16m, 4WP)
                                            Raft: 8 rounds remaining

                                            8i: The Manticore closes to Short range and Rushes. Both parties roll 8 dice, and SDC is Rushed.

                                            SDC: 7i (9/21m, 4WP)

                                            8i: The Manticore closes to Close range and swipes at SDC with his claws (13d). SDC ducks and weaves, spinning around the grasping beast’s deadly blows (9/14m, 1a, Evasion 7, Pearlescent Filigree Defense). The blow connects (7 successes) and SDC shunts Essence into his clothing (9/10m; Soak 9 vs Damage 12). The blow knocks the wind from SDC, but he avoids getting cut in half. (12i/4i)

                                            4i: With a flourish, SDC cracks his whip like a Dynastic lion tamer, striking out at the Manticore with everything he’s got as he blazes with Solar Essence (10 Accuracy+6 Excellency+2 Stunt+Willpower = 18d+1; 9/1m, 2a). The Manticore dodges, more deftly than even a graceful great cat could move, but the blow still connects (16 Damage vs 9 Soak). (8i/9i)

                                            Manticore: 9i
                                            SDC: 8i (9/6m, 3WP)
                                            Raft: 4 rounds remaining

                                            Round 4

                                            9i: The Manticore’s tail lashes out with its deadly sting (8d). SDC, lashes out with his whip taking careful aim at the arc of the Manticore’s attack (Parry 3+Stunt+2m Excellency; 9/4m). The whip deflects the blow.

                                            8i: SDC launches forward with another all out attack (4/0m, 2WP), hitting again for 4i. (13i/5i)

                                            SDC: 13i (4/5m, 2WP)
                                            Manticore: 5i
                                            Raft: 3 rounds remaining

                                            Round 5

                                            13i: Not knowing if he’ll get another opening, SDC launches a Decisive attack against the dread beast, striking for the eyes with a whip crack. His anima flares totemic, shining it’s nautical message out for miles around (4/0m, 1WP; 10d+1 vs Evasion 4). The blow hits, for a spectacular 10B on 13 dice, knocking the beast into its -2s. (3i/5i)

                                            5i: With an inhuman roar of rage, the Manticore bears down on SDC, throwing Willpower behind a Grapple attempt (7d+1). SDC spends the last of his motes to loosen his body, bending elegantly away from the blow (Evasion 3+Stunt+2; 0/0m). The Manticore’s blow goes wide. (3i/3i)

                                            SDC: 3i (0/5m, 1WP, 2a)
                                            Manticore: 3i (4WP, 10B (-2))
                                            Raft: 2 rounds remaining

                                            Clash: Man and Manticore Clash together in a desperate struggle of fist and fang. The beast claws as SDC. Sugar Daddy Captain draws on his years of experience, and Sun given skill, ducking into the swirling pattern of his totemic anima, co-ordinating his blows with the shifting pattern of light. The Manticore claws for him (3WP; 11d+1 = 9 successes) and he puts everything he has behind his attack (10 Accuracy+5m Excellency+2-point Stunt+Willpower=17d+2; 10 successes).
