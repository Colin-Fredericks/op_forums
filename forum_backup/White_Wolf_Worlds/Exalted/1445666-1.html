                        We've got a noncombat Air Aspect who just takes the five seconds it takes to enter the right Aura to do her thing outside of combat.

                        And we got the Earth Aspect Immaculate who gets in Earth Aura and Bonfire Anima in turn one, then just wrecking-balls the rest of the combat with Earth Dragon Charms.

                        And yes, that is limiting. A Water Aspect Swordfighter pretty much has to be a Single Point Stylist because Water Melee is all but unusable. Good luck being an Archer if you're a Fire Aspect. But if you're going out of your way to do something whacky in combat, then be prepared to homebrew -- you can't expect a cookie cutter solution to work if you intentionally break the mold.
