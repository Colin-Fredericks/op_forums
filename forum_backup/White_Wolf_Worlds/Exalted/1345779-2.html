                                On the seduction example given, I think that if your player approaches and tries to seduce a sexually non-compatible NPC and gets 23 successes, sure they aren't going to seduce that person, but as a GM I'd certainly rule that a similar non-sexual intimacy might happen instead, with the players consent.

                                I mean, think about it in narrative terms. You are approached by the smoothest and most delightful person of all time who flatters you and makes you feel special. Sure you don't want to do the nasty with them, but you'd feel something and it wouldn't be anger (unless you have some kind of intimacy that would translate that into anger). I mean I feel great if a gay guy compliments me, even though there is 0% chance of him getting lucky. And thats some average guy in a club/bar. 23 successes represents a level of smoothness, cool and sheer seductive awesomeness that is literally godlike. A botch might result in anger, but lots of successes? I'd GM that into something like
                                "You make an offhand compliment about his fetching outfit and after a short and enjoyable chat you feel that he isn't responding to your advances at all, however, given you've rolled really well, you manage to turn it into a friendly chat about his tailor and how good the outfit makes him look. You deftly extract yourself from the conversation some minutes later. Since you didn't manage to instill a lust intimacy, but you made a really good impression, would you like to instill a minor intimacy of well disposed to <your character> instead?"

                                On the cultural intimacies, I can see the wisdom of breaking the cultures down into a set of specific intimacies, not least because you won't need to remember/have separate lists of what each cultural intimacy means. I think it should probably be more of a buffet approach though.
                                Sticking with the example of Lookshy, maybe
                                Respect for the military
                                Respect for public service
                                Disdain for self centred advancement
                                Protectiveness to the scavenger lands with Lookshy as a benevolent guardian
                                Disdain for the Realm as a hostile aggressor

                                and then let the PCs choose what strength they have for each and whether there are any they think are inappropriate for their character. So a merchant of lookshy might be fine with self centred personal advancement (while understanding its somewhat frowned upon) and not have it as a intimacy, but might be very keen on protection of the scavenger lands and hence support for the military, mayeb because they believe that trade suffers when an area is too unsafe to travel.
