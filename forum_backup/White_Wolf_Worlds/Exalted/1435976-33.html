                                    Originally posted by Cleverest of Things View Post
                                    What portfolios of divinity have regional variants, like the directional war gods? Is it a Bureau of Destiny thing? (Ie there is a Western God of Journeys who is a sailor, a Southern one who is a caravan-master, etc)? Is War unique in this regard? Does it get even more specific (ie Camulos, War God of Albion; Tyr, War God of the Rajtali)?
                                    It's not unique (there have been references to the Peace gods also having such a division, and I think a few others), but still seems to be rather narrow.

                                    As far as specificity goes, this is one of those areas where it helps to remember that divine portfolios aren't hard coded into reality, they're designated by the Celestial Bureaucracy. There's a combination of politics, organisational pressures and necessities, and graft inherent to whether or not a lot of offices exist, especially at lower levels of resolution. Whether or not a specific minor state gets a dedicated war god can be a matter of whether or not there is enough war there to require a dedicated god to oversee the destiny of it, some manoeuvring within departments looking to extend their authority or curtail opponents, and if another god with some pull has a kid who could use a job.

                                    Also probably some occasions where a lower ranked god genuinely does the sort of job that warrants a promotion, and with no openings they just create a new one wholesale.

                                    If one wants to add a bit more satirical edge to Yu-Shan, they could even have occasions where a canny god basically invents an office for themselves and manages to get the paperwork around the subject funnelled through them to exercise a bit of authority and sign memos that seem to possess some insight, and rely on the general ponderousness and impenetrability of the system to keep them in place. It's a risky move, but there are so few censors that one could plausibly slip through the cracks for a good long while (and be on the waiting list for ages even if brought up on charges), and the very clever ones can take that time to tie so many things through them as to be indispensable.

                                    All of that said, I think there's supposed to be a certain inclination in the original decision makers of Heaven that War in particular was the sort of thing with particular delineations in type that corresponded well to Directions, in combination with the intensity of the subject matter.

                                    I have approximate knowledge of many things.
