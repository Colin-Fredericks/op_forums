    5-dot would have to be huge. Not necessarily ultimate power, but a very broad and visible power. It should not be confused with something a mortal could do, it should be obviously powerful, even if it's not flashy. The important thing when designing an artifact is to keep the goal in mind. You don't want to branch out into multiple themes of a concept, it should fit a fairly narrow path through the concept. You wouldn't make an artifact bow that just fires all arrows better, you make a bow that fires arrows better in a specific situation, or that fires emotions instead of physical ammunition, or shoots the user to the target instead of an arrow.

    In the guitar's case, it shouldn't make all Performance better, it should make one representation of Performance better. So while this thing starts with just "perform better" the last evocation is about letting someone run away because of the music. It turns to distraction. If you keep that evocation, the next one should follow that theme, otherwise it's just a stat stick. So if the highest current power lets you run away, then the next power should build on that and grant you something when you use it to run away, or something like that. Charm-sets are designed to be your broad purpose powers. Artifacts are designed for specialization.

                            Shirt of Wicked Brass (Orichalcum Chain Shirt - Artifact ***)

                            Forged in Hell by Ligier's green fire, the Shirt of Wicked Brass was a kingly gift for a Solar Exalt. Tainted in vitriol, the orichalcum links of the shirt have taken on a brass-like tinge, giving the shirt its name.

                            Evocations of the Shirt of Wicked Brass

                            A Solar or Infernal Exalt who attunes to the Shirt of Wicked Brass gains a non-Charm bonus die to all Resistance rolls against poison or disease.

                            Flash of Hellfire
                            Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Simple
                            Keywords: None
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: None

                            When the light of Creation's Sun reflects off the brazen links of the Shirt of Wicked Brass, it casts a green hue. A skilled wearer can reflect this baleful light into the eyes of his opponent, blinding her. This Evocation is a difficulty 2 gambit made using the wearer's (Charisma + Performance). If successful, the target is blinded for the remainder of the scene, taking a -3 penalty to all actions. The target can remove this penalty by rubbing the green pain from her eyes, a Miscellaneous action which cannot be placed in a flurry.


                            Retribution of the Damned
                            Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Counterattack, Decisive-only
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: Flash of Hellfire

                            Those who would strike the Shirt of Wicked Brass would feel the flames of Hell. The wielder may use this Evocation in response to any attack that beats his Defense, creating a special decisive counterattack that rolls (Charisma + Performance) against the target's Defense. This attack otherwise functions as a regular decisive attack. Retribution of the Damned does not count as the wearer's combat action and cannot be used in response to another counterattack.

                            Green Sun Wasting
                            Cost: None (+1W); Mins: Essence 2
                            Type: Permanent
                            Keywords: None
                            Duration: Permanent
                            Prerequisites: Retribution of the Damned

                            Malfeas' ire is toxic; so, too, is the fire of Ligier's forge. This Evocation permanently enhances its prerequisite, allowing a successful counterattack to inflict a variant of Green Sun Wasting disease on the target for the cost of one temporary Willpower. This disease has Virulence 3, Morbidity of the wearer's Essence, and an Interval of one day. This disease can be fatal to mortals, and stories tell of even Exalted who have succumbed to it, though these are surely apocryphal...

                                                    Claws of Brass and Fire (Orichalcum Razor Claws - Artifact ***)

                                                    Evocations of the Claws of Brass and Fire

                                                    A Solar or Infernal Exalt who attunes to the Claws of Brass and Fire for one additional mote can re-roll one failed die on any attack roll made with the claws.

                                                    Claws Strike True
                                                    Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Uniform
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    The Claws of Brass and Fire hunger for their enemies' flesh, and strike true to taste it. This Evocation allows the wielder to re-roll up to (Essence) 1s on an attack roll.


                                                    Green Fire Claws
                                                    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keywords: Decisive-only
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Claws Strike True

                                                    The Claws of Brass and Fire live up to their name, bursting into flame at the moment of impact. Flammable materials catch fire when struck by this Evocation. A decisive attack adds one automatic success to its damage roll. If the attack deals at least three levels of damage, the target catches fire and must contend with (wielder's Essence) lethal dice of damage every turn until he's able to extinguish himself. Fire from this Evocation tends to leave wicked scars on any surface it touches, often burning words and Malfeas phrases in Old Realm into flesh, wood, or stone.

                                                    Lash of Hellfire
                                                    Cost: 6m,3i; Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keywords: None
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Green Fire Claws

                                                    The flame of the claws can be hurled a short distance by a skilled wielder, lobbing a glob of waxy green fire at a target. An attack supplemented by this Evocation can be made at a target at up to Short range.
