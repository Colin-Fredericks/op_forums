    Armour Evocations: Feedback Requested

    07-04-2020, 12:15 AM
    Forged during the Shogunate, Ebony Fronds was first forged from steel and green jade, heated over ancient willow charcoal, then tempered with vegetable oil and soap and the sap of certain Eastern thistles for the Eastern Outcaste Busujima Clan’s eldest son, Shutaro, who wore it in many battles and in time of peace, set it where rain and sun could reach it. In due course, it passed into the hands of other sons, and less often daughters, of the Clan. As with their other great treasures, the Clan took great pride in knowing that they had never had to ransom it back from their enemies. After the clan’s destruction, it passed into the hands of Saeko Takagi, their former retainer and recently Exalted Dawn Caste.

    Created for a man noted for his fastidious nature, the armour keeps itself and its attuned wearer clean, as per the Collar of Dawn’s Cleansing Light. Given the great difficulty of keeping lamellar armour clean and pest-free with its intricate lacings, this is a considerable benefit. It also incorporates a hearthstone socket, which currently holds the keep’s Monkey Stone.

    Evocations of Ebony Fronds

    Leaf-Whisper Imitation
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Dissonant, Resonant
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: None

    Ebony Fronds’ overlapping scales become as silent and soft as leaves. This Evocation enhances a Stealth roll made while in plant-based concealment, lowering any penalties by the wearer’s (Essence).

    Special activation rules: As long as the wearer isn’t dissonant with jade, this Evocation awakens at no cost when she successfully ambushes a non-trivial enemy.

    Resonant: As long as the wearer’s roll beats at least one non-trivial enemy, she gains a point of Initiative.

    Unseen Forest Spirit
    Cost: —; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisites: Invisible Statue Spirit, Leaf-Whisper Imitation

    This Evocation upgrades Invisible Statue Spirit (Exalted, p. 403). While the Solar is in plant-based concealment, the Charm’s cost is waived.

    Trackless Jungle
    Cost: —; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisites: Elusive Forest Shadow, Traceless Passage

    This Evocation upgrades Traceless Passage (Exalted, p. 412). When the Solar uses it to conceal her tracks while moving through a forest, jungle, or other vegetation-dense terrain, she doubles 7s.

    Lightness of the Birch-Tree
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisites: Armoured Scout’s Invigoration, Leaf-Whisper Imitation

    When activated, this Evocation removes Ebony Fronds’ mobility penalty.

    Toughness of the Cedar
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisites: Lightness of the Birch-Tree

    Given a moment’s preparation, the wearer of the armour can cause its scales to grow a bark-like layer of additional protection, increasing Ebony Fronds’ Soak by 2.

    Ghost Who Walks
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 5
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Resonant
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisites: Trackless Jungle, Toughness of the Cedar

    When the character reaches Incapacitated, this Charm both stabilizes the wearer and creates the illusion that the wearer is indeed dead. The illusion remains for as long as the armour is attuned and the Evocation’s motes committed, and can be penetrated with a Difficulty 5 Perception+Awareness roll.

    Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens at no cost the first time the wearer is reduced to Incapacitated.

      Leaf-Whisper Imitation: 2m

      Trackless Jungle: Doubling 7s is a bit overkill. You might as well not exist as I don't think anyone could oppose that realistically. Make it a reroll to cover tracks instead, charms reactivated for free on second roll, and you keep the better of two results. This makes it a bit easier to recover from a bad roll but is not an autowin charm.

      Lightness of the Birch-Tree: Boring and pretty much pointless. Is 2m really worth the one point of mobility penalty? In most cases, no.

      Toughness of the Cedar: Boring. I'd give some side benefit, like letting the wielder subsist off sunlight in addition to the soak.

      Ghost Who Walks: This is Essence 3 at most. Stablization isn't that big of a deal. If you want to make it spicy, make it Essence 4, Duration instant, and the ability to let the Exalt live after a blow that would've plainly killed him.

              Double 9s would work, but you need to remember that doublers can get out of hand very quickly. I don't know what artifact level this is, but by guessing on the effects I'd say a 3. Double 7s is way too strong, but a double 9 or a reroller is easily within that power.

              Armoured Scout's Invigoration is terrible. It's overly expensive and those delving into armour really won't care about it's penalties. The only real point of significance is the -1 evasion. If you want it to be better, make it a 0m permanent effect. Reduce the mobility penalty of the armour to 0 and reduce any penalties related to mobility/Stealth through dense underbrush by 2 (Or 1 from defenses). This makes it more useful and stands out in a niche way that suits using it for it's designed environment.

              Simple charms are often basic effects early on or effects that while fairly straightforward are extremely powerful. This doesn't apply here. Boring in this case is when you know you can do better, especially for an Essence 3 charm, where effects generally leave the simple territory. Subsisting off sunlight is a basic niche but welcome effect. You can substitute anything else you want, I was trying to give it more spice. Make a charm that's "Wow that's really cool, I'm going to take that" vs "More soak is always nice I guess."

              Hitting incap in 3e doesn't mean you automatically die. What I would be proposing is a counter to effects that go "Kill this person upon death". Such as Soul Mastery, that one Solar charm in Miracles, or ST fiat by saying getting hit with a mountain destroying vaporizer cannon would also vaporize your character. Again, a bit niche. Such a power won't stop a dedicated foe from crub stomping you when you're down, but it would help in a major fight where you allies could drag you off.
