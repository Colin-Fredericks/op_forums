    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

    We have decent examples of beastfolk that have nothing to do with Lunars,
    An entire nation of two different types unified into a single society.

    but we also have enough examples of Lunars that have something to do with beastfolk, so between that and developer statements, I think that ship has sailed.



    I believe that the case, in character, for such an association will come in two parts.

    One will Lunars having native powers to bestow mutations upon people, which will apparently tend to involve giving them beast traits. Makes enough sense to me, Lunar magic in general having a bestial quality.

    The other is coming at the question of how and why they build armies from a different direction; they're not the establishment, they're coming in to lead people against the establishment. Lunars don't have an institutionalized process for bestowing them with armies, but they do have a playback of working their way into the heart of marginalized societies and radicalizing them against the Realm. In that space, the capacity to grant them a bunch of physical and supernatural Merits likely serves the need to turn people into fighters (possibly of a tricky kind that the Realm has difficulty handling) fairly quickly. It can also put that Lunar's signature on them, helping to build their terror brand.
    Which still leaves them with something less impressive than a WoD power.

    So is shapeshifting.
    Presumably something that looks more like in Earthsea, Ladyhawke or with the siblings of the sorcerer from the Conan cartoon than the free-wheeling, unrestricted Lunar variety, at least when it comes to animal-based shapeshifting. No forgetting your true nature or being restricted by time, here.

    More than that, Vance has said outright that Lunars will possess a number of powers that other people would need to be sorcerers to do.
    Technically, that's also been a part of the really weird Sidereal bag, hasn't it?

    It's right there in the Realm's chapter on the Caul, regarding the typical kinds of armies of His Divine Lunar Presence.



    In the First Edition? Should there have ever been any connection between the fairly straightforward brutal tribal people with animal features, and the fairly straightforward brutal tribal Exalted whose signature trick was turning into a scary person with animal features? I think by the conceits of the time, sure, there should have been a connection.

    The milieu of beastfolk has become more varied and sophisticated, but then, so has that of Lunars; they're not all running the playbook of creating gauntlets to turn petitioners into beastfolk.
    The milieu of beastfolk changed before the first Lunar book was out. CB: Night introduced snakefolk as being fairly well-established in most major urban locales across Creation, and having homes otherwise mostly in southeastern jungles. They're noted for being distrusted for their appearance rather than for any Lunar connections. Moreover, grouping together beings with animal features to the Lunars solely for that reason is a herring so red that in the Realm, only the Scarlet Empress is legally permitted to dine upon it or authorize its capture and culinary preparation. The Lunars number only about 400; that's a drop in the bucket compared to everything else with magical powers and an animal-based appearance. The Lunars shouldn't be any more strongly associated with beastfolk than they are with defending Creation from the Wyld.

                Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist

                I think that the most important difference in that scenario is the Lunar herself. The values that the Lunar demands in prospective beastfolk, the local cultural and political context of a Lunar going around offering the transformation and people wanting it, and the legacy imprinted in their society by the first generation of beastfolk having come from that association.

                Lunars will have Charms of their own to bestow such a thing, so they'll be able to do it on their own terms, rather than the terms of an associated spirit, or traits conferred by initiation into sorcery. It's a thing they will get to do, and can be presumed to do with reasonable frequency. It presumably differs from sorcerers or random powerful spirits by the context in which it is employed; there are probably not zero gods of the right kind who are in opposition to the Realm, but it's probably a less consistent thing.

                Sure there's a question of why the Lunars still need to confer blessings in the form of animal traits, to which I say... I don't know. Go ask the developers, why don't you?
