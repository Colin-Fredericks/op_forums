                                                            Originally posted by While My Guitar View Post
                                                            I have another question that isn't about killer rabbits and movie-related childhood trauma's.
                                                            Oh, but isn't everything really about that, when you drill down far enough, possibly in imaginary directions?

                                                            Recently, during a game of Dota, I realized that the character of Broodmother (basically, giant spider, can conjure up babby-spiders, monstrous in one-on-one combat.) Would be a very decent Lunar character.
                                                            Now, her abilties are very spider-y, she spins webs, spawns swarms of spiderlings at a time and drinks her enemies vital fluids to restore her own health. Would these abilities by limited to just her spider-form, or can she use these in her human form as well?
                                                            I could see a case for either. I'm certainly in favor of most such abilities being more effective in her monstrous spider-form, regardless of whether her human form can pull them off. I'm at this point guessing, if they appear nowhere else, that somewhat personalized Charm trees may be able to build off of DBT or the like, for particular use in that form.
                                                            Would these sorts of very specific abilities be Essence 5+ material? Your input is appreciated.
                                                            I think I know what you're referring to with the mention of Essence 5+, and I really don't think that's what the devs meant by it. Unless it's just the sort of power they want in the game for an Exalt and also don't want to be accessible to normal PCs (they mentioned Leviathan's whale-zilla bit as all they could think up) or that it represents a personal mastery in a somewhat specific way that's perhaps beyond what the splat is really about (which, per Holden's example involving a young Lunar learning an elder Lunar's technique that I still really really really wish hadn't been lost on the old forums, would also be something a non-E5+ character could learn at a lower Essence rating, even if it's not as simple as "pay xp, get Charm), I don't think it's meant to fall in the E5+ range. Admittedly, we also kind of don't know a lot about that, so I'm not sure how much good speculation does at this point. >.> I should also probably try to sound less authoritative on the topic, but it's the sense I got from it.

                                                            In any case, those sorts of powers feel like the sorts of things that Lunars -- being meant to draw off monsters and beasts -- should have solidly in their wheelhouse. I would find it very confusing, surprising, and disappointing if Lunars couldn't break out supernatural powers resonant with their totems until their Essence went beyond the assumed E1-5 play experience. That would seem directly counter to what the devs are trying to do.
