                                Ah, beg your pardon, misunderstood. Yes, that is the case. So as far as I can figure (and based on questions asked to the devs):

                                1. Animal Form: Dex (or Str depending on Charms) + Brawl (its always brawl for natural weapons) + Accuracy (if your total is higher than the animals, this is +1, otherwise +0)

                                2. Last Warrior's Unity in Animal Form: Dex (or Str depending on Charms) + Brawl/Melee/Martial Arts (whichever fits your weapon) + Weapon accuracy bonus (varies), Tiny or Miniscule Creatures have the withering damage of the weapon limited by their base Str.

                                3. Wood Dragon Claws in Animal Form: Dex (Cannot be Str as it is a light Artifact Weapon) + Brawl/Martial Arts (Razor claws don't use Melee) + Weapon Accuracy Bonus (+5), No limit on withering damage, requires sorcery

                                If you can swing it, I would argue 3 is preferable.

                                Tiny creatures normally don't have any inherent limitations on their damage (just that most of their forms don't do great damage to begin with), but Miniscule Creatures have this limitation:

                                Their decisive attacks don’t damage larger characters.
                                So, I think 3 is probably superior, if you can swing it, for tiny critters (miniscule critters just don't fight in melee, just don't, man).
