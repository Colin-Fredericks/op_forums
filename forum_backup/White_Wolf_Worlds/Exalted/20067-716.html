        Originally posted by Epitome View Post

        I'm confused, Dancing Ember Stride is a Simple Charm and Terrestrial WRR specifies that it counts as your attack for the round, so why can you use it after using a Simple Charm?
        On your turn you get one Combat Action.
        You may take a Reflexive Move action reflexively, or you may spend your Combat Action on a different Move Action.
        You may not take two Move Actions (including the Reflexive Move).
        If you wish to take more than one Combat Action then you must Flurry.
        You may not use the same Action more than once per round - no Flurrying Full Defence twice (using a shield).
        Attacks use the Attack Action -- even when those Attacks are granted by a Charm, so no using a Reflexive Charm to Attack then using your Combat Action to Attack again.
        Usually you can't Attack without using your Combat Action, but some Charms create Reflexive Attacks.

        But specific rules trump general: if a Charm states it lets you attack twice, or attack without using your Attack action, then it does what it says it does.

        Whirling Rhythm Revolution lets you make a Reflexive Attack. It does not count as using your Attack Action for the round (so you might be able to Attack twice). With the Terrestrial Keyword it DOES count as your Attack for the round (so in order to Attack twice, you'll need to find a Charm that doesn't use your Attack).
