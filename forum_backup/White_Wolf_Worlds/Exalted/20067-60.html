    Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
    If a human is born with a wyld mutation, say webbed hands, and they exalt as a Lunar, will they retain their webbed hands as that's considered their natural form? Or, will it they get rid of it as though it were a shaping effect?
    If its inborn, then its intrinsic and not a shaping effect. It's not like a Djala can have their shortness punched out of them by a Solar.
    Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
    What if the human isn't born with the mutation, instead acquiring it in a bordermarch previous to exalting?
    Normal mutation rules apply to mutations acquired by exposure to the Wyld, or some other shaping effect, prior to exaltation (or indeed, after). They aren't Wyld Taint mutations, so they're removable and they don't necessarily carry over to other true shapes.

    PS: Drawing the Second Breath itself doesn't remove mutations; acquired or otherwise. The only reference I can find to that is in Scroll of Heroes referring specifically to the exaltation of Half-caste children, which is basically a rule of "no-double dipping".

    PPS: Moonsilver Tattooing makes the Lunar immune to shaping effects, it doesn't remove them. Further, as effects that remove a shaping effect are often themselves shaping effects...

            Originally posted by Frostav View Post
            Is there a limit to how many spells a Sorcerer may know? Does it depend on their Occult or Essence or something else? I checked the White Treatise and it doesn't mention it (indeed it doesn't seem to mention how sorcerers learn spells at all).

            Nope, no limit at all, beyond needing the Initiation Charms. Once one has learned one, she can load up on all the spells of that Circle, with no limitation!

            As to HOW they learn spells...they just read the books, which describe the words and gestures needed to shape Essence to have the desired effect. Then they have to practice doing so, which is where the training time comes in. It's mentioned that accidents, from forgotten/mispronounced words or slightly wrong gestures, can cause the spell to explode (hence the original Twilight caste anima power), so lots of practice until one has a spell down pat is highly recommended!

                        Originally posted by Frostav View Post
                        Is there a limit to how many spells a Sorcerer may know? Does it depend on their Occult or Essence or something else? I checked the White Treatise and it doesn't mention it (indeed it doesn't seem to mention how sorcerers learn spells at all).
                        No upper limit other than XP you're willing to invest in it

                        As for learning, there's two methods - learning from a source like a tutor or from a library (both are pretty rare) or from the Salinian Working. The SW allows a sorcerer to reconstruct spells for sorcery by studying Creation

                        • Maybe they learn Death of Obsidian Butterflies by becoming a Lepidopterist who fills a room in his manse with rare butterflies from the east to boards with hair thin needles of obsidian with golden runes
                        • They might master Invulnerable Skin of Bronze by becoming an alchemist and distilling the raw essence of strength and valor from the bronze armor of twelve different heroes who died in bed into a potion that they drink.
                        • You could learn Cantata of Empty Voices by harvesting the last words of everyone in a choir
                        • You can decode the secrets of Threefold Binding of the Heart by studying the sweat on your husband or bride on the night of your honeymoon.
                        • If you want to master the Rune of Singular Hate, you might need to find two people in Creation that share the purest love Creation has seen in the last century and poison that love so much that they both willingly murder each other. Raiding the Cureluean Lute of Harmon Lute of Harmony's records in heaven would be your best bet for finding such a Love.

                        In short, actually learning sorcery can give you lots of plot options if your ST is up for it

                        Note that Necromancy never had a Salinian Working equivalent - the only way to learn Necromancy is to learn it from a teacher (and Deathlords have 90% of that stuff locked down)

                                    You gotta concentrate on stormwind rider, giving you the internal penalty on all your actions and DVs while maintaining it. Any ranged attacks outside of it take the external penalty. That kind of thing shouldn't be changed because then it becomes a great actual in combat spell as opposed to the great movement spell it is.                                     Parry DV covers blocking, it's using your weapon to stop an attack from hitting you. Grand Goremauls have a penalty to defense because even though they're magically nimble, they're still giant pillars of jade. It's pretty obvious to an opponent where it is and they can anticipate where it will be (which is why there's also no accuracy bonus). the good news is that if you take a 3 pip specialty in your weapon, it's suddenly as nimble as a Daiklave in both accuracy and defense!
