                            Originally posted by Maseiken View Post

                            Mind if I ask how you go about doing that?
                            I just give each Zombie/Thug/Owl-Headed Battle Butler 1 HP and the group's total hp equals the number of members. I don't pin Drill down to anything specific, but what makes sense for the group- Zombies never rout. Thugs rout after losing half their number or a particularly successful 3 point stunt. Soldiers with their Warleader have a bonus on rout checks, etc. It do my best to explain their reactions as what the players see, for example:

                            "As Forgotten Scholar flashes his blade and three heads tumble down in front of him, Audacious Crow's arrow strikes the brazier holding hours worth of oil and coal. The hundred pound bowl crashes down into the midst of the horde, separating them with a wall of fire as the screams of the pinned-downed bandits are consumed by fire. Captains Bael jumps forward and rips the first bandit he sees clean in half, in a gory display of brutal strength. clearly out matched and surrounded by chaos, the remaining bandits break and all begin running is different directions, caring only for their own lives".

                            Forgotten Scholar launched an immediate Decisive attack on his first turn, dealing 3 damage. Crow did a Stunt to bring down a massive flaming bowl of bronze in the middle of the group (crushing 2 guys and triggering a Rout Check), while Bael used grapple charms (on a Battlegroup....) and turned them instantly into damage (while imposing a Morale Penalty for his brutality). The Bandits were promised an easy raid on a peaceful town and barely had any incentives beyond their own desire for loot and panicked, failing their already low morale roll, and broke.

                            So in this case 50 bandits were dealt something like 6 wounds, but the Stunts and Scenario caused them to make a Rout check really early and the dice got lucky. When the Bandit Leader, BlackTooth, arrived, he made a command roll to unify them again and pressed the attack, but with less men then he would have.

                            It's quick and dirty, and the math is based more on how the scene "feels" then actual stat blocks. I still use the attack stats for when the BG attacks, but I streamline everything else into how you'd expect those foes to react and if they have a leader they get some morale bonuses and stuff.

                            EDIT: oh, I forgot, I sometimes change up the 1 Hp - 1 Body thing. The Owl Headed Battle Butlers were all lesser Fae who worked for a Fae queen and had Silver Maces for weapons. They fought in small squads but each one had 3 hp and still worked as a battle group. so 5 bodies, 15 hp, and one goes down at 3, 6, 9... wounds dealt.
