            Tl;dr Mortals use their souls to manipulate essence. Solars turn that skill to 11 with the aid of their anima banner, a physical feature unique to the exalted that allows them to magically manipulate their environment. They're super-enlightened-mortals.

            The mini-rant on my head canon:
            To me at least, the anima banner is the real, physical manifestation of the exalt's soul. That's why it's individualized to reflect them. I've seen stunts where a bird totem flapped to give the exalt lift in their jump.

            Outside of stunts, what do you think is happening when you use Call the Blade? You're using the invisible (sometimes visible) anima banner to pull the blade in. You tie up a large portion of your anima's power in attuning (lifting!) extraordinarily heavy artifacts. And yes, enlightened solars can grip stuff with their (invisible!) anima banners.

            Enlightenment progresses an exalt's control of anima-based telekinesis from resonant orichalcum artifacts to learned, limited telekinesis and straight through bizarre flight and more. They're super humanly skilled at manipulating the world through a magical set of invisible limbs.

            In my mind, the anima is what makes all of the exalted so powerful. The rest of the world doesn't have the soul extension (metaphysical super strength) the exaltation grants. They have to deal with a soul that doesn't have the structure (limbs) built for heavy lifting.

            Note: Straight telekinesis is difficult, requiring high enlightenment (essence). It's not popular in combat. Why go through the effort of fighting without spiritual leverage when you can fight enemies directly?
