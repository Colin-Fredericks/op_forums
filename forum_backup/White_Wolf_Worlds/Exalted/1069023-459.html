                    Originally posted by Khazadir View Post
                    Does Read Intentions count as a Social Influence roll for the purposes of Harmonious Presence Meditation?
                    No. Influence rolls are instills, persuades, threatens, bargains, inspires, and then any Charms or other effects that tell you to make a special inspire roll.
