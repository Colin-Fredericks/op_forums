                    #11990
                    02-19-2020, 03:03 AM
                    Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                    Ok. Long ticks. If you cast a spell using long ticks, does it mean that casting a spell takes seconds?
                    In 2E:

                    Ticks are ticks. Approximately equivalent to ticks of a clock, i.e. seconds. Note this is approximate.

                    Long ticks are sixty ticks. Approximately equivalent to minutes.

                    Long ticks are used in Mass and Social combat. Ticks are used in Personal Combat.

                    2E is a complicated game and I don't mind answering questions about it. But these are basic rules terms covered in the Corebook. Please preface your questions clearly denoting edition. (I don't believe 1.5 used "long ticks" but there could be a Power Combat answer here too..)
