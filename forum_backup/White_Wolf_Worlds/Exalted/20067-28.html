    Polar Mutants are caused by prolonged exposure to the Elemental Poles, although God-Blooded resulting from unions with Elementals would probably look and work similar; you could probably use the Elemental Powers that Creation's Elemental GBs inherit to model them. Interestingly, it is said that PM who breed with each other can create hybrid children, who exhibit a mix of both parent's elements!

    Autochthonian Polar Mutants are labeled:

    Lightning: Arcspawn

    Smoke: Ashbreathers (further subdivided into Ruin-Eaters and Renders)

    Crystal: Glass Walkers (!)

    Oil: Oilkin

    Steam: Steam-Blooded

    Void: Blightborn

    (The Pole of Metal is too "diffuse" to affect mortals in this way, apparently.)
