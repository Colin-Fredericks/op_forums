                    Originally posted by Sakii View Post


                    Just to be clear, a solar with craft(carpentery), craft(artifact) and craft (First age) can make a warstrider. And it wouldnt be gaming the system?


                    Bonus question: How can, for example, a solar carpentert create and artifact with a Magical Material if he doesnt have knowlodge on how to handle metals?
                    "​Creating artifacts requires
                    the crafter be a character capable of manipulating Essence
                    (such as an Exalt or sorcerer), have at least Lore 3, Occult 3,
                    a relevant Craft Ability rated to at least 4, and must then purchase
                    dots of Craft (Artifacts)."

                    What I believe Vance was saying is that you don't have to divide Craft (First Age) and Craft (Artifact) into more specific skills like Craft (First Age: Warstriders).

                    You still need relevant Crafts to be able to start a project.

                    Since Sorcey is relevant to the equation, the MMs do not necessarily have to be forged or beaten to shape. They can be considered reagents for the project. Even without Sorcery, knowledge of Craft (Artifact)/(First Age) implies knowledge of processes relevant to making reagents for what you need.

                    A Warstrider made by a guy with only Carpentry would have the remarkable craftmanship shown on it's wooden body, with carvings, grain display, maybe the MM was used to create a special varnish that protects and strenthens the wood and gives the project a distinct look. Or the magical process "veined" it through the wooden frame, or maybe it was absorbed by the wood giving it a distinct color and texture, or maybe it sprouts in MM leaves or moss, etc...

                    So I believe the skills you have interact with how a project turns out in a narrative sense. You´re not going to forge a full metal Warstrider plated in Orichalcum yourself if you only have Craft (Carpentery), Craft (Artifact) and Craft (First Age). At least not in a table I run.
