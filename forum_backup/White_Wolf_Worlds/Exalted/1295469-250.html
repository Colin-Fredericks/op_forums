    Originally posted by Iron Phoenix View Post
    Some Solar grapple-related questions:

    1) River-Binding Wrath enhances a "grapple attempt or control roll". This wording is ambiguous. Is a "grapple attempt" my initial Decisive attack against the target's Defense, or is it the Initiative roll against the gambit's difficulty?
    The attack roll.

    2) Dragon Coil Technique reads "In addition, when the Solar controls a clinch with Dragon Coil Technique, her withering and decisive savaging attacks and slams enjoy (Essence) bonus dice of damage." It further reads, "Finally, if the Solar uses her (Strength + Brawl) to land a grapple attack, Dragon Coil Technique expressly allows the Solar to grapple characters of prodigious size—tyrant lizards, river dragons, siaka and other similarly sized beasts are all valid targets for the Solar’s dread grasp." What does this mean? DCT is Reflexive with Instant duration. Do I "control a clinch with DCT" by using DCT to supplement the initial attack roll in which I land the grapple? Or do I have to activate DCT on every turn in which I want to benefit from its effects? It seems like the corrected text should be like Ten Calamities Technique - a Supplemental Charm that lasts until the grapple ends. If I do indeed have to use it every turn to benefit from it, does my grapple against an opponent with Legendary Size immediately end on my first action in which I don't use DCT to maintain the grapple?
    If you use Dragon Coil Technique, you get all the benefits for the duration of the grapple. Its duration is Instant because you don't have to commit the motes, unlike Ten Calamities.

    3) Shockwave Technique is Reflexive, not Supplemental, and has the Withering-only keyword. Yet it can also be used in conjunction with Crashing Wave Throw, which has the Uniform keyword. Crashing Wave Throw applies damage to the grappled victim being thrown, and Shockwave Technique applies damage to a small area of effect surrounding a target that the grapple victim is thrown at. Does this mean I can use Crashing Wave Throw to do a Decisive attack against the grapple victim, while using Shockwave Technique to do a Withering attack against a different group of targets? Since Shockwave Technique is not Supplemental, but Reflexive, and has a separate attack roll, it seems possible that it creates an additional attack which would make this allowable.
    Shockwave Technique enhances a withering throw, rather than creating one. If you want to pair it with Crashing Wave Throw, they both have to enhance the same withering attack.

    4) Lightning Strikes Twice, when used in conjunction with Crashing Wave Throw, allows me to make a grapple attack with the target's Defense set to 0. Is this a penalty that can be negated by Charms that negate penalties to Defenses, such as Fivefold Bulwark Stance or Flow Like Blood?
    Setting it to 0 isn't a penalty.

    Or is this intended to effectively make the grapple always successful, barring a botch or perfect defense such as Seven Shadow Evasion?
    I think the idea is that you set their base Defense to zero, but that they can still raise it with Excellencies and the like.

    5) Raging Wrath Repeated has a timing window of "If the Solar crashes an opponent she is clinching, she may reflexively activate this Charm". How does this interact with Charms such as Titan-Straightening Method? If I have 5 attacks with TSM, for example, and crash my opponent on the 3rd attack - do I get to complete my remaining two attacks and then activate RWR? Or do I have to utilize RWR mid-flurry? If I do utilize RWR mid-flurry, do I still get to do my two remaining attacks and then end with my 5 rounds of control back (plus my combat action), or do those rounds of control retroactively get added to TSM and change the flurry to 7 attacks remaining?
    I'd say you use it immediately after crashing them, but still get to complete the flurry.

    6) What types of actions are valid for a Withering or Decisive savage? For example, can I use Rampage-Berserker Attack as a savaging attack? Knockout Blow?
    You can generally use Simple Charms when you make savaging attacks.

    7) Shockwave Technique has rules for "If the Solar is crashed when she uses this attack", but the grapple rules state "A grapple will also immediately end if the grappler suffers Initiative Crash." Under what circumstances could this effect ever trigger? Should we view this as allowing for custom Charms and Evocations that allow a grappler to retain control of a grapple while crashed?
    I suspect that was an oversight, but a custom Charm could let you maintain grapples while crashed.

    8) If I have both Raging Wrath Repeated and Heaven Fury Smite, and I crash a target during a grapple - can I benefit from both and reset my combat action twice? Or do I have to pick and choose which effect to utilize?
    You only get the benefit of one, I think.

                            Okay, I’m sorry if this one’s a little heavy.

                            So I've been consuming Asians Represent's Critical Read series on youtube and one of the more criticisms that found resonance with me was that they had of Al-Qadim was that the middle eastern setting was being used to introduce historical sexism to a setting that had previously avoided such matters thus reaffirming certain biases towards the modern middle east and Islamic culture.

                            I’ve noticed over my years of playing and interacting with the setting that Chiaroscuro and its stricter gender roles has a tendency to be used as a vehicle for orientalism, including the seemingly prerequisite sexism which – were Coral in Compass: West not handled with the subtlety of a falling anvil, would’ve been the most visibly sexist place in Creation last edition. However, its strict separation of gender roles is difficult to disentangle from the significance of taking the grey.

                            My last relevant observation might not be something that can’t really be helped, in retellings of Chiaroscuro’s lore I’ve seen some erasure of the biologically male Delzhan becoming a Dereth in favour of treating it exclusively as a way for women to escape from being stuck at home raising children in a way which I feel sharply detracts from the trans/enby representation that group offers.

                            How are certain obvious pitfalls being avoided?
