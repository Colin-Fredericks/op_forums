    He had a pretty good demi-character arc too in the comics. First just hanging out with his circle because they seemed like okay people for the most part. Then after a douchebag monk thrust his fist into Kidale* and killed him he went into a big mopey slump because he fucking liked Kidale.

    Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
    Where is this written?
    Well to my foggy recollection in 2e you needed a sifu to learn martial arts from, but with the caveat that Solars could use basically anything as their sifu. Like you could look at a housecat and learn Tiger Style, or look at a candle and learn Fire Dragon all on your own. In 3e I don't think the stipulation that you need a sifu for anything exists, you can just buy dots of an ability or charms by investing in training times. So for a Dawn with MA favoured you can pick up the ability dots in a few days, dual training charms and dots after the first couple of dots if you want.

    In theory Topaz could just light a candle or a campfire and look at it going "Huh, so that's how fire is." and master Fire Dragon over a fortnight. That makes for a pretty bland story though.

Which lead to cool things like a Solar master martial artist carefully bowing and paying respects to his aged, first mortal sifu. Anyone who didn't show such respect was basically an automatic villain.

                            A dungeon crawl is a story. Lunar politics or Yu-Shan politics is a story. Attempting to infiltrate an immaculate monastary to learn one of their styles is a story.

                            Holing up in the middle of nowhere to train is downtime.

                            If you take Topaz's desire to learn all forms of sword mastery seriously, there is actually a lot of story left for her. Because learning all those styles is not an easy thing for a peniless anathema whose only skill is violence and who doesn't actually try to kill all her problems. If she has to learn Violet Bier Style and Fire Dragon Style and whatever other style from somewhere, that's an interesting story about how far away true mastery can be even for someone who seems to already be at the top.

                            Cause where the hell is she going to learn Violet Bier or Fire Dragon style? Who alive that knows those styles is going to be willing to teach her? If they are, at what price?

                            What if the only way she can actually learn fire dragon style is by watching immaculates that try to kill her with it? Even as good at swording as Topaz is, deliberately picking a fight with the Wyld Hunt to observe fire dragon stylists is not a healthy life choice.

                            So maybe you try to delve shogunate ruins for manuals that might not exist. Or try to barter with Lunars for manuals they might not have, and you can bet your ass that the Silver Pact is going to want something in exchange. Maybe the only ones the Pact has are on the Caul and Topaz needs to fight with the Black Lion against the Realm there. Traveling from the Scavenger Lands (where Topaz starts out by default) to the caul is a trek across basically the entirety of Creation. That is not an easy trip, and it isn't a short one, and unless she can swim to the Caul its not a cheap one. The journey to the Caul to find Sha'a Oka to negotiate with him for a Fire Dragon Style manual alone could be an epic chronicle of adventure, before even setting foot on the Caul.

                            Then there's Violet Bier Style. Is that even taught in Creation? Topaz might only know it from Bronze faction assassins trying to kill her or the complaints of lunars who faced the same. So if it can only be learned in Yu-Shan, then Topaz has to get into Yu-Shan somehow. Getting to Yu-Shan is even more trouble than getting to the Caul from the Scavenger Lands! Even if she gets into Yu-Shan, she'll need to deal with Yu-Shan Politics to actually learn Violet Bier. Even if she isn't interested in the politics, you can bet your bottom dollar Yu-Shan Politics is going to be very interested in her. So she'll need to juggle the politics as she learns the style.

                            And that's just Fire Dragon and Violet Bier of Sorrows. What about sword MAs that can only be learned in Hell? What if the Seal of Eight Divinities gets broken and the Autochthonians have sword styles that are completely new and different? Where will she have to go and what will she have to do to learn those styles?

                            Fuck. Now I actually want to play Topaz. Those stories sound fun and interesting.

                                        3i: Daddy pushes his advantage, launching another Decisive attack slamming his palm into Imma's face (0/4m, 14d).                                         Daddy: -15i (0/5m, 3a, 1WP, 2B/-0) [FONT="]Elegant Weapon Repertoire (Disarm, Flexible, Bashing), -2 Onslaught[/FONT]
