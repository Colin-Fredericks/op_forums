    How "official" is the Usurpation?

    12-15-2020, 08:55 AM
    One of my players took the "Pact with an Ifrit Lord" shaping ritual, and I want the ifrit lord in question (Lord Mahashwari Am, Baron of Illumination, Southern God of Beacons, Signaling Fires, and Lighthouses) to be a reasonably important NPC in the campaign. If a god designates a Solar (a Zenith, in this case) as his emissary to another god (I'm thinking of sending the Circle on a diplomatic mission to Ahlat), what would be the reaction in Heaven generally, and among Bronze Faction Sidereals in particular? Do the Sidereals have a legal leg to stand on? Can they officially order the Solars' deaths, or would they just try to arrange it while the other gods look the other way?

      From a Heavenly perspective, the Usurpation was an illegal coup. The Solars still hold the Mandate of Heaven. The great Sidereal trick was (by way of breaking the Mask) to make themselves immune to prosecution for the whole thing.

      Given the practical reality of all the Solar Exaltations being locked away for many centuries, the gods have adapted. Some have aligned themselves with the new status quo, others pine for the good old days, some do both. I don't see that your Solars are doing anything illegal, though of course that doesn't stop an unfriendly Sidereal from conspiring against them anyway, or pointing the censors at their Ifrit ally for an audit (for crimes real or fabricated). But if he's powerful enough to be an Ifrit Lord, his power base is probably largely terrestrial, and he probably has friends of his own... such are the machinations Sidereal stories are based on.

      Note that 3e doesn't really put much emphasis on this kind of stuff, like the Usurpation and the Mandate of Heaven, anymore. It happened off camera somewhere and as such isn't the focus. It's probably still *true*, if you want to use it, but I suspect we'll learn more about the current state of affairs if the Sidereals book ever comes out.

                          Originally posted by Hakkonen View Post

                          Que? What was this Mask, and why did its destruction put Sidereals beyond the reach of Heaven's laws?
                          The Mask is one of the constellations that form the structure of Sidereal astrology and fate manipulation. According to 2e sources, breaking the Mask made all evidence that the Sidereals were responsible for the Usurpation vanish. But it also permanently "broke" people's abilities to remember them, creating the current state of arcane fate, where people tend to forget their individual existence and ascribe their deeds to others or to random chance.
