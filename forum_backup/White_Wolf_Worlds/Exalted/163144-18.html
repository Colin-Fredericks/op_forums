    Gossamer Fingertips Posture
    This one's still in the ideation phase. I'd like to do something more interesting with it than a rehash of Someone Else's Destiny.

    Getimian Larceny
    Type: Supplemental
    Action: Reflexive
    Duration: Action
    cost: idunno. Something balanced. I'm not good at balance.

    The Getimian's pattern spiders wrap strands of gossamer fates around her fingertips, subtly tricking Fate into never responding to the Exalt's touch.

    When the getimian deliberately touches something with her hands, any effect that would trigger as a result of such contact doesn't occur. Traps remain unsprung, poison does not enter the body, the scolding heat of molten metal does not boil the skin, swords do not slice off fingers and marks do not feel fingers entering their pockets. Particularly clever or potent effects or magical effects specifically meant to detect such action may cause a clash of wills roll at storyteller discretion; in such case, the exalt benefits from [a bonus... +larceny?].

    Additionally, the charm has one of the following effects:
    Yin: if the character paid for this charm with her Yin pool, pattern spiders scurry along her hands and fingers, creating a cocoon around the moment of contact and dragging it into the deepest recesses of the exalt's soul, to consume and dissect. The exalt may "store" the effect for later, keeping the charm's mote cost committed.

    Yang: if the character paid for this charm with her Yang pool, she may redirect the effect unto anyone she is able to successfully touch with her fingertips (including the originator. This might mean a trap poisons the wrong target, a sword cuts itself or its owner, or someone else feels the thief's hand stealing from their pockets) - typically this requires direct contact, but attuned weapons and form weapons may be exempt from this. For [a surcharge] she may also unleash one of her stored effects, creating a burst of iridescent cobwebs and immediately ending the charm.

    This charm can be used to Clash barehanded against lethal and aggravated attacks.

        Sun and Moon Entwined
        Solar Occult
        The Sun and Moon are companions eternal, gathering strength from each other for all time. The Solar seizes upon the bond he shares with his Lunar mate and uses it to channel his essence. The Solar gives up a dot of Essence (not a mote) to increase the Lunar's Essence by one. This move is permanent, but can be repeated once for each dot of Solar Bond the Lunar mate possesses. The lost dot of Essence can be repurchased with XP as normal. The Solar does not lose any charms, but must meet the prerequisites again in order to use them. The Solar may not perform this technique twice in the span of 5 months, causes the solar 3 health levels of damage, and leaves the solar unconscious for 5 hours.

                Snatching defeat from the jaws of victory
                Infernal (Ebon Dragon) charm.

                Standing triumphant over his opponent fills the Dragon's Shadow with sorrow, for he is about to once more lose the thing he loves.

                The infernal can activate this charm any time he vanquishes or is about to vanquish a foe to create a momentum-shifting turnabout - the target seems to gain the upper hand in a completely unforeseen manner, enough to triumph over the infernal, but not so much that escape is impossible. The situation need not be life-or-death. It can be as simple as a game of cards or as complicated as a days-long political debate.

                This victory is hollow however, and the defeat-that-never-was hangs in the target's shadow, slowly poisoning her soul. For as long as motes are committed to this charm, the target gains an appropriate sorcerously-charged negative intimacy towards the Exalt that pushes them to pursue them further (such as Punishment for transgressions, Revenge for an other slight, Obsession). Additionally, the Infernal's proximity pushes the target to unknowingly sabotage themselves in everything they do, increasing external penalty of most actions by +1. Any time the target would take a course of action that would indirectly go against the infernal or his goals, the penalty instead increases by +2. Going directly against the infernal or his plans increases the difficulty by the exalt's Essence rating, for a maximum of [something.. +5?] as long as they share the same Scene. Lastly, when the target obtains no successes, 2s follow the Rule of 1 (both 1s and 2s count as botches).

                This effect ends once the Infernal is about to take revenge on the target, at the beginning of the action that would decide the outcome.
                [essentially, in the same circumstances that allow its activation, preventing this charm from stacking with itself.]

                An infernal can only sustain as many activations of this charm as his Essence score.


                Fate of all fools

                Upgrade to Snatching Defeat From the Jaws of Victory

                By spending [a surcharge] The infernal may activate this charm against a group, making it count as a single entity for the purpose of its prerequisite's activation limits. In this case, only the leader gains the relevant intimacy.

                Everybody in the group seems to desert the target, making the unit losing magnitude with every failed roll. Botches turn the mob against its former master - units split off and actively go against their now-hated leader.

                        Cultivated Jade Blossom Technique
                        Dragonblooded crafting charm
                        cost: variable mins: Craft 3, essence 2 type: dramatic
                        Keywords: combo-ok
                        duration: scene
                        prerequisite charms: Shaping hand Style
                        description:

                        The Princes of the Earth can enrich their lands. By meditating in a field from one dawn to the next, a dragonblood both draws susteinance from the ground - making food and drink unnecesary for the duration - but also lends her own essence to the ground, enriching it and greatly nourishing any plants sown in the field. At the end of the charm any crop growing in the field is fully grown, matured and ready to harvest. In the case of this charm being used in orchards only the harvestable crop grows, not the bushes or trees being harvested from - unless the surrounding area was planted with for example apple seeds, in which case apple trees will grow, but will not bear fruit unless the charm is used again on them.

                        The charm costs 4 motes per square mile x Essence of the Dragon-Blood.

                                            Convicted Flowers Beneath the Guilty Sky

                                            Terrestrial Circle Sorcery

                                            The sorcerer prepares a brew of rare and potently hallucinogenic herbs. A catalyst is prepared from a token tied to a sin or a crime, smeared in the sorcerer's blood and imbibed with the brew. The sorcerer names the sin or the crime, and can be as general or specific as desired; however, the spell doesn't heed names. "Consorting with Maurvile the Cannibal" would target anyone consorting with any cannibal.

                                            Invoking the scripture of the Guilty Maiden and cursing in all 5 directions, the sorcerer buries the token in the center of a town (or at a crossroads if outside of one).

                                            That same night and for [The Sorcerer's Essence] nights thereafter, small sickly-white flowers grow and bloom around the beds and doors of any and all who are guilty of the named sin or crime according to its wording. These flowers' pollen carries the effects of the brew, instilling hallucinations, terror and severe paranoia in the guilty. Most often, these hallucinations manifest as blood-streaked skies that sing the dirges of the guilty, and the sun and moon appear as inescapable eyes of celestial judgement.

                                            The sorcerer must be careful however - while flowers won't grow around the caster, their pollen is indiscriminate in its effects. If the caster is also guilty of the cursed sin or crime according to its wording, breathing in the flowers' pollen will have its full effect.

                                            Mechanically, all targets count as being afflicted with [a very potent and debilitating toxin that causes little damage]. This poison isn't supernatural however, and is subject to treatment, immunity and resistance as normal.

                                                Ah screw it. I have an idea for this.

                                                Tyrants' teeth, Lover's Mouth

                                                Liminal charm
                                                type: permanent.
                                                cost: none (5 motes, 1wp)

                                                The Liminal may replace his own teeth with any of those he finds - be they human, shark or river dragon - by ripping out the old and jamming in the new. This allows them to replicate the special attacks of the creature's mouth as long as the vast majority of the teeth are part of that set. The Chernozem's mouth remains as inconspicuous as any human's, hiding the disproportionate geometry and razor-sharp lethality of their newly-acquired maw.

                                                For example, the Liminal could replicate a boar's or elephant's charge, a tyrant lizard's bite, a Comodo Dragon's venomous attack, or even a Giant Spider's mandibles.

                                                This charm's cost need only be paid when the Liminal wishes to make use of the special attacks endowed by his monstrous teeth, at which point the Liminal must take a misc. action for the maw and teeth to take their positions in a grotesque display of popping joints, jutting bones and dripping ectoplasm. Once activated, the teeth are activated for the rest of the scene.

                                                Duplicitous filigree Adornment

                                                Getimian Craft

                                                The Getimian rolls Intelligence + Craft and fashions a temporary Filigree out of one of her personal pattern spiders, which looks like a small, beatifully intricate clockwork mechanism with a perceived Resources rating of the Getimian's Essence Rating. An exceptional success on the craft roll adds 2 to this value, to a maximum of 5. The decoration affixes itself flawlessly to any surface and adjusts its aspect to complement any outfit as the patter spider adjusts its shape and color. This newly created object may be given as a present to anyone, though its destruction means the destruction of one Getimian's pattern spiders, causing one level of unsoakable aggravated damage.

                                                The filigree subtly tugs at the fates of conversations and endeavours, having one fo the following effects depending on the mote pool used to activate it:

                                                Yin: The filigree dulls the interest of anyone interacting with the bearer, making them treat everybody's intimacies as one level weaker for the purposes of Social Influence - they simply don't find the character's word interesting in the slightest.

                                                Yang: The filigree augments the resonance in the bearer's words, allowing her to treat the intimacies she wishes to exploit in others as being one level higher. At storyteller's discretion, this may reawaken old (read: abandonned and forgotten) Defining Intimacies at the weakest level.

                                                (hey, if you can have combat bonuses in Dodge, you can have Social bonuses in Craft).
