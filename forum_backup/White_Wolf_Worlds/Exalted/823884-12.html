        My first attempt at creating... well, anything really... for 3E!

        Luminous Rapture (Orichalcum and Starmetal Powerbow) Artifact (3)

        There once was a knight who loved too much. His heart beat wildly for every beautiful young man or woman who crossed his path, and it tormented him that he could not control his passionate infatuations. After years of brief romances that all left him heartbroken, he prayed to Venus, sacrificing his sword, his horse, and his knighthood to her in a blaze of passion. Possibly inspired by his devotion, or possibly amused by his actions, or possibly simply inscrutable, Venus appeared before the once-knight and gave him two gifts to steal the passion from his heart. The first was simply the sight of her, who drove the thought of any mortal woman from his heart for all his life, leaving him pining only for the Maiden of Serenity. The second was this bow,

        Named Luminous Rapture by its first wielder, who forswore ever using a sword again and became a masterful archer, this bow was truly made for a Solar Exalt's hand. It strikes flesh as well as any powerbow, but its true strength comes when it strikes the heart. However, its powers cannot help but affect the wielder as well as the target, bearing the curse of the once-knight with the blessing of the Maiden of Serenity.

        The legends of its original wielder have been lost through the Shogunate. Some say he was himself a Solar Exalt who met his end at the hands of the Wyld Hunt. Others claim he was but a mortal man and never unlocked the true secrets of his bow. These same people suggest the bow was not meant for him, but was intended to be passed eventually into the hands of its true owner. Venus herself has never been asked about the origins of the weapon.

        A Solar who attunes this artifact can extend the Duration of Phantom Arrow Technique to One Action. It has never been attuned by a Sidereal wielder, though it is rumored to have special properties for such a wielder as well.

        Passion's a Prison
        Cost: +1m ; Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisites: Phantom Arrow Technique

        The Curse of the Once-Knight is carried in the depths of this bow, but through some perverse trick (perhaps the will of Venus herself), the curse is the first of the bow's magical properties to be easily unlocked. This Evocation enhances the use of its prerequisite. A phantom arrow enhanced by this Evocation deals no damage to its victim. Instead, it automatically creates in the archer an Intimacy of love toward the target. This Intimacy begins at Major intensity, but is otherwise treated as a normal Intimacy for all purposes.

        Shot through the Heart
        Cost: 5m, 1W ; Mins: Essence 2
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Passion's a Prison

        The truest power of Luminous Rapture is to share her wielder's love with those around her, if they can bear the suffering of their omnipresent adoration. This Evocation supplements a decisive Archery attack made with Luminous Rapture. This attack is made against the target's Resolve. If the attack is successful, it deals no damage. Instead, it functions as an instill action to create or strengthen an Intimacy of love. The archer must possess the same Intimacy at an intensity of at least Minor in order to instill the Intimacy in a target.

        Give Love a Bad Name
        Cost: +5m; Mins: Essence 2
        Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisites: Shot through the Heart

        Love hurts, it has been said, but Venus is nothing if not merciful. The Maiden of Serenity grants this Evocation the power to undo some of the hurt caused by love's labors. This Evocation enhances the use of Phantom Arrow Technique. A phantom arrow enhanced by this Evocation deals no damage to its victim. Instead, it automatically eliminates from the user an Intimacy of love toward the target. If the Intimacy was created by magic, the archer regains a single point of spent Willpower when successfully eliminating the Intimacy this way.

                So earlier I mentioned that I was working on several artifacts. Here's the other one that I'm ready to have critiqued:

                Consequence

                ●●●● Artifact: Soulsteel and Starmetal Direchain

                Appearance
                A surprisingly thin fighting chain, each link is only a half-inch wide. What its sleek design hides is its grave strength. Interwoven links of soulsteel, forged from the souls of victims of their own machinations, and starmetal, mined from the rise and fall of kingdoms big and small, form the body of the chain, garnished with wickedly spurred hooks, and each end terminates into a claw shaped in the image of owl talons.

                Attunement
                Benefit Attunement Cost: 1m
                - Decisive damage dealt by Consequence is Lethal
                - During a grapple gambit, the cost of a drag/restrain action only costs 1 turn and can be flurried with any other grappling action without the -3 penalty to either action.

                Evocations

                Karma’s Inescapable Embrace
                Cost: 3m
                Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Supplemental
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisite Charms: None
                One Line Summary: Add an additional range to grapple with and reroll Essence to the control roll for a grapple.

                Like ripples in a pond, action leads to reaction, deed and misdeed lead to karma. And by this, Consequence inevitably enfolds its prey. Consequence can now attempt to grapple someone within short range without moving closer. Also, during the control roll of a grapple while using Consequence, reroll up to (Essence) dice from wielder’s roll once, keeping the better of the two results. Should the grapple succeed from greater than close range, the wielder must drag the target closer before attempting a savage action or to throw them.


                Ironclad Grip
                Cost: -
                Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Permanent
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Permanent
                Prerequisite Charms: Karma’s Inescapable Embrace
                One Line Summary: prevent immaterial shenanigans and now savage attacks do aggravated to the raksha.

                As above, thus so below, fate binds us all; the mighty and the small. Spirits and other creatures capable of immateriality find their gift less reliable. Grappled opponents attempting to dematerialize must make a contested Essence roll against the wielder. Should the grappled gain more successes than the grappler, they will dematerialize as normal but suffer (turns left in the grapple) initiative damage. The wielder gains none of this initiative but does gain 5 initiative if it crashes the escapee. On a tie or failure, grappled cannot escape that way for the rest of the grapple, but loses none of the motes either.

                Also, the constraints of fate sear the very core of the fair folk within Consequence’s grasp, upgrading all damage to such a creature in a grapple with Consequence to aggravated.


                Tightening the Cycle of Violence
                Cost: 3m, 2i
                Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Reflexive
                Keywords: Uniform, Stackable
                Duration: Until Wielder’s next turn
                Prerequisite Charms: Karma’s Inescapable Embrace
                One Line Summary: Attacking the grappler makes the grappled easier to hurt

                Violence begets violence and will tear apart those caught in its grasp until there’s nothing left to beget violence. Following this teaching, Consequence’s clutches tighten in the face of opposition. Should the wielder be attacked while grappled, they may call this evocation. After each attack against the wielder until their next turn, subtract 1 from the grappled’s soak and hardness for each turn lost. If the victim of the grapple chooses to attack their grappler, subtract twice the value of the turns lost by their attack from their soak and hardness. This penalty lasts a number of turns equal the number of attacks attempted upon the grappler during this evocation + 1, even if this lasts longer than the grapple itself. The penalties stack across multiple uses though this evocation can only be activated once between turns and the additional penalties last only for rest of the oldest penalty’s time.


                Feeding the Ties That Bind
                Cost: 4m, 2i
                Mins: Essence 3
                Type: Supplemental
                Keywords: Decisive-Only, Perilous
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisite Charms: Tightening the Cycle of Violence
                One Line Summary: Turn damage from an attack into health gained

                Spilt blood has greased the chains of destiny and dyed the threads of fate since Creation’s original sin, the Edict of Succession. Upon rolling for damage on a decisive attack or savage action, the wielder may choose to halve the successes on the damage roll (round up) and then heal that many bashing levels of damage or downgrade that many lethal wounds to bashing. Should this heal more wounds than were damaged, gain 1 initiative after resetting.

                Repercussions’ Dread Reversal
                Cost: 5m, 1WP
                Mins: Essence 3
                Type: Reflexive
                Keywords: Counterattack
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisite Charms: Feeding the Ties That Bind
                One Line Summary: Free grapple when attacked that leaves the attacker very vulnerable

                It is the way of fate, that if one fights against it harder, they only become further entangled in its depths. Whenever the wielder is attacked by an opponent within close range and is not in a grapple, they may attempt to entrap the offender within its links. After either a failed attack roll or after damage has been dealt to the wielder (even if it was fully absorbed by hardness or soak), the wielder rolls for a grapple gambit, adding 1 success to the attack roll and rerolling (up to the lesser of the damage suffered by the initial attack, 0 on a miss, and the wielder’s Essence) dice on the control roll. Should the gambit succeed, the attacker is caught within the Consequence’s grip. For the grappler’s next turn, they may ignore the grappled’s armor soak and hardness, having caught them a compromising position.

                                This is my first attempt at a 3E artifact. They were designed for a character I never ended up playing. Please let me know what you think and what needs balancing.

                                Shattered Storm (Artifact ****)
                                Thunder’s Echoes and Splintered Lightning are a paired set of daiklaves. Thunder’s Echoes is the larger and heavier of the two. Splintered lightning is narrower and exceedingly sharp.

                                Tags: Lethal, Melee, Smashing (Thunder’s Echoes), Piercing (Splintered Lightning)

                                Each blade has a socket for a hearthstone in its hilt.

                                History:
                                Shattered Storm were forged during the First Age by a long forgotten god and their original names are likewise lost. They were composed primarily of blue jade, but each blade had a core made from frozen lightning harvested directly from the Pole of Air. Both were symmetrical double-edged straight daiklaves with designs similar to damascus steel decorating the length of the blades. Thunder’s Echoes was originally 5’4” long, 6” wide and its forceful blows could stagger any foe. Splintered Lightning was known to pierce any defenses and was 5’ long and 3” wide. Their original owner died thousands of years ago. They were rediscovered by Jochi Burke of the Silver Moose Tribe in the Age of Sorrows. They helped him survive numerous battles including one against a powerful Raksha noble which resulted in both blades being shattered. As they were sundered, they released a violent electrically charged essence explosion which wounded the Fae so severely it was forced to flee. It still suffers from a never-healing wound and believes a portion of its power has been stolen by the swords. It has vowed to never stop hunting Jochi. After the battle, he gathered up all the pieces of his treasured swords. He hoped to find someone with the skill to reforge them. Before he was able to find such a skilled smith, he was assaulted by a band Icewalker raiders from a rival tribe. Out of reflex more than anything, he attempted to draw both blades with a motion he had made hundreds of times. Much to his surprise, the swords responded, though in an unexpected form. All the broken pieces he had gathered up flew out of his pack and hovered in front him. Lightning arced out from the hilts and bound all the floating shards into 2 distinct blades. Jochi thought the broken blades were just scrap metal. However with enough time and effort, they may become more powerful than ever.

                                    Sharkbite

                                    Here is my 2nd attempt at a 3E artifact. My girlfriend's character is a pirate captain and also an artificer. Last session she crafted her first artifact: a cutlass named Sharkbite. We came up with these evocations as initial thoughts toward how the sword would work.

                                    Sharkbite has a damascus steel pattern. It is made mostly of black jade, but is translucent and has a tiny clockwork shark swimming inside. The shark can't actually get out but that doesn't stop it from trying to attack people it doesn't like such as enemy dragon-blooded..or Carl. Poor Carl. (Carl is one of our favorite a mortal crew members)

                                    Evocations of Sharkbite
                                    A Solar may pay an additional 2 motes when they attune Sharkbite. If she does so, Sharkbite gains its own mote pool with a maximum size of [Essence x Sail]. This pool gains 1 mote for every success the Solar rolls using Sail. These motes may only be spent on the evocations of Sharkbite.

                                    Flash Flood
                                    Cost: 7m; Mins: Essence 1
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: Withering-only
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: None

                                    The Solar sweeps Sharkbite in a wide arc, releasing violent waves of water which pummel and stagger anyone nearby. This charm creates a withering attack against all enemies in close range. If more than one enemy is hit, the Solar only gains initiative equal to the highest amount of damage done +1 for each additional target hit.
                                    This evocation leaves behind a knee deep pool of water 1 range band in diameter. This pool lasts for 1 scene. The Solar moves freely across the pool, gliding along the surface as if surfing. Anyone else suffers a -2 penalty to their mobility and evasion as long as they stand in the water.

                                    Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 1
                                    Type: Reflexive
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Instant

                                    This evocation can only be used if the Solar starts her turn standing in water that is at least knee deep. It allows the Solar to immediately move two range bands and automatically succeed at any disengage attempts even if this carries her beyond the limits of the water. This counts as the Solar’s movement for the turn.

                                    This evocation can be used to create either a withering or decisive attack. This attack is made at close range. Roll the attack as if the Solar was making a melee attack using Sharkbite. Add any stunt dice/successes to the damage roll in addition to the normal stunt bonus to the attack roll.

The shark will make a decisive attack against one foe either within the pool or within short range of it. Roll the attack as if the Solar was making a melee attack using Sharkbite.
                                    By spending 1 willpower, the Solar is able to shape the water to modify the shark, increasing its ferocity and bolstering its strength. Increase the raw damage of this attack by an amount equal to the highest craft skill possessed by the Solar.

This evocation creates a watery duplicate of any ship the Solar has previously claimed ownership of using Ship-Claiming Stance. The Glorious Solar Vessel is capable of traveling across both land and sea, providing the terrain is relatively flat. It sails about on waves of its own creation. While it is made entirely out of water, the Solar’s will gives it strength. It is as solid as any normal ship and can transport a full complement of crew or cargo.

The Solar can take her combat action to expend one of these sharks to make a decisive attack identical to Chum in the Water at no cost against anything within medium range of the vessel.

                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Permanent
                                                Prerequisites: Terrestrial Circle Sorcery

                                                Keywords: Decisive-only, Perilous
