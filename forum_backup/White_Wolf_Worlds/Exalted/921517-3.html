            Originally posted by Wick Black View Post

            This. Totally.

            Also, the old guy with the big sword? That is how Dawn Castes and Lunar Exalted should fight Warstriders. That guy is awesome.
            I'm a Classic Battletech player and one of the things I have loved back in the day was Elementals. Over to top warriors in powered armor who when they swarmed over your 100 ton mech, the would start ripping up the armor plating and eventually bring you down. They would be like ants tearing at it. Normal infantry guys died like nothing else. Elementals...these guys are what I think of when I think of a Exalted vs. A 'Strider. Will they take it down easy? Hell no. But they aren't hopelessly out classed. The circle might have to work together to do it too. The lone dawn is just going to slow it down, unless he gets smart, and thinks outside the box, otherwise a strider should give a lone dawn a very credible threat of being reduced to a red gooey paste. Is that going to happen, maybe 4 out of 10 times the dawn gets too cocky and ends up getting splattered on a 'Strider Daiklave, knocked out, crippled or failing to protect his target, in short they lose. Another 4 out of 10 times I figure he wins, within varying degrees of success. Such as driving off the enemy as opposed to outright victory. Maybe pushing the Strider to the limits of it's operational time, so the pilot opts to retreat or putting enough damage on the thing to make the pilot decide that winning this fight is too costly for their taste. Or yes managing to kill the pilot or "break" the strider. The remaining 2 out of 10 times the strider pilot fights the dawn to a stand still.

            Now that said I figure some things will shift these numbers around. Things like, the whole circle participating in the battle. The 'Strider pilot is a Dawn/Slayer/Dusk caste, weapons load out of the strider. Different levels of essence, does the Lone dawn have as their supernal their combat ability, or was it Resistance, or war?

            Now that said, honestly this thread caused me to start reading up on Imperial Knights, and I love how others brought up that you should have an intimacy to your strider. Plus since you bond to the machine with your soul it has fragments of the souls of every pilot and what they held dear floating around in it. So suddenly becoming a bit more like your Mom or Dad who used it before you, isn't so unlikely because now your bonded to those fragments of their souls. I just found this out but, apparently bonding to the Imperial Knight (if used as an example of a 'strider) causes you to develop that intimacy plus one to the duties and hierarchy of a knight. In in the terms of our setting the necrotic 'striders used by the underworld's forces that would be a Tie of Loyalty to a Deathlord. And a principal they approved of as well that you instantly gain and that cannot be weakened as long as you are bonded to your machine. Which I could see as something interesting in the way of Dynastic striders that suddenly the pilot feels a love like Fealty to the Empress, and has a defining principal of upholding the empire even when before they were considering bringing it down. I might like having that as a thing for 'striders in my setting.

                    The paradox that I cannot help but notice is people wanting for warstriders to be rare and require heavy maintenance while at the same time not be an overwhelming threat to an average Dawn and not be 'better' than any other given artifact.

                    I personally think that it should a rare Dawn who can take down a warstrider single-handedly with the odds favouring the more well-armed and canny opponent. I also think that if a warstrider is going to require extensive upkeep then it needs something to give it an advantage over somethong like say Volcano Cutter which has no visible downsides to its mere existence. My main concern with this is that 'superiority' between two equally rated artifacts should come from who has the greatest rapport with their artifact not a generic matter of making it into either Shadow of the Collosus versus 'I need to finish this fight before this thing breaks again.'

                            Warstriders are stated to be N/A Artifacts, so they need to be mechanically amazing, with the stats of articulated plate, smashfists and god-kicking boots combined as the absolute minimum for the least of them, the ones operating on the "power armour" level. The ones you pilot rather than wear should have their own appropriately amazing stats. Strength in particular should be very high. Lots of soak and hardness. Evocations based around amplifying the pilot's Charms to operate on the warstrider level. High attunement costs and the mecha versions should require hearthstones as well. Basically, they should be amazing and wondrous things you only bring out in time of dire need, when the situation has crossed the Godzilla threshold.

                                            I sort of feel that there's precious little that can challenge a 5 Essence Celestial Exalt Circle already (and it's not a bigger Celestial or maybe a couple of Gods), and a big Warstrider seems a fit match for a circle like that, or maybe for a single E6-7 Celestial.
                                            Maybe some low-tech clunky Warstrider made out of mostly iron and mundane material could exist and be way easier to defeat. But if I try to imagine a 15-20m tall block of Jade (or worse), suddenly the odds of the overpowered little guy with the silly huge sword should be rather low. Especially if there's another Exalt inside. Or if the 'strider uses the Dawn's Daiklave bigger sister.

                                            But again, I feel that the progression of power in Exalted, at least in 2E ramps waaay to fast in the 5-6 Essence range (especially consider it's just halfway to the theoretical top).
                                            I hope that in 3E this is a bit more spread out and Warstrider's balance plays a part in that. I'd hate to see them to pushed down in power just to be accessible early.
                                            On the other hand I'd be perfectly fine in seeing smaller, clumsier and weaker 'striders at that range.

                                            I guess my ideal would be some kind of Gurrenn Lagann progression of power, from the silly small and cheap robot used to mine and repurposed to fight, to the big badass one you get to release against an army or a behemoth.
