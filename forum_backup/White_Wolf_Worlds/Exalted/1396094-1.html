        Isator Levi I'm sorry if I got too heated in my last comment. You're cool, I'm cool.

                Eating Mental Anguish may be a little better depending how liberally you interpret the text. It puts a strong emphasis on emotions, which is not entirely covered by intimacies and limit. Inspire actions have very similar effects, are literally pure emotion overflowing, emulate intimacies for some purposes (it takes the place of a major intimacy to help/hinder persuasions) and are much easier to pull in combat.

                A social character can reasonably pull this off in combat unless every party involved pays the wp (which is not a waste! especially if you combo to expect that like a Nightingale practicioner). It's an easy 3sm per scene on top of whatever else you had.

                This segways into...


                Action expenditure has a bit more nuance than you are accounting, and it's an important one for combat. Any ritual-backed sm gain counts as your shape sorcery action. This has two benefits.
                1) This frees you to use some other action without losing sm (sometimes the angry tyrant lizard has an angrier mate and your circle's busy with the first one a couple range bands away)
                2) If you bank enough sm this is a way to cast "reflexively". This is limited for a terrestrial sorcerer, but a celestial or solar sorcerer can have pre-banked motes on top of this ritual and really make it shine.

                For example, Burning Passion. Your overhyped swashbuckler finally got to deliver his "You killed my father, time to die" speech to the trio of DBs that, well, killed your father. When you deal a killing blow to one of them the DM says you have gained 1wp from upholding your tie of hate towards the trio. Bam, 5sm. But wait! You already had 2sm leftover from a previous invocation of Burning Passion! Or maybe you had shaped those motes when attacking wasn't opportune (damn ninja's trying to kite you!). Instant Mists of Eventide!

                And yes this makes I See You even grosser. Burning 3-4 wp on JB through awakening eye and this ritual is cheap considering the spell will be out before you even act.

                                        It seems worth noting for the "Eating Mental Anguish" that while the limit effect is obviously restricted to people with a Limit track, the Intimacy effect works on anyone around, which means you can trigger it just by sticking Minors on random people you meet (which I take as intentional, since it's meant to make you more raksha-like). So you can instill, but you don't even need to go that far -- if anyone threatens anyone else, that will usually create a negative emotional Intimacy, as in many cases will doing something unpleasant in a large group. I'm using this one in a game right now and while it's early on, my impression is that you can make it work pretty well even if the rest of your party isn't helpful, but you have to actively hustle to try to make it ping in as many scenes as possible.
