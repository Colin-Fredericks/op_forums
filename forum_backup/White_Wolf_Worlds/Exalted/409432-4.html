                Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                The difference there is that none of the other Exalted were made by deforming another type.

                I mean really, if you choose to play an abyssal, but then go "But I'm not going to murder everything, that would be boring. Instead I'm going to fight for a just cause and be all heroic and shit" aren't you already playing a solar? Redemption is just the game/ST going "Yeah, you're not fooling anyone any more".

                No, because Solars don't always fight for a just cause and be heroic and shit. Some might, but you have plenty of others who are savage, conquering warlords who slaughter cities, or heartless assassins who kill for money, or lore seekers who would happy sacrifice thousands for some lost bit of knowledge. Solars are not Paladins and they don't always wear white hats.

                You can play a really nice Abyssal who goes around and helps people, and you won't have to worry about turning into a Solar. Because, "being a nice person" isn't what's needed for Redemption. It's seeking atonement from the Unconquered Sun and Creation itself, casting aside the underworld and becoming a champion of the living. That doesn't necessarily require being a saint.

                A cruel warlord who marches against the Underworld, closing Shadowlands and horrifically torturing ghosts until they finally submit to Lethe could certainly find the Unconquered Sun's favor and find himself turning into an Solar.
