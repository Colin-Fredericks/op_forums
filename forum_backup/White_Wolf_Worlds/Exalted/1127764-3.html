            If the Solar Exalted didn't come first, they honestly wouldn't be sufficiently distinguishable from the Infernals or Abyssals.

            If the Solar Exalted don't have the feel of legitimacy, then the argument of whether or not they can be a good thing for Creation becomes rather one-sided. The game and the world clearly didn't need them at the beginning, so why does it need them now? They're just introducing more chaos that the Dragon-Blooded have to deal with.

            But since the Solar Exalted come first, the game and the world is grounded in the fact that the Solar Exalted built and ruled a BETTER world than what Creation currently is. Well, "better", in at least some regard. But at least how much better becomes an actual question and two-sided debate, with pros and cons on both sides.

            Creation can never return to the First Age, and probably shouldn't even if it could... but it can never forget or ignore the First Age. It shouldn't be able to forget or ignore the Solar Exalted.

            That's why the Solar Exalted have to come first. Quite simply, they are the host whose Chosen make the biggest immediate impact on an individual level, and their return to Creation is one of the biggest impacts to the setting in its history. It's the most powerful and dramatic beginning for every Edition before and any Edition to come.

            This is Exalted. It has to start with a bang.

                                                        The real reason Exalted begins with the Solars is that the whole point of the game is to play a ridiculously powerful demigod, and while all the Exalted embody that to an extent, the Solars are the most straightforward and pure example. Starting with the most powerful splat is a deliberate thematic choice and affects the tone of the entire setting.

                                                        "Exalted is a game about playing ridiculously powerful god-kings who can change the world! To get you started, here's the weakest kind, who are by default heavily tied to the status quo." It just doesn't match with how the game is framed or marketed.
