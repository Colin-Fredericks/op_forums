                                Here's an idea based on Guardian Fog :

                                Epiphany-Inducing Workshop
                                Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Craft 3, Essence 1
                                Type: Simple
                                Keywords: None

                                        Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                        Basically, you're saying that the solar is to either specialize in making artisan works, or they are to lead a group to do things?
                                        Exactly.

                                        Now your artisan work might be some kind of exceptional tool: like an automated loom that can mass produce fabrics. A high end example of this would be the Distaff artifact (I linked you to in your other thread) or a Factory Cathedral (in whatever form that may take in the 3E paradigm).

                                        Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                        So let's see now. Difficulty for normal everyday objects, like shirts, normal clothing, blankets, dolls, all that sort of stuff... should be 2.
                                        lolwut?

                                        Page 185 and the Sidebar on page 186 (which specifically gives a Craft example).

                                        Normal, everyday tasks, are Difficulty 0.

                                        The standard fare for heroes is Difficulty 1.

                                        A trained seemstress doing her day-job doesn't have to roll, she just makes the clothes automatically.

                                        Your Solar hero looking to clothe the poor rolls at Difficulty 1 unless there's a significant complicating factor.

                                        I've made a shirt (from a pattern on a modern sowing machine) and I can tell you with absolute certainty that it is not as difficult as removing an appendix without adequate light!

                                        I would possibly make Missy McButterFingers roll at Difficulty 1 as making a shirt seems like a heroic challenge to her. Maybe pushing that to 2 if she didn't have any dots in Craft: Sowing. (I'd be more inclined to just ST fiat that she can or can't make the shirt -- I lean towards she can make shirts of obvious poor quality.) But anyone with two Dexterity and a single dot of Craft: Sowing can just make a shirt, no sweat.
                                        Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                        But what about attribute and appopriate craft? What attribute should be used?
                                        Page 185.

                                        You roll relevant Craft along with an Attribute which matches your task. If you stunt sowing as being a feat of your Intelligence or Dexerity then either should fly, but stunting it as a feat of Strength is going to raise eyebrows. Not saying it can't be done, but you're going to have to give a very compelling description of what your doing if you want the ST to tell you to roll Strength+Craft: Sowing.

                                        Generally, I'd say Intelligence and Craft (as Epitome said, that's what the Charms imply).
