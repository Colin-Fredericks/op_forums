                                    Originally posted by Crowley View Post
                                    What happends if someone finds the real name of an Abyssal?
                                    Unless there's some specific exception stated for sorcerous purposes, nothing, really. An Abyssal gains Resonance if answering to her old name (save to disavow it), but that's about it.

                                    And can a Sideral go through creation with out wearing a "Mask" ? if so, he gains paradox. right?
                                    The sort of "Mask" you're talking about are called "Resplendent Destinies," false identities and fates they craft for themselves so that they can be recognized as such. When they don't have it, they fortunately don't gain Paradox for it -- Paradox is vindictive, but it's not that vindictive. Instead, they experience Arcane Fate, which causes those they meet to completely forget about them when the Sidereal isn't right in front of them. Such people will remember someone doing what the Sidereal did, but even in the case of multiple witnesses, there's no chance all of them will agree on what the individual was like. None of them will make the connection on meeting the Sidereal again. This also affects written records and accounts: they tend to get lost, damaged, or rewritten to lose any such references.

                                    Those people made to forget do have a chance to remember, and most Exalted PCs are pretty likely to have the traits to do so reliably. Likewise, a Sidereal can willfully cultivate some memory of themselves through exposure to particular groups, represented by (I think) the Acquaintance Background, to make this recollection reliable. Even then, while these people do remember the character, it's made clear that they still have some difficulties doing so.

                                    I think the understandable confusion you had in referring to "Resplendent Destinies" as "Masks" is because of the reason for Arcane Fate: during the Usurpation, the Sidereals accidentally broke the constellation of the Mask. This had a metaphysically hefty impact on Sidereals as a group, and is the reason they suffer from Arcane Fate.

                                            To expand on this a bit...there are some very good reasons to operate without a Resplendent Destiny at times. Arcane Fate is kind of a mixed bag for the Sidereals. 99.999% of Creation cannot remember you when you aren't wearing one, and reality itself goes to absurd lengths to erase all signs that you exist (banks will lose your paperwork, the deed to your house will get lost, and so on). While this makes building contacts and owning stuff in Creation a massive pain, it also lets you operate with near perfect anonymity...which is great for the secretive Fate Ninjas. You can assassinate the ruler of a kingdom in broad daylight, surrounded by all his guards, with no disguise, while he's making a speech. And within a day, you could go play cards with the royal guard, because they forgot what you look like.

                                            Arcane Fate also gives you massive bonuses to disguise checks, regardless of how flimsy of a disguise you are using. A pair of Groucho Marx glasses counts as a full, elaborate disguise for a Siddie.

                                            Just wanted to check. But i believe that in first edición, its mentioned that Autoctonias air is extremedly dangerous and toxic to humans. Does this still applies in second edición? And what are The symptoms if breathing this air?
                                            While there are certainly parts of Autocthonia that are VERY bad for people...the air around the cities (and the rest of the Pole of Metal) is certainly safe to breathe. There are a lot of hazards in Autochthonia, (and some places don't even bother with having an atmosphere) but where most people live is pretty safe.

                                            Weirdly enough, Autocthonia's Elemental Poles are actually (bizarrely) safer for humans than the four Outer Poles in Creation. The pole of Crystal is pretty safe...the Adamant-caste Alchys live there, after all. There is a Metropolis (Loran) that extends into the Pole of Oil. The Patropolis Xexas is similarly situated in the Pole of Smoke. And normal humans venture out into the poles of Oil and Smoke on a regular basis for salvage work...they just need the appropriate protective equipment The only two poles that are so fantastically deadly that no one lives there are the poles of Lightning (constant threat of instant obliteration by electrocution) and Steam (you boil to death). If you tried to do the same in the Wyld-located Elemental Poles of Creation, you'd need potent Anti-shaping charms to keep your body from being transmuted into something appropriate to the Pole.

                                            To specifically answer your question, the pole of Smoke does have a toxic atmosphere...so the people who live in Xexas need respirators pretty much all the time. The effect is a toxic gas (2L/day, 3L, -/-, -1). There's also the threat of acid rain.
