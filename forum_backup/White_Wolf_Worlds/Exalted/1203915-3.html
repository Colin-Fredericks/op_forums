            This is only the very seed of an idea here, it is very unformed...

            I'm thinking about the portrayal of groups like the Amazons/Black Furies and the association of the mystical and feminine... and how in the context of the Realm, it's the masculine that is "other" from socially normative. So what would an all-male separatist group differentiated from the context of the matriarchy of the Realm look like?

            Well, in the culture of the Realm, men are associated with emotionality. "Proper" men are stoic to keep themselves in line - but men's inherent nature if not properly enlightened is to be ruled by their passions (same reason Fire Aspects regardless of sex are also associated with stoicism).

            So mystical masculinity in contrast with what's socially normative for the Realm would be all about embracing passion and exploring the interconnection of essence and the pure expression of emotion.

            I'm imagining that while this order/tribe is based out of... some kind of isolated stronghold, perhaps a former Immaculate monestary, or Shogunate era redoubt... their members take turns exploring the world trying to achieve great joys, anguishes, desires, etc, as the key to refining their Essence. I could even give them a Sorcery Initiation based around it!


            What do you think?

                    I have an idea I am developing for an Outcaste group. the are a set of sorcerer poet-kings. Obsessed with a functional type of immortality to retain their youth. They rule a kingdom in the east but towards the northern shore of the dreaming sea. They rule fairly to the peasants. Taxes are light comparatively to most kingdoms and each citizen is required to give a season of work every two years to the kingdom. The DB use this labor force as a way to build public works and defensive structures. Due to having a dozen or so of the DB be sorcerers. They invest heavily in sorcerous workings and due to this have rather impressive infrastructure and defenses foe the country as a whole. they have an outpost on their border where the guild is allowed to bring caravans by to trade with the kingdom. They local spirit courts of the kingdom are under the DB control as well but they work together well for the most part. Manse Fortresses defend key points in their country.

                                        I've been thinking about a small patch of Eastern Creation that served as an experimental plant breeding facility in the Shogunate. It was isolated and top-secret enough that it managed to avoid the Great Contagion before the Wyld invasion. Staffed by a few dozen mortal servants and laborers, maybe six or seven Wood-Aspect Dragon-Blooded, some Wood Elementals, and a pair of Raptok Dragon Kings (the daimyo in whose domain this facility was located was slightly more friendly to Dragon Kings than the Shogunate at large), the combined the sorcery, Charms, and Paths of its inhabitants, as well some ingeniously designed jade-infused plant life, allowed it avoid succumbing to the Wyld and the depredations of local fae until this very day. Naturally, with all the Wood Essence around, over the centuries all of these different bloodlines entangled, and the remaining survivors (maybe 30; a high birthrate is countered by high attrition against fae raiders) are all Terrestrial Exalted with literal Blood of the Dragon (Kings) in them. Most, of course, are Wood Aspected, but there is one Earth Aspect and a few Water.

                                        Recently, a Guild slave caravan chanced upon their island of Creation, awakening curiosity about the wider world they've been cut off from for so long. A few are now contemplating leading the remainders of the caravan (they slew the master and most of the guards and freed the slaves) back to Creation.

                                                    Hmmm... an idea has suddenly occurred to me...

                                                    ​I said before about a Lunar being the child of some Dragon Blooded, possibly Dynasts, who hooked up with another Terrestrial and had a bunch of Dragon Blooded children (I'd say that bloodline can get one to around half Exalted, so maybe five or six sired over the course of a couple hundred years), and then they do things a lot like what Lunars might do with their Moon-touched children. Not even necessarily Silver Pact stuff; it would be fairly fitting for one of those Lunars off doing their own thing.

                                                    ​Now I've got an image in which the Lunar is long gone, killed by the Wyld Hunt, and their surviving children, plus their children and grandchildren, have a grudge against the Realm. Probably not on a very long timeline, just back enough to have the members of the family at the top still remember their beloved parent and galled at the injustice of Immaculates killing them, who can influence their own offspring in the direction of doing something to undermine the Realm. Not a very large group, and should definitely have something going for them besides their old feud, but it's a picture I have for how the idea of Dragon Blooded loyal to a Lunar could have a twist.

                                                    ​For one thing, that they might maintain some traditions preserving Lunar tactics that were taught by the parent with regards to infiltration and sabotage. I'm seeing an occasion in which one member of the family went and joined up with an Imperial legion, waited for an opportunity in which they could sabotage the unit under their command in a manner that caused the legion severe trouble, and then disappeared.

If they only take found eggs who could be trained at Pasiap's Stair, then it's a lot of time and effort to put in, not to mention the risk, just to only really be in a position to hurt one legion.

                                                            An interesting idea that's at least somewhat undercut with 3E's Progenitive essence concept, but I'd always had an image of a DB kingdom that basically doesn't believe in the "bloodline purity" thing.

                                                            Basically extended clans of dozens of kids in order to try to play the numbers game and assume that the more chances to Exalt, the better overall average they'll have. Of course, the progenitive essence thing kinda undermines that, but originally their problem was that when any given DB dies, their estates and so on are likely to be carved up among a dozen heirs at the least, probably twice or more that. That and while they're located in the North in a rather nice space of farmable land due to a sorcerous working, the extent that the terraforming is progressing outwards is far too slow to manage their population. So either they start working on trying to conquer their neighbors (having the only large scale farmland in the area has made them a lot of allies nicely up until now) or they start having social breakdowns inside their borders as the younger scions start fighting over who gets what little there's left to own.
