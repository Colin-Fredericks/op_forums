    Something to bear in mind with questions like this is that there really isn't going to be one "right answer" that can be established. Saying "people will behave in this manner for sure!" goes against both reality (where people have free will, and can choose paths other than the most likely one), and good setting design (where you want enough freedom and flexibility in the world building that every chronicle can have a slightly different answer).

    So, pretty much every possibility between the two extremes of "the Great Houses are still fighting each other even as the Renewed Deliberative plants its flags in the Imperial City and atop Mount Meru" and "The Great Houses and every outcaste Dragonblood in the Threshold immediately closes ranks and takes a unified stance the moment a Solar or Lunar declares that they're planning to invade" is at least vaguely plausible.

    Of course, the most likely outcome is probably somewhere between those two extremes. Exactly how it actually happens depends greatly on the circumstances of the external threat, and, even more importantly, on the actions of the protagonists of the story, i.e. the PCs. The latter should be the biggest determinant of how things go down. A Circle of Dragonblooded should be able to overcome their families' factionalism and dislike of each other and get them to band together against the looming Anathema threat, or decide to take full advantage of the chaos, let the invasion happen, and then rally scattered DB forces to turn it back and set themselves up as the new Shoguns, Empresses, or whatever title they want. And a circle of Solars, Lunars, etc. should be able to carefully conceal their true nature long enough that the Great Houses don't realize that this is an Anathema invasion until after they've spent themselves on civil war, or use subterfuge and trickery and honest diplomacy to make some Great Houses think others are on the Anathema side, and exploit the civil war that way.

    In the absence of PC actions, though, I think the Great Houses' reactions, and whether they're willing to cooperate with each other or backstab, will depend on exactly how blatant the Anathema are in being behind the invasion. If the Bull of the North recovered tomorrow, and started conquering his way southwest towards Pneuma or straight south to Medo, and makes statements about how he plans to cross the sea and crush the Realm, most of the Houses would probably publicly put aside their differences, though some of them would probably at least think about trying to pull another Battle of Futile Blood on a different House. But since all the Houses are aware of the potential of that, and there's nobody to just command one of them to take the brunt, that's not as likely to be doable.

    Conversely, if an Anathema is more quiet, doesn't proclaim their true nature while building up a power base (or even if they do declare it openly, if their new empire is sufficiently far enough away from the Blessed Isle that they can't get there for a few decades), the Great Houses will probably focus more on defeating each other than fighting the Anathema threat.

    Another factor to think about in deciding whether the Realm unites against an Anathema threat or not is that this is 3e, and Dragon-Blooded are absolutely not pushovers or too weak to challenge "real" Exalted or anything like that. The Dragon-Bloods of the First Age were able to overthrow and imprison the Solars at the height of their power, slay a great many of the similarly-powerful Lunars, and force the rest back to the borders of Creation and keep them hiding in the shadows for centuries. Sure, the Wyld Hunt likes to kill Anathema as early as they can. But that's not because there's some kind of ticking clock of "if we don't off them before Essence 3, we literally can't do anything about them". The Realm, Lookshy, etc. can all reasonably look at the situation, even now with the return of the Solars, and say "we can wait a few months or years to deal with this problem, because right now, we've got bigger problems closer to home, with all the other Dragon-Blooded trying to kill me".
