            Korekore: obviously I don't need to tell my ST what I think but just for the record I agree with most of what korekore has here except that I would like "upwelling from oblivion" to be more in line with how Ignis "empowers" the solar exaltation. Yes they are influenced by him but he can't just yank on the solar leash and make them do what he wants. Solars tend to be like Sol because they get similar powers as him, but in Ex2 we never explored any other avenues for Oblivion powers other than "killing extras." I'd like to see Oblivion being more of a thematic influence and less an overbearing overlord in Ex3, with more end games than just "best murderer in Creation." Hence my idea where the "blackness" of the black exaltation comes from Oblivion and informs its charms, but that doesn't mean you have to take your character's lead from oblivion.

            Zelbinnean: thank you very much for sharing. Pretty much entirely on board with all your main points here. You and I do seem to be interpreting the canon for monstrances differently but I think we see the same problems. I also like the idea of treating it as a normal arcane link, with maybe an explicit understanding that a Deathlord always knows where you are through it (maybe skry-proof-proof?). Since I also am curious what we will get to use as triggers for the Great Curse in Ex3 I am a bit afraid to post any ideas here on how to add a carrot element to resonance but it's not a bad idea. Maybe in my version it could be that the black exaltation works through your lower soul and makes you more bestial when triggered, like a WoD vampire going crazy from hunger, but that indulging your hungers at this time gives willpower/motes?

            Nalak: you're getting angry because I'm telling you what you think and I'm getting angry because you keep telling me what I think. We should stop that. Here's what I actually want for abyssal revisions, I'd appreciate it if you posted what you wanted, not as a response to what I have here but simply as an expression of your hopes for Ex3:

            Resonance and Neverborn: no longer a tool of the Neverborn/Deathlords for making you play the evil psycho archetype. Abyssals might still be rooted in oblivion or the Neverborn but this is a thematic link and not "the NPC owns my character" bullshit. Resonance should still be very unpleasant for the character when triggered, as in Shards, but the triggering condition should have nothing to do with what a canon NPC wants from your character. Personally I like the idea of the abyssal exaltation itself being the source of resonance, but that this is not its idea of "punishment" so much as its way of motivating you to be all you can be. So if the Solar exaltation were like a really good personal assistant that got you everything you asked for the abyssal exaltation would be like a slightly psychotic drill sergeant barking at you to stop making excuses and do the thing. You get the same job done just as well though.

            Monstrances and Deathlords: I hate what they are in canon, and this thread has convinced me that scaling monstrances back to just arcane links would be the best thing. Let the Deathlords be scary enough on their own that just having them know where you are is threat enough to make you reconsider rebellion, which is only if you decided to make a loyalist in the first place. This should be completely optional.

                Resonance... I have mixed feelings on. On one hand, sure. It's a nice way to have something similar to, 'Reality hates your guts and it SHOWS'. But that's not what it is. It's, 'Your masters can kick you in your metaphorical nuts if you break their heavily restrictive rules'. On one hand, sure. Being bound directly to the neverborn is a HORRIBLE thing. But at the same time, you're not often. Pulling a number out of my rear end, my instinctive estimate is that there's 10, MAYBE 15 shards out there that don't have a monstrance. I haven't poked CoCD: Underworld, or MoEP: Abyssals that much. Dunno if they ever mention that. Anyway, back to resonance. I think that something as, 'DEATH DESPAIR GLOOM DOOM' as that is... OK? But it just feels like crap for crap's sake. Shoehorning you into a role (Unrepentant deathly supersoldier) for a reason I think we've all speculated on with varying degrees of rancor. I have no idea what it is, personally. The reason why I gave that a tentative, 'OK' is because it has bits and pieces of gothic lit type horror (Masque of Red Death-based resonance blight anyone?), which fit the theme of the Underworld very well. Just not abyssals.

                That having been said, I kind of want something like mounting penalties for operating in a place removed from the Underworld sooner than Resonance. Like, the more life-aligned a place you find yourself in the more it screws with you. Temple to TUS? "IT BURNS!" Wood manse? Twitching and running away. Creation? Mild unease, like mortals probably feel in the Underworld for other reasons like the general gloomy atmosphere... That can increase to pants-wetting terror when you see a necrotech beastie. I'm not talking about, 'Solar shows up, you fall down dead because hunter vs. vampire LOL'. I'm just talking about... I -guess- the idea of the, 'Revive Kills Zombie' trope if that makes any sense.

                Monstrances are a different story. Fundamentally, I have a weird appreciation for and hatred of them at the same time. On one hand, it DOES provide the classic, 'I have you now my pretty!' type situation. Bastila with Malak, Dorothy in the Wizard of Oz as mentioned, if you want a less torture-based but similar countdown to horrible deal. THAT is a good trope, fun to add an element of crisis. But at the same time... It vaguely feels like, 'Torture makes you evil'. Which I have mixed feelings about. I mean sure, you CAN go crazy from trauma, it's just a thing. But I don't like the idea of, 'Being tortured turns you into a baby-eating unrepentant monster'. It just feels like taking a victim and setting them up to get put through, 'Ron the Death Eater' treatment.

                Coupled with resonance as it exists now, I will admit. It does have a synergy between, 'Break you with the monstrance, rein you in with resonance'. But given resonance's problems, I just don't like the idea of it being able to so thoroughly screw over a player, taking them from functional hero one moment and putting them under a villain's thumb nigh-irreperably the next. Sure it would take a horrible ST to just rip any semblance of control away from your character like that? But the possibility that it could happen as part of the course of dramatic progression is a bit unsettling.

                Aaaaaand onto deathlords. Deathlords didn't get enough characterization. I feel like they should be their own, 'Thing' or class of entity. Painting them as uberghosts with solar charms and true immortality is stupid for a few reasons. First of all, it just... Kills how they feel mechanically. They don't feel like, 'I have deathly power over ghosts as a whole' any more than... Well, your average necromancer. Even their kill-stare is kind of weird, vaguely feeling like a necromantic spell. That having been said, given the lack of mysterious non-charm non-spell non-MA abilities as prevalent in exalted... This is one of their better points on a fluff-mechanics interaction basis to me.

                When playing an abyssal exalt, I have to say that I do prefer the idea of having a more meaningful connection to your liege. Before it just felt like picking a patron in D&D for your cleric. Distant and kindasorta there, but more a background fluff thing that informs your general and vague ethos than it does actually influence your immediate actions or your unique capabilities. I'm not asking for the hardcoded rules of resonance. More like, 'You have a few extra tricks exclusive to serving a given liege that only work in situations where they would act similarly'. Almost 2e's yozi charms with a deathly flair and less specific restrictions.

                    I can't remember exactly when this was first brought up but it's been confirmed for a while now.

                            Resonance: It should be possible to disengage this from ANY sort of guiding "intelligence" by simply saying that it is the "feedback" reaction that occurs when the unnatural energy of death that powers Abyssals runs up against life-affirming actions by the Deathknight. Done!

                            As to how to implement it mechanically, it seems that a lot of objections come down to players just not wanting Bad Things to happen to their PCs, and since the entire concept is that Bad Things Happen, maybe there's just NO way to model it without pissing off the players...

                            Monstrances: Canonically, there ARE Abyssals who don't have Monstrances, just as there are Deathlords who simply let their Exaltations fly free and exalt whoever they could find (personally, if anything, I dislike the notion that DLs can choose just *anyone*, even on the basis of their hair color!), so there's already more variety in the setting than some realize.

                            Restricting the DLs ability to reach the Monstrance might be a good idea, however; if they're all kept in the Labyrinth, so that one has to take a lengthy trip to get to them, that might give a Deathknight some breathing room. But personally, I like the notion that a Deathknight determined to go renegade has to come up with a plan to steal his Monstrance first...then maybe he wraps it in chains and drags it around behind him to put his enemies in...

                            Deathlords: With well-designed Arcanoi, from Ess 1 to 10 (and most ghosts being limited to 5), there's no reason why the Deathlords can't be dangerous, but defeatable, opponents while "just" being ancient, high Essence ghosts. Probably the biggest problem is that they can get pwned by anyone with Holy charms. Unless their Neverborn masters provide them with a constant defense against those. This can be tied into the Secret Weakness, which is something personal that offends the Neverborn enough that they temporarily withdraw their protection, exposing the DL to Holy attacks for the scene. Of course, you will want to give every splat some Holy powers, so as not to deprotagonize any of them...

                                    I think my list of acceptable resonance eruption effects would include:

                                    non-damaging but very obvious stigmata; aura of the pariah where social actions besides intimidate fail or are heavily penalized; non-damaging scenery damage like bleeding walls, peeling paint, rotting food etc; implementation of interesting but non-lethal taboos such as "can only survive on blood" for a set period; temporary memory loss or memory loops similar to ghosts during a haunting; heightened emotional states either for you or for people around you; exaggerated aversion to sources of life such as Creation, children, sunlight etc.

                                    Things I would do away with:

                                    health level loss, because I saw this get used way too often in situations where it either affected nothing or got characters killed; any and all reference to resonance being activated by an NPC with their own set agenda; mortal extra auto-kills, since this forced players to play tragic loners instead of encouraging difficult mortal-exalt relationships that drove the story; any reference to wound penalties, for the same reasons as my first exemption.

                                    Triggers: acting out of turn with your own desires, like a ghost trying to ignore it's fetters and passions. I like to imagine the exaltation super-charging your lower soul which freaks out if you don't do what it wants, hence the resonance effects. I'll admit that this mechanic is very very similar to the existing Great Curse though, and might need more to make it distinct. Ideas?
