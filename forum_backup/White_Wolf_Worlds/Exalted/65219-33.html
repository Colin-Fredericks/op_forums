    In 3e, Exaltations will still work without their progenitor's input. The difference is that said progenitors can still step in and direct them to Exalt chosen of their picking.

    This will also be true of the Fallen's Exaltations, but the Demon Princes and Deathlords are more active participants in the process. When they find someone that could resonate with their Divine Shards but hasn't attracted the attention of one, they will intercede. They will offer the person their "second chance" on conditions, applying a geas on the would be chosen if they agree.

    From this, Resonance and Torment spring.

    However, those chosen by a free-floating Shard without any input will have none of these restrictions apply.

                Hell has its holidays, and "Quietus" is one of the most popular. The constant cacophony of music and ambient noise in Malfeas never ends, for fear of Adorjan's dreaded touch...except once a year, when that Yozi, apparently exhausted from her ceaseless racing about, settles down to sleep for a period of five days, a deep sleep from whose dreams she cannot be awoken from.

                In this time only, the demons of Malfeas lay down their instruments and still their dancing limbs...and silence falls across the hellish landscapes. Only the sound of the grinding layers of Malfeas' stony flesh is heard, and even he, perhaps unconsciously, rests somewhat during this muted time. Conversations rarely rise over a whisper, and are often hushed altogether, with demons communicating solely through writing and other non-verbal means. Whether this is out of respect, superstitious fear of waking Adorjan, or just plain enjoyment of blessed silence depends on who you ask.

                There are two drawbacks to Quietus. First, is that Adorjan randomly chooses the time of her rest, so it almost never falls at the same time twice. This means that she could be halfway through it or more before the demon population even realizes it! Secondly, is that once she awakens, she is fully rested, and eager to be about once again. In her joy of blowing about, she WILL descend upon at least one sizable demon population, and NO sound, even those that normally keep her away, will suffice to prevent her from doing so (excepting, perhaps, the Demon-Wracking Shout). The rest of Malfeas' population simply shrugs, picks up their instruments, and once again resumes their otherwise relentless noise-making, glad to have survived yet another lottery, and hopeful for the next year.

                        Rise from your grave!

                        There was a period in the First Age were it was common for an Exalt to go around wearing little more than a loin cloth or chest bindings, as their own Charms provided them with all the protection against the elements (or assault) that they needed.                                                     • The first practitioner of the Quicksilver Hand of Dreams style was a Sidereal turned against Creation by things lurking deep in the Wyld. She taught the style to a young Sidereal after she reentered Creation, and when she turned against Creation rather than kill her he turned her into a dream and carried her inside of himself. Now her essence is spread across all practitioners of the art, as they share the burden of trying to heal her, using the style causes her essence to slip out, and the world forgets those who practice the style. (Basically the end of a major plot in a Sidereal game I ran)
                                                    • The body of Autochthon lacks the elements of Creation that make up the bodies of mortals. Using the plans for his own chosen Autochthon designed new bodies to house the souls he stole from the well of souls. Autochthonions are assembled in vat complexes before being decanted, and then raised to adulthood. They have brains of silicon crystal, muscles of cable and gears, and more. Soul gems grant them life and sentience, and their bodies lack a messy lower soul and are powered by their god instead. Should he die, their entire race dies. (Basically Autochthonions appear to be androids, which raises big questions for both sides when they enter Creation as to whether the other even counts as human.)
                                                    • Autochton has a limited number of souls and sub-souls that can oversee complex processes in his body. Knowing that humans are clever and adaptable, they exist within Autochthon to slowly take over overseeing functions of his body. There aren't a ton of jobs that consist of pulling a lever, some autonomous 3rd circle could handle that. Humans found cities at important and likely to malfunction organs and basically become pacemakers. They keep things working properly, take strain off of their god and find better and more efficient ways to do things.
                                                    • The eight nations of Autochthonia are just the current nations, over their history nations have risen and fallen, rebellions, civil wars, and cultural drift has led to very different cultures and technology developing between them. The tripartite exists in various forms, mostly because it provides a common way to understand and interact with rival nations. (I've got notes buried somewhere where I used different types of sci-fi dystopia as inspiration for the different nations.)
