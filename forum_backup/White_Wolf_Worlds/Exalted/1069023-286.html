                                                        Originally posted by shkspr1048 View Post
                                                        I'm having some difficulty with the text of Rampage-Berserker Attack (p.282). Could you give an example of the Charm's use in play?
                                                        1. Jotaro uses Rampage-Berserker Attack against Dio.

                                                        2. Jotaro makes a single withering attack roll and hits, gaining the 1 Initiative for doing so.

                                                        3. After comparing his attack roll to Dio's Defense, Jotaro determines how many total attacks the Rampage-Berserker roll represented, i.e. (1 + threshold successes).

                                                        4. Jotaro calculates damage normally, except that he doesn't add threshold successes to his raw damage. Once he's determined his post-soak damage pool, he rolls that a number of times equal to the total number of attacks.

                                                        5. Dio takes an onslaught penalty equal to the total number of attacks.
