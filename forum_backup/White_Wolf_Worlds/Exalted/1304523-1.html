    Link is the textbook Dawn, Solar, and Exalt in my mind. He's a hero, Hyrule's grandmaster-at-arms, reincarnating through the ages and granted divine power that manifests in a golden light. Now granted, my original estimation of Link's place in Exalted is derived from his Hyrule Warriors incarnation, who is manifestly the strongest version of the character. Also; Kirby might have more raw power, but Link is Nintendo's finest warrior, with only Samus being his equal in combat.

    I went with Infernal for Bayonetta, because her power is derived from a demon (Madama Butterfly; the giant hands and the shadow belong to her). The Mother characters are Infernals because, unless I'm terribly mistaken, all PSI is derived from Giygas.

    I made Samus an Alchemical not just because of her suit, but because her very biology is just as modular and open to modification as her Power Suit. I also went with Adamant Caste because she is so good at everything; combat, tactics, interactions with others, stealth, science... she is Nintendo's most capable hero.

    Also, I added Assist Trophies.

        Just because Bayonetta summons demons doesn't make her an Infernal necessarily; she also controls time as a major ability, which could point to Sidereal or Getimian. She just happens to also shapeshift is specifically into animals a lot, and has tons of Moon imagery throughout the franchise. Lunar sorcerer just happens to neatly fit everything but Witch Time, and even that might just be a spell or part of the Umbra Witch initiation, since it's just stopping time not traveling in it.

        (Also I hadn't even thought of her Shadow being her tell, but honestly that'd be super cool...)

        Makes sense for the Earthbound kids, but I still don't think Adamant specifically jives with Samus's aesthetic or MO. She doesn't do much with crystals or with being a stealthy undercover cop. She's a spec ops into Blight Zones, clearly, but not one that needs or wants to be undercover.

        For Assists, though:

        Skull Kid is a wood elemental, currently possessed by a third circle demon in the form of a Mask. (Or to pull out an old 2e homebrew based explicitly on Majora/Skull Kid: Achsam)

        Isaac is an Earth Aspect Sorcerer.

        Phosphora could be an Exigent under Viridi or an Air Aspect, or even an Air Elemental, to personal taste.

        Bomberman's role as a mascot and his color palette(s) would make me, personally lean Jade over Orichalcum.

                                                Some other thoughts on Touhou-As-Exalted:
                                                • Fairies are, by lore, very similar to 3e Elementals - beings springing up naturally out of, and representing, Nature. Though a lot of Fairies are hard to pin down as any particular kind of Elemental, they share an origin story, so if you wanted to rule that as more important than the fairy's specific nature, it's a route to go.
                                                • The abundance of gods in Touhou is probably a good introduction to how gods in Exalted works, to those unfamiliar with animistic religion
                                                • A lot of Youkai are hard to directly import to Exalted, as Youkai are directly fueled by the power of Belief, which isn't necessary to the existence of most sorts of beings in Exalted

                                                Side-Note about Reisen: her Insanity Powers (which are technically wavelength powers?) and Lunar Symbolism (plus the Rabbit Thing) make me want to lean No Moon with a variety of weird Mind and Illusion charms, plus some sort of "turn moonlight into a physical projectile" charm for her original Yu Yu Hakusho-esque Spirit Bullets. (Eventually used to upgrade attacks from the Moonsilver Firewand she gets in Legacy of Lunatic Kingdom) Plus her backstory of escaping the Moon to attain her own freedom feels like a Lunar exaltation.
