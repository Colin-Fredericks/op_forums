    My new Headcanon:
    There is a faith that is an offshoot of the Immaculate Order known as The Harmonious Path. It believes that there are only two dragons rather than five, one called Yagara who is completely white-scaled and Yinara who is completely black-scaled, and that they together balance Creation in harmony with Yagara controlling the Poles of Fire and Wood, and Yinara controlling the Poles of Water and Air, and their energies meet together to form the Pole of Earth at the center to stabilize all of Creation. Yagara is believed to pull the sun across the sky while Yinara pushes the moon, but since Yinara pushes the moon with her mouth, she takes some sort of bite out of it some of the time, on some nights even eating it entirely in her irresponsibility. Its adherents believe in seeing the harmony and balance in all of Creation and if one can see one's place within it and how to change it for the better to seize their own destiny with clarity and wisdom. In dealing with the spirits they try to seek ways for the spirits to be calmed down and work in harmony with everyone else, but will still try to defeat them if they do not cooperate. They however still hold to killing Anathema as they believe that the Anathema take away peoples abilities to choose their own destiny. They believe Dragon-Blooded are only special in that they found the enlightenment needed to determine their own destinies and not anyone else's.

    It is also a front for the Getimians in their war against Heaven and thus the Sidereals.

        So working on this Northgame notes, some other things which came up with the Bishop being one of the two major Deathlords about, thinking on the Shining Path and Abyssals in general. Something kind of talked about in previous editions is the idea of a Murderbuddha with Abyssals, but that it was fairly superficial way to go at things.

        With me recently having read most of Lord of Light (currently on hold for school year, but will probably finish over spring break), I kind of wondered what would be needed to build an actual Murder Buddhism, in a sense. So the Shining Path in my Creation is a lot of what folks say you can do with loyalist-to-the-Void Abyssals actually codified in a philosophy. Complete with considering living as part of samsara, ultimate annihilation as a form of Nirvana (though probably using the HIndu term for release, moksha instead just to make it less obvious to players) . In this setup, Abyssals are not just knights and holymen for the Bishop, but the ones who have really bought into it see themselves as a form of arhats. THey are enlightened beings who already will be annihilated on death. They stand then as guides to that path.

        So probably going to find ways to do a kind of death-flavored Eightfold Path and such, all while having a much more Catholic imagery basis to make it look nicely Exalted and Northernly. This will contrast with the again, "enemy of enemy" thing I want to do with Tear-Eaters, who I kind of get this more Incan or generally Mesoamerican thing with thier lich gods.

                        While we're on the topic of the Unconquered Sun...

                        Ignis Divine has fathered five children during his existence; no more, and no less. A set of triplets with Luna, a daughter by Merela, and Little Beam (probably but not definitely by Saturn). The triplets are the Golden Lord, the Pale Lady, and Janus, the god(dess) of eclipses. There's a great deal of speculation among Sidereals and certain gods in Yu Shan that these five children correspond to the five Solar castes; the first three being Zenith, Night, and Eclipse, the brawling Little Beam being Dawn, and Merela's daughter... kinda sorta maybe fitting into Twilight if you squint and tilt your head. Other say the child representing Twilight hasn't been born yet, and are working themselves into a frenzy trying to predict who the mother will be. Those who maintain that all five are represented already say that a sixth child of the Sun would either be his replacement, or a sign of the End of Days (or both!).

                        The Sun himself finds all this speculation and theorizing to be pointless and intensely embarrassing, and is genuinely confused about why his sex and family lives are of so much interest to everyone.

                                                        Streets and alleyways have their own little gods that help to maintain their domain through minor blessings and consuming vermin. They typically manifest in the form of an urban animal that lives in symbiosis with the human population but with a subtle touch of the supernatural to them.

                                                        In Nexus, for example they typically take the form of the urban fox but with a second tail while in Chiaroscuro they appear as housecats with unusually coloured eyes.

                                                        Normally the interactions of a street god are subtle, their 'priests' are normally elderly men and women who leave occasional offerings of food to help the local community flourish but some cities have designated urban shamans who build up a raport with several such gods gaining an information network in the process. There have also been instances of street spirits independently banding together to form their own courts - Nexus has gained an infamous one called the Fox Mothers that now operates a protection racket.

                                                            A relevant follow up:

                                                            Foxes in Nexus: Nobody knows quite how the urban fox came to be so prevalent in Nexus though the most credible theory is that they were introduced in great numbers by the Council of Entities as an ill-conceived attempt to combat the rodent problem. Though regarded as little better than the vermin they keep in check the fox has become an informal symbol of community in Nexus (foreigners would probably see this more as enlightened self-interest than genuine good will) with the saying "take care of the foxes and they take care of you" a reminder that the actions of little people have a way of adding up to greater consequences. As such it was only natural that the street gods of Nexus would take the form that they do and this saying was close to the heart of a young woman from Firewander who was chosen by Lunar.
