    Do you suppose there is room for a Solar Charm that lets you jump into a different persona when you use Battle-Fury Focus? Possibly also Ascendant Battle-Visage, and similar combat power-up Charms?

    Because I've always thought that Lunars had room for an upgrade to Dark Premise Duality, where activating Hybrid Body Transformation, Deadly Beastman Transformation, or Relentless Lunar Fury would allow the Lunar to instantly jump into (or out of) Shadow-Mind Meditation for the duration of the combat power-up Charm.

    Neither would let you change your combat Abilities, but you could gain other Charms potentially useful for combat. A Solar might cast aside his Performance Charms for terrifying Presence Charms, or his War Charms for leading men in exchange for Survival Charms for commanding his beast companion.

        Other Splats

        Let's look at how other splats approach the idea of becoming someone else!

        ​Dragon-Blooded

        The Terrestrial Exalted have a much more difficult time of compartmentalizing away their true selves. Still, it's startling what they can accomplish when they put their minds to it, and oftentimes in the Scarlet Dynasty, it's necessary.

        Loquacious Courtier Technique

        ​It's the Socialize Excellency, what more do you expect? Actually, though, it's got the Mute keyword. Pretty nice, if you ask me.

        ​Smoke-Wreathed Mien

        ​This one lets you sabotage other people's Read Intentions rolls for 3m.

        Watching the Salon's Shadows

        ​Few options exist to reduce or negate the penalty to your Guile for being unaware that you're under observation. Not only does Watching the Salon's Shadows boost your Guile against an unseen observer, it can grant you a roll to spot them, with double 9s.

        Smoke Without Flame

        Another Guile-booster, if the observer fails to penetrate your Guile, you can exaggerate the intensity of your apparent emotions or the context of an Intimacy you possess; a repurchase allows you to perform this exaggeration even if they beat your Guile.

        ​Ego-Dissolving Deception

        ​Spend an hour in meditation to rewrite one of your memories as a Psyche effect, drop the commitment to instantly restore your original memories. Very cool, much love.

        ​Enticing Flame Feint

        ​Protect your allies from read intentions actions, at a cost of 1 point of Guile per use.

        Implacable Dragon Mien

        ​A day-length Guile booster, this provides +2 Guile unless you want to hide a Major or Defining Intimacy based off an emotion; in Earth aura, it rises to +3 Guile, and even applies a +1 Guile for your emotion-based Intimacies.

        Rippling Mirror Face

        ​When someone is trying to read your intentions and fails, you can show them an Intimacy you discovered in someone else via read intentions earlier in the scene

        (For your consideration, there's also Wary Yellow Dog Attitude, which lets you use your Socialize in place of Awareness for Join Battle. Doesn't directly hide your intentions, but if you're going this deep into Socialize you'll want this Charm. Also worth looking at is Slippery Thoughts Technique in Integrity, in which you can substitute your Manipulation for Wits in calculating your Resolve. Pirate-Masquerading Method in Sail is handy for blending in at a wretched hive of scum and villainy.)​


        ​Lunars
        ​
        The Lunars are already good at physically becoming someone else, but what about when they want to blend in socially? Guile can be an important trait for a Lunar, as no matter how good your shapeshifting is, it's going to get awkward when you pose as an Immaculate and another monk uses Read Intentions to discern a Defining Tie of Reverence for Luna.

        The Changing Moon Anima Power

        ​Technically not a Charm, but it's an excellent start; if your anima is Dim, you get a constant +1 non-Charm bonus point of Guile. Very handy.

        Lunar Excellency

        Solid enough on its own, but gets much better when you remember that you can add another Attribute to your dice cap with an appropriate stunt. So make sure and describe how only a hero of legendary stamina could hold up this façade for so long, or how your quick thinking is all happening deep beneath the serene smiling surface, et cetera., and get those extra dice.

        ​Unless otherwise mentioned, assume the following Charms are in Manipulation.

        Moonlight Curtain Drawn

        ​Scene-length +1 Guile; if defeating your Guile would reveal information inconsistent with your current shape, it increases to +3 Guile. Not bad for 3m. A repurchase lets you extend its duration to one day.

        ​Cat-Eyed Enticement

        ​If you successfully assert Guile against someone in a scene, you can use this Charm to reroll a number of failed dice to influence that person.

        Argent Deceiver Smile

        Upgrades Moonlight Curtain Drawn - now if defeating your Guile would reveal you're trying to deceive someone, you can pay 2m to get the +3 Guile from Moonlight Curtain Drawn.

        ​Seven-Veiled Visage

        ​For one mote, (Essence) 1s on the opponent's roll to pierce your Guile subtract successes. If you're in a human shape the target has a Defining Intimacy towards, their failure to understand you throws them into despair, penalizing their social and mental dice pools for the scene.

        ​Sublime Façade Art

        You can substitute in Integrity, Larceny, or Performance in place of Socialize to calculate Guile, and can pay one mote to remove a point of penalty from your Guile (though you can't reduce the penalty for being unaware of your observer).

        ​Anemones Conceal Clownfish

        ​Boost your buddy's Guile, boost how high he can raise his Guile, and if he's your Solar mate, any motes he spends on his Guile are muted.

        Forgotten Self Wisdom

        ​Pay 3m to suppress an Intimacy you have that's incompatible with a form you're shapeshifting into. Ends if you leave that form, and if you use the suppressed Intimacy to bolster Resolve or for a decision point, you can't use the Charm again for the rest of the day.

        Labyrinth of the Beast

        Akin to the Solar Easily-Discarded Presence Method, if you successfully assert your Guile, and the person trying to read your intentions rolls at least one 1, they see only some animalistic drive (lust, hunger, et cetera.), and disregard you further. If you're in an animal shape, you can use it on even a successful read intentions (as long as they rolled at least one 1).

        Inconspicuous Feral Lifestyle

        ​As long as you're in an animal shape, acting like that animal, living the simple life of a beast, the cost of Labyrinth of the Beast falls to one mote, and can be used even against rolls with no 1s.

        River Reflects Desire

        ​After you assert Guile against a roll with at least one 1, the person trying to read you sees what they want to see, as opposed to the truth.

        ​Silver Mask Submersion

        ​Used when you assume the shape of a human to whom you possess an Intimacy, or an animal, you overwrite your personality with that of the shape into which you are sliding. You replace your Intimacies with those of the form you're taking (or a set appropriate to representing the animal's instincts), and repress any memories incompatible with your new identity. Now unaware of your shapeshifter nature, you can't voluntarily leave this form (though you still have full access to your Charms) until the story ends, though you can set conditions for the Charm to come to an end (such as a certain phrase being said, a character dying, or a certain amount of time passing).

        Will-Drowning Void

        Spend 6m to steal a Willpower from someone who fails to penetrate your Guile.

        Mind-Shattering Mystery

        Spend ​7m, 1 wp to roll to afflict a target who failed to penetrate your Guile with madness.
        ​
        Also worth considering:

        Inexplicable Mystery Incarnation

        ​Mute the motes you spend on increasing Guile, social influence, or read intentions, for 2m.

        Lying to the World

        Regain two motes when you successfully assert your Guile.

        Whispering Silver Serpent

        ​Supplement a lie with this Charm, and for the rest of the scene, it can't be uncovered with Read Intentions or Profile Character actions.

        ​Appearance: Mysterious Stranger Intimation

        ​+1 Guile against characters affected by Alluring Scent Technique.

        Intelligence: Crossroads Walker Entreaty

        ​+1 Resolve and Guile against spirits and fae, and moreover, bars them from attacking you as long as you and your buddies don't attack or piss them off.

        Wits: Shadow Mind Meditation

        ​Spend ten motes to and a Willpower to give yourself a "shadow-mind" that shares your Defining Intimacies (and your Intimacy for your Solar mate), but is only permitted negative Minor and Major Intimacies (nor is it permitted to voluntarily form them). Four hours of meditation lets you switch or from to the shadow-mind.

        Wits: Dark Premise Duality

        ​Your shadow-mind can have different mental and social Attributes from you.

                                                        When it comes to something like anima, I think that one's first approach to the persona Charms is not as a form of physical disguise, but about creating multiple configurations of your own personality for interacting with different groups.

                                                        Consider a scenario in which you meet with various factions, openly under your own identity and without any modifications to your appearance, and they all get different reads on you and different qualities that they can successfully appeal to. Maybe they even know that you're in contact with other groups, but each persona claims that to the others it's all a double agent act, and their genuine loyalty is to your group. How can that be disproven?

                                                        Or take away any form of dissembling at all. You become the monarch of a nation, and craft one persona for when meeting with the nobles, one for meeting with the civil service, one for the national diet, one for the common masses, and one for in private.

                                                        Which face is the original one? Which is the real one? Does it even matter?
