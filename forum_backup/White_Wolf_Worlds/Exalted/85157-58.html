                                                Heh, considering how the setting's religion places a certain value in ignorance, I just had the notion that Royalty using the blade of Want to cut infinity means that this cosmology's version of omniscience equates not to improbably being able to conceive of everything at once, but of being able to reduce everything down to the scope at which an individual mind can comprehend. In a metaphorical, deeply abstract way rather than literally destroying everything beyond one's perceptions; a sense of converting infinity into a comprehensible format in any given instance. Perhaps that is part of why it transcends causality; Zoss was still on the scope of a human being, but able to be anywhere he wanted and meaningfully perceive where he was simultaneously, so in any given interaction he can engage with something as though he has absolute knowledge, which is basically indistinguishable from him having such.

                                                ​A Wielder of Names shapes the world such that names are the truth of a thing rather than a comprehensible intermediary, at the same time as they shape themselves into a form that can encompass everything.
