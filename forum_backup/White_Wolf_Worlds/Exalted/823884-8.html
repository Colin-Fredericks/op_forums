        This is all I have so far ---- but I'd love some feedback:

        Acupuncturist’s Quills
        Artifact 3: Set of 20 slender Starmetal darts.

        The quills were created by a Chosen of Serenity for her Solar lover. Both were committed to attending the wounded in battles without taking sides. She was aware of the risks her lover faced in doing this work and created these quills as tools of medicine that had application in times of danger.

        Although fearsome weapons, the quills still hold to the principles of their creator and reward those who favor non-lethal solutions to conflict.

        Evocations of the Acupuncturist’s Quills
        These starmetal darts are drawn fatefully to the nerve clusters and chi meridians that direct energy through the body. When a Solar or Sidereal is attuned (5 motes) to them they get a +1 on all attempts to disarm and distract an opponent. Additionally, at the end of a scene all the quills are automatically returned to their cloth carrying case.

        Soporific Sting
        Cost 3m Mins Thrown 2 Medicine 2
        Type Supplemental
        Keywords Decisive
        Duration Instant

        By paying the cost for this evocation the quill secretes a powerful anesthetic that has the characteristics of Arrow Frog venom. This venom, however, causes no damage. If the victim crashes while under the effects of this anesthetic, they must pay 1 WP to prevent falling unconscious. This expenditure of willpower does not cure the venom; it only prevents the current threat of unconsciousness. If the victim crashes again while under the effects, the same penalties apply.

        If used medically the patient falls into a deep slumber that aids in healing. This provides +1 to any medical roll that involves a stunt using the quills.

        This evocation cannot be bought. It awakens automatically the first time the bearer of the quills defeats a powerful opponent and chooses to spare their life.

                        Thorn's Embrace
                        Cost: 2m/3m Mins: Ess 1
                        Type: Simple
                        Keywords: Perilous
                        Duration: Scene
                        Thorn's wielder feeds it with essence, and Thorn grows to encompass its host with hard roots and thick vines. The wielder gains the benefit of medium mortal armour, though the roots move with her, removing the mobility penalty. The wielder may coax Thorn to grow a particular way, styling the armour as they wish.

                        For an additional mote, they may cause roses to bloom on the armour, reducing the difficulty of Appearance based rolls by 1 as the wielder is suddenly clothed in blood red, purest white or occasionally stranger, equally vivid colours. The roses never blend in with the background, and even change colour to avoid doing so which increases the difficulty of stealth rolls by 1, by 2 in areas where plant-life is uncommon and by 3 in areas where plants should not normally be able to grow.

                        Thorn's Jealous Rebuke
                        Cost: 5m Mins: Ess 3
                        Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Counterattack, Perilous, Decisive-Only
                        Duration: Instant
                        When fed with sufficient essence, Thorn lashes out to protect its prey. When its host is attacked while under the effects of the previous charm, Thorn suddenly sprouts dozens of large spikes which pierce the attacker and his allies, allowing the host to roll a normal decisive attack and apply it to the attacker as well as any other enemies within close range. This charm may be activated in response to an Ambush or Surprise attack, provided the host has already been embraced by Thorn after activating the previous Evocation.

                        Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens immediately the first time the wielder suffers a wound penalty of -4 after an attack while under the effect of Thorn's Embrace. The very first activation may be free if the attuned cannot pay the cost of the evocation themselves.

                            If the exalt chooses to make an Inspire action, they attempt to inspire Awe in the unbridled mastery of Martial Arts that the wielder of Harmonious Savant possesses.                                     Thanks for all the great feedback Unsung Hero.

                                    I based the effects and mote cost of the first evocation off of Spring Razor's Howling Lotus Strike. For 3 motes it adds Arrow Frog Venom to the sword strike. I was aiming for a less lethal version of that effect.

                                    As for the magical materials I went with Starmetal because I liked the idea that the darts were drawn to pressure points and nerve clusters as if by fate -- but after thinking about it your suggestion for Green Jade makes a lot more sense.                                                         The Evocations of The Principle of Cutting
                                                        Cutting the Earth
                                                        Cost: 3m. Mins: Ess 2
                                                        Type: Supplemental
                                                        Keywords: Perilous, Decisive-Only
                                                        Duration: Instant
                                                        The Principle of Cutting is born of the narrative law of the Wyld, and binds the Fate of Creation to its narrative. Therefore, when faced with a foe whom its wielder directs it to cut, it will cut. This Evocation supplements a Decisive attack, halving the foes Hardness against the attack as the blade draws him into its narrative. Furthermore, should the Foe survive the attack, his natural soak is reduced by an amount equal to his wound penalty after the attack. If he is wearing non-artifact armour, his armoured soak is reduced by the same amount. These reductions last until the wounds are healed and the armour is repaired. Thus, a person with a natural soak of 3 and an armoured soak of 8 from normal armour who suffers from a -2 wound penalty after the attack would reduce his natural soak by 2 and his armoured soak by 2, lowering his soak from 11 to 7. If he was suffering from -4 penalties he would reduce it by a total of 8. If ANOTHER source of damage increased his wound penalties, including if he elects to suffer a crippling injury, his soak is not reduced until the wielder uses this evocation again.
                                                        Cutting the Mountain
                                                        Cost: 1m. Mins: Ess 2
                                                        Type: Supplemental
                                                        Keywords: Perilous
                                                        Duration: Instant
                                                        Prerequisite: Cutting the Earth
                                                        With greater understanding of the sword's narrative, the wielder learns how to draw even the greatest of foes and obstacles. This Evocation requires that the Wielder have an initiative of 10+. When he is, he may pay 1 mote to ignore the effects of the Legendary Size merit, or to launch an attack against entire structures such as houses, towers and mansions. He may even cut down a wall. However, the cut grows no wider, though they do extend for a greater distance. Thus, cutting a hole in a wall requires multiple cuts.
                                                        Cutting the Heavens
                                                        Cost: 10m, 1wp, 2a. Mins: Ess 4
                                                        Type: Simple
                                                        Keywords: Perilous
                                                        Duration: Scene
                                                        Prerequisites: Cutting the Mountain
                                                        The Wielder draws the narrative close, and intertwines it with fate as he raises the sword above his head. His Anima is drawn into the tangle of threads and as he brings it down, he plunges everything out to medium range into a realm of his own creation. The realm is coloured by his Anima and the narrative of the Sword. Within the realm, he suffers no onslaught penalties and may move to any point within the realm as he pleases. He may draw up barriers to protect his allies or to create obstacles for his foes. He may launch an attack against any individual within the realm. However, as the realm is bound to his soul, he may also be attacked by anyone within it as if he were a dematerialized spirit.
