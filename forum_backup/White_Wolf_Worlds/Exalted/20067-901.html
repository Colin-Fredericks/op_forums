            Originally posted by Ranger_Lord View Post
            Also, Become the Hammer says it can add dice to Brawl or Martial Arts based attacks and Brawl and Martial Arts based Parry, but it doesn’t specifically say it can be activated with Martial Arts Charms. Common sense says it can, but why leave that out of the text?
            Not a dev, but this is an easy one.

            Firstly, 3E is a natural language system that explicitly has a rule around applying common sense.

            Secondly, take another look at the actual rule on page 426.

            "Martial Arts Charms are not compatible with Brawl, or any other combat Ability"

            Become the Hammer is not a Martial Arts Charm. No conflict there.

            "unless they explicitly state otherwise"

            Become the Hammer explicitly states it can add to Martial Arts attacks.

            "Martial Arts cannot be combined with other combat
            Abilities"

            The prohibition is not using Brawl Charms and Martial Arts Charms; what's forbidden is mixing the abilities period. You don't get your Brawl Specialty on your Martial Arts attack. You can't use your Martial Arts Form Weapons with Brawl. And you can't use your Brawl Excellency on Martial Arts attacks. Unless there's a specific exception. Become the Hammer has a specific exception.

            It doesn't need a rule saying it can be combined with Martial Arts Charms specifically, because the rule you want an exception for doesn't actually exist. (The actual rule is far more prohibitive.)

                                                            Not really.

                                                            The Attunement section of chapter nine mentions making a 40 pound golden sword feel light as a dagger and swift as a breeze. That's crazy heavy for a sword.

                                                            ---

                                                            Wyld Shaping Technique specifies that it takes five talents of magical material to craft a grand daiklaiv. A talent of Jade is a thin slab weighing 68 pounds (about 31 kilograms).

                                                            That would be roughly ten times as massive as an historical zweihander, and that's not factoring in what the material would be alloyed with, whether their is significant lossage in the crafting, or most importantly the fact that a grand daiklaiv is usually portrayed as too ridiculously oversized for the comparison to have any meaning.

                                                            ---

                                                            So, basically, magical materials make osmium seem lightweight by comparison. But by how much....? Eh?
