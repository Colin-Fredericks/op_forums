                                                        Keywords: None
                                                        This slick, colorless gem so resembles water that it’s
                                                        invisible when immersed. When mounted in an attuned
                                                        hearthstone socket, the stone offers the opportunity to
                                                        awaken the following Evocations:
                                                        Aquatic Adventurer’s Spirit
                                                        Cost: —; Mins: Essence 1
                                                        Type: Permanent
                                                        Keywords: None
                                                        Duration: Permanent
                                                        Prerequisites: None
                                                        Like an otter, the Exalt is as comfortable in water as
                                                        on land. She adds two non-Charm dice to aquatic
                                                        movement actions, and measures how long she can
                                                        hold her breath in minutes instead of rounds. Armor
                                                        socketed with the hearthstone gains the Buoyant
                                                        tag.
                                                        Benthic Depths Adaptation
                                                        Cost: —(3m, 1i); Mins: Essence 2
                                                        Type: Permanent
                                                        Keywords: None
                                                        Duration: Permanent
                                                        Prerequisites: Aquatic Adventurer’s Spirit
                                                        The Exalt calls the ocean’s floor home. She measures
                                                        how long she can hold her breath in hours instead
                                                        of rounds, and neither the cold nor pressure of the
                                                        deep can harm or impede her. Furthermore, whenever
                                                        she successfully dodges an attack underwater,
                                                        she may spend three motes, one Initiative to move a
                                                        single range band downward.

                                                            We had a 1e charm that did this. Allow me to convert it.
                                                            Fish Swimming Technique

                                                            Cost​: 3m; Mins​: Athletics 2 Essence 1; Type:​ Reflexive
                                                            Keywords​: None
                                                            Duration​: One day
                                                            Prerequisite charms​: None

                                                            The Solar swims as well at those who call the ocean home. Upon activating this charm the Solar becomes able to swim in the water as well as he can move on land, suffering no penalties to moving in water. He may move or down a range brand underwater as a single action, including leaping one range brand out of the water to surprise the Solar’s foes. The Solar also reduces the difficulty of all swimming checks by 1, minimum 0. Lastly any speed enhancement charms that would function on land function just as well in the water itself.

                                                            At Essence 4, the cost of this charm is reduced to 0m.
