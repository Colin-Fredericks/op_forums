                    Has anyone managed to come up with consistent rules for when actions taken with one ability can benefit from a charm under another ability? Or is a case-by-case basis and ask-the-devs if unsure the best we've got?

                    (I say abilities because the restrictions for Lunar charms and the Lunar charms themselves are written a lot more consistently than the restrictions and charms are in the core.)

                    In the core the closest thing we get to hard rules for when you can and can't use charms of one ability with another are under the descriptions of the different Types of charms (Simple, Supplemental, Reflexive, Permanent) but the section has some problems.

                    To illustrate lets take the section on reflexive charms:

                    Originally posted by Core
                    A reflexive Charm creates a reflexive action or enhances a non-dice action, such as applying Defense against an attack, or holding one’s breath. In some cases, such as reflexive Charms to anticipate surprise attacks or to passively notice hidden details, the character may not even realize she is using the Charm until she already has! Characters may use any of their reflexive Charms at any time, so long as it makes sense for them to do so, but they can’t “stack” enhancing reflexive Charms in the same way they can’t stack supplemental Charms. Reflexive Charms which create attacks generally do so with the Ability they belong to, unless the governing Ability can’t normally attack or unless otherwise noted. Reflexive Charms which enhance static values may generally only enhance static values deriving from their governing Ability, unless otherwise indicated. For example, Dipping Swallow Defense will only remove penalties from a Melee-based Parry attempt.
                    Proplem: A number of reflexive chams neither create an action or enhance a non-dice action, f.ex. Heaven-Thunder-Hammer and Hail-Shattering-Practice which enhance an attack (a dice action) and penalize an enemy attack (which isn't any kind of action on your part) respectively. For these charms the rules are pretty much up in the air for when you can and can't use them unless they actually spell that out in the charm text (HTH does, HSP does not).

                    Now 'Charms can generally only enhance actions or static values based on their associated Ability, unless otherwise indicated' seems like it would be a good general rule (that's the rule in lunars with Attribute swapped for Ability) but since it's not a rule charms in the core have been written under, it has its own problems and f.ex. leaves all those sense-enhancing Awareness charms only benefitting Awareness rolls even when it would seem like having superhuman senses would help (f.ex. they wouldn't be able to negate sense-based penalties to non-Awareness rolls).
