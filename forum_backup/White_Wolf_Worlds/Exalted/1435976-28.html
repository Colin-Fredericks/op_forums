                                                    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                    I believe the preview document provided during the core book Kickstarter posited Mind-Hand Manipulation as the sort of power an Infernal could get from a shintai transformation, which were posited as things that could be custom built out of elements provided by their Charm purchases.
                                                    This was, it should be noted, under the previous devs. Vance's comments from a few years back on the fan Discord had them considering MHM for Infernal Lore or Occult, with a slight lean to Lore. We'll see if the idea of Sidereal House Charms has any effect on their thinking.

                                                    As for Devil-Body transformations (shintai's been deprecated in favour of Devil-Body), which we have more recent information on, the general idea currently seems to be that you buy the base Charm and get a number of points to use to customise it, and stuff that's likely to be included (unless balance doesn't work out) is other Charms increasing the number of points, adding new ways to trigger it, and allowing you to activate it more frequently, and going the other way, Devil-Body acting like Protean for Lunars and enhancing certain Charms.

                                                    Getting a specific Devil-Body feature to unlock a specific Charm, as with the previous devs' idea for MHM, is something Vance sees little reason to do except for a limited range of concepts and effects.
