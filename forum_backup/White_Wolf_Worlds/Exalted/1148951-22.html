            Holy shit, what a goddamn letdown. This is Peak Bad Shit.

            Random thoughts:
            Why is Leo such a bad fighter? I get that he's a coward, but he's still a headmaster. Oscar is still relatively weak.

            Why the split up? We had group fights in S1 S2 S3, they couldn't pull that off again? I know there's been some behind-the-scenes drama but why did this show get so goddamn bad?

            The only good animation bits were Weiss bullet whacking and Juane's Camera-Sword opener. Everyone else just does Swing-Swing-Jump back and talk about our feelings. This isn't RWBY, its a low budget Saturday morning cartoon from a developing country.

            Other than Weiss, can you show me one distinct fighting style from this fight? 'member when Yang fought Neo? 'member when we had the food fight? I 'member. Remember Team CFVY and how we learned about their personalities just from a little bit of watching their fighting styles and team interaction? Where the F did that level of character detail go?
            EDIT: Just started watching Season 3 tournament arc again, and it's INSANE how much better those fights are. For shame, RWBY devs.

            What is StrongMan McReluctance's deal? He doesn't have a weapon, but he can just... block hits real good? he can't put down Lie Ren and Nora? He's on the same Evil Pay Grade as Tyrion and (maybe) Cinder. Why couldn't he just roflstomp half the team? Even if he really just doesn't want to fight- why do show him effortlessly aikido'ing Lie Ren and Nora and imploring them to stay down? Literally nothing is conveyed about his character, personality, or abilities- and not in a mysterious way. For all I know he just has constipation.

            Ok, Jaune. Let's talk Jaune. When he gets righteously mad there's a little "ching" sound effect. Next episode is going to be his Semblance, which I've suspected for a long time is Paladin/Group Heal. So far, so good. I like that they worked his Pyrrha emotions into finally seeing Cinder again. But... why the hell does he just fall over in the fight with Cinder? She gets hit with Ruby's Silver Eye Hero Power, Jaune misses her with a stab, and then just... falls over. Cinder leisurely stands up and puts a foot on him, and then calmly walks away for the big finish attack. Jaune hasn't even been hit yet! Oh wait, I'm sorry, he get's knocked down once while Cinder is evil gloating, before he gets back up and keeps fighting. Did he push himself too hard? He fought harder against Cardinal and still was picking himself back up. Is that fall supposed to be him overbalancing? we've seen his footwork and basic swordsmanship skills rise throughout the series. He charged, stopped when Silver Eyes hit, and then attacked again immediately. So Whiskey Tango Foxtrot? It's 3 seconds before Cinder get's up, and then 3 more for her to walk over to him. He was literally just full-charging at her, was he supposed to be hanging on by a thread there? When she gets off of him, why doesn't he grab his sword and try to fight back- the sowrd he was trying to reach when she was on top of him? Jaune literally does not give up. That's his whole schtick. So why is he suddenly helpless and about to be ko'd? But we gotta get that final scene.

            Any special reason Cinder attacks Weiss?. If you stop the scene right at 13:34 you can see the layout of the room. Ruby is down, Cinder hates Ruby, and Ruby is right next to her. But she attacks someone across the room, over Ruby's prone body? Oh, and Yang, Qrow, Lie Ren, Nora, and Oscar don't have a chance to do something dramatic to save her? Also, this is super petty, but the spear toss animation looks kinda off. Weiss was across an entire ballroom.

            Hey, remember when a bunch of people fought another bunch of people and Jaune turned out to be really, really good at making Team Tactics? Or remember when Ruby, Weiss, and Yang showed off that they had combo attacks? No? That's ok, the Devs do not remember it either.

            Remember when a 4-on-4 fight was a 4-on-4 fight and not a bunch of nonsensical 1-on-1s? ya member?

            I am super not a fan of Ruby's Silver Eyed So Special Protagonist Power, by the way. I didn't like that get out of jail free shit back in S3 and I don't like it now. She has Anti-Villain Magic, and it's an unblockable wave. Kinda lame. It invalidates fights.

            No really, Oscar is just Whacking at Leo like an old lady with an umbrella. Professor Port and Oobleck would have handed that kid his shit, but Headmaster Leo can't? yeah yeah, psychology. Yeah yeah, headgame. Leo's opening move is to stand still, fire up his Duel Disk, Summon Exod- dial in fire, dial in rock, and then shoot it straight ahead at someone basically 3 meters away. A+ effort on that. Just Horseshit.

            No Qrow/Raven, ok. sure. Didn't hype that up or anything.

            Whiskey Tango Foxtrot is up with two people holding swords to each other and talking. It's super goddamn lazy. In Food Fight it lasted half a second between Pyrrha and Blake and then it fed into a fakeout/attack. This time two of the most dangerous characters in the series are just chatting while sword pressing each other.

            Lie Ren and Nora don't fight like a co-ordinated pair. They date now, c'mon guys. you built that up a whole lot. Pay it off. Even in the tournament arc they had some pairwork.

            Yang and Mercury don't fight. Big let down.

            Emerald has an amazingly useful semblance and doesn't use it enough. Legit she should have made Ruby swipe Jaune (disguising him as Cinder), that would have been good. She still does sue it, but c'mon. that's A game material right there.

            Not really a this episode thing, but why doesn't Nora ever lighting-dust herself for super strength? She has a massively useful semblance that is only ever used twice due to sheer chance. Lightning dust exists!

            Ruby and Emerald fight in a circle about 4, 5 meters wide. Ruby once speed-shot through a 100 meter cafeteria (without her gun) just to sweep up debris. She can't, i don't know, retreat? Jump back, and then make a couple of shots at Cinder with her goddamn Sniper Rifle!?

            Why doesn't anyone, literally anyone, team up?

            Why break up into 1 on 1 fights if you aren't going to give us 1 on 1 fights? It's literally just Weiss and Vernal.

            Weiss makes an ice block about ten feet wide as a time buyer, but she's in a giant open ballroom, and Vernal cuts through it. Ok, I'll actually forgive this one as being an in-character mistake. You know what could have helped? Lie Ren, Nora, or goddamn anybody trying to buy her some time. She's getting her ass kicked!

            Can we also talk about who fights who? Yang+Mercury, Qrow+Raven, and Oscar+Leo make sense. Jaune wanting to fight Cinder makes sense, but why anyone lets him doesn't. Lie Ren and Nora are his nakama. they have insanely good group dynamics and a lot of trust. Why don't they help him fight the woman who killed Pyrrha? Why is Ruby incapable of getting passed Emerald?

            whenever people are walking it still looks kinda janky.

            Ok, something Nice: Weiss doing the bullet-ballet was pretty rad. I like it. The quick shot in the background of Mercury and Yang darting around was pretty great as well, even though the exchange lasted about 1 second. The animation was ok, but the choreography felt a little more like s1. Still not at the level of s2 train, but not bad.

            Weiss reaction shot at the end was really well done. I like the futility of the hands- nice work team.

            annnnnd, yeah ok. those are my specific impression of this episode.

            This season is such a goddamn shitshow of a shit show. The fights are worse animated, worse directed, nonsensical, and less often. The plot has never been great but the show absolutely sucks at introducing things and the creators have no goddamn idea what they are doing. The pacing is bad, the dialogue is bad, the characterization is bad, and more important that ANY of that:

            They joy has gone out of the show. I don't feel the passion due to a Labor of Love. I feel a product designed to be cheaply produced and slotted into a lineup.
