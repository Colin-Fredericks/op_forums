                            Originally posted by Lanic View Post
                            Another one: how about Solar vs 3 Shikari, but now with Solar at Essence 5 and shamelessly combat-optimised?
                            Well this is pretty easy. The DB's stats don't really matter, I just assumed pretty well max for everything, with a few relevant stats at the top. It's a Solar vs 4 DBs, a wood archer, a water brawler, a fire swordsman and an earth brawler.

                            Fight

                            Solar attack 14 (17 damage)
                            Wood soak 10


                            Solar using 15m to full excellency and Sensory Acuity Prana, Rolling 23 dice for 16, getting 19i

                            Wood rolls in with a 4m for 2 success join battle, rolling 10 dice for a total of an impressive 14i.

                            I'll roll the water/fire/earth DBs if it matters


                            Solar 19i 26per 35iph 3a
                            Wood 14i 36 motes


                            Solar starts off the fight with 7m 1wp Heaven Sword Flash, 5m Fire and Stones Strike, 1m Divine Executioner Stance, 1wp Perfect Strike Discipline, 3m Excellent Strike, 4m 1wp Thunderbolt Attack Prana and willpower. The DBs will defend with a full excellency and stunt with willpower.

                            Rolling 23 dice against a max parry of 11 for 13, but with an added 1m Rising Sun Slash adding another 3, hitting by 5. Regaining 4 motes due to Foe-Cleaving Focus

                            Solar 19i 9per 39iph 3a
                            Wood 14i 36 motes

                            Then rolling damage. Rolling 19 damage for 10, doubling for 20, all close ranged DBs are slain.

                            Solar 6i 9per 39iph 3a
                            Wood 14i 36 motes

                            Solar uses his reflexive move to close into range with the wood aspect and withering with 1m 1wp 3a Peony Blossom Attack, with 1wp Perfect Strike Discipline, 5m Fire and Stones Strike, 3m Excellent Strike, and 1m Divine Executioner Stance. Wood will defend with a 6m full excellency and willpower.

                            Rolling 24 dice against an evasion of 10 and getting 7, with a rising sun slash for 1m adding another 5 and hitting by 2. Getting 2m back from Foe-Cleaving Focus

                            Solar 6i 0per 39iph
                            Wood 14i 30 motes


                            Rolling 18 damage for 9.

                            Solar 16i 0per 39iph
                            Wood 5i 30 motes


                            Solar triggers 3m One Weapon Two Blows, throwing another 10m excellency, 5m Fire and Stones Strike, and 1m Divine Executioner Stance. Wood defends with 6m excellency and a willpower for 9 evasion. Rolling 24 dice for 7, rerolling 6 1s for 3, and without a Rising Sun Slash only hitting by 1. Rolling 17 damage for 8, crashing Wood

                            Solar 30i 0per 20iph
                            Wood -3i 24 motes


                            Solar will now use 5m 1wp Invincible Fury of the Dawn, with 1m Divine Executioner Strike, 3m Excellent Strike, 4m Fire and Stones Strike, 4m 1wp Thunderbolt Attack Prana and 3m 2i Hungry Tiger Technique. Splitting his initiative 6/6/7/7/7

                            The Wood will split her remaining essence into excellencies 6/6/4/4/4


                            The Solar rolling 15 dice against evasion 7, hitting with 8. Rolling 12 damage for 3, doubling to 6.

                            The Solar rolling 15 dice against evasion 6, hitting with 9. Rolling 16 damage for 4, doubling to 8.

                            The Solar rolling 15 dice against evasion 4, rolling 5 and just hitting. Rolling 14 damage for 6, doubling to 12.

                            The Solar rolling 15 dice against evasion 3, hitting with 9. Rolling 22 damage for 9, doubling to 18.

                            The Solar rolling 15 dice against evasion 2, hitting with 6. Rolling 20 damage for 9, doubling to 18.



                            And with 62 health levels of damage to the Wood aspect I would say slain.


                            Well that's it in a single round.
