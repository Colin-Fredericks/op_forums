    So I have no idea how to judge encounter difficulty...

    07-16-2020, 10:24 PM
    ...so I've mostly been trying to go with what makes sense in-world, which has been working well so far. But as of last session, the local authorities are fully aware that the players are Solars and acting against them, and have logically decided that the best option is to hit them with overwhelming force. And now I feel like I kind of need an idea about how rough the fight is going to be, should it happen. So, uh... what kind of odds am I looking at here? Will the battle wind up being easy, risky, or overpowering?

    The players are all well spec'd for fighting-- they've all got good Dex and combat skills. All three are Essence 1 with 25 experience points. Most of their local support is in spy networks and public opinion, but they recently co-opted a sorcerer and the eight Agata she'd summoned.
    • The Eclipse has a Throwing Charm or two, Blinding Battle Feint, and enough stealth to do pull off combat stealth.
    • The Zenith has something like a half dozen Melee Charms-- I remember Excellent Strike, Fire and Stones, One Weapon/Two Blows, Glorious Solar Saber, and Dipping Swallow. She's been wrecking a lot of face in encounters so far.
    • The Dawn took a blend of White Reaper and Righteous Devil Dawn-- he's partway down both trees, and has an artifact weapon that functions as both spear and firewand (with a reflexive-loading Evocation).

    The opposition has limited resources, what with being on an island, and the players have been picking away at them for a while--just last session they smashed the last of their mortal soldiers. But there's plenty of supernatural power...
    • Two Dragonblooded, one Essence 3 Air aspect and one Essence 2.Earth aspect.
      • The Air aspect has the Breath Seizing Technique, Wrathful Winds, and Wind Dragon's Speed Charms from the core book. He's also got custom charms to extend his melee attacks as far as medium range, and to make a withering attack against all within close range (without gaining any of the initiative). He's got an artifact chain shirt.
      • The Earth aspect has Shattering Strike and Earthshaker Attack from the core book. He's also immune to being shoved around and has a huge +8 Soak/Hardness vs one attack charm.
    • A size 1 battle group of Immaculate Monks
    • A size 1 battle group of golems

    Many thanks for the help!

              Dawn.

              The PCs are asleep in their beds.

              The Earth Aspect and Golems are beneath them, sappers. They collapse the building. Roll (Perception+Athletics-3*) -- five successes means they notice what's going on and brace against the collapse taking no damage, three successes and they take a Short range fall and are left Prone, fewer than three successes and they take the full Medium range fall and are Prone. Regardless, the characters are left in a pit under their residency. This kills the sorceress and frees the agata, or at least incapacitates her and the agata fly her to safety abandoning the PCs.

              The pit is lined with pitch, which the Earth Aspect ignites. The Earth Aspect Withdraws through the sapping tunnels, collapsing them behind him. The PCs fight the golems (who have been rendered fireproof, and have Elite drill and Might 2) in the midst of a ranging bonfire hazard. The Air Aspect uses Voice on the Wind to deliver Command actions to the Battlegroup from a safe distance.

              The golems explode on death. The explosion knocks anyone in Close range Prone and releases cursed golem dust. Anyone in Short range must roll to resist poison equivalent to Curare as they risk inhaling the dust.

              Once the golems are defeated, it's only a Difficulty 2 roll to climb out of the pit. Impress upon the PCs that they really want to pass that roll. If the PCs fail/refuse, point out that they're below sea level on an island and at any moment the Dragon-Blooded could flood them out with seawater and drown them like dogs.

              Both Dragon-Blooded and the monks are waiting for them. The Earth Aspect will use Smashing attacks to try to knock the PCs back into the pit (Short range fall). The Abbott of the monks will use Command actions on the Battlegroup. The Air Aspect does their thing.

              If things really go south for the players, the Agata can rescue them -- but being unpredictable, flighty beings, this can't be relied on. If you need the sorceress to live, then don't have her and the demons bunk with the PCs.

              *No, the players may not substitute for (Wits+Dodge)

              ****

              That ought to give you a "risky" encounter without over much playing unfair.

              For an overwhelming encounter, have the sorceress betray them, and attack from the back of an Agata in mid-air in the final battle, no roll to avoid the fall without Charms, replace the burning pitch with lava, have the golems Ambush the PCs in the pit, climbing out of the pit now means closing Medium to Short to Close through Difficult Terrain, have the Immaculates pepper the PCs with poison arrows as they climb out the pit, and give the Air Aspect Yozi Venom and start them in Stealth.

              If you wanted to post the character sheets, I'm happy to advise further.
