    MadLetter's Charm Cascades - Lunars, Solars, Dragon-Blooded

    03-17-2019, 05:43 AM
    Hello there, folks!
    Once more the Kickstarter has come and (soon) gone, this time for the Lunars. That means one thing for me: Lots of work ;_;
    I have already begun working on the Cascades, and hope to be faster this time around than with Dragon-Blooded.

    A Note on my Forum Laziness
    As prior threads on the Onyx Path forums have shown, I am really bad at keeping this post up to date. I hope to remember it better this time around. Be aware that at all times you can get the full cascades over at the reddit thread (stickied now) here: Reddit

    If you want to contact me, I recommend reddit or better-yet on Discord (see end of this post).

    Typos and Errors
    If you see any typos or other errors, please make a note: in my GoogleDocs File

    Current Status of All Cascades

    Lunars are currently an ongoing Work-in-Progress, see below.

    Dragon-Blooded cascades have been created for all Abilities, but no Martial Arts yet. The documents still contain the wrong page numbers. Once the print version is out, I will change that. Since no Companion material has been released yet, I have had no chance to add them in.

    Solar cascades are on Version3 and await a round of bugfixes if I can get my ass off the chair and actually do it. Currently still lacking Arms of the Chosen content.
