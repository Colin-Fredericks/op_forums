            Originally posted by Ekorren View Post
            My questions are as follows:
            • Q1) What do you want to see in these books that you haven't really seen before?
            • Q2) What do you want to make grand comebacks from previous editions?
            • Q3) What don't you want to see in these books?
            • Q4) What do you want to discuss or hype about these books?
            Good questions

            1) I'd like to see some solid Patrician details (Isle and Threshold based). I'd be very happy if 3 families were named in each of the DB book's House write ups, even if it was just a single line. Then the Patrician families could have expanded details in the Realm book. Plot hooks would be nice, but 'how' the Patricians conduct business and seek to attract/hold favor with the Great Houses is something worthy of exploration. Are they just as cut throat as the Houses? Do they meddle in Threshold affairs? And what sort of influence can they bring to bear? In essence, they need to be more like the colorful Banner Houses from a Song of Ice and Fire (IMO) than the wealthy wallflowers from previous editions.

            2) Fun/useful stuff like the Palace of Tamed Storms, an 'updated for 3e' version of 2e's Chapter 7 Storytelling, and NPCs like the villainous Peleps Deled to stir things up.

            3) I don't want to see 700+ years of Realm history boil down to 'anything of minor consequence or greater... was only done by the Empress'. Let the Patricians and Great Houses have some historic mentions too, her Redness can surely delegate a few things (or just eat popcorn & watch)
            Also I don't want to see the following topic ignored: How 700+ years of sorcery/workings/Heptagram influence has changed or improved the Realm.

            Long time lurker

                • I'd like to see collaborative effects when multiple DB's use the same charm (or different charms that enhance each other). I don't think every charm needs this effect, but a solid enough portion of them that it's "a thing".
                • I see this as an opportunity to revamp collaboration rules for working together on things, something that is scarcely touched upon in the core book. I want to see 2 DB's fighting together to be more than twice as bad as 1 DB. Not only are there 2 of them, but they cover, bolster, and enhance each other.
                • I want to be able to tell the Great Houses apart at a glance. While I'd love to have multiple pages on each House to dive into, I'd like a single paragraph to show me how 2 houses with Military focus can be discernibly different so that it's meaningful to PCs that encounter those houses.
                • I could use more examples of Battlegroups that comprise Realm and Lookshyian soldiers for DBs to command. Archer groups, cavalry, siege, etc.
                • It has been hinted that The Blessed Isle is "Creation in miniature" where it's colder in the north side of the island, and hotter on the south side, and not just "temperate throughout". I'd like to see this maintained and used in examples of different places all over the Isle so people are reminded of it. For example, if the Heptagram is still in the north part of the island, it should deal with snow and ice during some parts of the year at least.
                • I'd like to see a fresh perspective on The Immaculate Order. I have no thoughts on how to do this myself, but something that brings new insight to the order that's not just a rehash of the past 2 editions. This could be excerpts from The Doctrine, to more examples of how the Order does it's duties both on the Blessed Isle and across Creation.

                            Originally posted by Gonzo View Post
                            I know this isn't likely to happen, but I dream with a special Battlegroup system for Wyld Hunts. Cause you know, DB's are like the primary antagonists for solars and handling a complete Brotherhood is hard for a single individual.
                            I don't think the Wyld Hunt needs a separate subsystem/modification of the Battlegroup rules, but a section on Wyld Hunt tactics and storytelling the Hunt, either in Dragon-Blooded, Realm or Vance's proposed Storyteller Guide (or as, like, a blog post that eventually goes in the ST Guide) would be welcome.

                            I'd like to see the Wyld Hunt treated from the perspective of the Dragon-Blooded in their own book, presented in a way that makes it easier to show them as neither evil nor dupes of a fake religion. The early chapters of God-Kicking Boot come to mind -- from the perspective of everyone in that scene, the Dragon-Blooded Satrap throwing down with the Lunar was pretty much the good guy. And, of course, the Solars and Lunars are so few and far between that, even with a special religious duty to prioritize those targets, the Wyld Hunt probably spends most of its time fighting Fair Folk, the dead, and rogue spirits.

                            I'd definitely like to see some more in-depth presentation on the Lookshyan Gentes, the Realm's minor houses, and Dragon-Blooded from the rest of Creation.

                            I'd very much like a pretty in-depth treatment of the struggle for the throne in the Realm book -- give the Roseblack some time to shine because she's awesome, give us some more substance on some of the dark-horse candidates, maybe point out what some of the pillars of each House's current position are, to give players and STs some idea of where to go when they want to shake things up.

                            Considering how heavily bureaucratic the Realm is, I'd hope we'd get some more fleshing-out of the Bureaucracy ability; I doubt they'll do up a whole Bureaucracy subsystem, and considering how obnoxious subsystem proliferation can get I'm not sure I'd want them to, but some more examples and guidelines would be welcome. The Project rules as they exist in the corebook don't exactly pop, at least in my opinion.

                            A little bit more information on the Shogunate would be nice -- enough to spark some imaginations, but leave most of it undefined. I think we're kind of getting into that with Arms, though -- the Era tags inherently make some part of the Artifact lore into Shogunate lore, and I think telling the stories of the Shogunate and the First Age through the wonders they left behind is very much in-theme.

                                            Seven Arrows is a Wood Aspect who rules the Kingdom of Diamond Sand in the far South. Along its eastern boarder runs the Darakhal Sultanate, an ancient city-state with a hereditary mortal king. Their general is called Ashen Breeze, a Fire Aspect who has spent much of her life protecting the Sultanate. Ashen Breeze and Seven Arrows despise one another, and the Kingdom of Diamond Sand and the Darakhal Sultanate have been in a state of war for the last century.

                                            One day, Seven Arrows receives word that a Solar Exalted has appeared in the wastelands two hundred miles south of his kingdom. He sends a message to Ashen Breeze, proposing a temporary truce between his nation and the Sultanate (the first one since war broke out 95 years ago), that the two of them might fight against the Solar. Ashen Breeze loathes Seven Arrows, but recognizes that the Solar Anathema represents a far more dangerous threat, and so she agrees.

                                            The two of them send out messages to the other nearby Dragon Blooded that they can. One message is sent to the Dragon Blooded simply known as Sapphire, who lives several hundred miles to the west, deep within the desert. She is an ancient sorceress who has been studying her magic longer than anyone can remember. Another goes to Silver Eyes, a young Dragon Blooded merchant with a big ego who has been causing problems with the Guild. Neither Seven Arrows nor Ashen Breeze like him, but he is a Dragon Blooded all the same. The final message is sent to Ledaal Tesriss, a Realm Dragon Blooded who arrived in the area a few months earlier to excavate and explore an old Shogunate ruin.

                                            The five of them soon gather together. They hold wildly different beliefs and come from different lands. Some of them outright despise one another. But the one thing they have in common is that they share the Blood of the Dragons, and each one recognizes that they are the first and last line of defense that Creation has to protect itself from the Anathema that would threaten it.


                                            Honestly, I can't wait to run some games with this as the premise.

                                                    I'd like the great Houses to not be flanderised. That's the only thing I'm actually worried about, since people keep saying "the houses are too similar, make them really different" (which I don't agree with, but there you go), so I'm a little worried that the Houses will become un-nuanced Houses of Hats (this is the spy house! This is the fundamentalist house! etc). But I think the fluff writers are good enough that it should be okay.
                                                    So, yes, nuance.

                                                    I'd like the Realm to have more of a sense of history. How did it develop? What happened to the 12 houses that no longer exist? What happened to all the Shogunate-era gents who were absorbed by the Realm? Often the history makes it seem like random DBs with no ties or backstory whatsoever appeared, screwed the Empress, and bam, great house.

                                                    I'd also like the dates to make more sense!

                                                    Mechanically, not much to say, as what I've heard sounds good. Surcharges out, charms that don't work against celestials out.                                                     I'd like a few more examples of non-Realm DB families.
                                                    I'd like a few more examples of minor houses.
