    So, how about sources?


    Spider-Man's Webshooters are an artifact with a lot of promise. Most if not all of the effects of the Distaff, plus plenty of its own! You could definitely use those to produce a garrote and strings for this Style, while at the same time this Style is completely separate from the Webshooter's natural Evocations, which normally produce more rope-like projectiles than string-like. More focused on mobility and capture than murder or manipulation.

    Naruto's Kankuro (finally remembered his name) and the other ninja generally used strings made of chakra/Essence; didn't use them for cutting or grappling anything, but for controlling their puppets.

    Sasuke used actual, physical wires. Threw a bunch of shuriken with wires attached to bind Orochimaru to a tree, and then shot flames down the wires to envelop and consume him. Maybe not quite this style so much as Solar Thrown's Whirlwind Hand of the Striker, though.

    It's been a long time since I read or watched Black Cat, so I'd need to brush up on Jenos.

        So I've been playing with the idea of using some Exigents to express the puppet ninjas from the Naruto series into Exalted, expressed as Chosen of the God of Animated Figures. I never considered the razor wire concept for them as there is acton of cool stuff these powers do just in Naruto. And while the show lets true masters take control of live humans, i thought it a bit extreme for an Exalt type that players wpuld see the powers of. Instead i want to use them to make himan scale puppets, maybe golems come to life, mess with corpses to fake zombies, or at the clomax of fighting one, have to use a non-functional warstrider by sheer force of will.

        But this entire concept sits between the space of most razor wire powers/effects and the body possession idea that Throne Shadow Style considers within its purview.

        The most 'control' I would consider establishing would be the complete ambush rule where you have them in a social scene by having the wire wrapped around them, using those rounds of control as actions caused by a Threaten move (obvious bodily harm). Magic it up a bit to make it mechanically feasible and it sounds like a lot of fun
