                                                        Originally posted by Grod_the_giant View Post
                                                        If I have take a charm for my Supernal ability that has additional effects at higher Essence levels (Heaven Thunder Hammer, for instance), do I get those benefits immediately, or have to wait until my Essence is actually at the right level?
                                                        Your Essence counts as 5 for all effects except for things that actually uses the value for a calculation.
