    Prey of the Weasel
    Cost: 6m
    Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisite Evocation: Bones of Rubber

    The weasel is a predator that shuns the tactic of stalk and ambush in favor of flush and chase. The exalt may reflexively activate this evocation to mark her prey, be it a person, animal, or item. Once the prey has been mark the exalt will chase it down till it’s hers.     Special rules: The exalt must see, hear, or smell her prey to activate this evocation and the sense used will determine the difficulty of tracking.     Just a +1 to rolls? For 6m? You can get +1 for 1m with an Excellency? Ain't nothing about this power that's amazing, let alone Essence 3. The scale of this power is Essence 1, but it's STILL overpriced and underbonused to be of any use. I would make this an Essence 1 power, make it start the whole tree. Give it a cost of 2m, and make it give +1 non-charm bonus for tracking. If they track the target successfully while the power is going, give them an additional +1 non-charm bonus to their stealth roll in an Ambush attack. That's an interesting power that's not too strong and would start this tree out nicely at Essence 1. Or you can go a different direction.

                    So, despite suffering a mental block on the great goremaul I requested, I was able to flesh out the armor I sketched out when The Unsung Hero asked for inspiration for a green jade artifact.
                    So I present to you, for your entertainment and critique...

                    Lakashim, the Pulse of Life
                    ●●●● Artifact: Green Jade Silken Armor

                    Appearance
                    Strands of green jade with the silk of some long lost race of moth are woven into a dozen braided pairs that come together in a spiral on the wearer’s upper left chest, above the heart.

                    Attunement
                    Additional Benefits: 0m
                    - The wearer can add their Performance ability to their join battle result instead of 3 when calculating initiative if they have a Performance specialty in Dance.

                    Evocations
                    Hear the Pulse of Creation
                    Cost: 5m
                    Mins: Essence 2
                    Type: Simple
                    Keywords: None
                    Duration: One Scene
                    Prerequisite Charms: None
                    One Line Summary: Sense all living things nearby and downgrade ambush and surprise attacks to surprise attacks and normal attacks, respectively

                    By closing their eyes and opening their senses to Lakashim, the wearer can hear the local beat of the Worldsong. For the rest of the scene, the wearer is aware of all the life around them, gaining a general sense of any creature with a functional heartbeat within medium range. This does not give them an exact location but will provide an accurate number of how many beings are in each respective range band, whether visible or not. With this information, the wearer is also better armed against unexpected attacks by those they have sensed, treating successful ambushes as surprise attacks, and successful surprise attacks as normal attacks, respectively. At Essence 4, the sensory range upgrades to long range. This evocation only works as long as the wearer stands upon Creation.

                    Pounding Dance of the Worldheart
                    Cost: 3m
                    Mins: Essence 3
                    Type: Reflexive
                    Keywords: Perilous
                    Duration: Instant
                    Prerequisite Charms: Hear the Pulse of Creation
                    One Line Summary: Move in tandem with opponents, losing less initiative from withering attacks

                    Once in tune with the rhythm of the Creation, the wearer now moves with a grace that only come from following the steps in the dance of all life. In a scene where the wearer has used Hear the Pulse of Creation, a sensed attacker gains only half of the initiative lost (round up) from withering attack against the wearer and does not receive any bonus if they crash the wearer. The wearer loses the full amount of initiative, the attacker can’t seem to take advantage of the situation nearly as well as normal.

                    Rebounding Inner Rhythm
                    Cost: 2m/1W
                    Mins: Essence 3
                    Type: Supplemental
                    Keywords: Perilous, Counterattack
                    Duration: Instant
                    Prerequisite Charms: Pounding Dance of the Worldheart
                    One Line Summary: Recover from falling with bounce in your step and a fist to their attacker’s face

                    Like the silence between heartbeats, sometimes the course of Lakashim falls to rise again with fierce determination. The wearer recovers more readily from being knocked down, gaining 1 initiative after successfully rising from prone. The wearer may ignore the penalty to flurry rising from prone and attacking the opponent that attacked them. In this case, the additional initiative is awarded before the flurried attack is rolled. If the wearer is capable of reflexively rising from prone, they must pay an additional willpower and treat it as a counterattack to take advantage of this evocation.

                    One Pulse, One Soul, One Act
                    Cost: -
                    Mins: Essence 4
                    Type: Permanent
                    Keywords: Perilous
                    Duration: Instant
                    Prerequisite Charms: Pounding Dance of the Worldheart
                    One Line Summary: When your action sync’s up with an ally’s attack, chance to share some successes between both of them

                    When many things move in harmony and act as one, Lakashim beats loudest. After Hear the Pulse of Creation, whenever the wearer attacks an opponent on the same tick as an ally, they both may reroll an unsuccessful die once, sharing all successes rolled.

                    Example: Wearer rolls 2 successes and their ally rolls 0 successes. They both reroll one die apiece, coming up with 1 success between both of them. The wearer now has 3 successes and the ally has 1 success.

                    This effect cannot happen if either the ally or the wearer delayed their action to act on the same tick. All allies that have a pulse that are attacking the same target may partake in this reroll, adding their successes to the shared result, but must do so after all other reroll mechanics. Those who cannot reroll an unsuccessful die cannot partake.

                                    Perfected Voice of Nothing
                                    (Orichalcum and Starmetal) Artifact •••

                                    Tags: Lethal, Melee, Balanced

                                    [Story Stuff]

                                    Evocations of Perfected Voice of Nothing
                                    Attuning to Perfected Void of Nothing unleashes the singing spirit of the blade, whose ethereal voice sings to the mind and soul of its wielder with enchanting power. As the wielder gains an advantage in battle, the spirit’s voice shifts within her. Further, a Solar or Sidereal who pays 2 additional motes to attune to Perfected Voice of Nothing gains the Aria levels’ passive benefits. They are as follows:

                                    • Sotto Voce (0 or fewer Initiative): If the character spends Willpower to enhance her Defense against a decisive attack, but the attack still hits, she may reduce its raw damage by (Essence) dice
                                    • Subito (1-4 Initiative): The character may aim reflexively for a withering attack.
                                    • Bravura (5-9 Initiative): +2 non-Charm successes to rush actions.
                                    • Furioso (10-14 Initiative): +2 non-Charm successes to oppose enemy Disengage actions.
                                    • Trionfante (15+ Initiative): The character may delay their action at no cost in preparation for a Clash; if they do not Clash by the end of the round, they lose the -2 Initiative as normal.

                                    The Aria levels are given quantities 1 (Sotto Voce) through 5 (Trionfante) for the purposes of evocation calculation.


                                    Haunting Refrain
                                    Cost:                                     3m per turn; Mins: Essence 1
                                    Type: Reflexive
                                    Keywords: Psyche
                                    Duration: Indefinite
                                    Prerequisites: None

                                    The wielder ushers forth the spirit’s voice from the blade, establishing a mystic link to her target so he hears the blade’s unsettling song. The wielder rolls (Essence + [Aria x 2]) against her target’s Resolve. If successful, the target hears the refrain at full volume, sustaining a penalty to all rolls equal to half the weapon’s Aria (rounded up) at the time of his turn. If the roll fails, the target only hears the murmuring voice at the back of his mind. The roll repeats every turn the evocation is maintained.

                                    This evocation can target multiple foes, with the cost per turn increasing by 2 motes for every additional target.

                                    This evocation can target battlegroups. Rather than penalties, however, successful roles deal (Aria / 2) dice of Magnitude damage, as the soldiers ensnared by the effects panic and either go catatonic or flee in terror.


                                    Hallowed Warcry
                                    Cost:                                     3m; Mins: Essence 2
                                    Type: Reflexive
                                    Keywords: Psyche
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisites: Haunting Refrain

                                    The blade unleashes a mental roar into an enemy’s mind, powered by the frustration of the wielder’s struggling fate, in an attempt to disturb his mind and essence.                                             This evocation can target multiple foes, with the cost per turn increasing by 2 motes for every additional target.

                                            This evocation can target battlegroups, with the cost multiplied by Size (minimum 3m).
                                            This... seems like a fairly weak Evocation to me. Essence + Aria starts out as 6 dice, and can't feasibly go above 10 in actual play (albeit 8 is more reasonable). This means anybody with a half-decent Resolve and an Excellency will basically never ever be threatened by it (despite the reroll per turn, which, I admit, is pretty nasty). I'd make it twice the character's actual Aria level, meaning it has a decent chance to hit even Resolve 5 over the course of multiple turns (unless your opponent uses Excellency/WP, but it's still a net win for you if they have to boost their defense by more than 1, given that it only costs 3m to maintain).

                                            Now, I admit, the consequences of Haunting Refrain actually succeeding are incredibly nasty (ongoing -3 penalty for basically forever), but the balancing factor really shouldn't be "it's never going to succeed against anybody important enough to warrant stacking penalties on". On the other hand, if it has a decent chance of success, "once it succeeds, you're stuck with the effect" is probably too powerful. So make it a reroll per turn, regardless of success.

                                            Battle groups, in my experience, are never strong enough for anybody to want to stack penalties on them, so the effect would be useless even if it wasn't grossly overpriced. Instead, make it do (Aria/2) dice of Magnitude damage each turn it's successful (as some soldiers are incapacitated by the cacophony or start fleeing), and keep the price a flat 3m.
