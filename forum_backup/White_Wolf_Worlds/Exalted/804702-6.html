                0) Obviously we have pretty different ideas of what Craft ought to look like, and there's no point arguing about whose vision is better. So I'll keep my commentary limited to the small picture.

                2) Might borrow the increment terminology. Really clarifies things. Not sure about limiting it to basic projects though. I think the speed boost should apply to statues and suits of armour and such. And the need-no-tools boost should apply to everything.

                3) I think it'd be worthwhile to specify that dismantling an object (not "and" object) with this Charm is much tidier than normal dismantling. Taking apart a door give you useful wood, not broken splinters.
                0) I'm not opposed to discussing things - really, my warnings at the top of the thread and again in the Craft post were just aimed to discourage some of the less enjoyable tendencies on this particular board. I'm sure you know what I mean. Your well-articulated and friendly disagreement is perfectly welcome.

                1) For reasons I can't quite articulate, I'm a fan of full-free-excellency charms. It's on the side of the tree, no one has to buy it, but I think it'll play relatively well. The craft charms are pretty expensive. To boost a roll with exploding 10s (5m), double 7s (7m, 1wp), disappearing 1s and 2s (6m) costs 18m. That's your entire personal pool and then some. If you want it to not count against the project's terminus (12m, 2wp), it's your entire peripheral pool. Not adding 10m to those costs (the full excellency) may be more useful than it seems.

                2) Ah, yes, good point. Didn't mean to exclude those things. I wish there were a better way to say it than "personal scale" - I want to make it clear that this means armor or a rowboat, not a major roadway or something bureaucratic-scale.

                3) Done. I had forgotten this was an option in 2e, since 3e doesn't explicitly call it out. Always enjoyed the disassembly aspect of the charm.

                4) Missing an "s" in "assess".

                5) Didn't expect you to remove the mechanics for assessing normal stuff. Any particular reason for that?

                6) Does this really need to be its own Charm? How often do you think "someone will try to break this later this scene, and it's worth my motes to make it tougher"?
                4) Assessssssssssssss

                5) I guess I was sort of thinking it was a bit obvious that characters can do that sort of thing with Craft. But I guess no harm in having a sentence of two describing something the skill can be used for other than making things.

                6) Pretty often! This is my second favorite craft charm, right after Art of Permanence. The city gates when the siege engines start flying. The sails of a ship I booked passage on when people are trying to set them on fire. This stick I just picked up and want to use as a crowbar or a sword. The charm should be a lot cheaper though, and affect bigger things to help differentiate it from its prerequisite. Reducing the cost 6m -> 4m and increasing the radius to Essence * 5.

                7) The Essence minimum should probably be 2. Might wanna specify that the OST bonus is permanent, too.

                8) 10m T-Rexes make me nervous.

                9) Turning the other two Charms into repurchases is probably a good idea, though. I'd be interested to hear about why you removed the permanent option, and about why you dumbed down the human statues.
                7) Done.
                8) You're probably right. I'm not really sure what the right cost is, but 25m seems too steep. Let's give 18m, 1wp a try.
                9) If it's empowered by your personal magic, it's a charm - if it's a permanent, self-sustaining feature of the world, it's an artifact. Also, there will be no charms in this rewrite that cost XP to use. Creating intelligence is definitely a thing Solars can do - but if you want to make people, I want to have a chance to make a story out of it, rather than a thing that happens casually and ends when you release committed motes. (Creating elementals somehow doesn't touch the same sentiment, I guess because elementals feel so much like a natural part of Creation, even if you are technically calling them into existence now)

                10) You've toned this down a fair bit. Took out the "signature", too. I'm curious why.

                11) This might be too big-picture to be useful, but I think "doing your thing inspirationally well" is fitting in any Ability. A Brawl Charm which strengthens the people who watch you fight would be fine by me.
                10) How did I tone it down? It's a pretty direct port of the book version of the charm, costing 1wp instead of 1xp. Oh, unless you meant that I changed it to basic projects only. Didn't mean to do that (same mistake as I made with CNNT above).

                The Art of Permanence is my second favorite charm in the game. Forever. That's a long time.

                With regards to the signature though, if you want your signature on something... sign it. :P

                11) I would say that if a character wants to do something inspirationally well, they should buy a few dots of performance and an entry level charm. Not all brawlers are inspirational - but those that are have dots in performance. To take that to a more meta level, the best characters are those that can't be resolved down to a single charm tree - I want to encourage taking a few charms here and there to round out your concept, rather than getting everything out of just one ability.

                    Originally posted by BlueWinds View Post
                    0) I'm not opposed to discussing things - really, my warnings at the top of the thread and again in the Craft post were just aimed to discourage some of the less enjoyable tendencies on this particular board. I'm sure you know what I mean. Your well-articulated and friendly disagreement is perfectly welcome.
                    Good to hear. And yeah, I know what you mean.

                    But I don't know if there's anything to say. A lot of the differences of opinion seem too simple and clear to argue about.

                    Well, I guess there is one thing. One of my goals was to make Charms that help you define your character. And a lot of the stuff you cut was written with that in mind.

                    In my opinion, EX3 does a good job of helping you say "I am the guy who dodges everything" or "I am the best doctor". You can use your Supernal, and the right Charm choices, to clearly stake out a space. Every Ability seems worth specializing in.

                    So I want it to be possible to say, "I am the Queen of Craft" or "I am the best mapmaker" with a Solar. And once you've said that, you need your investment in Craft to display itself in play. This is, I think, what the writers were trying to accomplish with the craft xp system; they wanted the Craft specialist to constantly approach things from a Craft-centric perspective. But they sabotaged this plan with dozens of tedious Charms that say nothing about your character and do nothing until you sit down to make Artifacts.

                    With Charms like The Craft Of War, Words As Workshop Method, Master Craftsman's Eye, Awe-Inspiring Feats Of Craft and Arrows Of The Dawn, I was trying to help the Craft specialist use Craft as their primary tool for solving problems. Your ability to make things never leaves you, even when you're naked in a prison cell, and you can use it to fight or influence people or acquire information or do any number of other things.

                    Originally posted by BlueWinds View Post
                    11) I would say that if a character wants to do something inspirationally well, they should buy a few dots of performance and an entry level charm. Not all brawlers are inspirational - but those that are have dots in performance. To take that to a more meta level, the best characters are those that can't be resolved down to a single charm tree - I want to encourage taking a few charms here and there to round out your concept, rather than getting everything out of just one ability.
                    Performance already does too much if you ask me, and it'd be hard to fit suitable Ability-specific inspiration Charms in it.

                    I think there's already enough in there to encourage using multiple Abilities. The paradigm I described above definitely rewards specialization, but there's still plenty of good stuff in other Abilities that your specialty won't give you.

                                Dodge is the most solid tree I've worked with so far. The charms are clear, concise, and mostly interesting/useful. It's long enough to have depth without being mind-numbing. Thumbs up.

                                Dodge

                                Reed in the Wind
                                Cost: 2i per 1 Evasion; Mins: Dodge 3, Essence 1
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: None

                                This charm supplements a dodge, raising the Solar's Evasion it by 1 for every 2i spent. The first (Essence) Evasion gained in this way do not count against the normal dice-adding limits.

                                At Essence 3+, if the attack misses she gains 1m. She may gain this bonus only once per round.

                                Dust Motes Whirling
                                Cost: 2m; Mins: Dodge 4, Essence 1
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Reed in the Wind

                                This Charm supplements a disengage attempt, granting it double 9s.

                                Shadow Dancer Method
                                Cost: 1m; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 1
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Dust Motes Whirling

                                This charm supplements a disengage attempt. The Solar does not lose the normal 2i for this action. If she moves back into close range of the same opponent on her next turn, she gains 2i.

                                Reflex Sidestep Technique
                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Dodge 3, Essence 1
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Post-roll
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Reed in the Wind

                                This charm supplements a dodge against an Ambush. The Solar sets her Evasion to 2 rather than 0. She gains +1 Evasion for every 1 or 2 her opponent rolled on the attack.

                                Leaping Dodge Method
                                Cost: 1m, 2i; Mins: Dodge 3, Essence 1
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Keywords: Post-roll
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Reflex Sidestep Technique

                                This charm may be activated after the Solar dodges an attack. She moves 1 range band in any direction.

                                Searing Quicksilver Flight
                                Cost: —; Mins: Dodge 4, Essence 1
                                Type: Permanent
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Permanent
                                Prerequisite Charms: Reed in the Wind

                                Whenever the Solar dodges an attack, her opponent loses 1i.

                                With an Essence 2+ repurchase, she also gains 1i.

                                Drifting Leaf Elusion
                                Cost: 1m; Mins: Dodge 2, Essence 1
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Post-roll
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: None

                                This charm supplements a dodge against an attack with 0 threshold successes. It misses.

                                Shadow Over Water
                                Cost: 2m; Mins: Dodge 3, Essence 1
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Drifting Leaf Elusion

                                This charm supplements a dodge. The Solar ignores all penalties that would affect her Evasion.

                                Fleet Dreaming Image
                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Dust Motes Whirling

                                This charm supplements a disengage action, allowing the Solar to perform it from short range. If successful, she moves to medium range, and as normal for disengaging, moves away the first time her opponent follows her.

                                Drifting Shadow Focus
                                Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Dodge 4, Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: One Turn
                                Prerequisite Charms: Reflex Sidestep Technique

                                This charm supplements a dodge. If the attack (or any other against the Solar before her next turn) misses, she redirects it against any foe other than the attacker within close range.

                                Seven Shadow Evasion
                                Cost: 4m, 1wp; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Searing Quicksilver Flight x2

                                This charm supplements a dodge, even if the attack is undodgable. The attack misses. It may also be used to defend against uncountable, reoccurring damage, such as a rockslide or being thrown into Oblivion.

                                It may be used only once per scene, but is reset by using Reed in the Wind to successfully dodge three decisive attacks.

                                An Essence 5 repurchase grants the Solar 1i, and an additional 1i at the start of her turn each round until she conceals herself or is struck by an attack.

                                Safety Between Heartbeats
                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Post-roll
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Searing Quicksilver Flight x2

                                This charm supplements a dodge. The Lawgiver's opponent loses 1i for each 1 in their roll.

                                Fourfold Shiver Binding
                                Cost: 4m; Mins: Dodge 4, Essence 2
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: One scene
                                Prerequisite Charms: Drifting Leaf Elusion

                                This charm may be activated after dodging at attack while not wearing armor. The Solar's Evasion is increased by one. This bonus does not count against the normal dice-adding limits.

                                Flow Like Blood
                                Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Dodge 4, Essence 2
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Keywords: Perilous
                                Duration: One scene
                                Prerequisite Charms: Shadow Over Water

                                Reed in the Wind only costs 1i per evasion gained. When attacked by enemies the Solar has Advantage against, she ignores all penalties to her Evasion. At the end of each each round during which she remained within close range of an enemy without getting hit, she gains 1i.

                                Rumor of Form
                                Cost: 6m; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Post-roll
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Shadow Over Water

                                This charm supplements a dodge. Each 1 the attacker rolled subtracts one success from his result. After the attack, the Solar immediately rolls to reestablish surprise with that many bonus successes.

                                Vaporous Division
                                Cost: 4m per damage; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 2
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Rumor of Form

                                The Solar may activate this charm when she fails to dodge a decisive attack, after damage has been rolled but before it's applied. For every 4m spent, she takes one less level of damage.

                                Sunlight Bleeding Away
                                Cost: 4m, 1wp; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 3
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Post-roll
                                Duration: One turn
                                Prerequisite Charms: Fleet Dreaming Image

                                This charm supplements a successful Disengage action. The Solar may retreat from her enemy the second time he approaches her as well as the first.

                                Thousand Steps' Stillness
                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 3
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Post-roll
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Safety Between Heartbeats

                                This charm supplements a successful dodge. The Solar gains Initiative equal to the number of 1s and 2s her opponent rolled.

                                Unbowed Willow Meditation
                                Cost: —; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 3
                                Type: Permanent
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Permanent
                                Prerequisite Charms: Thousand Steps' Stillness

                                Whenever the Solar dodges a non-gambit decisive attack from a dangerous opponent without using any charms, after the attack completes she steals all of his Initiative, setting him to 0i and crashing him.

                                Hundred Shadow Ways
                                Cost: 6m; Mins: Dodge 5, Essence 3
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Stackable, Post-roll, Perilous
                                Duration: One scene
                                Prerequisite Charms: Vaporous Division

                                This charm supplements a successful dodge. One non-excellency charm supplementing the attack, or a Simple or Reflexive charm that created it, cannot supplement or create further attacks that target only her for the rest of the scene (she could still be included in the area of an attack targeting multiple people, for example).
