        If I can be the weirdo who is way too intense about children's media, Po's entire martial journey is not about learning an accepted style - it is instead about accepting himself and his own body and place and strengths and weaknesses. In fact, while they're all referred to using 'styles' what it ends up being is more of a unique expression of self for each character/animal type. Po's 'style' is about his panda-ness, his size and his resilience and his faith in himself (even if he has no self-esteem through most of the first movie). He doesn't stop being fat, or a dork, or fairly slow on the uptake, or fluffy, or hopeful, or kind, or silly. He harnesses those things in order to become the best version of himself he can be, while always seeking more knowledge and experience (made much more explicit in the third movie) (which, not gonna lie, I totally got teary watching).

        If I were mimicking Po, I wouldn't be looking at MAs to do it - even for the harnessing of chi, I'd be going with Sorcery probably, or something like that. Or if I were, his character doesn't fit a lot of them and would mechanically be pretty weird with smatterings of everything. That said I am a straight-up Brawl fangirl so beware the bias.

        tl:dr - Lucy loves Po.

                            I assume ata-beasts or various types of Wyld mutants with non-humanoid bodies could probably create and study a "martial art style" though it would likely be incompatible with a human's martial art style.

                            An intelligent octopus might study and codify methods and techniques (katas) to strike harder or dodge attacks. It could be, in effect, "Octo-man Style." Unfortunately, humans wouldn't be able to practice it as they lack the 8 tentacles and cartilage body required to assume the stances and use the techniques of the style. In theory a Lunar might be able to learn this style and use it while in octopus or squid form, but not other forms.

                            Of course, this starts to get kind of silly and rather pointless really (do we really need kung-fu pandas?) so I wouldn't necessarily be adverse to it if the dev team just said that Martial Arts is for human and near human races and left it at that.
