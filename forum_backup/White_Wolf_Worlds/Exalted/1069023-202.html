                #3034
                07-27-2017, 11:14 AM
                Originally posted by Iron Phoenix View Post
                1) The aim action states that if you attack the target you aimed at on your next turn, you gain a +3 bonus. I believe this is written with the assumption that you must spend one turn aiming, and then the next turn attacking, because aim is a combat action that cannot be placed in a flurry. Righteous Devil Form allows you to reflexively aim, so I assume if you reflexively aim and attack the same target in the same turn, you gain the +3 bonus on the attack. Is this correct?
                Correct. This is also true of other things that let you reflexively aim, like Accuracy Without Distance.

                2) The aim action states that characters cannot move and aim during the same round without some form of magic to permit it. Righteous Devil Form makes the aim action reflexive. Is that sufficient to allow the character to move and aim in the same turn? Or are Righteous Devil Style practitioners expected to remain stationary if they want to benefit from their reflexive aim action?
                No, you'd need something that specifically likes that.

                3) If I am dual-wielding flame pieces, does Righteous Devil Form let me reflexively aim with both? Can I subsequently reload both with one invocation of Cloud of Ebon Devils?
                Because of the way dual-wielding rules work (i.e. a bonus on clash attacks if you're wielding the same weapon paired), it doesn't really matter. Short answer is "yes," I guess.

                4) Righteous Devil Form allows me to use my firewand as a medium melee weapon. Can a melee weapon be aimed?
                Yep. You can aim with any kind of attack.
