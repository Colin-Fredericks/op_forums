                        07-26-2017, 12:41 PM
                        Originally posted by ale137 View Post
                        To use Rising Sun Slash if my roll needs to contains a royal flush of 7, 8, 9, and 10?
                        As long as you have at least one of each of a 7, 8, 9, and 10, you can use it. I don't know poker well enough to know if that's actually a royal flush. x_x

                        What should be the rate of DB exaltation in primary school?
                        It's not something you can really peg to a simple statistical rate.

                        Are there mortal students in the Heptagram?
                        Very few.

                        Should one spend XP for merits or mutations granted by workings?
                        By default, it's covered by the XP the sorcerer spends on the working. If you want to let a PC who's receiving the benefits of a working pay that XP cost instead of the sorcerer's player, that's perfectly reasonable.

                        Whats the appearance rating of the sorcerers of Ysyr?
                        Ranges from 1 to 5.
