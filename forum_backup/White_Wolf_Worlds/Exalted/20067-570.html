                                                            Originally posted by Octopoid View Post
                                                            In 2E - Are Abyssals outside of Fate? I had thought they were, but I ran across a Stealth Charm that indicated it MADE them outside Fate, and that confused me.
                                                            No, Ex2 Abyssals were not Outside of Fate unless actually outside of Creation (such as in the Underworld) or using charms that made them so. However, they had a dark fate which, among other things, obscured them from sidereal astrological tracking.


                                                            Conversely, Infernals were always Outside of Fate and had a charm to disguise them as being subject to some fate so they wouldn't be exposed by sidereal investigation.
