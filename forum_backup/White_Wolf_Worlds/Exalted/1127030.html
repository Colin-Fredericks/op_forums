    Creationlikes and Pocketworlds brewing Thread

    08-10-2017, 06:08 AM
    So brought up in another thread and I figure it would be a fun thread to start. Basically your different ideas for Pocketworlds and Creationlikes. So basically minisettings you homebrewed different from Creation that you can pop in your games ranging from things like hidden islands under the sea with different mechanics, a reverse mirror side of Creation with no Gods on the opposite side of Meru, Autochthonia, Zenmu, ect... Let me start





    Many of my lil Creationlikes are things I could make into Shards and sometimes originally thought them up as but my desire to make the most use of them I usually placed them in a Corner of Creation, just somewhat hidden away. Many of these places were the result of wanting to mess around with some cool settings I have seen in Video games. Like I flat out have Thedas, I have it accessible from things such as Eluvians, and secret paths. And one I’ve least developed essentially Hyrule connecting to the East about once a century, or maybe only when the Master Sword is unsealed.

    Basically I have two methods of how other universes work, either they are close enough to Creation, sometimes even connecting, that they can poach Souls from the Well of Soul and sup on the Wyld. Or they are more clearly separated and have their own source for souls, and celestial bodies like the sun.

    Hyrule will just be a continent of old sorceries where three Solars, a Dawn, a Zenith, and a Twilight, are bound following a cycle of connected reincarnation. Spirits and Raksha exist but are rarer, with Behemoths and Elementals most common. Basically a setting for fun lil adventures where you can walk in the boots of Link, maybe after you knocked him out of it. Population??? Not sure!

    Thedas of all the pocket worlds this one is the one I most intend to be straight up the same as the video game, where as Hyrule would be just using common themes and names and styles. Thedas basically works on an inverted Creation geography where if you go west there are trees, and the great ocean is east, and the south takes you to cold artic. Gods are abundant, only a handful exist. Thedas was actually a piece of Creation on the Back of a Primordial who left during the War, this Primordial would be the Great Maker/the Fade, with a Fetich of the Golden/Black City. His Deva are the Demons and Spirits of the Fade. Humans exist here, and Dwarves are descendants of Mountainfolk breeding with humans who evolved cultures based off of the Mountainfolk. The Qunarri are a mystery, likely similar to the Lintha, so either a Primordial race, or descendants of humans who bred with a primordial race. I’m thinking this world has no Exalted, except for a few exceptions like a Lunar Witch of the Wyld Flemeth. I played around with Languages so it has its own Major Thedan Language (like Rivertongue a trade language everyone started learning), but has regional (1/4th) minor languages for each country. One of my groups PCs is an Avar mercenary/Assassin from here. The world probably steals souls from Creation, or a Huge amount were ripped into the Fade, which recycles souls for rebirth and serves as a plane of wandering dreams. Virtually absent of magical materials, but has Lyrium a magical Material unique to the setting. Mages are a result of the fact that the nature of the World and the Fade has made sorcerers much more common, as they seem to naturally pop off, and magic has a lot of political and religious connotation here. There are about 16 million humanoids, per the game.

    The Garden. A pocket world created by the Strong God Herne, where monsters fall from the Moon during events of the Lunar Tears. I blatantly nabbed this setting after playing FF8 again and totally read the Sorceress/Herne legend as basically the story of a Solar Creating his own world in the Wyld, creating his own humans and setting it so his Exaltation only exalts chosen females. The Moon is actually a portal to the Wyld. Guardian Forces are Spirits that can ride a mortal, giving them unique sorcerous initiations and making heroic individuals comparable to a Terrestrial Exalt, and use Artifacts. Demons, Behemoths, Elementals, Raksha, Gods and More exist and run rampant. Moombah and Shumi exist as examples of nonhuman primordial races. Chocobo exist, but in my Creation Chocobo exist there as well in a mythic position like Simhata. Moombah are a pretty innocent species, and Shumi are an old species that has a unique ability to chrysalis themselves, and their souls, into that of another, replicating even the immortal Mountainfolk or Dragon King Souls. Here I have the idea of using the Gardens and SeeD as having access to certain connections to sneak into the Scavenger Lands, to trade, perform missions for hire, and accept orphans. They have Autochthonian/Lookshy level tech. This game has a special place in my heart as my first Final Fantasy. Population not sure, I will probably mess with it eventually, but could be high as is a sphere planet in Elsewhere with four continents.

    The Kingdom/the Continent. This is a floating continent in the west, one that is usually just off the map into the Wyld but bumps into Creation proper sometime after the return of the Solars. I did a lot of brainstorming on this idea back in 2nd ed. The Architect, basically a Solar much like the Magnus before we got the Magnus, Created a flying mountain range and land along with it in the Wyld. The Architect believes himself to be responsible for the Usurpation, in truth a handful of Solar plotting against their fellows helped to cover the ambush of the Usurpation independently as they were also covering up their own schemes from their fellows. The Architect placed this land atop a Greater Elemental Dragon, a great winged bird the Roc, while surrounding it with the Greater Elemental Dragon of Air the horizon spanning Simurgh.

    The Architect hides himself from his Realm. Multiple nations populate it, with the current ruling nation taking the title of Kingdom. The Architects main experiment is trying to reverse engineer a Primordial. The architect attempts to create, transform and link the souls of multiple godlike beings. Things such as 2nd circle demons severed and stretched into the proportions of a 3rd circle souls, Greater Elemental Dragons, Hekatonhires twisted with Generative Forces, Perfected Gods and other more quixotic things. This was designed in the last edition before Infernals and obviously before Sorcerous workings, so it would work even better in 3rd ed with Sorcerous workings being a thing. The Fetish souls is a severed and recreated 3rd circle soul, who is dreaming an unborn, but whose dreams actually function as the process of Reincarnation in replacement of Lethe. The bowels of the world are caverns were the unquiet dead, Hekatonhires, Demons and Raksha dwell. The Gods dwell in the sky amidst the Dreaming Soul. Pantheons exist connected to each of the would be 3rd Circle Souls. In this world I have several Primordial Races still existing, including the Kupo, diminutive fuzzy white creatures with vestigial wings, whose souls crystalize after death. Partially due to the Kupo, who have Castes and a natural aptitude towards Lore and Crafting, there exists Airships. So when the Kingdom first encounters Creation there is potential for great Trade and War. I wanted the option to have an Empire that could in fact stand up to the Realm and their full Legions. The population would be at around 10 millionish, upwards of maybe 30 millionish depending on final size, but probably around the 10 millionish range.

    I also have a basic idea for a great cracked hollowed moon only seen in the far south east savannahs, the Moon known as Chrysalis is filled with a First Age Civilization that fears the lands below, and stewarded by massive Glyph shaped Godtitans created long ago. Not sure the population, but probably a few million, built along the inner shell of the hollow moon.

    And I have the Painted World. So in my current game they are on a continent located on the northwest corner of the map, Lahat the End of the World. It is off the radar of Heaven, and even has its own false Heaven, Highpoint/Kalangitan atop a giant statue standing atop their own omphalos. An Island off the northwest of this continent is the Archipelago of Pangarap, Both the God of Dreams and the Islands themselves. Upon these islands the souls of those dreaming in Lahat exist in the skies of Pangarap. There is a village upon one of these islands, the Witch Village Talisman, and many miracles are theirs. Time exists slower in the Court of Pangarap as they only perceive the dreaming hours. The village is only populated by Sorcerers and their experiments. Every Witch needs to perform some form of sorcerous working to benefit the village as a form of Masters and Doctorate thesis. And they range greatly. So the village has many different curious benefits from cabinets that produce marble bricks, breathable lakebottom meadows, low gravity forests, beds that put your minds into animals and Golems when you sleep. They experiment with personal worlds. Including caverns simulating Creation in miniature, with tiny cities.

    The village recruits from children, either from those created from clay and music, Lahat, or elsewhere in Creation. But they also developed another source. The Painted World. Painted from the palette of the Dreams of Oramus upon a canvas of the Mysteries of Cecylene Painted Worlds or pocket worlds. The main Painted World is the world of Gallery, a painting of darkness circling a single flame. This is a vast world populated by vast human kingdoms. They speak the painted tongues, and the darkness of the hinterlands speaking Black Speak in contrast to the White Tongue the first spoken language. The Sun is actually a great curtained window atop a clockwork array far in the sky. The Window is a curtained Painted World of infinite flame. It exists as a plane of Fire in which a Solar Exaltation was bound as an experimental Solution to the Solar problems. The day is the windows opening releasing light and heat in vast quantities upon the world. The Oceans of the world are produced from a Painted World of infinite pure water. There also exists a Menagerie Painted World filled with Beasts of all kinds, and numerous behemoths, as well as several demons. There is also a Tableau Painted World filled with Demons, want, and anguish. This serves as a secret Oubliette. Humanity in this world exists in vast numbers ( I may even go bonkers here and say hundreds of millions just to have crazy architecture. Time I’m thinking passes quicker in Gallery, to the point maybe where there have been multiple apocalypses and rising from the ashes for humanity. Humanity here actually originated from empty primordial husks, beings made of coal and clay which were some form of immortal lifeform created by the Primordial. Being without death it was simpler to just banish their souls and spirit. By the time of the Witch Village these husks were filled with humanity, causing them to become humans, giants and lords. Apocalypses are heralded by the failure of death to take hold for members of the populace, who rise from their ashes more monstrous after each death.



    I’ll probably actually repost this as a separate thread. :P And these are just some of the main pocketworlds, I have a few other ones underground, undersea, and in the vault of the sky.

      Some similar ideas I've had:

      Terra - The World without Essence - an idea of using our modern world as a parallel universe for a science based Exalted idea. Besides all the dangers that really exist, there is a men in black conspiracy chasing down any exalt that enters, a colony of Ereta’een (from MoEP: Alchemicals) abducting people and possibly local eldritch abominations being ticked off at these intruding super-humans. Also, on the aforementioned science based Exalts regain essence their naturally, and too much essence using curdles reality and makes bad stuff happen to the essence user.

      Yaenma - a kingdom infested with demon blood rule separated from Creation by the magics of a Third Circle Demon called Beyul; part of this extended Yozi idea.


      The Greater Crater - a massive France sized crater on the Eastern edge of Creation, far away and removed from the rest of civilization. The crater's walls rise as high as mountains - with few passes allowing access to those high kingdoms. The crater is filled with a great forest, but the trees exude a mist that makes the Cloud Sea, that the people sail over. Great Mountains in the internal forest serve as island nations, and a great whirlpool in the centre, where an ancient an forbidden temple lies - so great and unworldly, even the fists of a Primordial could not break it. Five main societies/cultures exist around here - worshippers of a long dead Solar Sorceress who ruled in the Shogunate and define their city states by her sorcerous workings; various island cultures dedicated to the Incarnae (including one island of four armed mutants ruled by Dragon King priests in disguise);the Ba'Luuk a godblood tribe of nomadic warrior priests descended from the Storm Mother who got assigned here by a bureaucratic mistake in Yu-Shan; a load of 'elven' cultures that live within the undersea/sky forests (all based off this thread); and beastmen tribes that live in the mist sea and based off of aquatic micro-fauna. What they fear, is an element-dragon blood-insectoid beastman empire (completely ripped off Dzibilchán from this thread and turned up to 11) invading from the South and spreading their hyper-racist/fascist ideals.

          I posted this one my own thread for it, and yes it is inspired from exactly what you think, but I thought it fit here perfectly as well

          Lalotai,

          5th Soul of Kimbery
          Womb of the Great Mother,
          The Continent Beneath Her Embrace
          The Land of Monsters

          Kimbery is the seas which devours all and her tides roam across the bones of her former Prince, causing Malfeas endless torment. Many believe that anyone other than her children that descend beneath her waves is torn apart by her fury and annihilated. But this is not true. Deep beneath the waves and below the currents that scour flesh from bone, where the light from the Green Sun barely penetrates lies Lalotai, 5th Soul of the Great Mother.
          Lalotai was once Rhea, Glorious Land of the Chosen, the supercontinent which floated upon the Great Mother’s back, and served as the Promised Land to her favored children, creations and servitors. This barzinai was an expansive consciousness of the land that protected, nurtured and blessed its charges and was worshipped in return, both as an aspect of the Great Mother, and in her own right. Rhea was populated by many True Lintha as well as other beloved races that served the Great Mother with exceptional skill or devotion were awarded estates upon Rhea, but most of the inhabitants of Rhea were barzinoa, other devas, and the Great Mother’s unique creations (behemoths).
          When the Great Mother herself was invade by the forces of the Exalted during the Primordial War, she could not bare for the Exalted to sack her most beloved and sacred of possessions. She protected Rhea, with storm and maelstrom, but it was to little avail. The Exalted’s ships braved the dangers, landed upon the Forbidden Shores, and forced an army across the face of Rhea. As a last ditch effort to save her most beloved possessions and servitors from despoiled by the merely presence of the Exalted, Kimbery drowned Rhea within her own waves. Vast walls of water rose up and let loose a deluge across the land snuffing out nearly all life across Rhea and scarring Rhea forever. The Exalted believed that their force had won a pyrrhic victory, losing several Legions but denying Kimbery her protected staging area.
          However when Rhea fell forever beneath the Great Mother’s embrace, not all died, nor was this the end of the barzinai, though it was the last time she was ever seen by the outside world or existed in this way. Only much later during the First Age was the fate of Rhea discovered by the noted Eclipse Caste, Chiheisen, the Explorer Beyond All Horizons. His vessel “The Armada of 23 Hulls” discovered Lalotai quite by accident while plunging into Kmbery's depth beneath the waves of the Sea-That-Marched-Against-The-Flame.
          Here, lit almost entirely by the phosphorescence of the ever predator native flora and fauna, was a land of horrors where no such land should exist. Chiheisen was enthralled. The sky was the Sea above, with only the merest hint of Ligier’s glory shining through her depths, and the vast behemonths that dwelled within her direct embrace swimming barely visible in her twilit realm above. The land itself was barren rock, upon which rivers flowed between pools with no rhyme or reason but their own. The lands were punctuated by vast geysers and glowing flora that seemed to grow in unpredictable patches as far as anyone could see.
          He immediately invoked his rights as a Quicksilver Falcon to hold court with the ranking demon of this strange land and demanded to be taken to the new land’s demonic ruler supreme. No servants came, no demons brought forth a Demon Prince, but a presence could be felt, and an awareness that made the crew of the Armada tense and irrational at times.
          Unabated, Chiheisen ventured forth from the Armada with a small Draconic Host at is back as an honor guard. Chiheisen explored the strange world before him spirits high and weapons readied. He searched the land and fought many skirmishes against the myriad of dangerous flora and fauna before concluded that the lands nearby his landfall was populated by dozens of unique Behemoths and marred by ancient ruins that had been ground down over time. Twice his expedition was forced into a full retreat back to the Armada, where her Essence Cannons and Chiheisen’s sailing charms saved the expedition from destruction.
          Chiheisen had only scratched the surface, for the ecosystem of Lalotai was filled with not only demons and behemoths but also entire nations of ancient races lost to Creation and even Creation’s histories. All of these things survive and battle one another for living space and food. Lalotai is a harsh world, a far cry from the Promised Land she was as Rhea. However she also serves another purpose, for those Demons that have bucked the Imperfect Hierarchy of Hell, upset one of the Unquestionable, or have been Exiled from their normal station… Lalotai is hellish dream come true, as Lalotai is a place touchable only by those within its bounds or with the permission of Kimbery herself. Should they survive the trip to find Lalotai, they are nearly immune to outside repercussions.
          Thankfully for those that know of Lalotai’s secret existence (very few even amongst the Unquestionables and the Divine Bureaucracies know of the barzinai’s existence) can uncover the secret hymns that praise the name of Kimbery, this along with making the proper sacrifices in a prepared location can open gateways between Lalotai and an appropriate body of water or acid. This portal appears as a powerful maelstrom that roars blasphemies against Heaven & the Incarnae for the crimes that transformed beautiful Rhea into ravaged Lalotai. These Ways are actually a form of First Circle Demon (Subikiai, The Maw of White Capped Blasphemy) formed of a sticky grease like acid constantly swirling beneath their surface. Their bodies are long like a snake’s or worm’s and can stretch for hundreds of feet. The Subikiai swim through Kimbery’s endless depths but when summoned or surfaced they created a powerful maelstrom in the water/acids. By performing the ritual above they open a path between the two locations is forged.
          It is by these means the the Lintha Pirates have on many occasions performed miraculous vanishing acts, escaping the Western Fleets of the Realm and many others. The Lintha actually have three great strongholds on the Sunken Continent, each controls a permanent gateway to either the Wyld or Creation. These gateways and the manses that create them were made by the ancient True Lintha, and only those with the Lintha Bloodline may activate/use them. These Gates allow access to corresponding secret Manses, one each is consecrated to Rhea, Lalotai or Kimberry. These Manses are a closely guarded secret kept from all others. Each of these Temple Shrines are also considered outside of Fate and Heaven’s influences.

              And I don't mean to bring up very very old posts, but this comes from my Headcanon post from a long time ago.

              The Western Star of Calibration / The Nova of the Deep Wyld & the Remnant Archipelago

              During Calibration there are a number of astronomical items that cannot be seen at any other time (See my previous post for more information). In the west a bright wide star sits forever in the same spot. It has been measured by astronomers and astrologers in the both the first and second age, and to their amazement its essence reads identical to the Daystar but an indescribable distance into the Deep Wyld. Heaven is silent what it is, or as to why it is seen during Calibration. Theories abound. Is it afterglow from the Daystar's western landing birth? Is it the Unconquered Sun's hidden retreat? It is impossible to know.

              The Nova Barrier
              When the Mock Creation of Cemunian (the Constellation of Bone and Sinew) was destroyed by the Daystar's one and only use of his ultimate weapon during the Twin Centers dilemma. It left an ever expanding hollow sphere of Solar Flame in the deep deep Wyld. This expanding fireball burns through way-points, the unshaped, and anything else that strays too close to its boundaries. The Nova Barrier impacted all of the Mock Creation, burning it away but it is not perfectly symmetrical. It is conceivable that either some of the structures or powerful beings left "shadows" or "holes" in the barrier. These are likely a thinning of the barrier, so rather than diving directly into flames of eternal solar judgement hundreds of leagues thick they maybe only a few miles thick, or perhaps an ever shifting passage way as the flames eddy and shift inside the barrier.
              -- To the Fey it is a constant reminder of the horrors that are the Primordials, their pet gods, and their Creation.
              -- To Creation it is a curiosity. The Twin Centers Dilemma has long been lost to history, and those that do know of it, do not speak of it for it embarrasses the Unconquered Sun's sensibilities.

              Inside the Nova is the remnants of the Mock Creation. A world burned to ash eons ago, but not everything was destroyed (yay perfect defenses!), but that which remained was not untouched by the experience.

              The Remnant Archipelago: The central element of Mock Creation was turned to Ash, stability crushed to powder, what remains is an Archipelago of islands dancing in a cloud of ash. It is unbearably hot and knows no true night as the Nova casts light inward toward the Archipelago as much as it does outward and from all directions. The closest these island worlds knows to night is when a dense potion of the Ash Cloud or Mock Water Clouds envelopes an island and blots out much of the light. The remaining elements are similar to Creation's but made in mockery of them, sickly, strange and foul.
              -- Mock Water, what little can be found, is undrinkable poison, quenches no thirst and can unpredictably change states (liquid, gas, or solid).
              -- Mock Air, in the Remnant is stifling, stagnant, filled with Ash, and devours sounds.
              -- Mock Wood, within the Remnants grows plants of a sickly pallor, with strange shapes, covered in thorns, filled with poisons, and are nearly all predator.
              Lastly Fire comes in two forms. Solar Embers left behind by the Unconquered Sun, and the Remnant Flames of Cemunian.
              -- The Solar Embers burn bright and true, with a furosity that lets them devour anything native to the Remnants. These dangerous seeds of destruction are greatly feared and occasionally used as WMDs by those foolish enough to try to tame them.
              -- The Mock Flames devour heat and light, as well as whatever they are burning. This produces an unnatural darkness and cool that most of the denizens of the Remnants cultivate to make their citadels and dwellings more comfortable than the unbearable solar heat of the Nova Barrier.

              The Survivors of the Archipelago: All of the mortal races, and many of the enlightened ones were destroyed by the Fires of the Daystar, but some souls of the Primordial Cemunian, as well as his Mock Gods and Mock Elementals did survive the Nova in one way or another. They have created their own societies, and some of them have created their own servitor races as well.
              -- The Mock Gods which Cemunian created were divine bureaucrats filled with hate and enslaved with chains of fate. Unlike the Gods of Creation which served helping to create a balance and fulfill a role in the perfect machine, the Mock Gods constantly fought with their equals, connived against their greaters and tortured their lessers. The concepts of Mock Creation existed at a constant stated of war, its hierarchy one of strength and strength alone. How much would Cemunian laugh at the current state of Yushan, and how much must his own bureaucracies revile the Unconquered Sun as the continue to drift towards the Mockery that Cemunian created. Only the stronger or most protected of the Mock Gods survived. Though lesser gods may have been created by Dark Stars in intervening eons.
              -- The Mock Elementals are beings of great elemental mockery /"purity" but unlike those of Creation they are rarely if ever intelligent, almost entirely beasts to be tamed and enslaved by the Mock Gods. Mock Elementals do not tend toward a draconic visage as they gain strength. There are no Mock Elemental Dragons, either lesser or greater. Instead they become a Demense and spawn more of their own kind. These living Demenses are still capable of acting and must be physically subdued to attune to.
              -- The Servitors: These can generally be anything that the Mock Gods or Dark Stars decide to create. From mortal slaves, to enlightened warriors, to sorcerously constructed constructs.
              -- Dark Stars, the Lost Souls of Cemunian: The souls of Cemunian are as the stars in his constellation, each burn with an unearthly light and like the souls of other Primordials are varied and manifold. The souls of Cemunian that stood in Mock Creation to direct its defenses against the Primordial Forces until the collision destroyed Creation and the Mockery with it. These souls were amongst those that felt the full power of the Daystar's ignition. The vast majority were annihilated as was the greatest amongst them a 3rd Circle Soul of Cemunian which stood with its greatest guardians in the Mock Sky over the desolate hellish mockery of creation and its central elemental pole. Those that did survive, in a shelter of somekind or due to mystic puissance combined with not being the focus of the attack. However the Daystar's assault did not leave any unscathed. It burned away their primordial connection to Cemunian. This extinguished their lights making them appear much like the Mock Element of Fire, devouring light and standing as solid shadows. Dark Stars, come in greater (2nd Circle) and lesser (1st Circle). These Dark Stars control vast realms and often rule over Mock Gods, Mock Elementals, or their own created Servitors.
              -- Fairfolk Explorers: It is conceivable that some of the Fairfolk may have made their way into the Remnant Archipelago through holes in the Nova Barrier or mystic trickery. These Fairfolk would need to assume a body or form compatible with Mock Creation (via Mock Elements, or the Hate, Sorrow or Darkness that Cemunian imbued it with). If such Fairfolk exist they would be few and far between as solar flames destroy fairfolk as readily as any other Creatures of Darkness. Additionally since there are no Wyld Zones in the Remnant Archipelago, they would be forced to take a Demense or capture Mock Elementals and Servitors to devour their essence to survive. This likely makes them reclusive Lords constantly fighting their own Demense for control, or desperate raiders constantly looking to take new acquisitions for their larder.

              Why go to the Remnant?
              Well in my story hook it is "By Accident" but there are a number of reasons that one could go to the Remnant. First it is an invaluable place for studying the might of the Daystar, after all it is the only place its greatest weapon was ever used. Second, it was created by a Primordial, and that means it may have all sorts of treasures and useful materials found no where else.

              Story Hook (How Mine Went)
              In my own adventure, a pair first age solars, a Sagely Twilight Craftsman (Ervaxius, Auspicious Forger of Perfection), and an Eclipse Explorer Prince (Chiheisen, The Prince Upon The Horizon), discovered the nature of the "star" and created a Reality Engine protected Generation Vessel and sent it out into the Deep Wyld on a millennia long trip to reach and investigate the Novas flames, and the Daystar's devastating power. In addition they wanted to explore the Mock Creation (if any of it did still exist) in order find anything of value there as well (and to sate their curiosity). When the Vessel finally reached the Nova Barrier the 2 solars were supposed to use a transfer gate to board and protect the ship. However they had died some 900 years earlier during the usurpation and without a solar on board with protective Charms the vessel struck the Nova Barrier and barely survived. The crew was not so lucky. The Hulk has now been drifting in the Ash Clouds of the Remnant Archipelago ever since, its gleaming hull of first age materials corroding in the Mock Elements. The PCs in my game discover that the essence of their Manse and many others in the area have been redirected to a previously unknown Manse beneath a first age ruin. They may also discover that there are reports of demons in the nearby landscape and that countless families have gone missing in the area. When they investigate they find the portal to the wreck of the Generational Vessel inside the Remnant Archipelago. When they investigate the ship a battle with Remnant natives causes the Passage to close. The PCs must find a way to re-power the passage to go home, or they can explore the Remnant Archipelago to their heart's content.

              Note: It is important to remember that though some of Cemunian's souls were in Mock Creation when it was hit by the Daystar's mega-weapon, the vast majority Primordial's Jouten, and Souls (2nd & 3rd) had already made their escape. Cemunian is potentially still wounded and wandering the Deep Wyld.

                          Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                          Very cool with the Mock Creation. So do you have it all the sky extends into the Deep Wyld, or is it because the Western Star is lower on the Horizon? So no 3rd Circles exist in this place? Do the fires burn Eternally, what fueled by the Wyld and their puissant nature? So there are no 3rd Circle left within? Is the world growing because the nova is slowly growing or does the boundaries remain roughly the same? So it is a poisonous world and one would need potent charms or a survivor's suit and supplies to endure it?
                          1) In my Creation, the Sky, especially the Calibration Sky, is a metaphysical representation of existence beyond Creation. Malfeas (the Calibration Star), Autochthonia (The Rune Star), Nebula, Comets, etc... are all visible during calibration, and occasionally visible during auspicious (or inauspicious) nights at the right locations (the Top of Mt Meru is a good place). The nova is the "Western Star" because of the metaphysical place it holds. It is the ending of an existence, and an echo of that destruction.
                          2) In my version no 3rd Circles still exist within, though you could have a lesser 3rd circle remain (if you did I'd make it eternally wounded by the battle). Mock Creation is ruled by 2nd Circle Dark Stars and Mock Gods, often using Elemental Demesnes as their capitals.
                          3) The Nova will burn eternally, becoming ever larger, but ever thinner. Think of it as a shell of constant volume, eventually it will become a paper thin wall that is painful to pass through but otherwise merely limits sight and teleportation across the barrier. In my game during the the First Age it was powerful enough to cripple a First Age Wyld Exploration Vessel and destroy its crew, and in the Second Age it was powerful enough to make Solar Exalted beneath essence 4 look at it like a death sentence to pass through it.
                          4) The world is intensely poisonous to not only mortals, but anything from Creation or the Wyld. This includes Mountain Folk, Gods, Elementals, and the like. For any such being to survive here they must have an environmental suit OR charms that allow them to survive environmental conditions and attacks.
                          -- On a side note: Demons of the right concepts and lineages would probably not only survive without a problem here, but thrive. A rather chilling thought about what they might be able to do with this place. Ghost would probably also be fine, but this world has no Underworld so it doesn't generate ghosts on its own.
