    The ST is looking over the player's characters:

    "Ok, let's see. Tim, you're the Zenith, huh? Good amount of Presence, Performance, and Lore here. Intelligence 5 and Perception 4, okay. That flaw should be fun when you get near a tavern, heh heh."

    "Chris, you're the Dawn? Wow, Wits 4, Intelligence 3 - kinda playing against type, but I can see it. Brawl 5, very nice, Lore 3 with a military background, okay. You've got a bunch of Archery, I see, but a Major Principle of "I hate ranged weapons"? Okay, interesting character trait."

    "Al is playing Twilight, I see. Intelligence and Wits at max, and Lore and Linguistics as well, I see. Craft decent too. I like that Major Principle of "I'm the best!", lots of potential there."

    "Beth, Night Caste. All the Dexterity, Wits, and good Intelligence. And I see you've maxed out Larceny, Stealth, and Athletics. And that intimacy towards "danger" should be fun."

    "And, last but not least, Gina's Eclipse. Maxed Manipulation, makes sense. Larceny high, interesting choice, and Presence. No dots in Performance? Okay, it's a character thing, I get that."

    "So, all of you have Resources 5? You're basically not interested in money at all, huh, you've got it all already? Well, based on your shared intimacies and talents, I think I can definitely work something up for all of you to do together..."

                    The Wyld Hunt has been pursuing the Solar Exalt Speaker of Kings for years now, but he's always a step ahead of them. During his last raid on an imperial supply dock, they almost got him, but he slipped away, and all they captured was one of the mortals he'd tricked into helping him. The campaign is played out in flashbacks as the Dragon-Blooded investigator interrogates and pressures the poor mortal, finally making him break down, realize he was working with an Anathema, and name one of his companions as Speaker of Kings. As the broken-down mortal leaves, the Dragon-Blooded, satisfied, sits back, only to realize a moment too late that the "mortal" made the entire story up as he went along and was almost certainly Speaker of Kings himself.
