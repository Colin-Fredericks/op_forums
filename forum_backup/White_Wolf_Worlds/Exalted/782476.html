    [Ex3] Sidereal Update Homebrew

    12-08-2015, 04:45 PM
    With the document now representing the current state of this project I will update the link here periodically:
    Version 1.1
    So we already have threads that are bringing (or have already brought) updates for Lunars, Alchemicals and Dragon Kings. So I thought that Sidereals should get some love too. This is going to be where I post my update for Sidereals. If anyone else is already doing one I'd love to hear about it though and see where we have approached things differently.

    I have ideas written up for pretty much the entire Sidereal charmset, but I have only just started to write up the actual charm text in the last couple of days so I only have a few finished. I'll post the first of them tomorrow and update this thread with more as and when I finish them. But to kick things off here is character creation for an agent of Heaven's Bureau of Fate (ie a non-Ronin).

    Character Creation
    Step 1: Character Concept and Caste
    • Consult with other players and come up with an appealing, heroic concept.
    • Pick out the Caste that fits best, and note down its anima effects.

    Step 2: Attributes
    • Mark down one dot in each Attribute.
    • Divide 8 dots among primary Attributes, 6 dots among secondary Attributes, and 4 dots among tertiary Attributes (see p. 121).

    Step 3: Abilities
    • Select five Caste Abilities from the relevant list.
    • All Sidereals begin with Martial Arts (though not Brawl) as a Favored Ability.
    • Select four other Favored Abilities, which may not be the same as Caste Abilities.
    • All Sidereals start with the following abilities: Awareness 2, Brawl 1, Bureaucracy 2, Linguistics 1, Lore 3, Martial Arts 2, Occult 2, Socialize 1, Stealth 1 and at least one dot in Archery, Melee or Thrown.
    • Chosen of Journeys must also have: Athletics 2, Resistance 2 and Survival 2.
    • Chosen of Serenity must also have: Craft or Performance 2, Medicine 2 and Presence 2.
    • Chosen of Battles must also have: Athletics 2, Dodge 2 and War 2.
    • Chosen of Secrets must also have: Integrity 2, Investigation 2 and Larceny 2.
    • Chosen of Endings must also have Dodge 2, Integrity 2, Martial Arts 3 and Stealth 2.

    • Divide 14 dots among all Abilities. None may be raised higher than 3 without spending Bonus Points, and each Favored Ability must have at least one dot.
    • Assign four specialities

    Step 4: Colleges
    • Sidereals start with 1 dot in each of their Maiden's colleges. Additionally they gain +1 dot in any college where they have the related ability at 3+, or +2 dots if they have the ability rated at 5.
    • Spend up to 7 dots on colleges.

    Step 5: Merits
    • All Sidereals start with the Language (Old Realm), Martial Arts and Mentor (3) merits.
    • Spend up to 10 dots on Merits.

    Step 6: Charms
    • Select 15 Charms, keeping in mind their requirements.

    Step 7: Intimacies
    • Choose at least four Intimacies, including at least one Defining Intimacy, one Major Intimacy, one positive Intimacy, and one negative Intimacy.

    Step 8: Bonus Points
    • Spend 18 Bonus Points.

    Step 9: Finishing Touches
    • Record Essence rating (1), Personal Essence pool ([Essence x 2] + 9), Peripheral Essence pool ([Essence x 6] + 25), Health Levels (-0, -1, -1, -2, -2, -4, Incapacitated), and Willpower (5).
    Caste Abilities
    Journeys: Athletics, Bureaucracy, Resistance, Ride, Sail, Survival & Thrown.
    Serenity: Craft, Dodge, Linguistics, Medicine, Performance, Presence & Socialize
    Battles: Archery, Athletics, Lore, Melee, Presence, Resistance & War
    Secrets: Integrity, Investigation, Larceny, Lore, Occult, Stealth & Thrown
    Endings: Awareness, Brawl, Bureaucracy, Integrity, Medicine, Occult & Stealth

    So because this is not a particularly interesting first post here are some of my personal thoughts on how Sidereals work. These are also the themes I am trying to keep to in this update, where I can.
    • Sidereals have smaller dice pools than other exalted and can manipulate the vagaries of fate. This to me indicates that they can't as easily exceed mortal limitations in the same way as other exalted. So while a Sidereal can consistently be at the pinnacle of mortal ability they don't go much beyond that. Example: A sidereal can perform a feat of strength requiring strength 5 with ease (relatively). However they find it impossible to go beyond human capability and say lift and throw a yeddim.
    • My count of how many charms there are in the Sidereal 'set', including some names that have been brought back from 1st Ed, sits at around 170. With individual abilities having between 5 and 9 charms. Once I've updated existing charms I may go back and add a couple to the smaller trees, but that will be after the first pass at least.
    • Finally Sidereal charms generally don't do dice tricks (recurring re-rolls or double x's). This leads to them having similar 'average' rolls as other exalted, though with much lower variance. This also means that there is a far lower likelihood of a Sidereal having an exceptional result such as a Solar might get.
