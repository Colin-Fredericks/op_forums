    State of Anathema for Third Edition

    04-09-2015, 06:38 AM
    This is Urs from Anathema.

    There was some discontent over the lack of news lately, way over in another thread.
    In this post, I'll try to remedy the situation and give you some insight into our collaboration with Onyx Path as well as our progress. Please understand that I can't go into details; I am NDAed and can only speak about the general outline.

    Our main contact in the writing team is Holden. We are in close contact with him, meaning: unmoderated, direct, private messages.
    Some weeks ago, he sent us a pre-release version of the rules, more than enough to build Anathema with.
    He answers any questions we have clearly and quickly, and generally does his best to keep us in the loop.
    Just to make it clear, Onyx Path is not responsible for our doings, errors or lapses, as we are not beholden to them. We did not ask for money, and will receive none. In fact, we - as a team - explictly decided not to make this a paid project, so we never had to discuss this with OP.

    Rules in hand, we obviously have a lot of work to do, reading as well as coding.
    Generally speaking, work is progressing orderly. We are working in a hidden repository, that's why even the tech-savvy among you didn't see any updates on GitHub for ages. Progress could be better, though, since our time and energy are not as plenty as they were back in 2013, when we first signed up for this.
    I have seen speculation that we only need to update the Charms - this is decidedly not the case, the changes required are much more extensive: There are assumptions in Anathema that just don't hold any longer, and we still need to figure out how to deal with a few major points.
    However, all of the minor changes and additions are done by now. With a version I have running on my desktop this very moment, I could probably adopt more than eighty percent of the Exalts I or my group have played in the past; dealing with some rough edges manually.

    All of this said, we can't promise to have a fully-fledged version the moment the PDF goes live - just as I said back in September.
    Also, OP and the Anathema team have yet to speak about content inclusion beyond the usual - we have left this issue open so far, because it is the least of our concerns.

    Finally, please bear in mind that Rich knows exactly as much about our work as you do, so he just couldn't give you more details.

    I hope this clears matters up somewhat.
    If you have any questions, please post below. I will try to answer them as good as I can.

    UPDATE: We have solved one of the puzzles mentioned above.
    UPDATE: Solved another puzzle. It gets harder from here on out.
    UPDATE: The code is now public. We needed to clean out the Charm data before publishing. Release is still some time off, as the puzzlers are unresolved.

                                                      04-09-2015, 03:44 PM
                                                      Originally posted by Rahod View Post
                                                      Question: Will this be only the character creation or will there be the campaign and storytellers parts included in the release?
                                                      It will be character management, that is, creation and growth.
                                                      Our ideas for campaign and storyteller support never really took off, part because we were busy enough supporting the myriad options Exalted has to offer, part because we just don't know what a should go there.

                                                      Question: Will you have a conversion option to take a character from 2nd to 3rd are we going to have to redo our characters we in the current release?
                                                      We do not plan to include a conversion option. Reasons are both technical and in the effort required.

                                                      Question: Will the release of Anathema have mortals as well as solars or will there be a wait for that?
                                                      Both. As far as I remember, Mortals are completely done already, and a release without Solars just wouldn't make much sense, since that is where the complexity lies.
                                                      At this stage, I won't rule out an early-access version with mortals only to get feedback on certain UI decisions.

                                                      Question: Will you be using the stock character sheet or will there be a custom design. Or will there be a option to choose between stock and custom?
                                                      We have offered custom sheets based on voidstates layouts for time immemorial, and will continue to do so. I expect them to be similar to what we have, with obvious changes for 3e.
                                                      If somebody brings us code to print into the stock sheet, we'll take it.
