                                  I managed to get the write up for Anapuul, and here it is. Keep up the great work lostlight.

                                  heres what I have

                                  Anapuul
                                  Anapuul was once known as Chsikabel, That Most Wonderful Chime, the most beautiful and vain of the Primordials. She was sent into a coma during the Primordial War, and was horribly vivisected in soul-pruning experimentation by the Lunar Exalted, ending in her Fetich Death. Locked inside Malfeas, Anapuul is now the most horrible of the Yozis, a genderless, faceless mass of potholes and barrows stretching deep below the surface of the Demon City. Despising individuality and vanity, Anapuul would see Creation dissolved into a mass of mud, its peoples assimilated into a single hive-mind, nothing standing out from anything else. It is entirely possible that a persuasive or anarchistic Infernal might tempt The Teeming Barrow into opening its Charmset, or perhaps The Ebon Dragon might entice it to lend its aid to The Reclamation, despite its disappointment in Malfeas and hatred for Cecelyne.
                                  Souls
                                  Fetich Soul of Anapuul: The Many In One, First Souls of Anapuul
                                  Chinensi, The Lady Most Beautiful, Third Soul of Anapuul
                                  Glees, the Wonderful Chimers
                                  Aneina, Child Of Eighteen Entrapments, the Warden Soul of The Lady Most Beautiful
                                  Balons, the Chortling Slimes
                                  Rikijaz, the Gilded Yojimbo, the Messenger Soul of The Lady Most Beautiful
                                  Kaatea
                                  Hoard Beetles
                                  Progeny of the Gilded Yojimbo
                                  Kaatea are a breed of insectoid demon that range from beetles the size of a man’s fist to centipedes bigger than horses, purpose-designed by Rikijaz. Their one unifying property is their ability to camouflage themselves in a particular environment – treasuries. When placed inside a treasury, a kaatea will examine the valuables within, cooing gently and caressing them with its many feelers, before curling up in a central place and disguising itself. The disguise is purely physical, with no element of illusion – the colour of the carapace shifts, the legs contort and join, the feelers wrap around themselves, with the final result being an item that both fits in amongst the other treasures of the room, and seems just slightly more valuable than the rest. A kaatea that folds itself up into a chest will arrange its feelers to form gilding around the “lock”, while a kaatea that contorts itself into a statue might shift chunks of its carapace into iridescent shades, mimicking inlaid gems. It is only when someone whose authority the kaatea does not respect enters and tries to steal them that they react – the lid of a chest clamps down even as its planks unfold into a multitude of needle-point legs, while a gold coin reveals a set of stingers that drip acidic venom. Because of the time they spend in such places, more powerful kaatea are great connoisseurs of artifacts and metalwork, often discussing the items they are placed amongst with their employers – after all, they’ve mimicked most of them.
                                  Labali, That Sorrowful Song, the Reflective Soul of The Lady Most Beautiful
                                  Rhangla, the Dozen Knucklers
                                  Lenono, Insect Who Crushes, Eighth Soul of Anapuul
                                  Haliledus, The Ne'erending Writhe, the Expressive Soul of Insect Who Crushes
                                  Yalleps, the Dimorphic Toad-Maidens
                                  Gichi, the Malevolent Fingernails
                                  Equmais, the Giggling Librarian, the Indulgent Soul of Insect Who Crushes
                                  Huknals, the Masked Imps
                                  Eregant, the Titled Shift, Fourteenth Soul of Anapuul
                                  Zakekeed, the Town-Weaving Serpent, the Wisdom Soul of The Titled Shift
                                  Figanady
                                  Lopsided Trinketeers
                                  Progeny of the Town-Weaving Serpent
                                  Figanadies are a humanoid breed of demon, perhaps best distinguished from humans by their lack of a nose. Or ears. Or mouth. Or eyes. Or legs. Or perhaps the large number of arms that sprout from seemingly random spots on their body. In any case, their slow, steady motions are rather unnerving, guided as they are only by the figanady’s supernatural sense of touch. It is this sense that leads figanadies in their most common profession, the creation of trinkets. Living in a world of touch alone grants them a peculiar aesthetic ideal, one with an alien appeal, and a figanady will spend his crafting-time constantly turning the item in question, over and over, until its feel runs perfectly toward his design. A number of figanady trinkets can be found in the galleries of Realm nobility, while more unscrupulous summoners and employers turn their talents toward producing perfect forgeries – indeed, most of the figanady stalls that dot the markets of The Brass City have sections hidden behind thick curtains to house those wares that might lead to the displeasure of a Citizen or (Malfeas forbid) an Unquestionable.
                                  Miga-jan, the Turning Duchy, the Reflective Soul of The Titled Shift
                                  Bristaltes, the Scuttling Sparks
                                  Of Chsikabel
                                  When the titans had gathered in the one place that was Zen-mu, there was one that came whose name was Chsikabel.
                                  She came to her brethren because she sought admiration, and for a time she found it, for she was undoubtedly the most beautiful among them. Even the Ultimate Darkness looked upon her with desire, though he hated her for it, and quietly swore to punish her for this presumption. Soon, however, her siblings found other things to fascinate themselves, and Chsikabel instead trailed behind their king. Mardukth sought himself in all things, and it pleased Chsikabel to draw his attention to her, even if only for a time.
                                  When the two kings of the titans came to war against eachother, That Most Wonderful Chime stood to the sidelines with her brother Qaf. She wrung her hands and preened and imagined it was her they fought over, while her unmovable brother was silent, for it made no difference to him (as nothing does or did).
                                  Upon the end of the war, Chsikabel was quick to comfort her fallen king, for she found his defeated attentions more soothing than the burning eyes of The Empyreal Chaos. All things may fall, she thought, except my own beauty.
                                  Death Of Chsikabel, Birth Of Anapuul
                                  In the time before time, when Creation had not yet been Created, there were the Primordials. Mighty beyond compare, majestic beyond rebuke. Among these titans, the most beautiful was Chsikabel, That Most Wonderful Chime. She was exquisite to the point that there could be no comparison, vain to the point that there was none allowed, and so utterly diverse in aesthetics that when Creation was formed there was not a corner that did not feel her touch. From Chsikabel’s idle design sprung creatures and sights of such beauty that He Who Bleeds The Unknown Word struggled to describe them, and of such total impracticality that She Who Lives In Her Name was sorely tempted to ban her from the planning process, held back only by Malfeas’ lust for the whimsical Primordial.
                                  When Creation was complete, That Most Wonderful Chime spent much of her time being admired by those creatures she could stand to look at – the most beautiful gods and goddesses of Yu-Shan, though each was practically deformed compared to the gorgeous Primordial. She largely disdained the Games, perhaps out of a fear that they rivalled her own perfection. Those times she turned hir gaze upon Creation, she set about improving it – a city with a dilapidated district throwing off its balance would be met with a gasp of horror, before being replaced by a perfectly symmetrical plateau of coloured glass. A lamed faun would be wiped from the world, an exquisite tableau of wax butterflies taking its place. An island whose shape Chsikabel found unsightly would swiftly find itself transformed into a pleasing tune carried on a perfumed breeze. The most beautiful Primordial spread her benevolence to all things.
                                  With the onset of the Primordial War, Chsikabel avoided engaging in all but the most perfunctory engagements, never allowing her holy world-flesh to be marred in even the slightest. Eventually, however, she was brought to battle. Four minutes and eight dead Celestials later, one of her Second Circle Souls was slain, an expressive soul in the form of a great feathered bell, named Emichi. A relatively minor loss, all things considered. Chsikabel evidently disagreed, as she let out an anguished cry and fell utterly comatose, braided fingers of glass and bone covering her beautiful eyes and blocking her perfect ears. Momentarily puzzled by this anticlimax, the remaining Exalts quickly grasped the situation and towed away her elegant jouten-form, taking each of her remaining souls prisoner alongside it.
                                  When the time came to seal the Yozis away in the inverted body of their king, the Exalted considered the problem of pruning their souls to allow such an imprisonment. None wanted a repeat of the rebirth of Adrián, after all. It was a spider-totem Lunar by the name of Salutak-Nei that proposed a solution. They had a comatose Primordial to experiment upon – why not do so? Soon enough the Lunar and his team of Solar and Sidereal comrades set about their vivisection, scaling the heights of the unconscious jouten-form and pruning soul after soul in an attempt to divine the workings of the hierarchy, while Chsikabel shuddered and convulsed in her nightmares of mutilation and imperfection. By the time they were finished, almost every one of Chsikabel’s souls lay dead, and all evidence that she had once been the most beautiful thing in all Creation was gone, leaving only an indescribable form of dripping bonelessness. The other Primordials were locked away, and what remained of That Most Beautiful Chime was tossed in alongside them.
                                  Within Malfeas, the stinking, sleeping carcass of what had once been Chsikabel sank to the bottom of Kimbery, viewed with disgust and sorrow by its bound siblings. It sank further still, becoming the absolute deepest part of Malfeas, a dripping series of caverns and barrows avoided even by the revellers of Erembour. The thing stayed there, unnamed and formless, existing only to dissolve any who touched it, subsuming them into its own tarry mass. It was not until Malfeas’ failed bid for escape through his Solar Akuma that That Which Was Chsikabel made itself known, piling another disappointment on the demon-emperor’s titanic shoulders. Among the gathered jouten that day was a faceless thing with skin of tar and far too many scuttling legs. It was announced by trumpeting demons as Anapuul, the Teeming Barrow, and there was not a Yozi there who did not recognise it as Chsikabel bitterly reborn
