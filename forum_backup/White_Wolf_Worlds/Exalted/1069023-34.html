                                                Thanks!
                                                Just wanted to provide a little angle on this!
                                                There are some charms that become powerful as the character progresses, yet maintain this power-scaling even when chosen as a supernal. I'm thinking that this is by design (to keep somewhat of a dramaturgical curve to the character's progress and their powerlevel).

                                                If we would assume that my assumption is true (or correct enough to work^^), then this would seem like a perfect charm to exemplify that design-idea! A starting character can get access to it through having Occult as their supernal (thus making the supernal give -something-), but may only create a little/small spirit through it's use. The more the character grows, or progresses through the story, though, the larger the kind/s of spirits they can create become. Becoming not only a means to meet the rising levels of drama/opposition in the story, but also becoming an external expression of the character's growth (in power or personality, depending on the kind of story it is!)
