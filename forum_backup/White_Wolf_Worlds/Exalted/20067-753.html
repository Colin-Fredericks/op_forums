                                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
                                Which one is Endless Obsession Feint again?
                                Basically, anyone you triumph over in a social scene will obsess over your victory, causing them to take penalties on many mental and social rolls for a number of days, after which they form an Intimacy toward you that could be positive or negative.

                                Sun King Radiance, in turn, causes successful social influence or use of Resolve to inspire those subservient to you; they can choose to take a Major Intimacy of respect for you, and emulating your actions gives them a bonus on social influence and Resolve for a scene.

                                Worshipful Lackey Acquisition causes an enemy who witnesses you achieving a goal through lecture, oration, et cetera., to serve you as if he has a Defining Principle to do so, for a number of days.

                                Keep in mind that all three are Permanent Charms.

                                ​
                                Endless Obsession Feint
                                Cost: —; Mins: Socialize 5, Essence 3
                                Type: Permanent
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Permanent
                                Prerequisite Charms: Wise Counsel (Flashing Soul Reform)
                                This Charm embodies the awe-inspiring resonance of the Solar Exalted, whose very presence shapes the flow of nations. This Charm is triggered by a Solar’s success in a social scene, where through social influence she achieves a significant objective. She might broker an alliance, arrange a wedding, cancel an assassination or convince the local Guild to decrease opium production. Any objective success she attains at the expense of another social actor in the scene leaves that character in a state of obsession. The Lawgiver is an enigma, and though he can’t remember the substance of her arguments, her casual wit haunts his dreams. For (Essence) days the character suffers a -2 dice penalty to all Bureaucracy, Craft, Investigation, Linguistics, Lore, Occult, Performance and Socialize rolls, with 1s on these rolls each counting as -1 success. When this obsession wears off, the target automatically develops a positive or negative Intimacy for the Solar, depending on how the days went. This effect may apply to multiple characters in the same scene.

                                Sun King Radiance
                                Cost: —; Mins: Integrity 5, Essence 2
                                Type: Permanent
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Permanent
                                Prerequisite Charms: Any five Performance, Presence, or Socialize Charms
                                In every word and action, the Solar manifests a core of magnificent integrity. Even the hearts of the wicked are filled with awe. Each time the Solar succeeds at social influence or successfully applies her Resolve, any witness who considers himself a follower of, subservient to, or less powerful than the Solar might be profoundly and forever changed in that moment. If the Solar’s action resonates with the character, he may accept an automatic Major Intimacy of respect for the Solar. On the back of this, he also gains a temporary boost of excellence on a scene of his choosing. During that scene, while acting toward a goal he wishes to fulfill, while using the Solar’s actions as a model, the character gains three bonus dice to all social influence rolls for the rest of the scene, and has his Resolve boosted by one.

                                Worshipful Lackey Acquisition
                                Cost: —; Mins: Presence 5, Essence 3
                                Type: Permanent
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Permanent
                                Prerequisite Charms: Underling-Promoting Touch
                                As the sun rises in prominence, shadows grow long upon the earth. Whenever the Solar achieves a goal through lecture, prophecy, oration, or other forms of performance, those who conspire against her are twisted against themselves. Enemies who witness her success must roll (Wits + Integrity) with a penalty of the Solar’s Essence, against a difficulty of the Solar’s strongest Social Attribute. Those who fail become enthralled with the Solar’s wisdom and greatness and are inclined to follow her and do as she commands as if they had a Principle defining them as one of her acolytes. The character does not stop hating the Solar, but must grovel, cloy and defer to her for (Essence) days. Occasionally when this effect ends, if the character was treated well by the Solar, his negative Intimacies for her are diminished or destroyed and he becomes one of her true allies.

                                                Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                                How do you see a character who possesses Sun-King Radiance, Endless Obsession Feint, and Worshipful Lackey Acquisition?

                                                To analyze this combination of charms, I'd first clarify what Sun King Radiance works on. It says it applies to "any witness who considers himself a follower of, subservient to, or less powerful" than the user. I think that "less powerful" should be interpreted in a social sense - that is, the witness accepts that the Solar has a place in a social hierarchy that they recognize and acknowledge that it's a place capable of exerting more influence than them. A Solar who's just stepped off the boat from the Threshold to the Imperial City and has no Backing or Influence that applies in the Realm is not going to be applying Sun King Radiance on all the peasants, for example, not even when they incautiously flare their anima and reveal that they have enormous spiritual power.

                                                That said, I'd look at the targets of each charm and interpret it based on the overlap. Endless Obsession Feint and Worshipful Lackey Acquisition apply to enemies specifically, so an enemy, upon witnessing her succeeding at a social goal that opposes them, through a type of performance, will become obsessed with her wit, and has to make a fairly difficult roll to avoid becoming subservient to her. If they then witness her succeeding at social influence or applying her Resolve, and that action would "resonate" with them, they can accept an intimacy of respect toward the Solar.

                                                This raises the question of what actions resonate with characters. I'd generally rule that that's tied to their intimacies. An action resonates if the witness' own intimacies would incline them to do the same thing, and if none of their intimacies pushes them to do something different. So the Solar just persuading a random merchant to sell her some bolts of cloth at a discount wouldn't resonate with the martial Dynast watching her, because the Dynast has no intimacies that would encourage him to bargain with a merchant or suggest that getting a good deal is a positive thing. But when the Solar challenges another Dragonblood to a duel, that resonates with the Dynast, both because it lines with his Principle for honorable, straightforward combat, and because he hates the particular Dragonblood that the Solar has challenged. If the Solar challenged a Dragonblood that the Dynast loved, on the other hand, the conflict between the Principle and the positive Intimacy would probably prevent the action from resonating.

                                                So, putting this all together, the combation of Sun-King Radiance, Endless Obsession Feint, and Worshipful Lackey Acquisition means that as long as the Solar accomplishes her goals through performance, her enemies will tend to grovel and scrape around her, and those enemies who she directly overcame in the process will be obsessed with her and worse at their own social actions. The enemies won't lose their negative Intimacies against the Solar, though, and outside her presence can probably take actions against her, though they'll be susceptible to social influence forbidding that. Enemies who have succumbed to Worshipful Lackey Acquisition (but not necessarily Endless Obsession Feint), if the Solar later takes actions or resists influence that resonates with their own beliefs, gain intimacies of respect toward the Solar. An intimacy of respect, of course, doesn't preclude also having negative intimacies, but it will mean that even after the effects of Worshipful Lackey Acquisition have worn off, the Solar will often have a useful hook to influence them later.
