    After playing for over a year, I finally got to creating evocations for my artifact. This is my first draft, so commentary and critique would be appreciated. I'm planning to add some more evocations, up to at least Essence 4, but am uncertain how I'd like to shape the tree – either an cross, with the next evocation requiring both of the Ess 2 ones, and then that allowing the upper two arms, or two parallel paths, with two combining evocations after the Ess 2 and 4 ones.
    I haven't checked the newer materials if Wavecleavers are their own thing in 3rd ed, but this would probably be one if they are.

    Surface Current
    (Black Jade Reaper Daiklave, Artifact ••••)

    Although Surface Current is known to be older, the first reliable records of it are from when the Empress donated it to a grandchild of Ledaal, as part of the hoard of her gifts to the newly founded house. Ledaal Valevuo carried it for many years, passing it on to a worthy younger relative when she retired from active duty. It was last seen in the hands of Ledaal Uhskals on his trip to investigate the Skullstone Archipelago, but he escaped with barely his life, and has been tight-lipped about what happened to him on his trip. Since then, there are tales of an newly exalted solar carrying it after his trip to the Skullstones, but these have not been confirmed.

    Surface Current is a gently curving blade made of black jade, with faint moonsliver waves traveling along the blade. Earlier records state that the guard separating the blade and grip was a small band of orichalcum and soulsteel, but according to the latest rumours, the hilt is now guarded by a small soulsteel cup. It has two hearthestone slots, one in the pommel and one hidden by the cup.

    Attunement: 5m
    Type: Medium (+3 ACC, +12 DMG, +1 DEF, OVW 5)
    Tags: Lethal, Melee, Balanced
    Hearthstone slot: 2
    Era: Late Shogunate?

    Evocations of Surface Current

    Surface Current carries it's wielder above things that might impede others, notably unstable or moving surfaces, or swiftly flowing fluids. A character attuned to Surface Current ignores all penalties from such things, and when activating charms that would counteract them (such as Graceful Crane Stance, Feather Foot Style, or using Increasing Strength Exercise to stride through a flooding river), their activation cost is decreased by 2 (to a minimum of 1 mote).

    Swinging Deck Vertigo

    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Resonant, Withering-Only, Stackable
    Duration: Instant

    When Surface Current connects with an opponent, a pulse of essence pushes through them, down their legs and on to the bottom of their feet, keeping them off-balance. If the withering damage done was greater than the (Essence) of the opponent, they suffer a special -1 onslaught penalty that lasts as long as the wielder keeps the opponent as their primary focus. This effect is cumulative, up to the wielder's (Essence) in penalties, but it does decrease by one each turn that the wielder does not act against that opponent.
    Resonant: At Essence 2+, the penalty is applied when the wielder lowers their opponent below their own initiative, even if the damage was not greater than the opponents (Essence).

    Railing-Colliding Nausea

    Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Swinging Deck Vertigo

    On a surging, slippery deck, the land-lubber is more likely to hurt themself than others. If you successfully parry, your opponent loses 1 initiative as you slowly turn the tide of the battle against them.

    Mendaciously Smooth Shorewaters

    Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Resonant
    Duration: One turn

    Using Surface Current teaches one to not show one's intentions clearly. When the wielder knows they are being targeted by an Read Intentions action, they may activate Mendaciously Smooth Shorewaters to gain +2 to their Guile until the end of their next turn.
    Resonant: The wielder may also activate Mendaciously Smooth Shorewaters as a supplemental charm with an instant duration when making gambits based on deception, such as Distract. The difficulty of the Gambit is lowered by 2, to a minimum of 2.

    Rip-Current Deception

    Cost: 1Wp; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Resonant
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Mendaciously Smooth Shorewaters

    Though the surface may be calm, the undercurrents can be fierce. When your opponent fails in their Read Intentions, you may activate this charm to give them a false motivation or Intimacy that they will believe to be true. Their belief will be sustained until it causes them to fail in a social action that should have succeeded, they see you act counter to what they read from you, or they spend 1 Wp to accept that they have been deceived.
    Resonant: You may pay 5m instead of 1 Wp as the cost.

        The Dealer's Gambit (Decks of Adamant-Edged Cards, Artifact *)


        Fluff Goes Here! First age Eclipse! Invented the Fivefold Deck. Went a little crazy and made this before trying for the Games of Divinity. Peach-blend playing cards made from the trees of immortality, all five magical materials and adamant.

        Each of the five decks of the Dealer's Gambit is made of 67 perfectly illustrated and exceedingly fine cards, roughly the size of a man's hand. The card faces are purest white, with symbols and decorations of the finest quality. They are coated in an incredibly thin layer of adamant. This makes the cards unable to be bent, marked, or defaced, though they are flexible enough once attuned.

        .
        .
        Attunement: 6 motes

        Type: Thrown Special (+9 DMG, OVW 1)

        Tags; Lethal, Thrown (Short), Concealable, Cutting, Mounted

        Accuracy: Close +4, Short +3, Medium +2, Long -1, Extreme -3

        Hearthstone Slot(s): None

        Era: Dawn of the Deliberative

        Special: Each of the five decks that make up the Dealer's Gambit is backed with a different magical material. They modify the statistics above for any deck of which they contribute to the Count.

        Orichalcum: Accuracy +1, +1 Overwhelming

        Moonsilver: Overwhelming +2

        Starmetal: Thrown (Medium)

        Jade: Damage +1, Overwhelming +1

        Soulsteel: Damage +4.

        Each deck resonates based on the material it is backed with.


        .
        .
        .
        Evocations of the Dealer's Gambit

        Counting Cards: A single deck has a Count of 1. Each additional deck added increases the Count by 1, and the attunement cost by 1.

        Resonant: If you are resonant with the materials used for a deck, do not increase the attunement cost for adding that deck to the Count.

        Winnable Evocations: If you defeat a significant adversary who is carrying one of the decks in either a game involving them or in Combat, you may unlock one winnable Evocation for which you qualify (including the one for their deck, should you end the contest owning it). This does not have to be in a lethal contest, but the stakes must be significant (a body part, significant resources, an Artifact or an onerous task, for instance). Friendly games or sparring matches without consequence do not count.


        .
        .
        Dealer's Shuffle

        Cost: 3 motes, or 3m 1 WP Mins: Essence 1

        Type: Simple

        Keywords: Dissonant, Resonant, (Winnable)

        Duration: Instant

        Prerequisites: None

        This causes each of the cards of the deck you are attuned to to vanish Elsewhere, and then re-appear randomized before you. This will have the side effect of retrieving cards which have been lost, stolen or palmed from the scene around him. This can be treated as an automatically successful gather ammunition action.
        By spending a WP this extends the effect to everywhere, even from a Sorceror's Tower, Elsewhere, Yu-Shan, the Wyld, Malfeas, the Underworld, or anywhere else the missing cards may happen to be.

        If you own two decks, you may Shuffle them into a single deck, increasing the Count to the total of both decks. You may also Shuffle a deck with a Count of more than 1 into component decks.

        Special Activation Rules: So long as the Exalt isn't dissonant with the deck, this Evocation awakens at no cost when the deck is attuned.

        Resonant: The wielder may pay 1 WP when involved in a game with someone attuned to another of the five decks of the Dealer's Gambit. If they do so, they benefit from any of the Essence 1 Evocations anyone in the game has awakened.

        Dissonant: If the Exalt is dissonant to the deck, he makes this Evocation winnable, but does not automatically awaken it upon attunement.


        .
        .
        The Arcana of the Most High

        Cost: -- Mins: Essence 1, Count 1

        Type: Permanent

        Keywords: Winnable

        Prerequisites: Dealer's Shuffle, Your Count must contain the Orichalcum cards.

        Heaven itself enforces bets made with these cards. Anyone who refuses to pay or offer what they have wagered in a game using the Dealer's Gambit suffers a number of botches equal to the Count of the deck, exactly as if the bet had been sanctified by an Eclipse. These botches STACK with those of such an Oath, if an Eclipse or Moonshadow has also sanctified the terms of the wager.


        .
        .
        The Fickle Faces of Luna

        Cost: -- Mins: Essence 1, Count 1

        Type: Permanent

        Keywords: Winnable

        Prerequisites: Dealer's Shuffle, Your Count must contain the Moonsilver cards.

        These cards give a liar's nature. Add the Count to your Socialize for the purpose of rolling your Read Intentions actions and calculating Guile while playing with the Dealer's Gambit. This is a Non-Charm dice bonus.


        .
        .
        The Maidens' Hand

        Cost: -- Mins: Essence 1, Count 1

        Type: Permanent

        Keywords: Winnable

        Prerequisites: Dealer's Shuffle, Your Count must contain the Starmetal cards.

        These cards remove the possibility of disaster. Any time you make a roll involving the Dealer's Gambit (in combat or card play), remove a number of dice showing '1' from your roll equal to the Count. This happens before any opponent charms that use those 1s, or the calculation of botches, but after any of your charm effects that may reroll them.


        .
        .
        The Elemental Array

        Cost: -- Mins: Essence 1, Count 1

        Type: Permanent

        Keywords: Winnable

        Prerequisites: Dealer's Shuffle, Your Count must contain the Jade cards.

        These cards give the owner a preternatural insight into cheating. Add a number of Non-Charm succeses equal to the Count to any Awareness, Investigation, or Larceny ability rolls involving the Dealer's Gambit. Any normally unrolled attempt to oppose these skills with the Dealer's Gambit immediately becomes contested, unless it comes from an effect with a higher essence than your essence + the Count.


        .
        .
        The Dead Man's Draw

        Cost: -- Mins: Essence 1, Count 1

        Type: Permanent

        Keywords: Winnable

        Prerequisites: Dealer's Shuffle, Your Count must contain the Soulsteel cards.

        The dread stasis of the Underworld drains outside influences from the players of these cards. Astrological effects that try to predict or affect the players fail, when it comes to the use of the Dealer's Gambit. (It may be your Dark Fate to be stabbed over a hand of cards, but the cards will fall as they may, without outside interference) Any other manipulation of luck except that of the Dealer's Gambit or the charms of the players themselves, such as a divine blessing or curse, an Eclipse Sanctified Oath, or any other such effect also fails to affect players for the purposes of the game, even fiat level manipulation of the loom of fate. (A blessing that made you a supernaturally skilled liar might still help you win a card game incorporating bluffing, but one that made you lucky would not).


        .
        .
        Five Elements Understanding

        Cost: 4 motes, 1WP Mins: Essence 3, Count 3

        Type: Reflexive

        Keywords: Understanding

        Duration: 1 Scene

        Prerequisites: The Elemental Array, Your Count must contain the Jade cards

        Treat all ranges as if they were one category closer when attacking with the Dealer's Gambit. This includes maximum range, as well as when aiming is required, and accuracy bonuses. If you already have an Understanding active when you use this charm, remove the WP cost of this charm, and replace that understanding with this one.


        .
        .
        Destiny Understanding

        Cost: 4 motes, 1WP Mins: Essence 3, Count 3

        Type: Reflexive

        Keywords: Understanding

        Duration: 1 Scene

        Prerequisites: The Maidens' Hand, Your Count must contain the Starmetal cards

        Add a success to any opposed roll involving the Dealer's Gambit for each 10 or 1 an opponent's roll shows. If you already have an Understanding active when you use this charm, remove the WP cost of this charm, and replace that understanding with this one.


        .
        .
        Underworld Understanding

        Cost: 4 motes, 1WP Mins: Essence 3, Count 3

        Type: Reflexive

        Keywords: Understanding

        Duration: 1 Scene

        Prerequisites: The Dead Man's Draw, Your Count must contain the Soulsteel cards

        Your attacks with the deck can affect dematerialized spirits. If you cannot see them, you take a -3 to the attack as normal. If you already have an Understanding active when you use this charm, remove the WP cost of this charm, and replace that understanding with this one.


        .
        .
        Wyld Understanding

        Cost: 4 motes, 1WP Mins: Essence 3, Count 3

        Type: Reflexive

        Keywords: Understanding

        Duration: 1 Scene

        Prerequisites: The Fickle Faces of Luna, Your Count must contain the Moonsilver cards

        1/scene if you have this Understanding active before you roll, you may reroll ANY roll involving the Dealer's Gambit (including damage). Any charms you used are used again for free on this reroll, but any opponent's charms must be reused at cost. This Evocation can not be reset. If you already have an Understanding active when you use this charm, remove the WP cost of this charm, and replace that understanding with this one.


        .
        .
        Divine Understanding

        Cost: 4 motes, 1WP Mins: Essence 3, Count 3

        Type: Reflexive

        Keywords: Understanding

        Duration: 1 Scene

        Prerequisites: The Arcana of the Most High, Your Count must contain the Orichalcum cards

        You gain double 9s on any roll you make involving the Dealer's Gambit. If you already have double 9s (or better) add 1 to the range of doubles. If you already have an Understanding active when you use this charm, remove the WP cost of this charm, and replace that understanding with this one.


        .
        .
        Perfected Fivefold Understanding of Creation

        Cost: 2 WP Mins: Essence 4, Count 5

        Type: Reflexive

        Keywords: Decisive

        Duration: 1 Scene

        Prerequisites: Divine Understanding, Wyld Understanding, Destiny Understanding, Underworld Understanding, Five Elements Understanding

        To understand a thing is to know how to destroy it. Attacks with the deck ignore hardness, and affect legendary creatures as if they did not have the benefits of the merit for restricting damage. They ignore any effect that would reduce the damage of a successful attack with the Dealer's Gambit below the Count before it is rolled. While this Understanding is active, you may spend 1 WP before making a decisive attack, if you do the attack does aggravated damage. While the Fivefold Understanding is active, all other understandings cost 1 WP and 2 motes less, and may all be active simultaneously.


        .
        .
        .
        Interested Parties and the Dealer's Gambit:

        The Orichalcum Deck was buried with the Solar who held it during the Usurpation, somewhere in the Scavenger Lands. Members of the Cult of the Illuminated have been asking about it.

        The Moonsilver Deck was held by Luranume for a time, though about ten years ago he lost it to a young changing moon, White Gloved Jack.

        The Starmetal Deck has changed hands many times, but last was seen in the hands of Nara-O, head of the Forbidding Manse of Ivy, some 63 years ago. He has almost certainly given it to someone to use since then, but of course, exactly who is a secret.

        The Jade Deck has been in and out of the hands of Plentimon of the Dice multiple times over the last century. He is able to share the magic of the deck through his divine powers, and seems particularly fond of catching unaware Dragonblooded trying to cheat him with it. He never keeps it for long, however, and last report had it in the hands of a young Lieutenant from House Peleps.

        The Soulsteel Deck was in the possession of a Sijanese noble dead, before his brutal end at the hands of a deathknight claiming allegiance to The Empress of Whispers. Sages are unsure if this is a reclusive and relatively unknown Deathlord, or another guise for one of them that is already known.
