    How about charms that utilize more long-term effects of medical knowledge for groups/area's? Something that may simulate immunization of populace against specific health hazards.

    Examples: Creating an elixir that in concentrated doses can sure someone of a disease within a few moments; but in small regular doses can provide an effective immunity to that disease?

    Also something that lets a solar who has followers/cult or rules a population make that population stronger over generations by influencing their health? Want your people to be just a little stronger or more agile? With this charm, after three generations your populace will exhibit those kinds of traits.All due to different health programs/taboo's that the charm gives some supernatural oomph to help them take effect faster.

    Medicine/health charms have some great (if a little un-epic feeling) applications in the here and now. But the ability can really stand apart if used over long periods of time on small populations.

                        So I saw the Lazarus Part of the thread and a thought occurred to me. When Jesus raised Lazarus from the dead, he was subverting the powers of death. In fact, Christians believe that's what Christ did: He made forgiveness of people's faults easily available to the masses thus saving them from their spiritual "death". In the real world, physical death is an event. Spiritual death is a condition (according to religious texts at least). But in Exalted, death is an actual tangible thing. It is a source of power. It is an overlord and it's minions are physical sword wielding antagonists. So if you're looking at making a Solar Medicine charm based on Jesus, I would suggest having a series of charms designed to subvert the power of death over the masses. This can involve curing the effects of living in shadow lands, destroying necrotech (or whatever it will be called in 3e), and possibly using your own 'death' to release the masses from the power of a deathlord.

                        Here's what I think this charm tree would look like:

                        1) "Trust me. I'm a doctor." (Mentioned in previous posts. Doesn't have to be an automatic success instead gives a small bonus by helping you look the part of the doctor, even if you are a quack). Essence 1
                        2) "Hold on! The doctor's on his way!" (Mentioned in previous posts). Essence 2-3.
                        3) "You're nothing but a bag of bones!" (Damages Necrotic creations). Essence 2-3.
                        4) "You're better now, but maybe you should consider moving." (Cures effects of living in shadowlands for long periods of time). Essence 2-3?
                        5) "I will heal your lands!" (Evokes small benefits to large groups. Nothing along the lines of "cure the contagion", but making people more healthy. Death of the solar results in widespread cure of ailments and protection for lands with a positive intimacy towards the solar). Essence 5.
