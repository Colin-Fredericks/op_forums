                    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                    Does it feel radically different to 2nd ed Lunars, or more like familiar Lunars but good?
                    It's got a lot of the 2nd edition feel but some charms are a little different because of the new combat system of 3rd edition. The Totemic stuff is really cool, instead of traveling down a particular charm branch and picking up 4 or 5 charms that your character will never use, you can hop into a charm tree if it is Totemic. For example, you may be looking at a DEX charm that is 3 or 4 charms down a tree and one will pop up that allows you to take it if you meet other requirements. It would say something like "if your spirit shape is Legendary then you can take this strength charm as a Stamina charm.
