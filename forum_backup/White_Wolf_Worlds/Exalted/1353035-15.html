                    I don't think every charm should be permanent, just the bread-and-butter ideas. Reducing surprise penalties, not perfect dodges. Increasing punch damage a bit, not doing your signature comet throw.

                    For example, a Dodge tree might look like this:

                    Spend motes, get + 2 Evasion.
                    When enemies miss you, they lose 1 initiative.
                    When enemies miss you, you gain 1 initiative.
                    Reduce penalties by 1.
                    Spend motes, perfect dodge. 1/scene. Solars and Abyssals only.
                    +1 Evasion. Solars and Abyssals only.
                    If enemies miss, spend motes, enemy takes fire damage. Dragonblood only.
                    Spend motes, make a roll. If you succeed, you were never there. Sidereals only.

                    That's just a quick example, but I hope it explains it.

                        Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
                        Strength of Chi was a bonus added to rolls based on Attributes, where 1-3 gave a +5 roll bonus, 4-6 gave a +10, etc. Not every technique used SoC, but it was quite common to see as a buff, and I could see Exalted making it more about the type of being since it's less about individual cultivation than what order of power you are a lot of the time. Some other universal mechanics could be tiered that way as well, or else made uniform, like supernatural balancing acts, acrobatics, or weightlessness.
                        There's a lot of little ways you can establish power tiers, even in a system where all things are equal. I think the Mastery/Terrestrial or Resonant/Dissonant keywords in Ex3's Martial Arts are a good start, since they give you three levels of power stratification in one Charm. That said, I think one of the nice things about Ex3 is that Dragon-Blooded need to be taken seriously on an individual level - I've seen lucky and well-built DBs who go for the kill beat unfocused, unlucky, and un-serious Solars with some regularity.

                                    If I ran again, I'd get two colors of plastic beads and force people to print charm cards.
                                    When you spend Essence, you do so by putting the beads on the cards. When a Charm ends, you put the beads in the "discard" bowl. At the end of your turn, you take five beads from the "discard" bowl.
                                    Physical manipulation works wonders for memory, though it comes at a cost to execution speed.

                                    The other thing I've considered is color-coded dice to help with pools and caps. Have a color for Ability dice, a similar color for Specialty dice, a color for Attribute dice, Equipment dice, Stunt dice. Have two more colors for Charm dice, similar to but distinct from Ability and Attribute dice.
                                    So when Sam Solar wants to know what his die cap is, I can point to the number of Ability and Attribute dice he has and his bowl of Charm dice and tell him to match colors. This would also let me use the Stunt dice as a physical reminder of "Hey you! Stunt that roll!".

                                                            It's easy enough to compress combat Abilities. Melee really means any chaotic fight; it's similar in meaning to 'fray', 'skirmish' or 'scrimmage'. (Heck, Thesaurus.com even suggests 'brawl'.) Splitting it into armed and unarmed isn't really necessary.

                                                            You could, therefore, easily have one close combat skill (Melee) and one ranged combat skill (Ballistics). Athletics could cover Thrown and general evasion, while Melee could cover parry and block actions. That means you've got a fairly narrow set of skills for use in combat.

                                                            Martial Arts styles are like Charms anyway, so you could just use Melee + a Martial Arts Specialty to act as gatekeepers to the Martial Arts styles. Likewise, you might put more fancy weapons behind a Martial/Exotic Weapons Speciality or similar, so that everyone can use knives but experts get to use the special stuff.
