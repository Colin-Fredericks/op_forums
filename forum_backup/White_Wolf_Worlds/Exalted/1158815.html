                  But my real question is... why does that artificier have a small blue monkey? Is the monkey dead? Is he just hanging out? Is he so fed up of these damn arguing exalts? Because that's his expression.

                  On a wider note, I like the way that, although the art no longer looks manga-style, they have given quite a lot of it an east-Asian feel, which makes it feel a bit more appropriate for Exalted's thematic base, a bit less euro-fantasy. Lots of lamellar-style armour, Chinese-style clothes. Even a Samurai-style set of armour.

                  Also, a lot of the art looks a similar style (I think most of it was done by the same team?) which makes it feel more unified than the art in the core.


                  Also, you can get a blue jade umbrella as your weapon. It protects you from rain!

                                      All right, let's see what we've got.

                                      ​(I promise that my review will be less scattershot than I have done for previous books)

                                      ​I like that cover; quite apart from being evocative of a scene in the Time of Tumult, it has to be the largest variety of artifacts ever gathered into one illustration, which should provide ample inspiration for what any given one might look like.

                                      Let's see... first chapter about making them, and then remaining ones about various lists divided by certain categories. Sounds good.

                                      ​So, Introduction:

                                      ​A list of various magical items throughout folklore in fiction is a very good way to start, both to root the subject in context and to provide further inspiration, before going into doing the same for those of Exalted.

                                      ​Now, an explanation of what those historical eras attached to previewed Artifacts refers to...

                                      Hehehe, fragmentary, mythic information, pieced together from innumerable sources with varying degrees of distortion by time, bias, or simply limited context. I couldn't have asked for better.

                                      Nothing much to say on the Resources, although they all look pretty solid.

                                      ​Now, on to the meat of the thing.
