    Originally posted by Blaque View Post

    The Octet are in near constant contact with one-another though and do actually do international trade and go to war. They're isolated from eachother about as much as any other Direction's peoples are, but it's not like Estasia hasn't had contact with Yugash for five millennia. They trade, share religion, share a writing system, and share the Orthodoxy to boot. There's actually a bit of Egyptian style conservativism which runs through the society which is why we can read a lot of the same texts wtih known variation over the three thousand years of dyanasties they had.
    The way it's described in the Compass (which I put more stock in), the nations brush up near each other with varying frequency, but each nation is essentially still isolated. As far as I know it's never occurred that any one nation could get to all of the other nations simultaneously though this may have happened. The way it's described, each nation only have one or two neighbors close enough that the trams connect and allow constant trade and/or tourism. Then after a few years (or months or decades), the trams close and retract, making it exceedingly difficult and dangerous to interact with the other nation. Sometimes a nation is completely isolated for years or decades at a time.

    This has been going on for 5000 years.

    There's never been a time where it was easy for all nations to interact directly with one another. The default state is isolation from most of the rest if not all of the other nations. People who travel to other nations are at risk of being stranded there if something happens to cut the trams. Citizens are actively encouraged to stay at their post. Vacations are few and far between (though they do happen I think)

    All of these factors mean that they would have language drift to an astounding degree.

    Edit: I'll also point out that constant contact via trade means nothing in regards to language drift. The Germanic language family is a lot younger than Autochthonia, and the nations don't drift apart and yet they engaged in constant trade and warfare, yet have multiple completely different languages.

                The thing about Autocthonia is that, if you're playing a game in Autocthonia, it's pretty reasonable to have languages for each nation. You're unlikely to need all 8. Your native one, 1 or 2 others because you're fighting them or whatever. Unless it's a game where you travel the whole of Autocthonia. And there are Tunnel People, Polar cities, etc.

                If you're playing a game in Creation, which then gets invaded by multiple Autocthonian nations, it might end up with a very high number of languages. Especially if you're playing an Alchemical (with your own language, Old Realm for spirits, 2 or 3 other languages from the Octet) who then has to learn Creation's languages as well.


                Saying all this, unlike 2nd ed, where in most games most of our PCs ended up having to buy Linguistics 4 or 5 unfavoured, and then the ST had to bring in a house rule for buying more languages, languages are just 3xp each. You can buy 5 for 15xp (or 5 of your starting merit dots), rather than 23xp. So it's a bit easier than before.


                You could use the "4 tribal languages for 3xp rule" and have them like that. So, there are multiple Autocthonian languages, but they're not very expensive to buy.

                                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                Considering system transparency, can you activate scene-long combat charms while you're in stealth and still do an ambush afterwards?

                                I would say yes, you absolutely can ambush in those circumstances. System transparency doesn't mean that everybody gets the info about the effects of your charms the instant you activate them, it means that the player, not the character, gets the info when you attack. So a Solar speced into Stealth uses Mind Shroud Meditation to gain a couple rounds respite, for example, activating some scenelong charms in the meantime, then attacking a Dragonblood, the Dragonblood doesn't get to say "Ahah! I detected the activation of Immaculate Golden Bow and Wood Dragon Form! I know you're an enemy again, and you don't get to ambush me!". But when the Solar actually attacks, it's still an ambush, but the Dragonblood's player does get to know the effects of Wood Dragon Form and whatever other charms the Solar is using, so as to best choose their own charms to use.
