    Some general questions

    08-09-2016, 05:18 AM
    1)Ephemeral entities, Open condition, Reaching
    A)Open condition
    Can they apply it by themselves on a living subkect, or does the "work in concert with the subject" pretty much means that they need concent?
    Example: we have a ghost haunting Bob. Bob is it's murder and anchor, and the ghost has influence 2 over him. Bob is sleeping peacefully with his 2 resolve.
    Can the ghost spend 2 scenes rolling, creating the open condition, and proceed to possess bob?
    Or is the only way for Bob to be actually possessed to willingly work alongside the ghost so that it can create the Open condition?

    B)Reaching condition
    Initially I though that an entity had to be materialized to use numina that don't have the reaching tag.
    P.e. a fire spirit materialized and used his numina to cause fire
    But some other numina don't make sense.
    P.e. sign. A ghost needs to materialize in order to write on a window? It doesn't make sense.
    So, the next conclusion was that entities in the twillight form are, in a sense, fully on the fallen world, and thus can use all numina like they were materialized. And that "reaching" is only for using numina over the underworld, the spirit realms, or even astral itself.
    Is this correct?

    2)Matter and equipment bonus
    There is already a matter 3 perfecting items in order to give them roll 8 and/or rote quality (perfecting)
    There is already matter 2 to shape matter (shaping) in order to make them worse.
    I assume that a mage, using the thaumaturgy rules CAN somehow give pure equipment bonus to simple matter items (making a sword super sharp, increasing the mass of a baseball bat, etc), maybe even complex items with reach (and probably a reflexive crafts or science roll).
    Does the above makes sense? And if yes, what would it be? Perfecting (Matter 3) or Ruling (Matter 2 like shaping)

    3)Knocked down tilt.
    In it's effect it says that "if the subject hasn't acted yet, it loses his action"
    In the resolution it says "if the subject hasn't acted yet it rolls dex+athletics to avoid the tilt"
    I assume the resolution is the correct one?

    4)Adamant Hand, yantra, touch, ordering
    A)Casting a spell on a target normally requires a touch (dex+brawl)
    Am I correct to assume that if you punch someone (str+brawl) and can cast a reflexive spell (p.e. exceptional luck) on the same turn, you can safely assume that you delivered the spell through the punch and you don't need to "touch him" again?

    B)stacking yantras.
    You punch someone with adamant hand, gaining +1 yantra bonus on your next spell cast in subsequent rounds, or to a spell cast reflexively on the same round.
    Reflexively you cast exceptional luck.
    Can you add your "free" yantra to the mix, or does the +1 from adamant hand counts as the "you may use one yantra reflexively with a spell"?

    C)You hit someone causing the knocked down tilt.
    You use exceptional luck reflexively to apply leg wrack.
    Does he suffers the -2 to the athletics roll to not fall down, or is this roll made before the spell?

    Big thanks to those who take the time to read through this.

      1.A)
      For spirits, the living subject must first be Resonant (they must do things that fits the spirit nature). Afterward, they can then start to make the subject Open with their Influence.

      For ghosts, you are right. The ghost attempts to give Bob the Open condition, and once it has done so, it can directly affect him with what powers that requires it.

      2.B)
      Reaching is only for Spirits or things that want to affect things on the other side of the Gauntlet. I never thought however, that It could also be applied to beings in the Underworld :P

      But you are correct that things in Twilight are in fact, in the psychical world. However, the Signs power doesn't require the entity to be Materialized. But some powers (I think), does require the entity to Materialize.

      2)
      Yes, it should be possible to increase the Weapon rating of the item and I would say it is a Perfecting practice.

      3)
      Both are correct, but I can see why they would be confusing. I'll try and give an example:

      Possessed Bob attempts to apply the Knocked Down Tilt to his "friend" Rod, and is successfully in doing so.
      Rod hasn't acted in the turn, so he loses his action for the turn. But, because he hasn't acted, he may attempt a Dexterity + Athletics roll to not be knocked down. He still loses his action, though.

      4.A)
      Not completly sure of the question but I will try. There is no Reflexive Spellcasting in the new addition. Attacking is an Instant action and so is casting a spell, so both can't be done in the same turn.

      4.B)
      As fare as I know, you cannot stack the same Yantras. Normally when using multiple Yantras, you increase the time it take to cast the spell (1 turn for Instant casting and 1 hour for Ritual casting). Adamantin Hand just grants you a "free" Yantra that doesn't increase this time.

      4.C)
      Not sure how casting a dice boosting spell applies the Leg Wrack. But as mentioned, you cannot cast a spell Reflexive anymore.

      But if he was under the hex before the Leg Wrack, he would gain the penalty.
