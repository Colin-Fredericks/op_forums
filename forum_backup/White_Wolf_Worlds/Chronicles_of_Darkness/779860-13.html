                    The funny thing is that I was thinking about that about an hour ago and started reading the "Magic Pants" trope on TvTropes. Honestly, I can see the Alterations going two ways when it comes to clothes:

                    1) The clothes get ripped apart because, obviously, changing the mass and composition of one's body is going to affect the clothes in such a way. Alterations are considered to be physical, biological changes that affect the material world.

                    2) The clothes don't get ripped apart because the Siren's Aquatic Form is tied to her other self in The Deluge Reality. By using her Alterations or accessing her Aquatic Form, she's just pulling part of her existence in The Deluge into the other Reality. Alterations are considered to be metaphysical manifestations that become corporeal.


                    I don't know if Castaways would use Breathless in a special way. I mean, I can imagine them using Breathless as a way to get Pneuma as a whole (since that's what Sirens would generally do with Breathless when not current wise) but not having anything more special than that.
                    I would say that Castaways, despite not having a Current, are able to stop a Breathless from following a Current by virtue of what the Castaways are in comparison to the other Currents. The other Sirens are busy moving alongside a flow and want to bring the Breathless along for the ride. The Castaways, effectively being stationed on land or on a rock rather than flowing with the Currents, are introspective and, rather than trying to change the world, they want to take a minute to reflect before they take on a Current. A Castaway interacting with a Breathless would essentially be a sort of "blind-leading-the-blind" situation, but it would provide the Breathless with some sort of grounding influence.

                                        Originally posted by SaulottheGentle View Post
                                        We need to come up with a system for Refractions. How exactly should they be carried out? Any non Siren rolls Resolve + Composure - the Siren's Serenity?
                                        As far as I remember, Refraction allows for a Siren to hide one Alteration for free for a single scene. Activating an additional Alteration puts a strain on the Siren's ability, which makes it harder for her to conceal it and the strain is amplified whenever the Siren needs to focus. I think that having the Siren roll Akashic Record + Lucidity (that's the Integrity stat now) to control her Alterations might work, as she's attempting to keep a hold of her alternate form, allowing bits of it to come out without releasing her full potential. The roll is probably needed for when the Siren activates an additional Alteration and when she is under mental or physical duress.

                                        Say this: A Siren is been jailed and needs to escape from her cell. There's a guard on post, so the Siren also needs to figure out a way to escape from the guard as well. The Siren decides to take the stealthy way out, so she, being a Polarian, uses one of the Alterations exclusive to her Waters, Camouflage. Activating the Alteration is easy, so the Siren doesn't need to roll her Lucidity. However, that was the first phase as it was meant to allow her to escape the bars undetected. The Siren decides to activate a second Alteration, Boneless, to slip herself through the bars. She rolls her Akashic Record + her Lucidity - 1 for the additional Alteration that scene. She succeeds and passes herself through the bars without alerting anyone. However, the guard notices that the person in the cell is no longer there, so they notify the other guards, which puts the area on lockdown. The Siren realizes that she needs to get away as soon as she can, which prompts duress and forces her to roll her Akashic Record + her Lucidity - 1 for her additional Alteration - another 1 because she succeeded in maintaining her form previously. If the Siren is then attacked after succeeding, she would roll AR + Lucidity - 1 for Alterations - 2 for two previous successes. However, if she decides to activate yet another Alteration, then she would need to roll AR + Lucidity - 2 for two additional Alterations - 3 for three previous successes.

                                        In terms of math, it would go: Dice Pool = Akashic Record + Lucidity - (# of Alterations -1) - (# of prior successes)

                                        The rolls would prompt when the Siren is stressed, when she is physically assaulted, or at the start of each new scene.

                                        Philosophical beliefs on what they should do or how they act now as a Siren? Normal Currents believe that because they know of the Deluge, they should stop it as a generality, but they can also do things specialized as long as it fits with their Current? The Abyssal Currents differ in their ways. Dark Passions and Wisdom can be in it for dealing with the Deluge, for themselves, for other people, etc etc. Mankind's Victory, Primordial Darkness and Void tend to be much more entrenched within the world and fighting a secret war using corporations and powers against each other that most other Sirens have to deal with cleaning up.
                                        The Currents are kinda like ways to interact with others or in what ways to influence the future. Like with the Current of Healing. Generally speaking, Sirens who follow the Current of Healing want others to recover, whether it is from emotional pain or physical pain. Some Sirens do so by helping others pick up the broken pieces, others help by allowing others to vent. The Normal Currents are sort of progressive and are trying to push humanity forward, whether it is from healing troubles, connecting with others, or facing an obstacle. The Abyssal Currents, in general, are more selfish in away or at least destructive.

                                        Dark Passions, like the Current of Conflict, asks for confronting obstacles. However, it is also significantly more aggressive and asks more for vengeance than anything. Dark Wisdom, similar to the Current of Wisdom, seeks knowledge of the world. However, the pursuit of knowledge takes priority over everything else, including self-preservation. Both are meant to interact with humanity in a way; the two may even encourage humanity towards ruthlessness. While it is a kind of extreme, they both can deal with The Deluge.

                                        Mankind's Victory and Primordial Darkness could also work into interaction with humanity and prevention of The Deluge. Mankind's Victory is more about allowing others to do as they want without restriction as humanity has demonstrated their dominance as a species. It partially deals with The Deluge by asking others to act without restraint; maybe this will encourage others to speak up without regards to the power hierarchy and such and make the changes necessary to stopping The Deluge. Primordial Darkness operates in the inverse, forcibly reminding humanity that, in the end, the earth will survive long after humanity has run its course. They would be enforcing the hierarchy, making sure to place the Earth on top.

                                        The Void would be an outlier, as they are the ones that actively want to bring the world into ruin by hastening the coming of The Deluge. It's still technically a way to interact with humanity and approach The Deluge; they're just kinda negative for the most part.
