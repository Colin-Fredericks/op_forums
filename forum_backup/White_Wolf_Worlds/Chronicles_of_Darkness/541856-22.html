    Terrance Jones: Terrance has always had problems fitting in. He has a dark sense of humor that comes from a long period of having really bad stuff happen to him and his family for years, what made it worse for him was the ability to see what was going to happen in Dreams before hand and no-one ever believing him. He grew up jumping from household to household as not many families have been able to deal with his Twisted sense of humor. He never meet any Children or much of the Supernatural before his own Self Devouring. After abit of traveling and seeing what the world is really like Terry decided to settle down and open a Hotel that provides a Unique selling point, "If you Sleep here you will have Nightmares" It has became a local Tourist trap and a minor Landmark in the city.

    Family: Anakim

    Hunger: Ruin

    Horror: (Shadow Giant) A Hulking Great shadow that hunches over and is wearing a faded old red Ringmasters outfit. It might not be the most Horrific of Monsters, But it is not his look that makes him horrific.

    Life: Loyal - "See I can live with you trying to wreck my stuff... but you hurt my staff.. That I cannot Allow you to get away with." If you are his friend or part of his staff he will move heaven and earth for you. Betray his trust... and he will end you.

    Legend: Seductive - "Come one, come all, see the Truth of the World as it is... Unfiltered" seeing all things as nothing but a playground of the mind he loves to Whisper secrets to people and let them come to there own Ruin from it.

    Lair: Blinding Light, Darkness, Cramped, Echoing.

He's only slightly miffed that he needs to meet up with people in their dreams. Ow.. and there's the Fact his Horror is Female......

    Family: Ugallu

    Hunger: Punishment

        Anthony Gote: If the Begotten consider each other to be part of one extended family, then Anthony is the black sheep who behaves inappropriately at family gatherings. For as long as anyone can remember, Anthony has lived a life of decadence. He wasn't incredibly wealthy, but he also showed no interest in becoming a productive member of society. A hedonist and a lech, Anthony squandered his potential in favor of satisfying his base urges. After his Devouring, this personality became more destructive, because Anthony is no longer content to ruin his own life, but now feels the need to ruin the lives of others, as well!

        Anthony Gote has built his legend around being a creature of temptation. When he finds a victim, he offers to help them get what they want out of life, often with little or no charge. It seems harmless at first, but over time, they come to depend on Anthony, to the point where they can no longer take care of themselves because they're used to having everything handed to them on demand. Once he feels they can't live without him, he severs contact and feeds on the terror that comes with them struggling to survive without his assistance.

        In his attempts to provide people with what they think they need, Anthony came into the company of a cabal of morally dubious mages and formed a kinship with them. It was from their tutelage that he learned about the occult, as well as a number of supernatural methods in which to obtain resources, or perform deeds that would be difficult (if not impossible) by mundane methods.

        Anthony's Lair is a place of debauchery and shame. It takes the shape of a densely overgrown forest, with a thick fog rolling through the trees to confuse the senses of those who pass through it. The pungent scent of musk fills the air. Visions of beautiful, naked men and women flit in and out of sight through the fog, the sound of playful laughter and sensual moaning can be heard off in the distance, enticing wayward travelers to head towards its direction in hopes of finding somebody to talk to. Unfortunately, those who take the bait have a better chance of encountering the forest's baphomet, than they do finding a trail guide!



        Family: Ugallu

        Hunger: Ruin

        Horror: Baphomet - Possessing the head and legs of a goat, the torso of a large breasted woman and the genitals of a well endowed man, Anthony's horror is immediately recognized as a creature of lust and desire. A pentagram star has been branded into his forehead, while his eyes are red like hot coals and his fur is as black as a starless night. A pair of black feathered wings grow from their backside, and look strong enough to support him in flight.

        Life: Selfless - Anthony is a man of the people, or so he would have you believe. He potrays himself as being incredibly generous towards others and is willing to offer his aid without asking for anything in return. At least until he gets hungry, that is.

        Legend: Seductive - Anthony prefers to avoid hostilities, especially when there's a chance he can turn a potential enemy into an ally. Rather than bring them to ruin personally, he prefers to subtly lead them down the path of self destruction, feeding once his victims realize they've caused their own downfall!

        Aspirations: Become a Cult Leader (Long Term), Seduce a Wiccan (short term)

        Lair: 6 (Undergrowth, Fog, Stench, Mirages, Echoing)

        Nightmares: The Void Is Waiting, Flying and Falling, You Must Obey, You Are Better Than Them,

        Atavisms: Wings of the Raptor, Alien Allure, Eye of Heaven, Mimir's Wisdom, Needs Must, Siren's Treacherous Song

        Victor Marls: From the snowy regions of Canada, Victor Marls was once a member of the mounted police force and had a decently successful career at the time. That changed, however, when he was sent out to arrest what he assumed to be another dangerous criminal who had a history of murder. Long story short, the trail turned cold, and pretty soon after that, everything else began to turn cold as well. Despite his training with the mounties, Victor mysteriously became lost in the forest at night and was facing the threat of freezing to death, accompanied by starvation. Alone and on the edge of death, Victor closed his eyes for one last dream, and that's when his quarry appeared before him. For reasons Victor still doesn't understand, they saved his life that night, but condemned him to a fate even worse than the one he would have faced.

        Victor has convinced everybody from his previous life that he died in the forest, and with good reason. He wouldn't want anybody he cared about to see him in the state he's become; a monster with an eating disorder, who spends more time commiting murders than solving them. Adding to the problem is the fact that Victor tries to deny his hunger for as long as he possibly can, which causes him to be in a nearly constant state of low satiety, which is a very dangerous state to be around when predators are involved. Thankfully he's met a brood that helps him deal with the changes that have occured in his life, but it's still a rocky transition.

        A self loathing Beast, Victor secretly has a death wish that he keeps from the rest of his brood. He would love nothing more than to be human again, but at the same time, he knows he doesn't deserve that life anymore. The deaths of too many people weigh on his conscience, and you don't go back to a happy life after that. Victor has recently begun harboring a fascination with the Retreat. If his horror were confined to the Primordial Dream, then at least he could prevent it from physically harming people (or so he believes). But he's hesitant to go through with it at this time. Lulling his Horror to sleep would require a lot of murders, and he's just not sure he can go through with it, even if it would save more lives in the long run. Such is the tortured dilemma of Victor Marls.

        Victor's lair is a grueling endurance test that mirrors the snowy forests he almost died in. Strong winds and heavy snowfall create an atmosphere of frigid air. Even those who protect themselves against such temperatures will find that the slick ice permeating the environment adds yet another complication to traversing the land. Fatigue and hunger rapidly sets in, draining victims of their strength to carry on as they prepare to lay down in the snow and await death.

        Family: Namtaru

        Hunger: Prey

        Horror: Windego - An impossibly tall monstrosity, Victor's horror possesses the head of a deer, the legs of a horse, the body of a man, the claws of a bear and the teeth of a wolf. It stands almost 15 feet tall, but is so horribly malnourished that it's nearly anorexic in body mass. It's ravenous eyes dart about in search of meat, any meat, with which to satisfy the unending hunger that plagues its gut.

        Life: Shy - Victor does not do very well around new people, and even his own brood have a tough time bonding with him in public. This shyness causes him to be quiet and withdrawn, unless circumstances compel him to take action

        Legend: Vicious - Because of the perpetual state of starvation in which he keeps himself, Victor finds it almost impossible to restrain himself when he feeds. By the time he's finished with his prey, the only plausible explanation for the state he leaves his victims in is that there was some kind of wild animal attack!

        Aspirations: Undergo The Retreat (long term), Kill a fat man (short term)

        Lair: 7 (Extreme Cold, Blizzard, Slick, Crosswinds, Heavy )

        Nightmares: Fear is Contagious, Behold My True Form, Run Away, You Are Lost

        Atavisms: Unbreakable, Storm Lashed, Relentless Hunter, Shadowed Soul, Limb From Limb, Looming Presence

            Another 2 Lovecraft-inspired Beasts (those stories are a treasure trove! more is sure to come)

            "I have seen the dark universe yawning
            Where the black planets roll without aim—
            Where they roll in their horror unheeded,
            Without knowledge or lustre or name."

            Robert Blake: An author of crime and horror short stories, Robert Blake is a shy intellectual, with an interest in architecture and history. His Devouring was unusual insofar as it seemingly was triggered by an artifact. The rush of power was underwhelming, actually, but the sense of fulfillment, of being something other than just a shy author was enourmous, and even though Bob leads his life pretty much a lot like he used to, there are some changed habits, of course.
            He hunts cults - after having decided on any group that keeps isolated, exploits their members and/or refuses them the right to leave, he starts researching on who the most important people are. He starts with the recruiters: when they sleep, he draws symbols on their forehead, with blood (he gets it from a butcher). Then, he goes after the higher ups: indoctrinators, people with pull and rights. They get a haircut, or just a shallow cut, whatever he feels like. Finally, he turned to stalking the higher ups... sometimes, their bodies turn up, hit by lightning in their own house. Should anyone attempt to repair the cult, take leadership, after all this, he disappears.
            The lesson Blake teaches is a simple one: "They can't protect you - they can't even protect themselves." He prides himself on the idea that he helps people get back on with their life.

            Family: Eshmaki

            Hunger: Prey

            Horror: The Haunter of the Dark. A dark, bat-winged, semi-material creature with a huge three-lobed burning eye for a head, the Horror's touch is corrosive and deadly.

            Life: Shy - Blake is not the most social of people, and tends to hold back a lot.

            Legend: Relentless- If Blake has decided you are his next victim, nothing will stop him from seeing things through.

            Lair: Blakes Lair is an old abandoned church, the different rooms the chambers. The Heart is the uppermost tower chamber and contains the Shining Trapezohedron. the Lair traits are Poor Light, Corrosive, Decayed, Thin Air (Dusty) and Electrified.

            Merits: Robert Blake is in possession of a powerful artifact: The Shining Trapezohedron. This "nearly black, red-striated polyhedron with many irregular flat surfaces" is a 4-inches big spherical crystal which grants him access to "You must have it" and "Mimir's Wisdom", and can be used to watch past or future events where the stone was present. As long as in the posession of a Beast, it adds +1 to its effective Lair. However, the Trapezohedron must be kept in a poorly lit place (which is why he keeps it in his Lair most of the time) and once a month, a life must be ceremonically extinguished in its presence. Otherwise, it's effects turn on the owner (Lair -1, if that would reduce Lair to 0 the Beast gains the Slumbering condition. Furthermore, she is affected by You must have it for a scene every day until either the gem changes ownership or the ceremony is held). There is some connection between Nephren-Ka and the polyhedron, and Blake assumes Nemes Hamadi must have created it.

            Nightmares: Behold, My True Form!, Fear is Infectious, You are not alone

            Atavisms: Needs Must, Shadow-Souled, Wings of the Raptor, Storm-Lashed, Limb from Limb



            Nemes Hamadi:
            Nemes is a scientist from Egypt, but also a highranking diplomat. In addition to being highly charismatic and having a lot of pull in governments and tons of resources, the industries are crazy for his inventions. Little do they know that the star-eyed idealistic do-gooder has a nefarious plan: ultimately, the relationships he makes possible will come crashing down, his inventions will cause new problems... and everyone will look to him for help. Eventually, they all will realized they are but toys in his hands. When he feels particularly vicious, he doesn't just destroy someones career, but their sanity as well. He rarely feeds deeply by killing though, since it seems less lasting, and he wants his quarry to know and remember. The lesson he teaches is "You are not enough".
            Every now and then, someone like Randolf Carter comes along, and reaches his goals despite Nemes' adversity. The Ugallu simply applauds them, considering them a chosen few, and carries on, because he knows something they dont: He will get to them some other time. Right now, his eyes are on a different goal: Inheritance is in his grasp.

            Family: Ugallu

            Hunger: Tyrant

            Horror: Nephren-Ka. A humongous pitch-black man with shining eyes dressed as a pharao, buth instead of a head he has a giant tentacle with a mouth.

            Life: Honest- Nemes does not lie, if only because he doesn't have to. He will get you to do his will just fine like this.

            Legend: Seductive- Nemes makes people do things they come to despise.

            Lair: Mirages, Maze, Extreme Heat, Blazing Light, Disruption, Engulfing, Crosswinds - a desert patch where Nyarlathothep can just watch his prey lose all hope, running around disoriented and helpless, before taking care of them, exhausted and weak, one by one.

            Nightmares: Fear is Contagious, You must obey, Everything you do is Worthless, You are alone, You deserve this, They are all around you, You can't wake up

            Atavisms: Eye of Heaven, Alien Allure, Siren's Treacherous Song, Mimir's Wisdom, Needs Must, Storm-Lashed, Looming Presence

                    Not even that hard;

                    The Lair of Stanislav Zaguche, the ancient Apex of Transylvania, puts the truth to his frequent claim that he is as much a part of the land as the land is part of him; more than once, only the manifestation of a fellow Beast's Horror is any indication you have left the physical world and are now within his soul. Due to careful and precise claiming and generation of Chambers (the Count is excellent at subtle, precisely targeted psychological torture), the Lair is an almost perfect replication of his hometown, the surrounding forest, and the castle in which the Anakim dwells. It even has people in it, though they're more extensions of the Lair than anything self-aware. Scratching the surface, though, reveals the truth; the real Barovia, while certainly not winning any First World Lifestyle awards any time soon, is aware the world around it is changing, and almost reluctantly keeps up with the world enough to survive in a modern Romania; the actual villagers love their cheap smartphones (it's what they can afford) and have AC units next to their fireplaces. The villagers of Lair-Barovia seem to living fossils from at least 80 years back, if not further; most of them don't have the slightest clue what a phone is, and they are utterly flabbergasted by modern clothing, let alone internal heating. While the earthly woods have been thinned out by loggers and the slow expansion of the town, the Dream's forest actively resists any attempt to make it more tame, and the dream-animals regularly patrol at the edge of the light, almost completely unafraid of humans. Above all is the mist; Barovia is foggy, but Stanislav's version might as well be a flightless cloud. Sometimes, it even gets so thick, you start to suffocate-there's too much water for lungs to extract oxygen from the cloudy air. This is, of course, entirely the result of the owning Beast's own personality; the Count is an old, old man, and his nostalgia for the heady days of pre-WWI Transylvania infects everything in his heart, let alone his Family's inherent nature as the Fear of Power; on some level, the Dread Lord of Ravenloft wants anyone modern to feel the distinct discomfort that comes from being out of their own time. It only becomes more obvious as any intruder or guest nears the family's castle, the inner Chambers surrounding his Heart; the castle is remarkably well-preserved in the real world, but the Primordial Dream's imitation is pristine and new, a call-back to its master's childhood and young adulthood before the Great War. Entering, however, gradually reveals the fractal patterns of regrets, psychological issues, and entitlement that drives Stansilav; the castle is borderline empty of any inhabitation, minus what ghosts and revenants the Count lets dwell within. Dust gathers, rooms stand unattended and eternally ready for guests and family that never seem to arrive-a profound sense of loneliness and emptiness pervades every single cubic centimeter of the Dream's version of Castle Ravenloft. Of course, anyone who has figured out the crazed obsession that drives the Count's nercomantic experiments may start to wonder if the castle is the "too quiet" levels that generally proceed a mad ambush. They would be right; Besides the batlike Dread Lord itself, the castle is infested with tiny little monsters born from the dream itself, each one born from a self-justification and cruel action the Count has done in his mad quest to reclaim Tatyana-to say nothing of the aforementioned ghosts and revenants, who become rather irate at anyone invading their landlord's soul.

                                                    Anakim Nemesis, the Spider Goddess: Ask anybody who knows about dreams and they'll probably tell you that they're symbolic depictions of your subconscious. If you dream about spiders, it means you're trying to run from your problems, or so the theory goes. This Beast has chosen to capitalize on this fear by preying upon those who attempt to shirk their responsibilities. Whether it's family, work, religion, governance, medicine, convicted criminals or public servants, everybody must make personal sacrifices in their life to ensure that society continues to function. And if they will not offer themselves willingly, then she will drag them to the altar by force!


                                                    In her human form, she is a Loyal member of the community who recognizes that the needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few. At times, she's even put needs of others above herself, like when she found out her own daughter was a dangerous slasher who needed to be taken off the streets. In the days since her Devouring, the Spider Goddess has become a judge within the judicial system, one who does not play favorites and resists all attempts at bribery and extortion. No singular person or family is above society, a fact you will become acutely aware of, should you find yourself in her courtroom.


                                                    In her monster form, she Relentlessly hunts down those she deems irresponsible. The torso of a beautiful woman attached to the body of a gargantuan arachnid. Eight eyes adorn her face, while her silky white hair spills down to her waist. A mouth of fangs with paralytic venom and a carapace thick enough to deflect bullets. The heart of her lair contains a stone altar, splattered with dried blood. When she captures her target, she places them bound and gagged upon it before conducting the painful ritual of sacrificing them. Hopefully the victim understands the symbolism behind her theatrics, or she may feel the need to pay them additional visits!


                                                    The rest of her lair takes place in a sweltering jungle with unfeasibly large spider webs laced throughout the trees and bushes. Victims find navigating through the jungle to be exhausting as no matter how far they run, they never seem to make as much progress as they would like. The weight of the world bares down on them, and some claim they have difficulty breathing, due to anxiety. Like all spiders, the Spider Goddess can sense the vibrations of her web, and she knows when another insect is traipsing through her lair.
