        Nexus Intrusion (Manifestation)
        (Requires Open Condition) An Entity with this Manifestation can force a portion of an alien and often-hostile environment into the physical world.                                     Re: Nexus Intrusion vs Weather Control

                                    There is definitely thematic overlap between the two. My intent with "Nexus Intrusion" was the idea that the entity super imposes its alien realm onto the world. The manifestation needs the flexibility to accommodate a wide variety of possible environments. My main concern with my first write up is that its not explicit about how much area it covers - I am thinking the effect should be limited to an area surrounding the entity - perhaps Rank x 3 yards. Another adjustment is leaving the Intensity of the Environment to a flat level 2 and only allowing essence to be used to add tilts. That way it still allows a clear benefit to Weather Control (greater area and potentially more intensity).
                                    Nexus Intrusion v.2 (Manifestation)
                                    (Requires Open Condition) An Entity with this Manifestation can force a portion of an alien and often-hostile environment into the physical world. Entities will typically manifest environments aligned with its nature (such as a Fire Spirit manifesting the Inferno Tilt) .By spending 3 Essence, creates a Level 2 Extreme Environment and applies an Environmental Tilt. Every additional 2points of Essence spent can add an additional Environmental tilt (to a maximum number of Tilts equal to the Entities Rank). This effect covers a (Rank x 3 yard) radius centered on the entity.The Manifestation lasts until the end of scene.

                                            Re: Scaling Size

                                            Originally posted by Vent0 View Post

                                            What about scaling the size to something like the Safe Place Merit(s)? Rank and they both go to 5, and it allows near-gods to affect vaster areas.
                                            There are a few limits with using Safe Place as a base. The CofD version of the merit uncouples it from the size and area - it now represent degree of security. Good for keeping out intruders, not so good for determining how much terrain the Safe Place actually covers (the book recommends using equipment to determine the property size). Ideally for the flavor and to keep a leash on the effect, I wouldn't want it to extend much farther than the Open Condition it is based on. I went with Rank x3 yards because that is how far a ghost can travel from its anchor , which seems like a good rough estimate to how far such an area would extend.

                                            Practically speaking , a rank 5 entity can open a Nexus Intrusion that has a radius of 15 yards/45 feet. Its a decent area - enough to be a hazard for anyone in the vicinity, but not quite enough to end up on the national news. Boosting it to Rank x 5 probably wouldn't break anything either.

                                                What do you think of these new powers, Reighnhell?

                                                Conjure Servant: The entity may create minor servants from thin air. These servants must be of the same category of Ephemeral Being and share the purview. Spend 2 Essence and roll Power + Finesse. A Rank 1 servant is created and lasts for one hour per Success, or one day per Success with 1 Willpower. For every 2 additional Essence, increase the servant's Rank by 1, which must be lower than the creator's. Halve the Essence cost if the creator is in its native realm. These servants share the Bans and Banes of their creator, start with Essence equal to their Rank, have full Corpus and a maximum Size of [highest between Power and Resistance x 3]. These servants may be improved, altered and compelled with the creator's Influences. If their creator is destroyed, these servants do not vanish. Any given creator may only have a total amount of Rank dots worth of servants existing equal to its [Rank x 3]. This Numen is limited to entities of Rank 4+.

                                                Pocket Realm (Requires Open Condition): By spending 5 Essence, the entity transforms an area with a radius equal to [Power x 100] yards into an environment that it can shape at will. This area is considered a Safe Place 5 and while inside the entity has Influence 5 (Pocket Realm) at no Essence cost. This lasts for one day per Success, or one week per Success with 10 Essence. The Pocket Realm can be made permanent with 1 Willpower point if the area is turned into a Verge. The entity may teleport between Pocket Realms once per scene by spending 1 Willpower point. This Numen is limited to entities of Rank 4+, and they can only have Rank + 1 Pocket Realms at any time.

                                                Mirage: As long as the entity succeeds on a Perception roll, it applies its Defense against ranged attacks. By spending 1 Essence, it may Dodge reflexively. By spending 1 Willpower, it may add its Rank as bonus Success on a Dodge.

                                                Influence Starter (Reaching): As per Firestarter, but is now defined as the creation of a minor instance of one of the entity's Influences, whatever they are. By spending 1 Essence and rolling Power + Finesse, the entity creates one small instance of one of its purview per Success within Power in yards. Unless further improved with the associated Influence, these only last for the scene. This Numen only has to be purchased once, as it allows the entity to create minor instance of all its Influences. Assume that the Equipment Bonus, Size and Availability of the created instance iare equal to those of a mundane equivalent.

                                                Open Third Eye: As per Mixed Blessing, but only allows the entity to bestow Supernatural Merits. To compensate, each time this Numen is purchased, the entity may choose [Rank x 3] dots of Supernatural Merits it can bestow, including Advanced and Epic versions, and spends Essence equal to 1/3 of the dots given. The target must be mortal but does not need to meet the prerequisites of the Merits.

                                                Spark Of Life (Reaching): The entity may bring inanimate objects to life. By spending 1 Essence and rolling Power + Finesse, the object becomes sentient and obeys the commands of the entity, which don't have to be spoken out loud but require direct line of sight. Objects without their own means of locomotion cannot leave their spot, unless the entity spends 2 Essence, which allows the object to levitate up to 10 feet above the ground and move around. Objects have a dice pool for operating themselves equal to the entity's Successes x 2 (maximum dice pool of 10), and remain animate for one hour per Success, or one day per Success with 1 Willpower point. If the entity also has Enchant Object, it may grant Numina and/or Influences to the animated object, which uses its own dice pool for them but needs to receive Essence from the entity.

                                                Enchant Object: By touching an object and rolling Power + Finesse, the entity may transfer its Numina and/or Influences to it. It must spend 2 Essence per Numen or Influence dot (maximum of Rank x 2 Numina/Influences). This lasts for one hour per Success, or one day per Success with 1 Willpower point. The enchanted object startes with 0 Essence, but may receive Essence from willing entities and hold up to [the Rank of the entity which created it x 5] in Essence. It may also activate its powers with Willpower points, and absorb Essence from something with the Open Condition related to the purview of the entity that created it once per day with a roll of Presence + Wits. If the person using this object breaks any of the entity's Bans or allows the object to be struck with any of the entity"s Banes, the object is destroyed immediately. The entity may have as many Enchanted Objects as its Essence supply will allow, but almost always demands something in return for one.

                                                Astral Gateway (Abnormal Rank 3+ Goetia Only — Requires Open Condition): By spending 3 Essence, the entity opens a portal to the Astral that it and other entities may use, applying the “Astral Gate” Condition to the location. The portal only allows the passage of Goetia; for material beings it simply becomes possible to meditate into the Astral, even if they are normally incapable of doing so. [Maybe Goetia of sufficiently high Rank and/or weirdness can have a version of this Manifestation that allows physical entrance into the Astral?]

                                                            Re: The Child Ghost

                                                            Rules as Written, a standard Ghost caps out at Rank power , and this fellow is going to need to be Rank 3 in order to serve the role you need. To do this with the least amount of house-rule, I would suggest that the Orphanage itself carries much of the spirits power. Having an Underworld Gate in the basement that opens on a semi regular basis would allow the Ghost to gather power in the Underworld, and then return o Twilight to collect more friends. The protagonists then have the two-fold issue of defeating the ghost and closing the gate so he cannot return.

                                                            The Orphan
                                                            Rank: 3
                                                            Attributes: Power 8, Finesse 6, Resistance 6, Willpower 10, Essence 20
                                                            Initiative: 12
                                                            Defense: 6
                                                            Speed: 18
                                                            Size: 4
                                                            Corpus: 10
                                                            Virtue Playful, Vice Selfish
                                                            Aspiration: To find New Friends
                                                            Influences: Anchor [Orphanage] 3
                                                            Ban: Must Find a New Friend once a Month
                                                            Bane: Consecrated Salt; Favorite Toy
                                                            Manifestation: Image, Materialize, Possess, Twilight Form
                                                            Numina: Dement,Hallucination, Host Jump, Maze (as the Dread Power), Mortal Mask, Sign, Telekinesis
