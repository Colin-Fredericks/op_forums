    Help me build my Orthodox LS !

    08-13-2015, 01:04 PM
    Hi folks,

    Please first let me introduce you to the setting, which happens to take place in a fictive version of Königsberg/Kaliningrad in northern eastern Europe. My goal here is to create an alternative place that would act as a melting pot of whatever i find interesting for a dark, oppressed and coercitive story taking place in an eastern Europe city.

    The population is a third German, a third local (Baltic and Polish population) and a third Russian.

    Being fictive, you can find in the city : a jewish ghetto and its golem story, some Ukrainian refugees and a large slave market, a powerful Russian mafia, a new group of extremely rich people who got fortunate after 1991, an old Prussian mood that seems haunted by romantic and death ideologies, and, of course, a very large orthodox Church currently building 5 new churches in town.
    The mood might go from Kronenbergs’s “Eastern Promises” to the episodes of Urasawa’s “Monster” to Eisenstein's masterpiece “Ivan Grozny”.

    We all agree that the Lancea Sanctum is, whatever the 2nd edition settings says, heavily bound to the Catholic Church. Now I love the Catholics settings, but I want something different here, I want a scary, dark, mystical and illuminated orthodox Church. I want a Church that believes in millenarianism and in the holy Russian soul, a Church ruled by hairy popes kissing golden icons in the smell of cloudy encens.

    Now, my bishop, which I recall a Starets, is a scary illuminated Nosferatu which looks like the historical Rasputin. His name, Theraponte, is taken from a side character of Dostoievski’s Karamazov, a monk full of hate and disgust for life, that also have personal daily relationships with demon entities (no shit, it's in the book !)

    As I want orthodoxy to be at the center of my kindred setting, how do I organise my LS ? Do i just copy paste the catholic one, changing names ? Is there any outlet, books or extra setting in the WoD for this kind of questions ? some other RPG ? Any metaplot in sight ? Kindred patriarchs background ?

    Obviously i won't get my players to attempt midnight masse, even if one of them has his character coming from an old odd monastery that works as an orphanage.
    My group also contains a esoteric bounty hunter in the search for some old religious treasures.
    The LS has always been the most obscure and undefined covenant in Requiem in my eyes (understand here that the problem is on my side, i just don't get that covenant as good as i get the others), and thus makes it especially hard to adapt.

    Do you guys have any advice, tips, database, movies or literature to share with me ? Basically i understand that i will have to work this from scratch, so any inspirational stuff or good ideas is very welcome here !

    Thanks !


    PS : i am not allowed to upload picture attachments; but i would like to share the visual stuff that i used to that point with you. Is there a way ?
    PPS : as english is not my native tongue, i hope all i speak here is understandable ! thanks for reading.

      So, Russian Orthodox Church.

      The first thing I want to say is that, despite being heavily Catholic influenced, there are plenty of ways of portraying the LS as anything but Catholic. The core of the Church is the Testament of Longinus (which is a good read if you get it).

      All in all, while there's a lot of details that are different between Catholics and Orthodoxes, most of those details will generally be lost when translated to vampire-hood. Orthodox, from the sounds of it, are less hung up on marriage, so that general stance of horror may be better. There's no standard for a Eucharist in the Lancea et Sanctum - there's no real standard for how Midnight Mass runs at all, in fact, so pretty much every city has the potential to be different. There's no Pope, only regional churches, so the default LeS is similar to the Orthodox church in this instance.

      The idea of the Original Sin promenant in Catholicism doesn't apply to vampires - you're damned by your own actions. But, overall, this major difference in cannon for the living really doesn't quite apply to the vampires. At best, an Orthodox LeS might not talk about vampires being Damned, but they still have a divine purpose to fulfill in the world.

      Mary doesn't apply to vampires either, though there is the Livian Heresy, which may still make an appearance in the Orthodox version.

      A large aspect of the Orthodox church is redemption through Good Works instead of the Faith Alone doctrine of the Catholic Church. This means that the LeS would put greater emphasis on hunting sinners and living as holy monsters for earning God's eyes approval.

      There are several different doctrines that differ on the question of what happens when a vampire dies - do they go to rule in Hell, or do they go to Heaven? It often switches, depending on individual games. That's one that'll have to be answered for your church individually.

                                      It's worth noting that the Lancea et Sanctum predates the split between the eastern and western churches by a considerable margin, and that (as I recall) the Eastern Roman Empire was a major center of the Sanctified before and (especially) after the fall of Rome. Eastern kindred are likely just as steeped in the trappings of the Orthodox Church(es) as western kindred are in those of Catholicism (and, to an extent, Protestantism). That said, the Lance in Russia certainly wouldn't use a Latin name.

                                      Also, the whole "salvation by faith alone" like is a Protestant thing; Catholics, as I understand it, still affirm the importance of Good Works.

                                      The point about the Sanctified being distinct from living branches of Christianity is a good one; I imagine that most Christians, with the exception of real fire-and-brimstone types, won't be very comfortable with the teachings of the Lancea et Sanctum.

                                      What makes the Sanctum work, in my mind, is the sheer hopelessness and futility of the kindred condition. As a vampire, you are going to hurt people all the time. You are occasionally going to lose control and kill people. You will be rejected by and/or outlive everyone you care about, unless you're reduced to ash before that point. Once the reality of that sinks in, I can see how the idea that, even as a monster, you're still part of God's plan, would become more attractive. It's a sect that makes sense for vampires, and doesn't really for mortals.
