                And if we were talking about virtually any other subject, I'd agree with you*. But from day one, Promethean's Divine Fire has been the exception when it comes to the Supernal. By all rights, it ought to be describable through (and thus subject to manipulation by) Supernal symbols, because everything in the Fallen World is†. And yet, it isn't. At least, not by any Supernal symbols familiar to any of the ten Arcana. If there are any Supernal symbols describing Pyros phenomena, they exist entirely outside the scope of mage experience. Which, in practical terms, is equivalent to them not existing.

                Among that part of the mage community that knows about Pyros, theories abound concerning its nature. How could it be otherwise? After all, mages are addicted to Mystery, and what is Pyros if not a big Mystery? Some say that Pyros manifests from some part of the Supernal that's beyond the reach of the Watchtowers and the ten Arcana; others say that it's proof of the reality of the Empyrean, the trans-Supernal source of everything the existence and nature of which some archmasters hypothesize. Still others say that it's Abyssal, or that it emanates from the Lower Depths.

                Yet all of these theories are either flawed or worthless — flawed because the Divine Fire doesn't conform to what is known about, say, the Abyss or the Lower Depths; or worthless because there are no practical benefits to identifying it as “exotic Supernal” or “trans-Supernal”: it's just mages attaching a name to something they don't know, in the hopes that naming it will make it comprehensible.
                * 2e may change this. But unless and until I see some indication that they will, I'm sticking to my guns on this one.

                † Except, of course, the Abyssal; but Pyros isn't Abyssal either — though that hasn't stopped some mages of categorizing it as such in a desperate attempt to get it to fit somewhere in their model of reality.

                            Now that I'm thinking about it, what I would like to see in the book is the ability to share some of the splat-exclusive merits.
