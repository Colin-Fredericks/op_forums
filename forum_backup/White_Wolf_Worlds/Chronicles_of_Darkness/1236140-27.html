                                    Originally posted by Michael View Post
                                    I meant personality-wise. Like, I get he's a big(?) guy with a jackal head, but that's not a character, that's a physical description.
                                    I don't know, while we don't have many surviving myths that would help to get a sense of Anubis' personality, his is one of the most consistent depictions across pop culture for ancient gods, even Egyptian gods. Silent, patient, deliberate, slow moving, focus on the job, the works. Not universal, but unusually regular, that's why I posted that.

                                    Other than that, I don't think it would be a positive thing to describe him further. As someone who'll actually interact with the PCs on a regular basis and possibly have a huge impact on the game by default, I think it's better to have him as this placeholder character with a clear-ish function, but open for the ST to put whatever they want.

                                    Princes and whatnot are characters that need good characterization because they color particular settings, the Judges color a very particular moment where they fulfill a very specific narrative role (and even then we don't really have that much on particular personalities), but Anpu/Anubis will pop out every now and then and interact up close, it's personal. This shouldn't be decided for the ST as it can mess with a lot of dials for the chronicle.
