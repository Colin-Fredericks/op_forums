                                                    Originally posted by marin View Post
                                                    Experiments or rituals gone awry. Still not exactly family friendly.
                                                    It does make me wonder how similar Deviants are going to be to one another. They're all altered, but does the "not entirely a stable template" mean that these alterations don't all tap into the same prime cause? It would make thematic sense, given what we know - and if there's an energy stat, it would make sense for it to not always be the same one - like one has Pyros, another has Essence, a third has some bastardised mix of Sekhem and Vitae, a fourth has an unprecedented type of energy, and a fifth is dependent on her Willpower. But that also sounds like too much of a mechanical nightmare.

                                                    Multiple feeding types, not all being available to every Deviant, would make sense. So, I guess, would having no supernatural energy type at all. And fuel's only a small part of my question anyway.

                                                    But apart from Hunter, all the games have a prime cause for their supernatural phenomena: even in Vampire and Promethean, where the clans and lineages have independent origins, Vitae and the Divine Fire exist above every individual type. So is there going to be an "above everything" for Deviant? If so, what is it? The only thing I can think of is some kind of "web of life" that the Deviant's body touches when rebelling against the changes done to it.

                                                    Or will it be like Hunter, where there's no presumed "above everything." And if Deviants partake of multiple natures, will it be possible to mechanically represent natures that brush up against the other game lines? And will it mean that the "blowout mode" might not be something available to all?
