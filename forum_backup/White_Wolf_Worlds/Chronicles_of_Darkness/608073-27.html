    For me the questions are how Deviant is going to be different than "Mortals with Supernatural Merits" from the CofD core.     However, I just worry that its something that we could have just done with a CofD supplement rather than a complete new game and that Deviant won't mesh setting-wise well with other merits say, the psychic merits from the CofD core.

                            Originally posted by Firanai View Post
                            Just out of curiosity, what kind of evil corporations and organizations from fiction would be perfect for this game? We obviously have the Umbrella corporation, but what other suggestions do you have?
                            ​From Marvel comics:
                            Project Rebirth/Department H/Weapon Plus; Joint ventures between the U.S. and Canadian governments, intended to create living weapon​s.
                            The Red Room; Russia's version of Project Rebirth/Department H/Weapon Plus.
                            Oscorp; Privately owned company, sometimes a defense contractor, prone to illegal/unethical medical experiments.
                            Roxxon; Energy company, somewhere between our worlds Exxon/BP and CWoD's Pentex in terms of environmental safety.
                            U-Men; Basically The Cheiron Group, they steal body parts from mutants in order to implant them into their own bodies to gain the mutant's powers.
                            ​A.I.M.; Advanced Idea Mechanics, a group of mad-scientist types. banded together in order to conduct research with near-unlimited resources, and without any form of ethical oversight. Also, formerly the science division for Hydra, so some Nazi/terrorist connections.

                                                        Originally posted by Firanai View Post
                                                        Just out of curiosity, what kind of evil corporations and organizations from fiction would be perfect for this game? We obviously have the Umbrella corporation, but what other suggestions do you have?
                                                        As a member of the SCP Foundation wiki, I am eagerly anticipating the chance to one day play a Deviant Chronicle that flips the traditional perspective of said site: you are not an Agent of the Foundation or another Group Of Interest who hunts anomalies, you ARE the anomaly on the run from these ruthless organizations. Here's a list of the factions that could be adapted to CofD and Deviant. Imagine what it must be like to be taken in by Dr. Wondertainment (TM) and be turned into a trademarked mutant with gimmicks meant to entertain as if you were a living toy, but said Doctor (if they are even a human being) has a very reckless and sometimes downright alien definition of what is fun. Or imagine that you are an orphan living in the streets, but you have a potentially spectacle-worthy talent or supernatural power, and you are approached by Hermann Fuller's Circus Of The Disquieting. The more you define your life and identity around the Circus, the more you find your gift seeping into you and changing you.

                                                        There's a mountain of excellent inspirational material there, but I wish you perseverance to overcome the archive panic you will feel when the extent of the setting becomes apparent. If by any chance you would like to narrow things down, you can use the Tag Search, and focus on tags like "humanoid". If that doesn't help, you can send me a private message and I'll try to help you.
