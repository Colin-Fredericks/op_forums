                            Actually looking at the platest document we know alittle bit about how Court Contracts work.

                            You much be a member (or possibly Court Goodwill), and they are shaped by your Mantle Instead of your Wyrd.

                            So where a Universal or Goblin Contract would be Attribute + Skill + Wyrd for activation, a Court Contract is Attribute + Skill + Mantle for activation.

                            We also know every court gets an Emotional Contract keyed to the court's emotion and the Talespinning Contract (since its universally available to every court I'm not sure why its not just a universal contract, but I suspect that the contract ties into the Courts individual story some how), and a third contract unique to the Court.

                            Also Contracts change your Changeling, likely for Mien some how, and this is likely true for Court Contracts.

                            They cost a 1 experience per dot, so they no more expensive then merits.

                            That's all we know so far about Court Contracts.
