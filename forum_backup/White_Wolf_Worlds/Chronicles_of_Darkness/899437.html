    Which Splats Are The Best For Playing An Übermensch?

    05-25-2016, 06:04 PM
    I'd say Demon, Beast and Mage. I'm not an expert on Nietzsche, so take this with a spoon of salt.

    Demons can have just about any characteristic as a Virtue or Vice. With the "Living The Lie" Merit, they can gain Willpower from the Virtues and Vices of all their Covers, not just the main one. The Agendas, Catalysts and espionage-centered gameplay encourage a very individualistic mindset. The existence of Antinomians shows that it's possible for Demons to not act solely based on the leftovers of their angelic programming. Even those who stick closer to their original function can learn, adapt and explore much more freely than Angels.

    Beasts do not have Breaking Points in the way other splats have. Their grim necessities can often lead to an inner turmoil, but those who embrace their heritage can defy the notion that they are just forces of destruction meant to die by the sword. However, this notion is often introduced to them by other Beasts, and the way some of them teach might be too dogmatic and invasive for an Übermensch. It seems that the Incarnates are the true Over-Beasts, as they are not bound to the rules of Heroes or even other monsters.

    The shared vocabulary of Mages and the four main Orders speaks for itself: Sleepers, Lie, Awakened, the list goes on. They are practically synonymous with the Will To Power (one of their alternate names is even "Will-Workers"). Though the Orders have different approaches to Wisdom, it favors carefulness and subtlety over any Sleeper notion of fairness or mercy. The Seers might be an edge case: they have no qualms about ignoring all the rules of mere mortals and stepping on them, but the will of their Exarchs always comes first.

    An honorable mention goes to Hunters and the Code they create for themselves in order to better uphold the Vigil.

    Bye.
