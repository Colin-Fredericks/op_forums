              Mortals do posses a few advantages, but their primary advantages are two things. First, on a big scale, numbers. They outnumber the other splats probably tens of thousands to 1. That doesn't help on a personal scale but it is a huge advantage.

              On a personal scale their biggest advantage is being underestimated. Supernatural merits don't typically rise to the power level of supernatural powers, but then monsters likely aren't expecting any supernatural ability at all from a human.

              Does that make them individually strong? Not really. They're probably the weakest thing in CofD on a personal level. But it does mean that a clever mortal, or even a not clever one with the right trick or two, can turn the tables on a supernatural foe.

              Of course there's also a level that's neither personal nor relying on strength of numbers and that's a more background type level where people are using things like resources, allies, contacts, and status to oppose one another and at that level mortals can do very well indeed.

                          Supernatural merits absolutely come with baggage. They're generic mechanics that you can reskin, so how they function and what limitations they have as a result are for that reason not generally described mechanically.

                          To give an example: Laying on Hands. The merit itself says that it's somehow related to the character's faith or devotion. Logically that suggests that the character has to maintain that faith/devotion or the power goes away. It's not mentioned in the merit, it's a feature of the narrative.

                          The big part of being a template is that narratively you have an easy justification for buying more powers.

                              3. Ignore the Darkness: The Darkness is wide spread, but it isn't everywhere, you can always find a place without the taint of the Darkness, a place you can call home.

                              5. Exploit the Darkness: The Darkness is rich, with untap potential, that no one have Monopoly in, and the Market demand what can't be obtain.
                              Example: A Company dedicating to collect Ghost Essence for spiritual healing.
