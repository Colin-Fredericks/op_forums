                                <shrug> The way I see the interactions of Axioms, Inferences, and Fields are:
                                Axiom: Defines the category of effect (Healing or Improvement are Exelixi, Destruction is Katastrofi)
                                Axiom Rank: Establishes the degree (Bashing vs Lethal damage, minor manipulation vs total Mind Control)
                                Inference: Is the specific effect (Health damage vs reducing object Durability, or Healing vs Attribute Increase)
                                Field: Defines the subject or method (Biology means it operates on or through living creatures, Mechanics means it operates on or through artificial constructions, Ectoplasmics means it operates on or through ectoplasm, etc.)

                                I think Field shouldn't be locked down at "This Field offers these Inferences/Effects". I see Field as more clarification, focus, and flavoring.

                                Wonder does [Effect][to degree] to/through [subject]. [Effect] is the Inference (grouped by Axiom), [to degree] is determined by Axiom Rank (possible the Inference itself, gate-locked behind a certain minimum Axiom rating), and [subject] is the Field.

                                Field is there so a Genius specializing in Biology can't just make a fusion cannon. Not without hefty justification how something biological is able to generate nuclear fusion. Their Katastrofi weaponry is going to have to use bacteria, or organ-produced acid or something.

                                That's just my thoughts.
