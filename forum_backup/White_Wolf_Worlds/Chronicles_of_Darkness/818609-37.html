                        Going to dump more ideas before I forget them:

                        The Many Flavors of Havoc
                        So, other than adding Faults, breaking/being destroyed, there are certain Catastrophic fates that may befall Wonders. Some examples include:
                        • Katastrofi Wonders often explode, damaging their surroundings an operators. A few Metatropi Wonders behave similarly (turned to dust by an explosion, or transmuted into fine silica being academic to victims). Protasia Wonders are also sometimes know to overload and explode. And the forces manipulated by certain Epikrato Wonders can run away to disastrous effects.
                        • Automata Wonders are the most likely to "go Orphan", attaining independence (and sometimes motion). Exelixi Wonders follow behind, though they often grant something else the independence. Some Metatropi Wonders have similar results.
                        • Skafoi Wonders can randomly displace things, or even open inter-dimension/temporal rifts (like Orphaning, in that there is a new "Wandering Monster", but it may have no relation to the source Wonder). A few Apokalypsi Wonders have "dialed" a few entities better left undisturbed too.
                        • Epikrato Wonders often warp or unhinge their victims, permanently scarring them. Metatropi Wonders are also known for this.
                        • etc.

                        Havoc and Consequences
                        Maybe Havoc could be something like [Obligation] + Inspiration? - Faults + Wonder Rank or Category? + triggerer's applicable Skill. Failure means another Fault (making it more likely to go wrong next time), Dramatic Failure a Catastrophy. Success, nothing happens (maybe a temporary -1 to the next roll in a scene?). Ex Success... Yay? Immune to Havoc next time or for the Scene?

                        Oh, and a Havoc Failure should probably be an [Obligation] Trigger - nothing tends to infuriate Mad Scientists more than "cretins" messing with their toys and breaking them.

                        Size Matters - Bigger is Better
                        I think there should be some mechanical impact and incentive to have Wonders of larger Sizes. Bonus to effects, cheaper to produce, etc. Larger Wonders have their own inherent disadvantages (just where are you going to store your 20 meter War Mech? And how much notice is it going to attract?), in addition to...

                        Raw Materials
                        I'm thinking part of Wonder creation involves coming up with at least* the Wonder's Size in materials. Clones and monsters require organic material to be composed of, super-computers need silicon and conductors, and if you are somehow generating energy fields or beings, that energy needs to come from somewhere. Bigger Wonders are stronger, but also more costly.

                        * I said "at least", because there may be room for a bonus (though not as big a one as permanent Size) for requiring Developmental Material - that Juggernaut Serum may only end up being Size 1 (for Consumable vials) or 0 (for an Internalize treatment), but it requires 200 liters of fluid to be refined, and the machinery to do so. You can even get creative - your Secret Kung Fu Technique (Internalized Wonder) may "consume" volumes of obscure Academics materials in perfecting it.

                        X and Y Splats
                        Does anyone think either should give (an option of) a free Field, or are Fields too idiosyncratic for that to be an option to list? Should Foundations/Baramins grant "in favored Axiom" discounts for Wonder Creation, since they are mostly groups of like minded individuals sharing knowledge? Easier to build a Robot when you have the information and support of the Artificers Union on your side.

                        I've got some comments on the "Foundations and Baramins are mostly gone" debate/idea, but I'll add that later.

                                                    Just realized that we should probably figure out what should be possible on each dot of Axiom. But here’s an incomplete write up for Fields.

                                                    Fields
                                                    [Fluff]

                                                    Gaining Fields and Inferences
                                                    Each Genius can learn any Field he desires without training, though training will significantly reduce the required time, should he have its Skill and Axiom requirements. Once a Field is learnt, he gains one of it’s Inferences for free, usually an Inference of one of his Favored Axioms. After that he may purchase Inferences either through spending experience or by raising Inspiration, which grants a free Inference in a known Field.

                                                    Favored Fields
                                                    Favored Fields are determined by the character’s Method (Artificer, Director, Scholastic, etc.), his Fellowship, and his own personal preference, with each granting one Favored Field. Rogue Geniuses (a steadily rising percentage of them) gain an extra Favored Field, this vanishes upon joining a Fellowship, replaced by one of said Fellowships Favored Field.

                                                    Obscure Inferences
                                                    Obscure Inferences are quite different, they require either a teacher or up to twice the required training times.

                                                    Sidebar
                                                    On the Precipice of Discovery
                                                    Whether or not they are sane the Inspired are scientists they are able to, nay, obligated to pioneer new technology as never seen before, even by their own peers. The Inferences presented here are not an exhaustive list, there is nothing to prevent players from adding their own custom Inferences or wholesale Fields other than the approval of the Storyteller. As a general rule, each Field should have a minimum of six Inferences, from at least four different Axioms. When designing new Fields and its Inferences consult the examples presented here and how they progress.


                                                    Field of Astronomy
                                                    Space, the final frontier. For as long as mankind has lived we have wondered what it was and lies within it, whether it was gods or aliens, this is undeniable. It is no wonder that this Field is fairly popular among the Inspired.
                                                    Required Skill: Science
                                                    Inferences:
                                                    • Deep Space Scanner (Apokaypsi ●+): Wonders using this Inference can be used to detect and scan objects in outer space, such as asteroids and exoplanets. Apokalypsi ● can be used to scan the entirety of our solar system for particularly large objects, large asteroids, planets, kilometer long space ships, etc. though it can only detect what regular high tech instruments can and even the most minor of stealth Wonders would hide things from it, and can remotely control any spaceborne Automata or Skafoi Wonders the character had set it to. Apokalypsi (●●) allows for a Clash of Wills to detect any cloaked objects and allows for the detection of extraterrestrial lifeforms and for it’s scans to reach throughout the galaxy. Apokalypsi (●●●) allows for scans to reach all throughout the galactic cluster, Apokalypsi (●●●●) scans reaches throughout the galactic supercluster, and Apokalypsi (●●●●●) allows for scans reaching all throughout the universe.
                                                    • Life Support (Exilixi ●): This Inference is meant to be used with the Space Station or Spaceship Inference below; This Inference allows for the spacecraft to support human life, with recycled atmosphere and artificial gravity. Exilixi (●●●) allows for any lifeform to survive in the Wonder,
                                                    • Orbital Destruction Cannon (Katastrofi ●●●): Wonders made using this Inference can fire from the surface to orbit and vice-versa, though they have a base Size of 6. Accuracy fluctuates wildly as the cannon is calibrated for surface-to-orbit fire and have a greater chance of missing the closer the target is and the smaller they are, though certain Apokaypsi Inferences may fix this issue.
                                                    • Terraforming (Metaptropi ●●●●●): Wonders created using this Inference can change the very surface of a planet, changing it’s atmosphere and soil make up to allow for human (or stranger) life. Barren planets would remain devoid of life and Terraforming
                                                    • Cosmic Shielding (Prostasia ●): This Inference blocks all manner of natural cosmic radiation and debris. Shielding from the weapons of spacecraft or other weaponry require other Prostasia Inferences. Prostasia ●● would also protect from collisions with spaceborne asteroids, Prostasia ●●● would protect even crashes from orbit.
                                                    • Space Station (Prostasia ●●●): This Inference allows for the creation of spaceborne structures.
                                                    • Spaceship (Skafoi ●●+): Wonders built using this Inference can travel to space, Skafoi (●●) can move planet to planet in a matter of days and travel up to the limits of the solar system in a few months. Skafoi (●●●) can travel faster-than-light, and may reach several nearby (for outer space standards) solar systems in weeks, and intra-solar planets in hours. Skafoi (●●●●) can go even farther, going from Earth to Neptune in minutes, reaching other solar systems in hours, and can even go to other galaxies in weeks. Skafoi (●●●●●) allows for casual intergalactic travel. Though without Prostasia cosmic radiation would kill any traveler on any trip lasting more than an hour.




                                                    Field of Dimensions
                                                    As each Genius learned during their Breakthrough, there are an infinite number of possible worlds, each with it’s own unique laws of physics. Several have learned to tap into those possibilities.
                                                    Required Skill:
                                                    Inferences:
                                                    • Dimensional Scanner (Apokalypsi ●●●●): Scanners built using this Inference can scan other worlds and can detect the nearest way into those worlds. It would also allow for communication between such worlds.
                                                    • Schrödinger’s Gun (Katastrofi ●●●●): This Wonder is able to project it’s attack through the dimensional barrier into alternate ‘Mirror’ universes or, when in a pinch, into the Twilight. Though it may be useful, some Geniuses find themselves under attack from other possible versions of themselves when feeling especially unsure of their own decisions, and many try to return the sentiment.
                                                    • Pocket Dimension (Metaptropi ●●●●●): This Wonder would allow for the creation of Personal Dimensions. This Dimension follows the physical laws of the Genius’ home universe and has space within it equal to (Inspiration x Metatropi (5) x 5) square cube of space. Many use such devices to store their Wonders and materials, and at times, their labs. Though such Dimensions are Bardo by nature and thus may suffer from Havoc, its similarity to the Genius’ home universe allow it to exist through only it’s own bound Mania. Exposure to worlds with different enough physical laws would subject it to the Mania requirements of regular Bardo.
                                                    • Dimensional Shield (Prostasia ●●●●): This Inference allows for shielding from extra-dimensional attacks and intrusion, calling for a contested roll between the Wonder’s creator and the intruding being
                                                    • Dimensional Portals (Skafoi ●●●●): This Inference allows Wonders to open gateways into alternate universes. A Skafoi (●●●●●) wonder built using this Inference can open ways into any other world, such as the Gauntlet, Supernal Realms, Hedge, and Arcadia. Though the denizens of such realms would rarely welcome such intrusion.
