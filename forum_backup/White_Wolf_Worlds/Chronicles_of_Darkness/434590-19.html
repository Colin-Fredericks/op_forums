                                                Some people worried that Call of the Depths might infringe upon Besieging the Tower of Will. Have a look at the 2E version and let me know if you're still worried Besieging the Tower of Will (Descendant Channel)

                                                The Leviathan's innate ability to casually dominate the herd is refined into a cold psychic sword that drives humans mad even as it enslaves them.
                                                  • Dice Pool: Presence + Intimidate + Sheol vs. Target's Resolve + Power Trait
                                                  • Action Type: Instant

                                                The Leviathan may telepathically communicate to speak unseen. The target is aware that the Leviathan is the one communicating with them. As the Leviathan sinks Deeper they move beyond communication to shape the minds, and souls, of their victims.
                                                • "Depth 0": Birthright of Sanctity
                                                • "Depth 1": At the cost of 1 Ichor, the Leviathan may create a psychic link to anyone within Sheol times 50 feet that they can perceive through natural means or Channels. The link remains for the remainder of the scene and allows the Leviathan to communicate telepathically at will.
                                                • "Depth 2": As above, and the Leviathan gains an alternative ability. Instead of forming a psychic link they may implant an order into the target’s mind. The order may be as complicated or as vague as the Leviathan wishes and the target will obey to the best of their ability. They are unable to creatively misinterpret orders, however they may quite easily genuinely misinterpret orders because:

                                                Drawback: From the moment the command is implanted to the end of the scene, the target takes the Madness Condition. A Leviathan’s psychic dominance is neither subtle nor kind to the minds of their victims.
                                                • "Depth 3": As above, and the Leviathan may spend an additional 1 Ichor to use the Depth 2 Iteration of this Channel with a delay. The Leviathan specifies a trigger and when the target experiences that trigger they immediately attempt to carry out their orders. The target is aware that they were the victim of some manner of psychic attack but without supernatural abilities they have no way of knowing the details of what happened to them.

                                                Drawback: The target suffers Madness in both the scene where the madness is implanted and the scene where the trigger is activated.
                                                • "Depth 4": As above, and the range extends to Sheol miles. The Leviathan still requires some way to perceive their targets without technological aid.
                                                • "Depth 5": As above, and the Leviathan may use the Depth 2 iteration of this Channel on up to Sheol + Wits targets in a single turn. All targets must be given the same command, but it only costs 2 Ichor to affect both of them.
                                                • "Depth 6": As above, and the Leviathan may spend an additional 3 Ichor to completely replace the personality of a single Wake vulnerable individual with a new personality of the Leviathan’s own design. The Leviathan needs to accumulate Willpower successes, but may roll as an Extended action making one roll per turn. This effect is permanent but it can be undone. Either the target’s on mental defences can purge the foreign elements given time and extreme psychological stimulus. Alternatively supernatural powers can reverse the effect. As a Leviathan is divine, their psychic powers can reach through the connection between mind and soul. Thus undoing the Leviathan’s influence properly requires purging both.

                                                Drawback: The implanted personality will suffer from Madness and cannot be cured, only restoring the original personality can end the Condition. If the target is a mortal, they will be Beloved; as being Beloved is in of itself a form of Madness there is no need to take the Condition.
                                                • Mind Blast (OO or OOOO): In Depth 3 or lower, the Leviathan may unleash a psychic attack as an instant action. It costs 1 Ichor per Sheol + Resolve shots. The Leviathan rolls Presence + Intimidation + Sheol, subtracting the target's Resolve + Power Trait. The effects of success depend on the number of dots in this Merit the Leviathan possesses. With the two-dot version of this Merit, the Leviathan inflicts Bashing damage with the Stun property. With the four-dot version of this Merit, the Leviathan gains the option to inflict Lethal damage with the Stun property. The Leviathan may choose to split successes among multiple opponents, subtract only the highest Resolve + Power Trait amongst the targets.
                                                • Assimilation (OOOO+): Purchasing this Merit creates a Memory Slot. In Depth 3 or lower, the Leviathan may devour the mind of a recently deceased Wake Vulnerable character. This requires a (Resolve + Stamina - Target's Intelligence) check on the part of the Leviathan. On a failure, the mind is destroyed. On a success, the Leviathan fills one Memory Slot and benefits from one of the following:
                                                  • A one-dot increase in one social or mental Attribute the devoured had at a higher rank than the Leviathan.
                                                  • The devoured's rank in one skill.
                                                  • Once per session the Leviathan may roll Intelligence + Sheol and ask the storyteller (or the devoured’s former player) one question per success. The Storyteller answers truthfully, from the devoured’s perspective.

                                                    I don't know if this has been answered or not, but...

                                                    2e has really boosted the power scale of monster races as a whole*, with two of our best examples being vampires degrading damage by one level towards Bashing (and personally I would have made it that vampires don't take bashing damage from weapons at all, it just makes no sense you can punch out someone who shrugs off having his shoulderblade blown into chunks) except from Bane damage, and Werewolves having both a damage protection (no Agg except from Banes and Lethal rollover) and suped up healing (to the tune of instantly regenerating all non-Agg damage each turn).

                                                    So, I was curious: how are Leviathans going to compare? Their whole "thing" is their horrifically half-divine nature and their freakish fecundity, so, to me at least, that suggests that they should have some pretty major league innate armor and/or healing, and I'm not just talking the extra health-blocks gained from their Size boost by shifting forms.

                                                    Also, does it make sense to anyone else that the Channels should include some options for acid and/or poison? Since bio-electricity is out due to the thematic association of lightning with Sky and thus The Enemy, acid and venom are the major "biological" options for nastiness.

                                                    Is the "spawn minions" mechanic still going to be in effect for Leviathans? There's a lot of primeval monsters that could produce monsters in turn to aid themselves - I'm sure I remember some mythological creature producing scorpions from its open wound instead of blood - so being able to spawn mooks or swarms in mid-combat, which'd be lower power but instantly available in comparison to the "seeding produced heirs", would b e kind of fitting for Leviathans.
