    Nameless War – Fan Approach

    03-02-2018, 11:00 PM
    As we are years before Tome of Pentacle to spot some official light on Nameless War events in Mage – let’s talk about it, in fans takes. Here is my interpretation of events - it's more guessing but based on 1E and 2E gameline materials. Strong inspiration for this I had from Jonathan Strange and Mr Norrell ( book or TV series ) and Hamilton ( rap musical about Founding Fathers of America ).

    Notice: We talk here about Nameless Movement, not faction or Order. We treat all the Nameless as Social Movement - with first groups like Pirate Republic of Nassau - that comes and goes until year 1900, when most of this Movement become Free Council. Prior to this, Nameless Movement is coalition of various groups and cults, sometimes fighting one another AND Diamond and Seers - similar to the later Civil Rights, LGBT+ and Environmental Movements. Basically Nameless War is one century of inter- and outer- Nameless Movements conflicts for fresh Nameless.
    1. Circa 1765 - Sleepers start their American Revolution ideas – with famous 'No Taxation Without Representation' idea. Revolution starts as movement.
    2. Since 1765 - Rise of Adamantine Arrow in Diamond Orders at large - it's the preparation for the War of Independence, in the end. Many young mages are preparing for fighting and join Arrow to be prepared, sensing coming conflict.
    3. Circa 1770 - Starts of proper American War of Independence – American – Britain tension rise to highest with Boston Massacre in 1770. Armed incidents are spreading in Colonise. Seers connected with British Empire are attacking Diamond in battle.
    4. 1770 - Destruction of transcontinental relations between Diamond and Seers Orders members with start of War for Independence. So New York Silver Ladder caucus stops to answer to the 'higher-ups' in London – and alike.
    5. Since 1770 - Rise of Nameless mages in numbers on general, from the chaos of War of Independence in Sleepers society. Weaken Diamond as organisation means more freshly Awakened are not recruited by using Labirynth, Cryptopoly or other typical mechanism. Those left alone new mages are more prone to rebel to authority of Orders in general - and so risen in times of Sleepers revolution - have strong antiauthoritarian views, building proper Nameless Movement and passing Revolution from Sleepers to Awakened society at dawn of 19th century, with information about French Revolution.
    6. 1785 – 1793(?) Creation of Nameless Movement. At the start, Nameless were group of Nameless Cults (Orders) groups fighting with Diamond, Seers or even themselves – after seeing that American and French Revolutions come to fruition. It's more accurate to talk about Nameless Movement than Nameless (global) Order, as each faction wanted to pull and win their side of narrative.
    7. Till 1793(?) - Post Sleepers American and French Revolutions, till start of proper Nameless War - Newly recruited in Revolution and not fully formed young Adamantine Arrows are 'bored' in peaceful live of Awakened now, when all they know is battle magic. When Nameless Movement start to take shape, many of those young Arrows are joining various Nameless factions, just to have excuse to fight with their old Orders siblings and in spite of their previous mentors.
    8. 1793 - War of Nameless has started in Awakened society, when Sleepers Revolutions are slowly ending. Reign of Terror in France push parts of Hegemonic Ministry to go into coalition with local Nameless Movement factions - and Nameless respond with violence, starting Nameless War.

                                  If you're looking for inspiration, I'd suggest reading up on the history of communism as an example of what is probably the most successful global movement. Not just in terms of violent revolutions everywhere, but where the leaders of those revolutions came from, where they got their ideas (not just the ideological ones, but the strategic ones), where they traveled to and who they talked with. You don't have to agree with communism to track the methodologies they used to spread their belief.

                                  In the same vein, I'd also suggest looking at the Non-Aligned Movement, which began during the Cold War as a kind of third option between the two major superpower blocs. They self-described as "history's biggest peace-movement" and can give some color to what motivations for Nameless organizational efforts may have been working towards.

                                          If your history of the nameless war doesn't have Pontiac's War, the Haitian Revolution, and the masonic conflicts in pre-independence Mexico City as more contributive, I'm not sure. I understand that the canonical books reference it as a defining moment, but I'd honestly really object to the idea that the slave owner/land speculator counterrevolution of 1776 has much to tell mages who eventually violently oppose the Seers. Now, if you posit the counterrevolution of 1776 as a seizure of state and power by Seers and possibly allied Diamond order mages I think you might get more traction to be historic.

                                          That said, it's likely that there were precursors to the coming nameless war in the pre-1776 colonial conflicts in North America, Africa, and Asia. You could also put precedent on the Glorious Revolution in England (see A Field in England).
