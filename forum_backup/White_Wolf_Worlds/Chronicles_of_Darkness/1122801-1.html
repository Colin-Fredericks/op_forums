        I'm (very slowly) writing the supernatural set up for Rome, as you can see from my signature. Secrets of the Covenants (and Requiem for Rome, obviously) have something to say about my city, but the only thing that directly contradicts what I've written is that the Prince of Rome is canonically a man, while my setting has a female Gangrel Prince. I did my write up years before the release of the book, so things could have changed in my setting, if you want. The position of the Prince was very shaky, so that is very much a possibility.

        I've ideas for both Mage and Changeling, but I haven't written anything yet. Since "all the roads lead to Rome" one of the main Mysteries of the setting will be the state of Rome as a "City of Doors" not unlike Sigil from Planescape, with lots of conflict between "natives" and people coming from all over the world. For Changeling I'd like a Court system based on the minor Arcana, so there would be a Gold (which is how Italians call the Pentacle suit), Cups, Swords and Wands Court, since our card games use the tarots as a playing deck.

        Italian nuisance. English is my second language, so be patient!

            BARCELONA:

            - Vampire: Ruled by Javier Montoya, an Ordo Dracul Prince since about 100 years ago. The other covenants have a lot of strong internal divisions, so he has supported some factions of those covensnts in exchange for their support in his princedom. Montoya is obsessed with Dragon Nests snd Ley Lines from Barcelona, specially in the one from Sagrada Familia, the strongest he's found and where he believes there is an ancient mystical power slumbering and waiting to awake.

            - Werewolf: An unofficial protectorate ruled by a coalition of two Lodges, one Forsaken (Lodge of St. Jordi) and one Pure (Order of the Dragonslayers) with the same Patron, an spirit of catalan culture called the Dragonslayer with s very strong symbolism related to St George (Saint Patron of Catalonia). Both defend catalan culture and have implanted an apartheid model against spanish werewolves (specially by the Ivory Claws, the Forsaken are more tolerant).*
            * For those who know about it, that's not any metaphore or paralelism with real live in Catalonia. It's just one idea I thought appropiate for Werewolf.

            - Mage: Libertine Dictatorship. During Spanish Civil War, the Seers made a great successful offensive in all rhe country. The Barcelona Counsilium decided they couldn't save the city and ordered to abandone it, saving whatever could be saved and destroying the rest. But Free Councilers (and some members of the other Orders) decided to resist, and against all the odds, they won and broke the siege (bring the Battle of Barcelona one of the most cheered events of European Libertines).
            After that, they refused to return power to the Consilium and stablished a Libertine Assembly to rule the city. During 60 years, under the guidance and counsel of Seimei, a Japanese Libertine (see Free Council book) the Assambky worked and respected the orders. But after her death, a young charismatic radical called Serrallonga, has become the "first citizen" of the Assambly and has converted it in a tiranny of the masses, exiling, punishing and "disappearing" any who publicy opposes to its decissions. In response, a cabal of young Thearchs, Els Cadells (The Cubs) have initiated a resistence movement againstthe Libertines for restoring the freedom for the Awakened from Barcelona.
