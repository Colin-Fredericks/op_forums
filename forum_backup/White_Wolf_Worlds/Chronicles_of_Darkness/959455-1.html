    Had the meeting, wound up not having to go to Atlanta because my brother had a meeting he forgot to tell us about, so here is an expanded version of my Supernatural innocents in Savannah post.

    Thanks, Savannah to me is a perfect place for a monster school, since it's walkable enough for kids to get around without parental help, just dangerous enough that the kids need to be somewhat street smart and has the tunnels for the school to put the classrooms for the more supernatural classes in beneath the normal part of the school. Not to mention that the city is full of shops and attractions that a supernatural child might find useful. Sin eater kids probably get most of their mementos at the many local antiques shops, Uratha pups would practice hunting at Skidaway state park and Oatland Island wildlife center, The area around the Georgia state railroad museum is the largest playground in the state, so we know what the neutral zone for the supernatural cliques is,(it has to be the neutral zone what with being a locus and a Hallow (and the only one they can use without their parents looking ) The mighty 8th air force museum is popular with Ugallu kids and occasionally the dumber sin eater kids will try to steal memento weapons from world war two from it (They usually fail because TFV isn't stupid), the smart sin eater kids hang out at Laurel Grove Cemetery and have secret santas there every Christmas (since the writer of the song jingle bells is buried there, but only sin-eater girl scouts are allowed near Gordon Low's grave), but the Juliet Gordon low house is off limits to non girl scouts, the supernatural scouts have some really weird merit badges, and don't ask about the special cookies....
    As for the additional info:

    The Jim Williams Charter School network isn't really a charter school, it's just registered that way as a meaning of only allowing in supernatural kids, even though the non-profit running it is actually a front for the supernaturally aware branch of the education department. Georgia's growing charter school sector has provided a perfect cover for the monster school, though the recent Latin Academy scandal in Atlanta has resulted in stronger state financial oversight of charter schools in Georgia that has forced the Jim Willaims charter school board to get creative in how they describe the more occult learning materials in the budget reports. In addition, the Jim Williams charter requires that the school be paid by the state based on outcome, meaning both grades and attendance, resulting in the schools having some very skilled truant officers.

    The explicitly supernatural classes are taught in the rooms adjoining the yellow fever tunnels, beneath the school basement where subjects such as AP Spirit psychology, First Tongue grammar and spelling and others, depending on grade level and supernatural type, can be taught without interference from mundane educational authorities. Like all schools in Savannah, the Jim Willaims schools have a distinctive school uniform. Jim Willaims mandatory school uniforms consist of khaki pants, a white shirt and a black vest with the Jim Williams crest on the front pocket and a sash in the color associated with the student's supernatural type (Brown/orange for werewolves and wolf-bloods, Dark Blue for Sin-eaters, Aqua for mages and Proximi, Grey for the offspring of the unchained, purple for beasts, red for dhampirs, teal for lucifuge kids and so on). parents of students with shape-shifting abilities must have their uniforms modified to suit them out of pocket.

    (wrote this next part in a bit of a silly mood because being Bipolar does that to me sometimes when I am cycling toward manic. disregard this next part if you think it's too silly)

    EDIT: actually now that I think about it, there needs to be some comic relief in every setting, and while ripping off Five Nights using The Lovecraftie Cheeses may seem silly to the players, being pursued by animatronic deep ones when you pissed off the Beast kid with Cthulhu as his horror is still scary to their characters.

    While most of the attractions in the yellow fever tunnels are aimed at adult supernaturals, there are a few hang outs for the child and teenage monsters there, including but not limited to, as the Weird Aeon (an arcade full of retro games from the 80s, along with a number of video games suppressed by TFV like Polybius, Astro Globs, LSD Dream Emulator, Skull and Crossbones, Half Life 3, The WoD MMO (because I'm one of those guys that has the WoD franchise exist in the CofD), Frankenstein: Bread Lust..... and the Battle Toads remake), Cthulhu's Pizza Buffet (Lovecraft themed Chuckie Cheese knock off linked to Weird Aeon and owned by the same stoner ex-TFV agent... don't worry, the animatronic shoggoths haven't gone Five Nights at Freddy's on anybody since the 90s. The place is considered to be the territory of a group of aquatic beast and changeling kids anyway) Third eye books and comics (mostly a normal book and comic shop, but includes a number of sci-fi and superhero works that were suppressed for predicting future events and fantasy and horror works suppressed for revealing too much, popular with the mage and proximi kids due to the large selection of grimores) Pick Me! Pet Store (pet store selling supernatural pets, great place to get a familiar for mage kids, sometimes teenage werewolves will get fucked up and enter the store to play with the talking dogs in Urhan form until they get kicked out by the thrysus that owns it) and the Devil's Toy-box (supernatural toys and games store, the sort of place to get a memento doll for a little sin eater girl's birthday, a copy of Jumangi for a thrysus kid to give to his bully as a prank or a practice weapon for any supernatural kid).

    Next time: Sin eaters and, after that, some stated-up example characters to hold you fine folks over while I figure out the Arisen and Unchained of Savannah.

        Here is the daugther of the Savannah apex that I posted in the Beast Characters thread:

        Makara Tyrant (from my Savannah setting): Normally the potential to become a beast doesn't run in human bloodlines, her father's is one of the exceptions. Supposedly he and his many half-siblings were born devoured, the spawn of an ancient incarnate who reached a state beyond that. The ancient sea serpent's spawn became the apex incarnates of their own hives, all of them linked into their father's primordial empire.

        She on the other hand was devoured after birth, by her father of course, and her horror looks much like his, a loch ness monster type creature that swims through the swamps and rivers of the primordal dream in her father's domain. Her tyrant nature mostly takes the form of being the smartest kid in the room, the one who knows how her peers' schemes will blow up in their faces before they even begin and enjoys asserting her mental superiority over the other local children, and making her owns plans that use them as pawns. That being said she isn't above playing the "spoiled princess of the apex" form of tyrant either. Her main kinships are with some local changeling kids with aquatic kiths and with a boy who.... she actually doesn't know what he is, to tell the truth, except that he is both incredibly dumb, absurdly lucky and somehow never stays dead while never remembering his deaths from their misadventures.

        in human form, she is a biracial 9 year old girl, capable of passing for white if she takes care not to let her Gullah accent slip and uses enough hair products. When she isn't wearing her school uniform, she usually wears the sort of clothes little rich girls usually wear, with subtle accessories drawn from her Gullah heritage.

        Her lair takes the form of a sunken amusement park in brackish water, not unlike if an amusement park in Orlando sank back into the swamp, with chambers made from the various places she's mentally dominated and terrified the other children of Savannah, though the latest chamber comes from the Disney Cruise her father took her on over the summer as a promised reward for straight As last year in school, the ship's counterpart in her lair a capsized wreck on the marsh floor.

            Alright, so here is the bare bones of the sin-eater write up for Savannah, I will add the rest later.

            The Avernian gates in Savannah are all over the place, they exist in the cemeteries of course, but so much of the city is resonant with death that they can be found all over the place, if it weren't for the fact that Savannah has more Sin-eaters than any other city in America, then the ghosts might have over run the coast a long time ago. The Avernian Gates most used by Sin-eaters are in the yellow fever tunnels, far out of sight of th mortal population.

            Savannah's sin-eaters are usually well-off, as mentioned before. The supernatural tourism promotion board arranges for them to have jobs at the various haunted businesses in the city where they get paid to ensure the ghosts bring in tourists, in addition to what ever their "official" position in the business is. The avernain gates are also open more often than in most cites, allowing ghosts and psychopomps out more often, including the swarm...

            Oh yes, the swarm, the Autochthonous depths in Savannah are home to a roving swarm of Psychopomp mosquitoes, presumably an echo of the insects that killed so many in Savannah via yellow fever. The swarm has always been dangerous both inside and outside the underworld, but has been weakening for decades due to the decline of yellow fever in Savannah, though rare outbreaks still occur. However, the recent Zika panic has many local sin-eaters worried, the Zika virus is spread by the same species of mosquito that spreads yellow fever and an outbreak that causes enough death and misery for the infants whose mothers are infected could bring the psycopomps to a level of power beyond their strength in the yellow fever days and put the whole city in danger. TFV has heard the Sin-Eater's worries, but it is not like they can go up to congress and explain that mediums who share their bodies with ancient ghost things told them they have to direct funding to fighting Zika or else Savannah will be eaten by ghost mosquitoes, forcing them and the sin-eaters to get creative.

            I will add the local krewes later, but her is one to hold you over.

            The Ghost Flippers are exactly what you would expect from the name, a krewe of realtors who use their powers to make money restoring and and selling haunted buildings in the historic districts to mortals who want to live in a haunted Savannah house or run a haunted business.

            The members of the Krewe are mostly women, though one of the founders brought her son on board after he made the bargain following an accident he had while helping the cat herders forsaken pack restore an old store they wanted to turn into a cafe. Said son has a pair of pyschopomp dogs, and a bird who follow him around in twilight. He claims their ability to survive outside the underworld is because they were his pets in life and he is their anchor. So far none of the other sin-eaters have challenged him on that claim, partly since the bird is one of the only creatures capable of fighting the swarm with regular success.

                Another example character, this time a changeling.

                Bilge the Johnson: Antiquarian Beast of the Crew of Blackbeard
                They call him bilge because.... well his mien is a rat man and he's part of a pirate crew, duh. He used to be a preservation technician with the Georgia Historic Society (not the secret branch, just the normal majority of the organization), but was taken by Duppy Jonah when the True Fae, posing as a human collector offered to help him unlock the truth behind what happened to Blackbeard's skull in exchange for helping him catalogue his collection of pirate artifacts. He accepted, desperate to prove to his overbearing parents that his obsession the stories of the artifact would pay, that his degree in historical preservation wasn't a waste, that they were wrong and he shouldn't have become a doctor or a lawyer like his brothers, damn it!

                When the job turned out to be more long term than he hoped, he snapped, packed all of Jonah's collection into his cramped room, sealed the door with them and chewed his way through the walls into hedge while Jonah tried to break in, taking a book containing the location of many pirate relics, including the skull, with him back to Savannah. Upon making contact with the freehold, he used the location of the skull to earn the title of Johnson (Crew historian) with the Blackbeard crew, the silver plated skull turned chalice becoming one their most sacred artifacts.

                Blige keeps detailed records of the crew's activities, while also assessing the value of any historic artifacts they come across. Since reclaiming his former life from his fetch, which was surprisingly easy since the fetch had wound up in the hospital right before bilge made his move, he has indulged his rat nature a bit, hoarding artifacts in his quarters in the 8th Sea cove where he and his motley keep their hollow ship, chewing on his bedposts, burrowing under a pile of sheets and junk instead of sleeping in a bed, that sort of thing. His favorite pets as a child were a pair of rats, so maybe that has something to do with it. His indulgence in ratiness is usually seen as a harmless quirk, though it does sometimes get him into trouble, like the current love triangle he somehow got caught in between an equally rat like Larcenist Beast of the court of Calico Jack, who sometimes steals historic artifacts that she needs him to assess the value of, and a                 Namtaru Nemesis who seems to represent the fear of so-called "memetic plague rats" (hate group members, religious radicals, conspiracy nuts, various science deniers, that sort of thing) as she puts it, who likes to help him create terror for him to.harvest glamour from.

                Right now his current project is trying to find out just what the hell is inside the magically sealed antique jewelry box that the sin eaters of the Ghost Flippers Krewe found while helping the Invictus restore an abandoned corner store in the area south of forsycth park. His kith blessing tells him that the box was locked and sealed inside the walls of the store right after Sherman accepted the city's surrender, but it won't show him what's actually in it. Not being stupid, he hired some help to make sure it wasn't something that shouldn't be let out, but while the magi said it was safe, they still couldn't tell him what it was other than that it was a cache of important documents that they couldn't read. Needless to say, the timing of the documents being sealed makes him wonder...

                    For now, here is the Beta of education.

                    Carl Norton: Bone Shadow Irraka of the cat herders pack.

                    Carl is the alpha of the cat herders pack, whose name comes from the local chain of Cat Cafes his wolf-blooded mother owns (and which sometimes double as pay by the day safe house motels for the local colony werecats).

                    A young social studies teacher about to begin his third year in the professions before the first change hit him, Carl was a natural choice to be the Beta of Education when the previous one was fired by the protectorate's alpha for a series of gaffes resulting from their poor leadership. As a result, Carl does his best to make sure all the young Uratha and Wolf Blooded get a fair shot at a decent education, which in his world view includes alternatives to traditional colleges such as trade schools. While his policies on higher education and career readiness are considered great by the various supernaturals in the Jim Willaims PTA and his knowledge of history is second to none, he is shockingly incompetent when it comes to the use of electronic and computerized devices, which means that students in his history class at the Jim Williams school often wind up having to help him resolve the most bizarre tech support issues simply because the school's IT staff refuses to help him anymore.                             here's the first bits of the write-up Unchained of Savannah:

                            The main agency in Savannah is often known as the "Sunday paperboys", a reference to original covers of it's founders, all of them staff and reporters at the two weekly african american newspapers in Savannah. While the agency still has a lot of influence in the papers, their big money maker since the 50s has been the sort of supernatural industrial spycraft that would normally only be found in a certain other roleplaying game. The fact that one of the biggest corporations in Savannah is Titlemax (because how are the Unchained NOT involved in a company criticized for predatory lending) means that the Agency makes even more money with their invention of the soul title loan, which is basically a soul pact in which the pactee can get the title to their soul back if they pay back an amount of cash, goods or services determined at the pact signing to the demon.

                            The main God Machine presence in Savannah is based around Morris Media group and Morris Communications, two separate companies founded by members of the same family, which together own a huge chunk of the travel guide business. Due to the historic district's strict laws regarding construction and renovation, the GM has had no choice but to build it's infrastructure in most of the city around the tourist sector and the Morris family companies and their travel guides are how it draws people to it's fronts, though most of that GM presence in the tourist sector is Logistical infrastructure meant to launder money to the secret GM research projects going on at the Savannah River Site and Savannah River National Laboratory down the river in South Carolina. The fact that this research is itself the lynchpin to pretty much every GM infrastructure in the US dealing with nuclear energy means that the paperboys have made the subversion of the Savannah river site their top long term priority.

                            The GM, not being an idiot, has placed it's concealment and defense infrastructure for the Savannah River infrastructure in places where the various Agencies involved can't make much noise without provoking a heavily armed response from law enforcement, with the Savannah River Site's elimination infrastructure being the Savannah Air National Guard Base at Savannah-Hilton Head international airport. The Georgia Air National Guard unit there has been given forged orders to bomb the Savannah River Site into dust if it is comprimised by "terrorists" though the influence of the messenger angel "Captain O'Reily" has kept them from realizing that those orders are illegal.

                            Next time: more Unchained stuff, including their opinions on the 6-item agenda and how their offspring fair in the Jim Willaims schools.

                                The Unchained support the 6-item agenda in their own odd way, they often propose goals to the supernatural natural tourism promotion board that will screw over tourist businesses linked to the God Machine (the most they can do without endangering their own assets) or support one of the lesser known businesses that the agency owns that make money behind the scenes, one of which is the factory that produces most of Savannah's famous to-go cups (Savannah's historic district being one of the few places in the US where it is legal to have an open container of booze in public (as long as the container is plastic and no more than 16 ounces... which also helps the unchained find drunken marks for soul title loans).

                                In general, the Agency's businesses related to the tourist industry are all behind the scenes ior else support the tourist industry rather than being attractions themselves. The garages that repair the trolleys for the trolley tours are another example, as are many of the hotels. The unchained also have an interest in the river boat casinos, though the Begotten get a big cut of that racket (turns out the Makara Apex doesn't like the Unchained making money on the water of his hive without him, who knew?). The fact that the GM can't damage these unchained fronts without harming it's own logistical infrastructure in the historic district has resulted in a tense sort of mutually assured destruction between the Unchained and the servants of the machine in the historic district, neither wants to fire the first shot that burns it all down.

                                The Deva Corporation left town during the same drama that forced out the Cheiron Group in the 70s. Rumor has it that the Unchained somehow started a sort of shadow war between the two corps (as well as Amalgama LTD and Last Dynasty) by making up a story about a weird supernatural frog from the swamps whose slime could cure certain cancers (nobody (except the unchained themselves) actually remembers what started the fight, but the fake frog one has become popular in the increasingly exaggerated retellings of the story). The fight ended with the assassinations of executives from all four companies by unknown parties and threats by TFV, VASCU and the Georgia Historic Society Secret History Division to have their friends in the IRS and the city government nail all four companies to a wall if they didn't leave Savannah.

                                The Jim Williams schools are one of the few relatively safe places in the world to send an offspring or fractal to get an education, for some reason, the GM doesn't seem to realize that the school even exists, for which some of the unchained think an integrator who vanished following the death of her son back in the 90s is responsible,having somehow uploaded a virus into the machine that keeps it from noticing the school, though the Begotten have their own opinion on that issue. Whatever the case, the offspring have a reputation among the other students as "Weird but useful." the offspring are the sort of students that would appear in an animated kids show as a sort of junior mafia-don... or maybe they're shadier versions of Ron Wesley's older brothers. They often find a niche smuggling any of the many things the other students want to have in school but aren't supposed (Any thing from forged doctor's notes explaining why they won't be at school to cell phones stolen out of the closet full of them that the teacher's confiscated to test answer sheets to (for the teenage students) the sorts of weird recreational drugs (including a few meant to be boost memory during lectures and tests) and pornography that a teenage monster going through the horrors of puberty might want (on flash drives, though sometimes they aren't REALLY porn stashes, the offspring, as the children of supernatural spies, recently developed a trick where they hide documents they don't want their parents and teachers to know about (like the aforementioned test answer sheets... and the blackmail dossiers on a few particularly hated teachers... and the word document full of the student body's theories as to what exactly Billy the head custodian is.... and the list of passwords for the network accounts of certain school admins... among other things), in a hidden partition inside a normally encrypted partition full of porn, if an adult forces the offspring to reveal the password to the encrypted files, they give out the password to the porn stash and the adult assumes that was what they were trying to hide, since it is impossible to know about the hidden partition without entering the real password.

                                So far the parents and such have yet to get wise to the trick (but then again, the unchained could know damn well about that trick but are proud of their children for thinking of it on their own, but even then they wouldn't tell the other Jim Williams parents about it) and it has allowed a fractal known simply as Chad (or was that Brad... Tad, maybe?) to run a out of the way teen party spot in the swamps outside the city. The spot is the wreck of an old riverboat casino from the 1920s or so that a krewe of teen sin eaters stumbled upon back in the 50s. They put the ghost of the passengers and crew to rest (but binding the ghost of the captain to guard from meddling adults) and fixed it up into a suitable hangout. over the decades since, each "manager" of The Coastal Empress (the ship's name) has added new items on to it, a cabal of teen magi in the 60s managed to create a generator and wire it for electricity, a pack of teen wolves in the 80s boosted some TVs and arcade games and put them in, a dhampir manager found an old bootleggers still in a forgotten cabin in the 90s and fixed it up to provide booze (lucky him having a former bootlegger for a father) and a computer geek wizened in the mid 2000s added pirated satellite TV and internet to the place. By the time Chad was named the new manager, it had become the social center of the Jim Williams charter school High school class (attempts by younger siblings enrolled at the Jim Williams charter K-8 class to sneak always resulting in amusing failure.) As a bonus, the gambling equipment still works .

                                story wise, the Coastal empress partly exists to fills the same role that the tavern does in a fantasy RPG , a place for teenage PCs to look for something to do when they aren't taking the STs hints (and thus a place for the ST to make the hints obvious through a supernaturally aware mouthpiece) as well a place for PCs to unwind after a tense story, and of course, a place to indulge in CofD takes on famous teen movie tropes (a role also played by the high school classes in the Jim Williams schools and the tunnel district).

                                The Fractal "manager" Chad is a friendly enough guy and the confusion about his name, should he be asked, has partly to do with the way he back-traced himself out of the Machine's notice by taking the data the machine had on him and cut'n'pasting it into the Machine's files on a guy named Tad from another school that liked to shake him down for his allowance when he was in third grade, which has dealt with both problems quite well. The name Brad came from his stigmata, which makes people around him feel like singing (possibly related to his father's cipher, which seems to involve music and it's role in creating hell), since at one point he got into a fight with some werewolves on the Jim Williams football team (don't ask him what the fight was about, he shuts that line of conversation down fast if it comes up.) and a group of chorus girls watching the fight in the underground school halls decided to make a theme song for him, which would been awesome... except they got his name wrong... and one of the girls was a witch he has been in love with since kindergarten, when she was just a proximi and she still thinks his name is Brad, but for her, he will be Brad

                                        Most of the tunnel entrances have been sealed off to the public. a fountain in forsyth park was built over one of them and another was sealed off very recently when they turned the abandoned (and allegedly haunted) hospital (at least I think it was a hospital, I might be wrong) on the same lot into dorms for a law school. Which is why I made it a supernatural meeting place in my take on Savannah, no mortals to worry about keeping secrets from.
                                        The tunnels play into a lot of Savannah's weird history (like the masonic autopsies to find a cure for yellow fever that are alleged to have taken place there) and any CofD chronicle there should take them into account.
