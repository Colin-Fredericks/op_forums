              If there was a Loyalist court, they would probably be the equivalent to the crime syndicate, as opposed to true members of the Free Holds. They lurk on the pathways of the Hedge, raiding travelers and hollows to appease their Keepers, and running various operations that oppose the prosperity and good nature of the Free Hold itself. They'd be recognized a Court in so far that they're an organized group, but it's not a Court any member of the Free Hold would actually want to interact with.


              Court of Supplicants:

              Emotion: Submission

              Info: Whether forced into service or doing it of their own free will, the Court of Supplicants are Changelings who remain in the service of their Keepers and strive to appease them with their actions. If the rest of the Free Hold is comprised of kings, knights, and upstanding members of society, the Court of Supplicants are the bandits, thieves, murderers and other cast offs who failed to live up to society's expectations. The only rules they play by now are the ancient and unfathomable whims of the True Fae. The Court of Supplicants are not openly welcomed in Free Hold society, and so they choose to live on the outskirts. They lay traps along the paths of the Hedge, ambushing travelers and raiding the surrounding Hollows. If they're lucky, their victims are killed in the initial attack. If they're less fortunate, they could find themselves chained and dragged off to the slave market.

              Although they are outcasts, the Supplicants have one significant advantage over the rest of the Free Holds; they can recruit new members at will! The other Courts must wait for a Changeling to escape the Hedge and then vie against each other for their membership. The Supplicants, on the other hand, simply need to grab a human and drag them back to their Keeper for a bit of brainwashing.

              This ability to recruit members in such a way ensures the Supplicants can easily replenish their fallen numbers, and remain a constant threat, despite the efforts of the Free Holds to clear them out.

              If the Supplicants do have anything that resembles a hierarchy structure, it is not publicly known to the rest of the Free Holds. Like a crime syndicate, they put on a front of being proud and upstanding members of society. Donating to charity and all that crap, but when they are away from prying eyes, they conduct their true business of illegal activities. Silencing witnesses, extorting the vulnerable for protection money, selling cursed foods and equipment. All dedicated to the purpose of breaking your will to fight back. They feed on submission, and they will do whatever they can get away with to get the point across that you can't beat them.

                          So, they may be targeted. I still doesn't lend itself to a Court. Loyalists generally won't ally themselves with loyalists of rival fae; hells, the two Loyalists could be set up to be rivals just as much as your example of the Huntsman versus Loyalist. Hells, two Huntsmen from the same True Fae could be subject to the same just as easily. Everything is pointing away from working together as a Court.

                          From a game design standpoint, what is the purpose of a Loyalist Court system? What does it do? Even more relevantly, if a Court did exist, why should the PCs care? Is it just an added layer of mechanical complexity that's effectively invisible to the player characters? How does it affect game play or the story told during each session?

                                                          I much prefer the idea of making it a Seeming - as it's the form of your "escape". i.e. all the Seemings reflect how you changed yourself to survive and escape. The Loyalist Seeming is pretty much Stockholm Syndrome, you changed yourself into a loving extension of your master, and "escaped" at their earned behest.

                                                          Appearance: Kith traits are exaggerated. This can easily lead the assumption a Windwing is a Beast, a Fireheart is an Elemental, etc. You fully embraced and relish what your master made you.

                                                          Blessing: Gain Clarity when you complete a task for your master.

                                                          Curse: Clarity Breaking-point from disobedience or failing a specific ordered task in a way that it can no longer be completed (someone you were meant to retrieve dies, you had a time frame that ran out, etc).

                                                          Seeming Contracts ideas: Like other Seeming Contract Clauses these would be theoretically learnable by others, HOWEVER the theoretical non-Loyalists who somehow found a teacher would lose a point of Clarity every time they use one.

                                                          - Fetch related powers: Sensing, Summoning, Commanding, etc Fetches. Even channeling Echoes (this would allow them to adopt a "False Mien" as need be to fake a specific Seeming); or possibly even creating new Fetches of themselves.

                                                          - Combine the concepts from the First Edition Contract of Thorns & Bramble with the Contract of Lucidity. You can use the thorns of the Hedge to strip away a target's Clarity/Integrity/etc. Possibly even imposing Soullessnes or Thrall Conditions.
