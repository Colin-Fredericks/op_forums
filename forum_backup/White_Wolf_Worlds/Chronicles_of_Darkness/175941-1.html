                Further observations:

                Characters with Unseen Sense: God Machine can feel and angel falling. (rulebook p. 39)

                Demons sometimes have to spoof characters with Unseen Sense: God Machine. (implied on rulebook p. 119)

                Everyone with a sense for the God-Machine's workings can under close inspection tell there is something fundamentally different about gadgets. (rulebook p. 187)

                Even demons with "Sense the Angelic" can only detect Numina, and not the angels themselves. (rulebook p. 198)
                Sensing angels is not mentioned in the Unseen Sense write-up of demonblooded. (heirs to hell p. 33)

                Conclusion:

                Based on these observations (and my previous one), I rule that Stigmatics cannot detect angels, only their numina. If it is necessary for the plot, you could always use the visions a Stigmatic suffers to make him find an angel. Also the game seems to be in conflict on whether character's with Unseen Sense: Godmachine can detect demons. Maybe Stigmatics can only detect Demons under specific conditions?

                                                You're absolutely right. Because while they're hacking that infrastructure, they get goosebumps when it's caretaker gets home.

                                                The Merit normally allows an individual an extra-sensory perception on a supernatural entity. (Example: Unseen Sense: Werewolves allows you to sense nearby werewolves.) However, it notes that in addition to the affects of the merit that are normally permitted, you can see normally hidden infrastructure, identify infrastructure and see the gears of the God-Machine. This note is in addition to the affects of the merit as normally given. So, the question is: What are you sensing?

                                                I can understand the argument for a demon, they've been disconnected, or a cryptid because they're not necessarily part of the Machine (on an intentional level). But an Angel is an integral part of the Machine - indeed, Angels are often infrastructure themselves - so it seems fairly clear then that Unseen Sense: God-Machine would allow you to sense nearby angels. Do remember that the merit does not allow you such accuracy as to necessarily pinpoint anything that might be hiding itself - such as with the Numina "Mortal Mask".

                                                Edit: Demon-blooded can use this merit to actually identify details of an infrastructure outright such as the Linchpin. Normal stigmatic characters are at the mercy of the storyteller as to what triggers it (though given the Stigmatic in "How an Angel Dies" constantly breaks the linchpin of the floating bridge infrastructure, it seems she must get the heebiejeebies from it) and seeing past Concealment Infrastructure doesn't necessarily mean you can immediately identify it as something bizarre (it might still be disguised as a ordinary building, just one that is invisible to normal human eyes). However, again, angels are infrastructure.
