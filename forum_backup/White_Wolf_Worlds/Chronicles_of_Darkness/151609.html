      Characters can absolutely take reflexive actions during other character's actions. Otherwise things like Celerity's interrupt ability would be worthless, and the Demonic Form Teleportation feature wouldn't need to specify that it can only be used during your action.

      However, an opponent's action does not constitute the start of a new turn. Indeed that the thinking about turns is completely off. It helps not to think of turns as individual characters taking actions in orderly an orderly sequential manner, then waiting for other people to take theirs. This isn't Final Fantasy or Disgaea. Everyone is acting simultaneously, in one flowing blur of motion. The initiative order is merely an abstraction for the sake of ease.

                                                          "Round" is a common colloquial term for a full initiative rotation of actions taken in combat, colloquially referred to as "turns". The nWoD rules use the term "turn" to mean the former, and "action" to mean the latter, but they describe the same concepts. The important thing to remember is that "per turn" limits apply to the full rotation of combat actions, not to each individual action within that rotation. Call it what you like, just know what the game means when it uses the word "turn".
