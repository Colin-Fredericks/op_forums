    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
    You can give the animal a message and command it to bring it to X, though. Or use Special Message beforehand on the animal. It just seems... redundant.
    I don't know how to tell you this, but "give a coded message to a living being that can then seek out a target" is not, in fact, redundant with "tell an animal to do a thing it would normally do" or "hide a message in a piece of art," considering that it combines bits of at least three different other Embeds to accomplish the specific effect of "use an animal to deliver a message."

    Special Message isn't going to work on an animal you haven't marked or trained in some way and Animal Communication isn't going to give an animal any ability to find a target without you telling it where to go. Animal Messenger sends an otherwise normal animal to a target you need no special awareness of to convey a message through non-linguistic means. That is the specific thing that is does, and it does it without needing to buy another Embed or do the legwork needed to prepare the vector of delivery to reach its target.

                    Necro'ing this thread because it's the first time I've created Ciphers and I'd appreciate feedback on my choices.

                    For Mr. List, a Tempter with a narrow vision of Hell:

                    Cipher: Bloom where you're planted.
                    The Psychopomp Jevnaiah Fell when he found a flaw in the God-Machine's design, and spent many years as a Saboteur determined to destroy the false god, root and branch. That changed when his Ring took down a piece of Infrastructure hidden inside an apartment complex; rather than destroy the buildings, List decided to suborn them, and establish a much more private Hell just for himself. He changed his agenda to Tempter, and his Interlocks are themed around protecting himself from outsiders at any cost.

                    Alibi + Lucky Break = Just Missed Him
                    Composure + Occult
                    List has at least two Covers, the manager of the apartment complex (for business) and the landscaper (for pleasure). When this Interlock is active, anyone attempting to locate one of his inactive Covers through mundane means encounters various random-seeming setbacks - they lose a business card, mistype an email address, show up at the office just when everyone's out for lunch, etc. The number of such setbacks equals the number of successes on the activation roll, at which point the Interlock ends and mundane investigation proceeds normally.

                    If someone is investigating List's Covers indirectly, the Interlock adds to the number of successes they need to accumulate on an extended action before List is compromised.

                    Supernatural means of locating a Cover are subject to a Clash of Wills while this Interlock is active.

                    Lucky Break + Special Someone = Come Into My Parlor
                    Manipulation + Empathy
                    While Special Someone allows a Demon to seek out a particular individual, this Interlock allows List to ensure that individual comes to him. The target of the Interlock experiences a series of random-seeming setbacks, coincidences and strokes of luck that result in their meeting Mr. List within [10-Primum] days of activating this Interlock; List doesn't have foreknowledge of where or when they'll meet, but he immediately recognizes the target of the Interlock on sight. (On an exceptional success, List can specify where the meeting will occur, but the timing is still open.)

                    Special Someone + Interference = Fall Guy
                    Wits + Subterfuge, 1 Aether
                    Whenever Mr. List loses Cover, he can redirect the apparent source of the disturbance to another Demon withing [Primum] miles of his location. That demon automatically gains whatever Condition List would have gained from the failed compromise roll, on top of any current Cover-related Conditions they may have. If this Interlock fails, however, List gains the Blackballed Condition.

                    Interference + Alibi = Where Are You Going, Where Have You Been?
                    Manipulation + Socialize, 1 Aether
                    This Interlock allows List to lay false trails by projecting a double of himself to multiple locations simultaneously. These projections can't take complex actions, and don't even last a whole scene, but they create a confused trail for anyone trying to pin down his actual whereabouts. Using this Interlock is an automatic compromise roll.

                                    Okay, so here is my very first Cypher and it's first two Interlocks for an NPC in my campaign of Demon. If the Great Ones of this forum could judge it, it'd help tremendously for the other five demons I'm working on - discouting the players !

                                    La Reina - A Destroyer who fell in love with her target because of the purity of her convictions.


                                    Cypher :The Righteous will inherit the Earth
                                    The Destroyer known as La Reina Fell when she was tasked with killing a woman fighting for the rights of the downtrodden. She could not accept that the God-Machine would want such a being to be destroyed and she instead ended marrying her target. While a Saboteur and a Destroyer, she realized that her true calling wasn't to annihilate stuff but to help the deserving reach their full potential and change the world for the betetr, thwarting the Machine in the process. She has so far unlocked three Keys and two Interlocks.

                                    Interlock 1 - Aura of the Righteous (Cool heads prevail + Special Someone)
                                    Manipulation + Empathy; Instant action

                                    The Demon designate one person she can sees, which becomes a Righteous One; for as many hours as Success on the roll the Righteous has the same effect as a successful roll of Cool heads prevail on the persons near the Righteous. On an exceptionnal success the effects last longer and the peoples near the Righteous get the Charmed Condition.

                                    Interlock 2 - Tools of Redemption (Special Someone + Right tools, Right Jobs)
                                    Wits + Craft - Supernatural Tolerance ; Instant action

                                    The Demon choose a person she can see; the target gets a +3 bonus on all her actions for the scene. On an exceptionnal success, the bonus is +5.



                                    As I said, it's my first time designing those and I'd love to get those rights before toying with the player's !
