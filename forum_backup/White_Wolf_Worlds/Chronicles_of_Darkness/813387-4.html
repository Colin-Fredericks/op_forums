                                        Alright, Manus and I have done some major work on the Tenno and I am satisfied enough to provide much of what was done. Unmarked Spoilers below.









                                        Orbiter
                                        Tenno require extensive facilities to maintain to their equipment, most especially their Warframes.
                                        To this end, the Orokin granted the Tenno Orbiters, specialized vessels to act as mobile resupply bases. Outfitted with the best Void Cloaks, Orbiters are something between ships and personal pocket dimensions for their sole occupant.
                                        Orbiters possess among other things, matter assembly plants, genetic foundries, extensive databanks able to digitally store an entire armory’s worth of mods and gear, and telecommunications able to access nearly the Origin System’s main network ~ all of this controlled by artificial intelligences called a Cephalon, a loyal (if sometimes buggy) servitor.
                                        Generally speaking an Orbiter tries to stay within hundreds of thousands of miles of its Tenno in the field. Its specialized cloaking device is such that it completely obscures the Orbiter from a number of means of detection including scanners, scrying, and outright magic.
                                        Due to the nature of the Orbiter, many spells or supernatural abilities designed to affect an inhabitant such as the Tenno himself outright fail unless the caster is already inside the Orbiter itself, as the Void Cloak.

                                        That is to say, the Orbiter is not invulnerable. Capital ship weapons will destroy it no problem, it’s just finding the thing that’s the problem.

                                        Tenno Template
                                        Mastery - While all Tenno are touched by the Void, not all Tenno manifest this power in the same way or have full control over it. Tenno divide themselves into ranks of understanding and power based on how much they have experienced and how much they have access to the Void.
                                        Features:
                                        Attribute/Skill cap increases after 5.
                                        No Mana Meter. Energy capacity is determined by Warframe. The Tenno itself does not bother keeping track of personal energy since they are all conduits of Void energy
                                        Void blast damage. All Tenno may launch a beam of destructive Void energy from their hands, the damage determined by Mastery.
                                        Downsides?
                                        Tenno are mortal, with all the problems that follow.




                                        Focus - The Tenno fought the Old War long and hard, long enough to permanently alter the way their own power could be shaped to specialize to different ends. This is called a Focus, a special and personal understanding of power and how to use it so ingrained into the Tenno, it cannot be changed.
                                        A Tenno awakening from Cryosleep has a single Focus which bestows a single Focus Power. This power is always available to a Tenno and has no energy cost, but they may only use it once per “Story” representing the uniqueness of this gift.
                                        The 5 Tenno Focus Schools are:
                                        Madurai - “Engage The Enemy” - Phoenix Gaze
                                        Zenurik - “Dominate The Enemy” - Void Pulse
                                        Uniaru - “Outlast The Enemy” - Basilisk Flare
                                        Vazarin - “Counter The Enemy” - Mending Tides
                                        Naramon - “Know The Enemy” Mind Spike


                                        Syndicate - Each Tenno has the choice of joining an organization of like minded individuals, all in the pursuit of their goals and reasons, even though such groups are not completely comprised of Tenno. Still, Syndicates provide Tenno with resources and material in the pursuit of their state goals.
                                        Joining a Syndicate grants the Tenno access to the Syndicate’s special weapons and items. Leaving a Syndicate causes the Tenno to lose access to these items.
                                        Six Syndicates are available to the Tenno:
                                        Steel Meridian -
                                        Perrin Sequence -
                                        Arbiters of Hexis -
                                        New Loka -
                                        Cephalon Suda -
                                        Red Veil -






                                        Warframes - Tenno do not often send themselves out in the field. For the most part, they operate Warframes remotely from their Orbiters, though there are occasions where they wear their suits into battle, granting them far more power, perhaps near limitless power… at the cost of mortality.

                                        Should a Warframe be destroyed, the Tenno may recall it back to their Orbiters for repairs.



                                        Operation Rules -
                                        When a Tenno operates a Warframe, the Warframe’s physical statistics are used in place of the Tenno’s even when it would be a benefit. Each Warframe comes with the 3 basic Attributes: Strength, Dexterity, and Stamina and is despite being an “armor” is treated as fully living creature possessing Wounds and Health levels as a normal person.
                                        Warframes may not be equipped with armor, though they all possess a not so insignificant amount of shielding and armor of their own.
                                        Warframes also possess Abilities, usually 4 of them. Some have more. All Abilities cost Energy to run, the amount of which could be stored is determined by the Warframe. Also, Warframes all possess a signature skill used to used the Abilities of a given Warframe. All abilities that require dice rolls involve Mastery + Warframe Rank + this Key Skill.

                                        Each Warframe has a Rank, going from 1 to 5. This determines the overall statistics and abilities of a Warframe. As the Warframe’s rank increases, these statistics and abilities either unlock or increase. All Warframes when first unlocked begin at Rank 1 and possess their first ability. In each Warframe’s entry, it tells you what increasing ranks bestows onto you.
                                        Special Warframe Abilities:
                                        Sword Alone: So long as a Warframe is equipped with a melee weapon and is forgoing ranged combat in order to devote more time and attention to melee, the Tenno may apply his full Defense versus firearms attacks, effectively parrying bullets.
                                        Mobility: Warframes are able to leap and travel in ways that a normal human could not, seemingly the result of gravity manipulation. Tenno may run upwards, horizontally, or cling to walls. They may also perform multiply the distances they leap by 5.

                                        Transference -
                                        Most Tenno operate their Warframes via transference, the process of remotely operating the Warframe via a remote control rig in the Orbiter. This allows the Warframe to be used as if the Warframe was his own body.
                                        However, this means that Void Energy does not flow as quickly to the Warframe as it would otherwise.
                                        Using Transference causes the Warframe to start with 0 Energy in its reserve, rising by 1 Energy for every hour the Warframe is operated in this manner for the first 24 hours. Past this point, the Warframe needs to be recalled as the connection between Operator and Warframe is hard to maintain and inefficient. After 24 hours, the Warframe gains only 1 Energy per day.
                                        The main advantage to transference is that the Operator’s life is never in danger, and should the Warframe be lost, another may be made to replace it or the old one can be salvaged.
                                        Suitless -
                                        Tenno are heavily discouraged by their “Mother” to never leave the Orbiters, yet some do, for reasons of their own.
                                        A Suitless Tenno is little more than a human with some Void abilities inherent to their nature.
                                        Should a Suitless Tenno need to defend himself, he is always free to use a Voidblast attack. A Voidblast is a simple roll of Willpower + Mastery which on a success deals Aggravated damage equal to Mastery +3
                                        Direct Use -
                                        A few Tenno choose to operate the Warframes regularly, but most Tenno who don a Warframe do so out of desperation. Using a Warframe in this manner is hazardous and risky, but the rewards are by far the greatest.
                                        A Tenno in a Warframe provides near limitless Void Energy to the Warframe, providing 1 Energy per minute.
                                        A Tenno may still use his Voidblast attack whenever he wishes.






                                        Excalibur
                                        Strength 4. Dexterity 4. Stamina 4
                                        Armor 3/3. Shields 5
                                        Size 5
                                        Health 9
                                        Speed Factor 7 (above human)
                                        Energy 10
                                        Abilities: Key Skill - Weaponry

                                        Ranks:
                                        2: Radial Blind, +1 Strength, + 1 Dexterity, +1 Stamina,
                                        3: Radial Javelin, + 1/1 Armor, + 1 Shield, +1 Energy
                                        4: +1 Strength, +1 Dexterity, +1 Stamina, +2 Energy
                                        5: Exalted Blade, +1/1 Armor, +1 Shield, +2 Energy


                                        Slash Dash
                                        This Ability causes Excalibur to dash between enemies while slashing them to pieces.
                                        Cost: 1 Energy
                                        Dice Pool: None
                                        Action: Instant
                                        Duration: Instant
                                        This Ability causes Excalibur to dash forward in a straight line of 8 meters. Excalibur makes a single attack roll versus the enemy with lowest Defense. This attack does damage to all targets in Excalibur’s path.


                                        Radial Blind
                                        This Ability creates a quick flash of light that blinds nearby enemies.
                                        Cost: 2 Energy
                                        Dice Pool: Rank + Mastery + Weaponry
                                        Action: Reflexive
                                        Duration: Scene
                                        Roll Results:
                                        Dramatic Failure: Something malfunctions and Excalibur takes 1 bashing.
                                        Failure: The flash of light is not bright enough.
                                        Success: Excalibur emits a bright flash of light, imposing the Stunned Tilt on all enemies within 12 meters. This doesn’t work on enemies that do not have eyes.
                                        Exceptional Success: The Ability also imposes the Blinded (Both eyes) Tilt for the remainder of the scene.


                                        Radial Javelin
                                        This Ability creates a radial explosion of javelins that impale nearby enemies. An enemy that can apply Defense against firearms can also apply Defense against Radial Javelin.
                                        Cost: 3 Energy
                                        Dice Pool: Rank + Mastery + Weaponry - Defense if applicable
                                        Action: Instant
                                        Duration: Instant
                                        Roll Results:
                                        Dramatic Failure: (No idea)
                                        Failure: Nothing happens.
                                        Success: This Ability is an attack against all enemies within 15 meters that deals (Rank)L damage.
                                        Exceptional Success: The attack also imposes the Stunned tilt.

                                        Exalted Blade
                                        This Ability summons a sword made of pure light.
                                        Cost: 1 Energy per turn
                                        Dice Pool: None
                                        Action: Reflexive
                                        Duration:Indefinite
                                        While this Ability is active the Tenno gains a (Rank)L weapon with a -0 Initiative Modifier. Whenever Excalibur makes an attack against a target , any enemies behind that target in a line of up to 15 feet take (attack successes + Weapon modifier - number of targets) lethal damage in the form of a wave of light.

                                                    Prometheans
                                                    It is perhaps the greatest irony the universe had ever know that the Prometheans were never considered by the Orokin to be threats or abominations. For reasons that modern dwellers of the Origin system cannot seem to fathom, the creatures that were made to be effigies of humankind were never selected for extermination or mass roundup as all the others have.
                                                    Whenever a Promethean was encountered, it was often advised to either leave the creatures alone and vacate the area to prevent being influenced by the Disquiet that affects them… atleast, so long as Firestorms and serious detrimental effects to the environment were not observed. They did not even seem to care for the transformative powers of the Divine Fire, viewing their own technological wonders: cloning, genetic engineering, robotics, and augmentation as superior.

                                                    The creatures were simply left to roam the spheres of Orokin influence, unbothered, unplastered. Perhaps the Orokin pitied them and decided to be merciful. Perhaps they saw no point to bothering with an exotic form of life that was mostly a threat to itself more than anything else. Or maybe, some Prometheans hoped: the Orokin were willing to accept the New Dawn.
                                                    When Orokin empire died, the Prometheans did not even notice their passing.


                                                    Wanderers
                                                    The Prometheans of today are the creatures that lurked in the darkness that have changed the least. They still wander, avoiding laying down roots in hopes of something greater: the only difference is that they are now unbound from a single world and given more than enough space to avoid humans whether they be in some deserted rock.
                                                    Still, the main downside to being away from … everything is that the Azoth that burns in a Promethean has less place to bleed off excess energies and disperse to. As a general rule: Wastelands and Firestorms take 4 times less time to on Spaceships or asteroids.








                                                    Prometheans in the Origin Systeml
                                                    Many Prometheans still seek the New Dawn. Yet, the question of humanity is even more muddled now than it ever was ever since Man could live for centuries and be born from a test tube.

                                                    Grineer: The most likely producers of modern Demiurges today are Grineer scientists who seek to desperately cure their kind’s genetic weakness and frailties. Instead of using metal and cut up corpses, these mad scientists spend inordinate amounts of time trying to “perfect” a single clone by shifting the forming biomass. Seen as heretical by the majority of Grineer.
                                                    Corpus: The businessmen do not like dealing with the Prometheans. Often, they are dismissive and just send them away from their installations as soon as they could. Perhaps an insight as to how the Orokin viewed them.

                                                    Infested: As far as the Prometheans are concerned, these abborations are Pandorans that hunger for everything and will act as accordingly. Strangely, the Technocyte nano-virus that the Infested utilize to convert flesh and metal into more of itself do not affect a functioning Promethean: perhaps due to Azoth.
                                                    Tenno: The Prometheans rarely meet Tenno. But when they do, it is with awe and curiosity.

                                                        Changeling
                                                        To the Lost, the Orokin were a mixed blessing.
                                                        They regarded Changelings as second citizens in their empire, but they were among the few people the Lost believe who could prevent the True Fae from abducting more ever again. Just because humankind reached the stars and became near immortal, that did not stop the monsters from abducting them. No, the Fey came along with them, hitching rides and openning new doorways beyond the Homeworld’s gravity well.

                                                        During their Empire, new Changelings were seldomly seen, with the numbers growing fewer with each generation as technology improved. It was thought that the golden clad humans would have eventually isolated Arcadia, barring all doors to it forever more.
                                                        Hope was destroyed. The Sentients rose, the Tenno rose to prominence, the Orokin fell. It was like listening to a story, about how Precursors would be destroyed by their own creations, letting those who remained to struggle out on the mud.
                                                        Without the Orokin to keep them away, the Keepers returned to find a System vulnerable and unknowing of their presentations. Entire ships or even moons would be abducted… and no one would even notice due to all the chaos that happened in the Fall.


                                                        Eternal Tales
                                                        Most Changelings today were born or have lived through the worst of the Orokin’s Fall, witnessing first hand the Rise of the Grineer and Corpus to practical empires. Compared to then, the constant, almost orderly power struggle seems almost relaxing when its competition was dozens of factions all competing for the ultimate prize.
                                                        With the advent of longevity treatments, a time difference of fifty years, though still significant, isn’t “major” as compared to how it was before. Often returned Changelings find their homes little changed… especially since it’s so rare for Fetches to be made to ruin them.





                                                        Changelings in the Origin Systeml
                                                        Changelings are forced to face their masters even more.The True Fae don’t even need to make fetches to escape unnoticed. Entire ships could go missing in the Orokin system and no one would bat an eye, thinking some other menace taken them.

                                                        Grineer: There is very little chance of a Changeling emerging from the Grineer. Just as well, there’s little Grineer do involving Changelings, their detection technology only reveals normal citizens.
                                                        Corpus: Almost as bad as the True Fae, as the Changelings believe that the average Corpus would sell out their own crewmen to the Slavers for quick credits. This does have some backing.
                                                        Infested: The average Changeling loathes these creatures, veiwing being mutated by them as yet another abuse to their souls… one they could never return from like last time.
                                                        Tenno: Those that know of the Betrayal look at Tenno with almost loathing, for they may not have taken if the Orokin still lived.
