    Idea for a Fan Gameline: Inspired by Invasion of the Body Snatchers?

    08-24-2016, 03:37 PM
    So one of my greatest fears is that I'm a big fat poser: that I don't ever bother thinking for myself, and I just regurgitate opinions fed to me by people that I respect.

    Body Snatchers are also one of the few recognizable horror tropes that aren't well-served by any of the extant gamelines (nor even the upcoming Deviant).

    I wanted to combine these two elements and try my hand at writing my own gameline: here's what I've mused thus far, and wondered if anybody might offer some advice on either making a gameline or the theme, inspiration, or anything in particular.

    Sources of Inspiration
    John Carpenter's "The Thing"
    The "Animorphs" book series
    "Invasion of the Body Snatchers"
    The "Prototype" video game series
    "The Matrix: Reloaded and Revolutions" (think Agent Smith in those films specifically)

    The Player Characters

    In this game, each player plays a linked network of human beings who have had their minds partially suborned by the PC. As the Power Stat goes up, the PC can bring more people into their network and maintain control at larger distances. Obviously gonna have to think up some rules to make controlling multiple humans not a headache ruleswise. Right now I'm thinking of some sort of rule that makes keeping your controlled targets too close to each other, or maybe making it so that you have to keep your attention so divided among the Controlled that being a one-woman onslaught penalty is impossible. I dunno.

    Anyway, the big question here is: Do you think I could flesh this out enough to make them a gameline? Or are these best expressed as an occasional NPC, perhaps designed with the Horror rules?

          Originally posted by atamajakki View Post
          Demons can already hollow out your loved ones and live their lives as Covers before revealing the,selves to be biomechanical alien horrors, and the Claimed and Hosts are both major body-snatching antagonists in Werewolf. Do none of those options so what you're looking for?
          To a certain degree, but the bigger scary theme I'm going for is "Are you who you think you are?" Each time a PC adds another person to their "network", they add that person's distinctiveness to their own, and the question of who is "the character" starts getting fuzzy. Especially if, say, the "prime" node gets killed and suddenly the PC's physical identity is lost.

          The Integrity stat would be something like "Identity" and be a measure of how secure the PC is in their own agency and being. Higher would mean that they feel very in-control, but that might come at the expense of the identity of the other members of the "network". I was also thinking of some sort of mechanic wherein the Virtue/Vice could be changed in response to certain breaking points, representing how the nodes are trying to re-assert their identities.

                      Might I give an idea? I know it may sound weird, but it's something I've wanted to see in the WoD for quite awhile. Although I lacked the inspiration to make it myself. =w="

                      The idea is basically Parasites/Symbiote, something like Venom and the Parasites in Skullgirls. To be more accurate, the main idea was mostly about the parasites of Skullgirls. In Skullgirls, parasites are creatures who bond to a single Host and augment/empower their host. Despite the name, they actually tend to form a mutualistic relationship with their hosts, and Parasites are actually sapient and have their own personalities. Some of them can even detach from their hosts and act remotely, being known as Remote Parasites. In Skullgirls, the Parasites seem to take the form of different body parts they replace. On one, her hair is a parasite with a face on the back and the hair can serve as tentacles. On another, the parasite has actually replaced the Host's skeleton and keeps a few of the organs intact for the host, replacing everything else with blood.

                      http://skullgirls.wikia.com/wiki/Parasite

                      In the Skullgirls ancient past, they were known as Theons and their hosts were known as "Avatars". Perhaps this could be adapted as "Avatar: The Parasite"?

                      Pardon, giving my musings and ideas on the concept, sorry if I missed the point in some way.^^"
