    Ichorous Symbiote [Homebrew mini-Template]

    05-02-2016, 01:07 AM
    Basically, a mini-template based off the Symbiotes from Marvel comics (e.g. Venom, Carnage). This is the rough draft, but I thought you guys might like to take a look.
    Ichorous Symbiote (●-●●●●●)

    Maybe it fell from the sky in a meteorite. Maybe you got it in some crooked deal with a creepy with yellow eyes. Maybe you made it from the blood of an innocent. Whatever happened, this thing has bonded to your skin, making you a deadly weapon, at a terrible cost. (“Ichorous Symbiote” and “Symbiote” are used interchangeably unless otherwise noted).
    Benefits:
    • You gain a pool of Ichor equal to your Size + Symbiote Rating. Each day, you lose a point of Ichor to feed your Symbiote. You can spend Ichor to activate Symbiote Merits
    • You have a Supernatural Tolerance equal to your dots in this Merit.
    • You gain climb walls at full Speed without penalty or any need to roll.

    Drawbacks:
    • If you are out of Ichor for 24 hours, you take resistant Lethal damage equal to your Symbiote rating, and continue to take damage every 24 hours you spend without Ichor.
    • The only way to regain Ichor for most Symbiotes is to kill and eat. By consuming a fresh kill (it must be raw, and they had to die by your actions in the same scene), you can regain Ichor. Animals of size 2 or greater only replenish 1 Ichor (smaller animals replenish none), and humans replenish 1 per Size rating. This is obviously a Breaking point for most characters.
    • You become a target for hosts of other Symbiotes. Symbiotes can detect the immediate presence of other Symbiotes; Symbiote hosts add their Ichorous Symbiote rating to their Size for determining the amount of Ichor they provide.
    • Symbiotes are sensitive, and have certain Banes. Often, they are things like fire, extremely bright light, specific loud noises, or other similarly energetic things. When attacked with its Bane, a Symbiote gains the Stunned Tilt. At 1 or 2 Dots, you have one bane, and gain another Bane at 3 and 5 dots respectively.
    • Finally, the Symbiote itself is sentient, and wants to feed on any meals it can find. At any given time when an opportunity for an easy meal presents itself, the host’s player can hand over control of their character to the Storyteller vis-a-vis the Symbiote in exchange for a Beat, as if they had chosen to turn a failure into a Dramatic Failure.

    Sidebar: Supernaturals and Ichor
    So, I killed a werewolf and decided to eat him. What now?
    By default, Supernatural beings offer no Ichor beyond what they offer from their Size. However, you could make a good argument for some offering more. I won’t make any hard rules for what splats do what, but as a good guideline, add the critter’s Supernatural Tolerance to its Size for determining Ichor. You might rule that certain splats offer reduced or no Ichor, or add certain rider effects. Maybe Vampires aren’t “alive” and killing them doesn’t count. Maybe feeding on a Promethean just burns you from the inside with Pyros instead of feeding you. Just go with whatever seems appropriate.


    Blood Hound (Symbiote ●)

    You can smell blood with uncanny accuracy, and can even distinguish between individuals, provided you’ve smelled their blood before. This is invaluable when hunting witnesses.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●
    Benefit: You can track blood by smell, and identify a person by the scent of their blood. Doing either is a Perception roll, with different modifiers:
    Tracking
    Minutes old +3
    Hours old +1
    Days old -1
    Weeks old -1 per week
    > 1 Month old -3
    Large pool (> 2 Lethal Damage worth) +2
    Huge mess (> 5 Lethal Damage worth) +3, 9 again
    Identification
    Distracting scents (perfume, etc.) -1 to -3
    Tasted blood +3
    Inflicted past Wound (any amount of Lethal damage) +1
    Chemical changes (medication, poison, etc.) -1 to -2

    Edible Knowledge (Symbiote, ●)

    You can retain more than just Ichor from feedings, holding a trace of your victims’ knowledge for a time.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●
    Benefit: When you feed from a person, you can select 1 Skill the victim had rated at at least 2 dots. You don’t suffer Untrained penalties for that skill for the rest of the Chapter. If the victim has no 2-dot skills that you lack dots in, you instead gain one of your victims’ specialties in a skill you have dots in for the rest of the Chapter. You can “lock in” the Skill either gaining a dot in the untrained skill or keeping the new Specialty by spending Experiences as normal, but you don’t need to waste time training or practicing.
    Drawback: You also absorb some personal memories and psychological quirks. For the rest of the Chapter, you suffer one Persistent mental or emotional Condition the victim was suffering, and replace one of your Aspirations with one of theirs at random. If this Aspiration is opposed to ones you already have, you suffer a Breaking Point (you must gain the Fugue Condition on Dramatic Failure).

    Frenzy (Symbiote, ●●●)

    You can overcome the Symbiote’s innate terror of its Bane and lash out at it.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●●
    Benefits: When you are confronted with your Bane, you can spend 1 Willpower or Ichor to roll Resolve + Symbiote rating. If you succeed, you do not suffer the Stunned tilt, and instead gain the Enraged tilt. You can choose to end the Tilt freely in exchange for 9-again on an attack against the source of your Bane.

    Hide Visage (Symbiote, ●, ●●●, or ●●●●●)

    Your Symbiote can hide inside your body, coming out when its needing, but letting you blend in otherwise. With more work, it can help you hide.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●
    Benefits: You can choose to hide your Symbiote, regaining the appearance of a normal human. I may hide in your bones, change into clothing, or become evil tattoos, but it’s not obvious to those unaware of Symbiotes. A hidden Symbiote can be brought out in an Instant Action. Those knowledgeable about Symbiotes can roll Wits + Occult to peg you as a host, but otherwise, you can hide your true nature from normals fairly easily. At ●●●, you can also change your appearance for a Scene, spending Ichor to add your Symbiote rating to Stealth rolls. At ●●●●●, you can hide from senses beyond normal human senses, including technological scans and supernatural senses (including the Symbiote's ability to sense other Symbiotes). Any directed attempt to detect the Symbiote supernaturally invokes a Clash of Wills, rolled against your Subterfuge + Symbiote rating
    Drawback: While your Symbiote is hidden, you can’t use any Symbiote merits except Blood Hound and Frenzy. You are still susceptible to your Bane.


    Mend Flesh (Symbiote, ●, ●●● or ●●●●●)
    Your Symbiote is smart enough to protect its host. You can heal at incredible rates, provided you're not too hungry.
    Prerequisites: Ichorous Symbiote ●, ●●● and ●●●●● respectively.
    Benefits: You can spend 1 Ichor reflexively to heal Bashing damage up to your Symbiote Rating, or 1 Lethal damage. At ●●●, you can heal up to 2 Lethal damage, and at ●●●●●, you can heal all Bashing damage or Lethal damage up to your Symbiote rating.


    Power Thief (Symbiote, ●●●●)

    Your Symbiote has become more mutable than most, allowing you to steal the powers of your victims temporarily.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●●●●●, Edible Knowledge
    Benefit: When you feed on a character with a Supernatural Merit of up to 3 Dots, you can choose to spend 2 Ichor to gain the benefits of that Merit for the rest of the Chapter. You don’t need to meet any of the requirements unless that Merit’s function is directly predicated on one of its prerequisites (for example, Breath Stealer explicitly relies on one being a Psychic Vampire). At the DM’s discretion, you may apply this to other, non-merit powers such as Werewolf Gifts or Vampiric Disciplines.
    Drawback: In addition to the built-in drawbacks of any power you steal, you lose access to a random Symbiote Merit while you have the stolen power. You can release the stolen power at any time and regain access to that Merit.


    Predatorial (●● or ●●●●)

    Your Symbiote exudes a sense of dread that you can direct at others.
    Prerequisites: Presence ●●●
    Benefits: You can spend 1 Ichor point to imitate "Lashing Out With The Monstrous Beast" (Vampire 2ED Core, page 92). Use Strength + dots in Ichorous Symbiote as your dice pool. Using this against mortals doesn't require Ichor, but using it against supernaturals whose Supernatural Tolerance exceeds your rating in IS requires 1 Ichor + 1 willpower point. Unlike the Vampire version, you use your effective Strength, rather than your permanent one. The 4 dot version of this Merit gives you 8 Again on the activation roll.


    Ravenous Hulk (Symbiote, ●●●)

    When hunger looms, you become an even more terrible monster.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●
    Benefits: When you have less than 4 Ichor, your teeth and claws have their damage bonus increased to +1L (stacking with Vicious Maw to +2L), and your effective Size and Strength increase by 2 each (this doesn’t effect your maximum Ichor pool, nor does it make you worth more Ichor to other Symbiotes). Once per Scene, you can spend 2 Willpower while in this state in place of 1 Ichor to use a Symbiote Merit or gain one of the benefits listed under Ichorous Symbiote.
    Drawback: Your hunger can often override your better judgement. When you kill a character while you are in this state, you must roll Resolve + Composure or be forced to immediately begin feeding on them.

    Suppertime (Symbiote, ●●)

    You have learned to use everything but the squeal.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●
    Benefits: Your maximum Ichor increases by 3. If a feeding would give you more Ichor than you could store, you still get the extra, which bleeds off at a rate of 1 per scene.

    Tendrils (Symbiote, ●●)

    Your Symbiote is not as bound to your anatomical constraints as you would expect, and you can lash out violently with tendrils when in danger.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●●●
    Benefits: You can spend 1 Ichor to sprout a single tendril of up to 1 meter per dot of Ichorous Symbiote in length. This tendril is prehensile and as dextrous as a normal human hand, but lacks any discernible digits, making it poor for wielding weapons (-3 penalty). The tendrils can be used to make unarmed attacks without penalty. They do not count as weapons, and you are damaged if they are injured.


    Symbiotic Intensity (Symbiote, ●●)

    Your Symbiote, in exchange for inhabiting your body, can grant you extreme bursts of strength.
    Prerequisites: Ichorous Symbiote ●
    Benefits: You can spend a point of Ichor when making a roll with a physical Attribute in the dice pool to add your Symbiote Rating to the dice pool.


    Thick Hide (Symbiote, ●●●●)
    Your Symbiote is especially dense, making you especially resistant to punishment.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●●●●, Iron Skin
    Benefits: You have Armor 2/2 at all times, even when using Hide Visage. You can spend 1 Ichor to increase this to 4/4 for a Scene, but this effect can't be activated unless your Symbiote is out. This armor stacks with Iron Skin and mundane armor, but does not work against attacks made with your Bane.


    Venomous Projectile (Symbiote, ● or ●●)

    Your Symbiote can direct its toxic nature outward, flinging venomous spines.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●
    Benefits: You can spend 1 Ichor to throw a poisonous spine up to (Stamina x 10) Meters. This is a range attack (Dexterity + Athletics - Defense), and deals exactly 1 Bashing damage and inflicts the Poisoned tilt. The ● version inflicts the moderate Tilt, and the ●● inflicts the grave Tilt.


    Vicious Maw (Symbiote, ●●)

    Your Symbiote’s teeth and claws become jagged and wickedly sharp, making killing all the easier.
    Prerequisite: Ichorous Symbiote ●●
    Benefit: You gain teeth and claws increase with damage +1L, and you can Reflexively spend 1 Ichor to give them Armor Piercing equal to your Symbiote rating for a turn.

    Enraged (Tilt)

    You have become filled with rage for some reason.
    Effect: You suffer a -3 penalty to any action other than physically attacking others otherwise using brute force. However, you receive +3 to any rolls that this Tilt doesn’t penalize.
    Causing the Tilt: The Frenzy merit, taunting or other behavioral triggers.
    Ending the Tilt: Killing or inflicting the Beaten Down Tilt another character.

    Use this in your game tonight!
    You may look at this, and think, hey, this is kind of neat, but I need a way to fit this a little better into a specific gameline. Well, look no further, as I provide you with some quick-and-easy hacks to put these into any game!

    Vampire:

    The Symbiotes can be pretty easily made into a Vampiric (or at least Vampire-related) phenomenon.
    • Instead of Ichor, Symbiotes use Vitae. They can't refine it as efficiently from their prey as vampires, so their eating habits are just the result of their wasteful digestion.
    • Those with the Ichorous Symbiote merit effectively possess Blood Potency 0:
      • When hit with a Vampire's "Lashing out with the Beast," they lose automatically. However, they can choose to resolve the resulting condition as they would the Stunned tilt if they have the Frenzy Merit.
      • They can spend 1 Vitae per turn (rather than 1 Ichor per turn)
      • They can't inflict blood bonds, create ghouls, or use Blush of Life.
    • They aren't Kindred, or even technically Embraced, and removing the Symbiote removes the direct influence, although the host is effectively the thrall to the symbiote's regnant.
    • Perhaps they are some kind of distant offshoot or cousin to the Striges?
    • Perhaps they are the result of some ill-advised attempt by a blood sorcerer to create a weapon in some long-forgotten war?
    • Perhaps they are an experiment by some mad Dragon to create a reversible embrace?



    Werewolf:

    Werewolves explain it like they explain most things: The creatures of the hisil.
    • Rather than Ichor, they feed off of Essence with some violent resonance. Maybe murder, maybe blood, maybe just the resonance produced by predation, as long as it's acquired in pretty much the same way.
      • If it's the latter, be sure to play up the similarity between the Symbiote's predator instinct the the Uratha's
    • You may wish to give them the power to move in and out of the Shadow, especially if you want to play up similarities to Werewolves.
    • They have an effective Rank equal to their total Symbiote Merit dots (including Ichorous Symbiote) divided by 10, rounded down.
      • Thus, starting ones would effectively be Rank 0, and have good reason to stay hidden, while maxed-out Symbiotes would hit Rank 4, and be respected and feared in the shadow.
    • Don't rule out the option of letting them interact with spirit Twilight, or even feed upon spirits, if it feels right for your game.
    • Perhaps they are some kind of Fungal host, a parasitic cordyceps that turns its hosts to feed it?
    • Perhaps it's the experiment of an Idigam, trying to make a weapon to fight the Uratha at their own game?
    • Perhaps it is and Idigam, shattered like the Shartha in time immemorial and attempting to reform itself by having the pieces naturally want to eat each other?



    Mage

    Rather than just shrug and say "mysteries, man," you could try to put a cosmological pin into the Symbiote. No mechanical hacks need to be made, so I'll just pose some possible places you could fit these into a Mage cosmology.
    • Are they some attempt by a Thyrsus to control the truths of Predators by distilling it into the ultimate predator?
    • Perhaps they're from some emanation realm of the Primal Wild that reflect its baser concepts of "eat or be eaten"
    • Maybe they come from Abaddon.
      • The Strix world is a decent candidate
      • Lower Depths are missing a part of reality in such a way that they are hazardous to our world. Maybe they prey on the living because their particular depth hungers for some form of life-force, in the form of Ichor?
    • Maybe they are among the horrifying creatures that may cling to life within the Gauntlet, and they only prey on the living because they need to to survive?

      Awesome! The Symbiotes were always one of my favorite parts of Marvel. Here is what I would add (I'll abbreviate the central Merit to IS):

      1) To increase your rating in IS, you must find another Host and consume their Symbiote. This usually involves killing the Host, but other methods may be discovered during play. This gives 1 dot, but the rating in IS of the other Host must be equal to or greater than yours. Once you have maxed out your rating, consuming other Symbiotes gives you Ichor points equal to their rating (excess points follow the rules under Suppertime). Alternatively, you may initiate a Gestation to create more Symbiotes [I'm unsure of the mechanics for this, will look over the Strix Embodiments later for inspiration].

      2) Symbiote Hosts are hard to kill. They add their rating in IS to their Health. They also heal one bashing damage each minute, one lethal every hour and one aggravated every two days. This means they can heal all Tilts like Blinded or Arm Wrack with time, even if these body parts were severed or otherwise destroyed. Should the Host receive aggravated damage through one of their Banes, each point heals every week instead of two days.

      3) Perhaps add more Banes as the Hosts increase their rating in IS? I'd make it 1 Bane at 1-2 dots, 2 Banes at 3-4 dots and 3 Banes at 5 dots.

      4) Each increase in IS gives you 1 dot of Physical Attributes and 2 dots of Symbiote Merits, which you can spend now or later. Also, your rating in IS allows you to have superhuman Physical Attributes. The limit is raised to [5 + your rating in IS/2 round up].

      5) Add the ability to crawl on walls and ceilings at full Speed.


      Have some more Merits:

      Predatorial (●● or ●●●●): Spend 1 Ichor point to imitate "Lashing Out With The Monstrous Beast" (Vampire 2ED Core, page 92). Use Strength + dots in Ichorous Symbiote as your dice pool. Using this against mortals doesn't require Ichor, but using it against supernaturals whose Supernatural Tolerance exceeds your rating in IS requires 1 Ichor + 1 willpower point. Unlike the Vampire version, you use your effective Strength, rather than your permanent one. So, if you have Strength 4, IS 4 and Ravenous Hulk, you roll 4 + 4 + 2 = 10 dice. The 4 dot version of this Merit gives you 8 Again on the activation roll. [I don't think the Seductive and Competitive Beasts really fit the Symbiotes]

      Hide Visage (●●●●●): You gain the ability to hide not just from the five senses of mortals, but also from technology like thermal imaging. You gain 9 Again on Stealth rolls, which stacks with the dice bonus of the 3 dot version. You may also hide from supernatural senses for the scene by spending 1 Ichor point, using a Clash Of Wills.

      Venomous Projectile (● or ●●): By spending 1 Ichor, you can throw small poisonous barbs from your hands as an instant action, using Dexterity + Athletics - Defense. The 1 dot version and 2 dot versions inflict the moderate and grave Poisoned Tilt, respectively. The maximum range is [Stamina x 10] yards. The barbs only cause 1 bashing damage, which is enough to alert the target about your presence if you're firing from an obvious spot.

      Thick Hide (●●●●): You have 2/2 Armor at all times, even if you are using Hide Visage. This stacks with the Iron Skin Merit and mundane Armor. By spending 1 Ichor, you can increase this to 4/4, but the Symbiote must be manifested and you must pay this cost for each attack received this turn. Attacks made with your Banes bypass this Armor.

              I'd be wary of adding that to the merit; otherwise, you take one dot of Ichorous Symbiote, and you get:
              • +1 HL.
              • +1 to an Attribute.
              • +1 to the Attribute Cap for your Physical Attributes.
              • 2 dots of other merits.
              • Much faster healing.
              • A supernatural tolerance trait of 1.
              • +0L natural weapons.

              For no drawback other than the one you signed up for (you can reduce the "oh noes, other symbiotes want to eat me" by taking Small-Framed.)

              I'd pay an XP for that in a hearbeat.

              Keep in mind that the spoiled minor templates from Hurt Locker tend to get very little from the core merit, and usually have to deal with a harsh cost to use their other "template" powers.

              • The Infected add their dots in Carrier to their pool to infect people with their disease; everything else revolves around their Conditions, which are both situational and harsh.
              • The Dreamers only get something really cool at the 5th dot of Subliminal Conditioning; everything below that can be reduced to "Spend Memory to make better trick/called shots or get benefits of Trained Observer • for one roll. Get Memory from Breaking Points."
              • The Plain have all violence ever become a Breaking Point.
              • The Lost Boys actually take more penalties for each dot of The Protocol they take, and get no direct benefits.

              I'd argue that, as it stands, Ichorous Symbiot is too good.
