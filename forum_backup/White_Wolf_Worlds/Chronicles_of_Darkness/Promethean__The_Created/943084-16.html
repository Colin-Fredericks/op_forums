    Nope. I'm going to have to disagree completely.

    I don't hate Promethean.

    I love Promethean.

    I love the Lineages, I love the Refinements, I love the Centimani, I love Pandorans, I love the aesthetics, I love Transmutations, and I've established how much I love Frankenstein and robots. But there's plenty there already that I love.

    So no, let's not say I hate Promethean. It just isn't true. You can say I hate Awakening, I guess (I don't actually hate it, I just don't think it fits in ChroD. I would probably love it as its own thing). But do not say I hate Promethean.
