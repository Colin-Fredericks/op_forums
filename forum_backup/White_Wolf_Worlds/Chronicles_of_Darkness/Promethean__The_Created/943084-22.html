            They have a sort of mini power stat called Magnitude that goes from 1 to 5, determines how much Pyros they can store at a time (in the form of potions, powders, salves, etc, called "preparations") and how many they can spend per turn, how much Pyros they can generate (generally a small amount per week), and how many Distillations they can know (a Distillation is an individual power from a Transmutation). They can also steal Vitriol (which is like Promethean's version of Arcane Experience from Mage) from Prometheans and use them to buy Merits that represent physical changes to the body or brain chemistry without having to meet the prerequisites. That means even supernatural Merits and Merits that normally require a specific supernatural template are fair game, as long as they represent physical changes, not specialized training. So, Giant Size is fine even though it's normally character creation only, but a Fighting Style wouldn't really work. Finally, they can also spend Vitriol to buy Dread Powers that are normally available to Pandorans.
