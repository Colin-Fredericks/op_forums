                                                        Guys, your all correct to an extent.

                                                        Remember that Pyros is based on real world alchemical principles, and those principles revolve around breaking down matter to it's basic form in order to understand the building blocks of the universe so that the practitioner could better understand how to improve himself/herself physically, mentally, and spiritual. In the Promethean: The Created core rulebook it's stated that Pyros is a naturally occurring force that informs all life and being, so hypothetically all forms of energy could contain a bit of Pyros. After all if a mortal souls could be made from raw Pyros then who knows what else it could create given enough refinement and time.

                                                        As for the original question I'm in the camp that believes a Promethean would register as recognizable but different to the scrutiny of Mage Sight. If there's enough Pyros gathered together to bend the rules of the universe enough to bring life to a lifeless corpse then any form of Mage Sight should be as equally confusing and unsettling as any other observation made about the Created...hypothetically of course.
