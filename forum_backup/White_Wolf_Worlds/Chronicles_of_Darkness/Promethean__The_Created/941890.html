    [2E] Extempore - The Matchless

    08-02-2016, 09:21 AM
    As we are hours to PtC 2E Advanced PDF we got Extempore 'Linage' spoiler ( you can read it here ), I want to talk a bit about it. And that I have mixed feelings on it.

    On one hand, I like we have 'make your own Created' group - my fellow CoD games fans pointed it sounds a lot like Deviant game. And I like I can now make characters like V from V for Vendetta - unknown 'abominations' that still try to be humans. Even more cool, I also could start as Extempore that becomes Progenitor of new Linage.

    On other hand, I do not like pointing it as universal 'we do not know what to do with game here'. Like why mages and werewolves automatically create Extempore instead of normal Linages Prometheans? When we are at this - I still is very much sad that 'unfleshed' members of normal Linages are now banned. We cannot have Galateids made from marble or Ulgan made from sacred tress, they all end as Unfleshed Linage, even when it goes against basic idea for new Act of Creation ( like replicating Galatea mythos with marble ).
