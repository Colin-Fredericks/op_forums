    Another Round at GMC

    05-15-2014, 09:25 PM
    Hey.
    I'm alive.
    Also, done.
    (Except for Transmutations. Fuck that).

    Check it out.

    Thank you:
    - Verge, for all the help last year. Suuuuper grateful I had someone as knowledgeable as you to bounce ideas with.
    - Kingsraven, for making me really think about this.
    - Eremite, for all your input and a good part of the Torment system I put in here.
    - DataWeaver, for providing a ton of critique, insight and smrts.

    I'm running (like, actually running a game) with these rules. I've said that like, three times now? One of them fell apart. The other one got put on hiatus. So one Promethean game with these rules isn't so bad; at this moment, everyone thinks these look pretty solid.

    I welcome critique and insight. So far, this has all been working well.

    I am worried about:
    - Conditions I made. Want to check for balance. I think the resolutions are fun.
    - Copyright. Blackhatmatt made the Wasteland stuff, and I only changed around some things (and made it all in one place); I hacked up most of the Disquiet stuff on my own, and the Torment section is based mostly off of Eremite's hack (which is, uh. Somewhere. I'll link it if anyone finds it).
    - Not being finished? I probably skipped some stuff. I have a lot of the "ground-rules" written up in other places, and my group understands implicitly (through my ranting and raving, of course) how these hacks work. If you find something and you're all "hey this isn't actually explained," just let me know. I'll edit it in (and 1,000 apologies). I'll try to catch all of the trouble-spots soon.

    Notes:
    - I'm leaving the Experience Costs as they are. Transmutations are not something I'm going to re-write; I find the system bearable, so I'm re-working issues (and power levels) as I find them.
    - Speaking of: any comments about Transmutations or suggested hacks to them would be great.
    - I'd love input on the System Hacks section too; super boring down there right now.

    My Promethean Homebrew.

    Yes, I made it the same way as a Promethean. No, the authorities haven't found me yet.

              So far I'm on page 4; and my first thought. This really needs a clear summery of just what "adopted" conditions are; both mechanically and fluff. It would be best if it appeared before the big chart.

              I shall now continue reading.


              P.S. Your praise was really flattering!


              I'm down to witnesses; I think that the dice pool to gain a breaking point through witnessing should depend upon the severity of the sin. Seeing murder and people's reactions should make it obvious that it's bad. Seeing complex emotional abuse will just confuse a Promethian. It won't tie 1:1 with severity, but as a rough representation it should work.

              Another thought: A quashamallim should be able to create a beacon or create an additional breaking point. For example, in the Promethian Corebook an angel told Mr Varney that if he stays put he'll learn useful information. That could easily be represented by a new beacon. A different quashamallim might show up after a promethian hurts someone and hurt him in tun; then it says "it's called pain, humans feel it". New adopted breaking point.


              Overall thought. If you used different styles of bullet point for different levels of indentation it would be more readable.


              Torment: I'm a big believer in reworking Torment significantly. A Promethean should always be in Torment, and the specific moments where you fail a roll and bad things happen should be called Release. For example for a Golem it's psychological torment to not be working, they crave work like addicts need drugs. Being somebody's tool is a freedom from that omnipresent pain, but Disquiet means that it always ends in a rampage. (You shut down your higher thought patterns in order to be a tool, then don't turn them back on until after you get attacked and retaliate)


              I was disapointed that Burdens never appeared. I really liked the idea of Burden conditions representing physical or supernatural weaknesses that vanish as the Promethean becomes more human.

                  EDIT: I just realized you have not included the rules for beacons. You talk about how to complete them, modifiers to them but not the basics of them. What are they? How do you get them? etc...
                  Yeah, yeah. That sounds like something I'd do. I edited it in.

                  In short, Beacons are a person/place/thing that guide the Promethean towards mortality. They replace Milestones. Think of them like a second set of Aspirations; if you pick "Dr. George Evans" as a Beacon, then you're probably fascinated with healers, medicine, saving lives; you want to understand disease and ailment; you want to help people or know what it means to help someone or figure out why the strong humans bother to care for the weak.

                  Instead of your Storyteller picking Milestones for you, you pick Beacons and work up to the "Milestone Moment" which is that confrontation detailed in the doc.
                  Resolving a Beacon basically means incorporating some part of it into your nascent self - hence why you adopt a Breaking Point.

                  Should I add anything else to that explanation/got any questions about it still?
