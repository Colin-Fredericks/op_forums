    I could see an argument for inverting the roles of Azoth and Humanity in the New Dawn: you need a high Azoth in order to have the necessary transformative power to become human (i.e., base the Crucible roll off of Azoth instead of Humanity); but without a high Humanity, you’ll end up as a homeless amnesiac whose life is little better than a Promethean’s. With a high Humanity, you retain your memories, find a place in human society, and possibly even end up representing the best that humanity has to offer. See my earlier post for how I’d revise the “final Azoth roll”; but substitute “Humanity” for “Azoth” as the basis for the extended roll’s pool.

    But I see no benefit from reversing their roles (even partially) anywhere else. Until a developer says that he’s considering using Humanity as a power stat, that’s my last word on the subject.

                Hmmm... I can't help but wonder now about Prometheans and the social construct of "race". I mean, on one hand Disquiet is Disquiet regardless of the politics surrounding a Promethean's skin; but on the other hand there's something to be said for the potential intersection of the Promethean experience and... say... how black children are perceived differently than white children (http://www.apa.org/news/press/releas...oys-older.aspx) or perception of how pain is experienced (http://www.plosone.org/article/info%...l.pone.0048546). And those are just the issues of psychological perception, to say nothing of over discrimination in arrest/conviction/sentencing rates for the same crimes, or the hireability of people with identical qualifications.

                It makes me wonder how a Promethean after the New Dawn is prone to view their own treatment based on race - whether they're desensitized to it having experienced so much worse as a Promethean, or more likely to be outraged at still being "Othered" despite everything they went through.

                                    Obligatory “varies by individual Redeemed” disclaimer.

                                    To me the interesting way of looking at this question is not “why would Redeemed agree with my values” but “why would they not.”


                                    I could easily imagine a Mimic who systematically learns about different races and consciously chooses a race without paying attention to their own skin colour (which may be rock, marble or patchwork). The New Dawn turns them into whichever race they selected.

                                    While this Promethian would obviously know that (Michael Jackson aside) people generally can't choose their skin colour. It would be interesting to speculate on what they might think of race in general.


                                    Another possibility, one I'm particularly fond of, is a Mimic (Mimics are good for this topic) who comes to the conclusion that the way people treat others is an inevitable side effect of in-group bonding; and thus accepts buying into the system as the price of being accepted by somebody – a massive improvement over the Promethian reality of being accepted by nobody.

                                    I like this one because it really captures the idea of a Promethian becoming human – rather than superhuman – while leaving clear room for further development in the next stage of the pilgrimage.


                                    Just a little brain-spew. All I think I can say is generally applicable is that Redeemed will probably learn the basics race, well in/out group dynamics in general, during their time as Promethians and that I find looking for the Redeemed's unique viewpoint is more interesting than trying to say which regular human viewpoint they'd agree with.

                                                        And how will Humanity interact with Pyros? Will lower Humanity allow you to store more, like high Azoth? That also affects the Transmutations-as-power. I think you're putting far too much on Humanity and I don't think it needs this - the powerstat system is simple and works.

                                                        To further explain what I mean by the Azoth/Humanity pairups... I like that, with Azothic Radiance the way it is, Prometheans can feel a high Azoth Promethean coming. There's some of the instinctive, Vampire-like, bigger-fish sensing. I like the fact that high Azoth is a problem for high Humanity-Prometheans, who face nastier Disquiet and Wastelands despite being so close to Humanity, so they'll want to bleed it off even though doing so weakens them. Equally, I like that the further gone or outright monstrous Prometheans are less inclined to care and might live in their Wastelands (until it gets too bad even for them) like horrific Fisher Kings, Tsar Bomba style.

                                                        Azoth covers a lot, and I don't think we can translate it all into Humanity without overburdening it and making a weird power disparity (that's what Beast: The Primordial is going to do, for one thing, but Beast will have it swinging within a given session, Promethean wouldn't). Even if we have low Humanity as the powerful end, with more Pyros and stronger Wasteland effects, it's still taking away the idea that a Promethean who is trying and evolving as a person still struggles with the Promethean condition and the kindling fire inside them.

                                                        Don't get me wrong, Promethean needs some clever rules and to throw out some traditional ideas and I love people throwing theirs out on here, but I just don't think the powerstat is one of them. If nothing else, it's going to make future supplements harder to write, mechanically incompatabile with past ones and make any crossovers a nightmare.
