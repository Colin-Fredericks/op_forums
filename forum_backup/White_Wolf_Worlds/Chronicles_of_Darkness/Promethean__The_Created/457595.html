    The Convoy; a tier 2 Promethean idea

    03-25-2015, 10:30 PM
    Hi everyone. I've posted a few regarding my ideas for Promethean, one of them being a way for the created to have a way to create a Temporary 2nd tier organization other than a throng. Well one idea has been developing this idea for a bit and I'd like to share with you what I have so far. It would be cool if such an idea could be added to the 2nd Edition, but that's just my idea lol.

    Which brings me to my ideas for a 2nd tier Created organization: the Convoy.

    Let's say, if we're going by pure hard numbers here, that the number of Created tops off more or less around 100 globally. Let's also suppose that the Created have been around for pretty much as long as humanity has. Or at least since the beginning of our human history ( roughly 12,000 years). The law of statistics would state that quite a few of those Created would've meet up, thanks to the honing in on their Azoth radiance, and form a throng.

    Now let's follow the logic, in a few centuries time frame, it's likely that a throng meets another throng, what then? True, it's most likely that the two would eventually go their separate ways. But sometime, when two throngs meet, they see the use of the old adage "strength in numbers" and the two (or perhaps more)join up.

    This actually might have happened before with the "society" that was found in New Orleans in one of the supplement book for the first edition. I think it was Pandora's Book. I forget which.

    I came up with the idea that when a throng makes a alchemy pact with another throng, they become a Convoy. This has some pros and cons to it.

    MECHANIC
    Pro: -each Promethean can share up to 2 points of Pyros with each convoy mate.
    - each created gets a -2 for each member of a different lineage agains their disquiet score. There is no penalty for members of the same lineage.
    -each get a +2 for working transmutations for every convoy mate of the same lineage.
    Con:
    - wastelands spread at one level higher then they normally would for every failed role.
    - pandorans in the region can automatically hone in on the location of any Convoy.
    -The Convoy can only stay in an area for 10 days minus the highest Azoth rating before a severe, short firestorm occurs in the Convoy's vicinity. (Also like in Pandora's Book).

    Optional rule: Cause virtue/ purpose

    Each Convoy is held together by a Cause. See, the Created walk their paths to morality differently from each other and each is their own individual. But in order for a Convoy to operate, each member must abide by a common Cause that unites each other. The Causes themselves can very: protection, hope, rage, survival, and all don't stay in one place for long (as most Created do anyway). With a certain Cause in mind, the Convoy then takes shape to better follow the Cause. A Cause for rage can take form as a notorious biker gang crossing Route 66. One of discovery might be a scientific team trekking through the wilderness of the Amazon. Even the leadership can differ, some convoys are ruled by a single iron first while another can have a democratic voting system.

    What this means Mechanically is that each character takes an addition Aspiration. Also each character can utilize their virtue or vice twice in a given scene only if one of the reasons for indulging is in honor of the Convoys' Cause.

    A Convoy however goes beyond just existing as a rather large throng. For one thing, it exercises no kind of authority or actual governing body over Prometheans like ogre "supernaturals". What it does to is allow a given Created to "play act" being a member of a society and what it means to be human in a group that shares similar ideals. When a Convoy inevitably dissolves, each member will walk away with a better understanding of human society and how that relates to being mortal. A Convoy never distracts Prometheans on their metaphysical journey, some see it as a necessary teaching tool about humans an their tendency to self-gather. However, that's not to say that some might latch onto a Cause so strongly that they will fight to keep the Convoy together long past the point of its temporary usefulness. And all Convoys are temporary.

    In the end all Convoys break apart. No group, no matter how well organized or what precautions are taken can survive long before the pull of the Pilgrimage forces the mega throng to disband. But there are important lessons to take away from such a grouping's formation. Being human is more than just being mortal, having a soul, and such. It's also about being a part of a greater whole as a member of a tribe, clan, or large family. Prometheans for all their hard work on reaching mortality don't have the luxury to take part in a mission, cause, or some organization where they feel that they are a thread of a much bigger picture. Most interactions between Created and humans usually are one on one. A Created that to attempts to join any kind of official group, be it a church congregation or a frat usually risks Disquiet so most find out the ins and out of such gatherings from a Convoy.
    .

    Story teller idea:

    With such concentration of divine fire in one area, an angel is bound to be attracted to its radiance. Once a Convoy is created, an angle eventually appears to the group. This doesn't mean that the angel is there to guide or protect the Convoy, although that can happen. Often, the patron that appears is an angel of flux; punishing and menacing (but never outright attacking) the convoy in an attempt to break them apart into their smaller throngs again.

    An angle might direct the Convoy to safe places in the wilderness to bleed off access Azoth, while another might haunt the local mortal population to the point that a bloodthirsty mob is at the Promethean's tattered cave or doorstep. However they do it, the Quashmellem do there best to either break up a convoy to push them on in their Pilgrimage. Alchemy and the Pyros abhors stasis, and a Convoy that stays together too long is going to be violently ripped apart sooner or later.

      Sample Convoys:
      Only a few Convoys are known to the Created. These are the ones that have had such an impact that they survive in the Rambles that pass between the Created. There might have been others in the past their existence erased by the long years of time.

      3000 BC
      At the dawn of recorded history, man was beginning to stake their claim onto the world. Across the sands of Egypt, an empire was taking place on the backs of greater wonders that has existed eons before. Even though the greater magic that had existed was now just a faint echo in power, the early Egyptians were still able to work wonders.

      While tales existed of great rituals that could bring the dead back to life, the knowledge of such magic was just a distant memory at best. That didn't mean that they didn't try to cast their own improvised spells over their deceased, the result just wasn't what they expected. The creatures that rose weren't their lost kings and leaders, but we're beings of great devastation and power.

      They're were greatly feared by those early Egyptians, but we're given great respect, for surely they were touched by the gods for their unearthly abilities. These few Created were isolated from those early settlements to prevent their wasteland from infecting them and we sent food and gifts from courtiers that would make the journey to their shunned palaces for their help when the people needed it.

      You see, infant empire was under sedge from barbarians and tribes men on all sides. So a deal was struck between those that lived and those who were "touched by Osiris" for in those days, men's souls were more fortified from the rigors of the supernatural and thus were able to strike deals and diplomacy with Prometheans. Those early few Created were allowed worship and a grand palace in the desert where no man would bother them in exchange for bringing to heel those that would threaten the early empire.

      A parade then crossed over the sands of the Sarah with an Osiris king as the banner carrier. It was a Convoy of conquest back when the souls of men were stronger and more tolerant of the Fire and could pose a match for even the Created. The Convoy met the nomads and the sands people out on open conflict; steel against alchemy and strengthened bronze and mortal magic against the energies of the Pyros.

      Victory was swift for the Convoy. Wherever their unholy feet tread, corpses were left. These only served to create more Prometheans and new "recruits". The Created (mostly Riven) that were discovered were either taken as slaves or destroyed on the spot. Some even converted to the Convoy in exchange for fealty to the king (not Pharaoh, for THAT title belonged to the actual Pharaoh back in the empire proper.)

      A Quashmellem of Flux followed the Convoy. One with the head of a jackal and the body of a serpent that always remained in the distance. It feasted on the bodies that were left in the Convoys wake. Their Cause was conquest, and a promise made to the one of the first Pharaohs of Egypt.

      1275 A.D.

      The largest convoy to have ever existed also bared arms but not of conquest but rather faith. Made up of trained soldiers, this Convoy traveled the roads and highways of the Crusades lending their skills to anyone willing to support them. They were more solders of fortune rather than mercenaries of God but that didn't mean there weren't a few devout Created who saw the zeal that belief in a higher power gave these otherwise weak-willed mortals and wanted it for themselves. Others, in an attempt to understand how and why humans could travel hundreds of miles to fight a war for an idea also joined. Still others only saw a means to get rich.

      Their "Pyros patron" was a multi winged adgyronous angle bathed in a pale light that gave cryptic dreams to anyone who came in contact, mortal or Created alike. Their Cause was faith and opportunity.

      1825 AD
      This Convoy lasted only one summer and followed the wagon trains during the Westward Expansion out to the California. It was unique in that it wasn't haunted by an Angel, but chased by a powerful and vicious Pandoran who wanted the precious Vitriol in their bodies.

      Their Cause was discovery and survival.

      1939 - 1947

      This Convoy was longest to survive by anyone's account. It managed to last for years by seasonally breaking up every winter and joining up every summer. They took advantage of the expanding electrical grid in the US, for communication amongst their members when separated as well for more supernatural reasons. This Convoy traveled the back roads and small towns of America as a traveling carnival/freakshow during the summer and fall season. The idea and execution was so well organized, and profitable, all under the direction of an egmatic Promethean simply called Mr. Crow.

      This convoy ended, as all do. But if it weren't for a detour they made in New Mexico that the convoy stopped to pitch tent in a town called Roswell . . . Whatever happened to everybody none know for sure. Very few, if any, of the original throng members are around to say why the Convoy suddenly dissolved after staying there for a few days. Only that they did, and with some haste.

      Mr. Crow is rumored to be around, be he hasn't shown up in decades. There are whispers thought that he's tramping through the backwaters of Louisiana and that instead of spreading a Wasteland, that he is followed by strange lights in the sky that snatch anyone foolish enough to stay in the same area as he.

      Their Cause was experimentation and community. Their constant dissolution gave the throng memes enough opportunity to cave their own Pilgrimages outside of the Convoy that no Quashmellem ever materialized to harass them as a whole. There were however reports of strange shadowy humanoid figures that stalked the carnival the last two years of its operation.
