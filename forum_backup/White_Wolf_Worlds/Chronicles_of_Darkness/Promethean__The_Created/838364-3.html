                        On a purely metaphysical level, I think that the problem with alternate New Dawns in Promethean, especially those that remove the weaknesses while retaining the strengths of the promethean condition is that the weaknesses derive from the strengths. The source of a Promethean's Transmutations, pseudo-immortality and all the other stuff is his Azoth, essentially a human soul burning too brightly to be contained. It is that very lack of containment that allows the promethean to work his transmutations and it is that which causes Disquiet and the Wasteland. Removing one requires removing the other. They are inseparable. A Promethean might learn new ways of accessing supernatural power, but as long as they use the Azoth, they will have to pay its price.

                            Originally posted by Geckopirateship View Post
                            Good thinking. I'm baffled by the insistence in 2e that anything other than progress towards humanity is “stagnation”, even progress towards other goals. So we should definitely keep the “refinement” theme.
                            It's not so much that anything other than progress toward humanity is “stagnation” as it is that anything other than progress toward no longer being a Promethean isn't a Pilgrimage. I'd like to see “Pilgrimage” be used to represent progress toward a state-change which, once achieved, renders the character something other than a Promethean, and for “Refinement” to refer specifically to a means of advancing Pilgrimage. (To clarify, Pilgrimage is going to be 2e's Integrity substitute.)

                            Which is not to say that “not advancing Pilgrimage” is the same as stagnation. Arguably, Promethean are incapable of stagnation unless and until they're able to develop a stable state, which is the sort of thing you've been talking about. It's not about transforming into something else; it's about achieving stability in what you are. Thus, a name that implies the quality of being inert (i.e., the most stable of all the chemicals) arguably fits the “Refinement” better than a name that implies strong reactions. In particular, the reason why aluminum is so rare in nature is because it reacts so strongly that it's almost always part of a compound.

                                                        So, maybe it would be best to list the upsides and downsides of being a Promethean, and from whence they arise?

                                                        Downsides include:
                                                        • Disquiet. Caused by the glow of the Divine Fire unnerving people. Possible solutions: Remove Divine Fire, prevent interaction of "glow" and people (via redirection, induced immunity, containment, or sporadic exposure).
                                                        • Wasteland. Caused by the Divine Fire scorching the land. Possible solutions: see above for Disquiet.
                                                        • Torment. Caused by unbalanced Humours/incomplete soul. Possible solutions: Complete soul. Balance Humours. Achieve Detachment (where the emotional surge does not impact rational mind and actions).
                                                        • Pandorans. Caused by radiance of the Divine Fire animating them. Possible solutions: see above for Disquiet.

                                                        Upsides include:
                                                        • Transmutations. Caused by Pyros manipulation. Possible sources: Internal Pyros (status quo), external Pyros manipulation and reservoir, equivalent abilities (???).
                                                        • Transhuman Potential. Caused by Azoth. Possible sources: Azoth (status quo)
                                                        • Durability and "Immortality". Caused by being a body animated by Azoth. Possible sources: Azoth (status quo), external animation or backup animation.

                                                        Some method that might allow each would be a "Half-Dawn", perhaps. Rather than trading Azoth for a new soul, the Promethean keeps both, shuttering the Azoth (via Athanor? Soul containment?) until needed. Another solution is to keep the Azoth, but somehow contain the emanations, redirect them somewhere "harmless", or render potential assets non-interactive with the emanations (change the wavelength, as it were).
