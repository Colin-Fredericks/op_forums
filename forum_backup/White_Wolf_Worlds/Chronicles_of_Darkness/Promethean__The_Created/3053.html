    The Long Journey: what do you want to see in Promethean's Chronicle book?

    11-04-2013, 08:44 PM
    What it says in the title. Who or what should be the default “plot drivers” of the setting (e.g., the God-Machine, the Strix, the Idigam, the Fallen World…)? How should the rules be updated? Some of my own thoughts:

    Given the game's focus on learning about humanity and striving to achieve Mortality, I think the default plot drivers should be humans: e.g., The Long Journey: the Mortal Chronicle.

    • Humanity should be reworked to start at 3 and build up from there, with higher levels of Humanity making the Promethean physically more human as well as mentally more human.
    ​• Lineages should be generalized to Humours, with the existing Lineages being retained as customizations of Humours, much like how Kiths customize Seemings in Changeling.
    • Athanors should be based on Refinements instead of Lineages.
    • Get rid of the ranking system for Transmutations. Handle them more like Embeds in Demon.
    • Include a box addressing if and how the Principle and its qashmallim differ from the God-Machine and its angels.
    • Revise Disquiet, preferably to interact with the Integrity of significant mortal characters (and with “mook rules” when dealing with characters who are individually unimportant). By default, assume that other supernaturals are immune to Disquiet, but give the Storyteller the option to have Disquiet interact with other supernaturals in their own way.
    • Revise Wasteland to streamline it, and to tie it more to use of Pyros in an area rather than merely the passage of time; address the question of if and how Wastelands recover as well.
    Last edited by Dataweaver; 11-04-2013, 10:03 PM.

              I don;t want a Qashmallim chronicle - I love those freaky angels, but 90% of the time (whether they're a messenger or harbinger of doom) they're walking, talking, burning plot device. They also have a lot of similarity with angels and demons, now. Them being reworked as a primary focus rather than a fringe weirdness would weaken Promethean, IMO.

              I want Pandorans expanded and made a bit nastier - Dormacy should work a bit like the Weeping Angels, perhaps, and there should be ways for Pandorans to ijteract with humans and endure long periods of starvation without turning into stone - Prometheans are a rare food source. I have no idea how the Lady of Chains manages.

              I agree 100% on Transmutations being more like Embeds/Exalted Charms. Humanity starting at 3, well, that's just common sense.

              I wouldn't have supernaturals immune to Disquiet at all though, especially mages (arguably the most human of the bunch). While I approve of weakening Wastelands (though I favour an Azoth-based system where Azoth 1 Prometheans ruin a few rooms and Azoth 10 Prometheans potentially blight entire cities - it's simpler, slower and harder to avoid than a Pyros-based system) Disquiet shouldn't have its teeth pulled.

              I like your Seeming/Kith idea... but I adore the Lineages. I really do. I think your 'kith' axis should be why the Promethean was made. because as it stands the Lineages are arguably more distinct by their intended purpose rather than their humour (except the Zeka); Galateids were made to be ideal lovers, Osirans to replace someone that was lost, the Tammuz to be slaves, the Ulgan to be a medium between worlds, Frankensteins to see if it could be done and Zeka.... because radioactivity? Using purpose and humour as your two determinants, each giving you access to different Embeds, would be a neat way of doing this that is simpler than making a lot of kiths and yet more customisable. Though I rather hope nobody makes a zeka for a lover...

              This system also fixes what I think we all see as an issues with Promethean's core, in that in has only 6 origin points for Prometheans, something every single supplement worked to undo without actually saying so - what with tons of new, unique Lineages, progenitors, weirdnesss like extempores and animal prometheans and the fact that Saturnine Night outright says the Zeka had at least three Demiurges.

                                          To me my biggest priority for the Promethian Chronicle - I would call it: The Pilgramage Chroncilces - would be to try to capture the way Promethians really hate their life. I think it's a little too easy to go around thinking that you're an awesome super-being wondering the earth (how many of us would like to go on a round the world trip) with no responsibilities. Pity that everyone else is an arsehole.

                                          To that end I'd probably do two things:

                                          Firstly rename Torment to Release. Torment is the natural state for all Promethians, and the episodes where you go on a rampage are the blessed escape. I'd probably try to make you roll frequently so you notice how inescapable it is, but most rolls just accumulate Torment points that make your next episode of Release is. Fluff wise, every Promethean has a natural state that's absolutely terrible, for example a: Golem is an unthinking tool. Trying to be anything else, such as a Golem thinking for herself, is horrible. It feels unatural, dirty, like being trapped in the wrong body, etc. So every time a Golem is given an order they roll torment, eventually the stress of saying no is too much and they walk up to the nearest human and report up for duty. Disquiet builds, and when the guy forgot to tell you to stop fetching water he tries to kill you.

                                          I see the Lineages (IMO this is a Lineage not Humour thing) as:


                                          Frankenstein - pulling in one hundred directions: Torment builds when you deny your impulses. Release makes you run around following the first idea in your head.
                                          Galataid - the perfect partner: Torment builds when you try to be yourself, not what someone else wants you to be. Release turns you into a Stepford wife, or whatever your lover wants.
                                          Osiren - Osiris: Literally. Torment builds when you show ungodly humility. Release means you think you are Osris and act like like He would.
                                          Golem - Tool: Even slaves are people, Torment builds when you try to disobey and order or follow it in anything but the most literal manner. Release turns you into an unthinking automaton.
                                          Riven - Not sure.


                                          The second thing is Disquiet. Disquiet really needs streamlining to remove bookkeeping, but in addition to that like to see it balanced so that you see a constant low level jerk behaviour to the Promethean rather than taking time to build up.



                                          Now for some of the smaller things I'd like to see.

                                          Alchemy and Mad Science rules. There's a lot of experimenting in the books, usually the Created themselves are experimenting on progeny. Lets have some rules for it. The fact that those rules can be reused for mortal NPCs is only a plus.

                                          More options for The Principle. Nearly every option given is some variant on preserving the status qou. That is stupid!. Firstly because it gives only one option, secondly because that one option doesn't fit the theme of the game. There should be at least one option for the Principle as The Pilgrimage on a cosmic scale.

                                          A book of "quests". Prometheans spend a lot of their time moving away from things, usually disquiet and half formed wastlands. Lets have options for moving towards stuff. From low level stuff like seeing signs saying "a big promethian meeting on this date and place" and travelling to the convention to high level stuff like a tourist trip covering: The Garden of Eden, Osiris's kingdom in the Underworld, Frankenstine's original laboratory and the crater Satan made during his fall from heaven to hell. Or heck, an epic quest to save the world. The idea that hero's learn a lot about themselves on quests, famboy -> jedi, is a popular one, and it fits in well with Promethean.

                                          Fix the rules for taking damage. IMO if you look at how Aggravated is handled it makes Promethians weaker than mortals. So here's my replacement. Promethians do not take any damage from Bashing or Lethal. Period. You can only hurt them with something that causes Aggravated Damage, or by doing so much damage that it wraps around, in that case inflict the Agg and ignore the rest. You can still inflict tilts though. The idea being that if you chop of it's leg, it's not exactly injured. There's no blood loss, there's no slow down, but it still can't walk on a missing leg.

                                          Introduce a golden rule: Every Promethian has a fair shot at the pilgrimage. You never have anything like an Extrepimor who can't create progeny. (Zeka and the like might still get into trouble afterwords, but becoming human is still clearly possible).

                                          Remove the need to create progeny. I've noticed a few people saying they feel the new dawn is bad / Promethean is not a hopeful game because of this. Anything that damages the themes that much must go; or possibly they're just outright wrong, but I kinda trust Jnsead's opinion.

                                          Humanity starts at 3. Promethians start with disadvantages that can get removed via milestones.

                                          Weird weird weird. This is a game about creation, and it's a game about people living outside of society. Those two things scream for weirdness. Stuff that dosn't fit into normal life. Just as a Promethian is always seeing new stuff for the first time, because they're probably like 3 years old, players should always be seeing new stuff to capture that feeling.

                                          Humour-seeming / Lineage-kith system.
