    Hari nef will show that struggle just right!

    I LOVE CHICAGO! She just captures exactly what i think when i think "Frankenstein". She has the rbavery and the angst,and when i think of her i think of a woman who could whatever the fuck the world will throw at her and rise up,even if what rises up it's not verry happy. So my first cast that's not an actress
    The Soccer player! Martha Viera! She's from the Brazilan soccer team,look at her,she's just the toughest lady on the block!

    Hakan
    This fellow here is the least hopeful of our stiched bunch,he's just doesnt like people anymore. To the point he pretty much gave up on being people. I Don't agree. But i can relate. In my Mind,Hakan was made from the corpses of older people With that in Mind i found someone who can portray Hakan's fierceness and anger
