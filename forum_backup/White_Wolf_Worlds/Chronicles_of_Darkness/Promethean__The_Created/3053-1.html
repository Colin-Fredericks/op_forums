        Eremite:
        I don;t want a Qashmallim chronicle - I love those freaky angels, but 90% of the time (whether they're a messenger or harbinger of doom) they're walking, talking, burning plot device.
        One of Blood and Smoke's developers (DaveB?) described the Strix Chronicle as about the clash between the suave vampires of pop-culture and the monstrous vampires of folklore, and those particularly bad cases where it's not a clash. The God Machine Chronicle is about the grind of a purpose you may or may not be part of (I'd suggest praying you're not, but if you did it might hear you).

        Those present a sort of default of what chronicles are about. But the default for Promethean is the Pilgrimmage - without that, it's not really Promethean any more. So I agree with you about a Qashmalim Chronicle - and extend the same sentiment to a Mad Science Chronicle, Zeka Chronicle, or Sublimatus Chronicle.

        Mortals is the only possibility that gets round that.

        But what I really think is that the Promethean book should be, in comparison to the others, scattershot. Promethean is suited to an episodic style, as the Created move around a lot, and move from milestone to milestone - and so I think the Tales should be about as many different things as possible - things that an ST can adapt into personal milestones to further personal Pilgrimmages. Rather than tiers as such, move out from the throng to the wider world: five tales focussed on other Prometheans or the environment, five tales focussed on human interactions with Prometheans, five tales focussed on human interactions with other humans, and five tales focussed on the wider mythos (a Qashmal's mission gone awry, a devastating Firestorm threatening Prometheans and humans alike, the machinations of a Sublimatus, the Unfleshed...).


        Stuff I'd want:

        The Bronze and Lead Refinements in the new core. I'm less fussed about the other two, though they'd be cool as well, but study of the flesh and forging a communal bond have a particular spice.

        I mentioned flights of fancy, above. One thing I'd like is for the Ulgan to be replaced with something based on the "Underworldly oracles" of myth. If we must have a Lineage crossing with an otherworld, I think the ghostly is better than Shadow spirits. Also, I think many of the motifs that attend mythical oracles work well for Promethean - the unkind truth, the obscure truth, the hostility and doubt shown to those whose visions aren't what the client wants, mad ecstasies.

        Some new corebook inclusion of Constructs and Extemporae (the former in with the Lineages, the latter perhaps in a Tale).

                                Verge:I think Dataweaver's right in that the linchpin of the Chronicle should be mortals, but I also think that if you're running Promethean and mortals aren't your leading and most dangerous antagonists, then you're avoiding the heart of the game.
                                Mortals and the Pilgrimmage. But it can't be about the Pilgrimmage per se, because it's too personal to each individual Promethean - that's why I suggested making it scattershot earlier, so as to give a wide palatte to incorporate into milestones.

                                But to combine scattershot and mortals... well, when it comes to making Prometheans, human obsession is the coin of the realm. Perhaps the same is true of other manifestations of the Fire. The Qashmalim seem to be drivers of events, and they certainly manipulate - but perhaps their actions are in answer to a driving obsession - someone wants something badly enough, and Qashmalim are answering that "prayer."

                                On the other hand, people sufficiently burned out and apathetic five succor to the Flux, not the Azoth - crush enough feeling out of people, and they're hardly there. Maybe they don't cause Dormancy like other people, and their faded communities become havens for Pandorans.

                                On the gripping hand, fury - in the sense of tempestuous feeling rather than anger - could be mirrored in Firestorms.

                                The World of Darkness has several otherworlds that react to the psyche of those who move through them, but in Gothic literature and classic horror films - which Promethean takes a lot of its feel from - the natural world's rather like that too, if the emotions are extreme enough. (I think the term's "pathetic fallacy"). So that's what the Fire does - it answers the heart, in more ways than just making Prometheans.

                                Not all the time, of course: it has to be extreme, just like it does when a Promethean is made. But maybe the Wasteland makes it easier - rather than turning the world to muck, it seeds it with Fire that then starts to answer the hidden prayers in people's hearts with Storms and Angels. Furious obsession is the coin of the realm, and Prometheans carry that realm around with them.

                                And that makes the Wasteland double-edged. Most Prometheans want to understand humans, and a Wasteland effect that writes humanity's foibles onto the landscape is one they might believe - rightly or wrongly - will help them do so. They have a reason to stay and let it develop (though of course hidden obsessions being nurtured into that kind of violent expression is also hella destructive, which ties back into Disquiet and torch-wielding mobs).

                                Ok, that turned into a bit of a stream. Hope it makes sense.

                                    I agree wholeheartedly with changing Disquiet and Wasteland to shore up bookkeeping.

                                    I had a thought regarding Disquiet that I think would work well(ish) with the new rules: Disquiet as Integrity modifier. Basically, if a Mortal were to ever glimpse a Promethean's true nature (or, perhaps, if they fail a Resolve+Composure roll), they receive a Breaking Point along the lines of the Prommie's Disquiet effect (not becoming obsessed with a Galatean, for example). Factors such as Azoth and having, er, Tormented that scene (among others, I'm sure) would make the Breaking Point even harder to ignore. For Supernatural Beings, these factors would determine were along Humanity/Harmony/Whatever the Breaking Point would sit (more negatives garnered, the lower on the chart it sits). Perhaps this effect could become a permanent thing should the Disquieted being be affected by it enough times.

                                    I dunno, I felt that this would be a cool way of handling it (without simply resorting to Condition effects), though I can't say how this would mess up the other systems.

                                    As per the "plot" behind the book, I think it should be the "Created Chronicles" and should focus on Creation vs. Creator, the founding of purpose within "ourselves" and in others, ties that bind, and the things that drive Prometheans forward. I'd like to see the Qashmallim pushed "back" a bit, made more of a mysterious event than simply a new spirit type. I'd like to see rules for Mortals utilizing Azoth and Flux for alchemy and weird science (and the resulting backlash that comes from such levels of maddening obsession and the utilization of forces beyond one's understanding). I'd like to see the focus less on "meta-purpose" (i.e. The Principle) and more on filling out a Promethean's personal mission. I'd also like to see Pandorans (and in some ways Centimani) seen more as "failures" of the Great Work, dead ends along the path to Humanity, than "bad guys filled with evil magic" (I'm not saying that's what they are at all, it's just the impression I get from reading the books).

                                    I'd also like to see rules and examples in this book for Prometheans building something of a Society, perhaps due to the PCs involvement. I think it'd be interesting to have some sort of Crunch for building up a brand new Supernatural society from Tier 1 to Tier 3 (or maybe even beyond... sidebar!), with rules and effects for joining various PC/ST created groups as well as having an idea regarding how much bullshit would potentially be involved in such an endevour.

                                                    So now we know that Blood and Smoke has a very different structure from God Machine chronicles... and one that I think is better suited to Promethean. In light of which - and with the benefit of some time - I'm revisiting some of my (and others') earlier comments.

                                                    Dataweaver:
                                                    Given the game's focus on learning about humanity and striving to achieve Mortality, I think the default plot drivers should be humans: e.g., The Long Journey: the Mortal Chronicle.
                                                    I'm not sure the word "mortal" quite works, but I still strongly agree with the sentiment.
                                                    ​Lineages should be generalized to Humours, with the existing Lineages being retained as customizations of Humours, much like how Kiths customize Seemings in Changeling.
                                                    I still don't care for this, unless the modification is much greater than for kiths. If mortals should be the touchstone, then the mortal obsession that drove a demiurge, and its ability to indelibly mark the resulting Lineage, should be a big deal. I could maybe get behind something like this:

                                                    Humour: Determines the form of the Wasteland (and implications if the Promethean joins a Pact), and governs favoured access to one set of Transmutations.*

                                                    Lineage: Is where the origin story comes in. Determines Disfigurements, Disquiet, and Torment. In setting terms, the latter two parallel the demiurge's obsessions, and determine which Humour becomes dominant in a given Lineage (out of setting, it's presumably more likely to be the other way round).

                                                    * Assuming the same Lineages, Refinements, and Transmutations: Alchemicus would go to Phelegmatic Prometheans,** Electrification to Choleric ones, Mesmerism to Sanguine ones, Sensorium to Ectoplasmic ones, and Vitality to Melancholic ones.

                                                    ** No, Alchemicus isn't a great fit for the Osirians, but it is a good fit for a phlegmatic bent.

                                                    Under this model, Refinements would grant favoured access to one set of Transmutations rather than one (their remaining one for the main five, their unique one for the supplemental four, and Irradiation for Centimani).
                                                    Athanors should be based on Refinements instead of Lineages.
                                                    I'm on board with this now. Athanors are about aspirations for the future, and that fits Refinements better than Lineage. I'd throw in a rule that once you've picked an Athanor, the "swapping into this Refinement is quicker and out of it slower than normal" rule applies to the one corresponding to your Athanor rather than necessarily Stannum.

                                                    TheKingsRaven:
                                                    Alchemy and Mad Science rules. There's a lot of experimenting in the books, usually the Created themselves are experimenting on progeny. Lets have some rules for it.
                                                    In this case, I would keep it simple, and allow humans a way of gaining access to Transmutations if they're off-the-rails enough. None of "spiritual purification" stuff though - that's for Prometheans - humans who mess with this are broken and break themselves further doing it.
                                                    So here's my replacement. Promethians do not take any damage from Bashing or Lethal. Period. You can only hurt them with something that causes Aggravated Damage, or by doing so much damage that it wraps around, in that case inflict the Agg and ignore the rest.
                                                    I think that's excessive, though I could get behind an "all nominally lethal damage is actually bashing" rule.

                                                    Me:
                                                    Also, I seem to recall Blackhat_Matt said that he'd be the one doing this if it was done, and that he'd not want to change the Lineages. So anything we want here is probably a flight of fancy ... [like] ... the Ulgan to be replaced with something based on the "Underworldly oracles" of myth. If we must have a Lineage crossing with an otherworld, I think the ghostly is better than Shadow spirits. Also, I think many of the motifs that attend mythical oracles work well for Promethean - the unkind truth, the obscure truth, the hostility and doubt shown to those whose visions aren't what the client wants, mad ecstasies.
                                                    If there were to be a Humour/Lineage division, then I'd be on board with adding a second Lineage for each Humour, and making the second Lineage something off the norm; for example:

                                                    Choleric: Maybe an Unfleshed Lineage, though I don't have any more specific idea than that.

                                                    Sanguine: Maybe a supernatural Lineage, like if that vampire-made Promethean from Saturnine Night managed to make more of his kind.

                                                    Phegmatic: An Lineage whose founder was an Extemporae from a serious natural disaster.

                                                    Melancholic: Both the Osirians and Tammuz have the attempted resurrection of a loved one in their origin, but neither of them reflect the loss/grief vibe. I would suggest replacing the canon Tammuz with a group that do, and put the slave vibe into a Lineage of actual golems (as in Constructs).

                                                    Ectoplasmic: My oracle idea, with the explicit myth that the demiurge and progenitor are one and the same; ie, that she called the fire down into her own flesh and made herself something new.
                                                    But what I really think is that the Promethean book should be, in comparison to the others, scattershot. Promethean is suited to an episodic style, as the Created move around a lot, and move from milestone to milestone - and so I think the Tales should be about as many different things as possible - things that an ST can adapt into personal milestones to further personal Pilgrimmages.
                                                    I wrote this when I assumed that any Chronicle book would follow the general structure of God Machine Chronicles. Since we now know that's not the case, I abandon it as a suggestion (especially since I don't think Tales are a very good fit for Promethean).

                                                    What I'd rather have is a breakdown something like in Blood and Smoke:

                                                    Who You Are (I think the second person fits Promethean better): Covers the Lineages, Humours if they're separate, and Refinements.

                                                    The Long Road: Covers the general myths, and what little society they've managed to scape together. And the general structure of a Pilgrimmage.

                                                    Clay and Lead: Character creation, Transmutations, generally how Promethan bodies work, that sort of stuff.

                                                    The Burning World: Covers manifestations of the Fire. Pandorans, Qashmalim, Firestorms, the Wasteland, whatever rules there are for letting mortals gain access to Transmutations.

                                                    No Rest: A variety of mortal templates (including a simplified Integrity system to go with Disquiet); and some specific sample people. The Disquiet (and maybe Torment) rules. And advice for/samples of: roadside stops like motels, diners and campgrounds; small towns; slightly larger towns; and city environments; together with the fracture lines in each that Disquiet can aggravate [I'm thinking in terms of chapter 1 of Midnight Roads here; short and numerous rather than few and detailed].

                                                    [Putting the Disquiet and Torment here was deliberate - essentially, the rules for interacting with humans going with the rules for the humans themselves]

                                                    And rule and storytelling chapters of course.
