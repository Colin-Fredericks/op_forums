    Refined Apostles: An attempt to create a Super-Template

    10-05-2021, 11:55 PM
    This is essentially my first draft for a way to give Promethean characters a "Super Template" while still having the New Dawn actually be meaningful. Based partly on some suggestions by RomulusGloriosus here, I added my own flavoring to the mix to A) Not plagiarize, and B) Justify the "Super" part of the template. So, here's what I have so far, feel free to ask questions or give suggestions!

    The Refined Apostles

    Originating from the Greek "Apostolos", or "Messenger," the Refined Apostles are simultaneously a goal post for many Created as well as a word of warning. They are those Prometheans who, before the New Dawn, dedicated themselves fully to one refinement in particular, yet still branched out enough to have completed the New Dawn to one degree or another. As a result of their devotion, they have retained a measure of their alchemical power as humans, a boon to be sure for some Prometheans who, while eager to become "real," still enjoy some of the benefits that their abilities bring. Attempting to become one however requires an absurd investment of Vitriol, time and sanity, and more often then not leads one to becoming a Petrificatus or simply dead. And yet, there are still a lucky few out there, less than 1 per year, who manage to succeed, becoming avatars and "Messengers" of their Refinements just as much as Qashmallim do for the Principle.

    How to walk on the path of Apostleship:
    1. Every Alembic available to a particular Refinement (8 in total) MUST be calcified using Vitriol XP. Most Prometheans, upon hearing of this step, immediately forsake the road, recognizing (not unfairly) that it is too heavy of a burden for an individual who is already searching for scraps of Vitriol to use in their journey. Some rumors say that even the current Apostles once used the Lacuna to obtain the needed Vitriol before reaching the New Dawn, but these are unconfirmed.
    2. The aspiring Created must forge themselves a specific Athanor, an "Apostolic Mantle," for their chosen Refinement. Doing this does NOT refund the Vitriol XP spent on calcifying. Sacrifices must be made in order to clear the road ahead of distractions.
      • "Apostolic Mantle"
      • Reduce the Pyros cost of Alembics within the Transmutation by 1. While Dampening the Fire, Alembics within the Transmutation ALWAYS treat Azoth as if it was the original value, not the diminished one. Finally, the Promethean is treated as always possessing the Refinement Condition and method for earning Beats (treated as either normal beats or Vitriol beats).
    3. Upon achieving the New Dawn, the Promethean must choose to retain their memories of the Pilgrimage. Would-be Apostles who choose instead to embrace their new lives only receive the benefits of the "Role Call" trait (see below) and a guarantee of protection by the Principle that their new existence will not be interfered with by outside supernatural parties.
    Apostle Systems:

    Apostles are humans who begin their existence with a "Greater Template" according to Imperial Practices. They use the "Magnitude" power stat, and start at Magnitude (Total Completed Refinements / 2, rounded DOWN). A Promethean who completed the bare minimum of Refinements would start play with a Magnitude of 2, while one who went the full distance with 10 Refinements would begin with a Magnitude of 5. In addition, those Apostles who somehow completed all 10 refinements can choose any Alembic they learned throughout play to retain, besides their fixed Alembics.

    Apostles possess a number of traits, seen below:

    Dedicated to the Principle: Apostles retain all of the Alembics available for their chosen Refinement, as well as the benefits of the Apostolic Mantle Athanor. Apostles add their Magnitude in Doors whenever a character attempts to dissuade them from their Refinement or a principle related to it. In addition, Apostles can always teach their Refinements to Prometheans willing to learn, and if an Apostle were to create a new Promethean, they would automatically begin in the Apostle's Refinement (breaking the normal rules of character creation.)

    Self-Contained: Apostles naturally produce and retain as Prometheans do, but their perfected bodies do not leak the substance out. Apostles do not suffer or cause Disquiet nor do they suffer from Torment. An Apostle CAN however produce or grow a Wasteland through spending large amounts of Pyros (11 - Magnitude). In addition, Apostles do not suffer from Conditions due to their Pyros as Alchemists do.

    Refined Avatar: When using Refinement Alembics, the Apostle treats their Magnitude as being twice as high (both for dice pools and persistent effects.) By spending a point of Willpower and the necessary amount of Pyros, the Apostle can use their Refinement as being an Influence with a dot level equal to their Magnitude. This can take a few different forms:
    • An Influence based upon the symbolic metal of the Refinement (Gold, Silver, etc.)
    • An Influence based on the Refinement's area of focus (Mortality, Mystery, etc.)
    • An Influence based on one of the Refinement's Transmutations (Deception, Mesmerism, Sensorium, Spiritus, etc.)
    • Any Influence allowed by the Storyteller which ties back into the Refinement and it's ideals.
    Role Call: As expressions of Refinements, Apostles can take on the "roles" which embody the Refinement. When acting within one of these Roles, an Apostle automatically improves their impression level by one stage. They also gain a dot of Anonymity (which can stack with other sources) as long as they act "in-character." This dot explicitly does NOT conflict with any dots in Fame the character possesses. An Apostle celebrity for example might be known far and wide for their Hollywood premier, but their personal life won't be looked into (or at least, not easily.)

    Sanctified Soul: By tying themselves so deeply to a Refinement, an Apostle secures their soul from tampering and usurpation. Whenever an effect would target the Apostle's soul, they can spend a point of Pyros to begin a Clash of Wills, using their (Highest Resistance Attribute) + Magnitude x 2. If the roll fails, the Apostle can reroll their dice by spending another point of Pyros. The Apostle can do this as many times as necessary, sidestepping the normal Per-Turn limit of Pyros.

    Character Advancement:

    Despite advancing as far as they have, Apostles can still progress in their abilities, further dedicating themselves to the concepts they hold dear. The following costs use normal XP:

    Trait (Cost)
    Attributes (4)
    Skills (2)
    Skill Specialty (1)
    Merit (1)
    "Outside" Alembic (4)1
    Magnitude (Max: 5) (10)

    1: "Outside" in this case refers to Alembics outside of the Apostle's Refinement.

          This is pretty nifty stuff (and homebrew Promethean content is thin on the ground so more is always welcome). I dig their potential uses as a story hook for various ends (Refinement tutor being the most obvious, but other potentialities dangle and glitter so tantalizingly).           Although you mention that Apostles "naturally produce and retain [Pyros I assume] as Prometheans do," it might be a good idea to spell out what exactly that entails. Do they regain Pyros at sunrise? Can they still replenish from ensconcing themselves in their former Lineage's element?

The only instances I can think of in official material that allow players to gain Influences are in Werewolf (where you're limited to the four classical elements locked behind four different Renown-types) and Geist (where either you're limited to a single Influence at the risk of leaving your body vulnerable, or you're playing one of the Absent whose section maintains a tacit understanding that your Influences conform to those things important to a Rank 2 ghost). What is it theme-wise that Refined Avatar is meant to accomplish (besides making Apostles feel more ethereal)?

              First, thanks for the review! I'm glad to see that you enjoy it, as I was a bit worried that the overall reception would be a bit more negative. Now, for your points:

              They do regain Pyros with the sunrise, but NOT from their former element, that's something I'll have to edit when I'm not on lunch break.

              The implementation of Influences was... something of a tug-of-war I had internally. I wanted something that would demonstrate the character's "allegiance" of sorts to the Refinement and be suitably powerful enough as a "super template" of sorts. My OTHER option was to have a character-unique "4 dot" power which united all of the themes of a Transmutation or even the Refinement, a la Ciphers from Demon the Descent or 7-dot powers from TC: Aeon. The issue with that was that I had no idea what rules or guidelines I could use for that. I could likely create something later, but I figured that Influences as an established ruleset would just be easier to implement.

              Finally, I'd rule it as the new Promethean beginning in the Apostle's Refinement, but nothing else. Otherwise you really get into the weeds with how that's supposed to work.

                      I've given some further thought as to the idea of 4-dot powers uniting the Refinement together. While I'm still not sure as to XP costs for that, or any other requirements, here's an example I thought up for Stannum:

                      Arcing Purge
                      By employing the use of Electrification to carry a current of flux repulsing energy, the Apostle can shoot out a bouncing strand of lightning that burns out the Disquiet in people's bodies... violently.

                      Cost: 4 Pyros
                      Action: Instant
                      Dice Pool: Dexterity + Athletics + Magnitude
                      Treat the bolt of lightning as a (Disquiet)B weapon which can tossed at any opponent within direct line of sight. Once the bolt has hit an enemy, it immediately attacks another nearby enemy suffering from the Disquieted Condition using the same dice roll, -1 success. This continues until there are no more Disquieted enemies, or no more successes. Any enemies hit by the attack immediately lose the Disquieted Condition without resolving. By spending a point of Willpower, the damage can be made to be Aggravated instead. Dramatic Failures (as shown below) can only occur on the FIRST roll.

                      Dramatic Failure: The purging lightning flickers through the Apostle's form, dealing (Magnitude)L damage and removing (Magnitude) points of pyros.
                      Failure: The lightning does not arc to the target.
                      Success: The attack works as specified, continuing on until there are no more targets or successes.
                      Exceptional Success: As above, and the victim does not suffer a penalty to their next roll to resist Disquiet.

                                  For that specific Refinement, yes. For any other refinement that you somehow finish all three roles for... no, you'd still get your Vitriol back.                                               Sorry for not answering your question right away, I haven't checked in here the past few days.

                                              So, the Mantle is an Athanor, and you can only make 1 Athanor per Refinement. Thus, if you created the Mantle, there's no chance of you creating a Refinement Furnace (I even made sure to state that you do NOT get a free Refinement Furnace from an exceptional success.) I don't know of any other way to refund the Vitriol besides that.

                                              Technically speaking, in the history of PtC there have been 12 refinements (the five simple, the five complex, Flux and then Pneuma from Dark Eras Companion which is a Pseudo-Gold.) So, having more Refinements than the typical ten as a concept is perfectly fine. Then come the two issues, however:
                                              1. The number of Refinements you complete as a Promethean determine your Integrity stat as a mortal, 1-for-1. Since the Integrity stat caps out at 10, it's not possible to benefit from more than 10 Refinements beyond that Exceptional Success benefit you mentioned.
                                              2. That twelfth Refinement I mentioned, Pneuma, was a failed alternative to Aurum. Where Aurum revolved around understanding humans and humanity, Pneuma is the philosophy that salvation (i.e. the New Dawn) comes from God, and is granted to the Prometheans via Grace. Since the Great Work is supposed to be something that's ultimately done on your own initiative (though you can obviously have help from others), the effectiveness of the Pneuma Refinement was quite low, and as a result died out much like natural selection among sub-species. Presumably, the reason that there remains only 10 refinements (and Flux, but that doesn't really count since it ALSO doesn't progress the Pilgrimage) is because those are the ten among dozens of possible substitutes and alternatives that remain effective and viable for becoming human. Since becoming an Apostle ultimately requires becoming human, other Refinements which don't result in newborn humans don't generate Apostles. This is why there are no Apostles of Flux (to answer your last question) and no Apostles of Pneuma. They are spiritual/pseudo-genetic dead ends.
