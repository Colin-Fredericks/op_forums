    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    Well, it's not Power Gaming if it is end of story - you will only get Perfected as those rare NPC ancient masters of Created that wanted to be their own kind rather than forced to be human by Principle. ( See an Extempore write-up for ideas on those. )
    Extempore have exactly the same drive to become human as every other Promethean. What they lack is the guidance that shows other Prometheans how to do it.

    Also: Disquiet, Wasteland, and Torment aren't the Principle trying to push a Promethean to become human; they're the nature of Pyros asserting itself. Pyros is harmful to the environment; that's why Wastelands form. Pyros is harmful to the human psyche; that's why people experience Disquiet. And Pyros is volatile and unstable; that's why Prometheans experience Torment. It's the nature of what a Promethean is, not an outside force punishing the Promethean for not doing its will — that's what Lilithim are all about. A Promethean that doesn't deal with Wasteland, Disquiet, or Torment is like a fire that doesn't burn.

    You want a Promethean whose goal is to settle down and live comfortably as a Promethean? Go Centimani, or settle into a Role. Sure, a qashmal will eventually come knocking to try to rouse you from the rut you're in; but only one will come, ever: qashmallim do not repeat failed Missions. So if you can overcome that one qashmal, you're free of the Principle's interference. Also, consider setting up a Hovel, so that you can live comfortably in your Safe Place without worrying about creating a Wasteland; and if you stick with a Role that you've mastered, you don't have to worry about Disquiet either. That leaves only Torment, which isn't going to go away so long as you have Azoth burning inside of you.

    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    Animals and trees do not sounds to me as something you can strife for in more individual cases - those that you started as 'body' from this kind. Even then, they clearly could be in the Self category.
    1. I could see a humanoid Promethean who encounters and befriends a dog growing to admire his furry companion, and eventually deciding he wants to become a dog, too. Yes, Prometheans with “become an animal” as a final goal are likely to be rare; but Prometheans with a final goal other than “become human” are rare to begin with.

    2. I don't know what category they'd be in; I could see a case for an animalistic Iron Role for a Promethean seeking to become, say, a housecat.

                    I think my ideal approach would be the hack the rules so that Prometheans are closer to vampires or werewolves: they have their kewl powerz but major drawbacks, but drawbacks that are manageable enough that they can maintain themselves indefinitely (yet dangerous and volatile enough that they're genuinely risky and mitigating with them and working around them provides plot hooks).
