    Okay. Now, I don't really feel like going through and reading the entire thread first, so I'm just going to jot down what I think first, then come back and scan the rest of the thread, and go from there.


    1) Rework all the Transmutations from the ground up. I've been preparing to play a Demon game, and it strikes me that the demon form you start with? The equivalent to nearly all of Vulcanus and Electification can be taken as a starting character, and only with a portion of your starting abilities. And those aren't the only ones like that. Normally, I wouldn't care about comparing one game to another. But it only highlights how much things are different in Chronicle books in general.

    2) Increase the number of base refinements to 8 - the seven alchemical metals plus radioactive Uranium. Hints on making other refinements. Not really important, but just something I'd like to see.

    3) Eliminate the Hisil from the base game. There's no call to bringing in an entire new realm into designing this game, nor the troubles and regulations it brings. Especially not when there are plenty of other local spirit phenomenon in Twilight you can deal with instead. The game should focus on the game's own spiritual beings first and foremost. Make the qashmal more important to the game than just plot hooks.

    4) Reworked Wastelands / options for a city based game. Nomadic life is all well and good, but how about the concentration camp, or the street bum as a Promethean society? Something different than just the same wandering roads would be lovely.

    5) All the options for alternative creation methods? Born from a statue, or having a human creator, or tulpa made flesh, or Unfleshed, or whatever? Built as an option into the core six humors. In fact, while we're at it, we need to rework the six humors so that the resemble their mythological counterparts more strongly. Frankenstein monster is good. Indeed, the core book pushed that a bit too much. But the golem, the spiritual sacrifice, the work of art, the resurrected dead, and the nuclear zombie should have their own themes stand out a bit more. Promethean is a game about the Created - all the different types of created monsters.

    6) Related to the above, but a better bane than just "kill it with fire!" It makes zero sense to use fire to kill a golem or tulpa. If anything, human masses are the bane, and I'd like to see that played up instead of just burning them. Like, those with heavy disquiet should hurt a Promethean more, or something similar. Ideally, each of the nine game lines should have a unique bane, imho.

    7) Rework Humanity. Rename Humanity as well. Humanity was a list of good VS evil acts. Never mind that humanity has its dark side. That we had two (count them, two!) refinements that focused on indulging the darker parts of the human psyche. We need the new Integrity to reflect

    8) We need an Aura. Vampire has Predator's aura. Mage is getting their nimbus to work like the predator's aura, and hints of a werewolfy one as well. Demons use their cover to spoof - they use an un-aura to hide. Prometheans can have an aura as well. And the fun part? The Aura can directly tie into Disquiet as well as becoming more human. This also makes Disquiet to be something you actively use - not sure on the benefit to the game, other than I think that it will add a bit more weight to the player. Do you push and gain more humanity, or do you sit and wallow and never connect or learn? Not sure if it would really benefit the game, but it seems to work awesome for the Big Three.

    9) Clones. Core book mentioned them. Work more on it.

    10) Lairs. More frankenstein monsters with tesla coils. More galatea art studios, or ulgan shrines. Make it happen!

    11) The default game is to have a Promethean start off as the created monster rebelling against their maker, or whatever. And then, they eventually grow into Pygmalion making his Galatea to fall in love with, or Frankenstein making his monster, or the rabbi trying to make a servant. The makers are supposed to be a huge deal, inspiring Torment into their creations. The whole I Hate You Vampire Dad trope dialed to 11. Needs more attention. Many people I talk to don't even know that its in the core book!

    12) Better descriptions of some stuff. Going to the wastes - are Prometheans effectively ageless or not? Agg damage and losing limbs. General cleaning up of details.

    13) I actually like torment as is, so I have no complaints either way. Given the bump to frenzy and werewolf's likely bump to their rage, we'll probably see a boost in power for falling into Torment, though.

                    Humour: Torment and Disquiet; associated Element and Wasteland. General information about its temperament. Possibly do a split-page write-up, with the top half of a page detailing a Humour (temperament, Torment, and Disquiet) and the bottom half detailing an Element (Wasteland, possibly hazardous manifestations that can be tapped for healing: e.g., electricity for Fire).

                    Lineage: Bestowment, Disfigurements, details for the Generative Act, and stories about its Progenitor. Possibly an Affinity Transmutation set or a preferred Refinement that serves as its “ground state” (the way Stannum does for all non-Nuclear Prometheans in the core edition).

                    I see two or three sample Lineages being given for each Humour, with a strong implication that they’re just samples, and that players should be encouraged to devise their own, using the existing ones as guidelines — and yes, I’d use this to encourage “Lineages of one”. Since the relationship between Humours and Lineages is one-to-many, listing a Promethean’s Lineage on the character sheet would suffice.

                    As for the Transmutations being more like Embeds: my main motivation behind that suggestion was that the pricing scheme for Transmutations is heavily reliant on the escalating costs that GMC got rid of. Transmutations don’t have to have their dot ratings purged; but if they don’t, the only workable alternative (aside from forcing them into purchase chains like Disciplines) would be to price them much like Merits: e.g., a one-dot Transmutation costs one or two Experiences, while a five-dot Transmutation costs five or six Experiences. I really don’t see any other viable options.

                                                    The Lineages "need" to remain as presented. King's Raven nailed it: Lineages aren't one Demiurge's act, repeated ad nauseam, they are generations of creatures who recreate the original sin out of necessity and identity, and that act is both sacred and profane every time. Furthermore, each and every Demiurge created their primogenitor in an act of divine reverence. Or irreverence. The Demiurges played God, and won.

                                                    That's not to say they are right for every game. But they add more to the game than breaking down their mechanics and saying, "or some dude made you in his basement, whatever". Prometheans exist in a world where they can trace their existence back to an act looked upon by angels, where the end of Sodom and Gomorrah has a very likely explanation, and where mysteries and and discoveries carry the promise of transmutation of the soul. Lineages ground player characters in that world, where humours do not.

                                                    Yeah, Lineages can be restricting, but there are means to open that up, if a player doesn't want to play what's provided.

                                                    And the Weeping Angels predate the universe. They are creatures who can only live in Heisenbergian Uncertainty, to the point they can't even look at each other, and they feed on potential, which is not unlike what the Pyros crave. Pandorans, clearly.
