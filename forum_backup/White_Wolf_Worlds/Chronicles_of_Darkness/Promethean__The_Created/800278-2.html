                                                            Deviant doesn't approach what's happened to the characters the same way Promethean does, because it draws from different sources - the Cronenburg-esque body horror of "what's happened to me?" rather than Promethean's "what am I?" Where you can mistake Promethean's work-in-progress protagonists as supporting transhumanism, Deviant's central conflict is set up differently.

                                                            But at it's heart, Deviant says "changing humans into monsters against their will is wrong," and explores what happens to the people who are changed anyway. That's not very transhumanist.
