                                                            Originally posted by SoulGambit View Post
                                                            Re: Low Humanity Antagonists

                                                            That was removed from Vampire for a reason.

                                                            Azoth being a problem for high humanity Prometheans is part of the problem. Progressing Mechanically should almost never be at odds with Progressing Narratively. If you do that, then you get all the problems Promethean currently has.
                                                            The fact that it was removed from Vampire in the new edition, doesn't the negate the fact that it happened, and that the writers (and I assume, multitudes of players) found it to be an enjoyable gameplay element for at time. We shouldn't scoff at old ideas, simply for being old. I dont feel like Promethean needs the thematic weight of feeling a "Big Fish comming", but I found it thematically apropriate in Vampire.
                                                            DISCLAIMER: I never played Vampire, but I could imagine that the entire predatory aura thing, might become a grind in-game. Despite its perhaps lacking gameplay weight, I still found it thematically pleasing.

                                                            To suggest that a high Azoth score becomming a problem, is in themtic conflict with its Pilgrimage nearing its end, is missing out on the nature of Azoth. Azoth was never a gauge of the Prometheans experience with interacting with Humanity, nor his understanding thereof. Azoth is rather a gauge of his inner stores of refined Pyros, his inner fire that keeps him animate. It is this inner fire and his unnatural animation, that causes mortals to fall victim to Disquiet, and which flares out to scour the world around him, coursing the Wasteland. A Promethean can potentially (although not likely) have 10 Azoth, and be no closer to finding Humanity than the day he first opened his eyes.
                                                            A Promethean with high Humanity and high Azoth will still be at odds with Humanity, because although his understanding of the Human condition has improved tremendously over the cause of his Pilgrimage, our understanding of him have not improved by as much as a hairs thickness. We're still scared and hateful towards the zombie-man who just spend the greater part of two centuries traveling the world studying and admireing us, even as his understanding soon will allow to become one of us.
                                                            If high Humanity could cancel out the effects of Azoth, what that mechanic effectively says is; "Your knowledge and understanding of the Human condition alone, is enough to quell the unnatural fear and aversion they feel for you". There would be little need for a true Magnum Opus, because the Promethean would allready be able to reap the majority of the rewards of this project, solely by virtue of increasing his Humanity.
                                                            As it stands currently, Humanity does play a big part in the Prometheans abillity to become Human, although Azoth still burns. It'll burn - and by proxy invoke Disquiet - untill the moment that the transformative fire undoes the Prometheans unlife, and turns it into true life. Its a constant, likely ever increasing, reminder of all the Promethean has to gain from his Pilgrimage and all that he is trying to escape. If you allowed that feeling to be subverted by merely raising your Humanity, you'd slay one of the cornerstones of Promethean as it stands.
                                                            If you feel like this cornerstone is detrimental to Promethean as it stands, I cant fault you for it. Thats a matter of taste. However, I dont personally believe that it is detrimental.
