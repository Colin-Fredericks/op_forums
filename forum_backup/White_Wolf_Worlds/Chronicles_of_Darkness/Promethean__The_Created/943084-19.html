        Regardless of whether or not I know how to write antagonists (YMMV), alchemists were written before Heroes, and I didn't get either of them "wrong." You just don't like them.

        VII is an interesting case, I think. I don't know if there were threads about making them playable (wasn't here back then), but they are handled in a interesting way. I think someone with better Vampire acumen than me *and* with actual Alchemist write up available to compare it could do some interesting analysis on the case. If I remember correctly, the thing with VII - at least in 2nd ED core - was that there weren't many "facts" stated about them, judgemental or not - they were portrayed more like a *occurence* than anything normal; like a occult mystery. Their section is literally a string of contradictory, mysterious possible origins that give hints and ideas to use in your own games.
        Basically, they aren't really even a concrete thing; they are a *conceptual space* to be filled on your own. And thus, I can imagine that there wasn't much complaining about them, because everyone could just delve into ideas-soup of imagination stimulation (because that's what basically VII chapter is) and come out with whatever they are pleased with.

                                                Originally posted by ArcaneArts

                                                Not only am I here, but I am here in spite of what feels to me like a very large presence of spiteful presences. I have found my method of dealing with problems here. I have a system that involves levels of response, levels of ignoring, and final lines for just flat out blocking people. At no point have I determined that just because people feel I'm wrong and get very angry at me about it have I decided that I need to so securely sever my relationship with these forums that I need to be banned. If it becomes too much, I can take a break. If it stops feeling worth it, I can just leave. I do not demand that everyone shut up and listen to what I say, because I know very well that these are not my forums and that I do not have infinite power here. I try to live in some form of acceptable harmony with the people around me. Sometimes, that means speaking passionately about my viewpoints. Othertimes, that means listening and learning.

                                                At all times, it means hedging my bets on whether I am going to become a better person through the conversation who not only learns something about the game, but about other people and the world, or if the conversation, due to my involvement or others is just going to ruin my day or someone else's. Because these are not my forums, but it is my communication, and my communication is how I break the barriers between people and actually connect.

                                                As for Promethean not being mine, you are also correct. Promethean belongs to Promethean, and therefore deserves attempts at understanding and respect just as much as any person here. It is it's own thing first and foremost, both as a product and as a compilation of the efforts and experiences of the writers and developers of it, all of whom had a vision to communicate. Promethean's existence, in all honesty, has more value in it than any post here, because the creative process has time and money and sleepless hours and headaches and coffee and wine runs and more put into it-more than any one person. Even if you do not like what it is, it must be accorded a certain amount of respect first, by the sheer miracle of it's existence. Any finished product is worth more for that reasons. Even Genius, which I am currently reading and raving to high fucking hell about, deserves the respect of what it is, the culmination of dreams.

                                                For the moment, I'm going to ignore my usual philosophy and continue that train. The biggest problem with the theme hackers of Promethean has often not been the fact that they want to change the game utterly and completely-it has been that they have wanted to do so with no respect for the people who enjoy it or the effort that went into it in the first place. These topics have not merely been a desire for a different vision-they have been a platform for people to express hatred for the game, it's makers, and it's fans, who have zero respect for how this game, as it is, might have affected the lives of those who have read it. As a counter example, WyrdHamster, for all his problems with it, has always been very thoughtful of the fact that other people enjoy Promethean as it is. While he has been insistent upon his own treatment, he has been very willing to openly and honestly accept that people do not agree with him. It's a reason why his thread on the subject is actually moderately successful. But the majority are often nothing but engines of spite and malice. As we have stated before, small wonder it's a topic that raises hackles.

                                                So, no, Promethean's not mine. But it sure as fuck doesn't belong to anyone who's got an axe to grind, whose primary purpose is to hurt and to exclude, to persecute those who do not share in the message. No game belongs to a person like that.

                                                Finally, the deepest problem, the one that seems to get missed over and over again, is that the the whole synthesis of the game revolves around a theme you all don't like. It's not enough to merely change surface elements, or even craft elements, but deep structural and idiomatic pieces. To change a game like this into what you all want is to honestly just create another game. This is why general discussion on the subject leans towards finding another game. It's genuinely, deeply, too much. Promethean will fight anyone trying to do this at every turn, and the game you have at the end is not it's own distinct thing. You might as well slice up a person's brain and put them into a robot suit, regardless of whether they wanted to or not. It's crass-vulgar. Useless, really. Better to just find a different game. When we say that, we're thinking of the people asking as much as we are thinking about the game.

                                                Kindly take your attitude and burn it in a fire. We're not your fucking enemies here, however much you might want us to be.

                                                I am so very, very tired.
                                                OK, really trying to make myself clear without ruffling any feathers. Here is what I, and I can only hope I understand my fellow Hackers well enough to speak for them, have a problem with:

                                                THERE IS NO "OTHER GAME" THAT USES THE THEMES OF ALCHEMY AND ARTIFIAL LIFE. We don't have somewhere else to go and discus what that game should be. Most of the ideas we would like to use for that game are already a part of this one. I can only speak for myself, but I sincerely mean no harm to a game that clearly means a lot to you. I wish you well, and I apologize for having derailed this thread; it was just that PenDragon had so eloquently described the issues I have with the game, namely it's focus being a bit TOO narrow for my very small gaming group.

                                                So, with that in mind, and knowing that Promethean isn't for everyone, it would be nice if, when you see someone is working on a hack, you just walk away. The game you love will still be there, safe and sound and exactly as intended; we're just using some...leftover clay. An ember or two of stolen fire. To put it another way, think of us as trying to build our own creature, but we don't even have a proper lab or castle, and all the local graveyards are very well guarded. You are correct, we are NOT enemies, and I don't want to be. But please don't tell me to take anything of mine and burn it. My attitude, at least the one I hold in my heart, but may not convey well in text form, is one of wanting to work on a project in peace, but having seen far too many labs set ablaze. I really do think Onyx Path trapped lightning in a bottle, and I want some of that lightning for myself.
