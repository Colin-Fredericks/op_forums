                Dates be damned, it was nigh on twenty years since the last preview. I remember having taken in Changeling: the Dreaming, and, having come out of my distraction, realizing that the upcoming Aeon preview should have rightfully been Promethean previews. I wrote an angry posting on the AOL WW forum, about how we were being led along. Yes, the newly minted "old" World of Darkness was a delight, and a new and bold vision for role-playing games, but what of the classic Chronicles of Darkness they'd skirted around for years? Why hadn't there been a Promethean reboot in the so-called world, which was just an epic re-imagining of the other CoD lines? So many people were made happy when Demons got their Judeo-Christian angelology, so why couldn't we have the mad scientist transhumanist rebi whose Phlogistonic Orgone batteries had been hinted at since early Werewolf? I later corrected myself when someone pointed out they were in Ars Magica, back in the Lion Rampant days, but my point stood. Anyway, I'd sure could go for another preview.

                                                            So, Milestones. We got six universal Milestones, thematically linked to alchemical processes, but only projectio has a hard and fast action associated with it. Projectio means drinking from a River of Death, and is also part of the final ritual of the New Dawn (but not exclusive to it). Multiplicatio refers to an act of creation, but can mean the passing of knowledge and experience. Fermentatio seems to about Vitriol, and revolves around its transmutation and capital-T Transmutations. Ceratio means connecting with other Prometheans, and through them, to the world. Separatio is about understanding the gulf between the world and the Promethean. Sublimato involves apprehending the Refinements and their place, and maybe the greater Pilgrimage as a whole. All are revealed through Azothic Memory, except for the Extempore, who have no direct connection to it, perhaps as a result of having no demiurge.

                                                            There are then prescribed Milestones. These are the prizes of Roles and Refinements, which will describe a few examples in the text. Instinctive Milestones are more freeform, descending from the Five Questions asked at character creation (as in the GMC character creation rules, but likely altered for the Created), and are much more personal. No word on whether Extempore have the instinct in question.

                                                            All in all, I don't think they've changed so much as they have been anchored to more concrete character choices and explicitly so. With Roles in the mix, this should work out for Storytellers looking for more guidance in developing Milestones. I think we're still in the dark regarding the larger framework of how the Pilgrimage has evolved, whether it's how the new trait works, how Roles divide of Refinements, or how Vitriol figures in the Beats and Experiences scheme.

                                                            Sidenote: creating a Promethean is no longer a required Milestone, meaning that the balance of Prometheans and Pandorans is no longer as clear. The little creeps probably persist through Firestorms and Centimani rituals and alchemical mishaps, but it seems less likely to happen as risk of the Pilgrimage itself.

                                                            LFP: Foreign Bodies (Deviant: the Renegades)
