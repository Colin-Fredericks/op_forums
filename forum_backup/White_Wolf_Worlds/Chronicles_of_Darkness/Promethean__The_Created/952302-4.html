        On Fuel: an unanswered question is how much Fuel a given character gets for his Mods, how necessary Fuel is (that is, when is "0" an appropriate cost); and how much Fuel each Gadget gets.

        Note also that another way that Mad Science is drawing inspiration from Hunter is that it's not trying to present a unified metaphysics to explain everything -- heck, that would be directly contrary to its central idea, that there isn't a unified metaphysics, only the illusion of one. That's why I never specify the nature of Unobtainium and Fuel, only their relevant properties. And that's why Mods and Gadgets probably feel a lot like Hunter's Endowments, with more of a focus on what they do than on how they do it.

        Mind you, within the context of a Breakthrough, you can have a unified metaphysics and answers as to why things work as well as what things do. To that extent, each Breakthrough extends the illusion of a sane universe at the cost of complicating the model one uses to understand it. But each Breakthrough also puts more of a strain on that illusion: in a general sense, every exception you have to account for hurts further efforts to maintain credulity. And what happens when you have multiple Breakthroughs that flat-out contradict each other?

        I'm thinking that the more Breakthroughs a Mad Scientist has achieved, the more of a penalty he suffers on further Breakthrough-related Breaking Points. Mind you, he won't suffer any Breaking Points for the weirdness of phenomena that are covered by those Breakthroughs that he's already achieved; his perspective has already adjusted to account for them (although he's still subject to Breaking Points when bad things happen pertaining to Breakthrough phenomena). But his next Breakthrough? Those Mind-Blowing Clues are more likely to blow his mind -- especially if the new Breakthrough's metaphysics clash with what he's already accepted. Finding evidence that contradictory Breakthroughs are both valid is not good for one's mental health.

            Another effect of there not being a unified metaphysics is that Breakthroughs can tap into the metaphysics of other gamelines. I've already indicated this when I suggested that a suitable Breakthrough could allow a Mad Scientist to tap into Pyros; I'm figuring that a Mad Science Breakthrough of "Pyros Alchemy" could replace Promethean 2e's Alchemist rules, and that the Genitive Act could be one of that Breakthrough's Feats. That is, if you want a Mad Scientist to create a Frankenstein monster a la Promethean, you can do it. And this by no means precludes a Mad Scientist with a Breakthrough unrelated to Pyros from crafting a created being, under different metaphysics; but that created being wouldn't be a Promethean.

            I'm pretty sure that a similar logic will apply to Deviant, once that game is out: that is, applying Mods to a subject runs the risk of bestowing a Breaktrough-appropriate template on said subject. Or perhaps it's possible to choose a Feat centered around deliberately bestowing a template. This would likely have a high Unobtainium cost and/or a serious Drawback, especially if you're bestowing a greater template; and it depends on the metaphysics allowing it.

            For instance, a Mad Scientist who develops a Breakthrough involving sublime symbols that define reality could develop Innovations that tap into Mage's metaphysics; but (for flavor reasons) they'd probably do so from a techgnostic angle, and the Feats would be akin to individual spells; the Mad Scientist would not become a mage. And although he might choose to research how to bestow the Mage template, such an effort would likely be doomed to failure, as mages haven't been able to figure out how to do so. And even if he did manage it, there's the matter of the mandatory Drawback: you'd probably get something like a False Awakening from Night Horrors: Unbidden.

            Likewise, I could see a Mad Scientist discovering God-Machine Infrastructure and Investigating it, and eventually achieving a Breakthrough that lets him develop Embeds and Exploits. He might even be able to figure out how to use Infrastructure to create a new Angel, probably an Exile; but he probably wouldn't be able to turn a human into a Demon. Probably. (Possibility: after Investigating the phenomenon of Covers, he figures out a way to reverse-engineer the process, and arranges for a human to take on a Cover; and further research allows him to treat a Demonic Form as a possible Cover, leading to a sort of "inverted demon". Of course, these things might require some Advanced Breakthroughs, and the Unobtainium costs for implementing the Innovations are likely to be prohibitive.)

            Ultimately, nothing is impossible for Mad Science, if the scientist is willing and able to pay the price. In theory, at least; in practice, it's limited to achieved Breakthroughs; and some costs cannot be paid. Put another way, the Storyteller has the tools of Breakthroughs and Unobtainium (as well as the good old-fashioned nerf-bat) to deny a Mad Scientist access to anything he deems excessive.

                        Interesting idea. My inclination would be to have the creation of a new Skill be something that can happen after a Breakthrough has been thoroughly explored — that is, once you've developed a number of Innovations for the Breakthrough, you can “trade them in” for a new Breakthrough-specific Skill, called a Field.

                        Think of it this way: a Field has tremendous flexibility that comes from having a largely comprehensive grasp of the Breakthrough; it is to the Innovations that the scientist starts out with as a Mage Arcanum is to a collection of spells, letting you devise new Innovations on the fly instead of having to painstakingly develop them one by one.

                        In keeping with the “Mad” part of Mad Science, this doesn't come without a price. Thing is, I'm not sure what that should be: achieving a Breakthrough already has the “your perspective is forever altered” thing. My gut instinct is to make this a “difference of degree” thing, where the altered perspective that was acquired with the Breakthrough intensifies to the point that it now counts as a Persistent Condition, a mental counterpart to the Drawbacks that Mods have.

                                                        A Few Thoughts

                                                        I have been watching this with great interest. I have a few ideas of my own on how to incorporate Occult Science (great term btw - more evocative than simply "mad" science) into my games. My ideas are largely merit based (the ability to perform Occult Science would essentially be an Endowment or Style). My explanation for what allows this is similar to yours, its not like a mage using his special powers to override reality, but more a person who discovers a fundamental aspect of the universe.

                                                        A few other observations

                                                        I like tying the Breakthrough mechanic to the Investigation system. whenever possible, I like hitching new mechanics to pre-existing framework. Its a lot easier to work with something you already know than to try to spin a new system whole - cloth.

                                                        For "Unobtanium", might I suggest re-purposing the word "Catalyst"? "Unobtanium has a pulpy feel that is a little less at home in the supernatural mystery/horror setting of CofD.

                                                        Re: Possible Breakthroughs
                                                        Here are few ideas that might work - The idea have in mind for breakthroughs are things that are theoretically possible (or slightly outside of possible), but currently beyond the grasp of Known Science.

                                                        Cybernetics: sophisticated mechanisms that can be grafted to living tissue to restore lost function or grant enhanced capabilities.
                                                        Leonization: Reversing or Stalling the Aging process, potentially granting greatly extended lifespans (or possibly immortality).
                                                        Matter/Energy Conversion: converting energy into matter and back again, cornerstone of replication and teleportation technologies.
                                                        Transferable Consciousness: Converting the mind into format that can be digitally duplicated, saved or transferred to different media.
                                                        True VR: Large scale artificially constructed environments that offer full sensory immersion.

                                                        Re:Possible Source
                                                        I like that this takes a bit from the "Gutter Magic" ruleset from Witchfinder. Here are some ideas of what could be considered Source.

                                                        Calibration: The Innovation requires a very specific set of programming, adjustment or tuning before it can function.This process is frequently lengthy and delicate.
                                                        Conjunction: the energy required is generated by specific temporal or astrological events. the Innovation can only be engaged at certain times.
                                                        Correspondence: The energy requiredis generated at a particular location.
                                                        Esoterica: Reagents harvested and distilled from the physical matter of Cryptids and other exotic organisms. Everything from vampire blood to unicorn horn falls into this category.
                                                        Mundane: The Innovation runs on common fuel sources, such as electricity or petroleum. Often requires requires exceptional amounts of mundane fuel to properly function.
                                                        Quanta: the most common energy source, drawn from the faint EM field surrounding most individuals. Often a higher degree of energy required to generate even modest effect. (This would be a standard Willpower cost)


                                                        The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
                                                        Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
                                                        The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
                                                        Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
                                                        Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

                                                            A catalyst is something that enables a chemical reaction without being consumed or changed by the reaction, leaving it ready to be used over and over again. It's pretty much the opposite of what Unobtainium is, where the whole point is that you use it up whenever you make something that's lasting and then have to go out and acquire more of it. It's similar to Source, except that it needs to be a lot harder to come by; that's why I compare it to this like Arcane Experience, dots of Willpower or your power stat, etc.: things that do not regenerate if you spend them, that only come back with effort on your part. Hopefully effort that translates into stories, which is why I'd generally prefer something more interesting and less generic than “spend an Experience” as Unobtainium.

                                                            I personally prefer calling it “Unobtainium” because that term is incredibly descriptive and accurate: it's something hard to obtain. But if another term is needed, maybe something like “Rare Source” would work, emphasizing its similarity to Source as well as highlighting its key difference.

                                                            I could maybe see Breakthroughs as Merits; but only for the purpose of character creation. After character creation, the only way to acquire them is through the Investigation system as previously outlined (which doesn't involve the use of Experiences, on the basis that accumulating Clues serves the same purpose that spending some sort of Experience-like resource does, and which in turn is why i ditched the original Insight notion); and even the ones you get during character creation assume that you did the requisite investigation during your backstory. Nobody ever just has Breakthroughs.

                                                            Also, I prefer calling it Mad Science rather than Occult Science because the latter emphasizes only the notion that it goes beyond Known Science, whereas the former focuses on the fact that the more you try to make sense of it, the less sense it's going to make.
