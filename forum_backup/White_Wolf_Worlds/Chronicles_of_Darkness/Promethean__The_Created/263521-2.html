            OK, so:

            1) Refinements in Promethean 2nd Edition = the 5 from the core + the 4 from Magnum Opus + Phosphorum (want to know what they're about? Go read "The Lesson of the Moth" by Don Marquis. It's short.)

            2) Centimanus isn't precisely a Refinement. You can't follow the Pilgrimage while you're a Centimanus.

            3) Sure, you can choose not to follow the Pilgrimage, even while you're on a real Refinement. You pick a Role you like, you stick with it. Maybe you pick the other Roles in that Refinement, even if they're not part of your Pilgrimage, and you master them. There are advantages and disadvantages either way.

            4) The Great Work makes you human. That's the goal. Lead in gold. The New Dawn. Promethean takes (and always has taken) the explicit view that this is a good thing, and the desire driving the Created, though they may stumble along the way. I, personally, feel the game would suffer if we spent a lot of time saying, "the game is about this, unless you don't want it to be, in which case whatever, have them turn into geese at the end, doesn't matter, robots."

            Obviously, you can do whatever you want with the game, and I encourage that. But I'm not devoting word count to variants when I barely have enough space to cover what I want to cover. Maybe in a sourcebook, but I'd rather do a Night Horrors book first.

                                                    Sounds as though Roles are channels within each Refinement, steps on its path. Not exactly ranked, but perhaps fulfilling their potential is one of the universal milestones mentioned elsewhere. Advantages and dis probably does refer to "hiding", sort of like Cover, the disadvantage meaning that if you do something too out-of-character, you risk deepened Disquiet. People got used to you fitting their stereotype, and then you showed your "true colors". You were really a "liberal", or a "tourist", or "full of airs". One of those hideous terms used to keep the gates of whatever in-group the Promethean has infiltrated. The advantage is that the Promethean can probably walk undisturbed so long as he toes the line, or carries the flag. These are the recent converts, the gatekeepers, and the aspiring stars of whatever movement.

                                                    For example, Aurum might have a "Congregant" Role. The Congregant joins a church or a temple. She not only follows the strictures, but she enforces them as least as fiercely as any fellow religionist. She garners a reputation as a zealot, but few doubt her faith, and in the time afforded to her, she is appointed an elder. All this time, she provokes next to no Disquiet, or she is able to refocus it on the lapsed and dissident. But one day she slips. She misses a service for some dubious reason, and it's enough to put a crack in her facade. Whispers of her vices begin to circulate, doubts emerge, and the congregation is moved to reclaim itself from her fanaticism, or doubles down on its own orthodoxy in order to liberalize her. Once the in-group has spoken, there's no winning its trust back.

                                                    Still, it's like reading tea leaves. Get to open dev, Matt!
