    Originally posted by MugaSofer View Post
    Yeah. If those elements change (as opposed to the dicepools etc.) then ... well, I hope they have very good reasons for it.
    The flat experience costs in GMC does present a challenge for this, methinks. Previously the reason why you didn't get a 5 dot Transmutation right off the bat was because for each level within a School that you skipped, there would be an experience penalty. Now that all powers cost the same, what's to stop someone from grabbing a powerful Transmutation like Flesh to Stone with their first purchase after chargen?

    Since a big theme in Promethean is learning and understanding, I actually think that more "trees" (like the tech trees in an RTS or Twilight Imperium) within each School would work well. They have to gain a certain amount of understanding before applying bigger principles, and each Transmutation could represent a greater understanding of strength or Pyros or the supernatural or whatever.


    I'll also be very interested to see how Werewolf's Gifts work in 2e. I think that will give us an idea of just how radical a transformation we could expect.

            My issues with Promethean mainly come from having to remember all the various rules for Azoth, Torment and what causes it, the methods of regenerating Pyros etc... I think it could be streamlined in a new Chronicle Book... I don't mean make it more rules-lite, but making the crunch snappier and easier to remember rather than having a rule for everything kind of thing. I find Promethean and Werewolf to be the crunchiest of the nWoD games though YMMV of course.
            I don't believe Disquiet really needs to be amped down... It only becomes an issue at higher levels of Azoth where it can stretch out to entire cities. If I wanted to play Promethean myself I think the main pull would be having the game centre on the family of the Throng... Outside of the support of others, as the Promethean's Azoth grows as they 'try to be real', they're going to be walking the roads. Of course some kind of support for those Prometheans on 'road trip' games would be cool also.
