                                                    Originally posted by atamajakki View Post

                                                    While I agree with you on point 2, it does fit Promethean somewhat well; there are so few Created that no one city is bound to have a ton of them, and dedicating your city writeups entirely to either the mundane world or non-Created actors does seem somewhat wasteful.
                                                    That's pretty much how I feel too. I think the write ups are neat, and there are some interesting plot hooks there, but they just don't have the same meat that I love from the other 2e corebooks. They are in no way bad... But they feel half formed (irony that). I'd rather have dropped three or four of them to give Tokyo and some of the other iconic cities some more development.

                                                    EDIT: Mythology, that's what the other books have that I feel is lacking in these settings. Every other Tokyo entry adds a substantial twist on the setting. While Tokyo's neat here, it feels like it's a bit one note. Same thing with the other settings. They offer great, interesting plot hooks and reasons to go there, but they lack the mythical, mysterious feeling that you get from Montreal in Vampire, London in Mage, Vancouver in Beast, and all three settings in The Pack.

                                                    So they are good, but they missed the mark on what made the settings in the other lines awesome. Hopefully when we get some more Promethean supplements, another stab at settings can be taken.
