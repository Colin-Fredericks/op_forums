        Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
        I was hoping about giving one Construct-related Bestowment if there would be two per character. But in PtC 2E character can have only one.

        Then I remembered that in 1E Strange Alchemies Constructs did get their special powers as third kind Bestowments. Maybe go this way again, in 2E fan rules?
        That could work: Construct Bestowments are a special type that you get in addition to your Lineage Bestowment, but only if you're a Construct. Most Unfleshed are Constructs; the few that aren't are Androids: assembled out of cloned or synthesized body parts rather than parts harvested from corpses. (Note: I'm intending to synthesize Magnum Opus' Constructs and Saturnine Night's Unfleshed into a unified set of rules for Prometheans that are made out of materials other than flesh.)

        Every Construct Bestowment has a Drawback as well as a benefit.

        Sample Construct Bestowments
        Hard Body
        Your body is made of stone or metal.
        Benefits: You have Armor 2/2, and your unarmed attacks do Lethal damage as if you were armed.
        Drawback: Your basic Speed is 3, not 5.

        Wooden Frame
        You're made of wood or some other lightweight and flammable material such as paper, plastic, or wax.
        Benefit: Bashing damage is halved.
        Drawback: fire causes aggravated damage.

        To do:
        • The Generative Act
        • Pyros, Vitriol, and the Constructed Body

        The Generative Act:
        • Turning a statue into a Promethean should be a lot harder than turning a robot into a Promethean, because the Pyros has to do more work: a statue doesn't have articulated limbs, motors to move them, or a power supply to drive them; it doesn't have sensors to observe the environment or a digital brain to process the observations. And it doesn't have a circulatory system to distribute Azothic-charged fluid throughout the body. All of these are things that Pyros can compensate for; but the more it needs to compensate for, the harder the generative act is. Magnum Opus suggests a -4 penalty to the generative act if the body is made of anything but flesh; I'd suggest mitigating or eliminating this penalty the more machine-like the body is, for the reasons stated above.

        Pyros, Vitriol, and the Constructed Body:
        • Arguably, constructed Prometheans shouldn't have any natural healing ability: healing should require either a mechanic or an expenditure of Pyros. Likewise, Magnum Opus suggests that a construct needs to expend a point of Pyros every day to remain animate, canceling out the free point of Pyros that a Promethean generates every day; and it suggests that the construct's lack of a circulatory system means that it's easier to steal its Vitriol. It also mentions that hiding a construct's Disfigurements is beyond the Divine Flame's capacity, though Saturnine Night's Unfleshed rules contradict this.

                    Athanors, 1e-style, are a Z-Splat that provides a pair of Traits that can be improved using Vitriol; a pool of Reagent that can be spent to mitigate various difficulties Prometheans encounter; and a Boon that provides a small Lineage-based power that can be traded in for a thematically appropriate non-supernatural benefit when the New Dawn is achieved. For 2e purposes, I'm reassigning each of these to some other aspect of the character: the Affinity Traits become part of the Roles; the Reagent pool becomes a type of (2e-style) Athanor that acts as an augmented version of the Vitriol Fount; and the Boons become an extension of a reimagined Bestowment system. In order:

                    Role Traits
                    Each Role has two traits associated with it. While you're in that role, you may spend Vitriol Experiences to improve those Traits.

                    I have not yet looked at he Roles with this in mind; any suggestions as to which Traits should be associated with which Roles are welcome.

                    Vitriol Fount
                    Like the Vitriol Founts presented in 2e, you can extract Vitriol from this version of the Vitriol Fount. However, you choose exactly how much is in there (by spending one Vitriol for each dot that's in there); it doesn't regenerate (if you want more in there, invest more Vitriol); and it generates a new “fuel stat” called Reagent that behaves according to the Reagent rules found in 1e's Athanors chapter.

                    Boons
                    Boons are now joined with Bestowments, in that Bestowments are now a type of Boons. You get the option to purchase a Boon at Azoth 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9; at Azoth 1, the Boon must be a Lineage-appropriate Bestowment; but you get it automatically. Later “Boon slots” cost one Vitriol Experience each to fill if the Boon is Lineage-appropriate; two otherwise. When you achieve the New Dawn, you may Redeem your Boons for thematically related non-supernatural benefits (see 1e's Redeemed Boons for examples). Reagent costs for Boons are recast as Pyros costs.

                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                        Automatons are “Azoth 0 Prometheans”. They serve as an expansion 1e's Clones, and are defined by their lack of free will (for which you'll find guidelines in P1e under “Clones”). Automatons have Lineages and Bestowments/Boons, but they have Integrity instead of Pilgrimage, and they lack Refinements, Roles, and Transmutations. 1e-style Clones are Androids (flesh-made Unfleshed) with at least a dot in Famous Face.

                        Automatons can be thought of as half-completed Prometheans: the body is already prepared, all that's needed is immersion in the appropriate element and investment of a dot of Azoth. Do that, and the Automaton is upgraded to a Promethean.

                                    Originally posted by Arcanist View Post
                                    Would Reagent be full Vitriol Experiences or just Vitriol Beats? Seems a little weighty to have them be full Experiences, especially since Pandorans can burn Beats as Pyros.
                                    It would indeed be Beats:
                                    Once a point of Vitriol is placed into the Athanor, it creates a pool of temporary points (similar to a character's pool of Willpower points) that can be spent for various effects. These points are called Reagents, because they effect change. Each Vitriol point that is “banked” into the Athanor increases the Reagent pool by one point. (It might be helpful to view Vitriol points that are deposited into the Athanor as dots, which allow for Reagent points.)
                                    — PtC1e, p.261

                                    For clarity's sake: Reagent can never be spent faster than a point per turn, and it recovers at a rate of a point per day. (Although I'm debating saying that you can spend an additional number of Reagent per turn equal to the number of Vitriol Experiences that you've banked in the Vitriol Fount.)

                                    Originally posted by Arcanist View Post
                                    Maybe Reagent could be used to make the effects of a Distillation in the Refinement's Transmutations permanent, with Storyteller discretion?
                                    Mmm… no. It's already been established that access to an Alembic costs two Vitriol Experiences; making a Distillations effect permanent should be harder to pull off, not easier. Reagent regenerates on its own, unlike Vitriol. Its existing uses are to temporarily boost your supernatural resistance by one, to reduce a Disquiet check against you by one die, to enhance your resistance against a Torment check by one die, to hide your Disfigurements for a turn, or to halve your Azothic Radiance for a scene. Its effects are supposed to be temporary. Heck, even the two uses that require spending a Reagent dot (i.e., a Vitriol Beat) — forcing a Pandoran into Dormancy and forcing a Promethean out of Torment — aren't what I'd call permanent.
