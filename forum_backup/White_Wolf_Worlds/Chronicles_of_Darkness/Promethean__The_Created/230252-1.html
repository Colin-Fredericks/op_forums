            Originally posted by bwgustaf View Post
            I don't mean transhuman i meant not becoming a "one human fits all" redeemed template and actually making it a choice based upon the promethean
            That’s what Athanors are for. That said, I’d prefer that the “final Azoth roll” be recast as an extended action where the newly-Redeemed character struggles over several weeks to regain and integrate his memories of his former life as a Promethean (either directly or through the creation of “mortal memories” that approximate his former experiences); that way, every Redeemed spends some time early on with amnesia, and many (i.e., the ones who are unable to achieve sufficient successes before hitting the limit of how far an extended action can take you) end up as permanent amnesiacs — as opposed to the current system, where you’re only in danger of permanent amnesia if you Redeem with Azoth 1–2 or so, and your amnesia is pretty much guaranteed to clear up in under a month (if you ever suffer any amnesia in the first place) if your final Azoth is much higher than that.

            Meanwhile, Exceptional Successes during this acclimation process should net you special rewards, whether or not you ever regain your memories. I’ve described elsewhere my thoughts on what a Hunter: the Vigil Second Edition should be like, and it included some optional “heroic mortal” Edges designed to stay within the realm of “being human” while at the same time pushing the hunter toward being “cream of the crop” types; I think those same Edges (Boosts to attributes, skills, or advantages; new uses for Willpower; immunities to the likes of Lunacy, the Lie, and/or Disquiet; etc.) could work well as exceptional success “bonuses” for a Redeemed mortal’s acclimation process.

            I’d also consider allowing the acclimation process to do somethung similar to an Unchained patching together a Cover, but without the need to take bits of peoples’ lives from them: when the Redeemed is done, he may not only have memories of a full mortal life (either replacing or in tandem with his Promethean memories); but the friends and neighbors that he remembers may well remember him, too.

            Originally posted by bwgustaf View Post
            and not simply "i've completely forgotten i was monster! let's go celebrate by getting me turned into a different kind of monster!"
            Frankly, anyone who thinks that “get Embraced”/“Awaken”/“become a changeling”/etc. is the logical next step for a Redeemed former-Promethean has pretty much missed the point of Promethean. Which is not to say that there’s no place for such things; but even “Promethean to mortal to mage” should be played as something of a tragedy.

            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
