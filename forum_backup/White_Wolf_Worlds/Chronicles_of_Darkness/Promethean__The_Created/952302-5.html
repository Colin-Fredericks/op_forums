                                            Alright, here's one of the ideas I have on hand.


                                            The Möbius Woods: Discovered originally by a group of national park rangers while looking for missing campers. The Möbius Woods are a spacial phenomenon where by walking through a specific path through the forest one enters an unexplored section of the forest that doubles back upon itself like a Möbius Strip. The Stip, once entered, appears at first to be impossible to leave. However, when the forest rangers eventually set up camp for the night and fell asleep (deciding that staying in one place and trying to figure out what's going on was better than blindly trying to escape) they awoke to find themselves and their belongings back in the national park. They would have believed that their experience was just a bad dream or the result of high anxiety from their search, but luckily for the scientific community one of the rangers had recorded one of their attempts to escape the Möbius Strip and upon viewing it again realized that they had indeed been trapped the previous day. Since that time only one team of scientists led by a field researcher from Null Mysteriis and an agent from Taskforce: VALKYRIE have managed to re-enter the Möbius Woods, and from their research the scientific community has been able to learn many things about the Stip and the flora and fauna found there.
                                            ~ Properties
                                            1. The Stip appears to have the same properties as conventional reality, and tests on the air, soil, and plant life all appear to be made of the same elements as their counterparts outside of the Strip. The same cannot be said for the fauna though.
                                            2. All fauna within the Stip appear to have evolved in a manner in which none have to sleep. All attempts to induce sleep, via drugs and tranquilizers, results in the death of the specimens (once the body falls asleep, all functions cease and cannot restart even when assisted.)
                                            3. There are no predators within the Strip, all fauna appear to have evolved without any natural predators, and their reproductive cycles seem to have evolved to ensure that the population doesn't balloon out of control.
                                            4. Currently the only known way to exit the Strip is by falling unconscious. Whether naturally or assisted all test subjects appear to blink out of existence until they are no longer within the Strip. Observations of the subjects reappearing back in the national park say that the same effect happened in reverse.
                                            5. Prolonged stay within the Strip is extremely hazardous for a human's psyche. The final experiment made before the VALKYRIE agent lost his faculties and started firing upon any flora, fauna, or field researchers present while shouting about their eyes and how they're all the same was an experiment testing the psychological effects in the human mind by exploring the Strip and generally living within it. In hindsight this was probably not the best choice on the team leaders part after researchers began complaining about a growing anxiety they couldn't quite explain the longer they stayed within the Strip, but casualties were to a minimum so this project was declared an overall success.
                                            ~ Conclusion: The Möbius Woods may have potentially hazardous psychological effects upon humans who stay for too long within the Strip, but the unique flora, fauna, and spacial properties are considered important enough for a second field research team to be sent in the near future.

                                            [Side note scribbled at the bottom: The missing campers that the park rangers were looking for were never found and presumed dead. No trace of them has been found in the Park or the Strip, but there was evidence hinting that they went down the path that led to the Strip's entrance.]

                                                Got another one, to help increase the sample size. It's definitely of a larger scope than the previous and draws heavy inspiration from the aesthetic of both the recent Dr. Strange movie and Monte Cook's Invisible Sun game.

                                                Reflection: Originally discovered by a Cell of Hunters attempting to stop a Coven of Witches from performing some unknown rite in Chicago. The Cell launched their attack just as the rite concluded and witnessed the cornerstone of the rite, a tall ornate mirror, appear to crack and shatter inwards. This revealed the intention of the rite, to open a portal to some other plane of reality. Before the leader of the Coven could step through said portal they were gunned down by the Cell, and the remaining survivors of the Coven were taken into custody by Taskforce: VALKYRIE. The mirror portal remained though, even though it closed upon the Coven leader's death the mirror itself retained it power as a conduit to another plane. Although the mirror remains in VALKYRIE custody as well, a team of scientists have been given permission by the US government to begin examining and experimenting with the portal under strict supervision and armed guard by VALKYRIE.
                                                The following report documents their findings.
                                                ~ Propeties
                                                1. The conduit, once transformed by the rouge occultists, appears to be a perminent portal to what we are currently calling "Reflection Space." The portal remains closed until some physical force from our side of the portal smashes into the cracked surface of the closed portal with enough force to break it. Once open, the portal remains that way for exactly 30 seconds before, for reasons yet unclear, the portal closes and the mirrors surface reforms to exactly how it was before it was shattered.
                                                2. The portal can be opened from the other side, but the force that's required to open the portal is three times as much. Our expedition team had a bit of a panic when they first tried to return, but luckily our VALKYRIE field operative was able to bring enough firepower to bear in order to open it from the other side. That being said, we don't recommend using firearms to open the portal from the Reflection Space side as we suffered a neadless casualty from one of the stray bullets.
                                                3. Reflection Space isn't naturally environmentally hazardous towards humans. The dimension as been commonly referred to by field researchers as, and I quote, "M. C. Escher's wet dream" but other than strange and often disturbing spacial anomalies it appears to be relatively unhostile towards human life. However...
                                                4. Reflection Space is inhabited by beings who can be quite hostile towards humans. These beings range from animalistic to intelligent, generally appear to have some sort of routine or function within Reflection Space, appear to be abstract reflections of places and concepts from Conventional Reality, and almost all react negatively when interacting with what they view as "intruders." Those entities that appeared capable of reason and sympathy for the field researchers simply indicated that they should leave immediately, before the "Knights" arrive to prevent the field researchers from "warping the Realm any further." Suffice to say, the field leader declared the away mission concluded shortly afterwards.
                                                5. Some portion of the population of Reflection Space appear to have an interest in escaping from Reflection Space into Conventional Reality. These beings tried ambushing the field research team as they were on route back to the portal. These creatures exhibited supernatural abilities relating to Reflection Space, and more horrifyingly towards adopting the appearance and mannerisms of the field research team. This led to a pitched fire fight, several more casualties on both sides, and the predicament where two identical members of the field research team came back through the portal. Both individuals were apprehended and quarentened, and after about a weeks time the imposter's supernatural mimicry wore off. After that, the intruded rapidly began to expire and perished only three days later. At this time, the government has no future plans to explore Reflection Space.
                                                ~ Conclusion: Due to the beings of Reflection Space being able to mimic humans to a degree that fools most scientific means, Taskforce: VALKYRIE has perminently locked away the conduit to Reflection Space as a matter of national security. However, the potential for scientific discovery involving Reflection Space remains a great temptation amongst the esoteric community. To this day there are still attempts to recreate the process of creating a conduit to Reflection Space, although as to date there have been no successes.

                                                    So, it's been over a year since this thread was updated. I like Dusksage 's suggestions, save for one thing: there's nothing about what kinds of Breakthroughs these pockets of weirdness might lead to.

                                                    In other news: I'm really, really looking forward to Deviant, as the more I learn about it the more its concepts seem to be kin to the concepts that we've been developing here. If I had to tie Mad Science to a single gameline, Deviant would be it. For instance, the way Deviant attempts to simplify and streamline (e.g., replacing Virtue, Vice, and Integrity with Conviction and Loyalty) is similar to how this project attempts to keep the mechanics focused and lightweight; the notion of Variations incorporating Scars directly mirrors how all Innovations have Drawbacks; the way that Deviant's Variations are going to go all in on the toolkit approach matches this project's aim to be as open to as broad a range of possible Innovations as possible; and so on. Even the tendency of Mad Science to drive those who study it to madness tends to echo the death spiral that the Remade find themselves in, though the mechanics are quite different.

                                                    If anything's missing from Mad Science (besides specifics), it's probably insufficient emphasis on obsession that drives the scientist to pursue more and more Breakthroughs and Innovations even after she realizes that it's not healthy to do so. In that regard, mad scientists are a bit like the Awakened in that they're addicted to “mysteries” — or rather, they find it harder and harder not to Investigate weird phenomena the more Breakthroughs they've had.

                                                    Rather than altering the Integrity stat (even to the extent of introducing something akin to Hunter 2e's Code), I'd rather tie such a mechanic to the inventor's Breakthroughs, much like her immunity to further supernaturally induced Breaking Points comes from what Breakthroughs she has achieved. In fact, the tendency toward obsession is essentially the “Drawback” that comes with that immunity: phenomena that violate a Breakthrough ought to be harder to ignore.

                                                    Basically, I'd like to keep the character traits for a “mad scientist” down to a list of Breakthroughs and their Innovations, plus whatever mental Conditions they've acquired due to their repeated exposure to Breaking Points.

                                                    How about this: uncovering a Mind-blowing Clue counts as satisfying a Vice: in addition to the Breaking Point, you also regain a point of Willpower. Meanwhile, achieving a Breakthrough counts as satisfying a Virtue: you regain all of your Willpower.

                                                        Another breakthrough I just had:

                                                        On the character sheet, Breakthroughs are represented by a section called “Mad Sciences”. Each entry on this list is a Skill — specifically, a variant on the Science Skill that works just like the Science Skill, except that it works for matters covered by the Breakthrough. The basic Science Skill can't be used for Breakthroughs, and the Mad Science Skills can only be used for their respective Breakthroughs. (Exception: Mind-blowing Clues that lead to a Breakthrough can be Uncovered using the standard Science Skill or by the appropriate Mad Science Skill; but in the latter case, they don't count as Mind-blowing.)

                                                        As corollary to this, you cannot achieve a Breakthrough without spending enough Experiences to buy the first dot in the appropriate Mad Science Skill, as well as having Uncovered enough Mind-Blowing Clues to successfully Uncover the Truth about the Breakthrough. Alternately, achieving the Breakthrough lets you use the Mad Science Untrained until you spend enough Experiences for the first dot. Otherwise, Mad Sciences cannot be used Untrained.

                                                        Innovations show up on the character sheet as “Mad Specialties”: Crafts Specialties for Devices, and Medicine Specialties for Mods. There might be other kinds as well (e.g., Computer Specialties for Innovative software; possibly Brawling Specialties for Innovative fighting techniques). You cannot take a Mad Specialty without at least one dot in the appropriate Mad Science, as you need to Uncover Clues using that Mad Science in order to “unlock” a given Innovation. Mad Specialties differ from regular ones in that they don't improve your dice pool; they “merely” let you do something with that Skill that you otherwise wouldn't be able to do.

                                                        Thoughts?
