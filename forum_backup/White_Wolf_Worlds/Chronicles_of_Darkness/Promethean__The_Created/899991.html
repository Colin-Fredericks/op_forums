    Transmutations

    05-26-2016, 09:52 AM
    Preview here: http://theonyxpath.com/prometheantransmutations/

    So what do you guys think? I feel like it'll be really interesting, especially for Transmutations like Vitality where everything seemed to be upgrades of the first 2-3 powers, or Alchemicus where many powers weren't really interesting as a single power.



    So let's try and see what Alembics could go in each Transmutation from the first ed. Corebook

    Alchemicus:
    -Perfecting (Master's Tool, Fortification, ???)
    -Changing (Transformation, ???, Resize)
    -Destroying (Dissolve, Degradation, Flesh to Stone)
    -Rebuilding (Identification, Alteration, Spark of Life)

    Corporeum:
    -Dexterity (Swift feet, uncanny dex, Rarified Grace)
    -Holistic Body (Autonomic Control, ??? Human Flesh)
    -Regeneration (Regeneration, Rejuvenation, ???)
    -Reflexes (Ingrained Reflexes, Perfected Reflexes, Serpent's Strike)

    Deception:
    -Chameleon (Chameleon Skin, Move Silently, Vitreous Humor)
    -Doppleganger (Color of Man, Mimic's Voice, Mask)
    -Ghost (Incognito, Leave no Trace, False Tracks)
    -?????

    Disquietism:
    -Quell (Alembic, Soothe Disquiet, Quell Disquiet)
    -Give (Rabid Rage, Iago's Whisper, Nameless Dread)
    -Scapegoat (Scapegoat, Progenitor's Curse, ???)
    -Release (Tension in the Air, Mass Hysteria, Shape Disquiet)

    Electrification:
    -Lightning Bolts ('nouf said)
    -Gathering energy (Insulator, Power Sink, Blackout)
    -Releasing Energy (Jolt, Generator, Regulator)
    -????

    Mesmerism:
    Well.... hard to tell since Prommies no longer have direct mind control....hmm...

    Metamorphosis: (I'd be surprised to be wrong on this one)
    -Adapt
    -Change into an animal
    -Create Armor
    -Change Appearance

    Sensorium:
    -Sight
    -Touch
    -Smell & Taste
    -Hearing

    Vitality:
    -Destroying stuff (Fist of talos, Titan's Throw, Battering Ram)
    -Jumping far (there's a mention in Mortal Remains that a Promethean jumped reaaaaaaaally far)
    -Punching hard (Vigor, Bludgeon, Defenestration Blow)
    -Too strong for the League (Thunderclap Blow, Wrath of Hecatonshire)

    Vulcanus:
    -Sensing (All the Sense [Thing], Sigil on the Wind and Firebrand?)
    -Manipulate Flames (Animate Firetouched, Firegrasp, Trigger Firestorm!)
    -Manipulate Pyros (Share Pyros, The Greater Need, Pyros Imp)
    -Refine Pyros (Electroshock Recharge, Crucible of the Will and Sublimate Phlogiston)

                                                  Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                                                  That seems troublesome for Aparte because it effectively costs 3 Pyros if you want to climb walls but still breathe air (1 to switch on Scuttling Spider and Blessing of Tethys and 1 to reverse the effect of Blessing of Tethys) but only 2 if you want to climb walls and breathe water.
                                                  It also costs 4 Pyros to activate Procrustean Shape without suffocating. I'm 100% certain that's not intended design.

                                                  Edit: Other note, the Passive function says "The Pariah cannot breathe water or survive in a vacuum without charging the Alembic", but even with the Alembic charged it's not possible to survive in a vacuum.

                                                  Double Edit: Some other questions/comments:
                                                  • Since I assume you cannot turn off Distillations at will (otherwise returning to breathing air wouldn't cost Pyros) that makes it possible to do something like activate Procrustean Shape to reach something, roll one success then be stuck with a four foot arm and -1 in combat (though I've no idea why longer reach on one arm makes you worse at combat). How would I counteract that? Would have have to pay 3 Pyros again to turn it off or switch the version?
                                                  • Malleable Skeleton mentions use in conjunction with other alterations, is that just a note in case of Exceptional Success? Or is it possible to reactivate? And if so, at what cost?
                                                  • I have to wonder about the balance between alterations. Long Arms grants +1 Defense, which is pretty negligible, especially for 3 Pyros. Altered Body gives successes in Armor, which is fucking tremendous. For instance, if I rolled three successes my options would be: +1 Defense, 10 Foot Arm, +3 Speed, Squeeze through gaps, or +3/+3 Armor. Of these things two are underwhelming, two highly conditional, and one is fucking amazing!
                                                  The more I think about this the more punishing the structure seems. Unless Pyros is fucking everywhere 3 is a lot to pay to reach one object that is far away, or gain a tiny boost in speed. If it were +[Successes] Defense, or a speed multiplier that'd be another thing, but as is it reads like a power that'll disappoint often.
