Dolphins made super intelligent by cybernetics or gene-manipulation or whatever are "transpersons/transdolphines" not "transhumans". Your mixing up the concept of "person hood" with humanity, the two are separate.

                                                            So to provide the definition of transhumanism:
                                                            • Noun - the belief or theory that the human race can evolve beyond its current physical and mental limitations, especially by means of science and technology.
                                                            So, as mentioned here, for one to be transhuman, one would first need to be human. Something Prometheans are not.

                                                            In addition, transhumanism engages with external forces to force an evolution e.g. advanced technology. Promethean is a game of internal "alchemy". The characters progress towards humanity via their own experiences, conceptualizations and efforts. Azothic Memory gives them a direction to follow, but its not like its an active external force, its an internal drive they have (like birds have to migrate in the winter).
