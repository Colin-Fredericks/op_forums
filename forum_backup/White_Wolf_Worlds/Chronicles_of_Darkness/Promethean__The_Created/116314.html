                          Creatures of the Lower Depths are semi-real; they are still partly defined by patterns and scannable with arcanas; but they are also partially "indefinite"; something missing but which is nevertheless what defines the most them; making them as strange as the beings of the Abyss because they are mostly what they are not.                           Arcana of Death can still partially define the mummies; but there is something else.

                          If a mage reads the soul of a mummy; he will see that he is both alive, undead, and something opaque and impenetrable for the arcanas.

                          Promies are also composed of arcanas and supernal models : their bodies, affinitys with Forces,ect......but the divine fire can only be very roughly seen by a high level of Mind and Prime. Because, depending on what writer is about, what references you look up, Prometheans could be undead, alive, differently alive, or any number of other things. Go with what makes most sense to you, because then its easier for you to be more consistent. If you want a Promethean to be a variant zombie, then its easiest to run it that way.                                                   I don't agree that Divine Fire is linked to anything that's easy to point at, Lower Depths or arcana or anything else defined by the other denizens of the World of Darkness. That's not to say such notions are WRONG, but I think that any labels being seen as The Truth™ robs that power of much of its, well... POWER, on a story level anyway.

                                                  One of the Promethean: the Created supplements (can't recall which, Saturnine Night maybe?) says that the very nature of Divine Fire is based on very specific measures, but it only flares in moments of impossible desperation. It presents a notion that once a demiurge has succeeded in the act of creation, once he KNOWS it can be done, that spark won't ever flare the same way again...

                                                  So, in terms of the topic at hand, I feel it's perfectly possible that one of the Created could look different to a vampire, to a mage, to a changeling, and none of those beings' respective powers would work as expected on one of the Created. Further, as my reading of Mortal Remains for Hunter: the Vigil suggests, different Lineages could all appear different to whatever supernatural senses are focused upon them. A mage looking at a throng of prommies might not immediately know that the Wretched, Orsirian and Galateid were all of single type.

                                                  I love how properly odd Promethean is in the World of Darkness, and any crossover that includes this line would, for my game at least, play up the unknowable nature of those creatures.
