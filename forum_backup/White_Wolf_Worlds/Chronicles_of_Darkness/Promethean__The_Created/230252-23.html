        Hi everyone,
        Kingme here!
        I used to be a part of the old forum but now that I hear that new material for my favorite game line, I just had to put in my two bits worth lol.

        I get that the Promethean line seeks for these half living beings to find humanity because, even though, they may have great power, they still are abominations of the natural order.

        That being said,there have been those few individuals that seek to find the joys of the Created condition with all that they are capable of (the refinement of lead). There are even other creat that are loath to give up on their supernatural edge. (I believe that one of these individuals was listed as a character in one of the source books in the New Orleans story line).

        What I would like to see is a bit of "culture" among the created to act as a bulwark against the horrors of the world and as a kind of temporary support group. Just like a throng is. Yes, I know that the rarity of the number of Created and their disquiet/wasteland effect might put any prospect of a Promethean "society" impossible at best; but I believe there are ways around this problem and reasons for a kind of "Underground Railroad" system.

        This would also make more sense in the 2e because appreciably their are ways to mitigate the wasteland and disquiet by taking on Roles. This means that some few Created can stay in one spot for a relatively long period of time and create a "home" for herself.

        Therefore, the felt good of running into more Created, even if there are more or less 100 of them in the world,meant that SOME form of longs instance network must have established over the centuries. (And because Azoth calls to Azoth). And I even have an idea on how to do that: through "signposts" or alchemy Enhanced Pilgrim marks

            ]Creation of "signposts": alchemy enhanced pilgrim marks that convey messages, warnings, letters, and teachings to other Created who can read them thus allowing a kind of network or Underground Railroad to exist among the Prometheans.[/COLOR]

            Sure, they can create marks and signs for other Created. But we're talking about creatures that are hidden among humanity that can exist for far longer than most mortals and are hunted by things that want their precious Vitriol. Signs etched into concert or tree trunks aren't going to cut it. You need something with a little more punch to weather the rigors of the World of Darkness[/COLOR]

            Perhaps create a transmutation set to reflect this need to connect to other created.
            1 dot. Create a weak Azoth radiance that lets other Created know that there is a pilgrim Mark.

            2 dots. Occult or otherwise hide the visual of a pilgrim Mark for others other than fellow Created. Three dots visual, five dots from supernatural senses or who the Promethean deems a worthy subject for the mark.

            3 dots. Create a shallow psychic imprint that imparts a general impression to the receiver. Warning, safety, food, shelter.

            4 dots send a detailed message to whoever wants to receive it.

            5 dots. Anchor an "imaginary friend" to a pilgrim Mark As a Pyros spirit that helps the Promethean to the best of its ability. The spirit gains 1 point of Pyros at the rising of the sun. Two points if it is near the humor of its creator.

            What I find most troubling about Promethean is that you can't seem to enjoy the fact that your alive and that MAYBE you might have some supernatural advantages over the average mundane mortal and see a side of the world that most can't. That's why I like the refinement of lead ( and perhaps this new refinement). At least you can enjoy the moments of light in the sea of darkness that is your half life. Besides, I would imagine that it would be harder to find humanity if you were a self-loathing Debbie downer who saw nothing of value in its existence. [/COLOR]

                                                    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post

                                                    I'm actually wondering right now if the first Nepri might have been created during the Old Kingdom in a failed attempt to replicate the Rite of Return.
                                                    Not a bad idea to say the least.

                                                    Also, not to derail the thread, I'll get back to topic after this post....but.

                                                    Vampire, Mage and Promethean are the three games that cross-over with Mummy quite well I feel.

                                                    Vampire has the historical possibilities of two immortal characters who have to face very different types of immortality (and the effect that these different immoralities have on the characters over the centuries and millennia) .
                                                    Mage has a lot of common background similarities that provide a lot of plot hooks for a creative ST.

                                                    Promethean has some similarities as well particularly in regards to identity. Promethean make their own identities over the course of the pilgrimage, Mummy's rediscover their identity over the descent. Interestingly 2E seems to be adding even more stuff that you can riff off between the two. Alchemists are very similar to a group of hunters that capture Mummy's (call Last Dynasty Inc) and use them to create super-medicine (and also make themselves immortal).

                                                    A little wrinkle in the fluff that I have always liked about Promethean and Mummy is that both Pyros and Sekhem are detectable to mundane science, and they are the only "supernatural resources" that are detectable in this way. So there is another link between the two if an ST want's to make something out of it.

                                                    Some Mummy fluff talks about aspects of the Rite of Return leaking into later cultures, but that no one ever managed to recreate it. It implies that most of these bastardized Rites did nothing. Some might have though (its a topic that might be covered to a certain extent by a Mummy supplement next year) might have produced "flawed copies" of the Arisen. Having he Nepri be one such attempt is a good tie between the two games if an ST wanted it to be.

                                                    Fitting in the other types of Promethean is a bit harder though, as their is not so much of an Egyptian link or the idea of the creative act as an attempt at rebirth or immortality. The Tammuz kinda work, as Mummy mentions that the ancient Hebrews defiantly got hold of some stuff from Irem. Ulgan could work as coming from Ubar a spirit infested rival culture to Irem that was destroyed in a war between the two. The others are a much harder fit.

                                                    OK I'll shut up about this now.
