                                                            Originally posted by Mangle77 View Post


                                                            I guess it falls on me to take up @Geckopirateship's flag for a bit:

                                                            It's not "yours" either, nor do these forums belong to you. That goes for all of the"YOU ARE ON FIRE!" crowd.

                                                            NO ONE is "louder" on the Promethean Forums than the people who want to shut us "Theme Hackers" down. There's a reason why I once compared them to "Angry Villagers"; It's an irrational, disproportionate response to other players having a difference of opinion about whether or not Onyx Path should have included OPTIONS for players who don't find much appeal in playing a splat build around trying not to play that anymore. While I respect the Devs creative integrity, I can't really agree with their vision, and it's pretty well established I'm not alone. The fact of the matter is, Pinocchio is just about the only story listed as inspiration in which the protagonist can/wants to become a normal human. A Shard, or some other form of alternative, MIGHT go a LONG way toward making the game more popular. Playing a person returned from the dead in an...imperfectly repaired body and fractured memories, instead of a Tabula Rasa. A being brought to life by Divine Fire, walking a path toward Divinity of their own. An Automaton gaining independence from it's creator, but not becoming a "meatbag" it's self. These are all things that COULD be part of Promethean, but first the devs need to be willing to listen to feedback, and the players who LOVE the game as-is need to stop attacking the players who want something different.

                                                            TLDR: For a game about Alchemy, it's most loyal fans are weirdly resistant to change.
                                                            Thank you. And for the record, my flag is a pirate flag except with the skull replaced by a turtle shell.

                                                            And here's another problem with the "play another game" thing: I want a personal horror game about being a created being based on the Creature, an android, and so on that uses themes of alchemy, coming to terms with being a monster, and personal development. The most logical thing for me to do for that is to alter Promethean. No other game is going to fit that desire as well as a modified version of Promethean. No other game hits those notes.

                                                            At the same time, I go to other games when I want other things. When I want a game about superhuman anime heroes doing over the top things, I go to Exalted. When I want a slightly more grounded and pulpy game, I go to Pathfinder. When I want Star Wars. But to bring this back to transhumanism, I don't expect ChroD to be about triumphantly leading humanity to a new golden age. I'm okay with becoming immortal making you monstrous, or gaining power making you inhuman. I have Eclipse Phase if I want a game that's actually about transhumanism. I'm not so much talking about transhumanism when I talk about immortality and Promethean, which is why I've been trying to stop using that term in reference to ChroD. It's a real-life ideology that I believe in, but it doesn't really describe well what I'm looking for.
