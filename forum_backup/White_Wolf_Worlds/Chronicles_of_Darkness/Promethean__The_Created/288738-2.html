            Speaking as a proud Social Justice Warrior myself, I see a game about a deeply oppressed and inherently despised minority trying desperately to fit in with society, despite the fact their own natures makes society suspicious of them. To use a rather brilliant metaphor from the blog comments, it's a game about playing transsexuals, pre-op.

            And the thing is, a Promethean will never be truly happy until he or she is at peace with their body, ie the New Dawn. Sort of like how transsexuals actually are helped by modifying their body so that it fits with their self-image and real gender.

            The metaphor would probably be better if the problems didn't end for the Redeemed, there are people who have very good reasons they are never able to properly articulate that are offended by the idea of a monster losing his or her monstrousness and living in peace as a normal human. But Redemption is the win condition, so I'm fine with that not being the default, and especially with the 2E core beating STs over the head that the New Dawn is, at worst, a bittersweet ending where something was actually accomplished.

                                                    Actually I'd argue that the problem is that disquiet is a metaphor for things that should be downplayed in a game that's supposed to celebrate humanity - but instead of being downplayed it's applied to the entire human population.

                                                    Essentually it's saying that the "desirable state" is being a bigot. Humans are desirable + all humans are bigots.


                                                    Now personally I don't go for the transsexual metaphor as strongly as some people. I know that the original reason for Disquiet was because everyone who could see the monster in Mary Shelly's Fraknenstien feared and hated him, so I don't really think it's far to say "well you could look at it in a different way, and if you do the metaphor becomes uncomfortable, therefore bad". But I do understand why some people dislike this facet of Promethean.

                                                    Now my personal solution is to keep Disquiet because that is the way it happened in Mary Shelly's book but introduce more pro-human themes to try and take the edge off it. I'd start with a mortal-only merit called "Civilised Morality" designed to represent the things that people say are good about civilisation. It helps you overcome disquiet and combined with high expression you can use it to quell disquiet in others. So you could have a scientist saying "I instinctively want to blame that stranger, but when I think rationally I know the problems began months before he arrived in town", or a priest turning to a Disuquiet fuelled mob and saying "let he who is without sin cast the first stone" and actually curing them of Disquiet.

                                                    That would turn the metaphor from "humanity is a species of bigots" to "humans' natural instincts are tribalistic and primitive - but they've found ways to become better than that." I think that's a cool message, and probably cooler than the current way Promethain is selling humanism.
