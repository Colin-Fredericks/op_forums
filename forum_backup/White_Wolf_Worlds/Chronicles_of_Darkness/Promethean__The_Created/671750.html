    Prometheans and Sedentary Life?

    09-20-2015, 06:12 AM
    I were wondering if is possible for a Promethean to have a sedentary life style, and things like a house or a job? There's a way to deal with the side-effects of his own presence?

      House: rent places by the month. As long as you move at least a mile the wasteland won't become strong enough to be a nuisance.

      Job: Get a solitary job. Night guard, trucker, gravedigger, gardener, etc...

      Disquiet: Unlike what some people think, Disquiet is a long process if your azoth is low. So as long as you don't see someone for more than a couple of scenes every week, you should be fine.

      Wasteland: if the job you get is night guard, the place you work at will become tainted and start deteriorating. It's not a problem if you're a gravedigger, but night guard, stripper or any other job like that will become hard to maintain after a month or two.

      Mitigating these effects: Forge an alchemical pact with other prometheans, learn a few Disquietism transmutations, ask a Quashmal, ask an Awakened, etc...

      My best bet would be that in the Second Edition of Prometheans, these things won't be as hard as they appear in the first edition, so you might be better to wait a little.

              I tried that once. And, to my surprise, I was roughly successful for a while. However, all that effort I put in proved to be in vain.

              There are ways to manage Disquiet / Wastelands and have the area be stable... until someone else breezes through town, messes everything up, and sends everything to hell in a handbasket. A firestorm, another promethean, a Centimani, one of the intelligent pandorans (sublimanti, I think?), one of the new alchemists or someone who's tracking down the Divine Fire to make Immortals, Elixers or clones out of your body. And that's not counting the fact that the quashmal exist to push the Created to continue their joint spiritual/physical Pilgrimage (at least from an OOC perspective).

              Sedentary life is only possible if everyone and everything leaves that prommie alone. Because once the ball starts rolling, its nearly impossible to stop without physically removing yourself from the area. So, I'd say its possible to run into an NPC that's like that, but I wouldn't suggest that PCs pull it off for a long term game.
