            Can you Calcify one ability without switching your Refinement? Can you Cacify an ability without actually taking it?

            OK, lemme try to unpack this a little, using an example.

            Avalon (one of the characters in my PtC game, and also the POV character in the short story "Dedication" in the Firestorm Anthology) is on Aurum. There are three Roles - Follower, Companion, and Leader. When she starts the Refinement, she's studying the Companion Role (it's the closest to what she was created for, so it's what she knows, which means it's a logical place to start). The two Transmutations in Aurum are Deception and Mesmerism. Since, as a starting character, she's got no Roles under her belt, she gets to pick one Alembic from each Transmutation. She picks Assimilation from Deception and Eros from Mesmerism.

            The chronicle progresses, and she gets close to mastering Companion, but then flubs something critical (in game terms, she misses the milestone that would let her master the Role). She decides she really doesn't get people very well, and she needs to understand her own magical nature. She switches to Mercurius (note that since this is a complex Refinement, she'd need a tutor or something, but one of her throngmates is on the Refinement and can teach her). Since she hasn't mastered any Roles yet, she gets the same deal: One Alembic each from both Transmutations of Mercurius (Alchemicus and Vulcanus, as it happens; she picks Stone and Ignus Aspiritus, respectively). She's trying for the Craftsman Role. She achieves it, which means she gets a new Alembic in each Transmutation (let's say she picks Elixir from Alchemicus and Cauterio from Vulcanus).

            Now, she's got a choice, here. She can stay on Mercurius and try for a different Role (there's some benefit to completely mastering a Refinement). She can stay on Mercurius and not try for a different Role; she's mastered Craftsman which means that as long as she stays in that Role, her Disquiet is much lessened...but she also runs the risk of being stuck in a rut, which can have consequences later (for the record, this doesn't happen the second you master a Role; my personal feeling is that if you master a Role you shouldn't get stuck in a rut unless and until it makes narrative sense, but more on that another time). She can also switch Refinements.

            Let's say that she feels she's got a good enough grip on people, by way of whatever she learned on Mercurius, that she wants another go at Aurum. It's a basic Refinement, so she doesn't need a teacher or anything, just a little time to switch gears (tee-hee. That's funny because she's clockwork). She goes back to Aurum, again shooting for the Companion Role, and she picks Assimilation and Eros again (she doesn't have to, but the player figures that they feel familiar).

            Eventually she masters Companion, meaning she gets another Alembic slot within Aurum. So let's say she picks Doppelganger and Penthos (Deception and Mesmerism, respectively), for while she's finishing up on Aurum. Same choice as before: Stay on Aurum and switch Roles, stay on Aurum and risk becoming stuck, or switch Refinements.

            Avalon decides that, while she does want to master Aurum eventually, she'll be better able to do that with a firm understanding of why people fuck things up so badly. That induces her to adopt Cobalus. The Transmutations there are Contamination and, hey, look at that, Mesmerism. But, because she hasn't been on Cobalus and mastered any Roles there, she only gets one Alembic from each Transmutation (she can choose to keep a Mesmerism one that she's already used or pick a new one, either's fine).

            However, since Cobalus doesn't use Deception, Avalon will lose her Assimilation, Anonymity, and Doppelganger Alembics. She's grown quite fond of Assimilation, so she decides to spend some Vitriol Experiences to calcify it. Now, the Assimilation Alembic is permanently part of her makeup. When she switches to whatever Refinement, that Alembic does not count against her total. That means that, if you're keeping track, she's now on Cobalus. She gets one Alembic from Contamination, one from Mesmerism, and she's got Assimilation permanently.

            If she switches back to Aurum, though, Assimilation doesn't count against her total. As such, since she's mastered one Role there, she'd get two Alembics from each Transmutation, plus Assimilation.
