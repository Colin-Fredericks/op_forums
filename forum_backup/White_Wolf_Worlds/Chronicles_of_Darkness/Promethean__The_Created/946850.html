    The God-Machine and the Principle

    08-09-2016, 07:08 AM
    I heard there is a sidebar in Promethean 2nd ed dealing with the relationship between the God-Machine and the Principle. Could someone please summarize it for me? I'd really appreciate it.

    As I understand it, the Principle is the ultimate source of Pyros, the Divine Fire that empowers Prometheans. My personal headcanon has always been that the qashmallim and the angels of the God-Machine often compete over worshippers and occult resources. I'm curious to see how this compares to the canon explanation. Thanks.

      The section offers multiple speculations of the Prometheans on what the God-Machine is.

      1. They're not related and don't interfere with each other.
      2.The Principle is an older version of the G-M that is still managing to hold on.
      3.The Principle and and GM are two opposing entities, the former embodying fire and change, the latter cold and stasis.
      4.The Principle has been pushed out of the world by the GM.

      Bear in mind, Prometheans don't actually know what the Principle is either.

                          My pet theory is that The Principle takes the role of the Gnostic true divinity, with the God-Machine as the flawed demiurge of the material world; it's why the Principle deals with incredible Pyros where the Machine can only manipulate base Essence, and the Principle can (indirectly, via Prometheans) create new souls while the Machine can only make Covers.                                   My own take is generally that the God Machine is essentially an attempt by The Principle to not just inspire creation but to enact its own. We create life in our image, and The Principle is no different. Rather than flesh or metal it made an opus out of the world itself, interlocking systems of concepts and ideas strung together until they self sustain and take on the appearance of life. It is incomplete though, a half thing that is neither worldly nor divine. What it seeks is its own completion, entailing projecting its influence and infrastructure forward and backward through time from the moment of its birth until it is has made itself inseparable from our universe, integrated into its every facet and transcended the sum of its infinite parts. Thus complete it will confront and overthrow its creator, making it free at last to act on its own imperative and reorder the world according to whatever wish it may have.

                                              My own conception of how Prometheans interact with the God Machine goes thusly: The God Machine knows the serial number of every soul in creation. Not just the ones that are currently alive, but those waiting to be born, those that have passed on, and those that will repeat the cycle. The number of souls is fixed, there will only be as many as there ever were and ever will be. It's like the conservation of matter; souls can neither be created nor destroyed, they simply change form and travel and shift phases The God Machine knows this to be true, and therefore it is an immutable fact. Everything is in its proper place, every interaction predicted and accounted for in the grand operation. Prometheans... are a problem. They are not made with souls and therefore are uncataloged by the Machine. As invisible variables they interact with individuals in ways that were unable to be foreseen, each conversation or passing glance nudging someone out their prescribed lane. If a Promethean achieves the New Dawn then something truly remarkable has happened; a new soul where none existed before. This is impossible, this violates an unyielding law of the universe, yet it happens just the same. To the God Machine this isn't just chaos, this is anarchy. Merely by existing Prometheans are a wrench in the God Machine's gears, the Pilgrimage a determined rebellion against the "way it should be." By achieving the New Dawn, they threaten to compromise its entire design.
