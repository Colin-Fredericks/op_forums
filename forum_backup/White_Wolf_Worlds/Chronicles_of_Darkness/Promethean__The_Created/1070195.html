    "What in hell is wrong with you!?" - A collection of quirks, flaws, and obsessions

    03-29-2017, 01:40 PM
    So I was thinking about how to present the otherness of the Created through roleplaying after my last chronicle ended. Players often forget that mechanics cannot do all the work for them, and so they were confused by the idea of losing their powers (yes, again) in exchange for mortality.

    So I thought that having a collection of quirks, flaws, obsessions and whatnot for players to skim through would allow for a better understanding of the alien nature of Prometheans, and their quest for mortality. Feel free to add your owns.

    -An unreasonable hatred of flowers, they'll sometimes wreck a garden for seemingly no reason or break flower pots on impulse
    -Always tries to hold someone's hand
    -Takes random polaroid pictures all the time. Even during stealth missions or important meetings
    -Explains their emotions or impulses when having them. It gets weird when Torment rises or when they start to feel love (or lust) towards strangers
    -Eats uncooked food only, including meat
    -Tries new haircut about once a week (always done by themselves and they have no clue how to use scissors)
    -Inflicts self-harm after what they consider a sin. Usually on the spot and immediately

Prometheans awaken with less understanding than a baby outside of how to walk and (I think) talk and whatever basic life info the Azathotic Memory gives them.                               I don't agree. A lot of them make sense if they experienced something to trigger their obsession. An encounter with someone, a trauma or simply a bad explanation of human social rituals can be triggers to their obsessions. Let's go over a few of the "weirder" ones.

                              -Break mirrors wherever it goes, ok so it hates it's own reflection because it forces it to see that it's still just a monster
                              -Draws eyes and mouths over its skin, they probably saw a teenager bored and passing time by doodling over themselves with a sharpie, and took it as inspiration for themselves.
                              -Always agrees verbally with people's oppinions. It wants to make friends and has a very basic understanding that shared experience or oppinions is a way to make friends
                              -Takes random polaroid pictures all the time, talked with a photograph who told them that photography allows them to capture a vision of the world and share that vision with others. Not understanding that the vision was more akin to the vision of a poet or that of a painter, they started photographing everything
                              -Struggles with S and Z, they are not easy letters to speak when your body is not your own

                              Also, because that intro gave me inspiration
                              -Sees human social rituals as legitimate rituals. Not sure what they're protecting from or calling forth

                                              Problems with object permanence.

                                              Now, people in general have this problem (it's what allows our brains to be like "ARRGH" instead of "Wait, what?" when the monster in Cloverfield sneaks up on Hud in the last scene, despite being big enough to decapitate Lady Liberty). Horror movies use this all the time; if you're not seeing it, it may as well not exist. Babies, of course, are amazed when you cover your face up and the pull off the blanket. Daddy's a wizard! (Hours of nonstop entertainment.)

                                              But take that same concept, and make it slightly less intense than a baby's and more intense than an adults and apply it to a Promethean, and you've got someone who is fucking baffled by the notion of telephones. I can't see you, but you're talking to me so you clearly exist. And so on.
