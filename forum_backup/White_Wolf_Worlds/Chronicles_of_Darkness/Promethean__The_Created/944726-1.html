                                                            Worth noting is that the Basic Refinements are the sort of things that would alchemically just fit into a Promethean's frame-they have direct roots in the Promethean Condition. The Complex Refinements are no so similarly rooted, as they require a closer-to-human state of perspective/being, in that they require a Promethean to really stretch to create the alchemical reaction and flow that.

                                                            Anyways, I find one big problem with this thread lies in the word "illogical". Well, yeah, course it might be illogical. It's alchemy. Science and logic is a part of it, but it's not the sole tools of observation-it's as much an instinctual, spiritual process as it is ordered and observable. it's not enough to see and think a thing, you have to then figure out how to make those ideas transmute and transform- substantiate it through translating them into Transmutations, creating a place in the world with them vying transforming them into Roles, discovering the common base of how this all transforms into Vitriol-all of which the book points out can't just be done by sitting in a lab and pontificating about the nature of life, because of the nature of alchemy and these beings that pretty much are alchemy, who need to do and experience and discover these formulas for self-actualization.

                                                            Of course it's not completely logical. It never was wholly logical. To try and understand it purely by logic is to miss half of the equation.
