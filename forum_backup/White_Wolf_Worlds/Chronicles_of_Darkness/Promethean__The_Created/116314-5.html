            That's why the Pyros mutates stuff in 50s-esque radiation style.
            It's why you get permutations on the Lineages.
            It's why you can make a Galatead out of flesh or marble, and why you can dream a Promethean into existence (which, you know. Very tulpa-esque. Thought constructs and all that).

            What's important about Promethean isn't the answer; the answer is mortality, that or becoming a monster the likes of which the world has never known.
            What's important is getting there. The Pilgrimage. It's a mystery how you exist (as encompassed by the fact that a Demiurge's notes aren't going to make a whole lot of sense and are incredibly difficult to find; think about it: the best advice you have about your condition and how to make another like you is barely accessible). It's a mystery how you become human.

            Mage's solve Mysteries!
            They solve their own mysteries though.
            Socketing the one gameline that is, by design, supposed to be outside context for every other character in the entire system into "Mage's can figure it out" cheapens both gamelines.
            You don't need to have a Promethean in a chronicle to have a patchwork man in it. There's far richer story potential in "he doesn't ping to your Magesight/you don't know" than "he's XYZ."

            Anyway, even if you do want Prometheans to be a solvable mystery in your Mage campaign (which is fine! It's yours twice now), wouldn't it be better for it to be something they can't figure out? Wouldn't it be better for them to have to take time to go explore it?

            Prometheans aren't human. Not yet. They aren't anything. This is why the game is cool - they don't know what kind of person they are yet. They aren't alive or dead or undead. They're making a soul.
            It's probably telling that the first thing a Mage has to do to actually learn about a Promethean is overcome Disquiet, and even realizing that such a thing is working upon them is a huge pain in the ass.

            My Promethean Homebrew.

            Yes, I made it the same way as a Promethean. No, the authorities haven't found me yet.

                "Pyros heats all substances and even ideas, melting them into new shapes and inspirations. It is not just form that it alters; that is a lesser power, available throughout Nature. Pyros also alters qualities, turning one thing into something entirely other."
                This. Although the terminology that Mage uses isn’t used here, that’s what Supernal symbols are: the qualities of the Fallen World’s Phenomena. And the whole purpose for setting it up like this is that mages should look at it and say “holy crap! What the hell is that?” Not just “I say, good sir, what is that strange thing you have going on there?”. A visceral WTF moment rather than mere curiosity. Incidentally, that’s also why mages aren’t immune to Disquiet.

                Maybe an archmaster could use his Ascension to rewrite reality so that Pyros is a phenomenon emanating from the Aether. Maybe. (What happens when an unstoppable force meets an immovable object?) But aside from that, Pyros is an “outside context” problem that mages simply aren’t equipped to handle.

                Shock’s reference is that Saturnine Night says that a mage can create a Promethean — but only by not using magic and doing it the way regular mortals do; and even then, his Gnosis takes a hit and he risks losing his Mage template. (I wouldn’t go quite as far as Saturnine Night does in that I’d leave the poor wreck as a Sleepwalker rather than a Sleeper; whether or not he has a signature on a Watchtower, he’s already broken free of the Sleeping Curse, and I don’t see it reasserting itself even if his Watchtower connection gets burned out.)

                                                So on a slightly more serious note than my last post: In my own head canon, I've long equated the Supernal with the concept of fact or being, and the Principle with the concept of intent. I also don't hold the Principle as "above" or "fueling" the Supernal in any way, one is the structure and the other is the blueprints.

                                                The Supernal's concept of being is the foundational aspects of existence that point in the direction it will go in; one cell divides into two at a time; cutting a human in half causes it to die; a single insect is incapable of the level of cognition of a human. The Principle's concept of intent is the movement in that direction, or the application of the aspect on that particular aspect of existence; why the cell divides in *that* direction, why it divides at all, and why it stops; why a human doesn't regrow lost limbs; that while one insect is incapable of higher cognition, a hivemind allows for more advanced and complex behaviors than the simplicity of the rules would seem to allow.

                                                Azoth, being the concentrated form of the Principle, changes this in the way of how the building would look if you built the blueprints in a different order, or randomly swapped aspects between blueprints; one cell could divide into three or four at a time, or lose its regulatory mechanism to stop dividing when it should; a human exhibits a starfish's or earthworm's fundamental intent of survival and growth, regrowing the missing half when split in half; an insect is able to force non-insect minds into its hivemind, increasing its cognitive abilities by orders of magnitude (okay, I just gave myself nightmares for the next month...)

                                                Prometheans are continuations of the effects that Azoth had on the blueprints of existence. Dead flesh reanimating. Stone sculptures stepping into the existence their forms mimic. Etc.

                                                            So, I'm not sure if this was fully addressed in this thread, but Prometheans have a "fiery aura" to Prime Sight.
