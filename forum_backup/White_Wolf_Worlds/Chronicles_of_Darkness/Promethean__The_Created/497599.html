    Refinements for a would be god?

    04-30-2015, 01:19 PM
    I'm planning a game where the central villain is a Promethean (osiris) and I want them to face them in three acts. First as Innocents, then add mortals then as supernatural beings.

    I'm trying to convey the villain trying to become his name sake, a god (rather than a puny mortal) . And I thought a good, first broad stroke wood be to work out his refinements. But I'm struggling to work out what would be appropriate...

    My nominations are Copper, followed by Tin, followed by Centimanti ... But the last one is almost too focused on being a monster and not a god. Also, while the first two focus on his general outlook, I envisioned the character as being far more manipulative than physically powerful.

    Thoughts?

      I think you're really looking at a Hundred-Handed here. The "proper" refinements are roads towards humanity, not away from it; a Centimanus embraces inhumanity rather than progressing from it. There is a certain level to which the Centimani learn not to fear the fearsome and shocking, but I see nothing contradictory in a Centimanus considering its inhuman state to be exalted and superior.

      More to the point, though, what is the difference between a monster and a god? The World of Darkness frequently blurs that line; it's scattered across with implications that gods of myth may have been monsters of a sort, and conversely, that past monsters may have been gods. Even the religious sense of the word "awe" still used in contemporary worship is a sort of holy dread, interweaving feelings of admiration and terror; multiple times, the first words angels speak to men and women in the Bible have to be "don't be afraid of me."

              It might be interesting to have him progress with them. When they first meet him, he's Iron and working on perfecting his physical form. At the next stage, he's moved on to Mercury and is refining his magical abilities. When he appears for the last time, he's completed his fall due to ignoring the Pilgrimage and is a Centimanus (and probably convinced he's achieved his goal). This gives him a story to parallel their own and lets his capabilities change over time so they can continue to be surprised by him at each new encounter.

                  I've toyed (briefly) with the notion of scrapping the Centimani as a stand-alone “Refinement” and replacing it with a set of Corruptions that riff off of the Refinements, each taking the principles of one Refinement and twisting its goals into something Centimanus-like. As such, I'd aim for there to be one Corruption for every set of Transmutations in the game.
