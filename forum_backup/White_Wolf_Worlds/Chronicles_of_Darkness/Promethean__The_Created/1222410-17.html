    Originally posted by Iguazu View Post
    I'm not thinking modern, but, Age of Enlightenment: 1700s. Just a thought of another supernatural running a city.
    The logistics of running a city, coupled with the general rarity of supernaturals overall and Prometheans in particular, means that part is comparatively straightforward, provided you can build up a functional support system, make inroads with the locals, and/or falsify the background necessary to keep people from asking too many questions.

    Open rule without carefully controlling visitors and appearances will be trickier, and Torment is in some ways the bigger issue than Disquiet (methods of resolving Torment are thin on the ground, it's harder to resist in a Wasteland, and one of the means of clearing the Condition is forming a Wasteland, which also makes Disquiet a bigger threat over time).

    Some of this is also down to running a city being hard; the types of supernatural creature most associated with it have cults dedicated to them or lean heavily into Social Merits and Merits that give them free Social Merits as part of their connections with other monsters, and Prometheans are neither of those things, but the tools exist for one of the Created to at least make a good showing of it for long enough that an inopportune qashmal, Pandoran, Firestorm, or other wrinkle can make a grander than usual catastrophe.

                                Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                                Daily silly question: Even the "True Friend" is affected by disquietism?
                                It's also relevant to ponder that the effect of Disquiet in the fiction isn't necessarily open hostility or enmity.

                                The person affected becomes irritable and prone to blame the Promethean on things, but that's just a surface level (even if quite intense) emotional response. They're still in control of their minds and are free to decide on how to act upon it. Up to and including being aware of the problem and able to look for ways to mitigate it, if they know what's going on.

                                A True Friend is likely to stay at your side just as much as without Disquiet, unless the player deliberately chooses otherwise, no need for adaptation.

                                                    Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                                                    So if my character is an unfleshed robot he cannot eat?
                                                    Your character has a functional mouth, period, even if it is literally a tear in the metal.

                                                    Unfleshed still have human-enough bodies, this much is a requirement (as far as requirements go for Prometheans). You have normal human needs and the physical means to act on them.

                                                    The secret to understand a Promethean is to know that you're not simply animated material, you're a human being, even if an artificial one. You're not human-like, you're not human-looking, you're not disguised as human, you're human sans soul. You're the result of an Alchemical transmutation into a human body.

                                                    Of course, the Divine Fire being what it is, exceptions exist for everything. But then exceptions must be treated as such, they require special considerations and rules. Even then, any aspect of your true body is real and you have to deal with it, people just won't perceive it as inhuman.
