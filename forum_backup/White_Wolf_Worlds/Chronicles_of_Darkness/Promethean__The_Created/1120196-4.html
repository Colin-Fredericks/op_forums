        Originally posted by mistress of whispers View Post
        1) Will there be anything interesting for the Qashmallim in the book?

        The book has several ready-to-use qashmallim. We've also made a slight tweak to how the Divine Principle uses them - it's not a huge change, but it does allow you to create recurring qashmallim for that little personal touch (&quot;omg not that dude again.... &quot. No new powers, since they do whatever you need them to do and that really sums it up. I feel like the ready-mades really present the perfect template for any qashmallim though: just fill in <x information> and your homemade messengers will be as detailed as you'd ever need.

        2) Anything you can tell us about any new antagonists for the Created?

        We're doing a new (de-)evolution of Prometheans, something that happens to them when they *really* fucked up the Pilgrimage. Those are brand new - one of our writers came up with the concept. The book also expands on rules and backgrounds for Clones and Zeky, which the corebook didn't have much room for. And the final chapter has a brand new Antagonist that's so awesome he/she gets a chapter all to themselves.

        3) What is your favorite part of the book? Which Antagonist?

        My personal favs are always the Centimani. Maybe I just have a thing for Flux, but I love the sense of chaos and despair, mingled with that 'eh, screw all of this' attitude.

        Is there anything about an interesting Sublimati in the book?

        Yup! I wrote the Pandoran section for the corebook and the sublimati are my favorite kind of freak. They're monsters, but also sentient so they know they're monstrous and maybe they want to be something more (question is then if they can be, which we deliberately leave unanswered). So yes, I'm very happy to say the new sublimati author came up with some brilliant monsters with a huge range.

        Also, I think it was mentioned that the book is going to present a new &quot;breed&quot; of antagonists for Promethean- is it still the case?

        We have the &quot;wow, did you drop the ball&quot; Prometheans, and Clones I mentioned above. Those are definitely antagonists, though the Clones might have a way out. Then there's Zeky, which are playable but come with dangers to their throng. The last chapter has a new antagonist that's both amazing and terrifying.
        Thanks Steffie

                                            #71
                                            06-10-2018, 11:11 AM
                                            Thanks for all the interest and questions everyone! Glad to see you're as excited as I am.

                                            Will this Lost lineage be one we know of or is it something brand new?

                                            The Lost Lineage has made an appearance before. With another Dark Eras book on the way, we didn't want to use Tormented to introduce yet another historical Lineage. So instead we're building on something we've already seen before.

                                            Curious on the change that can allow for recurring Dominions.

                                            It's really a very small tweak, so don't expect too much. Our goal was simply "can we come up with an IC reason that allows Storytellers to use their OOC favorite qasmallim" and, turns out, we could.

                                            more on how it the the Divine Fire interacts with the world that the Created lives in would be cool

                                            I definitely agree there. We know it's quite powerful, yet we've broached so little of how that power expresses itself. That's not something for a Night Horrors book, but if we get a Guide it could fit there. Same goes for New Dawn editing reality.

                                            Will anything on this book be related to the Contagion Chronicle?

                                            No, for two reasons. First is that the Contagion Chronicle was but a speck in Matthew Dawkin's eye when we were writing this one. Second is that we deliberately steered away from the God-Machine. You can definitely see traces of it, on account of the God-Machine being so cool that the book's writers couldn't resist, but we didn't want to delve too deeply into the God-Machine chronicles for what is very much a Promethean book.

                                            Will any of the demiurges presented be of supernatural origin distinct from the Prometheans?

                                            No, our demiurges so are human.

                                            Do centimanus forms throng with others centimanus?

                                            Can centimanus be cunning planners,or does Flux dissolve their minds?

                                            How long can a Centimanus live?

                                            Centimanus are Prometheans on a different, more destructive Refinement. This Refinement, Flux, doesn't advance the Pilgrimage, which is of course a problem. But they live as long as they normally would, can form throngs (with other Centimani and 'regular' Prometheans), and be very cunning. If they wanted to, they could even try to switch back from Flux to another Refinement - they usually just don't want to, because Flux is insidious in an almost addictive way.

                                            Will the book present Prometheans made from Animals?

                                            No, but we do have clones with animal DNA - think Dark Angel (the tv series) rather than Doctor Moreau though.

                                            Any example Extempore with or without example Bestowments to go along?

                                            One the first blog is up, you can comment on what you want to see next. Make sure to post Extempore then!

                                            I wonder if there will be Prometheans "defects"? (...) So is there a chance we have prometeans who have members replaced by Siamese objects or twins?

                                            You mean like scissor hands, but not as part of the Unfleshed? Prometheans endeavor to be human. So if they lose a limb, they're more likely to regrow it or leave it be, than stick a foreign object in. So while Pandorans or Clones might do that, it doesn't really make sense for a Promethean (unless they're Unfleshed, in which case they don't have much of a choice).

                                            Does the book have anything about the Redeemed?

                                            No antagonist Redeemed, though we have a mortal Hunter whose mind is so confused that you could easily rule she's a Redeemed. In general though, we aim for Redemption as a good thing - it shouldn't make you an enemy of the people you once (presumably) loved.

                                            Freelance Author and Developer for Onyx Path Publishing
