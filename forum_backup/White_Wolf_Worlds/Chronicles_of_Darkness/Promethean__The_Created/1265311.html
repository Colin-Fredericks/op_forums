                  The Hitchhiker (Pg. 57) doesn't specify which Attributes the Dread Power Briareus' Prowess boosts.
                  Same problem with Hive (Pg. 59), which also doesn't specify what the Beastly Mutation Dread Power does.

                  The Stack's Swarm Form Dread Power (Pg. 68) doesn't have a scaling dot rating like other Pandoran Dread Powers. Should it allow the Pandoran to transform into a Swarm of a Size equal to (Rank x dot rating)?

                  None of the Greater Qashmallim (Pg. 111-115) have Attributes or Numina.

                      My fault:

                      p. 75: "xScience (Alchemy) 4" -- cut the x
                      p. 75: The 1-5 rating for the Implant Dread Power is an artifact of an old draft I forgot to cut; it shouldn't have dots. Cut "+ Implant" from the Dice pool, and change Coeus's Dread Power stats so it doesn't have the (3) after Implant
                      p. 75: The minus sign before Stamina is missing from the Dice Pool for Implant.
                      p. 75: "or was correct it about its destiny" -- should be it correct
                      p. 76: "Daisy’s condition — such is the nature of Flux." -- The sentence here got cut off. It should read: "Daisy’s condition probably isn’t as neat and tidy as that, and it may be that it changes as time goes on — such is the nature of Flux." If all that doesn't fit the page, the whole sentence fragment could just be cut and nothing would be lost.
                      p. 79: "Its form is threefold, existing as a twisting, metal thing Prometheans recognize as a sublimatus: a facsimile of a home computer and the nanite spores it uses to infect its victims." -- the colon should be a comma, and there should be a comma after computer to make the list more obvious
                      p. 83: "Spark considers them a nuisance, even worth murdering" -- there should be a not before the word even
