    Sanctuary: A Community of Prometheans

    05-18-2014, 06:14 AM
    I am in the early drafting phase of a rather ambitious chronicle for Promethean: The Created, which will be based around a group of Prometheans being driven out of their city and learning of a community, Sanctuary, somewhere in the wilderness where the Created can settle. The site of this community will be so horrendously devastated prior to their arrival as to be uninhabited by humans and the flora/fauna so twisted that their wasteland effect will pass unnoticed, at least by mortals. Disquiet and wasteland will be the driving force behind getting them to Sanctuary. Having tried to remain hidden within the city, they were subjected to numerous beatings and threats, escalating to the point where a fellow Promethean is found murdered. Every time they attempt to find somewhere else to settle, disquiet and wasteland will bring them intense trouble. Although the twisted state of the lands surrounding Sanctuary make wasteland and disquiet harder to detect, that does not mean they have stopped, just that this allows them to build to a significant tipping point that will require the party to make fairly major decisions that affect not only themselves but also a much wider community.

    The plan is to give the players lots of reasons to go to Sanctuary, so that they do not feel too directed. For this reason, the group will consist of Prometheans who were abandoned by their creators and the game will be set after they have each spent time as lone-wanderers (not sure whether to roleplay this with them or discuss it during character creation). They have banded together as a small group for protection but, unbeknownst to them, this has intensified the hatred they face. Once on the road, they will have to find a place that seemingly only exists in rumours while dealing with the effects of their disquiet (this is the section that I need to most help with). So they will be going to Sanctuary in order to find answers and possibly settle there. At Sanctuary, there will be Created who think that they should move from wasteland to wasteland in order to avoid drawing unwanted attention, those who wish to remain and fortify the settlement, others who feel that they should create more desolation in order to expand their territory, and a group who feel that Sanctuary is an abomination against the natural order that will end in blood, death and madness. This final group will advocate the Pilgrimage to become human. I was thinking about having between 30-50 Prometheans in the community (does this sound reasonable?). I will make it clear that, as far as the player characters know, this has never been done before, making it a city on a hill for others of their kind. Given the high concentration of Prometheans, they will bring the attention of far worse enemies than hunters...

    The inspiration behind the chronicle is Clive Barker's excellent novella, Cabal, which was later adapted as Nightbreed. In Cabal, Aaron Boone is convinced by his sinister psychiatrist that he is a serial killer who suffers major psychotic breaks. Believing himself to be a monster, he learns about a place named Midian where he may be accepted. If I can mix Cabal with the bleak isolation of Frankenstein and the sense of moral confusion as to how best to survive in a hostile world found in The Walking Dead, then I will be exceptionally happy.

    This will be the second RPG campaign I have run and my first using the Storytelling system. Therefore, I will really appreciate any advice from more experienced Storytellers as to how I can achieve my aims for this chronicle. I will update the thread with new information as I write.

      The campaign I managed started at the far end of this chronicle. A charismatic Tammuz had gathered a few dozen Created into a township called Assembly. They occupied an abandoned silver mining camp overlooking the White Sands Proving Ground (where the atomic bomb was tested). Most basically scavenged supplies and food for the camp, while the leader attended to a secret experiment deep in the mine, one that yielded Azoth so pure that he decided to test it in creating new Prometheans. Yet on the very night of their inception, military forces and qashmallim drove into the camp, scattering the Assembled, and beginning the Pilgrimage for the player characters.

      In my notes, Assembly couldn't sustain more than 20 or so Created. The Wasteland spread for miles and miles, and the Prometheans had to range far and wide to maintain the camp's food supplies. They couldn't stay below notice for longer than a year (which I still considered miraculous in longevity), and the camp was prone to radical weirdness, not only from the Wasteland, but also from the leader's experiments on a captured qashmallim. The Assembled had left a series of pilgrim marks statewide which hinted at the town's existence. About half of the population were personally convinced by the leader's to found Assembly.

      I think the population should reflect your take on Promethean numbers. I look at standard Promethean having between one to five thousand Prometheans active at a time worldwide. 50 Prometheans would be 1-5% of the world's population in that case, which would be enormous if compared to most cities. NYC is about 0.1% of the global population, for comparison.

      LFP: Foreign Bodies (Deviant: the Renegades)

          Thanks for the feedback, Verge. I think that, given their scarcity and nomadic, isolated lives, any community of Prometheans will have a vastly higher percentage of their global population than a human city does in relation to our total number. However, I take your point and do like the idea of making all the Prometheans in the settlement distinct personalities, so perhaps closer to 30 would be preferable. I want the population of Sanctuary to be large enough to have a much worse long-term effect than standard wastelands. For this reason, I plan to start the chronicle in Kiev, with the disquiet the troupe causes reflecting the wider instability in contemporary Ukraine. Unfortunately, I have never travelled to the country and am thus trying to build up a sense of the place via secondary sources. There does not seem to be a particularly good travel guide to either Kiev or Ukraine on the market, so I am looking for other resources to draw upon. Does anyone know of RPG sourcebooks that cover Kiev/Ukraine?

          I want to create an atmosphere of supernatural intrigue by starting the chronicle with an Elpidos qashmallim warning the players of the impending attack by a group of hunters in visions of their bodies torn apart. Do you think that Mortal Remains would be a useful source book for giving details of how Prometheans can be destroyed by humans, or is this fairly straightforward? Visions seem a good way to offer clues without diluting the isolation aspects of the game, perhaps leading them to Prometheans who have heard of Sanctuary.

                  Despite the length of time since my last update, I have been working on this Chronicle over the summer and will launch it this Autumn. Firstly, I now have six players, rather than the intended four, which has changed the way I am approaching preparation. Thus, it will begin with the Redeemed who has been harbouring the PCs by moving them around Kiev bringing them together after a number of their fellows have been murdered by Hunters. He will explain that he had been trying, successfully up until recently, to shelter them from a harsh world, but that in doing so he has brought ruination upon them all and implores them to flee Kiev. On questioning, he will reveal that he was once one of them, but possesses very few memories from his past life beyond the profound sense of loneliness and persecution. He has attempted to research their condition, but little seems to have been written on what they are. When he has encountered more mature Prometheans, they have rejected his offer of safe harbour, either because they believe that the Pilgrimage to become human is the only way for them to find respite, or because they have heard of a community of their own kind that can actually provide the protection he promised.

                  My thinking behind this is that it will give the players some clear direction and a sense of urgency about setting off from Kiev, without too much railroading. I will work with each player to establish their back story and help them flesh out their Promethean's goals and disposition. To track down other Prometheans, they can either roam in search of Azothic radiance, look for news reports of out of character violence or freak crop failure, or seek out the spirits that feed off Disquiet. If they choose not to look for Sanctuary, the game will still work well, and I can seed clues that suggest many secrets leading to Mortality can be found in a whole community of their kind.

                  Any thoughts on the above?

                  While I was tempted to defer the start of the Chronicle until after the second edition comes out, I think that this will risk losing player interest and that we should begin as planned. In order to flesh out the Hunters, who will be the primary antagonists to begin with, I will pick up a copy of Mortal Remains to use alongside Slasher. Additionally, in order to flesh out my understanding of the Promethean condition, flux, and some of the weirder lineages, I will pick up all four of the supplements. The new edition sounds as though it may offer more options for less nomadic play, so I can get hold of a copy if and when the players reach Sanctuary.

                      It sounds like a great chronicle concept!

                      Although I haven't touched Promethean I do llove the storytelling guide, one of my favourites.

                      My only caveat to your approach is to not forget that it is a game. Even if you have a heavy focus on narrative (I do myself) you still need to convey the win/ lose conditions as clearly as you can without spelling it out.

                      This is particularly important at the start where players are finding their feet and getting to grips with their characters.

                      From what you've mentioned I would start in media res with the characters escaping their current 'sanctuary' led by the Redeemed, with hunters in pursuit.

                      This easily establishes the win condition, find a new sanctuary but also speaks to the theme of the chronicle. You can keep the hunters as a constant threat to leave and then reinforce the fact that the Redeemed needs them to leave, breaking their 'sanctuary' again.

                      Just my tuppence
