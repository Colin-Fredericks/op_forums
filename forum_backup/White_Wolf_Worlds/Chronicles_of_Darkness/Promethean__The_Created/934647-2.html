            Thank you kindly!

            I ran a playest chronicle set in Japan; my players made a Galateid worshiped as a goddess by a failing apocalyptic cult and a Frankenstein schoolgirl in a violent girl's gang. It was a lot of fun to play around with Japanese urban legends (those dogs with human heads are great, and we had a recurring one that was inevitably kicked whenever it appeared).

            Mechanics are a lot of fun, even if we spectacularly misunderstood how new Transmutations worked at first; once they were explained to us rather graciously, we had a lot of fun with them. Refinments and the Pilgrimage as a whole have a more distinct mechanical skeletal to them now, which helps the chronicle have a flow instead of relying entirely on a talented Storyteller. Refinements are now more about exploring specific aspects of humanity through Roles (I think some of those were spoiled?) which pushes each player character in how they learn and grow.

            All the major complaints about 1e have been addressed without gutting what makes Promethean the game I love, and the end result is a much more playable game about the beauty of humanity and building your own identity.

                                                    Originally posted by reseru View Post
                                                    It's the same for me, too.

                                                    What are some cool new merits?
                                                    What's Tokyo like?
                                                    How are Extempore mechanics advised?
                                                    Do Ulgans only deal with spirits, or do they handle ephemeral entities in general?
                                                    Prometheans get a dedicated Safe Space sub-merit (a la Haven or Sanctum) that lets them supress Wastend creation called a Hovel. There's a really fun merit that gives you rerolls on skills because its the body's brain remembering old abilities.

                                                    Tokyo wasn't finished in the draft I played, and so ours was a homebrew build. We had an Ulgan working in whatever that one huge shrine is, the throng helped some prostitutes in Kabuki-cho, the Frankenstein was obsessed with Akihabara, nothing terribly notable.

                                                    Extempore pick an existing humour to guide them and have example Bestowmenta plus the advice to modify existing ones. They lack Azothic Memory, the framework for the Pilgrimage imparted upon a Promethean's creation, and so have no inherent knowledge of how to conduct the Great Work. Many Extempore never finish their Pilgrimages.

                                                    The Ulgan Bestowment allows interaction with ghosts and spirits. I'd personally homebrew it to be a choice between spirit-focused Ulgan and ghost-focused Orpheans, but that's just me.
