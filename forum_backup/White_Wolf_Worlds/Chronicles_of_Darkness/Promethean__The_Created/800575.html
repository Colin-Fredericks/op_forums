    [Hack] Alternative New Dawns - Other endings for Pilgrimage

    01-04-2016, 10:42 AM
    Notice: This is totally unofficial fan material and point of view. If you think, like game developers, that Promethean should only be about becoming human - just stop here and do not write any further. This topic is only about extending game to other "endings". If you not agree with that idea - do not write, it needless and counter to the topic idea. We do not need any flame war here.

    Once again return topic of forcing Pilgrimage to be about becoming human. Inspired from two posts in latest discussion I open proper topic for this rules change ( hack ) to the base setting. As Promethean 2ED is just around corner I think it's proper time for it. Here are inspiration posts:

    Originally posted by ShadowKnight1224 View Post
    You could also repurpose the new dawn as a way to "refine", " distill" or "purify" Azoth so that it no longer causes Disquiet, Wasteland or Torment. You will still be using the same mechanics, you will preserve the theme, and you won't have angry fans saying you're ignoring a core component of the game. :P
    Originally posted by MCN View Post
    Perhaps the simplist solution would be to change the end-goal of the different Refinements? Each Refinement is a path towards the Great Work. Aurum reflects becoming a human by mimicking, changing to fit humanity. The path of Torment is about your base desires and living as you will. I remember someone in the past suggested that Mercury would transform you into an angel/quashmal. Etc. Its a simple and elegant solution to your question. Would that work for what you want?

    Tin has these Transmutations actually. I've always thought of Tin as "Flux lite." The Refinement of Tin focuses on understanding and controlling your Torment, and, by extension, Wastelands and Disquiet. Torment is, by defintion, the imbalanced Flux inherant within the Promethean. So, its basically a non-evil way of studying your monstrous nature and controlling it. Meanwhile, Cobalt studies sin and indulgence and other base desires for someone who wants to go that route. Actually, both Colbalt and Tin study Disquietism, which controls the monstrous nature parts.

    Actually, this last one is Centimani, flat out, no change needed. They're the Promethean equivalent to the cackling necromancer overlord wannabe.
    Proper rules will be when the 2ED corebook will be here, but for now we could use this just system roll on the New Dawn from page 195 of 1ED corebook.

    Hack - Alternative New Dawns

    In this rules variant, Created do not look only for becoming mortal - many do, others are not interested in this. They all lives are Pilgrimage, but each one of it goes in different destination. What end story of particular Created is based on the his last Refinement he choose when making New Dawn roll.

    Basic ideas of into what Created could transform at New Dawn:

    Aes - Qashmallim, directed to the guarding other Created
    Argentum - Created that can change Pyros in to other forms of "powers" ( like Mana, Essence, etc. )
    Aurum - Mortal human, as per normal rules
    Centimani - Lilithim
    Cobalus - Mortal human, with Vice-Ridden or Virtuous Merit
    Cuprum - Created but without Disquiet limitations
    Ferrum - Created without limit to Attributes rising by Pyros
    Mercurius - Qashmallim, dedicated to wills of Principle
    Phosphorum - ??? ( I really do not have good idea here what to make with character )
    Stannum - Created but without Torment limitations

    Waiting to hear your thoughts on idea.

              Ok, setting aside my prejudice to this idea, I'll have a look at this.

              Aes: this seems interesting if only a little weird since Quashmalims are supposed to be born and die for a singular purpose, but I suppose exceptions make the rules.
              Argentum: Why not humans with such a power? Or why not simply becoming a supernatural creature of your choice?
              Aurum: yup.
              Centimani: I would have rather gone with "Dead", since Centimanism is explicitely not on a pilgrimage, but I could settle for "Horror" (as in the generic, weird monster with no neat box)
              Cobalus: That's just Aurum with a cherry on top, I'm not too fond of it
              Cuprum: Nope.
              Ferrum: Nope.
              Mercurius: basically Aes, since the will of the Principle is to help Prometheans (usually)
              Stannum: Nope.

              Ok to explain a little: Pyros (The driving component of a Promethean) is the power of change. That power is then either used as Flux (the destructive change, the erosion, the mutation) or Azoth (the creative change, the evolution, the creation). As such, a Promethean should never stay a Promethean as his New Dawn.

              Becoming a Quashmalim is a nice Idea, wether a Lilithim or an Elpidos is not important, it's a cool idea.
              "Stay a Created but X" is not an interesting idea, since it could also just be a high-level Transmutation. Having no limit on physical boosts, for example, could just be a 4-5 dots Transmutation. Torment and Disquiet Management are already Transmutations, and they're both a lot less threatening in the 2nd edition from what we've heard.
              I think skipping the Human step and going straight for Beast/Werewolf/Mage can be interesting (and maybe even Deviant) since they're supposed to be either non-humans or super-humans (Mage)


              I think that all Basic Refinements should simply lead straight to "Human", while Complex Refinements could lead to something else. So, say:

              Aes: Athanor-Ghost (You become some sort of Athanor to help others of your kind find their place in the world)
              Argentum: Any other Supernatural splat, or maybe just a Spirit
              Cobalus: No idea
              Mercurius: Quashmalim (Or simply becoming a part of the Principle)
              Phosphorum: Not enough info.

                              Next iteration of New Dawns, taking your advices in account. After list I written mine reasoning on those.

                              Aes – Bronze – Qashmallim (Elpidos), directed to the guarding other Created
                              Argentum – Silver – Human with Lesser Template by supernatural the practitioner was obsessed with – like Ghoul for vampires, Wolf Blooded for werewolves, Proximi for mages, Fae-Touched for fae, etc.
                              Aurum – Gold - Mortal human, as per normal rules
                              Centimani – Flux - Qashmallim (Lilithim) or Horror ( from Chronicles of Darkness rulebook )
                              Cobalus – Cobalt - Mortal human, with Vice-Ridden or Virtuous Merit
                              Cuprum – Copper – Prefected Created
                              Ferrum – Iron - Prefected Created
                              Mercurius – Quicksiler – Qashmallim (Elpidos), dedicated to seeding the Principle
                              Phosphorum – Phosphor - ???
                              Plumbum – Lead - Prefected Created
                              Stannum - Tin - Prefected Created

                              Perfected Created means that promethean still is Created character, but he does not have Disquiet, Torment and Wasteland problems that rest of their kind still on Pilgrimage have. Idea on only one rule lifted maybe is thematic fitting, but in running will be a large problem.

                              I went with Lesser Template idea on the Argentum, as it in reality what practitioner of Silver is – a fan of some supernatural race. Lesser Templates still can become proper “monsters”, they have greater chance on this even, but it weirdly also very human to be obsessed like this on other beings.

                              I left Qashmallim transformations, as they are interesting and fitting to two Refinements. Also, from mine understanding of how Angels of Principle works – they have continued existence. They just summoned “when they needed”, so there could be hundreds of years before they return. But both Sentries and Ophidians works as “ultimate form” as such. Also, Mercurius should be more dedicated in establishing the power of Principle in the world, where Aes are those that guard Created.

                              With Centimani I left Lithim option, as it is interesting that your archenemy could return as power of chaos and destruction. On the other hand, becoming Horror ( like from Chronicles of Darkness rulebook ) is logical evolution for the Freaks. As Centimani is all about contradictions and most are NPCs – they have two endings to choose from. It still will be a Storyteller choice in the end.

                              For now we have 4 Refinements ending with becoming perfected Created, 3 about becoming Human and 3 about becoming Qashmallim. I would aim for Phosphorum with Human ending, maybe with some twist, only because we will then have nice even numbers between Refinements. Thinking about what we know about Phosphor, maybe mortal that is aging at twice rate? But this do not sound as something that players would want to aim for...
