    Lineage Ideas

    06-10-2025, 10:55 AM
    I've had a few inspirations for lineages and extempore kicking around for a while now. I'm not really planning on doing anything with them, but here's how I might approach them if I did. Any thoughts or suggestions are appreciated.

    These first two are from ideas that pop up in fiction with some frequency:


    The GELF – Genetically Engineered Lifeforms

    Inspirations – Neosapiens (Exosquad), Apes (Planet of the Apes), Eve (Parasite Eve)


    Of those inspirations, the Neosapiens from the critically underrated Exosquad are by far the biggest influence. They work particularly well as Prometheans because of their place in their story and particular traits that mesh well with the Promethean condition and set them apart from humanity.

    The Neos are a workforce of genetically engineered humans designed to be able to handle the harsh climates of other planets. They are quite intelligent but lack creativity and tend towards rigid thinking. They also can develop a degenerative disease that causes them to dissolve slowly into liquid. This doesn’t fit Prometheans, per se, but it does suggest the way that they make more of themselves.

    The Neo-mitochondrial creatures from Parasite Eve, though, work particularly well as GELF pandorans.


    Humour – Cytoplasm or Lymph

    This takes its name from the intra- or intercellular fluids. The imbalance of the GELF tends towards a lack of creativity and reflection. This manifests as an unwillingness to change course once they’ve started a path, to fall back on older patterns when they struggle, and in extreme cases to revert to primal behaviors.


    Torment – Rigidity and Regression

    When in the grips of Torment the GELF find themselves unable to adapt. They double down on whatever they are currently doing, especially if it isn’t working. If and when that fails completely they will fall towards atavistic behaviors, though whether that is their own older patterns, primal and bestial behaviors, or habits of the people they were created from varies from GELF to GELF.


    Disquiet - Narrowly Defined Roles

    People tend to compartmentalize the GELF into narrowly defined categories. They act predictably as long as the Promethean conforms to that role, but the roles are so narrowly defined that’s almost never possible forever. Even when humans treat them as allies it is less ‘friends’ and more as ‘tokens’ or as ‘domesticated.’ When the GELF do show unexpected opinions or wants, people are quick to recompartmentalize them in negative ways.


    Wasteland

    GELF wastelands tend to cause miasma and unhealth, most commonly in the form of mutations, growths, and in extreme cases malignancies.


    Potential Generation

    The GELF are not constructed, they are grown from appropriate sources. Like some other Lineages they most frequently use human remains, but it is not parts that they need, it is biomass.


    Potential Bestowments

    Clone Body

    The GELF might have the ability to reincarnate by transferring themselves to other sources of biomass.

    This one centers more around the motivation for creating the lineage in the first place. One recurring reason that the Mad Scientist toils in his lab is to create the ULTIMATE LIFEFORM! Mwa ha ha!

    Quite why they want this or why they think they are any judge of what counts as perfection or supremacy is anyone’s guess, but it is a motivation that I don’t see in any of the current lineages.

    This is great source material for a Lineage, but probably not one that takes to the Pilgrimage well. Ultimate Life Forms in fiction tend to consider themselves superior to humanity and don’t see the need to change into something lesser. The other main issue with using them as inspiration is they are very diverse. Their Torments vary wildly, though they are often blinded by their arrogance, and they could easily fit with many of the extant Humours. As such, each of the inspirations is more likely a member of a different lineage than a variant of the same core one.

    There isn’t a clear example that fits more with Promethean than the rest, though Adam might make the most sense in a CofD setting. I’m not familiar enough with that season of Buffy to use him as a template, though.



    The Ultimate Life Forms

    Inspirations – Cell (DBZ), Adam (Buffy), Daleks (Doctor Who), Doomsday (Superman)

    Humour – Any (or Ichor)

    Ultimate Life Forms could use any of the existing Humours, though Yellow Bile and Phlegm fit particularly well. If they do have a unique humour, I would suggest the name Ichor, the blood of the gods.


    Torment – Arrogance and Blindness

    While their Torment will be heavily flavored by their Humour, it should also carry a distinct element of pride. They don’t do well when their superiority is called into question and they don’t like subservience, which makes sense as in many of their stories they kill their creators.


    Disquiet - Hatred

    Most likely out and out hatred. Ultimate Life Form inspirations tend to be monsters anyways, here they will be seen as such whether their personality matches or not.


    Wasteland - Mutual Animosity

    In ULF wastelands, people just do not get along and do not trust in other people’s humanity. Competition and hoarding become common as things run down as if they were never maintained.

      I always thought that it was a mistake to make an Unfleshed Lineage, and that "Unfleshed" should have been implemented in 2e more like how it was in 1e: as a sort of "overlay" to the Promethean, a high-tech version of the Construct. Because it's not really about the Promethean's personality or powers so much as it's about what it's made of.

      But 2e did make it a Lineage; and it did so by shaving off the "made to serve a purpose" part of the Tammuz and expanding it to a full Lineage. This leads to the oddity of having five Lineages that are normally made out of flesh and blood but can be made out of inert materials instead, and one Lineage that must be made out of inert materials. Were I to run a 2e Promethean game, I would either ditch the Unfleshed Lineage, or I would keep it but allow for "fleshy" Unfleshed as well as the more common Unfleshed that's made out of inert materials.

      An example of a "fleshy" Unfleshed would be a genetically engineered lifeform.

      You might also look more into Clones.

      As for the Ultimate Lifeform, I'd be more inclined to handle that as a Refinement. In particular, I could see it as the Refinement of Argent (the "crossover Refinement") but with a focus on Deviants (the monsters who were created through an effort to improve on ordinary humanity).



      Though speaking of Deviants, bear in mind that one of the Origins for a Deviant is the Genotypal — the ones who were Born that way. This can be used for "Remade" who were never human, or even organic, to begin with: for example, androids or aliens. If your Ultimate Lifeform isn't pursuing a Pilgrimage, it might actually be a Genotypal Deviant rather than a Promethean.

      My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

              For this one, the idea of the Lineage came before finding an example. Prometheans can be created for many reasons, and childish fantasies and fears are surprisingly powerful. It wouldn’t be unreasonable for a child to accidentally create a Promethean to protect themselves against the dark, or to be a friend, or any number of reasons.

              This would be a Lineage that starts from (and can probably masquerade as) children’s toys and that has elements that push it to stay connected to that role. And there are plenty of stories of mistrusted toys to choose from. There are also a number of stories of toys that want to be more than toys.

              I would stress that Pinocchio is not a good example of this lineage, the best iconic source is instead The Velveteen Rabbit. That story also happens to offer a great name for the Lineage.


              Skin Horses – The Creations of Children

              Inspirations – The Velveteen Rabbit, Hobbes, Talking Tina (The Twilight Zone)

              Humour - Blood

              Really, they could have a number of humours but I think that passion of Blood turned towards a nurturing or protective role fits best.


              Torment – Dependency

              Skin Horses have a deep need to foster, most often children, and in the depths of torment that can turn towards infantilizing or objectifying or possessing that person. On the flip side separation from their charges induces obsession and depression, punctuated by rage.


              Disquiet - Unhealth

              Sooner or later people will become concerned about the safety of the toy. It will be seen as mentally unhealthy, infectious, or unsafe. When the Skin Horse manifests as a person rather than a toy, that person will be an unhealthy influence, or possibly as a poor soul that needs help, preferably help that comes far away from here. Whatever the case, they are not safe to keep around.


              Wasteland - Askew

              The Skin Horse wasteland reinforces their Disquiet. It spreads malaise, and while it doesn’t actually give people mental conditions it does play with lighting and sound to make senses unreliable in a way that puts people off balance.


              Potential Bestowments

              A Child’s Plaything

              The Skin Horse can appear to be a completely normal child’s toy.

                                                      Other than that, I'm unsure if the concept is particularly interesting as a Promethean Lineage rather than Deviants, but I'm not strongly against it. I find the Skin Horses more interesting as potential new Lineages.

                                                      The ULF falls on what ArcaneArts said: intent is less important than the process. Any Demiurge may be thinking of their creation as the Ultimate Life Form, but how they approach its creation is what really shapes the Lineage. Overall I think this intent is best explored in Deviant simply because it begs for the actual person of the "creator" to be explored in the game, something Deviant does.

                                                          It's an interesting question. The obvious option is that the fleshed versions are disproportionately made from the remains of children, but that's a mental image I really don't care for. I have fewer problems with the construct versions appearing as children, though.

                                                          In a more general sense, there are two elements that make sense to me. The first is that they have a proportionality problem. Not the mismatched parts issue of the Frankensteins, but an issue that their proportions drift towards caricature. They are supposed to look human, but in a more representative fashion. This is something that is true of the constructed versions too. The second is that even when made from remains, there is still an element of construction to them. Their skin may be tanned to leather, for instance.
                                                          In regards to the first route I think that that's definitely something that the table as a whole would have to talk about; apart from the one example Galateid in the 2e book, the books didn't seem to say much about how commonly the corpses of children were used in generative acts. In any case, the second idea that you had actually reminded me of taxidermy and I think that that is where the Lineage could find it's niche. I think that there is a bit of an overlap with the embalmed appearance of the Osiran Disfigurements, but the whole idea of preserved parts being used to construct some sort of plaything makes it seem distinct enough to me. In that case, Skin Horses would be defined by how their bodies are made up of processed, treated flesh, not in the way that a corpse is treated to preserve it for burial, but in the way that animal hides are treated to become useful goods, like you hinted at with the mention of leather. I think that the aesthetics of mass production and disposability could go a long way in helping to flesh them out. There is some overlap with the themes of Galateids, but one of the main thorough lines of their journey could be struggling to obtain a feeling of worth when they were constructed from cheap yet unsettlingly antiseptic spare parts already removed from their original state in nature. While that does come close to encroaching on Galateid territory, I think that the Lineage that you propose would be distinct in that they were never intended to meet or surpass human perfection at all, which is part of why the Galateid fear that they are nothing but an imitation of humanity is relevant to them.
