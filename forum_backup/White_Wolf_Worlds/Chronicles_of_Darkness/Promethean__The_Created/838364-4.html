                    There are plenty of threads on the Requiem/Masquerade forum about removing Humanity or the Beast; this is like that. You don't take away a Promethean's curses/banes/condition/what-have-you - even as an alternative end-game - without changing something fundamental about the game.
                    A lot of folks have strong feelings about this subject. I, for one, think it's pretty clear "you can't be transhuman without first being human," and I think an end-goal for "better than Promethean" would still end with being mortal but - as I believe I posted in the other thread - opening up "ex-Promethean-only" merits that do, whatever it is you want.

                    They still have to cope with those curses, though.

                    Earlier, you mentioned that you don't think Promethean is being true to the core story of Frankenstein. Would you please elaborate? I think a lot of posters feel very differently and it would be helpful (for me) to understand why you believe this/where you're coming from.

A lot of people liked it, clearly, but I think a lot of people are maybe just liking anything that says "no transhumanism" at this point because this thread for some reason is irresistible to them. One of the things I personally like about the Chronicles of Darkness is that it's so okay with heresy not only does it refuse the concept of canon and translate everything into vague and often repeatedly-retold Just-So stories, chatroom rumors, or scribbled diaries written by the mad, it actually once went so far as to publish an actual book called Blasphemies for the express purpose of ENCOURAGING this kind of thing.

                                        But you're saying that in a thread where the OP has repeatedly and in no uncertain terms outlined their desire and intention, you were unsure of either their desire and intention and defaulted to the assumption that THEY didn't understand what they were reading? I think there's something incredibly insulting about that. It bothers me that your sentiment is the one people are joining their thumbs-up voice with. It bothers me a lot, actually. But my impression is that there's a sanction here for telling OP this whole thing is wrong as long as you abstract it a little. I think Promethean is a great story and very cool, but I also think ripping its guts out to feed your dreams on is totally fine and those stories can also be great and very cool.

                                        Anyway, the idea that it is in any sense difficult to "kludge" is the one I think it's going to actually be productive to deal with.

                                        It is incredibly easy to homebrew template elements in the Chronicles of Darkness, because they're only conceptually interrelated and those concepts are easily rewritten.

                                        Let's take it for granted that being mortal is pretty lame in a world where everyone knows someone who disappeared into the night. In fact even Promethean 1E understands this, offering vague suggestions of superhumanity (TRANShumanity, even?) and uncertain boons for those who complete their Pilgrimage as incentive. The writers were certainly conscious that "give up your awesome powers in exchange for not scaring the children so much" wasn't going to be an easy sell for everyone. So, okay. Screw that whole thing. So you have to kludge something different. I propose that this is not difficult.

                                        You've got some obvious drawbacks (Disquiet, Wastelands, Torment) and you've got the idea of the Divine Fire. Let's say that those bad things only happen because demiurges are crap at making Prometheans and they express Azoth imperfectly by nature, sort of like how a red strobelight is freaky even though there's nothing wrong with the lightbulb inside. So a Refinement, Lumium or whatever, that shapes you (spiritually and perhaps physically) to refine your channeling mechanisms to alleviate the bad stuff, the strobe effect as it were. Shine purely. Going with the OP's desire for human connection and the guardian angel idea, let's actually reverse the equation and say you can cause a kind of Disquiet that makes people look to you as a leader and trust you inherently - if you want tradeoffs, and OP mentioned they do, they also get really good at reading you and you still can't turn it off, so people are ALWAYS trusting you and looking to you as a leader. You can create Wastelands that frighten and eventually burn things in Twilight - but indiscriminately, so you end up with clean, modern, spiritually-dead towns that, lacking the usual "immune system" of a spiritual ecology, rely on you to defend and are somewhat more vulnerable to physical things moving in. Rather than Torment, you enter creative fugues where you are driven to build and refine Great Works, possibly that produce small measures of Vitriol, but these things draw Pandorans like a lighthouse and often demand rare and dangerous components SOMEONE has to go and get before they can get down to business and ride out the compulsion.

                                        How's that look?

                                                        If the problem is that the Divine Fire is basically the mystical equivalent to Naquadria, why not just get rid of it entirely? No Azoth. No Pyros. The following Spheres are probably what you need to create a non-DF based Promethean: Spirit, Mind, Prime, and either Matter or Life depending on the model. If you want a purely digital character who exists as software inside computer systems, you can get rid of the Matter or Life and use Forces instead. Take spells that mimic the effects of whatever Promethean powers you want. Replace Disquiet, Wastelands, and Firestorms with whatever Paradox Flaws you find to be appropriate. Arrange a permanent working or two to give yourself the passive benefits of being Promethean (resilience, heal via electricity, etc). Your paradigm? "I'm a Promethean."
