    Originally posted by Geckopirateship View Post
    And here's another problem with the “play another game” thing: I want a personal horror game about being a created being based on the Creature, an android, and so on that uses themes of alchemy, coming to terms with being a monster, and personal development. The most logical thing for me to do for that is to alter Promethean. No other game is going to fit that desire as well as a modified version of Promethean. No other game hits those notes.
    I've ever favored the “play another game” argument; my own position is “know what you're getting into, and be respectful of the game as it's written and of its fans”. But with that said, I wonder how well Deviant will hit those notes? From what I understand, it's got two x-splats, one being “how you came to be what you are” and the other being “the nature of your Variants”. If those splats include androids and alchemy, respectively, would that not suit your needs?

    But my bottom line is this: hack away at the game all you want; change whatever you wish about it. But don't presume that your hack is better than the original in any objective sense; and if you do, don't take offense when fans of the original defend it. Don't take offense, and don't misinterpret them as being “opposed to change”, “reactionary”, or anything of the sort — in fact, don't address them at all. If you truly think that your version is objectively better, argue for that on the merits, and refrain from ad hominem attacks.

                                                It is entirely possible to explain why ones dislike an idea or approach without being super radically offensive to people's faces.

                                                Not Promethean related, but as an example, me and buddy of mine got into a conversation about video games came up, and Overwatch came up. I mentioned I didn't like Overwatch, and my buddy asked me why. I explained my position calmly, rationally, without getting on him for liking it, making sure I explained how I felt about the aesthetics, the gameplay, and the actual reasons why it just didn't grab me-namely that . I did not just got "Gah, it's all terrible terrible terrible, and people who like it should burn" because, honestly, I'm not going to pretend I get why people like it, and I'm not going to tell them they shouldn't play it just cause I don't like it. I am not the audience.

                                                Even better, acknowledge the other person's point before putting your out there. I'm not particularly thrilled by Overwatch, going back to that, but I understand that lots of people like it, and I can try to understand reasons. I mean, I can't for Overwatch, so I keep my mouth shut, but letting people know that you at least are trying to comprehend them makes them more receptive to what you have to say without you having to kick in the door.

                                                So, yeah, just some thoughts.
