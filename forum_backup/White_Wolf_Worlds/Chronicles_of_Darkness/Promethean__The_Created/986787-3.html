        Servility - Homunculus Elpis Replacement
        Homunculi are half-formed in both body and mind. Though they are conscious creatures and possessed of so many gifts both mortal and Promethean, the powerful link they share with their creators stunts their psyche. For many it feels as though their mind is not their own, while others show a childlike desire to please at any cost. Others feel as though they are little more than an extension of their creator's being. While this does not prevent any homunculus from becoming a unique individual with their own goals and aspirations, few find the independence to push past this limitation.

        Servility reflects the Aspirations of the homunculus' master. The homunculus need not have any express knowledge of these Aspirations, he understands his master's satisfaction or frustration through the bond they share. The homunculus gains a point of Willpower in the following situations:
        • The first time in a scene that the homunculus advances or hinders his master's Aspirations.
        • The homunculus sacrifices his dignity, well-being, or agency for the sake of his master.

        The homunculus regains all Willpower the first time in a chapter that his master achieves an Aspiration, or finds that Aspiration now impossible.

        A homunculus cannot spend Willpower to harm his master, or to contest or resist his master's actions. If his master ever attacks him, the homunculus immediately gains the Beaten Down Tilt. In Social Maneuvering between a homunculus and his master, the homunculus only ever has a single Door.

                            Unless you rule that Alchemists can use their Magnitude the way a Promethean uses Azoth in order to sustain their Pandorans, but I doubt that would work since Alchemists aren't pretty much running on alchemical reagents, nor can Pandorans normally derive Pyros from their flesh. Either way, it's possible for an Alchemist to control some Pandorans for a while, but I don't think it's very sustainable. Even if you allow them to use Magnitude like Azoth, they're still going to be taking a pretty huge chunk out of themselves faster than they can heal it.

                            Verge I'm loving the Servility mechanic, it certainly makes a lot of sense in terms of what we're working with so far. I also like the difference in early life and dynamic between Homunculi and Prometheans, in that Homunculi have a chance to learn the ropes of society more thoroughly than Prometheans, thanks to a lack of normal Disquiet, and they get a decent backstory out of it too. It also means they have the opportunity to make choices that a Promethean usually doesn't, decisions about their creator, their relationship with them, and how they'll move forwards. Prometheans are on a Pilgrimage because they must be, while Homunculi step on the Pilgrimage because they choose to.

                            I think there's something interesting tied up in that. For a Promethean, you could make an argument that the Pilgrimage is about discovering themselves, forging an identity and a place in a hostile world, drawing a line in the sand and saying 'this is who I am' and performing the Great Work so that the universe is in accord with this declaration. Homunculi still get that, but I think there's a subtle difference that I can't quite put my finger on.

                            In regards to people other than Alchemists making Homunculi, I don't see why not. It could be the only way an Extempore initially founds a Lineage, by creating Homunculi and then eventually exposing them to Life-Giving Firestorms. A Demiurge could make a Homunculus, and the relationship between the two is likely to be very different. In alchemical writings, the Homunculus basically exists solely for the purpose of deriving power from its tissues and fluids, or keeping it for its services, it is a sign of the mastery of your art, more of a status symbol than anything. For a Demiurge, the Homunculus can actually be what they want them to be. A child, a friend, a lover, etc. but the relationship is still co-dependent and likely not healthy. Whether the Demiurge knows it or not, the Homunculus still basically exists as an extension of their own desires.

                            If we're going to let a sublimatus make Homunculi, that would definitely say something fascinating about their state. If they can make Homunculi, can they become a proper Promethean? Can they go on the Pilgrimage? The main reason I'd see a sublimatus keeping a Homunculus around is that, firstly, they'd get a convenient Pyros snack that would be disinclined to run away, but they'd also get a servant who can deal with humans in a way they, or their fellow Pandorans, couldn't. This would be super-helpful for a sublimatus like the Lady of Chains, for example. There's one other thing that comes to mind, however, and that's the thing mentioned earlier about Homunculi providing their master with a point of Pyros per day. This would mean that a sublimatus could achieve true balance, something resembling an actual life so long as their servant remains alive.

                                            Disfigurements and Disquiet
                                            Homunculi cannot easily disguise their Disfigurements. They lack the the deceptions that spare their Promethean kin the worst reactions, and must rely on disguise, concealment, or Transmutations to avoid detection. The single exception is when they are in their master's presence, where they can bask in their rightful Azothic Radiance. Here the veneer cloaks the homunculus, hiding their true form and deepening their dependence. The illusion holds for little a few hundred yards at the furthest, and fades at the end of the scene if the homunculus and its master are separated. Any use of Pyros by the homunculus dispels the veil immediately, and it cannot be restored until the end of the scene.

                                            The grotesque forms of homunculi instill Disquiet in mortals, but in a form muted and fleeting. They trigger Disquiet normally, but if the homunculus does nothing to worsen it before parting with the affected mortals, then the Condition disappears at the end of that scene. Some things do cement Disquiet, like the presence of Prometheans or Pandorans, or other concentrations of Pyros or Flux, in which case the Disquiet resolves per its normal rules.

                                            The presence of the homunculus' master also complicates Disquiet. If the homunculus is currently disguised by its master's presence, but still inflicts Disquiet on a mortal, the master takes or worsens a Disquieted Condition instead of the mortal. While the master is Disquieted, the homunculus is also Tormented. The homunculus can reduce the Disquiet by a stage by avoiding any contact with the master, for a number of days equal to that stage. For example, Friday the homunculus has managed to drive her master into stage 3 Disquiet. If she flees for three days, she can drive him down to stage two, and if she hides for six days, she can come back and hope for a semblance of normalcy.

                                                            So I've been following along and this looks pretty exciting. Things that have me worried is that with an Elpis/Virtue alternative, it almost seems like an entirely new template is being made rather than the Revenant-like rules tweaking that inspired it. Not sure why a Homonculi couldn't just straight up have Azoth though. Seems like the master-monster relationship would be better focused on controlling said Azoth, like Homonculi have deep penalties to using things that require Pyros unless in their master's presence. And likewise they cause Disquiet and Wastelands with much greater frequency and strength unless within their master's presence. I think a mechanic like this would reinforce the dependence angle of the toxic relationship and also make opportunities for being uplifted into full Prometheanhood all the more appealing.

                                                            Regarding uplifting, I don't see a problem with Homunculi being generic (like Revenants) and the uplifting process is one of refining a blank Humour into one that can generate Vitriol. And obviously Homunculi cannot generate Vitriol Beats (or Experiences). And so cannot buy Athanors, Pilgrimage, or calcify Alembics.

                                                            Regarding Roles, not certain why a Homunculus couldn't have one. It would just be locked into its role and unable to progress until uplifted.

                                                            Regarding Integrity, I'd jettison it in favor of Pilgrimage locked into one because it's obvious that a Homunculus should want to be anything other than what it is.

                                                            Anyway, that's my 2 pence worth. Keep up the hacking. I'm looking forward to using/adapting the results.
