                                                Because he also happens to be a character in DC comics, I decided to stat up the Frankenstein Monster. About 3/4ths of the way through, I realized the outrageous side effects of Azoth 10. Considering I have him stalled on the Pilgrimage in the slums of the Metropolis, if he rejoins the Pilgrimage, he's going to Taint the entirety of new england. XD That's a scary thought. He's got quite a few calcified alembics, but he can't charge them without, again, yanking all of New England into a wasteland. So... despite his rather sizeable dice pools for most everything (Including a whopping +10 stamina on top of his already sizeable normal stamina.), hes actually.... rather useless lol. He has a hovel set up, where he spends his days drinking beer (its organic, so he can survive on beer alone) and watching reality TV shows.. Whenever he leaves, he jars half of his Azoth. He keeps some, just in case the Bride has found him. But really, he's stuck in a rut as a Copper Hermit, reminiscing about his days in the Creature Commandos and his friendship with Wonder Woman. His adopted "kid", Wonder Girl, is trying to get him started on the Pilgrimage despite the..errr... side effects, and has enlisted a Lead Osirian to forge a Throng with him, her, the nepri, solomon grundy, and possibly clayface. Its a rather sad idea, though, that because of Azoth 10's side effects, he can't do much without causing electrical fires through the entire densely populated northeast. I have a hard time imagining when he'd ever actually use his full Azoth, as he relies on his Hovel and Dampening the Flame to prevent... ya know... a Firestorm or something. Azoth 10 is seriously scary. Not because of dice pools, but how ridiculous a wasteland they cause by spending one single Pyros. I suppose that's why his Pilgrimage kept sending him to the North Pole.

                                                        You know, because a Wasteland Festers whenever one of its Festering Triggers comes along, an Azoth 10 Promethean really cutting loose doesn't, technically, need to leave those Wastelands they make sitting around for very long. In the space of a couple seconds they can start no less than three Firestorms at once. (They can spend 15 Pyros per turn, and need only five to bring it to a full Firestorm) Admittedly, that could conceivably give them, like, five Alembics for the scene, which could be insane.

                                                        Alternatively, we could have an Azoth 10 Promethean who happens to have the Blaze of Glory Alembic from the Luciferus Transmutation available. They could conceivably unleash all of their 100 points of Pyros at once, causing no less than 20 hurricane-sized Firestorms at once. That... is not an experience most people would survive. Seriously, a Promethean that powerful could level a small country. The changes left in their wake would be indescribable. We're talking people suddenly swinging from normal to rabid and back again as Disquiet levels fluctuate, Prometheans (and possibly Vitriol Founts) flooding with the precious distillation of the Pilgrimage, Created, Pandorans, and/or Alchemists alike drowning in or stripped of Pyros. Every Pandoran in the country could die, every person in that radius could suddenly become incredibly physically hale or decrepit, and that still isn't factoring any collateral damage from the Firestorms themselves.
