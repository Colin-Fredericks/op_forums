        Taidragon: When I was coming up with the Abyssal plot, it was problematic to put it in New Haven because something like that would not go unnoticed by the thriving supernatural community I had established New Haven had. I asked some friends who were from that general area originally and they suggested Glenwood was "not far" and "kinda run down" so I went with it. I then started adding to it. I wanted a pure headquarters, and it worked for that as the Pure are kinda messed up. The Balehound story was not in and of itself there from the get go (the seeds were) so when I started fleshing that out Glenwood seemed a good place and added a level of tension. Candle Cove seemed an ideal way to introduce the place. I have no idea what real Glenwood is like, it may be nice, it may be urban sprawl. One of my dearest friends is from Glastonbury, but I have personally never traveled further North than DC in North America unless we count a Canadian airport stopover.


        baileyborough: These are after action reports from a face to face game in my gaming room here at the house, all surrounded by Gargoyles and Living Dead Dolls (Okay, only last year or so has been in my house, the rest were my apartment). My other games in my sig are all either at my house or a friend's house. All the dialogue essentially happened in game; we are very heavy on rp.

        If you have ever read any of Speed Demon's journals over in Demon, those are a pretty accurate blow-by-blow description of events, and all the dialogue there happened in game too. In Changeling Lori's player takes minor creative liscense but it is overall accurate to in game dialogue.

        Now there is some fiction, but I try to make that clear when I am posting them when it is fiction not in game events. Recently, that has been Natalie stories, but there was a story about Doc and Gwen awhile back that was fiction too. That cover everything?

                    Tensions continue to escalate in stately Fishborne manor. Tensions of all kinds. Can they find the Bale Hound before things descend into chaos?

                    Doc’s player had to miss due to vacationing and such

                    Session CXLVIII

                    Sara’s Letters

                    Sasha -

                    Lords and ladies, I don’t like this. I don’t like this I don’t like this I don’t like this. I don’t need the Sight to see this going horribly, and I don’t like it. Fate may have decided to give us an answer or two, but she’s dealt us a shit hand.

                    OK, for starters - we’re going back to Glenwood, and that’s technically the good news. The spirit gave us a location for one of Bright Shadows’ headquarters but since that wasn’t her only one, just the one the spirit knew about, we need to move fast before she realizes it’s compromised.

                    On the other hand, the spirit is also a deceitful piece of shit. Oh, it might not have been capable of lying, but it sure did its best to get Angelique killed by a clever application of the truth. It insisted on calling her Master, going on about how much Bright Shadows missed her. It also called her Bone Wanderer and claimed she’d met with Bright Shadows at Murder Ballads recently.

                    That matched unfortunately with other things. Rawl had been poking around Murder Ballads while Angelique was getting things set up, so Noriko and Alice went to make sure he wasn’t up to anything because fuck plausible deniability. He could tell it was a place suffused with dark energy, some place Bright Shadows might be able to recharge, as it were. Once they’d started poking around, Rawl thought he found signs of Angelique, though Alice said it was merely similar, like at the bank.

                    That was a week or two ago. Angelique, when I described the vision I’d had of Bright Shadows, didn’t recognize her, but did have an anonymous hookup at Murder Ballads after Alice broke up with her. So, about a week ago. She didn’t remember a face, or a name, but had been certain it wasn’t anyone of the family - something Bright Shadows should have shown up as to her Sight. It doesn’t add up, but we don’t have enough pieces to do anything about it.

                    My lovely Angelique was terrified the entire time, mostly of what Eris and Noriko would do to her. I tried to point out that if the spirit truly served her, it would not say so in front of those two, and that perhaps if a creature of deception, whose major MO was to turn people against each other, bound an evil spirit to Angelique, it might not have been because Angelique was her best bud. That was possibly why they didn’t kill her before the ritual ended, but it didn’t help with the torrents of self-hatred and fear Angelique was under. She made some questionable decisions, including flat out asking Noriko to kill her.

                    She also told Eris about her past in about the worst way possible, at least at first, saying she was like this Bright Shadows. Not saying it was her mentor that had been, that she’d been coerced and tricked into it, that she’d been an apprentice at worst. She got to that stuff eventually, but her initial confession put murder right the fuck back in Eris’ eyes. And of course, Eris has to tell her people. Saints that they are, they’ve decided to probably kill her for being an unwitting tool when she was very young after she’s helped us deal with the evil spirits arrayed against us.

                    In the meantime, Doc would have exploded if he caught half of the intense looks our Little Red and Noriko had been giving each other, Mr Malard is going off the deep end especially where Angelique is concerned, I had to exert every bit of willpower I had not to tear Angelique’s clothes off on the walk from Malard’s to home, and my Natalie is getting a couple of cognacs to the wind before we go to track down a seriously dangerous bad guy. Not to mention, that other than Angelique having a second personality or something, we’ve got no idea how any of our information fits together, dibs has been called on who gets to kill Angelique, and I haven’t slept in way too long.

                    I really don’t like this.

                    You take care of yourself. I’ll get back to you as soon as I can.

                    Sara
                    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                    Sara glanced over at the beautiful werewolf, curled up in the corner of the couch. She closed her laptop. You ask much.

                    ‘I’m not asking you anything,’ Sara thought resignedly. ‘I’ve told you, we - you - don’t get to decide what it is appropriate for others to sacrifice.’

                    Is that so? Sara felt her geist smile sadly. I didn’t say you ask much of me. You ask much of her. Sara didn’t respond, reaching out to brush a strand of hair from Angelique’s face. She is so tired.

                    ‘She can rest soon.’

                    Will you let her? Or will you keep asking her to sacrifice that for your sake? For others’ sake? For the world’s? The geist rested her hands on Sara’s shoulders. What god do you call out to? What prayer do you send?

                    The dark haired Sin-Eater bit her lip and ran a finger along the curve of the other woman’s chin. ‘It’s not like that. It’s the Balehound’s influence. She doesn’t deserve to die.’

                    So sometimes it is you who gets to decide who deserves what? Sometimes the call to sacrifice is stronger than the call of desire? The geist’s voice was measured and calm. She paused until Sara began to say something before she continued. Or is it that you would prefer she bore the burden of sacrifice this time rather than you?

                    “That’s not...” Angelique stirred as Sara spoke aloud. Sara smiled quickly as the werewolf’s eyes fluttered open. “Sorry, love I didn’t mean to wake you. However... we should probably find the others, it should be about time to go.”

                    Angelique nodded tiredly and stood to leave. Sara watched her stretch before turning to stash her laptop, her smile fading and shoulders slumping. When she turned back around, Angelique was looking at her with concern. Sara smiled slightly, moving closer as her geist spoke again. What prayers do you send?

                    Sara looked down and took another step towards Angelique. “We will find this Balehound and stop her from hurting anyone else.” She laid a hand on the werewolf’s cheek.

                    As Angelique leaned down to kiss the Sin-Eater, her geist spoke softly, so even Sara didn’t hear. May the gods accept your offering, this time.


                    Natalie’s Tweets

                    Karen & Tatum

                    I realize you two are probably asleep. That’s fine, this is for if things go wrong. Or just in case insomnia has you.

                    It’s been an intense night. Sara and I (and everyone else) are okay. It’s not done. We are about to go back to Glenwood.

                    Angelique determined she needed to prepare a second prison for the Demon, so if she gets out of control she can imprisoned quickly, or if she takes hold of Angelique somehow. So Sara and Angelique went next door and got one, then Angelique began a three-hour ritual to create it.

                    After that started, Alice got a call from Noriko, that she had spotted Rawl near Murder Ballads. Alice told us and went to get her axe, so Sara decided to call Rawl. According to Rawl, he was looking for a cluster of negative emotions that could empower the Bale Hound, and the depths of Murder Ballads could do that. I have been improving the spiritual health of the place, but it still had some bad areas. There was an amusing exchange when Rawl assured us he did a ritual so no Uratha would find him, but as he was bragging Noriko grabbed him. Alice went off and we hoped nothing bad would happen.

                    I got a call from Noriko saying that Bright Shadow had been there, but it was some time ago. Rawl sensed her, and Alice interrogated Rory James. That fucker. Still, she wanted to prepare a trap. I agreed to talk to Alice about it. When Alice and Noriko returned we discussed talking to the management and finding out who rented the room she had been in (which Kinksters used for awhile but had recently changed. Karen any of your friends know about this?) and may can get more information. Rory is supposed to contact Alice if he sees something, though she understands he is a fucker. Of course, that may be moot.

                    Alice went up to see Eris the rest of the time, but it was obvious there was growing, rapidly, sexual tension between she and Noriko. I had never seen Noriko look at a woman besides me, so that honestly surprised me. But I did not know just how far gone Noriko was.

                    Sara and I talked some while Alice spent time with Eris, Doc worked on Erin, and Alexia and Chandra napped…or “napped,” and we discussed what Angelique said, that the issue seems to be taboos with this Bale Hound. Sara and I were less affected (though Sara admitted kissing Angelique outside en route back) because we are not with as many sexual/romantic Taboos. Sure, we have plenty in general, but few are romantic or sexual. I am more attracted to Doc than I usually am (sorry, Karen) and became surprisingly so toward Jacob near the end, so my “weak point” seems to involve men. And possibly wanting babies. Short people who are truly dangerous to fool around with, Sara’s libertine nature gives her few taboos, so she is substantially less affected. Alexia seems similar…while she puts some prudish rules down, she rarely resists her desires, though Chandra’s “topping off” on Sara and my breasts may indicate she is more affected than her wife.

                    Angelique got first the trapping ritual and then the binding ritual ready, and summoned the Demon. The Demon subserviently called her Master, and seemed if anything perplexed as to why she was binding it and not working with Bright Shadow, acting as if she had been the whole time. I admit this was working well, Eris and Noriko were staring her down with murder in their eyes. Alice and Alexia aggressively spoke up to defend her, and while I had the Passion Marionette attached to her, she remained in control with no strings tugged. Which is good, I hate using mind powers on people. I would rather just talk. The Spirit claimed Angelique (or Bone Wanderer) as she called her had met Bright Shadow at Murder Ballads, but claimed to be not present for the actual discussion. Convenient. Angelique doubted her sanity at this point, and after a point just trapped the Demon again…in the first vessel, which she earlier said did not work. She then rushed to Noriko and displayed her throat, and her eyes told Noriko to kill.

                    I honestly think she started to, but Alice and Sara intervened even as I yanked Angelique’s cords. Both women were talked down. Eris demanded an explanation, and Angelique yelled that she used to be a Bale Hound. Eris promptly joined team “Kill Angelique” Somewhere in the argument Eris realized she was on a sacred hunt and could consume the Demon and her Essence, and did so…the Demon was easily knocked out of the vessel with a swat, so she was never really trapped again. Angelique admitted she did not know why she did not use the vessel she spent hours making, saying using the old “Seemed right at the time.” So she was influenced…Sara said her emotions seemed a kaleidoscope.

                    Angelique did tall about her history, how her Mentor was the only other Bone Shadow she ever knew, how the pack was unsure and had driven her out, and confessed even she did not know how corrupted she was. It may have helped a little bit, but Eris still considered her a Bale Hound (and as people are flying off the handle and Angelique was a romantic rival, that was probably also a thing). She insisted she would tell her Pack.

                    Somewhere in there Noriko made a comment about killing Angelique, and Alice cast a fierce look at her friend. While Noriko had, throughout the night, stared back defiantly at anyone who tried to intimidate her, this time she looked stricken and curled up, sobbing. Once things were calm. Alice went to comfort her…and I swear, no lez glasses, it looked like they were about to kiss. Between murderous impulses and strange attractions, Noriko is absolutely deeply affected.

                    Most of us are going a little crazy.

                    The Demon had told us a location in Glenwood (she conveniently only knew one, though she pointed out there were two we had already been to so she actually knew three). We decided to move that night. Eris called Ildefonso, and of course told him about Angelique. His compromise was nothing now, but she would need to be dealt with when Bright Shadow was no longer a threat.

                    Sara called Zed then Rawl. Zed gathered the troops, while Rawl went to tell his Pack. I, well, needed some fortification for this, and downed one bottle of Cognac and started on my second when Sara came up and scooped me up and kissed me in front of everyone. At first I thought she was dealing with her issues, and she may have been, but she also pointed out she was trying to help with mine. I had been all but ignored in this drama, with my more libertine lifestyle shielding me a bit, but that meant I was getting little attention and well…yea. I admitted I was considering acting out, though in my defense I had not thought of it that way.

                    So to Glenwood. Again. You can imagine I am excited. Goddess willing, we will be back before sunrise, but I doubt it.

                    You two stay tight over there. No need to get involved in all this fucking Drama. Remember if we go silent, don’t go near anywhere we may be until you are contacted.

                    All my love and Sara’s too,

                    Natalie----Allison

                    End Session CXLVIII

                    Doc not being there slowed things down a bit. The intent was to cover his meeting with the Sleeping Giant Pack first, then have that define mood, but also to have Natalie and he having ramping up attraction as her body screamed “Want Baby Now!”

                    There were few mechanics or scenes undescribed. The scene with the Demon and the interrogation was really tense, and a lot of fun to play.

                    Okay, it’s been fun stringing folks along, but the first of two major steps to resolve the whole thing happens next session. Not an end to the story, but the step before…it’s complicated. You’ll see.

                    Comments and Questions welcome
