                    On topic? *shrugs* We keep ending up hitting Alien and Djinn as game ideas (pretty much every year a new game comes out and this thread makes a new appearance), then people keep saying that its just repeats of old games. Then debates go on and on about new themes.

                    Every time, though, there are bids for a number of writers for new concepts and new angles to explore in society. There could be games based on the horrors of supposedly faceless corporations on the world, for instance, with the supernatural aspects being a Tulpa (mascot character come to life as the actual "face" of the corporation?) I don't know, that would need a lot more fleshing out than I'm willing to give it right now. But that's just an example of something that could be done off the top of my head. There's the Fury game I've mentioned before. Liches could be a thing - a game of seeking non-vampire immortality at any cost. As always, I think that cat-shifters need more love somewhere as PCs; we've got wolves as a full game, serpents in Beast, and vamps make excellent spider metaphors. But cats never really get a fair shake so far.

                    I think we're moving into an area, however, where we need to be asking the question of "should there be new game lines?" Over-saturation is a thing, and we have had years where the theme was something like Night Horrors for every existing line instead of something new. I'm not personally interested in a million new games, but creating new spins on existing games, improving and expanding.

                                                    Originally posted by Alberto Barbosa View Post
                                                    So far as I can tell, there isn't anything for just plain robots, cyborgs, or androids that are not Prometheans.

                                                    There are plenty of options for cyborgs, androids, and robots, depending on just what you're looking for.

                                                    Deviants, as nofather mentioned, will definitely include cyborgs.

                                                    Cyborgs are one of the microctemplates that will be included in Hurt Locker.

                                                    Bleeding Edge has rules for cybernetics for anyone, template or not.

                                                    The endowment rules for Hunter could definitely be used for cybernetics, they're generic.

                                                    The Mage Chroniclers Guide has a hack for turning mages into mad scientists, in which case you could reskin the proximi rules for android, robot, and cyborg types.

                                                    Dark Heroes from Mirrors could definitely be reskinned as Six Million Dollar Man types.

                                                    If you want something that transforms (not necessarily into machines, but more along the lines of having retractable armor and the like), you could reskin the shapeshifter rules from War Against the Pure.

                                                    Not that I'm saying that OP couldn't make a line about androids, robots, and cyborgs if they so desired, one could play a werewolf type, magician type, or mummy type in Vampire, it didn't stop them from writing forsaken, awakening, or curse.
