    02-22-2015, 08:43 PM

The passages in the dark era preview imply that the Ubar conflict was a world war sort of thing, so I'm trying to imagine other national entities that may have been aware of or involved in the dispute.

                      My depictions of Irem almost invariably look at it through the eyes of the player characters, so I haven't really bothered to think about it outside of that context. The City of Pillars itself, and their lives within it, are really the only elements I have mapped out. As a consequence, my own version of Irem is remarkably self-absorbed. The characters are at the heart of the world's first great empire, and what exists beyond the incomprehensibly vast (from their perspective) territory claimed by the Shan'iatu is largely irrelevant. In keeping with this theme, I don't imagine Irem carrying out much trade outside of its own borders. Despite the existence of relics that allow for transportation to anywhere in the world, I have the empire being far more reliant on mundane travelling, and that means the constrains of geography keep them from going too far past the borders described in the books. Certainly no contact with the Americas, I doubt they even reached the Western Mediterranean.
