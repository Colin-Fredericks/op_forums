    For Tropicana, Light for fire/sunlight, maybe Temper or Boil for the Sear/Catalyze aspect as you are effectively heating something up to prepare, and Warmth as in comfort and pleasure.

    For Gardenia, possibly switch Plant to Growth as in literal and figurative Growth? It would allow each Water to have an Opus that is more figurative, along the lines of Freeze and Temper/Boil.

    For Vaporia, Wind, Weather or Sky, and maybe Pressure in a sense of a feeling of metaphorical weight or meaning (considering that the Vaporian Sirens are waif-like and are more-likely to appear to a mortal, tell them that they have a hard decision to make, and disappear without another word).

    For Discordia, Blood, Insanity or Chaos, and maybe Taint as in impurities or damage like rust or mold?

    ---

    I think that we may have to do something to the Opuses as some can be more literal while others are more metaphorical. Considering that [Travel] is one of the Conclusions, it would be odd to try to use [Freeze][Travel] to...stop movement? To travel through things that have been stopped? Or having to [Travel] through [Temper/Boil]?

    Dark, Death, Human, Technology, Light, Plant, Earth, Animal, Wind, Blood, and maybe Taint are fairly literal and would make sense to be [Travel]ed, [Restore]ed, or [Destroy]ed.

    Freeze, Fate, Sear/Temper/Boil, Warmth, Pressure, and Insanity/Chaos seem more metaphorical and wouldn't necessarily work with [Travel], [Protect] or even [Sense]. Because of this, I feel that I should go back on my idea to keep everything literal and instead have both literal and figurative interpretations?

    Take Freeze for example. When it was written in the document for the Polarians, it was stated that it stopped progress like it froze water into ice, which to me made it seem like it was more of a metaphorical type. Plus, the fact that it is called "Freeze" which is more of a verb makes it seem more like a Conclusion. Maybe renaming it to Frost and having the interpretation also include ice and snow. Or it could be a Conclusion that is exclusive to the Polarians?

    It would significantly lighten the amount of Opuses and even out the total between Opuses and Conclusions to have the Waters provide a choice between two Opuses and granting a free Conclusion. While [Calm] does allow for the chosen Opus to be released, it doesn't necessarily stop it dead in its tracks, which could tie into the fact that Sirens are from a different time and are able to slightly affect the way that time flows through special Conclusions.

    At least, it will make it so that Opuses aren't incredibly brain-busting to work through and allow for a greater pool for what the Verses would be capable of.

    If the idea does fly, then the Polarians would be able to [Freeze] things in time, Tropicans could [Temper] things and cause them to escalate, Gardenians could [Nourish] things by allowing them to be undisturbed, Vaporians could [Push] things by allowing them to be noticed immediately, Freshwaters could [Refine] things by allowing progress to move through them, and Discordians could [Disturb] things by causing them to experience a temporally displaced version of The Deluge. Then the Waters would look something like this:

    Polaria: [Freeze] Darkness or Death
    Tropicana: [Temper] Light or Warmth
    Gardenia: [Nourish] Earth or Animal
    Vaporia: [Push] Wind or Weather
    Freshwater: [Refine] Human or Technology
    Discordia: [Disturb] Blood or Insanity/Chaos (Chaos interpretations would include junk piles and crowds of people, basically things that are considered chaotic)


    Indeed. I imagine this to be actually a thing where Werewolves and Sirens wouldn't get along in crossovers. Sirens (at least most of them) don't mind sharing waters to regain Pneuma, whereas Werewolves always have some territory devoted to themselves. If Werewolves tried pushing Sirens away, I don't think that Sirens would sit down to take it though.
    I doubt that Sirens would leave anything to their own devices unless the Sirens were sure that nothing bad would happen. Sirens and Werewolves would probably have a tentative peace-like agreement that gets strained whenever a Werewolf feels like they need to establish dominance or protect their territory or whenever a Siren attempts to play peacekeeper or attract the attention of humanity. The two would definitely be sick of each other, but almost at the level of bureaucratic disagreements that would slowly lead into grudges and eventual murder attempts.

    I also feel like we need to start discussing how the Currents would factor in, considering that we have done a lot of talk about the Oceans which are now Waters.

The second part, the [Opus] refers to what is being sought after in the [Target] area, like finding all the [Light] within an [Area] of a room. The third word, the Conclusion, would affect the [Opus] within the [Target], so [Area][Light][Destroy] would snuff out candle light, bust out the light bulbs, or put a block within a city into a black out, as you said.             I will say that I still want to push for the Opuses to not be actions and to be based around the object that the Verse is meant to control/create/etc. [Freeze] being used to stop things doesn't make much sense if everything else refers to a concept that would tangibly exist. I feel that [Freeze] works better as a definition for things that are cold or have gone into cool-down, like a stopped car. Let me type up what I think the Opuses should feel like:

            Polaria: [Darkness] for shadows and the absence of light or even nightmares, [Death] for dead bodies and ghosts, and [Freeze/Frost] for things that are literally cold or are on "cool-down"

            Tropicana: [Light] for sunlight or light sources and fire, [Passion] for emotions and love, and [Temper] for things that are hot or are being heated up like a debate

            Gardenia: [Earth] for soil or stones and unrefined metals, [Animal] for creatures that aren't capable of free will, and [Growth] for vegetation or things that are capable of growing like mold or a movement

            Vaporia: [Wind] for air currents and breezes, [Weather/Sky] for things that are naturally occurring events like sandstorms or rain, and [Pressure] for air pressure or things that have a heavy metaphorical weight like decisions or guilt

            Freshwater: [Human] for mortals untouched by the supernatural, [Technology] for electronics or devices created out of refined metal such as cars, and [Fate] for fortune or things that are related to the idea of destiny

            Discordia: [Blood] as in blood or possibly even flesh, [Taint] for impurities or decay like rust or oil spills, and [Chaos] for junk piles or situations that can be described as chaotic

            I figure that, if we're going with the idea that "Some Verses contain conflicting or vague ideas that will cause them to fail" route, we could have a Tag system where Conclusions like [Travel] need Opuses that have the (Material) Tag so that there is something that the Siren can [Travel] through. Like, [Passion] isn't necessarily material, so a Siren can't create a [Passion][Travel] Verse but she is free to create [Darkness/Light/Human][Travel] Verses because those are material objects and have the (Material) Tag.

I would also presume that there are Opuses that are automatically favored or unfavored like [Water], [Sound], [Beauty], and such.
