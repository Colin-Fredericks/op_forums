    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    Some questions and thoughts on this:

    1 - How you explain Essence here, or their need for it, is immaterial. What’s the purpose of this added layer of complexity for the game design?

    2 - For whatever it matters, “typical” Jiang-Shi feed only on qi, not on qi AND blood. And qi isn’t Essence. Essence in CoD is a specific substance linked to the Shadow and its Spirit denizens. Qi is an energy/substance related to breathing and fuels normal life, it has zero to do with spirit.

    3 - As comic as it seems, their rigidity, not their breath-feeding, is their defining feature in both folklore and pop culture. They may be agile, even supernaturally so, but never supple, they’re stiff as dead matter, or even as if in rigor mortis.

    4 - Jiang-shi that don’t “eat” qi, which isn’t uncommon, eat meat. Again, not blood.

    5 - Tying their basic survival to a Discipline is a big problem. Disciplines are optional powers, and ones that anyone can theoretically learn on top of that. If you want to keep them needing a qi-like resource, being able to feed should come in their basic package.

    6 - If you want to keep it as a second resource, get rid of the need, it should only be used to fuel the Discipline that creates it in the first place, or as an optional alternative to uses of Vitae. And if you want to tie it to some stat, tie it to the Discipline level.

    7 - Wolves don’t have “Alphas”. Drop it, that’s an old and very dangerous misconception that’s used to justify a lot of harmful behavior in real life.
    1 - It's because I want them to have more connection to the world of ghost, spirit, and other ephemeral being, other than just their Clan Bane, and a few weird abilities. And also it would explain why their number is so low, other than their embrace being more special, but their embrace in the core rulebook isn't much harder than a normal embrace, they just need to find those people who are "pure", which I guess mean the Purified, but it wasn't really explain.

    2 - That true for actual Jiang Shi, but the Jiang Shi vampire aren't actual Jiang Shi, it's a reimagining of the Jiang Shi myth, kind of like the other Supernatural Creature within Chronicle of Darkness. In the official text of Vampire the Requiem, it's said that they have spirit like attribute, and their Bane is even call Ephemeral Curse, all of which fuel by Essence.

    3 - Jiang Shi in official Vampire the Requiem core rulebook text, have Celerity, and their Clan description doesn't mention anything that would make them rigid. And again, it's a reimagining, not an accurate protray of Jiang Shi from culture.

    4 - True, but it's a reimagining, and Vampire can't really digest flesh.

    5 - I change that now.

    6 - If I tie it to the Discipline level, it would just create more problem that it solve, and it couldn't go beyond 5, which you can go beyond, if you tie it to Resolve. And I want to still make it need, to explain why they're so rare.

    7 - I change that too.

                                Another question: why does the base clan give them extra abilities to spend the said essence? if you're hellbent on keeping Essence in, then there's no need for them to have 2 abilities built into where they can spend essence. Really, I'd even say 1 is too much - if Essence is essential for them, then at the base clan, their Bane/drawback should make them require it (and spend as is), and then give them ability to gather it (drain it from humans).

                                In this case, move Numb and Addicted abilities into In-Clan merits, which characters can choose to have. Notice how other base clans have their basic themes expressed through the bane and disciplines and then In-Clan abilities are merits (Dream Visions, Kiss of Succcubus, etc etc). I think same should apply here. Base Clan should have the bare minimum number of abilities and things to make it work.

                                if for your clan it is Essence (which I think it sohuldn't be, but) - then that's all there should be. Actually all of it could be their Clan Bane: "Every night you don't sleep in your grave lose a point of Essence, or suffer 1 aggravated damage. When gaining Vitae, you can instead gain Essence and you can store maximum of Resolve of Essence.".

                                Then have a 1 dot merit, "Drinker of Essence" which allows you to gather it via breath and not bite. Then another merit which allows you to spend essence to inflict Numb or Addicted.

                                The Discipline should step away from essence and it's use (although you could, if you really wanted to, have the discipline say that "characters with available Essence can spend it instead of Vitae costs associated with the Discipline". Have the Discipline be in Vitae costs and focused on air powers (given that it's replacing celerity, I'd make it a Physical like ability).
                                This is also potentially important because other vampires might want to learn this Discipline and they should be able to power it with Vitae.

                                Other abilities in what you currently have could become Devotions (This Discipline + Animalism to control the weather, perhaps??)

                                This in total would clean up the design of the Clan to make it more in line with other Clans, while keeping it's themes and access to the list of abilities you wish to have.

                                                            Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                                                            I feel like I heard the explanation for Essence multiple time, and each one is different, so can anyone tell me what Essence really is?
                                                            Spiritual energy. Exactly what this means differs depending on the type of ephemeral entity we're talking about. For spirits (which I believe is the type relevant for this topic) Essence is the energy that's derived from material sources they happen to resonate with (hence why the associated Condition is called Resonant).
                                                            Someone being angry generates anger Essence for anger/rage spirits to consume. A lit fireplace generates fire Essence for fire spirits to consume. A stone generates stone Essence for stone spirits. (The intensity of the source does influence how much Essence is generated, but this isn't really modelled by the rules for how spirits gain Essence. Also, once Essence enters the spiritual ecology, it can be passed around in various ways.)
                                                            An important part of all of this is that Essence is typed, i.e. anger Essence is distinct from fire Essence which is distinct from stone Essence.
                                                            Another important part of all of this is that while everything generates Essence, nothing contains Essence. This is why some people have opposed the idea that your Jiang-Shi would be able to suck out Essence from humans. There's just no Essence to be had. This is also why people think Essence is a poor fit for qi. The concept of qi much more closely aligns with Vitae (especially the breath kind that Strix uses) since that's actually found within humans.
                                                            Also, Essence, while generated in our world, is highly tied to the spirit world, meaning it's really not grounding the Jiang-Shi in the world of the living.

                                                            Now there is a very prominent example of Essence being gained by directly eating a human: Werewolves. They can also do it on wolves. However, it should be noted that werewolves are partly spirits, and merely mix their spiritual nature (drawing Essence from what they resonate with) and their physical nature (physically consuming things for nourishment). This only works because humans and wolves are Resonant for werewolves.


                                                            I also want to add to the sentiment that adding Essence as a separate fuel stat to track is adding needless complexity just from a pure gameplay perspective. Such things are better of as Bloodline Gifts, where there's a conscious buy-in to the new mechanics.
                                                            Additionally, Clans have a few things that differentiate them from each other, from favoured Attributes to in-Clan Disciplines to Clan Banes. Of these, Clan Banes are the only ones that add unique rules to the individual Clans. Now, Clan Banes can be complex, but they are meant as a drawback. Adding extra rules separates them away from the other Clans, which is what their official write-up already does by introducing special rules for Embraces and their teleportation power.
                                                            Lastly, having a Discipline that requires access to Essence makes it impossible to use for other Clans. (I also think Celerity is a great fit for Jiang-Shi based on their folklore, so it's certainly not the one I'd replace anyway.)

                                                            Now, having extra rules and even a truly unique Discipline are not necessarily bad things. It really depends on what your goal with them is. But I do get the feeling that you're trying to make them more in-line with the regular Clans and present them as a player option, which these additions runs counter for the above mentioned reasons.
