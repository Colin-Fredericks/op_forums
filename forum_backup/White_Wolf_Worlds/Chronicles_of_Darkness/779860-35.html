                        I would like to announce that along with finished Trace Mechanics, I've also changed the Trace section of each of the Currents! So far, each of them get an automatic bonus on one of the pieces of the trace.

                        Buoyancy and Acheron: Time
                        Conflict and Phlegethon: Range
                        Harmony and Lethe: Audience
                        Healing and Styx: Verse
                        Wisdom and Cocytus: Clarity

                        Oceanus: Whatever Echidna dictates is important for the individual missions her Sirens have. One Siren may be able to get a bonus on Time while another requires Range and gets a bonus on Range to suit her. :3

                        I hope this makes an interesting dynamic. I've also added in five new merits! I hope you all enjoy them.

                        [1] Sea’s Temperament
                        Most Sirens living close to water, mainly near sea, tend to gain this merit. Your character’s mood rises and falls with the sea, affecting her emotions and her Maelstrom. At low tide, your character’s mood seems calmer and emotions more distant. Your character gains a +1 bonus on attempts to resist maelstrom.

                        Drawback: Your character’s mood also rises with High tide, making her more active and irritable. During high tide, your character takes a -1 penalty to resist Maelstrom.

                        [1] Weather Sense
                        Your character has a knack for reading upcoming weather. Perhaps she simply knows that old fables about telling the weather rings true for her. Maybe the Siren’s Trace subconsciously tells her of things to come. The Siren may gain an automatic Trace roll concerning upcoming weather within the next 24 hours. The roll is considered to be free, and with each success the ST must tell of the weather type, Environmental Level(s) and/or if it was caused by a Quagmire or not.

                        [3] Eye of the Storm
                        The Siren’s Maelstrom is more like a hurricane. The entry and exit are furious waves, but if the Siren tries to focus then she may find the calm medium to hide until the storm passes. Whenever the Siren faces Maelstrom and she fails, she has the option of spending a point of Willpower to force a re-roll. The second roll’s results, no matter the outcome, are kept.

                        [1-2] Enchanting Voice
                        There’s simply something about the Siren’s voice that others find divine and hard to ignore. Each song makes others swoon, and each insult cuts deep. Sirens without beauty, but bearing this merit show that beauty isn’t just in physical appearance. This merit grants a +1 to all Social rolls that involve her voice at one dot. At two dots, this merit grants a +2 to all Social rolls instead. This Merit can stack with Striking Looks and Drowned Beauty merits.

                        [2] Aura Eater
                        The act of feasting from Pneuma feeds from the soul of the victim itself. Many cultures claim the soul shows in the form of an aura, which can depict the health of one’s mental and emotional state. To an extent, the Siren has this quality. For each point of Pneuma she feeds from another, she gains acute awareness of one of their emotions.

                            Happy to see that I've been able to contribute a few ideas. There's one more complex idea that has been playing in my mind over the last several days. Whether you find it to your liking depends if you want another character category. Siren is pretty robust already with Celestial Currents, Abyssal Currents, Breathless, Castaways/Therons, Echidna & Vodinaoi, Quagmires, Furies and Flensers/antagonistic humans. I leave it your judgement if another classification would be too much.

                            Although I see it more as a 'minor ascension' to one of the previous character types.

                            I know Undine: the Emergence had no connection to Siren, the initial draft taking a different direction with the merfolk myths. (Sirens and Leviathans make up some of its rudimentary splats). Its theme of a mythical being from the aquatic biome being drawn to and wishing to fit within humanity reminds me much of Promethean. It being a 1E based draft that was never finished, we do not know the full outlook of the game beyond this first theme. Without it being a retread of Promethean trying to be the mermaid becoming human doesn't sound like it would make a full game.

                            Although, such an idea could play into Siren. Question: could there be 'Undines' that have their origins as avatars of nature that became Sirens, their humanity a secondary aspect?

                            Or in other words, a Fury becoming curious about facets of humanity neither it or its Quagmire expected. From its own curiosity or the encouragement of other Sirens (of any Current) it builds a connection to the Song. And at a point, its crafted Vessel becomes a true Diluvian Form and with it a human guise as well. The new being is still a Siren but one from nature rather than humanity, finding its own place along the strife of Civilization and Nature.

                            Of course, this is a rare occurrence that can't really be planned for, as deceptively complex like making an Angel fall or achieving the New Dawn. Given how a Quagmire and thus the Fury forms both are violence incarnate against the ravages of Civilization. A budding future!Siren Fury would likely need an event at the creation of or early in the Quagmire's history that would form a Relationship with a human. Said human would both have to emphasize with the Quagmire and make efforts to further its cause. A Curiosity mechanic could track the sway of the Fury towards becoming a Siren, likely needing to beat the Resentment or Spite of the Quagmire. Let alone that becoming more like a human would jeopardize its ability to enact the Quagmire's vengeance.

                            The end result, a new Siren, would have its Rank reduced to a lesser Akashic Record rating, which limits how many of previous Alterations it had that it could keep. Potentially a fragment of its former abilities could be kept, such as its Vessel's material benefits, although a new Siren version would have to be added.

                            Drawbacks of this process is that the 'Undine' Siren (or whatever classification for the new entity) would start at lower Lucidity and perhaps be more prone to Malestroms (having extra triggers associated from its Quagmire). Plus, when bottoming out of Lucidity instead of a Banshee it returns to an equally vindictive Fury, now unbound from the Quagmire it generated from, never to redeemed again.

                            If this addition is viable, there could even be Character Creation only merits to reflect a player creating an Siren from a former Fury. Of course this begs the clarification: is the Fury a separate entity created the same time as a Quagmire? Or the Quagmire's acting avatar?

                                                    Alrighties. I would like to apologize for the lack of consistency between my posting. Inspiration flickers on and off, but I promise I will stay on this project until I feel it is completely finished.

                                                    First off, how does this look for Refraction revision?
                                                    Refraction
                                                    The Song beats forever through the Siren's soul, empowering them through the Siren's Akashic Record and forming out the Verses they sing. Although the greater the Siren's Akashic Record grows, the more the Song wishes to fly out and recruit more people. To this end, Siren's speculate it's favored recruitment method is to show off the Diluvian Form of it's Sirens. Why it does this, no Siren knows. They only know to call this phenomenon as "Refraction". Mundanely, it simply means the change in direction of a wave due to a change in it's transmission medium. Supernaturally speaking, Sirens use this term because when they enact a Verse or wield an Alteration, they risk being changed completely into their Diluvian form by force.

                                                    Refraction requires two things in order to work. First, it requires a Siren in their Land Form with an Alteration active or using a Verse. Without a Siren, Refraction obviously cannot happen. Second, it requires a mortal viewer with a soul and that is sapient. Animals and plants cannot trigger Refraction, and strangely to Sirens nor do the other supernatural denizens of the World of Darkness trigger it. Some Sirens speculate that they lack ownership of a soul, while others suggest that due to their nature they cannot necessarily be "recruited" by the Song.

                                                    Once the top two requirements are met, the Siren is forced to roll Resolve + Composure with their Akashic Record as a Penalty to this roll.

                                                    Dramatic Failure: The Siren is forced to change into her Diluvian Form as a reflexive action. Furthermore, all viewers of the Siren must roll their own Resolve + Composure. Success only grants them no effects, while a failure forces them to suffer the Spooked Condition [GMC 183].
                                                    Failure: The Siren turns into her Diluvian form as an Instant action. Depending on how well most characters can take it, they may suffer a breaking point upon viewing her form.
