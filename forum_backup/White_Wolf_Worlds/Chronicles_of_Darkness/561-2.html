                You're not the only one thinking "only technically a Conspiracy". It's tricky to say how large something like, e.g., The Union is. I'm not a fan of the size estimates in the core book, and there's the whole question of whether there's one Union or if The Union is an archetype of many similar Compacts. But in terms of organisational structure the Union and Hakollel are near identical - independent cells that trade information (admittedly the religious loyalty to the Shemot wielders leading each cell is different). Hakollel just summons angelic postmen that cannot be intercepted by anything short of an Archmage instead of a web forum.

                So basically. In organisation Hakollel is nearly identical to The Union. In size, who can tell? In Endowment/occult lore they're up there with the best. In political influence the only reason they have any at all is because Knesset is ridiculously vulnerable to minority influence. Does that make them a Compact or a Conspiracy? I'm actually tempted to say Compact, I personally consider organisation to be the big one.

                I might steal that idea for the True Shemot - I think the Orgs need to have more proactive goals and that works great.

                                            My knowladge of Judaism isn't that great; I was raised high holy days Jewish and picked up a little. Hakollel was a mixture of google and a bit of help from the old forums; besides the Shemot themselves at 99% game mechanics and only 1% Judaism.

                                            Shemot were designed to be useful powers from a gameplay perspective. I started with three archetypes: The community rabbi who doesn't hunt monsters, the lone monster hunter who uses Shemot against his enemies, the hunter who leads a cell and blesses them with Shemot. Then I made sure each archetype had at least five useful powers. There is not a 1-1 relationship between the trees and the archetypes btw.

                                            Shemot were also optimised for Cells with only one Shemot user; in the fluff Hakollel cells are usually one Shemot wielder and their loyal followers, which is why El based Shemot can affect the entire cell at once.

                                            Greater Shemot were designed to be "too awesome to ever use". In the fluff Hakollel's mission is to safeguard Shemot; so I wanted players to have at least something that they actually would safeguard and which really could cause a huge disaster if it fell into the wrong hands.

                                            And that's the core design principles behind Shemot. With those you should have no trouble creating your own, heck quite a few of them were adaptations of other WoD powers to the Shemot paradigm - there's some Benedictions in there, Vanglory 3. You shouldn't have any trouble creating more the hardest part will probably be finding authentic names for the powers, and if I'm honest I don't think it's wrong create something with google translate and handwave that those names only exist in the WoD. I just get a bit obsessive about these things (the amount of time I spent googling mesopotaian deities to name the last two Strains in Leviathan...)
