    I think Lunacy in Werewolf 2e would be a better model for the Wake - rather than penalizing all breaking point rolls, make perceiving a Leviathan a breaking point that inflicts Beloved status on a dramatic failure. The system you're presenting implies that breaking points that have nothing to do with a Leviathan (or any other supernatural event) can convert one to Beloved, which doesn't make much sense. Why, for instance, would a man who kills someone in a bar fight that escalated promptly decide, on realizing what he's done, to become the loyal servant of a fish demon that's in the neighborhood, but that he may well never have met?

    I also think that the penalties from the Wake should depend not on Sheol, but on Depth. A Leviathan passing for human would inspire dread and obsession for no observable reason, but nothing beyond that; approaching apotheosis would destroy the sanity of all witnesses and swiftly create a cult.

            Let me turn that question around at you? Why would a theosophist colony who has never met Cthulhu decide to don white robes en mass and await some glorious fulfillment? That's from Call of Cthulhu. People didn't need to perceive Cthulhu for his Wake to affect them, and neither do Leviathans.


            Anyway, the answer to your question is because he's standing in a psychic field that's constantly hammering him with "WORSHIP GREG!".

            In the moment of vulnerability that is a failed Breaking Point, when there are cracks in his self identity, the Leviathan's psychic power seeps into those cracks and "worship Greg" moves from being an outside influence into being part of his core self identity.

            He wouldn't imediately know who Greg is and head over to the temple of Greg and enlist in the cult. Rather when trying to put himself back togeather after the Breaking Point he'd connect his actions to his feelings of dread, anticipation, and eager curiosity; and become obsessed with exploring those feelings. If he met actually Greg afterwords then he'd recognize Greg as the source of those feelings and transfer his obsession across. But if he doesn't - see the Wake write up's section on isolated Beloved.

                                                            Here's that spirit Channel I was talking about yesterday. It's a bit mechanic-light and is perhaps a bit weak.

                                                            Piercing the Veil of Shadows (Ancestral)
                                                            To the Progenitors, both spirit and flesh were merely two sides of the same coin and each utterly beneath their notice.
                                                            • Depth 0: Birthright of Awareness
                                                            • Depth 1: The leviathan gains the ability to roll (Wits + Composure +Sheol) to notice Twilight activity within his Wake. He knows the approximate location of the activity, to within about a meter.
                                                            • Depth 2: The leviathan gains the ability to visually perceive beings in Twilight. He cannot hear or touch them.
                                                            • Depth 3: The leviathan gains the ability to hear and speak to entities in a state of Twilight. He still cannot touch them.
                                                            • Depth 4: The leviathan gains the ability to touch and physically interact with beings in Twilight. This cuts both ways. A leviathan can harm the entity but it can do the same to him.
                                                            • Depth 5: The leviathan becomes able to see across the Gauntlet, as the WtF Gift 'Two World Eyes'.
                                                            • Depth 6: In addition to the Depth 5 effect, the leviathan also becomes able to hear and speak across the Gauntlet.
                                                            Adaptations:
                                                            • Walking Between the Worlds (OOO) : In Depth 3 and below the leviathan gains the ability to cross the Gauntlet using the medium of water. This should be handled as if the leviathan was entering the Rift, save that Gatewaters provide no benefit and that penalties appropriate to the environment should be applied to the crossing attempt.
                                                            • Show the Path (OO( requires Walking Between the Worlds and Birthright of Fecundity)): The leviathan can bring others into the Shadow with him, provided that they are touching him and he has used the Birthright of Fecundity upon them this scene. This can work on creatures without an Ichor pool, but it still grants no Ichor, costs the leviathan 1 (or more) and acts as a poison of Toxicity 2.
