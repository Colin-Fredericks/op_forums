    A Discussion of Supernatural Energy Resources

    11-18-2013, 10:39 PM
    I need help understanding the various supernatural energy resources in the World of Darkness.

    I think Essence is the natural energy of the world, and pervades just about everything. Ghosts use it, Spirits use it, God-Machine Angels use it, and it can be claimed by Werewolves from both a spirit locus and by the consumption of human flesh. We know of a direct link between Essence and another energy resource; Aether, the waste produced by the God-Machine's refining of Essence.

    A few other energy resources interest me - Vitae, Pyros, and Plasm - with how they are related to Essence, and I suppose this is where I need help.

    Essence is pervasive, but generally invisible to mortal humans. It is within them, however (see; Werewolves gain Essence by consuming human flesh) and is open to them once they die (and they become Ghosts). Vitae, then, is the Essence within a living human, perhaps refined by a Kindred's supernatural chemistry. Or maybe I am missing something?

    Plasm can be gained by a Sin-Eater either by lurking in a place tainted by death (we can assume a Haunt is similar to a locus, albeit with a tie to the Underworld rather than the Shadow) or by fooling about with ghosts in a number of ways. Plasm seems to be either tainted or refined Essence; I can't tell if the tie is to the Underworld, or if the Geist within a Sin-Eater's body changes Essence even as it is absorbed. Many questions,a nd i have no answers.

    Pyros is very strange, and seems to be wholly separate from Essence. It is described as rare, and seems to only exist within Prometheans and Qashmallim. Prometheans can convert mundane energies into Pyros, and are themselves powered by it, though in the refined form of Azoth. It is a dynamic power source, and can be tainted (Elpis or Flux). Where does it come from? It seems to be completely unnatural.

    My only hint of a theory towards Pyros ties into the Gnostic theories proposed towards the Principle and the God-Machine; that the Principle fits as the "true god" and has a unique power source, while the God-Machine is the false demiurge and uses the Essence that is common in the physical world.

    This has been a long and meandering post, but I wanted to put this out here and hear from others as well. Discussion of just what Gnosis, Glamour, and Sekhem are would also be welcome.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

                          I think the idea that Essence is the "core" energy is somewhat flawed. Granted, that's just my personal take and has more to do with how I feel about far broader concepts like religion, faith, science, and mysticism.

                          I feel like there's some kind of underlying, "core" energy that moves the World of Darkness. Call it the God Particle (but not around a physicist), Super Strings, The Force, The Schwartz, or the Flying Spaghetti Monster's Secret Sauce, but this is the fundamental mystical energy that makes the World of Darkness function. Everything we see in splats are 'fragments' or aspects of that energy, either altered by passing through different filters or simply called something else because it is being observed from a different perspective.

                          Essence is this energy as distilled through the Spirit world and animistic concept of the Shadow. Essence is what it is and does what it does because the Shadow is what it is, and uses the energy in the way it does. Think of the Shadow as a still, and the Energy is grain and water poured into the still. They come out as something very different then when they went in. The Energy the Shadow uses becomes Essence because the Shadow uses it.

                          Vitae is quite simply the life force of human beings, concentrated by the vampire's own undead body into a more potent form. Think of it like "life extract." A vampire doesn't need the red corpuscles and platelets and plasma. They just need the force of life. The mystical energy that gives Vitae (and human blood, for that matter) its unique characteristics is the same energy that becomes Essence when it fuels the Shadow, but in this case is filtered through the biological processes of a living organism and that strange magic of "being alive," creating this mystical, distilled extract of life. When vampires consume blood and turn it into Vitae, they filter and modify it yet again, creating a new energetic substance.

                          Mana is the Energy that runs the Supernal World, and like the Shadow, the Supernal alters that energy because of how it makes use of it. The Mana that exists in the Fallen World is somewhat tainted by its passage through the Abyss (if I recall my Mage correctly), making it something yet again different from Supernal Mana.

                          Pyros is tricky. Since Pyros is both the Divine Fire itself, as well as the energy it gives off. Azoth and Flux are the opposing forces of the Divine Fire, creation and destruction. Pyros itself could be likened to the "heat" the Divine Fire gives off, harnessed to generate power. Pyros is the flame generated by the Divine Fire burning the Energy to sustain itself.

                          Glamour is again a byproduct of that magical process of "being alive." The Energy that gives all living things that spark of magic is again filtered through their brains and minds, and releases the intangible light of Glamour in bursts of emotion.

                          Plasm is interesting, in that it is partially a substance, ectoplasm, that which gives ghosts a Corpus. But Plasm as Bound use it is something else yet again. As Blood and Vitae are two different "phases" of the same "energy state," so too are ectoplasm and Plasm. The Energy that runs the Underworld becomes this substance, and like the dead themselves, and leak into our world, where it can be harvested by the Bound.

                          Anyway, call that my two cents.
