                        Having struggled with this game, which I absolutely ADORE, I see the following as hacks that I've wanted to implement if possible (and note, some have been covered here already):

                        Disquiet and Wasteland as scalable effects would make a nice compromise between the factions saying 'they're too harsh' and 'they're not harsh enough.' By this I mean that any troupe ought to have options for how these effects work in the game, and possibly ideas for how these effects can wax and wane through a chronicle rather than just a one-size-fits-all approach from the core book.

                        Decouple Humour from Lineage challenges some of the base assumptions of the game, but I feel that it could work to allow for greater variety. This hack – the one I've put the most work into, but still not a lot – keeps each Lineage having a 'preferred' Humour, but it also becomes possible to play a Melancholic Muse or a Sanguine Frankenstein. Mind you, this is likely a moot point with seven Lineages in Promethean 2d edition, but it plays into my third idea...

                        Let Humuor affect Refinements in a way that gives Humour much more vitality AND forces a character to really think about the Refinements she pursues. I see Humour as an unchanging Trait, but unlike Lineage, it demands some choice – on the part of the character's creator rather than the character herself. Anyway, I believe that a Sanguine character would approach each Refinement differently than an Ectoplasmic one might. Again, this could be a moot point in light of the ten Refinements we'll see.

                        ...As stated by others, I'm so very much looking forward to whatever comes out in this book. Unlike the 2d editions of other games, I do believe that Promethean deserves a serious overhaul, brilliant as it already is!

                                                    Mmm, no; not really. The Humours are based off of real-world (outdated) theories about how humours influence one’s temperament — even today, such things as “ENTJ/ISFP/etc.” personality sorters address the same sorts of observed “personality types” that the theory of humours was concocted to explain, albeit with different explanations.

                                                    Granted, the aforementioned theory had four Humours rather than seven; still, four of the original five Lineages were clearly designed with the four Temperaments in mind: compare the Frankensteins, Galeteids, Osirans, and Tammuz to the Choleric, Sanguine, Phlegmatic, and Melancholic Temperaments on the linked page, and you’ll notice really strong parallels. The fifth of the original Lineages was based on an explicitly inhuman Humour (Ectoplasm); and the Zeka Humour was defined less in terms of the humour itself and more by the fact that it had been contaminated by radiation.

                                                    These Temperaments are, per the theory, inborn inclinations of one’s personality: one doesn’t choose to be Choleric or Melancholic; one is born with those inclinations, and then either embraces them or fights them. Refinements, OTOH, are fields of study; they represent what you think you need to learn about in order to become more human — which is why I think that Athanors should be Refinement-based rather than Lineage- or Humour-based, as an Athanor represents what you want to get out of the New Dawn. Lineages or Humours represent where you started; Athanors represent your intended destination; and Refinements represent the route you’re taking to get there. So yes, Humour influences how you express your Refinement; but it does so in the same way that, say, a vampire’s Clan influences how he approaches his Covenant.

You also have the issue of whether or not the ST can and should accommodate Spiritus purchases for supernaturals of the sort she would not otherwise include, or give away plot points to forestall a useless power being purchased. If we were just talking ghosts and spirits, there already exist rules for dealing with them without resort to specialized Transmutations. If we were just looking at crossovers, the primary difficulty is Disquiet, which could be addressed holistically with a revamp to those rules. I'm not against Argentum existing, or even Spiritus, but I see the introduction of the various and sundry mysteries that even a single other-splat adds as muddying the waters. Great for a chronicle, but misguided for character mechanics.
