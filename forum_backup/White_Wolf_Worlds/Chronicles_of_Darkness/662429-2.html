                                So trying to break through the writers block .. been wacking away at the Cemetery... i have some prelim stuff i did.. two Mage legacies and an Arisen cult and they are very rough .. any thoughts?

                                Mages – The Awakened
                                Awakened have often remarked that the quiet nighttime of the Graveyard is perhaps one of the nicer things of the entire place. With connections to the Underworld to be had, and wealth of knowledge to be found in the tombs, the Awakened tend to see the Cemetery as one giant archaeological dig. The Awakened soul is immune to the effects of the Cemetery.
                                Those of the Moros path have an affinity to this place and are more likely to be found here exploring than any other of the paths. A few Apostates have learned that the Cemetery is a good hiding spot, using a local graveyard’s Death Gate as a way to hide from both Pentacles and Seers. Members of the Stone Scribes legacy have been spotted in the Cemetery from time to time as well, digging through the ruins, tombs and mausoleums, searching for artifacts to study. Two legacies have also been found to have had their origins here, the Death’s Eyes and the Stygian Knives, a left handed legacy of soul stealers.

                                Death’s Eyes

                                The dead leave many stories untold and so we search for them, to record them, pass them on, learn from them and grow from their knowledge and teachings.

                                This legacy is based out in the Cemetery, seeing it as the perfect place to learn the state of death. Many of the legacy are looking into the secrets that were left by the Reapers and the Inquisitors, hunting for answers about what happened to the Outer Realm and border march that connects it to the Fallen World. They hunt for their answers with the dead interred within the border march and the spirits that still exist here.

                                Parent Path: Moros
                                Nickname: Dead Heads
                                Orders: The Mysterium
                                Appearance: Those of this legacy tend to be rather pale, having spent a lot of time in the Outer Realm of Stygia. Most members are obsessively clean, though they tend to have a slight aroma of fresh earth about them.
                                Background: For those Moros mages who find the lure of death overwhelming, some decide that to best experience death, the outer realm of Stygia is perhaps a good place to study. This realm is in what many of the Moros path described as permanent state of death, stuck in that moment before going onwards to whatever exists beyond death. For those that decided to push their understanding, the Death’s Eyes legacy is a home for them to learn in.
                                Organization: Moros of this legacy tend to working solitary environments, tucked away in their studies in small havens either deep within the outer realm of Stygia or in small easily missed homes in the Fallen World. The typical Stygian haven is a usually a Bolthole that has been secured, warded and properly shored up with defenses. Most of these locations are full of stacks of paper and artifacts gleaned and excavated from the ruins around them, as well as possibly small rooms dedicated to dissection and proper examination of the flora and fauna of the outer realm. Members of this Legacy are not unsocial, and many of them maintain personal contact with other members of the legacy as well as other Awakened and sleepers on a regular basis. Those rare Death’s Eyes that loose themselves to the outer realm stop being members of this legacy and become something different, something of the left-handed legacy known as the Stygian Knives (see later).
                                Suggested Oblations: Thanking the spirits of the dead, leaving tokens of respect on graves.
                                Concepts: Spiritualist, mortician, seeker of truth
                                History
                                This legacy and its dark twin brother have their origins deep in the past and indeed, many are not quite sure when they first were created. Some obscure texts have references to the legacy during the time of the old kingdom in Egypt, while others are noted from even earlier in the history of man, with death cults among the Awakened who had travelled to the outer realm of Stygia.
                                Over the centuries the legacy took form as a way for Awakened to study death, but it wasn’t till the destruction of the border march and vanishing of the servants of the enigmatic Reapers that the legacy started to actually find itself. The strange death like state of the outer realm let the legacy push itself, creating its final attainments, but sadly it also saw the birth of its left-handed twin, the Stygian Knives.
                                In the modern nights those of this legacy are often sought out for their knowledge of death in all its forms, seen as keepers of esoteric knowledge.
                                Society and Culture: For the Awakened of this legacy death is nothing new, indeed it is something to be studied, respected, embraced and researched. Many of the members of the legacy are physicians of some type, using their medical knowledge as a way to enhance their skills and talents. Death Eye’s keep in contact in the Fallen World though all modern means from emails and chat groups to cell phones. Groups meet with other members of the legacy as well as other Moros path Awakened whenever they feel the need. The legacy is not seen as harmful and while albeit sometime they are viewed as bit weird, most Awakened have no issues with those of this legacy.
                                Induction: Joining into this legacy is actually rather an easy task for any of the Moros path. The simple desire to learn more about death will often lead the perspective student to seek out a member of the legacy who is usually fairly happy to teach others about their way.

                                Magics
                                Tomb Gate (Space 3)
                                Practice: Weaving
                                Action: Extended (target number = current gauntlet rating)
                                Duration: Transitory (1 turn)
                                Aspect: Vulgar
                                Cost: 1 Mana
                                Mysterium Rote: Dimension Door
                                Dice Pool:
                                The mage learns how to create a portal from the Fallen World into one of the outer realm borderlands, in this case the Cemetery. Variations of this magic can create quick short lived portals into any of the borderlands, though for the Moros who walk the path of the Death’s Eyes, it is Tomb Gate they learn so that they can enter the Cemetery. Of note is the variations of this spell the allow Awakened access to the other borderlands. This spell only accesses the Cemetery.

                                1st: Walking the Paths
                                Prerequisites: Death 1
                                In order to find out information, secrets, stories, indeed anything, the mage must know where to look and where to dig in the vast border land that is the Cemetery. This attainment links the mage with the realm allowing him to slowly gain insight into where to go and look.
                                This attainment allows for the mage to focus and meditate on what he is searching for. As he sits, letting his mind commune with the border march, he slowly gains an understanding as to where to look for this information. Initially it is merely a glimmering of the location, something that allows them to make the journey there and know when they are within the area. As they progress with their attainments this location information becomes more accurate.
                                Optional Arcanum: Fate 1
                                Those mages with Fate can use it help pinpoint the exact spot the information is contained at once they have gotten the general location from the initial mediation. This cuts the time to locate the item at its physical location in half.

                                2nd: Hearing the Whispers
                                Prerequisites: Death 1
                                The dead are not truly dead, they say, merely in a transitional state, readying themselves before the spirit moves onwards to the next incarnation or to heaven or hell, or whatever maybe. The dead talk. They whisper endlessly to themselves and the other dead. They argue, talk, sing, scream, cry and even create, depending on their state of mind, so to speak. The mage at this point in his attainments is able to tune into the cacophony of whispers, hearing them everywhere around him. By concentrating he can try to localize down to a single whisper. Once done, the mage listens for stories, secrets, information and other tidbits. With practice the mage is able to trace the whispers from conversation to conversation, listening to entire debates that happen among the dead of the Cemetery and Stygia.

                                3rd: Dead Speak
                                Prerequisites: Death 5
                                This attainment actually allows the mage to bring forth the dead, enjoying into conversations with them in order to ask questions directly. The mage must have information on the dead person he is trying to contact, including the location of their grave or tomb in the Cemetery or Stygia. Each dead person is different and they range from the calm and rational to the psychotically insane.
                                It should be noted that the dead of the Cemetery and Stygia can be somewhat difficult to deal with, and have been known to rise from their graves to deal with upstart living things that disturb their sleep.
                                Optional Arcanum: Spirit 5
                                Those with both Arcana are actually able to bring the dead of the Cemetery to life to aid them in a pinch. With a roll of Presence + Persuasion + Spirit against the local dead’s Resistance, the dead will rise and aid the mage as needed for a scene.

                                The Stygian Knives (Left Handed Legacy)

                                We alone know who has been given the divine gift. We alone know who has to be brought home. We alone here the call and we alone will answer it with the souls of the gifted.

                                Among the Legacy known as the Death’s Eyes are a few who answer a different call, becoming Stygian Knives. These strange awakened believe that they are serving a divine power, killing those that need to be killed and bringing their souls back to the Cemetery so they can be released into the border march to be sent to Stygia.
                                The Stygian Knives operate quietly, inducting members from only the Moros path who answer the call the Death’s Eyes legacy, which makes them doubly rare. The Knives use ritual blades forged of a material known as soulsteel (said to be made from their own souls), to kill their targets and trap their souls to be release later. With each soul successfully brought to Stygia the Knives add a ritual scar to their body marking their path on their so call divine way.

                                Parent Path: Moros
                                Nickname: Soul Stealers
                                Orders: The Mysterium and the Guardians of the Veil
                                Appearance: Like the legacy that they spawned from, the Stygian Knives are generally pale skinned from the time they spend in Stygia. Most member have ritual scars across their arms, chest, back and legs as well.
                                Background: For every ten members of the Death Eye’s legacy, one might turn to the left-handed legacy known as The Stygian Knives. These confused awakened see that it’s there duty to bring death to those that deserve it and need it, then bring their souls to Stygia where they belong.
                                Organization: The Stygian Knives operate as a loose cult, with members freely joining with other member when needed for missions and information sharing, as well as for rituals such as the induction of new members and the release of those souls brought to Stygia.
                                Suggested Oblations: Returning souls to Stygia, marking new scars
                                Concepts: Spiritualist, ritual murderer, cultist
                                History
                                This left-handed legacy came into being shortly after the destruction of the Cemetery and the vanishing of the Reaper’s servants. For some reason some of the Death’s Eye’s newer members wandered off, vanishing into the Cemetery. No one is quite sure what it is that causes this to happen, though a couple of the Stygian Knives have been noted as saying they were answering the call.
                                In the modern nights the Stygian Knives are a rare left-handed legacy, but are considered extremely dangerous.
                                Society and Culture: The Knives have a cult like society, following strange rituals for finding their targets as well as when a target’s soul has been released. Ritual scarring is a common method for this legacy to show progress in their learning of attainments, given by other members of their legacy in small, almost casual meetings where they gather and tell stories.
                                A first scar is given when the Knife is inducted, the second when he forges his blade, after that scars are given when souls are released, or great acts are done, and of course when they attend the death of a fellow Knife. The scars are made by other Knives as stories are told and are intricate and details, no two being the same. It is said that if one could possible decipher the scars it would tell the story of the Knife’s life and death.
                                Knives spend their time between hunts, operating very much like those of the Death’s Eyes legacy, researching the dead, sifting through the ruins for artifacts and knowledge and other such tasks. Of course much of this is for them to find the name of their next target. These targets could be anyone, from sleepers to other Awakened to any number of other supernaturals, as long as there is a “soul” to be taken.
                                Once a target is found, the Knives hunt their target down and the chosen Knife kills the target with his blade, trapping the soul in it. There could be one target, or many, and usually it is not uncommon to have a number of Knives equal to the target plus two on a hunt, though solitary hunts are done. The Knives return to the Cemetery and travel to the edge of the border march and Stygia. Here the gathered Knives place the blades which hold souls in to a small fire then recount the deed by which they took the souls. As each does this they will the blade to release the soul, which slowly rises then moves off towards Stygia. No one is sure what happens to the souls when they reach Stygia.
                                Induction: No one is sure what calls one to the Stygian Knives. Those rare members who have been captured speak of a call, not really a voice or song, just a call that they follow till they meet more Stygian Knives, usually at a small gathering. Here the prospective Knife is inducted into the legacy and given his first ritual scar, after which he will either witness a soul being sent onwards, the crafting of a soulsteel knife or the choosing of a target. It is just as likely he will merely sit around a fire and trade stories of life and death with those around him. Regardless, after the meeting the new Knife goes off with one of the older Knives to learn his new craft.
                                Two weeks after the first meeting the new Knife will go through the process of having his own soulsteel knife forged. This rite, while simply done, is quite distinctive and dramatic, especially to the Knife upon which it is done. All Knives go through this process. Over the course of a night of storytelling of the kills made by the other Knives the gather Awakened slowly draw forth a piece of the soul of the initiate Knife. This piece of their soul is melded into a piece of ore dug from the land in the Cemetery, then forged into a blade through the use of magicks by the initiate and the gathered Knives. The Knife will then carry this blade with them till their death.

                                Magics
                                Tomb Gate (Space 3)
                                Practice: Weaving
                                Action: Extended (target number = current gauntlet rating)
                                Duration: Transitory (1 turn)
                                Aspect: Vulgar
                                Cost: 1 Mana
                                Mysterium Rote: Dimension Door
                                Dice Pool:
                                The mage learns how to create a portal from the Fallen World into one of the outer realm borderlands, in this case the Cemetery. Variations of this magic can create quick short lived portals into any of the borderlands, though for the Moros who walk the path of the Death’s Eyes, it is Tomb Gate they learn so that they can enter the Cemetery. Of note is the variations of this spell the allow Awakened access to the other borderlands. This spell only accesses the Cemetery.

                                1st: Freeing the Soul
                                Prerequisites: Gnosis 3, Death 3
                                This attainment allows the Knife to trap the departing soul of the target they have killed in their soulsteel blade. This is similar to the Sever the Sleeping Soul spell, but where that one takes the soul from a living target, this one is only usable on the dying, and only on the chosen target. No mana is spent and a Presence + Intimidation + Death roll is required versus the dying person’s Resolve. There is no benefit from the trapped soul, it is merely trapped in the blade till released. Mortal souls are easy to take, but supernatural souls are more difficult and generally most will not be able to be trapped unless the wielder has reached the third attainment.

                                2nd: Bonds of the Legacy
                                Prerequisites: Spirit 4
                                The mage is able to harness the energy of those fellow knives around him, gaining strength and power. Each Knife who is present in the hunt can add a bonus to all their combat and perception rolls, as well as casting rolls equal to the number of hunting party divided by 2, rounded down, to a max of 5. This bonus will also apply to Paradox rolls.
                                Optional Arcanum: Death 4
                                If the mage is also has Death 4 he can drain the essence and power of his fellow hunt members to gain even more power, using their life to fuel himself in his effort to kill the target and save the soul. This act is usually an agreed on thing, as the hunt members freely give their lives to the primary to help him. For each hunt member that links themselves to the primary like this, the primary ignores wounds and wound penalties, as well as tilts and conditions, as they are instead transferred to the others of the hunt group.

                                3rd: A Soul Reborn
                                Prerequisites: Death 5
                                There are two benefits to this attainment. The first is that the Knife can now trap the souls of those supernaturals that still have one. Vampires don’t have one to take. Werewolves and other shapechangers do have a soul to be trapped, though the primal nature of these souls make it difficult for them to be trapped, adding a +1 to the difficulty. Werewolves will hunt those that steal their souls to the ends of the earth and beyond to rescue the soul. Mages have a soul that can be trapped. Changelings can be trapped. The living flame that serves as a Promethean’s soul is said to be trappable, but to date no Knife has ever had one of these supernaturals as a choosen. Hunters are human and as such their souls can be trapped. Geists are strange beings, their souls linked to a second soul, so to speak. This dual soul nature makes it impossible for the Knifes to take their souls, as the blade can only hold one soul, not two. Mummies souls are untouchable, and any Knife that tries will themselves blasted by paradox. Demons don’t have souls. Beasts are a strange thing, their souls hollowed out and replaced with the soul of the beast. It is possible to try to trap a Beast’s soul, but don’t expect it to remain trapped. Stories are told of a Knife who took a Beast’s soul only to find themselves drawn into the Lair where they had to face the soul itself. Luckily the number of times a Beast has been the target is countable on one hand.
                                The second benefit is the ability of the Knife to use the soul to help heal themselves when it is released to Stygia. When the release is done, the Knife takes a part of the soul and uses it to energize themselves, healing wounds and fatigue. This is similar to the Death 3 spell Devouring the Slain, but also heals wounds as well as willpower and mana.

                                The Stygian Blade (Imbued Item 4) - This “soulsteel” blade is the weapon the Knife uses when they make their kills. The blade has the ability to “steal the soul” of the victim, holding it in its lattice till the ritual is done to release it. A blade can only hold one soul at a time in this manner and cannot be used to make another kill till the soul is released. The Blades are an Imbued Item with the Soul Jar (Death 2) persistent enchanted within it.


                                Mummies – The Arisen
                                To the Arisen the Cemetery is a loose reminder of that strange dead land in which they went then they died and were reborn as an Arisen. Most look at the Cemetery with a measure of respect, knowing full well its dangers. Some whisper that some of the Deceived hide in the depths of the Cemetery, feeding on the alien necrotic energies, tended to by families of Ghuls and cults of slowly devolving mortals, plotting their strategies.

                                Arisen Cult – the Claws of the Shadowed Hawk
                                The Claws of the Shadowed Hawk have existed for close to three centuries, based around four Deceived who, after a disastrous series of plans led to them losing all that they had done over the centuries before. Initially the four managed with what little they had, their remaining cultists helping move their bodies towards a new location but their enemies were several steps ahead of them and they lost dozens of their remaining cultists and a large portion of their remaining resources. Now it should be noted that the Arisen are aware of the border land now known as the Cemetery as well as the outer realm of Stygia. The four Deceived managed to locate a Shadowland and, locating the Shadow Gate within, moved everything they had through the portal and into the border march. They traveled towards Stygia and found an old set of tombs that they decided was the best place to set up shop.
                                Initially they spent their time clearing the tombs, gathering the bodies to fuel fires and rituals, collecting the books, artifacts and other items and creating the tombs for the Arisen, the altar and ceremony room, as well as the living areas for the cultists. As time passed the mortal cultists started to devolve. The Arisen, aware of this possibility had their more learned members create talismans that would hold the effect at bay. They then used these talismans as gifts, given to those that performed well within the cult. The started recruiting members again from the Fallen World, using access points across the border march to setup contacts and associations. Needing fresh bodies, as well as supplies, arms and protection, the cult delved into various underworld activities slowly rebuilding themselves.
                                Initially they supported local pirates and freebooters, as well as local bandits and other ne’er-do-wells. Mortal dabblers in “dark magics” were also brought in to help bolster the growing priesthood of the cult. Those that were deemed unusable were left to devolve into ghuls. As the years passed by the Fallen World side shifted to trafficking slaves for cash, with some of the slaves being sent to the redoubt to become ghuls to help them grow their army.
                                As the Fallen World continued to advance the cult kept its numbers at a constant state, recruiting new members when needed to keep their mortal followers going, and letting those they did not need devolve into feral ghuls that lived in the depths of the tomb as well as wandering the outskirts of it as guards. Over the passing years the Deceived slowly reworked their cult, forging them into the Claws of the Shadowed Hawk who worshipped the Deceived as four gods of ancient evil.
                                In the modern nights the cult is hard at work conscripting criminals and other underworld and street people as its agents. Many of the people who are part of it are largely unseen by the general public, ignored and forgotten, which allows them to move about the city largely unchallenged. The Deceived use a combination of money, drugs, promises of power and actual low end magic being taught to keep their cultists in line and it works, most of the time. The four are now looking at finally expanding their operations with the upcoming Sothic Cycle and perhaps create a more powerful presence in the Fallen World, to which they have started to buy into a local corporation in order to create a secondary corporate styled cult over top of their more tribal one, (think the chrysaliss corporation from Cthulhutech).

                                The tomb temple in the Cemetery sits close to the point where one has to shift into the outer realm of Stygia itself, where the older, more massive and alien tombs exist. The tomb itself is a vast thing, almost city like in its design with a central building and surrounding temples and store houses. The small buildings have become rectories, training areas, sleeping quarters and other such things for the cult, with the four Deceived having their own rooms deep within the massive central building. Patrolling the ruins around the tomb are various packs of ghuls, as well as feral hounds and other scavengers. Of note is the lack of Flayed in the area, though the cultists are ok with this.
                                Fallen World locales tend to small shabby houses or apartments, though a couple of the gangs the cult controls have created temples to the four in underground locales and one old warehouse. The corporation the cult has slowly been infiltrating into is a tech company that back industrial robotics, and ships products worldwide, one of the primary reasons they were chosen. At this point numerous dock and factory workers unknowingly support the cult and a number of middle management are worshippers. The cult leaders are confident that they will have people in upper management within a couple years and are primed to put someone onto the board of directors through stock purchases by the end of the year.

                                The Claws of the Shadowed Hawk (Foundation – Tribal: This cult is organized around the four Decieved who act as “ancient gods of evil” whom the cult members have come to learn from in order to further their own greed for power. The members are inducted in grand ceremonies and group activities are frequent to keep them meshed in the cult. Those who defy the cult are punished, suffering from simple beatings to being fed to the devolved ghuls. Rewards come in the form of money, positions, servants, and arcane powers taught to the more enlightened.
