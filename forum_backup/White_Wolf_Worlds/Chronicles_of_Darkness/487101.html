                              Home smithing kit: ~65 dollars
                              3 ounces of silver: ~80 dollars
                              9mm mold: ~40 dollars
                              Butane torch: ~50 dollars
                              Loading press: ~150 dollars

                              A clip of werewolf killers? Priceless.

                              Naturally you need the base supplies too, powder, casings, etc, and a few hours of personal time but that's really it. In fact, most gun enthusiasts would already have all the basic materials requires, minus the silver itself.

                              So if you know somebody who has the above supplies you could get silver bullets for Resources 1 or 2.

                              To answer the question giving 30 guys a single clip of silver depends on what kind of guns they use. 30 guys with 9mm handguns would cost $3000 in raw silver, before any labor or supply costs. Automatic rifles would cost about the same due to less metal per bullet. These are purely based on weight, mind you.

                                          I find it hard to imagine someone actually using silver bullets against werewolves enough that they'd be able to go repair the damage it does. A human with silver bullets is going to be a seriously fragile glass cannon. Werewolves have tremendous speed and deadly weapons, my own character has a dicepool of 3 for brawl attacks in human form and can still kill an average human in one shot with one of the more combat-worthy forms. And disarming is just a matter of Strength and Brawl contested by the opponent's Strength and Athletics. Considering how many of the forms add to Strength you might have a huge advantage there.

                                                          I think the point is that Resources alone may be insufficient to obtain silver ammunition, or that additional merits or skills may be required, such as a few dots in Crafts to make the ammunition on your own (and maintain the firearms ruined by the silver), or Contacts, Retainer or Allies (or Fixer, Esoteric Armory, etc.) for them to make or procure it for you. The cost and availability of silver ammunition exceeds the cost of just the silver due to its scarcity in your nWOD. Silver purchases and sales, capable gunsmiths, etc. may also be watched carefully by werewolves and their spirit and mundane spies and allies. After you procure the silver ammunition, it's entirely possible that characters may also promptly get a visit from local law enforcement, Homeland Security, mental health professionals, Task Force: Valkyrie, etc.

                                                          Outfitting a platoon or other large group with regular sources of esoteric, no less relatively expensive, ammunition such as silver, may be the province of governments and large corporations (and Mages with Matter), and outside the purview of PC's without a very good explanation.

                                                          Dots in Resources alone should not be able to solve all your more unusual procurement needs. It is not always a stand-in for other Merits.
