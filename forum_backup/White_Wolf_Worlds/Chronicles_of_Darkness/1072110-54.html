    Here is my ongoing doc.

    Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post

    The Crown Jewels / Free Company

    The issue I have with the Crown Jewels is that they go against a core principal of Hunter: the Vigil: Hunters defeat supernatural opponents by using numbers, tactics, and exploiting their weaknesses. As written the Crown Jewels are the exact opposite. By trying to manipulate and manage Princesses, and compete for the loyalty of sworn they're going against Princesses on their strengths, not their weaknesses.

    The closest analogue would be the Bear Lodge who compete with Werewolves against their strength of hunting; but note that the Bear Lodge do exploit Werewolves' weaknesses with silver weapons, the Crown Jewels don't have an equivalent, and the Bear Lodge canonically have taken major losses. As a decentralised Compact they can afford those losses, but Princesses are much better at sharing information than Werewolves. If the Crown Jewels had a similar attrition rate to the Bear Lodge their secret would be exposed to the Noble community and it's game over.

    The Free Company is an attempt to take the core concept of a hunter group of Sworn who work with and support Princesses, but avoid the above flaws. For example, rather than competing with Princesses for the Sworn's loyalty; I made it a win-win situation if a Princess recruits a member to be her retainer.

    My vote is to use the Free Company.
    Well, I'd argue that the Crown Jewels target isn't the Princesses, but the Darkness. Given the mercenary spin, they wouldn't try to manipulate Princesses, just get Bequests from them. As for loyalty, they'd be more to pick up the Princess's Sworn once the Noble ends up dead (via other means/foes, of course).

    Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
    The Cold Guard (Vent0's version)

    It's still early days so it's hard to say too much. I like the Endowment. I like the philosophical opposition to nobles based on overcoming your sins / managing your sins and drawing strength from them; and I'd like to see their methods of fighting the Darkness thematically embody that philosophy. Bleak Oubliette is a good start (I did say it was my favourite ).

    I'm less keen on the bit about opposing nobles because nobles want to make people dependent upon them. It's not exactly wrong, but I think it's less interesting than “ overcoming your sins / drawing strength from them”.

    My vote: It's really too early to say. But it shows promise.
    Thanks. I'll try to work on the fluff elements this weekend.

    Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
    The Church of the Valley

    (The new name for the Sanitation Workers Collective. Link is here)

    Thus far I think they're the only group we have who fit the morally white/grey Conspiracy niche. The People's Guard are grey/grey at best because of their fanaticism, and Vent0's Cold Guard are powered by the physical embodiment of evil and can be corrupted by it so grey/grey at best; their philosophy of accepting and drawing strength from their own sins also strikes me as anti-heroic (I recommend embracing that, who doesn't like a good anti-hero?).

    And I think this group has a lot going for it. They have a philosophical objection to Princesses and a modus operandi that can result in the two groups working together or working against each other depending on the situation. There's a nice “protect the world that looks down on you vibe” to it. They explore parts of Hunter that rarely get explored – which is to say they put a lot of focus on actually acting like a conspiracy, infiltrating the corridors of power and manipulating them. But at the same time you can still run them in the classic hunter style of finding monsters and fighting them.

    Myself and MP also addressed the previous issues, giving them a religious angle to make them more distinct from the people's guard and a more defined origin for their Endowment.

    My vote: Include them.
    They still seem very similar. Their Endowment looks unchanged, last I saw. Where are they getting their powers from, again?

    Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
    I'd have a second, longer, interval for exceptional successes. Once every three months maybe.

    True. But I still favour soft limits over hard. Maybe tie it into Breaking Points. If you're over the safe limit you get a different, and nastier set of Conditions, from a Breaking Point roll; even a success is bad news.

    If you're over Safe Limit +5 a Success on a breaking point roll could trigger a mechanic like Werewolf's Death Rage, locking you out from making full use of your power surge. While a failure could lead to your cell outright being forced to put you down, if they can't afford to take the risk of locking you up until the tempoary shadows fade.

    You could also use Shadows / 2 in some cases, especially for direct damage effects. Since the safe limit can go up to 10 and I don't think Endowments will be pushing damage above five in most cases.

    Sounds good.
    Incorporated some of the suggestions.

Add in the idea that the Guard secretly cleans up the corruption that the powerful and careless leave behind them (the virtuous side of the Church of the Valley) and you have a solid concept: hunters who try to do good by stealth.
