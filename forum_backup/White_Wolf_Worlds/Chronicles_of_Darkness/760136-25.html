                                            For organising resources in Provinces, I'd take a leaf out of the book of RTS video games and give each Province a rating in different, fairly broad, categories. For example:

                                                        Except I went through the effort of removing specific resources... While different types of resources can work for a computer game, they don't feel particularly impressive in a Tabletop one without increasing complexity by alot.

                                                        .... Instead, what I thought up was that every Province would do 2 things:
                                                        The first is provide Wealth, representing resources of all sorts, biomass, minerals, Void materials, ect.

                                                        The alternative to using a Province for resources though... is using them for their features. Think of them not just as Loci, but also as magic items, giving benefits based on their "level" or how they're spent and this is often related to the type of territory it is, what's in it.


                                                        For instance.

                                                        We have this province that's classified as an

                                                        "Edenic Zone". This means it's got plenty of biodiversity relative to many other places. This can mean anything from a wildlife preserve, a zoo... or a farmland ect.
                                                        At "dots 3" it can be used to Generate 3 wealth. But it also has an effect tied to its genestocks like say....
                                                        "Spend Wealth from this Territory on Conscription to gain an extra Free Aspect instead of the usual benefits"

                                                        Or we can have....

                                                        "Mineral Field" representing places to get high concentrations of metal quite cheaply. This can be of course a mine, a crystal growth... or a scrap yard.
                                                        And it might have the option to say.
                                                        "Access to Juggernaught template."

                                                        While I originally thought of Principalities unlocking Servants, a part of me wonders about tying Servant unlocking with Provinces with Principalities being the system used to improve Servants.
