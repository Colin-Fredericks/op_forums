        Here's a thought (from someone who really hasn't been following this project or even read Genius in the past few years), Geniuses are about making things, right? What if they can overstep the bounds of their Axiom by making, say, 2 3rd level things that make it possible for another 3rd level thing to do something that should normally only work at the 4th level? OR what if they can go use mundane science to supplement their mad science by building more normal stuff into their design? That's how I would try to get around the limitations of my level if I were playing a Genius.

        I'll note that when I play stuff like Genius, Mage, and similar things in other games, and my characters build things, I try and get the details in to make them as realistic as possible before resorting to it just working because "Advanced Technology" / "Magic", so any design I'd make would probably do something like that. You guys might want to make sure your rules are taking that into consideration while you're thinking about that stuff...

                I think that's something that can be easily abstracted out of the ruleset, honestly. The issue is that Geniuses explicitly work outside real science much of the time, so bending over backward to make it fit our understanding of reality is kind of at odds. I think it's more important in Genius to make sure a Genius's work is consistent with itself. So, for example, you couldn't have a Genius obsessed with luminiferous aether couldn't create a hologram that relied on light's wave/particle nature, since it would be going against his (incorrect but internally useful) understanding of how light worked.
