    There is a legend whispered among the changelings in the City of Endless Summer: a Mirrorskin, officially burned by all four Courts under a cloud of suspicion. The rumors range from selling the secrets of one Court to another, to accusations of being a Loyalist. He hasn't been banished, however... no matter what Grandfather Thunder or the other monarchs might wish for him, there are greater, more mysterious forces compelling them to leave him be, for now. In fact, this conspiracy seems very concerned with keeping our beleaguered changeling pinned down in Miami and cut off from the aid of the Courts or anyone outside the Freehold.

    Still, not every changeling spurns him. There are always those in need of his special skills and knowledge, who have nowhere else to turn. There are also a few old friends within the Courts willing to lend a hand for a good cause. And so he survives, trading his craft for cash, favors and Glamour, quietly working under cover of suspicion and conspiracy to find whoever's responsible for burning him, so that he can one day fully take back his own life.

            The Neurosurgeon Knight was sane, once.

            Well, okay, not sane; he always had troubles, such as the idea that animals don't matter when you're learning the ins and outs of their biology. But he was a child, and he wasn't always a sociopath. That was before his sister and he were taken by the Burning Man. The Burning Man the common folk called, because his family was rich and they were not (actually, the Burning Man was just going to burn their family's barn, but then he noticed the two children and decided nobody would mind if he took them with him). He came back. His sister didn't, and it was by her sacrifice that he came back.

            And he came back twisted. He hated humans now, and what he saw of his father's dreams never convinced him to stop hating them. His young scientific genius lost what ethics it had; by the time he murdered his parents, the Knight already had a professional's knowledge of the brain, and by the time the doctor came to him, he possessed an intuitive knowledge that seemed almost divine. The doctor was impressed and scared, both by this mad wunderkind and his even more impressive ability to Skeinwalk. Faking sympathy to his plight, they build a machine together, a way to accomplish through technology what the True Fae could do naturally. Then the doctor betrayed the Knight, and the Knight was stripped of all but his brain and soul, forced into his own machine.

            The doctor was a very foolish man.

            The Knight is not, the Knight anticipated this, and he had long learned how to extend his own dreams into the waking world. Small effects, yes, but enough to send all around him to sleep, draw them into his own dream where he was effectively god and king. He selected a youth, a Cambion, to be the new vessel for his soul, and he overwrote the minds of others to become a legion of Incubi. He grew stronger, strong enough to manifest his own self-image outside the machine and vent his fury on the world that took his sister away and then sealed him within his own invention. And then three police officers, two mortals and a hidden changeling, came to his hospital.

            Three new people were drawn into the dream...

                    It was advertised as a paradise, a place where information flowed freely and you could create nearly anything you could imagine. Here, you could adopt a new persona, and say anything you wanted to whoever you wanted and suffer no repercussions, because there was nothing they could physically do to stop you. It wasn't until you entered this paradise that you encountered the threats it possessed. Armies of Trolls eager to destroy all that was good in the world, and massive wars that brought fire to the land. There were even cases of marauders hacking their way through your land, stealing your finances, spreading disease and taking control over your life. You tried to leave, but discovered you couldn't bring yourself to do it, as you had become entranced by this new world. Every time you tried to leave, your pulse would race and your nerves tingle as you quickly ran back to your safe zone. Here you could be sheltered from the outside world. Here you could be whoever you wanted to be. You learned how to destroy the Troll's food supply, and the fiery wars only continued as long as neither side relented. You even learned how to build a wall to keep the marauders off your territory!

                            Young Wade Wilson was never a well adjusted person. His mother died of cancer when he was very young, and his father was abusive. As a teenager, he was a delinquent and was kicked out of the military for bad behavior. After being diagnosed with cancer, he met a group of men who claimed to be from the government and promised to cure his cancer if he accepted to participate in a top secret biological augmentation program. However, these men were actually True Fae in the shape of Grey Aliens, and their experiments turned Wade into a deadly warrior. Wade was driven insane by the experience and refused to obey the commands of the Greys, who deemed him a failure and locked him away with other failed test subjects. He managed to escape from Arcadia with powerful Tokens and an amazing healing factor, but upon seeing that he was considered dead by the world, he snapped for good. He retreated into the Hedge and made more weapons, a Hollow and a Hedgespun suit to hide his deformity that was visible even through the Mask. He turned into a mercenary for hire with a love for bad jokes and chimichangas. His broken memories led him to believe there was some sort of cruel betting game among the failed test subjects, and he decided to give himself the same name: Deadpool.

                            (In game terms: Deadpool is a Courtless Ogre, and his kiths are Stonebones/Soldier. He has a special Merit that allows him to spend 1 glamour and roll Wyrd + Stamina, with each success healing one lethal damage or two bashing damage. He heals aggravated wounds like bashing wounds, and when his health meter is filled with aggravated damage, he falls unconscious instead of dying. He has Wyrd 7 and very low Clarity, and killing people is no longer a breaking point for him.)

                                                    There was once an African prince, a gold-cloaked boy who owned everything under the sun. It seemed his life would be happy- so happy, in fact, that the prince's uncle grew envious. He plotted murder, but had not the courage to carry out his ambitions...
                                                    ...not until a band of scavengers, dark-spotted and scrawny, approached him and offered allegiance. They had come from a shadowed land, a place on no map, and were hungry for life. With them as his pawns, the traitor felt emboldened.
                                                    Not long after, the king was crushed beneath stampeding wildebeest. The prince escaped only by chance, scrambling along cliff and canyon.
                                                    Then he fell into a wall of Thorns.

                                                    Years later, a man returns to the savannah, stronger and wiser. His choice of clothing is odd: a flame-red mantle and a lion's skin, so vivid and soft you would think it was still on the beast. He has endured much, he proclaims to the tribe, but none of it broke his spirit.
                                                    Not long after, the king is burned to death.

                                                        "In the jungle you must wait, until the dice roll five or eight"

                                                        It had seemed innocent enough. Just a game, hidden away beneath the roots of the tree your father pulled up to make an extension to his shoe factory. You couldn't explain why it was locked and chained in a chest, or why your heart hammered out a tattoo against your rib cage. Made of carved wood, with a green orb of glass marking the goal- filled with an unusual, swirling smoke just a shade or two darker than the glass itself. The pieces are easy enough to identify, carved seemingly from old ivory as they are- an elephant, a rhinoceros, a crocodile, and a monkey. Roll the dice, play the game, and pray to whatever you hold dear that you live. Land on the wrong spot, and the game isn't content to stay on the board, roll the wrong number, and expect to be hunted. Cheat- and pray that if you do you are never caught -and you'll find yourself in dire straits.

                                                        "You think that mosquitoes, monkeys, and lions are bad? That is just the beginning. I've seen things you've only seen in your nightmares. Things you can't even imagine. Things you can't even see. There are things that hunt you in the night. Then something screams. Then you hear them eating, and you hope to God you aren't dessert. Afraid? You don't know what afraid is."
