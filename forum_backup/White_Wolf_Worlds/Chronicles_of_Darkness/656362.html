    Builing a Lair - Thoughts and questions

    09-08-2015, 07:43 AM
    My goal in creating this thread is to discuss, collect and disseminate ideas and advice about Lairs. Everything from homebrew Traits to thoughts on how to tie together various Chambers thematically has a place here. I do expect it to cover Q&A so don't be shy about it either - I have a few queries of my own. I am only going to try and keep track of Lair based stuff here though.

    This post will be edited frequently to keep the Master list up to date.

    Traits:
    • New Trait: Wonderous
    • New Trait: Demolished

      I said I had a couple of questions; well here is some of them.
      1. How large is a single Chamber? Are we talking a single bedroom or a snow-capped mountain? A single storied house or 500 square metre garden maze? A subway station or the minimart at a petrol station? A forest clearing or the whole darn woods? Obviously there is some wiggle room here and multiple answers can be right but there is a limit. Some help identifying the line would be nice.
      2. How can non-Beast supernaturals 'stumble' on a Lair? I'm mainly thinking of how one of the Lost or the Awakened could conceivably enter the Primordial Dream (and subsequently a Bedgotten's Chamber). The simple answer of "can't happen" is less interesting story wise than the alternative.
      3. While individuals will react according their character; what do you think the general reaction for 'chamber-napping' is? By 'chamber-napping' I mean claiming a site as Chamber only to find out later that another Beast already has it in their Lair and now you're both Broodmates...It get's more complicated if the individuals in question already are a part of separate Broods. Technically, the RAW says that this just 'merges' the Broods but I doubt human psychology plus monstrous territoriality would let it be that simple. Of course you don't have to like your Brood so...[Clarification: Not asking for mechanical advice on this one, RAW works fine by me here.]

          I thought up a rough idea for a lair trait as well.


          Demolished

          Your soul is a thing of rage and hatred that mankind can not tame. It lashes at everything around it in an effort to vent its frustration. The landscape of your lair takes on a perilous form with fissures gouged into the earth, buildings in a condemned status, trees snapped like tooth picks and other symptoms of a natural disaster having torn its way through the place. Attempting to travel through any chambers with this trait are more difficult for one's enemies, as they must navigate the unstable terrain.

              I would set the base at the size of wherever the chamber was initially created. Like it wouldn't be too hard to believe that a chamber made in a forest and as a forest would be a similar size or that one created in the image of the apartment it happened in would be the same size. That said I would also say they have that vague sort of nightmare quality to them that makes it so the chambers are basically as big as they need to be in spite of a reasonable size. Like a locker room fashioned chamber that has the Maze trait could somehow enable a person to wander for miles
              and miles without encountering an area they've been before even though no locker room occupies a space listed in miles.

              Originally posted by FallenEco View Post
              How can non-Beast supernaturals 'stumble' on a Lair? I'm mainly thinking of how one of the Lost or the Awakened could conceivably enter the Primordial Dream (and subsequently a Bedgotten's Chamber). The simple answer of "can't happen" is less interesting story wise than the alternative.
              Well I can't speak from the etherial elements, but there are a few I can think of. The most obvious would be entering when the Beast has already opened the door to the lair. Like using Skeleton Key to force open a portal to the Lair and someone else going through before the portal has closed, or if you wanted to go with an odd coincidence have it happen because the Beast altered the destination while the other was in transit. Another would be walking in when a Beast has caused their Lair to suffuse an area through their tilts.

              As for less standard methods. I would say if you try accessing an areas umbral counterpart without a clear idea of what it looks like then you might be able to force open the door to a chamber if you're using the room a chamber was created in or recently accessed from.

              The one who owned the old chamber is probably going to be annoyed. I mean it gets harder to make a chamber in an area that already has one, so you had to force it. Not helped that you've not forcefully connected your lair and their lair, plus any existing broods, so I think fighting or at least arguing will come up over who has the right to the chamber and if no compromise can be reached it may eventually just move to one of them being made to destroy the offending chamber.

                      Context definitely plays a role in determining the size and detail of a Chamber. A Chamber based on a Skyscraper drawing inspiration from a height-induced panic attack will be less detailed than one inspired by homicidal rage carried out within one of its tenements, at least in regard to the tenement in question.

                      A non-Beast finding their way into a Lair would be as difficult, if not more difficult than casting sympathetic magic on the Beast itself, in that at the very least the character would have to know that a Beast makes it Lair there.

                      It depends on the Beasts in question, and local Begotten politics. If the Hive is a central political unit, and mutual support is an encouraged and accepted practice, than there shouldn't be any more enmity than normal for them.

                          Originally posted by FallenEco View Post
                          . . .How can non-Beast supernaturals 'stumble' on a Lair? I'm mainly thinking of how one of the Lost or the Awakened could conceivably enter the Primordial Dream (and subsequently a Bedgotten's Chamber). The simple answer of "can't happen" is less interesting story wise than the alternative. . .
                          I'd look long and hard at the Dreamspeaker Legacy. They talk extensively about "the Dreamborn" and "the Dream". And they even have a new Merit, "Dream Lands" . . . which certainly seems like it overlaps with the turf of the Begotten . . .

                          Now, the Legacy was developed long, long before Beast was even conceived of . . . But of all the mages, they seem the most likely to have the power to drop in on a Beast unannounced.

                          I'd also give a hard look at the Clavicularius. Not only do they specialize in goetic magics (and the Begotten have been described as "super goetics"), but they're known for building sanctuaries and retreats in the Temenos, which is right next to the Primordial Dream . . . The Key Bearers would likely be fascinated with (and possibly horrified by) the existence of the Begotten, and it wouldn't surprise me to learn that they could adapt their signature rotes to the task. Or find themselves stranded there, after a particularly spectacular failure . . .

                          But something that might be an option for anyone - supernatural or mundane - to visit a Lair would be in dreams. A Begotten is a primal fear made manifest, its Lair composed of human nightmares. It seems reasonable that people sleeping (and dreaming) in the mundane counterpart to a Chamber might find themselves accidentally stumbling through that Begotten's private realm . . .
