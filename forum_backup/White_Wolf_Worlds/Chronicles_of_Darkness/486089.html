    Which pop-culture Vampire belongs to which Clan

    04-20-2015, 03:59 AM
    Hallo everybody. I currently try to make the characteristics of the different Clans imaginable for newcomers. To do so I'm locking for examples of Vampires in movies, books and other media who match (at least roughly) one of the Clans. I am locking for several Examples, therefore I would be thankful for every suggestion.

    I think Daeva are pretty much Anne Rice, but what Vampire ells match them?

    Ventrue are Lord Ruthven and True Blood (because of Dominate and their constant trouble with their touchstones), but I think I missed something obvious.

    Nosferatu are Max Schreck and Murnau for sure, but are there examples of Nosferatu who are not ugly? Or ugly Nosferatu who are not funny but really terrifying?

    I thought Gangrel would be easy, but it isn't. Okay, Lost Boys and maybe From Dusk till Dawn, but did you ever saw Beast-like Vampires, who are or who are not transforming in to Animals, in a serious movie or book?

    Last one is the toughest I think. Which Vampires are Mekhet? Where did you saw the invisible? I think some of the many Vampire-Detectives could be a Mekhet, but which one is the most typical? Where did we saw a Vampire Cult? Which Vampire is such a stalker (no, not twilight Edward, yes he is a stalker, but he isn't good at it. I think twilight Vampires are Daeva, but that is a different story).

    Before you say Dracula, I have a Dracula-example for every Clan, because he shows characteristics of every. He is my example for a Über-Vampire.

    Thank you for ideas and suggestions.


    P.S. I'm locking for generalizations, so I prefer to say, the Vampire in this or that movie are like that specific Clan. I do not want to pick a specific Character if he is the only example in a given story and the other Vampires are much different.

                                      Eli from Let the Right One In strikes me as more of a Gangrel. Her vampiric, predatory nature seems to come across in more bestial, animalistic ways, especially in the book where she is described as being able to develop claws and membranes for flight. Not to mention her very matter-of-fact approach to her predation and the particularly brutal way she dispatches of Oskar's bullies at the end.

                                      As far as pop culture vampires that fit the Mekhet archetype, definitely Adam and Eve from Only Lovers Left Alive. They do not possess the usual super-strength, but are still very swift, very fast. They have refined senses, particularly Eve who has the power of psychometry (to know things about an object through touch alone). While they do not have powers akin to Obfuscate, the point stands that the both of them like to keep a fairly low profile and are fairly reclusive. The Mekhet-like obsession with knowledge is reflected in the obsessive nature of their hobbies; music and science for Adam, literature and the beauty of nature for Eve. Their interests in art and particularly Eve's interest in humanity would normally be seen as more of a Daeva trait, however they behave more like reluctant tourists of the human world. Witnesses, rather than participants. Detached and aloof. This strikes me as more of a 'Mekhet' way of acting as opposed to the balls-out hedonistic desperation for sensation and validation that marks the Daeva (Eve's 'sister' Ava is more of an impulsive, entitled Daeva with no sense of personal boundaries and endless appetite that is opposed to the ascetic tendencies of the two elder vampires).
