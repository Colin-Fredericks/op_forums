        Originally posted by DahQueenDiva View Post
        This might be a bit newbie, but what is the treasury? I get that it reflects the worth of stuff, but I'm not sure what purpose it serves. Can you eat stuff in it for breath? How do changes to one realm change the other? I'm kinda imagining smaug's ocean of golden, but across the whole world, with golden, jewel incrusted living idols to represent big businesses.
        Not a newb question at all; we're still working out what exactly the Treasury will entail. In fact, that's the next item on the docket for thematic adjustment.

        Here's what we have so far, and keep in mind most of this is flexible at this point:

        The Treasury: The Treasury, or rather the soul of the world, is a state of non-euclidean perception and reality that all dragons (from the Longs and Nidhoggr to the usurper Oroboroi) may enter by circling anticlockwise like a low-pressure system. In essence, circling lightens the soul, allowing it to drift into this higher perception.
        How the Treasury appears varies greatly by how much a dragon lightens her soul, and if she dives more deeply than her Heart can handle the light and power of this realm will burn her like fire.
        On its shallowest end, the Treasury reveals the inherent value of material goods (Assets), much like you have described; the world becomes awash in piles of riches. A dragon sees locations and people by how much she might benefit from taking them as Assets; Maidens wear apparel indicative of the kind of talent they might muster, the quality of a structure reflects its utmost potential (rather than its current state of dilapidation). If you picture a medieval filter painting over the standard reality that pretty much sums up the shallows.
        Go a little deeper and mortals start depicting personality traits physically across their body. At this state the dragon still maintains local scope but interacts with the surface thoughts of beings rather than their direct consciousness. Time is compressed here; a day's worth of interaction will translate to a single scene when the dragon returns from the Treasury. The world also twists; buildings begin to represent the organizations that are housed within them. Picture the Dark World from A Link to the Past, that was big inspiration for me for this depth.
        Even deeper and the individuals begin to vanish, amalgamating into massive ‘idols’ that represent the organizations in which they are involved. Space begins to warp at this perception; buildings that were separated by great geographic distance but closely related by ties of business and nepotism might be within walking distance of each other at this level of perception, or perhaps even connected by flesh and sinew. Walls and rivers begin to bleed, lightning courses through the air, and the sky takes on the appearance of myocardial tissue as a dragon at this perception is actually within the world Heart. At this stage, the dragon might accumulate months of interactions which compress to a single scene when returning from the Treasury. Killing one of these great Idols (Many of which are Kaiju-grade size, by the way) could very will shatter the organizations they represent).

        These time compressions are a peculiar thing; when the dragon returns the actions she has taken will not have yet occurred, but will play out over the days and months to follow. For this reason, a dragon may use the Treasury to ensure everything is in place to her advantage in the immediate future.
        Alternatively, the time compressions might work retroactively, this is a key point I’d like to discuss as we elaborate on the Treasury.

        A few key notes: At the deepest depths of the Treasury, an Oroboros might encounter the Great Wyrm, which is the embodiment of the Oroboroi. Let’s just say it’s not a good idea to pick a fight with this thing. On the other end is Saint George, the greatest Dragonslayer, who is an embodiment of the humanity the Oroboroi cling to. These two unstoppable forces are in constant conflict and it’s generally a bad idea to get caught in the crossfire.
        Also, Oroboroi who die awaken in the deepest depths of the Treasury and must find ways to ‘leaden’ their soul so they might return to their earthbound perception. This often involves collecting ‘sparks’ (name tentative) to restart their corporeal heartbeat so that they can regenerate.

                                        Originally posted by Iceblade44 View Post
                                        I like the Treasury doe I have some questions

                                        From the sound of it the Treasury will have Idols which I guessing will be the spirt-like inhabitants of the realm, does anyone have ideas how their mechanics going to work and how they interact with the materiel world?
                                        The Idols that were described are amalgamations of the population of the society that constitutes their parts. In the material world, these 'beings' are organizations, clubs, companies, and other lifeless ideas that are given life by the sum of their parts. As such, an Idol doesn't move in on the Daylight world because it already exists in the daylight world; its state as a living being is a personification that lets an Oroboros approach it from a different angle. Whereas in the Daylight world dealing with an organization can be a real hassle what with meeting appointments, swarms of lawyers, and various bureaucratic hurdles to cross; by contrast a dragon interacting with the organization as a living entity in the Treasury evades these complications and comes straight to the core of the matter.
                                        When one of these idols is wounded, the damage bleeds back into the real world over the course of several months (Time is compressed at that depth of the Treasury). For example, say an Oroboros attacks an Idol and removes the arm it uses to carry its weapon. Upon returning to the real world, news would spread that the Weapons Research division of the company the idol represents is suffering serious cutbacks leading up to the closure of that wing of the business. A lab exploded and killed six workers, and they are trying to distance themselves from the scandal.
                                        Conversely, should the Oroboros persuade the idol to disarm rather than take its weaponry away by force, the company's business model might be completely rewritten (perhaps due to a change in CEO? Or perhaps a merger?) and the removal of the Weapons Research division would be a result of this change in direction rather than due to the deaths of six people.

                                        So interacting with idols is easier than trying to change an organization in person, but it is harder to predict the results. It's sort of like hitting an old radio repeatedly to get it to work instead of opening it up and finding the actual problem; you might be able to get what you want but you might break something else in the process.


                                        Originally posted by Iceblade44 View Post
                                        From my view the Treasury sounds a lot like the Shadow, anyway to make it make more clear?

                                        Can other supernaturals enter the Treasury and if they can how they look at it (especially mages)?
                                        In a Mage-esque cosmology the Treasury would fall somewhere between the Primal Wilds and the Aether. However, the Treasury is the a state of perception rather than an actual place (much like Twilight is a state of perception), so being able to travel there with magic is questionable. Were you to house rule a way into the treasury, you would probably want to tie it to Prime magic.


                                        Originally posted by Iceblade44 View Post
                                        And from the splat mythology wouldn't the Treasury be the heart of one of the Longs?
                                        The Treasury is the Heart of Huang Long, the center wind who destroyed herself to create the universe. It is also the heart of every dragon (Nidhoggr AND Oroboroi... but not the four other longs) as each dragon is herself a fragment of Huang Long. In essence, the world heart is the combined potential of every Heart within the universe, and as such it and the Treasury shrink every time a Heart is destroyed and it and the Treasury grow every time a Heart is created. This is just as DahQueenDiva suggested.


                                        Originally posted by Iceblade44 View Post
                                        Wouldn't their be something like a gate or something, I like the circling idea and you should keep it but imagine this for a second imagine you see a guy stop out of nowhere and started circling and then disappears that this not make much sense
                                        Just as actions are translated up into abstraction as the depth of perceiving the Treasury increases, so to are actions translated down to practical application. A dragon interacting with the Idol of a corporation at the deepest depths of the Treasury would be seen in the real world making appointments, conversing with lawyers and receptionists, filing paperwork, and generally doing everything needed to achieve the changes she is triggering directly in the idol. So a dragon does not actually disappear when going into the treasury, she sets her body on autopilot.

                                        ...This would perhaps be easier to swallow if we removed the time compression element of the Treasury. Or perhaps the dragon does not take all the actions necessary to exact the change; maybe interacting at that level of perception drives other entities (such as members of the organization the idol represents) to take these actions in the dragon's favor, much like pawns on a chessboard. It is worth discussing further.


                                        At the moment the definition of the Treasury is in flux. I really want to discuss with everybody what works and what doesn't, and make it Dragon's thing instead of my thing. I've got a bad track record with systems and thematic elements that are just my thing, so let's make it everybody's thing.
