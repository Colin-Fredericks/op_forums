                    Did the werewolf in question survive this encounter?

                    Being prepared against Mages is crucial, and if the wolfie lived, the wolfie can come back with a pack. And they know this spell is something that can/will be used.
                    They prepare grappling hooks for grounding and firearms or at least throwing weapons, only engage in closed spaces where they can use prodigious strength to literally jump at Mages from the ceiling if it comes to that (grapple rules maybe? Mages don't like those)

                    And that's just for those specific ones. As others said, if they use the trick too much, word gets around, and then it's more reasonable that countermeasures are prepared.

                    If there are consequences from this wolfie encounter (i.e. their allies approach) make the encounter in a public space, so shooting random passers-by into the air will be a risk. This provides choices (yes they can launch a whole plaza into the air, that brings a whole new set of issues)

                    If your players like combat challenges, consider using environmental factors to provoke interesting creative spellcasting - in darkness, in a heavily-treed area with attacks from above as well as maneuvers behind trees and such. In an office building with corridors twisting and creating ambush possibilities. Both of those environments can involve lighting circumstances as well as provide interesting reactions to the gravity trick - swing off branches even if you're falling upwards, use added momentum, some office building corridors have low ceilings, barely a jump to engage into melee.
