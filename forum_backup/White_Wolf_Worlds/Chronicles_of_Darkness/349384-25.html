                So, Angels and Demons!

                Person of Interest is already named as an inspirational media in the Demon book, but I actually disagree on their read of John Reese. While he's certainly a Destroyer by nature, I'd actually put him down as a Tempter, no Saboteur. He'll go to extreme lengths to protect his friends, sure, but he actively resists getting pulled back into the games of global stakes and the future of humanity, instead focusing on the 'irrelevant' numbers and protecting his new life and friends. Even in the most recent season, which is by far the closest to an actual Demon game, he's more interested in the numbers than fighting Samaritan.

                (I haven't gotten my hands on them yet, but from what I've heard, Harold Finch seems more an Analyst than a Psychopomp)

                Root is certainly an Integrator, though a somewhat radical one given she wants the God-Machine replaced wholesale by a more benevolent iteration. I'd probably put her as a Psychopmp, given her vast number of connections and excellence at arranging coincidences and moving people and things around in her past life. Messenger could also work though.

                Sameen Shaw, by contrast, is without a shadow of doubt a Destroyer, but her agenda's a bit more unclear. I'd probably go with Saboteur; despite her sharing the same ostensible goals as Root and being willing to take the Machine at her word, recent events have definitely made her goal first and foremost to kill god.

                The Machine is a particular significant bit of subverted infrastructure, and the Subway is a bolthole.

                Greer, meanwhile, is a loyal Trumpet.

                Moving on to different media,

                John Wick was a Destroyer trying so, so hard to be an Antimonian. The Continental is a Temporal Agency

                Phillip and Elizabeth Jennings are loyal Angels of the machine, though they've come close to falling on quite a few occasions. Probably Swords, but the particular matrix keeping them around is both easy to sustain and hard to replicate, so while not quite Exiles (their orders come through loud and clear), they have a long leash and get used as Wheels and occasionally even Trumpets.

                The Man (in the Booth at the End) IS an Exile, and quite a minor one at that (probably rank 1, maybe 2). He desperately clings to the book, the linchpin of his last mission, using basic deal-with-the-devil stuff to get people to complete Occult Matrices and using the excess power to grant their wishes. Doris is a Tempter who tracked him down and would very much like that book, trying to get him to fall on the way there.

                        I have far too many ideas involving the CoD and RWBY.

                        Blake is a Beast Changeling (Dual Kith Truefriend/Runnerswift) with the pseudonym 'Belle' upon return.
                        Weiss is an Obrimos Mage who's either a GotV or unaligned with the shadow name 'Snow'
                        Yang is a Bastet Hatara shapechanger under the Sun-chaser accord with the nickname 'Goldilocks'.

                        Glynda is an Obrimos GotV under the Votiaries of the Ordained legacy--tasked to keep guard of Ozpin (see below). SN: The Goodwitch.
                        Ozpin is a high-wyrd Changeling (Probably Wizened Oracle/Fatemaker) and the Monarch of the Winter Court. Psuedonym: The Wizard
                        James Ironwood is also a Changeling, Elemental Manikin/Soldier and the King of the Summer Court. Pseudonym: The General
                        Qrow and Raven are Wind-folk Corvian Shapechangers under the Sun-chaser and Wind-Dancer accords.
                        Taiyang is a retired Hunter (albeit one with supernatural sympathies) (No details because I don't really know anything about Hunter)
                        Summer Rose was a Flowering Fairest who made a deal with her keeper so she could have a child.

                        Ruby is a standard mortal with more experience than many adults, let alone kids her age. Her main feature would probably be the Biokinesis merit (allowing for the classic heroic stunts that protagonists have).
