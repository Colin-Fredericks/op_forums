                        Little late to the conversation, but after reading those first page spoilers I just had to express my sheer giddy anticipation.

                        To my eyes Deviant is looking a lot like Mage: The Ascension. Not in the setting, or themes, but in the sheer breadth of potential. Allow me to explain: To me, the best quality of Ascension is just how much you can do with it. Basically any character concept vaguely aligned with the concept of "consciously performs miracles" is embraced and enabled by Ascension. I love that, and I'm getting the same vibe from Deviant. Reading just that little spoiler I must have run through a dozen or more brief character concepts, all different, and all seemingly embraced by the narrative and system.

                        For someone like me, who loves the sandbox above all else, Deviant looks to be a god send and (fingers crossed) a game I that will bend to meet my every need. Two thumbs up.
