                Originally posted by Darinas View Post
                You're welcome to join here^^ maybe by combining our ideas we can both get somewhere;
                Much obliged.

                I also have an idea for an internal plot that links the local Dark Cult/Tainted Place and one of the antagonistic Radiants. The idea is that the Cult is composed of psychiatrists who regard it as their right to abuse those mentally weak to develop disorders and exploit them for their own academic and monetary benefit, and their domain is a mental hospital so corrupted by the treatment of the patients and staff not part of the core group that it's become a Maze in the Dark World that occasionally bleeds over and causes nightmarish hallucinations that people have confused for a true haunting. One of the antagonistic Princesses is someone who was thrown there ostensibly because of her paranoid personality disorder, realistically because she knew things that would be immensely damaging to her parents' reputation. She Blossomed due to her desperate attempts to ease the pain of her fellow inmates and led a massive escape, whereupon she's refashioned herself as a walking Alice in Wonderland pastiche who acts a lot more eccentric than she actually is to sell her image, and channels her abilities as a Sword into upending society and conventional ideas of thought (she calls herself a "revolutionary against sanity").

                I also have something I unofficially refer to as "Inner Demons Pokemon" or "Mirror Universe Persona"; a lost art used in the immediate aftermath of the fall of the Kingdom to preserve humanity against the Dark by calling forth human evils to fight alien ones; in effect, a microtemplate revolving around the so-called "Sciomancer" or "Howler" calling forth their most animalistic impulses as a Dreamlander familiar which they send forth to accomplish their goals. Thing is, "animalistic" means "primal", not "evil"; the inner monster draws itself from humans as fundamentality altruistic animals with social impulses, and so it lashes out against creatures it regards as threats to its "tribe", and remains calm and friendly around said members - because of that, the monster (often referred to as a mazkim by modern Sciomancers, due to how it was rediscovered) is immune to Dark corruption unless its master is directly corrupted first. It was lost as the Dark receded, but it was rediscovered by adherents of the Lesser Key of Solomon (hence the term Howler; "Ars Goetia" literally means "Howling Arts"), and the theories of Sigmund Freud popularized the art among the occult and desperate set. Most of the new adherents died due to Freud being kind of an idiot about the actual composition of the subconscious mind (the mazkim is indeed a monster from the id, but human instincts contain the altruistic and self-destructive forms; more than one mazkim has come to believe its Howler poses a threat to their loved ones by treating it as an evil beast), but the survivors adapted, and refined the art. It's not a safe magic, or a sane one - but it's open to anyone willing to stare into the abyss and find something useful, and without Nobles, often it's the best possible option. Of course, that doesn't mean the Howlers can't become evil all on their own; a few have learned to spawn Amanojaku loyal to them from their mazkim. I figure of the two examples, one is an antagonist who sends her Amanojaku to possess people she regards as a threat to her suburb, making it a shiny, happy police state run by a cult of would be neighborhood watchmen who fear anything that speaks of the Darkness - even Radiants, since they think a Princess draws the Dark out, when they're fine keeping it asleep.
