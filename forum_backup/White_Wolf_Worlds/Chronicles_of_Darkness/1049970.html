    [2e Legacy Adaptation] Clavicularious

    02-16-2017, 02:55 PM
    So I finally finished my 2e Clavicularious write up and I'm looking for some critique on the attainments I've written with the help of my ST and maybe some ideas for optional attainments. I left most of the history and background info the same as was printed in the 1e version, so I'll leave that out. I also ended up having to adapt a spell from the 1e book, and that is attached to this post as well.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisite: Mind 2, Empathy 2, and Resolve or Composure at 3 or higher.
    Initiation: A prospective Seeker must confront his Vice demon in the astral, and resist its temptation.
    Ruling Arcana: Mind
    Suggested Oblations: The Rite of Castigation, meditation on past moments of weakness, engaging in psychological therapy (either as the therapist or as a patient). Scribing the Keys of Solomon in a durable medium.
    Yantras: Reciting the Keys of Solomon, A summoning circle, Sitting on a throne, Regalia
    Attainment
    1. Insight
      1. Prerequisite: Initiation
      2. After understanding his own failings, the Seeker finds himself better able to recognize the failings of others.
      3. Mechanics: This attainment emulates the mind 1 spell “Know Nature” with the free reach assigned to also learn the subjects Aspirations and Obsessions
      4. Optional: Spirit 1, the Seeker can recognize when another’s will is not their own. This optional attainment allows them to see spirits possessing others.
    2. Urge the Demon
      1. Prerequisite: Mind 2, Empathy 3 or Expression 3
      2. A Keeper-prospectus uses the knowledge gained by the previous attainment to influence another’s inner demons.
      3. Mechanics: This attainment emulates the mind 2 spell “Emotional Urging”.
    3. Calling the Demons
      1. Prerequisite: Mind 3, Empathy 3, Expression 2
      2. The Keeper preforms a great ritual, drawn from the Keys of Solomon, to call their Vice and bring it to the forefront of their mind. This allows the Solomonists to gain the benefits of indulging their Vice without actually giving in.
      3. Mechanics: The mage performs a ritual emulating the spell Goetic Struggle (attached below). When presented with the opportunity to indulge his vice, the mage makes a gnosis + mind roll vs. Resolve + Composure. If the Mage wins, he regains willpower, as if he indulged his vice, without taking the action. If the Mage loses, he must indulge his vice, without regaining willpower. Reach is used to add advanced duration.
    4. Bind the Demons
      1. Prerequisite: Mind 3, Spirit 4, Empathy 4, Expression 3
      2. The Elder-prospectus has learned the secret of Solomon. Using the Keys in a place of power in conjunction with a powerful ritual, the Elder-Prospectus binds his inner demons to fetishes to wear upon his person, removing the demon’s power to influence him and gaining a measure of control over its powers.
      3. Mechanics: The mage performs a ritual emulating Spirit 4’s Craft Fetish, using Mind 3 conjunctive to summon a Goetia instead. The Primary factor is duration.
    5. Suppress the Inner Demon
      1. Prerequisite: Mind 5, Empathy 5, Expression 4
      2. The Elder’s knowledge of Goetia and the mind allows him to completely remove the demon’s influence on himself or another for 1 hour.
      3. Mechanics: This emulates the Mind 5 Spell “Amorality”
    • Goetic Struggle (Mind 3)
      • Practice: Ruling
      • Primary Factor: Duration
      • Suggested Rote Skills: Expression, Academics, Empathy
    This spell “summons” an inner demon into a mage’s consciousness and binds it to his will. Then, whenever the mage’s Vice might come into play during the spell’s duration, he can mentally wrestle the demon (a contested roll between his Gnosis + Mind and his own Resolve + Composure). If he wins, he gains Willpower as if his action had reflected the Vice (even though he didn’t actually act upon it). The demon is put down and banished (this spell must be cast again to gain the same effect). If the demon wins the contest, however, the mage must act out the Vice, and he doesn’t get the Willpower for it.
    • Optional Reach:
      • +1: Advanced duration


    Thanks in advance for any help
