    The Anima Meccanica, a Moros Legacy of Entrepreneuring Inventors

    07-10-2020, 03:32 PM
    In short: A solarpunk Legacy of inventors that focuses on merging technology with spirits and sponsoring new green innovations. The Attainments and yantras reward them for coming up with all sorts of gadgets and seeking out exotic materials. They are able to create super formulas, which can be used to temporarily grant spirit powers and explore the Shadow on their own terms. At higher levels, they are able to build living, self-modifying gadgets, carry their entire arsenal with them and even grant permanent spirit powers. Do you want to roleplay a gadgeteer genius like Tony Stark or Edna Mode ? Then this is the Legacy for you.

    Greetings everyone, this is a solarpunk Legacy that employs the Spirit Arcanum. It is inspired by sources such as Iron Man, the Bioshock series, the Adeptus Mechanicus and Prey. They specialize in harnessing the power of the Shadow to create magitek. I also added design notes for those interested. Feedback is very welcome.

    The Anima Meccanica

    Origins

    “As we enjoy great advantages from the inventions of others, we should be glad of an opportunity to serve others by any invention of ours, and this we should do freely and generously.”
    - Benjamin Franklin










    Nicknames: Soleris, trailblazers, luminari, pioneers, artificers, maecenas, mechanists, spiriti dell’invenzione
    Parentage: Moros, Silver Ladder, Free Council

    Background:

    One of the greatest triumphs of the Silver Ladder was during the Great Refusal, where they were able to rally the Diamond Orders to support the Nameless. United, they dealt the Seers one of the most crippling blows in their history, a bitter wound whose effects linger even today.

    When the Nameless Orders reorganized into the Council of Free Assemblies, their entrance into the newly formed Pentacle proved anything but smooth. The Silver Ladder once more extended a hand to aid in this transition, starting many joint projects to improve the life of Sleepers.

    One of these endeavors was project Sole, an initiative to recapture the glory the city of Urbino had during the reign of Federico III da Montefeltro, the "Light of Italy", a great patron of humanist beliefs. By creating a think tank, they would foster the development of innovative and sustainable technologies. Weaving Supernal insights into the research, they would increase the chances of candidates Awakening.

    The Light of Italy would shine brightly once more, Urbino would draw minds from all over the world, many of which would become Sleepers or Sleepwalkers as they progressed in their projects. While the Libertines inspired and weaved Supernal ideas into the work of artists and engineers, the Théarchs would provide the knowledge and patronage to make them sustainable and widely available. In time this Cryptopoly would bring unprecedented prosperity to the city, making it into a global hub of invention.

    This made them draw a few conclusions. The first is that those who think outside the box are more likely to see beyond it. The second is that everyone has something to offer as an inventor, all they require is the infrastructure, knowledge and team to draw out their potential. In acting as patrons of inventors, new green technologies could create a better future, all the while weakening the grip of the Lie on the human spirit. In so doing, the foundations to create Hieraconis could be laid out.

    The great results of this joint venture would prompt them to expand it worldwide, sponsoring creators and entrepreneurs, as well as programs that incentivize people to embark on that path. The sun of project Sole no longer meant only the Light of Italy, but the light of humanistic beliefs and a celebration of the creative spirit.

    In order to uphold these ideals, they started to shape their souls to become their paragons and draw out this potential from others. The Shadow provided a way to do this, its boundless and chaotic potential awaited direction by the structured thought of humanity. Those that could harness all that was inherent to it could create wonders before undreamt of, sending the creative passion of users aflame and paving the way for a brighter future.


    Appearances:

    The Luminari often tend to favor those who demonstrate a mix of a scientific mind and an entrepreneurial spirit, having a love of working in both the business side and creating inventions.

    Prospective members often have a strong drive to innovate in their respective fields, seeking to do so in a sustainable way. When leading their teams, they endeavour to foster creativity, provide better working conditions and pursue excellence above profit. As such, when Maecenas go into business, their companies tend to have a solidly planned triple bottom line.

    Since the confection of clothes is one of the most ancient forms of expressing human creativity and craft mastery, it is traditional for Trailblazers to create their own garments. This allows them to construct an elegant image when acting as entrepreneurs, angel investors or negotiators. In everyday life, they tend to dress in colorful garments with a sporty look.

    Their raiments normally take the form of high quality clothes. Closer examination would reveal that they are optimized by using innovative mundane and supernatural materials, such as weaves and polymers. Strands of technognostic circuits are often mixed in as embroidery. Thus do they guarantee maximum mobility, defense and express their creativity. What would look like elaborate custom items (tie pins, brooches, rings, glasses frames, etc) usually are useful gadgets.

    Their inventions tend to have an asymmetric and fluid shape, with naturalistic choices of colors and themes, often resulting in an aesthetic inspired by Art Nouveau. While function always comes before form, the Spiriti dell’Invenzione believe in creating devices that showcase a harmonious integration of nature and technology, hoping to inspire others down the same path.

    Doctrine

    “Science knows no country, because knowledge belongs to humanity, and is the torch which illuminates the world.”
    – Louis Pasteur

    Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Science 2, two dots in one of the following skills: Crafts, Computers, Academics, Persuasion or Socialize
    Initiation: The prospective candidate must create a new green technology, acting as both an active developer and campaigner (either as an angel investor or team leader). They must then register the patent and have it manufactured.

    The final initiation happens in their workshop alongside the spirit of the fabricating company. There their soul is projected by an ephemeral hologram interface, allowing them to jointly invent its new form. Because of this, they normally establish their own enterprises and are very careful who they partner with, lest they be faced with an uncooperative or malicious spirit.

    Organization







    Pioneers can often be found at the forefront of the Silver Ladder and Free Council’s most ambitious technological initiatives. From creating new arcologies, to floating or underwater cities, to developing underground settlements, to new clean energy sources, they are always redefining the bleeding edge of magical and mundane technology.

    The Artificers often build a place to tinker with new designs. This normally takes the form of a workshop, filled with prototypes, blueprints and an assortment of customized magical tools. It's very common for Luminari to constantly personalize and improve upon their Dedicated Tool, allowing them to have a multitool for inventing their way out of any situation.

    The entrepreneurial spirit is strong within the Maecenas, a deep seated drive to create a better future and light the creative spark in every mind. As such, they tend to spend a lot of time working with and championing many new technological developers. Because of this, as well as their own creations, many Spiriti dell’Invenzione tend to accumulate multiple patents throughout their lifetime, providing a steady stream of income. Veteran members often construct entire dedicated research complexes, with advanced AI, smart holographic projector systems, small particle accelerators and many other wonders decades ahead of their time.

    While the Trailblazers are spread around the globe, a great number congregate for the annual Urbino Inventor's Fair. During this week-long event, new partnerships are created as they meet Sleeper and Awakened inventors alike. While great care is taken not to overtly expose Sleepers to the Supernal, they tend to fill the area with wonders made with their Attainments, giving the city a futuristic look. In this way, they hope to push back against the Lie and Awaken some participants.

    Theory

    The human mind has boundless potential. By creating tools, humans have been able to rise above their natural limits. Every time they met an obstacle, they used creativity and ingenuity to overcome it. The potential lies within every mind, each able to create a unique contribution.

    Not all are aware of their own capabilities. Yet when knowledge is made available, alongside access to the right tools, a synergistic team and a window of opportunity, innovations will spring forth. A great leader is an entrepreneur who is able to not only guide others down the inventive path, but also able to inspire them to hold onto higher values while creating.

    Leadership rings hollow unless one personifies the ideals they campaign. The Shadow is a reflection of human emotion, embodied in a chaotic form and holding limitless potential. But to those that know how to harness its power, to refine it, shape it and work it into mechanical wonders, the possible applications are endless.

    Lead a technological revolution by example, cultivate the boundless creative potential of humanity and invent a better tomorrow today.

    Magic

    “Sometimes when you innovate, you make mistakes. It is best to admit them quickly, and get on with improving your other innovations”
    - Steve Jobs

    Ruling Arcanum: Spirit
    Yantras: Succeeding on a Crafts or Science roll related to the spell (+2), engineering or crafting tools (+1, +2 if customized), workshop, lab or atelier (+1, +2 if they contain bleeding edge tech or magitek), environmentally friendly technology (+1 for something like solar panels, +2 for something big like an arcology or dedicated house/complex, +3 if it also incorporates magitek), blueprints (+1, +2 if stored in interactive media), software (+1, +2 if it contains advanced AI, +3 if it employs a spirit as the AI), gadgets (+1, +2 if magitek made or invented by the mage themselves), first field test of a new invention (+3), engineered materials or alloys (+1, +2 if they incorporate perfected or supernatural components), robots, androids, power armor or other types of inventions years ahead of their time (+2, +3 if they incorporate modular supernatural components)

    Oblations: Developing a new green technology or magitek device, tinkering with or repairing machines or equipment, giving a lecture on an invention, convincing someone to try out a new green technology, having a meeting with investors or the development team of an invention, researching the Shadow and its inhabitants, crafting a mechanical device or piece of equipment, writing a computer program, leading or organizing an expedition into the Shadow, looking for new inventors to sponsor, organizing an event to showcase an invention or an inventors' fair, dissecting or reverse engineering a piece of technology.

    Attainments

    First: Innovazione Personalizzata
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    Masterful warriors and artisans often speak of becoming one with their tools as a fundamental step in the path to elevate their skills to the next level. While the Soleri commend their devotion, they also recognize that a more streamlined interface could greatly facilitate this process.

    After a scene designing an interface using ephemera, this allows any tools to read the target’s intentions and move preemptively to best match them. This reduces all penalties on equipment (speed, dexterity, initiative, untrained, etc) by [Spirit] each. This can make a sniper rifle as intuitive to wield as a pistol, a chainsaw as simple to use as a dagger (provided the user has the strength to wield it) or moving in plate armor as fluid as in everyday clothes.

    This is a Potency primary Compelling of Spirit with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

    Optional: Matter 1
    In much the same way that the interface between user and tool can be optimized, so too can the High Speech be reconfigured to facilitate interaction with spirits and materials.

    The Mechanist gains the ability to speak in Lingua del Sole, a new type of mantra that can be used on Attainments, Matter or Spirit spells to allow them to be cast at sensory range. While it does not give a dice bonus like High Speech, nothing stops the use of High Speech as a separate yantra.

    Second: Progettazione Ispirata
    Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Science 3
    After optimizing the interface, the next step is to improve on the drivers. With but a touch, the Artificers can create disposable programs, which entice the spirits of tools to utilize their capabilities to aid users.

    This is a positive version of     Gremlins that transforms the first [Spirit] successes into exceptional ones. Should the user be under the effect of the first Attainment, the optimized interface grants them the ability to choose which successes become exceptional. Reach is assigned to instant use, advanced duration and choosing which uses receive the effect, with the restriction making up for the extra Reach.

    Optional: Matter 2, a Dedicated crafting or scientific tool
    The Spiriti dell’Invenzione all have their own preferred tools, which they revel in constantly improving upon and updating. In time they learn to take this one step beyond. By channeling their inventive impetus through their Dedicated Tools, they are able to manipulate materials like never before.

    This replicates     Shaping, with Reach assigned to instant use, affecting solids and reducing the rolls needed to create, repair or modify an object.

    However, the Maecenas are patrons of the creative spirit of humanity. Because of this, this Attainment cannot be used to warp tools or equipment, only to create or modify them (although it can still be employed freely on other materials).

    Third: Perfezionamento del Processo
    Prerequisites: Spirit 3, two dots in a second Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills
    Spirits are the ultimate specialist AI. Not only do they have a nearly unmatched knowledge of their subjects, but they can also develop a tremendous ability to manipulate them. More miraculous of all, they sustain themselves by promoting or doing their functions well, possibly creating a clean energy source. Ideally a Luminari would want to incorporate them into a project, but circumstances may not permit it. When time is short, the spirit is uncooperative, violent or the needs of the project are too great, they know how to quickly override its cogitation cores.

    This replicates Command Spirit with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration. In the case a Clash of Wills is provoked by an order going against the spirit’s nature or due to outside interference, the Trailblazer automatically scores [Spirit] automatic successes.

    Optional: Matter 3








    The potential of the human mind is limitless and the Shadow holds the key to unlocking it. While at this level the Luminari is still unable to permanently draw it forth, nothing is impossible for a creative mind. To this end, they have devised a way to refine ephemeral energies into a compound that can temporarily grant humans the ability to wield spirit and technomagical abilities.

    By spending a point of mana, the Soleri is able to quickly distill and combine ambient ethereal energies, transforming a consumable item (food, medicine, injectables, etc) into a Musa. This substance replicates up to [Spirit] points of a spirit or Angel's Influences, Numina or Manifestations, chosen at the time of creation. When consumed, it is reverse Claimed by the user, granting a single use of each stored ability. The user can choose when to activate or deactivate them.

    Perhaps its most astonishing property is to allow Manifestations to work in reverse. This permits humans to Possess or Claim a spirit, enter the Shadow in Twilight form or even Discorporate and reform at their home.

    This substance is designed to be fully in tune with humanity's inventive spirit, therefore any relevant rolls are made with the user’s Intelligence + the highest between their Crafts and Science skills. Whenever Rank is relevant, the user is considered to have [Spirit]. Should a Clash of Wills happen, the user scores [Spirit] automatic successes.

    Due to the flexibility they afford, it's not unusual for Trailblazers to incorporate Musa dispensing modules into their inventions as a contingency measure.

    This is a Potency primary, Spirit variation of Spell Potion, with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration. A point of mana is used to pay for the effect and activating Permanence, truncated to one mana per Legacy rules.

    Fourth: Espandendo L'orizzonte
    Prerequisites: Spirit 4, Science 4
    All Pioneers will eventually face a situation where they will not have the right tools available for the job. While they try to customize their field toolkit to be as flexible as possible, even this has limits. But that has never stopped the Anima Meccanica before and they have designed a way to carry their workshop with them.

    By touching an object, they are able to transmute it into ephemera and store it for later use. This allows them to manipulate the ephemeral forms to follow the mage or to be equipped once they release the effect on an item. Furthermore, this functions even in Realms of existence with no Twilight, ensuring their full arsenal is alway available.

    However, this has been optimized to only work on non-living targets, spiritual items (fetishes, talens, etc) and magitek.

    This is a variation of Twilit Body with Reach assigned to instant use, advanced duration and being immaterial even in Realms without Twilight, with the restriction making up for the extra Reach.


    Optional: Matter 4



    Having elevated their mastery of the mechanical and creative arts to sublime heights, the Soleri has made the breakthrough needed to create Dispositivos Montefeltro, a miraculous fusion of human engineering and the unbridled potential of the spirit world. These devices not only employ a clean energy source, but also possess capabilities and adaptability that make them seem like nanotech to the uninitiated.

    After spending a scene fusing a spirit into a physical medium and investing it with a point of mana, the Mechanists are able to force them to Materialize within it. This allows the spirit to use an action to freely sculpt the shape of the vessel, within the limits of its size and its purview. In addition, the spirit has full access to its abilities and supernatural powers, but cannot act against the Artificer or disobey their orders. Lastly, they can spend a point of Essence to fuel their vessel for a scene (heat a forge, fire bullets, fuel a car, etc).

    This allows the Spiriti dell’Invenzione to create masterful assistants. It is also often employed to invent living technology (weapons, vehicles, etc), with hyper advanced AI that shifts to accommodate the needs of the moment and has a clean energy source. Some of their more revolutionary projects utilize one Dispositivos Montefeltro per component, creating complex devices with modular capabilities.

    This is a custom spell mixing elements from Bind Spirit, Golem and Transubstantiation (Essence into whatever fuel they employ), creating a form mixing the characteristics of both a golem and a Materialized spirit. Reach is assigned to indefinite duration and a point of mana being used to pay for indefinite duration and Permanence, truncated to one mana as per Legacy rules.

    Alternatively, when working with a particularly untrustworthy or old fashioned client, or whenever they simply need an Essence battery, they can provide a more traditional solution. After spending a scene installing technognostic circuitry into an item and investing it with a point of mana, they can make it into a Fetish.

    Building on their previous work is much easier for the Luminari, allowing them to yield better results. If the item used was created, repaired or modified by the second optional Attainment, then its equipment rating can be added to the amount of Numina, Influence or Essence bound within it. Unfortunately, the use of this rudimentary technology also brings back the possibility of the technognostic matrix becoming overloaded, making it possible for the bound spirit to escape.

    This replicates Create Fetish with Reach assigned to indefinite duration and a point of mana being used to pay for indefinite duration.

    Fifth: La Frontiera Infinita
    Prerequisites: Spirit 5, two dots in a third Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills
    The spark of creativity and the entrepreneurial drive burn brightly within the Maecenas, their words echo a passion so tangible that even spirits can be put under its sway. This has allowed the grand inventors to recruit scores of spirits for even the most outlandish projects and permitted them to defuse delicate situations they created, such as binding unwilling spirits to their inventions.

    When socially maneuvering against any spirits, reduce the target number of Doors by [Spirit], with an additional one being removed against ones whose Rank is less than the Mechanist's honorary one. All of these rolls also gain 9-again.

    For a point of Willpower, a single roll gains 8-again. For a point of mana, it gains rote quality.

    Optional: Matter 5
    Having achieved the apex of the craft, the Luminari has discovered how to permanently take power from spirits and technological beings, by creating a Merveille. In this way, they craft their masterpieces by reinventing human evolution itself.

    Activating them requires rolling Gnosis + Spirit and consumes a point of Essence from its reserves. Once used, they produce a pseudo-spirit technognostic implant that is reverse Claimed by targets, permanently granting them (a Lasting effect) any desired combination of the contained Numina or Influences.

    Users may spend a point of Willpower, mana or resonant Essence to activate them. Because these are customized to be fully in tune with the inventive spirit of humanity, any relevant rolls are made with the user’s Intelligence + the highest between their Crafts and Science skills. Whenever Rank is relevant, the user is considered to have [Spirit]. Should a Clash of Wills happen, the user scores [Spirit] automatic successes.

    Creating a Merveille is no easy task and requires an Essence battery as a base material. The Anima Meccanica then rolls Gnosis+Spirit as an extended action to create it via technognostic engineering, with a target number of successes equal to its Essence capacity. Each rolls represents an hour of work.

    Once complete, whenever a spirit or Angel is brought low (defeated, humiliated, etc), a holder within sensory range can activate the device to permanently take any Influence or Numina they have. A Merveille can contain up to its Essence capacity in Numina or Influence dots. It may take a single one from each target at a time, provided it has enough space to contain it, and cannot override existing ones. The target loses the use of these abilities permanently, but can recover them by destroying the Merveille (although they do not instinctively know this). Merveille can only take abilities from targets they are Resonant with and destroying them does not mean previous users lose their abilities.

    A Merveille can also be activated to permanently remove a Resonant ability they have granted from a user within sensory range, which consumes an equal amount of Essence dots as the ability’s rating. This is normally employed to neutralize those that abuse the Artificer’s works. However, nothing stops the target from reacquiring these abilities.

    In addition, the Maecenas' near transcendental understanding of integrating the supernatural with technology now further elevates their rhetorical acumen, extending the benefit of the main Attainment to any mechanical beings, including cyborgs, Angels and Demons.

      What were you thinking design wise ?

      The first Attainment was inspired by the JARVIS AI from the Iron Man movies. It creates a better interface between human and machine, a Universal Spiritual Bus, via a mix of Unveiling the instinctual wants of the user to the spirit of a tool and Compelling them to act upon them. This allows for a lot more creative equipment combinations, which reinforces the theme of inventors thinking outside the box.

      The first optional Attainment is a reference to Lingua Technis from the Adeptus Mechanicus, which they use to quickly send instructions to machine spirits. In this case, it was repurposed as an alternate mantra that grants sensory range to technoshamanic spells. That way it captures the feel of the ability, while the drawbacks of using this type of yantra balance it out.

      The second main Attainment builds upon the first. Since the first Attainment reduces penalties, this one greatly enhances successes. Paired together, it means the cabal will be incentivized to favor utilizing tools. The restriction seemed like a thematic way to give them a bit more fine tuned control. The idea is that the interface gives them admin access to decide when to execute the optimization programs.

      The second optional Attainment allows them to craft whatever they need on the fly. However, there was a lack of sufficient Reach to enable MacGyvering. By focusing on their theme of inventors and creators, it provided a good way to restrict the more destructive applications of this spell. The second limitation came from the Sons of Ether from Mage: The Ascension, who utterly depend on tools for their spells.

      The third main Attainment is simple, but very useful. It's more bening application is to allow the Pioneers to get out of trouble in an emergency. The more callous ones will instead use it as a simple way to program spirits to work as their agents, lab assistants or as a rudimentary way to integrate them into inventions, which would come to haunt them later. Together with the second optional Attainment, it means they can even use it from a distance, which can have all sorts of interesting applications. It also allows them to capture spirits for the fourth optional Attainment.

      The third optional Attainment is based on Plasmids and Vigors from the Bioshock series. Making the potions be reverse Claimed bypasses the need for a Prime based infusion. This allows them to make a lot of mad scientist type super serums. The activation via a crafting or science skill roll not only builds on the theme of campaigners of inventions, but it also allows non mage companions to use these powers reliably well. Combined with the fact that Attainments are free from the Lie, it opens up some interesting problem solving approaches. Making the Attainment also replicate Angel Numina was added not only because it felt thematic, but this emergent property could be explored via plot hooks.

      The reverse Manifestations incentivizes them to explore and leverage the power of the Shadow on their own terms, which can lead to all sorts of plot hooks. It also allows this Attainment to continue to be relevant once the capstone is unlocked.

      The fourth main Attainment is a magical version of how Iron Man’s nano suit is carried. It allows them to store away their power armor, mech suits, turrets, robot army and any other amount of cool gadgets. Because it has no upper limit of the number of items, it means they can sneak an entire engineering lab’s worth of tools into any situation. It also allows them to equip them quickly, giving them the flexibility to adapt to just about anything.

      It also has a lot of unorthodox applications. The rope or handcuffs binding the mage can be stored for later use. Grenades can be deposited unpinned into the target's pockets or items stolen with a casual handshake.

      The fourth optional Attainment is the original reason for the Legacy. It was inspired by Oversouls from Shaman King, which altered weapons and granted them the properties of bound spirits. The nanotech style dynamic shaping is another aspect of Iron Man’s nanosuit that was used to reinterpret it into the technoshamanic themes.

      By altering the Golem spell and adding a Spirit component, it allows them to wield living weapons that can adapt to any situation. This means they can build power armor with all sorts of independent, detachable weapons. They can even create a medicinal multitool, like the Dr Blythe ability from HunterxHunter. It also permits them to construct a robot army or work on a giant mech with all sorts of different abilities. Depending on the materials or mechanisms employed, it can make them extremely durable and efficient. It opens up a lot of room for experimentation, which was exactly the intention. By being a permanent effect, it means the Spiriti dell’Invenzione can continuously expand on their collection of creations.

      Being able to make permanent Essence batteries that cost no spell control seemed like a vital ability, since it grants them a reliable method to power these bigger devices, so the capability to craft fetishes was added in. But it received a twist to synergize with their focus on personalizing tools.

      The fifth main Attainment is a spirit focused version of the Crown of Duty. The Maecenas are likely to get into trouble whether they go around hubristically binding every spirit they want or bartering with them for their cooperation. This allows them to get out of those situations, it also reflects how certain entrepreneurs are able to pull people into their ideas by sheer charisma.

      The fifth optional Attainment is a spirit version of the Neuromods from Prey. But rather than molecularly disassembling a Typhon after it has consumed a victim, the condition is instead inspired by the Siskur-Dah from Werewolf: The Forsaken. This makes the Luminari seek out trouble by hunting spirits for new abilities. It also allows them to systematically usurp the power of the strongest spirits in a city by engineering a symbolic defeat. Once the abilities are harnessed, it gives them an invaluable magical resource, which they can further fuel with the Essence batteries from the previous optional Attainment. Because the power source is more flexible, easier to procure and the window to use the powers does not expire (unlike the third optional Attainment), it becomes easier to use them reliably. The highest expression of their art is being able to take humanity in a new evolutionary leap, similar to nanomods from Deus Ex.

      The theme of interfacing with the creations of the God Machine is expanded upon once more, incentivizing them to seek out its secrets in their quest to create new magitek marvels.

      The original idea for this Legacy was to create a revised version of the Lord of the Inanimate. Shaman King was the first thing that came to mind, allowing them to employ tools that channeled the spirit’s full power. However, because this made Matter the primary Arcanum, it made it very difficult to create Attainments that synergized with the themes.

      The solution was to change it into a Moros Legacy, making the spirit Arcanum into the primary one. Because the original theme was not very resonant with the Path’s symbolism, it was altered to be about integrating technology with spirits. When expanding upon this idea, I wanted them to focus on a branch of sci-fi that was not already covered by existing Legacies. In addition, I also sought to explore how and why Orders other than the Free Council would develop a magitek Legacy.

      Solarpunk proved a fascinating choice, with its positive attitude, cosmopolitan outlook, focus on green technologies and natural aesthetics making it awesome in a refreshingly different way than other genres of sci-fi. Being relatively new, reference materials and images proved scarce. Thankfully the plane of Kaladesh provided one of the most well realized takes on it.

      With the outline defined, the next step was to establish their background. While all Orders incorporate technology into their methods, normally only the Free Council explores the depths of its possibilities with such dedication as to make a specialized Legacy. So it would probably start out as the result of a joint venture between it and another Order, which would then become fascinated by it and spearhead its development.

      Arcology is a big component in solarpunk, but it is experimental and requires a lot of resources to build. Therefore, they would require a skilled businessman to finance the development of the needed technologies, provide the necessary resources, gather talented minds and motivate them to build a better future. This seemed like the kind of project the Silver Ladder would naturally gravitate towards. Because they were the biggest supporters of the Nameless after the Great Refusal, it provided a good reason for them to develop a Cryptopoly together.

      The reign of Frederico da Montefeltro over the city of Urbino provided a great foundation to build their organization. Known as the “Light of Italy”, he was a just ruler, one guided by humanistic principles, a patron of artists and scholars alike. His personal scriptorum contained the most complete assortment of works in Europe outside the Vatican’s collection and the intellectual spark he lighted laid the foundations for the enlightenment. These ideas of excellence through humane leadership, along with the celebration of the human mind and creative spirit synergized perfectly with the themes of solarpunk. The inventor’s fair and the current state of the city were made by adapting Kaladesh into the setting.

      The Legacy's name can be translated to "The Machine Spirit", a stealthy reference to the Adeptus Mechanicus. But it can also be interpreted as "The Mechanical Core", representing how they believe human users and their creativity are the most important components in making any technology do truly amazing things.

      The nickname Soleris is a reference to Paolo Soleri, the person who invented the term Arcology and wrote the book The Bridge Between Matter & Spirit is Matter Becoming Spirit and Arcology - City In the Image of Man. Luminari is the italian word for luminaries, which builds on their solar symbolism and role as inspirational leaders. Artificers is a reference to a similar class in D&D, whereas Mechanists harkens to a faction in Thief 2. Spiriti dell’Invenzione means “spirits of invention” in Italian and could refer either to them or to the ephemeral beings they bind using their highest Attainments.

      A few of the names of the Attainments are taken from corporate IDs from Netrunner, whose slogans captured the feel I was going for.

      Their oblations reflect how they have a mind abuzz with ideas, how they endlessly seek to bring forth one more possibility from the infinite frontier of imagination into reality. They will likely be constantly tinkering, adapting, learning, sharing their discoveries with others or seeking to foster the spark of potential wherever they see it. They would likely do this with a mix of gusto, knowledge and artistry.

      Lastly, their yantras incentivize constant experimenting. The more they create or seek out exotic ingredients, the more powerful they become. Keeping things fresh by constantly bringing new gadgets into play is especially rewarded. Building ever more ambitious projects and successfully diffusing them can give them stronger advantages, especially if they figure out how to enhance them by using spirits.

      And that’s it, hope you had as much fun reading it as I had writing it. Thanks in advance for the feedback.
