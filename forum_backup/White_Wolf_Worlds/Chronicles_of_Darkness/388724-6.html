    We're going to 5500 BCE. We're going to the Vinča people in Southeastern Europe - the first people to invent writing, which they only used for magical purposes (hiiiiiiigh speeeeeeech), the first people to invent metallurgy, a culture that lasted near-on 2000 years and influenced everything from the megalith builders in the West, the Minoans to the South and the Sumerians in the East. A people whose principle inter-town trade was in ritual paraphernalia.

    7000 years plus change ago. The World is still Fallen from the Time Before Time, but this is an ambitious setting, and I'm really glad people voted for it with such fervor.

    Before the Vampires were anything other than Strix-possessed corpses.
    Before the shan'iatu built Irem
    Before the Sundering.
    There was something else.
    THis is daring.

    I like it.
