                                        1. I like the idea of no lasting damage... most of the time. I'm expecting that Kuruth will fix that right up, though, as the other guy runs out of time and is quickly eviscerated / freaks out and Kuruth's as well (but what happens then??)

                                        2. I REALLY want to know what Kuruth will do! I feel like it would really matter to this discussion.

                                        3. I'm looking forward to your white-rooming, PenDragon. I'm hoping that it will at least be possible for someone to be grievously wounded some of the time, even if it's a rare and tragic accident.

                                        4. Good point, Ray White, about it looking like Gauru will NOT pass out when they fill up on Lethal (since give them a few seconds and it will all be gone.) That means no more "take a big hit, lose consciousness, die when your wounds wrap around" anymore (or at least not if they're in Gauru.) Interesting.

                                        5. It certainly does seem like more damage output may be in order. I understand that Gifts can bump it up but to me, tearing someone in two in Gauru form shouldn't be the province of Gifts.

                                        6. Lastly, back on Kuruth (also, KURUTH! We needs it!), it certainly does seem like you can't just try to hold a raging packmate down and try to knock him out / wait it out, anymore. This is interesting, and I'm not sure how I feel about it. On one hand, it does feel very anticlimactic ("oh, Bob's gone mad again; just sit on him for a few minutes and he'll be right as rain"), but on the other hand - one bad roll and AN UNSTOPPABLE, UNKILLABLE SOAK-MONSTER will be unleashed. Which leads me onto...

                                        7. Kuruth! Again. For good measure. Please vote Kuruth, people!
