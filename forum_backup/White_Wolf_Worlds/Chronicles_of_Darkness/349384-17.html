            Originally posted by Mage Society spoiler
            A solitary is a mage who does not belong to any cabal or Order. Some hail from remote regions with few or no other resident Awakened or haven’t attracted the attention of an Order yet. Most of these join an Order once the opportunity presents itself.

            An apostate rejected membership in all the Orders for whatever reason. This is a daunting prospect when even casual membership in an Order grants access to so many resources – thousands of years’ worth of accumulated knowledge, grimoires, rotes, Legacies, artifacts, Imbued items, and secrets. Some apostates were once part of an Order and left (voluntarily or not), taking some its secrets with them. These latter apostates are distrusted by all the other Orders, who see them potential traitors or enemy spies.
            Solitary is not mage without Cabal - it's a Awakened that was "not touched" by Orders culture.

                                I tried making character sheets for SCP-076-2 and SCP-682, but I messed them up. Here's my second attempt.

                                Able
                                Virtue: Valorous
                                Vice: Furious

                                Aspirations: Fight Worthy Opponents, Engage In Wanton Violence, Find SCP-073

                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 7, Resolve 10
                                Physical Attributes: Strength 10, Dexterity 7, Stamina 10
                                Social Attributes: Presence 5, Manipulation 1, Composure 5

                                Skills: Academics (Ancient Warfare) 4, Crafts (Blacksmithing) 3, Investigation 2, Medicine 2, Occult 4, Science (Metallurgy) 3, Athletics 5, Brawl 5, Survival 3, Weaponry (Great Blades) 6, Animal Ken (Livestock) 2, Empathy 1, Expression 2, Intimidation (Violence) 5, Persuasion 1, Subterfuge 2

                                Merits: Area Of Expertise 1 (Ancient Warfare), Eidetic Memory 2, Encyclopedic Knowledge 2 (Academics, Occult), Indomitable (Epic), Interdisciplinary Specialty 1 (Ancient Warfare), Language (Many), Ambidextrous 3, Demolisher 3, Iron Stamina 3, Parkour 4, Air Of Menace 2, Closed Book 4, Iron Will 2, Heavy Weapons 5, Iron Skin 2 (Advanced 2), Martial Arts 5, Relentless Assault 5

                                Health: 16 (Size 6)
                                Willpower: 25
                                Defense: 13
                                Initiative: 12
                                Speed: 35 (Species Factor 18)
                                Armor: 5/5
                                Potency: 10
                                Dread Powers: Armored Hide 3, Fabricate (Obsidian Weapons)*, Hideous Strength*, Immortal, Monstrous Resilience, Reality Stutter, Regenerate 3, Unbreakable

                                *Fabricate: The creature can conjure objects from nowhere. By spending a point of Willpower and making a roll of an appropriate dice pool (minus the highest of the objects Availability, Size, or Weapon Bonus), the creature can produce equipment, items or weapons from thin air. The object is considered a typical item of its type and provides its standard equipment or weapon bonuses if applicable. Fabricated objects last until the end of the scene, at which point they vanish. Objects created with Fabricate typically have “flaw” (the objects might be fragile, deal less damage etc.)

                                *Hideous Strength: Able’s might is truly formidable; by spending a point of Willpower, he can increase his Strength by his Potency until the end of the scene. Furthermore, he adds his Potency as automatic successes on Strength + Stamina rolls made to perform a feat of strength, such as lifting a massive object or breaking free of restraints.


                                The Hard To Destroy Reptile
                                Virtue: Adaptive
                                Vice: Hateful
                                Aspirations: Escape Containment, Lash Out Violently At All Life Forms, Survive Any Attempt At Killing It

                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 6, Wits 8, Resolve 8

                                Merits: Area Of Expertise (Escaping), Common Sense, Danger Sense (Advanced), Direction Sense, Indomitable (Epic), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Escaping), Eidetic Memory, Trained Observer 3, Demolisher 3, Double Jointed (Advanced), Iron Stamina 3, Air Of Menace, Closed Book 5, Iron Will, Iron Skin 2 (Advanced 2), Killer Instinct 3 (Advanced 2), Relentless Assault 3

                                Willpower: 24
                                Initiative: 12

                                    I might as well post this one too. This version of Ukulele/Alto Clef is from this Foundation Tale.

                                    Agent Ukulele
                                    Virtue: Destructive
                                    Vice: Mischievous

                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 8, Resolve 10
                                    Physical Attributes: Strength 9, Dexterity 7, Stamina 10
                                    Social Attributes: Presence 6, Manipulation 4, Composure 5

                                    Skills: Academics 1, Crafts (Explosives) 3, Investigation 2, Medicine 1, Occult 2, Athletics 7, Brawl 4, Drive 2, Firearms 7, Larceny 3, Stealth 2, Weaponry (Cartoonish) 7, Expression (Humor) 3, Intimidation (Batshit Crazy) 4, Persuasion (Taunting) 3, Subterfuge (Lethal Pranks) 4

                                    Merits: Area Of Expertise (Cartoonish), Danger Sense (Advanced), Indomitable (Epic), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Cartoonish), Double Jointed (Advanced), Parkour 4, Iron Will, Table Turner, Heavy Weapons 4, Iron Skin 2 (Advanced 2), Killer Instinct 3 (Advanced 3), Relentless Assault 3

                                    Health: 10 (Size 5)
                                    Willpower: 25
                                    Defense: 14
                                    Initiative: 12
                                    Speed: 50 (Species Factor 34)
                                    Armor: 5/5
                                    Potency: 10
                                    Ban: Must obey the mission given to it by the one who holds the device that activates its transformation.

                                    Dread Powers: Armored Hide 3, Fabricate* (Cartoonish Weapons), Influence (Destruction 5), Monstrous Resilience, Numen (Seek, Teleportation*), Surprise Entrance, Unbreakable, Uncanny Regeneration*

                                    *Fabricate: The creature can conjure objects from nowhere. By spending a point of Willpower and making a roll of an appropriate dice pool (minus the highest of the objects Availability, Size, or Weapon Bonus), the creature can produce equipment, items or weapons from thin air. The object is considered a typical item of its type and provides its standard equipment or weapon bonuses if applicable. Fabricated objects last until the end of the scene, at which point they vanish. Objects created with Fabricate typically have “flaw” (the objects might be fragile, deal less damage etc.)

                                    *Teleportation: When the entity moves, it may reflexively spend 1 Essence to vanish and reappear at another location within its line of sight up to its (Rank x Speed) in yards away. Doing so adds +2 to its Defense for the turn. If the entity has the Seek Numen, it may teleport to the nearest location with a suitable Condition by spending 3 Essence. The entity can spend an additional 2 points of Essence to take a passenger with them (an unwilling passage may resist with a roll of Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance).

                                    *Uncanny Regeneration: Ukulele adds its Potency to his Health, and heals all bashing and lethal damage at the start of each turn. He downgrades a number of aggravated damage from mundande and supernatural sources euqal to Stamina into lethal.
