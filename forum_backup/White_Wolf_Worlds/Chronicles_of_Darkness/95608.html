    Ghost fighting and other advice

    03-21-2014, 10:30 AM
    I am currently creating a one-shot game for Geist based on the events in Russia and Ukraine. I am still new to creating these adventures so I would like some help creating it. The event I will be running it at takes place in Raleigh NC so if you are attending events there and are interested stop reading now to avoid spoilers

    The main advice I need is on ghost battles, how do they work? I can't seem to find anything in the corebook but I may just be overlooking it. I also need advice on enemies. I plan to make pre-gens with 70 exp points so how can I make enemies that are tough but won't cause my players to die? Dieing like swooping is bad. (Cookie to whoever gets the reference)

    I will also attach my current outline so you can see how the game starts and offer advice.

    Thanks in advance.

    OUTLINE:

    ACT I
    The group awakens in the ruins of a plane it is very cold and snow is falling all around them. They all have a vivid vision of the person who died in their place a wits + politics roll will reveal that their replacements died at the hands of a Russian soldier.
    If the group decides to search the plane’s ruins they will have to make a wits + investigation roll to look for information and wits + survival to look for supplies.

    Information:
    1 success, they find several bodies with Latvian passports revealing that they likely crashed in Latvia.
    2 successes they find a newspaper article on tensions between Latvia and Rusia over dig rights at a recently found ruin.
    3+ successes they find all of the above plus an iphone turned to an internet page announcing an all-out invasion of Latvia by Russian troops.
    Exceptional success: they find a dying pilot who tells them the plane was shot down by a missile.
    Survival

    1 success: they find a large blanket that can be used to keep warm while resting

    2 successes: they find a large map including their flight path so they can approximate their location
    3+ all of the above plus a gps system that gives them their exact location
    Exceptional success: they find a survival kit including a flare gun, a .22 rifle and large survival knife.
    They can try as long as they want to make these checks but must deal with exposure checks every 2 turns and after 5 turns soldiers will set upon them.
    Soldier encounter:
    A wits + politics (1 success) roll will let them realize that the soilders are not Russian but are actually some type of Latvian militia. An exceptional success will reveal that they are a pro-democracy miltia and woul be very friendly toward Americans.

    Wits + empathy (2 successes) can tell that they are there to investigate the crash and aren’t hostile… yet. An exceptional success will reveal that they will help if asked to.
    Wits + investigation (3 successes) will reveal that none of them have the required equipment to shoot down a plane. An exceptional success will reveal that they are not well trained with weapons either.
    If one of the two pregens with Russian language is used they will be able to talk with them, otherwise it will take3 successes on a wits + socialize check to communicate to them.
    Even if they fail their communication check they will receive an option to follow the militia they must either follow or fight them.
    Fight: two of the three soldiers carries an AK-47, one a shotgun if the party wins they must navigate the rest of the way through the forest to a town.
    The Town
    The group finds its way to a small town or is lead there by the militia. A graveyard is prominent in the town and several tombstones are marked. A wits + occult roll will let them read it on the fly or intelligence + occult if they go up and look closely. The markings warn that sin eaters in the area are being hunted. If asked a local will tell them the marking went up soon after the coup that deposed Putin. The group can attempt to leave a message on the tomstones with an Int + occult roll.

    By now it is late at night and they need to find a place to sleep, unfortuently there is no place so they will have to sleep in a drainage pipe for the night and make resolve + stamina checks to avoid 2 points of bashing damage and -1 penalties to all rolls. If they succeded in the roll to leave a twilight network message they will not have to stay there long enough to gain the penalties.
    While sleeping they are roused by the sound of someone approaching. The man who approaches is a Sin Eater as is obvious by his frog like Geist.

    (there was a coup and Putin has been replaced by a General who is being advised and ultimately controlled by a vengeful ghost perhaps Rasputin the general is killing off sin eaters
