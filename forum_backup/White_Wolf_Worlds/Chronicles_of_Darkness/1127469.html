                  MY TIME HAS COME.

                  In all seriousness, I have been making it a point to copy down and record as many fan organizations as I could since the old forums went down. Most of the organizations were put into a single Word document but I extracted them into smaller documents. I tried to format them based on the general structure that the official organization write-ups have had, with notes on what is currently missing from the fanmade write-up in comparison.

                  As of right now, this Drive contains what I could be bothered to archive. Organizations that had a relatively small write-up (missing substantial sections to the point that the write-up is more of a concept) were omitted. Organizations that were published in a pdf are unlisted; instead their respective pdf has been included in the Drive. Most of LostLight's organizations were omitted due to the SHEER NUMBER OF PUBLISHED WORKS made it difficult for me to keep track. Insofar, the only organizations that are officially MISSING are Compacts and Conspiracies created on Google Groups.
