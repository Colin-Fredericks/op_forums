    What if KotE was in Chronicle of Darkness

    02-14-2024, 09:56 AM
    So, KotE originally start off as VtM asia supplement, but it's turn into it's own gameline, but what if KotE was in VtR? VtR have other Wicked Undead creature, so why not have Kuei-Jin be one of them? And in this case 'Kindred of the East' refer to them being like the Kindred, but they're mainly found in the east, and not literally being Kindred just in the East. Just rework that they can be found anywhere in the world, but they have the most influence in Asia, and not just East Asia, and India, their Clan are divergent evolution instead of convergent, and each of the 'Covenant' are based on The Dharma, but less oriental, and have the 1,000 Hell be part of the Underworld that connect to Inferno, or something.

                                      Oh, you misunderstood, the Endless examples in Mummy core are:

                                      Blood bathers - who gain their immortality from bathing in the blood of their victims
                                      Body thieves - who have some mechanism to jump to a new (younger) body
                                      The Reborn - who are just reincarnations, keeping some memories intact, may require some ritual or quest to acquire their full memories.
                                      And the Eternals - who are pretty much Dorian Gray style immortals, anchored to a specific object.

                                      Then there are patchwork people, the Purified, and maybe one more.

                                      Thematically, they are all linked to Mummy only in that they make great NPCs as either antagonist/ally - because Mummy's relationship with memory is odd, and having someone in the party who can exist across time periods is useful (even if they have a new body).

                                      But mechanically they are a broad category of immortals which are mostly distinct from Kindred from not being undead (which would not be an issue for me).

                                      The question is socially and politically, how do they interact with Kindred society. What would other kindred consider acceptable, do you need a clan? (Not being able to embrace is one of the features of KotE) Would they be able to join a covenant? (KotE only had their Path like Dharmas, iirc, which might map to distinct covenants)?

                                      What else defines Kindred? The Banes? Their Cultue/social position (ie members of a Coteries/city-wide organisation)?

                                      Like I said, thematically I would go with themes of suffering, finding your place in the world, and trauma (from Lost) along with themes of second chances, and knowledge of the afterlife (from Geist), and not make a game which is thematically similar to Requiem.

                                      Also, not sure which Lower Depth to associate them with. Dis, the Inferno and Duat are the three I know anything about, I would not link them to Arcadia - there is already a whole game for that.

                                      But yeah, having a splatt (or even starting with a mini-template based on The Endless) and then asking where does fit in your game.

                                      How would the local vampire community interact with them? Would they join/form a Geist Krewe? Would they find safety within a changeling court? (Building powers based on Demonic contracts?) Would the local Mummy faction like another type of immortal pro/an-tagonist?

                                      In theory, with their shape-shifting power a KotE could join a Forsaken Pack. Learn Pack Rites, and develop shamanistic magic (hoping that binding/abjuring and understanding summoning magic can keep any Demons away).

                                      Edit: I guess under motivation, what I have is, what makes this concept different from Kindred (and the wicked dead/Revenants) because that is always that interested my about KotE.

                                      Making all different monsters of the night just be kindred is actually really boring to me - though I see why they went with "not all kindred" descend from one true source of power, as a way to make it distinct from VtM.
