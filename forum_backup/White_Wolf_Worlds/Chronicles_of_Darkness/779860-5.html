                            I would say that the Opuses (the What It Is part)would be fairly literal in their interpretation but they would be rather expansive in what they can do. With the Polaria Sirens that have "Darkness", "Death", and "Freeze" as Opuses, here's what is written up by SaulottheGentle:

                            Death, the word that connects to dead bodies and wandering ghosts with equal power.
                            Darkness, the ability to manipulate shadows that can equally conceal as much as they can illuminate, depending on whether they’re covering up with darkness or removing the shadows so light shines.
                            Freeze, the word that stops progress as much as it turns water to ice.

                            I would presume that "Death", while it refers to dead bodies and ghosts, would not be able to suddenly strike people dead nor would it work on things that are more based on symbolism, like a plastic skeleton that hangs in biology labs or a depiction of the grim reaper. With the Conclusions and Introductions, the "Death" Opus might be able to remove dead flesh or necrosis from a Siren or it could possibly allow the Siren to feign death or summon an Anchor (not literally) for ghosts and spirits to manifest around. With Light, which encompasses Sunlight, Fire, and Lightning and possibly light in general, it may be able to be used as a sudden burst of light or fire that may be able to cauterize wounds but wouldn't necessarily heal. With clever use, a Siren may be able to create a Verse that allows the Light to be used as a laser and partake in eye surgery, but that would require a very specific set of skills to use.

                            Given that there are six types of Waters that Sirens belong to and assuming that the Opuses are not crossed over, that would make a set of 18 unique Opuses with the possibility of more. If the Waters have some sort of cross-over with other Waters, that would mean that there are 8 shared Opuses, three each. I believe we are probably going for 18 unique Opuses and some along with the Conclusions, which could make upwards of 90 combinations of unique Verses, not including Introductions. Of course, because things are based on an interpretation-based system, that may mean that things will be less set-in-stone and more up-in-the-air.
