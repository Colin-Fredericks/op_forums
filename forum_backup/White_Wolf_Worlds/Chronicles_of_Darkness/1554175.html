    Ideas for helping players flesh out their characters

    10-05-2025, 06:14 PM
    Hi,

    I am currently in the middle of figuring out a little horror themed mage 2e game. I want to help my players to flesh out their characters and meaby help them get a better feel of them. I remember in the old wod in character creation there were something like 7 questions to ask your players to better understand their character concept. I know that there is not many of us playing mage aw 2ed. Meaby some of you have an idea what those questions I could ask my players should be.
    One of the questions probably should be. Hey, how did you awaken?


    Love ya. Thanks for help. Looking forward to your ideas

      What do you want to do with the game? Like, I'd personally go for asking about the mage's personal relationships, but if the game is going to be fairly self-contained that's not necessarily very useful.

      Maybe what their relationship to their Order is like? (Like, are you a proper ideologue of your Order, or are you on the fringes?)

      Frankly, 'How did you awaken?' is not that useful in my experience. I mean, it's not a bad idea for fleshing a character out, but it also doesn't necessarily tell you all that much.

          Offhand:

          1. Whether you have the Mentor Merit or not, who trained your character as an apprentice? Do they have any other students, whether from the same cohort as you or as your seniors or juniors in initiation into the Mysteries? What Legacy do they belong to, if any? Do they hold any positions of note in the Consilium or Caucus? (If they didn't have a singular mentor or are otherwise outside of the default framework of being a trained member of the Pentacle, you can split some of these questions up by notable teachers or other resources, whether that's learning their Inferior Arcanum from the same Nameless Order or having a rival who learned a Rote from the same Grimoire.)

          2. What Mysteries hold your attention? What precipitated your Awakening, and is that phenomenon still at large? Was your Awakening itself unusual in some way (whether in being the vector for your acquisition of an Artifact, Destiny, Familiar, or other Merit, one of the grander metamorphic Awakenings described in Signs of Sorcery, or something subtler not represented with game mechanics)? Has your Long-Term Nimbus brought any Path-resonant phenomena into confluence with your magical identity? Do you expect to keep your Obsession focused primarily on one type of phenomenon or move on to a different Mystery once you've learned all you wanted to learn from it?

          3. What makes you lose yourself? Regardless of whether you plan to make custom Acts of Hubris, what topics and contexts make you likely to act unWisely? Are you thorough in the separation of your Shadow Name from your mundane life? Who do you wish you could talk about the Mysteries with but can't because of the Sleeping Curse? Who can't you stand despite (or because of) their perhaps understanding you better than anyone else when it comes to your Obsessions? Are you often tempted to solve problems with magic when you think nobody is watching? How careful are you in general, and what toll has this level of attentiveness taken on you

              Here are some questions that might be helpful. The answers are meant to be provided alongside with an explanation, so that they not only help flesh out your character, but so you can also provide the Storyteller with them, which will help them in creating challenges for your character.

              Mortal questions:
              1- What is your earliest memory ?
              2- What is your favorite food ?
              3- What is your hobby ?
              4- What are your phobias or greatest fears?
              5- What is a traumatic memory that you have ?
              6- What is your fondest memory ?
              7- Who are the most important people in your life and what is your relationship with them ?
              8- What did you often daydream about pre-chronicle ?
              9- What is your spirit animal ?
              10- What is your favorite music ?

              Mage questions:
              1- How did you Awaken and who found you after it happened ?
              2- Who mentored you ? What kind of master were they ? Did you have fellow pupils ? What is your relationship to all of the above ?
              3- Why did you choose your Shadow Name ? What does it symbolize and what does it mean to you ?
              4- Which Great Rights do you recognize, which you do not ?
              5- What is your favorite and least favorite Arcanum ?
              6- What kind of Mysteries draw you ?
              7- Why did you choose your Order ?
              8- What do you think of the other Orders ?
              9- How often do you use your magic and how careful are you with it ?
              10- If you could Awaken one person, right now, who would it be ?
              Last edited by KaiserAfini; 10-05-2025, 08:13 PM.

              New experiences are the font of creativity, when seeking inspiration, break your routine.
