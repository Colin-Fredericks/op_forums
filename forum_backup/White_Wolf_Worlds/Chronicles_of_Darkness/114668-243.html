                            Originally posted by Poseur View Post
                            Can you set conditional triggers on spells, how so? And on ongoing spells? Like if i want to target for say werewolves with an ongoing spell with advanced duration that gives them agg damage each ritual interval as long as they are in the spells area. How to do that ? I dont want to hit humans, ghouls, changelings, spirits or anything else, just werewolves in this instance.

                            Like conditional duration only ends spells when the condition is met. But i want a spell that "activates" when the condition is met.
                            Conditional Duration (Fate 2 Attainment) + Hung Spell (Time 2 spell) is the standard method for creating triggered effects, but it sounds more like you want an ongoing effect that only affects certain targets.

                            Warding Gesture (Fate 2 spell) is required to selectively exclude subjects from an AoE spell. Afaik including/excluding certain kinds of targets isn't a standard clause, but it seems like something that should be possible as a Creative Thaumaturgy effect. My solution would be to just add a Reach to an AoE Warding Gesture covering the same ground as the damage spell, and in the specific case of werewolves require adding Spirit 1 so the spell can actually tell if something is or is not a werewolf.
