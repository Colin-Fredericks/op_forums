    Oneiromachy 2e

    02-29-2016, 05:58 PM
    So I have few questions about Oneiromachy, I understand if some can't be answered.

    First off can you mix related dreamscaping actions, like could you make a dream that creates a false memory and use that as the first of the 3 dreams needed to make it reoccuring? Or create a healing dream that teaches medicine, giving the informed condition to a medicine roll? Create a nightmare that is reoccurring?

    Secondly can you do dream combat in a dreamscaped dream, like you want to enhance the horror of a nightmare you created, so you make an attack roll against the dreamer. Or you created a healing or teaching dream, and someone invades it so you kick their arse?

    If a Baku and Succubus end up fighting over a dream, who wins the fight? If the Succubus loses does she survive as long as its not a too solid dream?

    Can you boost or scour the intensity of a dream vessel? Do you have to be in the dream to do so or can it be done by making or simply holding dream vessel.

    So hedge spinning appears to be the way to make a cobble thought bauble permanant, but could their be otherways, like using the animate device contract on it and making it a retainer via the retainer merit on an exceptional success? Or by intergrating it as a luxury in your hallow with the hallow luxury merit?

    And somone else pointed out that socialize didn't make sense for Convince the dreamer dreamriding, but wouldn't make most sense to use the skill most relevant to the attempt to convince the dreamer. Like persuasion roll to convince someone your goal is the most rationally or to wear someone down, a subltefuge roll to convince some with lies, an expression roll to move someone emotionally, a socialize roll to connect your reasoning to their place in society and the expectations proper conduct within said group, a medicine roll to convince someone they're sick, an intimidation roll threaten them etc... kind of like a normal roll to open someones doors?

              I'd personally say yes to mixing dreamscaping, as long as it's reasonable. I think that you could create a false memory of a dream being recuring but it wouldn't ACTUALLY be recuring on a mechanical level until it actually is. If that makes sense.

              Yes to combat in a dreamscaped dream. Why not after all?

              A quick glance at a Baku vs. Succubus showdown seems pretty close. That poor mortal. In the short term if the Baku wins and kills the dream's intensity I'd probably say that the succubus would come back later. Succubi only exist in dreams and don't go into the Hedge (That we know of! Maybe they look completely different in the hedge!) so they can clearly survive the dissolution of dreams and still wind up in the dreamer's dreamscape the next night too. Because of this, I think a succubus would win in the long term. A Baku would have to track down that specific dream bastion for Round 2. And a succubus has 11 dice for environmental attacks so even with the lower defense, that's where my money would go.

              Pre-use of the Vessel, my instinct here says no. Intensity is already controlled by the Dreamweaving (+Empathy) roll. You want it higher? Make it an extended roll.

              Making baubles permanent beyond hedgespinning... animate device? I'd personally say no. Just because an exceptional would make it permanently animated doesn't provide for permanent existence. The exceptional animation would just last as long as the bauble does. As part of a hollow, maybe? From an IC perspective that'd be hedgespinning it into something covered by the luxury merit and oocly just skipping buying the hedgespun merit. I don't think you can just bypass the essential nature of a bauble being a slowly fading dream. If you've got a bauble you're very attached to then maybe a changeling could bring it back into a dream with them and then roll to pull it out again?

              Agreed on Convince the Dreamer
