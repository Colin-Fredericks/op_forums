            I like the new Freshwater Alterations, but I sort of need to note something on the Waterwalking one. When I noted that Freshwater Sirens could walk on land, what I meant was that instead of having the stereotypical Mermaid tail, they would be able to have legs and stuff instead. I was also grasping straws abit on the Freshwater Sirens, so if anyone has suggestions for what I could write to specify their alterations, feel free to suggest. I was thinking of using marine animal appearances that fit the areas. Freshwater Alterations therefore would be draw from Freshwater animals.

            I think we also need to make rules on what the basic Aquatic form is without Alterations.

            +Can breathe underwater indefinitely.
            +9 Again to Athletics rolls in water
            +Speed is quadrupled when swimming
            +Gives access to all Alterations the Siren owns

            -Can only breathe above water for a number of turns equal to her Stamina, after which she must roll her Stamina. Each suxx gives 30 extra seconds for how long she can be without breathing underwater. If she stays up past that, then she begins drowning in air as if anyone else has begun drowning in water.
            -On land, she only has her mermaid tail and thus no legs. She can only move up to her Strength in speed, suffering a form of a permanent, if specialized Knocked Down Tilt when on land. (mitigated by the Freshwater Alteration that gives legs instead of a mermaid tail).

            If I may suggest as an idea for both two General Alterations and a Waterwalking one.

            General Alterations: Octopus Tentacles - Instead of forming a fish tail, the Siren's lower half splits into eight tentacles complete with suckers. Her tentacles give +2 to all Dexterity rolls involving the use of her tentacles, in which includes use of the suckers on them. The suckers along the tentacles also give the Siren 9 Again on Brawl rolls.
            Inspiration: Octopus

            Starfish Regeneration - The Siren's regeneration factor goes past the ability of normal Sirens. When a Siren loses a limb and it doesn't return to her body, instead of taking a negative condition representing her new found disability mark the damage that severed the limb as Resistant damage. Over the course of healing, the limb slowly but surely regrows until it is completely anew.
            Inspiration: Starfish

            Freshwater Alteration: Amphibious Legs - Instead of having a fish tail, the Siren has two somewhat-human legs instead. She may move on land without any penalties unlike the normal Aquatic form.
            Inspiration: Frogs

                            This project is still a WIP and thus heavily subject to change. :3 They could end up in the Tropicana Alterations later, I though that Octopi lived in all water climates though? If not, then that can be saved for Tropicana.
                            Let's simplify the process of coming up with Alterations by stating what is intended for each Water. Obviously, fish is an obvious inspiration for Sirens in general, but we should probably pinpoint what creatures are fair game for which Water.

                            We have Polaria pretty much developed, focusing on blubbery animals and whales. Basically, creatures that are iconic with the arctic circles. Does that extend to penguins, who are aquatic creatures that are commonly associated with Antarctica? Is it only for a set type of creature, based on their dependency on water? Does the basis of what we use for Alterations stop at birds but continue into insects, mammals, and reptiles along with fish?

                            Freshwater is definitely based on creatures that live in rivers, lakes, and ponds, but it also has a slight focus on amphibians like frogs, salamanders, and axolotls. It also has a basis in being able to go between land and water. This dichotomy might also lend itself to insects that start off in water when they are larvae, like mayflies, dragonflies, and other insects that transition from one way of life to another. There's also the inspiration of Duck for the Amphibious Legs Alteration, so it may work towards that idea.

                            With that, there are the Vaporians who are based on bays and temperate coasts. I suggested flying fish as a basis for the mermaid tail. Surprisingly enough, Vaporians could also extend to creatures that live in deserts and gain water by collecting condensation from fog, like beetles, scorpions, snakes, and the desert rain frog.

                            In contrast, I presume that Tropicans are found in tropical coasts and waters, leaning more towards coral reefs and warm open waters. Dolphins, sponges, jellyfish, and even sea turtles sound like they would be safe game.

                            Gardenians would be swamps, bayous, marshes, oases, generally areas that are rich with soil and plant-life. Hippos, crayfish, crawdads, crocodiles, caiman, beavers, anacondas, creatures that are associated with the untamed wild seem to be the motif for this Water.

                            As for Discordians, I'm at a loss. They're based on pollution and taint, but I can't really think of a marine animal that relates to pollution. As a cop-out, what if Discordians were actually the winged Sirens of Greek mythology? It would play in by having Discordians breaking the motif by being aerial and allow us to incorporate bird-Sirens.
