    Gateway Lair Traits

    10-31-2017, 03:10 PM
    For my own reference and hopefully others', I've decided to compile a list of 'gateway' Lair Traits, the ones that are fairly easy to re-create by mundane means or Beast powers in order to impose the others on a location or open a pathway, regardless of the location's original features. Don't waste time and effort searching for locations resonant with your Lair, create them. Especially useful for nomadic Beasts and Broods or when a character or group is away from any known location that was used to create or strongly resembles any of their Chambers.
    Blazing Light: fireworks or portable spotlight
    Blizzard: Storm-Lashed
    Burning: fires, Dragonbreath
    Crosswinds: Storm-Lashed
    Crushing: Caught in the Webs, Crushing Coils, or Vengeful Earth (Satiety expenditure)
    Darkness: cut off power, break lights, Death of Light
    Diseased: Plague Bearer
    Engulfing: Caught in the Webs, Crushing Coils, or Vengeful Earth (Satiety expenditure)
    Extreme Cold: Storm-Lashed
    Extreme Heat: fires, Dragonbreath
    Downpour: sprinklers, Storm-Lashed
    Earthquake: Vengeful Earth (low Satiety)
    Electrified: ripped power cables, Storm-Lashed
    Exposed: Weakness Exposed
    Fog: fires or smoke bombs
    Hurricane: Storm-Lashed
    Infected: Plague Bearer
    Isolated: You Are Alone
    Jagged: rubble from Cyclopean Strength or Titanic Blow
    Murmurs: sound effects, Siren's Treacherous Song
    No Exits: Illusion of Safety (low Satiety)
    Poor Light: cut off power, break lights, Death of Light
    Razored: broken glass or pieces of barbwire
    Slick: oil or other lubricants
    Stench: stink bombs or tear gas
    Stinging: pepper spray or tear gas, Infestation or Bugs Everywhere
    Swarm: vial of fruit flies, Infestation or Bugs Everywhere
    Thunderous: loud music or fireworks, Siren’s Treacherous Song or Storm-Lashed
    Toxic: Basilisk's Touch
    Unstable: bag of marbles, Titanic Blow (Satiety expenditure) or Vengeful Earth

    Any other ideas or suggestions?
