        OK, I've been pondering Miracles and how Potentia would work with them and I've been trying to think of categories which could determine how much Potentia you need to commit to a particular Miracle. I'm thinking that the length of time for which they need to be committed would be determined by the level of the Miracle and its duration while the amount would be by the actual effects.

        For the latter, I was thinking that the types of things you'd need to commit Potentia to would be: damage, range, area of effect,unnaturalness (conjuring a lightning bolt from a clear sky would be more expensive than doing it from a cloudy or stormy one) and penetration (how well you can penetrate supernatural defenses). Can anyone think of anything else? I'm not entirely sure how to model 'utility' miracles.

        On another note, do you think it would be a good idea to have demigods Potentia pools be comparatively smaller than the fuel pools of other splats, given how they have less trouble with regenerating it? It would also make the Fading a more pressing thing.

                        For the (re)creation of Icons, does this sound reasonable?

                        Forging Icons
                        If a Demigod’s Icon is destroyed, it is possible to create a new one, but it is not a process to be undertaken lightly, nor is it one without a cost. In order to create a new Icon, a demigod must go to a Temple and meditate upon what and who he is (requiring 7 successes on a Composure + Occult roll on an extended action with 1 hour per roll and a maximum number of roll equal to the demigod’s Luminance + highest Domain). Many demigods report that they experience strange, chaotic and confusing visions while in this trancelike state. These visions are often laden with abstract symbolism reflecting the demigod’s past, philosophy and Domains.
                        If the demigod achieves the required successes, he must commit Potentia equal to his Luminance for the adventure or a month, whichever comes first. A last vision will flash before the demigod’s eyes, an image of the item that has become his new Icon.
                        The Storyteller decides what this item is and it need not even belong to the demigod, although it will have significance to him and he will have encountered it.
