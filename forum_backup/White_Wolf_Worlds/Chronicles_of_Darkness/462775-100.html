                                        When directly creating a Wolf-Blooded it should be free. When a human character spontaneously becomes Wolf-Blooded during the course of the story you would represent that by buying the Merit.
                                        However, if all other characters are regular humans the ST might rule that being Wolf-Blooded should come with an XP cost (i.e. you buy the Tell Merit) to make it a bit more fair for the other player characters. But that's something between you and your ST.

                                        Edit: Another possibility is to have the character become Wolf-Blooded in the game. You and your ST could engineer a situation your character might have to roll for Lunacy, and it'll be a Dramatic Failure (even if the roll would show a success). Or come up with another way to become Wolf-Blooded. I haven't read the entire book yet so I don't know about other ways that might happen.

                                            Normally, being Wolf-Blooded is something that happens at birth or as a result of Lunacy, and does not cost experiences. The Tell Merit has Wolf-Blooded as a prerequisite, and is primarily for giving characters who are already Wolf-Blooded a second Tell. However, at Storyteller discretion, a human character might be able to take the Tell Merit to spontaneously become Wolf-Blooded.
