    Page 76: Psychokinetic Combat 3, Trapped Potential, doesn't specify a size limit on the objects you can detonate. The text pretty clearly implies that it's meant for size 0-3, given that the examples are a lighter or a welding torch, the damage is limited to three bashing, and the resulting Force is Size 3, but I could see someone trying to use it on a truck, which seems beyond the scope of intent but within the rule as currently written. (Or more humorously: "I blow up... THE EARTH")

    Also age 76: Sojourner is a neat upgrade to apportation, but while I assume it's intended to remove the drawback it doesn't say so. Thus, teleporting a person one mile would deal 1760 bashing, making it an extremely potent killing power but useless for its (presumably) intended function.

                Page 52: The rules for feats of strength which were in the draft of Strength Performance are absent. This makes the third maneuver "Push/Pull" impossible for the Storyteller to judge in play, and creates issues for the second and fourth as well.

                Page 135: The paragraph on explosives' Blast Area has two different descriptions of the trait - the second description starts at "This trait is listed in three values ...", and is clearly wrong, since the grenade table lists only one number under Blast Area. The last two sentences of that paragraph should be cut.

                Please clarify if "add the explosive’s Damage rating in automatic successes for additional damage" under Force refers to the automatic damage described under Damage, or is added to that damage. (That is, the formula is either Damage + successes on Force roll, or Damage + (successes on Force roll + Damage, if successes > 0), but I don't know which.)

                Does "Mundane armor only counts as half its ballistic rating when protecting against explosions" mean that general armor doesn't reduce explosive damage at all? And does supernatural armor apply against explosive damage at its full rating by default?
