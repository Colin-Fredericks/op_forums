                                        Originally posted by Raistlin View Post

                                                    Originally posted by Apex Predator View Post
                                                    Is there a reason why they can't get corrupted without a supernatural doing the corrupting?

                                                    If the theme is hunters are just as bad then there's no need for a supernatural creature to be pulling strings behind the scenes.
                                                    I mean, VASCU, the Ascending Ones, and the Merrick Institute explicitly don't have monsters at the top, and AKD is a bit of "Oh really?" sort of read(vaguely surprised no one called me out on it yet).

                                                    EDIT: Also worth noting the relationships of power here make it more mitigated than a lot of people make it out to be. For example, Valkyrie is funded by Vampires, not directed by them, which means that it's still the American Government directing super-soldiers against monsters with just a few cases of "Oh by the way, we also have this odd job". The Feast of Shulpae, while probably the result of Shuankhsen efforts, also realistically can't be led by them for the same reason the cannibal cult is a danger to the Arisen**. Les Mysteres gives the main credence of voice to the spirits but nothing stops individual cults in the confederacy from treating it more like an equal partnership. Whatever the power source for Malleus, it seems benign enough to just be there, and while Baudolino is definitely a monster running the show, he's also...human-enough, perhaps more so than Gallagher.

                                                    Point is that there's a spectrum to these things, and there's a lot more nuance in the portrayal than just "Conspiracies are monster-led organizations hunting monsters with humanity as pawns."

                                                    And the theme is not that hunters are just as bad*(or good!), but that power has it's costs, change is impossible without power, and desperation opens up doors reason leaves closed. Conspiracies are just the logical conclusion.

                                                    *That said, Hunters are one of the only two monsters that make a choice of being what they are, and the acceptance of one's self that most Beast's reach in their actualization definitely isn't as shady as the pscyhological steeling that comes with the decision that something has to be done about these not-human strangers-that-look-like-me.

                                                    **Yes, it's been clarified that the Feast of Shulpae can't actually do their thing against the Deathless and Lifeless, making a point here, moving on.
