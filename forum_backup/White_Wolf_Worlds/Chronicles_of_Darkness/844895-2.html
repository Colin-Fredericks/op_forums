                                                How about a 3D collect-a-thon platformer, like Crash Bandicoot, Spyro The Dragon, Psychonauts and Banjo & Kazooie?

                                                I'm thinking the protagonist would be a Hero, and the levels would be Lairs you have to maneuver through while avoiding traps and dispatching enemies carefully.

                                                The bosses would be Beasts. To vary things and shed some light into Hero's delusion of grandeur, you'd have levels with a different type of gameplay, and a supernatural other than a Beast as the boss.

                                                The more of these side bosses you defeat, the clearer it would become that the Hero is doing all of this for selfish and petty reasons. There would be hints spread through the game that the Hero will come to a grisly and ignoble end.

                                                For example: the part of the main menu where you can browse the Hero's collectibles (e.g. heads of slain Beasts, stolen items of emotional significance to other monsters, newspaper clipings "congratulating" you for "saving the day"), you would see implications that the Hero can't hold down a job and has abandoned a well-adjusted family because of the all-important Quest.

                                                There could be sidequests where you can try to get back to your friends and family, but these would be presented as boring (i.e. no collectibles or bosses, just a few branching dialogue options) on purpose, to convey how addicted to fighting monsters the Hero has become.

                                                When you get to the very end of the last level, the build-up and foreshadowing get a shocking pay-off: you stop playing as the Hero and take control of an Incarnate. You are invulnerable and terrifying to behold, and the ensuing "fight" can be ended with a few button presses. The more you delay that, the more frustrated the Hero gets, until they realize how screwed they are and try to run away in terror. While running, the Hero tries to steal a motorcycle and dies in a mere traffic accident.

                                                The main menu part with the Hero's trophy room now shows the stolen items either confiscated by authorities or returned to where they belong.
