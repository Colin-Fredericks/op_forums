                        Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                        Also, is there a way to "steal" a body of Cover, much more like Possesion, than creation of new being? Think Supernatural (TV show) demons and angles Vessels possessions. Is there anything like this?
                        Welll...there's one merit that, while it doesn't serve your immediate needs, can be used as a seed for what you want. The Sleeve Integrator Merit allows a Stigmatic possessing it to spend a WP to open himself up to merging with an Unchained for a day in exchange for learning some Embeds, acting as a new Cover in every way. In your games, what you could do is follow suit and allow some Stigmatics (or maybe all of them - it's your show!) to exist as another source of long-term covers as long as the Unchained can gain consent. Doing so will add another dimension to a war over Stigmatic allies, and it'll add some measure of the Vessels to your game at the cost of deemphasizing pacts a little.

                        You could even allow angels to do the same thing, although I probably wouldn't even if I was going for a more Supernatural twist on it. Do what thou wilt, though.

                                                        Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                                                        Apple of Discord. TO be fair the myth came before My Little Pony did it, but I can't think of someone doing a song to that.
                                                        That's hilariously close to the mark. I never thought I'd be doing research on My Little Pony episodes, but here we are.

                                                        The first seed of the idea for me came from Pulp Fiction, actually. We see the trope in The Maltese Falcon, and I was tempted to say something about MacGuffins but figured the developer would probably shoot that name down. So I went with the classic tale.

                                                        The "or person" language is there because I remembered There's Something About Mary and Helen of Troy, the latter of whom is closely connected to the Apple of Discord story anyway (she was Paris's reward for declaring Venus the fairest of the goddesses, and that sparked a war).

                                                        It's definitely one of those powers I would find a bunch of devious and arguably morally reprehensible uses for if I were playing in a Demon game. Things like dropping a quarter in a crowded public space and making my escape in the confusion. I hope others get good use out of it.

                                                            Okay, I really liked the book, but I couldn't wait til I was actually fully awake to read it, so perhaps a few things flew by my sleepy head, that being said.....

                                                            I'm not seeing a lot of point in embed tattoo gadgets. Other than imprinting near field powers to expand the demon's bag of tricks, unless one allows one to change the dice pool, but that's when the medium of the gadget causes the dice pool to change, is inking them on a demon warrant allowing the demon changing the dice pool? Because honestly that's not something I'd allow if I were running a demon game unless the book specifically says "Yes, the demon can change the dice pool by inking their embed."

                                                            The urban legend power seems a little weird to me, making urban legends sorta true so that demons can use them for facades. But urban legends are already true, there are alligators in the sewers, Bloody Mary collects little kid faces, and the Jersey Devil flies to this day (I used to want to add him to a hunter campaign, have him be the missing lucifuge member, but now with heirs to hell I could see him the result of a demon daddy going loud while the mrs was pregnant). So the premise of making something real more real just seems pretty darn odd to me.
