                                01-19-2017, 06:04 PM
                                Skype would provide some protection, either bonus dice or longer durations between rolls, but not full immunity.

                                The first time you used Skype you'd get a chance to regain Tranquility. If you wanted you could build an Evolution around using Skype which would replace the first time restriction with a far lesser restriction that you can attempt to regain Tranquility every time you solve significant problem by using Skype.

                                This Evolution sounds really powerful, and it is, but if Skype is made obsolete it can leave a Leviathan in a serious bind. There's probably an Ophion or two out there that thought the telegram is the final solution to the wake cutting leviathans off from mortals they cared about. With a bit more effort (it will probably be a pure roleplaying restriction) you could make the evolution work on anything similar to Skype, but it would be extreemly hard to make an Evolution that could still function after a fundamental technology shift such as replacing Skype with virtual reality or holographic projectors.

                                A School of Sand Leviathan who managed to make a future proof Evolution that functions with any instant communication technology can essentially be said to have won the game. Even if admittedly the difference between future proof and only working with Skype is zero in most games since most games don't have 100 year timeskips.

                                    Sample character.

                                    Anneke “Answer” Herbert
                                    Strain: Lahamin
                                    School: Abyss
                                    Concept: Waterpark Horror

                                    Undertow: The Nest-Maker
                                    Upwelling: The Mother

                                    STR: 2 DEX: 3 STA: 3
                                    INT: 2 WIT: 3 RES: 2
                                    PRE: 2 MAN: 2 COM: 3

                                    Sheol: 2
                                    Ichor: 8/12

                                    Willpower: 5/5
                                    Tranquility: 7 [3 bestial, 2 divine]

                                    Skills:
                                    Stealth 4 (Underwater Environments), Larceny 3, Athletics 3 (Small Spaces) (Deep Pools), Weaponry 2, Survival 2
                                    Occult 2, Science 2, Medicine 1 (First Aid), Crafts 1
                                    Animal Ken 2 (Aquatic Life), Intimidation 2 (Get The Fuck Back!), Streetwise 2, Subterfuge 1

                                    Atavisms:
                                    Lesser: Presence
                                    Greater: Wits
                                    Ancient: Dexterity

                                    Vestiges:
                                    Awareness, Predation, Elements

                                    Cult: Fervor 2, Piety 1, Zeal 2
                                    “Waterpark patrons, including the vagrants who squat in park facilities after dark."

                                    Merits:
                                    Fame (••)
                                    Gatewater (•)
                                    Home Turf (•)
                                    Double-Jointed (•)

                                    Anneke was born in a compound in Maine — an old seaside resort converted to a group home years ago, by some long-dead leviathan. There, she was raised on a doctrine in which Gnostic Christianity and Progenitor worship intertwined: children were taught to fear and adore the serpent-goddess Sophia, mother of the Earth, and to fear her son, who had imprisoned her in the physical world. Back then, Anneke was Answers-Contained-Within-The-Flesh Herbert, called Answer by her fellows, since the community had taken on the Puritan tradition of naming children after lines from its holy texts. Answer was never entirely satisfied with her life in the compound, and after learning that the world outside wasn’t a wasteland, she staged an escape through an old quarry beneath the hotel.

                                    As a photogenic late-teenaged escapee, Answer — who took the name Anneke from a phone book to commemorate her new life — got her fifteen minutes of fame across the U.S., inspiring negligent local authorities to crack down on the Maine compound. Once they were over, Anneke decided to put the past behind her. She moved to Arizona, embracing an atheistic worldview and a career as a waitress (to put herself through real high school), and that would have been the end of her story, if she hadn’t woken up one morning with the eyelid of her left eye gone.

                                    Her Metamorphosis started slow. Anneke thought it was a medical emergency — the doctors gave her eyedrops and scheduled a battery of tests. When she developed the power to see guilt, she was convinced she was delusional — her doctors prescribed therapy and antipsychotics. Then, the situation escalated: she ended up driving another waitress to a psychotic episode, and fled, realizing that the old preacher’s stories about serpent-spawn and dragons and the will of Eve had been true all along. About that time, she began to need water, to crave immersion. Sun City is landlocked; only after sequestering herself in a water park for a few weeks (and finding an underground reservoir to squat in), stealing Popsicles and raiding the snack stand, did she complete her Metamorphosis, and gain a measure of control over her form.

                                    Nowadays, Anneke’s Wake has begun to call to the patrons, some of whom discover her and begin to worship, and the waitress has been hunting for information about her, about the old Maine compound and her escape, though whether it’s as a supplicant or an Ahab isn’t clear. Some of the park's underground pipe systems have produced a natural Gatewater, and Rift exploration has provided insight to a girl with very little understanding of her situation and endless time on her hands; confronted with any kind of lead, be it Lahmasu, Hybrids or other Leviathans, she would commit all her resources to figuring out what, exactly, has happened to her. Left alone too long, on the other hand, she could easily lose the measure of stability that she found in escaping her childhood… and with the powers of the Tribe and an eschatology to grant unto her followers, Answer Herbert could rebuild her old faith in the depths of Sun City's artificial sea.
