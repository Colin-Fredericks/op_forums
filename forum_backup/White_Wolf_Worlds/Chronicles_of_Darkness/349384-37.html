                                                Blade Runner is either Changeling or Demon set a few decades in the future.

                                                If Changeling: Roy Batty and his group of replicants are a motley of Changelings taken by a Keeper known as the Tyrell Corporation (because corporations are people now, right?). Roy's name was Rick Deckard before his abduction. After his escape, Roy saw that his fetch had done too good a job of taking over his old life, so took on the new identity of Batty. While the Deckard fetch doesn't know what he really is, he finds himself particularly skilled at hunting down "replicants" (i.e. changelings) at the behest of Gaff, a Loyalist changeling in service to Tyrell Corporation. While being hunted by Deckard, Roy confronts a manifestation of his Keeper in the form of Dr. Tyrell and manages to defeat him. When Roy and Deckard finally confront each other, Roy merges with his fetch. Roy/Deckard then helps the changeling Rachel escape from Tyrell Corporation. Gaff, impressed with Roy/Deckard, takes pity on the two by giving them time to run before taking up the chase.

                                                If Demon: The Tyrell Corporation is Infrastructure of the God-Machine. Replicants are demons, and Deckard is an angel tasked with hunting them down. Rachel is a demon that has been recaptured and is in the process of reconditioning. During his final confrontation with the demon Roy Batty, Deckard breaks free of the God-Machine's influence and escapes with Rachel. In this version, Gaff is a normal human who believes the "replicant" cover and allows his friend Deckard to escape once he realizes he's a "replicant". Because the story is told from the perspective of angels and demons, Gaff's eccentricities (and those of the other characters, like J. F. Sebastian) are merely how they see humanity, which they don't quite understand.
