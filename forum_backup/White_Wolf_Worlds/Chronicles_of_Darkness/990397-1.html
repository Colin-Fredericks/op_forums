                        Well, revenants learn disciplines as out of clan, so I'd say it wouldn't be absurd for him to get no automatic disciplines, especially if he already knows a couple, being a ghoul. Then, once a vampire uplifts him, he'll get his free dots.

                        You can start that way at least. If it becomes a bit burdensome and he's really in need of more discipline, maybe you could be nice. I mean, he probably has sort of an idea what clan he wants, so you could give him maybe one dot of the disciplines of that clan, maybe barring the clan specific one (dominate, auspex, etc.)

                            Here's a little update on what we did and how it went during the first night back. We broke down what my revenant could get into two parts, so he'd get most of the vampire template, but not anything clan specific unless/until he got uplifted.

                            Pre-Uplift
                            - 3 Merit Points
                            - Mask
                            - Dirge
                            - Touchstone
                            - 1 Out of Clan Discipline Dot (No Specialty)
                            - Blood Potency 1

                            Post-Uplift
                            - 2 In-Clan Disciplines
                            - 1 attribute dot

                            Since he had drank from a Gangrel and a Daeva, he was allowed to choose from any of their nonspecific clan disciplines as the out of clan.

                            As for how the first night went, he came to, and had no idea why he was so thirsty. It clicked though when a friend dropped by unexpectedly. He was lucky and barely managed to resist frenzy, but he did run out of there without any exploitation. Nice and awkward LOL. He called his regent who promptly flipped out because THIS SHOULDN'T BE POSSIBLE. She's a Carthian Gangrel so she doesn't know that about this kinda thing. So while he's waiting for her to arrive, this nice couple is walking down the street past him, and this time he fails his frenzy check and promptly mauls one of them while the other was trying to smack him off. His frenzy ended JUUUST before killing this guy, and he ran off. Unfortunately for him he made his humanity check, and left a massive masquerade breach, what with to live witnesses.

                            He did manage to catch up to his regent/sire, and she remained ineffectual and had no idea who to even ask. So he suggested that this one woman from her childe's coterie might know something (she's a Mekhet Ordo Dracul, and PC). The Mekhet was able to figure out that he didn't have a clan, and he couldn't hold onto vitae. She pitched this like a good thing, being a bit of a mad scientist type. She said she'd try and help him with the blood thing, but he's not too optimistic right now! And that was how the first night went.

                            I don't think he'll continue to luck out and not kill anyone, so this is gonna get much uglier =D

                                                        Sorry to be late for the update, I had a pretty busy weekend! ANYWAY, I'd like warn y'all that unfortunately I did really well on all my frenzy checks despite not spending any will power </3

                                                        Since I'm keeping you all updated I'll actually give the suckers names, and a bit of the main plot to help clear up my writing. My ghoul-turned-revenant is James. His regent/sire is Vixie, a Carthian Gangrel who's been around a while. We also the members of the cotorie James has been hanging around (read PCs), Tessa, a Mekhet in the Ordo Dracul; Draven, an unaligned Daeva; and the other two who might come up in later posts are Cam, Vixie's childe who's also a Carthian; Ivy, a Ventrue wanna-be Invictus. The main issue at hand is that our cotorie and all their sires blew the masquerade to the changelings in the city. The Invictus, who are in charge, are basically looking to arrest us all. I'm still gonna try to keep these posts focused on the Revenant issues as much as possible.

                                                        As mentioned last time, right before the evening ended, Tessa managed to get 6 donated packets of blood (stolen from a hospital LOL) for James, so hopefully he wouldn't frenzy when he woke up the following night. Given that James's shitty little apartment is no Haven, he wound up moving some bedding into his closet and stuffed the cracks closed to keep the sunlight out. Super Classy. The real horror though was when he woke up the next evening. He was absolutely drenched in blood, and it basically looked like someone got murdered in his closet. He would have been really horrified by that if not for the fact he was pretty distracted by how unbelievably thirsty he was. He pretty much tore into the blood packs. Tessa had meant them to last a few days, but he went through the entire stock in one sitting. Even still, he was pretty thirsty (only 3 vitae!) but better able to access the whole OH MY GOD BLOOD EVERYWHERE! situation. A very ugly mess to clean up first thing in the evening >:

                                                        So after that he calls up Vixie to check in with her, and described what happened when he woke up and Vixie was like WOW THAT'S REALLY FUCKED UP I DON'T THINK I CAN HELP YOU WITH THIS ACTUALLY. Since Tessa had said the night before she wanted to have a talk with Vixie, James set up a meeting for all three of them in the back room of this bar later in the evening. James gets there first and about all he can notice it how easy it is to hear everyone's hearts beating with fresh, warm, beautiful blood. Still, he managed to maintain his composure until he got to the back room, though he just about chewed through his own lip. Vixie and Tessa both arrive and discuss some other pressing issues, and James gets a call from Draven, also regarding said issues. James brings up the whole I DIED thing and Draven pretty much goes OH THAT SUCKS. MAYBE VIXIE CAN HELP? (super helpful!) After that called wrapped up, Tessa decided that she'd take Vixie and James to see her sire (a coterie-mate of Vixie's, and also Ordo Dracul).

                                                        On their way out of the back room, James and Tessa notice some Invictus vampires rolling up out front. Basically, because of issues mentioned above, they're here to arrest the three. Buuut the bar is full of so so many tasty blood filled people. Unfortunately, James still managed to hold off on frenzying right in the middle of the bar, though now he's really Tempted, so we'll see if that luck continues to hold out. James goes out the front to play hero/distraction, while the other two slip out the back. He managed to hold his own pretty well in a 4-v-1 fight but they ultimately subdue him, so now James is off to vampire jail on charges of breaching the masquerade!

                                                        I WISH I WOULD FAIL ONE OF THESE FRENZY ROLLS. I thought for sure he'd have offed someone by accident at this point. Still, it's probably gonna cause some issues, since last they all checked, he was supposedly just a Ghoul. I'll keep you all posted until someone gets around to uplifting him.

                                                        EDIT: My GM likes to give out extra Beats for out of character work like journals, or art, or whatever. I wrote a little perspective piece, so I might as well toss it out here for for anyone who was curious.
