        Hunter (Malleus Maleficarum) / Leviathan.

        Something has happened in the heavens. Prayers are going unanswered and you feel a gaping void in your soul. Soon the very rules of reality are falling apart. First the more complex ones like Love, but even simpler things like the whether are starting to fall apart.

        But some trace of divinity remains. The Tribe are thriving. Mankind flocks to their cults in droves to fill the void in their souls with the Wake and the hole in their bellies with the bounty of the seas (it seems the Vestige of Elements isn't falling apart with the rest of the weather).

        Somebody needs to fill the empty thrones in the heavens, and there are no good candidates left. Can the church mentor Leviathans so that they may lead this world, can Christianity create a new theology that embraces man's darkest natures and turns them to good ends?

        Or is such a thing too horrible to contemplate? Will the church stand for it's principals and reject mankind's demons even as the very world falls apart? Fiat justitia ruat cælum.

        “There are no rules. Only Principles and natural laws.” - Promethius

        Full length fan-books I contributed too: Princess: the Hopeful, Leviathan: the Tempest, Dream Catchers

                96) Someone is gathering Supernaturals (a throng of Prometheans, a small krewe of Sin-Eaters and a few Mages to be exact) to strike against a particularly powerful Arisen. This will lead to turmoil and upset the Infrastructure, so the Unchained and Kindred try to start a counter-measure.
                When your group meets this "Someone", they are left even more confused: he is apparently a Mage, since the Mages present (Mysterium) consider him one of their own. Also, he has a Geist. The vibe you get is more the one of a Kerberoi than that of a Sin-Eater, however (if you can sense such things). And while Geist and Bound are partly-one, this one is infused with his Geist so much you could think there is nothing else there. And to top it all off, for some reason, the Prometheans like him and can interact with him just fine.
                To stop this 'war', you will have to find out who or what he is, why he wants to annihilate one of the Arisen and how he became what he is.

                        Just because we're almost finished at least one of these threads.

                        98: (Genius/Princess) One particularly clever Alhambran operation has decided that they need a way to harvest massive amounts of hope to ensure their city continues to survive. To that end, they've contracted out with an illuminated genius, promising him all the support he needs in return for giving them as much of the hope he harvests as they can bring back. Of course, local princesses and moral geniuses would take at least one or two issues with this situation. So what does happen when the earthly symbols of inspiration fight alongside living symbols of hope?

                        99: (Vampire/Promethean) A member of the Ordo Dracul is making waves. He's claiming that there are other ways to make childer, that don't involve turning anyone in the street. Unfortunately, his failures have escaped, and his only success is reportedly making trouble in the local area. The dragon has become consumed by perfecting his creations, and in doing so has alienated much of the local court and annoyed the local prince. Meanwhile, a throng has arrived at the will of the Principle, telling anyone in the know that they've been sent to stop a disaster in the making. It doesn't take a genius to put two and two together on this one, but for vampires nothing is ever so easy. What's the real hidden game under the smokescreen of reviving the dead?

                        100: (Werewolf/Mummy) An ancient spirit has returned from the distant reaches of the Shadow that only the oldest werewolves can remember. The spirit is one of conquest, and it's set up shop with a local militia group. Fortunately, there is one person who might known something about it. That person being the ancient undead priest-lord that leads their own cult in the county seat. Said ancient priest-lord has only just been woken up by their cult due to the fact that they alerted their followers to alert them the second such an ancient enemy returned to the world of the living. Surely, focused on the same target, the werewolves and living dead can leave the scene intact?

                        101: (Everything) Something is waking up under Yellowstone National Park. A recent spate of heavy seismic activity coupled with a sudden shift in several paranormal communities in the area can only mean that the "supervolcano" is something much worse. World ending worse. Practically anyone who can has sent someone to stop whatever is underneath the caldera from waking up, but everyone has a different way to do it. Werewolves and hunters argue over human sacrifices. Princesses and changelings skirmish over how to treat anyone in the area who doesn't know what's happening. Vampires are trying to claim wells of esoteric power from mages. Sin-Eaters...well, okay, they're pretty much the only ones who are trying to enjoy themselves, much to the annoyance of practically everyone there. The best guess is there's one full month until it does wake up. The real question is, can the various factions lay aside their differences long enough to focus on the end of the world? And if they can't, what will they do when whatever is under the ground is finally awake?

                            (Changeling/Your Choice):

                            (Non-Changeling perspective): People are being replaced with clones that have all the memories that they're supposed to have, but they've got some nice shiny new powers. Nobody would've noticed... except supernatural creatures are being replaced too, and they're coming back human. Somebodies gotta figure out what's going on and who's behind it. Especially because some of these clones seem to be up to something big...

                            (Changeling perspective): There are fetches everywhere, and more pop up by the day. The local freeholds can't keep up with the sudden influx, on top of desperately trying to fend off the Fae incursions and kidnappings that must be happening. And these fetches seem more organized than normal...

                            (I'm currently running this campaign, but where the main antagonists have found out how to make their own artificial fetches, and are using them to build a sacred-geometric sigil over the town. It's a cross between invasion of the body snatchers and fullmetal alchemist)

                                                        109: (Hunter/Beast, Demon, Dragon, Leviathan, etc whatever works best)
