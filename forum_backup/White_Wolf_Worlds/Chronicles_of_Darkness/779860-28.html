    I can now say with glee that Verses are finally done! At least, how they'll be is done! There may be tweaking required to make Conclusions or Verses better, so if anyone has any criticisms, feel free to give them! The Transform and Travel Conclusions are as follows!

    Transform: One of the more sorcerous/alchemical of Conclusions, belonging to the Current of Dark Secrets and no other current, this Conclusion allows for a Siren to transform her target into her chosen Opus for a time.
    Transform 1: Muta - You can cause cosmetic changes within the target.
    Transform 2: Cambiare - You may change basic sentient beings (plants or bugs) or objects of a homogenous nature into either the same or the other. You may give Pneuma to another person or Siren, or with the right Opuses you may turn Pneuma into Fuel stats for other Supernaturals.
    Transform 3: Variazioni - The Siren may now change any sentient (but non-sapient being) or objects of a heterogeneous nature into either the same or other. The Siren can change an object into gas, solid or liquid, The Siren may also cause drastic changes, such as changing gender, ethnicity, body structure or size. May even hand Merits for the duration of the Verse, but the Merit dots given cannot be more than the Siren's Transform dots.The Siren may even turn objects into Treasures.
    Transform 4: Transition - The Siren may now choose and transform any sapient beings (supernatural or not) into any of the beings from the previous powers and vice versa.
    Transform 5: Mordent - The Siren may transform anyone into exactly anything she desires without need to worry about the details. The limit to this power is the Siren's Imagination, Pneuma and Willpower pools. This also allows a Siren to become the embodiment of an Opus while it's active (such as a living shadow like most splats use).

    Travel: The Conclusion of Travel, which belongs to the Celestial Current of Buoyancy, makes all kinds of transitions, entrances and exits possible. Be it Physical destinations, trying to get through social maneuvering or moving emotions and thoughts are possible with this.
    Travel 1: Trobar Leu - Eliminates penalties when trying to reach a goal.
    Travel 2: Canso - Adds bonuses when trying to reach a goal with minor manipulation of the opus to help, or adds penalties onto people trying to get to a goal. Only applies when Opuses do.
    Travel 3: Trobar Ric - Allows for being able to go through chosen opuses as if swimming through them. For example, going through walls, flight and going through radioactive material is all possible with this. Allows for moving concepts elsewhere and opening gates to other worlds.
    Travel 4: Tenso - Allows for straight up Teleportation from one source of an opus to another, like the much used "Shadow Step" in the WoD. She may take successes from a concept's application, but she must later pay up for taking those successes where they weren't hers.
    Travel 5: Trobar Clus - Co-Location with bodies made of the Opus and twisting physical dimensions by the Opuses.

    I can't help but feel like Transform is lacking Conceptual Applications, and that Travel is very much lacking in the final power. Otherwise, these are the last two major things to set within the Verses section! I will put in a small sidebar on "whether or not verses can affect other supernatural splat powers". My basic answer is "it's up to your ST, to make it fair he may want to make it so other splats can do the same to Sirens".

    Verses Document: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...it?usp=sharing

    I believe the next step to take for Siren is to revise the Mood to Survival. I feel it better fits with Siren's themes. I know, there hasn't been any comments on the decision, but I'm assuming that since there hasn't been any, then there's probably nothing wrong with it the idea.

    The next step after this? I feel like it would be appropriate to finally go into detail on our two pure antagonist factions. There's Echidna and her Sirens, who continually seek to make the new species out of humanity that will survive the apocalypse, and the Flensers, who seek to eat Siren flesh to keep themselves young and seek to make new resources to allow humanity to come to their greatest amount of potential through technology. Specifically, I'm thinking Cybernetics a'la Deus Ex or the like.

    My main reasoning is to give the Flensers a sympathetic point of view. Sure, they can be selfish and destroying nature to get whatever resources they need, but they use said resources to give a much better quality of life to people by making their bodies better. Perhaps more than a few Flensers are those they've helped. Of course, why do they use Cybernetics when they're more concerned about Siren flesh to eat? Perhaps the Flensers are jealous of the Sirens, trying to become superhuman themselves. The one thing they haven't unlocked is longevity...so until they do, it's prolly better to steal it from Sirens until they don't need them anymore.

                                I'm proud to note that the Current of Echidna has been created! They're named the Current of Oceanus and are found before Phelegethon and after Lethe. :3

                                Abyssal Currents Document: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...it?usp=sharing

                                With this, I can truly feel like saying Abyssal Currents are done. I feel like I should go about and edit the History section for all of them, make them more mysterious on their origins and potentially vague on whether they exist today. I'm thinking of also revising/creating the backstory for Sirens, the only question I have for anyone watching is "what do you think it should be like"?

                                Going from the top, here's the ideas had on the Siren's Backstory that we made here and that was on the original Siren thread.
                                1: Echidna is a central antagonist to Siren and one of the very few, possibly the only one to survive the Deluge.
                                2: The Song is intriniscally tied to creating Sirens. It calls those who are trying to survive despite the odds.
                                3: The Sirens have a matron known as Ceto who has as much importance to Sirens as Luna does to Werewolves.
                                4: Sirens, at least the Celestial ones, accept that they were called to stop the Deluge from happening.
                                5: The Deluge is the apocalypse that destroys the world and can take on different forms. It can be flooding, natural disasters, human caused, ice age or heat death, etc. Can be speculated to be a restart button for existence.
                                6: There is a post apocalyptic remnant of the old world called the Celestial Ocean, where Survivors, Therons, Vodianoi and Echidna live that should be able to be travelled to for Sirens and as an exploratory part of the game. Treasures (Verses sealed into an object) and Trinkets can be found here.
                                7: The idea that the Bloop is a manifestation of the Song, and that the Song didn't start occurring until the Industrial Revolution where men and woman started to care less about nature.

                                Now the only question I have is, what would be a good backstory to make out of this? The idea I had involved old Gods, manifestations of the Sky, Sea and Land who made humanity and the world. They also made Ceto as the creator of the Sirens. However, the being of Land would go against the Sea, wanting the Sea's power of creation for himself. Failing in this, his rage is what caused the Deluge to happen the first time. The manifestation of the Sky and Ceto attempted to pull back time to avoid this, and in doing so created the Song, which would call forth people into becomming Sirens. Echidna was supposed to be able to get the fallen Sea's mutagenic powers of creation, in which she's still learning to use in making her Vodianoi.

                                Any thoughts? Ideas? Other thoughts or ideas for the Backstory? What do you all think?

                                            Alright. Now that my little vacation is over, I'm going to post some new stuff I've gotten finished for this game. For starters, I've created the Siren Sub-Splat, the Breathless! They're people who failed their Drowning, and because of it their soul is wounded. They exude an aura almost like a dying person calling for help, and as such, most mortals are drawn to them like flies to honey. What's more, the wound in their soul can be reached through by Sirens and touch other people. In mechanical terms, when a Siren feeds from a Breathless, they instead gain Pneuma from almost anyone else within sight of the Breathless.

                                            As for another note, I also have something else good to announce! The Introduction to Siren is complete! This includes the awesome Logo Yads Dom made awhile back, a Special Thanks to those who both originally created the project and those who continued to create it, a couple of introductory paragraphs, Theme and Mood, a few myths and facts of Sirens in the game, a How to Use This Book section and finally Sources and Inspirations!
