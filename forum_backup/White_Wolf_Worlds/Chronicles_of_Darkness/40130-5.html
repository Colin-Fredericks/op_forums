    Originally posted by YeOfLittleFaith View Post

    Part of my problem with that take on them is that it completely removes most of the ambiguity and doubt that comes with the question of what the Purified are. By just outright saying "they're spirits/they've become special spirits". Comparing them to Claimed who have permanently fixed themselves to their original body also rubs me the wrong way, since even special snowflake ones shouldn't work like that. They don't get to just reform their original host body from nothing. Like Purified can.
    Yes, it removes this doubt from players and places it squarely on the character/drama side. The ambiguity you're referring to is, IMO, largely an artifact of space-saving efforts by the writers, who seem to have intended Purified to be very spirit-like and to grow even more spirit-like over time. Our mod does something very WoDish, we think, which is to give characters an identity and motivation problem: they may think and feel that they are immortal humans, but they are not human anymore--or only echoes of human in a spirit of something primal. Slowly getting glimpses of just how alien they have become and having to contend with increasingly intrusive urges is compelling, to me. I'm not sure exactly what the draw is of making Purified ambiguous to the players.

    I also always thought of their Morality/Integrity as also marking how far from slipping into the spirit side of things they are - an additional indicator of the proximity of their minds to animist ephemeral beings. Because, relevant, Spirits do not in fact have an Integrity equivalent, so a Purified's Integrity falling to 0 could very well mean the same to them as losing human-like mentality entirely in favor of a spirit-like one.

    But without wanting to go on a huge tangent and lacking the brain fuel to go into an extended argument over it at this time, that conflict (their bodies and souls become closer to spirits', but how closer?) and how their unique natures can toss them into the middle of many situations involving ephemeral entities and other long lasting supernaturals, are very important parts of the Purified to me.
    You seem to have just described the very morality system used in our mod, only from the perspective of things that are humanish and become something more spiritish over time. Ours takes the premise that the morality is a mimicry that protects Purified in their interactions on the material side, much like Humanity protects the undead. In fact--and Kaye may have a better understanding about this since she wrote this part--Simulacrum is rather like Humanity: a memory or echo of how humans behave that helps the creature pass in public. The shock a character can experience is to notice that they aren't what they think they are, and that their humanity may only be a mental projection or a habit gradually being challenged by their hunger for Essence. It turns out there's a cost to making yourself eternally young and storing your entire soul not as flesh (which, after all, can now be endlessly regrown, brain and bone), but as ephemera of a certain resonance.

        I think that my last line is a keystone for me: It turns out there's a cost to making yourself eternally young and storing your entire soul not as flesh (which, after all, can now be endlessly regrown, brain and bone), but as ephemera of a certain resonance. Like, occultist, did you really think that you could kill yourself on a locus, make it so that your body can be endlessly resurrected even from a pinch of your dust, and still be a human?

        Obviously your soul has to have gone somewhere – it has to have become something. You can't do something that drastic and stay what you are. It's like a math problem: things have to equal each other on both sides. If you become deathless on one side, the material side, it's because you have completely removed everything that pins you to that side, using ritualistic death on an anchor between the two realms. But it also means that you are now pinned to the other side, the Shadow side.

            One more post before I forget. Your comments have been very thought-provoking, and I appreciate them. They are helping me figure out the philosophy underpinning this mod, and how it differs from other people's interpretations.

            I have a question. If purified are not ultimately spirits, then how can they be nearly destroyed in spirit form, reduced to tiny motes of essence, regrow themselves in spirit form, and then resurrect their fleshly bodies? What is that little mote? Why is it that removing its last bit of the essence and destroying it will kill it just as one kills a spirit, if they are not actually spirits? When that little nearly destroyed mote grows a little bit, regaining some sentience, what is it? Can it truly be referred to as a human spirit hybrid? It is barely conscious, its body is ash in some Hunter's crematory oven or soggy bones at the bottom of the ocean, and it will be a while before it has enough essence to knit its body back together and repossess it. During all that time, what is this creature? We take the stance that it is almost entirely spirit, and that it has been a spirit since the moment the ritual succeeded, even if the ritualist did not actually understand what it had become.

            If it is not a spirit, why does it adhere to the most fundamental spirit metaphysics, but utterly ignores human physics?

                They can already have a motivation and identity problem without outright stating what becoming a Purified did to them. I would not find Kindred nearly as interesting if the presence of the Beast and their relation to the Strix was explained. Both approaches may be valid, but I don't think the writers intended for us to interpret that as the Purified literally becoming spirits. They alter their souls to become more like spirits: right there there's already a wide breadth of possible conflict to explore. Removing the ambiguity, in my opinion, is not going to add anything to the storytelling - it's just shrinking what it offers.

                No, Integrity as I see it being used for the purified has different paradigms from the morality used in the mod (that you've in fact already pointed out). I don't appreciate the premise of Simulacrum as a "mimicry" of human behavior because it jumps from the proposition that Purified are spirits at their core by default, which I do not believe was the intent of the writers. It's another unnecessary thing from a possibly fallacious assumption that just overcomplicates them far as I'm concerned, because Integrity with logical caveats is enough. Calling Humanity just a "mimicry of how Humans behave" for Vampires is also grossly simplifying it.

                And even for those Purified with high Integrity that do not dabble much in the spirit world, the subject of what they are and what they have become will never cease being relevant. In fact, it should logically only grow more relevant with age. The doubt might not be that dissimilar from Slashers and Hunters with increasing number of Endowments. As well, because Purified are spiritually different from regular humans, does not make necessarily make them different mentally.

                Ultimately, I think we'll disagree because we don't look at the Purified from the same points of view. I will say the following: I respect the work you've put into this mod, and I acknowledge your effort. But personally, and subjectively, I do not agree with how it goes about them.

                EDIT:

                Because the ritual that makes them Purified alters their spiritual nature. They might certainly not be regular humans, but that alone does not make them true spirits, and they most certainly keep human mentalities, even if they now have to deal with a whole new host of things that human minds usually do not have to. (Again, because they become more like spirits does not make them spirits.) It's worth nothing ghosts also work on several of the same metaphysics, and even regular humans can leave ghosts behind following their deaths. And funnily enough, death is an important lynchpin in the rite to become a Purified.

                            I am thinking about phrasing for mechanical elegance (and thematic integration). I think that making this an innate quality makes sense. One of the reasons for making Projection an innate quality is that everyone has to buy it, and it's not like it's a tangible artifact like a Touchstone or a Haven or something. A Morphic Aura (not Predatory Aura) should also be innate if everyone playing a Purified would need it. I've integrated the small piece about sensing other Purified through physical touch. I based this on Simulacrum to reflect that Simulacrum is related to how easily they can pass and simulate human Integrity.
                            Morphic Aura

                            Purified exhibit an aura that many other supernatural beings can pick up on, but while in their physical bodies, their spirit nature attempts to conceal itself from other supernatural types. Other supernaturals may make a Wits + Composure roll penalized by the Purified's Simulacrum/Integrity to see through their human shell and register them as something different or noteworthy. Note that while Projected in Twilight or Shadow, there can be no attempt at concealment.

                            With Purified and embodied Spirits, the situation is different: Any Purified can sense a connection to Shadow and relative Chi with an unpenalized Intelligence + Occult roll, performed reflexively with skin-to-skin contact.

                            Buttressing: Purified spirit natures will recoil in response to Lashing Out, the one-dot Disciplines Awe and Dread Presence, and other powers that allow for a Predatory Aura-based resistance automatically. Not doing so requires the expenditure of 1 Willpower point. The resistance is based on Composure + Chi. If resisting Lashing Out, Purified cannot impose Conditions, but they may receive the Conditions Showing or Threatened (as appropriate) if they fail their defensive roll.

                                                WtF 2e was released this week! I have revised the mods I made for WoD2e for Purified based on its content, which provided a few more spirit-related things. Thanks, everyone, for the good conversation surrounding this.

                                                You can find the new revisions here: http://bit.ly/purified2e_hacks

                                                And here is a short table of contents with some notes on what you might find of interest, as it occurred to me that not everyone will want to click on a PDF right off the bat. Also, this way, if people are searching for something on the forums, they might actually find the info in the PDF.

                                                THE MODS

                                                Basic Hacks to the Immortals Text
                                                1. Based on the GMC and WtF spirit information, along with others' mods of Immortals that seem to have worked out well (those posited here, for example), this provides a quick reference for innate Purified abilities.
                                                2. The Morphic Aura that we discussed above

                                                Spirits in Flesh
                                                1. Essence: Harvest, addiction, and use
                                                2. Banes, Bans, Restrictions
                                                3. Influences
                                                4. Chart of Chi for conversion to a 1-10 scale, "Showing" Influence-based spirit nature
                                                5. Replacing Integrity with Simulacrum, which mashes up the Flesh:Spirit and slow degeneration from WtF and VtR respectively. (Purified are unique.)

                                                Purified and Other Supernaturals
                                                1. Some ideas on how their blood and other physical markers work
                                                2. Changelings and Mages
                                                3. What happens when Demons take a Purified as a Cover

                                                General Purified Conditions (I'm just listing these)
                                                1. Essence Hunger
                                                2. Influenced
                                                3. Interested
                                                4. Rapture Dependency
                                                5. Spiritlike (Persistent)
                                                6. Showing
                                                7. Threatened

                                                Hacks for Numina and Siddhi
                                                1. General Numina guidelines to offer a reasonable expansion of the ones from core Immortals
                                                2. Additional Numina
                                                3. Modifications for Siddhi to make them five-dot: They have been collapsed down to Siddhi of Flesh, Mind, and Will, and most of the 3-level Siddhi have been rolled into these. The ones that have not can easily be purchased as separate Numina.

                                                Merits
                                                1. Awesome ones about possessions and professions
                                                2. Curious merits that provide some supernatural benefits
                                                3. Drink the kool-aid and learn kung fu from Scientologists! (AKA Purified Mystery Cults)

                                                Thematics
                                                1. Purification processes
                                                2. Purified psychology: What's different, what's the same
                                                3. SLANG. You know you want to read it!

                                                The Human Artifice
                                                1. An optional hack if people want to go further with Purified awesomeness
