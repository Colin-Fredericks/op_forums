              It depends on how long the cocaine is left there. The usual course of events is that items left in the Hedge eventually become tokens, but there aren't anything like solid rules for how long it takes or what effect the token has. Especially as a limited use item it's less likely to become a token with a dot rating as opposed to a trifle (essentially a limited use 0 dot token)

              Another important question, how well did they hide it? There's every possibility of it being consumed by a hobgoblin or winding up in a merchants stall at goblin market.

              If you just want to apply a magical property, I would suggest that it ensorcells mortals, allowing them to see through Masks for a week. Changelings already react poorly to many kinds of drugs, it would probably inflict a nasty clarity breaking point.

                          Originally posted by Necrophear View Post
                          Are there different sections of Hedge?
                          The Hedge is not a big undifferentiated chunk of trails and thorns and thus the features of a given location within it are going to vary wildly as a whole. Tossing a bag of coke in a river-witch's millpond is likely to produce different results from burying it in a potter's grave at a ramshackle cemetery, and neither of those will have the same result as leaving it on the table at a goblin market or in the middle of the path from here to there.
