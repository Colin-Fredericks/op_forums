                    1st ED Spirit had that very weird curve where at levels 1-3 you did very little outside your specialty, at lvl 4 you started being versatile, and at lvl 5 you suddenly became omnipotent, because you could:
                    1. Create new spirits from scratch
                    2. Reshape existing spirits to fit whatever you imagine
                    3. Turn spirits into talismans that allowed you to use their powers paradox free.

                    This meant that master of Spirit suddenly could create Spirit of anything he needed, turn it into a talisman, and then use it's powers without fear. Getting to Spirit 5 was kinda like suddenly gaining 3 in all aracanas.

                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                        I can't help but feel you're missing the point of Spirit somewhat.
                        I'm not missing the point od Spirit; I'm just not seeing the Arcanum the same way you do.

                        If this was Mage: the Ascension we were talking about, you'd probably be right: the Spirit Sphere is the go-to Sphere for all things Umbral, be they ghosts, nature spirits, astral entities, or even stranger things. But not in the ChroD. In Mage: the Awakening, you use Death when dealing with ghosts, Prime when dealing aith angels, Mind when dealing with goetia, your Ruling Arcana when dealing with Supernal Entities, etc. Spirit is only for dealing with those Ephemeral Entities that come from the Shadow. This makes Spirit the odd man out, in that literally every single Arcanum can interact with ephemera and do other things, except Spirit, which can only interact with ephemera. And it doesn't even get to be the generalist when it comes to ephemera; it's just as specialized to Hisil spirits as Death is specialized to ghosts.

                        Also, I've never been fond of Life covering animal instincts (which, as written, are primarily about, well, animals, not humans — it specifically calls out the fact that those spells work because animals don't have minds) — so part of my hack is to recenter those spells as well over to Spirit, and to leave Life with healing and shapeshifting for the most part.

                        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                        Per RAW, the purview of Spirit only includes Hisil entities. What I do is shift the concept from “Hisil” to “the nonphysical part of a person that is the seat of emotions and character” (more or less) and make the Hisil stuff a spinoff of that.

                                        That is, in my ChroD, Spirit isn't primarily about Hisil entities and the Shadow. It's primarily about emotions, instincts, intuition, drive; that sort of stuff. Which isn't to say that you can't use Spirit to do the Hisil stuff; but doing so is like using Prime to affect Angels.

                                        EDIT: actually, it's not quite that simple. As ShadowKnight1224 points out, this emphasizes the “five subtle Arcana govern five aspects of the human soul” — but what Spirit governs manifests as an aspect of the human soul when you apply it to a human, and as Hisil-related phenomena when you don't. It's all the same “stuff”; just utilized differently.
