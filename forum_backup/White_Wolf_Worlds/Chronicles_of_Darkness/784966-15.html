            By the way, while you're thinking about that, I'd like you (or anyone else here) to critique the Mystery:

            The Mystery of Deep Purple

            Opacity: 3

            Surface Information [through Revelation w/o Scrutiny]:

            Death - Soul of user has been altered in some way by the drug
            Life - User exhibits extreme steroid use symptoms and suffers from an unnatural hunger
            Matter - Altered chemical substance similar to heroin, mixed with blood through Patterning
            Mind - User’s Oneiros has been altered, Acamoth Tethered to user in Mind Twilight
            Prime - Drug carries traces of Awakened magic, undecipherable Signature Nimbus
            Time - Drug vial is 3 months old, user has been under the effects for 3 weeks

            Deep Information [through lowering Opacity w/Scrutiny]:

            Fate - Users share their fates with one another (converging lines)
            Matter - The blood part of the substance carries an Abyssal taint, the magic drug is Lasting
            Mind - User’s Daimon is extremely weak or non-existent, Acamoth is causing the hunger
            Prime - Resonance of Compliance can be found as well as a trace Abyssal taint, Signature Nimbus causes drug to appear more translucent as if being X-rayed
            Space - Users share a strong sympathetic connection, even if unrelated

            Final Solution [at Opacity 0]

            Users share a realm in the Temenos: Purging the drug from the user’s system, severing their link to the hive in the Temenos with Space, and repairing their Oneiros with Mind can save a victim and begin the recovery process.

                Originally posted by Johnny Awesome View Post
                Oh, what about a ban and a bane for the Acamoth? I went with the empathetic Sanctifier of the Meek motif.
                At the moment you know more about the Acamoth than I do. Still, I'll make a shot in the dark and try anyway. Since the Acamoth represents a belief, its weakness can lie either in needing to uphold that belief, or in subversion of that belief. If you give the Acamoth additional symbolism, you can design its weaknesses after that, but I'm working off what I know.

                Let's start with the obvious:
                Ban: It can only affect users of Deep Purple. It might discover other means of access in the future.
                Ban: It can only affect perpetrators and victims of cannibalism. A shared Temenos realm circumvents this Ban.

                Dogma:
                Ban: Whenever a killer refuses to consume their victim the Sanctifier will intervene. It's always aware when this happens around its cultists. If the killer successfully pleads their case (succeeds at an appropriate Social or Mental roll), the Sanctifier may become traumatized.
                Ban: Both disrespecting and easing the suffering of the sacrifices are unacceptable. Sanctifier will intervene to correct that misconduct. If the perpetrator successfully pleads their case (succeeds at an appropriate Social or Mental roll), the Sanctifier may become traumatized.
                Ban: When approached respectfully, Sanctifier will happily share advice on any topic. After all, fulfilling lives make for both better sacrifices and priests. However, it won't knowingly go against its protégés interests by doing so.

                Blasphemy:
                Bane: Force-feeding one's flesh to an unwilling human being reverses the Ruler - Sacrifice relation. The Acamoth suffers from witnessing that act.
                Bane: Funerals as we know them are alien to the Sanctifier. Cremated or embalmed remains, or bodies allowed to decay act as its Bane.
                Bane: Whenever a cultist earnestly accepts mercy and help after being defeated, they become Sanctifier's Bane. There needs to be notable effort or sacrifice on part of the mercy-giver.
                Bane: People in an unequal relationship praying together.

                                                I pretty much decided to keep The Shinje as written.

                                                Here is the mystery breakdown I came up with if anyone is interested or wants to critique it:

                                                Mystery - Beast of Burden

                                                Shinje - Threefold Resurrection Spirit

                                                Opacity: 3

                                                Surface Information [through Revelation w/o Scrutiny]:

                                                Death - Phurba has Death resonance
                                                Life - Wu is weak
                                                Matter - Cage has been strengthened with a Matter spell
                                                Mind - Wu is distraught
                                                Prime - Traces of Awakened magic on the shattered cage
                                                Spirit - Gauntlet has been strengthened inside the zone of the cage, Phurba has a Spirit resonance
                                                Time - The cage was shattered two hours ago

                                                Deep Information [through lowering Opacity w/Scrutiny or other investigation]:

                                                Death - Wu lacks a soul (must contend with Transform Aura first), Shinje possesses two souls
                                                Fate - Phurba has a strong connection to Khatvanga/Shinje
                                                Life - The Shinje is capable of shifting forms
                                                Mind - Breach the Vault of Memory spell exists on Wu (Clash vs. 12 dice)
                                                Prime - Transform Aura spell on Wu so he appears Awakened with a soul (Clash vs. 11 dice), Otherwise he registers as Awakened
                                                Space - Phurba has an Intimate connection to Khatvanga/Shinje
                                                Time - Whatever is supposed to happen will happen within the next 7 days

                                                Final Solution [at Opacity 0] - Full extent of Khatvanga’s intentions and how the Shinje works becomes known. Likely only possible with Athaneum research.
