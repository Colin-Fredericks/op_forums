    111: (Beast/Leviathan/Mummy/Princess/Vampire)

    For millennia, five (5) individuals, a Begotten, a Leviathan, a Noble, an Arisen, and a member of the Kindred, have been keeping the peace between their respective races, and becoming best friends (to the point of considering themselves siblings) in the process. One day, one of them is kidnapped by an unknown group for unknown, but surely dastardly, designs. It's now up to the other four to rescue their friend, hopefully before anyone else finds out and decides to use this spread chaos, or (even worse) to ignite war!

    112: (Beast/Princess)

    Rumors are beginning to circulate in the Begotten and Noble communities of a Beast and an Enlightened getting together as a couple. A small team (the PCs) is called forth to investigate these rumors, and learn of the veracity behind them. No matter if they're correct or not, the team is to find the two and learn just what is actually going on.

        I've no idea where this came from, but it most likely has something to do with the various character ideas spinning around in my head..

        113: (Core/All) The PC Troupe have just stumbled upon a most peculiar find; a mortal able to sense the flow of supernatural energies without aid. And not only do they sense the usage of said energies, but also their gathering and storage, places of power (Avernian Gates, paths into the Hedge, Chambers, Arisen Tombs and so on), items imbued with power that doesn't follow the more mundane rules of the universe (Gadgets, Relics, Azothic Items, Fetishes, etc), the flow of said energies in their 'natural' state (Essence 'radiating' off of damn-near everything, Vitae circulating inside of both themselves and others, Glamour coiling in the air around those feeling intense emotions, the 'pooling' of Aether run-off from Infrastructure) and the presence of supernatural beings and creatures. All attempts to analyze this fellow turn up the same; they're nothing more than a mortal who just so happens to be extremely sensitive to the supernatural.

        What does the group make of this discovery? Should they kill this innocent person (who's been minding their own business whilst dealing with all the phenomenon going on around them) before they become a threat? Or try to convert them to the Troupe's cause? Or maybe something else...

        OOC: The Mortal is genuinely just that; a mortal. They just so happen to possess every possible variation of the 'Unseen Sense' Merit as warranted by the game-lines present (yes, even the stuff from Fan-Lines, if they're included).

                    116(Promethean/Demon)

                    A Promethean throng catch word that a Promethean has been taken by a Cult which seems to be experimenting in them. At the same time a Demon ring is aware of a special piece of infrastructure which the God-machine is working on. Both end up investigating the same Cult/infrastructure at the same time.
                    (The kidnapped Promethean is the linchpin to this occult matrix, and really you don't want to find out what the God-machine will do with this alchemical power).


                    117 (Demon/Mummy) While investigating what the God-machine is, some rumours lead your ring to a Cult worshipping some ancient being/God-like entity that supposedly has access to Occult powers similar to the God-machine's, maybe they can be used against it? But maybe the Cult is another front for the God-machine.

                    The Meret has uncovered some records of an Iremite plan to create a giant machine, something to alter the world in such a way as to seal the judges off from Duat? Perhaps it is still out there, if they can find it? Of course the Judges will want it destroyed, or disabled, while the Mummy's might prefer to take control of it.
                    In this story, the answer to "what is the God-machine?" Is an Iremite device to thwart the Judges.


                    With Demons infiltrating the Cult and Mummy infiltrating the God-machine, who will get what they want in the end?

                    118 (Promethean/Mummy)

                    A Promethean trying to figure out how to be human, a Mummy trying to remember what it meant to be human, crossing paths as both are driven to seek the same thing. An object has appeared, maybe a Relic or maybe an Azothic object, it seems to have some odd properties. People are drawn towards it and report various miracles upon touching it. As a result a small Cult has formed around it which attracts the attention of the Mummy's Cult, while the Azothic Radience attracts Prometheans.

                    Both Sekhem (refined Dedwen) and Azoth (refined Pyros) are in some sense life forces, maybe two sides of the same coin, maybe not, but an Artifact which contains both Sekhem and Azoth would have some very strange properties...

                        117
                        A surprising number of the supernaturally-inclined enjoy illegal underground combat sports. Sometimes they get something out of it, like blood, glory, or Glamour. Sometimes they're looking to recruit. Sometimes they just like the vibe.
                        Whatever their motive, illegal pit fights draw a diverse supernatural crowd and they often develop their own strange culture unique to the scene, depending on who participates.
                        The most common rule is simply not to unduly hurt the regular folks who make up most of the competitors, and using supernatural powers against them is seen as poor sportsmanship. Since the sort of people willing to step into an underground bloodsport tournament are often in demand from a variety of cults and other clandestine groups, there's often some set of (often complicated) rules about who can be approached for recruitment or less wholesome reasons, and ways to mark people to prevent poaching.
                        Naturally, there are more than a few supernatural denizens who think throwing down with a werewolf or stepping up to a literal ogre sounds like a fun night out, but these sorts of matches have their own rules in different, more secluded arenas with a very exclusive viewership. Sometimes this is purely for amusement or individual bragging rights, but in some places it develops into a full-on system of dueling, where monsters can send champions to settle disputes that go beyond their normal political spheres.

                            120 (Changeling/Hunter/any*)
                            Many of the workers at the Office of the Director of National Intelligence are a bit...off, all of the sudden. Some have picked up new tics, others have started spontaneously exhibiting symptoms of everything from Anhedonia to various personality disorders. A few, to the increasing alarm of the American Government, exhibit patterns of behavior that seem completely inhuman. Of course, that's because they are inhuman. More specifically, they're all fetches. Is this the beginning of a ploy to take over the country? The unfortunate consequence of some sort of Arcadian fad for bureaucracy? Part of a scheme by darker powers yet?

                            Whatever the reason, the American government wants (depending on which parts of it you're talking to) answers, a way to stop the apparent incursion, bloody and painful revenge on those responsible, or to sweep all this under the rug so things can go back to business as usual. The Barret Commission and TF:V are torn by the contradictory demands of a panicking national security establishment, while others in the government start wading into the Vigil in a bid to protect themselves or makes sense of the situation. Changelings, for their part, have the opportunity to make an unlikely and powerful ally-If they can prove their worth, and avoid being smothered under the ensuing fallout.
                            *Other supernaturals may be drawn in too, either due to a preexisting investment in the workings of the federal government, or simply because whoever is calling the shots can't actually tell a Fairest from a Forsaken.

                                #121 - GMC/Changeling

                                Your group, whether they be Angels on a mission or regular humans unknowingly doing the God Machine’s will, finds a nearby piece of Infrastructure is glitching out. This particular piece of Infrastructure has been taken over by a portion of The Hedge that has somehow infiltrated the perfect world built by the God Machine. No one other than your group has noticed this — nor do they notice that during these glitches someone wanders, or gets led, into The Hedge. After watching this malfunctioning Infrastructure your group finds that some of those who’ve went into The Hedge come back out. For all intents and purposes they look & act the same as they did prior to entering The Hedge. Over time it becomes clear that True Fae are only taking Clockwork Servitors & humans who have been similarly altered with clockwork machinery to be the perfect pieces of Infrastructure necessary for its plans. The God Machine does not offer any guidance on what to do to remedy the situation because this is not supposed to happen. Ever. Do you look for answers within the safety of other Infrastructure or do you risk entering The Hedge to find out more?

                                        123: (Werewolf/Promethean) A Throng of Prometheans have recently been created, an extremely rare confluence of events leading to Created of different Lineages arising within relatively close proximity to one another. A Spirit, for reasons unknown to even itself, is formed by the sheer volume of essence aligned to the new beings, and decides to join them both out of gratitude for inadvertently creating it, and a desire to continue existing.
                                        Cue road trip as this madcap group meanders across the world trying to figure out this whole 'existing' thing, trying to survive hostile Spirits, Werewolves, Sublimati, Pandorans and Alchemists, and attempting to become human/a Spirit of Humanity.

                                        124: (Princess/Storyteller's Choice) The Troupe comes to be attached to a Noble, enjoying a wonderful life that's difficult to find in this World of Darkness... Until the Noble's magic starts to go out of control, the Enlightened them-self eventually falling into despair, before finally transforming into a monster in a veritable conflagration of tragedy and sorrow. Eventually a group of other Nobles arrive, see the commotion and explain what's happened; the Noble the PC's knew was a member of the Court of Petals. The PCs, upon learning the deal of this Court and its supposed 'Queen' immediately swear vengeance, and set out to avenge their friend/the little one all Kratos-Style.
