    167) ...That Ought To Crawl

    An Ordo researcher has failed to come out of a regularly scheduled torpor on time. Upon investigation, his resting site bears... inconclusive suggestions of some kind of broad-area destructive force that left behind only traces of old blood and tiny bits of cloth.

    The first twist is that they were murdered. The researcher was working on developing some kind of Vitae-friendly necrophage worm, God knows why, which their assistant liberally sprinkled on the torpid corpse.

    The second twist is the reason there's no body is because it got up and left.

            Plot hook 169) A Mekhet elder with an interest in technology has been attempting to use Twilight l Projection as a means of listening in on phone calls and internet messaging. Unfortunately, his experiments have left him Hollow and his reflection is now somehow residing in the internet. The reflection has been sending emails to the elder’s enemies with humiliating details of the elder’s plans and past sins. It also was able to completely wipe out the elder’s bank accounts leaving him near penniless.
            The elder approaches the coterie with two tasks. Find out how to get rid of this reflection by any means possible before it does anything else to hurt him. Second, find a way to get his money back.
            (Quest Hint: The elder has heard rumors from his sire’s second childe’s primogen’s ghoul that there is reclusive member of an obscure Mekhet bloodline called the Lynx in the players’ city. The Lynx may be able to help the coterie...for a price, of course.)

                Plot hook 170)
                The crazy homeless man on the corner is preaching about the end of the world again. He’s been there, every night, saying the same garbage for the last ten years. He must have gotten some of his bible verses confused because tonight he’s talking about blood turning into water. Lo and behold, every Kindred in the city, no matter if drinking a blood pack or straight from the vein, only gets a half of the vitae they consume.
                The next night the man talks about vermin getting the scent of tainted blood. Rats, frogs, bats, and assorted vermin hound the Kindred throughout the city. Somehow they only seem to be attracted to the undead, leaving the kine unharmed. The third night the vampires wake up at night to ticks, lice, and mosquitoes swarming on them. Though small there are a lot of them and they drain what little vitae they managed to procure the last couple nights. As an added bonus the vitae makes the insects resistant to regular bug sprays.
                According to the bible there are 7 plagues left. The Prince wants this homeless prophet brought in for interrogation. The problem is that the Prince has already sent two coteries on this same task. The one survivor of these missions tells that the prophet called upon the name of God and burnt the coterie members to ash with the light of the sun in the middle of the night.

                    171) Prince Werner of Baltimore has sent nearly every Kindred in Washington DC a text, email, letter, or astrally projected message. It simply states “Prince Damien Cruze of Washington DC is hereby considered a traitor to all Kindred. Any who present his person or verified remains to Alexandria Werner, Prince of Baltimore, will receive citizenship, prime hunting territory, and ten million dollars in cash or wire transfer.” Prince Damien has disappeared. Some say he’s gathering allies to declare war on Baltimore. Other’s say he’s hiding from his own subjects.
                    Normally, blood hunts don’t apply to elders, and especially not to princes. They’re the ones who know where all the bodies are buried and have spent their unlives cultivating allies and slaves. Even then, the prince himself has got to be an apex predator to survive as long as he has. There’s no way that anyone is going to go against Prince Damien and win...right? And what did Prince Damien do to piss his neighboring ruler off?

                        172) It doesn't happen often; so when it does, it tends to turn heads. The Prince of the city is unaligned, swearing allegiance to no authority but himself, and somehow he's managed to keep his throne for over five decades! Recently, your coterie has stumbled upon evidence as to how he's able to maintain his authority. The Prince is a war monger, using his influence to manipulate the other covenants into feuding with each other, instead of rallying against him. The Prince knows you've learned his little secret, and has come to you with a proposal. He's finally grown paranoid of the other factions and wants them removed from his city. If the coterie works for him as double agents to tear their covenants (if applicable) down from the inside, he'll reward them handsomely for their efforts. Of course, there's also the possibility of acting as a triple agent and letting the other covenants know about the Prince's ambitious cull.

                            173)
                            Some of the Kindred of the city report an encounter with a darkly cloaked figure. Some of them remember teeth piercing their flesh, becoming paralyzed with euphoria so intense it prevented them from escaping, and dulling their senses to the environment. Afterwards they stumbled to their havens in a daze, awoke the next evening to grogginess, hunger, and tattered memories. One of the victims, a former blood doll who impressed her domitor enough to be granted the embrace, states that the feeling was exactly like the Kiss.
                            So what creature is preying on vampires like the Kindred prey on humans? What does the Prince, the Primogen, the factions want done with this creature if it can even be found?

                                174) The Kindred of the city are being plagued by a cell of hunters that are actually managing to take down elders. Piece by piece, block by block, they are taking the city. How do they do so? This cell is composed of IRS Criminal Investigation Agents*, and they’re going after the Kindred where it hurts the most; their wallets.

                                Shell corporations are exposed for dirty business practices and suddenly the elder who spent five decades building up her fortune is left nearly penniless. Some foreclosure paperwork is mixed up and a primogen’s bedroom wall is demolished by a wrecking ball at noon. Vampires needing a one way ticket out of town find that their credit cards are frozen. If they try to fly they’re told they are listed on the do not fly list and won’t you please come into this room with a window facing the eastern sky until a special agent and his buddies can arrive to sort this whole thing out.

                                These agents are well connected, and are able to use their fellow officers as pawns in their schemes (parallels between parasitic elders and using neonates as pawns springs to mind). Worse, they’re usually holed up in their offices in a heavily guarded government building that also houses the FBI, a component of the national guard, and the sheriff’s office. At home they live in a cop neighborhood with a line of squad cars lining the street. Can the players do anything to stop these hunters? Or do the Kindred of the city give up and admit that they’ve been beaten at their own game?

                                    175) The Village was a horrible movie, but vampires have never been known for having good taste. A group of Kindred have established a village/blood farm in the middle of nowhere. The hundred or so human inhabitants think the year is 1798 and have the technology level to match. The Kindred have accomplished this through taking orphans and drifters and using lots and lots of Dominate to rewrite everyone’s memories and to condition the Two Rules; never bar a Kindly One from entering your home and taking what is theirs through the Kiss, and never enter the Kindly One’s forest.

                                    The coterie stumbles upon this village. The kine seem happy enough. The Kindred seem to get their fill of precious blood very easily. Do they see the village as a sick form of slavery, the best thing since sliced bread, or a necessary evil? If they approach the local werewolves they find that the pack has been on good relations with the Kindred who established the village. They’ve been very interested in finding out if keeping humans at a pre-industrial tech and cultural level affects the spirit world. So what do the players do?
                                    Twist: A changling motley has come to the area and are ready to free the inhabitants of the village from the True Fae’s clutches.
                                    Double Twist: This entire charade has attracted the attentions of a True Fae. Things are about to get Wyrd.

                                                178) New Orleans Revolution no xp
                                                starts off with a covenant of choice but at least one in Lancea Sanctum

                                                You wake up feeling horrible. A wave of hunger rushes over you over you. You smell something in the air and it is familiar and mouth watering. It was the smell of blood. When you turned to the source of the smell there was a dog. You are famished and something compelled to attack the dog and you did. Maybe after a long personal fight with yourself before doing so but you did any way. The hunger was just too much to suffer through. When you attacked the dog fangs appeared in your mouth and you instinctively bite the dog, draining it of all of its blood. You are confused and started going through a surge of emotions. What happened? What am I? What did I just do? These questions and probably more have been going through your head as you drained the blood from the dog. When you finished drinking the blood a door opened. You were hesitant about going through the door at first but in the end it was the only way out and to find out what happened. As you leave your room you see others appear through doors all leading into the same room. You get a quick glimpse at each other before a screen turns on.

                                                “Welcome my newborn children. You have a lot of questions so I will cut to the chase. I am in need of soldiers and that is you my children. Soldiers of darkness. denizens of the shadows. I hope yo learn to enjoy the unlife. You are now a kindred or more commonly known as vampires. My colleagues created you and they will destroy you if you disobey me. You will help me take control of New Orleans one step at a time. In due time we will meet in person and until then do not ask any unnecessary questions. Before you are five files, each containing information about one of the five covenants. My colleagues will help you get accepted into the covenant of your choice, though I suggest atlas one of you choose Lancea Sanctum. Those who do so will have the opportunity to be the most influential piece to the entire plan. Choose your covenant wisely. You have 48 hours to meet one of my colleagues or you will be terminated. You are free to use this place as a haven. Always find shelter to go to sleep when the sun comes up.” after the message was done the screen turns off and the lights turn on revealing that the room was a dungeon and there was a door that opened above to your new home.

                                                    179) Dogs.
                                                    Well it's happened. Somehow the Curse has been spread beyond the human form. Maybe it was an elder's ghouled pets that died with vitae in their system. Maybe it was some Ordo science project. Either way a pack of feral dogs are undead. They haven't been hurting humans too much, preferring the vitae of other canines, similar to how the Kindred disdain the blood of animals. However stray dogs and even pets have started to go missing or have been found throughout the city torn apart. The dog population is down and the vampire dogs are starting to turn their sights on better tasting Vitae, from the Kindred themselves.
                                                    These creatures are pack hunters and a lone Kindred would make easy prey. Some of the creatures have displayed physical disciplines and even Obsfucation. Worst of all are rumors that the canines numbers are growing. Perhaps they've found a way to Embrace.

                                                            181) Can the Strix fall in love? The idea itself is sheer lunacy, but recent circumstances has made it a pressing question. One of your friends/allies (mortal or Kindred) has recently acquired a stalker. Their email, phone, and social media are regularly bombarded with bloody, disturbed love poems, both written and voiced. Though the mannerisms and word choice remain the same, each few days it's a different voice. Sometimes pictures are sent, claiming to be from the stalker. Sometimes they're of the friend in a variety of vulnerable or private situations; other times, they claim to be of the stalker themselves. In the latter cases, those faces show up the next morning on the news as particularly gruesome suicides. More importantly to you is the fact that in all cases, the stalker's eyes appear a cruel, avian yellow.

                                                            Has one of the Birds of Dis actually developed affection for another being, expressing it in its own twisted, monstrous way? Is this just some exercise in torture? Or is there something far more subtle and sinister afoot? Better figure out fast, because things are escalating; already the ally has walked into their home to find some poor soul beaten, bound, and drugged, tied up with a little ribbon and a note telling them to "Have fun!".
