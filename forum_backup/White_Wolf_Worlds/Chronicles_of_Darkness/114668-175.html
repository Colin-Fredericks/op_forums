                Do Postcognition spells also exist in the past you're perceiving?
                So for example, say you utilise Postcognition to perceive a scene where there's a Mage present. Will they (in the past) detect its formation in Peripheral Mage Sight?
                As a continuiation from this, can that past version of the Mage with the use of the correct Arcana dispel your Postcognition spell? Or utilise Focused Mage Sight to determine when you're casting from?
                Furthermore, as the future is unwritten, if that Mage understands that element of temporal physics, they'll likely understand at that point they're existing within an alternate history, and that their actions are so important that they'll echo through time to that future point.

                The most important factor to me though is that if Mages in the past can detect Postcognition, how dangerous is it for a Mage to use Postcognition on any scene where they know a Mage will be present?
                Say for instance you scry the casting of a concealment spell which a Mage seeks to use in order to hide his Sanctum from detection.
                His peripheral Mage sight will trigger, alerting him to the fact that someone is likely attempting to determine what he's doing, even if he doesn't have the Arcana to perceive the Postcognition spell specifically.
                As such, he may completely change his plans to protect his sanctum, likely changing events for a significant period of time after that, triggering the butterfly effect, and quite possibly leaving the present that the Mage exists in radically different.

                Even if you interpret the caster themselves as benefitting from the protection against Temporal Retcons formed from their own spells, you're still potentially causing horrific consequences for the present.

                With a simple use of Apprentice Time magic (Temporal Sympathy needed to view more than Unchanged pasts), you could though simply casting the spell redefine the present in such drastic ways such that you, and hundreds of thousands, perhaps even millions or billions of people might not even exist (albeit perhaps replaced with alternate history versions).
