    Werewolves Vs. Demons

    07-26-2014, 01:55 AM
    If you've been paying attention to the updates on the Idigam Chronicles, but the a Urathra are getting some very nice updating and streamlining of their systems and splats. As I was reading I was thinking that the WoD's most deadly predators versus its most resourceful and elusive spies would make for an interesting game of cat and mouse but...

    The two groups have few inherent conflicts of interest. The Forsaken, at last, tend towards the same territory (Cities) but other than that, they're not competing for resources, have different priorities, and want different things.

    So... can anyone give me any ideas on how to start a war?

    The only one that I can come up with is the one I've rejected: a false flag operation by a third party that gets them at each other s throats. I don't like that idea as it requires the involved parties to act like idiots: too much risk of railroading or the players derailing the plot. What are legitimate gripes that would get werewolves and demons to put knives at each others' throats?

          Easy: Territory.

          Werewolves are inherently territorial, and demons have to scrap for what they get, so pieces of suborned infrastructure are not going to be surrendered easily. Throw in the God Machine and spirits deliberately making things worse when they realize a conflict is a brewing and you got a nice turf war already set up.

          Also consider the value of people. Demons prey on humans in ways most Forsaken would have issues with, and werewolves hoard people demons might find desirable. None of that is touching on personal aspects, both of which demons and werewolves have reason to value dearly and fight for ferociously.

                  You have to monkey with the setting assumptions a bit for this one, but what if Loci are actually Occult Matrices (or vice versa)? Think about it, they're basically both just Essence factories. The main differences are just who made them, and how they view the site. To the Uratha, they are sacred places created by manipulating the surrounding environment in such a way that it resonates with the desired emotion or concept, and then strengthen that resonance until it creates a wellspring of that energy. To the God-Machine, they are tools created by placing the proper infrastructure in the desired location so that, via natural processes, the location produces the desired output. Now, which party is right, and which party just happens to be using the right methods but misunderstanding why it works is a question for the ST. It could be that the Uratha prescribe false religious significance to what is basically just a process of creating the proper infrastructure. On the other hand, it could be that the God-Machine doesn't even realize what it's doing is a spiritual process, it's computer mind only understands that A + B = Essence. Maybe both are true to a certain extent. Either way, if the Unchained realize they can more safely harvest Aether at the Uratha's spiritual sites, and/or the Uratha realize the Unchaned have been stealing some kind of energy from "naturally occurring" Loci, you've got your reason to start a war right there.
