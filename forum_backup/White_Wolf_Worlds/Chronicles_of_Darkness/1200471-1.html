            Hey all,

            So I spent some time building this stuff to put some of the discussion from this thread into practice. I'm posting this here with Vent0's permission (as well as considerable help in proofreading and brainstorming), in the hope that others can get some use out of it or use it as inspiration for further work. Much thanks to Vent0 for his help.

            What is an Artificer?

            A subset of Sleepwalkers who are capable of creating Imbued Items and often have additional abilities related to crafting.

            Creating an Artificer

            To create an Artificer, use all the rules for creating a starting Chronicles of Darkness character. Additionally, Artificers start the game with the Sleepwalker, Artificer and Schema User merits and one dot each in the Create Item and Item Resonance merits.

            Sidebar: Learning Artifice

            Artificers normally start off as Sleepwalkers and slowly gain proficiency in Imbuement under a teacher, who can be either a fellow Sleepwalker or a Mage with similar abilities. Those mentors normally have Schemata of their own, as well as a store of various items.

            In those cases, the first ability to be learned is usually Schema User, allowing for the reproduction of existing items. From there, they learn Item Resonance to both improve their facility with crafting and get more use out of using the Items themselves. From there, Artificer first allows for the recharging of items from a Hallow, and when combined with Imbue Item then allows for creating Items of one’s own.

            In general, the mentoring process (or continuing education when creating a ‘base’ artificer) can be modeled using the Mentor Merit, while later students can be modeled using the Retainer Merit.

            Artificers, Spell Control and Relinquishment

            Artificers are not Mages and, as such, do not normally have Spell Control for themselves. However, Artificers are Sleepwalkers and as such have some limited ability to hold spells within their souls as listed under “Shield Maidens and Flag Bearers” (Mage, p.303-304). An Artificer has a Connected sympathy to themselves and, as such, has up to three Imbued spells held within their souls, which can be increased as normal through the Banner-Bearer merit. Additionally, many experienced Artificers have apprentices and assistants who are likewise Sleepwalkers and capable of holding spells for the Artificers if they agree.

            Unlike Mages, however, Artificers must be able to create an item within their existing Control level. They do not get the option of increasing Reach effects as Mages do: If they have used all three available slots, they must first destroy or relinquish one of their existing items before making a new one.

            Additionally, Artificers lack the supernal knowledge to relinquish a spell in any but the most costly ways. When they use a Willpower point to relinquish a spell, add the Item’s highest Arcanum rating to the amount of Reach over the limit they have gone when determining the Paradox pool. Using a Willpower dot allows for safe relinquishment.

            Artificers and Paths

            Sleepwalker Artificers, not being Mages, do not have a Path and cannot, by default, create any magical items reliant on having a Path, such as those using the Apocalypse spell. In some cases, such as with Proximi, Artificers do have a Path and follow its rules instead.

            Merits for Artificers

            The following Merits are intended for (though not strictly limited to) Artificers and include the base Artificer ability.

            In addition, certain other Merits available to Sleepwalkers are particularly appropriate. These include Banner Bearer (Mage 2e, p. 305), Relic Attuned (Mage 2e, p.306) and Cup Holder (found here).

            Artificer (••••)
            Prerequisites: Sleepwalker or Awakened
            Effect: Your character has access to the Imbue Item Attainment despite lacking the requisite ability in the Prime Arcanum and gains the ability to Imbue any spell they are capable of casting. Furthermore, they may substitute their rating in the Arcanum used for Prime when rolling to create the Item. If your character ever achieves 4 dots in Prime, they may choose to lose this Merit under the Sanctity of Merits.

            Additionally, your character may charge Imbued Items and Artifacts with Mana from any source available to them. This Merit explicitly allows the use of Oblations at a Hallow, provided that the resulting Mana is immediately used to charge an Imbued Item or Artifact. Your character may use a similar process to expend Mana in order to create Imbued Items in the first place.

            Item Sensitivity (•)
            Prerequisites: Awakened or Artificer
            Effect: Your character gains the ability to sense magical items, be they Enhanced, Imbued, Artifacts or any other type of object imbued with magical power. For Mages, this manifests as an additional trigger to their Peripheral Mage Sight. For Artificers, it counts as the Unseen Sense merit attuned to Imbued Items, Enhanced Items and Artifacts. Taking the Spooked Condition additionally gives a hint as to how this item works.

            Create Item (•,••• or •••••)
            Prerequisites: Artificer or Awakened
            Effect: Choose an Arcanum. When creating Imbued Items, your character has effective dots in this Arcanum equal to half their dots in this merit, rounded up. This allows them to create Imbued Items (including the applicable Attainments) of this Arcanum.

            If the character possesses any dots in the relevant Arcanum, refund a commensurate amount of experiences for this Merit (1 for one dot, 3 for two dots, and all 5 for 3 dots).

            Specialist Crafter (• or ••)
            Prerequisites: At least one effective dot in the chosen spell’s Arcanum
            Effect: Your character has a specialized ability to Imbue certain spells into Items.

            At two dots, choose a single spell no more than one dot higher than the character’s effective Arcanum rating. She has the ability to Imbue that specific spell as though all her Arcana were one dot higher. At four dots, she may instead choose a spell up to two dots higher as though her Arcana were two dots higher.

            This Merit does not allow for the creation of Imbued Items that use the Imperial Practices, nor allow for any Arcanum’s effective dots to be above 5. If your character increases her Arcana ratings such that she can Imbue the spell on her own, she may choose to lose the merit and be refunded the experiences.

            Item Resonance (•-•••••)
            Prerequisites: Artificer or Awakened
            Effect: Your character’s soul has far more facility with Imbued Items than most of her kind.

            When creating or using an Imbued item, increase her effective Gnosis by the dots in this merit. This may not push a Mage above 10 effective dots.

            Empower Item (••)
            Prerequisites: Ability to Imbue Item
            Effect: When crafting an Item, your character may assign additional successes to increase the effective Gnosis of any user of the item, up to a maximum of their own Gnosis dots when creating Items. This may not push the user’s effective Gnosis beyond 5 dots.
            Note: When taking the Imbued Item Merit, this activation bonus should be bought the same manner as extra Mana: Each effective Gnosis increase is worth an additional Merit dot.

            Pattern Sacrifice (••)
            Prerequisites: Sleepwalker
            Effect: Your characters gains the ability to Scour your Pattern for Mana in the same way a Gnosis 1 Mage could. He may then immediately channel this Mana to an available container, or spend it in place of Willpower to activate any supernatural ability you possess. If your character does not immediately start spending Mana, it is lost unless he has a merit such as Cup Holder that allows you to retain it.

            Schema User (•)
            Effect: Your character may use Schemata: A set of instructions to build an Imbued Item that can makes use of a particular spell, even when you lack the ability to ordinarily craft Imbued Items. This requires all the normal rolls to create Imbued Items, though your character may do so as though he possessed five dots in both Prime and the relevant Arcanum. Attainments may not be incorporated into the spell.

            As a special rule, Artifacts always count as Schemata for the spells that they can cast. They are thus particularly prized by those Artificers who hold them.

            Schema (•+)
            Prerequisite: Schema User
            Effect: You possess a Schema and can use it to reproduce items. The cost of a Schema is equal to the number of unique Arcana used, plus one for each Arcanum over three dots.

            Reproduce Schema (••)
            Prerequisites: Schema User, ability to Imbue Items.
            Effect: You may reproduce a Schema as though it were an Imbued Item, using the same rules as though you were creating a Schema of your own. Doing so requires you to have at least one (effective) dot in all Arcana that exist within the Schema, as well as access to the Schema itself.

            Craft Schema (••)
            Prerequisites: Reproduce Schema, five effective Arcanum dots in the use of at least one spell, effective Gnosis of 3+ when creating Imbued Items.
            Effect: At the pinnacle of crafting ability, you gain the ability to create new Schemata. This requires an effective Arcanum rating of five dots in the spell used to create the Schemata and is done in the same way as creating an Imbued Item, with the following changes:
            • Schemata never hold Mana.
            • The roll time is eight hours.
            • Schemata can never be released for a point of Willpower.



            Item Enhancer (•+)
            Prerequisites: Artificer orAwakened
            Effect: Your character gains a floating set of points that can be used to create Enhanced Items equal to their dots in this Merit. These provide a bonus to a tool, a point of Structure, or a point of Durability to any physical object.

            Your may reassign points by spending one hour and paying a point of Mana or Willpower (as desired) for each point so reassigned. Unlike ordinary Enhanced Items, your character must pay a point of Mana or Willpower to activate the bonus for the scene (which only works for you). If the Item carrying the tool bonus is destroyed, the bonus returns to your character for assignment later. Finally, a character who loses access to an Enhanced Item can disenchant it remotely, losing the dots (and regaining them through Sanctity of Merits, the dots cannot be returned to Item Enhancer until the start of the next story).

            This Merit can go over five dots, though no tool may gain more than five supernatural bonus dice through any combination of supernatural powers that include this merit.

            Arcanum Enhancement (•)
            Prerequisites: Item Enhancer, ability to Imbue Items
            Effect: Choose an Arcanum. You gain the ability to assign your floating dots from Item Enhancer to persistent spell effects from that Arcanum as per the Enhanced Item merit. Doing so requires the ability to Imbue that spell into an item.

            Technomagic (••-••••)
            Prerequisites: Item Enhancer
            Effect: Your character is especially skilled at enhancing items she has built herself.

            At two dots, your character no longer needs to spend Mana or Willpower to activate the bonus to Enhanced Items she has created herself.

            At four dots, when assigning floating bonus dice from Item Enhancer, if your character assigns the dice to an object they created themselves, waive an amount of the dot cost equal to half her dice pool when creating it (rounded down), to a minimum of one dot. She still need sufficient total floating dots to enhance the item in the first place.

            Example: Alice wants to make an enhanced computer: She rolls Computer + Intelligence to build the computer (or any other viable Crafting dice pool: Let's pretend this is the right one). She's got Computer 3, Intelligence 4, and 4 dots in Item Enhancer. She currently possesses no items that have been enhanced.

            Her dice pool is 7 dice, which translates to a discount of 3 dots. Thus, she can give a +4 bonus to her crafted item, but only use one of her floating dots in Item Enhancer. If she only had 3 dots in Item Enhancer, she could only give a +3 bonus.

            Let's say Alice does make her computer and imbues it with a +4 bonus. She now has 3 floating dots of Enhanced Item left. If she wanted to do the same thing to another computer, she could only enhance it by up to 3 points, as that is the maximum she could now Imbue.

            Imbue Attainment (•)
            Prerequisites: Ability to Imbue Items
            Effect: Choose an Arcanum for which your character can craft Imbued Items (including through the Create Item merit). For this Arcanum, she can then create Attainment Items, treating each Attainment as though it were a spell (including the potential to Imbue higher-level Attainments through Specialist Crafter). Additionally, for the purpose of this Merit, Mage Sight is considered an Attainment available at one dot.

            Attainment Items function and are made - and costed, if bought - as Imbued Items, with the following exceptions and clarifications:
            • Attainments can be imbued into items at varying Arcana ratings (and count as a spell of that rating). This then determines the Merit dot cost of the Item, its difficulty in crafting, and its effects when used. An Attainment’s minimum rating is always equal to its prerequisite Arcanum rating, and its maximum rating is equal to your own rating in an Arcanum (including any Applicable bonuses from
            • Attainment Items do not normally activate: Instead, they grant their ability to anyone carrying them, though the first time in any scene they benefit from the power drains a point of Mana from the item in addition to any other costs - and the item fails to activate if it is empty.
            • The Attainment Item’s Mana may be used to pay for any costs used in the Attainment.



            Further, some specific rules exist for particular Attainments:
            • The effects of Mage Sight differ by path. Possession of this Item does not grant Peripheral Mage Sight.
            • Eyes of the Death, Mind’s Eye and Spirit Eyes work as normal for those with Mage Sight. Those who do not possess Mage Sight instead act as though having Peripheral Mage Sight (for purposes of this item only), and can gain the equivalent of Active Mage Sight (for purpose of this item only) by spending a further point of Mana.
            • Improved Pattern Restoration functions as normal (for physical restoration only) for non-Mages despite Pattern Restoration ordinarily being unavailable. The Item’s Mana may explicitly be spent for Pattern Restoration.
            • Targeted Summoning only summons Supernal Entities from your Path. If you imbue one of your Ruling Arcana, this instead grants the ability to summon an entity at all.
            • The Imbue Item and Create Rote Attainments cannot be made into Attainment Items.



            Path Mimicry (••)
            Prerequisites: Ability to Imbue Items
            Effect: Choose a Path that you do not have. Your character gains the ability to briefly mimic this Path. This requires contact with a strong sympathetic link to said Path - such as a Mage, Proximus, or an appropriate Artifact, Aedes or Verge. By spending a willpower point, your character can then forge an artificial connection to the Path, allowing him to Imbue an Item as though he possessed it. This connection lasts until the end of the story or until your character has finished creating the item, whichever comes first.
