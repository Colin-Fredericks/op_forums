        The main thing about Discordians, aside from their madness, is the fact that they want to push for change. All Sirens want to change the world in order to prevent The Deluge, but a Discordian is willing to take charge. Freshwaters want to experience life and be out with the people, but that only goes so far. Tropicans have that passion and force that can drive for change, but they are more focused on invested change. Gardenians are preservers who actively go against unnecessary change. Polarians are thinkers who come up with plans rather than being right in there. Vaporians are agents who slink in secrecy, but they operate differently than the Discordians.

        Discordians would consider themselves willing pawns in the game that Sirens play against a force they cannot predict. They are the vanguard who push forward in order to allow their brothers and sisters an opening. They are the first stand, taking up arms against the Flensers or the Furies or whatever decides to go at their Shoal. They are the fall guys, willing to damage their own reputation if it means that the real perpetrator can act without notice. In a sense, the Discordians are fanatics willing to throw themselves as martyrs if it means that the "good guys win". That is their internal logic. Who cares if everyone thinks that they're insane? Let them watch so the Vaporians can come in without notice. Let them be in awe so the Polarians can plan for a little longer. Even in a Shoal of only Discordians, they find themselves able to operate in all the chaos, splashing around until their ripples become waves.

        A possible inspiration for the Discordians is Ophelia from Hamlet or anyone who invokes the image of Ophelia. Discordians have been broken with the knowledge that there have been Deluges that have washed away worlds. Discordians are solidly aware of the dangers of the world; the Flensers hunger for the rejuvenating flesh of the Sirens, the Furies are burning with Nature's indignant rage, and Echidna whispers to the Discordians' brethren. This madness is like a safeguard, something that allows them to cope with the world.

        Now that I think about it, don't the CofD-friendly gamelines offer some sort of "passive"? Beasts have Birthrights which allow them some sort of benefit based on their Family, Demons have Conditions based on their Agenda, Vampires have Clan Banes, and Werewolves have Auspice Benefits. Sirens do have have an advantage when they enter their Favored Water, but that's a little basic and is universal, like the favored prey of Werewolves. Should the Waters also offer some sort of extra oomph? It'll help differentiate the Waters and add emphasis to having a variety of Waters.

        Say, the Freshwaters, because they thrive in the presence of others, gains some sort of bonus when they are within a crowd of people they know. And the Tropicans, because they put in their all when it comes to their passions, have stronger specialties. The Discordians could have some sort of benefit when it comes to their Lucidity, being made of stronger stuff or being protected by their madness.

                                        Now then, I believe we all were to brainstorm for the Y Splats? I have an idea on the representative animals/logos, including Reapers ideas for them.

                                        Buoyancy: Clownfish. It's a phonetic pun (they're the tricksters and thieves) with the image of a clownfish resting in the middle of an anemonie. There's tentacles that surround the clownfish that has dents or marks that look like faces, with maybe a fish or two caught in them with evil looking faces on them. I feel a good analogy for calling them clownfish is that "They know how to get allies and if their enemies try hurting them, they can get their allies, temporary or not, to get them in their place". It could also work as an idea of luring an enemy to where others more capable could get them in the Clownfish's place.

                                        Wisdom: Octopus. As Reaper said, it's fitting because they're the most Intelligent animal. I imagine as an image for them, it shows an octopus facing forwards with four of it's tentacles in some graceful pattern behind it. In it's front, it's two innermost tentacles hold a book and a scroll respectively. It's outer two tentacles help in holding the two things, but they're both damaged, showing how knowledge can be a bane as much as a benefit.

                                        Conflict: Swordfish. I imagine their symbol to be a simple show of three swords. One on the right is a pirate's cutlass, rusted in spots and with a red cloth over the handle. On the left is a graceful rapier, more akin to an ornament than a weapon, but it represents the nobility the Current of Conflict holds.The middle sword looks odd, unconventional to be a sword as there's no visible hilt to hold. However, the reason for this is because the middle blade is a Swordfish skull for their iconography.

                                        Lethe/Mankind's Victory: Horse. It's very unorthodox for a game set about water and marine animals, but there's a few reasons I chose this. For one, I imagine the symbol to be like a human heraldry of a wealthy family (perhaps befitting Mankind's Victory). For another, there's stories of how Horses and the Ocean have been together. Poseidon had created horses from the waves and made fish-horse hybrids known as Hippocampi. Kelpie and Kappas are both known to take the forms of horses to lure travellers to ride on them before diving into the water to drown them. And finally, the Horse is known as a symbol for Pride, that it will do what it wants if no control is on it. This fits Lethe throwing away control for their desires to screw nature and letting their pride do all their decision making for them.

                                        Styx/Primordial Darkness: Shark. I see Styx as the people who'd see themselves as the Apex Predator after becoming a Siren and wanting to go out against the petty humans. Having the Shark as an image is fitting for them.

                                        Phelegethon/Dark Passions: Dolphin. I know, also unorthodox. But I tell you now, they are assholes. These animals basically drag other animals or fish down to the bottom of the ocean just to rape them. They grope people, they kill porpoises because it looks like their children. Heck, dolphins kill their own children, have LOADS of STDs and kill pufferfish to get high off of them. They are evil as hell and the ways they are evil is fitting for Dark Passions.

                                                        Hmm, it could be that each Current helps a Siren refine her ability to Trace. I wrote before that Tracing functions similarly to the Psychometry merit in that a Siren may Trace by asking a specific question and reviewing the past of what she is Tracing. Perhaps the Currents influence how well the Sirens may Trace.

                                                        For example, a Siren of Conflict would be able to Trace things that are muddled and messy. They enjoy walking through old battlegrounds because they can play entire wars in their head through Tracing. They can see where a soldier lost his arm or where a cannon was placed to fire on the enemy. This extends to things such as crowded areas or national disasters that cause mass chaos. Most Sirens are unable to focus their Trace in order to navigate through this maze of disorder. For a Siren of Conflict, it's a bit trivial to understand what has happened where.

                                                        A Siren of Wisdom has an "enhanced" Trace. When a Siren Traces an office building, does she see the files being handled and passed from employee to employee? Does she see the tired look in the worker's eye after working overtime for an entire week? Sirens of Wisdom catch each and every detail down to the very hairs that cling to the intern's shirt after having a wild one-on-one with his higher-up in the restroom. A Siren of Wisdom sees all of these minute details in her Trace.

                                                        A Siren of Harmony has a knack for putting 1 and 1 together. When she decides to Trace, her mind wanders to another place, an outlying memory of the world that connects to what she is Tracing. Effectively, a Siren of Harmony is able to answer two questions when Tracing, lining up two different concepts and bringing them to the point in which they crossed over. The bomber who devastated a shelter downtown? He was on a train and made contact with an informant who gave him the information needed to formulate his plan. In a Siren of Harmony's mind, the lines connect with little issue.

                                                        A Siren of Healing goes to where it all started, to the first transaction made. She Traces two feuding families and sees their exchanges through the years, gradually accelerating back to the first moment that a dirty look was shot nearly a century ago. She Traces a doll that belongs to a missing child and sees the moment that she leaves the house as well as the abuse she had to endure over the years that eventually led to her running away. A Siren of Healing doesn't just see the branches when she Traces; she goes down to the very roots.

                                                        A Siren of Buoyancy is able to Trace in such a way that it allows her to see the secrets that lie in plain sight. The Siren Traces back through the fire and sees the face of the man through his mask. She sees through the tinted windows of his car and sees the empty can of gasoline and a crude map of possible marks. The Siren Traces the remains of an old tree and sees the trail of illegal dumping that leads to a signature being forged on a document. The Siren may not see the entire picture, but she sees enough to understand what has been undergoing in the shadows.

                                                        In order to balance the fact that they do not receive an associated Conclusion and no special effects for their Trace, the Castaways are able to Trace without spending Pneuma once per scene; their spectator nature makes it easier to see the Visions.

                                                        Maybe all of this could be associated with the Currents.
