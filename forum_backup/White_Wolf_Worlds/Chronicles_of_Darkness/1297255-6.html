            So, to again bring this back to an actual homebrew thread, I have added some more to the idea:

            Themes
            Redemption

            Salvation

            Mood
            Adventure
            The life of a hero is rarely boring, their connection to the dream draws them to hotspots of excitement as well as repeatedly bringing calls to action to their doorstep.

            Danger
            Heroes very existence cause the world around them to grow more dangerous, they are always at risk if harm and their loved ones doubly so as they don't have the heroes defenses.

            What is a Hero
            Defenders of humanity since time immemorial, heroes are those individuals with a connection to the Primordial Dream that drives them to fight evil and protect the world.

            Just a rough couple sentences, but a start. The Themes really need input from Camilla before they can take shape.
