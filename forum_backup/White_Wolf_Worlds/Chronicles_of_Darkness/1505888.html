    Dreamers (Minor Sleepwalker Template)

    03-20-2023, 04:16 AM
    Dreams are not just dreams. They are but the surface of endless ocean. Dive deeper, and you'll get deep into your soul, soul of humanity, soul of universe. Most people won't ever do that, but you can do it. You can find wisdom here, and power, and your worst nightmares - all without ever leaving the bed. You can find friendship there from powers that be, and you can change the world.

    But can you wake up?

    All people dream, but some people dream deeper. They can dream into the Dreaming - that's how they usually call Astral Realm - not only their Oneiros, but even Temenos and Anima Mundi. They call themselves Dreamers - not imaginative, but precise - and they explore Astral for thousands of different reasons. Many of them never understand that they do something else than experience exceptionally bright and colorful dreams, but those who do can acquire power and knowledge, if they are willing to risk their minds and souls.

    Dreamers often born as such; many of them report seeing deep, colorful dreams for their entire lives and cannot remember the point where such dreams started. Many others unlocked their dreaming abilities after successfully fighting off neurological diseases; with full recovery came realisation that their sleeping life has changed dramatically. A noticeable minority of Dreamers are, in fact, never awake - some people in comas retreated so deep inside their own minds that they came through on the other side; most of those quickly adapt to their new life, though many of them tend to think that they are actually dead and in some kind of weird afterlife.

    Most Dreamers are just normal people with unique and interesting life in dreams; since Astral is huge, chances of running into another Dreamer inside are very low. Sometimes they do, though, and then they form communities; some Dreamers spend a lot of their time searching for other Dreamers, both in waking world and in Astral. Many of them recognize that they are unusual, but since their powers are limited to Astral, they don't really try to use them to their benefit. Only a minority of Dreamers recognize they can invade dreams and Oneiroi of other people, stealing their knowledge and secrets, and even changing their minds; this same minority often find themselves dugging too deep for their own good and understanding they are not alone out there.

    Of course, it's not all stealing corporate secrets from mind of unknowing CEOs. Some Dreamers are trying to use their powers for the good of humanity. There is a lot which can be done by person with access to collective soul of humanity, and those Dreamers find themselves at battle with humanity's worst aspects personified, trying to change their domains or at least contain their influence. Some Dreamers take a different approach and try to influence powerful people in their dreams, subtly tugging their mind in preferable directions. Of course, this is harder than it seems, and sometimes they find that those people have supernatural patrons of their own, who may not like that their puppets started to have second thoughts - and sometimes they can track down source of that pesky influence.

    Out of all other supernatural beings, Dreamers are most familiar with the Awakened. Orders always have a place for another Sleepwalker, and Dreamers are more useful than most of them, being a great help for mages whose Obsessions involve Astral in one way or another. Dreamers also of great interest to Aletheans, since they seem to be more likely to Awaken than normal people, and when they do, their Awakening always is a Supernal Journey, starting as regular dream, where they go through the Oneiros, Temenos and Anima Mundi, before crossing the Abyss and going into Supernal - somehow recreating theoretical "old way" of Awakening from Time Before. This is a great risk for them, though, because if they fail at the Awakening, they never wake up, their consciousness shattered from trauma of failure, falling into Abyss, or at best, forever locked within Astral with no way out.

    While most Dreamers are just normal humans, in their dreams they can be mighty heroes, having the power to challenge even the most terrible nightmares. There are tightly knit groups of Dreamers carefully watching for signs of monsters haunting other people's dreams. When they do find such signs, they investigate such dreams and try to ambush said horrors. Since Dreamers are able to wake up at any time, and can quickly arrive back via guided meditation, they are uniquely suited for harassing their enemies, slowly draining them of willpower to fight. This often pits them against dangerous enemies, such as True Fae, Horrors from Primordial Dream, more predatory and callous Lost, and many others. This fight becomes much more dangerous when Dreamers fight out that their enemies are able to find them in waking world - something they try to avoid at all costs.

    Minor Template: Dreamers
    • Dreamers can enter their Oneiros at any time during their sleep (or meditation) with Resolve + Composure roll. They do not need a place of power for this. They count as having Gnosis 0 for Estatic Wind damage.
    • Dreamers can also leave Astral at any time with instant action and Resolve + Composure roll. They do receive Shaken Condition if they escape from dangerous situation in this way.
    • Dreamers are lucid in their own dreams, and instantly aware if someone invades them. With Resolve + Composure roll they can instantly wake up.
    • The dream form of Dreamers is more powerful than usual person's. Their Power, Finesse and Resistance use higher stats for calculation, like mages do.
    • Dreamers always have Resonant Condition for all Goetia. They can willingly shift it to Open Condition for chapter with successful meditation.
    • Dreamers can gain Arcane Experience from their journeys and quests in Astral. They can spend it on purchasing Dreamer Merits (see below).
    • All Dreamers have Sleepwalker Merit for free.
    Dreamer Merits
    Some Dreamers manifest supernatural powers, but for many of them their ability to dream deep is they only supernatural power they have. Within it they are able to manifest a wide array of abilities.

    Merit: Light Sleeper (•)
    Requirement: Dreamer; Not having Heavy Sleeper merit
    Effect: Dreamer has especially light sleep, which is useful, when somebody tries to sneak on you while you are asleep. When someone tries to enter a room where Dreamer is sleeping, or move around silently, Dreamer can roll Wits + Composure - Stealth of intruder; on success, Dreamer is wide awake and ready for action.

    Merit: Heavy Sleeper (•)
    Requirement: Dreamer; Not having Light Sleeper merit
    Effect: Dreamer has especially heavy sleep, which is useful, when you must stay asleep, no matter what happens around. When something happens around which can wake up Dreamer from his sleep, Dreamer can roll Resolve + Composure - penalty assigned by ST; on success, Dreamer is still sleeping.

    Merit: Dream Obsession (•)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Some Dreamers are more like Awakened they might one day become. They are not just having a fun adventures in Astral; they are obsessed with it's mysteries and secrets, risking their minds and souls to understand it all. Dreamers get Obsession (Persistent) Condition related to particular Astral Mystery and receive Arcane Beats from researching or experimenting with it.

    Merit: Powerful Psyche (• to •••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: The more experienced Dreamer is, the more powerful his mind and soul are. Dreamer gets bonus equal to dots in this merit for resisting breaking points while in Astral; he also subtracts his dots in this merit from dice pools of entities attempting to influence his mind (primarily, but not always, Goetia), and adds it to his Dream Health.

    Merit: Dream Power (• to •••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: For those Dreamers, their dreams are power fantasies, where they are more stronger, tougher and graceful than in real life. For each dot in this Merit, raise Power, Finesse or Resistance of their Dream Form by one dot.

    Merit: Dream Mien (• to •••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamers who have this Merit can look like creatures of the dream, beautiful and terrible and fantastic - unlike anything they look like in waking world. Dreamer receives bonus equal to dots in this Merit on any Social rolls that would be influenced by his looks; his enemies receive equal penalty on any attempts to find him in waking world by using his dream form as source of information.

    Merit: Dream Will (• to •••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamer has untapped reserves of willpower within his dreams. Dreamer gets additional amount of Willpower equal to dots in this Merit, which he can spend only on actions in Astral and powering his Dream Powers (see below).

    Merit: Dream Gaze (•••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamer is able to gaze upon dreams of other people. By looking at person and successfully rolling Wits + Empathy - person's Composure, he is able to get one piece of information about person per success:
    • His Vice and Virtue (or analogues).
    • His Aspirations and Obsessions.
    • His ability to sleep and see dreams.
    • His supernatural abilities connected to dreaming and mind control (one ability revealed per success).
    • What kind of dream did person had during his last sleep.
    • How many Willpower person have left.
    Merit: Communal Dream (•••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamer may take his friends with him. By sleeping or meditating together with group of people (upper limit is determined by ST and should give progressive penalties), Dreamer may take them on a Astral journey, arriving with them in "communal hallway" created from their joined dreams, from which group can move in any Astral direction. Dreamer is able to take inhuman creatures with him, as long as they have ability to see dreams (by default it is suggested that ephemeral entities lack such ability, and Goetia can be "taken along"). Their ability to enter Temenos and Anima Mundi is determined on case-to-case basis.

    Merit: Dream Power (•)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Powerful Dreamers often develop their own powers - often by making pacts with friendly Goetia in exchange for services in waking world, but sometimes just by finding occult secrets which work in Astral. Dreamer can purchase single supernatural power, constructed like hunter's Endowment; this power only works in regular dreams and Astral. This Merit can be bought multiple times, purchasing different Dream Powers.

    Note: Cost is reduced compared to Endowment cost, because it only works in Astral. In case Dream Power allows to have benefits extending to waking world (such as restoring Willpower, or getting beneficial mental Conditions), consider adding another dot of cost.

    Merit: Dream Influence (•• or ••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamer has power over sphere of influence in Astral. For two dots in this Merit, Dreamer has one dot of Influence (Chosen Domain); for four dots in this Merit, he has two. Instead of Essence, Willpower is spent for using said Influence. Chosen Domain should be relevant to concept and lived experience of character; animal trainer who especially loves dogs can have Influence (Dogs), while former general can have Influence (War). Additionally, Dreamer has bonus equal to half dots in this Merit for interactions with Goetia also possessing this Influence.

    Merit: Dream Citizen (•)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamer has deep familiarity with chosen realm of Astral - deep enough that it's denizens recognize him as one of their own and respect positively to requests of assistance and help. Within the chosen realm (which should be of reasonable size, approved by ST), Dreamer has +2 bonus to all social interaction with local Goetia; he also always has first impression in Social Manuevering with them one step higher, and removes one Door.

    Merit: Dream Familiar (•• or ••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamers may befriend a Goetia and bind it to themselves, like Awakened do. They can exchange Willpower and Essence between themselves, as Awakened do with Mana.

    Merit: Dream Mentor (• to •••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: Dreamers often find guides and mentors within Astral - most often it's their own Daimon, powerful Goetia representing drive for self-improvement. In addition to regular benefits of Mentor, Dreamer can summon his Dream Mentor from anywhere within Astral with successful Resolve + Composure roll.

    Merit: Dream Summoner (•••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: With that power, Dreamer is able to summon Goetia within his own Oneiros. He may summon Goetia he knows personally, or he can specify the type of entity by its Resonance. This is an extended action requiring 5xRank successes; Dreamer is using Resolve + Composure dicepool and may use dream equipment which is Resonant toward summoned entity. If successful, Goetia is present for one scene for conversation or pact-making; summoner does not control it, but may expel it with instant Resolve + Composure - Rank dicepool.

    Merit: Dream Gate (•••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: With that power, Dreamer is able to manifest a Gate in this Oneiros; that gate leads to known location in Temenos or Anima Mundi of his choice. With successful meditation, Dreamer can deconstruct or reconstruct this Gate with new location in mind; he can have only one Gate manifested at the time. Gate may be one-way or two-way; if it is two-way, any unwanted attempt to cross it from the other side is resisted by Resolve + Composure roll of Dreamer.
    If Dreamer dies, Gate may collapse with him. If it does not, instead of Oneiros it will lead to the location of ST's choice.

    Merit: Dream Demesne (• to •••••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: With that power, Dreamer is able to manifest a dream Demesne, his own personal place of power, with the following chosen benefits per dot in this Merit:
    • Dreamer has +3 to all rolls within his Demesne.
    • Dreamer can reflexively change his location to any point within Demesne.
    • Dreamer can perceive anything happening within Demesne.
    • Dreamer recovers his Dream Health twice as fast within Demesne.
    • Dreamer may host friendly Goetia within his Demesne; they are safe from Essence bleed, but he must spend 1 Willpower per Goetia every 24 hours to keep them that way. Goetia from his own Oneiros are exception to this rule and may come and go freely.
      Additionally, this Merits is subtracted from any unwanted attempts to dreamweave or use Astral Influences inside Demesne. Demesne can be placed in any part of Astral, including Dreamer's dreams; if it is symbolically destroyed by any means, Dreamer can manifest a new one by spending a point of Willpower.
    If Dreamer who has five dots in this Merit dies, he may not die completely. Death is a breaking point, but if he successfully rolls it, he may create a small domain within Temenos from his Demesne; his consciousness becomes a powerful Goetia within it with Influence over it's own Domain.

    Merit: Dream Shaper (• to •••)
    Requirement: Dreamer
    Effect: With that power, Dreamer is more adept at shaping dreams, receiving bonus equal to dots in this Merit.

    Stereotypes
    Kindred: I managed to enter the dreams of one of them, once. It was a big mistake; I barely escaped with my mind intact. All that blood, and suffering, and eternal hunger... I would almost pity him, but then I saw how many people he has eaten. They are too dangerous.
    Uratha: I met one of them in deep Dreaming, once. He was predatory and powerful, beast-like. I saw that he has capability to think complex thoughts, but there... it was like he was relaxing, allowing his thoughts to go away and just went with the flow. But I wouldn't want to meet him in real life.
    Awakened: I knew one of them, once. She was so bright and powerful in her dreams, and we've gone on many adventures together... but she never knew when to stop, always going a bit too deep, always a bit too hungry for secrets which made up most of her life. Now she stopped answering my calls, and I am afraid that I know what happened to her.
    Lost: Some nightmares seem to be so powerful and ancient, they can steal people from the real world. I wish I could fight them, but I can't find place from where they do come... and it's so hard to intercept them in other people's dreams.
    Bound: I wonder if dead can still dream. Not going to find out myself before my time comes, that's for sure.
    Created: Don't listen to what others say. They can dream, and many of them have same wonderful dream. I hope they find what they seek, even if they won't ever remember finding it later.
    Fallen: Nothing human under that cover. Just leave them be and don't agree to take their deals.
    Timeless: They have dreams. They just unlike anything I've ever seen, and longer than several of my lifespans taken together. Do not look too deep, or else they will look back in response.
    Begotten: Ancient hungers coming from forgotten dreams to hunt people. It does not matter if they wanted this to happen to them or not. We must fight them back.
    Renegades: ...why their dreams are so bright and messed up? Why they can't follow me into deeper Astral? It can't be just the trauma... is there something going on?
    Sleepers: I'd say they don't know how to dream. But maybe they know how to stay awake. Isn't that just as important? I am no longer sure where I should be, unlike them.

    Plot hooks for crew of Dreamers
    Totally not stolen from movies we all know and like... ah, who am I kidding?
    • Local Slasher was killed, but he is still alive in dreams. Local kids start to wake up screaming; some of them don't wake at all. Your crew has to find nightmare realm in which he took residence and make him face justice once again.
    • A powerful figure is bent on destroying life of your community; perhaps your village stands on oil reserves, or perhaps your neighboorhood is in need of some gentrification. Either way, you cannot hope to beat him in real life... but in dreams, he is just a human. Isn't he?
    • A mysterious figure approaches all of you with a quest - find rare and wonderful object deep within Dreaming, and he promises to make your wishes come true. The road is long and dangerous, though, and there is that lingering question... why that figure won't go and retrieve this object on his own?
    Dream Shaping
    Like changelings, Dreamers can participate in dreamweaving; they can enact paradigm shifts and spend Willpower instead of Glamour each time such spending is required.
