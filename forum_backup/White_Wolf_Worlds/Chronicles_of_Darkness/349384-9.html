                    The entirety of the surrealist and nightmarish visuals of Eraserhead could be seen as the slow Awakening of a feeble and tormented Sleeper who feels oppressed by his growing obligations in a mechanical and uncaring world. The strange blending of reality and dream intrinsic to the movie makes it hard to say if Henry's Awakening is a Mystery Play or Astral Journey. His Path is likely Mastigos, or perhaps he is going through a False Awakening as he gazes far too much into the Abyss (see Night Horrors - The Unbidden, page 128). The whole movie is excellent inspiration for creepy Awakenings and stories focused on the Astral, particularly on crushed hope and repressed urges.

                                        I was watching some Bayonetta videos, and thought about what splat she would belong to. The fact that she would be a walking breach of any splat's masquerade if she acted like in the games notwithstanding, my first hunch was a Destroyer Saboteur/Tempter. However, the things she does would be out of character for just about any Cover. Maybe her main Cover is custom made or hi-jacked from an Angel. Furthermore, what power could a Demon have that would mimic her ability to summon those monsters out of her own hair? I'd say that she creates Hellhounds with the Twilight Entity and Essence Eater Adaptations, allowing them to follow her unnoticed and feed from Angels. Her animal forms could be a result of Show Of Power, or a homebrewed Exploit that lets Demons become animals.

                                        Another option that came to mind would be an Elder Daeva. Her ability to summon demons could be mimicked with something like this Devotion I wrote once (Beast Made Flesh), which lets the user combine Protean, Vigor and Resilience to use his own flesh and Vitae as conduits for monstrous servants. My write up of this Devotion was poorly thought out, so I'd have to overhaul it. Her other powers can be represented just fine with the normal Disciplines. The Scarborough Fair and Love Is Blue (her quadruple guns in the first and second games, respectively) are Sanguine Objects. At first I considered her to be a member of the Circle Of The Crone, but she interacts with humans in a way that suggests a decent Humanity rating, and Cruac places a steep limit on Humanity.She's probably Unaligned. I don't know what equivalent to angels this version of Bayonetta would fight, though. She could be an ally of a Ring of Unchained and fight GM Angels as if she was a Saboteur herself.

                                                Come to think of it, Jackie Estacado from "The Darkness" works well as a Beast. The Darkness is an especially sadistic Horror, Jackie is an Eshmaki and his Hungers are Punishment and Power. When he attacks with the tendrils and serpent heads of the Darkness, he is using the Limb From Limb, Monster Of The Deep, Cyclopean Strength and Titanic Blow Atavisms. His affinity for shadows and stealth are a result of the Shadowed Soul and From The Shadows Atavisms. The terrorizing presence of the Darkness is likewise caused by the Looming Presence Atavism, and Jackie's abnormal endurance and healing speed are granted by Unbreakable and Relentless Hunter. The Otherworld levels could be Chambers in Jackie's Lair.

                                                All in all, Jackie could also be a Sin-Eater with a really messed up Geist.
