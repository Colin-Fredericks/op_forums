    [Minor Template] Influence-based Mages

    06-17-2016, 02:25 PM
    So, I was looking into allowing a minor template access to ephemeral Influences, and bouncing things around trying to balance them. I was also looking at Mage threads. Then it occurred to me (and I doubt I'm at all alone in this), that the Influence models could easily recreate a slimmed down, Mage-like minor template. So how would that look?

    Mage (minor template)

    Some, through dedication, worship, secret knowledge, or gifts, command powers of the world to work their will. These individuals go by many names - witch, mage, shaman, necromancer, theurge - but all are joined by their ability to Influence various forces to fulfill their desires. Through utilizing the spiritual powers of Essence, they can affect some of the same powers and abilities as ghost, spirits, and angels are capable of.

    Mechanics

    A Mage character has:
    Potency - Trait (or Supernatural Merit) rated 1-5, increases Essence allowed per Turn, Essence Capacity, and up to what level of Influence can Essence costs be reduced to 0. Counts as Supernatural Tolerance, and Rank for abilities.
    Essence - How much energy that can be stored to fuel abilities.
    Tradition - Determines themes, flavor, and their Principle Influences. Determines their Power and Finesse Attributes used in their abilities?
    Influences - Categories of reality through which they can work their magic. See Chronicles of Darkness, pg 130.

    Effects:
    0 - Sense
    • - Strengthen and Weaken
    •• - Manipulate and Scan
    ••• - Control and Scry
    •••• - Create and Destroy
    ••••• - Mass/Permanent Create, Mass Destroy, and Excise

    Duration:
    0 - One minute per success. No additional Essence cost.
    • - Ten minutes per success. No additional Essence cost.
    •• - One hour per success. The cost is 1 additional Essence.
    ••• - One day per success. The cost is 2 additional Essence.
    •••• - Permanent. The cost is 2 additional Essence.

    Note: Duration is additive, so Manipulate for 1 hour per Success is a •••• Influence.

    Sense: You can sense sources of Essence appropriate for the Influence up to a mile away. Cost 0 Essence.
    Weaken: You can penalize Defense of a loved one, make an emotion weaker, an animal or plant sicklier, or an object more fragile, reducing Health or Structure by Rank/Potency. This Influence can shift the Open Condition to Anchor, Resonant or Infrastructure for its duration. The cost is 1 Essence.
    Scan: Roll your Finesse*. You can ask the Storyteller for details relating to the Influence about the subject your are viewing. Costs 1 Essence.
    Scry: You can use the Scan effect upon a subject you can Sense (up to one mile, usually). Costs 2 Essence.
    Destroy: You can remove an anchor, banish an emotion, kill a plant or animal (Roll Power* + Finesse* as a 0L Weapon), or destroy an object. You can remove an Anchor, Infrastructure, or Resonant Condition in a subject for the duration of the Influence. The cost is 4 Essence.
    Excise: As Destroy, but it also prevents the subject from entering, being created, or having an effect in the area for the duration. If dealing damage, treat as 0A Weapon.

    Mitigation
    Essence costs can be mitigated in a number of ways, to a minimum determined by Potency (at Potency 1, • Influence effects can be reduced to 0, •• effects at Potency 3, and ••• effects at Potency 5; otherwise the minimum cost is 1).
    Principle Influence - (-1)
    Resonant Conditions - Resonance (-1), Open (-2), [Hallow?Controlled?] (-3).
    Spell (Major decisions pre-planned) - (-1).
    Ritual Casting - Minute (-1), Hour (-2).
    Special Tools - (-1, but the Resource or Availability cost must be greater than the Influence level).
    These can be combined to bring costs down so repeated use doesn't beggar the mage.

    Numina
    Mages can learn a number of special applications of their powers. If they can justify a use or expression of the Numina through their Influences, let them acquire it (Fire and Blast makes sense, but if they can justify Love and Blast, let them).     See Chronicles of Darkness, pg 136 for examples.

    Manifestations
    Mages can even alter their own state of being, or those of their immediate surroundings, much like Ephemerals with Manifestation.

    Materialize - Being inherently material, a Mage gets this for free. This Effect has no cost.
    Twilight Form (Requires Open Condition) - By spending 3 Essence, the mage can shift their form into Twilight. They suffer from Essence Bleed as per Ephemeral entities, but will instead Materialize if they run out of Essence (this can range from awkward to messy, depending on where they are forced to Materialize).
    Reaching - By spending one Essence, the mage applies the Reaching Condition to themselves. The activation roll is penalized by the local Gauntlet Strength. A mage might do this if they want to affect a Spirit on the other side.
    Avernian Gateway (Requires Open Condition) - By spending 3 Essence, the mage opens a nearby gateway to the Underworld, and applies the Underworld Gate Condition to the location.
    Shadow Gateway (Potency 3+, Requires Open Condition) - By spending Essence equal to Gauntlet Strength, the mage opens a portal to the Shadow that it and other entities may use, applying the Shadow Gate Condition to the location. The activation roll is penalized by the local Gauntlet Strength.
    [Might just replace the above two with the more general Realm Gateway Manifestation floating around the forums...]
    Image (Requires Anchor, Resonant, or Infrastructure Condition) - By spending 1 Essence, the mage may use their Influence to shape it into an image, or cast an image upon it. The can be reduced to no cost if the Influence is one of their Principle Influences.
    Fetter (Requires Open Condition) - By spending 2 Essence, the mage adds the Fettered Condition to themself. Living beings targeted by this Effect contest the roll with Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. If the Effect is successful, living targets gain the Urged Condition. A mage must be capable of Twilight Form to buy Fetter, and must be in Twilight Form to use it. Some mages report that bodily functions (including aging) cease while in Twilight, and that this is one method of a kind of immortality through haunting.
    Unfetter (Requires Fettered Condition) - By spending 1 point of Essence, the mage temporarily suppresses the Fetter Condition for a Scene, allowing them to use other Manifestation Effects or roam in Twilight. When the Scene ends, any Manifestation Effects used during it immediately end. If the mage isn’t back within range of their Fetter (see p. 134) when Unfetter ends, they immediately Materialize.
    Possess (Requires Open Condition) - By spending 3 Essence, the mage gains temporary control over an object, corpse, or creature, applying the Possessed Condition to the subject. Living subjects contest the roll with Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. A mage must be capable of Twilight Form to buy Possess, and must be in Twilight Form to use it.
    Claim (Requires Controlled Condition) - By spending 5 Essence, the mage gains permanent control over an object, creature or corpse, applying the Claimed Condition to the subject. Living subjects contest the roll with Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. A mage must be capable of both the Fetter and Possess Manifestations to buy Claim, and the mage must be in Twilight Form to use it. Long term consequences of merging one's self to other objects and beings are the subject of many dark rumors.

    Trait Experiences
    Potency 4 per dot
    Principle Influence 3 per dot
    Other Influence 4 per dot
    Numina 2
    Manifestation 3
    Tradition Template:

    Name
    Description
    Power: Attribute
    Finesse: Skill

    Essence Recovery Method:

    Example Traditions

    Necromancer
    Necromancers tap into the lore of the dead to seek knowledge and power over things in their mortal life. Many also deal in the trappings and associations of death as well.
    Power: Intelligence
    Finesse: Occult
    Example Principle Influences: Death, Bones, Passions, Blood
    Essence Recovery Method: As per Ghosts - being near Anchors (though having none of their own, any Anchor will do), or being remembered (for their power) by the living.

    Street Shaman
    Spirits are not restricted to the wilds of nature - every place and thing has its own spiritual reflection. Street Shamans deal with the spirits of the city, to make sure they stay in balance, or merely to advance their own goals.
    Power: Manipulation
    Finesse: Streetwise
    Example Principle Influences: Street, Dogs, Crime, Separation
    Essence Recovery Method: Street Shamans usually gain Essence by bargaining with Spirits. Spirits are usually predisposed towards those that further their reflections and cause their Resonance Conditions to appear. Most will give a Street Shaman their cut in exchange for such help.

    Machine-Priest
    Some heed the static hymns only few can hear. Worshiping a divine of infinite clockwork complexity, they further what they believe to be Its tenants - and if sometimes that matches their own desires, who can say?
    Power: Wits
    Finesse: Persuasion
    Example Principle Influences: Machines, Fate, Awe, Order
    Essence Recovery Method: As per Angels - being near Infrastructure is the most common method, though some manage to get their congregations/cults to make sacrifices to them to fuel their power.


    Notes:
    If you want, you can also use @amechra's Dharmas and Influences.
    If you have Mage: the Awakening 2E and want to use this minor template anyways, you can also model them like Ghost Mages (but not, you know, Ghosts). Influences would map to the Arcana, and Numina can be Mage Spells as if they were Praxis.

    Examples to follow. Feel free to discuss in the meantime. I'll post more when I have time.

      I've always loved the idea of 'Influence Magic' as a minor template or other sort of character option, but never got around to writing something up.

      Numina might work as a nice way to cover "Spells", just attach them to an influence level as a prerequisite, so long as the influence is thematically appropriate (Blast can work for nearly anything with a physical manifestation, but might not work with "Love").

      One idea might be to have the Path (or whatever you call it) correspond to an ephemeral type. Path of the Underworld/Necromancy/Death-Mages/whatever name might focus on ghost and similar based influences, while the Path of the Shadow/Shamanism/etc draw influence similarly to spirits, The Path of the Astral/Warlocks/etc equate to goetics, and Stigmatic-Mages/God-Machine Priests/etc work like Angels. That'd define the manifestation conditions they can interact with for their essence bonuses and replenishment, and would give you a guide for starting Influences.

          Example: Pyris
          Tradition: Something vaguely Zoroastrian like - Principle Influences: Fire, Truth
          Influences: Fire 4, Truth 3, Light 1
          Gnosis/Potency/Whatever: 4
          Essence: 6/14 (4/turn)

          Given this, Pyris can Create (for a duration dependent upon additional Essence spent, as per Influences), Destroy, Control, Scry upon, Manipulate, Scan, Strengthen, Weaken, and Sense Fire. Say they want to Create a flame from nothing. Create costs 4 Essence. That's steep, but they thankfully have a Spell that creates a bonfire (-1 Essence). They are in a hot area already, so we'll give them Resonant (-1). They can afford to take their time, so they drag it out into a ritual (-1). It now only takes them 1 Essence. If the area was Open to Fire somehow, it would only cost 0 Essence (since Fire is a Principle Influence).

          Talking to another, they can Sense the Truth of their words (Influence: Truth 0+), and by spending a point of Essence, Scan it for additional details. Being flexible (as one should with broad Influences), they can Manipulate that Truth, perhaps making the source (the speaker) divulge more or less, for 2 Essence (unless Mitigation brings the number down).

          Seeking to hide, they Weaken the Light in the area. Unfortunately, since Light isn't a Principle Influence, it is going to cost 1 Essence regardless of Mitigation. If they had Influence: Light 2, they might be able to Manipulate the light into perhaps moving around them, leaving only them in shadow, rather than darkening the entire area.

          Note: These examples are extremely rough and Alpha, and serve solely to give a general idea of what I am talking about above.

                          I offer you Dharmas and Influences, for some real expanded Influences fun. Just recognize that True Influence and True Dharma are essentially plot devices, and should have a different cost swapped out for the Willpower cost (Quintessence? )

                          Actually, just make it a Willpower dot, have True Influence/Dharma move things to a sympathetic-type range rather than waiving range altogether, and you're all set.

                          Under this set up, Pyris would be able to do the following (assuming Fire Influence, Truth Dharma, and Light Influence):

                          • They can gather general information about local fires, play parlour tricks with flames, talk to those flames, prevent fires from going out, and could summon and control fire. If they wanted to create a fire from nothing, that'd be an Invoke influence - that's just 2 Essence, so a ritual (-1) in an area Open (-1) to fire would be enough to conjure flames in whatever shape they wanted.

                          • Truth is an interesting case - they can detect the truthful, colour things with the essential nature of truthfulness (make something sound more plausible or "correct"), project themselves into a truthful statement (talk about trippy!), or give themselves certain elements of truth. Yeah, the higher rank stuff is weird as hell, but they are practically impossible to lie to and I guess Incarnate would do something similar to Scan? Depends on how you interpret it, I guess.

                          • Light would let them map out the general layout of nearby light, and could gather shadows around themselves by using Demonstrate (among other minor tricks). If they had Light 2, they could use Speak to ask a lightbulb to burn out or something similar.

                          If they were willing to pull out the big guns, Pyris can gather information on fire pretty much anywhere, could make fire flare a little bit at the sound of their name, or could learn the truth of a situation by looking at any of the component parts.

                          If you were to, say, swap out that Light Influence for a Light Dharma? That control over Light becomes much more... metaphorical. Maybe they could detect vampires by seeing their aversion to the light, and they could make moods brighter or otherwise make things more like Light.
