        Unlike in the Material World, in the Hisil eveything is composed of Ephemera. Raw ephemera is like unmolded clay: it is the stuff Spirits are maid of, but it is not active unless sufficient Essence has poured into it.

        Let's take your stone as an example:
        Unlike active ranked Spirits or even Muthra, the stone is just a raw ephemera that got it's shape by the minimum Essence of the symbolism acquired from its material counterpart. It will (most of the time) work exactly as a stone would work in the Material World, so sharpening it would be no problem at all. However, if that place has a particularly abundant sort of some kind of Resonance, like a big battlefield where people battled to death, the stone might acquire some different proprieties, still being a stone, but a little easier to use on violent purposes (allowing for combat bonuses, for example).

        The Hisil, is highly reactive to the Material World. Even if something is not a Spirit nor Muthra, Resonance will shape it's ephemera accordingly, making even inanimate objects very unpredictable by common knowledge standards. In resume, feel free to allow things to work normally in the Hisil, and then surprise your players by adding some nice twist to mundane activities now and then. The sharpened stone might bite back at its user, due to a betrayal having occurred on that place, or it might shatter to dust when used on combat, due to a promise of peace that was forged right over it.

                                    Originally posted by Darkatom View Post
                                    According to page 184, I would assume it would be able to keep up its essence reserves while in the proximity (several yards?) of other stones, even while in the material world.
                                    A dormant Spirit, specially a Muthra, won't lose Essence. Spirits enter dormancy because they lack Essence. So, essentially, a Muthra is a dormant Spirit that may, one day, wake-up if it accumulates Essence enough. This means that your stone probably has not even sufficient Essence inside her to mechanically measure as an Essence Point. Active Stone Spirits eat thousands of little stones to be able to remain active. You would not be able to invoke the Siskur-Dah agains that stone to claim it's Essence, for example.

                                    That said, the book isn't much specific about this hole "Spirit Awakening" part, so I'm only giving you my own interpretation of how to treat the case.

                                    Originally posted by Darkatom View Post
                                    But it would also have turned into a weapon, so might therefore diversify its diet to also consuming weapon resonance? or does it actively have to try to steal essence (also described on page 184) of a different resonance for that to happen?
                                    Since your Stone Weapon isn't really active yet, it can't hunt for Essence on it's own, but with sufficient exposure to lots of Essence and Resonance, it might awaken as a Spirit of that Resonance. As written, Spirits gain 1 Essence per day when near their main Resonance. Since the lowest Hursih has a 10 Essence pool, I would say that it would take that amount of days for an Spirit to awaken, problem is, they are normally consumed by bigger Spirits. In your case, the stone is being protected by the Uratha, so it might have an easier time developing.
                                    I would say that after several exposures to really meaningful combat, it would certainly awaken as a Murder Stone Hursih.

                                    Just remember that Spirits eat Essence, and if the Uratha uses that stone against the wrong kind of Spirit, it will just gobble it up and gain more power in the process.

                                    Originally posted by Darkatom View Post
                                    If it did diversify somehow, what would be needed to keep its essence pool stable? it's a spirit, so it should not be able to generate resonance by simply existing like a normal sharpened stone would, so perhaps it would have to be held (or even used) as a weapon every couple of hours/days (depending on what side of the gauntlet you're on), or is the idea of it being a weapon in the user's mind enough, and will it survive indefinitely as long as the user doesn't lose it?
                                    You are absolutely right on the first example. It will need to be exposed, daily, to a suitable Resonance Condition, gaining a minimum of 1 Essence Point. A material version of it will suffice to maintain it, but actions using it will also allow a chance for generating Essence. For example, using the spirit-weapon in combat would qualify as a Resonant Condition, allowing the Spirit to Gorge. As written:

                                    Spirits may attempt to gorge themselves on a source of appropriate Essence. Once per day, when in proximity (even if it’s on the other side of the Gauntlet) to a suitable Condition, a spirit can roll Power + Finesse, regaining successes in Essence. If the spirit is still in the Hisil, the dice pool is penalized according to Gauntlet strength. Page 184
                                    So, after the Spirit is active, it will use it's attributes to try and suck Essence from a Resonant Condition into itself.

                                                    Spirit senses are more than just being able to see the spirit twilight. Spirit senses also include when you 'reach' across the gauntlet with one or more of the senses native to your current form, there isn't really any tell that grants that ability. So I'd say no to spirit senses. Wolf-senses yes, though only with tells that let you take urshul or urhan, dalu wouldn't grant wolf-senses, you gain all of the other bonuses from the form and the tells don't say you don't also get wolf-senses.
