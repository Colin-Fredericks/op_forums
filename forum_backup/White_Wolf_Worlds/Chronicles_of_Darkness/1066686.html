      I have a couple of changes to bring the sidebar in my section in line with Hurt Locker and other updated rules, as per a discussion with Matt and David.

      On page 147, in the "Historical Considerations" sidebar, please change the damage for flintlocks to 2 for Pistols and 3 for Muskets:

      Pistol: Damage 2, Range 15/30/60, Clip 1, Initiative -2, Strength 2, Size 2
      Musket: Damage 3, Range 30/60/120, Clip 1, Initiative -5, Strength 3, Size 4

      Also, in the last paragraph, please change "Broadswords use the machete stats." to "Broadswords use the machete stats except that Damage and Size are 3."
