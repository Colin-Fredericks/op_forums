                        I noticed nobody has tried to summarize Mummy, so I'll take up the mantle and scribe something out...

                        Mummy: The Curse was the last 1st Edition Chronicles line before the new edition, but also represents the most thorough usage of the 1E-isms and conceits, such as weaving the 7 Virtues/Vices into the mechanics and powers and doing a lot with ghosts. By the same token, it's one of the strangest. In every CofD line before it, characters started off as fresh-faced newbs at the nadir of their power. They went from being naive greenhorns to (maybe) being uncontested supernatural badasses, full of power and knowledge.

                        Mummy deliberately inverts that advancement scheme. Mummies are all ancient beings, gathered together in a Stone Age African Empire and ritually sacrificed by Sorcerer Kings, bound to wander between the afterlife and this waking world doing the bidding of Dark Cosmic Gods called the Judges. Mummies are awakened by cults dedicated to them to carry out tasks, usually in the name of the Judges. When Mummies awaken they are at the zenith of their power: surging with divine power so intense their bodies can't even properly form around them (leaving them looking like walking desiccated corpses). As time passes, the power starts to dwindle, and a proper human shell grows around them so they can start to properly interact with human civilization. Eventually the power dwindles to almost nothing and they return to a state of death-like hibernation as their spirits descend once again into the underworld.

                        However, their memories are by design left shattered and torn. They awaken at the height of their power, but with almost no knowledge beyond their Purpose, their name, and scattered recollections of their previous awakening. As time passes and their power drains, they trigger flashbacks that let them regain their memories and start to piece together their many, many pasts and, most importantly, their identity. As a game, it's possible to run through multiple awakenings (called Descents) and have different players play the same mummy each time since the resetting of Memory means that they might behave like a different person (albeit with the same Virtue/Vice).

                        It's fascinating but naturally the weird nature of the game puts some people off. For me it was what got me back into the entire Chronicles of Darkness after a 5-year hiatus.

                        "Nihhina kalekal-zidu kal masun, kal manudanadu. Nihhina kalekal-zidu nukal shaghu-desasudu — nihhina kalekal-zidu kal innu-desasudu udhkal samm." Arthur Ashe
                        She/her, contributing writer for Scion: Pride 2021

                                        Looking at the splats from another point of view

                                        Vampire: you're a pale glorified mosquito who has to distract itself from how horrible its unlife is by playing a massive game of pointless politics and needlessly complicated intrigue because if you don't then you realize how terrible everything is, possibly attract an evil owl thing, go batty(heh) and end up becoming a ravenous monster.

                                        Werewolf: you're some poor schmuck with really bad anger issues and the ability to turn into a number of wolf themed animal forms. Also you're an animist and have to deal with a game of pointless politics and needlessly complicated intrigue with the spirit world because if you don't you'll realize just how terrible everything is, an angry spirit will probably murder your family and you end up becoming a ravenous monster.

                                        Mage: you are Ebony Dark'ness Dementia Raven Way, a seventh year slytherin who's overwhelmingly beautiful and has Draco Malfoy wrapped around your finger. You spend your time dealing with pointless wizard politics and needlessly complicated intrigue, hiding your magic away from the Muggles because if you don't then you'll probably attract the attention of an unspeakable Cthulhu horror who will eat your family or you make a deal with it and become a ravenous monster. Or it would be that way but you took three dots in time magic.

                                        Promethean: you're Frankenstein's monster with extra magical powers, your whole life sucks and you ruin everyone and everything around you just by existing. You're on your journey to learn how to become a real boy but have to constantly move around or risk attracting the attention of malformed abominations that eat your soul energy or despoiling the land around you into an apocalyptic wasteland, occasionally the pressure of it all gets to you and you might end up becoming a ravenous monster.

                                        Changeling: you were kidnapped by faeries and taken to their magical land where they stripped you of any semblance of humanity and tortured you for an indeterminate amount of time, possibly decades, until you escaped. Now your soul is all torn to bits, you have magical powers, a tenuous grasp on sanity and are being hunted by those faeries who very much would like you back. So you spend your time dealing with pointless politics and needlessly complicated intrigue trying to remain hidden because if you don't then you'll realize how terrible everything is, go crazy and end up becoming just as bad the the things that kidnapped you, or you'll just get kidnapped and subjugated again.

                                        Sin-Eaters: i actually don't know much about this, you're a dude who died and got a powerful über-ghost sharing space with your soul, now you're a ghost buster with magical powers who spends your time at pointless raves and needlessly complicated orgies until the über-ghost gets on your ass about completing some unknown goal. You're also really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really, really hard to kill.

                                        Mummy: again, don't know much. You're a dude who died a long time ago but due to an ancient ritual you keep coming back. You are filled with a burning obsession for something, though you may not know what and nothing will stop you from completing that goal even if it takes centuries, or even if you don't know what that goal is. You start out powerful but grow weaker as you spend time out and about, having only a small amount of time to complete that goal before returning back to your deathlike slumber only to return when you are called upon again or after some kind of thousand year cycle. And you're probably impossible to kill, that's about what I got.

                                        Beast: not quite sure on this one, you're a person who either had their soul eaten by a nightmare creature or you were that nightmare creature all along. Now you are basically Freddy Kreuger if he had better PR but probably without the urge to brutally murder everyone you meet... Probably. You have to go out and eat people's dreams because if you don't then the ravenous monster inside of you does it for you and is a lot less gentle about it, which then either creates or attracts an army of Gastons from beauty in the beast to come kill you. Also you are like everyone's third cousin or something like that, my knowledge of beast(as with sin-eaters and mummy) is very spotty.

                                        Demons: not the Judeo-Christian variety, you're just called a demon for convenience sake. You're actually some kind of weird biomechanical abomination that used to work for a nigh-omnipotent computer God but now you've escaped and are hiding from the computer god's "angels". To do that though you need to blend in, so you get into contracts and bargains with mortals in the hopes of taking over their life and exploring your new found free will.

                                        Hunter: you're a normal dude(sometimes) with a little more information on what's really going on in the world(sometimes) and you're angry about it(sometimes). However you are hopelessly outmatched against everything else in the world(most of the time) so you have to vent your frustrations out on bargain bin monsters that still are probably more than a match for you(sometimes).

                                        This is the uneducated man's opinion at a glance, and how I describe it to friends who ask me about the games.
