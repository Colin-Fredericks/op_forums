            How about something like this:

            Dread Fruit (**)
            Prerequisite: Family ties with a Hob Goblin, Goblin King/Queen or a similar Hedge Denizen.
            Description: Since you began associating with your strange edge dwelling friend unusual plants have taken root in your Lair. They bear the tell-tale thorny profile of the Hedge and take root and thrive despite seemingly hostile conditions. Whatever dream from Arcadia they sprang from they are now adapting to your own nightmarescape. When you sate your hunger they grow fat on the powerful fear that courses through your Lair and produce bulbous fruits not dissimilar to those from the Hedge. But your friend is quick to point out, these are no Goblin Fruits. They swell with something more sinister.
            Benefit: When you successfully sate your Hunger and move from the Starving to Sated, or Sated to Gorged Condition you may choose not to take a single point of Satiety and instead "store" this in your Dread Fruit. The Dread Fruit may hold Satiety up to your Lair Rating. Once per Story any Beast (or with Storyteller discretion, similar creature) may consume the Dread Fruit inside your Lair to gain the Satiety stored within.

            Pretty rough first draft typed directly into the chat, could probably use some polish. Thoughts on balance/usefulness?

                        Imitation of Life (••)
                        Prerequisite: Family Ties with a Fetch or Promethean
                        Effect: The character is shielded from supernatural powers that would detect her as anything but human. Dice rolls for such powers enter a Clash of Wills against a dicepool of character’s Lair + highest Resistance Attribute. This Merit also affects uses of Family Resemblance, making the character count as “fundamentally human” and possibly subtracting his Satiety from investigator’s dice pool (B:tP p.88).

                        Empty Words (••••)
                        Prerequisite: Family Ties with an Acartharos
                        Effect: Words spoken by the character have no metaphysical weight whatsoever. This offers him a measure of protection against certain supernatural effects. She can’t be bound by an oath based upon her declarations, curses triggered by uttering a secret do not activate and truth-detecting effects outright ignore her confessions.
                        Drawback: There is a specific circumstance under which this effect is suspended. Use Bans and Banes as inspiration. The character can exploit this exception for his own benefit, but it can be used against him should the secret ever come out.

                                        Multi-Headed (* or ***): Your horror possesses conjoined siblings that take the form of additional heads. Each head possesses its own level of sentience and shows a willingness to collaborate with each other... most of the time, anyways.

                                        Prerequisites: At least 1 alternate personality.

                                        Effect: At the 1 dot level of this merit, the Beast is fully aware of the existence of any split personalities they possess, and they may share information between each other, even while the others lay dormant. At the 3 dot level of this merit, the Beast may freely transition between which personality is in control of their body. Which can prove invaluable if one of the other personalities has a particular set of skills that the current one is lacking in.

                                        If the Beast obtains any additional personalities later on in life, their horror manifests an additional head to represent the new consciousness.

                                                    Twin-Souled (Creation Only) OOOO or OOOOO

                                                    Rather than one, your Beast has two Horrors, of the same or with 5 O even of different Families!
                                                    This allows them easier access to Atavisms (if both of the same Family -> In-Family Atavisms cost 1 xp less, if different Families --> twice as many In-Family purchases) and makes defending their Lair much easier (both Horrors act as seperate characters), which is bound to surprise any Hero expecting one Horror only. It also grants the Twin-Souled an additional two Atavisms, as each Horror has their own seperate Atavisms.
                                                    With 5 dots, Feeding is also easier, since the Beast can express their Hunger through different Families.
                                                    Finally, they can more easily resist Anathema if the Hero in question didn't do enough research, gaining a +2 to relevant traits (e.g. Defense) where applicable.
                                                    However, this comes at a price: The Lair is not big enough to fully sustain two Horrors to their full potential, so when calculating the Horror's stats, they need to share the 2*Lair bonus points, each getting half. Furthermore, when Feeding, 1 Satiety gets lost to the Horror's squabbling over the meal, and two Horrors burn through the Satiety twice as fast: A Lair 1-3 Beast loses one Satiety every three days, 4-6 every other day, 7-9 every day, and Lair 10 Twin-Souled would lose 2 Satiety per day. Atavisms are bought per Horror, so buying Unbreakable only applies to one Horror, which can influence fights within the Lair heavily. Atavisms do not stack. If both Horrors are of the same Family, learning out-of-Family Atavisms becomes harder: pay an additional 1 xp. Finally, unlike other Beasts, the Twin-Souled may be subject to two Anathema at any given time.


                                                    Essentially, this is an early power boost with immense drawbacks. Might be a fun merit for a chronicle or two, though.

                                                            Meanwhile:


                                                            Myth over Physics OOOO
                                                            Prerequisite: Cyclopean Strength AND Crushing Coils

                                                            Being strong enough to lift an airplane is a great ability - however, as many Beast's had come to realize, Superman physics don't apply to the real world, and from a certain size onward, their improvised weaponry tended to break apart. As usefull as it would be as a clubbering weapons, cars had a tendency to come off at the bumper or tire when swung around, and statues gave in to their own weight, leaving the Begotten usually with just a small boulder.
                                                            That is, unless they draw upon their Horror to stabilize their enourmous weapon - even when wielding something like the Empire State Building, held together by the World Serpent Jormungandr, it stays intact as a weapon.
                                                            Mechanically, the Begotten has to spent a point of Willpower, which allows them to lift something to the upper limits of their ability as a non-crumbling weapon. The most obvious benefit here is that with some luck, a Begotten can attack a number of enemies at the same time, like autofire. There are benefits to wielding an airplane as a club other than reach, however: the weapon gains a modifier of + (1/5 its size). When going over +5, the weapon instead starts at +1 Aggravated. Since these weapons are very cumbersome to use, the Begotten reduces their Initiative by (1/3 size). If this would reduce their Initiative below 0, they start gaining a penalty to the attack roll (to a maximum of -5). Depending on the scene, friendly characters have to dodge if an attack in their vicinity takes place, because the weapon does not differentiate friend from foe.
                                                            The enhancement works for a scene, but applies only to the one weapon - dropping it to use something else (e.g. because it was destroyed) means another WP expenditure is necessary.


                                                            Example:
                                                            During the Battle for Ellis Island between two Broods, the Statue of Liberty was detached at its base and falling over. The legendary Anakim Apex Jorel managed to get hold of the statue at the top of the torch, and proceeded to use it as a grotesquely oversized club against the enemy. Against all common sense, it did not break of at the arm, because Jorel drew on his Horror to hold it together.
                                                            Jorel's player wants to take out the enemy Brood's Werewolf, because he's tanking a lot but also is already wounded heavily. Due to the size of his 'club', he acts last, but thank's to his Makara Broodmate's helpful use of Monster from the Deep, most of the other group was unable to move this turn. Due to the drawbacks Jorel rolls an abysmall dicepool of 2, but lucks out and gets a success - with the weapon's modifier and the accumulated damage so far, he manages to deal a killing blow.
                                                            Even an Uratha's regenerative ability in War Form was no match to a lucky blow with the monument. The rest managed to move out of the way just fine, however, and decided to retreat for the day and plot their revenge.


                                                            Needs work, I know, it's a first draft, but I think you get the general idea.
