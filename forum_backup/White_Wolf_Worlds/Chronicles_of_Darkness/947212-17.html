                I am really excited for Deviant, and having seen some of the spoilers Daveb gave way back, decided to make a few guesses as to what could be in Deviant.


                Power Fuel-
                What with the character possibilities being so diverse, and their “state” being no guided one, I guess there is *not* a Fuel Stat like we have seen in other games. The closest I think we will get is similar to the “Conviction” add-on for Mortals/Hunters in a past shard book (Mirrors, I think). Likely, they will have a higher or seperate Willpower pool, which can (but not always) fuel their powers. A few hints put the “cost” of their Variations being the Scars, it seems. Scars likely can include Willpower cost, but not always. (Ex: Eleven doesn’t spend Willpower to activate her Telekineses, but takes Bashing damage per activation, though she can spend Willpower to augment her Variation).
                I’m guessing this because the Remade and the game feels very Driven, Determined, etc, and Willpower being the extension of that, makes me guess it will become more important to these characters, similar to Hunter (and some extent, Mage and Mummy). The least, they will have more oppurtunities to gain Willpower than the normal Mortal.


                Clade- “Host”
                A Host is Shinichi Izumi (of Parasyte, anime and manga), a boy whose right arm was consumed and replaced by an “alien” parasyte named Migi, one with its own intelligence and instinct, though little personality.
                A Host is Jackie Estacado, invaded by The Darkness, via patrilineal ritual that connects him with the (very powerful) embodiment of Darkness.


                A Host has *something* inside them, either physically or otherwise. It has infected them, and has a minor will of its own. Their Variations tend to have Scars where the other Entity takes control of the power for a time, or tries to urge the Host to do things he wouldn’t. These characters never feel alone, and their path is littered with accidents and tragedy caused by something they can’t rid of. In the end, they too often try to take their own lives (which the entity likely does not abide), or die trying to “remove” the entity inside, unable to undo the process that binded them. And that's if they are lucky, and don’t become a hollow shell, their identity and/or flesh subsumed by a hostile entity, as it uses its new Body for its inhuman ends.

                                Hm, you could still work Mages as Deviants- I like the idea that every formerly-human being in ChroD (ie, everyone except Prometheans and Demons) could be considered a Deviant, it's just that they've become common enough to create a set of shared characteristics. Like how one of the Requiem books speculates that the clans are technically unrelated types of vampiric beings that evolved convergently. And you could totally apply the Variation/Scar framework to other beings. Vampires have the Scars that they need blood and can't stand sunlight and so on. Mages are scarred in that they're driven by obsessions and their powers risk causing damage to reality (plus, the Power=Size and strength of the conspiracy chasing you fits Mages vs. Seers well).

                                And I mentioned Host as being something I hope is a Clade, because it's so well represented in the source material and so wonderfully ripe for horror. Plus it's flexible- you could use it represent an alien parasite, a sapient nanomachine colony, or becoming the vessel for an ancient god.

                                The "no fuel stat" thing is cool, and I can't wait to see it play out. I think that you could add a fuel stat as a Scar- have it be that the Deviant's powers rely on a limited pool of energy that they need to replenish some how. Another cool Scar would be that the Deviant only has access to their powers when in some sort of "super" mode, and you can add on drawbacks from there: it might have a limited duration, changing might be extremely painful, or the super mode might be so monstrous that it's impossible to socialize while in it. You could use that to represent a Deviant whose powers come from powered armor, actually. Though I'm also going to homebrew powered armor made from Deviants that lets them access some of the power of a Blowout without blowing up. I know this could unbalance it, but it's being planned as an optional thing ST's can throw in if they want to change the power level without actually having to alter the base mechanics.

                                As an aside, I LOVE the Variation/Scar mechanics. It's the single most elegant way I've seen to sum up what it means to play a superhuman character in a horror setting- all power is inherently dangerous and dehumanizing.

                                    Transformation sequences are reoccuring thing in the source material, especially the superheroish edge like Guyver, Blue Beetle (based on Guyver), Hulk, etc.

                                    The way we're currently handling it is not to have a supermode like Demon and Changeling2e; rather, there will be a variation that supresses other variations while active, along with their scars. When that power turns off, your true form reppears.

                                    Or, put another way, Bruce Banner does not have a Variation of "grow big and green when angry". Hulk has a Variation of "turn off size increase and strength increase, with their associated beserk rage and inhuman appearance scars, as long as you remain calm."

                                    Which is a fine difference, but an important one for our themes.

                                                    Yeah. We want to have transforming characters as they're so prominent in the inspirations, but to have them be optional. Deviant is, ultimately, about playing the now-inhuman monster, so it becomes less "you have a superform" as "you have a disguise". Active rather than passive camouflage.

                                                    Plus this way, if anyone hits a transforming Remade with one of those "your active powers turn off" effects so beloved of crossover material, they're in for a nasty surprise.
