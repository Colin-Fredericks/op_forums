                    Originally posted by Axelgear View Post

                    See, I don't get exciting out of any of that. I've danced to avoid used needles and had to hold my nose over summers because the people the next apartment over literally smeared their floors with excrement, and none of that was exciting. Interesting? Maybe. Definitely fun stories to tell at parties. But if someone told me &quot;Hey, wanna go live through that again?&quot;, I'd tell them to stuff it.

                    The Hedge is defined in the preview by pain. Not (just) strong and raw emotions, but pain. Suffering. Agony. Misery. Nothing about it says it can be avoided; nothing suggests that pain is merely a by-product. Pain is the goal; pain is the intent; pain is the central design feature. When characters go there, the expectation is that it will hurt them in some way.

                    That's not fun. It's something sensible people avoid. It's something that tells you every potential good thing you see is just bait in a bear trap, ready to snap shut around the arm that reaches for it.

                    This isn't to say that pain can't or shouldn't be an integral part of the Hedge, but it does better when it's implied; when it's a natural consequence; when the very thing that can cause the pain can also be a source of reward and wonder. Just like Arcadia.
                    Sometimes its worth risking pain or excepting pain. Example tomorrow I'm getting my last two wisdom teeth pulled and that will be painful, I've accepted that, but the benifits outway the discomfort of pain and swelling, improved oral health, less pain in the long term, ect...

                    The dangerous of the Hedge are like that. Yes there is pain and risk and danger, but its worth it because you can magic fruits that give ypu glamour and heal your wounds, places were you can build a magic home/sancurary were you are safer/more powerful then anywhere else in the universe, and removing your icons from the hedge makes less pain in the long term.

                    And I think at least for Changelings the pain can in of itself be enlighting. I assume that Changeling traveling through the thorns as opposed to trods experience breaking points over time, well suceeding on those can grant acutity condition and that can lead you gaining a point of clarity, instead of losing it.

                    So there are lots of reasons, tons for exploring and Changeling magic, and that doesn't include how cool hedge warping really is.

                                                    Also, I didn't see this until the quick revisions. I must say, I like them.

                                                    Does make the opening lines kind of unfitting though-the idea is that in the Hedge, the pain is often worth it. Like confronting your Shadow in Persona 4; it hurts, but it can lead to a better place.

                                                    Maybe pain doesn't make you stronger-but becoming stronger does often involve pain. Most of the good things in life do.
