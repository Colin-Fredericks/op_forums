    Hunter 2e Kickstarter

    12-12-2016, 10:30 PM
    So the newest MMN floats the question of Kickstarting the new edition of Hunter and all new edition corebooks going forward. I figured starting a forum thread to discuss the topic was the smart thing to do, as I know it's been discussed here prior.

    I'm all for it, especially because the possibility of funding supplements through stretch goals (and thus, guaranteed interest) is a smart move that ensures the health of the line going forward, something I know a lot of folks have been concerned about with the previous 2e books and their relatively few supplements so far.
