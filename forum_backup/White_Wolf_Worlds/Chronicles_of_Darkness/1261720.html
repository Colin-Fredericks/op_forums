    Uncle Yo's Chroniclers of Darkness - Audio Play Podcasts

    10-20-2018, 01:01 AM
    ...kinda surprised that no one has mentioned this here, but yeah.

    Uncle Yo, famed geek comedian, is actually a huge fan of Chronicles of Darkness. So much so that, a few years ago, he apparently held a panel where he would try to summarize, and pitch, all of the CofD gamelines in 10 minutes to each. Playlist here.

    (and apparently he did discuss Mummy, Demon, and Beast, but the camera's battery died so no footage)

    But from there he and other voice actors and the like collaborated together to make audio drama podcasts for each gameline. In seasone 1 he did:

    Vampire: the Requiem - "Be Kine to Your Neighbors"
    Changeling: the Lost - "Down the Rabbit Hole"
    Hunter: the Vigil - "Neighborhood Watch"
    Geist: the Sin Eaters - "Tombstone Kings of Queens"

    Season 2 has started and now he's doing Geist and Changeling again, but he's also doing new ones for Werewolf, Mage, Promethean, and Demon. Geist s2 is already finished, and Promethean is two episodes in.

    Geist: the Sin Eaters season 2
    Promethean: the Created
    Mage: the Awakening - "Mind over Matter"
    Demon: the Descent - "God-Complex"

    Season 3

    Changeling: the Lost 2e mini-series - "The Vatheck's Oath"
