                            I think a lot of Princesses begin as members of the Twilight Courts, especially if they Blossom really young (like 13 or 14): Mirrors (if they’re spoiled and a little self-centered), Tears (if they’re depressed) or Storms (if someone hurt them and they’re angry about it). Then as they mature a lot of them switch to the Radiant Courts: Hearts (romance and social interaction), Diamonds (intellectual stuff), Clubs (the earth and nature), Spades (jesters/tricksters) and Swords (passion).

                            Not sure if this is exactly on topic, but maybe this will be helpful in your campaign.

                                            Longtime reader, first time poster.
                                            So I've been wondering what to post first & I've decided to go with some darkspawn.

                                            "Fleshcarvers": these were medical doctors that got corrupted by the Darkness. They have a visceral feel to them, thus explaining the possible name I came up with.

                                            Heirophants: religious leaders, will either be found at the head of a cult of the Darkness or other religious organization.
                                            Alternatively, one could be wandering the land, attaching itself to whatever religious group it finds, causing the group to gradually warp into something foul, then move on when there was nothing more to corrupt.

                                            The Whispering Dark: this, I envision, is a unique creature of the Darkness. It is spoken of only in rumors with no definite proof of its existence. The rumours that speak of it say that it recruits for something called the Court of Midnight.

                                            I will be posting stuff on the Court of Midnight, along with info on "Saliga" a country deep in the Dark World, tomorrow, my time, because it's pretty late where I am at the moment.

                                                        Saligia: A small country located somewhere deep in the Dark World, far from any doors to the material world. Its presence here, so deep in the Dark World is a source of many rumours & the rare Noble who conclusively knows (not someone who has just heard the rumours) of its existence is troubled by the knowledge.
                                                        This country has a very visceral feel to it (maybe, not so sure about this point now).
                                                        The people struggle day in & day out just to survive here.
                                                        It is an oligarchy, ruled by a council comprised of the heads of the seven most powerful families. These seven families are each governed by one of the seven deadly sins (envy, gluttony, greed, lust, pride, sloth, wrath).
                                                        The country also has these "mini-provinces" embodying other negative concepts/ideas/etc. like apathy, despair, violence, vanity, fraud, treachery, etc. It is possible for one of the "mini-provinces" to get a set at the table of the big seven, thereby knocking down one of the current houses, it would be very hard however. Food for thought.
                                                        A while ago, it was found that the Inner Light of a Noble could be used to help Saligia survive, & the country sent out teams of warriors, trackers, beasts, etc. to find some & bring them back.
                                                        Once a Noble has been delivered back to Saliga, a bloody sacrifice is performed, harvesting the Inner Light for the good of Saligia.
                                                        Unbeknownst to Saligia, whilst performing bloody sacrifices of the Nobles provides large, strong bursts, should a Noble set down to live in Saligia their Inner Light would constantly leak into the country, albeit at a slightly weaker level. Oh they may have heard the theory, but why take the risk when they've already got a strategy that works?

                                                            The Court of Midnight: There are rumours of a Court of Nobles that cast their lot in with the Darkness, giving up & electing to join the "winning side". An impossibility to be sure, & yet the rumours persist.

                                                            Echo: people feel unsettled around this person, though they cannot pin why.

                                                            Practical Magic: the Silver Tongue (persuasion) or the Iron Fist (intimidation). A Noble must choose one of these two upon joining the Court.

                                                            Invocation:

                                                            Orders: A series of dark parallels to the Protectorates & Embassies, the Orders are each built to target a different Radiant or Twilight Court. There are rumours of other ones that target the Protectorates & Embassies themselves, & the other groups & creatures in the CofD setting. Some possible Orders follow.

                                                            The Order of Tarnished Images: targets the Nobles of Mirrors, building up their egos only to bring them crashing down. The members of the Order of Tarnished Images go out of their way to avoid recruiting because, just like their targets they have great egos & can't stand the competition.

                                                            The Order of Inverted Pentacles: oppose the Court of Pentacles.

                                                            The Order of Eternal Childhood: targets Searchlight & the Star of Bethlehem.
