    Geryon Series HF Blade ●●●
    The Geryon Series HF Blades are experimental melee weapons utilizing technology that oscillates the blade at extremely high -frequencies, greatly enhancing its cutting power. The materials and construction of the weapon make them incredibly durable, and can slice through even hardened materials without chipping of dulling its edge. The weapons uses a draw-cut blade, similar to a Japanese katana. Each sword comes with a scabbard that contains the charging apparatus and features a bio metric locking mechanism. The weapons proved far too expensive for practical use, and only six were manufactured.


    Damage: +3 Initiative: -2 Strength: 2 Size: 3 Durability: 4(7) Availability: 4

    Powers
    Balance: The weapon is exceptionally well designed, and also provides a +1 equipment bonus to attacks made with the sword.
    Biometric Lock: The weapon’s sheathe can be calibrated to a new user with a successful Intelligence + Craft roll. Once calibrated, the weapon can be locked, preventing the weapon from being drawn by anyone other than its user. Any attempts to tamper with or pick the lock suffer the -3 penalty from Bleeding Edge.
    High Frequency Edge: Once per Chapter, the high-frequency oscillation capabilities of the blade may be engaged. Until the end of the scene, the weapon gains the Piercing 2 tag and ignores two points on an objects Durability. Once used, High Frequency Edge cannot be used again until the weapon is sheathed and recharged for at least 6 hours.

    Drawback
    Bleeding Edge: The Geryon Series is the product of remarkably advanced, but otherwise mundane technology. Any attempts to analyze, repair, or replicate the sword suffer a -3 penalty.
    Gravedigger’s Shovel ●●●
    The “Gravediggers” shovel is a well used spaded shovel with a stout wooden handle and a rusted iron blade. The shovel has several unique properties, although it is not known if those abilities are the result of artifice or happen stance. Despite it age and worn appearance, the shovel is unnaturally sturdy and has passed through many hands over the years. The Shovel grants its equipment bonus to rolls involving digging and shoveling, as well as to Abjuration and Exorcism rolls against Ghosts and similar entities.


    Equipment Bonus:+2 Damage: +1 Initiative: -3 Strength: 2 Size: 2 Durability: 4(7) Tags: Knockdown Availability: 2

    Powers
    Damage dealt by the Gravedigger’s Shovel is considered supernatural damage.
    To use the following abilities, the possessor must spend 3 points of Willpower to gain the Bonded Relic Condition.
    Consecrated: The Gravedigger’s is considered to be a Bane against Ghosts and Death related Ephemeral Entities of Rank 3 or less.
    Gravedigger’s Toil: Any using the shovel to dig, tunnel, or otherwise move earth can reduce the amount of time it takes to complete the task by half. The Shovel also ignores 1 point of durability against objects made of soil, stone or wood.

    Drawback
    Hard Nights Work: Using Gravedigger’s Toil is physically exhausting; once the task in completed, the user takes the Fatigued Condition

        A few new Relics for today

        The Immortal’s Goblet ●●●●
        The Immortal’s Goblet (also called Il Calice dell'Immortalità) is an ornately carved wooden goblet whose origin dates back to 12th century Italy. The legends surrounding the Goblet have often led to it being mistaken for the Holy Grail. It was said to have belonged to Vezio Di Mauro, a reputed alchemist and magician that sought the secrets of eternal life, and performed many strange experiments that are said to have accomplished that goal. The Goblet is currently in the possession of an antiquities dealer in Milan (who appears to be in her forties but is closer to being a century old).

        Size: 1 Durability: 2(6) Availability: 5

        Powers
        To use the following abilities, the possessor must spend 3 points of Willpower to gain the Bonded Relic condition.
        Restoration: The Goblet can restore the body to health from injury and disease. By spending two points of Willpower, making a Presence +Occult roll and drinking wine, beer, or mead from the Immortal’s Goblet, the possessor may heal 2 points of Bashing Damage per success. On an exceptional success, the possessor may heal 1 point of Lethal Damage per success instead. In addition, until the end of the scene, when the possessor suffers a physical Tilt, they may spend 1 Willpower as a reflexive action, ending the Tilt immediately.
        Ageless: As long as Restoration is used at least once every 30 days, the possessor of the Goblet does not age, will not die of natural causes, is immune to non-supernatural disease, and gains a +3 bonus to resist poisons, toxins, and supernatural afflictions. This power only halts aging, and does not restore any youth or vitality already lost to time.

        Drawback
        Fading: If Restoration is not used at least once every 30 days, the benefits of Ageless are lost, the possessor gains the Deprived Condition, and the possessor reverts to their “natural” age. If they have lived well beyond a normal mortal lifespan, this reversion could prove fatal.

        Professor Comet’s Zeta Wave Goggles ●●●
        Professor Comet’s Zeta Wave Goggles are a set of bulky goggles made from metal, rubber, and glass. The Goggles have several buttons and dials along the sides and when not in use is kept in a storage case that contains its recharging and programming apparatus. The storage case also contains a detailed instruction manual, which addresses the reader as “The proud owner of a pair of genuine Professor Comet Zeta Wave Goggles” and is written in a sunny, optimistic tone. The manual also gives steps for repair and maintenance, but strictly advises against trying to replicate the device.

        Size: 1 Durability: 2(5) Availability: 3

        Powers
        Zeta Wave Scanner: The Goggles can detect various form of exotic energy, referred to as “Zeta Waves” in the manual. By spending a point of Willpower and making a Wits+Science rolls, the user gains the ability to perceive a specific “frequency” of energy related to an otherworldly phenomena or realm of existence, such as (but not limited to) The Astral, The Hedge, The Shadow, or The Underworld. The user gains the ability to see ephemeral entities and detect creatures entities related to that realm. In addition, the user gains a +1 equipment bonus to locate or analyze such beings. The Googles can only observe one “frequency” of energy at a time, but can switch frequencies once per turn.
        Security Features: The Zeta Wave Goggles must be calibrated to its user while it recharges. This calibration makes use of the users fingerprints and makes so only a person with matching biometrics can use the device. This process must be repeated once every 24 hours. In addition, design specifics of the device don’t conform to conventional principles and truthfully it shouldn’t function at all. While the device can be disassembled and reassembled relatively easily, any attempt to replicate it creates a non-functional (or dangerously unstable) copy.

        Drawback
        Disorienting: Wearing the Goggles induces a sense of vertigo; the user gains the Sick(moderate) Tilt until the end of the scene. This tilt does not apply on an exceptional activation success.
        Weird Science: The using the Zeta Wave Goggles opens the users mind to a much larger universe. Using the device is a Breaking Point with a -1 penalty.

                                    The Lockhart Glass ●●●
                                    The Lockhart Glass is an oval shaped regency style mirror that dates back to the early1800’s. The gilded frame is carved of mahogany and has a motif of ivy and hemlock. The mirror is in remarkable condition, and has passed through several prestigious collections. The mirror is named for its first documented owner, a wealthy Scottish heiress, who started every morning with an animated conversation with herself while gazing into the mirror.

                                    Size: 3 Durability: 2(5) Availability: 4

                                    Powers
                                    To use the following abilities, the possessor must spend 3 points of Willpower to gain the Bonded Relic condition.
                                    Confidence: By gazing into the mirror and speaking words of encouragement, the user of the mirror finds their resolve bolstered. Once per day, the user may make a Presence+Expression roll. If successful, the user gains the Inspired Condition.
                                    Mirror, Mirror: Once per day, by spending a point of Willpower the user may compel the entity within the Lockhart Glass to do their bidding. The Mirror Entity must obey any non-self destructive command within the scope of abilities that takes less than a day to accomplish.

                                    Drawback
                                    Possessed: The reflection within the mirror seem to have a life of its own. The mirror contains an entity that always has the appearance of the mirror’s user. It behaves like a normal reflection, but may move independently whenever it sees fit. The entity is a trickster, and takes delight in fostering the users dependence on it, as well as subtly gas-lighting them. The entity is bound to the Lockhart Glass, and cannot leave its confines.
                                    Mirror Entity: Rank 2; Influence (Reflections); Numina: Eye Spy (Mirrors, as the Dread Power), Hallucination, Omen Trance, Rapture, Sign
                                    The Scythe ●●●●●
                                    The origins of the weapon known simply as “The Scythe” are unknown. It is a farmers scythe, its haft made of bone-white ashwood and its blade a black metal that catches the light in disconcerting patterns. Antiquarians familiar with it trace its first appearance to the height of the Black Plague in medieval Europe and legends state it was stolen from the grip of Death itself (and that its master stills seeks it). The Scythe was last seen in 1999 in the possession of a self-styled “exorcist” in New Orleans.

                                    Damage: +3 Initiative: -3 Strength: 3 Size: 4 Durability: 2(7) Tags: reach, two-handed Availability: 5

                                    Powers
                                    Damage dealt by The Scythe is considered supernatural damage.
                                    Indestructible: The Scythe cannot be destroyed by mortal means, and is immune to damage for non-supernatural sources and regenerates a point of Structure every night even if it's been destroyed previously.
                                    To use the following abilities, the wielder must spend 5 points of Willpower to gain the Bonded Relic Condition.
                                    The End: The Scythe brings death. By spending 3 points of Willpower and making a successful Presence+ Occult roll, Weaponry attacks made with The Scythe deal Aggravated damage until the end of the scene. Powers and Abilities that allow creatures to return from death must succeed in a Clash of Wills against The Scythe.
                                    Reaper’s Call: The Scythe grants its wielder some power of the forces of death and mortality. As long as the wielder hold the Scythe within their grasp, they gain Influence 3 (Death). She can use this Influence in the same way as a Ghost, spending Willpower instead of Essence.

                                    Drawback
                                    Death’s Price: The Scythe was never meant to be held by the living. Any living, corporeal creature suffers 1point of lethal damage each turn they use The Scythe to make an attack or activate any of its powers. This damage heals at a normal rate, ignoring any supernatural abilities to heal or regenerate injury. Creatures no longer considered “living” (including Mummies, Sin-Eaters, and Vampires) lose Willpower or a point of their power source (Blood, Pillars, Plasm, etc) instead.
                                    Misfortune: Any who wield The Scythe are stalked by death and misfortune. The possessor gains the Danger Magnet Condition for as long as they possess The Scythe.

                                                Re: Lockhart Glass

                                                Limiting the Entity to its immediate surroundings was very deliberate. I wanted a versatile relic with many uses - having an intelligent creature as its main power was the best way to model the effect. It also creates an interesting power dynamic - the Entity is effectively trapped and compelled to obey, but it powers are all things that allow to it manipulate the user mentally and emotionally. Rapture is especially useful here, its a power that essentially lets the Entity dole out doses of high-quality bliss whenever it wants. It can probably smooth most disagreements with the user just by giving them another hit. I agree that Innocuous is a little redundant (and a little boring). I would not want to use Miracle (or other Wish granting powers). The Entity is more devil on the shoulder than genie in the lamp. I think I will go with replacing Innocuous with Know Soul (I like the idea that the Mirror Entity knows the individual using the Glass better than they know themselves).

                                                And a few more Relics!
                                                The Oberon Sword ●●●●●
                                                This sword is of unknown origin and unique design, taking the form of a slender great sword made of a silvery metal with a highly polished sheen. The edge of its blade is razor sharp, and never seems to nick, dull, or rust. The weapon is known for dealing telling blows; decapitating heads, bisecting bodies and severing limbs with disturbing ease. The weapon currently resides in a collection of rare and curious items maintained by a reputed sorcerer.

                                                Damage: +4 Initiative: -5 Strength: 2 Size: 3 Durability: 3(8) Tags: 9-again, two-handed Availability: 5

                                                Powers
                                                Damage dealt by the Oberon Sword is considered supernatural damage.
                                                Indestructible: The Oberon Sword cannot be destroyed by mortal means, and is immune to damage for non-supernatural sources and regenerates a point of Structure every night even if it's been destroyed previously.
                                                To use the following abilities, the possessor must spend 5 points of willpower to gain the Bonded Relic Condition.
                                                Through and Through: By spending 3 points of Willpower, Weaponry attacks made with the Oberon Sword gain the 8-again tag until the end of the scene.
                                                Vorpal Blade: Once per scene, when the wielder with the Bonded Relic Condition scores an exceptional success on an attack with the Oberon Sword against an opponent, they may apply one of the following Tilts: Arm Wrack, Bleeding, Leg Wrack, Pierced Armor.

                                                Drawback
                                                Aspiration: The Oberon Sword has its own Aspiration: Slay Monsters. Whenever the wielder with the Bonded Relic Condition acts in accordance with the Sword’s Aspiration, they gain a point of Willpower. However, if the Oberon Sword’s Aspiration is denied or ignored, it imposes a -2 penalty on tasks involving concentration and the Guilty Condition until it can return to its task.
                                                The Song of Bones ●●●
                                                The macabre item called The Song of Bones (also called “La Canción de Huesos”) is a flute carved from a human femur and inlaid with brass fittings. The object dates back the Spanish Inquisition in the early 1600’s and is reputed to have come from the body of a suspected witch. The maker of the item is unknown, but the craftsmanship has a competent but rushed quality. Despite this, when played it has a crisp tone and is in remarkably good condition despite its age and wear. The item is currently in the possession of a small cult operating out of Mexico. The Song of Bones grants its equipment bonus to Expression rolls when being played or to rolls to Exorcise or Summon Ghosts and similar Death aspected Spirits.

                                                Equipment Bonus: +1 Size: 2 Durability: 2(3) Availability: 4

                                                Powers
                                                Fracturing Note: The flute channels dark power, allow it to strike at the bones of an enemy. When the user plays the lowest note, they may spend 2 points of Willpower and make a Presence +Expression (- the targets Stamina + Supernatural Tolerance) roll. If successful, the target suffers 1 point of bashing damage per success and gains one of the following tilts; Arm Wrack, Leg Wrack, or Stunned. By spending an additional point of Willpower, the damage becomes lethal and the tilts become persistent conditions. Using this power to harm a living creature is a Breaking Point with a -2 penalty. Objects made primarily of bone can also be targeted, subtracting the objects Durability from the roll instead. Living targets that cannot hear the note (or don’t have a skeletal system) are unaffected.
                                                Resonance: The Song of Bones carries the Resonant (Death) Condition.

                                                Drawback
                                                Shun the Wicked: The power of The Song of Bones taints the soul of the user, causing the user to be treated with mistrust and contempt by those they encounter. When Fracturing Note is used against a living creature, the user gains the Notoriety Condition, which effects anyone with an Integrity of 6 or higher. This Condition resolves after [10 -Integrity] days.

                                                    Memetic by GibberingEloquence
                                                    The entity exists as a viral idea instead of a tangible and discreet construct, and knowing about it can be enough to infect someone. Abyssals possessing this Aspect gain the Aggressive Meme Numen for free, and can only use Manifestations besides Twilight Form after a certain set of prerequisites has been met. This usually takes the form of a ritual that, once complete, allows the entity to use all Manifestations. However, the prerequisites can also be a set of apparently disparate behaviors on part of the infected that gradually give more Manifestations to the entity.

                                                    All entities who possess this Aspect spread through a vector, which must be present for the entity to use Aggressive Meme. A vector infects a number of roll types equal to the entity's Rank. An entity themed around mad art can affect Intelligence + Crafts rolls made to try and create a sculpture of its likeness. Anyone who saw this sculpture would roll Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance vs Finesse + Rank. Failure indicates the target is infected by the meme for a number of days equal to Rank, or weeks if the entity gets an Exceptional Success.

                                                    Of note is that Memetic entities lack Corpus. They exist within minds most of the time, so they treat anyone who is infected as being Open. To contain a Memetic entity, one must subvert, destroy and/or eliminate knowledge of the vector. These entities' effective Corpus is measured by their Willpower points. Each contained episode of an Aggressive Meme use takes away 1 willpower point. For each point lost, the entity suffers a cumulative -1 penalty to all rolls. When they lose all Willpower points, the Open Condition is considered to be suppressed, but care must be taken to contain the vector so the meme does not spread again.

                                                    Although they cannot Materialize of use Influences on physical structures until the prerequisites have been met, Memetic entities can influence the minds of the infected via Urging/Influences/Numina so they act as proxies, often to spread the meme and attack those who want to contain it.

                                                    Presence of Paradox by amechra
                                                    The ephemeral being is thoroughly tainted by the stuff of the Abyss - treat it as if it were an entire crowd of Sleepers for the purposes of calculating Paradox dice pools, except it adds dice equal to its Rank rather than just one. In addition, it may spend Essence reflexively to cancel Mana spent to mitigate a Paradox dice pool on a one-to-one basis.
