    Quick idea for new Integrity system for Geniuses: I'd like to contrast Lemurians and Peers a bit. Yes, both organizations are stuffed with mad scientists, some more lucid or more in denial than others, but the way they look at their creations, mania, and other supernaturals are polar opposite. Peers are constantly in a state of cynical disbelief, they must disbelief their own paradigm to avoid becoming unmada slip-slopping into Illumination. When they confront a new unknown phenomena they must keep their cool and approach it firstly as a fraud, either by mania or mortal charlatan. This makes the Law of living without lies partially enforced in gameplay. Peers risk Transgression when they believe false information.

    Lemuria couldn't careless about Peers and their dumb laws. They have Archweltanschaaung! Every theory is correct! Every paradigm is true! The world is beautiful cacophony of contradictions of realities and Lemurians know it to be so. "Look at Bardos and manes! Aren't they proof enough that reality and truth are multifaceted?" If Peers rather disbelief truth than believe lies, Lemuria are the opposite. To Lemurians the slippery slope into Illumination isn't belief in lies, it's disbelief of truth. When Lemurian claims something as impossible especially if someone believes it, they assert themselves as someone "more" than that person which may quickly lead to isolation, destabilization, and Illumination. Lemurian's risk Transgression when they disbelieve in something (especially if that something is real.)

                                                I got board and decided to write up a morality trait. I went with Ethics for the name, since I looked at it from a different angle and it clicked nicely. Ethics always was my favourite word since it's nice and sciencey. And when I realised you could describe ethics as the product of "comprehension" it all fit - the Genius knows his morality is mad, thus he replaces his morals with a code of ethics from the consensus .



                                                Ethics
                                                From Up Here They Look Like Ants

                                                A genius' Breakthrough, once she has mastered his first Axioms and created her first wonders, is often accompanied by a godlike feeling of invincibility and of escape from the fetters of the mundane world. But a genius cannot escape humanity, at least while keeping his humanity intact.

                                                Instead, a genius is bound to humanity by ties of Ethics. The genius can never again trust his own sense of morality, of right and wrong. Instead he must rely upon the consensus to provide a code of Ethics. To the Inspired following the rules and dogmas of small minded mortals is as much a chain as an anchor, and Inspiration cannot be truly chained. A Genius will break free, he can only choose when and how: Not too often, and only after carefully thinking through the morality, consequences, and potential benefits of his actions.

                                                That's the ideal, and few Inspired live up to it. Even peers are willing to use their own moral intuition, while Lemuria sees it's philosophies as superior to the consensus. Those Paragons who retain their sense of Ethics are rare, especially as the pressure of finding test subjects, grant money, and a place to work grows more fierce as a genius grows in ability and Inspiration. Powerful geniuses often see themselves more as conduits for the raw stuff of Inspiration than as people who happen to be Inspired, and behave accordingly. Those acts that violate a genius' sense of Ethics are called transgressions. Too many, too often, with too little regard for their consequences, can drive a genius into true madness





                                                Modified Advantage: Ethics (Integrity)

                                                Good men don't need rules. Today is not the day to find out why I have so many of them.
                                                - The Doctor

                                                The Inspired are extreme creatures, capable of great and terrible transgressions. To fight the corrosive influence of those crimes against the natural order, geniuses focus on the rules and taboos of mundane humanity: Which they quantify in a code of Ethics.

                                                Ethics measures a Genius' wiliness and ability to to ignore their Inspiration, and how far they are from becoming a true mad scientist, one of the Illuminated who has lost all concern for the well-being and dignity of others, and who sees humans as we might see laboratory mice. As Ethics falls, the genius becomes less and less able to understand the rules of normal sane behaviour and less interested in doing so for any decent reason.

                                                Geniuses quickly learn that Ethics is more than morality: it is tied to the standards of sane and normal behaviour that humans apply to their fellows. Many acts that a genius can rationalize as for the greater good―or even demonstrate to be beneficial and useful―are transgressions, since they violate the standards of regular human behavior and erode a genius' ability to mimic sanity.

                                                Transgression:

                                                When a genius acts in a way that violates his Ethical standards, he commits a transgression. The genius must make a degeneration roll to see if he loses a dot of Ethics. If an act can be described by two different levels of transgression, use the lower one on the chart.

                                                ROLL RESULTS GO HERE

                                                Transgressions include, from least to most heinous:

                                                Actions that imply a lack of respect for the dangerous power of Inspiration,
                                                Actions that regular humans find gross, improper, or "cold" (whether or not they're actually immoral),
                                                Actions that physically or psychologically distance the genius from humanity,
                                                Actions that display a cold or reckless disregard for civilization and/or the rule of law, and
                                                Actions that stem from a genius' willingness to see humans as disposable, replaceable, or beneath consideration.

                                                The list below is not complete or all-inclusive; the Storyteller is expected to examine questionable actions from a genius to see if they fit with the general pattern of transgressions listed below, and to warn the genius' player of a transgression that the mad scientist is poised to commit.

                                                ETHICS CHART GOES HERE (The Obligation chart will probably work as is)


                                                "The more we do to you, the less you seem to believe we are doing it."
                                                -Dr. Joseph Mengele

                                                Ethics and Integrity:

                                                Beholden and unInspired manes possess Integrity. Geniuses, Inspired manes, and intelligent automata possess Ethics.

                                                Benefits of Ethics:

                                                Paragon Status:

                                                A genius with an Ethics of 7 or higher qualifies as a Paragon. People have difficulty imagining that the genius is up to no good. Even the most maladjusted genius, if he possesses high Ethics, is automatically seen as a trustworthy authority figure by regular mortals, someone to be admired for his intelligence and contributions to humanity. Anyone trying to tar the genius' reputation suffers a -3 penalty to the attempt, his indiscretions and gaffs are written off as a brilliant eccentricity.

                                                Drawbacks of Ethics:
                                                The Dampness of the Sea

                                                Iracus was warned not too fly too low or else the water would clog his wings. Exceptional Geniuses who maintain an Ethics of 8 more more find that living with so many rules cramps and limits their Inspiration. Some find it a welcome relief from the mania of Inspired life, most pace their cell and struggle against their chains, and all of them find it harder to draw on their Inspiration.

                                                A Genius who maintains an Ethics of 8 or more treats their Inspiration as two dots lower (minimum of 1) when using Deep Inspiration.

                                                The Heat of the Sun:

                                                TODO: Basically it works like Werewolf 2E's death rage triggers. The lower Ethics is, the easier Jabir is to trigger. When Jabir happens either your enemy gains a positive Condition (oops! You told Mr Bond your evil plan) or you take a negative condition (everyone on the Tenure Committee thinks you're crazy).

                                                The Broken Chains:

                                                Should a genius reach zero Ethics, she becomes one of the Illuminated. Freed from all ethical constraints, she becomes an abomination of science and forbidden secrets, able to see humans only as victims and objects.


                                                Science Hero (OOO, OOOOO)

                                                The Genius gains a Virtue, it need not be the same one they had before their Breakthrough but it must represent an altruistic drive to act for the good of others or for mankind as a whole.

                                                Whenever she is acting in accordance with her Virtue she treats her Ethics as 2 dots higher, to a maximum of 10. In addition if their Ethics is 6 or more (not counting this Merit) they may attempt to take charge of Mortals who are facing a chaotic and dangerous situation in which the Genius has unique insight by spending a point of Willpower and rolling Ethics.

                                                ROLL RESULTS GO HERE – if you've seen Doctor Who you know what's going on here.

                                                The five dot varient of this Merit allows the Genius to regain Willpower from her Virtue, as though she were a regular mortal.

                                                Drawback

                                                Whenever the Genius is acting in an immoral, selfish or low manner they treat their Ethics as two dots lower, to a minimum of 1. As a rule of thumb if the average mortal would consider it to be immoral or a moral obligation then a Science Hero must behave appropriately or else suffer the drawback.
