    Gilded Cage conversion

    02-04-2016, 02:25 AM
    Edit: Darn it, all my formatting went away when I posted. Let's see if I can fix that.

    Yes, I know somebody else has already done this, but it wasn't the way I wanted to do it. Blood Sorcery (the book) indicates that new forms of blood sorcery have different motifs, rather than different themes, and implementing new themes means having to define what those themes are.

    And more to the point, I want to be able to spin up new forms of blood sorcery quickly if I find I want one. Gilded Cage is just a good test case, to see whether I know what I need to know to be able to do the next blood sorcery discipline, not something I need right now.

    So, here we go...

    Gilded Cage
    Motifs
    Gilded Cage is territorial- grand architecture can make a hundred different statements, but the workings of a sorcerer of the Gilded Cage say one thing louder than anything else: This place is mine. The land itself bends to the will of its master, and its true lord will brook no challenge there.

    Gilded Cage is geometric(or perhaps geomantic, if you prefer) - it bends and directs energies in patterns, be they straight lines or circles or ever-widening spirals. Space, and the matter that fills space or the emptiness where it does not, inscribes a design that may be impossible to recognise from inside, on the ground, but is there nonetheless to the sorcerer with the eyes to spy its beauty.

    Gilded Cage is deliberate- like the Theban Sorcery that inspired it, the rituals of the Gilded Cage are carefully planned and plotted. It's not easy, it’s not fast, and it's not well suited to improvisation.

    Dice Pools
    One Roll: Intelligence + Academics + Gilded Cage

    Bloody Sorcery system:
    Improvised: Wits + Occult + Theme
    Creation: Intelligence + Academics + Creation
    Destruction: Intelligence + Crafts + Destruction
    Divination: Intelligence + Academics + Divination
    Protection: Resolve + Survival + Protection
    Transmutation: Wits + Streetwise + Transmutation

    Themes
    The linked themes of the Gilded Cage are Divination and Transmutation.

    Creation
    Creation rites are rarely used by practitioners of the Gilded Cage. When it is used, the effects are massive - Metropolis of the United Diagram adding to space itself. Somewhat lesser rituals have been known to add huge structures to cities overnight. Doing so without attracting notice in highly regulated modern cities is perhaps even more difficult than performing the ritual itself.

    Destruction
    The Architects of the Monolith are builders, and so generally much more interested in putting edifices up than tearing them down. However, their patterns are as much about empty space as the structures that define its boundaries, and sometimes a district just has to be swept away to make way for something new. Like Creation, Destruction is not favoured by the practitioners of the Gilded Cage, but when used its effects are often very impressive.

    Divination
    Divination is one of the mainstays of the Gilded Cage. The sorcerer attunes himself to the energy of the city, the ebb and flow of energy, people, traffic; and with the knowledge comes certainty of movement and action. While some rituals will alter the city to suit the sorcerer’s purpose, it is often far easier for him to take advantage of the existing patterns than to try to reshape large swathes of the pattern. Especially so when the pattern is just all laying there for he who opens his eyes to see.

    Protection
    Protection serves two purposes in the Gilded Cage: calling the city itself to protect its master, and preserving the city. The former can be seen as overkill, as putting an entire city in motion to preserve the existence of one architect is often much harder than ensuring that enemies never meet the architect. The latter use is much less common today than it once was, in the days when cities were besieged at their outer walls.

    Transmutation
    The alterations produced by the Gilded Cage are often far more subtle than transformations seen in other forms of ritual magic. The stone, brick and concrete of the city are far too set to easily shift. Instead, it is the unseen that is altered - energy, feeling, behaviour on a scale so large that nobody truly has a controlling intent. (One person has will, but the crowd is mindless.)

    Rituals
    The required successes I've given here are for reproducing something much like the original effect of the rituals. There's no reason many of the effects can't be varied per the Blood Sorcery rules for ritual factors, though. STs should be careful about range reductions, because it often is not appropriate (and even makes some rituals more powerful) if the sorcerer isn't using including all of the territory in the effect. For descriptions, see Blood Sorcery.

    About “Territory Size”
    In these bare-bones mechanical implementations of the Gilded Cage rituals, the term “territory size” is sometimes used. The territory in question is the location in which the sorcerer’s Gilded Cage applies. It may not include an entire city, but generally will include at least a precinct or a CBD (central business district). Territory size is the range required to cover this area. (It is not related to the Size ritual factor.) This can result in many Gilded Cage rituals requiring a lot of successes, which is fitting for effects that cover such large areas even if the effects are relatively simple.
    Also, note that territory size is actually dependent on where the sorcerer is at the time the ritual is performed. If the sorcerer is near the centre of the territory, it would be based roughly on the radius of the territory. However, if the sorcerer is near the outskirts the required range might double. Gilded Cage practitioners prefer to perform their rites from near the centre of their territory as a result.

    Path of the Prey
    Divination 2
    Duration +3, adds Potency to hunting roll

    Red Light
    Transmutation 2
    Range must encompass the ritualist’s territory. Duration +2 or +3. Potency increases travel time by 25% per success. This ritual is seemingly simple, but because of the large ritual factors involved the number of successes required will be unusually high.

    Green Light(affecting another)
    Divination 2, Transmutation 1
    Range must encompass the ritualist’s territory. Duration +2. Potency decreases travel time by 10% per success.
    Required successes: 5 + territory size.

    Green Light (affecting the caster)
    Divination 2, Transmutation 1
    Duration +3. Potency decreases travel time by 10% per success.
    Required successes: 6.

    Aura of the Monolith
    Transmutation 3
    Duration +3. Potency adds bonus dice.
    Required successes: 6.

    Gather the Herd
    Transmutation 3
    Range must encompass the ritualist’s territory. Duration +2. Number of targets +7 (for about 100 targets). Extra successes add to number of targets.
    Required successes: 12 + territory size.

    Eye of the Pyramid
    Divination 2
    Size must be big enough to encompass all of the largest building targeted, which means typically +4 (small apartment building) to +7 (large skyscraper). Range must encompass all buildings targeted, which means typically territory size, but 0 if the sorcerer targets a single building where she performs the ritual.
    Required successes: 6+.

    Lock the Gilded Cage
    Transmutation 2, Divination 5
    Range must encompass the ritualist’s territory. Duration +3. Potency extends the duration of the effect by an extra day per success.
    Required successes: 10 + territory size.
    The changes made to the behaviour of the city are not of a greater type than the related Red Light ritual, but to block egress from the territory entirely those changes must be deployed with the extreme accuracy of Divination 5. Note that counter-magic is not allowed under the Blood Sorcery system, so this ritual cannot shut down supernatural methods of escaping (but it can still counter effects that would aid escape, such as Celerity).

    Metropolis of the United Diagram
    Creation 5
    Range covers the distance between the sorcerer and her destination.
    Required successes: 5 + range.
    This ritual is an example of the great power invested in the rare Creation rituals of the Gilded Cage. It creates a new spatial connection - essentially a wormhole - between where the caster is and where she wants to be, and allows her to step through it.

          Pick a building and cast the ritual. For the rest of the night as long as you are in the building or its in line of sight, you can use the activation successes as a bonus to any roll using a social attribute or skill in line with the symbolism of the building. For example, An opera house can boost expression rolls, a law firm could boost manipulation or persuasion rolls, a telecommunications tower could boost presence rolls.
