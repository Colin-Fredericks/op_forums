        You are not correct.

        At 32 Points the Ban for the Pack is already a Rank 5 Ban. And would require a Pack of 7 (assuming you do not allow them to go above 5 dots each) to invest 32 Dots of Totem between them (good thing they are only 3 Points/Dot). You cannot take a more sever Ban than a Rank 5 to reduce the cost at that point.

        You CAN take additional Spirit Bans (which applies only to the Spirit and not the Pack) to gain more power for the Spirit, however.

                    Each dot of Totem costs only 3 XP. No Incremental Costs for Totem.
                    The Extra Ban is a Numen, as described below...

                    Ban of Power: By taking on an additional ban, spirits can increase their power. Each additional ban can give the spirit one of these awards: two Numina (a net increase of one), one Willpower or an increase of two maximum Essence. A spirit may take up to three additional bans, but they may only have one particular award. Each additional ban, including the first, must be more punitive than the spirit’s default ban.
