            In honor of the fact that Mage could be dropping any time now, here's a bunch of Mage ideas I've had, though obviously I haven't come up with normal names for them yet.

            •Thistle
            An Acanthus Théarch, my idea for Thistle is that he was the son of people who were very powerful and very rich. He grew up wanting for nothing and eventually grew into a spoiled sybarite who flew from party to party and relied on mommy and daddy's wealth and prestige to get him and his fellow hedonists out of trouble... until one day he stumbled into a scandal that (while his parents were still able to suppress) finally made them decide enough was enough. On the threat of being cut off, he was sent off on a "vacation" to a small cottage in the English countryside, which led him to notice the strange things going on, which led to him grudgingly befriending the brownies that lived in the walls, which led to a thrilling adventure where... well, long story short, he grew out of his entitlement and fickleness and now burns with a subtle ambition to do something meaningful. In regards to the Arcana, he tends towards the Subtle, with a special focus on Fate and Mind, the later of which he'd likely make Ruling through a Legacy. My biggest issue with him is that I'm worried I wouldn't have enough Merit dots to fufill his concept.


            •Brother Acheron
            A Moros Libertine, he was a pastor who over the years had become more focused on the trappings of religion rather than his actual faith, until his Awakening showed him what really mattered. Now he's stepped down from his position to not only work as a spiritual advisor to his fellow Mages, but to help the unquiet dead, who need someone to help them let go of the trappings that bind them to this world. He's taken to the study of the Arcana quite zealously, believing that studying them will bring him closer to God. He obviously focuses on Death, though I imagine he'd dip into Mind and Life (to diagnose and cure ailments both physical and mental) and Prime (for religious reasons).


            •Mutt
            Probably the one of these I've put the least thought into, my starting point for him was "Wolblooded Mage". Currently I have him as a Thyrsus Arrow, probably with the Tell that grants fast healing. My idea is that he grew up poor, and still comes off as a touch rough around the edges, "young, scrappy, and hungry" as a popular musical might put it. A brawler somewhat lacking in social graces, he never the less bears a fondness for the low of station. By day, he works into community outreach programs to deal with troubled and impoverished youth, and by night he turns his Sight on the forces that would prey upon these people. His primary Arcanum would be Life, and his magic in general would focus on self-buffing and facilitating his fighting style of "Punch it hard and fast until it stops moving."
