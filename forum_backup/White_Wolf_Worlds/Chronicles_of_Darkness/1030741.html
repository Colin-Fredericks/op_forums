    Supernatural Thermodynamics [Just For Fun!]

    01-12-2017, 04:06 PM
    because who has said that you can't use science to explain things in CofD? :P

    (note- as people may expect, this is not going to be 100% accurate, scientific speaking, but it is still fun. I blame my statistical thermodynamics professor for this :P).

    From the Thesis of Norman McFarland, scientist of the Null Mysteries

    Thermodynamics is a science studying the transference and conversion of different forms of energy into work. Supernatural Thermodynamics, in that case, is used in order to explain the so called form of "Supernatural Energy"- that is, energy as been conserved through a supernatural medium, such as supernatural beings. My study, however, has showed that the very term "Supernatural Energy" could be quit misleading, as different supernatural beings do not conserve or convert energy in the same manner. In fact, it has been shown that in order to fully understand the nature of Supernatural Thermodynamics, we have to define a new set of dimensions and units of measurements, parallel to the more standard model as used to the research of the natural world.

    First, before we could start investigate the nature of Supernatural Energy, we need to define the meaning of energy- a complicate subject by itself. For the purpose of this article, we shall define energy as movement or the potential for movement. We shall divide the forms of supernatural energy into two categories- potential energy (meaning, the energy the object may use to move) and kinetic energy (the energy invested in the movement of the item, which could be represented as Ek = m(v^2)/2 ). Transference of energy into the surroundings could through two ways- Work (or transference of energy required to preform a process) and Dissipation (the amount of energy "lost" to the surroundings). The total amount of energy in a system could be defined through the energy conserved in its dimensions and the appropriate variable which defined the amount of energy in that dimension. Each such pair of variables should be called Conjugated Variable, when one is called an "extensive variable" (that is, one which grows linearly by adding another system) and an "intensive variable" (that is, one which does not depends on the size of the system). Generally, the variable representing the dimension should be the extensive variable and the one defining the partition of energy would be the intensive one. Examples for such variable would be volume and pressure, electric charge and voltage or entropy and temperature.

    Now, after that sort introduction, we can start defining the forms of Supernatural Energy. First, we shall investigate the most common forms of supernatural beings, usually known as "Ephemeral Beings". That vast category covers, in fact, a great number of different phenomena, from the restless shades of the dead and primal animistic representation to bio-mechanical creations and mental constructs. Considering the vastness of this category, we shall first focus around the first two kinds of beings- most commonly known as "ghosts" and "spirits".

    While the very basic nature of either of those beings is quit different, my research has shown that the two do share certain common features in terms of Supernatural Thermodynamics. While one is based around the residual presence of a soul and the other is formed from coalescence of primal energy of the world, the mechanism behind them is quit similar. Through investigation of previous texts and articles about the subject, we can deduct that both of those beings require a substance known as "Essence", which we shall now define as the appropriate dimension (that is, the Essential Dimension)- which means we shall refer to Essence as the extensive variable. According to the occultists, the amount of power such being has is called "Rank". While it saddens me to use such a non-scientific term, in order to avoid future confusion with related articles we shall refer to Rank as the conjugated, intensive variable, with units of [energy/essence].

    If we assume that energy is uniformly distributed in the Essential Dimension (that is, that each Essence in the universe holds the same amount of energy), we could calculate the value of Rank in [kJ/esse]. Experimental measurements shows that the most simple of Ephemeral Beings have a Rank value of 10 kJ/esse, while the most powerful ones recorded may reach 50 kJ/esse. The progression in power, while seems linear at the beginning, change its trend starts sharply around the middle of its power. That level of change corresponds to a change in the phase of the system, indicating that once the Rank of the being reaches the 50 kJ/esse limit something fundamental change in the structure of the system, moving it into a new state (perhaps even on the quantum level).

    It should be noted that those results must be taken with cautions- for the lack of accessible ghosts with a Rank value of more than 15 kJ/esse and the inability to to preform frequent experiments upon high Rank spirits proves to be problematic at best.

    As it was mentioned in the last paragraph, no ghost may exist spontaneously (meaning, without preforming Work) in the material world with a Rank of more than 15 kJ/esse. That shows that this state of system in incredibly unstable, and that the Rank value of ghosts cause further instability in the universe. In order to balance that disruption in potential, the ghost is moved a tangent system to the material world- that is, the Underworld- which returns the world into an equilibrium. Spirits, on the other hand, do not suffer from such instability, which means two things- first, the Rank value of spirits is different from the one of ghosts, and as such is defined through a different Essential Dimension (meaning, spiritual Essence is different than macabre Essence, although the two are quit similar from the outside). Second, the universe has an higher spiritual Rank than macabre one, allowing it contain more powerful spirits. That shows that while ghosts look more "human like" than spirits, spirits are more integral to the world than ghosts- which may rise interesting questions about the place of humanity in the world.

    Anyway, enough philosophy. Returning to the subject, Both spirits and ghosts seem to exist in the material world in a new state of matter called "Twilight"- which seems to be an high-energy phase in which interactions between them and the physical are negligible. Changing from a Twilight state of matter into a physical one is called Manifestation. Such process usually require a transmission of energy to the environment in the form of Essence, making them into exothermic processes. Some create weak only weak interactions with the material world, such as creating an image (amount of energy released in the process, or enthalpy, is around 1 kJ/esse) while others create some powerful, and sometimes irreversible interactions similar to covalent bonds (enthalpy of around 5 kJ/esse). Certain interactions are unique to certain ephemeral being types, further indicating that the general division of such beings is misleading and may cause scientific miscalculations, as both are actually different systems.

    Now, let us investigate the nature of Essence as a dimension. First, all ephemeral beings seem to naturally lose Essence to the world through Dissipation. That further support the claim that the natural world has a low Rank value comparing to such beings. It further makes the case for the spiritual stability interesting, as it suggests that both the spiritual and the macabre Rank values are of the same size- but perhaps the Shadow (as the spirit world is called) simply has greater congruence with the material world than the Underworld has, allowing spiritual Essence to flow better than the macabre type. Such dissipation is hasten while the being is found in a more energetic state of matter- for such state in unstable and the universe moves the system back to equilibrium. It should be noted that in order to truly destroy an ephemeral being, it must lack all of its Essence- meaning that Essence is a form of movement which allows existence, similar to chemical potential. Such beings may also use Essence in order to strength themselves, reinforcing their very existence into the world. Through that interpretation, it can be deducted that the reason ghosts are forced into the Underworld at higher Ranks is simply that the world does not contain enough "macabric existence", making that state unstable. In sort, the world is alive, and as no place for the dead.

    Still, those dimensions does exist in the world around us- macabre Essence seem to be generated around ghostly anchors, that is places which reinforce the existence of the shade, and once someone is reminded of them. That notion reinforce the idea of "Essence as level of existence" and a proof that the world may convert non supernatural energy into a supernatural one. Spirits, however, reinforce themselves through appropriate sources of existence and actually have to take that energy by force from the world. That may hint that while spiritual Essence is a natural part of the universe, spirits are not, as unlike ghosts they lack direct interaction with the source of their Essence.

    Other than the basic functions of the systems, ghosts and spirits may channel Essence through two modes of Work- Numina and Influence. Influence is a direct representation of the kinetic energy of an ephemeral entity- meaning, movement through the world using the same dimension as Essence. By using an Influence, the system actually expands itself, transmitting energy to the environment and preforming work. Influences are always direct representation of the entity's existence, further supporting the claim of them being an action preformed in the Essential Dimension. Numina, on the other hand, is a set of specific functions available to the entity, or specialized forms of Work. The total level of Influence a system has is equal to their Rank value, while the number of Numina is governed by their Rank- meaning that while Influence is movement through constant Essence Numina is movement through constant Rank. It should be noted that ephemeral entities do lose Essence through Influences, but I assume that such lost of energy is done out of Dissipation (see the second law of thermodynamics). Numen generally cost more energy to preform, as they lose both energy to Work and Dissipation, further supporting the claim.

    Finally, before finishing that section of the article, it should be noted that certain non ephemeral beings do have Rank value of their own- Werewolves, for example seem to have spiritual Rank, while certain mediums have a macabre one. That shows that those beings may contain their energy through more than their own, singular dimension, and instead be able to move through other dimensions and effect the corresponding system.

    end of section

                                              As for the subject of thought and Essence, I recommend on Nuwisha Nutjob's observation for the difference between emotion and thought. While I have agreed to the concept of thought having the ability to Influence Essence, I have not meant that such process eventually leads to a "Unified Essence Field" theory or anything similar to that. Instead, just as Essence is being divided into different "modes of existence" which represent movement in the corresponding Essential Dimension, I believe that the human mind should also be divided into different "modes of thought", each representing another form of existence. Such modes of thought should be corresponding to the different Essence types which they represent the form of existence they perceive- macabre Thought (or "memory"), spiritual Thought ("emotion" or "instincts") and astral Thought ("imagination"/"creativity"). I further wish to add another case study which shows that humans seems to not include all different "modes of Thought" required on preforming Work upon all different Essential Dimension- that is what some call as "angels". Those beings of ephemera do not regain their Essence (meaning, reinforce their existence) through a direct mode of Thought, but through sacrifice (unless one is to suggest the way they reinforce their existence in such a way is not from the sacrifice itself, but from the willingness of the one preforming the sacrifice to do so, using a mode of Thought called "servitude").

                                              While the exploration of the Essential Fields as quantum entities does sound interesting, I have a problem with the ideal of ephemeral beings being the product of the Essential Wave Function Collapsing- for I support the Everett Interpretation over Copenhagen's. After all, different case studies (such as Boston and Seattle) seem to support that interpretation over the more conventional one. Besides, even if you take Copenhagen into account, it looks like ephemeral beings violate certain aspects of the quantum mechanics- such as Zeno Effect, for not only that constant measurement does not stop the passage of time for the system, it actually rise its energy beyond the original energetic level. However, I must admit that I am no expert on quantum mechanics, and if there is such expert he or she is welcomed to add her/his opinion.
