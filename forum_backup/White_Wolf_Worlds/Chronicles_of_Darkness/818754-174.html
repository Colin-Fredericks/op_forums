Just like how Dominate doesn't work on animals either, nor does Majesty, nor does Nightmare to the best of my knowledge. For animals, you have the Animalism Discipline. If you don't have Animalism, then your control over animals is limited (though using your Predatory Aura against them should work).                                                         I'd go one step further and say that most Specialties can work on most Skills. I wouldn't even divide it into Christian History/Christian Miracles because that divide comes naturally from what the different Skills applies to, and it makes Interdisciplinary Specialty hella more useful if you don't limit your Specialty in such a way. Just set Christianity on whichever Skill you want.

                                                        Edit: Some Skills where Christianity might apply (either as a direct Specialty or through Interdisciplinary Specialty):
                                                        Academics: History of Christianity, ease of studying related scripture, theology, recognising Christian rituals
                                                        Computer: Ease of searching for bible passages (if you'd have to roll for it)
                                                        Occult: Recognising biblical miracles, recognising Christian artefacts, angelology, interpreting omens relating to the book of Revelations, recognising Christian rituals
                                                        Empathy: Comforting someone who has a crisis of faith, see through lies about someone's denomination, receiving a confession
                                                        Expression: Holding a sermon
                                                        Persuasion: Theological argumentation
                                                        Subterfuge: Lie about your or see through lies about someone's denomination or faith

                                                        Some are definitely a bit more useful than others and some would benefit more from a related Specialty. I can definitely see priests take Sermons and Theology as Specialties in Expression and Academics on top of a more general Christianity Specialty (remember that Specialties do stack). It really depends on what you want to get out of it.
