                            Gotcha; I'll stop making suggestions about changing the powers

                            My suggestion for Swarm simplification, then, would be to Merit-ize it. Have Swarm and Servant be Merits. Each dot in Authority gives a free dot to invest in one of those merits. More dots in a single one means more power (greater size, health, dice pools, whatever); you can choose to throw all your points in one, or have a bunch of single-dot ones. So an Authority 3 Monarch could have one 3-dot Swarm, a 1-dot Swarm and a 2-dot Servant, three 1-dot Servants, etc. Maybe base Swarm off of Staff, with Servant off of Retainer or 1e Hedgebeast Companion or something.

                            Additionally, they can be bought as regular Merits. For the powers, if they already have a dot level, say that the corresponding Swarm/Servant needs to have at least that many dots to use the ability. This way, you don't have to track any new sort of resources, and you can have different playstyles: on Monarch who uses their minimum dots to get a few Swarms to maintain their Colony, but invests the rest of their XP/powers in themself; another who buys a lot of Servants, but isn't too much more powerful than them; one who overwhelms their foes with lots of weak bugs; and so forth.

                            EDIT: Making them Merits also makes the mechanics match the flavor of your biomass idea, due to Sanctity of Merits: you would be investing pieces of yourself into the Swarm/Servants, but then be able to get them back when they were destroyed. If you were tired of having them, or needed the XP for something else, you could destroy them yourself, get back the biomass/XP, and do other things with it.
