    Demon got free healing?

    11-18-2013, 10:17 AM
    I wasn't big fan of the old world of darkness - but then came new world of darkness. I was impressed - one thing that i really loved ( and hated lack of it in the ascension ) was one simply uwritten rule - no free healing. And then demon came and destroyed my dreams of system clean of this game breaking thing - its called on the mend. It's not only crazy powerful, but it also break things already estabilished in the book. As far i understand embeds are simple intuitive abilities, that look pretty concidental and exploits are powerful more out of this world abilities that are coming from powering up a lot the embeds. We do have really nice exploit - Break to Heal. Why it works is perfectly layed out with nice philosophy, it is costly as every other exploit - point of aether and compromise roll. And its great. But if we look like on the mend - its not weaker than break to heal! Yes we do have weaker exploit than embed , embed that cost nothing and even heals aggravated wounds! And why it's doing it? Because destroyers are masters of destroying things and can reverse it to heal... It have no sense. Regrow instantly a hand that was nuked off by mage by erasing it from reality is in fact REALLY supernatural. And having someone with this ability and 7 dice means that demons will get always fully healed after a battle which in my opinion kills all this paranoia feeling that this game i thought should be about. I really do hope this is just a draft and it can still be changed - if it's not - sorry but i am out. I do have a lot of core books of new world of darkness sitting on my shelf - i was really happy when i read god machine chronicle i really thought that there is a really nice new quality to the new polished system and all that is after that will be glorious. But now... All what i see is old world of darkness happening again - and its a sad day.
