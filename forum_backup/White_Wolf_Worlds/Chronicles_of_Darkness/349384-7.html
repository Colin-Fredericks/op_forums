        Betelgeuse “Beetlejuice”
        Virtue: Humor – Betelgeuse is an irreverent wisecracker who makes light of any situation, no matter how grim, dire, or morbid.
        Vice: Troublemaker - Betelgeuse is an incorrigible mischief maker and driven to cause trouble wherever he goes. He is also a lecherous, debauched creature that lives (un) life to its fullest.

        Rank: 3 [Ghost]
        Attributes: Power 8, Finesse 8, Resistance 8
        Integrity: 5
        Willpower: 10
        Essence: 20
        Initiative: 16
        Defense: 8
        Speed: 21
        Size: 5
        Corpus: 13
        Influences: Fear 3
        Ban: Speaking his name 3times in his presence forces Betelgeuse to discorporate.
        Bane: Sandworms
        Manifestation: Discorporate, Materialize, Possess, Twilight Form
        Numina: Awe, Dement, Flight of Shadows, Hallucination, Seek, Phantom Arsenal, Telekinesis

        Notes: This presents the title character (and “Ghost with the Most”) from the film. Betelgeuse is considered to be a very potent Ghost of indeterminate age (no one knows for sure, but he jokes about having lived through the Black Plague). He has immense raw power, he is quick witted (even though he is as subtle as a sledgehammer) and shows remarkable tenacity (even though he is a slave to his many, many vices). He find’s existence in the Underworld terribly boring and is constantly seeking ways to permanently anchor himself in the material world.

            Goliath

            Virtue: Justice; Goliath is bold and fiercely protective of his people. While occasionally stubborn, his sense of righteousness always guides his actions.
            Vice: Temper; Goliath’s anger is a terrible thing to behold, and he can become bitterly vengeful against persistent foes.

            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 3, Resolve 5
            Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 4, Stamina 5
            Social Attributes: Presence 5, Manipulation 2, Composure 3

            Skills: Academics 2 (Literature), Athletics 4 (Climbing), Brawl 5, Crafts 1 (Masonry), Empathy 2, Expression 3(Inspiring), Intimidation 5, Investigation 3, Occult 2 (Sorcery), Persuasion 3(Leadership), Politics 2, Stealth 4(Castles), Survival 4 (Navigation), Weaponry 2 (Improvised)

            Merits: Area of Expertise (Climbing, Leadership), Common Sense, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Demolisher 2, Direction Sense, Giant, Hardy 3,Inspiring, Iron Stamina 3, Iron Will, Language (Archaic English, Modern English, likely others), Professional Training 4(Warrior; Brawl, Intimidation, Persuasion), Safe Place 4 (Clock Tower), Status 5 (Manhattan Clan), Striking Looks 2, Style: Close Quarter Combat 4, Style: Grappling 2, Style: Heavy Weapons 1, Style: Street Fighting 5, Trained Observer 1, True Friend (Det. Elisa Maza)

            Willpower: 8
            Potency: 4 [Mortal Remains, pg. 104-105]
            Size: 6
            Initiative: 8
            Armor: 3/1
            Defense: 8
            Speed: 15 [Species Factor 6]
            Health: 11

            Dread Powers
            Dread Attack: Goliath has claws that are razor sharp and hard as stone; they are considered a +1 weapon with AP 1.

            Leaping: Goliath’s leaping and jumping distances are tripled.

            Monstrous Resilience: Goliath is incredibly sturdy and can shrug off grievous wounds. When he suffers a physical Tilt, he may spend 1 willpower as a reflexive action, ending the Tilt immediately.

            Roar: This Dread Power allows Goliath to unleash an unearthly bellow, striking fear into the hearts of his enemies.
            Cost: 1 Willpower Action: Instant Dice Pool: Presence + Expression + Potency versus Composure
            The roar can be heard for miles, but the Dread Power takes effect on any targets within 40 yards. Affected subjects are overcome by fear and gain the “Frightened” Condition.

            Sharp Senses: Goliath’s senses are especially keen. He receives +1 to all perception rolls, and can see clearly in almost total darkness.

            Stone Sleep: Goliath turns to stone during the day. From dawn until dusk, regardless of whether or not he is actually exposed to daylight, his body transforms into a granite statue with a Durability of 3 and Structure equal to his health boxes. Goliath is immobile and unaware of any outside events in this state and is exceptionally vulnerable. On the plus side, Stone Sleep always refreshes a point of willpower and completely heals any and all non-aggravated damage.

            Tail: Goliath has a thick, muscular tail. This tail grants Goliath a +2 bonus to rolls involving balance, and may be used to make unarmed attacks.

            Titan’s Strength: Goliath can automatically lift anything with a Size equal to or less than 9. If he throws lighter objects like a weapon, that item is considered aerodynamic. Goliath gains an additional 3 dots to any roll involving a feat of strength. These dice do not apply to combat actions, only feats.

            Unholy Stamina: Goliath may spend 1 Willpower to gain a +2 bonus to stamina. This bonus lasts until the end of the scene. Recalculate appropriate traits (Speed, Health, Initiative, Defense) using the new Attribute score.

            Unholy Strength: Goliath may spend 1 Willpower to gain a +3 bonus to strength. This bonus lasts until the end of the scene. Recalculate appropriate traits (Speed, Health, Initiative, Defense) using the new Attribute score.

            Wings: Goliath’s wings allow him to glide at 3 times his normal speed. As long as the he has a way to gain some altitude, Goliath can glide up to 1 mile in distance and can change direction at will. With a successful Dexterity + Athletics roll, he can swoop upwards; effectively climbing without using handholds.

            Notes:
            This write up is based on Goliath from the Gargoyles animated series. It uses the Monster rules presented in Hunter: The Vigil, and updated in Mortal Remains.

                Ok, so...my first attempt at writing stats for the Slender Man as a God-Machine Angel didn't turn out so well. I decided to try again, with an additional suggestion for how to use him in the NWOD.

                If Slendy is an Angel, the Proxies could be Stigmatics or low Rank Angels, the locations with irregular space-time are part of Infrastructures and the occasional mutated critters like the Rake are Cryptids. Here are the updated Angel stats:

                Virtue: Hidden

                Vice: Observant

                Rank: 4

                Attributes: Power 10, Finesse 8, Resistance 10

                Corpus: 17

                Willpower: 10

                Defense: 6

                Initiative: 9

                Speed: 14

                Max Essence/Per Turn: 40/12

                Influence: Darkness & Mist 4, Memories 3

                Manifestation: Twilight Form, Materialize, Discorporate

                Suggested Ban: The Slender Man cannot communicate in immediately understandable ways. He might use Numina, Manifestations, Influences and similar methods to manipulate people with varying degrees of subtlety and success. Despite this, any clear use of speech or the written word is beyond him.

                Suggested Bane: There is no definitive answer for this, but the Tulpa Effect is the most prominent idea.

                Numina: Ravage, Aegis, Awe, Hallucination, Implant Mission, Fluid Form, Regenerate, Sign


                Alternate Interpretation:

                He might have started out as an internet meme like in real life. However, the Mythos carried such powerful symbols that the resulting collective belief that the Slender Man could be real made him so. In this scenario, the Slender Man is a being from the Temenos. However, since he is the central figure of an entire mythology that portrays him as very powerful, he has higher Rank than the God-Machine Angel version. I also like the idea that Slender Man is simply the projection of an unseen entity into our world. So I made Astral Slender Man the Royal Avatar of a Rank 6 entity. Determining what said entity might be like is better off in the hands of more experienced Storytellers. Here are the Royal Avatar stats:

                Rank: 5

                Attributes: Power 13, Finesse 11, Resistance 15

                Corpus: 22

                Willpower: 10

                Defense: 8

                Initiative: 13

                Speed: 18

                Max Essence/Per Turn: 50/15

                Influence: Darkness & Mist 5, Memories 5

                Manifestation: Twilight Form, Materialize, Astral Sojourn

                Suggested Ban & Bane/Numina: Same as those of the updated Angel version, plus "Astral Road" (Use the Astral as a sort of hyperspace between two points in mundane space. May be combined with Astral Sojourn to bring others with you, but time may pass differently on Earth. Clash Of Wills might also happen. I can't think of detailed mechanics, unfortunately).

For those not familiar, Illusion of Time/Illusion of Gaia is a Super Nintendo action adventure game similar in vein of Legend of Zelda. The plot consists of the main character, Will, travelling to the wonders of the ancient world to uncover their mysteries, discover the fate of his father after a fateful trip to the Tower of Babel and to discover the what the meaning of an Impending comet means for the world.

                    Will (Illusion of Time - SNES)
                    Background: Will is a youth who can see the truth of the world, he alone can commune with Gaia, the living spirit of the world. Will engages on a journey to find the artefacts that would drive off Dark Gaia, an evil presence who rewrites the world every 800 years as it passes guiding the evolution of the world.

                    Virtue: Heroism
                    Vice: Trust

                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 3
                    Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 4, Stamina 2
                    Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 1, Composure 2

                    Skills: Academics 1 (History), Investigation 3 (Ancient Ruins), Occult 3 (Ancient Ruins), Athletics 4 (Sliding), Brawl 1, Stealth 1, Survival 2, Weaponry 3(Flute), Animal Ken 2, Empathy 2, Expression 2 (Flute)

                    Merits: Medium •••, Telekinesis ••, Psychometry •••, Interdisciplinary Specialty •• (Ancient Ruins, Flute)
                    Gear:
                    Flute: Melee Weapon - 0L, 2 Size, -1 Initiative, 1 Strength, Special: +1 when used for Expression. Will's Flute. Is a poor weapon but good at playing songs.
                    Psycho Dash: Melee Weapon - 0L, Special: Uses Telekinesis + Strength to attack. Armour Piercing 1, Ignores Durability 1. Will's psychically enhanced body slam granted by Dark Gaia. Smashes through walls, obstacles and enemies.
                    Psycho Slider: Melee Weapon - 2B, Special: Uses Telekinesis + Athletics to attack. Inflicts the Knockdown tilt if damage greater than target's stamina. Will's psychically enhanced slide attack granted by Dark Gaia. Knocks foes down and away.
                    Psycho Spin: Melee Weapon - 1L, Special: Uses Telekinesis + Dexterity + Athletics to attack. Requires a successful Dexterity + Athletics roll next turn to not fall over. Can be used to leap long distances. Will's psychically powered tornado spin. The spin moves incredibly fast and is capable of launching Will long distances but needs time to recover.


                    Freedan
                    Background: Freedan is the soul of an ancient Hero, one who suffered at the hands of the Comet the last time it came to Earth. Gaia can transform Will's body into Freedan's, gaining his skills and abilities but losing Will's own.

                    Sword (as Machete). Every knight needs a sword, right?
                    Dark Friar (as Psychokinesis). Freedan can manifest his psychic abilities to send spheres of hurtling aura flame at his opponents.
                    Aura Barrier: Melee Weapon - 2L, -4 Initiative, Size 4, Special: Concealed. Uses no hands. Cannot be used unless manifested with an Instant Resolve + Occult roll and lasts turns equal to successes.

                        Ok, so, I kept thinking about my previous write ups for the Slender Man. The True Fae version was the most accurate, but I figured I'd try to redo it, this time with the generic Hunter stats. It’s difficult to accurately portray the extent of Slender Man’s teleportation and remote perception powers. The Dread Powers are more like suggestions. However, it is often said that the Slender Man needs to inflict fear and madness before going in for the kill or taking the victim to a fate unknown. I decided to include guidelines for portraying this rising tension and dread of most Slender Man stories. I didn't give him a Virtue nor Vice, because I'm not sure if these specific traits can apply to him. I would greatly appreciate your two cents.

                        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 3, Resolve 4
                        Physical Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
                        Social Attributes: Presence 5, Manipulation 1, Composure 6

                        Mental Skills: Academics 1, Computer 1, Crafts 1, Investigation (Searching) 4, Medicine 1, Occult 3, Politics 0, Science 1
                        Physical Skills: Athletics (Chasing) 3, Brawl (Claws & Tentacles) 3, Drive 0, Firearms 0, Larceny 3, Stealth (Plain Sight) 6, Survival (Forests & Woodlands) 4, Weaponry 0
                        Social Skills: Animal Ken 1, Empathy (Children) 1, Expression 1, Intimidation (Appearing Out Of Nowhere) 5, Persuasion 2, Streetwise 0, Subterfuge (Mimic Sounds) 4

                        Merits: Interdisciplinary Specialty (Claws & Tentacles, Mimic Sounds), Eidetic Memory, Patient, Tolerance For Biology, Direction Sense, Ambidextrous, Double Jointed, Demolisher 1, Choke Hold, Quick Healer, Hardy 3, Iron Stamina 3, Fleet Of Foot 3, Light Weapons 5 (Claws & Tentacles), Retainer (Proxies), Iron Will, Striking Looks +2

                        Willpower: 10 + 7 Dots From Potency
                        Defense: 3 + Athletics/Brawl/Weaponry
                        Initiative: 9
                        Speed: 15
                        Health: 11 (Size 7)
                        Potency: 7

                        Dread Powers: Alter Memory 3, Black Out 5, Clamber 3, Dark Gate 5, Dead Flesh 5, Dement 3, Drain 5 (Sleep Variant), Dread Attack 2 (Claws & Tentacles), Dream Seeing 3, Dream Shaping 3, Dream Walking 3, Flicker And Flash 4, Giant Size, Haunting 3, Hypnotism 5, Lord Of The Manor 5 (Temporary: Forests & Woodlands), Lurker In Darkness 5, Mist Form, New Face 4, Revelation 3, Revivify 5, Sleep 3, Strange Form 3 (Tree-Like: +3 Dexterity, +3 Composure), Titan's Strength 5, Unholy Attribute (Stamina 3)

                        When a character fails a Breaking Point roll for Integrity or equivalent stat due to the Slender Man or Proxies, add 1 die to a pool specific to that character. Suffering a Dramatic Failure on the Breaking Point roll adds 2 dice. This dice pool has a limit of 10 and cannot be decreased, barring the use of the Informed Condition. The ST can roll this dice pool for the Slender Man once per chapter, contesting it against the character’s unmodified Resolve + Composure, with no Willpower expenditure allowed.

                        If the character completes an extended action to investigate and research the Slender Man, a +3 bonus is applied to her future Resolve + Composure rolls for the purposes of contesting the Slender Man’s dice pool. In the case of an Exceptional Success during this extended action, it may be traded for the Informed Condition if the action is completed. This Condition may be applied to a roll meant for resetting and locking the Slender Man’s dice pool. The target number of successes for this extended action is 20. Teamwork is allowed, but those who help may have to roll for Breaking Points and be marked as well. Ties on this contested roll go for the Slender Man.

                        As the characters delve further, Proxies both human and monstrous may be sent after them or take interest, possibly inducing more Breaking Point rolls. As the Slender Man approaches, the ST should give signs of his presence, such as audiovisual distortions in recording devices, small fragments of bodies or personal possessions, sudden sickness and strange carvings. Being found and captured doesn’t always mean game over. Despite this, the ST is encouraged to at least give the Amnesia Condition to the character in question. Additional changes are done at the ST’s discretion.

                                                Loki's Mask:

                                                The Mask is a Relic containing a Rank 5 mischief spirit that is one of the Royal Avatars of a Rank 6+ entity. Whoever wears the Mask is treated as one of the Claimed.

                                                Attribute Bonuses: +5 Strength, +3 Presence, +2 Intelligence, +3 Dexterity, +7 Manipulation, +5 Wits, +5 Stamina, +2 Composure, +3 Resolve. These increase derived traits and may bring the Attributes beyond 10 dots.

                                                Maximum Essence/Per Turn: 50/15

                                                Influences: Mischief 5 (May Be Used To Create Equipment, Weapons, Firearms And Temporary Skill Dots)

                                                Dread Powers: Armored Hide 3, Leap, Monstrous Resilience, Numen (Drain, Essence Thief, Hallucination, Mortal Mask, Speed), Reality Stutter, Regenerate 5, Swift, Unbreakable

                                                Merits: Indomitable (Advanced), Vice-Ridden (Mischievous), Ambidextrous, Crack Driver 3, Demolisher 3, Double Jointed (Advanced), Fleet Of Foot 3, Iron Stamina 3, Parkour 4, Sleight Of Hand, Stunt Driver 3, Barfly, Closed Book 4, Fast-Talking 5, Iron Will, Pusher, Striking Looks 2 (Cartoonish), Table Turner, Cheap Shot, Heavy Weapons 5, Martial Arts 4

                                                Liar's Tongue - Same as the one of the Unchained (Demon: The Descent, Pages 182-183)

                                                Fiendish Deceit - Gain an Interdisciplinary Specialty with Area Of Expertise for Mischief. Anytime this Specialty might apply to a roll, you may spend 1 Essence to give it 8 Again. You suffer no untrained penalties for Skills you do not have, as long as you use them for mischief.

                                                Tazmanian Whirlwind - Spend 1 Essence and roll Stamina + Athletics. You inflict the Heavy Winds Environmental Tilt around yourself with an intensity equal to Successes for one turn, capping at intensity 5. The Essence cost may be paid again on subsequent turns to keep this power activate.

                                                Incite Bedlam - Same as the one of the Lost, spending Essence instead of Glamour and using Rank instead of Wyrd.

                                                Ban: Can only be used at night.

                                                            You know how the relationship between power and responsibility is one of the main themes in Mage? And how Spider-Man’s adopted motto is “With Great Power, Comes Great Responsibility”? That encouraged me to write stats for him as a Mage, as per the crunch and fluff of 2ed. His Path works the best as Acanthus. His Order is Free Council because he is a hero and admirer of the Sleepers, not an unseen and dusty law enforcer. He is on good terms with the Guardians Of The Veil and Adamantine Arrow, however. Spidey may turn out to be more fitting as a Deviant, at least in crunch.

                                                            His Persona Merit (combination of magical style, Shadow Name and personal mysteries that grants Supernal Sympathy) is likely to be reasonably powerful. The Web-Slingers are an Imbued/Enhanced Item. His spider-sense, wall-crawling, enhanced physical capabilities in general, superlative reflexes and impressive luck are a combination of mundane Merits, Life, Mind, Forces, Fate and Time. The costume itself could also be Enhanced for greater flexibility and protection, maybe also Imbued with a Mind Veiling spell to improve the disguise. Spidey also uses Space to conceal his sympathetic connections to his loved ones, and for Scrying both friend and foe. Even without spells, his stats are still very formidable.

                                                            I am not sure how to translate his standard back-story into an Awakening, be it a Mystery Play or Astral Journey. His Nimbus might include webs as a symbol of personal connections, binding promises and karma. Spiders themselves might symbolize Fate: both of them can weave and cut webs in their own way, be it to connect, restrain, protect or harm. The members of Spidey’s Rogue Gallery work well as Deviants, Slashers and the occasional unscrupulous Mage. The symbiotes, though? They definitely work as scarily intimate Abyssals. Madame Web is a Mastigos. Aunt May, Mary Jane and similar characters could be Sleepwalkers, depending on how much they know about Peter.

                                                            Feedback is appreciated!

                                                            Virtue: Responsible/Inventive
                                                            Vice: Short-Tempered
                                                            Concept: Genius Superhero

                                                            Path: Acanthus
                                                            Order: Free Council

                                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 6, Resolve 5
                                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 6, Stamina 5
                                                            Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 4

                                                            Mental Skills: Academics (Journalism) 3, Crafts (Engineering) 4, Computer 3, Medicine 2, Occult 2, Politics 1, Investigation (Scoops) 3, Science (Biology) 4
                                                            Physical Skills: Athletics (Climbing, Gymnastics) 6, Brawl 4, Drive 1, Firearms (Web-Slingers) 2, Larceny 2, Stealth (Ambush) 3, Survival 2, Weaponry (Improvised) 4
                                                            Social Skills: Animal Ken 1, Empathy 3, Expression (Humor) 3, Intimidation (Bluff) 3, Persuasion (Diplomacy) 3, Socialize (Office) 2, Streetwise (New York City) 3, Subterfuge (Excuses) 3

                                                            Merits: Virtuous, Ambidextrous, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Crafts, Science, Expression), Area Of Expertise (Scoops, Engineering), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Humor, Gymnastics, Engineering), Patient, Common Sense, Indomitable, Professional Training 3 (Journalist: Empathy, Investigation, Persuasion), Trained Observer 3, Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Tolerance For Biology, Demolisher 3, Double Jointed, Disarm*, Iron Stamina 3, Fleet Of Foot 3, Fast Reflexes 3, Hardy 3, Parkour 5, Improvised Weapons 3, Martial Arts 3, Firefight 3 (Web-Slingers), Unarmed Defense 5, Quick Healer, Allies 3/Status 1 (Daily Bugle), Allies 3/Status 2 (Free Council), Contacts 6 (Journalists, Researchers, Law Enforcers, Free Council, Guardians Of The Veil, Adamantine Arrow), Resources 2, Striking Looks +1, Alternate Identity 1 (Spider-Man), Fame 2 (Spider-Man), Fixer, Iron Will, Inspiring, Pusher, Sympathetic, True Friend (Mary Jane, Aunt May), Web-Slinger Style 5**

                                                            *Spidey normally disarms opponents with his webs, or fists hardened with Life spells. He can do it with Weaponry too.

                                                            **Web-Slinger Style

                                                            Eight Eyes (•): Web-Slinging is practically the same as walking for Spidey. Most difficulties that people experience when trying to use the web-slingers never apply to him. Ignore environmental penalties to Perception rolls and Range penalties equal to your dots in Web-Slinger for the purposes of the namesake. Other Perception rolls and Ranges are not affected.

                                                            Multi-Purpose (••): Spidey knows very well that the web-slingers aren’t just for swinging around and tying people up. They’re also useful for keeping ramshackle repairs together, making traps, quick patch-ups for wounds and creative expression. He is considered to have an Interdisciplinary Specialty of Web-Slinging with Area Of Expertise built-in, even if the Skill in question is not Mental, lower than 3, or lowered from 3 through whatever means.

                                                            Pin-Point (•••): Spidey can calculate angles and distance with lightning speed and amazing precision, especially for disarming and stunning. Every 2 dice he subtracts from his Dexterity + Firearms pool becomes a +1B for attacks with webs. An Exceptional Success on this roll also inflicts a fitting Tilt, usually Arm Wrack, Leg Wrack, Knocked Down or Immobilized. Inflicting the Blinded Tilt doesn’t stand to gain with a reduced dice pool, as it only requires Spidey to roll Dexterity + Firearms – 3 for targeting the opponent’s head.

                                                            Arachnid Focus (••••): If he completely concentrates on a target, Spidey is virtually guaranteed to hit as intended. Instead of gaining +2 or more when making an All-Out Attack with the web-slingers, he may receive the Rote Quality. He may use this maneuver at the same time as the previous one, but doing so requires 1 willpower point.

                                                            Ultimate Web-Spinning (•••••): It is often when a split second can mean the difference between life and death that Spidey makes the most of his web fluid/Mana. He spends 1 willpower point, and all Mana costs for Reach and mitigation of Paradox are halved, round down. This maneuver may be combined with Arachnid Focus OR Pin-Point. The situation does need to be so drastic for him to use this maneuver.
