    The Salinian working in Mage the Awakening [Signs of Sorcery preview materials]

    02-24-2018, 11:15 AM
    So i was reading some Exalted stuff, and came across the Salinian working, which for those who aren't that familiar with Exalted, this is a ritual that inscribed caches of sorcerous knowledge into creation.
    At first, this screamed out Archmasters and Archmagic...
    But then i remembered the Signs of Sorcery preview had some interesting stuff on Grimoires and Daimonomikons...

    Namely this:

    Astral Grimoire (Mind ••• + Prime •)
    Practice: Weaving
    Primary Factor: Potency
    Cost: 1 Mana
    Withstand: Total Arcanum dots used in the rote
    Suggested Rote Skills: Crafts, Expression, Occult
    Scribing an Astral Grimoire is much like scribing an earthly one, except the vessel which holds the symbols exists only in the subject's Oneiros. An Awakened subject can cast the rotes from the
    Grimoire without having to meditate into the Astral, but if she does cast with the Astral representation of the Grimoire in hand, it grants a two-die Equipment bonus to all casting rolls.
    Summoning the Goetia that represents the Grimoire has the same effect.
    +1 Reach: The mage can scribe a Grimoire into the Temenos, making it available to anyone who travels there. The rotes can only be cast directly from the Astral representation or with its
    summoned Goetia, neither of which grants an Equipment bonus.
    +1 Reach: For one point of Mana, the spell's Duration is Lasting.
    +2 Reach: The mage can scribe a Grimoire into the Anima Mundi. Such Grimoires manifest not as books and scrolls, but as vast constellations of stars or rock formations. Figuring out how to read one constitutes a Mystery unto itself.

    If you squint hard enough, its conceptually quite close.
    ,
    Then i went a step further: would it be possible to scribe a Daimonomicon in the Anima mundi?
    From signs of sorcery
    Scribe Daimonomikon (Death, Fate, Mind, or Spirit •••• + Prime •; or Prime ••••)
    Practice: Patterning

    I guess scribing the Daimonomicon in the Astral would be mind 3 + Prime 4, with a +2 reach to scribe it in the Anima mundi.

    What would the Mysterium think of a Legacy that passes on it's Attainments and Magical lore through the Anima mundi?
    I'm undecided on this: on the one hand it does preserve knowledge; on the other hand... anyone could get it.
    In hindsight, the Daimonomikon might not be such a great idea, given the Sympathy yantra thing though...

              It's risky business. It's no safer than anywhere else, it can't be moved, you could potentially be harmed sympathetically (or really, perhaps just pestered) using it, and it's out in the open or anyone (with the requisite knowledge and skill to find it) to learn from.

              That being said, it could propogate your Legacy quite effectively if you're lucky, and word spreads. Though given the relative scarcity of travellers that deep into the Astral, and the difficulty inherent in interpreting Anima Mundai phenomena, you might not get even one person who just stumbles across it.

              It's a much better idea to share your Legacy with another person first, then store an Astral Daimonomicon in the Temenos Realm of your Legacy you have forged.
              That way, even if every living member dies, someone could still use Time Magic to open up the Astral pathway to the lost Temenos realm, and find a Daimonomicon to rekindle it from.

                                                  Salinians
                                                  The Salinian working

                                                  The archmage Salina, as part of her Imperial rites embedded arcane knowledge in the fabric of reality, as well as a simple enough path to follow for sleepers to awaken. Upon her Ascension, the Exarchs erased most of her changes; The path to awaken sleepers has been erased, and the caches of knowledge have been obscured; all that remains is a Daimonomikon inscribed in the Dreaming earth.
                                                  Those who shape their souls following the teachings of the Daimonomikon gain access to the obscured caches of knowledge created by the omens of Salina’s ascension.
                                                  Origins

                                                  Parentage: Obrimos, Free council
                                                  Background: The legacy’s Daimonomikon is inscribed in the the Dreaming earth, and keyed to unlock itself only to aspiring mages that meet a certain level of decency. Since decent people are encountered in all walks of life, Salinians tend to have very varied backgrounds.However, they do tend to have unifying/defining behaviours, such as a keen eye for detail and an appreciation for the beauty of nature.
                                                  Appearance: Due to the nature of the Legacy initiation, Salinians come from all walks of life; and they tend to look the part.
                                                  Doctrine

                                                  Prerequisites: Fate 2, Expression 2 and one of the following additional Skills at 2 dots or higher: Survival, Empathy, Animal Ken, Occult, or Science.
                                                  Initiation: The daimonomikon of the Salinians can be found in the Dreamtime. Due to Salina’s idealism, the secrets of the Daimonomikon are only revealed to those that meet the requirements set by Salina herself, which basically can be summed up as “Be a decent person”. It is an unwritten rule in the legacy to not initialize members in any other way.
                                                  Organization: Due to the “open source” nature of the Salinian daimonomikon, Salinians don’t really follow a formal hierarchy.
                                                  Theory: To those that know how to look, the fallen world contains a wealth of patterns, that when deciphered can reveal the secrets of reality.
                                                  Magic

                                                  Ruling Arcanum: Fate
                                                  Yantas: Being within sensory rangin the presence of someone you share a Medium or Stronger sympathetic connection to (+1), Being in the of someone your True friend (and vice versa) (+2), Succeeding on an Expression relevant to the the spell (+2); verbally explaining the symbols of the yantras to someone close to you (+1);
                                                  Oblations: Spending quality time with someone you share a medium or stronger sympathetic connection. Tending to an aspect of creation or it’s inhabitants. Teaching someone some knowledge you have gleaned from nature.
                                                  Attainments

                                                  First: Inspiration from nature

                                                  Prerequisite: Initiation
                                                  The Salinian develops a keen eye for the secret patterns of reality. The attainment emulates “Serendipity”, cast as an instant action, restricted to showing the mage interesting natural patterns.
                                                  Optional: Prime 1
                                                  Studying the pattern takes one hour and mimics the spell “Divination”, with Reach expended for Detailed questions.
                                                  Second: Creation is your classroom

                                                  Prerequisite: Fate 2, keen periphery
                                                  While studying a pattern, the Salinian sometimes glimpses actual supernal lore. This attainments emulates a modified version of “Shifting the Odds” (reach to obtain results within one hour). The mage temporarily gains dots in the Grimoire merit equal to half of his prime dots. The Rotes contained in the “grimoire” are subject to the following restrictions:
                                                  • They cannot already be known to the mage.
                                                  • The mage has to be able to cast the rote
                                                  • The number of highest arcanum in the rote cannot be higher than Mage's Fate Arcanum
                                                  Optional: Prime 2
                                                  The mage becomes a dab hand at observing the transient phenomena; mostly due to practice. This attainment mimics “Light under a bushel”, allowing the mage to make an extra number of rolls equal to Prime dots when scrutinizing a mystery.
                                                  Third: Grounding

                                                  Prerequisite: Fate 3, One required skill raised to 3
                                                  Sometimes, rather than a trove of supernal lore, sometimes, the insight provided by a pattern is more mundane, but no less useful. This attainment mimics “superlative luck” (advanced), and usually manifests as a hunch at exactly the right moment.
                                                  Optional: Prime 3:
                                                  The Salinian can enhance the natural patterns, highlighting the supernal significance of it. This manifests as one of two effects, either “Invisible runes” (Instant, Advanced duration) or “Scribe grimoire” (Instant, Lasting, Key). The first effect can be used to create runes that can be used as per the normal Runes Yantra, in which case the runes are only visible to those with Mage sight, and resist being erased using a Clash of wills (gaining auto successes equal to prime dots). The secondary effect allows the mage to turn the symbols into a temporary grimoire; this lasts for the duration of the Invisible runes, and can only be read with Active mage sight.
                                                  Fourth: Guidance

                                                  Prerequisites: Fate 4
                                                  The mage spends an hour studying a particular omen and gains the benefit of “Atonement”, cast with fate 4, with reach spent on allowing the quest to be completed by someone else.
                                                  Optional Prime 4: Building upon the previous attainment, Using Invisible runes as a yantra inscribed as a yantra benefits from “As above, so below”, (8 again, Instant, Advanced duration).
                                                  Fifth: Synchronicity

                                                  Prerequisites: Fate 5
                                                  Salinians with this level of attunement to the secrets of creation see reality in a fundamentally different way; the mage uses the changing portents to act and react almost precognitively. This mimics "Exceptional luck", with reach expended on affecting spellcasting and reflexive activation. The mage can gain the benefit of resolving one of the following conditions every turn: Steadfast, Inspired, Informed, Charmed.
                                                  Optional: Prime 5
                                                  When witnessing a spell being cast, the supernal symbols are reflected in the omens the mage is so used to reading. This attainment mimics “Display of Power”, allowing the mage to gain rote action on counterspell rolls.
