    Wyrd's Dark Eras Stuff Hub

    01-19-2018, 09:50 AM
    My Dark Eras fan materials hub topic. I missed here casual topics on very specific things I make - as this list would be too long. It's mostly new rules and settings stuff - minor questions have their own topics all over the forums.

    General fan materials:
    • Mr.Gones's Dark Eras sheets - By my design ( mostly )
    • [2E] Mass Combat Rules - Rules for running your pre-modern wars in CoD games.
    • [2E] Needed Merits & Conditions - Merits and Conditions that may be needed in your games and are missed in Dark Eras corebook.
    • [2E] More armor and weapons - More archaic weaponry stuff
    • [2E] Other Skills options - Skills usage not covered in official materials - like Sailing
    • [2E] War Machines - To use in your battles of previous Eras
    • Dangers of land travel - Before Steam Era, travel by land was very dangerous - here are ideas why - and what to throw at your players when they journey
    • Early Middle Ages resources - Things you may need to see or have to play in Eras like Wolf & Raven
    • Pre-modern travel times - Travel times of carts, boats and ships
    • [2E] Researching supernaturals - How to known what on Occult Skill dots known each character
    Fan Eras:
    • American Revolution - Start of Nameless Movement and modern countries
    • Arcadia Mountains - Fan supplement for Mage, Changeling and Werewolf set in one of most primeval regions of Greece - for To the Strongest.
    • Baptism of Poland - Clash of Christianity and pagan ways in 10th to 11th century Poland
    • Byzantine Empire and Eastern Europe in Early Middle Ages ( 330–1453 A.D.)
    • Constantinople – Capitol of Eastern Roman Empire, called Byzantium, is cosmopolitan city that's monsters must fight with shadow of Contagion inside Plague of Justinian in years 700 – 1000 AD.
    • Crusades - Christians conquest of Holy Land in 11th to 13th century
    • Conquer of Paradise – Portugal and Spain and their conquests in 15th century
    • Kuaru - Early Kiev - Settlement that became Kievan Rus, extension of Wolf & Raven official Era
    • Odessos – Early Medieval Odessa - Settlement of classical Greece, that later become one of jewels of Kievan Rus and Byzantine Empire boarders.
    • Medieval Ireland – Perfect place for your Vikings raids
    • Satanic Panic - of 1980s and 90s - For Beast, Mage and others
    Fan materials to official Eras:
    • [Sundered World] Hollow Keepers Legacy - Moroi taking power from locking souls and traveling to Shadow Realm
    • [Forsaken by Rome] Roman Uratha - More info or ideas for playing werewolves in Rome
    • [Wolf & Raven] Mother Wolf for Norse Uratha - How to reconcile Wolf, Moon, Odin and every other god in Norse Myths with Werewolf ( and CoD ) cosmology.
    • [Wolf & Raven] Hólmganga duels - How to run honoral duels in Vikings society
    • [Wolf & Raven] Lodge of Moon Hounds - Pure Lodge coming from Norse myths, hunting Auspices.
    • [Wolf & Raven] Nine Realms - CoD cosmology associations with Norse mythology worlds.
    Historical materials to gamelines:
    • [Changeling] Council of Elves Freeholds
    • [Mage] History of Awakened - (un)official Mage gameline Timeline from the start of humanity to modern days
    • [Mage] How Orders Conquared the World? - Discussion about how Diamond Orders spread around the Fallen World before their world hegemony.
    • [Mage] Nameless War - Fan approach on the most important conflict in modern Orders history, that ended with creation of Free Council
    • [Mage] Selling soul to the Devil - Like topics name
    • [Vampire] Byzantine’s Empire Kindred - Analysis of official materials and some fan ideas on how looks society of vampires in Constantinople and Byzantium Empire
    • [Vampire] Slavic (proto)Covenants - Vampire factions from Slavic lands before Christanization
    • [Werewolf] Byzantine’s Empire & Constantinople Uratha - Analysis of official materials and some fan ideas on how looks society of werewolves in Constantinople and Byzantium Empire
