                                    Agreed. Both on it needing a rehash and for why.

                                    Though the AH fighting style as a whole needs some rebalancing. For example, the 3rd dot is much more beneficial than any other save the 5th dot, even with its willpower cost. The 4th (damage->1 time casting bonus, w/9 again if damage is made resistant) can either drastically be abused (an orphan of proteus with this was... amusing) or very underwhelming. The first dot giving a -1 to any shield struck in melee of the same arcana as the AH's and the shield redistribution are okay. But the 1st is limited to a 1/10 chance unless you take it multiple times (thus having 2 dots in many arcana). The 2nd is pretty decent as long as you aren't silly about its use.

                                    The fact that it needed to be retaken for each arcana and required a decent number of prerequisites to take in the fist place meant that a lot of my players never bothered with it (except that orphan who realized he could use his 1st attainment to abuse the damage effect, or when I made the HR on arcana=merit level).

                                    Don't get me wrong, I think it was a cool idea. It just didn't seem to mechanically work for my players enough to not just take law of embodiment with more fighting styles (or at least go past the 3rd dot in something they they combined a lot, such as using it to shoot and heal on the same turn)

                                    Hopefully, if it returns in 2e, it will see some love.
