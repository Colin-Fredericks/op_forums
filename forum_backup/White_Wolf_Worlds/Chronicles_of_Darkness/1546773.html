    Chior of Hashmallim - 2E fan update

    12-08-2024, 08:39 PM
    Choir of Hashmallim
    Dominions

    Pages 188-191 of Summoners for lore

    Doctrine and Magic

    Parent Path: Obrimos
    Parent Order: Any
    Prerequisites: Prime 2, Forces 1, 2 dots of Academics, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Athletics, Expression, Intimidation, Persuasion, Occult
    Initiation: Perform a supernal summoning, meeting your angel, and forming a pact with it, which involes working with it to destroy an Abyssal Manifestation
    Ruling Arcana: Prime
    Yantras: A Cross (+1); A Bible (+1); A Prayer, Biblical verse, or hymn (+1 - +3 depending on the relevance).
    Oblations: Spend an hour in prayer; discussing theology; destroying abyssal manifestations.

    Attainments

    First Attainment: Eyes of the Aether

    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The mage’s angel grants them the sight of the Aether in order to do battle against the Abyss. All Abyssal manifestations can be scrutinized by the mage’s Prime sight instead of the normal, appropriate Arcana needed.

    The Dominion gains the ability to activate magical items on their person, masters teach apprentices to carry many magic items on themselves for this reason, and because one never knows what they will find themselves facing. This attainment functions like “Word of Command” (Prime 1, MtAw 166) with reach for instant duration.

    Second Attainment: Aetherial Cleansing

    Prerequisites: Prime 2, 3 dots in Academics
    The magic of the Abyss is dangerous to mages and the Fallen World, therefore the Dominions learn not just how to destroy the demons of the Abyss, but how to prevent their magic from ever taking shape in reality. This attainment enhances the Universal Counterspell attainment (Prime 2, MtAw 193) so that the numina used by Abyssal Manifestations may be countered. The roll is Prime + Gnosis vs Rank + the highest influence of the Abyssal manifestation.

    In addition, the mage may ask questions about any Abyssal manifestation within sensory distance. The number of questions the mage can ask is equal to their Prime arcanum, and the list of questions is the same as the spell “Know Spirit” (Spirit 1, MtAw 180).

    Third Attainment: Aetherial Avatar

    Prerequisites: Prime 3, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 3 or a second option from the list must be raised to 2
    The Dominion has bound themselves to the angels of the Aether, one specifically, and finally this Angel can be summoned to assist the mage in their battle against the Abyss. By spending a mana the mage may summon their angel for a number of turns equal to their gnosis. The angel is altered by the virtue and vice of the mage.* The angel cannot be destroyed, if all of its corpus is filled with Lethal damage it returned to the Aether. The Angel treated the presence of the mage as a summoning circle, as long as it remains with the mage it will safely return to the Aether upon the ending of the attainments durations or the destruction of its corpus.
    The angel has the following stats;

    Rank 2
    Virtue/Vice Same as the summoner, but filtered through the lens of the Aether
    Power [Lowest of the summoner’s Intelligence, Strength, or Presence]
    Finesse [Lowest of summoner’s Wits, Dexterity, or Manipulation]
    Resistance [Lowest of summoner's Resolve, Stamina, or Composure]
    Willpower Power + Resistance
    Initiative Finesse + Resistance
    Defense Higher of Power or Finesse
    Speed 10 + Power + Finesse
    Size 5
    Corpus Size + Resistance
    Ban Must battle, with the intent of destroying, any Abyssal Manifestations in the scene

    The angel does not have its own source of mana; anytime it must spend mana, it spends the mage’s, which counts towards the maximum mana per turn the mage may spend. It can spend the mana to cast the following spells, using its Power + Finesse as the spell casting dice pool; “Aetheric Winds” (Prime 3, MtAw 168) with an optional reach effect to strip essence from Abyssal manifestations, “Wards and Signs” (Prime 2, MtAw 168), and if the mage is an adept of Prime, “Celestial Fire” (Prime 4, MtAw 170) although it cannot use the reach effect to deal aggravated damage unless the mage is a master of Prime. Lastly, any praxis The mage has The angel may cast. The angel does not risk paradox from reaching, and has its own spell control separate from the summoning mage. The angel is an active magical affect that is affecting ed by dissonance and inflicts quintencces, just like all other Supernal angels.

    While the summoner is not in control of the angel, it does act in their best interest and seeks to destroy any Abyssal intrusions.

    Optional: Forces 3
    The Dominions never know where they will face the Abyss, or how the demons will reshape the Fallen World around themselves. This attainment functions like “Environmental Shield” (Forces 2, MtAw 143) and draws on the mage’s Prime magic to also shield against environmental tilts that are Abyssal in nature. It has reached assigned to advanced duration and instant casting.

    Fourth Attainment: Aetherial Exaltation

    Prerequisites: Prime 4, 4 dots in Academics
    The bond between the Dominion and their angel grows stronger. When summoned it gains a number of benefits based on the summoner’s Prime arcanum. For each dot or Prime select one of the following effects, each effect can only be taken once, unless otherwise noted;
    • Instead of using the lowest of the summoner’s attributes to determine the attributes of the angel, use the highest from the list. This can be selected once per attribute.
    • The angel gains extra corpus, based on the lower of the summoner’s Prime and Forces arcana.
    • The angel remains in the Fallen World for an extra number of turns equal to the lower of the summoner’s Prime and Forces arcana
    • The angel gains armor against the attacks of Abyssal manifestations equal to the lower of the summoner’s Prime and Forces arcana.
    In addition to enhancing their Angel, the mage can now imbue yantras with extra power, making them more potent spellcasting tools. This attainment functions like “As Above, So Below” (Prime 2, MtAw 166) with reach assigned to instant casting, advanced duration, and gaining the 8-again effect on spell casting rolls.

    Fifth Attainment: Weapon of the Aether

    Prerequisites: Prime 5, The optional skill used to meet the requirements of initiation must be raised to 4 or a second option from the list must be raised to 3 or a third option must be raised to 2
    The Dominion represents the fact they are the Aether’s weapon against the Abyss by forming their own weapon out of their mana, usually a flaming sword, but whatever is appropriate for the mage is the form the weapon takes. This attainment functions like “Platonic Form” (Prime 3, MtAw 169) with reach for instant casting, advanced duration, and the extra reach effect to ensure that the created weapon is the perfect ideal for slaying Abyssal demons, granting it the 8-again dice trick. The attainment costs one mana to activate, and the mage may spend additional mana up to their maximum mana per turn to charge the weapon.

    Optional: Forces 5
    The Dominions call down the fire of the Aether to burn away the demons of the Abyss. As an instant action, the Dominion can deal lethal damage equal to their Forces to anything they can see. The fire appears from above the target, as if coming from heaven, and burns with a golden light.

    *Summoners gives an example of an angel holding a set of scales while being blinded for a just mage, but wielding a sword and having blood-soaked wings for a furious one.

          "Do you hear that, its like a kind of choir in the distance, is that part of this Abyssal we are summoning ?"
          The veteran Scelestus started to sweat cold, he knew what it meant, and his newest apprentice was not ready to face the Aether's fury.

          Regarding the summoning, I think some additional details to the summon could be that it does not require a Demesne and the angel returns safely to the Supernal upon death. I think giving the option to summon normally for higher Rank ones would be interesting, with the quick summon being part of the upgrades from the fourth Attainment. It would require the player to manage if they have time to summon a more powerful ally and take some items to make a summon area, but that in itself might be cool. I think it might also be helpful to mention a few details on the Virtue and Vice, the Ban and the angel's Trial, to give an outline on its dynamic with the summoner.

          Regarding the optional Attainments, perhaps the first one could be a Word of Command with Reach assigned to instant use, so the Dominion is incentivizes to prepare imbued items as reliable tools. Then the Know Spirit one could become the second optional one, allowing the Abyssal to be studied more safely from sensory range.

          The third one could be an Environmental Shield with conjunctional Prime allowing it to protect from Abyssal environmental tilts, with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

          The fourth one could be Energize Object, allowing them to prepare specialty weapons (holy hand grenades, Hellboy style bullets, trapping their Sanctum, etc).

          The optional capstone could be As Above, So Below with Reach assigned to instant use, changing primary factor to Potency, advanced duration and gaining 8-again. The Dominion can now harness the power of their Shadow Name, guardian angel or any necessary tools for the job. Furthermore, they can empower the angel themselves and their allies.
