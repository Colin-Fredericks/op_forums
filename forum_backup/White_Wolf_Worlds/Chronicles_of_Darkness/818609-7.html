        Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
        Vent0: I am not 100% sure, but a Mania + Wondrous Experiences rule set sounds like the best option. Perhaps you make a Wonder permanent with the Wondrous Experiences, and as you grow in Inspiration, you can update your Wonders with Advanced Functions that require Mania to be activated. Sort of like how Mages get more free Reach for their initial spells as they increase their Arcana ratings.
        I was seeing it as: There are two ways to "stabilize" a Wonder - you can either incorporate detailed knowledge of your discoveries and "correct" physics (Wondrous XP), or you can keep readjusting and tinkering with it (Bound Mania). Starting Genii will likely need to use Bound Mania (lacking the Wondrous XP reserves), but they can later incorporate those discoveries into "refining" their old designs (paying for them with Wondrous XP, which frees up the Bound Mania). Of course, there might be benefits to doing both (like maybe bonuses against Havoc wrecking the Wonder, or penalizing others' use of your Wonder).

        With Wondrous XP, I'd have deconstructing and reverse engineering other Wonders give you the Wondrous XP spent on them. Provided they were already "stable". Unstable, non-supported Wonders fall apart or go Orphan.

        What about the Resources requirement(s)?

        I'm also hoping for/thinking about less... hazardous "installation" for Integral/Internalized Wonders. Dropping the damage (though perhaps remaining as an option), but probably sticking the recipient with a Condition.

        One of the things I'd like to enable is: Dr Eureka is trapped in a not-so-abandoned factory and about to meet some hemophages. The Dr needs to open the barred door, but currently lack the strength to do so. They decide to Kitbash a Wondrous Formulation to give them the might necessary to burst through. They have a few minutes, so they use Exelixi to Kitbash their "Applied Force Theorem" in a minute or so (if going with the "improvisation" option, they might spend Mania to temporarily "give" themselves enough Exelixi to do so). Now they have the Theorem, they rush Internalize it in a minute (or a few). Using their newly devised strength technique, Dr Eureka busts down the door, and escapes before they acquire an acute case of anemia. Of course, modifying their own physio-mental makeup like that has consequences, and they find themselves suffering from the Murderous Anger Condition (penalty to Social, and makes them Go For Blood at the slightest provocation) for a time.

        Thoughts, people? Does something like this fit your needs better amechra?
