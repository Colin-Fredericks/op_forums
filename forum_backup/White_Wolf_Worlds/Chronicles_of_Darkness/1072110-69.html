                    One thing to keep in mind is that the most important trait that leads to Blossoming is being the reincarnation of a Princess. Onceborn are a minority and require significantly more to Blossom. (This is why Princesses tend to come from backgrounds that resemble an anime protagonist; rather than adults who go to Cambodia and dig up landmines).

                    As a rule of thumb a Onceborn should have the Virtueous merit, strong beliefs, and be proactive about putting their beliefs into practice, and have that unquantifiable WoD mystery-secret-sauce that First Changes, Awakenings, and Blossomings all require. All of that certainly could be found in a member of Yuri's group. But I'd say Yuri's group are more likely to see newbies with past lives Blossom on their first hunt than veterans blossom after a long career builds up enough Light.

                    BTW, I'd actually say the most likely organisations to have members Blossoming are Network Zero and the Maiden's Blood Sisterhood, simply because they recruit younger and are thus there are more reincarnated Princesses who haven't Blossomed yet in their recruiting pool.

                                    #Ammit and (honestly) the Magisters.

                                    #Ammit
                                    They are a slightly different take on Hacktivism (as opposed to the more AV take of Network Zero). That they are a former violent cult (which is what Stormwracked Cells are) which realized the incidental harm they were doing and strive to better themselves, covers ground I don't think other groups do. They also seem to be one of the more understanding groups, so long as the target in question hasn't proven themselves a danger.

                                    Magisters of the Economie/Unlimited
                                    These are some genuine a**-holes, but they are understandable a-holes. You can easily see the privileged crowd re-appropriating other's powers for what they see as better management. They still do uphold the Vigil, even with their warped motives, and any they actually self-analyze would make great drama to act out in character. They also let you use the powers of the Light for possibly darker purposes (without throwing Twilight Princesses around all the time).

                                    Review of the others:
                                    Star of Bethlehem - They certainly serve a role, but not one I can see using very much.
                                    The Light Company - The mercenary aspect is nice, but they seem a little less mercenary than I'd use. They seem a little sycophantic (in a harsher light), though it does still support the closed-off Ronin-type character.
                                    Character Risk Analysis - PR Hunters-for-Hire are nice, and would probably be my choice after #Ammit, but there is a little character-type overlap with the SRO Hunter that I am still working through. (More my issue than theirs)
                                    The Sanitation Workers Collective - They are a little held back (for me) by confusion of what they actually are. A "poor-man's conspiracy" of various Help-types wielding jumped up folk-magic rituals. Okay...? There's nothing particularly wrong with them, they just aren't catching my interest at the moment.
                                    The People's Guard - Ah, Revolutionaries. There is something to like in their fire and desire for equality, but they hew a bit to closely to Zealotry for me to love. Still, a group I can see using.
                                    (The Cold Guard/The Remnants Pact - Well, they aren't done/in the document, so they aren't an option. I do like the Grey Warden feel, and "turn Darkness against Darkness" aspects.)

                                            Okay, just looked over the Doc again, and I'd have to say, my favorites are as follows:

                                            #Ammit
                                            ​You get to be a hacktivist and an obnoxious internet troll! How can I not find this compact interesting?

                                            The Light Company
                                            ​Supernatural mercenaries sounds like a very interesting idea to me. Kind of wish there was something like this in Hunter

                                            ​Star of Bethlehem
                                            ​A cool concept, even if it is rather narrow and niche in scope. Makes me feel like I'd be playing the parents of a super hero; and the sheer adult fear of it all could make for great roleplaying.

                                            ​The Cold Guard / The Remnants Pact
                                            ​Like Vent0, I'm liking the Grey Warden-esque feel they give off, but they aren't a finished concept. They are also making me think of SCP (not SPC) Foundation in their methods.

                                            ​As for what groups I honestly dislike... I'd pick CRA, People's Guard and The Magisters, but that's more because of personally views than anything to do with their concepts. If you want specifics, here you go:

                                            ​Character Risk Analysis
                                            This group of character assassins just rubs me the wrong way. The fact that they specialize in hitting a Princess where it hurts just makes me think they'd one day create a mass of Dethroned...

                                            People's Guard
                                            ​Yeah... a group that just loves killing little girls; that's not disturbing in the least...

                                            The Magisters
                                            A group of people who believe in mind raping people for profit... I like the idea of these people as much as I like Les Mysteres... I.E. not at all.
