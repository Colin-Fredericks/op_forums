                In addition to what's been raised so far - spells should pretty much do one thing (even some of the core spells abuse this a bit [Looking at you, Fate]), and the reach effects should be tuned towards doing that same thing but differently or better.

                This spell:
                1) Tells you when someone is lying and why
                2) Freely opens doors in SM
                3) Imposes Conditions
                4) Gives them a social debuff.

                The subject of the spell isn't consistent. The description describes that the subject is the person that gets the extra knowledge about people lying to them, but then:
                Reach 1: The mage is opening doors to the victim...because he helped them tell when people are lying? It doesn't really make sense as written
                Reach 2: This is written as if the subject is the person doing the lying, not the person that gains insight into lies. Which is it?
                Reach 3: Same issue as Reach 2

                A lot of this stuff doesn't really fall in the purviews of these Arcana either. Most of this should be Mind or Fate. That doesn't mean you can't use other Arcana, but you should approach it carefully. I'd split this up into a bunch of different spells, personally.

                1) Add a Fate • +1 Reach effect to Pierce Deception, that informs you of the intent behind the deception
                2) For your Life themes, here's an alternative:
                Societal Adaptation (Life •••)
                Practice: Weaving
                Primary Factor: Potency
                Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Socialize, Subterfuge
                All of human interaction is underwritten by a constant stream of instinctual responses and unconscious ticks - our society is as much a product of our biology as it is a product of the mind. A Disciple of Life can enhance the social instincts that drive her subject, making her keenly (and consciously) aware of the subtle body language that drives human interaction. This spell provides its subject a bonus on all social rolls to detect a living human’s intentions equal to Potency, for a number of rolls equal to Potency. This also applies to rolls to detect lies.
                +1 Reach: In addition to enhancing instincts and perception, this spell affords the subject some conscious control over their own body language and microexpressions. The bonus may also be applies to rolls to mask one’s own intentions, or to other social actions at Storyteller discretion.

                3) For your brand/social debuff thing:
                Brand of the Deceiver (Prime ••)
                Practice: Ruling
                Primary Factor: Potency
                Withstand: Composure
                Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Empathy, Socialize
                The world listens when an arbiter of Truth proclaims the guilt of a deceiver. An Apprentice of Prime is capable of placing a Rune, visible only to those with Mage Sight, on a subject with the Guilty Condition that clearly marks them as a liar or a traitor. What’s more, although non-Mages cannot see the rune, its Supernal Truth leaks into the subject’s pattern regardless, causing them to start any social maneuvering attempt at one impression level lower than they normally would.
                +1 Reach: The spell may be replaced on a subject without the Guilty Condition, so long as the caster knows they have lied about something within the current scene. Note that it has to be a lie - simply being factually incorrect won’t allow the use of this Reach effect.
                +2 Reach: As the above Reach effect, but the subject also gains the Guilty Condition.


                I'm not actually a huge fan of either of those spells and would not use them in my game - they seem like they're trying to hard to step into the domain of other Arcana to me (namely, Mind), but if you want to stick to Prime and Life, I'd have the spells look something like that. They took about 5 minutes each, so with a decent amount of tuning, the issues of thematic overstep could probably be resolved.

                                                Nah, it didn't derail. Discussions like this are why the thread exists.

                                                One thing to remember is that magic operates off of symbolism, not science or physics or biology. You can definitely provoke emotional reactions with Life (see 'Control Instincts') but there's some level of nuance to be grasped in how that will actually manifest compared to using Mind outright (compare to Emotional Urging from Mind).
