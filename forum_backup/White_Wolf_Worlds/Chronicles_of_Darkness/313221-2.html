                                                        Moderators: I would ask to change topic in to "Constantine & Arrow ( Comic and TV series ) as inspiration" as it will be more suitable to now content of it.

                                                        As dwelling more in to now developing occult background of Arrow series, let's see all the fuss over Lasarus Pit in the hands of League of Assasins from the season three and in full glory in season four.

                                                        In Arrow ( and DC ) Pit can prolong your life, but it makes you "wicked". Lately, it is shown it can even bring back the dead, only that the effects are more profound - and more you time being "on the other side", the less human you return.

                                                        So the rules are simple and mostly accurate with what 2ED Storytelling System is giving us ( God Machine Rules Update, page 41, for example ). Put simply, using Lasarus Pit is stealing your soul.

                                                        Prolonging the life

                                                        Character that use Pit to prolong his life is in the first stage of Soul Loss and get Souless Condition - only diffrance is that does not have regaining Willpower throught Vice as Breaking Point. Lasarus Pit makes you "wicked" and driven by your Vice, but do not want you to become madman - yet. Instead, character have new Breaking Point on re-using the Pit - it can be years of between each one, but sooner or later the character need to re-use it.

                                                        Bring back the dead

                                                        If character was brought up from dead in number of days equal to her dots of Willpower, she gains Enervated Condition. The difference from normal rules is that character can stop effects of this Condition by killing someone - it will make condition dormant for her Willpower dots in weeks. And each killing is Breaking Point for Integrity as always.

                                                        If character was brought up from dead later than number of days in her Willpower dots, she gains Thrall Condition. Unless soul restoring magic is performed, she will always be without a soul from now on.

                                                        Sum up

                                                        So what do you think? Is it working with to date shown rules in Arrow? Is it working with 2ED Storytelling System?

                                                            Constantine is hands down an acanthus-
                                                            he rides what he calls the synchronicity wave- basically the series of coincidences that throw him into every plot, making him at the right place at the right time, every time. His mastery of fate magic helps him sniff out loopholes and make wild gambles on risky prospects that frequently throw his friends under the bus, and most of his greatest triumphs from the comic book series come from when he exploits the deals and arrangements that Heaven/Hell has to operate by- divining the specific bans that Spirits have, or noticing possible breaches of contracts way before they happen.

                                                            Half the time most of his power comes from calling in favors that others owe him- ever wonder why they don't tell him to fuck off? It's cause he's got a kharma whammy bat if they do, hiding in his trenchcoat. Sure he has a bit of mind- to pull off the occasional jedi mind trick, but more often than not he's just along for the ride, relying on sheer luck(magic) to get him through. For further reference, He's referred to frequently as the Fool, embodying all the concepts of the tarot card that represents the Acanthus path, even being published on the fool's image in Vertigo's special edition of the tarot. He comes as one of the incarnations of his family line, The Constant One, a heritage of what's referred to as laughing magicians, magic workers who generate luck and trick others through oaths. John Constantine. Straight up Acanthus.
