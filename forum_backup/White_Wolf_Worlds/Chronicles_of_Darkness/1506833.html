    For Fun - Dissonance-free Dragonriders and Other Fantasy Troupes

    03-29-2023, 05:39 AM
    The occasional bit of levity or absurdity can be a nice counterpoint to the more monstrous aspect of Mages. To that end...

    What options does a Mage have for acquiring fantasy mounts? Like, a dragon, a pegasus, a giant tortoise with a palinquin, etc. Transform Life or Create Life are subject to Dissonance*, as is Shapechange, and you can't defy physics anyway without the easily-eroded Levitation.
    A Familiar -spirits being the most likely, though Goetia could do in a pinch with some assistance- could manifest into a rideable state. Just don't use a spell-created entity.

    Maybe a Fae Mount? Not sure if a Mage is eligible, but as long as you can feed it glamour and it has the Actormask perk, you can bring all kinds of creatures into the world. 'Course, the Mask eliminates some of the awe of "goddammit dragon rider".

    I dont know the other lines well, so anyone have options I missed?

    *For the first generation, at any rate, with the Reach effect.

                                                      Originally posted by jsnider3 View Post
                                                      I'd do the Reading Rainbow thing. Go into a bookstore, find the highest-rated spirit of Fiction or whatever, and see what it wants to let me ride Drogon to do my errands.
                                                      That's not really how Influences work; an Influence of "Create Fiction" is going to bring a work of fiction into existence, not make a thing that was fictional become real.

                                                      Also it's typically going to belong to a spirit of rank 4 or 5, which is not going to be encountered casually and be a bit perilous to deal with. The highest rated spirit you're likely to find in any random bookstore you go into is 2, and even if Influence: Fiction could conjure fictional things into existence, it's not going to do that at the "manipulate" level.

                                                      I think the Awakened will be well suited to discovering creatures that might be made amenable to riding with Life or Mind magic that already exist naturally in the Fallen World, some of which might be regarded as Cryptids and some that might have certain other quirks. I think it can help to get a trail to pick up for such things in folklore and urban legend about strange beings off the edge of the map, but perhaps the most immediate thing that can be done is calling upon those entities that bestow divined knowledge; a Supernal Being aligned with the Life Arcanum (especially an Atavism of the Primal Wilds) or visiting Stag or Snake of the Aeons might be used to acquire a starting point.
