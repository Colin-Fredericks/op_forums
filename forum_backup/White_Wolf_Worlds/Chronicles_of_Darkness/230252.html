                                      I’ve stated what I want elsewhere. Summarizing:

                                      • More choices in terms of how a Promethean is made. In particular, incorporate much of the demiurge material from Saturnine Night, and/or provide ways for mortals to gather Azoth and imbue a creation with it. In keeping with the title, one possible source of Pyros would be a Firestorm.

                                      • More options for what a Promethean is made of. Flesh should be the best “conductor” for Pyros; but constructs should also be options. Unfleshed should be a subset of constructs, with the creator being rewarded for his efforts if he incorporates articulated joints, motors to move the joints, a power system to drive the motors, a brain of some sort to direct them, sensors to provide feedback, and so on. Likewise, starting out with a humanoid form should be encouraged by means of simulating any inhuman features with Pandoran Transmutations that both increase the likelihood of the Generative Act going bad and interfere with the final Crucible. This is somewhat less important to me than the first option; but especially in the God Machine era, the Unfleshed option should be given more prominence.

                                      • A build-your-own-Lineage system, with the five Lineages from the orignal game being presented as worked examples. The way the Unfleshed are done in Saturnine Night would be a reasonable approach to use. However, I’d avoid the “make your own Humour” stuff from Saturnine Night’s demiurges section, or at least relegate it to a box to the effect of “five Humours aren’t enough for you, then make up more of your own.” In effect, the Humours should take the role that Lineages currently hold as the five-way division of most Created in terms of what they are.

                                      • Humanity should start at 3 and build up from there. One of the Promethean’s Aspirations should be replaced by a Purpose, which works the same way for the most part but represents the “programming” the Promethean was created with. The Purpose should also have a compulsory facet to it, which can be overcome using Humanity. Possibly give the Promethean’s creator the ability to make the Purpose harder to resist, to facilitate “rebellion against my maker” story options. Conversely, let the Purpose help the Promethean resist the throes of Torment, with it being more effective with the creator boosting it, so that neither the Purpose nor the creator’s resulting influence over his creation are entirely bad things.

                                      • There should be “Humanity 0 Prometheans” akin to Blood and Smoke’s Revenants: “organic machines” (or not-so-organic, if the construct/Unfleshed options are supported) that behave like clones from the original game — in fact, clones should be one example of these things; the Imbued from World of Darkness: Antagonists would be another. Like Revenants, there should be an option for an “automaton” to be upgraded to a full-fledged Promethean using an abbreviated version of the Generative Act.

                                      • Base Athanors on Refinements rather than Lineages/Humours.

                                      • Disquiet and Wasteland should be extrapolations of the mechanics found in Mortal Remains.

                                      EDIT: (to keep it all in one place)

                                      • Revise the “Final Azoth Roll” as an Extended Action where the goal is to accumulate enough successes to recover your memories and/or to integrate yourself into mortal society, with Exceptional Successes granting you little bonuses that leave you human but leaning more toward the “best of humanity” end of the spectrum. 15–20 successes seems about right for this.
                                      • Consider reversing the roles of Humanity and Azoth in the New Dawn mechanics: you need enough Azoth to transform yourself innto a human; and you need sufficient Humanity for your resulting life to be a noticeable improvement over your Promethean life: not enough Humanity when you Redeem, and you end up as an amnesiac vagrant.
