    The (Mad) Science of Magic

    02-20-2018, 02:18 PM
    Greetings all,
    Here is a fun thing that I've been working on, and thought I would share it in case anyone else found it interesting. For context, I'm playing a Mad Scientist in a heavily homebrewed crossover game, and part of the fun of that character is being forced to interact with magical phenomena, and somehow explain it from a rigidly non-magical scientistic point of view. Most of my Wondrous Devices so far have been building sensory equipment to actually see and analyze magic, which has allowed my character to start writing her own theories as to how and why some of this shit is happening (since she is not going to take any fluffy occult tradition at their word).
    In case it needs to be said, this is an extremely biased in-character opinion, not having anything to do with canon. Also, I'm not anywhere close to an expert in particle physics - this is all based on the stuff I understood while crawling Wikipedia. So, huge grains of salt all over the place. But it's fun to dream....

    Hope you enjoy!
    ~Seraph Kitty


    Dynamic Field Conductive Radiation (Mana)

    This highly versatile phenomena is best described as a form of radiation which transfers an energized particle across distance (and sometimes through matter) until it embeds in a molecular structure or energy field. Once interacting with an existing phenomena, the particle moves along any existing substructure until it finds a missing or flawed reaction, and organizes itself to correct the structure, as directed by existing energy patterns - thereby creating a “mathematically perfect” expression of the pattern. Commonly, it replaces any impurities in a substance or reaction, or serves as a perfect conductor of the process.

    Furthermore, it can conduct interactions between energy fields or forms of matter that would not otherwise interact - such as causing an psycho-reactive energy wave to transfer its pattern into the behavior of electromagnetic particles. This property suggests that the radiation may be a bonded cluster of various fundamental particles - specifically the fermians or “matter particles” (at minimum including electrons and ethons [see below]). (Although, rather than a cluster of fermions, it may be that this phenomena is caused by a new type of fermion particle that interacts with every field [see "pyros" below]).
    Due to its versatile applications, deliberate use of this radiation is the basis of at least one kind of “magic”. “Supernal mages” emitt some amount of this particle into every phenomena they affect, causing at times drastic reconfigurations of matter and energy. More concentrated emissions seem to be required to entirely replace one energy or matter pattern with another. Concentrated emissions can also stabilize the field alterations, preventing unintended cascading effects across fields. Its deliberate use in these effects destabilizes the radiation phenomena, causing a breakdown into other fundamental particles.
    Individuals who regularly utilize this substance describe being able to “collect it” into their bodies and emitt it at later times through conscious effort. This is likely due to the production of a specialized chemical protein which is particularly absorbitant of this radiation, and the release of it through production of the enzyme to break it down. This could be consciously triggered by using a specific emotional state to stimulate production of the proteins and enzymes through the endocrine system. However this process is entirely theoretical, and requires more invasive research to study.
    There is no current theory as to the origin of this radiation. Anecdotal evidence states that it can be found in discrete locations throughout the world, most often saturated into nearby matter, which suggests the presence of radioactive materials which emit this particle through decay - but the existence of this material is entirely theoretical.
    This radiation always interacts with neural activity, in a manner distinctive from its other properties. Foremost, it does so without becoming embedded or decaying, suggesting that it emits a psycho-reactive wave which easily synchronizes with human brain waves. In its passive form, wherein it saturates material objects, it heightens perception and elevates the perceived importance of whatever object or location it permeates. More concentrated emissions of the radiation (such as its deliberate application for field alterations) provoke such an elevated state that it triggers traumatic suppression. Most, but not all, humans are prone to synchronization with this brain wave; some, most commonly those who manipulate the radiation, demonstrate resistance to its neural interaction.


    Controlled Quantum Field Collapse (Wyrd)

    All particles exist in a state of quantum uncertainty, in which any number of states of being (and therefore interactions with other particles) exist in a range of probabilities. Certain esoteric forces can facilitate the sudden collapse of a localized quantum field in a controlled manner to choose a probable outcome.

    This controlled collapses causes a state-change in the localized quantum field, which can have all manner of practical consequences - such as affecting a person’s brain chemistry or any other organ system, causing particles to gain higher or lower energy states, ionizing a field to conduct a particular form of energy, or even triggering a chain of low-probability effects across a wide field to produce an unlikely “coincidence”.
    Since all quantum fields throughout an area interact with one another, a localized collapse can be “programmed” with applicable environmental conditions. For example, a predetermined controlled collapse could cause a heightened cortisol response in the presence of a spider (manufacturing a phobia), or instead produce more adrenaline (enhancing fight or flight reflexes) in the presence of gunpowder. These controlled collapses can thus be programmed in a series of if/then statements.

    Entanglement
    Most forms of programming a controlled collapse occur through quantum entanglement - the process by which quantum fields interact with one another. When entanglement is done by esoteric forces, it can force two localized fields to interact when they ordinarily wouldn’t (whether due to distance, the nature of the phenomena, or any other reason) - such as entangling the local quantum field of a person with that of another person, which can persist despite time or distance. Additionally, when quantum fields are already interacting, esoteric forces can program controlled field collapses into the existing entanglement.

    Variant: Psycho-Reactive Entanglement (sympathetic links)
    A commonly occurring phenomena of the psycho-reactive field (see below) is for the pathons produced by specific elevated neural activity to saturate an object or creature, applying a resonant frequency which is synchronized with those brain waves. This process can cause an entanglement between the quantum fields of the recipient and the individual. These psycho-reactive entanglements do not seem to cause any measurable change in the quantum field behavior of either subject, but can be manipulated for more deliberate entanglements and programming controlled quantum field collapses.


    Psycho-Reactive Field (Resonance)

    Certain esoteric phenomena point to the existence of a fifth fundamental force, creating a field similar in nature to the electromagnetic or gravitational fields: the “resonant” force, which governs the “psycho-reactive” field, and is carried by the (theoretical) “pathon” boson particle. Observed pathon radiation is primarily generated through neural activity, with heightened emotional states generating more pathon waves (i.e. more energy output). The interactions of the PR field can be predicted with wave-particle-field models similar to the EM spectrum.

    Although neural activity can generate new energy waves, an ambient amount of energy naturally exists. Pathons move through the universe similar to other high-energy particles, and when they bounce off of matter, they change frequencies - which can then be imprinted on local PR fields. In this manner, even simple organic and inorganic compounds (rocks and trees) can leave imprints in the psycho-reactive field.
    Similar to EM radiation, psycho-reactive energies exist in a wide range of “resonant” frequencies. As a frequency wave passes through the local PR field, it imprints its frequency signature into the field, changing how that field behaves. However, the impact of a PR wave on a field is often diminished by waves of other frequencies; therefore a local PR field only retains a “resonant signature” through repeated exposure to a single frequency.
    Once a field retains a resonant signature, it is able to generate its own psycho-reactive waves in particular circumstances. Much like moving a magnet past a conductor can generate a current, active brain tissue moving into an imprinted PR field will generate psycho-reactive waves, which the neural activity then synchronizes with - effectively altering the mental-emotional state of the creature. This can result in a positive feedback loop; once a local PR field retains a resonant imprint, it can be very difficult to alter.

    Variant: Glamour
    Some individuals have demonstrated considerable manipulation of psycho-reactive energy, most notably the ability to change its state between wave, particle, and field almost immediately through conscious effort. Their ability to do this allows them to absorb a PR wave traveling through them, and 1) re-emit that wave to synchronize another individual’s mental-emotional state, or 2) emitt it into an object’s ethon particles, transferring the resonant frequency signature into ordinary matter which will then contain an imprinted localized PR field, or to 3) convert it into a localized PR field with an imprinted resonant frequency. These individuals often have fabricated PR fields around their bodies, specifically energized to change how they are interacted with in the visual, electrostatic, and psycho-reactive spectrum - how they look, feel, and emotionally resonate with others.
    The dynamic state-changes of “glamour” compared to other PR phenomena likely contribute to how difficult it is to analyze or interact with it, as most techniques focus either on the waves or fields themselves, rarely using a heuristic approach.

    Variant: Conviction [homebrewed trait]
    While all neural activity can generate PR waves, some individuals display the ability to consciously generate potent field changes. This may be due to their specific brain chemistry causing frontal lobe activity to generate more energy waves, or may indicate knowledge of a specialized meditative technique which can consistently trigger specific emotional peaks - more invasive research is required to refine the hypothesis. These individuals can forcibly imprint local psycho-reactive field with their own specific emotional states; an effect which is short lived, but which can (temporarily) overwhelm existing frequencies. Each individual with such a talent appears to only be able to generate a narrow range of frequencies, but this is likely a due to which heightened emotional states they can have learned to consciously trigger in themselves.
    This technique is frequently used in conjunction with other phenomena. A consciously generated PR field might be employed to synchronize other people’s brainwaves to their intended emotional state, or it may be used to create a quantum field which they may entangle themselves to in order to program a controlled collapse. Many “magical” techniques rely on emitting dynamic field conductive radiation (see above) in order to effect a similar degree of conscious control over the local PR field.

    Macroscale Psycho-Reactive Ionization (ley lines)
    Across vast distances, the psycho-reactive field naturally develops patterns of energy transference. These form from the wide-ranging fluctuations which occur when a localized field becomes imprinted with a resonant frequency, and tend to form into "channels" which are likewise imprinted with the frequency. As channels link between various imprinted localized fields, the frequency transference takes a directional quality. Spots where channels cross can spontaneously imprint the localized field as the intermingling frequencies create fluctuations as they attempt to synchronize. These "nodes" can apparently retain multiple resonant frequencies for a sustained period of time. Since this contradicts the existing model, more research is required to refine the theory, but the current hypothesis is that the resonant channels are weak, wide-ranging attempts to synchronize frequencies across the entire PR field, and they consequently fractal into "micro-zones" where they clash with another frequency, not having enough energy from the original imprinting to mitigate one another. Thus, rather than a node having a local field with two frequencies, it is instead an area where the field is becoming polarized between two frequencies, with the divisions between them being too minute to properly measure.


    Resonant Ethon Particles (Essence)

    Ethons are the type of “mass particle” which is manipulated by the “force particle” of pathons - similar to how electrons are manipulated by photons. In much the same way, ethons can absorb and emit pathons, changing their “energy state” when they do so - the “energy state” in this case being the resonant frequency signature carried by the pathon. Pathons can carry a wide variety of frequencies, not all of which interact with each other when applied to Ethon particles; as a result there are several frequency ranges (or bandwidths) of exclusionary interaction.

    It’s theorized that ethons are a particle component of neutrons. While in the nucleus of atomic matter, changes to the resonant frequency of the neutrons’ ethon particles would cause fluctuations in the electromagnetic field - which could explain how the PR field interacts with EM, and could also relate to the weak and strong nuclear forces.
    As a type of mass particle, ethons tend to accumulate in the areas they first become pathonically charged. Ethons which resonate on the same frequencies will group together, almost as though covalently bonded, and will serve as a conductor for pathon energies in that frequency - similar to magnetized substances. In some ways, this is the “atom” equivalent of the psycho-reactive field. When a large amount of resonant ethons joined together, the energy pattern shared by the bonded structure can form complex permutations of their resonant frequency, as though developing thought, behavior, and even self awareness. Though unique in the realm of physics, it is not an entirely surprising possibility when considering the close relationship the PR field has to neural activity.
    Just as ethons can become resonant by absorbing pathons, they are able to emit pathons to cause complex energy reactions, primarily in the psycho-reactive field, but potentially affecting the EM field as well. Doing so causes an ethon to lose its resonant frequency, immediately unbonding from any other particles and returning to a near-inert state - although some patterns of emission can cause it to retain a degree of PR field reactivity.

    Near-Inert Ethon: Plasm
    After a resonant ethon particle with a frequency in the emotional/mnemonic range [ghosts] bonds with dark matter, and then emits its pathons in a large energy expenditure, it becomes unbonded from the rest of the ethon-bonded dark matter, but retains some residual amount of resonant energy, which is slowly shed in on-going pathon emissions. Some individuals have found ways to make use of this material.

    Near-Inert Ethon: Aether
    Certain processes which rely on ethon discharge [God Machine angels and infrastructure] leave the ethon particles in a near-inert state which continues to have a degree of resonant charge, but also becomes highly reactive to biological material. When it bonds with living organisms, it can trigger mutations, altered mental states, and even some ability to consciously manipulate the PR field for as long as the material continues to have resonant frequency emissions. Since biological mutations usually require PR emissions to be stable, affected creatures are incentivized to consume more of this near-inert particle, or their biological processes are prone to cease.


    Ethon-bonded Dark Matter (Ephemeral Substance)

    There is evidence of a dark matter substance which is highly reactive to PR energies - specifically to Resonant Ethon Particles. In its natural state (whether charged or inert), this material does not interact with the electromagnetic spectrum - rendering it invisible to all forms of EM radiation, and making it intangible to electrostatic interactions. This implies an absence of charged particles - perhaps suggesting that this dark matter is unbounded neutrons.

    However, by interacting with Resonant Ethon Particles, it easily adopts their resonant frequency signatures. Although the inert dark matter is responsive to Ethons found in any bandwidth range, it likewise assumes the exclusionary bandwidth spectrum of the imprinted frequency, and ceases to interact with other dark matter charged with frequencies outside of the bandwidth.
    When the dark matter bonds with ethons, the resonant frequency within the ethon “molecules” transfers to the dark matter mass and reorganizes it into a superstructure, informed by the specific frequency signature it adopts. These structures can be quite massive, equivalent in scale to complex organisms. As sufficient quantities of resonant ethos can adopt complex psycho-reactive patterns to the degree of mimicking behavior, when these energy patterns are applied to a dark matter mass, they can replicate the appearance and behavior of organic life (the mind and the body, respectively), but are contingent upon a stable ethon energy pattern.
    Whatever the nature of this dark matter in its inert form, it can be energized in such as way as to temporarily interact with the electromagnetic spectrum. The emission of pathon particles from a resonant ethon creates an energized cloud conducted through the dark matter. While in this energy being channeled through the dark energy mass, it becomes reactive to the EM spectrum by both reflecting visible light and interacting with electrostatic fields; once the energy ceases to be channeled, its EM reactivity likewise dissipates. This suggests an electron field is created through the process, but without a stable interaction of the strong force to bond them into permanent atomic structures. Based on this, the current theory posits that the charged dark matter state created by pathon emission generates a positively charged electrical cloud - attracting any unbound electrons into it to balance the charge, wherein they form electron orbitals, and consequently EM reactivity. When this energy dissipates, the electrons cease to be held in place, and the EM field collapses - returning the dark matter to its natural EM inert state.


    Multi-Field Boson (Pyros)

    Sparse anecdotal reports from fringe researchers suggest a hyper-catalystic form of energy might exist. Current research in the field suggests that it is an infinitely pervasive “sub-elementary force”; a field which allows the very possibility of change to occur in other particles, which may be the very definition of time-space. This field is too minute and fundamental to have any practical application, however some researchers claim to have harnessed highly unstable materials that cause concentrated fluctuations in this field - likely the boson particle which carries this field’s energy. Such particles, according to anecdotal accounts, naturally occur in very short-lived bursts of rapid energy change, but they require massive amounts of energy to form and very rapidly dissipate.

    This phenomena can be compared to the Higgs-Boson, which, according to the mathematical theory, can only exist in a measurable form through particle collisions of massive energy, and which will lose its isospin symmetry outside of these conditions, breaking down and being absorbed by fermions, which then gain mass. In much the same way, the “Pyros” particle would be another scalar boson whose field saturates time-space, but whose particle form will only exist in brief bursts of high energy, rapidly breaking down to give other fermions (and other bosons) the potentiality for change.
    If reports of this hyper-catalyst are accurate, the Pyros particle interacts with every other fundamental field, causing energy alterations in their respective particles; in brief, it is a particle capable of transferring energy to other bosons. In these anecdotal reports, Pyros interactions can not only add energy and accelerate reactions, but also remove energy from a system (likely transferring it to a different field) and accelerate entropy.

    If a multi-field boson of this nature does exist, it implies the existence of a multi-field fermion particle(s). A particle of that nature would likely act as a bonding agent with any other fermion, and would be receptive to energy transference from the bosons of any other field - a universal bonding agent and conductor. [See “dynamic field conductive radiation” above].
