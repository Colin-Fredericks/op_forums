    時空 Jikuu : generally it means a concept of time-space
    因縁 Innen: direct cause and indirect conditions which underlie the actions of all things (buddhist term)
    精神 Seishin： mind, soul, heart, spirit
    空間： Kuukan: void+space, how cool is that :P
    死 Shi: death. plain and simple
    物質 Busshitsu: material, substance
    生気: Seiki: life, vitality, vigor
    神明: Shinmyou: spirits of heaven and earth (term from Shinto, meaning all kinds of lesser and greater gods)
    理念: Rinen: (platonic) ideal, how things ought to be, foundational principle, idea, conception (characters: logic/reason/justice+idea/thought)
    力： Chikara: force, energy （character used in contexts like electric power and such)

                    Bonus content from my players, who decided that they had fun with the "Arcana in Japanese" request; this time, it's Chinese.
                    时间： Shíjiān, the concept of time
                    因果： Yīnguǒ: cause and effect, karma (buddhism)
                    精神： Jīngshén: mind, conciousness, mental
                    空间： Kōngjiān: void+space
                    死亡： Sǐwáng: death+perish
                    物质： Wùzhí: matter, substance
                    理想： Lǐxiǎng: ideal
                    力： Lì: force, power
                    生意: Shēngyì: life and vitality
                    神怪： Shénguài: spirits and devils (name for all spiritual beings)
