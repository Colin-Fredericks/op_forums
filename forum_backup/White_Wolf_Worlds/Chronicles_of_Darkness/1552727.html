    Obtenebration Homebrew for VTR I am working on

    07-29-2025, 04:13 PM
    So, I am working on a homebrew for Obtenebration combining a little bit from the Khaibit, but mostly from the VTM Lasombra and looking at offering it as an alternative discipline for Mekhet (would replace Celerity).

    I tried to keep the more powered up nature of Requiem disciplines in mind, but keep the versatility of Obtenebration which I think was its strong suit. The Khaibit focused on it being used for dealing with ephemerals and for assassination.


    Obtenebration


    The Beast demands obedience from all including darkness itself. It slips
    into the Kindred’s shadow, twisting it first then extending to the shadows of others. It progresses from the simple forming of shadows, to using them to harm, consume, move, or create.
    Obtenebration is the trademark Discipline of clan Mekhet which is the unearthly control over shadows, darkness, and the abyss.

    All but the most basic uses of Obtenebration are clearly supernatural and care must be taken when using it in the presence of mortals, who are filled with dread at the mere sight of it.

    The kindred themselves must also be careful for the shadows are volatile, improper use may see the vampire and not their foes as the one consumed by the darkness.


    Panoply

    The vampire's shadow warps and twists under the might of the beast, it drapes over them shrouding them in shadow for a moment, and when it pulls back it leaves something behind.
    With this treasured discipline the vampire is never unarmed or without protection, just as they are never without their shadow.

    Panoply creates a weapon or suit of armor out of living shadow, the creations can only be simple weapons or armor, a bow and a dozen arrows works as does a suit of chainmail but a gun does not.

    Panoply can create a weapon, and the damage is equivalent to your obtenebration dots, for 2 vitae the damage can be boosted by 1. Weapons made by panoply do aggravated damage to ephemeral entities.

    Panoply can create an armor, with a rating up to your obtenebration dots, for 2 vitae the armor rating can be boosted by 1.

    Weapons and armor created by panoply have a somewhat ethereal quality, they are made up of differing shades of shadow from the deep black to gray to dark purple, not quite see through but not solid in appearance either. The vampire using them must take care to either make them appear mundane through obfuscate or use them only in a situation where there will be no survivors or in one where the survivors cannot recall what they saw.

    Cost: 0 Vitae, (2 conditional)
    Dice Pool: Resolve + Occult + Obtenebration
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Scene
    Roll Results

    Dramatic Failure: The vampire not only fails to form a solid feature from shadow, but also takes 1 level of bashing damage from the failed attempt and one level of bashing for any 1's rolled.

    Failure: The vampire cannot manifest the shadows.

    Success: The vampire creates what they desire from the shadows.

    Exceptional Success: The weapon or armor is true shadow, it becomes weightless, and does not lower initiative, defense, or speed.​


    Shadowplay

    At this level the vampire is not limited to their own shadow. The beast flexes and the shadows of another cower before the might of the beast, the victim's shadow rises from beneath them and chokes the owner with hands of inky blackness, squeezing the life from them in a leeching embrace.

    Alternatively, shadows in the ambient area can be called to make the vampire appear unnaturally sinister, their eyes darken their pallid features stand out in stark relief against inky blackness and the room seems to grow smaller in their presence.

    Choke variant
    Cost: 1 Vitae
    Dice Pool: Strength + Occult + Obtenebration vs. Stamina +
    Blood Potency
    Action: Instant
    Duration: 1 turn, (2 turns on exceptional success)
    Roll Results

    Dramatic Failure: The vampire not only fails to command shadow, but the victims shadow cannot be commanded again for the night.

    Failure: The vampire cannot control the shadows.

    Success: The vampire's mastery of shadow is evident for every success achieved a mortal victim or ephemeral takes 1 health level of lethal damage, a vampire or other supernatural suffers bashing damage instead.

    Exceptional Success: The shadow lingers, binding for a time the character gains the stunned tilt.

    Intimidation variant
    Cost: None
    Dicepool: None
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Scene

    The vampire adds their obtenebration dice to any roll that makes use of intimidation.​​


    Shroud

    At this level the kindred are not bound to existing shadow. The Beast demands service from all including darkness itself. It slips into the Kindred’s hand or into their mouth wreathing it in viscous smoke, ready to consume everything. The vampire can expel it to create a cloud of inky blackness within 50 meters of their location. The cloud completely obscures light and even sound to some extent. Those who have been trapped within it (and survived) describe the cloud as viscous and unnerving. This physical manifestation lends credence to those Mekhet who claim that their darkness is something other than mere shadow.

    The tarry mass actually extinguishes light sources it engulfs (with the exception of fire), and muffles sounds until they are indistinguishable. Those within the cloud lose all sense of sight and feel as though they’ve been immersed in pitch. Sound also warps and distorts within the cloud, making it nearly impossible to accomplish anything. Additionally, being surrounded by the Shroud is suffocating as the murk smothers and agitates the victims. More than one unfortunate mortal has “drowned” in darkness.

    Mekhet can see through their own shroud perfectly.

    Cost: 2 Vitae
    Dice Pool: Manipulation+ Occult + Obtenebration
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Scene
    Roll Results

    Dramatic Failure: The vampire not only fails to command shadow, but takes 1 lethal damage and a further 1 lethal damage for every 1 rolled.

    Failure: The vampire cannot control the shadows.

    Success: The vampire's mastery of shadow is evident for every success achieved the shadow is 3 meters in diameter. Those within lose 2 points of stamina mortal victims with 2 stamina or less are killed, a vampire suffers this penalty as a temporary loss of 2 health levels. Those within it are given the blinded, and deafened tilts.

    Exceptional Success: The shadow grows darker and seems to still, characters stuck within the murk gain the leg wrack tilt to one leg finding it difficult to move through the tarry cloud and the stamina damage upgrades from 2 to 3 points.


    Shadowstep


    At this level the kindred are not bound to the physical plane as strongly as most. The Beast tears through all including darkness itself. The vampire has such fine control over the darkness that he may become it briefly and reform himself from other darkness close by. The vampire may Shadowstep through walls, floors, and even mystical barriers. The Mekhet simply steps “into” a shadow and re-emerges from another shadow a short distance away (or next to the barrier, if there is no shadow on the other side.

    Cost: 0
    Dice Pool: Dexterity+ Occult + Obtenebration
    Action: Instant
    Duration: turn
    Roll Results

    Dramatic Failure: The vampire not only fails to command shadow but becomes temporarily trapped in the abyss. They will linger in this plane for 1 day plus another day for every 1 rolled, and will lose the regular amount of vitae per day as if they rose normally. Additionally when the time is up they will be spat out in a random location 3 miles away plus an additional 3 miles for every 1 they rolled.

    Failure: The vampire cannot control the shadows.

    Success: The vampire's mastery of shadow is evident for every success achieved the vampire may teleport up to 15 meters.

    Exceptional Success: The trip through the shadows was pleasant the beast gains the sated condition.


    Nightshades

    At this level the kindred are known to the shadows that serve them. The Beast demands service from all including darkness itself and finally something tangible answers. The vampire has gained such power over Obtenebration's dark energies that he may summon creatures made of shadow itself. There is much debate as to the true nature of these night shades. Some claim they are damned souls brought back to the land of the living. Others state they are animated darkness like the Strix. Still others think of them as extensions of the vampire's own black spirit. A few Mekhet have pointed out that these may all be one and the same... The vampire drips the blood into his own shadow, creating a doorway through which the Shade enters this world. The shade can only emerge from the character's own shadow but may move about freely thereafter. Once created, the shade is ready to serve the Mekhet.


    Cost: 4 Vitae and 1 temporary willpower dot per shade
    Dice Pool: Presence + Occult + Obtenebration
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Nights equal to obten dots, can be prolonged by this value again by giving it 4 vitae
    Roll Results

    Dramatic Failure: The shade forms but attacks its master perhaps immediately perhaps days later, storyteller is encouraged to give no clues about the shades state, but the character may attempt to use auspex if they pass a mental roll of storyteller's choice against the shade.

    Failure: The vampire cannot summon the shade.

    Success: The vampire's mastery of shadow is evident the shade enters ready to serve.

    Exceptional Success: The shade gains an additional point to stamina, and strength.​


    Shade stats

    Size 5
    Speed 11
    Defense 4
    Armor 0-1 (if using panoply)
    Imitative 4
    Health 9
    Willpower: 5
    Physical: Strength 3, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
    Social Composure 1, Manipulation 0, Presence 0
    Mental Wits 4, Intelligence 2, resolve 4
    Skills: Athletics 1, Melee 3, Occult 3, Stealth 5
    Disciplines: Obfuscate 1, obten 1, auspex 1.

    Other Information: A Night Shade is not blinded by light (although flame injures it as does any other attack), and it is not affected by illusions or by darkness, natural or unnatural and can use its disciplines at no cost. Shades are destroyed instantly by direct sunlight.


    So I am looking for feedback on these I personally do not think they are too strong, at least not in comparison to some other discipline abilities, I am a little unsure about Nightshades though, I wanted a power that served a similar niche as ghouling or familiar with a higher cost but more power but I may lower the stats a tad.

    Panoply is more of a thematic power, a way for a mekhet to appear unarmed but if absolutely necessary brandish equipment that would breach the masquerade if not for obfuscate making it possibly ignored. It is based on an old power Aegis of Shadow that let a vampire create an armor that could soak bashing lethal, and aggravated with the exception of fire and sunlight granting 1 point of this armor for each success on a manipulation and occult roll difficulty 7. The aggravated damage reduction, possibility of a ridiculously good roll and casual fear ability is what made this a level 6 power. Without those aspects I think it scales nicely as a level 1 power a vampiric spy would use.

    Shroud is basically unchanged except for the extra successes giving an advantage and applying mechanical tilts instead of the effects it had in masquerade.

    Shadow step has some mechanical rules that actually give getting trapped in the abyss more downsides. And it was an essential power for an infiltrator, fitting right in as a substitute for protean.

    Shadowplay is perhaps the most changed, I stamina was simply far too weak, so I made it deal actual damage but did make it a contested roll and gave it the downside of in the event of failure it could not be attempted again on the same character.

    If you can think of adjustments or even changing the order or attributes used let me know!

                          Greeting, as a fellow VTR fan, I’m glad to see some new home brew, keep it alive(or undead)! Here are some of my thoughts.

                          Panoply : With the new version you posted it thinks it’s fairly balanced, maybe a bit weak. Obfuscate 1 can allow a character to walk around with full military gear with no real risk of getting caught and it grants them the benefit of anonymity as well. Panoply works in reverse; it grants weapons to those who don’t have them but they risk masquerade breach. Additionally, the damage being capped by Obtenebration dots means it requires heavy investment into the discipline to have tangible results. The real advantage I can think of is that it allows Vigour users to go ham on boosting damage without fear of breaking their weapon. Here’s what I wound change :
                          • I would remove the vitae cost entirely.
                          • Remove the need for a dice pool to keep it in line with similar personal buff power. (Protean for instance works like this.)
                          Shadowplay : Balanced, but imo, kinda boring. Shadowplay should allow an obtenebration user to manipulate shadows and solidify them to some extent. Personally, I would go something like this :

                          Shadowplay
                          At this level the vampire is not limited to their own shadow, they can manipulate or grasp things with their shadows. (Feel free to add any more evocative lore text i'm pretty bad at this)
                          Cost: 2 Vitae (or more, see text)
                          Dice Pool: None (See text)
                          Action: Instant
                          Duration: One scene
                          When activating shadowplay a vampire can manipulate any surrounding shadows within Obtenebration dots + Blood potency meters for a scene. They can manipulate how shadows manifest themselves, and use them to grasp things as if they were using their own hands. This allows them to manipulate objects, grapple victims, or even manipulate shadows to make themselves more threatening.
                          Any active use of Shadowplay takes an instant action and requires instant actions for subsequent turns to keep the effect. The obtenebration user can spend a point of willpower to make one of their manipulation more persistent.
                          • Grappling : The vampire can grapple with any character within reach that is in contact with any shadow. The obtenebration users use Strength + Occult + Obtenebration as a dice pool. Vigour does not add to the user's strength pool as usual. (Alternatively you can use strength + occult + vigor dots.) If the vampire uses a point of willpower to keep the effect active the shadow will keep repeating the same grappling action each turn. Finally, when taking the damage move in a grapple against an ephemeral being it deals lethal damage instead of bashing.
                          • Intimidation : If the vampire can add their obtenebration dots to intimidation. If they are wearing panoply armour, they do not need to keep concentrating on their manipulation to keep the intimidation bonus.

                          Shroud : The power is fine, but I don’t get the stamina reduction, maybe lethal or superficial damage would be more appropriate if it’s a life-sucking cloud? It could deal damage whenever a victim starts its turn inside.

                          Shadow steps : This is fine for a fourth level power, but cost 0 means it can be spammed indefinitely, is this intentional? If the idea is that they can use that power frequently, I would make the vitae cost 1 for a whole scene, this would allow a vampire to teleport frequently but not without cost. I would still have each jump require a roll to activate.

                          Nightshades : I’m on the fence with this one. The shades are both powerful and kind of weak to me and i have a hard time evaluating it.
                          • Any ‘’summons’’ with static stats is going to scale poorly, but they are also in line with animalism.
                          • They are good at stealth, kinda meh in a battle and can’t interact socially.
                          • Four Vitae + 1 will is too much. I’d probably go 1 or 2 vitae and 1 will per shades.
                          I personally don’t know if I would make summons the ‘’end game’’ of obtenebration. Maybe I’d buff other disicplines in interesting way, or open a new way to use the discipline, maybe it could allow the user to make shadow tangibles using the user's stamina or resolve dot as hardness for objects, etc. Nightshades could be a good Animalism •• and Obtenebration •​•​•​•​ devotion however.

                          The Sixth Discipline.
                          I agree with @monteparnas, giving them obtenebration and auspex would make them for a very powerful clan lorewise, having two unique disciplines would be powerful. However, if there’s one clan that could pull it off narratively is clan Mekhet, as they are considered the weirder clan.
                          Here are some of my suggestions :
                          • Make them a bloodline with Auspex, Celerity, Obfuscate, Obtenebration.
                          • Make them into a Mekhet or Ventrue Bloodline that gains Obtenebration. I can totally see the Ventrue ‘’Lord of shadow’’ aspect to it, and it makes them closer to actual Lasombra ; Callous with their touchstone, dominate as a discipline, animalism to push others to their worst self, etc.
                          • Give them Celerity, Obtenebration, Obfuscate and make Auspex a non-clan-specific discipline, granting every clan access to it as an off-clan discipline without needing mekhet blood specifically. This buffs everyone and doesn't remove the discipline. Auspex is often a discipline i find myself wanting with all my characters right there with obfuscate so it makes the choice easier.
                          • Create a six clan completely that use Auspex. Additionally, the damage being capped by Obtenebration dots means it requires heavy investment into the discipline to have tangible results. The real advantage I can think of is that it allows Vigour users to go ham on boosting damage without fear of breaking their weapon. Here’s what I wound change :
                              • I would remove the vitae cost entirely.
                              • Remove the need for a dice pool to keep it in line with similar personal buff power. (Protean for instance works like this.)
                              Shadowplay : Balanced, but imo, kinda boring. Shadowplay should allow an obtenebration user to manipulate shadows and solidify them to some extent. Personally, I would go something like this :

                              Shadowplay
                              At this level the vampire is not limited to their own shadow, they can manipulate or grasp things with their shadows. (Feel free to add any more evocative lore text i'm pretty bad at this)
                              Cost: 2 Vitae (or more, see text)
                              Dice Pool: None (See text)
                              Action: Instant
                              Duration: One scene
                              When activating shadowplay a vampire can manipulate any surrounding shadows within Obtenebration dots + Blood potency meters for a scene. They can manipulate how shadows manifest themselves, and use them to grasp things as if they were using their own hands. This allows them to manipulate objects, grapple victims, or even manipulate shadows to make themselves more threatening.
                              Any active use of Shadowplay takes an instant action and requires instant actions for subsequent turns to keep the effect. The obtenebration user can spend a point of willpower to make one of their manipulation more persistent.
                              • Grappling : The vampire can grapple with any character within reach that is in contact with any shadow. The obtenebration users use Strength + Occult + Obtenebration as a dice pool. Vigour does not add to the user's strength pool as usual. (Alternatively you can use strength + occult + vigor dots.) If the vampire uses a point of willpower to keep the effect active the shadow will keep repeating the same grappling action each turn. Finally, when taking the damage move in a grapple against an ephemeral being it deals lethal damage instead of bashing.
                              • Intimidation : If the vampire can add their obtenebration dots to intimidation. If they are wearing panoply armour, they do not need to keep concentrating on their manipulation to keep the intimidation bonus.
