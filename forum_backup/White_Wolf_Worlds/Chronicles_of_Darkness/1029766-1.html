    Originally posted by Caitiff Primogen View Post
    The only challenge I could foresee in an anthology style series is the amount of world building that would have to go into a series involving one of the major game lines. In a dedicated series you could parse most of that out over the first season and then just go from there, but in an anthology series you'd do all that and then immediately start over with something else which would then have to devote time to doing its own world building before giving way to the next thing, and so on.
    I suppose the answer to that might be leveraging existing material, if not characters or plot, from season to season. Like, establish "weird stuff" season A, but introduce "ghosts" which you focus on in season B, which introduces "spirits" for season C...

                    Originally posted by baileyborough View Post

                    Tone could be a challenge of sorts. Certain lines make easy comparisons for TV, but others.... at a basic level, I figure:

                    Requiem: Game of Thrones political intrigue
                    Forsaken: Sons of Anarchy
                    Awakening: some kind of detective show? Really unsure.
                    Promethean: ?
                    Changeling: court intrigue as well? Unsure.
                    Hunter: procedural?
                    Geist: no idea, but all over it.
                    Demon: Person of Interest
                    Mummy: ?

                    I mean, yes, this will never happen; the cost alone to have it not look like amateur hour would be eye watering. But a guy can dream.
                    Awakening works really well for an Occult Investigation show, that starts procedural but slowly builds its own overarching mythos. The images in my head of the initial pov character is a newly Awakened Acanthus, and there's a more senior Mastigos bad-ass who leads the Cabal that's training her.

                    Actually I may want to switch their genders, just so you can have "Actually I'm the warlock, and he's the witch"...


                    Mummy would definitely need to be structured with shorter seasons, but could be really cool and it deals with amnesia, mysteries, and gradual backstory reveals that the characters themselves learn and interact with through flashbacks that are borderline time-travel.

                                            Requiem would definitely be a ​Game of Thrones-style show, focusing on a lot of political intrigue. Actually, the set-up of ​Game of Thrones would work quite well for a Requiem show: a sudden power vacuum in a domain lights of fuse of political intrigue that explodes into large-scale conflict as everybody ignores a mounting existential threat. Shocking deaths would be par for the course, and vampire feeding methods being what they are, that could be the excuse for the Game of Thrones-style fan service if HBO picks up the show.

                                            I know people want to say Sons of Anarchy as the Forsaken inspiration, but I think it's more a hybrid of SoA and Blue Bloods, since a big part of Forsaken is the whole "policing the spirits" things, and SoA doesn't fully capture that angle.
