        Originally posted by Seidmadr View Post
        It would also be nice if Lodges were something one could come and go from. Perhaps be a member of them for a few years, but then when one has changed as a person, it'd be ok to leave, perhaps to found a lodge of your own, or join another that fits who you've become more. It would also make it contrast nicely with Legacies and Bloodlines.
        I really like this concept. Lodges that don't demand a life-long commitment, especially if an Uratha departs to join a more prestigious or powerful lodge. Extrapolating from this, I'd also like some of the higher power, more exclusive lodges to have their prerequisites include membership in one of the lesser lodges - if you can't succeed at being a member in one of those, you'll never make the grade for the more exclusive lodges.
