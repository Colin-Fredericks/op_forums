                                    Okay, general ideas for the Guard's endowment source. Pick your favorite.

                                    Sidebar: What is The People’s Will?

                                    No one in the Guard is exactly a mystic or occultist on the level they would need to figure out what exactly their endowment is. All they know is that it’s something special, and something they could use to change the world for the better. So, as the storyteller, you have options.

                                    1: It’s the Darkness

                                    The Darkness is a force, and it’s somehow sentient. Everything else pretty much flies right out the window when it comes to actually understanding this stuff. What everyone knows for sure is that the Darkness can stop magic in action just by being present, making it misfire horribly and taking away a tool that many creatures rely on out of necessity.

                                    2: It’s a Lost Relic

                                    Some relics are imbued with magic, why wouldn’t there be some that are a bane to magic? The Guard found a relic in the palace, a tool that the royal was hoping to bring to her queen in an attempt to possibly use it to save Alhambra. The location is a secret, but what is for sure is that its power is felt across the whole of the Guard.

                                    3: It’s Gestalt Energy

                                    Some fringe theories postulate that all of mankind has a collective psychic consciousness, capable of creating and destroying when a large-enough group of people agree on something as being “real”, or when an emotional drive is unified among a group of people. There’s nothing quite “magical” about the People’s Will, but it certainly is an unknown by the rest of the world. That doesn’t matter to the Egalitarians, what does is that it works against the royals.

                                    4: It’s Related to Witches

                                    Everyone knows that the most powerful magic users are witches, or at least they’re the most numerous and dangerous. Maybe they were working on something to stop an enemy group of witches, and things went haywire. Their creation turned on them, and unable to stop it the group was unable to tell their fellows about it. The royal found it, but didn’t know how to activate it. Of course, that implies that the Guard were able to find a way to make it work.

                                    5: Fucked if We Know

                                    Let’s face facts; the in this world things are just messed up. Whatever the People’s Will is, it isn’t something that just appeared one day because someone wanted it to. More likely, it was something that someone didn’t want to see the light of day ever again. Of course, in the middle of revolution nothing stands in your way.

                                                        Okay, some more ideas for the Guard.

                                                        Groups

                                                        The Channel are the means the Guard keeps contact with their various cells. Couriers, messengers, and network administrators, the rise of cell phones and email has only made them more valuable as the keepers of the names and lists of fellow Egalitarians.

                                                        The Action are the ground troops of the Guard, organizing the marches and strikes on their enemies. The most numerous, they’re often kept in check by not being able to move without the proper intelligence on where and what the threat is.

                                                        The Patrol watch for signs of the royals and other supernatural beings trying to gain control over the people; people acting oddly, non-sensical police crackdowns, and new trends in the local politicians’ actions.

                                                        Status

                                                        In the Guard, the ability to fight for the people against the paranormal and supernatural is an easy way to earn status, but even more vital is changing things for the better. Marching to change laws and social norms, dragging people kicking and screaming into the modern age, that wins points in the Guard’s eyes.

                                                        Status 1: You’re new, you’re angry, and you’re ready to march. Not only do you have the ability to throw in for Vox Populi, you also gain a dot of Allies in the People’s Guard.

                                                        Status 3: You’re a little longer in the tooth, but you haven’t lost your passion. You’re more an organizer now, planning marches and meeting with possible allies to accomplish the same goals. This means you have an ear to the streets, with three dots of Contacts in The Street, The Press, and The Poor.

                                                        Status 5: History is a cycle, and you’re experienced enough to help with the latest round. This has earned you not just allies, but friends. You gain tree dots in the Support Network merit.
