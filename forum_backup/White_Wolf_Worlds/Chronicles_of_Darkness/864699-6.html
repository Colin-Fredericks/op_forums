                                                        Originally posted by WHW View Post
                                                        Any particular reason why Ghosts outside of the Underworld may not progress beyond Rank 2? I assume that it's something Geist related; if I don't own Geist, don't play on owning and incorporating it into my games, is there anything that will stop working if this limitation is lifted?
                                                        Ghosts are effectively Underworld natives distracted from their "natural" roles by business in the land of the living.

                                                        A lot of Ghost and Underworld Stuff is implicitly in an unclear level of flux where it hasn't just plain not been covered, but assuming that elements which fit with larger trends in setting metaphysics will carry over into 2e, more powerful ghosts are more likely to have lost interest in or lost influence over the mortal realm, in addition to often having gotten too weird to be recognizably the people they resemble.

                                                        That last part arguably has the biggest impact on removing the hobble; when things that run on memory get weirder without having to spend time in weirder parts of the cosmology, something has to be done to account for the state of ghostly Twilight. The Underworld is where Ghost Stuff gets really otherworldly in the existing setup and if you let ghosts reach that point without extended leave of absence you need an explanation for how and why it happens.

                                                            Hmmm. We don't own any Underworld-mapping product (it would be Geist and Book of the Dead, right?), so we might shift the Ghost Focus from Underworld to material world, simply due to the lack of materials (and the fact that we don't play in games that do a lot of world-jumping).
                                                            So I guess that Ghosts that would evolve past Rank 2 would become more and more removed from their original form, and would have to find more and more twisted ways to feed, being less and less sustainable by being remembered as their living counterpart? I kinda like where it might go. What kind of feeding methods would you suggest for these grotesque twisted ghosts?
                                                            I mean, we could just leave them capped at Rank 2, but that feels like a waste of potential due to the fact that Underworld probably won't be a thing.

                                                            EDIT
                                                            Hm. Ghosts require 1 Essence per day to function, and regain 1 Essence by being near their Anchors. So they get just enough to exist if they never spend anything.
                                                            They can regain it by either being remembered and recognized, or by eating other Ghosts. First one isn't really what we are aiming at, and second one is hard, unless you are anchored to a place with a lot of other Ghosts; then you eat their Essence, let them hibernate for two days, and eat them again. Unless you beat up their Corpus, they won't become permanently destroyed.
                                                            Huh. So pretty much, your best option without adding another feeding method is to lure mortals into your lair, traumatize them to death in order to make them spawn a Ghost, then proceed to create your own farm of Ghosts using the above method.
