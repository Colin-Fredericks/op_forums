        Originally posted by Suleri Drals View Post
        That's interresting, could you elaborate on the ties between Arthurian times and Vampire?
        Sure. Though you don't just have to repost it there sarcastically, they can just read the reasons themselves. Or not.

        Actual links mostly revolve around the Holy Grail and the Bron bloodline, kings of Britain from this time period, cursed to be without kingdom until they find the Holy Grail. Said Holy Grail has always been a link to immortality (in case you need this in easy mode vampires are immortal), as well as legended as being full of Christ's blood, which probably isn't for everyone but something even the Ordo Dracul has been said to have interest in, not counting the obvious Lance connection. There's even the possibility the Grail legend refers to one of the mythological welsh cauldrons, something that comes up with straight Arthurian legend but that goes with the muddled history part along with the conflicts of Arthur and Bran the Blessed, different versions of what might be the same character.

        As for the themes, you have a land where society is collapsing and its people are constantly under attack. Picts, Scots, Jutes, Saxons, Angles, all preying on the area who lack Roman protection. 'No matter how callous you become, you'll need to move among them.' But how do you keep your Humanity, even if it's a sham, when they're living in these situations? It might fit for a wraith-like nosferatu who feasts on the dying, but the Lords and Serpents strive for more, practically need people, and so need to make a choice to do something in the era. Piety versus Blasphemy should be obvious, even with the West becoming heavily Christian you have them also pointing at neighbors with different practices of the same religion and calling them anathema, and that's before you get to the people who've hearkened back to the 'old ways' (and the older ways) with the retreat of the Romans. Old and New should be obvious, but in case not, this is a point where everything has changed. The Romans were building an Empire, the British were part of it. Now it's gone and all that's left is squabbling, chaotic factions. Remnants of the Empire might try to keep building while enemies might try to eradicate every part, but it's up to the vampires of that time period to deal with what's going on.

        Basically, the time period is the kind of thing that made Vampire Dark Ages popular.

        Vampires indeed meddle with history but that sounds very unspecific to me. (I mean, you could say the same about any era)
        The time period is called 'the darkest of the Dark Ages' due to the lack of historical records. Most of the stories out of the place can be tracked to events that happened elsewhere in different times. That's not even counting the obfuscation of the Romance cycle, and its thematic similarities to Vampire. If you look through the Ventrue and Mekhet clanbooks, though others dip into it, you'll see how this is one method they use to erase things they don't like. For instance what the Mekhet did to Akhenaten. And seeing as how Changeling and Hunter were already picked for the era, I thought Vampire would round them out nicely. I mentioned this during the poll, actually, if you care to look back and read further opinions on it. Though I didn't know which two lines would have been picked at that point, I thought it was going to be Changeling and Mage. The developers said they wanted more of an even spread of gamelines, however, and everyone already picked Mage for Pirates.

                                                        Pros:
                                                        • Great War/Western Front - Geist/ Werewolf/ Promethean - looks great and fitting! The only Era expansion I do not have anything to add, in a good way.
                                                        • Necropolis of Hawara – Promethean /Beast /Werewolf - looks unexpected. I like lines combo, only it does not in any way looks for me as 'Necropolis', not going with theme. But I still like looking on 'prehistorical' Promethean, Beast and Werewolf. It's just weird look to see it in this place and time.
                                                        • Golden Age of Sci Fi - Deviant / Werewolf - Deviant works great, no Promethean, but was sold on Werewolf part earlier to this, so okay.
                                                        Cons:
                                                        • King Arthur’s Britannia – Hunter/Changeling /Vampire - No Mage is BIG let down. As this part of Era will show up in Tome of Pentacle it's now a bit lower, but I'm still depressed on it. On longer thinking, I may use Vampire for my Vikings Werewolf game ( I already use crossover material with VtR ), but it still seems as really weird choosing. Hunter and Changeling parts I was already all in. But it still hurts we will not see Merlin the Mage in Arthur Times...
                                                        • Wild West - Changeling/Mage - This is bonkers! We do not have Mage in Arthur, but we got them in Wild West?! We got Nameless War parts in Reconstruction Beast era - Wild West do not seem any how more fitting to it. What will Diamond do in this times? It's 200 years after Europeans entering Americas - it will be mostly everything converted to Diamond, with few Nameless in Native American populations - that's it. Nothing new Mysteries - everything will be stolen to this time, already - nothing very big on social changes. I really do not see any more to do for mages in those time - beside some 'we have lone shamans' of Nameless, Diamond will not have anything to do in it - or worse, we will have very hard stereotyping - Arrows do not have wars to fight as they already are all convertered.
