    [2E] Vampires Nightmare vs werewolves Kuruth Triggers

    08-04-2017, 02:21 AM
    It's crossover question, so I putted it in general CoD forum. I have story about Tribes of Moon werewolves uncovering Nosferatu vampires infested monastery. When looking over Disciplines of Nosferatu, I uncovered that vampiric power can make target thinks he's greatest fears are coming true ( just see Face of the Beast, The Grand Delusion or Waking Nightmare ). Those things for Uratha are mostly their Kuruth Triggers - probably even those most specific ones. Can vampires Nightmare Discipline trigger Kuruth in werewolves target?

          No, Nightmare would not trigger Kuruth for two reasons. Nightmare triggers fear only, not anger. Death Rage is, well, rage. Nightmare does not, at the base level, cannot trigger the emotions to cause a rage of any kind. That's not what it does.

          The second reason? Kuruth has some very specific triggers - Spending too long in Gauru. Taking Gauru outside of combat. Taking damage from silver. Forced into Kuruth by an Elodoth. One category of the player's choice, listed on page 103: Moon, Other, Pack, Territory, Wounds. Of the personal trigger options, only Other really could possibly count as a trigger here (facing a non-mortal, non-werewolf), and that's the result of knowing you're facing a vampire, which has nothing to do with the Discipline. Hells, given the nature of Disciplines, the werewolf can't even realize he's faced with an Other at this point if he didn't already (Wolves and vamps tend to recognize each other on sight, but Nosferatu tend to have Obfuscate and hide themselves). Anyways, my point is that the Other Trigger (if that was taken) should have happened a lot earlier or after the Discipline wears off and the werewolf puts two and two together, depending on the presence of Obfuscate or not.

          In short, there is nothing about the nature of Kuruth that should go off from being hit by Nightmare, and there is no reason for Nightmare to trigger a Rage.

          Disclaimer - I'm talking about Werewolf 2e. Werewolf 1e had a "fear rage" that just involved running away, which is appropriate. 2e does not.

                  Originally posted by MCN View Post
                  In short, there is nothing about the nature of Kuruth that should go off from being hit by Nightmare, and there is no reason for Nightmare to trigger a Rage.
                  While I agree that triggers are not fear-based, it's worth noting that the Nightmare discipline is not just 'feel fear.'

                  Take a note of Waking Nightmare, which is fear-tainted, but otherwise just an illusion.

                  'This terrible hallucination manifests to all senses, though it generally can’t change more than a single person, object, or feature of the location. She could invert the colors in a building, create a shambling monster that stalks her victims, or give herself a nightmarish appearance. The Storyteller should work with the player to determine how big a change the vampire can make. Once created, the Nosferatu can control how the vision reacts while it remains in her presence; once she has separated from her victims, her creation takes on a life of its own.'

                  This is the Exceptional Success condition, and it would be understandable if the standard Success was assumed not to 'manifest to all senses.' But the vampire would effectively be making an illusion of something to the werewolf. I don't believe you would be able to trick a werewolf into believing they've been struck by silver with it, but one of the triggers is seeing your auspice moon in the sky, or seeing a werewolf you don't know in your territory. These would require specific information from the vampire which would be hard to find, except maybe with Auspex, but I think a vampire could conceivably trigger a werewolf if the Uratha had the right triggers, the vampire had the right Disciplines and knew of those triggers.
