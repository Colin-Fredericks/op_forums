                            Originally posted by Raistlin View Post
                            a) If the power comes from the Leviathan himself, why does he need a sacrifice?
                            It's all part of a ritual framework. The power originates entirely from the Leviathan, but it's a brutal mostly directionless power - as manifested by the channels.
