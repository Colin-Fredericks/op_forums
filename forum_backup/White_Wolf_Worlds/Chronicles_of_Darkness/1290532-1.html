    CoD to Storypath

    Weapon Maximum Range Bands

    Close (2m | 5ft)
    • Close Combat Skill | +1 DR @ Close Range
    • Unarmed
    • Melee & Reach

    Short (30m | 100ft)
    • Aim/Firearms or Athletics Skill | +1 DR @ Close Range
    • Civilian Flame Throwers
    • Shotguns, Sawed Off Shotgun
    • Sonic Pistols, Taser Pistols, Web Emitter
    • Plasma Pistol, Plasma Rifle, Plasma Thrower Heavy Weapon
    • Thrown Weapon (Grenade)
    • Hand Crossbow

    Medium (100m | 300ft)
    • Aim/Firearms Skill | +1 DR @ Close Range
    • Pistol, Revolver, Slug Thrower
    • Submachine Gun
    • Grenade Launcher (Only +1 DR @ Long)
    • Military Flame Thrower
    • Flechette Pistol, Flechette Carbine
    • Mini Laser Guantlet
    • Heavy Plasma Weapon
    • Short Bow, Light Crosbow
    • Longbow, Heavy Crossbow (Only +1 DR @ Long)

    Long (1000m | 3000ft)
    • Aim/Firearms Skill | +1 DR @ Close & Short Range
    • Assault Rifle
    • Rifles, Slug Thrower Rifle
    • Laser Carbine
    • Heavy Coilgun
    • Light Artillery
    • Laser Guantlet

    Extreme (1001m+, 3001ft+)
    • Heavy Weapons (Embanked Weapons)
    • Vehicle Weapons

    Out of Range
    • +2 DR per range band beyond the weapons maximum.

        CoD to Storypath

        Please note that I have never found a grapple system that I enjoy as both a player and GM. They range from D&D 3.5 & EX3 (Overly complicated), to D&D 5.0 (Vanilla Bland), to Trinity 1E & CoD (Almost there but whats with changing initiative – oh my head hurts). So here is hoping that Storypath becomes the one! Does anyone have any grapple systems that they really enjoy.


        GRAPPLE


        Declare a Grapple Maneuver (Instant)
        • See Grapple Maneuvers below

        Start a Grapple (+1DR)
        • Usually Dex + Close Combat vs Defense
        • One hand free is required
        • Extra successes add to the Control Roll

        Opposed Control Roll
        • Str + Close Combat
        • Active Player (Attacker)
          • Wins (Maneuver success with extra successes)
          • Ties (Both grapplers struggle with no result)
          • Loses (Defender escapes reflexively on his next turn)

        Grappling Condition
        • While grappling both characters suffer …
          • Defense -2 vs other attackers
          • Cannot use Mixed Actions
          • Weapon attacks in a grapple suffer size as a penalty (CoD Size) ??
          • No movement without the drag maneuver
          • External attacks may force a new control roll



        Grapple (Continue a grapple)
        • Break Free, Escape, Release (reflexive)
        • Control Weapon or Object
          • Draw a weapon or gain control of an opponents
        • Damage/Savage with…
          • Bite, Claws, or Natural Weapons
          • Bite & Feed (Vampires)
          • Melee Weapons (Weapon Size as a penalty)
          • Ranged Firearms (Weapon Size as a penalty)
          • Unarmed Attacks
        • Disarm Weapon or Object
          • Must success at control weapon first
        • Drag +1DR
        • Hold in Place (Neither may apply Defense vs incoming attacks)
          • Choke Hold (Merit)
          • Restrain/Immobilize +1DR (Tilt)
            • Apply Cuffs/Manacles +1 DR (Control + s>Str)
        • Hold at Bay
          • Gun to head, hand over mouth, knife at neck
          • Take Cover/Human Shield (Breaking Point)
        • Reverse Grapple (Reflexive)
        • Throw/Slam/Drop
          • Drop Prone (both go prone, no damage, grapple ends)
          • Slam (+1DR, 1B/L per success, grapple ends)
          • Throw (+1DR, 1B/L per success, grapple ends, close range)

        Roleplaying not Rollplaying or Ruleplaying

            CoD to Storypath

            COMBAT MANEUVERS (Simple Action)
            • Aim
              • +1e
              • No mixed, no move, no burstfire
              • If you apply Defense you loose Aim
            • All-Out Attack
              • Defense = 1 no bump
              • +1e attack
              • No mixed
            • Bite by a non-human Creature with Natural Weapons
              • Example sharks, wolves, ect.
              • Mortals, vampire, humanoids, must grapple to bite
            • Burstfire (Autofire)
              • Single Shot (+0e, 1 target, 1 ammo)
              • Short (+1e, 1 target, 3 ammo)
              • Medium (+1e, 1-3 targets, 10 ammo)
              • Long (+2e, many targets, 20 ammo)
              • If multiple targets Complication = Medium +1DR, Long +2DR
              • No penalty vs mobs or battle groups
            • Charge
              • Defense = 1 no bump
              • x2 Move
              • Attack
              • No Mixed
            • Coverfire
              • Medium and Long Burst, CoD 90, too much text
            • Dash/Double Move
            • Defend Other
              • Defender applies Defense in place of wards
            • Disengage (Opposed Athletics)
            • Distract
            • Dodge
            • Draw, Change, Pickup, Sheath, Sling, Stow
              • May begin with weapon unless ambushed
            • Drop Prone (Reaction or Action)
              • Automatically fail disengage, withdraw contests
            • Feint
            • Full Defense
              • +2 Def, No Flurry
            • Gambit (No Flurry)
              • Break Shield
              • Break Weapon
              • Disarm with Brawl (DR=Targets Close Combat Skill)
              • Disarm with Weapon (DR=Targets Close Combat Skill)
              • Disarm with Firearms (DR=Targets Close Combat Skill or object size)
              • Grapple 1s
              • Knockdown/Takedown 1s
              • Sweep
              • Tackle +1DR /+1 DMG
            • Killing Blow
              • Inflict the taken out (death on a lower tier)
              • Avoids most mundane armor
              • Breaking point
            • Misc Action (-1 Def)
            • Movement Maneuver
              • Acrobatics, Climbing, Jumping
            • Pulling Blow, Pommel, Flat, Hilt
            • Reload
            • Rise from Prone/Stand
              • Opposed Athletics vs Close Combat
            • Rush
              • Opposed Athletics from Short Range
            • Stealth
              • No Flurry
              • If Move DR+2
              • Opposed Stealth vs Awareness or Perception
            • Take Cover (Reaction or Action)
              • Light (Defense +1, Attack from +0c)
              • Medium (Defense +2, Attack from +1c)
              • Heavy (Defense +3, Attack from +2c)
              • Full (No Line of Sight)
              • Cover does not suffer Onslaught Penalty
            • Withdraw (Complex)
              • Athletics
              • EX3 = DR1, Goal 10, Each Round

                        Storypath is 'sister' (?) game engine to Storytelling System, on what Chronicles of Darkness run. The both share a lot of similarities to that extent that I treat Storypath as as 'sorta 3E' of Storytelling System.

                        Storypath is Onyx Path Publishing in-house system, so they are not restricted in usage of it as much as they are with Storytelling engine ( that is, de facto, owned by White Wolf ). Games ( and their forums ) that run on Storypath - Trinity Continuum ( various S-F ), Scion ( you play descendent of god ), They Came From Beneath the Sea ( 1950s comedic horror ) and Dystopia Rising: Evolution ( post-app with supermutants and zombies ).

                        Here is Storypath preview - https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product...System-Preview

                        This reddit came in depth about similarities and differences between two game systems - ​https://www.reddit.com/r/rpg/comment...rypath_system/

                                    For example I plan on using something similiar to Psion Power activation dice.

                                    Spellcasting Roll = (Gnosis/2 RndUp) + 1 + Arcana

                                    This sets a range of 2-6 dice similiar to Psi score plus Mode (Arcana).

                                    So Gnosis
                                    01 = 2d + Arcana dice
                                    02 = 2d + Arcana dice
                                    03 = 3d + Arcana dice
                                    04 = 3d + Arcana dice
                                    05 = 4d + Arcana dice

                                    Tier 3 (Target Number 7, Max Attribute 6)
                                    06 = 4d + Arcana dice
                                    07 = 5d + Arcana dice
                                    08 = 5d + Arcana dice

                                    Tier 3 (Target Number 7, Max Attribute 7)
                                    09 = 6d + Arcana dice
                                    10 = 6d + Arcana dice

                                    Yantras will be enhancements

                                    I am considering the same thing for VtR and WtF

                                    Disciplines
                                    (Blood Potency / 2 RndUp) + 1 + Discipline Dice

                                    Gifts
                                    (Primal Urge/ 2 RndUp) + 1 + Aspect or Gift Dice

                                    This would be a method similiar to Trinity or Mage.

                                    I could also use a method more similiar to Scion or Exalted, Werewolf with Attribute and Skill dropping the third set of dice from the Supernatural Stat (Gnosis, Primal Urge, Blood Potency). I like this version less.

                                            Aptitudes are themes or categories of powers like Biokinesis, Electrokinesis, Psychokinesis, Telepathy, Vitakiensis, etc

                                            Each aptitude has 3 modes, each focused on a specific aspect of the aptitude. In first edition pychokinesis had Cryokinesis (cold), Pyrokinesis (heat) , and Telekinesis (force). Modes are from 1-5 maybe 1-6

                                            Psi Rating is your overall strength in Psychic power (the closest comparison would be the CoD supernatural stats like Blood Potency, Gnosis, Primal Urge)? PSI ranges from 2-7.

                                                    Just wanted to post an update. After many months of long hours and real life work I was able to return and focus on this project a bit.

                                                    Please see the linked files.

                                                    First Storypath Vampire the Requiem Character
                                                    Storypath Character Creation with Splat Templates

                                                    Both files are rough drafts and works in progress.
                                                    There are items not let set, items being waffled over, so on and so forth.
                                                    Integrity, Humanity, Harmony, Wisdom I am not ready to discard them and need to Storypath them.
                                                    Your feedback is welcome.

                                                    Several of my design and modification goals are:
                                                    * One storypath character creation process only altered in the template section by the template and/or setting.
                                                    - For example if Scions are more powerful than this can be boosted in the template section.
                                                    * One storypath ruleset across all splats/setting, example Scale would be the same but used less or more in different settings.
                                                    * Edges are available in all settings
                                                    * A system that cross back and forth between CoD (VtR,MtA, CtL, WtF), EX3 and Storypath,
                                                    * Keep modify only the things from the non-Storypath systems/setting that feel absolutely needed.
