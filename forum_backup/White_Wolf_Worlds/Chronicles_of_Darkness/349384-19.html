The TARDIS could be represented mechanically as an epic Mobile Hollow, but it is a thinking being that might not be completely fae. He understands humans better than other True Fae, but doesn't have Clarity and can still be fickle and proud jerk.

        2) A Demon, like you said. The TARDIS would be a Lares (i.e. Exiled Angel with a building as a Cover).

        3) An Inspired Mane, as described in Genius: The Transgression. Gallifrey might be a Bardo.

                            I was going to post a full write-up for Saitama from One Punch Man as a Horror with Potency 10, but the more I thought about it, the more I realized that was just plain unnecessary. Instead, I'll use the Brief Nightmare system, but he'll be a Cheater (or "Arch-Cheater", rather) instead of a Horror.

                            Virtue: Humble/Determined
                            Vice: Ennui

                            Hack: Punching 15
                            Best At: Super-Heroics 25 (Increased by Hack)
                            Worst At: 2 (Being Proactive, Academic Pursuits, Not Looking Like A Stick Figure)
                            All Other Pools: 5 or 20 (If combined with Hack)
                            Aspirations: Be A Hero For Fun, Do The Right Thing
                            Willpower/Scene: 25 (Resolve 6 + Composure 4 + Hack)
                            Initiative: 15
                            Defense: 20
                            Speed: Effectively Limitless
                            Size: 5
                            Health: 35
                            Integrity: 9
                            Weapons/Attacks
                            Punching [Counts as supernatural damage and forces lesser opponents to use Down & Dirty Combat, 25 Dice with 8 Again, Bashing or Lethal]
                            Serious Series [Special: See Below]

                            Dread Powers: Colossus*, Hideous Strength*, Numen (Stutter Step), Prodigious Leap, Reality Stutter, Swift, Unbreakable, Unnatural Healing*

                            Colossus:                             As per the Idigam version. Saitama gains a General Armor equal to his Best Dice Pool, counts his Size twice for his Health and his Punching receives 8 Again against lesser opponents. Should an opponent get 5+ Successes against Saitama, he receives the full damage, but reduces it by his General Armor. Should the opponent get 4 Successes or less, Saitama only receives one damage of the appropriate type due to being Unbreakable.

                            Hideous Strength: By spending a point of Willpower, Saitama can increase his Strength by his Hack until the end of the scene. Furthermore, he adds his Hack as automatic successes on Strength + Stamina rolls made to perform a feat of strength, such as lifting a massive object or breaking free of restraints.

                            Unnatural Healing: Saitama heals all bashing and lethal damage at the start of each turn. Downgrade a number of damage from any aggravated attack into lethal equal to his Best Dice Pool (this stacks with Colossal and Unbreakable), be the source mundane or supernatural.

                            Serious Series: The stats above are for when Saitama isn't making any effort. When he does, he is considered to have a Supernal Scaling (Mage Chronicler's Guide, page 218). That is, add 16 dice to his Best Dice Pool for a total of 41 with 8 Again. Take the total amount of Successes and multiply them by whatever arbitrarily gigantic number you want, and go full anime with your descriptions of the ensuing destruction.

                                Ok, serious question: would the Doctor work as an antagonist in a Demon game? Not necessarily out to harm anyone, mind you. I mean something closer to a mobile natural disaster that you have to contain somehow. He mostly uses Lares!TARDIS to go on adventures, and even then he causes a lot of collateral damage. What if he had goals in mind for his Hell and made little effort to clean up his temporal messes? You'd have Demons and Angels alike trying to predict his next appearance and sabotage his plans, perhaps stealing the TARDIS and/or sending him to be reprogrammed into an Angel (or more likely just recycled).

                                Some of the beings that look for the Doctor could be time-traveling Angels, outer space Cryptids and weirder things still, like Bardos, free-roaming Idigam and Supernal/Abyssal Verges. This more proactive but ruthless Doctor would have good reason to keep hidden and on the move. Heck, you might need a multi-splat team of experts in wibbly-wobbly matters to track him down, and that's without going into all the contingencies and backup plans he definitely has. All the while trying not to make the time-line worse, and being tempted to explore.

                                Does that sound fun?

                                By the way, did anyone else think of the Cybermen when they saw the Dalga in GMC? As for the Daleks, I don't think they are Angels, or even created by the G-M. Maintaining the status quo is obviously not compatible with the extinction of all non-Daleks.

                                EDIT: On second thought, I was probably thinking of the Master. I still think it would be interesting to tell the story from the point of view of those who are caught in the Doctor's mishaps and try to stop him from causing more trouble.

                                            I've been thinking of ways to port GARO.

                                            For those who aren't familiar with the series, it centers around an order of magic using knights hunting down demons and the possessed in a modern day setting.

                                            My first and most obvious idea is would be that the Makai Knights are a Hunter Conspiracy, focusing on dealing with spirits and the claimed.

                                            A Werewolf lodge might work. There is a distinct wolf theme to the Makai Knights, most prominently in the design of their armor. Which combined with their focus on banishing demons, killing the possessed, and sealing gates to the demon realm, lends itself rather well to a Werewolf adaption.
