                                My group of mages has stumbled onto a pylon of Seers that I've taken from the Sanctum & Sigil book, starting around p137. If you have it, great. If not, here's the gist. Hedonistic Satanic cult leader William Harris was a mage and didn't know it, seeing his powers through his own lens for a while before the Seers actually got to and recruited him. He's more open minded about the Supernal world, but he's reconciled the Exarchs so that they fit into his belief system and has gathered other broken, mostly like-minded Seers to his cult and manor. They're styled after the Four Horsemen, with him being the architect of the Apocalypse, guided by the dreams given to him by the Exarch-Gods. The 'Apocalypse' is smaller scale than the word would suggest and he believes the goal is to wipe the city of all opposing mages. Still an impressive act if he pulls it off. The Horsemen are Death, a six-year old Awakened girl obsessed with, naturally, Death for whom people are as expendable as dolls and who has already killed her parents by animating the family pet they had put down. Pestilence is an introverted and agoraphobic teenage boy, I'd think the pestilence is more of the mind considering his outlook, and he's the Judas in the group, actively plotting betrayal while the others are willing to follow Harris until they see a better option. War is an ex-Adamantine Arrow who was tired of being used by his Order and decided to wage war against them, he's got a penchant for animating suits of armor he's fitted to be especially good as 'grunts' in combat. Famine was a repeated victim of abuse who hungers for dominance and strength and meditates in states of extreme submission. They keep a girl prisoner in the basement, switching her out when the last one dies or gets worn out. They torment the 'guest' with Mind and Life magics, and they use their Profane Urim, an artifact that allows for the entire pylon to use it simultaneously to achieve the psychic domination of people, which they seem to use for practical and 'entertainment' purposes.

                                Infodump over.

                                It's as ugly a place as its owners, I've styled it visually a bit after the Resident Evil mansion. A place built by extravagant wealth but neglected as the people inside turned to their own devices.



                                While working with it I noticed none of the mages had any strong spiritual leanings. Two people had Spirit 1, and I decided to put those dots elsewhere so they could remain entirely ignorant of the spiritual landscape, which has since become a Wound. And where there's a Wound, there's a Maeltinet.

                                So specifically, I'm looking for a Rank 4 Maeltinet, either pure spirit or immundi, that might fit given the atmosphere, events and occupants of the place.

                                    RE: Magath -a - matic

                                    I am looking forward to playing around with this. I haven't really create many Magath, but they are always great to keep the players guessing.

                                    RE: The Ugly Place

                                    I am giving this alot of thought - I am trying to come up with the proper representation of a spirit that would arise in the shadow of such powerful yet thoroughly toxic people.
                                    I am definitely going with Maeltinet/Immundi, so much so I had to take a stab at revising some the Inferno mechanics for 2nd Edition. Since it might a bit before I can put more energy into this, here are my notes so far. Feedback welcome as always.

                                    Here are the broad notes. I put down where Infernal Entity (since the word "Demon" is somewhat worn out by now) differs from baseline Ephemeral rules.

                                    Infernal Entities (Diaboli &Dominions)
                                    Rank: Dominions run the full range of Ranks. Ephemeral Entities that become Diaboli retain their Rank. Corrupted Ghosts become Larvals, Corrupted Goetia become Deceptors and Corrupted Spirits become Immundi.
                                    Native: Dominions are native to a ream called the Inferno (or simply “Hell”). Corrupted Ephemeral entities retain their ties to their original realms (if any).
                                    Virtue/Vice: Diaboli and Dominions don’t have a Virtue or Vice. Instead, they have a Malpraxis; typically consisting of a sinful act and the circumstances of when, where, or how it is indulged. Diaboli and Dominions recover Willpower when someone else indulges in behavior that aligns with their Malpraxis.
                                    Language: Diaboli and Dominions understand all languages, including dead languages or languages used by other supernatural entities. In addition, Diaboli and Dominions speak “Dragons Tongue”; a twisted glossolalia that is spoken and understood by the Infernal Entities, but cannot otherwise be translated or deciphered.
                                    Integrity: Diaboli and Dominions don’t have an Integrity trait.
                                    Influences: Diaboli and Dominions have Influences that relate to their Malpraxis, with Diaboli often retain influences they possessed before their corruption.
                                    Essence: Infernal Entities require the Corrupt Condition in order to regain Essence and prevent Essence Bleed. Depending on the nature of the entity, they may also be able to make use of the Anchor or Resonant Condition. Diabolists or Cultists sacrificing precious resources (metaphorical or literal), animals, or even humans to the Infernal Entity in its presence allow it to regain the Resources value of the item or animal, or the current Integrity of a human sacrifice, as Essence.
                                    Secrets & Lies: No supernatural ability can reveal a Diaboli or Dominions lies, half-truths and other deceptions for what they are. Anyone using the power will see the result the Infernal Entity wants them to see. Likewise, no supernatural ability can read the mind or intrude upon the thoughts of an Infernal Entity.
                                    Soul of Sin: Infernal Entities have the Know Soul Numina (the same as the Dread Power).
                                    Below are some supporting Manifestations, Conditions and Numina.

                                    Corrupt (Condition)
                                    Description: The subject of this condition is within the sphere of Influence of an Infernal Entity.
                                    Causing the Condition: Corruption can be caused by the extended efforts of Diabolists and unwitting pawns, or by the direct involvement of Infernal Entities. It can also occur naturally, typically in places where acts of evil have been committed routinely or where particularly heinous events have transpired. Powerful, high ranking Infernal Entities can use Influence to instill this Condition.
                                    Ending the Condition: Destroying the corrupt object or place can End this condition. Prolonged efforts to cleanse the affected place or supernatural abilities to alter resonance can also end the Condition. Finally, abjuration and exorcism may temporarily suppress the Condition.

                                    Whisperer (Condition)
                                    Description: The entity has secured itself to an object or creature. As long as it remains a Whisperer, the entity is safe from Essence Bleed. While this Condition is applied, the entity exists in twilight near the target, but is wholly insubstantial and invisible and cannot interact with other entities in Twilight without the use of additional supernatural powers. The entity gains access to the Adjust Reality and Sign Numina, even if they would normally not have these abilities. Besides these two, the entity cannot use any other Numina or Influence on the target and cannot use any other Manifestation Effects, but may communicate directly with the targets mind and influence the targets dreams.
                                    Causing the Condition: This Condition is created by an entity using the Whisperer Manifestation Effect.
                                    Ending the Condition: The entity can voluntarily end the Condition at any time, but doing so discorporates the entity with all the normal consequences. Whisperers are also easier to banish, and all abjuration and exorcism rolls against it receive a +2 bonus.

                                    Whisperer (Manifestation)
                                    This manifestation is common to Infernal Entities. With it they can slowly lure a targeted individual into sin and depravity. This is similar to the Fettered Manifestation, but does not require the Open Condition. By spending 2 Essence, the entity adds the Whisperer Condition to itself. Beings targeted by this Effect contest the roll with Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. If the Effect is successful, targets gain the Urged Condition.

                                    Numina – Adjust Reality
                                    This Numina allows the entity to subtly shift reality. This typically manifests a streak of luck or sudden opportunity, which the entity can exploit to their benefit. Once per scene, when the entity spends Willpower to add to a dice pool it may reroll a number a failed dice up to its rank. If spending Willpower to bolster Defense, it may force the opponent to reroll successful dice equal to its Rank. Alternatively, the entity can grant an individual other than themselves a die bonus of +2 to a single roll, as they adjust reality to accommodate their whims.

                                                Re: Whispers

                                                I modeled Whispers on the Fettered manifestation largely because when you read the text from Inferno, Whispers behaves like what would become Fettered in CofD. As such one condition goes on the Entity, which alters the Entities capabilities, while urged goes on the Victim, to represent the influence the being is exerting. I admit the way it plays reveals more meta knowledge than you might want, but most of the Condition system involves giving the players access to more meta knowledge, so it didn't seem outside the bounds of the system mechanics. As always, Your Mileage may Vary.

                                                Re: Malpraxis

                                                Re-aligning Malpraxis to 2nd Edition has been challenging. In my current write up, it doesn't have much more mechanical weight than most other Anchors. But in Inferno, a fair amount of word count went into explaining the interplay between Vices and Keys. My alternate idea was to have Vice and Key as separate anchors - recovering a point of Willpower where vice applies, but recovering full Willpower when Vice & Key apply. Any feedback on this would be appreciated.

                                                Re: The Maeltinet of the Manor

                                                Here is the writeup of nofather 's proposal. This uses my first draft of Infernal Entities as my guideline.

                                                Bleakwood
                                                The entity that calls itself “Bleakwood” inhabits the sprawling Harris Estate on the outskirts of town. It is the spirit of the manor that housed generations of the wealthy Harris family, but has corrupted by the depravity of a cabal of sorcerers that has taken up residence on the Estate. The entity has grown powerful on torment and pain inflicted for its own sake, and has grown into a “Maeltinet”, a servant of a greater infernal entity known as “Thurifuge’”.
                                                When Bleakwood manifests, it appears as a giant emaciated corpse with eight arms and four faces. Its body is wrapped in sickly ivy and dead leaves and produces the creaks and groans of a house settling when it moves.

                                                Rank 4[Infernal, Immundi, Maeltinet]
                                                Power 12, Finesse 10, Resistance 12, Willpower 10, Essence 25
                                                Corpus 24, Size 12, Initiative 22, Defense 10, Speed 22
                                                Malpraxis: Sadism committed on the Estate grounds.
                                                Influence: The Estate 3, Torment 3
                                                Ban: Cannot knowingly act against the Harris Family; Bane: Ritually purified bones of a Harris Family member
                                                Manifestations: Fetter, Possess, Materialize, Twilight Form, Unfetter
                                                Numina: Awe, Dement, Essence Thief, Hallucination, Know Soul, Left-Handed Spanner, Telekinesis, Twilight Abduction

                                                Know Soul: As the Dread Power of the same name (CofD, pg.146)

                                                Twilight Abduction: The entity can attempt to drag victims within one yard into an incorporeal state, allowing it to freely interact with the target. Spend three Essence points, and roll the entities’ (Power + Finesse) .The target resists with a roll of (Resolve + Composure + Supernatural Tolerance). If the entity wins, shadows drag the victim and his worn possessions through a sudden imploding tear in space. In an instant, the rift closes, and the victim enters Twilight for one hour per remaining success.

                                                            RE: Malpraxis Notes

                                                            That becomes the tricky part. An Infernal whose Key is a type of Action or Behavior (such as Betrayal or Retribution) is relatively easy to adjudicate.
