        I'm a bit sketchy on some of the choices made here but a lot of it's pretty good.

        I feel like I should contribute more than just... This, but I have to say that Mounted Combat feels like it needs rejiggering. Horses provide a lot of benefits; increased mobility is obvious, sure, but also a height advantage that forces your opponents to have to swing above their shoulders to hit you, greatly diminishing their capacity to do you harm. They also provide a lot of shock and awe.

        I'm also curious if Trunk Squeeze and Choke Hold synergize at all. Their benefits are very similar. Can you get a combined Squeeze Choke maneuver?

        Finally, a comment on the Berserker style that feels sort of emblematic of a common issue I have: The War Cry ability reduces an opponent's dice pools that turn but it costs the character an attack. This means that, if someone was playing a Wild Man style lunatic with this style, instead of charging in like a frenzied bear, foaming at the mouth and shouting incoherently, they pause, and yell, and then attack on their next turn after the effect has ended. This feels like a problem. A Berserker's style should reward them for running into the fray head-long without any sense of self-preservation, not rewarding team tactics which, ultimately, is what this maneuver does.

        ---

        My proposals on how to fix this would be:

        1. Mounted Combat should definitely focus on the motion and the charge, but give us something that makes it a true advantage. Fixed Charge doesn't exactly add a tremendous benefit (it could honestly be argued to be adding a disadvantage). Could we replace it with, say, something that allows for a roll to intimidate defenders? Those who fail either turn and run or lose their defense.

        2. Rearing Beast isn't exactly great. It lets the player risk losing a turn in exchange for a chance to attack with a pool of nine dice. The dice pool isn't bad, but most player characters who are dedicated enough to have four dots in a combat style are probably going to be wielding that anyway. Instead, I recommend creating a Tilt of some sort that's automatically applied to those who try to enter close combat with the cavalier; either they suffer attack penalties or risk being hurt/knocked prone by a thousand pounds of whirling muscle.

        3. I also recommend adding some sort of benefit for attacking fleeing opponents in here somewhere. Light Cavalry - and cavalry in general after the invention of the gun - had the job of chasing down routed opponents. As a build-off of the aforementioned routing ability that could replace Fixed Charge, perhaps some sort of benefit that'd let a cavalier be extra-effective at stabbing folks in the back?

        4. For the Berserker, have their War Cry be a part of an all-out attack (encouraging that method of fighting) and make it cause their opponent to hesitate. A crazed maenad charging at her opponent in the woods should utterly surprise them. Perhaps model it off of Cheap Shot, except allow Presence + Intimidation vs. Resolve + Composure; if the opponent fails, they lose their defense as they hesitate at the sight of a naked madwoman with claws bursting from the bushes with murder in her eyes.

        The Fighting Styles in GMC were more than just little tricks. Someone with five dots in the core Martial Arts style starts out relatively simple but, by the end, they're an always-armed nightmare that can beat down people in riot gear. Marksmanship makes precision shooting exceptionally potent for the right character with the right opportunities. I didn't get these same feelings - these feelings of weight - from a lot of the stuff here and I'm sure I could do a more thorough review later if you wanted more commentary, but I'm looking into a possible medieval game soon and Mounted Combat, Archery, Berserks, etc., are all important to me. Spearmen are too, but that style definitely had more of an understanding of making someone trained in the style a very different opponent than one who merely has the weapon to begin with.

        More sensible minds may differ. I'm still greatly looking forward to Hurt Locker. These are just my thoughts.

                Originally posted by Bunyip View Post

                Okay, just brainstorming here, how's this for potential replacement effect for Dirty Fighter (ignore costs, prerequisites and drawbacks for now):

                Effect: Your character fights rough and uses any underhanded trick to gain advantage. He'll throw sand in his opponent's face, kick below the belt, and beg for surrender to gain the chance to stab his opponent when her guard is down. In combat, the character gains +2 dice to inflict Tilts related to dirty tactics and unfair blows. These include Arm Wrack, Blinded, Deafened, Knocked Down, and Leg Wrack, as well as other Tilts as determined by the Storyteller. Each Tilt can only be applied once per opponent per combat, as she learns to beware the character's tactics.

                Other options I'm tossing up are 'On a successful attack, spend a Willpower point to apply a Tilt', thereby foregoing the need to roll for the Tilt, but adding a cost.
                I really like the idea of making it easier to inflict Tilts wilt Dirty Fighter, especially as it is a logical consequence of fighting dirty. It is important to remember here that Tilts are normally applied by targeted attacks. So this Merit could give bonusses on those targeted attacks, but that would cause a bit of overlap with other Merits that give those bonusses. Unless you decide that with this Merit targeted attacks are not necessary anymore, which is sort of weird.

                Automatically applying tilts for Willpower is also very nice, as it makes the Merit something more than a dice adder. Especially if it counts as some kind of reflexive secondary attack that only inflicts a Tilt and is always successful (if the primary attack is also successful). This way the character could perform a normal attack (or even a targeted attack to inflict a Tilt), and inflict a (possibly second) Tilt for a Willpower without having to worry about making targeted attacks. This would show some kind of mastery of dirty combat.

                                    Originally posted by Axelgear View Post

                                    I'm also curious if Trunk Squeeze and Choke Hold synergize at all. Their benefits are very similar. Can you get a combined Squeeze Choke maneuver?
                                    They're different grappling maneuvers (the Merits unlock them) so no--not unless you score an exceptional success or have access to some other special system that lets you apply them both.

                                    Now the funny thing is that in the real world this represents a body triangle, from which a rear naked choke is the most common submission. This reflects the real-world application, which generally softens someone up for a different submission.
