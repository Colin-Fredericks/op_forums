                                            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                                            Iceborn
                                            "Don't worry. A little cold isn't going to stop me"

                                            Taken by Keepers who made their homes in realms of endless winter, Iceborn were kept as sentries who roamed under the cover of blizzards. Their touch is imbued with biting cold, and many intruders who crossed their paths more often than not ended up as stiff corpses. Yet while most were numbed by the cold they now embodied, some manage to flee when memories of a warmer time thawed their hearts and urged them to flee.

                                            • Beast: A white wolf whose howl echoes with those of the winter winds.
                                            • Elemental: A living snowman with glistening patterns of ice instead of veins.
                                            • Fairest: A platinum-haired woman garbed in a translucent blue gown.
                                            • Grimm: Snowflakes decorate his flesh, their kaleidoscopic forms shaping lines of text which speak of cold, lonely tales.

                                            Blessing: Iceborn are immune to any penalties from cold weather, and can walk on ice without difficulty. If they spend a point of Glamour in a scene, they may cause anyone they touch to suffer the Extreme Cold Tilt at a starting penalty equal to their Wyrd. This Tilt lasts for a number of hours equal to successes on a Resolve + Occult roll. And if the victim is already suffering the Tilt, he adds to its existing penalty, and takes any dots in excess of 5 as lethal damage.
                                            This is awesome. I like this kith a lot. I do think that the blessing could use a little tweaking, though. First, the ability to inflict such a potent Tilt seems like it should either cost a point of Glamour for each person touched (if the character is playing freeze tag with her enemies, and the Tilt will linger), or one point of Glamour to create an area of effect with the Tilt centered around the iceborn (affecting all within the range, but easily escaped by fleeing the presence of the iceborn), rather than one point of Glamour to play freeze tag for the duration of the scene. If going the route with the lingering Tilt, there also needs to be some thought given as to what will happen if the victim tries to warm up to end the Tilt. I'd also say that, given how quickly the effective Wyrd could exceed 5 with Potent Kith, that you may want to consider the starting penalty of the Tilt to be half Wyrd rather than full Wyrd. As the blessing currently stands, an especially potent iceborn could walk into a crowded place, spend a point of Glamour, and then go on to slay every normal human that could be caught with a single touch.

                                                Okay, here is the revised shepherd kith. Thank you to everyone who gave me name suggestions for this kith. In fact, the purifier name has made me begin toying with an idea for a new kith, tentatively called a hallowheart. We'll see how that goes.


                                                Shepherd

                                                My friends, we cannot simply hide here and wait for mad gods to snatch us away again! Take up your arms, and let us drive this Huntsman from our door!

                                                The Others are not often religious beings, but on occasion, the trappings of religion suit their purposes, either to play at spirituality or to instill such feelings in others. The shepherds were often extremely religious before they were taken to act as the Gentry's holy men and women. A shepherd may have acted as a spiritual adviser, herding her Keeper's other servants with her words, or she may have been sent out into the world to form bizarre cults in her Keeper's honor. After escaping, most struggle to reconcile their experiences in Arcadia with the faith that they had held before.

                                                Beast: The horned shaman smells of smoke and speaks with a gravelly voice, and there is a vicious scar upon his throat.
                                                Elemental: A fire and brimstone preacher composed of actual fire and brimstone.
                                                Grimm: A handsome man clad in ritual robes sown from the pages of assorted holy texts from around the world.
                                                Ogre: A savage one-eyed monk with blue skin and a charming voice.
                                                Wizened: A smiling, squat little man with countless holy relics hung from straps of leather that cross and penetrate his skin.

                                                Blessing: Shepherds were intended to move large groups of people to act in the favor of their Keepers. Each shepherd gains the Inspiring Merit free of cost, even if she lacks the prerequisite. When using this Merit, instead of the usual penalties, she suffers a -3 penalty to the roll for a single listener, a -2 penalty for a small clique, a -1 penalty for a small crowd, and no penalty for a large crowd. When utilizing the trappings of a religion that the majority of listeners believe in, the shepherd may add her Wyrd to this roll. Additionally, by spending a point of Glamour, any listeners who have gained the Inspired Condition from the shepherd's speech temporarily gains either the Virtuous or Vice-Ridden Merit with a Virtue or Vice of the shepherd's choice. The temporary Merit persists until it is used to regain Willpower or for a number of days equal to the shepherd's Wyrd, whichever comes first. All affected listeners receive the same Virtue or Vice.
