                                                      OK, I finished the Ghost in the Machine Atavism. It's heavily based on Demon's Data Form, but I like it. It might be a bit OP though.

                                                      Ghost in the Machine (Ugallu)

                                                      Not all Horrors are mythical monsters, born from some distant Dark Mother. Some are new monsters , created by the very creatures that they prey upon. These Horrors do not dwell in dark caves beyond the sight of man, but in the very systems that mankind has created to help them.

                                                      Normal: The Beast is a creature of data and, as like calls to like, they can manipulate data with no need for an intermediary. The Beast cannot physically enter a data system, but can do so mentally. If the Beast can touch some method of inputting data, his mind can enter the system with a successful
                                                      Resolve + Computers roll.

                                                      While existing as data, the Beast can access any device on the network that she occupies; although she is still subject to issues of firewalls, aniviruses and the like (which may necessitate rolls to bypass). The Beast can also access information stored on the network and, with a successful Finesse + Computers roll, can enter the Internet from a connected computer system. Navigating the Internet requires a successful Finesse + Computers roll to transfer from website to website, but movement within websites requires no roll. The Beast can exit the system at any time, but exiting is an Instant action that causes her to sacrifice her Defence and subjects her to the Stunned tilt as she reacclimatises to a physical world.

                                                      While within a computer, use the Horror’s Power/Resistance/Finesse stats for the Beast.

                                                      A competent programmer can notice the Beast’s presence in the network with a successful Wits + Computers roll (if the Beast is trying to be subtle, contested by the Beast’s Finesse + Computers). Once he has noticed the 'rogue program’, the programmer can attack the program with Intelligence + Computers contested by the Beast’s Resistance + Computers. For every net success that the Beast scores, the programmer suffers -1 dice to further attempts. For every net success accrued by the programmer, he can choose to gain +1 die or to deal 1 Bashing damage to the Beast’s Willpower. If the Beast’s Willpower is filled with damage, he is ejected from the system and violently returns to his body, suffering the Stunned tilt. The Beast is free to do whatever he likes to try and slow his enemy.

                                                      Low Satiety: As the Horror’s hunger grows, so too does its ferocity. In its wrath, it tears through the feeble protections of its mortal prey like a dragon through cobwebs. The Beast can choose to either ignore firewalls, data protection and the like and grant those who might be monitoring the system +1 dice to notice it or gain +3 to Finesse rolls to bypass them.

                                                      Satiety Expenditure: With this Atavism, the Beast can become a creature of living data, sapient information encoded upon the background electrical charges of the world or within the systems designed by humankind.
                                                      Activating this Atavism at Satiety Expenditure level is an instant action. If the Beast becomes data without a method of transmission, she stays exactly where she was, intangible, invisible and incapable of affecting anything, for a number of turns equal to her Lair.
                                                      If she is touching something that can transmit or receive data, such as a USB port or a Bluetooth adapter, she can physically ‘download’ herself into the computer system that she is touching, in which she can exist for as long as she likes.
                                                      However, this ability comes at a price. If a programmer attacks the Beast, he inflicts damage it the Beast’s Health Meter and if the Beast suffers sufficient damage to kill it, it dies. The Beast is free to hide behind firewalls, escape to a different system or simply leave the dataweb.
