    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    I think it's a half-and-half thing; the idea that, in all times and places, groups resembling the four (or five, even) Orders inevitably arise from imperceptible influences won't be a thing, and there'll be more emphasis on the historical background of some of the particular terminology and rituals and metaphysical models. But at the same time, investigations into what is perceived under Active and Focused Mage Sight and looking into uncanny ruins will give some insight into the idea of archetypal social roles and the power that can be drawn thereof. One doesn't need to be anything close to a thearch. with the Elemental Precepts and the Convocations and the Lex Magica to start approaching the rudiments of "adopting a vizier role in society comes with some perks to magic", I think.
    Oh, agreed. I was speaking more of the first part you described (The "Orders inevitably arise from imperceptible influences" bit). That was were I was going with my post. I don't deny that elements may exist from a pre-existing culture, or that the Symbolism of the Archtypes posited by the orders can't be discerned and pieced together from studying the Supernal. Just that the Order's themselves don't appear no matter what Ex Nihilo, but arose from the natural evolution of mage culture and the study of the supernal, which in turn arose from the cultural development in the sleeper cultures (if there is no concept of specialized labor, then why would Mages know what they were looking for/at?*). Once they did have a point of reference and built on these, they still would have to have to build more upon it to formalize it (like any philosophical model) and then have to convince others that it is an accurate model (Once you encounter them). While mages had advantages over sleepers in achieving advancement of theses studies and their spread, there are still limitations that mages will face, as we have seen from the "To the Strongest" chapter, where more unified theories are just beginning to be posited.


    *This is associated with something I pointed out on another part of the forum:
    A mage using forces may be able to see and interact with Radio Waves, but before the sleeper discovery of what those waves are, the Mage can only understand them through the lens of the those waves' Supernal Symbolism, which might not give them a "scientific" insight about what thoae waves are. Essentially, the Mages would have an understanding of the radio waves and be able to understand any signal carried, but they wouldn't be able to relate what they are studying back to fallen reality since they only see it through the Symbolism of their sight and have no fallen analog to associate it with. For example, an Obrimos may think they are the wavelength of angel communication, while the other paths might see them differently as per their Path's sight, and none will see them as "a part of the electromagnetic spectrum, whose long wavelength and low frequencies allow for information transmission at significant distances" because that is the fallen, scientific explanation of them (yes, science also has a supernal reflection, but that is different then fallen explanations we have). The mage might see the waves as related to light, infrared or other parts of the EM, but they would likely contextualize these as part of related things in their Path.The Obrimos may see these as all parts of the Seraph of Blinding Light's manifestations in the world: the radio waves his voice, the visible spectrum his body, the infrared the warmth of his presence, etc. So, while they might create radio waves and design a "rockie talkie"by imbuing a stone with the ability to communicate via said waves, they likely will not be positing the theory of electromagnetism in 5300 bce, some 7100 years before sleepers came up with it, because to them, radio waves being their Supernal reflection actually would be an accurate explanation for them as they relate to the mage ("Who cares about the Fallen science explanation when the Fallen World is part of the Lie : I have the Supernal explanation". Though the FC would beg to differ...)
