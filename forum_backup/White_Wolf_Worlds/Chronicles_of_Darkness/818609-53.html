                                In 1E the Foundations followed an academic/Mad Science trope to a T, so that's were the 'engineering' emphasis comes strongly even if they weren't Artificers. There's a lot more occupations and interests to apply the Mad Arts & Sciences (and beyond) to, so broadening the Foundations a little bit more would be key. Which could be done with their initial Fields, perhaps a few Fellowships that have ties to the Foundations. At character creation the player selects the first most applicable Field for their Genius.

                                For example, as a foil to a graffiti artist character in a game I run on Sundays I introduced a Neid Artificer with a bizarre take on color theory for a paradigm, if you will, and they've been butting heads ever since. The Genius, Spectra Heliotrope, does have an automated paint factory and some walking paint missiles, but she also insists she is an artist first, even with the chemical and gun running she does on the side.

                                Taking another look at the 1.1 edition, here's some Field ideas for each of the Foundations. I trust others could come up with some more 'Inspired' titles for each. Obviously some could work across multiple Foundations.

                                Artificers
                                The Union of Artifice is suggested to be a slurry of various interests, enthusiasts and rejects of all walks of life and clashing aesthetics, bound together by the common driving to create, create and create with numerous ideas each day.
                                - Robotics
                                - Engineering
                                - Architecture and Construction
                                - Computational Programming
                                - Painting, sculpture and visual arts
                                - Chemical proliferation and potioneering, alchemy
                                - Toymaking
                                - Smithing
                                - Mechanics, Craftsmen and Tradespeople
                                - Gadgetry

                                Directors
                                The Fellowship of Manifest Direction (which needs a new title to separate from Fellowships, even if the 1E backstory wants to be cute) are the schemers and conspiracies that plot world domination and new governments alongside poaching each other's staff, conceptualizing new projects.
                                - Psychology and hypnotism
                                - Business and Economics
                                - Ariguture
                                - Performance arts
                                - Cinematopgrapy
                                - Political Science
                                - Law and leagalese
                                - Anthropology and sociology
                                - Brand marketing
                                - Social media
                                - Intelligence networking, Espionage
                                - Industrialization
                                - Fashion
                                - Occult rituals

                                Navigators
                                The Center for Circumferential Navigation includes explorers and trailblazers, daredevils that would rather test their gear in the field than be coped up all day in the lab. They tend to survive the risks as they quickly discern how to improve Wonders at the testing phase.
                                - Partical physics
                                - Archeology and paleontology
                                - Geography and geophysics
                                - Astrophysics
                                - Pan-Dimensional theory
                                - Oceanic studies
                                - Environmental science
                                - Temporal distortion & travel
                                - Military tech
                                - Paranormal debunking and documentation
                                - Stunt performance
                                - Field medic and warfare
                                - Animal control
                                - Law Enforcement
                                - Kineticthesiology and sports science

                                Progenitors
                                The Reformed Society of Progenitors contains transhumanists, demiurges and classical mad scientists keen on legacy as they improve upon generations of their wonders. They shape their lives and environments unlike any other, deeply passionate about having their projects exceed and continue where they can not.
                                - Biology
                                - Zoology & veterinary science
                                - Cybernetics and biometrics
                                - Death and undertaking
                                - Botany and landscaping
                                - Medical sciences
                                - Nutrition and culinary arts
                                - Ecology
                                - Cloning
                                - Extraterristrial specices
                                - Genetics and anatomy
                                - Pharmacology and quackery
                                - Necromancy
                                - Diseases, viral and microbiology

                                Scholastics
                                The College of Scholastic Theory does its best to document, label and rationalize everything unexplainable in Inspiration. By which most of the modern terminology has come from their lexicon but they are no more experts of the Mad Arts & Sciences than the rest of them. Still, the collegiate tries to explain and understanding everything of new wonders and mysteries before hand, attempting to finding stability in the uncertainty of Inspiration.
                                - Philosophy
                                - Literary arts
                                - Advanced mathematics
                                - Journalism
                                - Education
                                - Taxonomy
                                - Linguistics
                                - Astronomy
                                - Historical archeology
                                - Cryptographers
                                - Software development
                                - Forensics
                                - Lorekeepers
                                - Music theory
                                - Religio

                                            Originally posted by Dusksage View Post

                                            Yeah, that was the idea. There'd be a few Fields that are more commonly known, but the sky isn't even the limit. You can have a Field, and it can be totally outside the stereotype of your Foundation. There are plenty of Bio-Engineer Artificers, Machinist Demiurges, and Meditative Navigators. 2E Foundations are divided philosophically, not by discipline.
                                            • Artificers: They are materialists that believe that stuff can make the society they want. They make things, like machines, organisms, software, etc. They are defined by a worldview that places the concrete and "real" over the abstract and theoretical.
                                            • Directors: They are manipulators and administrators that believe that social hierarchies are the best tool to make their Opus real. The control things, seeking to manipulate societies into what they want, or make a new society in their image. They are defined by a wordlview that places the power of society above the power of individuals over the long term.
                                            • Navigators: They are mavericks and men of action that believe that the Opus can come only by force of will. They prefer action over planning, and decisive action and aggression is more appealing than hedging, hemming and hawing. They are defined by a worldview that places individual greatness above the fears and rules placed by society.
                                            • Progenitors: They are the creators and teachers of the Peerage. They believe that the only way to change the world is to change the people. Whether it's by education or... more invasive means. They have a worldview defined by both humanism and trans-humanism, where people are the most important element.
                                            • Scholastics: They are the philsophers, theoreticians and "idea guys." To a Scholastic, it's that ideas that change society. Machines and societies are great and all, but they were ideas first, and it's ideas that bring real change. They are defined by a worldview that places theory over practice, and emphasizes the power of knowledge.

                                            As a result, you could conceivably deal in any Field, and still be a part of any Foundation. It just depends on how you choose to apply yourself.

                                                    I figure they keep on being foils, but only the Peerage notices a lot of the time. It fits with how insular and hostile to outside influence Lemurians can be, to suffer repeated empathy failures with their archrivals.

                                                    Atomists: Foils to the Artificers; they're what happens when an Artificer is not conflicted about progress, or assumes it is a good in-and-of-itself. Not only do they place the concrete over the abstract, they are openly dismissive of the idea of an abstract mattering. Their disconnect with humanity is that they never factor in the fact that people will be using their devices, or if so that they will simply learn as they go along.

                                                    Etherites: Foils to the Navigators; they're the Great Man theory of history applied to the present. A lot of that is their universal pet Grand Unified Theories, but that's ultimately secondary to the idea that one man (and it's usually a man) can tame and understand the universe singlehandedly. Their disconnect is their tendency to see humanity as a bunch of bit players to a One-Genius(-And-His-Pals) Show.

                                                    Mechanists: Foils to the Directors: all the focus on society, complete disregard for the components. It's not that they don't believe in free will, it's that they believe it to be a net negative in the hands of too many people. Their disconnect is obvious, not seeing the trees for the forest...and yet it's often subtle enough to the point where they have the highest amount of Paragons among them minus the Oracles, since they do care very deeply about human welfare, it's just that how they care tends to be horrific (see; Borg).

                                                    Oracles: Foils to the Progenitors; coming from the opposite angle from most of their misanthropic peers, Oracles are outright human supremacists. They feel humanity is the only creature that matters in the long term, with certain stipulations for transhumans who "retain their souls", whatever the hell that means for a particular Oracle. Their disconnect is with context; while they do have enough genuine compassion for mortals to frequently be Paragons (hence why Illuminated are not associated with Oracle "crystal-tech"; most Illuminated were never Oracles), it's all too frequent for them to exclude certain subgroups from the Humanity Club, and they do not consider the effects their works often have on the environment around them.

                                                    Phenomenologists: Foils to Scholastics, they change the evidence to suit the theory. It's simple, but ironic; for a group that claims to be the embodiments of postmodern ideals, they sure like to cling to their pet theories of the mind in the face of all reasonable evidence. Their disconnect is always assuming the idea is objectively right, and if a person disagrees, they are an enemy to be convinced otherwise or destroyed.
