        It's worth noting that a lot of the terminology on Higher or Lower doesn't come from things like "better or worse", but instead more the more occult meanings tied up to a thing's conceptual nature versus it's actual implementation, etc. - such as "As Above, So Below" or a the platonic ideal of something being defined as being the most like itself as it can be instead of being some measure of objectively better or worse.

        So the Fallen World isn't a worse place because it's further from the Supernal, necessarily, it's worse because what separates it from the Supernal is concentrated anti-reality which sometimes seeps in through the cracks. You could theoretically have a setting where the Fallen World is fallen simply because it's harder to do magic because of the separation between the Supernal and the Fallen world, and that would be the only consequence. Sadly, they aren't separated by a neutral vacuum but instead something that's legitimately horrible, but hey, horror story.

        Likewise, the Lie, the falseness that makes people unable to see the world for what it is? That's because of the Exarchs, which are a -Supernal- force.

            Yes, that is how it works. Non-supernal magic is phenomenal in nature - that is to say, it works on phenomena, albeit esoteric phenomena. They exploit obscure and secret rules of the real world. Supernal Magic works by altering the symbol-truths that define the phenomenal. To put it another way, non-supernal magic exploits loopholes in the laws of physics to achieve a desired result, whereas Supernal magic temporarily changes the laws of physics to make the desired result the natural one. This has the side-effect of potentially allowing unreality into the phenomenal world.

                        Originally posted by Geckopirateship View Post
                        Yeah, that's basically what MtT ended up as. I'm thinking this is ultimately more a matter of time and emphasis.

                        There are spirits and archangels that exist on that level, and while there's no power tier breakdown between super duper cosmological beings, you can definitely see the God-Machine, the Principle, etc. as supernatural phenomena on the same level as the Exarchs, sure.

                        Archmages are fairly unique among theoretically playable characters in their power scale because that type of power is often at best completely removed and at worst actively harmful to the feeling of horror that these games go for; for Mage, it works precisely BECAUSE it's a game about the horrors of hubris, abuse of power, etc. etc., and even then archmages are most often used as plot hooks or NPCs; playable archmages are a niche, niche, niche game that is as wildly different from being a regular mage as being a mage is different from being a human. It's not a natural continuation, it's a weirdass offshoot into obssession and power that comes with unimaginable baggage. Other splats might eventually get stuff that plays in upper power playspace - I'm looking forward to the vampire Elder book that's being written - but I would caution against looking at the lack of playable peers among archmages as a lack of parity to be rectified, because it's not.

                                                    There's some things in this thread that are... Incorrect. Putting it charitably, and I just know Gecko's going to ignore what the books say in favour of what someone on a thread once told her, even if they didn't know what they were talking about, so, in no particular order:

                                                    Beasts have a much easier time navigating the astral than mages do, strictly by mechanics. Where the Primordial Dream is is not in any way mysterious: It's the area around the Omphalos at the far end of Temenos.

                                                    Only mages and beings with Integrity have an Onerios. Only those and Beasts have a personal connection to the Temenos. This is because those realms are the inside of a human soul, and nothing else has one. Other supenatural beings like demons can get powers to let them enter someone *else's* soul, or have a beast pull them into the temenos with its powers.

                                                    The world isn't Fallen because it's not the Supernal - there was a world before the Fall - but because it's damaged by the Abyss.

                                                    The Supernal Realm is not a physical place, and destroys anything that tries to treat it like one.

                                                    Other supernatural beings have Supernal symbols over them when viewed in Mage Sight, but then so does *everything*. Mages don't believe vampires are linked to Stygia just because they show up in Death Sight.
