    Space is the Murder Arcana

    07-20-2016, 06:37 PM
    The Space •••• spell "Secret Room" is ridiculous. Observe:

    Maxine Mastigos is next to a nice suburban domicile where she knows a dinner party full of crooks is in full swing. This will not do.

    Now, she's your average Adept of Space - she has Space •••• and Gnosis ••••, so she has 3 Yantras available, a 1 hour ritual interval, and 8 dice to swing at this problem. She decides to give Secret Room a try.

    First, she spends her Reach to swap the Primary Factor to Potency - this gives her a base Potency of 4. She takes a -6 on the spellcasting dice pool to increase that to 7. She wants to encompass the first floor, so she needs to take another -6 to the pool - leaving her at -4! She decides to snip off a piece of a curtain (+2 material sympathy yantra), draws a few runes on it in Sharpie (+2 Runes yantra), and chants over it in a low voice (+2 High Speech yantra). At the end of the hour-long ritual, she touches the wall of the house.

    Immediately, the first floor compresses into a one-yard-radius sphere. Anyone who can't fit in that kind of space (which covers all of the crooks) gets pushed out of the bottom floor, taking 7L for their trouble. There is no Withstand. There's no real way to figure out what the hell just happened (most people are going to think explosion, not "3 second spacial compression"). Depending on whether or not the ST wants to consider walls and such "subjects" when you're casting it on a space containing multiple rooms, you can also make a case that you've probably destroyed something load-bearing, collapsing the building.

    =---=

    The more I look at Space, the more obviously terrifying bullshit I see.

    Take, for example, Co-Location. You can roll Dexterity + Brawl to tap someone with your hand, then take a Reflexive action to swap them to the other location. Simples!

    Now, make yourself a soul stone, and put it somewhere actively threatening to life and limb. It's relatively cheap to get stuff into space (or near space). I hear space is pretty hostile to living creatures. Wrap an appropriate Ban around the soul stone, and it'll survive flying into the sun without problems.

    I really don't think I need to finish this train of thoughts.

    I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

    So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.

                                                      I honestly don't think sworn oath works that broadly as you think WHW. It forces a clash of wills if some supernatural power would change the actions of a subject, which has sworn the oath. So if you force somebody to go away from the door he swore to protect (action) or compell somebody to just watch while you break in (inaction), you get the clash of wills. So your examples for space and mind would work. Just outright killing somebody does not provoke a clash imho, since you don't force the subject to do or not do anything he wants. You simply take away the possibility to do anything at all. "Inaction by death" is kind of far fetched I think.
