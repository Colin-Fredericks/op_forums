                    Alright, I'm so inspired I think I'll take a crack at writing up a Conspiracy idea I've had knocking around for a while. Be noted that like the True Fae...and now that I think about it the Darkness too...I'm not that original, but I can make meeeean homage content.


                    The Cold Guard: The Watchers of the Darkness

                    "The Nobles love to tell a common tale about the origins of their society and the righteousness of their mission. Well, let me share with you a tale explaining the origins of our order: The Cold Guard was founded by a single woman from such a long time ago it no longer exists in recorded history. Back then there were eight citystates that were near constantly at war with one another, and each was ruled over by a sorcerer who wielded powerful magic relating to the human soul. Now, these citystates warred with each other so much that a fissure opened up within the earth itself and from it pored forth a horde of monstrosities that slaughtered all in their path. The horde grew so great that each of the citystates were forced to join forces, and yet even with their combined might the monsters couldn't be completely destroyed before more came poring through the deep fissure. This is where our founder comes into the story. She was a simple woodsman's daughter who lived far from the walled citystates and despite her good nature she and her family were no strangers to hardships. They looked out for each other and their small community, but were always willing to do what was necessary in order keep the community alive. All of this changed when the monsters appeared and slaughtered all of the villagers save for her, and only because she was covered in the viscera of the monster who almost killed her and thus the creatures couldn't recognize her from one of their own. Heartbroken but determined to avenge her family and prevent such destruction from befalling any other villages she drank of the blood of the creatures, a deadly poison that would do worse than kill her in time but cloak her from most of the horde, and followed the horde back to the deep fissure from which they arrived. Down into the dark she climbed until she climbed so far down that she arrived at...nothing. A great and infinite void from which the monsters must have come but was so still, cold, and empty that no other life could possibly live there. She knew she would not survive this journey, but when confronted with the void she knew fear like she'd only felt once before. And deep inside she cried out for something to be there, even if it was a monster. And from that fear, that desperate need, the Emissary was created from the void and emerged with the full knowledge of it's creation and the scope of the void so that it may answer the woman's questions and comfort her. It spoke of how the void is given shape and warmth by the essence of what humans call the soul, and how since humanity is covetous by nature only the essence that a human doesn't want to keep within themselves escapes to shine like distant starlight upon the void. Thus, when the citystates made war upon another to claim imperium and the sorcerers delved deep into the magic of the soul, hope, joy, love, friendship, and all that humans deem good was hoarded, while pain, sorrow, fear, despair, and doubt were cast out until it became a dim red sun that finally pierced the wall between worlds and allowed the void given shape by what humanity wishes to avoid to journey to the other side and consume the sweet warmth that had been denied to them. It spoke of how in time the sorcerers would discover the void and use it to become immortal monarchs over realms carved out of the human soul itself. It spoke of a time when the void would become Darkness, and actively seep into the souls or humans in a desperate hunger for warmth. It spoke of betrayal among the monarchs and a time one would come to rule over the Darkness itself. It spoke of the day humanity gains the power to destroy itself, and of the day humanity will first walk among the stars, how each will shape the void made Darkness. The woman listened to all of this, and deep in her heart knew it to be true. Thus, did she refuse to die there in the void. Instead she began to climb, as the Emissary watched from deep below. Near death, but never more determined she climbed back towards her world with the knowledge of what must be done to prevent suffering like she had endured. And at last she collapsed upon the soil of the world she was born into, and began the work that needed to be done. She let the citystates fall to the monsters, for nothing would stop the sorcerer's ambitions for godhood. Instead she gathered together those who were like herself, survivors who knew grief but accepted it as a part of who they are. She taught them all she knew, until at last she no longer cared about humanity and hungered for the sweet warmth hidden beneath supple flesh. That is when her fellows finally killed her, as she wished. The most dedicated among them drank her blood, and continue to do so to this day. Is any of this true...really? What do you think. Does any of this matter? Look at the world around you today and tell me that it doesn't..."

                    The Cold Guard is a conspiracy of monster hunters and humanists who know very well what the Darkness is, and why that no matter what the Nobility tries to do it will always remain. The Darkness is shaped by the failings of humanity itself, and until humanity can accept those failing there will always be Nests and Spawn cropping up to feed on the warmth of human beliefs and emotions. The Watchers have three primary duties that they strive to fulfill, called the Three Fronts. The First Front is the humanitarian front. The world will not become a better place unless people start treating others like people. Hunters on this front work to undo the damage wrought by Nests, as well as combat the influence of other social or supernatural predators. Often this front is used to either temper the fires of some of the more zealous trainees or to help veteran Hunter who are starting to feel the corruption of the Penumbrae setting in and want to prolong their lives and selves for as long as possible. This latter scenario can lead to some...less than desirable problems, but giving a veteran the potential to face their Fading with dignity while giving back something positive to the community is worth the risk as far as most of the Conspiracy is concerned. The Second Front is the Cold World front, where Hunters cut their teeth on the creatures of the Darkness. It's this front where trainees become official Hunters of the Conspiracy, and where the Cold Guard acquires the necessary components to create Penumbrae. Of all of the Fronts it's this one that sees the most combat, as well as the obvious higher turn over rate. Their expertise really shows though, for there's few mortal organizations that can match the Cold Guard's knowledge about Spawn and the Cold World. The final, and most controversial, front is the Third Front, the Sorcerer Queen front. This is also the most dangerous and morally reprehensible task that the Watchers must perform. The hunt of the Nobility, not "contain" or "reform" but hunt. They kill Princesses, because someone must show mercy to those poor souls. To the Cold Guard a Noble is just another kind of Spawn, one made for the most cruelest of purposes. They are human who contain a shard of the Sorcerer Queens soul and feed their immortal appetite for hope and joy by leeching out the light and belief of their vessel. The Watchers look no further than when a Noble's human self finally collapses under the weight of the Queen's power and reveals the Queen's connection to the Cold Realm, and how they claimed immortality. By killing these poor creatures they save them from such a doomed state, or at leasts that's what they tell themselves to sleep better at night.

                    New Endowment: Penumbrae.
                    ~ The Cold Guard takes in the otherworldly darkness of the Spawn in a ritually diluted manner in order to manifest lesser variants of the same powers the Spawn themselves utilize. The cost is a dire one, for the only end a Watcher can look forward to after the First Ritual is complete is either a painful death or eternal suffering as a Spawn themselves. The rewards are potent though, for not only do the Hunters become inured to the worst effects of a Nest, but also gain partial transformations that allow them strength in battle not seen in mortals except in the Nobility themselves. For each dot in Penumbrae a Hunter gains bonus dots to add to their Physical Attributes as they see fit when they manifest a partial transformation. They can spread them out however they wish, and lose the extra dots at the end of a scene. For every dot of Penumbrae they also receive a [Still coming up with name] that grants them a passive bonus just for having, but also an activated bonus that they can use during partial transformations. The major draw back is that once they possess a single dot of Penumbrae they register and count as Tainted creatures for all instances in which that would be pertinent. In addition, they also gain the potential to drop from Integrity 1 to 0, upon which the character is automatically retired as a Spawn as all of their experience points and merits liquidate for use in their next character [at the ST's discretion of course.] It should be noted though, that not all members of the Conspiracy possess Penumbrae. To undergo the First Ritual is a personal death sentence for the recipient, and special honors (and precautions) are taken involving said Hunter hence forth.

                                                        Well, the similarities between the Princesses and the Cold Guard is completely intentional. They are Princesses without the proper lighting, makeup, and inherint supernatural advantage and are ment to have all the right intentions in the world despite the fact their methods are naturally self-destructive. If the Nobles are Belief without Compromise then the Cold Guard is Compromise as their Belief. They are trying to help humanity, they want humans to have the strength and agency to face reality in all of it's darkness and beauty. They tell the junky that if they want to be clean they will help, but it will hurt and they mustn't give up on themselves. That they are beautiful and human, and all they've done wrong is make some bad choices. They Hunt Princesses for the same reason they Hunt Spawn, because despite what a Princes themselves believe their Belief is fragile and they will either die horribly or they will transform into a nightmarish Spawn reflecting the selfishness that lies inherent in all Utopian Ideology. To them the Dethoned are what all Princesses really are, only the one's who've yet to transform still retain the person who became the unwitting vessel of a greedy immortal god-monster.

                                                        I mean, they are blatantly based off the Grey Wardens from Dragon Age: Origins. They can range from noble to selfish, but they are humans who choose to try and fight the darkness given the only tools they're allowed while still retaining their agency as humans. They are antagonists for Princesses and many other Hunters, they are allies to many other Hunters and even some creatures of the night. They are meant to be morally grey protagonists or antagonists who force the players to confront the themes that are presented and make informed decisions for themselves.

                                                            Actually, I thought of a way to make the Stormite Compact their own thing distinct from normal Stormwracked:

                                                            They're not really radical. Okay, in a sense they are, they want to tear apart every single instance of Taint they can find, and piss on the remnants, but what turned them from a normal group of Queen-touched berserks into a functioning Compact was suddenly realizing how much collateral damage they were inflicting. Perhaps, once, the original members grew so ruthless that Tempestra wasn't enough to sterilize the Nest their own actions were forming, and they had a massive wakeup call. Of course, Storms rejected the lily-livered weaklings as soon as she realized their hearts weren't completely sunken in it anymore-but they still believe in her cause. They're just smarter and more cautious about it-and in a sense, more total. They've realized to really kill the Dark, you starve it.

                                                            Thus, what was once a terrorist cell became a hacktivist group. Since their proper leader is/was a fanatical Egyptophile, they call themselves #Ammit, after the demon who eats the hearts of the wicked. Much like the Devourer of the Dead herself, they don't really think of themselves as judges, but as executioners of those deemed by others to be evil, to have been already found "dead inside"-they just make that death total, so as to not pollute the world anymore. And they're very serious about this; if you can't convince at least a large minority of the community that your target is a source of the Dark and still launch a hunt, they will censure you, and you will not like it. As several now incarcerated racist trolls discovered (and keep on discovering, to the point where ferreting out the 3edgy5me "heroes of free speech" to drop the banhammer on is regarded as sport among the accepted members-their infraction forum is called the "Whack-A-Mole Scorecard"), this in particular includes "judgements" that are primarily based on conspiracy theory and prejudice; the reason Ammit is their mascot is that her mix-n-match nature of being a hippo, a lion, and a crocodile echoes the ethnic mishmash of the founders, and the people they recruit; Cults of the Dark prey on the weak, and the weak are often minorities desperate to escape their situations. Once you've convinced them though, the gloves come off and the inner Seraphim come out; #Ammit doesn't just want to stop you, they want to break you. Not kill, although that's usually on the table-fire does wonders for purifying Spawn. But it's not really the Nest themselves they focus on, but the "Children of Apophis"-people whose actions have contributed to Taint, continue to contribute, and will contribute in the future. To really make sure punishment is effective, you need to make them examples. They will find everything there is to an Apophite, and they will slowly, methodically undo everything about them, drag them down, publicize the slow destruction, and with any luck scare any potential Apophites back onto the straight and narrow. "This heart is something the world knows is heavy", they say. "We are the flooding Nile. Heavy things sink."

                                                            Ironically, this also means they're relatively pacifist when it comes to monsters. If you're not an Apophite, that means you're innocent until proven guilty. They look up to most Princesses in general, even if they have to suppress a snort at how naive many of them are. This means they often play moderator between the Nobility and other hunters, or other monsters-especially Beasts, since they don't see what the big deal is with the nicer ones (the Princess usually starts listing them, but by that point the Princess is on the ignore list for a while). Of course, the problem with them is, once they've decided you are an Apophite, you're dead to them-it takes a lot of effort to calm their rage, and even then they will never, ever trust you. Compound that with complete derision of people who they see as obstacles of justice (the Polaris Movement has absolutely no sympathy from them-they view concerned parents as a bunch of cowards afraid their little angels get hurt, sort of like actual human beings; better take away their consoles too, there might be violent video games!), and one has a recipe for a group that has no real neutral parties-they are your friends, or they are some type of enemy.
