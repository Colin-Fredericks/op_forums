                    Time to see what the Union and VALKYRIE have prepared for us...

                    Regular Schmoes- well, that's one way to put it.

                    Evil capitalists are evil capitalists.

                    Yeah, there is a reason that you don't just post "hey, I've seen a monster! Let's kill it!" on the internet. Monsters have computers too.

                    I start to wonder if perhaps a meld of the Union with some factions of Network 0 could be the key for the later gaining a Conspiracy status...

                    Regular schmoes showing monsters who is the boss is basically the core of Hunter's identity.

                    I remember that scene in Durarara!! where all of the Dollars worked together to save Anri, or the CROWD of Gatchaman. I think that this is the future of the Union (I wrote a bit about it in Going Up the Tiers)

                    Yeah, in the age of the internet and with Netowrk 0's infrastructure, they could indeed become something great- the Vigil in all of its bloody glory. The monsters won't like it, of course, but who asked them?

                    I really like the Fire Fighter concept.

                    I think that sewage workers are, like, the ones who know the best about the God Machine. Easy to miss, impossible to get rid off, and knowing all of your weak points.

                    Ammm I must say that the Politicals rise an interesting point- while in real life we could have a lot of discussion about whether or not gun rights should be limited, in the case of Hunter where you have monsters around every corner limited gun rights could indeed help the monsters more than help the mortals in questions... On the other hand, many slashers also use guns, so we are back to square one.

                    I must say that VALKYRIE's art feels a bit underwhelming..I mean, I know that drone fighting is the future, but still.

                    I get why it was said "agents in black" instead of "men in black', but it still feels a bit weird. Nothing too important, of course.

                    Yep, VALKYRIE is probably a name which shows up in every conspiracy theorists dictionary. I can perfectly imagine the "aliens guy" talking about the super secret agency which was founded by valkyries who were in fact aliens who helped the Nazis during the WWII but switched sides and taught America how to create the atomic bomb. Heck, this is canon now.

                    BUT WHAT HAPPENED IN ROSEWELL?!?!

                    Was there some data leak in 2013? *short google search* Ah, WikiLeaks. Of course.

                    Ooooooooh I like that they actually encourage false conspiracy theories around them in order to hide the truth better.

                    *looking at the last paragraph about the conspiracy theories of TFV's agents*

                    Yeah

                    It is nice to see that VALKYRIE's agents know that they are manipulated, and do their best to go around it.

                    Yeah, what is the big difference between demons and aliens?

                    Oh crap, TFV actually recruits civilians who just know too much for their own good? Yikes.

                    So between intelligence agents, scientists and cover up squad, who actually in the TFV fights the monsters?

                    Yeah, those weapons are 100% tech. There is no issue with bullets than can go through the Twilight or something :P

                    Also- yeah, there is nothing weird about advanced technology. It is not like it can BREAK A PERSON'S SOUL or anything. Technology is fine, guys.

                    I see they have kept the ammunition concept from the playtest. Nice.

                    Yeah, sure the RFID is meant to collect info on agents "in case they get lost". Not "if they say screw this, I'm having my own conspiracy with blackjack and blood dolls".

                    So they can actually trap a werewolf in a human skin. Man, Luna must be pissed off!

                    Yeah, it is nice to see yet another reference for TFV being one of the conspiracies more tended towards forging the "Vigil Alliance". And of course they are being compromised.. but probably that's not the issue. I mean, why the hell would the vampires who fund TFV's science project kill their own scientists? Something is fishy here. I blame the witches (or, at the very least, the Seers. You should watch what you eat, and Division VI is a poisoned dish).

                    And of course, you can;t be a TFV agents without a Mjolnir Cannon!

                    Come on, how no TFV agent asked themselves why there are so many Norse mythology references in their conspiracy? It is because they have HYDRA in their organization, that's why.

                    I must say I am a bit disappointed that the VDSB does not actually burns vampires like sunlight. I mean, sure, it may be too much to ask from Advanced Armory, but it could have been cool.

                    Nice writeups, overall. Now, we have one more and we could get into the bones of the book- splats are fun and all, but I actually want to see what Hunter has to offer beside its amazing organizations.

                                            And for the last Splats preview, we have the brand new Nine Stars and the long loved VASCU!

                                            Let's start from the Nine Stars-

                                            The opening quote is a bit bland, but ok

                                            So are serial killers a big problem in Hong Kong also in real life? Or is there a bit slasher movies culture? The writeup seems very suggestive of it, at the very least..

                                            Yep, they are indeed a police based compact, focused around supernatural based crimes (slashers, specifically).

                                            The fact that slashers are being described as an epidemic makes me wonder if this is something unique to Hong Kong, or if in the CofD, the sudden rise in monsters also means a sudden rise in a number of slashers- or, perhaps, it is mostly a rise in slasher activity.

                                            Yeah, you shouldn't mix your day job and night job- that's how your life turns into a living hell.

                                            If the bureaucracy would be what that kills the Nine Stars it would be so ironic.. even though I won;t be surprised if there is a cabal behind it. After all, slashers can grow out of the police just as hunters, and the Hunt Club are known to get friends in high places.

                                            Ammmm hunger in Hong Kong.. why does it sound so familiar?..

                                            *enter- the Eater of Names*

                                            Ah, you. Got it. Go away, please.

                                            Wait- find new slashers before they strike? Yeah, if regular people just become slashers with no warning in Hong Kong, something must be really wrong in this city. And by really wrong I mean "Stop Eating names You Monster" kind of wrong.

                                            (yeah, I know it could also be the Contagion, but the Eater of Names of cooler, IMO)

                                            It is interesting to see how the Nine Stars also have a good potential for interacting with other monsters. I think that if the current incarnation of the Council of Bones would collapse, the Nine Stars could easily replace them. Heck, they could grow independently into their own Chapter, and then unite with the wider conspiracy. That could help them deal with the slasher epidemic.

                                            I like it that because the Nine Stars are so young, that you can actually play one of the compact's founders.

                                            So they actually grade you stars for hunting monsters. Kinda cool, saying the truth.

                                            Amm, I would have preferred to see a VASCU agent art than the opening art of a chapter, but oh well.

                                            Wait- so VASCU is private/freelancer now? I do like they are more international in scale, however.

                                            Ahhhh ok, that explains it. Unit rivalry is never good in law enforcement, but is still an integral part of it. Everyone hates it when someone is too successful for their own good.

                                            So the mysterious Director of Operations was replaced without us ever knowing if the theory of him being a god machine angel was correct (not that there was much to support it in the first place, but the connection between the Room of Mirrors and Beneath the Skin is very suspicious).

                                            Yeah, so we have replaced the non officially weird Director of Operations with an officially weird mysterious benefactor. Yeah, good for you VASCU.

                                            Heck, maybe this is Dr. Wintergeen? It would make a lot of sense if she is behind the conspiracy's salvation.

                                            Yep, Wintergeen.

                                            You know, it would be extremely ironic if the FBI wants to use VASCU to sniff out Wintergreen, while Wintergeen is the one who bought the conspiracy under a false name.

                                            And yeah, of course someone wants VASCU dead- people with the ability to unlock minds are not someone people with things to hide wants around.

                                            And again, I must assume cabals in the government.

                                            Cooperation with TFV could explain that fiction, where a VASCU agents use terms from VALKYRIE's lexicon.

                                            I like the Hunters section.

                                            So even within VASCU the competition never stops.

                                            So the slasher epidemic is a global thing. in the end?

                                            I'm a tad disappointed that there wasn't some mechanical support for the Wintergeen Process, to enhance it is dangerous. Sure, the Endowments costing Lethal Damage does enhance it, but still.

                                            Yeah, the hall of Mirrors is still here and is still fucked up as ever.

                                            Yeah, melding your mind with that of a cold machine definitely does not scream GOD MACHINE INVOLVEMENT. Nope, not at all.

                                            Oh, there is no "secret plot hook" sidebar? That's disappointing.

                                            Anyway, the Nine Stars seems to be very cool, and I think I do like VASCU going private. I do wish to know more about the all "slasher epidemic" thing which is going on, but we'll have to wait for Chapter 7 for this...

                                                            And after finally finishing the C&C chapter, let's move into the bones of the game-

                                                            Yeah, the Vigil is powered by despair as much as it is powered by hope.

                                                            Same old, same old

                                                            So Touchstone must be something outside of the Vigil- which makes sense. Its point is to anchor you to the normal, day to day life and stop you from falling into zeal and madness. That's how slashers are born, after all.

                                                            Amm- so the concept is that all hunters in a cell must belong to the same tier, which is weird. I am pretty sure that as I understood it, groups like the Lucifuge and Network 0 do work with hunters of lower/ different tiers than they are.

                                                            So Tactics are the defining trait of the cell. Makes sense.

                                                            So Group Beats are the "signature Beats" of Hunter- again, makes sense.

                                                            The "Vigil turn you into a Monster" theme is well reinforced through Touchstones.

                                                            The Confessional Touchstone is a really cool example. The Nosy Neighbor gives a nice example for a negative Touchstone- you hate that gossip, but you must actively care for them so they won't start talking, giving you an hold outside of the Vigil. Even the people you hate can give you something to grasp on when you fight something with eat tentacles and a beak.

                                                            Yeah, not all Integrity 0 people become slashers- but it is much easier to become one when you hit rock bottom.

                                                            So the Vigil is indeed a transmutive process, which shields the psych of the hunter from things a regular person can't handle. The Code solidifies their principles, turning the mind of the hunter into something more resilient to the darkness. The whole description really makes me feel that my view of "hunter as a mortal evolved template" is correct- you are still human, but you have went to the edge of the human soul to gain the power to stand against the darkness, becoming mankind's both best and worst to survive.

                                                            Yeah, becoming an hunter is something you "unlock"- the last stand of the human soul against the night.

                                                            Ok, so I know that after the playtest there were some complaints about how inflexible the Code was in 2e, so let's see how it changed. The idea of "must protect people from monsters" is still a thing, but it does hint that some aspects of the Code are more personalized.

                                                            Yeah, we can clearly see that the writers have read the discussion in the subject- there is a different between the hunter's faith and their instincts. The Code is a representation of the later, not the former. The fact you believe in something doesn't help by much when the Vigil kicks in, but it could help in the form of Touchstones which support you when you violate the Code in the name of you ideals.

                                                            Yeah, the Vigil is not only a virus- it is a parasite. The despair of humanity and its hope for a better future rides you like a meat suit until you burn down.

                                                            Yeah, the Code is represented by what the hunter views- but the fact is that if the hunter knows that what they see is the use of Endowments or Supernatural Merits, the Code does not kick in. That is more ironic as the Lucifuge are in fact monsters, but knowing that they are hunters who use Endowments actually avoids it. It is not ok to use supernatural abilities- but it is ok if it is in the service of the Vigil. I wonder how far can we stretch that definition- I mean, would a warlock who devotes their lives for saving mortals from monsters and claiming to be a part of a cell be described as a person? I mean, while they do use Dread Powers (or their equivalent), what is the difference between them and Endowments if the hunter in question thinks they are Endowments? A monster who fights to save the innocents could, in that context, be described as a person- because they use their powers to fight something much more terrible. That gives a lot of flexibility in the Code, actually.

                                                            Wait- your quarry is another hunter gives you a +2? Well, if you wondered if hunters are monsters...

                                                            Helping a monster is still a negative modifier, but is not a breaking point by itself.

                                                            So an Exceptional Success in a Breaking Point has its own faults.

                                                            So yeah, a sidebar referring to the whole discussion- the Code is not meant to force you to act in a certain way. It means to make you pay for your choices. Just like how vampires can try and be as human as possible, hunters can be devoted to the Code as much as possible- but there is a price to pay, and violating the Code has a price on its own.

                                                            And here we emphasis again the difference between tiers in game- it is really a shame that each organization doesn't come with their own description of a Creed, and how its interacts with the Code. It could have been really helpful, IMO.

                                                            I also would have preferred if it wasn't a "one time deal".

                                                            Yeah, yeah, nobody cares about attributes and skills here :P

                                                            Merits are merits. I like the seance Devotee.

                                                            So two things- first, isn't it the first time that it is stated that zero Integrity removes your ability to use Supernatural Merits? Second, nice note that the Lucifuge are human enough to use those Merits.

                                                            Aaand that's it for now! I need to go to the gym. I'll review the rest either later today or tomorrow.
