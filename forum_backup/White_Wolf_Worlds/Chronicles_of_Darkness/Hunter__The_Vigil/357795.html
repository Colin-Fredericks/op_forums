    Hunters and the Theory of Everything

    01-02-2015, 06:22 PM
    Alright, I've seen around the place that Hunters don't play nice with the metaphysics of other lines, and egregiously so. So it has become my head-canon that this is literally so.

    Here goes:

    All mortals have a sort of "stubbornness field"; it's what lets them spend Willpower and what supplies general organizational functioning. Basically, humans got inertia. It's also why Supernaturals weren't responsible for any of the really big events in human history; it would be like an ant trying to deflect a bowling ball right before it hits the pin.

    And the Vigil amplifies this field; the sheer level of "Fuck Monsters" present in a Hunter cells brings it to noticeable levels.

    "You can't try any harder!" "FUCK YOU, RISKING WILLPOWER!"

    "You can't remember anything thanks to the Sleeping Curse/Lunacy/Quiescence/whatever." "FUCK YOU, I'MMA REMEMBER ANYWAY!"

    "Um, you can't just graft stuff onto a human body and expect it to work." "FUCK YOU, THAUMATECHNOLOGY!"

    Rites of Denial already kinda work like this ("Oh, you aren't repelled by garlic? FUCK YOU, YOU ARE NOW!") And it does help explain why Tactics are exclusive to Hunters (no-one else is able to muster the required levels of SCREW YOU I GOT MINE to make them work.)

    I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

    So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.

              I had a working theory for a while (even tried to make a story out of it, couldn't make it coalesce into something workable) that humans have a kind of vital spark that supernaturals lack. This manifests that, while vampires/mages/werewolves (well, maybe not werewolves... ) have the power, for some reason things don't really change. A vampire can try to his little dead hearts content to corrupt a city, but really all he's doing is jumping on and encouraging the pre-existing corruption: the static nature of vampires means they have difficulty really grasping change, let alone causing it in any meaningful sense. Werewolves can try to manipulate the spirit world, but humanities choices just seem to matter way more than spiritual urging. Plus, werewolves have enough time just keeping the screwed-up nature of the Shadow in check, let alone actually pushing it in a direction they want. Mages may have mystical knowledge, but there's always a mage countering whatever he does, because mages are contrary to each other just by nature.

              This is why the semi-humans of each group is so important. Ghouls, while they've been corrupted by Vitae and the lifestyle of vampires, retain choice and that vital spark, however dimmed or deluded they may be. Wolf-blooded can be, in theory, helpful to werewolves due to their walking humanities path, which werewolves can never truly do, at least after the First Change. Sleepwalkers may be aware of magic, but they're still fundamentally human, with limitations attached, which means they see things mages, blinded by their magic, cannot.

              *shrug* Just an idea I liked. Couldn't make it work in any meaningful sense, however.

                  Yes, I love hunter the vigil but it's a nightmare to integrate it with the other lines, I mean let's just think for a moment about the power of the conspiracies and compacts and how many there are. Let's imagine that all the compacts and conspiracies of the main book and supplements exist plus a dozen extra or more because the books heavily implies that all major countries and governments have compacts and conspiracies of their own. That's a ridiculously amount of people hunting supernatural creatures, some of them with some serious supernatural power of their own. If this was true then all supernatural creatures would behave like the changelings or demons, constantly paranoid of being captured or killed. Because taking into account how powerful some of the organizations are if they wanted they could easily wipe out all supernatural presence from a city. You have to do some major tweaks to the world in order to fit them. This is what I have done:
                  1-The population of supernatural creatures is high.
                  2-Supernatural creatures cooperate and help each other when it comes to survival.
                  3-Supernatural creatures have secret refuges in cities where they can gather. It can be a club, an abandoned neighborhood or an underground market. Here they exchange information, goods and favors.
                  4-Hunter organizations are reactionary mostly, they hunt and investigate the supernatural when it happens but rarely go beyond that.
                  5-Hunter organizations mostly act as pest control, killing or capturing the weakest members of the supernatural community.
                  6-when dealing with a very powerful supernatural opponent hunter organizations prefer to negotiate and reach some middle ground rather than all out war.
                  What do you think?
