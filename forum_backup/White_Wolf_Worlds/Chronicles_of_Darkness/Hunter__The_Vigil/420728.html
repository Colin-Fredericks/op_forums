    Are Hunters Monsters?

    02-26-2015, 06:35 PM
    This question came up recently in the Mage forum, and the consensus seems to be that Mages are very much considered one of the "monsters" of the WoD, so I thought I'd ask the same question of Hunters. While low-tier Hunters are fairly normal, high tier Hunters are part of secret societies, have access to unbelievable powers and often have to cut themselves off from their old lives - much like every other splat, and that's not even touching on the ideas of Slasher.

    What are your opinions, are Hunters just another monster of the WoD?

                                      I think it says straight-up in the manual that they are monsters, honestly.

                                      Anyway, everyone but Demon is, in some shape or form, a human. But all the same, all the gamelines represent monsters.

                                      Amechra gets it right, it's a state of mind, not a taxonomy. More specifically, "monsters" dangerously implies the connotations of ALWAYS CHAOTIC EVIL or even It Would Be Rude To Say "Genocide".

                                      Ironically, though, mechanically, as far as 1e goes, NONE of the gameline protagonists are monsters, as they can all have morality and cause breaking points when you hurt or kill them. However, ghosts, spirits, hedgebeasts, and other "non-humans" are totally fine for target practice with no second thoughts. Even the most horrific vampire prince enacting a plan to enslave a whole city is a morality hit if you kill him, (because "humanoid",) but blasting the ghosts of innocents with ethereal rounds for the giggles is morally A-OK!
