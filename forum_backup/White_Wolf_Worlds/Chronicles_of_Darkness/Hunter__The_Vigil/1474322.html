    New Conspiracy: Dirge Division LLC

    02-09-2022, 04:33 PM
    Hey everyone, I don't post up on here often but I had this idea for a Conspiracy rattling around for awhile and thought you'd like it. Tell me what you think

    The Dirge Division LLC

    The monsters that hunt mankind from the shadows do so with terrible weaponry: Fangs, claws, fire, wind, terrible twists of fate, even entering the inner sanctum of the mind and twisting thoughts and desires to suit their whims. But that’s not all they hunt with.

    Some creatures hunt humans with infernal bargains and devious deals. An handshake from one of them could give a person fame, glory, love, talent and skill, but in return the mortal’s soul would be devoured, their wills removed, their fate sealed to do the creature’s bidding, or any number of horrible payments made to the one who dangled the proverbial carrot before their mark. Some monsters do this to hunt humans, others do it to better their lot in society, and still others do it for unknown but no less nefarious reasons. It really is a perfect weapon; how is a person to say no to such a seemingly good deal that could improve their life in the short run? Fame, fortune, power. Theirs for the taking if only they sign on the dotted line or spill blood on the parchment. Even if the person in particular knew that their souls will be taken upon completion of the contract, most can’t understand or even believe the seriousness of their decision until the bill comes due.

    That is where Dirge Division LLC comes in. They are the wheelers and dealers of the Vigil. The woman in the sharp suit worth more than a college education and the power grip handshake. The shadowy figure from the alleyway who whispers promises that only he could fulfill. The Hoodoo loa whose charms she says can ease your pain and get you to where you want to be. The Man at the Crossroads with a bargain and a wide smile. These are the Hunters who defend humanity from the threats from he shadows not with guns and knives (although they are useful in a pinch) but with contracts, deals, bargains, and oaths.

    Dirge Division’s power lies in their Ledgers, a physical copy of all the names and types of deals the Hunters use to exert control over their “clients”. The Ledgers used differs from Hunter to Hunter; some take the form of a small leather bound notebook. Others come as a large old tome or grimoire, a retro pocket digital assistant, or a file folder stuffed with looseleaf papers. However it appears, the Ledger is bound to the Hunter as only they can use it.

    The Hunter uses their Ledger as a conduit to their clients and allows them to not only empower their customer, but also weaken and exert control over them. Dirge Division does this by having their members go out and make deals with their prey. Once a deal is struck, the Hunter writes their client’s name in the ledger, along with details of the deal, date, and sometimes the client’s signature.

    The origin of Dirge Division LLC officially goes back two centuries to Eastern Europe when the first Hunters were gathered and pledged their names to The Black Bible. But the truth is that for almost a century before that, the Black Bible was in possession of a Monk from Tibet who had kept it hidden from the world and used its power to help fight monsters in his region. The Bible itself is a massive book large enough to have to be carried by two people. But that’s only half the story. IT may look like an oversized gilded and ebony book, but in reality it’s a manifested demon of desire and despair. The story goes that a demon came to the nameless monk’s village in order to tempt the villagers there with granting their wishes but twisting them around so that only pain came from their desires. Only the Monk was strong willed enough to see through the demon’s game and made his own counter-offer. The Demon would get its change to toy with souls and make infernal pacts, but only at the Monk’s discretion. Soon, the pages of the Bible were written with the names of the worst sinners, monsters, and fiends, the Monk came across.

    Decades later (thanks to an exceptionally long life granted by the Black Bible) the monk met others like him, people who fought agains the darkness of the world wielding their own supernatural talents. Thus the Monk began to take others who felt like he did and gave them the power to make deals on his behalf. Thus, the first members of the Dirge were created.

    The first members were each given their own Ledgers. Each Ledger is in fact an extension of the Black Bible and allowed the Hunters to strike supernaturally enforced bargains and deals. Soon, the Hunters spread out and began to take the fight back into the darkness. Monsters, it turns out, can be just as greedy, sometimes even more so, than their human counterparts. The Hunters used this greed to offer the Monsters heart’s desires but always with a twist. The Hunter gained supernatural power over their prey and could use them as tools to further the Vigil. The more names the Hunter added to their Ledger, the stronger they became. In time, some Hunters held multiple creatures under their thrall and became expressly powerful in their own right.

    It wasn’t until the turn of the 20th century when the Dirge began to truly flex their muscles. At that time, the ranks of the Division had grown to the point that there were agents found in almost every continent and region. After having captured and controlled various monsters, some with a lot of resources and near eternal wealth and fortunes, the Dirge began to exploit their clients and soon became rich and materialistically powerful. Their central command in Asia soon established a shell company, Dirge Division LLC and began their operations into human society as a small international lending company and bank. That way, all their Hunters could have a base of operations which they could come to for help.

    As Conspiracies go, the Dirge is one of the smallest with the fewest members and each Hunter is usually given free rein on how to conduct their transactions and hunting styles. Some work as the Stereotypical Captains of Wallstreet sniffing out the creatures that look human and feed off of all the hard work humanity does to enrich itself. Others might ply their train as fortune tellers and occult shop owners in small mom and pop metaphysic stores. Still others work completely “free lance” with day jobs that have nothing to do with their Vigil; instead they hunt the nightclubs or dive bars for any potential supernatural threat in their communities. The Shell companies were set up so that any one Hunter had a place to contact his allies in the Conspiracy.

    The only thing that matters is that each Hunter in the Conspiracy must once a year give up their list of names to the “Central Office” for recording and filing purposes.

    But what the Division lacks in size, they more than make up for raw power and reach. Most of their backing comes from their clients. While other Hunter organizations might struggle with obtaining supplies for the Vigil, all a skilled member of Division LLC would have to do is check their phone contacts. Say you need to purchase high capacity weapons, just ring up that Vampire Elder who you helped secure a safe place to rest in the city in exchange for being the Hunter’s personal ATM. Have a cannibal Slasher cult loose in the hills that’s been giving your cell hell? Time to call in a favor with that Werewolf pack you bargained with and have them pay the killers a house visit. The Dirge Division sees the Vigil not as a straight-line war against the dark but a more like a game of chess. By making deals and creating pawns with monsters, you can force them to help you battle other, worse monsters. That’s not to say that the Hinters of Dirge Division don’t or won’t get their hands dirty. Reneging on a deal with these Hunters will cost the monster dearly and they would be so lucky if the term “I want my pound of flesh” was spoken as metaphorical once a deal with Division is struck.

    It goes without saying that only the best silver tongued Hunter could possibly be as good as to even approach a monster with a deal and not end up dead the second they meet. That’s why most Hunters are made up of business tycoons, police informants, small business owners, lawyers, bankers, private detectives, practicing pagans, petty criminals and amateur/professional occultists. These are the type of individuals who either know enough about the supernatural or have had brushes with it enough times to know some of the ins and outs and HOW to approach with a bargain (and who is worth bargaining with and who deserves a bullet to the head) whereas the other type are smooth talkers with a keen insight into business or have already had great success from running their own shop to selling stocks on the exchange. The other trait that most of the Hunters in this Conspiracy share is that most have the ability to be able to bullshit with the best of them. Being a good salesman isn’t enough you have to be able to sell fire to demons and salvation to angels. Improvisation and the ability to think on your feet is key for these types of people and many most likely already have contacts in several (ill)legitimate circles.

    ENDOWMENT: THE LEDGER *-*****

    The Ledger is the main weapon of the Dirge, as deadly as any blade or gun when welded properly. Each Hunter is given their own version of the Ledger based on their preferences but each has the power to make dreams come true, for a price. The Endowment comes in two forms, on that weakens the Client (Cost) and one that empowers the Hunter (Benefit). The rating of the Ledger Endowment also allows the Hunter to offer more to a potential mark. A Hunter with one or two dots of Ledger with either Cost or Benefit can only offer minor boons and blessings like winning a small lottery ticket or getting to that job interview in time. A member of the Division with five dots can practically remake reality for their client and give them their hearts wishes.

    General power
    For every dot in Ledger, the Hunter gains three Merits of any type to bestow on a client. Only one Merit can be given at a time per deal but multiple may be offered if the client gives up something of personal value. At three dots the Hunter can either remove or add any one appropriate Tilt or Condition the client wishes. At five dots, the Hunter can help set in motion a process to resolve one Aspiration of their client’s.

    COST: For each dot in Cost, the Hunter gains a +1 with all roll against a client whether it be socializing or combat. All roles against their mark counts as an exceptional success at three instead of five. Secondly, the Hunter always knows in a general direction where their customer is at all times. Lastly, the Hunter can inflict their client with a single Tilt or Condition per dot in Cost at any time. Only one can be applied at a time to a given mark.

    If a client breaks a deal, the Hunter can inflict a -1 penalty to all rolls per dot in Cost and any Condition the Hunter wishes until the client either pays some sort of restitution or returns to the deal.

    BENEFIT: For every dot in Benefit, the Hunter gains a supernatural Merit that doesn’t include its drawback. However there’s a catch. The supernatural power only comes to the Hunter when he has an ongoing a deal, so any member of Division with 5 dots of Benefit can’t use it unless they have at least 5 customers on the hook. Mose customers doesn't mean more power though.

      MARKETING:

      As with any good company, there are often separate divisions; each with their own purpose and methodology. It’s no different in the LLC. The main difference being that the Different branches of the Conspiracy make deals with different entities for different reasons. Most Hunters aren’t placed in these groups but naturally find their way to them based on the way they approach the Vigil

      DEALERS: The bread and butter of the Conspiracy. They are the ones that make the deals with the devils that hunt humanity. And if that doesn’t work, they’re more than capable of “terminating” a contract if need be.
      Free speciality: Socialize/Weaponry.

      BROKERS: On the opposite end of the spectrum, Brokers make deals with ordinary people to help protect them from supernatural influences. Most of their clients are other Hunters who are in need of supplies or supernaturally enhanced backup on their Hunts. This Division is the one that is most likely to give their clients a “fair deal” or at least warn their customers about the leverage the Broker may have over them before signing any contract.
      Free Speciality: Streetwise/Expression

      SALESMEN: These are the “freelancers” of the Conspiracy and the ones most likely to go after more mundane evils; the drug pushers, the mobsters, the corrupted politician, the bloodthirsty Slasher (if they're sane enough to even make deals). While their marks may be more human, the damage they can cause are no less horrible.
      Free Speciality: Persuasion/Intimidation

      STATUS:

      Status is usually made within the Dirge Division based on how many deals and bargains you make. The more names in your Ledger, the better your chances of rising up through the ranks. Optionally, if a Hunter nets only a few clients but the deals themselves are of such great importance to the company or somehow give Dirge power, a Hunter might find himself being invited to the upper echelons for the good work they put in.

      *- You’re a member of the one of the most powerful companies on the planet that’s trying to save it. Time to start showing your bones, making deals, pressing flesh. Or burning the flesh away until there's nothing but ivory bone. You get two free dots in Contacts and Allies to help get your foot in the door wherever monsters might be hiding.

      ***- You’ve made some impressive bargains and the creatures you have on your puppet strings dance to your tune. You get a free dot in Resources and another free dot in either Contacts and Allies

      *****- With the deals you’ve made and the power at your finger tips, you’re almost as supernatural as the things you hunt. You get a single Supernatural merit without drawback and another dot in Resources.
