    Tools of the Trade

    12-05-2015, 06:09 PM
    I've never played Hunter before, but getting involved in a game. I'm interested in playing an experienced Hunter that has just the right thing on hand for a given enemy without going crazy tech like Task Force VALKYRIE. More normal things like Silver Bullets, Dragons Breath Rounds for a Shotgun, maybe a Crossbow built to shoot wooden stacks at a Vampire, that kind of stuff.

    So what kind of stuff would be useful to a Hunter? And probably just as importantly where can I find rules references to them?

    I figure this is probably a good topic of discussion to have floating around anyway.

              Zip-ties, rope, tarp, stun gun, duct tape, container of gasoline, lighter fluid, lighter and matches, mace, mallet, bear trap, stake, rags and stick that can form a torch. Most of these are more for if there are people involved you might not necessarily want to kill, such as a person possessed by a ghost, a misguided cultist, a charmed servant and so forth. The video wouldn't necessarily help against vampires, they don't appear blurry in second edition. Do not underestimate mace, it slams targets with Stun and a nasty -5 penalty to all actions. Not so useful against zombies and ghosts but it can save you against a mage.

              Salt, when placed, forms a barrier to ghosts and demons that they cannot cross. When used in conjunction with a weapon, it allows one to damage and Discorporate ghosts. There are a few other items in the same book, the God Machine Chronicle. Flash paper, for example, can help identify vampires as when used it forces creatures vulnerable to fire to flinch, providing they fail a Resolve + Composure roll.

                  There are in fact two rules books that I would recommend for the kind of monster hunting equipment you are looking for. The first is the World of Darkness Armory. This gives you specs on all kinds of mundane equipment and how it operates in the nWoD. There is so much stuff in there that sometimes it's temping to say, "Pfft! Mortals don't need powers! Look at this AGTM missile launcher my character picked up off of the black market!" Still, even if the rules don't cover all of the weapons you're thinking of you can use some of the rules in there as a "best fit" for what you are trying to accomplish. Another rules book you might want to consult is the Slasher book for Hunter. In that book under tactics there are rules for making improvised weapons. Sometimes you need to pull a MacGuyver. Well the tactic of making an improvised weapon has that covered.
