    Washington DC: Dying is Easy, Governing Isn't

    As the seat of power for the most powerful nation on Earth, Washington DC is constantly being played by every possible faction of creatures which think they can control the lives of men. Unfortunately, such a city means every conspiracy on Earth has a presence there as well, including several conspiracies from other nations embedded in their nation's embassies. For now, DC remains relatively secured from the supernatural. How long this can last when the regular political climate is in such upheaval, no one can say.

    Ahl al-Jabal: Quiet: The hashashin primarily patrol the city's neighborhoods, and aside from an occasional rogue leech there is little for the compact to do other than be wary and ready.

    Aegis Kai Doru: Walking the Labyrinth: As the conspiracies claim, DC is laid out in an unusual fashion, and the Spear and Shield use it as the perfect test for their initiates. Drugging them up and letting them loose, these would-be guardians must seek out the occult mysteries laid out in the city, trekking through the mall to uncover the truths their masters would so desperately want them to uncover.

    Ascending Ones: Gutted: The Cult of the Phoenix was hit by a one-two punch in the beginning of the 21st century. First there was Ronnie's War on Drugs, which only fractured the hold the Ascending Ones had on the city's drug trade. Second was the 9/11, which allowed the government's monster hunters to smash hard on their other enemies as "domestic enemy agents". Reeling, the Ascending Ones keep their heads low in the nation's capitol.

    Ashwood Abbey: Bored: The DC Abe franchise is just...bored. The city is so well protected there's nothing to do, and even with fresh "entertainers" shipped in from out of the area the presence of both dozens of conspiracies and mundane Federal law enforcement means that the Abbey in DC is hemorrhaging members, and some inside fear it may even shut down entirely. Not a bad sign for the rest of the hunter community however.

    Barrett Commission: Everywhere: DC is the Commission's town, the place where it made its bones on the blood of vampires. Patrolling Congress, the Pentagon, and the lobbyists on K Street, the Commission is still stymied at every turn by the powerful conspiracies that block them from finding out what they most vitally need. Including trying to finally break through the information stonewall surrounding "VALKYRIE".

    Cainite Heresy: Trouble: As a cult convinced that vampires control everything, the Cainites see DC as one of their most obvious targets to hit. Of course, with the sheer security surrounding the city, they're typically stopped before anything even starts. So now the Cainites are starting to hit the conspiracies before they go after the government. Because after all, it's not like this won't raise more trouble down the line. Right? Right.

    Cheiron Group: Quiet: The Cheiron Group only has a corporate office in the city, with the majority of their lobbying done through a K Street firm that specializes in pharma lobbying. There's only a single cell in the city, charged with keeping an eye on the office. Meaning that Cheiron simply does business in Washington DC, no "side projects" allowed.

    Division Six: Concealed: Safely hidden in the shadows of the bigger players, Six is presently making hits on members of the Aegis Kai Doru, told by Mr. Jones that these "users" are on the path to potentially becoming reality deviants. Something the Division desperately can't allow. Operating out of the Smithsonian under the guise of "government contractors", the Division is presently hitting whenever a potential member of the "cult" strikes out on the Mall at night.

    Faithful of Shulpae: Prowling: While they face the same difficulties as the Abbey and the Hunt Club, the Faithful linger in DC like moths to a flame. They suspect that gods are flocking to the city, and the few local Guides they have eagerly seek further proof of this. While other hunters have yet to take notice of these activities, their vigilance against supernatural incursion means it is only a matter of time.

    Knights of St. George: Stockpiling: When the threat of the 1976 Bicentennial War was revealed, the Knights stepped up actions within the city proper. Fortunately for those inside the city, the early cells of Division Six had already done a good deal of heavy lifting, leaving the knights to continue preparations. 2076 will be here sooner than anyone will realize.

    Habibti Ma: Pushing: The families of the group have noticed that many of their lost loved ones seem to turn up in Washington DC without fail, and so have set themselves up in the area in an effort to both save the lost and lobby Congress. Operating right across the street from the city's Scientology office as a giant "Up Yours" to cults across the city, the group is presently lobbying the House for a bill that would increase the ability of law enforcement to crack down on dangerous cults.

    The Hunt Club: Pre-Occupied: Like the Abbey, the Hunt Club is careful where it steps in DC. No matter their connections, the fact is the group can't dare risk being found out because a senator from Kentucky got caught with the entrails of a call-girl in his hands. Some in the city have even suggested sticking to the Boston rules in the city permanently, which has soaked up a lot of the group's energy.

    Loyalists of Thule: Seeking: Despite not being one of the older cities in the US, Washington is a massive hub of esoteric material and information. Coupled with the Nazis brought into the nation during the heyday of Operation PAPERCLIP, the Loyalists have spent too many nights in Washington seeking out the secrets of their own past and the hidden pieces of America's history.

    Lucifuge: Stalking: How many times has someone believed some politicians are just too lucky to be just coincidence? That they must've made some kind of deal for their good fortune and power. The members of the Lucifuge know too well that this is true frighteningly often, though they think that the Kennedy and Bush families actually do just have damn good luck (for what that might be worth). As far as the Seventh Generation is concerned, their duty in Washington DC is to watch the halls of power for corruption of the demonic kind. Even they don't think they have the capabilities to go after the sheer amount of bribery and back-scratching in DC.

    Malleus Maleficarum: Meditating: Though the city is considered quiet to many hunters, the Malleus still feels that there is always a potential threat somewhere waiting for the right trigger. As Gallaher is preoccupied on personal projects, the DC Malleus cells consider it a more important duty to make sure that the vampires aren't making powerful necks into dinner. So they practice with their benedictions, studying them to learn to more effectively use them when the time so rarely does come.

    Network Zero: Problematic: While most hunters don't mind Network Zero in terms of principle, in DC the Secret Frequency are downright despised. The fact is that the function of government actually takes place in those oft-hated smoke filled rooms and back alleyways because those are the places you can speak frankly enough to get your actual points across. So when Network Zero is constantly bugging offices and those same back alleys, people get upset.

    Null Mysteriis: Distant: Though at one time having a respectable presence in the capitol, in modern nights Null Mysteriis has come to see Washington DC as a sort of lost cause. Too much political action and a growing sense that scientific inquiry isn't as important as figuring out what special interest gets the next years funding has made many members of ORAS leave and seek greener pastures, leaving those who remain simply carrying on in their own way.

    Tastk Force: VALKYRIE: Ruling: The Commission may be established, but TFV runs DC. No one moves without them knowing it, hits without them feeling it, or talks without them listening. Apart from essentially culling the Ascending Ones in the past three decades, they've kept Network Zero in (relative) check and have fought off actions by the Cainites for seven years. The salad days might be over soon however, as more and more regional TFV commands are becoming stretched to breaking point.

    Union: Hemmed In: If the regular workers of the city were to decide they'd had enough, every function of government would essentially cease. So Washington DC is actually considered a town relatively hostile to labor causes. This has made the Union in the city feel as though they're being pressured by the monsters controlling the government, turning many Union cells in the city to the arms of the Political faction.

    Utopia Now: Lobbying: Where Habibti Ma is simply pushing for a single bill, Utopia Now is trying to get sweeping legislation passed that would make it simpler than ever to accomplish their goal. When it's pointed out they're using their hated enemy to accomplish their goals in their favor, Utopia Now cells brush it off as simply "using a resource".

    VASCU: Operating: In their home city, VASCU wants to focus on perfectly explainable and (somewhat) mundane crimes and felonies. The FBI has too many senior agents sending more "curious" agents to the basement with the lunatics for anyone in VASCU to feel comfortable to mention some of the things they've "heard".

        In my own DC setting I have the Ascending Ones as a formerly powerful Conspiracy in the District who's influence has largely been dismantled. They accrued a great deal of strength by piggybacking on various secret societies and cults of Egyptian sacred geometry that used to be much more prominent in the city, while also having hands in radical black politics and esoteric Africana. Times changed though. Their traditional sources of power gradually diminished, then finally the war on drugs and the war on terror dealt them a one-two punch as TFV used the crack epidemic and 9/11 to bring the hammer down on their activities to the point that the AO are only a shadow of their former selves.

                For the Aegis and the Ascending Ones in New Orleans.

                -=-

                Aegis Kai Doru: Seeking: Faced with a well-established presence of local witches, shapeshifters, and vampires, the Aegis operated in New Orleans with a light touch. Individual cells tend to focus their efforts at locating and acquiring any relics as discretely and quickly as possible. While the years that followed Hurricane Katrina allowed them to secure a few notable items of power, the conspiracy remains a small group in the city.

                Ascending Ones: Syncretized: Like the Ahl al-Jabal, the Cult of the Phoenix does not identify too strongly with Islam to avoid painting a target on for the vampires. Their cell in New Orleans was greatly influenced by the local culture, though, to the point that elements of Voodoo were woven into their predominantly Christian practices. Louisiana's Ascending Ones thus tend to use their Elixirs to imbue an assortment of artifacts and charms rather than directly imbibing them.

                                So I don't know Demon...at all. So I'll be jumping right to the fan-games for now. So, Seattle!

                                Seattle: The Northwest Problem

                                Seattle is a major gathering point for some kind of massive group of mad scientists, and the local hunter community has steadily been overrun by the bastards since the 1960s. Trying to organize as best as possible in such circumstances, Seattle's hunter community is organized among the principle that the city itself is a lost cause with only a few small holdouts left inside. The area surrounding Seattle however is slightly more their speed, with suburbs and the greater SeaTac area their base to strike at the city in the hopes of reclaiming it.

                                Ashwood Abbey: Inquisitive: Not many members of the Abe have had contact with mad geniuses, and while not as "mystic" as a witch they do offer the same kinds of transformation abilities and even mind control. To even the most jaded sybarite, this is a rich opportunity to get their hands of a pet mad scientist like the nobility of the late Middle Ages and Renaissance.

                                Aegis Kai Doru: Striking: Geniuses have the ability to make what at first seem like relics, but are in fact something far more strange. Theories on how they do this aside, the AKD typically go out of thir way to destroy any trace of these strange objects due to their unpredictable and dangerous actions if handled by anyone by a mad genius.

                                Ascending Ones: Negotiating: The Cult of the Phoenix has been trying for three good years to finally open the city enough for the hunters to move in, trying to work out a cease-fire between a group of geniuses who believe that they are a danger to society, and a group of geniuses who think they have a divine right to rule society. Unfortunately, both are quite literally filled with individuals who suffer from whole DSM fulls of psychological illnesses, and negotiations are slow going at the best of times.

                                Barrett Commission: Overwhelmed: The "technologies" used by the mad scientists to control people's minds are in a way worse than a vampire's mind control. At least with a vampire, you can stake the problem and easily cover the evidence up with a good shop-vac. Worse, these mad scientists have a way to ghoul people, but in a different way than vampires that makes it almost impossible to tell if they're a slave or not. The Commission so far is playing hell trying to make a foothold in the city government.

                                Division Six: Busy: The fact that these people are somehow breaking apart reality but somehow aren't your typical reality deviants has the Seattle cells of Division Six running about in circles trying to get a handle on what exactly they are. Mr. Jones hasn't explicitly given the operations his blessing, but those who contact him most often claim that he seems to be incredibly fearful about Seattle, leading the cells in the area to go on the offensive and try to find out what these "people" are.

                                Cheiron Group: Contracting Out: SeaTac Health Solutions is in the business of medical research, and the mad scientists want to be taken seriously. SeaTac has had several mad scientists and geniuses contracted out for the past few years, and while the experiments sometimes get out of hand (It turns out that several bigfoot sightings since the 1980s are the result of said contractors) STHS has claimed massive advancements in thaumatech implants.

                                Long Night: Evangelizing: The Long Night have been on a push to turn much of the area into another region under their control. The major pushers are primarily under the aegis of the Faithful philosophy, seeing the fighting in Seattle as a chance to push closer to their ultimate goal. It doesn't help that many of the pseudo-science theories pushed by the geniuses can be fought on the same level as the more ignorant members of the Long Night moving into the area.

                                Loyalists of Thule: Active: The Loyalists have a history with mad geniuses, and none of it is exactly "rosy". Many made scientists worked with the Nazis, and to the Loyalists they are an enemy to by absolutely purged at all costs. Unfortunately, the nature of Seattle's situation means that the Loyalists are for now focusing on ingraining themselves within the local hunter community, to gain access to cells that could be useful in finding and eliminating their old foes.

                                Lucifuge:

                                Malleus Maleficarum: Talking: As is the case, people presume that the Catholic Church are vehemently anti-science. That isn't the case as Abbott Mendel would point out, but the Malleus does have a problem with mad scientists running around harming people. Still, several seem to merely be mentally ill, and the Inquisition has been carefully trying to reach out and make entreaties with these geniuses to try and make a common ground in the area. Curiously, the Church has had more success reaching these men and women than the Ascending Ones, the geniuses claiming they're "All too familiar" in their history.

                                Network Zero: Frustrated: With special effects having advanced to the point where anyone can learn how to make digital effects with the right purchases and time, never mind how good you can make practical effects on a cheap budget, most of the videos out of Seattle are treated as "publicity". This has turned Seattle's Network Zero into unintentional pariahs within their own compact, as every shred of evidence they trot out to the public gets brushed off as a hoax, and gives the geniuses more reason to hate them to "mocking their work".

                                Null Mysteriis: Active: In a strange twist, in Seattle Null Mysteriis is out in force. The mad scientists and geniuses may once have held doctorates and licenses, but the vast majority are dangerous quacks operating on, at best, fringe sciences. Each day reports are published on actions taken by the mad geniuses, and each night the members of ORAS will find these geniuses and not only destroy their work, but scientifically pick apart every reason why they got it wrong to essentially emotionally gutshot the mad scientist. This has made ORAS one of the most valuable groups in Seattle at the present time.

                                Talbot Group: Studying: While ORAS handles the breakdown, the Talbot center in the SeaTac area handles the geniuses who want to try and fix themselves. Being able to admit they're mentally unwell and in need of help, the Talbot center is currently treating three of the mad geniuses and two dozen of their "beholden". Giving them the best advice they can to survive without making horrible creations and giving them small tricks to try and curb their impulses, the Talbot center is working closely with ORAS to make a kind of psychological breakthrough on the topic.

                                Task Force: VALKYRIE: Watchful: While many have leveled claims of being corrupted at TFV for their bizarre actions towards vampires, it's nothing compared to the reaction they have when a mad genius is sighted. With a city full of them, TFV patrols the city nightly, keeping an eye on the actions of both the local hunters and the geniuses. Many claim that this is because TFV has found the mad scientists to be a mixed blessing in the past, and so do their best to keep a firm but steady hand over them to remind them of what real future technologies can do to anyone out of line.

                                Union: Fighting: In typical fashion, the mad scientists don't think the local villagers are important enough to interact with. Meaning that the local villagers inevitably grab pitchforks and torches. In Seattle, the Union is doing their level best to fight back against the mad geniuses, for fear that their ideas on automation and robotics might kill even more jobs across the country. Unfortunately, the nature of the Vigil in Seattle means that even with the internet, Union cells are hemmed in to their own neighborhoods trying desperately to stay alive.

                                Utopia Now: Hiring: While STHS is contracting, Utopia Now is outright hiring geniuses to work on their plan. Since arcologies are at best theoretical technologies, the cells in Seattle have realized that what better way to gain their goal than by simply hiring people who can create what they need, no demon hunting necessary. This doesn't mesh with William Horn's original plan however, and several cells in California and Silicon Valley are becoming rather confrontational with the Seattle cells.
