                                Originally posted by One Tin Soldier View Post
                                Ok, could someone please explain to me just who the Knights of St. Adrian are? Because all I've got so far is that they're ridiculous 80s action heroes. Which sounds a little too... ridiculous for this level of love. Don't get me wrong, I love ridiculous, but it tends not to mesh all that well with standard WoD fluff. So what's so great about them?
                                The Knights of Saint Adrian, or the Sidearm of the Angels, are essentially bounty hunters for 'Heaven'(It might be a God Machine plot, or it might actually be some other divinity wearing the same face) who hunt down demons with the power of their angelic tattoos.

                                There's a few reasons for the love. First off is that it is a very bold and upfront conspiracy, even if the 80s action hero is somewhat taking it too far. They don't have a complex motivation for what they do-they're demons and it's a paycheck and it's much like hunting down any other criminal as a bounty hunter, a certain bluntness is needed. The way they approach their jobs as hunter, as dictated by the Ink Endowment, is similarly blunt-three of the eight directly tie into brawl and require, you know, actually hitting someone(although the third one, LOVE/HATE, has a twofer purpose, and only one is violent), with a fourth one that basically makes you as superior in some trait(Namely-Strength, Dexterity, Brawl, Weaponry, Firearms) as the last guy you fought. They can have a certain amount of infinite resources in regards to firearms and vehicles, which invites reckless use thereof-you can totally smash a car into a building and not worry about it. The remaining ones might be....subtler? But it's just a refreshingly straightforward group with a no-nonsense attitude that, mixed in with a bit of vicious cunning, is very effective.

                                That bluntness might not be so appealing...if it wasn't for the fact that their enemies are demons and that it actually works. Something this unsubtle shouldn't even be able to touch the Unchained(or your world's equivalent), but all it takes it one hit to start some serious momentum

                                I'm not saying they can't be smart, or cunning-on the contrary, I think a lot of them actually are. They are, after all, bounty hunters, probably for normal criminals in between the demons as well. But when they take the stage, when they act, it's with the zeal of angels without the stigma of religious culture-rather, their zeal might seem angelic for its' potency, but it's born from road culture, bar fights, and a general salt of the earth background.

                                They're also refreshingly honest on the whole. Sure, something's screwing with them and pulling strings, but go below the client and there' actually very little the group faffs around with. It's a stark contrast from nearly every other Conspiracy there is.

                                So, yeah. I think that's why.EDIT: or even simpler, it walks the line between heroic kitsch and 'real' World of Darkness sensibilities. It's just grounded enough to be believable, and just absurd enough to be a metric ton of fun.

                                Plus, lots of people love 80s cheese.

                                Or in even shorter terms, they're just a lot of fun.
