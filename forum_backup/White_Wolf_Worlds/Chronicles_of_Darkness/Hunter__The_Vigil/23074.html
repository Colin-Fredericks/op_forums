    Suggestions for a creature ability please

    12-04-2013, 08:30 AM
    hi, im writing a story featuring a spirit-like creature with the powers relating to cold, sort of a "frost wraith" or something. basically, what im stuck on is how to handle its basic attack where it would inflict severe hypothermia/frostbite in a matter of moments. Ive read and reread the rules in nWoD rulebbok relating to cold and it doesnt seem to fit whats in my mind. What i would like to do is have the PCs make a 'save' if you will against the creature's attack (in which case Stamina should do the trick imo) just so they have a sense of resisting the things onslaught. What makes sense to me to do, and i would really appreciate suggestions here most, is make the 'cold attack' an attribute + skill/merit roll like any other attack, just to make things easier on myself. If anyone has any ideas or suggestions on this, or has cooked up a similar creature power, i'd very much like to hear them. also, i am rather new to nWoD (though i have played several other RPGs) and am still somewhat a noob on the rules, so if i apologize if this comes off as a boneheaded question

      Not boneheaded at all.

      For the frostbite, here's what I can remember
      In Changeling there's a sort of frost aura someone can surround themselves with, so that up to 20 feet out anyone approaching has a -1 to dice rolls because it's so cold (and another -1 for every five consecutive rounds they stay in the area).
      And the Extreme Cold tilt in God Machine Chronicle, which is similar, in that there's a -1 penalty to all rolls, increasing every hour until they hit -5, when they start taking lethal damage from the cold. In addition, bashing damage doesn't heal as long as you're in the extreme cold area (which could be in your case represented by the wraith's frostbite affect, perhaps only curable by a blessing from a priest or warming yourself in a fire started by a child, or something).
      Lastly I suppose you could do an invert of the Dread Power Balefire. So that if something actually gets close enough to touch the wraith they take some lethal damage from the cold.

      Some mix of those might be appropriate, weaker or more powerful as you need it.

      As for rolls, yeah, Stamina seems like a nice defense, perhaps Stamina/Survival, I want to say Stamina/Athletics but can't help but think being fit might be detrimental to the situation. As for its attack, maybe Presence + Frostbite, you could just give the inherent power of the wraith a few dots (maybe 'weak' frost wraiths get one dot, while the supreme ancient master frost wraith gets 5). Presence if it's an Aura, Strength if it's a melee-like attack, Dex if it's a beam or aimed thing?

              Just spitballing here; although the idea of a resistance roll is fine, why not have something like this: (also I'm not exactly up with all the rules so please excuse any gross errors!)

              Frostburn (• to •••••)
              Make an attack roll as normal (Melee, ranged, or Aura as appropriate). If it hits, in addition to normal damage mark Bashing damage in a number of boxes equal to the level of this Power, putting a snowflake symbol ( ) under those extra boxes. As long as a character has any wound penalties and also has boxes marked with a snowflake that character has -1 to all dice pools. If a character falls unconscious while it has wound boxes marked with a snowflake, they suffer from the condition Hypothermia.

              Hypothermia (Condition)
              The character is suffering from Hypothermia and is in grave danger of dying.
              Effect: In addition to extra wounds inflicted while unconscious, one bashing damage is converted to lethal each minute the character suffers from this condition.
              Resolution: An Int + Medicine roll, penalised by the number of wound boxes marked with a snowflake symbol
              Beat: n/a

              This makes Frostburn very deadly at higher levels; beware!!
