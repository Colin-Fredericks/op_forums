    Mirrors' Merits & Gifted/Second Sight Psychism?

    02-05-2014, 05:33 PM
    I have a character that is Psychic (physically) BUT I am confused about which would be best, Witch Finders' Gifted OR Second Sight Psychism. Which have you out there used and which do you all think would be better? Could/Should a Story-Teller allow both?

      Witch Finder's Gifted is a broad term that represents more than psychics; however the spell list to choose from is very much focused on magic and so it's hard to play what you might think of as a typical psychic.

      Second Sight has some mechanical problems, mostly with costs, but it's a very well focused set of powers with great customisation options. It's by far superior for a psychic. I would add jsnead's hacks to polish it up a bit.

          I don't know about Gifted, but Second Sight is...problematic. I'm working off the RAW here, so adjust for any houserules you may have.

          First, it's merit-based, which to be honest was probably the best way, but since you need to buy each merit dot seperately (and pay appropriately), they can get hella expensive very, very fast. 1 or 2 dot is fine, it's 2 or 6 exp. But a 3-dotter costs 12exp, 4 costs 20exp, and 5 costs 30exp. Now, normally this wouldn't be any more of an issue than it normally is, but many Second Sight powers have either been broken down into many sub-powers, each of which need to be bought seperately, and/or have a few pre-reqs, which 1) aren't always a power you'd necessarily want your character to have thematically, and 2) can sometimes make your power kind of useless until you buy them all. A good example of this is Pyrokinesis: a 5-dot Merit to be able to start the fire, but if you want to control the fire, you need a further 4-dot merit before you can start buying the controlling merit, a further 5-dot. So, we're talking 80exp to be able to fully utilise your power, plus a sideline that makes you immune to fire (which you may not want). This is worse in cases of powers like Thermokinesis/Cryokinesis, where they cannot exceed the lesser of your Stamina or Resolve. This obviously gets hella expensive very quickly for something that usually doesn't do much (and burns through Willpower like a crazy thing).

          So, Second Sight is good in theory, but only really works with some of the listed powers, and in my opinion desperately needs house-ruling.

                      One of the reasons I like Jsnead's rules, is that it handles the biggest offenders. For example it merges Thermokinesis with Cryokinesis and merges Pyrokinetic Shaping and Pyrokinetic Immunity into one 5 dot merit. That way it would only cost 10 XP instead of 14 XP to be a master pyrokineticist. This makes it more doable, especially if the character starts with some points in it during character creation.

                          The one positive thing about Second Sight being merit based and a bit scattered as far as what merits allow you to do what is that it really puts the brakes on power creep. This is a plus if you're wanting to try to keep the feeling of peril when being confronted with the unknown. I myself am trying to run a Tier 1 Hunter game (that will gradually evolve into including the Compacts and Conspiracies) but one thing I like and the players also have expressed an appreciation of is that even low level supernatural forces feel more mysterious and dangerous when you lack the abilities to figure everything out and squash it like a bug as quickly as you would if you were a Mage or Werewolf.
