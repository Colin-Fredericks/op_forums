                                                    Originally posted by mlvalentine View Post
                                                    Cool, okay. I cannot speak to how Conviction works in Deviant as its related to Mirrors back in 1E nWoD.
                                                    There was a system introduced in 1e's World of Darkness: Mirrors (p32-33) as a "power stat" (Supernatural Potency/Tolerance) for normal human characters, providing a handful of additional options for spending Willpower and gradually increasing their supernatural resistance and the amount of Willpower they can spend per turn. It has basically nothing to do with the Touchstone track in Deviant aside from sharing a name.
