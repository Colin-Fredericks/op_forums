    Conspiracy: The Irem Coalition, 'Project AMMUT"

    11-29-2013, 05:23 PM
    So I stumbled across http://forum.rpg.net/showthread.php?...-flipping-out/ and immediately fell in love with the idea of the Arisen being viewed as an international security risk. The mental image of a group somewhat like NERV tracking and dealing with the remnants of Irem was too good to pass up. This is unfinished (making an Endowment is so fucking hard, gosh dang) but everything else is ready to go. Enjoy!

    The Irem Coalition: ‘Project AMMUT’

    “Alright folks, listen up! We’re dealing with the angry survivors of a civilization originating in the Nile delta in what we’ve previously classified as prehistory. They were thousands of years ahead of the curve, and may well have had access to understanding we still lack today. An unknown event wiped them off the face of the Earth, but a number of their laborers were somehow enhanced and then placed into suspended animation, waking up for brief periods of time in the long years since. Many of them have created generational cults, some of which operate behind corporate fronts. These things are dangerous, on the order of Old Testament God without his morning cup of coffee, and they’re waking up in increasing numbers. Your job is to stop these things. Suit up and be ready to ship out in two hours.”

    Modern archaeology is an imperfect science, and its students know that there may well be unsolved gaps in history until the day time travel is invented. The current record of events is well-founded, and nicely explains the world we inhabit today. The things that do not fit tend to be swept under the rug.

    Irem refused to go quietly.

    A number of digs throughout North Africa have found traces of an advanced civilization that well predates the earliest Egyptian dynasties, and seems vastly more advanced than anything that old has any right to be. An unknown event – the prevailing theories are a catastrophic war or a virulent plague, though many alternatives are often debated – destroyed the civilization, leaving behind very little.

    Something stranger than pottery shards survived the fall of what the archaeologists call Irem. A number of individuals were ritually mummified and entombed, along with a vast number of relics. It is from one such tomb, discovered seven years ago, that most of the intelligence on Irem exists.

    Irem is more than just an archaeological curiosity. Those entombed individuals were not simply killed, but rather placed into a sort of suspended animation, the likes of which modern science cannot begin to understand. These mummies periodically awaken and almost always leave a path of destruction in their wake until their goal is complete and they return to rest.

    The mummies often seem to hold generational cults – some small and familial, others massive and masquerading as international corporations – that manage their affairs during the long years they spend in stasis. The cults sometimes awaken the mummy for their own purposes, while other awakening are prompted by grave robbers making off with their artifacts. Once awake, the mummy will stop at nothing to complete its goal. These beings have access to abilities that defy understanding; more than a few call them magic. The question of just how exactly these mummies wield such abilities pales compared to the threat they pose.

    A number of events throughout the Middle East and North Africa (and one very well covered up incident in London) forced the UN to take an interest in Irem. What was once a fringe theory became apparent as a multinational security risk, and even the skeptics were forced to take notice. A task force was authorized to deal with the mummy threat, given security clearances and a large budget.

    Welcome to Project AMMUT.

    AMMUT has operatives from numerous United Nations countries, a hodge-podge of those brought on for military capabilities and the researchers needed to understand the threat. This latter group is a strange mix of respected anthropologists and fringe theorists, along with a small handful of those trusted to try and understand the ‘magic’ the mummies seem to utilize. Almost nothing is known of Irem or the mummies; information of their numbers, capabilities, missions, and cults is essential to AMMUT’s success.


    Remi. she/her. game designer.

      The Enemy

      Project AMMUT exists to deal with the threat posed by Irem’s mummies and their cults. Some teams are little more than research crews, sent to re-examine artifacts in museums across the globe or on digs throughout North Africa and beyond. Others are muscle, sent to stop a cult or freshly-awakened mummy. Cult members are to be arrested and interrogated whenever possible, and killing them without reason is frowned upon.

      The mummies are a whole other ball park. These creatures are ill-understood and possess the power to wipe out a major city. If they were human once, then the process that placed them outside of time and the intervening years have long ago divorced them from human morality and thought. They are walking natural disasters, to be put down however possible. This is easier said than done.
      The mummies seem to awaken primarily based upon two events; either the cult brings it out of stasis, or one of its relics is stolen. AMMUT operatives work around the clock to find and shut down Irem cults; no cult, no one to wake the mummy up. Ironically, AMMUT ends up doing some of the mummies’ work for them; there are numerous task forces dedicated to prowling the international artifact and punishing grave robbers, returning stolen goods before the mummy’s wrath prompts it to rise.

      Sometimes, preventative measures fail, or AMMUT simply gets blindsided. This is when the strike teams are scrambled and jetted out to location. The threat posed by Irem’s mummies is still ill-understood, and so everything from artillery to amateur occultism rest in the strike team’s arsenal. The mortality rate is high, but this is an accessible cost to the UN; the price of failure is far higher.

      If the UN knows about the other supernatural beings that exist, they don’t acknowledge them. AMMUT exists only to combat the remains of Irem, and many among the project would laugh at the notion of vampires or ghosts. The veterans know better, of course.

      The public cannot be allowed to know about Irem and the mummies. Attacks are written off as natural disasters or terrorist actions whenever possible, and an unfortunate number of witnesses have been detained to keep them from talking. Mass panic would ensue if word got out that ancient monsters were stalking the world and destroying cities. The joke among seasoned operatives is that nobody wants to pay for all the textbooks to change their timelines.

          Hunters
          • You studied archaeology at Oxford, with a focus on early Egypt. On a dig, you found some artifacts that completely challenged the accepted timeline. Irem quickly became your life’s work, and AMMUT snatched you up as soon as you appeared on their radar. You were happy to oblige.
          • You were top of your class, and did all sorts of top-secret operations in the Middle East against terrorist organizations. AMMUT needed trustworthy military operatives, and your government put you at the top of the list. This is all more than a little weird, but you know you’re doing good work.
          • You’re a survivor of an attack that the media never covered. This thing strolled into town and started unmaking people with a few guttural syllables. Fire shot from its fingertips, and centipedes poured from your mother’s mouth as she died. AMMUT took you in as a security risk, and you’ve made yourself useful. You won’t rest until your personal vendetta is satisfied.
          • You were a spy, tasked with infiltrating a number of radical groups in the Middle East. After your last mission, you were transferred to the jurisdiction of the UN and sent to infiltrate a group far weirder than any Islamist cell. You saw shit you couldn’t explain and dutifully reported it, then a strike team swooped in and gunned every one of them down. Now you’re doing it again, and you still don’t know what the fuck is going on.
          Stereotypes:
          • Null Mysteriis: A bunch of scientists trying to explain all the weird shit that happens in the world. Some of them are too skeptical to be of use, while others believe anything you can find inside a cheap paperback from an occult bookshop. The few in the middle are perfect hires.
          • The Loyalists of Thule: These guys seem to be amateur archaeologists and fringe weirdoes, but they seem oddly sensible about the whole thing. I bet a few of their private libraries have information we’d die for, but the bastards always want to bargain for it.
          • Ascending Ones: Strike teams have run across a few natives who have access to abilities like the mummies do, but they work against the interests of Irem. I don’t know who they are, and I don’t like that they’ve been in this game a lot longer than we have.
          • Task Force VALKYRIE: These poor nutjobs. They’re like us, but underfunded and chasing shadows. More than a few have no idea we exist. One of them told me he killed a vampire once. A fucking vampire. Can you believe that?

                      Sorry for the delay! Friends came over to play Demon: the Descent. Back to business!

                      Divisions:
                      Project AMMUT loosely classifies operatives by specialty. Cells are organized by need, but tend to have at least one operative from each division whenever possible, so as to cover all the bases.
                      • ARCHINT (Archaeological Intelligence) covers anyone who studies Irem or the mummies themselves. There’s plenty of internal conflict between those who want an academic study of a currently unknown civilization and those that try to understand what really does seem to be magic. The information both groups have to give are equally crucial.
                      • HUMINT (Human Intelligence) has two faces, one pleasant and one sinister. Many HUMINT operatives simply serve as translators (many operations take place in nations that speak Arabic or Farsi) for their cell, and do an invaluable service to their teams. Others work as infiltrators for cults, gathering intelligence from the inside at high risk to themselves. More than a few of this latter group have met untimely ends at the hands of a mummy’s servant.
                      • At some point, the intelligence-gathering has to stop and the guns have to come out. That’s where Wetworks comes in. These men (and a few women) are drawn from the armed forces of the United Nations security council countries, and are elite in their field. Most of them are smart enough to not ask question and are familiar with the horrors of war. The turnover rate is high, between those KIA and those who mentally break under pressure.
                      Status:
                      As Project AMMUT is a joint task force, there are no particular standards for rank. Civilians and non-civilians mingle, and there chain of command only goes so far as "the people who give orders" and "the people who follow orders." Team leaders are assigned based largely on seniority; this tends not to be an issue, as the incompetent individuals rarely last long. Veterans are given more resources for their missions; as a UN-backed project, there is a sizable budget.​​

                      Status 1: You've been brought into the project and given a briefing on just what we're dealing with here. Gain a specialty in Academics (Irem) or Occult (Mummies).
                      Status 3: You've shown that you're worth your salt. You work well under pressure, you've survived hazardous missions, and you know full and well the consequences of failure. You have your own independent budget for operations, equal to Resources 2.
                      Status 5: You've seen mummies up close and you've watched more cults fall apart than most. Your job is largely paperwork at this point; your expertise makes putting you in the field a gamble, but when AMMUT chooses to, you go in with the best of the best. The project leads know better than to bother you with busywork; you gain the Staff merit at 3 dots.

                      Remi. she/her. game designer.

                                  Well, LDI seems to be shaping up for a Full Conspiracy treatment in Hunter: Mortal Remains, and based on their section in M:tC I'm assuming their endowment will basically be super-serums and medicines enhanced with Sekhem. Stuff like spray bandage that insta-heals lethal damage.

                                  So, ideally you'd want to go a different direction while still keeping on the mummy magic/Sekhem thread, but also trying not to step on other existing conspiracies. Which is a bit hard sometimes...


                                  However, SEEING as they've named themselves after the Devourer, and seeing as all the canon conspiracies have some dark secret at the top, how about corrupted Relics?
                                  Step 1> Seize Relics from Mummies
                                  Step 2> Bombard Relics with "Exotic Radiation" (read: Darkest Magicks) to "Reset the Occult Matrix" (corrupt them).
                                  Step 3> Relic now has several new properties, loses some old ones, and basically just functions to mess up the Risen.

                                  Quick and dirty rules.
                                  The relic loses it Beneficial power, but it still has it's curse, and effective dot rating.
                                  The Curse now not only effects mummies of the relics favored guild, it's actually WORSE for them.
                                  The relics tainted Sekhem calls out more strongly, though it does feel off. Mummies can sense the relic as if it were a guild relic of it's dot rating, or if it actually is a relic of their guild's favored type, as if it were 2 dots stronger (even if this produces the confusing result of sensing an effective Level 6 or 7 relic), but also feel a deep unease in it's presence.
                                  Each such corrupted relic should also have some added power that messes with mummies, perhaps a single reversed affinity, that the wielder my inflict on Sekhem empowered beings. Activate with Willpower?

                                  Lastly, the process corrupts the Sekhem into some sort of Devourer spawned foul unlife energy. A Mummy foolish enough to ignore their instincts, and it's overclocked curse, and to try to draw Sekhem from the corrupt relic is going to be unhappy. Each Corrupt Sekhem they drain ravages their Sahu, unbinding the magic that keeps them alive. Instead of Gaining Sekhem, they take one point of Aggravated Damage per merit dot of the corrupted relic. And they STILL have to make the descent rolls for destroying the Relic.

                                  Meanwhile, Shaunkhsen who EAT a cursed relic find that it does them good. It can either heal damage to their body, grant them pillar points equal to it's rating, or grant them a single Sekhem.

                                              So, short hand.

                                              Curse: Same. Double+ effect on guild that would normally have affinity.
                                              Power: Costs 1+ Willpower to activate. Targets only beings with Sekhem. If the item would normally grant some sort of mechanical bonus, invert it. Any +X to rolls becomes -X, Lowering target number instead raises target number, if it grants 8 or 9-again instead LOSE 10-again. If it would grant something like pillars it instead removes them. ETC. If the item has a unique non-stat power, then it requires more GM adjudication.
                                              Relic Dots == Merit Dots
                                              Counts as 2 dots higher for normal affinity guild to sense. Counts as Dots of affinity type for all guilds to find.

                                              Also, addendum to the list of Sekhem possessing beings. Anyone currently under the effects of a Last Dynasty International pharmaceutical that has been enhanced with Sekhem is also a viable target.
