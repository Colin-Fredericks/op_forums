                                    Hi everyone! Glad to see you're excited about the Kickstarter and are discussing the setting. Popping in briefly to mention that Hunter 2E does retain the toolkit mentality from First Edition. If you are interested in playing a 1E compact/conspiracy or want to create your own? You can do that with the rules in the Storyteller chapter. Ultimately, it's your decision what you're excited to play at your table. I do hope we blast through more stretch goals, because Tending the Flame has some awesome potential for Hunter 2E!

                                    I also wanted to talk about Mortal Remains. MR is considered a First Edition supplement. The patch provided does provide a rules update to 2E as it was designed. As I mentioned in The Story Told interview, Hunter 2E provides more rules that clearly distinguish the difference between someone who makes a commitment to the hunt versus their first brush with the supernatural. Besides the Endowments redesign, hunters also get a Tactics update, Teamwork, Touchstones, and more! As such, Hunter 2E is the current and most up-to-date iteration of the rules.

                                    Thanks! Enjoy the new conspiracy! We've also got two new compacts--one of which focuses on cryptids designed by Allen Turner!

                                            Thanks, Primordial newcomer. The history of compacts and conspiracies began to change when the Dark Eras line was added, much like DE affected Geist, too. All of a sudden global conspiracies either needed to have more history than they did or they had to be newer; compacts could (but not always) be temporary due to historical events/tragedies and changes over time. Knowing this, I wanted Hunter 2E to be a corebook that reflected that change while not fixating on Dark Eras. There were tons of compacts/conspiracies created as First Edition progressed, and I felt it made sense they'd be absorbed or dissolved because there are more denizens now in the Chronicles of Darkness than there was when Hunter 1E debuted.

                                            I do want to reiterate that not all of the supplemental Hunter organizations are affected by this. I tried to be careful while also providing rules to create compacts and conspiracies in the Storyteller's chapter so you can play the group you want. The example compact, Ashwood Abbey, was re-introduced for this reason. If you want to play any compact or conspiracy, I absolutely encourage you to do so. At no point do I ever want to say: "You can't play this!" Or, "Every group needs to resonate with you." Neither of those things are true. Hunter 2E is your game to use as you see fit.

                                            And yes, SWORN is the compact that deals with cryptids. We had fans who wanted Native compacts historically in Dark Eras, and I was lucky enough to find a Native designer who was excited to craft a modern compact. SWORN is Allen's vision. Compacts can be inspired by cultural needs--which is a very human thing to happen in a world full of shadows and darkness.

                                            I hope this helps and I appreciate the discussion. Not everyone is familiar with the Dark Eras line, I know, but as the developer I wanted to weigh my decisions for the new corebook. Thanks for listening!
