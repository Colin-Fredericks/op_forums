            I suppose I should say these things here.

            ✦ Why are they called Rampant/Otodo? They actually come off as rather calm in this write up.
            ✦ Why do they take the same path as the Lucifuge? There's not really any reason given for why they turn into monster hunters instead of just using their ogreblood to wreck shit. It just goes right from "they were the children of a fishing village that mated with oni" to "they fight their dark heritage".
            ✦ I'm not sure if you just messed up the formatting or what, but the sidebar flows right back into The Enemy on Beasts and Heroes.
            ✦ Aren't there several Japanese myths of non-evil oni? You don't get the cool clubs, but why go oni? Why not just yokai? That's about as generic as "demon" anyway. Or Akuma.
            ✦ Is it a new thing that Iron does Aggravated to Changelings? It typically just cuts through Contracts, but otherwise does no increased damage.
            ✦ How do the Otodo keep track of genealogical records? They're constantly traveling, but able to keep tabs on when someone they knocked up gets pregnant in a preindustrial society?
            ✦ Why do they even travel the Lonesome Road in the first place? One sample Hunter is someone born a "legacy Hunter", but elsewhere we're told that they're rare and solitary. How do they keep extensive notes on who they've slept with when they're also travelers? Are they carrying around huge "little black books"? The "keeping the lineage" aspect is neat, but it doesn't really feel like it's handled well. Wouldn't it be better for them to operate out of a central compound or something like that and take on wives or husbands? Those non-blood members of the clan could even provide support and aid.
            ✦ Why do they not work together more than a single hunt? For what reason do they feel that it's better to go alone? The Lucifuge are a modern group spread out across the entire world and there are only six-hundred sixty-six of them to deal with an untold number of demons and L'Enfant Terrible; why does a small family of ogreblooded stay away from each other instead of working together?
            ✦ Why are the Malleus Maleficarum a power in Japan during an era after the Christians were massacred? Why would they be a power at all? It doesn't make sense to include them when there are so many better Westerrn Conspiracies to involve; the Acheron Shipping & Trading Company, for instance. Even working in the first contact between the Lucifuge and Otodo would be interesting. Yasuke the Black Samurai bringing the Ascending Ones to Japan would be more reasonable.

            Now for the powers. I realize that you're not fond of "white room combat", but a lot of these really don't feel very thought out. I'm going to try to break down as well as I can why; I might even do a playtest of them, though, because issues aside it still sounds like stupid fun to play demon ninjas in Hunter.
            ✦ Seitokuken Why are their powers gained so differently than the Lucifuge? They don't need to work for it, they just wait six hours and take 2L, which is a pretty steep penalty considering that's going to stick around for four days. Hunters who are out of commission for four days are not very useful Hunters. The burned veins is neat, though.
            ✦ Does Seitokuken not threaten Integrity directly the way that failed Castigation powers do? "They might be a Breaking Point" without listing which ones would be a Breaking Point is also kind of half-assing it. Does Kaibutsu cause a Breaking Point (In either the Otodo or onlookers)? Is Shinigami Buki worse than stabbing someone? Saying that without elaborating on it is unhelpful and gives no real guidance. You've said "I think you should do something a particular way, but I won't say what that way is".
            ✦ Dorei-Sei is unfun. It doesn't really make for an interesting game if a character can face off against the demon they've been tracking and say "Kill yourself". It's anticlimactic and so powerful that it invalidates every other ability. The other Birthrights exist as back up if your Integrity is too low to make a command or if you fight something that isn't ephemeral. The fact that Integrity of PCs tends to be in the 4-6 range is the only real downside. Does using it against Spirits count as a Breaking Point? What about Thralls? It's also an incredibly wonky ability; you roll to activate it and as long as you get one success they're commanded unless they can Contest against your dots in Seitokuken.
            ✦ Hannya feels like it would be better off as full Aura Perception 2e style (like Auspex's) as opposed to what it is. It actually seems kind of weak.
            ✦ Kaibutsu is at once devastatingly powerful and incredibly underwhelming. You turn into a giant oni. You get +2 Size and Health. You get 2/1 armour. Your hands and teeth are lethal weapons. You can add between ● and ●●●●● dots to your physical attributes. You can--rather expensively--heal damage. But also all of your clothes are destroyed and you freak out mortals and show yourself to be a monster. Unfortunately monsters are unlikely to wait for you to spend several turns powering up. Even a character with high Integrity is likely to take at least two turns Hulking out.
            ✦ Kenshi as written does not make any sense. I'm not sure what it means by "seitokuken dots act as the monster's spirit Rank for the purpose of determining the ban level". That seems like characters with less Birthright are better at Kenshi. I also think that this power should refer to banes; Bans are mystical compulsions, while banes are the thing you smack them with until they die.
            ✦ Kigo is another of those needlessly powerful powers. The damage being half-Birthright seems a bit much, and it feels like you intend for it to involve a touch attack. It does a ludicrous amount of damage to vampires and the undead, to the point that it's better to slap fight them until they're covered in だまれ. Assuming they don't die before the third hiragana. The ability to track seems more useful when used to mark allies than it does with the Undead, but there it is.
            ✦ Shinigami Buki This power is also quite unusual. You roll once and then mechanically you do nothing for the next X+Y turns where X is your successes and Y is your Birthright. Presumably the demon will be trying to escape at this point. Is this an Instant action, or is it Reflexive each turn? Why bother even rolling, why not just "as a Reflexive action spend a point of Willpower and over a number of turns equal to Seitokuken the victim loses one Willpower per turn, and ephemeral beings take 1 lethal damage"? Then it would be more explicit that you can devote your actions proper to grappling.
            ✦ Shonetsu Jogoku is another of those ridiculously strong powers. A four damage weapon is nothing to sneeze at, though for most characters that's not even going to be as good as the weapon they all carry. The range is also so short that it might as well not even be a thrown weapon.
