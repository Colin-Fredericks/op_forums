                            Not really; but off the top of my head; an Endowed Compact of Gandalf-like mentor figures who specialise in divination, illusion magic, and astral travel. Their goals are, in no particular order.

                            1) Assist Heroes in the hunt.
                            2) Teach Heroes how to actually be heroes.
                            3) Ensure that the public believes in the idea of heroes.

                            In more details.

                            1) They assist heroes by essentially following them around, providing cryptic advice, and secretly working behind the scenes. They understand exactly what the Beast does to a Hero's mind, and while they're sympathetic, or at least they tell each other that they're sympathetic. They also say that they can't heal the Hero so they just play along. For example, rather than using their connections to buy guns for the hero and his followers, they might let slip that arms smugglers will be on the docks tonight. (This would be a lot easier to define once we know more about how heroes work)

                            Incidently you ever wondered why Gandalf can't solve problems with his magic. That's why they have illusion powers; it's great for making heroes think they're a powerful wizard shooting lightning bolts but it's really just a pistol. If they fought a beast themselves, well they could use a few tricks like fake walls or duplicating themselves, but in terms of actually hurting the beast they just have a gun. Heroes have, whatever they get.


                            2) If a Hero thinks he is perfectly capable of judging morality and is willing to do; it's important that they can walk the walk as well as talk the talk. If a Beast is lairng in an orphanage and the Hero has been eying up some petrol cans; find or disguise yourself as an beautiful woman who was fired from the orphanage for trying to stop the Beast and come to the Hero in tears. An instant Quest to rescue the orphans, hopefully next time he'll remember it's Heroic to rescue the kids without prompting, if not Heroes rarely get wise to the beautiful quest giver ruse. (Network Zero has noticed how often Heroes finding themselves rescuing beautiful young philosophy students and keeping them in their entourage).



                            3) I'm getting the impression that a Hero's powers come from the collective unconscious of some sort. I imagine the Compact frequently visit The Hero Archetype in the Temenos and make detailed notes of his Behaviour and then they'll call their contacts and make adjustments. If the Hero is too willing to sacrifice faceless extras in order to rescue The Love Interest they'll make sure to put in more scenes where the Hero rescues nameless extras and weaken The Love Interest's hold by writing more one dimensional characters who hang off the Hero's arm and look awestruck. Contrariwise if the Hero is ignoring the Love Interest they'll do the opposite; because it's too useful a way to manipulate real heroes.


                            I think the subgroups will be:

                            The Wizard - specialities in behind the scenes work; rather than push the Hero down the path they want, they pave the roads themselves. They create clues for the Hero to solve, and situations for the Hero to react to.

                            The Sidekick - they get hands on with the Hero and use psychology to manipulate. They might become the Hero's number two and carefully control what information he receives, pose as a love interest who's impressed by moral behaviour, or a damsel who keeps getting herself captured.

                            The Chief - Hero! Turn in your badge now. The Chief poses as the Hero's legitimate superior. If the Hero is a police officer he'll impersonate the chief. He might pretend to be the leader of a secret society of monster hunters who knows the prophecy and insists the Hero works for him now. The Chief uses his minions and resources to support the Hero, but feigned incompetence and reverse psychology is what keeps the Hero on the right road.


                            I don't think this group can be written until after the Hero's psychology and powers have been revealed. But for something that I thought up on the spot I think it's a very cool idea.
