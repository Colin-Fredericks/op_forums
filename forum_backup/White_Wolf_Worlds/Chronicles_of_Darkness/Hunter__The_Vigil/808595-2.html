                                Ok, this is an infant of an idea, but what if Benediction wielding Hunters have occurred individually throughout the Middle Ages. Just some faithful individuals manifest a few of these Holy Gifts (explicit ban on anyone wielding a Conspiracy's Endowment from manifesting these additional Endowments, but at Storyteller discretion, players may purchase Benedictions for other characters, but usually only starting the game manifesting one no matter how many points they've invested [that just improves your roll], learning more costs experience and in-game comes through visions).

                                Anyway, there's a powerful elder Lancea + Sanctum vampire obsessed with these miracles and trying to eat or embrace as many "Saints" as possible. That vampire has a bound ghoul in the Church who he's charged with turning the Inquisition on these "Saints" to collect them for him. That ghoul is of course Cardinal Ambrogio Baudolino.

                                In the "now" of the era, the vampire is being delivered these hunters by Baudolino still, so it can be via PCs (with help from the Lady of Milan) that he's able to create a separate underground to save others and move against his master.
