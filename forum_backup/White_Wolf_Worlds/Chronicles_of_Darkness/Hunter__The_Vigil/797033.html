              So for the core point, gloablising Compacts.


              To me, I think that saying here's what the Swedish Branch of the Long Night looks like, and creating an entirely separate Protestant faith based Compact that only exists in Sweden and fills the same thematic niche are equally good.

              So basically, I wouldn't sweat too much if a Compact feels very geographically localised and I certainly wouldn't want Compacts redesigned to make them more global. In that case I think a separate organisation would be superior to diluting a Compact.

              Network Zero is definitely global and I'm just as interested to hear how the USA and South Korean members interact as I am to hear about the South Korean members. The Union is so specific that there's probably entirely separate organisations filling it's niche of "Average Joes protecting their community" for different parts of America. Forget the Union in London, I want to know what the Union in inner city Baltimore is like. (Assuming the Ascending Ones aren't around for some reason).


              One thing I've always wanted is for each Hunter Compact and Conspiracy to start with a couple of paragraphs explicitly stating it's Theme. I think that would help quite a lot for globalisation.

              Once you see that the Maiden's Blood Sisterhood's theme is "diplomatic academic occultists defending the university and students"* written down like that it really gets the creative juices flowing.

              Just looking at that I'm already imagining their English equivalent as an ancient Oxbridge drinking society, with kooky traditions, outdated attitudes, and a more freemasonic flavour to their occultism instead of the neopagan vibe I get from the Maiden's Blood Sisterhood.


              *I'm torn between saying this is the MBS' theme, or that they have two complimentary themes.

              What are your top 3 favorite existing compacts and why?
              • Null Mysteriis - I like the fact their goal is non-violent, and I like the science angle. I hope in 2E they're less likely to jump to pesudoscientific theories and more wordcount is given to Science-as-a-methodology
              • Maiden's Blood Sisterhood - The diplomatic and intellectual approach appeals to me, but really I just want to play the charachter in their sample artwork (with some minor magical abilities, not enough to qualify as non-human in any meaningful sense - something like Second Sight's Thamaturges)
              • Ama San - It's bursting with good ideas; but I'd say the bit that really does it for me is the fact their power comes from growing up together in a genuinely supportive environment, with underwater hunting coming second. I really hope the roles will get updated from the draft I saw though.

              What are your thoughts on globalization and locations for the compacts you selected?

              I think that Null Mysteriis are already global. They're founded in London, but Paris is mentioned and they show up in the Philadelphia appendix. I'm fine with this, the real life scientific community has strong global links, and it makes sense that NM would be a singular organisation with a worldwide membership.

              The Maiden's Blood Sisterhood - I think I'd rather keep them as unique to one location. As compacts go, they feel small and intimate and I think that should stay. I would like to see groups who share themes with the MBS around the world, but from an in charachter perspective they're unrelated groups.

              Ama San - Ignoring the fact they're an Edo era compact; I genuinely think they can't work as a globalised group. MBS feel small and intimate; the Ama San are built entirely around the concept. If you don't know all the other Hunters from birth, it's not the Ama San. But small villages defined by a close relationship to the supernatural can work anywhere, consider Wall from Neil Gaimen's Stardust as another example. It's nothing like the Ama San, and yet it really is another manifestation of that same theme.

              How married are you to the history of the compacts?

              Is the unwritten implication of this question "what do you feel about rewriting the history to make a group more globalised?".

              If it is I'd be against that. I'd rather new groups are introduced as separate manifestations of the theme. However I'm fine with touching up a compact if it makes them even better at being their 1E self.

              For example I wouldn't to see the MBS rewritten to justify them as a more global organisation, but touching up their history to give them a bit more occultism in their DNA; a splash of neo-paganism, a bit more room for minor supernatural abilities like Thamaturges. I'd love that.

              We have compacts formed around

              A new theme? Without a doubt Exploration! The WoD has some lovely otherworlds and a Compact based around exploring one of them (my suspension of disbelief would start to crack if a Compact was active in multiple other worlds and I do think Exploration fits better at Tier 2 than 3) would be awesome.

              My preference would be exploring the Underworld, because it's the one where the laws of physics are the closest to normal, it has a lot of relevance to mundane life, and interesting places to visit.

              The obvious location would be Paris, because of the catacombs. Naturally different groups with different otherworlds to explore could exist.

                      I believe applying a global view on the Vigil requires a very measured approach, due to a certain feature of the setting: While it varies from Compact to Compact (and Conspiracy to Conspiracy), these organizations straddle the line between being actual entities with their own history, and being iconic examples of their type. The Union, for instance, has a historical background that is mostly secondary to their core theme - communities banding together against common threats. Their history is vague enough to allow greater flexibility, but as a trade-off they are less of an living organization and more of a template. Groups like it would crop up in multiple places but calling them by the same name would be off-putting and inelegant, even within the same country. I'm thinking over making a certain split enforced mainly by the layout of the book: Firstly, The Union described not as an organization, but as a certain template and then, for example, the "Rust-belt Grapevine" as a specific group with it's own history, which may or may not be on good terms (or even know about) with their Union-y counterparts.

                      From another angle, there is this: The way the game-line is designed, every Compact also has a monopoly on a certain method to the Vigil, which can feed into the following problem: A possible pitfall of expanding the Compacts abroad is giving the impression they are all there is to the Vigil, instead of just being examples of Hunter organizations. ​The text would have to underscore how many and varied Hunter Compacts are. The Stereotypes sections are somewhat at fault (it's more of an observation than an actual complaint), since they only mention the other known groups. Clearly, that's what a Stereotype section is meant to do, but it helps forget that the is more to CofD-World than just what's written up. Also, since globalization is a relatively new phenomenon, multiple Compacts would have developed in greater isolation. With the advent of globalization, any expanding Compacts would likely come across their local counterparts, with varying results. I believe that the text and its layout need to help delineate between Compacts and Conspiracies as templates and as specific organizations. It would be greatly helped by including build-your-own-Compact tools.

                          Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
                          The Union is so specific that there's probably entirely separate organisations filling it's niche of "Average Joes protecting their community" for different parts of America. Forget the Union in London, I want to know what the Union in inner city Baltimore is like. (Assuming the Ascending Ones aren't around for some reason).
                          I just wanted to say something about this. I worked with unions in Baltimore for years and talked about the "the Union" Compact with a fellow organizer and how it would look in Baltimore. We both kind of laughed about it. Reading through the Compact history it tries to build some sort of network between the different unions in the U.S. which is there to an extent, but there is also a lot of bad blood. In Baltimore alone remnants of the vicious split between SEIU and UNITE HERE still present in local union politics not to mention numerous company unions working against more radical unions. In this country this story is played out in almost every city to varying degrees with battle lines being drawn over organizing strategies, membership, and power. For my friend and me, this left us thinking what Union is this supposed to be because there are definitely some we know that would be just as willing to work with monsters. We ended thinking that it would be better to make a specific Union that organized intensely, but also trained its members in combat and forced them to look after their neighbors.

                          My point of bringing this up is not to just focus on the problems in the Union's development, but to point out how a single Compact can be hurt by being too inclusive towards their "theme". That being said I think certain Compacts definitely work better with vague theoretical boundaries (Network Zero). On the question about globalization, I don't know if the answer is to find a way to rewrite Compacts that already exist that don't work everywhere to all of a sudden work internationally. Perhaps working off of the question about themes the focus of globalizing Compacts should be on developing Compacts in other nations that willingly work together because their themes align enough. For The Union, you could have a Compact in South Africa based off the Shack Dwellers Movement.

                                      • What are your top 3 favorite existing compacts and why? These can be pulled from any book that’s been published thus far, including compacts that are no longer around.
                                        • The Union, first and foremost. I strongly sympathise with its straightforward ideas. No secret agendas, no conspiracies, just genuine good intentions. Sure, they have a dark side, like everything else in the CofD, but they are flaws I enjoy as a player and storyteller.
                                        • The Maiden's Blood Sisterhood is amazing. I only learnt of it today looking for the full list of Compacts (I needed a third choice), but I just love it for how progressive it is. I can already imagine a bunch of different concepts for it.
                                        • Network Zero. Another group with idealistic, straightforward good intentions. Again, they have flaws, but I'm okay with that.
                                      • What are your thoughts on globalization and locations for the compacts you selected?
                                        • The Union can easily be expanded to Latin America via the history of workers' movements throughout it. From Argentina's Peron presidencies to Brazil's Landless Workers, etc.
                                        • The Maiden's Blood Sisterhood's core concept (women banding together to defend themselves from the predators) can easily exist in different countries throughout the world, especially if the Compact doesn't do a diaspora, but instead the same concept pops up in different parts of the world and then they come in contact and share info and resources.
                                        • The article already covered Network Zero.
                                      • How married are you to the history of the compacts? Have those details come up in actual play for your group?
                                        • Don't really follow official fluff unless I absolutely love it. I tend to treat history as a suggestion at best. :P
                                        • Nope, not for any of them.
                                      • We have compacts formed around playing styles such as belief, hedonism, research, atonement, communication, defense, vengeance, hunting specific monster types such as vampires, etc. If you had your pick of “a” new style of play that’d be fun to play, what would be the theme and why? Where would it be based? Please try to avoid creating/naming new compacts, as I do want to reserve that honor for my future team.
                                        • Really want to see a compact like the Maiden's Blood Sisterhood, but dedicated to LGBT people. There are thematic parallels that can be made between the Hunter existence and being part of a gender or sexual minority. Secret-keeping, facing the idea that your loved ones might not understand you, the relief of finding someone you can share experiences with, and so on.
                                        • The theme could be "support". The new Compact could support individual cells regardless of how they choose to go about the hunt, whether they take up arms and kill predators, or they just gather intel, or they try to negotiate. The group, since it has had to accommodate all sorts of diverse identities with not a lot in common, would have learnt by now to articulate its strength as a facilitator and mediator, with the flaw that it obviously lacks focus and isn't terribly good at any one aspect of the Hunt.
                                        • The group could be based on San Francisco, to get the obvious location out of the way; or perhaps more interestingly, Greenwich Village (where the Stonewall Riots took place). Basing it on New York City would serve to reinforce the Compact's plurality and eclecticness, since NYC boasts a great deal of cultural diversity.

                                              I'm not sure if my comment got eaten, or if they were just so long they are taking longer to load, so I'm including them here. I really like the Korea idea that Monica talked about for Network Zero and think that will add a very interesting aspect to the game.

                                              Does the yet unrevealed First Nations Compact I commissioned for Dark Eras count?

                                              Of what we know -

                                              1. Favourite Compacts
                                              a) Ahl Al-Jabal - My GF is Ismaili and I have a degree in Islamic History, so this one is close to my heart in many ways. Ahl Al-Jabal is freaking perfect. It nails Ismaili beliefs and traditions, is a positive, anti-terrorist Muslim group, and get's the history of Nizari State pretty much spot on, which is rare thanks to the hashashin myths. Ismailis also have large presence in my home of Calgary, our mayor is one, and many of my friends from high school are too.

                                              b) Bear Lodge - Another compact that is local for me and can be found in a lot of the areas I lived in as a kid.

                                              c) Third spot is a bit tougher, but I'm rather fond of Utopia Now. They have a neat way of interpreting GM Angels and Demons, plus they have a sweet Bioshock aesthetic going.

                                              2. Globalization and location - Before I start this, I'd like to be on record as loving the idea of Network Zero having a South Korean dimension. I think that's a logical progression, and it adds new territory to the map of CofD. Great job! I've tried to take some inspiration from that and continue on with my own thoughts below.

                                              a) Ahl Al-Jabal is pretty easy to globalize, to the point that if globalization of Hunter 2e is a goal, i think it should automatically be included as a compact. They exist anywhere that there is a significant population of Ismailis, which means you could find them anywhere in the Middle East, Asia proper, North America, Europe, or Africa. In particular, Dubai, Dar Es-Salam, Syria, Northern India/Pakistan, and Calgary come to my mind as locations that could be used in conjunction with the Ahl Al-Jabal for the Slasher Chronicles. I'd also imagine that they'd have moved into former Soviet territories such as Tajikistan in the last 20-25 years.

                                              b) Not as much globalization here, although perhaps there have been some attempts restore relations with the Russian Federation by setting up Bear Lodges in Siberia? There could be a somewhat friendly competition between the Asian and North American chapters. An African "Big Game/Safari" chapter is another possibility, although that runs the risk of overlap with the Ashwood Abbey. Then again, that could lead to some not so friendly competition.

                                              c) Utopia Now has an interesting way of globalizing. They were originally based out of San Fran and want to build their paradise city in the middle of the Pacific. What if they became kind of a reverse Pacific Rim, seeding chapters all throughout the Pacific nations. Obviously, they would be drawn to places like Tokyo, Sydney, Hong Kong, and Seoul to find Institutional Demons, but what if they went for older angels as well? Things hiding in the volcanos and the ruins? If someone gets a beed on an institutional demon hiding in Ankor Wat, that would draw Utopia now like flies, so I could see them filling positions in Singapore, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Brunei and Indonesia just as easily. After all, Utopia Now is a start up, but in an increasing globalized and connected world, even a start up can have an international presence. Finally, and I think this is the neat part, Utopia Now seems like a logical compact to touch an area that hasn't been in any CofD product so far: Oceania. Fiji, Tonga, Samoa and the other Polynesian Islands are basically virgin territory that could have some interesting aspect to explore. I think there's a neat opportunity for overlap here.

                                              3. History - I'm a continuity nerd, so in general I'd prefer to see an expansion of past history rather than a rewriting. For Ahl Al-Jabal, please don't touch the history we've been given. It was expertly crafted. Rather, summarize it, then tell us what happened to the compact after they got kicked out of Persia by the Mongols. For the Bear Lodge and Utopia Now, I'm less tied to it.

                                              4. New Play Styles - I'll add my vote for a diplomatic/political compact. That's an area the game is lacking in, and I could totally see a compact trying to pull a Bismark on monsters, negotiating them so they attack each other instead of the humans.
