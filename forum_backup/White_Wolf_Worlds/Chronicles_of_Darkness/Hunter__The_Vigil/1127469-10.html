        I thought it might be that, but when I looked at the activity section I couldn't see anything. Turns out activity dosn't cover subfolders.

        You used to write science fiction and fantasy stories. Even sold a couple of short stories to Asimov's back in the day. Your career never really went anywhere but now your work saves lives. So maybe it was worth it.
        A bit basic, but no real problem.

        That white supremacist you punched at that rally was some kind of monster. A literal mythical one according to those older protesters. While it felt good and got his speech cancelled what they claim to do sounds like it'll be a more permanent solution.
        Yeah, I see what you mean by "too on the nose" and I agree with your concerns.

        Way too political, and completely out of charachter for Ride. They're descended from Freedom Riders not Black Panthers, and their modus operandi is rehabilitating Beasts (At least, that's how they see Beastcrafting), the story used to defeat the first Beast they met was one of forgiveness. Being the first to escalate to violence is not the Ride's style. They're more of a Daryl Davis kind of anti-racist.

        You've been called an SJW, a feminazi, reverse racist, among other less fit for print things for you vlog series on the socio-political implications of various aspects of pop culture. Some say your work is trivial but you've learned the power of narrative first hand.
        Also way too on the nose. And again way out of charachter for the Ride. The Ride are Christian and proud of it, which means that they're often conservatives. The insults listed here are directed at progressives.

        Also worrying about the socio-political implications of fiction is more of a Llannerch Athrawon thing, though LA are more interested in whether fiction promotes teamwork or individual heroes than racism, sexism, or just about anything people are arguing about now. Ride to Damascus care more about telling their story then worrying about what stories other people tell.

        Anyway. Here's some replacements.

        Kids these days have no time for Jesus, no time for anything but spaceships and explosions. But to you the important thing is not a 2000 year old carpenter, it's the message he gave. If kids only listen when that message comes from a wise alien you'll give them a wise alien. You're good at your job too, the Christianity is subtle, and mixed with enough action, plot, and characterisation to sell. That's why your talecrafting is powerful, and it often comes from an angle the Beasts you fight won't expect.

        Hating white suprematists was easy, forgiving them was hard. But you found that if you reached out to them, listened to their story and told them yours, then slowly, one by one they'd turn in their robes. Then you met her. She wasn't scared of her neighbourhood changing. She had no trauma in her past. Her parents never taught her prejudice. She just liked to put people down. That was when you met Ride to Damascus, they told you what she was and how to reach out to her.

        You aren't sure if the Beast wanted to help you or just wanted to feed. But when clinical depression pushes you into a rut, using fear and nightmares to try and push you back out only makes things worse. Fortunately Ride to Damascus was on the Beast's tail, and they stuck around to help you afterwords. So many authors think mental illnesses are easy to cure, and Talecrafting can turn that misconception into reality. Out of gratitude you stuck with the Ride, and you always have an eye open for another victim to help.

        Your life comes straight out of a story. You Talecraft for everything: To find a place to sleep, to meet a local guide, to find a Beast to oppose, and at the end of every chapter you ride off into the sunset. You might not be a faerie, but with so much Talecrafting and so many cruel twists of fate it's getting hard to find where the stories end and reality begins. Are you turning into somthing new? The first avatar of a new mythos to replace the Hero's Journey? Or are you just diving too deep into forces you don't understand?
