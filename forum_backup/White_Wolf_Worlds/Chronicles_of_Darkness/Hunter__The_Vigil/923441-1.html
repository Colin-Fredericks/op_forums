                                                    well, I've wrote that big and awesome post about To the Strongest with hunter organizations from Greece, Persia, India, Mesopotamia and Phoenicia, but the forums has turned into Ammut mode and devoured it whole, leaving me with only the sum up of the Neolithic Era...

                                                    Lighting the Candle/Primal State of Grace
                                                    - The Wolf Hunter Cult (the Nibiru, Hunter Caste. Those wolf masked dancers travel the wilderness both physical and spiritual and bind dangerous gods to deathlike sleep while maintaining harmony between the worlds)
                                                    -The Heart Breakers (Knights of St George, barbarian warriors who live outside the community. They believe the Omphalos Stones and the Wise to be destructive influence over the community and fight their influence, and by doing so they drew the attention of something from the wilds)
                                                    -The Shield and Spear (Aegis Kai Doru, Potter Caste. They are crafters and artificers who serve as the Wise's guardians both in the community and on the wilderness travels. Thanks to a special rite using the Omphalos Stones, they become ember bearers with the ability to use magical relics no mortal man should)
                                                    -The Marauders (Promethean Brotherhood, fallen communities who lost their Wise and were given a special rite by an unknown force. Thanks to the ritual, they can steal the Wisdom of others and protect their own villiges)
                                                    - The Forest Travelers (Museum Secretorum, Trader Caste. Those individuals risk their body and soul by travelling into the wilds and search for exotic resources and raw materials for their own communities and Wise and trade them with each other)
                                                    -The Grave Stricken (either Cainte Heresy or pre-Kigal Ebony Fangs, for Kigal would be destroyed around the time of Irem. Outcasts who were touched or harmed by the dead, making them to be treated as dead by themselves. They are the ones to preform burial rites who keep loved ones from returning, and deal with them when they do)
                                                    Potential Plot Hooks- Romohals, Nimrod in the Tower, Mistress of Honey (either Bird or some Underworld horror), packs of Cannibal Slashers
