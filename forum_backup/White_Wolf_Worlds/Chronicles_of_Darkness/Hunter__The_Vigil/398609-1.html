                Ama San- Sea Song (0-00000)
                While the Sea Whistle is the most common technique for luring the monsters of the deep, the Sea Song is a more delicate and powerful ability. Taught by the great Ryujin, the Sea Song can effect the sea and sky to calm storms and strength the waves. The sea Song has a number of uses- the first is enhancing the ability of the Sea Whistle, represented by adding the dots in the Endowment to the dicepool. That effect may influence even no sea monsters, but you reduce 2 dice from the roll. Second, it allows the hunter to control the weather- by rolling Endowment+Presence and paying 1 WP point per dot in the Endowment (you can reduce your dicepool to save WP), the user may either make the weather more stormy or more calm. Consider a "bright day" as 0, "rainy" as 1, "warm" as -1, "stormy" as 2 and "heat wave" as -2. you can't move between more states in the same time than your dots in the Endowment. Exceptional Success gives the WP back and Dramatic Failure turns the Weather the other way around. Finally, you can buy Dread Powers which are connected to sea monsters- but no more than one Power per dot in the Endowment.

                Azusa Miko- Kagura (0-00000, special)
                the bow shamans of Edo have learned the ways of spirits for hundreds of years, and by using their sacred dance they can bestow the gifts of their gods without being used as their pawns. Using the sacred dance, the priestess may transform the spirit world- and by doing so, the world of flesh. Each Kagura is based around a single Influence, and the Miko has an Essence pool equal to their Status. In order to activate the Endowment, the hunter must roll the Kagura+Performance, and if she Succeed she may spend Essence to activate the effects of the Influence up to her dots in the Kagura. She may regain Essence by meditation, maintaining a weekly taboo or as a gift of service from a ghost or a spirit.

                Bijin- Networking (0-00000)
                as masters of manipulations and information control, the Beautiful Ones can play a delicate game of influence over the monsters. The hunter needs to target a certain target and make a conversation with it, and than roll Endowment+Manipulation. Success means the hunter gains out of the target a number of favors, connections and wealth, as represented by a number of temporary Merit dots which can be spread among Allies, Resources, Contacts and any similar Social merit. Those Merits can only be used once, and after that the target is freed from its Favor. A target which tries to deny access to the Favor suffer for -1 per dot in the Endowment for the use of Social skills until she repays the Favor. An Exceptional Success means that the number of dots in doubled, while Dramatic Failure means that the hunter owes the target a Favor equal to their dots in the Endowment. The Merit dots gained by the Endowment must be connected to the target and to its status- you can't make a poor peasant give you Resources of 5, after all.

                Gimu- Divine Grace (0-00000)
                The samurai is protected by his owner and commitment, guided by his own Code of Life. As long as the hunter follows the Boshido and don't break his devotion, At the beginning of every Scene he may spend 1 WP and add his dots in the Endowment to his Defense, Attack rolls, Health (as temporary levels) or to any Skill he possess. The effects lasts until the end of the Scene. Breaking the code ends up with the hunter losing access to the Endowment until he restores his honor. The ST should be the one to decide which act may be considered as such "redemption".

                    Last Dynasties International- Infused Pharmaceuticals (0-00000)
                    a product of long research and occult knowledge, the members of LDI may enhance their mind, body and soul using special formulas. For one day per dot of Status (which represent the quality of the pharmaceutical in question) the hunter may spread her dots in the Endowment between her attributes, temporary increasing them. The choice may not be changed until the end of the duration. In an addition, an hunter may get access to a special pharmaceutical which grants Dread Powers which their total dot worth is not higher than her dots in the Endowment. The hunter gets access to the Dread Power for one hour per dot in the Endowment and may "recharge" her elixir once every [10-Status] days.

                    Deva Corporation- Luminous Advancements (0-00000)
                    being a part of a great Infrastructure, the hunter may channel its might through her and gain special Advancements for her used. In order to use the Endowment, the hunter must first "tune" to a certain Infrastructure, which requires rolling Endowment+Occult. Success means that the hunter may gain a number of Dread Powers equal to their dots in the Endowments, which are decided by the ST as powers which may advance the goal of the Infrastructure. The hunter stays tuned to the Infrastructure for one day per dot in the Endowment, although she may "tune off" at any moment. She may not be tuned to more than one Infrastructure at a time. The Dread Powers manifested by the hunter are usually manifested in the form of mechanical items or technological devices which may be operated only by the hands of the hunter. Exceptional Success means the duration is doubled. Failure gives a -1 modifier to any roll for tuning to the same Infrastructure in the same day. Dramatic Failure means an angel would be sent to investigate the "security breach" and deal with it.

                                            so- few more story hooks for how compacts would turn into conspiracies:

                                            Les Voyageurs- as fur merchants and hunters, they already had a good source of income. While the demand for the fur of wild animals did decreased with the years, the high class still knew how to appreciate good cloth- especially one made from mythical beasts and monsters. As the group started to invest all their resources in fashion business, their hunters has made contact with a group of native shamans who taught them a variation of the old skin-walking ritual. While today many animals are protected, one can't protect one beast from another- and thanks to the gifts of their ritual they may even stand as equals to their preferred prey- werewolves.

                                            The Scarlet Watch- Salem is an hard time, yet the contract of the Watch is stronger. While the families has fell from greatness and the vampires hunt the members as much as they can while Cheiron absorbs the high class members of the group, the contract was still maintained. Blood and Fate bind that compact together, and Blood and Fate allowed them to discover their old rites and returned them to their former status.

                                            Ahl al Jabal- the people of the mountain has always practiced occult methods in order to enhance their souls against the night. All it needed for them was a reason to grow- and the Arab Spring was just that. With the Syrian Civil War tearing their lands apart and the chaos which roams in Iraq, more and more people seek for a meaning, for a way to protect themselves both from human and supernatural evil, and the Ahl al Jabal has started to represent just that- a way for one to protect himself from the dark with the blessings of God.

                                            Heritage House- Thanks to the strong lineages of the House, the compact has produced many fine hunters, ones which were allowed to maintain double membership in other compacts or even conspiracies. While they joined all kinds of different groups (some have even awakened to the infernal lineage of the 7th Generation), they never forgot their shared origin. One day, when an ancient threat rose and endangered the world, it was the House who served as the linking chain which managed to unite many different groups together against it and banish it back to where it came from. Today, it still serves as the link between groups- and thanks to that neutrality, they gain access to the resources of their allies, both mundane and occult.

                                            Hunt Club- they have money, they have hierarchy, they have connection and members. However, falling into a Scourge Slasherhood is a constant threat for the members of the Club, which causes their own members to loose control and puts the Club in danger as whole. In order to learn how to control their murderous persona, they looked for ancient texts and found an Aztec tablet which taught them how to keep two faces- one for men and one for the gods. After learning how to keep their inner Slasher under control, they could have put a better use for their time- like culling the herd.

                                            I'll make the 4 Edo groups later :P

                                                Ama San- while time has passed, the tradition of the Ama kept on. Diving into the deeps, they used what they discovered there as a way to fund their humble community and protect their villages. Things changed when one of the prominent members of the compact dived deep enough to recover ancient treasures of a bygone age- the Tide Jewels of the Sea God, Kanju and Manju. Fearing the consequences of that power, Ryujin himself manifested before the community and asked for the Jewels, and the Ama San obeyed. Seeing the honesty and true spirit of the group, Ryujin's rage was replaced with joy. He took the jewels yet taught their ways to the group- the song of wave and storm. Under the Sea God's blessing, the group grew, absorbing similar compacts and eventually surviving into the modern days stronger than before.

                                                Azusa Miko- while Edo was an hard time for the Miko, the Meiji Restoration was worse. With the Emperor establishing new order through Japan, their practices were outlawed, leaving the group even more in danger. Under a common enemy, many former rival shrines united in secret, sharing the knowledge of both the spirits and the dead until they eventually developed the rites used to draw the Boundary and change the world with their sacred dances. Working from the shadows and gaining the support of their patrons, the group managed to survive until the fall of the Empire during the second World War. Since then, the group has started to grow back, and they don't plan to fall again.

                                                Bijin- since their foundation, all the Beautiful Ones asked for was gaining "access", and while traditions rose and fell through the years one things remained constant- the lust of people for beauty and pleasure, and the Bijin knew how to provide it. For them, rising in the tiers was a calculated move, a necessary step to achieve their goal- until eventually, they collected enough favors and made enough contacts in order to create their dangerous Network. Now, they are the masters of social interactions- and no fly is able to escape their web of contracts and connections.

                                                Gimu- among all of the compacts of the Era, the Gimu had their hardest time before them. The rise of Meiji and the end of the Edo Era marked the end for the old tradition. While there were rebellions and attempts to fight the Emperor's control, eventually the government won- yet their spirit didn't died out. While they were publicly abolished, certain military officials kept true to the old code in their heart, and even without swords they kept watching the shadows for the influence of monsters. Losing the World War looked like the final blow for the few who carried the flame on, yet as time carried on people has started to romanticize about the old times. Even without a weapon, people turned their eyes back to the shadows and saw the monsters who hide inside of them. Eventually, the descendants of the old Gimu has started to build themselves together, united by those who climbed the social ladder thanks to hard work. Eventually, cooperation with Zen monasteries, they unlocked the secrets of mind and body, as far as they adhere to their Code. While they are not a big power among the 3rd Tier, they are still there- and they are still growing.

                                                    and now a bit of fandom to fandom- Skin & Bones's compact

                                                    Carvers- Persona (0-00000)
                                                    Slashers view the world in a twisted way, and while that madness give them strength it could also be used as their weakness. That Endowment gives the hunter two benefits- first, it allows them to temporary rise their Integrity by spending one WP point per dot. You may not rise your Integrity more than your dots in the Endowment. The effect lasts until your next Integrity Breaking point, only that in case you fail that roll you don't lose Integrity or gain a Condition but simply return to your original score. That ability may be used only once per Chapter.

                                                    The second ability is used in order to manipulate Slashers- by rolling [Persona]+[Integrity], you may activate one of the 3 effects- first, you may force a Ban upon the Slasher (Bane if Exceptional Success) supported by the mania of the killer for a Scene. Second, you may shut down one of the Slasher's Undertaking Talents for a Scene. Third, you may temporary rise the Slasher's Integrity by the number of dots in Persona, revealing him to his own horror. He suffer for -1 per dot in Persona for all rolls in the Scene. (except of Integrity rolls). Dramatic Failure would either force a Ban on you, make you more vulnerable to the Slasher's Talents or suffer the penalty yourself.

                                                    Dirge- Last Blow Serum (0-00000)
                                                    Killing what is already dead is not an easy job- but impossible, especially by using that serum! After years of research, the Dirge managed to synthesis that secret weapon out of Vitae and other exotic materials. In order to use the Endowment, you must first prepare a weapon by covering it with the fluid. That effect may enhance any mundane weapon (that is, not one enhanced by supernatural power), although in the case of ranged weapon the Serum must cover the projectile. You may not have more doses of the Serum than your dots in Status: [Dirge], and they are refreshed at the end of every Chapter. When dealing an attack against a living dead, the target suffers from an additional level of Aggravated damage which may not be healed for the rest of the Scene by any means. In the case of a melee weapon, the effect may be used again for a number of attacks equal to you dots in the Endowment. In the case of ranged weapon, each projectile may be used only once, by a single dose of the Serum may last for a number of projectiles equal to your Endowment. By ST's decision, you may buy dots in a separate Serum meant for other kinds of monsters other than Zombies, although they should cost a bit more than the usual (extra 1 exp)

                                                    Silver Cross- Neo Medicine (0-00000)
                                                    while technology may create terrible weapons, it can also be used to heal. Using a cutting edge tech, the members of the Cross may stitch people up from pieces as easily as one may mold clay. While having access to their operation room, the hunter may add her dots in Neo Medicine into any medicine roll which allows her to heal a number of Bashing Damage equal to the number of Successes you get in the roll, a number of Lethal Damage equal to half of your successes or a single Aggravated Damage level. You may try the roll even without access to your operation room, but you suffer -2 penalty to rolls in case you have only an "emergency kit" or -3 if you don't have access to it. You get +1 modifier for every assistant you have (which must be someone with Medicine of 3 or above or someone with at least one dot of Neo Medicine) but that bonus may not be risen to more than +3 and only one person may attempt the roll in a single scene. In an addition, you may use Neo Medicine in order to heal supernatural diseases, conditions and even curses (as long as they don't remove a Major Template), but in order to do so you roll only Intelligence/Dexterity + equipment + Neo Medicine and must have access to an operation room. You may use Neo Medicine on the same target only once per Chapter.

                                                    Wordsmiths- Psychological Suppression (0-00000)
                                                    the more powerful the monster, the human is the patient- that was the conclusion of the Wordsmiths. Using a special psychological technique, the therapist may strength the one on the expense of the other. While talking to the target, the hunter may roll Manipulation+Endowment against the monster's Potency score. Success would reduce the monster's Potency score by -1 per Endowment dot to the minimum of 0. A willing patient may not resist the roll, and than the Hunter just needs to Succeed- although Dramatic Failure would instead backlash and force the inner monster to attack the therapist. Exceptional Success would remove another dot of Potency. That effect lasts until the target's next Breaking Point, and a single target may not be effected by more than a single Suppression at a time. In case the therapist know the target, she gets +1 for rolls, and in case it is not her first "treatment" by the therapist she gains +2.

                                                    Sons & Daughters of Cheshire- Disruption Field (0-00000, special)
                                                    Relics are dangerous, for they act as keys for stranger realms- yet those are not the only keys in the world, and just as keys may open doors so they can lock them. By using the remains of shattered relics transplanted to their own body, the Cheshire Society is able to banish evil powers from the world and keep them on the outside. This Endowment may be bought a number of time, and each time be tied to another supernatural effect (like Unseen Senses). By spending a single WP point, the hunter may generate a field which blocks the effect of that phenomenon- any attempt to use an effect related to that phenomenon in a radius which equal or lesser than the hunter's dots in the Endowment in meters would require a Clash of Wills against the hunter or would automatically fail. In a case of a being which is not "native" to the world, that creature is banished back to its native world. In an addition, the hunter may seal interdimensional gates for a single Chapter by rolling Endowment+Willpower, when a Dramatic Failure would force the gate open and Exceptional Success would destroy the Gate (Failure gives -1 to any future roll on the same Gate until success of Dramatic Failure). The gate must be connected to the phenomenon which the Field is tied to.

                                                    Terra Incgonita- Cleaner (0-00000)
                                                    using cutting edge technologies and questionable experiments, Terra Nullius has managed to develop a new botanic specimen which recycles the energy leaked from the Reanimated and use it to grow. Those plants, called Cleaners, may be GMOs, but desperate times means desperate means. Cleaners come in the form of seeds, until they are put inside a soil and are exposed to the Divine Fire of the Created. Once they are meant to be effected by Wasteland (or beings near them are exposed to Disquiet) the Cleaners start to grow, removing the Wasteland/Disquiet one level backward per dot in the Endowment. Any single Cleaner may absorb one such "dose" of Divine Fire until they reach their full Rating, and then they die out. Cleaners may also have a more "active" use- once having a direct contact with a Divine Fire Imbued Being (like a promethean, pandoran or even a qashmal) the plants start to grow without control, sending their roots into the flesh of their target. Each such Cleaner cause a single level of Aggravated damage per dot in the Endowment. Every hunter may have access to a number of seeds equal to 10*Status. A ST may allow the hunter to but different Cleaners for other phenomena (like vampires).
