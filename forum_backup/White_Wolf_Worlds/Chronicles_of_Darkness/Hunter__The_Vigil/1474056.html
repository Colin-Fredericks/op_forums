    Drugs, vibes, and Truth. An expanded Illuminated Brotherhood draft

    02-05-2022, 06:58 PM
    This compact, along with the Reckoning, Bear Lodge, and Night Watch, are ones I always thought were cool, but could use more to stand out. I decided to try with this compact first, and in the format of C&C. Feedback is very appreciated.

    The main point of this is to try and expand on the compacts with promise but just (to me) got looked over others.

    With the Illuminated Brotherhood, I focused on what their form of investigation means. They aren't merely psuedoscientists, nor are they a traditional occult society of studious scholars. I also wish to communicate the idea that the brotherhood is not necessarily stupid concerning the use of drugs and poking their heads where they shouldn't, but more that they want immediate results to be found.

    I feel the first draft is fairly weak, as it puts drugs too much on the periphery. That, and I feel it doesn't truly give that college kid culture the original write up has.

    Anyway, here it is. Please be honest with feedback, once i get this done, I hope to move on to different projects, and not necessarily concerning the compacts I mentioned before


    You saw what during your first high? Well, you've come to the right place, I assure you. As you probably know, we here at the Illuminated Brotherhood aren't the most...popular sort, but that's never been a problem for us. You're here for a reason, after all. You know there's a whole world out there. You know there are...things trying to get through to us, things that we only need open our minds to witness. But do you know about the others? No? Corpses tending to their feeding grounds, golems of flesh twisting the world where they walk, and things straight out of the garden wall trying to strike a deal. All of those and more are here. But why? We don't know the Truth. But that's exactly what we want to find out. Oh no, you don't have to take the drugs. But there are more things in heaven and earth, Horatio, and we intend to find out the Truth of such things. So get high, sleep in a haunted house, or hell, spy on those that prowl the night. And really, who cares about what others think. They can't handle the truth anyway.

    Opening the mind to Truth
    The Illuminated Brotherhood a compact that, on the outside, appears to be a harmful organization that puts its members in a dangerous cycle of risks and drugs for a goal that is vague at best. This is an opinion that is absolutely true.
    Not that the members of the brotherhood care. See, the hunters of this organization see a world filled with questions with no answers. They see a world with puzzle pieces, yet struggle to find what piece goes where. They see a world no one else seems to want to see, and that's enough for them to validated in their Vigil.
    It's also enough for them to still imbibe drugs. The Illuminated Brotherhood is often ridiculed for the insane risks they take, as they won't even remember it, but for them, one must open their mind to the Truth, and that by doing so will the world be understood. If that results in casualties...well what do you expect when research involves the otherworldly?
    In the end, it's all about finding the truth of the world


    But what is the Truth, really?
    A vampire is spied upon by three hunters as it feeds on a victim.
    The Loyalist of Thule wonders "what secrets does this creature hold"
    The Null Mysteriis hunter writes "the organism seems to prefer the blood of women over men.
    The hunter of the Illuminated Brotherhood whispers "what the hell is that vampire doing at a coffee shop?"
    The Illuminated Brotherhood definition of "Truth" is simple, yet complex. It is "Why". They don't seek academic study, nor do they really care about the bans and banes of monsters. They seek why the world has a "shadow" over it. They ask why vampires seem to hold territories instead of simply taking all they can from each other. They worry about the reasons why demons run from God.
    These simple questions have complicated and often contradictory answers. But it also means the brotherhood may not be able to answer what ban that fae creature has, the compact will be able to tell you why the fae creature struck a bargain with that man across the street, or why that ghost haunts that house, or even tell you just what "vibe" that spirit holds domain over.
    But what do drugs have to do with any of this?


    Psychedelics: Truth from illusions
    The very origin of the Illuminated Brotherhood came from mind altering substances, and that foundation is still strong today. The Brotherhood encourages its members to plumb their own minds when it comes to the supernatural, but why?
    Put simply, the compact believes all the world's questions can be answered through the ephemeral entities beyond the human sight. Call it a shared conscious, the spirit of the world, or afterlife, whatever you want, in the end the brotherhood believes these beings reflect the world. And it certainly appears this way.
    The building, not even decades old, begins falling apart. No one can tell why, but the drug addled man claims a stranger of strange presence came in one day and hasn't left since.
    High as a kite in his home, a Stoner has a conversation with himself concerning people doesnt like. When the high settles down, he realizes it wasn't really himself he spoke to.
    Spending the night on haunted grounds, a hunter of the Illuminated Brotherhood sees rats gnawing a thick, seemingly sold fog, with things slipping through the cracks.
    All of this doesn't mean they're always right. Quite the contrary, the Illuminated Brotherhood messes up. A lot. But that doesn't stop hunters from recording what they can through this method. They just don't want to risk discarding a possible piece of the puzzle.

    The highs...
    While the Illuminated Brotherhood is often characterized as junkies in over their heads, this isn't necessarily a bad thing.
    The brotherhood isn't cautious scholars like the Loyalists, nor are they reactive like the Union. No, the Brotherhood is brimming with wide eyed idealists who believe they are always just on the edge of discovery. Like college students wishing to join a frat, they gladly run straight into danger when asked. Does that lead to a high mortality rate, yes. Does it mean a hunter is hardly ever short on hands, yes. Not only this, but every member is encouraged to share their theories, piecing together the truth. This leads to the brotherhood having plenty of information to share with its members. This is especially true of the world of spirits. On a sub conscious level, the brotherhood has a surprising grasp of the ecology of spirits, able to feel out resonance ("vibes" in their terminology) of an area. Incidentally, this leads to them being better able to defend against spirits.

    And the hangovers.

    None of this stops the brotherhood from being a hindrance at the same time, though. Just as they will throw themselves in the ring, they expect hunters to put in effort too. They don't want a buzzkill who practices caution, they want a daredevil willing to run straight into danger for the possibility of learning something new. And just as they let out scraps of information, they also expect those scraps to be made into coherent whole. Come back with nothing to add, and suddenly the brotherhood will consider you "lame" and start a bit of a hazing (especially when under drugs).
    Worse, this doesn't stop those scraps from being outright wrong. The Brotherhood claims they want the truth, but they don't care too much about verifying it. Plenty of members have gained status from false information simply because they no one questioned just what came from drugs and what actually happened.
    Ultimately, it's up to the hunter to keep in the good graces of the brotherhood. Find scraps of truth, share theories, and be willing to assist a fellow member wanting to break into a witch's Lair. If not, well, don't be surprised when the pendant you were asked to keep turns out to be an anchor for a ghost (in their own words, just a harmless prank to teach you a lesson)

    The Movements
    Below are the prevalent movements the Brotherhood has relating to divining the Truth.

    The Children of Leary: Occult (hallucinogens)
    A prominent, but diminishing movement of the Brotherhood, the Children of Leary most represent the original incarnation of the Organization. Fixated on the use of psychedelics, the Movement focuses primarily on divining the truth through plumbing the depths of their own minds. This earns them respect for the deep insights into the ephemeral, but also derision for seemingly declining to put themselves out there in the field.
    The truth though, is that the supernatural comes to them, not the other way around. The Children of Leary know this, and do what they can to extract truth from deceit, whether that comes from the beings that stalk the edges of consciousness, or from their own drug addled minds.
    Secret: among the Children, rumors have begun circulating that the drugs don't make visible the invisible, but push through a barrier to allow contact. The Movement intends to try and remove it entirely, to push away the need for drugs and know for sure what's real from what's not. One member's supposed sighting of gnawing rats may just help

    The Spirit Seekers: Investigation (occult locations)
    Often accused of being thrill seekers more than their name sake, this movement believes the Brotherhood must go out in the field. They listen in on rumors, and ferret out what info they can concerning location of significance. These locations can range from the Church of a sacrificial altar to a suicide forest. This is fine and all, but they have a daredevil culture, and often encourage even foolish actions in order to "activate" whatever occult aspect they believe something may have.
    Secret: with the advent of shows such as ghost hunters, the Spirit Seekers hope to make money by commercializing their efforts. This will not sit well with the rest of the brotherhood, who wish to remain insulated rather than becoming a means of entertainment.

    The Watching Eye: stealth (stakeouts)
    Considered the most narrow minded, the Watching Eye may not be able to unveil how a cult formed and what it seems to want, but it can tell you about the behavior of the charismatic leader. Believing those spirits that have taken to the flesh to be the best area of study, the Movement hopes to piece together a working framework of truth. From late nights spying on a feeding leech spirit, to stalking the movements of a suspected demon, the Watching Eye believe this will not only help them find the motivations and workings of spirits, but also connect the disparate bits of truth concerning why they claim the bodies of people.
    Secrets: small contingent believes the best way to understand spirits, is to let themselves be "ridden". This has allowed the movement to make great strides in the behavior of spirits, so the fact some of these members are lost to these spirits is tolerated. Until it gets connected to them of course.
