                    To quote Awakening 2nd Editions developer, the Awakened are, barring a few saintly and Wise exceptions, Right Shits. There's a whole chapter in the book about how even the Pentacle are right shits (the Sleepers and Sleepwalkers Appendix).

                    The only remainder of the commentary is that there are no baby eating evil hunter organizations (Well, maybe the Hunt Club, and the failure to mention them Has Been Noted For Gameline Literacy), and while I'll freely admit that it doesn't help trying to engage this thread while I don't have the time to do so properly*, the escalation to this extreme and failure to deviate away from it leaves me wondering leaves me wondering if we have a point in actually doing that.

                    Like, yes, Hunter is a game of being a light in shadows, but it's also very much asking what that means, and the game does take a stance that some answers to that question are invalid. If you cannot comprehend the darkness, you cannot meaningfully be a light in it. Failing to engage with that darkness beyond an idea of evil is basically a fast track to Hunter's darkest depths as monsters, and that includes the organizations that emerge from basic hunter psychology.

                    Hunters aren't the good guys, and because of that, the organizations they form cannot be the good guys, but there is a whole world of nuance and thought on morality and ethics beyond that once you accept that Hunters are not the good guys. I don't really know what to say to anyone not wanting to engage with and understand the humanity present in pretty much any hunter that is the root cause for the creation and growth of those evil organizations, beyond "That sort of thing is textually called out with slashers" and "Why are you playing any Chronicles game at all?"***

                    *though, in mild defense here, a proper engagement means having to actually explain from the ground up, including but not limited to explaining the narrative and thematic way Chronicles approaches "evil" and monstrosity and to the end of understanding and allowing for a complexity of humanity as understanding, the fact that hunters being grouped as the same vampires, werewolves, witches, constructs, fairies, ghosts, mummies, demons, mythic monsters, and abberations is a meaningful and deliberate point, the way organizational principles and structures work in Chronicles of Darkness regarding those themes and how utterly divorced all of that is from simple black and white morality and instead with the themes and circumstances the games are talking about, the way Hunter takes every other gameline to task by nakedly going after the effects of their actions while stripping away the justifications offered by the circumstances, but then turns right around and does that to itself** as well, and laying out how the actionability of all that brings us right back around to honoring the humanity that drives people to monstrosity while still leaving room to combat that monstrostiy, and doing all of that in enough detail that it's not only clear enough for people who never read the books but is substantial enough that dissenting opinions dervied from it correspondingly have enough substance to actually be a worthwhile alternative opinion and not uninformed noise, and given all of that alongside the rest of my life, I hope I can be forgiven for not quite having had the time to do that yet.
                    **in contrast to Beast which strips justifications away from all other splats and shows them who they are to themselves-Hunter handling consequences of actions and Beast handling the consequences of being solidly covering internal and external consequences which honestly does a lot to make it clear what is that actual moral and ethical concerns of Chronicles on the whole.
                    ***I mean, I suppose we could also discuss the Princess Problem or the matter of expectations for tropes and archetypes for a game about monster hunters, but given that the conversation is hung up on a theme of "those who hunt monsters" that is is setup with the cover and built upon from there with reading, I'm not sure getting into particularly deeper reads resolves the issue.
