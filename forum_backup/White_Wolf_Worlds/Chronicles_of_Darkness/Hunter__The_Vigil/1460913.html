    Conspiring a Murder [Conspiracy specific Undertakings]

    09-22-2021, 07:19 PM
    Slashers, especially in 2e, are a big deal for Hunter- they both represent the antithesis for the Vigil, blurring the lines between a human and a monster and serving as a reminder for how low can the vigilantes fall. As such, while working on my AKD expansion fanbook, I've decided to include something slasher related in the antagonists section- and then it crossed my mind. While Undertakings are generally wide archetypes which describe how the slasher murders other people, what if some conspiracies would have their own, special, horrifying Undertakings? After all, between the fact that each conspiracy holds their own variation for the Code, them being under the influence of supernatural powers due to their Endowments and the strange philosophies they may believe in, it would make sense that such groups would produce a "specialized" form of Slashers, and that led me thinking about how we could represent that dark mirror to the conspiracy's values in the form of a special Undertaking unique to the conspiracy to which they once belonged.

    Now, I must admit that I felt some unease while writing the AKD's special Undertaking, considering that the process requires me to ask "which methods would I, as the slasher, use to murder people", which is one of the reasons I find the 1e Slasher book so terrifying. Still, the concept is intriguing, IMO, having some potential to lead us thinking how can hunters break in some extraordinary ways. For this, I decided that the Conspiracy Undertaking will always be a Scourge which could evolve from any Ripper, representing how the Undertaking is" bestowed" upon the member slasher just as Endowments are given to hunters. Another thing which I decided to be interesting is that unlike regular slashers, those ones will maintain their Endowments- especially considering that some may even keep some status in the conspiracy, having some service to it, or are produced by the Endowments and as such can access them. Taking that in mind, I wrote the first Conspiracy specific Undertaking- the Minotaur, those who are lost to the Labyrinth.

    Like I said, it was an uneasy experience- but now that Pandora's Box was opened, I had to ask myself how would the other Undertakings may look like? And that's what I had in mind.

    Aegis Kai Doru: the ones to kickstart this idea, I've decided to call this Undertaking the Minotaur, relating to the concept of a murderous monster being trapped in a labyrinth. The Minotaur is a product of the AKD's initiation rites, which cement the ancient Vow into the hunter up to a point where even their mind breaks under the stress of the Vigil, the Vow is still there to drive them further and further, killing without distinction as they start constructing their own "mental labyrinth" around them through strange acts of murder which look almost random to an outside. The Minotaurs are walking mysteries, and those who try to solve them are doomed to fall into their corruptive mindset, which makes the would be investigator even more venerable to the slasher- yet as oathbound warrior, the Minotaurs respect oaths and vows made by themselves and others, which makes them useful for their conspiracy as they populate their Labyrinths with slashers which would ignore loyal hunters and slay invaders without a second thought.

    Ascending Ones: and here we start moving from the very well defined concept to mere suggestions and ideas. The AO are alchemists, and as such it would make sense for their Undertaking be a product of alchemy- yet not "alchemy going wrong" as much as "alchemy refined into murder". The other aspect for their existence is faith, and so perhaps their slashers are a result of their elixirs refining their broken minds-n the AO use their own faith a furnace for an alchemical transformation, so what about their slashers having that furnace corrupted and transforming their bodies into tools for murder? Faith becomes hollow, and the cold nihilism, instead of turning poison into hope, poisons the world around them? Those Serpents are poisonous to the world, and can imbue objects with deadly toxins, yet are forced to obey to the signs of faith, for lacking their own devotion they become nothing but empty tools for the will of others.

    Council of Bones: a secretive and paranoid conspiracy, the CoB believe that they are the only ones pure from the darkness, and that arrogance may open a path for their own fall. The Vigil they uphold is one for the dead, not the living, and as members of the conspiracy lose the value of life, they find that the finger rest much more easily on the trigger, and with a terrifying calm are able to cull the elements they find bothering without being noticed by those around them. The Phantoms, as they are called, are extremely difficult to be found, being registered as dead to the world around them, allowing them to strike and vanish without trace, yet as they believe themselves to be dead, they too become venerable to the methods used to bind, summon and destroy the restless spirits.

    Cheiron Group: stuffing people with pieces from monsters is a very, very dangerous thing to do- there is a reason only TCG can manage this process, and while they can deal with the physical side, the psychological side is just as risky. As hunters of the company spiral down, they start losing their sense of self, or view the parts within them as having life of their own. They may start listening to them, talking to the dormant monster minds within their bodies, and eventually when they break a cacophony of terrified voices fill their minds, and they no longer know who they are and who they are not. The company, however, still views the Patchwork as an important asset, both because they contain thaumatech within their bodies and because those tools of destruction are powerful, even if unpredictable, weapons for the organizations military R&D division. Patchworks generally resemble a frankensteinian form of mythological beasts, such as centaurs, and are able to split pieces of themselves and put them within other bodies in order to spread their broken existence. However, all Patchwork still have a core which ties their mutilated minds, and by forming a resonance with that core the broken pieces of the slasher will turn upon themselves.

    Lucifuge: every Child of the Seventh Generation has a demon within them- their soul may be human, but the foul essence of hell is burning under the surface, waiting for the moment they'll slip into the darkness to erupt. While they may use the system of Castigations and the mental brainwash of the Lady in order to control those dark urges, a Lucifuge agent who lose themselves in the hunt will have their soul torn apart, and a demon emerging out. The Devils, as they are called, are the conspiracy's worst nightmare, being incarnations of dark evils and foul desires, hedonistic monsters who delight in murder most gruesome. The Devils thrive in sin, and easily tempt people to fall along their vices, drawing them into a path of self destruction until they reap their souls at the end of their own hell- yet just like the demons which spawned them, the Devil fear virtue, and are banished by signs of true faith.

    Malleus Maleficarum: as the willing servants of God, the MM are known to cling to a virtuous path- yet only the strong willed can withstand that path. Those who try to devote themselves to the holy mission of fighting the spawn of evil must remember their humility before the God- or else their faith will only be a shell covering a vast emptiness, and start murder in the name of a god they do not believe in. Known as the Inquisitors, those slashers once swarmed all across the Spanish Empire during the dreadful days of the Spanish Inquisition, yet even after it was disbanded the MM sometimes product those terrifying murderers who simply seek to glorify their own names. Inquisitors are driven individuals, able to transplant their burning fever and ideology in those who listen to their fiery speeches or read their dogma, spreading like wildfire among the faithful- yet true to their names, their goal is rooting out heresy, heresy against their own divinity. Being leaders of their congregations, Inquisitors needs to be surrounded by their flock- or otherwise they lose all meaning and sink in despair.

    Task Force: VALKYRIE: unlike most other conspiracies, TFV does not utilize sketchy supernatural powers in order to fight the darkness (at least, as far as they are aware)- yet there is something looming in the shadow of the conspiracy, and we are not talking about vampires. In many ways, TFV is THE conspiracy, a organization built into the shadows of the American government meant to serve as cover ups and hide the existence of the darkness from the masses, and while they are meant to fight monsters, sometimes lines are crossed and innocents need to be silenced- and some agents find it easier to remove civilian memories by removing the civilians themselves. As the hunters devote themselves to their country over their people, they start to find themselves resonating with the symbols of power- the eye in the pyramids, new world order, corridors of power and the invisible hands of wall street. The social constructs of the state become more real than the people of the country, and dying to serve the country should be viewed as a great honor- sacrifices made in the name of a greater good. The Illuminated don't simply serve their country, they are the country. The symbols of the conspiracy are everywhere, and they follow missions so secret, only they are able to receive, reading the will of the conspiracy within the symbols of power and deciphering it from the news. Those slashers may be viewed as insane, but they may get something right, being able to pull strings and abuse the system in ways no one should be able to, turning the bureaucratic machineries upon their victims, yet they must always keep their identity secret- or else their symbols would no longer recognize them as worthy.

    VASCU: it is hard to think about a murderer for too long without being in danger of falling into the same black pit. Gifted with the power to hack minds and see through the eyes of slashers, VASCU dance dangerously on the line between light and darkness, and it shouldn't come as a surprise that when the line is crossed, the result could be horrifying. VASCU agents know everything there is to know about slashers, after all, and with records upon records of murder hosted in their minds the slasher finds many references which allows them to trick being caught, becoming the most dangerous enemies VASCU has to deal with. Such slashers are known as the Noise, for they have the ability to temporary harness the talents of other slashers as long as they had time to investigate their minds and methods, leaving an inconsistent trail of bodies leading to countless false leads. However, the price that the Noise pay for their gift is their lack of their own personality cemented in the murders, falling into an empty, almost mechanical behavior when there is no murder to mimic.

    Ok, that's it for the "core eight". I'll post the rest tomorrow (god, that's much more work than I expected). Please let me know what you are thinking, and if you have any suggestions of your own!

              So let's check the supplementary ones and see what we get, shall we?

              Cainte Heresy: wild and zealot, the Cainte Heresy either die fast or end up shattering their own minds so hard that all that is left behind are splattered pools of blood. While their obsession with vampires stops most of them from falling into slasherhood, all it takes is for the already unstable Cainte to view innocents as collateral damage and slashers would quickly fall into madness. Considering that the Cainte are all, in a way, bloodbound as part of their Endowment, we can assume that their special Undertaking, the Bloodluster, will have a similar aspect to their abilities. The Bloodluster are pure, raw emotion, which could be infectious in a similar way to the Inquisitors yet in a very different angle- those slashers can leave residual emotions in their blood, and those who come in touch with it are driven mad by the slasher's murderous urges. Their victims have their blood mixed with that of the Bloodluster, who can use them in order to power their Endowments. Yet by infusing themselves with the blood of the innocents the slasher defines themselves as a creature of the grave, developing fear from the banes of vampirism such as sunlight and fire.

              Faithful of Shulpae: it is pretty obvious, isn't it? The Faithful of Shulpae already walk a very, very, very thin line as is, eating monsters as an act of worship and claiming it is ok because they are not humans. And for a certain degree, they are right- but some monsters are far more human than the others, and when the line grows more and more blurry and the hunger starts taking life of its own, the Faithful will find that there is more to be eaten than simple arcane power- like personality, will, thoughts, souls, and unlike magical abilities, everyone has those, allowing them to gain a rush from eating the forbidden delicacy (*OOC: writer goes throwing up after writing those lines*). Aaaanyway, when a Faithful starts targeting humans in order to state their endless apatite, they become one of the Raveners. Motivated by hunger alone, those slashers can adopt the aspects of those they eat, potentially even changing their bodies and accessing the knowledge of their victims. However, the hunger found within them is constantly consuming- they no longer find nourishment in regular food, and denying them their prey for too long and they would be consumed by the void in their stomachs.

              Hototogisu: It is all about control. Pull the strings, manipulate the masses, bind with contracts and steal what you can. Unlike most other conspiracies, the Hototogisu are a very social conspiracy, one which doesn't even kill monsters if they can enslave them- so it would make sense that they are less likely to produce slashers than other conspiracies. When they want something, they prefer to buy or steal it- but some people aren't willing to cooperate, no matter what the corporation offers them. And when you walk so much with monsters, it is easy to start embracing their methods- kill a CEO here, a R&D scientist there, and things should become easier, right? And besides, those people- they don't appreciate what they have, yet are unwilling to give it to those who know what to do with it. They are not worthy for those things. They don't want them as much as you do. You want it. You want more, more, MORE- so you steal what they have. You steal their money, their house, their family, their lovers. You'll take everything from them- leaving a hollow husk behind. The Nopperabo, as they are called, have the strange talent to worm their way into the lives of their victims out of nowhere as they slowly take over their lives, yet as the protocols of the conspiracy are still grounded into the fabric of their broken minds, they can not act without an agreement from their victim- and be denying their access to their lives, the only thing they is bash their heads as they watch what they desire through the window like a lost, misguided bird.

              Knights of St Adrian: serving as the bounty hunters of God himself, the KoSA are contracted to find and capture those angels which fell from grace and turned demons. While most of those hunters are devoted for their mission, sometimes devotion can get out of control, as the hunter starts targeting those the demon attach themselves to as a method to root out the devil- and the faith that they serve as God's hand makes it easy to believe that the end justify the means, up to the point that it is only the mission that they care about, and everything else could burn. While dangerous and zealot, the Seraphim are still useful for God's plans, being a ruthless arrow which pierce through any barrier until they find their target. Acting more like angels than humans, the Seraphim draw great power from sacrifices done in their name and the name of their missions, something which allows the conspiracy to still contract the service of those dangerous slashers- yet when they are found outside of God's path, they quickly go mad with fear and despair.

              Knights of St George: for the Knights, lies and falsehoods are a way of life. Found between the degenerating effect of magic upon the mind, the consistent manipulations of the Revelations which make the initiate to doubt their own reality and the haunting dreams of the Faceless Angels, it should come as no surprise that many hunters of the conspiracy are driven mad, and that from the shattered remains of their minds and souls rise horrifying slashers which were dreamed into existence. Born from the depths of the Faceless Angels deathless sleep, the Spoken each speak a voice of an angel which governs the seal of a demon. For those slashers, the world is a fluid, dreamlike reality, composed of demons and angels which speak in strange tongues and drive them to murder. When using the voice of the Angels, the Spoken can lie to reality itself, creating paths in places they weren't and finding knives and empty drawers, trapping their victims in the living, hallucinating nightmare which surrounds them. Yet just as they are creatures of lies, the Spoken are also creatures of fear- and they can't stand the face of a truth which expose their false dreams.

              Les Mysteres: standing on the boundary between humans and gods and having spirits riding their flesh on a daily basis, the sanity of a Mysteres hunter faces a constant strain, and when combined with the horrors of the Vigil that stress could eventually break their soul. Spirits, after all, are manipulative entities, and they constantly try to take control from the Mysteres in order to manipulate them to their own needs. Those hunters which comply to the will of the spirits generally get possessed and Claimed by them, yet some instead become a danger for the gods by themselves- they hollow bodies trap the spirit within them, devouring its power and making the hunter into a living god known as an Incarnation. Believing in their own raw power, the Incarnations demand to be worshiped, and are known to build temples to glorify their power. Those temples are extensions of the slasher's power, which obey the will of the false god in order to devour the chosen sacrifices. However, the slashers need symbols of power to maintain their stolen divinity, and when denied of them the devoured god will rebel against the flesh which holds it bound.

              Merrick Institute: generally, slashers are the product of trauma- and oh boy how much trauma do we have here! Like, close to Wanda Maximoff level of trauma. Each of the children from Merrick were tormented, abused and manipulated to the point their souls were projected from their own bodies in order to escape the horrors only to fight titanic incarnations of human fear- and even after the original conspiracy collapsed and the children crawled from the burning remains, the trauma remained. In their dreams, the children are free, representing an idealized form of themselves- yet as the madness spirals their form collapses as well, and their minds go through some self Devouring, corrupting their souls as they lose grip in reality and eventually fall into slasherhood. Like the hunters of Merrick, those slashers, also known as Freddys, are dreamwalkers- yet instead of stalking the lairs of mythical monsters or the astral reflection of devouring corporations, they worm into the dreams of other people, filling them with terror until they eventually come to finish the job. Such fall from grace is more common than you'll think- for while they fight beasts, most of their enemies are regular people who work in the Web of Pain, and the easier it is to bring end to those people it becomes easier to infect people related to them, or people who are just evil or useless or allow evil to prosper by doing nothing, until eventually only the desire to kill is left behind. While their nightmare touch is horrifying and they can manipulate their victims into sleepwalking murder, a dreamer which manages to fight their influence gain a great power against them, marking them with anathema similarly to how heroes hunt down the beasts of myth. While the conspiracy fears and fights Freddys, the remnants of the nameless organization which produced them find them incredibly useful- and use them to hunt down the rogue children and bring them back to the conspiracy's embrace either alive or dead.

              And for a special bonus, here are the two official compaspiracies-

              Network Zero: unlike most other groups, Network Zero does not seek to kill monsters- but mainly to document them, spreading information about them and revealing the truth to the masses. However, monsters generally don't want to get out to the light- which means that they need to be lured, and what better bait it is than the lives of other people? After all, how can you prove that vampires exist without showing them draining someone to death or a werewolf tearing an innocent person to shreds? Oh sure, such acts are only murder by proxy- but Network Zero also only hunts by proxy, and just as their Vigil is valid being exposed to such events on a daily basis slowly grinds their empathy, especially when they open accounts in the Dark Web where they share the gruesome ways victims die by the darkness. And sometimes, when the monsters to get enough likes, what bad would it be to stage the acts by themselves, streaming acts of murder to the whole world? Still, those hunters which generally rise from the group prefer to watch than to dirty their own hands, becoming Patrons for murder, spreading videos and documents about the most horrifying murders- and exploit other slashers for their sick entertainment, or frame others for their crimes by editing the videos and evidence. Patrons are known to live in the virtual world, being able to easily hack into computers and devices in order to spread information, spy over their victims and guide their sponsored to perform the acts of murder, yet unlike other slashers, the Patrons are far less capable in killing by their own hands, with the act being far less satisfying and messy to perform.

              Promethean Brotherhood: already on the verge of being a a cabal due to them sometimes recruiting potential slashers to their group, the Brotherhood seeks to steal the power of witches through the act of murder- which makes them both serial killers and witches. As a result, the PB have high tendency to generate slashers, ones which run on stolen fire and innocent blood. As is, the Rite of Hecate allows a person to steal the path of a witch to the stars, taking their magic for their own- yet as the constant acts of murder and experimentation with the Rite twist the mind of the hunter, they may start seeing new and strange interpretations for the ritual, where instead of stealing the fire of a witch they were to sacrifice the lives of the innocents to strange, chthonic entities- and as the hunter hits rock bottom, they unlock the secrets of the Rite, offering their minds and souls to the powers below the world and redefine the ceremony from pointing upwards to downwards. The result is commonly known as Orphics among the organization, a well guarded secret for the group is interested in discovering the details of the Rite yet fear (for justified reasons) that if their existence will be well known it would be enough for other conspiracies to dismantle their group and kill their members. Using the mutated form of an already dark ritual, the Orphics always kill in a ceremonial way, making offering to the sleeping powers from below the world- yet their magic is nothing but a charade. Instead of drawing upon actual magic, the miracles that the Orphic seemingly perform always has a rational explanation- but no one seems to notice. Their tricks and manipulations simply go unnoticed- no one sees the wires which pulls them in the air, not one notice how they replaced the card with one in their sleeves, and when they fix the lady after they cut her apart, everyone applause without noticing that she is till very much in pieces. Yet just like the regular Rite, this variation also runs on a burrowed time, and if no sacrifice is being offered to the hungry gods of the Below the tricks of the Orphic will not only be revealed, but all their acts will suffer from terrifying misfortune.


              ...And that's it! Unless I'll get VERY inspired to make some Undertakings for certain fanmade conspiracies, but I think it would be better for me to first actually write down the current Undertaking before just pitching new ones, right? Again, feel free to make you own suggestions for those concepts, as there are some which I am not sure about and may need to be improved or rewritten,
