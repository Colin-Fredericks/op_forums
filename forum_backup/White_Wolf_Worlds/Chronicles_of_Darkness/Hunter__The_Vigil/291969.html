    Conspiracy: The Order of St Anthony

    10-25-2014, 08:55 AM
    The Order of St Anthony

    The Gravediggers







    Ask any grandmother and you’ll learn the truth: The dead left unburied shall walk again; and the Eastern Bloc has unburied dead in legion. It is a holy duty to restore slumber to the dead, and the Gravediggers work tirelessly. Six foot by six foot their hands turn raw and bloody on the shovels. Their muscles burn, their stomachs rumble and they dig on.

    In 1453 an orthodox bishop named Ivanovich Vasin learned that there was truth in old wives tales when he was attacked by walking corpses. Wounded he walked to Moscow to warn the Patriarch, but Ivanovich never noticed that he died during the journey. When he arrived before Jonah of Moscow the Patriarch saw his ephemeral visitor as a sign from God and quickly agreed to sponsor the Order of St. Anthony.

    Five hundred years is a long time to uphold the vigil, and during those five hundred years the order has suffered greatly. Yet they did not suffer at the hands of monsters, or at least not monsters with fangs and unholy powers. Mankind is more powerful than any vampire or necromancer, and when mankind turned on their protectors the results were swift and brutal.

    In 1921 the Soviet Union began a campaign against the Eastern Orthodox Church. A key objective of this campaign was the elimination of the Church’s military wing; by all accounts the Order put up a damn good fight. They were well trained, disciplined, and Benedictions had a psychological component that went beyond their battlefield effectiveness; but despite these advantages they were outnumbered, outgunned, and ultimately defeated.

    [picture: A last stand is taking place at a small rural church. A barricade of sandbags blocks the doorway and two rural parishioners take cover behind the sandbags shooting towards the perspective. Behind them stands a priest holding up an Icon of St. Martin. Bullets fired towards the defenders miraculously curve away in mid air; but a rocket propelled grenade is in mid flight and on target.]

    The survivors spent three decades in hiding. Three decades moving from safe house to safehouse. Three decades relying on sympathisers to stay alive, any one of whom may be bribed, blackmailed, or a Soviet agent. Even during those dark days many still upheld their duties in secret - they were more concerned of the account they would give to God than the gulags.

    After those three decades things became easier. When the Great Patriotic War began Stalin and the Eastern Orthodox Church signed a truth, and the Church was allowed to rebuild. The terms of this truce strictly forbade the church from running it’s own military, but those terms were ignored. With support from the Church the Order began to secretly rebuild throughout the Soviet Union and even beyond, far from the eyes of anyone searching for them.

    With the fall of the Soviet Union the Gravediggers have emerged from the hiding places, ready to put down all the monsters created from a century of atrocities.


    The Enemy

    The Eastern Front, the holocaust, the purges. Modern history has not been kind to Eastern Europe. Pockets of spiritual and necromantic turmoil cover the continent, and the Gravediggers are holding the shovel.

    The Order of St. Anthony might have named themselves for the patron saint of gravediggers, but they do not see themselves as hunters of the undead. They see themselves as protectors of humanity and hunters of all Satan’s legion. Their specialisation is an act of necessity, not ideology.

    Ghosts are the most common dead being the Gravediggers encounter, and the Order considers them to be morally equivalent to humans. If a Ghost is incapable of speech they will give it an exorcism and the last rites. If the Ghost is lucid they’ll invite it for a final confession. The Order knows that many Ghosts depart on their own accord if their final requests are fulfilled and considers doing so to be a charitable act but they aren’t above convincing or forcing a ghost into exorcism if it’s unfinished business is immoral or impractical.

    The Gravediggers also work to bury the remains of the past in a somewhat literal fashion. They use Benedictions to purify the corruption left at the site of battlefields or atrocities. There are places where exorcists have been purifying taint for decades and isolated locations where the Order has trapped zombie hordes inside a ring of Benedictions powered by bunker-shrines until such time as the monsters can be put six feet under.

    Ghostwalkers are especially hated. The Orders consider Ghostwalkers to be possessed by demons attracted to the necromantic corruption. The Bound’s tendency to create death cults, to control the dead, and to adorn themselves with parodies of the sacred and occult are all evidence for a sinful nature. The Ghostwalker’s apparent immunity to exorcism only serves as further proof of their willing partnership with Satan.

    If the Order of St. Anthony thinks Vampires aren’t as bad as Ghostwalkers, the difference is slight. The leeches are kind of monster only fit for a deathbed repentance, emphasis on the “deathbed”. Witches and necromancers? They got their power from bargain with Satan.

    All Conspiracies occasionally but heads with other Conspiracies but the Order of St. Anthony is deeply entangled with the occult politics of Eastern Europe. In some countries it is the dominant Conspiracy but in other countries the Order must avoid the watchful eye of ex-Soviet Conspiracies who now serve the new governments or their own interests. The Eastern Orthodox Church is using it’s political issue to promote and protect it’s Hunters, but progress is slow. And let no one forget the long and turbulent history between the Eastern Orthodox and the Catholic Churches.

    Hunters

    You were a krokodil addict, you’ve stopped using but it’s too late. You’re dying, your body is rotting away, hell you’ve seen zombies better looking than you. But you’re not dead yet, you’re holding yourself together with willpower and Benedictions. If you’re going to die, you’re going to die doing the Lord’s work.

    You're an old man. You should be retired by now, but the Conspiracy needs your unique skills. In the 1930s you were a young hunter for the Serbian branch of the Gravediggers, one of the few significant branches to remain untouched by the Soviets. Refugees, the few lucky ones who made it, they came from all over the Soviet Union carrying what sacred artefacts and tomes they could. You fought alongside them, you learned from them, and you became one of the best masters of Benedictions in the Order, and now that the Gravediggers are moving back into their old haunts you're the one recovering what they lost. You interrogate ghosts, dig through burned out monasteries, and even organise occasional raids on old soviet facilities. It's a dangerous and important job.

    You were a farmer. To you the Reds and the Whites were far less important than getting food onto your family’s table. You’d chant any slogan people asked if it meant the government would leave you alone. Then they came for the Church and that was a step too far. A monk came begging to your door and you offered to hide him. But old habits die hard, when he heard vampires were hunting nearby he started going back at night. One night he didn’t come back, and you knew nothing until you saw him in a newspaper decades later. You wrote him a letter and started putting supplies in that old safe room just encase anyone needs it again.

    You were a priest during the times of the Soviet Union. One day you got a tap on the shoulder. He told you about the risen dead, the consequences of atrocities, he left you texts on Benedictions and told you to train. When the Soviet Union fell you got a call, and you're ready to do your holy duty.

    Your family was smuggled out of Russia by sympathisers, but there wasn't much opportunity for Eastern Orthodox Priests in America. You never went hungry, but you never had much either. Growing up poor you fell in with a rough crowd, the Bratva. You always feared the confrontation with your father, but to your surprise he came to offer a deal, not condemnation. He taught you things that kept you safe in the Underworld, and in return you sent weapons to his old friends. Now the Order's star is rising, and they've made a simple offer. Switch sides, become one of our men inside the Bratva. Fight for the good guys and work off that debt against your immortal soul. Benedictions are real, why not hell? You took the offer, and you took a lot of your loyal men with you.

    You were never a part of of the Order. You father was part of the Church, and when persecution started he fled west rescuing whatever holy artefacts he could save. Among them were the belongings of a monk who served in the Order and was betrayed by a man in your father's congregation. When you became a hunter you found those belongings, you and your Cell self taught yourselves Bendictions and used them to protect the small Orthodox community you were a part off. Working prayers made you faithful, and when you heard that the Order was reborn you pledged your loyalty.

    I shall not allow myself to die.

    Monasteries

    The Gravediggers were reforged during decades of Soviet suppression. With the KGB and KSI and their throats they could not afford the luxuries of internal conflict or theological dispute. This is changing, but the legacy of oppression stands strong. The Order of St. Anthony does not divide itself by ideology, but by duties.

    Each duty is named after a monastery. During the cold war members of the Gravedigger’s would say they studied at certain schools as a code to identify themselves. With the old factions of the Conspiracy thoroughly destroyed, the tendency stuck when the new factions were named.

    The Monastery of Blood are the most resolute of the Order, their mission is to face evil without fear and send it back to whatever hell it came from. Vampires, necromancers, and other monsters that cannot or will not turn from the path of evil are their prey. They are made to confess by any means necessary, then placed six feet under.
    Speciality: Crafts (Firestarting)

    The Monastery of Sweat serve as the leaders and organisers of a large but loose network of supporters and sympathisers who in turn run the network of safehouses, secure messages and equipment caches. Members are not expected to face monsters directly, but often go into dangerous and unknown territory to prepare the ground.
    Speciality: Survival (Safehouses) or Streetwise (Orthodox Community)

    The Monastery of Tears handle those monsters that can be redeemed and offer help to human victims of monsters. The Monastery somewhat specialises in ghosts. They heal the monster's emotional scars, bring justice to past crimes, and return the monsters to the grace of god.
    Speciality: Investigation (Records)


    Stereotypes

    Malleus Maleficarum: Our so called “Brothers in the Faith”. They do god's work but they're still the same heretics they were in 1053.

    The Brotherhood Prizrakov: Don't be fooled by the articles of incorporation. Our records of these witches stretch back almost to the Apostles themselves, which means they may well be older still. A lot of martyrs were made trying to flush them from those mines.

    KSI-21: Ghosts of the Социалисти́ческих suckling from ghosts of the Wehrmacht. They belong in a grave next to all the other horrors of the past century.

    Aegis Kai Doru: There's a reason we built our new headquarters in Serbia, and that is to stay away from the Aegis Kai Doru. They've been around since before Christ and they've spent that time chasing after every last trinket Satan placed to tempt them. One day they'll destroy themselves through their own sin, until then it’s better that the damned prey upon the damned than upon us.

    Status

    The Order of St. Anthony is a strict military hierarchy, Status is bestowed as a promotion by one’s superiors based on their assessment of who is fit for the duties of the next rank.

    The hierarchy's reach varies dramatically by region. In areas where the Church enjoys strong public and government support the Order has regular but clandestine meetings between members of the Church. In other areas hunters religiously avoid contact with their Conspiracy for extended periods of time; relying on dead letter drops and international travel to hide their association.

    * Wherever you go the Order's network of safehouses is ready to shelter you. You gain a dot in the Safe Place merit, what's more you can hold this dot in reserve to instantly gain access to a safehouse when you most need it – the Gravediggers have a lot of sympathisers who will hide a hunter in the attic, no questions asked. At the end of a session you may choose to relinquish your free dot and place it back in reserve.

    *** Your name has travelled far among the Order's network of sympathiesrs. You have 2 dot in Allies applied to Order Supporters. Your supporters are rarely combatants, but excel in providing resources, information and a metaphorical smokescreen.

    ***** You’ve endured more than most men. You’ve fought monsters that defied death, and you’ve outlasted them. If you’re reached this Status then the chances are you’ve endured oppression by the soviet union itself. It takes a lot to kill you, by spending a point of Willpower you may gain Resolve boxes of Temporary Health for a scene.

    Endowment: Benediction

    The secret rites of Benediction predate the East–West Schism by almost a millennium, and so the Order of St Anthony wields them with the same zeal as it’s Catholic counterpart.

    Though the Conspiracy's histories there has been times when scholars would travel to share rites, trade manuscripts and collaborate on research, there has been times when both Conspiracies locked their secrets away, and even times when both tried to steal research and prevent the “heretics” from “defiling holy power”. While the fundamental principles of Benediction are identical each Conspiracy has their own list of Rites - which Rites are known by which Conspiracy remains the discretion of the storyteller, though the Order of St Anthony are known to favour defensive Benedictions more than their counterparts.

    Some Benedictions have different effects or situational modifiers for different denominations of Christianity. When translating those rites between the Order and the Shadow Congregation the storyteller should adjust the rites to favour the user's denomination. Alternatively the Storyteller may split Benedictions between those that favour Catholicism, those that favour Eastern Orthodoxy and those that favour the user's denomination based on the saint's history and patronage.

    Merit Icon (O)

    You have performed a great deed in the Order’s name and earned the right to carry an Icon of a saint, one whose patronage and history fits your deed, into battle. An Icon grants a single extra point of Willpower that can only be spent on the Benediction petitioning the appropriate saint. This point of Willpower may be spent as part of the activation cost or for extra dice, but the one Willpower per turn rule remains in full force.

    To refill an Icon the Hunter must spend an hour praying with the icon as a focus, Icons can only be refilled once per day.

    Drawback: When the worthy channel Benedictions through Icons of the saints heavenly power remains within the Icon’s earthly form. These Icons are coveted by Witches, Demons and the Aegis Kai Doru. There's power in an Icon, and more in it’s ritual desecration.

    A Hunter who lets their Icon fall into the wrong hands can expect to lose Status, and the Order will go to great lengths to recover Icons consecrated through battle.
