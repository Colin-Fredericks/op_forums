    An idea for a Compact or Conspiracy that I've been sitting on for a while now is something of a take on The New Inquisition from Unknown Armies. A powerful African-American billionaire driven by an obsessive personal crusade to destroy the forces of darkness had used his considerable resources and connections to form and outfit his own private army to fight this secret war of his. They're not the best and brightest, or else they would've managed to land a legit job. They're the desperate, the downtrodden, the fringe class, the ones willing to do whatever's asked of them in exchange for a steady paycheck and a second chance at life. Their main goal is to track down and eliminate dangerous monsters, but there's another dimension to it beyond keeping the streets safe at night. See, monsters often have money, property, and other assets that their corporate overlord can use to strengthen and expand his own position. What's more, the cell that makes the kill gets a sizable cut of whatever they seize for themselves put right back into their budget, so there's plenty of incentive for them to risk their necks ferreting out well-connected creatures. If they have an Endowment, I'm thinking that it'd be something to let them purchase temporary social merits with this pool of untraceable blood money.

                    A Compact that’s actually an alliance of Hunter Cells filled with scientists and occultists who joined up in the name of both survival, and mutually enlightened self-interest, after managing to piece together enough through mutual communication to begin realising just how deep the rabbit hole is, and are seeking to figure out the ‘rules’ of the various magic systems.

                        The two Hunter organizations I've always wanted to create:

                        The Black Gun Carnival, The Kingslayer's Game

                        Loosely inspired by the Stephen Sondheim play Assassins, The Carnival is a compact of killers who murder those of High Status to exchange for prizes with the Proprietor of the Carnival itself, an interstitial space that opens up to those who would be willing to play the game. They usually hunt monsters because the supernatural world has many more kings, queens, sultans, etc. (Status 5 folks) that are more easily accessible than major political figures-which isn't to say that it's all monsters. Benefits include a person's black gun, the use of the carnival for transit and hiding, and of course trading kills of status for prizes. Every time I come around to that last part though, I get hung up on how it works.

                        The Heirs to Arcadia

                        You know the story of how a fairy princess comes to the surface world, gets blinded by the light, and dies, only to reincarnate as a human who, through trial and proof of character, can return to their throne, right? Well, who knows how true that is, but when the ideas for a changeling hunter conspiracy were being batted around, that was always my go to answer-a conspiracy of "princes" and "princesses" tasked by the fairies to brave the iron world and thorn maze to capture and return thieves, deserters, and traitors to their homeland, until such as they've proven their worth. Obviously the take as I'm talking about is a bit on the nose, and I don't want to neccesarily keep it like that-it's more interesting if there's just as much possibility they're right as they are suckers for a Stranger's game, but I always felt like this take was more appropriate than a lot of the other attempts for a changeling hunter conspiracy. Also it lets me riff on Changeling the Dreaming, and is yet another way I would also play with the magical girl idea in Chronicles (because a lot of those early magical girl shows were all about earning or drawing on their royalty).

                                            Okay, I've had a few different basic compact/conspiracy concepts bouncing around inside my skull. Here they are:

                                            Compacts:

                                            Ecto-Zone: A collective of mediums, paranormal investigators, and parapsychologists. If ghosts and spirits are bothering you, who you gonna call? These guys. When it's something else though, call somebody else.

                                            Where the group able to become a Conspiracy, their Endowment would enable them to better understand, communicate with, and if need be, fight with the ethereal.

                                            The Howling Moon Theatre: A travelling entertainer group, they use the power of dance and song to exorcise malicious ghosts & spirits, imprisoning them within specially prepared masks. They do this by putting on performances that either tell the story of a ghost's life, or that incorporate themes of a spirit's bane/ban.

                                            As a Conspiracy, they would grow from travelling entertainers to a full-fledged talent agency. Additionally, their Endowment would not only improve their ability to seal way ghosts/spirits but enable them to use the powers of the bound entities by wearing those masks.

                                            The Neo Ground Railroad: A loose network that specializes in helping others disappear from supernatural foes: a ghoul fleeing an abusive vampire; a changeling wishing to throw a Huntsmen off their trail; a beast that needs to get away from a particularly zealous hero; and many more. These folks have ways of helping others to disappear.

                                            As a Conspiracy, the NGR would obtain an Endowment that would grant their charges occultion, ward safehouses against unwanted guests, and protect their secrets from supernatural interrogation.

                                            Conspiracies:
                                            Unusual Investigations: A detective agency that often finds itself involved in jobs that send it's PIs headfirst into the supernatural. Seriously, you'd be surprised how many times a spouse isn't cheating but is in fact a vampire/werewolf/wizard or other something else. Their endowment allows them to track the supernatural, pierce through their lies, and ward off attempts at dissuading them from investigating further.

                                            Ancestral Wardens: Operating under the guise of ancestry research foundation, the Wardens are a group that studies and reveres the heroes of humanity's past. With their endowment, they call upon the spirits of these legends to aid them in defending humanity from the supernatural, especially the nightmarish beasts which feast on fear and the so-called "heroes" that leave a trail of destruction hunting after them.

                                            The Burnt Torch Society: A cult that believes fear is not something to flee or fight, but rather something to be embraced. Through embracing their fear, they tap into humankind's most primordial fear: Darkness. Turning the darkness into weapons and armor, they strike fear into those who believe themselves above such feelings.

                                            JDG Cleaning & Fumigation Incorporated: On the surface, JDG is just another professional cleaning and fumigation company, with their only oddity being that they are a subsidiary of Elysium Cryogenics. Look beneath the surface and you'll discover they perform clean more than windows and deal with far more esoteric pests. Through their Endowment, Projection, their cleaners exit their material bodies and confront haunting ghosts, squatting spirits, and other creatures who think their supernatural powers let them steal other people's real estate. Fans of the Old World of Darkness may find this group familiar...

                                            Well, this is what I have just off the top of my head, might edit this later with more ideas. What do you guys think?
