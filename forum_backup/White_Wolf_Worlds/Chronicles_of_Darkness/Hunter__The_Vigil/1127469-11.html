    And that means Llannerch Athrawon is officially ready to be shipped. Sweet.


    So quick point of order: the page count estimate is actually pretty on point; the Compact pages that I have lain out plus the introduction pages reach just shy of 60 pages currently. We have the meat of the fanbook already figured out so how are we gonna do the trimmings?

    Each chapter should have a short little snippet of what is in it, just about a page's worth of information. Maybe a fiction before? That'd be 5 fictions in total, 4 chapter snippets (we already have the Introduction just about set).

    And we should think about clean-ups and cuts. Insofar, the majority of the cuts might be from the Conspiracies due to their overall number and length of each write-up (and including their Endowments at the end of their write-up. Chapter 4 is effectively entirely safe from cuts but it needs some filler text to deal with segues from Endowments to Slashers. There's also a few references to organizations that we don't have that I'll have to clean out (Harlequin's Roses, etc.) The book itself already has the layouts done and dealt with so anything that is officially finished can be thrown in, with dummy pages thrown in for those that don't have a splash page up yet (will get to that when I come home).

    If I had to quantify how far we are in terms of getting the book to full completion, I'd say we are at a respectable 45-55% done. 20% of what's missing is going to be images while the rest is mainly writing. And that isn't including the editing and polish phases. We're pretty solidly far but we basically need to get the prototype out.
