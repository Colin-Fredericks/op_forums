    Changeling Hunters

    12-09-2015, 09:38 AM
    I notice that there are no conspiracies dedicated to hunting Changelings. They are sufficiently weird and different enough that they should warrant a Changeling specific Conspiracy. I imagine a group of Druids who worship the Tuatha De Dannan and believe the Changelings are the True Fairies' rightful property, and at the behest of their gods will track down Changelings to return them to their masters. Their Endowment should be The Wyrd, a form a Druid magic that can do things like block a Changeling's access to the Hedge or render null and void a specific Contract a Changeling has if the Druid is aware of it. They would also have access to Changeling Lore that would allow them to develop tactics that are geared towards catching The Lost.

    I'm sure that Tier 1 hunters would go after a Changeling if, say for example, they didn't know what a Fetch was or that their family member or spouse was a Fetch this whole time, and they happen to walk in on a Changeling killing its Fetch. Imagine the horror of seeing your loved one turning into a pile of stones a twigs after being stabbed in the chest. This sort of Tier 1 introduction to the Vigil could serve as a gateway for a Teir 2 Compact consisting of people who have lost loved ones to Changelings, not knowing that their loved one was a Fetch. A support group of sorts that looks for people who have suffered this specific loss and tries to hunt down the Changeling that did it.

    What kind of Compacts, and Conspiracies (if you have a different idea than mine), and new Endowments would you make for Changelings?

                                              The others I could easily understand, but changelings always seemed the cowering victims of the World of Darkness. I appreciate they can be monsters as easy as everyone else but they're about the only template I thought hunter books were justified in for the most part leaving alone in Mortal Remains.

                                              Maybe some sort of 'Truther' organization that believes the numbers of missing persons in the world are inflated by shills paid by the government who create fake lives and leave them? It might not fit depending on how common fetches are. Perhaps they think fetches are some sort of undercover agents sent by the government to spy on the populace?

                                              What about a group that is aware of the magical power of changeling contracts, and hunts them in order to empower or enrich themselves?

                                              In my changeling game there was a group called the Order of Prometheus, which was tremendously misleading to those well aware of the various gamelines. But they had seemed to make a deal with a True Fae who wanted the others destroyed and, in turn, were working to militarize the Hedge, having captured changelings and forced them into pacts to keep portals open. It was seriously risky business practically destined to blow up in horrific fashion but the concept of people who weren't fully aware of the danger trying to take advantage of it for nationalistic or monetary purposes rang true.

                                                          There's a fan-splat for Hunter, Skin and Bones, that features a hostile-to-changelings dedicated compact/conspiracy. It's made up of Fetches and people who believe that the Fetches are the real people, with some added lies spun by a True Fae patron.

                                                          The reason I bring this up is that because this, to me, is the most likely origin for a changeling-dedicated compact or conspiracy, especially one of a hostile slant.

                                                          The big problem with hunting changelings is that they have the strongest masquerade of any of the existent supernaturals. Even demons are more inherently visible than changelings are, courtesy of glitches. Once you find them, you then have to come up with a justification for hunting them; it's not impossible, as the previous posters have pointed out, but at the same time, changelings are kind of the underdog of the World of Darkness. Unless you've seen them go insane, or been on the receiving end of the bad ones' depredations, generally you're going to go after them because of pure xenophobia.

                                                          There's a reason why most of the compacts and conspiracies in Dream Catchers tend to have a generic anti-fae approach, or even are more inclined towards the True Fae first, unless they're misled about the actual relationship between changelings and true fae, or prejudiced by the presence of fetches in their ranks.
