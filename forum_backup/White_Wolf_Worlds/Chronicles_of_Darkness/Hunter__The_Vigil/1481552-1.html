    The Hammer
    No one is certain what could have happened that turned Jennifer Kane into a killer. Her life until that point had been fairly typical for a middle class woman with no prior history of violence, abuse or trauma. Then one morning she crushed her husband’s skull with a sledge hammer while he slept. Even when arrested she gave no reasoning or alibi for her crime other than “It had to be done”. Prison psychologists theorize that Jennifer is operating under the delusion that some individuals “need” to die to prevent some apocalyptic event from occurring. She served several years of her sentence, becoming increasingly uncommunicative as time wore on. While being transported to a different prison, she snapped her restraints and attacked the driver, causing the bus to crash. She escaped in the ensuing chaos. Jennifer is currently at large, and seems to have vanished into the city’s transient and homeless population. Where once her violence was considered an isolated incident, bodies with their heads dashed in have begun appearing with alarming frequency.
    Jennifer is a woman of above average height with a stocky build, brown hair, and blue eyes. She dresses simply in jeans, flannel shirts, and sturdy boots.

    Undertaking: Mask; Virtue: Patient; Vice: Dispassionate
    Aspiration: Prevent the Apocalypse
    Tell: Jennifer kills by blunt trauma to the head, and prefers to use heavy sledges and hammers

    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 3
    Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
    Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 1, Composure 3

    Skills: Academics 1, Athletics 2 (Lifting), Brawl 5, Drive 2, Intimidation 3 (Silent), Streetwise 2, Survival 2 (Squatter), Stealth 3(Urban),Weaponry 5 (Hammer)
    Merits: Atavism, Area of Expertise (Hammer), Damnable Certainty, Face in the Crowd, Professional Training 2 (Vagrant; Survival, Streetwise), Style: Heavy Weapons 3

    Integrity: 0, Potency: 3, Willpower: 9
    Size: 5, Initiative: 6, Defense: 5
    Speed: 13
    Health: 9

    Weapons/Attacks
    Unarmed [+0B melee, 10 dice]
    Sledgehammer [+3L melee; -4 Initiative; 11 dice; Knockdown, Stun]

    Talent: Killing Machine Frailty: Only the Kill
    Dread Powers: Jump Scare, Predator’s Sense (the Marked), Regenerate

    Killing Machine: Like a Brute, a Mask does not suffer wound penalties and passes rolls to remain conscious due to damage or pain. Additionally, the Mask does not need to sleep or eat, and only suffers a single point of damage from any attack regardless of the damage or number of successes. This applies to mundane and supernatural forms of attack; only environmental damage such as fire, electrocution, or falling bricks applies damage normally.

    Only the Kill: A Mask has lost the ability to communicate. They are illiterate and are limited to simple gestures or grunts. To understand what’s been said to them, they must succeed at an Intelligence + Composure roll.

        Would it be possible to make a request ? This slasher is a musician who carves instruments from the victim's bones and flesh, dedicating them with occult ceremonies and inscribing binding runes upon them. They trap the souls using a corrupted version of the Ghost Binding Ceremony and build a secret conservatory that has everything from pipe organs to zithers. They are the grand maestro of the orchestra. Each performance is both hauntingly beautiful and carries a tone of urgent panic.

        The mystery cult believes that the end of all existence attempts to encroach upon us each day, but only those who know the signs can see it. More importantly, only those with the right kind of musical instruments, properly tuned to the different resonances of the musica universalis have any chance of driving it back. In their minds, no act can be as vile as the bone chilling horrors they have seen from the void.

        So in short, the idea is inspired by The Music of Erich Zann (the instruments are inspired by SCP-2678), normally they would be a Conspiracy, but they are so out of their depth fighting Abyssal incursions that only a slasher is ruthless enough to lead them, because most others break much faster.

                Hell’s Belle
                As the story goes, a young woman was stranded on the side of a lonely desert road, her car broken down and the nearest town a few hours away. While trying to flag down help, she was instead set upon by a roving gang of criminals, who kidnapped her and spent days abusing and torturing her before leaving her for dead. But she survived her ordeal and gained a burning desire for revenge. One by one, she tracked down and killed each one of her assailants, before vanishing into the night behind the wheel the gangs leader’s GTO. She is still out there, they say. Prowling the night, seeking the sort of men that have committed acts of violence and cruelty against women. While her goals are sympathetic, her methods are brutal and sadistic. She prefers to use a heavy revolver and stained baseball bat, but will use any tool necessary to enact her revenge. The Belle is a tall, athletic, beauty with cropped black hair and green eyes. Her flesh is crisscrossed with raised scars that seem to accentuate her looks. Her clothing is rugged jeans and tee shirts and she sports a silver crucifix, stained with so much blood it appears red. She wanders the roadways of the southwest in a black Pontiac GTO.

                Undertaking: Legend; Virtue: Survivor; Vice: Vengeful
                Aspiration: Seek Revenge upon “Bad Men”
                Tell: The Belle can be summoned by women to seek vengeance against men that have wronged them.

                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 3, Resolve 3
                Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 5, Stamina 3
                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 3

                Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 3, Crafts 2, Drive 3 (Muscle Cars), Firearms 3 (Revolver), Intimidate 3(Men), Investigate 2, Stealth 2, Streetwise 2, Weaponry 3(Clubs)
                Merits: Atavism, Crack Driver 2, Damnable Certainty, Iron Stamina 2, Iron Will, Striking Looks 2 (Dangerous Beauty)

                Integrity: 0, Potency: 4, Willpower: 10
                Size: 5, Initiative: 8, Defense: 7
                Speed: 13
                Health: 8

                Weapons/Attacks
                Unarmed [+0B melee, 7 dice]
                Baseball Bat [+1L melee;-1 Initiative; 8 dice]
                Heavy Revolver [+2L ranged;-2 Initiative; 9 dice]

                Talent: Living Legend (Abusive Men) Frailty: Narrative Chains (Cannot harm a Woman)
                Dread Powers: Predator’s Sense (Abusive Men), Surprise Entrance, Unholy Prowess (Strength)

                Living Legend: Once per scene, when someone fulfills one of the tenets of the Legend’s “rules,” the player can roll the slasher’s chosen Attribute + Occult. Successes on this roll may be applied on one of the following: restoring spent Willpower, healing damage (one success for a bashing wound, two for lethal, three for aggravated), or applying the bonuses to rolls in pursuit of their goal. After this has been activated, successes that haven’t been spent are discarded. They may not be banked to use in a following scene.

                Narrative Chains: The Legend gains a unique and specific frailty enforced by the nature of the tales surrounding them. Additionally, characters investigating the scourge’s Tell gain an exceptional success on three successes rather than five.

                Pontiac GTO: The Hell’s Belle drives a souped up black muscle car that shows the dents and scratched paint of multiple vehicular altercations. She may use Living Legend to repair damage to this vehicle.
                Dice Modifier: -2, Size: 12, Durability/Structure: 3/15, Speed: 110, Availability: 2
                *Modifications: High Acceleration (gain 10 Speed per turn)

                Unholy Prowess: The creature is quicker, stronger and hardier than it looks. The creature adds one dot of Strength, Dexterity or Stamina per dot of Unholy Prowess.

                    The Innocent
                    Ashley Chase is a young woman who has been plagued by tragedy her entire life. From her sister’s suicide, to the botched robbery that ended the lives of her parents, to the accidental overdose that killed her grandmother, she has faced this adversity with bravery, good cheer, and faith. Of course, she has been quietly behind each of these incidents, growing bolder and more inventive with her plans as time goes by. She has cultivated a group of friends that share a taste for true crime and other macabre fare, who she hasn’t grown bored with yet. Her appreciable inheritance has allowed her to begin college with few concerns about tuition, and she has plans to join a sorority. Once she is more educated (both academically and practically), she will resume her “hobby” in earnest.
                    Ashley is a slender brunette with blue eyes, who dresses in a fashionable if somewhat conservative manner. She plays the part of a cheerful and pious young woman, and keeps her darker interests a well-guarded secret.

                        Kill Doll

Instead of being figures of revulsion, Posie came to regard such killers as heroes and role models. These dark obsessions became reoccurring motifs in her work as a photographer and film maker. When an acquaintance told her of websites on the so-called “dark web” that streamed snuff footage of actual murder, she was hooked. So much so, the only way to get closer to the action was to make her own content. Now known as “Kill Doll”, she has has become a “murder-streamer” displaying footage of her kills to an exclusive audience that must download a special app and bypass several layers of security before being allowed access to the stream. She has even gathered a coterie of ardent followers and employs several “assistants” that want to get closer to the action. As Kill Doll, Posie dresses in provocative, fetish-style outfits that balance sex appeal with freedom of motion. She always wears a full leather or latex mask to hide her true identity.                         Mystery Cult: The Network
                        There are persistent rumors that in the deepest recesses of so-called “Dark Web” special sites and peer-to-peer networks that offer “Red Room” content. In at least one instance, the rumors are true. A criminal organization known only as “The Network” provides such content. What started as an operation that traded in illegal snuff films on 8mm film transitioned to video, and evolved with the digital age. Now using a highly encrypted peer-to-peer platform, The Network provides “snuff” footage of violent acts,, from animal cruelty, to torture, to murder,, either recorded or streamed live during special shows (with encrypted currency transfers for “tips”). Needless to stay, several government agencies in the United States and internationally are pursuing the people behind the Network, but thus far have been unable discover their true identities.


                        Purpose: To make a profit from extreme, illegal content.
                        Relic: Erebus v.404; a program that grants access to an encrypted peer-to-peer streaming platform.
                        Doctrine: The Network, in addition to its snuff content, provides support and resources for murder, ranging from one-time assassinations to evidence-removal services for serial killers. While it is rumored that The Network was established by organized crime or wealthy degenerates, its true origins are unknown.

                        Initiation Benefits
                        ● - Viewer. Those who discover or are invited to The Network are drawn in by the extreme content, typically out of morbid curiosity, and quickly become desensitized to the cruelty and violence on display. They gain the Tolerance for Biology Merit.
                        ●● - Subscriber. Regular subscribers log in routinely, following their favorite performers and becoming familiar with the online names of other regular posters. They gain a dot in the Contacts (The Network Community) Merit.
                        ●●● - Patron. Patrons dedicate significant resources to The Network, and are willing to perform the occasional “favor” for increase their standing in the community. They gain a dot in the Allies (The Network) Merit.
                        ●●●● - Content Creator. No longer content to merely observe; content creators actively contribute content and provide services to The Network, and are compensated for their efforts.
                        They gain three dots that may be distributed between the following Merits; Allies (The Network), Cover Up, Resources, Untouchable
                        ●●●●● - Admin. Admins are privy to the secrets behind The Network, and are sworn to utmost secrecy and discretion. The powers behind The Network are all wealthy, influential, and will not hesitate to make problem individuals “disappear”. Members at this level practice utmost secrecy, gaining 3 dots in the Alternate Identity or Anonymity Merit.

                                The Marked
                                Debra Stroud had lived a good, if mundane life. She was a single mother who struggled to make ends meet for her and her son. She eventually found love and was looking forward to starting a family with her fiance. All of that changed during a weekend camping trip. During that weekend, while hiking through the woods, Debra walked into a clearing and disappeared in a flash of blinding light. She was found three days later and ten miles away, rambling incoherently, naked except for the strange symbols burned into her body. Except for her head, hands, and feet, these symbols cover every inch of her flesh. She remembered nothing of the time between her disappearance and reappearance. As time passed, she became more and more obsessed with deciphering the meaning of her burn marks, and started to developed a strange cosmology to make sense of it all. She concluded that she was chosen to be a prophet of deity she calls the Great Machine. Her increasing obsession and zeal has alienated her loved ones, with her fiance leaving her and her son sent to live with relatives.
                                She is compelled by visions to reclaim the Great Machine’s power from those that are unworthy; targeting mediums, psychics, occultists, and similar “gifted” individuals. Only individuals that could be considered mortal are chosen as victims; creatures that no longer fit that definition escape her attention.
                                Debra is a tall, blond Caucasian woman in her mid-thirties. Once she carried a bit of extra weight, but an intense exercise regimen has given her a lean, athletic physique. She keeps her hair cropped close and dresses in rugged, functional clothing. Her body is covered with faded burn scars that appear to be occult symbols branded into her flesh. It takes considerable effort for her to restrain herself from speaking about the Great Machine, and she is always on the lookout for the next sacrifice.

                                Undertaking: Hypno; Virtue: Inquisitive; Vice: Zeal
                                Aspiration: To reclaim the power of the Great Machine from the “unworthy”
                                Tell: The Marked cuts symbols into the bodies of her victims.

                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 3, Resolve 5
                                Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 4, Stamina 4
                                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 3

                                Skills: Athletics 3 (Running), Brawl 2 (Restrain), Computers 2, Empathy 2, Investigation 3 (Public Records), Larceny 2 (Escape), Medicine 2, Occult 3 (Supernaturally Gifted Mortals), Socialize 2, Stealth 3, Streetwise 2, Weaponry 3 (Blades)
                                Merits: Atavism, Damnable Certainty, Danger Sense, Gut Feeling, Iron Will, Relentless, Striking Looks 1 (Marked Flesh), Style: Light Weapons 2

                                Integrity: 0, Potency: 2, Willpower: 10
                                Size: 5, Initiative: 7, Defense: 4
                                Speed: 12
                                Health: 9

                                Weapons/Attacks
                                Unarmed [+0B melee, 5 dice]
                                Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 7 dice]

From the outside, it is a discrete building with few indications about it’s true purpose. The interior is well appointed, with smooth marble floors and tasteful decorations. The valuables are secured behind heavy steel doors with sophisticated locks and the entire place is protected by state of the art surveillance and security systems.

                                                        Safe Place Features: Concealed, Home Security, Vault*
                                                        Traps: Alarm System, Surveillance

                                                        Feature-Vault: A Vault is a room or large container designed prevent intrusion and protect valuable goods. Attempts to break into a vault requires an extended Intelligence + Larceny roll with a (2+ dots in the Safe Place Merit) penalty that requires (2x dots in the Safe Place Merit) successes, with an interval of one minute per roll. For attempts to physically break into the vault, the durability of the barrier is (2+ dots in the Safe Place Merit).

                                                        Alarm System: The vault door as a built in alarm. This can be either an audible alert or a silent alarm. Audible alerts cause the Deafened Tilt and make anyone nearby aware of the character’s presence. Silent alarms notify the owner of the property and any relevant security agents.
                                                        Trigger: Booby-Trap. A successful Wits + Larceny roll with a -2 penalty avoids to triggering the alarm. Any failed rolls while attempting to breach the vault also triggers the alarm.

                                                        Surveillance: Surveillance cameras at key places are connected to a motion detector. Anyone that activates the camera has their actions observed and recorded for the remainder of the Scene.
                                                        Trigger: Motion Detector. A successful Wits + Composure roll with a -3 penalty avoids to triggering the camera. A character may also use Larceny to deactivate the camera, Stealth to avoid the camera, or Subterfuge to hide their identity.


                                                        Relic: Griswold’s Knife
                                                        This blade was recovered from the personal items of a suspected serial murderer named Linus Griswold. It is a sturdy hunting knife with a handle carved from deer horn. In 1978, authorities discovered several bodies in the backyard of Griswold’s property in Alberta, Canada while investigating reports that Linus Griswold himself had vanished under mysterious circumstances. The case generated some interest in the tabloids and a brief manhunt for Griswold, who was never found. The knife disappeared from police possession, only to appear as an item of interest in a different murder case several years later, where it again vanished.

                                                        Griswold’s Knife is of exceptional quality and is a useful tool in the wilderness. It grants a +1 equipment bonus to Survival rolls. The wielder of the knife cannot voluntarily dispose of the weapon. If separated from it by circumstances outside of their control, they gain the Deprived Condition until they get it back.
                                                        Memento: Griswold’s Knife has been touched by the power of death, and resists damage and destruction. It is never damaged by accidents or environmental hazards, has a Durability of 5 and twice its normal Structure. If using Geist: The Sin-Eaters 2nd Edition, Griswold’s Knife gives access to the Key of Blood.

                                                        Damage: +1L, Initiative: -1, Strength: 1, Size: 2 Durability: 5, Availability: 2

                                                        Cost: 1 Willpower
                                                        Dice Pool: Wits + (Survival or Weaponry)
                                                        Action: Instant

                                                        Success: Once per scene, the wielder of the Knife can apply the Bleeding Tilt when she damages her victim with an attack. In addition, damage dealt by the Griswold’s Knife is considered supernatural damage and can injure ghosts and other such ephemeral beings.
                                                        Exceptional Success: The Bleeding Tilt may apply to creatures normally immune to damage from bleeding or blood loss.
                                                        Failure: The relic does not activate.
                                                        Dramatic Failure: The relic cannot be activated for the remainder of the scene.

                                                        • Tilt: Bleeding: The character bleeds profusely from an open wound. If she doesn’t staunch the blood flow, she may soon pass out from shock and blood loss.
                                                        Effect: Bleeding characters suffer a –1 wound penalty (or have existing wound penalties increased in severity by +1). Additionally, each turn that she remains bleeding, she suffers one automatic point of bashing damage.
                                                        Ending the Tilt: If the character takes a turn to apply pressure and some sort of cloth or tie a tourniquet around the bleeding limb, she can stop the flow of blood and end the Tilt. Any form of accelerated healing, even a single point of bashing damage, will stop the bleeding as well. Creature that do not bleed are immune to this tilt.
                                                        (Chronicles of Darkness: Hurt Locker p. 143)


                                                        Relic: The Questing Compass
                                                        This rare compass is crafted of brass and glass by expert hands, with thirty-two directional points upon its face, a built in sundial, and fits in the palm of the hand. The item was crafted by an unknown artisan in the mid 1700’s, and despite its age, remains in good condition. The instrument has a colorful history, and is rumored to have passed through the hands of sailors, privateers, pirates and explorers. The needle of the compass points the way towards a person, place or thing that whoever is holding the Compass wishes to locate. The Questing Compass is immune to ordinary wear and tear caused by the passage of time.


                                                        Size: 1 Durability: 2, Availability: 3

                                                        Cost: 1 Willpower
                                                        Dice Pool: Wits + (Investigation or Survival) vs. Resolve + (Larceny or Stealth)
                                                        Action: Instant
                                                        Duration: Until the next Sunrise

                                                        Success: The user must speak the name of what they seek; the Compass’s needle will point in the direction of the target until the next sunrise. If the target is making an effort to evade pursuit, they may contest this roll with Resolve + (Larceny or Stealth). Once activated, the Questing Compass grants a +3 bonus to rolls involving locating, researching or pursuing the named target. Attempting to locate targets obscured by supernatural powers triggers a Clash of Wills.
                                                        While the Compass is active, it’s user gains the Obsessed Condition regarding the named target. This condition does not resolve until the target is obtained, or a new target is chosen.
                                                        Exceptional Success: In a Chase scene, the user of the Compass has the Edge if they are pursuing the named target.
                                                        Failure: The relic does not activate.
                                                        Dramatic Failure: The relic doe not activate but the user still gains the Obsessed Condition.


                                                        Relic: The Scarab of Ra
                                                        The Scarab of Ra is an amulet of gold sculpted into the shape of a scarab and featuring a single large ruby mounted on the carapace. The amulet is an antique of unknown age but has a distinctly Egyptian style.

                                                        The Scarab inspires envy and draw hungry gazes. The possessor of the amulet gains the Hunted Condition, and start to encounter those who want to relieve them of their possession.
                                                        Vessel: The Scarab of Ra is imprinted with the mystical energy that flows through all living things. The Scarab immune to ordinary wear and tear caused by the passage of time. Wearing the Scarab refreshes an extra Willpower point after a full night’s sleep. If using Mummy: The Curse 2nd Edition, the Scarab of Ra is a Vestige with a Virtue of Resilient, a Vice of Selfish, and up to three Pillar points.

                                                        Size: 0 Durability: 2, Availability: 4
                                                        Cost: 1 Willpower
                                                        Dice Pool: Presence + Occult
                                                        Action: Instant
                                                        Duration: One scene

                                                        Success: Upon activation, the amulet provides 2 armor, which applies to both general and ballistic attacks. This armor also provides a defense against supernatural attacks, giving her a +2 bonus to rolls to contest an action or a +1 to her resistance Attribute when targeted by a Dread Power or supernatural effect. The Scarab also protects against fire. Until the end of the scene, reduce any lethal damage from exposure to heat and flames to bashing.
                                                        Exceptional Success: The duration of the activation extends until the next sunrise.
                                                        Failure: The relic does not activate.
                                                        Dramatic Failure: The Scarab of Ra does not activate, and cannot be activated until the next sunrise.

                                                        • Condition: Hunted [Persistent]: Someone who poses a serious threat to the character’s safety and well-being, physically or emotionally (or both), is after her. They may be intent on direct violence, or simply wish to torment her.
                                                        Beat: The character’s persecutors find her.
                                                        Resolution: The character stops her persecutors, either through legal means, changes in lifestyle that deny them access to her, or through more direct means, typically violence.
