                            Kakure Kirishitan (隠れキリシタン?, Japanese for "Hidden Christian") is a modern term for a member of the Japanese Catholic Church during the Edo period that went underground after the Shimabara Rebellion in the 1630s.[1][2] After the start of the Sakoku period in 1639, it was officially prohibited from teaching the European Catholic religion.

                            It didn't fall completely apart and the Malleus may have more cells in Japan than one would expect.
                            There's two reasons I didn't call them out here:

                            a) I felt that I was already letting a little too much Western influence bleed in. That's an easy trap.

                            b) It's a REALLY complicated topic. Like literally, numerous books were written on the topic. I don't feel like I could do them justice in a small space, and if I gave them enough attention, they'd start to take up disproportionate space in the material.

                            There's clearly a parallel here. It's very intentional. But I wanted to infer it, not outright state it.
