                                                        EDIT: Speaking of Slashers, I wanted a break from art and wrote up a Ripper and Scourge.


                                                        Minder (Ripper)

                                                        “All my little children are playing by my rules. Keep this up and you’ll have a great big supper tonight.”

                                                        When a murder kills their victims, it’s usually out of some sort of hatred; resentment builds up to a boiling point or revenge calls out from their heart. When a Minder kills their victims, they’ll always be sure to point out that it was because of their love. They only want the best for their special one and they’ll make sure that their special one will act and behave exactly how they should. If they raise a complaint, they get a lash across the rear. If they raise a concern, they get a strike across the face. If they raise a hand, they get a broken wrist. But it’s only because their Minder knows what’s best.
                                                        Minders are an insidious thing. The love and kindness that they show their victims are entirely genuine and sincere but the sudden flash of violence and sadism is all the more true. And to the Minder, both the care and the pain they give their victims are entirely because their victims deserve every little bit of it. The poor little babies need a soft hand to bring them into this harsh world but a strong fist to make sure they don’t fall to the dirty, terrible vices that the Minder disapproves of.
                                                        And the punishments they give is nothing more but a reminder that the Minder has the final say. If idle hands wander, the Minder will make sure that every little bone is broken so they can wander no longer. If a foul word is uttered, the Minder will make sure the acid burns smoothly to rid that voice of any bad words. And should their sweetie even think of fighting back, the Minder will make sure that their little bodies rest comfortably as they fill their body with the killing drink.
                                                        As long as their wards respect their Minder’s authority, the Minder will care for them, watching over them like a mother hen and ensuring that they will be nice and safe. No pain, no tears, just nice and still, perfectly safe. The world around them slowly fading out as they dream the eternal dream.
                                                        Background: Fundamentally, Minders revolve around their control. Perhaps they grew up in an environment where their autonomy was stripped away and given to someone in a position of authority. Maybe their lack of control on circumstances outside of their grasp led to embitterment and a need to exert their opinions. Or maybe they were given full control over the life and needs of a person and relished in that fact. Whatever the case, Minders desire to put themselves in a situation where their victims are unable to act for themselves and need an outside force to help them survive.
                                                        Hunters that become Minders are often retired and acting mentor to the next generation. They know the cost of incompetence and fear for the safety of their wards’ Cell. They lash out because they know that even a moment of inattention could cause casualties across the board. Gradually and subtly, they become less understanding and more dominating; they weed out the problematic hunters and push up their favorites, punishing the dissenters and rewarding the good children. Once their punishments become fatal, they have completed their conversion to a Minder.
                                                        Self-made Minders often start off with good intentions; they are actual parents with high expectations for their children and snap when they fail or they’re devoted lovers who push for their partner to fix their lives. Some are nurses that committed mercy killings to save their patients from suffering, others are teachers that honestly believe that sparing the rod will spoil the child. However the case, this does not absolve the Minder of their actions; their actions are abusive and reprehensible, scarring the victims that make it out alive.
                                                        Surprisingly, Minders may possibly have the lowest turn-out rate when it comes to murders out of all Slashers. Their modus operandi revolves around entrapping one or more individuals and slowly gaining access to various facets of their life, often putting their victims under their thumb for years before finally disposing of them once their victims decide to fight back. Some Minders even prefer to lobotomize their victims, leaving them alive but utterly and entirely dependent on the Minder.
                                                        Inspirations: Annie Wilkes (Misery), Yamazaki Asami (Audition), Blue Jones (Sucker Punch)
                                                        Attributes: Manipulation or Composure
                                                        Undertaking Skills: Investigation, Medicine, Brawl, Weaponry, Empathy, Persuasion, Socialization, Subterfuge
                                                        Talent – It’s For Your Own Good: Minders care for their ward, for better or worse, and when a Minder wants to keep their wards in line, they make sure that the sting is a reminder. Whenever a Minder inflicts any form damage on their target using their fists, a melee weapon, or some form of physical torture, they instill a fear that digs itself deep into the victim’s mind. Against their specific victims, Minders automatically inflict the Insensate Tilt. Against outsiders, the Minder rolls Manipulation + Presence against the target’s Resolve + Composure.
                                                        Frailty – I’m So Proud of You: If there is anything that will take a Minder’s thoughts out of their desire to punish, it’s seeing their ward behave properly. Anyone who has spent a scene or more in the presence of a Minder instinctively knows the Minder’s Virtue. Furthermore, when someone acts in a way or manner that fulfills the Minder’s Virtue, it fills the Minder with pride and happiness. The Minder considers the target to be a non-threat to both the Minder and their victim until the target acts in a hostile manner. This may only occur once per person per scene and will not prevent others from being considered non-threats.

                                                        VASCU Case Study: The Good Mother
                                                        From an interview with Isaac G, victim of the Good Mother:
                                                        “…there were times when she would purposefully starve us because we made too much noise going up and down the basement stairs or because we looked outside of the window for too long. Sometimes one of us would misbehave and we all would be punished because she wanted to teach us a lesson.
                                                        “I remember when she made us watch when Caroline took a slice of bread with her. Mother mixed up some bleach with ipecac syrup because she wanted to wash out Caroline’s body. Said she wanted that piece of bread back. Caroline didn’t stop throwing up until blood was running down her nose and onto her shirt. Then Mother made her clean up the vomit with her clothes.
                                                        “…my hand. I used to play the piano. Was going to school to be a classical performer. She…she told me that I kept playing the wrong notes. I knew I was playing it right. I KNEW I was. But she didn’t like it. Said I went too high or too low or too fast or too slow. I-my hand…she used the hot water be-because she knew I wanted to play b-b-but…then she held my h-h-hand in the…the drawer. A-a-and she…she…”

                                                        Master (Scourge)

                                                        “Dogs aren’t supposed to talk.”

                                                        While a Minder’s game is about control, there is a twisted logic behind the Minder’s behavior; the Minder sees their victims as an equal (somewhat) and wants to see their victims happy. With a Master, there is no desire to treat their victims with any sort of respect; they are the Master and their victims are nothing more than animals that bow at their feet. Rewards are few and infrequent while punishments are more proactive than reactive. They want to keep their pets, their dogs, in line and the most effective way to do so is with the fear of pain.
                                                        Stripped of their compassion, Masters have no true love for their victims. Maybe they feel some sort of pity for such a creature or a carnal desire for their flesh; Masters only care for their pets because they are useful, despite their disgusting figure and pathetic displays of emotion. They are entirely divorced from their victims, having no meaningful connection to the naked animals slobbering all over themselves. These dogs only exist for the entertainment of the Master
                                                        Masters thrive on the fact that they rule over a lesser being; even if they do not hold any exceptional power outside of this domain, Masters are more than willing to create the illusion that they alone are the core to their victim’s lives. Their pets have no need to think or want; their Master holds their leash and will make sure that they are given a life that they deserve.
                                                        And they, the Masters, are always looking for new pets to add to their collection. As long as there are dogs willing to beg, the Masters will come to round them up and place them in the kennel, along with the rest.
                                                        Background: When a Minder finds a new victim, they are overcome with a desire to protect. Minders, however twisted they are, work on an internal logic that they themselves are good people and are capable of rearing good people with their victims. They may harm but it’s only so that their victims can heal; it’s all because of their perverted compassion.
                                                        Masters don’t care for their victims. Masters consider themselves to be on a level above and beyond that which dogs are even able to manage. Minders that begin to see themselves as better than their victims or tire of having to care for all of their children are on the path to becoming a Master. Once they realize that their control over their wards can be further abused to force their victims to act against their own will, they start their descent.
                                                        The creation of a Master outside of being a slasher is surprisingly easy. A cult leader who can manipulate their herd into massive rallies and violent riots has all the shades of being a Master. Likewise, the CEO of an established company that sends out lackeys to harass and potentially drive others to suicide is more or less a Master awaiting the inevitable breakdown into pure sociopathy.
                                                        Their ability to not only dominate but eliminate any sense of self from their victims is what makes the Master a Scourge. Their victims blindly worship their Master because that is the only way that they feel any form of satisfaction. At their Master’s command they will act; if their Master wishes to kill one off for entertainment, they will gladly tear one another apart if it pleases. The invisible collar around their necks is what binds them to their Master’s will.
                                                        Inspirations: Sofia Lamb (Bioshock 2), Justine (Amnesia: The Dark Descent), Lola Stone (The Loved Ones),
                                                        Attributes: Manipulation or Composure
                                                        Undertaking Skills: Investigation, Medicine, Politics, Brawl, Weaponry, Empathy, Intimidation, Persuasion, Socialization, Subterfuge
                                                        Talent – The Hand that Feeds You: A dog should obey its Master. Whenever a Master captures a victim, they impart the idea that the Master is the only thing that matters in the world to them. The Master’s specific victims are unable to access Social merits relating to individuals outside of their relationship with the Master (Mentors, Retainers, True Friends, etc.). Furthermore, the Master’s specific victims under the Insensate Tilt as applied by the It’s For Your Own Good become fiercely loyal to the Master and their commands, acting with hostility to those who threaten or harm their Master; they will blindly follow the Master’s command no matter how dangerous. They also gain the persistent Amnesia Condition that pertains to any memory before they met the Master. This effect does not occur to outsiders affected with by the Insensate Tilt.
                                                        Victims can be deprogrammed with an extended action. As long as the victim is away from the Master’s presence, a third party may roll Wits + Empathy to attempt to rehabilitate the victim. The process takes one week per roll and requires a total of 10 + the Master’s Manipulation + Composure to fully deprogram as well as remove the Amnesia Condition. If the victim makes contact with the Master at any time, the process is reset.
                                                        Frailty – If I Can’t Have You: Masters are empowered by their control; denial affects them in ways they refuse to acknowledge. Whenever a Master fails either a Social roll or an attempt at using the It’s For Your Own Good Talent on a target, they suffer from the Deprived Condition in regards to the target’s submission to the Master’s control. Furthermore, as long as the Deprived Condition remains, the Master begins to suffer from a self-destructive streak; they take out their frustration on objects, pets, anything in order to vent. The Master cannot regain Willpower and suffers 1 point of Bashing damage for each day that the target spends away from the Master. This effect ends once the Master resolves the Deprived Condition by making the target submit to their power.

                                                            Rough outline for the Chameleon, what does it need?

                                                            Chameleon

                                                            “I’ve just got one of those faces.”

                                                            Robin Dunbar proposed a concept that the human brain only has the cognitive ability to maintain roughly 150 stable relationships. While some have criticized it the theory on the basis that this ignores the many social connections necessary to build up human civilization at all, no one can deny that they all have many people they can remember and care enough about to interact with on a regular basis. Everyone else is a face in the crowd, shifting through the observer’s life with hardly a thought about the anonymous beings walking about around them.

                                                            What happens to the people who manage through no fault of their own to slip between the cracks of everyone else’s sphere? Who somehow go nearly their entire lives without close friends or romances? Some just go on with their lives, quietly living as they do and ignoring the loneliness that aches at their hearts. Others feel offended by this, angered that their lives are somehow unseen and unheard by those around them. Maybe it started in childhood when they realized no one noticed when they broke a rule and got away with it. Perhaps it carried on later into adulthood when they finally broke and went to meet new people in the most lethal way possible.

                                                            A Chameleon is aptly named, able to slip in and out of any location simply by not drawing attention to themselves. Their targets may think they were nothing more than an accidental bump into a shoulder on the subway, a passing nod to a stranger on the street, but to the Chameleon these run ins were their determination for who they would kill next. Some of them kill because they feel slighted in some way by their victims. That they were being mocked in some way by their new target, walking around parading their value in front of the slasher. Others see it as an obsession, not unlike a stalker finally killing the very person they have their obsession centered on. If they can’t be the only focus on their target’s attention, no one else can be.

                                                            What makes a Chameleon dangerous is that they easily blend in anywhere. They’re the “killer cop”, putting flashing lights on the roof of his car and buying a prop badge to lure out the unsuspecting motorists he kidnaps and kills. He’s a lowly janitor, fired months ago but still coming in to work because no one cares enough to check his badge to see it’s out of date. All he is, is a face in the crowd that can’t be seen. It’s not that he’s a master of disguise, in fact many of the chameleons captured barely have what could be considered a “wardrobe” at all. Their “gift” is that the rest of the world simply considers them invisible.

                                                            Background: Chameleons don’t have what could be considered a “process” for choosing a victim. Most of the time the victim only knows the chameleon as, “That one guy” they see at work or in their apartment complex. To the chameleons, their victims is another human being that pays no attention to them. It may be that the chameleon considers them someone that mocks their “ignoring” the slasher, or maybe they’re the people who are the friends and family of the one person that shows the chameleon any sign that they notice them. In either case, the chameleon is a patient predator. They slowly move themselves into the lives of those they follow without ever being noticed as best they can. They track their targets, trailing them to and from work, about their lives, and inevitably either growing envious of the fact that other people care about them, or of the others who spend time with the person who shows them the attention they so sorely lack. Like their namesakes, Chameleon slashers are swift killers. Once their prey is utterly at their mercy, the slasher strikes without mercy.

                                                            Not many hunters wind up as Chameleons, they’re ingratiated too greatly with their cells to be unnoticed in the same way these poor souls are. The people who wind up becoming Chameleons are more often than not just people. Ignored by everyone around them, anyone who does show them even the slightest bit of attention can immediately find their lives turned upside down as their loved ones, coworkers, even employees at stores they frequent are killed off or up and vanish.

                                                            More twisted Chameleons realize that their only real interaction with humanity is via murder. Rather than wallowing in their loneliness, these killers strike out to find those they can kill. Such killers can hound the police for years, most famously in the case of a person known only as “The Smiley Face Killer”. Only two retired detectives have managed to put the information together that links the murders, but the FBI officially stated, “…The vast majority of these instances appear to be alcohol-related drownings.” In the background, VASCU desperately tries to catch up with the Smiley Face Killer, knowing that their target can be anyone, anywhere, and they wouldn’t even know it until they find another body in a lake or river.

                                                            Inspirations: Zartan (G.I. Joe: Rise of Cobra); False Face (Batman); Carlos the Jackal (Day of the Jackal)

                                                            Talent: A Thousand Masks

                                                            Frailty: Telltale Sign

                                                            VASCU Case File: “The Newsboy”

                                                            Notes from Det. Frank Bianchi, NYPD

                                                            Uniforms found another last night. Young girl, couldn’t be older than 22, no ID just like the others. Stabbed multiple times, left to bleed out, posed to look like she was homeless. Lucky for us, the spot where she was left actually belonged to a bum.

                                                            The captain’s getting on my ass about this one, but we don’t fucking know where to start. Every witness doesn’t remember anyone really standing out. They don’t remember anyone talking to the victim, her friends don’t mention anyone following her the past few days, it’s all turning up empty.

                                                            The only connection between the victims is that they all stopped at the same news stand to grab a magazine or newspaper. Owner said he didn’t see anything suspicious either, no one tracking them or acting like they were too interested.

                                                            Christ, they’re gonna have my shield if we don’t figure this one out soon. I’ll go back to the stand today, see if the guy might’ve remembered something since the last time we spoke.
