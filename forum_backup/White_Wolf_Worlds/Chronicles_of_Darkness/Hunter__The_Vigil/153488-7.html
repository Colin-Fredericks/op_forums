    143) Binge eating makes you lose weight, the greedy face unparalleled lucky streaks at the gambling table, murders go uninvestigated, pride comes before great success, and slacking off causes someone else to do your job for you while you still get credit! Someone or something is causing people's vices to turn beneficial, rather than dangerous and its up to your cell to figure out the cause. Because who knows what might happen to the city when it figures out life has no consequences!

        144) What do you when the kids have made deals with the local Witch? And not out of fear, but... love. She doesn't lie to them, and unlike their teachers or parents, is always there when they want a shoulder to cry on. She even brings them Christmas presents ever year. All she asked in return was that, if ever someone came around wanting her dead... they stand by her. Would you go through them just to kill someone for using magic?

        145) (Malleus Maleficarum) All the faithful have been following the words of... a Demon. Or at least, that's what you're told he is. But if he's a Demon, why does he protect his flock so fiercely from other Demons? Or for that matter, why would he hunt the Vampires down the street? Is there something you aren't being told? And why this focus in all his preaching about not being fooled by demonic trickery?

            146: You broke down the door, but no one was there. You kicked out the windows, but the second floor was clear. You started a fire, but the fire department never found any body. He wasn't a witch, fairy, ghost, nothing but someone your cell needed answers from. So how'd he escape?

            147: (Malleus Maleficarum) The pope has decided that he'll be coming to Philadelphia. You have one year to prepare the city for his arrival. Ally with any hunter who's willing to work alongside you, and pacify those that won't. Ensure that when the Holy Father comes to the City of Brotherly Love, he will be safe from those that would attack St. Peter's heir.

            148: You know that game that just got released? The one that was supposed to show a very conspiracy filled version of history? Someone decided the publisher and developer were revealing truths that hit a little too close to home. They sabotaged development, and as of now only one unaltered version of the game remains that could answer a lot of questions. Find that single unaltered copy, and the conspiracy's secrets.

                149: Cats are a mixed bag in folklore. Some cultures revile them, others treat them as minor deities. Which stories are true? You'd better find out, because a new cult in the area is killing all the cats they can find without giving a reason, and a lot of families want payback for losing their pets.

                150: The local vampire lord is dead, and for a short time things were awesome. But now things are getting worse than before. It turns out that vampires prefer a power structure to run things, and by killing the grand poo-bah, you've opened up a vampire turf war for blood slaves and money laundering. As distasteful and against everything you stand for as it is, you need to accomplish the unthinkable. Find a vampire with a backing, and help him take control of the city before it's too late.

                    151: Something really powerful, obviously supernatural and visibly terrifying is stalking your town. Fortunately it has one of the most broad Banes you've ever encountered, it will die if it's ever attacked by someone who does not believe in the supernatural. The catch is that they have to intentionally attack, you can't just give them a gun and a blindfold.

                    So you need to find someone who's mental filters against the supernatural are so strong they can look at this thing and still disbelieve it, but who you can convince to pull the trigger.

Unfortunately, your cell has a problem with one in your town. Specifically, the werewolves who want to conscript him, and the zealots who want to kill him. Or will someone have to make a sacrifice for the good of everyone else?

                                155: (MM or Long Night) There's a monster that can strip the religious and moral beliefs of whoever it gets into contact with for one hour. Entire congregations have dwindled away as people in mass lose their beliefs. What creature is doing this? And more than that, who could stand against it? Someone with no beliefs to lose? And how do you spot this entity without succumbing to its effects too? It does seem to strip Hunters of their memories relating to the supernatural in the same package.

                                156: (Task Force Valkyrie): There's a mole. You don't know who they are, or who they are working for, but team after team is getting utterly butchered by the monsters specifically preparing for their arrival. The casualties are getting to the point that Task Force Valkyrie will have to halt all operations until the mole is found. But... what will happen on American soil without the presence of Task Force Valkyrie?

                                    157: Local firefighters are being picked off on the job, being caught in burning buildings or dying from their hoses malfunctioning and pummeling them. Local departments keep saying it's all tragic coincidence, and that they're taking greater steps to ensure the safety of their men. You know that someone is looking to kill all the firefighters for a reason.Two questions: Why, and how do you stop them?

                                    158: For some reason, aliens from outer space are not a focus of the vigil, despite the absolute necessity of the fact that hunters need to expect anything. So why does it feel like hunters like to forcefully ignore alien abductions with claims of the fae, or other dimensions, or even the freaking government? Why doesn't anyone want hunters talking about UFOs?

                                                161: Someone is spreading false stories to rookies in your area. Telling them silver nitrate can kill any werewolf, that zombies aren't as dangerous as movies say they are, that vampires can be killed with a cheap battery-powered UV light. It's getting people killed or worse, you only found out because you captured a recently turned vampire who begged for death. He said before he was ashed that it was, "the man with the black eyes."

                                                162: The Dead Kennedys weren't wrong, football has started to get creepily cult-like. Your investigation into the local high school confirmed it when you saw the boys bleeding themselves over a football chanting for victory. If this is a cult phenomena, the scary question to ask is, how far has it spread in the sport?

                                                163: The local cops didn't ask too many questions, and your cell has always been cautious enough to keep most answers to yourselves. Then some cub reporter looking to cut his teeth and get set for prime time decides to start raising hell about all the unsolved "disappearances" in the town. The police are starting to feel the public asking questions, and they're up against the wall to find a reason they've been so ineffectual. Should you back down and risk the town falling out of your control? Or does this reporter need to learn not everything is newsworthy the hard way?

                                                164: (Silver Cross, Skin and Bones) One of your clinics in Cleveland called; there's something spreading through the vampires, and so through anyone they bite. They've narrowed it down to a strange inverse of hemophilia, tentatively titled "hemophobia"; when the skin is cut, the clotting is so severe that it can threaten to block vessels in the body. You're being brought on as a kind of on-site triage team. Find patient zero no matter their state of life or death, and figure out what the hell happened to the blood.

                                                    165: Many supernaturals live in fear of the possibility that if mortals ever rallied against them, they could be wiped out. Well your organization finally got wise of this and are planning to do just that. They're stock piling weapons and resources for an all out war against the freaks who think humanity is their plaything! But not all within the organization support this idea and believe countless people will be caught in the cross fire. So the question is, which side will your cell take in the coming war?


                                                    166: Someone has infiltrated the organization for the past several months and been killing officers and other high ranking members. You've finally discovered the culprit is a body snatcher, who's been using their immortality for assassination contracts. When the killer could be anyone, you can't afford the luxury of trust. So how do you catch this assassin before he destroys your organization's chain of command?

                                                        167: Sometimes, even hunters have moments of good luck. You won the lottery, or maybe a plane ticket, a weekend getaway, whatever. But after you've arrived at the resort and checked your bags, you start to notice little things about the other guests - habits, tics, body language...then it all comes together when you recognize another hunter. In fact, all the guests are hunters, from no-name cells who've just become aware of the supernatural to conspiracy bigwigs.

                                                        And then the lights go out, the escape routes get closed off, and the killing starts.

                                                        Someone went to a lot of trouble to get you all to an isolated location, away from your support and your armory. But you're a hunter. You've been in tough spots before. Sure, most of the hunters here hate each other, but you all hate monsters more, so just give the crowd one good speech to get them into the mood and you'll all be raring to go a-slaying!

                                                        ...Assuming that the killer isn't one of the guests.

                                                        ...Or that some the guests decide this is the perfect opportunity to settle some old scores.

                                                        ...Or assuming that someone planned this. Coincidences do happen. Maybe all you need to start a bloodbath is one fatal accident and a lot of paranoia.
