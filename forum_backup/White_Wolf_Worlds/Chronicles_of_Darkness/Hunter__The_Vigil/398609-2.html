        Narodnaia Volia- Anarchy (0-00000)
        False promises of freedom can only get you so far, and the hunger to disrupts unjust undead rulers can shatter every chain. While the blood of the Kindred is addicting, certain members of the group has developed tolerance toward it- and from there the rest is history. Each dot in Anarchy represent an additional step needed in order to form a Vinculum (that is, a member with 3 dots in the Endowment needs 6 drinks in order to become bound to a vampire). In an addition, by putting their hard won freedom in danger they may gain further power in fight against death's tyrants- by drinking a vampire's fresh Vitae, the hunter may gain access to a single Discipline the vampire has for the next 24 hours. The vampire only needs to know the Discipline in order for the hunter to use it, and the effective rating the hunter has in it equal's to the Endowment's rating. In order to power the Discipline the hunter may either spend WP points or cutting himself (inflicting Lethal Damage) equal to the cost of the Discipline (in case there is no cost, spend either a single WP point or deal a single Lethal Damage to activate the power). The hunter may not have two active Disciplines in the same time, and each time he wish to switch powers he needs to drink again another dose of Vitae.

            Cult of Mitra- Divine Fate (0-00000, Special)
            The human members of the Ivory Court protect their gods from the dark Daeva who seek to pull the Peri back to their iron cages, and Fate stands on their side when they do so. When taking the Merit the hunter choose a single Fate, which could be described in a single word (like Warrior, Scholar, Lover, etc). The hunter can use that Endowment in a number of ways- first, he can use that Fate to help them in their goal. By expanding WP point, the hunter may add her dots to a specific roll which works in the theme of their Fate (for example, a Warrior may add those dots when he attack seeking glory in battle, while a Lover would have to protect his special one from an enemy). Second, the hunter could roll her dots in a certain Fate in order to see the future- or, more exactly, her role in the story. She may add her Divine Fate dots to any Investigation roll which is meant to show her the preferred course of action considering her Fate, although she does not include the finale outcome of her role (including possible death). While the character may take more than one Fate, she must follow the theme of her chosen destiny- and if she defies it somehow (like a Warrior running away from battle) she would lose one WP point and would not be able to use that Fate for the rest of the Chapter.

                I personally don't think there need to be Endowments for every group. One of the greatest strengths of Hunter: the Vigil is the fact that it recognizes the existence of hunters without powers, both as individuals and groups, and that it gives us a distinction between groups with powers and groups without. I think that people who play these games get a little bit too hung up on the wikki powers and forget the rest of the character sheet. They forget that there are tactics, equipment, and other merits that could work to the player's advantage; many of which can be gained through role play.

                For example a lot of people seem to forget about Exorcisms and Abjurations as mentioned in the core rules book. Exorcisms and Abjurations are tactics that work like Endowments against ghosts, spirits, and demons, and would be used almost exclusively by The Long Night, being a Protestant Compact, and would resultantly make them a very effective compact against ghosts, spirits, and demons. I would not expect compacts like Null Mysteriis, Ashwood Abby, or the Bear Lodge, for example to use Exorcisms and Abjurations even if they knew about them. Even though Exorcisms and Abjurations are not hidden or secret knowledge, the knowledge of how to do them is different for different religions, and in some cases does in fact require specialized training. One example of this is the real life Vatican hunter conspiracy called the International Order of Exorcists (IOE). Seriously, it actually exists, go look it up. Every member of the IOE is flown to the Vatican in Rome to receive specialized training in the procedures, rites, rituals, and psychology of performing a proper exorcism. In fact, how the IOE hasn't made it into the Hunter RPG as a Vatican Conspiracy or Compact along side MM, I'll never know, but whatever. My point is there are other methods of having an organization leveling the playing field other than Endowments. Many Compacts may focus on training their members up in skills and tactics to level the playing field rather than developing Endowments. Bear Lodge might teach its members how to craft and lay traps for werewolves, like a bouncing betty land mine that sends silver shrapnel in every direction. It's not an Endowment, but it would work better on a pack of werewolves than a Doru relic.

                If you want your players to belong to a Compact, but still have powers or similarly awesome equipment to level the playing field, then may I suggest looking beyond Endowments and making the powers a part of your character rather than a part of the organization they are in. Take a look at Second Sight, and make some psychic powers as part of your character, rather than part of their organization. Or instead of making it part of your character, make it something they get through role play. Look into the Reliquary book, and have a non-Aegis Kai Doru hunter group (like Loyalists of Thule) go on a relic-based hunt that lands them a neat piece of equipment that they can use in a future story line. Aegis Kai Doru doesn't have to know about it. They may be a relic-centered conspiracy, but they are not all-knowing nor omnipresent. As for more mundane nifty equipment, if you want your werewolf hunters to have some big guns, have a look at the Armory book and see what some of the biggest guns are. If they want an AGTM missile launcher that does 20L damage, then let them go through the trouble of getting one on the black market to shoot the werewolves with. And I do mean all the trouble, including a possible visit from the FBI, which may or may not include the VASCU (which in turn could get your hunters conscripted into a VASCU suicide squad, which has role play potential of its own).

                My point is, let's not obsess too much over Endowments. They are only one aspect of the hunter game, and they are not even the most interesting aspect at that. As a friend of mine used to say, "It's called role play, not roll play."

                            so, let's talk Mystery Cults:

                            Chosen of Mammon: Primal Utility (1-5)
                            Money makes the world turn around, and everything has a price- and Mammon's faithful has the highest value coin to pay with. Primal Utility represent the sum of the connections and resources one gets thanks to the cult, allowing them to get their hands on anything they wish- for a sort period of time. That Endowment is a single, 1-5 merit, which act as some sort of Resources merit. However, instead of giving access for material wealth, it grants access to other merits: the cultist may get the effect of any merit which its rating is equal of lesser than her Primal Utility rating. That includes merits she usually does not have access to, like Supernatural Merits, other Endowments and even Merits meant for supernatural creatures. In order to get the Merit, the cultist must go through a "purchase" treatment- like going to the bank in order to get Resources or visiting some underground clinic for a psychic ability. The Merit is in effect for a number of days equal to their dots in Mystery Cult Initiation, and may not grant more Merit dots than the total rating of the Primal Utility.

                            Siblings in Arms: Big Guns (1-5, special)
                            The Sisters of the Machine Gun and the Brothers of the Bomb has worked for long time in order to fight against the Machine and its loyalists. Using reverse engineering and understanding of the God Machine's occult matrices, they have developed a special kind of weaponry- one which may grant humanity the edge needed to escape the terrible will of its tyrant. Each Big Gun is a separate merit, doted 1-5, which hold a single Numina per dot. In order to activate the Big Gun, the cultist must feed it with Essence- that is, the type it is aspected to when created. The Gun in question has an Essence pool equal to its rating, and may regain Essence either as the aspected Ephemeral being or by being in the presence of the destruction of such being. Needless to say, most Guns are Angelic in nature, but more than a few are bound with ghostly or spiritual nature, if not weirder forms of Essence.

                            Fellowship of the Finale Answer: Anomaly (1-5)
                            Seattle is a place where time shatter and resemble, and from those shards things may get out. Escaping their original timeline and ultimate fate, the demonologists has escaped their personal Hell and became into an anomaly on the face of the world. Anomaly is a single merit rated 1-5, which serves as some sort of "Cover" for the members, which masks them from agents of both Heaven and Hell, and each person who joins the cult becomes part of that anomaly. That Cover both works as a Supernatural Tolerance trait at an half of its actual value (rounded up) and allows them to Spoof as if its rating was equal to Cover. However, for the cultists Spoofing is a deliberate act- they can feel that something is searching after them or that perhaps they wish to make someone believe them no matter how weird is the lie they are going to tell. That act requires a Willpower point to do so. That "Spoofing" may also allow them to hide their thoughts or targeting them with sympathetic magic.

                            Church of Inti: Sunfire (1-5)
                            The light of the Burning Star leaves no one untouched, and those who worship the idigam as Inti and survived are able to channel its heavenly Essence into their bodies as they hunt after those werewolves who dare to challenge their god. Sunfire is a single merit, rated 1-5, which allows three different uses- first, it acts as Influence: Fire with an equivalent rating, although instead of Essence it must be powered with blood, self inflicting one level of Lethal Damage per required Essence. Second, the cultist may activate it in order to engulf himself with such golden fire, something which requires one Willpower point and one level of Lethal Damage. As long as the "Cloak of Fire" is active, the cultist may interact with beings from the spiritual Twilight as if they were manifested, and even see beyond the Gauntlet. That power is active for one minute per dot in the Endowment. Finally, one can burn like a star, releasing all of Inti's Essence as a single, terrible blast. In order to do so, the cultist suffer one level of Aggravated Damage- and deals a number of levels of Aggravated Damage equal to his dots in the Endowment. For beings which are harmed by fire or sunlight, that damage is doubled. It is considered to be a ranged attack, with a range of one meter per dot in the Endowment.

                            Voyagers: Kotoba no Kokai (1-5, special)
                            words and blood are the will of humanity, and by ruling both one may extract control over both this world and its shadow. The cultist can make special ofuda written in their own blood, each is connected to a single Influence among the five Chinese elements which were adopted by the Onmyodo- that is, Fire, Water, Earth, Metal and Wood. In order to make such talisman, the cultist must self inflict a single level of Lethal Damage, and in order to activate it he must throw it on the target and spend Willpower point. When making the Talisman the hunter rolls for Crafts, when Failure destroys the Talisman and Dramatic Failure creates a flaw which can not be seen. Exceptional Success grants a Willpower point. Once the Talisman is activated, it creates the appropriate Influence where it touches. By ST's decision, other elements or aspects of reality may be invoked using the Endowment.

                            Tick Tock Men: Bioclock (1-5)
                            The cult's obsession with the perfection of time has developed enough to create a new heart for its members- a clockwork machinery which always ticking, always measuring, always watching. Bioclock acts as a single, 1-5 merit, which channel the nature of time into their body. For every dot in the Endowment, the cultist may preform another action during her turn. While that power is terrifying, it does have a price- for the human mind and body were not meant to withstand such flow of time. After the end of the Scene in which the Endowment was activated (which requires a single Willpower point per use) the player has to choose whether to roll for Integrity with a -1 modifier per additional action taken during that Scene or suffering one level of Bashing Damage per such extra action.

                            I think those are all the canon Mystery Cults- feel free to tell me if I missed someone! :P

                                    so, let's add the new Dark Eras compacts to the list-

                                    Keepers of the Weave- Tale Binding (0-00000)
                                    Stories are meant to protect those who hear them, as they give face and a name to hidden terrors found in the night. With a form given to such fears, the dream may become reality, and stories becomes a weapon to hold against them. Tale Binding is a 1-5 merit which allows the hunter to both gain information about a monster so they may discover its weakness. By rolling [Tale Binding] + [Intelligence] + [Occult], the hunter may draw upon mankind's collective conscious in order to find a story which describes the monster's existence and weaknesses, giving them both the general information about their target and +[Endowment] modifier to every attack roll against that monster. By preforming [Tale Binding] + [Presence] + [Expression] roll, anyone who hears that story enjoys that bonus as well. Dramatic Failure would make it the wrong story, giving you or your friends -[Endowment] modifier, while Exceptional Successes also grants a Willpower point to anyone who hears the story while fighting the monster.

                                    A more interesting effect of the Endowment comes when the hunter tries to force a Tale upon the monster, forcing a persona upon it. In order to do so, the hunter must roll [Tale Binding] + [Manipulation] + [Expression] and expand a Willpower point against the monster's supernatural tolerance as normal. If the hunter wins, the monster is no under the spell- as long as the hunter keeps preforming the story, the monster suffers from -[Endowment] modifier as it start suffering from sudden memories of thoughts which does not belong to it but of the being the story tales, and the hunter may also choose a single weakness which would deal Aggravated damage to it. Those effects may only target monsters.

                                    Protectors of the Light- Ward (0-00000)
                                    The Protectors live together with the land and know it better than anyone else- they respect it, and in return it gives them a shard of its power in order to cleanse it from the foul monsters and keep the light shining. The Ward is a single, 1-5 merit which establish a connection between the hunter and the land. Each dot in the Endowment gives the hunter a different ability, as detailed bellow-

                                    0- Mind's Eye: the hunter can feel any material being in a radius of 1 meter per dot in Presence, although she doesn't know its identity, intentions or if it is a supernatural being.
                                    00- Earth Bound: the hunter may add a single dot to a Physical Attribute for free. It may not rise an attribute above 5.
                                    000- Spirit Sight: the hunter may now recognize beings in the Twilight, recognize if a being is a supernatural or not and discover if it is hostile, friendly or indifferent.
                                    0000- Land Keeper: the hunter may add another dot to any attribute she wish for free. The hunter may rise an attribute above 5.
                                    00000- Life's Fountain: the hunter enjoys Regeneration, as a Warden (see Immortals), may project his mind into the Twilight to scout her territory (as in Mind's Eye) by spending a Willpower point per hour (she may change frequencies by spending another) to interact with the beings in there and may transform blood into Essence to bargain with spirits (presented by self inflicting Lethal Damage).

                                    In an addition, the hunter gets one free Environmental Tilt per dot in the Endowment. By spending a Willpower point, the Tilt comes into effect in a range equal to the hunter's Presence, although she must spend a Willpower once per 10 minutes to maintain it. She may activate a number of such Tilts in the same time. Anyone who is not a member of the Protectors of the Light is effected by such Tilts. There is, however, a downside to the Endowment- for as the shard of light in the hunter grows, such her body becomes tied more and more to the land- by taking the first dots in the Endowment, the hunter limits herself to a specific region, and that regions becomes tighter and tighter as time pass by. Once leaving that region, the hunter lose access to her abilities, and suffers Lethal Damage as detailed below-

                                    0- a Continent, 1 LD per year
                                    00- a sub-region, 1 LD per month
                                    000- a country, 1 LD per week
                                    0000- a district/state/metropolis/region/any other sub-nation division, 1 per day
                                    00000- a city or a similar community/territory, 1 per hour

                                    the ST is the one to approve if a certain territory answers the demands. Traveling through mental projection outside of such territory is allowed, in case she has enough Willpower. For each dot in presence the hunter has, she may extend her territory by 1 km and postpone the damage by 1 time unit.

                                        and now- the Aztec Mystery Cults:

                                        Cuauhualtin- Ihhuitzoncalli (0-00000)
                                        The Eagle Warriors of the empire wear the shape of their companion as a crown and soar to the sky, granting them part of Quetzalcoatl's majesty. In order to use that Endowment, the warrior must form a blood bond with a companion eagle, which must be renewed once every 260 days. While renewing the bond with any eagle would do, most prefer to keep a long connection with a single companion because of the spiritual and emotional connection which is created during the ceremony. That Endowment is a single, 1-5 merit, which is based around the companion eagle as a medium for its powers. As long as the duration is active the Endowment could be used by expanding a Willpower point, although certain abilities may require the warrior to have a living companion to use. The abilities gained through that Endowment are:

                                        0- Emphatic Link: the warrior may telepathically commune with his companion and instinctively know its location.
                                        00- Sky Sight: the warrior may extend his senses beyond his body and into his companion, allowing him to see through its eyes and hear through its ears.
                                        000- Feathered Form: the warrior can take the form of an eagle by himself, as if he had the Skinthief merit.
                                        0000- Wind's Shield: when taking an eagle form, the warrior enjoys the benefit of Resilient Form Merit with a rating equal to the Endowment.
                                        00000- Blessing of the Quetzalcoatl: the warrior may channel a shard of his god's true form, giving him the Hybrid Form merit for free.

                                        In an addition, having even 1 dot in the Endowment counts as having the Skinthief merit in order to buy dots in Essence Pool and Spirit Powers, with his animal form being counted as the "flayed spirit"

                                        Ocelomeh- Cehualli (0-00000)
                                        The Jaguar Warriors stalk the shadows of the empire and run through the forest's darkness as living shades. In order to use that Endowment, the warrior has to have access to a jade jaguar talisman and self inflict one level of Lethal Damage before use. Once activated, the warrior and all of his gear are transformed into an half solid, black shadow in the form of a jaguar. During that form, the warrior may target beings both in the Twilight and the material world, although he may only be harmed from supernatural sources. Spirit or ghost specific abilities may not effect his form, although shadow twisting abilities may cause him harm. During that form, he may use his dots in [Status: Jaguar Warriors] as an Honorary Rank against either ghosts or spirits (decided during the transformation). The warrior may stay in that form for 10 minutes per dot in the Endowment, although the duration may be extended by inflicting another level of Lethal Damage. In that state, the warrior use the Ephemeral Being rules for Attributes, and he may also buy and use Dominions by expanding Willpower for activation.

                                        Nahualtin- Tezcatilpoca (0-00000)
                                        The world is found in constant movement, always changing and transforming. Even the gods take part of that cycle, and by looking in a smoking mirror one could become a god. That Endowment is a single, 1-5 merit which serves as an "energy state"- for each dot in the Endowment, the cultist gains a single dot of Source which may be expanded in order to activate Mysteries as if he was a witch. In an addition, for every dot in the Endowment the cultist gets one free dot in a Mystery. In order to replenish his Source, the hunter must cause 2 Lethal Damage per point either to himself or to a sacrifice. He may only regain Source that way, and can only activate Mysteries by using Source. He does not suffer from Paradox at 5 dots, yet any witch can recognize that the spells cast him are "tainted", and the same could be seen from his Aura. Also, that taint has certain extra side effects- each time the cultist rolls for Integrity Breaking Point, he suffer a -[Endowment] modifier for the roll.

                                        Huitzilopotchli the Many Wheeled- Tonalpohualli (0-00000)
                                        All goes in cycles, and that which happened would happen again. What Rise must fall, what's fallen may rise again. In the movements of the wheels inside wheels, the cultists of the Sun may see what is bound to happen, and by invoking the days of the sacred calendar their gods would answer. That Endowment is a single, 1-5 merit which has a number of uses- first, it is used for divination. By dealing a level of Lethal Damage to a sentient being, the cultist may enter into a trance and see the future as if she had the Omen Sensitivity merit with using the Endowment instead of Wits. The act requires access to the calendar in order to use. Performing a human sacrifice before the activation of that power grants +2 modifier.

                                        Second, that merit gives the cultist the ability to twist space and time. That ability is a passive effect, which helps the cultist during times of battle, shifting time in split seconds in order to escape a deadly hit or create invisible boundaries which may not be crossed. In order to enjoy that effect, the cultist must either inflict Lethal Damage to sentient being, he may add his dots in the Endowment to either Attack rolls or Defense for the next Scene (chosen during activation). That effect may not be changed until the end of the Scene, and only one such effect may be used at the same time.

                                        Finally, the understanding of the wheels of the world grants the cultist the ability to use some of his god's greatest gifts. First, the cultist gain a single Numina which could be powered with Willpower or Lethal Damage to sentient beings for each dot in the Endowment. Second, at 1, 3 and 5 the cultist gains and single Incept for free as if he was an angel. The hunter may buy additional Numina as if they were merits, but may not gain further Incepts. When a certain incept calls for an angel's Rank, use the Mystery Cult Initiation's rating instead.

                                            and for the upcoming Compacts in Beast's Mortal Remains, I present-

                                            Yuri's Group- Lifebond (0-00000+)
                                            People are afraid when they feel alone- when there is no one to support and help them, they are an easy prey to the nightmares lurking in the shadows. Together, however, they are able to preform great feats, showing those nightmares that they are nothing more than illusions made by their own minds. Lifebond is a single, 1-5 merit which allows the hunter to form deep, subconscious bonds with other individuals. The hunter may maintain a number of those bonds equal to the Endowment's rating, and establishing such connection requires a physical connection between the hunter and the target. Such Lifebonds could be shifted at any moment. Alternatively, the hunter could make such connection into permanent Lifebond, which requires buying it as a separate 1 dot merit.

                                            Once the Lifebond is established, the hunter gains the ability to strength his bound using his soul and willpower- every time the target needs to spend a Willpower point, the hunter may decide to spend one in her place. Plus, every time that target requires rolling Willpower or a Breaking Point, the hunter may allow her to use his traits instead for the roll. The hunter, on the other hand, can use the number of active Lifebonds as a Supernatural Tolerance Trait (not including permanent ones), and it gives him an additional amount of Willpower points equal to that number. Many of the hunters of the Group use the Endowment to support each other and help new recruits to deal with their traumas, but more than enough has chose a beast or a similar monster as a Lifebond in order to help it maintain its hunger and rehabilitate it from its old habits.

                                            The Reckoning- Reverentia (0-00000, special)
                                            All looks at the Lord's majesty with awe, and those who fear God's wrath are the ones to understand His divine plan. Those who forsake that fear are doomed to rebel against the Lord's will like Nimrod did- and like him, they will be doomed by that hubris. Each Reverentia is a single, 1-5 merit which draws power from the primordial fear man feels when meeting one of God's divine creations, and turns it into a weapon against those who stand before the Lord in twisted sense of pride. There are five types of Reverentia, each is based around invoking one of God's Archangels which channel that divine awe against their enemies, each reminds how useless you are without God on your side- Gabriel (who without him all is hopeless), Uriel (who without him all is dark), Selaphiel (who without him all is hidden), Raphael (who without him all is broken) and Michael (who without him none are safe).

                                            In order to activate a Reverentia, the hunter has to spend a Willpower point and roll [Reverentia]+[Presence] minus the target's [Resolve]+[Composure]. In case the conditions fit the Endowments themes, that hunter gains a +2 modifier (that is, activating Uriel in the dark, or Raphael near an old building). That modifier goes to +3 if the conditions resonant with the Archangel's sphere (Gabriel on an high place, or Selaphiel near the sea). In a case of Success, the target suffer from the Spooked Condition as she experience the Archangel's true form (Uriel whispers from the shadows, Michael watch from the heavens, etc), and suffer one level of Bashing Damage per [Reverentia] as the boundaries between the Divine and the physical become blurred. Exceptional Success turns the Condition into Shaken. Dramatic Failure would turn God's wrath upon you, making you to suffer from the Spooked Condition and a number of Bashing Damage levels equal to [Endowment].                                                 so in the Strange Alchemies sidebar in the PtC 2e book, there was a certain discussion about how alchemists and hunters interact with each other, giving some organizations which are likely to work with alchemists- however, let's explore the possibility of alchemist organizations which work with hunters and the strange gifts they may gain from it. While alchemist is a temple on its own right, we do have conspiracies which do alchemy (the AO as canon example)- so there is some precedent for it. Anyway, I've decided to use only those groups which have some "higher reason" to hunt prometheans other than refining them into pyros- may it be the word of god, dedication to old tradition or even simple profit, those groups may have interest at recruiting regular people to their structure in order to carry their mission, creating a true hunter group around them. I may work the others in similar way because there are not a lot of them if I'll be bored enough or if someone would ask for it.

                                                Lodowick Society- Divine Vision (0-00000)
                                                Prometheans are beings of stolen life, souls taken from God by force of madness. Such abominations, and other beings and people who try to steal God's fire, has to be destroyed- and the Divine sends its vision to guide his loyal followers in battle. The character may preform an Elpis Vision as if she was a promethean. Such Elpis Vision are always preformed as if they were forced by the character, and require meditation using christian symbolism (such as the Cross, images of Saints, meditating about heaven, etc). Considering that the character lacks Azoth, use the Endowment's dot rating instead. The hunter may only ask questions about the Vigil or phenomena which are related to the Divine Fire. As hunters lack connection to the Azothic Memory, both the Success and the Exceptional Success are identical to the regular Elpis Vision roll, while Failure acts as the regular Elpis Vision's Dramatic Failure. Dramatic Failure stays the same- the hunters of the society may be blessed by God, by you can't play with fire without getting burned.

                                                The Pristine Order of Auric Chalice- Aludel (0-00000)
                                                Only the worthy should use alchemy, and only they may work the miracles of the Divine Fire in secret- but the common people still has their use, and may gain some of the Fire's finished products as long as they serve the alchemists in hunting those unworthy to the that power. Each Aludel is a small object, usually in the form of an orb, containing sparks of the Divine Fire inside of it, and has a 1-5 rating. The hunter may only carry a number of Aludels equal to her Status [Conspiracy] rating- that means that a hunter with 5 dot Status may either carry a single, powerful Aludel or 5 weak ones. The hunter may change her armory by visiting one of the Order's bases.

                                                Each Aludel is based around of the aspects of Pyros- Elpis and Flux. Elpidian Aludels give access to any Dsitilation (other than those of the Flux Transmutation), using the Aludel's dot rating instead of Azoth, together with spending a Willpower point. The hunter must touch the Aludel in order to activate it. While using the Transmutation effect is the most common one, there is another effect, used only in emergency- releasing the Divine Fire to the world. Doing so requires destroying the Aludel, which is then treated as a Firestorm Catalyst, with a number of success equal to double the Aludel's rating. The Firestorm's Aspects should be decided in advance. The effect is activated automatically once the Aludel is broken. While the other may replace the Aludel freely, destroying too many Aludels may cause fall in Status.

                                                The other type of Aludels are Lilithian Aludels- each of those Aludels is based around a Flux Dsitilation, and activate as Elpidian Aludel. However, when destroyed, they create the effect of a Jovian Athanor for a number in a radius of [Aludel] meters, which is decided in advanced. The effect lasts for a number of minutes equal to the Aludel's rating double 2, and is focused around the place where the Aludel was destroyed. The user is not immune to any of the effects caused by destroying the Aludel.

                                                Amalgama's specialty is biotechnology, and it equips its specialized mercenaries with dangerous powers to help it forcing other alchemists into its web and destroy targets asked by costumers. Transhuman Potential is a single, 1-5 merit, which require the character to got through a harsh procedure to adapt her body to the mutating effects of Pyros- or, more exactly, Flux. By spending a Willpower point and rolling Stamina+Composure, the character may gain temporary access to Pandoran Dread Powers with a total rating equal to her dots in the Endowment. Dramatic Failure would cause her a number of Bashing Damage levels equal to her dots in the Endowment as the Flux mutate her body without control. In case a Dread Power requires Pyros, use Willpower instead. The powers remain in effect for a Scene, although the character may prolong it with expanding another Willpower point without having to roll once more. The character's body is mutated because of the effect appropriately to the powers manifested. The hunter may end the effects of the Endowment at any time.

                                                Phoenix Fire Guard- FP (0-00000, Special)

FP- or Fire Protection- is actually three sets of merits, each having a different 1-5 rating. PFP, or Passive Fire Protection, stops the passive effects of the Fire upon the community. In order to activate the merit, the hunter must spend time at "fire proofing" a place with an area of 10*[PFP] m^2, an act which requires hour hour per 10 m^2. Working with other hunters who act as supporters remove 30 minutes per hunter, yet no less than half an hour. Once protected, all individuals in the area may add the PFP's rating to any roll to resist a Disquiet effects, and Wasteland Festers as if it was one level lower and Fades as if it was one level higher.

In case the AFP wins, the Transmutation ends automatically.

                                                            White Heart Pharmaceuticals- Paranormal Infections (0-00000)
                                                            Through ages of research and work with supernatural beings, the company has developed a number of powerful pathogens for various uses- and through special vaccinations, only those members of the conspiracy are immune to its side-effects, turning them into ideal carriers of the occult diseases which simply wait to be unleashed. Paranormal Infections is a single, 1-5 Endowment, with each dot representing a single pathogen the character is carrying inside of her. Each such infection is designed as a 5 stages effect, with each stage granting a single Condition or Tilt to the infected and stages 2-5 each has a special "trigger" which cause the infection to go up one step. In order to infect a target, the hunter ha to touch the target and spend a WP point. Alternatively, exchanging blood with the target would always infect the other individual (the hunter may instead take a air-spread version of an infection, but it is considered as 2 dots of the Endowment). To resist the infection, the target has to roll for Resolve+Stamina against the character's Manipulation+Endowment. Supernatural beings which are normally immune to diseases still needs to roll, but may add their Supernatural Tolerance trait. Once infected, the target would suffer from the Condition/Tilt of the first stage and the disease would progress normally. Each time it does, the infected may resist the pathogen, but suffers a -[stage] modifier to the roll. Treating the infection requires either a supernatural treatment or gaining the serum from the conspiracy. The hunter herself is immune to any pathogen she carries, and to any none supernatural disease.
