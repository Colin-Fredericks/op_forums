                        Something that bears considering is a given Conspiracy's operational profile in a specific locality. Even TFV isn't going to kick in the door with the goal of eradicating every bloodsucker, shapeshifter, and sorceror within city limits. They're going to go after particular targets that either have something the Conspiracy wants or who's removal would work towards solving a bigger problem. They're also not above using proxies of their own. The books suggest that Conspiracies will recruit and supply lower tier hunter cells to do their dirty work while distancing themselves from exposure.

                        Then there's the matter of convincing your local Primogen/Alphas/Cabal of what's really happening. Even if you know the man behind all the seemingly unrelated disappearances over the last decade is a mysterious pharma rep with bees coming out of his arm and a fleet of black vans, you're going to have a difficult time rallying enough credulous support to go after him. Presuming you've done so the best you can hope for is to make your city hard for them to operate in, maybe even drive them out. The more you learn about them though the more questions there are and the crazier the story starts to sound. You'd need the resources and resolve of an entire Convenant to go after a Conspiracy in all out war. Another difficult sell.

                        Even then, you might lay havoc on the lower and mid level cells of the Conspiracy, but their leaders are going to make a habit of slipping through your fingers and the core ethos of the Conspiracy is just going to morph into a new form. Task Force Valkyrie is officially disavowed and dissolved by the U.S. government, but somewhere in a black budget line item there's a project called MKTWILIGHT that quietly sets up shop. Cheiron Group is dismantled as a corporate entity and its assets auctioned off, then a brilliant but troubled med student is inspired to perform dangerous experiments by a medieval tome bearing Cheiron's likeness.
