        I think you're making a simple mistake about the fundamental core of the Abbey.

        The Abbey are not a Compact of the upper class. They're a hellfire club, that's the place where upper class ladies and gentlemen go to escape the stifling rules that surround the rest of their lives.

        Originally posted by Hunter Core
        He held debauched court over one of the several so-called “Hellfi re Clubs”: secret societies of like-minded, well-connected men and women who sought to smash the taboos of the day. By modern standards, the recreational drugs, casual sex and public bondage-play were pretty tame. Rev. Ogilvy, a thinker ahead of his time, seemed to understand that. He urged his followers to break the rules any way they could.
        Obviously they'll have some rules, but I imagine all of them will just be codifications of one of two principals "Don't ruin my fun" and "Don't endanger other members".

        So take your example of turning up in jeans and a t-shirt. That could be construed as 'runing my fun' - "The theme of this evening's Kitsune hunt, is classic English fox hunting". But at the same time, I could just as easily see the Abbey saying "I have invited a mad scientist with virtual reality helmets and the most elaborate pornography. The dress code shall be jeans and those delightfully droll t-shirts that's all the range with programmers these days."
