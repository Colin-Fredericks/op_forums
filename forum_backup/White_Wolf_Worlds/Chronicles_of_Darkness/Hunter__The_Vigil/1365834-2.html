                                                    Originally posted by Primordial newcomer View Post
                                                    LostLight, I have a question concerning the Lucifuge if that's fine. do you think it's safe to say the Hunters are, in a manner of speaking, spiritually enslaved? I mean, I know many conspiracies keep their members under tight watch, but they normally get benefits from it. the Lucifuge hunters dont, it seems, and instead get almost mystically compelled to follow commands when they do get them.

                                                    I'm not saying that makes the Lucifuge herself super evil, but spiritually enslaving her hunters seems pretty bad (I assume she is also aware of what they do, being why she knows about the ones that try to escape)
                                                    I mean, you could indeed call it "enslavement"- but aren't we all slaves to our world and nature? :P

                                                    A bit more seriously, some Lucifuge agents are indeed brainwashed to go against their dark nature. Notice that the Lucifuge are aware that they do so- they don't try to sugarcoat it. A free Lucifuge member is dangerous, and while Hell never enslaves you, it does cheers for you as you fall into corruption. And not all members of the Lucifuge are brainwashed- many choose to fight evil and go against their nature.

                                                    Now, the Will of the Lucifuge is indeed a small part of the Lady's will which creeps into your mind and soul, and compels you to follow her orders- but notice that it is for those members which are in good standing with the organization. The Lucifuge is, indeed, the organization, and she lives in her children. You can leave (well, you can try at least), and then the Condition is resolved- but what would be your other option? Falling prey to demons who treat you like royalty until you fuck up the world?

                                                    You can serve in heaven or rule in hell. You either submit yourself to save the world or do as you wish. The demons come and whisper to you, promising you freedom, and how great and amazing you could be. It is easy to fall into temptation, easy to run wild in Hell and be loved and adored as you fuck up everything you once cared about. Hell doesn't takes your free will- and that's the beauty of it. You choose your own damnation, and when you fall kicking and screaming to meet your parents, you will know that you have done exactly what they wanted you to do, and they are so proud of you and you have no one to blame but yourself.

                                                    So yeah, you enslave yourself to the Lucifuge, both organization and a person. But it is a choice- well, usually. The other choice would have been to get killed, perhaps, or maybe they brainwashed you into making that choice. The other option would be letting you to roam free and bring hell upon earth. We are all slaves to our choices- and there is no freedom in hell, just understanding that you became a slave to your sins, and chains worn from free will while the Devil laughs in your face.

                                                    At least, that's what the Lucifuge tells themselves to sleep at night.
