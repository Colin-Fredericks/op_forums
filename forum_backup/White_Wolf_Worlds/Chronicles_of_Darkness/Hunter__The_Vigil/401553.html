    Vigil for Rome

    02-11-2015, 04:07 AM
    Another thread got me thinking about this again. I wasn't a fan of the canon version of the Aves Minerva in Paterfamilias. Because while "Ancient Roman Hunters" is a cool hook for uncovering ancient mysteries in a modern setting - in an 4th Century Roman setting, they need to be more distinct than than, especially if other Compacts and Conspiracies are in play.

    So, in theory, my idea for Aves Minerva is to make them "the Union" of the era. They wield no mystical Endowments, but are essentially a self-defense network of common people out of the Aventine (which is also where organized crime is at its strongest in the city). Their impact on Hunter lore comes not from great secrets or powers they protected, but from heroic deeds alone.

    That being the case I have a list of groups, I can theoretically break into 5 Compacts and 5 Conspiracies.

    5 Compacts:
    - Aves Minerva (see above)
    - Fraternitas Amissa (a for profit network of couriers and guards for travel through the dark wilderness between cities)
    - Shoftim (protectors of the dispersing Children of Judea, they may once have been a Conspiracy, but their Holy Psalms and centralized leadership were lost with the Second Temple)
    -???
    -???

    5 Conspiracies
    - Aegis Kai Doru (Orphic Mystery Cult reforming via Christian members in the shadow of Constantinople)
    - Collegia Chironos (trade network of educated Greek slaves, with secret medical knowledge far beyond the time, drawn from their worshiped brands)
    - Cult of the Phoenix (spread through what is now Parthia in the days of Achaemanid rule of Egypt; they marry Ancient Egyptian Alchemy to Zoroastrian Sacred Haoma)
    - Lux Mithrae (an order within the Roman Legions who fight the darkness for the Empire and for Mithras. They use Sacramental Bull's blood in their Endowments [steal the Red Rites from Aves Minerva])
    - ???

    So as you can see, there are 3 groups I'm uncertain on. Any of these can be Compacts or Conspiracies depending on how I model them and their Endowments.

    - Bacchantes (matriarchal mad revelers that Livy wrote on - of course in reality Livy's telling is very suspect, but in the World of Darkness it's fair game. As a Compact they'd essentially be the era's Ashwood Abbey; but as a Conspiracy, they'd be a Mystery Cult with some real mojo via their mad ecstasies)
    - Noctis Vigiles (Christian Hunters... I'm a bit unsure on their SPECIFIC ideology - in an earlier century I'd focus on eschatological fervor, but we're post Nicea at this point, so there's much more organized clerical and doctrinal authority for them to draw on and answer to; they're also not the underclass like Lancea et Sanctum in Requiem for Rome, because other than the "Apostate" calling himself Caesar, mortal Christians are in their political ascendancy, and their faith commands the Eastern half of the Empire unchallenged from Constantinople)
    - [Wunjo] (link goes to a literal image of the rune "wunjo" that is their name - a Germanic pagan group that seeks out monsters to fight for glory and honor. I'm leaning toward making them Tier 2, but given them runes with some mystic oomf would also make sense)

    Thoughts on how to divy up those last 3? Ideas to further flesh them out (Noctis Vigiles is obviously the most in need - the era needs Christian Hunters, but I haven't decided on more distinct themes for them)?

      Wunjo I'd definately make Tier 2; I would go so far as to say the wunjo don't have the runes, they just know people outside the compact who are willing to provide them.

      Noctis Vigiles. Hmmm, I'm not to sure about the history so this might not fit the time period. But maybe an Endowment that's primarally focused on magic resistance and undo magical effects, with a sideline in the ability to assimilate other people's magic and make it their own.

      The idea being that as Christianity eclipsed paganism pagan magics stop working, and as Christianity syncretised elements of other faith they're going to syncretise elements of other magic into their own. A loss for human diversity when they do it to the Wunjo, but a good thing when they do it to vampires.

      I'm not sure if it should be ritualised or if it should be a passive power of faith thing. But either way it should stop working for reasons at some point before neo-paganism, Buddhism, athiesm, etc start making inroads into previously Christian nations. And possibly before the MM was founded, if only to explain why it hasn't shown up in the cannon books.

      Bacchantes. I'd make them a Conspiracy. If only because we already have an Abbey without magic and this will seperate the two. Plus there's been a lot of work to diversify the Abby from just drug fueled orgies. Livy's portrayal would undo that diversification so magic might help round them out a bit and give them reasons and motives to be terrible people.

      I know that makes two Conspiracies. But I know that the Noctis Vigiles would fit nicely into a single 1-5 Endowment merit so you could cheat a bit.

                                                      Originally posted by herkles View Post

                                                      Not really. VASCU is very rooted in modern genre tropes that rose out of the Victorian/Edwardian period. Furthermore, Roman murder case prosecution were handled by private suit (i.e. the family of the victim, not the state, brought charges before the court, called witnesses, etc). So there were no governmental investigative bodies.
