                                            like I've said before, I have never heard about a stereotype connected to a "magical black jazz musicians", and I don't see why afro americans can't be the first people to discover a special technique connected to music.                                                         few new ideas I've thought about from the list-

                                                        Mori Kage- a clan of assassins originated from ancient Japan who use the Echo in order to create replicas of themselves after making an ages old pact with a court of great yokai. Their part of the deal is to hunt down escaped "pets" of the yokai and the false selves they leave behind.
                                                        Kopa Loei- priests and shamans from Polynesia who possess Mana and use their authority and influence over the world in order to maintain the balance between the living and the dead while seeking after the wisdom of their ancestors.
                                                        Valdaermen- originating from the wild warriors of ancient Scandinavia, they are a mostly male group who use the gift of their witches and shamans to inscribe magical runes to their weapons in order to continue the ancient tradition of fighting against the monsters as a test for themselves.
                                                        Church of the Sun- carriers of a noble and divine lineage of once pagan group, they now style themselves after angels and saints while using their gift in order to banish evil and protect their land.
                                                        Shepherds of Annwn- continuing the myth of the Cwn Annwn, that small compact who seek to banish the dark hounds after a years old tragedy and use a combination of blood magic and druidic rites.
                                                        Crystal Watchers- a group who serve a primordial force of nature which seeks to bring stasis and perfection to the world through freezing everything in place, and gain the gift of immortality in exchange for snuffing the Divine Fire
                                                        Euthothanos- executors who style themselves as angels of death and seek to cut free the miserable from the world while still having a human form- while making sure that the dead would stay dead
                                                        Celestial Chorus- people who use holy relics in order to transform their body into divine beings, which use their new powers in order to protect sacred artifacts through the world from being abused by monsters
                                                        Custodes Ignis- originating from ancient Rome, they use a special ceremony in order to connect themselves to the Furnace, an old power who makes them more than human by scorching divine tattoos to their flesh
                                                        Order of the Nameless- an occult order of gentlemen who have put themselves a goal to take over the Void and reshape the world in their own image.
                                                        Toc Faan- a cannibal cult which hunts down people with "special spark" and devour them in the name of their demon gods, and use those sparks to fuel their terrible gifts
                                                        Aided- necromancers from the British Islands who bind dead souls to their will by using a forbidden tradition of the druids who mutilate both the spirit and the body of their victims.
                                                        Apollonian Priesthood- a cult of sun worshipers who believe in the perfection of masculinity and guide people through their visions toward individual refinement.
                                                        ZMEI- if Valkyrie is Shield and the Architects are Hydra, here comes Leviathan! those psychics use the distilled essence of a captured test subject in order to enhance their minds. There is also some Leviathan: the Tempest connection in here.
