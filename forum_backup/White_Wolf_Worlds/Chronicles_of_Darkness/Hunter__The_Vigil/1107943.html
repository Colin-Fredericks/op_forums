                                                      Hmm. Alchemists seeking immortality is a pretty standard Promethean antagonist. Germans with ties to what happened in World War II is already parallel with the Loyalists of Thule. I'd look elsewhere.

                                                      Have you seen Netflix's Daredevil (season 2) and Iron Fist (season 1)? Maybe you could tie them into a group like the Hand, except one that has already found their Black Sky long ago (the immortal). Of course, that still makes them more Antagonistic than the Vigil players usually are. Maybe make them closer to the Lucifuge in concept, and make them rebel against a darker purpose (either the PC Conspiracy is rebelling against the core group, or the group itself - immortal included - is defying their purpose). As for a source, maybe a Cthonic God, deep in the Underworld? The group's immortality is part of a pact - death itself rejects them so that they can further their "master's" objective(s). If you wanted to expand upon their abilities (beyond resurrection), you could grant them various deathly, psychopomply, or related abilities. As a risk/counter-balance, actually using them might risk their immortality, as the Underworld God's minions might locate them easier (making the risk closer to a Demon's Compromise).
                                                      EDIT: Which, admittedly, sounds very close to a Sin-Eater, but does differ on the fine details.

                                                      Just some ideas. Try researching other historical or mythological attempts at immortality (like Koschei's method) for inspiration. Make your group tangentially related, give them a reason to fight monsters, and see where that takes you.
