    Presented here are a collection of Slashers that I have developed for my home games. I wanted to share them for anyone that might need a Slasher for their games. I will try to post a few every couple of days. Let me know what you think. And if you have any Slashers of your own, feel free to contribute.

    The Anarchist
    Simone Delacour, from even an early age, was as rebellious as she was intelligent. Her love of philosophy, mathematics, and especially computer sciences earned her admission to special schools for the gifted and talented, but her difficulties with those in positions of authority always made her an outsider. During her college years, she began associating with anarchist and discordian organizations, becoming increasingly radical over time. She adopted the pseudonym “Eris”, and dedicated herself full-time to tearing down a society that she perceives as hopelessly flawed and corrupt. She is brilliant programmer, hacker, and blackmailer, and she uses the information she gathers to manipulate individuals, inflame conflicts, and erode institutions. She is a hardened cynic, with every dirty secret she uncovers only confirming her misanthropy. Eris takes a particular delight in orchestrating the death and ruin of key individuals, then watching the often far-reaching fallout.
    Eris is an African-American woman in her late thirties, with a dark complexion and close cropped hair. She dresses for comfort and has a “punk” sense of style. She is reclusive and lives comfortably on money earned through blackmail and laundered through various business ventures.


    Undertaking: Puppeteer; Virtue: Idealistic; Vice: Anarchist
    Aspiration: Disrupt the Status Quo
    Tell: Eris strikes at targets she perceives as upholding a flawed society
    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 4, Resolve 3
    Physical Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 2
    Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
    Skills: Academics 2 (Psychology), Computers 5 (Doxing, Hacking), Crafts 2 (Electronics), Firearms 2, Investigation 4 (Blackmail), Persuasion 2, Politics 2, Socialize 2 (Extremists), Streetwise 2, Subterfuge 2
    Merits: Alternate Identity 2 (Eris), Area of Expertise (Hacking), Library 3 (Investigation), Mystery Cult 4 (Nihil), Professional Training 3 (Hacker; Computer, Crafts, Investigation), Resources 4
    Integrity: 0, Potency: 3, Willpower: 9
    Size: 5, Initiative: 6, Defense: 3
    Speed: 10
    Health: 7
    Weapons/Attacks
    Unarmed [+0B melee; 3 dice]
    Heavy Pistol [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 5 dice]
    Talent: Compelling Frailty: Falter
    Dread Powers: Damnation (Radicalize), Eye Spy (Hack Surveillance), Interface

    Compelling: A Puppeteer can profile a victim much like a Virtuoso, but additionally benefits from the 8-again quality on any contested roll against a profiled target. They also impose a penalty equal to the scourge’s Intelligence on victims’ rolls to notice traps or react to ambushes set for them in their nest.

    Falter: Whenever a Puppeteer fails a Social roll, it becomes a dramatic failure.


    Mystery Cult: Nihil
    No one is certain who founded the hacker collective known as Nihil, they have been responsible for several campaigns of cyber-terrorism, harassment, and information warfare that only increase with frequency and severity as the years go by. They do not seem to have an agenda beyond sowing anarchy and chaos, and on the few occasions the group has made a statement, the speak of “making the world pure once more”.
    Nihil recruits by finding individuals with a demonstrated talent for hacking or other high-end digital skills, and who display an anarchic or overall contemptuous persona. From there they begin to indoctrinate the individual, often having them perform criminal acts that incriminate them deeper into the organization. While authorities are well aware of Nihil (and worse yet, copy cats and pranksters that claim to members), they have little concrete evidence and feel it is only a matter of time before something truly horrific happens.


    Purpose: To use advanced computing and information technology to breakdown infrastructure and erode society.
    Relic: Nihil.Exe – a difficult to detect and remove virus that gives access to photos, cameras utilities, and social media credentials on the infected device.
    Doctrine: The Only Purity is Oblivion

    Initiation Benefits
    ● - Prospective Members are savvy computer users, who are coached in using their talents more effectively, and gain a Computer Specialty in Programming.
    ●● - Members of Nihil known how to hide their true identities and erase evidence, gaining a dot of the Anonymity Merit.
    ●●● - Members of Nihil support each other on any of their schemes and projects, gaining the Hobbyist Clique (Computers) Merit.
    ●●●● - Veteran Members of Nihil are ghosts, leaving nothing but dead ends and false leads; gaining an additional two dots of the Anonymity Merit and the Untouchable Merit.
    ●●●●● - the most hard-line Members of Nihil develop a web of influence and talents that seem almost other worldly; they gain three dots of Merits, which they may distribute as they see fit between Allies, Contacts, and the two dot version of the Technopathy Merit (Hurt Locker)

      The Anti-Christ
      Jeremiah Black believes he is the anti-Christ, and that it is his destiny to usher in the apocalypse. Born Jeffry Moorhead, he was raised by a struggling family that indulged in petty crime (drug dealing and theft) to makes ends meet. Jeffry became interested in occultism at a young age, captivated by the dark images and grim themes he discovered, eventually becoming a practicing Satanist and changing his name to “Jeremiah Black”. From there his obsessions grew, with his beliefs becoming extreme enough to alienate his peers. By the time he progressed to murder, he had gathered a coterie of followers swayed by his considerable charisma. He now has begun the next phase of his plans, a series of ritual murders that when completed will call his Father into the world to begin the End of Days.
      Jeremiah is a handsome Caucasian man in his late twenties, with wavy dark hair and piercing blue eyes. He dresses the part of a seductive occultist, but eschews tattoos and piercings (fewer identifying marks and a persistent fear of needles).


      Undertaking: Hypno; Virtue: Certainty; Vice: Arrogance
      Aspiration: To usher in 1000 years of darkness
      Tell: Victims must be ritually marked
      Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 2, Resolve 3
      Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 2, Stamina 3
      Social Attributes: Presence 5, Manipulation 4, Composure 3
      Skills: Expression 1, Empathy 3, Firearms 2, Larceny 2 (Intrusion), Intimidate 2, Medicine 1, Occult 3 (Evil), Persuasion 4 (Cultists), Socialize 2, Subterfuge 2 (Lies), Streetwise 2 (Drugs), Weaponry 2 (Knife, Improvised)
      Merits: Damnable Certainty, Defensive Combat (Weaponry), Professional Training 3 (Criminal; Larceny, Streetwise, Weaponry), Pusher, Staff 2 (Cultists), Striking Looks 1, Untouchable
      Integrity: 0, Potency: 3, Willpower: 9
      Size: 5, Initiative: 5, Defense: 4
      Speed: 10
      Health: 8
      Weapons/Attacks
      Unarmed [+0B melee, 3 dice]
      Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 5 dice; 9-again]
      Light Pistol [+1L ranged; 4 dice]
      Talent: Compelling Words; Frailty: Can’t Let Go
      Dread Powers: Damnation (Corrupt), Glitch 1, Know Soul

      Compelling Words: When making a Social roll of any kind to get a target to drop their guard for a moment or to heed the slasher’s words, the Hypno treats any success as an exceptional success. If using this moment to get close to the target and attack them, the scourge treats the victim as vulnerable to a Killing Blow, dealing damage equal to their dice pool for the attack plus any bonus for a weapon, with no roll required. If using this to demoralize or sow doubt in a target’s mind, the scourge can instead drain a point of Willpower from the victim to regain it; they cannot drain Willpower from a target more than once per scene.

      Can’t Let Go: Whenever a Hypno experiences a failure on a Social roll against a target she intends to kill, she becomes absolutely obsessed with the desired victim. She loses a point of Willpower at the end of every scene wherein she does not directly attempt to torment or attack the target. This effect ends when the target dies, or if the target manages to inflict damage on the Hypno.

          The Arsonist
          Even as a boy, John Everett was a strange individual. Despite a fairly typical suburban upbringing by moderately religious parents, John was noted for sardonic sense of humor, fondness for pranks, and problems with authority. When John was sixteen, his family perished in a house fire (which was due to faulty wiring) and he himself suffered severe, disfiguring burns. After recovering from his injuries, John became fascinated with fire. He claims that the flame speaks to him, and compels him to burn away the world to make room for a new beginning. He has little regard for the life and well being of others, and will burn people as readily as he burns objects and structures. After getting kicked out of the foster care system for acts of arson and vandalism, John has become a drifter, supporting himself with odd jobs and petty theft.
          John is a plain-faced young man, with freckled skin, bad teeth and bright red hair he keeps at shoulder length. He has ugly burn scars on this hands, arms and left side of his face, and dresses in dark clothing.


          Undertaking: Undesirable; Virtue: Mirthful; Vice: Rebellious
          Aspiration: Burn down the old to make way for the new
          Tell: John takes up residence in abandoned places, burning them down when he moves on
          Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 5, Resolve 4
          Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 3, Stamina 2
          Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 3
          Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Crafts 3, Expression 1, Firearms 2 (Incendiary), Larceny 3, Persuasion 2, Science 2 (Fire), Stealth 2, Streetwise 2(Homeless), Survival 2, Subterfuge 2
          Merits: Anonymity 2, Area of Expertise (Incendiary), Professional Training 2 (Criminal; Larceny, Streetwise), Natural Tinkerer, Resources 1, Safe Place 2 (Abandoned Warehouse), Tight-Lipped
          Integrity: 0, Willpower: 7
          Size: 5, Initiative: 6, Defense: 5
          Speed: 11
          Health: 7

          Unarmed [+0B melee, 5 dice]
          Flame Thrower [max 3L ranged;-2 Initiative; 7 dice, Incendiary]
          Talent: Natural Compass; Frailty: Rejection
          Natural Compass: An Undesirable has a near-perfect sense of direction, and gains a +3 bonus to Perception, Athletics, Investigation, and Survival rolls when present in their nest.
          Rejection: The Undesirable suffers a −4 penalty on Social rolls that do not involve slashers and other monsters.


          Nest: Abandoned Warehouse
          The sign on the outside say “condemned”, and rightly so. The warehouse is severely dilapidated and despite being targeted for demolition years ago, a complicated web of bureaucratic red keeps it standing. The place is a firetrap; with stacks of wooden pallets, broken furniture, and old newspaper just waiting for a firebugs torch. Despite this, the warehouse often houses squatters, drug addicts, and other transients.



          Safe Place Features:Escape Hatch, Home Security
          Traps: Collapsing Building, Fire Bomb
          Collapsing Building: The warehouse is slowly falling apart and has become dangerously unstable. Once the trap is triggered, the ceiling caves in and deals two levels of lethal damage and imposes the Immobilized Tilt, as the victim is pinned beneath the debris. If immobilized, escaping requires a Strength+ Athletics roll to shift aside the rubble. Dramatic failures often inflict the Arm or Leg Wrack Tilt to reflect twisted ankles and the like.
          Trigger: Structure damage to the Abandoned Warehouse. A successful Dexterity + Athletics roll with a -2 penalty downgrades to bashing damage and an exceptional success negates the damage entirely.

          Fire Bomb: Improvised incendiary devices placed around the warehouse are primed to turn the place into an all-consuming blaze.Once triggered, the trap inflicts one lethal damage to anyone within 3 yards of the device and imposes the Inferno Tilt on the area. Any Dramatic Failures rolled inflict one aggravated damage.
          Trigger: Tripwire. A successful Dexterity + Larceny OR Wits + Composure roll with a -2 penalty avoids the trap.

              The Beast of the Backwoods
              His family had foraged and hunted the woodlands on the far side of the mountains for generations. Over time, the family moved on, either by death or moving far away and never looking back. Only the Beast remains. If the he had a name, he has likely forgotten it. The locals only see him when he stops by a small country store for supplies, thrusting a fistful of old bills at the cashier and disappearing into the night. In the woods he calls home, he does not abide the presence of trespassers. Government surveyors, deep country hikers, hunters, and other people exploring the outdoors have vanished in those woods. When locals are asked about it, they simply shrug and say “The Beast got’em”, and they warn people to avoid those woods if they know what is good for them.
              The Beast is a hulking figure, easily 7ft tall and well over 350 lbs of muscle. His features are coarse, with dim eyes, a broken nose and a wide mouth filled with oddly pristine teeth. His hair and beard are hacked away with a knife and his cloths are a patchwork of old leather, flannel, and denim. Most of the time he spends his days hunting, foraging and erecting beautifully carved totems to strange gods. When hunting trespassers, he dons a cloak of skins and a mask fashioned from an elk’s skull. While fully capable of using a rifle, he prefers to trap his prey, then close in for the kill with his trusty axe.


              Undertaking: Mask Virtue:Vigilant; Vice: Savage
              Aspiration: Protect These Woods
              Tell: The Beast prefers to trap his prey or drive them into natural hazards before hacking them to death with an axe.

              Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 2, Resolve 4
              Physical Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 3, Stamina 5
              Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 4

              Skills: Animal Ken 2, Athletics 4 (Brute Strength, Hiking), Brawl 3, Crafts 3 (Traps), Firearms 2, Intimidate 3, Stealth 3 (Stalking), Survival 4 (Tracking, Woodlands), Weaponry 4 (Axe)
              Merits: Atavism, Cover Tracks, Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Masked Scent, Professional Training 4 (Hunter; Crafts, Stealth, Survival), Robust Health, Safe Place 3 (Hunter’s Cabin)

              Integrity: 0, Potency: 2, Willpower: 10
              Size: 6, Initiative: 7, Defense: 7
              Speed: 15
              Health: 11

              Weapons/Attacks
              Unarmed [+0B melee, 9 dice]
              Axe [+3L melee, -4 Initiative; 11 dice; 9-again, Two Handed]

              Talent: Killing Machine Frailty: Only the Kill
              Dread Powers: Home Turf (Woodlands), Unholy Prowess 2 (Strength, Stamina)*

              Killing Machine: Like a Brute, a Mask does not suffer wound penalties and passes rolls to remain conscious due to damage or pain. Additionally, the Mask does not need to sleep or eat, and only suffers a single point of damage from any attack regardless of the damage or number of successes. This applies to mundane and supernatural forms of attack; only environmental damage such as fire, electrocution, or falling bricks applies damage normally.

              Only the Kill: A Mask has lost the ability to communicate. They are illiterate and are limited to simple gestures or grunts. To understand what’s been said to them, they must succeed at an Intelligence + Composure roll.

              Unholy Prowess: The creature is quicker, stronger and hardier than it looks. The creature adds one dot of Strength, Dexterity or Stamina per dot of Unholy Prowess.


              Nest: Hunter’s Cabin
              The cabin sits in in a densely wooded section of the forest. Many of the trees have been carved with scenes of strange gods and gruesome rites, and chimes made from human bones hang from the branches and clatter in the wind. The cabin itself is a small structure, with two rooms, a root cellar, and tool shed out back. The place is decorated in bones, hides, and old family portraits. The area surround the cabin have been trapped with all manner of hazard, designed to cripple and maim anyone unlucky enough to find their way to the Beast’s home.


              Safe Place Features: Arsenal, Concealed, Home Security
              Tilts: Foreboding
              Traps: Bear Trap, Deadfall Trap, Pit Trap

              Foreboding (Environmental): The eerie carvings and the tree’s decorated with bones fills the air with an almost palpable sense of dread. Any mortal individual who enter the location must make a Resolve + Composure roll or gain the Spooked Condition.

              Bear Trap: There are multiple rusty bear traps scattered around the property. The jaw trap deals two points of lethal damage and ignores two points of armor or Durability. In an action scene, it also imposes the Leg Wrack Tilt. A character trapped in the jaws can attempt to escape as an instant action. Doing so requires a Strength + Stamina roll. Failure causes another point of lethal damage as the jaws dig in further.
              Trigger: Pressure Plate. A successful Wits + Composure roll with a -2 penalty avoids the trap.

              Deadfall: A trap comprised of large rocks and lumber hand suspended from the dense canopy of trees. Once the trap is triggered, the trap falls and deals two levels of lethal damage and imposes the Immobilized Tilt, as the victim is pinned beneath the debris. If immobilized, escaping requires a Strength+ Athletics roll to shift aside the rubble. Dramatic failures often inflict the Arm or Leg Wrack Tilt to reflect twisted ankles and the like.
              Trigger: Tripwire. A successful Dexterity + Larceny OR Wits + Composure roll with a -2 penalty avoids the trap.

              Pit Trap: A deep pit lined with sharped stakes and concealed beneath a tarp and piles of leaves. trap comprised of large rocks and lumber hand suspended from the dense canopy of trees. Once the trap is triggered, the victims suffers a point of lethal damage from the fall and the spikes, or one point of aggravated damage on a dramatic failure. Escaping a pit trap requires a Strength + Athletics – 2 roll to climb or jump out.
              Trigger: False Floor. A successful Wits + Composure roll with a -2 penalty avoids the trap.

                  The Blacksmith
                  Walter Douglas never fit in. Heavy-set and socially awkward, Walter never made many friends and was often the target of ridicule by his peers. He took solace in work, and became a talent mechanic, machinist and blacksmith. Most of his evenings were spent watching television, lifting weights and recreational metalwork. After a dispute with his employer turned violent, Walter experienced a rush of power that he had never felt before and quickly become addicted to the thrill. Now he kills to relive that same rush. In time, his murderous impulses came to include torture and mutilation. He is careful and meticulous, making sure to cover his tracks and dispose of the bodies in ways where they won’t be found.
                  Walter is a Caucasian man in his early forties, with brown eyes and thinning brown hair. He is plain-faced and stocky, with a muscular build from years of weight lifting and manual labor. He dresses plainly in dark colors, and wears a sturdy coverall when “working”.

                      The Child

Despite the efforts of therapists, Eric remains uncommunicative and often seems barely aware of the presence of others. His silence is only punctuated by the occasional shockingly intense burst of violence. Eric was entrusted to a group home, since few families were willing or equipped to take in a child with special needs and such a traumatic background. Then, one night during a raging storm, Eric escaped the facility, leaving a trail of corpses in his wake. His current whereabouts are unknown.
                      Eric is a handsome twelve year old Caucasian boy who is slightly above average height for his age with blond hair and blue eyes. He dresses in stolen clothing and is willing to kill and steal to obtain food, shelter, or any other needs.                           The Copy Cat
                          Fawne Martin developed a fascination with serial killers and the criminal mind will in middle school, keeping a scrap book of famous cases that she studied obsessively, earning her a reputation as “that weird killer girl”. Her obsession continued as she become an adult, and in college she studied criminal psychology, and has become well regarded from her skill as a profiler. She has also quietly been killing her own victims, using her knowledge to “recreate” the particular style and tell of other serial killers. So far, all of her murders have been attributed to other killers, and she continues her “secret hobby” with none the wiser.

                                                          The Gravedigger
                                                          The man called “The Gravedigger” lives in an old house squatting on a hill overlooking a large property with a large collection of rusted and broken vehicles and several acres of crops. The house and property serve as a focal point of the local community, who have long since abandoned conventional religion to worship the God of the Good Earth. The Gravedigger is their favored servant. Made strong by the will of the God he provides the flesh and blood that must be interred in the soil to keep the crops growing, and the God of the Good Earth appeased.
                                                          The Gravedigger is nearly 7 ft. tall and weighs over 300lbs. His craggy face is scarred and his eyes are dark. He wears a denim coverall and a hooded coat.

                                                          Undertaking: Brute; Virtue: Faithful; Vice: Territorial
                                                          Aspiration: To serve the will of the God of the Good Earth.
                                                          Tell: The Gravedigger must bury his victims in fields that produce crops

                                                          Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 3
                                                          Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
                                                          Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 4

                                                          Skills: Animal Ken 2(Dogs), Athletics 2(Endurance), Brawl 3, Crafts 3, Firearms 2(Rifle), Intimidate 3, Investigation 1, Medicine 1, Occult 2, Stealth 2, Survival 2 (Hunting), Weaponry 3 (Improvised)
                                                          Merits: Damnable Certainty, Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Hands of a Killer, Hardy 2, Mystery Cult Initiation (The Harvesters) 4, Professional Training 3 (Laborer; Athletics, Crafts, Survival), Safe Place 2 (The Old House), Trained Observer

                                                          Integrity: 0, Willpower: 7
                                                          Size: 6, Initiative: 7, Armor: 1/0 [Reinforced Clothing], Defense: 6
                                                          Speed: 14
                                                          Health: 10

                                                          Weapons/Attacks
                                                          Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
                                                          Farming Tools [+1L melee, -2 Initiative; 9 dice, Improvised]
                                                          Shovel [+1L melee;-3 Initiative; 8 dice; Knockdown]
                                                          Rifle [+4L ranged; -5 Initiative; 6 dice]

                                                          Talent: Unstoppable Frailty: Tunnel Vision

                                                          Unstoppable: A Brute never suffers wound penalties for any reason, and automatically passes any roll to remain conscious due to damage or pain.

                                                          Tunnel Vision: A Brute suffers a −3 penalty to all Perception rolls, and to rolls to avoid being surprised in combat.


                                                          Nest: The Old House
                                                          The Old House is a large multi-bedroom home that could have once be rightly called a mansion. It has since fallen into a state of disrepair, with broken and boarded windows, faded paint, a yard overgrown with weeds. A pack of large dogs patrol the property, and become aggressive towards anyone they do not recognize. The broken and rusted husks of old vehicles litter the property and acres of crops occupy the surrounding fields. Despite the disrepair of the estate, the crops are healthy and well-maintained.


                                                          Safe Place Features: Arsenal, Home Security
                                                          Tilts: Foreboding
                                                          Traps: Guard Animals

                                                          Foreboding (Environmental): There is a lingering, wrongness about the Old House and the surrounding land, filling trespassers with unease and dread. Any mortal individual who enter the location must make a Resolve + Composure roll or gain the Spooked Condition.

                                                          Guard Animals: The Safe Place is patrolled by animals, typical trained guardians or feral creatures that consider the Safe Place their territory, that will attack any intruders. Animals that detect intruders also make enough noise to alert the owners of the property to the presence of unwanted guests. Guard Animals make attacks and perception rolls with a dice pool of twice the dots purchased in the Merit and use Down and Dirty combat rules. All other rolls use a die pool equal to the dots purchased in the Merit.
                                                          Trigger: Failed rolls against the Home Security feature. Alerted Guard Animals may be avoided with a Stealth roll, scared off with Intimidate, talked-down with Animal Ken, or defeated in combat.



                                                          Mystery Cult: The Harvesters
                                                          There are lonely places throughout the American mid-west. Rural, sparsely populated places that have been fading for generations, but are not yet gone. Dark tales of tales of moral, physical and even spiritual decay surround such places, told by tavern patrons and whispered around campfire. Most of these stories are fabrications, fueled by persistent stereotypes about rural degenerates. But some of them are true.
                                                          It was during the 1930’s, in the throes of the Great Depression. Farm after farm failed as the Dust Bowl throttled the life out of many farming communities. Many families left to find better opportunities, others stayed. Some that stayed grew desperate and seek other ways to survive when conventional prayer has fallen on deaf ears. It is not known if the people found the God of the Good Earth, or if the God of the Good Earth found them. What is known is a deal was struck; Life for your Crops, Life for your People. Blood for the God. And so it has continued. To ensure the land remains fertile and the animals healthy, sacrifices are offered, buried alive or dead in the fields. Most the time, these sacrifices are unlucky travelers or nosy officials. Occasionally, Harvesters will make a “hunting trip” to a nearby city, and in lean years, they will offer one of their own.
                                                          Even stranger, the occasional outsider will hear the “Voice of the Good Earth”, and join the community willingly, thus ensuring strong bloodlines and more sacrifices for generations to come.


                                                          Purpose: To offer sacrifices to the God of the Good Earth, ensuring health and prosperity for the land and those who dwell upon it.
                                                          Relic: The Book of the Good Earth. A thick piecemeal journal, passed from leader to leader, that chronicles community events and details the scripture, rites and rituals of the God of the Good Earth.
                                                          Doctrine: The beliefs of the Harvesters are a strange synthesis of Christian, Pagan, and stranger occult practices. The God of the Good Earth makes its will felt through a chosen prophet, who is typically the leader of the community, and most members of the congregation are zealous in their devotion.

                                                          Initiation Benefits
                                                          ● - All who follow the God of the Good Earth know their scripture. They gain an Occult specialty in "Book of the Good Earth"
                                                          ●● - The Harvesters are attuned to the land. They gain the Direction Sense Merit.
                                                          ●●● - The Harvesters must be prepared to do violence in the name of God of the Good Earth. They gain a dot of Weaponry.
                                                          ●●●● - Some among the Harvesters see the signs and hear the voice of God of the Good Earth. They gain the Omen Sensitivity Merit.
                                                          ●●●●● - There are leaders among the Harvesters, who direct the congregation and convey the will of God of the Good Earth. They gain the Inspiring Merit.
