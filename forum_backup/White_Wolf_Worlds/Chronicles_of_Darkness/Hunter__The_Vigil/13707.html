    One-off antagonists: A Brainstorming thread

    11-18-2013, 08:32 PM
    Hi Folks,

    With the talk about "Threats Unknown" on this forum, I was reminded of a thread that was in the old "World of Darkness" forum on the WW site. It was a thread where you just posted weird and slightly creepy locations that you could set scenarios or scenes in; it turned out to be very popular and had over 100 entries before it ran out of steam. By way of example, here's one of them:

    Classroom 213: On the edge of town is an old school house. The place is still in use as a public elementary school. If you break in and get to room 213 on the second floor, sit down in the center desk and wait two hours without saying anything. If you do it right, you'll hear invisible nails scratching against the chalkboard of the class room.
    The thread is here: http://forums.white-wolf.com/default...=posts&m=86142

    I thought it would be pretty awesome to come up with a similar thing for Hunter antagonists - just a very short (2-3 sentence) description of a threat, or rumours of a threat, that ST's can take a run with, adding it to their campaign as needed. I'll kick it off:

    The wall
    Somewhere in the middle of town, in a run down alley, there is a wall with ever-changing grafitti on it. No one knows who does the paintings - there never seem to be any witnesses to the scenes changing. The pictures are sometimes so abstract as to be undecipherable; other times they clearly show a person, or a place. What no one has yet figured out is that the scenes shown on the wall are premonitions - whoever or whatever is shown on the wall disappears within 48 hours. Late at night the drifters and homeless that live in the alleyway hear distant screaming, as if the wall itself is trying to scare them of. Or maybe warn them?

    Possible Explanations
    • The wall is sentient, and is displaying these images as a warning to those who can see it
    • There is a madman with an artistic flair in the city, who paints these images as a way of preparing for his next kidnapping/theft
    • The wall is a glitch in the God Machine, and is displaying images from the near future before they are added to the world
    • Something else...

      Ward 13
      In the city's central hospital, there's a ward which is rarely spoken of. It's cursed, some say. The mortality rate in Ward 13 is far, far higher than any other although there seems to be no pattern to the deaths save one; the most common cause of death is complications from surgery leading to a massive brain hemorrhage and brain death. For obvious reasons this high death rate is kept as low profile as possible, but some doctors are now refusing to operate on patients from Ward 13 for fear of losing their patient.

      Possible Explanations
      • There is a spirit of knowledge trapped in the ward somehow; it must survive by draining the thoughts and memories of it's victims.
      • One of the night staff has gone mad, and is attempting to harvest organs to create a Promethean, or perhaps simply to feast on. They have discovered a near-undetectable drug that creates the hemorrhage but leaves the body's main organs undamaged. (Follow up hook: Who is supplying the drug, and what are they getting out of it?)
      • An infestation of Brain Worms* are in the ward, finding recovering surgical patients a ridiculously easy target to feed on. It's only a matter of time before they spread to other areas of the hospital
      *No, I have no idea what a Brain Worm is. That's up to the ST

      Note: Although both of these so far are locations, it's the "Thing" at/causing the location which is important. Some things will be location based (as these are) others will be more "wandering monster" types.

          The Cabin Outside of Town
          In the forest in past the edge of town, there's a small wood cabin with the door and all the windows boarded up. The forest around the cabin is clearly ill; the trees seem to be dying, the ground is a disgusting mush, and nothing seems to prosper other than an unpleasant fungus. There have been a handful of disappearances in the area over the past few decades, which have given generations of kids fodder for urban legends. The thing is, more people seem to be going missing around the cabin than ever before.

          Possible Explanations:
          • A Promethean has stalled in his Pilgrimage, afraid of the outside world. He sealed himself inside the cabin long ago, and rarely ventures out for food. The fungus and disappearances are the result of the Wasteland created by the poor creature.
          • The cabin is a piece of Infrastructure, though its purpose is unknown. The fungus in the nearby forest is harmless, but tainted by the machinations of the God-Machine; when inhaled, the spores create a benign infection that allows the host to perceive the servants and gears of the Machine.
          • There exists a portal into the Hedge within the cabin, constructed and sealed sometime ago by Changelings in order to keep people safe. Their plan was not completely effective, however; the energies of the Hedge bleed through into the nearby fungus, and those who stray too close are snatched by the Fae.

          Remi. she/her. game designer.

              (Note: I realise this one isn't exactly a new concept)

              The Cult of Beauty
              "Take years off your appearance!" the ads say. "Only 4 easy payments of $49.99!". "Come and see our beauty professionals now!" And it sure seems to work. Those who go really do seem to have less wrinkles, sparkling eyes and teeth, and lustrous, shiny hair. Only there's something funny going on - their skin seems to have this waxy, plastic look, and no one will talk about what the treatment actually entails...

              Possible Explanations:
              • It's actually totally legit. The waxy look to the skin is a treatment that must be applied daily, and washes off in a nice hot shower. This is a good one to throw at your players if they're overly suspicious and you want to throw a red herring at them
              • It's an operation being run by priestesses of Hathor/Aphrodite/Venus. Again the treatment is actually legit, but in this case the priestesses (and associated God) are gathering power from the devotions and rituals being unwittingly performed by the customers. What the customers (and most of the priestesses) don't know is that they are trading in years at the end of their life for years now - pretty soon now the older patrons will begin dying of heart attacks and internal organ failure - way too soon for it to be happening, but at least they're leaving a good looking corpse..
              • It really is a cult. The customers come and are hypnotised into a slumber while the creature feeds on them. Once it looked almost human, but now it consists of a mass of fat tissue, decaying flesh and pustules of other bodily tissues and fibres. Each time it feeds, it gets stronger, and soon it will be able to feed directly off humans without any assistance or subterfuge. Fat for the Fat God!

                  Shadowkin
                  That feeling you get in a not-quite-dark room, when you swear those shadows, half seen, are moving by themselves. The shiver that runs down your spine for no reason. The unnatural cold you feel whenever you walk past that particular alleway. These can all be signs of the Shadowkin, beings that exist only in two dimensions. While capable of independant movement, they far prefer to hop a ride in someone else's shadow - the only indication that someone is being ridden this way is a distinctive shiver that runs up their spine when their shadow and the Shadowkin merge. Very little is known for sure about these elusive beings, and it has proven impossible to capture one (how do you hold something that has no substance?) Even less is known about their motives, their desires or their reason for existence.

                  Possible Powers
                  • Shadowkin can read the thoughts of any sentient being they are merged with
                  • They are able to implant suggestions or compulsions while merged, usually while the host is sleeping
                  • They can feed off fear and get stronger when exposed to panic
                  • The very strongest of Shadowkin can create shadows of their own, in any shape they desire
                  • They can give their hosts nightmares and use that fear to feed off
                  • They are immune to virtually all attacks, save extremely bright light

                      Duckless Pond

                      Hey what is the deal with that pond just outside the park entrance? It used to one of the ten hippest Duck feeding spot for spinsters ages 62-84, but the last month or so there are no ducks anywhere near it! Its like they are boycotting the pond or something! Most folks are pleased with just moving over to the other duck ponds but there seems to be always one or two really ancient biddies still tossing crumbs to the pond, they must just be stubborn. Something else isn't right though, no living thing seems to want go near the pond, not dragonflys or any other pond enjoying critter. The real weird thing is while nothing alive seems to be in the pond anymore well past dusk some people have said they see strange ripples and bubbling coming from the pond, often after one of the sentinel crones has somehow disappeared while the viewer was tying their shoe or texting.

                      Possible explainations:
                      • Hey its a nice pond to sit at, maybe the Ducks left and these old ladies just want to sit by a damned pond, they don't have to answer to you. And ponds bubble, maybe its a mole drowning or something.
                      • The Pond's Hungry! Maybe the old ladies are physical manifestations of the Pond meant to lure in food, its eaten all the ducks and maybe now the old ladies try to toss in unsuspecting park goers?
                      • Maybe the pond leads to a Trod in the Hedge and the old women are fetches left by a Keeper with thing for grannies.
                      • Maybe the ducks used to guard our world from whatever is on the other side of the Pond. Maybe the Old Woman poisoned the pond to remove the feathery wardens of their murky patron and now they gather at the pond to listen to the whispered promises of what they have to gain by helping what dwells on the other side.
                      • The old ladies are ghosts. Someone made a habit of preying on the parks elderly patrons and dumping them into the pond. For some reason the bottom of the pond drags the corpses down and doesn't let them go. Now their ghosts haunt their small wet communal grave creeping out the ducks and hoping someone will take notice and bury them proper.
                      • Conversely the old women are a small cabal of abmortals tied to the pond, as long as they keep nothing living from entering the pond they can forestall their own deaths indefinitely. But how long can they keep this up?

                          Odd Station
                          You're flipping through the radio stations while driving around in a hurry, and you stop at an unfamiliar station. What catches you is a very familiar voice. It couldn't be but you get this feeling like its someone doing an excellent impersonation of you? But wait he outright introduces himself as you! He's requesting his favorite song or giving unusually salacious banter about you're significant other to the annoying Radio Host. What's the deal? He's even responding similar to you but with certain facts just off, such as a new promotion when really you're laid off or talking about a deceased relative as if they were still around. What the hell? What's the number to this station you have to get to the bottom of this.

                          Possible explanations:
                          • Maybe the person calling in is the real you and you're a Fetch? Sucks to be you I guess.
                          • Is a Mage ritualistically stalking you, and engineering this even just to psychologically unravel you?
                          • You're Car is haunted, perhaps the ghost wishes to distract you so you get in an accident, or even to instill the right emotions.
                          • You have driven to a peculiar spot and intercepted the radio signal from another world that is slightly off this world's history. Maybe it's even the true history and the world you live in is the altered world?

                              That Dog
                              You thought the dog was something you had left behind, a childhood nightmare from your childhood home. The dog wasn't why you moved away, or at least, it wasn't the only reason. But damn if that dog didn't make it an easy decision. It was some kind of mongrel, maybe part beagle, mostly brown with thin patchy hair and prominent ribs. It never barked or growled or snarled, but you used to catch it looking at you. Just staring. You saw it at school first. At lunch time. Just sitting in the grass outside. It was at home after that. Stood on the opposite sidewalk all day. By sunset it was in your yard. In the early hours of the morning you heard a scratching and a whining. You shivered and stumbled to the window. The dog stared at you with golden eyes like glaring headlights, blinding you. Its jaw opened like a snake and its bloated tongue hung out over his blackened and rotted teeth. You closed the curtains lay awake in bed. You laid awake almost every night after that, just listening to the scratching. When you left, you thought it had stayed behind. You thought it might get to your parents. You felt guilty about that for a while. Until your girlfriend found that stray. You told yourself it was an uncanny resemblance. Put the dog outside and laid down to keep your girlfriend warm. And then the scratching started again.

                              Possible Explanations
                              • That dog has been stalking people since your town was founded. The settlers took their dogs into the woods, and a hound returned without the hunters. It was covered in strange bites and died within the week. They buried it past the treeline. But they had to expand eventually. It's never had to chase anyone this far before, but it's willing.
                              • The girlfriend summoned the creature years ago, just to lead you to her. It's here now, purpose fulfilled. Is she a lonely witch? A stupid girl meddling with forces beyond her control? Or something ancient, immortal, and hungry? Even if the girl's ends are benign, the dog may not be dismissed willingly.
                              • The dog is infrastructure, a canid angel with fluorescent eyes and a thin coat of false hair, chasing you to your destiny, furthering the will of the machine in the cracks of reality. What will you do when you find out the dog wants you to do something terrible? What will you do when you find out you have no choice?

                                  The Town Founders
                                  Why is it the leading council of the heads of the Town Businesses look just like the black and white photos of the town founders? Are they just the descendants of them as many claim to be? Maybe the town just attracts old style beard enthusiasts? That seems weird because only half them claim to be descendants the others don't have any claims to a legacy whats so ever. While they aren't billionaires, and really I'm not sure any have over a million dollars they seem to hold the towns fate in their collective hands. Its strange most seem to be Bachelors and Widows, and traditionally have been according to the research you have done, so why do they seem to always get replaced with descendants previously unknown by anyone? And they always number in 13, that seems like a rather particular number...

                                  Possible Explanations
                                  • They are a group of Immortals who have chosen to carve out a tiny kingdom out of this tiny territory. Perhaps they are literally as eternal as the town itself and they cannot leave?
                                  • Maybe they are a collection of spirits or claimed or even ghost possessed individuals? Perhaps even representing the town and local businesses?
                                  • Perhaps the council represents a senate of priests with the mayor as the sole priest to the king of some head god of a lost pantheon, reflecting the in the living/material world what is reflected in the other world?
                                  • Perhaps they are alchemical clones recreated regularly over the years to guide the town to a prophetic future as a utopia or apocalypse?
                                  • Maybe they are just weird group of rich old white folks that made some strange tradition/secret society to emulate their predecessors for kicks, or just out of some sort of luck/superstition.

                                      Bloody Mess

                                      There's a spot in the park where it looks like everything is covered in blood. Old concrete blocks are covered in streams of rust, red flowers bloom, and a fungus the color of dried blood infests the tress. It's also utterly choked with mosquitoes, people will be practically coated with the little things should they wander through this area. Despite the efforts of park staff (or because of neglect) the affected area is expanding. Worse, several park-goers have come down with a painful, bleeding rash.

                                      Possible Explanations
                                      • The cause is perfectly mundane - an ordinary fungus is feasting on chemicals illegally dumped here, and ideal conditions exist for mosquitoes to spread a nasty bacteria.
                                      • A creature was buried here by other hunters, who dumped it without performing the necessary rituals to properly destroy the entity. Too damaged to escape and in too much pain to think clearly, the half-dead creature writhes in it's shallow grave while it's ichor pollutes the area.
                                      • Someone is cultivating this place to be the site of a future ritual. Everything here has been designed to evoke 'blood' from the flowers, insects, and irritants seeded at bus stops and park benches. At the next lunar eclipse when the moon turns red, the ritual will be performed.
                                      • An injured vampire sunk into the ground here. Starved and almost comatose, the broken body attracted mosquitoes and became their nest, even as the vampire exerted his will upon them in turn. When the insects feed, a few drops of blood are tithed to their new 'home.' When the vampire finally has enough blood to heal himself, a new and very dangerous form of vampire will begin stalking the city, a living hive full of blood-stealing insects.

                                          The Trucker
                                          He's seen most of America, because he always takes the longest routes. He's always on time. He never sleeps.
                                          Well, sometimes he has to, in cheap motels between jobs, when the pills run out. He doesn't like it, though, because his sleep brings bad things with it, and death is the least of them.
                                          Possible explanations:
                                          • The trucker's dreams are the gates of a prison, a black place where wicked things dwell. When he sleeps, the gates are wide open; when he dies, they'll never close again.
                                          • He should really be sleeping more. As it is, his weary brain has to take other people's sleep, and it's not very delicate about it.
                                          • The stimulant he takes to stay awake has a side effect: when it runs out, his mind goes elsewhere, leaving his body an empty suit of meat. There are lots of ghosts and demons in the old motels, it seems.

                                                      The Giant of Filth and Refuse.

                                                      "It's a bit of a local legend now. I guess I am too." The man grunts, limping over to the Giant's side. "Tourists love it," the vagrant sighs wearily.
                                                      He reaches a quivering hand towards his creation and brushes his fingers against it. The light rain splatters the man's quivering hand as he runs it down the side of the massive structure. It is a hideous, towering, and vaguely humanoid shape on the side of an old dirt road. It stands a dozen feet tall, made out of pressed mud and a whole lot of garbage. The old man pricks his finger on the jagged edge of a soda can at the giant's hip, but he doesn't seem to notice. He takes a step back, sweeping his sharp but ancient gaze from the sculpture, to you, and then to the large painted sign that identifies the Giant.
                                                      "It's not what I would have called it." The man grumbles. "I might have come up with something more clever, less cumbersome."
                                                      You wonder what that means. You wonder who did name it. You try to ask him. You're not sure you even managed. You stare transfixed at the Giant of Filth and Refuse. At any rate, he doesn't answer. He follows your gaze and stares with you.
                                                      "It's almost done." The man whispers.
                                                      Blood drips down his fingers and mingles with the mud at his feet. He reaches down and scoops up a handful of moist earth. He considers it briefly before he presses it gently to the side of the Giant of Filth and Refuse.
                                                      "Almost done..."

                                                      Possible Explanations
                                                      • "It was the recruiter, I guess. He told me the army would be good for me. Shipped me across the sea. If it weren't for him I wouldn't be homeless. Wouldn't be mad." The man was influenced by a supernatural creature a long time ago. It is a patient entity, but it will soon return for its
                                                      • "Do you like it? Most people are scared of it. I'm scared of it. Not you though. That's good. It's a gift. It's for you. Yes... I wasn't sure before. But I know it now. It is for you. It's always been for you." This possibility bothers him almost as much as it bothers you. The sculpture seemed like a waste of life, an endless and pointless endeavor that he couldn't make himself stop. Until you came. It all makes sense now. It is your burden now.
                                                      • "Press against the side. Yes, just there. Good... Can you hear it? The pulse. The voice. The life. What's inside? What's inside? Please. Tell me what's inside. Please *Sobs*" The sculpture is the vessel or the body for something ancient and infant. Great and terrible. It will soon shamble forth. The giant will walk.

                                                          The Whispering Blade

                                                          The police (or maybe your cell) arrived just in time. His eighth victim is expected to live, albeit with some horrible scarring and the need of a prosthetic or two. The killers swears that the knife he used in the killings is possessed by a demon, and no matter what he did to get rid of it, the knife would always return. After weeks of hearing the maddening whispers, he couldn't resist anymore and gave in to the terrible demands it made of him. Of course, considering that the killer had a history of schizophrenia, no one is buying it.
                                                          • The man is a schizophrenic and was being treated for it, dutifully taking his medication. He also stood to inherit a rather large sum of money after the death of his father. The man's uncle, after being written out of the will after a falling out with his brother, commissioned a dozen identical knives with hollow handles that held a long-lasting battery, a radio receiver, and a tiny speaker, disguised as a bit of filigree.
                                                          • The blade itself isn't special, but there's a powerful spirit of murder-by-knife that's rather fond of it, and has made use of it for years. The spirit is powerful enough to repair the knife after being destroyed, and arrange events that made sure the knife ended up wherever it wanted. Finding the ignorant wolf-blood was a bonus, he had no idea that the voices in his head were quite real.
                                                          • He's not crazy, he's psychic. Never realizing the nature of his psychometric powers, the doctors diagnosed his visions as schizophrenia, and the pills he was prescribed dulled those brain functions that controlled his powers. All was well and good, until he visited an antique store and became fascinated with a beautiful blade for sale. It had more history than anything else he'd ever touched - centuries of bloody, unholy history, and before it was lost, many have fought to possess it for various reasons. Mere days after images of the blade begin to circulate, either released by the police or during the cell's research of it, a great many new people are going to come to town, and they don't plan to leave without it in their possession.
