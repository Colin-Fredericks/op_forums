    I've posted before about the idea of having local twists on existing groups, but you've really outdone yourself with this; you managed to make what I would've seen as a thoroughly Christian organization fit into Japanese myth like it ain't no thing.

    The new powers are great, although I'm a little iffy on Kigo having an indefinite duration and I have to echo the sentiment that inflicting Soulless/Thrall is beyond the power scale of Hunters, especially given how hard that is to inflict even with a moderately powerful Mage. I think Willpower drain with Shinigami Buki is powerful enough without those Conditions, given how Dread Powers are fueled by Willpower. I'm also surprised that devouring a soul isn't considered a Breaking Point; that's pretty evil, even if you're doing it to a monster.

    Kenshi and Kaibutsu are both really the kind of powers that I read and immediately need to build a character around.

    Dorei-sei is written a little strangely, setting the target number of successes equal to Seitokuken dots, rather than 3/5 successes or a contested roll. The same mechanic shows up in Kaibutsu as well, and feels a little wonky.

    I can't wait to see the other groups; the Ama-san immediately catch my eye, but the Gimu and Hotogisu are both interesting as well. I'm sure the Bijin will make me cry, just the same as the 2e Daeva and probably the 2e Galateids as well.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

            Originally posted by MCN View Post
            Now I'm left wondering how the Otodo see the Inferno.
            Shinto, due to Japan's cultural squeamishness with the dead and the disease necrotized tissue carries, has a concept of evil being a disease. That's why you see the idea of people being corrupted by dark knowledge or malign power in anime a lot, to the Japanese mindset, their recklessness with handling the metaphorical body meant they came down with a bad case of Megalomanitis. That and the idea of selfishness inherently corrupting any goal.

            I imagine they began with the idea that Infernal demons are corrupted versions of other beings, and that discovering the Inferno is an actual dimension of its own motivated by hunger for darker passions would actually solve more of their questions than raise them. From there Buddhism would probably fill in the rest of the gaps, true Dominions being the inhabitants/prisoners of Jigoku (aka Naraka, with a Japanese name and ruler).
