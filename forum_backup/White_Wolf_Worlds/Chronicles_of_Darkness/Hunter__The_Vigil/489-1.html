            I'm late to the party, but while I'm not especially fond of any of the titles, I like Threats Unknown the best. It fits in better with the established two-word pattern for the Vigil threat books, but more, it just doesn't have the disadvantages I see in the other two names. Crime for All Seasons feels very punny and facetious for a horror game, more pulp-ish, plus "crime" feels like a loose fit for the predations of the threats detailed. Remains is better, and would be my second choice behind TU, but it feels inappropriately final; it has a feel like capping the gameline off. It also doesn't feel to me like it describes the subject matter of the book so much as its context; it feels like it says more about "stuff we didn't get around to covering" than it does actually describing what you will find in the book.

                So, I voted for Remains as I think it's a better title for this particular book. But I think "Threats Unknown" should be the title of another book - namely, a book of new antagonists introduced just for Hunter, not already parts of the World of Darkness. The ghouls from the Hunter fiction can be in there, as can more on various nightmare alternate realities, Lovecraft monstrosities, and just random monster-of-the-week fodder. Monsters at the frontier of occult horror that the established supernatural communities don't really deal with or know about. Hunters (and Mages) are the ones who are curious enough to look into reports of the unknown; seems like a perfect concept for a Hunter book.

                                            Remains.

                                            On one hand, it's the "remaining" gamelines not addressed by another book.

                                            It's also a book about remains. The Reanimated are stitched together from the leftover parts of living people. The Lost are what remains after they're torn by the Thorns. The Sin-Eaters are, in a sense, a form of living dead. The Arisen are the remnants of a lost civilizations. The Demons are the remains of the God-Machines projects, gone awry.

                                            Seconding "Threats Unknown" as a book for other things that go bump in the night, a sort of Night Horrors for Hunter (ideally updating some of the material from Antagonists as well).
