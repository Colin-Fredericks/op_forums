    Compact: The Reality Hackers, The Source Code to the Universe

    11-01-2013, 06:18 PM
    The Reality Hackers
    The Source Code to the Universe



    Author's Note: This group predates the God Machine Chroncile, I wrote them now I would tie them in much tighter. The angel would remain a Qashmallim.

    In the middle of the night Viktor Larsson awoke to the most terrifying fright of his life. Standing in his bedroom was an angel. Twelve foot tall with six burning wings and three faces: A man, a bull and a 1970s television. Shouting "Be not afraid" in a fiery echoing voice that would give a grown man a heart attack. The angel gave Viktor a tablet of perfected gold carved with the secrets of the universe and departed, Viktor fainted.

    When Viktor awoke the tablet was nowhere to be found, nor was there any trace of the angel. He began to write a blog post about the "weird dream" he had the other night. When he mentioned the tablet he found himself writing a little on it's contents. Then he found himself writing expansions on his points, then expansions on those points.

    It was three days before Viktor's friends were able to break into his bedroom - his doors and windows had fused with the walls and acquired supernatural toughness. By the time they arrived Viktor was half dead, and an enormous amount of source code was posted on the internet.

    Though Viktor survived he never recovered, from that moment on he would be plagued by exhaustion whenever he attempted to think hard. The story continues through his friends. Wishing to help they searched his apartment for evidence as to what caused his collapse, they found the code. Illegible but apparently it had compiled successfully. Some of the functions did nothing, some crashed the computer, and the function labelled "Scan for faults" returned a chilling message.

    Error detected.
    There is a localised error in reality manifesting as a contagious brain tumour transmitted by shared imaginations.

    The map reference was a half finished nursery, you couldn't not double check something like that could you?


    The Enemy

    To the Reality Hackers the primary enemy of the Vigil are the Errors in reality that their software detects. Things from beyond our universe that squirmed their way in through the cracks, breakdowns in natural laws and blasphemies against all existence. Most Reality Hackers encountered at least one Error when they were starting out, something to convince them that yes the supernatural really does exist.

    When other hunters hear this, the reaction is usually one of stark disbelief. More than anything this is simple mismatched assumptions, when other Hunters hear the term "Error in reality" they assume some alien monster god, a mass of pulsing flesh and twisted spacetime ready to doom entire civilisations. They exist and they're fucking terrifying, but the reality hackers know that for every impossible god there are hundreds of weaker Errors. Some are so weak they're almost unnoticeable without some sort of detector. Like that localised Error in probability that makes post-2008 pennies land on heads every time and was debugged by glueing two pennies together so they could only land on tails and causing a hyper-localised crash and reboot.

    Sidebar: What is a Error
    The exact definition of a Error in reality is somewhat vague and should be adapted to include your personal interpretation of the World of Darkness. As a vague guideline Errors are something alien to this reality intruding. You can also use the following chart for rough ideas:
    • Almost certainly a Error: Abysall Intruders, Things Which Should Not Be (see Second Sight)
    • Possibly a Error: Supernal magic, Faeries & faerie magic.
    • Almost certainly not a Error: Spirits, Ghosts, Astral entities.

    It dosn't matter if Promethians, Qashmallim or other Pyros related phenomina are Errors or not because The Code treats them as a special case. They show up, with safety instructions and an explanation of what they are and why they should be helped or left alone.

    This bias has not gone unnoticed

    Yet the Errors are not the only thing the Reality Hackers seek. The origin story is well known among their ranks and the Hackers have seen to much to dismiss the possibility of angels out of hand. The story has implications if it's true, if angels exist then surely God does too?

    To some members the Reality Hackers are a stepping stone to the various occult and spiritual communities as their investigation into angels leads them towards deeper theological and occult questions. Most tend towards the philosophies and ideas that suggest at least some sort of empirical reality: Either God exists or he dosn't. Either He is a loving merciful deity, or he's not.

    The spiritualistic Reality Hackers often bring what they learned back to the Compact, cleaning up and documenting parts of their software that the Compact previously lacked the frame of reference to understand. Some believe that the Code is the ultimate definition of reality and ignore ideas that don't seem to fit, others are open to the possibility that they only have the source files to a subset of the universe, and some take the idea of "code first" to it's logical conclusion and posit a kind of literal Deus Est Machina to which they have the source code, but not a user account.

    Beneath the picture is the caption: There is a bug in the universe. We have the source code and three cases of cola. Let's do this.


    Hunters

    You love programming, the harder the problem the happier you are and there's no problem tougher than trying to refactor the secrets of the universe from undocumented spaghetti code into something elegant.

    You admit it. You joined to get a social life and maybe impress some girls, and it worked. Hanging out with monster hunters comes with a real sense of camaraderie and being the experts on monsters so fucking weird the others don't even know the words to describe them gets you the respect you never got in high school.

    You alone can see the truth, not even the other hackers Understand like you do. But once you've finished fixing this bit of code it will prove you right, you'll show those fools at the institute then!

    When you were four your mother asked what you wanted to be when you grew up, you said "Iron Man!". You don't quite have a suit of powered armour but you are defending the public with science. Ok maybe you only go after the small errors but you're still going out at night with a home made hardware rig that you think looks pretty badass, and you know you've saved lives. That's cool enough.

    You always sneered at scientists with their microscopes and clip boards. You knew that real understanding could only come from within. The new age groups were all a disappointment, more interested in what they wanted to believe than seeking occult truths, but you always held out hope for the next one. Then you met some people who really had access to the secrets of the universe, it's still hard for you to admit that the path to the Truth is... computer science.


    Stereotypes

    VanDemon Electric: Once you make a little name for yourself fixing up The Code you'll get an offer from these guys. They'll employ you, and if you don't take the offer they'll sponsor you to keep fixing The Code. They're think they're going to save the world, and that our Code might help

    Null Mysteriis: You see them crop up on the mailing lists. They always have some theory to test or want help tracking something down. They're nice enough, and they pay quite well.

    Hakollel shel Hamerkava: I don't know if the angel was real, but I thought why not play it safe and go back to shul. Well I told the Rabbi why I was back and a few weeks later I was put in touch with someone who could invoke the power of god. He doesn’t know computers, I don't know Torah, we're both learning so much.

    MI18: This project attracts all sorts of crazies and some of our best contributors were more than a few screws loose. After someone I knew got sectioned I tried hacking in to see what happened to him and my computer was eaten by demons living in the firewall. Yes demons! What the fuck are we getting into?

      Status

      Status in the Reality Hackers is a loose confusing thing based entirely on reputation. Contributing good code, mentoring others, checking and approving code contributions all increase a Hunter's Status. The inherently unknown subject matter at hand and the verity of egos and oddball personalities makes this a lot more confusing as it sounds.

      Unlike a lot of Hunter organisations the Reality Hackers are not too worried about infiltration from other Hunter groups. The way they see it, it dosn't matter if you're paid by the Government, a multinational with "investments" in the supernatural or an armed group of fanatics who want another weapon to point at the supernatural. If you're contributing good code they're glad to have you on board. For the most part the system works due simple self interest, sharing what you uncover about The Code is an investment that will eventually give you more knowledge than you could discover on your own. And The Code went so public so fast that every interested Conspiracy is in on it. If any of them made a play to take control it would start a huge free for all, so for now they keep their distance and skim from the top.

      The Reality Hackers don't share their real names though, that would just be stupid.

      O: You have an account on the mailing lists and source code repository. Working alongside the Compact gives you some insight into the Code you working with. You get a free Speciality (The Code) in Computers.

      OOO: You're reputation has grown enough for one of the many curious parties interested in the Code to sponsor you to work full time, if they haven't tempted you to sign on with them instead. You gain 2 dots of Resources.

      OOOOO: Everyone knows your name and when they're stuck you're the person they turn to. In this case "everyone" includes some frankly scary people. You gain three dots of Allies divided among other Hunter Compacts or even Conspiracies, if you're willing to risk building a closer relationship than just answering their E-mails through a few proxies.


      Usergroups

      The Decompiliers Study The Code itself. They are dedicated to refactoring The Code into something readable and documenting what it does and how it works. A job which could take decades.
      Free Speciality: Computer (Programming)

      In contrast The Analysts are interested in the mysteries behind The Code. Was there really an angel that handed down holy code? If so does god exist and is he on our side? Should I start going to church? Or maybe the angel was just imaginary, or the brain interpreting inspiration an more understandable way, or interpreting a sudden activation of psychic powers. Wouldn't you just die to know the truth?
      Free Speciality: Occult (Angels).

      Finally The Troubleshooters find trouble, then shoot it. Ok they usually find trouble and help someone else to shoot it. Working with other more combat ready Hunters the Troubleshooters use The Code to track down Errors in reality and fix them before anyone gets hurt.
      Free Speciality: Investigation (Reality Errors)


      Endowment: The Code (O – OOOOO)

      The Reality Hackers claim they have access to the source code to the universe. The terrifying thing is that they might be correct. They call this by the uncreative but dramatic name of The Code. Currently they have been refactoring and documenting The Code for just under a year and it remains a barely understood mess of spaghetti code. Dot's in the Code represent a personal understanding of how to best use The Code.

      There are currently two features of The Code that are mostly reliable: Detection and Protection. All dicerolls to activate The Code use the following dicepool: Intelligence + Computer + The Code + the Equipment Bonus of the hardware running The Code - 5.

      Detection uses The Code to simulate the universe in the local area and compare the predicted results to the actual results to scan for Errors in the universe. Usually a mobile phone's radio receiver provides enough information for a comparason, but if the Error is audible to the microphone add a +1 situational modifier. If it is visible to the camera add a +3 situational modifier. The area scanned inflicts it's own penalties. The Hunter may scan a area with a radius of 5 feet with no penalty. Every additional -1 penalty doubles the radius. The maximum area is whatever reduces the dice pool to a Chance Die.

      Dramatic Failure: The hardware melts into a puddle of plastic and silicone.
      Failure: Make a second activation roll, but with only half the penalty for the range scanned:

      Dramatic Failure or Failure: The hardware melts into a puddle of plastic and silicone.
      Success: The hardware crashes and must be rebooted. Save the logs of course.
      Exceptional Success: The Hunter receives an error message or nonsense data. He is free to try again.

      Success: The Hunter receives the number and approximate location of every Error in the scanning area, either as a text list of displayed on as a map on any major mapping software of the Hunter's preference.
      Exceptional Success: The Hunter also receives a short summery of every Error. For example: There is a local Error in the laws of physics that makes the colour green burn to the touch.

      Protection uses The Code to reinforce the local laws of physics, weakening the powers of any Error in the local area... no one has the slightest clue how this works but they'd love to know. The Hunter rolls against the Errors Stamina + Supernatural Advantage. Protection may be used against multiple Errors at once, roll against the highest Stamina + Supernatural Advantage with a -2 penalty for every additional Error in the area.

      Dramatic Failure or the Error wins with an Exceptional Success: The hardware melts into a puddle of plastic and silicone.
      Failure or the Error gets more Successes: Make a second activation roll – the highest Error's Supernatural Advantage:

      Dramatic Failure:
      Failure: The hardware crashes and must be rebooted. Save the logs of course.
      Success: The hardware freezes and requires an additional activation roll of this Endowment to unfreeze.
      Exceptional Success: The Hunter receives an error message. He is free to try again.

      The Hunter gets more Successes: The Error gets a penalty to activate all Dread Powers equal to the Hunter's dots in The Code for the rest of the scene. Against powers that do not require the Error to roll, use The Code as whatever bonus makes the most sense. For example when exposed an Error in spacetime reduce the penalty to navigating impossible geometries by The Code.
      The Hunter wins with an Exceptional Success: The Error gets a penalty to activate all Dread Powers equal to the Hunter's dots in The Code + the Equipment Bonus of the Hardware running the Code for the rest of the scene.

      Sidebar: And the other features

      No one knows what The Code's limits are, there are certainly more features for debugging errors in reality that no one has gotten working reliably yet. Some Hackers theorise that full understanding of The Code will allow godlike control over reality. If you can change reality to protect against an error, why can't you change reality to make a pizza?

      The theory causes quite a bit of debate, is the world ready? A few analysts say that if an Angel really did inspire the code, then it must be ok. Others say that mankind is ready, or the only way they will become ready is by learning through experience with The Code. On the other side some people say that we're obviously not ready, and of course there are those who don't think The Code is ever going to give anything near to omnipotence.

      But The Code won't be understood or used to it's full potential any time soon. So what does this mean for your story. Simple, you can and are encouraged to introduce new features of The Code are too unreliable to work, except that one time when it is required for the plot. If you want to introduce an Error in reality but can't without it wiping out the players, that's where a new feature can come in.

      And save the log files, that's valuable data.


      When using The Code it's important to keep your batteries charged up. The Code eats through batteries like there's no tomorrow.

      For simplicity assume that a battery is worth five "power points" to an equivalent piece of hardware - so a mobile phone battery gives five power points to a mobile phone, but a larger laptop battery gives five power points to a laptop. Using Detection drains one power point, using Protection drains one Power point per turn!

      Reality Hackers often rig up some extra juice for their devices. It's not uncommon to see one with custom made hardware that uses entire belts of batteries all wired up to the same phone. Various low battery warning indicators are common too.

              Thank you.

              I would say the three most common ways to join are: You hear rumours in the online hacker community, you bump into one while working at a technology company or at university, you hear of them on the course of the Vigil. While I do think that each Compact/Conspiracy is it's own individual game and tend to design for single group cells, it just made sense that this group would do a lot of crossover. They're pretty neiche so teaming up with others to balance out a cell is really useful. They're also looking for knowledge outside their speciality so sharing knowledge is a good deal.


              At the top of the post I said that if I had written this later I'd have gone for a God Machine focus, rather than anything extra-dimensional. Errors would literally be a bug in the God Machine; someone dropped a coin down a storm grate and jammed a gear or a demon went around causing trouble. Now every electrical appliance goes slightly off in a small area (not fun if you have a pace maker). Since the hunters can't actually see the God Machine fixing errors requires the equivalent of metaphorically turning it off and on again, or making the God Machine change it's settings to bypass the damaged section. Basically gluing pennies together.

              The angel could either be a Qashmallim who gave humans something that would eventually let them control the God Machine because The Principle willed it, or the God Machine outsourcing it's repair work. Though I prefer a Qashmallim.
