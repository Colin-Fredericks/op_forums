    213: Old Ben Jackson, he's a fixture in the hunter community. He was the man that finally exorcised the Young property, and was the lynchpin figure when the area cells decided that the witches were a problem too big to ignore. He just sent out a message; terminal cancer from his years of work in the plant. His last request? Kill the vampire lord that he never could, and get his entire personal archive.

    214: (Ashwood Abbey) It was a good idea for a Halloween treat, hold a seance at the "good" Rev. Ogilvy's grave. Only the dear reverend has been away from the pleasures of the flesh for far too long, and has possessed one of your number, the local barrister. The man has a public reputation for being tough yet fair at the bench, as well as being a devoted father and husband involved in dozens of charity projects. If Ogilvy goes too wild, it threatens to drag the entire Abbey into the open.

        215) It's always tragic when a soldier can no longer imagine returning to civilian life. Recently, your city has been on the verge of being 100% supernatural free; the goal of every respectable Hunter for as long as anybody can remember. But despite victory being within their grasp, one particular cell has other plans. Their leader has become addicted to the war against the supernatural. Having a license to kill and an enemy that has no legal rights makes them feel empowered. So, while all the other hunters are distracted finishing off the last few stragglers, they concoct a plan to renew the war in earnest. They're going to unleash a wave of monsters upon the city so they can keep doing what they love; killing without consequences!

                    [Lights another candle for the thread]

                    218) One of your contacts has occasional, random prophetic abilities. Just yesterday, they sent your Cell a warning to "beware the Ides of March".
                    a) As the day draws closer, you've been noticing the silhouettes of owls perched on the branches outside your home. Leech activity in the area has dropped too. Wonder why.
                    b) The message also said something about spiders and tangled webs. Your Cell-mate has become worried that it might be a reference to their new girlfriend, who they say has been acting more agitated recently and also hates spiders.
                    c) You Cell starts tracking down leads to a big-time monster you've been pursuing. On the 14th, you've managed to chase it into a crossroads and lose track of which way it went. In the distance, you can see 5 towers, and each of your is arguing over which way it must have went.

                    219) Your Cell has accomplished much in the 5 years you've been together. Now an ally has asked you to perform a task they were never able to - rescuing their sister from some monstrous abductor that stole them away 5 years ago. Doing so will mean going into some kind of other-realm called "the Hedge", finding the trail, and stealing the sister back.

                    It was tough, but you've managed to find her. Unfortunately, you've also found doppelgangers of yourselves who claim they are you, taken away along with her, a few days ago. They have to be lying, but how do they know so much about your life prior to then?

                    220) One of your Cell (or an important Contact or Ally) has always been a tech-head. Well, they've just gotten the latest smart phone that is supposed to come equipped with a rudimentary Artificial Intelligence. It doesn't sound as mechanical and pre-recorded as other virtual assistants, sure, but AI? That's fiction. Except... this keeps using turns of phrase and reminding you of things just like a friend you lost contact with. Last you heard, they were going to work with some new tech company. Actually, it looks like the same one behind the phone (through a shell corporation)... Elysium Concepts Limited.

                        221) Your Cell was having a normal meeting, until the lights went out and you were all knocked unconscious by waves of men in dark suits.

                        Next thing you know, you come to in a dark room with a single light illuminating a terminal. Surrounded by monsters. Who say they are your Cell-mates. When you check, you are a monster too.

                        Then you are given your "mission" - use your knowledge of the area's supernatural factions to infiltrate the monsters' organizations and eliminate a threat. Do this, and you can go back to the way you were. But the clock is ticking.

                        a) The organization is an up-and-coming Conspiracy, that is trying out their experimental techno-occult mutagen on your Cell. Use the forms of monsters to join and destroy their societies, and the Vigil might have a new weapon in its arsenal. Don't worry, they promise to give you the anti-mutagen once the mission is successful. Provided you can take it in time, there should be no long-term consequences.

                        b) The group responsible is another Cell - turns out a group of monsters were going to usurp your bodies to destroy the local Hunter community from the inside. The other Cell was able to decipher a ritual that would, in a twist of poetic justice, turn the monsters' own trick back upon them. The other Cell is working on how to get you back to your own bodies... before your minds fade into oblivion and the monsters' wills return.

                        c) A Conspiracy calling itself CONTROL has been in the practice of implanting/infecting monsters with a kind of techno-occult Malware. They capture a target, perform The Procedure upon them, and can then know what it knows, and even influence their behavior. Even control them via remote piloting. CONTROL has conscripted you as "emergency assets" to deal with a critical matter using your exclusive experience. They have "activated" some of their "Supernatural Resources" in the region and put you in control - complete the objectives while control persists and they will let you go, or even extend an offer.

                        [This is designed for giving Hunters a taste at playing Monsters, Dread Powers and all.]

                                            225) For nearly as long as you have been a Hunter, you've had a particular monster who has been your arch nemesis. You've been trying to kill each other for who knows how long, and yet you two always manage to survive and escape somehow. But that might be coming to an end, because today, you awoke to find yourself in a place that was not your own, in a body that was much the same. It would seem that you and your nemesis have exchanged bodies while your sleeping, and now you've become the very monster you have sworn to destroy.

                                            But that's not the worst of it, because once you take to the streets in search of answers, your former cell shows up, lead by the impostor, who, like always, seems especially keen on erasing you from this world!

                                                        227) A gathering of hunters is been organized. It's being held in a big, fancy hotel. The kind you could only dream of getting a room at. You've gotten a invitation. It says "you+1".
                                                        When you get there you find hunters from practically every Compact & Conspiracy in attendance. There's a couple of hunters from some "Confederacies", whatever those are. Some of the hunters have used their +1 to bring a friendly supernatural with them. Some of the local supernaturals have crashed the gathering to see what's going on, & to be annoying.
                                                        So far all is good.
                                                        Then, early the next morning there is a bone-chilling scream. A dead body has been found. In one of the hotel's rooms. All you know for certain is that it wasn't natural causes, that the victim had attended last night's gathering of hunters, & that the police have already been called.
                                                        • This hook has a lot of pieces. To make it easier I have come up with some ideas for characters to turn up in the story. You could also rummage through various sourcebooks & threads to make up the numbers. Whatever works for you.

                                                        Potential Attendees
                                                        ● A middle-ranked (not a foot soldier, not one of the heads) Invictus ventrue who's come to represent Invictus. He has no desire to rise beyond where he currently is, because there are peasants, there are lords, & then there are those in between. I think he crashed the gathering.
                                                        ● A small pack of Uratha that work with TFV, fighting whatever threats may surface within their territory. Come in as the +1's for some TFV hunters.
                                                        ● An old willworker built like a brick shithouse. The mage has discovered that they are dying & upon learning of tjis gathering, has decided to hand over his life's work to all the hunters that he can. His life's work being the accumulated knowledge of the Abyss & it's "servants/phenomena/etc." collected over nearly seventy years of combating it. Crashed the gathering.
                                                        ● Some Horrors & Brief Nightmares.
                                                        ● A number of local supernaturals that have been sent in by their respective factions to represent.
                                                        ● A group of six so-called "demigods". One Egyptian one. One Greek one. One Nordic one. One Mesoamerican one. One Yoruban/Voodoo one. One Japanese one. Not here to attend the gathering, just rooming in the same hotel.
                                                        ● Random people with minor supernatural templates & Merits.
                                                        ● A shartha looking for a useful future vessel.

                                                        Supplementary Events
                                                        ● One of the hotel rooms is haunted.
                                                        ● There are a group of dormant Pandorans in the basement. They wake up if a Promethean gets close enough.
                                                        ● There is a colony of goblin-roaches in the hotel gardens.
                                                        ● A local spirit noble calls upon some of the hunters to give a ruling at a courtroom trial for spirits. They have been selected for this task as the spirits are having trouble themselves & the hunters are a neutral party.
                                                        ● Some of the people in the hotel are experiencing horrific nightmares. The nightmares appear to be of Begotten origin, but none of the Begotten that are here appear to be responsible.
                                                        ● Two werewolf packs are gearing up for a brawl in the middle of the hotel's foyer.
                                                        ● One vampire accuses another of messing with the memory of one of the attending hunter's.
                                                        ● A changeling accuses an attending hunter of being a copy of the changeling, & a servant of the "True Fae".
                                                        ● A Huntsman crashes the event to grab an attending changeling.
                                                        ● A willworker brought with them a mystical item that they made, to show it off.
                                                        ● A hunter becomes one of the Bound.

                                                        Various Characters
                                                        ● Hotel Staff
                                                        ● Hotel Attendees
                                                        ● Local Cops
                                                        ● Hunters that got invites to the gathering
                                                        ● People that got brought along as "+1's"
                                                        ● Local members of the supernatural community that have decided (or been ordered to) to "crash the party"
