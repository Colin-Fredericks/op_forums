    Is there any tentative interest in a Hunter book for Geniuses/General Mad Science?

    04-21-2014, 04:56 PM
    I recently obtained copies of Night Stalkers/Spirit Slayers/Witch Finders, and reading them has gotten me thinking about what a Hunter book focused on the Inspired would look like. What do mad scientists look like to the uninitiated? How might one perceive them as a threat? As allies? As an exploitable commodity? I imagine that such a book could also include a bunch of other mad science options in found in the World of Darkness--from the Free Council's concept of technéto the God Machine's occult physics to Divine Fire-compelled Demiurges.

    I've got a few ideas collected on what Compacts and Conspiracies would be most interested in the Inspired. TFV, the Cheiron Group, Aegis Kai Doru, and the Marduk Society all I feel would have a vested interest in super-intelligent inventors. Someone who can reliably build death rays and mind control devices is, obviously a security risk, but what insights (and breakthroughs in Advanced Armory) could one glean from studying their mad technology? On the other hand, the Shield & Spear might be quite miffed over the idea that there are people out there building Relics they can't get their grubby little hands on. On the Compact level of things, Null Mysteriis stands front and center. Who are these people claiming to be scientists but all the while pissing on Francis Bacon's grave? The Loyalists of Thule are going to sit up and pay attention to anyone calling themselves "Lemurians." Even though the Inspired normally fall outside of their scope, the Barrett Commission, the Talbot Group, and Searchlight all could end up fighting Geniuses who manipulate the stock market or kidnap people and turn into Beholden.

    Ah, Beholden. I can see a lot of Hunter stories revolving around them. A friend or family member starts acting strange, hanging around some weird guy who insists on being addressed as "Doctor" even though you're pretty sure there's no such thing as "Psychomimetic Chronobiology," which is what he claims to be his field of study. You ask questions, too many questions. There's some insane ranting. Rayguns are produced. On the other hand...Beholden psychology is both fascinating and oh-so-exploitable. The potential for a loyal corporate strikeforce who'd prefer to be told what to think over receiving a paycheck, true patriots who never question orders, fanatical vampire slayers who don't need so much routine brainwashing. The possibilities boggle the mind. Maybe, just maybe, if we could get one of these mad scientists to work for us...

    Clockstoppers are definitely another interesting point of contention. I'm sure they stymie and infuriate Null Mysteriis. You can't even get good empirical data on them. What does the Long Night think about these deniers of the decadent modern world though?

    Finally, some brainstorming over potential Inspired-hunting Compacts/Conspiracies:

    A Neo-Luddite organization that believes technological advances are spiritually unhealthy and/or acts of oppression against marginalized cultures.
    A group of scientists who want to oust Geniuses completely from the halls of academia, as they tend to give respectable researchers a bad name. (Could also work as a new branch of Null Mysteriis or the Barrett Commission)
    Adherents to an obsolete theory of the universe who see in Mania the chance for redemption.
    A techgnostic cult that worships an ancient, powerful Orphan.
    Seekers of a unified theory of everything, skeptics extraordinaire, with an Endowment that lets them wreak havoc with Havoc.

    Is this something that people here would be interested in? If so, I will keep posting things here, and begin drafting up something that could be cool. Also, we could make it a group project, like Dream Catchers. That could be fun.

              The core theme of Genius is "No one rules the world".

              Geniuses are the kind of people who grab the steering wheel, fight to turn this boat around, and fail. Hunters are the kind of people just trying to hold on tight, and maybe hold onto a few other people too.


              (Using The Union as an example). "The factory goes out of business, and the PCs try to put food on their families table" is as much of a Hunter!Genius story as "A mad sociologist traps the town in a giant force field just to see what happens, the PCs try to keep their families safe and fight for diminishing supplies of food and water". Though obviously, the second one is a bit more attention grabbing.

              Obviously, you'd also get the classic "Dr Mengle cuts people up, fight through his mutant zombies so you can shoot him", but the core theme is something big happens, you're trying to hold on.


              There's lots of cool ways you can take this. From TF:V trying to suppress Geniuses who create revolutionary energy supplies that will damage the American economy (and failing as utterly as the Geniuses trying to revolutionise the economy, No one rules the world). Network Zero documentries about revolutionary technology that could have been. Null Mysteriis looking forward to the next scientific revolution that will turn their world upside down (and being really happy that the Mad Scientists respect their sane brothers in arms enough to rescue them in powered armour every now and again)

                  Something I learned from a crossover game I helped run, Geniuses and Hunters might work together, but don't expect it to be easy. The key thing about hunters and geniuses in the same place is that, while hunters are primarily concerned with both keeping things quiet and not being stupid, where the geniuses focus on finding their own inventions and progress. That's not saying that geniuses aren't able to worry about people, but the difference in motives is glaring when you compare a Union member to your typical Staunen.

                  A big thing is the issue of Hunters going beholden, though I think it should be a case-by-case deal. I mean, Null Mysteriis are definitely gonna question, heavily, any Genius that decides to stop by and claim they've solved everything.
