        the nice thing is that the Beasts doesn't enjoy that extra attention- they are stuck around a bunch of fanboys who just decide to "please" their dragon the way they think it wants to be pleased. From how I read their preview, the "blessing" in the situation is the fact they participate in the Epic of "slaying the beast"- maybe they even believe that the fact the "god" decides it had enough of their shit and attack them is the way it answers their "tributes"- for from their point of view, it means the Beast has decided to allow itself getting killed by its worshipers. That means that if the first monsters they kill don't make their god descend on them, they need better sacrifices- something that the Beast truly desires. Like that vampire she hangs with a lot lately, or that pack of shifters she regularly hunts with. It is obvious that the Beast likes those monsters- and so by sacrificing them to it, they would please their god enough to bless them with its presence in person, giving them the right to kill it with their own hands.

        Also, I'm pretty sure Heroes would be pissed off from the Minutemen- beasts are the ones who rise heroes, fears gives power to courage. Such misunderstanding would surely make the common Hero very pissed off (and perhaps even the common beast, but they won't be stupid enough to correct the hunters, I think). Of course, that is if they'll even bother speaking with them. Heroes expect hunters to be follow, and don't seem to be interested in what they have to say. It also makes me wonder how would an imprisoned Hero in the hands of VASCU or a similar group with access to psychologists would feel when he tries to explain his "therapist" his roll in the monomyth, why what he does is so important and all the other dream symbolism of their Journey.

        As for the Dream Warriors- I assume those are the guys who co-opt Lairs? I assume that the idea of astral-projecting hunters who fight the nightmares of mankind was bound to happen. Not that I say they are not cool- they sound like a great concept and I wait for more about them, but I still hoped for an Australian group who search the Dreamtime for beasts, or maybe some corporation who takes over Lairs and terraform them for its own needs. Oh well.

                                                    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post

                                                    But think of what this represents: Conquering Fears-made-manifest, keeping it subdued through enlightened alchemy, and laying claim to a portion of the collective human soul... to transport your narcotics without interference by customs.
                                                    I actually had something like this feature in a game a while back. There was a hollow under a tree that led to other places. The folks who first discovered it dug it out into a cellar and put it to the most logical use at hand: smuggling moonshine during Prohibition. Eventually the feds caught up with them and not really knowing what to do with the weird secret room that was nowhere the land ended up in the hands of Task Force Valkyrie. Operation Fort had some fun with it for a couple decades before their funding for scientifically important but organizationally unactionable research dried up in the 80's. Strapped for cash, they decided to start doing what half the other covert groups were doing and began financing their research by using the room to smuggle cocaine. This ended badly. One of their shipments failed to materialize (literally) along with its handlers and the resulting recriminations ended up exposing them. They never figured out what happened to it. Most people assumed somebody got greedy and rerouted it, but a few think it may have accidently gone somewhere... else.
