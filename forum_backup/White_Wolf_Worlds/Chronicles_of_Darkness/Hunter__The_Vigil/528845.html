          As fas as I understand it, the Children of the 7th Generation is the "race"- meaning, there are only 666 of them at any given time. The Lucifuge and the Lenfant Diabolique (from Inferno) are two big organizations which recruit those individuals- either to deny or embrace their infernal lineage.

          However, there is another way to look at it. From Compacts and Conspiracies, we can see that the higher is a Child's Status, the longer she should live- and with Lady Lucifuge being the head of the conspiracy, there is a good chance that she is immortal. There is no reason why that wouldn't also be true for the Lenfant, with the Chaldeans being the founders of their own organization. What it shows that there is a strong connection between a Child and an organization/ cult which is founded around her. Also, the Lady has been presented many times as the organization itself, which may have been metaphorical- but maybe it is literal..

          My point is this- both the Lenfant and the Lucifuge claim to have 666 potential members. Occam's Razor means that those are the same members- but perhaps the razor isn't that sharp anymore. Maybe there are 666 Children at any given time, but if one of them rises to greatness and establishing her own organization around her, she by herself spawns a lineage of 666, her own "Children of 7th Generation", creating an whole organization around herself.

          Also, there is another option. Unlike how the Lucfiuge were presented in Hunter, Inferno gave a very different feeling about this "666" thing. It was presented as something more of faith and less of accurate-

          [Quote=Inferno]
          "The story goes that when one dies, another of their heritage may be born, and when one is born, another must die- the whole lot of them numbers 666 if you believe their propaganda"
          [QUOTE]

          If that's really the case, it may also allow that both the Lucifuge and the Lenfant have together no more then around 666, but this time it is an intentional act. It is a myth some have faith in, and others wish to preserve. They don't need to have exactly 666 members- just something close enough. Sure, it may be based around some real fact, but it could still be just a story.

                              you know that the Lucifuge were described as indistinguishable from the Co7G at Hunter's main book, right? (it is their very subtitle in the main book!) Which is the same case with the Enfant. Childern of the 7th Generation is the "template". Lucifuge/Enfant are organizations. Also, do you understand that Inferno called it "propaganda" not from an objective point of view, but from how an outsider looks on those claims? Plus, not everything presented in the Hunter books is "objectively true", even when presented as a fact. Witch Finders is a good example for that.

                              Also, like I've said, you can read it in a way that each of the groups has 666 individuals in them- but in that case, why should the Enfant's claim be called as false and the Lucifuge's as true? Because they worship demons they must be lairs? Why can't their "propaganda" be also true? Why can't the Lucifuge's "truth" be false? What you have in the end are two groups each claims to have 666 members, and they really don't like each other- and few other individuals who have somehow escaped both groups. The only reason why the Enfant can't be called as "potential Lucifuge" is because it seems that somehow those who are never found by the Lucifuge ends up in their hands (as you can see in the main book).

                              The only difference between the claims are that Inferno presents their numbers as something which may be seen as "intentional", where in the Lucifuge seems to be more of "natural"- but doesn't it rises questions? Like, why both groups have the same number of members? And if that's the case with the Enfant, why shouldn't it be the case with the Lucifuge? If it is really an intentional act, a Lucifuge would ask himself, how can he be sure that it is not the same case in his organization? How can he know that the Lady doesn't murder her own people, or send them to suicide mission, in order to get new members which could be more "important" then others? Because she is nice? How much you know about her anyway?

                              If that's not the case, then why would the demon lords would support both groups and limit their numbers to 666? How does their demonic genes work that they are mystically aware that by joining an organization they would limit the total number of the group's members, and that they are triggered by the death of another of their kin? Is it destiny? The will of Satan? How the hell that all of that works? It feels like something is manipulating them- and maybe the Lady have the answers. Also, does it mean that if you'll manage to establish you own cult around you would that mechanism start working for you- giving birth to a number of Co7G which are meant to be drawn to you? So in a way, the Lucifuge have the potential to start thousands of demon cults, each numbering 666 members? And what would happen if there are 666 cults of 666 demons all around the world? Maybe the Gates of Hell would be opened? Again, there is only one person who knows the answers, and she isn't telling.

                              In short, the way you read the Lucifuge is harsh- which may be good for your games, but it kinda kills a lot of story ideas. Considering that this is WoD, if you think you are 100% sure of how things meant to work, you should think again. The Wicked Dead is a whole book filled with thimgs which were thought to be "false" and turned out to be true for Vampire, for example.
