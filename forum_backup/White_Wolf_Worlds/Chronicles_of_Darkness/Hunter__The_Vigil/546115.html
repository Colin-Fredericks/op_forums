    Hunter Kinship with Beasts

    06-08-2015, 08:10 PM
    So, upon reading the crossover potential for Hunter and Beast, it seems that the Beasts can form Kinship with those Hunters that have Endowments that come from their bloodline, like the Lucifuge and also they can do so with Slashers...
    Other Hunters can go on dream-quests and sacred hunts within the Primordial Dream to become kin to the Beasts too.

    So this thread is two-part, to talk about potential Kinships that some Hunters and Beasts could share (and the custom Nightmares formed once a 'Family Ties' Condition is enacted between a pair), also some discussion on how you would include Hunters delving into the Primordial Dream through a Beast's Primordial Pathway/Under the Bed mechanic or other method (such as messing with a graveyard's Avernian Gate when a cell are on patrol for monsters there perhaps). A Hunter who has the boost of dice that a Beast's Mother's Kiss can give and the possibly larger Resistance trait a Beast can offer the Hunter and +1 teamwork bonus through the 'Family Ties' Condition.
