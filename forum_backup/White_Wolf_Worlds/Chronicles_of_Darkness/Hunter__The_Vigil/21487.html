      This looks really good, is this open development style? Because I have a few comments.

      A group of hunters with Anthropophagy can share a single corpse, but must split the desired Dread Power dots between each other, the character with the highest Anthropophagy dots gets first pick.
      The underlined section here implies there's some kind of "dread power economy" when the rest of the paragraph makes it clear that it's a "pound of flesh economy". Changing it to"

      A group of hunters with Anthropophagy can share a single corpse, but the monster must have enough flesh to feed the entire group...

      would probably be clearer.
      The human stomach is not meant to process that much raw flesh. For every pound devoured in excess of her Stamina dots, she suffers one lethal damage from the engorgement.
      I'd use the toxins subsystem here rather than engorgement. Assuming an average of Stamina 2, the typical person would be spending three days recuperating from eating what amounts to the meat form 12 McDonalds quarter ponders. Now I admit that's a lot of food, but IMO it's not enough to spend three days with a serious injury. Toxins just make more sense.

      It’s worth noting that murdering and eating a person is an Integrity breaking point. Make the breaking point roll at a penalty equal to the hunter’s Anthropophagy dots.
      I'm assuming that this dosn't stop becoming a breaking point after the first time because of the murder is always a breaking point rule; but how would this work with The Code?

                                          It just seems weird that the rules might say serial murder out of pure naked prejudice is mechanically supported by The Code, but doing it for power is too far for the The Code to work.

                                          And you know, I kinda like the fact that The Code supports such objectively horrible actions with no lower limit. As Slasher shows, the risk of going too far is a great theme to mine. To me the moment your Code says "eating monsters to get their sweet sweet magic is totally ok!" you're already taken a big step down the slippery slope.
