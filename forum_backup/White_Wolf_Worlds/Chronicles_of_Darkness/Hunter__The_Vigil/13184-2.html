                        I think the sidebar would have been well-served by taking the approach the similar one did for Cheiron - presenting several options. For purposes of my games, I tend to rule TFV has a deliberate alliance with both Famous Gil (though they may be unaware of its true nature in that case) and the Invictus. They're not subverted, or even collaborating, per se, they just have other priorities and made shady deals with the "well behaved, pro-establishment" vampires for funding and additional support for national defense operations.

                        That is, the Invictus has a phone number it can call when a small, isolated rural town has been overrun by a draugr outbreak. And Task Force: Valkyrie has a number it can call when it needs someone at Guantanamo bay to be Dominated into telling the government what Al Qaeda is up to. There's an obscure line in the VtR corebook about vampires who work for human agencies; I feel like it works both ways. The Invictus finds TFV useful, and TFV finds the Invictus useful. That is, if anything, a little more horrifying than a fully subverted group would be, IMO.

                            They have a big budget, but they also maintain absolute secrecy. A total lack of transparency is not great for a budget of any size in the first place, and almost all of it gets diverted to R&D. In my game, TF:V basically functions as the Shadow IRS. It used to be a secondary function, but the need for funding quickly dominated the agenda.

                            So, you've got all of these vampires. The young ones are very likely to be true leeches, able to pull together largish sums of money/other resources if they need to, but those resources are usually not theirs. Not only that, but they have a misplaced sense of pride and bravado, believing that as vampires, they are the true lords of the night. But they aren't, the US government is. These vampires are(usually) fair game, provided that they meet the right criteria. These are the sorts of vampires that most of the low status members of Valkyrie are going to get to know.

                            But there are also the old guys. These are the ones with the real power and influence, and most of all, capital. Do you think that they are paying taxes on these considerably large fortunes? When TF:V is in town, you bet your ass they do. To an outsider, it would look very much like Valkyrie has been corrupted by vampires, and their dirty blood money, But as far as Valkyrie sees it, they are the ones who are compromising the vampires, and forcing them to fall in line with the law. The ones who rebel are dealt with quickly, using cutting edge military weapons and tactics.

                            Vampires in the know talk a pretty big game of corrupting the government, but they are privately terrified.
