    Covenant: Hearth of Hestia, The Hearthtenders

    12-16-2015, 05:20 PM
    Hearth of Hestia

    The Hearthtenders











    Looking at the rites and practices of the Hearthtenders one would be surprised to learn that they originated from the legalistic pacts and treaties of the Lord Stewards; but if one were to peer back through history they would find that the Hearthtenders split of from The Union shortly after that brief time when The Union was known as The Women's Institute. The early Hearthtenders took the occult training provided by the Stewards to the WI and used it to forge their own treaty with Hestia and their own identity as blessed devotees rather than occult business partners.


    Picture: A woman wearing greek robes holds a bowl full of fire in one hand and an scroll on the other hand. She has a baby resting in the crook of one elbow.

    May your hearth be warm and your home be safe.


    The Enemy

    The Hearth may not hunt monsters, but they do hold a Vigil. The realities of urban life in World War England made it nigh impossible for the Huntresses of the Women’s Institute to go out at night armed and hunting inhuman prey, and so the WI trained its members as castellans: To defended their homes with both mundane and occult tools.

    The Hearth of Hestia take this to its logical conclusion. They’re a society of Hunters who want to protect those closest to them from the monsters in the Shadows, but who found a way to do so without having to go into the darkness and fight superhumanly dangerous monsters.

    Straddling the line between a Vigil, a support group for people who cannot deny the truth of monsters, and a new age religion; the Hearthtenders fight back the darkness in their own unique way and the priestesses of a fire goddess keep their candle burning bright.

    Devotees

    You didn't like your mother always being away on business, but you understood it was necessary to pay for her kids' education and luxuries. Until the day she decided she didn't like being tied down and you saw what divorce did to your family. You swore never to become like her, and ever since you've dedicated yourself to home-making for your father and three older brothers. It's fulfilling work, and the Hearth provides you with a network of like-minded friends and mentors.

    Joining a cult with real divine patronage just to annoy your pushy education and career obsessed parents wasn't the smartest idea. You don't enjoy playing domestic, you'll probably leave, but after graduation so you don't have to admit you were faking it this whole time. Until then you're stuck cooking and cleaning for a house of slovenly students with a fake smile, and without faith you don't even get blessings for it.

    Your mother was a Hearthtender and you've always known you would be a Hearthtender as soon as you're old enough to start your own family. You never understood why other women wouldn't want to be. Who would rather spend time in an office than playing with their kids?

    Stereotypes

    The Office of the Lord Stewards: Their contracts leave little room for faith and their duties leave little time for family, I would not wish their lives on my enemies but I don’t dare underestimate them. They tend an entire nation like I tend my hearth, they gave my grandmother her powers, and I suspect they could take mine away if they had a reason.

    The Union: Our parent, our brothers and sisters. Don’t forget them or what they sacrifice, be their warmth, let the light of your hearth guide them home at the end of every night.

    The Long Night: They seek to conquer darkness, but their future holds only fire and brimstone.

    The Lucifuge: There are many kinds of fire and many kinds of family. Praise Hestia for teaching us which kind to seek.

    The Athenic Church: I’m a housewife, not an idiot.


    Status

    The Hearth is two things to its adherents. It is a source of supernatural protection and it’s a social gathering, assisting in either of these tasks can provide a Hearthtender with Status. Making friends or providing time and resources to run the festivals will let your hearth’s flame rise to the top.

    O: You’ve been trained as an initiate into the Hearthtenders, take the Classical Greek Theology specialty in Occult.
    OO: A home is not a home unless people feel safe within, and your fellow Hearthtenders have given you advice on making yours safe. Take a dot in Safe Place, in the likely event that you already have the Merit increase the rating by a Dot.
    OOO: By this point a Hearthtender has assuredly made plenty of connections and gains the Hobbyist Clique supporting the Domestic specialty in either Crafts or Politics.
    OOOO: The highest Status is reserved for those who give the most, the ones who run the festivals and holy days that power Hearthtending. Like all good priests, take the Inspiring Merit.
    OOOOO: At the apex of the Covenant the Hearthtender gains the five dot Psychokinesis Merit. Naturally they apply the Merit to fire. Hestia’s influence modifies the Merit for all Hearthtenders, they ignore the drawback, add Hearthtending to all Psychokinesis rolls within their own home and to all rolls to use fire in a productive/domestic sense (the two bonuses may stack). However they take a -2 penalty to use Psychokinesis for combat or destructive purposes which may be counteracted by their bonuses. The Heath's Psychokenetic fire counts as holy. Hearthtenders who are naturally Pyrokenetic without the benefit of Status also take the above modifications as soon as they earn a dot in Hearthtending.


    Firesides

    Even in modern times when some third generation Hearthtenders are coming of age most Hearthtenders are converts, and the way to convert someone your faith as to offer something that they want.

    The most crowded Fireside is by the Watch Fire, where people join because they want the power of Hearthtending, a way to keep their family and their home safe that doesn’t require combat, membership in a demanding Conspiracy or years of dangerous occult study.
    Free Merit Dot: Safe Place

    Around the Altar Fire are the Hearthtenders who find spiritual fulfilment in what they do. Unlike many faiths Hestia’s blessings are tangible (the Pyrokenisis more so than Hearthtending), for some Hearthtenders Hestia is the only proven divinity they’ve encountered and for others her’s is the most fitting theology. The Altar Fire dominates the higher Statuses.
    Free Speciality: Expression (Preaching)

    Others sit by the Communal Fire because their friends are there. Religion has always been an excuse for social bonding and the Hearthtenders are no exception.
    Free Specialty: Socialise (Hearth of Hestia)

    Hearthtending (O - OOO)

    Prerequisite: Safeplace

    By and large the Heartender’s do not hunt monsters. Like all Hunters they acknowledge the supernatural but like most in the World of Darkness they consider it best to leave it alone, mostly. Through Hearthtending they call down powerful protective blessings on their home and their household, then they leave the supernatural alone knowing that it’s much more likely to leave them alone.

    Hearthtending requires two things, the first is to participate in a full calendar of holy days, rituals and observances honouring Hestia. The occasional missed day or illness is no issue provided the Hearthtender participates for most of the holy days and the Covenant itself runs all the events. The second requirement is to tend to one’s home and household, the better you care for them the more dots of Hearthtending you qualify for.

    Once you have dots in Hearthtending you gain the following permanent bonuses:
    • No monster can enter or project supernatural power into your house (or houses) unless their Potency is greater than your dots in Hearthtending or they have an invitation. The protection extends to the edge of your property line.
    • All members of your Household add Hearthtending as a bonus to resist harmful supernatural effects, in the same manner that monsters use Potency.
    • All members of your family get Hearthtending armour against fire, natural or supernatural, and related dangers like smoke inhalation or boiling water.
    • Monsters take Hearthtending as a penalty to notice members of your household unless they are specifically looking for them. A thirsty vampire’s eyes will slide over you when scanning the crowd for a meal. Even if a monster can overcome the penalty, you only need to run faster than the slowest zebra.

    For the purposes of Hearthtending a Household is defined as everyone who lives in the same home as you at least a third of the days in a year (this is a rule of thumb, and should be interpreted loosely) and who is connected to you by no more than three degrees via blood, adoption, marriage, or some combination of the three. Links of marriage become inapplicable after a divorce.

    Drawback: Hestia provides her blessing to those who honour her with festivals and follow her teachings by tending to hearth and home. A charachter who fails at either will find their blessings withdrawn. If a Hearthtender neglects her domestic duties or chooses to peruse a new less domestic lifestyle she looses her dots in Hearthtending (refund the exp as normal) and must roll Stamina - Hearthtending, on a failure she takes the Sick Tilt for three days as her body goes through withdrawal and adapts to regulating its temperature without divine aid. If a Hearthtender betrays or abuses her family she not only looses the blessing but takes Hearthtending dots of lethal damage, manifesting as spontaneous burns. Anyone with a dot of Hearthtending can instantly recognise the significance of those burns.

    Sidebar: Protagonist Focused

    As the Hearthtending Merit provides benefits for everyone related to the actual Hearthtender, it makes sense that the exp cost be paid by players benefiting from the Merit rather than be conveniently left to NPCs.

    However as they don’t have to do any of the work to keep the hearth tended, PCs buying Hearthtending without Status in the Hearth of Hestia must pay 2 exp per dot.

    Sidebar: Professional Training (Homemaker)

    The asset skills for a homemaker are Crafts and Empathy.

      All in all I'm quite satisfied with how the Hearth turned out. This came straight after Ride to Damascus who are, IMO, some of my best and so a tough act to follow. The concept doesn't have the depth of the Ride/Beast philosophical conflict, and Hearthtending lacks the raw punch of Talecrafting/Beastcrafting. But I think the Hearth are a fairly unique niche (the Ama San are probably the closest) and while it's a small niche they fill it well.

      The idea came when I was idly musing about WWII Hunters. The Union transforming into The Woman's Institute was a fairly obvious thing, since most of The Union's hunters and mortal recruitment pool would be shipped off to the front lines. Thinking a bit more about how the WI might hunt under the constraints of wartime England led to the "home is a castle" metaphor and I thought it was different enough from the union to be worth keeping around.

      There was no actual intent to make a contrast/compliment to the existing feminist Hunters of the Maiden's Blood Sisterhood or the Athenic Church (for one thing, the other female group - the Ama San - actually has a fair amount in common with the Hearthtenders), but once I thought about it the contrasts were obvious and I canonised them through the Stereotype of the Athenic Church. The Maiden's Blood Sisterhood would probably get on better if they weren't on different continents - as they're Tier 2, they're all round nicer than the Tier 3 fundamentalists of the Athenic Church - but the general contrast is the same. The Church/Sisterhood go out into the world and seize the day. The Hearthtenders focus on domestic stuff.

      I'm kind of disapointed that I can't legitimately refer to the group as "pyrokenetic housewives" because that would be an awesome sobriquet. But at the same time combat focused powers like pyrokenesis (despite the bonus cooking potential can be ignored, people have ovens) just wouldn't fit. I stuck it in as the ultimate status benefit for people who want that angle and because there had to be some fire related powers but ultimately this is a domestic group. It's not about burning vampires to a crisp with holy fire.
