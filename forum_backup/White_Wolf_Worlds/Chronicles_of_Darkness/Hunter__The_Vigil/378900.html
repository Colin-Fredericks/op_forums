    French/Parisian Hunter organisations

    01-23-2015, 08:03 AM
    So my next Werewolf chronicle is set in Paris. One of the pack is a Wolf-Blooded rather than a full werewolf, and our rough idea is that he's also a former Hunter that was part of a Conspiracy that's probably the French/European equivalent of Task Force: Valkyrie.

    Across the breadth of the Hunter: the Vigil materil, are there any existing Hunter organisations based out of France?

    I'm also interested in any Merits that popped up in any books that would be helpful for a Wolf-Blooded trying to keep up with a pack of werewolves. He's a 2e Wolf-Blooded, meaning that he has some supernatural capabilities (he's got no fewer than three Tells, for anyone who followed the Werewolf 2e dev blogs), starts with a pile of extra experience and is being built to fill certain niches that work well to complement the werewolves, but there's always room for some more interesting bits and pieces to help.

          Check my sig for Society de Hadies. A Parisian explorers society dedicated to exploring the catacombs and the Underworld beneath them.



          I also have a concept for a Mummy aligned Hunter Organisation called Château Europa; it's really European Union, but it's physically located in France and has a French history.

          Château Europa was formed after Napoleon's army discovered ancient Iremite relics, including the Rosetta stone, a key to translating ancient Iremite to more modern languages like Ancient Greek and Ancient Egyptian. With it was the secret to creating Name Relics. Napoleon founded Château Rosetta to study and use these Relics for the French Empire.

          Since it's formation the Château was destroyed by the French Revolution and then rebuilt during WWI, in time to serve as a key (non-violent, curses not guns) component of the French resistance. Post war it was re-purposed to as a politicians society dedicated to peace and the European Union.

          Thematically it serves as a foil to the Mummies and Irem. Mummies are old and unchanging, and the core belief of the Arisen is that nothing ever changes - society is still built upon the backs of slaves.

          The theme of Château Europa is that things do change. It started of as an organisation dedicated to conquest and Empire under Napoleon. It was then destroyed by an uprising of the common people, and became a symbol of passive resistance to tyranny during World War II. After the War to become a symbol of an "empire" created through diplomacy and old enemies working together instead of conquest.

          The thing I'm stuck on is their Endowment. All I know is that they should be Arisen like Relics, and be useful for politicians.

          However TF:V is very American so I can understand if you'd want something a bit more European. But I can't help you on that point.

                                              I remember working a few years ago on a French conspiracy called "Bureau des Circonstances Extraordinaires". Here is what I remember from what I wrote.

                                              The French equivalent to TFV is the "Bureau des Circonstances Extraordinaires". It is the successor of pre-WW2 "Institut des Etudes Paranormales", closed and pillaged by the nazis during the Occupation of France.

                                              The two organisations have few things in common. The IEP was as much a branch of the secret services as an research think tank regrouping the country finest occult investigators and academics. Its goals were less the control of the supernatural in France and its colonies than its study, both for the sake of pure knowledge and its exploitation for France's "grandeur" (Greastness). It was also one of pre-WW2 foremost Conspiracy, with branches all over the old French colonies, and an extensive central library, concerned not only with European mysteries, but those of Indochina and Africa too. It also had access to its own Endownment, a strange mix of occult and science processes (involving steam and electricity first, followed by early atomic sciences in the organisation's last years). Sadly, the emphasis on academics rather that fighting supernatural threat left it ill prepaired for the hunt that followed France's invasion. The most of Central Library's books and artifacts were taken and shipped to Germany, as well as most of the research personal who did not ran. Field agents were at the top of the Gestapo's hit list and decimated. The Japanese Forces did much the same to Asians branches in the territories they conquered. The remaining personal fled and hid, the more courageous of them joined the Resistance or their counterpart in Great Britain.

                                              The BCE was founded in the years following the Allies' victory against the Axis force. Its origins were an initiative of a core group mixing former IEP savants and field operative, as well as résistants and military personal who faced the supernatural during the war. The latter group brought a unique perspective to the new organisation watching for mystical threats to French interests. You see, the French Resistance regrouped many kind of men and women together: communists, capitalists, republicans, monarchists, christians, jews, atheists ... and para-human ENEs. The Résistants in this core group has not only fought the nazis supernatural arsenal, but they also had fought beside French mages, werewolves and even vampires against this horrible common foe. And they had kept in touch with their supernatural friends. Their help was a real boon, first when discretely passing the law establishing the BCE during the first chaotic years of the 4th French Republic, and then to provide a first class network of contacts that somehow compensated for the loss of their books, technologies and research personnal, which were taken from the Nazis by the American and Soviet Conspiracies (with no intend to give them back to the French). Getting back the files and artifacts who had been preserved in the few colonial branches left intact after the war never compensated for the loss.
                                              At first, the BCE worked alongside the young TFV (some would say under its supervision), but De Gaulle's rise to power in 1958 and the following separation of the French armed forces from NATO integrated commandment saw the cooperation between the two organisations greatly reduced. Even now, BCE agents rarely work as part of TFV operations.
                                              To sum things up, the BCE is a Compact whose main goal is to protect France and its citizens against supernatural threats while hiding as an unimportant division of the DGSI (Direction Générale de la Sécurité Intérieure). It operates mostly in France, but also in the former French colonies and as part of the Army operations. It has extensive datas about the supernatural in these areas (more than any other western supernatural state agencies), but it is starting to date in the case of former Indochina. Its strong points are of course the few relics, gadgets and documents that could be preserved from the days of the IEP, but first and foremost, it is its extensive network of ENE contacts and allies in metropolitan France, established by the resistants to begin with, and then maintained by their successors over the decades. Combined with the fact that French ENE organisations tend to be much more centralized that their American counterparts, it means that the BCE's awareness of their own supernatural environment is one of the best in the world (in this given Chronicle, only the British reached the same level of understanding).
