    Lets make a Chinese Conspiracy!

    10-15-2015, 07:55 AM
    China is big. China is powerful. China is a strong country, and is likely to be outside of VALKYRIE's reach. In sort, China deserves its own governmental conspiracy.

    Now, in order to make one I'll have to do some research, which is something I don't really have time to do since I return to the University in a few days and I already have a to-do list of around 20 hunter orgs, so for that reason I'm turning to the forums! From what I recollect from reading in similar threads, there are 3 main ideas- the canon one, which is based around drug use and constant paranoia, NineStars (the maker of Skin&Bones) idea for kidnapped sleepwalkers who are turned into something else and KingsRaven idea for a conspiracy which "hacks" into the God Machine in a way and manipulate occult matrices some way. Does anyone has thoughts about developing/mixing any of those ideas or new ideas for such a conspiracy?

          So there's Opium Warriors, Manchurian Candidates and hackers with what I'm guessing would be a Megaten bent.

          I could see the first two doing well, though the drug guys seem like a 'fallen' version of the Ascending Ones. I'm imagining a tong, specifically, maybe having become addicted to the 'black lotus,' which tends to be the go-to fictional drug for occultism, maybe turning them into a twisted version of the classic World of Darkness hunters, driven to the brink by maddening visions and compulsions. The hackers seem a bit much, to be honest I'd more likely to appreciate a sort of government agency that tries to make its own infrastructure. Sort of like a counterfeiters Utopia Now, with a lot more 'You don't know what you're dealing with!'

          I'm not sure if Ninestars' idea was more like Deviant or the Manchurian Candidate, but being a fan of the VII makes me think they could be cool as people who are brainwashed to respond to triggers, maybe instead of breaking points for seeing the supernatural, and then get spurred into action. Perhaps the compact or conspiracy itself could be made up of those who have shaken free of their programming, able to still retain their abilities but not walk around unknowing. If only because playing a completely normal and oblivious character who only gets to act during the fight is a bit of a letdown.

              Any mystical conspiracy dating from before the Communist regime would have had a very hard time making it through the cultural revolution. (It would be a good way of downgrading former conspiracies into compacts).

              On the other hand, the cultural revolution was a time the Chinese government cracked down hard on anything smacking of the religious and occult - some of which is backed up by real supernatural forces - so it marks a good time for a government monster hunting group to originate. On the other hand, it makes little sense for such a group to put together any sort of supernatural Endowment, implying something akin to Advanced Armoury would be more their gig. Or some form of superscience, and science includes social science, so maybe some sort of advanced brainwashing? Can get the truth out of people, make people believe them, free someone from a monster's mind-control, psychically drive actions, Manchurian a monster's retainer, that sort of thing. (Is this what KingsRaven had in mind for the hacks? I seem to recall something like that).
