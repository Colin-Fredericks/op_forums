    So, to add a bit to the Bureau des Circonstances Extraordinaires:

    The Enemy
    The BCE does not think of the Supernatural as a whole as an enemy. Its roots in the French Resistance give the Compact a more understanding position toward the ENEs in general, and the para-humans in particular (other agencies would deem this posture as too soft or even compromizing, but they would be wrong). The attitude of the BCE with the P and S type ENEs (look TFV in the corebook) is to carefully evaluate the subject, his deeds, motivations and acquaintances, in order to get a good idea of what kind of people he or she is. Then the high-ups of the Bureau will decide the appropriate reaction, based mostly on what the ENE does and a bit on what its motivations/long term goals are. What kind of supernatural being it is is almost never taken into account. The usual action will be to do nothing beside a light surveillance from time to time (unless a significant change occurs). This kind of behavior is the more common because 1) it lessens the risk of creating a threat were there is none to begin with, and 2) the agency's budget is tight, and his personal is for the most part overworked.
    In case of serious suspiscions of criminal activities, a more inquisitive surveillance program will be engaged, sometimes with the help of the organisation's ENE contacts and allies. This has led some of these mystical allies to supernatural evildoers who had previously eluded them (like in the case when BCE agents called a Pentacle mage contact to help tailing a suspected criminal mage, and the contact identified a Tremere Lich who had escaped them for 20 years). If the investigations leave the ENE clear of any criminal activivities, he will be left alone, unless some elements suggest he may be of use as a contact. In this case, the ENE will be approached with propositions for helping his country in exchange for a small retribution and the certainty he fullfilled his duty as a citizen. If it appears,at first or later, that money is not the motivation for the help, but duty, patriotism or other high feeling, the ENE will be called more often, or be hired as an external consultant or auxilary personal, with the possibility for the para-human to call on the BCE for some services.
    When the Bureau identifies criminal or dangerous acts from the target, its answer will depend of the gravity of the deeds. If they are considered minor and provable by non-supernatural means, the file will be transfered to the mundane police forces who will proceed normally. If the criminal acts are minor but provable only through supernatural means, the files will be transfered, by the high-ups, to their contacts in the relevant supernatural communities, who will then apprehend and sanction the criminal. Only when the target is evaluated a serious threat responsible of major crimes will the BCE bring the big guns. One or more teams from the Service Action will be mobilized, sometimes will supernatural support, in order to end the threat discreetly and permanently.
    One thing needs to be mentioned: the Bureau always takes these steps with para-human ENE. In cases they deal with extradimensional entities, their actions are very different: most of the time, they will quickly transmit the file to ENEs they think can handle the issue better than they can; if the agency intervene itself, the study of the case will take only as long as the research team think it relevant, then they will swiftly destroy or contain the problem.

    Hunters
    There was nothing left for a teenager orphaned by the butchers of Bosnia's War. You would have died yourself if not for the intervention of the NATO forces. Once you reached adulthood and had to leave the orphanage, you traveled to France and enlisted in the Foreign Legion, hoping to find a release for the rage boiling in you. Instead, you found clarity, focus and a chance to prevent others to see what you had. During a deployment in Ivory Coast, your platoon was assigned to help some diplomats guys to move some old crates to a ship, officially here as part of the evacuation. After surviving what freed itself from one of them during the shipment, you know there are monsters who are not human.
    You always liked when your GrandPa would go on and on about his deeds in the Resistance during Sunday's lunch, how it was when he meet GrandMa, and you were so proud to have two heros to look up to. When you saw your younger sister having her First Change, you were terrified for her, but your family was alright with that, and, once you had both calmed down, your grand-parents told the two of you a few more details about their story and the fight against the Nazis. Now, you and your sister have taken the fight, each in your own way, to make sure France remains safe.
    You were studying Cambodgian History and Anthropology at the Sorbonne. One day, while reading a text written by one of your predecessor from the 1930's, you stumbled upon some kind of coded message hidden in the syntax errors. You reported your discovery to your thesis director, he seemed amused. You cracked the code, discovering a set of coordinates in Cambodgia, he got you a job at a governement agency you never heard about before. Your first mission was to accompagny your mentor and a small team to the forgotten IEP safehouse you found. Nothing could have prepaired you for what you saw there.
    You are a freelance tailor, working on contract for Chanel, Yves Saint-Laurent and other great names of haute couture. The DGSI originally recruited you and gave you a complementary formation in electronics so you would be able to install hidden microphones inside the clothes of their agents and enemies of the nation. After you started doing repairs on housecall at the home of your client, you got a crash course in classic spying techniques, and you were good at it. When one of your more excentric customer saw you putting a bug in his new coat and attempted to kill you in a downright inhuman way, only the intervention of the BCE strike team saved you. You still do the same thing now, just for a different service.

        Services
        The BCE assigns its members into various services with roles depending of the agents' skills and origin . They can be part of different kinds of group: single service teams, multi-services teams (known as Task Forces, few of them are permanent, most being asembled to deal with a specific ENE or group of ENEs) or mixed hunter group (as an undercover or detached agent). The one who see the most field duty are part of these services:

        Service Repérages et Investigations (Free Specialty: Socialize (one kind of para-human ENE, mage, werewolf, vampire...)) is the one dealing with the "common" secret service work. Intel gathering, spotting supernatural phenomenons and beings. In case of the latters, they also are tasked with establishing the profiles who will determine the Compact's reaction as a whole and identifying the ENE's associates. Their last (and most vital) activity is to maintain the organization's contact and allies network among the ENE, especially among ENEs who are mid or low level in their respective conspiracies. The origins of these agents are the most diverse in the Compact, most of them coming from the French police and secret services (who already have a diverse recruitment to begin with), but also from any walk of life giving them usefull skills.
        Secret: Well, that's not really a secret inside the agency, but in some cases, relationships between résistants went a bit beyond friendship. And some old French families had members whose "uncommon" abilities were not spoken about in ordinary society. It is not said that some members of the Compact are only recruited for this reason, but it's not a disavantage either. Such members are not seen badly by their BCE colleagues, but other hunter groups might not like this fact and consider the organization compromized.

        Service Action (Free Specialty: Firearms (Hostage situations)) intervenes when a mystical source of danger has to be terminated. Almost all agents of the division come from the French military, some being soldiers who meet the supernatural and lived to report to their C.O, but most of them are transfers from elite army and police corps like the Commandos Marines, the Dragons Parachutistes, the R.A.I.D and the G.I.G.N. As a matter of fact, the engagement doctrine of this service is pretty much the same as the two latter regiments: first protect the civilians, then protect your own men, next try to catch the target(s) alive, the last part being often omitted, since the objective is almost always to kill the ENE target, but in case of need, the agents of this service are trained to do so. The priority of the operatives is always to protect the population, the training they receive reflect this. They also get the most frequent psych evaluation.
        Secret: Among the cells made solely of members of the Service Action (called the Intervention Units), the finest is the Commando Tanguy (named after Colonel Rol-Tanguy, who commanded the resistants during Paris' Liberation). Not only have its operatives the best training the Compact can give, but they also have the best, and strangest equipment available. For the BCE was able to preserve, and in some cases get back some remant of the Occult Science arsenal of the old IEP. These equipments hav a lot of drawbacks: there're old, rare and using principles now not fully understood if they ever were (which make them difficult to maintain in working order and impossible to reproduce in most cases). Not only that, but numerous organizations would like to get their hands on these. As such, those devices are never used outside of France or in presence of hunters of other groups. The only outsiders who saw them are the most trusted ENE allies of the BCE, and even them are not explained what they witnessed.

        Service Recherche et Soutien (Free Specialty: Academics (Research)) is what's left of the old IEP, founded by survivors of the Nazi hunt. Its role is mostly to study, organize and archive the data they receive from numerous sources (reports, occult books, what ENE contacts say...) so it can be used for the benefits of the personnal in the field and France's protection. Their archives, regrouped in the New Central Library with secured saves spread all over the country, are extremely varied, covering not only the occult side of the supernatural, but also the sociology of the ENE organizations and their evolution in History, mostly in France and in the former French colonial empire. The aim of regrouping all these informations is highly practical, being to reach a understanding as complete as possible of the various supernatural societies at home and abroad, so that the work other services create as few frictions and repercutions as it can. The SRS also has the task to find the old IEP safehouses hidden or lost during WW2 and get back their content, and while those located in France have been found a long time ago (as far as the BCE knows), a lot of the ones situated in the former French colonies are still undiscovered, just like their content, which are often dated but still includes usefull knowledge about both Asian and African ENE and hunter organizations. Given the interests of this service, it is no surprise that most of its member hail from various French Universities and Schools of higher education, although they have been known to recruit a few enlightened amateur, as well as some ingeneers, whose main task is to study the few Occult Science text and artifact left from the IEP.
        Secret: The members of this Service yearn for the IEP Golden Age, and do all they can in order to get back what they fell was wrongfully taken from their predecessors. As such, they push hard for expeditions to old IEP colonial safehouse, even when such action are not politically sound. But most problematic, a few of its members, mostly descendants of IEP researchers, are planing to get back the documents and items that were taken during WW2 by the nazis, even if they are now owned by various national Compact and Conspiracies, like the Russian Section 9... or the American TFV. The higher-ups of the Compact would never approve this, especially when it comes to their allies, but these people are not going to ask permission.

        Other services exist, who rarely send their agents into the field, but still need to be mentioned

        Service Administration (Free Specialty: Politics (Administrative Shortcuts)) is in charge of supervising the Compact and handling the ressources. Most of its members are low to mid ranked functionnaries in charge of the day to day of the Compact, and their lives is just as exciting as the one of the average office employe. As a matter of fact, a lot of the BCE paperwork could be handled by ordinary functionnaries with them being none the wiser to the true nature of their job, except that people from this service are taught a few tricks to cut the delays of the notoriously slow French administration. The other agents of this services, on the other hand, are the ones who give their orders to the field agents, negociate with the higher levels of the ENES' hierarchies decide the response to ENE activities based on the reports from Repérage et Investigations and Recherche et Soutien and send the execution and capture assignments to Action. These persons, all high rank agents (Status 4 to 5) transfered from the other services with years of field duty behind them, belong to the 7 numbered Executive Commities (1 - Paris and its suburb, 2 - North-West, 3 - North-East, 4 - South-East, 5 - South-West, 6 - Overseas Collectivities, 7 - Foreign Operations), which are themselves supervized by the General Executive Commitee, made of the BCE top-agents (Status 5+) and headed by the Director of BCE. Technically, the Director himself is under the authority of the President of France, but unless a serious crisis occurs, the two persons only see each other for the presentation of the BCE yearly report. A more regular supervision is done by a small unofficial group of 5 councilors (1 from the French Presidency and 1 from the Ministries of Interior, Defense, Foreign Affairs and Justice), each of them a former distinguinshed BCE agent.
        Secret: The current reforms of French institutions and its hunting of money wastes, while being quite slow, is making financing the Compact more difficult with every passing year. Previously, 80% of it went through the budgets of the presidency, the parliament, the senate and the army and secret services secret funds, with the remaining 20% coming through local institutions and municipalities budget. The spreading among multiple budgets had hidden the fluxes well enough since the BCE founding. But the growing power of control of the Court des Comptes (Court of Accounts) during the last decades has made concealing the funding very difficult. Already, local institutions and municipalities budget had been considered unsafe, as well as the presidency, too closely watched. And now, there is a growing movement asking for a tighter control of the two assemblies' purses, kept at bay now thank to the principle of separation of powers, but how long will it last ?

        Le Placard (the Cupboard) (Free Specialty: Subterfuge (Looking Busy)) reflects an annoying problem. One particular thing about the French Administration is that, once you're in, it is nearly impossible to fire you, except in case of major screw-up or criminal behavior, unless you resign. Sadly, while the BCE recruitment system is one of the best, it is not fool proof. For these useless and/or incompetent persons, the Compact created the Special Studies Sections, known collectively as Le Placard by anyone aware of their true purpose. Under that official pompous title are regrouped a number of assignement, spread all over the organization structure, who have one thing in common: they are useless, purposeless powerless dead end jobs, where, to quote a previous Director, "you receive useless datas from three differents offices, write a useless report, then you send it to three others offices where they will be never read, maybe burned for heat during winter". Agents assigned to this service all have Status 2 salary, but Status 1 influence, no hope of rising in rank, unless they take initiative and prove their worth (which almost never happen, since they are the least likely agents to be sent into the field). three quarters of them quickly realize their situation and resign after a few years (the intended goal) or make a mistake and get fired, but a few (often the least ambitious) accomodate themselves to this situation, and stick to their job until retirement. After all, the only requirements are to be present during working hours, write a report of ten pages per week and look moderately busy the rest of the time.
        Secret: No BCE agent would be recruited solely for his contacts among the ENEs, right ? Wrong. Sometimes, a very important and powerful para-human ENE needs a good, preferably safe job for a close relative in exchange for his help. If the relative has at least a modicum of skills, he will be assigned in a minor isolated job in a BCE Service, with a better paycheck than the one of his colleagues. But for those who are really good-for-nothing (or worse, dangerously incompetents), the Compact, has an answer: the Protocolar Interservice Supervision Group (unofficial name: les Inspecteurs des Placards - the Cupboard Inspectors). The Groups "offices" are located between the Madeleine Church and Boulevard Hausmann. The dozen men and women who comprize it are technically assigned the same kind of job as the usual Placard agent, but are not sanctionned if they don't do it, or even if they are not present during working hour (still it is prefered if they are, it makes the task of the guards in charge of their security easier). They are all Status 1, but get the salary of Status 4 to 5 agents, with other advantages, like the delivery of one newspaper of their choice at the end of the working day, the possibility to customize their office as they see fit, possibility to invite "acquaintances" at their office, lunches at good restaurants (but not 3 stars, there are limits)... They also receive good protection, by guards from the Service Action (usually they are veterans who have proven their valor but are worn by their years of service and given this relatively easy job to wait until retirement - though they are still fighting fit). Among others, this group includes: the mortal noble descendant of one of France eldest vampire (who divides his time between watching TV shows and call girls), the nephew of the major werewolf pack leader of Poitou-Charente (who turned his office into his private sport room), the cousin of a important mage of Paris' consilium (full of good will but terminally incompetent), one of the few true seer (codename Neostradamus, he is almost never at his office, prefering to visit Paris' Museum and socialize in clubs), among others...

            Status
            Status in the Bureau des Circonstances Extraordinaires mostly results from a combinaison of efficiency, time spent as part of the Compact and networking (both inside the organization and among the ENEs deemed useful), which sometime results in agents getting assignement that are beyond their skills. Also, unlike mainstream French administration, due to tight budget and lack of personnal, BCE looks favorably on operatives showing initiative, adaptability and creativity, especially if it results in less money spent and less colateral damages.
            Status 1: You passed the security screening and are now ready to deal with the extraordinary side of French society. You are overworked, operating on potentially dangerous matters and your salary rank has not improved much, but now you get free psychanalysis in order to keep you sane, introductions to some discrete circles and a few courses that taught you what make the people in some of these groups tick (more or less the same as normal humans). You gain a free specialty in one of kind of para-human ENE, which you can apply to Empathy, Persuasion or Intimidation (your choice).
            Status 3: You progressed in the Compact, both getting acquainted to the network built by your predecessors and adding to it. This opens you to worlds of informations, represented by two dots in Contacts, each one linked to a specific kind of ENE organization or a hunter Compact/Conspiracy (except the BCE obviously). Still, be carefull, you can have close relationships with some of these persons, but never forget where must lie your loyalty.
            Status 5: You are one of the BCE's finest. Whether you still work in the field, or stay behind your desk most of the day, you have shown that France's idea of supernatural defense works, with some of the highest potentates, supernatural or not, on your phone list and ready to help you ensure your country's safety. This is represented by three dots of Allies, each an important figure in a supernatural community or French administration.

            Stereotypes
            Ashwood Abbey: I lost a few contacts to these idiots, some of them I called friends. It seems that the rich diletantes fancy themselve the heirs of last century safari hunter, with Paris as a new Congo, not realizing that their quarries are human beings 8 times out of 10. Sadly, they are well connected, which is sometime usefull, but also prevents us to treat them with all the severity we deem necessary. So, if one of them goes beyond what you can stand, try to make it look like an accident.
            The Loyalists of Thule: Okay, I know these guys. Sure, they offered you help in that case in Reims, but never forget that their predescessors also helped the nazis bring the end of the IEP, destroying almost everything we had before WW2. They say they have changed, just made a mistake. Well for me, such a hell of a mistake can hardly be random. Always double check the intelligence they give, never allow them access to ours and make sure they do not get to IEP's safehouses before we do.
            Ascending Ones: The safehouses in our former colonies contained a wealth of information about this ancient order of warrior-alchemists, the oldest of them dating as far back as Pharaonic Egypth. Being able to last millenia protecting mankind is worthy of respect, as is their surprisingly open attitude toward the mystically gifted. Still, I can't help but worry when I see them entrust their potions to common hoodlums like the ones I meet in Marseilles last year.
            Task Force: Valkyrie: The Americans are our friends, have been since before they had Valkyrie to handle the more ... discrete side of the world. And as friends, we have no compulsion against telling them when we fell there are wrong. Remember what De Gaulle's attitude was: we are allies, not subordinates, and while they may tell us that we rely too much on our supernatural acquaintances, remind them that we don't have their budget or guns. We work alongside them, but it is by our own free will. And if they wish to operate within our borders, they'll have to do so with our clearance, and by our rules, respect goes both ways.

            Sidebar: IEP Occult Science Device request:
            The BCE was able to retrieve some of the wierd science items used by its predecessor. Sadly, what it got back is a mere fraction of the huge arsenal from before WW2. Moreover most of the diagrams and research which allowed the conception of these items were lost, barely allowing the Compact to maintain its legacy in working order. As a result, the high-ups does not have enough hardware to outfit all their agents and are reluctant to risk some of their Occult Science Devices into the field, outside of some special units like the Commado Tanguy or important missions whose success rely on a given object's use.
            Mechanically speaking, a BCE agent may request an Occult Science item only from Status 3 or above (representing a minimum trust from the Compact). The Agent's player rolls his character's Presence (or Manipulation) + Status (BCE), trying to reach a number of succes equal to the dot of the request device (multiplied by 2 in case of expandable items). The request, succesfull or not, may only be done once per story. The agent may only have one device lent to him at a given time, and must return the item (or what he has left of it) at the end of the story.
            Action: Extented; each roll represents one hour of calls and e-mailing to the section of the Service Recherche et Soutien in charge of safekeeping these rare treasures; the character may roll once, plus 1 for every social merit linked to the BCE (Contact, Allies, Mentor...), irrelevant of the dots in said merit.
            Roll Result:
            Dramatic Failure: The hunter somehow offends a person in charge or causes to believe the items will not be used appropriately (in case of an expendable device) or returned. Not only the request is not granted, but the character's request's rolls for the next 3 months will be with a -2 malus, unless he does something truly exceptional for the BCE.
            Failure: No progress is made toward obtainig the device. If the player does not get the necessary successes at his last roll, his request is considered unimportant and he does not get the item.
            Success: The BCE agent proceeds toward getting his request granted.
            Exceptional Success: In addition to extraordinary progress into obtaining the desired Occult Science Device, the character gets a few tips from the guys in charge of its maintenance, he gains a +1 bonus to the rolls involving the use of the device.
            Potential Modifiers: Agent's last mission success or failure (-2 to +2); agent has a gung-ho reputation (-1); agent recently brought back informations about the Occult Science Devices or the principles behind them (+1 to +3 depending of the info's relevance), agent brought back a relatively intact Device of a known model (+1, +2 for a 4 or 5 dots Device); agent brought back a previously unknown and relatively intact Device (+2, +3 if the requested Device is the one brought back by the agent).
