        128) It was a brutal battle, but you came out on top. There's one last thing you have to deal with - disposal. Your cell doesn't have the know-how to make sure this thing stays in the grave, but your contacts tell you of a generally amicable (insert supernatural/hunter of choice here) who has the resources you need. You just have to get to him before the next full moon.

        You'll have to avoid loyal cultists who want their master back, other hunters with their own plans for such an unusual corpse, attention from mortal authorities, nosy tourists, and everything else life decides to throw at you for the next couple of weeks.

        Road trip!

                                140) The ancient pagan religions were on the cusp of finding a way back into the supernal realms before they were driven to extinction by the Templar crusades. Whether the Hunters know about the realms is not important. What is important is that a cabal of time traveling mages hopes to ensure the crusades never happened... they plan to assassinate Jesus before Christianity is ever founded. Fortunately for the Hunters, the government has a time traveling machine to deal with threats such as these.                                         141) Modern pop culture has idolized the supernatural world. Stories that once invoked terror in your ancestors now spark admiration and curiosity among the youths of today. So when the monsters decide to announce their presence, this poses a major problem to hunter conspiracies world wide. Mages, Werewolves, Vampires and Changelings have all made their presence known and are disturbingly increasing their popularity with each passing week.

                                        How will the Hunters continue on now that the public knows their prey exists? Sure, there's going to be a chunk of the population who still fears and hates the monsters, but there is also a chunk who welcomes them with open arms. Many young and impressionable youth are lured to the supernatural by ambitions of power and freedom, or simply because they mistake the books and movies for the real deal. But then the real bomb shell hits, when the monsters announce on a global broadcast that the government has known about their existence for decades, and chose to keep it quiet from the people...

So while I have a lot of supernatual stuff in the open ideas, I'm not sure how useful they would be to you guys because when I stripped the Wod stuff out of the chronicle turned novel and made my own setting for it, I also added a bunch of my own stuff and stuff from folklore that doesn't fit with the Wod as written.

                                            That being said

                                            If you really wanted to make this bad for the hunters, make a anti-hunter compact based off a group like the southern poverty law center or the anti defamation league. a bunch of civil rights lawyers that hunt monster hunters through the legal system.

                                            maybe have those lawyers represent a monster a PC hunter maimed at one point (or a coterie/pack/cabal/whatever mate of a monster the PC killed).

                                            maybe have a hunter PC's kid find out what their parent has been up to from our civil rights for monsters group's website and react with horror and anger due to her having a cute monster boyfriend.

                                            better yet, have the PC's kid decide she wants to BE a monster, even if it means having to testify against and/or kill their parent to get the other monsters to trust them. ( these last two ideas come from my novel's version of the van helsing family, who've degenerated into a massive collection of westboro style fail)

                                            meanwhile, the hunter PC's other kid gets beaten up at school by a pack of teenage werewolves who then use the PC's identity as a hunter to claim self defense in court... and it works

                                                            (142) The Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City is unveiling a new exhibit called "Secrets of the Templars" in cooperation with the Vatican.

                                                            The exhibit has on display several Templar artifacts, including a strange typewriter-like device that dates back to the First Crusade, which was recently discovered in a sealed vault beneath the Vatican Library.


                                                            Just as the doors are about to open, a group of seven men on horseback dressed as Templar knights ride out of Central Park, and begin walking their horses up the steps of the museum right in front of the gathered crowds of media and spectators. At first people are cheering, thinking it is a publicity stunt by the museum and Vatican. But then when a security guard tries to stop the men from riding their horses through the front doors of the museum, the lead rider draws a sword and slashes him down, while the other six riders produce micro-uzis from beneath their cloaks and open fire, gunning down security guards, police, and spectators alike

                                                            They crash through the doors, and he lead rider dismounts and smashes open the display case, lifting the typewriter like device above his head and reciting an ominous-sounding Latin chant.

                                                            He remounts, and the riders (some of them with human shields pulled from the crowd) gallop back out the doors and down the steps spraying bullets as they go, before vanishing back into the shadows of Central Park.

                                                            (based on a book called Knights of the Red and White)
