                                          Okay, so looking through the powers now. First of all, awesome description of like the scars and stuff from changing their powers around, that's super cool.

                                          Are high Status Rampant effectively immortal, like the Lady of Milan or Chevalier Theleme?

                                          Dorei-sei, I think the mechanics are a little iffy, if not just the idea that you need to get successes equal to a certain number to beat it. That's sort of lame with how everything else has mostly stuck to the success = regular success stuff. Maybe the dots in Seikotusen count as a penalty to the roll? I dunno, but I'd prefer not to do that mechanic. It's fiddly, I guess?

                                          Hannya is cool, seems fine to me.

                                          Kaibutsu is pretty cool, and I think it's interesting that higher Integrity makes it easier to use, not sure if I like that. Seeing as its an extended action, multiple successes makes sense to me, though it also might make it less cool if it takes awhile to activate it. Just my 2 cents, people like their transformation powers quick & cool.

                                          Kenshi is awesome. The Rampant are scary effective banishers of Ephemeral Beings, it seems. Awesome.

                                          Kigo is a cool and interesting power. It reminds me of a power Sanctified could use in 1e Theban Sorcery, neat.

                                          Shinigami Buki is pretty devastating. You could see why other Hunters might be horrified if they do that willy nilly. Making them Thralls? Damn, that's mean.
                                          Shonetsu Jigoku is pure cool.

                                          I think I'd convert these for nu-Castigation if I played a Hunter game where Lucifuge were in.
