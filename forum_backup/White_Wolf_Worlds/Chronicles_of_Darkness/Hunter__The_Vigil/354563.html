    Help with a Fan-Made Hunter Conspiracy

    12-30-2014, 04:30 AM
    I've been playing around with the idea of a sort of Bounty Hunter Conspiracy. When I say "Bounty Hunter" I mean more in the Wild West meaning of the phrase than the modern one.

    It consists of a group of individuals who travel the Americas and sell their monster-hunting services to whoever needs it bad enough. They charge a shit-ton of money for their services, but they do great work whether it be dispatching a monster, or simply catching it and delivering it to the client in question.

    Part of their success has to do with their endowment, which consists of a specially made handgun which shoots custom made supernatural cartridges with varying effects to capture, incapacitate, or destroy their targets.

    The other part of their success has to do with their willingness to employ or befriend other supernaturals as accomplices, moles, and informants.

    Does this sound appealing as a hunter group?

      It sounds like a valid group idea, I'm not sure if I'd make it a conspiracy though.

      Armory reloaded has rules for making weapons with supernatural elements to it like a rune stone or the ashes of a seventh son of a seventh son, so I'd have to see what you were thinking of endowment wise, they might work as a well armed compact instead.

      For example, something anyone with Occult 4 and the technical skills could make would be this gun, inspired by something from GURPS:

      The Scarmaker. Legend says a man found a mine with this unusual red gemstone deposit in it. He cut himself accidentally and was surprised to see that the blood that had fallen on the gem crystalized.

      Testing out the blood crystal's strength, he was surprised when it set fire to his metal workbench.

      He built a most unusual gun, it has no ammo magazine, instead a needle shoots into your hand, taking one bashing point of damage from minor blood loss for each shot. In return, those hit by the blood crystal bullets are set on fire: flesh, wood, water, stone, steel, to the fires of blood it is all flammable.

                      I had a similar idea long ago. The way I see it your group could be considered the mercenary version of taskforce valkyrie. However as StSword said taking into account the wild west feeling you want to give them I would keep them as a compact. I envision them as an old and secretive society of bounty hunters who got lucky and formed by chance. Basically a bounty hunter was hired by a mage to take care of a supernatural critter, the mage gave him some magic bullets to take care of it. The bullets where so effective that the bounty hunter decided to kill the mage and keep his arcane knowledge to himself. He called some friends and used his new found occult knowledge to forge special weapons and bullets for his comrades. They started hunting supernatural creatures out of greed, to gather ingredients, knowledge and artifacts for more power. However as they began to get known people started to come to them to kill or catch other supernatural creatures. Seeing that they were already hunting them anyway getting paid for that sounded really good and that is how they began. What do you think?

                                              How about The O.K. Riders?

                                              I've been thinking about this, and part of me feels like a good historical precedent to this group would be Wyatt Earp (and his semi-miraculous survival in a number of gunfights) and the rest of his posse.

                                              Say, the reason Wyatt Earp survived the gunfights was because a sympathetic changeling saw fit to grant him enough luck to to go against overwhelming odds. Later in life, the changeling came and asked Wyatt to slay his former Fae master who was hunting him…in exchange for that deed, the changeling would ensure his story was publicized for future generations (because before his story was publicized public opinion of Wyatt and his group had been negative).

                                              This would give my group a historical rooting, and would relate how a group could become aware of the supernatural world and the goblin market...
