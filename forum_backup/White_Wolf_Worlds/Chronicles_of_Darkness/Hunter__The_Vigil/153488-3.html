                    75: (Task Force: VALKYRIE, VASCU) Oklahoma City? Guess what, there was a John Doe II. He was one of yours, and he went off the res in a bad way. Contacts your organization had kept the truth about Doe II buried, but recently the grapevine has picked up chatter about a man matching his description planning something big in one of America's worst snake pits; Elohim City. Practically every white supremacist group in the country sends their shiniest shit to Elohim for training, and your man is here. Records on why he went of res are still sealed, and no one who knew him is able to speak about it. What was Doe II's role in the bombing, and more importantly, why do it? Because whatever excuse he might give, there's 168 empty chairs that want answers.

                    76: (Null Mysteriis) Something isn't sitting right with your latest research. Bluntly, it's going too well. Every test gives you the same results, every lab runs back the same numbers, and even your interns are chipper and unnervingly helpful. You know you were researching how witches were able to affect people's very probability on a whim when the lab was empty, is that what's happening? Is a witch trying to drive you insane by giving you the world's best luck for a scientist? How much longer can you live a perfect life?

                            75 is related to the Oklahoma City bombing in 1993.

                            77: Your group of storm chasers/hunters has a rivalry with a pack of wolves in your area. You try and capture the storms on tape, they try and appease the storms by throwing innocent people into the funnel cloud. You're sure they're actually causing the tornadoes somehow, but to convince the other hunters in the area, you need to get them on tape.

                            78: Nearly every Civil War battlefield is haunted. Don't look surprised, it was a modern war with classical tactics, of course there'd be a lot of angry dead. Everyone focuses on Gettysburg or Lincoln's ghost, but there's now a very disturbing story coming out of the South, near Vicksburg. Somehow, a Neo-Confederate group has found a way to communicate with and rally the unquiet dead, and in Vicksburg that means a lot of rebel soldiers that died half-starved and near-crazed against the Union. You've seen it before with ghosts, but never on this scale. There's a lot of militia movements in the US, and if they found out that someone's managed to get in contact with honest Confederate soldiers, all bets are off. Nip it in the bud, and keep the South from rising from it's graves.
