    To me, Hunter is the original crossover game. Much like Beasts, the game is meant to take all the game lines, and use them as the primary source of plot hooks, except as antagonists instead of friends. Simply put, the point of Hunter is to have a game that focuses on humanity fighting back against the night and everything in it. The game has been called Mortal 2.0, and so it is. That is the appeal - being Buffy and the Scooby gang against the monsters.

    I also find the idea that we need to have watered down versions of the supes to be a bit annoying. The monsters have weaknesses for a reason, after all. While hunters are underdogs, they should make up for it with being clever and using evry advantage they can milk.

        Well I'm somewhat disappointed that the Hunter chronicle is going to be so antagonist focus - I was hoping for a more open and nuanced appreciation of the setting, its compacts and conspiracies, and the dark mirror of supernatural society that envelopes the Vigil - much like the up coming Mage chronicle.

        Now don't get me wrong, I like slashers as antagonists, but they're only one part of the supernatural landscape and they're as easily portable into a mortals or a mage or vampire chronicle as they are part of Hunter. OK, so they first appeared in Hunter, but so what?

        The most serious objection I have is that they've simply been DONE already. They have an entire book devoted to them, done and finished. And it was a pretty damn good book too. Plenty of good writing, art work, new compacts/conspiracies, archetypal monsters, etc. So, I've already bought that book; do I really need to have to buy a new (reduced) version, again? One that takes up valuable word count (along with another copy of the core rules). Hence, I think we need a slasher chronicle.

        As regards the original Hunter list of antagonists, there are clearly other groups that are woefully under finished, specifically demons and cults. Neither of these had their own books (like in the format of Slasher) and to my mind they are both potentially fecund sources of new material - not to mention the fact that they are laced with plenty of crossover potential (basically almost every supernatural splat has some type of mortal cult nowadays). So yeah, demons and cults, go for it, Onyx, take a chance on something that hasn't been done. We need a cult/demon chronicle, we don't need a slasher chronicle.

        My next objection would be that, to my mind, it's a bit lazy to go for Slashers. The book is done, and I'm worried that they're simply going to be copy over and update slightly the stuff that's already done in 'Slasher'. We might get some new sample antagonists, like the Indigam in Werewolf 2nd ed. but do we really need them? We don't need a Slasher chronicle.

        My next objection is probably toothless but I think its fair. I think slashers have been chosen because they're simply in fashion again at the moment. By this I mean to reference the popularity of Hannibal and True Detective (esp the latter) since these seem to fit the 'average Joe hunter verses almost supernatural' murderers mold. This is all well and good, and it provides some welcome inspiration for modern horror rpg fans, but I'm not sure it's a good reason to base a chronicle on slashers.

        My last objection turns upon the fact that slashers are far to idiosyncratic to base a chronicle on them. The Strix, the Indigam, the Huntsmen, et al, are all bound together by a common metaphysical rubric that is fundamentally connected with their protagonists, while slashers, for the most part, are not. Slashers make their way in the world of darkness without reference to any other entity; they are defined by their need to kill (and perhaps be killed in the end). The link between slashers and hunters is thin and tenuous. Plus I don't like the concept that 'most slashers were hunters who went a bit nuts' idea. Really, does that have to be the case? Does it even make sense? Certainly the archetypal slashers in film and fiction don't seem to suggest that link (Fred Kreuger or Jason as members of a cell? Nah).

        If they want to make slashers work for a chronicle then they're going to have to do some pretty heavy re-engineering so as to make them more than just hunters who start butchering people for crazy reasons (and maybe get ickle supernatural powers). I believe cults and demons would make a far better 'dark mirror' chronicle for hunter than slashers (especially cults).

                                Several objections to the objections:

                                First, Slashers are a dark mirror to hunters, it's called out even in the book. Hunters are by nature killers by necessity (at the very least); even the Loyalists of Thule all know how to operate a gun. A Slasher is what happens when they stop caring about the necessity and start killing because their ideology tells them to, or simply because it's the only thing they're good at anymore. All the rage and pain that creates a hunter, but none of the ethics or focus. Even their abilities reflect this-they don't so much gain preternatural skill as an assassin so much as the fact that they no longer have moral limits, which also turns off their mortal limitations. Not every Slasher was once a Hunter, but enough of them were to remind the Vigil that the candle flame can become an all-consuming wildfire if they forget why they lit it.

                                Second: cults already have a lot of focus in the nWoD 2E in the core, there's even a merit signifying membership in one with supernatural mojo. Making them the focus of the Chronicle would retread the book you buy to have a context for Hunter 2E to begin with. On the other hand, Hunter demons are, quite simply, boring. Not because you can't get stories from them, but because they are made for a very specific genre of stories; temptation. All well and good for anyone not a demon, but demons themselves, simply by occupying that niche? Can't really have a character themselves anymore, just an echo of a particular type of temptation (even Mr. White; all he's good at is murder investigations and asking the question if someone is willing to commit murder to solve a murder-the only thing sympathetic on a human level is his confusion as to whether his human identity was his original one, and it's pointed out it really doesn't matter that much to him). A Slasher also has a defining action, but killing and managing to get away with it contains within it the seeds for many different genres; victimization, action, investigation, politics (a clever and manipulative killer disguising their hunger for any death as death of a hated minority-which means manipulating their supporters out of the way). That, and there's a reason serial killers end up the villains so often-how a seemingly normal individual loses all aversion to death and violence is fascinating, and probably unique to each person.

                                Third, the book is there. It's also 1E, and linked to a system that no longer exists and its replacement has completely different context (a Slasher may have any Integrity-low Integrity means their drive for death is born from trauma, a high Integrity means they're so calloused they don't care any more). That, and Avengers need an update. And probably need rules for truly supernatural abilities too instead of just the two-or-four they're born with. And if "already been done" was honestly a problem, we wouldn't have 2E to begin with. Certainly not the Strix, whose B&S version is their third incarnation. And VASCU deserves to be in the core.

                                Fourth, if you really want cults and demons, there's other supplements to Hunter in the pipeline. Those two things probably deserve a book of their own, rather than Major Splat Under Hunter Rules books (although those books are pretty good, I'll admit).

                                                            The main problem with cults is that they usually are symptoms, not the cause. Cults happen because someone (or something) cause them to happen. When you add a new cult to your game, you usually need to think about everything that surrounds it- where they came from, what they search for, what motivates them. Usually, it ends up either with them crossing to another line (vampire/idigam/mummy/demon/abyssal/etc cults)or making them into a group of madmen, which is more or less Slasher with a twist. Cults are just everywhere- there is no single gameline which can claim them as their own "thing". Slashers, however, are something which is very "Huntery"- they belong to the Vigil world, and don't require much work to add them to any Chronicle. Just add water (or blood) and walla! an instant antagonist! Plus, I think that an antagonist chapter for Cults would devour more wordcount than a black hole, which could be used for better uses.
