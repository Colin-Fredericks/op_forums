    Ok, time to move to the next preview-

    *skipping general rules*

    Risking Willpower- the core Hunter feature :P

    So Risking Willpower is the purest form of despair- it always comes out of a place of weakness, when all hell breaks loose (literally) and the hunter is willing to sacrifice themselves in the name of the Vigil. If you are not on the edge, you can't reach into the depths of your soul to find that power reserve. I personally would have preferred if an hunter could also risk Willpower in other times of crises, but oh well.

    *skipping general rules*

    Teamwork and Tactics!

    So yeah, Teamwork is pretty standard.

    I must admit I really liked how the last fiction described hunters using a Tactic. It was really cool.

    So Tactics relay on the same source of power as Risking Willpower and the Code- drawing oneself into the edge, and gaining hope out of despair. Those chapters really emphasis that the existence of an hunter comes from being pushed into the corner, both mentally, physically and spiritually. I still can see other events that could push people to the edge to touch the same source of inner power, but I guess that pushing your mind daily against monsters is the best way to reach that state.

    The mechanics for Tactics look interesting. I think they really feel like how a Tactic should feel.

    Bind/Ward is, like, really cool.

    Yeah, Identification goes well with my thoughts about how hunters may mistake a normal person into a monster, and then hunt them as such.

    Yeah, Harvest is specially made for Cheiron hunters.. as well as Null Mysteriis.

    Freeing Mind is probably something used by the Habibti Maa.

    Overall, I think I really like how Tactics are being constructed in this edition. Not that different from 1e, IIRC, but still good.

    I must say I am a tad disappointed that hunters didn't got anything extra. I mean, Tactics, Risking Willpower and the Code were the same features of being an hunter in 1e, and the pre-Kickstarter description made me think that perhaps they are going to get some extra things to define themselves. Sure, those three are great, but I kinda hoped for something new?

    Anyway, Appendix review (which should be short, I think)

    Equipment is equipment.

    Wow, that's a lot of ways to skin a hellcat. Costume gear is cool.

    But your leg! Ah! It's caught in a bear trap!

    (ironically, the first example trap is a bear trap. Oh, Shia, Shia, Shia..)

    Tilts are tilts.

    Conditions are conditions.

    I must say that I miss those days where one of the appendices have actually detailed a supporting splat in details. Sure, last time we had those was in Geist, and Mummy stuffed it all into antagonists and such, and Hunter doesn't really have a support minor splat other than regular people, but still, I think it gave some deeper ground to the game.. oh well.

    Anyway, that's it! Next preview- Monsters, on the 26th! Stay tuned :P

                                        And here is our next preview- Mysterious Places!

                                        I must say that I am pretty excited towards this section- the blog post alone has inspired me to immediately write the Domus Sanctus as a compact that had to deal with tainted, hungry places. Monica has also asked us to give some extra attention to those chapters, for the represent new additions to Hunter, and as such I am going to do my best and give a deep look at those strange, mysterious places.

                                        So- let's start reading, shall we?

                                        Yeah, many times the true horror is not the monster itself as the place it lurks in. I mean, in the original Paranormal Activity movie, the demon is an unseen threat- it is the house which creeps you out. The best kind of horror, IMO, is the one in which you don't see the monster- you only see the place it calls home, and as such, the lair and the monster becomes one- the lair serves as the "face" of evil. The world is tainted, and there is nothing you can do about it other than burning it with fire.

                                        Yeah, so Nests are the standard "monster lairs", Tainted place are where evil seeps into the ground and bad things just happen, and Sentient places are when the place itself is the monster. Among the tree, I think that Tainted are the most terrifying- those are places where the world is just wrong, points in the world where the natural fabric collapse upon itself, and the darkness fills the holes. There is no secret agenda, not monster you can banish, no meaning in the bad things which happen there. People die and suffer and break and turn into monsters by themselves not because the place wants to ruin their lives, but because that's just how those places work. THAT is the natural order. This world is fucked up, and you are just another causality in the natural darkness of the world. It is like the difference between fearing a pack of hungry wolves and fearing a natural cataclysm. The first will eat you, but you can fight against it, or at least develop tools to protect yourself from it- but when the very world turns against you, when the earth crumbles beneath your feet and the sky spit lightning and hail, there isn't much you can do but covering your head and pray.

                                        Yeah, Bygones sounds really creepy. Like, evil places in miniature.

                                        I wonder how the AKD deals with Bygones.

                                        The concept of sentient places making Bygones reminds me of that Wheel of Time damned city which produced a dagger which carried its evil beyond its borders.

                                        So the AKD deals probably with True Bygones, I assume.

                                        Yeah, it seems that True Bygones actually grant Endowments to their users. I wonder what it means for conspiracies.

                                        Ok, so let's sum this section up- we have temporary Bygones, which are usually temporary creations which force a Condition when used. Then we have semi permanent Bygones, which are somewhat sentient and give both a benefit and a curse in the form of a Condition or Tilt. Finally, we have true Bygones, which are outright legendary artifacts which grant Endowments to their users, but also hold great danger. In D&D terms, temporary Bygones are potions, scrolls and charged items, semi permanent are the common wondrous items and true are the outright artifacts and leftovers of the gods.

                                        Yeah, mysterious places and/or Bygones make a great Chronicle for a Tier 1 hunters.

                                                    So, let's start the experiment, shall we?

                                                    Because the book presents the compact creation rules as somewhat different from the conspiracy creation ones, I'm going to do a little update for both tiers- the first would be, of course, the compact, which was chosen to be the Keepers of the Source. The reason? I like those bastards. Yeah yeah, I know, Mage says that they are wrong. i say that Mage is wrong and should mind its own business. Now after clearing this part of the discussion, let's see what we have here-

                                                    Concept: the Keepers of the Source are hunters which are motivated by their love for Mother Earth, and which seeks to protect sacred places from being used by monsters.

                                                    Foundation: the Keepers of the Source use their dowsing sense in order to track down the places they swear to protect, as well as notice when a monster is abusing it.

                                                    History: the compact was founded in the 70s in San Francisco, by a woman who called herself Sunflower. Blessed with a psychic ability called "dowsing" which allowed her to track down ley lines, she gathered a group of faithful to celebrate the powers of Mother Earth. One of those faithful, however, was an actual witch, which used Sunflower's maps in order draw upon the magical Source. The act was felt as a mental backlash for Sunflower, who then tried to convince the witch to return the magic she have taken- yet the witch ignored her pleads. While another member who was also blessed with the dowsing sense, Redgrove, wanted to react with violence, Sunflower objected, leading the new Dowsing Society into a diplomatic effort to explain witches and monsters that Mother Earth should be protected, not used.

                                                    Things changed in 1975, when the Society came into conflict with a pack of werewolves over a sacred sight. With twelve members dead and Sunflower maimed, Redgrove took over the group, leading it into a more violent way of action using ambushes and terrorism. Eventually, at 1985, Redgrove changed the compact's name into the Keepers of the Source, and led it until 1996, when he was killed by a strange lightning strike. Since the 9/11, the Keepers' tactics became more subtle, working as a part of protest groups meant to protect Mother Earth from both mundane and supernatural threats.

                                                    Factions: the Keepers of the Source follow three main philosophies. The Children of Gaia are the followers of Sunflower's original ideals of pacifism and education, believing that monsters must be taught about the consequences of their actions instead of engaging unnecessary conflicts. The Hand of the Mother, however, favor Redgrove's more direct and active approach, as Mother Earth is in dire need and there is no time for monsters correcting their ways, and that they deserve no mercy for defiling the sacred places. Finally, the Dynasts follow Redgrove's daughter, Karen- she believes that the compact is meant to leave legacy after it, and that while outright pacifism would achieve nothing, extreme violence is also not the solution, and instead the Keepers should find the middle ground, and create a better future together.

                                                    Status: Among the Keepers, Status is earned by both protecting Mother Earth from being defiled as well as by finding new discoveries about the dowsing sense and the nature and location of ley lines and Sources.

                                                    0- You have taken part in protests and dealt mostly in the mundane side of protecting Mother Earth, and as such showed you worthy for the other side of the compact. You are taught some new tools meant to help you in your Vigil for the Mother. You gain one of the following specialties, of your choice- Weaponry (Improved Weapons), Expression (Protests) or Science (Environmentalism)

                                                    000- You have fought against both supernatural and mundane threats to Earth, showing your worth to your allies and friends. You gain two free dots in Allies (Keepers of the Source) for free.

                                                    00000- You have been accepted into the inner circles of the Dowser Society and were gifted with one of their greatest abilities- the dowsing sense. You gain the Unseen Senses (Source) merit for free.

                                                    So yeah, compacts are pretty easy to convert- I've had some thoughts about giving the compact Gut Feeling instead, but I think that considering the more psychic nature of dowsing it is better represented as Unseen Senses. It could also be that only a selected few develop dowsing (which is an Unseen Sense) while the most simply learn to notice the ebbs in ley lines through investigation (as Guts Feeling), but both can work.

                                                    Now for some real challenge.. we are going to update a conspiracy. That is, Les Mysteres.

                                                    Concept: we wish to create an organization of shamans and spiritualists which try to bridge between spirit and flesh.

                                                    Hook: we want to maintain a many faced and syncretic structure for Les Mysteres, where individual cells develop their own tools and views for how to commune with the spirit world through their own religious lenses, all united by their will to maintain harmony between worlds.

                                                    History: shamans, mediums and spirit whisperers have always been around, standing on the edges of society as they serve as a bridge between the mundane and the mysteries. Deemed as witches and heretics by some, those shamans worked to serve as a channel between humanity and their gods, delivering the words of the beyond while pleading on the behalf of the people with the divine. While many of those shamans walked alone, during the time of the Oyo Empire those disparate people formed an alliance together, forming a small conspiracy which guided the people of West Africa to the whispers of the Orisha, the sea and sky. Yet, as the Oyo Empire declined and fell into civil war, the conspiracy has shattered- and with the coming of the British colonialists and the transatlantic slave trade, those shattered remains sailed across the sea, until they reached the New World.

                                                    Forced to convert into Christianity, the shamans turned slaves have learned to give new names to the old gods. After all, whether in Africa or in the Americas, be them saints or orisha, the spirits were still there, and the people still needed someone to talk with them on their behalf. In an attempt to combine aspects of Christianity, the many West African faiths and local American religions, new religions were born, and old rituals were incorporated into new structures to fit the cacophony of traditions and practices. And as Haiti claimed independence and revolted against the French rule, those who know the Mysteries, Les Mysteres, were there to call upon the gods and guide the once slaves towards freedom.

                                                    Through their spirit allies, Les Mysteres has started to contact other remains of the old African religion in the New Worlds, as well as followers of other religions. Each land had its own spirits, and as such it was required to learn how to incorporate the local traditions in order to maintain the balance between the worlds. Native American shamanism, Pentecostals and many other priests and spirit talkers found a place among Les Mysteres as the young conspiracy grew across the world. With the Great awakenings of spiritualism, new and innovative practices were added to the Mysteries, eventually even adding things like aliens and advanced thought-forms into the list of faces the conspiracy used in order to commune with their gods.

                                                    However, that variety was also the doom of the conspiracy. While syncretism was the catalyst for Les Mysteres to form, between the constant manipulations of the spirits, the general lack of trust by other hunters for their witch like practices and the constant attacks by werewolves and shapeshifters which claimed them to violate the boundary between the worlds, the alliance between different cells was simply not enough, and the organization have slowly broke apart. Some local cells and compacts still claim heritage from the original conspiracy, but many have just returned to their old ways of taking care of their local community. While the conspiracy is considered to be defunct, the embers are still burning beneath the surface. In the Caribbean and South America, the spirit emissaries still name themselves a Les Mysteres, and they constantly work in order to rebuild what was lost. They grew too fast and collapsed because of it- and a more syncretic attitude must be taken if the conspiracy wishes to rise from the ashes. Thousand voices who sing together may be loud, yet without a single vision the spirits would steal their voices to make them their own and a cacophony would swallow the world. The Vigil must be lite again, leading the way for the Mysteries to sing a new song for the spirits.

                                                    Recruitment: Once Les Mysteres offered its protection and membership to anyone who knew how to speak with the spirits. However, after the fall of the conspiracy, they became far more selective with their members, choosing only those which are willing to combine their world views together. While many of the conspiracy's members belong to afro-american religions, they do not forbid shamans of other religions from joining them, as long as they are willing to accept that the core belief that the truth has many faces, and that no single spiritual tradition knows it all.

                                                    Factions: Les Mysteres is still rebuilding from the wrecks, but some of its old factions has managed to survive together.

                                                    The Children of the Loa are the collectors of ancient traditions and spiritual literature, working actively to rebuild whatever was lost to the conspiracy by studying from thousands of cultures.

                                                    The Society of the Vineyard works to bind the Mysteres to the community around them, working both to serve their local people and spirits and to cultivate connections with other hunters in an attempt to forge trust and alliances.

                                                    The Watchers in the Crossroads seek to unite the many shamans through their devotion to fight werewolves and others who may prey upon the emissaries for their attempts to bridge between flesh and spirit.

                                                    Status: Les Mysteres recognize those who work to maintain balance between the worlds, as well as those who work towards unity through diversity and the rediscovery of new or lost spiritual techniques. Balance, creativity and new innovations are what the Mysteres seek for in order to rise from their ruins.

                                                    0- You have talked with the spirits and decided to serve as an emissary to the Shadow. You gain Occult (spiritualism) specialty for free.

                                                    000- You have fought spiritual abominations while protecting sacred sites, and served both your community and your gods, and have learned how to resist the whispers of the Beyond to maintain your will. You may treat your Resolve as higher by one when resisting effects related to spiritual possession or other attempts to control your mind.

                                                    00000- You have rose to become a new promise for the future of the conspiracy, and a potential leader to guide it. The other hunters gather around you as a spiritual teacher, giving you the equivalent of a four dots Retainer.
