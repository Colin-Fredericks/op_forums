        Due to the nature of the internet, you don't actually need a global conspiracy to combat a group like Network Zero. Focusing on Vampires for the moment, the writeup for the Mission in Blood and Smoke describes the local Invictus as having seemingly declared war on the internet. They most likely use a combination of automated scripts and human monitors to keep an eye on certain types of web videos, and have agents in the offices of Youtube and Google tasked with making sure that major potential Masquerade breaches get taken down for "terms of service violations." If the videos wind up being hosted someplace that the Invictus can't shut them down, they can always resort to DDOS attacks or have their agents try to hack the server.

        Meanwhile, you've got Project: ADAMSKI making sure that Youtube is always flooded with obviously fake "proof vampires exist" videos, so that Masquerade-breaking content is unlikely to gain much traction before somebody notices them and takes them down.

        That said, Youtube hasn't proven itself to be entirely successful in combating copyright violations, and they'd probably have the same problem with Masquerade breaches if enough people were dedicated to keeping them up (which, thanks to NetZo, they are). Furthermore, there are probably NetZo cells specifically dedicated to exposing Vampiric corruption within the internet services that they rely on, making the Invictus' job harder. NetZo probably has its own hackers as well, working to attack the web infrastructure of groups like the Invictus and ADAMSKI. In other words, "war on the internet" is probably a fairly apt way to describe what's going on in the modern World of Darkness.

                I think it's worth keeping in mind that Network Zero is the type of group posting about conspiracies involving inhuman monsters, along with videos of said monsters and pictures and stuff like that, they're more liable to be relegated to the domain of conspiracy theories rather than being taken seriously by the public.

                Not to mention that's not how the Masquerade is contained. The only types of pages the Invictus would ever go after would be ones in which one of the Kindred are somehow doxxed, because the only information you actually need to keep out of the hands of kine is the specific identity of vampires.

                They can post as many blurry videos as they want, it's not gonna magically change the world and make people less skeptical or interested in survival.

                It's not a matter of Network Zero mysteriously having all their videos taken down or anything like that. They post them and mainstream humanity doesn't care. But maybe that video is enough to make them a big deal in the Compact (i.e. higher status as you get notoriety) or other people notice these videos you're putting up, and deduce that you've found the actual supernatural, and they take an interest in you. Maybe that ends poorly.

                I feel obliged to point out that they tend to release information to the public via the internet, and the other group that tried using the internet to coordinate, the Union, got massively screwed over my monsters finding their way on and misleading people.

                Network Zero seems like the kind of group that would run into those issues a lot, unless they keep a private corpus of footage and information somewhere too, maybe some kind of backup site only members can access. Then they occupy a weird space as a group discounted by the "public" insofar as they know about them for batshit conspiracy theories, but they're a potent resource for hunters.

                I'm not really seeing where there's loss of potential in all of that. You don't have to block what the characters do. It's like that sidebar in Precinct 13, you don't have to keep the cops from finding out "a vampire killed this person" because anybody who values their job is not gonna put that in their report. Their higher ups don't want it happening either, not because they're plants by the vampires, but because having a police officer saying that on record makes the whole department look bad.

                The Masquerade is porous and self-repairing, if you think the problem is that they're just one weird internet video away from the World of Darkness Revealed, then you're being way too optimistic about how people receive internet videos that posit conspiracies and other wacky ideas.

                                                    you know that one of the reasons that Network-0 doesn't break and "Masqueraded" (which is, actually, a Vampire term not used in the lexicon of any other gameline. Like the Lie. Just wanted to point it out) is that the compact turn upon itself. They have a whole faction which tries to hide information from the general public. They lack a single leadership, which means that the different factions usually don't work together. You don't need some evil mastermind to control the group in order to stop their efforts- they do it fine by themselves.

                                                    Also, you need to remember that we are talking about people on the internet. Do you believe any crappy video you see online? Heck, I even doubt things I read in National Geographic's website! Why? Because I know that things which show up on the internet could easily be misinterpreted. Last time I've checked, there are real vampires in New Orleans, Megalodon is eating whales, slenderman climbs sky scrappers and there are tongue eating crabs in our tuna. Any sane people would look at it and say "come on!". Now, if that was the World of Darkness, chances are that those things are at least half true- yet people would still won't believe not because some hot shot vampire is pulling the strings (which is true, and that makes things even more complicated to proof), but because people are not willing to believe in them. Mages call that inborn cynicism "the Lie" and blame the shard of the Abyss in the Sleeper Soul, because that's what mages do- they give big and shiny names to things they don't understand. Most people would just call it "human nature" or a "product of the Western Culture", because we all love to blame humanity, the Western Culture or both for everything which is bad in the world.

                                                    The point is, that the idea of a group who uploads real monster videos to the internet and fails to convince the world that they are right does not breaks my "suspension of disbelief". Between lack of organization, infighting, efforts of supernatural beings and groups (including other hunters) to stay hidden and pure human arrogance mixed with cynicism, the chances of such a group to actually proof anything are slim, but they still try. Why? Because they are hunters, and that's what hunters do- they fight against all chances, be it by gun, by prayer, by wand or by a click on the mouse. That's all.
