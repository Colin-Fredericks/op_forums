    The Favors Association

    06-25-2014, 07:55 PM
    That loan shark who has threatened your life? The jerk of a boss who keeps threatening to fire you if you don't work overtime? Even the ex who will never leave you alone? All can be disappeared. It doesn't have to be a person removed either as a favor. You could have all your debts paid, a brand new promotion waiting for you at work, and even freeing you from prison. All that's required is a single favor, and one well within your capabilities. No investigation will trace back to you, or any other repercussion. All you have to do is remember a favor. But, whatever you do, don't refuse the favor, unless you want to be disappeared to a place where... horrid things happen.

    This is the promise of the Favors Association. It has few permanent employees, for they are just responsible for making sure the arrangement stays in place, and over arching administrative tasks. Most of what the organization really does is carried out by those who owe the organization for a past favor(or favors for a few that take rather high risks.) Becoming a permanent member itself can be gained by certain customers as payment for prior favors, however that requires them to have skill in negotiation.

    How does this organization carry out these favors? Where do all the people disappeared go to? Well, much can be accomplished when one makes a deal with the Gentry.
    In return for sending a certain number of humans to Arcadia every month, they grant good fortune for the heads of the organization, along with magic necessary for many of the favors. However, if they ever miss their quota of humans, a human from the organization is taken to insure payment. Frequently, this will land on someone who failed to carry out a favor involving kidnapping someone, therefore causing the missed quota.

    Other favors the organization indulges in has been returning Changelings to their Keepers in Arcadia, and, recently, helping the God Machine find its wayward Demons in return for being protected from its schemes. Vampires and Werewolves its not above helping, in return for the favors such parties can carry out, or even Mages when the price is right. Rather than throw them to the True Fae, they may instead expose them to Hunter Groups, such as the Long Night or Task Force: VALKYRIE. Hunters themselves have been too granted favors, in return for eliminating particularly difficult customers. The only group they don't do deals with are Changelings, and that's only because their connection to Arcadia is known to some of them.

    Everything goes back to favors, which it now collects of thousands of per day. But where did they came from? Rumors abound, from a group of Privateers finding certain human useful for their purposes long ago, to the discovery of a magic mirror allowing communication to anyone they need. What matters now is that they are everywhere, from Moscow and London to New York City and Beijing. Anywhere there are desperate people willing to make a deal, they lie in wait, with their charismatic offers of favors.

    There are three loose departments within the organization, each responsible for a different aspect of the favors dealt in. Some permanent members keep watch over each, but at this point, they mostly just need to do basic administrative duties, for now the favors are self sustaining.

    Collectors: When someone skips out on their part of the deal, its time for you to make up the difference. Not that you really deliver the punishment yourself, rather you just insure another entity can nab the person, such as the True Fae or the God Machine. Sometimes, that just means finding them. Other times, you'll need to do something a little more to insure it.

    Dealers: You're the one responsible for insuring the ring of favors keeps growing. Manipulate someone however is necessary to get them into a favor, or you'll find yourself on the bad end of a Collector.

    Negotiators: You need to maintain the careful deals struck with the Gentry, the God Machine, and countless other entities. Its only your very life and soul, along with thousands of others, on the line here.


    (Thoughts? Endowments(yes, this is a Conspiracy), and status benefits I'll get to later, I'm just interested in what you guys think of the concept.)

      The Customers


      There isn't really an enemy, for the Favors Association doesn't hunt anyone in the traditional sense. Rather, everyone is either a potential Customer, or the Deal Breakers. Deal Breakers either try to cheat out of doing their side of the Favor, or are those trying to prevent the Favors from continuing. Either way, the proper connections need to be called to deal with them, so that the budget of favors every year is always in the black. The potential Customers are the most important however, as they expand the favors, or in the case of rather powerful entities, allowing the enforcement of favors to begin with.

      Deals with the Gentry: Ah, the classic customer. Besides the need for carefully written deals, they're perfect. Their want for humans, and occasionally a wayward servant, are easy enough to fulfill. Problem is the fine print can be a pain with these types, and their demands are all over the place frequently.

      Deals with the God Machine: You want to talk about dangerous? This thing is the closet we have to God. Fortunately, its a machine, and doesn't have the irrationalities other clients can have. It mostly just wants its Angels back, however occasionally, it needs certain people for some reason. Just make sure not to break deals with it, or you may find yourself retroactively dead, or something worse.

      Deals with Vampires: Shady jerks, make sure to have enforcement ready when they try to wiggle out of their part of the bargain. Can be quite good at the whole manipulation thing however.

      Deals with Werewolves: Can be great muscle, but be careful not to anger them. Also a great force to call on when Spirits need to be dealt with.

      Deals with Mages: Can be useful, just make sure you have Hunters on call if they decide to try using magic to cheat.

      Deals with Changelings: Stay away! They know how this stuff works, and besides, they know who we deal with frequently. Only approach if you're going to get one of them dragged back home.

      Deals with Hunters: These are the guys you need when dealing with any of those magical types, and many of them can have other uses as well. And even better, if they try to screw you out on their responsibilities, the Gentry like them same as anybody else.

      Deals with Geists: Good when dealing with Ghosts, otherwise just leave em be.

      Deals with Demons: Too risky considering our prior arrangements. Keep watch for them though, the God Machine loves getting its servants back.

      Deals with Mortals: Ah, our favorite. Plenty of desperate chumps, and people more useful for our purposes even can easily be suckered into a deal. And there are plenty who want them if they try getting out of their end of the bargain.

      (So yes, a Hunter Conspiracy of Con Artists, to some extent. Because this was a hole that couldn't be left unfilled.)

                  And now, onto Status Benefits.

                  Status 1: You got played, and are now stuck with a Favor to carry out. Still, the basic training they gave you with tracking people should help when its time to start finding people to sucker, or when hunting your first Cheater. You gain a free Specialty in either Persuasion or Investigation, depending on the conditions of your favor, related to getting Customers or tracking Deal Breakers respectively.

                  Status 3: You're in deep, but hey, at least you learned some neat tricks from others stuck in your shoes. Gain the 9 again rule with either Persuasion or Investigation, depending on your particular specialty within the Association.

                  Status 5: You're now a permanent member, in return for your favors, however at least your high up enough that you don't have to do any of the grunt work. Instead, you're a lucky administrator. You gain the 8 again rule in Occult and Academics when related to dealing with the particular administrative tasks of your job.

                      The Hunters

                      What else could you do? Your life had been ruined by the twin problems of cancer and medical bills, so you figured, how could it possibly get worse? But now, you have to help psychopathic deities kidnap children and a mockery of God track down its wayward Angels. How many more times do you have to do this? You don't know, as every time you seem close to being free, something else comes along needing a favor, like a mortgage, or getting out of jail. You're beginning to wonder how bad it really is...

                      You know you screwed up even at the time, but you just wanted to finally be free. Ever since you were a kid, people bullied you into accepting shit, from your father to people in school and your own damn boss. That asshole at work kept threatening to destroy your career unless you worked overtime for no pay. So, you took the deal to have him disappeared, and now, your still kissing someone's ass. At least most of them get taken away or end up buried in the same shit as you are.

                      Ever tried to dig your way out of college debt? When the economy collapses and you majored in Anthropology? Sure, you have to help disappear people, but hey, at least your degree finally helps in a more substantial way. Even if you are helping mythic level psychopaths, at least they give you debt you can eventually pay off.

                      Alright, so yeah, jokingly agreeing to be promoted in exchange for negotiating deals with clients from Hell probably wasn't the best idea, but you were wasted. And yeah, removing your ex from existence was a bad idea, but you were sick of what it made you do. Besides, your entire existence has been dictated by fine print, making it for once is a nice change.

                      You're quite lucky. See, you get to just be an administrator until you die so your past of laundering doesn't catch up. But, problem is some of this stuff is just insane. Its one thing to do paperwork hiding some money, but its quite another to help erase someone's existence, or make sure that thousands of favors are all in the black right on time. No wonder the previous holder of this position was eager to die.

                          Endowments
                          (Note: This is a draft. I fully expect the need to change this in the future, but need thoughts on how they are for now.)

                          Everything done by the Favors Association is dependent on using powerful deities to enforce their deals. Further, their organization depends on a collection of ceremonies made for contacting each deity, or in some cases, making others known to them so they can be captured. These same ceremonies can gain one special benefits from these entities, in the circumstance it would grant them a good deal, but always remember the fine print.

                          Contact the Gentry(Requires 2 Status Dots): You can open a communication line to Arcadia, just requires charcoal and some big paper. However, remember you still have to discuss the terms with the Gentry. Dice Pool: Manipulation+Occult

                          Dramatic Failure: Get taken to Arcadia and made the eternal slave of the True Fae.

                          Failure: Gentry doesn't like your deal, and fortunately, considers you too boring to kidnap. Unfortunately, other Gentry now may be interested in kidnapping you.

                          Success: You contacted them, and they are interested in what you have to offer. If you're promising a human, just show them where. If you need their magic, they better be getting something they'd want down the line.

                          Exceptional Success: You've made an excellent impression on this particular client, and they would be interested in seeing more deals with you in the near future. Gain a 2+ modifier with future interactions involving the True Fae.

                          Suggested Modifiers: Have a mortal to offer already(+2), Have an escaped Changeling to offer(+4), Require magical help (-2) Re-negioate Deal (-4)


                          Contact the God Machine: (Requires 2 Status Dots) Well, you aren't really talking to the God Machine, as such a being isn't really going to be something understandable by humans. Rather, you're talking to an Angel who happens to act as a representative when this comes up. Requires a computer terminal and an internet connection to get to a very special website. Dice Pool: Manipulation+Academics

                          Dramatic Failure: The Angel kills you and uses your corpse gets used in a God Machine Project, or is otherwise disposed of efficiently.

                          Failure: You're spared, but the God Machine won't consider you again unless you prove yourself useful in a different function, such as a convenient sacrifice for a project.

                          Success: The God Machine is willing to help, however requires an offer now. If you're offering the location of one of its wayward Angels, just show it where they are, and you'll be rewarded. If you need magical help, don't expect it to take too kindly to the offer.

                          Exceptional Success: The God Machine likes your deal so much that you're considered a dependable source of good deals. Gain a +2 modifier to future interactions with the God Machine.

                          Suggested Modifiers: Have a Demon to offer already (+4), Have something it needs for a project( +2), Require magical help (-2) Re-negioate Deal (-4)


                          Contact a Hunter Conspiracy: (Requires 1 Status Dot): In theory, this just requires the right phone number, however the Favors Association can show the exact location of where creatures they want to hunt are. This ceremony then is essentially using magic to show already spotted monsters to Hunter Conspiracies, not just contacting them. Hunter Conspiracies are only interested in monsters, and within that, each conspiracy has different wants. For examples, the Cheiron group is more interested in rare monsters, Task Force: VALKYRIE only deals with hunting creatures on American soil or that harm American interests, and the Malleus Maleficarum are relatively easy to please, however especially are interested in monsters that seem to utilize "satanic" methods, such as black magic.
                          Requires a working phone line, and prior use of the Tracker Touch endowment, or spotting a creature that broke a Favor with the Association, which only requires a Favor Association member to spot them to activate Tracker Touch, on.
                          Dice Pool: Manipulation+Empathy

                          Dramatic Failure: Not only is the creature uninteresting to them, but they consider you a threat somehow, and will send Hunters to take you down.

                          Failure: The creature is uninteresting to them.

                          Success: The creature is interesting or dangerous to them, and Hunters will be sent to deal with them.

                          Exceptional Success: The creature is considered a high priority to take down, and you are now considered a valuable resource. Gain 2+ dice on any future interactions with this particular Conspiracy.

                          Suggested Modifiers: Have a creature to offer that falls directly in their interest(+4), have a supernatural creature that is directly threatening (+2), have a creature that falls outside their interests(-2), and have a creature that seems to be harmless(-2).


                          Tracker Touch: (Requires One Status Dot): The core of your organization's powers, and the one that makes it an asset for everyone else. By touching a person's hand, you leave a magical tracker that can show whoever you want where someone or something is (requires a suitable Contact ritual to show them the Tracked). In combat, this requires a successful Touch Attack, otherwise requires a roll of Dexterity+Subterfuge, to sneak it onto the person. Note anyone who has broken a Favor only requires an agent of the Favor Association to spot them to activate this.

                          Dramatic Failure: In combat, turned against you, and now you're of interest to various entities. Outside of it, spotted and turned against you.

                          Failure: Fail to activate it, but if out of combat, doesn't cause you to be spotted. In combat, allows another try.

                          Success: Tracker is activated, and now, that person can be easily found by a suitable organization, if they are of interest to them. Still requires the Contact Ritual.

                          Exceptional Success: The suitable organization already knows where they are, and the Contact Ritual is unnecessary.

                          Suggested Modifiers: Target knows who work for(-4), Target thinks you're trustworthy (+2), In combat (-2).

                                          Changelings and the Favors Association

                                          For those who know of it, and at this point almost all Changelings do, they... hate it. Almost as much as the Gentry at times, and at least as much as Loyalists or Privateers. Worse however, the reasons humans work for the Favors Association are... rather close to home. They don't want to be taken to Arcadia themselves, because they know what lies there, unlike most Mortals. They are reminded of it to intimidate them into continuing the task whenever they risk falling sort of their quotas. That they are willing to throw others into the path of the Gentry in a desperate attempt to avoid said fate is therefore understandable, if unforgivable.

                                          This causes each of the four Seasonal Courts to have a different general response to them, if found, and to prioritize different parts of the organization. Its also worth noting that, frequently being masters of fine print, Changelings can turn the tactics the Favors Association uses against it, especially if they can successfully hide their presence from them.

                                          Spring: "Lead them along, promising them a way out, but then... reveal the truth. If they want to be saved, they have to reveal their fellows from the organization, and then, pledge being a pledge, you will save them from the Gentry. But, expose them in the process, and insure they are truly punished for what they have done."

                                          Summer: "They are no better than Privateers, or Loyalist scum. Since they are mortals, care must be taken in hunting them down, but it must be done. They must be silenced before one more person ends up in the hands of the True Fae from their treachery."

                                          Autumn: "Who fears others more than the monster themselves? Especially if they know what is coming for them, and while we are not as terrifying a fate as eternal slavery, we can replicate much of that."

                                          Winter: "Follow them, find their fellows, and then, take them out. They are a threat to both us, and humanity at large that must be ended."

                                          The other response to the Favor's Association is an attempt to protect the mortal community from their presence. Those who are preyed on most are found, and alternative deals are offered. Freeholds around the world have found that cultivating better communities can do much to prevent the Favors Association from ever appearing, much less taking more people.

                                          (Hope you guys like it, and tell me if you think a response is rather out of character for a court.)

                                              Demons and the Favors Association
                                              Like Changelings, Demons are rather angry at the Favors Association, as it is a tool of the God Machine to capture more Demons. Unlike Changelings, they have far less care for the mortals impacted by it, and have a different range of responses to the God Machine.

                                              For example, they will frequently, if possible, trick low ranking Agents to give up their souls, and use their bodies as burn Covers with which to directly attack the Favors Association. Other tactics include the usage of agents as bait for the organization, so as to isolate and stealthily eliminate agents. Of course, Demons will frequently seek to vacate the area, however such tactics create their own problems, not the least of which being that it will not deal with the Favors Association, or the ceremonies they use to track Demons.

                                              Demons will, sometimes, attempt to use the Favors Association for their own ends, however this type of tactic carries many risks, not the least of which being the risk of Angels showing up. However, if done correctly, the Favors Association can be used to get Demons a truly staggering number of Covers, just by having their Agents slightly change some of their contract wording. And considering the alternate fate, one could even argue this is a moral use of the organization in a perverse way.
