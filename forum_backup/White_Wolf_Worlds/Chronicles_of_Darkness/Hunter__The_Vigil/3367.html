    Vigil For Rome

    11-05-2013, 07:57 AM
    I proposed a Requiem For Rome game to my group, and their answer was that they love Rome but have no desire to play as vampires. They instead suggested we do a Hunter game set in the Roman Empire, which sounds fun as hell. The group has since split (thanks, college) but the idea has still stuck with me. Any idea how to go about doing this?

    Clearly, none of the modern Compacts and all but a few of the modern Conspiracies would be viable. We'd have the nascent Cainite Heresy and the Aegis Kai Doru running around (maybe the Ascending Ones as well? I'm terribly unfamiliar with them). I think the Vigil corebook mentions a compact of Roman Empire hunters (the Aves Minerva) but went into no detail on them.

    How would you guys do this game? What advice do you have? I think it would be a lot of fun.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

          The Aves Minverva can essentially be a proto-version of the Cainite Heresey, if you want. There's a more detailed write-up for them in the Paterfamilias SAS for RfR. So, with them and AKD you have a couple of conspiracies to start with. In the RfR corebook they describe members of the Mithraic Mysteries (many of whom were veteran soldiers) as potential antagonists, no reason you couldn't tweak something like the Malleus with a more pagan spin to fit that. A few years back I was also interested in doing a Roman Empire Vigil game and I came up with my own ancient tech-based conspiracy centered around Archimedes and his secret war against the undead. Basically he wrote up a codex of fighting techniques and contraptions which you could use all manner of sketchy historical evidence to allow things as various as flamethrowers, repeating crossbows and grenades into the mix. Call it Ancient Armory and use it the same way as TFV's Endowment and you're set.
