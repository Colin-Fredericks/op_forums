    Adventurer: the Questing [Fantasy Setting Hack]

    04-28-2016, 07:20 PM
    Protecting their community? Killing monsters? Working in small, united groups?

    Did anyone noticed that hunters are a lot like adventurers from D&D?

    So, let's explore that option, shall we?

    The continent of Taelra is the most populated among the 5 continents of Korelron. It is divided into a number of great numbers and small kingdoms, desperate republics and united leagues. While the numbers of those nations, theocracies and city states is vast, they are separated from one another by great forests, vast mountains and raging rivers. Magic, while exist and known, is seen as an inhuman power gifted by mysterious powers, and it belongs only to those monsters who roam the Wilderness which surrounds those lonely civilizations. Simply going out from the walls is enough to endanger one's life- yet those bastions of humanity can't survive on their own. They require each other in order to survive, either to practice in peaceful trade or to wage violent wars. The common citizen fears the Wilderness and its horrors, especially when they slither into their cities and take root, blooming into heresies and creeping shadows.

    Hold a candle to guide your way in the forest. Light a flame to banish the shadows.

    The state may be hard- but is not hopeless. Few, brave people dare to challenge the danger and go out into the Wilderness, making a name for themselves as they weave legends of their victories- or die trying. Those adventurers hunt after monsters, root the evil from their home or follow the requests of their kings and rulers. They are recognize as operating outside the law of their kingdoms- something which is both a benefit and a curse, for while they are free from the rules of regular men, they do not enjoy their protection either. They life of an adventurer are not easy- but the promises for power, wealth and fame are enough to draw those with fire in their heart into the endless battle against the monster empires and barbarian tribes of the Wilderness.

    However, just roaming the Wilderness and killing monsters is not enough to make one into a true adventurer- those who wish to get the respect one of those daredevils get has to follow certain requests, missions asked by those who can not- dare not- go to the Wilderness and do them by their own. Those quests are the core of the adventurer experience- some are not that difficult, like carrying a certain message from one city to another or killing a lone monster. Others may save the life of an whole city- like finding a lost treasure or defeating a dread necromancer. As one may expect, usually the more important tasks are given to the more experienced adventurers, but there were exceptions- especially when the said mission must be done without the knowing of the local Guilds or Alliances, forcing the one making the request to turn to those few, lone adventurers.

    This is a world of heroism and dangers, of shining victories and harsh defeats- yet the story is whole the same.

    It is a story of light in the shadows, and may your legend be recorded in the Chronicles of Darkness.

    ........................................

    ok- so as far as I can see, there aren't many rule changed required for the setting to work- most of it would be only translation of fluff into a more suitable form in the setting- however, it is late in here and I have to sleep, so I'll share the few lexicon translations I've had in my mind

    The Party is the smallest unit of adventurers, usually consisted from 4-6 members each. No matter to which Tier the adventurers belong to, every adventurer has her Party- those few allies she can rely on, goes out with to the Wilderness and risk her life side by side. The Party is especially important for adventurers of the First Tier, for they lack the support of other adventurers and don't have the same ability to recruit new members as the different Alliances and Guilds has. Most of First Tier Parties are made from green adventurers who were either not accepted or decided not to join a bigger organization, although few, epic Parties of that group have gained glory and respect from the many citizens of Taelra.

    Lone Parties who survive for long enough may either find similar, like minded Parties and form an Alliance, or be accepted into one which shares their goals and agenda. Unlike the more stable Guilds, Alliances are fluid structures, who lack the rigid hierarchy of the former yet lack their stable access to resources and information. They usually operate as information networks and underground organizations, for they are not officially recognized by the nations as a formal representation of adventurers, yet they can not be ignored for the power they hold in their hands. Certain Alliances has tried to establish themselves as true Guilds, yet doing is not an easy feat. The main benefit of Alliances is that they share their information, resources and even members between one another without the rules and regulations of the Guilds, allowing their members a lot of freedom while gaining some support. While usually all of the Party belongs to a single Alliance, Parties who contain members from different Alliances or an adventurer who has multiple memberships is not an uncommon sight, especially since it gives their members a lot more access for information and resources- yet things could get complicated if and when the said Alliances turn upon the other, forcing their members to make harsh choices which may tear the Party apart.

    Example Alliances are the Singers of the Nights, who come from many faiths all united in their will to redeem the world from the darkness, the Black Rose Bacchanal, who ride into the Wilderness motivated by pure emotion and hunger for experiences and the Silent Castle Vanguard, who simply believe in doing the right thing and establish connection between the adventurers of many, lone cities.

    The movers and shakers in the world of the adventurers are the Guilds, great social constructs which were established by legendary figures who left their name in history, a proof that humanity can defeat it enemies and rise above the Wilderness. Guilds are powerful organizations, rivaling the power of nations and kings, and they usually establish themselves at one city or another even though they operate through most- if not all- of the continent. While First and Second Tier adventurers require hunting for new quests, the Guilds have steady supply of such missions as part of its agreements with the local governments and powers, giving them a huge advantage in terms of resources, intelligence and numbers. Not only that, each Guild has developed a certain form of sorcery unique to it, a special secret unknown to the regular people created by years of research and effort. While holding such magic is usually seen as a sign of working with monsters- something which is punishable by death in many states- the Guilds have a special authorization to use those powers as part of the Accords of Ujenda. Because of their jealousy to their secrets, the Guilds usually avoid from working together, and are very suspicious toward one another. Still, while the practice is discouraged, mixed Guild Parties do exist- and some of them even join Alliances, something which could be tolerated as long as they keep their mouth shut and don't share their Guild's secrets.

    Example Guilds are the High Artificers, who have discovered the secret of making powerful items which harness the forces of the world as weapons, the Order of the Holy Light, who belong to the Orthodoxy of Maacab and invoke the might of 2743 lesser angels through their blessings and the Warlocks of Zumerash, who learn diabolical magics from the demons of hell in order to defeat said demons and the other monsters of the Wilderness.


    So- any thoughts/suggestions about the setting and how to develop it/translate Hunter's organizations into it?

      so, a sort writeup for the 6 "core Guilds"

      Guardians of the Labyrinth- one of the oldest Guilds in Taelra, the Guardians seek after lost artifacts and terrible relics. For them, using those magical items is a way to gain the power of monsters without tainting their body and soul, and reclaiming such relics is seen as the Guild's highest priority. They are established in Annaresthia, a mountain surrounded nation formed from 12 different city states, with each having its own form of government and freedom in its inner affairs- yet when war comes or the monsters raid, they unite together in mutual protection. The mountains of Annaresthia are named as the "Labyrinth Mountains" and for good reason- for the Guardians has turned them into fortresses, digging deep into Earth's bones and forming great mazes which only they know, where they hide their precious treasures and secret knowledge. Each member takes an Oath beneath the 12 gods which patronize the Annaresthian City States, swearing to retrieve relics and destroy the monsters which they blame for an ancient catastrophe- that is, stealing the magic from mankind and the gods and destroy the Golden Age. Such faith is considered to be Heresy according to the Orthodoxy of Maacab, and as such they also vow for secrecy, knowing that the Orthodoxy is not above calling for an Holy War against Annaresthia and its Guardians.

      Cult of the Phoenix- rising in the Great Desert during a time of crises, the alchemists of the Cult are valued merchants and diplomats. Each of their members is taught with the secrets of potion making, which requires them transforming exotic materials and dangerous poisons into elixirs which grant the user inhuman abilities- although one wrong move or not having enough faith in the harsh gods of their land would end up in death. The technique relies upon invoking the Heliparthian pantheon through the act, which transforms the deadly poison into a supernatural gift. However, potion making is not their only work- they also trade in perfumes, make up, pigments, medicines and drugs. They are an extremely wealthy organization, having traders almost everywhere across Taelra who search for the exotic materials their potions require. The fact that they are so spread across the continent makes them into the ideal messengers and diplomats, and many quests given to those traders are connected to those themes. In their own country, the Empire of Heliparthia, however, they are a religious group of zealots, who has its place as warrior priests who fight in the name of their gods. They practice their own version of the Orthodoxy in their country, and Maacab is distant enough to "straight their ways"- although they still rarely use their services.

      College of Apocerthia- the greatest healers in Taelra are known to be able to cure any plague, injury, poison or curse- as long as the payment is right. Unlike most of the other Guilds, who bring only a trophy to proof their kill, the members of the College bring the whole body back to their cities, and some even dare to capture the beasts alive for their bases, which gives a much greater profit- although the risk may be too great. The doctors of the College research those bodies, taking them apart and preforming experiments in order to understand how the power flow through the monster's body and soul. Every member of the Guild must have at least some knowledge in anatomy and medicine, even if it is simply so they won't ruin the monster's body during the fight. Among all Guilds, the College has the highest understanding of how the powers of monsters work, and by using reverse engineering their are able to transplant those powers into their members, something which makes many other adventurers suspicious toward them. Still, it seems that the College is more than happy to hire adventurers of lowers Tiers (or even other guilds) for certain missions, and they do pay handsomely either with money or offering their skills as healers. They are based in Apocerthia, a small island in the middle of the Crystal Lake, which is itself hidden in the depths of the Shadow Forest near the Labyrinth Mountains. Many are suspicious of the Guild's true nature and goals, considering they put so much effort in hiding their activities while opening many offices through the continent- yet when the plagues hit the land and curses fall upon the citizens, people would even sell their souls to the College (which is a standard contract). Some think if perhaps the College itself has part in those outbursts, yet no one has managed to proof such connection.

      High Artificers- for many, the Artificer Guild is seen as no less than magic workers with the ability to trap light and sound into metal and stone. The adventurers themselves claim that there is nothing magical in their weapons and items- they are simply the representation of mankind's greatness and knowledge which allows them to craft mechanisms so advanced, that for the regular person they are no different from magic. The members of the guild, all require having some understanding about how those mechanisms work and how to make them, even though the blue prints themselves are hidden secret of the Guild, with each such armory having a special sub mechanism to identify if the one to try and activate it is truly a member of the Guild. The Guild itself claim to exist only to protect the citizens of Taelra, being established by the Gennian government itself- although they are still adventurers and operate by their own. Still, being founded by the government makes them more loyal and less independent than the regular Guild- and some think if perhaps the Guild has its own, secret operation founded from other sources- for more than once, their members are sent for special mission which require deep secrecy- even from the people they claim to serve. They are based in the snowy Republic of Gennia, which is actually a unification of number of states in that region, who while respect the power of the Orthodoxy are still free from its grasp, and while no citizen worship the old gods many wonder why do the Artificers name their armory after the false divinities.

      Order of the Holy Light- the Orthodoxy of Maacab is, by no doubt, one of the strongest organizations of Taelra, and the Order is their holy arm against the monsters of the darkness. Each of the adventurers of the Order is also a person of faith, having a rank as a priest in the grant organization of the Orthodoxy. According to their dogma, the gods has created mankind as their chosen people upon the face of earth in order to fight the terrors of the demons who were formed from the primordial chaos. The gods themselves, while powerful, are unable to directly intervene in Korelron's affairs, for their power may accidentally destroy the existence. As such, they sent their holy army of 2743 angels to bless the soldiers of mankind, giving them the powers of divine inspiration in order to defeat the monsters and reclaim the world from the darkness. While monsters are the main concern of the Order, as priests and bishops they do not forget that the greatest enemy is heresy- the root monsters from the cities and investigate cases in which civilians turn from the rightful path and fall prey to the manipulations of monsters. They regulate the religious affairs of most of Taelra's nations, hidding the fact that the many holidays, festivals and fasting are actually meant to cement the powers of their angels and strength their holy magics. While many has noticed that, in theory, their magic is no different that the powers of certain monsters- such words are considered to be heresy, for the difference is that those powers are granted by the gods and not demons. The Guild itself is based in the city state of the Maacab Theocracy, and ruled by the Archbishop of the Light, one of the closest advisers of the High Priest of Maacab- although their influence is strong everywhere the faith stands, which gives them a great influence.

      Warlocks of Zumerash- Once, Zumerash was a terrible place. Residing near volcanic mountain and crafted out of obsidian, its nobility was corrupted from the inside by demonic rituals which required blood sacrifices and soul damnation. For years the Orthodoxy has fought desperate battles against the demonic nation, yet the powers of its infernal patrons was strong enough to withstand their constant attacks. Yet, after years of heresy and destruction, the change came from the inside- a noble family led by a woman known as Lucia stood against their legacy of blood and started a rebellion, one which was supported through a pact between Lucia and the High Priest of Maacab. After few years of inner civil war, Lucia won the battle, establishing the noble families as a new Guild meant to defeat the terrors unleashed by their forefathers and bind the demons which manipulated them through years. Using secret techniques, new rites were developed from the corrupted texts, creating weapons which enslave demons and banish them from the world. Today, Zumerash is still seen as a living hell- yet its Warlocks has enslaved that Hell for the benefit of its civilians. Each of the Warlocks can track its lineage back to one of the noble houses of Zumerash, and as such being birthed into the House or getting adopted into the Infernal Monarchy are the only ways to join the Guild, which guards its secrets less out of trying to stabilizing their power and more out of fearing its corruptible influence upon weaker minds, which could create another Zumerash in Taelra. Unlike many Guilds, the Warlocks are willing to give second chances to monsters, trying to redeem them from their state. This is not a simple sympathy- for the Warlocks recognize the infernal blood in their veins, and they know that their ability to use magic is a sign that they by themselves not just as human as the rest. Lucia herself has lived for centuries and didn't aged for a day, and no one knows if it is a side effect of the rituals she learned or some hidden power she gained through a bargain with Hell. While they are despised and feared through the continent, their alliance with the Orthodoxy stands strong, and all respect their knowledge at dealing with demons and their like. Their greatest enemies are the loyalists to Zumerash's legacy, who try to reestablish the old order- with them on its top.

                  as for the four "specialists"-

                  Knights of the Dragon Saint- coming from the stormy island of Teribettiria, they are officially a branch of the Orthodoxy who took life of its own away from the continent. Their separation from the Orthodoxy was, seemingly, a pure political move which freed their kingdom from the influence of the Theocracy and put it out of its sphere of influence, although they do have a number of important differences in matter of faith and worship. While the Knights themselves belong to the Teribettrian Reformation, the truth is they only use that faith as a cover for their true nature- a deep heresy which use the names of 72 of the Orthodoxy's angels as a "window" to invoke the powers of terrible beings who exist on the "Outside". By binding their own minds to the dreams of those hidden gods, they are able to channel magic and form demons made of thought and memory, which they then use as weapons against their enemies. According to their own faith, the monsters are the creation of the death of a "primordial dragon", which was slain by the Faceless Angels who wished to drain its blood. Yet, some of it was spilled and from that wild magic monsters were birthed, and the Knights kill the monsters and sacrifice their magic back to their own gods. Because of that heretic faith, they use a system of "Mysteries" and "Revelations" which makes sure only members of certain level would know certain amounts of information. They usually use Reformic imagery in order to invoke their "anti magic demons", which includes symbolizing the Primordial Dragon in the form of their legendary founder, which has his descendants as the leaders of the Guild together with the Knightly Council of Teribettiria. Each of the members is considered to be a true knight of the realm, including noble benefits and duties, and receive the formal training their knights enjoy.

                  Birds of the Black Ozurel- one of the most scattered Guilds, the Birds are a zealot group, devoting their life to hunting down monsters through the Wilderness and avenging their beloved ones. Each and every one of them has lost someone important before joining the Guild, with many of them being recruited right after the tragedy stroke, and the reason was always tied to the monsters who hunt the world. The center of their power is found in a place called the "Disrukh", which no one really knows where to put on the map- most agree the place is found the the Chasm of Eternity, yet no one of those who returned has managed to find the legendary place, which according to the stories is a city of the dead hunted by ghosts and restless shades. The Black Ozurel is an heretic saint, one denied by the Orthodoxy, which according to the legends and whispers has devoured Death itself in order to return to the world. The members of the Guild do not necessary believe in the Black Ozurel, yet their ceremonies and rituals do require invoking him using blood offerings (their own blood, of course), which disrupt the powers of monsters and trap them in a mad world which deny their existence. While they are lonely people, they do communicate with their base at Disrukh and with each other by using a network of black owls made of smoke and shadows, which are the symbol of the Black Ozurel and a sign of him conquering Death. Most of them memorize their rites by the heart, not even trying to understand the logic behind it, nor don they care that this form of magic transforms their blood and makes them being touched by Death. The only thing which is important is taking down another monster and avenging the dead.

                  Spirit Circle Dancers- while most of the world is under the Orthodoxy's influence, the so called "pagan ring" exist through the tropic Equirsa island at the Eastern Sea and up to the Plains of Nuketa which behind the northern Glacier Mountains. The many different nations has united together in order to black the Maacabian influence, with each state maintaining its original faith, traditions and government. Some of the nations, such as Equirsa, has partly accepted the Orthodoxy, creating a syncretistic faith based around connecting their angels to the many gods they had faith with. Others, like the kingdom of Laquira, has maintained their faith untouched by foreign elements. The only ones to bridge upon the many differences are the members of the Spirit Circle Dancers- a shamanistic Guild which is based around the idea of syncretism and connection with their gods. Their rituals are usually wild and unexpected, requiring them to draw the attention of their primal deities and allow them to ride their flesh in order to invoke their powers. While the Orthodoxy brand them as heretics and heathen who allow themselves to be manipulated by monsters, the Dancers has developed a delicate system of taboos which allows them to maintain certain control over themselves and avoid becoming puppets of their gods. Walking a fine line between sanity and madness, they do not only serve their community by bridging the different communities in the region and connecting people with their gods, but they also go to the Wilderness and fight the monsters who hide there in order to protect their people and make sure their worship is not corrupted by outside influence.

                  Third Eye Institute- the life as an adventurer are not easy, and sometimes those who protect their people from the Wilderness become a bit.. carried away. They become frustrated or bored with their life and prey, and may start seeking other, more challenging opponent- other adventurers. While each Guild has a responsibility to put down its own adventurers, some prefer to ignore the problem, not to mention the many adventurers which are not bound by the Accords of Ujenda. As such, the Third Eye Institute was establish- a regulating body which supervise not monsters, but other adventurers. While they do go out to the Wilderness and fight monsters as any other adventurer, their specialty is working in the cities, finding both corrupted adventurers and hidden monsters. They use a special technique which opens their mind to the souls and thoughts of those around them, which requires a certain mechanism which was recovered from the Wilderness ages ago. They are officially based in the Ujenda Republic, which sits near the boundary of the Nuketa Plains and the Glacier Mountains- yet their members are spread across the continent as they search for renegade adventurers and corrupted Alliances. They are the least wealthy Guild, for Ujenda is not a rich land and they lack another source of income- not to mention that many of the other Guilds put a lot of pressure upon the different nations to not hire their services as they believe they would try to sniff out their secrets and steal their techniques. Still, no one argues about their effectiveness, and everyone agree to their authority and accept their agents as part of the Accords.
