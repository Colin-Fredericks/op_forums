    Convention: Bastet's Beloved, The Kitten Cuddlers

    08-19-2015, 12:46 PM
    Art by Reper Verse




    “Have you heard?” Said Spot. “Tibbers saw a dead man watching the college where they throw out fish every Thursday.”

    “But that’s the anointed one’s school” replied Scrufmuffin in horror.

    Tigger’s blood boiled in rage as he sniffed the latest news from a lamppost. “Come! Smell this!” he shouted loud enough to wake humans.


    Lugosi waited in a dusty attic. He was amusing himself by listening to the heartbeats of ignorant humans below and wondering which of the college girls across the street he’d make into his brides when he heard a soft thump and a tiny heartbeat behind him. Then another and another. He turned and a dozen reflective eyes stared unblinking at him from the shadows.

    Something with the weight of a grown man landed directly on the Lugosi’s shoulders knocking him off balance. Claws dug into his back and cut deep grooves into his ribs. The vampire rolled onto his back and sized a house cat and began to crush it between his hands.

    Two cats pounced in sequence, each shredding the muscles in an arm beneath their claws. Lugosi dropped the cat and began to shape the magic animating his body, forcing his arms to work without tendons when a cat leapt up and gouged out his eyes. The vampire stumbled backwards trying to track his assailants by their heartbeats, he picked a target and lunged by without supernatural speed the cat easily dived out the way. In mid stride a cat tackled Lugosi’s knee with the weight of a grown man and sent him flying. The pride moved in for the kill.


    In the World of Darkness a god is more than just an all-powerful long bearded man in the sky. A god can include choirs of angels or entire worlds just as you include your little finger. A God can even include, be, or be the product of (if such distinctions apply to the divine) natural laws.

    One such Goddess is Bastet of the Netjer, whose worship dates back almost to 3000 BC. Revered throughout Ancient Egypt as a protector deity, Bastet gave her sacred cats to the nation. Her cats would patrol the land, protecting it’s food supplies from vermin and protecting the people from cobras and worse threats.

    In modern nights… nothing much has changed. If Bastet smiles upon you, then the cats will keep you safe.

    The Enemy

    While the Goddess’ invisible support helps her Beloved quickly come to terms with their ability to talk to cats, they are still citizens of the World of Darkness. Most have little knowledge of the supernatural and little interest in seeking out the occult in dark corners to fight it, or otherwise. Even if they did the Beloved have no institution to transmit policy about different foes.

    It is not Bastet’s Beloved who have enemies, it is Bastet Herself, and Bastet’s enemies are anyone who threaten her Beloved.

    Vampires need to feed upon humans and cats don’t have the patience or the cultural knowledge to determine their feeding habits, if a vampire is in the area the claws come out. Beasts also prey on humanity and while a cat’s knowledge of Ancient Egyptian theology is on the same level as an eight year old catholic’s knowledge of christianity that’s enough for them to point out, often lethally, that teaching humanity is the job of priests and gods, whose motives are purer and whose methods are less traumatic.

    That’s not to say that cats have any supernatural ability to recognise vampires or beasts, or any culture of shared knowledge about them. Rather, once a cats see a vampire go for the throat in a dark alleyway they’re quick to judge.

    Against more powerful enemies, particularly if they have a connection to the divine like Leviathans or Mummies, cats are quick to suggest asking a higher power for help. Unfortunately cats don’t have international communication networks, today they don’t know where to find Bastet’s priesthood or if the priesthood still exists. Bastet’s Beloved who don’t realise that as the human they’re supposed to be the intelligent and forward thinking one often end up on a flight to Egypt with no clue what they’re going to do when they land.

    Hunters

    Not long ago the Vampire who claimed feeding rights over your neighbourhood vanished. Shortly afterwards the mismatched trio of newly embraced vampires sent to investigate vanished. Then so did the Prince’s agent sent to do the job properly. Now all the vampires eyes turn towards your neighbourhood. You don’t know this of course, you don’t know vampires exist either, you’re only nine years old.

    You’re a witch. Not a particularly powerful witch but you have some tricks and a black cat. You never tried to attract Bastet’s attention, in fact you always considered yourself more a Maiden, Mother, Crone sort of witch but when a goddess chooses you you know the only acceptable response is humble gratitude. New artwork and new theurgy textbooks it is. In truth Bastet isn’t giving you much power beyond her Mantle, but you wouldn’t dare be ungrateful.

    One day you can home and your boyfriend was different, scary. He insisted he loved you and only wanted to be together, but your cat saw the truth. When he was alone he walked through a door and on the other side he was a hulking man shaped bat. Together you’re on the run, stealing to survive. It seems he won't ever let you go.

    You’re a ranger in the safaris of Africa. You fight poachers and you love all the animals under your care, the lions most of all. You also have no idea why all sorts of weird people keep coming to you for advice or your blessing.

    There’s no polite way to put it. You’re a crazy cat lady. Unlike most of your contemporaries you’re actually competent at caring for a house full of cats. Your guests are well fed and healthy, which is why Bastet loves you. Another thing sets you apart from your contemporaries, your craziness comes from spending all your life in the vicinity of a Mummy’s tomb. The presence of a Goddess has help focused the oracular abilities inflicted by living in a Mummy’s arua. With your army of cats you will chase the vermin from your town, you will prove Bastet and the civilisation of Egypt superior to the brutality of Irem’s Judges and their scorpion cults.

    Stereotypes

    The Union: Dad came home upset that people at the factory were gossiping about me and Tigger. I think they lost interest, he’s much happier these days.

    Hakollel shel Hamerkava: Coca loves to point out flaws in the Torah. I asked the rabbi about some of Coca’s ideas and he smiled and gave me a book full of big words, but I could tell he was upset when he spoke to mum and dad.

    The Athenic Church: Mr Biscuit says my cousin’s friends will all end up old and lonely so I shouldn’t be like them. My cousin says I need to spend less time playing with Mr Biscuit and more time doing my homework if I want to make a meaningful contribution to society.

    Null Mysteriis: My science teacher is really fun. He asks me to spend lunch wearing a funny hat and talking to his kittens, and he gives me sweets and answers for my homework if I don’t tell anyone.

    Picture: A young girl in a cutesy dress is hugging a kitten. On the wall behind them the cat has no shadow; instead you can see a shadow of a lioness grooming the girl’s hair.

    I love my kitty witty!

    Status

    Bastet's Beloved shouldn't really have Status. There's no organization, no ladder to climb, heck. Most members could go their whole lives without meeting another. Yet there is something, when two of Bastet's Beloved meet they can both feel the mark of Bastet upon the other. It's an instinctive respect, closer to what one lion might feel for the pride's leader than human trust, but it's respect all the same.

    Because of this Status measures only one thing: How well Bastet thinks of you. The more you do for cats, the higher your Status.

    O: One moment you're an ordinary person, the next a Goddess has noticed you personally, even if only subconsciously. She has seen your works and judged them good. You gain a dot in the Mantle of Bastet, and an additional dot in Mantle for every dot of Status (maximum of five).

    OOO: At this level there is no benefit other than the increased Mantle, one of the drawbacks of not actually being an organisation.

    OOOOO: Bastet loves you so much that you gain some measure of cat like nature, a goddess warps the world with her attention alone: You can see in the dark, and gain 9again on perception rolls. You also get 9again on any athletics rolls and halve damage from a fall.

    Kindnesses

    Bastet rewards those who show love to cats. There's so many ways to show love that her blessings fall upon many different people.

    The most common people who receive Bastet's blessings have adopted cats into their Family. They play with them, love them, and pet them for hours on end. Family can be anyone, from young girls who sleep with a cat wrapped around their feet to old women with one last companion left among the living.

    Free Speciality: Animal Ken (Felines)

    Veterinarians are blessed for their hard work and dedication. They underwent years of education and training to master the skills they needed to help cats. Most work as vets, healing the sick and carefully nursing injured cats back to health. Others might be zoo keepers, own a pet shop or simply provide a home and food to dozens of stray cats.

    Free Speciality: Medicine (Felines)

    The rarest of Bastet's Beloved are almost as Feral as the cat's they adore. Among their number can be found rangers, explorers, hunters and even feral children who were raised by cats. They have won the respect of wild and dangerous cats, and that won them Bastet's respect.

    Free Speciality: Survival (Felines)

    Endowment: Mantle of Bastet (O – OOOOO)

    Bastet wasn't worshipped because she's cute and cuddly. The Ancient Egyptians held cats to be sacred because they dealt with vermin. They kept mice and rats from the fields and even hunted snakes.

    Bastet's Beloved rarely have to worry about vermin, not mice or... shall we say, vampires. When a cat judges a monster to be a serious threat to one of Bastet's Beloved (remember, cats are terrible gossips) the power of Bastet descends upon it. It's Strength and the damage rating for it's teeth and claws increase by Mantle. In additionally it's Size is treated as though it were Mantle points larger, both when calculating health and for other things like how hard it is to pick up or how heavy it is when it pounces upon a werewolf’s back. However it's physical size does not change.

    The usual rules for temporary health do not apply. Instead place the additional boxes to the left of the health track, when the blessing fades any damage in those boxes is miraculously removed.

    The Mantle also has it's uses outside of battle. It is added to all Social rolls involving cats and even a single dot grants the ability to talk to cats in a human like manner. In addition no cat will ever attack anyone with the Mantle of Bastet. A lioness might forcefully push the Beloved away from her cubs, or a lion might challenge a Beloved for control of the pride or to a friendly wrestling bout, but they will do so with their claws retracted. Exceptional circumstances such as mind control or aggression inducing brain damage (such as rabies) might require the cat to roll to resist hurting Bastet's Beloved. Cats add Mantle to those rolls, and to resist being dissuaded from their holy duties. Unprovoked aggression against a cat breaks this protection (if your character does attack cats, expect to justify to the storyteller how on earth she has a Mantle)

    The Mantle of Bastet exists in a strange quantum state of being both a supernatural ability and not a supernatural ability. As a Goddess Bastet’s more subtle influences aren’t supernatural so much as changing what is “natural”. Near one of her Beloved cat’s just are naturally strong enough to fight monsters, but it still looks weird to humans. Generally though, when a mechanic affects supernatural beings or supernatural powers, Bastet’s Beloved and their cats count as normal humans and the Mantel as a non-supernatural ability. Some powers are exception; for example they do trigger Unseen Senses keyed to the divine.

      To everyone who was confused about just why LostLight was so excited about a picture of a young girl holding a cat, here's what it signified.

      Back when Hunter: Mortal Remains was called Hunter: Monstrous Bastards I made a comment along the lines of "It's called Hunter: Monstrous Bastards not Hunter: Kitten Cuddles". I've completely forgotten the context of that comment but it took a life of it's own. I made a joke thread outlining a book Hunter: Kitten Cuddles. Then I decided it wasn't silly enough (it was basically a book on the non-Vigil side of hunter's lives) and tried again in a second thread presenting a lighter and fluffier hunter - one of the compacts was a babysitter's club for monsters and the "Conspiracy" of Kitten Cuddles was Bastet's Beloved.

      That joke group was surprisingly well received and remembered. Two years later and LostLight brought it up as soon as the words "talking cat" were mentioned in a hunter context. Thus I decided it was time to dig out my old notes and finish them of.


      The crazy cat lady in the sample hunters is, I think, the single most fun I had writing for Hunter. And that includes the time I made TF:V responsible for Disney Faeries. BTW The Hakolel stereotype is a reference to The Rabbi's Cat
