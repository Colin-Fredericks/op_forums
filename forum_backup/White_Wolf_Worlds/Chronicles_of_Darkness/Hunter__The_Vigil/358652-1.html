        Hunters are human and they will react with the same variation as any other human. Most will fear and react with violence to the supernatural, but they might form the occasional alliance of convenience and they most certainly will be unwitting pawns in the politics of the supernatural. After all, they know little about the supernatural and what mankind does not know, it fears. The violent reaction is made all the more likely since most supernaturals in the World of Darkness are actually evil or at the very least very dangerous to mankind and Hunters simply lack the skill and knowledge to recognize the different factions amongst the supernatural. Would you trust an ogre after just having rescued a bunch of kids from another one that was about to sell them to a Fae? Of course, being human also means that they can and will befriend supernaturals that have proven their trustworthiness although depending on the faction some combinations seem unlikely (vampires befriend the Inquisition springs to mind).

        Another is more whether the supernaturals would want to ally, let alone befriend, with the Hunters. In general mortals are weaker and ill-equipped to deal with the world of the supenaturals. To bring them into your world is exposing them to considerable risk. Of course, you could educate them, but will put them and you at another kind of risk. After all, your fellow supernaturals take a very dim view on revealing their secrets to mortals.

        In short in my campaigns, short term allies of convenience seems very likely to happen, but long term alliances seem extremely unlikely and more a one of a kind thing each with their own unique story and certainly kept private.

                            In my Hunter game I run monsters that are PC leveintelligent if its appropriate. Stupid monsters that are randomly and unpredictably violent don't live long in the World of Darkness before their own kind kill them off for risking exposure for the rest. This creates controversy about dealing with monsters: a Werewolf pack mowed down a group of protesters that a sorcerer had goaded to attack them. There's enough supernatural understanding between the Les Mysteries, Lucifuge and Malleus Maleficarum players that the Hunters know that the mob was possibly supernaturally urged and that the situation was tense and fast paced enough that another solution may not have been in the making. So some players are still advocating détente with this pack. Not all but they are well aware that a straight fight would be a disaster in which several of them would probably be killed and the Werewolves may have some role to play in managing the supernatural ecology. (A role they have cause to question after the Werewolves laughed off attempts to invoke ideas like sympathy for humans plagued by spirits in a tense stand off - they're a Pure pack.) The Les Mysteries player has also uncovered information that these Werewolves are placating an enormously powerful spirit with regular human sacrifice so its a very ethically messy situation. Some of the group agreed to patrol a local hotspot of nasty inter dimensional activity together with the Werewolves to clear their name of some nasty business the Werewolves suspect the Hunters of. Its not a happy situation for anyone but it is an alliance of sorts.

                                In short, yes it is possible. The biggest problem is both sides would need a very compelling reason to do so. Consider the hunter's point on view. Most weren't initiated into the World of Darkness by a nice mummy who just woke up and had a good day. No, they get introduced when their best friend forgets their name and after a particularly difficult fight, he punches his bets friend and drywall breaks off of him. Or they come home from a long day at work to find their wife no longer has any blood in her. Hunters usually have problems with making permanent peace with supernaturals because of how they came into their new job. They might come to an uneasy truce or even just a 'no killing each other' policy, but such things would not happen at anything above the cell level, unless you make your own compact, or inferno forbid, conspiracy. Now look at it from the monsters view. How fast does news spread that there is a peaceful, just hunter in the city? Good news never gets around. How fast do you hear a new hunter cell moved in and is beginning to kill your race? It spread like wildfire, as in either everyone knows about it or not a single soul is left alive to spread the news, which is news in and of itself, because bad news does spread. Each side has a large hurdle to jump over before lasting peace could be made, and it shouldn't be anything beyond a cell and a pack/cabal/throng/.....

                                While it is possible, it isn't likely. But you can use your ideas to shape how your cell acts. Lets say that your cell has been taking out cabals and all of a sudden, one of your members goes to his Sunday afternoon ritual with his college buddy and discovers he is a mage. Will he grab his salt from his trunk and take him out? Will he tell his cell and try to figure t out from there? Will he confront his friend about it? Will he not tell his cell to protect his friend? You could run with that and cause PLOT to happen. Your hunter is wolf-blooded, right? Did you tell your cell? Do you even know? How would he react to finding out some of his family are werewolves? What about catching one of them in his first change? What about you? What if you turn into a werewolf? How will the cell deal with that? These questions will help define you and your cell of their integrity and their determination. This makes for a less 'bag n tag' story, but a more though provoking chronicle. Try it out, or bring it up to your ST and see if he wants to try a morality hunter chronicle rather than a hunter story.

                                I'm letting one of my players run a hunter game and your idea is exactly his. He created a compact called the Kindheart Society where hunters only hunt the monsters who act like monsters and stop other hunters from hurting the "innocent". Part of the chronicle is deciding where that line is and what you are going to do about it. Meet the gray areas and try to keep your integrity. I'm playing a vampire hunter. It is his sole purpose, but he eventually discovered that a few vampires are just trying to get by, not causing any permanent harm to anybody. He still is not happy about it, but he is trying to hang onto his humanity and let them live. He doesn't trust them or even like them, but he can tolerate their existence. Due to PLOT and my agreement with ST, he became a vampire and now has to deal with his new existence. More stuff to think on instead of pulling out a dragon's breath trench gun and firing away.

As such I would generally treat every cell of Hunters (and the supernatural groups for that matter) as being particularly unyielding until proven wrong by the PCs. I certainly sometimes have to remind my players that they might be well aware of the intricities of e.g. Werewolf society, all their characters know about it, is how those creatures are depicted in books and movies which is mostly as murderous maneaters influenced by the moon who can appear to be very humane and friendly during the day. So yes, on paper cooperation makes a lot of sense because we as readers see a lot more of the picture than any inhabitant of the WoD ever does. In practice I would consider it being extremely rare, one of a kind situations specific to a particular situation and more often than not after hard work by one or more PCs

                                                Changelings and Geists in particular can be fairly amenable to a teamup.

                                                Changeling and Hunter are my two favorite lines, so I like coming up with ways to mix them...

                                                One of the big things about Changelings is that they aren't just capable of having positive relations with humans, but if you want to keep high clarity, it's basically essential. (And by "positive", I mean symbiotic, cooperative, both-sides-wish-for-the-best-for-each-other type relationships.) They don't drink blood or have dangerous hunts or socially disruptive claims on locii to gain energy. A changeling can harvest entirely from the hedge, pledges, or dreams without having any apparent negative effect on human society, and even an emotion harvest is only harmful when someone is careless enough to overtly harm, terrorize, or seduce just for the glamour. Most "good" changelings get along with humanity and want to protect it in general, and the fae creatures they are instinctively aligned against (hedgebeasts, privateers, clarity-bankrupt changelings that are a danger to everyone around them, Gentry) are generally harmful to humanity as a whole.

                                                Hunters are ironically potentially a Changeling's best friend. They know about the supernatural, so you only have to hide the parts of your supernatural life you have sworn to keep in confidence (and can simply say "I swore not to reveal that",) they tend to carry weapons, including cold iron weapons, and could be a handy way to beat back a True Fae, and you would generally have a pretty good idea that they'd be just as opposed to privateers and True Fae as you are. Compacts and conspiracies can also help with forging documents to get you a new life.

                                                Changelings, meanwhile, have a lot to offer if kept friendly. Magic healing is an obvious one - Warmth of Blood works wonders, and Faerie Healing or Burden of Life (if the changeling is willing to risk taking on the damage of a comrade in active combat) would easily keep hunters on the vigil, and out of the morgue. They also have access to loads of information-gathering magic. Many types of changelings can just use kenning or 1-dot contracts to see all sorts of supernatural stuff, and onieromancy and supernatural research lets them suss out difficult-to-find banes for fighting other supernaturals. Any hunter who trusts a changeling deeply enough to let them in would also be able to benefit from the dreamshaping of a changeling. A changeling psychologist could use onieromancy to help ease the traumas of the vigil, and scour nightmares into healing dreams or willpower dreams. (I like having house rules for onieromancy making morality protection/recovery easier.)

                                                The biggest problem would be keeping Hunters seeing the difference between the "good guys" and "bad guys" of the fae world. It's not like you can whip out your character sheet and prove you've got high clarity, (except for that one Malleus Mallificarum thing that lets you outright see a person's morality) and even if you could prove it, there isn't any proof it won't plummet. And of course, the only changeling most people see are the ones that blow their clarity and start doing reckless stuff in public. Fetches are an especially difficult sticking point, as murdering a stick-man that looks human can be just as disruptive to society as murdering a real human... which is all the more reason changelings need to think carefully about just shooting fetches by reflex. An aware fetch could easily get on a hunter's good side, as well. Changelings more willing to work with non-hostile fetches would probably go further working with Hunters.

                                                Mortal Remains also overtly mentions that some compacts and conspiracies are friendly to Changelings in general:

                                                VALKYRIE doesn't overtly work with changelings, but they see them as a threat to national security only in individual cases, not as a group, and they happily supply them forged documents. Simply telling VALKYRIE you're still an American citizen and still love your country, and want to help stop the threat of vampires or whatever as much as they do, and can supply information, access to goblin markets and the hedge if need be could easily result in at least a small-scale alliance. Besides, privateers are a direct threat to humanity, so VALKYRIE could help fight a changeling's fights for them, and still chalk it up as a win. You could potentially even have a whole freehold inside a VALKYRIE base made up of ex-military or other government employees who simply turned themselves in to their superiors when they escaped the hedge after being abducted while in the line of duty. (I remember Dogs of War also had something similar, but with werewolves...)

                                                The Union is potentially capable of letting changelings ally with them simply because they share common goals: Having a secure neighborhood for their families. Ogres and Wizened may have a deeper connection to the blue-collared types of the Union, as well, and may well be co-workers with the hunters. Just so long as they prove stable, and they can help point out the real threats, then only the fetch and attempts to blend back in with their old lives might cause turmoil.

                                                Ascending Order outright states in its section that they deal with changelings, if not officially, for bits and parts of hedge beasts and hedge fruit to make potions from. In fact, the biggest point of contention may be how ethical and patient the AO is: If they want to strangle their golden goose to cook it up for another of their potions, instead of relying upon the changeling to lay down their golden hedge fruits.

                                                The Lucifuge and Malleus Mallificarum officially don't care about fae, or at least, don't see them as major threats. It's not hard to see one that has something to offer striking up a short-term alliance of convenience that might grow into a longer-term one... if the Changelings are VERY careful to keep proving they are trustworthy, of course. A Catholic changeling in particular might have success with some Malleus Mallificarum cells from cultures that have folkloric traditions of heroic fae. If the changeling takes mass, confesses their sins, and helps take the fight to the Devil, and says it's all to regain their soul that was scourged by the demons beyond the hedge, that may well be enough to prove they're on the right side.
