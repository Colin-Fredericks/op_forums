              Well, let's look at one of the more common ideas involving skin-thieving in RL folklore: the wearing of an animal's pelt to attain or steal its power(s). Thus, the core ethos could follow that structure, in that everything has power and a name, and attaining one or the other grants you a measure of control or power over that thing. Gain both, and you are free to use the guise and identity of that other thing as you please. They are like a reverse version of the W:tF hosts — instead of gaining a fleshy shell to multiply, survive and wreak havoc on the world because it is their nature, they gain fleshy, sometimes even ethereal forms to control, keep order and, if need be, give their lives for whatever purpose you have in mind.

              This doesn't put them above using the guises of other humans for their needs. Think of the Faceless Men from Game of Thrones if you need a comparison.

                  Hmm, I'd take inspiration from the comic Crimson and the Red Hood's Revenge: the Red Hoods, signified by their reversable red hoods, red hoods while hunting, but wolf pelt on the inside, when reversed they take the wolf's power for their own.

                  Once there was a young girl in a red hood, and the big bad wolf ate her grandmother, fed the girl granny's flesh, and took her in her grandmother's bed. They say he consumed her, but when it was over, his skinless corpse was thrown in the river weighted with rocks, and she left with a new skin on her shoulders and a child in her belly.

                  The core of the red hoods is said to be that girl's descendants, but who can say, after all this time? They recruit from those victimized by werewolves and other feral monsters, give people the chance to take their destinies back, claim the powers of a monster for their own,

                  When their hoods are red, they hunt using hexcraft, the secrets of the spirits, begged, borrowed, and stolen, but wolfhooded they bay at the moon and hunt as a pack.

                  Are you a legacy? Taught how to hunt since you were old enough to walk, taught hexes at your grandmother's knee? Are you a wolfblood, paying back your own family's betrayal with magic and silver? Were you a victim? You're family devoured for essence or blood while the horror broke your mind?

                          I may have to break the endowments up in the same path that Knights of St George has them. One faction is geared towards being bloody bastards (Their newly sharpened predatory instincts demand to hunt something. Uratha, Mortals, even other Hunters are all on the list) and focuses being able to change into predatory shapes (Bears, Wolves, Eagles, etc),

                          Another is dedicated to peeking into the Shadow and dealing with spirits, while the last is weird...

                          For the last one, I'm toying with the idea of making it hive orientated. In the natural world, there is social order in crows, herd animals, and insects so their endowments would be up to the gills in being able to command minions and bringing to bare collective power upon enemies that piss them off. Much akin to what a Mummy Cult has but much more low powered.

                          The theme I might want to go with is the overall feel of constant bickering within the Compact. There are real ideological differences that are constantly expressed and the animal instincts in a lot of the members only amplify this trait. It may very well be that the lower status people that carry the weight of carrying the structure of the compact as the elders are much more inclined to rip each other apart rather than talk like human beings.

                                                          Personally I'd see them as a Tier 2 organization, similar to Network Zero, and with as much potential of elevation to Tier 3.

                                                          There are three ways I could see the organization:

                                                          The Primals: These are the Skin-Theives who claim the power of spirits to gain the might and cunning of a primeval era, a time when outside the settlement was full of predators for the hairless apes. Primals take on the form and might of spirits as a way to even the battlefield between Man and the things that go bump in the night, as a way to feel the strength of the storm and the swiftness of cheetah, and as a way to enforce their will on the neighboring human tribes. These are the ones whose teachings center on upholding both the Vigil and the Gauntlet.

                                                          In modern day there are a few Primals left, though even one with the combined might of Elephant, Rhinoceros, and Gorilla would be cut down by a handful of 6 year olds with AK-47s. The traditions passed down from father to son and mentor to student leave them prepared for when technology fails and the last electric lightbulb dims, and the predators return.

                                                          AKA: Regressives, Beast-men, Goons, Jungle Watchmen, Children of Gaia
                                                          Concepts: Ultra-survivalists, tribal protectors, stalkers of the night


                                                          Givers: These Skin-Theives study, absorb, and observe spirits to gain an awareness of an object or environment only appreciable to those who have existed as that object or environment. "To become a thing is to know a thing, and to assume its form is to begin to understand its existence" is a favored aphorism of theirs, and guides their philosophy on shapeshifting. With each spirit consumed and their existence studied from the inside, they feel they honor the spirit by fulfilling its nature while taking its form.

                                                          Givers in the modern day seek to understand the evolving interaction between Man and Nature, now that Man has imposed such change upon Nature. Their traditions teach of ministering both to spirits and Man, to guide the two into a symbiotic relationship. Teaching Man of their place as an equal member of Nature as opposed to master of it, and teaching Spirits patience at the complex, strange, and aggressive species that so often is blind to them.

                                                          AKA: Watchers, Receivers of Wisdom, Students of Gaia
                                                          Concepts: Zoologists, Scientists, Shaman


                                                          Neolutionists: These Skin-Theives show that the only constant is change. While their name is strictly modern-day, the philosophy of the Neolutionists is the original, from the very first time the hominid brain could begin to think: that of seizing their own destiny. These Skin-Theives hunt and consume spirits to change towards their own personal ideal of perfection. Most Neolutionists tend towards places of creation and change, hoping to integrate the fluctuating natures of like spirits into their own. Some enterprising Neolutionists bargain with more powerful examples of these spirits for use of their Numen instead of trying to reverse-engineer the abilities.

                                                          Each Neolutionist has their own ideal they seek to attain, but almost all agree it involves more than the body they were born with is capable of. Some of the more experienced Neolutionists can seem like something out of the mind of Lovecraft or Giger, though these are thankfully few, far between, and tend to disappear before long. If they disappear into the Shadow, are killed off by Hunters, are descended upon by other Neolutionists, or ascend to another plane of existence, nobody can say for sure. The actual effect of a Neolutionist's 'activities' on their genetic descendents is similarly up for debate, though certainly more than a few have guided their own development with an eye for passing on traits.

                                                          AKA: Freaks, Progressives, Loonies, The Clear-Eyed, Chrysali,
                                                          Concepts: Body-modifier, bleeding-edge technologist, Renaissance Man

                                                          Once is happenstance. Twice is coincidence. Three times is enemy action. Four times is Infrastructure.
