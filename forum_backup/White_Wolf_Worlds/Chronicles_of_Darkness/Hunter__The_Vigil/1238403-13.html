            Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
            Does the Code or the Vigil let's Hunter kill Human without suffering a Breaking point?
            Depends on your Code

            Maybe you have a code that says that a monster is anyone that hurts other people, so a mundane serial killer or a cop could work

            Maybe you have a code that says no witness, so you might kill a memeber of network zero

            Maybe your code tells you to kill a monster no matter what, so to kill a mage, you might car bomb a city block

            That being said, while the code will protect you form a breaking point, it can also cause breaking points. |

            Killing someone and then finding out their innocent
            A touchstone is a witness
            Your carbombing didnt work, and you instead realized you just killed a lot of people

            The Code is a tool for Storytelling a roleplaying, make it your own.

                            Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                            What is the benefit of joing a Compact rather than a Conspiracy?
                            More freedom and less additional pressure on your Integrity due to institutional mandates and secrecy. With a compact, the most you have to hide from your cellmates in a potential general sense is that there's an organized collection of hunters working together; conspiracies bring the kind of occulted poison-politics to hunter society that lodges bring to werewolves.
