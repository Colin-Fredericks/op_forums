        90. (Loyalists of Thule, but works for anyone). A coven of witches has stolen an archive of notes from one of the original Thule occultists, a record of an encounter between the Ahnenerbe and one of the Arisen in North Africa. The men at the time considered this immortal 'superman' from an ancient Empire (an Indo-European barbarian who recieved the Rite of Return) as proof positive of their Aryan Atlantis theory. These modern witches are hoping to hunt down this creature and reawaken it into their service. You're looking at a bunch of Neo-Nazis who might have a demigod on their side if you don't intervene /very soon/.

            91) You've had vampire's threaten your family, ghosts try to possess you, but somehow none of them just bug you like Norman. Your dull, friendly but annoying neighbour. What the hell is going on?

Then the "impossible freak accident" turned out to be an entire team of vampires cutting break lines, loosening safety barriers and planting hypnotic suggestions rather than a magical curse. The flying pillar of fire that blew up the museum and stole ancient Egyptian artefacts was followed back to a mad scientist's lab.

            Something big is going down, and it seems like everybody is trying to make sure somebody else is looking suspicious when it happens.

                93)You screwed up. The ritual worked, and the monster that everyone thought was unkillable is finally dead. Except that his 'monstrous-ness,' his supernatural powers, they didn't disappear. A few days ago, some white-collar nobody killed his jerkass boss in a manner you've seen before. An hour later, the guy was back to being plain vanilla human again.

                Last night, a gangbanger used those same powers to knock over a liquor store (as in 'onto it's side') and killed a half-dozen police officers before the powers abandoned him and he was shot and killed.

                Every three days, a seemingly random person within the city will gain the power of a dead monster for a short time.

                Oh, and that ancient text? Turns out, it didn't read 'cannot be killed.' It actually translates as 'must notbe killed.'

                        95) You've stirred up a hornet's nest. Quite literally. The local Werehornets didn't take very kindly to your organization snooping around their hunting grounds with lethal weapons. When hunters are found dead from dozens of bee stings that go straight through their body, the message is clear. Leave now, or we will have war!



                        96) 6 nights ago, a hunter went missing. 4 nights ago another went missing. 2 nights ago, yet another went missing. Something is picking off the hunter population every couple of days. Can the PC's solve the mystery within the next 2 days, or will another victim be claimed by this creature, whatever it might be?

                            97) A supernatural civil war is tearing the city apart. Normally, hunters would be all too happy to let the monsters take care of each other, but people are starting to notice. Last night, the first innocents were caught in the crossfire. This can't be allowed to go on. You have twice the amount of killing to do, unless you do the unthinkable; back one side and give them help to exterminate the other.

                            Tier 1: It's a pack of werewolves and a cabal of witches feuding over some sort of "energy well." You don't get it, you don't care, they need to stop.
                            Tier 2: An upstart among the vampires is rebelling against their political structure in town, and has made a deal with the devil to pull it off - literally. The Vampire King has resources on his side, but the Infernalist is utterly unafraid of is scything through the opposition with hellish fury.
                            Tier 3: The barrier between worlds has been torn apart, and the dead walk again; they rage against the faeries whose deals deprived them of eternal rest. Everyone knows you can't trust the fae, but the ghosts are single-minded in their pursuit of vengeance.

                            Remi. she/her. game designer.

                                                102) Something has severely begun to terrify rival hunters in your area. One was found hanging from a tree in the front yard, strangled with his own intestines. Another had her head torn off and impaled on a metal spike in her basement. A third was eaten alive and the bite marks match up with human teeth. The twist is that everyone of these deaths was the result of a suicide, something is driving the hunters to perform seemingly impossible suicides. Now the question remains: Do you want to hunt this thing down, or get the hell out of the city before it comes after you?



                                                103) Every hunter in the city is being affected, they're seeing the ghosts of people they could not (or would not) save from the monsters. These ghosts are out for blood too, claiming the hunters could have warned them of the danger but chose to keep it hidden. They mock them, asking how many more need to die before they finally blow the whistle, or the Ghosts claim their vengeance upon those who failed them.

                                                    104) Hunters from all across the city have abandoned the Vigil, seemingly without a reason. If you speak to them, they all seem a little bit dazed, and if the subject is brought up, they have no recollection of their past experiences with the supernatural and fervently deny such things exist. Two weeks pass before shit gets before the spiritual phenomena becomes too overwhelming to ignore.
