          Pretty much what Corvus Rex just said. Slashers are murder machines who will prey on any living thing around them, and who spiritually foul the area they're in through their depredations. A couple of sample storyhooks off the top of my head:

          Vampires:
          * The city is in an uproar over a Slasher who has stylized itself as a "vampire killer" - the Damned need to find out who the leak is, be it a human psychopath or a rogue fledgling, and plug it.

          * A mortal important to one or more vampires has been threatened, attacked or killed by a slasher. This impudence cannot go unpunished.

          * A slasher is going after ghouls and even fledgling vampires, attempting to murder them and drink their blood because of a delusional belief that this will give it superpowers and/or immortality.


          Werewolves:
          * Werewolves become aware of a slasher's presence in their territory when spirits of pain, fear, murder, death and bloodshed begin multiplying out of control.A Hunt must be called to stem this flow of corruption.

          * A choice territory becomes the site of a battlefield between a werewolf pack and slashers, either a Brute or Mask or a feral clan of Freaks and/or Mutants. The werewolves could be defending themselves, or invaders, or even just neighbors who never noticed each other until now.

          * A Locus of particularly dark nature has come into being in the private den of a slasher. Do the werewolves try to take the locus for themselves, tap it in secret, or snuff it out as "not worth the cost"?


          Mages:
          * Mages become aware of a slasher's presence and identity when Death magics bring them into contact with a growing number of ghosts killed by a slasher.

          * Mages attempt to research the possible magical origins of a Scourge Undertaking slasher, such as a Mask or a Legend. What can possibly go wrong?

          * Mages become aware of a Hallow, a sarira, or other magically important spot or location that is guarded by a Scourge Slasher.


          Changelings:
          * Pressure comes down on the Autumn Court when it appears one of their members has gotten so caught up in its fear-mongering that they have taken to murdering humans. The true culprit, unbeknownst to the changelings, is a Slasher who has picked up on the Autumn Court-spread legends and has made them key to its motif. Can the changelings find the truth before the Avenger manages to meld with the weavings of Fate and become a full-fledged Legend?


          Prometheans:
          * The throng encounters a promethean created by a mortal demiurge - a slasher, who becomes even more obsessed with making more "children" after meeting them.

          * A promethean discovers its attempts to create a child are being mimicked by a slasher that has seen its efforts at "harvesting" and "preparing" corpses.

          * A Cobalus promethean becomes a voyeuristic protector to a slasher, seeking to understand the true depths of human evil and depravity as represented by this monster.

          * A promethean or throng finds itself protecting humans from a slasher that gleefully turns its future victims against their would-be protector(s).

          * A promethean or throng contemplating murdering to produce progeny is given another look at themselves when they discover a slasher with similar motives.

          * A particularly depraved slasher's experiments with the corpses of his victims are producing pandorans.


          Sin-Eaters:
          * Sin-eaters realise a slasher is in the area when hordes of ex-victim ghosts come flocking to them. They have to stop the slasher or be driven mad.

          * A sin-eater's geist recognizes a slasher as responsible for its original death and demands revenge.

          * One or more sin-eaters died at a slasher's hands, either before or after the Bargain. It's payback time.


          Mummies:
          * A mummy realises that a slasher has infiltrated their cult - or that one of their cultists has turned into a slasher.

          * A nomadic mummy's cult awakens its master for protection when their travels bring them through the territory of one or more feral slashers.


          Demons:
          * A demon makes a Soul Pact for the life of a slasher - do they continue with the murderous original's ways, or do they try and cling to sanity at the cost of eroding their precious Cover?

          * Someone a demon cares about becomes victimised by a slasher.


          Anyone:
          * A slasher with a penchant for prolonged torture has taken you as a captive. Escape or fight your way free.

                                      That said, one thing a Storyteller has to accept by running Slasher vs. Supernatural is that, if it comes down to a fight, Supernaturals have a lot of ways of making things dead. Especially those who've been beefed up by 2e.

                                      My advice is to embrace this going in. Don't get too attached to your Slasher, and don't be surprised if your vampires or werewolves decide that they want to rip your Leatherface or Michael Myers or Freddy apart once they corner him. It's called Catharsis, and will probably be more popular with your players than just dues ex machinaing him/her out of there. They'll remember the story much more fondly, and even if they do talk happily about how they ripped the Slasher apart, they'll also be more willing to fondly recall how you frightened them with him. As opposed to mocking you for running a lowbrow Slasher Movie expy and how they all hated it.
