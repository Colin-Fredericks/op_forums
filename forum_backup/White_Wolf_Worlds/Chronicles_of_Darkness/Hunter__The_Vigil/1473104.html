    Conspiracy Theory: Task Force: VALKYRIE

    01-23-2022, 04:42 PM
    Oh boy here we go...

    There are few conspiracies worthy to that name as VALKYRIE. I mean, sure, as I mentioned before, all of the core conspiracies do relate to some kind of a real life conspiracy theory, even though they often play around with it in order to get all of the racism, xenophobia and antisemitism out (which, by all accounts, is no easy feat). Still, there is something with the American Government which just screams "CONSPIRACY THEORY". I think that most people do not have a complete trust in their government- but for an outsider, the amount of mistrust that American citizens seems to have towards their own governmental system makes you wonder how the heck the US is still a thing, as it feels a lot like this whole system was meant out of fearing the idea of someone holding too much power. Again, I do not claim to have deep knowledge of American culture and history, but again, there does seems to be some strong sense of paranoia towards the secret services radiating from the stereotypical averaged American- and honestly, I am not sure I can blame them. The US government did some very shady things in the past, and it makes sense that it would leave its effect on the population. Again, it is not like I have much of a reference, but where I live, the idea of our own secret agencies tracking our every step and listening to our phone calls is not something which makes use paranoid- we just kinda assume that it happens and shrug. It is not like they'll know anything that Facebook and Google don't know, after all.

    Ahem. Aaaaanyway, I think that this is part of how things are reflected in TFV. From the real life conspiracies about the Men in Black, there is always a sense of dread about them- a sense of fear. There are here to take your memories away, brainwash you, hide the truth, make sure that people WILL NOT KNOW to keep them silent and compliant. Again, I try to think about it from local eyes and really it is hard for me to feel that same sense of dread. In here, I think that the averaged citizen actually likes the secret services- they are viewed as cool, being almost superheroes. I think that some people trust them more than they trust the government itself. I am also pretty sure that if it was the CofD, and there would have been a VALKYRIE like analog in existence, I am pretty sure that people would more talk about them in the same manner of "those cool guys who do those extra secret things which we technically don't know that exist but come one, my sister's boyfriend served there for six years and told me everything about it". So... yeah, really not the American version of the Men in Black.

    On the other hand, TFV is also not very much the standard Men in Black- well, maybe more like in the movies where they are meant to be basically the good guys. Sure, they use misinformation and hide secrets and all, but they are still go around and kill some very dangerous monsters and protect innocent lives. There isn't much of sense of evil in TFV, even though there is still that lingering sense of mistrust- that's because in the end, TFV are foot soldiers. They are field agents. They are the ones who go around and get s*** done. Really, more soldiers that insidious secret agency which spies after your every step for unknown purposes... Now, the higher ups in TFV- that's a whole different can of worms. You are meant to suspect the higher ups. You, as a agent, should think to yourself "am I being manipulated?". That sense of distrust that the common American citizen in the CofD feels towards the mysterious Men in Black is what the common TFV hunter seems to feel towards the leadership of their own organization. They don't know who actually gives them the orders, or how their weapons work, or who funds the organization. They don't know who knows that the conspiracy exists- only that the vast majority don't. The RFID chips meant to activate their Endowments? Yeah, those totally do not track their exact location and state. Even more than other conspiracies (with except Cheiron), being a TFV agent means that you should feel paranoia, that you should feel manipulated, that someone or something is pulling the strings- the text is very supportive of such notion.

    Not that it stopped people from ignoring it.

    I mean, I can get it? People want to have big guns and use them without the baggage of a conspiracy, and it is fine. Also, yeah, people are used to have that sense of distrust towards the government when it is just a bunch of regular people- they just assume that shady things which VALKYRIE may require you to do is part of the general government doing shady things. As such, from what I have gathered from the fandom across the years (which I am not claiming to be an expert to take it for a grain of salt), TFV was relatively popular- just a cool American agency with super tech that fights monsters, Men in Black films style. Besides, the fact that unlike the rest of the organizations VALKYRIE was using SCIENCE to fight the darkness and not some dubious supernatural abilities (which some people are not a fan of), VALKYRIE looked like a relatively "free" organization. They didn't had some strange magic which required them to have some connection a supernatural entity, they weren't "corrupted" by monsters or something like. They were humans doing shady human things while overall killing monsters and covering up supernatural incidences. Nothing too weird here, move along, move along.

    And then C&C came, and all Hell broke loose.

    Task Force VALKYRIE, that one conspiracy that had no relation to anything supernatural, ended up being a puppet of the worst type of monsters- vampires. Sure, that's NOT what the text said or tried to say, but that's the meme that eventually came out of it. No matter how many times people tried to explain that vampires funding TFV does not mean that they control it, people still came under the impression that vampires are now the true "secret masters" of the conspiracy. I am pretty sure that if that reveal was today, people would have been singing It Was Vampires All Aloooong. For most people, it looked like it came out of nowhere- suddenly, out of the blue, the most mundane of organizations was discovered to be infested with blood suckers. Never mind that we actually had hints towards it across the books. I mean, sure, Night Stalkers may have had the most obvious hint, but their own original writeup talks about how, during their operation in Europe, they captured (among other things) "more vampires than they suspected could have existed". You don't just put such sentence in here if you don't intend of using it later. Besides, it is very much clear that revealing that information was meant to be used to catalyst inter-conspiracy espionage Chronicles where hunters find out about the vampires and need to decide if they should take them down, and if so how. Very much like the Hydra/SHIELD plot in Winter Soldier. Still it doesn't matter- whether there were hunts about it or not, it felt like the fandom, in general, didn't like that reveal- and if that's the case, what can you do with that information?

    Well, a bunch of people have just ignored it- which is fine. C&C's material is generally viewed as optional even in the CofD's standards, and if you want to ignore the whole vampire subplot then you should. Others have suggested a form of a more "balanced" approached, where TFV is not only funded and influenced by vampires, but also by mages, werewolves and basically any major faction of supernatural beings which is interested in stability, as well as a group of humans to manage those affairs. Basically, the Cryptocracy before the Cryptocracy was cool. Again, valid approach which could make sense under the assumption that the balance of power between monstrous factions should stop any one of those from taking over the organization. The mkost creative explanation was "Well, that said vampires- but they didn't say which vampires" which ended up with those funding the conspiracy being VII at best and Abraham Lincoln at worst (yeah, I KNOW it is a movie reference, but I haven't seen that film or even know how it is called, and it felt like a bunch of people actually liked that idea). And while everyone tried to get what to do with all of the vampire problem, I was more curious about something else in VALKYRIE.

    Why they have that name?

    I mean, sure, it sounds fancy and all, and secret agents generally have fancy names for their task forces and tools and such- but you can't ignore the fact that this name is, well, Norse in origin. German, more exactly. Not only that, it is not just the name of their organization- basically the a bunch of Advanced Armory is named after object of Norse mythology. Why to use Norse mythology as code names? Heck, why some of their Endowments are actually based around it- you can say "Advanced Armory as SCIENCE" all you want, but then you have freaking mistletoe bullets which kill vampires because this plant was used to kill Balder by Hod (and Loki, of course). There is something fishy going here- and things become even weirder when you consider the circumstances in which TFV was created. Oh, sure, they existed ever since Abraham Lincoln was killed (but not in the way you think- which, again, is part of why people liked the "Vampire Lincoln" explanation), but the TFV got its name during WWII. Around the time in which they captured the vampires and the other monsters which worked with the Third Reich. A state well known for how some of its high ranking members had some very unhealthy obsession with the occult- especially with things which aligned with their perception of the "true German culture", the proud Vikings and Norse people of old. And then you have a group from the other side of the war using such terminology which is discovered to be controlled by vampires which even chose a very, very interesting name for themselves. Valkyrie- the Chooser of the Slain, the ones to choose heroic warriors among the dead.

    Oh, if you hated the vampire twist beforehand, your gonna despise it now. Those are not just vampires which fund TFV.

    Those are Nazi Vampires.

    Are you still here? Good, let's carry on.

    Here is the thing- we know about Operation Paperclip. The US government have offered Nazi scientists escape in exchange for them sharing whatever they knew about the science and technology of Germany. Controversial, yes, but it happened. Now, this is the Chronicles of Darkness, and you have a situation in which TFV captured- never stated to kill, only to capture- a bunch of monsters, some of which are outright stated to be Nazi (I'm looking at you, daksha. No one is missing you and you know it). And now, you have a organization sent to capture a bunch of monsters and being rebranded as VALKYRIE during the time people hated the German, and you know that the most populous of those monsters- the vampires- still maintain a strong foothold in the organization even today. Let's be honest here- the CofD loves using pulp culture fiction and tropes, and the idea of the Nazis having some strange and occult superweapons which the US wanted is a big trope. It makes sense that the vampires, with their money and influence, have managed to not only get out alive from this situation, but they have done so by offering their "help"- they have money, resources, technology and knowledge, and they will be happy to let you use them. They'll just stay here, offering you advice, use their connections for you, help you get new and improved tech. You are in control, they are just here to serve you. Oh, governmental budget? That's not enough to fund your big weapons- here, take some more. Just do them a favor here and there- like, this vampire with a target around their head? He is important if you want things to move smoothly. You need them alive. And this vampire? She means trouble, you better put her down. Oh, you think I'm asking for too much? Here, take some more money for your weapons- I know a scientist who can develop this new tech you want. Oh, you don't want to do me a favor? No problem- but it would be a shame if your extra funding will get... cut short.

    I mean, it makes sense. TFV was a young organization without knowledge or resources. They needed help. They needed funding. All they had to do was to keep those bloodsuckers alive and squeeze them for all they worth. The agency should have remained in control- the vampires are simply a resource, nothing more, nothing less. That's how it should have been- but it didn't. Now, the vampires infest the conspiracy's higher ranking, and influence the decision making process. Oh, they are not in control- but they are there, and they know how to get what they want, usually. And worse- you need them, and they know it. At this point, TFV very much depends on them- do you how much the development and construction of new Advanced Armory costs? you can't just get the funding cut. And besides, yeah, they are evil bastards- but it is a price you need to pay in order to keep the Vigil going and America safe. Just make sure that the low ranking agents won't find out. They won't understand.

    Hey, I did told you it is a Hydra/SHIELD situation right?

    Ok, so we have vampires with some (hopefully previous) Nazi affiliation that put some big money in VALKYRIE- but what do they want? I mean, sure, having a voice in a big agency which deals with supernatural affairs in the strongest country in the world is very useful saying the least, but this is a Conspiracy Theory thread- it can't be the only explanation. There has to be something more in here- and I think there is. For that reason, I am going to first turn to the attention to their own Endowments- Advanced Armory.

    You may ask yourself "what there is to discuss about Advanced Armory? It's SCIENCE". And to which I will say "no, it's not- it's technology", and there's a huge difference about it. Science refers to the set of methodologies and theories which we use to describe our theories- using Relativity in order to accurately use GPS systems? That's science. Quantum computers? Science. Nuclear weapons? Science. Advanced Armory, however, goes reaaaaaaaaally fuzzy with their "science". On, sure, they have a lot of big guns and grenades and whatever else, but then they give us some really weird tech which you must ask yourself "are you sure it's science?". I am not talking just about things like the Hod Rounds- but also things like Gatekeeper Device or Etheric Rounds. Those Endowments do things which are not covered by the current scientific method- but they are well defined by occult. Oh, sure, you can just throw Clark's Third Law here and be done with it- but that's the point. There is no scientific theory within the CofD which allows one to manipulate supernatural abilities. On the other end, we know that such theory does exist- Deviant, for example. We do know that technology, plain old technology, can interact and even break the soul- but the theory behind the Divergence is ill defined at best. Technology can be applied to supernatural phenomena even without the technology itself being supernatural in order- but you need to have a working theory which describes that. By having such theory, you can develop technologies which would apply it, allowing you to interact with the supernatural world through plain old electricity and explosives. Hod Rounds is basically the most direct example of this- there is a theory which allows mistletoe to hurt vampires, and TFV uses their tech to do so, and this is why it is still Advanced Armory. Heck, maybe the whole RFID chips thing is also deruved from the same theory- technology which would make sense and work perfectly to secure Advanced Armory if and only if you were to actually include the supernatural within the theory- much like how gravity will work great if you'll just add tons of matter which you can't see or feel in any way, shape or form. Basically, that's the trick- TFV have developed a theory which allows them to use mundane objects in order to use and interact with supernatural entities. And why is it important, you may ask?

    Because if that's true, if the true secret of TFV is their theory and not the technology, if Advanced Armory refers to the appliance of a theory and not to having big guns, then what stops those Endowments from being older than they seem?

    Hahaha, you thought that we are not talking about an ancient conspiracy in a conspiracy thread. OF COURSE WE ARE TALKING ABOUT ANCIENT CONSPIRACY. Have you noticed what we are working with here?

    Oh, sure, VALKYRIE are not ancient- they have been around only since 1865 in their original incarnation and 1944 in their current one- but the theory, the Endowments, those may very well be old- potentially, very old. Some part of me is tempted to even claim that the theory may have been first applied by the Nibiru, but I already have my own version of those which I like, but it is still a possibility if you want to go full gunzo. But at any case, this theory is probably older than TFV, being derived from notes and information gained through the supernatural equivalent of Operation Paperclip. It may have been around during Nazi Germany, or it could be that the notes were first develops by the Americans and the used the knowledge from the defecting scientists (and monsters) in order to fill the gaps. It could be a concept which appeared here and there across history, applied with some success depending on the limitations of the era's technology- Hod Rounds probably had older form, other Advanced Armory may have had their own much more primitive appliances, which were far less useful and much more dangerous to use. Most, however, probably remained in as theoretical constructs, just like the sketches of Da Vinci and other dreamers across the ages. They KNEW it was possible, they understood the theory- but they couldn't have built it, they couldn't have constructed it. They understood how the occult worked, but lacked the tools to make their dreams into reality. But as time progressed, science and technology advanced as well- and eventually, technology came to the point were it was possible to bring those dreams into reality, to create the tools meant to fight the darkness using the abilities of Man. To use human will and craft in order to control the occult and fight the occult. Apply the theory of the supernatural through technology.

    And doesn't that ring any bells?

    I mean, think about it this way- Task Force: VALKYRIE was technically given its name BEFORE they captured the vampires. Their name, Choosers of the Slain, resonates well with the idea of capturing those who have died yet were found worthy in order to help the eternal war- and the slain, in this case, are vampires. And If Advanced Armory indeed revolve around technology utilizing the supernatural for humanity's benefit, of taking over the occult and make it into weapon through the act of human will, then doesn't using vampires for the benefit of humanity is right found in their alley? Isn't it just a part of their theme- or perhaps it is something even greater than that. Perhaps those vampires have a role to fulfil- they are in VALKYRIE for a reason. Perhaps parts of that theory include something which needs vampires in order to achieved- a new technological advancement unlike any other. After all, vampires are very influential and have some strange sympathetic magic in their Blood, which seems to be able to interact with all kinds of supernatural phenomena. Maybe by using Vitae as a channel, VALKYRIE have a potential method which would allow them to enforce their will upon the supernatural itself? Of creating new, greater mechanisms of human glory, which would submit all worlds and all nature to the humanity's will and benefit? To achieve the last, final goal of humanity, to truly rule the world through iron and lightning and fire? Something very much unlike the God Machine- for even the God Machine's occult physics would be taken over by that mechanism of pure technological advancement, which could perfectly work once you include the occult as part of the theory, by turning the supernatural into natural?

    Let's just hope that no one will find out about the vampires beforehand- they may accidently think that the bloodsuckers are the ones in control. They seem to think so, at least.

    And that's it for our dear old VALKYRIE! Next time, the last Core Conspiracy, the one who got promoted from a Supplemental into Main one and caused a lot of controversy in the process- VASCU. Stay tuned.

          Operation paperclip is an interesting tie in for the vampires, I like it. And I actually never like Valkyrie being "SCIENCE" . Always made me think "I guess Null Mysteriis are just a a group of idiots that VALKRIE occasionally poaches from when they get something right"

          I do have a question regarding those vampires. Is it possible they want to achieve a different end game? Is it possible to say these vampires may not want any of the wider aspirations to be achieved, and so do what they can to distract VALKRIE from its true objectives? Or are many agents stuck doing grunt work while the REAL soldiers to spec ops?

          Overall, I don't have strong feelings for Valkrie, but great job on hitting a lot of themes this conspiracy has that tend to be overlooked. Really enjoyed the read, and look forward to our now freelance VASCU
