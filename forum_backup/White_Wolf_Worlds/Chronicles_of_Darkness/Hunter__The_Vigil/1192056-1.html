        The basic idea of a group that hunts monsters, thus saving people, but does it just because it's fun... that's an interesting take on the hunt. The name fits. Just leave certain aspects as things that happen when a sub-group goes bad. whether mundanely bad or slashers, rather than as anything most of AA would ever do or tolerate. They're not the only splat whose more extreme versions don't fit any PC I want to play.

        They're the risk-takers, thrill-seekers. Sometimes that endangers bystanders, but mostly themselves. They hunt for fun, but only creatures they think are evil/dangerous. They get carried away. They aren't careful enough in choosing targets. But those faults aren't confined to AA.

        They're the hunters who will still sleep with a vampire, knowing what she is, hoping to wake up alive with most of their blood in their veins. But they like the thrill of not being sure. And it's themselves they're risking (though of course that does endanger their friends, as they might come back Dominated or in Vinculum).

                        Sure, the Abby's most twisted members might need to be hunted themselves, but I always saw them as the "If we're gonna do it, might as well make it fun" variety of hunter...which, btw, still makes them creepy as hell.

                        Ashwood Abby in my games appear as sibling Fraternity/Sororities at the local college (I'm sure they have another, "grown up" organization outside of Greek Life, but it hasn't come up in my game), they view hunting as both High Jinx and a social service. Of course they're gonna have fun going out and taking on the baddies, if you're going to do it do it in style. They've actually come to the rescue of the PCs on at least one occasion (one of my players is a member), showing up as their Sorority Sister was being taken away by her father. One call from her boyfriend and 2 black Escalades pull up on the runway of the local airport and a half dozen perky "blonde bimbos" hop out packing enough heat to make the head of a major cartel and his personal team of body guards (who I wrote up as seriously dangerous) turned tail and ran.

                                                            Originally posted by Tessie View Post

                                                            Anything that's classified requires both the correct clearance and the need to know the information. The president has top security clearance, but that does not grant access to everything. There are many IRL things the president does not have access to. TF:V isn't any different in that regard.
                                                            This is false. The president does not have a "security clearance" in the traditional sense, rather the President is the ultimate source of all classification and has an intrinsic "need to know" based on position. Any fiction that uses the conceit of something being kept secret from the president that involves national security is just that, fiction. The only time something might be withheld is if the person occupying the office was a subject or target of a counterintelligence operation and informing them would compromise national security, but even then shit gets tricky.

                                                            tl;dr: IRL, there is nothing the President does not have access to due to its classification.
