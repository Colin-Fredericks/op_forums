                    Originally posted by sikker View Post
                    Mortals don't have powerstats. As such, hunters do not have powerstats. It's possible to houserule, but by the RAW, hunters are as susceptible to dominate, obfuscate and whathaveyou as any other mortal.

                    A number of hunter Endowments from conspiracies have ways around this under certain circumstances. I wrote a fan-Castigation ritual for instance that gave (10 - Morality) as a stand-in for a powerstat to resist supernatural mind effects.

                    Mirrors also suggests adding a Conviction powerstat to particularly strongwilled mortals.
                    This. By default, the only thing that sets hunters apart from ordinary mortals is their access to Risking Willpower and their ability to earn and spend Practical Experience. My hope is that Hunter’s Chronicle reboot will take this further by doing away with the “step five: apply the hunter template” part of character creation, and folding the things described there (“choose a Profession”, “choose a tier and, if appropriate, hunter org”) into other steps (mainly step one; but also the “choose your Merits” step). When I’m in the mood for powering up hunters, I do so by expanding the options that hunters can buy with Practical Experience, with Mirrors being my go-to book for new options. Particular favorites of mine are Skill Tricks, Forbidden Knowledge, the aforementioned Conviction stat (with some minor tweaks), new uses for Willpower (tied to Conviction), and various Heroic Mortal Boosts (described in Strange Dead Love). I see this as a way to gradually call into question a hunter’s humanity (without flat-out denying it) in much the same way Hunter’s Code does. This gradual transformation from mortal to monster has been a theme in Hunter from the get-go, with Slashers as the extreme that can be reached once a hunter ceases to be human.

                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                    Originally posted by Krobeles View Post

                                    This might be tangential at best to the starting topic, but how does the forum feel about Mages being considered as Mortals, for the purposes of powers? My rationale is that despite all their reality altering powers and knowledge, Mages are still just Mortals who can tinker with reality.                                     Personally I feel that Mages are Humans but not Mortals.

                                    A Vampire cannot sire a Mage, a Wolfblooded that Awakens will never become a Werewolf (though a Legacy might let them emulate it), and Demons take damage from attempting to use them as a Cover; thus they cannot be Mortals as they do not function as Mortals.

                                    But, at the same time, a Mage is wholly reliant on their Arcana for any special powers, unlike every non-human Supernatural. A Changeling without Contracts still has Kith and Seeming powers, as well as the ability to make Pledges; a Vampire is immortal by virtue of their Vampirism, no special Discipline required; Werewolves can shapechange regardless of their Gifts; and Going Loud is neither Embed nor Exploit for a Demon.
