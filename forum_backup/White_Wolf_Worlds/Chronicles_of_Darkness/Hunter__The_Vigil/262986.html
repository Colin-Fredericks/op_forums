      Hi! Welcome to the World of Darkness. Hunter's a great game, I hope you get a lot of enjoyment out of it. Since you put this in the Hunter subforum, it's possible that you might have missed something important, and I apologize if my guess is wrong: the first edition New World of Darkness gamebooks require the World of Darkness Core Rulebook, the one that looks like this. If you just have the Hunter corebook alone, you don't have the full ruleset.

      If you are, in fact, talking about the Core Rulebook, the character creation rules in the beginning of the book should tell you how many dots you have to distribute among Attributes and Skills. Each Attribute begins with 1 dot (the dots you have available to distribute do not include these free first dots) and can be increased to a maximum of 5. Each Skill begins with 0 dots and can be increased to a maximum of 5.

      Feel free to ask any other questions that come up!

                                      For Attributes you start with 1 dot in each of the nine, these are seperated into mental, physical and social, just like skills are. You then get 5, 4 and 3 dots to spend for the three categories.

                                      The 11, 7 and 4 dots are what you place in the skill categories. Whilst Attributes get a free dot in each, skills always start at zero dots.

                                      When you make a skill roll, it's generally done with an Attribute and that makes the number of dice you roll in your dicepool. If you use a skill you have zero dots in, the unskilled penalty is the dice removed from your Attribute which you would roll alone in that case to make your dicepool up.

                                      Hope this helps you out

                                              Attributes cost new dots x5, Merits are new rating x2, Morality is new rating x3, Skills are new rating x3, specialties cost 3xp and willpower costs 8xp.

                                              When you use experience to go up more than one level, you need to buy the levels between. This means if you're raising an Attribute you have at 2 to 4 dots, you need to buy it at 3 dots (15xp) and then at 4 dots (+20xp, for a total of 35xp from 2 dots). You couldn't just spend 25xp to boost an attribute from 2 dots to 5 for example. At least thats how I always read the experience costs for nwod, I could be wrong.

                                              In the God-Machine update there is no escalating cost and things have a straight cost thats the same for each level, but unless you have Mortal Remains, Hunter:the Vigil uses the standard nwod rules, at least until a Hunter 2nd edition comes out.

                                                      Yes for both, you need to buy each level before paying for the next. Say you got Resources 1 (a one dot merit) and you want to raise it up to three, then you'd need to spend 4xp to raise it to 2, then 6xp for 3, a total of 10xp to raise it from 1 to 3.
                                                      Skills are the same, if you got a skill at 1 dot, you pay 6xp for them to be 2 dots, then 9xp to get them to 3, etc. Buying one dot in a skill you don't have at all would cost 3xp.
