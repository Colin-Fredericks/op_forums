                  OK I'll start posting them, I'll have some questions and comments along the way too, would be great if people who know what they're talking about could chip in and tell me where I've screwed up

                  IF YOU ARE PLAYING IN THE DUNEDIN'S VIGIL LARP PLEASE DO NOT READ ANY FURTHER, THERE WILL BE MONSTERS IN HERE YOU DON'T KNOW ABOUT YET

                  The Black Dog of Gwyllgi

                  Inspiration mostly from: https://suite101.com/a/the-otherworl...ghosts-a384602

                  Int 3 Wit 3 Res 4
                  Str 5 Dex 4 Sta 4
                  Pre 5 Man 2 Cmp 3

                  Athletics (Run) 4, Brawl 4, Intimidation 4, Stealth 2, Survival 4

                  Size: 5 (human sized, this is a Big Dog)
                  Initiative: 7
                  Defence: 7
                  Armour: 1
                  Attack (Bite) 9 (+2L), Knockdown: On an exceptional success, inflicts the Knocked Down Tilt.
                  Attack (Syphon): Requires a successful Grapple and Hold. or an unconscious target. Inflicts 1 Lethal per turn, heals the same amount.
                  Speed: 5, running 15

                  Commentary:

                  So this is supposed to be a pretty straightforward Monster Bash, and is a bit of an introduction to the God Machine rules for my players (and myself!) since it's the first time we've used the system. I started with the Guard Dog from the WoD Core and beefed it up.
                  • According to the rules, the Defence should really be 8 (animals get the higher of Wits and Dex, not the lower) but this is an intelligent being, so I'm calculating Defence normally in this case.
                  • I invented the Knockdown bit of it's bite attack; it doesn't really accomplish that much but I thought it was pretty flavourful. Now that I look at it though, an exceptional success on an attack check both pretty unlikely (starts at 9 then subtracts the subject's Defence), and also will be deadly - since it'll do at least 7 damage anyway.
                  • When I ran this combat I completely forgot about the armour; It wouldn't have made a lot of difference as the PC's were all using Lethal weapons anyway with one exception; so the (usually) 1L they were inflicting would have been 1B instead anyway - right? (They were attacking it with knives, except one gunshot (3L) and one pretty good punch (2B which the armour would have reduced to 1B). If I understand how Armour works
                  • The Syphon is something else I knocked up; it's slow, but it's this creatures way of healing.
                  • I'm not sure about the speed; I want it to be fast, but I can't figure out the Speed entry for the Guard Dog in the WoD core (species factor + Str + Dex???), so I just made it fast when running.
                  Comments?

                  Cheers
                  Viperion

                              Something my players killed off last night:

                              The (little) Dragon
                              Int 1, Wits 3, Res 2
                              Str 4, Dex 4, Sta 3
                              Pre 3, Man 1, Cmp 2

                              Skills: Athletics 3, Brawl 4, Stealth 2, Intimidation 3
                              Merits: Fast Reflexes 2, Iron Stamina 2

                              Size: 4 Defence: 8* Initiative +9 Speed 4/12
                              Health: 7
                              Amour: 2

                              Attack pools: Claw 8 (+1L)
                              Sand spray Breath 4 (can be Dodged, but not defended against) - +1L; 3/day, 1 target, 20 ft.

                              Other pools:
                              Hiding 6
                              Intimidate (Str) 7 (Active)
                              Intimidate (Pre) 6 (Passive)

                              *The Defence is 8 because I counted this guy as an animal (highest of Dex and Wits instead of lowest), and in my game Defence is calculated off Brawl or Weaponry, not Athletics. In a normal game his Defence would be 6.

                              The Sand Spray is a breath weapon; I decided that you could Dodge (with a capital D), but otherwise it was a ranged attack so Defence didn't apply.

                              The active/passive Intimidate was basically how I decided to do "HOLY SHIT IT'S A DRAGON" - when people see it, it makes a "passive" Presence + Intimidate check. It can also take an action to do an "active" Strength + Intimidate. Again, I diverted from the book somewhat here; as written, Initimidate is not resisted. Is this right? It makes forcing people to do what you want really easy... So in this encounter I made it resisted versus Resolve + Composure (I could have had Composure as a negative to the check too but went with the "make a check" as I think it's more dramatic that way).

                              The fight went pretty well for the Dragon to start with; it scared off one PC, and then Sand Blasted another one and rushed him. He Dodged it's bite and it missed. Then there were a couple of rounds where it bit someone and got hit for a bit (Armour 2 was helping a lot against the people that were hitting it with fists...). Then one PC Grappled it (and got bitten next round when the Dragon won the Grapple check) and one guy punched it in the face. For SEVEN damage. It was pretty epic

                              What do you guys think of it? It was supposed to be a baby dragon, hence only size 4, and not too dangerous. I was expecting it to last a bit longer than it did, but it did have 4 or 5 PC's wailing on it so the outcome was pretty expected.
