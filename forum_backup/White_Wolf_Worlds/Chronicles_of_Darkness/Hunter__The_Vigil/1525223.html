    Nemeses and Adversaries [Organizational Horrors- Brainstorming]

    11-08-2023, 06:57 PM
    So one of the things I thought about while doing my AKD splat book long time ago was making a subsection of Night Horrors dedicated for that specific organization. While I played around with other concepts from that book (Sovereignty, Paragons, Chapters, Conspiracy-based Undertaking, Cabals, etc), this one was more or less was just left floating around, so I thought it might be a time to return and revisit that idea and see how it applies to all orgs.

    The basic idea behind those Horrors is that while every org has their own personal enemies, we could adapt those enemies into being a bit more "personal", tying them to the themes and philosophies that the conspiracies themselves express. While we could do it with singular Horrors or NPCs, I generally always preferred them taking the form of "species" or cultural groups, but that's just my personal preference. As such, I'm doing some brainstorming along those lines, so please let me know what do you think! First post will be about the Core Eight conspiracies, but hopefully I will discuss other orgs as we move along through them- Currently I plan on 4 such adversaries per Conspiracy and 2 per compact, but we'll see how things roll.

    Aegis Kai Doru: this one is more or less set, as I've wrote down the whole goddamn fan supplement for them in the first place, so basically I need to choose which Night Horrors are the most "in theme" for them. While I tried to make them all appropriate to a degree, there are some which I find as more interesting and connected to the AKD: the first are the Adonai Aretz, which are a "Hunter name" for the Baal Haddad, with the common twist of them not necessary being werebulls but using all kinds of livestock animals (but I am willing to maintain the Bull-only angle as it is very fitting to the AKD). The main reason I find them as appropriate is the combination of shapeshifters + bulls + the use of relics in the form of idols are all just a perfect fit for a AKD antagonist. The second are the Daktyloi (a revision of my Cabiri)- it is expected that a Arisen-like entity would serve as an antagonist to the conspiracy, and the twist of the Daktyloi being Lifeless mummies who are powered by relics due to a flawed attempt at recreating the Rite of Return makes them into basically living relics, which would again put them at odds with the conspiracy. In order to close the Witches angle, I originally gave them mechanics for the Bound under the name of the Gigantes, making them into mythical monstrosities trapped in their own labyrinth like worlds, which could bestow great power on those who are faithful to them, but I guess that if it doesn't scratch the itch something along the lines of the Hem Khet, the relic devourers, could work. Finally, the fourth spot I keep for the Agathodaimon, who serve as metaphysical agents of the Labyrinths built by the conspiracy, created through geomantic resonances and relic occult energies, turning them into angel-like beings.

    Ascending Ones: while the AO do not have any specialized enemies to the same degree as the AKD, they do have certain enemies with which they hold special connection. Vampires are obviously the first, through their connection to the Cult of Set. A form of vampiric blood alchemists sounds like the best fit for them, or potentially a form of alchemical blood demon that the followers of Set craft using their own blood sorcery? Could even be called the Typhoon Beast. Another possibility would be using Sekhmet as a model, with those horrors being regular people who fall under sudden vampiric frenzy which transforms them into monsters- but we could also just use the Brain Eating Ants as a vampiric entity related to the time of witchcraft as described in the Empty Pharaoh section. The other connection is Promethean, which would give us a nice way to tie the Djinn as a variant form of Qashmalim, adding some twist to their behavior such as them being bound to certain vessels, and potentially their ability to grant wishes or something along those lines. Mortal Remains adds Mummies as another enemies which the conspiracy had to deal with through the ages, and while using some kind of demon from Egyptian mythology could be cool, I thought about using something a bit.. different. The AO are a ancient conspiracy with presence all across the world- a Amkhat like monster produced through the poison-alchemy ritual of Gu sounds like something that could be interesting to explore through this project. Alternatively, the Gu might be a product of blood magic, while the Mummy niche will be filled by the sorcerous creation of the Ants. Finally, I think that the last adversary should be Prophets- the conspiracy looks after them, after all, seeking to recruit them to the conspiracy. Who knows what kinds of terrifying miracles they might work as they invoke the name of God?

    Cheiron: Cheiron have a few things which we may need to tackle to deal with them. First would be the Beast angle, specifically with Cheiron being the teacher of Heracles, them weaving different monster parts together as a parody of Kinship and how their Slasher section basically describe Heroes before they were a thing. All of this leads me to think about a Horror called Centaurs, who are amalgamations weaved completely from the dead flesh of monsters which are created to hunt down other monsters, as they are able to collect their organs and add them to theirs, and also are able to perform thaumatechnology on people without the fancy equipment of the conspiracy in order to "heal" them. The second Horror is probably filled by a Deviant related monster- either some sort of a deviant or a monster created to hunt them down. Maybe we could style it as Hemitheoi, the demigods which the conspiracy creates through its experiments with "ambrosia". After all, Cheiron was the one who made heroes, it makes sense that the conspiracy will do the same. As for the third Horror I consider a Contagion based one- Witch Finders already mentions how Cheiron might be connected to a Pandora Box like object which spreads a flesh mutating plague, which is an option- let's call them the Anesidora, the Gift Givers, walking natural hazards who bear a special form of the Contagion. Finally, as Cheiron seems to imply some strange, eldritch entities, we might have them in the form of the Cabeiri, who serve as alien entities which seem to reach from far worlds, and who might be related to the origin of the Board of Directors.

    Council of Bones: the CoB seems to have two main points of focus- the dead and slashers. In terms of a slasher, we could potentially have a twist here and combine them with the Eaters of the Dead, having a slasher who kills ghosts- the Shrouded could be their temporary name, as they reap apart ghosts and wear their ectoplasm as clothes. The dead themselves could be a wide enough wealth of possibilities- for example, we could start with a zombie variant called Wights, which we could base around a earlier micro template I wrote called Jackals- those are people which Death forgot to "collect", leaving them dead but moving and with a burning need to eat flesh to maintain their humanity. A incorporeal form of undead could be given as Wraiths, ghosts which have somehow managed to capture a soul, making them much more "human" yet at the cost of becoming unstable and dangerous. Finally I think that another ephemeral threat would be the Shells, entities composed from amalgamated memories which were exchanged so much that they incarnate themselves within the dead ectoplasm and create horrifying melds of shadows.

    Lucifuge: demons, demons, and a bit more demons. I think that at the very least, an actual writeup for Infernals and the Possessed they create is required for the Lucifuge. Beyond that, we could use the Furies as a special form of demonic beings that hunt down evil and other demons, putting them in a strange position for the Lucifuge as the demons themselves are evil beyond any reasonable doubt, yet they "use" it for "good" acts. I also think that a God-Machine like demons are also of interest- perhaps a specific form of Angels called the Archons, the Grand Builders who enslave civilizations in order to form and protect Infrastructures and which seem to decay when exposed to Infernal beings- finding a way to differentiate them from regular angels is required, but I think that we could do so by focusing around building as a theme- instead of needs an Infrastructure to exist, they are entities which create and power the occult matrix, and are devoted to protect them from corruption. While I considered some form of Fiends for the last entry, I think that what truly scares the Lucifuge even more than demons are (actual) angels- so let's put here the Elohim, beings of raw, divine power who are incarnated from the hypothetical Empyrean with a single goal- to burn out all evil, and no, redemption is not an option. (Having some Abyssal horror might also be cool. God, I may end up extending this project to 5 Horrors per conspiracy and 2 for Compacts).

    Malleus Maleficarum: yeah, this one is pretty easy to follow. First, vampires- let's have a form of "holy vampirism"- heck, let's just go with Daywalkers (I considered the Blood Angels from the Testament of Longinus, but they were already covered in RfR 2e). Let's have vampires who have found "redeemed" themselves, becoming mad horrors which will no longer burn, and let's give them some sort of "divine revelations" and the ability to work miracles. That's basically the MM's worst nightmare. Next- witches. Again, let's go with the standard biblical horror and let's have warlocks, people who have gained supernatural capabilities by selling their soul to Satan. Yes, let's just be simple- Soulless witches who are obsessed with Vice, we don't need something more complex than that. As for demons... that's where it becomes a bit more tricky. I think that we could play on all the different sources of Benedictions (Theban Sorcery, God Machine, Sekhem Sorcery) to fill this point and make some sort of specialized Fiends- let's call them Scourges. Those demons are powered by guilt, possessing those who have committed crimes yet refuse to confess for their actions, constantly punishing them and feasting on their misery. Finally, I think that something that could fit the God Machine is appropriate- let's call it Saints, spirits of people who died as sacrifices in the name of their values, becoming divine in death and being bound into a strange, celestial Infrastructure.

    Task Force: Valkyrie: this one is a bit more complicated to consider but let's see what we have. Let's start from the easy part- Nazi Vampires. Those will be vampires who have a special ability called "blood refinement", which basically allows them to create special ghouls called Ubermensch- people who basically had their blood replaced with Vitae granting them tremendous power yet they are fully dependent on vampires to replenish their blood once their Vitae burns out. For the second one, I think about a deviant-like horror- the Flygja. Some sort of an AI helpers that inhabits android forms to help with the Vigil- but sometimes might also hack the RFID chip to take over a warm, welcoming body and "helping" the hunter directly (think the AI from Project Freelancer in Red vs Blue). Next, let's just have Valkyries, like the ones I wrote for my Awful Ice project. The Chooser of the Slain, who show up when strange Auroras burn in the sky, collecting dead hunters who fell in their Vigil to fight the coming "war". Finally, let's have something truly weird with the Code: Yggdrasil- a strange algorithm where pieces of it are found in strange computers all around the world, causing strange (specifically interdimensional) phenomena to occur while the hunters to TFV are tasked with finding the processes which run them and collect them back to base for unknown purposes.

    VASCU: slashers, psychics, deviants let's go. In terms of slashers, let's start from the basics of having a type of slashers that, for some reason, does not obey the regular rules- perhaps one that "hacks" the psychic processing abilities of the Wintergreen Process, or perhaps a slasher produced by the same methodology. The result is Faded, slashers that leave a psychic shadow whenever they kill which consumes evidence and corrupts Clues, making them invisible for both forensics and empathic senses. Moving to psychics, let's have the Psychopomps- they are seers blessed with the ability to see coming death, making them invaluable when hunting down slashers, yet using their deathsight has the price of establishing a empathic connection with the cause of death, which draws the slashers to them. Now, for deviants, let's have a fun little Deviant conspiracy for VASCU to deal with- the Black Sun, a strange group of elites that offers those they deem as "worthy" as sacrifices to alien gods, and sometimes those entities instead bless the conspiracy with the Flayed- their own personal Deviants. Finally, VASCU had been blamed for God Machine involvement for a while, so let's also play with it- the Umbrals, strange angelic beings who commune through mirrors and reflections which try to commune with VASCU agents by wearing their own faces and offering strange insight in exchange for bargains.

    Well, that's what I currently have for the main conspiracies. Please let me know what do you think, while I try to brainstorm some Compact based Horrors.

                                      So let's go through the list of Core Compacts:

                                      Ashwood Abbey: as it was mentioned a few times, the Abbey should have something demonic in them, due to the whole Vice and Hellfire things they have going for them. That already gives us the Tempters, which were already mentioned. Those are basically Willpower sapping entities, creating Addiction Conditions related ton the Vice of the target, yet while they give some sort of benefit they both steal Willpower from Vice fulfillment and limit the gain from Virtue fulfillment (possibly replacing them in a manner similar to what happens to Soulless people). The second Horror which is very asked for is the Maenads, entities which inspire bacchanals, causing people to be driven mad with passion around them as they are lost to their urges- with them drawing Abbey hunters who either seek the rush or with them crushing the Abbey parties, turning everything into full blown chaos and making them into a constant "nuisance" to their fun. The last idea is that of Lures- those are people who draw monsters towards them, due to having a higher amount of supernatural within their form (similar to Sleigh Beggy from the Ancient Magus Bride), and as such they are often being used by the compact as the "center of the party"- whether they like it or not.

                                      Long Night: the Christian fundamentalists who fight as messengers of God are another conspiracy with religious themes, and as such likely to deal with proper Horrors. The first would be the Grigori- standard Fallen Angels (or at least self proclaimed ones) that were caged by God. Let's go in an angle similar to the Earthbound from Fallen- those are grand idols which demand blood sacrifice and are able to possess those who come in touch with them or swear fealty to the bound demon lord. Going with the theme of using Fallen's apocalyptic world, we can go with the Messengers as angelic beings that endow hunters with the power to fight the darkness, yet their zeal drives them mad, turning them into "heavenly slashers" of some sort. Finally, let's have the whole "charisma" thing that evangelical sects usually have and flip it on its head- the Preachers are individuals with a unique Devil Tongue that can convince others that they have performed miracles, healed the sick and banished possessions, yet this is all just falsehoods- allowing them to establish dark cults of personality as their followers are dying in their name.

                                      Loyalists of Thule: well let's get the low hanging fruit out of the way and just put the Rmoahals as a Horror in a similar manner as I already presented them in my Dark Eras book- vestigial remnants of pre-human species lurking within people's DNA, waiting to be triggered into a horrifying and sorcerous majesty of the long gone Giants. In terms of a more human kind of antagonists (which are indeed appropriate for this compact), such as some Mystery Cult in the style of the Lemurians which is both explicitly racist and attempts at finding the "lost civilization of the Aryans" and "purify humanity". Another thing which would be appropriate for them is some sort of a God Machine related threat, considering that the compact (or more exactly its predecessors) are featured in Into the Cold- it could be the Thule Infrastructure, a sentient Infrastructure that corrupts the minds of those under its effect to adapt the Angelic Missions that lead towards re-creating Thule, yet at the cost of creating glitches in reality. Finally, something related to ghosts also seems to be needed to the compact- the Librarians are a special form of Eaters of the Dead that devour the memories of the ghosts that they eat, preserving them in their flesh. This means that they are both treasure troves of knowledge- at least, they would have been if they were obsessed with hiding it from the "unworthy".

                                      Network 0: the Truth Seekers are those hunters who have to constantly fight to reveal the truth. As such, the first enemy that they are probably going to have to deal with is the Antimemetic- similar concept to what seen in SCP, this horror seems to not merely defy perception and documentation, but also drains memories and obscure its surroundings- videos get corrupted, photos distorted, sound recording filled with white noise as they whisper and speak. By doing so, not only that the compact have trouble at documenting it, it also wrecks their own existing documents. Then we would have a more memetic horror- the Fun Time Meme. Suggested by Flyboy in the Discord, this Horror is partially based around the Smiley Face Killer Theory and serves as an information hazard- those who get exposed to the meme risk at getting brainwashed, becoming murderers who always kill in the same manner and leave the writing "Fun Time" on a nearby wall, only to completely forget that they have performed the act. Finally, let's go a bit Pain Prophet here and let's have the Silence- a strange phenomenon that takes the form of radio waves of an unknown source, where when received and translated creates a occult symbol that allows an eldritch horror to partially manifest in reality, devouring information in its solar corona.

                                      Nine Stars: The most basic Horror for those guys should be based around a form of slashers- the Four Perils refer to four artifacts where each contains a demonic desire to murder that transforms those under their influence into a special form of a supernatural murderer, devoted to bring ruin to wherever they go. On the other hand, we can go with the Eater of Names that seems to cause the slasher epidemic in the first place and have the Name Eaten as a Horror they have to deal with- those individuals have lost their True Names to the ancient Firstborn, and gain the ability to steal the names of those they murder in order to take their place in the world, yet as they avoid killing the hunger consumes them from within, forcing them to feast on more and more names to silence the everlasting call.

                                      Null Mysteriis: the scientific oriented compact basically seeks to understand the truth about the supernatural, which means that they are likely to deal with similarly scientific horrors. The Eveans are a special breed of Horrors that defy the ability of the compact to easily categorize it- known as the "Children of Eve" among the more mystical groups, those Horrors have the strange ability to adapt and evolve to circumstances, allowing them to constantly change their features and capabilities depending on the danger in which they are found (think the Hard to Kill reptile from SCP, just a little bit less OP). While the Eveans serve as the biological angle of the compact, the Resonance serves as the physical one- those are basically ephemeral manifestations of the fundamental force of the universe- time, space, gravity, entropy and so forth. In a way, they serve as the "angels of the universal constants", serving as both manipulators of reality and present cosmological question to the compact as they try to comprehend the idea that gravity as its own divine messengers.

                                      SWORN: the protectors of the indigenous people who seek to rebuild their past from the remnants of colonialism, they have to deal with both entities which represent invasive species and the horrors which now lurk without stories to protect from them. Let's start from the Water Serpents- great cryptids who reside in lakes that each holds their own set of behavior and codes which allow it to drown those who do not follow the rules that only they now remember. As the reconstruction of stories and mythic monsters fits a lot of Beast's themes, let's have the Boogeymen: an astral horror that is literally weaved from stories and urban legends, and will never be destroyed permanently as long as the story is being told yet the story also details the only way it can be put down (temporarily). As for the last horror, let's have something a bit more abusive- the Blue Rose is a special plant from the Hedge that both has supernatural capabilities and serves as a gate for the Fae to reach beyond the Thorns by basically terraforming the local environment into a Arcadian realm, allowing strange monstrosities to form and disrupt the local ecology.

                                      Union: as it was already discussed, the Gardeners serves as the first Horror for this compact- those monsters are memory interlopers, not merely establishing themselves within the minds of those they prey upon but also create a strange, hivemind like community, conditioning them to behave in the manner they perceive as "polite" or "acceptable", who also generally have powerful mind altering and illusion weaving abilities to those under their command. The second horror was again suggested by Flyboy- a Lowerdepthian species that feasts upon social connections and empathy- let's call it the Atropoi. Those Thread Cutters cause those who are affected by them to lose meaning to the "social contract", disrupting their attachments and causing them to be unable to functional parts of society. Allow a Atropos to roam free for too long, and society itself will fall into chaos. Finally, let's have the Toil- a strange condition that infects people through teamwork. Each Toil is a special form of mental infection, leading those under its effect to follow certain patterns and devote their lives to a process which holds no meaning- crafting perfectly even squares out of wood, digging circles in the ground or make pyramids out of skulls. Those who try to understand the meaning of the process or take part in helping with it risk at falling victim to the Toil, which only stops when all infected work themselves to death to produce a product without a buyer and which has no value. Those who try to disrupt their work, however, will anger those under the Toil's effect, who will not hesitate using violence to protect its "process".

                                      This is it for the Core concepts. Feel free to provide feedback on those, and I'll add them also to the outline of the project before moving to the next group of orgs.
