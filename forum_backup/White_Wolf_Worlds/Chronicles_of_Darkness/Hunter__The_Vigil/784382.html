    Homebrew idea: Batman in the WoD

    12-11-2015, 06:27 PM
    It was a favorite thought experiment of my friends and I to imagine how our favorite comic book characters would fit in the World of Darkness. Back before the release of the new WoD we'd imagine Batman as a Mage who didn't realize he was awakened, which would explain how he pulled off some blatantly impossible crap in spite of being an "ordinary human". Since the release of the nWoD I've re-imagined Batman as a Mortal. Perhaps even a hunter. All or most of his rogue's gallery would definitely fit in one Slasher category or another. As an amusing homebrew idea I made a character sheet for him. Then lost it. But the gist is that Batman is a peak human being, so he has 5 dots in all attributes, 5 dots in every skill, of course 10 willpower, a long list of equipment and vehicles, and a loooong list of merits that included a long ass list of contacts, a long ass list of allies, and one retainer in the form of Alfred Pennyworth (no endowments, he doesn't need them). He also has a Morality of 6 and suffers from the "Suspicion" derangement.

    Batman's villains, while not as well thought out, would fit within the following Slasher Undertakings:
    Joker-Maniac
    Harley Quinn-Psycho
    Two-Face-Freak
    Penguin-Freak
    Killer Croc-Mutant
    Mr. Freeze-Legend
    Riddler-Genius
    Poison Ivy-Avenger/Legend
    The Mad Hatter-Psycho

    If this sort of thing appeals to you, then I leave this here as an idea seed for running your own home brew Batman RPG using the Hunter: the Vigil game mechanics. You could also do something very similar with the CW version of Green Arrow.

Cause let's face it, there are some feats he's been able to pull off that don't make too much sense, even for a "peak human".
                  Wouldn't you know it, Amechra actually suggested altering the Rote Quality into a Routine Action, which does exactly that: Successes at 6+ on each die. I also came up with a Merit when I kept imagining ways to even the playing field between mortals and ephemeral beings:

                  Unyielding (5 Dots): Your character may spend 1 willpower point to double her Resolve, Composure or Stamina when applying it as a penalty to any supernatural powers that alter thought, emotion or body. He may also spend 1 willpower point to the gain the 8 Again benefit on contested rolls against such powers. The Tolerance granted by his Conviction still applies. This Merit stacks with Indomitable. Prerequisites: No Supernatural Template, Conviction 4 (Or all Resistance Attributes at 3+ if you don't include Conviction). The rules for the Conviction Powerstat may be found on the "Mirrors" supplement for 1ed NWOD, pages 32 to 35.

                  Let's say that Batman is a Hunter, thus capable of Risking Willpower. I'll give Batman a Conviction of 7 or maybe 8, which allows him to spend 3 willpower points per turn. I'll go even further and say that Batman acts almost like a Spirit: he is considered to have Professional Training 5 for all Skills that can be applied to crime-fighting somehow, but only as long as he is acting according to the Batman persona. His mindset simply won't allow for his full expertise to be used when he's being Bruce Wayne, which is practically an Unchained's Cover to him*. He can spend 1 willpower point to Risk Willpower when, say, disarming a thug. His Omni-Professional Training gives him 9 Again, which is upgraded into 8 Again by Risking Willpower. He can then spend a second willpower point to also gain Rote Quality/Routine Action, and a third to use his Conviction and gain +5 dice. All in all, he only spent 1 willpower point, without even going into the laundry list of equipment, Skill Tricks, Endowments and other Merits he's accumulated. I wouldn't go so far as to give him 5 dots in every single Attribute and Skill, though. He's not a god. However, after reading through Pendragon's Homebrew Hub, I also decided that Batman has the Prodigy Merit (which allows epic mortals to raise an Attribute or Skill to 6 dots) applied to Resolve, Stealth, Investigation and Intimidation. This would give him 1 Reversal Point for each of those stats, as defined in the "Imperial Mysteries" supplement for Mage 1ED, page 17.

                  Or, you could do what Anonymous once suggested in a /tg/ thread about giving stats to Batman: he's a Game Master PC. You are not allowed to look at his sheet.

                  I have character sheets written for him and Slasher!Joker, if anyone is interested in seeing them.

                  *There's an idea for those who want to really emphasize the psychological rift between the Batman and Bruce Wayne. He could also be an Ugallu with a Hunger For Punishment. If you use Genius: The Transgression, he could be a Klagen or Grimm.

                                                          I would be leery of giving the Bat 5 in everything.Despite how his meme has grown, even writers who lean hard into the "Bat God" interpretation allow the character to be vulnerable and have limitations, otherwise there is no drama. Especially in the a lower-power setting like Chronicles, I would be more inclined to give him 4's in most attributes (except Intelligence, Stamina, Resolve, and Composure). His primary crime fighting skills would largely be 4-5, but could easily be lower ( even at of the extended Bat Family, Dick Grayson is a better natural Athlete, Barbara Gordon is better at Computers, Cassandra Cain is an *arguably* better Martial Artist).

                                                          While the "Batman is the best at everything ever" interpretation is valid, I prefer the character who has limits and triumphs in spite of them.
