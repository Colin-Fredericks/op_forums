    Dataweaver: OTOH, I also don’t think that “tier” should be used as a synonym for “cells, compacts, or conspiracies”. Rather, Tiers should be described in terms of intended play-style ... Tier One is for those Storytellers who like the notion that hunters are no different from regular mortals ... Tier Two assumes that the protagonists are something special, though not supernatural ... Tier Three assumes that the lines between mortal and monster have been blurred, and that hunters have reason to question which side of that line they stand on.
    I mostly agree with this for Tiers 1 and 3; for Tier 2, I'd refine it a bity to include the Vigil having developed to the point where there's a definite philosophy of what the supernatural and the Vigil mean something more than monsters bad/protect innocent) - so Tier 1 has you seeing and reacting to the supernatural world, Tier 2 has you taking a few steps into it, and Tier 3 has you in danger of becoming part of it (Tier 4 in this model would presumably be like playing a rogue ghoul or a Banisher ). Under this model, Advanced Armoury would make sense for Tier 2, since it's essentially high tech. I'd argue that Relics can as well - despite the proprietary interest of AkD, they're gizmos everyone can use and no one can make, which makes them particularly emblematic of the idea that the supernatural world is something "beyond." On the other hand, Benedictions, Castigation, Elixirs (for which you have to develop your bodily immunities), and Thaumotech would all be Tier 3. The psychic powers of VASCU could be either one, depending on how you view the implications of the other planes they sometimes access (though the Wintergreen Process is certainly a Tier 3 deal). I wouldn't mind a general "Occulstim" Endowment, containing of ritual magics that you can lens onto any group you think would be more atmospheric with them (the Loyalists of Thule and Maiden's Blood Sisterhood are both rumoured to have magicians of some stripe).

            Tiers should be

            1) The hunters have just scratched at the surface of the World of Darkness, they are disorganized & unequipped to deal with the darkness

            2) The hunters have passed through the fire & flames & came out stronger, they know what they are dealing with now & the resources to combat it

            3) The hunters have the seen strings being pulled behind the world & now are pulling a few themselves, their territory is no longer a small town or neighborhood. They have manpower, resources & clout to effect real change

            This should be separate from Endowments. I can see a lot of different imaginings on how someone who isn't in a Conspiracy could have Endowments, though I fully recommend that there should be at least one Conspiracy for each Endowments or maybe two

            Benedictions - A country priest finds that the holy rites he performs regularly suddenly start to really work, & though he may not understand his place in his divine plan, he knows that he must answer the Call
            Castigation - This is literally about your bloodline, not your membership in a super special club.
            Thaumaturgy - A desperate vet, with heart complications, figures that if that man-wolf thing he shot could recover from the injury why not try cutting the regenerating organs & see if they don't wear out; A group of hobos that graft parts from the monsters that would prey on them, so they can better survive the mean streets
            Elixirs - A high school teacher learns about the occult & starts doing some alchemy, a drug lord's top producer finds a way to enhance the drug lord's enforcers
            etc.

            & I'd like to see support for every level of play. I like to play Tier 1-2 where the PC's are just average joes, thrust into the world, but I like them to have options & interesting things, like Castigation or Benediction

                                    I agree that the new chronicle book aught to have separated tiers (local, regional, global, cosmic) from organization scale (cell, compact, conspiracy).

                                    Also perhaps like a vampire joining a covenant some groups aught to get group specific powers, but that there aught to be powers available for all hunters too (like disciplines), or maybe all endowments aught to be allowed, they just aught to be more expensive for "out of group". Things like the ability to summon and banish "demons" (not necessarily DtD)(as someone put, like in supernatural). Maybe the "ritual magic" from witch finders could be something hunters could get too (fits slippery slope theme). More advanced armory things (liquid sunlight) (and the ability to create them), or hunter specific relics (colt's gun in the show supernatural, which would have been "advanced armory" in colt's time).

                                    More focus on tactics and team work. Some people have mentioned larger group ops, and I think that'd be a good idea.

                                    I also like the idea of hunters getting a Potency trait themselves (purchased like it), which can also be used for resistance. This is not an immunity, you just perhaps get a higher than normal rating against things like Lunacy, Disquiet, etc. Easily explained as resistance building, awareness, lack of disbelief.

                                    It's worth noting that I don't want hunters to become more inhuman, I just want to give them more of an edge. Some of which might be rules basically geared towards using the supernatural against the supernatural.

                                                    I agree that Endowments should be available at any level of Hunter Tier. For me, the tier system would more indicate the resources and activity of Hunter groups. Lower-tier groups would have more freedom, but less impressive resources. Conspiracies and such would be involved in much more politics and side activities, but they would also have more powerful endowments/equipment, as well support.

                                                    I agree with The King's Raven about the Aegis Kai Doru having a closer relationship with mummies, maybe even elevating them to the role of primary enemy for the conspiracy. I disagree with them having an Illuminati feel, because to me that would indicate a focus on gaining political power, which I don't think the Aegis Kai Doru is really interested in.

                                                    A number of people have thought up a Judaism-aligned conspiracy which I honestly think might not be a bad idea (I've liked both The King's Raven's conspiracy; Hakollel shel Hamerkava, and LostLight's conspiracy; Raziel LTD​).

                                                    For the Lucifuge, I think there should be an expansion on the struggle of fighting one's inner darkness. They're children of SATAN… I think that there might be some darkness there. I think that would be a great angle for the Conspiracy--half the time they are fighting creatures of darkness while utilizing dark powers strait from Hell, the other half is spent fighting their own personal demons; that little voice in the back of their head telling them to torment and manipulate the puny humans.

                                                    The Ascending Ones I think should be built up a bit. They have a very DC Comics League of Assassins-vibe to me, and I think it might serve to expand in that direction. A Muslim/Egyptian-based hunter trained in martial arts and meditative techniques, utilizing not only those potent alchemical potions to empower themselves, but also alchemically-poisoned weapons like swords and arrows.

                                                    I think it also might be a very appealing idea to expand The Loyalists of Thule into a mad-science conspiracy. I mean, it wasn't really any secret that the Nazi scientists were experimenting with the supernatural, so it might make sense that after the fall of Nazi Germany the Occult Science group went underground and now prowl the night, as much mad-scientists as they ever were. In their studies they utilize such devices as radios powered by the heads of dead Psychics and other bizarre occult devices.

                                                    For Task Force: VALKYRIE, some part of me thinks that Advanced Armory isn't quite enough. To me, TF: V is one of the few Conspiracies which might be open to using supernatural creatures in their hunt for monsters. Though they may not employ such tricky creatures as vampires and such, I could definitely see them utilizing psychics, sin-eaters, and even changelings as agents in the field (some monsters love their country, after all). Or, if not as actual operatives then perhaps as informants and spies.

                                                    As for the Malleus Maleficarum…I think Benedictions could be a bit more nuanced…like divided between prayers and blessed items (I always found it odd that though the Aegis Kai Doru used Catholic relics, the MM didn't). First you might have the regular benedictions or specialized prayers involving such things as ritualized fasting, self-flagellation, etc.. which would help channel lesser blessings and saintly might. Then you might have access to a number of very powerful, yet unstable, holy items strait from the Vatican's vaults--ancient reliquaries, the remains of various unofficial saints, nails used to crucify martyrs, etc.--to use as banes and weapons.
