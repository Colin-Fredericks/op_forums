        The Last Wilderness Rangers

        The Last Wilderness Rangers are an American scout group known to be a strange mix of liberal environmentalists and old-style huntsmen - the founder was a man who believed, deeply, in all the old legends of how to woods and wilderness had power, and how that power could be very dangerous. The Rangers are a growing sight in small towns, due to their very open recruitment drives - boy, girls, gay, troubled, clean-cut, all are welcome to join the Rangers. The reason for this is twofold; firstly, the group does believe that their lessons and instructions (primarily focused on living cleanly within nature and respecting the myths and legends of diverse cultures) do in fact help young people. The second reason is that with a wider net comes greater possibility of finding people who have the right stuff to be inducted in the Inner Circle.

        The Inner Circle are a secret group within the Wilderness Rangers who have both completed all the levels of Rangerhood (Cat, Fox, Deer, Hawk, Wolf) and have shown to current Inner Circle members that the the right mentality to join the Vigil. The idea is that the years within the Rangers would help foster a strong loyalty to the group and allow older, more experienced members to help guide the prospects on the right path to be an efficient an productive Hunter.

        The Rangers see their Vigil as the protection of the next generation (in a 'teach a man to fish' sort of way) and the watchers of the wilderness. they hunt down the strange creatures that roam the hills and valleys of the Americas, the small towns that abut the deep, dark woods. They listen to the old legends and stories, spreading them around the camp fires so that they are not forgotten.

                    so, I've lately looked on all my previous organization ideas, and I've found two old ones which I'm still interest in writing one day. Here are their temp names:

                    Memory Collectors- a compact of art collectors and historians, who see the whole Underworld as a huge library which contains all knowledge which ever existed. They collect both memorabilia (for they contain power for their very importance in human history) and Fetters (which hold the ghosts of the dead and allows them ton gain their knowledge). They are extremely interested in finding the ghosts and memorabilia of important people, like artists, politicians and similar famous people, and while the generally wait for them to die and hope that they would leave a ghost behind, some take a more.. active approach.

                    Solifacti- another Slashing Conspiracy, they originate from the Mendeleev. They are alchemists which sought after eternal life and the Philosopher Stone, until they came to the conclusion that the mythical substance was no other than the human soul- and so, they must harvest it. They use human organs in their alchemical formulas, and they use it in order to improve themselves, change lead to gold, create energy out of matter and more- yet they still didn't found the organ which contains the "soul"- and they are sure that when they would find it, they will gain immortality. Their Endowments may be similar to Elixirs, just with a dark twist.

                    and a two new ideas:

                    Emerald Gardeners- a conspiracy which seeks to gain control over the Hedge, for they see it as a potential way to create wondrous and terrible things. For them, it is a tool to be used by humanity, and they while it has a corrupting effect on the human soul, they are still willing to place a "seed from the Hedge" in their heart in order to be able to conjure its vines and bushes for their help. While it allows them to conjure weird things and create all kinds of weird materials, each dot in their Endowments breaks their mind, slowly but surely.

                    Wu Keng- yes, I know, but just listen. They are going to be "demon hunters", both lesser and greater, although they don't actually hunt the demons. They trick them. The are a modern creation based on an older version of the group, which have used the traditional cross dressing tradition in order to trick demons and null their pacts. Each member of the organization has an "alternative Cover" which is activate once they are dressed as the opposite gender, and any kind of supernatural effect which targets a specific individual (especially demonic pacts) target the alternative identity and their true identity as two different individuals. That way, they can make a pact with a demons, but when the demons wish to get his side in the deal they simply destroy the "Cover". While it also destroys what they gained from the pact, they can "cash" the effect in order to get Dread Powers.

                    any thoughts?

                                                    It really seems like the books have managed to cover most of the bases. Everything I think of is some variant of 'like that existing group, but with this instead of that.' But maybe I'm too close, being a fan of the game line.

                                                    So, in the vein of Ashwood Abbey, but with money instead of thrills, how about 'The House.' Essentially an underground gambling syndicate that has operatives that act to poke the bears, so to speak, provoking monsters into act, and betting on how they'll react. Obviously among the many problems with this is the big one that implies that they already know about supernaturals and how they act, which already makes them more informed than most hunter compacts. But, say, a bunch of wealthy folks (and some not-so-wealthy but connected to the underground necessary to make the operation worked) started having some death sports. Bum fights to the death. And one day things got weird when Something Happened. Maybe a demon went loud or a werewolf death raged or a promethean let loose. Either way, a group of insiders formed, realizing they could get wealthy folks into it. Sure, some folks could be fulfilled by a contract with the Bear Lodge or the Ashwood Abbey, but who wants to get their hands dirty when they could watch others do so from the comfort of their home and an internet connection?

                                                    While there'd be the folks who do the behind the scenes work (getting investors involved, scouting out staging grounds, maintaining the servers) the hard but well-rewarded work would be done by the folks who scout out locations and 'political' situations so they could send in the grunts, who do the real hard work, doing things like luring pure into forsaken territories, angels to demons, pointing hunters towards vampire havens and, rarely, just out and out kidnapping to drop things into a makeshift arena.
