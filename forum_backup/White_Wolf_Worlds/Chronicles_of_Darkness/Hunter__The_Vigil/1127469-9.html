        The Kings Raven Here's a first edit of the Sunrise Securities writeup. Sadly, I couldn't figure out how to show the exact position of the edits or upload the word doc. Just my two cents as peon, but I think this is a good length for a writeup.


        Sunrise Securities

        The Doorkeepers

        Slavery was one of the great evils of the 17th century. Entire communities were ripped from their homes, their cultures, and taken into lives of hard labor and cruelty. Hundreds of years were not enough to erase the scars, but within every act of destruction lies the potential for new creation. Generations born upon foreign shores without the culture of their ancestors filled the void with acts of creation. Jazz, soul, hip hop; art and music flowed from the African Diaspora and shaped the world.

        But the monsters who lived (and unlived) in slavery’s wake never left when the laws changed. If Vampires cannot buy people to profit and drink from, then the club scene, with its late nights and inebriated patrons, makes a fine replacement. But it is no longer 1860. Tonight African America stands strong in their castles, filling the halls with music and barring the door against the monsters outside.

        Sunrise Securities was formed by a happy accident at The Corn and Rooster, a Harlem jazz club owned and operated by Reginald “Doctor” Cobra: entrepreneur, bòkônon, and a fairly decent hand on the keyboard. At its height, The Corn and Rooster was the place to be for Harlem locals and white folk slumming it for the great music. By day the club rehearsed, by night the club danced, and upon holy days the club partied with the Loa. For a time, all was good.

        But dancers and music lovers weren’t the only people drawn to The Corn and Rooster. With time and experience, Reginald began to grow suspicious. He saw regulars leaving with different people every visit, people who always woke up weak the next day. He turned to spells and divinations and when he knew what manner of leech was sucking at the lifeblood of his establishment, he acted. Reginald gathered his employees and performed a grand ritual to petition Papa Legba - the Loa who guards all gates - to make his club a sacred space, safe from the predators which stalked the night outside.

        With the Loa’s blessing, business boomed. Nobody said a word, but everyone knew that The Corn and Rooster was the safest club in town. Reginald’s rivals began to grumble, and in their grumblings Reginald saw a business opportunity. Sunrise Securities was formed.

        The Enemy

        Let other Hunters venture into dark places and fight the monsters within. Sunrise Securities has their own mission: "This place is sacred, this music is sacred, and none shall disturb it; whatever happens outside these four walls is somebody else’s business, at least for as long as I'm on the clock."

        Vampires are Sunrise Securities’ most common foe. The image of the glamourous, sexy Vampire stalking the dance floor is well known for a reason. Vampires really do like to prey upon nightclubs and as far as Sunrise Securities are concerned, if they see a Vampire, they show them the door. Vampires are also the most vulnerable to the Compact’s Endowment (after all, Reginald petitioned Papa Legba for a tool against Vampires and he got what he asked for).

        After Vampires comes Changelings, the second most likely supernatural being to turn up at a nightclub. A Changeling’s emotional harvesting is far less harmful than a vampire’s blood drinking, so Sunrise Securities is more tolerant. Their bouncers can recite the standard Pledge of good behavior from memory in their sleep. Besides, Changelings can make some damn fine music. Some Freeholds even hire Sunrise Securities to provide bouncers for their own clubs, and the firm sees nothing wrong with taking the contract. Mostly though, Changelings stay beneath Sunrise’s radar.

        After that, there are other monsters who stalk the night looking for human prey. Beasts talk a good game about having a higher purpose, but at the end of the day, if they get hungry, an intoxicated clubber is easy prey. Sunrise Securities on the whole are in favor of otherworldly beings teaching humans lessons that are needed, if not always kind. They call those beings the Loa, and they don’t like the idea of amateurs bungling the job, especially if it hurts or traumatizes one of their charges. However, Beasts are powerful, and few Doorkeepers will willingly take one on alone.[SR1]

        Picture: A middle aged and very muscular African-American in a bad suit, the kind of man you might describe as a brick. He's wearing many gold chains, one of which has Papa Legba's Veve.

        You! Beat it!

        Stereotypes

        The Union: It’s disappointing how often “protecting our neighbors” stops where the ghetto begins. But they know how to fight, so we’ll ask for their help. Duty before pride, brother.

        Ascending Ones: Monsters around here have an odd habit of disappearing. I don’t know who’s doing it, but there’s a drink waiting for him behind the bar.

        Night Watch: You don’t push in here, you don’t bring your piece past this door, and we don’t have a problem.

        The Staff of Janus[SR2] : I ain’t going to faerieland, not for money or magic. I’ve got my door and my music right here.

        Status

        Status in Sunrise Securities is a formal affair handled by human resources. In a nutshell, the longer you’ve worked for Sunrise, and the longer you’ve gone without screwing up, the higher your status.

        O: You’ve gone through Sunrise Securities corporate training program. Take a dot of Trained Observer and a dot of Doorkeeping.
        OOO: You’re an old hand and have seen quite a bit in your time. Take two dots in the Street Fighting Merit as a memento of your experiences.
        OOOOO: You’re as much of a fixture of the club scene as the clubs themselves. Everyone around knows your name. Take the Barfly Merit and a dot of Contacts (Club Scene).

        Endowment: Doorkeeping (O - OOOOO)

        Bouncers employed by Sunrise Security are trained in the sacred and humbly named art of Doorkeeping. A sacred contract in which the bouncer assumes responsibility for the safety of all within their building. Sunrise's employees may be professional or they may take bribes. Some are polite and helpful, others like to throw their weight around. But nothing: Not money, not pride, not the club management, will stop a Doorkeeper from doing whatever it takes to ensure everyone visiting their establishment is protected until they’re safely on their way home.

        The more dedicated they are to this duty, the greater the potency of this Endowment, but even a single dot requires a firm commitment.

        Doorkeeping grants the following benefits while on duty:
        • A doorkeeper may Risk Willpower an additional Doorkeeping times a scene, providing they use their extra risks on rolls benefiting from Doorkeeping.
        • Add Doorkeeping to all rolls to notice trouble. This could be a Perception roll, a Wits + Empathy roll to notice someone’s looking for trouble, a Wits + Occult roll to recognize a vampire, or even a Wits + Medicine roll to notice someone’s reacting badly to drugs.
        • Against Obfuscate and similar vampiric powers, a Doorkeeper automatically rolls Wits + Doorkeeping as a Clash of Wills.
        • Each Dot of Doorkeeping provides an automatic Successes on Contested rolls to resist being persuaded, intimidated, or mind controlled into overlooking trouble on a Doorkeeper’s premises. If the roll is Resisted, the other character subtracts Doorkeeping Successes from their roll instead. Against Dominate, Majesty, or other similar vampiric abilities the effect grows to two Successes.
        • If a Doorkeeper tells a vampire they can't enter or to get out of their premises, they may roll Presence + Intimidation + Doorkeeping vs Composure + Potency. If the Doorkeeper wins, the vampire becomes psychologically unable to enter, and if they’re inside they must leave immediately. The effect lasts until sunrise, or until the Doorkeeper's death.[SR3]
