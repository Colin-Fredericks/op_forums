    Candlelight City [Shard]

    11-15-2023, 06:02 PM
    The CofD generally exists somewhere between the microcosms and the macrocosms- from one end, the characters deal with the local threats and challenges, from the other all kinds of intricate regional and global powers shape and clash across their territory. Generally, this is represented through the Tier system, moving all the way from local to cosmic, with Hunter basically establishing those concepts and solidifying it through the different orgs that compose the setting. However, lately I have a thought rattling through my head about shaking this concept up a bit- instead of weaving the global and the local together, let's fold them upon one another. Let's have a single, local setting, which is self contained yet has all of the moving pieces inside of it.

    Let's have the Candlelight City.

    Basically, the Candlelight City represents a generic, Hunter focused Mega City or a metropolitan area- something which is place which has enough people, businesses and organizations to be able to carry the weight of the setting as a whole yet local enough for the characters to be able to get from one edge of the city to the other in a relative short time, if they are using a taxi. The exact size of the city is less important, obviously- it should just be "as large as needed", yet "basically its own thing". In this Shard, Tiers still have a role, yet with a twist- instead of going wide they go deep. Tier 1 represents the City's surface level- some strange things which bump in the night, some strange mysteries or threats the affect the character's life, some basic fighting between hunters and monsters who need to cover their tracks. Tier 2, however, represent something which is much more constant- those are gangs, stores, small businesses and social movements that are shifting with the City's atmosphere yet very much colour and define it- the common hunter hears about them and potentially stumble upon them on a daily basis, while many still offer services to the general public. They represent much more complex threats as well, where numerous monsters work together or try to influence numerous people at once. Tier 3 basically represent the pillars of the City- those orgs might as well have built the City: the Mayor is funded by them, they hold key positions in the local government, they have signs and billboards screaming their names- yet at the same time, they are mysteries buried deep under the City's surface. Rumors about them are whispered all across the City, yet no one knows what's true and what's false- other than the orgs themselves, obviously. Monsters who work at that scale corrupt the whole local government, holding assets of interest and fund projects which could endanger millions. And what about Tier 4, you ask? This is far beyond the scope of mortals. This is the City itself- the very foundations upon which it is built, and which hold secrets that could shatter the human soul.

    So with that in mind, let's see how well we adapt the different orgs into the City setting, shall we?

    Tier 2

    Ashwood Abbey: join the Ashwood Abbey- the coolest club in town and a place well known for its debaucheries. There were numerous complaints and protests against the club throughout the years, with its name being connected to a number of scandals as some among the richest and most influential people were seen going through its blacken doors. This is probably why even after the different allegations about the club's distribution of drugs, wild sexual parties and occasional dead people found on the alley behind them at the end of the night, they managed to avoid being closed. Entering the Ashwood Abbey, however, is not cheap: you either pay the entrance fees or you don't get in (unless you get an invitation), and even if you can get in, not everyone has the same access. Those who pay for the basic level enjoy music, drinks, dance and whatever decadence their mind can conjure. Those who pay for the gold card, however, get special treatments, massages, private rooms and so forth. There are also rumors about the so called "black card"- and those who manage to pay for it are said to enjoy a "experience of a lifetime".

    Long Night: the local evangelical Church is viewed as somewhat radical in the eyes of most of the City's denizens. The so called Long Night Church is very vocal and well known for its intimidating congregations. Their followers go around the City's streets, warning that the End if Nigh and that the Rapture is upon us. Most people ignore them, but they do have groups of loyal followers, all of which believe that they must be warry of sin and prepare for the coming End of Times. As their followers go from door to door in an attempt to bring more stray souls to the embrace of the Lord, the leaders of the congregation work more actively to prepare to the coming Apocalypse. They gather weapons, food, and prepare shelters for the coming war against the Devil's armies- for they know that not all of their followers, if any, will escape the terror through the Rapture. In fact, the war has already begun- there are demons around walking in humans bodies, which must be slain in the name of the Lord, for only after their death the Christ would come and bring salvation upon the world... or whatever would remain of it.

    Loyalists of Thule: the Schulz family is among the university's greatest donors- for years, their scions went to study there in the faculty of history, and the building itself is named after them. Somewhat ironically, however, the family does not like to discuss its own personal story- how the now cursed patriarch of the family was a very close associate of Himmler, especially in the search after the mystical, pre-human civilization of Thule. While the family have constantly tried to repent for the sons of its bloodline, the taint has stuck and they have left Germany with their valuables and immigrated to the US, where they donate most of their money to fight racism and fascism alongside maintaining historical integrity. That's at least, on what their expenses are written upon. In truth, the Schulz also use their money on their own, secret society- the Loyalists of Thule. As it seems, their patriarch haven't only tried to find Thule- he succeed. There were monsters introduced to the Nazi regime thanks to his help, and he provided occult secrets which allowed a much greater disaster and suffering within an already horrible event. The Schulz moved to the Us to escape their past- but the hunt it down, for many of those monstrosities have left to America after the war, and in order to pay for the debt burned upon them by their bloodline, it is the duty of the Loyalists to put an end for the horrors of their past.

    Network 0: most people don't notice it- the QR code found on flyers, hanged near graffities, slipped into the email box. It is always accompanies with "SEE THE TRUTH" and the initials N0. For the average person, this is just a spam, most likely a link leading you to some fishing scheme or drug selling Telegram group. The truth, however, is much stranger- those who scan the code are lead to a "human verification page", including all kinds of strange quizzes, mazes and tiresome verification methods. Most people get bored midway and leave. Some, however, persist, reaching the final page stating "ARE YOU READY FOR THE TRUTH?". Those who click "yes" are lead to a secret link to a app called Network 0. By downloading it, the person gets access to a new, underground social media, one which discuss the rumors and anomalies that happen in the city. No payment is required- the truth is free for use, after all. All that is expected is that those who are members would share their own information in the platform. The darkness must be exposed, and those with access to the Network will be the first to see it.

    Nine Stars: look, tons of shit happens in the world. The City is one of the largest human settlements in the world, which only means that a lot of bad shit is going to accompany it. Murders, rapes, torture- everything the wicked mind can conjure will take place in it. When that shit is done by humans, then at least the police can get involved and bring those foul criminals to justice. The problem is when those actions are done by inhuman monstrosities. After all, it is not like monsters are registered citizens, and even if they are how are you going to proof to the judge that they brainwashed a person to jump themselves off the roof with just a glance? A lot of shit goes unpunished, and a lot of cops get very pissed that there is nothing they can do. That's how the Nine Stars were founded- they are those policemen and women who had enough, and decided that those horrors who can't be punished by the law will not be allowed to go unpunished. Sure, that means unauthorized use of weapons, misuse of police budgets and constant need in meddling in reports - all of which paints the group as not too different from corrupted police force and could very well send them all to jail - but justice must be done, even if they skies are to fall. After all, who is to say that the police itself is not corrupted by monsters?

    Null Mysteriis: working within the academy, the association known as Null Mysteriis is an interdisciplinary collective of scientists and researchers of all STEM fields: chemistry, biology, physics, engineering and so forth. On the surface, the organization works to advance cooperation between fields and expose the scientific mysteries and how they could be applied to day to day life. In truth, the association use its budget to investigate the phenomenal world of paranatural beings (or as commoners call them, "monsters"). The group works to both present the results of their research and new discoveries as well as cover on each other ion terms of using their funds as part of their true goal. The city is filled with paranatural beings, after all- a whole new scientific field which just stands empty. One day, when the public will be ready, they will publish their results, showing the world the truth that's hidden beneath the surface. Until then, they are fine with arguing over cookies about whether string theory provides explanation to werewolf shapeshifting or not.

    SWORN: the City is old- but the land is older. There were people there, once, who had their own stories and their own traditions, ones which were wiped out by the ancestors of those who founded the City on their lands. The stories are gone now- but the monsters they warn against have remained, and without those warnings they roam free, devouring innocent lives and festering within the City like a wound. That's where SWORN comes in: the descendants of the indigenous people, SWORN serves both as a protest movement meant to fight against racism and systematic discrimination against them as well as gathering the remnants of their ancestors' knowledge, seeking to piece together how they once fought against the monsters. As such, SWORN fight both human and inhuman threats to their community, keeping them safe from the dangers of the shadows as they rebuild the cultures which were lost to colonialism.

    Union: you don't mess with the worker unions. Big companies always try to abuse their employees, grinding them down for profit, and they do whatever they can just to make sure that they won't fight back. While unions are meant to deal with predatory companies, the Union recognize that there are other threats to their workers- horrors which actually seek to devour common people who just want to go to work and go back home in one piece. Taking the same tactic used against the hungry corporations, the Union works both as a community guard and as a political entity- the fight against the lone monsters who prey on those who walk alone in the dark and fight against the institutions controlled by dark monsters who try to enslave humans for their own foul goals. In a place as large as the City, it is easy for those monsters to worm inside and devour a life while most people ignore their screams- and it is up to the Union to maintain the sense of common goal and community in the City, making sure that when someone would call to help there will be someone to answer the call- with a club and a gun in their hands.



    So I planned on also covering conspiracies in this post, but it took longer than I expected and time is late here, so I'll try to finish conspiracies by tomorrow. Hope you liked it!

                  So while Tier 2 orgs generally didn't require much adaptation to the City (which makes sense, considering their different scale), let's now move to the more interesting case of conspiracies, shall we?

                  Aegis Kai Doru: the City's museum of history is well known for its comprehensive collection of relics and artifacts across many different cultures. Much of the museum's budget is dedicated towards expanding its collection, making different exhibitions to educate the next generation and present newcomers the wonders of the world (even though many complain about the legality of the methods in which those items are being procured). While the museum is supported by the City, it is in fact being maintained by a small family of Greek descent, who deal with both the required PR and the additions of new relics to the collection. Like all museums, the City's museum is also not fully opened to the public, maintaining a large archive who is claimed to deal with the more "delicate" items. According to the rumors, however, the museum's archive is much more than a "research only area"- there is a vast labyrinth beneath the structure, they claim, one which predates the museum, if not the City. The museum itself is merely a cover up, maintained by its sponsors as part of their sacred missions to collect relics of occult nature- and little will stand in their way to acquire new relics from those they deem unworthy to hold, which is anyone but themselves, protecting them with a Shield and Spear.

                  Ascending Ones: the criminal underground of the City is complex and widespread, yet one of the great powers controlling it is a collection of gangs and traffickers known as the Ascending Ones. Everything you need, the Ascending Ones can get- drugs, weapons, occult reagents and more. Everything can be bought and sold- for a price. Most people in the City try to avoid places claimed by the Ascending Ones, even though that many hunters turn to them in search for information and armory in their fight against the supernatural. As such, most people who hear about the Ascending Ones merely know them as the force which dominant drug traffic in the City- yet this is merely one of the many layers that the organization maintains. Sometimes, the agents of the Ascending Ones see a potential hidden in a certain costumer, offering them to join them "in prayer". Those who do discover that the Ascending Ones are not mere criminals- they are a cult, dedicated for seeking unity with God as they combine variety of drugs and poisons in their attempt to "Ascend". Those who want to leave discover that it is too late for them- the secrets of the Ascending Ones must be protected, and their combinations of drugs and poisons allows them a rigid control over their followers, for only their alchemical secrets can offer protection from them.

                  Cheiron: the St Cheiron Hospital is a private institute, well known for its advanced medical technology and high success rate at saving their patients from even the most terrible of conditions- as long as they have the money to pay for it. It is said that the institute has connections with the top pharmaceutical companies in the market, and that they maintain their own R&D facilities, developing new treatments which are later patented and archived. Their greatest advancements are in the field of organ transplant, maintaining a high percent of successful operations and hiring the best doctors that universities has to offer. The hospital, however, is not fully driven by profit- they also do charity, both for tax and PR reasons. However, some whisper that there is a catch in those "donations"- that many times, they are accompanied by contracts which give the hospital control over the body of the patient, or that they simply "lease" them the organs, or that they take part in strange experiments that violate the body and soul of the patient. All of those are, obviously, dirty lies, and the hospital will sue anyone who dares to claim them, as well as trying to claim that the reason that the hospital's management does not reveal itself to the public is because they have some dirty secrets to hide, or that they are part of some satanic conspiracy. They even offer their services for hunters- stitching their wounds, treating their diseases, putting monster claws in their hands, transplanting hives into their brains. Their doctors are very skilled surgeons, after all, and it will be a waste to let good old organs to rot away, regardless of their origin.

                  Council of Bones: there are many rumors about the City's graveyard- it is haunted, it is said. It maintains a gateway to the Underworld. It is guarded by a cult dedicated to the dead. All of those rumors are true. The graveyard is found right next to the university, claimed to be so due to the symbolism of maintaining knowledge after death. The transition between the university and the graveyard is almost nonexistent- there are no fences or walls, no signs or blockades. The road just flows into the garden, and before one might notice they are surrounded by graves. This makes the work of the Council of Bones much easier- the self proclaimed Chosen of Death, they are composed of scholars who seek to understand the true nature of ghosts and the undead. The vast majority of their members are found among the university's faculty staff, claiming to maintain an ancient bargain with Death itself from generation to generation meant to serve the dead and protect the living. Only those who are found of sufficient passion to the mysteries of Death are initiated to the cult, and only after trials meant to verify whether an individual ahs an affinity to the Beyond, and if their intentions are indeed pure. Those found to be abusing their gifts are all too welcomed to serve Death after their own demise, after all.​

                  Lucifuge: one of the oldest and richest families in the City, the Luciani are surrounded by mystery and whispers. It is said that they are the main funders of the current mayor, putting him in their debt and controlling his every action. That the matriarch of the family have lived for centuries, ever since that the City was founded, and that the family covers it us using false identities and fake names. That family have gained its wealth through bargains with the Devil, and that they still make sacrifices to demonic forces. The Luciani, themselves, rarely get involved with the general public, keeping their distance from the masses and barely even discussing with people from their own social circles, adding fire to the rumors surrounding them. Regardless of the rumors, the Luciani are treated as a noble family, and they keep their bloodline well supervised, leaning towards arranged marriages as the family document its complex genealogy. According to some leaked documents, those family trees go deep- all the way to the depths of Hell. Documents featuring Lucifer as the progenitor of the family line circle among conspiracy theories, as well as pictures of certain Luciani members fighting demonic entities, or possibly summoning them? The context is unclear, but if the rumors are true, the family seems to use a different name for themselves among their members and allies. The Lucifuge- those who escaped the Light.

                  Malleus Maleficarum: charismatic and handsome, Father Baudolino is the leader of the local catholic congregation in the City. Living in the St Longinus Church, he guides the community with beautiful sermons and gentle care. The Church itself is being cared by a small staff of nuns and priests, all are fully devoted to Baudolino and handpicked by him directly. Little is known, however, that the staff is not merely being trained in theology and care for the poor- each is chosen for their past skills in hand to hand battle, guns and other forms of attack. The City is a dangerous place, after all, and there is always danger to the faithful. The Church itself is being built like a fortress- high walls, spiked fences, locks everywhere... it even has an underground catacombs that according to the rumors is filled with weapons, food, and strange coffins who are bound in iron. The statues of gargoyles and angels watch from the Church's walls, threatening any coming danger, and the pictures of the saints guards the faithful within their walls, seeking any sign of corruption. The Church is basically God's bastion, prepared for war, and if the rumors are being told the priests are fully armed themselves, waging war against heretical horrors under the name of the Hammer of Witches all across the City, all in the name of God.

                  Task Force: VALKYRIE: a lot of strange shit is going around in the City, so it is a little wonder that the government have noticed the constant supernatural activity and have decided to deal with it. Formed to monitor, control and cover up the supernatural, Task Force: VALKYRIE's commanding base sits in a ghost building in the edges of the City- at least, that's what the rumors claim. According to the same rumors, those governmental agents care less about protecting innocent lives and more about regulating the status quo, making sure that the truth won't get out and that chaos will not rise. Their agents all work in some straw company called Midgard Inc, which serves as their cover (Midgard officially deny those claims and have plenty of proofs that they are, indeed, a real consulting company with numerous clients, the mayor included). They never act without orders, even though they themselves don't know who their superiors are and what goals they have- only that they must to keep the charade that the supernatural does not exist. That means not only hunting down those supernatural who cause mayhem and draw attention, but also turning against hunters who are not careful in their job or that try to get the truth out. Still, this is just a rumor, nothing more- that is, until you see agents in black holding weapons made through impossible technology. Then, you better run before you too will become part of the rumors.

                  VASCU: composed out of former special services agents and detectives who served in the police, the Vanguard Serial Crimes Unit is a team of private investigators meant to solve those crimes which law enforcement generally can't reach. There is so much crime in the City, after all, and so little budget to the police, that the authorities simply can't be everywhere all at once, allowing groups such as VASCU to grow- they collect the evidence, find the suspects, track down their victims and build the case, handing it over to the authorities all wrapped up nicely. As private investigators, while VASCU is often employed by private citizens who don't have their wrongs solved by the system, the company must still abide by law, getting proper authorizations when tracking down a suspect and avoiding violence when not in self defense. As most of the unsolved cases are generally being related to monsters, it is a little wonder that VASCU deals mostly with those threats, all of which makes it extremely difficult to build a proper case. Still, VASCU seems to have a pretty high success rate. Nobody known for certain, but the rumor claims that VASCU are not mere special security agents- that they are a product of an experiment, conducted in the City during the Cold War. One which was deemed as a failure and then dismantled, unaware that it did succeeded due to the lab rats hiding their own capabilities. That each of them is an awakened psychic, going through a special treatment called the Wintergreen Process, which awakens those predictive and calculative abilities through mental trauma. That they hunt criminals not because they want to, but because they are designed to do so, and ignoring the feedback will drive them mad. Whether true or not, VASCU are quite good at their job- even though that law breaking hunters are not immune from becoming their targets when a victim files a complain against them.


                  Aaaand that's it for the core conspiracies! Let me know what you are thinking while I consider the other orgs in the game.

                      Ok, so let's talk a bit about the Witch Finder orgs, shall we?

                      Division Six: in a place as crowded with mysteries and supernatural influences as the City, it is a little wonder that it has its fair share of conspiracy theorists. Originally a group of hunters who stumbled upon what they claimed to be "reality deviants", those hunters have clearly caught on something. A few days after researching some occult infrastructure, a person named Mr. Jones have came to them and presented himself as a representative of a local agency meant to deal with those threats. Complimenting them for their dedication and skills, he told them that the government have decided to recruit their services to fight reality deviants, something which the group have happily agreed upon. As part of their recruitment, each member went through a special process meant to "strength their minds against brainwashing", and they were granted guns and resources, as well as some of Mr. Jones's contacts. Now, Division Six serves the country in protection from monsters. Task Force: VALKYRIE have recently found out about the group, something which have caused some questions to rise- Task Force: VALKYRIE have never heard on this so-called "Division Six", and as far as they are aware, they are the only ones assembled by the government to deal with supernatural threats in the City. Still, rivalry between secret service agencies is not unheard of, and so VALKYRIE currently only keeps a close watch, all while trying to find out who "Mt Jones" actually is.

                      Keepers of the Source: the world is dying, and someone has to save it. The Keepers of the Source have started as a peaceful local protest movement in the City, rising against the rapid industrialization of the City which brought air pollution, greenhouse gasses and chemical spills. While initially focusing on demonstrations and strikes, the local government found it way too easy to ignore them, which caused their members to grow more and more aggressive. Some members have even claimed to feel the pain of the planet in their flesh, its screams filling their ears as it got abused by humanity. Things became worse when, in attempt to track down the source of the pain, the Keepers were lead to a gathering of monstrous entities who drained the lifeblood of Mother Earth in the middle of the Central Park. This was too much for them to handle- not only humanity defiles the land, but monsters destroy its spirit as well? While the Keepers have maintained their fight against pollution, they have also found a target in monsters, as they drain the Earth and spread their Taint, forming foul Nests all across the City's sacred places. Yet as many monsters have wormed their way to the high ranks in both the local government and industry, many Keepers have started practicing eco-terrorism, and lately most no longer differentiate between monster held assets and human held ones.

                      Promethean Brotherhood: the museum of natural history contains many precious artifacts, and as such the management makes sure to maintain the best security that money can have. It is not to say, however, that break ins never happen. A few years ago, a person called Jacob Hite have walked through the museum's latest exhibition about Greek culture, when one of the items drew his attention- a large marble tablet, covered in scripture in some unknown language. While he couldn't understand the writing, something in the tablet called him, almost making him understand through the figures on it its secret meaning- power through sacrifice. When asked about the tablet, the guide quickly claimed that the tablet was put out by accident, and that it should be moved to the archive due to the lack of information about it. Jacob couldn't have let it happen. Later that night, there was a break to the museum, and the tablet was stolen- finding its way to Jacob's basement. Covering his tracks behind, Jacob have researched the tablet for years, getting in touch with experts who knew how to keep their mouth shot. Eventually, they unlocked its secrets- a ancient ritual called the Rite of Hecate, where through a special sacrifice one could still the power of a monster. As this point, Jacob's basement looked more like an Orphic temple than anything else, and he and his coworkers have started their hunt in order to test and perfect the Rite. Now, the Promethean Brotherhood is a secret society led by Jacob's son (the Rite has its cost, and Jacob made one too many mistakes to get out of its alive), all dedicated for powering themselves with stolen power- yet by doing so, they gained their fair share of enemies. At the top of the list, however, are the powers behind the museum- the Brotherhood had to burn down their own base more then once as the Aegis Kai Doru planned to raid it. The tablet is theirs after all- and they want it back.

                      Knights of St George: Hosting a diverse and heterogenic society, the City has a small population of Anglicans within its borders. Those followers generally gather in the Church of St George, which is well known for its symbolism of the knight slaying the dragon. The Church itself is pretty simplistic is design, with modest design that almost blends in the background. Its congregation is not too devoted, generally gathering during Sundays and on special events only. As such, most of the time the Church stands empty, hosting only its few priests and most devoted congregation members. That doesn't stop the rumors from spreading, however. According to those whispers, the reason that the Church is being maintained even though its own community lacks interest in its existence is that beneath it, there is a bound demon, one bearing a draconic visage. That the Church itself serves as its seal, keeping it dormant under the power of prayers and sacred ceremonies. That the members of the Church are not some innocent priests- they are all trained in weaponry and martial arts, wearing armors and bearing arms as if they were the knights of old. That the leader of the Church is, in fact, a self proclaimed royal descendant- sometimes, even said to be of a bastard line to Richard the Lionheart himself! Worse, the rumors claim that those secret "Knights of St George" gather in the Church and practice their secret ceremonies not to merely keep the dragon asleep, but to drain its power. That they augment their dreams with the songs of the bound demon, deriving their own demons from their own souls. That they summon those demonic beings in their service- and that they seek out those who try to awaken the dragon from its sleep and slay them, for the wakening of the dragon might destroy the whole city, if not the whole world. And then, after thinking on all of those rumors, one would look on the modest, almost blank building, laugh it out and move on their way. There is no way something so devious hides beneath such a plain surface, after all. One would need to be a extraordinary liar to pull something like that off.

                          So let's move to our next batch of orgs- the Spirit Slayers, shall we?

                          Bear Lodge: the City is a overcrowded, stressed place- countless people go through it, interact, trade, scream, murder, all day long seven days a week. It is little wonder that from time to time, people need to take a break. The most popular resort location is the Forest- a few hours drive, where many lodges and tourist spots are ready for those who want a vacation, taking some time off and being one with nature. While there are many activities for those who go to the Forest, such as tracks, spas and guided tours, one of the more popular activities in those resorts in hunting- and the Forest itself is filled with all kinds of beasts, be them wolves, boars, deer, bears or some other, less natural variety. Among the many resorts dedicated for hunting activity, the Bear Lodge is the most prestigious and well known. Find a place to bury a body? get the smell of burned offerings out of your hair? The Recalbirators have everything you need. Just keep your mouth shut- the policy is always "no questions asked". They don't ask how you got to your little situation, you don't ask what they are going to do with the remains or how they know how to deal with the poisonous medusa head. And most important than all- if you hire their services, you keep your mouth shut. You tell no one about that instance, or otherwise you ruiin the cleaning up process. Due to their secrecy and efficiency, the Recalbrators are very popular among the criminal organizations of the City- the Guadanas and the Ascending Ones hire their services frequently, and the St Cheiron hospital have a few constant staff workers in their facilities, while LDI and Deva Corporations both regularly invite their services after they have a "mess up" in their lab. The organization still tries to deal with the leftovers of the Merrick incident, and God the children don't make their life easier as they keep go and burn down more and more laboratories. Even common hunters call for their help in time of need, and they are not above offering their services for monsters- they just need to make sure they keep their mouth shut about it. Hunters who seek after the truth, however, such as Network 0, the Keepers of the Source and the Schulze family, have a much more tense relationship with them, as they try to expose the crimes that the Recalibrators work so hard to hide.
