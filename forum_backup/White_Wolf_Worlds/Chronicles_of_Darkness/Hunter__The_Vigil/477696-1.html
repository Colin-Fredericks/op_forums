                                I've already said which is my least-favourite group, but I want to mention that I also love the Ashwood Abbey. They're honestly one of my favourites. They hunt not because they have to - wealth and privilege frankly probably inure them to most supernatural ills - but because they want to, and there is something so wonderfully intriguing about that. Most other Hunters have some extrinsic motivation; they want to protect their family/neighbourhood, they believe their mission is divinely ordained, they subscribe to some noble philosophy, they were born with tainted blood, etc., but the Abbey chooses to hunt. They see this world full of vicious nightmares that they never actually have to be a part of if they don't want to and choose to throw themselves at it with wide smiles. They're the wild game hunters of the modern day; the mad wanderers who ran to the unexplored (by Europeans, at least) corners of the Earth, throwing caution to the wind and risking everything for the sake of adventure. They are so jaded by living in a world where their status is largely assured that they have to risk everything on hedonistic pleasure-seeking.

                                When I think of the Ashwood Abbey, it's just as easy to picture an old man with a mustache that probably has its own seat at the UN lounging in a room full of rifles and big game trophies as it is the young trust-fund tommies smashing mailboxes with baseball bats out the window of a convertible. Honestly, the former probably comes more easily than the latter, because the quick-to-the-trigger kiddies probably don't last as long as those who have more respect for their sport.

                                The Ashwood Abbey probably does feel like a slap to the face of most Hunters but that's a part of why they're fun, just as, say, the Lucifuge calling on demonic power probably feels like a slap to many Hunters, or claiming your hunt is divine retribution probably feels like a slap to many Hunters, or profiting off your hunt feels like a slap, etc.

                                Not everyone's in the Vigil for the same reasons. Different motivations for the same action can and should be something that makes Hunters look askew at each other. After all, even the most supposedly benevolent Hunter is still a murderous obsessive.
