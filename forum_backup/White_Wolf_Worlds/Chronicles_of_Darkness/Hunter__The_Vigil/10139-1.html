                                            The source material delivers! Delta Green has an intense rivalry with MAJESTIC-12, the still-official government program that collaborates with Grey aliens and doesn't believe in the supernatural. They're equally frosty with PISCES, the United Kingdom's secret service for paranormal threats, on account of them being compromised by Azathoth-worshipping alien insect brain parasites. There's their allies in Russia, the terminally-underfunded GRU SV-8, and the dubiously-canon Section Disparu of France, who lack any supernatural knowledge but seek to undermine international US influence. The Vatican has its also questionably-canon Congregation, who take a scorched earth approach to the supernatural that makes DG look downright moderate. Then there's their classic enemy, the Karotechia, who began as Nazi Germany's occult branch of the Ahnenerbe and now desperately survive as a loose network of aging war criminals and fascist thugs hoping to grow a Fourth Reich with the ghost of Hitler (really just another mask of Nyarlathotep) at the head. Rounding out the bunch is The Fate, a gang with Mythos ties that rules the occult underground of New York City and has a hot-cold relationship with DG, on account of Stephen Alzis' (again, another Nyarlathotep mask - the guy gets around!) fickle moods.

                                            When the Dracula Dossier (for Night's Black Agents) releases, I'll be mining anti-vampire groups from it for further crossover; I believe NBA shares writing talent with a lot of great Cthulhu games.

                                                            Okay, I don't even know anything about Delta Green beyond it being a CoC spin-off involving black ops governmental task forces fighting the Mythos, and I still think that this works wonders. They're a great fit for the World of Darkness, especially when you play up the cosmic horror elements - we have spirits, the Abyss, the God-Machine, the Principle, and all manner of other oddities and horrors that fit right into DG''s motif, even if you never want to bring in the actual Mythos critters themselves. Hell, Mythologies for 1e VtR included a monstrosity called the Urbiphage that would fit right into a Delta Green campaign, at least from what admittedly little I know. Also, personally, if I were to try and fit DG into the NWoD, I'd try to adapt roles where I can. For example, if Majestic-12 doesn't fit due to being too tightly tied to the Mi-Go, you could always rework it that Delta Green's vendetta is against Task Force Valkyrie, since the latter managed to keep itself on the government's payroll and a steady supply of advanced gizmos, whilst DG was let go and has to make do with whatever occult trinkets it can scrounge up.
