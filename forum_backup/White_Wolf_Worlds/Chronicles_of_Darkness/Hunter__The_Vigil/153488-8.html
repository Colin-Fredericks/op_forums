    169: Dodging traps, looting temples, it's just another day for the Ageis Kai Doru. Well not so fast. This is a lost temple of the Ageis Kai Doru.

    170: (Task: Force VALKARIE): You got a call from a naval officer stationed near Somalia. It turns out these pirates weren't quite human, actually they're changelings. They're acting like they're straight from a swashbuckling pirate film, and the world's playing along. When one pirate managed to escape 10 trained marines with their wallets and the female officer's phone number by swinging on chandeliers they realised they need help. FORT is insisting that their magic specifically targets upstanding soldiers and law enforcement, the only options are massive overkill or for VALKARIE to reply with it's own misfits. But VALKARIE doesn't have lovable rogues, it has war criminals, psychopaths and the Geneva Convention violating scum of the Earth. Better that than massive overkill right?

    171: (Ascending Ones): Politicians are being found dead of mysterious diseases. On the streets preachers are spreading like wildfire, each claiming that Allah has sent his sword to punish those who have sold out the common people. As chaos spreads the people you've sworn to protect are beginning to suffer. Little do you know the group responsible is the Ascending Ones, armed with alchemical posions and elixirs to induce religious visions a branch of the Knife of Paradise has decided to reshape society. Not a rogue branch either, the Ascending Ones communication links aren't perfect and you never got the word.

    172: Sin-Eaters like the big parties, Mardie Gras, Carnival, but not as much as they like their own exclusive gatherings. That's why they come every year to your dying city (what other kind for Sin-Eaters?) to party like they die tomorrow. It's not like there's much of a masqurade left with so many weird people, in full Krewe colours, too drunk and high to think about secrecy. Most people shut their doors, close the blinds and cower, but the street gangs are becoming reflections of Sin-Eater culture. And it's working, something about a dying city is letting them draw real power from those faux-occult gang signs every group is adopting.

    [edit] So long as I'm on the subject of the Ascending One's playing politics.

    173: The Arab Spring, secular dictators falling and religious groups in to clean up the rubble. Who'd benefit from that? Mummies that's who. The Ascending One knows how to fight the Arisen, and they know why it's usually not worth their time. They've been pulling out of the Middle East, fortresses temples, drug warehouses. Move that shit somewhere safer like Africa. But they're not abandoning their duties, just adjusting their strategies. They're leaving behind cells of the best, hassassins. The high priests, the politicians the mummies put up, the cultists with the highest follower counts on twitter, if they can kill them fast enough the opportunity will fade away and Islamists rather than Iremites will end up in power.

    (Basically, the Arab Spring looks like it did in real life, because the Ascending Ones kept assassinating all the cultists trying to bring back Iremite theocracy)

            175:

            The Myth: Slender Man

            The Truth: The Slender Man isn't a spirit, or one of the Others. Slender Man is a phenomena. A demonic influence that spreads from person to person and gains power from delusions and terror. He is a nightmare given life by people. He spreads like a virulent infection, using his self-inflicted victims as proxies. His story can be snuffed out, but he cannot die. Even if you run, even if his manifestation vanishes, you can escape from him no more than you can escape your own thoughts. He ends where memory does - hidden over time and sleeping like an old tale. He doesn't need to chase you. His shadow becomes real the moment you believe it. But Slender Man had to begin somewhere. And somewhere, there must be a way to stop him for good.

            The Premise: Your friend or someone important in your community is gone. Vanished into thin air after months of acting strangely. Tracking them down reveals them as a victim of something far more macabre than it might seem at first glance. The last in a trail of deadly incidents and disappearances that has spanned generations. If you follow, you must do so through hazy details, questionable accounts, and strange clues. And be prepared, let you become the next link in a chain of madness.

                176: There are stories like it all over the place - the witch, the serial killer, the creature that peers through the windows at night. They pop up every few years, and people go missing as the monster follows a kind of occult schedule before the creature disappears once again. This one is real. You have the journals and rusted weaponry from a previous generation of hunters to prove it. The monster will appear again soon, and your cell is ready to take it down for good.

                Slight wrinkle - It looks like there are two of them now. But the patterns don't match, and monsters don't leave fingerprints. Everyone knows the story, and it looks like someone decided to play copycat, hiding their misdeeds behind the story of an urban legend. Can you afford to split your attention between a murderer and a monster before they both finish and disappear?

                            179) Most Hunters don't have a long life expectancy, but this one has somehow managed to make it. They're a veteran of dozens of conflicts, and has managed to survive long enough to rack up an impressive collection of scars. A pillar of the local Vigil, they've likely helped out and been helped out by the players a couple of times, and now they've finally decided the pc Cell is trustworthy. They offer the players a way to really step up their game, to really take the fight to the monsters. If they agree, the veteran will introduce the players to their friend.

All they need to do is drink deep, and they'll have the power to light the night. Is the veteran somehow telling the truth, or are they just blinded by Vitae?                                 180) Your cell's been gathering intel on this vampire for weeks now, but on a stakeout (ha!) of his haven, one of your team spots an unmarked van pull up out front, throw open the doors, and let loose a hunting pack of naked, pale /things/ that effortlessly found the bloodsucker and drained him dry. Similar reports pop up across town in the days that follow, painting a grim picture: someone - the government, maybe, or a big corporation - has things that crave vampires like they crave us.

                                181) An office worker casually smashes the glass case for the fire axe and cleaves a bloody swathe through his coworkers, but has no memory of the incident when the police finally stop his rampage. It's easy to write this off as an isolated incident of someone going postal... but it happens four more times in the same month, each time a normal, sane person lapsing into a fugue and swinging wildly with a hammer of hatchet. The media is spinning it as a violent mass hysteria, but the vigilant recognize it for what it is: possession by the spirit of a long-dead berserker. What brought this dead raider back to life? How can he be stopped?

                                And perhaps most importantly: why is he so far from home?

                                182) (Utopia Now): Stupid people think you're just one of the dozens of tech startups trying to become the next industry giant, but you know you're building the future - and you've done terrible things to make that happen. So when a Chinese billionaire offers to buy out the entire organization, it understandably sets off alarm bells. He seems to have more money than God himself, and it looks like he didn't exist before last week.

                                            185. Deep beneath the city are a system of catacombs so old their purpose has long been forgotten. In these dark tunnels dwell strange, sinewy, pale creatures that can see and somehow survive in this dank environment. This would be horrible enough alone, but things get much, much worse. These things are so inbred they cannot reproduce without human breeding stock. A cult of the wealthy elite in the city provides them just such, for a sinister purpose. You see, every now and then, a child born from these unwholesome unions is indistinguishable from a normal person, which a strange golden caul covering its eyes. In fact, they are always beautiful, charismatic, and incredibly lucky. The cult snatches these children up, adopt them into their families, and use the talents of the child as they grow up to ensure their continuing wealth and power. How will your cell deal with the horrors in the catacombs below, and can they escape the conspiracy in the city that works to protect these vile acts?

                                                186: The latest news is actually pretty good. The leeches aren't going out and biting unwilling necks, werewolves are keeping to cease fire lines, and witches just want to study their magic.

                                                So, of course, something goes wrong. Some dumbass mad scientist rolled into town with a small army of lackies, shouting from the hilltops that he wants all to bow to his will or die. To prove his point, he livestreamed the transformation of the city's oldest vampire from a withered husk of a man into a small rabbit. Which was then fed to the ghouls he turned into dogs.

                                                The important discovery is that this madman's creations can't even be touched by actual scientists who can explain why such creations can't work. So that's where your cell of rationalist Null Mysteriis members come in. Unfortunately, one group will fight tooth and nail to stop you; the Open-Minds the next town over who want to tout the "genius" as proof of their beliefs. Time to throw some science in this bitch.

                                                    187: Hunting for sport is already controversial among humane society groups, but even fellow hunters frown when someone kills an animal just for the fun of it. Recently, there have been reports of murders happening in the area. But the strange thing about the killings is that the victim is just left there for anybody to find. Nothing is stolen or planted on their body, there is no sign of a trophy being taken, no hidden clues or cryptic messages to decipher. Whoever the culprit is, they're killing people just because they have the power to do so, without any sign of a motive involved! It's up to your cell to put a stop to this bloodshed, and discover if it is supernatural in origin.
