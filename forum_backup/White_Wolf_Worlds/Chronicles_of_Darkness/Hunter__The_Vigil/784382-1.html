            On Converting Comic Characters into Game stats.

            The variance in capabilities and personality of these characters based on both the writer and incarnation of the character makes this sort of project tricky. Each of us has a interpretation we consider "canon". Batman ranges from a man so hyper-competent he can fight gods (and win!) to a street level vigilante that can barely keep his city free of muggers and pimps.

            At least for the Chronicles, my instincts always say keep the scale of the system in mind. The Storyteller system can't really do most of the power tiers in a DC/Marvel style supers game. But the system can definitely model the section the "mystery men" tier of the Genre. Using a Batman that is more detective and vigilante than Super Hero (as well as similar characters such as the extended Bat Family, Marvel's Daredevil and the Punisher, or pulp mystery men like the Phantom or the Shadow) seem to be be just the proper level of power conceptually and tonally.

            The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
            Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
            The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
            Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
            Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

                    I've been thinking about the possibility of having "Purple Gloves" Batman as an ex-Hunter who became an Avenger Slasher, and is on his way to becoming a Legend. That would turn our expectations of the character on their heads. As Bruce Wayne, he donates quite a bit of his immense wealth to humanitarian causes in Gotham and elsewhere, which actually helps to decrease the level of crime. The Batman persona is not glamorous for him, it is his skeleton in the closet. While wearing that suit, he is only barely aware that murder isn't justice. His relationship with the Joker would be the same, but even more tense. With no compulsion to spare criminals, the Joker would risk death every time he is caught. His goal would still be to give Batman the little push he needs to become a Legend.

                                                            Well, I'm hardly the most caught-up on the massive array of Batman rogues at this point in time, but, going by my own limited knowledge, this is what I'd suggest of the major names who don't immediately suggest just "crazy human" - for example, Penguin and The Ventriloquist are physically deformed (arguably, in the former case) and insane respectively, but there's nothing "meta" about them.

                                                            The Joker: I think the most natural "fit" for this character would be a Slasher of the Maniac undertaking, which reflects his twisted combination of insanity and charm, reflecting how he somehow manages to keep amassing loyal followers despite his tendency to kill anyone around him. If you want to play with the whole "I'm really an immortal agent of evil" thing he tried to mindrape Batman with in the recent storyline that killed them both off, he could be spun as some kind of humaniform Horror.

                                                            Harley Quinn: With her official "power up" at Poison Ivy's hands, I'd argue Harley Quinn is best suited for a Deviant basis. She is a cold-blooded killer, but it's not a driving compulsion like it is with other members of the Rogue's Gallery - she doesn't tend to rack up the constant bodycount her boyfriend and galpal do.

                                                            Poison Ivy: When statting Ivy, one needs to remember there's at least two versions of the character to look at; the pre-modern version was a skilled botanical engineer and chemist who just happened to be a murderous vamp and ecoterrorist, and the modern version, which has given her full-fledged "chlorokinetic" powers. For the pre-modern version, a Charmer or Psycho (depending on how much you want to emphasize her insanity) with a unique "immunity to poison" trait, mutated plants (use the Horror rules) as mooks and poisonous lipstick as a Signature Weapon would be my recommendation. For the modern version, I'd go with a Deviant or a full-fledged Horror, depending on just what kind of powers Deviants actually get.

                                                            Mr. Freeze: A Deviant without any question.

                                                            Killer Croc: As GibberingEloquence stated above, a Mutant or Freak (depending on whether you want the "deformed human" or "full-fledged gator-man" version of the character) Slasher is probably the best fit for Killer Croc.

                                                            Two-Face: I'd argue he quite neatly fits the Freak basis for a Slasher, the only problem being if you consider him murderous enough to qualify as a Slasher. He could just be handled as a low-Integrity human with a "flaw" that gives him a sharp penalty to some Social skills to reflect his physical deformity.

                                                            Mad Hatter: I'd argue either a Maniac Slasher with a fixation more on mind controlling than killing or else a Deviant with a technological basis to his mind control powers.
