    (Conspiracy) The Disillusioned

    07-10-2016, 01:50 AM
    (Idea came from a Conspiracy that comes from both Russia and Japan. Please leave constructive criticism, and I hope its useful for your own games)

    In the city of Tokyo, a man is killed by a single stab from an umbrella. That he has fangs, or is mostly bulletproof, does nothing to prevent his death from the poison flowing through his veins. He will be nothing but ash the following morning, unnoticed in the crowded city.

    A group searches San Francisco for an infestation that has been there since 1944. This infestation takes the form of creatures that kill children, and lay their young in the corpses of said children. The group has managed to kill hundreds of them, knowing their weakness, but many do not, and this has left many defenseless.

    Every year since 1993, thousands of mobsters are murdered throughout Russia. The investigations never even get started because the deaths just look like typical junkies overdoses. The only noticeable artifact is that some of the victims have traces of silver in their blood.

    Of the many suicides in the country of Bulgaria, some will be done against the victim's will. That they have access to a magic of sorts does little to prevent the ingestion of chemicals they won't know about until it is far too late. Nor will it prevent the suicide note that is written in their handwriting, or the prescriptions in their names that will appear afterwards.


    When an empire falls, it leaves much in its wake. Surplus weapons flow around the globe, causing chaos and carnage in their wake. Scars left by horrific crimes still linger and burn, while giving the fuel for more fires to burn, and more tyrants to rise from the ashes. But most of all, many are left with blood on their hands, and nobody left to assure them it was the right thing to do.

    What happens when what you had faith in is dead and gone? What do you believe in anymore? And, how can you make up for everything you did?

    An organization has answered those questions with poison and piles of bodies. In the Balkans, they are a grocery store chain, with pharmacies in every store, ready to spike prescriptions or food as deemed necessary. In Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Taiwan, they wait in dockyards to poke unsuspecting targets with umbrellas or alter their lunches with special ingredients. In Russia, they have a slice of the drug trade, and will make their target's favorite highs be their last. And most of all, in the United States, they hide as a pizza company with branches in California, Oregon, Washington, and Hawaii. It is still profitable despite all the customers dying of heart attacks.

    Regardless of source, it is hard for anyone, or anything, to defend themselves from attacks that are so unexpected. Quite useful when fighting things that are bulletproof, or something else unimaginable.

          (Posting the history now, and thanks for the feedback Lostlight!)
          History
          How did an organization of poisoners form and spread over such radically different regions? The answer lies in two states that no longer exist: Imperial Japan, and the Soviet Union.

          The very beginning of the organization began in 1947. That year, a doctor from Japan, whose identity has long been lost to secrecy and history, would be the first member of what would become the Disillusioned. The doctor had immense guilt and shame over what he had done for Imperial Japan as part of the military, however, lacked any real means to make up for it. That is, until he found out about Imperial Japan had done in desperation to defeat the Allies in World War 2. It had made deals with many supernatural entities, and had even created creatures of its own. None of this would prevent the defeat and destruction of Imperial Japan, but the things it supported still remained.

          While a few of these entities were in Japan, most of them were in countries that had once been part of the Empire of Japan. That was when he realized what he could do to, in some manner, make up to those he had helped hurt. Using his extensive medical knowledge, and careful study of the creatures in question, the doctor created a variety of poisons with which to kill these creatures. This allowed a man who was very possibly quite old and frail to take out things far stronger than him. But for him, it was also that he didn't want his former victims to know it was him that protected them.

          With time, the doctor realized he needed help. Recruits proved easy to find for his crusade however. Some were driven by guilt like him, others were driven by shame, and there were a few who just wanted to continue carrying out violence, just needing a new cause. The group standardized the doctor's poisons, refined them, and invented more to deal with new threats. More than that, the group found various new ways to deliver the poisons, although they ended up focusing on delivering poisons through everyday objects.

          This was done because the first group tended towards, overtime, killing entities near docks, or in some other manner, the ocean. Sneaking poison into the food for a specific sailor, or dock worker that the group would only have brief contact with wasn't practical. Additionally, in the case of Japan's more monstrous creations, the poison frequently needed to be delivered in a more forceful manner, as one would deal with say, a rabid predator. Hence, this required poison delivered through everyday objects, such as umbrellas or pins, so as to quickly inject targets before they went on long trips in the sea. For more bestial targets, various tools were made that were carefully disguised as fishing tools, to allow the creature to not only be killed, but also captured, so that its corpse could be properly disposed of. The goal in this case was to not only kill the creatures, but make sure no one would ever have the means to make them again.

          All of this would become known as the Imperial Section of the organization, in reference to Imperial Japan, and would be the first part of the organization. Its operations, to this day, are focused in former provinces of the Empire of Japan, however, they have had to expand to the United States after finding out Japan's creations have also ended up there. The need to cover the entirety of the Pacific, given the sheer number of areas Japan spread its creatures, necessitated more recruits. This combined with the realization that they weren't the only people who had been part of countries that no longer existed, or had guilt attached to horrible things they had done for it. It would also result in a new means of recruitment.


          The next wave of recruits would come from the former Soviet Union. Seeing Communism fall, and Russia been torn apart in the aftermath made them thoroughly disillusioned. Seeing many of their peers become mercenaries, or enter organized crime hardly helped with this. Like the Imperial section, they found many monsters their government had aided, or in some cases, created, throughout Russia proper, and states that had once been part of it.

          They would find the organization at just the right time, as the Imperial section was fast running out of its original recruits, and they brought the perfect skill set for the organization's needs. Unlike their Japanese counterparts, many would end up having a background in organizations like the KGB, which gave them the training necessary to carry out a very different style of poisoning to their Eastern counterparts.

          Their targets were frequently embedded in the state, were rich businessmen, or were big in organized crime. While this made them powerful, they also had routines, and many had addictions to drugs, sex, or something more esoteric. Therefore, a target was analyzed in depth, and then poison was delivered through something the target consumed everyday. Even money itself could be poisoned with the right means.

          Besides a new tactic by which to poison, the ex-Soviet members also introduced a new means of recruitment. Called the,"Outer Ring," various members were inducted into the group with only the knowledge they were helping to kill monsters. The means by which poisons were made, and in some cases, that poison was even the means by which they were killing targets, was not something they knew. These Outer Ring agents sometimes didn't even know they were hunting monsters, but were instead employees of companies that could be given very strict sets of seemingly innocent instructions which allowed the delivery of poison.

          The Imperial Section by now had members which owned large corporations, and therefore, could make any employee an, "Outer Ring," agent with the necessary tools. What would become the Party Section, referencing the single Party nature of the Soviet Union, would instead focus on using members of organized crime groups, who had more knowledge of their tasks compared to their Imperial counterparts, to be Outer Ring members. Both sections found they could eventually gain core agents through this Outer Ring as well, as frequently used Outer Ring agents would naturally prove themselves, or be weeded out by their very tasks.


          One last section would arise however. Its origins would be the chaos in countries like Bulgaria, Romania, and Hungary after the fall of the Soviet Union, along with the failure of shock economics. They had been promised the riches and ideals of the West, but instead, gained poverty, and governments that were just as corrupt as their Soviet counterparts. Disillusioned with their new states, they became known as the Activist Section, from their origins of those who had campaigned for their own states. While their states hadn't created monsters, they certainly acted in coalition with them, and allowed them to run amok.

          Rather than guilt or shame, they were motivated by rage and disappointment. They would be the only section to recruit from victims of their creations directly, something considered ghastly by the other two sections, as they themselves weren't perpetrators, or at least, didn't view themselves that way. Their joining also cemented the nickname for the organization, the Disillusioned.

          The tactics of those in the Balkans were a blend of the other two. They didn't feel the need to be quite as in depth as those in the Soviet Union, but weren't as quick or improvisational as those in Japan. Instead, they blended the two by creating a chain of grocery stores that many potential targets would use. Once a target was identified, a poison was matched to them, and then, put into whatever the target most consumed. The consumption patterns of typical targets, such as Vampires and Werewolves, were studied, to allow procedures to made for their mass elimination. For example, if a target consumed lots of meat, they could know to poison the meat at checkout in just the right manner that the target wouldn't know about it until it was far too late.

          Over all, by the current year, the Disillusioned have grown quite large, with a small inner circle managing hundreds of thousands of potential hunters through the Outer Ring system, and using poisons to eliminate countless targets...

              The Enemy
              The organization hunts monsters either aided or created by the regimes they once served. This gives quite a variety of enemies to hunt, and gives the organization one of the largest areas to cover of any hunter organization in human history. Further adding to their workload is that humans who worked with the regime, and more importantly, have continued to harm the regime's victims, are open as targets. As if all that wasn't enough, they consider it their duty to protect people they helped hurt, or in the case of the Activist Section, failed.

              On top of all of this, in recent times, both the Party Section and the Imperial section have carried out operations outside of their usual hunting grounds, called Foreign Operations. They range from the United States to modern day Germany, because they have found creatures from their regimes there. Additionally, schemes concocted by their former masters involving supernatural entities are still playing out in these countries. Finally, supernatural entities empowered by said regimes are frequently loyal to their ideologies, a horror neither Section can stomach.

              This wide array of targets is a reason the organization's separate groups are so regional. For example, the Imperial and Party sections will certainly trade information, techniques, and poison recipes for hunting, but they will rarely hunt in each other's regions, because they simply cannot spare the personnel for such an endeavor. They do work together directly on foreign operations, however, these operations are temporary in nature, and once they are complete, they split back to their usual regions.

              The sheer variety and number of targets lead to the Outer Ring strategy, so as to give access to far more potential recruits than the organization would otherwise have. In addition, the poison focus of the organization allows persons usually far too weak to ever fight monsters to do so, and allows a tailored response to bizarre threats, such as some of the creatures created by former regimes. Additionally, it allows the organization to remain hidden despite its now immense size, and for them to protect people from afar without those they protect knowing, as all the sections of the organizations prefer to...

                                  Sections

                                  Despite being regional, the sections take their names from their former ideologies. This notably has caused each section to, in many ways, become an opposite to the ideology they once followed. Their methods also now reflect the ideologies they have become increasingly attracted to. Even the poisons they pick now follow this.

                                  However, they are all unified together by their decision to use poison, and pooling resources to hunt down those whom they once served. More than guilt, or shame, there is a rage that simmers behind the organization. A rage at those who had lied to them, or cheated them. All of this is in their true name, the name that isn't shared until one rises to the very top.

                                  Nani no Teikoku, the Empire of Nothing. When there are no empires of any type remaining, then the organization shall fold, or that's at least the plan. This new purpose, despite being in Japanese, actually comes from the Activist section of the organization. While they currently focus on destroying the remnants of the old Empire, soon, they may go after any state they consider to possess an Empire.

                                  Their unified means has also done much to unify the organization. In order to insure it doesn't warp into yet another Empire, every section of the organization hides an essential set of poison recipes. If any of one of the organizations warps into something horrific, the other two can deny it critical resources, and then begin the process of destroying it. This practice evolved out of something the original doctor who founded the organization did by denying the entirety of the organization's primary weapon to any significant person or group within it. While this has caused inefficiency, it does bind the organization together by forcing co-dependency.

                                  Imperial Section: Originating from the Empire of Japan, they have become something new. Poisoning the monsters that the Empire of Japan had helped, or even created causes has caused them to be primarily based in the Pacific.

                                  The Imperial Section's origins has had very interesting impacts on its ideology. They have become to feel almost paternalistic to those they had once harmed by taking care of their children, and trying to fix societal problems that plague the areas Japan once occupied. While they have a strong aversion for ever being noticed by those they protect, everything short of that is at least considered. This has ironically made them attracted to Socialism, despite owning mega-corporations and the warnings cast by the Party Sector.

                                  The section has very improvised means of eliminating targets, using everyday objects, from umbrellas and pins, all the way up to, at the highest level, gloves. The recipes kept by the Imperial Section are Instant, for they kill targets in, at most, minutes, and frequently, seconds.This fits well with the section that is forced to get so close to targets.

                                  Party Section: Originating from the Soviet Union, they poison the monsters helped or created in the name of Lenin, Stalin, or even just Socialism. They are primarily based in Russia, with the Ukraine, Belarus, and the Baltics important subsidiary areas. Poland, and the Balkans they leave up now to the Activist section.

                                  Like the Imperial section, they have changed ideologically. Unlike the Imperial section, it comes more out of necessity, rather than fondness, as the Party section cares little for Capitalism, it just finds it better than the alternatives. They have seen its excesses during the 1990s, but consider it an acceptable system if the worst excesses can be reigned in. They themselves are forced to indulge in said excesses given they are now primarily based in organized crime.

                                  Their primary tactic is to study a target, and find out what they like to consume. After some time, a poison is slipped into something they consume every day, and they die. Frequently, they also act strangely, so as to discredit them, which is helped by that the poison is made to look like illicit drugs. The recipes kept by the Party Section are Mind-Altering so as to discredit and humiliate targets, not just kill them. Sometimes, targets aren't even killed, they are made into social pariahs that lose all influence because of random and unpredictable behavior.

                                  Activist Section: Originating from ex-Soviet satellites in the Balkans, and recently appearing in Poland, they poison monsters, and now corrupt officials who they feel betrayed the promises of the 1990s.

                                  Of all the sections, the Activist Section is the most defined by rage. They feel the states they live have constantly been under subjugation by either the East or the West, whether it be literally or ideologically. Having been burned by Capitalism so rapidly after the failure of Communism has left the group feeling the potential of their countries has been wasted by one foreign state's ideological crusade after another. Both other sections see the haunting of an angry Nationalism possessing the group, something that they are all too familiar with the impacts of, whether it be in their country's past or present.

                                  The primary tactic of the Activist section comes out of a regional group of grocery stores that double as pharmacies. Here, tens of thousands of employees are unknowingly used as spies and a delivery mechanism for the slaughter of appropriate targets. The Activist Section has become very good at using in store surveillance to identify likely targets. This has allowed an almost automated system of killing targets, although there are many cases where a more personal touch is required. In either case, although especially in the latter, the Activist section keeps Agony poison recipes that cause the target immense pain before killing them. In addition, these poisons are frequently gory in a manner that makes people fear the organization in the region.

                                  The Imperial, Party, and Activist Section all need each other's poisons from time to time, enforcing the organization solidarity.


                                  The differences between sections in methods, regions covered, and ideology all come together to make the organization extremely difficult to detect as a whole. That their most common name is just a nickname doesn't help matters. Even for other Conspiracies, detecting the Conspiracy, despite its sheer size, is a pain, because of just how diffuse it is. Some say they may even be trying to hide from something darker...

                                          Poisons of the Disillusioned






                                          The conspiracy's poison specialty demands much of its members, because rather than being something that's part of their bodies, or something given to them, members are expected to know how to make the poisons on their own. Actually using the poisons is quite easy, however, making them is a different manner entirely.

                                          To use any of the conspiracy's poisons requires a successful Science+Intelligence roll, which allows usage of the poison in a later scene. Because poisons need to be tailored to their targets, one needs to know what type, if any, supernatural entity the target is before making the poison. In addition, until Status 3, a member is restricted to the poisons of their particular Section. Any poison of equivalent merit dots in the Merit Poisons of the Disillusioned and of your section until Status 3 can be made.

                                          Outside of remedies by the Conspiracy, or spotting the poisons before they enter your system, target gets a single Stamina+Resolve-Toxicity roll per day to resist the impact of the poison. Unless otherwise specified, requires 3 successes.

                                          Below are a few examples of the poisons the group possesses.

                                          On Demand Cardiac Arrest (1 Dot)

                                          Invented by the Imperial Section, and a name with a grim sense of humor, the poison gives the target a natural appearing heart attack in the space of twenty seconds. This is to give just enough time for an assassin to put space between themselves and the target. Death will appear as a natural heart attack, although this can raise an eyebrow with particularly healthy targets. Requires the usage of an umbrella, pin, or other implement that can, if only lightly, penetrate opponent's skin.

                                          Toxicity of 3.

                                          Mother of Rage (1 Dot)

                                          Invented by the Party Section, this poison will usually kill a target one week from ingestion. While subject is impacted by poison, they must make three Resolve+Composure rolls, per day, to avoid flying into a rage. The successes required go up each day the person is impacted by the poison, starting at two successes and ending at needing eight successes.
                                          Assuming someone doesn't kill them during a rage, the target will appear to die of an overdose on a stimulant, such as methamphetamines. Most often delivered through tricking the target into smoking it in some form.

                                          Toxicity of 3.

                                          Vengeance of the Starved (1 Dot)

                                          Invented by the Activist Section, this poison will kill a target in two days. Targets are known to violently vomit for hours upon hours before dying to the poison. In post-mortem, poison appears as a rather nasty stomach illness, especially as the poison comes with the necessary germs for this to appear to be the case. Most often delivered through meat based products.

                                          Toxicity of 3.

                                          Universal Allergy (3 Dots)

                                          Imperial Section poison that kills in seconds, and appears to be extreme anaphylactic shock to any onlookers. This poison, unlike On Demand Cardiac Arrest, can be done by just touching the target anywhere on their skin. This type of poison is most commonly delivered wearing special gloves to take advantage of this.

                                          Toxicity of 5.

                                          Father of Gloom (3 Dots)

                                          Invented by the Party Section, this poison kills in the space of about two weeks. Before hand, the target will become extremely depressed, and frequently suicidal. Assuming the target doesn't take their own life before the end of said two weeks, they will appear to die from an overdose of a depressant like alcohol. The poison has the additional benefit of being a potent truth agent, allowing an interrogation of sorts to be done of targets before they die to help find more targets they could know. Most often delivered through pills, often made to look like prescription medication.

                                          Toxicity of 5.

                                          Vengeance of the Silenced (3 Dots)

                                          Poison invented by the Activist section, and will kill targets in three days. Causes massive bleeding from the gums, and the rotting of death hours before death. Has the side effect of rotting the rest of corpse away, to the point of leaving no usable evidence of the corpse to work with. This causes most of those killed by this poison to become missing person casings before being forgotten entirely. It is most often delivered through sugar heavy products, like cake or certain brands of cereal.

                                          Toxicity of 5.

                                          Dramatic Death (5 Dots)

                                          Extremely fast acting toxin that kills targets in the space of one second. The rapid death it causes, even by the standards of the Imperial Section, causes the poison to be reversed for circumstances where many targets have to be killed in rapid succession. This has caused those using it to hide the poison behind what appears to be martial arts, when in reality they are just using dramatic movements to hide a powerful toxin. Those looking at from afar will seem to see someone killing with a single punch, which has lead to a variety of its own myths. Some have even suggested this poison has unintentionally influenced pop culture a few times.

                                          Requires 5 successes, and has a Toxicity of 7.

                                          Master of All(5 Dots)

                                          A terrifying concoction of the Party Section, this poison doesn't kill, or at least, not initially. Rather, it turns someone into an addict to a drug more addictive than any other, including heroin. This addictiveness is paired with an inability to enact violence against the controller of the drug. So, the only hope of getting more fixes is to do what the person who has more of it says.

                                          Resisting the addiction requires a Resolve+Composure roll, and requires 7 successes. Each day poisoned by Master of All creates a larger and larger penalty to resisting the addiction, ending on -5 penalty to resisting the drug.

                                          This poison will eventually kill, as inevitably, the person will use far too much of it at once. After death, it will appear like a heroin overdose, or similar, in any post-mortem analysis. When the poison is used, as the Conspiracy brutally punishes any use of the poison that isn't absolutely necessary, is to gain control of not only a target, but those around them. The most common delivery mechanism are needles, so as to make it appear like heroin.

                                          If one overdoses, requires 5 successes on a Stamina+Resolve roll,

                                          A poison crafted by the Activist section to not only kill a target, but murder those around them as well. After an hour has passed, a target will die in an explosion of infectious gore. This gory attack, besides being quite painful, will kill anyone hit by the gore as well from its extreme toxicity. Area impacted is usually three meters, or about nine feet.
                                          This poison is only used when targets are known to be soon arriving with a group of targets, such as a Vampire going to a place with many other Vampires within the timespan of an hour. Dozens, and in one case, hundreds of targets have been eliminated using this horrific poison. Misuse of this poison is a death penalty from how horrific the consequences are. It is most often delivered in finger foods, such as fries or popcorn, so as to insure the target ingests the poison on the way to a gathering of many other targets.

                                          Requires 5 successes, per day, to survive, and has a toxicity of 7.
