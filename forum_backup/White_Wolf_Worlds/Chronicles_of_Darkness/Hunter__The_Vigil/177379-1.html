                            May I make a suggestion Flyboy.

                            I was rereading this old thread and I noticed that you've merged Crews and Status - I'm not sure that's appropraite for this group. What you have at the moment for Status looks great but I'd suggest adding the traditional three subgroups as well. As a rough idea:

                            Specialists - Tier 1 Hunters, members of the Union, and other associated oddballs or monsters who've been doing this for longer than the Outfit. The Family takes them in, gives them support, access to the Family's black market supply lines: military grade weapons, grave dirt from Transylvania, fall guys for the cops, special dispensation to take a group with you once you find the vampire who killed your family. In return they train the Outfit's soldiers and kill whatever monster the outfit tells them to kill.

                            Local Boys - The outfit's social experts. If a mage wants something stolen from a Museum in Italy a Local Boy cuts the deal. If it's time to invite all the cops in your pocket to a party local boys arrange everything. Public relations with the people living in your territory, that's the Local Boys job.

                            Enforcers - The normal fighters in contrast to the Specialists. The sort of muscle men you'd expect to see in real life Mafia outfits, with some training from Socialists to top things of.
