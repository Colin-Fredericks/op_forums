    Conspiracy: Ronin Enterprizes

    09-22-2015, 03:19 AM
    Ronin Enterprizes

    When the Endless came from on high, they learned that this world was far more armed than initially thought. Though the world itself has largely remained unprepared or has rarely considered the possibility of an alien visitation, it was a bit of a surprise for the Regents involved to find that their first landing was met with a hostile response…
    Soldiers armed with techno-organic armor battled their nascent vanguard and slew many before being contained and destroyed under a tidal wave of Drones. Yet the incident has left the Reagents wondering how the attack could have been carried out to begin with.
    Their opposition knew of their landing site and possessed certain technologies far in excess of what this world should have been capable of. They would have pried the information the knowledge from their attackers’s corpses, but searching through memories and genetics was rendered useless when their bodies were turned into useless, formless liquid.
    Since then, numerous attacks have been launched against Monarchs who were newly out of their cocoons, many ending up slaughtered. In retaliation, Monarchs have been deployed to search and destroy these assailants whenever they could be found. Yet the Reagents have made no ground in searching and defeating these upstarts and removing them from the roots.
    Some, more cynical Monarchs believe the Reagents allow them to exist to ensure that only the strongest of their kind survive; others say that the Reagents created them themselves and that the very existence of these “Hunters” was another factor in their grand experiment.
    Still, one question has always remains; who gave them these technologies?
    It is even a wonder if their adversaries even know the answer to that.



    Ronin Enterprises is a Japan based Private Military Contractor that has its fingers in nearly every first world country. Its activities for the most part are fairly standard for PMCs: train and deploy soldiers and equipment to fight in whatever war that needed them and for the most part, it’s soldiers are all fairly normal in their equipment in their mannerisms.
    However, the company offers “special” services to those more keen to their more… secretive activities. For amongst the rank and file are a small and very infrequently deployed cadre of highly trained and supremely equipped “commandos”.
    Each man is clad in head to do in a lattice of seemingly flesh like armor with steel plating covering strategic locations called a nano-suit. A single suit is able to endure most small arms fire without so much as a bruise to the wearer as well as bestow near superhuman fighting ability to even an average solder. The secrets of its origins and its fabrication are unknown, save that only Ronin Enterprizes makes some of most powerful armor in the world - and it is only they who are able to use it via a combination of currently uncrackable gene-locking and post mortem self-destruction.
    Though Ronin itself disavows their existence, those privy to them know that it is these warriors are the reason why the company name references Samurai in the first place.. For the Ronin are Samurai without masters.



    The Enemy
    Ronin Enterprises does not in of itself have any particular foes. For the most part, the organization simply accepts missions given to it by the highest bidder, typically the Samurai operatives are only deployed in the most high risk zones, which often includes situations beyond the norm.
    More recently however, the Corporation has opted to place jobs involving alien life at a higher priority than others metanormal foes, in particular any missions that might potentially involve the Monarchs and the Reagents. The reason for this is unknown.
    Most commonly, Samurai have been deployed to delay or investigate potential Colonies and if need be, burn them to the ground and slay their Queens. That kind of job involves quite of a bit of hazard pay.

    Hunters
    You’ve been training for this job since you were a kid. Your parents died and Ronin needed bodies, so you accepted even going so far as to take any experimental treatments whip up. Now, you’ve made your way to being a full fledged Samurai and were gifted with a Nanosuit of your own. Well, hopefully your luck doesn’t run out like it did to everyone else in your Squad.
    Samurai were cavalry, but not in the same way European knights were. No, they were archers and you know it. Unlike the others, you don’t like rushing in guns blazing and chopping off xenos in Blade Mode. No, you know perfectly well the being a Samurai meant being a patient archer. Although, you did kind of have to compromise with your CO and not take a bow into this next fight...
    You should be dead, but you aren’t. One moment you and your squad were trying to escape a sinking submarine, then next each of you were gunned down. You were very sure you died then. Then next thing you knew after that, it was suddenly morning, you somehow were shoved into a this weird get up and you were perfectly fine. Although, now you wonder if you can actually take the suit off.



    Status:
    0 - You just made it into the Samurai Corps. You’re now a full member in the secret club that hides just beneath the surface of the organization. You are issued a Nano-suit free of charge and may spend merit dots purchasing features for it.
    000 - You’ve seen and slain monsters, even probably in one on one and came out on top. You’re not the best there is, but you’re getting there. You’ve worn your Nano-Suit on enough missions, that you know it can help you push your body past its normal potential. You may buy up to 7 dots on physical attributes and skills, but are limited to a human maximum of 5 outside of a Nano-Suit.
    0000 - At this point, you’ve probably slain armies of things if you tallied your body count together. At this point, you’d be a Samurai of Legend… if anyone actually published the things you did. You gain Inspiring and may raise your Attributes and Skills to 8 instead of 7.

    Stereotypes:
    Null Mysteriis - Keep a close eye on them. Few other groups are willing to accept extraterrestrial threats exist, let alone provide valuable research data on the nature of the invaders.
    Ashwood Abbey - Hobbyists the lot of them. They go out finding monsters and often nearly end up causing themselves problems. I can accept their money, I just cannot trust their lack of honor… and decency.
    Taskforce: Valkyrie - You see them often, mostly because the Americans have been involved in our country since the last World War. Can’t say I dislike ‘em though; their guns certainly make up for their lack of interesting armor.
    The Cheiron Group - Our primary competitors. They might got lots of grunts working everything, but I bet each and every one of them is regretting having … whatever it is they got grafted to their bodies. I’m also betting my health plan is better than all of theirs put together.


    ===
    Endowment:
    Nano-Suit ( - or 00000; Special)
    The Nanosuit is Ronin Enterprise’s sole endowment, yet despite this it is rather flexible and adaptive.
    It is largely composed out of Carbon Nano Tubes (hence “nanosuit”) organised into a flexible mesh that resembles muscle tissue. Unlike ordinary muscle, these nanotubes absorb most small arms fire and blunt impact and energize the wearer’s musculature.
    It also directly neurally uplinks into the wearer’s brain in order to read movements and coordinate the body. However, not being the one intended the nanosuit disables most of its features.
    In game terms: the nanosuit provides a 3/5 Armor rating and has no minimum strength requirements or penalizes the movements of the wearer, provided he is authorized.
    Additionally, the suit provides oxygen in oxygen deprived environments, such as outer space, possess a built in radio, and prevents the wearer from bleeding out.
    There is also no need to brace for any man portable weapon with high recoil (such as certain High Velocity rifles)
    If the wearer is not authorized, the musculature of the suit is improperly powered and unresponsive. The armor rating instead is 1/2 and the suit penalizes Speed, Initiative, and Defense by 2.
    Special: Every Ronin operative with atleast one dot of Status in the organization receives a Nano Suit for free. However, replacement suits require that the operative buy one at 5 dots.

    Additionally, the Nano Suit may be upgraded to access special modes.
    By reflexively spending a willpower point, the Samurai wearing a Nano-suit may engage a variety of different modes. These modes however do not come with the default Nano-suit and must be purchased separately. Only one mode be activated at a time.
    Power Mode (0000)
    The Nano-Suit’s CNT muscles are energized to increase power output dramatically far above their norm.
    By activating this mode, the Suit’s wearer may increase her Strength by +3 for 3 turns or multiply her jump distance by 5 once. The wearer is fully able to benefit from the increased Strength for two turns and is free to finish it off with the increased jump.
    Blade Mode (00000)
    Considered one of the more “noble” things about the Nano-suit is that it fully allows the wearer to achieve skill on par with many famous Samurai via an uplink to the internal computer systems in the armor.
    By activating this mode, the Ronin treats his Weaponry skill as being 4 if it was less than 4, or receives a +1 bonus to his weapon skill if it was already at or above 5, for the remainder of the Scene. He also applies his full Defense against firearms but is required to substitute Weaponry for Athletics for calculating Defense while the mode is active.
    Note that this mode does not provide the Ronin with a melee weapon and that a melee weapon is needed for this mode to function at all.
    Speed Mode (0000)
    There will often be times where additional speed on foot is necessary when combating foes. The Nano-Suits leg muscles can directly be charged with energy to increase mobility either to outrun an opponent or escape danger.

    Armor Mode (0000)
    The Nano Suit might be tough, but it is still not that far out from the realm of modern armor. Enough gunfire will eventually punch through it. By electrifying the CNT muscle fibers to harden them against attack, the nano-suit becomes far more durable.
    By activating this mode, the Nano-Suit’s Armor ratings increase to to 6/8 for one Scene. The wearer also takes no bashing damage from lethal attacks if the attack’s damage was completely negated.





    ===
    Design notes:
    So, as part of the bid for creating a gameline involving alien bug swarms as the player splat, it was only natural to throw some version of powered armored combatants to act as their Hunters. I choose an eastern flavor, though I am a little bit of a novice when it comes to the East and would need to revise this a little.
    The Monarchs in part of the first half of the intro blurb refers to another, still developing project; said alien bug swarms, technically the leaders of said swarms. But I figure that a Hunter Conspiracy about powered armor alien hunting mercenary samurai was deserving enough to exist regardless of the status of the rest of the project.

    Influences were taken from several video games, particularly Halo, Warframe, and Crysis (the last one in particular for those at least a little versed in the lore)

    The Endowment is designed to make them feel really tough and capable even compared to other Hunters given the suite of abilities and durability Ronin have without even requiring to pay in.
    It is kinda necessary to make them this… powerful as the Ronin are setup to fight a player character splat that is easily capable of actually fielding small personal armies. Ergo, the Ronin need to have at least on paper the likeness of a one man army each.
