    Originally posted by Michael View Post
    That's the basic foundational ideology of the Diamond orders. It's their myth of a golden age when everything was better, however they choose to define 'better'.
    Well now we've gone from talking about what Atlantis/Irem were like, to talking about what their heirs think of them.

    While it's true that there's a lot more explicit evils in Irem; Mummies also have bad memories and lots of cultish stuff going on, and a massive difference in values from modern people - so they quite easily could think of Irem as a paradise.

    Meanwhile the Free Council don't think too highly of Atlantis, and it most probably wasn't actually a paradise.

    In short, both nations are complex and with a bit of justification you could use either as the counterpart for a Paradise Lost theme. Pangaia on the other hand, doesn't really work for the AKD.

    Originally posted by Michael View Post
    No one intended to destroy Atlantis as well. Also, it wasn't just Atlantis that was destroyed, it was the world as it was before the Ladder. Irem doesn't have that. The Judges were there before, and they are still here.'
    While Irem not destroying an entire reality is a fair point - I don't think it's that important thematically. As for Atlantis, given that it was a crowning symobl of humanity's potential, I think the Exarchs did want it gone.


    Originally posted by Shock View Post
    Hunter in my opinion spends too much time focused on C&C. While this good in drama and introducing other themes but still lacks on giving focus on why people hunt. Sure you have duty, and holy blessed homicide, and all that jazz but the reason for the Vigil is personal and I feel the mechanics as written don't reflect that.
    How would you make mechanics to support that?

    Originally posted by atamajakki View Post
    I'd also love to divorce Tier from Compact/Conspiracy. A VALKYRIE cell should just as easily be a single fireteam that hears from their higher-ups every once in a blue moon for a Night at the Opera (Tier 1) as they are a jet-setting group of international military troubleshooters (Tier 3). Tier is essential to Hunter play and I'd like as many of the groups as possible to function at all levels.
    I don't really have any objection to support for Tier 1 TF:V, heck it's already there. I just wish they'd also add support for Tier 3 TF:V.

    That said, I would hope that the Tier 1 support would give better reasons for you being Tier 1 than "Conspiracy brass screw over the little guys because WoD".

    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    I’d also like to see the “Antagonists” section of the Chronicle Book include a sample write-up for at least one monster who doesn’t fit any of the standard templates
    You mean Slashers? I kid

    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    One of the things I loved about Night Stalkers and Spirit Slayers in particular,and also in Witch Finders to a lesser extent, is that their monster sections didn’t try to mimic the basis gameline’s protagonists too closely: e.g., Night Stalkers let you make a variety of different kinds of vampires, most of whom were substantially different from the Kindred.
    I kind of wish we did a little more on that in Dream Catchers...

                                                        Originally posted by Krobeles View Post
                                                        I would like to see a mechanic for building your own Compact/Conspiracy. As in, players rack up experience, man-power and finance throughout the game, and the lonely Hunter cell slowly developes into something much bigger and grander even as its members gains increasingly more contacts and allies through their work and Professions.
                                                        This would be very, very awesome.

                                                        In addition, I think I'd like Tactics to be reworked as they're the other major advantage Hunters use alongside Endowments, but they never seem to get any love? Or at the least I tend not to see them being discussed as readily as Endowments, which is kinda understandable given how working as a team isn't quite as flashy as praying on some armor. Tilts and Conditions can help codify what Tactics can do and how well they do them.

                                                        And this is a thought I had just now that may or may not be practical, but maybe Hunters could also use the Reversal Points idea from Imperial Mysteries? Archmages have them because they're individually powerful entities with tons of "friends", connections, and boatloads of attribute dots, so the idea that they can set up some crazy gambits is to be expected. While Tier 2 and 3 organizations don't have the sheer supernatural weight of a single Archmage, they are still well-connected orginizations with tons of "friends", connections, and collectively speaking a boatload of attribute dots. These "revisions" would take some sort of setup though, since, well, Conspiracies have red tape that the PCs need to cut or be burned with. Maybe in order to use a Reversal Point, a condition must be met for some period of time before the points can be "called in" to be used, representing the set up and the eventual payoff. Break the condition though, and the point is lost.

                                                        I'll admit, this might not be the best idea for Hunter for reasons that other posters will come up with, so I guess in the end what really, really want in a theoretical 2nd Edition Hunter is some really new and awesome stuff that makes me go "holy fuck, Hunters own!"
