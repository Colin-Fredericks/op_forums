                                    Look what I found sitting around my documents folder

                                    These were an unusual one, in that I wrote them as antagonists for Vampires rather than protagonists.


                                    Sunrise Securities

                                    The Doorkeepers

                                    Slavery was one of the great evils of the 17th century. An entire people were ripped from their homes, their cultures, and taken into lives of hard labour and cruelty. Hundreds of years were not enough to erase the scars, but within every act of destruction lies the potential for new creation. Generations born upon foreign shores without the culture of their ancestors filled the void with acts of creation. Jazz, soul, hip hop; art and music flowed from the African Diaspora and shaped the world.

                                    But the monsters who lived and unlived in slavery’s wake never left when the laws changed. If Vampires cannot buy people to profit and drink from then the club scene, with it’s late nights and inebriated patrons, makes a modern replacement. But it is no longer 1860, tonight African America stands strong in their castles, they fill the halls with music and they bar the door against the monsters outside.

                                    Sunrise Securities was formed by accident at the Corn and Rooster, a harlem jazz club owned and operated by Reginald “doctor” Cobra: entrepreneur, bòkônon, and a fairly decant hand on the keyboard. At it’s height the Corn and Rooster was the place to be for Harlem locals, and white folk slumming it for the good music. By day the club rehearsed, by night the club danced, and upon holy days the club partied with the Loa. For a time all was good.

                                    Dancers and music lovers weren’t the only people drawn to the Corn and Rooster. With time and experience Reginald began to grow suspicious, he saw regulars leaving with different people every visit, people who always woke up weak the next day. He turned to spells and divinations, and when he knew what manner of leach was succking at the lifeblood of his establishment, he acted. Reginald gathered his employees and performed a grand ritual and petitioned Papa Legba, the Loa who guards all gates, to make his club a sacred space. Safe from the predators which stalked the night outside.

                                    With the Loa’s blessing business boomed, nobody said a word but everyone knew that the Corn and Rooster was the safest club in town. Reginald’s rivals began to grumble, and in their grumblings Reginald saw a business opportunity. Sunrise Securities was formed.

                                    The Enemy

                                    Let other Hunters venture into dark places and fight the monsters within. Sunrise Securities has their own mission: This place is sacred, this music is sacred, and none shall disturb it. Whatever happens outside these four walls is somebody else’s business, at least for as long as they’re on the clock.

                                    Vampires are Sunrise Securities’ most common foe. The image of the glamourous, sexy, Vampire stalking the dance floor is well known for a reason, Vampires really do like to prey upon nightclubs and as far as Sunrise Securities are concerned if they see a Vampire they show him the door. Vampires’ are also the most vulnerable to the Compact’s Endowment (after all, Reginald petitioned Papa Legba for a tool against Vampires, and he got what he asked for).

                                    After Vampires come Changelings, the second most likely supernatural being to turn up at a nightclub. A Changeling’s emotional harvesting is far less harmful than a vampire’s drinking so Sunrise Securities are more tolerant. Their bouncers can recite the standard pledge of good behaviour from memory in their sleep. Besides, Changelings can make some damn fine music. Some Freeholds even hire Sunrise Securities to provide bouncers for their own clubs, and the firm sees nothing wrong with taking the contract. Mostly though, Changelings stay beneath Sunrise’s radar.

                                    There are other monsters who stalk the night looking for human pray. Beasts talk a good game about having a higher purpose, but at the end of the day if they get hungry an intoxicated clubber is easy prey. Sunrise Securities on the whole are in favour of otherworldly beings teaching humans lessons that are needed if not always kind. They call those beings the Loa, and they don’t like the idea of amateurs bungling the job, especially if it hurts or traumatises one of their protectees. However Beasts are powerful, and few Doorkeepers will willingly take one on alone.

                                    Picture: A middle aged and very muscular african american in a bad suit, the kind of man you might describe as a brick. He's wearing many gold chains, one of which has Papa Legba's Veve.

                                    You! Beat it!

                                    Stereotypes

                                    The Union: It’s disappointing how often “protecting our neighbours” stops where the ghetto begins. But they know how to fight so we’ll ask for their help. Duty before pride brother.

                                    Ascending Ones: Monsters around here have an odd habit of disappearing. I don’t know who’s doing it, but there’s a drink waiting for him behind the bar.

                                    Night Watch: You don’t push, you don’t bring your piece past this door, and we don’t have a problem.

                                    The Staff of Janus: I ain’t going to faerieland, not for money or magic. I’ve got my door and my music right here.

                                    Status

                                    Status in Sunrise Securities is a formal affair handled by human resources. In a nutshell the longer you’ve worked for Sunrise, and the longer you’ve gone without screwing up, the higher your status.

                                    O: You’ve gone through Sunrise Securities corporate training program. Take a dot of Trained Observer and a dot of Doorkeeping.
                                    OOO: You’re an old hand, and have seen a bit in your time. Take two dots in the Street Fighting Merit as a momento of your experiences.
                                    OOOOO: You’re as much of a fixture of the club scene as the clubs themselves. Everyone around knows your name, take the Barfly Merit and a dot of Contacts in the club scene.

                                    Endowment: Doorkeeping (O - OOOOO)

                                    Bouncers employed by Sunrise Security are trained in the sacred, and humbly named, art of Doorkeeping. A sacred contract in which the bouncer assumes responsibility for the safety of all within their building. Sunrise's employees may be professional or they may take bribes. Some are polite and helpful, others like to throw their weight around. But nothing: Not money, not pride, not the club management, will stop a Doorkeeper from doing whatever it takes to ensure everyone visiting their establishment is protected until they’re safely on their way home.

                                    The more dedicated they are to this duty the greater the potency of this Endowment, but even a single dot requires a firm commitment.

                                    Doorkeeping grants the following benefits while on duty:
                                    • Doorkeeper may Risk Willpower an additional Doorkeeping times a scene, providing they use their extra risks on rolls benefiting from Doorkeeping.
                                    • Add Doorkeeping to all rolls to notice trouble. This could be a Perception roll, a Wits + Empathy roll to notice someone’s looking for trouble, a Wits + Occult roll to recognise a vampire, or even a Wits + Medicine roll to notice someone’s reacting badly to drugs.
                                    • Against Obscurificate and other similar vampiric powers a Doorkeeper automatically rolls Wits + Doorkeeping as a Clash of Wills.
                                    • Each Dot of Doorkeeping provides an automatic Successes on Contested rolls to resist being persuaded, intimidated, or mind controlled into overlooking trouble in a Doorkeeper’s premise. If the roll is Resisted the other character subtracts Doorkeeping Successes from their roll instead. Against Dominate, Majesty, or other similar vampiric abilities the effect grows to two Successes.
                                    • If a Doorkeeper tells a vampire they can't enter or to get out of their premise they may roll Presence + Intimidation + Doorkeeping vs Composure + Potency. If the Doorkeeper wins the vampire becomes psychologically unable to enter, and if they’re inside they must leave immediately. The effect lasts until sunrise, or until the Doorkeeper's death.

                                                    -----

                                                    Should probably do an update about our numbers. Insofar:

                                                    12 Compacts out of our current 29 total Compacts are considered complete.

                                                    17 Conspiracies out of our staggering 59 total Conspiracies are considered complete.

                                                    6 Cults, Cabals, etc. out of our 17 total Cults, Cabals, etc. are considered complete.

                                                    That makes 35 complete Organizations out of 105 total Organizations being fully complete.

                                                    The average write-up consists of 1 splash page and 2 written pages, with 1-2 extra pages given to account for formatting. This means that, bare minimum, Chapters 1-3 will account for 175 pages; 60 pages for Compacts, 85 pages for Conspiracies, and 30 pages for Cults, Cabals, etc. This does not include outliers such as Schutzgruppe, which increases their specific page number by 3+ pages. This also does not include Endowments, which I am unsure if we want to include in the organizations themselves or move them into the Endowment section of Chapter 4. This would be roughly...let's say 2-4 pages each, probably 50 pages total with variation. That brings up the estimated minimum page total to 225 pages plus change.

                                                    For comparison, Dream Catchers is about 247 pages while Skin and Bones is 116 pages not including the chopped Compacts and Conspiracies. The official Hunter: the Vigil book is a little less than 400 pages. The splat books for Vampires, Werewolves, and Mages are all shy of 250.

                                                    By these numbers, we are going to create a rather substantial fanbook. There are a few organizations that are only "incomplete" because they lack a Stereotype section; it would be marvelous if we can get this organizations up and running. After that, it would be good for us to actually cut some of the organizations that are "weaker" out before working on completing any other Organization. Personally, I feel that my own organization, the Broker's Guild, is rather weakly-written and would like to either drop them or rewrite them entirely.
