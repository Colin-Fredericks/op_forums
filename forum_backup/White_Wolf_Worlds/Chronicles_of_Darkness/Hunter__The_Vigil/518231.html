    As you may see, most hunter groups come from either Europe of the America, which is kinda expected. Something interesting which I've found was that most of the canon compacts come from North America, and most of the conspiracies from Europe. While the Americas as whole have many groups, all but one (the Regency of Thorns) come from North America, and all are based around European settlers, mostly in the US. While Africa and Asia have a few hunter groups, they are very centralized- all of the African groups are from Egypt while most of the Asian groups (with the exception of the Aki Ten and the eastern branch of Hotel Mascaron) come from the Middle East. Oceania, however, has no hunters in it- at least, from the groups I've gathered. I think that it shows were new homebrews and canon groups are needed- European and US groups are all nice and well, but it makes the rest of the world kinda empty.

    please note me for any mistakes, either in the locations or the writers of the organizations.
