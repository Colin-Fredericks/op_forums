    Conspiracy: Oakley Institute - Monster Tamers

    08-18-2015, 04:25 PM
    [The picture is of a doctor kneeling down towards a boy with a red baseball cap. Behind him are small creatures, mostly animal like, but featuring traits that seem outright strange. He holds a small device, small enough to fit in the child’s palm.]

    Gotta Catch 'Em All!

    Monsters have always lurked among mankind, hiding in the forests, under bridges, within the darkness beneath our beds. Many of these creatures possess are powers and abilities above what humans are capable of, from causing earthquakes to breathing fire and shooting out lightning. Monsters are often savage and territorial, like animals were far more power than they should have.

    ...But they’re still just animals. Through out history, humans have tried to control many of these creatures, to bring them under heel. The results are mixed, most monsters are simply too fierce, too powerful. Others proved to be too human and ironically incapable of accepting “domestication”. Still, people have tried, often hiding their attempts from the world at large, largely in secret.

    However, things changed within the past thirty years. A brilliant, yet secretive cabal of environmentalists and researchers managed to figure out a solution, a way to tame the beasts that threatened them.

    Named for the head researcher of the project. The Oakley Device was small enough that a child could fit in his palms and possessed many unique and powerful abilities. Its light alone could pacify the simple and animalistic creatures of the world, but taken further, this beam could draw a creature into itself, effectively trapping it in the device. When released, the beast is no longer as feral as it once was and obeyed the whims of its new master. And that was just the start. The machine responded to the wearer’s emotions and desires, allowing the creatures taken into it to act as conduits for their master’s will.

    Suddenly, the beasts in the fields that could terrorize villages by themselves seemed no longer as frightening, for humankind had its own beasts to combat it. Creatures of flame found themselves fighting creatures of water, living shadows faced the light. More monsters could be captured and brought for the human cause. It seemed as though humankind was now in greater position to sieze even more control over the world, mass production of the so called Oakley Devices.

    Yet that didn’t happen. The day before the grand unveiling, heavenly and hellish beings descended and destroyed the research lab and slew many of those responsible for the device. Thousands of hand held miracle machines were disposed of in one fell swoop.
    Now, none know how the devices came to be, the technology that made them was lost. All that remains now are a small handful of what was meant to be start of a whole new world. Rebranding themselves as the Oakley Institute, in honor of the long gone inventor, the semisecret society stirs.

    Today, they run a recruitment drive, bringing in young folks and showing them the supernatural world, but in a different light towards it than other conspiracies. To them, it doesn’t matter if a lizard breathes fire; it might be a young dragon, but that means it must still be cared for like any pet, albeit with more care. And sometimes, the kids take them home, hiding them within the small pocket universe that anyone can hold… and the adults in particular often carry small armies wherever they go.

    The operation is small, and mostly secretive for now; they don’t want a repeat of last time, but who knows what’ll happen in the future?

    Storytelling:
    You’ve been doing this since you were in grade school. You and your friends were once trapped in another dimension when all you really wanted to do at stay at Summer Camp. In retrospect, it was a blast, you met your partner then. Although no one will ever forget the fact that you used to wear goggles everywhere you went.

    You and your sisters were all accepted at the institute, but you know you’re the only one with any talent, no matter how much they said they had the looks. That was fine by you, you could kick their asses. Besides, you needed them to help gather money for that aquarium… or gymnasium. You’re still not sure which yet.

    After your brother died, you went through his things. You found what you thought to be a toy of his, you didn’t understand what you had possessed real power, not until you abused it and caused far more suffering than you know what to do with.

    Status:
    0 - You’re not all that experienced controlling the creatures yet, but you’re a Tamer now. You still fear them, but at least now you got the power. You may be just a kid or a young man, all told, but you signed up and that’s what counts. You possess an Oakley Device and a Partner and may spend merits to modify them.
    000 - You’re not as young as you used to be, that much is clear. You’ve traveled far and met many of the strange and wondrous creatures that inhabit our world, maybe spent years on it. You know them well. You may choose to reroll any failure or dramatic failure that results from any social rolls dealing with nonsapient creatures.
    00000 - When the ten year olds dream about being the best Tamer, they were talking about being like you. Sure, you don’t travel as much as you used to when you were young, but your adventures are the stuff of legend. At this point, you control creatures that in some other time might have been thought of as minor gods. You gain the Inspiring merit for free.

    Endowment: Oakley Device
    While most of the Oakley Devices have been destroyed during the purge, many still remain to be used.

    The device may capture and hold supernatural creatures inside of it, sealing them inside its internal pocket dimension.
    Capturing a monster requires (think of rules later)

    Once captured, any of number creatures may be let out of the pocket dimension in a nonhostile state. Such creatures possess their usual natural behaviors with the sole except that they are not actively aggressive towards humans and will flee when attacked.

    However, creatures let out of the Oakley Device may be used in combat. Doing so is taxing and requires that the user must spend a willpower point to conjure his creature in a combat ready form. Only one such creature may be out when released in this manner. Summoning another creature causes the other to be recalled into the device.

    Controlling a combat creature is mentally taxing and as a result the creature has no instant actions of its own. In order for it to possess instant actions, you must forgo your own, in effect, spending your own action to give orders.

    With the exception of Partner creatures (see below), any creature slain when released from the Oakley device are slain and are effectively dead.

    Partner Creature
    Some creatures possess a stronger link to the Oakley device than others. Said creatures are often somewhat exceptional examples of their kind, some even possessing a rudimentary understanding of language before capture and often submissive. Said creatures do not die easily and when they fall in combat are simply forced to return to their owner’s device.

    By default, all newly christened and fully fledged Tamers receive a Partner creature as such creatures are often breeded in large enough numbers to be seen as successful examples of domestication, for a small and scattered group that is.
    (To define, stats)


    Shared Will: (00)
    As the Oakley Devices act as a bridge between man and magical creatures, it stands to reason then that the human will can sometimes crossover it. The Tamer may spend Willpower to pay willpower costs for his creature.

    Capacity: (--- or 0 to 00000):
    Oakley Devices might contain small pocket dimensions, but there is a hard limit to the number of different creatures that they could store. By default, Oakley Devices may store a single creature, typically a Tamer’s first partner. Though, the Tamer may force and exert his will over the device, forcing it to increase how much it can contain. Each dot in this merit raises the maximum creature capacity of the Tamer’s Oakley device by 1 up to a maximum of 6 at 5 dots.

                                  The creatures under the Oakley Institute’s control are a wide bunch, ranging from living shadows and rocks, to slightly more mundane things like flaming roosters and seals that breathe frost. This is made more complicated when some monsters somehow… evolve into stranger, more advanced forms. Some creatures, despite their animalistic behavior are able to possess inhuman cunning and problem solving when it comes to certain fields.
                                  As a general rule and as a system for classification, Oakley agents figured out that it was best to separate monsters into different levels, defining the rough capability and power of a specific creature. Some creatures, particularly those well trained achieve statistics above the norm.


                                  In game terms: creatures have their statistics determined by Level, in a similar manner to Rank for spirits. Level determines attribute caps, with a seperate limit for mental and social attributes. As level goes up, the so do the number of dots. Level also is substituted for Power Stat when a Power Stat like trait is required to be resisted upon.
                                  Creatures, before factoring Level (Level 0), possess 1 dot in each attribute, no skills, a default size of 2, and no dread powers. The chart below lists how powerful a monster grows at certain levels.
                                  Level Physical Cap Mental/Social Cap Attribute Dots Skill Dots Boost Dread Power dots Willpower cost
                                  1 3 1 5 6 0 1 1
                                  2 4 1 7 9 1 2 1
                                  3 4 2 9 12 1 4 1
                                  4 5 2 11 15 2 6 1
                                  5 5 3 14 18 2 8 1
                                  6 6 3 17 22 3 11 2
                                  7 7 4 20 26 4 14 2
                                  8 8 4 25 30 5 17 3
                                  9 9 5 30 35 7 20 4
                                  10 10 6 35 40 10 25 5
                                  Each point of Boost may be spent on increasing monster size by 1 or increasing the monster’s storage of willpower by 1.


                                  Because of how variable monsters are, it is best advised Storytellers use their better judgement when allowing Tamers access to multiple monsters.
                                  Additionally, high Level monsters require proportionately more energy to coerce and conjure using the Oakley device as defined by "Willpower cost", representing the psychic effort the user must expend to summon and control the beast in a combat setting. In a noncombat, nonhazardous environment, the Willpower cost does not need to be paid as the creature is naturally willing to want to level the Oakley Device when able; creatures simply don't want to fight most of the time.
