            Originally posted by Flyboy254 View Post
            So, here's a question. What's the big differences between the Aki Ten and Les Mysteries?
            A lot, actually?

            The Les Mysteres serve the spirits- they serve as a bridge between humanity and the spirit world. They dance with demons and angels, gods and archetypes. They serve as a channel, allowing themselves to be possessed by the Shadow, while serving their patrons on the side of Flesh. They maintain that harmony between man and spirit, and hunt for the Forsaken, for they defile that complicated relationship by severing Flesh from Spirit.

            The Aki Ten, however, are not shamans, nor are they mediums. They do not serve the spirit world, and they do not seek balance with its denizens. They want to rule it, force it to their will and use their power upon the spirit world to serve humanity. Hisil is a wild realm, and it should be tamed, maintained and bound so it would serve mankind. They want to conquer the Shadow specifically and the supernatural world in general. Their Endowments reflect it- they aren't possessed by the spirit, but they summon bound shikigami to fight and die in their battles. Its not companionship- its servitude. When a Les Mysteres hunter hear about a great spirit which has awakened and pose a threat upon the world, they seek to negotiate with it and return the world to harmony. When an Aki Ten hears about it, the first thing they'll think is "how can I bind that ancient god to my will?"

                                        well, while I did preformed a research when I wrote them (including reading an essay about the history and beliefs on the Onmyodo and doing a class about Japanese culture, history and aesthetics in my university), I have used some artistic freedom for their modern incarnation (I have said a number of times in their writeup that even in the setting they aren't the "true Onmyodo" as much as they are a modern creation formed by a number of powerful families which mixed up with people who were inspired by them- hence their tension with Japan's mages). I don't think I have portrayed them too different than any way modern Japanese pop culture does, and besides- that belief system is, technically, dead. Any connection between the original tradition and the people who call themselves onmyoji in modern time is very thin, more or less the connection between those hunters and the original tradition. I also don't remember reading anywhere that the Onmyodo has much emphasis about communing with the spirits like mediums- they were more about fortune telling and exorcism, with the responsibility of talking with the spirits directly being something much less "noble" and in the responsibility of the miko (as the Azusa Miko are in the setting).

                                        Again, there isn't a lot of information about the belief system itself- at least, I haven't found such. I have done the best I can with what I did, but I don't think they are too far away from the actual Onmyodo- sure, there are differences, but the reason for those is found in their writeup. In real life, the Onmyodo practically died during the Meiji Restoration- but what if, in the CofD, the shattered pieces of that organization has somehow preserved themselves until not only that they survived in the modern world, but actually survived by adapting themselves to it, at any cost it may take? That's what I wished to discover with the New Way.

                                                        Edit: Politician profession up!

                                                        Politician

                                                        “We have a saying in Gaffney. ‘Shake with your right hand, hold a rock in your left.’”

                                                        To be a professional in your field is one thing. Being a career politician is something different entirely. Finances, law, education, land management, all have to be rolled into one singular package in order to get anything done. Deals must be made, wheels need greasing, and sometimes you just need to force someone to see it your way or no way at all.

                                                        Then you add in the other problems of the world. Vampires trying to control the money in a city, witches doing their best to use townships as their own personal magic testing grounds, even groups of so-called “hunters” using the people in a region as patsies and fall-guys. That isn’t how government is supposed to work, or if it is it’s supposed to only happen for the people who are in charge.

                                                        Politicians are of course learned in political science, and many make it a point to realize some education in areas they care deeply about, such as educational or environmental issues. This makes any politician skilled in academic research methods, provided it’s on a subject they support. It also helps that they often have a host of social connections capable of giving them the necessary support to bring their plans to fruition. This makes them adept social movers and capable academics, if only in a limited fashion.

                                                        For the Vigil, a politician is everyone’s favorite ally. Even on a local level, they have enough pull with local authorities to make it so investigations can quietly die down or focus an investigator on another matter. Politicians in the know can be even more dangerous against a monster than any well-armed hunter, since they at least know what to look out for when a monster tries to take control over them in their own office.

                                                        Politicians typically find themselves working closely with the Barret Commission against the actions of vampires in their cities and counties, though they also can find themselves working alongside the Union if they find themselves in need of votes. Most conspiracies actually prefer to keep politicians at arms-length due to their relatively higher profile compared to more “working-class” hunters,

                                                        Rank 1: Alderman/Local supervior
                                                        Rank 2: City Council/County Commision
                                                        Rank 3: Mayor/State Legislator
                                                        Rank 4: State Senator/Congressman
                                                        Rank 5: Senator

                                                        Skills: Politics and Persuasion

                                                        Concepts: Kindly mayor; Firebrand independent; Backroom manipulator; Legislative magician; Conservative crusader; Jovial representative; Lobbyist’s best friend; Hand-shaking baby-kisser; Policy wonk; Vengeful power-seeker

                                                            A second draft on the Aki Ten MI-18 hunters

                                                            Aki Ten


                                                            You thought studying computer science would be an exciting path to success. A master of cutting edge Virtual Reality technology you'd have built a name for yourself. Or you would have had your VR startup not been bought out by the Aki Ten as investors abandoned you in the early 90s. Now you spend your days monitoring the projector device network for security vulnerabilities and watch other men use your technology to control Gods. That should have been you.

                                                            The world moves too fast these days. No one has any respect for tradition or making sure things are done properly. Not you though, you're careful. You make sure to do the full rite not just the streamlined version the computer asks for. It takes longer, a fact your cellmates have bemoaned after many a battle, but the Shikigami seem to appreciate your efforts. At least they seem to serve you more readily than your more modern coworkers.

                                                            You don't think you're of noble birth at least you can't prove that you are. Your family history has been lost. Yet you have a gift for summoning and binding Shikigami. Some of the more traditional older members of the conspiracy view you as a threat. A young upstart here to take their rightful place. You have no such ambitions and are quite happy to serve under them but they have been growing ever more spiteful with you.


                                                            MI-18

                                                            Hunters

                                                            Your grandfather was one of the greatest agents that British Intelligence had ever known. Your father while not as great was well regarded for his service. The Boy's Club mentality of the agency expects great things from you as well. Unfortunately you don't seem to have inherited their knack for espionage. You do have the raw physical talent and have trained with the best but you can't stand the sight of blood and intensely dislike violence. You managed to play it cool through training but you'll be receiving your first assignment soon. You're not sure you'll be able to keep up this facade.

                                                            Every Mad Scientist wants a beautiful assistant. That's where you come in. You approach suspected Mad Scientists and integrate yourself into their life. Most were the sort of boys who had no hopes of even talking to a girl like you. They never see it coming when you put them down. If only the boys upstairs could appreciate your work, you've been due for a promotion for years now. The gentlemen that they are they'd never say it to your face but you know they don't consider you fit to even have your current position.

                                                            As an English Gentleman you're expected to keep a stiff upper lip and always maintain a poised and sophisticated outlook on any situation. You do your best but the things you've seen; a man in Frankfurt meticulously stitching together murdered prostitutes to make his ideal lover, a cult in Dubai wiring people's brains into a massive analog computer, swarms of all consuming, self-replicating nanomachines nearly descending upon Cartif. You'd like to forget it all and just live out a normal life in ((Rich British Neighborhood Here)) but retirement isn't an option for an agent of the crown.
