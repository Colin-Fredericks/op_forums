        230) (Silver Cross, Skin & Bones) You've decided to help out at the "Silver Cross" clinic your uncle volunteers at. He was vehemently opposed to this but you're not really sure why that was.
        First day on the job, everything's going good, no drama.
        Some guy just walked in with a weird wound on his arm. The guy's friends follow him inside. Apparently the "wasp nest" in his arm is damaged & his usual doctor is unavailable. And then there's the person? whose tentacle is wounded.
        Your uncle's life is so much more interesting than you thought it was. How again did you get this job?

                    233) Dissecting a new monster for unusual thaumatech has resulted in a contagion of sorts.
                    Those that catch it develop a high fever. Once the fever passes a power will develop (draw from Mage, Changeling, Vampire, Werewolf, Hunter, Demon, Princess, Leviathan, Siren, blue books, etc. for powers). These powers are unaffected by whatever it is that keeps the mundane populace from noticing them.
                    Several of the newly empowered have gotten it into their heads to be superheroes/supervillains/etc. & an organization has grown to take advantage of these empowered.
                    Meanwhile, the Cheiron Group is scrambling to take back control of the situation, & many other supernatural factions are looking smug about Cheiron's predicament whilst adjusting their own power bases.

                            235) (TFV) A case has recently been relabeled to being a Black Book case. The case involves a creature that has been identified as the Narrow Man.
                            The Narrow Man has no facial features to speak of. It is very skinny & wears a fitted suit. It is extremely harmful towards anyone who notices it, alternating between mind control & homicide. This might make some people decide that the best thing to do would be to just leave it alone, but the creature is a local urban legend & many ghost hunters, teenagers, etc. have gone out looking for the bloody thing. Additionally, evidence suggests that the Narrow Man goes out of its way to get noticed, it might be that it literally cannot effect anyone until they notice it, at least directly.
                            In spite of all this the primary reasons why this case has moved to the Black Book is that body of the Narrow Man can be killed, but then the "spirit" immediately takes over the nearby body of someone who has noticed it, warping the newly stolen body into a perfect replica of its own. We've already lost three(?) teams to this thing.
                            PS: creature also appears to have spatial distortion capabilities.

                                236) You've recently crossed three sister witches with very different powers.
                                The eldest taps into the ephemeral currents of the world to mimic the powers of twilight beings.
                                The second born rely's on the power of blood or Vitae to fuel her magic.
                                The youngest steals her power more directly from the flesh & blood of the animals (& stranger things) she kills.
                                You've taken on far more powerful foes so this should be a cakewalk, right? Well, it turns out no, these three sisters are doing you some serious damage.

                                                        242) Every so often recently someone dies a grueling death. The corpses all wind being shredded like so much confetti. It's a mess.
                                                        Investigating the victims histories reveals that every one of them was guilty of something. Abuse, drug dealing, rape, one was a serial killer.
                                                        You are conflicted. Should you allow this because none of the victims were not good for the neighborhood? Or should you hunt this creature down, because they are still a killer & might get it wrong one time? Please ignore the fact that you yourself are a killer & that this may very well be you.
