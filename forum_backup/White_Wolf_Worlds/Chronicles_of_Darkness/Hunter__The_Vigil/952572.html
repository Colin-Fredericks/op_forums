          If I'd make a comparison between Task Force VALKYRIE and a counterpart group in the WoD, I'd honestly go for the Technocracy. It's obviously not a one-for-one comparison, clearly; there are many facets to the Technocracy not reflected in VALKYRIE. The dehumanization, the cynical Machiavellian spirit of Control, the supergovernmental conspirators above any responsibility to others or any lack of resources. But for the contingent of folks who liked the angle of rank-and-file Technocratic field agents who believed in the greater good but went to extremes and compromised themselves to maintain it, I see a similar spirit at work with hoo-rah VALKYRIE special ops teams, busting out the Advanced Armory and playing fast and loose with due process to keep the free world working. Jackboots on the ground.

Especially if it can convince their pinched agents that they have to "go undercover" for a while, and can spin the narrative that they've been "flipped" if for some reason they realized the truth and start trying to tell other agents.
