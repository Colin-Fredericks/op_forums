    Shard- No More Shadows

    05-13-2015, 02:34 PM
    so, I've thought about some old ideas and new ideas, and eventually I've started to think about a possible Shard. In this setting, the monsters are no longer the dark overlords who torment humanity in the night. Instead, they are lost, scattered and broken. Maybe the hunters have won. Maybe it was always this way. The reason doesn't matter- only that in this setting, the monsters are still a threat, yet only as a nameless and unorganized threat which terrors humanity.

    Without a strict structure, mankind knows that monsters walk among them- maybe it was a revelation which has started following an apocalyptic event. Maybe it was always this way. The reason doesn't matter- only that in this setting, the hunters are the big guys who both protect humanity and rule it. Hunters are seen as heroes, people who are willing to sacrifice their life against the darkness.

    Yet, not everyone can be hunters. Without trainings and experience, most people would die an horrible death. In order to make sure no "vigilante" would risk his life and the life of others, there is a pan-organization which works to make sure no one would do anything foolish. Maybe it was a reaction for many different hunter groups discovering that others exist. Maybe it was always this way. The reason doesn't matter- only that in this setting, once every few months the leaders of all great conspiracies and compacts meet together for dinner, share their information, make new pacts and find new rivals.

    This is where you enter the picture. Are you a member of one of the Great Watcher Organizations who work in the Parliament to protect your nation? Perhaps you are one of those vigilantes who simply try to do what they believe to be right, whether the "hunter police" like it or not. You could also be a member, maybe even a founder, of a small group who tries to either stay independent or make a name for itself in hope for being recognized. You could also be a person who were recruited by force, someone who tries to protect a monster from the hands of the hunters or even a former member of the great groups who discovered an horrible secret about whom they thought to be the "good guys". The reason doesn't matter- only that in this setting, you are an hunter, and you intend to make the world into a better place, one way or another.

    There are no more shadows to hide in.

    Step into the light.



    so, I'm looking for ideas for how the dynamics between the different hunter orgs would work, and how the world would function. For now, I plan on covering the canon groups, my groups (look at my sig) Skin & Bones' groups (google it) King Raven's groups (look at his sig), Dream Catchers, some ideas I'm planing to post and few other groups I've found in the internet (everyone would be linked to their rightful owners :P ). Any opinion, or material written, would be welcomed.

      Well, some things that immediately spring to mind when I read this...

      Ashwood Abbey may be on the "outs"; as they were basically hunters for the thrill of it, they probably object to the idea of licensing and other legal restrictions, which likewise gets the other Great Watcher Organizations steamed at them. Especially if Ashwood continues using their own wealth and social status to flaunt the rules.

      The Malleus Malleficarium has probably seen a drastic swell in prominence, even being an open organization. With real witches, vampires, werewolves, wolfbloods, ghouls, etc now known to exist, publically, there may be actual inquisitions and witch-hunts.

      In contrast, the Long Night is probably shrinking fast; many individuals may either see that the destruction of the "hidden horrors" as organized powers has defeated the threat of Armageddon, or simply deduce that the MM having "holy miracles" at their command whilst the Long Night doesn't is a sign of god's favor on the MM.

      The Loyalists of Thule have probably started to shake off their old "atonement" schtick. They and Network Zero may be fusing (or have fused) together into a sort of central data archive for all Hunter-related and collected lore, even if they still butt heads over the classification ratings.

      The Cainite Heresy is doubtlessly riding a thrilling wave of success in this Shard; if the MM isn't running an official inquisition anymore, than the Heresy most certainly will be. In fact, the Heresy may in fact be under fire from the GWOs, because they're too dangerous as loose cannons to be allowed to run rampant. Which is what they would do; they're fanatics, willing to destroy anything and anyone in the sake of exterminating vampires.

      The Union is probably skirting the edge of legality. It depends on if the GWOs is willing to recognize the Union as a legitimate hunting faction, especially because both are obvious candidates as sympathisers or sponsers for vigilantes.On the other hand, it may be the fundamental "grassroots" GWO, being the easiest Hunter organization to get into,

      The Bear Lodge is probably an open society, an elite hunting group that many Hunters aspire to join.

      VASCU and Task Force Valkyrie have both gotten a lot more authority and funding as a result of the fact they can openly admit to being psychic cops and the US's armed forces.

      Division Six has probably ceased to exist - no more Panopticon to do the funding and targetting for them - but its former members may have been assimilated into TFV.

                                  So- canon organizations. Because I don't want t close any option usually offered in the canon, all of the groups are going to exist in the setting- one way or another. Conspiracies are going up first, and the compacts latter.

                                  Task Force: VALKYRIE- a core and founder member of the Great Watcher Organizations, they see themselves as the skeleton upon which all the organization is built upon. They have the veto right in the Inner Council, being able to approve or disprove any other decision. They see themselves as the only "true" hunters in the group- those who don't seek the power of magical artifacts, make weird magics, moved by religious zeal or carrying dark blood in their veins. They also have some hidden connections and deals with certain monster groups, which help them as informants and sponsors.

                                  Malleus Maleficarum- if the Task Force is the skeleton, the Malleus are the heart of the GWO. Sure, not all hunters- not even the most- are Christians (not to mentions Catholics), but the Shadow Congregation give the other groups their "moral compass". They can invoke God, after all- or at least, His saints. While the other groups make sure there won't be any religious persecution, in the place under the rule of the Church one could feel the shadow of the Hammer in their back. They organize crusades and inquisitions meant to conquer (free!) lands ruled by the monsters or discovering hidden ones. Vampires are their main target, although witches are not that far in line- and especially those monsters who use false religion for their own agenda.

                                  Aegis Kai Duro- the Guardians of the Labyrinth are a mysterious group. Unlike many other hunters, they focus their efforts on items, not people. They are archivists and scholars who seek after hidden treasures and salvage them from the hands of the monsters. Relics are dangerous, after all- especially in the hands of monsters. They do have some clashes with other groups (the Malleus still tries to get back the Baptist's severed head), the Task Force believes that they are the best to protect those ancient relics from being a threat. They also keep an open eye for the experiments they preform upon witches.

                                  Ascending Ones- in order to rule the underworld, one must be a part of it. The Task Force is aware for the Cult of the Phoenix's dealing with drug traffic, but they prefer the devil they know upon the devil they don't. They have gave the group a monopoly upon drug traffic, although they still make sure their trade won't get out of control. Thankfully, being drug dealers is only one part of the group- they also know many great things, deal with occult and balance the Malleus so it won't get out of control. Usually, they just let those two groups to fight each other- both politically and physically- out of the assumption it would keep them all in line. They are also used is diplomats when dealing with organized group of monsters is needed, or when the rivalry between two hunter organizations is going out of control. Too bad they don't share their Elixirs.

                                  Cheiron Group- The all world's health insurance! Hunting monsters and making medicines out of them! Using vapiric blood to live forever, and witch's brain to rise you IQ! Among most of the other groups, Cheiron is truly one of the most prosperous from the situation. They pay handsomely for any monster given to them, and by using their skills they heal almost any kind of illness- for a price. The Task Force is trying to control them, and force all kinds of limitations upon their business- but their lawyers usually finds a loophole in the laws enforced by the GWO. The reason the group still exist is simple- its their money. They are the blood which brings the whole organization to life, and without them the other groups would suddenly find huge debts showing up of seemingly no where. Not to mention that they have few biological weapons hidden in their warehouses- just for case.

                                  Lucifuge- most of the other members of the GWO don't like the children's presence. Many claim that they are monsters by themselves, and should be hunted down like the rest of their kin. Yet, not many monsters are so dedicated at hunting down others of their kin, and their natural abilities to hunt down demons and other ephemeral beings is a great resource for the GWO. Plus, it seems their leader has a very.. intimate relationship with the leader of the Malleus, and the two have a delicate alliance. There are many rumors about the subject- from romantic affair which lasts for centuries up to a delicate plan to destroy all the monsters by killing Lucifer himself- or by bringing him back to Heaven. Still, with both the task Force and the Church on their side, the Lucifuge know that no one in the GWO would turn against them.. although it doesn't mean they are liked in there.

                                  Knights of Saint George- the Knights need to keep a facade when dealing with the GWO.The Malleus doesn't like them, and the Lucifuge sniff around too much. Publicly, they present themselves as the Anglican parallel for the Malleus, descendant from the hidden secrets of the Templars who have learned how to break the spells of witches and other reality warping monsters. Most of their own members don't even know the truth- even less then before. The knights by themselves spread dozens of different lies about the "truth" behind their organization, just in case that if the truth would ever leak out people would treat it just like another rumor. They are usually distant in the GWO's meetings, not speaking much and using the dark rumors around them as a weapon. No one trusts them- but for now, no one has proofs against them. For now.

                                  Les Mysteres- those scattered mystics, shamans and mediums are actually not a part of the GWO. Instead, they are independent, serving the gods, angels and demons of their own small communities. Unfortunately, certain members of the GWO see their gods as simply another monster who threats humanity- the Church sees them as pagans and heretics who sacrifice their souls for dark demons. The Lucifuge fears they may be possessed by beings from hell. The Knights think they are duped by witches. Still, while some groups dislike them, the Task Force isn't willing to brand them as a threat, if only because it seems that where they are, the Extraidimensional Entities are more silent and the shapeshifters seem to be in disadvantage. Still, they don't enjoy their protection directly, and sometimes there are violent clashes between them and other groups.

                                  Cainte Heresy- The Heresy is another group on the very edge of the GWO's map. They are fanatics, seemingly unconnected to each other, all having some weird magic which they use to hunt down and destroy monsters- especially the undead- especially vampires- at any cost. There is a lot of pressure on the GWO to stop the "Heretic Terrorism" from harming the innocents, and more then once the GWO had to act against them to stop an act which may have destroyed a monster- but would have also take dozens of innocent lives with it. Still, the group isn't officially considered as an illegal organization because the Task Force has vetoes the decision. Apparently, they are in touch the with power behind the conspiracy, or their "sources", and they try to get to a deal everyone would be happy from it. No one has ever saw those "sources" in the dinner parties, and some claim that they aren't even real- and that in fact, the Heresy is a creation of the Task Force to do illegal acts by themselves. It also seems that the Church and the Lucifuge know something on the subject, but their aren't telling.

                                  VASCU- If the Task Force is the Skeleton, the Church is the heart and Cheiron is the blood, VASCU is the brain. Their main concern is supervising all other organizations, making psychological evaluations and making sure no one would get out of control. They search for hunters who are a bit too happy with their job and track them down, making sure that they won't fall "too far". Their therapy sessions are mandatory for any hunter, and no one is accepted to any GWO organization without their permission (except of the Lucifuge, but VASCU isn't sure how to deal with their demonic minds anyway). In case an hunter goes renegade, or starts their own compact, or- in the worst case scenario- turns upon their own kind, VASCU enters the picture, and ebing the only group which may act without a Council decision, they have avoided many of the Task Force possible vetoes. Their willingness to deal with monsters to catch renegade hunters, however, isn't very lovable.

                                  Faithful of Shulpae- another independent group, the eaters of the gods are found repulsive by most other hunters. Their act is on the boundary of cannibalism, the Malleus and the Knights don't trust their "god" and the Lucifuge believe there is some sort of dark entity working behind the scenes. Yet, they usually don't attack regular humans, and the way they worship monsters eventually ends up with the monster dead- so the Task Force is more then willing to tolerate their acts. Actually, Cheiron has discovered a great interest in the group, for the way they infuse the supernatural power of the monsters into their flesh is done in a process the Group can't yet copy. Still, the Hungry don't like Cheiron, and tries their best to avoid their diggings and worshiping their gods silently. Recently, however, the Ascending Ones and the Lucifuge are having a joint effort to investigate the group, for both have legends telling about dark pharaoh who didn't stayed dead, and who ate the gods in his way the heaven. They believe that there may be some demonic influence upon the group, and that the monsters the eat feeds that demon. Still, the two groups risk a lot by doing so- for if the Task Force would discover their illegal operation, it may withdraw its protection, leaving them to the wolves.

                                  Knights of Saint Adrian- the mercenaries of Howard Investigation Services can not be tamed, not even by the efforts of the GWO and its Council. From the GWO's perspective, both God and the Devil may be a threat, especially since the angels who pay them to hunt demons and other monsters are as suspicious and weird as the rest of the beings who dwell in the shadows of the world. Yet, the group does work with the Council as a private company, and is sometimes invited to their dinners and meetings in a special "outsider" status. The Malleus isn't too happy about their presence, and both the Dragonslayers and the Lucifuge are wary around them (Lady Lucifuge herself never met with Casey, and she never goes to the dinners the last is invited to), the second from their fear of being connected to demons and the later from the fear of being connected to angels. Eventually, the Sidearm of Angels are a great resource for the GWO- and Cheiron has hired their services more then once.

                                          Ashwood Abbey- the hunters of the Abbey may be "free spirited" as they call themselves (or "deranged motherufckers", as others call them), but in the end, there is some order within the chaos- you can't be a member of the Abbey without a license, and the license cost a lot of money. That's why, when the GWO was just established and decided to shut the Hellfire Club up, the first thing it did was to freeze their accounts and take over their money. The act has almost destroyed the group- when mysterious person called the Patron made a contact with the unofficial leader of the Abbey's "pyramid scheme". By paying a bloody price, the members of the compact has gained accesses to an incredible amount of wealth and dark abilities which allow them to manipulate the basic imposes of others. Now, they stand independently, using their wealth to lure new members and make the Task Force look another place. The Lucifuge, however, insist on investigating them. They say that something smells funny- and it seems that the Patron's homeland stench has cling to the members.

                                          Long Night- with the rise of the Malleus, the Long Night has started to sink. Many of its more zealot members has converted their belief to the one of the Church after seeing the great miracles preformed by them. Like many other compacts, the diverse group has started to fall apart, and its members easily fall prey to the hands of the monsters. While it looked like the end of the small group, it all changed after a series of mystical events which ended up with the revelation of a divine being.. an angel. The being has gifted them with the gift of grace, and sent them to spread its word and gather those who were meant to save the world. While their zeal was seen negatively at first in the eyes of the GWO, and the Long Night didn't wished to "taint itself" with the manipulative government, lately there are touches between the GWO and the 14 Prophets who try to make them join the organization. For now, however, they remain independent.

                                          Loyalists of Thule- With the establishment of the GWO, the Task Force has saw a great interest in the Loyalists. Holding obscure knowledge and occult rituals, it didn't wished to destroy them as much as making them join the growing organization. Yet, before the Three Old Man could reach a decision, something unexpected happened- they died. Nothing special in process- just natural death. Yet, while they were alive, the only thing they knew was that they had to cling to their sins- and even in death, they couldn't let go. Their death has connected every member of the compact together, creating the great mental connection called the Archiv. Since then, each time a member of the Loyalists has died, he joined the Archiv, donating his experience to the whole as the finale price to pay for his sins. With the ghosts of the Three Old Man whispering in their ears, the Loyalists has joined the GWO, and they work together with both the Aegis Kai Duro and the Network-0.

                                          Null Mysteriis- among the GWO, the scientists of the Null Mysteriis are seen as fools. Their theories are ridiculous, and their attitude is arrogant. The only reason that the GWO hasn't shut them down is that they don't think it is necessary. They usually don't go out and hunt down monsters, onyl record their destruction and invent wild theories on the subject. The members of the compact, however, don't care about it- they prefer to be left alone, thinking by themselves about the nature of reality and the secrets of the universe. Cheiron follows their acts, however, in case that one of them would truly find a breakthrough. They even finance their researches, whicyh allowed them finding new ways to manipulate the mystical energy of the monsters.

                                          Network 0- the Secret Frequency has grew in power during the rise of the GWO. With so many monster videos showing up in the world, the group has became quite famous for their ability to track down monsters. Their importance has rose even higher when they got their hands on their own satellite, spreading their messages to the masses and even developing an app which allowed them to see through the masks most monsters used to hide among people. While the GWO didn't liked the anarchistic group at first, slowly they learned that they better get along with them then trying to destroy them (which was practically impossible. Shutting the group down was like shutting down the internet). While the Network isn't an official part of the GWO (they aren't willing to work with the "government"), the GWO simply follows their transitions and use them for their advantage against monsters. Among the members of the GWO, only the Loyalists are in direct contract with them, for the two groups recognize each other as natural partners in collecting information and using it. The only problem rises when the Network start investigating the GWO itself- broadcasting secrets most people would prefer to stay hidden.

                                          Union- an organized group of vigilantes is the GWO's most horrible nightmare- the the Union is the emboiodment of that nightmare. When the GWO has started to regulate hunters, the members of the Union has fought for their independents- yet without resources or a solid structure, they have started to fall apart. That was when they, and many other groups with similar agenda, has gained a weird invitation in the form of an app. Someone, using the guise of "Y", has created the Hunter-Net as a mean to connect all the different hunters, help them work together and protect them from incoming attacks made by the GWO. Since then, the Union has been listed as an illegal organization, and there is a big bounty on their head- yet the group carries on. Some within the group think that Y may be a member of the Task Force, who use it in a similar way they use the Sources to regulate the Heretic Terrorists, yet most scoff that theory away. Y have helped many members against the efforts of the Task Force and the rest of the GWO members, and everyone can start a "game" in the Hunter-Net, not just Y. Still, there is something fishy about the range and the timing of the app, and some fear that someone is manipulating the Union for its own advantages.

                                                  now, for the rest of the canon compacts

                                                  Division VI- when the GWO was established, they discovered that many of the so called "hunters" were actually corrupted by the influence of monsters. One of those groups was the Division VI. Once the Division was discovered, the GWO has made a direct effort to eradicate them as a compact and get rid of their manipulators. Yet, the monsters who use the Division were already step ahead of the GWO, and sent an emergency message that the government has been influenced by Reality Deviants in order to turn against them, and that they needed them to save all kinds of people the deranged "hunters" tried to kill in order to get control over the world. With the direct help of the monsters, the Division has managed to survive- although that now, they are perfectly aware to the fact that some of their higher ups are actually Reality Deviants by themselves. Still, desperate times, desperate needs- the government is corrupted by the influence of the GWO, who allow all kinds of Reality Deviants and shady characters into their ranks. Demonspawn, religious witches, drug dealing alchemists. It is better to support the monsters who are not in control then the once who are, and if the Reality Deviants use them, they would use them in return- especially since they give them a new, shiny equipment.

                                                  Keepers of the Source- the Dowsers has gone through a major change since the rise of the GWO. Under the guidance of Karen Redgrove, they turn from an anarchistic hippie terrorist into a solid, green movement. Thanks to their talents at mapping the world's Ley Lines, they have managed to both find natural resources and locate information about the mystical places which monsters use to "fuel themselves". Their mission was to protect Mother Earth from being used by the monsters, after all- and their leader decided that the best way to do so was to join the GWO. While they are in odds with the Knights of St George (who wish to destroy those "sacred place" for being a threat) and are seen as young and inexperienced by many other members of the GWO, their understanding of the Ley Lines and ability to track down those mystical places who could be used in order to set an ambush against monsters was more then enough to make the Task Force allow their entrance.

                                                  Promethean Brotherhood- Like the Faithful of Shulpae, the Firestealers are in an uncomfortable position in the GWO. From one side, they are on the verge of being slashers, ritually murdering people for power. On the other hand, they focus their efforts on witches and similar monsters. Unlike the Faithful, however, they don't actually eat their victims- and many of the other hunters in the GWO has done terrible things for monsters. Still, the problem is that the ritual they preform actually turns them into witches, which may be too uncomfortable for many among the GWO. The Knights of St George were the most dedicated in objecting their entrance to the GWO with the Aegis Kai Duro not that far behind, although the Lucifuge and the Malleus also objected for losing many members to the hands of the Brotherhood. Because of those efforts, they were left as independent for a long time- until suddenly, the Aegis Kai Duro has started to put a lot of pressure of the GWO to allow them to join. No one is really sure what has created the change of winds, but after a long negotiation by using the Ascending Ones as diplomats, the Brotherhood has committed to stop preforming experiments upon members of the GWO, which has lessen the objections of the Church and the Children (the Knights still objected, but no one really listens to them anyway). There are many whispers about the cause for the act- especially since they joined, the Brotherhood and the Aegis seem to work on a very special project.

                                                  Bear Lodge- the Lodge was one of the first groups to join the GWO. Seen as the elite hunters who dare to hunt against all chances has made into very popular choice, and their knowledge at ambush tactics and experience at hunting shapeshifters as truly draw the attention of the GWO. Their renown was so great, that it even drew the attention of the shadows gods, who have granted them of their blessings to help them hunt. Now days, the group serves especially in the front- the the edge of civilization, guarding the borders between the human and monster lands and even getting into the wilderness in search after certain targets. Among the GWO, no one is as respected as the Bear Lodge, and every time one needs to go into the wasteland he better use their help.

                                                  Illuminated Brotherhood- the Brotherhood is mostly ignored by the GWO, considering that most of their time is invested in getting high instead of hunting monsters. Still, the Ascending Ones are not too happy with their presence- for their experiments with creating new drugs is sliding into their territory, and harms the flow of their money. In order to satisfy them, the GWO has declared the group as illegal, and if they do something too foolish (like selling their drugs in public or start chasing after their visions) they put them in jail- but they don't invest a lot of effort in closing them down. If the Cult of the Phoenix really wants to get rid of them (or assimilate them), they should do it by themselves. For now, the Brotherhood enjoys their freedom- and making new discoveries in their drug labs.

                                                  Talbot Group- that compact was approached by VASCU, who recognized the need in using in therapy sessions for their hunters. While the Group was more then happy to lend their abilities to the GWO, they had one problem with their attitued- they didn't wanted the monsters dead. Instead, they believed that they could help the more human monsters to cling to their former self through therapy. For that reason, while the group does works closely with VASCU, it refuse to join the GWO- instead remaining independent and trying to help the monsters the best they can in order to keep their human visage. That make some of the more zealot organization to wish to act against the Group, but as long as VASCU protects them, there is little they could do- that is, if they don't wish to be interrogated about many, old crimes.
