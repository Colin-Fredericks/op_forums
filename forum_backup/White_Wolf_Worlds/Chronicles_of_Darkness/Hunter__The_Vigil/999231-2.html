        well, I think I'll start with my own groups' writeup, and then give full attention to Seattle's Demon angle.

        New Orleans:

        Cage of Shadows: Underground- while the heresy of the Night Mother was never welcomed among both the living and the dead, the cult is especially despised in the city. Having no more then a few cells, they draw most of their members from among the Jewish population of the city, and they use the already high crime rate in order to hide their practices from both vampires and the hunters who may target them. While they hide, they make sure to keep an open eye and check vampire activities, going out of the shadows only when they recognize the influence of one of the Lilim over the local vampires. In fact, the only reason the cult has managed to survive that long was due to the local leeches recognizing their usefulness and getting rid of the owl-demons, and the cult is well aware of it, making sure to keep their shrines to Lilith hidden and attack vampires only when they can make sure they could make other hunters to take the blame.

        Scharlach Spinnwebe: Supplying- where other hunters find themselves cornered by the bloodsuckers' influence, the Bloody Guild seem to prosper. Worming their way into the city by building a strong network among criminals and corrupted politicians, those hunters has managed to establish a powerful reputation among the leeches. Their blood dolls network has became a useful infrastructure in the eyes of a descent number of bloodsuckers, eliminating competition and creating a ordered feeding ground for any vampire in the city. They integrated so well, in fact, that even without their brainwashing blood magics they have enough allies among the vampires to protect them from potential threats in the All Night Society (not that it didn't helped them to do so). Lately, there have even been some discussion at allowing an "emissary" from the Guild to join the Prince's court. However, all that cooperation with the bloodsuckers has made them to be seen as betrayers and corrupted in the eyes of the local hunters, making them a descent number of enemies in the side of the Vigil.

        Ebony Fangs: Unyielding- little hunters has lost as much as the Fangs during the years, and they do not plan on losing any more than they already did. Ever since the fall of the European Branch, the main power base of the Fangs was in the city, where the Gatekeepers themselves sit and navigate the conspiracy's course in an attempt to survive the changing eras. With so many homicides and crimes being preformed in the city, a fair number of both ghosts and potential recruits are easily found (and that even without including Katrina's influence), giving the conspiracy a bit more room to breath then in almost any other place in the US. In fact, unlike in most places, the Fangs are even able to allow themselves to deal with monsters other than ghosts, attacking leeches and other forms of living dead. Their main target are those vampires who practice a perverted version of Voodoo, twisting the dead and binding them to their own will. Considering that this includes one of the more powerful vampires in the city called the Baron, countless attacks has been preformed against the conspiracy through the years- yet all has failed. The Laws of Irkalla stand strong, protecting their power bases against their enemies- and nothing sort of betrayal would make them fall.

        Followers of Set: Spiraling Down- with so much chaos, violence and blood in the city, it is a little wonder that New Orleans has captured the attention of the vampiric cult known as the Followers of Set. The Cradle of Chaos almost worship the city itself, seeing it as a bloody incarnation of their god. As part of a late trend in the cult, the practice of operating a human faction of hunters in order to protect both the living and the dead from terrible monsters from beyond the veil has started to grow in the city, and the mortal Followers of Set has prospered as one of the few hunter groups which are not under the constant threat of getting killed by the leeches. However, the very structure of the organization makes them too easy to be manipulated, and the high concentration of vampires also means more backstabbing and manipulations among the vampires itself. That has made many of the Sethite leeches to see those hunters as less of a shield and more as a secret weapon to be abused, turning them into assassins meant to hunt down rival vampires and other threats to the cult. More than once those manipulations has even turned one hunter against the other, as members of the Cradle has started to fight among themselves. Seeing as the circumstances practically tear both organisations apart, some among the hunters start to wonder if perhaps they should change the "conditions of their employment" in order to focus on the things which really matter, like dealing with the sudden awakening of sleeping gods.

            Denver:

            Nibiru: Damage Control- For the members of Marduk's Star, Denver is an example for what would happen if they fail their job. With their presence too weak in the area before the coming of the Old God, they were too slow to act against the monstrosity- which cause the deaths of many innocents until it was defeated by the hands of werewolves. Out of feeling responsibility to the situation, the conspiracy has since tripled its presence in the region in order to deal with the legacy of the idigam and destroy or bind the corrupted spirits and monsters it left behind. Some wonder, however, if it shouldn't be better to leave those scraps to the werewolves of Denver and focus their efforts around the newly come Moon Banished.

            E.V.E Institution: Harvesting- the high concentration of werewolves has since made the city into a point of interest to the scientists of E.V.E. With so many lycanthropes which could be used to make the precious Moon Serum- not to mention the large population of Spiders- ignoring Denver would be a missed opportunity- on the other hand, so many werewolves also mean as much as many ways to be killed, which means that if E.V.E wish to survive in the region, it must be careful. As a result, E.V.E does not maintain a constant presence in Denver- sending a single group of Test Subjects to capture as many werewolves and spiders as possible and return to base. While it is dangerous, the so called "empty wolves" of the place do present few interesting possibilities- their essence is much more mutable after being defiled by the mad god, allowing so many new possibilities to evolve. Plus, it allows Test Subjects to use their powers a lot more freely, knowing that the werewolves would probably think that they are just one more experiment of the fallen idigam.

            Church of the Blood Moon: Isolated- the Wold Shepherds do not want to be in Denver. They can trust no one in the city, and their songs may draw more werewolves than they can handle with. Still, the Archbishop of Night feel they must at least try to protect the innocents from the taint of the Wolf Devil, and so he makes sure the conspiracy would have at least a single cell in the wilderness of the Rookies at any given time. Many in the conspiracy think on serving in Denver as a form of punishment for those who break the conspiracy's rules, and those who survive rarely wish to speak about their experiences. They rarely make contact with any other hunter group, other than a casual meeting with he Bear Lodge. In the city of wolves, you can never trust a sheep, after all.

            Aki Ten: Investigating- only after the rise and fall of the idigam did the Aki Ten started to show interest in Denver. The rumors about mad gods, corrupted spirits and all other kinds of horrors crossed the sea, drawing the attention of the New Way. Their main goal is to understand what exactly happened in Denver, what truly was the spirit-monster which twisted the spirit wilds and how can they make sure it would not happen in Japan. Some of their members, however, has different goals in mind- they come to the region to bind the corrupted spirits to their service, exploiting their powers for personal gain. While using a tainted spirit as a shikigami is seen as a taboo, for it is said to corrupt the hunter's soul, it doesn't stop few of the less conventional members from doing so. Worse, some among the leaders of the conspiracy don't wish to research the legacy of the Moon Banished in order to defend themselves from it- but in order to find a way to bind one as their own shikigami, granting them god-like power. Good luck with that.

                    On to San Francisco!

                    San Francisco: A Royal Pain

                    A city like San Francisco is bound to have its odd characters (Hello, Emperor Norton I), but for hunters in the region the problem isn't so much limited to those creatures that would harm humanity. The self-titled "princesses" claim to serve a higher calling to heal and save mankind, but all a good portion of the hunters see is groups of young girls, and even teenagers, running around blasting weird things with magic beams and yammering on and on about friendship being magic. Still, they're doing good, and San Francisco hunters are becoming split on the best way to handle the girls before things reach a boiling point between the two hunter philosophies on the princesses.

                    Aegis Kai Doru: Magpies: The relics found near a princess are valuable enough that the Spear and Shield seek them out as fast as possible when reported. Massive amounts of power with relatively few or less painful drawbacks compared with witch relics or fae creations make a princess' accessories prized items among high ranking AKD members.

                    Ascending Ones: Cooperating: Like the princesses, the Cult of the Phoenix would rather see peaceable actions taken rather than outright violence. Working with a group of "ambassadors", the Ascending Ones have brokered several agreements between hunters and princesses that are considered fair enough to keep in place for a few years now, with the hope that such agreements could one day be permanent.

                    Ashwood Abbey: Angry: Princesses hate the Abbey, and the feeling is mutual. To the Abe, the princesses are the ultimate killjoys in their willingness to try and work with monsters and moral hunters. Several incidents between the Abe and various princesses have led to open conflict, with Abe members killing princesses where they can be found. This has in turn left the Abe with fewer friends than usual, since more than a few other compacts consider the princesses at least less dangerous, and even allies.

                    Barrett Commission: Investigating: Princess implies a royal title, and to the Commission another government trying to operate in the United States is suspicious to say the least. Securing the city and county for themselves, the Commission are working to find out more about this "kingdom" that they've heard about.Hopefully it's just a kind of organizing principle rather than an actual government, because if it isn't then the Commission will have to confront what they see as a potential group of assets.

                    Cheiron Group: Investing: Local Cheiron affiliate Redwood Health Supplements and Nutrition has been looking into the princesses for years, and are apparently interested in the idea of bringing some of them into their confidence. Even more surprising, a number of princesses have accepted, to the shock of several cells who have dealt with Redwood before.

                    Division Six: Pushing: Where Seattle seems to actively frighten Division Six, San Francisco presents what seems like opportunity. Division Six has three cells in the area, and all have been rumored to be meeting with several more militant hunter cells in the area in order to start tearing down the power structure of the princesses in the city. While these negotiations have allegedly been successes, several other hunters have noted that Division Six only ever seems to strike princesses that typically aren't considered even remotely involved in the day to day fighting in the city.

                    Hunt Club: Pining: After the heady days of the 1960s and 70s, the Hunt Club has started to languish recently. Their best members quieted by age or arrest, the club know that they're running out of recruits thanks to how "happy" the princesses are trying to make things. They have a trick or two still up their sleeves though, and hopefully they can bring back their best times sooner than the princesses can realize.

                    Illuminated Brotherhood: Fascinated: Compared to many US cities, San Francisco have a relatively benign spiritual landscape. Of course there are always going to be negative and dangerous spirits, but in a city filled with princesses there are an abundance of spirits of joy, hope, and community building a vastly different spiritual landscape than what many in the Brotherhood are used to. This has led them to wonder if princesses might be able to directly affect the spiritual world with their guidance on how to do it.

                    Keepers of the Source: Partners: As concerned environmentalists as well as dedicated hunters, the Keepers have come to respect and work alongside princesses, and are one of the largest compacts to push for a peace between the two groups. Keepers cells and princess "nakama" have become a dangerous force in the city, as the Keepers will lobby politically while the princesses use the distraction to root out the darkness in the city.

                    Long Night: Conflicted: On one hand, they're clearly doing good. On the other, they clearly aren't using the gifts of God. To the Long Night cells, they have had to wrestle with themselves over whether or not to join in the group of cells that want to see the princesses teaming up with the hunters, and standing apart and becoming regional pariahs.

                    Loyalists of Thule: Scouring: The name "Alhambra" keeps popping up in writings related to the princesses, and the Loyalists are wondering if maybe it didn't inspire the name of the palace complex in Granada, Spain. More importantly, it has several similarities to the lost continents of Atlantis, Lemuria, Thule, and Mu, leading the Indebted to wonder if it might not be a physical location they can find. So the Loyalists are on a massive sweep for any and all information regarding the lost city.

                    Lucifuge: Confused: So wrapped up in trying to save monsters from themselves if they can, the Lucifuge are at a loss to explain princesses in their worldview. Beings so wrapped in love and compassion aren't the kind of foe the Lucifuge are used to, and several times a Lucifuge member in California has had to quickly explain to a princess that they're fighting the same enemies before they get their asses kicked.

                    Malleus Maleficarum: Set Against: The Malleus had a time dealing with the princesses in Scotland, and has since spread the word that they're no friend to the Church or their flock. The Malleus are doing their best to spread the word in San Francisco about the princesses, how they literally steal hope or create dangerous cults just as much as acting like they're saving the world. A few unaffiliated cells have listened, though the larger hunter community feels that the Malleus are letting past actions judge present events.

                    Network Zero: Ecstatic: Princesses have their ideas on secrecy, but Network Zero in the city has earned their trust to the point where they feel perfectly safe and willing to work with the compact by willingly showing off their abilities and taking NetZo cells with them on their fights against what they call "The Darkness". This has turned Network Zero into the city's leading experts on the princesses, their goals, and who they're fighting.Which has made many NetZos giddy with excitement.

                    Night Watch: Peaceable: On the West Coast, the Night Watch prefers a more community-focused touch compared to the violence in the Midwest and on the East Coast. Respecting this stance by the Watch, several princesses have actually become honorary members, walking the same patrols and riding the same subways as the Watch patrols. Many hunters warn that this is getting too close for comfort, but the Watch are more than willing to accept the help that other hunters have typically denied them.

                    Null Mysteriis: Planning: Since the princesses seem so willing to work with hunters against the strange and unknown, Null Mysteriis in San Francisco is in the early stages of setting up a series of tests to see if the magic of a princess can't actually be the first paranormal phenomenon that can be truly quantified and measured.

                    Talbot Group: Thankful: Princesses are much more mindful of psychological pain than most hunters even care to acknowledge, and their work will often involve getting help for those left behind after a raid or street fight. Several princesses have volunteered to work with the Talbot Group in San Francisco, helping the group find a place for wolf-blooded, recovering ghouls, and sensitives learning about their abilities along with the more mundane but no less important cases of mental illness and counseling.

                    Task Force: VALKYRIE: Hunting: Where the Commission is trying to piece things together, TFV isn't waiting around to find out. Mentions of a "kingdom" are officially considered a threat to the functioning government, and MRUs in northern California have been ordered to eliminate princesses wherever they're found, interrogation is only an option when they can be taken alive and by surprise.

                    Union: Wary: As a majority of San Francisco seems to fawn over the princesses, the Union in the city wants little to do with them. Of course they'll accept help if they need it, but they aren't so willing to throw open the doors for the princesses the way other groups are. This has led to a partnership of sorts between the Union and Malleus, both carefully watching the princesses for fear of what they might do if too many hunters let their guard down.

                    VASCU: Searching: More than a few serial killers and kidnappers have operated in the San Francisco area, and VASCU is worried that there are still more roaming the region that they haven't found. Though the search for the Zodiac is just about a lost cause at this point, the reality is that there are still several unsolved serial murder cases that the unit believes should be considered high priority.

                        Boston

                        Museum Secretorum: Fascinated- so many witches gathering in one place also means so many beautiful wonders to add to their collection. One of the conspiracy's largest museums is found in the city, and it is well guarded by the members of the group to make sure no witch would be foolish enough to try and steal their precious collections. Many new potential recruits could be found among the many students of the city's colleges and universities, and the Museum usually send "talent hunters" to lure such students to their path. As whole, the Museum prefers to avoid actually hunting witches to their collection in the city, knowing that one wrong move and they'll bring the whole willworking population upon their heads, and as such most of their time is invested at getting interviews from mages and other supernatural beings and collect some rare items. Unknown to most of the witches, however, one of their factions (called the Mysterium) has made a certain alliance with the hunters- the Museum would let them access their archives and investigate some of their rarer phenomena, and in exchange certain renegade witches (called "Left Handed") would be sent to their collection as their "punishment".

                        Order of the Void: Getting Armed- the followers of the Nameless has a long term goal, and they believe that Boston may be one of the finale moves in their grand game over the Void. The Inner Council has lately announced the beginning of Phase Two, and Boston seems to going to take a major part in that plan to achieve the blueprints of the Abyss. While it is all very hush-hush, all the hunters know that a war is coming, and that they should supply and gather their resources toward the coming battle. Their target- a local cult named simple as the "Red Word". While the Order has never liked those witches who communicate with the Void (especially after the events of the Burned Over District), and their cannibalistic rituals are more then enough to make every member of the conspiracy to want to put a bullet in their heads, the Inner Council seem to have their own plans- something about time travel, or alternative histories. While the war would be bloody, thankfully most of the witches seem to hate such cults as much as the Order does. They might even help them- as long as they won't discover that the Order is a cult all by itself.

                        A.S.T.R.A.L: Threat Detection- why so many mages seem to gather around the region? What is so special about the city to make witches search for it? A.S.T.R.A.L don't know, but they are bent to find out. All their data resources seem to show that Boston has some importance to the mages, and that means they have to find the source and burn it clean. Until then, the book burners have a strategy to deal with the threats of the city- they donate the to local colleges in order to get a foothold over information, targeting both potential members who seem to relate to their ideology and students who start to show signs of starting to Awake, giving them enough time to monitor the potential threats and devour the Awakening before it even takes place. Some other members, however, seem to relate to the more "traditional" strategy of their company- they break into the houses of witches, devour their grimoires and relics and drain every Source they can find. Some even preach to the locals about hidden dangers, taking the roles of priests or just traditionalists who fear the change in the city's nature. Sure, exploiting racism and harming innocents is wrong- but the dangers of uncontrolled knowledge are far worse.

                        Nemontemi: Driven- while the group's headquarters is found mostly in Latin America, with the rise of Hispanic population that strain of cursed blood has found its way through the US- and Boston became the focus of their attention. The star gods of the void are drawn to the acts of willworkers, after all, and a city with such an high population of mages means that the chances for such an event to happen is just are just as high. Few local cells of the Star Children operate in the city, ignoring most of the internal politics of the witches and focusing their attention around those magicians who speak with the Abyss and summon its denizens as servants, although the local soul thieves also seem to be draw their attention, as their broken soul is tainted by the same darkness of the Beyond. The most disturbing reports are about the cult of the Red Word- their cannibalistic behavior and the rumors about their faith in a world which is yet to come once the previous would end are just too similar to the old rituals of their peoples for them to ignore. Perhaps they are another group of people manipulated by dark gods. Perhaps this time, they are right. And maybe, just maybe some of their own support the cult from the shadows. Whatever the case, the have to check it out- and destroy the cult if needed to.

                            Detroit

                            Ashen Collective: Scavenging- there is nothing the Alchemist of the Grey like then plundering a dead corpse. They gathering of the Reanimated has drawn their attention, and the general decline of the city made them very interested in experimenting on what would happen to a place which its rot is accelerated by the gathering of the living dead. Many believe it is only a matter of time until a major Firestorm would swap the city, devouring the rot and giving new life- and they plan on capturing its flames as it would happen. Others are just as happy at taking over old assets and make them into traps meant to capture the Reanimated to their researches. Even regular corpses aren't safe from their touch, and they are willing to pay a lot for a steady supply of test subjects- no matter how and why they died.

                            Custodes Ignis: Sort Handed- the Fire Guards saw the fall of many empires through the years, and they know the signs. Detroit is only a symptom, not the disease- but the problem should be dealt with before it is too late. Many of them would have been happy to blame the Reanimated for the rot, but they know that while the supernatural explanation could be comforting, as in giving them something to do, sometimes there is something they can do. They still deploy their cells at an attempt to cleanse at least some of the effect created by the prometheans, relieving the supernatural influences of the Disquiet and the Wasteland over the city- yet the root of the problem is deeper than that. There is little they can do with mundane fall of an empire, no matter how much they try. Perhaps it is time to find a new empire to serve- China could be a good candidate, perhaps.

                            Sabaoth: Crusading- while the Burning Serpents serve as mercenaries, they never forget their true calling- that is, to clean the world from the taint of stolen fire. The reanimated who come to a city to which they don't belong, and souls touched by them sense the fear and turn upon each other. The general atmosphere of distrust allows easy recruiting to the group which gives them someone else to blame- someone to hate, to despise and fear. The reanimated are the reason Detroit is failing, they say, and you should fight against those who don't belong to this world. The truth of the matter is not a concern- all that matters is that it is "you" against "them", and that you should join the angels in their crusade against the walking dead.

                            Ebony Fangs: Cautious- the emissaries of the underworld has always found themselves growing where others rot. Detroit is dying, and while it does its ghosts are left behind- restless shades of the past clinging to life. Knowing that a fall of such a city without a careful management could be a disastrous event, few cells of the Fangs has already started a some sort of "funeral" to the city. Unlike in the more rich regions of the US, the fall of Detroit also means higher chances to recruit, bolstering their numbers enough to be able to maintain a constant, worthy to note presence in the city. The reanimated in general has captured the interest of the group- as being neither truly dead or alive, the Laws of Irkalla seem to operate funny near them, treating them as either (or both or none) of the states. With their population being so rare, the Fangs see Detroit as an excellent chance to study those beings and retrieve at least some of the information lost in the European Branch.

                                Miami

                                Orchestra Fatalibus: In Plain Sight- while the Singers' true power base is found back in Europe, Miami is their largest center in the US. Drawn together with the many musicians, dancers and other performers, the conspiracy has established a strong network of information sharing among each other, and even adapted some of its ages old songs (both magical and communicative) into fresh new styles- rumba, techno, hip hop and even rap all have their own fans, and it is their duty to give the people the music they wish for.. especially if more people listening to their songs means more people influenced by them. By using the influenced masses as both as part of their information network and in order to uncover hidden fairies, the Orchestra is able to confuse, misdirect and befuddle their enemies, sending them toward secondary threats while the true power lies hidden.

                                Garden of Hesperides: Involved- unlike some other hunter groups, the Herbalists did not came to Miami in order to hunt the Fairies- they came to help them. They walk inside the thorny Hedge in order to find new lost souls and direct them to the great Courts in order to help them adapt to their new situation. They also use their skills and Silver Roads in order to harvest and supply rare Goblin Fruits to the changelings, and they hold a number of pacts and pledges with the local Courts for mutual help and alliance. Considering the large amount of time they spend in the Hedge, those hunters always keep an open eye in a case they'll hear rumors about a coming Huntsman, sending a word of warning in advance and give the changelings some time to prepare- and in worst case scenario, their Silver Roads may offer a temporary escape from the Wild Hunt.. as long as you don't stray from the path, that is.

                                Chasseurs de Tetes: Zealot- the Headhunters seem to hate Miami with burning passion. A land so well controlled by nobles and fate is the antithesis for all they work for. The Idea seem to rage against the city, summoning hunters from across the sea to come and fight until their last breath in the name of freedom. Such sudden outbursts of the Enraged come and die out every some time, as they raid local power bases of the fairies and cut off their heads or die trying. Meanwhile, the changelings have since managed to understand that those attacks seem to be connected to each other, but they don't seem to manage to piece a full picture of their enemy. Their magics seem to fail around them, and the Wyrd itself is cut by their hands. They blame them for a group manipulated by some Huntsman or even a Gentry, and search the Hedge for further information about who those "Headhunters" may be. Ironically, the Headhunters don't hunt only the changelings in the city- Florida had its fair share of serial killers, and the Chesseurs are not above tracking new potential recruits and convert them to the Idea, or cut off the heads of those who can't rise above their murderous nature. After all, its not like they are using them anyway.

                                    And we're on to San Diego/Tijuana!

                                    San Diego/Tijuana: A Fishing We Will Go

                                    As the Vigil is a worldwide duty, so to is it international in San Diego and Tijuana. Even with a border bisecting the metro area, the hunters in the region have created an entire cottage industry centered around getting cells through to both cities to carry on the Vigil in spite of the US and Mexican authorities. The bigger problem is the reality that the region is being overrun by a race of fish-men who are intent on being worshiped as gods by any human under their controlling presence. The monsters don't even need to make an effort to control the minds of men, their simple existence is enough to twist a man's mind to abject worship.

                                    Aegis Kai Doru:

                                    Ascending Ones: At War: There is no other way to describe it. The Cult of the Phoenix is fighting a war in Tijuana. A fish-woman by the name of Tia Ramona has been working to dismantle their operations in the local drug trade, and she and her followers have succeeded in taking down many of the conspiracy's more prominent members. While the local Ascending Ones are fighting back to protect their holdings from what seems to be a dispute over territory, some suspect that the Leviathan's campaign is being carried out for more personal reasons. But their drug connections have come in handy for getting people across the border, and it's enabled the Ascending Ones to call in favors they aren't shy about using.

                                    Ashwood Abbey: Chartering: In the minds of the Abbey membership, San Diego is practically akin to a fishing vacation. Setting their clubhouse out on the coast as close to the border as possible, the Abbey will charter to sail as close to Mexican waters and go against any fish-men they can find. That several international incidents have nearly occurred thanks to the recklessness of the Abe doesn't seem to matter.

                                    Barrett Commission: Networking: With the threat of the fish-men so pervasive, the Commission knows that securing San Diego and putting the people who can save Tijuana in power will be next to impossible. The Commission is trying to create a system of hunters to use as proxies, directing them to the threats that are the most obvious to both sides in order to break the monster power base in city government and make a buffer to the south.

                                    Cheiron Group: Fishing: The "leviathans" are complete gold-mines for the company, and their office in San Diego is constantly sponsoring "biological expeditions" off the shores of both countries to steal the most choice pieces. There's also rumors of some kind of "rift" where even more amazing samples and specimens can be found, and that the leviathans know exactly how to reach it.

                                    Faithful of Shulpae: Growing:

                                    Long Night: Warring:                                     "Though Shalt Have No Other Gods Before Me". The Long Night are often peaceful, but a direct affront to their God like the fish-men will most certainly not stand. The Long Night has been waging a hard conflict against the fish-men and their cults, doing their best to try and draw the poor deluded fools away from their false idols and back to the loving arms of a merciful deity.

                                    Loyalists of Thule: Fleeing: The Loyalists believe that even if one of the cities can be cleared, the other will still remain a fetid hole of monsters. Orders from Munich are that the Loyalists in the city should grab what they can and get out if they don't want to be abandoned. A few are waiting as long as possible, trying to spread what they safely can to other hunters before the Loyalists leave entirely.

                                    Lucifuge: Unrestrained: Fighting against evil that is outright evil isn't hard for the Lucifuge. The Seventh Generation have tried before to make some kind of contact with the leviathans, and it ended with fifteen of them dead at once. Such a loss is too much to bear even if their numbers replenish with each death, and the Lady has decided that such creatures are too far gone to save no matter what mercy can be given.

                                    Malleus Maleficarum: Stalwart: The leviathans have been recorded multiple times in the Church's history, from the coasts of the Levant to cults devoted to Jörmungandr in the north of Europe. Knowing that these threats can destroy whole communities and even small nations, the Malleus have used their power on both sides of the border to at least shore up the defenses, ensuring that the monsters can have no easy time moving between the two nations.

                                    Network Zero: War Correspondence: The Secret Frequency has been smuggling and posting footage from the border for a good fifteen years by this point, trying to get the information to other cities that might be wrapped up by the fish-people. Unfortunately the fish-men don't like this, and Network Zero has been forced to fight back. Worse, reports are spreading that the videos are having the complete opposite effect.

                                    Night Watch: Stymied: Since the fish-men can promise their followers safety and security, it often means that local leeches are a prime target for their cults. This has left the Night Watch in a bind, since their more public efforts to protect their communities are focused more on mortal criminals than the paranormal. While this has given the Watch a more favorable reputation in San Diego than in other US cities, it has left the leadership wondering how much good they can do for their fellow hunters.

                                    Null Mysteriis: Floundering: Though some of the group think that maybe the fish-men are some kind of proof of the "aquatic ape" theory, most are more focused on the reality that these things are problems. Dissections of corpses reveal multiple atavistic growths in each one, whether it be gills, scales, or even proof of hermaphroditic reproductive capabilities. None of it has helped ORAS develop anything more concrete however, and the fish-men are sending their cultists after them to keep their secrets safe.

                                    Talbot Group: Overwhelmed: You would think that the cults are bad enough, but deprogramming a fish-man's followers is only part of the problem. It turns out that some individuals suffer an opposite effect, an intense hatred that leaves them consumed with a self-destructive mission to kill all the fish-men they find. For now describing them as "Ahabs", the Talbot center in San Diego is facing more cases of both brainwashed cultists and rage-driven Ahabs.

                                    Union: Battered: Left adrift on both sides of the border, the working stiffs of both cities have been overrun by fish-cults, lunatics driven to kill fish-men wherever they're found, and the conspiracies leaving them to dry. Still the Union continually builds up, grabbing families left in ruins by the fish-cults and forming cells practically every week. Casualties are high on both sides of the border, but a major Union push in Tijuana might be the key to secure at least the Southern border.

                                    Task Force: VALKYRIE: Building: As a major military city, TFV has had to contend with the fish-men in San Diego for some time. For years it was a running tit-for-tat fight, but recently command has decided that they've had enough. Intelligence has been found that places at least one subversive leviathan element within Marine Corps Recruit Depot San Diego, and that's one too many. TFV is building up a strike force, with several MRUs seconded to the mission. Soon, they've sweep the city, and into Tijuana if necessary, to clear it out.

                                    VASCU: Forgotten: With all the major players in San Diego focused on the fish-men and vice versa, VASCU has been tracking several individuals they don't realize are Ahabs. The agents have also been subject to constant psychic pressure from the creatures, but have formed their own support system to make sure they can stand through it and fight. Possibly making themselves immune to the creatures, VASCU may one day be the trump card the Vigil needs in San Diego, and possibly even Tijuana.

                                                Okay, with all the major fan game lines done and with no ideas what cities are left, I had an idea for a Slasher-centric city.

                                                Cleveland: Fittingly Named

                                                In the US, one city has gained a dubious and dangerous reputation for the people that seem to flock to it. Out of all the cities in the US, the city has an almost magnetic pull to those who kill for killing's sake. Slashers are just as drawn to the city, and the Vigil in Cleveland is focused on trying to make it through another night without a double-digit body county of innocent people being racked up.

                                                Aegis Kai Doru:

                                                Ascending Ones:

                                                Ashwood Abbey:

                                                Cheiron Group: Recruiting: You have to be a special kind of crazy to be part of Field Projects, and most slashers are just the kind of crazy Cheiron is looking for to capture the big money projects. Sure, you lose a few cells to competition, infighting, arrest, but it's a small price to pay when you literally get a baker's dozen of werewolf bodies or staked vampires for research even two weeks. Unfortunately, the scuttlebutt around Cleveland is that some kind of doctor is in the area, and making life hell for Cheiron.

                                                Hunt Club: Ruling: The city is the Hunt Club's unofficial headquarters at this point, with the ranking members making annual and even bi-annual trips to hone their skills and talk over their scores with their peers. Controlling the city through a mixture of blackmail, extortion, and just inviting the powerful to join them on their romps, the Club have little to fear from the local hunters and even less to fear from those "moralists" at VASCU.

                                                Long Night:

                                                Loyalists of Thule, Lucifuge: Seeking:                                                 The Loyalists don't think that this many serial killers wind up in Cleveland out of coincidence. There must be an occult reason for why it's happening, and the Loyalists are using their own experience with the murderous to make their own theories. The leading one is that there's a kind of power hidden in the area, one that these murderers and slashers are somehow drawn to. Unbeknownst to the Hunt Club, the Lucifuge have a presence in the region and are seeking them out. The constant murders by the club have attracted the attentions of Milan, and a cell has been sent from New York to try and halt the bloodshed if only for a time. The more pressing matter is, like the Loyalists, trying to puzzle out a reason so many of the murderous are being drawn like moths to a flame in the city.

                                                Malleus Maleficarum: Bound: The Malleus in Cleveland are actually hindered by the Church itself in trying to handle the problems involving slashers. Explicit orders have never come from Bauldino or Gallaher on what to do about the city, and the cells are trying to pull themselves together for some kind of plan to fight back against the murderers without being thrown in jail. The only out they can see is using the Cleveland Emerald Society as a recruiting ground for police that might be willing to fight.

                                                Network Zero: Dying: In a sick joke out of Wes Craven, the NetZos in Cleveland are dying each night because they're trying to put out the truth. Since slashers are still somewhat human, the Network isn't as bothered by the conspiracies as in other cities, but the slashers more than make up for it since Network Zero keeps trying to stop them by putting their faces and victims up across the internet and in people's faces.

                                                Night Watch: Strong: In a city like Cleveland, the Night Watch stand as a beacon of hope. Riding public transit with mace and (if they can get the right permits) a pistol, the real heavy hitters on the Watch stay out of the spotlight as best as possible to strike at the slashers where they can be found. For once acting in close concert with the police, the Watch are still stymied whenever they confront a killer who uses their social status to evade the law time and again.

                                                Null Mysteriis: Studying: Since their paper on slashers was published, ORAS have become the de facto experts of slashers, and have setup a facility in Cleveland trying to puzzle out whether they can be saved from themselves or just drugged and left to languish in a cell. Two relatively sane (for a very loose use of the word) slashers have been found, one who keeps killing criminals and one that suffered grievous abuse for a major birth defect. With the Talbot Group still far and away in either New York or the West Coast, ORAS psychologists are gently trying to convince the two that not every problem needs to be resolved with outright murder. Suffice to say, it's slow going, and the second slasher is starting to give the staff disconcerting glances when they come in the office.

                                                VASCU: Building: Knowing full-well that the city is under the grip of the slasher cabal known as the Hunt Club, VASCU agents are preparing the mother of all legal smackdowns. Experienced agents have been brought in to help catalogue every piece of evidence and poke holes in every alibi. Wintergreen agents have been put on dozens of stakeouts, tracking and marking each night that a murder has occurred when a suspected member is in the same area. Such an operation might have been put down by the special agent in charge of the office years ago, if Washington wasn't so paranoid about their being compromised that the whole thing is being kept locked tight from them.

                                                    New York

                                                    Ebony Fangs: Overflooded- there is not place to show the Fangs' weakness like New York. The big apple is, more or less, the American Dream personified. A nihilistic group such as the Fangs find themselves at odds with the general atmosphere, and many of them ends up as another "crazy homeless guy" on the streets. Add to that the great number of ghosts in the city and the well founded network of ghostwalkers, and it is a wonder the hunters of Irkalla haven't all went crazy already. Many of them fear the city, knowing that once they'll make a single step into its limits the screams of the dead and the visions of horror just won't stop. Those who do live in New York force the Old Laws for protection on daily basis, doing whatever they can in order to silence the dead just to get one night of nightmare-free sleep.

                                                    Pomegranate Deme: Prospering- for the Memory Collectors, New York is a treasure. So many restless souls waiting to be bound into their little boxes, so much knowledge waiting to be recovered and collected to their storage rooms. They maintain dozens of small antique shops which deal with fetters and bound dead bellow the surface, and meet up in graveyards in order to preform their old rites. If not only for all of those nosy "sin eaters", who spend so much time and stealing their fetters and freeing the bound ghosts, the Collectors would have already taken over the city. Still, the high concentration of Eurydices and Orpheuses has its own benefits- for who knows what hidden secrets of the Bellow hide beneath their masked faces?

                                                    Order of the Nameless: Steady- ever since the days of the Burned Over District, the Order's base of operation was and stayed in New York. Maintaining a number of alliances with the local witches, the Order was more or less accepted as a regular occult society with a wide network among the so called "apostates". While the Order has learned from its past mistakes and no longer allow witches position of power within the organization, the Inner Council does accept Auditions with the local mages to give them advises about Abyssal incursions and share their knowledge about the worlds beyond. Since the beginning of Phase Two, such meetings has became even more frequent, as the competition over the Black Sun Map force them to use any asset in their disposal.

                                                    Silver Syndicate: Filthy Rich- the hunters of the Syndicate proudly claim that no other hunter group is as a "New Yorker" as they are. Growing for the bottom of the streets, their leader's craftiness and their strong alliances with the local spirit world has made them into a power to reckon with. Almost all lower tiers hunters have an account at "Vigil Insurances" or have their accounts and real life businesses managed by the company. While the spiritual ecosystem is, more or less, under their control- the so called "ghost plague" is a problem. Worse, it seems that those people who died once in the past see little to no need in things like "life insurance". While the world of the dead is an enigma for the Syndicate, like every good company it already has plans of expansion. Donnie has collected few highly renowned grimoires about the Great Bellow and the shades of the dead, and already has plans on how to exploit the city's "natural resources" for his own good. Sure, the Syndicate's hold over mundane and supernatural assets has already made them into filthy, stinking rich- but if New York has taught them something, is that you should never settle for what you have. That's how you get eaten.

                                                            I actually don't have any of the 2E books or any information about what's going on in them.

                                                            Which is why I'm moving on to some of the lesser developed fan-games, because I can at least make a reasonable presumption based on them compared to stuff I know nothing about. If anyone else has any cities they think would be good to add, jump on and hit it.

                                                            Las Vegas: The Devils Playground

                                                            Sin City isn't just a cute like sobriquet that got slapped on Vegas for the vices that literally keep the city's economy from sinking into the sands. Demons have moved into the city in force, tempting men and women to their darker deals and ideas on how to get ahead. Though hunters have always been present in the city, since the mob left Vegas the Vigil has suddenly faced even harder times against their enemies. It appears that the mob was enough of a tempter for humans without demons getting involved, but with their grip destroyed by the Feds the demons have moved in en masse, knowing that the desperate would flock to them.

                                                            Pittsburgh: Getting Ugly

                                                            With so many of the "hunchbacks" in the city, hunters are conflicted on how to treat them. Some believe that the poor bastards should be protected from themselves and their enemies, since no one else seems willing to. Other hunters feel that they're monsters even to other monsters, so better to kill off the disease before it can spread. A third faction of hunters feel that they're being too harsh of the hunchbacks to just kill them, and that their willingness to do anything for a master is exactly the tool the Vigil needs in Pittsburgh. It isn't unusual to have entire "cancer cells" of hunchbacks running nightly on the orders of a compact or conspiracy, or even a well-connected lone cell. This has threatened to turn into an arms race, with hunters preparing to snatch up as many "I-Gors" as possible for their own ends.

                                                            Atlanta: Sickening

                                                            Stories coming out of Atlanta are that there's a kind of disease spreading, some kind of literally smart bug that affects people in different ways. The only reason that it hasn't broken out completely is that the Feds are keeping it so tight under wraps that not even Network Zero has much on them. After the panics about Ebola though, no one can really blame them. Fortunately, the Vigil in Atlanta is relatively well organized, considering the massive Federal presence in the city that no one wants to call down on themselves. So each night, cells are trying to figure out just what is the story behind these rumors of a strange dangerous disease.
