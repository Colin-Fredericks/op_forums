    126) The PCs are hired by members of another pack to babysit their children, who all went through the first change young and were put into a pack (which holds the school they go to and the shopping mall down the block from said school as their territory, plus their parents territory) together by their parents, while the parents are off handling a crisis (possibly created by the pups) in the hisil. The pups are that particular combination of smart and bored that leads to act out in spectacular ways, such as pranks on other supernaturals, which should be fun for the sitters...

            128) A pretty powerful Crescent Moon without pack makes it a habit of killing people in a ritual manner and leaving their bodies for the authorities to find.

            The PCs might not approve. Upon asked, the Ithaeur (who doesn't appear to be all sane) will state that the killings are absolutely necessary in order to keep an insanely powerful spirit/demon/god/thing away.

            Now, the pack shouldn't have any real problem stopping the werewolf serial killer, but if they do so, they'll very soon start getting really, really bad nightmares of something getting through the Gauntlet and wreaking havoc on the mortal world - that is, if they don't offer any sacrifices.

                129) A pack of werewolves with bizarre, machine-themed Gifts has cropped up, and has been proselytizing wildly about a "God-Machine", claiming its superiority over even Mother Luna. Worse yet, their bizarre gifts show that that may actually be true.

                130) A Werewolf has been blessed by Helios for the first time since the Urfarah's death, resulting in an Uratha of the Selenelion Auspice, changing at a Lunar eclipse with the Sun still visible in the sky. All the local Packs have been courting her to join them, and lead them toward final victory in the war against the Pure, as prophesied by Ithauer sages.

                131) The recent Blood Moon Eclipse has produced a fearful Gishtahu/Surrarahu hybrid, possessing the wiles of the Eclipse and the cannibalistic madness of the Blood Moon. If he's not caught and subdued, he could caus incalculable damage to the local spirit ecology.

                132) Your Pack stumbles upon a place where there is no gauntlet, the very last Border March. It seems like a fantastical, beautiful place, until a massive, beating heart is found at its center. The heart incites Rage in Werewolves. Some claim that it's the heart of the Ur-Farah. Is it? And if it is, what should be done with it?

                133) Somehow, a Wolf-blooded in your Pack has Changed...but not into a Werewolf. He was instead chosen by Helios, becoming a being of flesh and fire. Will he remain loyal to the pack? And why would Helios decide to emulate his sister Luna?

                Genius: the Transgression 2E is a thing that's being worked on.

                    Crossover hook with Requiem....

                    134) A Storm Lord from a neighboring pack has been found dead after disappearing on a lone hunt. An investigation reveals that a relative or close friend of his had been Claimed by an insidious yellow-eyed spirit of smoke and shadows, who used him to commit a series of still-unsolved murders. Unfortunately, things soon go from bad to worse. Several similar spirits are sighted in the area, Claiming more people to carry out a ruthless campaign of killings that leave widespread panic in their wake, it's enough to bring together all of the Uratha in a Siskur-Dah. But just as the packs completed the rite and move to carry out the Hunt, they suddenly find themselves attacked by a hit squad of Kindred whose leaders are convinced they're in league with the spirits.

                                                    142) There is a new pack in the town. However, they are very strange. None of them has any marks, or signs that they have been removed. Instead a strange golden script runs in thin bands all over them. None of their gifts is known to any werewolf in the vicinity and their totem is... strange. Spirits reported that they worship a strange stone tablet with a single rune in their loci.

                                                    143) A well known ghost wolf had disappeared in a shadow and was presumed dead till a week ago. Now he claims to have been cured from the moon madness and indeed is much calmer and collected then other werewolves. The other point? His wolf forms have changed, strips of rainbow scales run through the fur like a demented form of luna's brands and a set of heavy antlers sprouts from his head. And what is this talk of the rulers of life and spirit he is proselytizing about?

                                                    144) Someone had built a fortress in Hisil. It's not a spirit reflection of the real world, it's a genuine building erected directly in the shadow. Overnight.
                                                    A strange barrier bans both spirits and werewolves from entering it. Just when both were going to get their act together and figure out what that thing was a host of minor spirits rose from it, all carrying the same message:
                                                    "The Shadow Amphitheatre is open! Come, watch, fight and win!
                                                    Fights to death, fights to win! Shadow Chess and Mage Duels on weekends!
                                                    entrance fee 1 Essence, participation fee 5 essence, Prize - 100 Essence for day's winners!"
                                                    *Resonance and Essence spill are taken care off. No freeloaders.

                                                    145) A strange priest has taken a sway over the neighboring pack. He promises deliverance from rage and madness. The werewolves following him indeed seem a Lot calmer and controlled. They also react and deal with any trouble on their territory with incredible speed. But, why are their marks warped so strangely? What are those new strange moon gods and spirits they have taken to honoring? and why some spirits claim to have met strange silvery apparitions that no werewolf can see?

                                                        146) A new dance craze has taken over the city's youth culture. This dance, which is kind of like a stripper's take on the macarena mixed with a very sexually suggestive twirling motion but performed by two dancers instead of one, normally wouldn't rate on the Uratha's radar except some of the lyrics seem to be in first tongue and a pack you know in LA says that one of the record producers involved is a member of that, cult the Umaraha'e that has been causing so much trouble lately. Add in the fact that teenage Uratha who perform the dance seem to be going mad and mutating into hideous forms make it obvious that the hit song "Black Spiral" needs to be suppressed before it creates a bigger problem.

                                                        147) the movie industry has recently moved into the protectorate's area in a big way, as a result of tax credits the regional government put in place. As a result, the local spirit politics have been disrupted by the arrival representatives of Ilthum related to Hollywood who have overthrown the old spirit court and established the Court of the American Dream Factory. This normally wouldn't be a problem, the protectorate found that the old spirit court was... frustrating, to the least. The problem comes from the fact that the three humans that have the biggest stake in this, a studio executive, a producer and a Chinese diplomat, are well known for being behind some of Hollywood's worst trends (read any of cracked.com's articles on Hollywood business practices and Chinese influence over the industry for ideas). The result is that the hisil has become a commercialized mockery of itself, with the last straw being when the local Cahalith started getting ill-fitting product placements in their prophetic dreams.

                                                        Looks like someone's going to have to save the shadow, but how is the protectorate supposed to take on an entire industry and a foreign government?
