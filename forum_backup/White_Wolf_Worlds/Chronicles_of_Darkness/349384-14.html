                The Krampus

                For this write up, I am using the Horror rules from Chronicles of Darkness to present a version of the Krampus.
                The creature known as “The Krampus” is the shadow of St. Nicholas, inspiring dread and dealing punishment to the wicked. To most, he takes the form of Mr. Peter Black, the esteemed business partner of Mr. Nicholas. But during the dark weeks surrounding the winter solstice, he takes his true form; a shaggy, horned, goat legged horror. As the Krampus he leaves cryptic warnings and bizarre “gifts” as a reminder that misbehavior will be punished. For the worst offenders, he claims their souls, dragging them in chains to a place that can only be called “Hell”.

                The Krampus
                Virtue: Just; Vice: Cruel
                Aspiration: Punish the Wicked, Inspire Virtue through Terror

                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 5, Resolve 5
                Physical Attributes: Strength 7, Dexterity 5, Stamina 5
                Social Attributes: Presence 6, Manipulation 4, Composure 5
                Skills: Athletics 4, Brawl 4, Crafts 2 (Gifts), Empathy 4(Guilt), Intimidation 6, Larceny 4, Occult 4(Folklore), Stealth 5, Subterfuge 4, Survival 4, Weaponry 4 (Chains)
                Merits: Danger Sense, Eidetic Memory, Indomitable, Language (All), Lightning Reflexes 2, Style: Parkour 5, Striking Looks 2(Nightmarish)
                Potency: 8
                Willpower: 18
                Size: 6
                Initiative: 12
                Defense: 9
                Speed: 18 (Flight 23)
                Health: 11
                Powers of the Krampus: Hunters Senses (Wickedness), Immortal, Madness and Terror, Know Soul, Soul Thief, Surprise Entrance, Vanish*

                Vanish: The Krampus can disappear in the blink of an eye, leaving behind bitter memories and harsh lessons.

                        Doctor Steel is prime inspirational material for Genius: The Transgression. He started out as a Grimm, but soon became much more like a Hoffnung. What is interesting is that he's not truly crazy: he feigns an over the top insanity as part of his persona. Despite this, he really despises the current state of the world and wants to take over it to build an utopia. And his songs are damn catchy, to boot. He makes me want to run a Genius game where the PCs and NPCs are Internet celebrities who fight each other for both funding and viewership, all while presenting their more direct conflicts as part of a show.

                        On a much darker note, we have Salad Fingers (warning: disturbing content). He's probably a changeling with Clarity 0 who lives deep inside a mostly deserted part of the Hedge. Or maybe he's a mage with Wisdom 0, and some or all of those random, creepy things that happen around him are actually caused by his out of control spellcasting.

                                I am trying out the new Horror rules from CofD, so I did a quick write up of "The Blob" from the 1958 classic of the same name.

                                The Blob

                                Best At: 10 (Consuming Flesh, Oozing through Obstacles)
                                Worst At: 2 (Moving Quickly, Thinking)
                                Aspiration: Consume all Flesh
                                Willpower/Scene: 6
                                Initiative: 5
                                Defense: 2
                                Speed: 7
                                Size: 5
                                Health: 12
                                Banes: Cold
                                Dread Powers: Consume*, Fluid Form*, Natural Weapons (Digestive Ooze 1), Regenerate 1, Surprise Entrance, Unbreakable, Wall Climb

                                Consume: The Blob receives a +3 modifier to grapple checks, flowing over its target in a wave of acidic ooze. If the Blob is successful, it assaults its captive with digestive acids, applying successes on its roll to establish a grapple as lethal damage. It also applies its natural weapon when using the Damage maneuver while in a Grapple.The Blob may regain 1 point of Willpower per point of damage inflicted, heal one point of Health or gain 1 point of Size.

                                Fluid Form: The Blob can liquefy itself into a fluid by spending 1Willpower. This lasts until the end of the scene, during which time it can flow down drains, under doors, or immerse itself into another body of liquid and swim through it at twice its normal Speed.
