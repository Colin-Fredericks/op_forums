    These are some groups/factions that I thought of for the chronicle in question, they are just the thoughts of a Brainstorm so forgive me for the simplicity, I would like a second opinion to make sure that no idea was too incoherent and if anyone has any ideas too I am grateful.

    Private company: Basically the same idea that I mentioned at the beginning of a private company that captures and studies supernaturals to use, study and (possibly) replicate their abilities, however I decided to expand this idea a little; while part of the company, the most mundane part, only thinks about the profit of the venture, the real bosses would be other supernaturals who have other objectives, among the options I thought:

    - The owner of the company is actually a kindred, or a group of them, who hope through research to find a way to get rid of their weaknesses, such as their curse or even vulnerability to the sun, if this is not possible, they could still use them as tools against their enemies;

    - The company would be a tool of the God Machine that intends to use other monsters to exert its control or sees them as anomalies that must be neutralized. My doubt about this scenario is that I don't really know how GM reasons, so I don't know if this would be a typical attitude of the system. In the second case, I could alternatively use an angel or fallen one who is acting independently and uses this group to study (or erase) other monsters as he believes they are GM creations or errors;

    - This one is quite forced, but it can be used, the owner of the company could be someone who saw the future (an arisen or a mage) and is doing everything to prevent some terrible event from happening, like a possible apocalypse, so he founded the company and looks to the supernatural for any way to acquire power, alternatively he could just be a madman who believes he has seen the future, but is just delusional. I know this is really weird, but I needed to comment.

    Carthano Family (Don't even ask where I got this name): Basically it's a mafia family that uses the undead and ghosts in their business, basically the Giovanni from WOD, but with much less detail. In this case, a large part of the group would be made up of Ghost Eaters and necromancers. They would be a minor faction and just an occasional headache, nothing too complicated and could be used as a hook for the Sin-eaters if someone wants a character like that. Now if they were to have a more impactful role, I thought the head of the family could be a vampire, a arisen, some kind of immortal, basically someone very old who has been in this business for a long time and uses someone as a puppet to command the operations without him needing to show himself publicly, or in sunlight in the case of the vampire.

    Beast Corp's: It's a provisional name, basically it's a group of devoured that operate with other factions or privately and use their skills in exchange for money and favors, but that's just superficially, internally this group is a Tyrany, every devoured in the city ends up being forced to join, once inside the group they get money and to a certain extent guaranteed 'food', as long as they follow the rules. The leader of the group is an incarnate beast who strongly believes that the Dark Mother's children are superior creatures to other monsters and that they eventually will be at the top of the food chain, he goes so far as to massacre any group that is forming, especially changelings, believing that they are the most vulnerable, or Promethians, as he finds them shameful, but he does not attack larger or already well-established groups, as he knows that shouldn't be starting fights with everyone. It is also because of this group that heroes aren't a problem in the city, they quickly are killed.​

                        There are a lot of ways to do it, and a large part of that depends on how you want your players to feel.

                        Big Damn Heroes: I think tying the Deviant's conspiracy to the Uratha's enemies is an easy way to tie those two together. For the mummy I would probably have the Judge's be interested in what the conspiracy was doing and the Mummy PC is supposed to stop them, but perhaps avoid killing certain, key individuals.

                        Little Fish: An alternate way of doing it is have the PCs all in one specific location when something big and bad happens and they get caught up in it. Over time they gradually get to realize they were all manipulated into being at the location by either a person, an organization, or even the God Machine and they're all just puppets.

                                        Okay, the final group looked like this: Two Uratha, two Beasts, a mummy, a sin-eater, and a deviant who was once a mage. As for what makes everyone form a gang (?), for that I'll need to explain a little about the state of the city and let me warn you that I didn't fully understand ST's intention with this, so I'm sorry if there is any inconsistency.

                                        In the city in question where the chronicle takes place there is an abnormally high number of supernatural creatures, but those who establish the greatest control are the kindred who control most of the high positions in gorvement and in high places, followed by the werewolves who have a good portion of the city From what I understand there were several packs and they operated almost like gangs. This all changed when a local company, Biovolt, was acquired by an anonymous buyer and it sundenly became interested in the supernatural. Both Kindred and Uratha soon noticed this and each decided to prepare their own countermeasures, but Biovolt was faster and carried out a series of successful surprise attacks, on shelters and meeting places, all in the space of two or three days. A faction of beasts was formed due to what happened and occupied a good part of the other groups' former hunting areas. This situation was only so favorable to their interests because they managed to make an agreement with Biovolt quietly.
                                        The end result of these attacks was that the werewolves lost many of their number and were forced into hiding and many of the survivors are now wanted criminals, but not all of then. As for the kindred, they practically disappeared from sight, many of their hiding places were discovered, minions killed or captured, but the truth is that some remain in the city just waiting for the dust to settle or looking for a chance to escape.

                                        Returning to the pack, the two uratha belonged to one of the packs that were attacked by Biovolt, in their case, their pack was called for a meeting from another packs, as they arrived late for reasons not yet specified, they arrived after end of the attack, so they avoided the worst, but they weren't going to last long without the protection of a pack, without others of their species they could turn to. They got in touch with Castro, the Arisen.

                                        Castro had some followers working at Biovolt, but they disappeared after its acquisition, this was around the time he awakened again in this era, he suspects it was the work of another Arisen, as the purchase of the company doesn't fit with his memories and so he decides to investigate, but he cannot count on his minions for certain tasks and even he would have problems alone considering the kind of firepower that Biovolt has at its disposal now, so he sought help from unlikely places, with the Uratha and in exchange for temporary shelter he wants their help and that of others.

                                        Jack, the sin-eater, is a nomad, but he came to the city after his aunt Nonna who was also a sin-eater and part of a krewe, but when he arrives, everyone disappears and whatall suggests is that they were captured and the pack of two Stooges had a protection agreement with the krewe that they seem to have ignored for reasons they refuse to talk about and now Jack is demanding their help and that's why he joined the party and also because he needs a place to stay and Castro has money. An extra detail Nonna and the uratha's pack leader were lovers. I know nothing about the ghost.

                                        Now the beasts, one of them is a ghoul from a kindred who needs to isolate himself and the beast is now his eyes and ears in the city and also the one who brings him food. The other is called Bea and joins the group allegedly for protection, but in reality she is being intimidated by the beast faction to spy on Castro, and she is also the ex-gf of one of the uratha.

                                        The deviant was once a mage, but he was transformed and he doesn't know how because he lost his memory of the last two weeks and woke up inside a luxury hotel with a man in a coma next to him and all this in the middle of a shootout that practically destroyed an entire floor (Yeah, this is crazy). He ran away and ended up bumping into the group, they didn't explain to me how, but Castro decided to take him in as he could be a clue about what was happening inside Biovolt. Some more details are that he was suspected of having participated in the hotel shooting; The ghouled beast already knows him from a meeting that he had with his master during the period of amnesia, but he keeps it a secret and the man in a coma in the hotel room was part of Nonna's krewe, meaning it's a very delicate situation.

                                        This is how the group ended up, it seems like a decent prelude or the beginning of a parody of a Mexican soap opera and a generic South American gang film, usually Colombian for some reason.​
