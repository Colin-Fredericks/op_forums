    Going to be running my first mortal World of Darkness game and I'm excited!!

    01-12-2017, 09:56 AM
    So here's the back story,

    I currently have a group I run where we play in a Vampire: The Masquerade game which used to meet either every 2 or 3 weeks online. Two are in the US and two are out. Also two of the players have new jobs, so basically scheduling now leads us to playing roughly once a month. I'm happy I still can run the game, but I have been going nuts in the break.

    One thing we started recently is taking a break from the overall game "Vampire: The Masquerade - Twin Cities by Night" and have little mini side games that last 3 or so sessions. Our first one is actually this coming Sunday, and will be a Sabbat game called "Vampire: The Masquerade: Juarez on Fire" which takes place in 1942 and will have slight references to the Twin Cities by Night game.

    So one mini game I thought about running was a mortal game that takes place in Bismarck, North Dakota in 1987 and plays off the Satanic Panic conspiracy craze that was all the fad then. The thing that frustrated me most is that, while there are books dealing with playing mortals in OWOD, it just came off as boring.

    So someone who plays in my VtM game kept telling me about the NWOD game "The World of Darkness". Of course I have heard of all the NWOD games, but honestly never played one. He spent a couple of hours telling me about the rule set and I was friggin sold. Now I am just waiting for the damn book to get in the mail (we will be playing 1st ed since that seemed to fit more what I wanted). The system just sounds rich and what I need. Mind you there will still be references to mortal stuff in TCBN but I will never run a "cross over game". I just like throwing Easter Eggs in.

    Here is the information for the game I gave to the players, any feedback would be appreciated.

    Okay gents,

    After bouncing some ideas off of some of you and hashing some of the concepts I wanted for the game here is the “official” statement on what the game will be, what the theme is, and what my goals are. Of course the main goal is to tell an awesome story with you guys that you will enjoy playing in.

    Game Name: World of Darkness: The Ultimate Evil

    Game: The World of Darkness 1st Edition from the New World of Darkness

    How often we will play: Whenever there is a large break between established games. IE a one month break between sessions. Number of Players: Who ever can play at the time. In no way is this mandatory and if this game doesn’t interest you please don’t feel pressure to play. I will understand. If a session is 1 or 2 players I would still run the game. That will be explained in the concept for the game.

    Concept: This is a game that will have a horror/conspiracy theme where the players will be playing mortal characters. This will not be as railroaded as Twin Cities by Night, or even the Juarez on Fire game will be. There will be a plot, but for example if your character wanted to go to a country/western bar because shit is becoming too much for him to handle and he needs to decompress, we can play the scene and see where it goes.

    Themes: This will be a horror/conspiracy game that will deal with the subject of dealing with an incident from childhood that has impacts on adulthood. I’ll give some examples later on.

    Character types: Mortal character in their early 20s from Bismarck, North Dakota. You can get creative. Want to play a car mechanic who has relationship issues and anger problems, lets run with it. A middle class father of 4 working a 9-5, sure thing!

    Story Background – In 1977 your characters (who are between 12-14 at this time) are attending a summer youth program for troubled kids. Your childhood could be troubled in the fact that you come from a poor family and have gotten in trouble for petty crimes, or you come from a wealthy family and just don’t want to listen to your parents, we can get creative.

    In this summer program there was an 18 year old girl who worked with a group of you (maybe up to 10 kids) whose name was Amanda Porter. She was a very beautiful blond girl with blue eyes who was very caring to all of you. She was a devout Christian, but never came off as preachy or judgmental. She realized that you all had your own needs, and wanted to help you conquer them. She had some form of impact on each of you (we can get into details when doing background). While to most of the workers in this program it was just a summer job dealing with brats, she cared for all of you like siblings.

    When the summer started drawing to an end, she married her high school sweet heart who was enrolled to go to school in Stanford. When there were two weeks left to the program, and before school started again, you guys witnessed an incident involving Amanda. One day you guys stayed after the program’s day ended to help Amanda clean up and noticed as she was walking to her care afterwards a group of three people come out of a park car and confront her. It was two males, and a female. They were screaming and cursing at Amanda. She looked scared but tried to hold her ground. The confrontation got pretty intense until one of them noticed the group of you guys on your bikes watching it. They quickly got back in their car and drove away when they saw you.

    A week later, Amanda’s parents through a going away BBQ and invited you all to be guests there along with others. While at the BBQ one of you noticed that one of the guys lived on the same block like 6 houses down on the opposite end of the street. Being the upstanding kids you were (/sarcasm lol) someone had an idea to lie to your parents about staying over at someone’s house but instead camp out in a field that older kids often did so they could drink beer etc etc. That evening someone had an idea to go creep on the guy’s house who confronted Amanda and maybe do some good ole vandalism on it to teach him a lesson. Instead of vandalism you guys witnessed something that to this day you can’t quiet remember.

    Two months later, Amanda was found dead in a church on the campus of Stanford, raped and murdered. The last time you guys were together as a group was at her funeral. Her death had an impact on your lives at the time, but that thing you witnessed….whatever it was….soon became a faded memory. Her death remained unsolved, and eventually became a cold case.

    10 years later, in 1987 you all are confronted by a journalist from New York City. He tells you the reason he wants to talk to you is Amada’s mother told him about how before she moved to California she got in an argument with a group of people, and how Amanda told her mom the only reason the argument didn’t elevate even more was because they saw the group of you standing there.

    This reporter wants to know what you remember of the group, if anything, and while he is at it, he is willing to offer good money to you to help be a consultant in a series he plans to write. You get the impression that while he is a legit journalist, he may be into some weird ideas about conspiracies and stuff.

    Random Notes: So I want this game to be not only about horror/conspiracies, but also about character reflection and discovery. As the game progresses some character may start remember some details clear, while others may not at all. Imagine a scene like this….

    Character 1, 2 and 3 are sitting in a cheap booth in a dive bar in Bismarck, North Dakota. The reporter slide a mug shot of someone on the table and asks the characters if they recognize him. Character 1 doesn’t know this guy from a hole in the wall ( after I tell him), character 2 vaguely recognizes him (after I tell him her does) and is curious, but character 3 (after I tell him the fact sets off fears, and he remember in flashes this face, and *insert something happening here) recognizes the face and is swarmed with a memory long forced out of his mind. The player of character 3 says his character starts to sweat and a look of fear over comes his face.

    Character 1’s motivation would probably to just get this cash and help find out who this dude is, character 2’s may be to find out how he knows this guy, and 3’s could be to warn everyone, or to keep it to himself and find out who this guys is. BTW this story will deal with real life events (Amanda Porter is sadly based of a death of a Bismarck resident in 1974 that is still unsolved) and conspiracies people to this day believe that take place in the area. To be honest, you characters could very well maybe travel out of state too.

    Any questions please ask!

God Machine Chronicles ( one half of 2E corebook ) is interesting if you like concept quasi-Matrix intelligence running your CoD setting.                               I only pointing to the fact that 2E rules were written not only because books need to be sold. People pointed rules inconsistency in 1E rules all over, now known Ephemeral Beings rules as being crown jewel of this thing - like, I love ghosts stories ( half of my CoD games starts with ghosts, or ghost-like events ) but running them on 1E was... maybe not a nightmare, but surely a mess. In 2E games it's almost poetry with shades of once living.

                              I run 1E games for almost a decade ( had a 2-3 years pass then ) and I can tell you ST overburden is now much lower in 2E as you do not need to think for each and every edge case scenario. ( Those things still creeps out from time to time, but it's really small problem now. )

                              2E rules are based on PLAYERS agency - by the centered mechanics of Aspirations and precisely Conditions - first are characters motivations that if PC fulfills they get XP for, second are various mechanical and narrative effects, but if you role play them or risk with them - you get XP.

                              But if you like to run with much more 'ST creative freedom', or just 'let's roll with everything simpler' - 1E rules will be good for you.

                              The only real problem of 1E mortals rules are Morality rules that makes every players struggle black or white - OR everything illogical mess if your games go in to grey areas. Whatever to be said, Integrity, as concept, has much more sense for 'monster facing mortals' games. But to implement Integrity you would need to take at least few Conditions as 1E derangements... Now that I think about it, it's still much logical connection than connecting Judeo-Christian Morality with insanity in the 1E by corebook. And Integrity, from it's fundament, is character defined, not thrown over to you, as universal to anyone on the world ( well, mostly - killing is still universal Breaking Point for any character in system ).
