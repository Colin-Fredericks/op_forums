                                    * Eshmaki Predator

                                    * Youngest child with one or two MUCH OLDER siblings -- like a fifteen and eleven year age gap, so they were leaving home just as he was getting old enough to miss their absence. Grew up mostly alone in a great big house with the occasional babysitter, latchkey, always hated being alone because SCARY AND NEVER REALLY ALONE BECAUSE *SOMETHING* WAS THERE THAT NOBODY ELSE COULD/WOULD SEE.

                                    * Became gregarious and outgoing as he got older so he would *have* to be alone. ENFP? Possibly. Got way into both athletic and artistic pursuits to fill pretty much every moment with some sort of activity that would keep his fears at bay.

                                    * Got waaaaaaaaaaay into backpacking/hiking/mountaineering/adventure sports in college/early adulthood and on one such expedition with a group of friends got caught in a bad situation -- I'm thinking an avalache/rockslide/unexpected blizzard -- something that forced him to literally or metaphorically "cut the rope" that someone else could survive and in the ensuing spiritual katabasis finally met the SCARY THING THAT WAS STALKING HIM HIS WHOLE LIFE and BECAME IT.

                                    * Back and forth about whether or not his friends/family think he's dead. "Thinks he's dead" means he essentially had to adopt a new identity. "Came back *different*" means he's still got a friends/family support network -- i.e., access to greater resources -- and people he's likely walled off a part of his new existence from, generally for their own protection.

                                    * In any case, he came back *different.* Still artistic and athletic but less outgoing, more reserved, and his art took a generally darker cast than it had thus far, his predatory nature starting to shine through in a manner that makes spirit hunting in the Primordial Realms of those souls effected by his imagery and music always a basic possibility.

                                    * Owns a home on Marrowstone Island, which is his primary residence and the location of his home studio for more traditional artistic pursuits -- multiple physical media and plays multiple instruments -- as well as having a habit of leaving spontaneously arranged artistic displays scattered all over the island. His neighbors would probably be less charmed if they realized their true purpose. He maintains a "hunting camp" in the Picket Range of the high North Cascades that is exactly what it sounds like -- where he goes when he needs to feed on flesh and blood. He mostly tries to confine these predations to other (nonhuman) predators, though there have been a few unlucky backpackers whose bones he was forced to put to artistic uses over the years.

                                                This is pretty similar to one of Anteros's ideas, but I wanted to share it anyway. It's loosely inspired by a friend of mine, though he's actually mostly a nice guy. :P

                                                A serpent broods among the kelp beds and broken ships off of San Francisco's Ocean Beach. Some say that the cold of the arctic currents and chilling fog of his home has seeped into this Makara Tyrant's soul, but the truth is that the cold is simply part of his nature. Few passions stir his steady heart, and his ice-blue eyes reveal no hint of emotion beyond whatever mask he chooses to present.

                                                The serpent lurks within several of the disparate subcultures that make up the City's kink scene, and seems to have an uncanny ability to attract a certain type of prey. Those who come to him are eager to feed his hunger. They crave debasement, draw pleasure from being bound and silenced, and thrill at the sensation of true helplessness that he grants them. He feasts at the threshold where terror meets ecstasy, and always leaves his meals begging for more.

                                                The serpent never does any permanent, physical harm to those from which he feeds - at least, not on purpose. Indeed, he abhors the sight of blood, and he views those Beasts who indulge in more grisly feasts with distaste. When push comes to shove he prefers to dispose of his enemies by smothering - either drowning them or squeezing the life from their bodies like a monstrous python.

                                                On the other hand, the serpent has little awareness or care for the emotional wounds he leaves in his wake. He pushes those who provide his meals to the very limits of their comfort zone, and sometimes well beyond, and once their willing submission has ceased to satisfy his hunger, he abandons them like an apple core or the wrapper of a candy bar.

                                                            A Mummy/Beast crossover character idea:

                                                            Makara Predator, lives two lives: one as a guide and survivalist in the deserts of Africa. She knows how to get from town to town safely and quickly, and how to read the moods and weather shifts.

                                                            Second life is as a high priestess/recruiter for a cult. Her Prey in this case is people who seem like they'd make good cultists, and she takes them out into the wastes and harries them towards the Tomb. If they die, clearly they weren't actually cut ou; if they make it, then they get a chance to become a cultist. Either way, she gets what she needs.

                                                            "Nihhina kalekal-zidu kal masun, kal manudanadu. Nihhina kalekal-zidu nukal shaghu-desasudu — nihhina kalekal-zidu kal innu-desasudu udhkal samm." Arthur Ashe
