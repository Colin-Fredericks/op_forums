            I think a key thing about the portrayal of Dracula in the novel is that the influence of his life as a statesman under duress gives him a mind set towards attempting a more sophisticated approach to being a vampire.
            There's a "simpler" reason: the novel's Dracula is a sorcerer who studied in Scholomance. The novel is unclear about which of the many feats he display is due to vampiric nature or to his magical knowledge.

            Another theme common to both the Ordo, the Tzimisce and Vlad Tepes III is sheer audacity. It is a common way to interpret the way Dracula (the actual Voivode) defied both the Turkish and the other Voivodes and Walachian nobles. The Ordo defies nature (in the broader sense) itself, refusing to accept the limits of the Curse no matter what caused it. The Tzimisce defy the proper form of living beings.

            But the Ordo is not the Tzimisce. Sure the Path of Metamorphosis taught the Tzimisce to look for Transcendence, but the philosophy is based in constant change and method introspective. The Ordo looks everywhere, applies a rational, empirical approach, and seek to crystallize the findings, through Coils, Mysteries, Scales. They share and discuss results (to an extent), their take assumes Transcendence as a goal to be codified through collective work, a goal for the institution and its methods. Metamorphosis is a spiritual search for personal enlightenment.

                    Originally posted by DarkKing View Post
                    While the core mechanics may have presented the idea that you want to try remaining human, not all of the covenants seemed to agree with that sentiment.
                    That's a misunderstanding.

                    Humanity in VtR doesn't measure proximity to mortals. It measures your ability to feel the character of one.

                    It's not about nature, but about comprehension. The analogy to a psychopath fits, though it may need refinement:

                    Empathy is the human capacity to recognize in others their own mental process. Feeling fear or anger is an emotion. Recognizing that other people feel fear or anger and mentally picture what it's like is empathy.

                    Autistics have a hard time processing Empathy, frequently get it wrong, but the notion is there. Psychopaths don't, or has it reduced. Not get it wrong, don't get the very idea except the most abstract way. They observe people behave, and so can learn their reactions, but have no idea of how their perspective could be, even imagined.

                    So Humanity here is your Empathy. A vampire may know human behavior abstractly with skill dots, but Humanity measures their ability to mentally picture the mindset of a mortal.

                    Your feelings of being close to humans are just a your thoughts problem. You can have very low Humanity and still think yourself not really that different, that's philosophy. Or feel you like them as family, that's sentiment. But can you imagine how it feels to be like them? That's Humanity.

                                                Originally posted by DarkKing View Post
                                                That kind of sounds like it overlaps with Autarkis, though.
                                                Only as much as political disagreement overlap with apolitical hermits.

                                                You're an Anarch when you're a part of Kindred society and think the Prince is doing a shitty job at running it. You have opinions and want to see political change.

                                                It's important to observe that the game doesn't originally put Anarchs as opposed to the Cam, only to the actual current Prince. Most Anarchs are expected to be Camarilla in practice, very few really proposing a strong departure. After all, the one Anarch movement that did so is the Sabbat, whose common ground isn't really the religion, but the position against the Traditions and power of Elders.

                                                Autarkis don't give a fuck, to society, politics or princes. They go their merry way to the edge of town and shut themselves, everyone else be damned.

                                                Carthians seek political experimentation. While this means uncommon structures, it also does include at least some Traditions, so many of them would be Camarilla full stop. And it also involves a complete political theory rather than just local disagreement, which is not at all what Anarchs are about.

                                                The Anarch Free States are a demonstration of the distinction: they're what happens when the local Anarchs go overboard because they're not the Carthians or Autarkis.

                                                Were they Carthians, they'd have a plan to build a new system with solid premises. Were they a bunch of Autarkis, it would be an unclaimed area where scattered independent individuals take care of their own business with very little or no contact with other vampires.

                                                Being Anarchs who don't actually defend such unified framework, just share a loose sense of dissatisfaction, they got nothing to work with and straddle between improvising and ignoring the problem while hoping things will hold for at least a few more days at a time.
