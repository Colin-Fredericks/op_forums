    Infinite Macabre: Night Without End

    11-09-2019, 04:31 PM
    Alright, so you might already know I've been working on an space opera shard where Kindred are akin to a parasitic horrors that masquerade in the bodies of anything they can slip into. Since VtR 2e released Guide to the Night, I've been par-TIC-ularly intrigued by the Night Without End section (pg. 26-28), and have used it to help devise a more developed playground for people wanting to explore a interstellar empire controlled by the Kindred.

    Here's the elevator pitch:

    In the year [x], the Allied Earth Directorate (AED) discovered Stygian Gates – one planet-side of Mars, the other orbiting the edge of the Solar System – leading to different sectors of the galaxy. Through the latter Gate, an arc housing many human pioneers (and perhaps a half-dozen kindred) discovered they were millennia in the past, in a quadrant of the galaxy that was far from home. Initial efforts to return home, however, revealed the Gate had gone dormant.
    By the time the Gate was once again active, the AED found not only descendants of the lost settlers, but a sprawling, interstellar empire calling itself the New Terran Dominion (NTD).
    • The only part of Kindred space that visitors are welcomed in is the Elysium quadrant where the SG is located. All the better to lure new blood should they try deeper into the territory.
    • Knowledge of zones with access to Earth, or Kindred as a whole is strictly controlled, with some worlds, or even entire systems being run by a single Prince. This allows for any kind of world the players would want to explore without being labeled as invalid. All however, must either pay tribute to the Dominion, hide their existence, or expect all-out war.
    • A possible explanation for the cainites is that they are a reality plague on “normal” vampires, revealing traces of the parasite in Vitae here there were none before. Another is that the cainites were originally Visitors (Immortals pg. 124) but were weaponized or corrupted in some terrible process.


    Details on the "Cainites":

    • All relevant data biology-wise: http://madnessforums.com/forums/inde...pic,180.0.html
    • May also utilize concepts from the "Night without End" chapter of VtR's Guide to the Night supplement. If so, then the NTD is a government that split from Earth as it became dominated by Cainites.
    • The Steel Paradox from the Ascendancy chapter of Guide to the Night is in effect, preventing cainites from implementing augmentations, even if their hosts were embraced with them.
    • Khanwulf, Dr. - "The parasite actually extends across a range of T-dimensions, such that the scalar energies it creates and physical features facilitated by it are actually folded into such dimensions (an effectively limitless number of them) awaiting "merging" with prime reality under the influence of life-energy generally contained within the medium of blood."

          Lemme know if you guys have ideas, suggestions or considerations about the setting.

          Also! I wrote some sciency-sturf for how ppl explain Disciplines and Devotions! Treating them like mutagens introduced into the host body by the Cainite, perhaps over a period of time or heavy use of said-Disciplines. I like the idea that things like teleportation or telepathy can be rationalized by quantum entanglement and dimensional phasing -- but lemme know how to explain or flesh-out the details, since I'm clearly not a science-major! ^_^;

          Scientific Notes – Disciplines

          Animalism
          1 – After examining the subject among animals within a number of environs, the designated Discipline has shown the ability to communicate and command multicellular lifeforms. The host does not secrete pheromones, nor require physical contact with a given animal. The cainite, instead, has developed a form of mutagen that, when released, augments the host’s vocal cords. To the average human, nothing has changed, but a secondary, subtler pitch emanates as the host speaks, somehow compelling obedience in the targeted animal. Current theories are as follows: the audio frequency directly influences the brain by way of the audial cortex, with additional sensory information, such as body-language, natural scent, and memory offering both the animal and subject a better understanding of what is expected of it – though it is clear only sound is necessary to command the target. This does not make the animal capable of complex commands, but somehow the host is able to interpret said-animal’s actions in such a way that basic information can be relied. Attempts to replicate the results with recorded audio transcripts have failed. Subject’s attempt to command animals through electronic devices such as phones, video screens or 3D holograms have also failed.

          2 – Normally, xeno-cainites are only capable of embracing other members of its host’s species, or, theoretically, those with close enough similarity to said-species. The subject here has managed to bypass this limitation. Like with a human, the animal is fed Vitae laced with microscopic, larval versions of the xeno-cainite. From there these specialized larvae feed on the synthesized compounds within the liquid before burrowing deeper into the animal’s body. Perhaps due to the genetic material they acquire from the Vitae, the larvae are forced to improvise on the non-human host, which proceeds to form a “cytopathic reanimation” of the corpse; forming complex neural structures enabling re-animation of damaged cells and restoration of lower level vitals. Alongside adapting the cadaver till capable of subsisting solely on a carnivorous diet, despite requiring none of the requirements necessary to live. The larvae are able to maintain the host for a number of nights dependent on said-creature’s physiology and the richness of the cainite’s Vitae, though later transfusions fail to perpetuate the animation. The cainite is also capable of communicating with the subject, regardless of physical barriers. One theory suggests the cainite is subconsciously able to form a genetic-based quantum entanglement between the two, provided both are still functionally operating. More tests are needed.

          3 – The subject has proven capable of storing the entire genetic sequence of any predatory species its fed upon. The xeno-cainite utilizes them as a template for synthesizing a unique, pheromonal psychosis tailored to specifically attract species correlating with said-genetic sequence – once the cainite triggers the synthesis and spills its own blood. Somehow, the pheromone is able to operate even in environments were olfactory senses are suppressed or obstructed. Non-predatory or carnivorous animals show the exact opposite reaction. Subject has also been able to synthesize a cocktail of general but dangerously effective pheromones to draw any and all predatory species in range to attack whatever its laced Vitae has come into contact with. In some instances, and in either or both uses of pheromone, the animals prove subservient to the subject’s will, perhaps a minute adaptation of Phase 2 of the Discipline. Surprisingly, once exposed to UV radiation, these effects wear off instantaneously as the Vitae disintegrates.

          4 – Contagion protocols were activated after realizing the subject had released Vitae-laced bacteria into the test chamber. This pathogen, capable of surviving in any environment outside of extreme heat and direct UV radiation, requires being inhaled by animal lifeform – human or otherwise – within range, or in direct contact with the cainite’s Vitae after the subject spilled it. Those who’ve imbibed the subject’s vitae appear far more susceptible as well, perhaps energizing larvae in the bloodstream with a signal to amplify the Discipline’s effects.
          Any animals unable to resist go into a feral rage, the bacteria manipulating brain chemistry regardless of whether or not it operates off Oxygen. From the cerebral arteries, the bacteria proceeds to cause enough of an imbalance to induce a reduction in cognitive function, causing irrationality and paranoia – while neuropathic action in the frontal lobe compounds the symptom into severe emotional and behavioural abnormalities. The process even goes so far as to induce poly-necrophagia or outright cannibalism in the victim upon killing anything during the episode.

          5 – The final phase of the Discipline has been met. The subject instinctively deposits Vitae to surround a location. Following similar patterns found in previous phases, the Vitae released is far more potent, and somehow avoids destruction by UVR. Hypothesis: Vitae is able to sustain itself on gross matter, breaking it down into basic components that it can then use to perpetuate the effects of the Discipline. Regardless, the Vitae deposits create a boundary that influences all animals contained within the perimeter, releasing the cainite-bacteria into the air no matter how far deep inside the territory the subject is. Again, theories of quantum-entanglement between the Cainite and Vitae would help to explain how this is possible, regardless of distance or obstruction.

          Auspex
          1 – Subject’s nutrient flow to the occipital and temporal lobes has increased, enhancing their sight and hearing. At this stage of the Discipline, they prove able to trigger a bolstering effect to their cerebral cortex, specifically enabling them to processes sensory information at record-breaking speeds. Everything from body-language to apparel to environmental minutiae - to even one’s spatial positioning formulate a pattern that the subject knows on a subconscious level, though continuous usage of the Discipline appears strenuous.

          2 – Honing the abilities found in the Discipline’s phase one, the xeno-cainite has adapted to focus its perception on a singular target for maximum effect, subconsciously acquiring and processing information at an approximate of 50 femtoseconds on average. When asked for further details, the subject could not recall anything save the hallucinatory visions and information that they ordinarily could observe, suggesting that once the vision is projected - and key information acquired, all other factoids is rendered as junk data and forgotten. Efforts to resurface said-data or locate via brain scans turn up nothing. It's as if the data was never there to begin with.

          3 – In the third phase of the Discipline’s development, the subject’s subconscious processing of sensory information has evolved from purely organic, living targets to inert matter and environmental tells within their immediate surroundings. Trillions of minute details are compiled on the focus of the discipline and processed at the same approximate speed as with the previous phase. And, much like said-phase, the subject fails to recall such calculations beyond the results and observations any ordinary human could make note of. One particular abnormality is the kindred’s inability to repeat the process on a target they have already analyzed within a short period of time. Rationales for this are ongoing, and will require further study to deduce.

          Celerity
          1-5 – Nutrient flow to the parietal lobe increased, improving movement and coordination. Combined with augmented neural pathways and specialized, quick-firing muscle fibers, the subject has the capacity to dodge bullets if given forewarning.
          However, when fully activated, the subject’s use of this Discipline moves at such speeds impossible for even other kindred to perceive. At first -

          Vigor

Surges of contractile proteins combined with constant micro trauma to increase muscle mass that, while dormant, make the host appear no different than usual, perhaps with subtle tonnage or bulk. Not the case when fully triggered. In either case, subject proves able to overpower non-augmented humans with minimal effort. Further development causes an exponential increase in host muscle mass, brought on by steroidal and anabolic boosts synthesized by the xeno-cainite, without any noticeable side-effects to the host’s brain-chemistry. Somehow, the parasite is able to compress and fold the host’s musculature in on itself to maintain said-host’s natural shape – with 20% chance of minor bulking found across all subjects to date, as of this recording.

                  Ghouls
                  The process by which ghouls are made is found in the Vitae they ingest. The compound’s “sweetness” hides a number of microscopic larvae that – while cainite in origin – are incapable of growing large enough to kill or convert the host to full vampire. Utilizing the material acquired from the Vitae, as well as minute amounts of the host’s own blood, these symbiotes enable the benefits ghouldom espouses. However, because of their nature, the larvae also cause a chemical change in the host, one that slackens said-host’s ability to resist the parent cainite. It takes no more than three doses of Vitae to grip the host in pure adoration towards the cainite, though until they reach this level of strength, larvae from other cainites are known to hunt down and kill rival symbiotes the ghoul acquires from feeding off vitae.

                  In the first "feeding", surges of testosterone and estrogone hijack the imbiber’s emotions and create a desire towards the Cainite. In the second, surges of Dopamine and Serotonin compel the subject to constantly think about the Cainite, and triggers intense rushes of pleasure. By the third, overdoses of oxytocin and vasopressin force the subject into a permanent state of worship / acceptance - treating the Cainite as if suffering from Celebrity Worship syndrome or outright erotomania.


                  Embrace
                  On record, there are 2 ways by which Cainites procreate, though only one is deliberate. The first is by way of the Embrace, a term […]. The cainite feeds until the would-be Kindred is exsanguinated, wherein the sire’s body begins processing then synthesizing Vitae that is then released back into the corpse’s body. Similar to the process Ghouls go through, the Vitae houses microscopic larvae that swim through the bloodstream. Unlike said-process, these larvae are not sterile, and gather within the cadaver’s heart chambers, wherein the exits are sealed and the larvae gather into a ball before hibernating for one week or less. During this period, the worms feed on the vitae, swelling till they fuse together. The sealing around the exits become attached to the metaphorizing cainite, forming neural lining that subsumes the host’s original nerve endings. Pushing outward to become the familiar tendrils of the parasite species.

                      I also wrote some info for other kindred-esque xenos. Here's the first, though I'd like suggestions how how to better flesh it out... (no pun intended).

                      Penanggal

                      As written in Night Horrors: Wicked Dead pg. 67-73, the Penanggal (plural: Penanggalan), are vampiric entities that can detach their heads from their bodies. In a space-opera setting, they are a bit different.

                      • A Penanggal's natural form is similar to that of a Cainite, in that they are comprised of several powerful tendrils that center on a head. Unlike the Cainite, the Penanggal does not focus on nesting around the heart, but around the brain. Much like an octopus descending upon its prey, the vampire envelopes its mass over the host's head, its tendrils then dig into the soft tissue of the neck as it asserts control over the limbs and digestive track. The penanggal has a mantle that then seals up the holes thus made by its insertion, and become seamless over the course of its assimilation.

                      • The head is not necessary for assimilation to occur. All that is needed is a mostly intact carcass of flesh and bone, the penanggal asserting control by replacing the brain and nervous system. Meanwhile, the host's digestive track is subsumed into one or more of the penanggal's inner tendrils, allowing the parasite to alter the organs size, density, and then hollow out the body's interior. At this point it will attempt to track down and fill itself with large quantities of ethanol or similar such preservative chemicals.

                      • In a strange process requiring said-preservatives, the penanggal who bathes itself and its home therein becomes immune to the harmful effects of UV radiation, so long as they remain firmly inside their shell. Most likely this is due to a secretion of the vampire's, requiring the other chemical compound to take effect. Strangely, the penanggal becomes vulnerable to sunlight etc, should they eject from the shell. Stranger still, they cannot utilize the abilities of Disciplines within their stolen body. It is believed whatever organ allows for the processing of Vitae is either rendered inert or re-purposed to synthesize the compound necessary for the penanggal to resist UV radiation.

                      • Perhaps as a side-effect of dwelling within and feeding off their shell, the "head" of the vampire will gradually over the course of weeks or months begin to shift, resembling features of the host, acting as camouflage for the creature to hunt while remaining hidden amongst the herd.

                              Navy, Army, Special Forces
                              • Ideally, any ship equal to or larger than a frigate is commanded by kindred vetted and blood-bound by a superior within the chain of command. When no longer viable, a ghoul following the same criteria will suffice, but only temporarily. This is, in part, because both ghouls and dhampirs cannot enact vinculum on their subordinates, which is necessary to ensure absolute loyalty in either a small crew, or key personnel within a larger vessel.
                              • In all branches of the Dominion military, ghouls, dhampirs and kindred are given ranks of command (the lowest being Captain). Only kindred may become generals, admirals, or authorized to lead special forces like revenant and coterie kill-teams.
                              • Incendiary, cybernetic, and UVR-based weapons were banned long ago across the Dominion, with the exception of the military. Even then, these weapons are only given to a select few, either for taking out anti-Dominion kindred, or in the most extreme circumstances. For the common rank and file, ballistic-, sonic- or pressurized air-based firearms are supplied.
                              • Naval ships contain no windows, instead using camera feed to "simulate" the effect on walls, without the risk of sunlight penetrating the hull. Red light is a common feature within these ships, used along with red-tinted eye-ware to mask blood and lower chances of frenzy. Soldiers are given armor made specially for masking the scent of blood - results have varied - and while personnel are each given a rebreather, said-devices are no use for entering areas off-limits to non-kindred, where oxygen can be pulled to suffocate intruders. Those that survive may wish they hadn't.
                              • Because of the logistical nightmare that would occur trying to turn every soldier into a ghoul, only officers are given the vinculum, becoming bound to their immediate superior, who in turn is bound to their immediate superior, all the way up to the top brass. All other staff and personnel are made compliant through Disciplines, charisma and rigorous training. Those that become problems are either “convinced” to be otherwise, or quietly put on the menu.
                              • When their bonded superior is unavailable, all ghouls and blood-bound kindred are given blood-packs disguised as mutagenic serum to be applied via auto-injectors to avoid the risk of civilians or unindoctrinated subordinates from mistaking imbibers of drinking "blood". Kine or unauthorized ghouls caught using another’s blood ration are quickly reprimanded, with repeat offenders being placed on a watchlist. Often, the ghoul whose ration was used is also reprimanded, with threats of being cut-off and corporal punishment being applied where needed.

                                  Government
                                  • Orbiting the Dominion's capital world of Obol, Damocles Station acts as both the seat of governance and trade port. This is in part due to the Stygian Gate's proximity, which compelled the kindred to shore up their defenses after it became active. Any ship exiting the gate is noted by information buoys, which also broadcast a warning for all newcomers to make their intentions known. Whether or not they do so, however, security ships are sent to intercept and board the vessel. After passing inspection and questioning, they are permitted to leave or dock at Damocles. For most, this is as close as they are allowed to venture within the NTD: a massive fortress with all the wonders of its nation on display. All while hiding the truth behind its dark masters.
                                  • To the public, NTD is a parliament-elected monarchy, with the emperor presiding either till death or willingly stepping down. In reality, the emperor is a puppet of a council comprised of nine kindred. Each represents a Covenant sanctioned by those already in power, or with enough leverage to manouvre onto the council (by force, even).
                                  • Traditionally, each Dominion-controlled star system has at least one space station acting as a miniaturize version of Damocles, in that a localized parliamentary system elects a governor to act on behalf of their star system, and so forth. Of course, the reality is that all the power remains in the hands of the Kindred.
                                  • Outside the capital sector, worlds are granted some level of autonomy, provided they accept the Dominion's sovereignty and pay taxes. Each world, however, is ruled by at least one Kindred Prince, who in turn elects an emissary and ambassador to represent their interests off-world. Multiple principalities are commonplace on highly populated worlds, usually forming a Council of Princes that votes on their emissary.
                                  • Covenant Worlds or even whole star system are common enough, though some are hidden from public Way Station directories to avoid unwanted attention. Planets where the truth of Vampirism is open knowledge are heavily monitored, sometimes being quarantined or even destroyed if utterly under the thrall of hostile factions.
                                  • Despite its many flaws, the Dominion boasts some of the greatest advances in medical science. Blood-based forms of disease, including cancers such as Leukemea, lemphoma, and multiple myeloma, are all but eradicated on worlds fully integrated into the NTD. This is not the case with planets on the periphery, especially those Independent or Criminal factions trying to hide.
