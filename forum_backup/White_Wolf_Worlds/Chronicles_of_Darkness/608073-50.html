                                    I love coming up with ideas for inter-splat conflicts, and it was fun coming up with ideas for how various splats might create Deviants. I also kind of feel like some Deviants might feel a special kinship with Vampires, Changelings, Sin-Eaters, and Mummies- they might even think of those beings as other Deviants, just more powerful ones whose means of creation turned out to be repeatable and relatively stable. And you could easily have a Deviant who came from an attempt to create an "artificial" replica of a splat. The thread touched on the idea of an attempt to duplicate the Rite of Return, but you could do it with any splat. A secret Autumn Court project to create their own Changelings, an Idigam that wonders if it's possible to turn humans into werewolves, a guilt-wracked Demiurge who tries to turn himself into a Promethean so he and his creation can be together, a necromancer who experiments with binding ghosts to people in increasing number and power...

                                    A lot of potential for plot and tragedy with a "counterfeit" supernatural being interacting with the genuine article.

Changeling 1e was interesting because you could very easily create True Fae who were, for all intents and purposes, copies of major splat characters.                                         Assuming that it's the way the writers are taking Deviant, I'm expecting to see characters who can easily model Ghouls, or Wolf-blooded, or Stigmatics. Somebody who can push their physical abilities beyond human limits, but needs to have a high iron intake to compensate... somebody who was bonded with a wolf-spirit to try and create a werewolf... somebody given all sorts of biotech by a transhumanist cult.
