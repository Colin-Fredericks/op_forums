        Jump-jets (Angelic Propulsion)
        When one hears the word “angel”, invariably images of radiant, terribly beautiful and horrifying inhuman beings descending from above on great wings come to mind. Unfortunately not all angels of the God-Machine are graced with the ability to fly, but to compensate for this earth-bound servants of the machine are often capable of incredible feats of mobility and agility. Angels with this form ability may not be able to soar among the clouds, but they can rule atop skylines and nimbly move about the cities of man with a fluid grace that more than makes up for it.

        Appearance: The angel possesses several thrusters on it's back and legs, which neatly seal themselves when not in use to prevent dirt and other particulates from clogging them.

        Mechanics: The angel is capable spending a point of Essence in order to quadruple their vertical jumping distance and quintupling their horizontal jumping distance. In addition, should the angel find themselves falling from a great distance they can spend Essence points in order to perform either short bursts or a continuous burn with their Jump-jets to slow their descent, lessening potential damage taken from the fall by one point per Essence expended in this manner to a minimum of 1 point of damage taken from said fall. Finally, this Propulsion can be used to give boosts to unassisted leaps, essentially allowing them to “ jump” while in mid air- essentially allowing them to re-roll a failed jump reflexively, though this safety feature can only be used once per turn.

        Note: This Propulsion can be retained by demons, where its jumping distance modifiers are reduced by one, and is powered through Aether.


        Early Warning System (Angelic Modification)
        Danger hides behind every corner and within every shadow in the World of Darkness, just waiting to reach out and inflict tragedy upon the unsuspecting- especially the unsuspecting. It is the job of many Guardians to be ready for when it does, to stand between the horrors of the night and those they are sent to protect, so the God-Machines grants them the tools needed to prepare for the worst before it truly happens.

        Appearance: The angel's body plays host to a number of early warning devices. The appearance of these devices can be something as artificial as an array of motion sensors to tiny biomechanical creatures that resemble diminutive birds that squawk and move their tiny wings in alarm in response to danger.

        Mechanics: The Early Warning System adds a + 5 to the angel in questions' Initiative score and grants a + 3 to Perception rolls made by the angel. In addition, these sensory devices or creatures alert the angel should a potential enemy attempt to sneak up on or attack the angel from behind, rendering the angel immune to surprise attacks from behind – though supernatural powers that enhance stealth or render the enemy otherwise undetectable are still able to function at a – 2 penalty.

        Note: This ability is also available to demons, and remains unchanged.


        Shield of Faith (Angelic Process)
        The enemies of the Machine are legion, and while on an individual scale few ever are a match for it's servants in direct combat the foes of God are crafty and employ many tools of war to bring the loyal angels low. Fortunately the God-Machine has long since designed ways to defend against such forces, and its greatest warriors possess mighty shields to turn aside the burning metal arrows of the Unchained.

        Appearance: The angel has several metallic studs embedded in its shoulders, chest, forearms, legs, and along its spine. Should a metallic weapon or electrical charge strike the angel a nimbus of golden energy flares into existence for a brief moment as it nullifies the attack.

        Mechanics: The angel possesses a electromagnetic shield generator that renders it completely immune to electricity in any form, be it natural or supernatural, this includes form abilities such as Electromagnetic Pulse and Electric Jolt. In addition, this energy shield provides less impervious, but still mighty defense against weapons and projectiles composed of metal, granting five points of armor against metal melee weapons and complete immunity from firearms whose caliber is smaller than .50- these rounds simply are harmlessly deflected away from the angel's body regardless of the distance of the shooter. Naturally weapons that are not made of metal, that use unorthodox ammunition ( such as the Hod Rounds of Task Force: Valkyrie) or are of a high enough grade of ordinance ( such as the forementioned .50 caliber weapon or something even more powerful; a tank shell for example.) are not impeded by the Shield of Faith.

                                                        ____

                                                        Retroactive Targeting System ( Angelic Technology)
                                                        The God-Machine is omnipresent; it's superstructure ( and Infrastructure) existing throughout the world past, present, and future. Angels are likewise quantumly stable, and a number of Guardians or dedicated hunter-angels are equipped with the hardware to analyze damage inflicted to their structure by previous attacks in order to predict future hostile actions with progressive aptitude- scanning enemy body stances, calculating the impact velocity and trajectory of projectiles, and other more esoteric methods of occult analysis.

                                                        Appearance: The angel possesses a third eye upon their forehead. While the eye can resemble almost any mechanical or biological optic sensor, all of them seem to possess a thousand yard stare, only focusing in on objects should the angel be injured.

                                                        Mechanics: Should the angel suffer a point of damage due to being the target of a hostile character the technology reflexively activates, granting a + 2 bonus to combat rolls against that target, which now benefit from the rote quality. In Addition, this Technology also functions in response to projectile weapons such as firearms; upon being wounded by a successful firearms attack roll the angel is then able to apply it's defense against future firearm attacks by that user, as well as gaining a + 1 to it's defense against said user. Subsequent wounds inflicted by opponents impart an additional + 1 bonus to the angel's defense and combat rolls, capping a + 5 for both modifiers. It should be noted that these bonuses only apply to the opponents who wound the angel ( ex. Jimbob has shot Exampiel twice, thus she has a + 3 to her combat rolls and + 2 to her defense against Jimbob, but she has no bonus modifiers against Jennysue, who has yet to attack her.).

                                                        While this Technology can function against multiple opponents at once, the angel suffers a - 3 to their defense against opponents who have yet to wound the angel. Not only that, as the angel's defensive and offensive systems are so focused on targeting those who are already catalogued in the targeting matrix any damage inflicted is increased by one point ( an attack that does two damage would do three and so on). The trade off for this is once they do land a successful hit, the matrix then locks on to the new opponent as well, and the penalties are replaced with the bonuses described above.

                                                            It took a bit longer, but I finally got around to creating the mind transference Process. I will confess this one was a bit more difficult to write due to it being more suitable to a Numen than a form ability, but I then got inspiration from another one of my favored role playing games and well.. here it is
                                                            ____

                                                            Cortical Stack-trap( Angelic Process)
                                                            Humanity as a whole is obsessed with the concept of transcending their own mortality, in some form or another. Whether it is going to a paradise realm after they die or becoming one with the living universe or something as mundane as achieving immortality through the digitalization of one's consciousness - the desire to persist beyond the end of one's natural life is omnipresent. The God-Machine understands this desire to escape death all too well and has devised methods to exploit it that are legion, this Process being but one of them. With it an angelic servant is able to transfer the mind of a mortal into a cybernetic device born of his own biomechanical flesh, an ideal way to reward faithful servants with the promise of an immortal body.. or a trap for inglorious heretics from which there is nearly no chance for escape.

                                                            Appearance: At the base of the angel's skull is a triangular implant, which can be removed to reveal that the device is a small, biomechanical tetrahedron that pulses with a faint blue-white light.

                                                            Mechanics: This ability is unusual as it relies on the angel to infuse it's own ephemeral flesh with a human momentarily in order to function. The angel removes his cortical stack-trap and inserts the device into the base of the target human's head with a Finesse+ Resistance roll - the target's stamina + defense ( if the target is resisting, if not the penalty is only their stamina), inflicting a single point of aggravated damage should the roll succeed as it enters the target's skull. Upon success the Angel rolls it's Power + Finesse + Rank - the target's Resolve + Composure ( if resisted), succeeding this subsequent roll the angel stores the target's mind within the device, which it may retrieve. This process leaves a biomechanical interface port behind where the initial wound was inflicted, allowing the mind to be re-transferred to it's original body should the angel choose. This device is surprisingly modular and can interface with most forms of machinery that is capable of accepting a data storage device, uploading the mortal's consciousness onto a digital medium for a variety of purposes- in which the mortal is then capable of manipulating whatever device they are uploaded to with the appropriate skill or attribute rolls ( As a rule, being a digital entity robs the character of being able to make use of most physical or attributes aside from Dexterity, since they no longer have a body. Exception occur when the character has access to a form that allows for interaction with the physical world- a piece of industrial machinery, a vehicle.. or a robotic drone body being some examples.)

                                                            The target's own body falls into a state of suspended animation that to all the world resembles a coma, albeit one where the victim impossibly requires no sustenance to maintain the body's life functions. As long as the target's mind is separated from the body this suspended animation continues on, though the body is still very much mortal an can be killed - which forever destroys any chance the target's mind has of returning to an organic form ( well sort of, the cortical stack-trap could be inserted into another living body that has been the victim of this Process.. but that holds its own problems).

                                                            These cortical stack-traps persist for as long as there is a consciousness within them, and the angel may generate new ones with an expenditure of a willpower point and five points of Essence. In addition, the angel may possess a number of active stack-traps in use equal to their Rank+ 1
