                                            I'd like to see an overhaul of the Gift list in a big way. I would like to see, in roughly this order:

                                            1. Fewer bullshit loopholes in Gifts. Besides making the Gift less useful, it makes it harder to remember how it works and requires more look-ups. Can I Detect Acorns three times per day or five times per day? Well, let me go look it up and bring the game to a screeching halt. If it's not an important enough detail to bother stopping the game and looking it up, then why the Hell is it even in the book? I really would prefer we avoid times per day restrictions, form restrictions, and target restrictions unless they really make sense.

                                            2. Fewer bullshit scales. I see a lot of powers that go bullshit-bullshit-kinda useful-bullshit-OP as fuck. Yeah, I get the purchasing plan, but Promethean manages to do the same thing with half the bloat and nearly none of the crap powers. If you want to keep jumping to the head of the line expensive, fine, but don't make it feel like a punishment to go the long way.

                                            3. More consistent design. Mummy is pretty consistent in it's effects which makes it easier to remember how an effect works. Gifts are all over the map. Let's have a tad more structure.

                                            4. If Renown stays tied to Gifts, I'd like a given Renown type associated directly with a Gift list rather than with separate Gifts. For example, all Strength list Gifts are Glory Gifts, all Evasion Gifts are Cunning Gifts, etcetera. On the metagame level, it would make combinations easier and clear without a lot of flipping back and forth and planning. I also think it would help round out more about what a given splat is about depending on what Renown is associated with their affiliated Gift lists.

                                                I'd also like to see how Essence is gained change. StephenLS said many years ago touching magic rocks was dumb, and he's not wrong. This is how I would like to see it.

                                                The Moon: Each night, when you call to the moon, you get a point of Essence.

                                                Territory: Your pack, if you have one, holds a territory. Each dawn that begins with your pack still holding that territory, every member of the pack gains a number of essence depending on the size and occult features of that territory; some will be worth more than others.

                                                Spirit Hunting: Smaller, weaker spirits - those below your Rank - can be hunted and consumed for Essence.

                                                Spirit Bargains: You can trade favors to spirits in exchange for Essence directly. Favors should be weird, difficult, or dangerous.

                                                The Pack: You should be able to freely share Essence with other members of a bonded Pack.

                                                Personally, i think these would be more interesting than the current set-up. If it results in too much Essence floating around, some methods can e reduced or Essence costs can simply be increased.
