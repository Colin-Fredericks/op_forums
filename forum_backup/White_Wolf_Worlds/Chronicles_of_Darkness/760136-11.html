    Oh goodness, there's been lots of discussion over the weekend. I've read through it; some responses:

    1) Swarm ideas: Diplomacy or Espionage could get an info-gather task; the former finding public/mortal information, the latter finding secret/supernatural information. Military could have some kind of defending-an-area thing. Economic...act as temporary dots in Resources? Honestly, I'm having some trouble with these; Colony-management sounds less interesting to me than dealing with the fact that you've been infected with an alien microbe and now have weird powers and mutations and you're not sure how human you still are and you keep having these megalomaniac urges to take over the world.

    2) Powers: I agree that Records and Clocks should be merged. Based on the structure so far, it looks like each has one power that they and only they get; the other two are secondary for another group. For Marshal secondary, I'd like to give them Crowns, but that means that Diplomat's new power needs to be unique to them. Maybe splitting Crowns into single-target and multi-target (along the lines of Dominate and Majesty; former is more powerful/stronger control, while latter affects more targets but is weaker)? With Marshals sharing the multi-target one. Scepters for multi-target, Crowns for single-target. Which leaves Stewards, who have to share with Marshals. Hmm. Well, armies need supplies; stewards grow supplies. What if there was a growth-power, something for generating a lot of small things, like food/bullets/etc? Call it Seeds. Distinct from Hammers, since Hammers is more about single big things? So then the list looks like this (italic marks unique powers)

    Marshal
    Blades - melee
    Bows - ranged
    Plates - armor, defense
    Scepters - subversion, conversion
    Seeds - resources, supplies

    Chalices - poisons and potions

    Spheres - scry
    Staves - warp, space

    Stewards
    Hammers - create
    Plates - armor, defense
    Records - post/precognition, time
    Seeds - resources, supplies
    Staves - warp, space

    Diplomat
    Crowns - command, control
    Quills - telepathy, peaceful action
    Records - post/precognition, time
    Scepters - subversion, conversion
    Veils - shadows, flight

    Shade
    Blades - melee
    Censers - smokescreen, gasses, incense
    Chalices - poisons, potions
    Cloaks - illusions
    Veils - shadows, flight



    Well, the Colony aspect is to tie into the theme as a noble; the raw resources and power of your nation are yours to command. In the game play, it's mostly a manapool for large scale powers beyond just yourself. The "Management" will largely be the issue of wondering if using all of your colony's resources for one thing leaves you vulnerable to another Colony, a roleplay risk, if you will.


    But we've spent quite a deal on Colony haven't we?


    Anyways... I actually never decided on what hammers is for other than being a tool for creation. But I suppose for now, Seeds will work as something to do with playing with resources someway. Maybe early on it's about stealing resources.

    I also actually never really noticed that there was a theme of every Caste having exactly one unique power. This was mostly just a side effect of the way I split things up, but I think I'll keep it this way just for theming. Admitedly, maybe I need to switch Blades for Bows for the Shades to make Blades the unique, but eh... buh my assassins!

    I'll go make a quick write up for Plates, ugh Shields, soon. I keep forgetting which name to use!


    But while I do so.... I actually need to decide how I want to "theme" powers. Specifically, the dice mechanics they will use, the limitations, clauses for them happening. How to describe how they work? Do I go for more aggressive or passive abilities?

    There's obviously the nobility theme in the naming... but what about the secondary theme? Do I paint for more "evil"? Body horror? Eldritch subversion and corruption?
