    [Hack] Removing Lineages

    08-12-2016, 04:44 PM
    So, finishing up chapter three, and I'm getting the distinct feeling that Lineage doesn't really do much. Mechanically speaking, they give you two Bestowments to choose from, and a Humor, which determines the element you need to sleep near to recover Pyros, and seems kind of like it's supposed to determine your aTorment, but then Torment is the Vice replacement and doesn't seem to actually be tied to Lineage. I guess your Humour is supposed to flavor the way your individual Tpent is expressed, rather than determining specifically what your Torment is.

    That seems pretty barebones for an X-Splat. Lineages fill a bit more of a narrative role, specifying the process by which Prometheans of the Lineages are created, and adding some details like Tammuz' relationship with language, but overall I'm not convinced Promethean needs X-Splats. Heck, it doesn't even really have Y-Splats in the traditional sense, with Refinements are supposed to be something you regularly change. So why not take it a step further and make Promethean the Splatless line?

    The Extempore give us a model of what Prometheans might look like if we removed Lineages. Work with your Storyteller to determine what process went into creating you, and pick an appropriate Bestowment to your Creation from among the 14 examples or use them as guidelines to create your own. Pick an appropriate Humour from among the examples (the four traditional Humours, plus Ectoplasm/Ephemera and Oil) or create your own using the examples as guidelines. Your Humour determines what element you need to sleep near to regain Pyros and flavors your Torment.

    The most significant difference I see this making is that you could have stitched-together Prometheans Creating robot Prometheans, Prometheans buried in earth and bound by words creating Prometheans by covering bodies in spirit-stuff, Egyptian-style Prometheans creating Greek-style Prometheans, and any combination in-between with Extempore popping up left and right. I think I'm ok with that implication, though.

    Is there anything I'm overlooking? Any important part of Promethean's themes I'd be leaving out by running everyone like Extempore? Any suggestions for how to refine or improve this idea?

              I don't see anything wrong with the idea of stripping out Lineages. Their best use is for new players to grasp what ideas are in play in the setting, or to reference their legends. They're also great for letting a creature that begins as tabula rasas to have a sense of identity. If you think your players are going to fill in the gaps themselves, then you'll probably lose little of value.

              That said, I do like Lineages. As someone who fell head over heels in love with PtC1E, I felt they had a huge part in selling the game to me. 2E seems to dispense with a lot of this stuff, but I was a fan of how the details of each creation ritual and the stories and legends of each Lineage connected them deeply to the mythology. Almost every Promethean could draw a heritage all the way back to a single act of divine inspiration that rivaled the creation of Adam, and existed as proof of the Principle. Just as important was the unspoken use of "lineage" not just in the familial sense, but in a classical sense of scientists and philosophers forming a lineage of tutelage (Socrates instructed Plato, and Plato Aristotle, and so forth). Roots like these legitimized the Pilgrimage more than Azothic Memory does, in my reading, but it is what it is, and much of how Lineage and Humor was reflected externally is gone now, making Lineage more ornamental than iconic.

                      Originally posted by blackhatmatt View Post

It seems like they just do the parts of Promethean that I like best better than the Lineages do. So, I'm considering just hacking Lineages out entirely and saying everyone is Extempore. Thoughts?

                          Bare bones for an x-splat? Ha! Geist and Demon have worse - they get one affinity power at Char Gen, and no extra charge for non-affinity powers after, and that's about it. Its especially notable in Demon to the point that the game acknowledges that its entirely in the Unchained's heads, completely an artificial divide with no meaning. And Lineage has a huge impact on Disquiet, Torment, and Wastelands as well as Bestowments, none of which should not be underestimated.

                          To be honest, though? You don't need x-splats in any game, easily fixed through a simple houserule. Its simplicity within itself to remove clans, there's been plenty of methods to remove Paths in Mage, and so on. However, x-splats exist for a reason - they're easily recognizable archetypes that resonate along common tropes for people to engage in, which does help people when they're understanding and interacting with the game. It helps them engage with the story. That's the main benefit in any game - easily recognizable tropes that can be evoked for story reasons.
