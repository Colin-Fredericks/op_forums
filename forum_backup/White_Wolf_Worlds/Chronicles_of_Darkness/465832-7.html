    Our heroes descend into the Catacombs to find the lost star, but even doing so what is her agenda. While weaker than Agrippa or Blackstaff the serpent is not without fangs. Revelations within show the truth behind Dana’s condition, but only leave questions about her old flame.

    Session XXIII

    Marek’s Thoughts

    26 April 1922

    Well, the trip to the catacombs was productive. Not necessarily sure if it was entirely a good thing, but we completed what we set out to do. First of all the rest of the Pack, minus Dacian (He was still guarding Skadi) came to the Opera House and we all started looking for the lever or switch that would open the hidden door we’d found. It didn’t take long for Wolfram to find it and pull it, opening the passage to the Catacombs. He decided to stay behind and study the play with Harlow. Nathan, Dana and Lizette stayed to watch the door, and our “guest” while the rest of us crossed the running water. Tatiana gave Dearbhail a boost so she could glide over the water, while the rest of us swam. We made our way with what little light we had and found yet another lake, or something much like one. This one was further across and the water was running faster. We decided that we could need rope and more light if we were going to continue with our explorations. So we retraced our steps. Harlow found us some rope and Lizette, Dana and Wolfram joined us this time. Wolfram used a gift to make his body glow like firelight. It came in very handy. So we all made our way back across the first lake and used the rope on the second one. Each time we crossed Greta turned into water and appeared on the other side. That is a handy gift.

    Once on the other side we ran across two skeletons carrying swords. The skeletons themselves were very old, probably pulled from the Catacomb walls. The swords were much newer. Wolfram did not believe that they were animated by a Sin-eater. His opinion was that it was some other kind of magic. In short order Greta took care of both of them and ended up throwing them both in the water to be carried away by the current. We continued on for a while before Alla and Connie picked up the sound of one very fast heartbeat ahead. Tatiana and I heard someone moving, and then I heard the telltale sound of a round being chambered in a rifle. I halted the group and Wolfram called out to identify us to the scared individual with the rifle. We figured it was Emilie, and we were right. She walked slowly out of the next chamber, still holding her gun in a ready position. We explained what we were doing here and that we’d been sent to come find and protect her. Her concern was that she would not be safe until after the play was over, and maybe not even then. She was worried about more cultists coming to find her. Some of our number were trying to convince her to come back to the surface, while others were not trying to pressure her either way. At first I don’t think she actually believed us, but we eventually convinced her of our good intentions.

    Emilie for her part decided to come back to the surface with us and allow us to protect her from the cultists and from Father Blackstaff. She still wants the play to go on with her changes but is afraid that Blackstaff will kill her or have is men do it. I can understand her fear, but if she stayed in the Catacombs it would only be a matter of time before he found her and killed her. His men were already waiting outside. If they decided to come in and followed the path we did she would have been trapped. Her skeletons, while threatening to non-combatants, were not much to deal with for anyone with any experience fighting. Greta took out both by herself without breaking a sweat.

    We made it back to the Opera House without issue. The rope and Wolfram’s light both coming in very handy. Once back Emilie said that she would be at the practice for the play but specifically told Harlow that she doesn’t want anyone to know that she’s been found. That should provide some modicum of security, for a while anyway. The less people that know that she’s back the better. What is the phrase? Loose lips sink ships.

    While we were searching for Emilie a couple things came up. First off Dearbhail took Dana aside to talk to her in private. I heard her anyway since my wolf senses were activated, but I don’t think anyone besides Tatiana and myself heard. Dearbhail said something to Dana about treating Lizette right and to not hurt her feelings. She explained that Lizette loves several people and that it didn’t make her feelings for Dana any less. Dearbhail also threatened to come after the archaeologist if she hurt Lizette because she’s afraid of commitment. I can see her doing it too. Dearbhail may seem like the sweet non-combatant, but she’s got paired knives that she is pretty good with. Dana assured Dearbhail that she did not intend to hurt Lizette. Apparently Dana has trust issues because she’s been betrayed by one too many people she considered friends. I can understand that.

    During their conversation Dearbhail also claimed me as her special wolf. Not that I think Dana was interested in me, but it came up during Dearbhail’s speech about how close our Pack is. I had to smile at that. I rather enjoy the Irish firebrand’s company in and out of bed. I have not felt quite so close to anyone else, though I’m not sure if what I feel for Dearbhail is love. I know that I love my brother and my father, but that is different. That is familial. With Dearbhail I wish to keep her safe but that feeling is stronger with her than it is with the rest of the Pack. Don’t get me wrong, if anyone hurts a Pack mate of mine they will regret it, but if someone hurt Dearbhail I would take pleasure in tearing their throat out and watching them choke on their own blood. I rather prefer to see Dearbhail happy and smiling though. I know that something is not quite right with me. I don’t feel emotions the same way others do. Things are muted. I just know that I care for the Irish singer and like to see her happy. I just don’t think I’m very good at showing it though.

    As far as the play goes Wolfram figured out some things about it. The play is designed to put the audience into a trance like state that may make you susceptible to a spirit or other similar being. The goal it seems is to put you in contact with a god. The original version would possibly put the audience in touch with Hades or something like that. It is the Orphean version. The adjusted version of the play would put you in contact with Dionysus or perhaps Persephone. Still not sure that option is much better. A god of chaos would likely cause a good bit of problems for the people involved. I don’t think Hades, or whoever, would be a better choice though. My personal vote is for neither.

    An interesting thing that was mentioned by Emilie was that the storm we are currently experiencing is connected to Dana somehow. Dana believes that it is because of the artifacts. Seems feasible to me. We don’t know what they do and she has been in close proximity to them for a while now. The storm didn’t start until a day or two after she arrived in town with the statues. So did the storm just take it’s time to build or did something else happen after she arrived that would have triggered it?

    After all of that there was a short discussion on where Emilie would stay. Greta was adamant that the actress not stay with Connie since the slight vampire seemed especially interested in turning her into one of her own kind. Greta was very much against this and threatened Connie, and Alla to a much lesser extent that no one was going to turn Emilie. I think being killed by vampires has colored her world view quite a bit in regards to them. Tatiana tried to calm things down, which worked for the most part.

    Once that conversation was done Greta went on her way and the rest of us quickly decided what to do with our cultist “friend.” I was fine with just taking him a little ways off and killing him. I have a knife. Tatiana thought that would be too much of a waste and suggested that our vampire friends get something out of it. So, Alla fed off of him, then Connie did. Connie ended up killing the man by taking too much blood. I suppose it doesn’t matter how the situation was ended, as long as it is done. We did not need Blackstaff hearing about anything that man had seen or heard.

    **** **** ****

    27 April 1922

    Well, we finally got some answers out of Dana today. Before breakfast she asked those of us in the Pack to come upstairs and talk to her in her room. As soon as the door closed she asked us to make a Pledge that we would not speak of what we heard with anyone outside the room. We agreed since it was an easy enough thing to do. She also agreed not to speak to anyone about what she had learned about us. She then went on to tell us about how she, like us, was connected to an artifact that granted her, in essence, immortality. It was not the same kind of technology that the Golden Eagle or the statues are. It is something different, but it essentially does the same thing. She can be wounded, but she will heal faster than usual. She can be killed, but it will not be permanent. She also will not age. I think our aging will be slowed due to the Eagle, but don’t think it will be stopped altogether. Dana said that she had an interest in studying the Golden Eagle, but has no desire to take it since she is already immortal. Her main aspiration for living so long is to learn as much as she can about the world around her and its history. She seems to truly just wishes to gain more knowledge so she can understand the world around her.

    She also told us that she believes that Iris would not have any problems trying to kill her if she felt the need. Dana also mentioned that Iris has a way of “interfacing” with the kind of technology that the statues are. We’re not sure how she can do it, but it is clear that we are far from the only unique creatures around. Dana herself can interact with the technology in some small way, making light and such. She can only do more by using her Wās. She does have other abilities from her association with the device that makes her immortal. She can strengthen objects past the normal tolerance and can sense if an object is magical and other basic information about it. She could not get specific details off of a hedgespun item, she’d just know it was magical. That would be an interesting talent to have if you ask me.

    Apparently Iris also likes to collect these same kind of artifacts and has been a patron to several archaeologists over the years. Perhaps because she has such a unique interaction with the artifacts Iris is also very keen on people not investigating her past too closely. Anyone that Dana has sent to investigate Iris’s past too deeply has ended up dead or missing. So, she is definitely hiding something but it is difficult to find out what. Unless she becomes a threat to my Pack I am content letting Iris keep her secrets. Dana’s opinion is that Iris is dangerous but useful. Sounds fair to me.

    After the initial conversation about abilities we discussed what we needed to accomplish going forward. We talked briefly about the plan to protect Skadi. I think what we’re going to do is have Alla impersonate Skadi and take her place tonight in the new room. Before she goes there however she is going to spend some time in a freezer so she will radiate cold the way that Skadi does. Doesn’t hurt to be as realistic as possible. We will also need people to stay and protect the archaeologists and Emilie. Not sure if we’re going to do that in one location or split up. I would recommend against splitting up.

    We talked about seeing if Greta could help with our various protection jobs, but some were unsure if she’d be more of a help or a hindrance. She is rather impulsive.

    In the end the conversation turned to the play, as seems to be the case a lot lately. We talked about whether or not we should interrupt the play to keep the magic from activating or if Alla should subtly change her lines or something along those lines to derail the magic, but keep the essence of the story intact. Either way we do it will have to be discreet. Agrippa seems to have an interest in the play and may be been tangentially responsible for Emilie hearing about it in the first place. Maybe she knew that the actress would want to put her own religious spin on it? Emilie does worship Persephone which is related to Dionysus. And Agrippa, and Connie for that matter, have been referred to as Maenads. If I remember correctly they are worshippers/followers if Dionysus that can be rather violent if the mood takes them. Considering the power that Agrippa has we should probably do what we can to be as discreet as possible if we are going to sabotague the play. I think Alla’s plan was to confront the elder vampire about the play and its purpose without mentioning Agrippa’s involvement in the whole affair. I guess we’ll see how it all goes.


    Tatiana’s Conversations

    Volchonok, would you stop your yapping!

    No, I am not going back over the water.

    I do not know what I shall do today.

    No, volchonok, “I don’t know” is not the same thing as “yes, I will go across the water because I wish to get away from annoying whining volchonoks”.

    I am sorry you could not get across using the rope. It is not my fault; I do not know how to make spirit rope.

    If you do not wish me to say so, you should not whine.

    I am not helpless without you, my worriful volchonok. Greta could be wherever she wished, with so much water, and besides, a few skeletons are no real danger.

    I told you about the skeletons! The creatures with no flesh? Remember?

    I’m sure you would have loved seeing if they still had marrow. Greta handled them with no problems, though. They were Emilie’s anyway.

    Ah, I see volchonok, now it comes out. Come now, you got to see me help our songbird fly many times, almost as many as she did.

    She did look quite beautiful, did she not? And it was nice to have her so close in the dark, to hear her heartbeat in her fingers. Ah, but she says Marek is her special wolf.

    She did, while you were sulking, when we came back to ask the writer man for rope.

    I do not know, I wasn’t paying attention. She was speaking with Dana, something about Lizette being in love with many people, and us killing people she does not like.

    You keep saying such things, volchonok. Dearbháil did not scold me. Perhaps she looked at me sternly after I shook dry, but that is all.

    Pshaw, I do not care how that one looks at me. The actress became far less interesting once we found her.

    I do not know. She has magic? She can make skeletons walk and knew what Greta and Wolfram and Alla and Constancia were right away. And her play is magic, Wolfram says.

    She was not properly grateful at all. And now we have someone else to guard. I still say we should just kill all the cultists. Then we will not need to guard her. And she will not be able to make Greta angry at Constancia anymore.

    Because Constancia wants Emilie to become a vampire.

    What? Volchonok, where do you get these ideas? Who has taught you ‘jealous’? No, I am not jealous. I don’t want to be a vampire.

    No, I just do not like that I had to be growly at Greta so she would not try and kill Constancia.

    I did. If we were to kill the cultist we questioned, there is no reason to have his blood go to waste. It is not against the Litany to let a vampire consume a human.

    No, growly is not like lecturing. Greta can still show up in my bathroom.

    It is true, it is amusing to watch Wolfram’s face when he lectures. He goes all noble, and sputters so when you tell him the truth.

    I don’t know, volchonok, I stopped paying attention to watch his face.

    Oh, you mean before that? We were talking about putting Alla in a freezer so she could pretend to be an ice giant better. Dana thinks Greta could help and said we should bribe her with telling her things.

    She did say many things, Dana did, that were important. If we must kill her, we will not attack her, but some object that is probably not this was. And if we are to protect her, it is good to know she will not stay dead, and that there is some important object.

    The object is hard to break. It is her magic, she says. I do not think we will need to though. Dearbháil likes her and the archaeologist and Lizette are friends again. Even Wolfram and Dacian have liked her more since she told us of her magics. I still do not understand her and her wanting to know everything there is, but that is for the others.

    I already said, I do not know. It will not be across the water though. Perhaps I will see what Greta is doing. It will be more fun, whatever it is, than writing a play over again so that it does not make people dream of God. Or sleeping in a freezer.

    Where would we run, eh volchonok? Perhaps when it comes time to leave the city again. But not this day, volchonok, not this day.


    Lizette’s Plans

    26th April 1922

    We have rescued Emilie. It remains to be seen if this is a good thing.

    Everyone (sans Dacian) descended on the theater, and it took little time for Wolfram to find the lever that opened the hidden way beneath it. It was pitch dark beyond the light from the doorway. Harlow was around but kept some distance. We needed to get across the lake where water had flooded in from the catacombs due to the rain and swollen the lake. Wolfram stayed behind to study the play, while Dearbháil, Tatiana, Alla, Marek and Greta went in. Dearbháil apparently glided over the water with Tatiana’s help, where the Werewolves swam across. Alla did, but needed help from Tatiana. Greta just did that creepy teleporting across water thing she does. Nathan, Dana and I went back in to keep an eye on the door in case cultists returned.

    A short time later, they returned and declared they needed rope and more light. Harlow went and fetched rope, while Dearbháil buttonholed Dana who went and talked to her in a side room. I have a feeling it was about me. When we reconvened, Wolfram explained that both plays, if performed, would cause a magical effect, bringing a dreamlike state to the audience and opening communion with…something. The Orphean version he was unsure with what, but the Dionysian version would be with Dionysus, or some entity claiming to be Dionysus. As Dana noted, creating contact with something at least claiming to be if not being a god of revelry, chaos, madness and wine did not seem like a good idea.

    Speaking as a Catholic, I don’t like the idea of creating a communion with some ancient god a good idea at all. That this sort of paganism exists in Paris is sort of disturbing, and I am unsure what to make of it. It’s hardly my place to lecture; I am a terrible Catholic, but nonetheless…

    We ventured below, with Wolfram glowing rather brightly. Tatiana tossed Dearbháil over the lake, and she secured a rope on the far side, which we used to get across. Well, all but Greta who just sort of teleported, and Tatiana who swam to show off. Nathan remained behind with Harlow at Dana’s instance, her noting he was less resilient than the rest of us. Despite the current, I had an easy time getting across, and Alla apparently had an easier time of it then before.

    I’ve never traveled the catacombs without a light, so I can only imagine how unnerving it was the first time through, especially with the silence replaced with the sound of rushing water. I spotted a glyph from the Autumn Court indicating the area was not part of our direct turf but not super dangerous or tied to anything specific. All sorts of things use the catacombs, I can only guess what some of them are, but the rushing water both meant it was safer from them but the environment was more dangerous. Also, I may need to bring Wolfram again his fiery light was terribly useful.

    Wolfram and fire. Yum.

    Eventually we came to a wide river of water, with two skeletons of the far side, wielding swords. The skeletons looked old, and brittle. Tatiana began to swim across with the rope, while Greta quickly and easily teleported across the river and overcame the skeletons out of hand. She pinned one at first while keeping the other at bay, but eventually tossed them in the river, despite Dana’s comment about “bypassing guardians rather than destroying them.” Tatiana set the rope and we carefully made it across, though the current was much stronger here. Eventually the Werewolves smelled her and Alla heard her heart, and Emilie emerged from the darkness with a rifle.

    Emilie was not happy to see us. Yes, she had been trapped in her shrine, but it was semi-purposeful. Having been tracked first by Blackstaff then Agrippa, she had sought refuge in her shrine beneath the city, and the rising water kept both away from her. She was, needless to say, not pleased about two Sin-Eaters and two Vampires (Greta and Connie were with us as well as the usual Wolfram and Alla.) Discussion had few surprises, beyond Emilie was remarkably forthcoming. Yes, she changed the play. Yes, she did so in service to Persephone for her son, Dionysus. She “found out” Harlow would be coming with the Orphean holy book and “helped” with a few revisions, revisions that would turn the invocation to “her god” though while we assume (based on context) that it is Dionysus, it could be Persephone. I am not sure Emilie Barteau is completely sane.

    Connie was certainly interested. Interested in turning her into a vampire. Emilie recognized her as a maenad, but was unsure about being turned. She said she would need to “Commune with the Goddess.” Greta expressed a threat to kill Connie if she turned her, or to kill Emilie. This went over like you would expect, especially with Tatiana who seemed upset her friends were fighting.

    In the end, Emilie agreed to come back with us. She followed Dana’s advice to stay in the hotel, as her home is almost certainly being watched. Hopefully we were not noticed on our return. Harlow saw us, but I doubt he is in league with Blackstaff. Emilie swore him to secrecy for now.

    Now I rest in Dana’s room. She wanted a bath and to change, and would be out in a moment.

    Mother of God.


    27th April 1922

    I think Dana has appropriately apologized as well as shown her feelings. I suppose I might get used to this, but good God I can’t imagine how.

    Dearbháil was out early (given how late we got back) getting glamour, and we decided to meet for lunch, then return the rooms to discuss our next move. We used Dana’s room, and she even shut the windows so Alla could join us.
    We looked at our timeline. The fake was is arriving today, the real was tomorrow, the 28th. Ideally we want to hopefully end the storm by or before the 30th, as the play is that day, and the wedding is on the 1st. Whose idea was it to have a play like that on Walpurgis anyway?

    We also discovered Dana is a sort of semi-immortal being called an Eternal. She has bound her life energy into an object (which is not the was) and cannot be killed without it being destroyed. She can also strengthen objects and have some idea of their function, and presumably age. Nothing flashy, but a good kit for an archeologist. No word on how she discovered how to do this, however. Her status is why she is not seeking the Eagle, however.

    We also discussed how to resolve the issue with the play. Wolfram intends to research a way to have Alla flub her part just so she disrupts the spell without it going haywire, and the rest of us need to determine how to prevent Blackstaff, Emilie, or Agrippa (or possibly Connie) from wrecking havoc and sidelining the play or having Alla controlled. Alla intends to confront Agrippa soon more directly.

    Tonight though she masquerades as Skadi, she will sleep in ice to give off cold and use her acting skills to disguise as that fairest. There will presumably be an ambush prepared. With everything going on, I could use a little ambush.

    Mayhem!

    End Session XXIII

    Eternals can be found in World of Darkness: Immortals in the back. Dana did not outright lie but implied there was an artifact involved, but it was in fact crafted by her.

    The play is a sort of ritual for Communion, as per the spell in Second Sight, while bringing the Audience into a dreamlike state. What would be contacted they can only speculate. Context suggests a Greek god, but who knows what would actually be contacted.

    Spelunking and conversation took much of the session…despite only two or three major scenes.

    We are going to miss a session due to vacation timings. In two weeks will be stats for any PC I can easily get a hold of stats for…I expect Alla, Lizette, and Wolfram but we will see. See you then. Comments and Questions welcome.

                                                Heh, okay, actual answer:

                                                I run 2 games per week. My weekly prep time is near zero. Because I am a freak. I have Attention Deficit Hyperactive Disorder (ADHD). This was hellish in school. This works to my advantage in game. My mind has "tracks," which is to say I can be running a scene with one "track" while planning the next scene with a second "track." I can have outright conversations with myself IC because each "track" is following one speaker, and I essentially have two "subroutines" going each of which is a different personality. Once a PC or NPC has their "subroutine" in my mind, I can talk IC as them endlessly without thinking about it, because their "subroutine" is well fleshed out. You get that with authors talking about voices. Character voices or "subroutines" crystallize quickly for me, and they can go so independently I have to stop and think about/catch up with what an NPC just did, because my focus was elsewhere while I ran that NPC and I did not realize what they were doing. That is not normal. It also means that it is harder to plan strictly, because for example often Dana is going to do whatever the hell Dana is going to do, even if it runs afoul my plans (Dana is a pain that way). Last session Dana pulled a gun and kidnapped Iris. I was not planning that; she just did it.

                                                So while I am crazy, I am so creative my mind can barely cope. This means it does whatever it does, and often this involves scaring the hell out of myself while alone at night, because my mind is a bitch that way. But, it means I can often just let it do it's thing. This has a huge impact on how I run. For most games, not including Golden Eagle, this means the games essentially run themselves. Most games have a ton of prepwork initially and little afterwards. Balance of Shadows had almost 3 months of work before session 1. Since then, while I have worked on future plotlines, I have sat and worked on prep for an imminent session approximately three times in six years, and those were all cases where I had to prep combat. And some of the initial work is still not touched on. We touched on a plot for that game last Sunday I worked on February of 2010. Now, I have prepped for Geist…the Haunted Hotel plot I had later, but I worked on it a month before it happened in game. The skeleton of the Bright Shadow Plot? Before the first session.

                                                This means I create scenarios more than plots. What’s going on? Who are the personalities? I work that out first. Then, I make sure there is a way for the PCs to deal with it. In the late 90s I ran several “Big Empties”, Vampire games with huge, intricate plots and major movements and bored PCs because they never saw any of it. But these outlines are rarely prepping for the session immediately. Some are worked out way ahead because I got an idea in the car (or had a nightmare, the case with the Hotel BoS one), some are, literally, planned from spark to finish in the scene before they begin, while I am running that scene, because I got an idea and set my second “track” on it while my main one does the scene. Next scene I introduce a plot that did not exist when we sat down for the game an hour ago, and by the end of the first scene of the PCs dealing with it, 30 minutes after it’s genesis, it is done and ready.

                                                Golden Eagle is something of an exception. It’s not a big sandbox, it is little sandboxes, and thus Golden Eagle is more work than every other game combined. I prep scenarios, not sessions, even then. We have ~2-3 sessions of Paris left, and I am prepping Berlin story. By the time we get done, it should be (mostly) ready. Then I won’t need to work on Golden Eagle again till the next scenario after Berlin. I mean, I may do so, but won’t need to. Still, compared to the small prep time for other games, that is a lot.

                                                The main prep time is pictures. I am always adding NPCs, and everyone needs a picture. So webcrawls for pics do happen. Sometimes a single picture creates an entire scenario in my head; In Night Train, Nikka’s entire storyline came from a single picture. I looked at the picture, I had a plot. Same with Chalcedony; it was a picture I had from Castle Falkenstein; I looked at the picture and said “Now who would you be in Changeling?” and it all came out. My games are very driven by PC and NPC personalities, this is a big part of why. My players are happy to spend a lot of time exploring their PCs and NPCs more than Dungeoneering, which is not to say a damn thing against Dungeoneering, but I know where my talents lie.

                                                This means, incidentally, I suck at crunch heavy games. I can be a bit airy fairy with WoD or CoD stats, but crunch can be rough. My Deadlands: Lost Colony game slammed to a halt because while I had a sandbox, it was far, far too much bookkeeping to plan the many combat encounters that went with that game. Both because it was a lot of combat, and because I could not eyeball it and say “Okay, she probably has 3 Dexterity, 4 Athletics…say 2 Wits…” if combat came up I did not expect, so I had to prep for anything like that the PCs might do. It was a good game, but it fell apart for that reason (and then Malibu Dream House started with the same group and went well.)

                                                So I run 4 games a fortnight, two a week. I can do this because they are low prep and I am a freak of nature. But if I can clarify anything lemme know.
