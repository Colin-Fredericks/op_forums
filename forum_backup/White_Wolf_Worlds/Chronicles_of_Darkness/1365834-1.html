        In that spirit, as well as for fun, what would you alter, add or subtract from the current write-up of the Council of Bones?
        Yeah, I think that indeed what bothers me the most about them- they feel like hunters who uphold the Vigil and nothing more, which is admirable but feels a bit hollow as they have little to no grasp in the world around them, especially for a conspiracy. For example, if they were allowed to serve as actual mediums and spiritualists, it would have grounded them better into the world IMO- they would have had a stable infrastructure, a source of income and actually not doing anything too bad. By using their talents for personal gain, for example, they would have felt, to me, more human, more flawed, more easy to connect with.

        I also really don't get why they should execute an hunter for using their Endowments before people who are not a part of the Council- other than stopping cross organization games with them, which I don't how common they are in the first place, in a world where many people are touched by the supernatural and conspiracies are known to grant special powers to their agents, killing your members for talking with a ghost before outsiders makes little sense. I mean, even if they were to use their Endowments openly, people who know about the occult would think they are some "regular" mediums, that is, people with Supernatural Merits and such. In the world of hunter, the fact that you can talk with dead people or scry through a mirror is, while not common, not unheard off, and could easily be disguised as something unrelated to the Mark, of which most Council members actually don't know anything.

        I would have also liked if there was some information about their earlier incarnations, like it was in Cheiron and the AO- some Eras where they were in power, as well as some deeper exploration of their infrastructures and their tie ins to the wider world. Like the Golden Age of Islam, or some cultural variations of the past for history development, or that they would actually have some representation as mediums in the wider world- from that old lady who will read your fortune for a couple of coins to that local channel medium who speaks with your dead grandma. The AO have esoteric mysteries and drug dealing. Cheiron have their pharmaceutical juggernaut. The MM have the Catholic Church, and TFV have the american government. Even the Lucifuge and the AKD have a strong infrastructure, from the genealogists of the former to the mystery cults and labyrinths of the later. The Council has some cool things, don't get me wrong- like their initiation rites and such, but it feels more like a loose association which will murder you if you are not acting as they want you to. It feels like you are not getting much from joining them, but have to pay a lot.

        So in short, I think that what bothers me is their seemingly isolation from the world and their almost altruistic devotion for the Vigil. The Wardens of Light were also felt as being "just" an honest and good compact, and I also don't like them by much. Again, other people seems to like them, and my motto is that not every organization is for everyone so I would avoid claiming "that is how I would change them because that way they would have been better", especially since Hunter is one of the games that encourages you to write your own splats. To each is own, as they say.

            IIRC, the Council has the prohibition against showing outsiders their Endownment because they are afraid of being seen in the same vein as Lés Mysteres and being hunted down to the brink of extinction again.

            That last part is one of it's more interesting aspects, IMO. They keep getting practically wiped out. Some have pointed out that ghosts could be behind their continual renewal but given how both how their destruction is written and the description of their organizational practices, I'm inclined to disagree.
            The dead are what wipes them out frequently; vampires, ghosts, the Bound (not just sin eaters) and possibly even the Arisen are all targets of the Council and sufficient exposure of the Council would result in a terrible backlash. Easily more than enough to wipe out a chapter, and if there is a lead to another chapter, well it just dominos out.

            Survivors can rebuild, adapt the techniques and tricks to they know to their needs, changing some of their methods as they have recreate others whole cloth, hoping it is enough to keep the candle of their dying Vigil alive. At this point, I doubt anyone left in the council has a clue as to the origin of the Mark of the Reaper. Heck, I think the reason their write up is so light on their history is everything prior to the destruction of their immediate predecessor was lost when they fell.

            Elsewhere it has been said that a member of the conspiracy shows up in the Geist Fiction Anthology and it was implied that the 'councillor' did not know/recognise a Sin Eater. Given how long they've been around, that makes no sense unless the Council is ignorant of the history of the dead. They could have been almost anything in ages past, and the modern members would never know.
