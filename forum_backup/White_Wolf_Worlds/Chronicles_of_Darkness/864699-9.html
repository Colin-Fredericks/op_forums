                                                World of Darkness core, first edition, p130, 'If it’s a tie or neither side gets any successes, the effort fails altogether or successive at tempts (see p. 132) may be made in subsequent turns, if appropriate. The Storyteller decides if the effort can be attempted over and over until one competitor wins. If both try to grab a fish that swims downstream, they both probably have only one chance before the fish is gone. If one tries to hack into another’s computer, the effort might persist as long as the intruder likes or until fail-safes shut him out. If either side of a contested roll gets a dramatic failure, he either fails utterly or his opponent automatically achieves the equivalent of an exceptional success.'

                                                For vehicle Availability, since Availability is basically cost,you can just use cost. First edition had them splashed across the books, I'm not sure if they're going to come up in second, where it's assumed everyone has a vehicle or access to one.                                                             Originally posted by Caladriu View Post
                                                            Opinion question: What Skill would you use to win a skill-based strategy game like chess or Go? Maybe Politics? I like the idea that knowing group dynamics would be helpful. Or like, Intelligence plus a Social Skill to manipulate your opponent. In a Dark Eras games I might use Enigmas or Warfare.

                                                            Maybe it would be best to run it as a sort of Mental Chase.
                                                            A strictly strategy game would probably be Intelligence or Wits + Academics or Investigation, depending on the exact game and the methodology. Things like social rolls or the like probably would be additional rolls you could make to provide bonuses or penalties; Wits + Empathy might give you a +2 to your roll because you can guess what they'll do, and Manipulation + Subterfuge might let you give off a false impression of your plans (or cheat). It'd probably be an extended, contested sort of roll otherwise, with specific ways of accruing bonuses or penalties and deciding target numbers again depending on the game.

                                                            EDIT: I do agree in a Dark Eras game Enigmas or Warfare would be very appropriate.
