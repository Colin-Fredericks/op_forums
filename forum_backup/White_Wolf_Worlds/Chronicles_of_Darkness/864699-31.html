                                    Originally posted by Mr.F.I.X. View Post
                                    Dumb question: is the +3 bonus from spending a point of willpower in addition to the maximum +5 to a dice pool?
                                    Spending WP is a single bonus. Multiple bonuses stack normally. The rule you're referencing concerns individual mundane bonuses not being able to go above a +/-5, because that's what differentiates a bonus or penalty as mundane or supernatural. So, no, if you have a nice set of clothes that affords you say, a +3 to seduce someone and you spend WP on top of it, do feel free to let the player roll at +6.
