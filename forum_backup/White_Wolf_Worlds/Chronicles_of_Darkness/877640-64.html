    Originally posted by projectmoon View Post
    My players have found a vampire, and of course, this means they want to make Vitae. I have created three spells to deal with Vitae. I based them on spells presented earlier in this thread, as well as the first edition rules for creating blood that vampires can drink. I have discarded the 1e notion that Prime is required to make actual vampiric VItae, instead ruling that it is under the purview of Life (although maybe there's also an argument for Death). Due to the alien nature of the vampiric curse, these spells depart a tiny bit from the regular creative thaumaturgy rules. This mostly concerns the inflicting of damage, representative of the vampiric curse's cost. It always gets what it desires, one way or another.

    Channel Vitae (Life •••)
    The mage is able to channel Vitae between herself and vampires, strix, ghouls, other living targets, or inanimate receptacles.
    • Practice: Weaving
    • Primary Factor: Potency
    • Withstand: Stamina (or Resistance)
    • Suggested Rote Skills: Intimidation, Occult, Survival
    The spell allows the mage to move a quantity of Vitae up to the spell's Potency between one or more vessels she can touch, including herself, vampires, ghouls, strix, other living targets, or physical containers. Transferring the vitae into a subject forces the effects of Vitae consumption upon the it: vampires gain more Vitae, living targets may become ghouls, and so forth. If the Vitae is transferred into a container, it maintains its nature as Vitae for the Duration of the spell before reverting to normal blood.

    The mage may channel a number of points of Vitae per turn equal to her Gnosis-derived Mana per turn limit.

    +1 Reach: The mage may channel as much Vitae as she wants in one turn, ignoring her Gnosis-derived Mana per turn limit.

    +1 Reach and +1 Mana: When the vitae is transferred into a subject, the effects of the Vitae are suppressed for the Duration of the spell. This effect suppresses the effects of Vitae for one subject, and can be applied multiple times.

    Vampirism (Life ••••)
    The mage patterns the blood of a living subject into vampiric Vitae and draws it from the victim.
    • Practice: Patterning
    • Primary Factor: Potency
    • Withstand: Stamina (or Resistance)
    • Suggested Rote Skills: Brawl, Intimidation, Persuasion
    • Cost: 1 Mana
    The Vitae drawn is usually in the form of blood oozing from wounds, but can be a more exotic substance, depending on the method the mage uses to "feed" on the subject. This spell generates one Vitae per Potency. Since the spell directly patterns Life into Vitae, the subject is not harmed. The quality of the Vitae is relative to the subject (animals produce animal-quality Vitae and humans produce human-quality).

    The Vitae remains supernatural until the Duration of the spell expires, at which point it turns back into regular substance, unless it has been consumed. The use of this spell is an affront to the vampiric Curse, and damns the mage: the mage must include themselves as a subject in the spell, and they take 1 resistant Lethal damage per Potency.

    +1 Reach: The mage may apply one suitable Condition or Tilt to the subject per level of Potency, representative of the vampiric feeding.

    +1 Reach: The mage may freely divide the damage between subjects in the spell. While the Curse damns the one who meddles with it directly, it is less "concerned" with other subjects. Other subjects do not suffer resistant damage.

    Add Death, Mind, or Spirit ••••: The Mage may draw from ghosts (Death), goetia (mind), or spirits (Spirit).

    Add Prime ••••: The Mage can convert the Vitae into tass in a form of her choosing. The tass functions as Vitae and Mana, and if ingestible, can be consumed by Vampires. The tass created has the resonance keyword of "vampirism."

    Create Vitae (Life •••••)
    This spell creates Vitae from nothing.
    • Practice: Making
    • Primary Factor: Potency
    • Suggested Rote Skills: Empathy, Medicine, Occult
    • Withstand: Blood Potency, Stamina, or Resistance (depending on target)
    • Cost: 1+ Mana
    The spell creates an amount of pure, inert Vitae equal to Potency in the subject, which may be a vampire, strix, living target, or container. If created outside of an animate subject, the mage chooses the form the Vitae takes (and must have a way to contain the resulting Vitae), and the spell is Withstood by the mage's own Stamina.

    As always, the vampiric curse takes its debt: the mage must include themselves as a subject in the spell, and they suffer 1 resistant lethal damage per point of Potency.

    Vitae created by this spell is not addictve. Furthermore, the vitae created by this spell can sustain a vampire of any Blood Potency. The Vitae created by this spell becomes normal substance after the Duration expires, unless it has been consumed.

    +1 Reach: The mage may freely divide the damage between subjects in the spell. While the Curse damns the one who meddles with it directly, it is less "concerned" with other subjects. Other subjects do not suffer resistant damage.

    +1 Reach: The mage may bypass the vampiric curse almost completely by substituting Mana for suffering. The Mage may spend Mana to reduce the damage the spell inflicts, one-for-one. The mage can spend as much Mana as desired, though they are still limited by their Gnosis-derived Mana per turn limit.
    I'd add a Death 5 add on for ways to produce Vitae which gives it the Property to create Revenant (as per Vampire the Requiem), but through the normal means of what happens when someone who has previously had Vitae in their system dies or is exsanguinated.

    That side effect being a Lasting opt in to the fickle nature of accidental Revenant creation inherent in the Curse.

    Maybe that's too close to Archmastery, or maybe it is more similar to a side effect of a minor template, ghouldom, and thus okay. Is wolfbloodedness attainable with Spirit? I mean now it's a possible side effect of Lunacy...

        Firstly, I think this is something that should be rare, difficult and dangerous. To that end I like the health cost. I’d also add that this shouldn’t be something anyone with enough dots in the Arcanum could do - I’d charge an Arcane Experience for the privilege, or at least add the roleplaying requirement that you have to have experimented on a vampire or been taught by someone who has, kind of like the Scelestus Paradox control. I’d also add the requirement that the mage needs to have Vitae in her system, at least at some point during the learning process. I like the idea that a mage can’t replicate the curse of vampirism, but she can harness it - if she’s willing to let it touch her.

        Secondly, I would strongly argue that since vampires are fundamentally creatures of Death, manipulating them and their essential energies falls under the purview of that Arcanum. If you really wanted to hammer home that this is weird, rare magic though you could rule that it requires both Death and Life. After all, what is Vitae but the energies of Life converted into fuel for Death?
