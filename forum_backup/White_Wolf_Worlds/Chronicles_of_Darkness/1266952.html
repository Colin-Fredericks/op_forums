    Chronicles of Darkness "Revelations"

    11-13-2018, 11:00 AM
    Hello Folks,

    I bring to you the first chapter in our three part miniseries, Chronicles of Darkness – Revelations!

    In the small town of Toil, Illinois in the year 1938 all seems well until one summer day when things straight out of the Bible start to manifest themselves in stranger and eventually terrifying ways.

    https://soundcloud.com/twin-cities-by-night/episode-219-chronicles-of-darkness-revelations-chapter-1


    Chronicles of Darkness - Revelations is a three part Chronicles of Darkness game set in the White Wolf Role-Playing Games setting of the World of Darkness.

    "Revelations , is a "No Security" Horror Scenario from Hebanon Games"
