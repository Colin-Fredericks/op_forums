                                        I noticed an error in the Brazilian Highlands timeline. The Vice-royalty of Peru didn't lasted there until 1842. In practice it was expelled in the early 1600's, replaced by conflicts between Brazilian "Bandeirantes" and mostly independent Jesuits. In the late 1600's and early to middle 1700's the Portuguese crown established formal colonies all over the place and in 1750 the treatise of Madri recognized it as Brazilian territory.

                                        Among other things this makes a difference because for the Spaniards and Jesuits enslaving natives was a crime, the Jesuits particularly worked with the natives, while the Bandeirantes enslaved and massacred them with overwhelming force and violence, which would make a lot of difference in a CoD game.

                                        EDIT: The same applies for the Amazon Basin, but a little earlier due to a lack of economic interest in the region by Spain.
