                      A spirit....couldn't actually eat a Changeling? They can only "eat" other spirits because they're all made of Essence; Changelings can produce Essence (like any other living thing), but are still physical matter. So the whole mistaken identity would end pretty much as soon as the actual spirit tried to take a bite. (Like if you tried to take a bite of a delicious chocolate cake, only to discover it was made of wood)

                      As for the initial question, I think HaplessWithDice has the right of it- a newly-free Lost could plausibly stumble from the Hedge into the Shadow (or perhaps even directly from Arcadia), but likely will quickly run afoul of the native spirits. If they're lucky, they might run across some werewolves. If they're really lucky, those werewolves might be persuaded/bribed/tricked/etc. into taking the Changeling back to the physical world with them.

                      I think that might work as a nice origin story for a Lost/Forsaken crossover chronicle, where the Changeling(s) was under the thumb of a Keeper who liked to hunt, and had a secret gateway directly into the Shadow. The Lost used that portal in their escape, and fell in with the Forsaken pack on the other side.
