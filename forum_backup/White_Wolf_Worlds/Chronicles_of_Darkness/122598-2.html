                                                        Yeah, I've been doing the 10 Merit dots for all characters for years now, and it doesn't "break" anything, it just gives you a little more wiggle room.

                                                        As for why I didn't vary the terminology too much in the Promethean chapter: Do note that hunters don't use those terms, but I wanted the reader to understand the what the heck I was talking about it. I actually don't like it when a game takes a concept from another game line, and then renames it, but otherwise makes it obvious it's exactly the same. I like my WoD integrated.

                                                        Now, in-character, a hunter doesn't know "Galateid" from "shinola" unless they happen to stumble across some Promethean's written Ramble or something and find the term. The interesting thing about Prometheans is a hunter cell could theoretically kill five of them (good luck!) and never know they've fought five similar beings. So there's not going to be one simple term that hunters use to describe Prometheans, as that would imply that hunters know to categorize them in the same way.
