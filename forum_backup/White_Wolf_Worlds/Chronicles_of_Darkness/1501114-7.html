                                                Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                                                Is there anything I need to convert, to have Thaumaturge in Chronicle of Darkness?
                                                The first thing you need to do is work on what you mean by Thaumaturge. The word itself means a worker of wonders and miracles. Is that what you mean? If so, the basic book has stuff like abjuration and banishing.

                                                If you mean a Tremere esque Blood Mage, Requiem has 2 types of blood magic you can pick between, the holy Cruac and the abominable Theban Sorcery. Take either one and congrats, you're a blood mage.

                                                I think Second Sight: Third Eye has a minor templates that expands on supernatural merits called thaumaturge as well.

                                                    Originally posted by ShadowcatX View Post

                                                    The first thing you need to do is work on what you mean by Thaumaturge. The word itself means a worker of wonders and miracles. Is that what you mean? If so, the basic book has stuff like abjuration and banishing.

                                                    In which case you can have a mortal thaumaturge.

                                                    Alternatively Mummy has a system for mortal sorcery, which may or may not be powered by blood, depending on the specific rituals you learn.
