    Gotta Go Fast-Which Splat Is the Fastest, Round 2

    12-20-2022, 06:06 PM
    The last time we did this, most everything was 1st Edition, and we had some truly glorious and hilarious results, such as a Centimani abombination who goes insane while running at insane miles per hour while flailing their too manylegss behind them and a Sin-Eater fusing with military hardware and just winning the game outright. But times change, and so have the splats and the things they might exploit, so it's time to ask the age old question: Who's the gotta-go-fastiest?

    As a basic set of rules go, a) We're not interested in who can go the farthest distance, we are interested in who can traverse the most distance, so no teleportation or Beasts establishing Chambers on two different sides of the universe and then having a jaunt through their Lair or the like-we want pure mph/kmh, b) You can employ fanon and homebrew shit, but try to address canon shit first, and c) we are interested in the characters moving themselves at ludicrous speeds-you can't get in a car in and go or take advantage the make-up of your character makes them excellent rail cannon shot, but if you can fuse with a jet and go, that counts. If I think of anything else, I'll bring it up.

    So, go forth, in the name of the Sanic and the Barry Allen and the Quicksilver, and Speed Some More, Amen.

    (I hope this is useful, but more importantly I hope this is hilarious.)

          The current favorite by my estimation, courtesy of Shunned by the Moon, is a devourer werewolf with the Sun-Chaser enlightenment.

          This is, admittedly, interpretive, but assuming that "run tirelessly at the speed of the day's change" means "you can run fast enough that the sun stays in the same position in the sky," that translates as high as a little over a thousand miles per hour. Powered by cannibalism, because the Shadow doesn't do free gas.

          A close second from Deviant, without getting into narrative shenanigans with the End Stage, is that with Flight 4 (Velocity) at Scar Power 5, a transformed can move at Speed 500, or approximately 341mph; with Enhanced Speed, that doubles to about 682mph. (You could try to read the Fleetness upgrade as allowing that to multiply further to as much as 8,523mph, but that entails interpreting "overland" as inclusive of "flying through the air over land," which is kind of pushing it.)

              Something I've actually used to the point of our group considering house ruling it:

              Lightning Strike from the Beast Players Guide. This is especial good when the criteria is total distance traveled rather than pure distance. You need a fairly combat capable Beggoten (not that hard to make though), and then just pop on the Satiety expenditure power. The theoretical distance that can be traveled is literally infinite: there just needs to be enough targets spaced out appropriately. Obviously the dice could end this if you get unlucky since you need to deal damage to a target each time you move up to one.

              I got to ~750 Speed for ne turn with it once, and only because I ran out of targets, not because I failed to deal any damage. And this was in a rural area (which helped because it spread the targets out a bit as they thought it meant they had us surrounded so I could make the most out of my up to 50 yards a target zipping), but I couldn't just keep going either.

              While the dice would eventually get you, in a crowded city, if you didn't care about all the random people your smacking around for a speed boost, you could traverse a huge distance; esp. since backtracking isn't going to work against you, but instead work for you..

                      I just knew I had to have a go at this!

                      In my homebrew rules for combined arms combat, I currently have playable rules for the AIM-54C Mk. 60 Phoenix, which I established as having a range of up to 130 nautical miles when firing the missile in pulse-doppler single-target-tracking mode. It reaches 130 nautical miles in 120 turns. I went with the assumption that each turn is 3 seconds long, so 120 turns becomes 6 minutes, or 360 seconds. This works out to approximately 667 metres per second, or Speed 2000 -- for a 1960's era air to air missile. This is only slightly slower than the two thousand pound Armour Piercing projectiles fired by the US Navy Mark 7 16"/50-calibre guns which would be fired from the battleships north of 800 metres per second in WWII era configuration, for a total of Speed 2,400 -- and I have playable and functioning rules for the US Navy Mark 7 16-inch/50-calibre Guns with both the Mark 8 Armour Piercing and Mark 13 High Capacity shells.

                      In the near future, I'm planning to add the Arleigh Burke Flight III Guided Missile Destroyers, which carry RIM-161 Block II Standard Missile 3s. These missiles are a component of the Aegis Ballistic Missile Defence system and are intended to be capable of striking intermediate-ranged ballistic missiles, and make the AIM-54C Mk. 60 Phoenix look like a child's toy by comparison. If I was to stat up the RIM-161 Block II Standard Missile-3's kinetic kill vehicle, which approaches a speed of 4.5 kilometres per second, this makes anyone that can merge with machinery reach speed 13,500.

                      If I had nothing else better to do and an unlimited amount of time and committment, I would add the Ohio-Class nuclear ballistic missile submarines to Chronicles of Darkness, which carry a payload of two dozen UGM-133A Trident II (or D-5s, if you're a Brit) submarine-launched intercontinental ballistic missiles. The actual range of the UGM-133A hasn't been publicly confirmed by the United States Navy, but common consensus suggests it to have a range held to be in excess of six thousand nautical miles. After being blasted clear of the water and being taken into orbit by the booster, during the terminal phase, the Trident-II's multiple independent reentry vehicles will approach a speed in excess of eight thousand metres per second on their way to the target site, or speed 24,000 during the last few seconds leading up to a nuclear apocalypse.

                      But wait, there's more!

                      If we stipulate that the vehicle itself must be capable of being recoverable (ie: not end up being blown to pieces) and is capable of being manned, this brings us to Apollo 10, which attained a speed of 39,897 kilometres per hour on its way back from the moon, a record for the fastest manned vehicle that remains unbroken since 1969. Rounding to the nearest whole number, this is Speed thirty three thousand, two hundred forty-eight, or Speed 33,248. I don't intend to stat this vehicle, but this establishes an upper limit on "how fast" 1960's era technology could take a human being, or something that could merge with it.

                                                  I wanted to make a speedster-style character a while ago and did a lot of calculating and jiggery-pokery to find what works, which led me down a rabbit hole of finding the absolute highest speed a character could travel. With the right setup, a Promethean, Rahu Werewolf, Mage, and Mutant Deviant can combine to reach speeds of utterly insane proportions

                                                  The main assumptions for this are that everyone has access to the necessary powers, and the maximum possible levels in each relevant attribute, skill and so on. Dice rolls use every 5 dice=1 success as a base assumption, and the mage has access to the best bonuses possible as yantras. Finally, all additive bonuses apply before multicplicative bonuses. It is already so unlikely in-world it's not worth even considering, but for the hypothetical I found this quite fun.

                                                  So, the Promethean has 10 Strength, 10 Dexterity. They boost their strength with the persistent effect of the Vitality Alembic Unconquered (+10), the Metamorphosis Alembic Bestiae Faces, using the Form of the Barghest Distillation (+1), the Coporeum Alembic Motus, Exemplary Athleticism Distillation (+3) and Uncanny Athleticism Distillation (rolled after all other boosts for +18), boosting strength using Pyros for the one turn needed (+10), and then is buffed by the rahu through Totemic Empowerment (+1), Reflected Facets with Crimson Spasm (+10, 5 successes on a roll of 25) and Primal Strength (+5), and the mage slaps on a Potency 24 (I may have to recheck this but I think all the yantras and ritual casting etc. can get this far) Honing the Form, for a total of 92 Strength.
                                                  Dexterity is boosted through Exemplary Athleticism (+1), the Corporeum Alembic Charites, Athletic Grace Distillation (+1), Totemic Empowerment (+1) and another Potency 24 Honing the Form on Dexterity, for 37.
                                                  Species factor is modified by Fleet of Foot (+3), Form of the Barghest (+2), and the Metamorphosis Alembic Aptare, Procrustean Shape Distillation (+6 from 30 dice rolled) for a total of 16

                                                  All up, the promethean's additive speed is 145. This would be about 174km/h or 108mph. Already real fast, but we're about to shoot for the stars (literally) with multiplicative bonuses

                                                  So, the mutant can bestow Enhanced Speed at magnitude 5 to the promethean using Sacred Flesh. The promethean's strength is 92 for the turn we care about, so their speed using Enhanced Speed is multiplied by 92*5, getting us to 66700 speed for one turn. We multiply again by 24 for the mage's Acceleration, by 3 for the Zephyrus Alembic Swift Feet combined with Exemplary Athleticism, by 3 for Totemic Empowerment as we use the Speed Numen, and then by 2 for the Reflected Facets Predator's Unmatched Pursuit form the werewolf (some readings imply the double speed bonus can be applied multiple times, with would actually make this one either *2^4 or *2^8 depending on how the extra cost of Reflected Facets is applied to the power).
                                                  Our current speed here is 28,814,400. This is 34,577,280 km/h or 21,487,248 mph. Still within the bounds of general relativity. For now

                                                  If you don't care about controlling your momentum, you can go higher with a Velocity Control hitting the promethean at top speed for 24 potency, or *2^24 of that speed. This is well and truly breaking the laws of general relativity as we hit 483,425,412,710,400 (483.4 trillion) metres per turn, or 580,110,495,252,480 (580.1 trillion) km/h, or 360,496,206,346,309 (360.5 trillion) mph. The speed of light is "only" a bit under 1.1 million km/h. Up to the ST how our current knowledge of the speed of light will affect a character attempting to travel at this speed

                                                  Hope you enjoyed this bonkers thought experiment and the massive speed values you can technically have within the bounds of the game, and if all the rolls are in your favour or you have some dice tricks handy like rote, you could have a slightly more ridiculous speed. If your players ever think to try this, however, I suggest you remind them of things like air friction, the escape velocity of earth, and the incredible g-forces involved.
