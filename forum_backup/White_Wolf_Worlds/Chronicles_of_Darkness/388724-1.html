                Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                Is there a clarification on what we're exactly talking about for "Neolithic", since that's a period crossing some four thousand years and a lot of distinct social models.

                As you say, could be all sorts of places and times. The Neolithic is everything between the development of agriculture and the widespread use of metallurgy. It's an ocean of time, larger than the timespan of all the other Eras put together. The last known human species other than our own died out around the start of it.

                You could look at the first cities - and I have friends who are doing the research on early settlements in Syria (as much as that's possible nowadays) - you could look at the height of the Stone Circle builders across North-West Europe; Stonehenge was three or four monuments built on top of one another. You could look at what it's like in the vast spaces between, where the largest human settlement you'll ever see is a collection of huts.

                Big topic. If it wins the vote, we'll start narrowing it down.
