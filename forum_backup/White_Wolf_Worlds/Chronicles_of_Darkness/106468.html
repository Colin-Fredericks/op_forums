    PEACH: Contracts of Blood

    04-05-2014, 04:08 PM
    Some of these might step on the feet of Sorrow-Frozen Heart a bit, but I tried to make them distinct enough. Constructive criticism is requested and welcome!

    Contracts of Blood

    These contracts, forged by some of the darkest and most dread of the True Fae, deal with the visceral subjects of blood, pain, and injury. The use of these Contracts is often a painful, messy business, involving bloody displays and sacrifices. Both Ogres and Darklings possess an affinity for these Contracts. Ogres often learned them while inflicting the harsh penalties their masters required for rulebreakers, while Darklings were often forced to commit (and endure) unbearable things in order to survive their tenebrous Durances. Some of the Lost whisper about a tie between these Contracts and the mysterious blood-drinking Kindred, though no concrete connection has ever been found.
    Note: All of these contracts offer a +1 dice bonus if the Changeling has taken Lethal damage, and an additional +1 bonus if he’s suffering wound penalties.

    Scent the Dripping Red (•)
    The Changeling develops a sixth sense regarding the subject's injuries and ailments of the blood. This includes wounds that are hidden by clothing as well as internal injuries. In addition, she automatically knows if the subject suffers any poison or illness, though this Contract doesn't grant knowledge of the exact disease or toxin.
    Cost: 1 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Wyrd + Medicine
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The character tastes the subject's blood

    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: The character's blood curdles within her veins, inflicting one point of bashing damage and levying a -1 penalty to all physical actions for the rest of the scene.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: For the rest of the scene, the character instinctively knows how many health levels the subject has lost. In addition, she knows if the subject is currently suffering from any poison or disease effects, even if symptoms have not yet begun to present themselves.
    Exceptional Success: The character gains either +2 to Medicine rolls to treat the subject or reduces the called shot penalty to strike the wounded penalty by two. In addition, she learns the identity of one poison or illness the subject suffers.
    Modifier Situation
    +1 The character has tasted more than a drop of the subject's blood.
    +1 The subject is suffering at least one Lethal wound
    +1 The subject is suffering wound penalties.
    –1 The character has never tasted the subject's blood.
    –1 The subject is currently uninjured.

    Barbs of the Lash (••)
    The Changeling calls out to the blood in his enemies’ wounds, calling upon it to increase the suffering they feel. In response, the blood roils, inflicting pain and impeding action.
    Cost: 2 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Wits + Wyrd - Stamina
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The character has sincerely expressed hatred or admiration for the subject, or vice versa.

    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: The Changeling’s own blood rebels, inflicting a wound penalty of half his Wyrd to all actions for the rest of the day.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: The pain of the subject’s wounds immediately grows much more intense and distracting. If the subject has taken any wounds, she immediately increases her wound penalty by one (for example, a character not suffering any wound penalties is levied a -1 penalty, where a character who is already suffering a -3 penalty finds this increased to -4. This increased penalty lasts for the rest of the scene. Only one invocation of this contract can affect a character at once.
    Exceptional Success: The subject’s wound penalty is instead increased by the character’s Wyrd.
    Modifier Situation
    +1 The character has inflicted at least one point of Lethal damage to the subject this scene
    +1 The subject is suffering at least one Lethal wound
    +1 The subject is suffering wound penalties.
    -- The character wields a weapon which deals Lethal damage
    –1 The character wields no weapon, or a weapon which deals only Bashing damage
    –1 The subject is currently uninjured.

    An Ecstasy of Torment (•••)
    The changeling focuses on her own pain, feeling the power to be found within her own spilled blood. Thereafter, the spilling of her own blood offers the Changeling no pain - instead, it spurs her on to greater concentration of action.
    Cost: 2 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Intelligence + Wyrd
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The character pierces her skin with at least half a dozen pieces of barbed metal, which remain in place for the duration of the Contract.

    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: A flood of mismanaged pain incapacitates the Changeling, who immediately falls unconscious for the rest of the scene.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: The character no longer suffers wound penalties for the duration of the scene. Instead, if she would suffer a penalty due to injury, it is instead transmuted into a bonus. If the character has a power (such as the Iron Stamina merit or the fifth clause of this Contract) which would prevent her from suffering a wound penalty, she still receives a bonus to all actions equal to the wound penalty she would suffer, if she didn’t have that power.
    Exceptional Success: The subject’s wound penalty is instead increased by the character’s Wyrd.
    Modifier Situation
    +1 The character bears at least one point of damage which is self-inflicted.
    +1 The character marks or anoints herself with a small amount of blood when activating this
    Contract
    –1 The character possesses the Iron Stamina merit
    –1 The character is currently uninjured.

    Preach the Sanguine Word ​(••••)
    By anointing up to his Wyrd other people in his own blood and sharing a whispered word, the character introduces others to his bloody enlightenment. This allows them to gain strength from pain in the same manner as the Changeling does by using An Ecstasy of Torment.
    Cost: 3 Glamour, 1 WP, 1 point of Lethal Damage
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Wyrd
    Action: Instant and Contested. Roll the target’s Composure + Wyrd to resist (though he may choose not to do so).
    Catch: The character reopens her wounds to anoint each subject of the Contract, suffering one point of bashing damage per target beyond the first.

    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: Stigmatic tears of blood flow from the eyes of every character anointed in the invocation of this Contract, inflicting one point of Lethal damage.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: Those anointed by the character experience the effects of An Ecstasy of Torment, gaining a bonus to rolls which would otherwise be penalized due to wound penalties.
    Exceptional Success: The wound from which the character drew the blood to anoint his fellows heals surprisingly quickly. The Lethal damage paid to activate this contract is changed to a point of Bashing damage.
    Modifier Situation
    +1 At least one subject already suffers wound penalties
    +1 At least one subject already bears at least one point of Lethal damage
    -- All of the subjects suffer at least one point of Bashing damage
    –1 Per each character who is to enjoy the effects of this Contract
    –1 A subject is currently uninjured.

    Speed the Blood (•••••)
    By leashing all of the blood within her frame and forcing it to remain contained and to flow despite injury, the character ensures that she will be able to function regardless of any damage she takes. Being killed will fell her, but nothing before that will do so. Even if sliced to ribbons, she will not die to blood loss while this contract is in effect. If, at the end of the scene, the character’s health track is still filled with damage, she must immediately begin making Stamina rolls to remain conscious - and if filled with lethal damage, she begins taking additional damage every minute, per pages 173-174 of the World of Darkness rulebook.
    Cost: 4 Glamour, 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Resolve + Wyrd
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The character impales one arm with a knife or dagger as she invokes this Contract, inflicting two points of Lethal damage. This imposes a -2 penalty to rolls involving the use of that arm that none of the powers of this Contract can reduce.

    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: The character’s blood, offended at her poor showing, abandons her through every pore and orifice it can reach. Her health track is immediately filled with Lethal damage, and she begins bleeding out..
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: For the rest of the scene, the character need never make a Stamina roll to remain conscious after her final health box is filled with bashing or lethal damage. No matter how many wounds the character takes, damage alone will not cause him to fall unconscious. Once the character’s health levels are completely filled with aggravated damage, he falls unconscious and dies as normal..
    Exceptional Success: The character suffers no wound penalties for the duration of the Contract. In addition, his supernatural toughness grants him a single temporary health level.
    Modifier Situation
    +1 The character imbibes more than a mouthful of blood before invoking this contract.
    +1 There is at least 1 pint of blood strewn around the scene.
    –1 The character is uninjured
    –2 Nobody in the scene currently sports a Lethal injury

                      I'm re-reading it and there's some issues for me with the Contract. I like the concept and the basic ideas but the implementation... this is an extraordinarily weak Contract, but since you're looking for input I'll try to explain why I see it as such as best I can with some ideas that may allow some additional tinkering. This is going to be fairly harsh, but I do have some similar Contracts to point towards that may help with further fiddling.

                      -This isn't a Goblin Contract, Preach the Sanguine Wyrd should not have an additional 1L cost; anything like that should be worked into the Catch. The innate bonuses for being wounded etc. are just situational modifiers; presenting them before their Clause listings doesn't really change much.

                      -Speed of Blood is very underwhelming for a level 5 Contract despite seeming rather ooh and ahh initially; Contract of Sorrow-Frozen Heart already heavily outshines the "I'm not dead yet!" aspect with a more interesting mechanic. I'd honestly place it at a much lower rating than it is currently; being able to use it upon others is hand by... it's really rather mediocre. The extra Health level doesn't really add much (compare this to Stone 5 and what it does on top of Wound Penalties and all of its boosts; it's barely a radar blip). I'd recommend at looking at the Contract for some inspiration or tinkering. Look at Grief is Stronger than Death at level 3... it put this Clause to utter shame in my eyes.

                      Right now it's only real advantage is if you're full of Lethal Damage; Contract of Stone 5, Sorrow Frozen Heart (at 1 and 3) and others overshadow it massively in utility overall.

                      Stopping blood-loss and damage upgrading (at least in my opinion) isn't remotely a level 5 Contract worthy effect; for me that's a level 2, potentially even a level 1. If I were to suggest transcending pain and wounds, perhaps Willpower gains each time your struck and wounded could be interesting along with another effect, but I'm just brainstorming a bit there. It needs much more teeth to it to make worth the purchase; as is, if you want to see something that delays death and suffering while being a "wow, that's cool!" Clause, Sorrow-Frozen Heart 3 all the way.

                      Compare this to Instant Expertise (though far more specific on what gets a bonus the utility is higher) and similar buffing Contracts to actions; it's rather underwhelming when you can much more readily get a bigger bonus for less. Hell, look at Hearth 2; why would I want this Contract by comparison? I -may- get some actions off when on my last legs, but at that point the concern isn't Wound Penalties, it's that next incoming blow that may put me down especially when likely to be fighting to remain conscious if so much as plinked.

                      If I were to suggest an alternative it would be one where you inflicted your current wound penalties upon your attackers, sharing your suffering with them to offset your own, and perhaps looking at the pain = invigoration at another level. Honestly at that point them getting hampers and me getting the hell out of there is a much better idea if that wounded, otherwise it's more of a "Dying blow" power. If that's the cause, Sorrow-Frozen Heart 4: Remorseless Strike does a far better job of reflecting that.

                      -Barbs of the Lash is extremely over-costed for what it does. In practice it's a rather general -1 penalty for a roll and 2 Glamour. A beefy Exceptional Success aspect really means little in actual gameplay; they're not reliable and when Contracts (and other nWoD powers) rely on that to be what you're -actually- wanting, what you get is a weak Clause that -might- make a meaningful impact if you're lucky that barely does anything when it consistently works. I wouldn't take it as presented, it's just not worth the Glamour investment nor the level when I have better alternatives.

                      If it could temporarily boost wound penalties like, half my Wyrd rounded up for a wounded individual, or inflict that kind of suffering for a short time I'd consider it even if it only lasted a number of turns. The additional -1 penalty in of itself just doesn't cut the mustard.

                      Level 1 isn't bad though it has no real explanation as to how it allows (or why for that matter) you to make called shots more effectively. It needs a better explanation, and even then it still suffers from "the real utility is if you get an Exceptional Success" syndrome. As I see it what you have is a very weak set of Clauses that primarily play upon the subject or user being near death to get fairly minimal advantages. Conceptually they ideas look rather good but mechanically I wouldn't buy the Contract at all.

                      Look at some of the other Contracts in print that already do similar effects mechanically or conceptually. Consider folding some of the Exceptional Success effects into the basic success effects. Take a look at combat Tilts as of GMC (and Conditions; I actually have a Bleeding Out Condition to reflect staked living targets kicking around that might be applicable). Though not a great Gift List, I also suggest looking at the Blood gifts from W:tF for some additional inspiration; you're on a good track but you really need to make sure the Contract powers are something people would actually purchase rather than worrying about balance so much it neuters the set. They can be trimmed down later if something seems too much.

                          Fair. I'll talk through my thoughts, and maybe you can present some opinions.

                          This contract list began as "Ogres and Darklings are one Affinity down to the rest of the Seemings." I decided that the best themes to link the two items were blood, pain, and injury, and that's where this contract list came from.

                          I'm pretty married to the *concept* of the first clause - "sense others' injuries," both because it suits the "first clause offers info" pattern lots of others follow and because I like that a mostly-offensive, mostly dreadful Contract list has a first clause that's equally, or even more, useful for a healer. My initial concept combined the basic and exceptional success effects, granting the bonuses as well as the general status. I was advised to split it up, though I'm certainly open to combining them again.

                          I wasn't sure about Barbs of the Lash. I may just wind up wrapping it and the other wound penalty manipulators into a single clause which lets you inflict, inflame, or ease wound penalties by half your Wyrd (or your full Wyrd on an X-Sux). Does that sound more apropos?

                          An Ecstasy of Torment was a throw-it-in clause borrowed from a concept out of another game entirely. I'm okay with replacing it, though I do want to include some sort of "gaining power from injury" ability, whether it be the user's own or someone else's. It could be as simple as the "take willpower from damage" idea, but I feel like there could be more hooks to it. The fourth clause was just an outgrowth of it.

                          The fifth clause is the other one that I'm very fond of in *concept.* I want those with five dots in this contract to be terrifying, inexorable figures who *just. won't. die.* On the other hand, the thing can't be an instant "I win" clause, because the bad guys can have it too. The current incarnation was basically just a port over of the Promethean powers of toughness. Maybe there's a way to tie it into the tale of Koschei the Deathless, who stored his life in a secret place and couldn't be killed as long as it was hidden? Say that the character hides his life in an object, and can't be killed while it's there, but if it's broken he suffers all the damage he'd have taken while it was out?

                          Now that I think of it, I want at least one more power that involves the drinking of blood as part of the contract. Could be something to gain Glamour or Willpower, heightening the "does this tie to the Kindred?" question, or it could have some other effect.

                              Some additional musings on it, more brainstorming than anything (in the time I have today):

                              For Barbs of the Lash: seems appropriate, you could also do something success based (Half your successes rounding up) though I think half one's Wyrd does still run into the issue where consistently until you're Wyrd 3 + it barely does anything beyond mimicking the first dot of a merit or a trivial penalty on others. Full Wyrd is likely a tad much; I always look at what happens when you have potent Wyrd PCs and NPCs, so perhaps just allowing the alleviation or increase at half successes? It'd turned into a resisted roll with a penalty of their Stamina at that point most likely, but I think that makes the use fair. I do like the idea it could alleviate pain as readily as inflict it.

                              The real kicker is duration; if it lasts turns, it's handy in combat but rarely outside to have a persistently weakened foe or keeping yourself clear headed, in which case it might be better set up as a Tilt, perhaps with a -2 to -3 penalty to actions from burning agony on others. It'd cease after combat, though in that instance it'd be better off as a Contested roll. If it lasts a scene, that range of penalties (-2 to -3) is fair. If it's something beefier and success based (potentially getting very high penalties) I'd keep it per turn and Resisted.

                              On stabilization; Level 5 did that before with your power, that's an effect that it should allow at level 1 or a higher level; for living targets who bleed out, go into shock and upgrade Lethal to Agg, I'd -love- to have a power as a Changeling accessible I could do on myself, or, hell, even to others that would stop or delay that, even giving bonuses to Medicine rolls to help them. Eternal Spring has raw healing ability, but something that consistently gives stabilization effects would be nice at low levels.

                              On level 5: I'm not sure if you have access to what Sorrow Frozen Heart does, so here's a quick post of it (hopefully without stepping on any toes): Grief is Stronger than Death

                              The Changeling is so immersed in his own sorrow that her injuries are irrelevant.
                              Prerequisite: Mantle (Winter) 2 or Court Goodwill (Winter) 4
                              Cost: 2 Glamour
                              Dice Pool: Resolve + Survival + Mantle (Winter)
                              Action: Instant
                              Catch: The character contemptuously tosses a chunk of cold iron to the ground.
                              Dramatic Failure: The character's attention snaps back to her body, leaving her vulnerable and clumsy. Add a die to the next attack directed against her during the scene.
                              Failure: The character does not benefit from the clause.
                              Success: The character staves off the effects of injury. When she is struck in combat, tally the damage (any abilities or equipment that would affect it apply) and leave it aside for the rest of the scene. The character can "ignore" damage equal to her successes. Do not apply it to the character's health levels. Once the scene ends or she runs out of successes, inflict all of the damage that she "ignored" in the order in which it was inflicted. This takes effect over successive turns, effectively "paying back" the wounds that were delayed.
                              Exceptional Success: As a standard success, but the character also benefits from one point of armor until the power's effects end.


                              The other mechanic that works along those lines is similar to Resilience; damage reduction (though not armor since that can be bypassed and doesn't really play up the walking hamburger mechanic). You still appear horribly wounded, but it's more cosmetic and heals with active effort. If Changelings had a taste of that (definitely not a full bore resilience, but a temporary version of the passive effect may be valid) where the visuals remained until healed at the standard rate, that might be interesting. Temporary health levels work but those are fairly nice though they aren't my personal preference. If visually the character looks as mangled as they should be, but the Clause downgrades damage, that's another potential; walking bbq but what should be Aggravated treated as Lethal etc. without falling unconscious at 5 or something akin to that may be a good alternative..

                                  Okay. A revision/replacement for Barbs of the Lash. I don't have the GMC update, so I'm not sure about all the Tilt and Condition talk, but I tried to take your advice into account.

                                  Stir the Ruddy Tide (••)
                                  The Changeling calls out to the blood in nearby wounds, invoking it either to increase the suffering they cause or to twist it into delicious pleasure.
                                  Cost: 2 Glamour
                                  Dice Pool: Wits + Wyrd (vs Stamina + Wyrd for any who resist)
                                  Action: Instant and Contested
                                  Catch: The character has sincerely expressed hatred or admiration for the subject, or vice versa.

                                  Roll Results
                                  Dramatic Failure: The Changeling’s own blood rebels, inflicting a wound penalty equal to his Wyrd to all actions for the rest of the day.
                                  Failure: Nothing happens.
                                  Success: The character may choose whether to ease the target's pain or to inflame it. Either effect lasts for a scene.
                                  If he chooses to ease the pain, then the subject's wound penalty is reduced by half of the Changeling's successes, rounded up. If the successes reduce the wound penalty beyond zero, then these additional successes become a bonus to all the subject's rolls, though this bonus is capped at her previously-existing wound penalty. If the subject is bleeding to death, her bleeding immediately ceases, stabilizing her (although no healing takes place and further injury can open new wounds, which will bleed as normal).
                                  If the character increases the pain, then his subject's wound penalty is immediately increased by half the changeling's successes, rounding up. If the subject was not previously suffering a wound penalty, one is inflicted regardless of injury, as her own blood roils and curdles within her. This can increase the wound penalty beyond the standard -3 limit, though the total wound penalty cannot exceed -6.
                                  Exceptional Success: The subject immediately heals one point of Lethal or Bashing damage if the Changeling was soothing her wounds, or suffers one point of Lethal damage if the Changeling was further exacerbating them.
                                  Modifier Situation
                                  +1 The character has inflicted at least one point of Lethal damage to the subject this scene
                                  +1 The subject is suffering at least one Lethal wound
                                  +1 The subject is suffering wound penalties.
                                  -- The character wields a weapon which deals Lethal damage
                                  –1 The character wields no weapon, or a weapon which deals only Bashing damage
                                  –1 The subject is currently uninjured.
