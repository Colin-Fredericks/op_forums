        Beneficent One.

        I have spent long and hard at the translations of the texts you bequeathed unto me. While I have spent all that was needed to fully clear the text of any errors, I plead your forgiveness; there is almost certainly problems in this English version. Not because of lack of competence on the efforts of my allies in academia, but because I am sure a significant portion of these books are not Medieval Arabic. The author seems to have either invented or miswrote several words, and both theories have large problems that cannot be accounted for. The invented words don't generally follow Arabic conventions, but they seem awfully consistent for misprints.

        Also, there's something else; he mentions your name.

        The Travels of Abd-al-Hazred

        ...It was at that point that I finally arrived at the final truth that had so long troubled me. The vision marked my form of humanity as small because it was small, a tiny part of the whole of Creation, A beauty eclipsed by grander beauty, a meaning lost in meaning. It was always a foolish thing to assume "the image of god" was two arms, two legs, and a mind to somehow confront the difficulties posed by such a form that has neither the strength of a beast or the magic of smokeless fire. No, Creation was far greater than me, the Earth, and even the stars in the Heavens themselves. And I knew from my vision, the beholding of the ocean daughter's soul, that even the fragment of the image that those humans embody was omniferrous and flexible, with even the capacity of becoming as Bahamut itself, a living foundation of the world.

        But the limitations posed by humanity, I realized, were still vexing my ultimate goal. I realized my initial scholarship was naive, in and of itself; it is not just the hadith that are flawed, a thousand canons written by a million different flawed humans, it was that humans are flawed to begin with. How was I supposed to truly behold the mind and image of Allah if there was no being on Earth in my learned experience that even begins to approach the complexity of even this minute, forgettable corner of the True World? No, I would have to approach the zenith of scholarship not as a philosopher alone, but an engineer and architect. I would have to understand the foundations of Creation to understand where to even begin in my explorations of its palace, and from there understand and behold the Builder of the palace.

        What better place to begin than by seeing the world as Bahamut does, from the foundations itself and eternally looking upwards? And what better way to become as Bahamut than through interrogation and mimicry of its descendants?

                And I have something to contribute myself


                Dagonites

                [Picture: The Symbol of Dagon: A wooden carved statue representing a bust of a man. His cheeks are hollow and his eyes are bulging like a fish’s, but he has a full Sumerian-style beard. The bust has claw marks imprinted on it.]

                [Picture: Central. A young woman with shoulder-length black hair. She wears a light gray business suit with a white baby-tee underneath. In her left hand is a pair of glasses, half-folded. In her extended right hand, she is holding a practical butcher’s knife, blade first as if she was offering it to someone. ]

                Theology

                The world is a raging tempest and man is a drop of water before the storm. Disease, famine or an invading army could crush anybody without even noticing, and so men tell themselves it is otherwise. If I pray tonight the crops will have rain tomorrow. If I burn these incenses my daughter will be healed. If I sacrifice my firstborn son the gods shall grant me victory in battle. The children of Dagon hear their prayers, and they shiver in pleasure.

                More than any other Strain, the Dagonites revel in their divine power. Their divine instincts whisper to them, telling them to open their eyes and see the naked truth of a world where the strong eats the weak. Telling them that nobody can depend upon morality or justice, that if such things even exist they it is only because someone had the power to back them up. Most of all it tells them that they are gods, they alone have true power. And yet their human mind looks out and sees differently, they see the need to hide from a mighty humanity, they see weak men thriving under the protection of the law. Every Dagonite resolves this contradiction in their own way, or is torn apart by it.

                Their cults reflect the same truths. The Wake imposes upon their Beloved, not a feeling of powerless, but an understanding of how powerlessness they always were. To a Dagonite's cultist submission to a Leviathan is, paradoxically, about seizing control. If they could only present their god with the correct offerings they could control the rains. If they could only become high priest then surely they would have the power to look any king in the eye and say no. A Dagonite's cult is a vicious cesspool of backstabbing, power-grabbing, and competition for the God's favour. Dagonites love every minute of it.

                Until the cult's turmoil attracts the wrong attention and the Dagonite realises that even a demigod is powerless before a true deity.

                Morphology

                The line of Dagon is traditionally considered as the meeting point between primordial muck and higher life. As the heirs to the Progenitors’ Fecundity no Dagonite is truly a single creature. The Strain is inhabited by or swims in symbiosis with lesser creatures which are, in fact, simply another part of the Leviathan's body. The most common Dagoinite traits are those of the simple fish, a clade which has multiplied beyond all others in the sea. Bulging eyes, gaping mouths and slimy scales, a fish's most unsettling traits, can be found in abundance. A Dagonites' symbiotes typically echo barnacles, coral or maritime plantlife but more mobile creates are far from unknown. At their extreme a Dagonite may be an entire school with no primary body and some of the most ancient Dagonites are entire ecosystems that travel the ocean assimilating everything in their path.

                The most common manifestations of Predation among Dagon's get are the most insidious. Paralytic toxins and incapacitating venoms. Needle thin barbed spines. Weapons to disable and control. Anemone cling to their bodies dripping with venom or a cloud of thin tentacles spreads around them laced with toxins. Those who favour the Vestige of Elements typically express it through their Fecundity, pushing it to greater extremes until the ocean around them is more microscopic life than water. Awareness touches Dagonites lightly, and typically manifests as an exaggeration of fish traits. To the Strain a Fish's bulging eyes are a icon of confidence and power, able to see the harsh truths of the world without blinking.


                Progenitor

                Dagonites claim their ancestry in the Progenitor Dagon. An endless wellspring of life, Dagon's womb contained the power of creation itself and his rule held dominion over the harvests, the rains, but also over rituals and religion. It was Dagon who taught mankind rituals and observances to control the a tempestuous world and so his get honour him as the Hierarch, as Dagon's rituals were free of the theology and covenants that bind mankind's gods Dagon is also honoured as the Arch-Heretic. Modern Dagonites boast that their ancestor was the last Progenitor to fall before Marduk and the rituals he bequeathed to the Tribe are the weapons that will one day reclaim the heavens.

                While Dagon is honoured by his descendants, compared to the other Strains relatively few Dagonites relate to Dagon as a deity. The typical Dagonite believes Dagon's death was final and honour their ancestor as a paragon and a culture hero. He is the example that shows how a god should relate to his flock and an inspiration for those Leviathans who still fight their ancestral war. As a consequence Dagon is the progenitor with the most consistent depiction among the Tribe's art. He is a man with bulging fish like eyes, a full Sumerian beard and a fish's tail; his belly swollen in pregnancy.

                Metamorphosis

                Transition begins with the flesh. The first a Dagonite notices of his heritage is some physical affliction. These are impermanent and fluid markers, attempting to peal away a patch of scales will cause them to melt into flesh. Just as it seems like the fledgeling may come to terms with their condition the effects spread outwards. Attempting to remove an affliction causes it to burst in blood and small sea life. Small animals bearing outlandish mutations follow the Leviathan in slavish loyalty, the Wake begins during this time. Many Dagonites have to question where they end and other creatures begin. Who is an individual with their own thoughts and desires and who is merely a part of them to be used as one uses their own arm? The creatures in her blood? The loyal animals who follow her around? Her Beloved? Everyone?

                Due to the insular and cultic nature of many established Dagonite families, the arrival of a new Leviathan is typically responded too with well rehearsed rituals. This can provide a sense of stability both to the young Leviathan and her mortal family, however depending on the nature of these rituals some Dagonites honestly would have preferred naked panic. Away from cults Dagon's blood seems to lead towards no particular reaction.

                As his puberty progresses a Dagonite feels a growing instinct to emigrate, and for good reason. The stereotype says they tire of family and seek unclaimed lands to conquer, and while there is some truth in this they are in fact instinctively fleeing their own blood. Should the Dagonites remain, whether out of familial loyalty, a deliberate (and usually successful) attempt to breed more Leviathans or some other reason, then the touch of Dagon's blood continues to strengthen. Whether mystical effect of Dagon's blood or just the result of so many Leviathans possessing the powers of Fecundity in the gene pool, the family homeland is soon plagued with feral Lahmasu and mass graves filled with the young victims of horrible diseases.


                Ecology

                Family

                The Dagonite Strain is cited as the most fruitful but least nurturing of the Strains - the bloodline begets miscarriages, stillborn and birth defects, but also twins, triplets and even larger clutches. The social dynamics of a Dagonite’s family are as unhealthy as the genetics and plagued with infighting, with members constantly disowning their blood and setting off into the wider world. It takes powerful social factors to keep a Dagonite family together, and that usually requires a cult.

                Thus Dagon’s family tree takes shape. Scattered around the world are the primary bloodlines, cults where the Dagon’s heritage is most concentrated, and most numerous for those cults don’t have a social expectation that you’d stop after a few kids. Surrounding those cults is a enormous but thin web of exiles, refugees and disowned relatives. Those distant cousins are typically assimilated into mortal culture, most are unaware of their heritage and aside from a tendency to large dysfunctional families and genetic disorders they are indistinguishable from regular anyone else. As the generations progress some cults fall, others are formed, and the distant cousins marry each other or get indoctrinated into cults just a little too often to be chance. When enough different Dagonite bloodlines meet new Leviathans are born.

                Perhaps the most fundamental question you can ask a Dagonite is “were you born into a cult”? A Dagonite born into a cult is surrounded with knowledge of the Tribe and endlessly pliable followers, but the size of a Dagonite cult means they may face immediate competition from other young Leviathans. In addition they are unlikely to have had any positive role models. Due to the pyramid shape of most cults, the odds are that a Dagonite grew up on the bottom rungs, envying the elder's’ power and possibly fearing them too. Upon their first metamorphosis power is thrust upon them without any of the experiences they might have gained through climbing the ladder.

                In contrast a Dagonite born outside a cult is probably born into a life of conflict, if only typical family drama, but this conflict is tempered by immersion in mortal society which while flawed is superior to the Tribes in every way. His family is unlikely to have much knowledge of the tribe. If he is lucky they might have a heirloom in the attic, or perhaps he overheard a few mentions of the bad old days before his mother walked out on the family, at best the family may have uncovered some genuine knowledge after investigating Hybrids or Lahamsu children.

                Reproduction

                In it's way Dagon's blood is the one that makes the most sense to modern understandings of genetics. While Leviathans of any Strains are most commonly born to bloodlines that have given birth to Leviathans before this tendency is most pronounced in the Dagonites. The more Dagonites in your recent ancestry, the more Lahmasu in your immediate family, the more likely you are to be a Leviathan yourself.

                Because the blood of Dagon weighs genetics heavily, if one Leviathan begins his transition it's likely that others will soon follow. As many as five or six may appear, typically among siblings and first cousins over two or three generations. While this period can be extended even further it stereotypically ends when the young Leviathans yearn for control and power and leave their birthplace to forge their own petty kingdoms. The family splits, new converts are brought into the fold and the bloodline thins for a time.

                Lahmasu

                The Dagonites lay claim to the Lahmasu Abuu, or "fathers" (singular Abum, often read "Patriarchs"). The bloodline is distinguished by both grotesque fertility and the appearance of overlarge eyes, scales, and similar piscine features as the blood of Dagon becomes more pronounced - visual markers that betray the "purity" of a bloodline and signify, in the minds of the Lahmasu, greater connection to the revered Tribe.

                Abuu share much of the same instinctual understanding of power as their Leviathan cousins, though as Lahmasu their instincts are quieter and easier to ignore. When an Abbu listens his instincts tell the Dagonite philosophy from a different angle. While a Dagonite instinctively feels that morality and justice only exist at the sufferance of those with power, an Abuu instinctively feels that somebody, themselves, needs to enforce morality. However the Abuu share the Dagonite’s instinctual belief that only divine beings possess power, and Lahmasu are barely divine. At their best they are vissers and priests who help Leviathans use their powers productively or pios priests in humanities churches. At their worst, and under the Wake Abuu are usually at their worst, they are scheming would be puppet masters. Either way Abuu are driven to size power and use it to create a society for their people.

                Vestiges

                Fecundity, Elements, Predation, Sanctity

                Symbols and Associations

                The Sea of Harkand, the Cilician Sea, the vice of Pride.

                Concepts

                Ubermensch, Contract lawyer, backwoods patriarch, small-town mayor, preacher, head cheerleader, militia leader

                Quote

                "When you put your trust in me, that makes me responsible for what you do. I know you did it out of love, and I know you regret it now. But what happened is still inexcusable. And I’m sorry, I really am, but I can’t trust that it won’t happen again. For what it’s worth, I will miss you."

                And this is just me having a bit of fun, it probably won't go in the final product.

                Leviathan's like having Beasts around, both instinctively understand the importance of family and you cannot overestimate how useful it is to have someone you can tell to Honour Thy Father and take a middle management role in the cult without screwing it up. Beasts in turn appreciate how easily it is to hold a family dinner when all you need is a bathtub full of water, and hey, if you get powers through kinship who wouldn't want to be kin with gods?

                However Beasts are not quite immune to the Wake. The instinctitual fear and subservience affects them too, but the wakes effect is most pronounced upn the Primordial Dream. So long as a Beasts Lair is at least two dots lower than a Leviathan's Sheol then they add the traits "ocean" and "stormy" to their Lair. A Minatour standing next to a Leviathan may find his maze is knee deep in sea water and filled with heavy wind. If the Lair already had one of those traits it will not duplicate or get double power; however the Leviathans presence will always provide sufficient resonance to draw the Lair forth.

                One thing a Beast should never do is invite a Leviathan into his own Lair. The Primordial Dream is mankind's, not the Tribes. Leviathans have their own, the Rift, and a Beast who brings a Leviathan into their own Lair is trying to contain the entire Rift within a handful of chambers. A Beast who even attempts such a thing is instantly aware what is happening, if they don't change directions then the Rift will burst into the Lair like a tidal wave, destroying it utterly and then pouring forth into the primordial dream. If left unchecked the leak would eventially prove be catastrophic for mankind and worse for the Tribe - but powerful Conspiracies and even Gods would notice the collective mindscapes of two Tribes at war.

                    Oceaneads

                    Who am I? Am I more than a face in the crowd? A cog in the machine? What makes me special?

                    Perhaps it is the god you follow. She named her chosen people, and you are one of them. You wear the icons, you sing the praises, and that's why you're more than the unwashed masses. Perhaps you even live up to her teachings.

                    It's not that Oceaneads see themselves as teachers or leaders, the best of them do but this is their human self making the best of far more primordial instincts. Deep down Oceaneads just like control. There's something inside each one that sees how desperately mortals clinging to them for their identity and loves it. Some Oceaneads enjoy tormenting their beloved, teaching them contradictory beliefs and watching the fallout or asking them to them violate their sense of self, the parts of it that don't come from associating with her. Most enjoy the fact they don't have to, their instincts are for control, not sadism, and watching cultists desperately trying to please her provides satisfaction.

                    The cultists that follow an Oceanead are inflicted with absolute knowledge of their own insignificance by her Wake. In response they heap praise upon their deity. They place her on a pedestal as a symbol of everything they are not and hope some of her glory will reflect upon them. Oceneads find themselves surrounded by fawning sycophants who constantly wage passive aggressive wars for the Leviathan's attention and favour, most Oceaneads are amused by how pathetic it is.

                    Morphology

                    Oceaneads are renowned among the tribe for their grace and beauty. Their forms draw from the most vibrant and colourful of sealife, traits taken from dolphins and tropical fish can be found in abundance. Other Oceaneads reflect the majesty of whales or mythical creatures: Aquatic dragons and sea serpents with a regal continence and shining scales. Most strikingly the blood of Oceanus can retain human characteristics, some Oceaneads are essentially mermaids even in their most divine form. An Oceanead's body may extend beyond the biological with halos or aura's that display the Strain's revered Sanctity, but this is only a minority and such Leviathans are no greater than their unadorned cousins, no matter how much they boast otherwise.

                    When Oceaneads inherit the Vestige of Predation they do so with elegance. Their claws are sharp and precise weapons in gleaming colours, more a rapier than broadsword. Their toxins and venoms are resplendent, as is the warning colours that sometimes manifest upon their forms. Their smile becomes even more alluring with a mouth full of elegant fangs. The vestigate of Might harkens to the Greek Gods of old with large yet tasteful muscles. In contrast the Vestiege of Elements rarely has visible manifestations, when it does it is a halo or aura that is either formed of the elements itself or visible only by disrupting the elements which pass through it.

                    Progenitor

                    The Oceanids reverar Oceanus as their ancestor. The king of the seas, the rivers, and the rightful ruler of the other Progenitors. That last one is hotly contested by the other Strains, but for all the churn and uncertainty within the Tribe’s mythology the image of Oceanus as a ruler shows up with remarkable consistency - perhaps merely a commentary of his descendants’ lust for power.

                    In person Oceanus was said to be a terrible being, who demanded unwavering loyalty and would remake the minds of entire nations if denied his due or turn families against each other in a night of blood and madness to make examples. Modern Oceaneads see this as an lesson, a parable of how far the tribe has fallen, if only because the ones who see Oceanus as an aspirational figure rarely survive long

                    Metamorphosis

                    It starts with a feeling, one day a new Oceanid wakes up to the sound of her heart pounding in her chest. Her palms are sweaty, adrenaline is pumping and she feels like queen of the world. At this early stage her goals are unchanged, merely pursued with greater confidence and, with a small measure of her inheritance, fulfilled to satisfaction. If she is not scared by hints of the supernatural behind her successes she may even enjoy it for a while. This cannot last, a young Oceanid's powers grow to meet each challenge. As her abilities becomes increasingly uncontrolled, she must confront her greatest challenge: mastering her own abilities and the quest for Tranquillity.

                    Oceanids are unique in that their transition affects not just them but their close (both genealogically and geographically) family as well. These changes are permanent and the results are among the most healthy, and often physically human, Pelopsids to be found. Unsurprisingly this can be a time of great expansion, metaphorically as members rise in wealth and status, but also literally with successful courtships and newborn babies.

                    Ecology

                    Family

                    An Oceanead instinctively sees their family as a regal bloodline and between their instincts and their Progenitors inheritance they usually get one. Typically an Oceanead begins with a large family which is then paired down by internal conflict until few members remain. The Strain is not known for voracious breading, at least not by the tribe's standard, but even an Oceanead's relatives are blessed with easy access to relationships and physically healthy children, Oceanus' inheritance runs towards unhealthy minds within sound bodies. Then the joyous early years give way to times of strife and hardship as a family full of narcissistic and conflict prone personalities begin their inevitable infighting. As the casualties rise and factions emerge the Oceanead gets the aristocratic bloodline she desired, with all the worst traits that implies.

                    Oceaneads tend to be involved with their families, and it is the Leviathan's intervention that keeps the family from falling apart. Her presence give the family something to fight over and keeps child services away. Typically the prize is simply the Oceanead's favour, made irresistible by her Wake, but a successful Leviathan has no shortage of more material rewards to bestow. By the time things get really bad, when patriarchs or matriarchs start culling relatives to keep their branch pure and pleasing to their deity, the family is often so far removed from mortal society that assimilation is unthinkable and the conflicts will last long past the Leviathan's departure or demise. Such families depend upon a new Leviathan's arrival to bolster their numbers otherwise the conflict will inevitably drive them to extinction.

                    For all that a childhood in an dynastic family is awful, the experience can provide a Leviathan with a wealth of useful skills and resources just as it robs them of a healthy human childhood which they might use to remember morality and humanity. The infighting can teach an Oceanead much about politics and family conflicts that will prove useful in similar conflicts with her fellow Leviathans, while the family's aristocratic self image means that Oceanead families are among the most likely to preserve Hairlooms and information about their lineage which can provide a great boon to a Leviathan who delves into her history. Inevitably people will seek to escape unhealthy dynamics and a Leviathan born away from the conflict will often find that the topics older relatives won't talk about are just as telling. Oceanead families rarely truely escape their past, being apart from it will define generations as surely as being a part of it would have done, though less negatively, and the main dynasties are still there waiting for a Leviathan to come asking for the family tree.

                    Reproduction

                    Chronologically the lasting Oceanid bloodlines are among the most reliable for the tribe. Within a family the number of generations between Leviathans remains constant to within a few generations. The Strain as a whole, however, does not have a set timetable and even within a family the period may slowly change over time, increasing or decreasing by a few generations every cycle.

                    While the Oceanids can reliably tell when their inheritance will next be bestowed knowing where it will appear is a lot harder. A new Oceanid can be born to almost any direct descendent of a previous Oceanid. The only noticeable pattern is that all the Oceanids from a single cycle, typically just one or two, are roughly the same age. It's even possible for the inheritance to permanently move away from the family through the line of an illegitimate child or a single family member who cuts his ties; often this causes either celebration or a frenzy of family reunions as the family tries to find and reforge ties with the newborn Leviathan before their inheritance is forever lost.

                    Lahmasu

                    The Oceanids call their Lahmasu the Pelopsids. A Pelopsid tends to preserve the grace and beauty of their Leviathan ancestors but without any of the tact or social charm. Physically the Pelopsids are the most likely to wear their heritage lightly, the result is typically “exotic beauty” rather than “hideous mutant”. When they don’t look human a Pelopsid is still going to look good, colourful scales are in, slime and gaping mouths is out. Sometimes a Pelopsid is both at once, if a Leviathan meets an actual mermaid, it’s probably a Pelopsid.

                    This outer beauty is not reflected internally, or at least not by the Strain’s instinctual nature. Oceaneads possess an instinctual sociopathy and a lust for control. Pelopsids just have the sociopathy. They lack the talent for manipulation and so many Pelopsids simply resort to base brutality or mental domination.

                    In a Leviathan’s Cults the Pelopsid’s psychic abilities and the ability to walk among humanity make the Strain useful specialists for subtle work, while the Strain’s tendency towards Might and Predation means no one should underestimate a Pelopsid’s use as front line combatants. However Leviathan’s often use Pelopsid’s as trophies or ornamentation, a role most Pelopsids find innately unsatisfying and stressful.

                    Vestiges

                    Sanctity, Elements, Might, Predation

                    Symbols and Associations

                    The Sea of Fars, the Ionian Sea, the vice of Lust.

                    Concepts
                    Club diva, big man on campus, queen of the suburbs, reclusive CEO, bright young son of a political dynasty

                    Quote
                    "People love beaches, perhaps now more than ever. You know why? Because there’s no roof. You’re totally exposed – the more you’re naked, the more you’re at ease. It’s the place in the world where you’re the most defenseless. Don’t you think it’s interesting that everyone rushes there?"
