        Not to threadjack, but a similar exercise I find interesting is to figure out what a band composed entirely of one supernatural type would be like. For instance, I love the idea of an all-vampire band with ghouled roadies and a huge herd of groupies. I like to think of them as goth, but chances are they’d have a pretty acclectic sound, with influences from throughout music history. Or a werewolf pack that takes the form of a band, their totem being a spirit of rock.

                    Werewolf is a Storm Lord of the Gibbous Moon auspice (I honestly can never remember their names in WtF), who performs bare chested, or at minimum in a vest, so as to fully show off his ripped physique, killer abs and tribal tattoos. With his long flowing hair and dark broody eyes he absolutely oozes dangerous masculinity to such a degree that anyone even slightly attracted to men goes crazy seeing him on stage.
