    Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
    Vent0: I like Finders Keepers, Seance and Fax. I believe Forge The Legend could done through a Gadget containing Show Of Power or Urban Legend.
    Probably. I just thought to codify something about making faux "Relics". Easy copy for something from folklore, but inherently fragile due to the fact it is a knockoff. A Gadget is inherently more stable. <shrug>

    Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
    Miracle sounds iffy. That kind of power seems pretty well covered by Pacts, and its part of the fun to roleplay your Demon buying an debilitating illness or inconvenient relationship to increase their Cover. It's one of the ways they get a better understanding of what mortals go through, even if it's not in a compassionate manner.
    I know. Pacts are by far more efficient and less Compromising. The idea for Miracle was for more immediate relief from Conditions - if your agent is Frightened, or Sick, you might need them operational now, not after you've had time for a quick contract. Pacts also only cover Persistent Conditions and Tilts, while this can remove more transitory ones. It can also be used offensively to strip beneficial Conditions like Informed. Not all miracles are beneficial.

    Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
    Banish sounds like it should be temporary, even with an Exceptional Success. The Demon could specify that a group of people are protected (no need to limit the group's size by Primum, just use ST fiat), while others can be affected by the Tilt in a surprise tactic, like how Moses parted the Red Sea and then closed it to drown the Egyptians once the Israelites had crossed. We already have Exploits with names like Sermon, Affliction and Rain Of Blood, so why not go ahead and call this one Parting The Red Sea?
    Banish is meant for more: the area is on fire (Inferno Tilt), let's remove that for a bit so we don't fry. If the surrounding area is still burning, it will restart once Banish expires. Again, it's more about temporary breathing room.

    Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
    I like your maneuvers for Phoenix Pattern, except the last one. I know you tried to live up to the Style's name, but Going Loud and keeping even a partial Cover sounds like having your cake and eating it. It also takes away the incentive for Demons to build burner Covers in case they need to Go Loud, thus opening another way to investigate them. It occurred to me that a Style that improves Going Loud and one for optimizing the regular Demonic Form should be separate. They are quite distinct conceptually: the first is about sheer power, but the other is more about minimizing the risks of Aetheric Resonance/Compromise.
    Cover reclamation was actually the whole point, but I've wrestled with how useful to make it. I might cap the reformed Cover at the level of the "spent" Cover or Primum, whichever is lower - that way you can't shred a burner Cover and get more than you spent. Another option is giving you Cover Experiences, instead of full Dots (meaning at most you can whip up a Cover 3 Patch-Job post throw-down). The idea is to make Going Loud a bit more attractive as an option, especially for higher Primum Demons who get less benefit from it as they grow in power. Going Loud still inflicts a fairly major Glitch, and leaves you Hunted for at least a bit of time (so you aren't just Going Loud to win arguments). And even with this as stated, you are losing any Cover-dependent Merits, since you are making a new Cover after losing the one you burned.

                        Supply and Demand (Mundane)

                        In their previous existences as angels, Psychopomps often required an intimate knowledge of the flows of the resources and commodities that their Infrastructure demanded. This Embed calls upon the vestiges of that ability, allowing the demon to extrapolate from an object's current location and interactions with the world around it where it came from and where it's going.
                        Dice Pool: Wits + Investigation
                        Action: Instant
                        Roll Results:
                        Dramatic Failure: The demon not only fails to divine the origins or destination of the object he is examining, he disconnects himself with the flow of causality, imposing a -3 penalty to all rolls involving precision, such as called shots in combat, lockpicking and so on.
                        Failure: The demon fails to divine the object's origins or destination. He may try again with a cumulative -1 penalty for every failed attempt.
                        Success: The demon touches and object and receives instinctive knowledge of either its intended next destination or its last destination. This will only reveal where it is intended to go and will not predict accidents or unplanned detours. If it is not intended to move, the demon will feel only its last destination. This Embed can function on living creatures, but is unreliable in such a case, as the living will often change their minds about where they are going.
                        Exceptional Success: The demon knows both the object's last destination and its coming one.

                        There's going to be an Exploit or two to complement this one coming soon, as well as possibly another Embed. I'm trying to do stuff based around the flows of the world, of both commodities and more esoteric things like ley lines, feng shui esque what-have-yous and so on.

                            Rivers of Gold

                            The whole world is in motion. From the orbits of electrons to the movements of humans to the turning of the celestial clockworks, nothing is without motion or energy. Even the occult energies that the God-Machine manipulates as a matter of course move on their own, forming flows and rivers across the world. A demon with the correct knowledge and insight can manipulate such subtle flows and thus control the movements of more earthly things.
                            Example Prerequisites: Supply and Demand, Raw Materials
                            Dice Pool: Intelligence + Occult + Primum
                            Action: Extended (1 day per roll)
                            Cost: 1 Aether/roll
                            Roll Results:
                            Dramatic Failure: The demon loses all successes accumulated so far and cannot attempt to use this Exploit on the same thing again for another (11-Primum) weeks. Furthermore, he must roll for Compromise at a -2 penalty as the God-Machine notices his manipulations of the world.

                            Failure: The demon accrues no more successes towards his goal. He may either abandon the attempt or gain the Flagged Condition.

                            Success: You accrue successes towards your goal.

                            This Exploit can be targeted at any 'flow' of commodities, whether that be water (a river), food, jewellery, people, ley lines or anything else, provided that the flow is ongoing (it won't work on a singular delivery). Essentially, this Exploit allows the demon to create a kind of pseudo-Cover in order to change the course or destination of the flow. For example, the demon could cause a regular pizza delivery to a Wal-Mart to always drop off a few boxes on a particular street corner or divert a river so that it no longer supplies cooling water to some of the God-Machine's Infrastructure.
                            The number of successes required to divert the flow are adjudicated by the Storyteller, but should be around 3 for a simple, harmless redirection such as the aforementioned pizza van, about 5 for a more involved one such as the river, 7 or so for things like securing a shipment of jewellery every month and 10+ for dealing with beings of the God-Machine or interrupting its Infrastructure. The Machine's metaphysical 'weight' makes it harder to manipulate anything to do with it.
                            This Exploit cannot be used to cause the flow to cause damage. In the case of the river, it could not be diverted to flow through a town or a house and nor could the pizza delivery van be caused to crash into that corner. It cannot be used to cause Breaking Points.
                            Due to the Cover-like properties of the diversion, the only ones who will question the redirection are beings of the God-Machine, other demons or Stigmatics. If the redirection is wildly out of character, like leaving a box of gold on a doorstep, the one making the delivery can roll Resolve + Composure to 'snap out of it'.

                            Exceptional Success: Extra successes are their own reward.

                                        I think a lot of these could be worked as Interlocks. That's my go-to if I was playing and wanted to use a homebrew Embed.

                                        Procrastination: The user can "put off" dealing with something for a time period inversely proportional to severity. So a user about to go bankrupt could make it so that her money doesn't run out until the next day, or someone who was shot could make it so the wound didn't start bleeding or hurting for a turn.

                                        Confirmation Bias: When the Demon tells someone something and activates it, the person starts seeing evidence in support of whatever the demon said on TV/in magazines/online. So if the Demon says "Don't trust the Deva Corporation", they'll be flipping through channels and pass a news story about how they polluted a lake. Or they Google "deva corporation" and the top hits are news articles about their shady business practices.

                                        Exploits:

                                        Titan's Armory: Temporarily enlarge any weapon held by the user into a larger and more formidable version. Unlike a Gadget, it doesn't gain an Embed or Exploit power and only gains size, durability, and power. However, this power can be used on weapons that are already Gadgets. Ideally meant to be used in Demonic Form: a Gigerian cyberdemon looks a bit silly brandishing a gun or a sword meant for human hands, but this Exploit beefs it up into something more fearsome. This could be a Demonic Form power or Interlock, as well.

                                        The Land of Morpheus: The Demon can send a target or group of targets into an incredibly advanced and realistic virtual reality simulation that they control, which can be used for a number of purposes: pleasure, interrogation, planning, and training. Not sure if this one could be an Interlock or an Embed. I just really like the idea of Demons being able to create virtual reality spaces. I think I might make the Embed version a separate one called Deep Immersion that causes the target to get so immersed in anything they read/watch/play that it's capable of inflicting Tilts and Conditions on them.

                                            Canary (Mundane)
                                            Time is a flexible concept to the God-Machine. Even many of Its Angels find it more of a suggestion as opposed to an undeniable fact. Consequences of events can echo forward and backwards for those tuned in to see them. A Demon must always be careful not to give signs of themselves away - the most clever look toward potential timelines to avert Compromises that would have happened.
                                            Dice Roll: Wits +                                             Subterfuge
                                            Action: Reflexive
                                            Dramatic Failure: You mistakenly seed the wrong timeline, and pick up false positives at in opportune times. The Storyteller is encouraged to "warn" you of an impending Compromise when it would cause the most paranoia.
                                            Failure: You fail to read temporal currents, but at least you know you need to keep on your toes.
                                            Success: You are able to subtly seed an alarm into your Cover in the future, detecting the telltale ripples cause by Compromise in the past before it occurs. For the rest of the Scene, you are warned of an incoming Compromise check up to 4 minutes (or turns, in combat) before it occurs, though with no information other than a Compromise will be triggered. This is given current circumstances, and the Demon's own actions in the intervening time can (and for most, will) change the predicted outcome.
                                            Exceptional Success: Your alarm also conveys short, applicable context and other data regarding the Compromise check. If it would occur because you are acting out of character rifling through a desk and a guard catches you, you are aware that someone catching you will trigger a Compromise. If you are impersonating someone who always kisses their wife upon first coming in the door, and you were unaware, you would be informed that failing to kiss the person's wife would trigger a Compromise.
                                            Notes: It is up to the Storyteller how descriptive they should be regarding the information conveyed. Some may choose to be stingy with details, to preserve the suspense, while others might simple give a full disclosure just to speed things along. The information is limited to what the Demon can surmise triggered the future Compromise check, however.
                                            [Comments: Name obviously comes from the whole "Canary in the mine" thing. While a decent alternative to an early warning system when infiltrating, this can also help when you are trying to feel out an improperly researched Cover. It isn't a sure thing, but it helps.]

                                                Speak of the Demon (AKA Sacred Word, Say My Name, Invoke) (Vocal)
                                                [Desc]
                                                Dice Roll: Wits + Expression
                                                Action: [Instant or Extended?]
                                                Dramatic Failure: [Compromise?]
                                                Failure: [Nothing happens, attempt wasted]
                                                Success: [You "flag" one of your "names" for X time. Anyone who speaks it, lets you know their exact location and some general information about them (appearance, how it was spoken).]
                                                Exceptional Success: [The connection is stronger and you are virtually "there" for 1 Turn. Long enough to listen in, glance at the speakers surroundings for more context, or tag them with other Embeds (such as Tag and Release, Across a Crowded Room, etc.)]
                                                Note: The "name" you target is one that both you identify with, and the speaker identifies you with. If they are talking about "John Black" but don't associate it to you, it doesn't trigger. Demons sometimes seed "summoning rituals" with names that have been tagged with the Embed so that they are aware of those seeking Pacts.
