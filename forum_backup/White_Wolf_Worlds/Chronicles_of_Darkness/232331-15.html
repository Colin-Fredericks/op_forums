    Hmm... I work well with examples. I understand it might be too early for you to be ready or comfortable with giving some (might skew discussion, and there's already so much we know about in flux)*, but I'm curious. On the "different Seemings access/use Contracts differently, through some undetermined way," could you give an example of a couple of Contracts/Clauses you're reasonably sure will be similar, and how you're thinking the relevant Seeming might get less out of it or be barred from it?

    * How contracts work, how Seemings and Kiths work and what they mean, etc.

    As a somewhat broader question, I think you've mentioned the Kith will be more potent than it's been. I remember hearing some talk over the years that Changeling as a splat was intended to be set at a lower "power level" relative to others, in strict terms. That's sort of the crux of the rest of this post, so please disregard this entire spiel here if I'm off-base on this point. I just remember hearing it as an explanation for why Changelings don't get, say, any sort of "turn someone into a frog" or similar powers. There's also been observation in this thread about how some of the Seeming/Kith blessings have been, well... a little unimpressive (that water-breathing example). I come from an Exalted background, where an excessive level of strictness has been a source of frustration. I've been encouraged by the new line from the devs for 3rd edition: some splats will still be broadly stronger than others, which is fine, but they're not generally going to be denied something if it's appropriate for them to have it, even if that particular thing is stronger than a "more powerful" splat gets. Basically, I'm wondering if you have a similar holistic plan for Changeling, that the first consideration for what Changelings can do and should be is what's appropriate for their themes and nature, and what will be fun and cool for them as a gameline. Concern about where they stand relative to other WoD splats isn't unimportant, but I feel it should be secondary.

    Again, going off of hearsay that may well be nothing, but figured it couldn't hurt to raise the point. >.>

                        Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                        It's frustrating to keep running into the same walls with Beasts and Elementals.
                        I think the main issue with Beasts and Elementals is that back in 1.0, the other seemings were defined by what you did, while Beasts and Elementals were defined by what you were. So its easier to draw a line from the other seemings to a method of escape/expression of agency. IE - Darklings sneak, Fairest manipulate, Ogres smash, Wizened plan. It's harder to nail down a single unifying concept as to how Beasts or Elementals would escape or assert their agency because those seeming are more about passive identity. Also, I feel like the big reason nobody wants to delete or change these seemings up too much is because now that seemings color your character, adding Beast or Elemental stuff to a player's character is going to be in pretty high demand (Why have a regular dragonwoman when you can have a dragon woman made of VOLCANOES?!) specifically because those two seemings would have the most broad/drastic choice of physical alterations. Which, thinking on it, may be an issue of its own.

                        That being said, I think just up and mashing the two into a singular ~*NATURE JUNK*~ seeming just sounds kind of silly and a little too broad a concept.

                        On another note, my other concern with turning seemings into an expression of your humanity/agency is that a major theme (at least of 1.0) was that all your changes were forced on you, with the courts acting as the expressions of humanity/agency. So I'm hoping the writers find a way to reconcile that.

                                                        Okay, so I thought on it a bit. I'm going to throw these out there. I tried to pick ideas that would put them in a similar place conceptually as in 1.0, with them being somewhat separated from the directness of the other seemings. (Elementals especially.)

                                                        Beasts didn't escape alone. Whether its silently stalking after another escapee, following along with fellow slave out of a sense of loyalty, or protecting the ragged youngster as they make their way out, beasts are drawn to others in some way. Few animals are truly solitary, even predators seek out other creatures if only to feed. Beasts are social above all else, but they lack the manipulative aloofness of Fairest. They don't use others in the way a Fairest or Darkling might, though they may be used themselves. Their agency is defined by choosing to follow the escaping changeling and trusting that said changeling knows where they're going.


                                                        Elementals escaped by realizing that they were never truly bound. All they had to do was let go of whatever was holding them back. They let go of their complicated "self," becoming something more basic (more elemental, even), and simply leave. The escape itself can be violent or stealthy or what have you, but they are all defined by their relinquishing of their superfluous "humanity" and holding on to only the parts of themselves that really matter (What those parts are depend on the individual).
                                                        To quote The Buddha:
                                                        Even so, monks, whatever isn't yours: Let go of it. Your letting go of it will be for your long-term welfare & happiness. And what isn't yours? Form isn't yours... Feeling isn't yours... Perception... Thought fabrications... Consciousness isn't yours: Let go of it. Your letting go of it will be for your long-term welfare & happiness.

                                                            Hmm… I like the Elemental idea; but I think Beasts need their agency to be something more along the lines of “trust your instincts” than “follow someone else out”. Acid test: how well would these work if applied to a non-bestial, non-elemental Kith, such as the aforementioned Muse?

                                                            Also, how well do the Blessing and Curse(?) of a Beast or an Elemental work when the Kith isn’t bestial or elemental? I don’t have my book handy, so I can’t check on them myself. Do they work fine as is? Do they need some reinterpretation? Or do they need an overhaul?

                                                            Finally: given this new setup where Seemings and Kiths can be mixed and matched: could we get an “adjective form” for every Seeming’s name? “Fairest Muse” rolls off the tongue, as does “Elemental Muse” and “Wizened Muse”; “Darkling Muse” strikesme as appropriate, too, though it’s a bit more questionable; maybe they should also be called something like, I don’t know, “the shadowed”? “Beast Muse” is just awkward; but “Bestial Muse” works fine*. “Ogre Muse” just doesn’t work at all, and “Ogrish” just feels wrong.
