    As for many before, first choice would be Mage (Mystagogue Acanthus) but since that's out of the question:

    Hunter is a template, right?
    Ashwood Abbey Hunter. There's no 2E yet, but assuming a same or similar amount of Merit points (plus some for being old):
    Resources 5
    Status(Ashwood Abbey):1
    Bacchanal 5 for getting some longevity goodies in trade from various supernatural contacts I can get through this, and it's rather nicely diverse in utility in general
    Atariya: Damn Lucky 5, Mr. Lucky 1, Easy Come, Easy Go 1

    In addition to all other Hunter things from just character creation, the Atariya Merits make for a reckless but less easily killable hunter of the weird. Adding to that obscene amounts of money and massive parties with supernaturals and famous people, and we end up with someone nothing like me. But sounds fun


    A significant advantage to the more monstrous templates I didn't see mentioned is losing the Integrity mechanic; I see that as a strong removal from humanity, and a very attractive thing - humans get worn down by the various traumatic events in life. Even if it's not the WoD, effective immunity to trauma-based unraveling is excellent on its own.

    So for a more monstrous splat, Ventrue with lots of Dominating and a nice set of humans for blood so each is fed upon only once in a while. Not the best of lives, but could be quite good, and effective immortality is a nice boost

                In what way does it cut other than away from humanity?
                For the human I am now, being unaffected by trauma sound monstrous, but if I were to be a monster (Vamp, Wolfie, Mage, etc) the traumatic things would be much more frequent, and it would unequivocally be a good thing to not be torn by such. My vamp self might sentimentally prattle about woefully being cut off from humanity in this way, but it's definetly good.

                For the Abbeyists, power does corrupt, if anything Mage shows that rather nicely, the Atariya examples too, and the very existence of the Ashwood Abbey as well.
                So yes, the new self I crafted there would spiral into depravity and immorality and ultimately death very quickly, so much so that the potential longevity that he might trade from whatever supers he drew into the bacchanaliae would be of little use. Years, if lucky (hey, Atariya, so years). But what a death it would be, likely during the grandest Bacchanal manageable - Libertine.
                The background logic of it all is that if I'm importing a fantasy, let it be the fantasy of grand pleasure.

                If I were to pretend to be a little wise (if Wise is out of the question :P) I would go with the answer that Michael Swaim gave for his choice of "superpower" in a cracked.com's After Hours episode: "I'd be Batman. Then I'd stop being Batman and just be a rich guy."
                So a Hunter at Tier zero or one, that's a template, ish. Either none or a small number of Hunter friends to take out that one thing we noticed. Maybe finance them later.
                Resources 5, because being rich is our world's version of a monstrous superpower one has to stay human in the face of, Allies to help me take out that one thing that got me hunting and then never hunt again.
                And I would still load up on Atariya stuff because it's so very convenient: like Mage, the only drawback is the very advantage you get, and your potential failure in dealing with it. Since I'm down 1 in Abbey Status and 5 in Bacchanal (the more grounded me doesn't really do social butterfly things anyway :P) I can go more fully into the lucky thing, and grab some stuff to actually use on society without being too conspicuous: See The Flow 5 makes me choose who does well and who fails miserably, in many situations. Governments would rise and fall on my judgement's call.

                This version has many pitfalls as well. Likely a longer and more fullfilled life than the Ashwood guy in my previous incarnation, but let's face it, the Atariya go down hard too. Likely mowed down by a dozen gangsters or somesuch after I get exceedingly careless and sky high on the feeling of invincibility.
