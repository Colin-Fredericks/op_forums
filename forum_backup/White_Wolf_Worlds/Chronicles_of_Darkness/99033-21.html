                Session LXVIII

                Lorelei’s Diary

                I don’t have a whole lot of time right now (don’t want to waste a minute of potential snugglage with Oleander), so I’m going to make this entry pretty brief.

                First, the matter with Desiree. Before we went with her to meet the monarchs and discuss her fate, we had another unexpected visitor. Chalcedony showed up, escorted by everyone’s favorite kitsune, Shi. Apparently someone attacked Little Girl Blue last night, in a situation that sounded shockingly similar to what happened to Desiree. In fact, if what Desiree says is true, then the man Chalcedony described as her attacker was almost certainly the same shooter that tried to take her out. More thoughts on this later.

                Chalcedony was (understandably) shaken up about the whole thing, and asked if she could stay with us for her protection. Now, none of us were particularly wild about those two staying under our roof at the same time considering the historical animosity between them, but we just couldn’t turn Chalcedony away in cold blood, so the two of them ended up sitting awkwardly across from each other at our kitchen table.

                While Chalcedony was nervous and quiet, Desiree seemed almost gleeful in subtly tormenting the other girl, playing on her insecurities and fears and making her more tense by the minute. Finally it became too much for me, and I asked to speak to Desiree alone. She shrugged and joined me in Calamity’s office a few moments later.

                “You’re not helping matters,” I growled at her.

                She grinned sheepishly. “Sorry. We’ve been sniping at each other for so long that I guess it’s just second nature now.”

                “She’s scared for her life,” I pointed out, “and you’re riffing on that for a few giggles.”

                She looked suitably abashed after a moment. “You’re right. Sorry. I’ll tone it down from now on.”

                She made to head back into the kitchen, and I stopped her. “There’s more,” I said resolutely. “Look, Desiree, I know you’re in a difficult position right now, and I’m trying to be sympathetic. You’re a friend, and I hope that still holds true even after everything that’s happened over the past day. For what it’s worth, I think you’re on the level, and I’ll do what I can to help you. But I’m also good enough at manipulating emotions to know when someone is playing on my sympathies. Now maybe you have a good reason for doing so, but I want to make sure you know that if your actions hurt any of my motley, I will let Calamity have you.”

                She looked… hurt. Disappointed. It made me ashamed. She’s good.

                “I don’t want to hurt you, Lori,” she said softly. “I don’t want to hurt any of you. I promise. I just… don’t want to betray the one who saved me, either. Okay? I’ll make a pledge not to hurt you or abscond with you… or with anyone in your motley, if you want me to.”

                A little disgusted with myself, I allowed as how that wouldn’t be necessary and agreed to leave it at that for the time being, and we went back into the kitchen together, where Desiree made a good show of being nice to Chalcedony until it was time to take her to meet the monarchs.

                Meanwhile, Calamity and Shi headed out to Chalcedony’s place to check it out. Considering what the Hollywood Courts had done to Desiree’s apartment, it seemed like the prudent thing to do. Instead of shotgun-rigged deathtraps, however, they only found more questions in Casa Azul For starters, it seems like our dancer friend has quite a few cell phones. Like, maybe four. All of which seemed to be registered under different names.

                They also found a Hedge-crafted blade… to hear Calamity describe the weapon, it was some sort of blue crystal machete or something like that. I didn’t give it much thought; lots of people have Hedge-crafted stuff in this Freehold. Hell, I have a few outfits and a doctor’s bag myself.

                Of course the cell phones raised some red flags, and when Shi and Calamity got back we asked Chalcedony about them. She was initially a little cagey, but eventually told us that the phones had been gifts… from Marguerite.

                We went to ask Marguerite about this, unfortunately interrupting her rehearsals. She’s been rehearsing her number pretty much non-stop all day… though I’d never put it this way in her presence, her nerves are oddly endearing and her determination to do well in her stage debut is positively cute. In any case, though my French lover was a bit defensive about the whole thing, she did admit to having given Chalcedony some of her ill-gotten gains. So that was one mystery solved.

                The meeting with the monarchs itself was fairly uneventful, but I feel like I should mention a few minor points that came up tangentially. First, the Night Court had sent a representative to observe the proceedings, a charming if somewhat odd fellow by the name of Salk. We of the motley introduced ourselves out of politeness, and he seemed like a decent sort to me, but I don’t think the monarchs were particularly happy with his presence there, by and large.

                Second, notable by her absence was Madame Spidertoes. I would have thought she’d want to be present for such an important event, and I can’t help but wonder if something even more important was occupying her time.

                Desiree proceeded to spill her guts about the Hollywood Courts. I’ve already hit most of the major bullet points previously, so I won’t rehash them here. She went into great detail on the shifting political landscape of Hollywood… who was sleeping with whom, which monarchs had actively tried to assassinate which others, et cetera. From what I could tell, nobody in Hollywood trusts anyone else, and the whole Freehold is on the brink of collapse because of it. In fact, according to her, the Night Court is gaining new members faster than any of the Seasonal Courts in Hollywood.

                Desiree also shared her opinions on strategy to some degree, including her thoughts on the best way to seduce or eliminate each of the Hollywood monarchs. As I’m not the most strategically-minded of songbirds, I’ll leave the judgment of her plots to those who are better suited to it.

                I noticed during the deposition that Princess Jolie was irritable and, well, downright snippy. She offered to let Desiree stay with her until this whole thing blows over, but none of us thought that was a particularly good idea. Even if Desiree is on the level, it’s not a very savvy move for the Princess politically. We offered to take her in at the Dream House – the quintessential neutral territory in the Freehold – and a few minutes later it was a done deal.

                As things were winding down, I pulled her aside and asked if I could speak to her privately. She agreed, and the two of us left the room.

                “Are you alright, Princess?” I asked simply when we were alone.

                She sighed. “I sure can pick ‘em, huh Lori?” she asked after a moment. “I start getting close to Desiree, and it turns out she’s a hypnotized Loyalist spy. I think things are going well with Felicity, and she starts acting squirrelly and getting distant.” She turned back to look at me and smiled wryly. “Sorry, sweetie, I guess I’m just in a mood.”

                “No one could blame you,” I said in my best supportive friend voice. “Look, this thing with Felicity will blow over soon. I talked to her last night; she’s just exploring her options before she decides to commit to you. She’s crazy about you. I recognize the signs.”

                Jolie gave me a shrewd look. “I never said that if Felicity became my consort she’d have to swear off other lovers,” she said levelly. “She’d still be free to sleep with Insincere Marguerite if she wanted to.”

                I took her hand in mine. “I’m not sure Insincere Marguerite would be as accommodating, honestly. But don’t worry about it, just give her time. Felicity’s a smart girl; she won’t let this chance slip away from her.”

                “Like you did?” Jolie asked with a raised eyebrow.

                I dropped my gaze, but kept holding her hand. “I never claimed to be smart,” I muttered softly. “I don’t regret my decision; I love D. But sometimes I wonder…”

                She brushed my hair out of my eyes and forced me to look at her. “Sorry, Lori… I’m just teasing you. Mostly. Though it has been too long since the two of us got together…”

                “Agreed,” I said breathily. What is it about her that brings out the beast in me so powerfully?

                “Anyway,” she said lightly, breaking the spell, “what’s your take on this whole Desiree thing? Can we trust her?”

                I thought about it for a minute. “I… don’t know,” I admitted. “I’m sorry, I’ve been thinking about this all morning, but I just don’t know. She’s obviously a master at manipulating the crowd onto her side, and she’s playing with the Freehold’s emotions with every word she says. But then again, she might do that even if she’s on the level, knowing how most Changelings react to Loyalists. I confronted her about that this afternoon, and she didn’t try to hide that she was doing it, which earned her points in my book… but even then it occurred to me that she was saying and doing exactly the right things to get me on her side. I’m sorry, but like I said… I just don’t know.”

                Jolie nodded sympathetically. “It’s a difficult situation. What does your gut say?”

                I thought about it. “For what it’s worth, I think we can trust her… for now.”

                “Thanks,” Jolie said, then leaned in and kissed my forehead softly. “Don’t sell yourself short, Lori. There’s more in here than you give yourself credit for. I want you to know that I value your opinions, okay?” And then she was gone, leaving me grinning stupidly in the hallway and occasionally reaching up to touch the spot where she’d kissed me.

                I got a call a minute or two later, and felt absurdly guilty when I glanced at the caller ID and saw that it was D. She’d stayed behind at the Dream House to make sure that Chalcedony was safe, and wanted to let me know that she had spotted Shi skulking around the grounds peering through windows and being generally creepy. I told her that he had been the one who’d escorted Chalcedony to us, and that he was probably just making sure she was okay.

                I’m no longer convinced that this is strictly the case, but now I’m getting ahead of myself.

                So we made our way back to the Dream House and started prepping for the show. I had a pretty light night, with only one solo (“Fever,” a la Peggy Lee). I spent most of my time backing up the other performers and running things from backstage. I’m finding – much to my own surprise – that I sort of enjoy the role of behind-the-scenes manager.

                In any case, the show went pretty well, with only one minor hiccup early on. When Luna showed up just before curtain, she had an interesting story to tell. Apparently she met Shi in the park earlier today, who had given her a letter to deliver to Chalcedony at the Dream House tonight. Based on her account of the encounter, it must have happened just prior to his arrival at Chalcedony’s house... which was just before he and Chalcedony came to the Dream House.

                All of this begs the question… why would Shi give Luna a letter to deliver to Chalcedony if he was heading to her house himself?

                We opened the letter. Can you blame us? It contained only two words: “I know.” So what creepy game is Shi playing? What does he know about Chalcedony (if anything)? Why was he hanging around the Dream House all day keeping an eye on her? And perhaps most importantly… was he the one who attacked her last night?

                Maybe I’m being paranoid here, but I also wanted to take note of something in case it turns out to be important later. When Shi brought Chalcedony to the Dream House earlier today, he seemed to have developed a nervous tic. He was tapping his staff as he walked around and talked to us. It’s probably nothing… but I’ve never seen him do that before. Was it an affectation designed to annoy or unsettle the people around him? Something targeted specifically at Chalcedony, maybe? Or could it be indicative of something stranger… is someone impersonating Shi for a change?

                So maybe I was a little bit distracted by weighty questions of Freehold security as I belted out my torch song tonight. Whatever the reason, I was fully prepared to let Oleander sweep me off my feet for our date tonight. She was wonderful this evening. The two of us left together as soon as the final curtain fell (I had an overnight bag packed) and headed back to her place.

                Wine and chocolates first.

                A bubble bath together in her garden tub followed.

                Then a massage while soft classical music played. Giving me back massages has been a difficult proposition since I returned from Arcadia, but Oleander proved more than equal to the challenge. It felt heavenly.

                She’s sleeping now. Occasionally I glance across the room to watch her breasts rise and fall with her soft breathing. With everything that’s going on and everything I still have to do, I just can’t sleep. I’ve been alternating my time between writing in this journal and studying a medical textbook I checked out of the library. I know I’ll need to use my Contracts to pep myself up in the morning, but I think it’s worth it. As I’ve said before, gaining a greater understanding of how the human body is supposed to function and fit together seems to give my healing powers a little more oomph.

                Still, I might head back to bed in a few minutes. Even if I can’t sleep, I can cuddle my dozing sweetie. And that’s a pretty appealing thought right now.

                Lorelei Grace


                Calamity’s Little Black Book

                Nine years, seven months, twenty-one days

                Haven't written in here in a while. I guess with Dr Emmett it just hasn't felt as pressing. But I don't see her till Tuesday and right now it's early AM Monday and I'm sitting here perched on the roof in the shadows watching the back approach to the Dream House and the deck my bedroom opens onto. Gawain is standing vigil inside while Desiree sleeps in our bed. Jacob and Luna are standing guard outside of the basement guest room while Chalcedony sleeps in there. Lori is off spending the night with Oleander. Insincere Marguerite is nestled between de la Rocha and Julia. Becky is passed out drunk by now... alone. Fuck, I wish I was there or she was here. Maybe I should have accepted her offer to help guard these two. At least she wouldn't have spent the night alone. Fuck, maybe that was the reason she offered?

                I need to get a grip. She's strong. She can deal with this without me. It hurts though, seeing that pain there. I swear, every time I see it, soothe it in her dreams, it makes me hate Jolie just a little. I know I shouldn't. She is caring and kind-hearted. That's why Becky loves her. That's why Charlotte loves her. That's why everyone fucking loves her. And fuck knows that just because you care about someone doesn't mean they have to love you back. This isn't a fucking video game. You don't buy love with gifts. You don't earn love with gestures. It happens or it doesn't. And fuck if you get to pick who it happens to. But dammit don't tell me how wonderful love is when I'm holding a woman I love while she cries because the one she loves doesn't love her back. Love is crueler fucking monster than I'll ever be.

                * * * * *

                Wheels are spinning, I can feel it and I can see it all around the Freehold. Everyone seems off-kilter. Something is wrong but I don't know what exactly. I don't know how to get ahead of it. I don't know how to stop it before people I care about get hurt. Hell, I'm not even sure if I can stop it.

                * * * * *

                Desiree is a traitor. That much I do know. She was sent to stop Hollywood from unleashing the Grandmaw and to destroy it if at all possible. Only they caught her and tortured her until they could rewire her scripts and send her to spy on us. It all unraveled and now she's determined to help us against Hollywood. She's also said she won't turn on her 'former' master. If her story is true, and I honestly believe it is, she was another pretty girl come to Hollywood and lost to the meatgrinder. The Director plucked her out of a spiral of selling herself for the drugs she needed so she could forget what she was doing to survive. Saved and given the spotlight she'd come searching for, of course she's loyal to him. Preying on the weak and lost is just the thing a Gentry would do. Hell, at this point I believe what she's selling. I do. She wants to strike at Hollywood for what they did to her and because it's what she was sent to do. And hell, because they are fucking insane and feeding a mad Gentry that might well destroy everything. So yeah, everything she told the monarchs is as accurate as she can be.

                It's the what comes next I'm worried about. After the Grandmaw is dealt with. After Hollywood is no longer a threat. When the Director comes to reclaim her. When he calls her home. When he sends someone to remind her of what he's done for her. How much he could do for us. I don't think she's his girl now but I cannot help thinking how easily she could be again.

                And then our other guest... Desiree knows her. She is one of the Director's and that means she is programed and scripted. Desiree says she is a cruel and mean-spirited soul. And why would she lie about this? We are way past any point where throwing suspicion on her takes it off Desiree. But are we past rivalries between two of his performers? Maybe Shi will tell me why he's targeting Chalcedony tomorrow. (Yeah, I can dream...) I don't like her but I cannot let that color my actions here. I don't like the way Insincere Marguerite is smitten with her. It's... weird. It's not that her being into someone so pretty is unusual, but the way she's acting about it is so out of character. I mean, seriously, she's been giving her gifts and watching her. She keeps insisting that nothing is amiss because Chalcedony hasn't asked her for so much as a glass of water but the whole thing is just wrong. This is the woman that stated that she was going to have Felicity sucking her toes in public and suddenly she's shyly crushing on someone from afar? Insincere Marguerite does not fawn and crush on someone. She claims them. Yes, she's working on getting that aspect under control, but I cannot believe the same woman who was fighting down the impulse to drag her newest pretty off into the darkness Friday night can turn around and be all innocent schoolgirl crush today without there being something more going on. I just don't know what it is.

                And why was Marie-Helen guarding her after the shooting attempt? Where was Ginny? For that matter, I can't say I've seen her recently and she never contacted me about working at the show. Hmm. I mean, it might just be a coincidence that she was spying on Chalcedony for Queen Charlotte when she fell head over heels for her and then as soon as Insincere Marguerite started she also became smitten. Yeah, and maybe Lucien Torrez is just an honest businessman. Shit, I am going to have to see if I can't get a word with Charlotte while I am at court tomorrow.

                Fuck, I wonder if it's too late to change my mind and tell Insincere Marguerite not to look for Anastasia. She hasn't had time to really do much yet. What was I thinking? I need to let go... It's over. Done. In the past. I need to stay away from her. Let her just live her life. Unless... Unless it still haunts her. Fuck, that sounds arrogant doesn't it? That she's still hung up on me after twenty years... It's not like I vanished. The sinister bitch tormented her. For all she knows I turned on her and hurt her. Her best friend... Her first love... Shit... I just don't know what the right answer is. Maybe this is the best way. Insincere Marguerite can find out how she's doing. Find out if she's laid that past to rest or not. I will do what's best. I'll stay away if that's what she needs. I won't seek her out unless I know she's still carrying those wounds. Not unless I can help her. I won't hurt her by dredging up the dead past if it's behind her. I won't. And if I know. If I know she's happy and well and whole somewhere that would be enough. Then I could stop worrying about her. Then I could let her go. I just need to be sure it's what she really needs.

                Well that was fun. So apparently letting one of the dancers that doesn't live here sleep here brings out the murdering ghost. I heard something from the fountain. Jacob was scouting about because Chalcedony was awakened by a feeling of terrible dread. When we opened the door everything was.. off. And then we saw her rise up out of the fountain briefly before disappearing. I activated Shade and Spirit but she wasn't there anymore. Heading down to the basement, Chalcedony was quite rattled and I could see that the other ghost was there. Jacob and Luna took our guest out of the room while I stayed and approached the ghost. She warned me not to let any of the dancers sleep in the dream house because she wouldn't always be there to frighten them and keep them from falling under the other's sway. She told me her name when I introduced myself but withdrew before we could speak more.

I spoke to Jacob and Luna about it and then returned. Clearly between the ghosts and the less than defensible layout we are just going to have to take people to the hollow house to guard in the future. It is not haunted and with all the work we've done it's both highly secure and pretty damn swanky.

                NO. I will NOT go there. We're doing this the right way, even if it's not the easy way and so far it sure as fuck has not been. And it's not going to be. And we are going to keep fucking doing the gorram right thing no matter how fucking hard it gets or how much I might want to hand Desiree to Becky and lick the blood off her claws when she's done with her before we move on to finding out what color blood Chalcedony bleeds. That is not how we are going to play this shit. I am not going to do things Darke's way. I am not going to be that monster.

                Not again.

                Never again.

I'll be able to confront the other. Lay it to rest and make the Dream House safe. And once I know how... once I know I can do it... that's when I go back to Belleview. That's when I deal with the unfinished business I left behind.

Got a few hours before breakfast yet, think I'll make sure all the actual managerial tasks are up to snuff and then I need to work on a number for this week. Sun's coming up so I'm done here. Dawn is for honor duels and pitched battles which is totally Gawain's department to deal with.


                Jacob’s Journal

                It’s like déjà vu. Same story, different day. Chalcedony, accompanied by Shi, came to the Dream House today. She was concerned about what had happened or almost happened. Apparently yesterday she was shot at by a man in a trenchcoat as she was about to go into her apartment. She had just gotten back from the Rose and Thorn and spotted the guy with the gun. She managed to jump into her house and avoid the shot. After that the gunman left. Her description of the shooter is very similar to the one that shot at Desiree, and it was only about an hour later. He may have decided to try and take out both girls in one trip. She came to our place because she had heard that Desiree had been shot at as well and she was worried. I don’t blame her, though I might have sought help yesterday if I were her. Personally I would have gone after the gunman, but that’s just me. Well, maybe Calamity. Actually, definitely Calamity.

                Speaking of Calamity, she went to go investigate Chalcedony’s apartment with Shi to see if any surprises had been left there. They didn’t find much of interest save a hedge crafted, blue of course, machete and four cell phones, all different models. Calamity gave me the names of the phone’s original owners and I did a google search on them. Didn’t really come back with anything that stood out. On a couple of the guys there was no information that I could find. The phones themselves were a little suspect, but I figured that we needed to get more information before we came to any conclusions on our own. Meanwhile, Desiree was being perfectly sociable and seemed to be thoroughly enjoying unsettling and confusing Chalcedony the entire time.

                After Calamity and Shi returned they showed Chalcedony the phones and asked what the deal was with them. She said that they were a present from Insincere Marguerite, who backed up the story when asked. Insincere Marguerite for her part kept out of all the socializing that was going on in the house. That in and of itself stood out as a little odd since Marguerite is generally one to flirt with our friends and acquaintances. She also seems to have a thing for Chalcedony lately. She’s been watching the dancer on stage and sometimes during shows. The others think that there is something untoward going on with those two. Specifically that Chalcedony is using some sort of power on Insincere Marguerite to fascinate her. I honestly don’t know what’s going on with those two, but it was a little glaring in its absence today. Marguerite did eventually come over to the group after Lori asked her about the phones. This was after Lori’s private conversation with Desiree. Apparently Insincere Marguerite had been practicing her act for the show tomorrow night. If that’s where she’s been all day she must be nervous about her act. I think she’ll do fine though.

                As expected Sandoval came by to collect Desiree for her “interview” by the Monarchs. He said that the rest of us in the motley were all invited to be present for the questioning if we so desired. After a moment’s discussion it was decided that Marguerite, Julia and De la Rocha would stay behind to keep an eye on Chalcedony while the rest of us went with Desiree to meet the Monarchs. All of the monarchs were there, as well as a man named Salk who represented the Night Court on behalf of the Warlock of Night. I thought it was interesting and a bit odd that he was present, a virtual stranger, while Madame Spidertoes was absent. I would have expected the elder member of this freehold to be there.

                Desiree for her part offered to just give the information that she had gathered and then answer any questions that she had not already addressed. The monarchs seemed to think that this was a good idea and Desiree started talking. She was very thorough and gave a good bit of detail in her deposition. She gave us information on the Monarchs of the Hollywood Courts, where they lived, their strengths and weaknesses, and who in the freehold got along with who. She even provided information about their military strength, numbers, human allies and their capabilities. The only thing that she did not know was the exact location of the Grandmaw. She did know that they were feeding Changelings to it in a bid to keep it sated enough so that it would not try and actively escape to seek food. Desiree did say that the Hollywood Courts’ defenses are strong and that it would be hard to get into their freehold without at least their human allies seeing us.

                Another interesting bit of information that Desiree shared was something about the Night Court. She said that of all the Courts in Hollywood that was the one that seemed to be slowly growing in size faster than the other Courts. She also said that they were becoming more subtle and cunning in their actions. Salk did not offer any insights on this little tidbit. Not that I remembered anyway.

                And as we knew already, the Hollywood Courts are keen on retrieving and using the Table. They seem to have an idea of what it can do and might want it so they can change the Grandmaw into something else, something more docile or loyal.                                             The Lady never did trust that Gentry again. Her anger towards him remained fresh in her mind and he was never invited back, once he recovered from us killing him of course. It wasn’t until later that I found myself on the receiving end of her anger and rage. In her mind I was stealing her Treasure away from her. I was stealing Delight. I didn’t let Rook take her back and to this day the Lady is denied her prize. I don’t know if Diane ever really realized what she did for me when I was a guest of her Freehold. I wonder sometimes if she still thinks of me as a monster, or if she saw the changes her kindness had wrought. A part of me would like to think that she could see the good starting to come through, though I know it isn’t likely that anyone from her Freehold saw me fighting Rook to protect Diane. In their eyes I’m still the monster that took her in the first place and came to bring her back. Sometimes I don’t know how my motley mates look at me and not see the same thing. I suppose I should be happy for the blinders of friendship.

                                            No, I Regret Nothing

                                            “Bon-Bon?”

                                            Julia entered dining room. Insincere Marguerite sat in a chair facing the wall, a complete lack of expression on her face.

                                            “May I talk to you, Bon-Bon?”

                                            “What do you want, Julia?” Irritated.

                                            “I…just…well your birthday is coming up, I wanted to ask what you wanted to do for it. I mean, we could all go out, we could find an abandoned hospital to creep into…”

                                            Insincere Marguerite looked up and stared at Julia with disbelief.

                                            “Well we hadn’t talked about it,” The willowy girl said quickly with a forced shrug.

                                            The Lurker sighed. “Julia I don’t feel like discussing this right now. I am sorry.”

                                            Julia looked at her, her expression hardening. “I don’t either. Insincere Marguerite, I am worried about you. Listen I don’t…do…a lot of social stuff well. Translate this from Aspie to English, if you would, okay?”

                                            Insincere Marguerite raised an eyebrow “I…what?”

                                            Julia walked up and took Insincere Marguerite’s hands in her own. “I love you. I need you. I always want to be with you. Lorelei loves you. Maria loves you. Hell, Flores loves you…”

                                            Insincere Marguerite opened her mouth, but Julia put her hand on her knee. “One more moment, my love.” Insincere Marguerite nodded.

                                            “We belong to each other. I belong to you. We are Oathed, Pledged. If this is your problem, it is mine too. And I will get through it with you, or fail with you. Your fate is also my own.”

                                            “But…Julia my crimes are not yours. And…My Dearest does not know you!”

                                            “She will”

                                            “And you will try to kill her!” Insincere Marguerite sat up sharply.

                                            Julia had an eerie calm settle over her face. “Only if she is not your friend. I don’t hate Felicity because you like her so much. Okay, maybe a little, but not enough to hurt her. I want to hurt her because I do not believe she has your best interests in mind. Indeed, I think she is using you.”

                                            The Tunnelgrub looked skeptical.

                                            Julia continued, “White Maria is not my favorite person, though she is, I confess, extremely pretty and talented. But still she is not using you whether she is a jerk or not. You are getting the same thing from each other. What you don’t get, and I can’t seem to communicate, is Felicity is not your friend, as far as I can tell. And I don’t really think Chalcedony is either, though I don’t know for sure. “

                                            “You think she could use me?”

                                            “I don’t know why she and Lord Leonidas broke up, but what better way to win him back than bring the most infamous thief the Night Court has ever had to heel? The one who humiliated him? And she is so aristocratic!”

                                            “You think she threw her relationship with Princess Jolie away for another shot at being Lord Leonidas’ girl?”

                                            I don’t know,” the Artist replied with a sigh, “But I don’t trust her, and I think she at least originally saw you as a distraction and a challenge.”

                                            “Like I saw her as, hein? “ Insincere Marguerite looked away.

                                            Julia moved so Insincere Marguerite faced her again. “I don’t care. I don’t care how good or evil you are. It does not matter. My lot is with you, now. I’m yours,” She leans forward.

                                            “Deal with it.”

                                            Insincere Marguerite looked down, then up.

                                            “Very well, Julia. I will deal with it. “

                                            “What is our plan then, Bon-Bon? With Felicity? With Yvette?” Julia cocked her head.

                                            “I must speak with Felicity,” The Lurker sat up. “I can decide nothing until I do so. You, Jolie…everyone wants answers. I can give none until I speak to her. I can only wait, and hope I retain myself.”

                                            “And Yvette?”

                                            Insincere Marguerite gave her lover a long look.

                                            “We wait. When we find her, we decide how to add her to our family.”

                                            Julia exhaled. This would be harder than she thought.

                                            “Our family, Julia.”

                                            “Our Family.”
