        Someone ressurected through the use of the Worm Pipe (HtV: Night Stalkers, pgs. 146-148 and Nosferatu, pgs. 67-68) definitely would count as tainted in such a way, same for a person whose heart was taken by a Child of Phobos (Blood Sorcery, pgs. 55-59), i'd guess.

        Anyway, if one wants to explore the idea of witchcraft, hexing and vampirism mixing up i suggest a good look at Night Stalkers and Nosferatu & Mekhet clanbooks. For beings that straddle the line between human blood witch and vampire i would also suggest Blood Sorcery (duh), Ordo Dracul (the Vedma bloodline, that claim to have created the Gangrel and not the opposite) and Night Horrors: Wicked Dead (for the Cihuateteo - at least one version, there's another in Shadows of Mexico, but i digress) for reference and ideas to mine and mix up.
