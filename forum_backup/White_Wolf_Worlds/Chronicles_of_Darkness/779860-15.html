                    Originally posted by Yads Dom View Post
                    For a second I thought the game was abandoned again. What a relief. I'm going to re-read the Reaper compilation so I can have a nice and round idea for the backstory
                    In terms of what we have for backstory, we already established what The Deluge is and means and how it plays into the dual realities of Sirens. There are a few points that could be built upon, such as the idea of Serenia, the sunken library that contains all of history in The Deluge. We can also discuss the sort of "divine" backstory, with Ceto and Echidna. We could even talk about Nuwa, a Chinese creator goddess with a serpentine body who saved a world raging with wildfires, floods, and predatory animals by restoring the pillars of heaven and created humanity from clay, or even Noah, who created an ark and allowed for life to continue after the flood.

                    I have a few free-floating thoughts about The Deluge and what it means throughout History, which was a Big Name Theme:

                    1) The Deluge has happened before, though it was more regional than it was global. Each of these little floods was Nature's way of eliminating a troubling aspect before it grew too large, which is why there are figures like Noah or Nuwa who helped humanity through the floods. Now, The Deluge has threatened to take over on a global level.

                    2) Serenia, the drowned library that contains all of history, contains information about previous realities in which The Deluge occurred and in which the preceding Sirens transferred their consciousness forward. It is far down in The Deluge, deeper than the Hadal Zone. It is under the control of Echidna, a Siren who has abandoned humanity in order to create a species that can outlast the effects of The Deluge.

                    3) If The Deluge is a sort of reset button that has been pushed multiple times, there should be a sort of "backup" that allowed the Sirens to continue their existence in a different realm at a different time. Whereas The Deluge is a literal thing that has swallowed up the material world in one reality, there is a sort of "in-between" area that allowed the Siren to continue existing and prevent The Deluge. This is the actual area where a Siren becomes a Siren, rather than within The Deluge.

                                I think the general process goes like this.

                                1) Humanity in Dimension A does something really stupid --> Nature, in response, hits the reset button and drowns everyone in The Deluge

                                2) Humanity in Dimension A is drowning from The Deluge --> Their consciousness is transported into an "in-between" area between Dimension A and Dimension B

                                3) Dimension A Humans "Hear the Song" and "Surface" --> Their consciousness is warped so that their being is that of the Sirens
                                a) When Dimension A Humans fail to Hear the Song but they Surface, their consciousness warps and becomes a Breathless. Breathless are unaware of what they are.
                                b) When Dimension A Humans fail to Surface, their consciousness warps and becomes a Banshee. Banshees are unaware of what they are.

                                4) Dimension A Sirens become aware of what they are --> Their consciousness is transferred to Dimension B, where it blends with their consciousness from Dimension B

                                5) The Sirens now exist in Dimension B, where Dimension A has been abandoned.

                                            To keep this sort of "decay" from reducing the number of Sirens and humans in the dimensions as the Sirens die out, the Dimensions keep a running tally of the number of entities in the world. When Dimension A dies from the Deluge, the 5 Sirens die out and 2 of those non-Sirens become Sirens. The two Sirens then travel into Dimension B and are melded into 2 of the 20 non-Siren entities.
