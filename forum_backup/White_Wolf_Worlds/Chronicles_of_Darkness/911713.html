    Just for Fun: Donald Trump as every supernatural splat

    06-10-2016, 12:31 PM
    In a spirit of light hearted fun lets see how we could fit Donald Trump into every single splat. I shall get the ball rolling. (I don't think it needs to be said, but feel free to present your own interpretations of splats I or someone else already did.)

    Lets make the WoD great again.


    Vampire: Donald Trump is a Carthian Mekhet

    Mekhet you say? Not a Gangrel?

    Think about Donald Trump purely from the view of his prey, the political machine. He was always there, behind the scenes, a big name donor. But he was always a distant figure, a phone call at donation time and a bit later, he calls you for a favour. It's been years since you thought about him, then you blink, and suddenly he's right there, fangs around your throat.

    Trump's political campaign is The One You Didn't See.

    And yes, it's also the One You Can't Kill; but translating the Mekhet's sobriquet to politics works a lot better than the very physical Gangrel's in my opinion.

    As for the Carthians, it goes without saying. He's leading a populist revolt against the Republican establishment, he's a hugely successful rebel, and he's probably going to make a huge mess of everything if he ever becomes the establishment.


    Mage: Donald Trump is a Mastigos Libertine

    As a Mastigos Donald Trump sees and exploits minds and flaws. For this version of Trump the theories that he is a master manipulator are completely true, but he also works on a grander scale: seeing and exploring the flaws in the Republican Party.

    As a Libertine… well. Viewed through the lenses of hubris “Humanity is magical” transforms from a celebration of sleeper cultures into “my sleeper culture is magical. Not those foreign sleepers”.

    Democracy Seeks the Truth transforms from a belief in debate, consensus seeking, compromise and giving everyone a voice into naked populism. Whatever the people vote for must be true and moral, and with enough campaigning and propaganda they will vote for ME!

    Destroy the Followers of the Lie… Trump has a violent rhetoric, but the incumbent Democratic presidency is busy performing assassinations via drone... I'll just play this one safe and just say 2/3 is bad enough.


    Geist: Donald Trump is a Stricken Paragon

    (Note: Paragon's are a homebrew archetype I wrote a long time ago. Short version, their thing is being community leaders – a politician fits nicely).

    They were dead. The American isolationists, the labour movement, the followers of the old ideas, they were all dead, killed by the neoliberal consensus. Politicians placed flowers on their graves in exchange for votes, and then they ignored the dead.

    Trump saw what no one else could, he saw the beauty and power in death. It was a superficial and patchwork vision, but it was enough. His words were sweet poison and the dead, so eager for anything with life's sweetness drunk deep. Trump bound them to him, he created an army of the dead and set forth to conquer the living.


    Beast: Donald Trump is a Makara Tyrant

    The establishment politicians and hangers on are out of their depth. Why are people cheering for insults that cross the line? What are these memes on twitter? There's violence at political rallies?! We cannot survive in this environment!

    There was gold in the water, the promise of votes and power. Every step out to sea seemed small and inconsequential. A Democrat calls Republicans fundie creationists here. A Republican has lunch with Rush Limbaugh there. Pander to your base a little, got one small step further than the other guy, get a few extra votes, another drop of power. Walk further out to sea, don't notice the widening gulf between two Americas until the water closes over your head. And there's something else, something horrific, something native to these waters. It thrives in this environment, and you're in it's power now.

    The Heroes should have seen it coming. They should have warned us. But Heroes don't do that any more. Even as we struggles to swim back to shore they shout to swim deeper, swords drawn, attacking a Trump rally is a grand dramatic act. If it gives him more votes, Heroes rarely see the flaws in their own plan.


    Genius: Donald Trump is a Director

    (I can't think of any strong leaning to one Catalyst or another. Though his petty grudges somewhat suggest a Neid).

    It should have been impossible because it is impossible. A political campaign based on obvious lies, obvious contradictions, bluster and with no actual policies, all targeting a demographic who traditionally had little political influence. It is impossible, but Donald Trump is a Genius, he could see what nobody else could see, a way to make it work beyond any politician's wildest dreams.

    And yet he is mad. He is driven by pathological ambition, and like most mad geniuses he is destined to see his own creation turn upon him. Maybe not yet, but soon, not later than after election day. He has built a Wondrous political campaign, but his own campaign and image will surely cripple him as president through all the enemies it makes. If he looses he will be unable to contain the response from his followers, and the genie is out the bottle. Not even Trump can predict what the Republican party will look like when the 2020 election comes around.


    Princess: Donald Trump is a Grace of Mirrors

    Monumental ego: Check.

    Overly simplistic world-view: Check

    Grandiose promises with no strategy for fulfilment: Check.

    Probably will lead his followers into tragedy if no one stops him: Check

    Self editing memory and warped perceptions: Check

    Childish temper tantrums: Check.


    Leviathan: Donald Trump is a Thalassan following the School of the Sun

    Beyond the realms of human knowledge are creatures so alien and terrible that to even understand their nature is to abandon all sanity. When they emerge from the Depth madness follows in their Wake.

    Donald Trump is one such creature. When the stars and political climate were right he emerged, towering over an unsuspecting political establishment. The success of his campaign is as incomprehensible as the non-ecludian architecture of R'lyeh. His very existence warped and deformed the political landscape until all of it orbited him. His campaign speaks to people through the primordial lizard brain, and when it does voters revert to the ideologies and interest groups of a bygone era. Even Trump's enemies are affected, and are dragged down to his level. Once unthinkable, violence now looms over the democratic process.

    Trump himself does not even need to do much. His existence and presence alone are enough. Wherever he goes, primordial chaos and savagery follow in his Wake.

    Vote Trumpulhu in 2016! Why settle for a lesser evil?
