    Many of my points from my previous post about the matter of Funding still apply, although I'm going to revamp that into a new model I'll call Budgets. Instead of being a Merit-like subsystem Budgets could be represented by special Conditions, much like the Catalyst Conditions or Demon Agenda Conditions. For the system to be viable it will have to integrate with different mechanics but be simple to use.

    a) it should be optional for the player if they even need a Budget. They are not required for Wonder creation per sae, although Budgets can negate penalties and achieve a basic success in the result of failure in Wonder creation.
    b) as a special Condition, the Budget will have an always active minor boon and drawback whenever the Budget is active. You earn a Beat from meeting the struggles to maintain the Budget and can resolve it once per Story for potent effects.
    c) Budgets can be redefined between stories, indicative of setting and network changes in the Chronicle.
    d) Budgets are stackable for two key reasons; their effective rating increases the boon of their perk and also serve as a Touchstone for Breaking Point pluses and minuses along the Integrity/Obligation 2.0 meter

    A Budget could be gotten from role-play or a dice roll much like how Clues are generated in 2E Chronicles of Darkness. At the end of a sequence dedicated to showing how a Genius goes about gathering materials and requirements for their inventing, a special roll is designated. Much like the Elements of Clues, the effective stacks of the Budget are gotten from what played into the results of the roll. +1 for exceptional success, +1 for skill expertise (4+ rating), +1 for specialty, and +1 for meeting an Aspiration, amongst other criteria.

    To me it's fitting to have some kind of special system for how a Genius goes about setting a Budget. To answer the earlier question of "what does a Genius do?" in regards to gameplay and storytelling, I would say for Geniuses they are striving for the means to work their Inspiration. They are trying to establish livelihoods and do everything they can to get their invention, their great visions to work in spite of all the risks, Faults, Havoc and failures predisposed towards creations of Mania. I know one of the original themes of the game, the Mood of 'Bitter Disappoint' is not a popular one. When taken to the extreme it suggests that Genuises will always fail, which is not a desirable circumstance for any character let alone the player choosing to join a game of Genius. The bitterness is there to make the success all the more sweeter when it happens, when the Genius can look back at what it took them to accomplish.

    No one said creating a Wonder would be easy. Of course, you want your Wonder to do preciously what you anticipated but it's still going to be shackled with Faults, it will still be prone to Havoc. It caps your effective Mania unless you go the Kitbashing route and take the time to rebuild it every scene. And if having to navigate hoops to make your clone servitor or awesome gadget feels likes its too much, go ahead and commit the worst Transgression you can think of. You'll get a juicy Wonder empowering Larvae.

        Thoughts on Wonders, Axioms, and fields. (might be duplicated - need to review)

        What about defining what a wonder is and can do by the intersection of Axiom (what something does) and Field (what something applies to). Examples:
        • Say you have the Field of Necronics (Ghosts, basically).
          • With Katastrofi, you can damage/destroy ghostly things, things about ghosts (Essence, Anchors, etc.), and/or damage things with ghosts (replicating a ghost Blast Numina, for example). Katastrofi Rank (and/or Postulates?) determine the degree of the effect, but the field limits it to Ghostly stuff - it can't affect Spirits or Goetia, and a Ghost Shield spell (or similar effect) would protect against attacks, just as if it came from ghosts.
        • Field - Biotics
          • With Exelixi, you can restore/improve biological things, but not mechanical. With Apokalypsi, you can scan lifeforms (and for lifeforms), but not other subject material.

        If you want broad, does/affects anything Wonders, you load up on fields. If you want powerful specialists, you take a few fields, but dive deep into the Axioms. The final results/effects can be negotiated with ST, given guidelines (if you can figure out how to make an Economics+Katastofi weapon work, good for you).

        It isn't quite the dramatic power increase Geist 1E gets out of a similar system, since you still have to actually build Wonders to exploit the options open to you.

                    What are people's thoughts on this take for Wonders and Havoc:

                    Wonders and their affects can be largely grouped into 3 categories - Advanced, Hyper-tech, and Paranatural [Terms subject to revision]
                    Advanced Wonders are those like you might see existing in a few decades time, or in certain Hard Sci-Fi. It isn't possible now, but nothing indicates it might not eventually be. Though still subject to Havoc, it is only triggered under in depth analysis and manipulation by Uninspired. Just because Inspiration showed you how to build a compact plasma battery to power it, doesn't mean you labelled things properly or put in safeties. The effects of Havoc are usually limited to the Wonder falling apart, or at worst blowing up. These are very useful for Research, since they are mostly/entirely "real", just poorly understood.
                    Hyper-tech Wonders are those so advanced they can scarcely be comprehended. Their effects maybe, but how they work? You might see examples in Soft Sci-Fi. Still, more into the category of "Well, physics doesn't say it is outright impossible yet...". These are vulnerable to Havoc even from someone Uninspired touching or performing basic analysis upon them. When even the Genius doesn't rightly know how their Accelerated Regeneration Serum works, X-Rays or an uncalibrated electromagnetic field (like another person's touch) might trigger odd behavior. The effects of Havoc sometimes render the Wonder inert, but most Genii aren't quite that lucky. These still have some Research value, as something in them is grounded in actual reality (even if in the more occult depth of the pool).
                    Paranatural Wonders are those that make physicists/biologists/etc. cry. They flagrantly violate physical laws, and the Genius's best explanations of how they work fully earn them the "Time-cube crackpot" moniker. The best Inspired researchers have been able to explain their operation/existence is through some extremely loose interpretation of Many Worlds phenomenon and overlapping realities and their laws. These are exceptionally vulnerable to Havoc - even the slight effect of an Observer running the correct calculations of what should be going on can collapse the quantum BS waveform that enables it to work. Blowing up is often the best case scenario - extra-dimensional/temporal intelligences have been known to co-opt such Wonders before. Their research value is more "well, some reality might work this way, but ours sure doesn't". NOTE to Paragons: Don't use these unless absolutely necessary - they break Integrity as casually as they do physics, and a passel of Beholden is the last thing the Peerage needs.
