                                                        I've been thinking about You Are Meat, and while I really like the Normal and High Satiety effects, I've got to agree that the Satiety Expenditure effect is, in technical terms, hella OP. Permanently severing a supernatural character's soul with almost no chance of recovery (and in the case of the Deceived, literally no chance of recovery, since the Deceived are barred from the Primordial Dream) should take more effort than spending one point of Satiety and passing a dice roll that's usually stacked in the Beast's favor anyways. That goes double for Mages, since they permanently lose their magic under the effects of You Are Meat; compare with Excision, the lobotomy Tactic from Witch Finders, which is significantly harder to pull off, significantly easier to recover from, and noticeably in theme for H:tV, illistrating that (unlike Heroes) Hunters can approach the Vigil in ways that don't involve murder (I was genuinely disappointed that Night Stalkers didn't have a Dracula-esque Tactic for treating vampires with blood transfusions. Likely a bridge too far, but still).

                                                        The most straightforward fix, I think, would be to say that most supernatural characters are immune to the Satiety Expenditure effect. Beasts are already immune due to their souls being "far to powerful" and it would make sense for other supernaturals to be exempt on the grounds that their souls are to powerful/weird/nonextant to be permanently affected.
