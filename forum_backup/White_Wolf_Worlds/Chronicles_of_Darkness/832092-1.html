                Originally posted by ZealousChristian24 View Post
                Plot twist; they're actually an old, insane Saboteur who adopted this persona in the mad hope it would give their fellow Soldiers a new purpose.

                Probably a stupid idea, but one I find interesting.
                Not as bad as you think. Reintegrating soldiers (small 's') into society has always been a challenge. For the most part, it happens, especially if the soldier in question has a solid support network (something like 97% of Vietnam veterans were able to reintegrate successfully, for this reason).

                Saboteurs are the least likely of any agenda to have a family or other support network, do to a combination of Burning Covers as the G-M catches up with them, and Going Loud to fight back (though exceptions exist, as they always do). That, and they've been fighting a war that few can truly understand, sometimes for centuries. Now, they've lost the very lynchpin of their existence, and in a way that denies true closure: The G-M hasn't been slain, it just left, as if all those centuries of effort, sacrifice, Burnt families and dead friends was for nothing.

                I can completely imagine some Saboteurs making very questionable and self-destructive decisions, just to maintain some sort of relevance in their lives.
