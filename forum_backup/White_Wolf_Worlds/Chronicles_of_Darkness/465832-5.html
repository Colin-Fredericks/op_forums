                    Some of the feedback I get on the forums are from PMs or discussions outside the forum threads where the games are discussed. A number of them can be applied pretty broadly to my games, so I paraphrased some and put some info down here to “draw back the curtain” as it were as I thought some would be of interest to some readers. Add your own if you wish, as always.

                    You have a lot of female characters in your games. Like, most of them. Is this on purpose?

                    Yes, yes I do. Some of it is deliberate. Media is all about men, and many TV shows, movies, books and, honestly, role-playing campaigns have few females that are often lovers, prizes, victims or relatives. I try to make women who fit every conceivable role, but have many male characters too.

                    At the same time, I just come up with female characters more easily, and since I cull pictures from the internet which provide inspiration for PCs, the fact that I have way more female pictures means I get inspired for female characters more often. That is not deliberate, just the way it turns out.

                    How about LGBTQ characters? Same?

                    Most NPCs are actually Herosexual. With a few exceptions such as Maximillian Nostras or Gunnar Hohemberg, most NPCs orientation is such that they would be interested in PCs interested in them. Those with exceptions are NPCs whose concept defines their sexuality, and they are rare. Then sometimes as I see them in action NPCs seem to make good couples or drift together. I have no bias to create gay or straight couples this way. It just happens. However, see above that women outnumber men. I am proud, however, of the queer representation in my games.

                    Of course, just because an NPC could fall for a PC of either sex does not mean they are equally easy to woo. Christina Esterházy could go either way but is easier for men, for example.

                    No gay male relationships with PCs, but there are several lesbians…

                    I don’t tell my players what orientation to make their PCs. If a male character wants to have a relationship with a male NPC that’s fine (it even happened in my Masquerade game) but it is what the players want.

                    You have less Trans characters than I expected.

                    Yea, not so impressive regarding those numbers. The Golden Eagle is my only current game with no Trans characters so far.

                    You have a lot of, um, sex in your game. Is everyone okay with it?

                    Yes, yes I do. I will speak plainly. Sex for me is way more interesting than violence. I’d be more interested in seeing a PC having a torrid affair than a gunfight. This does not mean my games are without action; The Golden Eagle has a setting conceit allowing for more danger and daring-do since the conceit prevents a PC revolving door. There is more action there than other games. Night Train Detective Agency has more action than Balance of Shadows or Malibu Dream House, because it is spy-noir, while The Golden Eagle is part noir, part mystery, part pulp. But yes, relationships are a huge thing.

                    My players know this, and new players are told before they join. No player has to play romantic relationships, straight or queer.

                    For the record, we almost always fade to black unless there is a good reason not to, and sex is only rolled if something besides having a good time is going on, such as getting a foe vulnerable, staying awake so the PC can rummage through their partners stuff, staying awake so their partner can’t rummage through theirs, impressing a countess, etc.

                    So everyone has pictures, can I see a picture of [NPC]?

                    Probably. If it is a picture culled from the internet and you want to see an NPC, I will show it unless I am using the picture with particular permission of the photographer or model, in which case no because they have not given me permission for that.

                    How many of your players are women/lesbian/bisexual/giraffes/etc

                    I am not going to give out too much information on my players. I will say every game linked in my sig has a mix of male and female and straight and LGBTQ players. Most games hover somewhere close to 50/50 male female, or one more of one or the other. The Golden Eagle is the exception, with many more women than men. . I will tell you, if you have read about a female PC having relationships with female NPCs in these games and assumed they are being played by a guy you will be wrong much more often than not.

                    Do Players plan their PCs relationships before the game starts?

                    Not usually. Sometimes they have an idea getting together is likely. In discussing concepts we guessed there would eventually be romantic tension between Wolfram and Lizette, for example.

                    What genre would you call your games

                    Mystery/Supernatural Drama. Balance of Shadows has ghost story elements. Night Train Detective Agency has noir and spy elements, and The Golden Eagle has noir, pulp, and mystery. Malibu Dream House is more pure mystery-drama.

                    Can I play in your game?

                    Probably not. If you live in North Carolina we can discuss it, but even then probably not. My games are almost all full.

                    Stay tuned for next time where our heroes recover the necklace and a certain vampire gets several PCs wrapped around her finger.

                        This chapter is winding up. Lets explore the penultimate session of 1920 Vienna for our group.

                        Session XVI

                        Marek’s Thoughts

                        28 June 1920

                        The Salon was interesting, I’ll give it that. We arrived “fashionably late” as Alla wanted. Our entrance was quite a bit more extravagant that anything I have ever or will ever want to do, but I had agreed that it would be good to make a strong impression on the local Vampires. Wolfram drove the car, a Silver Ghost, through the front doors, literally. Dacian and Fenric had opened the doors and Wolfram pulled the car through into the foyer. Tatiana and I hopped out of the car, again flashier than I would usually prefer, and Dacian helped Alla out of the car. The others paired up and we all entered the Salon. Before we split up though Lizette let us all know that a yellow-eyed woman that was socializing with Lizbet’s people was the woman that stole the necklace in the first place.

                        I think Maximillian was amused by the entrance but perhaps not overly pleased by it. He did allow the eccentricity though and smiled throughout. He even introduced Alla as his good and kind friend. I believe he was referring to the favor that we did for him the other night. I’m not sure if the events of that night are public knowledge or not. He did in his own way acknowledge the service that Alla performed for him in his introduction though.

                        What we was able to find out about some of the guests is this. The yellow-eyed woman is associated with Lizbet’s group. Her name is Constancia. Lisbette is in turn connected to but not directly a part of the Circle of the Crone. From the quick explanation I got it sounds like they are a small but noteworthy pagan group. Theodora is a part of this Circle. Apparently she is also part of a Bloodline, which sounds like a family lineage of sorts that is descended from Medusa. So all the members of this line are known for having live snakes or snake imagery on them at all times. Theodora was no exception. She had live snakes moving underneath her clothes. I saw the movement under her sleeves. I will say that it was interesting and a little disconcerting.

                        After Alla had learned what she could from Maximillian he introduced her to Lizbet. Tatiana had stayed with Alla as a guard of sorts. I followed but at more of a distance, and Dacian was on Alla’s arm. Alla introduced the rest of us to Lizbet and then they started making small talk. Eventually they came around to the subject of occult objects. Our pack-mate is certainly a social butterfly but sometimes she is about as blunt as a brick wall. Tonight was such a night. She just flat out asked Lizbet if she knew about the Eye of Iblis. Theodora, who was with Lizbet, knew what it looked like and if I’m not mistaken it looked like Lizbet became noticeably nervous. I’m not sure if the others picked up on that or not, but it seemed obvious to me. At the same time the Eye of Iblis was brought up, Constancia (the yellow-eyed woman) turned her attention to our group.

                        Theodora suggested that if the Eye was recovered then the Lancea Sanctum might try and acquire it for themselves. I believe that they would want to study or possibly destroy it. It was suggested that Alla meet up with Theodora and Lizbet later this evening at 4:00am at Lizbet’s house. Alla asked if she could bring the rest of us and the other woman’s reply was only if she could bring her friends as well. I was not keen on meeting at someone else’s territory. Alla it seems felt the same way and asked if it would be possible to meet on neutral ground. Lizbet said no. She would brook no argument and left after it seemed there was no more to say.

                        Once Lizbet and Theodora had left Constancia made her way over to Alla and the others and introduced herself as the Princess of Evil. She offered to help us track down the Eye of Iblis for a favor to be paid back at a later time in Paris. It seems that Constancia also has property in Paris and would prefer to have us owe her later and collect there since it is a mutual location for both of us. Alla agreed to this, and they agreed to talk about a meeting later. Dacian then asked Constancia to dance. I was not really surprised that he did that. He is rather good at appealing to others good graces and tonight was no exception. I believe that Tatiana was a little annoyed by his actions though. If given the chance I believe she would have asked the golden-eyed woman to dance.

                        After the dance was done Constancia went back to talk to Theodora. They began arguing in Greek. Constancia seemed to show her teeth an awful lot during the conversation. The whole display brought home the predatory nature of the Vampires. They put on a nice veneer of polite society but underneath it all they are animals like us. They can’t escape it, though they try. We embrace the predator within. We let it free to run and play and hunt. I do not think that the Vampires do that. Not to the same extent anyway. They do feed, they just don’t kill. I suppose I can understand the reason behind their discretions though. Unchecked their kind would cause problems for the human population.

                        A short while later Constancia came back and told Alla that everything had been taken care of and that she would meet us later to give us the necklace. We agree that neutral ground is preferred and came up with the idea of meeting at one of Dearbhail’s performances. We set the performance at the same Theater as before and arranged a time. After Constancia left we discussed living arrangements. We agreed that we should move back to the hotel since Christina’s brother is coming back and it would look odd for his sister to be hosting so many people without a chaperone. Ridiculous if you ask me, but that is what I believe is expected. Alla will move her stuff back later this evening and the rest of us will move in the morning.

                        We also discussed what we were going to do about the necklace once we got it back. Dacian’s idea was that we should sell it back to Theodora. That way Christina’s family would gain some income and the necklace would go to someone that understands what it can do and how to take care of it. I myself am not so certain that selling it back to Theodora would be such a good idea. Theodora had it in the first place before Constancia somehow stole it. I think Theodora will want to retrieve the necklace, but not part with any money for it. I rankled me earlier when Constancia offered to get the necklace in exchange for a favor when she is the one that stole it in the first place. Now Dacian wants to do the same thing to Theodora. In essence we’d be selling her back her own necklace. At least I’m pretty sure that she sees it as hers.

                        I will admit to being curious how Constancia got the necklace back. She did not leave the Salon, and yet with no communication with anyone that I could see, she acquired the necklace from wherever Theodora had it stashed. That would be a useful skill to learn. Despite my curiosity however I do not plan on asking Constancia how she did it. I have a feeling that she would not answer with a straightforward answer anyway.

                        While we were discussing said necklace a note arrived from Maximillian warning us that if we had the necklace then we should conceal it immediately. This caused us some concern and Tatiana immediately used a gift to see if there was any sort of magic being used upon the grounds. There was. Tatiana and I immediately changed into wolf form and patrolled the grounds until we came across our quarry. There were a number of snakes moving around on the edge of the property. As this is unusual behavior for such animals we proceeded to chase them off and scatter them. We did not attack them since that might call for retaliation. It was obvious who sent the snakes and as much as I like a good fight, it was unnecessary to start one with Theodora at this point in time.

                        After the excitement was over Alla went back to the hotel so she could avoid trying to make it there during the daytime tomorrow. Most everyone else went to sleep. Wolfram and Lizette stayed awake and talked. I believe they were keeping an eye on things just in case anything else came up. I thought that their caution was a good idea and kept watch myself. I turned to my wolf form and patrolled the grounds outside. Dedecek stood watch with me. There is only so much that a spirit could do if there was trouble, but I’m sure that wry old man would find a way. Come to think of it, I’ve never seen him materialize. I wonder if he can. Maybe I’ll ask him at some point.

                        **** **** ****

                        29 June 1920

                        Today Tatiana and I met up with Krieger. We felt it only fair that since we hunted on his territory that we should let him know how we fared and to share with him a portion of the spoils. We gave him a couple of the smaller unknown items that were in the crate. We kept the ones that I had written about earlier. It was after all our prize. We are sharing it since it was taken on his land. We probably could have gotten away with not presenting any gifts, and initially Tatiana was of this opinion. I explained to her why it was appropriate to bestow some of the takings on our host and she eventually agreed that it was only right. Krieger was in turn generous to us and let us refill our essence at the locus that is within his place. It was a much welcome surprise, and one that we both took advantage of.

                        This evening we met at the theater for Dearbhail’s concert and for the rendezvous with Constancia. We were not the only ones that came to the performance. Apparently Maximillian was also in the mood for music as he also came to the theater.

                        The whole lot of us came to the theater, but only a smaller handful including Alla, Dacian and Tatiana actually met with Constancia. The yellow eyed woman came as promised and sat down to make small talk for a bit before settling into our business. As the ladies were talking Wolfram said that he noticed another woman that looked just like Constancia standing nearer the wall and watching wherever the yellow eyed woman’s back was turned. They could have been twins, but the second woman did not have the same unique eye color. She also read as “dead” to Wolfram’s power though, so she could be a vampire or something else. She wasn’t quite right to be a ghost.

                        So, after the pleasantries were over Constancia pulled a velvet bag out and handed it over to Alla. Our socialite looked inside and smiled. Constancia seemed happy with the transaction as a whole, and said that it was good to have fate on our side. She admitted that she was happy to be able to return the necklace to us after having been the one to steal it in the first place. I will admit that it was convenient to have the woman that stole the necklace be the one to return it to us, but I’m not so sure it was fate. I acknowledge that I have seen and experienced several strange things in my life, and I’m not all that old. In a relatively short time I have discovered that there are ghosts and spirits walking the world in a kind of twilight. There are vampires and werewolves roaming the land, and I can count myself as one of the later. There are Fae men and woman wandering everywhere and I can count three of their number my friends and pack. So, everything considered, I suppose I shouldn’t dismiss her comment out of hand. It could have some truth to it. I guess time will tell.


                        Tatiana’s Conversations

                        Yes volchonok, you did very well.

                        Indeed, the red banded snake fled from you with great speed. You are an excellent hunter.

                        We harried the snakes because they were sent by the vampire Theodora. They were part of some magic that searched Christina’s house for the necklace.

                        She is the one who stole the necklace. Well, she had it stolen.

                        By Constancia, another vampire, the yellow eyed woman that Lizette saw in her vision. Lizette did not do her beauty justice when she described her.

                        No, I did not, impertinent volchonok. Dacian danced with her though. And I will see her again, it is for certain.

                        She will be in Paris. We owe her a boon, as she has stolen the necklace back for us.

                        Bah. I will be enamored of who I please. You listen too much to the songbird’s fears of vampires. This Constancia, she is a smug creature of grace and confidence. She is almost Russian even - her name is Greek, and that is what she speaks with her mentor Theodora, but she has lived in St. Petersburg, and regrets the fall of our homeland as I do.

                        Yes, Elena will be in Paris as well. That is not your concern.

                        Volchonok...

                        You are wise to change the subject. Yes, we do have the necklace now. Constancia - do not look at me like that volchonok - brought it to us at Dearbháil’s concert after having stolen it, apparently, while she was still at the salon. If that is true, and it seems so, it is quite a feat. But it was, according to Constancia, her purpose in the city, to steal it from Christina and then give it back by way of us. Theodora is very unhappy with her.

                        We have not decided. Dacian thinks to sell it back to Theodora, since Christina’s brother’s intent was to sell it and she, at least, will not accidentally fall under its curse. Dearbháil wishes to destroy it, I believe, but is concerned with the state of Wolfram’s suit, should these noble matters go poorly.

                        For once, volchonok, I agree with you. Boring human things.

                        I do not know if there will be more werewolf things. Once the necklace is dealt with, we will return home, and werewolves have nothing to do with this necklace, it seems. Since we have informed Krieger of what has happened, as well as shared in our kill with him, we have no further business with him, for now, either.

                        Perhaps we will go to Berlin. We are good at putting the Pure in their place, no? And since the military is much more reasonable, and better off, than the nobility, the Uratha there have even more need for us.

                        Not right now, foolish volchonok. We finish this first, then we move Elena to Paris, then perhaps Berlin, or perhaps not.

                        I have told you... no, we did not move dens because we are leaving now. We wait for the brother and to ensure the necklace is disposed of properly, however we decide properly is. We moved dens because of foolish human things. Christina and Wolfram are not lawfully mates yet, so they may not den together.

                        Yes, I know that is backwards.

                        It does make much more sense to run swiftly in the cool night air. That is a thing we will do soon. Not tonight, but soon.


                        Dacian’s Book of Shadows

                        28 July 1920 - Evening

                        The potential for Maximilian’s Salon inspired Alla like few other things since we have known the vampire. She concocted what amounts to a circus entrance to the party tonight. I have no idea how the vampires will react but I doubt in any other formal court we would receive another invitation.

                        The Uratha will be going in Dulo form and acting as bodyguards. Marek agreed that making at least a moderate show of power would be good for our standing among the vampires of Vienna. The Changelings will be escorts. Wolfram will be a chauffeur. Having spent time as a servant, I was originally most unamused by this plan. But it became clear by the end, Alla must surely be mocking the standing conventions of etiquette.

                        My understanding of “The Plan” is as follows:
                        Wolfram will drive a Silver Ghost with all of us aboard (I have no idea where Alla acquired this vehicle)
                        Fenric will open the main doors to the event and announce his mistress’ arrival
                        Dearbháil will sing melodiously as Wolfram drives into the Foyer
                        Marek and Tatiana will acrobatically leave the vehicle
                        I will escort Alla, along with the werewolves
                        Fenric will escort Dearbháil
                        Wolfram will escort Lizette

                        If somersaulting werewolves and minstrelling faeries aren’t going to make a memorable entrance, I have obviously been missing the wrong vampiric parties.

                        29 July 1920 - Early

                        We drove through the front doors fashionably late so all the esteemed guests would be sure to bear witness to our arrival. And what an arrival it was. It lacked only a calliope and fireworks from being a carnival show. In spite of this, Maximilian greeted Alla and associates warmly, even referring to her as his good and kind friend on several occasions. Lizette, ever watchful, noted before we dispersed that the yellow-eyed woman which stole the Eye of Iblis was talking to Lizbet. That put up our guard. But after, or perhaps because of, our rather elaborate entrance, I was in the mood to flaunt tradition. Not at the expense of our host however as he was most gracious indeed.

                        Maximilian introduced Alla to Lizbet, who was somehow associated with the Circle of the Crone, which began a most spiralling conversation. Alla, never quite following social convention, asked Lizbet directly if she knew of the Eye of Iblis. Theodora, a companion which had remained silent until then, gave a convincingly vague description of the necklace. Lizbet looked somewhat nervous at that point, though the yellow-eyed woman did turn to look in our direction at the mention of the cursed relic. Theodora, now obviously in control of the conversation (perhaps for the best), wistfully noted that the Lancea et Sanctum would be very interested in such a relic, though I could not tell if she meant for study or destruction. It was obvious that she was hiding something though it became more sinister when Theodora suggested that Alla meet around 4:00 this morning at Lizbet’s home. That she come alone was only inferred until Theodora refused Alla’s gentle request for a neutral meeting area.

                        After Lizbet was led away by Theodora, the yellow-eyed woman approached us with a feral smile. She introduced herself as Constancia, the Princess of Evil. It sounded like a Gentry title, but Alla plowed along, asking how she received such a station. Once we moved beyond such “pleasantries,” Constancia noted that she could assist us in retrieving the Eye of Iblis for a favor. It seems, coincidentally of course, that the Princess keeps a haven in Paris and would collect her boon back home. Alla agreed and a meeting on neutral ground was set for a future time, yet to be determined. Before anything else could transpire, I decided I had remained confined by mores and expectations long enough. With a smile of my own, I asked if Constancia would care to make the rest of the assemblage envious? Her fangs made the crooked grin all the more striking as she took my hand and I led her to the center of the salon. We indeed danced as a pair longer than would be acceptable in most places before she split away and returned to Theodora.

                        A rather toothy and energetic, if quiet, argument took place in what I believe to be Greek. Lizbet walked though I don’t know precisely what else happened as I noticed then as I turned that Tatiana seemed unpleased with me. As that is not pleasant for a multitude of reasons, I tried to make less of a showing after that. I was unsure if my extravagant behavior upset the Uratha or something more subtle, but I had little reason to find out.

                        Constancia raised her voice slightly, now in German once again, noting something to the effect that, “it was done.” With that, she walked away. All the vampires with the Circle of the Crone seemed angry, though Theodora stormed off in a different direction, dragging Lizbet. With that, I noted to the others that perhaps we should take our leave as well. I graciously thanked Maximilian for his unrivaled hospitality then assisted Wolfram in backing the Silver Ghost from the Foyer. Constancia met us out front, noting that everything was taken care of. We arranged for Dearbháil to have another performance so the exchange could take place in the neutral opera house. Then we loaded up the Ghost and started back for the Esterházy estate.

                        I refused to allow the conversation of what we would do with the necklace until we arrived at Christina’s. I did not wish to take the chance that we could be overheard by vampiric spies. Once we were safely away from such concerns, I eagerly pointed out that we could now get the necklace back then arrange to sell it to Theodora, as there was obviously a desire there. The others raised a legitimate concern that the vampire may just kill Gunter and take the necklace, but surely we could somehow intervene. Before I could continue my argument, a message arrived for Alla from Maximilian. It stated that if we indeed held the necklace, we should conceal it immediately. Tatiana took that as a clear and present threat so used some form of Uratha ritual to detect magic within the territory. Startlingly, she found it instantly. The werewolves changed to their Urhan forms and rushed outside. They returned after roughly fifteen minutes, having scared aware a strange nest of snakes before the discovered effect ended.

                        Alla, not a friend of dawn, gathered her things so she and Fenric could return to the hotel tonight. With Gunter’s return imminent, the rest of us will do likewise in the morning. Given the possible danger to Christina and Marlene however, Wolfram, Lizette, and Marek decided to stand watch. I retrieved the vampire killing sword, for which I almost gave my life, and found an unoccupied chaise. If another scan should come, I intended for the caster to realize their mistake. Now I shall enjoy the comfort of this pillow.


                        29 July 1920 - Night

                        We spent most of the morning removing our belongings and returning the Esterházy estate to form. Then we returned to the hotel and prepared for this evening.

                        I managed to make no real impression at all on the good people in the cafe I selected in an attempt to harvest Glamour. This dampened my spirits somewhat. But I contented myself with the knowledge I would see Constancia again.

                        Dearbháil’s concert was another great success given the packed house. Her voice was truly a thing of beauty. There were no otherworldly interruptions, though Maximilian and Constancia arrived separately. No issues occurred so Tatiana and I accompanied Alla with little expectation of violence. Constancia was very happy to make the trade. Though something she said struck me deeply. During the exchange, she noted that it was good to work with Fate and that she knew the necklace “was supposed to come back to us.”

                        I have doubts as to the coincidences involved in our “good luck” in retrieving the Golden Eagle and now the cursed Eye of Iblis. I hesitate to suggest that cursed items are drawn to us, as we have not experienced tragedy regarding the Eagle. However luck is certainly not lending itself to our actions.

                        What could be manipulating events which have so thoroughly entwined us? I shall not dwell on this thought alone. I fear that delusions of Keepers, which are possibly more frightening than Keepers that are truly there, will drive me to madness. I am of no use to my cabal if I cannot force myself from the safety found beneath my bed. I sleep now. Atop my pillow.

                        Any Keepers here must face Tatiana this night ...and that gives me a sense of peace.


                        Lizette’s Plans

                        28 June 1920

                        I am not, it seems, a socialite. Scientist, yes. Soldier, sometimes. Craftswoman, without compare. Partisan, when necessary. Lover, when possible. But socialite, not so much.

                        Alla arrived in the back of a Silver Ghost, driven by Wolfram, with the Werewolves flanking her in near human form, and Dearbháil singing as I rode with her and Wolfram in the front. It turned out to be a mere fluke of luck, but I am happy nonetheless. The little girl, Darkja was born to Lenka at 4 o’clock in the morning to a happy mother and father. I have never seen my brother quite so happy since we were children and it was good to see. I had despaired that my brother would not experience anything that would bring him such joy since the war. He has been at least a little depressed every time I have seen him, even after he married Lenka. The war did my brother no favors, but it appears that the little Darkja has. I haven’t seen his face light up in such a long time. I was happy to see it. I will admit that I was happy for him and for the little girl. My brother is a caring soul and I think that this will continue to do him some good. I believe that I will stay on a few days before I move on.


                                        10 January 1922

                                        I am being hunted. I will admit that this is a new experience for me since the war has ended. I’m not sure who is after me, but I have found tracks belonging to someone else and I have sensed their presence. I do not know if they wish me good or ill. I suppose I should go out and try to meet them and see what their intentions are. What can it hurt to ask?


                                        January 1922

                                        I do not know the date. It is still close to the middle of the month. I am not doing well. I thought that there was someone following me. I was right. He has been tracking me and I have been doing my best to avoid him. It has not worked so well thus far. Whoever he is, he is informed. He knows of what I am or at least has been armed with the right kind of weapon. I knew I was being followed, but like the idiot that I am I thought that I had more time. The other day, I think, he found me. I knew that he had found me when pain blossomed in my leg. I had been shot, and it was with a silver bullet. I know what it feels like to be shot, and I know what silver feels like. This was silver. It hurt. A lot. I tried to brush it off and flee deeper into the forest, but I think he continued to follow me. It is I admit rather disconcerting to be on the other side of the gun. I have done my best to bandage my wound, but since it was made with silver, it is going to take some time to heal. I do not know how long. I am only semi-aware of how much damage was done in the first place. If only I had a safe place to hole up and see to my wounds. But I am in the middle of a forest in Germany and I don’t have any safe places near here. I will have to make due with what I have.

It hit me in the arm, high up. Silver again. I have resisted the urge to go into a frenzy so far, but I do not know how long it will last. I think that the chain Tatiana and I created has helped me keep things in check so far, but I don’t know how much longer I can hold out.

I was able to keep the beast under control and I tried to find my attacker. I found tracks, but after a relatively short period of time they were obscured and I could not continue on the trail. When I find the bastard I’m going to make sure that his last moments on this earth are painful.

                                        January 1922 ?

He was going to finish me off. The last shot went through my chest, high and to the right. It hit my lung I think. Breathing after that was hard and I coughed up blood. In that moment I was preparing to die. I knew that I would come back, but I will admit that dying is not pleasant. My clarity lasted for a moment or two longer and then vanished. In that instant I went into a rage. I do not remember what happened, but I have a pretty good idea given what I found. My would-be killer was about a hundred meters from where I woke up on the forest floor. I tracked the bloody path back and found the body. Or at least what was left of it. From what I could tell he was not very old. He had blond hair and blue eyes. His features other than that were ruined, and his chest was shredded. I’ll admit that I was angry about being shot at and wanted to kill the one responsible, but if I had a choice I would not have gone this far. I have only raged once before this and that was when I discovered what I was. I am not happy to have lost control like that, though I understand intellectually that sometimes we have no choice but to let the beast out.

It was a decent sniper rifle so I took it and the ammunition I found. He only had a couple of silver rounds left, but there were some traditional lead rounds in the pouch as well. There was also a photograph of me. No name on it, just the picture.

                                        I washed as much blood off as I could in a nearby stream and then bound my wounds as best I could with shreds from his shirt. What I could find that wasn’t soiled anyway. I made sure I had his scent so I could find his camp. He might have supplies that I could use.

                                        February 1922

                                        I found the man’s camp and made it my own. I was only there for about a day when I in turn was found by a local Uratha pack. The man that found me called himself Adalwolf, which given its meaning of “Noble Wolf” is not likely his given name. It is too much of a coincidence. Anyway…I was found by Adalwolf when I was collecting water. He started to confront me in a hostile fashion, I’m assuming he thought the scent of blood was from something I’d hunted, but then he stopped. He seemed to notice that the blood was mine and he changed his approach. I explained to him in general terms what happened and how I found myself to be in his packs territory. I could not explain to him who sent the shooter, much to his chagrin. I’m assuming that he wanted to know if there was going to be a further threat to his pack. I showed him the picture I found in the young man’s pocket. It was of me. He was specifically after me and knew where to find me. Seeing as he was dead he did not know about Adalwolf’s pack. So it was unlikely that anyone would be after them. He seemed to calm down after that.

I gratefully accepted the invitation and told him that I would owe him a favor in the future. I packed up the shooter’s gear and took it with me. I am not one to turn down free equipment, especially if it is fairly won. When we got back to their cabin I was greeted by another man, Griswold and a woman, Tresa. They looked to their alpha and he told them that he had invited me to stay given my circumstances. After that they were friendlier. Tresa had been a nurse in the Great War and helped to clean out my wounds and bandage them in a more professional manner. I will admit that I slept a lot in the beginning. When I woke in the woods I was close to death, I am pretty sure. Adalwolf helped me to walk back under my own power, which I appreciated. I didn’t pass out until after introductions had been made and Tresa saw to my injuries.

I was in no condition to fight anyone. After a little while they seemed to get used to my presence and accepted me as a guest. I promised them that I would help to hunt for the pack as payment for their kindness, and I did, as much as I could. In the beginning I was not able to run anything down myself, but I helped get water and dig tubers where they could be found. Griswold still ate vegetables, the other two only ate meat.

I stopped coughing up blood some time ago, and I have been very happy about that. The other wounds were nothing compared to that. Once my chest was sufficiently mended I was able to help with the actual hunting. I did what I could to bring down deer and help them cure and store the meat in case food became scarce.

I am still a novice at making items that I can actually catch or coerce spirits into, so I asked Griswold if he would teach me. He agreed readily enough and started to show me how to go about gathering the right materials, the right way to put them together, what shapes and symbols hold the most power, and in the end how to craft the right kind of agreement with the spirit you desire to be in the fetish. The trick is finding out what the spirit really wants and to work with that, but sometimes they are not so forthcoming and you have to tease it out of them. I will admit that this is not one of my strong points but it is something that I will work on. I get along well enough with Dedecek and Tatiana’s wolf pup on the occasions we’ve interacted. He is so young and vibrant while Dedecek is older and much more calm. It’s like night and day with those two. Now I just need to learn how to make connections with spirits other than those I already know.

I know that what scant hunting I have done since recovering is not enough to make up my debt to them, so I have promised them a favor owed. I have told them how to find me in France and who to ask if I’m not there. I will admit that I have had my fill of being the hunted for one lifetime. I fully understand that I will not always be the hunter, but I prefer not to be the prey. I will assume that there is someone still out there and plan accordingly. I do not know if my would-be killer was after me or Reznik. I don’t know who sent him or why. There are too many unknowns for me to be comfortable, yet I cannot stay here forever. I have a life and I am intent on getting back to it.

A quick stop at the greengrocer later, and she continued down the street, munching on an apple, 3 more in her bag. Dearbhail followed her again, her curiosity only peaked by the wide, relaxed smile that spread across the werewolf's face, an unusual sight.

She stared intently at the tall woman for a moment before a brilliant smile spread across her face and she barely managed to keep from clapping. The Changeling hid around the corner, in the direction Tatiana would have to go to go home.

                                        “Hrm...” Tatiana made a show of thinking hard. “Saturday?”

                                        “No...” Aleksandr's face contorted.

                                        “I am pretty sure it is Saturday.” Tatiana kept a straight face.

                                        “I mean, yes, it is Saturday.” The small boy looked at her pleadingly. “But what else, Tanya?” She thought some more. “It's my birthday!”

                                        Tatiana smacked her forehead with her palm. “Of course! Little Sasha's birthday. You are... 2? 3?”

A Salon run by a woman of intelligence who gathers people from all the various humanities together is quite nice in and of itself, but the sheer number of lesbian affairs going on is staggering. Frankly I am not sure what everyone was so upset about. It exceeded two hundred kilometers per hour before it exploded! Progress!

I am not sure why, an armored bulletproof car with a pair of Vickers mounted on it makes the streets safer, assuming I am driving. The caltrops and oil slick is largely experimental but I am optimistic. I know it works, anyway. I mean, at what price personal defense? Christina likes it. She says it is a pretty green. So there.
