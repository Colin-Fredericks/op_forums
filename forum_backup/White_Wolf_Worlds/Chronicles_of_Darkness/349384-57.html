            Actually, here's something off the top of my head for one of the Sentinels baddies-no stats, just Anchors and Dread Powers:

            Dawn Cohen, First Citizen of the Sun

            Virtue: Caring

            Vice: Ego

            Integrity: 6

            (As a human mutant, Dawn thinks in human terms. While she has a lacking ability to emphasize with others she does not know, she is not truly a sociopath-she does not roll for degeneration when committing manslaughter or property damage in the name of her fellow Citizens, but does when other Citizens get hurt during an operation or when faced with an opponent she regards as "lesser"-ie, anyone not possessing a supernatural template-gaining an advantage over them or herself).

            Dread Powers: Death Rage (incapacitating more than five Citizens of the Sun in Dawn's vicinity)^, Influence (Light, Life) ***, Home Ground (Direct Sunlight), Numina (Blast, Firestarter, Telekinesis)

            Death Rage Dread Power: Unbreakable

            ^ New Dread Power: This entity, when faced a trigger, promptly loses its composure and starts attacking the source of its emotional distress in a berserk frenzy. Unfortunately, this often comes with the full expression of its potential as a Horror, displaying Dread Powers and increased Attributes it does not normally have access to. The Death Rage lasts for a scene or until the triggering stimulus is removed, whichever comes first. After the end of its rage, the entity takes a -2 penalty for all actions until its next restful slumber, and cannot use Death Rage while the penalty lasts.

            Ban: Dawn cannot use her Life influence to heal herself, or Telekinesis to do anything but lift herself (ie, it's a game representation of the ability to fly).

            Bane: None (yes, this means she is functionally invincible when Raging. Removing her support is a risk.)
