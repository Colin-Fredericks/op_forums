                                        Joining a Pack is a bit more complicated than that. For most other Supes, it's quite difficult. Vampires have the disadvantage of not being as active in the real world, and being blind to the spirit world early on, so in a Pack, they tend to have responsibilities more inline with those of mortals or Shadow-blind Wolf-blooded: gathering resources, working contacts and occasionally manning the walls.

                                        Beasts, on the other hand, are still creatures of this world, although their powers and their Lair give the pack an alternative route to a target and new tactical options when Essence is tight and they can't safely travel through the Shadow. Obviously, the challenge comes from earning a place in the Pack.
