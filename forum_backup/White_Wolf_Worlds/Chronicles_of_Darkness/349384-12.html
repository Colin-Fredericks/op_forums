                    The Mummy

                    For this write up, I am presenting Imhotep, the titular character both the 1932 and 1999 version of “The Mummy” as I believe he would appear in Mummy the Curse.

                    This version of Imhotep was an Iremite priest and member of the Su-Menent Guild, who was respected for his wisdom and scholarship. In his life prior to the Rite of Return, he was involved in a secretive affair with a woman named Anck-su-Namun, who was a member of the Mesen-Nebu Guild (and betrothed to an Iremite noble). He believes now that he was chosen for the Rite to separate him from his beloved. Over the centuries he has learned that Anck su Namun was also given the Rite of Return, and they were briefly re-united during the 1rst Sothic turn, and again during the 3rd.

                    Imhotep has currently been active since 2012. He uses the alias of “Ardath Bey” and is a wealthy and influential art and antiquities dealer. He strives to balance his duties as Arisen against his desire to locate Anck su Namun. His considerable resources indicate that she has not yet awaken in the current Sothic turn, and he fears some terrible fate may have befallen her.

                        This might be a bit of a cheat, but Mur Lafferty (a former White Wolf writer) is working on a series that feels a lot like a variant nWoD setting. In search of a new job, a black-listed author returns to New York City . . . where she continues to strike out. But a small, "oddly" obscure ( ) startup is willing to take a risk on her . . . and she promptly finds herself the new managing editor on the Shambling Guide series. The world's premiere travel guide series for the monsters of the world.

                        Because of the disaster that was her last job, Zoe is searching for a fresh start as a travel book editor in the tourist-centric New York City. After stumbling across a seemingly perfect position though, Zoe is blocked at every turn because of the one thing she can't take off her resume —- human.

                        Not to be put off by anything — especially not her blood drinking boss or death goddess coworker — Zoe delves deep into the monster world. But her job turns deadly when the careful balance between human and monsters starts to crumble — with Zoe right in the middle.
                        Off-hand, I see elements of Requiem, Promethean, and Mage (particularly the "House of Ariadne" Legacy) in the series. There's also a nice shout out to the Wraith Player's Guide from the classic World of Darkness series.

                            The Phantom of the Opera

                            The Phantom of the Opera, as seen many different incarnations over the years, from the original serial novels published in 1909, to the Lon Chaney silent film in 1925, to the popular musical in 1986. This version of the Phantom blends elements from the sources above. I considered a view different variations (including a Freak Slasher and a Sin Eater), but ultimately went with a Ghost.

                            This version of the Phantom is a legend that stalks the hallways and hidden passages of the Palais Garnier opera House in Paris, France. The details of his life are jumble of conflicting rumors, some describing a violent, malformed madman, others describing a brilliant artist scarred by the cruelties of his peers. The stories agree that his obsession with a beautiful soprano in the year 1881 led to a series of accidents and murders, all of which culminated in the death of the Phantom at the hands of an angry mob.
                            His forlorn spirit lingers on to this day. He considers the opera house his domain, but on rarely makes his presence known (typically if he disagrees with a casting choice or music selection). He is searching for the one that has the voice, pure and sublime, that can sooth his spirit and allow him to move on. Until then, he will haunt the Opera House, a phantom in name and in truth.

                                Something I preparing for mine newest RPG club game to run. Behold father of modern Alchemy!

                                Nicolas Flamel

                                This version of Flamel is Archmaster, previous Moros mage. Living once as Parisian member of Silver Ladder in XIV century, working with poor and Jews of from Île-de-France, he dedicated his life to unlocking Mystery of immortality to give it back to people. His Sleepwalker wife Perenelle helped him in his research for decades. Finally, he unlocked secrets of Philosophical Stone, change of base Matter in to everlasting Life - and become Archmage by this very act. But the price for this was the life of his wife. When he became immortal, she died from the old age, now and forever written in reality. Flamel then understand the principle of Stone – it can grant you life, only it costs the life of others. This Quintessence is simple – a life for the life.

                                Flamel know he could not continue his works in open, especially after departure of wife, but as once a Ladder mage, he tried to stir some possible alchemist in their studies. He faked his death with her, change identities and traveled world, bringing his own legend with him as stranger talking about himself. Once or twice in generation he proposed the Elixir of Life to those he thought are worthy – only to made them aware that it would take someone others life to do it. If they agree, he would made the Elixir, but then left the prospect suddenly – they would have only one more life time to uncover secrets of Alchemy, or they would parish. But he taught some time those that declined his offer, marking a true Wisdom.

                                As to his whereabouts, nothing is sure now. Some said he omits Ascension War to continue his research, even after over half millennium of it. Others say he went to New World the first time Europeans sailed to Americas and there is somewhere, hiding in the middle of nowhere from the Orders and their brutal politics over Mysteries. And others say that Flamel finally get to Ascension, reconnecting with his wife in Supernal – but he left a body made of red crystal that is whole made of Philosophical Stone.
