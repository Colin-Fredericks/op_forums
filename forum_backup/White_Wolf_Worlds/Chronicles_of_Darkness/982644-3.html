        I've been busy reading The Devil You Know, the excellent Grand Finale of the Brimstone Angels Saga, to comment much here, but I've finished it so here are my thoughts.

        Dreamform and Dreamwalking rules are awesome, I had been concerned when I heard the rules were being rewritten to fit the already existing dream form rules, that what made the original dreamwalking rules cool would be gone, but not only were they maintained, they were improved.

        They now work fine with Mages, Goetics, and Beasts, while maintaining cool ideas like the too solid tilt, but also add some cool stuff.

        Changelings are paradoxically more powerful, but also more vulnerible in dreams. They've been juiced up, but at the cost of it being possible to actually kill them in dreams even without the Too Solid Tilt.

        A Changeling with Wyrd 10, 2 Resolve, 2 Stamina will have Physical Health 17 and Dream Health of 22, +10 to speed, ect...

        Very powerful.

                    10-09-2016, 11:14 AM
                    First off, thank you for letting us look at the evolution of the gameline and being so open. I look forward to buying the final product on release! Overall, this is a fantastic job with a lot of good ideas. Hats off to the writers / devs. Going to do a look for errors and points of confusion pass.

                    Character Creation
                    * A "create Motley" step or sidebar may be prudent here, since Oaths can affect your beginning traits and are things some (but not all) groups will want to think about at the start.
                    * An aside, a "Climbing the Ladder" (VtR2e) style process for creating courts would be a neat thing to introduce in a later session or web thing. Or maybe a fan could do it as a post on a forum.
                    * Should mention that the first dot of Mantle is free here if that's the case, otherwise you get someone who only has read character creation and doesn't add it. Or an "oops" moment when they realize it and have to go back to adjust points. The example character creation implies it isn't free, in which case the section under Oaths confuses me.
                    * Character Creation mentions Virtue and Vice, but later on the document says Masque and Mien.
                    * Putting Size under "Derived Traits" implies, RAW, that Changelings in dreams increase their size by their Wyrd. I'm not sure this is an actual problem, since size is somewhat meaningless in dreams.
                    * Experience Costs are listed twice, but that's probably deliberate. They also don't match, and the first one mentions "Morality."
                    * I like how the theme/mood of the game is reinforced through the examples you provide. It definitely feels like Changeling, even here.

                    Changeling Rules
                    * An aside, a merit to let a Changeling strengthen the mask for longer periods of time would be cool for those Changelings who try to live as humans. 1 Glamour / day or something.
                    * Strangely, Iron can be affected by Contracts (i.e. "Control Elements [Metal]"). Maybe it shouldn't be? I see literally every table that plays Changeling that I've been at rule it that way.
                    * Embracing the Wyld being mutually exclusive with dropping the Mask is interesting. Probably a balance decision. Does dropping the mask with embracing the wyld also call the Huntsman?
                    * You decided to not make Bedlam something that happens at a lower Wyrd?
                    * This section says that Wyrd limits the number of Pledges a Changeling may have access to, but no limit is actually given either here or in the Pledges section.
                    * No Seeming Blessing requires the expenditure of Glamour, despite the section on Glamour saying they frequently do.
                    * The Exotic Aires merit is mentioned but not listed. I assume it's deliberate.
                    * With Clarity, I feel the lack of listed penalties means Changelings rarely lose clarity from breaking points. I've played two Changelings, both with a Wits + Composure of 6, and have never failed a breaking point roll. One hits breaking points frequently, since he's a therapist that has "use magic to alter someone's mind" as a breaking point.
                    * Using Willpower to add Clarity to resist or contest supernatural powers is extremely powerful.
                    * "instead add her Clarity to her dice pool in a Clash of Wills (see p. XX)." is a bit vague. Does it replace the attribute, the wyrd, or both? Also, with how high Clarity gets, this may slant the dicepool too much in a Changeling's favor. Unless it replaces both, in which case it's fine.
                    * The Touchstone merit is mentioned, but we don't get to see it. Assuming this is deliberate as well.

                    Dreamwalking
                    * Love the new rules. Much more easily compatible with other gamelines.
                    * I like how the number of different rolls were cut down. It looks like Changelings need Persuasion, Occult, Empathy, Expression to be "an oneiromancer."
                    * Dream Riding doesn't mention making small edits to a dream now. Is that covered under Dreamscaping?
                    * I like Dreamscaping being much easier now.
                    * My therapist character wishes Psychotherapy was better at curing conditions, since many don't require a roll to cure them. Maybe as a merit?
                    * "After creating the same dream three times in three nights and spending a point of glamour with each to imprint it upon the dreamer, the victim's mind is sent into an unnatural, obsessive spiral." The way this is placed in the text is weird. I think the intent is an explanation for how reoccurring dreams are made, but the way it is in the text implies that it can be done with any dream?
                    * With Hedgespinning, and Tokens in general, it's not clear whether or not someone crafting tokens / hedgespun stuff pays for it if they make it after creation.

                    Merits
                    * Does Acute Senses apply even when Clarity drops below 7? Also, VtR2e has a merit by the same name... I'm not sure if they matters or not? Probably not.
                    * Bounty has been a merit me and my table have wanted for a long time. Thank you.
                    * Defensive Dreamscaping should probably add "while within a dream" to the last line.
                    * Can a Changeling take Diviner? It's prerequisite is Lucid Dreamer, implicitly the merit, which Changeligns can not take. Also, taking it above one dot feels like a meh investment, but that's just me.
                    * Elemental Battle mentions Become the Primal Foundation, which isn't a thing anywhere else in the document.
                    * Elemental Battle's first dot mentions that it increases the damage dealt to people who attack the Changeling with Brawl or Weaponry attacks. Cloak of the Elements no longer does that by default. Is this dot meant to add that functionality?
                    * Aside: I swear you would save so much wordcount if you made rounding up Wyrd in calculations that use half the user's Wyrd a default rule.
                    * Any chance of getting Hungry Leaping Flames to use a more brain-y dicepool? :P
                    * On that note. As written Hungry Leaping Flames can be used without the Contract active. Is that intended?
                    * Hungry Leaping Flames doesn't subtract defense. Is that intended?
                    * Fae Mount is still hard capped at 5 dots! How can we have epic steeds?
                    * Hedgespun offers an uncapped bonus to Defense, which may be an issue. The "Catch" should probably be part of the Drawback since it's not a token. Also, what happens if I don't spend a dot to make it equipment or a raiment?
                    * Should Hedgespun have a system for offering better weapons/armor?
                    * Does Legendary Language allow the Changeling to speak some sort of Omni-Language or does it give the Changeling Language [Everything that fits the criteria] as separate languages?
                    * Prophet is strangely written. It seems to be an intrinsic quality of the Changeling, but it mentions using mortal's dreams. "A character can only benefit... once per story" implies that other people could somehow get the condition, even though I don't think that's the case? I feel like most of the rules text could be replaced with "Once per Story, your character may choose to have a Prophetic Dream. This could be her own dreams, or represent visiting a mortal she shares a pledge with."

                    Goblin Fruit
                    * Yay Goblin Fruit! Been waiting for this one.
                    * Scarthistle is interesting. Do the tattoos count against your limit once they are tattoos? Do multiple tattoos stack?
                    * Mantaesthetic Tea and Thistleflower need durations.
                    * Over all, I like these. I'm left wanting more, but I can wait for the book.

                    Pledges
                    * Good clarification that only Changelings can negate Sealings (and Huntsmen with a roll).
                    * It's mentioned elsewhere that Pledges can gain Glamour, but there is no mechanic for that.
                    * Are Pledges no longer how one Ensorcells mortals?

                    Tokens
                    * Gossamer Raiment refers to itself as a merit. Is this accurate?
                    * There seems to be an unusual amount of overlap between Gossamer Raiment and Hedgespun Raiment. Is this a duplication?

                    Entitlements
                    * Yay entitlement rules! Another thing I was looking forward to, and I'm glad it didn't disappoint.
                    * On Entitlement Privledges, I think the Token option is meant to be that it costs a number of points equal to twice the token's value?
                    * Can Privledge Tokens by stolen or used by someone else? Presumably they can't be created outside the entitlement.

                                    "* Embracing the Wyld being mutually exclusive with dropping the Mask is interesting. Probably a balance decision. Does dropping the mask with embracing the wyld also call the Huntsman?"

                                    There is a difference between Dropping the Mask action, and simply removing the mask as part of Embrace the Wyrd.

                                    For removing the mask as part of Embrace the Wyrd its free, because they're are no mechanical effects if you choose to do so, it just to make your self more showy while weildjng Embracing the Wyrd.

                                    Dropping the Mask on the other hand its a potent magical act that requires you spend on Glamour directly on it, which empowers its effects, so you get the mechanical effects, good and bad.

                                    So they're very different actions, but not mutually exclusive.

                                    So for example you can Drop The Mask in turn 1, then Embrace the Wyrd in round 2 and enjoy the benifits and draw backs of both at the same time.
