            More horror things!

            Little Nightmares is a straight up changeling durance- the main character becomes either a gristlegrinder or leechfinger at the end.

            House of Leaves is what happens when an incarnate gets corrupted by the Abyss. The weird meta things are the beast hunting through the dream- read it, and it can find you.

            The videotape from the Ring isn't haunted, at least not in the conventional sense. Samara was a particularly powerful Arcanthus, and in her dying moments laid one hell of a curse on the world.

            Freddy Kruger is a vampire, with dream-based powers letting him feed on dreams. He keeps coming back because no-one's found his toporic body to destroy.

            Heavy Rain includes VASCU's attempt to create a technological version of Teleinformatics, field tested by tracking down a Maniac called the Origami Killer. You can decide for yourself how well it went.

            Cloverfield is an important warning to werewolves as to what can happen when an Idigam goes unchecked for too long.

            The tabletop game Chronicles of Darkness contains kindred, I think, although I'm unsure, they're quite subtle.

                                                            Koro Sensei (Assassination Classroom)
                                                            Virtue: Compassionate/Cunning
                                                            Vice: Petty
                                                            Aspirations: Bring Out The Best In Others, Foil Attempts At Assassinating Him

                                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 8, Wits 10, Resolve 8
                                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 7, Dexterity 10, Stamina 7
                                                            Social Attributes: Presence 5, Manipulation 6, Composure 8

                                                            Skills: Academics (Strategy) 5, Computer 4, Crafts 5, Investigation (Assassination) 6, Medicine 4, Occult 3, Politics 3, Science 5, Athletics 8, Brawl 4, Firearms 4, Larceny 4, Survival 3, Weaponry 4, Empathy (Individual Needs) 6, Expression (Teaching) 4, Intimidation (Sudden Seriousness) 4, Persuasion (Bringing Out The Best In Others) 5, Socialize (Jovial) 3, Subterfuge 3

                                                            Merits: Area Of Expertise (Assassination, Bringing Out The Best In Others), Common Sense, Danger Sense (Epic), Encyclopedic Knowledge (Academics, Investigation, Science), Fast Reflexes 3 (Epic), Indomitable (Epic), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Assassination, Bringing Out The Best In Others), Professional Training 5 (Assassination Teacher: Investigation, Empathy, Expression), Trained Observer 3 (Advanced), Virtuous (Advanced 3), Ambidextrous, Demolisher 3, Double Jointed (Advanced), Iron Stamina 3, Sleight Of Hand, Inspiring (Epic), Iron Will, Pusher, Sympathetic, Table Turner, Untouchable, Untouchable Style 4, Close Quarters Combat 1, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Unarmed Defense 5

                                                            Willpower: 26
                                                            Defense: 30
                                                            Initiative: 22
                                                            Speed: Special (See Absurd Agility)
                                                            Health: 14 (Size 7)
                                                            Potency: 10

                                                            Dread Powers: Absurd Agility*, Influence 3 (Assassination), Know Soul, Mimir’s Wisdom*, Numen (Blast, Occult Sight*, Omen Trance), Reality Stutter, Shifting*, Surprise Entrance, Unnatural Healing*

                                                            *Absurd Agility: Koro Sensei adds his Potency to Perception rolls and his Defense. His Defense is applied against ranged attacks, and his Dodging rolls receive the Rote Quality and are done reflexively. His Speed is considered to be enough to win any Chase against mundane opponents, and he can fly up to his full Speed. In the case of supernatural opponents with powers that significantly enhance their Speed, roll a Clash Of Wills against Koro Sensei’s Dexterity + Potency. Furthermore, Koro Sensei may spend 1 Willpower point to reduce the time taken by most Mental and Physical Extended Actions into 1 turn per roll. Spending 3 Willpower points makes each roll reflexive, allowing Koro Sensei to multitask to extreme levels.

                                                            *Occult Sight: Koro Sensei is sensitive to supernatural phenomena. He notices the active use of supernatural abilities within the range of his senses and may make Perception rolls to approximate their distance and direction. In addition, by spending a point of Willpower, he can see through powers of concealment, illusion and deceit with a Clash Of Wills using his Wits + Potency as his dice pool. Lastly, he always knows when he is being targeted by a supernatural power and may mimic the Aura Reading Supernatural Merit, but without drawback.

                                                            *Mimir’s Wisdom: As per the Atavism (Beast: The Primordial, page 127). Koro Sensei is considered to always be at Low Satiety, and can mimic the Satiety Expenditure effect by spending 2 Willpower points.

                                                            *Shifting: As per Idigam version (Werewolf: The Forsaken 2ED, page 222). This Dread Power can be used to mimic his miscellaneous powers like Molting and Absolute Defense.

                                                            *Unnatural Healing: Koro Sensei can only receive aggravated damage from weapons specifically designed to be his Bane (any other potential source of aggravated damage is downgraded into lethal). He also heals all lethal and bashing damage reflexively each turn. Should he be attacked with his Bane, he downgrades a number of aggravated damage equal to Potency/2 into lethal at the end of the turn.
                                                            Last edited by GibberingEloquence; 06-27-2017, 03:42 PM.
