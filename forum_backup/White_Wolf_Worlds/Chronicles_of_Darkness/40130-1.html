    I literally just joined the forums so I could respond to this. I love Immortals and have been thinking about this for a while now. Having played a Purified PC in a game for a few years, I can definitely say that I love it. On the other hand, I think that Purified lack a lot of the problems of other creatures in the World of Darkness in that they don't have to struggle constantly against a Beast like Kindred or deal with the aftermath of being Abducted like Changelings, the Fallen World effects in Mage, &c. They are, to put it bluntly, actually awesome.

    My thoughts about Purified and GMC are ways in which I would improve Purified to be less amazingly awesome so that they can suffer from the same problems as every other end-point supernatural. I have decided that the real horror for Purified is that they are probably 100% spirit and just in denial about it.

    To that end, I wrote up all of my brainstorming and put it in a Google Doc, linked below. A lot of it is based on things I've read on this thread, but I've added a lot of things based on constantly ruminating on the ideas I expressed above. My GM experience is limited to Changeling and Mage, so I'd be happy to hear what people with broader GM experience think about all of this.

                                I am currently storytelling werewolf, and have a player character which just became Purified. (That was a fun story to run on its own; she was a Second Sight shaman, and the cord that tethered her to her body was cut, so she discorporated; it's been a long, trying hike back from the spirit wilds, changing and being bound into webs of obligation as she goes. If there's a chance for it, I highly recommend role-playing out the experience.) Anyway, I did a full, personal write-up for the splat, which drew heavily on your work here, Octavo.

                                I'm mostly speaking up because I also gave Purified access to influences, however I handled them rather differently. Since spirits are their influences, I gave them influences which relate to aspects of their psyche: virtue, vice, beliefs, personality traits, existing derangements, concept, narrative or social role, Jungian archetypes - essentially, Astral influences which would apply to them as a character. Picking up new influences from spirits would be hard (without becoming magath), but they are able to pick them up from ghosts and astral figments (or perhaps angels). I also decided that at Chi 7 (spirit rank 5), they can gain the influence "Humanity".

                                My Purified character also has a warped ephemeral form - exaggerated by her influence dots - and a spirit name, both of which make her easy to mistake as an abstract spirit, for those who aren't already familiar with the rare choir of human spirits.

                                I agree with quantumelody in how handle the "downside" to being Purified - that they are actually spirits now - but I didn't expand on that very much. In Immortals, it's explained that they get woven into the politics of the Shadow, in service to choirs and incarnae as a world-walking agent; that, along with the ordinary limitations that come with spirits, is all I have planned. But I do like the emphasis on new sensations, and growing disconnect to humanity as their new morality trait (on the other hand, that may be counterproductive with my assumptions that they are humanity spirits; then again, it could be a disconnect from their human life and a pull toward humanity in general . . . )

                                I have the feeling the way I've written up Purified for my game is going to quickly lead to a over-powered character, to the point of only working well in mage chronicles. However, my game is nearly at 700 exp (pre-gmc system) so everything is kind of skewed in that direction . . .

                                I rather like the Purified as a template, and expanding the rules for them is something I'm particularly interested in. I've enjoyed reading everyone else's approach to the subject quite a bit. Here's my write-up

                                            I think this goes to show that given an initial condition and a final condition, there's enough in the WoD for people do do the intermediary steps in different ways! I'm really happy that resurrecting this thread has led to such awesome discussion.

                                            My decision to make the Influences more narrow exists because I think that it should be a limitation similar to the limitations of other spirits. Beyond the initial dots of Influence, I think that the only way of expanding one's repertoire should be through consuming spirits or harvesting a ton of Essence from a locus with those kinds of qualities, just like what spirits do to broaden their areas of Influence. We're approaching the psychology of the Purified in different ways (i.e., I don't consider them spirits of humanity), and this difference definitely shows in the thought processes we've had surrounding Influences! I do like the idea of having a Spirit name in Twilight and Shadow, and I will probably incorporate that.

                                            Through some conversations, I realized that I have a sentence in the document that should probably actually be its own section. My general assumption is that the ritual, poison experience, or quick, fast, and dirty version of the Purification Ritual create a mote of Essence that comes from both the ritual/suicide and the locus itself. The Essence is at high spin (after all, it has coalesced into a compact body from a dispersed area, much like a protoplanetary accretion disk) and on the exact spot where the Candidate is standing, so when the Candidate dies, their mind and soul are essentially ripped apart on the Shadow side where they are standing and infused into that mote of Essence. The spirit that comes out of it starts at Rank 2 because the Purification process and the meshing of the human soul into a mote of a lot of Essence as opposed to the tiny motes that birth regular spirits is a really powerful generator. The addition of the human soul and mind to the mix provides the spirit with a structural grounding (as the mind is reconstructed from the fragments and the soul parts absorbed; this is why Purified cannot be ghosts) and a fast tie to the dead body lying on the locus. At the end of the process, the creation of the Purified from the two components "flings" them from the locus to a random point in Shadow, and then the Purified has to walk back to the Shire.

                                            Question for you: Influences work in body in your case? How has that worked out in game play?

                                                        Siddhi of Summoning


                                                        Anyone may summon a Spirit, if they know the proper rituals. The Purified do not need to learn the proper rituals. Instead, the Siddhi of Summoning ensures that when they call, spirits answer.
                                                        Coming-and-Going Ways (Summoning •)
                                                        Cost: 1 - 3 Essence
                                                        Dice Pool: Presence + Persuasion + Chi + Gauntlet Strength Modifier versus Resistance
                                                        Action: Instant
                                                        Duration: 1 Scene

                                                        One of the Purified may exceed the normal, mortal limits on summoning Spirits. The Purified may pay the cost of certain Manifestation conditions for a Spirit; this roll is only penalized if the Spirit does not wish to have their Manifestation conditions paid for. Coming-and-Going Ways may only be activated in an area with an Open condition corresponding to the Spirit they are attempting to bring forth.

                                                        This Siddhi does not have to target a specific spirit; however, the Purified must specify at least which type of Spirit they wish to call forth, at which point this Siddhi's effects are applied to whatever corresponding spirit is closest.

                                                        The Purified spends their chosen amount of Essence and then rolls:

                                                        Dramatic Failure: The Purified severely botches their invitation; the area they were trying to summon the spirit into loses the Open Condition.
                                                        Failure: The Purified fails to draw the Spirit from Twilight.
                                                        Success: The benefits of this Siddhi vary based on the amount of Essence spent on it:
                                                        • 1 Essence: The Purified either activates the Image Manifestation on behalf of the Spirit, or grants the spirit the Reaching condition.
                                                        • 2 Essence: The Purified activates the Fetter Manifestation on behalf of the Spirit, choosing which object or creature the Spirit becomes Fettered to.
                                                        • 3 Essence: The Purified activates the Materialize Manifestation on behalf of the Spirit.
                                                        Each Manifestation or Condition uses the number of successes rolled for Coming-and-Going Ways for the purposes of determining how long they last.
                                                        Exceptional Success: Extra successes are their own reward.

                                                        Summoning the Wandering Shade(Summoning ••)
                                                        Cost: 1 Essence

                                                        While it is far more strenuous, Purified may extend the benefits of their sponsorship to Ghosts as well as spirits, tracing their will through the place in their soul where their Death should be.

                                                        Whenever the Purified uses Coming-and-Going Ways, they may spend an additional point of Essence to affect a Ghost instead of a Spirit.

                                                        Calling Distant Names (Summoning •••)
                                                        Cost:                                                         1 Essence

                                                        Of course, it is not truly summoning if you can only bring forth what is already there, isn't it?

                                                        Whenever the Purified uses Coming-and-Going Ways, they may spend an additional point of Essence to have it summon a Spirit (or Ghost) from anywhere, regardless of distance. If the Purified's roll for Coming-and-Going Ways is successful, the Ephemeral being takes one turn to arrive at the Open Condition that the Purified is standing within, at which point the normal effects of a success for Coming-and-Going Ways take effect.

                                                        Using Calling Distant Names applies the Mystical Connections modifier to the roll.

                                                            In my opinion the Summoning Siddhi from the book do not need any change. Your version only works at places where the Open Condition is already present, which means that the character sometime needs to do a lot of research and to laborous mortal rituals to create this condition. I do not really like this.

                                                            I would just use the original version, with the addition that all levels generate the Open Condition (or at least the Resonant Condition).

                                                            Enabling Manifestations also isn't really necessary I think. Purified can already see beings in Twilight, removing the need for the Image Manifestation. Granting Reaching does not make sense in terms of theme, especially as the spirit will come to you after the summoning, which means that Reaching is not necessary. Letting the spirit Materialize is already part of the original third power. The only manifestation that really misses in the original power set, is Fetter, but you could also have summoned a spirit with Fetter in the first place, if this is that important to you.

                                                            EDIT: I do think your new version is nice and coherent, even if I think the changes are unnecessary.
