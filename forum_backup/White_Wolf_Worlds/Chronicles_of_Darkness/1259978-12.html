                            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                            Please; I was asking for serious advice.
                            Generally, blocking people that only ever have inane contributions is pretty liberating.

                            For the topic at hand - I don't have anything on the Vault yet, but my wife has published some tabletop content before. What we usually did was look around for artists whose work we liked - on Artstation, DeviantArt, or even Tumblr - and then messaged them about commissions. Prices tend to vary pretty extremely - only you can really judge whether or not something is worth it to you, but the most important part about exploring pricing like this is to ask several artists for a commission quote on the same piece.
