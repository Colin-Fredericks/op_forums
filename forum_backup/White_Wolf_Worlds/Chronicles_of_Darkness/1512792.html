    [STV] Pretty Guardian Faerie Knights

    05-25-2023, 07:32 PM
    I've finally gone and published my first Storyteller's Vault product: The Bishoujo Senshi Yosei Kishi, or Pretty Guardian Faerie Knights.

    They're a Changeling focused Conspiracy that actually makes bargains with what they call the Seelie Fae in order to take on the blessings of Pactio and fight monsters.

    They're magical girls in the Chronicles of Darkness and their themes blend the inherent despair of the neverending Vigil with an optimism and hope for the future.

                          I love this product; though I personally treat them as a variation of the Fae-Touched from Changeling rather than as a Hunter "Conspiracy". It just feels like a more natural fit.

                          (In a similar way, I'd be inclined to take the Hunter 1e "Cainite Heresy" and "Les Mystères" Conspiracies and remake them as variants of VtR 2e's ghouls and WtF 2e's wolf-blooded, respectively. Basically, any Hunter group that seems to partake of the powers of another gameline feel more like they ought to be variants of the "lesser templates" associated with those gamelines.)

                          The other way I'd consider doing it would be as an Acanthus Proximus Dynasty. Given how Mage 2e's Supernal World is an overlay of the Fallen World, that would make that version of Faerie Knights deal with a "Dark Forest" that's more like the chimerical world of Changeling: the Dreaming than the otherworldly realm of Changeling: the Lost's Hedge. That might be more helpful in crossover games where the Dark Forest might otherwise get sidelined. And if you still want the CtL-style Hedge, remember that Supernal Arcadian symbols aren't exclusive to the material world, and might even be stronger and/or more common in the Hedge…
