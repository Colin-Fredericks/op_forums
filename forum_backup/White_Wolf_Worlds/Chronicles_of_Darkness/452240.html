    Deed-names

    03-21-2015, 11:39 PM
    Given how often the concept of deed-names comes up*, I'm surprised at how little they are talked about in the 2e book. I just ran a search and the phrase comes up once, and then it was just to say that Bone Shadows list them off when gathering. There's no discussion of them being a thing in the greater Uratha culture, nor is there anything about how one gets one.
    Was the concept dropped entirely in this edition? If so, I'm curious as to why that was.


    *several to most of the stories in the Idigam Chronical Anthology feature or talk about them, most of the characters I've seen mentioned in passing here on the forums have them, two of the three wolves in Chris' game have them and the other is specifically called out as not having one

              It probably remains, just exists as something they couldn't elaborate on due to word count.

              When you're introducing a character to someone who in the know, it makes sense you'd still rely on a more Earthly identity. The deed names make more sense as a wolf and Hisil thing, something your totem and other spirits in the area might call you. A name the beshilu might fear or know means a pushover. I have one for my character but so far everyone's called him by his 'real' name.

              I'm pretty sure renown brands were carved into you in first edition similarly to how they are now, I don't think that's changed much.
