                                                After looking through the Kickstarter PDF, I thought to put my head to how the hybrid Agendas might work:
                                                •Saboteur-Traitor: Perhaps the most obvious, these are holy warriors of every stripe, from noble(if bitter) paladins to terrorists with a cause, with a mind to poison the hearts of mortals to "the demons in our midst!"
                                                •Integrator-Traitor: A difficult mix, only rivaled by Integrator-Saboteurs. In the end, the best idea I have for these are missionaries who hope to find a way to "infect" the Machine with their personal ideology through merging with it.
                                                •Inquisitor-Traitor: Theologians and Occultists, they believe their God requires them to learn or discover something, or that their knowledge will earn them salvation. Tend to have greater knowledge of other supernaturals than most Inquisitors.
                                                •Tempter-Traitors: Patrons and Priests , the ones who put a lot in the collection plate either because they can or because they think they need to buy salvation. Tend to be some form of ethical hedonist as opposed to their less constrained counterparts.

                                                        In World of Darkness, sin is power. People feel better if they do “bad things” in name of they Vice. Monsters of the night rave in they darker side, gaining strength in return. Fallen are demons that after rebelion against God-Machine were confronted with existence as creature of Vice. After the Fall, they could empower by serving it. Also, sinning is so sweet.

                                                        And then they find there is literal Hell. Not metaphorical dimension of freedom most demons looking for. But real, Old Testament type Hell. Many call it Inferno. But to Fallen it’s called home. Or at least they want to. For now, it’s place were they Demon Prince patrons live.

                                                        Those ancient beings, old as humanity itself, are very interested in once angels, now demons.They send they lesser servants, spirits, ghosts and other things transformed by the sins they serve. And those massangers are preaching to Unchained so they coud join in Inferno. Some of them do. And so Fall lead to the Fallen.

                                                        But of course they don't forget about God-Machine. As false tyrant that once rules they lives, it should be stopped. Or at least every beautiful angel he makes been brought to Inferno's Hosts. And Fallen will make them Fall.

                                                        The Descent: Most Unchained wants freedom. They look for Hell in what there aren’t any masters and slaves, only themselves. But without other beings, there isn’t any sociaty, there isn’t hierarchy. Hell is empty and all the devils are here - like they say. But Fallen don’t want to be alone. As once angels, they crave for belonging, orders, purpose. But they can’t return to the God-Machine. They found human side in themselves, individuality, identity.They don’t want to loose those precious gifts of Fall. And so they decide they will reign in literall Hell instead.

                                                        Fallen are both masters and slaves. They work for one of the great Patrons, doing “his work”. But always dreaming on the day when they walk in to Inferno, and took the throne for themselves. They will finally be those that send commands, not obeying them.

                                                        But for now, they join with rest of Unchained. They forging Vice in to powerful tool of they both inner strength and influence on others, They preach to demons about they own free will, and that they should rule. They tempt angels in to Fall. And of course, they hide outside of eyes of God-Machine, as it hunts the like the rest of the kind. But at least they have purpose in they life. Someday, when they will be powerful enough, they will Descent in to Inferno and be it’s masters. Now will they gather the sins of the world.

                                                        Organization: Fallen join in loose cults dedicated to one of Patrons. They idealise Seven Deadly Sins, working to sustain it in the world, and by it, empowering themselves for they Descent. From time to time they make loose alliances between cults, especially, as they local mission dictates help from in name of many sins.
