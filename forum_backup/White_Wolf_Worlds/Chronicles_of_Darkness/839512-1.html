                            I suppose writing things half asleep while in a car tends to create quick replies without elaboration on my end. I'll address what I mean.

                            I meant that it's easier to write a game involving werecreatures as opposed to nontraditional entities.


                            Originally posted by nofather View Post

                            You mean like a spirit of the hunt? Werewolves aren't animals, they're hunters.
                            Yes but werewolves also tie that in with wolves, it's kind of self explanatory really in their title.
                            If you want to do a different concept without relating to another animal at all, you need to do more work. Not just in mechanics and crunch, but also in writing. Animals come prepackaged with loads of concepts that they relate to that you can draw from and select.



                            I suppose that's true, no one in the history of fiction has ever turned into fire or other elements, or transformed into an anthropomorphic representative of other concepts, like Sandman's Endless. And there are a lot of movies about weregazelle.
                            Shapeshifting would be harder to write as well.
                            It'd would be possible here, but any writer would need to go through more creative hoops. This is especially true when you attach that restriction of "no animals". There's work arounds like angelic beings and other supernatural stuff like living shadows and bones, and whatever... but such fantastic forms are not always suited towards the camoflague role.

                            You'd also need to expend more creative thought to designing the forms, more if you do try to expand on it to equal the five forms of the animal/human scale.
                            It would be easy sure to create the human, near human, and half-human-and-other forms. Self explanatory. The others would be dicier.

                            I mean for instance. Let's take an earth elemental from say a mountain god.

                            He's got:
                            Man form
                            Tougher human form.
                            A form like a stone golem.

                            So is he supposed to turn into a boulder for his other forms. I guess it's camo, but well, is he supposed to remain in animate?
                            The same goes for "weretree"
                            This gets even more confusing when you want to do "Werecities"; does an entire city block spawn from no where? What about were-emotions?
                            I mean, how does your completely non-human form work without doing animals peroid?


                            To make a long story short, I take the view that it's easier to write about werecreatures than other things. Perhaps you can call it laziness.

                                What do mountains symbolize? Permanence, immovability, fortitde, obstacles on paths to greater things or danger. From there you have long-lived and hearty beings that challenge others to better themselves or treacherously to doom them.
