Iron itself is a fun element (being the point at which fusion nor fission nets your more energy).

            Cast iron is going to be mostly pure since many of the impurities are either burnt away or separated during smelting. The whole smelting process and relative softness of iron (which was really rather hard and holds a better edge when compared to bronze) was one of the driving forces behind the invention of steel. In some galactic twist of irony the semi-conducter revolution went through this same process where at first we though almost pure silicon was the way to go when making microchip and then realized we can get way better performance when we adulterate the silicon with other materials and eventually replaced most semi-conductor manufacturing with gallium-arsenide.
