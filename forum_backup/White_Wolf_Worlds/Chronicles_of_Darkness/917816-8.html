                                                    Basically, there are two ways this goes.

                                                    One, it's as Charlaquin and others are saying; functionally, you are the same person, and you don't even know anything has changed. In this case, there's really no difference at all. It has no effect, for gameplay or otherwise, on the game. It's really a pointless and academic concept. You can't really do anything with the information that changes the game.

                                                    In the other, you are aware of this fact. In this case, you're basically playing with Demon's themes, not Vampire's. You are a monster that took someone else's life as your own, trying to mimic it as best as possible. That's a fine theme, but it's already covered by Demon. Vampire has its own themes, and this concept damages those themes in favor of another line's themes. It's true that CofD covers these themes with some lines. That's perfectly fine, and I find those lines interesting for their own reasons. But Vampire is not Demon or Promethean. It's Vampire. And Vampire's mechanics and fluff should support its own themes, not another line's.
