        Relic: The Colt ●●●●●
        “The Colt” is a heavy revolver made by Samuel Colt in 1835. When used with the supernatural bullets made specifically for it, the gun can kill nearly any supernatural being.
        The blessed rounds fired by the Colt are a deadly bane to any supernatural being. Against Supernatural targets, the weapon deals 4 aggravated damage with the Rote quality, and bypasses any armor or damage reducing abilities the target might have (even to targets in Twilight). Even creatures that might otherwise be immortal can be destroyed permanently by the Colt.
        Damage: 2L (against mortals) Initiative: -2 Capacity: 6 Range: 20/40/80

        Relic: The Impala ●●
        Dean’s 1967 Chevrolet Impala does not technically have any supernatural or unusual properties, but means far more to the Winchester’s than just a mode of transport. The Impala has the Battery of Will ability (Reliquary, pg. 88). This ability may only be used by Sam or Dean. To anyone else, the Impala is just a sweet ride.

        Relic: Ruby’s Knife ●●●●
        Given to the Winchester’s by the demon known as Ruby, the knife has properties that allow it to slay demons.
        Against Demons and those Possessed or Claimed by demons (similar to the type detailed in Inferno), the weapon deals 3 damage, has the 8-again quality, and is considered a Bane (dealing Aggravated Damage and bypassing any Armor or other damage reducing abilities). Some high ranked Demons have been known to resist the effect of the knife, but they remain thankfully rare.
        Damage: 1L (against mortals) Initiative: -1

                                    Freddy Krueger
                                    Rank: 3 [Ghost]
                                    Attributes: Power 8, Finesse 8, Resistance 6
                                    Willpower: 10
                                    Essence: 20
                                    Initiative: 14
                                    Defense: 8
                                    Speed: 21
                                    Size: 5
                                    Corpus: 11
                                    Influences: Nightmares 4
                                    Ban: Freddy is wounded by virtue. Characters fighting Freddy who have regained willpower from their virtue gain a +2 bonus to all dice rolls, resistance traits and Armor against Freddy for the remainder of the scene.
                                    Bane: Fire
                                    Manifestation: Fetter, Possess, Unfetter, Twilight Form
                                    Numina: Aggressive Meme, Dement, Hallucination, Phantom Arsenal, Seek, Telekinesis

                                    Notes
                                    I am using the character as depicted in the original A Nightmare on Elm Street series (primarily 1 -3).

                                    ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

                                    Jason Voorhees

                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 1, Wits 2, Resolve 5
                                    Physical Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 3, Stamina 6
                                    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 1, Composure 5
                                    Skills: Athletics 3 (Pursuit), Brawl 3(Choke), Crafts 2, Firearms 2, Larceny 2, Intimidation 4 (Silent), Stealth 4(Ambush, Forest), Survival 3(Tracking), Weaponry 4
                                    Merits: Atavism, Choke Hold, Direction Sense, Giant, Heavy Weapons Style 3, Improvised Weaponry Style 1, Iron Stamina 3
                                    Willpower: 10
                                    Size: 6
                                    Initiative: 8
                                    Defense: 6
                                    Armor: 1/1
                                    Speed: 14
                                    Health: 11


                                    Dread Powers/ Talents & Frailties

                                    Blinded by Blood: All Perception rolls made by Jason suffer a –3 penalty. On any opposed rolls made to notice a hiding opponent, ties always go to the opponent.

                                    Crushing Blow: 1w; All bashing damage that Jason deals with his body (fist, foot, head, elbow, whatever) is instead done as lethal damage. This power lasts until the end of the scene.

                                    No Mind but for Murder: Jason cannot speak, write or generate language in any meaningful way. He might be able to indicate a direction or intent to kill with a nod of the head or a wave of the knife, but complicated gestures are beyond him.

                                    Revivify ●●●●●: 1w; Stamina + Survival + Revivify. Jason can use this power to return from the dead, provided his entire health track isn’t full of aggravated damage. Successes heal two bashing or one lethal damage each. On an exceptional success Jason rises fully healed of all damage, aggravated or otherwise.

                                    Unholy Stamina ●●: 1w; Add +3 to Stamina until the end of the scene.

                                    Unholy Strength ●●:1w; Add +2 to Stamina until the end of the scene.

                                    Unstoppable Killing Machine: Jason does not suffer wound penalties and does not need to check for unconsciousness when his Health track fills with bashing damage. He does not need sleep, food or air. Every successful attack made on Jason inflicts one point of damage, no matter how many successes are actually rolled. This includes supernatural attacks. Incidental damage (falling, electrocution, drowning, fire) inflicts its usual damage.

                                    Notes
                                    This write up is Jason as he appeared in the Friday the 13th series (primarily 3, 4, and 6)
                                    Jason is ungodly strong and tough, and has taken a degree of injury that would have killed anyone else several times over… and that was before he became undead. Jason is developmentally disabled and therefore has low intelligence and manipulation, while his stature and demeanor give him a high presence. Jason is relentless and remorseless, and though he has proven vulnerable to trickery, he largely remains focused on his single minded rampage. Systems wise, Jason is the archetypal Mask Undertaking Slasher, with a collection of Dread Powers to augment his abilities further.

                                        Excellent write ups! Much better than the True Fae versions I wrote elsewhere. Only one problem: Freddy needs to be Rank 4 before he can have Influence (Nightmares) 4. If you make him Rank 4, I'd suggest raising either Power or Resistance. I'd also give him the Avernian Gate Manifestation as well as the Regenerate Numen. Finally, he'd have the Cunning Virtue, Sadistic Vice, and Integrity 0.                                         I'd also use these ephemeral being house rules:

                                        Normal Attack = Power + Rank

                                        Willpower = Resistance + Rank (max 10)

                                        Maximum Essence / Per Turn = Rank x 10/Rank x 3

                                        Initiative = Finesse + Resistance/2 round up

                                        Defense = Finesse + Rank/2 round up (max 10)

                                        Speed = highest between Power and Finesse + Rank

                                        Corpus = Size + Resistance, as before

                                        Thus, if Freddy had Power 9, Finesse 7 and Resistance 12, he would have Willpower 10, Max Essence 40, Max Essence Per Turn 12, Initiative 10, Defense 6, Speed 13 and Corpus 17. Seems more balanced to me.

The only thing I disagree on Integrity - a ghosts Integrity is fixed at the level it was when they were alive.Freddy was a serial killer who was taking frequent breaking points for killing, but was together enough to keep his activities well hidden for a long time - I would peg him at a Integrity 4.

                                            As for Jason, I tried to restrain myself a bit. It would be too easy to give him every combat merit at 5 and call it good, but I went over the movies (particularly 3and 4, when he was still "mortal", and 6 (when he became an undead killing machine). That being said, after looking at the Heavy Weapons and Frenzied Assault Merits, Jason having 5 in each would not be inappropriate (campers beware).

                                            Once again, we are in agreement on the Virtue/Vice for Jason, but Integrity 0/1 feels like a poor mainly (mainly because Jason may not be human enough for something like Integrity to be applied).
                                            Its a question that I think may be answered with Hunter 2nd edition. Many of the slasher rules were tied to Morality, and Integrity operates different on both a mechanical and thematic level.

                                                I decided to make stats for the Slender Man, as a God-Machine Angel.

                                                Programming: Psychopomp

                                                Virtue: Hidden

                                                Vice: Observant

                                                Rank: 5

                                                Attributes: Power 15, Finesse 9, Resistance 12

                                                Corpus: 20 (Size 8)

                                                Willpower: 10

                                                Defense: 7

                                                Initiative Mod: 11

                                                Speed: 20

                                                Max Essence/Per Turn: 50/15

                                                Influence: Perceptions 5, Sigma Radiation 5

                                                Manifestation: Twilight Form, Materialize, Avernian Gateway, Shadow Gateway, Astral Sojourn

                                                Ban: the Slender Man is immensely powerful, but he is constrained by the power of the human mind. At the start of each chapter (game session), the Slender Man uses the Aggressive Meme Numen, spreading an evolving mythos about itself to the people of the region. If he steps out of the rules of this mythos, he is reduced to Power 1, Finesse 1 and Resistance 1 (with decreased Advantages) and loses access to Numina, Influences and Manifestations for one scene. The rules must be reasonably coherent in the consciousnesses of the people in the region.

                                                Bane: anyone who was taken by the Slender Man to a Facility, reconfigured for the God-Machine, reclaimed their original configuration and returned to their home will deal aggravated damage to the Slender Man with all Brawl, Weaponry and Firearms rolls.

                                                Numina: Aggressive Meme, Ephemeral Weapon, Ephemeral Armor, Essence Thief, Regenerate, Mirage, Speed

                                                    an old favorite -

                                                    Alien/Xenomorph Warrior
                                                    Xenomorphs are terrifying opponents. They are fast, strong, and tough, bristling with natural weapons, including claws, two sets of jaws, spear like tail, and corrosive blood. The entire gruesome package is wrapped in a durable exoskeleton and guided by savage instincts that seem bent on expanding the colony and consuming everything in their path. They operate much like social insects, with larva, drones, warriors, and a Queen all operate as a part of a hive-like collective.

                                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 1, Wits 3, Resolve 4
                                                    Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 5, Stamina 4
                                                    Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 1, Composure 2
                                                    Skills: Athletics 3(Climbing), Brawl 3 (bite/claw), Crafts 2 (Resin), Intimidation 3, Larceny 2, Stealth 4, Survival 4
                                                    Merits: Fast Reflexes 3, Fleet of Foot 2, Hardy 2
                                                    Willpower: 6
                                                    Size: 5
                                                    Initiative: 10
                                                    Defense: 8
                                                    Armor: 2/1
                                                    Speed: 19 (Species Factor 8)
                                                    Health: 9

                                                    Dread Powers

                                                    Agility: The Xenomorphs are fast and mobile. They can scale across walls and ceilings with ease (of course, they are still affected by gravity and are limited to structures than can support their weight). They may squeeze through narrow spaces as if they were one size level smaller (Humanoid Incubated Aliens are Size 5). In addition, they can leap 4 times as far as a human.

                                                    Animalistic: Xenomorphs are guided by instinct and operate on the level of exceptionally clever animals. They may even figure out rudimentary uses of simple devices but anything more complicated is beyond them. They communicate with each other with a complex series of pheromone signals and their vocalizations consist primarily of shrieks, hisses and growls. The creature’s instincts allow it to use the Higher of Wits or Dexterity for Defense.

                                                    Carapace: Xenomorph’s are covered in a durable, glassy exoskeleton. They are considered to have Armor 2/1. Xenomorph’s do not suffer wound penalties and do not fall unconscious until their rightmost health box is filled with lethal damage. Their dark coloration gives them an additional +2 dice to Stealth rolls made under cover of darkness or in poor lighting. The armor bonus granted from their Carapace does not apply to injuries caused by fire or extreme heat.

                                                    Fearless: The Xenomorphs mindset does not recognize fear in most situation and they are immune to any fear causing supernatural compulsion. Likewise, any method of supernatural coercion that works on humans or animals automatically fails as well. If successfully intimidated by mundane means, the Xenomorph will retreat and more than likely stage another attack at a later time. The only thing that seems to honestly frighten the creatures is fire (see further down).

                                                    Natural Weapons: The Xenomorph makes use of several distinct natural weapons

                                                    Bite/Claws: The Xenomorph can attack interchangeably with their claws and mandibles, treating either as a +0 weapon attack.

                                                    Corrosive Viscera: The Xenomorph’s blood is incredibly corrosive. Any creature or object that comes in contact with the substance is damaged as if burned by Bunsen burner (+2 damage). The corrosive blood of a Xenomorph is considered to have Armor Piercing 2.

                                                    Mandible: Xenomorphs have a secondary set of jaws, which lashes out with incredible speed and force. The additional power allows the mandible to punch through armored resistance. This is a +1 weapon attack with Armor Piercing 2.

                                                    Tail Barb: The Xenomorphs long whip-like tail ends in a sharp barb. This is a +2 weapon attack with Armor Piercing 1.

                                                    Resin: Xenomorphs can secrete a durable, sticky resin. They use this resin to create hive like structures or cocoon victims to be eaten or impregnated with larvae at a later time. This substance has a Durability of 2 and a xenomorph can create an amount of Resin equal to its size every 24 hours (with regular meals).

                                                    Senses: The creature’s sensory organs are shielded behind the transparent carapace of its face. It can see effectively in pure darkness and has a keen sense of smell.

                                                    Vulnerability: The creatures fear fire; they must make a (Resolve +Composure – (Size of the Fire)) roll to approach an open flame. Characters brandishing open fire as a weapon gain a +2 equipment bonus to Intimidate checks against a Xenomorph.
