                        05-01-2020, 10:34 PM
                        Originally posted by Horodrigo View Post
                        About the Darkling blessing of 2ed.

                        Is it possible to become a part of a laser beam?
                        "Touch something insubstantial […] transubstantiating into smoke, shadow, a sunbeam — whatever's handy."

                        As a storytelling tool this is pretty clearly keyed to things that an imaginary viewing audience could perceive through narration. Laser beams in fiction are generally either substantial enough to harm you or invisible outside of a medium that would itself be a valid subject for the seeming blessing.

                        If the answer is yes, them, if I point the laser 1km away and them shut it down, where do I reappear? At the end, middle or beginning of the beam?
                        Where you started or at whatever point along the length you moved to at your normal Speed in the intervening time. It's a power that lets you move unseen and somewhat unfettered by gravity, not a means of turning a high-powered flashlight into a makeshift bullet train.

                        Also, can I meld my gear(backpack) with me when I use this ability or my clothes just fall into the ground?
                        What literature have you read where the latter would be the way magic powers used by fairies works?

                        If you're wearing it or carrying it and it's not substantially larger than a person, it can probably be assumed to go with you.

                                            Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                            So, for example, if a fae captures a newborn and turns him into a crying "teddy bear" for his fae "children", then if the real mother rescues the newborn he could return as a Bestial Playmate?
                                            See "old enough to be a person" and "meaningfully experienced anything." Infantile amnesia and an unengaging task are going to make developing the Clarity of this character challenging in a pretty dull fashion.

                                            The literature tends to indicate that the Fae are more likely to kidnap a mortal for a proportionally superficial or obsolete reason (pretty eyes on a plain assistant librarian, left in an attic after the untimely demise of the Lord they were to be gifted to, etc.) than they are to send for a transdimensional acquisition to make easily-replaceable qualities, which means your hypothetical changeling newborn is special enough that he probably doesn't secure escape or rescue until he's old enough to understand that being a noisemaking comfort object for monsters is more screwed up than normal.

                                            I'm not sure what the market is for literal infants as far as playable characters go, and there's ample room to argue that recovering a mortal newborn from Faerie is always "early" enough in the process that their fetch disintegrates and the child can grow up as normal as any other character with a supernatural encounter of that intensity in their background does.
