                                  Originally posted by Yumlum View Post

                                  How are Wraith different from Ghost in CofD?
                                  Wraiths aren't substantial different in most ways, but everything else is. Wraiths struggle to visit the living world, there's vast organizations and machinery, even to the point of forging souls into currency (IIRC, it's been a while). Meanwhile in CofD the ghosts typically start in twilight which is basically the skin lands, the individual realms in the underworld are more independent and ruled by godlike beings.

                                  Now as to ghosts and wraiths specifically, wraiths have a part of themselves urging them towards oblivion but that isn't something that would be immediately obvious.

                                              Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                                              How are Wraith different from Ghost in CofD?
                                              Wraiths are souls of the dead, while Ghosts are explicitly not - they're sapient echoes of the dead. It's a very esoteric difference that wouldn't be obvious to mundane witnesses, or even to the dead themselves - both Wraiths and Ghosts are people, after all. Still, the same supernatural powers (Arcanoi or Ceremonies) and cosmic forces (Oblivion or the Underworld) may affect Wraiths and Ghosts differently as a result.
