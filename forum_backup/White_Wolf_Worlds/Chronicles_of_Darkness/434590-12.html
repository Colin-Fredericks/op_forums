        Having Marduk destroyed by an alliance of Leviathans after they've been improving themselves for thousands of years and Leviathans have not completely goes against the theme.
        Think of it as a "pruning" -- at the time, Marduk was still, on some level, a cabal of immortal "magi" who consumed Leviathan flesh to live. In terms of theme, consuming Leviathan flesh for immortality would definitely carry a cost on a symbolic level -- why not assuming the curse of stagnation? Sure, Marduk's tech advanced, but until the deaths of the Wise Old Men who ran the whole show at the hands of Leviathans, they were using the same pseudo-ritualistic, hyper-secretive occult conspiracy methodology that served them faithfully in the early days of civilization. The extermination of those who consumed Leviathan flesh allowed them to grow as an organization; they haven't conquered the world yet because their leaders still eat mermaids to survive, which gives us a good reason for there to be a status quo as opposed to "Marduk will rule the world in fifty years and their Golden Age is only a matter of time."

        In other words, it's in theme -- Leviathans can do big showy shit and utterly wreck the day of the conspirators, because they're good at waging war and they've got cults everywhere, but their success is ultimately Pyrrhic and marks a mere blip on their path to eventual extinction; only their corruption in Marduk's ranks keeps them from being chum.

If Leviathan were directly parallel to the Chaoskampf, it wouldn't be a game, because our protagonists would be electrocuted corpses -- Leviathan's got Lovecraft in it, too, not just Babylon, and part of Lovecraft is that the descendants of Dagon have to be a legitimate threat on some level.

It's the same with the Pentacle, for example -- the Silver Ladder is idealistic but has the ethical lure of temporal power and an excuse to ignore Wisdom. The WoD clearly doesn't require absolute conformity to the themes set out in canon game lines; why should we do that when the alternative is a) interesting and b) justified in a Watsonian sense?
