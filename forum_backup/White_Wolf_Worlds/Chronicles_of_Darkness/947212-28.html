                                                    Those are a bit much. How fun would it be to play a character whose concept is, 'Kill yourself or the world ends.' Every choice you made besides turning yourself in is pure selfishness. Plus you would never be able to justify it to anyone. 'Well, I didn't want my family and everything I loved to end, but I understand the hobo life is for you and you don't like the government. No hard feelings about stealing my money, either, a hobo's gotta eat, right?'

                                                    It's still the Chronicles of Darkness, nothing is so objectively good or evil, or right.

                                                    It's probably the kind of thing you could homebrew but come on. You really want those as one of the primary options? Besides, if it was a matter of life and death for the entire world wouldn't it be likely they could find someone completely willing to do it? Even if it was a matter of 'you are the only one capable of doing this,' for such a grand and necessary goal it seems like they'd, you know, ask you about it first so you didn't wake up, freak out and run.

                                                    At least dim it down to things like the other god not flat out about to destroy the world but, perhaps, destroying their religion. Or not a definitive invasion but 'just in case' such a thing ever happens. And some more indefinite 'we might be able to put your marrow to good use,' rather than 'yes we can definitely cure all these bad things if you just let us cut you open. And yes please don't concern yourself that our bosses are the wealthiest and oldest men in the world, having invested heavily in pharmaceuticals, they're definitely going to share the discoveries they make off your body.'
