        150) The players hear rumors in the Shadow about a mortal in a nearby territory. Suposedly they bear no touch of Wolf or Moon, but the Spirits have actually begun to worship them, converting simply from being in this person's presence. They've attempted to do anything they can to earn their idol's approval, alien mindsets and insane zeal mixing into a number of disasters the pack that runs that area has attempted to cover up. And the most disturbing part? After encountering the other pack in person, the PCs realize that the half-Spirit children of Wolf and Moon are merely resistant to this bewitchment. Already, the other pack has become violently territorial and willing to do anything to protect their "precious"...
        150B) As above, but the other pack is Pure, a mostly Fire-Touched group that have convinced themselves that this person is their messiah, who they're planing to kidnap so they can "educate" them as to their "destiny". Will the PCs be able to save this person without being aflicted?

            151) It was almost undetectable at first. The world's population of rats began to decline, first subtly and then quite rapidly. Within a matter of months, any rat that wasn't already in a cage had seemingly vanished off the face of the planet. The humans were preparing to investigate the phenomenon, and that's when everything went to shit. An explosion of spiritual essence, several times larger than anything you've ever heard of, right in the heart of United States of America.

            Every physical entity within 200 miles was stricken with leprosy on the spot, unleashing a plague that caused withering and decay in all it touched. Even the great Storm Lords are bristling their fur in realization of what is to come. The Plague King has been reincarnated... and this time, there's no Father Wolf to put him down!

                                                    Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post
                                                    158) ...But then a pack on the outskirts of the protectorate dropped out of contact, and were later found in the service of a Tempter. They're serving it willingly, and they claim that the Demon has figured out how to create Gifts and carve them into Werewolves. Are they legit?                                                     It would mean they are Bale Hounds or at very least be considered as such by other Uratha. Most likely both if they admit to it. I am pretty sure they won't appreciate the nuanced difference between a Vice spirit and/or Vice spirit-claimed and a demon, except they might see the demon as worse because they have a harder time affecting it. Sound like a fun adventure to tempt the PCs on and have them choose between being brutal monsters that slay evil or brutal monsters that serve evil, or possible some other gritty dark choice.

                                                    160) A stern beautiful Norse-looking woman, whom has been said to fly through the air, has been murdering Uratha with near impunity with a silver spear of all things. A couple packs claim to have killed her but later she's back without a scratch and whichever members of the offending pack that advocated dealing with her are violently murdered, either then or eventually. This is story is shared with you by a wild-eyed and tearful Cahalith from the far coast of the continent who is begging to join your pack for protection, because they confess to be hunted by this woman for speaking out against her. The Cahalith hasn't slept, washed, or eaten in days and is quite pitiful. Does the Cahalith deserve help or to be put down for bringing such a being to your city?

                                                    (This version of Nemesis Replaces Stutter Step with Hunter's Sense: Those with the Transgressed Condition, And her Surprise Entrance power only works in scenes with those with the Transgressed Condition), carries a Silver Spear +3L instead of a Dagger, which reforms with her as if it were part of her, and lastly She has Staff 5 Covering these skills Drive, Larceny, Socialize, Streetwise, and Persuasion representing the pool of those that had the Transgressed Condition looking to absolve their Guilty condition as well.

                                                    Transgressed (Persistent)
                                                    Effects: You have Transgressed against a Nemesis and are marked for punishment. You can feel the weight of your sin upon you like a lead blanket, and feel the dread of something is coming to punish you. This feeling begins as soon as you get this condition and lasts until you don't have it anymore. You are a valid target of her Surprise Entrance and Hunter's Sense Dread Powers. Also gain Embarrassing Secret or Notorious Condition depending on whether this Transgression is known as those that know of your mark will treat you differently.
                                                    Cause: Opening the Doors of others to act against the Nemesis, attempting to use hard leverage against the Nemesis, deliberately causing any type of injury to the Nemesis. Any of the previous against the Nemesis' s patron.
                                                    Resolution: Sincerely confess your guilt to the Nemesis, beg for forgiveness and accept the Guilty and Broken conditions if she forgives you through her Doors being opened only through soft leverage. Die at the hands of the Nemesis.
                                                    Beats: The Nemesis uses Surprise Entrance to enter a scene you are in.

                                                    Note: Concessions to alleviate the Guilty Condition are requested in the form of bringing others whom have Transgressed to the Nemesis.

                                                    STORYTELLING TIP: The reason Uratha have yet to be spared is the trend to pridefully attack or attempt to intimidate threats and how unlikely they are to sincerely confess and beg for forgiveness. In this matter mortals have a great advantage over Uratha, they know their frailty.
