            Mesmerize on its own might not work with that command since it's too vague (edited my post to reflect that since I forgot about that limitation) but Dominate victims aren't mindless automatons. They're still themselves and should carry out commands as they would naturally interpret them (as long as the commands are valid for the used Dominate power). That said I think a simple Merit could allow someone to default to the simplest possible resolution of the command rather than carrying it out naturally. Perhaps: Literal-Minded (•••)

            Making someone speak the truth or recount memories via Dominate does have some overlap with Auspex, but overlap is not a bad thing. It can also be very different since Dominate will get the oral version filtered through the victim's interpretation of their own memories and quite a lot of omissions of seemingly unimportant details that Auspex 4 would show directly to the interrogator. Unlike Auspex, using Dominate in such a way would also not trigger a Clash of Wills against supernaturally altered memories since you're forcing the victim to access the memory rather than accessing it directly.
            For finding out the truth Auspex is still by far superior, with the small drawback that you can't provide any proof (like a confession) for what you claim to have seen, should others doubt you.

                                        Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
                                        Do Kindred lose their sex drive?
                                        Kind of. They lose the hormonal drive, but they can still experience arousal (indeed under the Blush of Life they can evince all the effects of sexual arousal) and have sex. It is still "a nice things to do" (and obviously a good hunting strategy) but there is nothing in their nature actively and directly motivating them towards sex. The ecstasy of feeding replaces all that.

                                        Now, the above is a very general answer. It is strongly implied that Clan and perhaps even Humanity can impact this. Daeva might experience something close to a human sex drive, while Mekhet are at times described as in a state "monks would envy".
