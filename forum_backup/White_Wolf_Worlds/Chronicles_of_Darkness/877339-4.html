                        03-05-2017, 11:51 PM
                        So as the spam of placeholder posts may suggest, I just had an idea that is both wonderful and terrifying. It went something along the lines "The settings included in the 2e books are kinda a snapshot of those locales when the books were written/published. Plus LA has lots of Demon/Mage crossover potential. I should make timelines for them. If I'm doing that, I should include the Seattle Splinters, since that was one of my original motivations for making a timeline of everything. If I'm doing that, I should include Seattle setting from Demon Core. If I'm doing that, I should include the settings from the 1e Core and I set the "eras" extending 10 years back from when the books were published, that give me some eras set in the 90s."

                        "Holy shit that's a lot of eras."

                        So yeah, this is going to keep me busy for a while. Here are the baseless assumptions I've made for the eras I just added.

                        - The "eras" begin 10 years prior to the year first book featuring them was published
                        - The "eras" end the year the last book featuring them was published, even for books like Hunting Grounds: The Rockies, which intentionally and explicitly don't give dates
                        - I've given names to eras that don't have a moniker (mostly 1e settings and the featured regions in MtAw 2e) and used the names of the featured locations otherwise
                        - The focus of these areas in modern, although relevant historical information will be included as deemed necessary
                        - Tokyo is an ongoing setting until OPP says they aren't going to use it anymore. Since the title of the Tokyo setting would be stupidly unwieldy if I included every title, I am simply going to call it "The Crossover"
                        - Eras will be kept chronological in the first post, but I will not continue to rearrange them in individual posts to keep the entire thread chronological.
                        - Due to their unique nature, the Seattle Splinters will get their own section/post.

                        Now, with this massive addition of timelines to this pet project of mine, I appreciate any help people can give, especially if you are from one of the featured locations. Please feel free to post in this thread anything that you think may be useful/interesting, particularly if you think I missed an important event or connection.

                                                            Bad news is that I have once again found a new limitation.
