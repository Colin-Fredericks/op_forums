            Originally posted by miss_minty View Post
            So. Yay. New book. And I'm always all over new demon stuff. So. How much of this if any can I use if I don't have demon the fallen. Like is the translation guide 100% useless without having both cores?
            Notable mechanical widgets that don't at all require Demon: the Fallen to reference or grok:
            • House Conditions: A new Agenda Condition for each of the seven Houses of the Fallen, pretty easily adaptable as custom Agenda Conditions for Descent characters: Stormy Temper, Self-Doubt, Straight Talk, Curiosity, Better with Tools, Cynical and Outsider's View.
            • Converted Merits: Certain Merits from DtF are converted as nWoD Merits, and twelve of these do not reference the original text and do not require conditions native to Fallen and not Descent: Angelic Aura, Angelic Gaze, Atavistic Form, Acute Sense, Forgettable, Good Night Vision, Daredevil, Enchanting Voice, Lucky, Code of Honor, Fast Learner and Eye for a Bargain.
            • Demonic Form Abilities: The apocalyptic form powers are either directed to preexisting analogues in Descent or converted as new demonic form abilities (sometimes both). Nearly all of the converted form abilities provide the full rules to use in Descent, including a sample appearance in Descent's biomechanical aesthetic, and most of the ones that reference the text of Fallen have names that kind of tell you what you need to know anyway (Casts No Reflection and Immune to Poison, for example). There are 11 new Modifications, 15 new Technologies and 11 new Processes. (No new Propulsions.)
            • Hybrid Stories: The final chapter is pure fluff and could be interesting reading for gathering Descent inspiration even if you're not specifically planning to cross over with Fallen.

            Lores are a little more involved. Lores are structured in 1-5 dot scales like vampiric Disciplines, with a new evocation at every dot of the Lore, but not every lore provides converted rules for all five of its evocations; lores that can just port over dice pools and effects fairly straight are omitted and just reference a chart for adjusting difficulties. For the most part, I would say that I would really want to have the DtF book to reference when reading over even the lores with detailed converted rules. There are some evocations that run so long that they might function on their own, but they're not the rule; if nothing else, most lack useful context.

            But for that many usable demonic form abilities in that little space, for that small a price? I'd go for it. My character in my weekly Descent game happened to recently raise Primum, so I've already swapped in Casts No Reflection and Relentless for a swank mechanical stealth form, in addition to a homebrew power that isn't Eyes of Fate, but which was inspired by reading Eyes of Fate.

            — aaand shit, in the process of tallying these up for you I noticed a significant erratum after the errata thread's already closed. The apocalyptic form power Immune to Falling Damage says to substitute Abruption Jets or just use as written in Fallen, but doesn't specify what form ability the "use as written" form falls under. I infer it would probably be a Modification from the trend with similar powers, but it's a significant omission.

            Edit: Removed Good Recognition from the usable Merits list. For running one of the Fallen in the Storytelling System it's fine, if a bit niche, but it's mostly irrelevant to the Unchained, who already exhibit devilish memory.

                                                        08-09-2015, 10:13 PM
                                                        Tolerance and Fuel:
                                                        The character can either have 1 to 10 dots of (permanent) Faith or Primum.
                                                        The character can either have a track of 10 (temporary) Faith boxes, or the standard nWoD fuel box panoply of Aether.
                                                        These are treated as mutually exclusive; mechanics either refer to Faith or Primum/Aether, but not both.

                                                        Integrity:
                                                        The character can have a Torment track, which is treated in the Storytelling System pretty much as Integrity. It's a ten-dot track that can be read either way horizontally. Reading filled dots from the left tallies Integrity, while reading open dots from the right tallies Torment. So, for example, ●●●●●●○○○○ indicates Integrity 6 and Torment 4.
                                                        The character can have Cover ratings, which are a separate ten-dot track for every Cover possessed, up to a maximum number of Covers equal to Primum (or, presumably, permanent Faith).
                                                        These systems work in parallel and don't interfere with one another, so while the Translation Guide generally expects you to track either Torment or Cover, it is possible to have both simultaneously.

                                                        Infernal Ranks:
                                                        The character can be classified by a House and a Faction or an Incarnation and an Agenda.
                                                        In both cases, the former axis determines associated powers (favored Lores or Embeds). However, while straightforward Unchained have an ongoing Condition bestowed by their Agenda, the same mechanic used for Fallen in the Storytelling System derives from their House, not their Faction.
                                                        Therefore, these should probably be treated as mutually exclusive.

                                                        Magic Wizard Spells:
                                                        The character may use the system for Lores or for Embeds and Exploits to import powers from one game into the other.
                                                        The character may also use a hybrid system which assembles Embeds, Exploits and Interlocks into a five-dot progression to produce custom Lores (with the first, second and fourth evocations of the Lore being Key Embeds, the third evocation being an Interlock and the fifth an Exploit). In this hybrid system, a single Embed can even occupy space as a Key in multiple Lores, at the same or differing dot levels.
                                                        Original recipe Lores can also coexist with either these hybrid Lores or with classic Embeds and Exploits. Hybrid Lores do not seem designed to coexist with classic Embeds and Exploits, however.

                                                        This Isn't Even My Final Form:
                                                        The character may either have an apocalyptic form with four normal abilities and four Torment abilities, or a demonic form with three Modifications, two Technologies, one Propulsion, and one Process, developing more abilities as Primum advances or with certain Merits.
                                                        These choices are mutually exclusive, and as apocalyptic forms have Torment abilities, apocalyptic forms can't be used as-is without a Torment track. Powers of one type are converted for use in the other type, however.
