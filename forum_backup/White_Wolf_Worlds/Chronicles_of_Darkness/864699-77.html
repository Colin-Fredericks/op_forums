                Weapons have a different type of equipment modifier from other equipment. A set of lockpicks gives you +2 dice to rolls that can use them. Weapons instead add their modifier to damage (if you hit). A machete gives +2 successes to a successful attack roll, which means +2 damage from being hit by the machete.

                Crafting equipment is limited by what's reasonable for that type of equipment. You can only do what's logical. For example: You can't craft a set of lockpicks that gives you +5 to rolls because that powerful lockpicking tools is ridiculous. You also can't craft a +5 knife because it's practically impossible to make a knife that deadly.

                                    Enhanced Items are items enhanced by spells. Spells treat weapon modifiers and regular equipment bonuses as the same so any increase to the equipment modifier would increase damage.

                                    Enriched Items does specify it as a dice bonus, but since it can grant armour ratings to armours it's perfectly balanced to increase the damage of a weapon instead.

                                    Changeling is pretty good at separating equipment bonuses from weapon damage modifiers (due to rewrite of the core rules, which is why it's called equipment bonus instead of equipment modifier). As such, Blessing of Perfection would not replace the damage of a weapon, but it would replace the equipment bonus that weapon has if it can also be used as a tool. For example, a Wyrd 3 changeling would have +3 on relevant Craft or Survival rolls with their hunting knife but still only do +1 damage with no bonus dice if used as a weapon. (As mentioned, Blessing of Perfection replaces the bonus, not increases.)

                                                        Originally posted by Weaverchilde View Post
                                                        Deviant Question (crossover-ish implications)

                                                        Scars that change a character on a fundamental level, such as giving cybernetics or changing them at the atomic level. Does Inhuman Digestion revert them back to 'normal' for a short period? What about Immunity with Area?

                                                        What should happen when something like Oxblood Mold (NH - EA p.131) comes into contact with a Deviant?
                                                        Effects that shut down Variations and Scars should be judged on a case-by-case basis for what makes narrative sense, usually favoring a lack of radical change. Think of Scars and Variations not as causing but reflecting fundamental changes. You are not a cyborg because you have Computer-Aided Processing and a Frozen Heart. You have Computer-Aided Processing and a Frozen Heart as an outgrowing result of being a cyborg*. Remove these things with something like Inhuman Digestion, even permanently, and you are still Remade. Your cybernetic transformation simply is no longer enhancing your mental processing or dulling your emotional affect. Suppressed Variations and Scars aren't undoing your transformation, but impairing or glitching out the results thereof.

                                                        Note also the specific effects of a written power. Immunity cannot suppress the effects of Scars, and an Area Immunity doesn't distinguish between wanted and unwanted effects, so it doesn't make sense to key Immunity 3's effect to the powers of deviants and apply Area to it, because your own Immunity will attempt to shut down your own Variations (arguably including Immunity itself). Area Immunity can still suppress Variations at lower levels. For example, an Area Immunity to Fire will clash against most uses of Pyrokinesis. Oxblood Mold, meanwhile, just inflicts dice penalties on the use of supernatural powers. It leeches supernatural fuel energies too, but most deviants don't have supernatural fuel energies to leech.

                                                        *If you want to take this a step further, to be perfectly accurate, you have Computer-Aided Processing and a Frozen Heart as a result of suffering Divergence. Your Divergence in turn was caused by your implantation with cybernetics. Variations and Scars are results of your soul cracking, and unmediated metaphysical transformation is how you crack a soul. If you killed a deviant with Computer-Aided Processing without damaging the implants, and then extracted them, they probably wouldn't be able to reproduce the effects of Computer-Aided Processing, unless you installed them in somebody and cracked their soul open.
