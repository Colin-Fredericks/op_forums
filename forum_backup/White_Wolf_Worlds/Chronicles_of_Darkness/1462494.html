    Chapter- In The Shadow of Conspiracies [Tier 2.5]

    10-02-2021, 08:31 PM
    Being the first game to officially introduce Tiers, Hunter added a new level of complexity when designing Chronicles, defining the scale on which it would operate- local, regional or global. While the concept has since evolved, the game, naturally, has tied this scale to the power level of the characters (in a way not that different from Deviant, even though the later took a much more methodical path for it)- basically, higher the stakes, the stronger the character. 1st Tier hunters only need with the monster in the neighborhood, and as such they don't need a wider network or magical abilities to fight them (like, they could help- hunter mortality rate is high, of course, but there is a difference between killing that specific bloodsucker and having to deal with the vampire's enthralled police henchmen, vitae addicted politicians and their other undead allies). Hunters, of course, could deal with threats above or below their tier- but a cell of 1st tier hunters having to deal with a global conspiracy will discover their arsenal is very much underpowered compared to that of a conspiracy.

    While Hunter was the first to establish those lines, it was also very quick to blur them, giving us all sorts of organizations which does not fall nicely within the Tiers- Witchfinders gave use the Promethean Brotherhood as the first "2.5 Tier" org, being a compact rising to be a conspiracy, a rank into which Network 0 later joined. Eventually, we also got other groups in between the Tiers- the Scarlet Watch as a former conspiracy which have lost its Endowments, the Reckoning as a compact which is barely above Tier 1, and the Reclaimers as a Tier 1 cell on its way to become a compact. In all of those instances, the game maintained the strong correlation between power level and Tier, but here and there the seeds for other implementation of the concept were put, slowly taking life of their own- the concept of "conspiracy regional variants".

    The first to represent this idea were the Otodo- originally being presented as a "Japanese faction" of the Lucifuge, Dark Eras did gave their own different origin and philosophy, yet their Endowments were basically Castigation with a different cultural influence, not to mention that while the lines were different their origin was all the same- Hell. Then Dark Eras 2 came and brought the Order of the Black Knight, which was outright stated to serve as a Tier 2 organization yet with a connection to a Tier 3 group- the Aegis Kai Doru, having access to special Endowments drawn from the Arthurian legend. Eventually, the concept was outright stated in 2e, where the book mentioned the existence of localized factions of conspiracies which have Endowments and ties to a larger conspiracy, but act in a semi independent way, and the Council of Bones, while using the term "chapters" to describe its factions, also used it to describe its local factions- and considering that the word "chapter" does follow the Tier C naming convention, I have decided to use it in order to describe this concept in a more detailed way- Tier 2.5 organizations who while are tried to a conspiracy, still act on their own, something I first implemented within (you guessed it) my AKD supplement fanbook.

    Taking that into account- let's imagine for a second how the different chapters for each conspiracy may look like, shall we?

    Aegis Kai Doru: as detailed in their said fan splatbook, the AKD's chapters are basically different mystery cults, which is appropriate to such a group. Taking their mythology into account (which details the four Labyrinths) and their Greek origin (which makes it very easy ti tie to Alexander the Alright's conquest), it is easy to create a number of chapters related to both of those concepts- the Persian Soldiers of the Sun related to the Labyrinth of the Tomb, the Egyptian Madaeu Netjer of the Labyrinth of the Crocodiles and the Order of the Black Knight which I tied to the Labyrinth of the Marsh, while the AKD as its own entity remained connected to the Labyrinth of the Isle. As variations, it was also very easy to give each of those chapters a different ancestral oath relating to one of the ther monsters of the CofD- the Soldiers became involved with alchemists and promethean (for alchemy is a form of artifice), the Madaeu got the very well desired connection to the Arisen and Irem (due to relics) as well as general necromancy connection, and the Order, naturally, had a connection to the fey (which do have their own form of relics). To those three I've added the Lycos Agema, being werewolf hunters with some Beast connection and a nod to the historical strife between Athens and Sparta, and the Anatolian Sibylline Archive, which involve itself with "living relics" in the form of the Ananke, as two forms of regional Greek groups with a shared history for the AKD. Finally, two more factions were added- a modernization of the AKD in the form of the Ozybirmgs, which plays on the AKD being ancient titans and has their whole "omphalos AI" thing going on, and a dark faction as the Tecuanimeh, which offer blood sacrifice to relics which they view as embodiment of the Aztec gods. As with 7+1 being the standard for the AKD, it would make sense to use the same for other conspiracies, right?

    Ascending Ones: now, the difficult thing about the AO is that the organization as a whole is already a not very cohesive group- C&C emphasize that by specifying that each of the factions is kinda its own thing, with one being religious militia, another is drug dealers and the third is hermetic alchemists. While it may make things complex, I still think that there are a few nice ways to get around that. The first would be the Order of Immortals, which would represent a Chinese analogue to the AO which practice their own form of alchemy, something which is very asked for (with potential connection to the Triad, considering the AO's criminal angle). The name is temporary, of course- they may be tied to the Soldiers of the Forbidden Sun from Dark Eras, but I'm not ure I am too fond of making that tie and currently prefer the Soldiers being their own thing. To this we can add the Order of the Auric Chalice- originally a Alchemist Guild from Promethean, they are mentioned to hunt down "unworthy alchemists", and would give a good representation for "European Alchemy" (which is derived from the Egyptian one, but nevermind that). Now, considering that Chapters are Compacts which are tied to Conspiracies, we could tie some of already existing compacts to the AO as its chapters- the Ahl al Jabal draw on the same concepts which the AO used as the Hashashins of old and are mentioned to have some supernatural abilities, so making this tie could be interesting. Another group which could be similarly connected is the Illuminated Brotherhood, as they do practice the use of chemistry in order to research the spirit world- something which would draw the AO's attention and allow them to grow beyond being a college group. Of course, if we want a dark faction, we could just have the Cult of Set as a lingering corruption with the AO which tries to rebuild the old frenemy, using Vitae and vampire contacts in order to produce their elixirs, which again uses the themes of drug traffic and addiction. That leaves us with 2 more- so perhaps a groups with a religious bent? Like, maybe a Christian or a Jewish chapters? Or maybe go for something completely different- like North American medicine men or Western African organization? Or seek other variations of alchemy? A more traditional Egyptian organization? There are many different options for the last two, as it seems.

    Cainte Heresy: From one hand, the Cainte is a bit problematic to work with, considering that they are so damn fractured. On the other, the fact that they are fractured does make the idea of independent chapters a much greater breathing room, as different chapters can be very, very different from one another while still being a part of the same organization, and as long as they follow the Sources they are very much free to act as they see fit. As such, we could, for example, have a group styling themselves after the Aves Minerva- perhaps they are a group of Cainte which have survived for long enough time to start asking questions and researching their history, creating a more solid structure as a result. On the other hand, they could be hunters who decided to uncover to history of the Vigil, and after stumbling on the hidden history of the Birds, they suddenly started getting messages from the Sources which deemed them worthy. Beside that, perhaps a Catholic group may also be appropriate, but I am not sure which one. Perhaps we could model them after the Cage of Shadows, being a Lilithian heresy- for while I have wrote them as a Jewish heresy, after rereading them it occurred to me that mixing blood and judaism can lend to some... unwanted connotations (even though we could perhaps preserve them as Jews, but some revision is required anyway). We could also, perhaps, have chapters based around attempting to poison vampires in some way, like my idea for the Scharlach Spinweebe with some revision, or a cult of blood anointed warriors of Mithraic origin like Glamourweaver once imagined them to be, or one related to the Sons of Phobos or a similar blood cult. Like I said, being so disparate, we have a lot of freedom in styling them- as long as we make sure that they hate vampires, we are good, I think.

    Cheiron Group: where for some groups chapters are usually occult factions and cults, for Cheiron they should be subdivisions and daughter companies which Cheiron either established or bought. All across the Web of Pain this conspiracy has left its mark, making it a powerful organization with many local chapters. While it is tempting to make LDI, Deva and Amalgama into its Chapters, Deva is canonically too large to be bought by Cheiron, and a few people are uncomfortable with Cheiron buying out LDI, so let's give up on that path. Instead, some of my older groups, like the chemical company of Yam VeNahar Industries or the biological research project of EVE Institute could both make good candidates as Cheiron's chapters. The criminal Ningishzida Trading Network, being an underworld organization dedicated for illegal organ trade, also makes an interesting an appropriate chapter for the conspiracy. While not buying the above companies, Cheiron could develop its own daughter companies dedicated for similar research, like having one dedicated for Sekhem or the God Machine, or a company which produces beauty products while being focused around vampires to do so. People like evil corporations which are dedicated to harvest certain monsters- and those ones could indeed give many interesting possibilities. We could even through in the Ama San, with Cheiron harvesting the traditions of the ancient compact to have a large, sea monster based japanese company which lures and hunts after the dwellers of the deep.

    Council of Bones: yet another very disparate organization, the Council is still very young in its current incarnation, which means that local chapters are still very different from one another and again giving us a lot of space to play with different ideals. A mexican organization seems very appropriate, due to the whole Day of the Dead and the syncretism behind it, for example. A chapter of mediums from the Age of Spiritualism which joined the conspiracy would also be interesting to see, imo. We could also take a lot of inspiration from Ascensions' Euthanatos, who are a collection of disparate death cults, giving us groups like the irish Aided or the Orphic Pomegranate Deme, or even relating them to the Thugee cult, or one with Iremite origins, or Scandinavian rune casting. Heck, the Azusa Miko could make very interesting candidates for being the conspiracy's Chapter, as we do know they are still around, and aagain it would make sense for the Council to not just create new chapters but also to absorb existing medium groups.

    Faithful of Shulpae: Ok, so here I'm kinda a bit blank- the Faithful are a large cult of monster eater, most of which are mummy focused, which again should give us some freedom in describing them, but they also feel much more cohesive. Perhaps Yam VeNahar would make sense more as part of this conspiracy, using factory methods in order to devour monsters? They do seem to fit them better. We could, in theory, make the Promethean Brotherhood a chapter of the Faithful, but I'm not sure how well those groups do work together, making me wary of such a unification. Perhaps the servants of other hungry entities could also work- like servants of Fenrir, Cronus or Tiamat. A Promethean related one feel very asked for, considering the themes of hunger and devouring, as well as one based around werewolves and geists. In short, if it is hungry and scary and worthy for worship, it is something the Faithful would seek to feed, I think. Perhaps even my relic eating conspiracy, the Maw of the River, would make a good fit for them, even though I also consider making them a lesser template.

    Hototogisu: honestly, the Hototogisu feel too small imo to start producing chapters- while they are young, they are very organized, making them very unlikely to start producing other factions across the world with very new cultures. It is possible, however, that like Cheiron they would start buying out companies of interest around the globe, which would be very different from each other- and would make good chapter candidates. The question is where to draw the line between things Cheiron would buy and ones the Hototogisu would, which has to be defined in order to separate the two organizations. Taking a more local angle, they may be the ones to have the Ama San and the Azusa Miko as its chapters, seeking to both preserve and modernize Japanese traditions, even though the former seems like a better choice than the later.

    Knights of St Adrian: yeah, I'm drawing blank here. Perhaps a middle eastern parallel like the Swords of Jibril from Dark Eras? Perhaps we could have them as contractors for other supernatural entities, like one which hunts down Deviants for the Web of Pain, or one which works for the Gentry? Or even one more focused around other types of demons, like infernals or fiends? There are a number of potential directions, but nothing too defined, imo.

    Knights of St George: the easiest one will be to have a North American remnant from the time of the Salem Witch Hunt, one which would call itself the Shadow Court and have a much more honest protestant view over the cultic practice of the Knights. While they are very focused around mages, perhaps chapters which cover other forms of magic- like alchemy, necromancy or sekhem sorcery? Those systems do lack relation to the Faceless Angels, however. Maybe a Beast focused one, considering their Goetic abilities and draconic symbolism- or even hero hunting one? Maybe one which deals more with spiritual entities, hunting demons and abyssal monstrosities and their like.

    Les Mysteres: I have actually more or less have a number of factions for the Les Mysteres, considering I worked on their Civil War scenario. The Tenders of the Dionysian Vineyard represent European witchcraft and mystery cults, the Great Spirit Society is based around north american shamanism, the Tianxia Order uses Chinese geomancy in order to shape the shadow and Shango's Thunder seeks to return to its original, West African traditions in the price of removing syncretism. To this we could add perhaps the Bear Lodge by giving them the sponsorship of a powerful spirit which saved them from ruin, and maybe a more UFOlogist chapter which communes with the strange Void Leviathans, a Christian chapter which seeks to experience the Holy Ghost or even a chapter which dedicates itself for the service of the idigam, being unable to understand with what they actually deal with.

    Lucifuge: being the ones to kickstart this whole mess, the Otodo are a great example for how different yet still true to its origin can a chapter of a conspiracy be, being very japanese yet still resonating with the Lucifuge. To this we can add one of my Strains project groups- the Tartaruchi, who like the Lucifuge are descended from Hell even though they are much more about punishing the wicked (such as slashers) over redeeming their blood. Considering the nature of Inferno and its foul spread across dimensions, we could easily have other families which have demonic blood yet are very different from one another- the Benei Topheth, for example, have a connection to a long dead spirit lord, and as such resonate with aspects of the Shadow, but we could also have a lineage more closely resonating with the Maeljin and their like. Other groups could be related to dream demons of corrupted Goetia or ghosts which got touched by Inferno's grasp, like a revision of the Tatatu o te Po. If we want to have a dark faction, we already have one in the form of the L'Enfant Diabolique, which would make a lot of sense being a rival chapter who seeks to become the conspiracy.

    Malleus Maleficarum: like the AO, it is very tempting to simply use the MM's own factions as chapters- but again, that may be much too limiting and give some very boring results, so instead let's try to look outside of the orders of the church and towards other people who may be able to use benedictions- for example, KingsRaven' Order of St Anthony would make some very interesting Orthodox chapter of the MM, who while is, again, independent, is somewhat dependent on the MM due to being smaller in size, while allowing the greater conspiracy to more easily operate in Eastern Europe. A similar protestant chapter may also be appropriate- even though finding one which is not covered by the Long Night or Anglicanism could be difficult. Similar forms of christianity could also make sense, especially Middle Eastern ones, or even african groups (I would have suggested the Ethiopian Church if I didn't already had plans for a certain conspiracy covering that territory). We could also go to an heretic territory, having a Cathar chapter which deals more with being of ephemera than vampires. Of course, the Inquisition have also left its mark on the organization, and the Los Inocentes may still live within the MM as self proclaimed Inquisitors of the Vigil- and how much the slasher influence was removed from them could be open for interpretations.

    Merrick Institution: an interesting case for Merrick is that the organization, while new, still hunts down the remnants of the older one, giving some sort of a dual perspective for new chapters- from one side, we could have groups where Merrick hunters gather under a common goal and create new foundations for the conspiracy, like those mentioned to have bases in Canada, or which try to work with Australian Aboriginal people in order to investigate the Astral. On the other, we could have the chapters of the defuncted Institution, roaming in the Web of Pain as they make their own experiments and plans for the children of Merrick, This unique duality is actually very interesting to explore, and while I don't have a solid idea yet it does seem to have some potential.

    Task Force VALKYRIE: As the secret agency of the strongest power in the world, TFV could easily have chapters in the form of agencies which operate within the organization- for example, having a chapter which deals much more with "space/interdimensional threats" working within NASA, not to mention that both the Barrette Commission and Division VI were folded into VALKYRIE in 2e, making them into good chapter candidates within the conspiracy. Outside of the conspiracy, we could have the agencies of other nations which do work closely with the american agencies, making them, while independent from TFV, still connected to it- like the British MI18 or the Israeli RAM, as well as a EU based one, or even a Japanese group in the style of Strike Force Zero from WoD. To this, we could add Jormungandr as the secret servants of the vampires within the agency, meant for a Hydra like game.

    VASCU: finally, being private investigators which help hunting down slashers, VASCU may still have a remnant within the FBI, making it its own chapter. In addition, people are talking about the Society of Twelfth Keys as a path for VASCU to interact with Europe, so we could make it its own chapter. It is very tempting to let VASCU to incorporate Nine Stars as a chapter, yet it feels very unfair for them to get absorbed when they are so new in the setting. Perhaps having a group tied to the God Machine would make sense, especially as the seeming connection between them and certain Aztec cults, which could add a darker dimension to at least some chapters of the group, as well as, potentially, a Russian chapter of psychics, which was mentioned as possible "honest yet very underbudget" conspiracy which have joined VASCU after them going private.

    And that's it, more or less- at least for now. A lot of those concepts still need work. Honestly, I don't think I'll manage to make 7+1 chapters for them all, but perhaps having between 3+1 abd 7+1 could work, with very small conspiracies (like Network Zero or the Promethean Brotherhood) being too small scale to develop their own chapters. Again that's just the raw concept- please let me know what do you think!

For that reason, in the original document, chapters have their Status: {Chapter] being respected among the parent conspiracy, even though in such cases its effective value is half of its rating. While I, for simplicity, gave them the same status benefits as the conspiracy, you could have them maintain the original benefits/have their own without it breaking anything.
