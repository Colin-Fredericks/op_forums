            Feast for the Lost
            Dice Pool: Power + Socialize or Occult – 3
            Time Per Roll: Ten Minutes
            Duration: One Scene
            Bonus: +2 to all rolls if the Initiate knows, and has prepared the feast in accordance with, the targets Legend.
            Cost: Satiety equal to Rank or Lair
            The ritualist prepares a symbolic feast designed to entice a Horror from the Primordial Dream. This is intended to draw forth those Begotten who have undergone the Retreat, the Merger or the Divergence. At Storyteller discretion this can also be used to summon forth Horrors that have “died” and returned to the Mothers Garden or other less definable denizens of the Primordial. The participants of the ritual must offer up Satiety equal to or higher than the Horror’s Rank or Lair (whichever is appropriate) as part of preparing a meal for the creature. Assuming the ritual is successful the Horror appears and remains for the duration of a scene as it feasts on the offerings. The Horror has a neutral impression with the ritualist for the purpose of social dice rolls. While such creatures can be summoned, not all of them can be conversed with in the traditional sense.
            Exceptional Success: The Horror is pleased with the offering and the ritualist gains +2 to any social dice rolls with it for the scene.

                Horrible Regalia (Intermediate Rite)
                Dice Pool: Finesse + Crafts + Empathy or Occult - 4
                Time Per Roll: 1 minute
                Duration: Permanent, until either the Regalia’s destruction or the Herald’s death.
                Bonus: +2 to all rolls if the Initiate’s entire Cult, or at least a ~75% majority of it, is in attendance
                Cost: Material (Wood, Metal, Bone, Cloth, Jewellery, Precious Metals/Stones, Etc...) from another plane (Hedge, Underworld, Mother's Land, Black Ocean etc.), Beast's blood (1L), Herald’s Blood (1L).

                This Rite allows the creation of a set of Regalia Reminiscent of an Initiate Beast's Horror. Like Horror Mask, the Regalia must be pre-made. The Herald of the Initiate whose Horror is given tribute needs to be present and has to participate in the Rite. The material must be otherworldy, otherwise the Rite automatically fails.
                Wearing the Regalia allows the Herald to which it has been bonded to act as the Initiate’s substitute for the purposes of performing a Ritual. (Once per scene.)

                Like the Horrific Idol, the Regalie has an unsettling effect on Mortals whilst being worn: When they are in the presence of the Regalia-wearing Herald for more than 3 turns and look at it, Mortals lose 1 WP and take the Spooked Condition (or a similarly appropriate Condition, e.g. Shaken, Frightened, Swooning, Confused, etc.).
                Supernaturals roll Resolve+composure.
                If they fail dramatically, they fall into Kuruth/Frenzy/Torment/etc.
                If they fail, they also take the Condition and lose a WP.
                On sucess they resist the Idol's effect.
                On an exceptional Success, they get Steadfast + 1 WP.
                If multiple Heralds, each wearing their own Regalia, are present, these rolls are resisted at cumulitative -1.

                Cults, on the other hand, take a +3 to Integrity rolls when in the presence of the Herald, and do not suffer a negative Condition nor lose a WP when looking at it. Instead, they get the Inspired Condition if they’re working on a task, the Steadfast Condition in Combat, and gain 1 WP
                The rite lasts until either the Regalia is destroyed or the Herald dies, at which point the Horror recoils. The Beast also suffers 2 points of Lethal Damage, which ignores armour, from the metaphysical backlash. If the Herald still lives, they take the Spooked and Frightened Conditions.

                Regalia may be required materials for other Obcasus Rites, or add a +2 Bonus.

                I made this Obcasus Rite using the Horrific Idol Rite as a base (thank you, Wormwood!) and taking inspiration from the fact that the male, Ancient Egyptian Priests of Anubis would wear masks fashioned in his apparent likeness.

                    One With The Land (Advanced)
                    Dice Pool: Power + Survival or Occult – 6
                    Time Per Roll: One Day
                    Duration: One Lunar Cycle
                    Bonus: Bonus dice to all rolls equal to the Rank of the Spirit being summoned for the Rite.
                    Cost: A shamanistic ritual which generates the Open Condition for a Spirit which matches the nature of the Beast's Horror.

                    The Beast attunes her Horror to the local spiritual landscape. She is sympathetically connected to a number of landmarks within the affected area equal to her [Survival or Occult]. This covers the entire conceptual bounds of the city, town or other type of municipality. Her enemies can violate and vandalize such landmarks in order to inflict Conditions and Tilts against her, and Heroes may also inflict Anathemas by destroying the landmarks. In exchange, the Beast can use any doorway, archway or reflective surface within [Horror's Power X 50] meters of such landmarks as gateways for the purposes of Skeleton Key, but only for the Shadow.

                    The Beast can also summon spirits within the affected area which match the nature of her Horror. This requires the Beast to open a Primordial Pathway for the spirit to cross over into baseline reality via the Lair, and the spirit automatically returns to the Shadow once the Primordial Pathway closes. Roll normally but gain bonus dice equal to Survival or Occult, and suffer a penalty equal to [Highest Between Gauntlet Strength And The Spirit's Rank]. At the ST's discretion, the landmarks might develop the Resonant (Fear) Condition, and thus attract attention from the Uratha, Thyrsoi, Ulgans and such. The Beast must know the spirit by name, which might require a Research [ChroD Core Page 139]. A Dramatic Failure reaches out to a type of spirit which is exactly the opposite of what the Beast intended, who can now exist freely within baseline reality until banished.

                    Exceptional Success: The Beast achieves Exceptional Success with 3 Successes on rolls to open Primordial Pathways for the purposes of summoning spirits, as well as using Skeleton Key within the landmarks. Furthermore, both of these rolls no longer cost Willpower. Lastly, the Beast can select twice as many landmarks and is not penalized by [Highest Between Gauntlet Strength And The Spirit's Rank].

                                Shackle The Hunger (Basic Obcasus Rite)
                                Dice Pool: Power + Expression or Occult - 2
                                Time Per Roll: One hour
                                Duration: Permanent
                                Bonus: Rote Quality if the Beast is Ravenous during the Rite
                                Cost: Chains, ropes or equivalent covered in 1 lethal damage worth of blood from both the Beast and the mortal, which are to be attached to a Size 5 effigy of the Horror worth Resources 2

                                Normally, Beasts aren't subject to the psycho-spiritual necessities of other monsters that keep their monstrous appetites in check. The only fetter to a Beast is his own philosophical understanding of what is right and wrong: he suffers no internal negative effects from being absolutely monstrous. With this Rite, the Beast symbolically chains down his Horror by willingly swearing loyalty to a mortal, who becomes his Touchstone. This allows the Beast to keep a stronger grip on their Hunger. "Mortal" is defined here as a character who has Integrity but is not a Hero nor Slasher.

                                Said mortal must be within the immediate vicinity of the shackled Beast, and give informed, freely offered consent to becoming a Touchstone. Trying to force or coerce the mortal prevents the Rite from working at all. During this, the Beast must recite whatever promises he thinks are appropriate, but they must involve the explicit intent to protect the mortal when it is needed and to control the Hunger. This becomes easier if the Beast is Ravenous because it works akin to religious fasting and symbolically represents a greater display of dominance over the Horror.

                                Upon completion of the Rite, the Beast gains the following benefits: suffering risk or hardship for the sake of the Touchstone soothes the Horror's Hunger, resetting the interval before it loses Satiety [BTP Core 111]. In addition, acting on the behalf of the individual represented by the Touchstone counts as fulfilling the Beast's Life trait. If the risk or hardship is significant, replenish all Willpower. If using the narrative method for Satiety loss, the functioning of the Rite is modified. Add [Expression or Occult] to [Lair] for determining how many times the Satiety Loss can happen per Story. This represents the Beast finding renewed purpose and opportunities for growth in struggling against his Hunger.

                                As a drawback, losing attachment to or alienating the Touchstone inflicts the Shaken Condition or equivalent. If your Touchstone dies, you must roll Resolve + Composure or lose all Satiety and remain Ravenous until you can find a new one and redo the Rite. This roll must be done each day after the death of the Touchstone, with a cumulative -1 penalty for each day after the first. The Beast can remove this need for a new Touchstone by sacrificing a Willpower dot.

                                Exceptional Success: The Beast and Touchstone count as True Friends in relation to each other.

                                Notes: At the ST's discretion, a Beast who becomes too negligent with his Kin and Legend for the sake of a Touchstone might receive cryptic visions and omens from the Dark Mother expressing her disapproval. If such negligence persists for too long or otherwise becomes extreme, such messages will become a lot more unpleasant. If the Beast is Ravenous and the potential Touchstone would count as a way to regain Satiety, the Beast can spend 1 Willpower upon initiating the Rite to avoid Feeding on the mortal for the Rite's duration. This does not add +3 to the roll.

                                Furthermore, an ST may rule that this Rite works on non-consenting Touchstones. However, this requires additional effort and complications. If the potential Touchstone is unwilling, and you're chained up, it follows that a third party is preventing the mortal from escaping or refusing. Which is a thematically horrific scenario and brings up a lot of potential conflict, such as the forced Touchstone giving your secrets to a Hero. If performing the Rite upon an unwilling mortal, the regular benefit of Exceptional Success cannot apply. Pick a beneficial Condition for the Beast, or any of the other typical results for an Exceptional Success.

                                Do keep in mind that this would change the symbolism behind the Rite. As an example, the Rite would require the Beast to attach the chains or equivalent objects between themselves and the mortal, while the effigy watches them both like a heretical priest during a forced marriage. The resulting pseudo-Touchstone would not be appreciably different from any other victim of your Hunger, with all that entails. The players are free to request that the Merit work exclusively on consenting mortals, should they find that coercion misses the point of Touchstones or if such scenarios are something they find to be beyond their personal boundaries.

                                Lastly: if the ST needs more concrete guidelines for representing the disapproval of the Dark Mother, he may use the following. If the Beast goes for more than a certain period actively and persistently avoiding any contact with her Kin, not acting upon her Legend and not Feeding her Hunger, the Dark Mother takes notice. Said period could be days equal to [Resolve] or [11 - Lair], for example. The Beast suffers her Lair in Resistant Lethal Damage, the Touchstone finds themselves in danger or beyond the reach of the Beast, or any other complication which seems the most dramatic and thematic.

                                    I adore the idea behind this Rite. It's a way of balancing Life and Legend in a gameline that devoted most of its word-count to the latter. A very similar Rite could brand Beasts with self-inflicted Bans that represent their dedication to a cause. Both of these could act as a sneaky way of introducing philosophies into the game, in absence of an official Z-Splat.

                                    As for your homebrew, one conceptual change I would make is to allow for non-consenting Touchstones. There is precedent in other games, where the Touchstone is neutral or even badly disposed towards to the PC. Having to protect such a Touchstone could easily heighten the drama. On top of that, an ordinary person earning an unusually helpful stalker is prime horror material.
