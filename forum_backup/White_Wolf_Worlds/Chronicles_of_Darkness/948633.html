    Flaring Disfigurements (or Not) When Using Transmutations

    08-11-2016, 11:52 AM
    A big deal is made about whether a Promethean reveals their Disfigurements when using their Transmutations, or if they don't and take a Condition instead; but try as I might, I can't find where in the book this system is explained. Has anyone else located it?

      It's in the first two paragraphs of the Transmutations section on p117.

      The Created body recognizes no difference between philosophy and working, and their internal alchemy becomes externally manifest. Doing so always reveals a Promethean titan’s feet of clay, however, either by rending the fragile physical alchemy that hides her disfigurements, or by causing a potent supernatural Condition to dog their steps.

      Each Transmutation comes with an accompanying Condition, complications of the Azothic emanations of the Alembics. Charging the Alembics of a Transmutation without revealing disfigurements is always considered a possible source of the Condition, unless otherwise stated.

          Page 171, Disfigurement paragraphs reveal that, whenever you spend pyros for anything, be it something innate or a Transmutation, or anything else, your human-seeming mask breaks down.

          That paragraph that Satchel quoted on page 117 provides an alternative to appearing as a monster in front of humans anytime you use a Transmutation in the form of the Conditions.

          That's pretty much it in a nut shell.
