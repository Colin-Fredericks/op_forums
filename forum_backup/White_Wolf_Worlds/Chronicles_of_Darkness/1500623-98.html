                                            Resistant lethal damage runs a more direct risk of killing you because bleeding out can't be solved by just getting lucky on Stamina rolls, but that's the domain of Scouring, which is directly limited in how frequently you can do it and gives you a method of doing so that doesn't deal Health damage if you're doing it to regain Mana instead of clearing out Abyssal taint from your Pattern — as deliberate actions either of those is incentivized to be done in circumstances where you are not likely to be taking more damage, because, as mentioned before, mages don't get Beats from taking wound penalty-level damage the way most other splats do.
