        Really liking the discussion that sprang up here =D Didn't expect that.

        Originally posted by nofather View Post

        Query. Idle, not a serious sticking point. These surveys pop up a lot, and I always get tangled when the questioner specifies, 'in our world.' Our world, as far as I'm aware, lacks a Shadow, Supernal forces, strix, and so on. While in some cases, like this strix or True Fae, their lack acts as a benefit to the supernatural, in others like mages and werewolves it's a huge disadvantage, as they lack access to a lot of powers. For the most part I figure I'm reading too much into it, because I'm increasingly tired and stressed and seek clarity in posts that is often assumed or simply not offered by the poster. But, basically, how much of the supernatural's baggage comes with them? Does a vampire have to deal with elders? Does a werewolf have access to the Hisil? Will a mage provoke Paradox? And so forth.
        Actually, originally I meant it exactly like you mentioned "in our world", with the addendum of things necessary for your setting to work to exist e.g. spirits/hisil are a thing, Elders not necessarily though, which would make the whole point of some covenants moot, however. Which is why I took the easy way out, assuming a complete shift to the world of CofD (as in, you actually life in it and simply don't know it yet).
        Anything else would, imo, require a hack and change too many variables (e.g. you as the only vampire there is would be something very different from being part of a kindred society. You as the only werewolf would turn it a lot more horrifying, whereas a "magical change" into a Changeling without a durance or the threat of the Fae or any of the downside to your personal life would make them a first-rate pick)

                                            Tempted with werewolf... But I'd be worried about causing real harm if I got out of control in the wrong place. Plus, I don't know if any other werewolves and being a lone wolf loses some appeal. If there are others... I've gotta hope they're not jerks. Too violent for me as well. Still.... Tempting.

                                            If I had to embrace a supernatural type, warts and all, I'd go with a Beast. There's the requirement to feast, but I have some control as to who I target and to what extent I go to, so how much damage I cause can be mitigated with consideration. Losing control of the Horror for a night means that someone has a pretty bad nightmare, potentially, but I think that's preferable to ripping out throats. And that really gets to why I think it might be the better choice for me. I can rationalize a bit of awfulness.

                                            Beasts have some appealing advantages, though. Exploring the supernatural world is second nature to them. The Lair can be so evocative. And even if you come across something horrific... It's not really something that will likely shock you. Plus, Beasts have quite a bit of personal power. Eye of Heaven, Mimir's Wisdom and Needs Must are Atavisms that are great for advancing yourself.

                                            If I had to be a monster, I think I would prefer being a monster that's okay with being a monster. Maybe I could be an ethical monster? Be able to direct my predation onto targets that I'm sure are "deserving"? Maybe... Maybe not. There would probably come a time where I took things too far, and I would probably lie to myself that it was bound to happen, that monsters do monstrous things and that I can control myself so that I'm a better monster.

                                            In the end, though, I'd still be a monster. Maybe I could live with that.
