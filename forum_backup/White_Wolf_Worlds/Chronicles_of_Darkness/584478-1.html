                Over the Top not "Out of Theme"...

                I get that Promethean's central theme is alienation but alienation is a theme in all of the games. It doesn't really need to be taken to the nth degree just to make Promethean unique. IMO, a lot of mileage could be gotten out of a mechanic like -- innocents are immune to disquiet -- because it would act like the pinch of salt that brings out the alienation flavor to the fullest.

                Moreover it's a kludgey mechanic to deal with in cross-over settings. "Because you exist" is a deus ex machina raison d'être for everyone hating on Prometheans. Most supernaturals have enough reason not to keep Prometheans around simply from their effect on mortals and the Wasteland effect. Having Disquiet also affect supernaturals detracts more from the game than it adds (takes the deck from being just stacked against the Promethean to being untenable). Recall that most supernaturals have shinsengumi levels of 'violence first, diplomacy later' for problem solving methods.

                As for Beasts, the way it's written now, there isn't any logical reason for any supernatural to like Beasts so their feelings of other supernaturals doesn't really seem that pertinent to me.

                                                            Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post

                                                            I dunno, I mean, Prometheans are one of the few supernaturals with a clear, theoretically achievable "happy ending" possible for them. I mean, yeah, Changelings escaped their captors...to a life of fear and paranoia; Demons can achieve Hell. Maybe. And it won't necessarily be what they intended. Prometheans are about the only splat where they can truly hang up their sad hats and say "I did it. Time to retire."

                                                            Nobody else gets to do that.
                                                            That's all very good in theory but the realities of gameplay work out differently. It's a soulcrushing slog to actually achieve the so-called "happy ending".

                                                            The problem that the left and right hooks of Disquiet and Wasteland lead to very rinse and repeat type of storytelling where the character(s) is(are) safe in one place for a little while before inevitably having to move again. Hypothetically this is fine if one wants a BSG-like game; however, even a show like BSG is essentially about the connections that the characters make to one another and the people they're traveling with. Promethean 1st ed does(did) not have this kind of added dimensionality. There's no pressure release valve.

                                                            Very few players want to play a game that in essence is an analogy for what it's like to suffer from depression, except of course the game has zero up moments so it's even worse than actual depression. Even when you have such a rarefied troupe (and more likely it's just one player in your troupe) that is willing to take on the challenge the game has zero upbeats where the end goal actually seems achievable. It's basically a race to see if you character(s) can get off of the merry-go-round of doom before they face-plant into the bottom of the abyss like a lawn dart (F-16) with engine failure.

                                                            So yeah, I'm looking forward to a system that makes the end the goal more achievable but also throws in more distractors that can lure / divert characters off of their pilgrimages.
