The transliteration into the Latin alphabet was deliberately 'off' - because in the writer's opinion a Shadow name is intended to deceive or, at the very least, to conceal. Going from letters on a page to speaking or hearing is a process that depends a great deal on where the speaker, hearer or reader comes from, which accent is the most familiar to them.
