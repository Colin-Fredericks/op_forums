                                I'm not sure how to make that mechanically distinct to how Beloved usually are. The more I think about it, the more I think it's best to leave this as a Merit for PC cultists and not something Leviathans can buy for their cult.

                                Anyway, I was a little board so in the spirit of Crossover, here Hunter Conspiracies and Wake vulnerability.
                                • Ascending Ones: Character's treat Elixir dots as the Virtue Merit while protected by an appropriate Elixir. (There isn't one in the books, but obviously the Ascending Ones would have a potion to protect their minds. Elixir of the Fiery Heart should provide some lesser protection, lets say it reduces the Wake penalty for Breaking Points) (In my WoD Alchemy/Divine Fire is Empyrean)
                                • Bastet's Beloved: Character's treat dots in Mantle of Bastet as the Virtue Merit. (In my WoD Bastet is Empyrean - in fact, if I say something counts as the Virtue Merit just assume it's related to the Empyrean in my WoD)
                                • Athenic Church: Character's treat dots in Devotion as the Virtue Merit.
                                • Hakollel shel Hamerkava: Anyone protected by El Elyon gains a honorary Virtue Merit equal to the speaker's Shemot dots. Elah Yerushelem can block the Wake.
                                • Hearth of Hestia: Hearthtending counts as a Virtue merit for all under it's protection.
                                • Malleus Maleficarum / Order of St Anthony: Character's treat Benediction dots as the Virtue Merit while protected by Epipodian Safeguard. The Blessed Protection of St. Agrippina can blocks the Wake.
                                • Office of the Lord Stewards: A successful use of Pact of Protection confers Wake immunity for the duration. Warding can block the Wake.
                                • Staff of Janus: I never wrote them an equivalent of the Blessed Protection of St. Agrippina. Pretend I did.



                                Basically: the general rule is that if the Endowment is Empyrean and applies direct mental protection then it probably gets treated as the Virtue Merit. If it's Empyrean and the Endowment Merit can be reasonably described as a virtue, it counts as the Virtue Merit. If it's non-Empyrean and applies direct mental protection then it gets treated as Supernatural Tolerance. Area protection powers like the Blessed Protection of St. Agrippina work as normal, and block the Wake if the defences are strong enough.

                                                    Today's update is Phronesis and Eunoia, the twin Integrity traits that PenDragon proposed way back in the day.

                                                    I had trouble posting the sample triggers, so you'll have to head over to the Systems and Advantages doc for those. Suggestions for triggers would be welcome.

                                                    Phronesis

                                                    Phronesis is the ability to judge right from wrong, to know how to act and speak in a manner that’s moral and socially appropriate. Phronesis is what makes the difference between a civilised thinking being, and an animal that can only act upon it’s instincts.

                                                    Thoughtless or savage actions risk Eroding Phronesis. With high Phronesis a Leviathan is in control of their actions and emotions, but as Phronesis Erodes they become passionate, instinctive, or “hot-headed”. With Erosion they become little ore than a savage animal, a slave to their instincts barely capable of forming a chain of thought long enough to realise what they have become.

                                                    Phronesis chains the Leviathan’s divine nature. As it falls the Leviathan becomes increasingly reliant upon their Undertow and inflicting havoc. If Phronesis is ever lost entirely, the Beastal Nature overwhelms the other two creating an Typhon.

                                                    Eunoia

                                                    Eunoia is the ability to have another’s interests at heart. In it’s original context Eunoia referred to both the perception and fact of a speaker having the audience’s interests at heart the relevance to a Leviathan speaking to their cult is self evident.

                                                    Acts that dominate or use others for one’s own purposes risks Eroding Eunoia. A Leviathan with high Eunoia is able to perceive the desires of others and may choose to put another before themselves. As Eunoia Erodes a Leviathan develops sociopathic tendencies, with further Erosion they all but forget that people are thinking beings with their own interests, and are quite incapable of caring.

                                                    Eunoia chains the Leviathan’s divine nature. As it falls the Leviathan becomes increasingly reliant upon their Upwelling and displays of dominance. If Eunoia is ever lost entirely, the Divine Nature overwhelms the other two creating an Opheon.

                                                    Tranquility

                                                    Unlike Phronesis and Eunoia which describe specific aspects of the Tribe’s psychology, Tranquility does not actually exist within the world of Leviathan: the Tempest. When a Leviathan speaks of Tranquility, which they frequently do, the word is being used as a rough synonym for “how well I’m coping”. When the game’s rules refer to Tranquility, they simply mean the lower of Phronesis or Eunoia.

                                                    Sidebar: Keeping it Simple.

                                                    Groups who don't want to track two separate traits can merge Phronesis and Eunoia into Tranquillity. Doing so will not create any serious problems with the mechanics, though you are encouraged to "eyeball it" and choose only one Phronesis or Eunoia drawbacks depending on which is more fitting.

                                                    Effects of Tranquility
                                                    • When a Leviathan erodes to Phronesis four or less they lose the ability to regain Ichor without indulging their beastal nature. Track any Ichor gained separately from the regular pool. When the Leviathan regains Willpower from their Undertow or regains Ichor from a Havoc check, afterwards they may regain Ichor like normal for the remainder of the scene.
                                                    • At Eunoia four a similar effect happens, and can be resolved by regaining Willpower from an Upwelling or Ichor from a Domination check. If both traits are at four or lower, each resolution provides access to half the Ichor gained in a scene.
                                                    • A Leviathan takes a -1 penalty to Composure rolls at Phronesis 4, which increases by one for each additional dot of Phronesis lost. The Beastal nature is svage and instinctual.
                                                    • A Leviathan takes a -1 penalty to Empathy, Persuasion and Socialise rolls non-divine individuals at Eunoia 4, which increases by one for each additional dot of Eunoia lost. The Leviathan finds begins to forget that mortal beings are more than just an extension of the gods they serve.

                                                        New Channel: The Hydra's Rebirth. I tried to add some alternative abilities in here but apart from the ability to heal others (with risk!) I couldn't think of any. All in all, this one's pretty close to 1E, but with more Adaptations.

                                                        The Hydra's Rebirth (Descendant Channel)

                                                        A spark of unnatural life coruscates through the blood of the Tribe, its immense potency granting their very organs the ability to reproduce and evolve.
                                                        With this Channel a Leviathan can heal from injury in an instant. Cells divide at an astounding rate, the Leviathan’s body pulls in extra dimensional mass, consumes foreign organisms or transcends time itself to use healed or not yet injured limbs.
                                                        Like the Hydra of myth, a Leviathan’s wounds may be cauterised to prevent healing. If an electrically charged weapon is impaled within the Leviathan’s form the wound it inflicted cannot be healed until the weapon is removed or it runs out of power.
                                                          • [*=-1]Dice Pool: None
                                                            [*=-1]Action Type: Reflexive
                                                        • "Depth 1": Birthright of Fecundity
                                                        • "Depth 2": The Leviathan may expend 1 point of Ichor to heal Depth points of Bashing damage. If this removes the injuries that cause a Tilt, the Tilt is also healed.
                                                        • "Depth 3": The Leviathan gains the ability to produce an Ichor saturated food or drink from it’s body with miraculous healing powers (it’s also a miracle cure for infertility, even menopause can be reversed). Consuming this, which must be done straight from the Leviathan’s body, heals damage as though the Leviathan spent the same amount of Ichor on this Channel at their current Depth. Mortals and Lahmasu can benefit, Ahabs and Atolls will vomit it up without being affected. Other supernatural creatures are at the Storyteller’s discretion.

                                                        Drawback: The Divine Ichor is not safe for mortals, and even Lahmasu find the potency of a full Leviathan’s ichor dangerous. At the end of a scene roll the total Ichor ingested (including wasted points) - Depth. If the roll results in more successes than the character's Genetic Conditions they take a new permanent (and inheritable, sometimes resulting in outright Lahmasu children) Genetic Condition. Hybrids are treated as having two less Genetic Conditions due to their unstable DNA, while the Healthy Genetics Mutation is counted as an extra Genetic Condition.
                                                        • "Depth 4": You may expend 2 Ichor to heal half Depths (round up) points of Lethal damage.

                                                        Alternatively the Leviathan may spend 2 Ichor to purge their body of diseases, toxins or parasites (those rare few with enough supernatural power to harm a Leviathan). They spend 1 to 5 Ichor depending upon the severity, and if necessary make a Clash of Wills roll. If the Leviathan is touching someone they may roll Stamina + Intimidation vs Stamina + Supernatural Tolerance to transfer the intrusion into them. Otherwise it is expelled as an ephemeral Spirit and is free to do what it wishes (usually find someone, possibly the Leviathan again, and turn itself back into materium).
                                                        • "Depth 5": When attacked by a penetrative weapon, such as a spear or harpoon, the Leviathan may heal so quickly that the weapon is trapped within their body for digestion. This requires healing all the damage the weapon inflicted before it is removed from the Leviathan’s body (usually one turn). Supernatural weapons usually require a Clash of Wills.
                                                        • "Depth 6": Water is the element of life, and so long as the Leviathan is at least half submerged they can draw life from water, increasing their Safe Ichor per Turn by two points while using this Channel.
                                                        • "Depth 7": You may expend 4 Ichor to heal 1 Aggravated level of damage. Every time you do so, increase any non-Stamina Attribute by one for the remainder of the scene. No more than (Stamina + Sheol) Dots of extra Attributes may be active at once.

                                                        Adaptations
                                                        • Aggressive Assimilation (OOO): In Depth 4 or below the Leviathan can convert Lethal or Aggravated damage into Ichor at a 2:1 ratio. Any Ichor gained in this fashion vanishes at the end of the turn and can only be spent on The Hydra’s Rebirth, excluding Adaptations.
                                                        • Blessings of the Chirurgeon God (OOO - O): When using the Depth 3 iteration of this channel The Leviathan may produce sustenance that can be preserved for Sheol days and always acts as though the Leviathan was in Depth 7. They must pay one point of Willpower per dose created, but a dose can contain as much Ichor as they wish and cannot be divided. Once the Willpower is spent it takes about an hour before the dose is ready.
                                                        • With Golden Hair (O): Any person healed by the Depth 3 iteration of this Channel, including by Blessings of the Chirurgeon God, is treated as having Intimate Knowledge of the Leviathan for as long as the Leviathan remains in their system (about 33 hours on average).
                                                        • Dionysian Relief (O): In Depth 1 or lower the Leviathan can spend a point of Ichor to cure a hangover. This Adaptation is rare, given the enormous quantities of alcohol needed to get a Leviathan drunk most don’t bother or only drink for the taste. However Leviathans that take the edge of their condition with alcohol drink like the gods of old, and consider a point of Ichor to be a bargain for escaping the consequences.
                                                        • Resurgent Corpus (OO): You may expend a point of Willpower to treat your Dexterity as equal to your Stamina for the purposes of Defense. This lasts a scene.
                                                        • Dread Return (OOO, requires Sheol 3): That is not dead which can eternal lie. This Adaptation costs a dot of permanent Willpower to express and may be activated at any point after your health track is filled with Lethal damage, including after death. Doing so causes the Leviathan to fall into a trance-like stasis that appears, to any observer, just like death. During this state the Leviathan is dreaming, will not bleed out and heals damage at the standard human rate, but must spend 1, 2 or 4 Ichor per point of Bashing, Lethal or Aggravated damage. Until you recover you may only regain Ichor through the worship of your cult. If your body is fully annihilated (by incineration or some other means), you are truly dead.
                                                        • Phylactery (OOO, OOOOO, requires Dread Return, Sheol equal to dots): With Dread Return the Leviathan reforms from the largest part of their body, but with this Adaptation they develop a specific organ, unique for each Leviathan that serves as the focus of their reincarnation. The phylactorial organ is Size 1, and grows to Size Sheol in Apotheosis. If the organ is destroyed without killing the Leviathan then this Adaptation is nullified until the organ is healed. The practical effect of this Adaptation is that the Leviathan can rip out their own Phylactery and throw it to safety. This fills their health track with Aggravated damage, but your still beating heart sinking to the bottom of the ocean is better than leaving your body to regenerate right next to the Marduk agent who killed you and knows to disintegrate the body.

                                                        The five dot version increases the time a Leviathan can survive without their phylactorial organ from just long enough to hurl it into the distance to Sheol hours.
                                                        Drawback: The Leviathan must split Ichor between their body and their phylactery. Their body cannot regain Ichor in any way after the separation.
                                                        • Reap the Whirlwind (OOOOO, requires Sheol 5): Once per week, when you would be slain in combat as a result of filling your rightmost Health box with Aggravated Damage, instead you are restored to maximum Health and Ichor and reflexively shift to Apotheosis Form. The Transformation takes it’s usual time, but during this time you instantly heal all damage. This change is not optional but does not cost Ichor. Distribute (Stamina + Sheol) dots among your Attributes as if you had healed all the aggravated damage with Ichor. Among younger members of the Tribe, this Adaptation is sometimes affectionately referred to as "One-Winged Angel."



                                                        And here's Mortal Devouring Armoury. I'm trying something new for this one, to emphasise the diversity of the Tribe's biology, I think it works - obviously there's lots of room for expansion, once I reread Hurt Locker's weaponry rules. Feel free to suggest stuff.

                                                        The Leviathan's Primordial form bristles with cruel natural weapons.

                                                        In Depth 1 the Leviathan gets 1L Damage natural weapons. In Depth 2-6 they gain one armoury dot to apply various upgrades to their natural weaponry and in Apotheosis they gain 4 armoury dots. A Leviathan may gain no advantage at a particular Depth in order to buy a more expensive upgrade later, The upgrades are chosen when purchasing this Channel, and decisions are permanent. However the Leviathan may redesign their armoury every time they raise Sheol.
                                                        Armoury dot may provide:
                                                        • +1 Damage (O, stacks to a maximum of 7)
                                                        • The weapon inflicts Aggravated damage (OOO)
                                                        • The weapon’s damage is treated as +2 against grappled targets (O)
                                                        • Armour piercing 2 (O, stacks to a maximum of 14)
                                                        • +2 to Brawl rolls (O, stacks to a maximum of 14)
                                                        • Treat damage as 2 higher for the purposes of inflicting Tilts (O, stacks to a maximum of 14 )
                                                        • Reduce penalties for called shots by 3 (O)

                                                        Adaptations
                                                        Weapons of the Hybrid (OOO): The Leviathan gains an additional armory dot at Depth 3.
                                                        Weapons of the Monster (OO): The Leviathan gains an additional armory dot at Depth 5.
                                                        Weapons of the God (O): The Leviathan gains an additional armory dot at Depth 7.
