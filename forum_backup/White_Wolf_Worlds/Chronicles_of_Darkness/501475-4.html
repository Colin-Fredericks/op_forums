                Check out Halfworlds, which is an original series by HBO Asia. The premise involves gang wars and criminal underworld dealings by a secret society descended from the demons and supernatural creatures of Southeast Asian folklore. Season 1 was set in Jakarta, and Season 2 is in Bangkok.

                The tone is visibly dark and slightly cyberpunk, set against the backdrop of the megapolises of the region. This could easily be Demon, Changeling, or Vampire. One of the pluses for me is that its mythology is Eastern, which is a rare thing when the usually tropes are drawn from Western folklore.

                                Joe Ledger Series: This a great inspiration book for demon, hunter, and even vampire.
                                Book 1:Patient Zero (2009) - This Book Covers Joe Ledger Fighting off terrorist group that found away to make zombies.
                                Book 2:The Dragon Factory (2010) - Joe Ledger and the (Department of Military Sciences) go up against genetic super soldiers
                                Book 3:The King of Plagues (2011) - Joe Ledger and the DMS Fights a Global Conspiracy group Known as the 7 kings akin to the Illuminati
                                Book 4: Assassins Code (2012) - He Fights Vampires in the middle east that stole nukes
                                Book 5:Extinction Machine (2013) - He Fights the men in black and an alien conspiracy (AKA God-Machine)
                                Book 6:Code Zero (2014) - Joe Ledger Fights a Genius Hacker using the zombie from the first book to try and destroy the world.
                                Book 7:Predator One (2015) - This time a terrorist group use a Quantum computer to take control of the U.S military infrastructure
                                Book 8:Kill Switch (2016) - This time Joe Fights The God-Machine. Yes Their is a God-Machine in this book
                                Book 9: Dogs of War (2017) - I have not read this one yet but it says. A freelance terrorist uses the latest generation of robot dogs to deliver WMDs into cities across America. Ultra-realistic robots in the sex industry are used to spread designer plagues

                                This book series is a great wealth of ideas for any GM
