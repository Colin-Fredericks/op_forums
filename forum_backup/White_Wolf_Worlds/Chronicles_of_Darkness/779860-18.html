            So Malformations are open to all Sirens, like the Centimani Path for Prometheans. For Sirens unaligned with Echidna, perhaps each Malformation reduces her dice pool to resist Maelstrom. Also, I just realized that I referred to the Frenzy-State as Turbulence instead of Maelstrom in my earlier posts. Whoops.
            I'm not very well read on the Centimani Path, so anything similar to them are unintentional. I would say that that would perhaps be a fine idea for Sirens. How about we list it like this?

            Echidnal Echoes Condition (Persistent)
            Malformations have three DNA inside of them. Human, Fish, and Echidna. When the Siren takes a number of Malformations greater than her Akashic Record, the part that is Echidna begins to infect her like a virus, making her begin to sympathize with Echidna's goals, and find it harder to ignore them. Take the Siren's Malformations - Akashic Record if the number of Malformations she owns is greater than her Akashic Record. This is a bonus to all rolls that aid Echidna's plots...and a penalty to things which would go against them or herself.
            Bonus/Penalty: ______
            Beat: When the Siren follows through with Echidna's plans. It must be enough to activate the bonus.
            Causing the Condition: The Siren has more Malformations than her Akashic Record.
            Resolving the Condition: The Siren's Akashic Record grows to be higher than the number of Malformations she owns.
            Resolving the Condition: The Siren removes her Malformations entirely.

            Scylla's Condition (Persistent)
            Despite the loyalty that Echidna's Sirens show to her, there are many who take her Malformations without her knowledge or consent. With this, she cursed her illegal cells, for those who do not align with her must find to do so...or find that they shall fall to their nastier side.
            Beat: Take a beat everytime the Siren falls to Maelstrom with this Condition.
            Causing the Condition: If the Siren owns Malformations but isn't aligned with Echidna (I.E. Is in a Current without true loyalty to Echidna).
            Resolving the Condition: The Siren removes her Malformations entirely.

            The reason I call the latter "Scylla's Condition" is because the monster was a nymph Hera was jealous of, and thus she cursed her to become a monster. As well, I also imagine a way to remove Malformations (on Sirens at least). Simply put, Echidna's Malformations become a part of your DNA when she puts them in, and to take them out would be like ripping out a part of your genetic code to reassert itself. I think that taking off a Malformation would be an extended roll, the Threshold being the total number of Malformations you have and the dicepool is Akashic Record + Lucidity. You can choose any number of Malformations you have, even all of them if you wish. The reason the Threshold is all of the malformations is because its Echidna's DNA resisting being pulled out. When you reach up to the number of successes required, the Siren is able to take off the Malformation....however, she then automatically takes Resistant Aggravated damage equal to the number of Malformations she had taken off.

            Just in case if it's not known, Resistant Damage cannot be healed magically (not even mages can do it) and it must be allowed to heal at a natural rate. And yes, it's possible for a Siren to kill herself by getting off too many Malformations at once. I would also like to note that taking off Malformations off of a Vodianoi are impossible save for turning them completely back to normal. I would like to think that how Echidna gives Malformations to her Sirens and her Vodianoi are separate ways. With the Sirens, she does it like plastic surgery with the finest of tools. With Vodianoi, she's like a back alley surgeon whose only implement is a rusty chainsaw.

                            To be fair, I feel that returning a Vodianoi or Breathless to human would not be such a simple task. Echidna and her Sirens make it a point to not let them return where they were taken from (although slip ups are entirely plausible to happen), and even if they enter the Deluge to search for the Vodianoi of the person kidnapped, it wouldn't be easy because the person IS going to be deformed from what they originally looked like, and two, you still need to dig through their lizard brain and more animalisitc behavior (and Echidna's influence) to find the human underneath.

                            As for Breathless and Sirens first time entering the Deluge is basically the same, meaning that a Breathless beginning to drown must be significant enough for what would cause a Siren to drown. Perhaps a person Drowns because they missed some bill payment for the fifth time and their house is gonna get taken away. A Siren surfaces by figuring out or clinging onto the hope of resolving it (plausible with Verses). The Breathless will claw their way to survive it no matter the cost to themselves, (their aura of making people like them may let them off the hook for now, but they're still in danger of losing their house if they don't meet it the next time). The Breathless could be relieved of his status if he managed to fulfill the thing that caused him to drown, which in this case would be being able to pay off his mortgage and truly own his house. He would remain Breathless even if he lost his house, because he hadn't fulfilled the reason of "keeping it". I imagine now that moral sirens would seek to make Breathless back to normal humans, and less moral ones keep them as they are to feed off of Pneuma.

                            In my mind, Vodianoi are actually de-evolved humans. They're pretty much wracked with trauma that Echidna causes in making them what they are and it's so bad that they've lost their sapience. It doesn't help that part of their DNA is now fish/marine animal and as such they gain the same responses of an animal. I feel that, like in the story of the Little Mermaid, the way a Vodianoi can be redeemed is through the love of a human they knew in their old life. They need to have it hung in their face like a hook with a wriggling worm, and like a fish they need to be enticed to follow it before they latch onto the hook.

                            Think of it like this, Vodianoi cannot interact with humans in a normal way since they're at the same level as animals. Even though they've lost their sapience, they still do carry their sentience and thus can recognize people they've seen or known before and have feelings such as love. As such, redeeming a Vodianoi I imagine would be almost akin to stalking them (preferably from the Deluge and using powers to let the Vodianoi (and the Siren redeeming him/her) see from the deluge and into reality. Which in turn brings back the vodianoi's human feelings. The feelings the Vodianoi must feel are not the syringe injected drug like false feelings that Echidna gives to them for herself, they must be true feelings of love. Any kind of love will do, but it must be specified, directed and judged. It must be the kind of love that can touch the soul of someone. The process is basically like re-emphasizing the human genes, the higher thought that came upon the lizard brain which made humans more than just instinctual animals, with their humanity (even if it's twisted if they're not the best of people before becoming Vodianoi) coming back out. If their humanity manages to overcome the fish features, all of their Malformations will rip off as their DNA rejects the animal like parts and their sapience returns. If they're in the Deluge, they're thrown up back into reality. Wet, maybe abit cold, but undoubtedly alive. And I believe that they'd also flatly forget their time as a Vodianoi, as the animal thought and fish are pushed out in favor of human thought.

                            For example, a Vodianoi going along the drowned city of Chicago sees a lady through the veil of the Deluge. He doesn't know why, but he remembers feelings of warmth from her touch, the same feelings he gets when he sees the shiny band stuck on his webbed hand....and can't help but feel a twinge of rage as he sees another man talking to her and taking her into an alley against her will. He might pop out of the Deluge and brutally slaughter the man, only to run away like a scared animal when the woman screams. But he'll keep watching her, feeling his once doused emotions come back lighting like Saint Elmo's Fire....and Echidna's cells will try to reject by drowning the fire underneath orders to further her plots. If the fire is great enough, he may even look on his hand and see that it's not just anything. It's his wedding ring, the same she has on. The next thing he remembers is the feeling of ripping. Fish parts ripping away from his body. Fish memories ripping away from his brain. Himself ripping out of the deluge. The woman wakes up, hearing the sound of a thud and seeing her unconscious husband on the bedroom floor. Cold, unconscious, sopping wet and been gone for months....but breathing, alive, and back.

                            Of course, the above story is only possible because the Vodianoi wasn't being watched. In almost all cases, Vodianoi will have leashes around them. However, it's possible to change the leash to your hands and teach him things he once knew if you try hard enough. Mechanic wise? It would be an extended roll with one roll per session and if you make it at the end, you're back to normal. Story wise? There's gonna be alot of sessions where you must play out and actively help the beast return to human and not scare the one of their affections away (it would discourage them to continue connecting afterall). This is how I imagine Vodianoi redemption to be like.

                            And as for the Happy Ending bit, that is something I like, but I keep it only for when I serve as a Storyteller. When I'm working on a game, I try to remain professional and suggest only what feels as fitting. I mostly just suggested the "get out of splat" methods since most Subsplats do have ways to return back to normal humans. That and that love is a part of the game, so I felt it would be fitting for the Vodianoi and the stories of how love can redeem a demon back to being a human.
