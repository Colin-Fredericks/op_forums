              I think it's worth buying but I may be partially biased...

              But if you have any questions about it, feel free to ask.

              Originally posted by Crazy_Ivan View Post
              I think it was worth it. Few more powers would've been nice, like new versions of Enchantment and Internal Alchemy, but I can homebrew them easily enough. Cleaner, updated systems for powers, templates and dark cults are nice. ^^
              The idea was that I didn't want to replicate things too much. Internal Alchemy is partially covered by Biokinesis at the low level, and then the higher level I made its own merit , Ephemeral Transmutation (more expensive since it's no longer template-exclusive, admittedly). Admittedly it'd likely be good to have a mental equivalent of Biokinesis to round that out. Something to add to my list for if I do a major update of the book down the line.

              Similarly, Enchantment is covered by Psychic Empathy (which I even mentioned is sometimes called "Enchantment") which a THaumaturge can then use their merit to ritualize.
