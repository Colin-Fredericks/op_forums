        A thing worth mentioning as concerns spells giving more bang for your buck than Sanctum dots alone: setting aside the question of how much time you spend casting inside your Sanctum versus out of it, what a Sanctum is means that, at the ground level base assumption, your Sanctum is most under threat when you are inside it — the nature of a Safe Place and the requirements of Sympathetic Range mean that, in the absence of hostile probing in violation of a Gold Law, precious little is getting into your Sanctum that you didn't let in yourself.

        Heavily warded Sancta are often both shared and near-constantly occupied, and in the event that you have to keep spells tied up in veiling or warding a subject in your personal Sanctum long-term you have the aegis of a Shadow Name and the fallback of Rotes to protect you against being caught unawares by connected parties away from the secured location you've set up shop in — most of the time, though, you'll be keeping obscure things with few traceable sympathies in your laboratory where you have at least one free slot to spend on locking down the perimeter once you're inside. Needing to rely on powerful shields and baffles is for stuff above the pay-grade of "place where I hang my wizard hat" — for that the Merit you want is Imbued Item, not Sanctum.

            This is solely my opinion, but I think I have a point haha

            The straight-up, no nonsense approach I take is that the Sanctum merit serves 2 purposes.
            1) [Primary] It's a requirement to obtain a Demense. I view it as Library being a requirement for Advanced Library, which is much better overall than plain Library. Demense is where it's at.
            2) It's a base of operators for Sympathetic magic + a Mage fortress

            Inside your Sanctum, you can buff yourself *a lot* without taking penalties - especially important when you don't have high stamina/gnosis. This is important setup for casting Sympathetic magic. You're also effectively stronger inside your Sanctum - no penalties for being a walking spell-machine.
            A Demense is pure gold.

            Ultimately, it is a Safe Place + can be used as a fortress in the sense that you are, in a real sense, stronger inside it than outside it -- and so is your cabal. Remember, the cost can be split.
            Also remember that you can have supernatural guards for a Sanctum, over-and-above what you can have guarding a regular Safe Place.

            And hey, if you're into flavor, it is bursting with it.
