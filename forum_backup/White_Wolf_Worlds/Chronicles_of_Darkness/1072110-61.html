        Nightmares are dreams too...

        That said, yeah. I hate to criticize such ghoolishly cool sketches, but I thought the Wardens were more the honey trap that suckered you in by dulling your senses and presenting you with the perfect world. I don't see such masters of deception appearing like this to the Magisters.
        Wardens are shapeshifters and illusionists, they can appear as whatever they want, so really there's nothing wrong with their true form being ugly. If anything it adds to the creepyness when you consider they can spend an entire illusion showing up as your loved one, until you finally break through the illusion and realize THIS is what you've been interacting with. Moreover, according to the book they were created by the Darkness, so I wouldn't expect them to look nice in their true form.

        As for the rules regarding Dreamlanders, the book indeed isn't very clear; from my understanding, they use the 2e rules, only they don't have Corpus (they use Willpower instead) and ignore Manifestations while in the Dreamlands.
