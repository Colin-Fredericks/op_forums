    Contagion Chronicle Wants?

    12-05-2017, 01:00 PM
    Basically, what’s in the title. What do you want to see in the Contagion Chronicle (Crossover Chronicle) that you aren’t 100% sure will be there?

    Personally, my three things are
    1. Support for crossover with subsplats, including ones from supplements.
    2. Rules for regular non-hunter mortals in crossover games, and how to handle them.
    3. Beyond protagonists and protagonists working together, and antagonists and antagonists workout together, I really want to see reasons antagonists and protaginists of different splats would work together, and what it would mean. As well as protagonist v protagonist fighting and antagonist v antagonist fighting, but that’s pretty unsaid.

      My primary wish is that the Contagion itself not be something blatant enough to force different supernatural societies to come together, but something subtle enough that only societies that have already come together tend to detect it.

      Co-Secondary wish is a selection of true mixed societies and factions.

                                  Note: In Changeling 2e, you can have courts which get their power from a pact with other splats, so that'd be a good way to show some crossover. You can even have a crossover court system, like courts of blood, moons, sigils, and codes.

                                  Blood's patron is ancient primordial vampire who is locked in torpor until the agreed upon date, with their bargain requiring the changelings to use oneiromancy to eternally protect the kindred from the horrific dreamscapes which normally plague a vampire, and in return the court gains protection against their own nightmares allowing them to ward areas from the gentry by spilling their own blood.

                                  Moon's patron is a mighty pack of hunter in darkness werewolves who claimed the local hedge as part of their territory, where as long as the changelings maintain the trods for their hunts the local spirits will keep their eyes open for huntsmen and gentry ironside. But if the pack was all killed, the court's power would fade from the world.

                                  Sigil's patron is a free council acanthus who tries to study all possible symbolism that can be found from ancient fairytales and folktales. By having the changelings recite their stories from the lands which warp time, the changelings find that they begin to see omens which hint at a gentry's approach. The patron takes special interest in changelings which were taken long ago and yet escaped in the modern day.

                                  Code's patron is a mysterious alien entity who has worn thousands of faces, speaks in riddles and code, and offers deals to courtiers who cross it's path. Because of it's history of making deals and it's odd mindset, some rumours claim the patron is one of the true fey. Members of the code court always seem to have an alibi and often seem oddly mundane for entities of wyrd. Every 4 months the Code court is sent a message from their patron, and it is the responsibility of the courtiers to have this message sent out until everyone in the Code Court knows it, but if the message is spoken by the King and Queens of any of the other courts suddenly the cover stories of the code court fade from people's memories.

                                              Honestly, Up until I realized that the Contagion Was the focus of the so called 'Crossover' Chronicle, I wasn't much interested, Since I quite enjoy threading similar themes and working out the small mechanical bugs that come with Crossover, so such a supplement would probably only be a 'cannibalize ideas' sort of thing for me. but with the different direction (A threat or something that is dealt with by several splats working together) seems to have a lot more original content for someone like me.

                                              As for things Im not sure the game will have but I want?
                                              1.Crossover factions. A sort of psudosplat for the contagion chronicle specifically.. Thematically, perhaps Defined by how to deal with the Contagion?
                                              2.The above to include some traditional antagonists, as well. I mean, Having angels or Huntsmen might be a tough sell, but the otherwise 'evil protagonist' Style enemies could probably fill in for their more playable counterparts, as far as fighting the contagion goes.
                                              3. a place for hunters/mortals in this style of Chronicle. Perhaps a bit of a long shot, since the whole pitch seemed to be about the supernaturals, but what can I say, despite my username, Im a fan of Mortals.

                                              Im sure there is more I havent thought of, but those are the ones that come to my mind immediately.
