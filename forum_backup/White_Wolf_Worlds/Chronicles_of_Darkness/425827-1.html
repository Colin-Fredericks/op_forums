                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            Reading this amazing stuff and I rubbing my hand to use Djinn in my London Mage game - I have Free Council Thyrsus Startegos that come from Iran and is master binder of Djinn in his home.

                            This leads me to few questions on your material...
                            Sure, let's answer them

                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            1. Is it possible to "rise" the normal spirits in to Djinn status? Maybe with help of Spirit and Mind Arcana - first for ephemeral body and second for independent thought and personality? London is not very strong on having original Arabic ephemeral natives in it so I need to know how to populate English setting with them, a bit.
                            Not as I intended them. Spirits and true Djinn are two different races of ephemeral beings. You cannot turn a Spirit into a Djinn in the same way you can't turn a Ghost into an Angel. That's not a problem though: Djinn are everywhere. The examples I wrote have a strong arabic vibe because they were all created for my game, but don't let that stop you. They are a sort of midpoint between human and spirit, not bound to a certain place or culture. If you add the fact that many are trapped into mobile vessels, this means you can always find one around if you know where to look.

                            You can still craft a Spirit to look like and think like a Djinn, though. Spirit, Mind, maybe some Forces to simulate a Rukh catalyst and you're done. Now that's something most Djinn will be incredibly offended about.

                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            2. How far can summoning be performed? If I have Iran mage in London can he summon Djinns from his home town?
                            As far as his summoning powers can reach, which is probably not that far. Djinn sent on a quest will travel far and far away, but it's very impractical. He would have an easier time summoning Djinn from the London area

                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            3. What is status on Goetia kinda side? You mention Clavicularis in text, but they are Astral mages first. Are Djinns connected to Astral somehow?
                            Not much. Their thoughts have a vague reflection in the Astral but it's weaker than that of humans. The Clavicarius connection is only there because that Legacy draws some mystical imagery from King Solomon, which was one of the greatest Djinn summoners in known history. I had to address them a bit.

                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            4. Where Djinn are "being"? They have their own Twilight strata, like ghosts and spirits have different?
                            No, at least not in a wide sense. Unmanifested Djinn are either in Twilight like spirits do or in their own Sanctuary, which is a separate dimension not unlike a Demon's Bolthole


                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                            Maybe not say that Marid are the same connected to Water as Efreet to Fire, but that there are some strong themes and many of Marids are bound to water, somehow - but not all of them?
                            It might be a solution, but it still does not sound right to me. This is something I thought a lot about before making a decision, but I still think the one I made was the best one. I'd rather avoid doing that, not only because it might cause some confusion and is not exactly loyal to the sources, but also because it helps me differentiate Marid from Efreet and play with their rivalry in a way that's not that of the natural elements. Sorry, but this is a point I'm fairly adamant about.

                                    No need to complicate things. In their true shape, they just look like Djinn. Creatures of variable anthropomorphic shape more often than not showing shades of natural elements or clear supernatural nature within their form. Technicolor hair and skin are frequent, legs made of clouds, slippers and turban are optional. Some resemble angel, other demons and others might even appear human at first sight. They don't have to answer to a local culture of sort by default. Think of them as humans, when it comes to the variety of their mind, attitude and personality. Djinn have their own culture but some might embrace human (or supernatural) ones. keep in mind that they can also alter the shape easily: in the end, they look whatever you want them to be.

                                    Djinn are not local to a single place, they just are (which, by the way, it's not my canon but comes straight from the Quran). You can have one not much different from what you'd expect a spirit of the moors to be living along one that seems to come straight from "One Thousand and One Nights" and make them fight an Efreet that pretty much looks like a Fire Elemental from a fantasy game. That's part of their charm.

                                                    Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                                                    Moving on. Sanctuaries. Do you have any ideas behind them for mechanics? Because I can picture a self-contained pocket dimension requiring a lot of Essence and time to establish. Unless that's not the case, that is.
                                                    Actually yes. I always pictured them as cost-free and relatively easy to establish, as if they're part of the Djinn (this was before Beast and the lairs, which actually I could now exploit to improve Sanctuaries). It also plays well with the whole "Look at my power and amaze, mortal!" thing

                                                    Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                                                    Regarding Rukh Catalysts, do all Djinn have them? And if so, howw does that interact exactly with the benefits you mentioned for Djinn gaining a Ban and Bane? Is it supposed to be similar to the differences between unbound and Coalesced idigam? Try giving me an example with one of your sample Djinn.
                                                    Almost all of them, and those who don't usually will pick one soon. Without one, you have way less methods to recover Essence, making a Djinn life quite more complicated than they would like to (not to mention those forced into servitude, which are forced to take one if they had none) Essentially, you take a Ban and Bane for the privilege of having a Rukh Catalyst. Keeping with the concept I had from the beginning of Djinn as a midpoint between spirits and mankind, a Rukh Catalyst is the conscious choice of merging with an aspect of the world and become empowered by it even if gives you some weaknesses.

                                                    It has some common aspects with Uncoalesced Idigams, the most glaring one the lack of Influences, but it does not come into play very much. Statistically speaking 99% of Djinn have a Rukh Catalyst because, aside from the mechanical benefits, it's less than a big deal than for Idigams. Taking a Rukh Catalyst is comparable to a rite of passage for the adult age for Djinn, the moment you recognize yourself and your ideals and give them shape with the help of something you feel affinity with.

                                                    The degree of consciousness and deepness of this choice may vary. For example, Zahira thought something along the lines of "I'm the might and the fury of the earth given shape and I want to instill terror in the heart of my enemies", Cyrus was more "Wow, I do like the glitter of gold and the effect it has on people", Akraig is as huge and as powerful as the calm depths of the ocean and Pisha loved hanging around open graves so much that one day she just happened to make those her catalyst. Rukh Catalysts can change, but is even harder than changing one's Virtue and Vice. Zenebatos was a simple Janni associated with birds but he went through a lot, became a Shaitan and his identity and power changed so much that he picked a new Catalyst (though Shaitan cheat, but it gives you the idea)

                                                    Generally speaking, Djinn see the one of their kind without a Catalyst as young and immature at best and foolish and urespectful at worst. Picking a Catalyst really is a way to affirm themselves and declare their identity, even for powerful Djinn. Unless you're the Unconquered, but he's special
