                                Sirens are incredibly healthy creatures; there are even legends of hunters searching for mermaids just so they can consume their flesh and gain immortality. The truth is that, while Sirens can live significantly longer than humans, it isn't necessarily immortality. The longest time that any Siren has been ever able to live is close to 300 years. Most Sirens never even come close to that age before they are hunted down or die from complications. Of course, that's not to say that Sirens are weak creatures.

                                While Sirens do not have a regenerative power outside of their Verses, they are notoriously difficult to pin down. As long as a Siren still has Pneuma (Breath), she will not suffer from her pain. If her rightmost Health box is marked with bashing damage while she still has at least one point of Pneuma, she does not need to roll Stamina to remain conscious; the breath of life within her, the will to fight and survive The Deluge, allows her to push through her pain. If her rightmost Health box is marked with lethal Damage while she still has at least three points of Pneuma, she does not take an additional point of damage each minute; the pain of bleeding out cannot compare to the suffering felt within The Deluge. However, once she drops down to no Pneuma or two Pneuma respectively, reality ensues and she must make a Stamina roll or receive a point of Damage each minute until she regains Pneuma or is exposed to her Waters (still pushing for Oceans to be called Waters).

                                Because Sirens have a connection to The Deluge, they have the ability to Trace events back to the source, akin to following a river to its source. In their minds, visions of rushing water flows through like a movie reel rewound. The Siren can see the moment in which a lonely child loses hope in finding a friend. The Siren can see the moment in which a murder victim is taken away by the killer. It is a vivid scene that plays in her mind, telling the secrets that have been hidden, forgotten, or untold. In game, a Siren may spend a point of Pneuma to trace through an object or person's experiences, similar to the Psychometry Merit. Unlike the Merit, a Siren asks a question before she Traces her object or person and attempts to find experiences that would answer her question. For example, a Siren wants to trace the events before an arson occurred. Her question is "Who was the person that started the fire?" She spends a point of Pneuma and begins to see visions throughout time. As she's passing through, she sees several people in the area before and during the fire, including a man leaving the house before the fire started and a woman throwing garbage over the house's fence moments before smoke begins to appear. The vision stops. She doesn't necessarily know the exact person that committed the arson, but she has two possible suspects. She may recall the vision if she needs to, but she cannot refine it until she finds the people in the vision and attempts to Trace them. She may also attempt to Trace the scene of the arson again to get a different question answered, such as "How long did the fire burn?" or "Who was in the house before it burned?" Her Traces need to be specific to the object or person she is Tracing.

                                Sirens are also capable of calling to another Siren, akin to the way that whales call out to other whales. It isn't necessarily a conscious act, however; the Song that resonates within each Siren continues to sing, even if a Siren is not singing a Verse. Depending on a Siren's Akasha, the Siren is able to feel the presence of the Song within others. It may be another Siren in the city. It may be a Banshee, howling in pain. The Siren may also be able to hear the lack of Song within others, finding people who are Breathless. The Siren may also increase how far her Song travels by singing a Verse or by simply spending a point of Pneuma to release a quick wave of the Song, like a flare that can only be heard by the Sirens.

                                Sirens do have things that work against them, however. Because a Siren is a product of both civilization and nature, she feels profound pain when she is affected by unnatural toxins. Chemicals such as bleach or various pesticides are utterly toxic to Sirens and deal aggravated damage to a Siren. The exposure to the chemicals also cause their ability to resist pain to go out of wack. When a Siren is exposed to chemicals while she has damage in her rightmost Health box, her ability to ignore its effects is temporarily lifted until she can remove the points of aggravated damage. And while Sirens can survive pain, it only really works as long as her body is connected to her lungs. If a Siren is ever to lose a body part while she is in her human form, she must find medical help immediately before the lost part dissolves into sea foam. Flesh cut off the Siren's body will only last for 1 hour when exposed to the air before it dissolves. If a Siren is decapitated, it's a done deal; the Siren's head dissolves into sea foam while the body is fresh for an hour. If the body part is placed on ice, however, it may last for several months. Some hunters that know of this capture Sirens and force them to live in a tank as they systematically cut off pieces of her body in order to steal away some of her longevity.
