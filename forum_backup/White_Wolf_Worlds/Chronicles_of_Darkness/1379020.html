    Unchained in Manch-Vegas: Session 5 - Give me Liberty or give me Blockbuster

    04-06-2020, 02:07 PM
    Cast:
    • Liberty Griswald - Guardian/Tempter. Works as a Bodyguard for the Empire Security Company.
    Campaign Report:
    Liberty Griswald decided to go out and track down some weapons for his Ring. He went to the Crossroads Tavern and asked the Demon Bartender, Mac, where he could get his hand on some supernatural weapons. She pointed him to the Seamstress, a Demon who specialized in wearable Gadgets.

    Griswald came up to a Korean Seamstress shop. The old woman asks in broken English how she can help him, but as soon as he reveals his intent she drops the accents. She ushers him into the backroom where she has a dismembered corpse for some sort of ritual. He is slightly spooked by the brazen display of Demonic activity, but soldiers on. He was a soldier, not one to spend time on occult rituals. The Seamstress offers to make his driving gloves into Gadgets with the Knock-out Punch Embed in them. Because he’s a new customer she offers him a one-time deal, one gadget for one pact.

    Griswald was not familiar with dealing in pacts and went to a nearby bar he frequented. The waitress Tammy was a friend of his and he knew she was looking to be better at shooting her pistol. Griswald used his In My Pocket embed to create a laser sight for a gun, he knew it wouldn’t work, but it would act as a good prop to hide his pact. He offered her the laser sight to help her accuracy in exchange for something on her person. All she had on her was an ancient Blockbuster card that she used with her family and friends. Griswald accepted the terms and wrote a contract on a cocktail napkin (In game terms, he gave her 2 Dots in Firearms in exchange for 1 Lesser pact for a permanent duration).

    Griswald returned to the Seamstress’s shop where she told him his gloves would be ready in one day. She warns that she can’t just go around making Gadgets without raising suspicions, so if anyone else needs something they’ll need to purchase from her existing stock.

    At his office at the bodyguard company he works for, Griswald’s boss let him know that a Detective Jane Bullet called for him in regards to the attempt on his client (Alan’s Boss from Session 1). Griswald said he already gave his statement and that the police said he was done. His boss shrugged and let Griswald go on a job. It went smoothly, but when Griswald got back to his apartment his Landlady harassed him for working odd hours. He gave a snarky response back and then noticed a woman with a detective’s badge standing in front of his apartment door.

    GM Notes:
    • Preparation
      • Initially my goal for this session was to:
        • Get everyone more comfortable with their abilities.
        • Get everyone familiar with making Pacts.
      • Work I did before hand:
        • Sent everyone quick reference images with their Embeds/Exploits/Demon Form abilities.
        • Sent everyone a copy of the rules for all their abilities.
    • Session Start
      • Two of my players were supposed to show up, but only one did. Which allowed for a more personal story to be told.
    • What could I have done better:
      • I'm still uncomfortable with Conditions.
      • I need better quick reference material for myself. I have a DM Screen, but it's missing some key information for me.
      • I am making an XP tracker because it's getting hard to tell people what they can do with it.
    • What went well:
      • Liberty's player is a lot more familiar with how his character works and is getting the hang of things.
    • What do I need to not forget:
      • Liberty Griswald
        • Tammy was a person he had a lot of trust in and he formed a Pact with her only to give it to someone else.
        • .
      • Jane Bullet
        • A Demon Hunter, one who is a Demon herself, has seen the account of Griswald's handling of the Sleeper Agents. She's suspicious of him, believing he could be a Demon or other supernatural entity.
      • Seamstress
        • Will likely give Liberty his Knock-Out Gloves.
        • Will also likely further work on Tammy as a cover resource.
    • Notes for Next Session:
      • Griswald needs to interact with Jane Bullet, she shouldn't immediately figure out a he's a demon, but definitely should establish herself as an antagonist to him and his Ring.
      • Everyone else needs to get Gadgets and Pacts next sessions.
    Please let me know what you think. Advice is greatly appreciated as this is my first DtD campaign.

          There has to be a bit of balance - something valuable relinquished. Otherwise Demons could give three dots of resources in exchange for an old shoe. Such exchanges would be open to abuse.
          I'll break it down.
          1. He did collect all of Tammy's history at Blockbuster. The Blockbuster card itself is symbolic of that. When Griswald picked up the Blockbuster Card he could tell it had enough history for a small bit of Cover XP, not enough for a full dot of cover though. He physically took the card to make the deal look less like a deal with the devil and more like a standard exchange.
          2. Demons are allowed to weigh the advantage to the Mortal, but such a deal is leaving money on the table according to the rules. It's possible, but it's not as beneficial to the Demon. I've also justified to my players that if they give out too many really good deals the Angels might catch wind and go after their clients. He probably should have asked for something with a bit more history, but he wasn't making the Pact for himself and didn't want to expose his true nature to his friend..
          Page - 191 of Demon the Descent core rules:

          If the human’s aspect points total more than those of the demon’s side of the bargain there is no additional Willpower cost, but few demons give more than they take.
          • This is what I've been using to justify giving more than they take.
