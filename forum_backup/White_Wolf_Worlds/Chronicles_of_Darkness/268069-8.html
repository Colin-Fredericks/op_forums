                                                            Trust in Me

                                                            It had been, Adria Watson decided, the most surreal week of her life.

                                                            Dr. Hargrove had been an excellent advisor for the Political Canadian Society the past few weeks. He was a history professor, sure, but he knew a lot about youth movements, socialism, and that sort of thing, and with his guidance the Political Canadian Society had been expanding it’s membership, and was even looking at maybe having a convention on campus. When she went for a late night meeting with Dr. Hargrove the other night, she as proud of how things were going.

                                                            So was he, as it turned out. Enough to feed her some of his blood, making her feel stronger and more confident than ever before. She was not sure what all of it meant, but a growing feeling she had was that the notoriously nocturnal professor may be a vampire. Which would make her something more like Renfield, maybe, but she was not much of a fly eater.

                                                            But it did put an almost humorous take on this evening.

                                                            Adria had gone to Kiki’s to hang out a bit with Callie and possibly meet Louise or something. Then in came Naomi Cruz, who was certainly a…complicating factor in her life. Since meeting the Latina, Adria had both sometimes disliked and frequently desired her intently. Naomi was involved in many of the same cliques, and while she never argued against the Politically Canadian Movement, she never identified with it per se. She often danced around it, and their debates sometimes bordered into flirting. Then Naomi all but disappeared for a week, leaving her feeling bereft…and frustrated, feelings that were similar, as it would turn out, to Deanna Lee, the girl Naomi was stringing along. The result was, in retrospect, perhaps Adria’s biggest mistake, hard though it was to regret the encounter.

                                                            Tonight, Naomi was all smiles and charm, and moving in for the kill faster than Adria was prepared for. Then Callie and Penelope were there, one thing lead to another, and now Adria was mummified to her neck and hanging suspended horizontally seven feet off the ground from the giant spider web that filled Naomi’s bedroom. Naomi had become a shining golden haired woman with gold fangs and long, golden almost metallic looking legs, and she was carefully arranging a similarly mummified to the neck Callie and Penelope. Another girl was completely mummified on the bed, Adria did not know who she was but it did not bode well, especially when the Spider leaned in and bit Penelope, leaving her looking listless as she hung next to Callie.

                                                            Once she had made sure that Callie and Penelope were comfortable, the golden woman moved back across the room toward Adria, her long, delicate golden legs moving carefully in a spidery slow crawl across the room, her human body suspended in the air. She was smiling.

                                                            “I hope you are comfortable Adria. I wanted to talk to you a bit.”

                                                            “Ah, Naomi? Should I call you Naomi?”

                                                            “Sure, it’s not accurate but if it makes you feel better go ahead.”

                                                            “What else might I call you?”

                                                            “You may call me Clytemnestra.” The Spiders deep dark eyes intrigued her. She was so pretty…

                                                            “What are you?” Adria forced herself out of her reverie.

                                                            “What am I,” The Spider chuckled, then clucked slightly. It was the prettiest chuckle Adria ever heard. “The technical description would be god, though I am not sure that I fit the description you would apply to the word.”

                                                            Adria cocked an eyebrow, “Was Naomi ever real? Or just you the whole time.”

                                                            The woman frowned, her eyes seeming to darken. Adria felt a chill.

                                                            “Naomi was and is real. She is an aspect of me. Just because she is not solely and exclusively a human college student does not make her not a real person, with real feelings, and real loves. Deanna, for example.” Some hurt was evident in the Spider’s voice. And a little anger.

                                                            Adria nonetheless looked defiant, despite being suspended in the air. “So am I a meal? Do you want me to beg? Because I will not. Even if I end up like her, “ as she glanced at the mummy on the bed.

                                                            “What…?” The Spider glanced over and smiled, “Oh, that’s just Am. She likes it when I do that. Can you breathe okay Am?” The mummy wriggled happily.

                                                            Clytemnestra looked back. “Listen, Adria, you are not here for me to torture. Your business with Naomi is your business, and you two can work it out between you. I am looking to expand my influence beyond my worshippers, and you have a large social clique, and are well respected, so I had been planning to talk to you even before the vampire grabbed you.”

                                                            “Hargrove. It figures.” Adria sighed, “He is your enemy?”

                                                            “A god does not have a mere Vampire as an arch enemy. We just have competing spheres of influence. And I am not one to kill a vampire out of hand for hunting in my territory. I am going to have a word with him. In the meantime, though. No seeing him alone, no drinking his blood.”

But you will survive the experience with no ill effects.” The Spider smiled. “But I want to give you more of what you want, in exchange for your influence.” The Spiders’ legs shifted, almost like she was pacing, one leg onto the bed one onto a table, as Clytemnestra’s body was moved slightly forward. “You want power. Power over people. To no longer be the outsider, like in High School. Serve me, I can give you power. Stan will only make you his tightly controlled servant. I won’t control you, like you even now feel the pull of his blood. Serve me, and do so with your mind all your own.”
