    My Saboteur/Tempter hasn't made this one yet, but I have the embeds. The target item is mostly irrelevant with two exceptions:
    1. Small for purposes of package delivery
    2. Huge for purposes of grand display
    Apple of Discord + No Quarter

    Joey eyed the bike messenger as she held out the small box. Too small for a head, maybe a hand. He shook it and looked confused at the lightness of it. The messenger was sent packing when she tried to get Joey to sign for the package. He delivered it to the boss immediately.

    Giuseppe "The Grater" Gratelli looked up from his dinner.

    "Joey, why you bother me with this? When you see a man eating, do you think 'Oh now's the time to give him something else to do?'"

    The henchmen sheepishly reached for the box before he was slapped away. "Chooch!"

    Giù tore open the box to find a tissue paper-wrapped item no bigger than a cell phone, but seemed to be wavy instead of flat. The rest of his crime family looked on in silence from their place at the table. The tissue paper came off without issue to reveal a cheap 3x5 panel image of Giuseppe's wife, Caprice, laying nude in what was obviously a hotel bedroom. The odd shape of the medium somehow made the image more lurid.

    Caprice was out of her chair and screaming for the picture before anyone else could move. The capos soon jumped in, but not before Giù stabbed Marco with a dinner knife. Gunfire exploded from within Gratelli's as the bike messenger strolled down the block with a slight smile on her face.

    OR

    Cassandra could not believe her life! She finally got her sculptures the attention they deserved and now she was about to open her first major gallery. She owed everything to the old man in the slums who convinced her she could be a famous artist. The ratty brown bag "contract" he had her sign was just the metaphor she needed to see her true potential. Of course, she turned him away when he tried to ask her a favor later, but that was completely beside the point.

    The house lights dimmed and the spotlight beamed down as the cover was lifted. Cassandra couldn't believe her eyes. The smooth curves of her interpretation of the Mother Goddess were distorted into hard angles and jagged points...but she loved it. This bizarre creation was the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. One of the gallery guests approached the base and Cassandra jumped in front of her.

    "Get back. This is mine and you can't take it from me!" Cassandra stood defiant as several more guests seemed to move to touch the masterpiece. HER masterpiece.

    Cassandra fell unconscious after the champagne tray smashed into her face for the third time. The waitress turned away from the artist just in time to be slashed with a broken bottle. From an apartment window two blocks away, a young man took video of the chaos.

                Inflict Stigmata + Right Tools, Right Job

                When one straps this watch-like device onto one's wrist and activates it, small prongs and wires begin to burrow into the user's flesh. It's uncomfortable, but not necessarily painful. The Gadget grants the user a Supernatural Merit most relevant to whatever the user's situation may be, remaining for as long as the device is connected to the wearer's flesh. A slightly different version of this Lambda exists, replacing Inflict Stigmata with Show of Power, but for most, the persistent nightmares aren't worth its boon.
