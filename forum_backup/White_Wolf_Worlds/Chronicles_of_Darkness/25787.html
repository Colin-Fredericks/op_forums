    Abducted: The Chosen?

    12-08-2013, 10:50 AM
    So Sikla Alkis and I were talking the other day, and for a little while now I've been thinking about a way to use aliens in WoD without resorting to the Cthulhu mythos. So, I decided that, why not not focus on the aliens, and instead focus on their mortal agents on Earth? And for another swerve, there's no overarching umbrella for each splat? Instead, each X-splat is constantly fighting each other, unlike the various covenants and lodges and stuff that have to grudgingly share the night. Already have some preliminary ideas for the five player factions.

    Mulloch: The red-eyed green warriors who exist to expand their empire, this time through the use of human proxies. Great fighters, but their chosen have such a one-track mind that social skills are non-existent. Their bases are in their saucer-shaped craft, and the primary focus of their mutations is on a human's physical and mental abilities.
    Recticulan: The classic gray alien, small bodied, big eyed, and utterly focused on finding out everything they can about everything. They specialize in everything knowledgeable, but their chosen are so reliant on technology that their physicality is laughable. Their bases are their flying wing ships.
    Reptoids: David Icke's wet dream/nightmare, the Reptoids have been on Earth for centuries. Though they aren't nearly as in control as they like to brag, they still have the ability to grant their chosen great psychic powers and social boons, but without physical skills to accompany. They base themselves in cigar-shaped craft, often underwater.
    Locusts: A race without a name, the chosen of the Locusts are utterly focused on scouting out Earth's resources for the eventual invasion. These chosen are often mentally broken, but also ready to do what they must, meaning that there are few penalties for morality loss. The Locusts base themselves out of techno-organic craft.
    Aes: Old gods of mankind, having left the people of Earth after discovering that there are other worlds between worlds. Their chosen have been instructed to protect and guide mankind, but with a strange morality that, while helping their psychical and social skills, has left their long term planning to suffer. The Aes chosen often congregate in runic craft that vaguely resemble ancient chariots.

    The Greater Collective: A group of races that have been brought together by force so that no one can ever be alone again. The Collective wants to absorb all life to become whole, and so never be a single useless entity. The other races combat it with gusto, but the Collective knows it is better than to be a single part in a mismatched whole. Collective chosen have access to each others skills, but their traits are still their own.

    Anomalies: Miniature black holes, warps in space-time, tears into quantum reality, etc. The truth is that the universe has some weird shit going on in it. There's plenty of theories on how it all happens, but until someone can get a better idea, all the chosen can do is try and repair what's been damaged like a kind of planetary janitor staff.

    Theme: Struggling Apart: None of the alien races like each other, and as such their chosen don't either. Unlike mage orders or werewolf packs, it's almost open war sometimes between the chosen of two different alien races.

    Mood: Lonliness: Unlike every other splat, the chosen are often forced to work solo, never having the companionship of someone who can at least understand what it's like to suddenly be grabbed out of your bed and turned into something else. Add in that four out of the five races care nothing for humanity, and the fifth has a touch of blue and orange morality, and you're very much alone.

    Again, this is supposed to be getting the idea of aliens away from the Cthulhu mythos for a while, so what do you guys think?
