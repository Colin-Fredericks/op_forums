            As this is 2e only, I'll skip the Bound.

            From the 2e Changeling stuff (though this is somewhat subject to change):

            Earth Bones Ogre. Earth Bones gives you X/X armor equal to half your Wyrd, and you can spend glamour for more ballistic armor for a turn. And you don't suffer wound penalties.

            Grab the Potent Kith Merit (increases your Wyrd for calculating your armor by 1), Cloak of the Bear's Massive Form (Ogres can choose animals that are size 12, which is a huge boost to Health, on top of any boosts to Stamina appropriate), Cloak of the Elements 3 (3/3 armor that stacks with the Kith armor), Chrysalis (if your ST lets you stack it with with the previous two, which is a maybe in my mind, but if so 2/1 armor +1 Size for each success after the first).

            That package alone makes for an insanely tough customer.

            Tacking on Red Rage of Terrible Revenge is a bit of questionable choice for a tank, since it deals 1 lethal per turn to you, but you get a boost equal to your successes in Strength, Initiative, and general armor, as well as Wyrd/2 boost to Stamina that does boost Health for being an Ogre.
