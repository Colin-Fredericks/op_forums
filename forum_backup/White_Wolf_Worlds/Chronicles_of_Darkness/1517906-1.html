        I'll be extremely blunt when I say this:

        Las Guadanas​ are very clearly based off of the Adolfo Constanzo cult -- up to and including the incorrect attribution of blood/human sacrifice to Santa Muerte, when the religion in question was Palo Mayombe. They're a criminal cartel worshiping La Santa, who is a religious figure that has already been incorrectly used numerous times by individuals in various pieces of media. The fact that the writers are aware that Santa Muerte goes back to "Aztec Death Cults" (which is an extremely ... blase way to say that Santa Muerte is a long-practiced tradition that has been passed down through the generations) indicates that there is the capacity to do research. In particular, the nail in the coffin here is that Las Guadanas are a Santa Muerte-worshiping Drug Cartel. I don't think I need to explain what the problem is here.

        South America has been used consistently throughout the history of CoD/WoD as a "mysterious evil death cult criminal sanctuary", for lack of a better term. To see this done yet again, to see a semi-closed faith so deliberately misrepresented, and to see people so immediately ready to just... put the pedal to the medal on this is extremely disconcerting.

        If you want to have a compact based off Santa Muerte belief, that's fine, but ... like, research should have been done, and you really, REALLY should not have involved the cartels, especially since this is VERY clearly based off of an ACTUAL real life cult that has caused Santa Muerte to have a bad reputation.

        It gets very old to see, time and time again, Mexico/the Global South used as an "OOOH SPOOKY SCARY OOOH CULTS OOOH BLOOD SACRIFICE" zone. There's so much more to it than that, and the fact that these racist concepts are just left uncommented on and celebrated doesn't sit well with me.

        Las Guadanas could very easily be a supremely interesting group, playing off of the positive aspects of La Santa, and the positive views of Death in general.

        I don't think I'd be so frustrated if it were for the fact that the authors know She has been passed through the generations by these "Aztec Death Cults" (as has been so eagerly said), which means that someone like. Google searched La Santa, searched the faith, saw everything else, and ... decided to go with the portrayal that continues the line's use of Mexico/South America as an edgy zone for blood sacrifice, criminals, and murder.

        Anyways, I'm hoping that Las Guadanas is changed when it's expanded upon. It gets really, really old, seeing the same things passed off over and over again.

        EDITING TO ADD: I'm actually also irritated because I KNOW Onyx Path does research. Scion works with Native individuals to respectfully portray the faiths in question and the cultures involved. I KNOW OP does actually put in the work not do rehash these old offensive stereotypes with other lines. Is it just ok when it's Mexico, then? I know Hunter and Scion are different writing teams but there's something that just gets under my skin about it.

        I need to make it very clear, too, that part of my issue is just looking at the way South America has been treated in the canon before. While there are a few positive examples, the OVERWHELMING majority of content is... well, like this.

                    God, those were some chaotic couple of weeks... Well, let's get back to this thing, shall we?

                    Ashwood Abbey

                    Our dear old beloved hedonists, the Ashwood Abbey are somewhat of a divisive subject within the fandom- it looks like you either love those bastards, or hate them with passion, and it is easy to get why. There is a reason why the original Hunter 2e team have considered to unite them with the Hunt Club and make them into a full blown cabal, something I am personally happy didn't happen. There is some dark honesty with the Abbey that you can't get with other organizations- that in the end, monsters are not people. This is the very base of the Code, and that separation is essential for it to work. And if monsters are not people, then you should not treat them as people. And if you should not treat them as people, than it is ok to kill them, right? After all, that is the Code. And if it is ok to kill them, it is ok to harvest them, right? Cheiron are doing that, the Bear Lodge are doing that, Campion are doing that. So if you can harvest them, than clearly you can also play with them- people hunt animals all of the time. Oh, sure, you could speak about it as "immoral", but in the end, hunting is just something humans do- kill for fun, and it is ok because they are not human. Heck, Shulpae even eat monsters- this is only ok if we are talking about something which is definitely not human. So if you can harvest them, hunt them for fun, eat them and so forth- why to stop here? Monsters are not people, and so it is ok to not treat them as people. And if there is some dark, sick fantasy that you have buried within the back of your mind, something you could never, ever do on another human being because it is sick and twisted- than it should be ok if you will do it on monsters, right? After all, they are not people. They are Fair Game.

                    Sure, any sane person would tell you that this is taking things too far- after all, there are things which we consider to be immoral even when used against none-human entities. Animals, in Western perspective, have some rights- right to minimize their suffering, for example. Yet not everyone agree on those rights, and some people, especially those who are somewhat detached from the rest of society, have a different view on things. When you are powerful enough, the laws of society no longer apply upon you. When you have enough money, you can throw it on your problems and make it disappear. When you already have absolute control over the life of others, morality is just a thing you can bury beneath tons of hard, cold cash. And if you have those sick, twisted desires, and all of that power in the palm of your hand, wouldn't it be better that instead of practicing those desires on people, that you would do it on none people? And what better use there is for it than practicing them on those who actually prey on humanity and cause misfortune to others? Heck, you could probably even write it off as charity service! You should get a tax relief for that!

                    Rich people have their own morals- just like hunters.

                    Now, even if we are to consider the Ashwood Abbey as being composed of the rich and the powerful, it is still not enough for them to be a conspiracy- a bunch of rich people who go around and kill monsters for fun is a nice concept, but that's just it. A bunch of people. However, the Abbey is far more than that- it is not merely a social gathering, it is a Hellfire Club. Heck, it is a network of such clubs. It is a social structure aimed at allowing the platform for rich hunters to go around and kill monsters. Every hedonistic rich bastard can go around and stab vampires in parties, but doing so with the logo of the Abbey? Now, that's the extra. The Abbey is a brand, and rich people are more than happy to pay for the brand, to show that they have a class. They are not just bastards with money, they are bastards with money who can afford themselves to pay membership fees. Not only that they are playing the most dangerous game and go through highly risky parties, they either pay a significant amount of money to do so, or sometimes are being sponsored by the compact in order to do so under the brand of the Abbey. Cells of the Abbey are supervised, they have membership fees and license, and if you are to stop pay the fees, or start breaking the rules of the Hellfire Club (like, for example, starting to kill people instead of monsters), the license is being revoked. The Abbey can't let its brand to be contaminated, after all. They need to make sure it represent what they want it to represents- this is a sign that the parties are of a high quality, that the prey has been scrutinized, that the game is being well defined. And if you are using their brand without their permission- well, the Abbey would have to do something about it, and that might mean that for some very sad consequences, you are no longer counting as a person. Again, very unfortunate- but the brand must be maintained, after all.

                    So this is the thing that is often missed about the Abbey- the rich people? They are not the driving power behind the compact. They are its costumers. The Hellfire Club is there to offer services, such as the brand, the parties, the social network and the activities (hence why the sponsor talented hunters). The Abbey is a business- and a well oiled one, too. In some ways, as C&C mentions, it could be conside4red a pyramid scheme: only members of the Abbey are its own clients, who pay huge sums of money just to operate under a brand given by a mysterious sponsor. Someone makes tons of profit out of rich bastards who want to kill monsters with class, basically playing them like fools. A lot of money flows through that mechanism, and it is that mechanism, not the rich bastards, which could be the leading factor for them to become a conspiracy.

                    But if that is the case- what is the plan?

                    Well, rich people playing "the Great Game" is a trope and existing conspiracy theory which is already represented within the Abbey- so on the night to night activity, the Abbey keeps offering the same services to its costumers- and potentially focusing more on initiating Bacchanals, sponsoring competitions and talents in order to satisfy the desires of the rich people who take part in the would be conspiracy. The person at the head of the pyramid, however, might have other plans in mind- after all, they have power, money and control. As long as they decide to just let hunters to kill and get killed with style, there is nothing that needs to happen. But if they are to decide to actually do something with all of the resources they have, well, that might be the point where things would become interesting. Yet, considering that we are likely talking about someone who is very selfish and cares only on a material gain, what could make them to actually change direction in such a manner?

                    To pay a debt, perhaps.

                    Look, let's stop beating around the bush. This is an Hellfire Club. It is oozing with vice. Lust, greed, gluttony, wrath, pride, envy, sloth- the Abbey is all of those and more. It is a fertile ground for Whisperers to rise, to infernals to grow, and to archdemons to feed. Heck, the whole brand of the compact might serve as a testament for such a demon. The hunters of the Abbey can not be satiated, and it is very likely that some entities who watched from the cracks between worlds have learned to exploit them. After all, we are talking about people who already have everything they might wish except of fun, and so fun they will have. The head of the pyramid, unlike the costumers of the Abbey, might not have been born to money- instead, it might have well been someone who cut a deal with a dark being in order to gain money, and perhaps as they cut more and more deals for power, the debt for Hell grows as well, and then there is only one way to repay that debt- with souls. With blood. That, however, is unethical- you can't just sacrifice the lives of people to save yours! That's immoral!... Thankfully, the compact is specialized at finding those whoa re not people, and what better way to pay their debt than by using those monsters- and the vice that the Bacchanal produce - to satisfy the demon lords who lurk in the shadows of the conspiracy?

                    With that scenario in mind, it also helps to define what kind of Endowments the conspiracy version of the Abbey might look like- from one end, it should be about vice, about pleasure, about sin and taint and fun. On the other, it should be about control, about resources, about the ability to throw money at the problem and get what you want. The result of that mashing of ideals should be something along the lines of Infernal Investment (well, they probably won't put "infernal" in the name, but you get the gist). The members of the conspiracy pe4rform the Vigil as an act of devotion to a demonic Testament. The acts of the Vigil become a secondary (or tertiary, in case of Vice-Ridden people) Vice, allowing them to regain special "infernal" Willpower, which can then be expanded upon all sorts of subtle supernatural abilities, mostly ones which control, submit, bind, twist and corrupt others. Break the will of the monster, infest it with desire, confuse, drug, devour explore, claim. Get what you want, remove what you don't. Make others touch you, do not get touched. Make the world answer your truest, darkest desires, all in the name of the Vigil. If conspiracies are about making monsters into people, then the Ashwood Abbey's are Possessed, and their infernal vestments are being worn proudly, all in the name of the Vigil. Hell has fire too, after all- and it banish the darkness all the same.

                    Well, in that case, you might ask, what is the goal of the Ashwood Abbey?

                    To make sure that the fun will never end.

                    Unlike most other conspiracies, the Ashwood Abbey do not desire an end to the Vigil- there can only be fun as long as there is a Code, only as long as that some people are not people. They want a full blown, global, everlasting Bacchanal, a party which will never end, a Weirdmagedon so to speak. They want the darkness to be revealed, monsters to be driven out of the shadows, for morality to collapse and for vice to overflow the world. A world where the strong control, where the weak obey, where passion is currency and all live in their desire. A world overridden by monsters and hunters, all of which dance while the Abbey looks on the burning world with pleasure. The Ashwood Abbey wants a Hell upon Earth- and when the streets will be soaked red, when the gates of Hell will be breached, when desires will take form in flesh and the legions of Inferno will march upon the world, those who are able to pay the fees will be spared, will the others will become monsters through Hell's foul touch. And how can they pay the debt? Money? No, you fool- Hell has no use for money. Blood. Pain. Desire. Souls. That's the currency. Practice the Vigil, join the Bacchanal, and Hell will spare you- for as long as you pay the debt. Refuse, and you will be a Fair Game. Live in your sin- that's how the debt will be paid.

                    Rich people have their own morals.

                    Like Hunters.

                    Like Demons.

                    Finally, some break to sit and write. This was a fun bit- I hope you enjoyed it, and stay tuned for the next pieces in this project!
