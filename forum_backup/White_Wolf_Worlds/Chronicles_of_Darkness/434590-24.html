                                So, I was reading the Leviathans and Beasts thread over in the Beasts forum, and it made me curious about something. I don't know if this has been spelled out in the docs so far or not, so I'm asking here.

                                In general, WoD games tend to follow a principle of scaling powers, where the higher your Power Stat, the mightier you are. How strongly does Leviathan follow this principle?

                                I ask not because I think Leviathan wouldn't follow that general principle, but because, in the wake of the Marduk Society debacle/underground fire, I was curious just how powerful elder Leviathans are planned on being.

                                The reason why? In my opinion, high Sheol Leviathans should be, pardon the language, fucking terrifying.

                                Leviathans are supposed to be Old Gods still lingering in the mortal world, to the point that TKR said in one post that Leviathans are one of the few things he envisions in his headcanon to legitimately claim the position of Divine. And while we may have outgrown our need to worship them for comfort, that should not, in my opinion, negate the fact that humans should be terrified of them.

                                Look at it like this; we know all about hurricanes, earthquakes and tidal waves now. We know what causes them, we understand where they come from, we no longer believe we need to sacrifice people to keep them from happening. That does not stop us from being scared of them. We take desperate precautions against them, we avidly watch for them, and even then, when they hit, all we can do is huddle in safety and pray that we'll make it through alive.

                                And that, to me, is what an Elder Leviathan should be. A living disaster. Sure, they may not want to be - part of the tragedy of being a leviathan is to be alienated from humans at every step; like Mages, their powers only emphasize how different they are, how they can't be with humans no matter how much they want to. But that doesn't change the fact that when a God speaks. the world trembles.

                                Sheol 1-2 Leviathans being fairly weak and easy prey, comparatively speaking, is fine. But when the Sheol 10 monsters crawl from the deep, that should be an "oh, fuck me..." moment for hunters. To stand before such an abomination is to forsake the right to laugh at your primitive ancestors and to understand intimately why they crawled on their bellies along the beach beneath the gibbous moon, offering tokens of blood and gold to the Masters from the Sea. You may stand defiant, you may use your arsenal to drive it back into the sea, but you know in your heart that you only slowed it for a time.

                                Leviathan: The Cult Chronicles are, at their heart, a horror game. And whilst some of that horror comes to the Leviathans themselves - the horror of being outcast by what you are, the horror of seeing how your elders and future selves are as alien to you as you are to humans - more of that horror should be the impact on humanity from a Leviathan's presence.

                                I have never read Delta Green, myself. But this description of one piece of fluff, describing how a retired operative has to be killed after a backed-up septic tank causes him to experience PTSD flashbacks of an anti-Deep Ones mission and butcher his family, haunts me.

                                This, to me, is essential to the "feel" of Leviathan. The horror from humans is living it. The horror from Leviathans is know that you did this to them - even if you didn't want to.

                                Many pages back, there was an intense argument about the proposed nature of the Marduk Society. In passing, I feel now that, whilst I wasn't happy with their portrayal before, I could be happy with it now... if it takes these motifs into account.

                                The price of being a hero is the terrible, soul-crushing toil of battling Leviathans. It is murdering cultists whose minds have been shattered beyond repair, staining yourself with innocent blood so that greater numbers of innocents may never face this fate themselves. It is standing toe to toe with creatures who would rend your body and soul asunder with ease and knowing that even if you succeed this day, the scars will linger with you forever. It is knowing the injustice of it all, that you could do so much more, but you can do nothing to help; for you are the thin line that keeps back the wrath of monstrosities that could destroy all that humanity has built.

                                And, on the other side of the battlefield, the Leviathan weeps; it did not want this, it did not want to hurt these people, but hurt them it did, for such is its nature. It cared for them as best it could, trying to be as kind as a being born of cruelty can. And now it must watch them die, cut down whilst screaming prayers to a god who cannot save them, their confusion and loss bubbling like blood between their dying lips. And it knows that, even if it survives today, it will all happen again in the future. But it cannot let it end in death. The beast that is their flesh will not let their life just slip away. The god that is their soul will not tolerate such insolence from mortals. And so it rises, to continue the age-old bloody dance, as it will tomorrow and tomorrow and tomorrow...

                                        Inspiration comes and goes in waves, things have been a little slow recently but I did just create a Channel inspired by Yog Sothos. This replaces Orberos' Kin (and probably Memories Before Time as well)

                                        The idea of being able to obliterate your past without consequence came from Genius' Manes. It seemed fitting that as creatures of primordial chaos Leviathans aren't bound by orderly things like action -> consequences. That said, unlike Archmages who also have a similar trick I think the most fitting use is to investigate your family history and make small changes to the very recent past. Tearing up all of reality, while awesome, is more a (arch)Mage thing than a Leviathan.
                                        The Key and the Gate (Descendent Channel)

                                        The Leviathan is not merely a thing of one Space-Time continuum, but allied to the ultimate animating essence of existence's whole unbounded sweep.
                                          • Dice Pool: Intelligence + Composure + Sheol
                                          • Action Type: Instant (no matter how long is spent in the past)

                                        The Key and the Gate allows the Leviathan to stretch his perceptions across Time itself, at first they may only perceive but at deeper Depths a Leviathan may change the past.

                                        As multidimensional beings of primordial chaos Leviathans frequently create temporal paradoxes and rarely care that they do. Damage to the fabric of spacetime is never predictable, but most commonly a Leviathan just ignores paradox. One who goes back in time and kills her grandmother returns to a timeline where her parents were never born and changes have spread forth like wind from a butterfly’s wings; but she appeared ex nihilo at the point of her emergence and followed a similar life path from that point. The same usually happens if you go back and kill your rivals grandmother.

                                        Killing the Leviathan themself is more likely to work, the closer to the present you do the deed the more likely it is. However if the Leviathan survives they’re instantly aware of the attempt, if the Leviathan has this Channel they’re also aware of exactly when the event took place and might pop back to see who did it. However there are risks, while a Leviathan is travelling back they are as vulnerable as they would be in the present.

                                        Even in extreme situations - if a Leviathan went back and turned her home nation into an atomic wasteland - she will likely populate the new timeline with enough fragments of the original to preserve her life path. However such stupidity will fray spacetime to the breaking point and likely draw furious protectors of reality from all corners of the World of Darkness and beyond. That’s if the Leviathan doesn’t return to the present to find she’s torn a huge hole in the spacetime continuum and is now floating outside reality (Leviathans can survive outside reality, but they generally don’t like it there and it’s hard to get back). Intelligent Leviathans stick to making small changes to the recent past.

                                        Fortunately this Channel endows the Tribe with an instinctive understanding of spacetime. It’s far from perfect, but a Leviathan gets a bad feeling about any action which would cause too much damage.

                                        “Depth 0”: Birthright of Awareness
                                        “Depth 1”: By spending one Ichor the Leviathan may inhabit the body of his past self. She has no control over his actions but complete control of his senses. She may focus on things her past self does did not and may spend Ichor in the present to activate Channels in the past so long as they are only used for observation. If the Leviathan has changed their past they see their past in the present time line, this is useful for filling in any blanks left by changes to the past.
                                        “Depth 1”: The Leviathan can perceive disruptions to spacetime. Depending on the disruption’s nature this may be automatic or require a Wits + Composure + Sheol roll.
                                        “Depth 2”: If the Leviathan stands in a location that exists in more than one timeline they may choose to peer into any timeline they wish, and may use other Channels to enhance their perception. In addition the Leviathan is instantly aware if another version of themselves exists in any of the other timelines.

                                        Sidebar: Thy Greatest Enemy
                                        It’s rare to find temporal duplicates of the same Leviathan. Even when the timeline is split before the Leviathan is born, it is more common for all but one iteration to slide between timelines and become part of a merged being with personality and memories from every iteration.

                                        Which is not to say it never happens. When temporal duplicates of a Leviathan exist they are all part of the same organism. Not in the way your hands are both part of you, there is no “head” containing a core brain above both Leviathans, instead think of conjoined twins.

                                        Because they’re technically the same individual both Leviathans are vulnerable to the other’s psychic Channels and easy targets for Rituals. Naturally Leviathans dislike anyone having this power and it is not unknown for Leviathans to lead their Cults into war to destroy or assimilate their alternates.

                                        “Depth 3”: By spending two Ichor the Leviathan may see out of the perception of their Ancestors, they may not see through other Leviathans or go past a Leviathan on their family tree unless they can find a detour through human or Hybrid branches. The roll is automatically Contested by Composure + Supernatural Advantage, and this means that seeing through the eyes of another Leviathan is extremely hard though if a Leviathan achieves it she may use any Channels they have in common to enhance their perception.
                                        “Depth 4”: The Leviathan may now use the Depth 1 iteration on their future self and the Depth 3 iteration on their discendents. As the future is in constant flux Leviathans may create any paradox they want without consequence; usually.
                                        “Depth 5”: In the World of Darkness there is a past, a constantly moving present, and infinite possible futures. At this Depth the Leviathan can perceive not just their future self and future descendants but all their future selves and descendants and peer into possible futures other than the most probable one. For example the Leviathan may peer into the future where they decided to sacrifice their entire cult to acquire some guarded information (simply deciding to do that then changing your mind later isn’t enough, since it was always more probable that you’d rescind the order before acquiring the information).
                                        “Depth 6”: By spending four Ichor the Leviathan may not only inhabit the senses but take full control and act to change the past (or the future, though there is rarely any point in doing so).
