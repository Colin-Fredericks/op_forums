                                                Originally posted by Stupid Loserman View Post
                                                The best part was easily Hybrid Stories: The final chapter provides something the past guides haven't: several setting alternatives for a cosmology that allows for the Fallen and the Unchained to coexist in a single world as two distinct types of fallen angel. There are some good ideas here, and the prose helps to break up the constant referring between books. A small majority of them lean towards folding the God-Machine into the Fallen cosmology as a sort of sub-phenomenon or deviation, but they generally do so pretty well, without damaging either perspective as characters. Another option has a simple but elegant solution that uses the fallen to represent an occasional byproduct of possession by demons from the Inferno. I would very much like to see a chapter like this in any future translation guides.
                                                This by far was my favorite part, and so far my favorite translation guide as well. Personally I think it explained and integrated both games best out of all of them. Quite a feat considering how disparate they seemed to each other as compared to the other template editions. Now hopefully after Mage Awakening comes out they could consider making a second edition version of the Translation guides in the same fashion.

                                                    Actually, after reading the following passage on page 62, I wounder if the author saw my homebrew system (Demon the Possessed). I'd be so proud and grateful of notice if I'd somehow inspired someone at whitewolf. Even if not the case, I like the idea.

                                                    Reformed Possessors
                                                    "I came into the world as a possessing spirit eager to corrupt it. I would bring a little bit of the Hell that was visited upon me by God to the Earth that He banished me from. But instead of becoming a second voice in my victim’s mind, her consciousness joined with mine and her memories became my own. I’m still a demon, an angel who defied God and spent eons in torment, but I’m also her, also human. I can’t tell you how great a gift that is."

                                                    The fallen angels are the demons of the Inferno. They are creatures of sin and abomination who corrupt and occasionally possess humans with the misfortune or poor judgment to invite them into the world. Occasionally, for reasons none of them understand, demon and host dissolve into each other to create one of the fallen. This tempers the madness of the demon and allows it to overcome its Torment enough to remember that it was once an angel.
