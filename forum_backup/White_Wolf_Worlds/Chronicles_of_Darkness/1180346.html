    Warden of Tartarus: Homebrew Left-Handed Legacy

    01-29-2018, 01:16 AM
    Jack,
    I finally have some information for you. I was able to find a single reference matching your description of the phenomenon. Oddly enough, it was a detailed description of a seemingly unique Legacy that a Mystagogue encountered about a century ago. The author apparently penned the report to warn away future generations from using such dangerous methods (naturally, it was buried, untouched, in an unrelated section of the library). Now, you know what I think of the Pentacle's "left-handed, right-handed" claptrap, but, well, this one makes me nervous. Not that the abilities of Legacy are in and of themselves wholly repugnant, but the mage who manages to master's them without losing life and sanity would be...truly     dangerous. The interviewee (read: prisoner) in the account called himself a Warden of Tartarus (Tartarus being the realm in Greek mythology where the Titans we're kept and punished). I've attached my synopsis of the Legacy. If you want the primary source, you'll have to convince the Boston Mysterium to let you "borrow" it. Second shelf, third row from the bottom, in the section on "edible yantras".
    Winds guide,
    Icarus

    Wardens of Tartarus

    Origins

    Parentage: Mastigos
    Background: He who guards the gates of hell may find he is trapped there as well. There exist rare accounts of these strange warlocks who hunt, capture, and, often, enslave spirits. They are always Nameless, seemingly bound to no Order or greater organization of the Legacy. Their abilities are similar enough, however, that a common thread can be traced. The Wardens (so named from a century-old confession from a captive member of the Legacy) hunt ephemeral beings, from the hungry spirits of the hisil, to the unquiet dead, and even stranger things like living nightmares that prowl the Astral. The Wardens know all too well, however, that what is not flesh cannot truly die, so instead they seal away the demons in the most secure prison they can construct: within their very soul, their Oneiros. And if one is to reign over hell, at least the in-mates can make themselves useful. The most dangerous Attainments provided by this legacy allow the Warden to make use of their captives' strange and myriad powers. Doing so inevitably risks the captives slipping free, and then the jailor might find himself joining the damned.
    Appearances: Wardens are so few and far between there is no real pattern to be found in their dress or mannerisms. However, if their captives begin to break free, they may start to exhibit symptoms not unlike Possession or Claiming. Their appearance may warp hideously based on the spirit they are currently channeling as well, though only advanced members of the legacy have the ability to do so voluntarily.

    Doctrine

    Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Intimidation 2, Investigation 2
    Initiation: It is unknown what initiation into this obscure legacy might look like. It seems that most members simply re-discover it on their own. It probably involves some encounter with an emphemeral being, however.
    Organization: The Wardens have no organization to speak of. Indeed, there is no account of more than one even existing at the same time.
    Theory: Hades was not the dark god who delighted in death. He was the one willing to do the dirty work, making sure the dead stayed dead. Dangerous and foul things walk this Fallen world. Even when their body is destroyed, many spirits simply reform and wreck havoc elsewhere. Wardens have the permanent solution. They carve out a soul-space where they keep dormant spirits from regaining Essence and reawakening. If a Warden happens to benefit from accessing the abilities of their charges, well that just makes it easier to keep the world safe. Other mages, ones with the gall to question their methods and sacrifice, are usually simply avoided. A Warden never really alone, anyhow. Sometimes the in-mates roll-over and mumble in their sleep, which can make it hard to concentr...

    Magic

    Ruling Arcanum: Spirit
    Secondary Arcanum: Mind
    Yantras: invoking a captive relevant to the spell's effect (+1 to +3, depending on captive's power and relevancy, yes extra powerful yantra is a feature); symbolism relating to Hades or Tartarus (+1); (could use more)
    Oblations: Wandering your Oneiros, making sure the inmates are secure; studying the abilities and weaknesses of ephemerals (could use more)

    Attainments

    First: Take Prisoners
    Prerequisites: Initiation
    The Legacy's signature ability, this Attainment allows a the Warden to capture dormant spirits and seal them away in their Oneiros. When a spirit loses it's final point of Corpus from lethal or aggravate damage, the Warden makes a Clash of Wills against the spirit reflexively. If the Warden succeeds, the spirit reforms inside the Wardens Oneiros instead of the usual "suitable area". In all likelihood, the captured spirit will remain there dormant, unless conditions in the Oneiros change such that the spirit can regain enough Essence to awaken.
    Situations where a spirit may regain essence include: whenever the Warden rolls a Breaking point, whenever the Warden triggers a paradox, whenever the Warden invokes the spirit using a legacy yantra and rolls an exceptional failure on the spellcasting roll, and at any point the Storyteller rules the conditions in the Warden's soul change such that they become resonant with a captured spirit. For example, if the Warden has captured the goetic manifestation of a man's violent impulses, and the Warden were to begin acting on similar impulses, this could lead to the goetia eventually awakening.
    When a spirit regains essence equal to it's missing Corpus, it may spend a point of essence to reawaken and, if it chooses, appear at the Warden's physical location. However, while the spirit is within the Oneiros, the Warden has the Open condition relative to it, which could lead to possession (or worse).
    With additional Arcana, this Attainment works on other types of ephemeral beings. Death 2 extends it to ghosts, Mind 2 to goetia, etc.
    Optional: Mind 1
    In the process of shaping his soul into a suitable Tartarus, the Warden learns the corridors of his mind inside and out. This Attainment mimics the spell "Perfect Recall", with reach assigned to instant use.

    Second: Inescapable Prison
    Prerequisites: Spirit 2, Intimidation 3
    A prison is useless without walls. This Attainment mimics the spell "Ephemeral Shield", with reach assigned to Advanced Duration.
    Optional: Mind 2
    A prison also has to keep troublemakers out. Those who seek to enter the Warden's mind find themselves lost in endless dark mazes, This Attainment mimics the spell "Mental Shield", with reach assigned to Advanced Duration.

    Third: Panopticon
    Prerequisites: Spirit 3, Investigation 3
    The inmates will police themselves if they believe the Warden could always watching. This Attainment mimics the spell "Shadow Walk", with reach assigned to instant use and Advanced Duration.
    Optional: Mind 3
    As long as the inmates are cooperating, they can be useful. The Warden can draw upon the knowledge of his captives to receive +2 to any one Skill. This Attainment functions the spell "Enhance Skill" with reach assigned to instant use.

    Fourth: Come Loudly
    Prerequisites: Spirit 4, Intimidation 4
    Corruption must be rooted out by force. This Attainment mimics the spell "Banishment", with reach assigned to instant use.
    Optional: Mind 4
    Prison riots can be dangerous when the inmates are unknowable horrors. The Warden can reveal to those around him the truth of the terrors he keeps at bay. This Attainment mimics the spell "Psychic Assault", with reach assigned to Advanced Area.

    Fifth: Rule in Hell
    Prerequisites: Spirit 5, Intimidation 5, Investigation 4
    At what point does the jailor of the damned become damned himself? A Warden can now channel the dark powers of his captives directly. This Attainment functions similarly to the "Craft Fetish" spell, with reach assigned to switch the primary factor to Potency and to Advanced Duration. The Warden can only use this Attainment on spirits he has captured using Take Prisoners, though the spirit is not bound to an object, but remains within his Oneiros. Additionally, anytime an activation role to use a spirit's powers is a exceptional failure, the bound spirit regains one essence.
    Optional: Mind 5
    You think you know monsters? You have no idea. But the Warden can show you. This Attainment mimics the spell "Terrorize" with reach assigned to instant use and to inflict the Broken Condition.

    Hey, it's a homebrew Legacy I came up with for an NPC a while ago and thought I'd post it. Of course, the character was pretty secretive so the players never learned more than a little hint about the legacy. Thoughts? Validations of my existence? I worry that, mechanically, the first attainment is more a liability than a Dangerous Forbidden Technique, but, as is, it does an awful lot for what's supposed to be the equivalent of a one-dot spell.

          I'd be a bit more specific about what skills and abilities can be provided by a given spirit, as well as the horrors presented, but I like the ideas for the attainments.
          Basically, there should be some more design to showcase the specifics of the captured ephemera. After all, a dude who gathers up various people's impulse to collect cats or something should have a weaker menagerie than a guy who hunts down spirits of murder and twisted appetites.

          If you tweak the idea slightly, so the creatures are often Awake, but starved for Essence and chained by the mage's will, they could be getting flogged for disobeying the mage. That could be the 3rd optional, since it jibes so smoothly with the attainment.

          Reforming in the mage's Oneiros and not being able to interact with their native realms would suck. The attainments could grant carrots and sticks for that sort of thing. Once they are properly subservient, the Warden can set them to tasks that they will excel at. Rewards could be some scheme for allowing them controlled amounts of Essence.

          One optional attainment (say, replacing that 4th) could be a telepathy variation that places the mind of a subject into contact with the mage's prisoners. This could allow a prisoner to use their influence or numina on the target. (generally an act that generates their forms of essence) Indirect control could allow them essence if they obey and withhold it if they don't (with some slipping by, subtly.)

          Grump, grouse, and/or gripe.

                                                  I somehow forgot to mention this in the write-up, but the attainments are intended to be perfectly functional on abyss critters (spirit for gulmoth, mind for acamoth, etc). In fact, my original elevator pitch was "Gaolers of Ialdabaoth meets Fangs of Mara". Locking non-beings away in your soul sounds like such a terrible idea that I could totally see some Mages trying it. The problem is that anytime they cause a paradox, every abyss inmate is regaining essence.

                                                  I am glad to see people like the idea . Though it seems we can all agree that the mid-level Attainments are somewhat lacking... I didn't want to stretch the mechanics too far in the original write-up, but with people's feedback I'm considered playing around with them some more.

                                                  How about this idea: the primary effects of the 2-4 dot Attainments are instead are a scaling version of the "fetish" spell, like the current 5-dot attainment, but limited to Rank 1 spirits at two dots, Rank 2 at three, etc, but have it also ignore the spirit's Withstand so that you aren't weirdly limited to only getting 1 dot of influence from a rank 4 spirit at maximum level. Or is a higher-ranked spirit's better dice-pool enough of a benefit? Using their captive's powers is supposed to be what makes these guys scary, but trying to make that work within the Attainment system is proving troublesome, so I'm open to suggestions .
