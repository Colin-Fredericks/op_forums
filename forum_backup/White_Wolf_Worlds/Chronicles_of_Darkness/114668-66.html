                Nah, when you target an area, spell that would check for number of subjects just checks for any and all subject in the area and applies spell to it.                                                         As far as I can tell, the two main roadblocks to these ideas are:

                                                        1. You can't really have a spell recast itself on command. You can have it recast itself every ritual interval, but you can't have it go off, say, every time you snap your fingers, or every time someone walks into the area.
                                                        2. You can't have a spell exclude people from its effect that it didn't exclude before, and you can't exclude someone based on a Key (as far as I can tell). The only way to exclude someone from an area at all is by casting Wards and Signs on them beforehand.

                                                        For the pigeon hole to work, I think you'd have to be satisfied with all dead pigeons reviving every (ritual interval). (EDIT: assuming that the revival was Lasting, rather than subject to the duration of the spell. If the duration determines how long they're alive again, the spell won't re-cast)

                                                        For the map generator, if you just cast it yourself, you could combine it with Wards and Signs, then exclude all previous targets from the casting so that no one gets a second map. There's no way for it to be completely automated and send a map to someone who just walked in, though. The closest you can get is a 5-minute ritual interval with Gnosis 9.


                                                        EDIT: You can have a spell go off once in response to a trigger with Hung Spell and Conditional Duration. So with booby trapping your home, it would work if you were OK with resetting it after it went off.
