    While re-watching Gregory Horror Show, I thought it would be a pretty good fit for Changeling, with Gregory House as an Arcadian realm.

    It's a fairly obscure series, so I'll provide a Link, and a short description too.

    The narrator, who's point of view we see from, gets lost in the woods, eventually arriving at the "Gregory House", a hotel full of strange people with unnatural characteristics. Nobody is as they appear, with most guests taking the form of animals or objects, and typically obsessed with one particular topic. As the narrator stays at the hotel, they gradually break down, losing track of their memory, their sanity, and even pieces of their identity.

    Will they be able to escape in time?

        Which of the 2ED Seemings do you think are the most fitting for Batman and the Joker?

        The former sounds like a Wizened. He feels broken by his origin, and attempts to fix himself by becoming one of the greatest detectives of all time. His gadgets could be imitated with 2ED Contracts of Artifice, which was an Affinity Contract for the Wizened in 1ED. Also, the Wizened Blessing negates environmental and circumstantial penalties to the Wizened's chosen craft, which is similar to how he is not affected by fear. As for Kiths, I'd say he is a Slewfoot + Brollachan, for Greatest Detective and I Am The Night, respectively. Instead of having Windwing as his third Kith, he glides with a Token that sprouts mechanical wings attached to his back. As for Contracts, he'd have 2ED versions of Darkness, Smoke, Oath And Punishment, Stone and the aforementioned Artifice.

        The Joker is harder to pin down, naturally. He describes himself as an Agent Of Chaos in "The Dark Knight", and he tries to prove how close everyone is to his madness in "The Killing Joke". He almost never explains anything about himself, to the point of preferring a multiple choice past. He loves to rip apart the bonds and rules that people think they can trust, and he has an artistic bent in what he does. And worst of all, he is highly persuasive. All of this makes me think he is a messed up Elemental Muse + Whisperwisp + Maker. Because of this, he has a much higher Wyrd than Batman. His lethal prank items would be Tokens that he summons with the Discrete Conjuration and Hidden Reality Contracts, rather than the Blessings of the Razorhand and Venombite Kiths. His Clarity is up for debate, and also depends on which version you're using as a base.
