        I agree with you on all points Second Chances. Being in South East Asia, I would love to see more hot spots beyond Europe (not that I mind Europe).

        Here's my wish list:
        ​
        Rodrigo Borgia Rome
        Clandestine Lancea et Sanctum and Invictus politicking during the splendiforous reign of the worldy Pope Alexander V. French invasion, Milanese aspirations, jealous cardinals, wild-eyed monks, and papal mistresses.

        The era of Charles V and Suleiman the Magnificent
        It's a titanic showdown between two 'Defenders of the Faith' as they vie for the soul of Europe while consolidating their grand empires. Fortresses are sacked, and Martin Luther's Reformation runs rampant. In the midst of chaos, Hunters question their allegiances, and turn on each other - Catholic vs Protestant vs Muslim.

        Haitian Revolution
        Latin America's first independence movement and modern history's largest slave uprising is the backdrop to the fleeting, combustive alliances with the Haitian Freehold. Here the Gentry take on French, African, and Vodou flavors.

        Japanese Occupation of the Philippines and the Liberation of Manila
        The Imperial Army invades the then-American colony hours after Pearl Harbor. Includes the Battle of Corregidor, the Bataan Death March, and the tragic Liberation of Manila. The Exarch of War reigns, while Manila's Awakened fight a losing battle to preserve the city. When the smoke clears, Manila will become the second most devastated city of World War II.

        Iranian Revolution of '78-79
        Argo from a Promethean perspective. Angry mobs, the clash of Westernization with Islamic piety, and the return of Khomeini all while the Created desperately seek to understand the human experiences of love, faith, and sacrifice. Elsewhere the Arisen attempt to reclaim forgotten relics across modern Persia.

        Hong Kong 1997
        Britain returns the port of Hong Kong to China. The transition may be peaceful, but not for the island's Kindred. Tempers flare when local vampires, used to running their own affairs, must now accept overlords in Beijing.

                                            Here's a few pitches, I'll add more later...

                                            Qin Dynasty Vampire: Covenants that rose of the Hundred Schools of Thought. The Legalist Covenant (which in addition to bureaucratic governance of vampires is also the culture's "we are monsters who are supposed to keep humanity in line through fear" faction) is beginning to enforce 'Shu' (the Masquerade) on the rest of the Kindred. The Confucian Covenant (feudal rule + serving as active "ancestors" with filial relationships with living family and necromantically with the passed) is fighting back HARD. Meanwhile a Taoist Covenant explores the mysteries of Blood Alchemy. If the chapter is expanded, it incorporates "Immortals" updates for 2nd Edition.

                                            Wolves of Bau (2nd Editon): Updated with the revelation of how (relatively) recent the Sundering was, this setting is represented as being before/during the Oaths of the Moon. The rulership of Bau is a failed attempt for the Uratha to carve out a place for themselves in the world before turning instead to readopting Father Wolf's cause. The Oaths of the Moon are presented as a Rite that exists in the setting, readopting the lost auspices at the cost of losing the special advantages of the Bau setting.

                                            Underground Railroad Changeling: The thematic link is obvious, and the (dangerous) use of Trods to facilitate the escape of slaves opens tons of plot potential. You also get the veneer of of "Antebellum romance" with appropriate focus on the real darkness of the era. With expansion, I'd incorporate it Brazil, Dahomey & Abeokuta, linked by Trods along lines of the Wyrd to the US's underground as well.

                                            Haundenosaunee Hunter: Set during the birth of the Haudenosaunee (Iroquois Confederacy), the Conspiracy the "Sons of Sosondowa" are brought together by Hiwatha and the Great Peacemaker themselves to carry out the Great Law against cannibal witches and their cults. Probably lean to the earlier date interpretation, tying it to the 1142 solar eclipse.

                                            Dante's Italy Geist: Late medieval italy as the Holy Roman Empire and the Papacy wrestle for power. Ideally this setting will tie in Inferno updates for 2nd Edition, with a whole metaphysical plotline about the Underworld accessible from Italy's Autochthenous Depths are being corrupted by demons, making Sin-Eaters all the more important to save the dead.

                                            Fifth Turn Mummy: Update "Infinite Macabre" to 2nd Edition. The search for Alaru in the stars themselves.

                                            Near Future Demon: Update "Bleeding Edge" to 2nd Edition. The hook for this setting is that the God Machine is maintaining multiple "futures" (well... presents) simultaneously, and the Unchained have the ability to move between them. So you have the "total global surveillance state integrated directly into the infrastructure of the Machine itself" future - you have the "rival megacorps vie for power comodifying Pacts and Embeds" future - you have the "environmental catastrophe" future - etc, etc.

                                            "Terrible" Russia Beast: Ivan the Terrible serving the role of Apex, links the birth of this diverse empire through fear pretty well to Beast, with plenty of room for other supernatural weirdness for Beasts to be Kin to.
