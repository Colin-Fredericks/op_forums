                                    Hmm, fear is not really an emotion much familiar to me, except for the rational, pragmatic kind, so I have difficulty to relate to this subject, but I can try. I think I'd be mostly scared of those supernaturals that harm or kill people seemingly at random for no apparent good reason, like slashers, or are mindless or with no self control, like the draugr, or try to ruin the world, such as the Scelesti or the Seers. Otherwise, several supernatural splats fascinate, not scare, me because of their power, potential for immortality, and awareness about the true nature of the universe, more or less the same way people are often fascinated by big cats, wolves, or bears, despite the potential danger. Others splats I find pathetic, such as prometheans and mummies. I'm the kind of supernatural wanna-be who would walk up to mages, beasts, vampires, or changelings, and ask how to become one. Even werewolves or geists seem a better deal than being a puny mortal.
