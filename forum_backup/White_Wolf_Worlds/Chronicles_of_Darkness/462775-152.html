                Oh, yeah, I'm totally behind the idea. I just don't think it's explained that well. The Predator's Jaws sidebar is phrased in a way that makes it seem dependent on vampires having a generic weakness against the supernatural, which they don't.

                A few clarifications, though:
                First off, it's only their bites that have this quality. Other natural weapons such as claws does not.
                Secondly, Uratha bites counts as Banes for all spirits (and other ephemeral entities) regardless of Rank.
