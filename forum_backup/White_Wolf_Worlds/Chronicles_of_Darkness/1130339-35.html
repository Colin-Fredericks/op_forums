                            Originally posted by Konradleijon View Post
                            So what Is the Contagion ?

                            We know a symptom, from dev commentary, is a shuffling of weaknesses and strengths; an infected vampire may lose their flammability but become allergic to water. We've also heard that it's an expansion on the intersital (I have no idea how to spell that) terrain (the alternate, eldritch locations that overlay the mortal world) presented in the Horror Recognition Guide and Shadows of the UK (there's also an angel in Night Horrors: Enemy Action who regards eradicating "creatures of raw entropy" as part of his mission to secure Infrastructure, which everyone thinks refers to Contagion-born monsters). Theory is that it appears to be a normal disease unless you are aware of the supernatural world, then you notice its true effects are an infection of reality itself.

                            Me, I'm angling for "we don't know, but this eldritch bacteria/fungus seems to be a symbiote or vector and people infected seem to always get this too", because I like dealing with microbiota and medical horror.
