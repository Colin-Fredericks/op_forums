                                I imagine that for mages, description of their history in mythic terms, of a lineage directly descended from traditions originating in the Time Before, is a thing widely employed for ritual purposes, as the introduction for new members and to illustrate points about their ethos and endeavours by analogy, at the same time as there being a long history of scholarship concerning their literal background that is readily accessible even to the people who aren't dedicated to studying and developing it.

                                Like, even a Medieval thearch might talk about being descended from the Voice of the Dragon and describe motives and actions of Atlantean refugees when presiding over caucus or instructing a newcomer, but has ready access to preserved accounts of mages centuries before about the actual founding of their Order, the development of their philosophy from the ideas that preceded it.

                                    The Shadow is, in a very real sense, the emotional-historical record of the world, and not only is it constantly eating itself, but individual sets of werewolves are constantly tinkering with it to make it serve their purposes (or trying to).

                                        In mage terms, the three main crossings of people to the Americas were -

                                        1) Long before the Sundering, as humans crossed into Alaska and migrated south.

                                        2) During the period the Tremere were a Diamond Order and pushing the Diamond into attacking Nameless Orders they wanted to eat, the largest of which was the Norse one. While the Norse were colonising what's now British Columbia. So Vinland would have been Enemy Territory at worst, and Just More Non-Diamond mages at best.

                                        3) About 80 years of exploring America and trying to meet local mages peacefully despite the Sleeper explorers being anything but, until the Seers of the Throne are created back in Europe and everything goes Wrong Forever. The Diamond probably tried to recruit local Legacies and cults into the existing Orders the same way they did across Asia and Africa for centuries before, with the same mixed success - we know from Boston Unveiled that Salem is the first Consilum in the New World but almost immediately falls foul of a Mystery the native mages could have told them about, and from Dark Eras that the Caribbean Orders have to go as far as New York to find an authority and even the 19th century USA is majority Nameless.
