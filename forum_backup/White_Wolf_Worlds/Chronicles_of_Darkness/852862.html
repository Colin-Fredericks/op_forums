    Pack Merits - crowd sourcing

    03-25-2016, 04:45 AM
    You may have noticed in Stew's recent developer's blog about The Pack that he mentioned 'Some merits that apply to the pack as a whole'.

    Well, finally having some info released about The Pack means that I'm in the mood to write Werewolf again. (Who are we kidding? I'm always in the mood to write werewolves!)

    I know what's in the book (I wrote those parts), and I know the extras I've already written to be released afterwards as either an officially stamped web enhancement, or as unofficial forum content. Either way, more stuff for you.

    What I can't yet do is tell you what is included. What I can do is ask what kinds of things you folks think should be pack merits? Give me your ideas - I'll take the ones that fit the systems in The Pack and aren't already done and write them up alongside the other bonus merits.

    Unfortunately you won't find out which I've done (or how they look) until after The Pack is released, but that shouldn't be too much longer. (He said, hopefully.)

    So whaddya say, forumites? Anyone have any merits they think a pack (as a whole) needs?

              I suppose I see most of pack stuff as also territory stuff. So maybe some sort of 'False Trails,' merit that works as a sort of pseudo security, a lesser, mundane form of Maze Ward designed for anyone trying to track down members of the pack, it would increase the time required by a certain amount depending upon the Merit dots. I figure it would either be tied into or there would be a separate Merit that had a chance to warn you if someone was searching or asking around for packmates within the territory. That said I understand there are, for the first at least, gifts that can emulate this, but this would be an entirely mundane thing wolf-blooded and others could help with if one didn't have the necessary facets.

              Perhaps a sort of miniature spirit court merit, something that represents a kind of spirit you have curried favors with and are more likely to lean on your side or look out for you or send you warnings, but only with the minor ranked spirits. I guess that could fit under Contacts or Allies, though.

                          Territory features?
                          I was trying to think of something like that too. But outside of Dedicated Locus, what would there be that's worth basing a pack Merit around?

                          In one of my first games we had a meat locker. And I keep thinking of a great pit in a place that used to be a silver mine. One of the issues that came up with my first werewolf was that, 'The People Do Not Kill The People,' is hard to have a middle ground with. If you attack someone, you risk breaking it. Beating up a zealot isn't likely to change their minds, even the Fire-Touched have more ways to convert people than simply pummeling them. My character thought very highly of that part of the Oath, which is where the meat locker came in. It was someplace to stash an enemy you couldn't kill, letting them out when the rest of the situation was dealt with. Of course now with the variety of abilities werewolves have that let them get out of places like that you would need some extra oomph. Locating it on a Barren might help. Some sort of 'Barren Box,' perhaps acting like an inverted Safe Place?
