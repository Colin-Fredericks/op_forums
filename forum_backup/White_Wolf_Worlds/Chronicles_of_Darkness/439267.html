    Hurt Locker Notes

    03-12-2015, 11:05 PM
    Come. Talk Hurt Locker.

    Drafts are right here.

    This is where I want to keep the discussion. Keep those rules in mind. In fact, reprinting them here for posterity:
    • First off, notes need to be in one place. I've set up a forum thread for that. I'll pay attention to that thread.
    • Don't bother commenting on grammar, spelling, and punctuation. These are super raw drafts.
    • Please don't reiterate points. I'm not looking for consensus or "votes". If someone said, "this issue exists", don't second it. I don't want to bog down the conversation. If you have a new point or thought, cool. But don't dogpile the conversation, please.
    • Some points don't need context. "This has seven dots, Merits don't go to seven dots" doesn't need context. But, "This is unbalanced" is not really a point that helps anyone. That's subjective. Now, if you played with it, and it created a specific experience at your table, I want to know.
    • Speaking in experiences is good. If you played with something, I want to hear the experiences it created at your table.
    • Please note the positives. This is partly because I want my team exposed to positive notes as well as negative, but also because I want to see what works for you and creates positive experiences. I'm in the business of creating positive experiences.
    • Don't be insulting. Don't insinuate motives from my team. Just address the material; don't try to bring in assumptions and political issues.
    • If you make a "this doesn't work" style note, couple it with what you think would work. That'll help us frame your commentary.
    • Understand that there's some overlap here. A few people did different versions of the same Style, for example. This was by design. I'm not going to "take one but not the other". But I might strip apart duplicates for alternative maneuvers or similar. I wanted more raw ideas to work with.
    • I will be reorganising and renaming these. Many are a little more specific than I intend. I want to make them more generally useful. If you have thoughts, awesome.
    • At a certain point, I'm going to call an end to notes. At that point, I have to send over my notes to my team, and I can't go back and integrate more ideas.

          Awesome stuff! Here's my thoughts so far on the normal merits:
          Cohesive unit is a little confusing; does it 'refresh' whenever you form a team for a new purpose, or give a new purpose to an existing one? Or do you need to basically lose the merit afterward and buy it again? That said, I really like the idea of having more leader-oriented merits like this.

          The one dot support merit likely shouldn't work on Persistent condition, I'd think.

          At any cost similarly looks like it needs to be made clear how often you can use it. I imagine that's per scene?

          Defender is pretty awesome, and I like how it works both as a defensive, do-gooder type buff and as a fuel for vengeance.

          Know the territory is another really good 'leader' ability, and also seems to be a very good way for a mental oriented character to provide a mundane bonus to combat. I really like that.

          I like the thematics behind Power Object. Reminds me of goetia even if it doesn't force you to indulge on a failure.

          Body as Weapon being a normal merit to do a couple of things that used to be pure FS fare is pretty nice.
          Same with Dirty Fighting, though it seems like it could be a dot lower in cost, to me, specifically because it's not a Style.

          I love the idea of the drone style; I assume there's going to be rules for things like drones elsewhere in the book?

          Fair fighter is a nice addition for giving social characters a way to help in combat too (other than calling in allies or retainers).

          Punch Drunk seems... I dunno, I like the idea of it, but it seems a bit too potent, offhand. I imagine playtesting will help find out for sure though.


          I'll glance at the fighting styles a bit later.

                      A lot of interesting stuff, very enjoyable. I didn't read everything in exacting detail, but below are my comments on what I've read so far.

                      The Dominate Center Line merit: what does "a similarly armed opponent" mean exactly? My assumption is that the defense bonus would apply against any one attacker using a melee weapon or brawl attack, what melee attack would be different enough for it to not apply?

                      Grappling Fighting Style: Dynamic Guard merit:
                      "While prone, your character uses his body to shake and redirect his enemy. Reduce the opponent’s dice pool to grapple by your character’s Dexterity. Unfortunately, your character can’t get up from prone while using this maneuver."
                      Does this mean reduce your opponents dice pool to establish a grapple with you, or reduce your opponent's dice pool in the opposed grapple test once a grapple is established (what my group loving refers to as Wrestlemania)?

                      Grappling Fighting Style: Lock Flow merit: I think this merit is awesome, I love the idea of getting a bonus to grapple by declaring your intended maneuver.

                      Kino Mutai Fighting Style: Trained Bite merit: I would suggest stating whether and how this applies to Vampires or other human shaped creatures with lethal bite damage.

                      Staff Fighting Style: Thwack Weapon merit: It strikes me that this attack (which, granted, can only disarm) ignores your opponent's defense in favor of forcing a contested roll. I haven't had a chance to try it yet, but I think this might be frustrating for a character who has invested in a strong defense.

                      Weapon and Shield Fighting Style: I expected to see a merit that benefits shieldless allies through the use of your shield. That just seems, to me anyway, like a natural area for shield wielding characters to excel. Not a problem, just something I noticed.

                      Armed Restraint merit: This merit is awesome and makes me laugh at the possibilities. I assume you would use Weaponry for the grapple attempt using this merit, but that should probably be specified.

                      Ground and Pound merit: It's not totally clear to me how this merit works when used in a grapple. Do I have to declare I'm using this maneuver before I attempt the opposed grapple roll? If so does it give me the rote quality on the opposed grapple roll?

                                          Dirty Fighter is pretty cool, but I don't see the point of the Drawback. Just because someone fights dirty doesn't mean they will betray friends and so on, does it? The drawback implies that it does. All in all, I think the drawback is illogical, and not worth the benefits.

Or... do you get the penalty AND have to make individual rolls against everyone within reach? If so, it is very unclear on how it works.

                                          Other than this? Looks real good!

For free, one can spend a willpower point to get plus three dice to the roll, which averages to one success, and doesn't suffer a dexterity penalty for a day after, and three precious merit points to make the damage lethal? Wouldn't a roll of quarters do the same thing, for essentially free, no penalty, and no willpower expenditure? A that can be replaced, no not replaced, shown up, by 10 dollars worth of change is kind of a loser.

                                                  Don't get me wrong, love the concept, but the execution needs to be more awesome.

                                                  Dirty Fighter, nice, but the penalty seems a little out of joint with the merit. It's not like, logically, because you hit below the belt people will think you'll just attack them out of the blue. Perhaps a penalty when applying soft leverage (you aren't trustworthy after all), or something else more specific? And if you do keep it as is, is this cumulative with the penalty from face of a thug, err, sorry, air of menace (by the by, I'm totally going to call air of menace face of a thug whatever its name is).

                                                  Fair fighter and dirty fighter probably should have a prerequisite- don't possess the other. Perhaps you thought this was obvious, but it might be worth a little word count nonetheless.

                                                  Punch drunk- interesting, but what about supernaturals? Do werewolves heal twice as slowly here? Do mages or vampires effectively suffer no drawback whatsoever?

                                                  War Cry- What's the point supposed to be? Other than it being used defensively, and well when I think going berserker I don't think of defensive fighting. Maybe if with an exceptional success it could cause targets to run away?

                                                  Manic brutality drawback- wait the berserker takes damage equal to the armor and the weapon takes damage, how does that even work?
