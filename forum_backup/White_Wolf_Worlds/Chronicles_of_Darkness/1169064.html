    The Perfected Stone (2e Legacy)

    12-20-2017, 09:11 AM
    Hey guys. I wanted to make an obscure Legacy for my Moroi Libertine (she's an sculptor obsessed with creating the perfect work). None of the Matter based quite fit (Uncrowned Kings was close but not quite, Tamers of Stone and Forge Masters were a distant second). So I started making a Matter focused Moroi legacy, but couldn't make Death work as a secondary arcana.

    Anyway, so I decided to change it to Space (meaning Mastigoi, and have her join through Order or Praxis) with the idea of a focus on dedication to craft and isolating oneself from distractions. Dedication in the form of becoming 'close' and attuned to your work. The point was also to take a different route as far as the Legacy, I noticed most are full-fledged organizations or cults with influence and reach, so I wanted to make one that was incredibly obscure, weird, and incredibly tiny. This is what I've come up with. Feedback is always appreciated. Also, full disclosure, I used 21C Hermit's and Flinty's homebrew stuff and may have borrowed an oblation or two. Thanks for all your work guys.

    Questions:
    I'm still blanking on a fourth secondary attainment. Is that even strictly necessary? My best idea is Perfected Sympathy on one's own work, Reach on Duration and Rote.
    Does anyone think the 5th Space utility effect is too powerful?
    Do you think I can justify making Space the primary arcana (admittedly just so I could get it as ruling). I also feel like it doesn't quite fit as Mastigoi.
    Does the secondary arcana of a Legacy have to primary for the path?
    Just trying not to abuse my ST's leniency.

    The Perfected Stone


    Parentage: Mastigos, Free Council

    Oblations: Meditating in solitude and in darkness, extended and repetitive behaviour, self harm in pursuit of one’s Aspirations or Obsessions, any night spent without sleep.

    Yantras: A tool of one’s own making (+1), spellcasting to aid one’s own craft (+2), casting a spell while seriously injured (+2), contemplating in one's own imperfection (+1, +2 if the reflection leads to depressive thoughts)

    Prerequisites: Matter 2, Crafts 2, and one of the following at 2 or higher: Academics, Expression, Medicine, Science, or Survival

    History: The Perfected Stone is a deeply obscure Legacy created by Lena Thwaite, an Alchemist who became obsessed with perfecting an immaculate river stone she found in the Canadian wilderness. For over a week, Lena refused to eat, sleep or leave her work for any reason. When she emerged from her sanctum, gaunt and scarred, but satisfied with her perfected stone - she had in her hands the first truly flawless object in the world - and a Legacy was born.

    Theory: Perfect work demands perfection from all sources, therefore a perfection must be born of perfected tools, perfected materials, and perfect focus, but above all else, utter dedication is key.

    Attainments


    Infallible Insight •
    Prereq: Initiation.
    To perfect one’s craft, one must intuitively understand her materials. This functions as the Matter 1 spell Discern Composition with Reach applied to Instant Cast.

    Add Space 1: The Perfectionists have a knack for finding objects worthy of their attention, and will leave no stone unturned in their search for perfection. This functions as the Space 1 spell Locate Object with Reach applied to Instant Cast. If the subject of this attainment is an object the Perfectionist has created herself, she becomes aware of its location no matter its distance, unless the object is in another realm. Curiously, this attainment does apply to any children the Perfectionist carries to term.

    Inviolate Endeavour ••
    Prereq: Matter 2, two dots in a third skill from the initiation skill list
    The Perfectionist must cast away contaminants and impurities with prejudice. This refers to people as well as materials. This functions as the Matter 1 spell Lodestone with reach applied to Advanced Duration and Instant Casting.

    Add Space 2: This functions as the Space 1 spell Isolation, Inverted (See below) with reach applied to Advanced Duration and Instant Casting. A Perfectionist does not suffer from long term effects of this isolation.

    Intricate Measures •••
    Prereq: Matter 3: Crafts 3
    The Perfectionist brooks no interference. None may see her work until it’s completed. This functions as the Matter 2 spell Invisible Hoard, Reach applied to Duration and Instant Cast.

    Optional Space 3: This functions as the Space 2 spell Secret Door with Reach applied as above.

    Immaculate Means ••••
    Prereq: Matter 4, three dots in one of the previous initiation skills.
    The Perfectionist has no need for tools, she realizes her vision through will alone. This functions as the Matter 2 spell Shaping with Reach applied to to Duration, Instant Cast, and to working solid materials.

    Optional Space 4: ?

    Unwavering Dedication •••••
    Prereq: Matter 5, Crafts 4
    Dedication and diligence eventually yield perfection. This spell functions as the Matter 3 spell Hone the Perfected Form with Reach applied to working non-metals, and making the craft Lasting. Bonus spell factors are applied to scale.

    Add Space 5: The Perfectionist’s work is always with her. She can cast spells upon any item created with the above attainment as if it she were touching it, and never needs a yantra, or to spend Reach for Sympathetic Range.

    Spells

    Isolation, Inverted (Space•)

    Practice: Compelling

    Primary Factor: Potency

    Withstand: Composure

    Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Intimidation, Subterfuge

    Boundaries and barriers are a lie, but it is sometimes useful to lie. This spell subtly warps space and distance around the subject, making them seem impossibly distant and more foreboding.

    Any attempt to interact or influence the subject costs 1 Willpower. Even then, any dice pools are penalized by the spell’s Potency. Prolonged exposure to this spell (roughly a day per dot of the subject’s Composure) may provoke breaking points or adverse Conditions like Shaken or Spooked.

          It really sounds more like a Mastigos Legacy which managed to break through the Matter Inferiority element to me, but that's just my two cents.
          The focus on perfection and refinement and sheer dedication sounds very much like something that would be related to the Path of Scouring.
          Which would mean you could more easily incorporate the Space optional Arcanum.

          Personally I'd give the Space 4 Optional Arcanum the ability to craft objects of up to Size 5 with an instant action, as you combine the fluidity of the item with your ability to teleport parts of it an rearrange its structure at will to instantly move every molecule where it needs to go. Reach into Instant Casting, cast at a standard "base" size of up to 5.

          Unfortunately it really doesn't sound like a Space Primary Arcanum, those are usually about connections, meaning, distance, and so forth.
          It's feasible if you want to recast it as a "what do these objects mean to people" Legacy about the connections between people and 'things'.
          Also in terms of conjunctive Arcana for a Legacy, it's strongly suggested, but not mandatory for the conjunctive Arcanum for a Legacy to be one of the Ruling Arcana from the parent Path.
          Thing is though, make your own unorthodox attainments, and you can make the conjunctive whatever you like.

              Thanks for the answers both of you.

              My idea behind choosing Space as secondary was partly one of convenience (my character has Space), but also I was trying to get a bead on this idea I had of dedication to one's work translated as becoming close to one's work, narrowing one's world to just oneself and the project. The scope of this Legacy is very small, intentionally. So Space as in, removing the distance between you and your work, becoming one with your work, and shutting everything else out. I tried to mean that both literally and like, metaphysically, I guess? I was having trouble realizing that in the mechanics, and I didn't want to lean too hard on just isolation spells (Secret Door, Ban, Secret Room, Pocket Dimension, etc.) so maybe that all got lost in the shuffle.

              I definitely was trying to avoid Mind, though, or becoming too similar to the Uncrowned Kings. I could see the argument for it, but I feel like Space really hammers home the weird, obsessiveness that I had in mind for the Legacy. Very open to suggestions though and I shown otherwise, certainly.

              And, I think you're right, BestBert, Mastigos might be the way to go. I got hung up on the Matter thing, but I guess it doesn't.... matter. I like the idea of messing with Scale on the 4th attainment. Hmm. I will fool around with some stuff and see what I come up with.
