                    And now for a "heretical" Channel

                    Reflections Upon the Deep (Ancestral Sanctity)
                    The thoughts of one of Tiamat's Brood are not restricted to inside of their mere skulls. Their daydreams drift freely, swaying the perceptions of lesser minds easily.

                    Depth 1: Birthright of Sanctity
                    Depth 2: The Leviathan can weave a simple psychic impression upon the mind of another - a trick of the light, an unidentifiable sound, a smell they can't quite place, etc. The Leviathan can change the stimulus as desired from turn to turn. The affects one target within view and lasts only as long as the Leviathan concentrates. The Leviathan may choose to spend 1 Willpower point at any time while concentrating - this persists the psychic illusion for the rest of the Scene without needing concentration.
                    Depth 3: The Leviathan can now project multiple figments at once - either separate instances, or different senses - equal to their Intelligence.
                    Depth 4: Illusions become more detailed, resembling real things. Targets who do suspect their false nature must succeed at a contested Wits + Composure vs the Leviathan's Manipulation + Subterfuge to pierce the deception.
                    Depth 5: The Leviathan can also remove things from sensory perception as well as add them. They could replace the sight of a door with empty wall, or a lurking Cultist with a rustle of leaves.
                    Depth 6: The daydreams can now replace entire landscapes, shaping impressions of a Scene's location to what the Leviathan desires. Particularly detailed changes might require an Extended Action (target number set by the Storyteller) to fully envision.
                    Depth 7: The Leviathan's thoughts become manifest throughout its Wake. Given time and imagination, it could conceal it's entire cult compound, create a maddening labyrinth, or surround a town in a tableau of horrors.
                    Sidebar: The Art of Deception
                    Illusions are just tricks of the senses, right? You might think something is crawling up your leg, but it can't actually hurt you, surely. Yes, the illusion itself cannot - but if you walk into a pit expecting solid ground, that can hurt you. Phantom sounds can distract (and bestow the Distracted Condition), and a panorama of frightful things can drive one mad - especially if the Leviathan takes care that only you seem to see it.

                                That Hideous Strength (Descendant Might) OR Overpowering Strength of Titans (Descendant Might)
                                Empowered by divine Ichor, the Tribe can shred mere artifice and land frightfully potent blows.

                                Depth 1: Birthright of Might
                                Depth 2: By Reflexively spending 1 Ichor, the Leviathan can ignore up to their Sheol in Durability from objects for their turn.
                                Depth 3: You no longer need to spend Ichor to use the Depth 2 ability against objects with a Size less than half your own (round down).
                                Depth 4: By Reflexively spending 2 Ichor, prior to making a melee attack, the Leviathan can smite a foe off their feet and out of their senses. If the final damage exceeds the target's Stamina, they take the Knocked Down and Stunned Tilts.
                                Depth 5: Each use of the Depth 2 ability against an object reduces its Durability by 1, as it cracks and weakens. When using the Depth 4 ability, rather than inflicting Knocked Down and Stunned, the Leviathan can lower the target's effective Stamina against physical Tilts being applied by 1 for the rest of the Scene.
                                Depth 6: When moving, the Leviathan can make a Reflexive Brawl attack against each object or foe that impedes their movement.
                                Depth 7: A Leviathan, by charging its muscles with divine Ichor, can strike with the force of artillary. By Spending 4 Ichor, the Leviathan can make a brawl attack that is treated as an Explosive, with the Leviathan's current Damage rating, a Blast Area of Size/2, and a Force of 0. Even if the attack misses, the target is still in the primary blast area. The Leviathan never suffers damage from their own explosion, but any allies caught in it are not so lucky.

                                Adaptations:
                                Shockwave (••••): When using the Depth 7 ability, add the Leviathan's unmodified Strength (before adjustments for Depth and Atavisms) to Force.

                                            Proposed Birthrights:

                                            Making is so that each grant an Active (for 1 Ichor) and Passive effect, scaling according to the number of Channels inside that Vestige known. Active is 1/2 round up (so every odd Channel) and Passive is 1/2 round down (so every even Channel).

                                            Awareness:
                                            Active - 1-3 bonus Elements found when searching for Clues.
                                            Passive - Reduce sensory penalties by 0-3.
                                            Elements:
                                            Active - Create a fog cloud Size*2 in diameter that inflicts a visibility penalty of 1-3.
                                            Passive - Reduce the rating of Extreme Environments by 0-3 so long as there is moisture.
                                            Fecundity:
                                            Active - Create the Undergrowth Tilt with a Durability of 1-3.
                                            Passive - Increase healing rate by x1/x2/x3/x4 (round up).
                                            Might:
                                            Active - Gain a +1-3 bonus to Athletics for Stamina in Turns.
                                            Passive - Increase Strength for throwing capacity by 0/100/200/300%.
                                            Predation:
                                            Active - Gain a +1-3 bonus for calculating Chase threshold or on rolls to gain the Edge.
                                            Passive - Track via Scent up to 0/500/1000/1500 yards.
                                            Sanctity:
                                            Active - Reduce a target's effective Composure by 1-3 for the Scene.
                                            Passive - Social Maneuvering against the Leviathan has +0-3 Doors.
                                            Vitality:
                                            Active - Reflexive; Downgrade 1-3 damage from an attack.
                                            Passive - Gain 0-3 Health.

                                            Feel free to comment, suggest replacements, etc.

                                                Not sure if the right place for this, or even if King Raven is still working on it (or will notice) but here are my Birthrights styled by the two current ones.

                                                The Unseen Giant (Birthright of Might)
                                                The power of Leviathan is an often overt and obvious thing, bulging muscles and titanic claws, but the might of semi-divine is not without subtlety. Even the flimsiest of Earth’s Most Terrible Tribe possess a might that cannot be seen, but is no less real. By spending a point of Ichor the Leviathan may increase his Strength by +1 until the end of the Scene, this may increase the Attribute beyond it normal limits.

                                                If the Leviathan has mastered three Channels of Might then the Strength bonus increases to +2, at five mastered Channels of Might then the Strength bonus increases to +3.

                                                The Blind and All-Seeing King (Birthplace of Awareness)
                                                You can run from the King, but you cannot escape his gaze, and for the Leviathan this is a truth beyond doubt. By spending a point of Ichor until the end of the Scene the Leviathan suffers no penalties to Perception from ANY source up to the range of its Wake (except for Total Cover, you don't get to see through objects), even if the Leviathan is Totally Blind (from birth or injury) they can temporarily see perfectly up to the edge of its Wake. If this would run counter to a Supernatural ability, roll Clash of Wills as normal for that effect.

                                                While this Birthright is activated you do not suffer penalties to Combat or Defense rolls due to poor visibility (such as Darkness) or distance (such as Long Range).

                                                Abyssal Minds, Eternal Thoughts (Birthright of Fecundity)
                                                It’s often said that Leviathans are mere beasts that are bereft of Inspiration and Intelligent, this could not be farther from the truth. It is ultimately from the ‘abyss’ of the human mind that all ideas spring from, and for those who are blessed with great Fecundity it is a very deep abyss indeed. By spending a point of Ichor the Leviathan may increase his Intelligence or Wits by 1 until the end of the Scene, this may increase those Attributes beyond it normal limits.

                                                If the Leviathan has mastered three Channels of Fecundity then the bonus increases to +2, at five mastered Channels of Fecundity then the bonus increases to +3. Alternatively you may split the Bonus between your Intelligence and Wits.

                                                The Yellow King’s Command (Birthright of Sanctity)
                                                A Leviathan’s divine nature means that authority is a natural part of their existence, and one that is inherited in their very blood. A God commands, the Mortal follows...or least that’s what the God expects to happen. By spending a point of Ichor the Leviathan may roll Presence + Expression vs Resolve + Potency on a Wake Vulnerable individual. On a success the Leviathan can give that individual a One-Word command that it must follow to the best of it’s abilities, if this would require an Extended Action the effects of this command last until the end of the Scene.

                                                Commanding someone to “Die” only causes them to play dead... before or after a theatrical ‘death scene’.

                                                Domain of Azathoth (Birthright of Elements)
                                                The Primordial World was one of crashing waves against rampant storms, and pillars of flame that warred with thundering slabs of stone. In these early days of chaotic maelstroms the ancient Progenitor ruled over the very fabric of the world with impunity, but in the modern days the world is almost still in comparison. Despite this more orderly world the descendants of those ancient being still hold some meager command over the basic building blocks of creation. By spending a point of Ichor a Leviathan can select one of the four Primary Element (Fire, Air, Earth, or Water) and create, alter, or manipulate up to 1 cubic-foot of that element within the range of their Wake.

                                                Most Leviathans use this Birthright for small quality-of-life benefits, though a few ingenious and malicious ones find some disturbing (and deadly) uses for it. If used as an ‘attack’ the Leviathan only rolls their Sheol vs the targets Defense and only deals 1 point of damage Bashing (Water and Air) or Lethal (Fire and Earth) damage that can be further reduced by armor, unless Bans and Banes apply (unless your nemesis is the Wicked Witch from the West you probably shouldn't waste the Ichor this way).
