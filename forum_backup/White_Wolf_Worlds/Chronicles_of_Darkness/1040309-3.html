        Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
        Here's a few pitches, I'll add more later...

        Qin Dynasty Vampire: Covenants that rose of the Hundred Schools of Thought. The Legalist Covenant (which in addition to bureaucratic governance of vampires is also the culture's "we are monsters who are supposed to keep humanity in line through fear" faction) is beginning to enforce 'Shu' (the Masquerade) on the rest of the Kindred. The Confucian Covenant (feudal rule + serving as active "ancestors" with filial relationships with living family and necromantically with the passed) is fighting back HARD. Meanwhile a Taoist Covenant explores the mysteries of Blood Alchemy. If the chapter is expanded, it incorporates "Immortals" updates for 2nd Edition.

        Wolves of Bau (2nd Editon): Updated with the revelation of how (relatively) recent the Sundering was, this setting is represented as being before/during the Oaths of the Moon. The rulership of Bau is a failed attempt for the Uratha to carve out a place for themselves in the world before turning instead to readopting Father Wolf's cause. The Oaths of the Moon are presented as a Rite that exists in the setting, readopting the lost auspices at the cost of losing the special advantages of the Bau setting.
        I'd absolutelly love both of those settings. Years ago, I began to write a Chinese setting for Requiem (never went beyond first drafts), and I also created covenants based on the different schols of thought of ancient chinese philosophy.

        My thoughts about Dark Eras 2:

        1) I'd love it to be so successful that Onyx Path finally decides to write some Dark Eras gamelines, although limited, as WoD Dark Ages, Sorcerers Crusade, Wild West... I loved Requiem for Rome, but it was too short. Or at least, although they were oneshots, that each gameline, in the curse of one or two years, ahd at least an historical setting book.



        2) As someone said before, I think there should be Eras that are important for each setting:

        Vampire: The main historical setting was The Camarilla, but that was done with Requiem for Rome. So, may be a Rome setting during the Stryx Rules, during Roman Monarchy, that would end with the Julius Senex revolt and the stablish the Camarilla. It would be a nive way to update RfR rules (benefits of the Wings and Julii weakness) without making just an update to 2nd Edition of RfR, but offering something new. Another possibility could be a settin centered in the barbarian kingdoms after the Fall of Rome, centered on the transition from Camarilla to the birth of the Invictus.

        Werewolf: A setting after the Fall of Urfarah but before/during the birth of the Tribes. Glamourweaver idea of the City of Bau is perfect (I liked it a lot but it was soooooo short), so I wont expand on it. Other possibilities could be the coming of the Idigam and the Brethens War from 1st Edition Corebook. But Id really offer my firstborn for a Sumerian werewolf setting.

        Mage: Tha Nameless War that ended woth the birth of the Free Council. Its the most important event from mage in last centuries, and it would offer an interesting twist as showing protolibertines and Diamond mages as enemies in an open war. Another possibility could be a Roman setting that showed the institutionalization of the Darshanas in full Orders.

        Hunter: I think there are three settings linked to important moments on Hunter history: Ancient Mesopotamia with the Nibiru and Ancient Rome with the Aves Minerva. Another setting could be the U.S. Independence war, with the Cheasnut Street Compact from Philadelphia, but may be it would too much similar to Dobting Souls.

        Geist: I think that some historical moments with a very hig death toll could be interesting: The Black Death (or the Justinian's Plague), the Conquest of Americas, First World War (and the Spanish Flu) or Second World War.

        Mummy: All the Sothic Circles have been done, so I'd suggest may be an Irem Dark Era, may be crossover with Mage around the Irem's war against the Kir-En-Gi dark mages of Kish.

        Promethean, Changeling, Demon and Beast: I don't remember any so important history moment about first two and I havent read the other two.

        Later, Il'l continue with other settings Id like, although they are not as decisive moments of the history of the splats.

        OTHERS (Spanish): Demon: The Redemption, Bloodlines: The Forgotten

                                        More Dark Era settings in Asia:

                                        Akbar the Great and the Mughal Empire
                                        1556. For half a century, a tenuous peace persists in India under one the Mughals' most visionary rulers. Akbar's court is filled with artists, scientists, and holy men. In contrast to the Western Diamond Precept, Mughal Awakened refine the Darshanas to their own accord. The Jnanashakti subtly guide the emperor as he fuses elements of Islam, Hinduism, Zoroastrianism, and Christianity into a syncretic and controversial new religion. In search of righteous warfare, the Vajrastra join Akbar's mansabdari ranks. With the exotic kingdoms of India awaiting conquest, they will soon have their fill.

                                        The Taiping Rebellion
                                        1850. The Middle Kingdom is thrown into civil war when an apocalyptic religious sect revolts against the Qing. As Hong Xiuquan - a man who claims to be the younger brother of Jesus Christ - rallies half a million followers to create a new heaven on earth, so too do the solitary Demons of Nanjing jealously guard their long-denied freedoms. Yet nothing lasts forever. Every day, the Taiping Kingdom's enemies grow in number and power. Will the Unchained stay, or flee once again?

                                        Japanese Conquest of Southeast Asia
                                        1941. Manila, Singapura, Batavia, Saigon, and Rangoon. Follow the secret networks of Kindred across Southeast Asia as their cities fall before the Empire of Japan. Will the Covenants survive the grand ambitions of the so-called 'Asian Co-Prosperity Sphere', or will vampire-kind burn along with their fiefdoms? As a bonus, this chapter will provide various new Bloodlines unique to the local cultures such as the Aswang, Langsuir, Pontianak, Riri Yaka and Jiangshi.

                                                    Originally posted by nofather View Post
                                                    Someone will probably point out that a writer has already said otherwise, but I imagine if we're ever going to get a kitchen sink setting, it would be in the Crossover Book, which would show examples of said crossover in said kitchen sink setting.
                                                    I don't recall hearing anything like that. But I could see it beng something they wouldn't want to tackle unless they Crossover Chronicle comes out first. I wouldn't mind such an era, though (Renaissance Italy would be my go-to for it).

                                                    Originally posted by Uxas View Post
                                                    Geist: I think that some historical moments with a very hig death toll could be interesting: The Black Death (or the Justinian's Plague), the Conquest of Americas, First World War (and the Spanish Flu) or Second World War.
                                                    I think we're already getting the Black Death in the Companion (for Geist and Vampire). Rather than the Conquest of the Americas, I'd rather see the Triple Alliance: the beginning of the Aztec empire rather than its end.

                                                    Promethean, Changeling, Demon and Beast: I don't remember any so important history moment about first two and I havent read the other two.
                                                    The eras I know of for Promethean feature giant parallels of the Wasteland and Disquiet. One parallelling the Pilgrimmage might be interesting: ie, one where there is a big systematic change overtaking society. The early Soviet Union perhaps (to emphasise a planned and deliberate transformation). Or the spread of Buddhism in Central Asia (to emphasise a transformation championing enlightenment).

                                                    Beast... it's a facet of the setting that Heroes were originally supposed to distill wisdom from their confrontations with Beasts and pass it on to the populace at large. But the mental landscape of humanity has become too noisy, and Heroes don't really do this any more. An era where Heroes still work as they're "supposed to" - where they're both enemies of Beasts, but also kind of their partners, would be interesting.
                                                    Or else, the time of the Brothers Grimm - they might be called fairy stories, but Beasts reflect the monsters in those tales more than changelings do.

                                                    Originally posted by reseru View Post
                                                    I feel like we all jumped on that during the polls, too, so part of me is wondering why the developers didn't find it interesting. I'm still glad they went with Cleopatra because that's pretty iconic, and it turned out to be an interesting chapter as well.
                                                    Probably because of how contentious the subject of non-Iremite mummies gets. People who don't care about those tend to advance twice-risen and/or lost memories as a solution for people who want them - whereas to people who do see the lack of them as a problem, those suggestions tend to come across as making it even worse - and there are people who dislike the idea of non-Iremite mummies enough that they'd reject any attempt to cater to the idea or include suggestions on representing them. Either way, I can see the writers preferring to go for an era that'd cause fewer arguments.
