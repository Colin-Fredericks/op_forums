        Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
        Bound Mania, but cost-less Hibernation mode
        This is my preference.

        I was also thinking that there should be a many-in-one wonder that reduces bound mania costs. If you have enough skill dots to traverse on the tech web.

        To take that concept from the top.

        Axioms are split into individual abilities called Infrances. So Apokolypsi 2 would be split into one Infrance for each type of scanner, etc. Each Infrance has a cost (lets call it Sparks (of Inspiration)) and an associated Mental Skill. A biological scanner would cost 1 Spark and require Medicine. A mad science scanner would cost 1 Spark and require Science. When creating a Wonder a Genius has one Spark per dot in the lowest of all associated skills.

        Because biological scanners are connected to mad science scanners on the tech web a Genius could create one wonder that scans both biology and mad science at the same time. Just so long as they have at least two dots in both Science and Medicine. It would only require one Bound Mania

        Wonders like this either do both at once (you point the scanner at something and gain both science and biological information) are target sensitive (your ice beam does damage and applies Immobilized if you point it at a person, it applies the Icy environmental tilt if you point it at the ground) or have a switch between modes (you biological scanner and healer has different buttons for scanning and healing).

        -------------

        All of those would only Bind the Mania of the most expensive Infrance they include. However all Wonders would have an additional flat cost of 2-3 bound Mania to make the default a few powerful signature wonders plus lots of kitbashing. (Kitbashing also allows you to use Infrances you haven't spent exp on. But you have to start on the web at one of your favoured Axioms or an Infrance you brought).

        And to provide exceptions to the above they'll be Merits like:
        • Crazy Prepared: Once per session you can transfer a Wonder from hibernation in your Laboratory to your pocket by spending it's Bound Mania cost (and binding the spent points). The player retroactively declares that their charachter predicted they'll need the Wonder and brought it with them. The Wonder must be size 3 or less, but a Crazy Prepared Genius can declare that their self-piloting airship has been hovering nearby or that their Beholden is watching the clock and ready to teleport a wonder to a predetermined location.
        • Signiture Wonder: The Genius has a single primary wonder, usually a vechicle, suit, or robot. Any Wonders Integral to their primary wonder reduce the Bound Mania requirement by X (where X is flat cost). However other wonders (except the prime wonder itself) increase their bound mania cost by Y (probably 1)
        • Posthuman: Like signature Wonder but for grafted or interlaized wonders in your own body.


        Larvae could also be more available too. With basic Larvae (e.g. graverobbing) only requiring that you take a Condition and only more powerful Larvae requiring serious Ethical breaches. Larvae could also provide bonus Sparks; with some powerful Wonders like doomsday cannons costing 6+ sparks all by themselves meaning that only the most powerful Geniuses can build them without questing for a McGuffin.

                In regards to character groups, of course the present Foundations and Baramins are good for default use for pick up and play. I wouldn't say they're exhaustive of all Mad Scientist archetypes but it would likely be better to have extra specialization via the Fellowships for those that want home brewing tools.

                -------------------------

                Bound Mania sounds better for general Wonder creation, as more stable Wonders that are Experience based could come from Fellowships (perhaps a Fellowship could become a shared project that stays consistent compared to other creations of Inspiration).

                I've been wanting to make Wonder creation more robust as well, in doing so how each Axiom can be differed by pairing with other skills. Primarily, we need more than the 5 mental skills used within the 1.1 guide for wonder creation. At minimum, all of the Mental skills plus applied Physical and Social skills. Axioms, in terms of what they do, are grouped by verb. They are more like the Practices that define Spells in Mage (per Arcana) or the Manifestations of Geist: the Sin-Eaters (paired with Keys).

                In these systems powers are created by pairing fundamental and thematic aspects into a supernatural ability. Axioms provide the foundation of what a Wonder or Inspired creation does. But does the thematic strata to pair with Axioms really need to be something new? When Skills are already provided to be plenty robust, as an Axiom and Skill are already used within the Wonder creation dice pool.

                Infrance and Sparks sound novel enough, but if the Infrances divvy up an Axiom by skill and the Sparks are derived from skills, why not cut out the middle man and just use the character's own Skill ratings? The Axiom is the only supernatural stat for Genius to define the scope of a Genius's capabilities, ideally scaled from minute every specific kinds of Wonders at low rating to broad and groundbreaking at high Axiom rating. A Genius could still attempt to make a greater Wonder beyond their current rating of the Axiom if they have a penalty (the remaining difference) and more Faults or other drawbacks.


                In my notes I've been thinking of every applied use of a Skill a Genius would use for their projects and occupations. Note that after creation a Wonder will likely be operated with a different skill pool.

                Mental
                Academics
                For history, research, social sciences (microspective) and psychology

                Crafts
                For mechanical construction and engineering, robotics, design and artwork

                Computer
                For programs, data analysis, hi-tech electronics, algorithms and AI

                Investigation
                Forensics, analysis, understanding wonders and tracking

                Medicine
                Health and disease, biological components, surgery, organic chemistry

                Occult
                Dead, paranormal and other supernatural involvement

                Politics
                Macrospective social applications, civil service, behavior on societal level

                Science
                Energy, subatomic particles, ‘normative’ and miscellaneous science


                Physical

                Firearms
                For artillery, projectiles, maintenance, scoping

                Weaponry
                For melee, maintenance, smithing

                Survival
                For landscaping, terraforming, ecosystems and natural architecture

                Social

                Animal Ken
                For training, (crypto) zoology

                Expression
                For artistry, artwork, music, and literature

                        Hmm, my first gut reaction is to say that having the Wonder creation system use a tech tree would be another step towards diminishing the Genius experience. The player wouldn’t think they are creating anything new, just unlocking options on a tech tree and thereby making Wonders more predictable, boring, and less creative.

                        I also don’t see why Axioms are no longer rated with dots, as regardless of tiers or effects your Axiom adds dice to the Wonder creation pool.

                        -------------------------------------------------------------------


                        That being said, when taking another look at the Infrance and tech tree system it sounds like a much more elegant way to present Variables, the first ‘subset’ properties for Wonders in the 1.1 guide. In the first guide you kind of just ‘knew’ Variables upon being a Genius, some with a few Axiom prerequisites, either split between an individual Axiom or general use. I always felt that was unrefined, although Variables were also seen as rigid to some from only connecting expected properties together.

                        I also like the idea of if you can’t meet the prerequisite normally, by not having the proper Axiom or whatever, you could cheat the effect from chained knowledge bringing you about to the Infrance/Variable by a number of linked types instead of the normal prerequisite rating.

                        Instead of there being a universial tech tree (as Mania and Inspiration are not presented as being that predictable) what if each Genius has their own personal ‘tech-tree’ that they develop over the course of play? It is not predetermined, like you said about chaining the properties in any order, as beyond the starting general or simple Axiom granted Infrances/Variables the Genius may link up each specialized application only if thematic, categorical, Wonder type or Axiom is shared.

                        A Genius who has dabbled with organic beings has a sudden change of passion, they add ‘Mechanical Scanner’ to branch from their previous ‘Organic Scanner’ that previously represented meat-tech quasi-living components that derived biometric information. Further dabbling with heavy mechanics bring the Genius along a new flourishing branch of their tech tree but they of course still have their starting organics branch to fall back on.


                        A personalized tech web would also benefit mechanics for Variable/Infrance creation and customization I wanted to introduce at a future time. The basics are that to add more of a researcher and experimenter feel to the game, the Genius is able to give their hypothesises validity in the form of new Variables and theorems if successful. To add to their tech web the Genius requires it to link with at least one other property, more would be better. Another Genius could then ‘reverse-engineer’ that custom Variable by finding a way it would stick into their tech tree, likely because of a very different linkup.

                        There could also be rules to suggest (if the web of a Geniuses Infrance/Variables is capped by Inspiration stat in some way) that the Genius could invalidate old applications for new ones if they lacked the space to add more desirable options. Its effectively a self contained Sanctity of Merits kind of scenario within this system, as no new costs are needed to trade Infrances for others. However, the Infrance’s position along the branch can cause problems if it forms the only bridge with a web of options the Genius still wishes to possess. Reflexively, the Genius will have to spend a point of Mania for each Variable they wish to redefine (read, connect it back to the tech tree). Broken off options are either lost for no refund or add increased, potent Faults when used or other drawbacks. When the Genius is able to find a way to bridge broken Infrances back into their web these drawbacks are removed.

                                This is the Wonder Creation roll outlined on pg. 143 in the Genius 1.1 document:
                                Inspiration + Attribute + Skill + Laboratory Equipment + Beholden Ability - Wonder Rank
                                Add an additional die if the wonder is of the genius' favored Axiom.




                                Let's break this down as to what is going on at each step.

                                Inspiration, Attribute and Skill is going to be the base dicepool.

                                Laboratory, Equipment, Beholden and Teamwork are occasional modifiers that will only contribute if you have beneficial merits reflecting these elements.

                                Wonder Rank is derived from the combined Axioms in the desired Wonder, so I was incorrect in saying that you add dice from your Axioms but the rating of Axiom is still important as it acts in penalty. Creation of a Wonder with Skafoi 1 and Metatropi 2 will be -3 altogether.

                                You get a bonus die from a favored Axiom being used, determined by Catalyst and Foundation.


                                Now here's where other factors NOT listed on this page come into play. From pg. 136 further penalties are mentioned but where not included in the formulae above.

                                If the Genius doesn't have a Resources rating matching or greater than Wonder Rank, the difference of higher Rank and Resources becomes another penalty. This brings up the 'Where your Resources comes from' discussion again. If it's considered that important I've felt a new system should be put into place to tie into this, call it Funding or the Budget a Genius has to have the materials, space and energy necessary for their projects. If you don't have the Budget for it, Creation also gets another penalty. It would also cover the long list of potential deprived, environmental and similar drawbacks mentioned on pg. 136.

                                Then the particulars for Kit-bashing get mentioned, but this plays into how long it takes to create a Wonder based on its Size. Which doesn't factor microscopic or intangible Wonders, nor things like literature or coding by word/page count. Default is said to be 1 day on the pg. 138 Time Taken chart, but this really means six hours per day, and everything goes screwy from there. Basically penalties start doubling as you reduce total creation time, or you get further +1 die for taking longer time to 'perfect' the Wonder.

                                There's far too many sticking points on what can't be done, in a game about Geniuses that can regularly screw with the laws of nature and science as powered by Inspiration which has few consistencies. For one thing, I don't believe the Attributes should be limited to two Mental (Intelligence or Wits) or 5 Mental skills to create a Wonder. It should be determined if Physical, Mental or Social attributes are more important to the kind of Wonder being made. And see my earlier thoughts on applied Skills, although you would still use the lesser of multiple Skills that would cover the Wonder in question.

                                You can also enhance creation with Mania and Willpower. Willpower can be used once, usual +3, and Mania adds 1 for 1.

                                ----------------------------

                                The idea of a personalized web is that for every group of Geniuses that do all do 'common' kinds of mad science tech, you have the other Geniuses that fly in the face of that with baffling inventions that don't follow any previous convention. It's implied throughout the book that other Geniuses can't make the projects, wonders and theorems of their peers work for themselves. (Unless they take the Universalist merit). Consistent, nigh 'perfected' Wonders that work for multiple Geniuses only come from the members being in the same Fellowship.

                                If the Genius is governed by their Aesthetic and their Paradigm (am I missing any traits?) whose to say they'll always draw from previous sets of Variables?

                                With an Infrance as a cluster of Variables, if I gather it correctly, and knowing that no great web/list will be exhaustive, how do you plan to categorize these Infrances? And how they bridge to each other. Keywords, categories, archetypes.

                                This may be as snarled of any nest to smooth out as any, since the freeform system still needs some easy to comprehend guidelines. The most elegant freeform system I've seen in awhile is the Verse powers introduced in Siren: the Drowning. Each Verse has Introduction, Opus and Conclusion. Intro sets the target and duration, Opus is the thematic and Conclusion is the effect. Conclusion and Axiom are the most alike, being tiers of effects. Presumably Infrances are the thematic packages?

                                    So I've recently had a thought for how to outline an Inspired Web/Tech Tree to bring some organization into the kinds of pseudoscience a Genius could be involved in. It brings additional definition to several terms suggested throughout this thread.

                                    Magnum Opus
                                    What: A modified Aspiration and Virtue
                                    • Steps towards completing the Magnum Opus grant Willpower and/or Mania
                                    • Allows for gain of Inspired Beats (Supernatural Experience)
                                    • Rigid, difficult or impossible to change until completed or setback
                                    • A Characters starting Paradigm(s) reflect their Magnum Opus
                                    • No Experience cost of its own but laborious to achieve
                                    The Magnum Opus, initially, forms the root of the budding tech tree. The main categories or branches of the tree are Paradigms (alternative: Facets, Infrances)

                                    Paradigm
                                    What: the theory, outlook or worldview the Genius labors under; an approach towards achieving the Magnum Opus
                                    • provides the Pesudoscience law behind their Wonders and experiments
                                    • amplified, codified or penalized by Unmada fields and Bardos
                                    • combines with Axioms when creating Wonders and such phenomena (the thematic)
                                    • requires systematic upkeep/'proof' or loss of application

                                    Paradigms are further fragmented into Postulates (Variables, Sparks)

                                    Postulate
                                    What: what the applied Paradigm does, the desired effect or output of a Wonder
                                    • Does at most one thing, highly specialized (like a merit or ability)
                                    • Scaled along Axiom dots as governed by Paradigm
                                    • Created and modified from an Experimental process
                                    • Wonders operate via Postulates, and the max Postulates in any Wonder is the Genius's Axiom dots + half Inspiration at time of creation

                                    Secondary to the Paradigm is the Aesthetic, first introduced in the original Genius document

                                    Aesthetic
                                    What: how the Wonders, experiments and efforts of a Genius are styled by materials, process and cosmetics
                                    • Secondary overlaping criteria with many Paradigms, Unmada fields, Bardos and similar
                                    • Easy for the Genius to collect and change out, fad based
                                    • Amount likely scaled with Inspiration

                                    Now, what is the tech web actually formed from?

                                    Rationale
                                    What: the bridging element along the Genii's tech tree from conceptual or abstract association.
                                    • Requires 1 bound Mania to form the link
                                    • The pairing must make sense for the linked concepts, even if it is adjectively false or untrue
                                    • Postulates branch off from Paradigms
                                    • If a Postulate is also valid for another Paradigm the Genius can link to that cluster
                                    • Alternatively, if there is an overlap of Aesthetic the Genius can temporary connect to obscure Postulates/Paradigms, adding them to the tree with bound Mania as normal
                                    • More links means more consistency (increased bonus or lessened Faults or some benefit)

                                    So in effect, the Genius develops a web of personalized technology and theorems that operate for themselves. This is merely the organization of such a system, mechanics and limits to be determined. Paradigms, the main aspect of the tech tree/web (Needs a better name) can be gotten different ways. The Genius could perform an experiment to validate a Paradigm or Postulate (modeled after Investigative Clues, to be posted in future), adapt or adopt a Paradigm from another Genius. A Collaborative could draw from each other's Paradigms for an increased cost/some sort of minor penalty. And Paradigms form the basis of Fellowships and Baramins, or could be gleaned from Bardo travel.

                                    How this would interact with Inspiration, Obligation, Havoc, Faults and other systems is to be determined. The future second edition of Genius would provide many examples of Paradigms, Postulates and Aesthetics but also show that these are not exhaustive examples, encouraging creativity from those that want to home-brew but providing enough ideas and suggested links for pick up and play.

                                                Batman has one of the most recognizable rogues galleries for a reason. As DC's flagship franchise both the Caped Crusader and his villains are drawn from pulp adventures, mystery and horror in different ways that one could use as inspiration for Genius. I'm of the opinion you can use other media to inspire your CoD game's for different venues. Both Hunter and Genius and other supernatural lines could draw different ideas from Batman.

                                                Batman himself is a gadget based hero, something the more active Foundations like the Navigators would become. Iron Man is another example of a tech heavy 'superhero.' From the villains you have those shaped by their science (Mr. Freeze, Poison Ivy) their brand of insanity (Joker) or a mix of both (Harley Quinn, being a former psychiatrist). The use of bizarre science in nearly all superhero media means you can easily play such characters in Genius.


                                                Not that I mean to bring up the Mage and Genius comparisons again but it serves as the easiest explanation for determining Genius characteristics. For Mage they scale each Arcana they know, or a purview of influences they have. The Thematic quality of their powers. At each dot of Arcana they can do more with it, with 13 Practices or 'Actions' they can do with Arcana at their given level. It's makes a very straightforward and predictable setup. If you can learn a character's ratings of Arcana you can predict what they are capable of. Geist has a similar system with Manifestations (actions) and Keys (thematic). For home-brew, the system for Siren: the Drowning's Verses does the action and thematic pairing very brilliantly, along with a changeable 'target' for the powers.

                                                Genius approaches the supernatural abilities differently. You scale the Actions from 1 to 5 dots. Each Axiom is a verb. It is a category of what Wonders can do, with each increasing rating getting more groundbreaking/gamebreaking. The trouble of this is that it's harder to determine what perquisites you need for a certain Wonder. The 1.1 version of Genius used common super science tropes and had write-ups/Variables in the axioms that suggested certain kind of Thematic parallels to each Axiom, although this was never concrete. Siren's Conclusions, the verbs, are very similar.

                                                I'm fine with sticking to Axioms as the only set of Supernatural traits. To me, it would be more of a disservice to Genius if there was an overarching 'thematic' system to pair with the Axioms. For one, it tells the player you can only player certain kinds of Mad Scientists, certain occupations and niches. Others aren't allowed. Second, as presented Inspiration and Mania are not consistent or predictable. This is the biggest contrast from Mage; in Mage the character becomes awakened to the Supernal Truths of the world, the existence of magic. The magic is still magic, with Mages all studying the same Truths.

                                                For Genius there is no 'Truth' behind everything. Even the Principle of Promethean has more consistency behind it. All theories are valid until disproven. Even when disproven by others a Genius can keep their Wonders ongoing from sheer belief and insanity. The inherent Un-Truth and Uncertainty of Genius is something to be embraced, these a qualities that help it stand out from other game lines.

                                                The obvious drawback, I realize, is that this type of freeform is hard to balance from a game mechanics perspective. For players without the DIY attitude persisting in Genius they will need some sort of reference to draw from when determining what their characters can do. To that end yes, have lists of various kinds of effects you can get from Wonders and example builds completed. There should be the emphasis that these are only examples with more room for customization, not the be-all-end-all.

                                                I've tried outlining the scope of each Axiom dot by dot but even that gets very circumspect with my first drafts. At basic, I suggest that Axiom 1 be very specific to Axiom 5's very broad application. And at Axiom 3 it can interact with other kinds of supernatural subjects and powers (like Numen or other venues).


                                                I also have a thought for other Mania gain, although that is saved for a future post.
