    Originally posted by Karavolos View Post
    I vote for "Floodings" being the name for "miniture-Deluges." The last question I have is how Nature can be sentient; is it akin to Gaia in Werewolf: The Apocalypse where the planet itself is alive to some extent? Is there some outward force that looks down and go "this is messed up" a lá God(-Machine)? Or is it one of those "you decide lel ;D" that White Wolf sometimes do with the mystery being left up to the Storyteller? Other than this question I feel everything I'm curious about has been answered.
    That's the main thing that I'm sorta blocked on. I'm writing with the implication that Nature is able to feel but attempts to restrain itself. It works for the most part, but every now and then its "emotions" bleed into the world. Kinda like a parent who keeps a facade in public but mumbles their true feelings when the doors are closed. That sort of negative emotion affects the children, who may then actually act on those feelings. That's sort of the logic behind the Furies, animals that have been imbued with Nature's resentment and desire to harm humanity. This includes unnaturally feral animals and "dire" beasts, like giant spiders that prey on humans that wander in the basements of abandoned houses. It's a twist on the idea that the Earth is a living organism. I would say that it is canon that, considering we have a running lineage of sorts with Ceto and Echidna, some Sirens believe Nature to be a being named Gaia who gave birth to Ceto, and by proxy the Sirens themselves and Echidna. This would be a specific sect of Sirens and isn't entirely true; it's just a belief that some hold.

    It would be very interesting to think what a Werewolf would think when they speak to a Siren about their cosmology. It also sounds like a headache that could end in a bloodbath.
