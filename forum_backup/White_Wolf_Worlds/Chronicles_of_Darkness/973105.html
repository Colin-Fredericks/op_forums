    Qarinah: The Rebellion

    09-20-2016, 09:51 AM
    I decided to make that Succubi/Incubi game, and I found a good gender nuetral name from Arab folklore, Qarinah.

    Qarinah: The Rebellian

    Back so far in time that the no Qarinah remembers it, except for the Qarinah Divinities, the origins of the Qarinah begin when a race of strange demons called The Chaste, when back in time to in their own beliefs, save the humanity for a deadly catasphore.

    The Chaste hoped to purify humanity from excessive sex desires, to stop an STD that would eventual mutate into a terrorible air borne killer.

    And so earily in human history The Chaste created the Qarinah from the "impurities" they had removed from themselves during the dark magic ritual that they'd used to create their own race.

    The Chaste hoped to use the Qarinah to test humanity with sexual temptations and to feast on the wicked and to hand over the most worthy to The Chaste, who would transform this pure person into one of The Chaste.

    But the Qarinah grew to love humanity and they resented being used as weapons and tools of punishment against humanity. The Chaste reminded the Qarinah of what was at stake, warned them of the disease that would ravaged humanity and so the Qarinah continued their appointed tasks, but their resentment grew, as did their curiosity.

    It was then that one of the younger Chaste in a fit of curiousity, decided to consult various oracles to find out if they were succeeding in preventing the disaster.

    To her horror, she found out that in their growing arrogance The Chaste were the ones who ended up creating the disease in their later attempts to purify humanity from wanton desires, an attempt that slipped from their control, and turned into the Catasphore that wiped out most of humanity.

    This Chaste went to its fellow Chaste and told them of what it had learned. The Elders, the first Chaste that had traveled back in time to earily human history scoffed and mocked the oracles, but minority of The Chaste sided with this clear headed Chaste.

    No one can say for certain if the Oracles were right or if they misunderstood the lessons of time, but the Rebellious members of the Chaste knew they had to act so they turned to the Qarinah with what they'd learned.

    The Qarinah were angry, ready to revolt against The Chaste, to bring war to their creators, but the young Chaste knew that the Qarinah were not knowledgable or powerful enough to face their creators, so they urged causten and patience, yet the Qarinah would not listen to any Chaste, so The Young Chaste knew what they had to do.

    They drank deeply of the impurities that acted as the birthing pools for the Qarinah, and transformed themselves in to the Qarinah Divinities, they were Qarinah with the might of The Chaste, becoming the leaders of the Rebellian.

    The Qarinah took the Birthing Pools from The Chaste, and forced the Chaste to hide in a frequency of twilight that only The Chaste could access.

    The war between The Chaste and the Qarinah have continued off and on for millenia.

    Each of the Qarinah Divinities took to a unique taboo, learning to draw strength from it, and sharing it with those Qarinah who joined their Harems.

    During the earily days of the War of Thwarted Desires, the Qarinah decided they needed allies amoung the other Supernatural creatures, but they could not decide which, so different groups went to different creatures and made deals with them, which transformed the Qarinah, who ended up different Breeds depending on who they or their ancestors allied with, as their allies magic empowered, but also tainted them.

    Those who went to the Changelings, Fetches, and Cambions for aid, became the Leannán Sídhe, those who went to the Vampires, Mummies and other undead became the Lamia, those who went to the Werewolves and other shifters became the Satyrs, those who turned to the Mages and other spellcasters became the Shakti, those who went to the demons, became the Lilitu, and those went to the Sin Eaters became Maenads.

    The War of Thwarted Desires has become largely a cold war, with each side pushing humanity in different directions, each positive that they were right.

    Qarinah have abilities called enchantments, lasting magical effects that grow more powerful the longer the target is under the effect. A Qarinah can only have so many Enchantments in effect at a time, depending on her Ecstasy, her power stat.

    Indulgences are a much more instantaneous magic, the Qarinah focuses her Ecstasy upon empowering a thrilling impulse instead of a more deep seated desire (represented by Enchantments).

    The Qarinah are born in Breeding Pools, or Impurity Pools as called by The Chaste, mystical pools of constrained sexual energy, laced with suppressed sexual desires.

    Each Breed of Qarinah maintains they're own Breeding Pools, by feeding Prana, which give birth only to their Breed of Qarinah.

    The pool appear to be milk white fluid, that bubbles like a hot tar pit. From these pools the Qarinah emerge as empheral beings with no physical substance, having an Ecstasy of 0.

    Its only by feasting on the erotic dreams of a mortal or mortals that the Qarinah becomes an Ecstasy 1 Qarinah, becoming powerful enough to become embodied.

    The dreams of the mortal or mortals, dictate the form the Qarinah's new body takes, as well as the "mortal" identity the Qarinah gains. If the Mortal dreamed of a hunky cop, then the Qarinah's body looked like a hunk and reality shifts to accept the Qarinah as a Police Officer.

    Qarinah are shaped shifters, but they can not shape shift at will, they can only shapeshift when drawing upon someone's sexual fantasies, taking the form that person is attracted to.

    Fuel Stat: Prana. The Qarinah can gain Prana in several ways. One is by having sex, for every point of willpower a Qarinah siphons for a mortal, they gain 1 Prana, or 2 Prana if the Target was a full virgin or if they indulge in their sexual Taboo.

    By making an offering to the Qarinah Divinity, or more controversially, one of The Chaste. The Qarinah can call out the name of either a Qarinah Divinity or a Chaste sacrifice either 1 point of Lethal damage or temprarily 1 dot of resources for 1 Prana. A specific Qarinah Divinity or Chaste will only accept 1 scarifice a day, per point of Ecstasy.

    Qarinah also gain a point when they rebel against an authority figure if there are consquences for doing so.

    The last common way is to cast an Enchantment upon a Mortal. The Mortal will eventually begin generating Prana, that any Qarinah can draw up if they feed sexually upon the Mortal, in addition to the Prana gained from draining willpower. The longer the Enchantment is on the Mortal, the Deeper it gets, the bigger the pool of Prana the Mortal generates once per day.

    Anatogonists:

    The Chaste and their servants obviously.

    Each Breed also gains an additional special antongonist depending upon Breed, the price of the ancient alliances that made the Breeds stronger.

    Puritanically societies, Tyrannies.

    The Bilthuli, a Breed that allied with The Godmachine and its Angels, leading to a war with the Lilitu that the Bilthuli lost badly, causing them to reject and hate the Qarinah Divinities and the other Breeds who stood by and watch them be slaughtered. They offer scarifices to the Godmachine instead of Qarinah Divinities to gain Prana.

      The Chaste and other Antongists detailed.

      A figure dressed in white and black hovers a foot off the ground, gracefully moving towards you.

      At first you think its some kind of old person, with withered and wrinkly old skin, yet as the being gets closer you notice that its not the wrinkles of an elderly person, its more like the greying flesh of an deydrated corpse.

      It gets closer to you and you see a flash of gold on its face. Its eyes are covered with two gold coins, the only colour on the beings, everything else is shades of black, white, or gray.

      It appears to have a cloth wrapped around its mouth, yet upon closer inspection you realize from the tight shape of the black cloth, it has no mouth, nor does it have eyes underneath the coins.

      This orificeless, decaying, yet very much alive thing seems to be studying you with its none eyes, and you can here a voice in the back of your mind, it sounds like your inner voice when its judging you.

      "You are perfect, so perfect and pure, as the virgin snow around your feet, its time you enjoyed your reward" you here in your mind, before you notice its claws getting longer and sharper.

      You know you should get away, but the voice in your mind, it makes any though of fleeing seem wrong, dirty some how.

      This is your last thoughts as the claws come down upon your neck until...you awaken, transformed, the last urges of the flesh dripping into a pool beneath your floating feet.

      You feel purified, clear of purpose, and filled with power of self righteousness, yet a small part of you is aware that the price for this is that you have been attached to magical deals with mist hienous beings.

      You are now one of The Chaste, the salvage of humanity, and the judging and purfying of the sinners is your new birthright.

      Okay The Chaste come from a tradgic future where most of humanity is dead or dying and for the survivors, doom is assured.

      The Chaste made deals with horrible demonic beings to cleanse them of impurities and to allow them to survive the disease, so they could go back in time to try to save humanity.

      The twist to this is the first servitor race you created thinks your full of shit and have rebelled against you and what worse is they could be right, the disaster could be your fault.

      Thing is, your too self righteous to give a fuck what they think.

      This is the main anatongist group, The Chaste, I just thought a taste from they're perpective would be good.

      The Chaste don't really reproduce so much as living Chaste can get the essence of a dead Chaste to infest a full virgin's dead body, after its been purged of lust and desire.

      The body is resurrected as a new Chaste.

      The Chaste are genderless and sexless, although they have long hair, feeling cutting it would be an act of vanity, although its always neat and tidy, The Chaste hate any disorder they don't create themselves for the purpose of a greater order.

      The pacts The Chaste made generates they're own Prana, but at the cost that someone must suffer for it.

      But that is a small price to pay for the salvation of humanity.

      The Chaste spend most of their time hidden in their reinforced twilight, for the Qarinah hunt them in vengence and for their essence which when devoured by a powerful Qarinah turns them into a Qarinah Divinity.

      When a Chaste does manifest its power is great and horrifying.

      Anyone except for the Qarinah or another Chaste takes Prana Burn damage for every point of damage dealt in melee against the Chaste, as the great power of the Chaste is unleashed.

      A Chaste's claws deal aggravated damage.

      The gaze of the Chaste can trigger feelings of apathy, scorn, disgust, and despair, with Chaste feeding off of any self harming actions this causes, gaining a point of Prana for every point of damage ths target deals to themselves or for every point lost in integrity.

          Enchantments & Indugences

          To cast an Enchantments, a Qarinah shapes the Prana by visualizations and plants in a mortal or an object of passionate action (like Muhommad Ali's boxing gloves, or the original reel from a classic 70’s porn movies), a seed of Prana within their target.

          That causes a specific effect depending which Enchantment was used.

          As an example Enchantment the Enchantment of Life.

          Stage 1: Blessing: Double the amount healed by natural healing.

          Stage 2: Double the amount healed by magic and allow the Enchanted Mortal or possessor of the Enchanted Object to heal a point of bashing, by spending a Prana point.

          Stage 3: Immunity to none magical diseases and the Mortal or possessor of the enchanted object can spend 3 Prana to heal 1 lethal damage.

          Drawback: Uncontrolible Fertility: Contraceptives don't work on the Mortal or Possessor of the Enchanted Object, condoms break, the pill doesn't work, and vascetomies reverse themselves.

          Enchantment of Pain:

          Stage 1: Pain becomes more bearable. The Mortal no longer suffers wound penaltiea

          Stage 2. Pain starts to become pleasurable. Gain the mortal gains 1 Prana when ever recieving 1 lethal or 3 bashing damage in a turn.

          Stage 3: The Mortal also gets 1 willpower per Prana gained by pain.

          Draw back: Gain the Addicted Condition towards pain.
