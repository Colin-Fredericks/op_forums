                                              so... let's bring this thread back to course?

                                              Personally, I believe that just saying "aliens" or something like that is too simple for the setting- the reason is that the chances are that you are right, in a way. In a world of conspiracies and dark things in the shadows, real conspiracy theories has to be bigger than the usual. It is not that there are vampires in the church- it is that vampires are a parasitic lifeform from outer space, and that werewolves are the hybrids of such aliens with mankind, and mages are radio stations who make contact with faraway plants. Or perhaps the devil is the true power behind it all- sin eaters are souls which escaped from Hell, mummies are people who were sold to the Prince of Darkness by an ancient demon worshiping culture and the God Machine is a tool meant to become Satan's body on earth built by countless cults through history. Or maybe everything we think that exist is just a virtual reality in which we are all trapped, and the supernatural creatures are corrupted programs- changelings are codes which were corrupted by the GENTRY virus, prometheans are bugs in the system of virtual accounts without users which are "fixed" when a new user finally takes their place, beasts are people who have a cheat which allows them to access a virtual sub-space with a new customized body features.

                                              Or maybe something stranger still- those are the Chronicles of Darkness, after all. In such weird and broken world, it only makes sense that conspiracy theories would be a lot weirder.
