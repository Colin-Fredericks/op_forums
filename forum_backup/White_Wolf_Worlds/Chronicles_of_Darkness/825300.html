    [CoD/Mage] Magnum Opus - Actual Play

    02-07-2016, 03:04 AM
    Few weeks ago I started new mortal chronicle in preparing to the coming of 2ED Mage: the Awakening. I was planning on running it only on few sessions and switching to fresh Mage, but delays in publishing of new corebook came in my way. Instead, until pointed otherwise, it’s a mortal investigator game on rules from Chronicles of Darkness corebook – only spiced a bit with elements of Mage here and there, laying ground work go Awakening of characters. I have also a bit problem with a plot as it is – but it will be marked in the end of third post, when I catch up with story as it is.

    It’s my first Actual Play in English so point me to things you want to know. It will be written in shorter form, as language of Brits is my second one, making me write it much longer than I would want with my limited free time – and for any mistakes I apologize in advance. Also, there could be longer pauses in my writing back, as putting those things really took me hell lot a time compared to taking notes in Polish. Game is with bi-weekly sessions, for my buddies in RPG club in my home city of Lublin in Poland.

    Themes
    Marked in chronicle title, Magnum Opus is strongly based on ideas of Alchemy. Precisely Alchemy best described in Uncrowned Kings Legacy from Mage: the Awakening 1ED corebook. This Supernal Alchemy is transformative Art that change your Mind, Body and Soul – and all the World around you to reflect that. Story is deep submerge in to your own psyche and power to become something more – and to get something more in the process. Characters get Philosopher's Stone on first game session – but their real Magnum Opus has only begun. Or at least this is the plan for this game.

    Setting
    Chronicle is located in small town of Wendell in Massachusetts. It’s very much „my New England”, with large dose of licentia poetica. Think about this community much more as one of settlements you found in Stephen King’s books or like Twin Peaks from David Lynch series. Add a spice of Lovecraft here and there and you will understand the feel I want to convey. It has chosen real world town name only so I could gauge distance to Amherst and Boston easily. To Amherst it’s little over 30 minutes ride. To Boston it’s a bit under 2 hours to get by car.

    Wendell from Chronicles of Darkness is small town “in the middle of nowhere”, surrounded by dense forests and nearby river, with less than 900 citizens. You can go in to it by riding about quarter of an hour from main roads – but you still feel a bit isolated. It has one church of congregation calling itself Good Christians that is gnostic cult, in reality. It’s ruled by the Reverend Joseph J. Kleckner - under 40 self-proclaimed Hand of God, Holly Lawson ( known as Miss Holly ) - few years older leader of the parish community, Walter Boddie – middle aged, caring ( to citizens ) Mayor and Justin D. Hale – young but devoted worshiper which is said to do anything for his faith.

    Settlement has small clinic in that Lian is nurse under one doctor Jacob Martin. It has a bit prominent cemetery (connected to Good Christians) and one funeral home run from generations by Hall family. There is a dinner (did not prepared it yet, details soon) and school combining everything from elementary to high school levels. There is also bakery next to grocery store, run by old Russian immigrant, Vladimir Vladimirovich, in that James got job few months back. Beside the town, on the hill, for over 150 years stand Victorian mansion, with gargoyles and a like gothic elements, now empty for over two decades from it’s last buyer. There are some weird kid’s stories about that place.

    As to greater setting, chronicle take place in New England from Mage line, based mostly on Boston Unveiled material. I develop slowly an update on it to proper 2ED setting – more can be read here, in Mage subforum topic. Plan is to let character resolve cult plotline in Wendell, Awaken them and dwell in to Mystery of Philosopher Stone a bit. Then Boston Concilium comes to them in persons seeking Alchemy, wanting to get what characters got from Alchemist.

    Players Characters
    Story protagonists are living in Wendell for some time. Two are raised here, one is citizen for a few years now and only James is half year in town. What they have in common – besides getting Philosopher Stone – is that they are pushed a bit sideways by the town’s community. They live here, but also are not part of this place, not really. I marked for what players and I are aiming with Path of PCs – character themselves, obviously, do not know what Path they will get into.
    • James Rodney – twenty-something local baker, that is something hiding before the rest. He tries to join the community of Church of Good Christians and is Wendell citizen in only last half of year. Aiming for Acanthus Awakening.
    • John Beckett – Englishman after thirty, liking trench coats, mythology lecturer in Amherst with dark past. Lives in Wendell few years back, coming from Boston. Aiming for Mastigos Awakening.
    • Lian Mavis – silent and calm young nurse from local clinic, devoted to the Church of Good Christians. Born and raised in Wendell. Aiming for Moros Awakening.
    • Madline „Marble” Hall – incredibly pale, but innocent high school girl that helps her few years older brother Richard in running family’s funeral home after death of both parents. Born and raised in Wendell. Aiming for Moros Awakening.

      Magnum Opus
      Opus Magnum – Session 1
      Story starts precise week before Easter of 2016 year. In small town of Wendell in Massachusetts, just after dusk in Sunday of Spring Equinox - 20th March 2016 - four people were invited to long empty Victorian mansion on the hill, near town. Inside, they found old, bearded man that entertained them and said to them he is Nick Fleming, the Alchemist - and propose to make for them Philosopher's Stone, that each can grant them one wish! Only that price for each one of Stones will be one human life the Stone will take in sleep. After some debating, four strangers agreed to terms of Alchemist and pointed vague conditions on that people will die for their Stone - John pointed that will die first person from Wendell that call for ambulance. James asked that neighbor from across will. Lian pointed to very sick patient. Madline said that will die first client that enters funeral home. And so it happened.

      Note: I was not sure if I need to roll on Integrity Breaking Point in this situation. Character agree to kill other people, but also did not know if this is not really some bad joke or something – as they do not know about Magic. Also, I rather stick to Breaking Point rolls after the confirmation on characters actions – it typically comes with “oh dear, I did it” moments. Still, I did not roll on Integrity even later, when character were also confronted with those actions – as rules were hazy on to this and I did not wanted to break the flow of the game.

      Later, just after visit in James and Lian tried to make to the mass that start Holy Week in local Church of Good Christians, but they were late because of their meeting with Fleming. The dedicated religion characters were throw Disapproving Look by the leaders of community, mainly Miss Holly.

      Next day, on Monday 21st March, protagonists returned to their work - but as hours passed, more people in the town were found dead. First, 30 something Miss Shannon Evans come to Hall’s Funeral Home with her 5 year old son Max come early in the morning. Madline let her in, even when she understood it could be a death with for the woman. Almost crying, Shannon told about body of young dead husband she found in the bad today morning. She plead to help her and prepare ceremony by Madline and Richard alone, as she does not have nerves. Siblings agreed to take the “case”.

      Note: Yes, Mr. and Mrs. Evans were neighbors of James. Players were suspecting two more deaths in this morning.

      John having free day because students canceled classes because of spring break, wanted to stay at home. Lian pickup early the ambulance call - patient with stomach pain. She go with old and small ambulance from 70s the clinic and with help of man’s neighbor – put him in car - as quick as she could. She put rest the man after giving him some painkillers. He died in sleep before Doctor Martin come to him.

      After only an hour next call is made to clinic – Miss Cooper, neighbor of Freddie Butler, said that come to him to eat lunch with old man – but he died in his sleep. Nurse know well Mister Butler – she over looked him 4-5 times a week as he was having severe case of Parkinson's. Lian called for the Madeline to help her get next body to funeral home.

      When they were in the car, Reverend Kleckner called to Lian, wanting to know what’s going in his weird deaths in his “flock of sheep” and talking something on cleaning the souls of those that break their rules. He made emphasis to ask if Lian will be on evening mass.

      Rest of work day come rather calmly – Madeline and Richard had enough work with those bodies as it was. James called only to Lian that his boss, Vladimir, is having some problems – knowing about his heart condition she come quickly and took baker to the clinic. This time Doctor Martin could help the patient, but Vladimir Vladimirovich need to go for a few days on observation to the Amherst Hospital.

      After work, in evening, Lian and James came to the Church of Good Christians on 18 mass. Five minutes after this hour, temple was locked from the inside and service has started. It was rather typical devotion – until sermon. Reverend Kleckner said short but flaming speech about people testing God and their faith. That those that left today are pointing ways for Good Christians must be straight, and their actions – more determined. That in this year, Easter sacrifice must be fuller. He summoned by name Lian and James to stand up. He obligated them to get the offerings at most at Saturday evening mass. For Justin Hale, committed worshiper known from getting all that Good Christians need to – he said to get one offering for community. Urged all faithful to – as Knives of Abraham – were ready to fulfill will of God – just like Abraham killed his son, Isaac. Just after the mass, Lian explained privately to James what a sacrifice exactly is…

      Note: As you suspect, Good Christians are not so good – or rather good in our morality sense. Their “sacrifice” is human they want to “liberate from hell of life”. More about them is to be found here.
      At about 19, all characters "heard" the Flemings call of them to the mansion - they all come and in Victorian stylish salon each of them get their own red, palm sized, slightly pulsing crystal from Alchemist, radiating some kind of power - Philosopher’s Stones.



      After handing them, Fleming bade characters farewell - and disappeared on their own eye, like he never was!
      Note: I probably should made roll characters on Breaking Points for seeing surely supernatural effect. But I reasoned that characters are Sleepwalker now, very slowly starting Awakening, so only magical effect should not come to them. Also, he only disappeared, not changed in to unspeakable beast or something that terrifying.

      Characters explored mansion, but found almost nothing than furniture unused for decades - beside great Victorian portrait of some alchemist from at least 150 years old over their heads in salon, the size of one man. Looking exactly like the Fleming that just gone!



      Well, the clothes of modern Fleming were from end of XX century, not half of XIX one. But still, resemblance was uncanny!

      Note: Same as above – “weird things are going” was not enough, in my mind, to make Integrity Breaking Point roll. Also, some family members have very much the same feature as their ancestors, sometimes…

      Deeper search of mansion uncovered four hand written books in their previous host study – the only left in there - one in classical Latin, from around late Middle Ages, one in old French, from Renaissance, one in old English, from times of Shakespeare and Queen Elizabeth - and one filled with odd symbols or pictographs, difficult to assess from what age. All bound in leather and on very old paper, like personal journals from few centuries back. All have some weird diagrams. Each character took one book – James took Latin one, Lian French, John English and Madline the odd one.



      Note: Yes, “odd symbols and pictograms” are High Speech Runes. In reality, all books are more or less the same book – called Magnum Opus – working as Daimonomica for Uncrowned Kings Legacy. The most Rotes encoded are in High Speech book, but also it’s toughest to crack.

      Typical page of the book…


      Finally, seeing how Fleming looks a like to the pictured alchemist on the portrait, Madeline is dedicated to show the painting to professional art historian. The four of them help them to get it to John’s pickup and Madeline is appointed with him to go next morning to his collage in Amherst. Group has split up and went to home to think about Stones they get for the lives of others. Tomorrow is a day too…

      On Thursday 22nd March, things went much calmly, but not without complications. James and Lian had normal day in work. He only called to Lian if she could as Doctor Martin to help him study Latin. Doctor agreed to teach him on working days just after 20, each night.

      Note: Character trying to get Language Merit.

      Madeline was preparing to go with John early in the morning, when her brother Richard reminded her that they have two funerals. Lucky or not, before funeral at 12 that was from previous Richard’s client, came older women with little Max. It turned out she is mother of Shannon Evans and come to Wendell to straight things with Hall’s Funeral Home. Grandmother of Max told Madeline that her daughter died in the sleep last night – probably could not live without husband. As most of family is from Boston, older women asked Madeline to send corpse of Mister Evans to the State’s capitol, on address pointed by her. Sibling agree, talked about logistics details and went to first and only funeral today.

      Being free at 14, even without changing clothes from here formal black suit, Madline and John drove to his collage in Amherst. On campus, they searched for Professor Erika Johnson from History of Art. Even when she is in hurry to leave to home as students canceled classes, she accepts John and Madeline. Interested, she asks how they get the portrait – when she does not hear the answer, she determined work needed. She does not promise to end everything before the Easter, because employees took leave – but she will get know to John. Before 16 they ae back in to Wendell.

      At 18, James and Lian again goes on mass, just after dusk – with tradition of Holy Week there is another mass. This time Reverend Kleckner is much calmer, do not refers in his sermon to latest events in town, highlight only need to follow the ruts of their faith. Lian even waits specially after mass to ask about his milder tone – Kleckner answers that there is no need to extensively frighten people, because it only cause panic in the flock.

      At night, John for a moment goes to the mansion. Now building seems totally abandoned. He checks for a moment library that is completely empty. Almost, to be precise. On shelves, scattered on whole room, there are three tomes of XX century book about Alchemy as a way to understand one’s mind and psychic development – Carl’s Gustav Jung Psychology and Alchemy. Just like some have planted…

      In the same time Lian finds in Internet translator of French, just few towns away. Madline searched by occult pages about this weird symbol language – on of the forums there was mentioning about it as “Language of Mediterranean Sea”, from the beginning of Greece and Egypt civilization. And after this discovery, they all went asleep.

      Note: And this is end of Session 1. Madline just get first Clue in greater research on mysterious symbols run on Investigation rules from Chronicles of Darkness rulebook. More idea on Mystery of High Speech can be read in this topic.

          Philosopher’s Stone – Session 2

          Wednesday 23rd March started normally for residents of Wendell – but ended with dark truths.
          John Beckett has been suddenly called to his collage in Amherst on business matter – so he was not available for this and next day.

          Note: John’s player could not join us on game session.

          Because of ill health of Vladimir Vladimirovich, James Rodney looked for the help to run bakery for the couple of days. And indeed, later in afternoon come to him Jacob Safar, young, twenty something half Arabic boy that moved with his mother to the town just after New Year. James shown him for the rest of the work day most important secrets of job. Madeleine Hall and Lian Mavis spent the rest of the day over dead and living persons, respectively.

          After Wednesday mass in Church of Good Christians, “gifted” by Fleming went to study their Philosopher’s Stones, in their own homes. Madeleine, after few uses and experiments on her Stone – like watching it color and slight pulsating light. Then she tried to listening to it – hearing faintly sounds of heart beating inside she would be a bit scared – unless she was so calm and rational girl she is. She even humming to Stone! Finally just decided to close her hand and very hard to think about her dream - eternal life! The thunder of white light and massive headache a tearing her consciousness made her fall on the floor. She awoken with the return waves of migraine, but she felt so… alive.

          Note: I made Philosopher’s Stone to work mechanically as constant spell on the character that can mimic Practice up to 6 dots in Arcanum ( yes, slight Imperial Mysteries can be achieved with it ) or up to 5-6 dots in Merits. Players wrote those effects as special free given Merit. Madeleine choose “Immortality”, so she got effect replicating Life 5 Regeneration and general “longevity” from her Stone.

          In the same time, James also experiments with Stone. He almost on first try activated it and gets his own lifelong dream – he finally get to know knowledge of languages he always wanted! He understood it when he just could read all the Fleming’s Latin book.

          Note: James get 5 dot Language Merit, meaning he can choose up to 5 Languages he now knows, beside English. To not let player to feel a bit cheated in choosing static power in game, I said he can change this list of those 5 languages once per day for the Willpower Dot. (As game Trait) Character can buy back later on those Dots by cost of 1 Experience each.

          Lian still do not think about her dreams or Stone. She tries to arrange translation of book from old French via Internet, but the costs for those would be in thousands of dollars. Madeleine and James spent rest of the night on studying the weird language of book and reading the content of Alchemists book, respectively.

          Madeleine dig for few hours on the Internet for the writing in “her” book, this time using a bit uncovered occult meaning behind those marks and connecting them to historical secret languages. She finds that some marks looks like those found in Enochian – mysterious lounge of angels that has been uncovered to the John Dee and Edward Kelley, two famous English occultists from XVI century. From their records, Enochian was language in that God created world, and before banishing Adam – he contacted angels and his Creator in it.

          Note: Madeleine uncovered second Clue in her research on the “weird language”. As she was using second time her Occult Skill to Uncover Clue, she was having cumulative -1 penalty. She used the only Element from first Clue (about “Language of Mediterranean Sea”) and used it to cancel penalty. The “Enochian Language” Clue has 1 new Element in it – Madeleine will probably use it for the new Uncovering Clue, next time she will research subject. If she will use third time Occult Skill to Uncover the Clue, she will roll with -2 penalty.

          James, on the other hand, is reading the Latin version of book to the pillow. He uncovers its titled Opus Magnum and about Alchemy. As he is totally layman in matters of myths and mysteries, he cannot grasp the meaning of the text. Closest to him are diagrams in it, looking like some chemical formulas, only without larger sense to him. The books first part is about something called Nigredo. James tries to find something online on this term. He founds some Satanists page about sacrificing children to the dark forces in Nigredo. Spooked, he has a bit problem to lay in to rest at night...

          Note: James do not have any dot of Occult. Seeing as how small dice pool he will have, players choose to make Dramatic Failure from I Intelligence+Occult roll on Internet research – and that’s why Satanists link. I gave character Spooked (Alchemy) Condition and player took a Beat from worsening his Failure.

          On Thursday 24th March Madeleine and James works from the dawn. James shows the ropes for the young Jacob. Madeleine lays next “clients” from last days with her brother. Lian in her free time in job… talks with Mister Butler in the broom cupboard of clinic, hearing his heart bit in Stone. He’s also calling, vaguely to her. But she cannot crack what he is says…

          After the mass, all three meet at James house to made meeting over the identity of their mysterious patron and what is content of books he left them. James translates for them Fleming’s books – two that he can, at least. This weird language of Madeline’s book is reasoning with few words in his own head, but he cannot understand it, how much he tries. Both codex he can translate are transcriptions of roughly the same work from two different eras and languages – classical, Medieval Latin and old, Renaissance French. Books title is the one and same – „Opus Magnum” – Great Work.

          He read it to both girls. Text starts with very odd poem:

          “In the name of the rose, in the name of the cross: The covenant is mysterious.
          Torn darkness is in dark vaults.
          Red seals the mouth.
          In the intoxication of my sensations, I hear enchanted whispers.
          The clocks stand still.
          Gray things are reflected and flicker on the wall.
          The chime sounds gloomily.

          In the sea of desire, in the knowledge of the believers: torment is frightened.
          Pitiful anxieties in pale faces; they are ready for the ritual.

          Great work finds me.
          I am pure like white blood.
          Great work binds me.
          Take me into your circle.

          The eternal rights are changing.
          I will leave life silently.
          I will bring the end through truly tested faith.

          The chest is cut through.
          The innocent will be sacrificed.
          Therefore it is necessary to clean my blood.
          I am greatly dead.”

          Reading this aloud, James even want to toss books and shut it up for good. Both girls are calming him down a bit.

          Note: James resolved his Spooked Condition, getting Beat for ending Condition. And yes, poem is lyrics of E Nomine song Opus Magnum – I advise to listen it for inspiration.



          Madeline is almost sure that Nick Fleming is not he other than Nicholas Flamel himself, great Alchemist of Paris!

          All three read more about Nigredo phase online. The information are very interesting:

          „Process of transmutation for metals in Opus Magnum starts from darkening phase called Nigredo. Then mix everything with everything, in a sealed alchemical retort, destroying and killing essence of now substance, when finally ending, she could in longer run of changes could transform its nature in to perfect…”

          Note: It was typical Intelligence+Occult roll, with slight bonus for having Internet.

          Madeline even found information that Nostradamus connected Nigredo with passion of Christ on Good Friday - which is tomorrow! They talked more about it all and Already they have to split – when
          to Madeline got text from John…

          Note: And it’s end of Session 2 with small cliff hanger. Now, you can help me all a bit…

          Plot Problem
          As you can see, I have a bit problem to explore Action element in game. We are two sessions in the play and the most Action inducing scene was when Nurse tried to get quickly to patient that called 911. I was thinking about using Justin Hale as menacing rivalry that wants to push characters – but I do not see this before Saturday evening. Or at least when Lian and James decides to get “sacrifice”. Maybe you have some suggestions as to how rise the adrenaline of players in a setting like this? I also thought that with longer play as mortals, maybe adding some Horror to the story would be nice idea. If you think about good one, let me know.

                  I’m coming back with sum ups of game sessions from last two months – look for more of my posts in next weeks. Waiting for comments and advises from you.

                  Note: In this game session I used three brand new ghosts I made based on Philosophers Stone’s victims, listening to 2ptTakrill advice. Full stats will be shared when players will end plotline. But you were warned – this game is now officially a ghost(s) story.

                  Nigredo, part 1 – Session 3
                  „Process of transmutation for metals in Opus Magnum starts from darkening phase called Nigredo. Then mix everything with everything, in a sealed alchemical retort, destroying and killing essence of now substance, when finally ending, she could in longer run of changes could transform its nature in to perfect…”

                  It’s morning of Friady 25th March 2016 in Wendell, Massachusetts. Only few hours ago, in Thursday evening, lives of „gifted” by alchemists Nick Fleming were overturned. After suddenly interrupted deliberation at James Rodney, when he needed to quickly leave his house, Madline and Lian wetn to local dinner „Valerie’s” - run by stout Valerie Tucker. They looked on to next courtship of Robert Bustos – veteran from Vietnam, more or less in age of Valerie, that eats almost only in this local. He lost left arm on war, decade ago. He stills try to rouse Miss Tucker.

                  When girls eat their dinner and excellent apple pies, there started weird things. First Madleine’s fork fell on his own under table. After, when they split up because Madliene had round around Cemetary this night, poor Lian would swore that in her pastry human fingernail! Valerie very much took over the problems with her cooking. And much more when over there was nothing wrong in it as fingernail were not in dish.

                  Note: I used here Telekinesis Numina of ghost connected to Madleine and Image Manifestation of one anchoring to Lian’s.

                  Later, when Madline lonely „patrolling” Cemetery – according to centuries old tradition of Hall family – she meets clearly weeping women standing near tomb stone of with written name “Jack Evans”. She instantly recognize Miss Evans on that she knew is dead!

                  Note: First Integrity Breaking Point in chronicle! Player rolls Resolve+Composure -1 for witnessing non-violent supernatural, but get’s +1 for high Integrity ( value 7 in this case ). Madline pass it, so she only got Spooked Condition.

                  When Madline enter in discussion with her, she wants to prove her that she is dead – girl lead her to funeral home, but Miss Evans cannot bypass buildings threshold, standing motionless before doors. High school girl enters inside to get documents. When she returns, resentful ghost of Miss Evans tries to attack on Madline for nothing – but she turns papers from her hands.

                  Note: Miss Evans ghosts do not „know” she is dead – and when confronted with the fact, ghost lose her Integrity ( from 7 to 6 ). Furious and confused, she lashes on Madline with Telekinesis Numina.

                  In the meantime, Lian Mavis is very naughty lady. She takes her old, work ambulance ( like from Ghostsbusters ) and goes to Lubrock – even smaller town, about 15 minutes in dens New England of Wendell. There she goes on border of settlement with forest and hides car near local rock bar. Lurking in the bushes, Lian waits for her prey to come out of premise. Indeed, few minutes later comes out very drunk black hair twenty something girl, that however, very stubbornly wants to return to home on foot. Lian just wants to grab her, but see in bar window some man watch over girl. When drunk passes her, nurse surprise her with chloroform and drag to ambulance, where she straps her to stretcher. However, men runs from bar and yell „Alice, Alice”. Lian knows she can have problems. Men clearly hear something and he went in to direction of bushes with nurse – but he changed his mind, barely three metres from her! Lian drive to Wendell with this year offering.

                  Note: When Lian was seen by the men, player roll Composure + Stealth vs Wits + Composure of men. She passed the test, but barely, so why men almost get her. Just when leaving Lubrock I asked player to roll Integrity Breaking Point – she kidnapped girl, even if it is all to destroy the Good Christians cult in the end. She rolled her Resolve + Composure + 1 ( for Integrity 7 ) + 1 ( for good intentions, I treated it as working under Virtue ) – 3 ( for kidnapping itself, I treated it as torture ). This player did not pass and character get Guilty Condition. Lian is eaten alive by her remorse, that is fighting under her consciousness.

                  It’s 1 a.m. in Friday 25th March 2016 year. Lian comes to Wendell. Madline, on her route in Cemetery, sees ambulance entering town In the middle of the night. Interested, she shadow nurse to church of Good Christians where with mayor Walter Boddie they hide young girl from Lubrock, Alice. They go down to secret crypt under alter where is marble catafalque like from medieval torture chamber with shackles and mask that muffle screams. Both straps unconsciousnes girl.

                  Walter Boddie - Wendell's mayor


                  At the same time, John hides across behind next bushes, interested in commotion under church. Madline and John watch in two cult members in their wicked actions. John makes pictures from hideout of whole events. Finally, Madline is confronting mayor and Lian – high school girl is threatening couple, but quickly they bounce it that Wendell is in control of Good Christians. Abashed, Madline leave and returns to her house. When they end, mayor and nurse split their ways in the middle of night. However, John has pictures of them!

                  Note: John get three Leverage Conditions from his taking photos – on Mayor Boddie, on Lian and on Madline for partaking in possible kidnaping. Madeline tried to intimidate two other with Presence+Intimidation roll, but Mayor and Lian defeated her in contested rolls as they use teamwork rules to win on Resolve + Composure roll and marking Wendell as their territory. After whole action Lian makes Integrity Breaking Point roll for condemning Alice to at least imprisonment, with the similar modifiers as last time, only marking -2 instead -3 for the torture variant.
