        My Wishlist (as a Storyteller that doesn't really like Crossover because of the added complications).

        Mechanics
        Cross-Template Powers: I really like Disciplines, Gifts, Contracts and such lets A Template dabble in another templates domain. At least one example per template.
        Cross - Over System Hacks: Ways to modify the game mechanics that smooths over the rough edges when power collide. something a bit more in depth than just declaring a "Clash of Wills".
        Cross - Over Merits: I could easily see an Ambassador merit that lets an outsider count as a member of that template socially. Beasts already have this to a degree, but have ways to extend it to the other Template would be excellent.

        Storytelling
        Shadow Society: This was mentioned up thread, but a chronicle where all the supernaturals in a domain answer to the same authority would be very fun. Examples could show how different ruling parties could operate ( a council of representative from each Template all the way to a single powerful ruler).
        The Band: Running a group comprised of multiple templates, with several example groups. Expanding on material in "The Pack" with more detail and from the perspective of the other supernaturals.
        Common Enemies: Explore how a supernatural would deal with the unique enemies of a different type of supernatural. What happens when Vampires encounter the Hosts, or a Changeling encounters one of the God Machines Angels, or when a Promethean gets tangles in a Seers plot.
        Themes: Unifying and combining the different (and occasionally conflicting) themes stressed by each line. Keep the focus on the CofD's genral philosophy to keep the interactions personal to the characters involved. Its all fine and good to think of the neat ways the assorted powers can interact, but why would these characters want to interact seems more important.

        The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
        Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
        The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
        Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
        Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

                                                Originally posted by atamajakki View Post

                                                It was a bad guess about a game that doesn't exist yet, so sorry for getting it wrong.

                                                An interesting line has to drawn between the Circle's &quot;revel in your monstrosity&quot; and the Ordo's &quot;surpass your capabilities.&quot;
                                                To me, the line has always been that most of the Ordo use the "better monster" mostly as rhetoric and hyperbole, as a way to freak people out and sound badass. Most of them tend to see what they're doing in more practical terms: how can I be as immortal and as powerful and as not-terrible-drawback-having as possible? Sure, some of them might identify with the idea of the monster, and some of them put a "we are the demons" spin on the whole gnostic thing 2e gave them, but most of them don't really consider themselves monsters. The difference is, when the Circle talk about monsters they really tend to mean it, and the dominant philosophy there involves some degree of identification with monstrosity. Of course, you could draw another interesting parallel in that both of them tend to think, or try to think, in scientific terms detached from good and evil. The Circle teaches that vampires are natural, and are natural predators, while the order tends to approach things from a scientific, evolutionary viewpoint. Ironically, the order can be more mystical than the Circle: some Ordo members believe that their state is a curse from god and that what they do is an act of rebellion against him, while the Circle doesn't really believe in the abrahamic god at all and holds that vampirism is simply a natural feature of the world.

                                                    I've been toying around with the idea of something like crossover-powers that are sort of like if a devotion or conjunctional spell required another supernatural ability to activate instead of just multiple of your own. It'd basically work how it sounds, allowing supernaturals who work together to find ways to combine their powers in various ways, and the exact effects could be anything from "This happens if you use X immediate power and they use Y" to something more like a Scale being combined with a Werewolf rite to produce some long term effect or experiment. Mages, who can already do just about anything, would benefit from things like a power that might normally need tons of reach having that be covered by the 'assistance', whereas most others benefit from doing something entirely new and probably more powerful than normal.

                                                    So, basically would be nice to see some sort of system that covers different ways to combine supernatural powers, not as something anyone can do on their own, but to give crossover groups a little system to cover the inevitable "What happens if he makes my skin wrap into a talen?" and whatever else pops up.
