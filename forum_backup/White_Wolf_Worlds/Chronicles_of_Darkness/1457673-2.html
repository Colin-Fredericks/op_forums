                                    I know from other threads that this is how you prefer to envision and play Changeling: The Lost, but it's not a universal take on it. In the game Seemings are enforced by your experiences in Arcadia (which ultimately do derive from your Keeper) but are also very wide and generslly without any specific descriptors (Beasts and Elementals being exceptions, as they are assumed by the game to be based on a single animal or element). Kiths are what grants specificity and is the big factor in determining things such as your Mien, but Kiths are only sometimes forced upon changelings by Gentry; many times they're gained because of other reasons not directly related to their Keepers.

                                    I also think a lot of players (maybe not your own players, but others who'd read this theoretical book) won't appreciate how much you make their Keepers influences their characters. It's also weird to me how all Keepers tend to enforce the same specific roles based on human stereotypes when Gentry are supposed both diverse and alien.
                                    Yeah, they do conform to ideas that'd fit into human folklore to a large degree, but any roles they enforce on their "wards" are according to their own alien desires and not according to six specific archetypes that apparently all True Fae have a desire to emulate.

                                                To give you an idea of what I am envisioning, here is one of the npc characters and am building for the chronicle.

                                                Madame Butterfly is a Nikkei Fairest Nightsinger and the inspiration for Pierre Loti’s Madame Chrysantheme, Jon Long’s short story Madame Butterfly, and Puccini’s opera of the same name. In 1858, she was a young geisha named Kiku Aikawa, she was lured by an American naval office, who was also a changeling racketeer from the Winter Court, who married her and then sold her to one of the Western Gentry. This was actually a common tactic used by American loyalists to kidnap and she Japanese women to the Western True Fae. When escaped the Hedge, she found herself in San Francisco in the year 1903, where she became a popular opera singer for, ironically, Madame Butterfly. She used her popularity as an opera singer to help win concessions for the Japanese American and Nikkei changelings. After the 1906, San Francisco earthquake, she helped set up the Nikkei freehold in South Park. By 1912, she had become the Spring Queen in Japan Town. In modern times, she acts as a mediator between the seasonal courts and the courts of Japan.
                                                Appearance: Despite being a centurion, she still looks like a beautiful young woman in her early twenties. This is due to a combination of being a wyrd 8 changeling and having the long of days merit. Her skin a very pale and baby, smooth, and her hair is a magnificent main of black. Behind the mask, her mien as moonlight on snow white skin, pointed ears, deep purple eyes, and her hair is many different tones of black.
