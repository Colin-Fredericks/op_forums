                    Okay, so I'm pretty sure that we've decided that the school is going to be a "normal" private school (with a mix of Gothic and modern architecture due to renovations) that has supernatural stuff under the surface. What we need to decide now is the nearby town that the students hang out in and the teachers live in. I personally want the school to be on one of the Georgia barrier islands.

                    Preferably Tybee island, which has a great story hook in the form of a Mark 15 nuclear bomb that the air force accidentally dropped there back in the 50s and never recovered. It is also close to Savannah and all it's paranormal stuff (ask me about the tunnels beneath the city and the altar they found down there). It has a history of pirate activity (because pirate ghosts and pirate treasure are great hooks) and was a major resort spot, meaning that the Ashwood Abby/the Silver ladder/the Invictus/the Ivory Claws/ a long lost wealthy sin eater krewe/any wealthy splat might have left something there after a party long ago that needs to be taken care of.

                    St. Simmons Island would be my 2nd choice, it is a hub of the African American Gullah culture which preserved many african rituals, words and folk tales despite slavery, home to many plantations (presumably haunted), a mass suicide of recently arrived slaves right before they banned the import of slaves from africa (presumably creating a haunting) and traditional home to two native american tribes with an interesting ritual,

                    St. Catherines Island would make a good spot for a field trip. It is probably incredibly haunted and home to some powerful death and storm spirts due to the 1893 sea islands hurricane, which killed all but one person on the Island and destroyed every building, The fact that Island is mostly wilderness is good for nature type splats (St. Catherines is owned by a private research foundation and isn't open to the public, but I could see a wealthy private school being allowed a field trip) in fact, the barrier islands of Georgia were a birthplace of the ecology movement.

                    And of course, putting the school and town on an Island makes it easier for the government to seal it all off when a paranormal disaster hits the Island...
