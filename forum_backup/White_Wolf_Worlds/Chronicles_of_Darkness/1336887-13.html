                                                    09-26-2019, 09:16 PM
                                                    Minty and @Inodiv are correct regarding Instability. Good question about whether the boost from medium is "retroactive" if you were maxed out on all your Variations and then get a major Instability. The text isn't explicit. I'm inclined to to say it probably shouldn't be, since we have a system for adding new, unentangled Scars that assumes players have a decent amount of say over the Magnitude of those Scars. It isn't likely to come up tooooo often, but I'm open to others' thoughts on the subject.

                                                    @espiritdecalmar: I like that train of thought. Fun concept. You could even have the Progenitor and a victim in the same cohort.

                                                    @Dataweaver: Also a really cool character concept.

                                                    With all the neat stuff folks are already doing with Forms, I looking forward to seeing what people come up with once the other half of Chapter 3 is posted!

                                                    @SirDragos is on target here. Higher-Magnitude Scars come with mechanics that impose direct penalties/problems, but they're a source of Beats when they create significant story complications just like Magnitude 1 Scars are.

                                                    RE: Super Soldier, I shouldn't be at all surprised that they chose now to spoil that. Absolutely perfect during the week when we're previewing the chapter with all the other Forms, and it seems to be working, since we pulled in over $1,000 in new backers within a few hours of that going up. Less than $1,500 left to go until the next Stretch Goal, now.

                                                    I might do "Composite Clades" in a future supplement. In the meantime, pick the Clade whose Adaptations and Variations are most useful to you. Deviants are full of weird exceptions and statistical anomalies, no matter how much the conspiracies would like to place them in neat categories, it's okay if a character isn't a perfect fit for a single Clade.
