                            I'm glad you are having fun with your character. I honestly think this system is really amazing at empowering each auspice.

                            Honestly, all you need to be a silent killer Irraka is the first moon gift and a half decent dice pool. Rote action will do most that work for you. Get the Stealth gift Cunning facet for the rote actions on stealth and no one will ever see you you coming. Add maybe the Fading merit for some additional stealth and you're in business. As far as tribe, it really doesn't matter. The irraka gift lists give you everything you need (Feet of Mist and Slip Away for making a clean get away, for example). The tribe might add to the approach.

                            For example, the Iron Masters give access to the Technology gift facet for shutting down lights (that one is Purity, I think) gift which is useful for bewildering prey and making damn sure that no one sees what happened and who did it. As well as the knowledge gift for making sure you're killing the right guy (Know thy prey). The Hunters give access to a bunch of nature gifts which help if you're playing in a more rural setting, Nowhere to Hide, a merit that makes it so that no one can ever really escape a Hunter who is tracking them, and the Warding gifts - All Doors Unlocked is great for breaking and entering, or trapping your prey and keeping them right where you want them.

                            As far as the Blood Talons, they might give you a bit more damage, and that's all well and good if that's what he wants. A Blood Talon Irraka is a consummate badass, but I think you already have the damage. If you're rolling 6-8 dice with a weapon modifier of any kind and rote action, you're going to kill most things on the first strike. Maybe you'll need a second strike to put the poor creature out of its misery before you disappear back into the shadows.
