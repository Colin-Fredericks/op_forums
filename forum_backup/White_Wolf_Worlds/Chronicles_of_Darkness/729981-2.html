    Originally posted by Acrozatarim View Post
    From what I recall off the top of my head, I think they tend to carve off/burn the victim's spiritual brands as part of the attempt to scourge the auspice off them.

    Of course, the rumours say that this is just to dislodge the actual essence of the Auspice itself from its physical moorings and that, were you able to see an Auspice just by itself, before it rooted itself in flesh, it'd be a sparkling shard of moonlight, almost like a small Lune.

    Then again, I know this pack that said they found their way into a strange and rather cosmopolitan market in a place-that-is-not, crowded with weird creatures and spirits selling all sorts of impossible things. One of the creatures was selling what it claimed were auspices, little silver talismans of the moon that, according to the merchant, could replace an existing auspice with an entirely new one (albeit in an agonising, flesh-shuddering experience as the new auspice tore out the old one's roots and burrowed in). 'Course, none of the Uratha took that deal so they don't know for sure if it was legit or not...
    Oh yeah! I remember reading that, though I don't remember if it was in the Pure book or a section on 2ed. Anyways I was thinking of making a rite that will give a werewolf back their auspice by putting their auspice shard back into them, kind of like putting a soul back into a soulless body.
