        Ok everyone, here's a quick update on what's going on.

        Changes from the 2nd and 3rd editions
        • Oroboroi are no longer bound as strictly to the Leadership theme. Instead, they are loosely bound to a broader theme of Responsibility and Worthiness.
        • The Old Gods are no longer part of the basic origin story. They will be mentioned in a later chapter as the “Bomb in the Room”, to quote this copy of Idigam Chronicles sitting next to me.
        • The "Vault" has been shelved for the Dreamtide.
        • Dreamtide Travel and Manipulation are now part of a merit called "Dream Sorcery", which can be gained by non-Oroboroi occultists, and require Blood, Breath, Willpower, or Jadeite.
        • “Furnace” is now “Heartstring”, to signify that it is not a property of the Heart itself, but its connection to the Oroboros.
        • Breath is gained by eating things, like in 2nd, and we're working on fleshing out just what this may entail. We're thinking of them as being similar to Vitae.
        • "Mandate" is now "Conviction", to reflect the new focus on Worth rather than good leadership. It's a sideways system, and the extremes to fall into are Hubris (the hoarding, rampaging dragon) and Guilt (the human who is no longer worthy of their Heart)
        • Oroboroi start with 12 Ablutions: 3 from the Method, 3 from the Common, 3 from any Oroboros list, and 3 from the Storyteller's discretion.
        • Ablutions can now be gained and lost as a result of public opinion through a process called “The Reimagining”. Compare this to the Renown-based Gifts in Werewolf
        • There is now an Ablution list with stuff that the Oroboroi only have access to through external means.
        • "School" has been morphed into "Purview", which is a manifestation of the former-human's highest ideal.
        • Oroboroi are now completely immune to fire, except in Human Guise, which is burned away to reveal the Dragon beneath.
        • Slayers, Dragonborn, Pilot Lights, and Deep Ones will have their own sheets and rulesets.
        • There's gonna be WAY more stuff to pull with the Deep Ones.
        • Alot of things have been rephrased and retuned for new edition stuff.

        What's been done so far
        • Written out a detailed secret lore, explaining to us what exactly Oroboroi are and what physics are at play here, from which we'll be pulling the mechanics.
        • Also, Dank Memes.
        • Finalized some really core decisions on what's going on in this book and where we should take it
        • Built a mockup character sheet which has new terms and systems in a bit more detail.
        • Started work on the Transitional Rulebook.

        What needs to be done by next week
        • Finish and release a Transitional Rulebook.
        • Have someone look through the new Mage book to figure out what's going on there now.
        • Get a basic sheet out.
        • WE NEED ARTISTS!!

        Moving Forward...
        The current plan is to have a Transitional Rulebook released some time next week. This won't be particularly pretty, nor will it have most of the stuff in it, but it will show exactly where we're taking the game for now. Moving forward from there, we'll be asking for people to try and make characters based on that book, and to give us feedback on the general direction we're moving in. We'll also be looking for Playtesters, and new teammembers in general, ESPECIALLY some artists.

                                                        General
                                                        Method: Slayer
                                                        Philosophy: Materialism

                                                        Merits
                                                        Dream Sorcery [4]
                                                        Dreamtide Lair [6] (Mushroom Kingdom)
                                                        - Note: The Mushroom Kingdom is an Island in the Dreamtide which Bowser and his Cult have taken over
                                                        Cult [5] Koopas and Goombas (Note: Dreamtide creatures)
                                                        Retainer [3] Kammy Koopa

                                                        Ablutions
                                                        Scales [5]
                                                        Spines [3]
                                                        Inferno [3]
                                                        Roar [2]
                                                        Talons [2]
                                                        Colossal [3]

                                                        Purviews (needs work)
                                                        Money [3] - ? ?
                                                        ? [] - ??

                                                        Other Traits
                                                        Heartstring [5]
                                                        Conviction [10] (Full-On Hubris)
                                                        Conditions:
                                                        - Hunted (Mario & Luigi)
                                                        - Madness
                                                        - Missing Emotion: Conscience (Traded to Deep One)
                                                        - Human-Guise degradation: skin and hair becoming noticeably abnormal, while his hands become increasingly shriveled and claw-like.

                                                        Other
                                                        Peach would be a 3-dot maiden, but is lost merit dots, seeing as she hates him now.
                                                        The Kids would all be well-groomed Pilot Lights, with Baby Bowser the inherritor-apparent.
                                                        Mario would be a Hunter with Lucid Dream and Dream Travel.

                                                        Bio
                                                        Bowser got his Heart a long time ago, but due to the Reimagining, it's no longer important: the man he used to be is long gone. The CEO has been considered a successful businessman with a reputation for ruthlessness and self-importance, but was able to maintain his composure in private. He had a beautiful and kind wife named Peach, his "princess", who kept his head level at the worst of times (Maiden 3) Their best friends were a pair of brothers (Hunters) who knew his secret and shared the couple's love for Go Karting, and the 4 of them had built their own private island in the Dreamtide. It was the presidential bid where he went too far: by running under the platform of a childish bigot, people began to see him as a rambling egomaniac, and soon, the weight of more than a nation full of people afflicted him with the Reimagining, turning him into a monster.

                                                        Bowser used to be close to Peach, and would call her his "princess", but she turned on him when his Hubris became to great and his life in the Dreamtide made him increasingly less human. When he traded his Conscience to the Deep Ones, that was the final straw. When she tried to leave, he kidnapped her in a dream, dragging her off into his lair, putting her waking body in a persistent coma. Her mind is now trapped in a prison in the Dreamtide, and she will never wake up unless she can escape.

                                                        Mario is a Hunter with Lucid Dream, Dream Travel, and a few other merits which make him nigh-unkillable in the upper-Dreamtide. He and his brother Luigi (another Hunter with similar stats) are now trying to get Peach back, and reform Bowser from his old ways, but they're starting to realize that he may be too far gone, and Mario is struggling with the fear that he may have to kill his old friend at this point...

                                                        Bowser is currently trying to keep control of the Mushroom Kingdom, while simultaneously attempting to win the US Presidential Election. As it goes on, however, he falls more and more into madness, especially as comedians attempting to satire him continue to invoke the Reimagining: for example, constant satirical comparisons to Hitler have turned him into a Hitler sympathizer. As he changes, their satire gets more extreme, catching him in a never ending feedback loop.

                                                        Meanwhile, unbeknownst to them, the ripples of what's going on in the Mushroom Kingdom are slowly making their way across the Dreamtide. Both the Waking and Pandemonium are beginning to be affected by their little private war, but much more troubling to some scholars among the Deep Ones is that the ripples are gaining strength as the battle wages on, and if they were to reach What Lies Beyond, the realm where even the eldest of the Deep Ones do not dare to trespass.... The Deep Ones aren't exactly the kind to just hope that the Old Gods are heavy sleepers.
