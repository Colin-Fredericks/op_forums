              Do we have a firm enough date on the drying period to say that for sure one way or the other? Wikipedia unhelpfully places the time of the green sahara between 11,000 to 5,000 years ago. Osiris died in 5293 BC (he was killed on a Sothic Turn, and the next Turn is when the Rite of Return is invoked in 3832 BC, so working backwards...hurray math). The Shan'iatu spend a few centuries after murdering their god just sort of moping and hanging out beside the river, and we don't know when exactly they get the band back together and build the Nameless Empire. Mid-fourth millenia, presumably. From my understanding the Sahara desert probably does exist at this time, but the border between desert and savanna is much farther north than it is now (in other words, its much smaller). But that could still mean Irem is surrounded by deserts- references to the Sphinx in Dreams of Avarice imply it was built somewhere near ancient Heliopolis and modern Cairo. This could put it far enough North to be within reach of our baby Sahara.
