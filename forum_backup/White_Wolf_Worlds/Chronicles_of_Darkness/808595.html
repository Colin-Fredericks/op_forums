    In the Name of Torquemada [Dark Era, Brainstorming]

    01-14-2016, 08:34 AM
    The Dark Era Kickstarter has presented us a number of possible Eras, although many of them didn't managed to get in- and one of those unchosen Eras was the Spanish Inquisition. While I've toyed with developing it in the past, I didn't had a lot of time and lost interest. With the new Hunter 2e Open Dev posts and representation of Cabals, I've regained inspiration in the Era again, and wondered if it may be a good chance to complete my old idea.

    As the title of the thread suggest, I'm taking inspiration from Slasher for that Era, basing it around the fiction of the Lucifuge in the book (that is, Theleme's encounter with Brother Alphonso). While the fiction present him as a lone case, I've toyed with Cabals (in concept, if not in name) a number of times before- and from that came the idea for the Innocents, a dark cancer inside the Inquisition who seek heresy everywhere and kill in the name of a god they no longer believe in.

    The basic premise of the setting is that just as the Inquisition hunts down heretics among the Catholics, the Innocents hunts them among the Hunters- anyone who isn't "pure enough" is chased down, although the definition of "pure" is subjective: The Innocents goal is to define what does it mean to be an "Hunter". Anyone who is working with witches, demons and other creatures of the darkness should die, no matter how small the offence is. People are going to have to choose sides- either they follow the fanatic dogma of the Innocents or they are branded as heretics and doomed to the fire (and that is with an addition for being Catholic, of course). Some groups join their goal, others try to lay low or escape- and those who fail are tortured and killed. In sort, the Innocents don't hunt monsters: they hunt people, and they are more than willing to justify their killings with fabricated evidence and the fear they inspire.

    On the other side of the map, you have the Heirs of the Crown: warlock and demonologists who bind demons to their flesh. They are, by any standard, heretics- yet they are also hunters. While they may do it for selfish reasons, they banish demons and free the possessed from the clutches of Hell. However, because of their heretic faith, they are hunted down by the Church- and they are not alone. The Lucifuge, long time enemies of the Heirs, find themselves escaping the same fire. The Ascending Ones are persecuted for their muslim faith and pagan elixirs. The Aegis Kai Duro pack their relics and leaves Spain in search for mythical treasures. However, just the same people support the dark crusade- Acheron Shipping & Trading Guild may be doing some very bad things to themselves and others, but they have enough money to make the Innocents close their eyes in exchange for their help. The Cainte Heresy, a very different organization from how it works today, also support the Inquisition out of their fanatic fear from vampires in the Church. And among that chaos, certain new groups arise- the foundations for the future Silent Imperative are laid by the Lady of Milan, the Scarlet Watch's founding families meet together with some "help" from the Innocents and the Malleus Maleficarum are eventually formed in order to replace the Black Cabal- but while new candles start to burn, they also cast long shadows upon the world. While in the service of the Imperative, three of the Lucifuge's agents are getting captured and tortured by the Innocents, their eyes are blinded in the process. Blaming the Lady for their suffering, they turn to their parents for help and escape. They haven't forgot their pain, and neither they forgave the one who sent them to their doom.

    This is not a setting for blurring the lines and fighting the inhuman. This setting is about harsh decisions, about black and white- either you are an Innocent or you are an heretic, and while hunters butcher one another in the attempt to decide who is worthy for that title and who is not- the monsters celebrate, slipping through the cracks and cast their lot with either of the sides: after all, with so much blood being spilled, you should not be surprised that the scavengers start to gather with their hungry eyes. It is an holy war without gods, an heresy which no one dare to speak. Hide your children and stay always on the guard- for when the Innocents march the streets, everyone is guilty.

    After all, not even the Vigilant can expect the Spanish Inquisition.




    (suggestions, thoughts and ideas are more than welcomed!)

                      Thanks for the compliments

                      And yes, the Innocents are Hunters who went out of control and turned into a Cabal (the "Black Cabal" may be the origin for the word in order to describe an organized group of Slashers in that setting). About the name- I am aware of the Innocents book and the Children's Crusade, but I think that the name is good for them because the dissonance it creates and the way it is used by the blue book (and yeah, the Black Cabal is their subtitle, like the Secret Frequency for Network 0). We can avoid confusion by using the Spanish term (Los Inocentes).

                      I think that Torquemada would be something similar to the Pope Paul- a symbolic figure with some loose connection to their formation, but not a direct leader or founder. I want to avoid the "Beethoven was an Alien" trope. They respect the Grand Inquisitor, but have their own leadership. I also think that considering the range of the Inquisition's influence, they may actually be a third tier Cabal, Endowments and all, but this is still a matter of development.

                      And about other Compacts of the Era- I'm still not sure. I need to read more about the Inquisition, decide on the actual time frame, think about the role of the existing groups, etc. Considering this is an homebrew and we don't have the Dark Era's tight wordcount per chapter, we can detail new groups without a specific problem- but for that, you need to make research. If you, or anyone else, has ideas for such groups and other ways to develop the Era, I'll be more than glad.
