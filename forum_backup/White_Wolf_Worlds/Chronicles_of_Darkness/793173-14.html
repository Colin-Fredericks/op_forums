                The Dream Eating Vermin

                There is a crawling, many-armed thing that lives in your house. You don't know it because it hides very well.

                It watches you when you are sleeping, and keeps the things that go bump in the night away so you can rest easy. Sometimes it plays tricks on and leaves gifts for you while you are awake to try and cheer you up so you will have good dreams.

                In return for its protection, it eats pieces of your dreams to sustain itself. Sometimes it gets bloated and happy with what it eats, and leaves little eggs for you as thanks for letting it stay.

                Protect these eggs. Bad things happen to those who don't.

                Best At: 10 (Protecting Those Under Its Care, Hiding And Playing Tricks)
                Worst At: 2 (Communicating Verbally, Understanding The Nuances Of What Humans Enjoy)
                All Other Pools: 5
                Aspiration: Ensure People Rest Well So It Can Eat Their Dreams
                Willpower/Scene: 6
                Initiative: 10
                Defense: 5
                Speed: 15 (Flight 25)
                Health: 12

                Weapons/Attacks
                Very, Very Cold Proboscis [+1L, Armor Piercing 1, Melee, 10 Dice]

                Bane: Sunlight
                Ban: Can only eat the dreams of those who have indulged their Vice at least once within the week

                Dread Powers: Chameleon Horror, Influence (Vice 1), Madness And Terror, Natural Weapon 1, Numen (Drain, Hallucination), Surprise Entrance

                EDIT: Should I write the eggs as a Relic? I imagine they grow into more instances of this Brief Nightmare, but offer good luck while you protect them.

                                Re: The Bogeyman

                                I set the up the Bogeyman here. I used the Hobgoblin creation rules from the Changeling 2E preview,so I figured that write up was more comfortable over there.

                                Re: Slashers

                                Since it it will be a while before we see the Slasher rules from Hunter 2E, I am using a modified version of the Horror rules for the next write ups. Here are my modification notes -

                                In most cases, Slashers are still (mostly) human. Consider “paranatural” slashers, ones that are still capable of presenting themselves as regular individuals, to be equivalent to Potency 1-2 Horrors. While “supernatural” slashers, ones that have abandoned any pretense of normalcy are equivalent to Potency 3-4 Horrors.
                                Slasher trade raw power for more cunning and resources. Slasher have a Trait Limit of 5 for paranatural slashers and 6 for supernatural Slashers. In exchange, Slasher gain 5+Potency additional dots of skills and 5+Potency additional dots of Merits. In addition, Slashers receive one fewer Dread Powers than an equivalent Horror and have the talent and frailty appropriate to their Undertaking.
                                Ashley Chase
                                Ashley Chase is a young woman who has been plagued by tragedy her entire life. From her sister’s suicide, to the botched robbery that ended the lives of her parents, to the accidental overdose that killed her grandmother, she has faced this adversity with bravery, good cheer, and faith. Of course, she has been quietly behind each of these incidents, growing bolder and more inventive with her plans as time goes by. She has cultivated a group of friends that share a taste for true crime and other macabre fare, who she hasn’t grown bored with yet. Her appreciable inheritance has allowed her to begin college for few concerns about tuition, and she has plans to join a sorority. Once she is more educated (both academically and practically), she will resume her “hobby” in earnest.
                                Ashley is a tall, slender blond with blue eyes, who dresses in a fashionable if somewhat conservative manner. She plays the part of a cheerful and pious young woman, and keeps her darker interests a well-guarded secret.


                                Undertaking: Genius
                                Virtue: Cautious; Vice: Vindictive
                                Aspiration: Arrange the perfect murder
                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                                Physical Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
                                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 4, Composure 3
                                Skills: Academics 3, Athletics 1, Empathy 3, Investigation 3, Larceny 2, Socialize 2, Stealth 3, Subterfuge 4, Weaponry 1
                                Merits: Eidetic Memory, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Investigation), Hobbyist Clique (The Murder Club-Larceny), Patient, Resources 3, Striking Looks 1 (Pretty), Untouchable
                                Potency: 2
                                Willpower: 9
                                Size: 5
                                Initiative: 6
                                Defense: 4
                                Speed: 10
                                Health: 8
                                Weapons/Attacks
                                Unarmed [+0B melee, 2 dice]
                                Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 3 dice]
                                Talent: Profiling; Frailty: Intolerance for Chaos
                                Dread Powers: Hypnotic Gaze, Know Soul

                                Profiling: Even on casual observation, a Genius can predict what a given person will do in a basic way based on appearance, body language and bearing. The Genius’ player rolls Intelligence + Empathy, contested by the targets Manipulation + Subterfuge. If the Genius succeeds, he has a good enough understanding of the person to predict his actions. The Genius wins all ties in contested rolls against the target(s), and always wins initiative against him.

                                Intolerance for Chaos: Geniuses might understand on some level that they can’t allow for every freak of chance, but in practice, they get frustrated if they miss something or if something happens that they couldn’t have predicted. If a Genius’ player fails an
                                Intelligence roll, the Genius loses two points of Willpower.

                                The Gravedigger
                                The man called “The Gravedigger” lives in a dilapidated house squatting on a hill over looking a large property with a large collection of rusted and broken vehicles and an old cemetery occupying a remote corner of the acreage. The house and property serve as a focal point of the local community, who have long since abandoned conventional religion to secretly worship a God of Harvest and Sacrifice. The Gravedigger is his chosen servant. Made strong by the will of the God he provides the flesh and blood that must be interred in the soil to keep the crops growing, and the God appeased.
                                The Gravedigger is nearly 7 ft tall and weighs over 300lbs. His craggy face is scarred and his eyes are dark. He wears a denim coverall and a hooded coat.


                                Undertaking: Brute
                                Virtue: Dutiful; Vice: Jealous
                                Aspiration: To serve the will of the Harvest God
                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 4
                                Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 3, Stamina 5
                                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 4
                                Skills: Animal Ken 2(Dogs), Athletics 2, Brawl 4, Crafts 3, Firearms 3(Rifle), Intimidate 4, Stealth 1, Weaponry 3(Improvised)
                                Merits: Choke Hold, Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Hardy 2, Indomitable, Iron Will, Style: Improvised Weaponry 1
                                Potency: 2
                                Willpower: 10
                                Size: 6
                                Initiative: 7
                                Armor: 1/0 [Reinforced Clothing]
                                Defense: 7
                                Speed: 14
                                Health: 11
                                Weapons/Attacks
                                Unarmed [+0B melee, 9 dice]
                                Shovel [+1L melee;-3 Initiative; 9 dice; Knockdown]
                                Rifle [+4L ranged; -5 Initiative; 7 dice]
                                Talent: Unstoppable; Frailty: Blinded by Blood
                                Dread Powers: Jump Scare, Surprise Entrance

                                Unstoppable: Brutes do not suffer wound penalties of any kind, and need not make the reflexive roll to stay conscious when the Brute’s Health track is full of bashing damage. Instead, when the Brute’s track fills with lethal damage, the player makes a reflexive Stamina roll to remain conscious and active. If this roll fails, the Brute falls unconscious and begins to bleed out. If it succeeds, the Brute can remain active until he suffers one more wound, at which point he is Incapacitated.

                                Blinded by Blood: All Perception rolls made for Brutes suffer a –3 penalty, and on opposed rolls made to notice a hiding opponent, ties always go against the Brute.

                                Mr. Suit
                                Mr. Suit is a name given to a suspected serial killer by the FBI agents that have been tracking the case for over 15 years. Unlike many such killers, who display obsessive and repetitive behaviors, Mr. Suit frequently switches methods and tactics; at different points using blades, guns, strangulation and even explosives to dispatch his victims. If not for his habit of periodically leaving a witness alive, it is likely his murders would never have been connected to each other. So far 18 known victims have been attributed to Mr. Suit.
                                Mr. Suit is described as a tall, athletic man who wears a well-tailored suit (typically black or gray). He sports black gloves and an apron if the work is going to be messy. He always wears a mask; the masks design varies but he seems to favor a rubber gas mask.


                                Undertaking: Psycho
                                Virtue: Clever; Vice: Competitive
                                Aspiration: To be the most successful killer that ever lived
                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                                Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3
                                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 4, Composure 4
                                Skills: Academics 2, Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Crafts 2(Explosives), Firearms 3, Investigation 3(FBI procedure), Stealth 3(evade pursuit), Streetwise 2, Subterfuge 3, Weaponry 4
                                Merits: Allies 2 (FBI), Defensive Combat (Weaponry), Indomitable, Resources 4, Style: Fast Talking 3, Style: Light Weapons 2, Untouchable
                                Potency: 3
                                Willpower: 11
                                Size: 5
                                Initiative: 8
                                Defense: 8
                                Speed: 13
                                Health: 8
                                Weapons/Attacks
                                Unarmed [+0B melee, 7 dice]
                                Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 8 dice]
                                9mm Pistol [+1L ranged; 7 dice]
                                Talent: Deadly Distraction; Frailty: Obsessive
                                Dread Powers: Hunter’s Senses (victims), Jump Scare

                                Deadly Distraction: When the Psycho engages a target in conversation, they can cause the target to drop their guard enough for the Psycho to exploit the moment. This cannot be attempted in combat or when other witnesses are about. The Storyteller rolls Presence + Persuasion, contested by the victim’s Wits + Composure. If the Psycho’s is successful, the target lowers their defenses. Depending on what the Psycho is trying to accomplish, this might allow them to access to a building to recover a weapon or tamper with evidence. If the slasher wishes to attack the victim, he can make a Killing Blow with whatever weapon he has at hand, inflicting damage equal to the relevant dice pool with no roll required. This might not actually kill the victim, but it certainly inflicts a terrible wound, leaving the victim at the Psycho’s mercy.

                                Obsessive: Psychos just can’t let something go. Once a Psycho chooses a victim, and attempts to charm her (either by using Deadly Distraction or mundane Social attempts) and fails, the Psycho cannot just leave her be. He must break her down, hurt her, scare her and finally kill her. The Psycho can be patient, but most of them aren’t equipped for it, and wind up impulsively attacking the victim (who is by no means unsuspecting and helpless). If the victim inflicts damage upon the Psycho, the Frailty’s effects end and he can flee. The Psycho will eventually return to finish the job, however, which is problematic for the victim, but can also be used to track or trap the Psycho.

                                    Slashers

                                    Where that book said no, Frailty chronicles says a resounding “Yes!”
                                    That is because were that book defined Slashers solely in context to Hunter’s that prey upon mere humans, we are looking at it as a phenomenon universal to all beings with a soul, dealing with extremely painful isolation from the mundane world and irrevocable severing of their capacity for the most important type of relationship.

                                    Slashers are horrors which were once beings with freewill and a soul but have lost all emotional ties that tether their psyche to the mundane world so it floats, off to the dizzying altitudes of utter madness and reduce them to mere puppets to monstrous instincts that lay at the core of every persons’ being. Slasher are the monsters that have lost the spark which is the source of having a conscious, a soul, and thus freewill.

                                    There are two tiers for becoming a slasher, a Lesser Undertaking and a Greater Undertaking; the first is gained when an ensoulled character only has one Stability Advantage and the second is gained when the character is reduced to zero Stability Advantage. While both Undertakings grant power they come with a steep metaphysical price, freewill and even being considered an ensoulled being; In concrete game terms ensoulled beings are the only ones suitable to be player characters and gaining the Greater Undertaking makes the character a NPC which should not be played by its original player. (This is important for those that play LARP to keep the mechanic as a meaningful loss caused by the behaviors that lead to becoming a slasher, sever emotional ties the other players may have had to the character while portrayed by the player in question, and represent the startling internal change of the character that would deeply disturb those that once knew them).

                                    A Character with only a Lesser Undertaking is flirting with total corruption but still has a chance to be redeemed. If they gain a Support which raises their Stability Advantage to two or greater they lose all lose the Lesser Undertaking. An impressive story arch for a character can be seeking Vengeance for a Support and losing Support relationships along the way until they gain a Lesser Undertaking and either come back from the brink through the strength of their remaining Support, their supernatural friends, or something within themselves. Having tasted that power, woe to those that transgress to far against such a character because they are faced with the temptation of going down the road again when their Supports are killed.

                                    There are five Lesser Undertaking and Five Greater Undertakings, feel free to mix and match. There are “natural” combination, and those are as follows: Avenger/Legend, Brute/Mask, Charmer/Psycho, Freak/Mutant, Genius/Maniac.


                                    Lesser Undertakings
                                    All those with a Lesser Undertakings gain the Jump Scare dread power on page: CoD 146
                                    They also add the following Tag every kill of an ensoulled being they take part in.

                                    Calling Card
                                    There is a clue which conveys a message for potential investigators. The message must be a hint towards one of the Slasher’s Aspirations. Upon discovering the Clue the investigator understands the message, which is usually a leap in logic to understand the connection of several facts known to the investigator about the killing. Noticing a Calling Card requires an exceptional success on a roll using Investigation on the records or evidence of the killing.

                                    Sample Messages:
                                    Pack Aspiration: “Defend the family from outside attacks.” Resulting Message: “”Family is very important”
                                    Guardians of the Veil Aspiration: “Maintain the conspiracy protecting the supernatural from the wilting touch of the mundane “ Resulting Message: “Anything seemingly supernatural is a hoax.”
                                    Daeva Aspiration: Gather love and adoration of others” Resulting message: “Being the center of someone’s world is all that matters.”
                                    Lost Aspiration: “Never be trapped in Arcadia again.” Resulting Message: “Imprisonment is worse than death.
                                    Personal Aspiration: “Defeat a worthy opponent.” Resulting Message: “This is an offer to play a deadly game with investigators.”

                                    List of Lesser Undertakings
                                    Avenger
                                    Kills to avenge right or imagined wrong. These slashers gain the Hunter’s Sense dread power on page: CoD 148 This is always focused on their preferred prey.

                                    Brute
                                    Brute is big guy (or gal) who just wants to kill. Not very smart, and also very crude, he just kills. But he can still understand the potential risks, and can plan his attack. As a benefit brute doesn't feel pain. These slashers gain the Unbreakable dread power on page: CoD 148

                                    Charmer
                                    Charmer is nice guy who charms others and lures them into false sense of security. When time is right, he strikes. Charmer's benefit is that he if he makes good first impression, person will believe him, even if it is against common sense or logic. These slashers gain the Hypnotic Gaze dread power on page: CoD 145

                                    Freak
                                    Every freak has his own reason for killing, but it is usually related to his appearance or what it has caused. As a benefit, Freak quickly learn his surroundings. These slashers gain the Surprise Entrance dread power on page: CoD 148

                                    Genius
                                    Genius kills his targets with his intellect. He tends to play mind games with his enemies and lure them into trap, but they can't relate to worms that are beneath them. As a benefit, Genius can profile target when he is met. These slashers gain the Know Soul dread power on page: CoD 146

                                    Greater Undertakings
                                    All those with a Greater undertaking gain the following persistent conditions that can’t be resolved:

                                    Obvious Lunatic
                                    It is obvious to anyone not on the brink of becoming a Slasher themselves that you are dangerously without moral compunction or need to comply with even the most fundamental social norms. -5 to any social interaction with any being with more than 1 Stability Advantage except hostile intimidation.

                                    Putrefied Metaphysical Miasma
                                    Ephemeral Beings, Horrors, Major Templates, Minor Templates, and Complete Templates with two Stability Advantage or higher experience an extremely unpleasant sensation when in your presence, a profound impression of your “wrongness” and they feel no instinctual hesitation toward engaging in violence against you. For those with minor, major or complete templates the Compunction, Hesitation and PTSD rules do not apply when dealing with you.

                                    List of Greater Undertakings
                                    Legend
                                    Legends have become one with their urban myth and now their actions conform to seemingly arbitrary rules that children make rhymes out of. These slashers gain the Immortal dread power on page: CoD 145 . Additionally they gain a simple Ban for every Bane they possess, Personal or otherwise.

                                    Mask
                                    Masks aare nothing more than killing machines and are often portrayed as wearing a mask because it remove the appearance of any human emotion. Even when a Mask doesn’t wear an actual mask their face is speechless and expressionless. These slashers gain the Regenerate: 1 dread power on page: CoD 148. Masks are completely incapable of most social interaction. They are unable to Open Doors in social contests against others and their doors may only be opened when confronted with their Banes and/or Bans, to put it into mechanical terms. They also have the Mute Condition which may not be resolved.

                                    Mutant
                                    Twisted into aberrant shape by their own corruption the Mutant sheds the camouflage of appearing human, because they don’t, and becomes visibly identifiable as a monster. These slashers gain the Natural Weapons: 3 dread power on page: CoD 147. Mutants automatically gain the Hunted Condition when in urban and semi-urban environments.

                                    Psycho
                                    The Psycho is always a Slasher heavily tied to a location and broken relationships. It may be his family home and motel where his mother emotionally abused him until he murdered her, a lakeside campground where her son was neglected and bullied to death, or the neighborhood where he looked to ‘gain his manhood’ from a prostitute that scorned him and his violent insecurities remade him into a killer. These slashers gain the Homeground dread power on page: CoD 145. If this Slasher ever intends to kill someone they gain the Obsession Condition focused on killing that person until they believe that person is dead.

                                    Maniac
                                    Some Slasher have a way of getting into the minds of other. Maniacs are masterminds or demagogues that manipulate or radicalize other to cause the death and carnage they crave. These Slashers gain the Implant Mission numina as a dread power on page: CoD 137. Each one has a specific die pool appropriate for their machinations, chosen then they gain this Undertaking. If a Maniac fails in an attempt to use Implant Mission against someone the Maniac Gains the Competitive Condition in relationship to that person.
                                    So using that system a Slasher with a combination of Lesser and Greater Undertakings has three dread powers putting them on part with potency 1-3 Horrors. Of course the above is written presuming my homebrew which has Atrocity, a universal applicability for Personal Banes and a version of Hell is Other people updated to second edition and made universally applicable as well, but for a general guide for assigning Dread Powers for Slashers, it's handy.

                                            A few more slashers - I am aiming to have a Slasher for each archetype.
                                            Hell’s Belle
                                            As the story goes, a young woman was stranded on the side of a lonely desert road, her car broken down and the nearest town a few hours away. While trying to flag down help, she was instead set upon by a roving gang of criminals, who kidnapped her and spent days abusing and torturing her before leaving her for dead. But she survived her ordeal and gained a burning desire for revenge. One by one, she tracked down and killed each one of her assailants, before vanishing into the night behind the wheel the gangs leader’s muscle car. She is still out there, they say. Prowling the night, seeking the sort of men that have committed acts of violence and cruelty against women. While her goals are sympathetic, her methods are brutal and sadistic. She prefers to use a heavy revolver and stained baseball bat, but will use any tool necessary to enact her revenge. Belle is an athletic, statuesque beauty with cropped black hair and green eyes. Her flesh is crisscrossed with raised scars that seem to accentuate her looks. Her clothing is rugged jeans and tee shirts and she sports a silver crucifix, stained with so much blood it appears red. She wanders the roadways of the southwest in a black Ford Mustang.


                                            Undertaking: Legend; Virtue: Survivor; Vice: Vengeful
                                            Aspiration: Seek Revenge upon “Bad Men”
                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 5
                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 4, Stamina 5
                                            Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 2, Composure 4
                                            Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 4, Drive 4(Muscle Car), Firearms 3(Pistols), Intimidate 3(Men), Investigate 2, Stealth 2, Streetwise 2, Weaponry 4(Clubs)
                                            Merits: Crack Driver 3, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Iron Stamina 2, Iron Will, Striking Looks 2(Dangerous Beauty), Style: Street Fighting 2, Style: Stunt Driver 2
                                            Potency: 3
                                            Willpower: 12
                                            Size: 5
                                            Initiative: 8
                                            Defense: 8
                                            Speed: 13
                                            Health: 10
                                            Weapons/Attacks
                                            Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
                                            Baseball Bat [+1L melee;-1 Initiative; 9 dice]
                                            Heavy Revolver [+2L ranged;-2 Initiative; 8 dice]
                                            Talent: Strength from the Tales (any act of violence against a woman); Frailty: Trapped in the Story (Ban: Cannot harm a Woman (but can defend herself), Bane: Cactus Flower)
                                            Dread Powers: Immortal, Surprise Entrance

                                            Strength from the Tales: The Legend can draw strength from those acting in accordance with her myth. Once per scene, when someone fulfills one of the tenets of the slasher’s supernatural “rules,” the Storyteller can roll Presence + Intimidate. Successes on this roll can be applied to one of the following: restoring spent Willpower, healing damage (one success for a bashing wound, two for a lethal, three for an aggravated wound) or applying bonuses to rolls made in pursuit of the Legend’s agenda. The Legend does not have to use the successes in the same way each time, but cannot split the successes on any given chapter.

                                            Trapped in the Story: All Legends have a weakness. and they come in two forms: Bans and Banes. The Legend selects one of each. A Ban is either a compulsion or a behavioral restriction. These occur in certain situations unique to each Legend. A Bane is a weakness similar to those many supernatural creatures possess, something that can cause the Legend great harm. Banes cut instantly through all forms of armor, and damage suffered from a bane cannot be healed until the bane it is removed.

                                            The Kind Fellow
                                            The Kind Fellow considers what he does to be a higher calling. To those that are in pain and suffer, he frees them from this life to continue on to a better place. He moves from place to place, adopting new names and using false documents to gain employment. He prefers to work in hospitals, retirement homes, and hospices, typically as a nurse, orderly, or even janitor. He ingratiates himself to the patient, subtly convincing them that death is the only way out. He prefers to kill using gentler methods, such as suffocation or poison, but will strangle if necessary. The Kind Fellow sees stabbing and bludgeoning as needlessly cruel, and will rarely employ these methods.
                                            “The Kind Fellow” is a pleasant faced, slightly balding, dark haired man in his mid-thirties. He has a stout build, but cultivates a slightly paunchy physique to appear less intimidating. He changes hair style or sports glasses or other accessories to conceal his true identity.


                                            Undertaking: Charmer; Virtue: Caring; Vice: Envious
                                            Aspiration: Help the Sick and Injured
                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
                                            Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
                                            Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 3 (Grapple), Crafts 1, Empathy 3, Medicine 2(Poison), Persuasion 3(Patients), Science 1(Chemistry), Stealth 3,Subterfuge 2(Impersonation), Weaponry 2
                                            Merits: Anonymity 1, Choke Hold, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Numbing Touch 2, Professional Training 2 (Orderly; Brawl, Medicine), Style: Grappling 2, Sympathetic
                                            Potency: 2
                                            Willpower: 9
                                            Size: 5
                                            Initiative: 6
                                            Defense: 6
                                            Speed: 12
                                            Health: 8
                                            Weapons/Attacks
                                            Unarmed [+0B melee, 7 dice]
                                            Talent: Disarming; Frailty: Thin Veneer (Rebuking his “Kindness”)
                                            Dread Powers: Home Ground (Hospitals), Hunter’s Senses (The Dying)

                                            Disarming: When a Charmer meets a new person, the Storyteller rolls Presence + a Social Skill. The Skill might be Expression, Socialize or Persuasion. This roll is contested by the listener’s Intelligence + Subterfuge. If the Charmer wins, that victim gains the Charmed Condition.
                                            Note that Charmers get one chance at this for a given person. First impressions are everything. If the Charmer fails to meet or exceed the target’s successes, she sees through the Charmer to the Psycho beneath. She might just feel that the Charmer is a phony, not necessarily dangerous, but the warning bells definitely go off (which probably makes her the Charmer’s first target).

                                            Thin Veneer: All Charmers have a trigger, chosen by the Storyteller. This might be bringing up a certain topic, a particular song, being touched in a sexual manner, or just being called by name rather than by title. When this trigger occurs, or when someone who resists the slasher’s charms calls him out (“You’re fucking creepy, dude” is enough), the Storyteller rolls Composure. If this roll fails, the slasher lashes out — usually verbally, but sometimes physically. At this point, anyone who fell under the Charmer’s wiles before has a chance to snap out of it (players roll Wits + Subterfuge; success means the Charmed Condition is resolved).

                                                More Slashers
                                                Red John
                                                Even as a boy, John Everett was a strange individual. Despite a fairly typical suburban upbringing by moderately religious parents, John was noted for sardonic sense of humor, fondness for pranks, and problems with authority. When John was sixteen, his family perished in a house fire (which was due to faulty wiring) and he himself suffered severe, disfiguring burns. After recovering from his injuries, John began to display curious abilities, such as heightened strength, resistance to pain, and a strange affinity with fire. He claims that the flame speaks to him, and compels him to burn away the world to make room for a new beginning. He has little regard for the life and well being of others, and will burn people as readily as he burns objects and structures. After getting kicked out of the foster care system for acts of arson and vandalism, John has become a drifter, supporting himself with odd jobs and petty theft.
                                                Red John is a plain-faced young man, with pale, freckled skin and bright red hair he keeps at shoulder length. He has ugly burn scars on this hands, arms and left side of his face, and dresses in dark clothing.


                                                Undertaking: Freak; Virtue: Mirthful; Vice: Rebellious
                                                Aspiration: Burn down the old to make way for the new
                                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                                                Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3
                                                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
                                                Skills: Athletics 2, Computers 2, Brawl 3, Expression 2(Jokes), Larceny 3, Persuasion 2 (Pity), Science 1 (Fire), Streetwise 3 (Homeless), Stealth 2, Subterfuge 2
                                                Merits: Anonymity 2, Iron Stamina 1, Professional Training 2 (Criminal; Larceny, Streetwise), Psychokinesis 3 (Fire), Style: Fast Talking 3, Untouchable
                                                Potency: 2
                                                Willpower: 9
                                                Size: 5
                                                Initiative: 7
                                                Armor: 1/1
                                                Defense: 6
                                                Speed: 14
                                                Health: 9
                                                Weapons/Attacks
                                                Unarmed [+0B melee, 8 dice]
                                                Talent: Revulsion (body heat); Frailty: Deformity (burn scars)
                                                Dread Powers: Fire Elemental, Numina (Firestarter)

                                                Revulsion: Fighting a Freak is hard, because it might mean touching them. All attempts to grapple or touch a Freak in combat suffer a -3 penalty. Once a grapple is established, attempts to immobilize or perform other maneuvers incur a -1 penalty, and the grappling character’s Strength is considered one lower for purposes of escape attempts.

                                                Deformity: All Freaks are hideous to the eye. No matter what the source of their ugliness, birth or accident, normal people cannot help but stare at them. Many mock them, or shun them thinking that their disgusting visage is contagious (and maybe it is). Apply a –2 penalty on all Social rolls if the other parties involved can clearly perceive the Freak’s Deformity, and treat any Presence and Manipulation failures as dramatic failures. This penalty does not apply to Intimidation attempts, of course.

                                                Wyatt James
                                                Wyatt James was once a member of an organization called “The Church of Revelation”. He and his wife joined the organization as a way to cope with trauma he experienced while serving in the military. The Church of Revelation was an apocalyptic cult, preparing for the impending End of Days. After moving to the Churches sprawling compound, Wyatt became disenchanted (and frightened) by the increasingly radical tone of the churches sermons. When he and his wife tried to escape, they met with armed resistance, and she was killed in the attempt.
                                                Over the course of a year, Wyatt murdered the leaders of the organization, and burned its compound to ashes.
                                                Wyatt continues his crusade, rooting out and extinguishing cults and organizations that he deems “false” or “evil”. He tries to limit the carnage to leaders and organizers, but more than a few lay members have been caught in the crossfire, which only compels Wyatt to make sure “their sacrifice was not in vain”.
                                                Wyatt James is a solid, athletic man in his early thirties with dark hair and brown eyes. He crops his hair close, wears a full beard and favors rugged practical clothing.


                                                Undertaking: Avenger; Virtue: Faithful; Vice: Unforgiving
                                                Aspiration: To destroy those that pervert or corrupt the Gospel
                                                Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 3, Resolve 4
                                                Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 4, Stamina 4
                                                Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 3
                                                Skills: Academics 1(Bible), Athletics 2, Brawl 3, Crafts 2(Explosives), Drive 2, Firearms 4(Pistols), Intimidate 2, Investigate 3, Occult 1(Cults), Stealth 2(Surveillance), Subterfuge 2, Survival 2(Doomsday Prep), Weaponry 3
                                                Merits: Defensive Combat (Brawl), Iron Stamina 2, Iron Will, Professional Training 3 (Soldier; Crafts, Firearms, Survival), Style: Close Quarters Combat 2, Style: Firefight 2
                                                Potency: 2
                                                Willpower: 9
                                                Size: 5
                                                Initiative: 7
                                                Armor: 1/3 [Kevlar Vest]
                                                Defense: 6
                                                Speed: 13
                                                Health: 9
                                                Weapons/Attacks
                                                Unarmed [+0B melee, 7 dice]
                                                Knife [+0L melee;-1 Initiative; 7 dice]
                                                Heavy Pistol [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 9 dice; 9 again]
                                                Talent: Working the Room; Frailty: Nothing but the Mission
                                                Dread Powers: Hunter’s Senses (Cultists)
                                                *Wyatt James exchanged a Dread Power for an additional 5+Potency points of Skills

                                                Working the Room: Avengers might try to kill their victims one by one, but sooner or later they wind up with a roomful of people that require their attention. The rules for Defense vs. multiple opponents do not apply to Avengers. No matter how many times in a turn an Avenger is attacked, apply his full Defense to all incoming attacks.

                                                Nothing but the Mission: Most slashers are obsessive, but Avengers take it to a whole new level. The Avenger has the Obsession Condition in regards to pursuing their chosen targets. When faced with the choice between pursuing a target and taking some other course of action, the Storyteller’s must roll Resolve + Composure. If this roll succeeds, the Avenger can choose to let the target go (for now). If the roll fails, the Avenger remains focused on his mission and ignores the other situation.

                                                    More Slashers

                                                    Eris
                                                    Simone Delacour, from even an early age, was as rebellious as she was intelligent. Her love of philosophy, mathematics, and especially computer sciences earned her admission to special schools for the gifted and talented, but her difficulties with those in positions of authority always made her an outsider. During her college years, she began associating with anarchist and discordian organizations, becoming increasingly radical overtime. She adopted the pseudonym “Eris”, and dedicated herself full-time to tearing down a society that she perceives as hopelessly flawed and corrupt. She is brilliant programmer, hacker, and blackmailer, and she uses the information she gathers to manipulate individuals, inflame conflicts, and erode institutions. She takes a particular delight in orchestrating the death or ruin of key individuals, then watching the often far-reaching fallout.
                                                    Eris is an African-American woman in her late thirties, with a dark complexion and close cropped hair. She dresses for comfort and has a “punk” sense of style. She is reclusive and lives comfortably on money earned through blackmail and laundered through various business ventures.


                                                    Undertaking: Maniac; Virtue: Idealistic; Vice: Anarchist
                                                    Aspiration: Disrupt the Status Quo
                                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 5, Resolve 4
                                                    Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
                                                    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 4, Composure 4
                                                    Skills: Academics 2(Philosophy), Athletics 1, Computers 5(Hacking, Viruses), Crafts 2, Firearms 2, Intimidation 2, Investigation 4(Blackmail), Persuasion 3, Politics 3, Science 2, Socialize (Anarchists)1, Subterfuge 2
                                                    Merits: Anonymity 3, Area of Expertise (Hacking), Investigative Aid (Computers), Professional Training 4 (Hacker; Computer, Investigation, Science), Resources 3, Safe Place 2, Untouchable
                                                    Potency: 3
                                                    Willpower: 11
                                                    Size: 5
                                                    Initiative: 7
                                                    Defense: 4
                                                    Speed: 11
                                                    Health: 8
                                                    Weapons/Attacks
                                                    Unarmed [+0B melee, 3 dice]
                                                    Heavy Pistol [+2L ranged; -2 Initiative; 5 dice]
                                                    Talent: Compelling Madness; Frailty: Obvious Lunatic
                                                    Dread Powers: Eye Spy (Hack Surveillance), Madness & Terror (Online Interaction)

                                                    Compelling Madness: Maniacs only need a few minutes of observation to know a victim better than he knows himself. If a Maniac observes a character for five minutes, the Maniac can profile him. The Maniac rolls Intelligence + Resolve resisted by the targets Wits+Subterfuge. If successful, this functions as the Know Soul Dread Power (Chronicles of Darkness, pg. 146).
                                                    In addition, if the target has a current Integrity of 5 or less, the Maniac may also use Social Maneuvering to influence that targets beliefs and goals; whenever the Maniac opens the targets final Door, they may spend a point of Willpower to substitute a targets Virtue, Vice or Aspiration for one of the Maniac’s own.

                                                    Obvious Lunatic: Maniacs are dangerous, and it’s obvious to anyone who talks to them. They can keep it under wraps for a few minutes, but beyond that time, people around them feel threatened. Maniacs suffer a –3 penalty to all Social actions with others, unless that person is susceptible to their logic (currently suffering the Guilty or Leveraged Condition, or having a similar Virtue, Vice or Aspiration). The Maniac may spend a point of Willpower to negate this penalty until the end of the scene.

                                                    Maria Muerte
                                                    The woman named “Maria” began her life abandoned on the steps of a church in Mexico City when she was only a few weeks old. The child was marked (some would say cursed) with a strange deformity that rendered her hairless, and with atrophied facial muscles that gave her a skull-like countenance and limited range of expression. Even at an early age, she demonstrated unnatural strength and outbursts of anger that left her peer terrified and the nuns convinced she was the spawn of demons, and all were relieved when she ran away from the orphanage as a teen. Since then, she has become an infamous enforcer for the Cartels, employing her inhuman strength to maim and kill at their behest. True, she would probably murder on a whim for free, but has come to appreciate money and notoriety.
                                                    Maria is a tall woman with a hard, muscular physique. She is completely hairless, and has thin lips, sunken eyes, and hollow cheeks which make her face a living skull. Her skin (except for her face) is covered in elaborate, beautiful, tattoos.


                                                    Undertaking: Mutant; Virtue: Bravery; Vice: Violence
                                                    Aspiration: Finds ways to channel or indulge her violent impulses
                                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 4

                                                    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 3, Composure 4
                                                    Skills: Athletics 3, Brawl 4, Drive 2, Firearms 3, Larceny 3(Pick Pocket), Intimidate 4(Torture), Investigation 2, Occult 2(Superstition), Subterfuge 2(Disguise), Stealth 3, Streetwise 3(Cartel), Weaponry 3
                                                    Merits: Closed Book 2, Fame 1, Iron Will, Multi-Lingual (English, Portuguese), Professional Training 3 (Criminal; Intimidate, Larceny, Streetwise), Resources 2, Status 3 (Cartel), Style: Street Fighting 4
                                                    Potency: 4
                                                    Willpower: 12

                                                    Initiative: 9

                                                    Speed: 16

                                                    Machete [+2L melee; -2 Initiative; 9 dice]

A Mutant might have sharp fangs, gnarled claws or even bony outgrowths from his forehead. His skin might be as tough as leather, or covered in a heavy layer of callus. The Mutant has either a natural attack method (claws, teeth, horns, etc.) that inflicts lethal damage as a +1 weapon or to have 1/1 points of armor

                                                    Sensitivity: Mutants inspire fear and revulsion in all who see them. The Mutant suffers a -2 penalty to dice pools for Social rolls (except for Intimidation) against any character that can see or has seen his face, and any failures in such circumstances become dramatic failures. In addition, Mutants are all hypersensitive to some form of stimulus — bright light, powerful antiseptic scents, certain textures, sudden shifts in temperature, and so on. When the Mutant is confronted by their particular stimuli, they must make a Resolve+Composure roll or gain the Frightened Condition in regards to that stimuli until the end of the scene.

                                                        The Green
                                                        The entities known only as “The Green” are strange vegetable lifeforms, existing across a multitude of bodies but sharing a rudimentary conjoined consciousness. The organism has slender, leafy vines that blossom with fragrant yellow flowers and sprout plump seed pods near its roots. The creatures are nocturnal, and are capable of moving under their own power once the sun sets. They seem to have a remarkable aggression towards (some would even say hatred of) animal life, and will ambush such creatures when they are vulnerable (such as when asleep or distracted). The plants can produce cruel weaponry when it chooses to attack, manifesting sharp thorns dripping with digestive enzymes, poisonous secretions, busts of irritating pollen, and strangling tendrils.
