                        So you can't "teach" Disciplines, only share blood.
                        This is incorrect.

                        It is true that often vampires develop new Discipline dots seemingly at random or simply as a result of repeated exercise of lower dots, but you can (and sometimes must) be taught.
                        • In the VtR 2e Core rulebook (p. 83) it explicitly states that "as well, Disciplines sometimes require teachers". You may develop Clan Disciplines and Common Disciplines without a teacher, but Uncommon Disciplines require a teacher.
                        • The merit Tutelage (Guide to the Night, p. 127) is about reducing costs of Disciplines and Devotions as part of an extended mentor-student relationship.
                        • The original Chicago-based Requiem novels have a character seek out tutelage to learn Obfuscate and it has a scene that covers a moment of said tutelage.
