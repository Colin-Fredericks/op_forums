    Name: Emily Valentine, Austin
    Emily was raised as a military brat. The closest she ever came to having a true home was when her father was stationed in japan. They called it a miracle that she was able to survive such temperatures. No one believed her about the ghostly woman made of fire but in doing research she found out about a woman who was burned alive near her house that looked just like this woman. This convinced her that ghosts must real and from that point on she has drove head long into studying the occult and things about ghosts. Emily even believed that ghosts might have some kind of connection to physics. To share her research she started working at a late night paranormal talk show and soon became not just a researcher for the show but a co-host.
