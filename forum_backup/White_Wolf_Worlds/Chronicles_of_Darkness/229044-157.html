                                                    Originally posted by SquamousBeauty View Post
                                                    Are there any rules as to how big a beast's soul is, or is that a player's choice? And when in the underworld/hedge/whatever, does a beast stay in their human shape?
                                                    Dream Logic, therefore Size is meaningless. A good narrative rule of thumb is that all Dream Travelers have a Species factor added to their Health based upon their Trait limit, and the Beast retains full access to all of their Atavisms at all Satiety levels, and can activate Satiety Expenditure effects with Willpower. Therefore, your effective Size for your Beast Soul is 5 + 1 for every point of Lair above 5 + Twice your Lair Rating if you took Looming Presence.

                                                    As far as I can tell, unless you draw your Lair into a given area via a Primordial Pathway, you are always in human form, Inheritance not withstanding.
