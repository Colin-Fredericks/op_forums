        It feels like the rules are inconsistent.

        Pg 120 mentions the semiotics and type of spell (not Arcanum) - based on typical order use of a Demense

        An Arrow’s training room helps with spells involving duels, preparation for battle, and self-mastery.
        A Guardian’s Veiled Room helps with spells of disguise, misdirection, and uncovering truths.
        While a verge is described as being useful for any type of spell so long as it uses one of the ruling arcana of the associated watchtower.

        But then on pg 243, the verge and Demense are described as having the same rules regarding Semiotics. With

        A Stygian Verge that’s filled
        with metal but no aspects of Death won’t help a mage bind ghosts, but will help him create strange alloys.
        Which is inconsistent with what was described previously, and it is unclear whether the naturally occurring supernal phenomena (in a verge but not a Demense) are sufficient to provide the environmental Yantra bonus.

        Do you need a Demense to be aligned toward Order related things? Or can you just make use a Moros Soulstone and fill the Demense with metals to invoke Matter? Rules unclear.

                                                    Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                                                    hod do organizations in nwod in general keep from becoming global? Whats the reason preventing it?
                                                    Nothing really. There are quite a lot of global organisations. Though it does depend what you mean by 'global', and that shouldn't necessarily be seen as synonymous with being powerful.

                                                    Practically speaking though, there's no centralised state with a bureaucracy to act as intermediary between the monsters of the CofD. Therefore, most of the monster societies are based on personal ties which kinda only really works for a relatively small area. That's not to say those kinds of organisation don't become bigger, but they also become more brittle and liable to splinter with that expansion.

                                                    The organisations that are able to maintain a global presence for long periods of time are generally either highly decentralised as the Pentacle, or are backed by some metaphysical force that keeps them inline.

                                                            Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                                                            how do organizations in nwod in general keep from becoming global? Whats the reason preventing it?
                                                            As Michael said, nothing. If it makes sense for them to go global, they go global, and actually a lot of them did.

                                                            That said, this doesn't necessarily mean a lot. As in real organizations, local branches have more to do with local occurrences than with overarching plots. Not that those don't happen, but they're not standard fare. A global organization is more likely to be able to bring resources from other places to bear if they need, but local organizations usually have a firmer grasp on local power dynamics, so it's kind of a trade-off.

                                                            In the end there's little difference between a global or local organization in most places, most of the time. Global orgs will be present through local branches with mostly local resources or a limited supply of foreign resources, and local connections to help them acclimate. Local orgs are perfectly able of pulling connections if needed to act on a global stage if needed, that's not as hard as it seems. And in fact, any supernatural nature of the organization has no bearing in this aspect, save for the odd otherworldly branch.
