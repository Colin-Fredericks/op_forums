In my opinion, Dissonance has as much an effect on a non-supernal supernatural quality granted to a to subect as it does to a non-supernal -anything- granted to the subject.

                                                Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                And the problem here is that the Sleepwalker's friends are under no compulsion to believe him and Dissonance does not "target" mages directly. A rat with gills challenges "rats don't have gills" rather than "you cannot give mammals the features of fish" and liquid diamonds challenge "diamonds are not liquid at room temperature" rather than "you can't cold-melt diamonds."
                                                You are right - they don't target the mage directly; But in this case they aren't. They are targeting the spell of the mage. If the spell of a mage causes a rat to breath underwater, it will be challenged as 'rat's don't breath underwater'. However, if the spell of the mage gives the rat's owner the capability of turning the rat into a rat with gills with a cool supernatural merit, the rat with gills is not going to be subject to the dissonance - the supernal effect is that you -can- give mammals the features of fish, and they are too busy challenging than 'rats don't have gills'.

                                                In this scenario, the -only- valid target of Dissonance is the Forge Destiny spell. The Electrokinesis is not, because it is not supernal - the use of the merit involves no spell, no magic for the purposes of the Awakened perception. Like your rat, people do not challenge "You cannot give people the ability to use Electrokinesis". I'd argue they don't even challenge "People don't have Electrokinesis". They challenge "You cannot control electricity with your mind".

                                                Put another way, they do not challenge what the Sleepwalker has. They challenge what the Sleepwalker is doing.

                                                Finally, though its also a bit off topic - I feel like if you did this - gave a Sleepwalker the ability to cause this much mayhem and get people looking into the supernatural - that it would most definitely qualify as a breach - you are exposing the Sleeper Culture to the Mysteries through your lack of foresight into that sleepwalkers character. It may not be -Supernal- Mysteries, but it definately qualifies; I mean heck, just so much as getting an investigation going in the general area of a mage is enough to be a breach - getting the town square crying 'witch' at your Sleepwalker "friend" is not going to get any brownie points *G*
