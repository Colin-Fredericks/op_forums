    [GMC] Hotel Mascarone - Some Tweaks

    01-03-2015, 10:03 PM
    I personally adore the Hotel - they are definitely going to be in any future game of Hunter I run. However, I tend to use the 2e rules, so some tweaks are in order.

    1 - Guest Morality
    Integrity and Morality are completely different things; this much is plain. As such, I don't feel like the standard "Morality Degeneration waits until the end of the check-in" really fits; therefore...

    While the Guest is Checked-In, the rest of the world takes on a sociopathic unreality; as such, they do not suffer Breaking Points due to violence against other characters - much like a dream, or a video game, their pain or death means nothing.

    2 - Condition: Trigger
    As part of this Tweak, the current correlation between the Mandrake Induction and Derangements is severed. Instead, the Hunter possesses one instance of the Trigger Condition, as well as an additional instance for every 4 dots of Figments they have.
    Trigger [Persistent]
    You have gone through the Mandrake Induction, and have started to develop certain mental fractures; as a result, you suffer from inappropriate emotional fluctuations. Each time you gain this Condition, select a trigger and an emotion; each time you encounter that trigger, you experience that emotion regardless of the other context of the scene.
    Then, either resist the trigger, or go with the flow. Resisting costs you one Willpower; going with the flow gives you one Willpower, but also causes you to Check-In.
    Causing The Condition: The Mandrake Induction
    Beat: Going with the Flow while Checked-Out.
    Resolution: N/A
    3 - Figment Updates
    Crimson Face Card - Instead of an opposed roll, any attempt to discern or remember anything about the Checked-In Hunter's appearance other than their mask takes a [Red Delirium] penalty. Additionally, they gain a [Red Delirium] bonus on any Wits + Subterfuge rolls involving their mask.

    Vermillion Knack Split - You may spend 1xp to add two dots of Merits to your Knack Library.

    Argent Veil Dismissal - The memory loss due to this Figment is represented with the Amnesia condition; instead of creating a new Amnesia condition each time you Check-Out, simply add any new erased memories to the extant condition.

    Pale Shadow Coat - Spending Willpower to access Figment dots counts as hitting one of their Triggers.

    Chalk Tabby Silhouette - Going with the Flow restores two Willpower instead of one, and you may reflexively choose to experience any emotion connected to one of your Trigger conditions.

    Sapphire Snake Servant - Anyone who possesses at least two Trigger conditions regains an additional point of Willpower whenever they rest in a place cared for by the Retainer granted by this Figment.

    4 - Status:
    Since Unseen Senses no longer requires a roll, the +2 bonus applies to any Perception rolls related to your Unseen Sense.

      And here, as a bit of a bonus, a Slasher Guest.

      Lady Marlene
      Now dear, stop making such a fuss - have you no sense of propriety? Please, your kicking just gets your blood everywhere.

      No one is exactly sure of where Lady Marlene came from, or it (or rather, she) really works - the members of the Mindszenty Movement that have heard of her guess that she was a "guest" who somehow left her host and has parasitized the general chain of hotels. Of course, they aren't sure. At all. For all they know, she's a figment of the Greenscreen, or something much stranger.

      Personality: Regardless of her current physical body, Lady Marlene acts like a lady - she follows Miss Manners (or at least a grim parody thereof) to the letter. She also likes spreading this propensity to others; her Mixed Blessing Dread Power takes effect through small (and cruel) lessons in good manners.
      Whatever language she speaks, she speaks it politely and properly, adhering to the rules of grammar and proper pronunciation regardless of whether or not her host has that much of a grasp - some recorded incidents have involved her carrying on full conversations in a language her host only had a passing familiarity with.

      Methods: The Lady prefers poisons and other "clean" methods of killing; she detests getting blood on her hands. Of course, getting a "champion" (or "champions") to kill someone for her is a perfectly legitimate way of removing an obstacle.
      She predominantly targets artists and dreamers, convinced that they are just another form of the faerie threat; her perfect diction tends to slip around them, letting alien syllables and twisted rage drip into her speech.

      Infection: While checked out, Lady Marlene may reflexively spend 1 Willpower to transfer herself to any person who has passed through the Mandrake Induction within the same scene. She arrives checked out, and her valise (see Systems for details) disappears from the possessions of her former host.
      As long as she is present in a Hunter, that Hunter suffers from her Bans; due to their nature, she prefers female hosts (though she'll take what she can get, in a pinch.)
      Once she's arrived in another "hotel", she can "murder" the other guest - doing so immediately checks them out, and allows her to check-in instead. If she leaves after murdering the guest, any attempt to check in by the Hunter in question just results in them falling catatonic for a Scene. They also take the Soulless Condition - whatever she did, it left their soul broken.
      If the Hunter regains a soul somehow, they also regain a guest, preventing them from going catatonic when checking in. Of course, it's a new guest, with all that entails - their mask theme changes, as do their tastes (and their memories).

      Systems: Lady Marlene is both a parasitic personality and a Slasher; as such, she follows the following rules.
      • Lady Marlene possesses a three-point Willpower pool; while the Hunter is checked-out, Lady Marlene may only spend her Willpower to either check-in (which costs one Willpower), or to jump between bodies.
      • While checked in, the Hunter (or, rather, Lady Marlene) may spend Willpower from either pool, and regained Willpower can be placed in either pool. Additionally, their Vice is replaced by "Proper" while the Lady is checked in.
      • The checked-in Hunter gains Nightmare ••• along with the following Talents: Disarming, Dread x2 (Ban: Lady Marlene must reveal herself around artists, dreamers, and madmen. Ban: Lady Marlene loses her Defence while she is wearing trousers.), and Queen of the Cacophony. The Hunter suffers from the Bans regardless of whether or not they are checked-in.
      • The Dread Powers gained through Dread are Dream Seeing ••, Dream Shaping ••, and Mixed Blessing •• (Vice-Ridden: "Proper").
      • When using Dream Shaping, Lady Marlene may spend an additional point of Willpower to check out and transfer herself to whoever she was working on. This can allow her to spread to people who haven't actually passed through the Induction, but the Condition created through the Dream Shaping must be a Trigger condition.
      • Anyone playing host to the Lady gains the benefits of the first two dots of the Red Delirium and White Dementia; her masks always take the form of a delicately embroidered silk blindfold.
      • Her (and by extension, the Hunter's) Knack Library consists of Barfly, Sympathetic and the first two dots of the Etiquette merit.
      • The first time anyone checks-in while playing host to Lady Marlene, her valise shows up somewhere among the belongings of her host. Inside is an assorted collection of brimmed hats, antique dresses, and poisons unrecognized by human science. While checked-out, her host subconsciously ignores the valise - if someone draws attention to it, they'll respond as if it were nothing out of the ordinary.
