                I've been rewatching the MCU movies lately and have thoughts on thematic equivalents within CofD.

                SHIELD: SHIELD is Task Force Valkyrie. Semi-Secret quasi-government organization that deals with the unusual either by weaponizing or covering it up. The otherwise human SHIELD affiliated characters (Fury, Coulson, Hill, Hawkeye, Black Widow, etc) are Hunters.

                Iron Man: At first glance he'd appear to be a Wizard or Genius but after watching all of his movies in a month it's obvious that everything that makes Tony Stark Iron Man is the result of trauma. Making him a much better candidate for a Changeling (his name is a nice little bonus too). His obsessive tinkering (especially when under stress) and controlling tendencies suggest Wizened as his Seeming.

                Ten Rings: The unusually international but distinctly non-white terrorist organization is a collection of loyal Changelings under a True Fae with the title "The Mandarin" and "The Iron Monger".

                Hulk: He'd be a textbook Deviant if there were any rules for it yet. So going back to his thematic aspects we have a radioactive monster that mostly wants to be left alone but is constantly hounded by those who hate and fear his very existence. Making him a good candidate to be Zeka Promethean, one with really good residual memories.

                Abomination: Was a horrifying monster made in response to the Hulk. Possibly another Promethean (made from the body of a soldier he killed in a torment induced rampage). Another possibility which fits with the gradual transformation set up he has in the movie is a Cheiron Group soldier hopped up on stolen Pyros that slowly make him just as monstrous.

                Whiplash: A huntsman preying on Tony's self doubt and fear in an approach.

                Warmachine: Fae-Touched best friend of Tony's wearing Token armour.

                Thor: The dynamic Thor has with the modern world, a world only vaguely resembling the world he knows is present in both Vampire and Mummy but Mummy caries the divine powers and worshipped as a God much better. So this Thor is a Mummy, part of a small sect of Arisen who's cults found their way to Nordic Europe and inspired Norse mythology.

                Loki: A Deceived, who manipulated Thor's cult into waking him into a berserk rage. He may or may not actually be Thor's brother, my knowledge of Mummy is spotty so I'm not even sure if that's possible.

                Jane: A Null Mysteries Hunter who had infiltrated Thor's cult with intent on studying it's slumbering benefactor.

                Captain America: Cap has a similar relationship with the modern world to Thor but to a lesser degree. A Deva Vampire super soldier allowed into the service of Taskforce Valkyrie in response to the actions of the Red Skull (See Below)

                Red Skull: Ordo Dracul Nosferatu who openly through his powers in with the Nazis. Red Skull begins an alternate approach to perfecting the Vampiric condition. He begins experimenting with the living and God Machine infrastructure to physically perfect them before they are even embraced.

                Chitari: Strange and unknowable with minimal context for their existence. These guys scream Idigam. Loki exploited some Infrastructure to release them into the world.

                Extremis: An unstable extract of Goblin Fruit that can physically restores and even enhances the human body. As with anything of the Wyrd it twists it's users turning them into Fae but most people can't withstand that strain and after a while turn into living bombs.

                "The Mandarin" and AIM: Aldrich Killian and Trevor Slattery are both Loyalist Changelings under the thrall of "The Mandarin". Maya Hansen is in the process of escaping (probably going to become a Darkling).


                This post is already a bit on the TLDR side so I'll stop here at Iron Man 3 and see what people think thus far.

                        Of course, the problem is that the show isn't "We'll never be happy unless we become human, Steven! OFFSPRING STEVEN!

                        Then again, that's the problem with adapting things to a horror setting. In a superhero comic, mutant =/= monster, but in a horror setting, an atomic mutant inevitably has to be inherently monstrous in some way.

                        In other settings, the Noisy Tenant-verse (Burgrr, Awful Hospital) is totally a Changeling story. Dr. Phage is a True Fae, definitely. The whimsical dream-logic of the setting makes it Arcadian for me, as opposed to Abyssal, Idigam, or GM related.

                        Speaking of Changeling, Dorohedoro probably works best there. It's about normals vs. magic users, but it's very hard to map it to Hunter or Mage, since the wizards in Dorohedoro aren't really like any kind of mages or sorcerers in ChroD, since they're people born with one specific power. And their whole deal with living in the Magic World and periodically popping into the human world to hunt/transmogrify random people is very Gentry-ish. I might make the Devils the Gentry and have some of the magic users be Loyalist Changelings, actually. Kaiman would be a Coldscale Beast (or possibly Ogre), Nikaido would be Kithless, Noi would be...what's that Ogre kith with a healing factor?, En would be some sort of custom fungus-Kith, and so on.

                        I don't even have to change anything for What We Do In The Shadows, it's literally already a completely perfect Vampire: The Requiem story.
