    Originally posted by Haberdasher View Post
    Cold War Poland or the Troubles Ireland for Werewolf.
    Haberdasher, I'm really confused why do you equal Cold War in Poland with Werewolf - as Polish guy, I really cannot see PRL times as something you 'hunt for' then. Maybe for next standing in lines for food or clothes or on the Church mass as well resistance rallys, but this does not sound as very RPG good setting to me.

    Polish people resistance against Soviets was not blowing up cars or hunting Party higher-ups – it was much more peaceful revolution of demonstrations, strikes and at worst, martial law at peak of social discontent. In many ways it looked like Human Rights Movement fight on the West. And our local Catholic Church was more than resisting element – religion was heart of opposition. ( That’s now is biting us in our asses, as Church being so important to opposition in PRL means it dictates now lives of Polish people in country – so LGBT+ people, like me, have very hard under it’s ‘rule of souls’ in modern Poland. )

    On the other hand, we had long local fandom idea for the 'Iron Curtain' settings in Poland at that time for ( then ) nWoD - let me remember them and maybe add few more...

    'Iron Curtain' Eras ideas
    Vampire - Undead are capitalist subvertists, drinking from workers population of new state. In reality, some of Party's higher-ups are wanting to become immortal. Invictus if fighting to sustain it’s assets, were Carthian Movement say ‘We are voice of people!’ and Lancea et Sanctum is helping resistance in 'Showing those atheist wrath of one true God – and the Hell waiting for them!’

    Werewolf - I really have problem how to marriage animism and Sacred Hunt in this time period, even if I really would want.

    Mage – Seers of Throne are on the high tide in totalitarian regimes – they always were. But Diamond Orders rebellion is on the rise in Poland, pulling strings in resistance. Even if some Awakened have sell out to Party. ( Real world facts – there are some evident that our own ‘occultists’ were very interested in fighting regime. Real world Mason Lodges helped opposition in PRL times. )

    Promethean – Soviets made many strange experiments – Zeka Linage being only one of them. Also times of hard lives under communist rules made many people to pilgrimage for work and life sense.

    Changeling – Like my buddy summed up it before ‘Fairy tale about tractor and harvester’

    Hunter – See Promethean on monsters. On the other hand, many layers of Soviets secret service and fight with resistance can also make nice story twists.

    Geist – Party’s regime have started with much blood on it’s hands. Now the dead want the revenge for crimes of the past.

    Mummy – Do not know line, but it probably would have problem to jive with Iron Curtain countries.

    Demon – We had Russian Revolution Era in Dark Eras Companion book.

    Beast – Great ways to explore this Era, I think. Beasts are about moral lessons what people SHOULD NOT do to another – and communists society were full of people like that. On the other hand, interesting would be some kind organization of Beasts that say ‘No, we will be better than those true monsters – Soviets’. Showing that people are worse monsters than real world monsters.

    Deviant - See Promethean and Hunter sections.

        Alright, I had a few eras I'd like to see, in no particular order or organization:
        • Satanic Panic (Beast/Inferno/Possibly Hunter): It's the 80s. It's a pretty sweet decade, except for one problem. A woman's repressed memories of a horrible ritual, presided over by a monster-man in a blood-red robe, came to her in a dream. Actually, there were not memories, and it was just a dream, but people lost their shit anyway. Thanks to that one cavalier beast, and the urging of some Heroes, has made this time one of the most dangerous times in human history for the Begotten, simultaneously turning eyes away from the real monsters on Wall street, ranging from mundane greedy assholes feeding lesser Demons, to full-on cults devoted to greed and avarice.
        • Book of Exodus (Demon/Promethean): God's children wander Deshret, seeking their promised land, not knowing that demons and monsters walk among them. And they're the ones keeping them alive. Moses hears His word, and while it's usually right, revelead to him by glorious angels of fire and lightning, other times, other angels of metal and cold calculation whisper to him. The Demons, who would later teach Solomon, subvert the false Angels that would lead the tribe of Israel astray, and those ancient Golmim formed of the dead receive missions to protect His favored people. Can they reach the Promised Land? And which Angels first spoke to Moses of it?
        • Post-WWII USA (Mage/Hunter): The War's over, and America's full of piss and vinegar. The economy's booming, and soldiers have come home to start families and return to civilian society. Some people aren't so entertained with the domestic life anymore, though, and have taken a page (I'm not sorry) from the superhero comics they read on the front, taking to the shadows to fight evil. The Free Council is experiencing an unprecedented boom, making the Diamond and Seers both nervous, as Hunter Compacts and Conspiracies grow to record sizes. It's a damn good time to be a human being in America. Unless you're black. Or a woman. Or gay. Or you just happen to not have a soul, or have a ghost in you, or something. If you've read Watchmen, you kind of have the basic idea of this era.
        • Warring States Japan (Werewolf/Geist): The islands of Japan are in a state of war, and the Shadow has been thrown into a similar uproar, and war-spirits and their entourages grow fat and powerful. The Bone Shadows demand that the Uratha remain occupied with the state of the Shadow, while the Iron Masters claim the only wqay to quell the Shadow is to end the fighting. And if an Uratha is left as the Shogun, all the better. What neither party realizes is that the dead have a stake in this fight. The spirits of ancestors and the hungry ghosts of fallen warriors have been manipulating the wars, either to further their living kin's agendas, or to take revenge upon their killers. Those touched by death are at their limit already, but the realm of the dead seems to becoming over-full, threatening man, wolf, and everything in between.
        • I can't think of a specific era, but I want to see Changeling/Mummy something. They have Fate at their core, but they have wildly differing conceptions of Fate, it'd be neat to see those juxtaposed. Plus, I just wanna see some Huntsmen and/or Charlatans get fucked right up by a Mummy. Hell, there might even be a combination Freehold/Mummy Cult with a Bulwark built around the Mummy's Descent.
