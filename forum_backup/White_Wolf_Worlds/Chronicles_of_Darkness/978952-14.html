                                                        Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                                        I'm from Poland, but plan to run ( in some future ) game about Geist created in 9/11 in NYC - Am I disrespectful to victims by using this event as base of chronicles on Second Chances?
                                                        Nah. My feelings on it are this. It is fine to tie real world atrocities in with a fictional setting if the group you play with is cool with it. As a reader, I have read fiction that uses atrocities as plot points, and i have enjoyed them.

                                                        Your game idea sounds great. If you lived here, you wouldn't be able to run it with dry eyes. I was lucky not to have lost anyone personally, but I generally avoid the internet on 9/11. The day is burned into my fucking mind. I remember sitting in my first college class. The first plane hit and the teacher laughed, because planes have hit WTC before. Little planes. We thought "no big deal" until every first responder in the class got up one by one as their cellphones/beepers went off. I remember not being able to get in touch with my parents. They worked in Brooklyn, but we really had no idea what was going on. Was this the only target? What else was coming? I remember the funerals for weeks afterwards. Driving past processions, memorial services and the grim weight that settled on everyone. Then there are the stories that come out. My friends dad who was running late, and he saw the plane hit the building where he works. Where all his friends work. The soccer coach who went to work an hour early to make copies for his kid's youth team, and he wouldn't be there if it weren't for that. He died, making copies for his kid's soccer team.

                                                        I was lucky. It still impacted me greatly. I know many people who were not lucky.

                                                        The personal horror games of CofD actually really match with those emotions.

                                                        In WoD, they could do a good job with it. A good example is Wraith. It dealt with the great war in a fairly elegant way, and integrated it into the metaplot of the hidden history without changing the cause.

                                                        The problem is their focus on the Punk setting, and the ridiculous number of people who believe it was a "inside job". I can already read the "government conspiracy" side bar.
