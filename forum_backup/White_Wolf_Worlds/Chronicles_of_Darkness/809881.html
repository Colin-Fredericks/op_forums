    New York by night

    01-15-2016, 06:36 PM
    My PCs are about to take a trip to NYC to learn more about a Belial's Brood coterie that started there. There are coming from Chicago and I'm using the Chicago sourcebook with Prince Maxwell and all that stuff. I want to show them that now, not two cities are quite the same and I was wondering about mood and theme for NYC. I discarded the Lancea Sanctum (NYC epitomizes the North not the Christian Belt), it's a modern city so not the the Circle, I want to show them something different so not the Invictus, NYC financial blood is Wall Street so not the Carthian Movement. Therefore I was thinking Ordo Dracul, it would make sense : the Defiants, building a new nation whatever it takes, spitting in the face of destiny and saying "watch me".. So what would be your NYC in requiem ?

      I don't live in New York, so I'm not gonna give you any specific advice, just this one thing to consider: the Covenants in Requiem can, in fact, bear many faces. For example, the Circle doesn't have to be a bunch of nerds and neopagan weirdos, but stockbrokers, loan sharks and businesspeople that are so succesful thanks to the help of their spirits. The Invictus may be a street gang instead of a bunch of jaded old vamps in suits. The lancea can be crusaders, fighting crime on the street of Bronx. The Carthians may, for once, be in charge, but not as politicians, but trendsetters - hiding in plain sight, when every wannabe millionaire from Wall Streets wants to have their works of art, technology or whatever in their homes.

      There are dozens of options out there, shaped by the nature and, yes, soul of any given city.
      As for the Theme and Mood,.. I don't think a city as huge and diverse can really have these. They are more of a traits of your chronicle, so it largely depends on how you want to play it.

          Well, for my group's setting of New York, it's so big that there's basically a couple domains throughout the city. In New York, the Invictus discard feudal practices of hierarchy and the like for a corporate identity. The Conspiracy of Silence is known to its Kindred as the Company, it's leadership form the Board, and its master is the Chairman. They use corporate lingo to disguise their activity, and manipulate a large number of proxies through actual corporate fronts.

          I was sort of inspired by the Board Meeting Elysium event in the supplement Invite Only which takes place in New York. I think the city is too big and weirdly balkanized for any one Kindred or even Covenant to claim domain over it, but you could have multiple claims and multiple territories.

          I'm from Manhattan and have spent most of my life in New York, so that informs my knowledge.

          I think New York certainly does have themes unique to it. While all cities are inevitably "faster" than living life around it, New York is pretty unique in the high energy that it exhibits. People are very busy, very stressed out, and always moving from one thing to the next. Time is valuable here. Incidentally, that's why it's been called the rudest city in the country.

          It's also very, very diverse, and you can have all sorts of interesting clashes between people of different cultural groups, different religious groups and others over the most incidental things. People with completely different experiences live right up next to one another, so those kinds of contrasts are really easily observed.

              I like the idea of New York City being overpopulated, yet so incredibly lonely as everybody unabashedly ignores one another. The streets are bustling, public transport is overcrowded, and traffic congestion is never-ending. Kindred are found everywhere in New York City, and yet they largely ignore one another's existences. It's mostly made up of disenfranchised, unaligned, rejected souls, notwithstanding any company. The small groups that do congregate can only rule small pieces of New York City (a building, a company, a street); the city is simply too competitive, diverse, and large in scope for any one Kindred or Covenant to control outright. The nits of Kindred that do get together pose as mortal gangs, church congregations, youth groups, boardroom executives, rarely do they overlap or associate with one another - the church goers and gang bangers stick to their own kind. Masquerade breaches, murder, gluttonous feeding, excessive embraces run amok in The Big Apple. Nobody exists to reign it in. Nobody to give a shit.

                  I'm running in NYC and I'm from NYC.

                  First thing you need to know is NYC is broken up into 5 boroughs. Each one was originally gonna be its own state because they are so large. Now I don't know if you wanna use what I've been doing but I broke NYC's Vampire community down into this.


                  Manhattan = Invictus
                  Brooklyn = Invictus / Circle of the Crone / Lancea et Sanctum
                  Queens = Circle of the Crone
                  Bronx = Carthian Movement
                  Staten Island = Ordo Dracul

                  Each borough has it own prince and they come together in a counsel like the UN (Being the UN is in NYC) and deliberate different things.

                  I think if I had to Give NYC over all a mood. Its Cold Fast and Weird. You can walk down a high tech street one moment and run into a street that is kept old for historical reasons. Train lines going anywhere you need and hidden night clubs.

                  I thought it was be interesting to have Circle of the Crone have Queens under control and be at war with Brooklyn (Also Called Kings)
