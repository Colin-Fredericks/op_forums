    Originally posted by ShadowKnight1224 View Post
    Well, I feel like the Sin-Eater take on "the sanctity of death" is a bit hypocritical considering their circumstances (and the existence of the Revenance merit for high-tier krewes). IMO Geist should do away with that and embrace the fact that Sin-Eaters are not going to be a natural part of the life-death cycle. Being undead means Sin-Eaters are immortal by default (instead of requiring Revenance), are considered undead by supernatural effects that care about such things (such as the Death arcanum, wards against undead, anti-undead Endowments, etc.) and serve as a better reminder of their second chance than an alien voice in their heads.

    As for Mementos, the problem is that they are purely a fluff-inspired mechanic, and the fluff itself is very superficial. The idea of the Sin-Eater as a collector of "cool stuff" fits with the hedonistic fluff about second chances. The problem is that Geist takes that fluff and just doesn't do anything with it. There's no purpose to the second chance, because Geists are inhuman and mysterious, so their reasons are basically "whatever works for the plot." If you put all the flavour together, it just gives the idea that Sin-Eaters are just random people who won the death lottery and now get cool powers, cool stuff, cool mythology and symbols, and cool places to visit. The Underworld also feels like an accessory, and that's because Sin-Eaters as a whole just don't have motivations beyond whatever the player assigns to them.
    These are among the reasons I've been a proponent of rebranding Geist into something like Revenant: the Risen. IMO the whole second chances thing is a bit overwrought.

    What might actually be helpful is if the big 2e version of Geist wait until we get a proper CofD Wraith game. In fact I wouldn't be sad if Wraith and Geist were paired up and shared some antagonists. A "cool" idea (people's mileage will vary here) is if they shared a rulebook a la one of those old double sided SF/Fantasy paperbacks you sometimes saw back in the '70s. On one side is Wraith on the other is Geist (or Revenant), it'd have to be as thick the M20 rulebook but hey, almost everyone gets what they want (obviously they'd be separate PDFs).

                                                            Seems like we have the same problems with the game. The titular Geist really isn't a factor in the game. The primary problem is the tonal dissonance that comes from the celebratory feel of Krewes and Sin-Eaters when compared to the unfinished business and brooding death themes that Geists and the Underworld bring to the game. Much like Werewolf 2e brought all new focus on The Pack and The Hunt, I think a Geist 2e should focus on the last two things--Geists and the Underworld--and not the first two things. Now YMMV and there's no issues about that but I'll be blunt, I don't actually care that you don't like my (or anyone else's ideas). I wouldn't be posting my ideas if I agreed with yours.

                                                            Now let me get to answering your initial question. First a little background: Wraith: the Oblivion is (and always has been) my favorite World of Darkness game. So when a "spiritual successor" came out for it in the "new" World of Darkness, I was excited. And it is my second favorite 1e game (right after Mummy). That doesn't mean I think its a good game though. It isn't. Quality wise Geist 1.0 was barely better than the Changing Breeds blue book. Geist 1.1 really didn't make it much better, it merely made it playable. So depsite the fact that I like Geist more than most of the other 1e's, it's simply not a well put together game. It's best parts are the things it inherited from Wraith--the Underworld, Keys, Mementos, dualistic characters. All the things that sit on the Revenant rather than the Psychopomp side of its DNA.

                                                            As I see it if 2e doesn't focus on the titular Geists then it should be rebranded into something like Sin-Eater: the Illuminated. A title that captures the Psychopomp aspect of Sin-Eaters. But that's also a game that doesn't need Geists or any of the baggage they bring with them. However, that might also be a boring game because psychopomps lead the dead to their rest. They're people who know things. They have answers. And that doesn't actually mesh very well with the overarching mood of the Chronicles of Darkness which is one of interwoven and nesting mysteries. Better is a game that doubles down on Geists. However, that's a game that doesn't really need Sin-Eaters except as a possible minor template or unless it wants to inherit the Psyche/Shade duality from Wraith. In the first case, it's a game that should be rebranded to something like Revenant: the Risen. In the second case we might not need to rebrand it but it becomes a game that needs some very strong mechanical teeth to fully develop the dualistic aspect of Sin-Eaters and Geists sharing one body. A mechanic that has players share characters is the best way to accomplish this.

                                                            So it isn't like I have any one idea of what the 2e game should look like. I have multiple ideas based on which of the book's themes will become the focal point of 2e. You might not like any of them but the nature of 1e isn't such that it can survive as is if it goes through the 2e treatment. I put it to you, the game's title is Geist and it would be best if 2e focused on them and not Sin-Eaters.
