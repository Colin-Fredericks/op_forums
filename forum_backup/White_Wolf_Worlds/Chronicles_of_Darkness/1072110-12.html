                                                        Two ideas: 1 Compact & 1 Conspiracy. Both inspired by the idea that both were created by the Nobility but either quickly or slowly spin out of control and took on a life of their own.

                                                        Excalibur: the Hand of the Crown

                                                        "Don't you see? People like you are the keys to humanity's future. You aren't enemies, you're separate pieces of a puzzle that unlocks the hidden potential in all of us. The enemy is the contagion that would degenerate us all into monstrosities, not each other..."

                                                        Excalibur, or Project: Excalibur, was created by a team of scientists who all had first hand experience with the Nobility in some form. Some were psychically linked to the Empaths in the form of some knightly vow, while others were witnesses or bystandards to some of the tremendous feats of superhuman powers the Nobility are known for. All of them came away from the experience with the same conclusion: these Empaths are the future of humankind, but their own human development and culture is hindering their growth into the perfect humans.
                                                        And so, the scientists set out to properly examine and codify the methods and powers of the Nobility in an attempt to discover how a normal human can develop such intricate psychic abilities and deep empathy. But to do that the scientists needed to play to the social ques that Noble society uses to explain their empathetic drive and justify the violence necessary to lessen the psychic trauma from experiencing humany's shared suffering.
                                                        So, Excalibur came up with a plan: they would become the advisors and knight errants of the Nobility themselves. Working as one part method spy and one other part field researcher, the Hands would seek out Nobles, earn their trust, and work within their society to discover their secrets and the full extent of their latent powers.
                                                        Sounds easy enought right? Well, sadly the project turned out to be far more complicated than that. And surprisingly not from the sheer amount of violence a Noble must become accustomed too either, but instead from said Noble's own society. Apparently the "Courts" are far more fractious and splintered than the Hands originally anticipated. With each "Court" having a code of beliefs that they are unwilling to compromise on and many "Courts" being openly hostile towards one another (three particular one's specifically) it became apparent very quickly that if something wasn't done then the Nobles would spend all of their energy bickering with eachother.
                                                        Luckily a solution presented itself fairly quickly, although it was a step that only the most dedicated of the Project could commit too and would shape the nascent Compact to these very night. Something needed to unite the Nobles together in a common cause, and that cause was the threat of the Spawn. So, Excalibur began the heinous but necessary task of cultivating Nests in areas where the Empaths gather together. Then, they would go undercover to offer their services to the Nobility so they could then study them while they fought against the Spawn that they helped cultivate. All in the name of human progress.


                                                        Magister United: The Merchants of Dreams

                                                        "I'm going to spell this out for you plainly: I'm a rich philanthropist who donates thousands of dollars to cancer research. You're an angst ridden teenager with the power to destroy a small neghborhood. I'm the hero of this story. You're the monster."

                                                        If there are two emotions that define the human condition, then they must be envy and want. Or, at leasts that's what the Hunters of Magister United believe, and they make it their buisness to provide for those who both covet and desire.
                                                        Originally an organization called the Magisters of the Crystal Crown, their entire purpose was to serve as guardians and councilors to a young Noble who believed himself to be the heir to an ancient Queen of Queens. His powers were great but he was so certain of his abilities and divine providence that he was eventually confronted with a threat he couldn't overcome by himself (a vampire lord began converting monetary assets that were originally the Noble's) and rather than compromise with his selfish beliefs he fled like a coward as his servants were fell upon by the vampire's minions. Few Magisters survived that fateful night, but one inparticular who did came to a revelation: a Noble for all their might was bound by the mantle of nobility and were punished on high for breaking said code of ethics, but a human was not. If a human could somehow acquire a Noble's power without the empathetic connection that can cripple a Noble then that human would have the power to change the world...or, make a shit-load of money.
                                                        And thus, did that Magister set out to find a way to steal the powers a Noble. And afte years of searching through esoteric texts and occult manuscripts he finally found his answer. He tracked down the Noble who he served so long ago and over drinks he played upon the Noble's vanity and easily re-earned his trust (as the Hunter suspected, the Noble had not changed in any recognizable way beyond simple age.) Over a final toast the Magister drugged the Noble and brought his body to a glass coffins and locked it in golden chains. That night, the Dream Spider came to him and thanked the Magister for returning the "Missing Ward" to her custody. Since he had returned the Noble just as the grimoire had instructed the Dream Spider gave him the Noble's ornate mirrored scepter, and upon awakening the Magister didn't find the magical token but could feel it's power none the less from within himself. Just waiting to be called upon. And thus did the Magister use his new found powers to become filthy rich, and founded the Hunter group that would continue to grow into the Conspiracy that it is to this very night.
                                                        Hunters of Magister United are buisness savy men and women who mingle with the rich, make strong political connections, become founders of charity's and other philanthropic ventures, and oh yes...they kidnap Nobles and sell them to the Wardens of the Dreamlands in exchange for a sliver of their Inner Light.
                                                        To a Merchant of Dreams the Hunt is both a buisness venture and a means to achieve godhood. To them, the divine powers of the Queens should belong to the merchant class, not spoiled Nobles who are more likely to crash and burn rather than create anything real or long lasting. They are doing humanity a service by stealing the Light for themselves, they're making dreams actually happen. Or, at leasts that what they tell themselves. Either way, the Merchants can wield tremendous political and financial power when on the hunt. And while they primarily hunt the Nobility they are well aware of the other threats in the night and more often than not take action to rid the community of them...unless of course they stand to make a profit by having them around...


                                                        New Endowment: Usurpation
                                                        ~ The Merchants broker with otherworldly dream entities in order to steal the magical powers of the Nobility. By successfully captureing, sedateing, and performing the Rite of Somnus they may being to purches dots in Usurpation. By gaining dots in Usurpation the Hunter also gains a Dread Power related to the Noble they bound and delivered to the Dream Spiders equal or below the total number of dots they possess in Usurpation. These powers are all tied to a Regalia that manifests whenever the Hunter activates the power, although this Regalia is always malformed or twisted in often dream-like ways. Multiple Nobles may be captured by a single Hunter with Usurpation, and for each they may potentially add new Dread Powers whenever they increase their Usurpation dots or when next they perform the Rite of Somnus.
