                                                        Time to answer some of these questions:

                                                        Originally posted by Tessie View Post
                                                        So, to what are the Sworn factions sworn to, and who has declared the False factions false?
                                                        Without a transcript I don't know what's said in the podcast. I'm travelling right now so I can't listen.

                                                        Edit: Also, considering that the Sworn (most likely) don't call the other Sworn factions False, why are there five different Sworn factions? Or are they really subfactions of a larger organisation? If yes on the latter, is it more similar to the Sworn in Ordo Dracul or the Orders in the Diamond?
                                                        - The Sworn factions are sworn to destroy / neutralise / quarantine / manipulate Contagion.
                                                        - Most False are called such by members of the Sworn, though some wear the name with pride. They think the Sworn are twats and are quite happy to be different.
                                                        - The five Sworn factions in the book are the largest ones in the current era. They typically form during major Contagion outbreaks. Some have fallen apart, some have turned False, others have persisted.
                                                        - There is no larger faction of Sworn. The Cryptocracy agree with the Jeremiad that the Contagion needs "handling," but not on how they do so.
