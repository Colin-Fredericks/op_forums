        Stolen from other topic on similar matter as Alternative New Dawns...

        Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
        Bear in mind that as long as you're trying to develop something that's supposed to coexist in a game with Prometheans-as-written, you're going to run into problems. For instance: “monsters aren't better than humanity, so much as they're different.” This is largely managed by the fact that monsters have flaws and weaknesses as well as strengths. Now take these “super-Prometheans” that are the end goal of your alternate New Dawn (BTW, don't we already have a thread for that?): they retain all of the benefits of being a Promethean and none of the drawbacks (because having all of the benefits and a better capacity to manage the drawbacks doesn't require a New Dawn, just Saturninus). This makes them better than human — which is the whole point, as I understand it.

        So if there's a “better than human” endgame option out there, why would any rational and informed Promethean pursue the “human” endgame? As I said in the other thread, I don't have a problem with non-human “New Dawns” (become qashmal, become animal, become plant, etc.); I only have a problem with the “have your cake and eat it too” Promethean-without-drawbacks and similar alternatives — and even then only if you try to put them alongside “become human” in the same setting.
        My answer is simple here - Humanity seeking Created are looking for stoping to be Prometheans. What I mean here is more better shown on exmaple. Let's say we have newly Created "Adam". He was shun by the mob and humans for his inhumanity in his current state. Yet, he meets beautiful girl he falls in love with. He wants to be like a man from her dreams. He wants to stop being this "thing". And his bad memories of life of Promethean push him to become human, even when there could be some ways to still be Created but "perfected".

                    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
                    Kind of a variation, but what about (some of) the Refinements having a New Dawn of: Remove/Change some aspect of Promethean existence? You might still have Cuprum remove the Wasteland (for example), but to remove Disquiet, they need to complete the Refinement of [Whatever]. Integrates with Promethean's changing their Refinement, and makes it so each is a particular goal or step. It does extend the "Pilgrimage" a bit, but if you want "Promethean, but none of the downsides", that seems fair.
                    I don't think "Promethean but with none of the downsides" ought to be an option, for the reasons Dataweaver says. That doesn't mean you can't have a Refinement based on the idea, but what you give it instead are the Transmutations, and maybe some unique Synergies, that mitigate and manage the weaknesses. No New Dawn where it all turns to sunshine and rainbows, just an indefinite process of making better and better umbrellas.

                    If you do want a New Dawn style removal of downside, then something of the power has to go too. If you don't cause a Wasteland, maybe you can't hold Pyros inside yourself any more (you'll be burned just like the you used to burn the ground) - the analogous roll to Mortality is the creation of an external Alembic to hold your Pyros. If you don't suffer Torment, you lost the ability for transhuman potential, say: those represent the same force pushing the Fire out through your body. I'm not sure what would go with Disquiet.


                    Another option might be to make a succession of minor templates (like the ones that are starting to show up a lot in the general CoD threads) to replace human. The New Dawn makes you basically human, but with extra traits reflection the best qualities of the Refinement used to get there. Even Aurum can come with an innate empathy and high ability to connect with people. Cuprum may be a solid knowledge of yourself (which makes it easier to parse and cope with external influences). Ferrum may just be a high athleticism. Stannum may come with a forcefulness most people can't deny (anger transmuted into inspiration, basically). If you don't want to venture as far from human as a minor template, then a range of specialties and/or merits representing these traits might cover it. (Aurum, Cuprum, Ferrum, Stannum, and Aes might all work for that - with Mercurius as becoming a Qashmal, Argentum becoming a minor template related to one of the other supernaturals, and Cobalus and Plumbum becoming respectively de-Wastelanded and de-Tormented Prometheans - in this case, there'd be no de-Disquieted Promethean option, which fits maybe, given that wanting to stay Promethean is a rejection of humanity and claim to be better than it - and yes, I realise I haven't included Phosphorum in that list, unless it works for what's in my next paragraph).


                    One thing that gets forgotten in the human/transhuman debate on Promethean is that this is world where souls definitively exist, humans have them and Prometheans don't, and thus it's humans rather than Prometheans who have a shot at eternity, even if they must transcend their mortal body to do it. So what would a Refinement look that regards humanity as a necessary transitional stage, but the actual goal is a good afterlife?

                        Well, regarding souls, remember that they are interchangeable. If a Promthean wants to have one, all they need to do is "acquire" one, then make it theirs, somehow. Maybe a Transmutation? Is this "cheating"? Sure! But why should that stop them?

                        As far as Human+, what about each Refinement "completed" lets you get a free Merit upon becoming a shiny new human? Much like the Athanor bonuses from before. The more you master, the better your new life will be (and the closer to your former power you'll be). Up to and including simulating actual Promethean abilities (albeit at reduced power). Like Mummies who've achieved Apotheosis.

                        Makes the New Dawn a Cost/Value consideration of: Fast, so I can stop being an unnatural monster hated by all, or delay it and enter humanity with improved knowledge and/or power?

                        Which also invites commentary upon the Redeemed with a lot of Promethean power: were they so attached to their old abilities that they couldn't let go an appreciate humanity, or did they take their time and savor their understanding of humanity, and their powers are the result of their enlightenment?
