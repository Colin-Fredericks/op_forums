                                        #400
                                        04-18-2017, 10:19 AM
                                        1. If you have Fighting Finesse tagged to a Specialty on Grappling on the Brawl skill you may use the effects of Fighting Finesse on all Brawl rolls where the Grappling Specialty applies.
                                        2. Yes.
                                        3. It depends on what your group finds reasonable. There's no hard rules on how focused a Specialty can be. However, I recommend looking at the examples (starting at page 31 in Chronicles of Darkness) to get a sense of what the devs intended.
                                        Edit: I'd personally allow either example (and apply both on the same situations), but only one of them because of the overlap.
