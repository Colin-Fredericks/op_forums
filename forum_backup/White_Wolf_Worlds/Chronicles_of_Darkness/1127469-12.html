                                    Okay, here's the drafts of the intros for the Compact and Conspiracy sections.

                                    As near as the scholars of the Vigil can tell, the earliest compacts were founded in the Fertile Crescent and Nile Delta as groups of hunters came together during the earliest foundations of civilization. Since then compacts have risen and faded with the tides of the Vigil, finding that forming bonds of mutual trust made them safer against the monsters than waiting for the inevitable revenge killings.

                                    For much of history the Vigil compacts tended to remain regional operations, restrained by geographical limitations and inabilities to quickly communicate with each other. Even today most compacts tend to restrict themselves to a single area, lacking the resources and numbers to match with the monsters over distances. More pointedly, compacts have tended to have specific memberships based on who founded them. One group founded the Bahamas was formed only from fishermen on the coasts who protected their homes against the creatures from the Caribbean’s various blue holes. They outright refused several other hunters who tried to join from inland, believing that their lack of experience on the seas would be a dangerous hindrance to their typical Vigil.

                                    Compacts are fluid creatures; no two are alike. Some model themselves along paramilitary lines, with strict schedules and duties to perform. Others are little better than gangs, focused on a singular threat rather than any kind of overarching philosophy. However they are organized, the common thread is that there is no “typical” compact, any more than a “typical” monster.

                                    The largest struggle in a compact typically involves coordinating within itself. With the most famous being the example of the division between Null Mysteriis’ Rationalists and Open-Minds, just because two groups fall under the same umbrella is no guarantee that they can see eye-to-eye. Many compacts have fallen to civil war, infighting becoming a fact of life rather than a temporary problem. A compact in Canada known as the Mountain Men were destroyed in the 1800s, the wedge issue whether or not they would accept native tribesmen into their number and their so-called “heathen” ways. As they fought, the creatures that stalked the Canadian wilderness ran about unimpeded until a special force of the North-West Mounted Police was formed to finally end the rampages with the assistance of the same communities the Mountain Men ignored.

                                    For all their faults, in regions where the major conspiracies have little presence compacts are the rallying point for the local Vigil. Lone cells in too deep and travelling hunters looking for assistance will contact the local compacts looking for help, as well as being sometimes-arbiters between various supernatural factions who want a go-between when they need a cease-fire or negotiation. No monster like each other, but hunters hate all monsters equally. This turns compacts into valuable balancing forces where they take root, for the sake of everyone living under their aegis.
                                    Conspiracies rightly believe that they are feared by all, human and monster alike. While some consider this a terrible fact, the conspiracies and their leadership see this as a useful tool. Monster or man, the mere appearance of the men in black or inquisition is often enough to make people shut up and do what they’re told. The ones that don’t are typically the ones that go missing and are never seen again.

                                    Conspiracies are often abused for the time it takes for them to organize, for the secrets they keep not only from the world, but from their own membership. These abuses ignore the reality of what the conspiracies must face each day. These conspiracies must contend with evils so ancient and great that most men can’t begin to fathom them. Lost societies hidden beneath the Earth and under the waves. Beings from beyond space and within time that mock physics and mangle reality. Other, darker conspiracies aligned with the monsters with goals so depraved that their own sins pale in comparison. When the cells and compacts are overwhelmed and the monsters gain power, the conspiracies don’t hear much complaining about secrets and deceptions from the hunters they pull from the fire.

                                    While internal struggles are just as evident as in a compact, the hunters at the level of a conspiracy know that there are bigger matters at stake than their own petty desires. The herd mentality is both a blessing and a curse for conspiracy hunters, as it gives them a leadership but also weights them with bureaucracy. While everyone loves to pin much of this stereotype on Task Force: VALKYRIE, several other conspiracies have their own stories to tell of toxic internal cultures. The Ascending Ones have had several cells under the Jagged Crescent go rogue, believing that with their drug money and access to alchemic elixirs they could strike out on their own without the weight of the Knife of Paradise or Southern Temple. More frightening, few of these rogue cells have ever been neutralized or brought back into the fold.

                                    Even if a conspiracy has been formed relatively recently, their files stretch back for centuries and their numbers are massive. Even conspiracies like the Lucifuge can count on numbers to overwhelm their most serious problems. Webs of informants and patsies act as the footsoldiers of the conspiracies, both providing disposable assets and a stable recruiting pool for those rare hunters who show the perfect mix of initiative, skepticism, and suspicion to fight for such massive organizations.
