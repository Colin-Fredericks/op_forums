    Minor Template: The Incarnates

    02-18-2016, 01:57 AM
    I'd originally posted the early brainstorming for these folks over on the Hunter forum as a Conspiracy, but realized that they make much more sense as a Minor Template of a similar sort as those in Hurt Locker. I'm not terribly proud of the mechanics, which are admittedly rather bare-bones, but they're functional enough for the moment. My intent was to have the, as low-powered antagonists for Vampire and Mummy, or something for Mages to puzzle over. Without further ado:

    THE INCARNATES

    The soul is immortal; it does not decay with the death of the body, and finds life anew elsewhere in time and space. A woman who spends today building websites carries within herself a spark that has seen everything since mankind first tamed fire, but she cannot remember it save for snatches of odd imagery in half-remembered dreams. Forgetfulness is the curse of mortal beings, for without it they would live without fear of an end they know to be temporary.

    Yet a rare few cannot forget. They slip into fugues and awake with dead languages scrawled across their walls; they see photographs of far-off places and are overwhelmed with a deja vu too overwhelming to easily brush off. Most of these individuals keep quiet about their episodes, ignoring them or even seeking treatment to combat their odd 'intrusive thoughts,' but soon enough the sensations prove too much to bear and force them to a breaking point.

    Another self takes over, filled with fears and desires of an age long past; it falls to the possessed to negotiate a peace with this or else be driven mad by their mind literally cracking in two. The old soul is given a new chance at life, often with a demand - be it for knowledge, or vengeance, or hedonistic delights - in exchange for subsiding back into the subconscious until needed. With proper tools and guidance (meditation, exotic drugs, etc), the process can be repeated in order to find the ghosts of past lives.

    No organization binds them, but many Incarnates cross paths in lives both current and past, and trade stories whenever they do so. Some speculate that they are heirs to an unknown bloodline of some sort, or that they are destined to escape the great prison of reality after this lifetime. Others discuss enemies that they have chased through the rise and fall of empires, things undead or undying that vex them to this day. Of this latter group, some take up the hunt anew, armed with the skills of ages past.

    SIDEBAR: While certain AAA video game franchises may promote the idea of Incarnates whose personas are arranged along lines of lineage, no such neat order binds them; nothing prevents a young man in Delhi from reliving a past life as a Norse raider, or a German woman from experiencing the memories of an escaped Yoruba slave. Sex likewise is irrelevant for the Incarnates; for the soul, something as fleeting as the organs of a given body is trivial.
    SIDEBAR: DON'T BE A DICK
    This template exists to let you play around with past lives, internal dialogues, and archaic skillsets. It is not a tool to make your Storyteller's life a living hell, nor is it the path to ultimate narrative power. Your character can have a Persona who was French nobility; she was not Louis XIV. Maybe in a past life he was an occultist; this does not mean he personally knows where the Plot Device is buried. Do not use this to derail your campaign or give yourself all the answers. Do not come up with something stupid, like five different ninjas. For the love of God, do not come up with something racist. Do your research. Read a book.
    MAKING AN INCARNATE
    Incarnates are mortal human beings, and follow the character creation rules as such. To add the Incarnate minor template, simply add a single dot of the Persona template for free. The character no longer suffers a Breaking Point from witnessing the overtly supernatural.

    MERITS

    Persona (1-5 dots):
    The character has successfully recalled one of her past lives, and can call upon it when needed.
    Effect: For every dot in this Merit, assign one Specialty and two dots in Skills; these should reflect the abilities of the past life (so a Persian thief would likely have Larceny and Athletics, but most likely not Computers). With the expenditure of a Willpower point, the character can draw upon these extra abilities until the end of the scene. This merit can be taken multiple times, to represent different past lives.

    Old Glories, Old Hungers (2 dots):
    Human culture may change, but human nature does not. Those who lived in ages long past had moral strengths and failings all their own, and can share them with their present incarnations.
    Prerequisite: Incarnate
    Effect: Choose a Virtue and Vice for each Persona the character possesses. When these Personas are active, the character uses both these additional Virtues/Vices and their own.

    Babel, Unbroken (1 dot):
    The tongues of the modern world are young indeed, with recognizable dialects spanning a scant few hundred years before becoming too archaic to decipher. Other languages have been dead for centuries, but find life once more among those with the will to remember.
    Prerequisite: Incarnate
    Effect: Gain the Language Merit at one dot per Persona possessed. This should reflect the tongue that the Persona knew in life, so while a Venetian cardinal could reasonably speak Latin, he probably wouldn't speak Hindi.

    Muscle Memory (3 dots):
    While the average Incarnate can recall the talents of her past lives, some can take the ability even further and push her body to imitate a form long dead.
    Prerequisite: Incarnate, at least one Persona at 3+
    Effect: Choose an Attribute for each Persona; while that Persona is active, gain an additional dot (though not over a rating of 5) in that Attribute. This choice should be within reason; a royal astrology isn't likely to have Strength selected, for instance.

    A More Enlightened Era (2 dots):
    The modern world enjoys a certain level of comfort and safety previously unknown; atrocity and violence are witnessed through a flatscreen monitor, not firsthand. People in the past had to be made of sterner stuff to cope with a world that was more explicitly hostile, and the Incarnate can draw upon this strength to handle moral instability.
    Prerequisite: Incarnate
    Effect: The Incarnate can ignore one Breaking Point per scene if they have a Persona active, provided she can explain how that past life's Integrity wouldn't have objected to it.
    Drawback: This suppression has a drawback in the form of a new Breaking Point trigger based on the Persona that is in effect whenever the Persona is active; one of the Knights Templar may not have any objection to inflicting violence, but he is sworn to chastity and would react poorly to a violation of his oath.

    Cacophony of Voices (3 dots):
    Prerequisite: Incarnate
    An Incarnate experiences a unity of self unknown to those unlike them, and draw power from the explorations of their souls. Outsiders have a much more difficult time breaking through the wills of many made the will of one.
    Effect: The character gains a bonus equal to the number of Personas they possess (to a maximum of +5) to resist any supernatural powers designed to compel, influence, or otherwise mind-control them.

                                  It might be 8 or so months late, but I just took a look at these guys. Verdict - iffy.

                                  Persona
                                  I read this two ways - either5 dots gives you 5 instances of 2-dots-in-skills-and-a-speciality-which-cost-one-Willpower-per-scene or 10-dots-in-skills-and-5-specialities-that-cost-one-Willpower-per-scene. If it's the latter... it's very excessive. Willpower's relatively cheap in 2e, so spending 1wp a scene to effectively get 20 bonus XP spent on Skills and Specialities is... no.
                                  How I'd Handle It: Persona is a one-dot Supernatural merit that gives you one Skill dot and an appropriate Speciality that you can access for a Scene by paying 1wp. You can take it multiple times, and it works as a base for the other merits Incarnates get.

                                  Old Glories, Old Hungers
                                  This follows in the trend of being too much - you're getting both Virtuous and Vice-Ridden multiple times for 2 XP. Sure, it's contingent on you spending Willpower... but it gives you two extra sources of Willpower, making recovering that Willpower much easier.
                                  How I'd Handle It: I'd make it an extra Vice or Virtue (which can vary based off of your current Persona) - I'd also toss the player a Beat if they avoid their "normal" Vice/Virtue in favor of the one granted by the merit.

                                  Babel, Unbroken
                                  This is a much more reasonable merit - having more languages never hurts, and it's pretty thematic.
                                  How I'd Handle It: Pretty much the same way.

                                  Muscle Memory
                                  This is also pretty nice - I do wish you'd stop the unnecessary finger-wagging of "stay in-concept, please", though. It's kinda off-putting, and belongs in a sidebar rather than rules-text.
                                  How I'd Handle It: I'd add a 5-dot version that let you hit 6 dots in that Attribute - it seems appropriate and kind of thematic.

                                  A More Enlightened Era
                                  This is cool - I like Integrity hacking, though, so that's a given. I don't personally hand out more than one or two Breaking Points a scene (at most - I find they get less dramatic the more common they are), so the benefit might be a smidge strong in games like mine.
                                  How I'd Handle It: I'd let the player "swap out" an answer for their Personal Breaking Points as a side effect of activating their persona.

                                  Cacophony of Voices
                                  This is an OK merit - it's your resistance trait. It's fine, I guess?
                                  How I'd Handle It: Eh, it's fine as it is.

                                  Overall
                                  I have three issues with the Incarnates.

                                  My first is that Persona and OGOH are too nice, as they stand - Persona is also a bit ambiguous at the moment about whether or not you can have multiple Personas active at once. My third issue is that they're numbers. None of their merits give you new stuff to do - an Incarnate just looks like someone with a lot of XP and a leaky Willpower pool.

                                  Why not give them neat stuff based off of the whole "I remember past lives" schtick? Stuff like a variation on the Common Sense merit based off having literal centuries of life experience, or stuff that builds of Eidetic Memory? A "Soulmate" merit related to the True Friend merit, for someone you keep meeting again throughout the ages? Heck, a merit that let you "pick up" Aspirations from a past life and gave you some cool little bonus when you pursue them would be pretty cool.

                                  My third issue is that you don't trust your audience. You don't need to tell people to stay in-concept - the only people that won't do that are also the kind of people that won't listen to you when you explicitly tell them. And the whole "you can't be a reincarnation of anyone famous, or have any past-life memories directly related to the scenario - because that makes you a jerk" is weird - why can't I be a new incarnation of Mata Hari or Tesla? Being King Louis XIV doesn't mean you have access to his vast personal power and treasury - having a past life that knows where the McGuffin is hidden doesn't prevent it from having been moved in the interim.

                                  So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.
