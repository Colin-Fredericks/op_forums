                                    So ever since Beast got announced, I've been picturing these guysand this lady as Beasts. I've got a few ideas as to what they are, too:

                                    WARNING: Large pictures

                                    Dick Roman, leader of the Leviathans
                                    Family: They're gross, and have huge mouths full of teeth: but really that's probably just an Atavism of some sort that they manifest before feeding. The threat that the Leviathans posed in Supernatural was that they were everywhere, could be anybody, and controlled the media and a significant chunk of the government and large businesses. Plus they're basically impossible to kill, except by gathering up a series of nigh-impossible-to-acquire ingredients that will only work once per use.

                                    I peg them as Anakim, just barely followed by Eshmaki.

                                    Hunger: At first I was going to say Prey. The ultimate goal of Dick Roman, their leader, is to turn humanity into cattle: fat and too stupid to be anything but obedient. His M.O. however is to gather Power. Political, monetary. He practically gets off on it.

                                    So of course, he hungers for Power.


                                    Eve, Mother of Monsters

                                    With a name like that, you'd figure she's The Dark Mother. And I can grant you that that's not a bad guess, but she's handled without much aplomb in the show, so I think she's closer to just being a powerful Beast herself, maybe one of those Incarnates that Matt mentioned.

                                    Family: Namtaru. Her primary ability seems to be the ability to create new monsters. Not just the ones that exist already, but entirely new ones that are horrible and hard to kill (Jefferson Starships). The creation method resembles a virus/icky thing, so that's why I picked Namtaru.

                                    Hunger: This one was weird. At least until I remembered what her deal was: She came back out of Purgatory to find the one who had been kidnapping and torturing her family/children.

                                    So I figure it's clear that she hungers for Punishment.

                                    "Nihhina kalekal-zidu kal masun, kal manudanadu. Nihhina kalekal-zidu nukal shaghu-desasudu — nihhina kalekal-zidu kal innu-desasudu udhkal samm." Arthur Ashe
                                    She/her, contributing writer for Scion: Pride 2021

                                        The Boogeyman



                                        'Sup, brah? Why you lookin' at me like that? I'm just here to smoke.


                                        Okay, seriously. If you're a child of the '80s like me, then this motherfucker gave you nightmares for years, full stop. If you say he didn't, then either you're a big fat liar or you didn't watch The Real Ghostbusters.

                                        Anyway, he's a perfect fit for Beast. If you're not familiar with him, then first of all congratulations on the many hours of sleep you didn't miss out on! Secondly, here's the rundown:
                                        • He lives in a crazy M.C. Escher-esque otherworldly domain filled with doors. Doors everyfuckingwhere. Each one opens into a closet in the real world.
                                        • He eats fear. Needs it to survive. Seems to be the only form of sustenance he requires.
                                        • His M.O. is basically to enter a child's room at night via the closet, scare the living shit out of them, then head back to his domain before repeating the process until he's full.



                                        The Boogeyman's Lair.


                                        As I mentioned above, he seems to be Eshmaki/Power. Eshmaki seems to be the "demonic/classic thing-that-goes-bump-in-the-night" Family, and I went with Power because he seems to enjoy terrorizing his victims, reveling in his ability to make them cower. The other Hungers don't really make much sense. He's not Prey because he doesn't harm his "victims"; Ruin is out unless you posit that long-term psychological trauma is the POINT of his nightly visits rather than a side effect, which is directly counter to the information given in the episodes in which he appears. Punishment makes no sense as his targets appear to be picked at random and, while he shows vindictiveness when hurt or humiliated, there's no indication that this informs his choice of victims. The only Hunger that might possibly work other than Power is Hoard, if you envision him stockpiling the fear.



                                        Maybe he's saving it up so he can pay a surgeon to correct that gnarly hydrocephaly he's got goin' on.


                                        His "harvesting fear" schtick suggests a strong kinship to the Autumn Court of Changelings, while his "victimizing children" MO seems similar to the True Fae. I can see a crossover Chronicle where this guy takes some Ashen into his lair and protects them from the Gentry, only for them to start feeling like maybe they've merely left the fire for the frying pan... or vice versa.

                                                    Originally posted by Aiden View Post
                                                    Also, a Beast's Lair has a Heart that destroys the Lair if it gets destroyed.

                                                    I was rereading Demon today and got to the bit about Linchpins and Infrastructure. Kinda noticed the similarity.

                                                    Then I was thinking about a Demons reaction to a Beasts Lair once they were inside. Particularly since they are not considered family apparently.

                                                    The confusion and misconceptions were amusing to imagine.

                                                    Also Dark Mother as a "rival" to the God Machine is an interesting idea I feel. She comes from basic concepts of a chaotic primordial world that predates the "order" of human culture and civilization. Something that the God Machine is heavily invested in and embodies thematically via a cold sterile/messy body horror take on order.

                                                    This is not to say that I can see the Dark Mother as a direct opponent to the GM, merely that they each represent opposing thematic forces and narratives. As such I find it interesting that their are some metaphysical similarities between their creations.
