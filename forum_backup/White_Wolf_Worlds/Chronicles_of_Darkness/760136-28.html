    I've got Colony stuff improved once again. Just bear in mind Wealth represents "Harvested" resources.

    Starting Colony Actions
    Unless otherwise stated, a Monarch does not need to be involved in her Colony’s actions. Willpower is not allowed to be spent on Colony actions.

    Conscription Create an army, a Swarm to do your bidding. Roll Military + Authority. Successes translate to the finished Swarm’s Rank. The Monarch then is able to assign the Swarm’s attributes and allocate Aspects as need be. This process can take an hour for every success rolled, though the Monarch need not be present through all of it.

    Wealth may be spent for additional dice on a 1:1 basis.

    Spy Order your colony to Spy on a target location or creature via dispatching smaller Endless. Roll Espionage + Authority to determine their effectiveness at stealth and camouflage. You receive only basic details such as “target is flying away” “target notices us.
    Most opponents also recieve a -5 penalty to detect the spying as most foes do not anticipate bugs turning against them. This penalty may be reduced or eliminated for some more knowledgeably or supernatural foes.
    Wealth may be spent for additional dice on a 1:1 basis.

    Harvest Order your Colony to harvest the Resources in a Province. Roll Industry + Authority, with each roll taking an hour or more depending on distance from the Colony. Successes translates to a number of Resource being removed from the Province and moved to the Colony, up to the limit the Province has remaining.
    Harvested Resources perish or are consumed at the start of a new Story, unless Stockpiled, another action.
    Resources cannot be spent on this action unless the Province requires other resources.


    Research Order your your Colony to perform research and experimentation on your behalf using Knowledge + Authority as a dice pool. Each roll is one hour.
    Wealth may be spent for additional dice on a 1:1 basis.


    Influence Influence another party by dispatching a diplomat of your own. ? Roll Diplomacy + Authority, where Successes translates to X? Only able to be done once to a given party or persons per Story.

    Wealth may be spent for additional dice on a 1:1 basis.

    Mechs: Because even the Chronicles of Darkness needs robot fights.
    DarkFrame: Crossover setting that puts Chronicles of Darkness in the far future that is Warframe.
    Monarch: The Endless You are an alien ruler, charged with maintaining a people who you shape to suit your needs.

                And Provinces are refined further into this shape. Merged Traits and Tags to the same thing for each of communication.


                Provinces
                Provinces are the measurement by which territory is handled in Monarch, with each Monarch possessing at least one Province within the Realm which holds their Colony, this is traditionally given to a new Monarch by petitioning to serve and swear fealty to a Monarch with a number of Provinces. A Monarch is awarded more Provinces through being granted them by higher powers… or taking them by force rather than spending Merits, though Merits can be used to improve Provinces.
                When a Monarch’s final Province is lost to another Monarch, instead of losing the Province, traditionally, the Monarch must swear fealty to the other and become his Vassal, though this doesn’t always happen.
                The size of a Province is varies, though due to spatial warping it is often most common for the area Provinces contain to be much larger or wider in the Beneath than it is in Realspace. Some could be the size of a zoo, a park… or a single office building in the real world, but turn into massive structures in the Beneath.
                Provinces provide a variety of resources, which all directly translate into Wealth that may be spent on Colony actions or be exchanged to other Monarchs. Resources replenish every Story, provided at least a week has passed between points in a Chronicle. Courtless Monarchs in particular also rely on being close to their own or Allied Provinces to be able to communicate to their Colonies.
                In addition all Provinces have special traits and properties that influence how the work, either by providing unique access to certain things, providing special benefits for specific resources in their region, or by
                A Province consists of the following Traits.
                Name A name by which it is addressed or called. Depending on the tastes of the controlling Monarch, the dictates of the local King, names are malleable. With the name of the Province, comes a Title which refers to the Province in formal turns. I.E. Duchy of Bradersik, County of Parador, Island of Mareten, Field of Xyig.
                Resources All Provinces possess at least one raw Resource with a rating of atleast one which once harvested is converted into Wealth (usually) of equal quantity for the Colony and is then able to be spent on various things. Resources replenish once per “Story”.
                The most common types of resources are.
                Biomass, representing both food and genetic material.
                Minerals, representing inorganic, usually mundane materials.
                Radioactives, representing volatile and powerful substances.
                Other resource types are available, and might or might not be specific or unique resources produced by the Province when worked.
                The Resource ratings of a Province can be increased by spending merit dots representing time spent developing its infrastructure either in or out of the Beneath. However, the risk of that is if you lose the Province, that means unless significant damage and property damage has been caused, the the new owner benefits from the previous owners expenditures.


                Traits This is where things get interesting.
                Every Province has one or more Traits, representing unique effects or mechanics specific to the Province.
                When a Province is created, the Storyteller assigns it a relevant trait based on what he or she feels is most appropriate. A Monarch does not get to decide what Traits a Colony possesses.
                Traits can be good or bad, depending on what they are and a Province can have any number of them.
                The Storyteller is able to also remove or add new traits, usually by basing them on plot events or having changes reflect an alteration of the Status Quo.


                Sample Traits


                Template Foundry All Monarchs are able to create Servants, but without the proper infastructure, genetic sequences, or the like, they are limited to creating only the most basic Servants. With a Template Foundry, she is able to create a more powerful, more advanced creation.


                Hazard X The Province is dangerous for some reason, killing Drones fast enough to be a problem. Maybe radiation, forgotten beasts, or more. Harvesting actions done in the Province require that Wealth equivalent to the Hazard rating be spent, representing aggressive replacement costs.

                Evolution Pit A Monarch is powerful but not perfect. She is only a shadow of the True Endless’s perfection. However, some places possess a small fraction of Endless experimentation, largely the result of unfinished but surviving experimentation. By hunting and consuming Biomass from this area, maybe she can approach a True Endless’s power… until her own flawed sequences reassert themselves.
                The Biomass of this Province may instead of being spent in Colony actions, may be consumed by a Monarch. By eating 1 unit of Biomass, the Monarch grows stronger in one fashion. Usually, this is an attribute increase of some magnitude or an increase in her Natural Weaponry, or Shields.



                Servile Mechanoid The Province has a control center, one compromised by Endless growths and corruption. This control center is known to be the primary control apparatus for a powerful construct within the Beneath.
                It is a trivial matter to permanently dispatch some Drones and Foremen to maintain and control this growth and in turn control the construct. Doing this requires Knowledge 4.
                Though not a “real” Endless by any means, this entity can act as a Servant, especially since controlling its command node is taking a good chunk of your psionic powers to do so.
                However, once destroyed, chances are it cannot be reactivated unless its control center can rebuild a replacement.

                Dwellers Not all of those in the Beneath are Endless or Geodics. Sometimes humans find themselves trapped here and stuck for generations, forming settlements. Sometimes, they aren’t human. Those don’t matter to you though, so long as they pay the taxes for your protection.
                Within this Province is a settlement of non-Endless, treated as a seperate Colony with Attributes at the Storyteller’s discretion. They are minor and are subservient to those strong enough to subdue them. Controlling them may have side benefits, but the most obvious one is that the harvest and deliver Resources from the Province to their controller.
                At Storyteller's discretion, they may interact with Traits from other Provinces such as being able to endure the Hazards of another Province at no risk to themselves, being able to control control the Servile Mechanoid, or even be needed to operate Infrastructure of a given Province..
                Regardless of what the Monarch has them do, they can only do one thing.

                Infrastructure, Resource The Province is heavily developed, possessing facilities to aid in Harvesting in a specific resource such as bio furnaces, bore mines, or other extractors. Harvesting actions do not require a roll for a particular resource, though it still requires time to harvest it. This does not affect harvesting other resources, though that does not rule out the existence of other Infrastructure.

                Defensible Terrain The terrain here is favorable for defense. The defending Swarm (usually the owner’s) takes less 2 damage from other Swarms engaging it here.



                Sidebar: “It’s all mine!” A Province is only ever really owned by a single Monarch. Though she may allow Alliance members to benefit from the Province as a show of friendship, this does mean that often times she has to sacrifice some functionality or limit her own use of her own property. This is especially true for Provinces with strong or useful traits that have limited uses in a given Story.
                Another option exists however, in that she may allow others even those outside of her Alliance access to a Province’s resources or ability… for a price.
