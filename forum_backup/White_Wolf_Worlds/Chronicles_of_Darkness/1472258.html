    Antagonist for Public Use: Stolos the Bio-Occultist

    01-11-2022, 04:07 PM
    I made this guy for the Genius 2E Discord as an example of an Illuminated (a mad scientist who has completely abandoned ethics).

    It was pointed out that he's also a great Deviant antagonist, so I'm leaving him here for others to use. Please excuse some of the Genius 2E terminology.

    Stolos the Body Thief, Iluminated Bio-Occultst

    Quote: Your contribution to medicine cannot be understated. Now hold still, there's some enhancements I left there that I'm going to need, and there's still a few tests I want to run.

    Backstory:

    The creature once known as Hijiyama Souma, (a fairly traditional Japanese-American, he put his surname first when given the option) doesn't think of his pre-bodiless past too often. He probably remembers it well enough; part of him downloading himself to a new body involves reconfiguring the brain into something that can support his nuclear dynamo of a mind, and the fact he's still Illuminated instead of accidentally replacing himself with an amnesiac clone is proof it works. It's just irrelevant, like so many other things. But he knows relating how he became a Klagen inspires sympathy, and thus, potential temporary allies, so he's rehearsed the story of how his husband was turned into a vampire and eventually committed suicide after his survival instincts drove him to one too many atrocities, with Souma Catalyzing in the wake to ensure nobody else would suffer like he did. How he, upon understanding what he was, noticed how variable Mania was, and how (relatively) easily it could have Procedures imposed on it, creating a new scientific process from a dream - a repeatable one, not unlike how metanormal beings reproduce. It was a desperate, irrational hope, thinking that the Mania component of vampire blood could be isolated (let alone that it even existed) and used to make a vaccine, but there have been worse options for a Magnum Opus. He's become quite the expert on the metanormal, chasing proof of a Grand Unified Theory of Metabiological Mania, and he's willing to trade for his knowledge...?

    He also remembers how it all went (according to those who still care about amusing concepts like ethics) wrong, as he knows he should lead people away from that to avoid being exposed, which would interfere with his experiments.

    How he realized that he would need to become a living model of a metanormal if he ever wanted to understand their "living Procedure", and from there, that there were so many species of metanormal that he could not be just one - he would need other models. More than that, he needed to experience life as other models, and get a perfect view of their life cycle, strengths, limitations, and worldview. He could modify himself, but that was inefficient - better to create as many subjects as possible, and dance between them. It'd certainly help with changing identities in case the Peerage or even his ostensible allies in Lemuria grew horrified by his experiments and willingness to destroy himself in the name of a cure. It ended inevitably - one day, Souma left his body, and Stolos entered the other, with a sudden realization of how infinitely more efficient it would be if he stopped caring about his identity or past. Or really, much of anything else.

    Today, Stolos is somewhere between a serial killer and an urban legend - or rather, several. In truth, he's not all that great at hiding his crimes, but he changes MO so often - because he's examining different aspects of different species of metanormal beings, some of them violent, some not - that he's been pegged as several different killers, a misunderstanding he happily exploits; while he can never be away from his experiments for long (partly due to his Triggers, partly due to his obsession with efficiency), he knows how to make it seem like he's either not affiliated with, or even hunting down, his other identities. It helps that he's a master actor - while he never stays long in a body without modification, he knows how to use an identity to attain resources.

    Sometimes, that resource is safety, if he feels someone has genuinely harmed his precious efficiency - he will make that person a target for the most dark aspects of his current model metanormal he's studying, systematically destroying their lives and reveling in power. He does not stop swapping bodies when he does so, however - which is one of the few reasons the Peerage is slowly starting to realize the Santa Monica Werewolf, the San Diego Vampire, the Maine Wendigo, and whatever other legend he's made before fleeing to another city while the disruptive heat dies down are linked, along with the occasional "lucky" survivor of his latest bodyswap - even he doesn't realize the capabilities of the hybrid Manes he turns his current body into, and if his latest victim somehow escapes when trapped in his old body, he lets them go to observe if they become the progenitor of a new species - which inevitably means a confused, traumatized, and unique monster with a massive amount of mental confusion from both having had an Illuminated in their brain once and not being the original resident is wandering around, desperately looking for Mania or another metanormal energy to convert into it. The fact this has happened in several cities during a killing spree themed after a metanormal is a big hint that something deeper is happening.

    As a side note, Stolos takes his name from a demon in the Ars Goetia, a teacher of astronomy and Earth sciences that took the form of a great owl. Those keyed into vampire lore may also know of a species of "born vampires" who take the form of shadowy owls who possess the corpses of the dead and living alike, sometimes transforming them into unique monsters. If one were to know this and confront him about the hypocrisy, he'd be flummoxed - he is something resembling that now, and his morbid sense of humor survived Illumination. Irony is inherently funny, not hypocritical.


    Description:
    It almost doesn't matter. While Stolos has some body dysmorphia issues, they aren't quite so pressing as to be distracting, and thus, inefficient - really, if anything, he's happily lean into a body's natural appearance if he wants to manipulate someone the previous owner knows. But it's also efficient to relieve the stress when he's already upgrading and testing out new designs, so all else being equal, he surgically remakes himself into one consistent vision; a mildly attractive Asian man with blue eyes and dirty blonde hair, often with heterochromia and a finely defined musculature. This isn't how Souma looked, it's what he found attractive - and Stolos, naturally, regards what he'd think as attractive as efficient for social manipulation. Of course, it's often hard to tell under the enhancements that he's even in an originally human body, let alone it's something, in a different timeline, he'd think was hot.


    Storytelling Hints:
    Some Illuminated, upon the boiling away of everything in their personality except for what supports their Inspiration, become poisonous stars that set the world alight with nuclear light. Stolos is the more dangerous, slow-burning variety - the kind who resembles a normal Inspired, right up until one realizes every single personality trait he has that is evident beyond the quantum thoughts of raw Mania is based around his Magnum Opus. Nothing matters except his work, and anything he does is geared towards efficiency and to make sure each cycle of his experimentation gets as much data as possible, with as short a time towards the next cycle as possible, as safely as possible. And unfortunately, Stolos is very good at judging what is safe, and what he needs to be to be safe - unlike a true sociopath, Stolos is more than capable of delaying gratification, because the only thing that matters is the data. He will be anything, and can be anything, even trustworthy or a good resource, if it leads to a long-term benefit for his experiments. About the one thing a normal Genius may have over him is patience; Stolos becomes frustrated if he is forced to delay an experiment or redo preparations, so if threatened his next experiments have a tendency to turn spiteful. But even then, it's still not quite hate - he simply recognizes that turning a threat into a new resource for his research is (you guessed it) efficient, using the energy from a fire to put it out. On the occasions he drops his many identities, deciding that dealing with people honestly is the best course of action, what strikes others is that for all of his cruelty, Stolos still thinks what he does is out of love - if he finds Unified Theory of Metabiology, he will have established a starting point to fix everything wrong with every metanormal, the mutated cells will no longer have to be cancer in the body of society. That many other cells will mutate or die is, simply, irrelevant.


    Note: If a Deviant, Stolos is a unique form of Autourgic Renegade; he thinks of himself as striking against the world that took his beloved from him and treating its diseases in the process. His mission gives him a reliable trio of Loyalty Touchstones (the memory of his husband, his mobile lab, and his ultimate mission), and he's generally found in a state of Catharsis - he doesn't care enough about things to hate more than he loves. Yes, this is a cheat not normally possible to players - most people are not so utterly monomaniacally focused on their goals that they regard hopping from body to body and leaving a bunch of confused Ferals in their wake an acceptable cost to medical science, not even Deviants.
