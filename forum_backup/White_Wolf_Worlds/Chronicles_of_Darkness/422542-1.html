                                        I actually just got done reading through some of the Brainspew discussions for 2.0 and feel a bit better. I think there are a few wonky things there, and I'm sure that the grab-bag nature of the contracts might lead to optimizers and people who like gaming the system having characters with a bunch of random, unrelated powers, but you can't design around the fact that some people are going to abuse any system.

                                        Now, as for what I meant by 'WODify' well, in some ways I'm talking about in terms of themes, but some things I see in other World of Darkness books that don't really interest me. End Times/End of the World stuff, for one. Equinox Road was really interesting and I think that part of that, and Changeling as a whole, is the more personal nature of the journey, rather than this epic cosmological battle involving, say, Exarchs, or the apocalypse, or so on. As well, I liked the Court system as the chaotic, variable, non-unified thing it was, and I'm hoping that sticks. I look at Mage or Vampires and it's like, 'A small handful of orders, worldwide?' The grand narrative between a small number of castes and a foe, for instance, always left me a bit cold. Whereas, even just counting the Courts we're given, there are the four seasons (which got the most love but a ST can totally help other Courts out), 4 directions, day and night, and then another on a similar *sort* of theme but very different, and then there are suggestions to make your own, hints at some other ideas (I think they mention political parties offhand) and plenty of variety to create this chaotic, really vibrant world, whereas there's always that temptation to pare things down: Five clans (with sub-bloodlines) and seven Covenants, or five orders versus two evil (or at least antagonistic) orders (Seers and Banishers) and so on and so forth.

                                        It's that element, as well as the interesting side-powers (I could play some really interesting Changelings without taking a dot in any contract using things like Pledges, Talecrafting, hedgspun items and token-mongering).

                                        On the other hand, seeing as they mentioned that they're going to be cutting down the number of custom Court Contracts to one for the core book in order to fit more Courts...I might be worrying over nothing.

                                        WoDify probably was a bad phrase for it, but I was introduced to the World of Darkness via old Vampires and several things like it, and it never really clicked with me until I read Changeling: The Lost. So I'm mostly talking about those elements of world of darkness that I had seen (even if they aren't, actually) as 'typical.' Which is probably a bad way to phrase it, so apologies.
                                        Last edited by The Laurent; 03-09-2015, 02:25 PM. Reason: Clarification.
