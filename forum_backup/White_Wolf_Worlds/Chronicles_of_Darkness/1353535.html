    VTR 2e Blood Scourge

    12-08-2019, 09:08 AM
    I was recently told that Blood Scourge does not deal Lethal damage to vampires because it doesn't specify that it does in its text like Protean 4 does. I decided to embark on an internet journey to find out whether it deals L or B to vampires and have since been convinced that it indeed does L to vampires. How can I convince my ST that it does?

    Proof #1 My own argument that it seems very unlikely the Lancea would produce a Theban Ritual for the sole purpose of dealing Lethal damage to Mortals, when you can just pick up a gun.
    Proof #2 An excerpt from Secrets of the Covenants where a Lancea member uses Blood Scourge to "tear the flesh" of their enemies. That doesn't sound like Bashing damage to me.
    Proof #3 Blood Scourge in Requiem 1e deals Lethal damage due to the sorcery tag Destruction, at least to my understanding.

    What more proof can I use? They think it's unbalanced but considering how much unbalanced stuff there is in Vampire altogether, it seems unfair that you should spend 1 Vitae & 1 WP, an hour in preparation and then produce a Whip using Blood Sorcery just to deal Bashing damage to enemies of the faith for 1 SCENE. I could do that by just using a SWORD. It makes no sense whatsoever for it to deal Bashing damage.

      I agree with your ST.

      Point 1. You can’t take a gun or knife or baton with you everywhere. You “can” cast Blood Scourge and have it ready in a moment’s notice in places where you can’t carry weapons.
      Point 2. Bashing damage eventually upgrades to Lethal damage, ergo repeatedly beating someone with a scourge is going to result in torn flesh. And by the way, a scourge isn’t traditionally just a multi-cord whip, a scourge had barbs, blades, sharpened stones, or glass embedded in them specifically for tearing or cutting.
      Point 3. That’s first edition. In first edition, butter knives did Lethal damage to kindred. That’s not applicable in this edition. Currently swords do Bashing damage to vampires so unless you have a merit or supernatural augmentation to that sword, no you aren’t just picking one up and doing Lethal damage to kindred. Your argument about the activation cost and casting time is irrelevant since it’s equally applicable to every other ritual.

      On it’s face, I think it’s unfair that Cruac and Theban Sorcery bypass resistance checks. But that’s offset by the fact that individual “knows instantly” that they’re being targeted and have a limited time window to move out of reach of the spell or disrupt the casting.

                  Originally posted by Corebook, page 90
                  Kindred take only bashing damage from many sources that would normally cause lethal damage to humans. This includes most non-supernatural weapons. A sword slices through a Kindred body the way it would a human; it’s just that vampires don’t have the same need for stable flesh, working organs, and intact blood vessels. A mortal whose viscera splay across a city street is most certainly doomed, whereas a vampire recovers after only minor inconvenience and a small investment of Vitae.
                  So it comes down to whether you consider Blood Scourge a supernatural weapon or not. I would say, yes, it is supernatural, because it's a whip made of enchanted blood flowing out of your wrists.

                  However, do note that tearing flesh meaning lethal was a 1e thing. In 2e, knives do bashing to vampires because, while they do tear the flesh, vampires don't really need that flesh the same way humans do.
