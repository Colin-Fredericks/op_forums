                                EDIT: I didn't read Arcane's post before posting this one. I agree with Arcane, old school in the middle of renovations is wonderful.

                                Now let's talk about the educational theories the school staff follow. If only because the school's polices could create story hooks
                                If this is a modern private school for rich supernatural kids, then I imagine that it avoids some of the mistakes often made by public school system and is embracing the latest trends in education in part because they have the money needed to do things right.

                                For instance, many school are adopting a program where instead of Fs students get Hs, which means that the student will be given a chance to complete the assignment right (either during study hall after school or even during Saturday school). The idea is that it gives failing students a chance to catch up. A group of students having to make up an assignment they got an H on after school could easliy be a hook for a story

                                Also, year round school might be a thing at this school, many schools are doing a year round deal where you get a 15 day break for every 45 days you go to school. The idea is that it makes it so the teachers don't have to waste time reteaching everything that was forgotten over the summer while still having the same number of school days. Ultimatly, this is so that we can have vacation adventures outside of spring, winter and summer break and so that any summer court changlings don't feel left out of the school hi jinks.

                                Oh and the cafeteria! the dietary needs of some the students could lead to some interesting. stuff I have an idea of the blood substitute variety that could be served there, Vampire watermelons (I'm serious). Some of you may have heard of these from the DiscWorld books, but the late great Sir Terry didn't come up with them himself. A produce of Romani folklore, these fruit leak blood, roll around on their own and make a sound like "Brrl brrl". So what I am think is that rather than being vampires that are watermelons, they are watermelons altered to make human blood instead of watermelon juice. What do you think?
