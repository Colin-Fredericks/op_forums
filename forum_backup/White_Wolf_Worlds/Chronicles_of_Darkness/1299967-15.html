    I hope my compiled citation from Vector chapter is not too much, but it shows how Sworn are generally split over all of monsters ( and hunters ) factions in CoD universe - showing more on the idea what follows around them.
    • Cryptocrats – Authority Vector – Typical members are: beasts, demons, mages and vampires. Joins them Winter Court, Cheiron Group, Task Force: Valkyrie, Maa-Kep, and Undertakers from Sin-Eaters.
    • Jeremiad – Frevor Vector – Typical members are: beasts, hunters, mummies and Prometheans. Joins them Integrators, Lancea et Sanctum, Long Night, Lucifuge, and Malleus Maleficarum
    • Rosetta Society – Semiotics Vector – Typical members are: mage, demons, changelings, hunters and werewolves. Joins too them Circle of the Crone, Sesha-Hebsu, Mourners from Sin-Eaters and Tammuz.
    • Ship of Theseus – Realignment Vector – Typical members are: Prometheans, demons and Sin-Eaters. Joins to them Free Council, Carthian Movement, Ordo Dracul, Iron Masters, Spring Court and Network Zero.
    • Zero Hour – Intel Vector – Typical members are: Forsaken werewolves, hunters, Deviants – and members of Adamantine Arrow, Saboteurs, and Furies from Sin-Eaters.
    Interesting to me are Undertakers in Cryptocrats, Mourners and Tammuz in Rosetta - or why there are only pointed Forsaken in Zero Hour, not whole Uratha race?
