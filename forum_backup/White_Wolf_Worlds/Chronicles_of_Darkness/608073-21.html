    Originally posted by StSword View Post
    I read this book once, don't remember the title, but I thought it had an interesting premise- A man learns a martial art, and begins developing past human limits, but the martial art starts mutating him into something inhuman.

    Lovecraftian ki powers, basically.

    I'm wondering if that sort of thing will work in Deviant. Teaching someone a language of magic, but learning it alters the mind or even the body, mathematical equations that break the mind but unlock psychic powers, biofeedback techniques that mutate the body in monstrous ways, etc.

    Makes the deviants a bit more complicit in their own alteration, but no one said they were told what the results would be, of course.
    That would be a boss background. Dave has said some Deviants are the product of their own work, so I'm sure it should be doable.

At most, their bizarre anatomies and physiology could give them significant bonuses when rolling Stamina to resist powers like Vinculum, a werewolf's scent tracking, a Mage's Life Arcanum and so forth. Not immune, mind you, just very resistant. Kinda like how Deadpool's relative balance between cancer and healing factor makes it hard to use psychic powers on him, or how the Hulk's anger is so intense that reading his thoughts and controlling him is only possible for masters of sorcery like Loki or Doctor Strange. You could have a Merit for Deviants that also applies this extra resistance when rolling Resolve against supernatural powers. Their resistance against mundane and supernatural control of emotions would probably stay the same, though.
