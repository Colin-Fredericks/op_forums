                                  I'm not entirely convinced these settings are useless in 2nd Edition.

                                  Lily, Sabre, and THorn was explictly written for Changeling 1st, sure, but the Underhill based settings of Three Kingdoms of Darkness, Requiem for Regina, and A Grimm Dark Era aren't really that hard to read under 2nd Edition's purview, and for the most part all you have to mechanics wise is apply new 2- and 4- Dot Mantle benefits and otherwise read the Bulwarks as the Give and Takes of the courts in accordance. Grimm Dark Era's weirdnesses of faerie magic wildly changing as fairy tales become more codified can easily be the product of oaths, a Contagion, or just some general fucking weirdness. The Grimms themselves are useless, but that's a minor detail. Most of the contracts work as is, and even correspond to way they're presented in Dark Eras 2.

                                  New fans might be confused, but either they'll seek answers or just adapt. The settings, for the most part, still work quite well*.

                                  Seems like a lot of hooplah over nothing that serious, is all I'm saying.

                                  *Assuming you like Three Kingdoms of Darkness and the changeling section of Requiem for Regina in the first place, which....Well, I didn't and don't.
