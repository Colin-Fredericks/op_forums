    What's YOUR Custom Kiths?

    06-24-2014, 12:53 PM
    So yeah, I'm curious to see what sort of fan made Kiths (if any) you guys have come up with for Changeling the Lost. Below are a handful of my personal creations. Would also appreciate some feed back to see if the powers I gave them are any good. I'm not sure if I made them too OP or not.



    Ogre Kiths


    Ashura: A culture themed Kith of low ranking demigods who are known to possess 3 faces and six arms.

    Blessing - Effortless Multitasking: By spending Glamour, the Changeling can produce additional limbs for the remainder of the scene that enables them to perform up to 3 separate actions at the same time without suffering any penalties for dividing their attention. In combat, this can also be used to perform up to 3 simultaneous attacks in the same turn.


    Greatbeards: Sea fairing Ogres who were used for pirating during their time in Arcadia. Their name is derived from such beings as Blackbeard, Whitebeard, Bluebeard, Redbeard, Brownbeard, and so on.

    Blessing - Captain on Deck: By spending Glamour, the Greatbeard can enhance their commanding presence to rally their allies and intimidate their foes. For the rest of the scene, the Changeling gains +2 to Charisma and Intimidation, as if they were a pirate captain leading the assault on an enemy vessel!



    Beast Kiths


    Tough Hides: Tortoises, shellfish, and other beasts with armored plating instead of exposed flesh.

    Blessing - Armored Shell: By spending Glamour, the Changeling can fortify themselves with natural armor. The amount of armor they gain is equal to the amount of Glamour they spend (+1 armor for 1 Glamour, +2 for 2, est). The armor bonus lasts for the remainder of the scene.



    Hunting Hounds: Expert trackers who hunted down fellow Changelings for sport during their time in Arcadia. Often plagued with guilt after escaping, and distrusted by their fellow Changelings because of their deeds.

    Blessing - Tracker's Nose: By spending Glamour and sniffing a person or their belongings, the Changeling can memorize their scent. From then on, they can track their location by smell, which comes in clear even over various other scents in the area. The subject must have been in the area within the last 48 hours or the scent goes cold for that location.



    Nightmares: Equines who were used to transport their masters through the dreams of mortals during their time in Arcadia. After their escape, they found they have a higher affinity for the dream world than most Changelings.

    Blessing - Dreamwalker's Natural Habitat: The Nightmare gains +2 on any attempts to use offensive powers while in the dream world.



    Darkling Kiths


    Shells: Completely traumatized by their time in Arcadia, these Changelings have forgotten how to express their emotions. Social introverts, they find it even more difficult to associate with others than normal.

    Blessing - Roll with the Pain: By spending Glamour and touching a subject, the Shell is able to render them extremely resilient to trauma by suppressing their ability to feel. For the duration of this power (rest of the scene), the subject is granted a bonus of +1 Willpower for each point of Glamour spent on them. This Willpower can be spent like normal Willpower points, but all of the bestowed Willpower will vanish after the scene, potentially draining the subject of all remaining Willpower if they are careless with its use! The downside to this power is that because the subject is emotionally suppressed, you can not harvest Glamour from them while they are under its effects.



    Fairest Kiths


    Concubine: Forced into the sex trade during their time in Arcadia, these Fairest have learned how to use their sex appeal as a weapon to coax others into dropping their guard.

    Blessing - Royal Consort: By spending Glamour, the Concubine can make those around them more susceptible to their advances, as they find it easier to trust the Changeling and open up to them. For the duration of the scene, the Concubine gets a bonus of +2 to all persuasion and seduction rolls. Additionally, this bonus applies to the next attack they perform against that subject (treated as a surprise attack). However, attacking a subject instantly negates the power and can not be reused against them for the next 72 hours.




    Wizened Kiths

    Pixie: The remains of children who were abducted by the Gentry when they were too young to fully understood what happened to them. Becoming mischievous pranksters after their escape. they now amuse themselves at the expense of others.

    Blessing - Child's Play: By spending Glamour, the Pixie is able to construct a booby trap (they call it a Prank) which will be set off when someone fulfills the condition to activate it (stepping on a wire, opening a container, est). The more Glamour they spend, the more complicated the prank can be from as simple as a can of powder, to cartoonishly rigging an entire room with a series of complex gadgets.

    For each point of Glamour spent, the Pixie can rig 1 additional step to the trap, as well as increase its damage potential (Bashing or Lethal) by +1. So by spending 3 Glamour, they could rig a trap that has 3 steps to it, and inflicts 3 points of either Bashing or Lethal damage. For every 2 steps the trap is comprised of, it takes 1 round to hit. So a 1 step trap would hit instantly, a 2-3 step trap would take 1 round, a 4-5 step trap would take 2 rounds, and so on.


    Additional Effects and Costs

    Poison - +1 Glamour equal to its potency

    Blind (2 rounds) - +2 Glamour

    Deafen (2 Rounds) - +2 Glamour

    Knock Down (1 Round) +1 Glamour for Small, +2 for Medium, +3 for Large targets

    Unconscious - +1 Glamour equal to amount of hours desired. Subject can be forcefully awoken by special means.

      Off the top of my head, a lot of these seem overpowered if only because they scale-remember, most kith blessings are around +2, +3 for those that only come up in really specific circumstances. Concubines and Greatbeards, with their kith bonuses set to flat +2, would look balanced to me. I can't remember the name off the top of my head but I think there's already a Beast kith that grants armor, and 2 per dot of Wyrd is absurd-at Wyrd 3 that lets you spend 1 Glamour and effectively be invincible for a scene unless the GM starts throwing explosions around. Nightmares also get too much of a bonus-even at only +2, a flat bonus to all actions taken in dreams just seems OP to me.
      Hunting Hounds are a good idea, although I don't think you need to make them pay another Glamour to continue tracking a scent.

      My favorites of these thematically are the Shell and the Pixie, both of which I think need their blessings redone. The Pixie's blessing needs at least some damage/Glamour expenditure guidelines, and the Shell blessing is useless to anyone without derangements. Maybe Shell's blessing could be something Willpower-focused?

      I drew up one or two Beast kiths a while ago while I was bored, let me see if I can find them.

                                  Got some more. ^_^


                                  Scarred Ones: Tortured to death and revived an uncountable number of times during their stay in Arcadia, these crazed Ogres no longer fear death in any form! Their bodies are often marked by hundreds of scars, as if they were sewn back together!

                                  Blessing - Wouldn't be the First Time: Having experienced countless tortures at the hands of the True Fae, the Scarred Ones have extremely high pain thresholds. By spending Glamour, they can negate Wound Penalties on a 1 for 1 payment




                                  Beverages: These liquid Elementals were turned into drinks to satiate the thirst of their masters. Milk, wine, beer, est, they still carry the touch of their durances in their veins.

                                  Blessing - Quenching Blood: By spending Glamour and cutting themselves, the Elemental can transmute their own blood into a certain type of drink. If consumed, their blood will not only quench a person's thirst, but also transfer the spent Glamour over to them. The amount of Glamour spent determines how much is transferred. Using this power causes 1 lethal damage, plus an additional +1 lethal for every 2 extra Glamour spent. So spending 3 Glamour would inflict 2 Lethal damage upon the Beverage.



                                  Traders: Wizened who were placed in charge of their master's financial accounts, or whatever passed for such in Arcadia. Legendary investors, bankers, and merchants, they have a knack for acquiring wealth.

                                  Blessing - Midas Touch: By spending Glamour and touching an inanimate object, the Banker can vastly improve its market value. When attempting to sell or trade the designated object, they get a +2 on all rolls involving haggling or persuasion.




                                  Imp: Child sized devils who are the remains of unruly children. It is speculated that the Gentry may have believed their parents did not do a proper job disciplining them, but no conclusive proof has been found to support this. Their time spent in Arcadia has turned these wizened into vulgar brats, who manage to effortlessly goad others into violent outbursts.

                                  Blessing - Little Hellspawn: The Imps are infamous for their disrespect and improper conduct. By spending a point of Glamour, the Imp can perform a display that is so insulting, their target loses the ability to think straight and becomes more prone to violence. The subject must roll a save of Wits + Composure to avoid falling for the trick, but as long as the Imp continues to spend Glamour, they can maintain the display until their subject finally snaps! The cost of using this blessing is 1 Glamour for each target they attempt to effect for that turn (3 targets would require the spending of 3 Glamour).

                                  Subjects who fall for the Imp's display suffer a -2 on all social and mental rolls for the rest of the scene, but receive a +2 on all physical actions.





                                  Toon: A culture themed Fairest who's beauty is depicted in popular cartoon series, more specifically Japanese anime. Supernaturally gifted warriors, they can withstand unbelievable levels of abuse before falling in battle. Their durances were often spent providing entertainment for their Keepers in the form of TV and comic book characters. How the Gentry came to learn about cartoons and comics is anyone's guess.

                                  Blessing - Cartoon Violence: in many popular cartoons, the characters are seen to endure attacks which would pulverize most humans, and yet get right back up and continue the fight. These Fairest are able to mimic this level of resilience to a certain degree. By spending Glamour, a Toon can soak 1 point of damage on a 1 for 2 cost (spending 4 Glamour soaks 2 damage, est). This power lasts for a number of attacks equal to half their Wyrd, up to a maximum of 5 attacks at level 10, minimum of 1 attack at level 1.

                                  When it wears off, they must spend Glamour again to reactivate it, or their protection will fade and they become vulnerable again. Once they spend the Glamour they are stuck with that amount of protection until it wears off. Meaning they can not spend 3, and then upgrade it to 5 before the 3 wears off.





                                  Minotaur: The bull headed wardens of labyrinths, dungeons, and mazes. These Ogres were used as jail keepers to prevent their fellow Changelings from escaping Arcadia. Ironically being prisoners themselves, their freedom came on the day they accidentally stumbled upon the hidden exit during patrol.


                                  Blessing - Dungeon Master: Having spent so much time patrolling halls and corridors, the Minotaur has manifested a 6th sense when it comes to finding their way around. By spending a point of Glamour, the Minotaur gains a +2 on attempts to navigate any indoor structure. They have a subconscious awareness of the location of everyone and everything. This effect lasts for the remainder of the scene, or until the Minotaur stops spending Glamour to renew it. If the Minotaur steps outside, they likewise deactivate the power.

                                      Excalibur: Transformed into sentient weapons and wielded by their Keepers throughout many battles, these Elementals have an unrivaled comprehension of how to fight with their former bodies.

                                      Blessing - Bond of the Master: Having spent time as an actual weapon, the Excalibur has a deeper understanding of how to wield it. At character generation, the Changeling selects 1 specific weapon class and gains a +2 bonus on any attack rolls made with that weapon.

                                      Class Examples: Short Swords (1 handed), Long Swords (2 handed), Axes (1 handed), Battle Axes (2 handed) Hammers (1 handed), Warhammers (2 handed), Spears, Pole Arms (Staff), Clubs, Maces, Daggers, Bow & Arrow, Crossbow



                                      Gorgon: Mortals who were so beautiful, they offended an envious Gentry. These Ogres have been twisted into hideous creatures in order to satisfy their Keeper's vanity.

                                      Blessing - Gaze of Stone: When they were in Arcadia, anyone who looked upon a Gorgon's face would be turned to stone. After their escape, this curse has been greatly weakened, and can even prove beneficial to a cunning Gorgon. By spending Glamour, the Gorgon can inflict paralysis in those who look at their face, lasting for a number of hours equal to how much Glamour is spent. A physical shock (such as pain) can break the spell, however, as the victim is knocked back to their senses.

                                      The subject must make a composure roll in order to retain their mobility in the face of such a disgusting figure.



                                      Shade: Living embodiments of darkness, these Darklings are virtually impossible to capture at night. They are highly effective spies, assassins, and other covert agents in the Free Holds.

                                      Blessing - Can't Catch a Shadow: Shades are at one with the darkness itself. When they are standing in a shadow, they can spend Glamour to become incoporeal darkness. They must continue to spend 1 Glamour for each round this blessing remains active.

                                      The blessing is dispelled by either stopping the Glamour payment, moving outside of the shadow, or banishing the shadow with some kind of light source. While incorporeal, the Shade is immune to conventional attacks, but is likewise restricted in what manner of attacks they can perform.




                                      Tendril Arms: Octopi, Squids, Jellyfish and other forms of tentacled Beasts. The Tendril Arms are naturally talented wrestlers, who have a knack for grappling their opponents into submission.


                                      Blessing - Tentacle Hold: The Tendril Arms gain a +3 bonus when attempting a grapple attack. This bonus applies to both initiating the grapple, as well as maintaining it should the subject attempt to break free.



                                      Teddy: These poor bastards were changed into stuffed animals and used as toys for their Keepers to play with. Having endured countless tea parties, playing dress up and other acts of public humiliation and degradation, these Wizened have no tolerance for children and become quickly agitated with anyone who comments on their cute, plushy appearances.

                                      Blessing - Just a Doll: By spending Glamour, the Teddy can enter a state in which they appear to be an inanimate object, like a common stuffed toy. They are still aware of what goes on around them (making this an effective blessing for spying), but they can not react to their environment. Just a Doll requires they spend 1 Glamour to last for the remainder of the scene.

                                      The effect can be canceled early to regain their mobility, but costs an additional Glamour to do so. If the Teddy has no Glamour to cancel it, this can leave them extremely vulnerable to attack!

                                                  Crossposting from Arkham. I know the Leviathan kith blows the Swimmerskin/Waterborn out of the water, but I really don't think something as situational as "breathe water for a scene" is potent enough to be a whole kith blessing. The Manikin kith was my guideline for the powerlevel on that - very situational bonus, and a cost break on Contracts - and I think it works fine.

                                                  Beast
                                                  Leviathan: Beneath the surface of Arcadia’s seas lurk all manner of especially dreadful Fae, dead but dreaming in lightless depths. A Lost taken to such a domain must either learn to breathe water or quickly die, and as such develop The Lungs of a Fish: by spending a point of Glamour, the Leviathan may breathe underwater for the rest of the scene. They may also purchase Contracts of Elements (Water) at a rate of (new dots x5 Experience Points), to reflect their affinity with the brine.
                                                  Darkling
                                                  Grotesque: Within Arcadia lurk all manner of hideous or faceless horrors that react with fury to a human visage, shaping flesh like clay into it is horribly distorted. The Lost that suffer such a fate Wear No Mask: by spending a point of Glamour, they may reveal their true face to a single target, inflict a -3 penalty to all rolls for a number of turns equal to the Grotesque’s Wyrd unless they pass a Resolve + Composure roll. The Grotesque may only Wear No Mask once per scene.
                                                  Elemental
                                                  Massive: In the cold reaches of space lurk black holes so massive and dense that everything, light included, is drawn into its gravity and crushed beyond any hope of escape. Some Fae lurk in similarly crushing realms (or are massive enough to have this gravity themselves), and impart upon the Massive the gift of Apocalyptic Gravity: by spending a point of Glamour, the Massive may add his Wyrd as a bonus to any rolls made to grapple.
                                                  Fairest
                                                  Hierophant: Many Keepers style themselves as deities, demanding those they abduct worship them with proper fervor or suffer immensely. Those who stand out in these cults are elected priests and become Hierophants, gaining the Zealot’s Fervor: the Hierophant gains a +2 bonus to any roll made to rile up a crowd and may spend one Glamour to gain the Inspiring merit for the rest of the scene (if he has the Inspiring merit, then he gains a +1 bonus to that Presence + Socialize roll).
                                                  Ogre
                                                  Cancerous: Some portions of Arcadia are pestilent, inhabited by creatures of blight, corruption, and radiation that bring slow death to all but the hardiest of Lost. Those that survive are riddled with growths, gaining the Tumor’s Toughness: the Cancerous may spend a point of Glamour to ignore all wound penalties and penalties caused by pain and discomfort for the rest of the scene. They also gain a +2 bonus to rolls made to resist poisons and disease.
                                                  Wizened
                                                  Architect: Many Keepers rely on bizarre mathematics to keep their impossible domains functioning properly, and the understanding of these strange principles is a rare an unpleasant gift indeed. For those who have beheld the cyclopean towers of Arcadia, there is nothing more delightful than the study of Hypergeometry: the Architect gains a +1 bonus to any rolls made to conceal or protect his Hollow. He may also buy the Hollow merit and merits relating to Hollows (Archive, Bastion, etc) at a discounted rate of (new dots x1 Experience Points).

                                                      Ogre Kiths


                                                      Oafish: The world's strongest creatures are often the stupidest. Lost who became Oafish were given the exclusive task of bashing heads and weren't supposed to think any further beyond that. After having an uncharacteristically high moment of enlightenment, they managed to escape back to the mundane world, but they continue to suffer headaches whenever they try to think too much.

                                                      Blessing - Brawn over Brains: When it comes to talents, the Oafish are far more gifted to brute strength than cognitive functions. An Oafish is able to buy Physical attributes at 1/3 the normal experience cost and physical skills at 1/2 the normal cost. However attempting to purchase Mental attributes costs 3x as much experience, and attempting to purchase mental skills costs 4x as much



                                                      Juggernaut: Living wrecking balls, the Juggernauts are Lost who became nearly unstoppable forces on the battlefield. Plowing through enemy ranks with enough force to send people flying, only a fool would stand between these Ogres and their target.

                                                      Blessing - Unstoppable Force: By spending a point of Glamour, the Juggernaut can empower their next physical attack to send enemies with a Size of 8 or less flying a number of yards equal to their Wyrd level x3. This effects all enemies they have successfully hit for that attack, not just the first one to be impacted, so if there is a clustered group, they could very much send them all hurtling through the air! Enemies who size exceeds 8, must roll a fortitude + composure save or be knocked down, but are too heavy to actually hurl thru the air.





                                                      Atlas: Forced by their Keepers to carry impossibly hefty loads, these Lost would obliterate any power lifting competition! While in Arcadia, it is said they were used to lift entire cities, continents, or planetary bodies. After escaping to the mundane world again, they find they have retained a portion of their titanic strength.


                                                      Blessing - Weight of the World: By spending a point of Glamour, the Atlas can add 2x their Wyrd score to the maximum weight class they are able to lift over their heads, up to a maximum of +20 at level 10. This only applies to the amount of weight they can lift, and does not have any additional benefit to their combat effectiveness, unless they plan to throw an immensely heavy object.




                                                      Hoodsman: Huge, silent executions given a black cowl and a massive axe, these Lost where used to deliver punishment upon other Changelings who defied their Keepers. Their freedom came on the day when they found themselves the object of their Keeper's disdain and realized they had to escape before they were placed on the chopping block. The cowl they wear seems to have fused itself with the flesh of their Miens, rendering it impossible to remove.

                                                      Blessing - Public Execution: The Hoodsmen were used to perform swift punishments to appease their masters. For every 2 levels of Wyrd, the Hoodsman inflicts an additional +1 Lethal damage when using a bladed weapon, up to a maximum of +5 at level 10, and a minimum of +1 at level 1.

                                                          Morals: although given feral bodies, these Beasts retained their human intelligence and the capacity to speak. The reason they did not escape their durances sooner is because they lacked the proper biology to do so; it's kind of hard to pick a lock or hold a weapon when you don't have any fingers. Morals are such creatures as the Billy Goats Gruff, the 3 Blind Mice, the 3 Little Pigs, the Big Bad Wolf, or the Tortoise and the Hair, just to name some examples.

                                                          Blessing - Moral of the Story: In many fairy tales, the animals tend to outsmart their enemies in cunning and deceptive ways by playing upon their Vices and insecurities. By spending a point of Glamour, the Moral gains a +2 bonus on all Social rolls for the rest of the scene. Morals are quite fond of teaching their enemies an important life lesson by using this Blessing to get the upper hand.

                                                          Kiths - Any Beast + Author




                                                          Selkie: These mythical shapeshifting seal folk amused their Keepers by changing between two different forms, often several times away. In order to facilitate the change, they were given a magical pelt to wear and remove depending on their Keeper's present mood. Unfortunately their lives were bound to the pelt at the time of receiving it, and they are extremely protective of them to the point of paranoia. Many Selkie are beautiful to look at, and so their Keepers used their Pelts as blackmail to keep them enslaved. One day, the Selkie manages to steal their pelt away and escape through the Hedge again!


                                                          Blessing - Selkie Pelt: By spending a point of Glamour, the Selkie can summon their Pelt into their hands and don it in the same action, or they can remove the pelt and banish it in the same action. When wearing the pelt, they transform into a Seal, and when not wearing the pelt, they look like humans. When banished, the Selkie Pelt returns to whatever hiding place the Selkie wishes.

                                                          WARNING: Both the Selkie Pelt and the Changeling have the same number of health boxes, and damaging one of them harms the other. If either one is killed, they are both destroyed. Additionally if the pelt is moved from its hiding spot by anyone other than its owner, the Selkie can not summon or banish it again until they manage to physically touch it. This means anyone who steals their pelt can hold it for ransom or use it to blackmail them.

                                                          Unicorn: The very symbol of purity and innocence, these horned equines are so pure that their mere touch banishes contaminates on contact. Unfortunately their durances were spent in the form of janitorial duties, cleaning up the filth and pollution that infested their Keeper's domains! All recorded Unicorns who are willing to discuss it have confirmed they were virgins prior to their abduction, which suggests their Keepers may believe virginity has some relation to purity.

                                                          Blessing - Cleansing Hoof: No disease or filth can touch a Unicorn's body without being obliterated instantly. By spending a point of Glamour and touching a person, place, or thing, the Unicorn will cure any disease or poison afflicting them. When touching the ground or a body of water, the Unicorn is able to affect a range of 3 yards equal to their Wyrd level, up to 30 yards at level 10.

                                                          Harpy: Vicious bird women who were incapable of getting along with anyone including each other. Whatever reason for their abduction, many earn their freedom when even their own Keepers are no longer able to tolerate their Incessant Griping. Many Harpies appear as ugly hags marking them as part Ogre for all to see, and they have the foul tempers to match!

                                                          Blessing - Incessant Griping: The voice of a Harpy is a shrill and grating sound which quickly grows on everyone's nerves. Enemies who are within earshot of this and are capable of hearing it suffer from a -2 on all action rolls as they are distracted trying to block the Changeling's merciless voice. The effects of Incessant Griping last for 1 round equal to their Wyrd Score, making it more potent over time as they continue to accrue further problems to deal with.
