    Asian Changeling book

    08-25-2021, 10:57 PM
    In a discussion with Shinsoryuu, we both shared our interest in a Changeling book that focused on Asia. This is especially interesting to me, because I was born in Tokyo, Japan and lived and worked in China for three years.

    I would really enjoy a corebook that explored the different legends around Asia, looked at the difference between the Western and Eastern variations of Arcadia and the Hedge, and explored the long historical connection between the East and West.

    One way I did the was through Cathayans; Burmese, Cambodians, Chinese, Koreans, Laotians, Japanese, and Vietnamese the were made the changelings of WESTERN true fae.

    I also remember, the someone had created a court system based on the legends of Journey to the West.

    How have you guys used Asia lore in your changeling games? I am interested in hearing peoples ideas.

      Less ‘game’ and more ‘brainstorming and procrastinating ramblings sitting at the back of my brain.’ I’m writing the below down to save it somewhere other than my grey matter:

      The Four Princes take their name from Sino-Korean Confucian art motifs of the same name. Originally branches of the Directional Courts, during the Joseon dynasty they have inherited and supplanted them. They reigned in Seoul and the Kyongi province, competing with the regional Seasonal Courts of the other provinces. The rivalries continued until Imperial Japan attempted cultural ethnocide on the peninsula in their last throes during WW2, shattering both sides’ power bases. In modern times the two sides have reconciled, but in favor of the Four Princes due to their hegemony over the capital and claims to tradition.

      Each Court claims one of the Princes as their name, honors the Virtue attributed to them, and harvests the emotion at the Root of that virtue according to Korean neo-Confucian philosophy.
      The Plum Blossoms Court upholds the Virtue of Compassion, and harvests the Root emotion of Pity. Their Mantles evoke the scent of Asian plum fruits, the gentle eastern wind of spring, growth spurts of wood and grass, and azure hues.
      The Orchid Court upholds the Virtue of Propriety, and harvests the Root emotion of Concession. Their Mantles evoke the scent of Asian orchid flowers, the warm southern wind of summer, flames burning with purpose, and vermillion hues.
      The Chrysanthemum Court upholds the Virtue of Justice, and harvests the Root emotion of Shame. Their Mantles evoke the scent of chrysanthemum flowers, the refreshing western wind of autumn, the sheen and clanging sound of metal, and white hues.
      The Bamboo Court upholds the Virtue of Wisdom, and harvests the Root emotion of Judgment. Their Mantles evoke the scent of bamboo sprouts and snowed earth, the cold northern wind of winter, hidden flows of deep waters, and black hues.

                  The draft for Changeling society in Japan had courts based on the stuff Kaguya asked her suitors to find. You had:
                  • The Court of the Fire Rat, whose emotion is defiance
                  • The Court of the Dragon's Jewel, whose emotion is conformity/tradition
                  • The Court of the Begging Bowl, whose emotion is harmony/peace
                  As another reference to Kaguya, the changeling with the highest authority in Honshu is called the Moon Princess. Moon Princesses are always female and also have a Leadership Council that helps communicate the various needs and platforms of regional leaders to her.

                  I think the draft for "Japan Lost" can still be found somewhere on this forum, though you may have to dig around a bit to find it. Please keep in mind that it was written around a time when some concepts were different than what ended up in the final corebook (e.g. Huntsmen in the previous drafts were very different than the finalized Huntsmen).
