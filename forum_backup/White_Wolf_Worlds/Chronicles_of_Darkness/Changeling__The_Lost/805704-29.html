You assume the changeling is acting like a Fairest, nobly sacrificing themselves for others. It doesn't have to be that way. It can be that the darkling enjoys being in danger, facing what nobody else does, taking on the monster everyone fears. And if it helps his friends, then hey, bonus.

                                    Originally posted by ReshyShira View Post

                                    Originally posted by ReshyShira View Post
                                    As for the last thing regarding 1/6th being backstabbing scumbags, that might actually be within the theme of the game. For instance, if no one trusts the Darklings then they'll be on the fringe of society, playing into their themes of abandonment. It would also be pragmatic, in a way. If the freehold is attacked, they can easily distance themselves from it. They aren't as tied down by the courts as well (Not counting mystic oaths).
                                    I think Loyalists cover the "outcast" and "backstabbing scumbag" Seeming a lot better than Darklings. Darklings, to me, are the rogues of the freehold. Some may be backstabby, but others can play on the "lovable rogue" archetype and be cast in a more positive light. Hell, from the Darkling themes, you'd think there would be an equal amount of darklings who accept betrayal as a part of who they are and darklings who loathe betrayal because they've suffered it themselves.


                                    Originally posted by ReshyShira View Post
                                    Lastly on the concept of character growth, the problem is that their curse inhibits their opportunities at growth. Maybe this has been addressed, but I think that the curse would be better if they lost clarity whenever someone betrays them that they trust (or think that they've been betrayed), rather than it being a breaking point for the mere act of trusting. As far as I know, from what has been released so far, there does not seem to be any way to circumnavigate your Curse. This means, to a greater or lesser degree, that the Darkling can never truly trust anyone.
                                    It is entirely possible that, if you don't mind your Clarity going up and down fairly often, to trust people as a darkling while making the hard decisions for them. In fact, both things complement each other that way. The changeling loses sanity when they trust, so they reclaim their agency by making the hard choices for those they care about.

                                    Originally posted by ReshyShira View Post

It's entirely possible that a darkling enjoys taking risks because they are things others avoid, because doing that thing everyone is too afraid to do gives them a sense of agency that reminds them of their escape. Hell, take this example of a darkling that escapes Arcadia: A bunch of changelings are locked up and forced to do whatever, and the Keeper says that whoever passes the Trial of the Seven Fires can claim the Golden Key and escape. However, there is only one Key and only one changeling can escape. The changeling that chooses to go through the Trial that everyone else is too afraid to do, claims the Key (and technically "betrays" the others by taking their means of escape) can easily become a daredevil that thrives when they face something that others are too cowardly to face.

Any Durance where the Keeper forces changelings to step on each other in order to escape and/or survive spawns darklings that didn't mean to betray anyone and may feel horrible about it.
