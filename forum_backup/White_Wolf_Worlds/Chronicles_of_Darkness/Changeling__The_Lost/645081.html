    3 new Contracts - Critiques sought : the Hunt, Dominion, and Fleeting Dreams

    08-30-2015, 03:23 AM
    Greetings all,

    I've drafted 3 antagonist Contracts for my upcoming chronicle. This is my first time trying my hand at writing Contracts, so I'd really appreciate any Critiques or Feedback you can provide.
    I'm also uncertain about my dot ratings, cost, and catches, so pointers for those would also be great.

    I think I have final versions of these contracts which I really like and work well with the concepts behind them. I'm still a little uncertain of their mechanics as far as balancing goes, so I'd really appreciate critiques of dot ratings, cost, and how well the mechanics work; most especially for Hunt 1 & 2 and Dominion 4 - clauses that have been giving me issues, and which I just reworked.

    [Note: I designed these my home-brewed setting, specifically the Loyalist Courts I'm using. They should still work elsewhere, though. If you're interested or want context, my write-up for that faction can be found Here.
    I also tried to include both 1e and 2e rules where possible for flexibility.
    The spoilers weren't working with the Conditions, so I just removed them rather than having them broken up into 2 balloons.Sorry, I know it disrupts the flow.]

    Thank you for your help, and hope you enjoy
    ~Seraph


    Contracts of the Hunt (Loyalists)
    Loyalists of any variety are charged with the grim duty of kidnapping humans for their masters in Arcadia; these Contracts were forged by their masters to significantly empower the changeling’s abilities to do so. It is to a dark purpose that changelings learn these Contracts, but merely using them does not require a Clarity roll - though they do propel the changeling toward choices that do.

    Tracing the Fading Footsteps (•) [Troublesome Clause; New Draft - see post #7]
    Use of this Clause ensures that the changeling will be able to find the target again, once they’ve chosen them to be taken by the Others.
    Cost: 1 Glamour to mark + 1 Glamour to follow
    Dice Pool: Survival + Wyrd versus Wits + Stealth
    Action: Instant and Contested (resistance is reflexive)
    Duration: One Season
    Catch: Gift an object to the target; they do not have to accept it, it can merely be placed on their person. This has the Drawback of leading the changeling to the object, however, rather than the target.
    The cost to mark is waived for any target with whom the changeling has a Pledge; the cost to follow is waived until the first sunrise after marking a target.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling marks another character within eyesight instead of their chosen target. If they gave an object to their intended target, it immediately changes hands through the whim of Fate, unbeknownst to the changeling.
    Failure: The changeling is unable to mark the target to be followed, but won’t be aware of this until they attempt to do so.
    Success: To activate this Clause, the changeling spends 1 Glamour and rolls for activation to commit one target whom they can see to be followed. From then until the next change of the seasons, the changeling may spend 1 Glamour to be led to the target. They will not know where that location is, they will merely be guided there on the wings of chance. Ordinarily, they will follow the target’s most commonly (or most recently) used route to the their location. The changeling will know when they are approaching line-of-sight with the target, allowing them to stop before they are noticed.
    This Clause cannot be used to track targets who are not currently on the same plane of existence.
    Exceptional Success: The changeling will get some insight into where the target is at the moment they begin following the target.


    Spotting the Vulnerable (••) [Troublesome Clause; New Draft - see post #7]
    The Loyalist on the hunt uses this Clause to search out a particular weakness in the crowd, helping them better select the ideal victim for their needs.
    Cost: 1 Glamour + 1 Glamour (optional)
    Dice Pool: Wits + Wyrd versus (highest) Resolve + Subterfuge
    Action: Instant and Contested (resistance is reflexive)
    Catch: The changeling poses a riddle or a leading question to the group; it must be at least logically related to the trait they’re looking for. No one is forced to answer, but will have subconscious reactions which reveal who has the sought trait.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling focuses in on a detail which they misinterpret for the weakness in question; the target they find not only isn’t vulnerable to the weakness being sought, but likely has a significant advantage over the changeling which they aren’t aware of.
    Failure: The changeling finds no one which has the weakness they’re seeking, and isn’t certain if they overlooked something or if no such target it present.
    Success: When using this Clause, the changeling specifies a Vice, Flaw, or Derangement (or Condition) which they’re searching for. Upon a success, they are either able to notice in a crowd who has the weakness specified, or they learn that no one in this area does and are refunded the Glamour. Anyone which the trait immediately stands out to the changeling. When they find someone with the desired trait, the changeling has the option to spend an additional Glamour to learn a few details about their particular weakness.
    Storytellers are encouraged to allow closely related traits to all be recognized, rather than being overly specific about what needs to be specified: for instance, looking for Gluttony may also find people with an addiction; a player also should not need to specify between mild or severe versions of a derangement.
    Exceptional Success: On an exceptional success, if multiple people have the weakness sought, the changeling automatically focus on which one is most exploitable; in addition, they also learn another one of the target’s traits which they can take advantage of.


    Will-o-the-Wisp (•••)
    The changeling weaves together a simple illusion or a series of them designed to catch a target’s attention and draw them, curious or impassioned, to a location of the changeling’s choosing.
    Cost: 1 Glamour per illusion (per roll)
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Wyrd versus Intelligence + Composure
    Action: Extended and Contested (each roll represents about 10 seconds)
    Catch: The changeling has stolen something that the target wants back, and is using that as the illusion.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: If the changeling dramatically fails a roll or loses a round in the contest by 5 or more successes, the target is clued in that something is wrong about the situation. They may stop following the illusions altogether, or may decide to continue suspecting that it’s a trap.
    Failure: On any roll that the changeling fails, the illusion that they made didn’t lure the target any closer, but didn’t lose their interest. Perhaps something started distracting the target, or their illusion appeared in the wrong place or with an off detail which resulted in them not noticing. On the next roll, the changeling takes a -1 penalty.
    Success: The changeling weaves together a string of simple, short-lived illusions. They may be the sound of fighting, or crying, or the glitter of gemstones; whatever form the changeling desires. These illusions affect only one or two senses, and last only a couple of rounds - enough to peak the curiosity of their target. Each round in the contest, the changeling creates one such illusion to draw in their target to a desired location. The target number represents how far away and how isolated the changeling is trying to get the target to be: getting them to enter a shop they pass by from drawing their attention to the window may only take 1 success; luring them down a string of alleyways where they have total concealment from casual observers may take 10.
    The changeling must have line of sight on both their target and the illusion in order to use this Clause, but may do so indirectly, so as with live video feeds.
    Exceptional Success: If the changeling rolls 5 more successes than their target on any roll in the contest, the potent illusion tugs heavily on their heartstrings, causing the target to throw caution to the wind; they take a -2 penalty on further rolls in the contest and to Perception rolls for noticing their surroundings.


    Divide the Herd (••••) - [thank you AceofAlmonds!]
    This clause physically separates a target from a group with a barrier. It's not very useful on an open plain, but in a limited space, it can separate a mark from their friends, or provide an effective roadblock against pursuers.
    Cost: 3 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Subterfuge + Wyrd - target Dexterity
    Action: Instant, contested
    Catch: The target is currently lost in a maze (including the Hedge).
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: A barrier springs up, but contrary to the user’s intention (separating them and their target, or trapping them with their pursuers). The barrier is treated as having two success.
    Failure: No barrier appears.
    Success: A barrier, up to 50 feet long, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick, springs up between the target and the nearest group of people, and lasts for the scene. If the barrier intersects with an existing barrier (such as a wall, ceiling, or the Thorns), it stops at that point, but in such a way as there is no gap. The wall looks like it's made out of a logical material for the area: concrete in an alley, rock in a canyon, thick brush in a forest. However, the wall is treated as having a Structure and Durability equal to successes rolled. Climbing rolls take a penalty equal to successes. The barrier acts as full concealment, and muffles sound from the other side.
    Exceptional Success: No further benefit beyond more successes.


    Memory’s Forgetful Fog (•••••)
    After successfully capturing someone for the True Fae, their greatest risk is that of discovery - and the retribution that follows. To better cover up their grim taks, they employ this Clause to cloud over suspicious memories in the minds of those closest to the missing victim.
    Forming this curse is a complicated weaving of Glamour, looking like the grand curses of the sorceresses in classic fairytales. It takes some time and privacy to complete, and must be done in the target’s home or the last place they were in the mortal realm.
    Cost: 3 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Occult + Wyrd versus Presence + Wryd
    Action: "Instant"; it takes about 15 minutes to complete.
    Catch: If a Fetch is left in the place of the target, the Cost is reduced to 2 Glamour. Instead of entirely forgetting the target, those cursed instead forget any unusual circumstances surrounding their disappearance and replacement.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling weaves a curse of Glamour, but the effect backfires. Rather than suppressing memories of the target and their disappearance in the minds of those affected, the Glamour highlights the suspicious circumstances and draws increased attention and scrutiny.
    Failure: The changeling fails to create the curse. They are aware of the failure, and can attempt it again, however successive attempts take a cumulative -1 penalty.
    Success: Upon successful use of this Contract, the changeling creates a curse which fogs memories about the target, pushing them from the minds of those closest to them. Family and coworkers just forget that the target is part of their lives, and avoid thinking about them. This doesn’t suppress any memories, per se but causes those affected to redirect their thoughts and attention. Memories involving the disappearance of the target and unusual circumstances leading up to it are pretty potently blocked. As a consequence, they can notice something is wrong if another person asks about the missing target, which causes them to confront the strange blank spots in their memory.
    Once a victim of this curse starts to confront the memory gaps and the fog which causes them to stop thinking about it, they can begin to overcome it. If necessary, this can be handled as an Extended action using Resolve, aiming to achieve the changeling’s Wyrd in successes; however it is best handled as a result of intense therapy or investigation into the matter.
    Each success on activating the contract effects one person who was close to the target; the changeling has no knowledge or control over which people are effected, however the curse first targets witnesses to the disappearance or suspicious events leading up to it, and those most likely to notice the disappearance.
    The Storyteller is encouraged to roll this Clause for the player in secret, to enhance the character’s paranoia over not knowing who, or even how many, they affected.
    Exceptional Success: Those affected by this curse will not simply forget the target, but will invent a story to justify their disappearance to themselves; they will share this story to any who ask about the target, thus belaying the suspicions of others.


    Contracts of Dominion (Unseelie)
    The “Unseelie Loyalists” are loyal only to their own power over others - the power of the Fae that the covet. They make use of these Contracts to fill mortals with falsehoods which drive them to becoming beholden to the changeling and the changeling alone.

    Whispers of Crippling Doubt (•)
    In order to work their way into the hearts of their target, the changeling spins a Glamour which fills their mind with lies and doubts - of their self and others - making them more pliable to the changeling’s suggestions.
    Cost: 1 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Wyrd - Target’s Composure
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The target has broken their given word to someone within the last week.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling overplays their hand; the target instead gains a +2 bonus to resisting their manipulations, and becomes suspicious of their intentions.
    Failure: The Glamour fails to take hold, and has no effect on the target.
    Success: The target suffers a -2 penalty to the manipulations of the changeling for the remainder of the scene. This penalty to applies to their Resistance against Social actions, and may be applicable against some Mental actions as well at Storyteller discretion.
    Exceptional Success: The target suffers the same penalty to actions they take in opposition to the changeling’s goals (if they have some indication that the changeling would be displeased with the action).


    A Poison For Trust (••)
    To better secure their influence over a mortal, the changeling Glamours them with suspicions against their loved ones, isolating their target from the people that can keep them safe.
    Cost: 1 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Subterfuge + Wyrd versus Composure + Empathy
    Action: Instant and Contested (resistance is reflexive)
    Catch: The changeling creates and besmirches a symbol of the relationship (a photo, written name, likeness in a doll, friendship pendant, etc), and casually discards it at the site.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling’s lies fall so far off the mark that the target retreats into a deeper relationship with the subject of this Clause. Until the end of the Chapter, the target can restore a lost Willpower point or reroll a failed degeneration roll once by having a meaningful interaction with that person. Of course, if that person betrays them, it will be a powerful blow to their psyche (changelings roll for degeneration at Clarity 2, and mortals acquire the Suspicions derangement or roll for Integrity loss).
    Failure: The Glamour fails to take hold and has no effect on the target. The changeling cannot attempt to poison that relationship again for 24 hours, and future uses of this Clause against the target take a -1 penalty.
    Success: The changeling chooses one relationship of their target’s to poison them against with suspicions of manipulation and betrayal.The Glamour casts doubts on all of that person’s motives and actions toward the target, and highlights anything which could be interpreted as manipulative. That person takes a penalty equal to the Changeling’s Wyrd in all social interactions with them for the remainder of the Chapter (or several days).
    The changeling must be aware of the nature of the relationship and who it is with (either seeing them or knowing their name), and must have the target in sight when they activate this Clause.
    Exceptional Success: The target also suffers from the Suspicions derangement or condition. Any failed Social roll which the person fails toward the target triggers the effect, in addition to being set off by other accidental misfortunes.


    Slave of Desire (•••)
    Just as pimps sometimes force drug addiction on their workers to control them, the changeling is able to create a potent emotional addiction in the target with Glamour. What the addiction is to and what the “high” feels like can be decided, in some degree, by the changeling when they inflict this upon a mortal - and it can be entirely whimsical, though usually it is to an experience related in some way to the fae’s nature.
    Cost: 2 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Presence + Wyrd vs Resolve + Wyrd
    Action: Extended and contested; target is equal to Willpower. Each roll represents one minute.
    Catch: The changeling imbibes a highly addictive substance.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling himself becomes addicted to the experience he tried to afflict.
    Failure: The target resists the attempt to addict them; they become aware of what the changeling was trying to accomplish, and may bolster their will against any further attempts: the target adds their Composure to future rolls to resist this Clause.
    Success: In order to use this contract, the changeling must provide the experience they hope to addict the target to, while coaxing them with Glamour and force of personality into total intoxication. When the changeling reaches a number of successes equal to the target’s Willpower, the victim is cursed with addiction. The experience could be anything from listening to a particular song to holding burning charcoal. Generally, the changeling will try to choose something that only they can provide it, however the experience must be general enough that anybody else could provide it, if they know what it is.
    Exceptional Success: In addition to creating the addiction in the target’s mind, the target also associates the changeling with the high feelings from the experience, granting the changeling +1 to Social rolls against the target for as long as they remain addicted.


    Condition: Glamoured Addiction (Persistent)
    Your character is addicted to experience chosen and created by a fae. While not as potent as a chemical addiction, the experience does provide an enchanting sense of bliss and relief. You need to indulge your addiction regularly to keep it under control; being unable to feed your addiction for a period equal to [11 minus the Wyrd of the fae who caused it] days can result in the Deprived Condition.
    Resolution: Regain a dot of Integrity, lose another dot of Integrity, or achieve an exceptional success on a breaking point. Finding a way to relieve the symptoms of deprivation while not giving into the addiction for an extended period of time will end its hold on you.
    Beat: Your character chooses to get a fix rather than fulfill an obligation.

    Condition: Deprived
    Your character suffers from an addiction. She is unable to get her fix, however, leaving her irritable, anxious, and unable to focus. Remove one from her relevant dice pools: at minimum this affects Resolve, and Composure based actions, but other Mental or Social actions may also be penalized at Storyteller discretion. This does not influence derived traits.
    Resolution: Your character indulges her addiction.
    Beat: n/a

    Derangement: Glamoured Addiction
    Your character is hooked on a experience chosen and created by a fae. If he doesn’t get it regularly, he gets anxious and goes out of his way to satisfy his need, even neglecting more important duties. If the opportunity to indulge arises, the character must roll Resolve + Composure to resist. After a period of [11 - the Wyrd of the fae which caused this] days, the character suffers withdrawal symptoms of anxiety, irritability, and lack of focus. This applies a -1 penalty to most Mental or Social actions, especially Resolve or Composure based dice pools.
    As with mundane addictions, this can be beaten by prolonged withdrawal from the experience, and usually requires finding someway to relieve the symptoms of deprivation.

    Gift of Entrapment (••••) [Troublesome Clause; New Draft - see post #7]
    The changeling spins together something their target desires out of scraps and Glamour, earning their trust with a gift, while driving them further into reliance on the changeling’s help.
    Cost: 2 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Crafts + Wyrd (versus Resolve + Empathy; optional)
    Action: Instant; contested at the time of the next misfortune (optional)
    Catch: The target expresses a wish or desire out loud before they notice the changeling.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling apparently produces something which the target needs to accomplish something they desire. The Glamour holds for just long enough for them to attempt to use it, at which point it is revealed as scraps - which itself may get them into further trouble. The target knows the changeling peddled fool’s gold.
    Failure: The changeling can create nothing useful. Repeated attempts suffer a -1 penalty (cumulative). The target may also stop believing that they can assist, and ignore them.
    Success: The changeling produces something which the target desires, preferably to get them out of trouble of some kind. The changeling must have at least general knowledge of what would help to attempt this. If their mark accepts the gift, it becomes as good as the real thing, and does indeed accomplish whatever they were hoping for. However, it guarantees that another misfortune soon befalls them, and that they will think of the changeling in trying to solve it; this does not necessarily force them to return to the changeling, but will give them the impression that they can help in this circumstance too…
    A Resolve + Empathy roll may be required to resist returning, at Storyteller discretion, which must meet or beat the successes earned on the activation of this Clause.
    If the target does return, the changeling suffers a cumulative -2 penalty if they attempt to use this Clause again to solve the next problem. It works to best effect if they have other means of helping… once the debt has been accumulated, of course.
    Exceptional Success: For the remainder of the Chapter (or several days), the changeling gains a +2 bonus [or 1 Door is automatically opened] in attempts to convince the target to do something.


    Courting Forgiveness (•••••)
    If ever the changeling is revealed to have harmed, manipulated, or otherwise betrayed someone they seek to dominate, swift use of this Clause bribes them back into the target’s trust and good graces, glossing over any malign qualities of the changeling.
    Cost: 3 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Subterfuge + Wyrd versus Resolve + Composure
    Action: Instant and Contested (resistance is reflexive)
    Catch: The changeling’s offer satisfies the target’s Virtue or one of their Aspirations.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The target is irrevocably scorned. They become hostile toward the changeling, and are unlikely to ever be convinced to change their opinion. They will seek to undermine the changeling however they can.
    Failure: The Glamour has no effect, and the changeling’s deceptive offer likely angers the target.
    Success: The changeling provides an explanation, a gift, or a promise to the target, laced thickly with a Glamour that causes them to forgive and gloss over any past behavior or quality of the changeling’s which they objected to. The changeling must offer one of these to the target, and must be aware of their current hostility and what provoked it, in order to use this Contract. The target must also have once held an Excellent impression of the changeling. If successful, all past grievances are forgiven in the target’s mind, not merely the one which set them off; the target’s impression of them immediately returns to Excellent.
    This impression will not fade, however the target is under no compulsion to keep such a good impression of the changeling; their permanent “natural” impression is merely returned to Excellent. They can still be swayed against the changeling, with difficulty, and the changeling may ruin this impression through their own actions. This Clause may only be successfully used against a target twice; their third betrayal is the last straw...
    Exceptional Success: The target has a Perfect impression of the changeling for the remainder of the Scene. Any future attempts to convince them of the changeling’s ill intentions suffers a -3 penalty, at least until the changeling directly harms the target.


    Contracts of Fleeting Dreams (Seelie)
    Those in the Seelie Court of Loyalists, see their masters as being archetypes of ideals which would improve the mortal world, if only they could bring their Stories here. This Contract was forged to twist mortal hope, belief, and dreams, the better to spin fairy tales out of their lives.
    Each Loyalist holds a particular story and ideal above every other as their source of inspiration and hope. This conviction makes it more difficult for them to use these Contracts to spin different stories. As such, any use of these Contracts which follow the interest of their House take a -1 penalty; the Seelie Court in general, a -2 penalty; and all other uses, a -3 penalty - only using these Contracts to be in line with their chosen story are unpenalized. Uses which weave a story in contradiction to the one they exalt may be grounds for a breaking point against Clarity 4, at Storyteller discretion.

    The Heart Reveals (•)
    The changeling learns the details of one of the target’s wishes for their life. The changeling selects how deep a dream they learn before activating this Clause (see modifiers below).
    Cost: 1 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Wits + Wyrd vs. Composure + Wyrd
    Action: Instant; Contested (resistance is reflexive)
    Catch: The target has confided in the changeling within the last week.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling receives false information, either learning a hope that the target does not have, or getting the details of it completely wrong.
    Failure: The changeling learns nothing about the target.
    Success: The changeling learns one of the target’s Aspirations or another kind of goal; usually, anyone else involved and what is currently preventing the goal are also revealed. See the modifiers below for the types of goals which can be sought out.
    Exceptional Success: The changeling learns an additional goal at that level or one above it.
    [Modifiers: -- immediate concern; -1 short term goal; -2 dream for the future; -3 wishful thinking]


    Mask of Glamour(••)
    Using this contract, the changeling wraps dreamstuff around a target in a way very reminiscent of a Mask. The effect isn’t strong enough to alter appearance; instead it has the very subtle impact of provoking a particular emotion in people they interact with. The changeling chooses what emotion manifests when they weave this Contract; everyone who interacts with the target feels the same emotion, and that emotion can’t be changed after the Contract is used. Although, due to its short-lived effect, a persistent changeling can follow the target to compel different emotions in different people, wreaking havoc with their social life. The emotional response people have is not overwhelming - they aren’t compelled to act on it - but it does change their perceptions and attitudes toward a person, and may inspire action, depending on the inclinations of those affected.
    The changeling must have line-of-sight to use this Contract on target; any other magical effects which affect people's’ emotion toward the target provokes a Clash of Wills.
    Cost: 2 Glamour; + 1 Willpower (optional)
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Wyrd vs Wits + Wyrd
    Action: instant, contested (resistance is reflexive)
    Catch: Used upon the target’s first meeting with a significant person (first meetings after a long absence do also apply).
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: Through the Changeling’s mismanagement of glamour, or perhaps from the target investing their own hopes and fears into the Mask, the magic backfires, and takes the opposite effect.
    Failure: No effect. Further attempts to use this contract on the target this scene take a -1 penalty.
    Success: The glamour wraps around the target, and invests a very subtle Mask (or a subtle change to the Mask, in the case of changeling targets) which causes anyone they interact with to feel the designated emotion towards them. This manifests as a +/- 2 modifier on the target’s social interactions. Those affected by interacting with the target have their emotions and perceptions of them distorted by the emotion, but are not compelled to take any action. In cases where it is particularly tempting to act on the emotion, those affected can roll Composure + a relevant Skill to resist the urge.
    This clause lasts for one meaningful social interaction that the target has, which could range between an instant action or a few minutes. This might mean a brief chat with a stranger, or several minutes of conversation; in general, only social interactions the target has reason to care about apply. Prolonged interactions, such a job interview or a speech, may count as one scene not one social interaction, but the opening remarks could be tainted by the emotion - which may leave an impression that lasts. A Willpower point can be spent to increase this duration to the length of a Scene.
    Exceptional Success: The curse lasts for 1 scene, or 1 chapter if a point of Willpower is spent.
    [Modifiers: the changeling has affinity with the emotion (Virtue/Vice, Mantle) +1]


    Answers in Dreams (•••)
    By manipulating the dreams of the target, the changeling can create associations between feelings, situations, individuals, and belief. The associations made in dreams affect the target’s interpretation of the world, and can be used to change their feelings, reactions, or beliefs. This forced association will unravel with each night of restful dreams; however, if the changeling repeatedly changes the target’s dreams, the association can become permanently ingrained into their subconscious.
    Loyalists also make regular use of this Clause to shape mortal dreams in a way that allows the True Fae to enter, in mimicry of their Dream Poisoning techniques. This usually involves investing a meaningful association with the Fae’s Title, but can also be done by investing the wonder of Arcadia.
    Changelings, and others who have complete control of their dreams (Lucid Dreamer merit, or other supernatural powers) are not automatically immune to these attempts to change their dreams, but they are far more capable of fighting them off. The intruder has to succeed a Wits + Subterfuge roll versus the victim’s Wits + Empathy + Wyrd to even attempt the change, and the victim may decide to alter the dream anyway. It takes a subtle and persistent fae to tamper with the dreams of a changeling.
    Cost: 2 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Intelligence + Empathy + Mantle vs Resolve + Composure
    Action: extended, contested (resistance is reflexive): goal of target’s Willpower
    Catch: Successful use of “Forging the Dream” (Dream ••) removes the need for this cost.
    ROLL RESULTS
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling creates an emotional association, but far from the one they were intending to make. The target gains the “Delusional” condition or derangement, believing that the changeling is trying to harm them, or another belief which hampers the changeling’s goals. The changeling cannot attempt to change this delusion through further use of this Contract, and the delusion will not fade over time as usual - they have to prove to the target that they are not out to get to them through mundane means.
    Failure: The changeling fails to create an emotional association through the target’s dreams. They may not attempt to influence the same subject in their target again until the next time they sleep - although other subjects can be influenced.
    Success: The target gains the “Delusional” condition or derangement (see below). The delusion associates one subject with one emotion, each of the changeling’s choosing. The delusion will quickly fade away as the target has natural sleep: after one successful use, the delusion will fade after the next full night of unaltered dreams. The changeling may choose to reinforce the same delusion over several nights with repeated use of this Clause. Each additional successful use (on the same target, regarding the same subject) increases the duration of the delusion by one night of natural sleep. If the changeling reinforces the same delusion for a number of sequential nights exceeding the target’s permanent Willpower, the delusion becomes so ingrained as to become a permanent part of the target’s personality.                           I've reworked my three troublesome Clauses, and think that I have good final versions for how I want these Contracts to look. Critique of these new versions would be greatly appreciated.

                          Following the Fading Footsteps (Hunt •)
                          Use of this Clause allows a changeling mark a target with glamour, allowing them to track the target later by the lingering ripples of emotion.
                          Cost: 1 Glamour to mark + 1 Glamour to follow
                          Dice Pool: Survival + Wyrd versus Composure + Stealth
                          Action: Instant and Contested (resistance is reflexive)
                          Duration: 3 days
                          Catch: The changeling marks an object instead, and gifts it to the target. Doing so means they need not spend Glamour to start tracking, however they follow the object’s trail, not the person’s.
                          ROLL RESULTS
                          Dramatic Failure: The changeling marks another nearby person instead of their chosen target. If they gave an object to their intended target, it immediately changes hands through the whim of Fate, unbeknownst to the changeling.
                          Failure: The changeling is unable to mark the target to be followed, but won’t be aware of this until they attempt to do so.
                          Success: The changeling choose a target whom they can see and marks them with Glamour. While the Glamour mark persists (3 days), it leaves a trail of emotion which the changeling is able to track, radiating off of the target. The changeling must spend another point of Glamour each time they begin tracking, to attune himself to the particular trail; once they do so, they are allowed to make tracking rolls to follow the target, using their ability to sense emotions rather than by mundane forms of tracking. The changeling can only hone their senses in this way for an hour at a time - requiring additional glamour if they wish to continue.
                          The Glamour trail is only left behind in the mortal realm; in the Hedge and Arcadia, the ambient Glamour overwhelms any trail left behind; other planes operate on different laws, in which Glamour leaves no lasting mark.
                          Exceptional Success: Finely attuned to the echoes of Glamour from their target, the changeling will recognize the places that the target spend considerable time or felt strong emotions along their route.


                          Spotting the Vulnerable (Hunt ••)
                          The Loyalist on the hunt uses this Clause to search out a particular weakness in the crowd, helping them better select the ideal victim for their needs.
                          Cost: 1 Glamour + 2 Glamour (optional)
                          Dice Pool: Wits + Wyrd versus (highest) Resolve + Subterfuge
                          Action: Extended and Contested (resistance is reflexive); the amount of time is chosen by the changeling when the Clause is used (see suggested modifiers below)
                          Catch: The changeling poses a riddle or a leading question to the group; it must be at least related to the trait they’re looking for, but need not be delivered directly.
                          ROLL RESULTS
                          Dramatic Failure: The changeling focuses in on a detail which they misinterpret for the weakness in question; the target they find not only isn’t vulnerable to the weakness being sought, but likely has a significant advantage that the changeling isn’t aware of.
                          Failure: The changeling finds no one which has the weakness they’re seeking, and isn’t certain if they overlooked something or if no such target it present.
                          Success: The changeling chooses a specific type of weakness which they try to seek out in a crowd. They may be looking for a physical disadvantage, a compulsive behavior, someone who is trusting, or someone who could be bribed or lured away - or similar categories or situations where the weakness would apply. Upon a successful roll in the contest, any individuals to whom the type of weakness may apply stand out to the changeling. Greater successes in the contest reveal increasingly hidden disadvantages: one success may reveal those who are currently suffering from or acting on a weakness; three successes may reveal those for whom the weakness could apply an immediate disadvantage, but which currently isn’t obvious; five or more may be needed to notice those with weaknesses that don’t have visible signs, or which are subtle or abstract. If at any point, the crowd’s resistance roll reduces the changeling’s net successes, they lose track of individuals which had previously been revealed, with no clear way of finding them again.
                          Once the changeling has noticed individuals to whom the type of weakness may apply, they may choose to select one target and pay the optional cost to learn the details about their particular weakness. By doing so, the changeling ends the Clause, and loses track of the other individuals that had been revealed.
                          The Storyteller may decide which types of weakness are too broad or too narrow for the purposes of this Contract, and may choose to levy additional penalties based on the type and it’s difficult to notice.
                          Exceptional Success: If the changeling earns 5 or more net successes in a single roll, they may choose to end the Clause and reveal only the most exploitable individual with that type of weakness.
                          Suggested Modifiers: time per roll: one action -3, one minute -2, five minutes -1, ten minutes 0, half hour +1, one hour +2. Size of crowd: five or fewer +2, about 20 -0, about 100 -2. Category: physical -0, social -1, mental -2, supernatural -3.


                          Gift of Entrapment (Dominion ••••)
                          The changeling spins together an object their target desires out of scraps and Glamour, earning their trust with a gift, while driving them further into reliance on the changeling’s help.
                          Cost: 2 Glamour + 1 Willpower
                          Dice Pool: Crafts + Wyrd (versus Resolve + Empathy; optional)
                          Action: Instant; contested at the time of the next misfortune (optional)
                          Catch: The target expresses a wish or desire out loud before they notice the changeling.
                          ROLL RESULTS
                          Dramatic Failure: The changeling produces an object which the target apparently could use to accomplish what they desire. The Glamour holds for just long enough for them to attempt to use it, at which point it is revealed as scraps - which itself may get them into further trouble. The target knows the changeling peddled fool’s gold to them.
                          Failure: The changeling can create nothing useful. Repeated attempts suffer a cumulative -1 penalty. The target may also stop believing that they can assist, and ignore them.
                          Success: The changeling produces an object, woven out of scraps of junk and Glamour, which the target can use to accomplish their goal - preferably to get them out of some kind of trouble. The changeling must at least have general knowledge of what type of object would be helpful, however neither the changeling nor the target has control of what the object will be; it will be a simple, commonly available object which can help the target. Creating something unique or supernatural is beyond the scope of this Clause.
                          If the target accepts this gift, it becomes as good as the real thing, and does indeed help them accomplish whatever goal they were aiming for. The target immediately has an Excellent impression of the changeling, and will for the next week (or the remainder of the Chapter) and the target gains the Leveraged condition toward the changeling [1e: the changeling gains 9-again in Social interactions with the target for the next week]. The target may choose to roll Resolve + Empathy to resist asking for the changeling for further help, and must beat the successes earned on the activation of this Contract in order to resist.
                          This Clause may again be used to aide in the misfortune caused by this curse, however the curse is cumulative; it becomes increasingly more harmful or inconveniencing to the target, and causes cumulative -1 penalty to the use of this Clause; the changeling begins to become the target of misfortune as well. Repeated use of this Contract may delay the curse for a time, but it will eventually catch up with them, and may be deadly to both the target and the changeling by the time it does.
                          Exceptional Success: The target instead gains a Perfect impression of the changeling [1e, the changeling gains 8-again on Social rolls toward the target]. This benefit lasts for 1 day per -1 penalty on the roll.
                          Suggested Modifiers: -1 per Merit dot that would be required to obtain the item by mundane means (most often, this will be Resources).
