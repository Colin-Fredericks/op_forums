    Help me build Goblin Market Membership as a Mystery Cult

    03-22-2015, 10:01 PM
    Hey, I'm playing in a Changeling 1.5 game (GMC rules where applicable) as a "Back alley (of a Goblin Market) Oneirophysic." An idea I had was to model Goblin Market Membership as a Mystery Cult. It feels pretty appropriate, putting the buying and selling of intangibles as the 3rd dot power. Looking for feedback and help filling out the later dots.

    1 - Market Regular. The first thing someone wanting to survive the Goblin Market must learn is how to barter and how to close a deal. They gain a Persuasion specialty related to Cutting a Deal.
    Drawback: Freeholds and Courts are loath to deal heavily with people who might sell them out for a quick buck. Your maximum ranks in Status [Freehold] and Court Goodwill are reduced by 1 for every two dots in this merit, rounded up.
    2 - Proffered Customer. Merchandise flows freely both ways when you're involved. While being good for business doesn't get you ripped off any less, merchants often sweeten the pot with juicy bits of information about merchandise and customers--past, present, and potential. Sometimes this information isn't even suicidal to act on. You gain a dot in Contacts [Goblin Merchants].
    Drawback: Your reputation proceeds you. You gain the Notoriety condition with Changelings who know of your dealings. Notoriety does not apply to other Changelings with this merit or Changelings who have ever purchased from the Goblin Market from you or with your aid.
    3 - Merchant. You accept their offer and pay the exuberant (OOO) price for membership, and must pay again every time you must renew your oath. You are now bound by and protected by Market Law (see below). You are recognized as a merchant, and are more likely to get a fair deal so long as you do the same for your fellows.
    4 - Market Executive. You are the merchant other merchants go to when they need something.
    5 - Goblin Mogul.

    Oath of the Market
    Type: Corporeal Oath. You swear on a formal contract signed between you and one of the Market's official pledgesmiths, in the presence of all other Merchants. If you break this Oath, all members of the Goblin Marker immediately know.
    Task: Forbiddence (-2; Follow Market Law. See Goblin Markets page xx. Note that each market may have slightly different laws.)
    Boon: Special (+2; you may buy and sell in intangibles. See Goblin Markets page xx)
    Curse: Vulnerability (-3; Physical)
    Duration: Year and a Day (+3)

      Based on the material in Goblin Markets I’d arrange the Mystery Cult like this:

      Goblin Market Initiation (• to •••••)
      Goblin Markets are oft distrusted by changelings due to their high prices and willingness to exploit any tragedy to turn a profit. Still, the markets are often the best last resort for the desperate and can provide the careful customer with mystic boons they cannot find anywhere else. Those that join the market can make it their livelihood so long as their ability to cut a deal in their favor it top notch.
      Drawback: Any changeling that spend enough time around a goblin market to gain any dots in this Merit is likely to be viewed with suspicion by the local freehold.

      • Market Regular - Regular customers of the market quickly learn the how and when of their local market. Gain the Market Familiarity Merit at one dot. (Goblin Market pg 14)

      •• Goblin Laborer - Your character might be an apprentice to a merchant or may provide labor to the market such as working as a guard or enforcer. This has earned them the right to sell to market goers without being in violation of Market Law. Gain the Goblin Merchant Merit at one dot. (Goblin Market pg 34)

      ••• Goblin Merchant - As a full merchant you’ve paid your dues and sworn to uphold Market Law in exchange for the full protection of said law. Gain the Goblin Merchant Merit at three dots. (Goblin Market pg 34)

      •••• Market Stall - Your character has put enough time into the market and paid all necessary fees to use of one of the markets valuable stalls for storing their goods and trading intangibles. Gain the Market Stall Merit. (Goblin Market pg 34)

      ••••• Goblin Mogul - A goblin mogul runs the local market. The payments needed to gain Market Initiation ••• and •••• are made to the reigning mogul as are the oaths to uphold Market Law.
      Drawback: The Goblin Mogul is the one responsible for providing stalls to merchants and enforcing Market Law. Failure in these duties (which can sometimes be expensive if not dangerous) can lead to the Mogul getting “removed” from their position by upset merchants or even the collapse of the entire market as unpaid debt and chaos disrupt any normal business.

              Thank you. The idea of the fifth dot is that you get "free" stuff from anyone that wants the third or fourth dot every time the renew their pledge. Basically a steady, albeit occasional, income of stuff to add to your wealth with the obvious drawback of running a market. Alternatively you could give two dots of Wholesale Wares (Goblin Markets pg 35) if you want to stay closer to established material.

              Or, just spitballing here, but if you wanted a more "spider in the web" feel for the person that runs the market you could give the Market Mogul knowledge of every sale in the market. They know who bought want and with what price at every market, a powerful ability if you want to track down information for blackmail, or track the power plays in the local freehold. It could be cool to be the master of the market who is shunned by the freehold but has enough knowledge of their going ons from what is bought and sold to insert themselves into the changeling politics anyway.
