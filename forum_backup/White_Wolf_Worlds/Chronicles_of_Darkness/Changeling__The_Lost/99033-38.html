                                                        Preparations for the battle with the Loyalists. Conversations to reveal family status. And Julia’s…interesting painting. No Calamity or Gawain journal, but both were present.

                                                        Session LXXII

                                                        Lorelei’s Diary

                                                        And so it begins. I’m sitting here writing in this journal as the others endlessly pore over our plans, hoping to cover every eventuality before we drive to LA to attempt to spring our trap on Rook and Hunting Hawk. It seems more constructive than sitting here and quietly pondering how useless and afraid I suddenly feel.

                                                        The last couple of days haven’t been so bad, all in all. Movie night was fun; Elena and Natasha joined us for a double-feature of stop-motion animated films chosen by Julia. Both were directed by a man named Henry Selick (and not – and I’m told this point is vitally important – by the famed Tim Burton), and both were enjoyable on many levels… though I think the first made many of us uncomfortable.

                                                        The film in question was called Coraline, and it was based on a children’s book written by Neil Gaiman (the same author who wrote those Sandman comics Julia lent me last summer). On the surface it was just a creepy kid’s adventure story about a little girl who runs afoul of supernatural predators and displays uncommon bravery and pluck in overcoming them. Beneath the surface, it made me wonder if Mr. Gaiman has had personal experience with Arcadia. The film’s villain, a doppelganger of Coraline’s mother with buttons sewn into her face where her eyes should be, was definitely Keeper material. Now don’t get me wrong; it was a fun film… it’s just that a lot of us were sort of cutting our eyes back and forth at each other when certain things happened.

                                                        The second film was called The Nightmare Before Christmas, and it was a lot of fun (and had slightly less potential to send changelings into bouts of PTSD). The idea behind it is that the king of Halloween (a dapper grotesque by the name of Jack Skellington) is out exploring one day when he discovers Christmas, and decides that he would like to bring people joy instead of scaring them for a while. He takes on the mantle of Santa Claus and royally screws things up, while his political enemies seize their opportunity at home. It was cute, charming, and in places hilarious. I think we all enjoyed it immensely.

                                                        Jacob had the most drama on Monday night, however… he told Elena that he’s her brother! She took it… well, I suppose. She had to leave the company for a while to process it, but that’s only to be expected (Dad did the same thing when I revealed my identity to him). She returned before the end of the night, and seemed to have made peace with the revelation, though she was still a bit shy and unsure of herself for the rest of the evening.

                                                        After the films I retired with D, Marguerite, and Julia. I wouldn’t have minded a little hanky-panky that night, actually, but didn’t feel like initiating anything. What can I say? The others were being so sweet and supportive and loving that I just got caught up in the emotional sharing, and it felt like it would have been wrong to interrupt that just for a few hours of carnal gratification.

                                                        Tuesday was pretty uneventful for me until the show. One thing I should mention is that Elena spoke up at Sex Breakfast and asked how she could help us in our efforts against Rook and Hunting Hawk. She got a sort of determined look on her face and said that she would “do what she could to help out her brother.” She gave Jacob’s shoulder a squeeze as she said it, and I found that very encouraging.

                                                        The idea of putting her at risk while we go after Rook was repulsive to me, however. After a quick chat with the motley, I gave Davey a call and asked if he could drop by the Dream House for the show tonight, since the motley wanted to ask him for a favor. He agreed to listen to our pitch (in exchange for us waiving the cover charge), and we all settled into our normal routines.

                                                        Elena also asked a fairly sobering question at one point… she wanted to know how she could tell if she was a fetch. That’s… a pretty good question, actually. As far as I know there’s no foolproof way to know who is a fetch and who isn’t until they reveal themselves and begin to degrade like Mary’s fetch did. I’m not sure if she has reason to believe that she might be a fetch, or if it was just idle curiosity, but the question certainly gave us all a lot to think about.

                                                        As the afternoon wore on, we got a visit from an unexpected friend: Austin dropped by. He wanted to talk up his chestnut party to the local changelings at the Dream House tonight, and courteously decided to clear it with us first. Though opinions of the party itself are mixed among the motley, we all agreed that there was no harm in letting our friend do a little impromptu advertising among the Lost community this evening.

                                                        I still haven’t decided if I’m going to attend or not. I know, it seems like a no-brainer, right? A big, organized sex party with a competitive element to it… you’d think I’d be the first in line. But lately I’ve been rethinking that side of my life quite a bit. I don’t know if I’m finally getting enough erotic attention from my bevy of girlfriends to satisfy me, or if I’m starting to question my own nymphomania at last, or if I’m just distracted by Hunting Hawk’s presence in the area, or what. Maybe it’s just the anonymity and lack of meaningful emotional bonds between partners that has me less than enthusiastic… I usually like to have some genuine connection to my lovers, however tenuous or fleeting it might be.

                                                        Anyway, we agreed to let Austin talk up his little soiree, and then went our separate ways to prepare for the show. Before I got into my usual routine, however, Julia wanted to show me a painting she had been working on. It was undeniably beautiful, but a little (to use Marguerite’s word) “creepy.” The painting depicted Julia and Marguerite – inexplicably the same height – dressed in Edwardian wedding finery, obviously engaged in the final stages of some sort of mutual suicide pact while guests (presumably from their respective wedding parties) raced to stop them from following through. The romantic side of the painting was obvious… Julia and Marguerite would rather die than live in a world which would not allow them to be together, which is oddly sweet. But the morbid aspect of the scene was a little overwhelming, and though I could appreciate the quality of the work, it was too dark for my taste. I tried to give an honest assessment of the painting without hurting my lover’s feelings.

                                                        Calamity and I didn’t perform our “Bloody Creature Poster Girl” number last night; we’re giving it a rest at least until the weekend. I did a couple of my standbys; nothing I had to think too hard about. I’ve been so distracted by recent events that I haven’t really had time to work on anything new.

                                                        Rowan didn’t seem to mind, though. I caught her eye as soon as I came out on stage the first time, and tipped her a wink and a smile, which she returned with interest. After I was done on stage, I stopped by her table first. I told her that we had some motley business to take care of (implying that it had to do with the Loyalists in town but not stating it directly), and apologizing for the fact that she’d have to fend for herself while we dealt with it, but promising that I’d see her soon and be all hers for the rest of the evening.

                                                        Austin was enthusiastic in his advertising, and he managed to get a fair number of people to sign up for his chestnut party. After speaking briefly with D and myself, he said he would mark us both down as “maybes.” I’d say that’s fair. I’m pretty sure Calamity is going, though I don’t know for certain about any of the others. Andromeda is pretty excited about it, and I understand that the orders for her special products are picking up. Joan was less enthusiastic about the party, and declined to discuss the event directly.

                                                        So after the bulk of the crowds cleared out, the motley met with Davey. He had apparently assumed that we wanted him for some sort of sex thing… I’m not sure if that’s a sad reflection on our motley’s reputation in the Freehold or if that’s just the sort of thing that people usually call on Davey for. In any case, once he understood the situation he was more than happy to offer up his aid. He’s going to impersonate Elena at the art show so that Jacob’s sister won’t actually be in any real danger. I have to hand it to him… he didn’t bat an eye at the danger to himself, he was just eager to help us resolve the situation however he could.

                                                        I should probably note that Shi also joined us for this planning session. No one invited him, and I’m not sure why he felt the need to be there, or what sinister fun he might be planning. However, none of us were confident of our ability to make him leave, and we all knew that if we tried to force him out it would just strengthen his determination to be a part of the meeting, so in the end we decided to bear it. I hope nothing too bad comes of his involvement.

                                                        D, Marguerite, and Julia went off to have some fun with White Maria after the meeting. I’m pretending that I didn’t notice the eager way D was looking at White Maria during the conversation leading up to our parting… I prefer to think that my love has gotten over her dislike of the other girl rather than admitting that she’s probably looking forward to abusing that poor submissive thing. Sigh. I guess as long as it’s consensual…

                                                        And hey, maybe D was just excited by the fact that White Maria seemed to be wearing nothing under her robe aside from (working) Christmas lights. That little wrinkle certainly seemed to please Marguerite…

                                                        I rejoined Rowan in the theater area after the meeting, took her by the hand, and led her up to my bedroom. I know that I haven’t been much in the mood for lovin’ lately… but in this instance, Rowan’s feelings were both more delicate and more important than my own. She needed to know that things haven’t changed between us… that I don’t blame her for what her House did, and that I don’t judge her for how she acted when she was under its influence. So I took her to bed, and the two of us made love slowly, lovingly, and passionately until we fell asleep in each other’s arms.

                                                        I woke up the next morning to see her sitting up on her side of the bed, watching me… and smiling. I mean, really smiling for the first time since the incident with her House. It was good to see, and it gave me hope that she might really be recovering from the trauma she suffered.

                                                        “Morning,” I said, smiling in turn and stifling a yawn.

                                                        “Morning, Lori,” she returned softly, leaning in for a gentle kiss.

                                                        “You staying for breakfast?” I asked once we disengaged.

                                                        She nodded. “I can stay for a little while… but I don’t want to get in the way of whatever it is your motley is planning. Besides, I need to get back to my new place… I’m still trying to get it how I like it,” she said a little shyly.

                                                        “I can’t wait to see it,” I replied. “Let me know when it’s ready, and I’ll stay over at your place next time, okay?”

                                                        She nodded. “Just… give me a couple more days to get everything perfect,” she said with that cute little nervous flutter of hers.

                                                        Sex Breakfast was business as usual. Once the guests had cleared out, we settled down and began making our plans for that evening. Though things started out simply enough, our tactics shifted and mutated enough that after a while it seemed like we were planning for nearly any eventuality. Davey will be on-site, impersonating Elena. Calamity will use a contract to impersonate Natasha. D will be there incognito, with her distinctive hair pulled up under a hat and her mask strengthened to make her skin look darker. Julia will be there openly, which makes sense since that gallery has shown her own work on more than one previous occasion. Marguerite, Molly Nuit, and Jacob will be waiting in the wings, invisible (yes, apparently Jacob can do that now). Gawain and I will be down the street at a local café, hopefully far enough away that we won’t attract attention but near enough to lend our assistance if it becomes necessary.

                                                        I know it’s irrational, but I’m scared. I’m finally starting to piece together the shattered remnants of the life the Empress took from me… and now one of her agents has arrived in the area to take me back. I know my friends won’t let that happen, and I’d place every cent I have on them in any confrontation against Hunting Hawk. But that doesn’t stop me worrying. She took so much from me before. Please, God, I’m begging you… don’t let her take anything else.

                                                        Lorelei Grace


                                                        Jacob’s Journal

                                                        Okay…so I nipped things in the bud. I asked Elena if I could talk to her in private and she agreed. I then explained about fetches and how they are usually left behind when someone is taken. I then told her that it was no coincidence that my name was Jacob. It took a moment for that to process in Elena’s mind, but I could tell as soon as it did. She said that explained some things. I told her that time passes differently in Arcadia than it does here, and that sometimes people are gone for just moments and years have passed for them, or they are gone for decades and only a short time passed for them in the Fae realms. I said that where I’d been taken time passed quicker there and I aged more than the two years that passed in ‘the world.’ She seemed to take it all in stride but did say that she needed to do some thinking by herself. I said that would be fine and to find me later if she had any questions.

                                                        All of this made movie night a little more awkward than it could have been, but it was a good thing that I told Elena the truth. It would not be good for her to develop feelings for me that are not appropriate for a sibling. I did not want to hurt her feelings any more than I had to. So, we watched “The Nightmare Before Christmas,” and “Coraline.” “The Nightmare Before Christmas” was fun and entertaining. “Coraline” was kind of disturbing for those of us in the room that were Changelings. It bore more than a little resemblance to Arcadia and our Keepers. Coraline had a choice to accept the Other Mother’s offer of a happy life in the Other World, but she refused. She did not want to have buttons sewn into her eyes and lose her ability to go home. The Other Mother started out as a sweet and attentive mother, but when Coraline refused her offer to stay she became much more terrifying. It reminded me of The Lady. The Lady started out as a friendly and sweet Queen that only wanted to help the poor young man she found freezing in a cave. During my service to her she was a fair monarch, if a bit cold. She always treated me and Rook well and we were happy to serve her. That was until I refused to do her work. I refused to bring Delight back to The Lady’s realm and then I met the other side of my Queen. Just like Coraline met the other side of the Other Mother.

                                                        After the movies were over most of the group decided to go to sleep. Some of us however decided that it would be a good idea to keep a watch. So I took a watch with Luna. Calamity took a watch with Natasha and Gawain took a watch by himself. Nothing happened, which was good. Calamity and I left for our therapy sessions early in the morning. On the way there Calamity admitted that she went a little too far in subduing the Russian agents. She knows that she was wrong and that she wants to be better. That she should be better. She also said that she was planning on talking to her therapist about what happened with the Russian agents at today’s session. I did not blame her for what happened. I did not criticize her actions to her face because I knew that it would not do any good. She obviously feels bad about what happened and realizes that she needs help to be better and to not do anything similar in the future.

                                                        When we got back from therapy Gawain was starting to make breakfast for the lot of us. I made the usual large pot of coffee and doled it out the way people liked it. I know that Elena prefers tea, so I made her a cup of Darjeeling. So, Gawain made quiche for everyone and it was delicious, as usual. I don’t think I’ve ever had anything made by Gawain that wasn’t great. I believe that Natasha and Elena agreed that Gawain’s cooking was good.

                                                        While we were enjoying Gawain’s excellent food we discussed our plans for Wednesday. Gawain suggested that we call in Davey and see if he’d be willing to impersonate Elena and go to the Art show in L.A. Elena had originally said that she would act as bait at the Museum but to my satisfaction no one seemed keen on letting her do that. I don’t know what I would do if anything happened to my sister. Right now she is my only real connection to my family. I haven’t even considered approaching my parents yet. That would entail telling the Fetch what he is and sometimes that doesn’t go so well. I only have to think of the incident with Mary’s Fetch to see that. And despite not looking so much alike any more she is still my twin. We had always shared a close bond that I treasured. A lot of people fight with their siblings, and while we did do that on occasion, we also got along really well. I don’t want to lose that. I don’t want to lose her. She even asked if it might be possible that she was a Fetch. I told her that I did not believe that was the case. I do not remember seeing her in The Lady’s realm and I know that is something that I would have noticed. I do not want to consider the idea that the young lady that I have been talking to since I got back has not been my real sister.

                                                        So instead Davey was called and he agreed to stay after the show to talk to us about what was going on. We did not explain on the phone what we needed him to do. We figured we’d just tell him in person later tonight. After that was taken care of Gawain and Calamity went over to Desiree’s house to start working on the dream healing to help her get over her fear of making decisions. Hopefully this will work. It is not good for Insincere Marguerite to have to play the part of Desiree’s Keeper. It does nothing good for my motley mate nor does it do anything good for Desiree. She needs to realize that we all make good and bad decisions and that it is just a part of living. No one is perfect. Goddess knows that I’ve made some good and bad decisions in my time. I thought at the time that serving The Lady was a great idea. Turns out that it wasn’t really. It was actually one of my bigger blunders and I ended up hurting a lot of people, but I can’t take it back. I can’t undo the decision to serve her or what I did in her service.

                                                        On a lighter note Austin came by to talk to the group about his upcoming Chestnut party. I think some people were interested in going. I was not one of them, but others seemed interested nonetheless. We also told him that he could inform patrons at the club about his party as long as he wasn’t too disruptive. He agreed and came back for the show later in the day. He made his rounds like he said and wasn’t too pushy or anything about it. He did not however tell Shi or Molly about the partly. I guess he either knows that they’ll say no or he didn’t want them to come for some reason. I didn’t ask which was the case.

                                                        The show itself went smoothly. Lorelei was as graceful and amazing as always. The other singers and dancers did their usual wonderful jobs, but Lori always seems to shine the brighter. I think she likes that. Anyway, I talked to my usual group of people tonight. Molly was again at the bar and she asked if I was up to anything interesting lately. I hesitated for a moment before admitting that we were in the process of laying a trap for a Loyalist soon. She was interested in this and became more serious. She offered her help when and if we should need it. I told her that I would call her when we had finalized our plans. Molly may be a bit naïve when it comes to normal social interactions, but she is a pro when it comes to hunting down Loyalists. She knows how to tail them, silent and invisible. And she knows how to fight should the need arise. So I thought she would be a good addition to the team on Wednesday.

                                                        After the show Shi stayed along with Davey. It didn’t seem like there was much of a way to get rid of Shi so we just explained to Davey what we wanted him to do. He agreed to disguise himself as Elena on Wednesday in order to keep her safe. We then planned out a bit more of what we were going to do. Calamity would go disguised as Natasha. Someone would use the power called Cobblethought on her to make her resemble the Ukrainian girl. Insincere Marguerite and I would go into the museum, invisible, to make sure that nothing happened to Davey. There are so many doorways in the museum that it would be ridiculously easy for Rook to make a gateway to the Hedge and make off with “Elena.” We didn’t want anything happening to Davey, nor did we want Rook to get away. Julia also volunteered to go to the museum to keep an eye on things, but not be part of the group. She is a frequent enough patron, and sometime contributor of art that it will not look out of place for her to be there.

                                                        Meanwhile we needed someone to stay at the house just in case Rook got wise to our plans so Gawain asked Joan, Andromeda, Gloria and Desiree to stay behind on Wednesday to keep an eye on Natasha and Elena. They all readily agreed to protect the girls while we hunted Rook and possibly Hunting Hawk. I guess the biggest problem that we are going to have is that the museum is going to be full of people and security guards. We have to figure out how we’re going to get Rook out of there without alerting too much attention to ourselves. I suppose the most likely solution is to open a gate to the Hedge and pull him through with us. Not all of us will need to be involved in that, just those that are actively subduing him. Julia for example can leave of her own accord after we get Rook. This is all presuming that things go according to plan of course. There is only so much planning we can do. So much is up in the air about what Rook will do and how we will handle it. He is smart. He may figure out that something is going on and get out while he can. He is going to be more wary of our Freehold after his run in with Darke. I can’t blame him for that though. All we can do is plan based on what information we have and improvise where necessary.

                                                        After we discussed our plans for Wednesday Lori left to go speak to Rowan and Calamity started regaling Natasha with tales of her outrageous exploits, like opening a gate to the hedge in the door of a car that was plummeting off a cliff to the ocean below. She was definitely showing off for our guest. And while she was doing that I called Darke to let him know what we had come with as far as plans go. He said that he would have to call Dahlia and let her know as well so she could track Hunting Hawk. The plan was for her to pick him up as soon as we have Rook in custody. All of that sounded good to me. We’ll see how it goes in practice.

                                                        **** **** ****

                                                        It is Wednesday morning. You would not know by the normalcy of the day so far that later today we’re going to try and spring a trap on a Loyalist that may or may not still want to kill me. Sometimes I wonder if I should be trying to rehabilitate Rook. I do not know if he will be amenable to changing sides or not. I do not even know if he will be amenable to letting me continue breathing. Darke and Twitch did some pretty horrific things to him so he may just want to kill the lot of us if he could. I remember the good times that we had as partners, but I shouldn’t forget that when I betrayed the Lady he was quick to attack me and take me down. He almost killed me in that fight. Granted he did immediately take me to the Lady to be healed. I think he went further than he intended that particular day. I do not know how far he is willing or able to go now. If Darke’s programming works as it was intended it may not be possible for him to hurt me directly. That doesn’t mean that he isn’t clever enough to find a way to get around that if he wanted to though. I guess it won’t hurt to try and convince him to change sides. If he fights back too strenuously or hurts too many people then it may be necessary to kill him. We won’t know what happens until later today, so I suppose there is no point in worrying about it right now. Yeah, now if that advice would only work.

                                                        So, breakfast went like normal. We had a couple of extra guests today. Rowan and White Maria had joined some of the ladies last night for a good time and stuck around to have breakfast with the group. We had general conversation until they left. Once they were gone Julia asked us if we would like to see her latest painting. She was especially proud of it and wanted to show it off to the rest of us. We agreed and went up to take a look. Insincere Marguerite went first, then Lori and De la Rocha. Then the rest of us. I will give it one thing, it is very beautifully painted, if a bit disturbing in its content. The painting is of Julia and Insincere Marguerite both in wedding dresses standing close together and holding guns to each other’s heads. The look of absolute love was plain in their eyes and expressions. In the background there was a large mob running towards the couple, presumably aiming to separate the couple and keep them from pulling the trigger. Like I said very pretty, but disturbing nonetheless.

                                                        After we took a look at Julia’s painting we talked some more about out plans for the afternoon. De la Rocha came up with the idea of tucking her hair up under a cap and wearing that and some plain clothes to keep her disguised. At first I did not think it would work, but thinking about it some more made me thing that that it just might work. De la Rocha is noticeable because of her purple hair extensions and very visible clothing. If she were dressed in drab clothing and hid her hair she could blend in with the rest of the nondescript crowd. That way she could curse or bless anyone that needed it. Basically she could give the allies good luck and the bad guy’s bad luck at her choosing. It sounded good to me. Also, Molly could be there invisible with her gun if it was so needed. And Marguerite would be there to keep an eye on “Elena” at all times. I plan on going along invisibly as well, but that is to keep Davey safe and to capture Rook if I can. I would prefer to be there when we take him down. I do have a personal interest in this whole endeavor after all. Here’s hoping that all goes well.


                                                        Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

                                                        Circumstances remain overall favorable for me.

                                                        Juliette recovers well from her surprise at discovering Hunting Hawk was in the area. Last night she actually made love. Without me, sadly. But Rowan was there, and she is not as much for the…togetherness…we bring.

                                                        For me, yesterday White Maria texted me and requested a purgation for the evening. She does that sometimes…since our first evening she finds these little sessions helpful for her. Of course, they involve regressing to her Durance. I play the role of her Keeper. It’s…an uncomfortable role, these days.

                                                        Which is a good sign for I, I suppose.

                                                        Maria and Julia were there last night. Maria channels her distaste for White Maria into such things I think. Maria has had something of an…edge…to her past few nights. Maybe because we all took some nights off from carnality. Whatever it is, she has an edge.

                                                        Anyway, it occurs to me, when things settle, I might try to get White Maria some help from Calamity, like Desiree is getting. It’s a dicer situation because if she was not struggling with issues with her Durance, it would be harmless fun. I may need to talk to Calamity about it.

                                                        I have also been getting a flurry of texts from Felicity. I will need to see her for dinner soon.

                                                        Meanwhile Natasha and Elena are doing okay under our care. Jacob finally broke the news, and while Elena was upset, she seems to have recovered. Jacob seems okay too. Natasha, for her part, made a serious play for Julia’s, well, not affections so much as attentions, I suppose. Julia likes her women a bit more feminine than Natasha’s more intensely butch aesthetic, so it did not work in Natasha’s favor. Natasha was all about going after the Loyalists, but I think we have talked her out of such displays of bravado. Hopefully. I will privately advise Andromeda to keep a particular eye on her.

                                                        And Elana asked how she can know if she is a Fetch. Concerning, perhaps.

                                                        Calamity asked me about Stasia, who Natasha reminds her of. This time why I had not said anything. I simply said I had not completed my investigation before I got…sidetracked, by issues between us, which was true.

                                                        I’ve been keeping the hold decorated for Christmas. I hope we get the second tree from Oleander. I am still putting decorations up here and there. I wonder if anyone noticed I swapped our usual muzak for French Christmas carols?

                                                        At least White Maria was feeling Christmassy. She was wrapped in Christmas Lights and Tinsel under her Kimono. Ah, she does know what I like.

                                                        Movie night was awkward with them because of Jacob’s revelations. Somehow, in the sense of somehow meaning because of Julia, Coraline and Nightmare Before Christmas. Julia was so happy. Those movies, the latter particularly, always put her in a pleasant mood.

                                                        Speaking of Julia, so she has been thrashing about for how to paint something that would represent our love and depth of devotion to each other. She recently completed her first attempt to do that. It’s lovely. It’s also creepy. A representation of herself and Iself is in it, both in Fin de Siècle bridal gowns, with a large wedding party, two really, rushing toward us in the background. “Julia” and I are the same height, rather than she being several centimeters taller, and we stare into each other’s eyes with what I have to admit is brilliantly rendered both deep abiding love and deep abiding sadness.

                                                        We also have pistols pointed at each other. It is a suicide pact, and the painting captures the final moment of life for these two versions of ourselves. The onlookers rush to prevent it but will not be in time. The subjects will be together in death, if they cannot in life.

                                                        Aside from the subject matter the painting is incredibly good. Jarringly intense. I just find it a bit disturbing. You know, seeing an I about to die.

                                                        But we have Loyalists to find. We will soon be going to the art show, and we have a decent plan. Maria, almost unrecognizable in plainclothes, will be there to bless and curse as needed. Julia will be there as, well, an artist patronizing the show. Molly, Jacob and I will be sneaking around to jump out if Rook gets too close. I am well able to incapacitate. Davey and Calamity will be disguised as Elena and Natasha there. Meanwhile Juliette, Gawain, and Luna will be nearby at a café as reinforcements. Oh but how can it go wrong?

                                                        I don’t mind hunting Loyalists as long as there is Christmas music.
