    A couple that have turned up in my games:

    Winter Break: When a winter courtier is doing something the court wouldn't approve of or doesn't match the court stereotype. "Did you see Silas partying up with those frat boys? I guess he's on winter break."

    Poor Life Choices: A term for a changeling who has to keep being rescued by their motley mates or their court, usually in sympathy for those who have to keep up with the rescuing.. "Did you hear that they had to rescue Adrian again? Man, Poor Life Choices."

            Fae-holes- Privateers and loyalist that mingle with changelings and Humans a like, just to kidnap or deceive them. " Can't believe those guys did that... Bunch of Fae-holes. "

                    Highlander: A Changeling who slays their Fetch. "Man, she just went Highlander on that Fetch yesterday!"


                    Toll Troll: A bouncer, guard, or watchman who can be bribed to allow access.

                    Blind Mice: A derogatory term for a Motley who constantly get into danger. "Well if it isn't the 3 Blind Mice!"


                    Jester: A term for deceptively cunning Changelings who hide their wit behind a display of stupidity.


                    Frog Prince: A term for an ugly male who craves intimate affection from beautiful women.

                                            How about some school based slang?


                                            Prep Schooler:                                             A derogatory term for Changelings who are actively trying to become Gentry. "When I see one of you damned prep schoolers, I just want to kick your teeth in!"


                                            Public Schooling: A basic or learn-from-experience level of understanding the workings of Arcadia and The Hedge. "Man, the public schooling system is pathetic these days!"


                                            Hall Monitor: A guardian who bars free passage in The Hedge.


                                            Principal's Office: The domain of a True Fae. "I haven't heard from her since she got sent to the Principal's Office"


                                            Drop Out: A Changeling who attempts to abandon all ties to the Fae world, and live as a normal human.


                                            Expelled: A lost who is banished from stepping foot within Arcadia. "What did you do to get expelled? Must have been pretty bad!"


                                            Teacher's Pet: A Gentry's favorite Loyalist.


                                            Pre-Schooler: A freshly captured mortal, not yet affected by the magic of Arcadia.


                                            Bully: A derogatory term for a Lost who abuses their powers, especially targeting weaker victims.


                                            Class, the: The collective slaves gathered by a Gentry. "Settle down now, class. We have much to do today!"


                                            Class Pet: A Lost who's status is beneath that of most other slaves. "Okay, now who's turn is it to feed the class pet?"


                                            Clique: Motley who share a common theme, style, hobby or personality traits.


                                            Substitute: A Fetch


                                            Freshman: A Lost who's Wyrd level is between 1 and 3


                                            Sophmore: A lost who's Wyrd level is between 4 and 6


                                            Junior: A lost who's Wyrd level is between 7 and 9


                                            Senior: A Lost's who's Wyrd level is 10 or possibly higher
