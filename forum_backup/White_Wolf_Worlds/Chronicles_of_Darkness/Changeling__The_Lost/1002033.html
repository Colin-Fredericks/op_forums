    Developing the Admonitoria Concept

    11-11-2016, 09:37 PM
    I really like the Admonitoria concept and hope it will be explored/developed further. To promote it, I introduced two vampire type fairies in the Fae Entitites thread. Also, here are some ideas for merits to give Admonitoria characters.


    Taste of Blood (2 dots)
    Prerequisite: Academics 3, investigate 1, and either science or occult 3.
    Effect: By tasting a targets blood, they can get a great deal of genetic information from them. This includes ethnicity, heritage, lifespan, strengths and weaknesses, and whether they suffer from a genetic flaw.

    Redcaps (3 dots)
    Note: I am aware that the description of Admonitoria states that they cannot store vitae/glamour, but perhaps that can be edited. It would allow the player to explore the concept of how some types of fae (Redcaps being the most well known) use blood to charge their spells/magic. I think it would be cool if it was revealed that the Admonitoria had their own unique form of blood magic.
    Prerequisite: Academics 3, Occult 3
    Effect: The Admonitoria can store blood in a device, usually a cap, to use in their Thaumaturgy.

    Scent of Blood (3 or 5 dots)
    Prerequisite: Blood Potency 1

    Effect: Like sharks, the Admonitoria have a keen sense of smell for blood. at the three dot version, the Admonitoria is always aware whenever at least a drop of blood is spilled. At the five dot version, the Admonitoria can sense when life is nearby. No roll is necessary for either level of this merit.

    Doppelgänger
    Prerequisite: Taste of Blood, Blood Potency 6
    Effect: The Admonitoria can take the form of any individual whose blood they have drunk. Note: If they drink the blood of a supernatural being like a mage or werewolf, they can’t mimic their supernatural abilities. Though they CAN mimic the blessings of changelings.
    Tags: None

                              Claws of the Beast (2 dots)
                              Prerequisite: Blood Potency 3, Gangrel Heritage
                              Effect: Calling on the powers of the beast, the Admonitoria unsheathes three inch long claws that deal aggravated damage. Unlike pure vampires, who can only use claws of the unholy while frenzying, the Admonitoria can use these claws by spending a point of glamor/vitae.
                              Drawback: this power puts the vampire and fairy nature out of line, so the Admonitoria must make a five dice balance roll when using this ability.

                              Attuned to the Elements (1 to 3 dots)
                              Prerequisite: Protean 2, Blood Potency 3, Gangrel heritage
                              Effect: The Admonitoria can adjust to extreme environments; such as extreme heat or cold, the ability to breath underwater, or the ability to adjust to and survive in caverns hundreds of miles below the Earth surface. Each dot in this merit allow him to adjust to one type of extreme environment.

                              Beasts of Legend (1 to 2 dots)
                              Prerequisite: Protean 4, Blood Potency 5, Gangrel heritage.
                              Effect: This power allows the Admonitoria to become of the fae monsters of legend. These include not just generic monsters like dragons, griffons, and unicorns, but also manticores, chimera’s, hell hounds, man beast forms similar to those of werewolves and other shape shifters, or even some Lovecraftion abomination. The Admonitoria can become one legendary beast per dot The Admonitoria gains all the powers of dice bonuses associated with the beast.
                              Drawback: Along with the powers and strengths of the monster whose form you take, you also gain their frailties and weaknesses.
