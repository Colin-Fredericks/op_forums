    Suncursed:

    "I've been here the whole time. Didn't you notice one of the decorations is missing? "


    From gargoyles, to garden gnomes, trolls and even some iterations of dwarf. There are many tales of those who transform into inert stone at the touch of sunlight. Changelings who became Suncursed were forced to adapt to a nocturnal life cycle, moving about only at night and taking shelter from the sun, lest they be rendered defenseless. Knowing they could not move during the day, their Keeper never thought to place guards around them during that time. That is until they found a way to break the spell and regain their mobility in daylight!


    Wizened: A wee little man with a big nose, white beard and pointy hat. He loves to tinker with machines as a hobby.


    Ogre: A pale skinned troll with giant muscles and tiny brain, he's lost too many good meals because he wasted the night away conversing with them.


    Beast: A fearsome gargoyle who possesses the horns of a goat, wings of a bat, tail of a lizard, face of a demon and claws of a dragon.


    Blessing: While in Arcadia, the Suncursed turned to stone upon exposure to sunlight, but they discovered a way to regain their mobility while in such a state. When exposed to sunlight, the Suncursed gains +3 to their Defense rating as their body hardens like rock. Additionally by spending a point of Glamour, the Suncursed can add their Wyrd to Stealth rolls and is able to use them without penalty for hiding in plain view. Even if the room is fully illuminated with a crowd of people, onlookers will simply believe them to be a statue meant for decoration. This disguise only works if they are remaining still, as people are bound to take notice of a statue walking around the place.
    The honeysouls most definitely feel pain when being eaten. They are still being injured, even if it is done deliberately to give another character a benefit. Of course, they might be more likely than others to develop vorarephilia.

    I like your suncursed, although I would make it Armor instead of Defense given the flavor. I'd also say that their bonus to Stealth rolls should only function when they are holding still to keep the statue theme going. Still, very good job.

            Mageslayer
            "Your magic shall not save you!"

            Those transformed into Mageslayers were made into weapons against the very magic of Arcadia itself. Their durance saw them destroy enchantments and kill sorcerers with ruthless efficiency. However, when they finally chose to defy their Keepers, they were able to repel whatever magic was used to try and subdue them.

            • Beast: A lambent-eyed owl with bone white feathers.
            • Elemental: Sculpted from mountain ash, this entity's presence causes an adverse reaction to the senses of any unfortunate witch.
            • Fairest A white-haired warrior with golden .cat's eyes and an intricate array of sigils marked upon his body.

            Blessing: Mageslayers gain two dots in the Contract of Abjuration, and can always invoke it as a Reflexive action. They also gain a +3 bonus for contesting or resisting any harmful supernatural powers.

                        Sentinel
                        "Just so we're clear. If you hurt her in any way, I will hunt you down and make you wish you were never even born!"

                        Lost who were forced to protect what their Keepers judged as most valuable, whether it was a person, place, or thing. Put to task as watchdogs, palace guards, and bridge trolls, they developed both a unique sense of territoriality, as well as a protective instinct to their charges. Needless to say, it was those same qualities that drove them to escape either to protect someone from the wrath of their Keeper, or to seek out what mattered more to him in the world he was taken from.

                        Blessing: Sentinels gain three dots in the Defender Merit, and add half their Wyrd (rounded up) to their Initiative when fighting in a Hollow or Safe Place they own. If they spend a point of Glamour while touching someone, they may grant their target a minor blessing which gives +2/+2 Armor. This bonus is doubled if the subject is a Touchstone.
                        I like these guys a lot, although I agree that their flavor is pretty similar to the Troll. The blessing is different enough, though, that they'd synergize well together. Given that the rest of their blessing protects an individual, I could see the bonus to Initiative coming up in a Safe Place owned by their target rather than themselves. I'd also say that the Armor granting portion of their blessing should have a duration, perhaps something like a number of turns equal to their Wyrd? I could also see them gaining the Danger Sense Merit directed towards the object of their defense if they have an active Pledge going to do so. For Seeming descriptions:

                        Blackguard
                        "All is fair in love and war. I just ended up having the last laugh in this fight."

                        In the Game of Immortals, the rules of engagement allow for more than just 'fair play'. And the Gentry create Blackguards for many of the more shadowy threatres of war. As mortals, they may have been jaded and pragmatic, looking after themselves and willing to get results with whatever tricks they had. Unfortunately, this knack for survival was what aided them when they backstabbed their Keepers and made their escape.

                        Blessing: A Blackguard gains three dots in the Killer Instinct Merit, and can apply it reflexively on any creature. If he spends a point of Glamour, he may raise his effective dot rating in the Merit by half his Wyrd (rounded up).
                        I would need to track down and read the Merit to be able to give a proper critique of these guys. That said, I like the flavor of them.

                        Spelldrinker
                        "That was a neat party trick. Got any more?"

                        In a land where magic exists in abundance, the Spelldrinkers existed as a means to warn their Keepers of hostile sorcery, as well as ward them from such powers as well. They were essentially arcane lightning rods, painfully conditioned to absorb magic so that they may take their power unto themselves, or turn it back against the offending witch.

                        • Darkling: A dark robed figure with pale skin and discolored eyes. Emulating the code of the Sith, they learned how to usurp the power of their rivals and turn it to their own ends.
                        • Fairest: Standing firm in the face of the Gentry's wrath, neither sword nor sorcery can frighten the Freehold's champion as they march into battle.
                        • Elemental: An energy being whose body flares up in the presence of magic.
                        • Grimm: Lines of runic script are etched upon his skin, a spell of protection in narrative form.
                        • Ogre: Raw magic saturates his flesh, and leaks out as wisps of smoke from luminescent scars and bleeding wounds.
                        • Wizened: Small, crystal diodes have been grafted to their flesh, generating a protective network when they sense incoming magic.

                        Blessing: A Spelldrinker benefits from the 8-again rule on all kenning rolls, and rolls them for free as reflexive actions regardless of his Clarity score. If he successfully contests or resists a supernatural power used against him, he may store the power for the remainder of the scene unless he uses it sooner. The Spelldrinker can store a number of such powers equal to his Wyrd for a scene, after which they dissolve into Glamour that is absorbed. The powers of other supernaturals always yield one point of Glamour.

                        Note: A Spelldrinker uses his own dots in Attributes, Skills, and Wyrd when rolling for powers acquired by his blessing.
                        This is an interesting one. It does seem very powerful, although I can't deny that the concept is cool. I would limit their dice pool to the lower of the original caster or the Spelldrinker's own, possible with an additional penalty to using powers without a fae origin. I'd also make it so that when it dissolves, it is one point of Glamour regardless of which kind of power was used. The kenning rolls benefit is an interesting one, and I like it. I can't remember, was it a reflexive action for regular changelings whenever their Clarity was high enough, or did it take an action? The Seeming descriptions are great.

                        Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                        Here's another kith from me, folks.


                        -+-

                        Watcher
                        "I saw what you did, back there..."

                        They say that seeing is believing. And for those made into Watchers, that statement is especially true. Their Keepers made them into spyglasses and telescopes, or imbued their vision with potent magic so that they may extend their sight across long distances.

                        • Beast: A feline huntress whose pupils dilate whenever she prowls through the shadows.
                        • Darkling: Despite having spent so long in the darkness, nothing has ever avoided being seen by his wide eyes.
                        • Elemental: A man made of finely polished wood, with eyes made of thick, reflective glass.

                        Blessing: A Watcher can never be permanently blinded, and always benefits from a single automatic success on any vision-based rolls. If he spends a point of Glamour within a scene and take a turn to concentrate, he may extend his telescopic vision to observe anything within a number of kilometers equal to his Wyrd. While this ability is active, he takes a -3 penalty to all other actions.
                        This is an interesting blessing, but it reminds me rather a lot of the cleareyes. I really like the Seeming descriptions you've got for this one.

                                                Siren
                                                "Come. Join me here while I sing".

                                                Inspiring the old myths made by sailors in the past, Sirens were made by aquatic Keepers to lure the unwary into the depths of their realms. Few ever turn away once they hear their songs, and the sites where Sirens are known to gather soon become littered with the bones of those who drowned in their efforts to reach them.

                                                • Elemental: A benevolent rusalka who flies out of the water in the spring to bring rain to the fields.
                                                • Fairest: A sea nymph with avian and piscine features. The talons and wings of the former, and gills and iridescent scales for the latter.

                                                Blessing: The Siren is able to breathe underwater, and can fly for a number of minutes equal to half her Wyrd (rounded up) before she needs to spend a turn without moving to rest. If she spends Glamour and sings, she can enthrall listeners by rolling Manipulation + Expression vs. Resolve + Wyrd. Enthralled listeners subtract the changeling's Wyrd from all rolls to notice their surroundings and suffer from the Swooning Condition toward the Siren for the scene.
