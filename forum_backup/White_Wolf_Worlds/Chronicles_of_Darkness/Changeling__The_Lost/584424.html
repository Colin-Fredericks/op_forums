    Changelings becoming human?

    07-09-2015, 05:43 AM
    Equinox Road describes how a Changeling can reach Wyrd 10 and then transform into one of the Gentry. Would it be possible for a changeling to reduce her Wyrd to zero and become human again? Will either of these be possible in 2nd edition?

      I can't speak to whether these possibilities will be discussed in the 2nd edition book, but stuff like this is generally beyond the scope of the normal rules anyway. If it's possible for a Changeling to become fully human once more, it would presumably require the kind of legendary feat that a whole chronicle could be built around. There has been some discussion of "Icons" in second edition, which seem to be things that a Changeling left behind in Arcadia when they escaped, and I imagine that regaining one's humanity would involve recovering those.

      In first edition, Autumn Nightmares presented the possibility of a Changeling merging with their Fetch, and thereby reclaiming the piece of their soul that their Keeper used in creating the Fetch. That seems like another reasonable prerequisite to reclaiming one's humanity. The Changeling would probably need to undergo some kind of quest in the Hedge as well, since the thorns themselves may have town away portions of their soul as they were carried off to Arcadia.

          If such a thing were possible, you would probably need a Clarity rating of 10 in order to completely suppress your memories of Arcadia. Similar principle to Golconda, in which you utterly deny your Beast.

          The Wyrd isn't just having access to magical abilities, it is the essence of Arcadia itself. It has bonded with you, filling in the gaps of your soul that were torn away when you were captured. That means in order to remove it, you're going to have be torn apart again so that your human soul can be patched back together. Becoming human should neither be easy nor pleasant, kind of like somebody going through rehabilitation after experiencing a traumatic event.

                                                          Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                                                          Yeah, I'd be really uncomfortable with the idea of a Changeling becoming human again because of the implications that has when taken in the context of the trauma metaphor.
                                                          Realistically this would be the case. You can forgive and overcome but not really forget or turn back into what you were before. On the other hand this is a storytelling game so a better question to ask is not how it can be accommodated using the system but rather, if that's the kind of story a troupe wants to tell, what's the best way to go about it?

                                                          Note that I agree that it isn't something that should be possible, as MCN notes further below (or above this particular post) you can overcome trauma but you still bear the scars. Nevertheless, a lot of stories gloss over this (just look at the recovery / "return to normalcy" that Fitz went through during the course of AoS season 2 -- people with brain injuries (or a lot of other kinds of injuries) don't return to "normal"). Even if it's against the preferences of some of us, I don't see a problem with speculating as to how the game can be used to accommodate such a story. As has been pointed out, it'd probably look like Vampire's Golconda. IIRC, oWoD Changeling actually had a bunch of rules and text devoted to answering this question. You (the original poster) might want to have a look-see at that material for ideas how it might be adapted to the Lost. Most likely it'll be combination of factors (recovering bits of soul from the Hedge, reducing Wyrd to 1, and increasing Clarity to 8+).
