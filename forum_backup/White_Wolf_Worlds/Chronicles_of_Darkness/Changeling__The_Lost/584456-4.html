                        Oh! So, if someone were to become immune to their Keeper's torments through a mind over matter approach or sheer force of will, that metaphorical escape would manifest as a literal escape? So, if they're escaping into their music, they might find a trail of sheet music into a tunnel that leads them to safety (Wizened). Or if they adopt the unfeeling nature of stone to endure torture, they literally become one with the land and can pull themselves out of the earth in the hedge (Elemental). Or, maybe, they withstand horrible torture and refuse to rat out their fellow prisoners, so then the Wyrd causes their guards to drop the key just within reach of his cell door (Fairest). Technically, they're not doing anything different than someone who's toughing it out - endurance/fortitude/willpower is still the thrust of it, but the Wyrd makes it into a literal "breaking out" sort of escape. Their internal resistance is transformed into an external means of gaining freedom. I can get behind this. I feel like I can easily modify players' concepts if something like this comes up, with this in mind. So, thanks for that, Charlaquin.
