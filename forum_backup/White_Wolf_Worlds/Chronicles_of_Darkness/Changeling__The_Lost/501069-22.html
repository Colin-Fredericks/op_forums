Are we annoyed with that argument because of long histories with it mixed in with our own readings? Considering I've been in the community as a solid presence for five years and a lurker for two, and Satchel's older than I am forum wise? I think it's fair to say that also warrants an "Okay, we've made our point."

                                            Nothing goods comes out of attaching motives to posts unless those motives are stated. It's a reductionistic and mean-spirited thing to do, as bad as 'veiled insults'. A person using it implies that there's no point conversing with them because they have taken a view of another and everything they say and won't budge from it. What's the point of the internet if not to at least have substantial communication?

                                            We all want this to be a good game. I assume if we're here, that's our central motive. I disagree with a lot of what's been said here-I even somewhat disagree Dymphna who wrote the piece!-but I assume that we're all here because we think this game deserves to be excellent. I think some of the ideas presented are stupid, I think some of the attitudes about this game are horrendous, I think some of the interpretations put forth are amazingly trite and dull-But I'd rather assume everyone means the best than assume we're trying to shit in each others games.

                                            I might have the wrong attitude about this. I probably do in some places! But I think it's serviceable.

                                            So, I'm sorry, but here a wrong has been done. This was not a good thing to do. Let's not do it again. Ideally, we should all take a breather, let the thread rest, wait for the next preview, and go and be excellent people, assuming the best of one another, while disagreeing with each other. But barring that happening, let's just not do this again.
