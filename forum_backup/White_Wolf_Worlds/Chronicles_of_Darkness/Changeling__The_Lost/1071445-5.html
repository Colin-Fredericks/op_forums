                                                        Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
                                                        That makes sense. And there's no reason you couldn't do the same with male characters who gain the Goblin Queen Condition.


                                                        It is, I just think Goblin Queen sounds cooler, and it seems like a double standard to be worried about the default name for the Condition being gender-neutral when no such concern is forthcoming about the default title for vampire leaders.

                                                        Honestly, it sounds incongrous to gender-locked old me...but I can think of an interesting history behind "Queen" being the unigender title;

                                                        I'd say that term came soon after eusociality was fully discovered, when the Lost did a full study of Goblin Queens. It was originally thought that all of their direct minions were Oneiroi born from the Queens themselves. In effect, they were the "mothers" of their minions, more like the reigning mother of a hive than a true regent.

                                                        Of course, this was also around the point where the dominant Lost culture was based around a grand phobia of insanity and prejudice against those who were dealing with Clarity issues. Those were the days of the hippie movement, something that quickly earned the suspicions of changelings when it turns out hippie behavior tends to induce breaking points. As a result, anything that resembled the New Age was regarded as inherently suspect and likely enthralled by the True Fae. Thus, the Hedge was regarded as far more of a trap than it actually is, and all hobgoblins unknowing minions-so, of course, the fact that some changelings not only adapted to it, but so well that the Huntsman can no longer find them, was a bit of cognitive dissonance that could only be solved by largely pretending that Goblin Queens who willingly agreed to oaths of non-betrayal were not, somehow, goblins.

                                                        As the Red Scare and hippies passed by, most Lost realized how stupid this was, but the name stuck.

                                                        (It should be noted that birthing Oneiroi for a set of minions is entirely possible, it's just not always how a Queen gains her court).
