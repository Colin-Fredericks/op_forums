                                                            I don't see the Fairest as natural leaders... They're the changelings flung into positions where the others are expecting them to lead, either as ones who escaped with the Fairest from Arcadia on that one fateful day or just due to the Seeming itself and how it is gained. Their Blessing activates essentially when they blag or fast-talk a decision... Their Curse reinforces this where it comes in when people get hurt from these spur of the moment decisions. These are people woefully unprepared for a role that's been thrust upon them by taking agency in one specific moment of time and they're being remembered for it and a repeat performance is expected. Maybe it just comes down to that they're the prettiest one about, people put their trust in them down to their looks and now they have to follow through because the house of cards is going to tumble if they don't do something.
                                                            They're not natural leaders, if anything they're battlefield promotions.
