    Question About Fae Titles And The Grimm Seeming In 2ed

    09-15-2015, 09:36 PM
    To become a Grimm, do you necessarily have to assume the role of a character in a traditional and well known fairy tale, or can it be something more modern and not strictly tied to fairy tales? I would like to play changelings who consciously model themselves after the ideas of Alice from Alice In Wonderland and Sherlock Holmes, for example.

    How do the True Fae change their Titles between their manifestations of Realm, Actor, Prop and Wisp? They probably don't turn Realm into the other types because that is where they keep all their stuff and servants. But what if a True Fae decides to store a Title currently invested into a Realm as a Prop in her pocket to transform it into a Realm again somewhere else in the Hedge? Do all the buildings and other structures of the Realm suddenly transform into homogeneous thorns and brambles vaguely shaped like what they were before? I assume investing Titles into Realms outside the Hedge is impossible, though clever use of Contracts and Pledges might let parts of the Realm connect to the mortal world. Also, how often can the True Fae change their Titles from one type to another? I assume it has a limit, like once per scene or chapter. I didn't see any mention of costs like Willpower dots or anything like that. I can't find answers for all of this in Equinox Road.

    Bye.

      No commentary on the Grimm, since, you know, it's not out. Buuuut.

      First off, there's a distinction between Realms and Domains. All Strangers have a Domain-they have a set with pieces and actors, but the set is not important. The underground kingdom in Pan's Labyrinth that we never really see is a Domain, and if we stretch our imagination a little, the labyrinth and 'gate' are also Domains-interesting and pertinent to the plot, and yet not really wholly significant on it's own-hell, even the household is more important than the labyrinth. A Realm, on the other hand, is Important. It's Mount Doom, it's the Goblin King's Labyrinth, it's the Stone Table, it's the Amityville House. These are places where Things Happen, places that have their own character and story and set of Inevitabilites.

      Secondly, I would argue that a Title can't shift until it fulfills it's purpose in the Legend currently being played out, but by and large a True Fae by the rules as written can shift them whenever they need to. That sounds lame, so like I said, I would argue that a Title is locked into a Role until the Legend it's engaged in is played out, at which point it and the associated Domain shift to Arcadian whim.
