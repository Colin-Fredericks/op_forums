    Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
    If you are born in the Hedge do you automatically become a hedge denizen?
    There is no textual statement one way or the other. Goblin Queens can hand over their place in the Hedge to a human child and may engineer an agreement for someone to give one to them, and the classic bit of mythology about being careful not to eat the food in fairyland or you won't be able to leave has a lot of interpretations, but both of those situations generally apply to babies who've been born already; being a hobgoblin can mean you were made into one or it can mean you came into existence as a part of the Hedge and without magic to look into it (like Goblin's Eye) it's hard to tell which kind a given hobgoblin is.

    What seems like a reasonable basic assumption to me would be that, without a biological parent who has at least some amount of Goblin Debt or other supernatural oddity, a child will not be born a hobgoblin.

    (The Hedge Denizen Condition is largely immaterial to this question unless you are making the assumption that a newborn child or infant has Traits suitable for a player character to engage with that are not themselves the product of Goblin Contracts and/or Dread Powers granted through the Condition.)

                            Originally posted by Sikander View Post
                            Does Court Goodwill ever get you the Court's extra method of gaining Glamour? It's not associated with any Mantle dot. So do you get it at Goodwill 1, Goodwill 3 (3-2 = 1, the first time Goodwill grants you effective Mantle), or never? Do Fae-Touched with Goodwill get it?
                            No explicit statement to that effect, but given that it's the primary thing distinguishing a changeling with that Court's Mantle from an honorary member with Goodwill (and there's no hard cap on the number of Courts you can have Goodwill with), my reading is "no."

                            Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                            Can Changeling become a True Fae or something close to a True Fae?
                            There are mechanics for it happening in 1e (Becoming Nightmare in The Equinox Road, plus the abbreviated/altered version in Dancers in the Dusk to go with the system hack where your Wyrd rating can move around wildly), and 2e has not presented any information that contradicts the possibility. It's a failure state to become a heartless magical solipsist in a game where your character's continued well-being depends on sustained connection with their former humanity; the 1e mechanics largely boiled down to "after you reach Wyrd 10, at an unspecified point determined by your player and the Storyteller, you start rolling Clarity breaking points every time you wake up until you either drop to Wyrd 9 through magical detox or hit Clarity 0, jump into the Hedge, and run off to Arcadia to complete your metamorphosis."

                            Powerful entitlements may textually enable one to become "something close to a True Fae," particularly taken in combination with manifesting Regalia and/or becoming one of the Empty.
