                                                            Originally posted by Two-Shadows View Post

                                                            My misinterpretation is on me, not you.

                                                            I still worry about the huntsmen's potential for hijacking/derailing a game.
                                                            You're not alone in that. Personally, I think it's an idea that needs expansion, I just see how it can be expanded to avoid becoming too much of a spotlight hog.

                                                            I will say that True Fae not hogging as much is probably a good thing overall, I got sick of Fae as Pyramid Head, every time they showed up.

                                                            They're supposed to be more mysterious and powerful than that, even if they're very personal tormentors. Again, could help show how the Court System actually does keep them away.

                                                            Hey, here's a thought-some Huntsmen are just as much victims of the True Fae as their quarry is, but they don't really have a choice. Also, their Ban equivalent is the circumstance where they may actually abduct someone, otherwise they're under Wyrd law to avoid directly attacking, though not harassment. This is generally a state that isolates the prey in some fashion, whether they be oathbreakers, motley-less, or lack a human identity.

                                                            I'd also recommend to avoid them being one-trick ponies; they hunt, but they don't necessarily hunt for changelings, although that's a big percentage of their paycheck. Many are on other errands in the mortal world for True Fae, or even between jobs and using their abilities in the mortal world for their own agenda.
