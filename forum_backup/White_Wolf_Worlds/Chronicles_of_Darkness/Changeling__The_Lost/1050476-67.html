            I was thinking about the extra side effect of a bargain with supernaturals.
            I will soon have a motley of 3 players working with a Promethean throng of 2 players, and probably they will strike a group bargain.
            Apart from the normal benefits i m considering the idea of adding this not foreseen extras (a smaller version of miasma rules):
            -Prometheans doesn t cause disquiet to the motley
            -changelings gains extra dice harvesting from the throng s milestones
            But the prices for this is:
            -all motley s fetches becomes part of the throng, and can start the pilgrimage under the promethean tutelage (with all the added rules from dark eras 2).
            True fae and huntsman pursuing the changelings become subjected to a modified version of the fetches extempore disquiet, they mistakes the fetches for the corresponding changelings and tries to capture or kill them instead.
