        Yeah, I definitely don't know if it's specifically the author's or developer's intent, but it's one of the most appealing features for me that Seemings have detailed example backgrounds.

        I've had good and bad experiences with Changeling character creation, but the good ones always told a story including who they were before they were taken, how and why they were taken, what they were made to do during their Durance, and how and why they escaped. Unfortunately, more often I just saw detailed descriptions of character appearance with a slapped together background that was mostly there to justify why they looked the way they did. These new Seemings write ups all tell stories, and the kind of stories that I've seen lead to well-developed, interesting Changeling characters.

        I'm thrilled that Seeming will mean something more than just an archetype, and that there are robust examples to draw from for players who may not be used to thinking about their Changeling characters in that way. So hopefully you'll understand why I find it so disheartening to see so many people object to the detailed example backgrounds.

        While I too find the the percent values somewhat arbitrary, I actually don't disagree with the underlying impression that background is an extremely significant determining factor in what Seeming a character ends up in. I just don't think that the character's background has to cleave to the examples. Past experiences help shape characters, Changeling or otherwise. Ultimately, a character's Seeming comes down to how they escaped, but that decision should make sense for the character, and when writing your character's background, you should consider how it may have lead up to your character's capture and their escape.

        That's my 2 cents, anyway.
