    CTL 1E The Slumber Courts

    01-07-2024, 03:26 PM
    Hi, I wanted to make a court set, but i wanted to challenge myself more than usual. So i made those courts in 1e CTL instead of 2e since the former is less able to handle more Courts than the latter. Let's take a look,shall we?




    (1e) The Slumber Courts

    They are the youngest of the Great Courts of the Lost, created less than 300 years ago. Yet the ideal they adhere to is ancient, The Lost are creatures of dreams and should play to their strengths. The Slumber Courts are slowly finding purchase all over the world, as the mysteries of dreams entice the Lost either because they're power hungry, need safeguards against dream predators, or are just curious about what dreams may become.

    Power Breakdown

    ​

    All Great Courts need a power breakdown to repel and confuse the Faerie, the Smynber Courts method is the Map of Dreamers.

    Each Freehold has a Map of Dreamers, it shows how many people capable of dreaming are in the Freeholds' territory. The Map of Dreamers divides the Freeholds into three parts, based on the types of dreams the dreamers have. But here's the thing.

    The three territory's size doesn't have to be the same. The territory of one Court can be much smaller than another, yet The Slumber Courts accept this. That's what repels the Gentry, accepting lesser territory is anathema to them.
    ​
    ​


    The Courts.

    The Court of Dreams
    (The Catchers, The Court of Joy)


    There's no such thing as dreamless sleep. The Court of Dreams knows it.

    When you sleep, you always dream even if you don't remember. The Dream Court sees dreams as much more than stories your subconscious tells you, many of them have a religious understanding that dreams are reality itself.

    They are the ones who keep dreams records, who find meanings in dreams, and who re-enact the drama in their heads in the life around them. To them, dreams are just the next step in reality, and more importantly, they are visions of what reality could and should be.

    Courtiers

    They are sages, cerebral and whimsical types even among the Lost. Sometimes hedonistically chasing the next high, sometimes looking for the next insight through asceticism.




    Rituals:


    They use rituals to go unconscious in many cultures, from entheogenic drugs to a simple glass of milk. They like to try to sleep in weird positions and often like to paint their dreams.

    It is common for their courtly gathering to include more speeches than necessary. They seek to inspire others to follow their ambitions
    ​​

    ​

    Heraldry:


    Their colors are green and pink, and their sacred animal is the unicorn. Clouds are often seen in their shields.




    Mantle:


    The Mantle of the Dreams Court is bright, it makes everything around seem muted by stark contrast to it. It emanates a weird mirage, like the heat on the road in the desert

    At Mantle *-*** features from pleasant dreams such as birdsong might play around and at Mantle ***** actual physical dream objects appear if just for a moment


    At mantle * the Changeling can fall unconscious anytime he chooses,( doesn't mean he can't be woken up though)

    At Mantle *** the Changeling gains +1 on all Oneiromancy rolls

    At Mantle***** the changeling

    may once per scene use his Mantle as Armor for (Wyrd) turns.



    Emotion: Joy


    Dreams are a source of joy, they lift us and inspire us. They leave us wanting more of life. And thus Joy is the Emotion of the Court of Dreams and they foster it in themselves and others. Anything goes, from being a hospital clown to someone who beats evil politicians in humiliating ways.


    Court of Daydreams

    Reality is nothing without a little escape and the Court of Daydreams knows it.

    Their dreams are those waking ones, the ones where you just want to be somewhere other than you are and so invent a new reality in your mind if just for a few seconds. They understand that you can't daydream and as such they believe in taking inspiration from them to change reality,until reality and daydreams are completely blurred.


    Courtiers:


    They are artists, especially writers. Always helping create new realities, scientists helping this reality find a new edge, and revolutionaries trying to find a new reality through the right actions.


    Rituals


    The Courts of Daydreams love a good holiday and they always enjoy Christmas, Halloween, and Thanksgiving in their most stereotypical way. Those Holidays are seen as living daydreams in the middle of reality


    Heraldry


    The currents of the wind and kabuki masks feature prominently in the Court's heraldry


    Mantle


    Their dreams are those waking ones, the ones where you just want to be somewhere other than you are and so invent a new reality in your mind if just for a few seconds. They understand that you can't daydream and as such they believe in taking inspiration from them to change reality until reality and daydreams are completely blurred.


    Emotion: Longing


    Longing is the heart of Daydreams, the desire to be there and not here. Longing must be fostered so the world can improve but it must be encouraged with care lest it turns into depression. Even the loudest members of the Court of Daydreams try to be subtle while fostering Longing, it’s an ephemeral emotion but its effects are powerful.


    The Court of Nightmares


    Nightmares are powerful things, the Court of Nightmares knows it. They believe the more Nightmares they cause the more power they will have. It is a simple mission with a thousand and one permutations.

    They are the horror movie monsters, the ones who always leave one survivor to tell the story. They are demagogues of power and desperation, they are the other embodied.


    Courtiers:


    Killers, hunters, monsters. Desperation is their bread and butter, they must keep the world in a state of frantic madness, so it doesn't fall into boredom and banality. For that sacrifices must be made, no pain no gain


    Rituals:

    Their Rituals are specialized, each of them has a specialized way to do their mission and thus a specialized ritual. Baking pies with the meat of your enemies, scaring people with make, setting fires

    …anything goes really


    Heraldry


    Broken earth, struck by lightning, swords, and hunting dogs such as mastiffs sell figures in the Court iconography.


    Mantle


    Their Mantle both exudes an aura of predation of the courtiers, making them much more animalistic and inhuman. At Mantle *-*** this is subtle such as yellow eyes or a smell of sulfur but at Mantle ***** they look downright demonic


    Emotion: Desperation


    Desperation drives people on. It makes them want things if that is just to enjoy life. It makes the world more interesting. Like with spices, a life with no desperation is simply too banal. they feel all the monsters and monstrous actions (with a few exceptions that should be obvious) have a place in this world, for they make the world interesting

      the following contracts are based on the Contracts of Reflections from Equinox,but hopefully different enough to be their own thing.



      Contracts of Perception


      Dreams and Reality are the same thing, this is the truth. This Contract lets a changeling understand this truth and use it to their advantage


      Seeing What He Dreams (•)

      The Lost can look into his target eyes, or touch him and see what he’s been dreaming or daydreaming about.

      Cost: 1 Glamour

      Dice Pool: Empathy+ Wyrd

      Action: Instant

      Catch: The Target is someone who is the Changeling’s True Friend, as per the merit.


      Dramatic Failure: The Lost thinks he saw the truth, but he actually saw the opposite.


      Failure: he simply cannot see anything


      Success: The Changeling can see what the target has been dreaming and daydreaming about, what always gives him useful and relevant information about the target's goals and plans. this only covers the past week


      Exceptional Success: The Changeling can see through the past month


      Appear in his mind (••)

      The Lost can send a message via the dream ways, a message only his target will see

      Cost: 1 Glamour

      Dice Pool: Composure + Wyrd

      Action: Instant

      Catch: The Target is someone is the Changeling’s True Friend, as per the merit.



      Dramatic Failure: The Changeling gains a blinding migraine trying to send the message. he is blinded by a scene


      Failure: The Changeling simply can’t send the message


      Success: The Changeling sends a simple two-sentence message to anyone in (wyrd) miles with whom he has interacted before, only the target can hear the message


      Exceptional Success: The message can be sent in (wyrd) 10 miles.





      Can’t Hit Dreams (•••)

      The changeling infuses himself with the ephemerality of dreams, making him hard to hit or grasp


      Cost: 2 Glamour

      Dice Pool: Dexterity + Wyrd

      Action: Instant

      Catch: The character attacking the changeling an enemy of his, someone who truly hates the Changeling



      Dramatic Failure: The Changeling slows himself down, gaining a -1 penalty in combat rolls


      Failure: The Changeling can’t infuse himself


      Success: The Changeling gains a bonus to his Dexterity and rolls to Dodge equal to his success


      Exceptional Sucess: Extra successes are their own reward



      Dreams Build Bridges (••••)

      The changeling can travel from one place to another and use dreamers as gates


      Cost: 3 Glamour

      Dice Pool: Athletics + Wyrd

      Action: Instant

      Catch: One of the dreamers is the Changeling’s True Friend, as per the merit.


      Dramatic Failure: The effort gives the Changeling a blinding migraine he is blinded by a scene


      Failure: The effect doesn’t take


      Success: If the Changeling knows two people who are currently sleeping or at least daydreaming he can touch one and teleport to the vicinity of another


      Exceptional Success: The changeling can step back

      and forth through the people for a number of

      turns equal to his Wyrd without spending more Glamour.



      Illusion becomes Reality (•••••)

      The changeling can reach into a drawing, painting or other form and art and remove the idea of any object with a Size equal to or less than his

      Wyrd x 2. Once removed, this idea is a solid object.

      Cost: 3 Glamour + 1 Willpower

      Dice Pool: Wyrd + Larceny

      Action: Instant

      Catch: The changeling removes an image of an object

      owned by someone who is in True Friend to him. per the merit

      Roll Results

      Dramatic Failure: The object shatters loudly

      and violently. If the object is solid, it will inflict

      one point of lethal damage from the shards of broken glass

      or metal. If the reflection is from a pool of liquid, the liquid

      erupts from the pool, spraying everyone standing within a

      yard of it.

      Failure: The effect fails.

      Success: The changeling can remove the image of an

      object currently reflected in a painting, drawing or another art form, effectively conjuring

      a solid duplicate. However, the actual object must have

      actually been represented in this surface; the entirety of the object

      or person must have been represented in the art and

      the image must be sufficiently clear that the object is

      perceivable, detailed, and relatively unstylized

      The changeling can only pull out full-sized objects,

      not those that have been increased or reduced in Size.The object’s Size cannot be

      more than the changeling’s Wyrd x2. A changeling with

      a Wyrd of three could remove an object with a maximum

      Size of six, while a changeling with a Wyrd of six could remove

      objects of up to Size 12, which allows them to remove

      both cars and small boats from art. Any object or

      a living being removed from art is an exact image. this only lasts one scene. The Changeling can also do the same with Dreams, albeit he needs Oneiromancy to see into them



      Exceptional Success: The idea of the object remains. solid and substantial until the next time the Sun rises

      or sets.
