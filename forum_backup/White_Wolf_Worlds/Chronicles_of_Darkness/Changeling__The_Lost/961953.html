    New York City Goblin Market

    09-01-2016, 07:11 PM
    ​So I'm running a Mage the awakening game and my PC's are heading to the goblin market next session. They are after a mage that escaped from the guardians of the veil. They can't track him and think he might have sold his name at the goblin market. (Which he did). They managed to track down the information how to enter it, and have not had any contact with any changelings or their ilk yet.

    I got my roommate to read over my market I came up with and he though I should post it on the forums so others could have a look, use it, or what have you. I got inspiration for many sources when coming out with my vendors, and hope someone else can get some good out of it.

    Enjoy.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------





    New York Goblin Market
    Location: Central park
    Time: Gates can be accessed on Wednesday, Between sundown and sun up.

    Gate: Cedar Hill Bridge (Bottom gate)
    Key: Must draw a door in chalk under the bridge, and knock 2 times, then 3 very fast, run the 4 fingers of your hand down one edge of the chalk erasing it, and lose some change. (Door will form along the chalk outline, chalk shakes off when it closes)
    Other side: Brick wall of surreal graffiti. There is some black ivy with red diamond shaped leaves overlapping it forming a door shape. There is a dark red and green flower with white dots and red thorns that resembles an African Daisy growing in the cracks inside the door shape. It looks like a handle. (Flower moves and snaps at people trying to grab it)

    Gate: Bethesda Fountain (Middle gate)
    Key: Must take a breath underwater and start drowning, find a penny thrown in the fountain and swallow it.
    Other side: There is a fountain that resembles a group girls made out of stone wearing dresses in the style of the 1800's. They look like they are dancing around the center, but are not moving. (Usually) The center has a pile of skeletons crawling over each other trying to reach a golden gem encrusted chalice in the middle. The skeletons look to be real, and covered in posies that look like human skin with a red spotted rash. There is water pouring out of the skeletons mouths. There is a circular rusted metal trap door in the water with a large metal ring and chain in the middle. Pulling the chain open triangle flaps inside and in the shape of the circle. After the circle is open, the chain pulls you through the portal to the other side.

    Gate: Hutt in Shakespeares Garden (Top gate)
    Key: There is a old wooden door that the key has gone missing to. You Can use a key the you own but don't know what it belongs to to open the door, after destroying something you father owns. (If deceased, something he considered his when alive)
    Other side: There is a giants head with one large right arm stitched to it's forehead. There are a dozen or so spikes dug into it flesh and chaining it to the floor and walls. It looks to be in it's 50 or so, and is has a ring of thinning red hair around the back of its head. It has thick bushy eyebrows and stubbly facial hair growth, like a week of growth. It's a little red in the face, and sweaty, as it looks uncomfortable. You have to let it put you in it's mouth and you come back out into the real world. [Sounds like Louis C K. “ Hey. Hey you. I want to talk to you. I want to talk to you about getting inside my mouth. I think you want to get in here. HEY YOU! I'm talking to you.     You come across a long alley with strange creatures walking around perusing the various shops and stalls. The walls are mainly red brick, and you can see large trees, and huge thorny veins over the walls. There are glass lanterns with metal frames along the wall at various locations providing light. The sky overhead is overcast, with a dark greenish ting. The walls are covered in graffiti and writing, posters, and, signs, and alien ivy plants and moss. They tend to move and change around when your not looking. The majority of the stands are wooden with stripped or solid colored tarpaulin roofs. They resemble an old European farmers market.

    There are also large hobgoblins patrolling around looking for trouble. [Thumpers: Over 7 feet tall, shades of green and yellows in color, big thick fingers, noises, ears. They have fierce underbites with thick teeth shaped like bullets. They look unintelligent, [Most have 1 int, but high notice rolls] and dangerous. They are wearing black slacks, long sleeve white shirt which are rolled up, black suspenders, and no shoes. All of them are carrying a long man catcher slung over one shoulder with a strap, and knobby club.

    Can make a notice roll to spot various red eyed ravens perched high in the market, watching over things.

    Mr. Bigs, Galeaebub #1
    The Hob that started it all. Set up a permanent market over 150 years ago. He Stands at 3'10, and has dark green skin the texture of a frog. He has a large nose and ears. He has angular cheeks and a pointed chin. His has a short brow. He has gold plated round pointed teeth. His eyes are sunken and yellow. He is wearing black pinstripe pants with a gold long sleeve button shirt. He has dark red double breasted vest on over it and a black western style tie. He had black armbands around upper forearm and there is a gold pocket watch in his pocket, and a black handkerchief in another. He is wearing alligator shoes.

    RP: Very efficient, cold, logical, calculating, very matter of fact, no riddles, wants things to run smoothly, keeps order over everything else [even profit, which is second], money is his ally, and business is business, don't hold a grudge, can forgive if beneficial, brutal to those who breaks his rules, don't kill often, prefers to maim troublemakers. (Gotta teach them a lesson) Resides in a Wooden office with many large mirrors instead of windows. [One-way mirrors] Office suspend over the heart of the market. Connects by wooden stairs, rope bridges, and ladders

    Always travels with 2 oversize Hobs named Knuckles and Bruno.

    Market Law
    • No Violence.
    • You Get What’s Advertised.
    • Honor Your Deals.
    • No Refunds.

    Almost all stalls keep a basket with a few goblin fruits in it, and a few Oddments or Gewgaws for bargaining.
    Haggling
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Persuasion + equipment versus Manipulation + Persuasion + equipment
    Action: Extended and contested (3-10+ successes required; each roll represents a couple of rounds or minutes of debate)
    Both Parties roll to meet the difficulty.
    Results:
    • If the winner has only a simple majority of successes, he must concede to half of the competitors demands
    • If the winner has double the competitors successes, the winner must concede to a quarter of the competitors demands.
    • If the winner has three times the competitors successes, no concessions are required.
    Suggested Equipment: Access to the Guide to the Goblin Market (+1), pertinent background information on the market vendor (+1), Goblin market stall (+2), resources to bribe (+3), information to blackmail the opposition (+1 to +5), impressive clothing (+1),
    Possible Penalties: Lack of market etiquette (-1), Offending a merchant (-1 to -3), language barriers (-2 to -5),
