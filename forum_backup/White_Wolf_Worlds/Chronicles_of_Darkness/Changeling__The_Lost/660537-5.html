                        Originally posted by CSSchulz View Post
                        Considering that several kiths give access to 3 point merits, sometimes improved versions of them, and almost always in addition to other benefits, I think it would need to be more than 3 dots, even with a prerequisite. Looking at the Kiths, I wouldn't hesitate to burn 4 or 5 merit points on many of them.
                        A lot of Merits give access to more than their dot values worth of other Merits and advantages. Professional Training and Mystery Cult Initiation are good examples. For 3 dots of PT you get 4 exp worth of value (2 dots of contacts and 2 specialties), plus 9-again on 3 skills. For 3 dots of MCI you get 4 exp worth of value (a specialty or 1-dot Merit, a 1 dot Merit, and a Skill dot or 2-dot Merit). From my observation, the kinds of Merits that give a higher return on their initial exp investment are usually Merits that help develop the character by necessitating background details and/or character ties to justify the purchase. I'd say Dual Kith fits that bill, because if it's still character creation only you'll have to account for how both of those Kiths resulted from your durance, and if it's not, you'll need to build your character's story to explain how they went through another change equivalent to what was done to them by their Keeper.
