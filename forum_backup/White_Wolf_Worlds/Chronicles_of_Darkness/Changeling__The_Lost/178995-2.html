                Unmakers: Elementals of rust, decay, corrosion and time, the Unmakers were tasked with breaking down and destroying anything their Keepers felt they no longer wanted to keep around. Places, things and even people fell under their lethal jurisdiction.

                Blessing - Hand of Destruction: Once per scene, the Unmaker can spend a point of Glamour to turn their touch into a destructive force. The Unmaker has to actually touch something (or someone) by making a successful grapple or an unarmed attack. If they succeed, they inflict an amount of Aggravated damage equal to the number of successes rolled on a living being, or destroy twice as much an object's construction. Hand of Destruction has a disintegration effect when used, causing victims (or at least parts of them) to dissolve into a pile of dust.

                Hand of Destruction has a Dice Pool equal to the Unmaker's Wyrd score, and can be saved against by the victim making a successful Fortitude - the Unmaker's Wyrd role.

                        Woops. Yeah, I was hoping for some feed back on that. There are two things I'm looking for in these threads. 1 - to see ideas others have come up with. And 2 - to hear feedback on those ideas. I thought that limiting its Dice pool to their Wyrd score would be appropriate, since it starts off only doing maybe 1 or 2 aggravated, and they need to increase their Wyrd to improve its damage, at the risk of whatever comes with an elevated Wyrd.



                        Beast

                        Double Blooded: Owlbears, Hippogriffs, Gryphons, the Double Blooded are Lost who share the features of two different Beasts. Their durance was spend in some kind of twisted Fae art gallery, in which their Gentry showed off their more creative side to patrons by twisting a pair of animals together. One night they managed to escape when the museum was closed and the guards fell asleep on watch.

                        Blessing - Hybridized Prowess: When using Contracts of Beasts, the Double Blooded can invoke the powers of two different animal species at the same time. They might turn their hands into the paws of a lion and their mouth into the beak of an eagle, or they may sprout the wings of an owl and gain the strength of a bear. The abilities they can combine are dependent upon how far advanced their training in Contract of Beasts is.



                        Wizened

                        Pigmy: The legendary tiny warriors of the jungle, Pigmy were used to guard their master's domains by hiding in the thick foliage and firing poison laced weapons at intruders. Dragging them back to their villages to be sacrificed. Their escape came by separating themselves from their tribe either because they refused to to become a willing sacrifice, or something else scared them off. Pigmy often take the Dwarf flaw, being very small in stature even in their human forms.

                        Blessing - Pigmy Poisons: The Pigmy are masters at weaponized venom. By spending a point of Glamour, they gain a +2 on Crafts when attempting to brew toxins for the rest of the scene. They can then proceed to dip their weapons such as darts or blades into the venom to prepare them for combat.

                                                        Ephemeral Wanderers: These strange Darklings are enigmas even amongst the other alien creatures of Faerie. What were once people just happened to be walking about one day when all of the sudden one of the Others happened upon them. Perhaps they walked into the path of the Gentry and earned its wrath, or perhaps the Fae in question thought it was "rewarding" an aimless wanderer--whatever their reasons, the Gentry took the hapless mortal to Arcadia where they stripped them of chunks of their flesh and bone and set them alone to wander plains of shadows and ghosts for all time. Some managed, by some miracle, to wander back through the Hedge as one of the Epehemeral Wanderers. They are the Dullahans, the traveling skeletons, and meandering spectres of lore...Each of them seems to be missing pieces of their bodies while the rest glows with strange colors--some seem to be missing their heads, others the smalls of their backs or stomachs, while others still seem to have no flesh at all and move about with only a skeleton. Though technically they still possess their flesh (its just transparent) they are truly a haunting sight to behold.
                                                        Blessing - Ghost walk: Ephemeral Wanderers gain a +2 to navigation rolls, and once per day may spend a point of glamour to enter twilight for a scene.
