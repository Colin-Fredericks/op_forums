    Freehold of the Palisades Setting / Actual Play

    01-01-2015, 05:29 PM
    Inspired by Baron Samedi's excellent Malibu Dream House actual play thread, I've decided that I want to try my hand at Storytelling a Changeling game, starting from the ground up. At the moment I am looking for players in my local game store, and in the meantime I am developing the setting. I welcome all comments on the game, especially as a new Storyteller to the setting. My one small caveat is that I have more or less no problem with "out of time" characters, either as NPCs or as PCs, so long as they don't become overwhelming. It is going to be a settled part of the game that these sorts of Changelings, though rare, do exist. I only bring this up because, from my on-line research, it seems to be a contentious issue!

    If you like anything from the setting or game, please feel free to steal it.

    The game will take place in the Freehold of the Palisades, which covers northern and central New Jersey following this map.



    I've written up a history of the Freehold as a starting point both for myself and my players. Here it is for your perusal:

    Freehold of the Palisades History

    North/Central New Jersey

    Before 1776

    Almost nothing is known of how Changelings in northern and central New Jersey organized themselves before December 26, 1776. On the day after Christmas, George Washington crossed the Delaware river and attacked the Crown's Hessian mercenaries in what would become known as the Battle of Trenton.

    Whatever structure pre-revolutionary Changelings had fell on that day, leaving nothing but a about half a dozen written records, mostly in the form of journal scraps. These journals, mostly containing personal information of little use today, indicate that something like the standard Western European seasonal model prevailed in the area.

    1776-1812

    Following the Battle of Trenton, a Changeling Hessian mercenary known today only as Obrist "Colonel" Weißekopf survived by fleeing into the wilderness. The Continental army never found him, and Weißekop took it upon himself to reconstitute a Freehold for Changelings in the area. Weißekop had served as mercenary previously for some Polish szlachta, and his time spent in Poland saw him abandon the Summer Court of his earlier years for the Sun Court that was especially prevalent in the eastern reaches of the Polish Commonwealth.

    Weißekop sensed an opportunity to spread the diurnal Court system to an area dominated by the seasonal Courts. Changeling historical scholars surmise that Weißekop gathered the remnants of local Summer and Winter courtiers to form his new Sun Court. The founding document of the court, named the Noontime Declaration, was signed and sealed as a pledge between the founders of the new Freehold of the Palisades on Kupala Day (according to Polish custom June 23-24, the pact was signed at noon of the 23rd) 1777, near what would become Fort Lee, New Jersey.Colonel Weißekopf was named King of the Sun and General of the Noontime Pact. Neither the title of General nor that of King stuck to Weißekop, and historical records continue to refer to him as a Colonel. Six other names appeared on the Noontime Declaration; nothing is known about 5 of these Changelings other than their names: Probus, William Gafferty, Smiling Henry, Frederick Carthan, and Paul Plooster. The sixth name (and only woman), Wendy Bath, is mentioned in one of the pre-1776 journal fragments as being the Winter Queen of "East New Jersey." The same journal names her a "damnable Witchtooth," indicating her Kith and likely Seeming.

    The Noontime Declaration makes no provision for a Moon Court to compliment the Sun Court. While this gave the Freehold of the Palisades unprecedented political unity, the mystical symbolism of the day turning into night and vice versa was missing. This symbolism is, amongst other things, used to confuse the Kindly Ones and prevent them from noticing Changelings pledged to a diurnal-Court ruled Freehold. Consequently, tales of loyalists, privateers, and even True Fae troubling the Freehold from this period are significantly higher than in other Freeholds of the same time period. The Palisades would have to wait until the War of 1812 before a proper Moon Court would be established.

    Known Seemings/Kiths of Historical Figures in this Era:

    Colonel Weißekopf- Elemental Airtouched

    Wendy Bath- Ogre Witchtooth

    1812 - 1877

    Shortly before the beginning of the War of 1812, in 1810, Colonel Weißekopf abdicated as King of the Sun. Officialy, the retirement was due to age. The Colonel's inability to control True Fae incursions into the Freehold, coupled with his refusal to allow a Moon Court to "fill out" the magical protections of the diurnal Court system, likely contributed to his ouster. He passed on the title King of the Sun to his successor, who promptly decalred that the title General of the Noontime Pact was unique to Weißekopf and no Changeling in the Freehold could even claim it again. Colonel Weißekopf faded then from the records, mostly, until 1817, when his funeral was recorded as being well-attended.

    Weißekopf's successor was a Changeling known as Gerald Baines, who served in the human world as an Episcopalian vicar in Philipsburg Township (later Philipsburg) in Warren Counry. Baines assumed the title King of the Sun, and already had the title Fae Bishop of Belvidere from Weißekopf, which he kept as his secondary title. During his rule he was mostly known as Bishop Baines or Bishop Gerald.

    Baines was a compromise candidated between the Freehold members that wanted Weißekopf out and those who supported him, as Baines was well regarded as being a paragon of Sun virtues, but relatively uninterested in Freehold politics. As it turns out, while Baines was personally loyal to Weißekopf and his memory, he understood the need to have a Moon Court established in the Freehold. His goal was to do this by finding a politically weak person that could become King of the Moon without much actual power. This would, he hoped, complete the diurnal symbolism for the Wyrd's sake, but without causing all of the political and cultural friction that occured in most diurnal Freeholds.

    Though there were a good number of courtless in the Freehold who had the proper attitude and interestes to be come the Moon monarch, Baines preferred an outsider or newcomer who would be politically weak. The opportunity for this came during the War of 1812, when the British captured and sunk a number of a ships along the New Jersey coast between 1813 and 1814. In addition to the mundane sailors that washed up on the shore, this period saw a large number of new Changeling arrivals from Hedge. The records indicate that one of these refugees, named Zebulon Weeks, declared himself King of the Moon and Envoy of Scorpio in 1817, attracting his first courtiers from many of the courtless involved in criminal activities or otherwise not welcome in the Sun Court.

    There is no record of King Zebulon ever meeting with Bishop Baines, but unlike Weißekopf who drove off or killed anyone attempting to claim royal Lunar authority, no response came from Baines. The Moon Court was finally established. Within 7 years, the amount of troubles coming from the Hedge and Arcadia fell off significantly. Whether through coincidence or secret compact, King Zebulon troubled the Sun Court and its subjects only just enough to keep up appearances, and tried to make his courtiers do the same. This created a lot of grumbling in the Moon Court, but Weeks managed to keep it under control until 1877. In that year, a bloody coup saw King Zebulon overthrown and replaced by Bogumil Sobczak in an event known as the Setting of the Scorpion. Many of Zebulon's supporters were killed and the King himself was forced to suddenly flee into the west (never to be seen again).

    Sobczak was an immigrant from eastern Poland, a pagan, and use to a much more aggressive stance from the Moon Court toward their sunny brethren. Much to Sobczak's delight, Bishop Baines and the Sun Court were shocked and disgusted by the coup.

    In the latter part of this period, the American Civil War occured. While the war certainly affected the personal lives of many Changelings, it did not have a large political impact on the Freehold because all of the war was fought in the South. In fact, the period right after the Civil War was a bit of a windfall of Glamour for the Sun Court, since many, many soldiers returned, their souls burdened with shame and guilt over things they had done during the fighting.

    Known Seemings/Kiths of Historical Figures in this Era:

    Bishop Gerald Baines- Beast Truefriend

    King Zebulon Weeks- Dual Kith Beast Venombite/Truefriend.

    1877 - the Great War

    Sobczak assumed the title of King of the Moon and created for himself the secondary title of Supreme Volhv of Chernobog. He was mostly known as King Bogumil. King Bogumil greatly escalated the cold war between Sun and Moon to mirror what he was accustomed to in Poland. This put Baines and the Sun Court on their collective back foot, as they were used to the relatively quiescent Moon Court under Weeks.

    The sudden degradation in relations between the two courts upset the humors of the already elderly Bishop Baines, and he took ill 7 years after Sobczak's rise to power. Unable to get out of bed most days, a regent was appointed to govern in his stead. This regent took the form of Reeve Vauxhall, Baines's secretary and a Magistrate of the Wax Mask. Regent Vauxhall was unpopular with the Sun courtiers, first of all because his Manikin features combined with the melting wax face granted by his Entitlement created a highly disturbing visage, and secondarily because of his brusque and unfriendly manner. He was tolerated, however, because he enjoyed the direct confidence of Bishop Baines (who was well loved) and his refusal to take any gruff from King Bogumil and his court of grotesque horrors.

    The conflicts between the Sun and Moon courts during this period are too numerous to list. Bishop Baines finally expired the day before Kupala Day (June 22nd) in 1900. Regent Vauxhall's long period of rule had left the court with no clear successor, so Vauxhall continued as regent without claiming royal perogatives. This proved to be mystically disatrous, because without a royal figure, the Sun Court did not technically exist and the protections of the Wyrd on the Freehold frayed.

    During the Edwardian era and through the beginnings of World War I, True Fae interest in the Freehold steadily increased. Vauxhall, in order to preserve his power, refused to name a new Monarch, but also refused to claim royal authority himself so as not to lose face amongst his Entitlement fellows, who valued service as opposed to outright rulership. Everything came to a head in 1917, when Regent Vauxhall's old Keeper brazenly emerged from the Hedge in his full glory, and dragged the poor old Manikin back into Arcadia kicking and screaming, all in very public view.

    Thankfully the grumpy, power hungry Manikin left a will naming Robert "Bob" Duncanson as his successor. Duncanson had served as Knight in the Freehold and Fetch-killer for a long time, and this was accepted with a minimum of grumbling. Duncanson assumed the title of King of the Sun, and kept as his secondary title Knight of Sun's Fire, granted to him some years before by Vauxhall acting on behalf of Bishop Baines (somewhat ironic, given that he was not a fire-aspected Elemental). Following the Freehold's tradition amongst the Sun monarchs of being mostly known by secondary titles, Duncanson was usually referred to simply as "Sir Robert."

    Known Seemings/Kiths of Historical Figures in this Era:

    King Bogumil Sobczak- Darkling Palewraith

    Regent Reeve Vauxhall- Elemental Manikin, Magistrate of the Wax Mask Entitlement

    Sir Robert Duncanson- Elemental Earthbones

    1918-1950

    Sir Robert and King Bogumil continued to clash, though not with the intensity they formerly had, during this era. For Sir Robert, it was monstly a matter of age- he had come to the throne already in late middle age. For Sobczak, the death (for all intents and purposes) of his old nemesis Vauxhall seemed to steal the mischevious fire for causing disgust out from under him. The King of the Moon, too, was feeling his age.

    In the late 1940s, after World War II, the current Moon Monarch moved into the area. Supported by the foreign resources of the Phantom Tong, Secret Suzan quickly established herself as a power in the area. Originally courtless, she swore fealty to the Moon Court shortly before the death of Sobczak in 1949. King Bogumil's chosen successor, Tomasz Niezychowski, abdicated less than a year later in favor of Secret Suzan. To this day no one knows exactly what dirt Secret Suzan had on Niezychowski that could cause a Moon courtier enough embarrassment to give up a royal title, but the former King of the Moon returned to his native Poland in 1957 and contact was lost with him. Suzan became the "Dual Queen", taking the traditional title Queen of the Moon and creating for herself the title Queen of Eclipses.

    Suzan was much more interested in causing disgust in the righteous by stimulating people's vices, and a grand expansion of Moon Court involvement in prostitution, human trafficking, and the nascent drug trade occurred. Sobczak and his ilk prefered to shock and disgust people with their overt displays of paganism and blasphemy (of Christianity only, of course. They were deadly serious about their own faith in Chernobog and Veles). Suzan was nothing if not magnanimous, however, and Sobczak's inner circle eventually came around because she left them to their own devices.

    Known Seemings/Kiths of Historical Figures in this Era:

    Queen Suzan- Fairest Shadowsoul, Phantom Tong Entitlement

    King Tomasz Niezychowski- Wizened Miner

    1950-1997

    In the early 1960s, Sir Robert's age began to show- he became slightly senile, and concerned about putting his earthly affairs in order. Perhaps providentially, a very unusal Changeling emerged from the hedge. Maurikios, claiming to be priest abducted from Constantinople in the early 1400s, entered the Freehold on August 27th in Elizabeth, New Jersey in 1962. Though his claimes of being of great age were not (and cannot be) verified, the ancient priest was completely mistified by modern technology and was almost immediately killed by cars (which he thought were some kind of fae demons come to reclaim him for his keeper).

    Thankfully Maurikios was discovered by local Blackbird Bishops loyal to the Sun Court, which allowed him to survive. Maurikios's skill at spiritual direction (and the Omen contract) soon earned him a place in Sir Robert's inner circle; the old Episcopalian even converted to Greek Orthodoxy after long discussions with the elderly priest from another age.

    When Sir Robert died in 1970, he indicated that his beloved confessor and spiritual guide, Maurikios, was to take up the reigns of the Sun Court. Maurikios assumed the title King of the Sun, and became the Metropolitan of All Fae. Many outside of Sir Robert's immediate circle of friends were suspicious of Maurikios as some kind of fraud, even a mole put in place by Secret Suzan or one of the True Fae, but their fears were unfounded. By the early 80s, most everyone came to love their anachronistic old Metropolitan and his medieval sensibilites, not in the least because he was very willing to meet with and council all Changelings in the area, even Moon courtiers.

    Things were not to last, however. As time passed, Maurikios's Byzantine (literally) view of morality began to clash with many younger memebers of the Sun Court. These younger courtiers were raised on the more liberal morality of late 20th century America, and began to feel that it was Maurikios and his supporters who should feel shame for their antiquated and "bigoted" opinions- not alleged sinners.

    A faction began to grow in the Sun Court known derisively, but then formally, as the Modernist Sun. The faction started out small, but began to grow over the years. The Modernists also had far more contacts with the human world, and were able to make use of the growing power of anti-discrimnation laws, powerful identity interest groups, and other facets of contemporary politics to force their ways on Maurikios's faction (which became known, eventually, as the Orthodox Sun).

    Things came to a head in 1997, when the unofficial leader of the Modernist Sun, a Changeling named Chris Grand, publicaly challenged Maurikios for the title of King of the Sun on Kupala Day. Maurikios refused, calling Grand a unrepentant sodomite, and a man who makes virtues out of vices and vices out of virtues. The supporters of each candidate quickly began hurling accusations and invective at each other, attempting to shame the other group away from power. The event even turned into a small riot that had to be broken up by police, something which had to be assiduously covered up by Secret Suzan in order to protect the hidden existence of the fae. This event became known as the Great Schism, and it precipitated a brief civil war in the Sun Court.

    Known Seemings/Kiths of Historical Figures in this Era:

    Metropolitan Maurikios- Wizened Author

    Chris Grand- Ogre Cyclopean

    1997- Present

    The Great Schism provoked violence between the two factions of the Sun Court. In addition, the dispute over who was King of the Sun caused the title to, in the eyes of the Wyrd, be defunct. Without the balance of Sun and Moon, the eyes of the Gentry once again began to become focused on the Freehold of the Palisades.

    The first person to realize this was Secret Suzan. Using her extensive network of spies and criminals, she quickly brokered a truce between the two factions of the Sun Court that exists to this day. Each year, on Kupala Day, power would pass between Chris Grand and Maurikios. When he is in power, Maurikios would be known as King of the Sun and Metropolitan of All Fae. When Chris Grand is in power, he would be known as King of the Sun and First Citizen of the Freehold. In years when they were not power, the other would relinquish all titles, and become a commoner.

    This truce in the Great Schism remains in force today, with a careful balance of power between Maurikios and Grand, enforced by Secret Suzan (secretly, of course). Power is due to pass from Maurikios to Grand on Kupala Day (in the Freehold, June 23-24) of 2015.

              The Court of the Sun

              Foundation Date: June 23-34, 1777

              Rulership over the Freehold: Variable; from Sun-up to Sun-down every day.

              Ruler: The King of the Sun, a title held either by Maurikios, or Christopher Grand, and switiching between the two anually on Kupala Day.

              Common Practices

              Lucky Sevens: Both the Moon Court and the Sun Court consider the number 7 to be sacred to the whole Freehold. The seven founders of the Freehold in 1777 likely contributed to this, but important things tend to happen in the Freehold in years including the number 7, or on days and years that are a multiple of the number seven. Rare is the Changeling, even a courtless, who does not have some king of stylized representation of the number 7 stashed somewhere in his house.

              Kupala Night (& Day): The Sun Court in the Freehold of the Palisades observes this day according to Polish custom, on the 23-24 of June. The traditional activities of this day have been deformed somewhat by the Great Schism truce, which stipulates that power is to pass from the leader of the Modernist faction to the leader of the Orthodox faction on this day, and back again the next year. Therefore the focus of the day is on the coronation of the new King of the Sun, though the coronation ceremony is usually followed by traditional Kupala day activities. Especially in the case of years that the Orthodox faction takes power, the coronation is followed by a large number of baptisms.

              Bishop Baines Memorial: On July 7th, his birthday, the Sun Court commemorates the memory of the most beloved Sun monarch to reign in the Freehold, Bishop Gerald Baines. Baines is something of a combination between a folk hero and a saint for Sun courtiers, in large part because a painting of his Mask survives from his time as vicar in the little Episcopalain parish of Philipsburg. It is considered gauche for Sun Courtiers to not have a copy or print of this portrait somewhere in their possession, either hanging in their house or in a small miniature kept in a medallion locket. More religious Sun courtiers will typically attend or have a religious ritual said in his honor on this day. More secular types will simply raise a glass in his memory at dinner. It is traditional for Sun Courtiers of sufficient means to give gifts of sherry, the Bishop's beverage of choice, on this day to any Changeling who may ask for a bottle. Needless to say, a large number of Moon Court drunks love this holiday too.

              Factions

              The Sun court is split by a very bitter divide between the Modernist and Orthodox factions. Typically, people of a more secular bent tend to align with the Modernist faction, while Christians tend to align with the Orthodox faction, though this is not an iron rule. People of neither ideological persuasion tend to fall into the orbit of one of the other faction through personal connections and experiences. For example, a Muslim Sun courtier who is on the recieving end of one of Maurikios's legendary rants against the perfidy of "Mohammedans" might be pushed into alignment with the Modernist faction, while another who thinks women ought to remain covered out of modesty might be driven into the Orthodox faction when Modernist Sun courtiers send his boss screenshots of his "misogynistic" message board postings, and he finds himself out of a job.

              Generally speaking, the Modernist faction is numerically superior, wealthier in human money, and is more well connected to local human power structures, especially the New Jersey state government, media, and academia.

              The Orthodox faction tends to have more experienced (though not necessarily older) Changelings who have a deeper understanding of the Wyrd, more Contracts with arcane forces, and even contacts with other supernatural creatures. The Orthodox faction boasts the great majority of Changelings with an Entitlement in the Sun Court.

              As is common with the Sun Court, both sides favor high Clarity and it is not uncommon for Sun Courtiers to maintain Clarity at 7 or even 8 dots (though of course they would not describe it this way).

              The King of the Sun

              Real Name: Maurikios

              Name used amongst humans: Fr. Maurikios "Maurice" Lenardos

              Name used amongst Changelings: Maurikios

              Titles: In years when he is reigning over the Sun Court, Maurikios claims the titles King of the Sun and Metropolitan of All Fae. In years he is not reigning, he loses all titles and becomes a commoner

              Theme Song: Mendelssohn's "Yet Doth the Lord", the fifth movement of the Elijah oratorio: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UzudEVr6gM8

              Mask: Maurikios appears as a Greek Orthodox priest in his mid-late 70's. He has a full flowing white beard, a black robe and kalimavkion with a veil. He has a slightly bent posture and moves slowly, requiring a cane to walk. Though he really is a hieromonk, he is not really a bishop. However, when acting as Metropolitan of All Fae and King of the Sun, Maurikios will wear clothing and vestments appropriate to that station on ceremonial occasions.

              Mien: To be honest, Maurikios's mein is not all that different from his Mask. His stoop becomes even more pronounced. The lines and wrinkles in his face become even deeper. His thick hair and beard fade away to just a few diaphonous strands. His fingers grow longer and become stained with ink.

              Early Life: Maurikios began life as the son of simple cobbler in Constantinople. As a young boy he was considered naturally bright, and he was taught to read and write by his local parish priest. The boy's attentive devotion to sacred things during the Divine liturgy, as well as his excellent penmanship, saw him enter the Chora monastery at the relatively young age of 15.

              Maurikios became known, if not as an original thinker, as very well versed in theology, an excellent confessor and spiritual director, and as an illuminator and transcriber of both sacred texts and mundane philosophical works. His life would have happily passed in quiet prayer and contemplation of Divine things in the Chora if not for one small fact: the Byzantine Empire was tottering and the Emperors were rapidly losing their ability to defend Constantinople.

              The end came in 1453, when the Ottoman Turks sacked, then occupied, the shining jewel of Eastern Christendom. In the chaos and confusion of the sack, Fr. Maurikios tried to flee, running through the winding, narrow streets of the city. Then she appeared to him, a holy vision of the Mother of God. Beckoning him into a dark alleyway, promising hope of salvation from the Mohammedan hordes for her devoted monk, Maurikios could do little but obey her summons. It was a choice he came to regret.

              Durance: The vision was not the Virgin Mary, but something altogether more sinister. A terrible True Fae calling herself the False Theotokos, as soon as Maurikios entered her Rancid Cloister, she threw him into chains as her slave.

              The Kindly One took the form of a pregnant Madonna, but when she was ready to "give birth", her stomach split wide open to reveal a horrible grey womb that disgorged terrible creatures. Sometimes the creatures were fetches, sometimes Hobs, sometimes they were even young True Fae. Other times the False Theotokos opened her womb to stuff a human captive inside, only to disgorge him at a later date as a horribly deformed Changeling.

              Thankfully Maurikios managed to avoid this dire fate, but he was changed all the same. The False Theotokos's abiding love was for blasphemy, which she assiduously wished to spread in the mortal world, and her true hate was for Christ, whose reign in the world she assiduously desired to end. She commanded Maurikios to copy the sacred texts and works of philosophy in order to give them to her profane children, that they might be better prepared to go out into the world to spread error. Eventually she had Maurikios not only copy these texts, but tutor and debate with her foul progeny in order to hone their skill in attacking and denuding sacred things.

              Maurikios could not resist. If he refused to write, he would be whipped, then starved for days. Eventually the False Theotokos would just snap her fingers, and he would write anyway under magical compulsion. He tried to remain silent when forced to give lessons on sacred things to decidedly devilish creatures, but he would receive the same treatment, and then forced by magic to debate anyway. Maurikios resisted bravely for the first decade. After that... he put his faith in God to deliver him, and just did as the False Theotokos bid him.

              Maurikios could not say how long his Durance truly was. Sometimes, it felt like 100 years, sometimes 1000, sometimes just a few months. Maurikios does not know what he said or did, but he does know that he convinced the False Theotokos that she did not need an old monk like him any more. The Kindly One agreed, and had one of her Ogre servants/children grab Maurikios by the leg and heave him out of the Rancid Cloister. Maurikios remembers falling, being hit repeatedly by branches and torn by thorns on the way down, but nothing more specific about the journey. He tumbled out of the Hedge in Elizabeth, NJ, in the early 1960s.

              Changeling Life: Maurikios was dazed and confused, at first, by the modern world. Most immediately, he was struck with amazement that there was another continent, another two continents, out beyond Gibraltar, and humans had reached them not long after he had been taken.

              He couldn't understand how he had found his way to this time and place so distant from his own, but he finally understood a few days after he emerged from the Hedge.                                   The Court of the Moon

                                  Foundation Date: December 24, 1817 (Winter Solstice/Korochun)

                                  Rulership over the Freehold: Variable; from Sun-down to Sun-up every day.

                                  Common Practices

                                  Lucky Sevens: Both the Moon Court and the Sun Court consider the number 7 to be sacred to the whole Freehold. The seven founders of the Freehold in 1777 likely contributed to this, but important things tend to happen in the Freehold in years including the number 7, or on days and years that are a multiple of the number seven. Rare is the Changeling, even a courtless, who does not have some king of stylized representation of the number 7 stashed somewhere in his house.

                                  Korochun: This variable feast takes place on the Winter Solstice every year. The night is given over mostly to revelry and the playing of pranks on Sun Courtiers (usually of a harmless variety). Chernobogists (see below) take part in the revelry, of course, but this is also a sacred day for them. Rumors circulate every year that they take part in "pranks" of a significantly darker nature than other Moon Courtiers on this night in order to please their god.

                                  Washington's Birthday: Many of the original founders of the Sun Court had loyalist (of the British, not the Fae, kind) sympathies during the American Revolution. When the Moon Court was founded, Washington's Birthday (February 22nd) was semi-officially adopted as Korochun 2.0 for the purpose of annoying and disgusting Sun Court members. As time passed and the original political meaning of the holiday wore away, the holiday lost this flavor and instead became a general revelry/thanksgiving celebration for Moon courtiers. With the advent of "Secret" Suzan's reign and her connections to the criminal world, it became common for Moon Courtiers to use this day for vulgar displays of wealth, especially if the wealth was gotten via illicit means. It should be noted that Changelings in the Freehold always celebrate Washington's Birthday on his actual birthday, February 22nd, not on the official day set by the Federal government.

                                  Factions

                                  The Moon Court's internal factions do not hold even a tenth as much rancor for each other as the Sun Court's internal factions do. That said, relations are not always rosy.

                                  Bogumilists/Chernobogists: This faction consists of people who were closely tied to the reigns of Bogumil Sobczak and Tomasz Niezychowski The "outer party", so to speak, primarily consists of people of Eastern European extraction, and are known as Bogumilists. These people tend to have strong connections to the old country or are recent immigrants- a person of Lithuanian descent whose family has been in the United States for 5 generations and who is fully assimilated into American culture is far more likely to be unaligned than to be a Bogumilist. Bogumilists tend to be Poles above all else, and this sometimes leads to Russians, in particular, not being welcome in the faction (though some sneak through anyway). After unaligned Moon Courtiers, Bogumilists are probably the largest faction.

                                  Chernobogists are the "inner party" of the Bogumilist faction. They are almost exclusively Poles and they make their dislike of Russians (and Germans, should they find any), fairly plain. Most importantly, Chernobogists are neo-pagans who follow an ersatz religion cooked up by old King Bogumil based on ancient Slavic mythology and, if they are to be believed, ancient truths he learned during his Durance. They take Chernobog to be their patron deity with a lesser emphasis on Veles as a secondary god. Chernobogism is not something you will find on the shelves of the New Age rack of the local chain bookstore; it is made of decidedly darker, sterner stuff. Consequently, Chernobogists tend to live on the fringes of human society, and many actually dwell in the Hedge itself. Without exception they are experienced occultists and tend to be deep into contracts with darker things (Darkness, Moon, and Lucidity being favorites amongst them).

                                  Chernobogists & Bogumilists made their peace with Queen Suzan long ago and do not actively oppose her reign, but nor do they particularly care for her meddling in their affairs. Suzan has thus far kept up the appearance of leaving them to their own devices (which in truth, she mostly does), but she is a master of deception and it is unlikely they would ever notice if she did decide to interfere.

                                  Phantom Tong: This factions ties itself most closely to "Secret" Suzan. The name of the faction derives from Queen Suzan and her inner circle, who are all actually members of the Phantom Tong Entitlement. Most of the people who would be considered part of the faction are merely aspiring to the Entitlement (at best), or are poseurs (and sometimes sycophants) at worst. With few exceptions Phantom Tong faction members are involved in criminal activities, usually centered around what Police departments used to call "vice"- drug production, shipment, and sales, and prostitution/human trafficking. Some of the less successful Changelings considered members are simply petty thieves or muggers, while some of the more successful have never seen a dark alley or whorehouse because they are million dollar embezzlers or tax cheats. The Phantom Tong is probably the third largest faction, though it is by far the wealthiest.

                                  Scorpion Legitimists: The smallest faction (there are perhaps 12-14 Changelings considered members), Scorpion Legitimists are Moon courtiers who believe that all of the Moon Monarchs since Zebulon Weeks have been illegitimate, because Weeks was overthrown in a coup. Obviously, these events happened long ago and most Moon courtiers consider them hardly relevant today, however, Scorpion Legitimists have always been assiduous about "adopting" a protégé and passing on their political beliefs. Scorpion Legitimists tend to be eccentric, even by Moon Courtier standards, though in relatively harmless ways (for example, one is an enormous proponent of the crackpot Time Cube theory). The faction is not to be trifled with, however, because its members tend to inherit the fortunes of their mentors, including mundane money, large and well protected Hollows, and powerful (4-5 dot) tokens. Despite their seemingly harmless eccentricity, Scorpion Legitimists are often personally powerful as well- they refuse to pledge fealty to the Freehold's Moon monarch, and instead undertake the arduous quests necessary to make individual pacts with the Moon itself. Scorpion Legitimists enjoy better relations with the Sun Court than any other faction of Moon Courtiers, and a number of the more powerful ones are on friendly, speaking terms with Metropolitan Maurikios who has a great deal of sympathy for their cause.

                                  Unaligned: A simple majority of Moon courtiers in the Freehold do not have strong ties to any particular political faction. They generally are loyal to Queen Suzan but do not make a great show of it, nor do they attempt to emulate her like the Phantom Tong aspirants do. Otherwise, it is very hard to categorize them.

The One Who Whispers from the Outside made some changes to Susan; he covered her in the same darkness that was his own substance, and he stole away a little bit of her real name, giving her one that was just slightly off.

So he stole little girls and turned them into his wives. Suzan was one of these. He never spoke... except when he lay down with his wives at night. Oh, he never touched the girls, other than to sidle up against them in the perpetual darkness of his realm. He would whisper to them though, his bat-fanged mouth full of darkness hovering over their ears. He told them secrets, dark things that even he could not bear to know alone. Things about the true nature of the Wyrd, about what was really out there in the night sky.

                                              The One Who Whispers from the Outside was not devoid of compassion, however. He knew that the burdens he had to share with his dear little wives were taxing. He made each one a promise; they would listen to his secrets for seven years, and then if they wanted, he would let them go back to the real world. Suzan suffered in silence for those 7 years, but when the time came for her "marriage" to end, she jumped at the chance. The One Who Whispers from the Outside spent one more night with his beloved wife, tenderly whispering his darkest knowledge to her on their final night together, and in the morning she awoke in an unoccupied bedroom of a Hong Kong whorehouse.

                                              Changeling Life: Suzan was 17 and half mad when she returned to the human world. It was fortuitous, then, that the whorehouse she woke up in was owned and operated by the Dai Lo Dai of the Hong Kong Phantom Tong. Jishen Wu took Suzan under his wing, rebuilding her broken psyche over the course of the next year, into something usable, something slightly criminal. Suzan tried to look for her family during this year, and for any sign of a Fetch, but she couldn't find them. Jishen mentioned that shortly after she had been taken, the Japanese had occupied the city and it was possible her family had been killed. He'd never heard of anyone named Caixia.

                                              In 1948, Suzan was ready to serve as an instrument of the Tong. With the skills in criminal undertakings she had learned at the foot of the Tong's leaders, combined with the mystical knowledge she had learned during her Durance, Jishen knew she would be able to spread the destabilizing mission of the Tong beyond the borders of East Asia. Flush with cash to set up her own operations, Suzan was loaded onto a plane and sent to Port Newark with standing orders to set up the Tong on the eastern seaboard of the United States.

                                              For a year, Suzan did as she was told, still somewhat dazed by how quickly her life was advancing. With King Bogumil on his deathbed and her power in the underworld firmly established, however, something clicked in Suzan's mind. She didn't need to be anyone's lackey or concubine any more. Putting together her own personal talent at deception with her network of criminal informers, she dug up some deeply buried dirt on Tomasz Niezychowski, King Bogumil's likely successor (something having to do with being a secret informant for the Nazis in occupied Poland). She blackmailed him out of royal office and seized the throne for herself at the tender age of 20, all without the approval of her Phantom Tong "superiors" back in Hong Kong.

                                              From that day forward she has ruled over the Moon Court of the Freehold of the Palisades with an equal combination of deception, black magic, and criminal wealth. With the Great Schism of the Sun Court in the late 1990s, Secret Suzan was the first to realize that without a King of the Sun, the Freehold was in danger from the True Fae. She brokered the Truce and sees to enforcing it when it looks like one side (usually the Modernists) is going to push out the other faction. This has the added benefit of keeping the Freehold fairly destabilized. In truth, however, Suzan secretly prefers the Orthodox Sun to the Modernist. Suzan's criminal rackets all rely on people with traditional notions of morality being disgusted by her and her supporter's activities. If people think that taking hard drugs is a simple lifestyle choice, or prostitution is sanitized as "sex work," almost the entire Moon Court in the Freehold will have to change its paradigm on Glamour collection. It would be destabilizing for her own personal rule, and that is one form of instability Suzan is not willing to see come to pass.

                                              Suzan's open rulership of the Freehold did not go over well with her Phantom Tong elders back in Hong Kong. In the mid 1980's her reduction of standing with them forced her to relinquish the title of Dai Lo Dai of the Freehold to another. These so-called Dai Lo Dais tend to not last very long if they openly antagonize her. Thankfully the current holder of the title, Salacious L, is her viceroy and a member of her inner circle.
                                              .
                                              Personality & Motivations: After her Durance and experiences with the Phantom Tong, whatever was left of the innocent little Susan Goldner is long gone. Secret Suzan is a hard woman, and she does not suffer fools gladly. There is a terrible wildness lurking just behind her eyes, and though she has never been known to indulge it, its very threat keeps many people in line. As she has advanced in years and in power, she has developed many enemies. It is a well known fact that, in order to protect herself, she has mostly withdrawn into her Hollow, and only interacts with her trusted seconds. What is not quite so well known is that her mastery of various contracts makes her almost impossible to recognize or find if she does not want to be found. Many Changelings, both Moon and Sun, would be surprised how much Suzan gets around while disguised.

                                              Suzan tries to maintain as careful balance between keeping the Freehold "unstable" as is required by her Phantom Tong ideology, and making sure that her own reign is secure. The key word there is "tries." In reality, Suzan clearly favors her own power and position over loyalty to any ideology. She hasn't been drummed out of the Phantom Tong yet (in no little part because there are plenty of Dai Los just like her), but she is not popular with Tong "purists."

                                              Notes: Suzan's Hollow requires its own explanation. The Hollow is called the Villa Claustrophobia. It was given to her by Jishen Wu when he inserted her into the Freehold; she does not know how he learned of it or where it came from (a secret that she is very frustrated she has never been able to learn). The interior of the Hollow appears as an early 20th century mansion, with some simple electricity and refrigeration, running hot and cold water, and even a couple of rotary phones that somehow connect into the real phone network.

                                              The Villa has no exterior- about two inches away from every window is a wall of hard, gray, cracked concrete. The only openings that do not lead to this wall of concrete are the Hollow's three gates. The first gate, which opens into the Villa's grand foyer, is a disused entrance to a boiler room located on the outside of a seedy strip club in Perth Amboy. The door is around the corner from the front of the strip joint in a trash-strewn alley whose only occupants are rats and the occasional drunk taking a piss. This gate cannot be accessed at all while the sun is up. While the sun is set, anyone who carries an amethyst (of any size, even a small shard will do) on his person can pass through to the Villa Claustrophobia. The second gate is located in the kitchen of the Villa, and from the inside of the Hollow takes the form of the midden door. This gate opens into a small cave next to a stream in Stokes Forest. It is one-way only and is primarily used by the Villa's staff to get rid of trash, though people can exit from this gate as well. The final gate is known only to Secret Suzan; it is a hidden escape tunnel that is located in her sleeping chambers. This gate leads to a storage closet in the old graduate school building of Princeton University. To enter the Villa through this gate, the user must recite Job 2: 9-10 (in any real language) before opening the door and stepping through.

                                              The Villa Claustrophobia has a number of minor magical conveniences (like electricity, water, and phone service, even though it should be impossible). The larders and refrigerators of the Hollow restock themselves with fresh food at sundown on Friday, every week. Despite being completely enclosed in concrete, the Villa never runs out of air (though there is a pervasive smell of stale air). The Villa is served by a troupe of 14 anthropomorphic mice (they're all about 3 feet tall) that clean, cook, and wait on Secret Suzan and her guests. In addition to this, th Villas has two major magical qualities.

                                              The Throne of Velvet Power: The Villa contains a throne room with a grand oaken throne cushioned with black crushed velvet. Anyone who posses a Mantle rating from the Autumn, Winter, Moon, or East courts that is sitting on the throne gains the Rote quality on all Manipulation rolls to an audience that shares his mantle. If the audience is people with different mantles, no mantles, or a mixed audience, the bonus on the Manipulation roll is simply a 9-again roll. The Throne cannot be removed from its position in the throne room by any known power, but it is otherwise a normal chair, and can be destroyed or harmed. Anything that does serious harm to the chair destroys its magic forever.

                                              The Mirror of Objectification: This ornate mirror hangs in its own special room. The mirror can produce a scrying sensor in the real world anywhere within the New York metropolitan area. To place a sensor, the user must speak a location (and only a location, telling the mirror to scry on a person always fails) and make a Manipulation + Occult roll. The scrying sensor will last for 5 minutes per success on the roll. After the scrying has finished, the mirror must recharge for 1 hour per success rolled on the Manipulation+Occult roll. The scrying sensor can be moved by the Mirror's user; it can move anywhere in the designated location, and up to 15 yards away from the boundary or exterior of the location. The user simply tells the mirror where to move the sensor, ie. "Turn left 90 degrees and move six feet". The sensor has the same visual acumen as a human with 20/20 vision, moves at an average human walking pace, and can be told to zoom in in order to read text or get closeups.

                                              The Mirror can only see objects and plants, not any kind of animal, human, or supernatural being. Anything worn or closely held by such beings vanishes as well. The mirror emits audio as well, but it cannot hear anything it does not have the ability to see. It can, however, hear recordings of such beings.

                                              There is one exception to these rules: the Mirror can see and hear Prometheans and other creatures that use Pyros or Azoth just fine. Prometheans appear in their human disguise, but if a exceptional success is rolled on the Mirror's Manipulation+Occult roll, their Disfigurement is revealed as well. Secret Suzan, in using the mirror, has glimpse a Promethean one or two times (though she never saw their Disfigurements). Though her skill in Occult is high enough to be vaguely aware that Prometheans exist, she has no idea that that's what her magic mirror saw.

                                              Much like the Throne of Velvet Power, the Mirror cannot be removed by any known force from the wall on which it hangs. It is otherwise, however, exactly like a normal mirror, and if shattered or otherwise seriously harmed, its magic is gone forever.

                                                  The Viceroy of Eclipses

                                                  Real Name: Kevin Lai

                                                  Name used amongst humans: Fu Look, officially, but goes by his nickname Salacious L to most people.

                                                  Name used amongst Changelings: Salacious L, some Changelings also know his "real" name Fu Look.

                                                  Titles: Viceroy of Eclipses, Commandant of Fort Lee, Dai Lo Dai of the Phantom Tong

                                                  Theme Song: Pebbles - Mercedes Boy https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXZAm2NlMEE

                                                  Mask: Salacious L is a Chinese man in his late 20's or early 30's, but more importantly, he is probably one of the most physically attractive men you have ever seen. Everything is perfectly symmetrical. He's in excellent shape but isn't outrageously muscular. His lustrous black hair is never out of place, there's a mischievous twinkle in his eye, and an arrogant-but-irresistible smirk on his lips. Salacious L is a pimp, a very successful one, and dresses in expensive, slightly outlandish suits and gold jewelery that reflect this. Underneath all of this he has a series of very well done tattoos that would mark him as a member of a Hong Kong-based triad, but the symbols refer to no group that any actual gang member would know.

                                                  Mien: In his true form, Salacious L's skin becomes a dark, navy-blue color. His tattoos vanish and are replaced with what look like tiny diamonds embedded in his skin, that catch the light and sparkle like tiny stars. If the moonlight catches his eyes in just the right way, they appear clouded over or even translucent, but just for a moment.

                                                  Early Life: Kevin Lai was born in Manhattan's Chinatown, and grew up just across the river in Englewood Cliffs, New Jersey. His parents were from Hong Kong, originally, but had immigrated to the United States seeking better economic opportunities in the mid 1970s. His father was an electrical engineer and his mother a home-maker; both were active in their local Chinese Methodist church.

                                                  Kevin Lai was not much to look at as a young man- slightly pudgy, wan skin. His parents kept him away from high-status activities in favor of more studies, music lessons (that he was terrible at), and church groups in the hopes that he would eventually become a doctor, lawyer, or engineer. He came to resent the beautiful people who were so obviously inferior to himself, but who always got what he wanted. There was little he could do about it though. When he was 15, in 1997, his parents sent him to a Methodist sleep away camp in upstate New York for the summer. It was there that he met Amy Chan, and it was there that she would reject his clumsy teenage advances. Embarrassed beyond all knowing, he ran out into the woods behind the dormitories.

                                                  Salacious L doesn't exactly remember what happened, because he was bawling and his eyes were filled with tears, but he knows he was hit over the head with something heavy and blunt.

                                                  Durance: The Recombiner was not like the Kindly Ones that other people talk about. It wasn't a person... it was more like a place. A collection of cartoonish Rube Goldberg machines that none-the-less did terrible things. It would continually process and re-process Changelings, taking them apart and putting them back together in the most painful way possible. Kevin Lai was subject to this mad treatment for five years. Each day he would go into the machine, and after being poked, prodded, pulled, sliced, and pinched, he'd pop out the other end completely different. One day he was Gristlegrinder. The next a Manikin. The next a Leechfinger/Bright One. The Recombiner didn't just change his Seeming or Kith though. Sometimes he'd come out as a tall blond-haired, blue eyed German, others as a drooling hunchback pygmy. Once or twice he came out as a woman.

                                                  Then, one day, he emerged from the output end of the Recombiner and looked in the mirrors the surrounded the holding pens where the Recombiner's "inputs" were stored between sessions. And he saw that he was beautiful... exactly as he imagined himself being in his dreams. At that moment, one of the mirrors silently slid aside, revealing a long straight tunnel that went so far he couldn't see the end. Kevin didn't need any more encouragement and took off running. It wasn't long before he found himself back in his home town, Englewood Cliffs, emerging from a toilet stall in the public park.

                                                  Changeling Life: Kevin's first order of business was to kill his Fetch. He didn't want to be reminded of what he had been, or what he suspected he would become. He was right- the fetch was a boring, fat, ugly, sexually frustrated Computer Sciences graduate student. The Recombiner had left Kevin colder inside, and he had no problem planning or carrying out his Fetch's murder. He even watched the funeral from afar, to see his parents one last time as they grieved over their poor boy, shot during a drive-by intended for gangsters but gone horribly awry. They wouldn't even recognize Kevin now, he was so different, so he decided that would be the last he saw of them.

                                                  It was the first he would see of Secret Suzan, however. She approached Kevin after his Fetch's death, impressed at how easily he had orchestrated it. She said that she was having a bit of trouble with one of her rivals- the Dai Lo Dai of the Phantom Tong. It seemed that the Changeling was put there by the Entitlement's elders in Hong Kong, and he and Suzan didn't see eye to eye. If Kevin could eliminate the rival for her, the Queen of the Moon would reward him handsomely.

                                                  It took a while, but Lai managed to arrange for the Dai Lo Dai's death, completely free of any connection to Secret Suzan, in an unfortunate boating accident. Secret Suzan is many things, but she doesn't renege on deals. Kevin got a new identity, a high position in both the Phantom Tong and the Freehold, and control of the prostitution ring that his "predecessor" had previously under his control. Originally just going by the street name "L," he eventually became known as a "Salacious L" for the ease with which he could pick up women.
                                                  .
                                                  Personality & Motivations: Salacious L revels in his position as Secret Suzan's envoy, and when needs must, enforcer. He's also technically the highest ranking Phantom Tong member in the Freehold, outranking even Secret Suzan. To Changeling "newbies" Salacious L plays up the "vain, incompetent playboy" act, but anyone who has been around the Freehold knows that he is, in fact, a vain extremely competent playboy, and not to be trifled with.

                                                  When he's not working out, lounging around one of his "massage parlors" (his prostitution ring is based out of Fort Lee), he is hitting up the best hotspots he can to pick up women. Salacious L isn't lusty, really- its not the sex he's after. He's much more excited about people appreciating his beauty and wealth. He has a habit of occasionally going out in public (even outside of Halloween) with the Mien on full display so that he can wow people. He figures that he's literally a pimp, so he might as well make the most of that reputation for eccentricity.

                                                  At the moment, Salacious L is fully loyal to Secret Suzan. Still, if her organization looked like it was going down, he'd serious contemplate jumping ship and making his own play for King of the Moon.
