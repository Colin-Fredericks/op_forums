                                    Hey guys. Here's the write up I've been working on for the Weisse Frau Kith (my favorite of the Underhill Kiths). I feel like I'm on the right track, but the second aspect of the kith blessing is what’s been giving me the most trouble. I’m thinking the changeling can either give another character a +1 die bonus to resisting clarity/breaking point rolls, or just give another character that +1 bonus to their next roll, whatever it is I want Weisse Frau’s to be something of a support class, socially speaking, so some guidance there would be aces.

                                    Weisse Frau

                                    Alright honey, have a seat, I'll tell you exactly how I can help you.

                                    These changelings are created by their keepers for a very specific and to some extent, reviled purpose. To lure individuals into the hedge. For while the gentry can seduce a person through the hedge, or bind them in a net and drag them through, but one thing they can’t do, aren’t capable of doing, is to connect with people, create a genuine bond that can coax people through the thorns. So for that, they groom their weisse frau, changelings that already have a natural aura of friendliness and amiability, and aplify that. Sometimes the weisse frau know the fate they are subjecting their companions to, having been broken by their keepers, or threatened. Other times, they sincerely believe that they’re doing their targets a favor. But eventually, one can’t take it anymore. They learn the truth, or stop being able to stomach the truth they already know. And when they do, they escape, or simply never return, renouncing their past and their keepers. Now they stand protect those around them, the weak and young especially. Perhaps if they protect enough people, it will make up for all those they’ve already damned.

                                    Darkling: She’s plain, non-threatening, blends into the background. But she’s a comforting presence, and she has a beautiful voice. That’s the only part you can really remember-everything else was so…plain, so generic. But that voice, it lingers in your ears. It soothes you like a drug, made you want to stop and chat and get to know this woman. You don’t care about the thorns scratching you-she says they’re all safe.

                                    Wizened: She’s like everybody's favorite aunt, or grandmother. She always knows exactly the right words to make you laugh, or to calm you down. Her eyes are big and genuine, as if they’re searching your soul for what makes you smile. Her skin is so soft you just want to use her like a pillow. She seems so small and harmless and sweet, why wouldn’t you follow her through that archway? She would never hurt you-look at her, she couldn’t even if she tried.

                                    Kith Blessing: When the Weisse Frau makes an empathy or persuasion roll to calm or sooth a target, they earn an exceptional success on three successes instead of five.

                                    (EDIT: the following came from the brilliant Taidragon)

                                    Trust In Me: When the Weisse Frau is with someone in emotional turmoil, be it a Breaking Point or receving Clarity damage, she may spend a point of Glamour to fix their attention upon herself and become a fixed point in an unstable malady. She can do this even if she isn't aware of the event itself, so long as she is offering some form of comforting presence itself for the person undergoing the trauma - it is a reflexive action for her. If it is a Changeling undergoing Clarity damage, they reduce their effective Wyrd for Clarity damage by the Weisse Frau's own Wyrd; if this results in effectively 0 Wyrd, the damage is reduced to a Chance die. If it is a mortal undergoing a Breaking Point, she may instead bolster the roll with half her Wyrd (rounded up). She may do this once per character per scene.

                                        I'm fond of the resisting Clarity/Breaking Points personally, Lady Gray . Here's another Kith, done for a Mage game I'm in where one of the characters is married to a Changeling (and due to Time hijinks, we're still not sure HOW). I'm playing a bit with the Kith Blessing, making it a bit broader in what rolls it affects, for limitations based on a Condition.

                                        Flowering

                                        Love is a funny thing, but I'll tell you this: No one has ever loved the way you do, for each love is unique.

                                        The Language of Flowers isn't an archaic thing in Arcadia, and there are those who were living messages in it. Most often living symbols of Love and Hate. As such they are intimately tied to Love, even if the flower they were has nothing to do with Love. Their natures were both flower and animal. Beasts could be Snapdragons or Lily Tigers. A Darkling could represent messages not so much of an emotion but it's lack or something negative, such as a blackened and dead rose. The Fairest was almost a living stand-in for the one sending the message, being a messanger of Love, and a lover themselves. Ogres were hulking figures with pelts of moss and wild thistle, giving messages of Hate and Vengence. An Elemental was more plant than animal, a flowering treant or something similar. The Wizened often had more artificial flowers of wire and metal leaf bound to their hair and flesh. But in all cases, they were messangers for their Keeper. And often suffered the more for it.

                                        Kith Blessing: When the Flowering rolls Persuasion on someone Swooning (as in the Condition) towards them, they gain an exceptional success on 3 successes rather than 5.

                                        Love Springs Eternal: The Flowering can spend a Glamour to release a sweet and cloying perfume that reduces the effective (except for Willpower) Composure of a target by net successes on an opposed Presence + Expression + Wyrd vs Composure + Supernatural Resistance up to a minimum of 1 for the rest of the scene. This affects them when within 10 yards/meters of the Flowering. It's hard to keep one's head clear when a Flowering takes action without getting away from them. If the target's Composure would have gone to 0 or lower based on successes, they are considered Swooned towards the Flowering.

                                                    I was thinking about this one recently. It's fairly straightforward, but I wanted to update one of my favorite kiths anyway.

                                                    Swimmerskin

                                                    The water is lovely today. Care to join me?

                                                    For as long as they have preyed upon humanity, the Strangers have had a preoccupation with oceans, lakes, and all manner of watery landscapes shallow and deep. Some ensconce themselves in Titles such as "Dragon Prince of the Eastern Sea" while others just harbor a fascination for all the treasures sunken and swimming that can be found below the waves.

                                                    Mortals who are turned into Swimmskins tend to already be fish-out-of-water types, those who feel like they don't quite belong, or harbor a deep longing for new vistas. A connection to water certainly helps, and plenty of members of this kith were fishers, divers, or marine biologists in their human lives, but above all appears to be the desire for a completely different situation.

                                                    Others are transformed out of pure necessity; it's difficult to attend to the Mermaid Queen's needs if you're just going clutch your throat and then stop moving after a few minutes, after all. A Swimmerskin might be transformed into a shark, have a bitter-tasting ball of kelp stuffed into her mouth, or wake up on a submerged operating table with a curved breathing apparatus sewn into the sides of her neck.

                                                    It's not talked about much, but many times Swimmerskins feel conflicted about their place in the world. Those especially who already felt a connection to water before their Durances might feel that they owe their former Keeper something akin to a debt of gratitude for the "gifts" the True Fae bestowed on them. In times of Loyalist witchhunts, suspicious eyes may fall on those who spend far too long beneath the waves. Regardless, in Freeholds whose territory includes large bodies of water, the a Swimmerskin's presence can be invaluable. On may help finance his court through diving for sunken curios, while another patrols a serpentine river in the Hedge for fluvial threats.

                                                    Beast: This changeling has the skin of an eel, and all the grace to match. When he smiles, his mouth is full of needle-like teeth--all the better for gulping down fresh fish.

                                                    Fairest: She spends her free time sitting at the edge of a lake, her legs tracing currents in the water, combing her luxurious blue-green-blond hair and singing the loveliest song you've ever heard, one full of both wonderment and homesickness. On a closer look, there is a nearly-translucent webbing between her fingers, and a dusting of shiny scales on her cheeks.

                                                    Kith Blessing: When the Swimmerskin makes an Athletics roll while underwater, achieving three successes counts as an exceptional success.

                                                    Ocean's Kiss: A Swimmerskin can breathe unpolluted water just as easily as air. He may extend this ability to another being by spending a point of Glamour and touching their lips. This lasts for a number of minutes equal to the changeling's Wyrd.

                                                            I've been running through and trying to mechanically update a number of Kiths from the initial previews (plus one homebrew original). They're missing fluff, but I feel they should work pretty well.


                                                            Truefriend
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Truefriend uses Empathy to comfort or understand a companion, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            True Bond: If she touches someone she truly cares about and spends a point of Glamour, she may roll Presence + Expression to inspire and reinforce them. If she succeeds on the roll, both she and the person she touched gain the Steadfast Condition. Truefriends automatically resist any powers that would force or coerce them to betray a friend who currently benefits from this gift.

                                                            Draconic
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Draconic uses Intimidation to cow another through a show of force, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Dreadful Form: The thick scales of the Draconic turn one point of Lethal damage taken from every attack into Bashing damage. If she spends a point of Glamour, her unarmed attacks deal Lethal damage for the rest of the scene and her scales may instead turn one point of Aggravated damage into Lethal or ignore one point of Bashing damage altogether.

                                                            Razorhand
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Razorhand uses Weaponry with the intent to kill a fae creature at night, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Darkblade: The Razorhand has a natural weapon with 1L damage, which causes lethal damage even to creatures which normally take bashing damage from lethal wounds. If she spends a point of Glamour, a Razorhand can also cause her next successful attack with this weapon to automatically inflict one of the following Tilts: Arm Wrack, Blinded, Deafened, or Leg Wrack.

                                                            Smith
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Smith uses Craft to build or repair objects with a practical function, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Maker's Deftness: By spending a point of Glamour, the Smith can repair, modify, or sabotage any object with inhuman speed. If the repair or alteration would require an extended action, he makes rolls on the extended task once a turn.

                                                            Airtouched
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Airtouched uses Athletics to jump, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Lighter Than Air: The Airtouched suffers no falling damage except from falls that reach terminal velocity. If she spends a point of Glamour, she can multiply her jumping height and distance by three for a single roll, and slowly glide instead of falling for the same distance.

                                                            Windwing
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Airtouched uses Athletics to ascend through the air under the open sky, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Wind-Carried: The Windwing may spend a Glamour and roll Stamina + Athletics to fly at twice her Speed for Stamina turns. Regardless of her real weight she may land and perch on anything that might hold the weight of a small child.

                                                            Blightbent
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Blightbent uses Survival to navigate or forage in a poisonous, radioactive or otherwise blighted environment, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Plague-filled: The Blightbent reduces the level of any manmade extreme environment and the Toxicity of any drug or poison by 2. If she spends a point of Glamour she may roll Stamina + Wyrd to produce a cloud of caustic smog with a radius in meters equal to her Stamina. The cloud either causes a concealment penalty or deals lethal damage with Toxicity equal to half the successes on this roll, round up. The cloud lasts for a scene without outside intervention.

                                                            Broadback
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Broadback uses Athletics in a contest of endurance, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Stoic Forbearance: The Broadback suffers no penalties due to fatigue, and she may spend one point of Glamour to add her Wyrd to Doors in Social Manuevering, adding up to +5 doors.

                                                            Coyote
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Coyote uses Subterfuge to leave a favorable opinion of himself during a first impression, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Face of a Friend: When attempting Social Maneuvering, the Coyote may spend one Glamour to start one impression level higher than she otherwise would.

                                                            Antiquarian
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Antiquarian uses Academics to evaluate or research ancient lore, books or artifacts, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Keys to Knowledge: While touching an item more than a century old, the Antiquarian may spend one Glamour to reflexively roll Wits + Investigation + Wyrd. This allows her to perceive the most emotionally intense moment tied to the object, letting you ask the Storyteller a yes or no question related to the scene for each success gathered, or gain the Informed condition pertaining to the item.

                                                            Render
                                                            Kith Blessing:                                                            When the Render uses Brawl to destroy an object or structure, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Crumbling Touch: If the Render spends a point of Glamour, for the rest of the scene she ignores worn armor in unarmed attacks and durability in damaging objects.

                                                            Skitterskull
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Skitterskull uses Athletics to climb, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Impossible Counterpoise: The Skitterskull suffers no penalties to Athletics rolls when climbing even the steepest surfaces, and by spending one Glamour, she can triple her Defense when Dodging instead of Doubling.

                                                            Venombite
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Venombite uses Brawl to grapple with the intent of delivering a venomous bite, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Kiss of Death: The Venombite may spend a point of Glamour to deliver a venomous bite when using a damage move as part of a grapple. She applies the Moderate version of the Poisoned Tilt to the receiving character. Outside of combat the venom deals damage on a turn basis and has Toxicity 4.

                                                            Waterborn
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Waterborn uses Athletics while submerged in water, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Like a Fish: The Waterborn can breathe freely underwater, and if she spends a point of Glamour then for the rest of the scene she swims at her full Speed and may make Empathy rolls to sense and understand the strongest feelings felt in a body of water or its immediate vicinity. In open water like the ocean she treats an area of five kilometers like a single body of water.

                                                            Swimmerskin
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Swimmerskin uses Athletics while swimming, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Ocean's Kiss: A swimmerskin can breathe water as easily as air, and may extend this ability to another being by spending a point of Glamour and rolling Wyrd + Stamina while touching their lips. This lasts for a number of minutes equal to Wyrd.

                                                            Runnerswift
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Runnerswift uses Athletics to run from a foe or predator, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            League-Gallop: The Runnerswift may spend a point of Glamour and reflexively rolls Dexterity + Athletics. For the rest of the scene, she adds two plus the successes to her Speed, and inflicts a penalty on all dice rolls attempting to track or follow her equal to successes, up to -5.

                                                            Flamesiren
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Flamesiren uses Expression to fascinate or entrance, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Moths to the Flame: Spend a point of Glamour and roll Presence + Expression + Wyrd as an instant action to perform, contested by anyone who witnesses the Flamesiren's enchanting show with Composure + Supernatural Tolerance. She can inflict a single audience member who fails to resist with the Swooning condition. Anyone afflicted with the Swooning condition in this way who touches the Flamesiren treats her as an Extreme Environment with level equal to half her Wyrd round up.

                                                            Soldier
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Soldier uses Survival in open conflict with an enemy or in a war-torn battlefield, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Master of Arms: A Soldier does not take Initiative penalties from weapons, and she can spend a Glamour after a full night's sleep to exchange any dots of a Fighting Style Merit she possesses for the same number of dots in other Fighting Style merits. Fighting Styles obtained this way can ignore Attribute and Skill requirements.

                                                            Lionheart
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Lionheart uses Persuasion to inspire another to follow correct or honest conduct, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Binding of Honor: Once per session, the Lionheart may strengthen a Sealing without the need to spend Willpower. If a changeling attempts to refute such a sealing, the Lionheart may spend a point of Glamour and force him to roll a Clash of Wills of his Wyrd + Resolve against her own, in addition to the usual cost of undoing it. However, the Lionheart is unable to refute a single Sealing placed by a person under the effect of Binding of Honor.

                                                            Skogsra
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Skogsra uses Stealth to conceal herself or stalk in the wilderness, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Fauna Affinity: If the Skogsra spends a point of Glamour, then for the next scene she can communicate with any wild or feral animals and give them simple commands they will obey with a successful Presence + Animal Ken roll, unless the animal is already under supernatural control or the command is obviously suicidal.

                                                            Whisperwind
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Whisperwind uses Investigation to gather information through hearing or conversation, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Secrets In the Breeze: The Whisperwind can hear with perfect clarity up to a block away for each dot of Wyrd she possesses, to a maximum of five. By spending a point of Glamour, she can make someone else able to hear her whispers clearly at the same distance.

                                                            Gravewright
                                                            Kith Blessing: When the Gravewright uses Occult to learn about ghosts, three successes count as an exceptional success.
                                                            Medium: The Gravewright can see and hear nearby ghosts in Twilight, and may spend a point of Glamour to add or remove the Anchor, Open, Controlled or Materialized Condition to or from an object, person or location, so long as the condition's prerequisites are met. Attempting to restore a condition the Gravewright removed in the last week creates a Clash of Wills.
