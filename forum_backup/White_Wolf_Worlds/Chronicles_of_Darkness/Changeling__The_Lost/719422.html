    What Are the Huntsmen?

    10-23-2015, 11:52 AM
    I often forget to check the front page news, so it's possible I may have missed this.

    But do we actually know what the Huntsmen are supposed to be at this point? Are they the new version of loyalists (i.e Changelings that serve the Keeper), or are they a completely different species like the Hobgoblins? The Lost seem terrified of them, which if they were just simple Changelings, shouldn't be that big of a deal. So I'm a little curious about the subject.

    If they're loyalists, I'd like to think it would be possible to play a Huntsman chronicle, but if they're something else completely, it would be nice to know what they're capable of.
