    Kiths: The Missing Seeming Ideas

    10-21-2015, 10:04 PM
    So we've got a pretty good list of the Kiths so far located here: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1.../preview?pli=1


    But as you'll probably notice, we only get 3 examples of Seemings for each of the Kiths. So I figured why don't we post our own ideas for how the Seemings not included in the preview could be encountered or interpreted in the game?


    Tunnelgrub (Elemental): A humanoid skeleton floating within a quivering, gelatinous ooze.


    Waterborn (Wizened): A deep sea diving suit with a pair of brightly glowing eyes shining within the helmet like searchlights.


    Woodwalker (Fairest): A forest nymph with pale green skin and hair the color of Autumn leaves. A network of tattoos spans her body, resembling creeping vines.


    Snowskin (Ogre): A frost giant in the form of a hulking man with icy blue skin and braided hair as white as the snow.


    Razorhand (Fairest): A wandering Ronin dressed in pristine robes and who is never seen without his favorite sword within arm's reach. His demeanor is often solemn with memories of the lives he has taken.


    Polychromatic (Ogre): Their skin has been transformed into prismatic armor, their Keeper having embedded color shifting stones of various sizes and shapes into its surface.

                                                      Beast: At first glance, you mistake the chocolate brown to be the color of her fur, until you get closer and realize she is actually made out of chocolate sculpted into the form of an anthropomorphic bunny. Rich caramel flows through her veins while the scent of dark chocolate wafts off her like a perfume. A chunk of her right ear is missing, with teeth marks indicating it was bitten off by an unknown assailant.


                                                      ----------------------------

                                                      True Friend (Wizened): He used to spend hours riding around on his Keeper's shoulder, parroting his master's ideas and otherwise serving as a Yes Man to feed their ego. The metal collar and broken chain is still worn around his neck, a permanent reminder of his service. He has the body of a green skinned imp with a lithe, almost child-like body. Bright red horns grow from his scalp, and he has a prehensile tail that ends in an arrow head. The tiny wings growing from his back once offered him the power of flight, but are too small and weak in the normal world to be of much use anymore.


                                                      Troll (Fairest): A knight clad in dark armor standing firm with a posture that dares anyone to cross the line they have drawn in the sand.

                                                      Skitterskulk (Ogre): Wicked claws like a grappling hook and powerful leg muscles to propel itself upward. The lumbering creature scaled cliffs through shear strength, rather than graceful agility.
