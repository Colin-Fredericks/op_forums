    Seems There Are More Seemings

    09-17-2015, 02:51 AM
    In the most recent Kiths post, Dave Hill recapped some other spoilers involving brand new Seemings!

    Apparently there will be a new one in the Dark Eras supplement.

    That one’s literally about embracing a story to escape, taking on a role from a tale in order to get the edge and flee Arcadia.
    Perhaps these folks will be called something like Players? I bet they'll be similar to the Wizened in terms of Blessing/Curse revolving around the "role" they played in the tale.

    Dave also mentioned that you could look at this as the eighth Seeming. I feel fairly confident that he's referring to the Seemingless, but I could be wrong.

    Then we have the first supplement for CtL2e, entitled The Hedge, which will have one more...

    They escaped by embracing chance, chaos, and luck. They closed their eyes and jumped, putting their faith in the universe.
    I'm not sure if The Hedge is going to be a sort of players' guide, but I find this one really interesting. It kinda feels like their themes could faith, destiny, and providence. Do they feel they have a debt to repay? Are they still on their journey?

    I'd love to hear what you guys think, especially if you have more dirt from other postings!

                                      Originally posted by Metamurpho View Post
                                      I'd love to hear what you guys think, especially if you have more dirt from other postings!
                                      I know I've always found it useful to get more stuff in one place. I think the thread on the Hedge Supplement book was the first place Dave talked about Seemings 7 and 8 in any real detail. I think I got the main beats from that conversation in the spoiler box below. Dave encapsulated the heart of them in the blog you quoted, but you'll indeed find some more flesh to put on them.

                                      Originally posted by MachineIV View Post
                                      Spoiler alert.

                                      I'm not 100% sure the eighth will go in Hedge yet. Not sure. Probably. But, thinking.

                                      The seventh, for Grimm's Fairy Tales Lost, is someone that leveraged the power of stories to escape. They played a part. They internalised a tale, because tales are a language the Gentry have to listen to.

                                      The eighth, if I end up doing it, is someone that wilfully and fully abandoned herself to luck. Not someone that escaped by pure chance. But someone that literally relied on random chance, who saw a choice, and chose chaos. This is someone that saw an open, dark well, and thought, "There's a chance I could survive," then shrugged and jumped.
                                      Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
                                      I kind of thought that was what all the Seemings were doing - they were divided based on the specific story.
                                      Originally posted by MachineIV View Post
                                      It's KIND of the opposite. You're in their story, and you just say, "Enough. This is where I take authorial control. MASSIVE REWRITE, FUCKER."

                                      Whereas this Seeming plays along to win. She looks for the right, narratively appropriate story beats to win. It's the difference between taking control by forcing the story to change, and taking control by actively deciding to play along.
                                      Originally posted by MachineIV View Post
                                      1) They're absolutely 2e. The old Seeming model would be almost impossible to support with additional Seemings, because you would have to devote so much space to adding Kiths in order to make them even comparable to the existing ones.

                                      2) We're calling them Grimms', after the famous brothers. This Seeming didn't necessarily begin in the early part of the nineteenth century, and it most certainly didn't end there. But it became something worth noticing there; the people knew the language of the Gentry in a whole new way. I don't have a name for the eighth Seeming yet.

                                      3) Seventh Seeming is my Changeling: The Lost Akira Kurosawa homage.
                                      Originally posted by MachineIV View Post
                                      I'm considering [Loyalists] a Seeming. They did escape, but their choice was to abandon one kind of captivity for another.
                                      Originally posted by Omegaphallic View Post
                                      If Loyalists are a Seeming, then wouldn't that make Grimms the 8th, and the Risktaking Seeming the 9th Seeiming?
                                      Originally posted by MachineIV View Post
                                      Technically yes.

                                      But I don't count [Loyalists] amongst the list when I talk about the other two, because the assumption is, they're not by default intended for players. That's not to say you couldn't play one or whatever. Just that the game assumes you're playing someone who broke free.
                                      Originally posted by MachineIV View Post
                                      Loyalist Seeming comes from making the choice to serve as method of escape. If you don't make that choice, you're Seemingless.
