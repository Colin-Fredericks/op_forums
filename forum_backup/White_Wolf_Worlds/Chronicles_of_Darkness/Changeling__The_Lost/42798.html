    Goblin Chronicles(?): Stopgap GMC changes for Changeling

    01-05-2014, 06:49 AM
    While any Chronicles update of Changeling: The Lost will probably have some more significant overhauls (like I'm sure a predatory aura-like fae mien), there are at least a few simple things we can do in the meantime. I'll go through the core book here and, with B&S, GMC and Demon in mind as far as mechanical changes, come up with a few suggestions. These won't be as extensive as a full treatment probably receives, but it's something.

    Character Creation
    Changelings get 10 Merits at character creation. They may spend 5 Merit dots to increase starting Wyrd by one dot.


    Merits
    With the exception of Supernatural Merits as usual, changelings can purchase Merits out of the GMC book. As per the book, Social Merits might require some explanation, but use common sense and Storyteller discretion.

    Hollow
    Similar to the Bolthole Merit from Demon: The Descent, Hollow now

    Mantle
    See below.

    Contracts
    Smoke 4: Murkblur
    Success inflicts the Blinded Tilt for the duration (see God-Machine Chronicle, p. 208).

    Artifice 3: Blessing of Perfection
    If the object so blessed is a weapon, its bonus is equal to the changeling's Wyrd divided by 2 (rounding down). The character divides these bonus points as she wishes between the weapon's Damage and Initiative ratings.

    Darkness 1: Creeping Dread
    Success inflicts the Scarred Condition on all affected targets.
    Darkness 2: Balm of Unwakeable Slumber
    This causes both the Insensate and Immobile Tilts as the target suffers from a magically-enforced sleep. Both Tilts immediately end if the target is harmed in any way or endangered (truly vigorous shaking or slapping a sleeping target also work), but otherwise the target remains asleep until she is accustomed to waking up. On an exceptional success, the target is Drugged for the rest of the scene after waking up.

    Elements 1: Cloak of the Elements
    Ignore any damage from the relevant extreme environment while the clause is active.
    Elements 2: Armor of the Elements' Fury
    The armor provided is 1/1. It downgrades one level of firearms damage to bashing, and subtracts 1 point from overall damage. As with weapon changes, the elemental Wyrd damage applies as a Damage bonus after a successful attack roll. It no longer applies to the attack roll itself.
    Elements 5: Become the Primal Foundation
    Armor gained by transforming into a solid element has a general rating equal to Wyrd, and a ballistic rating equal to half the character's Wyrd, rounded down. The damage provided by a damaging element once again applies after a successful attack roll, as if delivered by a weapon with Damage rating equal to half the character's Wyrd.

    Fang and Talon 1: Tongues of Birds and Words of Wolves
    An exceptional success puts the Bonded Condition on the animal.
    Fang and Talon 3: Pipes of the Beastcaller
    Exceptional success grants the Bonded Condition along with the other effects.

    Stone 5: Red Rage of Terrible Revenge
    Armor gained is 1/1.

    Vainglory 1: Mask of Superiority
    Maybe adds the Swooned Condition?
    Vainglory 4: Mantle of Terrible Beauty
    Those who roll fewer successes than the changeling suffer from the Frightened and Humbled Conditions. On an exceptional success, even those who roll more successes are Humbled in the character's presence. This is in addition to the changeling's bonus to Intimidate rolls.
    Vainglory 5: Words of Memories Never Lived
    Success puts the False Memories Condition on listeners. On an exceptional success, the targets are all Mesmerized for the duration as well.

    Fleeting Spring 5: Waking the Inner Faerie
    Inflicts the Obsession Condition on the target.

    Eternal Summer 1: Son of the Hearth
    This power negates the penalties for extreme environments of heat or cold in the affected area (determined by the user as normal). Also renders targets immune to Extreme Heat and Extreme Cold Tilts.
    Eternal Summer 5: The Lord's Dread Gaze
    The attack roll is Dexterity + Athletics - target's Defense. If successful, apply a Damage modifier equal to the changeling's Mantle divided by two (round up).

    Fleeting Autumn 2: Tale of the Baba Yaga
    This applies the Frightened Condition to targets.
    Fleeting Autumn 5: Mien of the Baba Yaga
    Also applies the Frightened Condition to targets.

    Fleeting Winter 3: Faces in the Water
    Applies the Broken Condition to the target for the duration (and others suffer the -2 penalty to influence the subject).
    Fleeting Winter 4: Fallow Fields, Empty Harvest
    Inflicts the Joyless Condition (see below).
    Fleeting Winter 5: Every Sorrow a Jewel
    Causes the Insensate Tilt.

    Eternal Winter 1: Jack's Breath
    Creates a level 2 or 3 extreme environment (cold), depending on the degree to which the room is cooled.
    Eternal Winter 3: Riding the Devil's Jawbone
    Applies the Extreme Cold Environmental Tilt on the affected area, which doesn't affect the changeling herself.
    Eternal Winter 4: Fallen from the Timbers
    The roll is Dexterity + Athletics - the subject's Defense. Success adds half the character's Wyrd (rounded up) to the damage, which is lethal. Subject still suffers other penalties.
    Eternal Winter 5: Witch's Paradise
    Creates a level 2 extreme environment over a wide area by default. Each additional five successes does one of the following: increase the level by 1, double the radius, increase duration by 1 hour.

    Goblin: Diviner's Madness
    After divining, you suffer from the Madness Condition for one day.

    New Condition: Joyless
    Your character can no longer feel positive emotions, only coldness and crushing negativity. With no capacity for joy or happiness, you become unable to regain Willpower by acting on Virtues or Vices (or their equivalent) and suffer a -2 penalty to all Social rolls.
    Possible Sources: Dramatic failure on a detachment roll, Fleeting Winter clauses
    Resolution: The character experiences an overwhelmingly joyous new experience (the birth of a child, the profession of deep-seated love by someone)


    Special Rules and Systems
    Nothing really changes by default. However, I am not much of a fan of Mantle as it stands being a Merit. Honestly, since it seems like most supernaturals will have a sort of predatory aura equivalent, I think Mantle should be folded into fae mien in the changeling version of a predatory aura. Not sure how to compensate for its inclusion in seasonal Contracts, since using Wyrd all the time wouldn't necessarily fit (and could get much more powerful with Wyrd 6+). Yet half Wyrd is too low, I think. However, I don't think Mantle is really something you should pay for on its own. If you include it in the "aura," its effects are felt on some level even by mortals, but just covered by the Mask.

    It's late, I'm tired, I will consider it more tomorrow.

                      I'd simply (or not so simply) wrap Bedlam into their Aura. Get too big a dose of the weirdness they project (and hide via the Mask), and things start to get unhinged. It's something I'm working on, given my long-term game with my Co-ST has Changeling and Werewolf antagonists; I don't see a good reason to short change them, and I always felt Bedlam along with Hedge-sculpting had too high of base Wyrd requirements.

                      I want to give them 3 permutations for each Attribute + Wyrd used; they may use Finesse rather than Power though just to present some differences. I'm thinking a form of Bedlam for the Social or physical (emotions/impulses running unchecked), Madness for the mental or social(though I'm not entirely sure I want to use the Madness Condition) as individuals start to see portions of Faerie and the Wyrd bleed over onto things like a low-Clarity Changeling, and... something for the physical. Or, switch it up some, have one that occludes clear memories, a nod to The Mask where you lash out with it's supernaturally obscuring properties, likely a Mental attribute base.

                      Honestly, I've little problem with Mantle as is beyond filling in the 2 and 4 dot benefits.                           I wouldn't remove the Mantle Merit, is it should be possible for someone with a high Wyrd to have a low Mantle and the other way around. Your Mantle is your connection to the Court (and season) and has nothing to do with your connection to the Wyrd.

                          Mantle currently is a combination of Status within a court and a magical aura (with some other benefits). Someone with a high Mantle cannot necessarily boss his court around, but is still respected. The other way around high Mantle is required for some Court positions. You could make an argument for differentiating between the magical connection with your Court and Status within your Court, where having a high Mantle could make it easier to gain Status. What position you have within your Court would then be a function of both Status and Mantle.

                                  An idea that hit me in the shower earlier for Mantle mechanics: Make them work like the Agenda conditions for Demon: The Descent.

                                  For those that aren't familiar: DtD allows you to choose an agenda, which gives you a condition that you can resolve for a bonus and meet a condition for a beat that lasts once per session. Each court has three divergent, but appropriate focuses (mostly, but I'm thinking we should keep it simple): The Signature Emotion, The Season/Direction/Time of Day and the Agenda (War, Espionage, Occultism, Rebirth). As such, pick one of the three for your character with the mantle and it gives you the condition helpful in aiding their cause.

                                  An example for Summer (this is very rough): There's no reason that you couldn't apply these conditions to your contracts of the court either.

                                  Enraging
                                  Beat: Encourage or convince someone to anger when they wouldn't have otherwise.
                                  Resolution: Expend this condition to gain a Bonus of Mantle Dots on any test related to anger.

                                  Heat of the Sun
                                  Beat: Endure or create an environment where you suffer from a harsh or bright environment.
                                  Resolution: Expend this condition to gain a Bonus of Mantle Dots on any test to create heat, light or fire.

                                  Noon Light Knight
                                  Beat: Initiate a contest or battle when diplomacy, stealth or other means would have sufficed.
                                  Resolution: Expend this condition to gain a bonus of Mantle Dots on an action in the first round of Combat.

                                  The social bonus for same-court should probably help set impression levels if you want to use the Doors system. Likewise it should help inflicting bedlam of your chosen emotion.

                                  Thoughts? Concerns? Mathematical alterations?
