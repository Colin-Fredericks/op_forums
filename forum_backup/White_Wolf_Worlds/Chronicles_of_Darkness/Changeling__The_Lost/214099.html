    Cold Iron Bullets

    07-30-2014, 08:45 PM
    Not sure if this should go into Changeling or Hunter, but are there rules for Cold Iron bullets anywhere? I could swear there are, but I can't find them.

    Me and some friends are currently having a disagreement about whether Cold Iron can be fired from a gun and still be called Cold Iron. The argument is that because gunpowder is a manmade fire, it turns Cold Iron into just Iron. I disagree, but I'm looking for rules that say otherwise. And also my Hunter might need some Cold Iron bullets.

      Rites of Spring carries the entire discussion on Cold Iron.

      I'd argue for simplicity's sake that firing a cold iron bullet does not screw with it to the point of losing it's metaphysical properties. The reasons for this are two. One, making cold iron bullets without heating it takes a shit ton of work, and it's not something most players will do. Two, bullets generally aren't retrievable, and as such using cold iron for bullets is wasting resources. In short, it's a troublesome weapon-no need to make it worse.

                                  I spent a year running Changeling and I still get confused on this subject. So, bearing that in mind... isn't Cold Iron the one with the incredibly misleading name? Cold Iron can be heated and forged, it just has to mostly be unrefined iron. So, as long as you find a way to manufacture iron bullets, you can probably make bullets that break through fae defences.

                                  Which leaves Hand-forged Iron, the cause of aggravated damage to the Fae, as the one that can't be introduced to flame. And, well, a hand-forged bullet seems like it would be pretty hard to pull off in a way that wouldn't see a modern gun jam. I seem to remember that I pretty much ruled it was impossible for my group, although I do recall debate over whether it would be possible to do with a blunderbuss. Can't remember if we came to a verdict on that one, no one really wanted to go to the effort of getting a blunderbuss.

                                          Sigh, this is the volcano discussion all over again.

                                          Cold Iron is one of the most aggravating concepts in changeling the lost.
                                          First, cold iron is pure Iron, minimal traces of other elements on the mix.
                                          Second, rites of spring brings in the "flame caused by the hand of man" idea, which I still think is dumb, because it creates a thousand and one exceptions, (solar forge, volcanic forge). Previously Iron that was physically cold was good enough, now people complicate it.

                                          If you use a home made gauss rifle it would count as cold iron, if you used a sling it would...silliness.

                                          The point of tempering and pounding iron is to pound out the impurities of the metal, there is no way the spark from gunpowder would be heating up the iron enough to smelt in the milliseconds the bullet exists in the barrel, now the problem is the effort to shape an iron bullet without using a cast mold

                                              It would be impossible for a human to hand-forge a bullet with enough precision to not jam, but I would probably allow a Smith or maybe an Inventor to do it. The whole "putting man made flame [to iron]" thing is meant to be poetic, I think. Just touching it with fire shouldn't be enough to break the magic, it's the process of tempering it to remove the natural impurities that does it. The purpose of the gunpowder is to propel the bullet, not to temper it.
