    New Entitlement: The Black Powder Brigade

    04-09-2014, 05:31 PM
    How much powder when you hear hungry Briarwolves roar?
    One measure? Two measures?
    More! More!

    How much powder when thorns are growing through the floor?
    Three measures? Four measures?
    More! More!

    How much powder when there's traitors running for the shore?
    Five measures? Six Measures?
    More! More!

    How much powder when goblins are arming themselves for war?
    Seven Measures? Eight Measure?
    More! More!

    How much powder when the Gentry are knocking at the door?
    Nine Measures? Ten Measures?
    MORE! MORE!

    Once upon a time there was a pair of faerie princesses, sisters, who liked violence more than anything in the world. You might say they had fetish for bloodshed. The flashing blades, the sound of steel tearing through flesh, the skill of a champion. Their lust was insatiable, and every year they would wonder the world collection fighters from armies and tournaments according to their own unkowable tastes.

    Now it so happened, as it inevitably must, that the world changed beneath the unchanging faeries. One day they arrived at the edge of a battlefield to watch and choose their next champion, but these soldiers did not fight with swords. Napoleon and Wellington's armies fought with gunpowder. Neither sister knew quite what to make of it and so they decided to take the combatant with the most kills to it's name and see what they thought. When they chose one of Wellington's cannons it insisted that it was nothing without it's crew, so they were taken too.

    Over the centuries the sister's collection had grown into two armies that fought each other for the amusement of their distant keepers. Yet they were armies in name only, there was no discipline or professionalism. The “soldiers” had as much to fear from their own as the enemy. Squabbles over scarce rations or accidental insults could turn lethal.

    The cannon, and it's crew, made a better game of it than most. They set up a system of watches and used their numbers to stand up to the most brutal of their fellows. Free from hunger and paranoia they could turn their thoughts to home and plans of escape. When the time was right they herded a squadron of their enemy away from battle, disguising their escape as a noble pursuit they slipped away forever.


    The precision of a march, dashing uniforms each exactly alike, the drums, the gleaming cannons. An army has a glamour all of it's own, a glamour that once drew the attention of the Gentry and now has become a potent weapon against them. Not just a cannon or a volley of muskets, but military discipline to overcome faerie madness. A soldier's loyalty to overcome the temptations of Arcadia. These are what make the Black Powder Brigade a true enemy of the Gentry.

    The cannons don't hurt either. The Brigade are specialists, most Summer Courtiers can fight but sometimes you need to do something... larger. Destroy a fortress full of rowdy hobgoblins, shoot down a dragon. The Brigade are the summer court's big guns. Experts in logistics and military engineering, the Brigade bring all manner of hedgespun artillery, communications technology and quickly assembled battlefield fortifications to the fray.


    Member Titles: The Black Powder Brigade as several titles forming a chain of command: Redcoat, Gunner or Sapper, followed by Bombardier or Corporal, Serjeant or Colour Serjeant then Brigadier.

    Gunners operate the artillery and other equipment the Bridgade is famed for. Redcoats are infantry who defend the heavy equipment and it's operators or the engineers as they work. Sappers are those engineers, quickly assembling flat-packed hedgespun fortifications before the battle or building defences in a freehold.

    Bombardiers and Corporals command between three to eight men. Bombardiers also have command of heavy equipment, Corporals don't.

    In any field of deployment the highest officer is a Serjeant. Colour Serjants possess the same rank but typically serve the Brigade far away from the front lines.

    At any one time there is only one Brigadier. By tradition, but not in reality, the Brigadier reports directly to the reigning Seasonal Monarch (in every freehold). Practicality makes this impossible, in reality the Brigadier runs the Entitlement and it is the Serjeant who reports to whichever in-season monarch is nearby.

    [Picture: A goblin in a spotless uniform similar to a classic British Redcoat leans against a brass cannon smoking a cigarette]

    Requirements: Wyrd 2, Resolve 2, Strength 2, Dexterity 2, Stamina 2, Mantle 1 (Summer), Firearms 3 or Firearms 2 with a speciality in Artillery, Science 2 (Or Science 1 with a speciality in Ballistics or Physics), Socialise 1

    Joining: The Black Powder Brigade doesn’t expect to find enough trained soldiers with experience in operating artillery to fill it's ranks. Any Summer courtier in good standing can join by finding a Serjants and applying, several Colour Serjants travel the Freeholds full time as recruitment officers.

    When enough applicants have expressed their interest they will be informed to make their way to the Brigade's camp (it moves around the hedge) for basic training. This is a brutal, but not cruel, process that can get any dedicated recruit up to scratch and teach the specialised skills that the Brigade requires. When they have proven themselves and acquired all the requirements to join the Entitlement their membership is formally accepted.

    Mein: When working with their cannons their hands and clothing get covered with black powder, but at any time that they are not their clothing is sharp, their colours brighter, and the metal shines. The members with higher mantle are followed by the sound of a distant marching drum, the higher the mantle the closer it sounds. However each one has a single crease, soot stain or scar. The reality of war hidden beneath the glamour.

    Members have a military bearing: their posture is perfect, their backs ramrod-straight, and when they walk, it looks like they're marching.

    Background: The Brigade is unsurprisingly popular with Changelings abducted from a military life but since most people were never part of the army most Changelings weren't either. What makes a Changeling escaped from Arcadia take up the king's shilling?

    For many it's the sense of purpose and belonging. The Brigade is renowned for it's esprit de corps, any member can count upon finding camaraderie and perhaps a sense of purpose. Changelings return from Arcadia seeking a new life, like men through all of history they hope to march of with the armies and find something better than where they came from.

    For other's it's the glamour and glory. Faerie armies rarely have to lie in muddy trenches with empty bellies when the earth can be pursued to offer up it's bounties – faerie war is like a story, full of heroic charges, desperate defences, inspired gambits and dastardly tricks. There's not so much boring patrols or digging out latrines. It's certainly no safer than mundane war, but it's a lot more Glamorous. Many Changelings have taken the commission while imagining marching into some besieged Freehold to beating drums and gleaming uniforms with the eyes of every local Fairest upon them.

    Others still come for the most Summer reason of all. They wish to fight back against their oppressors, for revenge, for justice, for just for Wrath. They see no better gun than a cannon, and no better armour against faerie madness than military discipline.


    Organisation: The Black Powder Brigade is an army, and they're very proud of it. The entire Entitlement forms one single chain of command, and every Changeling knows their place in it.

    At the top is the Brigadier, or “Brig”. The Brig's role is administrative and ceremonial, mostly it revolves assigning Changelings and Equipment to “campaigns”. A campaign being a single decisive attack against a hobgoblin fortress or a long entrenched war between a Freehold and a True Fae's army.

    Each campaign is led by a Serjeant, who is given the honorific of Acting Brigadier. Though a particularly small campaign may bestow the title on a member of lower rank. The Acting Brigadier commands other Sargent. Each Sargent leads one specific operation – be it maintaining a camp, leading bombarding a fortress, or organising the campaign's logistics.

    A campaign can take place anywhere on Earth that the Seasonal courts hold sway. The Brigade like to pretend that if a Seasonal King or Queen orders them to arrive, then like any military they are bound to obey. In practice there's a lot of monarchs, and only one Brigade, so the regiment chooses it's own deployments.

    One thing that the Brigade is very clear about, is that they are not mercenaries. They do expect to be paid by whatever Freehold they are currently working for, but they are paid wages rather than a fee. The Brigade does not fight for money, but for King, Freedom and the Summer Sun.

    Privileges:

    Hedgespun Raiment (O) When a member joins they soon acquire a uniform woven out of the finest cottons, dyed red with sunset, tasselled with woven sunlight and starched with military pride.

    Band of Brothers: The camaraderie and discipline of The Black Powder Brigade help them stand firm in terms of opposition. When rolling to resist fear or any supernatural power that will force them to betray (including by abandoning them) fellow members of the band a soldier gets +1 for every member of the Brigade present, capped to the highest Presence of the present Brigadiers.

    Draft Horse's Might: The Black Powder Brigade have an unusual ability to move equipment easily. Providing the combined Strength + Stamina of every Brigadier pushing is greater than the Size of the equipment, the equipment is on wheels, and the equipment is Hedgespun or a Token then they can push or pull the equipment at their full Speed. Pushing a several ton cannon at a flat out run is quite common for the Brigade.

    Sidebar: Gleaming Cannons

    The Black Powder Brigade are specialists in the big guns of war. Most Brigadiers are artillerists or combat engineers, and the rest guard the artillery and engineers. Players and Storytellers may consider looking at World of Darkness: Armory for equipment to base the Brigade's Hedgespun war machines on. The following sections make a good staring point:

    Artillery: Page 106
    Tank Gun: Page 110 – probably the closest thing in WoD: Armory to a Napoleonic cannon.
    Demolitions: Page 112
    Rifles: Page 72 – no commander fields artillery without infantry to protect it.

    The Brigadiers load their weapons with sparks collected from the summer sun, which means those cannons can both smash through and burn their targets. A cannon ball covered in napalm is probably the closets mundane equivalent. They are also beautiful, glamorous, creations in gleaming brass and regimental colours. Their Mask makes them look like film props – well made but obviously non-functional.


    Rumours of the Brigade

    You think that they're the big guns? No, they're holding the big guns back. They've got everything up to Hedgespun nukes hidden away. Enough firepower to destroy Arcadia in a stroke, but if they do that there's no war. If there's no war there's no Brigade. If there's no Brigade they're just a bunch of lost lonely souls. So they're holding back on us.

    The Brigade, have you really thought about what "camaraderie of solders really means"? Course you haven't. You see a lot of the Brigade were military men before they were taken, and they really believe that brothers in arms are allies to the bitter end, so who do you think they went back and told? There's whole squads of Ensorcelled who've been rising up the ranks for years, when they get to the top spot we're going to find ourselves conscripted for the front lines, and if we don't all toe the line and serve the army the Brigade won't hesitate to “court marshal” us all.
