                    Originally posted by DaemonChrno View Post
                    I really like the book. The fluff is really amazing and I love all the courts that has been added

                    It seems to me that some of the changeling stuff in the book suffer from a lack of common nomenclature:
                    - the Directional Courts from the Three Kingdoms of Darkness chapter lack a common bulwark, court contracts and court approachs ;
                    - the Contracts of the Directional Courts from the Three Kingdoms of Darkness chapter possess strange dicepools associated with the level of mantle and who seem to be based with the first edition mindset of Skill/Attribute + Wyrd and not the Attribute + Skill + Wyrd of second edition ;
                    - the Rose Courts from the Requiem for Regina chapter lack court contracts and court approachs ;
                    - the Contracts of the Blade from the Lily, Sabre and Thorn chapter are first edition while the rest are second edition ;
                    - the Contracts from the Grimm chapter lack the exceptional success section and seeming affinity. Also, their dicepools seem to be based with the first edition mindset of Skill/Attribute + Wyrd and not the Attribute + Skill + Wyrd of second edition ;
                    - the Grimm seeming from the Grimm chapter lacks the non-Clarity bonus of his blessing who has been previewed.
                    It's just plainly confusing.
                    I thought I recalled hearing that Dark Eras would be the last first edition material for Changeling, which would have been fitting and more easily accessible, since 2nd edition isn't finalized yet. It appears that that isn't the case.

                        The Lily, Sabre and Thorn chapter is clearly intended to be first edition, but it's not the same for the other ones who are clearly intended to work with the second edition:
                        - Three Kingdoms of Darkness chapter: the courts are second edition (mention of goblin vow and a mantle intended to give bonus 1 to 5) and the contracts are second edition (they got seeming affinity) ;
                        - Requiem for Regina chapter: the characters introduced are second edition (they have not the first edition spread of kith/seeming), the contracts are second edition (and perfectly in tune with the preview we got) and the courts are second edition (bulwark, goblin vow and a mantle intended to give bonus 1 to 5) ;
                        - Grimm chapter: the courts are second edition (and use the correct rules we got previewed), the contracts are second edition (multiples references to Beats, Integrity & Conditions) and the new Grimm seeming is clearly second edition.
                        The problem that I have is that the mechanics of this three chapters are intended to work with the second edition of changeling, but they are not in check with the latest development. And what make it worse (and feel like there was some kind of mistake that can be fixed) is that there are Contracts (the ones from Requiem for Regina) and Courts (from the Grimm chapter) that use the correct second edition rules that we know for now.
