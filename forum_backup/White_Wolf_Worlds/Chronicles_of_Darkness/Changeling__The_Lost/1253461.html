              A verderer is made for hunting, not head-to-head combat. They are built to be scary for their capacity to be anywhere and look like anybody and ignore the weaknesses common to the fae and act as the manifest will of one of the most personally terrifying things in specific changelings' lives. They are seldom operating on such a tight time limit that they can't afford to pick their moment and regroup later. Every Huntsman is effectively a high-Potency fae Horror with no frailties, immunity to iron, relatively unlimited humanoid shapeshifting, the ability to turn predators into proxies, and regularly-accessible gear that gives them 8-Again to use it and lets them recover Willpower when they do so.

              The scope of available Dread Powers is such that you can customize a Huntsman about as thoroughly as you can any hobgoblin, and the specific wheelhouse of the Huntsmen includes power options that are specifically troublesome for changelings, like ferreting out their Touchstones or inflicting Paranoid or just showing up out of nowhere. Making use of less specialized Dread Powers opens up yet more options — a Huntsman with Reality Stutter or Mist Form Immortal or Hypnotic Gaze or Lethe's Embrace provides its own forms of threat even if it isn't sporting a sledgehammer of exceptional quality.

              The defensive game for Huntsmen is basically identical to the defensive game for changelings: Avoid damage by not being there when the check bounces. What Title does this Huntsman bear?

                                      In terms of a straight slugfest Natural Weapons gives the creature +1 damage and +1 armor piercing to a maximum of 3. Unbreakable (the dread power, not the atavism) makes them nearly immune to damage unless the attacker rolls an exceptional success. If that's too powerful/swingy for you (and Cyclopean Strength might render it useless if the Beast is Lair 3+), Werewolf had a Natural Armor dread power that was 1-3 dots of armor.

                                      Changeling has some pretty powerful combat contracts. Red Revenge is a massive physical buff (+3 to physical attributes, 3/3 armor, among others) with the downside of not being able to focus on anything except attacking the nearest living thing. Talon and Wing grants easy access to agg damage if the Changeling already has lethal unarmed (possibly from the 2 dot Lethal Mien merit). Elemental Weapon can be really, really potent. It creates a weapon from a nearby element, which has the regular stats of its kind plus an extra buff to damage, initiative or range per success. With a good roll you can arm yourself with a +5 sword that... shoots fireballs or something? I'm not sure how adding 20 feet of range works with a melee weapon, I'm imagining the sword lasers from Zelda.

                                              Besides the direct combat powers already mentioned, changelings have access to a number of Contracts meant to make combat a non-issue or tilt things in their favor through trickery. Paralyzing Presence inflicts the Insensate Tilt, Unravel the Tapestry gives you a second chance at avoiding death, and Leaping Toward Nightfall simply neutralizes an assailant for a few days. Might of the Terrible Brute drains an opponent's Strength (or Dexterity if you're a Beast), while The Royal Court stops combat from happening in the first place. The Common Spring Contract Dreams of the Earth puts a target to sleep for a few minutes--time enough to set up a coup de grace. Or you could use the various emotion-manipulating powers of Court Contracts to make your target not want to fight you.
