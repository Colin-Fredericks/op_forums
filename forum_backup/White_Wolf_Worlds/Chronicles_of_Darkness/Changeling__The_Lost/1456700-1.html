                        I am hoping to see more ideas for major folktale heroes and villains reimagined as changelings. Below is my idea for Mordred as the Winter King in the U.K. I am currently editing his stats, but plan to eventually send as a potential character for a Night Horrors rule book. I have already turned in Edgar Allen Poe, and hope more people will send in their ideas as well.

                        While there are many retellings of the death and aftermath of Arthur and Mordred, here is its true telling. As Mordred lay dying, the King of the Tuatha De Danann, Nuada of the Silverhand, spirited him away to Arcadia, for he believed that Mordred could be the tool with which the Bristish Isles were returned to the Tuatha De Danann and made a part of Arcadia. Mordred was transformed into a terrible creature with the strength of an ogre, yet who flows like shadow around mundane and magical defenses. He was also trained in fighting styles that were ancient by the time the Tuatha De Danann came to Ireland. He was to be the champion who would drag not only Ireland, BUT ALL of the Isles; England, Scotland, and Wales into Arcadia. Yet before he had been a rival for the crown of England, he had been a hunter dedicated to the protection of mortals against the forces of darkness. It was the vows that he had taken as a member of the Order of the Black Knight, that allowed him to break the magical compulsions and controls the Tuatha De Danann had on his mind, and fight his way out of their hollowed hills and back into Britannia.
                        When Mordred returned, the seasonal courts were in disarray from the death of King Arthur and the fall of Camelot. Hundreds of former subjects of Camelot were being dragged into slavery in Arcadia. Mordred came to the halls of the Silent arrow and declared himself its new King. He knew that neither Spring nor Summer would accept him, for they blamed Mordred for King Arthur’s death and the fall of Camelot. Yet Mordred was determined to do good and protect the people of Britannia, whether Changeling or mortal. He made pacts and alliances with Morgana Le Fey and Nimue, two of the oldests and most powerful members of the Autumn court. For the next hundred years, Autumn and Winter would be the most powerful seasons in Britannia. The Ashen court would use their secrets of the occult and knowledge of the magics and laws of Arcadia and the Hedge to keep the True Fae and their armies at bay. The Silent arrow fought like a wounded and hungry wolf during winter. They launched guerrilla attacks against loyalists, racketeers, and even vampires of the Legion of Green and their Green Knights. Eventually, The Spring court grew brave and hopeful enough to be joy and revelry, while the Summer court rallied and slew many of the terrible beasts of the Hedge, including the Questing Beast, which foretold Arthur’s death. It was the servants of the True Fae’s to feel fear and sorrow. Stability was returned to Britannia and the Seasonal courts.
                        In this way, Mordred became one of the unsung heroes of the British Isles.

                        Thanks to the works of writers like Sir Thomas Malory, Mordred is reviled as a traitor, spoiled brat, and criminal by most changelings and mortals. Despite this, Mordred has spent that last 1,500 years protecting the British Isles from not just the True Fae, but also Vampires, Pure tribe werewolves, evil mages, and ghosts.
                        Mordred has three frailties that greatly affect his action.

                        His first minor frailty compels him to protect the British Isles as long as he lives.
                        His mien is so monstrous that mortals that gaze upon it suffer lunacy.
                        Changelings that gaze on his mien suffer disquiet.
                        To protect against this, Mordred wears the armor of the Order of the Black Knight.

                        Mordred’s mien is truly a monstrous sight to behold. He is powerfully built and often mistaken for a member of the Beast or Ogre seeming, though he is in fact a Darkling. His skin is jet black/pure obsidian. His pure gold eyes glow like that of a wolf’s, his ears are large, pointed, and doglike, and his mouth is filled with razor sharp fangs. Shadows literally cling to him.

                            Although Robinhood is revered in a heroic figure who stole from the rich and gave to the poor, in reality he was a well born cut throat, who used the common people for his own ends.                                                 Avalon is something I did actually use in game, but some what of a different take. Essentially, it's the oldest known Western Freehold. I kinda wanted an old changeling the dreaming vibe, mixed with Lord if the Rings to an extent. Here's kinda a summary.

                                                Avalon, the Fortunate Isle, land of beauty and youth

                                                Nobody really knows how old Avalon is. According to it's historians, it has existed since prehistoric times. They point to the Journey West, an inexplicable feeling among some changelings that by travelling west, across the waters, that they will find Paradise. Hyperborean, Avalon, Tir na Nog, the Blessed Isle. It was all of these things, more and less.

                                                Some point to it's fusion of Garlic and Norse roots, and believed it was once a Freehold on the mainland, at a fusion point between the cultures that would later colonize Britain. Indeed, the Avalonians believe themselves to be the Tuatha de Danaan. When life on the mainland became too dangerous, even with their bargains with the Sun and the Moon, they created a colonizing force that first took Britain, and when the Journey West called them, to Ireland.

                                                Almost all scholars in Avalon agree that it began with the Seelie and Unseelie, day and night, courts. It is said that then, the twin Kings of Avalon, Avallach (whom the freehold is named for) and Annwn ruled in an infinite war, and had control over the mortal tribes they lived amongst. It is said that Viking Invaders, even thousands of years later, recognized the divide of Light and Dark, and many of their changelings found Avalon. Legends of the conflict between "good faeries and bad faeries" still echo to this day.

                                                The Avalonians are unsure of when the seasonal courts were born, but remember when they adopted the Seasons, in the days of Joseph of Arimathea and the Holy Grail. It is said that Avallach became King of Spring, Annwyn became King of Winter, and a mighty changeling known as the Morrigan became queen of Autumn. The first Queen of Summer is unknown, but many suspect it to have been Nimue, Morgause, Ygraine, or Arthur himself. The legends of this period are sadly broken, and mostly written my human survivors of the "war of Camelot" and a further Journey West.

                                                As these conflicts pressed on, Avalon tightened up. It witnessed the war between Arthur and Mordred. It witnessed Morgana le Fay evolve from a changeling into one of THEM. It felt the ways of man pressing ever closer, a danger to their freedom. And so, they once again took the Journey West

                                                Some avalonian scholars believe they left with the Vikings to find Iceland, Greenland, and eventually Vinland. No one is sure. Others say the first American colony of Avalon was Roanoke and Croatan. Sadly during those ages, as well as much of Avalon's history, information is lost.

                                                Most scholars agree, however, that Avalon was a strong presence in New York by the time of the American Revolution. They had already integrated Norse Elves with (what would some day become the UK)'s ideas of fairies. But as more and more native american changelings warred with them, or we're adopted or colonized by them, Avalon evolved. It adopted elements of Animism, and remembered that the time of elves was over. And so, they prepared for the Journey West.

                                                They eventually began the Oregon Trail. And you know how many people died of disentery (XD)

                                                Now perched in the forests of the Pacific Northwest of the United States (and Southwest of Canada), they are looking to their future.

                                                Through most of history the Journey West took them to undiscovered lands. Now, they are beginning to wonder what is next. Hawaii? Japan? Russia?

                                                Aavalonian culture is based on everything they've adopted from the Greeks, the Celts, the Anglo-Saxons, the Norse, the native Americans, the Egyptians, the African Americans, the colonizers, the industrial revolution, the Victorian era, the modern hippes and new age movement. Avalon has evolved, but it hasn't forgotten itself. To this day you can still find an elven maiden on a bridge singing a mournful song in old Finnish, or a grumpy mechanic who'll fix your car as long as you don't mind his gobbledegook mishmash of curse words. Byou can find Hollows that were made thousands of years ago and moved, as well as a hollow built yesterday by the crazy entrepreneur that no one believed was possible. It has long since grown too big for a single island to encompass it,and so it has spread in the Forest of the PNW, and changelings still feel the call to "go west". Already, Japanese, Hawaiian, and Russian changelings are a regular sight in Avalon, as well as envoys from China and Korea.

                                                Avalon doesn't know it's next move. It was only a few hundred years ago that they realized their "colonize and acculturate and adapt and adopt" mindset became disturbing to the monarchs. They are unsure if the Journey West has ended, or if they must take another step forward.

                                                Tonight, their culture is like renfaire and comicon crashed together. While most citizens know better than to dress that way in public among humans, an avalonian court function has everything from ancient Greek and gaelic styles, to Victorian and steampunk, and even now cyberpunk. Native American, African, and some hints of cultures further West like Korea, Japan, China, Hawaii, Australia, etc have been slowly making their way into Avalon. The Avalonians adore them, and are beginning to realize that they've survived long enough to hold a place in the world.

                                                Emissaries from Tokyo have begun to arrive, as some Avalonians keep going west seeking something new. Avalon itself may be solid, but they can't go around the world and expect to find no one there. Theyre faced with the truth. "What IS the West" is a question many are asking. "We don't have the right". "We deserve to help whoever we can, even if they don't understand us" "who are we to say any of those things?"

                                                Avalon is in turmoil. It's hard to be that big and powerful without pissing someone off. Avalon has been built on the backs of so many cultures that it cant even excuse itself anymore. How tightly it's caste system (based on seeming and power levels) is is just one of it's many problems. While none of it's founders are known to exist today besides Morgana and Nimue, Avalon is still not sure if it's leaders are the same old changelings that rules before whether literally or metaphorically.

                                                Is Avalons time up? Can it be fixed? Should they try to journey West again? Do they need the support of ancient immortal changeings? Why does Morgana have such a beef with them? Was Arthur real, can he come back? Are you happy with your place in this supposed "perfect" changeling society? Are you still Lost?

                                                Edit: I designed this freehold to be kinda.. bad. Ish. On one hand, it's a colonizers. On the other hand, it adopts and loves and accepts what it comes across. I wanted a freehold that is beautiful and powerful like the elves in LotR, but I also wanted to have the players question their values and what they've done to survive.
