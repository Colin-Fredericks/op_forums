                        Lorelei’s Diary

                        Wednesday night, just before showtime. Okay, so after the rather morose ending of my last journal entry, I took stock of my situation and decided that I really did need to try to get a bit more sleep. Emotional turmoil over Marguerite’s situation notwithstanding, I was physically, mentally, and emotionally exhausted, and Spring contracts can only do so much. So I crawled back into bed with Maria and allowed myself to drift off surrounded by the heat of her body and the smell of her shampoo.

They’re going to take her to the clinic, but she doesn’t want to go. They thought you might be able to help, if you’re up for it.”

Then she turned back to me. “I’m not sure what it was you dealt with last night, Ms. Grace, but Rowan is displaying symptoms similar to a changeling who is having difficulty adjusting to life outside of Arcadia… life away from her Keeper,” she elaborated. “It’s not quite the same thing, of course, but we should be able to employ similar techniques to help her through this. Don’t worry, we have a lot of experience with cases of this type. If you’d like to take a seat, I’ll come and speak with you as soon as I know more.”

I’ve been trying to practice the lingo a bit to get a little closer to Maria, who is sincerely worried that I won’t like hanging out with her friends (and eventually family) if I can’t understand what they’re talking about. It’s going to take time, but we’re probably looking at days of recovery rather than weeks. We’re going to keep her here, if that’s all right.”

Helping changelings deal with traumatic experiences is their job, and they’re really good at it. I’m sorry I can’t stay here with you, but I don’t want to get in the way of you getting better. But I promise I’ll be there for you once you’ve fully recovered, okay?”

I had a date with Joan and Andromeda that night, so I wasn’t going to be in the show, which left my afternoon relatively free. I had just made up my mind to see if Calamity wanted to put a little more polish on our number when the doorbell rang.

I dropped into a curtsey and moved aside to let her enter. The deference seemed to amuse her on some level, but I could tell that she had come on serious business.

I would also like to meet with the resident members of my Court. And finally,” she paused and turned her occasionally unsettling gaze in my direction, “I need to speak with you privately, Lorelei.”

                        “Yes, my dear,” Charlotte replied. “Is there somewhere we can speak privately?”

                        Calamity nodded us toward her office. “I’ll let Julia and Luna know you want to speak with us,” she said as she moved toward the stairs. “And… what about Insincere Marguerite?”

                        “I need to speak with you about Oleander,” she interrupted me abruptly.

                        My heart sank. “What about her?” I asked.

                        Charlotte sighed. “Last night she called me, asking for permission to distribute a very potent poison. I refused to grant said permission, citing the need for further investigation into the circumstances, and she immediately asked me if this was a request or an order.” A look of half-amused disbelief settled on her lovely features. “She said she wanted to know exactly how she would be defying me, so that she would know how much trouble she would be in for.”

                        I lowered my eyes. “Dammit,” I whispered, “Your Majesty, I’m so sorry. I never meant for Oleander to act against your wishes. Everything was happening so quickly, and I was anxious to do what I could for Rowan, and Madame Spidertoes suggested the chemical…”

                        Charlotte raised a hand and gently placed it on my shoulder. “You are not in any trouble, Lorelei,” she assured me in soothing tones, “and neither is Oleander. I told her that I would make it a request. But I wanted to speak with you about her defiance and what it might signify.”

                        Charlotte considered the question. “Oleander has been allied with the Autumn Court for quite some time now, and she has always been an exemplary subject. Her work has been top-notch, and her loyalty beyond reproach. But – and I intend no slight when I say this – before last night, I would have described her as something of a cold fish. I have never before known her to act impulsively based upon emotional mandates.”

                        Eventually the Queen turned to look me in the eye. “I have considered this situation carefully, and have come to the conclusion that Oleander’s actions may have less to do with some change in her personality, and more to do with you, Lorelei. You inspire an unusual level of loyalty in those around you.”

                        I nodded soberly. “I’m blessed with good friends and faithful lovers,” I agreed, “but I swear that I never intended to convince her to go against a royal order. I’ll be more careful in the future…”

She is a capable ruler, and she truly wants what is best for everyone in the Freehold, but the pressures of the monarchy are wearing on her, and I’m not certain how much longer she will be able to bear them. I’d like you to consider stepping in to take her place.”

But I think you would be suited to the role, and I would not see Jolie burdened any longer than necessary. While her relationship with Desiree seems to be alleviating some of her stress…”

It was fortunate that she was up to spot Yvette.

                                                            It is still not known exactly what Yvette was able to see either. Calamity saw her when she was heading out, not the other way around, so we don’t know where in the house she went before she was spotted. It is likely that she was here to spy on Insincere Marguerite. It is also possible that she was here to take something or to leave something behind. We’ll have to be extra vigilant in the days to come to see if anything is missing or if something was left.

                                                            Later, at breakfast we all had leftovers from the Thanksgiving meal, as is tradition. It was pretty good. Cold turkey sandwiches are the best. Anyhoo…we brought up the idea of getting cameras again with Insincere Marguerite and she agreed that it would be a good idea. Luna offered to go out and get them and I said that I’d go with her. This was shortly after Lori and De La Rocha both left to go have lunch with Lori’s father, the minister. I was struck again how lucky she is to be able to do that. I hope she realizes that. Though, knowing Lori, she does.

                                                            Shopping for cameras was not as bad as I thought it might be. We went to a security store, which is not a huge spot for Black Friday shopping, so it was not nearly as busy as other places were. Parking was still stupid and the traffic was horrible, but the actual shopping wasn’t that bad at all. We got a few cameras to put up behind stage. They are not too obvious, but are not completely hidden either. It will be known that the area is being filmed by anyone that walks backstage and happens to look in the right places. We will also of course tell the performers that the cameras are there and why. We wouldn’t want them to spot them at a later time and confront us about it. It would look shady on our parts, but if they know from the get go why the cameras are there then there will be less problems.

                                                            As we were getting back from shopping Luna and I spotted Felicity pulling up in her car. We walked slowly so we could meet her at the front door and see what she needed. She asked to speak to whoever handles the financials for the Dream House. I will admit that I was curious, but I led her to the front room while I went off to go look for Calamity. I told Calamity that Felicity was here and the two of them went off to go talk in private. Felicity left a short time later.

                                                            Lori and De La Rocha got back to the Dream House and revealed that everything had gone well with the lunch. They all talked and Lori explained that she was in a relationship with Insincere Marguerite and Julia as well as De La Rocha. Apparently her father took things pretty well. I got the impression that he had some reservations about the whole polyamory thing, but not enough to cause any problems. The other thing that she told us was a little more troubling. She said that her father had received a letter from his “daughter,” the fetch. Apparently it arrived shortly after Lori first made contact with her dad. So it kind of sounds like her fetch is aware that Lori made contact or felt compelled to reach out to her estranged father. Either one is kind of troubling.

                                                            During all of this Insincere Marguerite, Julia, Calamity and Gawain had been decorating. Luna and I joined in when we got back. It was fun to just hang out with friends and decorate for the holidays, and wear Santa hats. Marguerite was really in the Holiday spirit and wore a nice Santa hat. She found one for me and some for the others. Lori’s had mistletoe tied to the bobble at the tip of the hat. She collected several kisses. Insincere Marguerite was especially excited in general and had too much fun slinking along on the walls to decorate those hard to reach places. Calamity joined her on the walls and between the two of them they got a good bit done. Gawain just started handing up decorations to them and letting them hang them. It was an efficient way to get things done if you ask me. We had decided early on that we were going to do public decorations for the guests and employees to see and enjoy and then do a separate tree just for us later. I think the idea was passed around that we might could ask Oleander to help us find a tree from the Hedge that would look like it had perpetual snow on its branches. I thought that sounded like a great idea. As much as I do not want to be reminded of my time with the Lady, I kind of do miss the snow and the crisp cold air. Winter always seemed so clean and fresh to me. I miss it sometimes, especially here where it’s so hot all the time. But this is where my sister is and where my motley is, so this is where I am.

                                                            While we were decorating and after Lori and De La Rocha joined us, Julia mentioned that her own parents had a very open relationship with another man. Apparently they were friends with this man before and after Julia was born. Julia did some looking into this man, that she remembered as a childhood family friend, and found out that he is a successful professional artist. She said that she had suspicions that this man was her actual father. It would explain where she got her artistic talent from, since neither of her parents are creative in that way. I do not know if she is going to talk to her parents about this or not. I guess time will tell.

                                                            Calamity also revealed to the motley that Felicity had decided to reimburse us for some of the cost of the Thanksgiving dinner. We did spend a good bit of our own money for food and drinks for the feast. We were more than happy to do so and were not expecting recompense, but I will admit that I was not upset that she offered to help offset the cost of the dinner. She also said that Felicity wanted to bring Leonidas to the Dream House to talk to Insincere Marguerite and maybe work things out between them. Calamity made sure that sure that Julia and De La Rocha would behave themselves while Leonidas is here, whenever that is.

                                                            **** **** ****

                                                            Today is the big day. Nightqueen Skitters arrived not long ago with a woman named Lizette. The original plan was to just skype with them, but they arrived at the Lighthouse not long ago. Together they made an imposing pair. Nightqueen Skitters has a rather intense look about her with really long fingers and nails. She seemed fixated on faces and asked some of us if she could have ours. When she got to Gawain she stopped to consider before offering him a face of his own. We all politely declined giving up or receiving faces. Lizette on the other hand introduced herself but was otherwise not all that chatty. She is definitely Nightqueen Skitter’s bodyguard though. She came armed with a pair of chainsaws across her back. They seemed almost huge given her short stature, but I don’t doubt her ability to wield them. Once introductions were over we asked if they knew were Yvette was. The pair looked honestly unsure and stated that Yvette had left the Paris freehold a little while ago and they weren’t sure where she was. We told them that she had paid us a visit in the early morning hours the other day and they seemed genuinely surprised. After that the interviews started. Nightqueen Skitters talked to Insincere Marguerite first. Their talk didn’t take too long and Marguerite didn’t look too upset at the end of it. Though she seemed to be closing off her usual expressive face. I suspect it’s just nerves.

                                                            After Insincere Marguerite, Nightqueen Skitters and Lizette talked to Julia. Julia was keen on being the second person that they talked to and we didn’t see any reason to hold her back. I believe initially we weren’t going to have Julia talk to them given her rather submissive attitude when it comes to Insincere Marguerite, but she was very interested in talking to them. It would have looked strange if we’d prevented her from talking on her fiancée’s behalf so we didn’t. After that we all had private interviews in turn. Lori came out of hers with a pleased look on her face. I think that she’s still nervous since a decision has not been made yet, but behaved as though things went well for her. When it was my turn I told Nightqueen Skitters and Lizette that I trust Insincere Marguerite a great deal and believe that she is a good person. I acknowledged that she had done things when she was in Arcadia, but that most of us had done things that we’re not proud of while we were there. I told them that I truly believe that people can change from what they were in Arcadia and become better people, different people. I also said that people need a second chance to change or otherwise they won’t be able to. I believe Insincere Marguerite has been given a second chance and is making the best of it. She has been doing an admirable job of turning her back on what she once was.

                                                            I know more than most how hard it can be sometimes to completely change yourself. Or at least go back on years of habit or training to become someone new. I served the Lady for years loyally. She was my sovereign and I loved her. Finding out what she was really like was hard, as was leaving her. I didn’t really have another choice. I did not want to go back to what I had been and she wasn’t going to take no for an answer. So, I had to leave. I have been given my own second chance and I’ve taken it. I have done my best to change my ways and turn my back on everything that she stood for. What I stood for, for years. I do not deserve the chance that my motley has given me. I do not deserve the leniency that Boss Lowjack has given me, but I will admit that I am selfish and I’ve taken it. I have embraced the chance that my friends have given me and am doing my best to become someone new. Times have been hard lately, but I’ve been trying. I believe that I can do it too. I have the support of my friends. I have the love and confidence of my girlfriend Luna. That more than anything I think has really helped. She is like me in many ways and understands what it is to change. Here’s hoping that Nightqueen Skitters understands as well and sees that Insincere Marguerite has truly become someone new.

I dozed, and it did not abandon I. I awake hearing talk of I, so I joined them in the kitchen. All but Julia. I told them I was fine. It was sort of true. Everyone was concerned. Yvette could hurt I. It seems likely. Luna insisted on cameras, and went out with Jacob for them. Which was nice. I need to speak to Julia.


                                                            Ghosts


                                                            Two women rush through the winding paths of the Hedge, both are pale, one perfect porcelain, the other lean and feline. Together, they escaped Arcadia. Together, they had overcome the odd mossy hobgoblin that sought to make a meal of them. Now, exhausted, they find a safe place to hide and get a little sleep. They traded watches. Blonde and blue eyed, the lean one watched first. The Hedge was beautiful at night. They had traveled some way, but compromised to move part at night part day, and she hoped to be home under the next cover of darkness. She gazed up at what probably were not stars, but labored to stay awake. A glance beside her showed her why: the perfect features of the woman who meant more to her than anything, who she risked everything to bring with her while she fled. A few hours later, she woke the doll-like woman beside her, and curled into a ball to sleep next to her Dearest, who watched over her much as she had.

                                                            She awoke alone. At first, she panicked. Was her Dearest in danger? She looked for signs of a struggle. Then she noticed Yvette’s things were gone. She had been abandoned. She was alone. Her face went from shocked to devastated slowly. Did she even care enough to escape now? Insincere Marguerite sank down to her knees, into the shadow of the tree, skinny arms around her skinny body. A breeze blew across her, and she began to weep.

                                                            Blue eyes opened. She was in the Dream House now, approaching the “henhouse”. She entered the private hall the family lived in, and knocked on her door.

                                                            “Julia?”

                                                            The door opened. Julia stood in the room. She had been crying, and she had a suitcase. Insincere Marguerite stood, disbelieving, at her fiancée packing and sobbing softly. Leaving her.

                                                            “Julia what are you doing?”

                                                            “I’m…I’m getting out of your way, Insincere Marguerite” Julia didn’t look up.

                                                            “What? No. No no no no no!” The Darkling raised her voice, hands out in front of her. “Why? Why would you do that? Why would you leave me?” Fear was in the Lurker’s eyes, the pitch of her voice raised.

                                                            “You have found your Dearest. Yvette.”

                                                            ‘But you are…”

                                                            “I will not be number two in your life, Insincere Marguerite. I am sorry. I can’t. It was hard enough to compete with a ghost, but I certainly can’t now” The taller woman turned away.

                                                            “You are not…you won’t…I proposed to you, Julia.” The French Changeling struggled to keep her words in English. They barely came at all. “That proposal did not hinge on not finding Yvette.”

                                                            “Do you love her?” The Artist was tense, almost cold.

                                                            “Yes.” Insincere Marguerite looked thoughtful. “I am honest with you, so yes. Well, I love the woman I knew, before returning from Arcadia. And the woman she knew, she no longer really exists, whether she loves or hates her.” A shrug.

                                                            “So I am in the way.” The Nightsinger’s tone was even.

                                                            The Darkling was shocked again. “Julia Lynn Oldacre, you are not ‘in the way’. You are the woman I shall marry, and spend a long life with. Hopefully a life we spend with Juliette and Maria. But I will be with you regardless. Maybe I can make something with Yvette. Maybe I can’t. But if Yvette cannot accept that you will be Mrs. Insincere Marguerite, than I shall turn her away.”

                                                            “You are not serious.” Julia walked over.

                                                            “Have I ever lied to you?” Insincere Marguerite looked defiant. She also looked up; Julia being half a foot taller she had little choice.

                                                            “God Marguerite I…Insincere Marguerite I…”

                                                            “You may call me just Marguerite if you wish my love. You and the family. You have won my heart. You have earned it.”

                                                            The Artist clasped her Fiancée to her chest and began to weep again. Happy tears. They fell on the Tunnelgrub’s flaxen hair.

                                                            “I won’t leave you.”

                                                            Insincere Marguerite smiled, listening to Julia’s heartbeat. Today was going to be a good day. They would decorate for Christmas they would be together. The Wyrd had thrown everything it seemed to have at her, preying on her Vices, losing her best friend, a temptation too great, even this old flame reappearing with probably sinister motives. Insincere Marguerite was unbowed. She knew as long as she had her family she could survive anything.
