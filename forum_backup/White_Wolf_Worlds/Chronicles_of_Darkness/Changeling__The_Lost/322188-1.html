            Just as a teaser, here are most of the Darklings:

            Sidebar - Harvesting Glamour from Emotions: A Changeling may attempt to harvest Glamour from another character's emotions. They may not harvest from the Gentry, the denizens of Arcadia or the Hedge, or from other Changelings (a bitter reminder of their inhuman state). They may only draw Glamour from a given character once per scene, and each Changeling who has done so earlier that scene imposes a cumulative -1 penalty on the roll to harvest.

            Harvesting Glamour requires that the Changeling make a roll appropriate to their methods; an Ogre might use Strength + Intimidate when attempting to draw strength from fear, while a Spring courtier might use Presence + Persuasion to... coax some Glamour out of someone. A Changeling may harvest Glamour in combat - doing so is an Instant action, and therefore takes up their turn.

            A Success on a harvesting roll gives the Changeling 1 Glamour; an Exceptional Success gives them 3 Glamour. If the emotion is especially sharp, intense, or consuming, the Changeling gains an additional point of Glamour. Conversely, attempting to draw Glamour from an especially weak or transient emotion applies a penalty from -1 to -3, and any Failures are automatically Dramatic Failures.

            Each Changeling Court has an emotion associated to it; whenever a courtier harvests Glamour from their Court's emotion, they gain an additional point of Glamour.


            Antiquarians: Keepers of dark lore in darker libraries, Antiquarians benefit from Archivist's Diligence; they gain a +3 bonus to all Research rolls made in light no brighter than a candle.

            Gravewights: Blessed (or cursed) with the ability to interact with spectres, Gravewights possess the Medium's Gift; they may hear, see, and speak to Ghosts in Twilight.

            Leechfinger: In Arcadia, they were the hunger hidden by the dark; even after their return, they retain the Hunger for Life. Whenever they successfully harvest Glamour from an emotion in a way that involves physical contact, they may choose to deal 1L to their victim. If they do so, they reduce the amount of Glamour they gain by 1 and heal 1 HL of Lethal damage.

            Lurker: Back in Faerie, they hid in the shadows and fled from the unblinking eyes of their captors; they still can Flee like Shadows, imposing a -2 penalty on all attempts to perceive them while they're in the dark.

            Mirrorskin: Mirrorskin were shadowy spies or infiltrators during their Durance; they still have Flesh like Clay, allowing them to mold their appearance. This grants them a +3 bonus on any Wits + Subterfuge rolls they make to disguise themselves.

            Nightsinger: Nightsingers serenaded and cajolled in the dark; with their Nightingale's Voice, they could sing with preternatural strength and clarity. Whenever the Nightsinger sings in the dark, their voice carries twice as far, and they gain a +2 bonus on the Expression roll if the song is intended to provoke an emotion or entice the listener.

            Razorhands: Razorhands were used as murderers and assassins amongst the Gentry (though those deaths never stuck) - they still have their Faithful Demodands. As such, a Razorhand may pull a mundane Knife out of the shadows as a Reflexive action; if the knife is separated from them somehow, it returns to the darkness the first chance it gets.

            Tunnelgrub: Some unlucky Changelings were buried in the dark and forced to find their way; they can still Wriggle and Writhe, allowing them to act as if they were 2 sizes smaller for the purposes of fitting into tight spaces.

            I have most of the Beasts covered, as well as the above Darklings, and a few of the Elementals. My basic philosophy is to hand out a passive benefit, because your Kith is what you are, not some power you're drawing on.

                            Alright, I'm musing on a different route for Kiths; I looked at what I worked on, and it felt too... compartmentalized. So I decided to experiment with a system of custom Kiths.

                            Every Changeling suffered through their Durance differently; as much as a Keeper may attempt uniformity, their "wards" always end up diverging in some key point or another. Regardless, they all changed and were changed through-out their long stay in Faerie; their monstrous or magnificent Miens are a testament to that change.

                            Each Changeling develops changes within their Mien; these changes are usually lumped together with similar changes in groups referred to as Kiths, thematically linked collections within a greater Seeming.

                            System: After selecting their Seeming, a player should answer the following two questions:

                            • Why was [Character's Name] stolen away?
                            • How did the Durance change them?

                            Using their Seeming and those questions as a guide, the player and their ST should sit down and go through the following steps together.

                            1 - Describe The Character

                            The player should describes how they envision their character's Mien. Are they a mountain of animate snow? A rakish man with a smile that's FAR too wide? An old hag whose hair is liquid sunlight? If they are having difficulty, the ST should certainly toss in suggestions. It's important to keep in mind that the Strangers lack imagination; it is perfectly OK to describe a character's Mien in terms of a favorite fictional character.

                            2 - Decide on a Mechanical Bonus

                            After deciding on the character's Mien, the player should select one of the options off the following list.
                            • The character gains a +3 bonus on rolls within [circumstance].
                            • The character adds +2 to a Resistance Trait (or applies a -2 penalty to another character's roll) within [circumstance].
                            • The character adds +2 to a derived trait within [circumstance].
                            • The character adds or subtracts 2 from their Size or an Attribute for the purposes of [circumstance].
                            • The character has a +0L Brawl weapon built into their Mien.
                            • The character may conjure a specific Size 1 melee weapon in [circumstance]; it decays into moondust and dreams moments after it leaves the Changeling's hands.
                            • The character has Armor 1/1 built into their Mien.
                            • The character may inflict a specific, minor Personal Tilt on another character with an appropriate roll.
                            • The character may, when harvesting Glamour from Emotions in a way that involves physical contact, transfer a point of Willpower or a HL full of Bashing or Lethal damage to or from their target.

                            The circumstances in which the bonuses occur must be narrower than the circumstances where they can get a bonus from their Seeming. While there can certainly be overlap, they should not always coincide.

                            3 - Rounding Everything Off

                            After deciding on the hard mechanical bonus of the Kith, the player and the ST should come up with a "narrative convenience", a minor power that should emphasize what the character was taken for. Whether its a Fairest whose voice carries further than it should, an Elemental who can talk to the rain, or a Beast who can breath underwater like the fish they were (and are), it should be some small but flavorful addition to the character, representing an inhuman feature of their Mien.

                            4 - Wrapping it Up
                            The player and the ST should then work on a quick one or two sentence write-up of the exact benefits the player's Kith gives them, giving the powers a thematic name. The ST should take note of it; they may want to use it for an NPC at some later point.

                            Optional - Pre-Built Kiths

                            An ST might desire a little extra control on the process of selecting a Kith, or could simply wish to push a theme. In that case, the ST should predesign a few Kiths, skipping over step 1. Then a player just needs to pick a Kith and describe their character. If their group had previously constructed a Kith, the ST should feel free to hold on to it and offer it as a pre-built Kith.

                            Theory - The Boundaries of Kiths

                            Kiths are ultimately subsets of a greater Seeming, and therefore should be related to the overarching theme of the Seeming. Beasts should be ultimately be animals, for example, while Darklings should somehow tie back into the sheltering darkness.

                            Examples:

                            • Ted is a player in Rita's Changeling game, and he wants to play a Beast based on an Owl. When he describes, his character, he emphasizes the wide, unblinking eyes and the strange silence of his feathered body.
                            Mechanically, he chooses to give Mr. Hoots (Ted's terrible at names) the following Kith:
                            Nightstalker
                            These Beasts were predators in the night, hunting down their prey in terrible silence. They are still a Silent Killer; they impose a -2 penalty on all Perception rolls made to locate them based off of hearing. In addition, as long as there is at least starlight, they can see as if it were bright as day.
                            Rita could then hold onto the Nightstalker Kith and offer it to players in future games.

                            • Beth is another player in Rita's game, and has decided to play a Fairest; moreover, she's decided that her character was taken to Faerie because of her radiant smile. After some deliberation, Beth and Rita come up with the following:
                            Shinesmile
                            These Fairest often look fairly ordinary until they crack a bright, toothy grin. They can always show of Those Pearly Whites, a smile so bright it can strike someone blind. By rolling Presence + Expression - 3, they may give someone the Blind tilt in both eyes until the beginning of their next turn. Their teeth are also always immaculately white, and they never develop cavities or need a crown.

                            I'm pretty happy with the versatility here; a group should build up a repertoire of interesting Kiths over a few games, or could even have a brainstorming session before a chronicle where they make up a few Kiths.

                                    And Mantles!

                                    Being a member of a Court grants access to certain perks; beyond the normal "not being found by the Gentry" benefit, courtiers also gain one additional point of Glamour whenever they harvest Glamour from a particular emotion, dependent on the Court in question.

                                    However, certain true perks are hidden behind a Court's Mantle, a metaphysical cloak that a Changeling can draw around themselves.

                                    Mantle (• - •••••)
                                    Prerequisite: Membership in a Court
                                    Effect: Characters may add their Mantle dots to Social rolls they make with regards to other characters that are a member of the Court. In addition, they gain a special benefits as their number of dots in this merit increase:
                                    [1]: The character's Mien begins to shift slightly, showing their adoption of their Court's Mantle; they may learn their Court's Contracts as Affinity contracts, and gain their Court's Minor Boon.
                                    [3]: At this point, the character is recognizably a member of their Court; they gain the benefits of their Court's Kith in addition to their original Kith, though they get to choose their "narrative convenience".
                                    [5]: The character is the pinnacle of the Court's representation; they are eligible to be Crowned, and gain their Court's Major Boon.

                                    Spring Court
                                    Emotion: Desire.
                                    Court Contracts: Eternal Spring, Fleeting Spring
                                    Minor Boon: The Courtier gains a dot of Striking Looks related to beauty or desirability; this can allow them to have Striking Looks •••.
                                    Major Boon: The Courtier always starts off with a Good Impression when using Social Maneuvering on someone that they haven't met before.
                                    Court Kith - Bloomchildren: This Kith belongs to the Spring Court, and is inevitably developed by those that delve deeply into its secrets. Universally lively and enticingly "innocent", Bloomchildren benefit from brief Spring Flings; the character gains a +3 bonus on any roll to entice or seduce a character they've known for less than a day.

                                    Summer Court
                                    Emotion: Wrath.
                                    Court Contracts: Eternal Summer, Fleeting Summer
                                    Minor Boon: Whenever the Courtier spends Willpower to add dice to a Strength roll, they add 5 dice instead of 3.
                                    Major Boon: The Courtier is immune to the Beaten Down condition, and have an additional Health Level.
                                    Court Kith - Summerbloods: These Changelings are filled with anger and passion of Summer, driving them forward, motivated by Purest Rage; the character benefits from Armor 1/1 from the rage in their skin, turning aside blades and bullets alike. This armor stacks with any actual armor they may wear.

                                    Autumn Court
                                    Emotion: Fear.
                                    Court Contracts: Eternal Autumn, Fleeting Autumn
                                    Minor Boon: Whenever the Courtier uses Wyrd Sorcery, they add a +2 bonus to their roll.
                                    Major Boon: An Autumn Courtier using Wyrd Sorcery ignores the first Twist on their spell.
                                    Court Kith - Spookshows: Autumn Courtiers are scary, and they know it; perhaps its their blank eyes, scaly grins, or jutting limbs? Whatever the case, it can't be denied that they take Ghoulish Delight in fear; the Courtier may give another Character the Cowed Tilt using social skills other than Intimidate.

                                    Winter Court
                                    Emotion: Sorrow.
                                    Court Contracts: Eternal Winter, Fleeting Winter
                                    Minor Boon: Other characters who spend Willpower to enhance a roll resisted by the Courtier only gain one extra die instead of three.
                                    Major Boon: The Courtier may ignore the Extreme Cold tilt entirely.
                                    Court Kith - Chillhearts: These Changelings are filled with sorrow, both for time lost in their Durance and for those who haven't yet escaped. Their brows are heavy, and their eyes are always filled with tears, because they are Drowning in Misery; any Perception rolls to notice the Changeling made by characters that aren't specifically looking for them take a -2 penalty.
