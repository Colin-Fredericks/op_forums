                  I've managed to come up with some base ideas for part of it, having the "story" the freehold bases itself around being the Revolutionary War.
                  Location: Washington DC

                  Name: The Capitol

                  Freehold Base: Revolutionary War

                  Local Slang:
                  • Redcoat: Loyalist
                  • The British are coming: Gentry are launching attack
                  • Tar and Feathering: Organized operation by the Court of Liberty
                  • Founder: Court Ruler
                  • Kings: Gentry
                  • Revolution: A new Court Leader is appointed
                  • Congress: Meeting of Court Rulers
                  • Valley Forge: Disaster
                  Courts:
                  • Court of Liberty (The Sons, The Spirits, The Snake's Head, The True-Born):
                    • Base: The Sons of Liberty
                    • Emotion: Outrage
                    • Role:
                      • Organizers
                      • Messengers
                      • Spymasters
                      • Smuggling
                    • Symbols:
                      • A Divided Snake
                      • Crossed Muskets
                      • Two Vertical Stars
                    • Rituals:
                      • The Tea Party: An organized festival centered around the destruction of captured items deemed a threat to the freehold
                      • The Revere: A race that occurs every seven years on April 4th, one part race and one part scavenger hunt the winner becomes the new court ruler
                  • Court of Freedom (Revolutionaries, The Minutemen):
                    • Base: George Washington
                    • Emotion: Patriotism

                                      I was just struck with an amazing idea about the courts.

                                      The court of Fidelity is lead by the courts greatest tactician, as they serve as the freeholds primary defencive fighting force and a good fighter would be better off serving on the front lines.

                                      The court of Unity is actually lead by two Lost, it's greatest researcher and it's best diplomat, as to reflect both sides of Benjamin Franklin.

                                      No Court holds any specific territory within the freehold other than the respective seats of power, and they all help keep the others in check and from holding to much power.

                                      This makes the courts function like a weird reflection of the U.S. government.

                                          This is a minor point, but Washington, D.C. didn't actually exist during the Revolutionary War. It was established and built in the early 1790s to be the seat of government, almost a decade after the War ended. Of course, the war and the founding fathers still make a good theme, but it may alter the feel of the theme to know that the Freehold had no history in the war itself (or, alternatively, that the Freehold moved from Philadelphia or Boston when the city was established).

                                          Also, I like your ideas about local slang. Have you considered "Georges" as an alternative to "Kings"?

                                                  One part of the setting you might want to consider, too, is the local Hedge. Having worked in several DC government buildings, I can tell you many are miasmas of cubicles and hallways. Many are frequently having their internal arrangement rearranged, which can further further add to a feeling of confusion and disorientation -- not unlike what I would imagine the Hedge to be like. I don't think it would be unusual for particularly bureaucratic goblins, hobs, and the like to be plying their sundry trades in such a Hedge.

                                                          The story I have in mind is about the Freehold's interaction with the world around it, only the court of Fidelity is truly combat focused, as it is the Freehold's primary fighting force. Washington would be a location of particular importance for a large number of supernatural factions, and as a result the relations between the Capitol and say, the Vampire's Sabbat would be strained as they both compete from the shadows for influence. The Capitol is so immersed in the Revolution because it was built by Lost who fought in it, they migrated from the other New England Freeholds as the town was being created. the Freehold itself I think would pay particular attention to Human society, at least to help maintain a connection with what they lost. They also most likely have pledges with and/or ensorcelled members of the federal agencies that base themselves in Washington and members of the government.
