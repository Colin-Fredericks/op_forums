I don’t think Liam would try and find me with the intention of doing me harm. He was always the reasonable one. He was keen on trying to sway me over to his side, and renounce the Lady. Too bad it didn’t work then.

I told him how they were acting and what happened with them. I suggested that they send someone into the Hedge to see if they reacted to someone that was “Light shy” or if they reacted at all if someone just walked through their ranks without intending harm. Lowjack seemed to think that this was a good idea, but suggested that it not be me because I had coaxed a response out of them the last time that I went to the Hedge. I also told him about Veronica and our ideas concerning the possibly new Changeling. He said that he had Dahlia already on it. I was heartened to know that my Monarch was already on top of the issue. That meant that we wouldn’t have to investigate ourselves. I’m sure that Calamity was with me when the idea came up to investigate Veronica, but Lori was decidedly not. She seemed to think that we should take Veronica at face value and believe that what she said was true. I am generally not one to believe that what is said is necessarily true until it is proven to be so.

Hailey was at the bar and I decided to take a chance and ask her about the interesting rifle that she had with her yesterday. She acknowledged the gun and said that it was a disintegration rifle. I said that sounded interesting and asked her to let me know as soon as it was available to the public. I don’t expect it to be available to someone like me for a while, but am still interested in it. Granted, I am more of a knife kind of guy, but am willing to entertain the odd gun here and there.

We seem to have more and more escapees from Arcadia flooding in, and not all of them appear to be from the Director. My gut tells me to be wary of anyone that is new just because of past history with the Director and his programed people. I don’t know what to make of Carla yet though.
