    Legendary?

    04-23-2016, 05:06 AM
    There have been some questions about introducing Epic Level/Legendary Changelings capable of confronting the True Fae head on. This made me wonder which mythical characters or historical figures could be introduced as Epic Changelings?

    Here are some suggestions:

    King Arthur Draconic Fairest Monarch of the Summer Court in Britain.

    Merlin Wizened Oracle and the Grand Vizier in Britain.

    Edgar Allen Poe as monarch of the Autumn Court...(idea for his story) He started as a fae touched, who gained the fairest seeming in his quest to rescue his wife Eleanor from a True who ruled a kingdom in the sea.. As such.. I see her as having the Nix kith. The Edgar Allen Poe who died in drunken poverty was Poe's Fetch.

    Jack the Ripper as Razorhand Darkling of Autumn Court... They never caught him, because he had been whisked away to Arcadia.

    Vasilisa the Brave as fairest seeming (I am in Beijing, China so can't read Google documents of kiths) of the Day Court.

    Koshchei the Deathless as Giantkin Oger of Day Court

    Rasputin as Darkling of the Night Court.

    As why they would still be alive.... I can see Arthur gaining immortality from drinking from the Holy Grail, Koshchei and Vasilisa from a pact made with a True Fae (Baba Yaga), Poe Potentially using a contract or pledge with death/ True Fae to gain immortality.

                      Edgar Allen Poe's story has developed a bit more in my mind. He was contacted by an emissionary (A loyalist or hobgoblin) of a rival True Fae of the Sea King who revealed that his bride Eleanor who had died was really a Fetch and Eleanor was in the clutches of the Sea King. After entering the Hedge and surving it's many dangers, Poe came to the Sea King's domain and confronted him. The Sea King was very amused by this fae-touched who demanded his bride back, and intruiged enough, that he said he would let he and his bride leave free if Poe could survive the horrific challenges the Sea King gave him. Drawing on his skill as a writer, Poe was able to defeat and even kill the many huntsmen the Sea King sent after him and even solve the complex puzzles. In doing so, Poe gained the Author kith. He and Eleanor returned to the mortal world in time to make his fetches funeral.

                      Poe is currently one of the most powerful and respected Autumn Monarchs in the Southern United States. Despite being over a hundred years old, his mask and mien have the appearance of a man in his early twenties, leading some to whisper that he gained immortality through a pledge made with one of the True Fae or Death, itself. He is very old fashioned in his manners and perspective, despising modern technology. He refuses to use a computer or ride an automobile.

                                                      Along with your kith blessing evolving, I also think you seeming blessing should evolve. As I understand it, your seeming is how you made your identity your own again. It makes sense that as your wyrd evolves through your choices and actions, your seeming would also evolve and become more obvious too.

                                                      Here’s my idea for how the fairest seeming evolves.
                                                      Wyrd 6, the fairest gets a +1 in all manipulate, persuasion, presence, and socialize rolls.
                                                      Wyrd 7: The characters is immune to all penalties in appearance (they don’t suffer from bad B.O., they can walk through a puddle of mud and not get a speck of dirt on their shoes or clothes, and they don’t need to spend hours in front of the mirror grooming themselves) and all social difficulties are halved.
                                                      Wyrd 8: all difficulties in persuasion, presence, and seduction are reduced to zero. When a character succeeds in any of these actions, they can give a target the swoon and inspired conditions, or the swoon and infatuated conditions, or the swoon and wanton conditions, though no more then two conditions at once.
                                                      Wyrd 9: The character is immune to all fear affects and all attempts to get the fairest to betray their allies or friends fail.
                                                      Wyrd 10: The Fairest and his allies can stand up to the most terrifying of True Fae and be unafraid. The Fairest can give his allies the same immunity to fear affects and betrayal that they have. They also get a +3 in finding their opponents weak point. The True Fae suffers a -5 penalty in laying a blow on the fairest or their allies.
