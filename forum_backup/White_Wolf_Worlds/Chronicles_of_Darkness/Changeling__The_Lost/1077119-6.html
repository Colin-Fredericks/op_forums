                                                Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
                                                Driven more by narrative and/or whimsy that math and "sense".

                                                Hard to concretely explain.
                                                Based on what Rose has said it's influenced but not controlled by stories and narratives in the real world. It's worth keeping in mind that that in no way limits things to fairy tales, though even if it did, those stories have pervaded every culture and nook and cranny of our globe and for every one you've heard there's probably a million you haven't. Goblin knights is a pretty good example of that, as both are pretty much straight from fantasy stories and in between invasions and terrorizing cavemen they would probably need something to do to keep busy.

                                                In this it doesn't seem much different from the old Hedge, which was also supposed to have these things, but examples of which tended to be less bizarre, at least in appearance.

                                                I'd guess that with the existence of these hedge ghosts, things will be more dangerous. One of the key elements of those 'don't stray from the path' stories was that there was something to tempt or otherwise lure travelers from the path. Visions of lost love, endangered family or friends, treasure or imminent threats. First edition had the danger element of going off the path, but it really didn't add any reason to. The Thorns were bad, thus don't enter the Thorns. Hopefully the ghosts, or something, will offer pitfalls so the Thorns can actually come into play beyond boundaries to 'ring out' opponents.

                                                Another guess is a big reason to go there is likely to be tied into the players Durances and backstories. You may not know what to do now, but when you're looking at four stories detailing the growth of the player characters you'll probably be able to come up with something suitable.
