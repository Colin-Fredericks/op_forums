                Oh, since people asked and it was fresh in my mind:

                The Lost Courts
                Two other Courts faded with the times. The first was the Cowry Shells, named after the Cowry Shell Born of Swallows. The second was the Jeweled Branches, named after the Jeweled Branch from Horai. These were the other two tasks given to suitors by Kaguya Hime in the Tale of the Bamboo Cutter.
                The Jeweled Branches were a highly animistic, spiritual group. They dealt with ghosts and the natural spirits. They never really fit in with the freehold at large, and indeed many simply ceased to participate or fled the region some time in the late 17th century, citing disgust with a curse the Lost placed on the dead of Honshu.
                The Cowry Shells represented decadence and wealth. They believed themselves natural nobility, deserving of praise and gifts. The Court existed largely to perpetuate itself; members selectively recruited those who could bring further riches into the order. They embedded deep within aristocratic families. They fell out of prominence in the late part of the nineteenth century, shaken heavily by the changes during the Meiji Restoration. Then, after World War II, the last remaining members pledged allegiance to other Courts, overwhelmingly the Dragon’s Jewels.
