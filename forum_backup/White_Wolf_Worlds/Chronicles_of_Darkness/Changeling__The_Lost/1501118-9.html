        Originally posted by Seregnore View Post
        Well, in any situation there size of health track becomes a concern I would take an ability to wake up incited of dying.
        If you are going into a situation where your Clarity is at risk, it is to your advantage to not be forced out of the dream faster because you got spooked too many times. Not every fight is to the death, but most setbacks cost time to make up.

        Also was not able to find anything about spending additional Glamour on using gates of ivory (other then taking extras with you) - and using gates of horn requiters you to spend Glamour on entering the Hedge, and locating them - a separate can of worms and risk on itself.
        Oneiromancy costs Glamour, finding the exit to a Bastion is substantially trickier without it, and you definitionally have to find an exit to a Bastion if the starting point on your journey to the Dreaming Roads is your own Bastion.

        About social attributes - it seems that characters with high social attributes would just use oneiromancy more often.
        Yes, and characters who don't have high Social Attributes still have cause to mess around in dreams.

        Another question about attributes there are a lot to mentions of Resolve + Composure rolls, some of them are unique to the dream form. Would it be a Composure*2 roll? Or there are some exception I overlooked - for me changing Mental attributes for Social is a bit odd.
        Use the character's Resolve + Composure. They still have those stats and the roll in no way benefits from how a character's Dream Form interacts with the world.

        And is there description who and why uses Social attributes and who uses mental - there are only changelings and others mentioned, but what about Others? Fetches? Are everyone in a same bucket as mortals?
        Things that are not changelings are others. If you're using another monster type with specific rules for Dream Form (e.g. mages using the higher of their Mental and Social Attributes, the Begotten using their highest Attribute in each category, mummies using various combinations of Pillar ratings, etc), those entities use their respective rules.

        Changelings use Social Attributes for their Dream Forms because the world they inhabit is an interpersonal one, for a broad definition of "people," and control of emotional state and affect matter more to their ability to project power in dreams than the exercises of will and wit and intellect that characterize the more meditative approach to dream-diving mortal methods are familiar with. Mages have had their souls transformed by their connection to celestial power and thus can get the best of both those worlds; the Dark Mother's children keep their inner selves in the realest part of the Astral Realms and are accordingly able to draw on their Physical might as well as the other methods; and for the Arisen everything boils down to the various strengths of their souls.

                                                Originally posted by Omichli View Post
                                                Do Names work the same way as in Mage the Awakening with Shadow Names?
                                                No, because a mage's Shadow Name is a constructed magical identity. The only capital-N Name in Changeling is the core of a True Fae's expansive identity; the "true name" referenced in Fae mechanics is the more general occult phenomenon of a name that serves as a mystically potent identifier of a given person, which is one reason changelings tend to use a different name in their new lives.

                                                Do True Faes instinctively know the Lost's Name to be able to track them down or send Huntsmen after them?
                                                Any given Fae to which you are connected may have gotten ahold of your name through direct or indirect magical channels, but as with everything else, the Fae may want to possess knowledge of a character's name and therefore will have to go to the trouble of getting it to get it.

                                                Also, do Names affect the Changeling in some way physically or metaphysically that may shape their identity?
                                                Not by default, no.
