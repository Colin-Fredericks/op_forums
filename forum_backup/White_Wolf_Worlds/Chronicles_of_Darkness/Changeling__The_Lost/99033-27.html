                Session LVII

                Gawain’s Memories

                Nov 13, late afternoon

                Alright. This time, I think things really are back on track. After a couple more days of brooding and eggshells and pranks, things are a little easier around the Dream House. Not to where they were before or anything, but we’ll get there.

                Calamity did skip sex breakfast, probably cause she expected more wedding talk, you know? There wasn’t actually - we talked a lot more about the Dream Bastion and what all it’s for. Lori doesn’t like the idea of having to worry about being attacked in dreams but... my Keeper was the Dream-Lord, so I feel better being more safe than sorry.

                There was some kind of private conversation between Lori and Insincere Marguerite and Julia after breakfast, that Insincere Marguerite didn’t really want to have and Lori came out of really sad, but that didn’t seem to linger long. Maybe she was sad about being toxic or something, but that wore off at four, so they’re probably OK.

                The big distraction that was Leanne Sheen probably helped too. She was another Changeling from Hollywood who was a high mucky muck but defected and was offering info. The Monarchs wanted her checked for programming first though. It seemed, you know, reasonable, and she was amenable, so we’re gonna get that done tonight. It’s important to keep her safe - I’m sure Hailey's doing a good job until they return though.

                She’s really hot, with really pretty eyes and long red hair. Between that, and the fact that she’s a Leechfinger/Muse from Princess Joli’s durance, and her name... well, it’s not hard to guess why Princess Joli might be nervous of her. She did recommend to Lori to keep mortals away from Leanne specifically, too.

                Lori also talked to Princess Joli about us growing stronger. Apparently we’re super good at it, what with all the Glamour we live in and the way our Wyrd’s build off each other. Lori’s wings are re-feathering, Jacob’s got frost covering parts of him and Calamity’s a little sharper. I didn’t even notice my changes, but Calamity pointed out I’ve got a little silver gilt tracing around some designs on my armor that are more intricate than they used to be. Insincere Marguerite said she felt more powerful and it may just be observer bias, but I think she is even more double jointed and boneless than she used to be. Apparently we need to be on the lookout for, you know, frailties too. Insincere Marguerite figures her is to do with being barefoot, and Julia that hers is to not destroy paintings. I have no idea what mine is, but I guess it is good to keep in mind, in case we get weird impulses and all.

                Anyway, after all the serious talk Calamity surprised me by asking me out to the amusement park on the beach! It was awesome - as she put it, we rode rides, ate tons of junk food and lost at rigged games. Well, expect I totally won one. I smoked the ‘knock over the bottle’ game and got Calamity a big green teddy bear that’s as big as she is. Everyone thought it was adorable - I thought we were gonna have to pry it way from Julia. Somehow, the story of how I got it had Insincere Marguerite using ‘milk bottles’ as a metaphor for boobs and calling it a delicate one. Sometimes she’s really weird. Of course, then she became kitten sized and rode around in Julia’s cleavage, so maybe I’m burying the lede.

                Nov 14, early afternoon

                OK, so that was a weird night. It started off alright - it was a good crowd, Ginny showed up, Leanne didn’t disappear in the intervening hours. Hailey was there too, and vented loudly to Lori - I couldn’t hear her, cause I was still at the door, but she looked pretty upset. Still, venting seemed to help and D kept her company after Lori had to go prep.

                The show was going well, and Calamity was just about to go on to give a super awesome performance of ‘Love Stinks’ when Monica stormed in and started arguing with Hailey. When they took it outside, I took my leave from Desiree and Gloria and followed them. Monica tried to shoo me off, but Hailey asked me to stay and besides, violence was, you know, in the air.

                So... I think -between what they said and Lori’s Cliffnotes explanation between sets - Monica is dating Dominica and Hailey is worried that it’s a rebound/fling thing and that Dominica will get hurt and so she warned her? At least, Monica called Hailey a stupid jealous two-faced whore for trying to get her girlfriend to break up with her and Lori said her girlfriend was Dominica and... just, man. Anyway, Monica threatened to give her an ass full of Pontiac, and I said I couldn’t let her do that, so she stormed off and Hailey ran inside crying.

                De la Roche went after Monica and Lori went to help Hailey, so I went to ask Jacob if any of it made any sense to him. It didn’t. Calamity did say, later, that there’s anything I coulda done to make it better even if I did understand, so I felt a little better after that though. De la Roche did find Monica and threw her through a wall. After Monica tried to hit her with a car. You know, normally I’d say I’m glad De la Roche’s my friend, but so is Monica. Hailey did decide to stay the night at the Dream House, so she crashed in my and Calamity’s room while we did the dream thing in the Hedge House.

                We slipped into Leanne’s dream easy and found ourselves in a Scottish castle where her second grade English teacher/mom figure was yelling at her and a goldfish in a fishbowl watched her from every room she went into. We could tell pretty quickly she had the programming, a simple thing like Gloria’s. I had this idea to release the fish as the symbolic gesture to break her out. But it resisted and when I looked carefully, I could tell it was some kind of Incubus.

                That was the first time I’d ever seen an Incubus. We figured it was probably up to no good, and it didn’t seem responsive, so we jumped it and Calamity took it out right away. That kinda blew up the gentle manipulation thing, so we just talked with Leanne. I found a journal with her orders in it in a desk and handed it over. I guess it was kinda a test, but she moved right away to destroy it, tossing it into the spontaneously lit hearth. I promised to make a dream prison for her, in case it came back. I just hope it won’t... recorporate, or whatever it does, and immediately go tell Hollywood.

                Anyway, Calamity and I got to spend a few hours in our Bastion before she had to go train with White Maria. It wasn’t very big yet, but it was already shaping up as a great moss covered mound, with a carved stone door and hewn hallways. It was comfier than I remember the Green Chapel being, with a few more rooms, but it was a nice, peaceful place. I went back to it after she left, cause her training was pre-non-sex breakfast, which is way too early for, like, anything.

                Sex breakfast was, well, kinda bizarre, even for us. I mean, we went from Julia’s mom’s paintings of nude young ripped Elvis with a huge cock, to paintings of a nude Virgin Mary, to having sex in churches. De la Roche did promise to sign us up for hedge beast fighting training, which came up somewhere in there, and Lori stated emphatically that she did not want to defile any churches. Not even ones with padded kneeling benches.

                And now the day gets busy. Lori and I are going to check on Monica - De la Roche needed Spring Court contracts last night and Monica might too. Joan is dropping Andromeda off so she doesn’t have to be alone while Joan deals with Court business that De la Roche hopes is not beating up Day Courtiers. At some point I need to work on that dream prison - who knows how long before the Incubus comes back, you know? First is Monica though, since she might have the same business Joan does. I just hope we’re back in time, cause I need to make sure Joan’s not involved in any diurnal bullying...


                Lorelei’s Diary

                The last couple of days have been… enlightening, I suppose. They’ve had their ups and downs, but overall I think we’ve come out ahead.

                Oleander woke up about an hour after I finished my last entry to find me staring at myself in her bathroom mirror. I’m not sure if she really got what was going on, but once I pointed out the new feathers to her, she figured out why I was so interested in my own reflection pretty quickly. She must have seen how concerned I was, because she pulled me back to her bed, and the two of us cuddled for about half an hour more before we decided to head over to the Dream House for sex breakfast.

                The gang was all there by the time we arrived. Well, mostly anyway… Calamity was conspicuously absent from breakfast. She claimed that she needed to practice her new act, but I can’t help but wonder if she’s still avoiding Marguerite for some reason. Gawain tried to draw attention away from her absence by boasting about what a good liar he is… which was admittedly a funny enough assertion on his part to be mildly diverting.

                Gloria and D were sitting together and both looking pleased as punch about whatever they got up to last night. It… didn’t make me as jealous as it had in the past, and I think that’s a good thing. I think I’m getting used to the idea of the two of them being together, now that I’ve been reassured that D’s feelings for me haven’t diminished. Gloria was also sitting relatively close to Desiree. They had an empty chair between them, but it was progress, and I can only see that as a hopeful sign.

                Sex breakfast proceeded with the requisite amount of banter and teasing, and everyone seemed to be enjoying themselves. Gawain brought up the idea of creating a Bastion, which is apparently a sort of permanent fortified dreamspace which the motley could share. It would always be there, on the edge of our dreams… a sort of fall-back position in case we ever get attacked in our dreams.

                In a somewhat tremulous voice, I mentioned the fact that I’d never even thought about getting attacked in my dreams before. I never knew that it was something I needed to worry about. Gawain told me that it can happen… there are plenty of things that can slip into your dreams and do you harm, in fact. But he assured me that the Bastion would be a safe place, where I could hold my own until help came or I woke up. Oh, yeah, and we’ll also be able to slip into each other’s dreams using the Bastion as a bridge.

                I looked around the circle at all of my friends and flatly asked them to “knock first.” I have some dreams which I don’t intend to share! Well, maybe with D…

                In any case, as things were winding down, Julia took me and Marguerite by our arms and led us insistently out of the room, claiming that the three of us needed to talk urgently. I allowed it, as I figured it was going to be wedding talk, but Marguerite struggled a bit, claiming that she wasn’t “ready” to have this conversation.

                Once we had a little privacy, I turned to look at Marguerite, who didn’t meet my eyes. I really started to worry at that point, as she’s usually so bold and forceful. “What is it, sweetie?” I asked as kindly as I could.

                She started talking softly. She mentioned that she still lacks confidence on the stage. She brought up the fact that both Queen Charlotte and the Warlock of Night were in our audience last night. She said that, when she performs, she’s always afraid that something will distract her at an inopportune moment, and that she’ll mess up as a consequence. Then she looked me in the eye and told me how much it upset her when I distracted her during the show last night.

                “I know that I did the same thing to you,” she said before I could respond, “but you are a professional performer, and you are at home on the stage. I am not. And after Calamity’s betrayal last week, I could not help but feel betrayed by you as well.”

                I was floored. “Oh, Marguerite,” I said, and for once I hope she could see the tears welling up in my eyes. “Oh my love, I’m so sorry. I never meant… I didn’t even think…” I couldn’t come up with the words for a few minutes. “It was supposed to be a prank,” I said at last. “It was just supposed to be mischievous and light-hearted… like what you did to me. I didn’t stop to think about how it would make you feel. Please believe me, baby… I’m not cruel, I’m just stupid. I’d never do anything to hurt you.”

                I was almost sobbing by that point, and Marguerite looked a little teary-eyed too. “You are sorry?” she asked plaintively after a moment had passed.

                “I’m so sorry,” I said immediately. “I will never do anything like that to you again, Marguerite. Just… just say you’ll forgive me, please. I don’t want to lose you over some stupid prank… some stupid mistake.”

                She nodded after a moment, and then embraced me. I think we’re going to be okay now, but that was too close for comfort. God, I’m such a stupid, thoughtless bitch sometimes! How could I hurt someone I care about so much that badly… and not even realize I’m doing it? I guess I was just too wrapped up in my own shit to pay attention. I was too consumed by my issues with D and Gloria, and too excited about my upcoming date with Oleander, and I just didn’t think about how that sort of behavior would affect Marguerite. And now of all times…

                So needless to say, I was more than a little subdued when the three of us returned to the kitchen. D cottoned to the fact that something was wrong at once and hurried toward me, holding her arms out. I remembered just in time why that would be a bad idea. “Poisonous!” I squeaked as I scurried away from her.

                D sighed, and turned toward Oleander. “When will it be safe for me to kiss her again?” she asked irritably.

                Oleander did some quick calculations on her fingers. “She should be free of the toxins by four o’clock at the latest,” she said after a moment. “Anything before that is living dangerously.” She looked away from D with downcast eyes. “Look, de la Rocha… I’m sorry about this. I didn’t plan it this way. I try to schedule my excursions into the Hedge for times when I know Lori’s spending time with you, so that we don’t have to take these measures…”

                After a moment, D smiled again. “It’s cool,” she said softly, and then turned to me. “Are you okay?” she asked, with a hard, pointed glance at Julia and Marguerite.

                I nodded. “I’ll be okay,” I assured her. “We’ll talk about it later.”

                A few minutes later, there was a knock at the door. Gawain and I both opted to answer it, and I’m kind of glad we did. Halley was there with a very attractive redhead neither of us recognized. She introduced the newcomer as Leanne Sheets, yet another recent refugee from Hollywood. She was apparently a part of the monarchs’ inner circle, and is offering substantial intelligence on their plans and movements in exchange for her safety. Considering what we’d learned about Gloria’s conditioning, the higher-ups decided that it would be a good idea to send her to us for a thorough screening before taking any further action.

                Of course we agreed to help however we could. We asked a few simple questions (Gawain used his contracts to read her motivations, and I think Calamity might have done something similar), and agreed to look into her dreams that night to see about removing any directives Hollywood might have placed there.

                I got an odd feeling when I stood close to Leanne. I mean, she was beautiful, but this was more of a physical reaction; a certain chill in the air on the edge of perception. I might not have recognized the signs if I hadn’t spent so much time around D, but since I have, it really stood out. Whatever else might be true about our new visitor from Hollywood, Leanne is a Leechfinger. One glance at her face is enough to tell anyone with eyes that she’s also a Fairest.

                Over the course of the conversation, I discovered that Leanne and Jolie shared a Durance, so I thought it might be prudent to get the Princess’ take on her before we made any further moves. I pulled out my phone, moved into a vacant room, and hit the speed dial for Jolie’s personal cell.

                The long and the short of it is that Jolie doesn’t trust Leanne. The two of them did know each other back in Arcadia, and apparently the memories are not happy for the Princess. She is inclined against trusting Leanne, and strongly advised me to keep her away from our performers (or really any mortals) at the show this evening… though she admitted that her own bias might be coloring her view of the situation.

                As we were about to say goodbye, something occurred to me. “Princess?” I asked a little hesitantly.

                “What is it, honey?” she asked.

                “Well… I noticed this morning…” I began, then took a deep breath. “I’ve been out of Arcadia for about ten months now. I know you’ve heard my story before… how I tore up my wings on the thorns as I passed through the Hedge. Ever since then, my wings have been pretty ragged, and there’s been nothing I could do about that. This morning I noticed that new feathers are starting to grow in.”

                “Well that’s a good thing, isn’t it?” she asked after a moment.

                “Yeah,” I answered hesitantly, “but I’m just wondering what it means. Is it a sign of my growing connection to the Wyrd? If so, why is it growing so quickly? Is this something I should be worried about? I don’t want to lose myself…”

                “Hold on there, sweetie,” she said with a chuckle. “Don’t make a big deal out of it just yet. You and the rest of your motley are in sort of a unique situation. Your respective wyrds complement each other really well, and to an extent they also feed off of each other, so you’re developing powers a little quicker than most. It probably doesn’t hurt that you guys are living smack dab in the middle of a glut of regular glamour, too. You might want to talk with your motley about it; see if any of them are experiencing similar effects, but I wouldn’t worry too much about it just yet.”

                I breathed a soft sigh of relief. “Thanks for the advice. Anything else I need to know?”

                She thought for a few seconds. “Well… if your connection with the Wyrd has developed far enough, you might start to notice little behavioral quirks… we call them ‘frailties.’ It shouldn’t be anything too serious just yet, though.”

                “I’ll pass it on to the others. Look, thanks for talking with me about this stuff, Princess. Sometimes I still feel like a kid when it comes to the facts of changeling life… there’s so much I don’t know…”

                “We’ve all been there, Lori,” she replied easily, “it’s nothing to be ashamed of. Give me a call if there’s anything else you need to talk about, okay?”

                “Will do. And I’ll pass along your thoughts on Leanne, too.”

                We said goodbye after that, and I made my way back into the kitchen, where the group was avidly discussing what to do about Leanne Sheets. I added my two cents, incorporating both Jolie’s opinions of the newcomer and my own suspicions regarding her appetites, considering her shared durance with Jolie and her status as a Leechfinger. A few of my friends gave each other knowing looks at that; apparently there’s something about her name that already had them suspicious, though I don’t claim to understand the reference myself.

                Once that topic had run its course, I asked if I could speak with the motley privately, throwing an apologetic look at Oleander, Gloria, and Desiree. The three accepted the request gracefully enough. Oleander gave me a quick peck on the cheek (so as not to re-poison me) and bade us farewell, while Gloria and Desiree decided to go out for soft serve together. It looks like Gloria’s really trying to patch things up between the two of them. Here’s hoping it works out.

                So I sort of laid things out for the group as Jolie had explained it to me. Turns out that the others had noticed subtle changes in themselves as well. Marguerite has gotten more flexible (more thoughts on that later). D is ever so slightly paler. Gawain has developed this little silver filigree against the green of his armor. Calamity has gotten a bit “sharper.” Frost is now running down Jacob’s arms. I didn’t notice any overt physical changes in Julia, but she assures us that she’s felt the increase in her connection to the Wyrd too. I have no idea where the hell Ace was for this conversation.

                Only Marguerite and Julia had noticed anything like the “frailties” that Jolie mentioned. Julia finds herself unable to deface or destroy painted art, while Marguerite has become even more opposed to wearing shoes for some reason. I hope whatever frailties I develop aren’t shoe-related; I don’t know if I could bear it.

                Yes, yes… I’m a stereotypical girl, obsessed with fashion and shoes. Sue me.

                So as we were talking about frailties, Gawain mentioned Madame Spidertoes, and made a sort of pantomime motion with his hands, pretending to wash clothes. Of course this set D off like nothing else, and she lashed out at him about making fun of her ex. I put a hand on her shoulder and admonished her as gently as I could, telling her that Gawain didn’t mean to make fun or show disrespect. I think she knew that deep down, because she seemed more embarrassed than pissed after that.

                So the two of us went up to our bedroom and spent the next few hours just enjoying each other’s company. We eventually joined Julia and Marguerite in the attic, where Julia was painting. At four o’clock on the dot, several alarms went off around the house (apparently my lovers were eager for my touch… they’re all such sweeties!), and wanton carnality ensued. Julia held herself aloof at first; she spent most of her time painting the three of us as we took our pleasure.

                So the show tonight. Well, as always, the show itself was a lot of fun, though things were developing in the audience which made me sad. First of all, I noticed that Halley was there, looking even more depressed than she had the last time I spoke with her. Of course I made a bee-line for her, plopped myself down in the chair next to her, and asked her what was wrong.

                “Monica and Dominika are going out,” she admitted after a moment. “It’s a terrible idea. Monica is making bad decisions in response to getting dumped again, and Dominika is going to end up getting hurt because of it. Believe me, I know…” she said, looking so miserable that I had to reach out and take her hand.

                “I know you want to do what’s right, honey,” I said, “and I know you’re still carrying a torch for Monica, and I imagine it must hurt like hell. But there’s only so much you can do to help them out. If you’ve told them how you feel about it, I don’t know what else you can do. They’ve got to make their own mistakes.”

                Halley sniffed and nodded. “I guess you’re right,” she said after a moment.

                I left her to her own devices after that, as I was needed backstage to prepare for the show. I confess to taking a small amount of malicious glee in selecting my number for this evening: a deep cut from one of Billy Joel’s earlier albums called “Say Goodbye to Hollywood.” I picked it because I thought the title was apt, what with Leanne’s sudden arrival and Gloria’s situation and all. Gawain later pointed out to me that some of the lyrics could be misconstrued as a warning from Hollywood to those who might desert. I guess I’ll have to pay closer attention to my selections in the future.

                There was a disturbance late in the show, after my solo had already passed. Monica showed up, spitting mad about how Halley was trying to “sabotage” her new relationship because she was a jealous two-faced bitch (her words, not mine). She and Halley went out into the parking lot to talk things out, and Gawain followed them to make sure things remained civil. It’s a good thing he did. She was really mad. In the end, he had to remind her that the Dream House is a place of safety for all changelings, but she wasn’t in the mood to listen. She wanted to settle the score, and her rage kept building. I believe Monica mentioned something about hitting Halley over the head with a Pontiac at one point. Halley was obviously reluctant to let her do so, and we weren’t about to let her get hurt.

                Monica eventually stormed off, but Halley was inconsolable by that point. I felt so bad for her. I’ve seen a lot of relationships go bad over the years (okay, if I’m being perfectly honest, I’ve caused a few of those relationships to go bad, though I’m not proud of it), and it’s always hard to watch. Halley was an emotional wreck, and terrified that Monica would exact some sort of painful revenge on her if she left the premises, so we offered to put her up for the night.

Marguerite made the not-so-subtle suggestion that those two could share while Halley took the room that became open, but nobody much went for that option. In the end, Calamity and Gawain agreed that Halley could stay in their room, as they were going to be doing the dream healing thing with Leanne in the Hollow that night anyway.

It sounds like the two of them had a real knock-down, drag-out fight in the middle of a parking lot somewhere. D claims that she put Monica through a wall, and I believe her. When she got back to the house, my honey was battered and bruised and cut up all over… but she also looked very happy.
