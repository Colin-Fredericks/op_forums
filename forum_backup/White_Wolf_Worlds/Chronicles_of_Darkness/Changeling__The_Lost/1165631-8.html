    Here's a custom Kith I cooked up earlier tonight.

    Pixel
    I still can’t get over how weird it is to be using electronics that aren’t me. Mind, that’s easy enough to fix.

    The Gentry aren't known for their love of modern electronics, but not all Keepers are behind the times. Many find the digital age fascinating, enough that they wish to have their own versions at hand. Sometimes a Token will suffice, but most find that the cost is too tedious for the benefits the hardware grants. The Gentry thus find that the best way to get the true digital experience is to find someone who has experienced it themselves. Pixels are the end result, serving as either the hardware for the Gentry to enjoy their online fun, or the living software for electronics that a Keeper happens to take a fancy to. More than one programmer, repairmen and or those who spend more than an hour a day on their machines have found themselves as the new Clippit offering suggestions for what their Keeper would want to view.

    Life as a Pixel is a hard one. Even without their masters dictating what they must do, the flow of information that runs through or past a Pixel often acts like a sedative, facts, figures and fictions buzzing too fast for them to keep a hold of who they are. Sometimes such data is simply torture for them to sift through, only the looming threat of destruction keeping them going fast enough to stay ahead of the requests. Even if they don't slip up or make an off comment, however, the Gentry are fickle and may scrap or delete a Pixel for only doing what they are supposed to do, and not what their Keeper wants them to do. Others are just forgotten, turned off and left in some dusty corner of a manor filled with other trinkets and baubles to experiment with.

    Whatever their use, it is in the downtime that a Pixel often finds the time to simply disconnect themselves from their work. A Pixel that serves as a desktop may literally unplug themselves from the wall and lumber off, whereas another who served as a web browser could find herself longing for the places on the screen and remember they once actually went there before stepping past the monitor. A lack of data, a line of similar requests allowing for multitasking or even simple commands taken too literally can lead to a Pixel trying to escape their Keeper's realm. Not all make it. Those who do often find themselves fitting right back into the world of facebook and pizza Ironside has become.

    Fairest: All of her features are slightly granulated, and her eyes are back-lit screens that go black when she sleeps. She is also an avid programmer, and the first on the scene to ascertain the Freehold's digital security.
    Wizened: He is the Freehold's best hacker, and is often found wrapped up in his work. His hair is wire, his eyes are cracked cameras, and his fingers click with every move they make, but when push comes to shove he is the best at his job.

    Kith Blessing: Whenever a Pixel uses Computers to hack into or create a program, they achieve an Exceptional Success on three successes.

    Patched In: By spending a point of Glamour and succeeding a Presence + Computers roll, a Pixel may touch and patch themselves into a piece of computer hardware, be it a smartphone, an aging library desktop or even something as simple as an electronic safe door. So long as it has a computer chip in it and an interface they can utilize, a Pixel can patch themselves into it. Once they have done so, they are integrated into the system, allowing them to move the hardware as it if were their own body and reflexively access any documents or files as though they were the owner/admin of the machine. Passwords and firewalls may still stand between them and what they wish to access, but any Hacking rolls for a Pixel happen reflexively and with the hardware's own equipment bonus benefiting them, practically at the speed of thought.

    While Patched In is active the Changeling is physically attached to the hardware they are accessing, which may or may not impede movement. They can detach themselves reflexively at any time, or be forcibly removed. Forceful disconnecting requires a successful Strength + Brawl roll with successes exceeding those of the activation roll for Patched In; doing so deals a point of Lethal damage to the Pixel and, if fragile enough, could damage the hardware as well. A Pixel may also be counter-hacked if someone else infiltrates the hardware they are using, though the Pixel may fight back using the hardware's equipment bonus as their own. If the hacker succeeds, Patched In ends and the Pixel takes two Bashing damage.


    Let me know what you guys think.

                        What about making it so that any time someone tries mundanely to place a Condition on them (specifically a socially based one like Leveraged or Swooning), the Riddleseeker can spend a point of Glamour to challenge them to a riddle contest. If the Riddleseeker wins, the Condition settles on their opponent instead, but if they lose their opponent must reveal the answer to the riddle. You could call it Riddles in the Dark, and what one has in their pockets would be totally fair game. That’s just a rough idea, of course, but I think leveraging their riddles in some way to gain power over someone or squeeze information from them is the angle you’d want. Just don’t make the roll require the same Skill as their skill trick.
                        Alternatively, you could give them the ability to reveal information in a manner that people don’t understand, disguising it through the Subterfuge Skill. Basically, if someone applied Leveraged or something similar and demanded your password to resolve it, you could offer it in riddle form to resolve the Condition. They’d need to solve the riddle to gain the information.

                                            Mannikin
                                            I’ll do it for sure…But how do you want it done?

                                            The Gentry wanted something from you. And it decided the only way that was going to happen was if you could bend and flex to their design.

                                            They made you into a doll with soft plastic features and interchangeable outfits. Or maybe they made you into the archetypal dummy or stringed puppet straight out of Pinocchio. Whichever it is, you were remade into something that could be positioned to any role your Keeper saw fit. You were their toy.

                                            But not anymore. Your joints still have the ball bearings placed there so long ago but now you choose how they move and you choose what role you play.

                                            Wizened: You were made to fill a role and expected to excel at it. Time and time again you tried to fit the model given to you but was never enough and still you’re a perfectionist in whatever roll you’re to play.
                                            Elemental: You were made, remade, posed, reposed, discarded, painted, and encased. Always changing and always moving. But like a winding river, this same momentum of change was also your ticket to freedom. You freed yourself, inch by inch and pose by pose but eventually you freed yourself. You’ve been changing your entire durance and it earned your freedom, why stop now?

                                            Kith Blessing: When a Mannikin uses Expression at another’s behest or benefit, achieving three successes counts as an exceptional success.

                                            Role Player: A Manikin is adept at posing and fitting in as different roles, even ones that are someone new to them. They can spend a point of Glamour to imitate and single action or role by rolling Dexterity + Subterfuge + Wyrd. The pretend action is just that, pretend and does not actually do anything other than fool on-lookers. However, if an exceptional success is made, a +2 bonus is conferred if the Manikin chooses to attempt the action for real.

                                                    I had an idea for a kith conversion yesterday. It's somewhat straightforward while also attempting to maintain the metaphorical aspects.

                                                    Broadback

                                                    Tired already? Can't say I'm ready to quit just yet. Hop on, I'll give you a ride.

                                                    Some Gentry are attracted to tenacity and stubbornness. Perhaps they see a bit of themselves in such traits, or maybe they just want a challenge to break. Other Fae just need a pack mule or minotaur, and the other aspects come later. Regardless, Broadbacks are changelings with the traits of goats, oxen, donkeys, horses, and other hoofed beasts of burden, coupled with a strong will and enduring constitution.

                                                    Fairest: This goat-legged satyr is always ready to party. He even has this trick for opening bottles of beer on his horns. You should check it out.

                                                    Ogre: Even under the Mask, people would describe them as "built like an ox." Sure, with their broad, looming frame and long ivory horns, their more than a little bit of a bull in a china shop, but that's never slowed them down before, and won't now either.

                                                    Blessing: When making an Athletics roll to cross rough terrain, an Exceptional Success is achieved with three successes instead of five.

                                                    Stoic Forbearance: Choose a Resistance Attribute. When the Broadback makes a roll other than Perception that uses the chosen Attribute, they may spend a point of Glamour to add their Wyrd (to a maximum of +5) to the dice pool.

                                                            Something I literally just finished:

                                                            Fireheart
                                                            As the flames charred my flesh, their light burned its way into my mind. It’s still there, you know, dancing behind my eyes in the back of my brain. Do not try to hide anything from me; I will find it every time.
                                                            Even within their own demesnes, the Others still have need of fire. One True Fae might require a glorious chandelier to light a ballroom, while another needs its heat to warm their parlor. Still another might need to burn away a grove of withered Thorns to clear space into which they may expand their holdings. Such endeavors are costly, and rather than simply invoke Contracts or influences (which might be outside their purviews), many Gentry will invest that Glamour to create a Fireheart.
                                                            Mortals chosen for this Kith are invariably curious. Their eyes and minds are always open to new experiences or knowledge, and they will go out of their way to find them – sometimes endangering themselves to do so. These people are easy marks for even a remotely competent privateer, so the True Fae rarely need to involve themselves directly in the hunt, save for the manufacture of the fetch. Too often, such a mortal simply walks into their own abduction by the nose of their own inquisitive nature.
                                                            The durance of a Fireheart invariably begins in pain, as their Keeper literally burns Glamour into their flesh to effect the change. The pain ebbs, of course, but the burning never stops. Once back in the real world, these Lost usually turn their Fae-keen eyes and ears toward investigative work. Many become detectives or spies for their chosen Court. Some align themselves directly to their Freehold, using their talents to root out privateers and other threats.
                                                            Darkling: Pale blue or green flames dance behind the character’s eyes. Similar flames lick across their skin like animated tattoos.
                                                            Ogre: Wisps of smoke trickle from between the character’s teeth and constantly waft around their wrists. When angered, a red-gold fire ignites in their mouth and around their fists.
                                                            Wizened: The character’s eyes resemble lumps of burnt-down charcoal – white and gray with the dim red glow of cooling embers.
                                                            Kith Blessing: When a Fireheart uses Investigation to locate something that is hidden or concealed, achieving three successes counts as an exceptional success.
                                                            Flickering Acumen: A Fireheart’s senses are unusually keen and in constant motion, allowing them to predict movements and react accordingly. A Fireheart may spend 1 point of Glamour to add half of their Wyrd rating (minimum of 1) to either Wits-based dice pools or both Initiative and Defense for the remainder of the scene. The changeling retains the Defense bonus that this Blessing provides in any situation where they would normally lose their Defense.
