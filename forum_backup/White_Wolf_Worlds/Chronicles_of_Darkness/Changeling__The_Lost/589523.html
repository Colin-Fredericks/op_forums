    [2ed] Any mention of Entitlements, Hedgespinning, Hedge Navigation or Hobgoblins?

    07-13-2015, 05:28 PM
    With a few exceptions, the benefits for joining Entitlements were very underwhelming. I would rather have a few, well developed and nuanced Entitlements with reasonably potent boons than what we had in 1st edition. I'm not saying the Entitlements we have right now are terrible, mind you. The Scarecrow Ministry, Knights Of The Knowledge Of The Tongue, Order Of Bridgemasons, Goldspinners' Guild and Lord Sages Of The Unknown Reaches were really cool concepts.

    I always thought the rules for Hedgespinning were a little confusing. When are you supposed to include the Token Maker Merit? The process described by that Merit is arduous enough to be nigh impossible for changelings who are busy with (mostly) normal lives. I get that some would want to make the ability to craft magic items rare so that people don't say "why swear Pledges and go to Goblin Markets when you can make your own Tokens?". But it still seems excessively hard to me. It can take years to make a 5 dot Token if you don't spend lots of XP on Wyrd and Crafts. Also, can the True Fae reliably craft Tokens or not? I distinctly remember texts which said yes and no. I figured that they mostly steal ready Tokens, explore changelings with that Merit or let objects from others planes of existence be infused with the magic of Arcadia.

    My issues with Hedge Navigation are similar to those I have with Hedgespinning. Clearer and more concise rules would be great. Conditions representing different degrees of disorientation, the psycho-reactive changes of the Hedge as a whole and the effects it has on other major templates would be awesome.

    I don't think Hobgoblins need a lot of alterations, rules-wise. A few more friendly hobgoblins wouldn't hurt, and one or two Hedge Towns with good worldbuilding would also be excellent.

    Yes, yes, word count and deadlines. I know. I'm talking about the possibility of addressing these suggestions in future supplements. If we make it clear in a reasonable way that there is significant interest in this additional content, the greater the odds of it coming true.

    I could be wrong, though. Maybe there's not enough interest on either side. Your opinion is very appreciated, especially if you are working in the development of 2nd edition.

    Thanks in advance!

    Bye.

                  Sure, I can share what I know of his plans.

                  I know that he has them.

                  EDIT: As a note in regards to my current relationship with Onyx Path and on Changeling particularly, I can allude to stuff that hasn't been brought up yet, and when it comes to me sharing the results of my playtest with y'all, I can mention things via [REDACTION]. I can point at the hole with discretion. The prime example so far has been my references to something done with Pledges in the Court Creation thread-you know that something has been done with them, and there's a few pointers at the usage of the particular thing about them that I mentioned.

                  But as a playtester, it is not anywhere remotely near my position to reveal things to people. I am neither the developer nor the writers, and I cannot speak to the whens and why of the material.

                  This isn't just a anticipation building thing, this is a matter of making sure that the material is refined enough people to inherently keep from dissuading people upon seeing it. It's done for the good of the community as much as possible. Beast has been a real learning curve for me on this, because a lot of stuff I thought was fine got revised a whole lot, and among the whole heap of lessons I got from that, one of the big things is the potential backlash that could happen from releasing information too quickly. Without going into details, some of the earliest drafts on the powers on that game would have made you guys give up on Beast as a pure ball of lameness, and considering the fight Beast has had with those refinements, that could have been damning.

                  I intend to do a full re-read of Changeling 2nd's materials once the semester is over and a second review for David as a result of this experience, and I consult with him decently regularly as I setup my own game about what I can tell you is happening at my table-once we receive second drafts and I properly begin my game, I assure you I will put up an open Actual Playtest Report about it(I even plan on putting up the prologues done before hand-and if it takes too long and I get Dave's blessing, we might start playtesting with first drafts, though I'm not keen on it), and even see if I can badger my players into talking about their experience in the games. Will I point at holes not brought up by David yet? Sure, if it's cool with him, I intend to point at things to generate conversation and interest. But I'm not going to reveal anything. You'll have to wait on David and his crew to turn those pages. I don't want to ruin the story by telling things out of their due order.

                  Now, if my relationship shifts in anyway, I'll let you know what it's shifted too and what I can and won't do. But we have to get there first.

                  EDIT: For the record, none of this changes the above statement. I know he has plans, and that's it. He has mentioned using the Bloodlines framework as a stopgap for any Entitlement work I'm doing right now, but that's all it is-a stopgap.
