      The descriptions are very good, not only to visualize, but to set the tone of the place.

      But I think it's too much and too little. Too many places and too little description of what they do in the story. It's a quick, superficial description of basic activities that don't really say why those places matter in a story, how those basic activities matter to the local Changelings, what conflicts or story seeds are there to be explored. And the number may even hinder you later as you locked yourself with ten established places that may not fit the city anymore as you develop the setting/chronicle.

      You could reduce to 3 or 4 places and increase word count. Use the AI to make several versions of each if you need ideas, but alone it won't make good plot hooks, even if it may help making evocative presentations for them. I'm working with AI too, but I have to say that if you want the tool to help you with quality, you also have to accept that it isn't there to reduce your workload, just change the process.
