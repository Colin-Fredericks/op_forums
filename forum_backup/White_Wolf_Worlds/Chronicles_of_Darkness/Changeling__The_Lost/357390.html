    Life without the Masquerade

    01-02-2015, 07:44 AM
    , while a decent option for Changelings, nonetheless is not a perfect option.








    The sight of your home – what used to be your home – for the first time in years is, alone, a shock that brings tears to your eyes.
    Yet, that's no reason to turn back. You came back for a reason.
    The face of the woman who answers when you ring says all.
    “Who are you? What are you here for?”
    Judging by her face, your own appearance also says all to her.
    “I... I live here.”
    You'd prepared a speech, an explanation, but all of it has fled your head. It's only your heart and memories speaking.
    “You must be mistaken, ma'am. I've been living here for years.”
    Of course she has. You were absent, after all.
    “Look. You know... you know what happened. Well, it happened to me. This is my home.”
    This should call for further clarifications, but it hurts even thinking again about the events, about what your life became until now.
    “I don't know what you are talking about.”
    To be honest, you're not entirely sure she knows what's going on. Though looking at her face an expression alone, you are certain she should.
    “This is where I live. My home.”
    You're not sure what you expected coming here, you realize.
    “I don't even know who you are. This is my house, and I have the documents to prove it. Who are you?”
    It's not like They took your papers with them or made an effort to keep them, let alone give them back when you escaped. How could you prove anything?
    “I-”
    She eyes what fundamentally differences you from her. The marks left by this place and by They who saw you as property at best. They'll never be erased.
    “Go away, please, or I'll call the police. Don't disturb me and my family again.”
    You ponder for an instat the use of violence to reclaim your one and only Home, but what good would it do you? You wouldn't succeed anyway.
    You turn back.

    -----

    You turn the TV on.
    “- t's what you say, what we're all being told by the authorities, but how much of that is true? How can we be sure they didn't ask for it? And look at what they have today, the influence they hold over us, can you truthfully say they didn't gain anything from-”
    Channel 2.
    “-ld attacked again by a madman with an iron knife. So far, 2 dead, 5 wounded, one of which in a critic-”
    Channel 3.
    Some cartoon for small children, with fairies and gnomes.
    Channel 4.
    The trailer for an incoming movie, based very loosely on Whatshisname's best-seller, retelling those fateful years spent – no, lost – Over There. Starring Brad Pitt, Emily Watson,Orlando Bloom, and Benedict Cumberbatch in the role of the villain. None of them, or anybody in their family, has lived it, you know it. You catch yourself wondering why they didn't bother hiring instead somebody like you, sombedody who did--
    Then you remember again, if you'd been proposed the main role, you'd have refused, because the last thing you want is to relive it again in any way, and there's no reason to think you're the only one. Even if that retelling is vastly embellished, even more removed from reality than the actual experience (hah!).
    (That, and no one is that known an actor anyway.)
    In fact, it's probably going to attract Their attention. It could come back, attracted by the turmoil of negative emotions and resentments, not even having to use magic to turn people against you.
    You sigh and turn the TV off.

    -----

    You cancelled your appointment with your shrink.
    As it turns out, he wasn't really equipped to deal with the knowledge and details of how people like you, made of flesh and bones and dreams and fears, were turned into animals, objects, furnitures, just like that, and that nobody, not even you, can provide an explanation - nor a description - that sounds remotely logical.
    Nor did he seem ready to deal with hearing what you were forced to do to survive to escape, even if you are not proud of it in the slightest.
    They don't say in these books how you're supposed to deal with it. Somehow, you did, but being told about it leaves a big impact on those who never lived it.
    When you think about it, you don't really see a reason to bother with another shrink.

    -----

    On the one hand, you understand so well your friend's fear and anger, intertwined, feeding each other. The paranoia that They will come back somehow, that anybody could be on Their side and plotting against you, that this could happen all over again.
    On the other hand, the chill you get when you hear her sometimes is not something you can ignore, and she sounds and acts more and more like Them.
    Whenever you catch yourself silently cursing the innocents who blissfully live in the distant, theoretical, incomplete knowledge of that hell, whenever you see them staring at you knowing who you are, whenever one of your quirks and weaknesses reminds you you're not longer like them anymore, you fear you're going down the same road as your friend.
    It would be better for everybody to remove yourself from the rest of humanity. There are large communities that segregate themselves from what you'd call the Sane World; you wouldn't be alone if you did.

    -----

    Stop saying “never again”! You nevere really believed it in the first place.
    It hasn't really stopped happening. They believed themselves powerful enough to beat Them. But it's an illusion. They are not just individuals; they are Ideas, they are Hate, they are Inward-turned Selves.
    Let's talk about those who are individuals, though.
    They are still around, exiled, but living peacefully, free from negative consequences, free from haunting memories, free from their victims. Everybody knows They're still here, in human disguise, under new identities. Your kindly old neighbour could be one of Them.
    The government knows that, but they're not doing much to hunt them down. Nonetheless, their crimes weren't erased, even if their victims were. They could even go back to their old, inhuman ways.
    Fortunately, there are other survivors like you who can't stand the apathy and silence. You're already pinpointed the identity and location of one such criminal.
    You all will take things into your hands, if nobody else will. Judges, jury, and executioners, if need be.
    And when you're done with your exiled torturers, you will turn to those who never were pursued in the first place, and are operating in complete impunity, only because they have the good sense now to attack people no one cares about anyway.










    [Resemblance to real-life events and reactions to them is fully intended.]

    Part-time table flipper.
