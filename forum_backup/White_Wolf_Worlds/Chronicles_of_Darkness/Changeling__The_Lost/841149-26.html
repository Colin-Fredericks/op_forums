    Here is a new attempt at the wonderwell. I took away the ability to gain temporary Goblin Vows and replaced it with the ability to go into Experience debt in order to gain a new one, so that the wonderwell would still need to purchase any new capabilities gained through the pledge.

    Wonderwell

    I can make your fondest wish come true. There's just one tiny little favor you need to do for me in exchange.

    Arcadia is a place of beauty as well as terror, and all too often a desperate or greedy mortal is seduced into the waiting arms of the Gentry by the promise of untold power and riches. Some Keepers delight in the corruption of mortal wishes, while others feel that granting wishes is a fair bargain for a mortal's freedom. Wonderwells are often chosen to lure such mortals, granting their hearts' desires while slowly drawing them deeper into the Gentry's influence. Her close interactions with humanity makes it difficult for a wonderwell to forget her previous life, leading many to perpetual sorrow. Mortals who are moved to pity for their plight should be wary, though, as a wish for a wonderwell's freedom often comes at the price of their own.

    -Beast: They are covered in shimmering fish scales, with small fins at their joints.
    -Darkling: Attractive yet sinister figures, with small horns upon their heads and warm smiles upon their lips as their offer hangs in the air.
    -Elemental: Skin composed of gray bricks, with water pouring out from between the stones, and eyes that gleam like two silver coins.
    -Fairest: Beautiful men and women, clothed in nothing but beautiful jewels and sheer fabrics, with legs fading into mist.
    -Ogre: Huge and intimidating figures composed of smokeless fire, their colorful eyes promising reward and punishment alike.

    Blessing: Wonderwells are masters at navigating the tortuous network of obligations of the Wyrd in order to grant the desires of others. Each wonderwell gains two dots of the Goblin Vow Merit at character creation, and may choose to spend Glamour instead of Willpower when crafting a pledge with an aspect of the Wyrd. When entering into a pledge, the wonderwell may enter into Experience debt in order to gain a new Goblin Vow related to upholding her end of the pledge. The wonderwell may acquire more Goblin Vows than other changelings, up to a maximum of 5 + Wyrd. Additionally, whenever a wonderwell succeeds on a Social Skill roll against a character, she may spend a point of Glamour for an attempt to uncover the target's true desires. Roll the wonderwell's Wyrd contested by the target's Composure. On a success, the wonderwell learns whichever Aspiration that the target would most want known. On an exceptional success, the wonderwell learns all of the target's current Aspirations.

                        Made some updates to the Logician kith blessing. And I also reworded the Doppleganger Blessing somewhat. Critique is appreciated, as always.

                        Doppleganger

                        Blessing: By spending a point of Glamour, a Doppelganger can create a duplicate of himself. Each duplicate has a number of Health dots equal to the changeling's Wyrd, and will persist for the duration of the scene unless dismissed sooner. They also act instinctively to assist the changeling, functioning as secondary actors in teamwork actions involving a Physical Skill. The Doppelganger may also take full control of a single duplicate by spending another point of Glamour. While controlling the duplicate, he cannot engage in any strenuous activities with his own body, and will suffer a -3 distraction penalty on all other actions. The Doppelganger may only control one duplicate in this manner at a time, and have up to a number of active duplicates in a scene equal to half his Wyrd, rounded up. Each duplicate after the first incurs a -1 distraction penalty on all actions (which stacks with the penalty for taking full control), and causes a point of lethal damage to the original if killed.


                        Logician

                        Blessing: A Logician gains the Common Sense Merit, and a dot of Intelligence which can take him above the usual limitations imposed by his Wyrd. He may also apply the benefits of Common Sense once per scene, and takes no penalties to rolls which use Mental skills. If he spends a point of Glamour, he may add his Wyrd as a dice bonus to all rolls for memorizing or recalling information for a scene.

                        -=-

                        I proposed the lethal damage per duplicate killed for the Doppleganger as an additional deterrent against overusing the kith's blessing. For the changeling, this isn't some expendable tool at his disposal. It's a copy that is, for most intents and purposes, him.

                                        I am finally making the attempt to update one of my first edition kiths. Critique is welcome.

                                        Stitchborn

                                        Don't worry about me! It's just a scratch! Keep going!

                                        Before their Durances, most stitchborn had either a soft and comforting personality or a keen fashion sense. These hapless changelings were taken to be made into the bizarre fripperies of the Gentry or plush accessories to decorate their luxurious palaces. Despite the torments they suffered, most stitchborn are able to maintain their original personality traits upon their escape.

                                        Darkling: Burlap skin covers a thin frame, with stalks of straw jutting out of his raw wounds.
                                        Fairest: Her skin is made of the finest black silk, stitched together with beautiful gold and silver threads.
                                        Grimm: His linen skin is covered with colorful embroidered images of wondrous tales.
                                        Ogre: Beige mohair fur has been stitched and restitched to the ragged overstuffed figure, and one button eye is missing.

                                        Blessing: A stitchborn gains the Raiment of Moon and Stars Contract for free, and may ignore a number of points of bashing damage equal to his Wyrd each turn. Additionally, the stichborn may make attempts to repair damage to his flesh once per day. The changeling may sew or direct another to sew any rips in his flesh, rolling Dexterity + Crafts + Wyrd. Each success on this roll downgrades one level of lethal damage to bashing or repairs one level of bashing damage. This action requires a minimum of three turns for each wound that the character is currently suffering to complete, and suffers normal penalties for lack of proper equipment if the stichborn does not have a sewing kit available.

                                        New Contract

                                        Raiment of Moon and Stars (..)

                                        By transforming ordinary clothing into a Hedgespun raiment of impossible beauty, the changeling ensures that the wearer will be the belle of the ball.

                                        Cost: 1 Glamour
                                        Dice Pool: Wits + Crafts + Wyrd
                                        Action: Instant

                                        Roll Results
                                        Dramatic Failure: The clothing created is completely inappropriate, and the changeling will be unable to remove it until the sun next rises.
                                        Failure: No new article of clothing is created.
                                        Success: The changeling transforms an ordinary set of clothing into a wondrously beautiful set of finery, seemingly composed of impossible materials such as gemstones or the sky at twilight. The outfit gives the wearer an equipment bonus equal to the changeling's Wyrd for Social rolls intending to gain or keep attention. The outfit remains until the sun next rises, at which point it reverts to tattered rags.
                                        Exceptional Success: The finery created is especially durable, remaining for a number of days equal to the changeling's Wyrd. Additionally, for the first night, the finery will give a single point of Armor to the wearer.

                                        -Darkling: The Darkling may create an outfit that blends into the crowd, giving the wearer a bonus to avoid attention rather than gain it.
                                        -Fairest: The Fairest uses the higher of her Presence or her Wyrd for the bonus granted by this Contract.
                                        -Wizened: By spending a point of Willpower, the industrious Wizened may create one outfit for each success rolled on the Contract's activation.

                                        Catch: The changeling is bestowing the outfit as a gift within an unusual container that the recipient has never seen opened. The outfit persist within until it is opened, at which point its duration begins as normal.

                                                        Here is my attempt at a kith that calls upon the "Devil at the Crossroads" archetype that is sometimes found in fairy tales:

                                                        Oathdevil

                                                        Silence! I don't want to hear your protests or excuses. You agreed to our bargain. I'm just here to collect.

                                                        Woe be to those who encounter the oathdevil at the crossroads, for his friendly demeanor obscures the hidden barbs of his agreements. These changelings, chosen for their literal minds and attention to detail, were often employed by their Keepers to track and punish their wayward servants. Upon escape, an oathdevil is likely to weave himself an intricate web of influence and owed favors in whichever freehold he claims as home.

                                                        Fairest: She is inhumanly beautiful, with a lovely voice and seductive mannerisms that distract from the dainty horns upon her head and the fire in her eyes.
                                                        Grimm: His vellum skin is covered in minute contractual language, with larger signatures inked in blood foreshadowing the fates of those who would cross him.
                                                        Ogre: A large and menacing figure, with two spiraling horns and three golden hairs upon his head.
                                                        Wizened: His fiber optic tail twitches of its own accord, and his horns are constructed from the bones of an oathbreaker.


                                                        Blessing: An oathdevil is a dangerous changeling to cross, as the pledges that he creates carry severe consequences. When sealing a statement, the sealing is automatically considered strengthened. By spending Willpower to strengthen it further, the oathdevil may impose the Leveraged Condition on the subject. This Condition only lasts for the duration of the sealing, but will become Persistent if the pledge is broken. While the subject is Leveraged, the subject's resistance to the oathdevil's Contracts suffers a -3 penalty. Additionally, the oathdevil may track the subject as though they shared a personal oath together. By spending a point of Glamour in the presence of someone with the Oathbreaker Condition, the oathdevil may apply the Leveraged Condition, which persists until the Oathbreaker Condition is resolved.
