                                        Originally posted by Taveena View Post

                                        1 and 3 work. 2 is a little tricky because there's gonna be some edge cases where it's not clear how you'd combine 'em.
                                        True. Regarding method 2: some Kiths have only a passive/always on power (Author and Coyote, for instance) while others only have powers that have to be fueled with Glamour (such as Brewer and Leechfinger). Besides, I think those sort of blendings of two Kiths are probably better covered by the ST adjudication described under Modifying Kiths. As far as the third option goes, switching Kiths at will creates weird cases where characters lose attribute or skill dots when activating other powers and isn't much of a hindrance in other cases (I think it would lead to a lot of characters having a "combat mode" Kith) making it far too attractive. I think a 4-dot merit with no prerequisite makes sense, possibly with some sort of minor drawback.
