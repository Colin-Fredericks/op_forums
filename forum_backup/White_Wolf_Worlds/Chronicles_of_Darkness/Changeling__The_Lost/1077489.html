    Help me build a 2E Starting Changeling

    04-12-2017, 11:58 PM
    As the title said, I need help building a starting character from Changeling 2E's rules. I've never made a character before, so I need your help to make his stats better and some advice on what to give him to round him out.

    Jace Floria

    Concept: Terrifying Ninja
    Seeming: Darkling
    Kith: Brollachan

    Attributes: Intelligence 1, Wits 3, Resolve 2, Strength 2, Dexterity 4, Stamina 2, Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 3

    Skills: Crafts 3, Medicine 1, Athletics 4, Brawl 3 (Gauntlets), Stealth 4 (Shadows), Intimidation 4, Empathy 3 (Fears)

    Health 7
    Willpower 5
    Defense 7
    Speed 11
    Initiative Mod: 7

    Merits: Fighting Finesse (Grappling) (2), Area of Expertise (Shadows) (1), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Shadows) (1), Killer Instinct (3), Potent Kith (3), Mantle (Autumn) (1/Free)

    Wyrd 1
    Glamour 5/10 - 1 Glamour per turn

    Contracts
    Boon of the Scuttling Spider (2)
    - Dice Pool: Dexterity + Athletics + Wyrd (9 +2 if Shadows Specialty Applies)
    - Success: Allows you to walk or run along any surface that can support your weight, even along walls and ceilings
    - Exceptional Success: Gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity
    - Darkling Clause: The character is surrounded by a shroud of darkness that extends 5 meters from her body. Anyone else acting within the shroud or attempting to attack someone within it suffers the Blind Tilt.
    - Catch: The character is following the path of an actual spider along the surface he is traveling.
    Creeping Dread (1)
    - Dice Pool: Manipulation + Empathy + Wyrd (6 +1 if Fear Specialty applies, +2 if Shadows Specialty Applies) Vs. Resolve + Wyrd (an average of 2 or 3)
    - Success: Target suffers a -(Wyrd) (-1) penalty to resist fear or Intimidation
    - Exceptional Success: Target suffers the Shaken Condition
    - Darkling Clause: Target also suffers the Spooked Condition
    - Catch: Contract is invoked while in the shadows
    Night's Subtle Distractions (2)
    - Dice Pool: Dexterity + Stealth + Wyrd (9 +2 if Shadows Specialty Applies)
    - Success: For the rest of the scene, targets suffer the changeling’s Wyrd as a penalty to notice or spot her. The changeling can affect a number of targets equal to her successes on activating this Contract within a 50 ft. area.
    - Exceptional Success: The changeling can make Stealth-based actions reflexively
    - Darkling Clause: Gain an Exceptional Success with 3 successes
    - Catch: Contract is Invoked outdoors at night

    Seeming Blessing: Clarity of Abandon
    Gain a free point of Clarity by making a bad choice for others.

    Seeming Curse: A Shade's Distrust
    Lose a point of Clarity if you blindly tust anyone, even your closest friend
    Darklings never suffers penalties to their senses in the dark. They cannot see through a blindfold, but they can see perfectly with no light whatsoever.

    Kith Blessing (Brollachan)
    He gains half his Wyrd (2) to all rolls to establish a grapple (4 + 3 + 1 + 2 + 2 in Shadows) (10/12)
    Gains the 8-again quality to all Stealth rolls
    If he spends a point of Glamour once he has grappled someone, he can make a Presence + Intimidation + Wyrd (2 + 4 + 4) (10) vs. Composure + Wyrd roll. If successful, he can choose to inflict either the Insensate Tilt or the Frightened Condition on the target. A single target can only suffer from one of these at a time.



    Roleplaying Stuff

    Mien: Martyr (Would Do Anything For A Friend)
    Mask: Terror (Loves Scaring People)
    Aspirations: Be better at Stealth / Intimidation / Fighting (Long Term), Train Dual Wielding Pistols (Short Term), ??? (Short Term)
    Court: Autumn

    When he was young, Jace's life was filled with fear. Fear of the dark, the monster under his bed, of the things that lurk in the night. He was afraid of losing his friends, of being taken away from them. He always put on a brave face though, telling himself that there was nothing to worry about, that nothing was going to happen, that he was just paranoid.

    He was right to be afraid.

    He was taken by the Gentry at the age of 12. He was dragged into Arcadia as he screamed and screamed until his throat was raw, yet no one heard him.

    Either through coincidence or the Keeper’s cruel sense of Irony, He became the monster in the dark, the thing that goes bump in the night. The thing keeping his fellow captives in their Keeper’s Domain, the thing that kept them in line.

    And all through this he was still afraid, not of the dark where he lived, or the monsters that were his cohorts, or the other things that lurk alongside him, oh no.

    He was afraid of his Keeper, who forced him into this form.

    He was afraid of the Huntsmen, who dragged those few that got past him back.

    He was afraid of his friend’s rejection, the friends he was still loyal to even after all that had happened to him.

    It was that last one that pushed him over the edge.

    He ran, as fast as he could. Ran from his fellow monsters, who were hounding him. Ran from his Keeper, who would try to take him back. Ran from Arcadia, his prison. Ran from his fellow captives, who he abandoned to save himself. He ran and ran until he reached the Hedge and found a way back. He was free, he had won.

    He returned to his home after all that has happened, and found an impostor had taken his place. His friends had never even noticed he was gone. Some suspected something had happened, but the impostor was a good enough imitation to not be suspected as such.

    He caught the impostor alone and faced him. The imitation was taken aback but displayed no outward sign of surprise or fear. He knew better though, he looked into the eyes of the thing that had stolen his life and saw fear, saw confusion, and conviction. He looked into the things eyes and knew that It’s composure is simply a mask to hide from It’s fears. He looked at the Fetch in the eye and saw that he would not let who he thought of as his friends come to harm. Jace looked at the Fetch and saw so much of himself.

    He took a long hard look at himself, at who he was, at what his life had become in his absence. Old friends had changed or moved away, siblings grew up and left, mementos from his childhood were sold or given away. He looked at his life and realized that it wasn't his anymore. Hadn't been for along time.

    He would have thought that he would be angry, at the Keepers who had taken him from his life, at the Fetch that had replaced him, at the friends and family that didn't even realize he was gone, but he wasn't. He just felt empty. He briefly considered killing the Fetch for this, but realized that it wouldn't solve anything. So, he left. He left his friends, his family, his what was life before he was taken and left the Fetch to take care of it.

    Some would say that him leaving behind everything from his past with a piece of his soul was poetic. He just thought it was sad.

    Now he travels the world, trying to find a new life, a new purpose. New friends to protect, a new home to defend, and through it all he seeks the strength needed to fight against the Keepers, against the Huntsmen. He seeks the strength to protect all that he holds dear. He seeks strength so that he wouldn’t have to be afraid anymore.

    Contracts
    Mirror Walk (3), Vainglory (Mantle of Terrible Beauty) (2+), Inanimate Communion (Usage Bonus) (1+), Might of the Terrible Brute (Elemental) (2), Light-Shy (2), Paralyzing Shudder (5)
    Entitlement

                                                      Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post
                                                      I know a lot about how he got into the Durance, though. He was a foster kid, always moving from one home to the next for as long as he can remember. He was the kid who everyone said looked shifty, or dangerous, or lazy or any number of other stereotypes. To survive that kind of life, you have to be the perfect kid, obedient and a good worker. Problem was, he never really could pull that off. Inevitably, the foster &quot;parents&quot; replaced him with other, cuter children; or he punched another kid after they made fun of his old clothes and his bad grades (not his fault he'd switched school districts halfway through the first semester); or he just ran away when the home situation got dangerous. It was not what anyone would call a happy childhood, but as he walked down the streets on the day before his 19th birthday, he could only think about how much worse it was going to be on the streets. He had a high school degree, the clothes on his back, a backpack, a phone and a little bag of all the money he'd saved over the years, but he didn't have a bank account or the credit to get a loan on an apartment. He was going to be on the streets.

                                                      And this is where I kinda get lost and stuck. I have a concept for his Keeper, but not fleshed out. And I have little idea how he got caught or got out. I just have the concept of a bunch of Keepers who think of humans in the way that the worst of European missionaries thought of the native populations they &quot;ministered&quot; to. They are horrified by our primitive &quot;normality&quot; and see it as their duty to teach us civilization, otherwise known as the alien logic of faerie. So they're running a sort of Victorian Domby Hall/Work House Domain, but without any regard for Euclidean geometry or the laws of time and it's trying to teach you that you should be ashamed of thinking that gravity pulls you down instead of pushing you purple or just doing whatever the hell I want it to do.

                                                      Drew a blank on how he escapes, but his post-durance life is where i started the character, so I know this part very well. His durance has stunted his growth and made him mentally a grizzled old cigar-smoking, hard-drinking noir detective, but forever physically stuck in the body of a child. With makeup and a fake beard, he can convince the gullibe for a little bit, but he supernaturally exudes this aura of &quot;help this poor baby&quot;. He makes money doing the kind of things private detectives in real life actually do, usually finding dirt on shady husbands or looking for runaway kids. He never meets the clients, doing all his business through a site that he's promoted as an urban legend through various fake accounts on various forums. But if you really need to find someone who doesn't want to be found, you come to him.
