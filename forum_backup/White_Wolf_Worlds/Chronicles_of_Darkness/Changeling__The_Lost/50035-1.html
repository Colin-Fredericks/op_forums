        I guess the reason I prefer an application of Iron along the lines of what myself and QuantumShard stated earlier is just how stupid and arbitrary the definition of "cold iron" is. So, is blood (which has a high concentration of iron) anathema to fairies?         I mean, in folklore it is said that iron horseshoes hung above your door keeps the fairies away. Horseshoes aren't "cold forged" or crafted without the application of human supplied heat. But then again, that is a folklore thing ... why on Earth would the game designers use that as an inspiration instead of this weird new age throwback that is more reminiscent of Kryptonite than fairy taboos?

                                                Reviewing the information so far I think I am going to go with a more restrictive view on how Iron works. At least for this game that I am running.
