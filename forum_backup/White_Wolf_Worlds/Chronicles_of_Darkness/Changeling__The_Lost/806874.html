    Turning a Vampire into a Changeling

    01-12-2016, 06:20 AM
    Hey guys,

    so, in our last session of my CtL (1st ed) game something rather unpleasant happened to one of the characters. After being caught by a vampire, i rolled a percentile die to see if there would be any chance of him being sired and alas, i rolled a 99 (on a scale of 00 to 99). So, formerly a darkling, he's now a mekhet at blood potency 1 which doesn't really come in too handy when considering that most of our chronicle takes place when the sun is up.

    Here's the deal: I want to create an opportunity to turn the character back into either a changeling or a mere mortal, but I am lacking great ideas. Currently, my thought process was like this: A vampire is essentially the corpse of a human animated by Vitae, so killing a vampire would turn him into a mere corpse and make reanimation as a human possible. Only problem is that, as far as I am aware, all vampires turn into ashes once they die, thus leaving no corpse to be reanimated.

    Actually reanimating a corpse is another problem. Sin-Eaters are capable of doing so, but the group (as in, the characters) doesn't even know they exist and I don't wanna throw in another game line into what is currently a Changeling game with heavy VtR crossover elements. Mummy is coming to the chronicle soon as I have prepared a story arc using one of the Cursed as an antagonist.

    To make pondering this subject a little easier: The group consists of four characters, two of which are at wyrd 1 and one at wyrd 2 (plus a vampire, obviously). I personally treat changelings as humans who have their soul mutated by the influence of the gentry, turning them into changelings. So their bodies are actually mortal bodies plus a few positives that come from being able to use glamour, contracts, etc.

    If you have some ideas about the topic at hand, please help me out, guys

              Originally posted by Dragotarify View Post
              I personally treat changelings as humans who have their soul mutated by the influence of the gentry, turning them into changelings. So their bodies are actually mortal bodies plus a few positives that come from being able to use glamour, contracts, etc.
              The advantage here is that you can rule that mutation is still present, just overtaken by the Blood - ie, turning him back into a human will automatically turn him back into a changeling.

              Something from a goblin market that restores life to the dead... you could rule that it's powerful enough to overcome the vampiric nature and restore someone to human (and thus him to changeling). But they owe some hobgoblin big for it - or if they've paid the hob already, they owe someone else big for whatever it was - or if they stole it, that's still hanging over their heads.

              If the sun is the main logistical problem for the campaign, maybe a market will also have something that protects against it (the Sun agrees not to burn this vampire so long as he converts to the worship of a Sun god, and encourages one person a day to do the same). Or agrees not to the burn him on any day where he interacts with changelings but no vampires the previous night (essentially, if he acts like a changeling, the Sun promises to treat him like one). Or both.

                          Other ideas for un-turning the vampling (or compensating for sun-destruction):

                          -Create some kind of ritual, such as draining the changepire of all blood, then replacing it with blood from his motley-mates, and have the PCs find out about it. This could bring the victim back to life, but at the price of being tied to the life of the motley: if the motley disbands or a member dies, the victim dies, too. (Maybe it only works if you're tied to the Wyrd and can pledge to a motley, which is why other vamps haven't pulled it off.)
                          -Encourage the player to take Goblin Vow, either with the sun ("If you promise to hurt me less when I go out, I'll build a bonfire every night this week") or with something opposed to the sun, like shadows ("If you shield me from the sun, I'll knock out every streetlight in the city"). Have the player use the vow when they need to be day-side, and shift the setting slightly so being day-side is less common.
                          -Lessen sun penalties in-universe, either for all vampires, or just this one. Can the character be shielded by blankets/protective gear? Is their damage rate one lower, due to the Wyrd's influence?
                          -Have the player join up with Ordo Dracul. They've got some tricks to mitigate the sun, right?
                          -Is there a Spring courtier in the party? If the vampling gets injured for being in the sun, can he heal himself with blood from his motley, then have the Spring courtier heal the damage caused by feeding? (This one is a little munchin-y, I admit.)

                          Side note: If the player sticks with being a vampire, and is a Mekhet, but still Wyrd-touched, would he start to manifest the Alucinor (dream-pire) bloodline?

                                                  Oh, don't get me wrong, I do know (at least, a little) about Changeling the Dreaming, although I never played it. But thanks for clearing up the details, interesting to know how different these games were. And who knows, maybe I'll get the chance to try classic world of darkness at some point.

                                                  In terms of percentile dice deciding whether something unlikely happens or not, even though I do understand the notion of this being something whimsical, I feel like it something that absolutely fits Changelings mood. I probably wouldn't have went with it this way if it was a Vampire the Requiem chronicle, because vampires, to me, are very calculating and cold in their approach. So whimsical "I am going to role a die for this"-decisions wouldn't fit. To me, it's always about how the way I determine things fit into how the game should feel. For changeling, I want chance and randomness to be a huge factor. For vampire, I would not.

                                                  To return to the topic at hand and maybe expand it a little to "Can a changeling be turned into a Vampire in the first place": I made this decision in advance when I was determining the structures of vampiric and changeling society in our setting. I knew that the characters would at some point have direct contact with the Kindred and thus, I wanted the vampires to feel like, well, vampires. And one of the most vampiric things to do, besides burning in sunlight and all that good stuff, is to bite someone and turn said person into a vampire.
                                                  So, to make it short. I didn't determine whether or not a changeling could be embraced by rolling dice. I just determined wether or not the vampire in question had any interest in embracing the character because I was actually improvising the whole session.

                                                  Anyways, as it turns out, the player of the now-vampire has made up his mind that his character wants to experience being a vampire at least for a while before makin up his mind. Thing is, this won't make it easier for him to undo the whole thing if he doesn't like being a vampire. But oh well, he doesn't know
