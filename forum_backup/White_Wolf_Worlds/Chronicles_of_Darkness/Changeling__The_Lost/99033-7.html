The whole subject of Desiree was clearly tough on her since she's still in love with Jolie, but we were all in agreement that it seemed much too clumsy. The Director is far more well organized. Tommy is sure (so sure he bet MY pinky finger on it) that it's a setup and someone wants to get rid of Desiree. That's not exactly a short list of suspects unfortunately.

Honestly, if she keeps this up (especially now) and something happens... The Spring Court isn't exactly keen on some of the aspects of Charlotte and Jolie's relationship (no matter how consensual it was) and while they might not be fond of Desiree, she is still one of their own. I suggested that perhaps keeping my favorite river hag busy with some court business so she doesn't have the free time to stalk Desiree might be the best solution. I hadn't realized before that Queen Charlotte is more than a little fond of the ogress herself. The way Tommy laughed when Charlotte considered tasking her with security at the masquerade made me want to throw something at him but Charlotte had that covered.

They wanted to ask me a few innocent questions (there is NOTHING innocent about this pair. Nothing.) In the spirit of cooperation I decided to sit and chat with them for a while. (Also because I was curious to see just how blatantly sexual their shadows were going to get with my own. Fuck, I have never been jealous of my own gorram shadow before but DAMN...) They did not honestly ask anything beyond completely general questions about how things went in the area and what to expect at the ball but fuck if they weren't pouring on the seduction and charm. And they are such amazingly delicious little sweetlings... Perfect little succubi that did everything (and promised to do so much more) in such seamless unison. Fuck, I would almost expect the other to bleed if I cut one of them. (And there IS only one way to find out for sure...) Yeah, they just sat and talked but their shadows... Scarlet's was massaging mine's neck while Natalia's was rubbing my shadow's feet and sucking on its toes... Fuck it was hard to concentrate and make sure I wasn't saying anything I shouldn't like how much I would love to see the perfect bruises that their perfect skin would bloom into or hear their sweet sweet cries... Or how perfectly sweet their blood would taste on my razors... Or, you know, any court secrets. Fuck, just writing about them has me squirmy and wet again. Now I need a cold shower to go with that drink...

    Once they left (shadows linked arm around each others waist of course) I resumed my plans to call Gunnar. He was NOT a happy camper. He does not trust the Night Court in the least and he does not think this alliance is a good idea. He does not like the twins skulking about sweet-talking everyone. He told me straight up to avoid them and that if he could it would be an order (silly man, like that would stop me...) I assured him I hadn't spilled any secrets and pointed out that the game had certainly been fun to play. He was not amused. He was less amused when I pointed out that fucking wasn't what I'd be doing if I lost control anyway. Enough so that he asked me quite earnestly not to start a diplomatic incident by killing them because then he'd have to deal with me and he did not want to have to kill me. It was sweet, I didn't know he was so fond of me.

    As far as Felicity, he thanked me for checking to make sure we weren't interfering. But he assured me they hadn't been doing this. Seems Jolie isn't Felicity's first monarch and so despite her exile, they can't touch her without starting an incident (dammit, who CAN a girl stab in this fucking city?) and were actually quite content for her to be over there minding her own damn business. So the last thing they want is someone hassling her and making her seek allies and get involved. And then there is this mystery invitation that throws her back into the middle of everything. He's deeply suspicious of her and warned me about getting involved with her. And when he warned me to be wary of her 'letting' Insincere Marguerite seduce her so as to get her claws in her I had to admit that it was pretty much exactly what my darkling sister had been doing.

    So when I got home, I was horny as fuck and full of doubts about the Night Court that I hadn't had before and unable to stop seeing Charlotte's broken-heartedness and feeling for the stress both Tommy and Gunnar were under. Gawain and Insincere Marguerite cornered me. Gawain set to working my tense neck with those strong hands while Insincere Marguerite got in close to question me. I told them a bit and then gave my fellow darkling the whisper that I'd met Scarlet and Natalia. She drug me aside promptly and we spoke. She found the fact that Spidertoes had set this into motion a bit suspect as well and she seemed concerned about the twins. She suggested that they were a distraction, acting so blatantly like spies to distract from whomever was actually ferreting out secrets. She also warned me to be careful of them but admitted that they were every bit as spectacular in bed as they advertised.

I mean, so many maybe's and what if's and unclear motives. What if the handmaidens are just having some fun getting to know (and fuck vigorously) their new allies? (Hey, I can dream dammit!) And that's when Gawain started to tell me about the Contract he'd learned through his court. Holy fucking shit, it is EXACTLY what we fucking need to help untangle this shit web of intrigue. It was only about half an hour till the doors opened so I didn't bother with full sentences, I just kissed him hard and took him upstairs and had my way with him. It didn't really douse the inferno in my loins but it did take the edge off and Gawain certainly seemed to enjoy my attentions.

I think the events of the day had taken a toll on everyone because we all retried pretty directly afterward. Thankfully the Spring Court wasn't ready for Gawain to do his dream work so I was able to pick things back up where I'd left off before. Maybe I was feeling a little insecure about him being with Lori or a little guilty about how hungry I was for those handmaidens but I threw myself into showing him how good I could be for him with gusto. He didn't stop me but once I'd worn us both out he just... he gathered me close and he held me. No one else can do that without making me remember the Whispering Voices. After every punishment They would gather me broken and bloody into Their arms. Their touch was cool and soft and light but Their strength was there. They would pet my hurts and brush away my tears and stroke my hair. They would whisper how much They didn't like having to hurt me... how much They wished I could be good... how no one would ever love such a naughty girl....

    But They were wrong. Gawain does love me. I am a monster, a vile, twisted, horrible little thing that delights in hurting people and breaking things and he knows it. He knows and he loves me anyway. I cannot understand how or why but he does. And I fucking need that so much it scares me.

It is likely that someone in the Freehold or close to it, is manipulating Desiree to get her in trouble, or is using her because of who her Keeper was. Calamity also reported that the break-ins at Felicity’s house were not done by the Summer court. She talked to Gunnar and he assured her that drawing attention to Felicity was the last thing that the court wanted to do. They were perfectly happy with her not being involved in any Seasonal court business. I think that they are afraid that if she becomes involved too much in the Seasonal court business that it might bring down scrutiny from the Day court, or others. And since Felicity used to be the consort of the Lord of Day, if anything bad should happen to her in our town then payback would be rained down on our heads. Pleasant thoughts, yeah?

    After that whole conversation happened, Insincere Marguerite asked to talk to Calamity in private. A few minutes later they both came back. Insincere Marguerite told us of two young ladies that were affiliated with the Night court could be dangerous and that we should avoid them. Their names are Scarlet and Natalia. I have not yet met them so I don’t have anything really detailed or specific to say about them. I only know that Insincere Marguerite was warning the rest of us about those two young ladies. She also mentioned that she does not know what they want, so their motives are as of yet, unclear. I will admit to being curious as to their goals, but am not all that eager to meet the two young ladies that have caused quite the stir. Maybe I can just avoid them altogether?

    It would be nice if things were clear, but they are not. Nothing in life is ever clear it seems. Somebody is manipulating a prominent, if reclusive, Day Courtier. Not sure why yet either. Whoever is manipulating things around her is good. What is the Night court up to? Do they have something to do with the break-ins? They could be trying to draw attention to one of their own so they have an excuse to be involved in what’s going on here. Then there is also the person(s) who is messing with Desiree. It seems like they are trying to make her look like a Loyalist, but to what end? Do they just want her discredited, or do they want her dead? And who is doing the manipulation?

    All of this is enough to give anyone a headache. As if my thoughts aren’t full enough lately, I need to add this to the pile. I would love to chalk it up to a pile of political bullshit and be done with it, but I know that it’s not as simple as all that. If I want to keep my head above water, so to speak, then I better start paying attention to all this political BS and figuring out what is going on. Question is how to do that, keep my motley safe from threats local and not so local, and try and make up for at least a little of what I’ve done. Good question, huh?


    Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

    It’s been busy, and I have been unable to focus on my dancing. Julia and I did sneak down to the stage when the others were asleep, put on some Nightmare Before Christmas and waltzed around a bit one night, but that is all. Sadly.

    Calamity was practicing today during the day, and besides, Desiree came by. Julia and I were going to practice some more (as much as she decries romance, she loves it when we dance especially old-fashioned. She gets a dreamy, distant look and long smile) Desiree was looking for Gawain, who was apparently seeking to regain some lost masculinity by having sex with Lori. Silly Gawain, it has nothing to do with that. Simply.

    Not that I blame Gawain for sleeping with Lorelei, but pursuing her right away like that does nothing for his dignity nor respectability, both of which he seems to desire to cultivate. Apparently.

    Desiree has a big problem. She does not remember the previous evening or morning, time that she apparently spent in intimate company with Jolie. And here I wanted to be the only one that happened with. She was terribly upset (I would be too) and concerned she really was a Manchurian Candidate for her Keeper. Gawain, Lorelei and Jacob decided to promptly sell her out. They were nice about it, but she is under lock and key awaiting a chip in her head. Note to self: If I think I may be being controlled by a Gentry do not, under any circumstances, tell Gawain, Lorelei or Jacob. Absolutely.

    Jacob and I checked her house, and found only that she had gone out after dinnertime as the daylight people reckon it and returned in the late morning.

    Once Desiree had been cuffed and carted off for telling the wrong people, Calamity headed off to talk to Queen Charlotte, about other things. Flores had arrived, and despite her ill timing sought to help us prepare for the ball. Jacob and Gawain took her up on it, so Julia, sensing there would be no midnight practicing, snatched me off to practice on the roof. Waltzing on a roof is interesting, and a dance floor will probably seem easier. Definitely.

    Once Calamity returned (and probably assumed Julia and I were a bit out of breath for the wrong reasons) she reported Tommy Rawhead and Charlotte suspected Desiree was being set up. That does make sense, though I am unsure that it is the case. I am not prepared to say for sure. It just seems unlikely someone mind controlled her into bedding Jolie and then forgetting it. To what purpose? I mean, it’s not common Director behavior, as I understand it. No, I suspect we don’t know what is going on, and I am not positive Desiree is not lying due to suspicion cast on her as a result of her bedding the Princess. Possibly.

    I also discovered something slightly troubling: Scarlet and Natalia are in town. The Warlock of Night’s “Handmaidens,” I spent time with them just before the incident with the Lord of Day’s house. They are excellent lovers; I learned my techniques involving underarms, inside of the knee, and behind the ears from them, techniques that my lovers most appreciate. I shudder to think about my time with them, but I shudder more that hearing they were in town, I wanted to throw myself at them. I will not be out of control. Absolutely.

    I don’t know why they are here. Calamity said they were sashaying around the Lighthouse asking questions. Surely anything relevant the Warlock can simply ask. She is not afraid to. I wonder at their intentions. I’d suspect they are here to individually seduce each single monarch, but I have never even seen them go to the bathroom separately, much less have sex. It does make me concerned about just what they are planning.

    I am also worried their hot passions were part of what spurred I toward my assault on the home and dignity of the Lord of Day. I wanted to please them, and…I don’t think I should discuss it further. I don’t think they set me up, but I do recall Felicity’s words. Clearly.

    Calamity also noted Spidertoes intense interest in having the Night Court alliance. She threatened Gunnar over it, though I am not sure she actually likes Gunnar so I am less worried than Calamity is. Still, something to watch, doubly if Scarlet and Natalia are slinking around. Definitely.

    Having been around Jacob, I am fairly sure Natalia does not have a genuine Russian accent. I wonder if hers is from elsewhere or if she is faking it. Maybe he will know if he hears her speak.

    The evening went fine. Flores remained, as Julia expected, and we spent a fine night. I did my best to shut out my fancies of Scarlet and Natalia’s whispered promises in my ears.

    I did warn Julia. She can do as she chooses, though I suspect that will mean allowing herself to be bedded by them if it comes up. Probably.

    Breakfast was, as always, interesting. Flores flirted heavily; she seems incapable of not doing so. She put De La Rocha off when she left, and Julia attempted to be supportive, which, well, seemed to start building an erotic charge between the two. It happens very easily with Julia. Frequently.

    Julia was also quick to defend Chalcedony, not that she knows her, but her disappearances. One would not know now that she has warmed up to us, but Julia is terribly introverted.

    Speaking of such things, Julia has made clear her hate for Felicity, and implied to I she intends to do her harm. She does not seem to know her, but it smacks of jealousy. I am pledgebound to not abandon her as much as she is pledgebound to belong to me, she is not getting cut out of the picture, and would not even if there was no pledge. I would simply collect Felicity. I have not exerted control of her to not harm Felicity, but I will if I have to. Sadly.

    Speaking of Felicity, I got a warning from Calamity she may be manipulating I. This had occurred to I me and I was not upset she said it, though she clearly chose her words carefully to be respectful.

    But back to breakfast, De La Rocha headed to work, trailed by a slightly worried Lorelei. Captain Jack showed up to plan hijinx with Gawain. It was fun. Discussing what Calamity may wear (because Captain Jack may disguise as Calamity) lead to Julia and Lorelei deciding a shopping trip was in order. Calamity and I were drug along.

    Still, it was not all bad. I stood under Julia’s parasol, and helped her pick out black, lacy, feminine dresses that made I me smile. We picked something in gold for me for the ball to match my mask. Then we shopped for Lingerie. Julia bought myself, Lorelei, and the absent De La Rocha something and declared we must wear them for each other tonight. I look forward it. Perhaps I will even forget what Scarlet and Natalia promised about our next meeting…

    Yes, I will. These girls deserve no less. Definitely.

    End Session XXIV

    Not much to add. The players seemed very conflicted over how to handle Desiree. It was a powerful scene. I enjoyed seeing them working at it.

    Also making sure they have plenty of small subplots for the ball to take an interest in. What are these femmes fatale up to? Who invited Felicity? What is the Warlock of Night and Madame Spidertoes true intentions? Is Desiree going to be up to something? And my goodness did we just arrange for Captain Jack to be in a dress again?

    So many questions. Some may be answered next time. Comments and Questions welcome.
