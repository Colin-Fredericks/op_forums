    YOUR Custom Kiths (Newly Imagined)

    05-05-2015, 08:09 PM
    So a while ago, I made this: What's YOUR Custom Kiths for players who had come up with new ideas for Changeling the Lost.We listed which Seeming each Kith belonged to as well, so we knew what to play.


    But now with 2e coming out, we've discovered any Kith can be of any Seeming, and so this new version of the thread will be a little different from the old one. Now posters are encouraged to give a description of the Kith from multiple Seeming perspectives. Idealy, giving at least 3 descriptions, in theme with the teaser that was released previously. Though feel free to add all descriptions if you feel like it or when the other Seemings are revealed.


    -------------------------------------------------


    To start us off, I'll be importing some of my ideas from the previous thread. Granted, I've had to change their descriptions a little to fit with the new theme.



    Concubine: Abducted by perverse Keepers, the Concubines were added to their master's private harem or forced into the sex trade for public use. If a Concubine was not beautiful prior to their abduction, they often become so within their durance. Their time spent in the bedchambers left them with astonishing charm and social skills.

    Blessing - Royal Consort: By spending Glamour, the Concubine can make those around them more susceptible to their advances, as they find it easier to trust the Changeling and open up to them. For the duration of the scene, the Concubine gets a bonus of +2 to all persuasion and seduction rolls. Additionally, this bonus applies to the next attack they perform against that subject (treated as a surprise attack). However, attacking a subject instantly negates the power and can not be reused against them for the next 72 hours.




    Darkling - The last time that bastard tried to force himself on her, she let it happen... at first. He didn't expect the shank that had been concealed under the pillow, and when he was naked and disarmed, she struck, plunging her home made weapon into his throat. Her mask may be beautiful, but her mien shows the truth. Her disheveled appearance and blood stained clothing reveals her violent escape.


    Fairest - A prostitute? Bull shit, you could swear this guy was a knight in some Gentry's court! The flowing blond hair, the six pack abs, the perfectly unblemished skin, square jawline and those muscles. On occasion, you can still hear the freeholds tell the story of how he lead his fellow whores out of that Gentry owned bordello in Arcadia. You can also hear the rumors that he now owns a gay bar somewhere in the city. Damn it, why is it always the hot ones!?


    Beast - When people ask him if he's ever made love, he says 'no' and he is only partially lying. As a Concubine, he did not waste time with foreplay or passionate romance, he pounced his lovers and rode them like a bitch in heat. To humans, he is believed to be crude, perverted and shameless, but his fellow Lost can see past his mask, and the dirty dog for what he really is.





    -----------------------------


    I wanted to do more, but these are hard to convert into multi-Seemings.

                      Genie: Sometimes you want something but don't have the knowledge or power to obtain it on your own. When that happens, you need a Genie, a being with the power to grant almost any wish you can imagine. While serving their Durance, the Genie was the ultimate slave, wielding a level of power that rivaled even the Gentry themselves. Unfortunately that power was not at their beck and call, for they could only unleash it at the whim of their masters. Their escape back into the mortal world has significantly weakened their reality warping capabilities. But they still have a knack for getting people what they desire, no matter how far-fetched it may seem!


                      Blessing: As You Wish - By spending a point of Glamour, the Genie can endow themselves or others with a bonus to any dice pools aimed at fulfilling a specified request, but suffers a contrasting penalty to any dice pools that do not relate to fulfilling it. This bonus is equal to the Genie's Wyrd and can not be applied to direct attempts at resuscitating the dead, taking a life, or interfering with love.

                      While under the effects of As You Wish, the recipient gains the Obsessive condition in regards to satisfying the wish they are bound to, being driven by a compulsive need to fulfill it. This blessing can only be applied to the Genie using it, or a mortal (non supernatural) person. A mortal can only benefit from the effects of As You Wish a maximum of 3 times from any specific Genie. Once they satisfy 3 requests, they must find a new Genie if they want more.


                      Darkling - Not all Genies are trustworthy. The Darkling finds amusement in granting a literal interpretation of the wishes made to them. They escaped their Keeper by turning their own words against them, leading to disastrous wishes!


                      Fairest - Some Genies convince others to free them voluntarily. The Fairest managed to form a companionship with their Keeper who wished for their freedom as a sign of respect. Once free of their captor, they went out to explore the world, finding their way back through the Hedge. Sadly, it seems their Keeper now regrets their wish!


                      Ogre: Genies could not directly use their powers to kill, but you'd be surprised what you can live through. Possessing the might of a True Fae, the Ogre let sail a magical onslaught upon their Keeper's realm, leaving their servants bloody and unconscious in their wake as they fled back into the Hedge to return to the mortal world. Sadly, the rebellion seemed to have taken a lot out of them, and their powers are only a fraction of what they once were.



                      -------------------------------


                      Satyr: It's true that Arcadia is a realm of madness and terror, but it also is a place of dreams and beauty. The Satyr's durance was spent in hedonistic revelry, their days and nights filled with drinking, feasting, dancing and fornication. But all good things must come to an end, or so they say. One day the Satyr remembered they had important responsibilities and left the party behind, a decision which their Keeper took as an insult to their skills at providing a good time.


                      Blessing: Life of the Party - Satyrs know how to have a good time, that's kind of what their durance was made for. By spending a point of Glamour, the Satyr receives a +2 bonus on Social and Craft rolls for the rest of the scene. The Social bonus does not apply to intimidation rolls, while the Craft bonus applies to food and music.


                      Fairest - Alas, you got stuck as the designated driver of the group, which meant no alcohol for you. You had no idea the Keeper's wine had magical properties that enthralled the minds of those who imbibed it. You alone were responsible and clear headed enough to call it quits and leave while the rest of your friends were passed out drunk on the floor. You managed to get back home, but now you have a problem, your friends want to keep partying and they still need a driver!


                      Beast - Traditionally Satyrs are depicted to have the legs and horns of a goat, but you were a literal party animal. You were so high on life that even your Keeper was having trouble keeping you under control as you smashed and broke things around you in a euphoric haze. Finally the bouncers had enough and tossed you out on your ass so they could clean things up. Unbeknownst to you, your Keeper seemed to like your enthusiasm and has since fixed up their realm to be much more spacious than it was before. They claim they're able to accomodate you now, and they want you back!


                      Ogre - Just when everybody's having a good time, you have to go and ruin the mood. You're one of those angry drunks who vents their frustration on others when they've hit the bottle. In a drunken rage, you start picking fights with the other party goers, disrupting the festivities. Your Keeper wasn't in the mood for frivolity after that and kicked you out. But your Keeper is the forgiving sort and has decided to give you a second chance. They even promised their next party will be alcohol free, just for you!




                      ---------------------------


                      Unicorn: The embodiment of purity itself, the unicorn is described as being a beautiful horse with a single horn growing from his forehead. The legends claim that a unicorn will only submit to one who is pure of heart and will fight against those of lesser intentions. One day, they realized their Keeper was not the virtuous being they thought they were, and refused to heed their commands any longer.


                      Blessing: Purifying Hoof - Unicorns are said to have the power to cure disease and poisons with a touch. By spending a point of Glamour and touching a subject, the Unicorn is able to cleanse diseases and poisons up to a toxicity level equal to their Wyrd. If this power is not being used on a living being (such as a poisoned water supply), they may cleanse an area up up to 10 feet equal to their Wyrd.



                      Beast - You had the demeanor of a wild stallion, a creature who was raised on the open planes, not locked in a stall on some farm. The last time your Keeper attempted to saddle you, they found your spirit was as free as your body when you bucked them to the ground and raced off into the Hedge. You made it back to the mortal world, but now your Keeper sees you as a challenge. Successfully taming you would be worthy of bragging rights to their fellow Gentry!


                      Darkling - It is said that those wishing to capture a unicorn require a virgin to serve as bait. That's how your Keeper obtained you, by using an innocent to lure you into their trap. Every time you tried to fight back, your Keeper would use the maiden to lure you back into their clutches. One day, you had enough and you did something deplorable; you forcefully took their innocence. Now with no bait to lure you, your Keeper was unable to stop your last escape attempt as you fled back into the Hedge!


                      Ogre - Children often like to believe the Unicorn is a gentle and friendly creature, but some legends attribute the Unicorn to being violent and deadly towards those who they deem unworthy. One day, your Keeper found themselves on the receiving end of your horn as you flew into a rampage, provoked by something you can't quite remember anymore. A simple thing like impalement wouldn't slay your Keeper, not in their own realm. But it did leave them incapacitated long enough for your escape. Of course now they've recovered from their injuries and they're demanding satisfaction for the grievances you've caused them.

                          Weescamperer (Shamelessly copied from Lady Lens): In the great reaches of Arcadia, there are Gentry with all manner of interests and desires. Many pattern themselves on the elves of Norse legend, the Wild Hunt of the Celts, and similar beings. But there are also those who pattern themselves or some of their captives on smaller things, such as the pixies in Victorian illustrations or the gnomes made famous in Wil Huygens' book, or small animals such as mice and songbirds. Outside of Arcadia, these individual revert to their natural size, but the smallness never entirely leaves them, however tall, broad or deep they might be normally.

                          Blessing: Suddenly Small - By spending a point of Glamour, the Weescamperer can reduce his size by his Wyrd rating for one scene, to a minimum of 0, at which point the changeling is roughly mouse-sized. This shrinking reduces the changeling's Health accordingly, but no other Attributes. Speed and jumping distances are unaltered, and the character's Stealth pools (and in some cases other pools, at the storytellers discretion) are increased by Wyrd. Some actions become close to impossible if made too small, such as driving a car or using a normal-sized assault rifle.

                                  But I did come here with a kith of my own:

                                  Sanguinaire: Changelings who make up one of the building blocks of life, Sanguinaires were trapped in rivers of blood, made up cells swimming the veins of a massive body, or were used to feed terrible vampiric hungers. They alight with strange passions, being driven by something more fundamental than mere emotion.
                                  Blessing (1e) - Blood Will Out: Sanguinaires can spend a point of Glamour and mark an object by touching it with their blood. For the following week, the Changeling knows what direction the marked thing is located, and can find it when it's within 50 ft. If the item and Changeling are in different realms, such as the Hedge, the Sanguinaire knows it's not in this world, but no more information than that. The Elemental can mark up to three items in this way; marking a fourth will cause the first to lose its mark.
                                  Blessing (2e) - Blood Will Out: Sanguinaires know more than their share of what passions are born by blood, and get 8-against to all Empathy rolls. They also can spend a point of Glamour and mark an object by touching it with their blood. For the following week, the Changeling knows what direction the marked thing is located, and can find it when it's within 50 ft. If the item and Changeling are in different realms, such as the Hedge, the Sanguinaire knows it's not in this world, but no more information than that. The Elemental can mark up to three items in this way; marking a fourth will cause the first to lose its mark.

                                  Beast: Bat-wings and fangs, or a long proboscis for a mouth. In the jungles of hell, there's fruit aplenty, but true strength came from the strength of others. At first, you only drank the nectar of flowers, as instructed, but when grabbed by a massive hand, you bit, and drank the nectar of your captor's veins before flying off into a safer forest.
                                  Elemental: A humanoid figure made entirely of blood, or perhaps a red cell, shaped into a bipedal form. You lived in the river that was also a heart, but constantly fought against its pull. It wasn't until you dived deep into the river, became one with the flow, that you could escape, and spill out into the Hedge.
                                  Wizened: A bent figure, bearing blood-filled sacks attached to its flesh. You were nothing more than a blood bag, to be taken from when needed, until you remembered your medical training, and administered yourself to the fellow captive bleeding out from her latest escape attempt. Together, with you as the support and she as the drive, you went together, and this time, made it into the green place of the Hedge.

                                                  Since some new kiths were displayed today, I wanted to finish up one I had been thinking about some time ago.

                                                  Infectious

                                                  Yeah, I know he's hot, but you definitely don't want to sleep with that guy. He hides it well, but he's definitely got syphilis. Don't believe me? Here, let me show you. . .

                                                  Some people are stolen away to Arcadian realms of contagion and rot, lands where the air is thick with illness and a single cough can spell the end of an entire fae-village. These changelings were turned into plague rats, patient zeroes, or test subjects for strange vaccines against fantastical ailments.

                                                  Darkling: The changeling's face resembles an old plague doctor mask, and he exudes a faint, sweet perfume. Though his body is covered in symbols of healing, his fingernails are jagged and filthy.

                                                  Fairest: The changeling always looks wan, like she's just coming down with something, her face tinged red with fever. If anything, the symptoms of her eternal illness only accentuate her loveliness.

                                                  Ogre: Covered in dark striations and festering buboes, the misshapen being resembles the sort of creature that might escape from a lab in a low-grade sci-fi-horror film.

                                                  Blessing: The changeling is immune to mundane disease, and always adds his Wyrd to rolls to resist supernatural illnesses. With a few moments' interaction he can accurately diagnose anyone with a Wits + Medicine + Wyrd roll, even if the subject isn't displaying any symptoms of infection. By touching someone and spending a point of Glamour, the changeling can inflict the moderate version of the Sick Tilt, or upgrade a moderate sickness to a grave one.
