    Dark Eras 2e Changeling Review *Spoiler Alert*

    05-30-2016, 08:40 PM
    SPOILER ALERT.




    Okay, so I'll get the straight forward ones out of the way. Loved Three Kingdoms Changelings, the new 2e directional courts are awesome, with their capstone mantle ability of each court is interesting and useful.

    The directional contracts are useful and flavourful, but Changing Winds would likely have to be regulated by the local Freeholds, and I can see none directional court Freeholds making it illegal altogether, it could cause way to much chaos.

    I think the Mandate of Heaven is the Bulwark of the Directional Court, even if it doesn't say so in so many words.

    I also loved the Victorian Changeling, great courts, cool contracts, in fact some of my favourite contracts. Adored every single contract and I'd happily used them all.

    Contract of Abjuration is freaking brutal, a great tool to fight other supernatural splats.

    Contracts of Air and Darkness could be so much fun, especially for Darklings. Really looking forward to using that one.

    Now for the Grimms.

    My favourite parts were the the Grimm Seeming and the magic beans! Love the magic beans.

    Now the Grimms, to fix the Grimms I'd just make the none clarity part of the Seeming Blessing, the exact opposite of the Seeming curse, as long as the Grimm stays in a role, attempts to evade the Gentry and Huntsmen recieve the rote quality.

    So Seeming Contract Affinity, I'd just have Grimm pick the Seeming that they feel closest to (aside from the Grimms of course) and use their Seeming Affinities for now. A temp solution for the Grimms.

    I do wish the mechinical effects of assuming a rolenwere clearer. I think your mien shifts to the role.

    The other trifles and tokens were wonderful.

    I liked the Entitlements, and I wonder if they accurately represent how entitlements are handled in 2e. So far they look alot like they did in 1e. Still cool entitlements.

    I really loved the Inspirational Media section.including philosphical material was inspired.

    Requiem of a Dream (may have messed the title up), isn't just a book its a movie with Jennifer Connelly, who I've been a fan of since I was a kid watching Labyrinth.

    The contracts don't have Seeming affinities, but as they only workon Childern, I doubt anyone who isn't focused on Childern as a plotline/theme for their Character in a major way, will every take them permantly. It makes make sense to use a Goblin Vow to gain temp access when needed, so the little issues aren't going to cause a major issue.

    I like the courts of the Arts and the Sciences, I can see this being a common court in University Towns, although I can see regional difference like adding a History, Theology, Philosphy, or Social Science Courts (the social science court who be a split in the science court, with the old science court becoming the hard science court).

          Some of the little blurbs on other supernaturals in the none changing eras can be really useful.

          The Demons Berlin Changeling blurb actually fits really well as a result of what happened during the brothers Grimm era of Germany and its Demon blurb.

          During the Grimm Era, the increased amount of Gentry in German, some how appears to have caused the influence of the God Macchine in Germany to be greatly diminished.

          Hundreds of years later the God Machine has great influence in Berlin, and the Summwr Court has largely tamed the local hedge creating a golden age for German Changelings, which means a diminished influence for the Gentry, to the point where the then Winter Court fears an alliance amoung the Gentry will result.

          I'd suggest that the God Machine is secretly supporting the Changelings of the Summer Court in the hopes of undermining the Gentry in Berlin and likely throughout Germany.

          If the Gentry ever find out, it could lead to a war between not the Changelings and the Gentry, but outright war between the Gentry and the God Machine, perhaps in a later time period like the Present.

          I also wonder if Pangaeans travelled into the Hedge. I mean if some had access to the Fate Arcana, then there is no reason why they couldn't open a gateway to the Hedge and flee beyond the reach of Father Wolf.

          Perhaps some some unusually powerful Hobgoblins and Hedge Beasts were former Pangaeans who used the Hedge as a refuge from Father Wolf.

          Now for the Skinchanger Merit, what if a Skinchanger found out about the Gentry and learned how to make a skin out one of the Gentry by some miracle.

          It would be a mortal could become a member of the Gentry and would likely be treated as such by the Gentry, but an observant Changeling would know the different and could use said Skin Thief to their advantage. And one wonders what would happen if said Skin Thief visited Arcadia.

          You would have to change the Essence Pool Merit to Glamour Pool, and Spirit Power to Fae Power (getting Pledges, possibly including Goblin Vows instead of Influences and Contracts instead of numena or manifestations.)
