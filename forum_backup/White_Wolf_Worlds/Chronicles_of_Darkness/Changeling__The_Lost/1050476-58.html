                            So...slight rules debate happened elsewhere regarding Hedgespinning and how the whole excess successes thing works.

                            In the game I was in, we had it so that if you rolled excess successes when you were in the Hedge, you would declare that you're banking them for Hedgespinning. And at a later time, the character would spend the successes to enact a Hedgespinning action.

                            ...then I was pointed out how that's not how excess successes for Hedgespinning works. That there is no banking successes involved, that once the PC gets their excess successes, they must spend Glamour and use them immediately after they've seen what they rolled. And while not stated explicitly, that means the only realistic chance that a changeling has to pull off a Paradigm Shift is if they do an Extended Test or a Teamwork Test. This correct?

                                05-14-2020, 03:29 AM
                                It's all on page 205 in the Changeling book:

                                "Any mundane action a character takes in the Hedge may help pave the way toward Hedgespinning, although since the shifts reflect the emotions and general mental state of everyone involved, the results can have unpredictable side effects on exceptional successes and dramatic failures. Before each action, the player decides whether she wants it to generate shaping successes or not. If she does, the action takes a dice penalty depending on where she is: −3 on a trod, −2 in the Thorns, or −1 elsewhere. If she generates successes in excess of what’s necessary to succeed at the action, she may immediately spend one Glamour and allocate those extra successes to Hedgespinning."

                                So let's say you are using Brawl + Strength to punch a Hobgoblin in the face. You announce 'I am Hedgespinning' before making the roll, roll it with -1 because you are neither in the Thorns nor on a Trod, and get 6 successes. You can allocate any number of them to a shift and keep the rest (if any are left) to the actual punching.

                                Now, if you do a Paradigm shift, there is an option that lets you bank successes for future Hedgespinning attempts:
                                2+: Bank a number of successes to add to a later shift within this scene (subtle or paradigm) equal to successes spent minus one, to a maximum of five banked successes
