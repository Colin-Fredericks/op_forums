                But, to comment on the whole Beast thing, I think a key difference between Beasts and Fairest may be one of how personal each is.

                To put it another way, Fairest are defined by being perfect, sort of, but in a sense, unreachable. Their beauty is unnatural, and they're also separated from humanity by other stuff(consider in the original they're the ones that have a Clarity penalty for a reason.)

                By contrast, Beasts are approachable, and can be connected with. Sure, they were reduced to being instinctual, and have connections to animals most people wouldn't get. However, they aren't unnatural. Thoughts?


                For Beasts and Ogres, there are more issues. Ogres do have the origin dealing with violence shaping them, however that's a hair splitty thing when compared to beasts. One way to differ this though, using the example of Hunterheart(which may not appear), is this.

                A beast hunter heart represents the animal that kills you because it needs food, not because it hates you, or wants to kill you for sport. By comparison, the Ogre Hunterheart hunts you because its fun, and or they want to make a point out of hunting you down. One represents the uncaring aspect of nature, (yes, that brings them to elementals, but there's ways around that), while the other is, depending, either representing the abuses of society, or a bully who pretends to have an honor code.

                To differ the Beast here from how Elements have lost connection with humanity, again, Elementals are more representations of objects. By contrast, Beasts are representations of natural creatures, and how they act. Well, does a wolf care, other than how you threaten it, or whether its hungry? In this case, its primal needs versus being an object.

                Thoughts?

                                        Okay, having thought a little, let me analyze a kith from the original, Earthbones, and see what you guys think of how i try to interpret it here. Now, this may not even make it again, so I'm just using the Kith to demonstrate a point. Obviously, tell me if I'm grossly wrong from how development has changed things here.

                                        To begin, lets take a Elemental Earthbones here. Well, that you could mean in Arcadia... you were literally rock that made up a larger structure. Part of coming back, to show Elemental as a linking element, was that you embraced part of that, i.e. accepting that you come from a building essentially, but also sought an identity greater than that. The impersonal aspect of this, assuming this stays part of being an Elemental, is that much of how you define yourself is still tied to being an object, however an object of great importance. You would never look at see, monuments the same way again, because you know what it is like to literally be a part of one. Notice I'm not putting a nature theme here, because I'd argue Elemental, which perhaps may change to something else like Inanimates perhaps, are going to be more represented by being objects than being natural, if they were to stay an independent Seeming. Maybe. Speculation of course.

                                        If you're a Beast Earthbones, this could mean you were essentially the equivalent of some burrowing animal, trained to do the equivalent of digging up treasure or similar for your Keeper. When you broke free, part of that meant essentially becoming a wild animal, linking yourself back to the human world by being someone outside of your Keeper's, or anyone for that matter to some extent, control.

                                        Those are the two I got. Thoughts? Does this meet with how Durance is going? Hope this helps somehow.
