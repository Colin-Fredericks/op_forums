    Understanding Seeming and Kith

    01-29-2016, 08:54 PM
    Since the Huntsmen thread has been derailed onto the topic of what it means to be a Darkling, I thought I'd start a thread for people to ask questions and talk about Seeming and Kith.

    I've got something of an avatar character you might have seen me bring up, and here I'm using him as an exercise in marrying Seeming and Kith, as well as opening him up to critique. I'm largely writing about his version as a high level NPC ally/mentor, though some of this could be seen as my aspirations for his future if I play him as a PC.

    Ziggy Zapf
    Kith: Gameplayer
    Seeming: Beast
    Court: Autumn
    Notable skills: Computer, Occult, Athletics, Brawl.
    Notable merits: Parkour, Fighting Finesse (Brawl)

    Durance: Ziggy was developing a survival game where a player controls a pack of collectible monsters in a savage wilderness, but was stuck in Development Hell. A True Fae offered him the chance to make his game "come alive" and he didn't realize how literally she meant it. He spent most of his Durance as a disembodied game warden and allegedly impartial referee for competitions between her and other Fae. His Keeper was very demanding about how she wanted the game to be, devolving his vision into little more than blood sport. She often threatened to turn him into one of the monsters and fight for his survival.

    Escape: Utilizing "glitches" he wrote in the realm's Wyrd/code, he designed his own monster form, optimized for agility and stealth in the ever-shifting terrain. Since his Keeper focused entirely on combat, the monsters she sent into the arena weren't prepared for an opponent who simply ran and hid. He ignored being mocked as a coward since he saw no dignity in resigning himself to endless death matches, and simply waited for the realm's enormous moon to literally shift the tides (of islands floating in the air) in his favor and provide him a path to a hidden exit in a far off briar patch. Once he got a strong enough lead on his pursuers, he reveled in his new body, simply enjoying his freedom of movement as he effortlessly jumped impossible gaps, climbed absurd heights, and tumbled through all the gaps in the briar patch, emerging in the Hedge. A side effect of his escape, however, is a rekindled love of the hunt in his Keeper.

    Ziggy's mein is something of a hodgepodge, but his most notable features are a long pair of lopped rabbit ears and powerful legs ending in furry paws. He deviates by having a canine muzzle and a lion-like tail, but he still refers to himself as "mostly rabbit." He features various markings in his white fur that appear as tattoos on his mask: Purple and green 'circuit board' lines, and a gray upside-down pixel heart over his left eye. A purple line extending down from his left eye branches to make the upside-down heart into a spade.

    Beast's flauting of social conventions:
    • Nudist: Ziggy prefers to wear as little clothing as possible for informal occasions, though he can dress to impress when he wants to. When he's in his den, writing code or researching, he does so in the buff.
    • Body optimizing: Ziggy essentially escaped by modifying his body following (and bending) the rules of the video game his Keeper abducted him to develop in Arcadia, so Ziggy feels a need to keep "improving" his physical form, including cosmetic alterations like tattoos, piercings, and altered eye colors. He often gets his fix at the local Goblin Market.
    • Avoiding/declining/mocking "honor duels": Ziggy places reason before honor or machismo. He'd rather use logic and rhetoric, or failing that, a civilized non-bloody game to resolve conflicts. He doesn't care if people think he's a coward, because he knows better.
    • Fighting "dirty": If he can't avoid a confrontation with other people, he'll generally fight to disarm, pin, frustrate, or wear down his opponent, rather than kill or beat them senseless. When fighting monsters who can't be reasoned with, however, he'll go for a quick, merciful kill. He'll also use mobility and stealth to his advantage.
    • Unconventional strategy: When he plays games, he'll often favor unorthodox strategies, invoke obscure rules, metagame, or take advantage of glitches, in the case of video games. Sometimes, if nothing is at stake, he'll do strange but impressive feats just to show off his understanding of the game rules, even if it costs him victory. If something important is at stake, however, he'll happily drop artistry and sportsmanship and play to win.
    Tropes invoked:
    • The MissingNo / Easter Egg characters: To separate himself from his Keeper's story, he's been making allusions to W.D. Gaster from Undertale, who exists hidden in the game, with well-hidden followers talking about him being erased from the timeline and thus the game's story. The markings on his face evoke the cracks in Gaster's skull, and he's gone so far as to add a small pair of holes through his palms. His "formal" wear includes a green turtleneck sweater and a hedgespun lab coat made of white moonlight, its interior lined with "Matrix style" Zapf Dingbats. He uses a font motif like WingDin Gaster in naming things, and can read and write in dingbat fonts. He also hopes to mirror Undertale's idealism, using non-violent methods to confuse his often bloodthirsty Keeper. He refers to his Keeper as "Helvetica" as an oblique way of calling her dense and neo-grotesque.
    • Trickster Rabbit: He also references the archetype behind Br'er Rabbit, using his wits to survive instead of relying on brute force. He's less of a social manipulator, and more of an "outside the box" logical thinker.
    • Moon Rabbit: He fosters lunar associations, having used the moon of his Keeper's realm to his advantage. He's also discovered an inordinate fondness for rice cakes.
    • Techno-mage: He does his best to mix computer science and sorcery, and sees the Wyrd as something like a source code to reality.
    Hallucinations at low Clarity:
    • Nightmare imagery of his body being corrupted, breaking down into pixels or "sprite garbage."
    • Perceiving small differences in objects that video games use to mark traps, especially with floor tiles, boxes, and other repetitive square/cubical objects.
    • Monsters springing from containers when he opens them.
    • Anything involving foxes, after his Keeper's fox motif. Seeing fox-tailed "impostors."
    • People speaking in sound effects.

                                      Working on another NPC, who was originally a member of Ziggy's first Motley.

                                      Kith: Palewraith
                                      Seeming: Ogre
                                      Highest Attributes: Resolve, Strength, Composure
                                      Notable skills: Investigation, Brawl, Stealth, Empathy, Intimidation, Subterfuge.
                                      Notable merits: Giant, Closed Book, Grappling
                                      Virtue: Kindness
                                      Vice: ?
                                      Court: Winter

                                      Durance and Escape: Cooper Emerson was a meek child and often bullied. He dealt with this by learning to fade into the background and hide his emotional state. From this detached perspective, he became a talented observer of human nature. This uncanny knack for hiding in plain sight appealed to his Keeper, who abducted him to act as a spy and tracker. At one point, Cooper's Keeper had heard of Ziggy's game realm and the creative differences he had with Helvetica, his Keeper, and in examining the playing field figured out the gist of Ziggy's escape plan. Wanting a clever changeling for himself, he challenged Helvetica to a match as a pretense for getting his own team of hedgebeasts, lead by Cooper, into the realm. After defeating Helvetica, he moved Cooper's team to the briar patch to wait for Ziggy to come in monster form. Standing near the exit, waiting to intercept their prey, Cooper suddenly realized what was going on around him. He was there to capture someone else who had been going through the horrors of Faerie, just like him. He realized this would be a rare chance to escape and prevent someone else's escape from being thwarted. He ordered the hedgebeasts under his command to spread out under the pretense of casting a net for their prey. One by one, he started killing the beasts. He started stealthily, exploiting the game mechanics to add their strength to his own. He grew from a small sneak to a Yeti-like giant and with the transformation, he attacked more directly and brutally. Once he was done, he escaped through the briar patch into the Hedge, eventually meeting up with Ziggy. The two managed to work past their suspicions of each other and escape to Earth together, eventually forming a Motley.

                                      Mask: Cooper has become a much larger man than he ever was when he was still human, making it unlikely that he could ever reclaim his past life. Ziggy was in a similar state, becoming smaller. He appears as a muscular black man over 7 1/2 feet tall, but retains his knack for fading into the background, which many people find quite alarming for someone his size and build.

                                      Mein: In bright light, Cooper appears Yeti-like, covered in white fur that contrasts his dark skin. To Ziggy, he resembles Umaro from Final Fantasy VI. Spending much of his time outdoors, he tends to wear a large raincoat or cloak. He has the ability to conjure a largely blank white wooden mask to cover his face, and often does so if someone manages to touch a soft spot in conversation. In low visibility conditions, his white fur and mask become translucent, bending the light around him, allowing him to nearly vanish.

                                      To those who can see through the calm, emotionless facade he's practiced all his life, Cooper is a genuinely kind and sensitive person, regarded as an archetypal gentle giant by his friends. Though he has a largely pacifist philosophy, he secretly enjoys being able to intimidate people into submission using his contradictory traits of size and stealth together. Resigned to being separated from his old life, he works as the caretaker of a graveyard. There, he often comforts the grieving people at funerals and those visiting graves. Though he feels a little guilty about it, he doesn't shy away from harvesting sorrow when the opportunity presents itself. Given his nature, some urban legends have formed around him, usually shared by children, about him being something more than human guarding the peace of the graveyard, burying offenders alive in freshly dug graves. Superstitious criminals are often reluctant to work in the vicinity.

                                          More character sketches:

                                          "Helvetica": Ziggy's original Keeper. He refers to her as "Helvetica" as an oblique way of calling her dense and (neo-)grotesque. She usually manifests in female form, and has an obsession with "honorable duels" and various other forms of bloody competitions. When Ziggy escaped from her, rather than face her monsters in honorable combat, he inadvertently rekindled her love of the hunt. Additionally, seeing Ziggy advance to the title of Sorcerer of the Autumn court in Buena Vista, she's decided to view herself as the honorable warrior fighting against an evil sorcerer cowering behind minions and poisoning the royalty with sinister advice. For now, though, she's content to act as Andy's mentor figure, waiting for the opportunity to come out of "retirement" and avenge her pupil's upcoming death at Ziggy's hands.

                                          "Andy"
                                          Seeming: Loyalist (appears to be Fairest)
                                          Kith: Draconic
                                          Virtue: Faith
                                          Vice: Pride

                                          Referred to by Ziggy as "Andy," a font designed to resemble a child's handwriting, Andy was a delusional otaku who jumped at the chance to become a "hero" under Helvetica's tutelage. She convinced him that he had dragon blood in his veins and offered to train him in martial arts. He readily accepted indoctrination, having his sense of morality twisted into a parody of Bushido that often exempts him from the concerns of mere mortals. He is utterly convinced of Ziggy's evil and dishonorable nature. His blessings, contracts, and skills combine to make him into an animesque superhuman samurai. He refuses to acknowledge his supernatural powers as "magic," rationalizing them as the product of his swordsman training, chi, and warrior spirit, which, in his mind, are distinct from Ziggy's wizardly style.
