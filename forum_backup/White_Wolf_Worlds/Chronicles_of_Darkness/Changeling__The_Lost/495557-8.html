                            Well the biggest problem is that by the rules a Waterborn's ability only lasts for a scene an costs glamour to maintain. I'm not sure there's a definite limit to how long a scene can lasts but I'm guessing that they're probably not capable of living a fully aquatic lifestyle. Meanwhile the other two just have the benefit of being able to spend up to thirty minutes underwater at a time. Too put that in perspective whales typically can go for around ninety minutes without air, and dolphins (and most other aquatic mammals) generally only manage around 5 to 7 minutes underwater at a time. Really for all intents and purposes a Swimmerskin could live a fully aquatic lifestyle if they wanted to and probably be better at it than a lot of real aquatic animals.
