                                While we were talking the idea of Dream healing occurred to me and I mentioned it to Molly. I said that Calamity is really good at Dream healing and might be able to help her remember her past in a more safe and controlled way. I didn’t say those words exactly, but said that it might be better to view the memories while in a dream. Molly thought that this was a good idea so when Calamity looked in my direction I motioned for her to come over to the bar. She arrived moments later. Molly explained what was going on and Calamity said that she’d be more than happy to help Molly remember her past. She was rather gung-ho about the whole idea actually. She did caution Molly that once she remembers her past she won’t be able to just forget it again, and that she has to be sure that she wants to see the good as well as the bad aspects of her past self. Molly seemed sure that she wanted to go ahead with the Dream healing idea and asked if Chalcedony could be there in the dream with them to offer support. I wasn’t so sure that Chalcedony would want to be there since she thinks this is a bad idea, and Calamity said that she didn’t think it would be good for her to be there. She said that the healing should just be her and Molly. Molly eventually relented and asked if Chalcedony could be there in the room while they did the Dream healing. I told Molly that I would be there too to offer my support. I left off the part about me not trusting Chalcedony to be left alone in the Dream House while everyone slept. Which reminded me. Molly thanked me for not putting down her relationship with Chalcedony like others do. I don’t trust Chalcedony overly much but I can see how much Molly really likes her. She probably even loves the blue girl. If I say anything bad about Chalcedony I would just be pushing my friend away and I don’t want to do that. So, I smiled and said something noncommittal, but did say that I wanted to support my friend.

                                One thing that Molly asked me to do before we did the Dream healing was to talk to Lowjack, Master Darke or Doctor Bright about who Shayna was and what kind of person she was. I think Molly wanted to have another opinion on the type of person she used to be before she “meets” her past self in dreams. I told her that I’d talk to Lowjack and get back to her about that. It seemed like a good idea to me. Lowjack would have known what Shayna did and how she did it. He didn’t authorize the use of the Table to change her, but he would have been aware of her as a loyalist before she was changed. He could offer some insight into the person that she used to be.

                                All of this talk about past memories made me think about just how much past actions shaped me as a person. Calamity said as much too. She said that the past, the good and bad, shape who we are. I agree, so I guess I can see where Molly is coming from. She doesn’t have a past. Nothing to shape who she is except the past year’s worth of memories. She is so awkward socially because she literally doesn’t have much practice at interacting with people. She’s a good spy and fighter because those are the skills that they kept when they remade her. It isn’t fair. I have to believe that knowing who and what she was in the past will not change Molly too much. It will of course change how she views certain things, but I have to believe that she will also remember her present self and her current friends. Not all loyalists are irredeemable. I wasn’t. I don’t know exactly how D̶e̶l̶i̶g̶h̶t̶ Diane did it. I don’t even think that she did it on purpose, but the more time I spent in her company the more emotions I felt. The more I connected with the people around me as just that, people. They weren’t targets anymore. Even Travers. That man wants to kill me. He almost did, but he’s still a person in my mind. Just not a very nice one. I came back from being Rime. I came back from being a loyal Knight to the Lady of the Winter Winds. I didn’t come back to who I was before I was taken. I have already said that I just changed too much, saw too much, and did too much to be that boy again. I can’t go back. I can only go forward. I just hope that my friend takes that journey forward with me.
