        Originally posted by Omegaphallic View Post

        Which entitlements would you do as courts?
        The Solstice Court would seem an obvious choice, though if one wants to emphasize to the "wannabe court" angle it'd possibly be better off as a mystery cult. Other groups such as the Phantom Tongs (for whom being a nebulous organization is sort of the point), or the College of Worms (whose "Let's study fate" purview I don't think is specific to translate into a discrete role.) The Legion of the Iron Wall should probably be some sort of organization to remain recognizable, though it may possible to have a small set of Titles (Drill Sargent, Quartermaster, etc.) with buff and leadership focused effects. (Thereby making the beneficaries of their abilities the "legion" in an honorary sense.)

        Of course, in some cases the 1e entitlement could translate into both court and Title. (I.E. any number of members of the Toll-Taker court may gain glamour from hunting bounties but only one gets to be Boba Fett.)

        I don't think there's really magic formula here for what makes the best conversion. Probably should go with whatever seems the most interesting to the writer. (and 1e entitlements are already an eclectic grab bag of abilities so its hard to say one way or another from a purely logistical standpoint.)

                                        I must say that the way Entitlements are presented kind of remind me of the mange "Are You Alice?"- I don't want to spoil (and it has been ages since I read it), but the principle of "playing a role" which grants you certain privileges as long as you stay in role, and take them from you once you defy it, while basing some sort of an erratic clauses and interactions which seem to be invented by someone mad at best, really do give that kind of vibe into it. It is funny that as 2e went all the way in order to detach from the "Wyrd as a force of stories" theme, while entitlements seem to bring it from the back door. You play a character in a story not your own in order to enjoy the benefits it grants you. Sure, you may want to stop taking part in the play- but doing so always require you to find a proper replacement, as the story still being told. You may act outside of your role, of course- you are still a being with free choice- but the story doesn't like it, and it may end up with some dire consequences.

                                        In a way, entitlements are the ultimate spit in the face of the Gentry- they tell their own stories, but are trapped and defined by them, unable to get out. Changelings are kidnapped and forced against their will to take part in those stories, unless they manage to get out. Entitlements is about living in the story of someone else, but choosing which story you live in, and which character you play. It is the ultimate victory over the True Fae- that you can make a choice they can not, and that you have the freedom they will never have.

                                        I think I like that.
