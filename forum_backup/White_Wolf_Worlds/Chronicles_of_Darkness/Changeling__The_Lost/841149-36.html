    Mirrorshade
    "Magic mirror on the wall, who shall be the next to fall?"

    Every mirror can be a looking glass for the Gentry, and Mirrorshades were blessed with this same power for their durance. Some served as observers and spies, peering through a myriad of mirrors in search of new knowledge for their Keepers to use. Others, however, became guards and assassins, hiding wherever one can see his reflection until they emerge to deal the killing blow.

    Blessing: The Mirrorshade gains the Glimpse of a Distant Mirror Contract, and gains an Exceptional Success on it at three instead of five. If he is touching a reflective surface that is either as tall and wide as he or as wide as he is tall in both dimensions, any roll made to find him suffers his Wyrd as a penalty.

    -=-

    Critique and ideas for Seeming descriptions are appreciated, as are Seeming ideas for the Spellbinder kith I posted a few days back.

                                                        I've been collecting custom Kith examples from across the web, the majority written during 1E Changeling so in need of revisions. I also need to have a second pass over the list to note how Wyrd is utilized so I won't post many at a time.

                                                        (from QSF Changeling)

                                                        Hollowmade
                                                        Not every Changeling can recall a durance spent being the building, place or structure within Arcadia. Shaped into extensions of their Keeper's realms, there were nothing but the platform on which others went about. Since that them some have become architects or builders, holding appreciation for well made structures. Others considered themselves blessed to finally be able to move and not be spread out over square acreage.
                                                        • Beast: like a great crustacean, on the back of its shell a multitude of housing coral
                                                        • Darkling: preforated, pierced and zippered across gaping wounds leading to internal cavities
                                                        • Elemental: twisty Hedge growths, alien fae structures or golems of brick and motar

                                                        Blessing: Within them is a closet sized space only they can access, storing and retrieving items for 1 Glamour each. The total combined volume of objects, by Size, cannot exceed the Changeling's Size + Wyrd. Once stored the objects (at Storyteller's discretion) are unreachable for anyone else yet age and decay at their normal rate. Additionally, any Hollow merit the Changeling owns gains 2 free dots of features when they reside in it, or 1 free dot of features when given permission when guest to another's Hollow. Different features can be selected at each visit.

                                                            Merchant (From Angelus Morningstar's Kith Compendium)
                                                            • Beast: A maneki-neko, tanuki, magpie, pig or another animal associated with good fortune or greed.
                                                            • Wizened: Eyes that can expand into telescopes to appraise items and weird calculators built into their arms.
                                                            • Elemental: A living abbacus made of levitating gems and coins that constantly move from one spot to the other as they crunch numbers in their mind.
                                                            Blessing: Merchants gain the 8 Again rule for all trade negotiations, the Market Sense Merit, and a free Interdisciplinary Specialty with Area Of Expertise for Goblin Markets. By spending 1 point of Glamour when touching an object, they may use the the Psychometry Merit, adding her Wyrd to the roll and suffering no penalties. The questions must be focused on the object's potential uses or drawbacks in trade negotiations.
