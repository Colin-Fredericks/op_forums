                      Originally posted by TerrorCooper
                      I personally hold the headcanon that True Fae become as Solipstic as they do because of the fact that any possible 'beings' they might come across generally tend to be little more than animated parts of themselves, be they Actors, parts of a group of Wisps, or the denizens of a Realm. That, and they generally aren't given much chance to learn how to rise above their instincts.

                      Thus, let me try to spin something here, using the Game of Immortals from Equinox Road, and the above headcanon, as a base foundation/set of assumptions for the whole thing.

                      Say that a Motley of Changelings makes their way into Faerie and somehow stumbles upon a freshly 'spawned' 'clutch' of Gentry. Thanks to a combination of shenanigans, misunderstandings, noodle incidents, and poorly worded turns of phrase, the changelings somehow end up adopting the Others as their collective children, taking them back to earth.

                      Over the following years, the group of Gentry learn how to rise above their instincts and recognise other people as people, rather than as Objects to be acted upon by the lone Subject (aka themselves). When they eventually end up moving the majority of themselves back into Arcadia, this capability eventually leads them to becoming the longest-lived group in the entirety of their realm.

                      …I think that I might have somehow created the basis for a near-unique C:tL story.
