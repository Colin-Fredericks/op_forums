                                        So in in an effort to improve upon one of my example Blessing/Curse pairings I tried to encompass the current leadership role, absorbing some of the idolization mentioned and tried to keep it all within the theme of Beauty and Madness. Still not the best of write ups and I am unhappy about the language but here goes nothing,

                                        Blessing: Thou art fairer than all who are here. Fairest escaped their Keepers thru guile, seduction and charm by proving themselves to be the fairest of them all. The truth is that confidence, beauty, and charm are all traits that make people heap expectations upon you. Any time the Fairest takes those expectations and runs with them using her charm, guile, and beauty to manipulate someone else into doing her task for her she reaffirms her sense of self. Once per story, a Fairest may manage a task through her fairness alone and she gains a point of Clarity for free.

                                        Curse: But more beautiful still is...Fairest sense of self is strongly anchored to how others see her. She needs to feel admired and looked upon for her counsel, even when she clearly isn't the best choice to give one. When the illusion of her fairness shatters, by her own hand or through others, so shatters her sense of self. If the fairest is shown to be a charlatan, fraud, or just plain incompetent to handle her tasks and is confronted about it she suffers a Clarity Break.

                                        So the basic idea here is that the Fairest are inspiring, beautiful and/or charismatic enough to draw attention and the basics of the current write up stay, that is people tend to expect the Fairest to lead, counsel and be perfect at everything but should their plans fail and their efforts be foiled then they suffer for it, ever seen how villains take bad news when their minions fail to deliver that is the point of Clarity break here and for the heroic it plans failing usually, but not always, means someone got hurt which again is a Clarity breaking moment. It's not about the collateral damage but about their self-confidence, when the latter is rattled they risk loosing their Clarity. The main difference to the original Fairest write up is that the Curse does not care about people being hurt unintentionally or otherwise unless said consequence somehow makes the Fairest seem incompetent. So it wouldn't be a Clarity break, from the Curse, if a few innocents suffered if the plan as a whole worked and the larger benefits outweigh the minor suffering caused.

                                        Then again I am doing this in the middle of the night so I might not be at my best coherency.

                                            I'd say something like this would work better, capturing both the leadership and the beauty angle in a broader package of image:

                                            Blessing: Standing High Upon a Pedestal. Fairest escaped their Keepers through seduction, persuasion, leadership, but ultimately by proving themselves to be the Fairest of them all. Once proven to be the Fairest, they are always the Fairest. People can see the Fariest's greatness, and expect her to live up to it. One who charmed her Keeper with pleasant conversation will hear the neighbours gossiping about how good company she is, even if she has never spoken more than a few words to anyone. A fairest who led his fellow slaves in revolution will find all eyes turning to him in a crisis. Once per story a fairest who lives up to her image and uses it to make others do as she wishes gains a point of Clarity for free.

                                            Curse: Falling from a Height. Moving on from Arcadia is hard for anyone, but the Fairest have an advantage. She may have forgotten how to trust or how to talk to other humans but her image shines before her. The script is easy to learn and the part is fun to wear, isn't Prince Charming better than Harold Smith anyway? The Fairest builds her new life upon the part she claimed in Arcadia, but what is easy for an hour is impossible to do for a life time. No one can be the perfect lady every hour of every day, eventually the changeling slips up and it feels as though the rug has been pulled from under her identity. Any time the Fairest cannot live up to her role she suffers a Clarity Break.


                                            You can get something very similar to the original 2E Blessing or Curse by taking the image of leader; but you can get something very similar to 1E by taking the image of Dancer, or... most of the 1E Fairest Kiths really.
