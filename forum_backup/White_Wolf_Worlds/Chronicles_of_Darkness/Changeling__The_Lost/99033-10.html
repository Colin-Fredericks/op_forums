                    Okay

                    Julia Oldacre was one who sort of surprised me how different her planned role to her in game her role was.It's easy to forget Julia is a high Mantle Autumn Courtier, whose original introduction was her watching/stalking Lori. Then Insincere Marguerite seduced her. Julia was intrigued by the mysterious woman, and was never the same again. Insincere Marguerite's personality makes people underestimate Julia, but she is fine with that. She murdered her fetch, and her attraction to Lori stems in part to how much Lori resembles the beautiful women who often meet dark or tragic fates in her work. Indeed, one possibility was for Julia originally was to be the unlikely murderer if her desire to create one of her dark Gorey-esque tableaux in real life overcome her, which means Flores and Lori are no longer in danger. Maybe.

                    Maria De La Rocha is a good place to note how I handle NPCs and romance regardless. Most NPCs are potentially bisexual to PCs, but may well not be to NPCs. DLR left to herself would have never gotten into a lesbian relationship despite (or, partially because of) her romance with Madame Spidertoes. Females have a harder time seducing her, but Lori can seduce anything. Of course, being in a romance with a woman means making her confront her sexuality, which means more baggage for a female PC, but she is more open to commitment with a woman than man, so there is that.

                    Flores is a sweetheart. She does not get why people are so hung up on sex, but she really wants to make people happy. Total flower child. Probably won't be murdered in an artistic fever by Julia now, which means I need to find something to do with her. People seem to like and want to protect Flores which is cool, she is an innocent and may be one of the most legitimately good people in the freehold. She has no dark side to speak of, unless people consider being a sexual tyrannosaurs a dark side.

                    Becky Wan the overworked Clavager looks pretty sinister, and is a potent and dangerous fighter. She's not really evil though, just crafted by the gentry that way. Not up to anything untoward the PCs know of, but you know, she really, really does NOT like Desiree...

                    Princess Jolie's picture was actually put forth by Lorelei's player. She is absolutely the sweetest soul eater you will ever meet. She is smart, charismatic, the Spring Court adores her, will kill for her, and will ignore heinous crimes she commits. Even the PCs were disinclined to take her down.

                    Queen Charlotte is beautiful, cunning, and dangerous. Not as powerful as, say, Tommy or Spidertoes, she is a people person, being a predator, and is cunning and wise enough to lead and pretty enough to fight for. If anything is working against her, it is her powerful, capricious, and frankly mad patrons within the Court.

                    For Lucian Torrez I picked Brian Thompson to play the heavy. Lucian is friendly and charismatic, and willing to kill out of hand for frighteningly little provocation. He is what I call an agenda'd NPC; not a direct villain, because the PCs are not on a collision course with him, but certainly someone they could find themselves in a position where they must ally with him or become his enemy. The PCs have worked to keep themselves out from his way for now. They are wise.

                    Master Darke keeps secrets like beehives keep bees, and his are ten times and dangerous. Ruthless, amoral, cruel, callous, and the biggest thing keeping the Gentry out of Malibu/Santa Monica/Venice Beach.

                    Hope that is helpful.

                                    It is fun to have different Court arrangements My first game Beyond the Sea for Changeling: The Lost featured a situation where the Winter and Autumn Kings were incapacitated by a loyalist agent, and the Summer Queen was soon after it started. The primary defenders of the area were high Wyrd, low Clarity Changelings and the whole city was surrounded on three sides by swamp and one side by ocean...the two ways out were a single road (which required crossing a bridge to connect to land) or by sea, playing up the isolation, and a group of (mostly) humans all but controlled the area though political dominance...the leader was a powerful Thaumaturge with a Spirit Patron. There were lots of ghosts and a somewhat lovecraftian storyline going on as well. The end result was isolation, dangerous marches around the city, and mad and unreliable leaders. There were no Sun or Moon Courtiers, but there were a small contignent of Directional Courtiers in one part of town who did not mingle with the Seasonals. By the end, a PC was Autumn Queen.

                                    Unfortunately, only one player reliably did journals, and his journals were a colloquy of different personalities that represented different people he as a mirrorskin imitated. He was not MPD, more a consensus. This meant that his journals were arcane if you were not present for the game. One player did a single journal (which did not actually record any of what happened in the game so much as his reflections on being a Changeling) and while we had one player in game for six months or so who did journal more coherently, the nature of the PDFs the player produced were not such that I could copy and paste it...which means I have sadly little in the way of records of that game.

                                    Malibu Dream House is a whole different set up. There are many more Changelings and the Courts are relatively stable. Much of the threats are beneath the surface, and there is less crossover with other WoD games (probably because I am now running Geist and Demon both of which feature more crossover). There is still a major, threatening figure who is not quite an antagonist but the PCs must dance to his tune if they seek to avoid that (in Beyond the Sea played by Wilfred Brimly, in Malibu Dream House by Brian Thompson, very different characters,)

                                    Totally as an aside, despite having only two common players (Ace and Insincere Marguerite) the Seeming layout was kind of similar between them. Beyond the Sea had 4 Darklings, 1 Elemental, 1 Fairest, while Malibu Dream House has 1 Beast, 2 Darklings, 2 Elementals, 1 Fairest. (Courts were different, 2 Spring, 1 Summer, 2 Autumn, 1 Winter vs. 1 each Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Dawn, Moon. Maybe they were not that dissimilar)

                                                            The Masquerade has begun! We don’t get all the way though, but we can get the story so far from our Motley. Calamity’s player has elected to hold off and do the whole Masquerade in one go, so they can set the emotional tone of it when they have the whole story. Fear not, you will hear from the stabby redhead soon enough. But for now, it’s time for the Masquerade!

                                                            Session XXV

                                                            Gawain’s Memories

                                                            June 15th

                                                            So, the first half of the ball was super awesome. A little bit frustrating, cause I just couldn’t get my Contract of potential to work, you know? I did manage to figure out that Chalcedony has known Desiree for long enough that they had to have been in Arcadia together, but that’s it. I guess I was distracted. There was plenty to be distracted by and I was kinda fidgety, you know, waiting for the shenanigans. And the Greening.

                                                            The Greening was just perfect. De la Roche and Lori went first - they showed up together, so I guess De la Roche worked out her stuff. We left early, cause we were security, you know, so Lori was still glued to her phone and fretting when we left. Me and Calamity both felt better, I think, when they walked in together. They danced together a lot too, though Lori’s dance card was crazy full - I dunno if she got to just chill, ever, everyone wanted to dance with her.

                                                            Joan and Andromeda went second. They even Pledged themselves to each other, with Joan promising to kill anyone who harassed Andromeda. So, she’s not any less super intense. Not that I expected her to be, you know. Really though, they were super adorable all night, being completely twitterpated over each other.

                                                            Then and last, it was me and Calamity. We each Greened each other, cause that seemed the most right. I had to go down on one knee so she could reach my head, so I took that moment to say the kinda tweaked fealty oath I’d prepped. It wasn’t a capital-O oath, or anything, but it seemed the most ‘me’ thing to say, you know? Calamity told me again that she was my monster, there in front of everyone and then we kissed. I kinda forgot everything else cause of that kiss until the mask slipped down and bopped me on the head. I had had to take her mask off to put the ivy on, which was good - I may have made it, but I really didn’t want to kiss the creepy rabbit mask.

                                                            It was amazing, just the best. That by itself made the night and along with the shenanigans with Captain Jack and the good news about Desiree, I was pretty sure I couldn’t be happier. Desiree wasn’t quite as happy as I thought she’d be - not that she was ungrateful or anything, you know, she was happy and super glad it could be fixed, but it also occurred to her that her friend that escaped with her, that she turned in for starting to be loyalist-y, probably just had the switch flipped. That is a bit of a bummer, but hopefully one that won’t have to happen again, you know? For now, Sandeval’s going to see if the Court can’t track down an Oneirophysic and I’m gonna work with her in the meantime. With that and Captain Jack’s help with the phones, I’m sure we’ll get everything figured out.

                                                            Speaking of Captain Jack... shenanigans were fucking had. They went off perfectly too. He played some ‘villain music’ just after 9 and pounced on Calamity, dragging her off to the closet. When he came out in a Captain Jack sized version of Calamity's bunny costume, me and ‘Calamity’ danced again. I almost ruined it when Calamity came out in a Calamity -sized version of his costume. She told me she would, but then when I saw shirtless Captain Jack in tight pants, a luchador mask and a giant back tattoo of a dragonfly with a chihuahua in its mouth, I didn’t put two and two together until shirtless Calamity came out in tight pants, a luchador mask and a giant back tattoo of a dragonfly with a chihuahua in its mouth. It was, well, distracting, you know? She stayed in it til Captain Jack came back in his old costume, so I got plenty of time to stare after shenanigans.

                                                            But I managed to tear my eyes away at the time, and after a bit a shard of ‘Calamity’s’ glass flew off into Lori’s boobs. I got fake angry and then he kissed me on the cheek and ran with me running after him. When we got to the ballroom, he managed to surreptitiously punch one of his fake boobs out and it went bouncing everywhere, so he started chasing after that. Once we’d made a circuit, a rope was hanging from the skylight and he got pulled up and away with his bouncy boob while I shook my fist at him.

                                                            He came back after a bit, of course. He kinda just slipped back in, you know, but then nothing could compare with his first entrance. Eight shirtless dudes singing O Fortuna while carrying him on a palanquin. Then he got introduced as ‘Captain Jack Martinez of the Dusk Court, Just Some Guy, You Might Have Seen Him’.

                                                            The titles were cool. Us in the motley kinda had a theme. Lori was Siren of the Malibu Dream House. I was Sentinel of the MDH. Insincere Marguerite was Watchful Eyes of the MDH. Jacob was Master of Spirits of the MDH. Calamity was Flashing Blades of the MDH. And Ace, of course, was Minder of the Egg of the MDH. Ace was his usual crazy self. He kept trying to talk without a British accent - I guess so that no one could tell who he was behind the mask? At least he didn’t bring the caiman...

                                                            Other people had cool titles too, and it was interesting to note some of the patterns. Most people were Lord or Lady, except Captain Jack was Captain Jack and Moon was Mystery Moon. The Hispanic folks were Don and Dona, again, except for Captain Jack. Julia had the title ‘Creator of Wicked Eerie Tableaus’. Felicity was ‘Solar Mirage’ and Joan was the ‘Blazing Summer Sentinel’. The Handmaidens had matching titles, of course - Scarlet was ‘Some of Them Want to Use You’ and Natalia’s was ‘Some of Them Want to Abuse You’.

                                                            The Handmaidens kept making me feel uncomfortable. Probably it was the way they kept circling around my motley mates like sharks. Just the ladies though, they left us dudes alone. Ace did dance with the Warlock, but I think he started that, you know? She seemed pretty cool, dancing by herself and having fun. He danced with her a couple of times actually, and everyone else seemed kinda standoffish. The Handmaidens though seemed like trouble and I couldn’t get a read on them. Calamity could tell I was bothered and promised she wasn’t going to leave me for them or kill them, which wasn’t really what I was worried about really, but good to hear, I guess? They did seem more human? at least when they were interacting with Captain Jack, even if that did mean him spinning Scarlet above his head. It would just be nice to know what they were after though.

                                                            Jacob did some dancing too, even if the Handmaidens didn’t go after him, with Insincere Marguerite and Molly Nuit. He didn’t get a chance to dance with Luna before the Greening, cause she showed up right then, but he brought her a drink right off and he and her and Calamity talked some. I think Calamity was helping her figure out the heels, cause Luna looked super uncomfortable and unsteady walking in them. She seemed more steady after talking for a bit, and hanging on to Jacob of course.


                                                            Lorelei’s Diary

                                                            Okay, so for once I get to start this diary on a high note. I have a lot to write about, but for the most part it’s happy stuff. For the most part.

                                                            Saturday afternoon was nerve-wracking as the motley got ready for the Masquerade. After breakfast I took a shower, did my hair, applied makeup, and got dressed, all the while keeping my phone within arm’s reach and checking for messages from D every five minutes. I’m sure it must have amused and/or annoyed the hell out of the rest of the motley, but Julia (the big sweetheart) seemed really concerned about me, and spent most of her time trying to calm me down and reassure me that everything would be all right. I really need to thank her for her concern.

                                                            When D finally did call, I answered immediately. It’s hard to sound nonchalant and aloof when you answer a call within two seconds, but I think I made an admirable effort. “Hey, babe,” I said as I answered. “How are you doing?”

                                                            There was a pause, and I could almost hear her grin slightly. “I’m fine, Lori,” she said. “I’m okay now, I promise. What time do you want to get to the Masquerade tonight?”

                                                            I looked at the clock. I’d actually been hoping to arrive fashionably late, but my desire to see D as soon as possible made me change my mind in less than a heartbeat. “Seven,” I said, “I’d like to get there around seven.”

                                                            “Great,” she replied sedately, “I’ll be at your place by six-thirty, okay?”

                                                            “Okay,” I said. I was trying to play it cool, but I just couldn’t keep it in any longer. “I can’t wait, D. I’ve missed you.”

                                                            “I know,” she said. “I missed you too, Lori. Thanks for giving me the time I needed. I’m ready now. I love you.”

                                                            “Love you too, D,” I said, and we disconnected.

                                                            I turned around, and Julia was there. She smiled at me, reached up and brushed a tear from my cheek. “Told you so,” she said softly, and I kissed her in gratitude.

                                                            When D actually arrived, I was speechless for a moment. She was gorgeous. I mean, I’ve always thought D was beautiful, but she really stepped things up for last night; she was like fashion model beautiful. She hadn’t really changed her hair; it was still that lustrous black with the shocking purple streak in it. She was wearing a long purple dress that accentuated her figure and complimented her coloration nicely, and she had makeup on… honest-to-god makeup!

                                                            Purple lipstick. Purple hair. Purple dress. For an instant, an image of the Empress flashed through my mind, and I felt something swelling inside me. I don’t know if it was fear, revulsion, or love, but whatever it was, I fought it down before it could show on my face. What I have with D is real, and I’m not going to let the Empress ruin it. Not now that I’ve finally escaped her.

                                                            “I’m back,” D said simply.

                                                            We arrived at the Masquerade looking glorious. I noticed Felicity watched Lorelei enter, so I swooped in quickly to talk to her. Felicity was indeed interested in talking to me…privately. We had a dance and then spoke in a corner to ourselves.

                                                            She noted we were the most powerful, most respected Night and Day Courtiers outside downtown LA, possibly in the state, and it would behoove us to work together. As she told us before, she has no desire to unseat the Seasonal Courts. She does wish to leverage our influence a bit, though, though allies of our Courts, including the Motley. I asked her if she sought to be Day Queen of some sort and she sort of smiled and shrugged, but reiterated that she does not want to see the Seasonal Courts removed from Santa Monica, noting if they were, we would go back to being smaller fish in the area. I told her I liked her cunning and kissed her. Aggressively.

                                                            As most do in my experience, after going limp a few seconds in surprise she returned the kiss. She seemed more amenable to my advances afterwards. I reminded her she was going to be seduced. I also told her her life might depend on not upsetting Julia, that Julia was my partner, and would be involved in any scheme we were. There was some discussion of Julia, and I informed her I needed to go find her, but that I would come back before long. Truthfully.

                                                            Felicity is, however, a remarkably good kisser. I mean, not as good as I, but then who is?

                                                            I danced with Julia, and then after she asked I told her again it was perfectly fine for her to dance with Lorelei. I plan to myself. I myself asked one more time if she wanted to do the Greening, and she responded the same as before that she wanted no part of anything so twee. Apparently.

                                                            Jacob wanted to dance next. I am very fond of Jacob. He seems relaxed somewhat, though I did not search his eyes. He is shy, poor lad, and I was considering trying to introduce him around, but Luna’s arrival suggested such may be unnecessary or even dangerous if she gets jealous. I don’t want to accidentally seem to be trying to break them up. Definitely.

                                                            I also danced with Willow briefly. I had just finished when Scarlet and Natalia showed up. They greeted me warmly (and made a point of also greeting my tongue, which was annoying as it is just part of me) and expressed interest in continuing our…rapport…where it left off. They paced around me, hungrily, like sharks. I was polite, but I did not encourage their advances. Calamity is a clever girl, and she is correct; they are indeed up to something. Perhaps they see me as a way to increase our Courts power in Santa Monica without Felicities rather political concerns about her own power, since they are themselves very influential. Perhaps I really am just that amazing, as Julia suggests. Probably that one. Probably.

                                                            Somewhere in all this I danced again with Felicity. Afterwards Captain Jack, Gawain, and Calamity carried out their tour-de-force, which involved at times Captain Jack dressed as a playboy bunny and Calamity running around bare chested dressed as a luchador. Julia thought it was terrific fun and even I thought it was amusing. People will certainly be talking about Captain Jack, Gawain, and Calamity after this.

                                                            During the time before the Greening I danced with Julia again and kissed her on the floor. Publicly. I saw Luna had arrived and made for Jacob. At first I thought she was drunk but no, she was unsteady on heels. Poor thing. I don’t even wear shoes most of the time. Heels? I cannot even imagine! I have not worn them since I returned to Earth. I certainly did not return to wear heels!

                                                            After a bit, they Greening occurred. Mostly Motley members, Lorelei and De La Rocha went first, then Joan and Andromeda, then Calamity and Gawain. I thought it was sweet, even if Julia acted like she was watching people play Andy-I-Over. Strangely.

                                                            With that, it was ninety minutes until the meeting, and I had more dancing to do. Good thing I did not wear shoes! Definitely.
