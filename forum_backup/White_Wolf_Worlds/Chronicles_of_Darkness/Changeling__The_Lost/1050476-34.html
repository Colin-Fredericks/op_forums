                Originally posted by DRaziel View Post
                - Someone asked this question but no one answered: Can the effects of dropping the Mask be applied somehow in the Hedge? If not, that means a changeling can be more powerful in the real world than in the Hedge.
                By RAW, I don't think dropping the Mask and its mechanics are intended to be used in the Hedge.

                The mechanical effects described are written with the assumption that you're doing this Ironside, considering the multiple references to being witnessed by mortals. And since while in the Hedge, a changeling's Mask isn't present at all, dropping the Mask wouldn't be a thing.

                And I don't think trying to do the inverse would work either: if anything, putting on the Mask could be seen as a DENIAL of your fae side. So no more liberal exceptional succeeded Contract rolls for you.

                                    Originally posted by Anachronistic Jam View Post
                                    Do Fetches have a Mask & Mein?

                                    If so, can Changelings see through them by default?
                                    I do not think they do.

                                    The text makes multiple references to how, when a changeling comes back through the Hedge and check in on their previous life, they see someone that looks exactly like them in their place. So presumably can only see their "Mask" and not the detritus that is composed of their actual form. However, just by the fact that the Fetch is a doppelganger of them, the changeling should, in theory logically deduce what it actually is.

                                    So the notion of being able to see a Fetch's "Mein" is unnecessary.

                                        The chapter fictions imply that fetches do have a Mask and Mien. "He sews his button eyes back on every morning, thinking they’re eyeglasses, and kisses some other woman. She’s cute, I guess, but doesn’t seem like our type. Or maybe just not mine."

                                        "When I turned slightly past, I could see our reflections in the bathroom mirror. Mine looked the same — snub nosed, sleepy-eyed, blonde roots growing in under bottle red. His... blurred like someone had smeared a handful of Vaseline on the glass. There, his face was patchwork, and his flat, button eyes hung loose. When I turned back to James, his bright-blue eyes lit up, and his smile pulled up the rims of his glasses. In the mirror, cloth shuffled itself and formed a gap shaped like that smile. I didn’t flinch when he rested his flesh (fabric) hand on her knee and leaned in for a kiss. His face felt warm and unshaven and familiar. But when I peeked through my lashes, the cloth face in the mirror was rubbing against hers."

                                        The mechanics section does not specify, but there are a couple of points in favor. Fetches are built like Changelings, and the text says that the Adding Supernatural Template step "varies a bit". It then lists five ways the fetch template is different from the changeling one, but I think we can reasonably assume that traits not specifically identified as different follow the same rules for Changelings.

                                        Second, the Normalcy Echo makes Fetches undetectable to Fae Magic, so by default it must be possible to identify them somehow. That could just mean kenning and the like, but given the above points I treat Fetches as being as visible to Changelings as Changelings are to them.
