    So, like the title says I'm new, not just to Changeling but to WoD in general, so I'm doing character building exercises to learn the system and generate usable characters for if I ever get into a game at the same time.       First, when you purchase a Contract up to a certain level you get every ability up to that level. With your Eternal Spring 3 you have access to the level 1 and 2 powers as well as the third. The exception to this is Goblin Contracts which you purchase as one off abilities.

      Second, you are correct that duel kith only gives the kith blessing and not any seeming bonus/penalty. Duel kith is really good because kiths in general are quite strong compared with anything else you could spend those points on.

      Rules out of the way lets talk thematics. Court Contracts, such as Eternal Spring, are taught by changeling courts and as such aren't something your character would have learned in Arcadia and come back with. That's fine for a character that's been around long enough to join a Court but if you're looking towards making a new fresh from the thorns changeling it's an issue.

      For Seeming was your character made into an animal or forced to be animalistic in Arcadia? If so they should be Beast. On the other hand were they forced to labor thanklessly and punished if their work was ever less than perfect? Then you want to go the wizened rout.

      WoD games tend to be less combat focused than other games and as such your character doesn't necessarily need any combat skill. If you do want just one combat skill to invest in tho make it Firearms. Guns are really effective, much like in the real world.

      Finally, what Contracts to chose. Really comes down to what you want to do. Changelings don't have allot in the psychic/telekinetic department - that really belongs more to psychics and mages. Judging from the videos linked you might like Artifice 5 for mad science, Animation or Communion for minion summoning, or Dream or Forge for item summoning.

          Changeling is one of the best in the series, because there are practically no limits to what you can do. Arcadia is the world of dreams and nightmares, so the possibilities are literally anything you can imagine.

          The only downside is that once the Lost enter the mortal world again and become Changelings, much of the power they once possessed is greatly decreased. If they had the power to fly in their Durance, for example, they find they can only glide now. Your Changeling may have been a powerful Jedi who had the power to fling star ships around, but after their escape, they'd probably have trouble moving a large rock for a while.




          And as Helel indicated, your Seeming and your Kith are based on how you were used in your Durance.


          Beasts - Lived as wild / domesticated animals.

          Ogres - Forced to resort to brutality to survive in Arcadia.

          Wizened - Menial slave labor.

          Darklings - Forced to live in the shadows.

          Elementals - Forces of nature or part of the environment.

          Fairest - Social elites and prized possessions.


          The Fairest have a Kith known as Draconic, which also deals with animals. But unlike Beasts who were simple animals, Draconics were magical creatures of great prestige, intelligence and splendor. Things like dragons, griffins, sphinxes and the like.


          Don't focus on what your character looks like when picking a Kith, focus on what they represent.

                  Hmm, I think there's a jewel elemental Kith that deals with healing too. Di Cang if I'm not mistaken (haven't checked in a while). It doesn't actually heal them, but it does block out pain and suffering from those it uses its blessing on.


                  Keep in mind that you can be a member of one Seeming and look like another. Farwalkers, for example, are Ogres who resemble beast-men like the Sasquatch, Yeti and so on. And then, of course, are the Draconic I mentioned who look like animals, but are actually Fairest.

                  Think more about what your changeling's seeming / kith represents, then base your appearance off that. If you focus exclusively on what they are (instead of liberal interpretations), the possibilities are much more restricted.




                  For example, if you want to make Carbuncle from Final Fantasy:




                  Carbuncle: Is a turquoise colored fox with a ruby embedded in its forehead, it is one of the weakest and yet most useful Espers in the series. The ruby has the power to create a magical barrier that repels spells back at the caster. Carbuncle is sometimes depicted as being very playful and occasionally helps summoners locate other powerful Espers.


                  Seeming - Beast (fox based)

                  Kiths - True Friend (playful and helpful) + Di Cang (protective aura created by the jewel in its forehead)
