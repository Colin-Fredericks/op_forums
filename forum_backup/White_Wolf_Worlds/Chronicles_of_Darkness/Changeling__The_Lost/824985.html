    Multi-Kith Merit in 2E?

    02-06-2016, 01:24 PM
    Would the Multi-Kith merit from the first edition of Changeling be appropriate for the second edition, given the maleable nature of the kiths? I ask because, the more I look at the prospective list of kiths we have been shown already, the more I want to make a Changeling with the Airtouched effects but more in terms of physical prowess, with their keeper having been wanting them to either entertain them fighting "monsters" like a television show costumed hero. Problem is, that description also matches the Playmate kith, which I would also enjoy having for this Changeling.

    Do note, I'm not asking if this merit will be included, more if it should be included. Part of why the Multi-Kith merit was effective in 1E was at how relatively minor the effects of the kiths were, whereas those effects are more pronouned in 2E (Chimeras in particular have powerful utlity no matter which gameline they're in for having a pronounced ability to negate supernatural effects on them, and Gravewrights have power over ghosts to rival Sin Eaters).

                                              The problem with requiring high Wyrd as a gate, is that since Kith is supposed to reflect what happened in your Durance, you're basically forcing people into putting a huge amount of Merit dots into raising Wyrd and getting the Merit, since it makes the most sense at start.

                                              I don't know what the final version might look like, but given how much more potent the Kith Blessings are already, I'd rather something where Dual Kith grows with Wyrd rather that require it. I would do something like having neither Kith Blessing express fully (most have multiple effects) at Wyrd 1, and then as Wyrd goes up more aspects express until you hit high enough to get the full benefits of both Kiths (or maybe even more than that).
