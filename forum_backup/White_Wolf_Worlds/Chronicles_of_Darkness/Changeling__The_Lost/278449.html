    Fictional Characters as Lost - Fate/Stay Night - Just for Fun

    10-11-2014, 07:49 PM
    So, as a fan of the Nasuverse/Type Moon setting, I at one point had this thought wondering what if the entire Nasuverse was some Keeper's scenario and all the characters were people brainwashed into believing they were the characters. Perhaps not even always the same people fitting into each role. Assuming some managed to escape through the Hedge, perhaps by accident, and came to the real world thinking they were those fictional characters.

    What I came up with for Rin/Saber/Shirou was something like this:

    Rin Tohsaka - Fairest Dual Kith - Shadowsoul Succubus. Shadowsoul because of her tendency to use intimidation fairly frequently. Succubus because in the Visual Novel game paths there is a sex scene in each major story path that consists of Rin deciding that tantric sex will solve some major life-threatening problem. Two paths its her and Shirou and in one path it is her, Saber and Shirou. Even though those are parallel timelines wherein she was supposed to be a virgin, if it were Durance, they could simply be wiping her mind each time to make her forget they've done these things before.

    Saber/Arturia Pendragon - Fairest Dual Kith - Draconic Gandharva. Draconic because she is supposed to have the heart or spirit of a dragon (though replace the bonus Brawl with bonus Weaponry). Gandharva because Saber's Charisma and aura of command is rather extreme in the games and anime. She'd probably have more contracts in the nature of Stone and Vainglory, stuff related to melee combat and leading people into battle.

    Shirou Emiya - Wizened Dual Kith - Soldier Smith. Soldier because of his affinity for Blades and Smith because his basic magical ability is to either reinforce objects which later became creating objects from thin air, especially swords. I can't think of anyway within the Changeling rules to duplicate Unlimited Blade Works. The closest I could think would be if you took the Elemental Contracts with regard to "blades" and had him summoning a rain of blades. I mean, UBW is essentially a state of altered reality in which every sword he has every seen in his life exists, stuck into the ground, complete and perfect even to the point of duplicating magical abilities and even the latent sapience in some of those swords that have almost a mind of their own.


    Anybody else got some fictional characters that might be interesting if it turned out their lives were Keeper's game?

                                                  In sense of a loose nod, I had Gaia responsible for creation of Gangrel progenitor, but that's because she believes strongly in the survival of the strongest as the guiding principle in how species should evolve, and thus she constantly challenges her children. In a world full of danger, supernatural and mundane, humans are more pressed to develop such traits that increase their collective survival as the species. Including tools and technology to fend off the predators, as anything steming from human ingenuity is valid for her. Also, the weakness to sunlight and fire with which Gangrel are burdened makes it more challenging for them to prey on humans. If she "blesses" someone, it always comes with more burden to overcome than benefits. She was actually amused how the first Gangrel struggled with the Beast and weaknesses.

                                                  Unlike Nasuverse Gaia, she actually is fond of humans (in a twisted way), but like a mother that wants best for her children she exposes them to hardships so they get stronger.

                                                  PS. Other Kindred Clan have different origins than Gangrel, I but I haven't figured out yet how they came to be in my personal mythology.
