                                            So each Crown is displayed differently and as different objects but only one explicitely says that it is located on the head. See below:

                                            [Spring]
                                            The Crown wreathes around the queen's head, cascading down her shoulders to a resplendent robe of young blooms.

                                            [Summer]
                                            Mahogany branches and emerald leaves braid together in the Crown, wrapping down into a magnificent breastplate.

                                            [Autumn]
                                            Red and yellow leaves crown the queen, fanning out behind her like great wings.

                                            [Winter]
                                            Frozen ashes make the Crown, flowing down into a shimmering mantle of gray and silver.

                                            - Spring's Crown specifically mentions that it rests on the head before bustling into a robe.

                                                Originally posted by xiongrey View Post
                                                A few questions, two of them are cross posts from the kickstarter topic (since this is the more appropriate place to put questions.)

                                                1. Can a Mage accidentally exit from one's oneiros into a Dreaming Rode in the Hedge?
                                                Unlikely, but anything is possible. I would also argue that such a gateway would trigger Passive Mage Sight.

                                                2. Can the Dreaming roads enter into a Beasts labrynth/soul stuff (not sure where the Beast's 'Soul' lies but I hear it has something to do with a Dreamscape.
                                                The Beast stuff lies in the Primordial Dream which, despite the name, isn't a dream but part of the barrier between the Temenos and the Anima Mundi. You can enter into either the Temenos or Anima Mundi, so the Primordial Dream is likely included there as well. All else fails, you get to the Primordial Dream by entering the Temenos and walking a bit until the PD.

                                                3. Another quick question, but this time on Court Crowns.

                                                So each Crown is displayed differently and as different objects but only one explicitely says that it is located on the head. See below:

                                                - Spring's Crown specifically mentions that it rests on the head before bustling into a robe.
                                                - Summer's Crown says that it rests where a normal breastplate would, but it words it as "wrapping down into..." which makes it ambiguous if the crown is also on the head or not.
                                                - Autumn's Crown says that it is on the back, but the wording before it is "leaves crown the queen". The wording of 'crown' as a lowercase verb would indicate it resting on the head as well but again: ambiguous.
                                                - Winter's Crown says that it is a mantle (a cloak/cape mantle) and so would indicate it rests on the shoulders but again the wording of "flowing down into a..." is ambiguous.

                                                Spring's is not ambiguous at all, Winter's is less ambiguous than the others (as the cape can be the one 'flowing down') but it is still not clear.
                                                There's part of the chapter that talks about how you can just absorb the "crown" into the Mantle / Mein, or physically manifest it, or turn it into a token. In fact, you can actually change all three states during the course of your season.

                                                All in all, I wouldn't peg on any one manifestation for any season, and just go by what seems to be appropriate for each person in question.
