                    Make a pledge to never deal with a huntsman.

                    <Huntsman, disguised as another person> Oh hey would you pick up some milk for me? Here's the money and I'll let you keep the change for doing it.

                    And then suddenly we have an unwitting oathbreaker *and* the changeling the Huntsman disguised themselves as is probably about to get strung up.

                    Make a pledge to never make a deal with a huntsman to get a token of reprieve.

                    <Huntsman, again disguised as someone else> Hey would you do seemingly innocuous thing that gets me closer to my quarry in some fashion you could never conceivably know? Okay cool. btdubs, here is a token of reprieve for all of your good service and/or here is a goblin fruit I totally didn't hollow out and put a token of reprieve in just to fuck with you.

                    Same thing.

                    -------------

                    Using pledges to keep out the huntsmen puts you in an arms race against your ability to word things against the huntsmen's ability to exploit them. It's much like the war against computer viruses, except your antivirus can only update once per year/season/etc. and if it ever fails once the whole program collapses in an irreplaceable way. If you really think this is such a good idea, feel free to try and craft some fool proof pledges and I'll show you how a huntsman can have a field day with them.
