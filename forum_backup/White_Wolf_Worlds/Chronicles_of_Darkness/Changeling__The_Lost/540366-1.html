                                        Originally posted by proindrakenzol View Post
                                        That they have Tokyo as a major setting for every splat?
                                        And for whatever it's worth, this is literally just for three big reasons:

                                        1) I like big cosmopolitan setting books. I really loved Chicago. However, those books are wildly impractical, and are unlikely to ever get approved under our current model. So like the way Radar from MASH smuggled a jeep by dismantling it and sending it piece by piece, I'm taking the opportunity to build a setting like that piece by piece.

                                        2) I know Tokyo better than any city aside from LA. I feel like LA's gotten plenty of attention from White Wolf over the years, so I wanted to do something different. I really disliked World of Darkness: Tokyo.

                                        3) I like to play with conceptual continuity. I like the idea of a little concept that's in a lot of different books. In the case of Tokyo, it's my way of playing with the assumptions of the game lines a little bit. I've been playing and writing these games for a long time. The Tokyo sections of these books are a chance for me to do something different, and both entertain and challenge myself as a writer a little by doing something left of center.

                                        (Well, also Tokyo's massive population makes it a really viable choice for such a project. But, yeah.)
