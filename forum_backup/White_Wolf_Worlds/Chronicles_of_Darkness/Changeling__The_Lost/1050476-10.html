                        Originally posted by Gurkhal View Post
                        If I'd like to run a character or NPC as a satyr inspired by Greek mythology, what kind of kith would be adviced for that? I look through but didn't find anything I really liked.
                        There's many routes you could go for that. The simplest would be a Beast Seeming with whichever kith most accents the aspect of satyrs that you favor. A Runnerswift or Steepscrambler could both work if you're looking for athletic ability. If you want them to have an enchanting song, you could re-skin the Nightsinger to use pipes rather than singing. A Brewer could offer anyone the finest wine around. Those are just a few thoughts off the top of my head.
