    I want to share some of the source material that inspired by idea for the high wyrd fairest to give people an idea of how I envision playing such a character.

    Tomie: Complete Deluxe Edition
    The complete classic horror series, now available in a single deluxe volume. Murdered again and again, one girl always comes back for more...

    Tomie Kawakami is a femme fatale with long black hair and a beauty mark just under her left eye. She can seduce nearly any man, and drive them to murder as well, even though the victim is often Tomie herself. While one lover seeks to keep her for himself, another grows terrified of the immortal succubus. But soon they realize that no matter how many times they kill her, the world will never be free of Tomie.
    Tomie: Complete Deluxe Edition (Junji Ito): Ito, Junji: 9781421590561: Amazon.com: Books

    Fatale, Book 1: Death Chases Me
    • Secrets, lies, horror, lust, and monsters from the time before time all collide in Fatale: Death Chases Me. In present day, a man meets a woman who he becomes instantly obsessed with, and in the 1950s, this same woman destroys the lives of all those who cross her path, on a quest for... what? Ed Brubaker and Sean Phillips' bestselling series will leave you craving more!
    Amazon.com: Fatale, Book 1: Death Chases Me (9781607065630): Brubaker, Ed, Phillips, Sean, Stewart, Dave: Books

    Fatale, Book 2: The Devil's Business
    • In 1970s Los Angeles, Josephine can't hide from the forces of Hollywood, Satanic Cults, and creepy 8mm films collected by wealthy deviants. And when a struggling actor and his wounded friend cross her path, all hell will break loose, leaving ripples that echo all the way to modern time, where Nicolas Lash falls deeper into Josephine's spell.
    Amazon.com: Fatale, Book 2: The Devil's Business (9781607066187): Brubaker, Ed, Phillips, Sean, Stewart, Dave: Books
    Fatale Volume 3: West of Hell
    • From the dark days of the depression, to the middle ages and the old west, these tales of horror and myth and the mystery of the Femme Fatale reveal secrets even our heroine doesn't know about yet. Bold and experimental, this is pulp noir horror at its finest!

    Amazon.com: Fatale Volume 3: West of Hell (9781607067436): Ed Brubaker, Sean Phillips, Dave Stewart, Bettie Breitweiser: Books
    Fatale Volume 4: Pray For Rain
    • Sex, drugs, rock-n-roll, and ritual killers all collide in the strangest and most exciting volume of Fatale yet. A strange woman with no memory stumbles into the lives of a struggling grunge band in mid-'90s Seattle. Is she their muse? Will she save them from "One-Hit Wonder" status? Or will she be something much worse, especially since there's a psycho killer on her trail?
    Amazon.com: Fatale Volume 4: Pray For Rain (Fatale (Image Comics)) (9781607068358): Ed Brubaker, Elizabeth Breitweiser, Sean Phillips: Books
    Fatale Volume 5: Curse the Demon
    • The final book in the epic FATALE saga by Brubaker and Phillips. All the threads have come together, and the mysterious immortal Femme Fatale Josephine has one last chance to escape her fate.
    Amazon.com: Fatale Volume 5: Curse the Demon (9781632150073): Brubaker, Ed, Phillips, Sean, Breitweiser, Elizabeth: Books

                I have been trying to consider how to best describe how a changeling's identity is warped and changed and the best comparison is Hunter the Reckoning: Fall From Grace. As a hunter becomes more successful in the Hunt they are given greater access to greater powers including Divine Edges. However, the price is having the hunter's personality totally rewritten and a divine madness.

                What happens to a changeling is somewhat similar. Their blessing's evolve to almost divine abilities, but the cost is their personality starting to be rewritten to what their Keeper originally desired and their frailties. Some changeling's fight against this, through developing their court mantle AND entitlements, but even here their personality is rewritten to better represent their court and the story they have chosen to represent/entitlement.

                To save a little of their original personality, some Changeling's develop their kiths, which was the quality that first drew their Keeper to them.

                                        Originally posted by Madhatter View Post
                                        Entitlements are one of the ways you create your own story/identity. I envision your seeming being an identity given to you by your Keeper. I CAN see you seeming evolving based on the changeling's actions and how it connects to their kith in some way.
                                        That's not really what I was focusing on when I brought up Entitlements. I merely meant to point at how Entitlements are added on top of the normal Changeling template to evolve the changeling.

                                        My point about individualising your NPCs is not that they get to choose how they evolve. It's that the Gentry get a lot more room to decide how their former wards evolve.
                                        You mentioned how Fairest could be knights in shining armour, succubi, ballerinas and banshees. I think the best way to go about that is developing "evolved Seeming traits" for the particular archetype the Keeper is going for rather than trying to fit all possible subtypes of a Seeming under a single set of evolved traits. The evolved Fairest traits you've developed works a bit for all four of those subtypes, but they also fail to reinforce anything about those subtypes other than their beauty.
                                        What I would do is:
                                        • Have the changeling chosen to become a shining knight exude an aura of respect, but be compelled to always act chivalrous.
                                        • Have the changeling chosen to become a succubus irresistable to look at, but unable to gain Glamour through any means other than Harvesting/Reaping from people.
                                        • Have the changeling chosen to be a ballerina enchant people through their dance, but also unable to stop dancing whenever they have started.
                                        • Have the changeling chosen to be a banshee have the most enchanting and/or terrifying voice ever, but unable to ever speak without wailing as loud as they can.
                                        And unlike Entitlements, these roles would be completely forced upon the changelings by their Keepers. It's definitely based on their forced Seemings, but individualised to fit the plans their Keepers had for them specifically instead of all of them developing the exact same evolved traits. Those four Fairest still face the same problem, of being forced into a role their Keeper has chosen for them, but now they have unique challenges to overcome and become a lot more interesting for your players. It also gives you a lot more freedom in designing your NPCs because you'd be able to create a lot more varied conflicts for these NPCs. The one downside is that it's a lot more work this way, but it's work I'd welcome if I were to ST a similar game.

                                        But this is my last post in this thread because I really do feel like an asshole who's saying "no, bad way of running your game" every time I post about this, but that is not my goal. In the end, you do what you think works best for your game. I'm just writing this last post because I think some of the core points of my position hadn't come across clearly and I just wanted to explain them.

                                                    The reason I am being so stubborn is that most of my fairest characters were not physically beautiful as mortals (though they did gain strike looks when becoming changelings).
