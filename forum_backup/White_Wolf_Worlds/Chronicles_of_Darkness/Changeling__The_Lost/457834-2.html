            I second Poets Of The Fall, Nox Arcana, Nightwish, Pan's Labyrinth, Nightmare Before Christmas and Queen II.

            Several songs by Creature Feature have a spooky vibe that reminds me of me darker parts of Changeling. "The Unearthly Ones" and "The Greatest Show Unearthed" sound like they're being sung by the Gentry, for example.

            Skillet has some powerful lyrics that are fitting for those moments that changelings share. "Awake And Alive" is like a skyward scream of Agency and Hope, while "Falling Inside The Black" is a reminder of the lives that were taken from them and all the sorrow that comes with that loss.

            "Forevermore", "Cinnamon Girl" and "I Would Anything For Love" all remind me of the "Heart's Oath" Pledge that changelings in love swear to one another.

            In contrast to those three love songs, there's "Stuck With You", "I Hate Everything About You" and "Taste The Flesh" are closer to the questionable relationships that many changelings get themselves into.

            Dvar is a really weird band that I can't even categorize, and all their songs are in a made up language that sounds like it came from hobgoblins or a dream. "Iih Rah" brings to mind a changeling being chased through the Hedge by many tribal hobgoblins. Try listening to their songs with your eyes closed and imagining scenes in the dreamscape that fit them, it's almost like the band was made for that sort of thing.

            More songs in the next post...

            Bye.

                Enya has lots of beautiful songs, but the one that sticks out for me is "Long Long Journey", which speaks for itself.

                "Aquarium" has a whimsical air to it that makes me imagine the more pleasant yet mysterious parts of the Hedge, with pixies flying about.

                Gheorghe Zamfir's way of playing the pan flute is really good for setting up that mystical forest atmosphere. I especially like "El Condor Pasa".

                "Come Little Children" is a Gentry using enchantment to lure its victims away.

                "Once Upon A December" becomes rather creepy if you think of it as a changeling feeling nostalgia for the wondrous parts of their Durance.

                Castlevania OSTs work excellently as well. My favorite OST is easily the one from Lament Of Innocence, which includes "House Of Sacred Remains" and "Ghostly Theatre".

                The same can be said for Shadow Of The Colossus OST, which deserves a trophy. The songs for the overworld fit nicely during an exploration or flashbacks, while the epic songs for the Colossi themselves make me feel ready to take on the True Fae.
