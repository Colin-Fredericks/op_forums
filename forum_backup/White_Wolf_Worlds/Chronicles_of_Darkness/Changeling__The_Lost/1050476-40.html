    Away from my book as well as lazy and have three Harvesting questions:

    1) Are there any benefits to an Exceptional Success on a Harvest roll besides gaining 5 or more Glamour (which is pretty sweet already)?

    2) If the former is true, can kith abilities (specifically the ones that modify ES) effect Harvest rolls?

    3) Can you harvest emotions induced or strengthened by Contracts? Pretty sure the answer is a hard no to prevent possible massive glamour loops and weird fridge logic, but I just want to be sure.

        Even though I recently read CTL 2e from cover to cover, I'm still coming to grips with this splat, so I don't know if I can answer your questions with any certainty. I will give it my best shot though:

        1) There is nothing in CTL 2e about gaining an exceptional success on a harvest roll, so I'm going to assume nothing happens other than gaining more glamour.

        2) I don't think there is a hard rule against using your Kith abilities in conjunction with harvesting, but what would be the point? Gaining an exceptional success on 3 successes instead of 5 isn't going to do anything for your harvest, unless you rule that gaining an exceptional success on 3 dice means the same as gaining 5 successes, thus giving you 5 Glamour for 3 successes.

        3) The core CTL 2e book says nothing about this, but if you think it's safer to say "no, you can't harvest emotions induced or strengthened by Contracts" then go for it. With these sort of things I usually decide on a case by case basis. If I think it makes sense and doesn't break the game, I'll allow it. If I think my players are trying to game the system or gain an unfair advantage (unfair from a game perspective), I'll no longer allow it.


        Also, yes, I 'liked' your post because of the house-rule suggestion.

            The general rule for ES is to gain a relevant Condition (or impart one on another character, if it makes sense). Just do that whenever something doesn't list any Exceptional effects. Perhaps something to make it easier to manipulate the emotional state of those you harvested from, or a Condition on them giving them a harder time expressing the harvested feeling for some time because you gained so much Glamour from them.

            As for the third question, I would compare how much Glamour the Contract costs to use in that way with how many extra dice on the Harvest roll you can reliably get from using the Contract. If you can reliably gain more Glamour than it costs by using the Contract, I'd probably rule that it can't be used.
            Contracts that were to enforce a feeling rather than manipulating a character towards it might also be a no from me just because it's less the character and more the magic. It's their feelings, but not their own feelings, if that makes sense.

                            Yeah, so if you are in dream form and you happen to start a combat inside someone's dream, you basically roll Presence + Brawl or Presence + Weaponry or Manipulation + Firearms, instead of Strength + Brawl or Strength + Weaponry or Dexterity + Firearms.

                            It basically means that Social primary characters will likely be the strongest in dream form. I find this a weird but interesting design choice personally. It means that your anti-social Ogre buddy who always reliably crushes skulls with his fists might suddenly find himself rather useless in dream combat, while the weak but socially savvy Fairest is potentially the heavy hitter in dream form.
