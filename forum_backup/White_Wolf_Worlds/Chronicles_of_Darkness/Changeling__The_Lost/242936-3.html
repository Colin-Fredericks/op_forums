    Question: Do you intend on "Contracts on the Fly" or "Improvised Contracts" to be a thing at all, in any form? Goblin Vows, etc etc. Do you intend on a "Wyrd Sorcery" system at all, similar to Blood Magic or Mage or what have you?

    Also, been thinking about dicepools. I also feel icky about Attribute/Skill + Wyrd, but I also feel that it works if not better idea can be had. I also can't think of a better dicepool at this moment.

    A quick trip into the potentially absurd: do we need dicepools at all? What if changelings buy success(s) or stages or effect or etc by spending more and more glamour or fulfilling catches or standing on their head. Whatever. Penalties are replaced with a surcharge on the cost.

    Alternative or perhaps synergistic with the above, what if Contracts are something you roll against instead of something that the Changeling rolls? If a Changeling has a contract that says they can pick up a truck. Then they pick up the truck. Period. No roll. If someone wants to contest that then they have to roll with some penalty.

    Just spitballing out of left field here. *shrugs*
