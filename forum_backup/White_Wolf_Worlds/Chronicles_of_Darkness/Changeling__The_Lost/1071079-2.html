                    So I'm signed up for a 2e changeling game, playing a Darkling hitman for a court based on the Triads (in a free hold based on the various Mafia like orgainizations around the world. Italian, Irish, Chinese, Russian, and Japanese). When I first heard of Magic of the silver screen this sounded great! A contract that would like my knife fingered razor hand do his dirty work undetected, even walk out of the building in a blood soaked suit and have people just think they saw a film shooting...

                    Then I read the whole thing and...not as useful to me as I would like. I think I need something a little different.
                    What I need is a contract that makes people forget my face (ala laughing man from Ghost in the Shell: Stand Alone Complex). A contract that corrupts the memory of everyone who looks at me for a time. Let's see....

                    Sanguine Vissage Concealed (Shield - Common)
                    The changeling calls out to nature of the Witness and demands to be shielded from their acusitions. Corrupting the memories of all who view them.
                    Cost: 1 Glamour
                    Dice Pool: Presence + Stealth + Wyrd - Resolve + Tolerance. Use highest in group.
                    Dramatic Failure: Everyone notices you, and can remember every detail about you with uncanny clarity. +3 to recall or draw the changeling.
                    Failure: Nothing happens, good luck.
                    Success: Your face and identity are hidden from others. Those who look back upon their memories see you as if looking though a shower door, this effect lasts for 1 hour. This imposes a -3 on any attempt to recall or draw the changeling.
                    Exceptional Success: The memories of those who looked upon you become so disjointed they don't just forget what you looked like but forget which way you were headed, the color of your clothes, or if you got into a car or rode off on a iron pony. Subejects gain the amnesia condition, effecting memory of you only.

                    Beast: If the contract is a success your victims memories become even more scrambled and the remember you as an animal fitting for your Kith.
                    Darkling: An excepeptional sucess happens on 3 successes rather than the standard 5.
                    Loophole: The changeling paints their face in a victims blood before fleeing
                    Last edited by HaplessWithDice; 10-30-2017, 09:27 AM.
