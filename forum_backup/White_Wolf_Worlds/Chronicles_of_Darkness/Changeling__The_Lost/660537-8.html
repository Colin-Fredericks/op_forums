                        Artist -- Cool. Feels like a great fluff choice. I'd like clarification on what you can get out of an exceptional piece of art somewhere in the book. This is also a pretty nice Kith for social maneuvering.

                        Author -- I almost want to ask this to be opened up to other skills. Persuasive essays, propoganda posters, etc. should all really fall under this purview.

                        Blightbent -- Great all around. Does what I would want it to do if I were playing that type of character.

                        Bloodbrute -- It feels kind of like a bunch of random mechanical things were thrown into a pot, although that may be the writing style. It's very "she can do this... and this... and this... and this." Moving the unarmed damage portion closer to the improvised weapon portion and making the transition smoother would help. I'd also love it if the negate surprise component worked on everything, even if you had to make it not-perfect or if you had to make it cost a point of Glamour. The reason is that I feel like Bloodbrutes should *totally* have Ultimate Ninja Warrior contests with deadly result. Like a True Fae pointing at an obstacle course with a thing at the end and saying, "see how far you can get." That's cool. I'd also like it if Bloodbrutes just got the first dot in the Improvised Weapon Fighting Style instead of sort of getting part of it.

                        Off hand something like, "A bloodbrute is always ready for a fight. She gains the first dot in the Improvised Weapon Fighting Style (page XX) for free and her unarmed attacks can inflict 0L damage. By spending a point of Glamour, she can make any roll to detect surprise or a trap (page XX) a Rote action."

                        Brewer -- Then one day, a Brewer dipped his hand in the pacific ocean and almost all of the world's water into alchohal. This was not as cool as his fraternity said it probably would be--the dead fish smell was just dreadful.

                        I'm just being facetious and teasing. ^_^

                        A limit on quantity may be prudent, as would a clarification that touching a recepticle that holds the liquid works. Whether or not the brew is obvious to the drinker is also important to determine. Otherwise it's a pretty solid kith.

                        Bright One -- Cool power. Does exactly what I'd want it to. How to end the blinded tilt could use better wording. Technically, the blinded tilt implies that the person can't still see the bright one.

                        Broadback -- I feel like +Wyrd to doors and +Wyrd to contested rolls is a smidge redundant. Social Maneuvering is rarely used by the ST against player characters. One of those for fluff purposes and for NPCs is great. Together it kind of feels like a wasted choice. I'd also like more "able to drag things for miles without tiring" and less "not gonna listen to you." I just feel like if I'm taking Broadback, it's for the purposes of doing some impressive thing for a very long time. If you do change one of those, my vote is on keeping +Wyrd to doors and doing something else with the glamour spend portion. +Doors means that other people have to be as tenatious as the Broadback to make headway, which feels appropriate.

                        Brollachan -- I keep thinking this is an anime thing for some reason. Probably the -chan at the end of its name. Anyways, it feels like a pretty solid, all around concept. It is by far the most potent combat kith to date. May want to consider nixing the +2 bonus to grapples. The Insensate / Frightened thing is going to be used more by straight up attacking people and less by ambushing them from the shadows and disabling.

                        Chatelaine -- Pretty fun kith. I like the immunity and feel like it could lead to a lot of fun. Wouldn't the immunity by default clash with things, though? Getting the free success and the immunity and a glamour spend thing is kind of O_o in the face of the Author only getting the free success and one "other." It's not a huge deal, though.

                        Chimera -- I have some pretty strong concerns about the glamour spend portion of this Kith. Rote action on contested rolls to resist all supernatural powers makes them fairly unassailable by anything that isn't direct physical combat. I mean, ultimately this kith's strength is determined by how frequently contracts and creatures from the hedge rely on those powers, but it sets off my alarms.
