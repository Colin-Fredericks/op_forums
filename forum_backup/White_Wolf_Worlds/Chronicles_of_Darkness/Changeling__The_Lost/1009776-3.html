            And now... the voice of dissent. Changelings and mages, to me, seem to actually be opposites.

            Its no secret that Mages are considered the creme de le creme of supernaturals. They're 1%, the elite, the privileged people that look down on other supernaturals. They're encouraged to use their magic to bind subjects to study and analyze for their obsessions. Their very presence warps the world around them. Literally - their nimbus is pulling down parts of the Supernal and overwriting reality bit by bit, and you're slowly transforming people via Nimbus Tilt. Changelings, meanwhile, are defined by -fleeing- from the Gentry - magically privileged beings that shape the realm around them at whim. They're the ones that were the subjected to all the things that mages are inclined to do.

            In a very real way, the Awakened are like humanized versions of the Gentry. And changelings bear striking similarities to the entities that mages bind to their purpose. Now, at high Wisdom/Clarity, you can see the difference - both mages and changelings are still people, unlike the Gentry/bound spirits. However, the less Wise / perceptive lose sight of that, and the sympathy breaks down. Fate magic, by all rights, should be horrifying to changelings. The Lost rely on pledges being relatively consistant for mental stability. Mages can throw that out the window. Meanwhile, changelings are extremely slippery, avoiding magic and any attempts to hold them still for study.

            This all creates a far more complicated relationship than just meeting up and then possibly getting along after some misunderstandings at the nature of Arcadia.

                        Changeling Arcadia definitely has the feel of a Lower Depth to me, taking into account how broad a catch-all that term can be. "To the Fallen World as the Fallen World is to the Supernal" seems like a decent description of the realms of the Gentry - petty worlds ruled by small gods and defined by supernatural lies that even the most ordinary human may recognize as such. The second edition Mage book even states that symbols from the Fallen World may take on great significance and power in the Lower Depths, which seems to be true of Changeling Arcadia. It's also a realm that can be distinguished from the mortal world latgely in terms of what it lacks - a realm without death, without growth, without any truths aside from those lies the Gentry decide to believe...

                        This interpretation also has the advantage of making Changeling Arcadia a place mysterious and hostile to Mages, which they can only learn about second-hand. Most probably believe that, as with other Lower Depths, no one, Sleeper or Awakened, could possibly go there and return. And yet... Changelings do... and if a Mage were to discover that truth and accept that Changelings are more than just beings from the Lower Depths masquerading as something half-human, I'm sure that would become *very* curious very quickly.
