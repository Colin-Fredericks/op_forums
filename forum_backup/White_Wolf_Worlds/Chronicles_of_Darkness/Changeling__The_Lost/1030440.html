    How to pick Shadow Name's for a consiliuns worth of npc's..

    01-12-2017, 01:19 AM
    Hey All,

    I'm running into a creative block with picking shadow names for all the npc's for the chronicle I'm writing. Having decided I just can't have everyone named after a character from Arthurian mythology or the Mabinogion I rapidly feel like I'm scraping the bottom of barrel. I also realise at some point I'm gonna need to come up with a improve names mid game for that-third-arrow-mastitgos-from-the-right ect and there is definitely seems to be a law of diminishing returns when it comes to plumbing lists of various mythological figures and deities, abd most awakened would be really weary with those sorts of Shadow names because of the metaphysical wiether that thier narrative brings.

    What resources or other sources of inspiration do you use for Shadow Names?

      Generally speaking, I go with mythology and religion; gods, saints, angels, demons, etc. There's tons of resources on that out there.

      That said, you really don't need to go with that. Many Mages will choose Shadow Names in purely descriptive terms; sometimes in English, sometimes in another language. Shadow Names with a cultural weight are a double-edged sword, since they draw you into their narrative, whereas a name like Golden Eyes or Convergence-of-Rivers (Goldie and River to their friends?) is much more personal; it may not have mythological weight, but you can give it such through your actions and deeds.

      If you've got multiple Mages from the same cabal, name them on a theme if you feel like it. Otherwise, just improvise at the time. If they're just a bit character, don't worry too much.

          Break out the adjectival phrases and tack them on a regular pseudonym picked out of a baby-names book — Addison of the Iron Spade, Marilyn the Red, Two-Hit Luke, that sort of thing.

          Alternately, stick with less storied names. Themed cabals are good for this — a cabal built around the seasons or the noble gases or characters from the Wizard of Oz gives you at least a handful of things to call a wizard.
