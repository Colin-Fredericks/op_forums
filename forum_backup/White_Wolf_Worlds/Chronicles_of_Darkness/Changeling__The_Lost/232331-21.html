    Originally posted by proindrakenzol View Post
    For those that enjoyed doing Kithless: maybe a sort of &quot;Blank Slate&quot; Kith where the choice of Seeming ends up being the primary definer as an option? Like, &quot;your keeper used you as whatever was needed at the time: playmate or prey, wildfire or guard, servant or thief; always different, always mutable&quot; and &quot;your agency finally defined you, before you were in a state of flux and by your own will you solidified into something more.&quot;
    I rather like the notion of the Fae-Touched and Enchanted that appeared in Equinox Road, though their mechanics needed work: basically, they’re humans who were taken to Faerie and who managed to get away before the transformation into a changeling was complete. In effect, Enchanted Mortals were only there long enough to acquire the ability to see through the Mask; and the Fae-Touched were there long enough that they started to transform, but didn’t quite get far enough to become changelings.

    For the Enchanted, I could easily see taking the same route that God-Machine Chronicle did with “Unseen Sense: God-Machine”: there are certain mortals who, for various reasons, automatically see through the Mask. The reasons can vary drastically, including but not limited to “you were taken to Faerie, but were rescued before it could change you too much”.

    The Fae-Touched, OTOH, ought to be Changeling’s counterpart to Vampire’s Ghouls: they were taken to Arcadia, and it did start to change them; but they managed to get out before it had finished breaking down their soul. A Fae-Touched would be a lesser template with “Wyrd 0” and several Changeling-like features; but they’d still be human, having made the harrowing trip back through the Hedge before their Integrity was reduced to zero. The relevance to this thread: with the new takes on Kith and Seeming, I cannot see a Fae-Touched having a Kith: that becomes part and parcel of actually becoming a changeling, and you cannot have one without the other. But I can see them getting a Seeming under its new definition. So if you want a Seeming without a Kith, play a Fae-Touched mortal.

    Conversely, very few humans make it back as fae-touched; those who do had help, or get back half-mad (i.e., Integrity very nearly depleted); probably both.

    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

        So I read this entire thread on two bus rides and am sad I wasn't part of this from the beginning - while I still haven't gotten to play a game of CtL, I love the fluff and have done some CtL-related writing.... Anywho, big apology if my phone, which I am posting on, kills my formatting - when I get home tonight I will correct it if I can.

        So two things which I think might start new conversation.

        Firstly: if Seeming is Agency, does that mean when your Keeper catches you, they will take it away? Seeming is an identity you have used as a launching-off point, the act or process which has reframed your invention of self, an active portion of you not made by your Keeper. They might want prey, but not clever prey - servants, but not brutal servants - etc. It is more integral to you than a mask they are taking off, but still little more than a bad habit or flaw they need to smooth out. I have this image of someone’s Beast-ness being peeled away like the skin of a banana, leaving the poor re-captured Lost as baseless and stunned as could be possible. Y’know. Awesome.


        Secondly, there has been alot of discussion about how Darklings betray or sacrifice, or Elementals unbind or disassociate, or whatever… that got me thinking of psychological defense mechanisms, which I think are just as quite flavorful to a game of Beautiful Madness. According to wikipedia, Anna Freud numbered them as

        1. Repression
        2. Regression
        3. Reaction formation
        4. Isolation
        5. Undoing
        6. Projection
        7. Introjection
        8. Turning against one’s own person
        9. Reversal into the opposite
        10. Sublimation or displacement.

        I don’t know about you, but I am immediately drawing parallels to different Seemings - different tactics and techniques of preserving or re-creating the self and different ways of leveraging your personal currency during your time of distress to move beyond it.

        Hope some of this helps! ^_^

                            It also seems backwards to read it as "Seeming decides your escape."

                            Your escape decides your Seeming. If you write up a backstory first and apply the character traits second, your Seeming is determined by how you wrote the escape. You're only limited if you approach it from the intent of "I'm making a Fairest," but that's because you're limiting yourself by deciding your Seeming first and then writing your background to match it.

                            There's nothing wrong with either approach from a player use perspective, but if you're picking traits first you're always going to limit your background options because you need to explain how those traits came about. The limitation is in the style of character design, not the concept of Seemings being defined by the character's escape from the Gentry.

                                Another thing to remember is the Seeming is described as agency, not specifically escape, which is one reason I think it's important to focus on the Why aspect. It internalizes the conflict instead of making it about flipping switch X or performing action Y. While the end result can certainly be a burst of action or motion or decisiveness, what's important is the build up that. It might take years, it might take seconds, but that emotional tipping point strikes me as what's important about the way new Seemings are going to work.

                                                Please bear with me here, as I’m still trying to organize my thoughts as I write this… (also, I don’t have my books handy; so I may botch the terminology.)

                                                Maybe Kiths and Seemings shouldn’t be completely decoupled. That is, give each Kith a “favored Seeming” that serves to organize the Kiths into six groups that parallel the Seemings. Conceptually, a Kith’s “favored Seeming” hearkens back to 1e’s conception of Seemings as collections of Kiths that are related by how the changeling was treated in captivity: Kiths that favor the Bestial Seeming were ones resulting from the Gentry treating the changeling like an animal; Kiths that favor the Fairest Seeming were ones resulting in being “pampered” by one’s jailors; Kiths that favor the Wizened Seeming were ones that were somehow diminished by their time in Faerie; and so on. Mechanically, a Kith’s “favored Seeming” may manifest as a curse shared by all such Kiths. In terms of game design, the purpose of giving Kiths favored Seemings is to let you aggregate Kiths into thematically similar groups, and to speak in terms of 1e’s definition of Seemings when it makes sense to do so: you can speak of Elemental Kiths (i.e., Kiths that favor the Elemental Seeming), Darkling Kiths (i.e., Kiths that favor the Darkling Seeming), Ogre Kiths, and so on.

                                                A Kith’s Favored Seeming would not be the same as the changeling’s actual Seeming, which would represent how the changeling regained her agency as described above. Conceptually, the relationship between a Kith’s Favored Seeming and a changeling’s Seeming is that the former represents the most natural means of reclaiming one’s agency from the type of abuse the changeling endured in Faerie (which requires specifying the type of abuse), while the latter represents the actual means the changeling used to reclaim agency. Mechanically, 1e’s Seeming mechanics would get broken up between these two things, with a 1e-style Seeming Curse going with 2e-style Kiths of a given Favored Seeming (and being directly reflective of the “scars” inflicted on them in their Durance) and a 1e-style Seeming Blessing going with a 2e-style Seeming (and being directly reflective of the changeling’s agency). Also, a Kith’s favored Seeming could be used as the basis for “Kith Contracts” that are designed with a certain collection of Kiths in mind, existing alongside Seeming Contracts that are designed to capitalize on the changeling’s “Seeming Blessing” in one way or another.

                                                I would not try to bias the selection of a changeling’s Seeming toward his Kith’s favored Seeming in any mechanical way: no “system bribes” for correlating the two nor any form of “disincentives” aimed at not correlating them. Keep any such biasing purely in the realm of “fluff” and out of the realm of “crunch”.

                                                In a nutshell, break 1e-style Seemings in half, keeping their original role as “groupings of related Kiths”, “nature of the Durance”, and “frailties” in the half that gets incororated into Kiths while building 2e-style Seemings around 1e-style strengths and the notion of Seeming-as-agency.

                                                My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
