        Guys! Guys! Hear me out.

        Remember how Batman and Joker almost always take center stage when they show up, and how everyone else usually ends up as a narrative satellite?

        This made me think about making them both True Fae "best friends" that are locked in a really old Feud due to metaphysical force of habit and twisted affection. Let me elaborate a bit.

        In 2nd edition, the Seeming is a Badge/Scar Of Agency. So the Gentry only have "Seemings" for the purposes of determining what their Miens look like and what their Affinity Contracts are. They are both good at hiding, lying and scaring people, so they are likely pseudo Darklings. Joker looks like an exaggeration of his usual self: his clothes are even more garish and filthy with old bloodstains, foul chemical gases waft from his lapel-flower, blades poke out of his sleeves and other openings, his smile is practically another Gentry, his skin is paler than Death's, his hair is made of ill, greenish moonlight. Batman becomes Strix-like in appearance, but still humanoid and rippling with might. His armored suit becomes a bunch of floating obsidian plates that are united/shaped like an approximation of muscles.

        According to Equinox Road, changelings that reach Wyrd 10 might start having unbelievably potent dreams of their Durance, gradually dropping in Clarity until they hit rock-bottom. The changeling hears the Gentry calling him back home, does so and becomes one of Them.

        In "Batman" (1989), it was the Joker who created Batman. In "The Killing Joke", it was Batman who created Joker. This could be reflected in a mystery where players try to figure who Gentrified who in order to have some advantage over them and possibly end their Feud.

        As observed, Gotham would simply not exist in real life. Its crime rates would be unsustainable. Also, Gotham often has this sort of telepathic, dramatically appropriate weather. So let's turn Gotham into a Hedge Town that was built, broken and rebuilt many times by Batman and Joker. Hedge Gotham is like a bar for them: they go there for fun with no strings attached, rather than fighting in their actual Realms for Titles. Batman's Realm is like the Batcave, but with more emphasis on the "cave" part. The hi-tech stuff gets replaced with, I dunno, some kind of bloody Magitek? Joker's Realm is like a mercurial, nightmarish combination of circus, carnival, asylum and chemical laboratory.

        The Batfamily and the rest of the Rogues Gallery are probably a mixture of changeling/mortal Loyalists, hobgoblins and less important Gentry. You can portray one or more of them as members of other splats, of course. Whatever works.

        As for the inevitable question of how to portray Bruce Wayne as not-Batman, let's say that evidence points to Bruce being the Fetch that Joker left behind when he kidnapped Batman long ago. Instead of killing Fetch Bruce, Changeling Bruce spares him and gradually turns him into an ally. Fetch Bruce is still an ultra rich technology businessman with hands in various human stocks and even Goblin Markets. All the better to provide the mundane and Token bat-gadgets that Gentry Bruce can't reliably craft anymore.

        To make things even more confusing, let's say there is also an equivalent Fetch Jack for the Gentry Joker.

        Any thoughts? Compliments? Complaints? I'm all ears.

        Bye.

                                Never thought I would've found the occasion of posting this old thing but here's my take.

                                The Legend of the Dark City
                                The Feud around the Dream of Gotham
                                Premise

                                A powerful group of True Fae emulating/ or hailing from the dreams of the Batman Universe decided to express their need to conflict as a battle between their realms. Their Feud is a complex contract destined to last centuries and to enable the oath-circle to stave off stagnation and destruction for that time. It is important to note that these Fae are not the only ones emulating Batman and his foes, so others can join them or appear in the physical world.


                                Due to the wide popularity of their oneiric spawning ground, these Kindly Ones are very powerful, each has five titles described and most have one or two more active outside the Feud. The Feud itself is manifold; some of its forms are obviously violent, flocks of goblins smashing each other to expand their turf or the Dark King directly confronting one of its rivals Actors. Others are calmer like the quest of the Icy Knight to awaken its lost love or even The Breather will to infect its rival’s domain with fearful memes. The conflict goes up and down based on what makes the most compelling story, titles



                                Mr Freeze : The Knight Clad in Vestments of Sorrow Winter
                                Poison Ivy : The Lady of the Thorny Ruins Spring
                                Bane : The Prince of the Venomous Arena
                                Scarecrow : The Breather of Fearful Dreams Autumn


                                Joker : The Fool who Paints the Heavens Crimson South
                                Double-Face : The Double Faced King of Coins West
                                Penguin : The Bird who Takes Trinkets for Souls East
                                Al-Gul : The Demon Sovereign with Lazarus’ Face North

                                Batman : The Dark King of the City of Dread

                                Geography

                                For this Feud, the True Fae decided to congregate around the Batman’s Realm (figured by the Wonder tower) and open their borders wide. In practice, that means that, to mortals and changelings visitors rare as they may be, the city is larger than any mortal city with the landscape shifting in accordance to the ruler of the zone. Races of hobgoblins and sentient chimeras serve as servants and gangster and the war while others are purely civilians there for the villains to torment and the heroes to save. Even them, one must remember that the Feud-Stage is not and never has been a city and is even further away from reality than the Umbral reflection of one. Buildings change place in a whim, climate can switch seasons in one moment and each change affects more the mind than the body.

                                Characters

                                Mr Frezze : The Icy King In the Armor of Mourning
                                Main Actor : The Tearful Knight Clad In Vestments of Sorrow
                                Secondary Actor : The Unreachable Lady flying Through the Mist
                                Prop : The Armor of Mourning
                                Prop: The Instruments of the Hopeless Surgery
                                Realm : The Icy Spires of the Forsaken Castle

                                Poison Ivy : The Queen of the Forest upon the Ruins
                                Main Actor : The Lady Clad In Flowers and Tree
                                Secondary Actor: The Lord of Cats Tireless Hunter.
                                Wisps : The Protectors of Nature
                                Prop : The Flower of Black Mercy
                                Realm : The Thorny Ruins of the Urban Jungle

                                To precise according to the old design Appearance/Virtue-Sin/Goal-in-the-Feud/Arc

                                The Knight is constantly clad in the armor of mourning which grants him most of his power. He is a durable fighter able to manipulate the physical elements of the Endless Winter to destroy his opponents. The Legend he uses most often is to send his ghostly mate to a rival and try to recover her by all means. The pain he inflicts during his expeditions spread his domain.

                                His Lady is his perfect opposite save for their shared link with winter. She’s airy and mystical, light and emotional where he’s physical and intellectual. Her legend is strange, so strange some said she’s but an aspect of the Knight, the lady he cannot save. She wanders in the city affecting hobgoblins with sorrow and despair, preparing them for consumption.

                                His realm looks like a forgotten laboratory which could belongs equally to an alchemist or a scientist. By the rules of the Feud , one could turn the place against him but it is difficult. It is there that it creates ice goblins and others beasts to defend his domain.
