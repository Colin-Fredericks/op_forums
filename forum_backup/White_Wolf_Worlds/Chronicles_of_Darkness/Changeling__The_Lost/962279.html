              Onyx Path projects are like snowflakes.

              There's really no way to accurately predict how long a given project will take, or even how long a single stage of a given project will take, until it's almost finished. Pretty much, until something reaches the "In Layout" stage in the Monday meeting notes, don't count on it coming out within the year. It might, but don't count on it. Once it's "In Layout", it's probably a safe bet that it'll be done in a few months, but you never know, sometimes it's several. Once it hits "Proofing", then you can start getting hyped about an impending release. You won't get a release date until the Monday before the advance PDF comes out (at which point they'll say "It'll be out on Wednesday"), but that's the point where I let myself get more excited with each passing week. Sometimes that hype train keeps going for a couple months, but I know I've got enough steam for that, and your mileage may vary.

              As for Lost 2e specifically? With it still in the "Development" stage (not even post-editing development!) I think Fumus' "early 2017 estimate is very optimistic. If I were to take a shot in the dark, I'd say summer 2017, and I wouldn't even be confident in that estimate. In the mean time, there's been more than enough material previewed to playtest it, or to cobble together a 1.5e.

              Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.

                                              Originally posted by K9ine View Post
                                              Don't know much about it, I decided not to bother buying 1st edition because I love the 2e rules. What are the changes your speaking of?
                                              There have been a lot of changes made that we've seen. One of the biggest is the interaction between Seeming and Kith. Changeling's C-Splats, Seemings, were further divided into sort of sub-splats called Kiths in 1e. So where in Vampire a Daeva is always a Daeva and a Gangrel is always a Gangrel, in Changeling a Fairest could be a Dancer, or a Draconic, or a Truefriend, or a Muse... There were about a dozen or so Kiths per Seeming in the core book and more were added in later books. Well, in 2e, Kiths are completely independent of Seemings. So, in 1e a Muse was always a Fairest and a Tunnelgrub was always a Darkling, short of Dual Kith shenanigans. Dual Kith was a Merit that (surprise) let you take a second Kith, and pretty much everyone took it, often as a way to get the Kith they wanted that was normally associated with a different Seeming. So, 2e cuts out the middle man and just lets you pair any Kith with any Seeming.

                                              There's also the standard 1e to 2e updates, like the Integrity stat (Clarity) being reworked, and the powers being re-structured. Another big one is that the Y-splat, Courts, is being made customizable. Where 1e had the 4 seasonal courts in the core book and some additional courts in later books, 2e is getting rules for designing your own courts.

                                              Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.

                                                      Originally posted by K9ine View Post

                                                      So there seems to be a lot more character customization than in vampire. Do your Kiths decide your discipline equiviant?
                                                      Your kith doesn't impact on your Contracts (the discipline equivalent), but your Seeming and which Court you're in do. All of the general Contracts have a default effect and a small number of additional little benefits that you can use if your a certain Seeming, and at character creation you have to take at least one Contract that would give you one of those benefits, though you can spend xp to get benefits of a contract associated another seeming. Courts have a small number of associated Contracts, these contracts are cheaper to buy for members of their court.
