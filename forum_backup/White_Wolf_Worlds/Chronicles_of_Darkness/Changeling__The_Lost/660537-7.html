    Originally posted by A_Newfie View Post
    I'm a little surprised that no-one has brought it up from a balancing point of view... But what happens if the player takes Jewel-eyes and Riddleseeker/Roteater/Snowskin/Tunnelgrub.
    You get +2 to your Attributes and they may exceed the natural limit of 5. Heck, if you take Jewel-eyes and Roteater, does that mean you can start the game with 7 in Stamina?

    I mean, sure, it sounds pretty damn cool, but I'm not sure if there is any exp-cost that can make it balanced nor any reason as to why a player who is the slightest bit of gamey would jump the chance to gain that quite powerful buff to their character.
    That's a good point. Perhaps the Dual Kith merit could have a caveat that duplicate bonuses don't stack. So you could get a plus one to a skill from one Kith and 9 again to the same skill from another, but not plus two to a single skill by combining two Kiths that each have a plus one, for instance.

    I definitely think that looking at the Kiths with Dual Kith in mind makes some imbalance among the Kiths apparent. It would be difficult to correct, though, since many of the Kith benefits are difficult to price. I'm also not sure how concerned the writers are with balancing the Kiths, for that matter. But the imbalance does make setting a value for the Dual Kith merit difficult.

Personally, I've never had a player take advantage of the merit in that way, but I've only run a couple of chronicles worth of CtL and the players I've run it for aren't prone to that sort of power gaming. I used it for NPCs in a few instances where I wanted to emulate a monster from folklore or had a specific concept that was best described by a combination of two Kiths. When I've had players take it, it was either because they really wanted a Kith from another Seeming, or because they had a good concept. I'm crossing my fingers that the writers can come up with a way of making the merit balanced, but if they can't, I'd rather have the merit available and just be strict about granting ST approval for it.
