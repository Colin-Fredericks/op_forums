                Windrider:

                You don't have the balls to tame me!


                Long seen as a symbol of nobility and pride by magical and mundane parties alike, those who became Windriders were used as mounts to carry their masters across sky and sea. Most of them were created by combining the features of a predatory bird with some other type of animal. Both parts blending seamlessly together and being fully functional as if such hybrids were natural creatures. Only the most regal were permitted to harness them, while anyone undeserving was gored in the attempt. Their escape came on the day when their esteemed masters were judged unworthy to saddle them, thus prompting the Windrider to rebel! Although they value their new freedom, they still offer rides to those who have earned their trust.


                Fairest: A gryphon fit for a king. From the torso upwards is the body of a giant eagle, but from the waist down they have the legs and tail of a lion.

                Ogre: The wings of a dragon and the body of a lion, this manticore tragically lost its scorpion's tail during aerial combat.

                Beast: Angelic wings attached to the body of a horse, they now run wild and free across the sky, forever denying the bridle of their captors.



                Blessing: Windrider often bare the features of two or more dangerous animals. Their unarmed attacks inflict Lethal damage due to their savage claws and fangs. Additionally, their time spent as airborne steeds allows to fly at twice their normal movement speed for six times their Wyrd in minutes. Spending a point of Glamour will double this duration. While flying, they may carry a passenger who is within their capacity to lift, but suffer -1 speed for each size rating of the passenger in question.

Additionally, their time spent as airborne steeds allows to spend a point of Glamour to fly at a speed in yards equal to x3 their Wyrd for the rest of the scene.

                    It is an interesting concept. I don't think that you need to specify that the multiple animal parts are scaled, as I think that that would be assumed. A gryphon that only had the wings of an ordinary eagle on a full-sized lion body would look pretty ridiculous, after all. Perhaps instead, refer to the perfect grace with which the parts seamlessly meld? The idea that they were a noble kith that only allowed the worthy to ride is a fascinating one. The blessing comes across as a more potent version of the windwing's blessing. For endurance, the windrider wins hands down, getting an entire scene for a single point of Glamour as opposed to a few minutes. At their lowest level, a windwing can win at speed (minimum 7 with 1 Strength, 1 Dexterity, and no Fleet of Foot) against the windrider (minimum 3 at Wyrd 1 with Potent Kith 0), but even with that in mind, at Wyrd 1 with Potent Kith 5 the windrider (Speed 18) can match or exceed any windwing below Wyrd 6 (Speed 18 at Strength 5, Dexterity 5, and Fleet of Foot 3). At the highest level, for considerably less Experience investment, windriders (Speed 45 at Wyrd 10, Potent Kith 5) easily exceed the windwing (Speed 38 at Wyrd 10, Strength 10, Dexterity 10, Fleet of Foot 3). Looking at the rest of the blessings, windriders gain Lethal damage on attacks, compared to perching on things that could hold a small child and the ability to fly for a couple turns without Glamour. I think perhaps the blessing needs a bit more refining in order to balance it with the others. Still, I think this has a lot of potential.

                                                    Basilisk

                                                    "No, my gaze won't turn you to stone. But you'll wish it did... at least your death would be less painful that way."


                                                    One of the most deadly creatures in mythology, the Basilisk was created to destroy any life that crossed its path, sentient or otherwise. So virulent were they, that merely breathing was enough to kill the environment in which they lived! Their durances were tragic existences of complete isolation, for none would dare approach them, lest they begin convulsing in agony. Eventually, the Basilisk realized they could use this to their advantage and escaped the prisons that held them! Known to some as the "king of reptiles," many Basilisks exhibit a reptilian motif in their design.


                                                    Ogre: A freakishly large snake, far more massive than any natural species found on earth.

                                                    Fairest: The body of a lizard, the head of a rooster, and a crest shaped like a royal crown.

                                                    Beast: A near sighted crocodile who exhibits an additional pair of limbs between the first four!



                                                    Blessing: The Basilisk gains the Evil Eye Merit, and can use it without any drawback with the 8-again benefit to its roll. Additionally, its scaled hide serves as extra protection against those who would see them harmed. They automatically gain 3/1 Armor, which stacks with any other armor they might be wearing over their body. If they spend a point of Glamour, the Basilisk is able to exhale a cloud of deadly poison with a Toxicity level equal to half their Wyrd, rounded up. This cloud has an effective range of 3 yards equal to their Wyrd.
