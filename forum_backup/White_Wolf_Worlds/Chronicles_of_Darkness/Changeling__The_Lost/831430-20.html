    Okay, having now taken the time to read through the entirety of the previewed Contracts I have some more in depth thoughts.

    First off, I really love these. I love how they've been streamlined to eliminate wasted design space. No more of 1st edition's rigid five dot progression, no more dud clauses padding the actually interesting stuff, no more splitting powers up so that you need two clauses to use a power effectively. Every dot in here is worthwhile and it's glorious. There are so many cases where two similar contracts have been folded into one, taking advantage of exceptional or seeming effects.

    Unmaker's Destructive Gaze was always just a ranged Touch of the Workman's Wrath, and it fits perfectly as a Wizened specific enhancement. Light Shy was functionally an enhanced Shadow Patch and now it squeezes both mechanics into the base effect and the exceptional.

    The one that caught me most by surprise was Become the Primal Foundation sneaking in as the Elemental variant of Phantom Glory. It makes sense when I think about it, and it combines what were already two of my favorite capstone powers.

    Also, I cannot overstate how pleased I am that Might of the Terrible Brute has a dex option now. Dex builds are a lot of fun and have some great opportunities for interesting synergy, but the lack of abilities to boost dex made them prohibitively expensive.


    My one major concern is Changeling Hours; it has some incredibly broad leeway to cause trouble, especially the Fairest effect. With all the ways to boost dice pools it's not too hard to get an exceptional, and freezing something in time permanently feels rather beyond the scope of what Changelings should be easily capable of. Especially applied to people, this contract gets weird fast. I'm not comfortable with the possibility of freezing an enemy in time permanently. Also, what does "newly made" mean applied to people? Do they turn into babies?

            I posted this earlier but I think it got overlooked.

            I think Tying the Knots of Fate and Glimpse of a Distant Mirror could use a bit of a boost, because neither of them can match the most famous faerie tale equivalent.

            For Tying the Knots of Fate that's Sleeping Beauty. Tying the Knots explicitly says that you can't dictate the outcome of the event, but in Sleeping Beauty the curse was "prick your finger on a spinning wheel, and die".

            A simple solution would be to boost the Fariest and Ogre variants. Fairest can specify a positive outcome, Ogres can specify a negative outcome. I'd also suggest adding a Wizened which variant lets you add a loophole to existing fates (if you know they're there). The changeling thus specifies a loophole that will prevent the fate, or a situation which will reverse the fate depending on what's more suitable.


            For Glimpse of a Distant Mirror it's Snow White. The Contract has no ability to perform the "Who's the Fairest of them All". A simple solution would be to add a Fairest varient that lets you find who's the best at something in a chosen area.
