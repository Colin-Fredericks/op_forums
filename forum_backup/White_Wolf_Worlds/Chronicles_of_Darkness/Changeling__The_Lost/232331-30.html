                            Well, if we're doing Seemings as a metaphor for how you regained control of your life, I'd honestly prefer it if you broke Seemings completely.

                            That is, rather than trying to force every possible way characters could have decided to retake control of their lives into 6 pre-established molds that weren't constructed with this narrative in mind, make up a new batch of Seemings from scratch, such that Seemings fit the narrative, rather than square-pegging everything. The fact that there are about 15 pages of people trying to talk themselves into believing how this could actually work is actually a sort of proof in and of itself how difficult it is to really square that circle. It's better to have a clean break in that case and vaporize old Seemings, like the way that virtues and vices just plain get vaporized in the new edition.

                            Again, I think that kiths are the most defining thing about how you choose for your character to live, and as such, making that the thing that someone else supposedly chose for your goes contrary to any notion that the character is in definite control of their life.

                                            Hmm, I'm really interested to see how this turns out, although I'm scared how the Wizened seeming will be translated into the new paradigm. I've never seen them as just crafty and intelligent. In 1e out of all the Seemings, the Wizened were the definition of abuse, torment and pain amd pointless torture, which goes beyond the capability of the human mind or body to comprehend. And then if you're lucky, the Keeper forces you to do something, to learn and execute a specific task/craft/art and learn it well. And then comes the moment you are able to escape on your own accord with all the knowledge and skills and spite your earned from your Keeper. They symbolized triumph through persistence, cunning and being able to sacrifice in order to be free.This is what really grabbed me initially to the Wizened, and I feel like much of it will be stripped away in order to turn it into "agency".

                                            The other seemings I can easily imagine and I think they would work great in 2E, I'm acually excited to see how they "translate". But I only cringe when I think about the Wizened as agency. Will I be able to recreate my Wizened Chirurgeon, abducted by Grey Alien Keepers, who has spent 7 years in intense and pointless torture, and then 7 years of service (vivisecting other changelings, messing up their bodies and torturing them) and then escaped after 7 failed attempts? If I can replicate that in 2E I would be super happy, as this is one of my first concepts to hook me into the game
