    08-03-2014, 08:58 PM
    So I get that a changeling suffers clarity loss for using magic in front of mortals, but what exactly do mortals see on their end? Does the mask disguise the magic or do they see it for what it is?

      According to the previews of Mage's Fallen World Chronicle, witnessing Supernal Magic is a Breaking Point for mortals; I would imagine that anything similarly overtly-supernatural would impact a mortal mind in similar ways.

          A big part of the reason why Changeling's lose Clarity on that is that there's no hiding it from mortals. Any forgetting it is on their own part-they see the sprites dancing in the Fireheart's hands before he sics them on the rapist, they see the shadows bubble off the Whisperwisp's hand before she takes their sight, and the they see the diamond light of the Bright One as it takes their heart.

          In that moment, a human knows that magic is real, that the old stories are true, and that the Good Folk must be appeased. They might forget and rationalize it later on their own, and it may not be as alive for them later as it was then(thus keeping them from crossing over into minor template territory), but that's a choice they make by and large.

          Naturally, breaking down because you had your world shatter in that moment might make it hard to accept and thus properly remember, but peoples is peoples.

          SHORTHAND: Nothing hides Changeling magic except what the Changeling does to hide it themselves.

                                  My thoughts:

                                  Changeling magic does not, innately, appear supernaturally to mortals. That is, if a Changeling uses Fleeting Spring to learn a mortal's desires, the Mortal can't tell. The Changeling himself sees whatever special effects are thematic to him, but they're a personal hallucination.

                                  However, some Changeling magic has overtly supernatural effects, and those effects aren't hidden by the Mask. An Ogre Gargantuan who uses his Kith blessing will appear, in the eyes of humans around him, to be transformed into an 8-foot tall human, though his Mien will not otherwise show itself.

                                  It might make for a fun houserule to have mortals Ensorcelled by a Lost share in his contract hallucinations, but that'd be Houserule Territory.
