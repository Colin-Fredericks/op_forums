                                    As a general rule, I'd advise against giving the Autumn Court a specific whole extra power set, and instead focus on working within the established Court construction system. To that end, I'd rather give them bonuses to some specific action(s) or custom merits, and maybe tie it together with their Contracts, rather than just make Cruac 2: Electric Boogaloo.

                                    Off the top of my head, perhaps merits or Contracts tied to enhancing their Kenning, so that they can differentiate different types of supernatural phenomena? Or the ability to sort of pre-bargain a Goblin Vow; letting you know what the deal is up-front and do it in advance to get the desired result, rather than doing it out of desperation and risking a nasty curse. Just spitballing, but there's a few thoughts.

                                    It doesn't even have to be all that much; just enough that, when something supernatural and spooky is going on, everyone looks to the Autumn Courtier first for advice.
