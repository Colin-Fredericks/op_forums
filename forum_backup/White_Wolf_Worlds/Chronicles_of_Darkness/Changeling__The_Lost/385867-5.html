                                                It's a particularly pointless distinction, I've always found, since Changeling population has never been tied to local human populations. More than any other World of Darkness creature, they embody the principle of "there are as many as you need for the story". Like, to the point that it's actually almost implied to be an in-universe law. A small country town could have a Changeling population that dwarfs the population of New York, which I think is given as 37 in Lords of Summer. With the variation on the ground being so considerable, a world-wide population would be functionally useless. It provides no practical gauge at all for the purposes of building a setting.
