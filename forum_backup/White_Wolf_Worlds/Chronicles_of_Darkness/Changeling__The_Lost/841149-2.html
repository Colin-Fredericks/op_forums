                        Here's a tentative write-up for my Traveler kith's Blessing. Comments and suggestions are appreciated.

                        -+-

                        Blessing: The Traveler can spend Glamour or Willpower to invoke the City-Walker Merit, and instead suffers penalties based on how low his destination's population is from the minimum requirement. He may also use this to enter and exit the Hedge without the need of a gateway. If he spends additional Glamour, he may bring one willing traveler with him for each point spent.

                                        Interesting idea, but we already have Kiths that focus on social manipulation, Dearheart, Flowering, Nix, Flamesiren, Illes, Larencist, and to a lesser extent Shadowsoul.

                                        The goal of the Kith's minions isn't social manipulation, its that many of the great villians have minor disposable minions to throught at their enemies.

                                        That why I choose the staff merits its perfect way to show faceless minions, without clogging up gameplay. They do their one auto success and then they're gone, driven off or killed by the &quot;good&quot; guy.

                                        And I see the minions actually being part of the Villians mien, not true individuals, semi automous at best, and killing them off as being no damaging tothe Villian then cutting his/her finger nails. Its also allows the minions to look more fantastical.
