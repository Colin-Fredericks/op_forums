                Edit: Further more, if you heat up steel again and melt it, it does not separate out into iron and carbon. It stays one new thing, steel.
                Iron and steel are generally very different, but the truth is it's a spectrum. You won't find any pure iron. It all has different amounts of impurities, and different types of iron has different names because of it. Wrought iron, cast iron, pig iron, etc. Steel just happen to have a higher rate of carbon, and there's no natural cut off point. In fact, cast iron contains more carbon than most steel types.
