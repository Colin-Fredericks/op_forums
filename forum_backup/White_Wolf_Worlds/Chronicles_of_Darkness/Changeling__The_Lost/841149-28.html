            Will-O-the-Wisp

            "I told you not to trust her!"


            Not every abduction the Fae perform is an obvious one. Sometimes, they like to make it seem like an accident so that it people don't go asking too many questions. To conduct these abductions, they create Will-O-the-Wisps, ephemeral beings who appear in the mist at night and entrance mortals with their hypnotic allure. The hapless victim would then proceed to chase after the wisp, leading them straight into the clutches of their new masters! One night, the Will-O-the-Wisp decided to change things around and chase after the mortal instead, eventually following them back through the Hedge!


            Elemental: An entity composed of ghostly flames. It produces no heat, but wavers enticingly before your eyes.

            Fairest: A beautiful maiden with a miniature sun shining around her head. Her presences fills you with a sense of security.

            Darkling: A mysterious figure, wrapped in a tight cloud of luminescent mist. The cloud blinks hypnotically when your eyes try to follow their movements.


            Blessing: Will-O-the-Wisps spent their durances leading people into danger and have become quite proficient at tricking others as a result. They begin with the Nevertread contract for free at character creation and gain exceptional successes on 3 instead of 5. Additionally, by spending a point of Glamour and rolling a contested Wyrd + Manipulation against Willpower + Composure, they can force a subject to devote their attention on the Will-O-the-Wisp, causing them to momentarily forget about what they were doing. If the Will-O-the-Wisp leaves the area, and the victim has not overcome the effect, they will feel compelled to track them.

                I think that 8-Again without drawbacks is way too potent for something that can apply such a brutal Tilt. I'd say having the Merit is great as is, given how effectively it could take someone out of a fight. I'd add their Wyrd to the activation roll for it instead of the loss of drawback or the 8-Again effect. As an aside, great idea with the Evil Eye Merit being used for this. A similar kith that I'd been working on (diregaze) had simply reduced Speed and Initiative with a look, and could sacrifice Defense to lock eyes and inflict the Stunned Tilt. The Merit actually reflects a petrifying stare a lot better. I also think that the Armor is a bit potent, given that it is superior to any other kith's armor out of the box, though admittedly the metalflesh can become superior by spending Glamour and the earthbones manages it by increasing Wyrd or Potent Kith. Given the potency of the rest of the blessing, giving 1/1 Armor for the scales like the draconic and coldscale get wouldn't be bad. The poison cloud seems like it could use a bit of work, as well. A brewer creates poison of the same Toxicity, but requires someone to ingest it to be effective, while the blightbent creates a more potent cloud, but a potentially much smaller one.

                                                    Here's a new Kith from me. Seeming descriptions are welcome. As is critique.

                                                    Stormtouched
                                                    You thought you had me cornered? Out here in the open with a storm brewing? Well, I'm about to prove you wrong.

                                                    Imbued with all the fury and power of their namesake, Stormtouched already embodied it in some way as mortals. Some may have been prone to violent mood swings, while others had a certain relentlessness in their actions. A few may have even taken because of their fascination with storms. In any case, their Keepers had them exposed repeatedly to waves of lightning until they filled every fiber of their being. Yet while this transformation made them into living weapons, many were also made more empowered by the experience. This sparked defiance against their captors, and eventually culminated with their flight through the Thorns.

                                                    Blessing: Stormtouched have three dots in the Psychokinesis Merit, and can spend Glamour or Willpower to manipulate electricity with it. They may also heal any damage they suffered by exposing themselves to electrical currents. If a Stormtouched is outdoors during suitable weather conditions, he may spend Glamour and roll Resolve + Occult to call down a bolt of lightning. If used as an attack, the roll is penalized by the target's Defense, and deals bashing damage equal to half the Changeling's Wyrd, rounded up, plus successes in the roll.
