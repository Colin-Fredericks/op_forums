    Why Call Them Kiths and Seemings in 2E?

    02-18-2016, 06:58 AM
    One thing I don't get about Changleing 2E is that it completely changes what Kiths and Seemings are, yet still keeps the names, which now seem really awkward. Don't get me wrong, I'm not complaining about what Kiths and Seemings have been turned into. In fact, I really love the changes as they truly encapsulate a changeling's nature, it's just that the changes should come full circle into new names for the new concepts.

    Seeming suggests something that is an outward appearance that is deceptive in nature, which makes sense for what it was in 1E. But in 2E, the Seeming reflects what the changeling did, not what their Keeper did. Kith means neighbors, friends, or other relationships that aren't as close as family but closer than acquaintances, which worked for what they were in 1E, but not for what they are in 2E. Perhaps the term Seeming could replace Kith (that would make sense since Seeming in 1E was what the Keeper turned the changeling into) and Abandon could replace Seeming (it's related as both a verb and noun).

    On that note, perhaps some of the Seemings themselves should also go through name changes. I can see Elemental, Ogre, Fairest, and Wizened, considering their ties to fairy tales and how they relate so well to how they break from their Keepers, but ones like Darkling and Beast don't make much sense in the new context (to me, at least). Again, not that the concepts for them are bad, just that the names don't suit, which isn't surprising when you consider what they were originally crafted for.

    But maybe I'm missing something here, what do you guys think?
