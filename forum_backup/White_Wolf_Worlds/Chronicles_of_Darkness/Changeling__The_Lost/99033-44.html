        Lorelei’s Diary

        Our Sunday night show looms, and I’ve already made plans for after, so I should keep this brief (well, as brief as I ever get, at least). Immediately following my last entry, I did in fact pounce upon my fiancée, and much frolicsome fun was had by all. Okay, maybe not by “all…” Julia was a bit perturbed that I had distracted her model, and insisted that I re-apply the body oil D had been wearing so that she could finish her painting. It was an onerous task, but I think I bore it well in the end.

        That’s how it happened that I was in the bathroom washing up when my phone chimed. I glanced at it, and was surprised to see that I had gotten a text from Felicity, asking if I’d like to come over to her place for tea, as she had a few things she’d like to discuss with me in person. Assuming that this had something to do with Rowan, I quickly texted back that I would head over to her place in a few minutes.

        It was a nice day, and she’s right down the road, so I decided to walk. She met me at the door and greeted me with a friendly hug before escorting me into her sitting room and bringing out a genuine tea service. It was really good tea, too, once I had sweetened it to my taste… some flavor the British had adapted from a traditional Indian blend. Ceylon or Assam Valley, I think… she gave me several options and then steeped the tea bags after I’d made my selection, so I don’t remember exactly which one I picked.

        In any case, it turned out that she did want to talk to me about Rowan. It looks like my sometime paramour is improving, but still has a long way to go before she’s ship-shape once more. In an effort to get Rowan talking about something other than me or her House, Felicity has encouraged the selkie to talk a bit more about herself and her history. As we had suspected, she was lost in Arcadia for much longer than is common. Felicity said that her best guess was that Rowan was Taken in the late 20s or early 30s… she didn’t know that World War II had happened until it came up in conversation.

        I was floored by that revelation. I mean, I probably understand culture shock better than most changelings, as I was in Arcadia for about thirty-six years, but this is something else entirely. I’ve never heard of a mortal being gone that long and still managing to find her way back. Felicity theorized that the supernatural connection Rowan shared with her House might have been strong enough to lead her back to the world. Oh, that’s right… Rowan’s connection to the House apparently goes way back.

        The rest of our conversation was mostly centered around Rowan’s recovery. She’s doing better, but isn’t ready to see me or talk to me yet. I get that, and I definitely want what’s best for the poor girl… but it didn’t make for a very pleasant conversation. Long story short, I probably won’t be able to see her until after the New Year. Felicity said that the two of them might visit the Dream House before then (Rowan finds it comforting and familiar, as it reminds her a bit of the Follies), but that it would probably be best if I didn’t engage with her on more than a cursory level until she’s ready.

        In fact, Felicity thinks that it might be good for Rowan to speak with someone else for a while… someone with whom she might share a bit of common ground. There are several changelings in the Freehold who lived through that era, and Felicity believes that one of them might succeed in making Rowan feel more comfortable in this strange new world. Unfortunately, neither of them excels at putting people at their ease.

        Madame Spidertoes and Annika. One is just creepy and off-putting to an extreme degree, while the other is not really much of a “people person,” as they say. Oh, I suppose there’s Tommy as well, but… no. Just no.

        In the end I suggested that Madame Spidertoes would (marginally) be the better option, though I’m self-aware enough to admit that I have a bit of a bias against Annika on a personal level. Sure, Gladys is a bit scary, but she genuinely cares about the changelings of this Freehold, and I think she’d want to help if she could. Annika, on the other hand, is very withdrawn and haughty, and I think her attitude would put Rowan off as much as it does me.

        Sigh. I really hate to say this, but I’m starting to think it would be better for everyone involved if Rowan and I just called it quits. Don’t get me wrong; she’s a beautiful, bright, selfless girl, and I consider myself quite lucky to have earned her affections. But at this point, I can’t see how the two of us moving forward is going to do anything but continue to hurt her. She wants someone who can devote all of their attention to her, and she deserves someone like that… but I can’t be that for her. I’ll never be that for her.

She seems honestly resolute in her determination not to screw things up for the quad, though it’s obvious that she’s intrigued by whatever is developing between the two of them as well. She also paid us a high compliment, saying that we seem to have been a good influence on Marguerite, who has mellowed and “softened” a bit in the last eight months or so.

        It was starting to get late by the time I left her house (with another chaste hug on the doorstep), so I put a little hustle in my step as I walked back to the Dream House. Now, particularly sharp readers (and again, what the hell are you doing reading my private diary anyway?) might recall that in my previous entry, I found a note from Calamity and Jacob stating that they had gone into the Hedge to hunt something and blow off a little steam. You might also remember that I stated that I trusted them not to get in over their heads and challenge anything too dangerous…

        …which only serves as further proof of what an idiot I can be from time to time.

        So yes, I strolled into the Dream House, hoping to grab a few moments with D before I had to put on make-up and dress up right. I climbed up to the attic and came face-to-face with Calamity and Jacob, looking like they’d come out on the wrong end of an intense argument with a combine harvester.

        “Oh, Christ on a Triscuit,” I muttered darkly as I took in their slashed and bloody clothing. “What the fuck, guys?”

        Jacob grinned. “It was a good hunt,” he said simply.

        Calamity was more enthusiastic, and began giving me a blow-by-blow account of their fight against something she called a “Thorn Dragon.” As near as I can tell, it seemed to be a sentient portion of the Hedge that could form into a draconic shape to attack its prey. It was apparently difficult to deal with because it would occasionally revert to a tangled mass of thorny vines and scatter, only to re-form in a different location.

        “Sit,” I said firmly, placing my finger over her lips to stop her boasting. She gave me kind of a hurt look as I let the healing energies flow through me and into her wounds, and shot a reproachful glance in D’s direction. “I know what you’re thinking,” I said with a little grin, “but it’s cute when she does it.”

        Jacob was even worse off than Calamity, and I had to use the contract twice to get him back into a vaguely acceptable condition. “Look, I know you two are working through some stuff,” I said before I let them go, “but you’re family, okay? I worry about you.” I’m not sure how much of that penetrated, though; they both still seemed pretty pleased with themselves.

        So after I patched up Tweedledee and Tweedledum, I decided it was high time to get backstage and start wrangling the show. We had a pretty decent crowd last night, and everybody gave it their all (including Calamity, whose bruises you could barely see by that point). It wasn’t our most spectacular outing, but we did well enough, and the crowd seemed pleased.

        Oleander was up at the Girlfriend Table, along with Julia and Becky. Stasia had opted to spend the evening at the Rose & Thorn, and D had planted herself at the bar beside none other than TJ. I made my way over to them first, where I was subjected to a conversation which nearly blistered my ears… even with my admittedly limited understanding of the Spanish language. I’ll refrain from repeating any of it here (well, except to mention that I consider myself something of an expert on Maria’s anatomy, and most of TJ’s allegations regarding such are patently false). Let’s just say that the fine art of trash-talking has found its champions in the greater Malibu-Santa Monica area.

        “You two having fun?” I asked as I allowed D to draw me into an easy embrace.

        “Oh yeah,” TJ nodded. “Your girl here had to run after the dust-up last night. She didn’t want you to worry about her. So we didn’t have time for the usual banter. Just makin’ up for lost time.” He glanced down at the ring on my left hand, and rubbed his chin thoughtfully. “Let me just say, considerin’ how hard de la Rocha hits these days, I’m glad you’re the one wearin’ the rock.”

        “What’s up, babe?” D asked as I turned back to her.

        “Oleander’s here tonight,” I said. “I know you and I haven’t had much time together these last few days. You want me to see if she’d be okay with you joining us?”

        I leaned in to kiss her. “Okay, but don’t make any plans for tomorrow night. Brightstar’s coming over for movie night on Monday, and I’m supposed to have dinner with Joan and Andromeda on Tuesday, and I don’t think I can wait a whole four days for more alone time with my fiancée. Okay?”

        She nodded. “It’s a date. You want to go out or something? Maybe slip out of here once you’re done with your numbers?”

        “I’ll think about it,” I promised. “We’ll do something fun.”

        So I moseyed up to the Girlfriend Table and planted myself in Oleander’s lap. “Well hello to you too, Angel,” she said with a grin.

        Honestly, I don’t remember much about the conversation that followed. I know we kept things light. I know someone made a comment about my chest at some point, and I laughed it off like everyone else. I mean, I make no secret of the fact that I’m proud of my looks, right? That being the case, I figure I have no right to complain when someone else calls attention to my assets. Though I confess it gets a little old after a while.

        I think Oleander picked up on this on some level, as she was very affectionate and, well, loving throughout the evening. In the end, the two of us retired to my room without D, who quietly assured me once more that she would be fine staying with Julia and Marguerite again.

        I swear, for someone who has the reputation of being cold and dispassionate, Oleander can really be the sweetest girl in the world when she’s given the chance. I mean, D likes to cuddle too – though I’m not sure she’d ever forgive me if she found out that I actually committed that little factoid to writing – but it seems like Oleander’s first impulse is always tenderness instead of passion. It’s nice.

        I am positively the luckiest girl in the greater Malibu/Santa Monica area. I have so many wonderful people making me feel loved and wanted.

        Sex Breakfast proceeded as usual after I got back from church this morning. Oleander had to scoot pretty quickly to get back to her lab, but seemed to be tickled pink by the fact that she was leaving with a large box stuffed with some of the remains of the Thorn Dragon my friends had killed. I’m sure she’ll find a good use for it.

        Breakfast was fun, I suppose, but I confess that the off-color banter didn’t amuse me as much as usual, possibly due to the fact that it was already wearing on me from the night before. At one point yet another comment was made about my boobs… from Marguerite rather than Julia this time. She implied that I would not require a life preserver if I should happen to fall into the ocean. I know she didn’t mean anything by it, I know she loves me, and I’m not mad at her or anything, but like I said above, it gets a little old sometimes. I mean, I have people ogling me every night up on stage. Enough is enough. I expect my friends and loved ones to see something different in me.

        I mean, what does that image of me say about my role in the motley, or in the Freehold itself? What do I really bring to this group, when all is said and done? Legs, breasts, and wings. Yep, that about covers it. If anything ever happens to me, maybe the others should fill the void I leave with Colonel fucking Sanders.

        I’m not being fair. I know that. Nobody in this motley sees me like that (except for maybe myself on some level). It’s just so hard to get away from the stereotype. I’d blame the Empress, saying that this is what she made me, but honestly… this is what I made myself before she ever got her lavender mitts on me. Let’s face it… I’ve always been an attractive promiscuous dumb blonde. It’s why Kyle wanted nothing to do with me in High School. God, the first time I thought I might really like a guy… and he couldn’t stand me because I was so damned vapid that I couldn’t keep up with the conversations he had with his little circle of friends.

        There’s more at stake now, of course. Calamity and Jacob’s little adventure yesterday has really driven that home. The Contracts of Spring allowed me to heal the worst of their wounds and stabilize them… but Jacob still looked like a hundred and fifty pounds of juicy ground chuck when I was done. I just don’t know enough about medicine to use the contract effectively. I had to go back and look over my medical books for a while, and then try to use the contract on him again later before we made a real dent in the bulk of his injuries. I wasn’t smart enough to figure out how to help him on my own.

        That won’t do. What if my lack of knowledge means the difference between life and death for someone next time? I can’t take that chance. I have to work harder. I have to sleep less, and get more medical books from the library. I have to try to take some classes at a local college, to learn everything that I can. Even if I’ll never be smart enough to be a real doctor, I need to do what I can to keep my loved ones safe.

        Okay. In what is possibly a counterintuitive segue, I’m going to drop the self-deprecation for a few minutes to talk about what I have planned for my night out with D. It came to me as the group of us headed out to introduce Stasia to that wonderful soft-serve ice cream place down the street (gummy bears on ice cream! Genius!). I think D and I are going to hit a rough biker bar and start a fight again, like we did last Summer. Hopefully this will get my love riled up enough that I won’t feel so bad about having missed out on her company following her brawl with TJ the other day.

        Yes, I know that I just chastised Calamity and Jacob for picking fights and endangering themselves yesterday. And yes, I know that I’m giving in to my lusts and planning an evening of debauchery a scant few minutes after resolving to change my image and focus more on my studies. What can I say? I’m a woman of many contradictions.

        But hey… I haven’t had the chance to spend quality time with my fiancée in a few days, and she wants to go out with me, make me feel like the prettiest girl in the world, beat some poor shmucks up to defend my honor, and then spend the rest of the night lavishing her attention on me. Who am I to say no to that? I can always study tomorrow, right?

        Lorelei Grace
