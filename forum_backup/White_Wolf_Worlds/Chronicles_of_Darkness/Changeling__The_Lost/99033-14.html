                            Insincere Marguerite is a blazing hypocrite. But generally dislikes violence. She also could not just vanish at the time that story was set, she maxed Smoke later.

                            On 2e I will have to see the finished product to know for sure. Apart from not using beats, MDH is fully played in 2e systems already, though, except where there is only a 1e system. Compared to other WoD games, though CtL 2e is a very different game from what I have read though, again, I will judge the finished product not piecemeal.

                            Honestly, I will probably Frankenstein in aspects of the systems I like and drop those I don't. As I understand it, every MDH PC would be impossible to make in 2e and would have to lose a Kith and all their Seeming abilities, though I could be wrong. That means we get cases like Lori can't have her super harvesting AND wings, both are which see a lot of use, Calamity has to lose her innate knives or whisperwisping abilties that are key to her evolution into the whispering voices, etc. I could be off on this, of course.

                            OTOH, some of the Kith updates are really good, and I will probably use them ( I really like new Palewraith and Windwing, for instance). I suspect I will use the new Clarity rules, though I will need to read them first. I may use the new Seeming Clarity loss/gain rules even if I keep the old nuts and bolts as well, as a more fluid Clarity would benefit from them. I am inclined to think as Contracts will be (IIRC) very different, I will keep the old system for MDH and possibly use the new system in the next game, though if I misread and they are fairly compatible I may just update them...

                            In general I have adopted new game systems whole cloth (Requiem, Forsaken, and most of the 2e GMC update save beats, which are unpopular with my group more than my own issue) so it is not impossible but, as I say, 2e is very different than 1e. We will see. I won't decide till I have the book, and I won't decide alone anyway but have us decide as a troupe.

                            ETA:

                            No Romances were planned before the game. Lorelei and Insincere Marguerite were a surprise to no one but not planned.                                             08-03-2015, 03:01 AM
                                            Things start to come to a head with the White Maria story. Our Motley takes action and deals with the problem. Or do they?

                                            Due to stuff going on IRL no Calamity journal this time. Her player has promised a double journal next time dealing with both sessions.

                                            Session XXXV

                                            Gawain’s Memories

                                            June 26th, morning

                                            Well, that all accelerated quickly. It was a lazy afternoon, full of relaxing and working out and a little bit of business, then Becky showed up and things kinda exploded. Well, not right off, kinda a delayed fuse, you know, but anyway, the afternoon was not a good tell for the evening. At least it all ended well.

                                            The reason it all exploded when Becky showed up was that she had a favor to ask Calamity. She’d figured out that whoever was terrorizing White Maria was going to again tonight. Well, really, oracular sources just said something bad was going to happen to her, and we didn’t want to assume that was just her, you know, tripping on something or something.

                                            White Maria was going to the pier to see a local band perform, so Becky wanted Calamity to, you know, shadow her, keep her and anyone in the range of her SMG safe and hopefully catch the terrorizer. Calamity enlisted Insincere Marguerite and Jacob to help with the shadowing part, and me and Lori to be nearby, for a quick getaway, and in case muscle or healing was needed. Of course, that meant catching the others up to speed.

                                            Lori was not happy to just now learn about it. To be honest, I hadn’t even thought about it - I guess I figured Calamity would tell her? I was also a little distracted. Calamity, though, hadn’t told on purpose, hoping we wouldn’t, you know, ‘Scooby Gang’ into it. I guess we kinda did anyway though...

                                            Anyways, the basic plan was pretty simple. Insincere Marguerite would steal the clip from White Maria’s gun, so she wouldn’t, you know, freak out about not having it, but still wouldn’t be able to panic and fire wildly at whoever spooked her and also what was behind them. We couldn’t just tell her she was safe, cause then she might act different and scare the terrorizer off and Becky really wanted to catch them. Insincere Marguerite would stick close even after that, while Calamity and Jacob were prowling around. Lori and I stashed ourselves further down the Promenade, closer to the parking lot, at a little coffee shop place. Note to future self: do not let Lori drink 5 espressos in a matter of hours. Definitely should have cut her off. She might have started vibrating.

                                            With all that, I think things worked? We definitely got more info, you know? We didn’t see much action until the concert was about over, when Jacob texted to warn us that White Maria was leaving and almost immediately after Insincere Marguerite warned us that there was an unconscious dude in the back of the van. I gave Lori the key, since she’s better at driving that me, and we wanted to unobtrusively follow White Maria home.

                                            Insincere Marguerite popped into the back by herself a little bit down the road. Apparently, she’d dropped Calamity off to follow a different lead and she explained about the dude. He was one of a bunch of ganger dudes that were eyeing White Maria, waiting for something. She’d chloroformed the one and they’d dispersed without doing anything but that.

                                            Lori poofed him awake, in between grinning manically and announcing what a tense high speed chase this was, as she checked her blind spot and used her blinker to change lanes, all, you know, a mile or two above the speed limit. I got a good growl going, holding him by the shirt collar. Of course, he didn’t speak English. De la Roche was called over, and between the two of us, we had him terrified. He said they were supposed to observe White Maria, and, if they got a signal, kidnap her to a particular warehouse. We got his boss’ name, an office for him and the address of the warehouse, but that’s it.

                                            We shared all that with Calamity when she joined us outside White Maria's house. She recognized the boss’ name as the big guy in a decent sized, localish gang, based in LA. It was a kinda moot point though, since her lead had confirmed it was Lucien and filled in the details on the Sundown Kwan story. According to them, she’d been the girlfriend of the head of the Tong gang – you know, the mortal one, not the Phantom Tong. She’d had a lot of control of the gang through him, and with her powers they’d thrived. Especially when she’d allied with Darke. After she didn’t back down based on some unspecified ‘warnings’, she’d been killed as an example, specifically to Dark Kimberly, who was something of a rival for the gang boss’ affections. She did know about the hit beforehand, and didn’t say anything, probably either for fear of her own life, or to win that rivalry.

                                            So Calamity wanted to warn White Maria off, hoping to do that better than the terror campaign, since the presence of the gang dudes was probably a sign that Lucien was tired of waiting for that tack to work. Lori and Jacob went with, Lori to hopefully convince White Maria to drop it without bringing Lucien’s name into it, and Jacob to offer to hide her, if needed. Insincere Marguerite, me and the girlfriends went on home, since we didn’t wanna swamp White Maria or anything, you know? Plus, I was pretty sure I couldn’t help there and it was close to the time to meet Desiree.

                                            Calamity and the others did get back before we’d started the session. Just before though so I didn’t get to ask what all happened. Calamity looked bummed enough, and was muttering darkly about dragon slaying, that I doubt it was good, you know? Also, she apologized for not being able to help with the dream stuff, but Becky showed up about the same time Calamity did and they clearly needed to talk.

                                            We wished each other luck and she continued on to the bar while I went upstairs. I only hope she had as much luck as I did. Everything went smooth and as good as I could have hoped. Desiree is a really light dreamer - I had to focus hard, again, to not shatter everything when I stepped in. It did make it easier to shape things back into the theatre, with our half-finished construction work backstage. It was fun, almost like a game, with paint fights and spurts of tension releasing destruction in between the always awesome work of building a strong foundation and the familiar task of helping a performer make some art with stage scenery.

                                            Going over things carefully near the end of the night, I was happily surprised to find no traces of the switch. Once we’d torn up the wire leading to it, and rebuilt the stage to Desiree’s specifications, it had vanished. And not just moved either, I’m sure of it. Dream Desiree didn’t really know what we were doing, but my happiness was infectious, and the dream dissipated with her kissing me. It was a nice way to wake up.

                                            It’s early yet, maybe I can go find Calamity and give her a similar awakening.


                                            Lorelei’s Diary

                                            Okay, so things have accelerated, and a situation I barely knew existed has come to a head. I won’t say things are perfect, and we’re going to have to play things carefully for a while, but I hope we’ve stabilized things in the Freehold for the time being.

                                            We’d been discussing the redecoration of the Dream House before everything went down. The House is going to officially buy some of the posters Julia’s been creating of our headlining performers (including a few of yours truly) and put them up in the entrance hall and stage area. It gives the house a very bijou feel, and I think it will greatly enhance the ambience of our little theater.

                                            So Becky Wan came by this afternoon before the show to speak with Calamity. After she’d left, Calamity gathered the motley to ask for help. There’s been bad blood between White Maria and Dark Kimberly lately. White Maria has been looking into the death of one of her friends a few years ago, and she blames Dark Kimberly for the incident. Kimberly has gone into seclusion at Annika’s place, but someone has been terrorizing White Maria for the past week or so.

                                            Calamity has known about this for a few days. So has Gawain. And Jacob, apparently. I’m not sure why they chose not to let me in on the “fun.” But things came to a crisis point tonight. Becky got word somehow that something bad was going to happen to White Maria tonight. She knew that her friend was going out to Santa Monica pier to listen to a local band, and she assumed that whatever was going down would happen there. She wanted Calamity and maybe some of the rest of us to keep an eye on her, make sure she was safe, stop her from hurting anyone else (she’s taken to carrying a gun wherever she goes as a precaution), and if possible, draw out whoever has been tormenting her.

                                            Of course, we agreed to do what we could. White Maria’s not exactly a friend (rumor has it that she’s something of a bitch), but I don’t want to see anyone get hurt. And besides, this sort of nonsense is bad for the Freehold. So I called in one of our contracted mortal performers to take over my acts for the night, and we headed out for the pier.

                                            I also called Oleander before we left. She answered on the second ring. “Hey, Lori,” she said, and I could hear the eagerness in her voice. “What’s up?”

                                            I sighed. “Sorry, honey,” I said, “something’s come up. I won’t be performing at the Dream House tonight, and I might have to take a rain check on our date.”

                                            I could hear her face fall over the phone; it was sort of amazing. “Oh,” she said simply.

                                            “I’m really sorry,” I hastened to add, “and I wouldn’t do this if it wasn’t important. It’s Freehold business… kind of.”

                                            “No, it’s okay Lori. I understand,” she said quickly.

                                            I reached for something to say. “Look, maybe the motley’s chore won’t take very long,” I said. “If we finish early, I’ll give you a call, and we can still get together, okay?”

                                            “Okay,” she said, sounding a bit mollified. “Only… give me a call when you’re done anyway, please,” she continued, “just so I’ll know you’re okay.”

                                            “I promise,” I said. I’m glad I did. “Oh, and by the way, I had something I wanted to ask you.”

                                            “What’s that?” she asked.

                                            I paused before continuing, pondering the best way to bring it up. “D wanted me to ask if you would be open to her joining the two of us some time. You know… intimately.”

                                            There was silence on the other end of the line.

                                            “If you’re not comfortable with that, we’ll both understand,” I said quickly, “and there will be no hard feelings from either of us, I promise. I just wanted to let you know that it was an option.”

                                            “Okay,” she said. “I’ll need to think about it. I know you’re trying to include me, to make me a bigger part of your life, and I appreciate that, but… I’ll just need to think about it for a while, okay?”

                                            “Sure thing, honey,” I said warmly. “Don’t obsess over it, okay? Whatever you decide is fine with me. Now I should go; I need to get ready to head out. I’ll call you tonight.” We said our goodbyes and I left to dress for the pier.

                                            Our plan was for Calamity and Jacob to position themselves stealthily on the pier while Marguerite tailed White Maria invisibly. If possible, Marguerite was going to remove the clip from White Maria’s weapon without her realizing it. D – who hangs out on the pier all the time, and thus would not arouse suspicion – was going to be nearby in case the others needed backup, while Gawain, Julia and I – who are not known for our stealth abilities – would be a few blocks away ready to come in if called. We didn’t want to be too close, because if White Maria spotted any of us she would probably realize that something was up, and it might blow our chance to nab whoever had been terrorizing her.

                                            So most of my evening was actually pretty tame, y’know, comparatively speaking. I spent an hour or so sipping espressos with Gawain and Julia, and then we got a call from Marguerite informing us that White Maria was on the move and that there was an unconscious Chicano gang member in the back of Gawain’s van. While Gawain made sure the ganger was properly subdued, I drove his van, following White Maria at a safe distance. And hey! I don’t think she spotted us even once!

                                            So we parked ourselves down the block from White Maria’s place and I channeled a bit of energy into the ne’er-do-well until he was awake. Then we had to wait twenty minutes for D to join us, since our guest didn’t speak English and none of us spoke Spanish. When she arrived, D gave me a quick kiss and then got to work. A few minutes later she stepped out of the back of the van, dusting off her hands theatrically, and informed us that he and his friends were supposed to kidnap White Maria and deliver her to a warehouse near the docks. Someone named Alessandro Piña had told them to do this.

                                            I gave her a critical glance. “You enjoyed that, didn’t you?” I asked, quirking an eyebrow.

                                            She grinned. “I don’t know what you’re talking about,” she said nonchalantly.

                                            “Tu madre y tu padre?” I asked. “Was it really necessary to threaten his parents?”

                                            She shrugged. “It was effective,” she returned. I couldn’t really argue against that point, so I let it pass. For now.

                                            Marguerite showed up a few minutes later, and Calamity not long after that. They had a strange tale to tell. The one who’s been terrorizing White Maria is Gunnar Friday. Well, okay, to be perfectly honest, Gunnar’s name never actually came up directly, but considering who else was involved, by the end of the night even I didn’t need a textbook to calculate that particular logarithm. Marguerite was able to chloroform him tonight before he got too close to White Maria, and teleported him back to the Dream House through a pair of handy mirrors. Calamity had been interrogating him there while we kept watch on White Maria’s house.

                                            Gunnar was reluctant to tell her anything, but she eventually managed to wrangle the whole story out of him. Years ago, a young changeling named Sundown Kwon became the girlfriend of the head of the local tong (a sort of Chinese analog to the mafia, if I understand the term correctly). Rumor had it that she had a lot of influence over the tong leader, and could pretty much do as she pleased. Her biggest rival for his affections was none other than Dark Kimberly, who was about seventeen years old at that time.

                                            Now the tong had been getting more and more entrenched in Santa Monica – thanks in part to Sundown Kwon’s surreptitious use of her changeling powers to help them out here and there – and a lot of people thought that Kwon was using the tong to influence Freehold business too. It was widely believed that Kwon had officially allied the tong with Master Darke. A lot of powerful people didn’t like that, and someone issued a warning that Kwon and Kimberly should back off… if they knew what was good for them. Kimberly listened to the warning… but didn’t pass it on to Kwon. Kwon died as a result.

                                            Fast forward to a few weeks ago. White Maria began looking into Sundown Kwon’s murder again, possibly in response to Kimberly’s shooting at the Rose & Thorn. Someone was afraid of what she would find, and began trying to scare her into giving it up. But Kwon was her friend, and she didn’t give up the chase easily. She thought Kimberly might have had something to do with it, as her investigations had revealed that Kimberly knew about the threat to Kwon’s life and didn’t say anything. But the truth is not so simple.

                                            According to Gunnar, Lucien Torrez killed Sundown Kwon (or at least ordered her death) all those years ago, because the tong was infringing upon the territories controlled by his contacts in the area’s Hispanic gangs. Torrez didn’t want White Maria’s new investigation to reveal his complicity in Sundown’s death. Gunnar claimed that he was actually trying to save White Maria’s life by terrorizing her; if she gave up her investigation, Torrez would have no reason to “silence” her by other means.

                                            Based on the cadre of Hispanic gangers on the pier tonight, we’re guessing that Torrez is just about out of patience.

                                            Calamity, Jacob and I went to talk to White Maria, hoping that we could convince her to drop her investigation before things turned deadly. The others left to drop the gang member off somewhere private and secluded. White Maria was surprised to see us, but she was willing to let us in so that we could discuss her current situation. I had suggested that we try to leave Torrez’s name out of this, but Calamity was having none of it. She told Maria exactly what was happening with no holds barred.

                                            I tried to salvage the situation by leveraging Lucien’s reputation. White Maria agreed that Torrez is too powerful for her to take down, and that he could almost certainly dispose of all of us put together without too much trouble if he put his mind to it. She agreed to halt her investigation into Sundown Kwon’s death.

                                            Unfortunately, she wasn’t willing to call off her grudge against Dark Kimberly. I get that Kwon was a friend of hers, and I guess I can understand why she’d be mad at Kimberly for standing aside while her friend was in danger… but holding on to that much hate for so long can’t be healthy. I tried my best to get her to let it go, but in the end she would only agree to consider dropping her grudge if Dark Kimberly was willing to make some sort of atonement. I agreed to ask her, but I’m putting off that chore for another day.

                                            Once everyone was back at the Dream House, Calamity called up Becky and invited her over to explain things. I don’t know everything that she said (I wasn’t privy to the conversation), but I hope she was able to calm Becky down enough to keep her from doing something stupid. If Becky goes after Lucien Torrez, she’ll just get killed. And if he traces her information back to us, then we’re all in big trouble.

                                            As promised, I gave Oleander a call. It was close to midnight by that point, and I was tired and more than a little fed up with all the unchecked hatred and revenge talk flying around. I admit I wasn’t in the most social of moods, and I kind of wanted to just be alone to unwind for a bit, but a promise is a promise.

                                            “Hi, Lori,” she said as soon as she picked up. “Is everything okay? Are you…”

                                            I smiled despite myself. “I’m fine, Oleander. I think things have pretty much wrapped up over here. If it’s not too late, I can be at your place in about half an hour.”

                                            “Okay,” she said, and then, a little shyly, “only…”

                                            “What is it?” I asked, curious.

                                            “Well,” she continued, “I was hoping we’d have a little more time, to talk, and, y’know…” I almost couldn’t hear the last word, “…cuddle.”

It’s been a few nights now since I made love, so I told them I would be along, I felt the need to try and comfort Calamity. Gently.

                                                I am a master thief. I am an excellent technician. I am a beautiful, well-bred woman. I am a smooth seductress. Good at comforting people I am not. I entered with Jacob, took Calamity’s hand, and looked her in the eye as she spoke. Her friends were upset, and people she cared about, like Dark Kimberly and Becky, were on opposing sides, and Gunnar whom she deeply respected she had been forced to hostily confront. She was deeply unhappy. As she spoke of her guilt, I placed a hand beneath her chin, looked her in the eye and told her this is why she is not a monster. Plainly speaking from the heart is difficult, even painful for me, and I left rapidly after. I am not cut out for it. I am not Sincere Marguerite. It was like when I told the girls I loved them.

                                                It’s very difficult. Painfully.

                                                I only hope I helped, and that Calamity did not misinterpret my rapid exit. I am glad Jacob was there…after opening up that much I needed to get away.

                                                My worry for Calamity and residual disquiet at having been so open with her aside, it has been a good night. I acquired many valuables and impressed Julia, even if she was more interested in the marijuana and tampons than phones. I had stolen a powerful spymaster, though I will have to meet him to ensure there is no further hostility. But I proved I could steal him even from a crowded place, and without his knowing what was happening.

                                                I am truly magnificent.

                                                My time with De La Rocha and Julia was pleasing. De La Rocha is slightly concerned about Oleander, I think, but less from a “Lori will lose interest in me” and more “I don’t want Oleander as my enemy.” I relieved her tensions partially by returning her to her fully submissive mind. I think she can go even deeper without Lorelei there and no fear of not being butch enough for her. Regardless, she was pleased by it, and Julia and I spent some very quality time together too. The night was fun, and a good opportunity to blow off some steam and relieve some tension.

                                                And surrounded by such pretties, I remembered how magnificent Insincere Marguerite truly is.

                                                End Session XXXV

                                                If New WoD has rules for Chloroform I could not find them. I had the subject make a penalized Stamina + Resolve where the penalty grew if they could not struggle out. Insincere Marguerite tended to invoke Touch of Paralyzing Shudder and strike more quickly than her subject could get a breath, which lead to Gunnar and average joe guy being knocked out quickly. The thug actually went down almost immediately. Insincere Marguerite used her full Professional Merit along with Shadowpatch to get a rote action for Stealth and got 15 successes. Gunnar’s eight successes to spot trouble did not match, and she (barely) saw him. Calamity rolled just shy of spotting Gunnar but got a lot of other info. So a lot of the drama for Calamity, Insincere Marguerite and Jacob came down to Stealth and Investigation rolls, the latter of which Calamity and Jacob were addled on due to low Clarity.

                                                Lorelei also rocked some Shadowing rolls keeping an eye on White Maria.

                                                Because of events going on in the news at this writing, I wanted to discuss my use of a Latino gang in this session. Yes, I had Latino criminals as an element. This was due to connection to Lucian Torrez who, honestly, is one of the most dangerous and sinister people in the city, and yes he is Hispanic. But (at least I feel) my portrayal of Latinos in the game has been fair and not intended to make them seem like criminals. De La Rocha, Captain Jack Martinez, Flores and Oleander are good friends and allies of the group, and less closely allied but still positive characters like Sandoval and Juan-Carlos Hypnos are out there as well. So while I had Latino thugs, and not for the last time, I want to be crystal clear at my intentions, which is a balanced view of a major part of California’s population, and avoiding stereotypes as much as possible. We do have a crimelord, but also a punk chick, a wrestler/wrestling manager, nightclub owner, botanist, mancandy and professional dream warrior. Hopefully this is a good spread.

                                                Similar with Asian characters, there are 6 living at current (Becky Wan, White Maria, Dark Kimberly, Shi, Moon, and Davey) none of whom are currently involved in crime, and Dark Kimberly (and honestly, the late Sundown Kwon) merely dabbled where they shouldn’t have.

                                                I don’t have enough Black characters, currently just Dominika. I do need to do something about that. Dominkia is not, at least, any stereotype beyond “Noob” really.

                                                Anyway, that is it for this session. Next session is mostly an RP session with fun on the beach…or what passes for such with a group with a bunch of Darklings. See you then, comments and questions welcome.
