                                            Originally posted by GottaGoFeast View Post
                                            I understand the gist of what you're saying. Changelings, being half-fae, would have tinges of blue and orange in their morality. I am just concerned that this new perspective will make it excessively hard for changelings to connect with non-changelings, especially mortals. I appreciate the caveat that the ways of losing and gaining Clarity specific to each Seeming happen rarely, but I would be glad and quite relieved if Clarity was defined in no uncertain terms in 2ED, so as to prevent STs and players from approaching the experience of being a changeling from the wrong direction.
                                            Err... Its not exactly that Changelings have blue and orange morality, so much as that Clarity just doesn't measure morality at all. Clarity is a measure of a Changeling's grasp on reality. Things that call to mind their experiences in Arcadia can be traumatizing (causing Breaking Points), and like a veteran having a flashback, recalling that trauma can potentially damage their ability to discern fantasy from reality (loss of Clarity). Things that reinforce their sense of agency reassure them that they are greater than their trauma and strengthen their ability to recognize what they actually perceive versus what they merely imagine (Clarity gain).

                                            Changelings absolutely have a difficult time connecting with non-changelings, just as victims of severe trauma have a difficult time connecting with people who live relatively sheltered lives. That's one of the central parts of the drama of Changeling - you've been places and experienced things that other people just can't understand or identify with, but now you're back in the real world and one way or another, you've got to find your place in it. Fortunately, there are some others that have had similar experiences, and they can help you, but you also can't only associate with those people and shut yourself off from everyone else. Or rather, you can, but it wouldn't be very healthy. So you do your best to connect with other people despite the fact that it is excessively hard.

                                            Don't worry, from what I've seen, Clarity is extremely well-defined. I really really want to talk more about it once it's spoiled, or once the game comes out (which ever comes first) because, unless it's changed significantly since the version I've seen, it is my absolute favorite thing about 2e Changeling.
