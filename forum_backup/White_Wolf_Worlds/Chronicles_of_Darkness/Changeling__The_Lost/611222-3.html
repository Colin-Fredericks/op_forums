                                                        I'm reminded of a question I'd asked some time ago, about whether a Changeling without a Seeming might develop one after the fact. Possibly related to that -- and perhaps more relevant than most to a Loyalist -- I wonder whether a Changeling's Seeming could change under extreme circumstances.

                                                        It's easy to imagine the narrative of a Loyalist who, through experience and dramatic action, tries to make a clear break with that life.* I can just also see interest in both of the big ways it could play out. Perhaps a later change in Seeming is possible: a rare Changeling who undergoes a sufficiently extreme change in perspective and/or life events might become something else. Or perhaps the consequences of that choice are irrevocable: all Lost retain the will and power to shape the courses of their lives, but what they chose then will always color them.

                                                        * Less optimistically, a Changeling of a non-Loyalist Seeming eventually seeks patronage under one of the Gentry as the closest thing to peace there is to find.
