                Well, from what I can see this is going to be a controversial opinion. Oh well, here goes nothing! I can't get behind this draft. Almost not at all! That worries me because CtL is my favorite line out of all the White Wolf products. So what's bugging me about this?

                The first issue that jumps out at me is that the writing feels sterile. It feels like I'm reading bullets on a power point slide trying to bash out as much exposition as it can without letting it breath. Plus, there doesn't seem to be anything to draw the reader in. The text just jumps right into talking about the Fairest without giving the reader a chance to get a clear picture in their heads. The first edition gave a short fairy tale attempting to illustrate the overarching concept of the Seemings, and to me those helped not only with understanding the Seemings, but also the tales and the folklore that they sprung from. However, that could be first draft stuff! That kind of flavor could be skipped over in favor of just getting the Seeming concept on paper. Perfectly understandable.

                My second issue is more fundamental. I don't like how the Fairest's core concept has been changed. They went from being the epitome of Faerie's beauty to the Leader Seeming? Why exactly? I've seen it posited that it's to parallel common reactions to abuse, but that rubs me the wrong way. I would rather have my character's reaction to the trauma suffered in Arcadia determined by their personality than by their Seeming. That kind of parallelism is much better suited to the Courts (since I've seen it argued that they are based off the 5 stages of grief(minus one)) because which Court you end up in is your character's choice. This change seems to want to dictate how my character acts and the demons that haunt them, and as a player I want those details to be left up to my imagination. I also agree with the people who expressed their dislike for having a designated Leader Seeming. Not every player who wants to lead will want to play a Fairest, and not every player who wants to play a Fairest will want to lead.

                This is all just my opinion, and it could very well be a bad reaction to change. I'm certainly not going to rain on anyone's parade if they like this conception of the Fairest over another. I just don't see myself enjoying the direction this draft has started out on. Okay falling asleep on my keyboard now. Hopefully there was some useful feedback in that rambling mess!
