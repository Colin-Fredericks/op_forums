                            Remember, this is Arcadia, where "blending into the background" and" becoming part of the scenery" are not just turns of phrase, they're literal transformations. The act of abandoning your ego and becoming one with your environment is a means of escape, because the Wyrd transforms you into an embodiment of the environment. That gives you a way to sneak away, or a new means of locomotion your Keeper might not have accounted for.

                                                Originally posted by Anteros View Post
                                                Do you mean they all gain dots Elemental Contracts when they gain their Seemings?
                                                No, they become the elements. Just like a Beast becomes a wild animal, a Darkling becomes the darkness, a Fairest becomes akin to the Fair Folk, and an Ogre becomes a hulking monster. The Act of taking agency in Arcadia physically changes you, and those changes allow you to escape. In the case of elementals, that agency comes from letting go of the self, becoming instead part of the world around you, which makes camouflage much easier and may have other advantages as well. Contracts are a thing you learn after the fact, to bring a bit of that Arcadian Wyrdness into the mundane world you ran back to.

                                                    I wouldn't say 'all' by any means, but it's a possible route, in principle.
