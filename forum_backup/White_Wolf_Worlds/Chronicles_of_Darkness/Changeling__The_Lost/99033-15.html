                            Session XXXVI

                            Gawain’s Memories

                            Jun 27th, morning

                            Lori has the best ideas. Just, the best. A beach trip was an awesome celebration/distraction for/from everything I think even Julia enjoyed it, in her way. Lori suggested it at breakfast. It maybe started as trying to, you know, distract us from the fact that her wings get all fluttery when she has enough coffee, you know? But it kinda snowballed once we got a couple of the darkling majority to agree. De la Roche, of course, can’t let the sun win, Insincere Marguerite agreed so long as she got a nice big kerchief and Julia just got a promise she wouldn’t have to leave her umbrella shade. Calamity agreed when I looked pleadingly at her, so it was a date. We even got Ace out, though he spent the whole time sunning on a rock.

                            And it was great! Bikinis and surfing and volleyball and a picnic and sandcastles. I hadn’t been surfing since I got back, but I picked it up quick enough, even with a rented board. I was able to give Calamity some pointers too. She and Lori and Insincere Marguerite all caught on quick. Insincere Marguerite was even balancing on her tongue.

                            We split up into the Lovers (De la Roche, Lori, Oleander and Insincere Marguerite) and the Fighters (me, Calamity, Luna and Jacob) to play volleyball. We Fighters lost. Just barely though, even with De la Roche’s flashing shenanigans. Not that I minded - a fun game and boobs is even better than a fun game and winning, you know?

                            After our picnic and sunscreen break, it was sand castles time. Calamity got a bit impatient with one of the walls of the structure (that was admittedly starting to look unfortunately like my Bertilak’s manor) and it collapsed. I stuck my tongue out at her and started a new one, though I didn’t get too far, you know, what with the laughing at her second project - a shark’s fin cutting a wake through our ruins. I had told her she wasn’t allowed to fake a shark’s fin in the water, before when she mentioned glamour harvesting, so she kept looking furtively at me and making faces.

                            It was an awesome time and led into a great show. Calamity dressed up really nice and did her thing. It was an Annika night and she was great, like usual. She seemed, you know, a bit grumpy though, even with Calamity. I hope she’s OK and Madame Spidertoes got to talk to her like she wanted. I got to hang out with her and Moon some, which was cool.

                            After the show... well, that wasn’t so fun. Gunnar had told Calamity that someone was selling drugs from the Dream House. Turns out it was Jacob. I wouldn’t really mind, though heroin is a hell of a drug, but he was doing it for Darke. Maybe it’s bad that, you know, that’s what bugs me but... it’s people’s choices to do drugs. And legally, the Dream House is not really directly implicated in it. I just don’t like the idea of supporting Darke in any way.

                            Anyways, with that shared around, we got out the ice cream, booze and movies. We were just chatting at first, some about our fetches, cause Julia had looked into Insincere Marguerite's, cause she wanted to know her last name. It’s DeLisle, though Julia thought it was Blessent, cause that’s her married name. Calamity and Lori had talked about their fetches some before, but I think I confused them all by saying mine was a Duke. And De la Roche mentioned she didn't have one. I knew that happened sometimes - Peter, the Dawn Court guy I met on the way down to Santa Monica, didn’t have one. De la Roche was able to pick her old life back up though.

                            We also talked about escaping. Lori had heard a song, Lorelei by Styx, while she was singing Amazing Grace. Thus, her name. De la Roche ran into Monica and got punched out. Julia used to be able to paint paths in the Hedge, so that’s how she got out. Jacob knew his way out, what with the stuff and all. Calamity told our story, though she tried to say I led her out. I might have broken a way through, you know, but she knew the way home. It was good to have happy storytelling time though.

                            Once we got the liquor out, we decided to watch Cannibal, so Lori could approve it for a performance. We agreed yellowface for Insincere Marguerite crossed a line, so we’re just gonna have her be obviously French (it’ll be hard, you know?) and pretending to be an Indian. It gets the same joke across anyway. We wound up having to explain Smurfs to Lori part of the way through. See, she wondered if shpadoinkle was a real word, and Calamity explained it wasn’t by saying it was like the word smurf, just, you know, a stand in for whatever. So we went through that whole thing, which is really kinda disturbing and racist, thinking about it.

                            The Cyclops got Insincere Marguerite talking about why she avoided rural America. Apparently it was him, and ‘The Field of the Dreams’. I shouldn’t make fun of her for that quirk, cause she does speak English really well, and that’s an easy translation mistake to make, coming from a romance language, you know? I mostly just mention it cause I did correct it at the time so Lori could look it up easier (and since Insincere Marguerite’s description was the movie about ‘the baseball field full of dead people’ I thought maybe she should?). When Insincere Marguerite got defensive, and Lori responded by saying her own French was impeccable, Insincere Marguerite almost had an aneurysm. She'll forgive Lori eventually, I’m sure.

                            So, it was a great day and a great night after. It felt like forever since I got to spend the night with just Calamity, even if it had only been a couple. She’d been much more... calm? centered? something about all the mess of the night before than I’d expected. I doubt her conversation with Becky went better than expected, you know, since she’d stayed up all night playing poker with Jacob. Maybe her therapist was onto something with the whole meditation thing. Anyway, it was nice seeing her be ok, and falling asleep with her and waking up next to her. Hopefully day is that good too.


                            Lorelei’s Diary

                            I feel good. Even in the middle of the shit-storm surrounding White Maria and Dark Kimberly, I feel really good. I think every once in a while we need a day like yesterday to unwind and take a break from the stresses and concerns of our daily lives.

                            So as I was saying, I woke up yesterday morning with Oleander snuggled in my arms. I must have shifted or something, because she opened her eyes and blinked sleepily a few times. I saw something settle in behind her eyes before the smile touched her lips. “Good morning, beautiful,” she said as she stretched.

                            “Right back at you,” I said, kissing her forehead.

                            “So, you want to hang out here for a while,” she asked, “or do you want to head back to the Dream House for the infamous ‘Sex Breakfast?’” The curiosity was written clearly across her face.

                            I chuckled. “Sex Breakfast sounds wonderful,” I said, “but only if you’ll come with me this time.”

                            She agreed, and twenty minutes later we were both en route to the Dream House (in separate cars). Gawain was just getting started when we got back – omelets! – and was happy to set another place at the table. He was in a great mood, as he’d managed to remove the trigger from Desiree’s subconscious during the previous night.

                            Desiree had already left, but the rest of the usual suspects were all present and accounted for. D was fiddling with some sort of hand-held video game that Marguerite had stolen from someone at the pier the previous night. I pursed my lips disapprovingly, but decided to let it pass. For now. Things were going about like usual, with the innuendo flying around the table hot and heavy. Oleander didn’t really contribute much to that, but she took it with good grace.

                            I’m not sure what got me thinking along these lines, but something suddenly occurred to me. “Hey,” I said quietly. It must have been something about the way I said it, because everyone stopped talking and looked at me. “Well,” I said, “I just thought… we’ve lived here together in Malibu for almost two months, right… so why haven’t we ever taken a day and gone to the beach together?”

                            Gawain was all for it, and Oleander smiled warmly. “Sounds like fun,” she said with a sparkle of mischief in her eyes, “but how are you going to convince the pack of darklings?”

                            I thought about it. “Well, D’s no problem,” I commented easily. “She thinks that if she hides from the sun, the sun will somehow win, and she’s vowed never to give it the satisfaction. Right, sweetie?”

                            “Damn right,” she commented, walking to a window and miming that she was watching her nemesis carefully.

                            Marguerite opined that she would be fine as long as she had a large kerchief to keep the sun off her face, and Calamity – like D – decided to face the sun without flinching, but Julia was a tougher nut to crack. “Can’t we just go for a stroll along the beach after sunset?” she asked plaintively. “You know, moonlit walks on the shore? It’s romantic, right?”

                            I sighed, and might have rolled my eyes a little. “That would be missing the point of the beach, honey,” I said gently. “Plus, we’ve got a show tonight, and it’s a Friday. We can’t just skip out two nights in a row.” She eventually relented, on the condition that she be allowed to bring a large umbrella to keep out the hated sunlight.

                            Honestly! Darklings!

                            Oleander ran back to her place to get a suit, and the rest of us got changed and packed up. I wore a nice dappled blue and green bikini that goes well with my wings (and, I’m told, with the way my eyes appear to look through the Mask). D put on a somewhat more sedate bikini – purple, natch – and grabbed a couple of surfboards that have been sitting in our garage for a while.

                            Most of the others wore attractive if not remarkable suits (Oleander showed up in a sedate one-piece with oval windows along the sides), but Marguerite decided to go all out in a barely-there bikini that left her ass almost completely exposed. It’s a nice ass, so I didn’t think we’d get any complaints.

                            We all piled into Gawain’s van (parking is murder at the beach) and headed down to the shore. Some of the others rented surfboards from a little hut off the beach, and we made our way down to the waterside. Julia immediately set up her umbrella and hid herself away beneath it, but the others were really getting into the spirit of the thing. Oh, except Ace, who just found a nice warm rock in the sun and curled up.

                            First, D taught me how to surf. It’s not that hard once you get the hang of it (though I suppose my wings give me a slight advantage where matters of balance are concerned). I felt a little bad, though, because D was spending so much of her time and attention on helping me that she didn’t get to really cut loose and show what she could do. Gawain was pretty impressive out there, and I saw D narrow her eyes at him, like he was challenging her or something.

                            I tried to defuse the situation by suggesting a beach volleyball match, but that just stoked my honey’s competitive fires even higher. Calamity suggested that we divide the teams into the “Lovers” (me, D, Marguerite, and Oleander) and the “Fighters” (Calamity, Gawain, Jacob, and Luna). D was so into the competition that she didn’t even bat an eye at being lumped in with us “lovers.”

                            The game was pretty close in the end. I get the impression that there were some dirty tricks played on both sides – distractions and whatnot – but I didn’t actually witness anything, so I kept my mouth shut. We’d drawn a bit of a crowd by the time we were through… six smoking hot girls and two hunky guys in swimsuits jumping around on the beach tends to do that. We couldn’t resist showing off a little. I saw Marguerite return a serve with her tongue at least once, and I used my wings a couple of times to get some extra “hang time.” When the scores were tallied up, the Lovers had won the day, but only just barely.

                            That seemed to satisfy D’s competitive streak. She was able to relax a bit and enjoy the day after that. In fact, she took her board out and went surfing again, and was able to pull some much more impressive moves this time. I mostly waded in the water and cheered her on, before heading back to the beach, lying on my stomach, untying my bikini top, and wondering loudly if anyone would like to apply more suntan lotion to my back. There were a few volunteers.

                            I had never really thought about it before, but it is going to be really difficult to maintain an even tan on my back. I know it’s what they call a “first world problem” these days, but still… sigh.

                            Calamity and Gawain busied themselves making sand castles while I preened and showed off for the crowds. I even managed to do a little bit of advertising, and I think some of those guys are quite likely to drop by the Dream House before too long.

                            And speaking of, all good things must come to an end, and so it was with our lovely day at the beach. We made it back to the Dream House with minutes to spare, and performed to a packed house. I think the show went quite well, though everyone could tell that Annika was not in the best of moods (she was presumably upset over the situation with Dark Kimberly), and it might have affected her performance adversely. I feel really really bad about the tiny shivers of glee that sets off in me whenever I think about it.

                            For my first number, I dressed up in a slinky evening gown and sat on a swing inside a gilded birdcage. I sang a song called “Learning to Fly” by Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers. Taken on its own, the song is cheerful; even uplifting. But I infused it with a sense of loss and longing so profound – based upon my own few memories of the freedom true flight grants – that it became a very sad song. I hope Jacob and the few other Winter Courtiers in the crowd enjoyed it.

                            I miss flying. Freud said that when you dream about flying, it’s supposed to represent sex. Sometimes I wonder if that works in reverse, too, and I’m so addicted to sex because when it’s good it reminds me of flying…

                            I lightened things up a bit in the second set. I actually made a last-minute substitution, got back into my bikini, and wallowed in self-parody as I belted out “Cause I’m a Blonde” by Julie Brown. I’ve noticed that, of all things, dumb blonde jokes have persevered through all the years that I was gone. People will tell the same jokes that I heard when I was a kid, convinced that they’re fresh and witty. Those of us who happen to have light-colored hair will never escape from the stereotypes, so it seems like the best thing we can do is own it.

                            Plus, that song is fucking hilarious.

                            Unfortunately things began to get a little tense after the show. I’d been wandering around schmoozing and paying my respects to the local bigwigs (still in my bikini, but with a kimono tied loosely over it) when I heard someone hissing quietly at me from behind a pillar. Wandering over that way, I found Julia looking a little worried.

                            “What are you going to do about this?” she asked softly.

                            “What?” I asked, confused.

                            She gestured her head to where D was sitting with Monica at one end of the room. Then she shook her head in the other direction, to where Oleander was sipping a drink quietly. Once she’d pointed it out to me, the tension between the two of them was obvious. Neither one of them looked like they wanted a confrontation, but there was a palpable stress on the room’s social dynamic.

                            “Shit,” I swore softly.

                            “If it was me,” Julia advised soberly, “I’d slip out the back door and not stop running until morning. But you’re better at this stuff than me.”

                            I sighed, approached each of them, and asked if I could speak with them privately for a few moments. I led them both to a secluded table in the back corner of the theater, took their hands, and started to speak earnestly.

                            “Look,” I said softly, “I don’t want things to be awful between you two, okay? I care about you both, and I want to keep spending time with both of you. So let’s talk about the elephant in the room.”

                            Oleander looked at her own lap. “Don’t worry, Lori… I have nothing against de la Rocha, and I certainly don’t want to fight her or anything. And I don’t want to monopolize your time, either. It’s just… a little awkward right now.”

                            D nodded her agreement. “I don’t want to fight either, babe,” she said, giving my hand a squeeze, “and I hope I’ve proven that I’m not the jealous type. But sometimes you just have to flex your muscles and show your teeth, you know?”

                            I couldn’t quite suppress a sigh. Oleander squeezed my hand. “Look, the point is that neither one of us wants to make this difficult for you, Lori.” She looked away for a moment and seemed to reach a decision. “You spent last night at my place. I’ll head on home and let you catch up with de la Rocha, okay?”

                            D turned to her and said something interrogative in Spanish, and Oleander nodded.

                            “I do want to spend tonight with D, Oleander,” I admitted. “If you’re sure you don’t mind…”

                            She smiled and nodded. “I can catch up on my Netflix queue,” she said lightly as she stood up.

                            “I’ll see you soon,” I promised.

                            “Count on it,” she said, and then she was gone.

                            D looked sort of sheepish when I turned back to her. “Sorry about the Spanish,” she said softly. “It’s just that when I’ve hung out with her in the past, we usually speak Spanish. I wasn’t thinking, and it just slipped out.”

                            “No worries,” I said with a smile. “I should probably try to pick up some of the lingo myself, if I’m going to keep making a pest of myself and hanging out with you. What did you say?”

                            “I just asked her if she was sure, that’s all.”

                            D and I went upstairs to join a conversation already in progress with the rest of the motley. Several of them expressed sympathy for the situation from which I had just emerged, and Calamity light-heartedly suggested that the perfect solution would be a three-way orgy between Oleander, D, and myself. I glanced at my girlfriend and shrugged noncommittally, as if to suggest that it was worth a try.

                            D sighed and rolled her eyes. “Look, I’ve come to grips with the fact that I like girls, okay? But that doesn’t mean I’m attracted to every girl I meet.”

                            I smiled and took her hand. “Joking,” I said, “mostly. But look, D… I love that you’re willing to let me have this thing with Oleander… but a part of me also loves that it bothers you just a little bit.” I looked into her eyes for a minute, oblivious to the others. “Sweetie, you know that if you say the word…”

                            She kissed me before I could finish. “We’re good,” she said simply after a minute.

                            “So,” I said, turning back toward the others with a blush, “what were we talking about?”

                            Jacob sighed. “I have been selling heroin for Darke,” he admitted with resignation.

                            That kind of killed the lighthearted mood. Apparently Gunnar mentioned something to Calamity about someone selling drugs out of the Dream House, and when she raised the subject with the motley, Jacob admitted that he had been doing so ever since we opened. Though none of us were particularly happy about the situation, once we talked it through and realized that we could probably insulate the Dream House itself from his actions if things get legal, I think we came to terms with it. He’s not forcing the drugs on anyone, and he’s not selling them to kids or anything like that. Like it or not, people are making a conscious choice to buy his product.

                            (I confess that I did ask if he had access to any other illicit substances. I have no interest in heroin, but I confess to being a little curious about this “ecstasy” drug, and I wouldn’t mind dropping acid again at some point. Unfortunately, he answered in the negative. Looks like I’m stuck with whatever pot I can grow on my balcony or bum off Julia for the time being).

                            Conversation turned to other things quickly. Julia mentioned that she had done some digging and discovered that Marguerite’s real last name is Blessent. Marguerite was quick to correct her, informing us that this was the name of her Fetch’s ex-husband. She seemed pretty steamed that Julia had gone looking into her past like that, but she didn’t make a big deal out of it, and in fact told us a few minutes later that her mortal name had been Marguerite DeLisle, but as she doesn’t like the person she used to be, she doesn’t feel any real kinship with that name anymore.

                            That got us talking about names. Calamity doesn’t have a last name at the moment, but in this day and age that’s not really much of a handicap. I told the story of how I came up with my current moniker, basing it on the two songs that helped me make it out of the Hedge.

                            Talk then turned to our first same-gender sexual encounters. Julia talked fondly about waking up with Marguerite lying on top of her (something I’ve experienced for myself), and that got me thinking about the first time I slept with Missy and Jane. It was in Arcadia, of course, so parts of it are still pretty hazy in my mind, but I know that I joined that party late… the two of them had been sleeping together for at least a few weeks before I finally joined in out of sheer loneliness. The two of them had each other, and I had been a third wheel, and I was tired of it. There were no men around aside from Hunting Hawk, and as they say: “any port in a storm.” I’m glad I’ve always been adventurous. I’ve never looked back.

                            D’s story was a bit disturbing. She remembered waking up in an unfamiliar bed in an unfamiliar room. She slowly began to remember the events of the previous night, and gradually turned over… to see Madame Spidertoes perched on a chair and watching her in absolute silence.

                            Yeesh! I’m glad none of my darklings are quite so existentially creepy.

The others want to do a production of this film for the Dream House and I applaud them for it. I am curious to see what they come up with. I may have fallen asleep through some of it, but then I have been up for two days straight. Speaking of which I need sleep. I will write more if all goes well tomorrow. If nothing is written in this journal after this entry then I will bid you a fond farewell.


                                    Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

                                    Today has been a good day. I’ve come down from a nice late night snack on the roof with Julia. I expressed how I felt about my privacy intrusion, she discussed her frustration that I was withholding my family name, and we got back in a good place with each other. Fortunately.

                                    I had not really considered how my withholding a name might affect her. Admittedly, she did no say it bothered her, but even semi-even relationships are new to me. The implications of her interest have also not been missed.

                                    Today started as it often does. De La Rocha was playing the portable game system I acquired when Lorelei and Oleander arrived for breakfast. I felt (without even touching her) De La Rocha tense but she kept playing. Lorelei played it some but feigned ignorance of video games. This despite having played more modern ones with us. Video games are not her favored sort of games. Lorelei was super keen, however, on going to the beach. The beach is…not my first choice, but several wanted to go, so I decided not to be a killjoy. I put on my kerchief (the French Flag one) and a bathing suit and stepped out. Julia wore a long one-piece bathing suit that was conservative, and a long tee shirt with some Goth girl character having coffee. Lorelei and De La Rocha wore bikinis, of course. Oleander had to go home for a suit and Jacob called Luna so there was some time for them to arrive. Luna for her part wore a one-piece but forest green and not quite as covering as Julia’s black one.

                                    There was surfing, in which Gawain did a rather remarkable job especially for a man his size. Others of us tried with less spectacular results. I hear Malibu is a major surfing location, and can see why. It was easy to pick up extra boards…only De La Rocha owned any, and she used hers. She does like purple. Obviously.

                                    Surfing was fun. The water is, however, a touch cold. Then there was the volleyball, the “lovers” (Lorelei, De La Rocha, Oleander and I) against the “fighters” (Calamity, Gawain, Luna, Jacob). How De La Rocha got in a team called the “lovers” over a team called the “fighters” remains mysterious. I did use my tongue and flexibility to show off a bit, then we waded in the water some, at which point De La Rocha did some very impressive surfing, I suppose the waves cooperated more with her then. Then it was all sunbathing and sandcastles. Happily.

                                    Julia, for her part, put up a black pavilion umbrella and sat on a beach chair and watched us through her sunglasses, quite happy to not be in the sunlight. I thought I was photophobic.

                                    Eventually we went in, all of good cheer. The show went all right, I am given to understand Annika was not in a good mood. Spidertoes was in the audience and we spoke briefly. Gunnar arrived to get his gun and battery for his phone. I manifested myself, and we sat awhile and talked. Gunnar conceded we were, in fact, a serious threat to each other. He could seek revenge for being trussed up and kidnapped, as was his inclination, but depending on how complete it was, I might come after him again and leave him in a situation he was less likely to walk out of. He sought an end to hostilities, so we made a cease-fire pact, that we would not act against the other…without a courtesy note ending the protection a reasonable amount of time before the first strike. This seemed fair (I was not after him anyway) so we agreed to it and he was on his way.

                                    Lorelei found herself in a tense moment as the show ended, as Oleander and De La Rocha were…well not staring down but sort of watching like great cats in the same cage. There was tension, and it was growing. Lorelei managed to defuse the situation for now, with Oleander headed home and De La Rocha staying with her. I hope it does not become a serious problem. Neither Oleander with her poisons nor De La Rocha with her aggression and aptitude for curses are people I would want as my enemy.

                                    While that went on the rest of us had one of the most meandering conversations we have ever had. Calamity had discovered someone was dealing, and it turned out that someone was Jacob, dealing Heroin. I felt this was a poor choice. Heroin is a drug cops notice. They may be slow to move against weed (especially in LA) but Heroin is a problem. Still, it was his deal and I was unprepared to move against him. The conversation obviously had him uncomfortable, so I made reference to Julia and weed, and after mentioning a distaste for her dealer Gawain recommended his old landlord in Santa Monica to get weed from while Calamity recommended Austin. Lorelei sauntered over and noted she was growing now, using her Spring magic. Spring magic is interesting.

                                    I wonder why Gawain did not join the Summer Court. He is good a being angry. Maybe he needs weed.

                                    Names had come up, and my last name came up when De La Rocha asked, and Julia said my last name was Blessent. This is untrue, I was confused a moment then I realized…she used the old picture of I to find my Fetch, using the reverse Google search. I found this disquieting. Julia had tracked down my fat replacement and could have found my family. I quickly told her my name was not Blessent, the name of my Fetch’s ex-husband I never married, but DeLisle.

                                    I never told them that because Marguerite DeLisle was the person I was before being taken. Insincere Marguerite is how I returned. Marguerite DeLisle was a fascist with the Front National. I am Insincere Marguerite, not Marguerite DeLisle. Still, it has caused Julia some joy to learn the name. I am surprised by how she has taken to it, whispering it to herself or writing it, given her lack of interest in romance. But she has a romantic soul and a poetic heart; she just hides it. She is, perhaps, one of the most passionate people I know. If only she would allow herself to see herself that way. Romantically.

                                    I digress, from there we moved into the awkwardness of our first same sex encounter. Lorelei’s and I’s was in Arcadia, but Julia talked about waking up and mistaking my hair for hers before realizing I was laying on her (I am rather light), and De La Rocha spoke of waking up, lying it bed a few minutes, and looking over to see Madame Spidertoes silently watching her in a chair eating a sandwich. That one won for being surreal. Calamity for her part noted she has never slept with a member of the same sex. Just Gawain. Because of his armor. Naturally.
                                    Sadly the boys did not have one. Shame, I have no idea how two men would handle wake up or that sort of thing. Same as anyone else I guess? Maybe I can ask Jack.

                                    We decided to watch some movies, to cap off a good night,. I suggested Mulholland Drive but we just watched Cannibal the Musical. We will watch Mulholland Drive Monday, I suppose. We discussed options for our burlesque interpretation, and I made some notes while Julia scribbled “DeLisle” all fancy over and over. There was a sidetrack about the Smurfs, which Lorelei somehow had not heard of despite them going back to the 60s, and Lorelei for reasons that could only have been her own asserted her French was flawless after Gawain corrected my English, leaving I unsure how to deal with such a blatant falsehood without saying something ugly, so I just said nothing, I don’t think. I was too addled to recall clearly, honestly.

                                    Afterwards, the couples retired. I ascertained that Jacob was okay then went for a rooftop picnic with Julia to cap off the night. Romantically. Julia said hearing me say that name made it more real for her. Perhaps I will reclaim it, but I will always be Insincere Marguerite.

                                    We held hands and slipped back down into the house. It was a good day.

                                    End Session XXXVI

                                    Total minor mechanic note, Insincere Marguerite had to use Contracts of Separation to balance on her tongue. When she has weight she cannot support herself with the appendage without spending 1WP per turn. Also on the subject of our expat, she speaks imperfect English as MDH has been using the old language rules. Everyone got a 3 point and 2 point language, and while most had English primary, she had French primary English Secondary, similar to Jacob with Russian and English.

                                    On Vollyball, we used teamwork. Lorelei got extra dice for her wings, Insincere Marguerite got extra dice for tongue and activating her tunnelgrub flexibility. DLR made a roll to cost Gawain dice by flashing him, and Calamity used her Whisperwisp ability to distract DLR and cost HER dice too. Lorelei’s player rolled for the lovers and Jacob’s player for the fighters. Obviously the lovers had…certain advantages.

                                    Well most of the rolling was at the beach, beyond Lorelei rolling for performances. The RP was awesome.
