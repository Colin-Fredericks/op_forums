    RE: Goblins

    Originally posted by milo v3 View Post
    Wait... so Goblins are horrors, but changelings who are turned into goblins use mechanics with no similarities to horror mechanics... that's odd.
    Not Necessarily.

    "Horrors" by themselves are not a specific creature type (like vampire or werewolf), but a open category of monster that don't fit neatly into an existing gameline. They are intended to allow a storyteller to easily generate antagonists. Its that flexibility that makes it a good choice as the mechanical backing for the diversity of creatures that would be considered Goblins. It is likely they will be using the basic "Horror" framework, with Goblin-specific adjustments (think in the same way ephemeral entities use the same basic rules, but have additional mechanic and traits specific to type).

    I expect Goblins to have a Wyrd score, but will function in mostly the same way as "Potency" does for a standard Horror.

    The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.

    Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

        "Hobgoblin" (or "goblin") is a term that just means a person or creature that belongs to the Hedge and is infused with its nature, and they can vary wildly. So the Horror rules, which are CofD's universal mechanism for building supernatural creatures that don't belong to a strict category and are Storyteller characters, are a natural fit here.

        And in the same way that you can build a vampire with the Horror rules that isn't Kindred, you can build a goblin with them that isn't a Goblin Queen of the kind that the Condition creates. There are lots of different potential origins for a goblin--the Condition is just one of them. If a hobgoblin has a specific origin, like being a former changeling or what have you, then you can use those rules instead.

        So yeah, a goblin antagonist can definitely trade in Glamour--after all, the Contracts they sell you use it! The Horror rules allow the Storyteller to create all kinds of unique goblins, and Willpower is the abstraction that system uses to represent whatever a given unique monster might be doing.
