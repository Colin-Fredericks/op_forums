    Dark Eras for us!

    03-30-2016, 03:35 PM
    So from Dark Eras Changeling has gotten:

    Three Kingdoms of Darkness (220-280)- Combo setting with Geist (2ed)
    -Directional Courts (4 Directions and Center)
    -6 new Directional Contracts

    Requiem for Regina (1593)- Add on to the vampire setting. (2ed)
    -Rose Courts (Blood, Snow, Ash)
    -3 new Contracts

    Lily, Sabre, and Thorn (1600s- early 1700s)- Pre-Revolutionary France (1ed)
    -Seasonal Courts
    -New Contract of Blades
    -3 new Tokens
    -New Entitlement: The Knights of St. Collen

    Grimm Dark Era (1812-1820)- German Fairy Tales (2ed)
    -Freehold of Golden Creation (Sciences, Arts)
    -4 new Trifles
    -4 new Tokens
    -6 new Contracts
    -Grimm Seeming
    -New Entitlement: Companions of the Resigned
    -New Entitlement: The Order of the Story Heroes

    So it's quite a bit! Thoughts, opinions, hopes, and dreams? I've already got a Grimm character I want to play now, and I wish Fabula Rasa still had their Elizabethan Lost game going...

                              I may have skipped ahead to read about the Grimms...

                              But yeah, that era is currently experiencing a "story re-write" of the Hedge and Arcadia. Previously, all the little towns in Germany have slightly different stories, meaning magic works slightly differently there (and going to a new town gives you a -1 to all magic stuff until you get acclimated). Then, the Bros Grimm published their book, and everyone started reading it. And in the towns where a lot of people read it, the Hedge and Arcadia started shifting, just a little, until it matched the stories that people were reading (or at least, better matched). The town goes through a month of Wyrd updates, where Contracts go haywire and Masks might drop for no reason, and then, it's fine, back to normal. But, between that town and any other town that experienced this, their magic is now same-y enough that there's not a penalty for traveling to and from those places. They have the same (enough) stories. All because of the power of literacy.

                              Implications being, of course, that separate areas have separate stories, and traveling between them causes things to work just a bit differently. As places share stories (through books, newspapers, internet), they become more similar story-wise and Wyrd-wise. Meaning early eras may have had these variations through each town, but more modern ones share stories by state, nation, even globally. (Also means that, if stories get shared enough places, Arcadia will change to match. Slenderman was once just part of the internet, but he's popular enough that the Black-Suited Being can snatch you away to fairy-land now...)
