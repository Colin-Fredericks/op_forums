      [Huntsmen] are bounty hunters, not dragons.
      [Tv Tropes Smartass]Technically, a Huntsman could be a capital D Dragon, but that raises the question of (A) how they managed to get in a True Fae's good graces to the point where they became an effective second-in-command, and (B) why the solipsistic Fae thought that having a second-in-command in the first place was such a great plan. Good story hook, that.[/Tv Tropes Smartass]

      Seriously though, nice Outline. I love the implied icons system, kind of like anchors for living changelings. They are kind of like ghosts of long-destroyed people, eradicated in the crucible of the Durance. The difference is that a changeling is likely more than what they left behind..Also, love the fact that we have a create-a-Court system now, and the fact that mortal allies are called "potential targets." And this is why Huntsmen were what the Chronicle was about, not the Gentry.
