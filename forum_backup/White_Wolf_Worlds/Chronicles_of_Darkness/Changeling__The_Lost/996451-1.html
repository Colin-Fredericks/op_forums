                                I will risk to post an opinion that is contrary to the concept of Vampire/Changelling and other hybrids.

                                I'm not a fan of mixing supernaturals. In fact, I don't like it at all.

                                I recognize the work behing the Admonitoria, don't misunderstand me.

                                Is that... mixing supernaturals, for me, it feels like "world of darkness goes munchkin" or "goes D&D" (behold my elf warrior/thief/mage/arcane archer/assassin!!).

                                It makes me remember also a module of the old world of darkness (I don't remember the name right now) were the antagonist had vampire disciplines, garou gifts and awakened magic... Or the trilogy of novels of the Masquerade of the Red Death, were in the final pages two characters presumed of having all kind of supernatural powers.

                                I felt like the authors were saying "behold, my super NPCs Vampire/Mage/Werewolf that breaks all rules!! Mwahahaha! Are your pants still clean? Mwahahaha!" (it has been 10 years or more since I read it, so my memory can betray me, but this was what I felt when reading the module and the novels).

                                No, I don't like this mixings at all.

                                Want a changelling vampire? Take the leechfinger kith, or work with your Storyteller to readapt it.
                                Want a mage werewolf? Take a Thyrsus and specialice him in the correct Life and Spirit spells.
                                Want a Vampire Mage? There are oficial rules to adapt the Tremere and the Giovanni from Masquerade to Requiem.
                                Want a Beast/Promethean/Geist/Mage? Errr.... you better prepare your wallet to buy lots of pizza for your storyteller and the rest of PCs...

                                I repeat, I am not saying I don't like the writting or the Admonitoria or the work behind it (witch I recognize).

                                I also like the tragic love story told between the Vampire and the Changelling, trying to save her embracing her. But I don't like the tragedy resulting in a Changelling/Vampire hybrid...

                                I'm saying that mixing is not of my liking and the reasons why not.
