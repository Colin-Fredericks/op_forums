              You soooorrt of can. "Can I cover the narrative bases" maps well enough.

              Contract of Dream is in 2e. Pathfinder is ported directly. Dreamsteps replaces Dream 5. Cobblethought is now a basic rule. Dream 3 is arguably a core rule for changelings, but there is nothing to replace the concept space of "make super dream-only weapons and armor." Im not sure that space is a good idea anymore, however, given the emphasis on physically being in the dream. Dream 2 is of limited utility in 2e, given that Dreamsteps is a thing. The narrative part of using a picture to manipulate dreams from afar isnt covered but, again, goes against the themes of 2e dreamwalking.

                              Originally posted by Raistlin View Post
                              A minor note: Do Huntmen have access to contracts? in the playtest there was no mention about that, and it feels strange the changelings would be allowed to keep such an powerful advantage over their nemesis.
                              At the moment, they do not. Specifically, Huntsmen have the mortal template, plus Yearning abilities and 25XP. I suspect this is in no small part to encourage the ST to have the Huntsmen serve as trickster antagonists and not obvious combat brutes. You don't need alot of combat ability when a simple touch can kill someone and take their place.

                              The Huntsment disguise themselves as a cop and slip on a pair of cuffs to the changeling, who expects to just slip out of them. Then, to the Lost's horror, he finds he cannot excape the shackles, nor can he escape the giant police dog's gaze as the cop car drives off through a Hedge gate. Or perhaps the changeling is taken while asleep and unwary, exhausted from fleeing for the night in an unsecured location. Or is betrayed by a fellow Lost who bargained for a Token of Reprieve. Or was attacked by a living statue, akin to the Weeping Angels of Doctor Who.

                              Giving Contracts, however, would encourage a "charge forwards and confront in combat, drag the target to the ground, and have at thee!" mindset. Match up against each other. I don't think that's the intent of the game, however.

                                                      "Chapter One: What Comes Before Here, we talk the fundamental aspects of what makes a changeling. We discuss kiths, which are the mutated forms forced upon changelings by the True Fae. Then, we discuss Seemings, which are the methods of escape manifested. This all occurs for your changeling character before play starts.

                                                      Chapter Two: The Roles We Play Then, we give a primer on where we are now. This is a high-concept overview of Changeling: The Lost’s most important game concepts. New players should be able to understand and dive in just understanding this introductory material.

                                                      Chapter Three: The Laws We Live By These are the rules for changeling characters. This tells you how to actually make your changeling characters, as well as the mystical abilities they wield. If you’re already familiar with the Chronicles of Darkness core rules, this section will build upon that knowledge.

                                                      Chapter Four: The Tools We Wield This chapter is a truncated, Changeling-specific version of the Chronicles of Darkness core rules. This allows you to play with this book alone, without referencing other texts.

                                                      Chapter Five: The Things We Fear Here, we discuss the fae monsters and other threats to changeling existence. This includes a primer on the Hedge, the gateway between the flesh world and Arcadia.

                                                      Chapter Six: The Company We Keep Here, we show you how to build your own freeholds and Courts for play, as well as providing a handful of sample freeholds from all over the world as plug-and-play examples.

                                                      Chapter Seven: The Stories We Tell This is material to help tell stronger stories, and frame the book’s content. This gives tricks and tips for building a better game, and hitting on the strengths of Changeling: The Lost in poignant ways.

                                                      Appendix One: Babes in the Woods This chapter offers a supplementary or alternative mode of play, in the form of humans touched by fae magic, who show subtle signs of the strange. These characters can add depth to your stories, or offer a different way of telling stories about the fae.

                                                      Appendix Two: Conditions This reference chapter details the various Conditions mentioned throughout the book, for ease of access in play. "

                                                      From the introduction.

                                                      So we've seen Chapter 1 & 3, large parts of chapter 5, 6, & Appendix 1 but we haven't really seen yet Chapters 2, 4, & 7. Appendix 2 will most likely be filled mostly with what
                                                      we expect will be in there.

                                                      The First supplement The Hedge, obviously have more detail on the Hedge, hence the name. But its been suggested that it will include a new Seeming, several new courts/freeholds (with the court contracts that go with them), and talk about how freeholds effect the local Hedge. I'd be shocked if there wasn't new Tokens and Goblin fruits/Oddments.

                                                      So ask yourself what else should go into the Hedge Supplement.
