            18) Two legendary Changelings have come to the free holds, Hanzel and Grettle. They plead for sanctuary to hide from their Handler, the Gingerbread Witch who has sent her Gingerbread Soldiers to drag them back to Arcadia! The PC's are assigned to be their bodyguards until the situation can be resolved.

            Grettle is a Wizened that was forced to prepare fattening meals for her brother. Hanzel is an Ogre that was forced to perform hard, manual labor for the Gingerbread Witch, only being allowed breaks to binge on his sister's meals before he was sent back to work by the merciless witch.



            19) The PC's find themselves imprisoned in an arena and forced to take part in death battles for the entertainment of others. The terms are simple, they need to achieve 100 consecutive victories to win their freedom. Lose, and their winning streak is reset, forcing them to start over from number 1. Even death is no mercy, for those who are slaughtered are soon raised again by the sadistic master of these competitions! Can the PC's satisfy the conditions to escape with their lives, or will they be forced to spill each others blood for eternity? And what will the PC's do when they find themselves changing with each competition they endure?

            A Changeling Origin story

                                    A Loyalist Campaign

                                    26) The PC's are all slaves to the same Keeper, and their master has given them a task, destroy the entire Free Hold of -city name here- so that their Keeper can lord his accomplishment over his rival Gentry. The PC's now have to figure out a way to systematically bring down a whole society of Changelings from capturing escaped slaves to slaying them for their disobedience, to spreading dissent among the ranks. If they succeed, their Keeper promises to elevate them in status among the rest of their servants. If they fail... well best not to think about that!



                                    Villainous Campaign

                                    27) The PC's have been friends for years now. They escaped from Arcadia together and have been in the same Motley for as long as they can recall. Over the years, their Wyrd has increased to extremely high levels (8-10) and they are at risk of ascending into True Fae. Before they leave behind the mundane world and become practical demigods, they decide they'll need servants to tend to them in Arcadia. They begin to target mortal and Lost alike, rounding them up for the trip through The Hedge. The PC's have decided that 10 slaves each would be an appropriate number before they make the trip. Needless to say, the Free Hold will absolutely not tolerate this! Can their enormous power keep them safe against an army of pissed off Changelings, or will numbers overwhelm them before they can carry out their plans?

                                            29) One of the PC's has been pledged in an arranged marriage to a member of the Gentry before she was even born, thanks to her grandparents. The first time her fiance tried to claim her, the trip through The Hedge warped her mind and left her disoriented and confused, she saw him as a monster and fled before they could be married. When her fiance tracks her down again in the mundane world, he explains how they'll live in a luxurious castle in the beautiful countryside with dozens of servants and she'll wear the finest of vestments every day. Sounds like every little girl's dream come true. Perhaps it would be if the betrothed didn't take the medieval knight act quite so seriously. Turns out he has some very old world views on the behavior of women, namely that they should be quiet, subservient, and are expected to produce heirs for their husbands. Oh and the whole "punish her if she doesn't comply" law, just like the ye olden times! This doesn't sit well at all with the bride to be, but can she find a loop hole and break the pledge before the day of the wedding, or will she be forced to return to Arcadia forever because of a mistake her ancestors made?

                                                            Here's one I did a long time ago:

                                                            In Denver, Colorado, there exists an old church that holds a treasure like no other within its catacombs. Known as the Eternal Casket, this legendary token has been guarded by the Court of Solstice since its discovery five years ago. But it is what sleeps inside the casket that attracts changelings to the place.

                                                            Her name is Emilia, or St. Emilia, as the Court of Inbetween has taken to calling her. Within the casket, she sleeps the dreamless sleep. Alive, but trapped in timelessness. Never aging, never waking. While in this state, the Fairest Bright One blesses all who look upon her with the most precious commodity a changeling can know: a sense of clarity.

                                                            But not all are willing to let her remain within the casket. Despite what her guardians say about her having willingly entered the glass coffin, there are those who believe them liars, and demand her release. Others say that it is through her sacrifice that the Freehold of Denver has survived for so long, creatures of the Hedge, or even Arcadia unable to near the church because of its "holy" occupant. Besides, surely the needs of the many out way the needs of the few, right?

                                                            Regardless of one's perspective, a hedgefire war has sprung around the Casket, one that is between the release, and protection, of St. Emilia.
