                    Honestly it just sounds like you've had a particularly unfortunate run of bad fanfics or possibly need to find a better venue than wherever your currently reading them. Like everything on the internet, some fanfics are terrible and some of them are great. Some of them are by the numbers cliches, and some of them are quite unique.

                    Anyway, back to the topic at hand.

                    The True Fae are like watching a movie in a language you do not understand without any translation. You can glean some information by context and observation, but there are whole portions of the whole picture that are simply impossible to understand. That's how the Fae should be (to my mind). Their actions are their own, and while some portions of their goals should be made manifest (the lady with the violent eyes seeks the moon rose that blooms once every hundred years on the site of a bloody battlefield) part of their existence should never be revealed or be entirely alien (e.g.- what does she even need with the rose? Why does she seem satisfied when the characters thwart her attempts to retrieve it?).

                        True Fae can't really be bundled under a single group, really. They're all different, even if they have similar/overarching methods and ideas. Keepers can be pleasant beings, but only so long as you agree with their pleasantness; being trapped in a gingerbread house that always feeds you can be hell to those who hate sugar, or want to go outside, for example, but heaven for those who love sweets and just want to keep eating it. The Keeper could be genuinely ignorant about your own wants/dislikes (after all, who wants to not have some of their plum pudding all the time) or cruel about it (you'd eat and eat and eat until you exploded because they want that), but the point is that they perceive what you want as being what they want, and in their eyes what goes against that is wrong or inconceivable enough to warrant them "fixing" you.

                        ...of course, there are those who subvert that too. Saying all True Fae act in the same way, outside of being alien, is underestimating them. Otherwise Changelings would have an easier time dealing with True Fae.

                        (my original post had me comparing them to people with dementia; this was in poor taste, and it was partially fueled by an ugly confrontation with my grandma who has the condition and became confused when she was here during a visit to see her family, hence the rework)
