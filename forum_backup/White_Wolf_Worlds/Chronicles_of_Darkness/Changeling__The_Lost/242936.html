    Changeling 2.0 Brainspew 2: Contracts

    08-28-2014, 06:10 AM
    Right now, we're chattering over Contract structure. The way it looks, they're going to come in four flavors:

    Universal Contracts

    This is a Contract. It has a single name. It does what it's supposed to do. It'll have a level, roughly equating to its cost in Experiences. So a level 4 "Contract of Storms" will cost four Experiences. It's level 4, because it does pretty intense things, more intense than a level 1, 2, or 3. But it's important to note that there's no 1, 2, or 3 required. We'll lay out some guidelines for roughly what each level should be able to do. Guidelines, though, since there are always fiddly bits.

    Seeming Contracts

    Seeming Contracts will be very broad Contracts that have multiple flavors, by Seeming. When you purchase one for its level in Experiences (so a level 4 costs 4), you get your Seeming's manifestation of it. For an Experience each, you can unlock other Seemings. If your Seeming isn't represented on the Contract (most will have 5, not 6), then if you want that Contract, you have to pay the extra Experience to unlock a different Seeming's manifestation when you pick it up.

    So, spitballing. This is totally brainspew I plugged out in thirty seconds to give you an idea. Don't concern yourself with the specifics.

    Contract of Strength (2 dots)
    Makes you strong!
    Ogre: You get a direct Strength boost. Bam. Favored Seeming for this one.
    Elemental: You can draw on an example of your element to use its Structure instead of your dice pool for feats of strength.
    Fairest: When someone makes you look bad, your next strength action gets their successes added to yours.
    Darkling: When taking an action when nobody sees you, add your Stealth to your Strength.
    Beast: Grow a Size. Gain a Strength. Gain a Stamina.
    Wizened: Too bad, so sad. If you want Contract of Strength, pay an extra Experience and take another Seeming's manifestation.

    Court Contracts

    These are contracts unique to one angle of the Court. So if we're doing a Court based on the maiden/mother/crone trichotomy, you would have a Maiden Contract, a Mother Contract, and a Crone Contract to go along with your Maiden Mantle, Mother Mantle, and Crone Mantle. These are like Universal Contracts, but you need an appropriate Mantle to buy them.

    Goblin Contracts

    Nobody really makes deals with goblins, do they?

                                              I'd prefer (Universal) Contracts to be layered, with multiple Clauses. Moving towards the model where a Contract does one thing and costs X amount seems way too straight forward for what are essentially deals made between the Fae and facets of reality. Since when did the Gentry become so direct?

                                              As a twist from 1st edition though, when Lost 'sign up' to a Contract they don't just get the first clause - they sign up to everything. Purchasing the Contract gives them access to every level of the power (1 to 5) but they have to be careful of the fine print, and can only safely use clauses they have 'initialed' (by purchasing with Experiences).

                                              The Contract itself lets them invoke the higher clauses but doing so comes with an increasingly steep surcharge (as they have to satisfy the Contract's penalty clauses), depending on the difference between the level they have purchased to and the level of the power used. One or two levels higher have a smallish (but still significant) cost, whereas invoking the highest levels when you don't have the right involves a huge (possibly game-changing?) cost to the character, such as having a very one-sided pledge forced upon you, or extending an invitation to your Keeper to come get you.
