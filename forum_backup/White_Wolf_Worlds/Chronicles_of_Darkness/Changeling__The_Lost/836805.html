                              Well, you can do 3 things here to acomplish that:

                              Originally posted by Modyfing Kiths
                              In addition to the option of players and Storytellers working together to create entirely new kiths, they can also modify existing ones.
                              1. Get the Dual-Kith Merit (rename it to Greater Kith, or something, the effect of the merit is the same). Get Draconic and Windwing, now let's change some of the kith's blessings:

                              2. Modify a little here:
                              Draconic (Or Greater Draconic Kith part I)
                              His thick scales transform the first Wyrd points of lethal damage he takes in a turn into bashing damage and he can do lethal damage with his unarmed attacks. He can spend a point of Glamour to add Wyrd to all Intimidation rolls for the scene. He can spend a point of Glamour to unleash a breath of fire, he rolls Dexterity + Athletics and add half his Wyrd(rounded down) to the damage modifier.

                              3. Modify a little there:
                              Windwing(Or Greater Draconic Kith part II)
                              The windwing can fly at twice his Speed for as many turns as he has dots in Stamina, but must spend a turn without a movement action before he can fly again. Spend a Glamour to increase the duration of his flight to a minute per Wyrd. During flight, he can use his Defense against Firearms. The windwing can land and perch on anything capable of holding a small child, regardless of his real weight. The windwing can spend a point of glamour and a instant action to grow to titanic proportions, add half her wyrd(rounded up) to her size for the scene.

                                              I wrote a fire-breathing kith for 1e for one of my players with the exact same wish. I've modified it for 2e; it might help if your player wants a more area-of-effect attack, rather than a single hit or the Pyrokinesis merit.

                                              Doomspitter
                                              You break into my house, steal my tokens, and expect mercy? Oh no, little worm, I'm going to make you burn.
                                              Doomspitters embodied the monsters that deal death from a distance: chimeras that spew fire, serpents that spit acid, banshees that shout pain, and, of course, dragons. Many were set to guard a castle or treasure, some were set in coliseum battles, and others were taken to fill the fairy-tale of the hero slaying the beast.
                                              Beast: A lion's head, goat's body, and snake's tail, panting flames
                                              Darkling: Wrinkled, haggard face, wrapped in a dark cloak, whose screams shatter glass
                                              Ogre: A hulking lizard-man, with lashing tail and jaws dripping fire
                                              Blessing: Doomspitters are immune to any damage, Conditions, or Tilts caused by the element they spit, such as fire, acid, or sound (chosen at character creation). Wyrd times per scene, they can spend a point of Glamour to spit that element. The Doomspitter picks a center point within 20 feet; the effect targets everyone except the Doomspitter in a close range of that point. The Doomspitter rolls Dexterity + Athletics; everyone else in the area contests with Dexterity + Athletics. Dramatic Failure: The attack goes wild, causing 2 lethal damage to the Doomspitter, or to an area of the GM's choice. Failure: The shot fizzles out in your throat, or a target dodges out of the way. No damage. Success: The target doesn't dodge, or doesn't dodge well enough: targets take 1 lethal damage for every success the Doomspitter gets above them. Exceptional Success: As a success, and the target takes an appropriate Condition (such as Sick, On Fire, Pain-Wracked, or Chilled).
