                                I was looking at my sheet for the Doctor as a True Fae, and it got me thinking. The Doctor is supposed to be a renegade among his species. So it follows that the Gentry version probably doesn't have Titles, so as to not attract the attention of other Gentry who want to enter a Feud or harm his loved ones. Because of this, he only has Wyrd 5, rather than Wyrd 5 + number of Titles. He willingly exiled himself from Arcadia and practically all Feuds, so he has different stats from a normal True Fae. He can't use Dream Warping, but he can use the other types of Oneiromancy like a changeling would. His Immortal Flesh has been transformed into the Regeneration from the show. Because of this, he is hurt by mundane weapons just like a human, and worse yet, he is still weak against cold iron and hand-forged iron. To compensate, his Empathy, Computer and Expression Skills don't have the limits of normal Gentry. He does not roll a chance die when attempting to create something new. He can harvest glamour from selfless emotions, but cannot receive glamour by breaking something or someone into the constituent parts. His Virtue and Vice work like those of a normal human, so he is not Ruled By Passion.

                                Of note is that he knows how to create Hedgespun and Tokens. He likes to craft a new and thematic Hedgespun item to carry around when he Regenerates. He is so good at Hedgespinning that he does not need a Title in Realm form to mimic the TARDIS. His fae equivalent of the TARDIS is an epic level Hollow, with Size and Wards maxed out. The Hollow TARDIS is also Mobile, with the method of transportation being Space-Time Travel. The Hollow TARDIS can enter the Hedge, but it can't teleport once it is inside the Thorns, so the Doctor has to use other modes of transportation. It can still fly inside and outside the Hedge, but its altitude and speed aren't great, and its maneuverability is bad enough to be dangerous. Its heart/central processing unit is a mostly stationary, powerful and self-aware Hedgespun Automaton who tries communicating or seeing the outside world sometimes. K9 is another outstanding example of the Doctor's aptitude with Hedgespinning. The Sonic Screwdriver is a 5 dot Token focused on versatility instead of huge dice bonuses or grandiose powers. Its Drawback is that it doesn't work on one or a few materials common to whatever general area in space-time the Doctor is currently in, most famously wood. The Catch might be that, when a non fae uses it, they end up breaking or losing an important belonging, device or other item within the scene.

                                The Daleks could be a small group of Border Reavers (Autumn Nightmares, page 122) which got so mutated that they had to build Hedgespun life-support systems. Because of their desire for revenge, they also equip themselves with extremely resistant armor and very cruel weapons. The other monsters, like the Weeping Angels, Silents and Cybermen, could be weirder-than-average hobgoblins. The Master is another Charlatan Gentry, but he is still a vicious, arrogant madman. The Companions are either Fae-Touched or straight-up changelings. There is no rule that says only Gentry with Realms can turn humans into changelings. Nergal (Autumn Nightmares, page 79) has no Realm, but still turned a mortal into Grandfather Thunder (1ed Core, page 338). The Doctor's Seeming is Fairest, but since he is not a changeling, it's only used for designing his Mien and for determining the benefits of 2ed Contracts. His kiths are Maker, Smith and Telluric.

                                EDIT: Then again, the Doctor supposedly stole his TARDIS, which is an obsolete Type 40. This makes me think the main engine/consciousness that powers his Hollow’s space-time travel is a severed and abandoned Title in Prop form. To be frank, the TARDIS could be a lot of things.

                                EDIT 2: nothing wrong with making the TARDIS a Title in Realm form and the Doctor himself an Actor. That could attract the attention of other True Fae more often. However, the Sonic Screwdriver is not a Prop, since Props are supposed to perform their tasks perfectly.

                                            As for the Devil Fruit, they could have been left behind by a Gentry who achieved Wyrd Transcendence. I'd say the Devil Fruit don't turn people into changelings: they are either acquired by Gentry somehow and a captive mortal eats it, or a mortal finds one in the Hedge and the resulting Contract is only activated after he is kidnapped and turned into a changeling. It is likely that eating or just possessing a Devil Fruit makes you a special target for Gentry and Huntsmen. In either case, the first Frailty the changeling develops is somehow themed after water or the sea. Given that one character cannot have multiple major templates in NWOD, the Devil Fruit only give their powers and curses to those of mortal or fae blood, excluding the Gentry. Tony Tony Chopper is a good example: he was a Hedge Beast or some other hobgoblin who had the luck of eating just the right Devil Fruit. Should a werewolf try to eat one of them, all that will happen is that he will spit out the chunk he bit and wash his tongue. I'd say the Devil Fruit even regenerate until eaten by a mortal or half-fae, and send out waves of Glamour that guarantee they will always be found.

                                            Lastly, your suggestion of what being a Pirate King means to Luffy is suitably long term and epic.
