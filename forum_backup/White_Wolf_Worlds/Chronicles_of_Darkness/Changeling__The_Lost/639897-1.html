    On the anime/music front, I recommend the original .hack//sign anime (trailer), along with Yuki Kaijura's soundtrack for the show. Its set in an immersive VR massive multiplayer online RPG, but plays with many of the same themes as Lost. Running from an abusive life, the player Tsukasa turned to fantasy as a temporary escape. Then SHE spoke, and a pact was struck. And now Tsukasa can no longer feel his VR wraps, or log out. The game world is now his home . . . whether he wants to stay or not. . .

    The anime itself was crippled by an extensive focus on the "normal" players - but that could work for a Changeling campaign, particularly if you want ideas for what it would be like for "normal" humans at risk of getting dragged into the affairs of changelings.

    As for the soundtrack - I don't think there's an official video or authorized uploads online. But if you search for them, they're there, and work really. Yuki Kaijura used Latin and Spanish music, slowed way down, to create very haunting vocals. In particular, look for "In the Land of Twilight, Underneath the Moon", "Key of Twilight", and "Aura".

    In a radically different direction - If you're looking at a crossover with the God-Machine (or maybe Promethean), I highly recommend Orgy's Fiction (Dreams in Digital). Or, for more of a gritty/street game, Welcome to the Jungle (by Guns'n'Roses) is a good choice..

    Originally posted by Madfox11 View Post
    Curious about movies like Labyrinth, granted it is obvious, but it fits so perfectly with Changeling even if it has a happy ending and is somewhat lighthearted for WoD...
    Labyrinth (trailer) works well for both Changeling games (both Lost and Dreaming), as does Coraline (trailer). The otherwise forgettable Mirrormask (trailer) has a great scene for Lost, where the girl finds a way to spy on her Fetch, and starts yelling at her to stop kissing a particular boy, because "he's horrible".

                As yesterday started next season, I point you to...

                Once Upon a Time

                Epic story of characters from fairy tales that come to human world and try to make their both halves fairy and mundane life work together - by founding their new identity after them. The series revolves on themes of good and bad ( or Virtue and Vice in WoD terms ), heroes and villains, and in the end how the lines separating them are very thin. Almost all of characters would be of Fairest or Grimm 2ED Seemings - because even the "wicked" are "most perfect of the all". It's really a superb modern fairy tale and great source for Changeling's ideas. And you can - literally - be ripped of your heart!
