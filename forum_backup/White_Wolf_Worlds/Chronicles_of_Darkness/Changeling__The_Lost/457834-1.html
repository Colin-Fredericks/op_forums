        I will always recommend Queen II for all things faerie. Despite the picture, this is not where Bohemian Rhapsody comes from. If the titles can't convince you, the music might:

        Procession - the Gentry striding thru the Hedge
        Father to Son - what sends the soon-to-be-victim out into the night
        White Queen - the capture
        Some Day One Day - the lament of the parent
        The Loser In The End - the conviction of the Gentry
        Ogre Battle - the reality of the Durance
        Fairy-Feller's Master Stroke - the madness of the Gentry
        Nevermore - the lament of the Changeling
        March of the Black Queen - the escape as the Gentry pursues
        Funny How Love Is - the shining beacon thru the Hedge
        Seve Seas of Rhye - the triumphant return home
