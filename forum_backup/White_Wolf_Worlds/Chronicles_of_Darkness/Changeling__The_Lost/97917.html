    Balancing the kiths: Thoughts

    03-24-2014, 08:50 PM
    So, we all know that the kiths, as written, are about as well-balanced as a drunken acrobat on a high line in a hurricane with severe mommy issues. I'm thinking it might be a good idea to work up some houserules to adjust the most egregious examples.

    To that end, what do folks see as the most overpowered/underpowered Kith examples?

    I know that the Shadowsoul blessing is just out of line overpowered. On the other end of the line, I've always felt like Manikins get the short end of the stick.

      Eh, I've never really felt too fussed about the strengths and weaknesses of many of the Kiths. They're not really a big influence on the game.

      That said, an Elemental Dual Kith Gargantuan is stacking absurd amounts of health.

      Stonebones versus Earthbones has always struck me as a bit funny. Indeed, many of the Elemental Kiths in the core book aren't terribly impressive, but I guess they counter that by having a stellar Blessing.

      On a slightly unreleated note, Beasts have hands down the worst Wyrd Evolutions in Equinox Road. Your reward for hitting high Wyrd is a new Specialty in Animal Ken? Really? Even if you didn't have rather nasty new Curses to cope with, that would be pretty laughable when paired off against things like the Elemental boosts.

      There are a lot, and I mean a lot, of Kiths out there. Most of them seem underpowered, to be honest.

      Is the Shadowsoul Kith the one that gives the option of a free point of Defence even against shooting? Because that always struck me as pretty comparable to Bright One.

              I have two Kiths I changed in my Houserule Document. I am gonna copy it with my note on Elementals so it makes sense:

              Elementals:

              Curse: The Elemental Curse does not apply to those Attributes or Skills when used with with Elemental Affinity contracts, but do apply to non Affinity Contracts and Court Contracts as usual.

              Reason: The Elemental Curse is harsh, and it makes sense it would apply less when addressing natural elements.

              Manikin Kith: Manikins also get Contracts of Animation at x5 as well as Artifice, and Animation and Artifice Count as Elemental Affinity Contracts for purposes of the weakness above.

              Reason: See above, plus the Manikin Kith could probably use a second contract anyway in case Artifice is not desired, since a Manikin with Craft 1 and no Artifice effectively has no power.


              Fairest

              Shadowsoul Kith: Shadowsouls do not get Darkness as an Affinity Contract.

              Reason: Have you read it their blessing? Jesus. Also, Contracts of Darkness have very little to with Shadowsouls MO. It feels tacked on.

              Autumn Court:

              Mantle: The Mantle 1 ability for Autumn Court not only applies to any Contract using Occult, but also Low Magic (from Second Sight) or Rites of Things that Must Not Be (also Second Sight) or any other supernatural effect the Autumn Courtier is using Occult in the roll for, such as Exorcism.


              Also, I examined Minstrel Kith as overpowered, but given the limitations I decided to not nerf it.

                                                  Originally posted by ArcadianAwakened View Post
                                                  At least the way I would run things, a specialty in bladed weapons is too broad to have from character creation and I'd insist they pick knives or swords or something to that tune.
                                                  Even so, it is significantly less potent than pretty much any other combat based Kith. Though Razorhands depends a bit on whether or not you let the specialty they get apply to the weapons they can summon for themselves (as written that Kith is a bit messy).

                                                  but it's not terrible, and I feel like a wizened is more likely to come out of arcadia with more experience weilding blades than guns or clubs (the latter seems more fitting for ogres). draconics… are kind of overpowered, they probably don't need the option to reroll attacks until wyrd evolution.
                                                  Obviously there are different preferences for how potent a Blessing should be (I think Draconics are fairly close to what I think is a good level for combat focused Kith; though I don't like the thematic nature of their Blessing in the slightest). However a specialty is particularly weak on the grounds that it isn't something you couldn't just buy. An Oracle can buy Common Sense, but that at least gives them access to those mechanics twice as often. A Hunterheart that takes Lethal Mien gets a damage bonus.

                                                  Even if you consider Blades too broad for a Specialty, to benefit from that broadness your character actually needs to use multiple types of blades that would fall under different Specialties otherwise. A character that's a trained swordsman and gets kidnapped to Arcadia to serve in a Fae army... comes back with a slightly broader specialty in Weaponry. Most Kiths at least have some benefit for stacking Blessings with their mundane counterparts.

                                                  As for the "Arcadian soldiers = blades" thing, that is a much narrower view of Arcadia than the books present. Arcadia is not CtD's Dreaming that's stuck in perpetual quasi-Medieval fantasy. A Durance could reflect lots of different cultural concepts of being a soldier including ancient cultures that did not use blades as their most symbolic weapons. It could reflect a modern or sci-fi military. However the Soldier Kith does not facilitate any of those.

                                                  In the RPG.net thread I mentioned, I actually suggested radically altering the Kith's Blessing to reflect a more "Wizended" approach to combat. Essentially, something close to True Friend or Playmate with a combat focus. The Wizened Soldier isn't a better fighter, they're a better squad-mate. Which covers any sort of Soldier concept, is something that can easily be done that can synergize with mundane teamwork/group fighting Merits rather than not, and has some meaningful mechanical weight behind it.

                                                  I've never really felt like the water-themed kiths have all that much going for them, they're useful enough when you need to go swimming but large bodies of water never really come up in my games, and I have trouble thinking of chronicles where they'd be consistently useful unless it was written with the "well we need to get aquaman in there somehow" mentality.
                                                  This is true, but if water is a meaningful part of the game, those Kiths have a very nice bonus.

                                                  That said, something like 80% of humans live near a major body of water (half of the total pop. on coastal alone)... so it isn't that much of a strain to make water relevant to a game. The vast majority of major cities are coastal, on a major river, or on a major lake.

                                                  I think a bigger problem is the nWOD completely lacks decent swimming rules. It's hard to make water-based Kiths mechanically relevant when there's no base-line rules to compare them to.
