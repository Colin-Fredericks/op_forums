    Conqueror of the Universe, Frieza (Dragon Ball Z)


    Name: Lord Frieza. A tyrannical alien who invades and conquers inhabited planets either to auction them off or keep them for himself.


    Actor:The Father (King Cold). Spoils Lord Frieza by giving him nearly anything he wants, but is mercilessly cruel with anybody else.


    Actor: The Son (Kuriza). Heir to Lord Frieza's empire, his head is vaguely shaped like a chestnut.


    Wisps: The Soldiers (Various alien races enslaved by Frieza). Regarded as expendable, Lord Frieza does not hesitate to kill if they get in his line of fire.


    Wisps: The Ginyu Force. An immensely strong pack lead by the body snatching Captain Ginyu. They have a reputation for performing choreography before and during battle.


    Realm: The Frieza Empire. A galaxy spanning empire with more than 100 planets conquered by Frieza alone!


    Prop: The Mothership. A circular shaped craft which Frieza travels the cosmos in. It is capable of carrying over a thousand Soldiers inside!

                                            Name: Darkness and Light

                                            Domain: Final Fantasy

                                            Actor: Final Boss. Monolythic in size and godlike in power, it's body is an almagamation of religious symbolism. This Actor only appears when Darkness or Light begins to overwhelm the other and is believed to be the avatar of the stronger Fae.


                                            Actor: The Four Crystals. Governing the elements of Fire, Water, Wind and Earth, the crystals maintain balance and order within the realm. In times of great crisis when Darkness or Light become too powerful, the crystals select four warriors to journey forth and restore the peace.


                                            Wisp: Judges. The local law enforcement, they are tasked with keeping the citizens of Ivalice under control.


                                            Realm: Ivalice. A vast country home to a multitude of different races. Its culture is unique for combining medieval fantasy and science fiction in the same era.


                                            Prop: Magicite. The magic of Arcadia transformed into a crystaline substance, Magicite can be used as an alternative power source for machinery, granting them a nearly infinite supply of clean running energy!

                                            ------------------------

                                            Details: The domain of Final Fantasy is home to two Gentry who share it with each other, though not by their own choice. Darkness and Light are in a never ending war for dominance, one side representing evil and the other representing good. As long as they remain in a stalemate, the domain has some resemblance of order to it. But should either side become powerful enough to win the war, they inadvertently begin to destroy the domain they built together. In order to avoid the death of their realm and continue their mutual survival, they created the Four Crystals to serve as arbiters in the war. If they detect one side is beginning to overpower the other, they select four warriors to aid the weakened Gentry in restoring the balance.

                                            It is unclear whether these warriors are another manifestation of the Fae or if they are Changelings blessed with enormous power, but by working together, they venture through the realm, defeating scores of monsters and finally confronting the avatar of Light or Darkness, defeating them in battle and bringing peace to the realm. Once both sides have recovered from their losses, the war is back on!

                                            The Hedge which surrounds and secures Final Fantasy takes the shape of a thick mist that emanates from tunnels and fissures beneath the ground. Those who venture inside the mist draw the attention of monstrous Hedge creatures that attack on sight. Thus, most inhabitants of Ivalice avoid the mist, unless they're attempting to escape, of course.

                                                    The Hellraiser Cenobites (from the first film, the second, and the comic series) work very well

                                                    True Fae: Leviathan
                                                    Actors 1-4: Pinhead, the Female, Chatterer, and Butterball
                                                    Prop: the Lament Configuration
                                                    Realm: The Monastery of the Cenobites


                                                    Spoilers below this point
                                                    Each of the Actors is a title wrapped around a former Changeling that never actually escaped from the clutches of Leviathan; each one has forgotten their origin, and surrendered to the title that they act as the base for. They also wield emotional powers similar to or based on the Courts of Spring (Desire), Fall (Fear), the South (Ecstasy) and the North (Suffering), which are the emotions they tend to feed on. Each also has the Mantle benefits of the North Mantle, though their Meins take on unique alterations rather than the usual ones of the North Court.
