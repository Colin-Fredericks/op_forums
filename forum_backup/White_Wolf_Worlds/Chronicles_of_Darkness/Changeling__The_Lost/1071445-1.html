                                        The default Contracts have always been made by the Gentry, going back to 1e core. It wasn't until Equinox Road that non-Gentry ever struck any... So, Arcadian Contracts is pretty much just a formalization of that old idea. So, Arcadian Contracts are by the True Fae (and other such arcadian denizens), the Goblin Contracts by the Hedge, and Court Contracts by the fae of Ironside. Seems pretty logical.

                                        Sword for strength, Shield for defense, Crown for leadership, Jewels for manipulation and temptation, Mirror for perception and self-transformation, and Steed for movement.
                                        Ogres being swords, Fairest being crowns, Beasts being Steed does seem fairly obvious - not only the abilities but the names are pretty clear links. The other three aren't as clear - I'm thinking that Jewels will be Darkling (subtlty and manipulation of senses for invisibility), Mirror for Elemental (self-transformation into an element is a thing, after all) and that would leave Shield for Wizened. Not really sure how the wizened themes of artifice is really lending to "defense" but then, I wouldn't put perception and self-transformation, strength, leadership, temptation nor movement as their affinity either. Honestly, "Defense" is a hard one, since the closest thing to defense contracts are Separation / phantom glory / red rage of revenge. And those are traditionally Fairest / Ogre abilties. Or Elemental, with Cloak of the Elements. Not really wizened. I wonder how that's going to work.


                                        I'm not really sure how I feel about these affinities being so different from seeming-themed feels. 1e Fang and Talon, for instance, has self-transformation, movement, and leading animals at least. That's three Regalia at least. Contracts of Darkness had... well, that was always an odd grab bag of fear manipulation and movement. I feel like we're stepping away from having anything related to Seemings here. I mean, I found it pretty awesome that Boon of the Scuttling Spider now had a Beast clause for making webbing. That doesn't feel like a thing as much.

                                        I mean, it makes sense. If the vast majority of Contracts originated without Changelings or their Seemings, then why should Seemings get benefits? And the Regalia themes do tie into the existing Seemings' Escapes. Fairest lead others. Thus, crown. Ogres relied on strength (sword). Beasts ran away (steed). Darklings used trickery and underhanded tactics (jewels). Elementals transformed into raw elements and escaped that way (mirror). And Wizened protected the core of their humanity by building a robot body around it (shield). Well, not necessarily a robot, since it could be non-robot parts, but you get the idea. Built a shield around their heart, "the choice to devote herself to the thing that brings safety, comfort, and praise." So, all these new Regilia logically flow from the Seemings and how they escape. It all makes sense that way.

                                        However, it feels a step removed from what it used to be. I think we're going to be a bit more difficult filling in old concepts with a new way to have these contracts work. Just because you're an elemental doesn't mean you'll have an affinity for everything raw elements anymore. Wizened won't automatically be artificers. And so on. Not sure how I feel about it at the moment.
