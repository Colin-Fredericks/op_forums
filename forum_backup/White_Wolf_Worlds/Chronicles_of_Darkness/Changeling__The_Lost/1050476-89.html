                                                        Originally posted by Shadowdragon View Post

The Merit doesn't say you take a base item and add bonuses to it. Enhanced Item from Mage specifically says you take an item and increase its existing stats. Hedgespun Item seems more like you create an item from scratch that just mimics a regular item, or may have been a mundane item once but gets transformed into something else.
                                                        Sure it does: "Extraordinary Equipment: an appropriate +1equipment bonus, armor rating, or weapon damage modifier."

                                                        If you have a 2L sword, an extraordinary equipment sword would go up to 3L, 4L, or 5L depending on your merit rating. It's kind of how 'Improved Alacricy' gives you +2 to speed, without assuming your speed is zero and now you have 2 or 4 or 6 speed if you are wearing the item.

                                                        EDIT:
                                                        As a counterpoint: why would anyone pay 3 xp for a +3 equipment bonus Hedgespun dress, when you can just get mundane equipment that does exactly the same without any xp investment? If it doesn't add on top, there's a lot of cases where you either pay XP to get a worse version of a mundane item or one that's the same.

                                                        Now, what I would have liked the merit to say is whether +1 to armor rating is +1/+1, or +1 in either general or ballistic armor. I've seen people arguing both cases (because of the Hedgespinning shift armor), but also, getting +3 to general armor is just really useful. Iron making it evaporate isn't really a big deterrant from getting it, since Banes ignore all armor anyway, so wearing a kevlar vest or fullplate doesn't even matter anyway if someone comes at you with Cold Iron.
