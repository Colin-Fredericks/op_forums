    Hedge Colonies

    08-11-2015, 12:45 PM
    I was reading American history a few days ago and I noticed a lot of stuff about how our forefathers claimed the wilderness for themselves. The early colonists had to deal with dangerous weather, food and water supplies, and the occasional Indian war. This inspired the idea of the Hedge Colony, a vast Hollow formed by changelings either brave enough (or stupid enough) to form their own little state/nation right in the Hedge. One has to stop treating the Hedge as a barrier dimension and more like the American wilderness: free space for the taking, but dangerous to grab hold of. So what do you guys think of my idea? I mean I would have to figure out some kind of mechanic for this concept, but I'll do that later.

              I don't think Mirrors is necessary for rules about the Hedge, as the rules for the Hedge are already in existence and are far closer to the flavour of untamed wilderness than woundgates. A freehold in the Hedge needs to have a profound defense against Fae/Huntsmen/Goblins, as well as against madness. There are a couple canonical examples, one ending in mass fae invasion, the other from rapid clarity decline among the citizens. In your pioneer example, the Fae/Huntsmen would be hostile natives and enemy nations. The madness part would be the diseases faced. Protection from Fae would be relatively straightforward, keeping the Bulwark up and never letting it slip, ensuring your freehold's approaches include the human world (so that theyre safer in the Hedge). Sadly, to keep sane, the colonists need human contact and human normalcy. This part would probably be the hardest, and be part of the ongoing game and story arcs. its a mad world theyre living in.

I remember a few years ago reading about how a Southeast Asian nation was hunting down these metal spikes left behind by a former enemy to defile their land. It gave me the idea of using some kind of Cold Iron spikes driven into the ground to kill off/stave the Hedge's influence on mortal minds. There is still a danger to Morality/Integrity just from being in the psycho-active Hedge, but the idea of free land up for the taking is just too good to resist for the courageous settler or changeling.
