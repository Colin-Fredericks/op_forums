    So we all know that given the proper set up, practically any other genre can be converted into a working Changeling: The Lost scenario. What kind of ideas do you guys think would make a suitable cross over?



    Attack on Gentry: Attack on Titan parody


    Premise A - An army of Changelings have rebelled against their Keeper and are making a stand against his servants while desperately trying to make their way to the Hedge's exit.

    Premise B - One of the Gentry has a macro fetish and decides to amuse himself by destroying a city and devouring its residents. Having amassed hundreds of Changelings over the centuries, he sets his loyal followers loose upon the city one day and watches the carnage unfold.


    Titan Shifters - Ogre Seeming


    Military Police and the Nobility - Fairest Seeming



    Recon Corp. and Garrison - Wizened Seeming and Beast Seemings



    Titan Fodder and Civilians - Darkling and Wizened Seeming



    The Walls - The Hedge


    Retaking the Walls - Escaping the Hedge


    Common and Abnormal Titans - The Gentry


    Colossal Titan - Lord of the Domain

                  Well the basic premise is this:

                  100 years ago, Titans appeared in the world and started devouring humans for no explainable reason. They can survive entirely on sunlight, so there's no biological need for them to eat humans. Over the century, humans were driven to the brink of extinction and now only one major city remains standing, protected by 3 walls spanning many miles in diameter so that humans have room to make villages and settlements. Humans have also devolved into a victorian steam punk era of technology having to rely on gas canisters, flintlock rifles, and black powder cannons to fend off the Titans. The walls are 50 meters tall, while most Titans don't reach higher than 20, so they've been safe. One day, a 60 meter Titan shows up at the gate, kicks that shit down, and vanishes before Titans storm through the first wall, massacring the people inside. Three years later, he shows up at the second gate to wreck their shit, but they manage to plug up the hole in time. The anime from then on is about them trying to retake the first wall from the invading Titans and plug up the hole.


                  The series is still ongoing, but that's the plot for beginners.

                      Other candidates


                      American Mcgee's Alice: The game is quite literally about madness and dreams and provides a perfect example of how the Gentry aren't the playful, compassionate Fairies you read about!


                      Silent Hill: While I've never played Silent Hill, I believe that the core premise of the game is that the town is formed out of the dreams and subconscious of the story's protagonist and follows their road to recovery as they confront their inner demons. Could be a potential example of dealing with Clarity.


                      Spyro the Dragon (original trilogy): The games feature constantly traveling to different realms via portals and encountering beings who are being tormented by more powerful creatures for their simple amusement. The premise might focus on journeying to various Keeper realms and helping Lost escape their grasp.


                      Legend of Zelda: With the Dungeon Masters as Keepers, Ganon as the domain's lord, and using Link and the Sages as the PC's, I think the Zelda series could work very well for a story about fighting back against the Gentry and liberating the realm from their control. Many events in the Zelda franchise are also quite morbid or Lost material if you think about it.
