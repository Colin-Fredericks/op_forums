                I would focus the merits on "improving" the natural changeling condition - so, give strange boosts to collecting glamour, incite bedlam, navigate the hedge, picking goblin fruits, oneiromancy.

                Example:

                Blend et coagula 00
                Requires: 4 Coke, 3 Burger

                As the Burger court teaches, it is possible to take a symbol, or a dream, and make it real.
                On the other hand, the Coke court teaches that it is possible to distill quite everything - relation, things, people in their essential meaning.
                Blending the two concepts, wise changeling of the Yellow Smile may take out the platonic concept of objects themselves to bring them in dreams (and vice versa).

                A changeling may distill the nature of a single physical object, destroying it in the process - nothing have to remain.
                The object has to be mundane, so tokens, goblin fruits and similar things can not be used.

                During dreams, the changeling may "call on" the object, summoning it on her side. It is possible to use such object in order to have a +2 equipment bonus on all the suitable rolls (Narrators are advised to allow "wide" interpretations for the use of the object. For example, a spear may be uset not only to combat, but to jump, open doors, with a flag on it to improve morale, etc...)
                On the other hand, a changeling may destroy the object in the dream - in this way, the physical item will appear, unmarred, at her side once she awakes.

                Each time you destroy the object (in the physical or dream world) roll Resolve + Occult.
                If you fail the roll, the item is forever lost.

                                Originally posted by Baroqo View Post


                                Tatterdemalion, your idea is really nice - it reminds me a lot the old legend about the tithe to hell.
                                Would you mind to share the notes? Maybe we can flesh it out a little bit more, if you like.
                                I'll see if I can track them down. I've had a few HDD wipes since I last tinkered with the idea, but I may have it backed up. The Tithe to Hell was definitely in mind with that; a darker undertone regarding rumored (or true) tendencies of those Courts to tithe one soul to the Gentry every appropriate time to maintain their safety or as a mortal misunderstanding that they as a whole were marked as the Tithe to Hell and in turn become Changelings if they get back out. Less "The Fae give something to hell" and more "nope, you were marked by something you did, or who you were; you just got taken there before you died because of how interested Hell was in you." I wanted some wiggle-room where some Changelings actually believed they -did- die with enough that it could well be possible without any actual system shifts for these Courts. Its not like ghosts aren't materialized, and can't get twisted into something else via the Hedge or Arcadia... so how many of these Changelings all 6th Sense-like didn't realize they were dead until they entered "limbo" and "hell"?

                                Last I remember I was working on a terminology shift using the Qlipoth as a start (I was wanting to intergrate other cultural elements, so it wasn't too directly related to Abrahamic faiths, but it was the initial stepping off point) right down to how they referred to Seemings and other elements, as well as a very distinct outlook on what Arcadia -really- was. Which, of course, was Hell itself to these folks. Guilt and regret complexes would play heavily as part of the trauma these Court members would be contending with.

                                It could be a window-dressing change for a variant Changeling as a whole, or easily just the response a group that sprung up from a particular set of Gentry and regions of Arcadia who didn't really get exposure (or buy into) the meta-terms of Changeling. The Thorns, a Limbo of sorts, filled with mutilated and twisted lost souls (Hobgoblins) and wandering demons (Hobgoblins again). A lot of Dante's elements as well without a direct port over, but the City of Dis? Many of the layers of hell? Lots of ways to spin these into realms of Arcadia. Nevermind once you start using demonlogy in the context of Titles for Gentry and play around with options for one-sided Pledges and more...

                                Something of a mish mash from various sources, as well as a bit more focus on these courts regarding the relevance of Fetches. Some spinning mortal of mythology regarding all this to fit their own experiences more so it was more two way rather than just Changelings aping mortal outlooks, art and religion alone. I wanted to get these rolling for the game I'm running right now, but it just didn't pan out time wise. I wanted to have them around to present a differing option or viewpoint on the world Changelings lived in, even alongside a more traditional Seasonal Court. Some heavy friction due to in some instances vastly differing outlooks and treatments of things, even if the mechanics underlaying it all were effectively untouched outside some general option usage from later supplements.

                                It's still something I'd like to play around with sometime, time allowing; we have D;tD, I loved D:tF, we have Inferno and all sorts of things to use for the Infernal in nWoD, but this side-jaunt always stuck with me as having a fair bit of potential even as a set of Courts alone. I didn't want to have to use anything really outside of Changeling itself, leaving the other things optional considerations for those wanting to interconnect things more. There are some overlaps with existing courts, but, eh. That's already there with the sheer amount of material the Seasonal Courts have, so it isn't really an issue unless one makes it into one. That said; using Inferno Vestments as inspiriation (definitely not a direct port over) for the Courts of Vice/Sin Contracts? Definitely some extra mileage to be gained from there.

                                    Some random ideas about what the changelings may believe:

                                    Fetches in Dante's Divina Commedia it is said that souls are, after the Judgement (i believe), given a new phisical body. Maybe changeling believe themselves to be the souls of their own body, and Fetches their soul-less, previous self? In this case, the legend for which "you can merge to your fetch" would ring awesomely.

                                    The tithe to hell Maybe there is a "pact" in the freehold for which, every seven years, every court has to bring a particularly "virtuous (or damned)" individual to make some kind of gate of hell (i.e. a road to Arcadia) close, for other seven years. The Court which bring the most "delicious" gift is given some kind of boon.
