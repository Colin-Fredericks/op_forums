                                            So i am writing up a Changeling the Lost second edition setting, set in the small city of Harstad, Norway and the surrounding area. For that setting, I thought it would be cool to design two courts to serve as the locally established freehold. The current idea that I have are for the two courts to be as follows:

                                            The Court of Love: This court unlike spring doesn't focus on the desires surrounding love, but more on the attachment itself. Love for them is everything from romantic love to the love of a good workout or a family member. Their way of coping with their durance is focusing on building relationships and attachments to things and people in their life. They also are very much culturally inclined in fostering love that other people are feeling((or should feel in their opinion)). This can make some of them really annoying matchmakers that would even make Aphrodite groan, or they can almost like be a traveling band of cheerleaders who actively help people become friends or pursue their dreams.

                                            The Court of Hate: The Court of Hate on the other hand have little interest in "coping" or "moving on" from their durance. They want revenge first and foremost, they aren't happy with just protecting their freehold or sending out armies to fight against the Gentry in the Hedge. Hate is cold and slow compared to the immediate explosion that is wrath, the members of this court want to hit the Gentry where it hurts. So in a lot of ways the members of this court act more as spies, saboteurs and schemers as opposed to being warriors. Their hate is everlasting and their emnity will never die. This hate even extends to other enemies of their court.

                                            So what i wanted some help with was threefold:

                                            1. Help with Designing their Mantels

                                            2. Find out how these two courts share/transition their power between the two of them

                                            3. Come up with Court Contracts

                                            Ideas for helping to develop these two courts is also helpful, i have been considering adding in a third court to represent the middle so to speak: The Court of Indifference...But i wanted you guyses take on that before proceeding .

                                                These guys are mostly NPCs, unless you're in a really strange game, but I'm rather proud of this, so here it is

                                                Freehold of the Crashing Waves
                                                A mobile freehold, acting as privateers, coming in like a storm, snatching changelings, tokens, esoteric valuables, symbolically powerful mortals, and even other supernatural beings. They divide power like any other freehold, with each court fulfilling a specific function.

                                                Court of Sea
                                                Signature Emotion: Curiosity
                                                The Sailors are the eyes of the privateers, scouting and searching for their next base of operation. They govern the freehold's movements, guiding them past the snares and dangers of the hedge, leading them to their next base camp.
                                                Harvest: they get a glamour when they let their curiosity get the better of them.
                                                Mien: they appear weathered by travel, and smell of sea spray
                                                Patrons: their patron is Sea, specifically Sea as a patron of exploration
                                                Ascension to Rulership: when the time comes to move on, an old tricorn hat is placed on the kings head, and he leads them on their way.
                                                Give and Take:
                                                Mantle:
                                                *Ignore the Goblin Debt Cost of the Mantle Mask Contract.
                                                **Become inured a Clarity trigger
                                                ***Gain +2 to all rolls made to navigate the hedge
                                                ****Become inured to a second Clarity trigger
                                                *****Reroll a failed Hedge navigation check

                                                Court of Sand
                                                Signature Emotion: Satisfaction
                                                The Dealers are the rulers of the freehold at rest, setting up both their real world and hedge accommodations while they set up their temporary bases. They also are in charge of selling the freehold's stolen goods and loaning them out as mercenary fighters.
                                                Harvest: whenever you make someone happy with a deal that ultimately hurts them, gain a glamour
                                                Mien: they track phantom sand behind them, and the sound of clinking coins follow them.
                                                Patrons: they are patroned by Market itself, not any specific market, but the idea of them.
                                                Ascension to Rulership: when the freehold strikes semi-permanent camp, an ornate coat alights on the rulers shoulders.
                                                Give and Take:
                                                Mantle:
                                                *Ignore the Goblin Debt Cost of the Mantle Mask Contract.
                                                **Become inured a Clarity trigger
                                                ***Gain the Market Sense and Brownie's Boon merits
                                                ****Become inured to a second Clarity trigger
                                                *****Gain 3 dots of the fast talking style


                                                Court of Sabres
                                                Signature Emotion: Terror
                                                The Buccaneers take over in battle, warriors and thieves, they endeavor to either attack with no warning whatsoever, or to inspire pure terror in their enemies beforehand. They are the most wild of the courts and have the highest turnover rate as well.
                                                Harvest: when someone refuses to challenge you because they fear you, gain a glamour
                                                Mien: they grow tattoos as they grow in power, their scars also stand out in sharper relief.
                                                Patrons: They are patroned by Terror, who grows fat on the fear they inspire
                                                Ascension to Rulership:
                                                Give and Take:
                                                Mantle:
                                                *Ignore the Goblin Debt Cost of the Mantle Mask Contract.
                                                **Become inured a Clarity trigger
                                                ***Gain 9-again on intimidate checks
                                                ****Become inured to a second Clarity trigger
                                                *****Gain 3 dots of the fighting style merit of your choice
