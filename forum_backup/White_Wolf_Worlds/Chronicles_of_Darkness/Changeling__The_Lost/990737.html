    Post Your Durance!

    10-20-2016, 09:38 PM
    The Durance is one of the coolest parts of CtL, but it's something that sometimes people have trouble with, so why not have a thread where we can post Durances, either ones our characters have or just ideas?

    Brittany Martin always loved fantasy. Fantasy novels, Dungeons and Dragons, fairy tales, anything else like that. Her favorite pastime was to walk in the woods behind her house and dream she was in a faraway land, on some adventure. But as the years wore on, she had to realize that the world had little time for fantasies. So she found herself walking in the woods once again on her seventeenth birthday, desperate to escape from the college application process, out for one final burst of fantasy. And that was when the Lady approached her, riding out of the forest on her noble steed. She offered Brittany adventures beyond her wildest dreams in a faraway land, the chance to become a hero. She's lost things from her memory, but she can never forget that she went willingly, no matter how much she wishes she could.

    Beautiful as the Lady's domain was, Brittany wasn't particularly prepared to be a knight. Playing D&D doesn't prepare you for having all the bones in your leg smashed by the troll you were sent to hunt. But she just barely triumphed, and managed to limp back from her first "quest" to have the bones painfully regrown by a "healing potion". Quest after quest passed, and each time Brittany nearly died but managed to triumph and drag herself back. She gradually improved, learning swordplay and riding and growing stronger and faster. It was painful and dangerous, but she was living out her fantasies, wasn't she? Who cares if she spent an entire month in agony while her skin and organs grew back after being immolated in the death spasms of the first dragon she killed? And killing dragons got easier. And that was enough to make her ignore the peasants laboring to death in the Lady's fields or the chained servants doing her bidding. Speaking of ignoring, while the Lady lavished praise and attention on her at first, she became more and more distant. She lost interest in Brittany. She found more knights. Soon, her quests were delivered by messengers and not the Lady herself.

    The quests got easier, though. Not just because she got better, but because the Lady found new jobs for her. Catching runaways, keeping her gladiators in line, putting down peasant revolts...by that time, she was so absorbed into her role that she didn't question it. That was, until she saw the old man. She hadn't seen the Lady for months. No dragons has attacked the kingdom lately. It was just a routine job: hunt down a "traitor to the kingdom". So she rode out after the escaped servant, chasing him long and hard until she finally came to the wall of thorns around the kingdom. She dismounted her house at the edge of a vast lake and approached him, and he simply knelt down before her and waited. She raised her sword, and looked into his eyes, and that was when it all came down. The sight of the man's wizened, desperate face finally broke through the years of conditioning. She looked away, to the lake, and the noble knight saw the features of a dragon upon her face: scales, a forked tongue, and curling horns. She ran for the thorns, pushing through them, the pain no obstacle as she made her escape.

    Now, she works as a Summer Court mercenary. The fervor with which she attacks the Gentry and her servants is the only thing she's found that gives her a reprieve from the guilt.

    Brittany "Britomart" Martin is a tall woman with an athletic build, slicked-back bleached blonde hair, and perfectly bronzed skin, though she tends to carry herself in a rather nervous, edgy manner. In her Mien, she has black, pointed nails, reptilian pupils, a forked tongue, and spiraling horns coming from her head. When she Embraces the Wyrd, her horns grow longer, her teeth turn into black fangs, and her nails lengthen to slightly curved claws. A reptilian tail grows from her back, and wings of flame and ash ignite on her shoulders. Her clothing is replaced with armor that looks as if it was once gleaming gold, but is now rusted and webbed with numerous cracks from which steam rises.

      Lionheart was stolen out of reality to fight in an endless medieval fantasy war for a cruel Keeper's amusement, with two armies made of enslaved hobs and kidnapped Lost eternally clashing and dying in the muck of a battlefield of a forgotten legend. He realized that this conflict would never end, that more people would be taken in order to fight and die without purpose, and instead rallied both armies under his banner; if they would die here, then let it be in a glorious, suicidal charge against their tormentor, rather than pointlessly killing each other. Deals were made, oaths were sworn, final speeches given, and as the two sides met on the bloody plain and began their doomed assault... he fled. The screams of the soldiers who'd united in his name echoed in his ears as he abandoned them, their last act of defiance easily masking his escape, and he returned to reality wounded far deeper by guilt than by any blade or Thorn.

      (Lionheart later went on to join the Summer Court and rise to its throne before militarizing the freehold and readying them for open war against a bloody Charlatan in an attempt to redeem himself by successfully dying in battle in another suicidal battle; he was killed by his motleymates who saw no point in leading an entire freehold to ruin in order to assuage one man's shame.)

          August spent his durance as part of a group of changelings who are forced to shapechange over and over and be NPC's for their Gentries attempts at mastering the relatively new storytelling medium of codified roleplaying games, and two of the rules are that PC's can leave whenever they wish and if a PC is killed they will remain dead until the party members choose to resurrect them.

          This gave him the opportunity to be what he always wanted to be, do things he could only dream of... but he had no choice of which thing he'll be each day. He got to do fantastic things and he cannot deny that he enjoyed many of the times he had in his durance... but dying hundreds of times, being forced to be something he isn't, having to act opposite to every part of your personality just because that's what the story needs today, having to do horrible things because today the gamesmaster decided you'll be a villain... He couldn't take or deal with that for an eternity.

          In the end, August eventually was used as skilled assassin NPC allowing him to get the drop on a PC who had left the group for some scouting. Just as he was about to kill the Gentry PC he had ambushed he stops and instead searches the helpless PC, quickly finding his character sheet. Immediately, he takes on the role of that PC, defying his place as an NPC. But instead of immediately leaving as is his new found rights would allow, he instead decides to continue the adventure and begins fighting his fellow party members, purposely putting them in positions so that the other NPC's can take the character sheets too. He was able to defeat three of the PC's before he decided he'd have to leave or die himself.

          He is now Mirrorskin/Fairest courtier of the Sand Court.

                  I actually though a duranced based on Bedazzled with Liz Hurly would be cool, but with my own twists.

                  This is inspired by Bedazzled not complete recreation of it below.

                  Character has a crush on a beautiful woman he keeps seeing in his dreams, someone who keeps meeting briefly at work, but while friendly and polite, she doesn't really notice him.

                  In a moment of lonely dispair while by himself he whispers he'd give his soul to be with her.


                  His future Keeper hears this, and appears in the room, appearing ravishing. She introduces herself as the Supreme Devil of Unspeakable Desires and that she's come to deal for his soul.

                  She offers him three wishes and he makes them all on the spot. He wishes to have the power to seduce women, he wishes to be rich and he wishes to be powerful.

                  The Devil tells him to follow her into the Hedge and he does, the thorn rip at him, but he continues until there is a a flash, and he appears in a Palace, his Palace, with the girl from his dreams on his arm.

                  Sadly the Kingdom he rules is at war and hours after arriving and bedding his wife, his Palace is swarming with enemies and the Palace is burning and his own wife is carried off screaming by lusty raiders as a prize of war.

                  He calls for the Devil and she comes and agrees to try again at granting his wish right and a flash of light, and he's in a gorgeous chateau in the Alps with with the woman from his dreams and her Olympic Snowboarding team joining him in his Chateau to wait out the storm.

                  Everything is perfect, he's playfully seducing the snow boarders until the Chateau of his gets hit by an avalche.

                  He calls out out for the devil just as it hits and a flash and he's now in a tropical paradise sipping drinks and chilling on a nude beach, when just as things get interesting, a massive shark arrives and starts eating swimmers.

                  He calls for the devil again and this continues with each fantasy going horrifically wrong.

                  Eventually he's like enough, enough!

                  But the Devil tells him a deal is a deal, and that she has already claimed his soul, along with the rest of him, so things continue.

                  Eventually he realizes that the devil kept her word to him about making him a great seducer, which she did by making his touch sexually charged and hypnotic.

                  So he uses it on her and finds out that there is a special rite (her taboo) that when done summons her and allows the summoner to command her.

                  He also learns she's not really a devil, she's a fae and he learns he vice, virtue, greatest aspiration, ect...

                  He betrays her trust and uses the rite and then demands she set him free which she does, abit pouting.

                  She tells him one more thing, which really pisses him off. The woman from his dreams, woman is his huntsman, that she stalked his dream as bait, creating false memories of them meeting, and leaving hints that he should offer his soul for her, and that now that he's escaped, she'll be hunting him to return his soul to the rightful owner.

                  Having betrayed the trust of the Devil, who he now just calls Liz, who had turned him into a Dearheart when she granted his wish, that betrayal transformed him into a Darkling, although he only knows that because she explained it too him.

                  He remembers the rite which he uses to enslaved his former Keeper, just revenge as far as he's concerned (Farie Favour merit, which he reinvests by preparing the rite again after using it).

                  He's always on the look out for the woman from his dreams, the huntsman who tricked him fearing and yearning for her at the same time.

                  This oddly close relationship has freaked many fellow members of his Freehold, but no one wants to fuck with someone who can enslave his Keeper, even to a limited degree, so no one fucks with him and they're even polite to him, at least to his face, but rumours and talk abound about him, suggesting he's a loyalist.

                  For his Keepers part, she finds it all to be a fun game and is oddly amused by being his slave and she very flirty with everyone around her, very naughty.

                  His mien is he has glowing red eyes, horns, and a massive floating Grimoire that is actually his heart.

                  Part of the rite to control his Keeper involved cutting his own heart out, magically turning the still beating heart into a Grimiore and writing the rite inside of it, which he did in her domain (which is why it didn't kill him).

                  Hitting the Grimiore does it no damage, not even a scratch, to damage it, one has to stab the empty cavity where his heart used to lay. This severs the link between him and his heart killing him (effectly its akin to stabbing someone in the heart).

                  All his contracts, pledges, his Keepers rite, his Mantle, and the secrets he gains via his kith blessing are all contained in his Heart/Grimiore, written in his blood, which has the title Heart of Betrayal embossed in Gold letters on the black leather cover.

                  It smells faintly of blood and leaks shadows.

                      Another character I'm working on was used as a living instrument of arson by a crooked Fae businessman with a sun for a head. Phoebus works by offering people increasingly expensive deals, then offering them "odd jobs" to pay off the debt, which, of course, only ever gets deeper. One way he does this is by giving them dangerous jobs involving sabotaging his various rivals in Arcadia and expecting them to pay for potions and tools to complete them (or regrow any limbs they lost doing them), and of course he's happy to give you a loan to buy that new sword. With interest, of course. He's not running a charity. She was sent to burn buildings for him, and he talked her into buying an "elixir" that would "improve her performance", which was what made her a Fireheart. He is unfailingly polite, and no matter what criminal enterprise or scam he's involved in, he remains absolutely certain that he is a legitimate and upstanding businessman. Calling this into question is best not done, as it is the only thing that can make him lose his temper.

                      She has bright red metallic skin and orange gemstone eyes. Her body seems plastic and flexible, shape and mass usually shifting slightly. Rope fuses encircle her, forming patterns that look like muscles growing outside her body. Her skin is embedded with shiny black lumps that ignite when she's excited or using her powers. When she Embraces the Wyrd, the black clusters warp into exhaust pipes that constantly vent smoke and flame. Her fuses light and rip free of her body, flailing around her like tails. Her flexible flesh hardens and reshapes itself, giving her a large, imposing appearance with wide, artificially spherical shoulders and hands. The smells of smoke and chemicals permeate the area around her, and bits of the environment spontaneously catch fire.

                              Can you post here if you want help fleshing out your Durance? Because I basically have a character that's pretty well-defined post and pre-Durance, but I'm having trouble with his durance.

                              I know a lot about how he got into the Durance, though. He was a foster kid, always moving from one home to the next for as long as he can remember. He was the kid who everyone said looked shifty, or dangerous, or lazy or any number of other stereotypes. To survive that kind of life, you have to be the perfect kid, obedient and a good worker. Problem was, he never really could pull that off. Inevitably, the foster "parents" replaced him with other, cuter children; or he punched another kid after they made fun of his old clothes and his bad grades (not his fault he'd switched school districts halfway through the first semester); or he just ran away when the home situation got dangerous. It was not what anyone would call a happy childhood, but as he walked down the streets on the day before his 19th birthday, he could only think about how much worse it was going to be on the streets. He had a high school degree, the clothes on his back, a backpack, a phone and a little bag of all the money he'd saved over the years, but he didn't have a bank account or the credit to get a loan on an apartment. He was going to be on the streets.

                              And this is where I kinda get lost and stuck. I have a concept for his Keeper, but not fleshed out. And I have little idea how he got caught or got out. I just have the concept of a bunch of Keepers who think of humans in the way that the worst of European missionaries thought of the native populations they "ministered" to. They are horrified by our primitive "normality" and see it as their duty to teach us civilization, otherwise known as the alien logic of faerie. So they're running a sort of Victorian Domby Hall/Work House Domain, but without any regard for Euclidean geometry or the laws of time and it's trying to teach you that you should be ashamed of thinking that gravity pulls you down instead of pushing you purple or just doing whatever the hell I want it to do.

                              Drew a blank on how he escapes, but his post-durance life is where i started the character, so I know this part very well. His durance has stunted his growth and made him mentally a grizzled old cigar-smoking, hard-drinking noir detective, but forever physically stuck in the body of a child. With makeup and a fake beard, he can convince the gullibe for a little bit, but he supernaturally exudes this aura of "help this poor baby". He makes money doing the kind of things private detectives in real life actually do, usually finding dirt on shady husbands or looking for runaway kids. He never meets the clients, doing all his business through a site that he's promoted as an urban legend through various fake accounts on various forums. But if you really need to find someone who doesn't want to be found, you come to him.

                              I forget his name, but it was just a silly placeholder at the moment. In terms of appearance, he doesn't look majorly inhuman, but everything he wears immediately becomes dirty and ragged and he always looks like he's just been beaten. I was toying with making his mien be a constantly crying child with clothes made of his tears when I was considering making him an elemental, but then the concept changed.
