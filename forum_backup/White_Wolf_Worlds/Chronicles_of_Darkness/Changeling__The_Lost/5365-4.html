    74) Fool's Gold:

    Somebody, somewhere, stole the wrong trinket from one of the True Fae. Upon initial inspection, it seems to be an object of immense monetary value, but it has a curse placed upon it. Anybody who gains possession over said trinket is slowly consumed by greed, to the point where they eventually descend into megalomania and will slaughter anybody over the tiniest scrap of wealth. Now, this trinket is out there somewhere in the Freehold and it's tearing the community apart as everybody seems to want to get their hands on this thing!

    To make matters worse, the trinket can only be destroyed by the willful actions of its current owner, which becomes increasingly more difficult the longer they are exposed to its effects. Never the less, you know what you must do. For the good of the Freehold, you must seize control of this treasure, and hopefully destroy it before its power corrupts you as well!
    Does giving into the corruption turn you into a dragon, perhaps?

    75) Lore

    "Add one lore you know to this letter and send it to another person. And, do not tell others of more than ten stories written in here. Break this, and a letter with your name in it will spread. I am the dark place, the underground, and the mastermind. I am coincidence, destiny, and miracle. I am meaningless and meaningful. I am the lore. Until the day I win against fact..."

    E-mails that start with the above paragraph, each containing a dozen or so short urban legends and creepypastas, have been circulating around the city's faerie population. Web-savvy courtiers have noticed that its a translation of something that used to be a hit in 2ch more than a decade ago, but the 'lore' contained in each mail is not like what were posted in 2ch back then. The freehold is on high alert, trying to find who or what is sending these e-mails, and if possible how to stop it.

    Possible Scenarios
    • The lore is a tool used by a crafty and potent Huntsman who adapted somewhat in the contemporary era. This was discovered by the motley-mates of a changeling who spread more than ten stories to see what would happen. He narrowly escaped the thing that came out of his laptop by escaping into the Hedge, and was not seen ever since. Now the changelings know just how dangerous these e-mails are, but it has already spread throughout the city. Possibly the entire country.
    • Fact-checking the background of each lore reveals that they're entirely false, such as one story about 9/11 describing it as happening on 1999 instead of 2001. Exactly thirteen hours later, whoever did the fact-checking finds that the false background has become the truth, with only themselves remembering what was the truth. Even the people who know of this phenomenon are vulnerable to this change when someone else enacts it. One of the changelings propose a preposterous yet chilling analysis of what could be transpiring - the world itself is being replaced by its fetch, piece by piece.
    • Changeling sorcerers have discovered that each story is an instruction that teaches Goblin Contracts, draped in elaborate symbolism and hidden behind mundane language. One problem is that it doesn't take a changeling to learn them. The more skilled and luckier mortal occultists, and even certain supernatural ones, have successfully learned these Contracts and are using them with impunity. It's only a matter of time before the goblin behind all this comes to collect all those debt in one fell swoop.

        76) Satyriasis

        The King of Spring has a sensitive, personal matter he's been requesting your motley's help with. Over the last couple of years, he's had trouble... performing, as it were. Not trusting mortal medicine to work on his supernatural physiology, he decided to hit up the Goblin Markets and see if they had anything that could restore his virility. It turns out they did, and within a few minutes, the King of Spring had his hands on a new form of male enhancement supplement brewed from the distilled juices of an unidentified Goblin Fruit.

        Well, the king is well past the emergency 4 hour mark and now he's experiencing the possible side effects. Namely, his constant, throbbing malehood. He's tried everything to make it go down, but it doesn't seem to work, and he can't possibly hold public appearances while he's in this state. So he's asked you to track down the manufacturers and see if there's any way to resolve the situation. Spring is just around the corner, and if the king shows his face at court while pitching a tent, he'll be laughing stock of the Freehold!

                78. The Shattered Self:

                The Winter Queen has learned a fatal weakness of the Night Hermit, a Gentry known for its persistence in capturing or eradicating the Changelings who settle in a certain city. Before she could deliver this truth to her Court and the hungry blades of the Summer courtiers, she was caught by one of the Hermit's loyalists and slain. What the Hermit did not know, could not know, is that at the moment of her death she enacted an Oath with the Wyrd itself to deliver this message to her people. Instead of her essence lost, scattered to the labyrinth of ivy, it shattered and burst from the Hedge. By virtue of their connection to the Freehold, all who have been touched by the magic of the Winter Queen now has a shard of her essence within them, a small whorl of her memories and personality. Together they must unite the pieces through the dreamscape to reconstruct the memory of what she knows...but they should beware, all who were touched by the Queen's magic has a part of her, including the assassin who caught her in the first place, slaying all pieces they can find to protect their Keeper.

                79. Premier:

                A new television show has been muscling its way onto the tvs in the PC's city. It always comes on late at night, often when someone is alone, and usually in the presence of Changelings. The show is always the same, a grainy still of a long corridor, dark with thorns. At the far end, the glint of black water can be seen slowly approaching the camera. In the Hedge around the city the dry, packed earth has begun to grow damp, marshy. The dreams of the mortals are filled with seascapes and skies that ripple and flow like the waves of the sea. Even the gentry attacks seem to have broken off, sometimes in the midst of their plans...the mortals they infect harvested of their glamour and left alone. Something is encroaching on the city through the Hedge, something beyond even the Gentry themselves...but what? How long until it arrives?

                80. Gentrification

                There is an old legend in Detroit among the Lost that call it their home. They say the Hedge of the city is protected by a guardian Fey who shelters the escaped and spirits away their enemies for a Durance to burn away their darkness. Long has the Steel Crown Freehold maintained this legend and long have they benefited from the power of their mysterious benefactor. In the long time since their prosperity, Kings and Queens of seasons have risen and fallen, the truth of the legend lost like so many who come. But recently a Hound of Summer has come to Detroit and slain the guardian fey, too vengeful to accept its protection. Only then has the truth of its inception been revealed. Three titles, claimed by the Name-Taker Spear in battle against the gentry many years ago and bound to the Freehold of the Steel Crown. He or She who sits in the throne of Autumn inherits whichever titles remain, transformed into a Gentry to protect the Lost of Detroit. With no more guardian, the Titles are once again free to be claimed by the one who will take Autumn's seat next. Some vie for power, others for peace, and still others would see any dead who would see themselves transformed into the likes of their captors. What do the PC's do? Let it play out or join the fray?

                    81) Black Fox

                    Nobody thought it possible, but it happened. Somebody within the Freehold managed to scare off a team of Huntsmen that were pursuing them. I don't mean they found some sort of contract or clause that caused them to abandon the chase, either. Rumors are saying that the Huntsmen took one look at the guy, then turned and bolted their way back towards Arcadia like frightened children. Obviously, the first people to talk to would be the Autumn Court, but they claim to have no knowledge of anybody who could possibly do that within their organization. And whoever they are, they have yet to come forward and identify themselves to the rest of the Freehold. So who is this mysterious Changeling that can intimidate such single minded creatures like the Huntsmen, and why are they choosing to remain anonymous?

                                    83: Town of the Fae (Origin story)

                                    In a secluded village near a forest, all humans seem to have developed supernatural powers. Fae live in the woods and prey upon neighboring, mundane villages while your village acts as a safe-house for survivors, mostly young children. You play human orphans who are developing powers in the local, surprisingly large orphanage. The orphanage lends able children to the village as commonplace workforce in exchange for meat, grain and goods.
                                    One night, you spy the local amiable trader leading captured, wounded strangers into the woods. It is soon clear that there never were any neighboring villages - only a portal. The trader, the butcher, even the orphanage keeper - all grownups wear masks and they don't take in children out of the goodness of their hearts. They took you in to fatten you.
                                    This isn't a village. This is an Arcadian slaughter house.

                                        84: Royal Pledges

                                        A Goblin Lord's magical crown has been stolen by a changeling, and he wants you to get it back - or else.
                                        After finding the culprit, it turns out the crown is on the Winter King's head. He had the crown stolen to magically block out his debt to the Gentry:
                                        The Gentry helped him to the Winter throne and now he must give them three changelings from the Freehold or suffer his life.
                                        You are left with three alternatives:
                                        Shall the King give the Gentry their due, shall he keep the crown, or do you condemn him to death?

                                            85: The Unwanted Child

                                            Last night, you had a sexual encounter with the woman of your dreams. She left almost immediately after, but this morning you found a basket on your doorstep with a newborn baby and a hastily written note pinned to the handle.

                                            "They're your problem, now!

                                            P.S: Thanks for the good time!"

                                            Further investigation into this child reveals you are somehow its biological father. Even worse, the child appears to possess an unnatural longevity and will not age like a normal human. Clearly, this infant is chimerical in nature, and you have been saddled with the responsibility of raising it.

                                            Or have you? Maybe last night's experience was a hint as to how you can resolve this predicament?

                                                86: Invitation to a Slaughter

                                                The Ashen Court's bargain is this: when Autumn reigns, the Gentry and their pawns must give clear warning before an attack. The more powerful the Gentry involved, the further in advance this warning must come, with the notice period usually varying from a few hours for the weakest of the Others to a lunar month for the strongest.

                                                So how does the Freehold react when, on the night of the Autumn coronation, a number of loyal servants of the Freehold are found slaughtered in the Hedge, their cooling corpses carved with a formal announcement of an attack scheduled precisely a year and a day from now?

                                                    87: Cold War

                                                    The Winter Court has done something unprecedented, they've decided to go on the offensive! Not openly, of course, that's never really been their style. But they have started to employ the use of covert operations and sabotage against the enemies of the Freehold. While some people are happy to see the Winter Court finally getting off their asses and doing something useful for a change, many others are growing very nervous. The Winter Court has always preferred the path of least resistance, remaining out of sight and out of mind. So who or what could possibly have convinced them to take a more proactive means of defending themselves?

                                                        88: Her Darkest Hour (historical campaign - WW2 Britain)

                                                        The legends say that King Arthur never truly died. That one day, at Britain's darkest hour, he will return to save his people. Now, with Nazi bombs pulverising the capital every night and the looming threat of full-scale invasion, plenty believe that darkest hour has come. As people increasingly remember the old legends, their desperate hopes are heard. Deep below the Somerset countryside, something is stirring.

                                                        But Arthur is a king of myth, with very little historical basis. And anything that can sleep for 14 centuries before reawakening cannot be human. So just what kind of creature is the King-Under-The-Mountain? And what will happen if, or when, he emerges?

                                                            89: An Expanding Freehold

                                                            A Changeling notorious for not taking up a Court but still pledging loyalty to the Freehold has vanished for a few days, and returned with an offer for the Freehold’s leaders. They have gone into the Hedge and managed to make a Bargain with a strange entity, one that, at least inadvertantly, allowed them to escape Arcadia. Now they are the Monarch of an entirely new Court, and wish to join the Freehold and add to its diversity. The problem is the Freehold has gone by just fine without this new Court, and for all they know the new Court will muscle in on their already existing Changelings.
