    aaand another homebrew contract!

    Shape the Heart’s Desire (Spring, Royal)

    The patron of Spring sometimes takes things into their own hands- or more specifically- makes people take things into their own hands. The changeling has a heart to heart talk with the target, and bursting out of their heart a simulacrum of themself comes to help them out.

    Cost: 3 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Wits + Empathy + Mantle vs Composure + Wyrd (unwilling)
    Action: Instant
    Duration: Until the desire is fulfilled, or one chapter (whichever comes sooner)

    Success: The target spawns a being born of the target’s heart desires, appearing as a brightly colored humanoid based on their desire. They are the person’s desire, and know the target very well, succeeding on all Empathy rolls to read the person’s emotions and desires (as they are them). The avatar acts as a Retainer with a dot rating of up to the Changeling’s Mantle, and has specialties based on the desire.

    The avatar acts as a mentor, guide, and helper to the target. The Changeling has no control over the avatar once they create it, as it is fully loyal only to the person who it spawned from. Although the target knows the avatar isn’t real, they still see them as a Touchstone.

    A person can have only one avatar at once.

    Exceptional Success: The avatar is incredibly capable, and gains a +3 to help the target in all ways, shapes, and forms.
    Failure: The Contract fails.
    Dramatic Failure: The avatar is a being of the target’s darkest, most cruel and evil desires.

    Loophole: The target is in a state of denial against their emotion.
    Might I use this Contract in a game I’m running right here on this very website?

            I DID NOT COME UP WITH THIS IDEA! A very talented person named Guest did, and he has given me permission to post a heavily modified version of the Contract here.

            March Hare Apparent (Spring, Royal)

            The changeling touches a mundane animal symbolic of spring (a rabbit, a bee, a dove, etc) and infuses it with the essence of the season, transforming the creature into an avatar of growth and life, a loyal servant of the Antler Crown.

            Cost: 3 Glamour
            Dice Pool: None
            Action: Instant
            Duration: Until the sun next crosses the horizon

            Effect: The animal, suffused with the essence of spring, begins to grow until it reaches Size 4 in a humanoid form with clothes. It grows a basic intelligence, its feelings become well disposed toward the changeling, and it can speak one language that the changeling herself speaks. The animal acts as a Retainer with a dot rating equal to the Changeling’s Mantle. The creature retains all its natural abilities, and their natural instincts count as its area of specialty for the purposes of the Retainer merit. The Changeling can only have one avatar at a time.

            The animal gains Wyrd 1 and a maximum Glamour pool of 6, with one goblin Dread Power based on its nature.

            At the end of the Contract’s duration, the changeling may spend Experiences equal to the Retainer merit’s rating to make the magic permanent. They no longer count towards the maximum avatar count. You can transfer the magic over to someone new, but doing so causes a clarity attack with damage equal to the Changeling’s Mantle.

            Loophole: The changeling makes a crown of ivy and flowers and places it on the animal’s head, bestowing a fitting noble title, such as Prince of Spring or Baroness of the Lushest Meadow.

                    Another homebrew Contract! Would like feedback on the power level of this one...

                    Gorgon’s Gaze (???, Royal)

                    The Changeling lowers their mask for a split second, and gives a silent howl in fury. The target must be looking at the Changeling directly, and not through indirect visualization, such as through mirrors and cameras.

                    Cost: 3 Glamour + 1 Willpower
                    Dice Pool: Presence + Intimidation + Wyrd - Stamina
                    Action: Instant
                    Duration: Instant

                    Success: The Changeling’s wrath turns the target to stone, inflicting the Immobilized Condition for turns equal to the successes rolled. The immobilized target turns into an object, having Durability 5.

                    Exceptional Success: The target’s body remains stone-like and rigid after de-petrification, suffering a -2 to all physical actions for a scene.
                    Failure: The Contract fails.
                    Dramatic Failure: The Changeling’s wrath turns on herself, suffering Immobilized for two turns.

                    Ogre: The Ogre may ignore three points of the statue’s Durability when attempting to destroy it.
                    Elemental: The Elemental may target up to half her Wyrd in additional characters with one invocation. They must still be looking at her.

                    Loophole: The target locks eyes with the Changeling for more than a turn.

                    (inspired by Flesh to Stone from Promethean)

                                Another homebrew Contract for you all!

                                Guardian Angel (Shield, Common)

                                Have you ever had that little voice whispering on your shoulder, telling you, “you can do it”? Did your pen ever seem to glide along the page, helping you write that manuscript you always wanted to? That might be your Guardian Angel. The invocation of the Contract consists of a short (minimum 10 second) talk with the target, encouraging or promising to help them.

                                Cost: 2 Glamour
                                Dice Pool: None
                                Action: Instant
                                Duration: Until the task is fulfilled, or one chapter (whichever comes sooner)

                                Effect: The changeling designates a mundane action or task that they vow to help the target with somehow. When the action happens, even if the changeling is nowhere near them, they may assist reflexively with a teamwork action. Those in tune with the Wyrd might see a glimpse of an ethereal version of Changeling guiding their target through the action. A target can only be affected by one instance of Guardian Angel at a time.

                                Beast: The Beast gains +3 bonus dice and 8-again on their teamwork roll.
                                Fairest: The Fairest may designate up to her Wyrd in additional targets with one invocation, however, it must be the same task.
                                Ogre: The Ogre may grant Steadfast Condition to the target for the duration of the Contract.
                                Wizened: The Wizened’s teamwork action doesn’t have to be the same as the target’s primary dice pool, but it must make sense somehow.

                                Loophole: The changeling is somewhere in the scene cheering the target on when the action happens. If they are not, the Contract ends before it takes effect unless she pays the Glamour cost.

                                (inspired by Command from Promethean)

                                    Here's a very cool homebrew contract I made!

                                    Unyielding Will (Shield, Royal)

                                    Cost: 1 Glamour
                                    Dice Pool: None
                                    Action: Instant

                                    Effect: Any supernatural attempt to read or control the Changeling's mind or fate (ex. Goblin’s Eye, Spinning Wheel, Tumult) triggers a Clash of Wills, showing glimpses of Arcadia on a failure. If they know the effect is happening, the Changeling may willingly let the effect go through.

                                    For the purposes of Oneiromancy, this only affects the dreamer as an oneiropomp, and not their entire mind.

                                    Beast: The Beast’s glimpses are especially horrifying, and the Changeling can inflict a Clarity 3 attack on the aggressor if they’re a Changeling, or Spooked if they are not.
                                    Darkling: The Darkling may make the aggressor suffer the Blinded (both eyes) tilt for their Wyrd in turns if the target fails a Clash of Wills.
                                    Elemental: If the Elemental is inside a location that displays his element, he can spend a point of Willpower to roll twice for their Clash of Wills and keep the best result. This does not add +3 dice.
                                    Fairest: The Fairest may extend the benefits of this Contract to up to her Wyrd in willing targets, paying 1 Glamour each per additional person. Having sworn an Oath to the target counts as fulfilling the Loophole.
                                    Ogre: If the Ogre spends a point of willpower, Unyielding Will lasts until the sun next passes the horizon.
                                    Wizened: The Wizened knows when a Clash of Wills attempt happens, even when it succeeds or fails. They know the exact details of what the aggressor attempted to do, and gets a brief flash of the aggressor.

                                    Loophole: The Changeling has swore a pledge to himself (see Deed Vows).

                                    (inspired by Mind Shield from MtAw, and Cutting the Strings from VtR)

                                        and another! Important note about this one, I DID NOT COME UP WITH THIS IDEA! The idea comes from Eternal Noire.

                                        Princess-In-Rags (Crown, Common)

                                        The changeling calls upon the tale of the Donkeyskin princess, forced to flee from unwanted attention.

                                        Cost: 1 Glamour
                                        Dice Pool: Presence + Empathy + Wyrd vs Resolve + Wyrd (hostiles)
                                        Action: Instant/Contested

                                        Success: The Changeling appears non-threatening and harmless, and cannot be the target of physical, mental, or social attacks. She also becomes unremarkable, losing all dots in Striking Looks or Fame for the duration, appearing as “some guy.” Those who have a reason to perceive her as a threat or hostile may roll Resolve + Wyrd to resist the Contract, gaining +3 bonus dice if she has brought them any kind of harm within the same scene. The Contract ends if she takes any aggressive action, such as an attack or shouting at someone, or inflicts any kind of harm or supernatural effect on anyone.

                                        Exceptional Success: People pity the Changeling, and will do minor acts of kindness to help her.
                                        Failure: The Contract fails.
                                        Dramatic Failure: People immediately see through the façade, and the changeling sticks out like a sore thumb. To enemies, she looks like the biggest threat that needs to be eliminated in the scene.

                                        Fairest: The Fairest gains an exceptional success on three successes when invoking this contract.
                                        Darkling: Any attempt to recall the Darkling’s presence or describe her features suffers a penalty equal to her Stealth.
                                        Ogre: The Ogre may invoke this Contract on one of her companions instead.

                                        Loophole: The changeling is dressed in bland or shabby clothing (no Fashion bonus) and is carrying no visible weapons.

                                            Another homebrew Contract!

                                            Blood on My Hands (Sword, Common)

                                            What have I done, and who have I become?

                                            Cost: 1 Glamour
                                            Dice Pool: None
                                            Action: Reflexive

                                            Effect: The Changeling gains 8-again on Brawl and Weaponry dice pools.

                                            At the end of the Contract, the Changeling suffers the Guilty Condition. If the changeling invokes Blood on My Hands again while Guilty, they suffer a Clarity 2 breaking point.

                                            Fairest: The Fairest gains 8-again on Empathy, Socialize, Expression, and Persuasion to emotionally hurt or tear someone down.
                                            Ogre: The Ogre suppresses the negative effects of all Physical tilts, and cannot be Beaten Down.
                                            Elemental: If the Elemental deals more damage than a target’s Stamina, they can inflict one Tilt: Arm Wrack, Blinded, Deafened, Knocked Down, or Leg Wrack.

                                            Loophole: The Changeling throws the first blow in a conflict before anyone can react.

                                            (inspired by Killer Instinct from WtF)

                                                And another custom Contract! Remember, the most updated version of these are in the document.

                                                Doorway to Nowhere (Steed, Royal)

                                                The Changeling performs a ritual in front of a dormant Hedge Gate, and steps through…

                                                Cost: 2 Glamour or 3 Glamour + 1 Willpower
                                                Dice Pool: None
                                                Action: Instant
                                                Duration: Instant

                                                Effect: For 2 Glamour, the Changeling goes through one portal and exits out of another Hedge Gate she has seen before within (Wyrd x 10) miles. This doesn’t need to be in the same realm, as a Changeling can enter in the Ironside and exit in the Hedge.

                                                For 1 additional Glamour and 1 Willpower, the Changeling spontaneously creates a Hedge Gate anywhere that she has visited before to exit out of, even if there was not one there before.

                                                Doorway to Nowhere cannot enter Hollows or Bastions uninvited.

                                                Beast: The Beast may instead spontaneously create a Hedge Gate nearby someone she has a Pledge to, instead of a location she has visited before.
                                                Darkling: The Darkling may attempt to enter Hollows or Bastions with a Clash of Wills.
                                                Fairest: The Fairest may keep the connection open for up to her Wyrd in turns.

                                                Loophole: The changeling has the keys to both of the doors, OR The changeling has buried a vial of her blood at both locations.

                                                (inspired by Paths of Desire and Harmony of Portals)

                                                        one homebrew Entitlement updated from 1e to 2e!

                                                        The Magi of the Gilded Thorn
                                                        Prerequisites: Wyrd 2, Occult 2, Survival 2

                                                        Heraldry Token: Briarwood Staff (• to •••••)

                                                        Its mask looks like a simple walking stick, but its mein looks like a staff entangled with the Hedge’s thorns, always eager to return.

                                                        Upon activation, the staff may “mark” locations inside of the Hedge. When seeking marked locations, act as if you are on a Trod (token rating). This doesn’t penalize your Hedgespinning rolls, nor can others benefit from being on said Trod. You may mark up to (token rating) locations, while marking locations above the limit removes the oldest marked location.

                                                        Attempting to mark a hostile Hollow provokes a Clash of Wills.

                                                        Catch: Spend at least an hour inside of said location studying the local environment.
                                                        Drawback: Being in the Hedge for so long isn’t healthy. Gain the Withdrawn condition.

                                                        Wisdom of the Thorns (••••)
                                                        Glamour gain
                                                        Enhanced new Specialty
                                                        Additional Thread

                                                        Two Additional Abilities
                                                        Gain Hedge Sense, and gain 9-again when Hedgespinning or while navigating the Hedge, and to find Icons, food, shelter, and goblin fruit there.
                                                        You are immune to mundane and Hedge Environmental conditions and tilts of up to an Extreme Environment level of (Wyrd/2). This ability only protects against indirect damage, like heat and cold and minor hazards like hail. The Magi can still drown or be crushed by crashing waves.

                                                        Touchstone: A mortal whom you have frequently led out of the Hedge, despite your horror stories.
                                                        Curse: Clarity attacks suffered from seeing others suffer the perils of the Hedge add damage dice equal to ranks invested in this merit.
                                                        Beat: Whenever the Magi goes into the Hedge instead of the Ironside in a way that would hinder himself or his motley, take a beat.

                                                            and another homebrew Entitlement updated from 1e to 2e!

                                                            Hound Tribunal
                                                            Prerequisites: Wyrd 2, Subterfuge 2, Stealth 2, 3 dots of Brawl, Firearms, or Weaponry

                                                            Heraldry Token: Cloak of the Hound (• to •••••)

                                                            Upon activation, any attempt to notice the Hound takes (token rating) as a penalty. Any surprise attack made adds (token rating) to the attack roll. If lying about themself, add (token rating) to the Subterfuge roll.

                                                            Catch: The Hound is performing a task in service of someone else.
                                                            Drawback: The Hound suffers a -2 to all social rolls (besides Intimidation and Subterfuge) until the sun next crosses the horizon.

                                                            Judgement and Justice (••••)
                                                            Glamour gain
                                                            Enhanced new Specialty
                                                            Additional Thread

                                                            Two Additional Abilities
                                                            The Hound may spend 1 Glamour and 1 Willpower to mark a target by having touched, smelled, seen, or otherwise sensed a person or object. They can tell in what direction it lies, and gain the Rote quality to locate it. They can have (Wyrd/2) marks at a time.
                                                            Gain Untouchable, even if you don’t meet the prerequisites. Gain Anonymity (Wyrd/2). This stacks on top of any Anonymity the user may already have, as well as the benefit you get from being in a Hollow.

                                                            Touchstone: A mortal who has pledged themselves in service of the Hounds OR a mortal who is a target of the Hounds.
                                                            Curse: Clarity attacks suffered from harming the innocent add damage dice equal to ranks invested in this merit.
                                                            Beat: Whenever the Hound pursues justice in a way that would hinder himself or his motley, take a beat.
