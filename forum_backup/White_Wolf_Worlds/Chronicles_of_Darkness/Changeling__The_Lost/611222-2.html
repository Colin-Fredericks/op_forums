                    1) They're absolutely 2e. The old Seeming model would be almost impossible to support with additional Seemings, because you would have to devote so much space to adding Kiths in order to make them even comparable to the existing ones.

                    2) We're calling them Grimms', after the famous brothers. This Seeming didn't necessarily begin in the early part of the nineteenth century, and it most certainly didn't end there. But it became something worth noticing there; the people knew the language of the Gentry in a whole new way. I don't have a name for the eighth Seeming yet.

                    3) Seventh Seeming is my Changeling: The Lost Akira Kurosawa homage.

                                        Originally posted by proindrakenzol View Post

                                        "Lost at sea" is a trope. But beyond that it would actually be really easy: there are three main ways to navigate in open ocean - by the Sun, the Stars, or the GPS satellites. The third one obviously wouldn't be available in the Hedge and the first two are rather mutable within the confines of the otherspace.

                                        And even if you don't dick around with celestial non-constancy there are two things that govern how sail-powered ships move: wind patterns and oceanic currents. Set up a maze of strong, fixed wind/current patterns and now you have a maze without walls and no real reference points. Have fun with that.
                                        Don't forget the Saragossa Sea like seaweed and the junkyards of ships from all ages in between various riffs, reefs and rocks and finally fog, rain and large waves Anyway, I distinctly reading about the Hedge in other environments in one of the 1e books. The winter one?

                                        As for a book on the Hedge, yes please, preferably one with a good discussion on the economics of goblin fruit and how they interact with the PCs. Or is that going to be in 2e? My players simply seem to be ignoring goblin fruit as a potential resource, both because I have some difficulty with imaging how to deal with them going into the Hedge to gather them or how to prize them and because my players keep treating them as rare magic items that should be saved for the perfect time. I am contemplating going the Numenera/Strange way with their use of artifacts and outright tell my players that they start with X items and if they use them it will be replaced within a session.
