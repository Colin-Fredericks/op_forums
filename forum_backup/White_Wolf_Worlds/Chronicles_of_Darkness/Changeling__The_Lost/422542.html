    Speculation: Changeling Courts 2.0

    02-27-2015, 11:31 PM
    As said in title, I thought some speculation on Changeling Courts in 2.0 is going to work might be fun.

    So what courts are going to be included in the book do you think? I mean we know a 1001 nights theme is going to be in it, and I think Miami will keep the Seasonal Courts, but what about Budapest, what about Tokyo, and Cape Town (that's in South Africa right?).

      Well, the thing about the old courts was that, while Directional Courts were technically there, the skewing of contracts was laughable. Each seasonal court got 3 different sets of contracts, while all the Directional Courts had to share the same contract. Did I mention that Spring gets the ONLY decent healing spell with no massive drawback? The court contracts, in general, were just more powerful than everyone else's contracts. (Which is probably specifically because they were so exclusive - they were the reward for putting up with a court.)

      I think that not having contract "lines" will help things. Rather than a massive block of contracts, you might have a giant grab-bag of contracts, and they might be divvied out between the different courts of any given court system, with maybe a few left-outs, and a few special snowflakes only fitting for a specific type of court. (I.E. that fire laser thing in Eternal Summer might be for Summer and Sun but not for anything Directional.)

              Well, I mean, "unique court contracts" might be the key, here. That is, you have a big pool of court contracts that most court systems will divvy up between their respective courts, and then a handful of unique court contracts that are specific to that particular court.

              That is, you might have an anger/war related contract, and have it common to Summer and West (of the Directional) since they have similar domain over those sorts of concepts, and in a Dusk/Dawn court, it might go to Dusk if it is especially destructive, or maybe Dawn if it is related to inspiration.

              Hence, no matter what court system you choose, you don't have to make up a giant list of contracts to go with them, and players aren't getting barred access to a huge block of cool powers. You just all get together when making up a court, and divvy up the common court contracts by the themes of the court, then maybe add a couple uniques to add flavor.

                  So far as Mantle goes, they all pretty much followed the same general path (excluding the useless Autumn mantle).

                  1 dot: You get +1 to the most relevant application of a skill to the court.
                  3 dot: Another +1 to something more broad and useful, or a coupon for skills or merits
                  5 dot: Reroll the 1 dot skill, or +1 to something useful almost all the time.

                  I suspect there will be a "build a mantle" ruleset that generally follows something similar in scope. Generally, it's a merit you have to buy in tiers, and has other benefits, so it won't give benefits more mechanically powerful than the EXP it takes to buy the mantle in the first place.

                              I suspect it needn't be an outright merit - We're talking outright magic, here, so they may be weak powers somewhat like a kith's power. Keep in mind, Summer's mantle involved outright +1 armor and +1 health. Winters applied a malus to other people's perception rolls by contrast. The Hunter Status merit also gave mostly a ton of merit bonuses (mostly social), even in 1e, while Changeling Mantle mostly directly affected the character, as it was the difference between knowing a guy, and an innate aspect of your own power.

                              Also, there were a lot of skill bonuses, rather than merits for much the same reason. In terms of EXP, specialties are equal to a 1 dot merit, and skill dots are 2-dot merits. Pledges, interestingly, worked in much the same way - each +1 of a pledge was just 2 EXP in benefit.

                              Beyond that, there was the special mantle bonus for the crowns of the monarchs, like Summers adding Mantle dots to Initiative, or Autumns having Harvest of Whispers add bonus glamour for contracts. Obviously, not everyone gets one of those, but they'd need mechanical guidelines, as well.
