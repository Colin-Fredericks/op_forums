        I really like the idea of 4 dots and an additional trigger.

        Also, to weigh in on the build vs roleplay side... no. It's not a binary. Mechanics should inform the roleplay, and vice versa. If your character concept involves being really good at a particular skill, but your choice of abilities make you less capable at it than someone who ostensibly doesn't specialize in that area but picked better merits, that's going to be incredibly frustrating. Sure, you can tell a really good story about coming to terms with mediocrity, but that should be because that's the story you want to tell, not because poor dice pools keep forcing it on you.

        And honestly, I have an easier time starting from the mechanics side. I'll see a couple of merits or contracts that have an interesting interaction and work that into a concept, thinking about what kind of person would invest in those abilities, or what kind of durance would lead to them.
