    Jacob’s Journal

    It’s the day before Halloween and things had to go and get “interesting” again. The day started out normal enough. The lot of us helped De la Rocha move out of her apartment and into the Dream House. It was kind of a bittersweet event for De la Rocha. She apparently moved into that apartment after she broke things off with Spidertoes in a bid to show her independence. Now she was giving that up to share a room with her lover and a house with several other people. She wasn’t living in the apartment anymore. She just went back to make sure nothing had been stolen and to throw out any food that had gone bad. It wasn’t practical to keep it, but I can understand why it would be hard to give up. It must feel like losing something of her independence. But we’re not ones to make others feel like they’re penned in, so I think that De la Rocha will come to believe that she actually hasn’t lost that freedom she craves.

    Later, after Calamity’s act Madame Spidertoes came by and gathered up Insincere Marguerite, Calamity and Julia. I would say that she was getting all the Darklings together, but De la Rocha was not included. Maybe it was an Autumn Court thing? Though Marguerite is in the Night Court. She does do a lot for the Autumn Court though…she could be an honorary member by now. Anyhoo…they eventually came back out. As Spidertoes was leaving she asked Lorelei if she minded if De la Rocha escorted her out to her car. Lori said it was up to De la Rocha, but seemed really tense about the whole thing. In the end De la Rocha and Julia, who looked delighted to go, escorted the elder Changeling outside. I don’t know the whole story, but from what I’ve gathered Madame Spidertoes has been flirting with De la Rocha, and since the older woman had a previous relationship with the Latin beauty Lori feels threatened. I think she might be afraid that Spidertoes is trying to woo De la Rocha back to her. For my part, I’m not sure. Spidertoes does seem to keep those feelings of fondness alive for those that she’s been involved with, and continues to take care of them in various ways. I can see why Lori might feel threatened. I just don’t know what advice to give, so I have stayed silent on the matter.

    After the Autumn Court meeting Insincere Marguerite stopped to watch Chalcedony dance on the stage. I didn’t think much about it until Julia came up behind her and just about pushed her over. How focused was Marguerite that she let herself be pushed? Makes me wonder if there is something to Insincere Marguerite’s fascination with Chalcedony. Anyway…after all that was over Calamity gathered up the motley and said that she needed to talk to us. She said that Madame Spidertoes had gathered up the Autumn Courtiers to discuss a threat that Tommy had discovered. She brought it to those she trusted first since she was afraid of the information getting out to the wrong people. Apparently they discovered that some people from the Hollywood Courts are sending mortals in to our area and are setting up a base of sorts in an old strip club called Foxy Boxes. The fear is that they are being sent to our neck of the woods to spy on our freehold. They also have a Hedge beast in the club for protection. So they are worried that they will need said protection, which doesn’t sound like a clean conscience to me. It does sound likely that they are up to something.

    Madame Spidertoes wants to keep the number of people that know about this to the absolute minimum. She hasn’t even told the Monarchs. She told select people from her Court, namely our motley mates, and gave them permission to tell the rest of us. She says that she doesn’t know if there are any Changelings in the freehold that are actually spies for the Hollywood Courts, but didn’t want to take the chance. So, we discussed the mortal spies and what to do about them. Calamity seemed to be leaning along the lines of killing everyone there, dealing with the Hedge beast and leaving. Insincere Marguerite was keen on the idea of just using her “sleeping cloth” on them. She was quite adamant that anyone she knocked out with the chloroform not be killed while they are incapacitated. She repeated herself several times. I got the strong impression that she was trying very hard to influence Calamity away from killing them all. Lori was also very much against killing the mortals, saying that knocking them out and taking their computer and/or other information would be good enough. I am not sure where I fall on the whole matter. I generally try to draw as little attention to myself as possible, and killing the mortals would definitely draw unwanted attention. The police would get involved and could very well trace the crime back to us. I don’t know if we are all good enough to not leave some sort of evidence behind.

    That covers the practical aspect of the matter. There is also the moral aspect, and I’m not sure that I would actually mind all that much if some of the mortals died. I guess it depends on their reaction to us. If they just give up or run I’d be tempted to just let them go. If they try to hurt or kill us then all bets are off though. That is one thing I do not tolerate. But is it right to kill them? I suppose I could just incapacitate them and leave them behind to crawl back to the Hollywood Court. My worry is what information they might already have on our Freehold. We can’t exactly pull a “Harry Potter” and remove the memories of what they’ve learned about us. Practically it’s not wise to let them live. They are a security risk and could leak information that we can’t afford to let out. On the other hand I’m not sure it would be right to kill them if they aren’t actively trying to do the same to us. I don’t know what to do. Part of me agrees with Calamity’s practical approach, but there’s that small little voice of doubt in my head. Killing should be wrong, shouldn’t it? So why am I unsure?

    **** **** ****

    It’s Halloween, Samhain…and today definitely had a more “interesting” start than usual. After I’d finished making and handing out coffee for the group Lowjack called and said that he needed to talk to Insincere Marguerite as soon as possible. Okay…it was implied that he wanted to talk to her right away. So, I told Marguerite about the meeting and she got ready to go. I offered to stay with her if that was allowed and she said that she would appreciate that. I can understand that. I figured something was up, but didn’t know what for sure. I initially thought it had to do with the Loyalist that Darke said was looking at me and Insincere Marguerite a while back. Not sure if that Loyalist was Rook or someone else though. If it was Rook I am curious why the Lady would have him look into Marguerite. If it’s someone else that was interested in us then we still don’t know who they are, and that makes me nervous.

    So, we arrived at Lowjacks bar and were immediately ushered into the backroom. We were met by Lowjack, Vik, Molly and Willow Black. Boss Lowjack motioned for me to stay but close the door, which I did. He then opened with the information that Tamara had been attacked and nearly killed by the Handmaidens last night. He was pissed. I have never seen Lowjack so angry in all the time I’ve been here. Granted that’s not all that long, but still. Lowjack is usually composed and calm, but this afternoon he was furious. He said that he had made arrangements that if any other members of the Winter Court should be attacked or killed then one of the Night Courtiers would be killed in retribution! He said that plans were already in place. So even if it was him that was killed the plan would still be executed…so to speak. It was kind of implied that Willow would be the first target. It wasn’t said straight out, but the implication was there. Willow did not take things well and started to take a step forward. She got about half a step before Molly pulled a gun and aimed it right at Willow’s head. The Night Courtier stopped.

    Willow objected to Lowjack’s ultimatum and looked to Marguerite for help. Boss Lowjack then implied that there are others in the Night Court that are jealous of Insincere Marguerite’s position within the Court and may be looking for a way to get her out of the way. He inferred that Marguerite and Felicity stood in the way of Willow Black and Olivia Gold respectively. Apparently there have been talks or whispers about the Night and Day Courts getting a monarch within the freehold, and my motley mate and Jolie’s lover stand in the way of the crowns going to certain people in their respective Courts if/when created. I’m not sure if he has facts to back up those accusations or if it is just speculation, but it was clear what he was doing in any case. Divide and conquer has been a tried and true method for centuries.

    I can understand the desire to protect one’s own, which is why I was a little conflicted today. I understood Lowjack’s desire for retribution should one of the Winter Court be killed, but it was also implied that Marguerite might be one of the targets and that is not acceptable. She is my motley mate and my friend and I will not allow her to be hurt. I hope that I am not forced to choose between my Court and my friend. I respect Lowjack, he seems to be a good person and genuinely wants to help people, otherwise he would not be in the entitlement that he is. I really don’t want to side against him, but I really don’t want Insincere Marguerite to be a target. I know I should have said something at the time, but I’ll be honest. I was more than a little bit shocked by the ultimatum. Not that Lowjack made it, because I can understand why he did, but because I was being put on the spot in a way.

    I talked to Lowjack after Willow and Insincere Marguerite stepped out. I enquired how Tamara was doing and asked if I should get Lori to come by. Boss Lowjack said that two Spring Courtiers were already seeing to Tamara and that she was going to be okay. I offered to talk to Lorelei about coming over to help. He said that wasn’t necessary. I nodded my understanding but left it as an open offer in case additional help was needed. I did not think that Lori would mind assisting Tamara should the need arise.

    After all that was over Insincere Marguerite and I went back to the Dream House to talk to the rest of our Motley. We explained what happened and the discussion began. The others were shocked at what had happened to Tamara and at Lowjack’s response. Some were also not sure if John had proof that Insincere Marguerite and Felicity were in actual danger, or if he was just trying to sow dissent. It could have just been pure speculation on his part as well. I don’t know for sure. I didn’t want to ask him flat out if he had proof in front of Willow or Marguerite. I figured he wouldn’t tell me while there was an audience anyway. I’ll have to ask him later. Perhaps once he has calmed down a bit.

    Throughout all of this there is still one question that has gone unanswered. Why Tamara? Why did the Handmaidens decide to target her and nearly kill her? And was she supposed to have died and got lucky, or was she left on the brink of death as a warning of some kind? I’m not sure exactly how a Leechfinger’s powers work, so I don’t know how much control they have over whether or not their victim survives. They clearly have been targeting Tamara for months now, but this is the first time they have gone so far. They drained her once before that I know of to the point she needed healing, but it wasn’t life threatening then. This time it was. So why? That’s what I want to know. I also wanted to know why we weren’t threatening the Handmaidens if they were the ones that hurt Tamara in the first place. I asked Lowjack this and he said that it was politically bad for us to target the Handmaidens. Any harm that befell them would make the Warlock of Night upset and we apparently can’t afford to make her mad. So because the real culprits cannot be targeted, Lowjack has moved on to the next most prominent members of the Court to use as scapegoats. Not sure how many members there are, but Lowjack implied that there were more than the two in the room with us.

    I can understand not wanting to make the L.A. Night Court monarch upset, but surely she has to realize that her people need to be disciplined for what they do wrong. Willow Black is supposed to be their minder while they are in town, but apparently she has not done a very good job of it so far. They have slipped their “leash” a few times now, and this time someone was badly hurt. I don’t find it acceptable at all. Part of me really wants to teach them a lesson despite Lowjack’s wishes. They are protected by the Warlock and can basically get away with anything they so desire. They should be held accountable for their actions just like the rest of us. If any of us from the Lady by the Sea freehold should pull anything similar in L.A there would be hell to pay, so why do these two get away with almost killing someone?

    **** **** ****

    Okay, some time has passed since this morning and I have calmed down somewhat. It is time to get ready for the masquerade. Several of us are dressed in our new Hedge spun clothes, courtesy of Gawain’s awesome skills. Gawain’s new armor makes it look like he’s got forest shadows moving across his chest and back. Insincere Marguerite has a tawny leonine dress, which looks really nice. Kind of creepy that she’s still kind of following in her Keeper’s footsteps fashion-wise, but it was still pretty. Lorelei dressed as Neil Gaiman’s Death, in Victorian/Goth finery. Very cute. I always liked that character. Let’s see, Calamity is dressed as a droog from “A Clockwork Orange,” I am wearing my new ice armor…it’s actually made of ice. I thought about wearing the new sheath that Gawain also made for me that produces an ice blade when I need one, but figured it wouldn’t be polite to go armed. I think Gawain did a great job on it if you ask me. And last but certainly not least is Luna. She decided to dress as Lara Croft. Totally hot! I think a new Tomb Raider game came out while I was gone, but I remember playing the older ones and liking Lara’s character.

    There was something going on about Joan and Andromeda. I think the gist was that they were going to be dressed in costumes that the other didn’t know about and that would hide their identity. They’re going to go separately and see if they hook up with each other and maybe have a dalliance with someone new. Some of the others, Lori and Gawain included wanted to find out what Joan was dressing as so they could try their hand at seducing her. I guess we’ll see how that works out. Me, I plan on dancing with anyone that wants to, but am not looking for any “hook-ups.” I have Luna and she is more than enough for me.


    Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

    Why is everyone but me completely irrational? It is as if my lovers and Motley lost their minds.

    The 30th was the day De La Rocha moved into the Dream House in full. It should have been a happy day. But she was deeply moody. Sure, there is some sadness leaving a place, but she had not lived there in months. She worried about Spidertoes, and Lorelei really, really worried about Spidertoes. They need to talk to Spidertoes. But no they do not, well, mostly not. Sadly.

    Opportunity knocked when Spidertoes arrived that evening. De La Rocha had gone to greet her, and she arranged a meeting with Calamity, Julia, and I. Spidertoes is unaccustomed to explaining herself, which to be fair to De La Rocha is annoying, but she took things in the absolute worst possible way: Utter Rejection. I discovered post meeting that she took her frustrations out on a punching bag, and upset Lori, who took out her frustrations on a punching bag. Weirdly.

    When we got out of the meeting, I paused to admire Chalcedony’s dancing (which was very good) and Julia pushed me and Calamity flipped out about Chalcedony controlling me. I heard Julia walk up, she has a familiar tread. I just did not expect to be pushed. Calamity warned me of Chalcedony “having a hold of me” but that hardly seems true. We always speak briefly when she comes to dance but it is a polite exchange. I almost as often have one with Marie-Hélène. We haven’t kissed, she has shown no interest in me, and has not even asked me to fetch a glass of water. And Calamity thinks I am controlled and seems to have convinced Julia of this. This is a non-academic problem, as Calamity is on a crazy uptick and wants to go murdering people while Julia can be violent in her defense of me. Unfortunately.

    So when Spidertoes was headed out, and perhaps sensing Lori had concerns (by perhaps sensing, I mean noticing the obvious tension on her face) Spidertoes politely asked Lori if she minded if De La Rocha escorted her out. Lori answered this consideration of her feelings by snapping that De La Rocha could make up her own mind. De La Rocha elected to do so, but with Julia there for Lori’s sake. For her part Julia just seemed happy to bask in Spidertoes gothic mysteriousness. However, Lori did not address the elephant in the room about her feelings (despite Spidertoes giving her an opening) and De La Rocha did not use the relative privacy to discuss it. I fail to understand this, though perhaps she would wind up being like Calamity’s amateur Kremlinology.

    The matter was simple. Agents of the Court of Fallen Stars had expended into our turf, into a former stop club called Foxy Boxes. De La Rocha explained it is a vagina reference. There were, well, boxes moved in, some agents at work, a hedge beast, and a computer system. We were tasks with killing the beast, dealing with the humans, and acquiring the data. Simple, no? While Calamity and I could probably do it ourselves, getting the Motley involved made sense and quite frankly raised the chances of an easy job. Fortunately.

    But Calamity insisted on a Bloodbath. Gawain clearly disapproved, Lorelei disapproved, and I made it clear we had a non-lethal means of dealing with humans. I would point out Julia disapproved, but I did and it goes to follow. Anyway dead humans are a waste. One nearly never needs to kill. Certainly not now. Dead humans also bring attention. They bring cops. Cops that could analyze a bloodstain if Calamity is hit by the beast or bullet, Cops that find a nearby camera that shows some of us enter, cops who won’t accept “freehold business” as a reason. Hedge Beasts, with their ultimate genesis from the Gentry, are one thing, but dead humans bring cops, and Calamity does not need cops right now. Especially given her blood fetish. Almost as importantly, Calamity is in therapy to not be a murder happy monster, but an actual contributor to society. But when she sees an opportunity to murder humans, she is eager to do so, despite the fact it has been clearly shown doing so makes her crazier. Like a child who experiences pain from stabbing a fork in a light socket and does so again, Calamity fails to learn. This makes me sad, as I like Calamity, but I consider having to use the sleeping cloth on her for her own good. Reluctantly.

    Also this person, who apparently thinks going crazy from killing people and having cops looking for you is a good idea, feels I am not exercising good judgment on Chalcedony. At this point if she told I my approach to Chalcedony was wholly rational I would rethink it. Obviously.

    Calamity, Lori, and De La Rocha are not the only ones who have lost their damn mind. I was summoned in the morning by Lowjack, who brought Willow and I into a room with Jacob and a pair of thugs and threatened our lives if anything happened to Tamara due to the Warlock of Night’s Handmaidens. Has Lowjack lost his mind? Making matters worse he revealed how much Olivia and Willow would benefit from my and Felicity’s deaths, saying we are just a few Day and Night Courtiers from having crowns, in front of Willow. I lack strong feelings about Willow, but I cannot believe she would be plotting against I me. Of course, I won’t indulge blind trust. I will be wary. I am not happy with Lowjack, however, in part due to Molly putting a Gun to Willow’s head, which almost got her blinded. Dangerously.

    Something must be up with Tamara. I do not like the Handmaidens at all but the Handmaidens do not choose victims randomly. Could she be a spy or suspected spy for the Court of Fallen Stars? Perhaps she makes people “disappear” into the Grandmaw. I am not going to accuse her, but a with Willow, while I do not think it the case I will remain wary. I would not mention this to Lowjack, he would probably order Molly to shoot I. Unwisely.

    Now we have the Masque soon, I have my lovely outfit, and people need to get their shit together. Perhaps I will speak to Jacob and Gawain, who do not seem crazy, about this. Or maybe I will discover they have some bizarre problem of their own. Gods I hope not. I need some sanity right now. Certainly.

    Oh, Joan will be there under cover. She is not known to be but De La Rocha spilled the beans. Lori, Gawain, and Julia bribed her for information. I myself did not. If I sleep with her and she ever buys me a drink someone in the Court will decide I took a break from being Chalcedony’s love slave to enrapture her with my vaginamancy. Clearly.

    Well, let’s see if I can actually enjoy myself tonight. Hopefully.

    End Cession XLV

    Tension rise!

    We actually covered the beginning of the Masquerade, but all decided it would be easier to cover in one go, so covering that next time.

    They really cover things this time. One point of clarification would be the crowns. According to John Lowjack, Willow and Olivia have been sneaking Day/Night Courtiers into the periphery of town, just like Insincere Marguerite squatted the Dream House. They want enough power for the Day/Night crowns to appear, but have discovered that Felicity and Insincere Marguerite, who resonate intensely with their court, would receive the crowns, rather then themselves. Thus, the two are “in the way.” Is it true? Well…

    Insincere Marguerite’s costume is not just the lion dress, but a leonine Venetian mask. DLR does not have a mask, but given her habit cover all of her head but the face itself, her distinctive hair is out of sight so she does not need one.

    I think that covers everything, but if I missed something let me know. Comments and Questions welcome.

                    Big recap this time! We cover both the Masquerade and Foxy Boxes raid. We can do this as the Masquerade was the last ~1/3-1/4 of the previous session, so we already got a lot of play in, so longer journals. Lori focus’s more on the Masquerade, Jacob on the raid, Gawain on the aftermath of the raid, Insincere Marguerite…on particular elements of both, let’s say.

                    Let’s dig in

                    Session XLVI

                    Gawain’s Memories

                    November 1st, afternoon

                    Well, and fun was definitely had. Even if my shot at Joan didn’t get me anywhere (not that I can blame her, Lori was looking hot and really laying on the charm), I certainly wasn’t disappointed and everyone had a great time, and that’s what matters, you know?

                    I did get to dance with Joan a few times. It was fun - she did a good job pretending to be Koozebanian, you know, from the Muppet Show. I mean, not in looks, but that’s where she said she was from. I spent pretty much the whole time dancing, in fact, with De la Roche, and Flores (who was dressed as the Baroness from GI Joe) and even Princess Joli. She was dressed as Strawberry Shortcake and even smelled like strawberries, all over. She totally spent the night with me and... wow. It was an amazing night.

                    Everybody else had fun too, I think. Calamity came in to ‘Singing in the Rain’ cause, you know, Clockwork Orange. She got in her share of dancing, with a 5’2” Darth Vader (who turned out to be Dark Kimberly) and Flores (who she spent the night with). I thought she seemed, you know, a little worried at one point, but she was definitely alright when she came back into the ballroom, so I dunno.

                    The costumes were fun - Shi was a coyote, Queen Charlotte was Batgirl. There was a Tinman, the Red Death and a female Link. Captain Jack showed up, in true Captain Jack style, to the Invader Zim theme and was dressed as Gir in his dog suit. Oleander was there as the Question, De la Roche was pretty sure, and Julia confirmed it as breakfast.

                    It was a pretty busy breakfast. Joan didn’t come back with Lori, but Princess Joli, Flores and Dark Kimberly stuck around. We spent most of the time guessing who was and who slept with who. Well, mostly it was trying to guess who ‘the Koozebanian’ was, since Lori wouldn’t say. She did let it slip that Andromeda had been there (she was the Red Death, not Darth Vader like we all kinda thought).

                    Once they all left, we started planning for the evening. We figure the Foxy Boxes thing should be dealt with sooner before later, you know? And our Darklings work better at night, of course, so our current plan is to wait until nightfall, have Insincere Marguerite, Jacob and Calamity sneak in and see what’s up, maybe sleepy cloth any human guards there were. If feasible, Insincere Marguerite’s gonna grab up the computer, then we’ll deal with the Hedgebeast. We’ll see how long it survives enemy contact.


                    Nov 1st evening

                    We will not be Darke. Desiree will not be another Molly or Victor. I refuse. Whatever we have to do, whatever rules we have to bend, I will make sure she returns to her very self. And, if necessary, I will personally deal with the consequences.

                    The raid went fine. Insincere Marguerite, Calamity and Jacob dealt with the four guards handily. They were just some guys though, a local security firm that thought the Hedge-dragons were komodo dragons. One of the dragons was roughly hippo shaped and it had two mastiff-sized partners. Calamity dealt with one of the small ones quickly, then went after the larger one. They almost had it down by the time I knocked down the door and charged in. I managed to sink my sword into its heart and finished it off. Not before Calamity get hurt, and Jacob got hurt by the second little one before he managed to put it down as well, but Lori and De la Roche got them healed up.

                    We decided to try and skin the dragons, so we left via the Hedge. Once we got home, Jacob and I help Calamity log onto the computer, then got to the dragons. We made pretty short work of it, until Calamity came and gathered everybody to let us know what it was that she had found.

                    First off, they wanted the Table, and knew that the Autumn Court knew where it was. They didn’t realize they needed Andromeda for it, or had their own Mothership escapee, so they weren’t planning on abducting her. That last bit was the extent of the good news. Well, also that they were terrified of Madame Spidertoes, but that just shows they can follow some forms of realistic logic.

                    They had lots of files on their spy. She had been a successful agent of the Director in Hollywood, the most successful yet. They have figured out that the Director’s people are implanted with scripts - if a ‘scene’ begins, they automatically follow their role, even if they weren’t really a Loyalist. The Courts in Hollywood managed to brainwash this successful agent (who had, in fact, been a Loyalist), forcing her to believe she was not a Loyalist, and that she had never been to Hollywood. Also, they managed to somehow alter her scripts, so she would get in close to the Monarchs here, and report back to Hollywood.

                    Up until this point in the description, I had been willing myself to believe it was Chalcedony it was talking about, that it explained the recent oddness and her reluctance to have the conditioning removed. But then Calamity said they had figured out that someone here had been messing with the scripts, and that then, a different someone had removed them. They were worried, she said, because she kept building dioramas, dioramas that echoed the brainwashing sessions the Courts had put her under, sessions that very likely involved her being in the middle of a circle of other people, being looked down upon from their position of authority, watching. It turns out that while Calamity and I were talking the others went to the Hollow, so we went there to get Desiree’s keys. We explained our plan and she seemed willing enough to go along with it and handed over her keys. We left the ladies in Gawain’s capable hands and headed to Desiree’s apartment building.

There was a lot of blood outside and we found the front door cracked open. There were no police or sirens coming so we decided to take a quick peak inside. I’m glad we did. We saw a blood trail leading away from the apartment to the parking lot. Apparently she healed herself in the car. When we went inside we found that every door save one was open. And the one that was shut was her bedroom door. We figured this was a bit suspect so Calamity crawled along the ceiling and started to crack the door open a bit. She found a trip wire connected to a shotgun on the other side. She deftly disabled the trap and we packed up the shotgun to take with us. Looks like the Hollywood Courts already know that she’s alive. No need to booby-trap a dead person’s bedroom door after all. We then figured it would be best to leave and did so. We headed back to the Dream House and left Desiree’s car at the apartment. No need to call attention to it. I made sure to wipe my prints off of every surface I could think of that I had touched. Don’t need to add police interest where it’s not wanted.

Maybe it’s just prolonged exposure to the Gentry or the fact that they have been holding another living being captive for so long. Not sure which one it is, but whatever the reason it gives us an advantage. Our Courts get along, and together we can plan something to keep the Court of Fallen Stars out of our business.

                                            At that point it was getting late so some folks, Lorelei and De la Rocha, went back to the Dream House. While others, Calamity, Gawain and Desiree, stayed at the Hollow. Calamity was planning on staying up through the night to keep a watch and make sure nothing happened to the occupants. I thought it was a good idea so I went back to the Dream House and decided to stay up all night there to keep an eye on things. It is possible that if the Hollywood Courts knew that Desiree was alive then they might have trailed her to our place and it would be prudent to keep a watch. Nothing happened, which was probably good, but I’ll admit that a part of me wanted the Hollywood Courts to try something. I don’t like waiting for an ambush. We don’t know what the Court of Fallen Stars is going to do and I kind of want the waiting to be over.

We had already started the hides curing and had frozen, salted and otherwise stored all the meat we could. So, we had dragon steak for breakfast, which is pretty awesome to be able to say. They were also really good which is a bonus. Who knew that dragons tasted good? I sure didn’t. My contribution was of course, coffee. I made an offering of coffee to Lori in exchange for a quick Spring Court “pick me up.” I think Gawain and Calamity did the same thing if the offerings of toast and other food stuffs was any indication. It was strange to see her on a Sunday lacking the ponytail and glasses, but I think she skipped church this time. I think I overheard something about hedge wine and overindulgence. Poor Lori. I think she needed that coffee.

Apparently Lowjack is going to talk to the other Monarchs this afternoon to discuss the whole “Desiree problem,” and until then we’re going to be watching the aforementioned Desiree to make sure she stays safe. And stays put. Well, mainly to keep her safe. I still don’t think it’s a good idea to just let her go after all of this is over, but who am I to go against the Monarchs? Well, truth be told that deal was made by Dahlia. I’m not sure how much weight her word will have if the Monarchs decide something different. I guess we’ll find out this afternoon.

Apparently she has become close friends with our resident flower. So, we called Flores who said that coming to the Dream House was fine with her. As I mentioned before, there’s not much point in killing Desiree after she gives up her information so the prudent thing to do would be to kill her before she has that chance. So, we invited Flores back here. Hopefully if anything does happen we’ll be able to keep everyone safe.

Something Jem, or Jem and the Something. I don’t remember what was said. I was only paying so much attention at that point. I do remember something being said about G.I Joe. I know there was a couple movies that came out a few years ago, but they were talking about some cartoon by the same name. Sounds like it might have come out around the same time as that Jem show. Again, I only paid so much attention.

She said that she found out that she was a sleeper agent for the Hollywood Courts against her will, and how they were now trying to hurt her because she was going to give information to our Monarchs. Flores was all for giving it to the Hollywood Courts for trying to hurt her friend. It wasn’t much later that Sandoval showed up wanting to talk to Desiree. After a moment’s hesitation Desiree just told him that he could talk in front of the rest of us. I think she had a good idea of what he was there to talk about and figured there was no point in trying to hide it. Doing so would be pointless and go against the whole “open policy” that she’s adopted with us since she remembered the truth.

It was implied that they considered her important right now because she had information that they needed, but they weren’t sure if that would change afterwards. Gawain got really protective at that. I think he took what Sandoval said as a threat to Desiree and was not happy. There was some more discussion, but it came down to the fact that Desiree really wanted to get the information that she has down on paper somewhere so she will no longer be a target of the Hollywood Courts. She was also very keen on getting the Hollywood Courts away from the Grandmaw or the Grandmaw away from them. Whether that involved killing the Gentry or not she didn’t seem to care, as long as they were away from its influence.

Of course, that’s if her information is true. We don’t have a lot of intel on the Court of Fallen Stars. At least I don’t. Maybe the Monarchs do and will be able to weed out the truth from the bullshit. It’s true that she is valuable to us as long as she has information we need. She seems keen on telling us all that she knows, but then her usefulness might be at an end. Do we let her go after Sandoval takes her deposition or will she conveniently have more information that we might find interesting?

Though I didn’t remain loyal to my Keeper, I gave a similar kind of deposition to Lowjack about the Lady’s strengths and weaknesses, friends and enemies. Am I still useful to John? Is he keeping me close because he trusts me or because he has something on me and can use it to his advantage? I certainly am in a mood, aren’t I? I guess this whole thing just brings up my own trust issues. I guess we’ll see what Desiree’s next move is after Sandoval gets her statement and she meets with the Monarchs. I think the plan is that we’ll keep an eye on her for now and then she said that she wants to spend the night somewhere different tonight. She said that she doesn’t want to endanger us should the Court of Fallen Stars come after her. Her concern might be genuine or it might be faked. I’m not sure. I’m tempted to say that she’s just playing us. She’s supposed to be one of the best and is very skilled at playing the “long game.” My guess is that for the moment our goals are the same so she’s cooperating. Who knows, maybe she just wants to get out of here alive and get back to the Director. Maybe she has some other ulterior motive. I’m going to continue to be the pessimist, or as my father would say, realist, and watch her carefully.

I felt the desires come; the thing in the dark began to rise behind my eyes. She depends on I.
