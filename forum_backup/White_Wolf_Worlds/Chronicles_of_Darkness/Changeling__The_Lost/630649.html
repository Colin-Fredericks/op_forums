    Contracts of Tendrils

    08-18-2015, 12:26 AM
    So I had this idea for a contract based off of a Cthulhu mythos ideas. Please do keep in mind that this is my first custom contract and I haven't actually done this before so any constructive feedback you have will be helpful.

    Also I apologize if it turns out that there is already a contract out there like this.

    EDIT: I have updated the contract with what I have so far. There are a few areas I could use some suggestions on so feel free to mention them. Also I am considering Nyrufa's idea about using derangement (I was thinking for Dramatic Failure).

    ---

    Contracts of the Eldritch
    These contracts allow the Changeling to summon eldritch tentacles to do his bidding. The Changeling can then use these tendrils to attack or trap enemies. The fear of the eldritch has become your weapon and now all must either submit or lose their sanity.

    While there are many strange creatures in Arcadia not all of them agree with the ways of the Fae. Some are even willing to aid in the escape of Changelings if they so desire. However don’t be fooled while some of these eldritch creatures are willing to aid you they don’t appreciate being taken lightly.

    They are being of hatred and fear and if need be they won’t hesitate to turn that hatred against you if they believe you are wasting their time.

    Horrofic Shape (*)
    The Changeling mimics the shape of an eldritch beast. The being can look like anything but is generally composed of teeth, eyes and tentacles. Anyone who sees this shape suffers a -1 to Composure and Resolve rolls.

    Cost: 1 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Wyrd + Occult
    Action: Instant
    Catch: Any suggestions would be greatly appreciated.

    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: Instead of becoming what he attempted to mimic now the Changeling perceives all characters around him as the horrific monstrosity he tried to be. As a result the Changeling suffers -1 dice modifier to Composure and Resolve rolls (this last for two hours).
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: You are successful in mimicking the monster you wanted the intruder to perceive.
    Exceptional Success: Not only do you mimic the monster perfectly but intruder suffers a -2 to dexterity rolls as well.

    Suggested modifiers:
    1+: If the Changeling took on the form at night.
    1-: If the intruder’s virtue is Fortitude.

    Tendrils of Sight (**)
    The Changeling can summon tentacles to sprout forth from any surface. When they sprout forth from this surface an eye will appear in the middle. The Changeling can then walk away and observe what the eye sees from a distance, however to do so they must have their eyes covered in mud for ten minutes. Mortals have difficulty seeing the tendrils in the dark; however a mortal with either the Occult skill of three or the Intelligence attribute of three can notice it.
    Also if the tendrils are damage while the Changeling is observing what the tendrils see, the Changeling must roll as if he is in close combat with a single enemy.

    Cost: 2 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Wyrd
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The Changeling had the tendrils sprout from a body of water in a dark area. Also the Changeling must have mixed some of his blood into the mud that he applies to his eyes.

    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: Tentacles sprout forth from one of your eye sockets rendering that eye useless for a day. As long as you have this ailment you -1 die when using social skills.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: The Changeling is successful in summoning the Tendrils of Sight and they shall remain in that spot for two hours. The Changeling can then recall what the tendrils are seeing while being some distance away.
    Exceptional Success: The image the Changeling observes from the Tendrils of Sight is much sharper and in greater detail.

    Suggested Modifiers:
    1+: The surface is within the Changelings Dwelling.
    1-: The surface is illuminated.

    Eldritch Gaze (***)
    The Changeling opens one of his eyes to reveal in its place what appears to be the eye of an eldritch being. Individuals being looking at by the Changeling’s eye feel as if they are being touched by multiple tentacles and find it difficult to move.
    However this ability is risky and can only be used safely for five minutes. Once five minutes are exceeded every two minutes the Changeling must have three success when rolling five dice otherwise they will lose a Clarity.
    The Changeling may stop using the ability by either covering or closing the eye that is being used for this effect.

    Cost: 2 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Occult + Wyrd – the invader’s Resolve
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The eye has been covered up and hasn’t seen the light of day for three days.

    Roll Results:
    Dramatic Failure: The eldritch being to whom the eye belongs is displeased and as a result the Changeling loses one Clarity and for the next five minutes is -2 when it comes to dexterity rolls.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: The invader is now afraid of the Changeling and suffers a -1 to stamina and athletic rolls. This applies until the Changeling cancels this ability.
    Exceptional Success: Any suggestions would be greatly appreciated.

    Suggested Modifier:
    1+: The invader is looking directly into the Changeling’s eyes.
    1+: If the Changeling’s vice is Wrath.
    1-: The invader’s virtue is faith.

    Entangled Reach (****)
    The Changeling summons forth tentacles the sprout from his arms. The Changeling can use the tentacles to either grab something or to attack an enemy.

    Cost: 3 Glamour
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Wyrd – the invader’s Stamina
    Action: Instant
    Catch: The changeling cuts his arms using a razor so that the tentacles have an area to sprout forth (1 lethal damage). Also the flesh was cut within a dark location.

    Roll Results:
    Dramatic Failure: The tendrils sprout forth and immediately attempt to strangle the Changeling that summoned them. After the Changeling has taken 2 lethal point of damage the tentacles will then disintegrate.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: Allows the Changeling a chance to grab items that are ten feet away. It also allows the Changeling to engage in close combat at ten feet with a +1 to Dexterity but a -1 to Strength. Any damage rolls dealt to the Changeling at ten feet as a result of close combat (with the tentacles) is -2 but if the attacker is attacking the Changeling in close combat (with the actual Changeling) they gain a +1 dice modifier.
    Exceptional Success: Instead of immediately disappearing after combat the tentacles ensnare the target reducing the intruder’s dexterity by 2 as long as they are ensnared by the tendrils.

    Suggested Modifiers:
    +1: The knife had previously been used to damage the target.
    +1: The Changeling and the Intruder are in a dark area.
    -1: Either the Changeling or the Intruder is in an illuminated area.

    Damsel of the Eldritch Tendrils (*****)
    The Changeling summons the figure of a female woman, who calls out to the intruder. The illusion will attempt to lure the intruder into an embrace. If successful the intruder will not only realize the illusion but find that they ensnared in your tentacles.
    The invader must be within three feet in order for the Damsel’s tentacles to be able to grapple him. Also the ability only lasts for one hour.

    Cost: 3 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Wyrd + Occult – the invader’s Resolve
    Action: Instant
    Catch: You summoned the Damsel in a puddle of water that has been mingled with your blood and is surrounded by shadows. Also the Changeling has a photo or memento of the female he wants the Damsel to mimic.

    Roll Results:
    Dramatic Failure: Instead the Damsel calls out to you in an attempt to lure you instead. Also if the intruder wasn’t aware of your location they do now.
    Failure: Nothing happens.
    Success: The Damsel is summoned successfully. If the Damsel succeeds in luring the intruder into an embrace the intruder must roll for close combat (strength + brawl). In order for the invader to escape they must roll Dexterity and Athletics.
    Exceptional Success: The Damsel is summoned and if the Damsel is successful in luring the intruder the tendrils gains a +1 to Brawl.
    Suggested Modifers:
    +1: The intruder is unaware of your presence.
    -1: The intruder observed you summon the Damsel.
    -2: The Damsel was summoned in a brightly lit room.
    +1: If the intruder’s vice is Lust.

    ---

    If you have any suggestions on how to improve this contract or how to put it better into game terms please feel free to let me know.

    Thank you for reading. I look forward to your feedback.

          Mechanically, I think it's overpowered.

          Tendrils of Sight: Too powerful. Having a magical always-on spy-eye that you can observe from/remember at will and that mortals can't see is too powerful, and a day is too long, for a one-dot. I'd expect this at a three-dot, at least. For nerfing it down, have it last a shorter amount of time, lose the "hard for mortals to see" bit, and require a "come back to get the eye" before viewing what it saw. (Mirrors 2 is the closest parallel I can find, and that requires mirrors at both ends, and only lasts for a scene.)

          Barrier of Tentacles: No range or area is given for where the barrier is or what it covers, and there's no time limit for how long it lasts. All Dexterity at -2 feels too powerful, and closer to a 4-dot. (Comparing to Thorns and Brambles 3: area is limited by Wyrd and successes; the effects penalize Speed and Athletics rolls by successes) Finally, the listed Modifiers imply that there will always be either a +1 or -1 to the roll (there's nothing really between dark and illuminated).

          Protective Tendril: Waaaaaay too powerful. There's no auto-block powers in Changeling. Increasing Defense/Dodge would be fine, but an auto-block is too much. (Maybe it might work as a Goblin Contract, but as a four- or five-dot with a truly terrible drawback.) And the exceptional should not do auto-damage; see below. Finally, I think there's a typo in the Catch.

          Entangled Reach: Waaaaaaay too powerful. Nothing does auto-damage like this. (Elements 2, to compare, does damage to weapons that hit you while it's up, but not to other people, and requires a roll in order to harm others.) I could see the tentacles acting as an ensnarement, but there needs to be mechanics on how to escape the snare. Alternatively, the tentacles could provide a bonus to grappling, brawling, or touch attacks (though it may be more appropriate at a lower dot level if it only does one of those things). Finally, normally a Catch like this would detail an amount of damage done to ones arms in order to use it (1 lethal, perhaps).

          Damsel of the Eldritch Tendrils: Odd combination of too powerful/not powerful enough. Auto-damage, again, isn't a thing; everyone always gets a roll to try and avoid stuff like this. I could see this power working better as "eldrich lady that distracts everyone in the room and lures them closer, only to be grappled after x rounds/when in x yards of the apparition." And then have the foes be ensared/attacked/whatever. Having the apparition attack is fine (if uninspired), but either she has to roll to hit, or the target gets to roll to avoid. Finally, you should say what roll would be required "in order to escape", possible Dexterity + Athletics? Maybe Strength + Athletics? But it should be said explicitly.


          Thematically, I don't think the flavor fits well; it feels very limited in theme. All the descriptions seem very monotone: every one can be boiled down to "put some tentacles on it!" Other contracts are more variation in their appearances. I'm also puzzled why the first dot lets you see things; eyeballs coming out of nowhere is Lovecraftian, but not very tentacle-y. If you want the power to be called Tendrils, I would expect more tentacles/ivy/vines as a theme, rather than Cthulhu. I'd also expect at least one growth-power, maybe a hold-person-power rather than a cause-damage power, and possibly a break-through-walls (creeping vines breaking a stone wall apart) or sneak-in-places (thin tendrils fitting through cracks you wouldn't expect).

          On the other hand, if you want to create an elder-god-inspired set of powers, you could keep most of the powers (I'd drop two and three for something more thematic), rename it to Contracts of Eldrich or something.

          I do like the potential of the Damsel of the Eldrich Tendrils, and I think that there's nothing in existing books that is very similar.

                  Generally speaking, I would make the powers more limited before increasing costs or making failures worse. I'm not sure if you'd like counter-proposals and other ideas for powers for these contracts, or if you'd just like feedback on what's there. I'll stick to the latter for now.

                  Tendrils of Sight: The proposed mechanics are much more complicated. It is currently at a one-dot power level (since you lose your actual sight in order to use it, and it doesn't last for very long). However, I still don't like the arbitrary "mortals can't see it" part; I can't think of anything else that works like this (Smoke 5 lets you be invisible, but that's to everyone, and it's a fifth-dot power). As I understand it, Contract powers are one of the few things the Mask doesn't cover (as in, if you shoot a harmful beam of sun with Eternal Summer 5, onlookers see you doing the equivalent of a Care Bare stare, with all the resulting Integrity/Morality/Clarity hits).

                  Protective Tendril: Having the tentacle act as Cover, granting Armor, or adding to Defense are all ways this could be re-done with the same goal, if you wanted to keep it.

                  Entangled Reach: I'm not entirely clear on what the changes mean (except the grab things 10 feet away; that's clear). What do you mean by "engage in close combat"? Melee combat? Getting +1 to Dexterity and -1 to Strength is punishing most Brawl or Weaponry fighters (who all use Strength, unless they take one particular merit), and Firearms get penalties already in that close of range. I don't understand the damage rolls part at all. Is this still contested by Composure?

                  Damsel of the Eldrich Tendrils: How long does this last? Can the Damsel target only one person a turn? Can they change targets? How are they dispelled? How do you tell if the target has fallen for the illusion or not? I would compare this to something like Vainglory 4: the activation is a contested roll between the Changeling and onlookers (humans get one roll for all present, based on the highest stats, while supernaturals get one roll each); the Changeling needs to get at least one success for the power to work at all, but the targets' rolls determine how they're affected; lasts for a scene or until dispelled. The Damsel is different in that it's not an area of effect, but one created thing attacking one target at a time. (Side note: if it's an illusion, why/how does it cause damage?)

                  (Unrelated to the mechanics: I would change the names of each level so they don't have the word Tendrils, and don't have repeating phrases; call the first one The Eyes Have It or Distant Sights, the last one Damsel of the Eldrich or Eldrich Damsel, etc.)

                                      I feel like the adjustments you’ve made to some of these don’t quite work from a power-scaling perspective. I’ve come up with a counter-proposal of the powers (different order, replaced middle power, simplified mechanics) as well as details as to why I think they work better this way.

                                      Contracts of the Eldritch
                                      While all Contracts are made with outside powers, most are easy to understand. Emotions, objects, animals, darkness, even magical constructs make a kind of sense. But there are strange, distant powers, too, ones that mere humans cannot hope to fully view, much less understand. If these eldritch powers had emotions, they could be said to be consumed by hatred and fear, but they go beyond that, into something pulsing with power and strangeness. Contracts of the Eldritch tap into this utter inhumanity, granting their wielders a measure of their strength.

                                      Note: All Contracts of the Eldritch are Clarity 4 sins when used in front of non-magical humans, rather than the usual 6.

                                      Reasoning: The existing description talked mostly about tentacles, which are no longer the major presence in the powers. I added the Clarity drops because these powers should beget madness with their use, but shouldn’t cause an auto-drop (nothing in 1e causes auto-drops AFAIK).

                                      Eye See You (*)
                                      The Changeling causes a single, unblinking eye to sprout forth from any surface. This eye may be looked through by the Changeling that placed it, as though it were their own.

                                      Cost: 1 Glamour
                                      Dice Pool: Wyrd + Occult
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Catch: The Changeling creates the eye underwater.

                                      Roll Results
                                      Dramatic Failure: No eye is created. Tentacles sprout forth from one of the user’s eyes, rendering that eye useless for a scene. The user suffers a -2 to visual perception checks.
                                      Failure: No eye is created.
                                      Success: The eye appears where placed. This eye lasts for an hour, or until it takes a single point of damage. While it exists, the Changeling who placed the eye can look through it at any time, from any distance, by closing their eyes and concentrating.
                                      Exceptional Success: The eye lasts until the next sunrise or sunset.

                                      Suggested Modifiers:
                                      +1: The surface is within the Changeling’s dwelling.
                                      -1: The surface is well-lit.

                                      Reasoning: I liked this as a 1-dot power; it works a little like Reflections 2, but not as versatile or long-lasting. I dropped the time limit down to one hour, the cost to 1 Glamour, changed the name (because my version lacks tentacles), changed the pool (Occult made more sense to me for this one) and simplified what needed to be done to see through the eye. I also dropped the “only certain mortals can see it” part, because no other contract works like that. I changed the catch to be simpler and clearer (and dropped the mud bit), and changed the Exceptional to extend the duration.

                                      Horrific Shape (**)
                                      The user appears inhuman, somehow wrong. Maybe their legs have too many joints, or their arms flow like tentacles, or extra mouths seem to grin from under their hair. The strangeness cannot be precisely pinned down, but does make the Changeling better at frightening their foes and rivals.

                                      Cost: 1 Glamour
                                      Dice Pool: Wyrd + Intimidation
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Catch: The user spends five minutes repeating some menial physical task (sweeping the same patch of floor, polishing the same spot on a table, digging out the same hole) while staring at another person.

                                      Roll Results
                                      Dramatic Failure: The user changes to be inhuman, but only internally. The user takes one Lethal damage from the trauma wrought on their body.
                                      Failure: The user appears as they did before.
                                      Success: The user’s Mask and Mien shift to become unheimlich and strange for a scene. The user gets a bonus equal to successes when making Intimidation or other fear-causing checks.
                                      Exceptional Success: No further benefit beyond more successes.

                                      Suggested modifiers:
                                      +1: Contract is being used at night.

                                      Reasoning: Shape-changing in other Contracts tends to be at 4-dots (partial) or 5-dots (complete). Flavor-wise, this power was almost the same as Fleeting Autumn 5, though that has harsher mechanical punishments for the target.
                                      I changed this power to be more of a general uncanny appearance, rather than something specific (so it didn’t overlap Fleeting Autumn 5 as much). I took inspiration from Fleeting Spring 3 (look like someone your target wants to see) and Vainglory 4 (scare everyone with how you appear). I changed the benefits to be in line with a 2-dot power, dropped the second suggested modifier, changed the pool (since it’s related to Intimidation, that made sense for the pool), and added a catch.

                                      Thirty White Horses (***)
                                      The user summons a giant eldritch appendage to protect them, usually a toothy maw, but sometimes a wall of tentacles or dome of wings. This barrier gives the Changeling brief respite from their foes, although it doesn’t last very long.

                                      Cost: 2 Glamour
                                      Dice Pool: Resolve + Wyrd
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Catch: Both the user’s blood and an enemy’s blood have been spilled on this ground within the past day.

                                      Roll Results:
                                      Dramatic Failure: The barrier is summoned, but in the wrong location, and lasts for three turns (as a success). It may protect one of the Changeling’s enemies, trap one of their friends, or be similarly inconvenient.
                                      Failure: No protection is created.
                                      Success: A protective barrier surrounds the user, and lasts for three rounds. The barrier drops at the end of the third round after summoning. The barrier starts one foot away from the Changeling in all directions, including above and below, and does not move. It blocks all light, but not air, and acts as full concealment with Structure and Durability equal to successes.
                                      Exceptional Success: As a success, and the user can choose to drop the barrier early.

                                      Suggested Modifier:
                                      +1: The contract is used on a night of a new moon.
                                      -1: The contract is used inside a building with a floor and ceiling.

                                      Reasoning: Given the changes to the former Horrific Shape, and that there’s already another eye-power, Eldritch Gaze felt a bit redundant. So I redid the old Barrier of Tentacles to draw on the third appendage of the eldritch, mouths/teeth. (The other two being eyes and tentacles, which have powers of their own.)
                                      It’s named for a riddle (“Thirty white horses upon a red hill; first they tromp, then they stomp, then the stand still”) and was based on Elements 2 and Thorns and Briars 4. I could see this one being traded with Entangled Reach, but it might need a slight power boost (and the latter a slight nerf) if that were done.

                                      Entangled Reach (****)
                                      Tentacles spring forth from the user, grabbing and pinning the Changeling’s target. The target must start out within melee range of the user.

                                      Cost: 2 Glamour
                                      Dice Pool: Manipulation + Wyrd – target’s Dexterity
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Catch: The changeling slashes their arms using a razor, causing one Lethal damage, and bids the tentacles to spring forth from the bleeding wounds. When using the catch, the user cannot move out of melee range of the target; if they do, the tentacles immediately disintegrate.

                                      Roll Results:
                                      Dramatic Failure: The tendrils sprout forth and immediately attempt to strangle the Changeling that summoned them. The user takes two Lethal damage before the tentacles disappear.
                                      Failure: No tentacles appear.
                                      Success: Tentacles spring from the user, and immediately grab the target. The target is considered grappled and held by the tentacles. The target loses their Defense against all attacks, and halves their Speed. The target may attempt to break free with Strength + Brawl, needing cumulative successes exceeding the user’s successes in activating the power. The tentacles last for the scene, or until broken out of. The user may move away from the target (if they have not used the catch); the tentacles detach from the user and continue holding the target.
                                      Exceptional Success: As a success, and the target is immobilized: they have no Defense, no Speed, and cannot take any action requiring their arms or legs except to try to break free.

                                      Suggested Modifiers:
                                      +1: The user and target are in an asylum.
                                      -1: The air is dry and hot.

                                      Reasoning: The benefits from this one were complicated. I changed it to simply grapple and pin the target. I also changed the modifiers; the former ones were so broad one of them always applied. Changed the penalty to be target’s Dexterity (because Stamina made less sense), dropped the cost to 2 Glamour (because it’s not that absurdly powerful).

                                      Damsel of the Eldritch Tendrils (*****)
                                      The Changeling summons a beautiful creature that draws in onlookers. When the enraptured are close enough, though, the illusion drops, revealing the summoned creature is actually a thing of squamous tentacles.

                                      Cost: 4 Glamour + 1 Willpower
                                      Dice Pool: Wyrd + Occult vs each target’s Resolve + Composure
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Catch: The Damsel is summoned in a pool of water mixed with a pint of the user’s blood.

                                      Roll Results:
                                      Dramatic Failure: Hideous laughter is heard as the Damsel scorns the call. The user is targeted by the Entangled Reach power with a basic success.
                                      Failure: Nothing is summoned.
                                      Success: A beautiful being appears in front of the user. If a target rolls the same or more successes than the user, they see the creature, but feel uncompelled by it. If a target rolls fewer successes, they are compelled to move towards the summoned being at normal speed, if able. (A target who dramatically fails moves at a run.)
                                      The Damsel takes no action until touched, attacked, or surrounded by at least four people. At that point, the Damsel shifts to appear as a terrible mass of tentacles, eyes, and mouths, constantly shifting. Everyone within 5 yards of the Damsel is targeted by Entangled Reach, with successes equal to the successes rolled on the initial power. The Damsel remains for a scene, or until she is no longer holding any targets.
                                      Exceptional Success: No further benefit beyond multiple successes.

                                      Suggested Modifiers:
                                      +1: No one by the user sees the Damsel being summoned.
                                      -1: The summoning room is lit by the sun.

                                      Reasoning: While I love the imagery that Damsel of the Eldritch Tendrils implies, I opened up the description to allow for more sorts of seductive beings. Further, this power affects multiple targets, so I changed the pool and modifiers to reflect that. I also changed it from having no stats and infinite attacks to being an AoE version of the 4-dot power.
