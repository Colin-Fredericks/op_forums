        Imp: In the old days, parents used to scare children with stories of monsters taking them away if they misbehaved. But details on what happens to those children afterward are vague at best. A Keeper creates an Imp because they find a child's behavior to be unacceptable and wish to raise them in a properly disciplined environment. Unfortunately the child is not very pleased with the sudden change in their lives and found ways to vent their frustrations upon others without being caught. One day, they had enough of their Keeper's harsh child rearing and ran away from home.


        Blessing: Feigned Innocence - In order to avoid punishment, Imps became very good at throwing suspicion off themselves. They gains the 8 again benefit on any subterfuge rolls for the purpose of hiding their true intentions. By spending a point of Glamour, the Imp can add their Wyrd to Stealth rolls in peaceful areas.



        Ogre - Although small in stature, the Changeling possesses demonic features including a pair of horns, fangs and bright red skin. The day of their escape came when they finally could not take anymore lectures and punishments, so they lashed out in a violent temper tantrum.


        Fairest - The face of an angel, the mind of a devil. The Imp appears innocent and charming on the outside, but possesses a mean streak. Their escape came as a surprise (to their Keeper) who believed the Changeling had been successfully reformed.


        Wizened - A dirty, unkempt child dressed in tattered rags. Forced to work long hours in an effort to build a healthy work ethic, their childhood exuberance was replace with spite and hatred. They finally managed to escape by sabotaging the work station and taking advantage of the chaos.

Once per scene, you may spend a point of Glamour to automatically gain the Informed Condition for a Socialize or Politics roll related to authority, the chain of command, or other exercises of formal social power.                                 Mimic: In the world of Arcadia, there are many treasures and artifacts of power that rival Gentry plot to steal from each other in a bid to increase their own status. Some clever Gentry came up with the idea to plant decoys in order to lure thieves to their doom and ensure their own security. Changelings who are turned into Mimics spent their Durance as a living booby trap, taking on the forms of valuable treasures or household objects, only to transform into monstrous figures once the unwary came within reach.

                                Blessing: The Mimic is a master of disguise, especially when ambushes are involved. They gain a +3 bonus on attempts to successfully conceal their identity from others. By spending a point of Glamour, the Mimic can disguise themselves as objects or valuables of a size rating equal to their natural size or smaller. This disguise is purely cosmetic and does not effect the Mimic's actual capabilities. A Mimic who becomes a sword does not take on its properties, for example. Thus it would be useless as a weapon in battle.


                                Darkling - A mysterious figure dressed head to toe in dark clothing. A series of porcelain masks are strapped to its clothes, each one depicting a different facial expression


                                Elemental - A torso made of fine silk with a golden rope tied around the neck. The silk has holes in the sides and bottom where green, scaly limbs emerge from, while a grotesque head pops out the top of the bag.


                                Fairest - A beautiful maiden who appears to be wearing a giant pile of money. Her dress is sewn together from golden coins while priceless jewels adorn her head, arms and feet.

                                    Toon: Slapstick comedy is often amusing to watch, but have you ever considered how the characters feel when you drop a safe on them? These Changelings do, because they experienced it first hand. Forced to take the role of comic book characters and television shows, the Toons spent their durance entertaining their Keepers by being subjected to painful and humiliation situations. One day they finally broke the 4th wall and escaped back into the real world!

                                    Blessing: Toons are difficult to kill, they should be with all the slapstick humor they were put through in their Durance. Toons possess a natural armor rating of 2/1 for the purposes of resisting damage. By spending a point of glamour, the Toon is able to ignore wound penalties for the rest of the scene.


                                    Fairest - A living anime character, the Changeling appears more physically attractive than any natural human should be. Their hair might be smooth as silk, or branch out in spiky tufts, and might also be abnormal coloration such as blue or green.


                                    Ogre - An action cartoon from the 90's, the Changeling is intended to look extreme or awesome but comes across as corny or ridiculous by modern standards. They put more emphasis on acting tough and looking cool than actually thinking about the situation at hand.


                                    Elemental - A children's public broadcast program, the Changeling resembles an inanimate object brought to life and given anthropomorphic features. Their very existence stirs up questions about the nature of life and sentience to those who are interested enough to consider it.
