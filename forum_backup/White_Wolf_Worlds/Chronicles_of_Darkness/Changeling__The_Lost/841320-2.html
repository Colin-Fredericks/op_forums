                        Mr. Ghast

                        Concept: Reformed Boo

                        Kith: Gravewight

                        Seeming: Elemental

                        If he's being honest, Ghast quite likes his new name, and prefers it over his old one (though he still keeps it around for good measure, wearing it like a protective amulet against the Big Nasties out there in the modern bureaucratic world). He was taken by the lord of a decrepit manor in the middle of a misty wood, a Keeper who was fascinated by the concept of ghosts, specifically, ghosts as ahistorical, mischievous spirits. Prankster wights. Fun-loving haunters. Granted, in Faerie, practical jokes can be incredibly lethal, and the falling chandeliers, bloodthirsty pianos, and artfully-weakened floorboards over spike pits were constant dangers to visitors.

                        Mr. Ghast was turned into a ghost. He doesn't think he was killed, per se; the Gentry merely reshaped his body into a more ethereal form. Some days, he was possessing an armchair to wrap itself around an unwary guest. Other times he appeared in the reflection of a cracked mirror, blood streaming from his eyeless sockets. The Ghost Lord loved nothing more than to lure travelers to the manor and see how long they would last in the spook house. Those unfortunates were often drafted as new recruits in the Ghost Lord's domain.

                        There was always a way out, though, some trick to solving the house's puzzles and traps and getting out alive on the other side. Maybe the Ghost Lord had a sense of fairness, or maybe it was a Catch in the Contracts that held his domain together. Whatever the case, there was always a way for the clever and resourceful to get out alive. Ghast became obsessed with one such hopeful, a changeling sent on as a diplomatic envoy be a foreign Keeper. He followed her around the manor, unseen, manipulating events just so, to give her a fighting chance of surviving. She came out the other side, frightened, shaken, a long cut down her left arm, but unmangled, still alive and with all her internal organs.

                        Infused with essence of the ghost house, and with a sudden burst of memory of being something other than a spectre, Mr. Ghast found himself outside, in the Hedge. From there, he beat a path back home to Earth and too-solid flesh.

                        Nowadays, Ghast is trying to figure himself out. He still enjoys scaring people, to an extent, but malevolent deception is a Clarity Breaking Point for him. Hurting others through misdirection reminds him of his time in the ghost house, and how nothing could ever truly be what it seemed. But on the other hand, it's what he's good at. Perhaps the Scarecrow Ministry will take him, one day, if he proves himself. He's also been learning some things about ghosts--real ghosts, the ones with histories and survivors they left behind. Most are fully uninterested in pranks. This is new to him. Maybe he can provide them with a home, a special haunt of their own, a safe space to gather and remember their lives.

                                                Name: BunBun

                                                Concept: Strip Club Owner

                                                Kith: Dancer

                                                Seeming: Beast

                                                Description: Covered in bright pink fur with long, floppy ears and a short, fluffy tail, BunBun resembles an anthropomorphic bunny. His physical build is so girly, he can be mistaken for a transgender at first, but the stern expression on his lapin face makes it clear he is in absolutely no mood for jokes. His mask resembles a young man with the build of an acrobat and with pink dyed hair. He is frequently dressed in black leather / latex outfits.


                                                Details: The subject of a cruel joke by his Keeper, the one known as BunBun was possessed of a violent temperment and controlling attitude. So when he was taken away, his Keeper transformed his body into a harem boy, who's features were cute and effeminate. Being forced to dance for his much stronger master, BunBun's aggressive tendencies only grew until they finally exploded in a violent outburst. During his last performance, he delivered a flying drop kick to his Keeper's groin and proceeded to leap out a window to freedom, crashing through the decorative glass and landing in the real world.

                                                Despite having a body that many would consider harmless, BunBun is both respected and feared by the rest of the Freehold. Since his escape, he has become the proprietor of a bisexual strip club (Sweet Release) that caters to supernatural clientele. He rules this establishment with an iron fist and cracks down hard on anybody caught disrupting the flow of business in his club. He has sent many trouble makers to the hospital already and is not shy about doing so again. The employees who work for him claim that they are safer with BunBun than they are on the street, but he can be a demanding task master in exchange for the protection he offers.

                                                BunBun is the leader of his Motley (named after his club) for more than just his leadership skills. The rest of the Motley actually work at Sweet Release, which makes him their boss since he owns the place. Not every worker at the club is a member, but enough of them are that he's able to keep tabs on the club's activities.

                                                    Name: Pasop

                                                    Concept: Street Snitch

                                                    Kith: Troll + Minstrel

                                                    Seeming: Darkling

                                                    Description: In his mien, Pasop exhibits the body of a cartoon meerkat straight out of a Disney production who stands at a scrawny 4'0 tall. His eyes are often scanning the environment as if he's expecting something to jump out at him at any moment. When in his mask, he appears as a dark skinned boy with short black hair. He is fond of wearing baggy pants as he claims they fit better in his mien. He is also known to wear a recon vest over his shirt, which he keeps packed with supplies in case he needs them.

                                                    Details: It is not uncommon for the True Fae to find pleasure in mortal entertainment, especially when they have no imagination of their own. This was the case with Pasop's former master who had a great love for cartoon animals. More specifically, it enjoyed worlds in which the animals were perfectly sentient, yet still hunted each other for food. After he was taken, Pasop awoke to find he had been dumped in a scenery that looked like it belonged in The Lion King. He also quickly noticed that he was now in the body of a cartoon meerkat and only 12 inches tall!

                                                    During his stay in Arcadia, Pasop had two roles that his Keeper demanded of him. The first role was that he act as sentry for his meerkat colony, warning them of incoming predators so they could take shelter in their underground burrows. The second was that he was expected to be able to participate in spontaneous musical numbers that often came without any provocation or rehearsal. Needless to say, this was a very embarrassing time in his life, but he managed to escape one day when he refused to warn the colony of an attack. Instead, when Pasop detected the predators, he fled to safety while leaving the others behind as a sacrifice to appease the hungry carnivores, eventually finding his way back through the Hedge and into the world of humanity once more.

                                                    Pasop's name means "Lookout" in Afrikaans, which is appropriate given his role there. Since his escape, he has resumed this service by prowling the streets of the Freehold in search of dangerous predators (like the Huntsmen) and informing nearby security of encroaching threats. He is also constantly bragging about his new mix tape and encouraging people to listen to it!

                                                    Name: Hex

                                                    Kith: Witchtooth

                                                    Seeming: Beast

                                                    Description: Her mien appears almost human with the exception of having the eyes, tail and ears of a black cat. Both in her mien and mask, she wears Gothic attire consisting of a fishnet outfit worn underneath a black tank top and skirt. She also wears buckle strapped boots that go up to her thighs, and black gloves that extend to her elbows. Her hair is long and black, and she wears dark eyeliner to give her eyes a more feline style. Her physical appearance suggests she is 16 years of age.

                                                    Details: Hex's Keeper found her during her goth phase that was common among angsty teenage girls. Normally the curses she tried to place on people were just a way to vent her frustration and get on with it. She couldn't have known that last spell would actually get the attention of an otherworldly being! Intrigued by her performance, the Fae promised to satisfy her grudge while teaching her about real magic. Hex agreed, but didn't realize the cost of her bargain until too late.

                                                    True to its word, her Keeper took her back home and began to instruct her in the use of magic, but the spells she had her instructed in were of the black arts. Among some of the more shocking rituals she took part in included stealing the life force of children, blood sacrifices, and whoring her body out to demons! This wasn't the kind of witchcraft that Wiccans believed in, this was the witchraft that normal people believed in. She had to escape, and after months of practice, she developed the spell that would enable her to do so.

                                                    Hex discovered a spell that would enable her to change her form, presumably to avoid her Keeper's notice while she fled to the human world. She chose the form of a black cat (she still doesn't know why) and slunk off into the night while her Keeper was preparing another ritual. Upon her return to the human world, Hex attempted to undo the spell but found it was significantly harder than before. She ended up fainting from exhaustion only to awaken later in a Freehold infirmary. The stress of the miscast spell caused her to suffer amnesia, forgetting her real name. She also forgot most of the magic she learned with her Keeper, including how to fully regain her human form, leaving her as a partially transformed hybrid. Yet despite losing most of her accumulated knowledge, she retains a natural talent for the occult and with the help of the Freehold has begun to relearn what she lost.
