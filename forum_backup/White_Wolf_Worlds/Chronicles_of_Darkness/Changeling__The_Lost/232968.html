    Changeling 2.0 - what do you want to do with your Contracts

    08-18-2014, 01:19 PM
    So while the Kiths/Seeming spew is generating interesting discussions; I thought I'd start a space for people to spew what cool faerie powers they want to be able to use in game. Is there a cool power you saw on TV or in a faerie tale; put it down here.


    Here's my ideas.

    I'd like a power that lets you vanish, but people think you're still there until they look. I'm thinking of Fools Gold from the League of S.T.E.A.M (comedy aside, that's exactly how I imagined a hunter vs fae fight would go) where the Leprechaun teleports away and the Hunter grappling him dosen't realise until he looks.

    Illusion powers; from large mirages to just changing your voice.

    Magic Mirrors; I'm a big fan of magic mirrors and at the least I'd like the ability to spy on someone, to teleport from mirror to mirror, and the classic "who's the fairest of the all?" Prophecy and postcognation would be nice too.

    Prophecy in general. I'd like both sane and mad oracles to be an option for PCs.

      Powers to transform other people: to me, their absence in C:tL has always felt very conspicuous, given they're the first things that come to mind when I think of fairy tales and fairies. True, it would likely need to interact in a particular way with Clarity, and hold a certain social significance; knowing someone even has such a power would probably be an enormous trigger condition for many Lost.

      Powers for a sense of normalcy in others: dunno if this is too specific, and I know a few current clauses already touch on it incidentally (like the one in Vainglory that puts out an impressive vision of the mien, which people just accept, and then forget afterward), but something to make ordinary mortals perceive any supernatural weirdness around them as relatively normal, and possibly dismissed or rationalized as a dream afterward, has the right feel. There are too many fairy tales where the protagonists hear beasts or the wind or whatever speak to them and just take it in stride. Perhaps with more surgical versions, so that single magical things/traits/actions seem unworthy of comment to all onlookers or even applying that to something mundane and out of place in a social setting (going to a fancy dress party decked out for battle) could be good space for it. To use a name from TVTropes: Unusually Uninteresting Sight as a power.

      Instilling consciousness and/or speech (and animation) to objects, beasts and the like: may already be done, but this is far too cool, with far too much potential to explode in a careless Changeling's face.


      Hmm... Somewhat related, but I wonder what's happening with Pledges. I could see some Pledges that automatically use and renew the effects of a clause -- or hold the effects in reserve, perhaps as a punishment for breaking it. If that's a direction the team wants to go, thinking of ways to build it in now would be best. Using some of the above, perhaps a bargain struck with a mortal to raise his team of loyal guard dogs to intelligent, speaking creatures so long as he maintains it, but reduces him to a dog himself should he do something to violate it.
