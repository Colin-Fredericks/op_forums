                I think a part of that is just that huge lists are completely incomprehensible for some people. For all the unnecessary duplication in 1E Kiths, at the very least it whittled down the hundred-ish Kiths down to about a dozen or less for any individual character once you selected a seeming. It's one of the problems with Embeds in Demon as well: The possibility space is freaking enormous and lots of people get decision paralysis. I worry that the free contracts will have the same issue: While I didn't necessarily agree with the devs on the free-flowing vs tiered structure contract thing, it does make it more comprehensible at one end.

                Rough groupings of about a dozen kiths would probably do a LOT to make it more manageable - bite sized chunks all the way.

                        Decision paralysis in 1e wasn't really that great even with Kiths by Seeming, because there were overlapping Kiths and Dual Kith that completely muddied the water.

                        I think that if you have a decent idea in your head about what happened in your character's Durance, picking a Kith from the big list really isn't that hard. You should be able to skim and narrow it down to a most six without much fuss. I think the problem comes more from if you're looking to Kiths to inspire your character, because then it's harder to just narrow it down to the ones that fit what you already had in mind.

                        I'm not sure there's a practical way to group them. At least not in the text. Once the book is out, it shouldn't be that hard to plop them into a spreadsheet or something that allows for you to organize them interactively by assorting them according to different groupings to find a cluster that you're looking for more quickly.
