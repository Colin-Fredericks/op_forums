    Name: Alexander Sinsoul Seeming: Darkling Kith: Dearheart/Shadowsoul Court: Brass

    Merits: Dual Kith •••, Potent Kith ••••, Hedgespun token: Grimiore Sinsoul •, Court of Brass Mantle ••, Goblin Vow: Grimiores •.

    Pledges: Court of Brass, Toronto Freehold.

    Contracts: Control Elements: Light ••• (darkling effect), Inanimate Communion • (viewing user verison) (Darkling effect), Know the Competition • (Darkling effect).

    Mien: Alexander has horns of polished Brass, and a long tail with an arrow shaped tip. In the palms of his hands is Alexander's Sigil in glowing purple light. His Shadow is indistict with glowing eyes and its movements seem to be independant of Alexander's actions.

    Mask: a normal dude, brown hair and eyes.

    The Grimiore Sinshadow has Alexander's glowing sigil on it and it seems to be made of living flesh that moans obscenely when touched. The pages inside are also made of flesh, and they contain rites and secrets that have appeared in dreams Alexander's had or manipulated.

    Alexander created the tome from a dream he had in which it appeared. He combined its cobble thought bauble with a grimiore he bought at a used book store and the screaming orgasm of a young pretty Obrimos Mage he'd convinced to help him.

    Its mask is that of a leather bound volume with a generic pentagram on the cover.

    To get full value out of the Grimiore Sinshadow he uses his Goblin Vow: Grimiores.

    Alexander joined the Brass Court because he worships the Wyrd, believing all Gods and people, "real" or fiction, are aspects of the Wyrd.

    Keeper: Alexander was out bar hoping and trying to pick up chicks and failing horribly, when a radiant beauty walked into the bar he was in. He started chatting her up and for once things were going extremely well.

    She invited him to her home and he thought be was getting lucky. He couldn't have been more wrong.

    Sadly for Alexander she'd confused him with a skilled player and decided those skills would be useful in controlling her Changelings.

    Upon entering her bright kingdom she used her light to burn her sigils into his hands, both to enfuse him with her inhuman passions and to mark him as her property. This made him a Dearheart.

    She got careless at the end and unleashed a blast of light that left Alexander briefly blind with a memto, his shadow had taken on a life of its own.

    Alexander then seduced his Keeper, learned how to control light from her, and then used it against her.

    He created a hologram of himself leading her into a trap while he teleported via shadows out of her Kingdom.

    Upon emerging into the Hedge he was marked by his betrayal of his Keeper, growing horn and a tail. The sigils on his hands changed from his Keepers to ones that symbolize the evolving essence of his soul.

          High on a Hill Motley
          Sharing a Durance under a Gentry who fancied itself a shepherd, the High on a Hill Motley started out with the trio of "brothers" Bill, Lee, and Gruff. Greta joined later, after making it clear she had no wish to return to their Arcadian relationship (and bore them no ill will for their means of escape). Currently, they seek a new town after the collapse of the Bulwark in their old Freehold, as well as any fellow Changelings that have escaped the Lonely Goatherd.

          Bill Goat
          Fairest Steepscrambler (goats are nimble)
          Hedonist/Passionate
          The Motley owes their freedom to Bill. After he was captured and forced to be a goat, Bill spent a long time planning his escape. At first, he tried to rally the entire herd to come with him, but most were too far gone to care. But between Lee's knowledge and Gruff's might, he found the one bridge out of Arcadia guarded by one of the weaker trolls. He was able to trick the troll into letting him and Lee pass, with the promise that the next goat through would be much chubbier and tastier.
          Bill looks like nothing so much as a satyr: goat feet and tail, nubby horns, shorter than average, cock-sure grin, and a penchant for being shirtless. He gives the impression of being much taller than his five-foot stature is. He likes being involved, and throws himself wholeheartedly into whatever task catches his fancy. Sadly, his fancy is much easier to catch than sustain. He's still a little worried that Greta resents him for tricking her, even though it resulted in everyone's freedom.
          Contracts: Vainglory 2 (Splendor), Riddle-Kith 3
          Possible Courts: Spring, South, Jade, Snow, Pearls, Gold Rose, Garden

          Lee Goat
          Grimm Polychromatic (goats are stubborn)
          Impatient/Honest
          Lee spent the least time taken, or so they assume; they're the one in the Motley who remembers the most about the real world, and the only one who recalls their former name. This acuity came in handy during their escape; they saw the threads of the story of the Three Goats forming, and inserted themself into the role of the Middle Brother, knowing where the bridge out could be found.
          Lee is the most goat-like of the Trio: hairy body, rectangle pupils, jutting jaw, curving horns. There's something ill-defined about them; the middle brother tends to be overlooked, after all. Lee's good at research and finding patterns, but has a bit of a Cassandra complex; many warnings they give tend to go unheeded. It's a recurring (and frustrating) event where Lee will say something, no one will take heed, then Bill will repeat it and be praised.
          Contracts: Talespinning 3, Changeling Fate 2
          Possible Courts: Fall, North, Jade, Hart, Sand, Blue Rose, Swamp

          Gruff Goat
          Ogre Roteater (goats eat everything)
          Trusting/Kind
          Gruff spent the most time in Arcadia; he remembers almost nothing from before his Durance. He had made many escape attempts on his own, but until Bill lead the way, he always ended up getting "rescued" before he could beat his way free. He was the one to knock the troll off the bridge and into the water (after she had been tricked by the other two). Escape has done wonders for Gruff; while he still uses his muscles rather than his head, he avoids violence, preferring peaceful (if physical) solutions.
          Gruff is nearly seven feet tall and five feet wide, with huge curling horns, shaggy beard, and an almost palpable musky odor. He's very aware of how intimidating he is, but does his best to compensate. He's a soft touch, and always willing to help; this has led to him being taken advantage of repeatedly. He trusts Lee to point him in the right direction.
          Contracts: Might of the Terrible Brute 2, Shared Burden 3
          Possible Courts: Spring, West, Brass, Pine, Song, Red Rose, Garden

          Greta Sharptooth
          Elemental Gristlegrinder (eats goats)
          Gluttonous/Fair
          Greta was one of the many monsters in the Lonely Goatherd's lands; the usual thing would be for a goat or two to wander off, and either rescued and admonished by the shepherd, or eaten by a monster. Greta lurked near bridges, as that was prime goat territory, but always in fear of the river beneath. It wasn't until the Trio's escape knocked her in that she learned to go with the flow, letting the waters wash her out of Arcadia and into the Hedge.
          Greta's as tall as Gruff, but not quite as broad; blue-black water has replaced her arms and portions of her torso, while the rest of her body appears to be rough-shaped brown dirt. Her teeth are flinty and numerous, but carefully aligned and maintained. She tends towards boisterousness and self-indulgence. Sometimes she and Gruff gross everyone out in terrible-food-eating contests.
          Contracts: Control Elements [water] 3, Cloak of the Elements [water] 2
          Possible Courts: Summer, South, Ivory, Pine, Pearls, White Rose, Garden


          Shepard
          Loyalist Skogsra/Woodwalker (tends goats)
          Relentless/Kind
          Contrary to the Motley's beliefs, Shepard, the one that kept the trio and rescued goats from Greta, was a fellow captive. Since the escapes, Shepard has been tasked with returning the errant flock and troll. This confrontation almost happened in their last town, but the Motley's flight at the Bulwark's fall postponed the reunion. Soon, though, soon.
          At first glance, Shepard looks almost human, dressed in old-fashioned robe-like clothes appropriate to the job. Upon closer inspection, there's nothing underneath the clothes; it's cloth all the way through. Shepard is focused and wily; stubborn as any goat with a flock-tender's cunning. Shepard's voice is melodious, and sometimes lets out a warbling cry to communicate with other animals.
          Contracts: Nevertread 2, Thorns and Brambles 3 (Leechweed, Briarpatch)

                      I considered Broadback, but I ultimately liked the crunch from Polychromatic better. Looking just at mechanics, Broadback benefits are about resistance and toughness; Polychromatic are about not being compelled to change your mind or fall to influence. Also, I wanted to go with less-obvious options, which is why none of the trio are Beasts, either.

                      Greta's a troll, but not a Troll. Her Durance wasn't about telling riddles or protecting an area; it was about eating delicious goat. She didn't catch goats to protect the bridge; she lurked around the bridge to catch goats. If she picked up a Dual Kith, I'd choose Shadowsoul or Woodwalker, depending on if she was hiding under the bridge or in the woods nearby. (I'm also of the opinion that a character should have a minimum Wyrd before getting Dual Kith, hence why only Shepard got one.)
