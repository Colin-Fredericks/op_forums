Hell, even Lorelei expressed a keen desire that the fetch should die when we were talking downstairs. The look in her eyes mirrored the one in Calamity’s to some extent, which was not something I ever thought I’d see. It’s not something I wanted to see either. She has ever been the voice of reason, of humanity in the group. I know I don’t feel things the way she does. Honestly I don’t know if I can. The same goes for Calamity. She feels things very strongly, whether it’s anger or love. For me, everything is muted. There are the moments of sharp anger or affection, but it doesn’t seem to last. People used to say I was cold, and I know they were not trying to make a pun. I was efficient and unemotional when I executed my duties for the Lady. I didn’t really blink when I killed someone, unless I got blood in my eye. I just didn’t think about it. I performed by duty, and I did it well. I try to make myself believe that I am better than that now. In some ways I think I am just a bit. The death of the little girl wouldn’t have made an impact on me before, but now I am mad. I felt sorry for the girl whose life was cut short, and for the parents that will grieve, but mostly I just feel a desire to kill something. Is that progress? I don’t want to kill in cold blood, now it’s fueled by a desire for retribution.

                                                        To be honest I didn’t want to see the desire for violence in Gawain’s eyes either. He comes across as such a sweet and somewhat innocent guy. I know that he’s not without blood on his hands. I don’t know how many times he had to play out the Green Knight story, but it was enough. He doesn’t usually let that side show, but I could tell that he was angry and might possibly wish to inflict violence on the walking scrap pile. I do have to give him credit though. He was clearly angry and wanted to do something about it, but he stayed with Mary and Insincere Marguerite to keep them safe. Being a body guard is sometimes not the most exciting job, but it is very important and Gawain takes the safety of everyone in the House seriously. I admire him for that.

                                                        Once we discovered the car in the driveway we knew that we had to do something about it. Torrez told us that there were three squad cars parked nearby, ready to move in if need be. One of which was a K-9 unit. That made me think that I’d have to go back over the floor in Mary’s room with a strong cleaner. It might make it obvious that something was cleaned up, but the smell of blood would be worse. Maybe we can repaint a couple of rooms upstairs and use paint and turpentine to cover the smell? Would also be a good excuse for pulling out old rugs and bringing new ones in.

                                                        Anyhoo…the car. We quickly thought up and shot down a few ideas on what to do with the car. In the end it was decided that Calamity would drive off as fast as she could and plow the little sedan through the barrier on the Pacific Highway sending the car and its supposed passenger to a watery doom. She planned to open the door right before she went over and create a portal to the Hedge using the open doorway as a frame. I wasn’t keen on the idea, but Calamity insisted that she could do it. She also made the shadows a bit heavier in the car so it would be really difficult for the police to identify who was driving. I knew that Calamity could do it, and it did go off without a hitch, but I was still unsure that it was a wise idea. I didn’t voice that opinion though. We didn’t really have a better idea and I didn’t want to sow any doubt in Calamity’s mind.

                                                        Calamity returned soon enough through the gate in the actual hedge and let us know how everything went. She stayed for a while after Torrez arrived. He mainly checked his tablet and phone while we chatted amongst ourselves. I tried to be hospitable and offer to make drinks for everyone. Torrez turned down alcohol, Calamity did not. ‘Course, neither did I. I didn’t let myself have more than one drink though. I knew that Calamity was going to go to the Hedge to be with Gawain and make sure that Mary was safe as soon as she was able to. By that point De la Rocha had already taken Lorelei upstairs to get some rest. De la Rocha is good for Lori. The way they look at each other is sweet, and they are so devoted to one another. It’s good to have connections like that, someone that can understand you and fill a space that you cannot. Hopefully she will be able to comfort Lorelei and help her deal with the anger and horror I saw earlier. I wish I could let her un-see what was in that room. I would take that memory from her in a heartbeat if I could. Hell, I’d take it from the lot of them if I could. Calamity would not thank me for it if I did though. Not sure that Gawain would be happy either.

                                                        After a while it appeared that the police would not be making a visit to the Dream House. They thought that Mary went into the ocean with her car and were in the process of looking for a body. Torrez said something about finding a burned female body to use as a decoy for Mary. Something tells me that I don’t want to think too hard on where he would or could obtain such a thing. Too late. Thought about it. Still not going to ask though.

                                                        After Torrez left, Becky who had come by earlier also made her own way home. We assured her that we would continue to look after Mary and that it was no problem. I assured Calamity that I would keep an eye on Lorelei and De la Rocha so she could go to the Hollow. She nodded in thanks and took off. And so that’s why I find myself sitting on a folding chair in the upstairs hallway. I don’t think that the fetch would try for a second time today, but I wasn’t about to take any chances. I have a good vantage point here and can see Lori’s door and hear any noises from downstairs. When the others return I may see about trading out with someone so I can get some rest, but we’ll see about that when the time comes. I am prepared to stay up if the need should arise. I’ve done it before. I will make sure nothing gets by me while I’m on watch. Anything that tries will meet my blades, and I’m not in a very charitable mood right now.


                                                        Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

                                                        Looking back, I noted everything was probably going to go to hell, and I was right. Though it was not as bad as it could have been. Thankfully.

                                                        So the show went well. Madame Spidertoes was in the audience, with Moon and Dionisia, and I visited her briefly. She seemed pleased to see I. Flores was there with De La Rocha and Julia, as her club has not yet reopened. I visited them a time or two also. Lorelei got to speak to Princess Jolie, but still seemed glum about it. Calamity spoke to Spidertoes and seemed pleased about it. It was a good night overall. Generally.

                                                        I had planned on going to sit on the bell tower of the St. Monica’s Cathedral with Julia so she could sketch nearby buildings and late night pedestrians. Julia has some awesome little black and purple, um, mini binoculars? I am unsure the English name. I could ask Gawain but would probably confuse him. Anyway, Calamity went with us invited earlier, and on a lark I asked Jacob, who also likes sitting on the roof of tall buildings, to come as well. They both seemed happy to do so. Calamity especially was in an excellent mood.

                                                        I was as well. I wrapped myself around Julia, sitting on her shoulders some when she sat up or laying atop her as she stretched out on her stomach. I smelled her hair, and smiled. I looked at some of the dark, evocative sketches she had been doing and smiled again. I looked at my dear friends all around I and was content. Then Calamity’s phone rang, suddenly.

                                                        We were called back to the Dream House. It seems Mary’s Fetch had been there, and as before had undermined her sense of security by defiling her room. Calamity got back with great alacrity, and we found her clothes were impaled to her bed with knives, and a bucket of blood, and scalp of a skull, had been rigged to dump on her from her closet. Gawain had discovered, and thankfully mostly sidestepped, the booby trap. Her bloody clothes were there as well. I frowned and checked my phone, and found her Fetch had decapitated a child in view of it’s parents and fled with the head. The police had a suspect they were not commenting on, and there was a sketch of her, sans her large nose, naturally.

                                                        Lorelei was had mostly calmed her but Mary was getting mentally unstable. Her eyes showed her Fetches campaign of psychological warfare was working, and she was beginning to crack. I checked the mirror in her room, and found her Fetch had, in fact, used the Mirror to enter and leave the room. That her Fetch can mirror travel deeply concerns me. It is not proper to use my mirrors in my house to travel. Those mirrors and this house are mine! I should…probably not say that out loud. Definitely.

                                                        We decided to take Mary elsewhere. The police coming was a threat, and her Fetch could strike again. I took she and Gawain through the mirror in Lorelei’s room to her room in the Hollow. Once there, I broke that mirror. Regrettably.

                                                        I laid out Mary to sleep with the help of some chloroform for her own good, and have been sitting, writing, and chatting with Gawain, who has been standing a vigil. A few minutes ago Julia returned, noting Calamity was okay and talking to Lucian Torrez, and that Lorelei had gone to sleep.

Apparently she drove Mary’s car off the cliff on the Pacific Coast Highway while being chased by police and slid through the door into the Hedge. Lucian had determined the police were not coming and gone home. Becky Wan had come and gone. If we keep her from the mirrors Mary should be safe here. As for I, I am taking Julia to seem my room in the Hollow and leaving Mary to Gawain and Calamity. She is sleeping peacefully so it should be fine.

                                                        Hopefully.
