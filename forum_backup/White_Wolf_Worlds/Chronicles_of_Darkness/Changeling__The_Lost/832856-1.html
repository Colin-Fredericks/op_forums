                                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                        If you and your fetch have left each other alone long for enough that you're both Wyrd 10, there is a bigger problem at hand than the number of Echoes in the book.
                                                        You don't have to leave each other alone in order to reach Wyrd 10 without having resolved the fetch. I see no problem with an epic struggle between changeling and fetch that lasts all the way to the end. (Which is not to say that it's likely to happen; only that there isn't necessarily a problem if it does happen. The game certainly shouldn't assume that it won't happen, especially when the issue can be addressed by as little as one more Echo.)

                                                        Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                        Specious claim. There are more than five other categories of Traits and narrative qualities you can use to differentiate one fetch from another already and Mimic Contract alone can theoretically replicate any given Contract as a point of divergence from other fetches.
                                                        Nothing specious about it. Yes, there is some differentiation with a few of the Echoes; but my point is that by the time you reach Wyrd 9 as things are now, every fetch will have, e.g., Normalcy, Heart of Wax, and Summon Shard, even if such Echoes don't suit the fetch's concept — unless the Storyteller chooses to gimp the fetch in order to maintain conceptual integrity, or to make up new Echoes of his own.

                                                        As for the other categories of traits, oh frack. I had meant to specify "in terms of Echoes"; I was thinking it when I wrote up the post, but failed to put it into words. Mea culpa. Suffice it to say that I know that there's more to a fetch than its Echoes, but that my intent was to point out the near-equivalence of a high-Wyrd fetch's Echoes. Because, you now, you start running out of choices as you near the top end.
