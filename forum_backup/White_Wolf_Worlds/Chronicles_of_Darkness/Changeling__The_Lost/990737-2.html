                            This is my character's, his name is Victor Cowen
                            Victor was captured at the age of 10 by a member of the gentry while he was walking back home from Karate Practice, born a Yank he was living over seas due to his father’s job. The gentry that took him, Count Inderline of the Surtr Hold, had fallen into a recent gentry fad of games of living chess. The Gentry would stand in for the King, and all other pieces started as pawns. Should they cross and get promoted they were allowed to pick their new roles. This particular Gentry had been on a losing streak but when he saw a youth who was skilled with a spear he snapped up the boy as fast as he could. The rules of this game of chess varied from normal with one small difference. They had to actually best the enemy in battle to capture them and if they were defeated they were captured instead.

                            Victor was turned into a pawn and given a the basic gear of his new role, but also given the duty of training the other boys and girls how to fight, since the Count had better things to do. Victor’s strong sense of justice quickly lead him to be respected both as their teacher and as one who would always be by their side. He was well loved by the others both for his handsome face and kind instruction. Yet at other times when they were in danger his wrath would help him in protecting the younger and less experienced pieces.

                            Soon his team started to do better, though going against more complete teams was still hard. But soon they were putting enemies in check, and Victor fell in love with Charity. One day he hatched a plan and they arranged to drive her forward and she became first pawn among them to be promoted and she became the Queen, and a Fairest Dragon. Charity’s friend Alison became Jealous of her and convinced their keeper that as the queen only he should be her lover. And so they were separated. She coveted Victor and his love for Charity so she stole the opportunity to stand next to the queen and became the queen side Bishop. When Victor got his promotion he became the queenside Knight.

                            Alison the Bishop took as her rewards for putting the other gentry into check Victor. The hurt on her friends face made her happy each and every time, for she knew that Charity could not lay her hand upon the burning knight of their set. It made Alison happy. Meanwhile for his reward Victor chose Tyrfring, the blood thirsting sword.

                            Eventually hey had a full set, and their power was great, as they crashed through foes. Though as the gentry are prone to they grow bored with their entertainments and s the count discarded his chess set but took the kingside pieces and gave them new lives elsewhere. Before they starved to death Charity lead the queen side to escape into the hedge and back to their lives. Victor’s blazing ferocity helped them cut their way through. Then, just when they are nearly through Alison turned on Charity and tore the heart of chest, laughing. She stole a kiss from Victor in the thorns, while he was paralyzed by the death of Charity, and promised to capture him for her own again on the other side.

                                He was your average guy in South Korea. And just like every adult male born there, the law dictated that he serve the military either as a conscripted soldier or some form of officer in his youth. Knowing very well how anachronistic and repressed the experience of years of mandatory military service are and will be, the man sought a way out. No such luck, though - it's not like he had connections to illegally fake his medical records or assign himself to easier posts, or the guts to wound himself and feign an accident.

                                It was then he saw a random flier on the way to his health checkup, something about guaranteed fulfillment and excitement. Oh, what the hell, the man thought, and stepped out of the asphalt road leading to the proper building, and on to some underused road. He really should've read the fine print past all the jargon. It did say experience "unlike anything you'd find in this world."

                                Sleeping and training with men of all shapes and sizes, definitely not Korean. Quarters adorned with classic camouflage patterns, but layered over architecture both modern and medieval, familiar and strange. Equipment and weaponry that for some reason included broadswords and halberds alongside hand cannons and fusiliers, and tower shields alongside kevlar. Training regimens designed to either make a man into a one-man army or let him whimper and die in the half-frozen mud. Marches along mountains crowned with thunderstorms and canyons filled with mists that had something hungering inside them. Five weeks of training had passed - or was it five years? Five days? The man was assigned to a unit within the mountains and forests. Which had frequent skirmishes with armored and armed soldiers equipped strangely as himself and his comrades, even more frequent outbreaks of wild beasts the man was sure that they weren't native to any part of the planet, and nigh continuous labor building new barracks and hoisting of heavy military equipment that he had no idea what they were parts of.

                                Twenty-one months had passed, or so the man thought it had. The man just reasoned so because he had seen seven seasons pass by, but never counted how long each one lasted. That winter did last awfully long, though, seeing as how he and his fellow soldiers had to chip away a glacier that formed behind the barracks. The man still remembered that mandatory military service in the ROK Army lasted for twenty one months. So when he went to the commander and talked about release from duty, the elfish officer sent him off to confinement for daring to speak up to him.

                                That was the last straw on this naive man who by this time figured out that he was not your normal army. So he decided to break out. He banged on the walls of the guardhouse, the loud warnings of his wardens be damned, screaming profanities and curses. When his fists turned red and blue from bruises, he started to kick. When his feet and knees all broke, he started to throw himself at the concrete. When all of his skin blistered open and the guards finally decided to take him out for his own safety, the walls crumbled down. The man was crushed by the debris and tangled by camouflage veils and his own uniform, but rose up with newfound vigor and started to run. He felt parts of rock and cloth fell off him. Or was that his own flesh and bones? Either way, he rampaged his way out. Guards shot blank ammo at him to subdue him, but he ignored all the pain. He rushed straight through the wires surrounding the barracks, through the thorns of the lush forest beyond the camp, and ended up right back where he took that discourse on the asphalt road.

                                An Ogrish Soldier and a Broadback, member of the freehold of Seoul
