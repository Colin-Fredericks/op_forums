    11-21-2021, 09:05 PM
    A changeling and scion mix.
    Hi everybody! So! If you are unaware, in both editions of Changeling: The Lost, fae player characters can make a contract ,a Bargain ,with literally anything, be it abstract or concrete. Examples include a contract with the tides,and with the Confucian principle of companionship.
    This last one inspired me to make a group of changelings that made a Bargain with a deity from classical mythology. I confess I'm not super sure how those can exist in the Chronicles of Darkness,but honestly? If you can make the Bargain with a Confucianist principle,a God it's not too far fetched. Some say Confucius is a God after all. As to build hype for Kith and Kin and Scion:God (both upcoming products by Onyx Path Publishing) I picked the Ilhim. canannite Gods. They are set to show up in Scion:God.
    Lost Dynasty: The Baal
    Most changelings were slaves,one way or another, that's perhaps the saddest fact of their lives. Even after escaping, many struggle to regain a sense of hope. Because from their point of view, their enslavers seemed to be gods.
    What if then, they met real gods. Truly numinous beings? Beings that literally hung the moon and sun in the sky?
    Suppose then,that for their own reasons those deities are A-Ok with Bargaining with the scions?
    The Baal,or the Lord's are what you get from that. The Changelings from ancient canaan didn't let the true Fae break their spirits, they found the great gods of their lands
    It is said the Bargain was simple,the Gods would grant the Lost a Mantle of Power if they pointed the way to Arcadia, for Hammon and his kin were hungry for conquest. The Bargain was struck
    Some even say they acted as charioteers for those deities in a siege on Arcadia itself. No one knows if they succeeded
    In modern age,the Ba'al are open for any changeling who chooses to convert to the Ilhim religion. After proving their loyalty,they receive their first mantle dot and their story is forever bound with the Gods.

    Mantle of the Baal (• to •••••)

    Their Mien become hot and dry like the desert, they eye colors are reddened ever so slowly, the sounds of clashing blades are heard in the distance….
    • Gain bonus dice equal to your character’s Mantle on physical rolls to defeat an enemy in a strategic manner. this means you have to plan the attack,it can’t be mindless.
    •• Gain bonus dice equal to your character’s Mantle on mundane rolls to directly build something physical and concrete
    ••• Gain three dots of the Fast Reflexes merit (changeling p. 123)
    •••• Gain two willpower points every time making a sacrifice to the gods cause you a major setback
    ••••• spend 1 willpower point to inflict the Demoralized Condition on a enemy with a touch;



    Contracts.
    Love and War (Commonl)
    The changeling prays to Astarte: goddess of love and war for 7 minutes and it can make people either aggressive or lustful
    Cost: 2 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Presence + Occult + Mantle vs Opponent’s Willpower
    Action: Instant (takes 7 m inutes)
    Roll Results
    Success: The victim gains a condition representing their new lustful or angry state,it lasts a scene
    Exceptional Success: The Condition lasts for 48 hours
    Failure: The Contract fails.
    Dramatic Failure: The changeling takes 1 lethal damage,due to the prayers not being enough.


    I’ll Play King, You’ll Play Soldier.
    If a loyal friend or servant of the changeling is defending him,he can make them stronger.
    Cost: 2 Glamour + 1 Willpower
    Dice Pool: Resolve + Occult + Mantle
    Success: The subject gains 9-again quality on rolls to defend the scion

    Exceptional Sucess: The subject gains the 8 again quality instead.
    Failure, The Contract fails.
    Dramatic Failure: the changeling can’t use the contract for 96 hours.
