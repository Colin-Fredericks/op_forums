                            "Here’s the intro text to Notary.

                            "You do understand that when you agreed to bind your children and your children’s children to that Other unto the seventh generation that they were being savagely literal, yes? There's no exaggeration with the Gentry. No, stop crying, I'll help you figure this out."

                            Not all pledges are signed on paper or carved into stone. Some are literally living documents. Notaries are changelings who were taken to solidify pledges between two or more True Fae. The agreement is written in their blood, etched across their skin, and imprinted on their soul. They are both witness and pledge, and they are kept under close supervision by their Keeper lest their pledge vanish into the thorns.

                            This makes Notaries dangerous to be around. A True Fae will stop at nothing to retrieve a lost pledge, sending Huntsmen, loyalists, and even other True Fae after escaped Notaries. Many freeholds wouldn't bother keeping such a ticking time bomb around, except for the fact that members of this kith have an incredibly valuable Blessing. Notaries escape by finding loopholes in the pledges that bind them to the Others and walking right out the front door. On Earth, they're often viziers, lawyers, mediators between courts, and, if all else fails, the trickster who outsmarts the True Fae into leaving their freehold alone for yet another season. All Notaries can perfectly recite the pledge they were kidnapped to hold, and any changes made up until their escape."

                                    Ok, based on me being more than a touch bored last night and this morning, I looked over several past kickstarters from Onyxpath and other creators to gather data and hypothesize a kickstart timeline. After Cavaliers of Mars, the word i've heard is that Changeling the Lost 2E and Trinity Continuum will be the next two kickstarters. Given a 30 day funding period and a 3-5 week space between kickstarters of the same creator, my hypothesis for the remainder of 2017 and 2018's onyxpath kickstarter is as follows (barring those delightful bits of entropy that makes life interesting and delay events:
                                    Cavaliers of Mars: 2017 Sept 19 – Oct 19 (30 days)
                                    3 weeks later
                                    Changeling 2 Kickstarter: 2017 Nov 9 – Dec 9 (30 days)
                                    let's say 4 weeks due to Christmas and New Years
                                    Trinity Kickstarter:2018 Jan 6 – Feb 5 (30 days)
                                    3 weeks later
                                    (I pause here, because the next ordering is unclear. A number of 2018 kickstarters were announced at the 2017 GenCon: Scion (Demigod/God), Contagion Chronicle, They Came from Beneath the Sea!. Deviant the Renegade was not announced as having a kickstarter in the brochure, but as it is a new CoD line, it will have one i'm sure. It has also been said that Geist 2E, Hunter the Vigil 2E and Mummy the Curse 2E may have kickstarters depending on how the Changeling the Lost 2E one does (I've no doubt it will do well, so I exptect at least 1 of the three will also get a kickstarter. Based entirely on where these various books are in the development blog ordering I suggest the following order of kickstarters starting after Trinity in early 2018:
                                    Geist 2 Kickstarter (at development longest and likely to go to approvals as time permits): Feb 24 – Mar 26 (30 days)
                                    3 weeks later
                                    Hunter 2E Kickstart: Apr 16 – May 16 (30 days) (at redlines)
                                    3 weeks later
                                    Deviant Kickstart: June 6 – July 6 (30 days) (at first draft)
                                    3 weeks later
                                    They Came from Beneath the Sea: July 27 – Aug 26 (30 days) (more recent add to First Draft)
                                    3 weeks later
                                    Mummy 2 or : Sept 16 – Oct 16 (30 days) (not yet on monday meeting posts)
                                    3 weeks later
                                    Contagion: Nov 6 – Dec 6 (30 days) (not yet on monday meeting posts. and having all CoD lines updated to 2E already would be beneficial to the writer and development team)

                                    Scion 2 Kickstarter was advertised, but no indication of writing of it has been done; understandable given that the Scion Origin and Hero rules must be completed before Demigod and God can be made. It's entirely possible work and writing has begun behind the scenes, well before an announcement, as was done with Geist 2E, but as the Onyxpath generally has the bulk of the core text written prior to a kickstarter I'm planning on it being late in 2018 in the place of either the Contagion Chronicle, pushing that back to early 2019, or in the place of a 2E CoD game - Geist, Hunter or Mummy).
