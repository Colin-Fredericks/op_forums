                        Lovely entry into the series all around, from the journals to the events that they depict. Even with the drama of Lori's father coming into the secrets of the Malibu Dream House, Lori's fraility coming to light, and certin past offences still not quite being forgiven, the sequence of events feels appropriately, well, fun. I loved the competition between Gawain and De La Rocha, particularly the breakdown of their respective contracts, Julia's enthusiasm matching if not surpassing Lori's (who knows how long she's had that dress stashed away) and how the group reprised past movies to give Molly Nuit a good showing. It really makes the group feel like an open family that doesn't mind more members coming in. I'd praise more details, but then I'd effectively give a summary of the session of my own, which I feel wouldn't be appropriate for someone who hasn't attended one. :-p

                        I am left wondering how Molly felt about going to place such as Disneyland though, especially with a background as devoid of a frame of reference as hers. Even for a Changeling, it'd probably seem quite strange.

                        Lori's father made me initially think of him being an active part of the Hunter groups, particularly the Lucifuge considering their heavy Christian themes, since certain groups are actually pretty supportive of Changelings (or at least don't consider them hunt-worthy, thank you Mortal Remains). However, the more I think about it the less I really think so. The various groups don't really seem like they'd allow a single agent of theirs roam freely (especially the Lucifuge) without some kind of case, and barring Lori's mother's death there doesn't seem to have been much foreshadowed of what would cause him to take up the Vigil. Granted, I am looking at the game with a degree of separation, so it's entirely possible I'm missing swaths of hints the Baroness has dropped. His behavior does rule him out as being a Hero from the Beast game-lines though - I'm sure that while there are exceptions, my understanding of Heroes generally makes them less than genuine in their generocity. Lori's father goes above and beyond the call of compassion to a bunch of supernatural strangers without any real gain aside from his daughter's happiness.

It depends on how strict the definition of her "singing" is for her fraility though, and I have to wonder what circumstances would bring her to know the tune of the song but not the words - Lori seems to have a good head for lyrics.

                        On a bit of a darker note, I am left worried about the victims of Insincere Margeurite's thieving, particularly of the harder drugs she pilfered. While it's ultimately on their heads to go to Disneyland with such drugs at all, addicts can have some...well, very extreme reactions to not having their fix, and the harder drugs tend to have proportionally deeper hooks in that regard. Makes me wonder how many of them would have had to make degeneration rolls from missing that kind of fix (yeah, my imagination goes odd places at times).

                                Session LXI

                                Gawain’s Memories

                                Once Lori and De la Roche got back from ice cream (after Annika was gone of course) De la Roche pulled us non-girlfriends aside. Well, Lori stayed, for moral support, you know? De la Roche wanted to tell us more about her Durance and Twitch, so maybe we’d be less upset at him. It didn’t really work, but I can see a little where she’s coming from.

                                Her Durance sounded pretty horrible. Real, you know, zombie movie-esque, what with the whole dilapidated hospital with mostly mindless creatures lurking beneath. It was good to hear, at least, that she didn’t blame herself for what happened there, in Arcadia, at least anymore than any Catholic feels guilt about anything, apparently. But once she came back, and got her memories back, she feels like she should have let Twitch know, you know, that the Maria from the hospital didn’t exist anymore and wasn’t coming for him.

                                And, yeah, well, she probably should have. She had good reasons - well, you know, maybe more like excuses - the shock might have killed him, pledges might have demanded people kill her anyway, that kinda thing. Even if it was a mistake though, even if it was selfish, it’s not a carte blanche for him to do what he likes with no blame. She didn’t take his agency.

                                I do feel sorry for the guy. I always have, when I’m not being super judgey. He’s seemed kinda hapless. And, you know, super broken. By Arcadia, not De la Roche. But, he has, by some accounts including his own, done some pretty shitty thing. And without qualms. Those were his choices, how he decided to deal with the fear.

                                It does seem like she’s coming to good terms with things though, you know? Being with Lori helps, since she figures they’re soulmates and being taken, and chasing Twitch out was the only way they’d have met. Kinda like me and Calamity being together helps my girl out. Anyway, hopefully our responses also helped, maybe at least a little.

                                The show went well, of course. I caught up with Leanne, making sure her dreams had been alright. They had, though she hadn’t noticed the Incubus before either. She did mention that Hollywood had some kind of dream master who had a Morphean dream god patron guy. That... sounds sketchy. And unpleasant, you know, considering those guy’s ties to Gentry already. Leanne didn’t know much about him though, except everyone was afraid of him. The Monarchs even had pledges that he wouldn’t go into their dreams.

                                It was super nice talking to her. She’s really pretty and crazy charming. I tried to reassure her about the Autumn Court folks - Moon, Dionysia and Ginny. She was nervous about them spying on her. I guess there’s some tension with her dating Queen Charlotte. I’m not really sure, you know, what's up with that. Moon didn’t mention anything about it when we talked later.

                                I did head over to that table after I talked with Leanne, partially because Calamity was over there talking super intensely with Dionysia, while Moon looked nervous and Ginny amused. I brought her a drink with one of Jacob’s awesome new fancy shaped ice cubes in it. After a bit I was, you know, glad I did - they were talking about talecrafting.

                                Moon made it sound good at first, talking about breaking the Gentries’ stories and all, you know? But I think there was something more I missed. Cause zie also talked up how it was addictive. The examples didn’t help Calamity and Dionysia’s case much, like using talecrafting to come into large amounts of money only if none of your friends or family had you as a beneficiary of their will/life insurance. Or, say, if you tried to restore tragically lost friendships and it happened because you lose a loved one... not great. Speaking, you know, as a loved one.

I wasn’t gonna say most of the things I was thinking in front of Dionysia, who is, you know, very pretty and clearly addicted. But Calamity agreed to start small and not without research. The conversation was still kinda getting to me, but I gently reminded her about her rounds and that got her out and about and not focused on it. I kept chatting with Moon. Apparently, Salk’s been courting hir, but zie’s a little unsure. I dunno much about the guy though, so I wasn’t able to help much. Ginny sure wasn’t - she really wanted to punch someone, Salk, Shi, random dude, she didn’t seem picky. She restrained herself for the night though.

                                November 19th, evening

                                Today was a good day. It even started off good, with Rowan joining the usuals for sex breakfast. She seemed really nervous, especially after Insincere Marguerite, you know, snapped a salt shaker up with her tongue. And even more after Lori stepped out with Jacob. But then she had a talk with Insincere Marguerite and was slightly less so.

She had work and plus there was, you know, the whole slap on the wrist thing for her punching the Viscount. He and Old Billy were running things, with Oleander there to wrangle the handful of Hedgebeasts they had to practice with. After, the Viscount treated us to a steak dinner where Percival insisted he was a vegetarian until I suggested he try the tofu steak.

                                The show was cool too. I mostly hung out with Gloria and Desiree, who were having a drinking contest. There was giggling. Lots and lots of giggling. Lori made a big announcement about her girlfriends, to make up for, you know, asking Insincere Marguerite to hide their relationship from her dad, I think. It was super adorable anyway. And man the hope in the room spiked when she started off with pointing out she was poly and bi.

I thought she was talking about the talecrafting stuff again at first, the way she talked about it. I dunno why she thinks so - Leanne seems super nice. I mean, Gloria agreed with Calamity. I think the phrase was ‘Don’t evil Leanne, she’s fuck’. She also threatened me with a blowjob from Desiree if Calamity did sleep with Leanne. I’m not sure how I feel about that.

                                Lorelei’s Diary

                                If this keeps up, I’m going to have to start inventing new phrases to describe how happy I am. Is it possible to be on cloud ten? Or to be in eighth heaven?

                                To clarify, the last two days have been great. Even the bumpy patches have turned out all right in the end, and I think by and large things are on the upswing. The motley’s in a good place, the Dream House is doing great business, and people’s spirits seem to be on the rise in general. And as for my romantic life…

                                After D and I got back from our ice cream date with Joan and Andromeda, things got serious for a little while. She’d mentioned that she wanted to talk with Gawain, Calamity, and Jacob… to go into more detail about her Durance and to help them understand her position on Twitch. I asked her if she wanted me there, and she gave me a noncommittal answer and shrugged. Coming from D, that’s almost like a neon sign pointing straight to her insecurities, so I joined her in the kitchen as the others gathered.

                                It wasn’t so bad in the end. I know it’s hard for my love to talk about the things she did in Arcadia, and I know that it frustrates her that none of her friends are willing to let her feel guilty about them. She’s convinced that Twitch was a paragon of virtue before she tore him down and broke his mind. There’s probably an element of truth to that as well, but I can’t help but wonder if she’s looking at the good doctor’s former self through rose-colored glasses.

                                She desperately wants some sort of closure for that part of her life. She’s worked so hard to regain the person she was before she was Taken, because she can’t bear to be the thing they turned her into. She doesn’t slink in the shadows. She doesn’t feed off of other people (excepting the occasional bit of health stolen from her opponent in a fair fight). She even explained that her occasional irritation at Calamity stems from our friend’s tendency to accept the changes Arcadia put her through rather than seeking to reclaim her lost life.

                                The others agreed that D had given them a lot to think about, but maintained that Twitch had committed heinous acts since returning from the Hedge, and needed to pay for them. D elaborated that part of the reason that Twitch does what he does is that he’s still terrified of “Maria.” He doesn’t know that the monster that tormented him in Arcadia no longer exists; he still sleeps with the light on so that she won’t be able to pull him into the shadows. Part of D’s guilt comes from the fact that she’s been too scared to put his mind at ease on that point. After all, we don’t know what pledges are in place between Darke and Twitch… the Freehold’s former spymaster might be oath-bound to destroy D if he finds out that she’s Maria.

                                “Oh, and one more thing,” she said softly, giving my hand a squeeze as the two of us sat perched on the edge of the table. “I feel a lot of guilt over what I did to Twitch… I can’t help it. I was raised Catholic.” She smirked in her usual cocky way, but I could see in her eyes that she was working up to something serious. “But guilt isn’t the same as regret. I don’t regret anything that happened in Arcadia. I don’t regret chasing Twitch out of the Hospital. If I hadn’t done that, I wouldn’t have met any of you. I wouldn’t have met Lori.”

                                She turned so that she was looking at only me, and she took both of my hands in her own. “Both of our souls were made whole because of what I did to Twitch. Laugh at my superstitions if you want, but I know destiny had a hand in it.”

                                I couldn’t speak. All I could do for the next few minutes was stare at my love. I could feel the tears running out of the corners of my eyes, but I know that I was also smiling fit to burst.

                                After we dispersed, I pulled D into an empty room and kissed her fiercely. “Thank you,” I said after a moment.

                                “For what?” she shrugged.

                                “Maria… what you said in the kitchen… it was without a doubt the sweetest, most romantic, most beautiful thing anyone has ever said about me,” I said, tearing up again.

                                She looked a little uncomfortable. “I’ve said it before,” she pointed out, “a few months back when we were talking about the difference in our ages…”

                                “I know,” I said, “but the way you said it just now… well, I just want you to know that I agree with you one hundred percent, Maria. I don’t… I mean, with everything that’s been going on, with me singing here and trying to study medicine and preparing to rescue Missy and Jane and everything, I’m not always exactly sure where my life is going. I don’t know where the future will take me. But the one thing I’m absolutely sure of is that in five years, or ten, or fifty… wherever I am, and whatever I’m doing, you’ll be there with me.”

                                She embraced me and kissed me fiercely then. “Damn right,” she said after a moment.

                                We disengaged after a few minutes, and I left to get ready for the show. I didn’t have any new numbers that night; just a few torch songs to set the desires flowing, but I think I really knocked them out of the park. It was one of my better nights. I threw D a nod and a wink during my first number, but after that I saw Rowan in the audience, and kept my eyes on her. She seemed keen to return my interest in kind.

                                Before I get to the main event, I’ll talk a bit about what else was going on. Molly was back, and seemed to be genuinely enjoying herself (mostly hanging out around the bar and chatting with Jacob). Leanne was there, looking smug as usual. I said hi but didn’t linger at her table… I haven’t trusted her ever since Jolie warned me against her. I think Calamity spoke more extensively with her later, but I confess my attentions were elsewhere by that point.

                                And speaking of Calamity, she hung out with her fellow Autumn Courtiers for most of the evening. Moon, Dionysia, and Ginny were all there, and the four of them seemed to be having a fine old time. I saw Calamity and Dionysia engaged in a spirited discussion about something, though I didn’t eavesdrop to find out what it was. It’s kind of funny… I didn’t know the two of them knew each other that well, but to look at them you’d think they were the best of chums.

                                So fairly early in the evening I dropped by Rowan’s table and promised her that I’d return shortly – after I’d made the rounds – so that we could talk properly. Before I made my way back there, I planted myself next to D, who was sitting with Desiree, Gloria, and Julia at their usual table.

                                “Plans for the evening, babe?” she asked me, raising an eyebrow suggestively.

                                “Maybe,” I admitted, throwing a glance back toward Rowan. “Rowan’s back tonight, and she’s been sending me signals…”

                                D chuckled. “You gonna club that selkie?” she asked with barely suppressed mirth. I’ve said it before, and I’ll say it again: I love a girl of refined taste and class.

                                “Not if you need me tonight,” I said seriously. “I know this afternoon was hard for you…”

                                D shook her head. “I’m fine, babe. Go have your fun. Julia and Mags will take care of me.”

                                I narrowed my eyes. “How much money do you have riding on this?” I asked under my breath.

                                She had the good grace to look guilty as she shrugged.

                                “Okay,” I said slowly, “well, have fun tonight honey. I’ll see you in the morning.”

                                As I rose, D threw a triumphant look at Julia, who returned it in kind. As I walked away, Julia and Desiree each took hold of D’s hair and started to pull her out of the room. Curious, I motioned to Rowan that I would be right back and followed them.

                                “What’s going on?” I asked once we had some privacy.

                                D’s smile faltered. “You might not like it,” she warned me.

                                “Are you okay?” I asked softly.

                                “Oh yeah,” she replied with a grin.

                                “I won the pool,” Julia offered, “she’s mine tonight. Not Insincere Marguerite’s… mine.”

                                I allowed myself a little sigh of relief. “So this is just a sex thing?” I asked.

                                D nodded.

                                “Well okay then,” I said with a grin. “Like I said, have fun tonight. I’ll see you in the morning.” I leaned in for a tender kiss – which was a little awkward, considering that Desiree and Julia still had a grip on her hair – and we parted for the evening. Confidentially, I think Julia won some sort of bet against D… but one that D wanted to lose.

From time to time it bothers me that she has a need I can’t really fulfill. She’s suggested that I might want to give it a try some time, but I… I just don’t know if I can. Maybe part of it is because she’s talked about how she left Madame Spidertoes because she was too controlling, and I don’t want to risk falling into that pattern. I dunno. I’m glad she can get what she needs in that respect from Marguerite (and, apparently, Julia).

Rowan loves the beach (which isn’t really surprising, all things considered), while I mostly bemoaned the fact that living with a bunch of darklings has limited my beach time significantly. We actually have a lot in common, considering the obvious differences between us… bird and seal, air and water, and all that jazz.

                                I admit that I was laying it on a little thick. Yes, I was hoping to seduce her, but I swear that I would not have been trying if she hadn’t been responding so obviously to my flirts. And of course I didn’t put any glamour into my efforts; I was just honestly trying to see if I could spark her interest with my natural charms. Using glamour and contracts to seduce someone feels like cheating to me.

                                So when she started talking about her house – a cute but modest cabin by the shore – of course I leaped at the opening. “It sounds lovely,” I said breathily.

                                “Would…” she began shyly, eyes downcast. “If you’d like to see it,” she tried again, her voice very soft, “I’d be happy to show you. It’s not far from here.”

                                I smiled and took her hand in mine. “I’d like that,” I said warmly.

                                We made it out to the parking lot before she stopped. “Lori,” she said, and I could hear the tremble in her voice. “I need… I need to ask you something.”

                                “Of course,” I said, a little confused.

                                “If this is going where it seems to be going,” she said hesitantly, “then I need to know. Is this just sex to you? Because I’m not… I don’t do that. I don’t want to be a one-night stand. I’m not saying that I expect us to be together permanently, or anything, but… I don’t want…”

                                “Rowan,” I said seriously, putting my hands on her shoulders. “I like you. I enjoy hanging out with you, and I like talking with you, and I’m attracted to you. I’m willing to see where this goes, if you are. But even if things don’t work out between us, I’m not going to disappear from your life, okay? I’ll take your calls, I’ll talk to you when you come by to watch the show, I’ll hang out with you when you’re feeling lonely.”

                                She sighed. “Okay,” she said after a moment.

                                I held up a finger. “Now having said that,” I continued, “there is something I need you to understand. I’m polyamorous, Rowan, and I have four girlfriends. Anything that happens between us is not going to be exclusive. And you know de la Rocha, the girl I was with earlier tonight?”

                                She nodded. “The Latina with the purple hair,” she said, “you’ve pointed her out to me before.”

                                “She’s not going anywhere, Rowan. I need you to understand that, more than anything else. If you’re not going to be okay with that, then we probably shouldn’t go any further. I don’t want you to get hurt. But if you can live with it… I’d love to see where this is going.”

                                She thought about it for a minute, and I could tell that she was really thinking about it. After a bit she gave me kind of a quavery smile and said “let’s give it a shot.”

                                We got into her car and she drove us up the coast to her place. Since the course of the evening had been decided, the sexual tension was thick in the air, and Rowan was clearly as eager as I was… maybe more so. We kept our hands to ourselves for the whole trip, and hardly spoke at all.

                                Her house really is a charming little cottage just off the beach, which must have set her back a pretty penny in this area. She kind of nervously showed me around for a bit, but seemed unsure of how to proceed. I suggested a moonlight walk along the beach, and she readily agreed.

                                We walked in the surf for a while. My skirt was short enough that I could wade in until it was almost to my knees, but begged off going any deeper. My wings get really heavy when they’re wet, which means I’m no longer much of a swimmer. She didn’t push it, though any fool can see how much she loves the ocean. After a few minutes of quiet conversation, my hand found hers, and a dreamy smile settled on her face.
