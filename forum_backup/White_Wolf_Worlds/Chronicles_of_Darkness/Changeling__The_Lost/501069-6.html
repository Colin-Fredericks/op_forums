                For those who say you can't make an evil Fairest... Filamena pointed out the curse is about collateral damage, unexpected complications. The Evil Queen can avoid her curse by being aloof and cruel. If she leaves others to handle decisions (despite the fact they come to her), she evades her curse and blessing both.
