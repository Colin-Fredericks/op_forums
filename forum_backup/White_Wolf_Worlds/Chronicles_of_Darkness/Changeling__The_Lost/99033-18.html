            Gawain’s Memories

            July 14th, late night

            No time for my usual afternoon check in today. It’s Bastille Day - well, I guess it’s pretty soon to be that yesterday was Bastille Day, but anyway, Insincere Marguerite got super homesick. Julia was crazy worried - I mean, you know, we all were. It was weird, seeing Insincere Marguerite so... emotional, so sad. I didn’t figure I could say much to cheer her up, and she had Lori and Julia with her - she didn’t say anything until De la Roche left for work, so she wasn’t around until later.

            So anyway, I started making a proper French dinner. Maybe not, you know, a very Bastille Day dinner, if my quick research was to be believed, but my vague memories of Paris included at least one dinner of Banquette de veau, and it seemed like something I could handle. I threw in a more summery salad, with goat cheese and apples and walnuts, and finished it off with cream puffs and crepes for dessert. I think Insincere Marguerite liked it.

            Calamity and Julia helped me some too. That was when Jacob took Insincere Marguerite off urban exploring, so Julia wasn’t hovering around her. She wanted to learn how to cook French cuisine, so I explained my steps, you know, more than usual. Cutting up the fruits and vegetables seemed to help Calamity some too. She’d gone to talk with Annika and Dark Kimberly about the ‘incident’ as we’ve taken to calling it. We spent most of sex breakfast arguing over whether it was worse cause Flores got hurt or worse that Becky would have succeeded at killing Dark Kimberly without Flores’ interference.

            De la Roche went over what she had heard - while Becky and Dark Kimberly had been, you know, avoiding each other, they were both in court yesterday. Dark Kimberly said it was because Princess Joli came back to the Springs early to meet with Desiree. They argued and Flores had intervened and managed to deescalate the situation. Then, according to Davey, Becky lashed out for no reason and according to Sandeval, Dark Kimberly said something cutting about White Maria in Chinese and Becky attacked in response. Dark Kimberly said she had said something innocuous about White Maria, asking if Becky’d seen her recently or something. Becky, when Calamity called, didn’t say anything in particular about it, but that she was just really angry.

Problem still was, no one there but Becky or maybe Sandeval had the connections to use the obvious Contract, Fleeting Summer. Louie and Gunnar had the Smoke to be there without anyone knowing, and the connections for Fleeting Summer. Haley and Juan Carlos had some Smoke, but probably not enough to be unnoticed. Then there’s always some other weird powers, you know, like the one used by the person who shot Dark Kimberly at The Rose and Thorn...

Even with Julia and De la Roche almost coming to (fake) blows over the fact De la Roche’d slept with Davey, and bitter interpretations of life lessons from Pinocchio and Edward Scissorhands, we were all having fun, together. Tomorrow there’ll be mysteries to solve and tonight we’re home and safe.

            Lorelei’s Diary

            Huh… it’s been nearly three weeks since I wrote anything in this diary. I guess the time has sort of gotten away from me, what with Highsummer and the play coming up later this week and everything else I’ve been concentrating on. I haven’t had a lot of spare time.

            I’ve continued working out with D and Monica every couple of days, and I think it’s paying off. I’ve gained a little bit of muscle definition, and I’ve noticed an increase in both strength and stamina. I doubt I’ll ever sport a real six-pack – and let’s be honest, that probably wouldn’t be the best thing for my career in the burlesque industry anyway – but at least I feel like I’m a little more prepared for the physical rigors of the journey to come.

            And speaking of my impending jaunt into the Hedge, I’ve also been preparing in other ways. I’ve slept in the Hedge House a few more times, and I’ve gone in twice more with Monica and once with Oleander. I think I’m starting to get used to the eerie, otherworldly feel of that place. It’s really not so bad in there as long as you’re careful. Some of the thorns can even be beautiful, in a wild, harsh way.

            I’ve also spent some more time at the range with Calamity (though D, after that first disastrous trip, has opted not to join us again). I doubt I’ll ever be a real sharpshooter, but my aim isn’t bad. And Jacob has taught me a bit about tracking and survival in the wilderness, which has been fun. Oh, and Marguerite has deigned to show me the basics of stealth as well. What with rehearsals and all, my afternoons have been pretty full for the past few weeks.

            As have my mornings. After my Darkling darlings drift off, I tend to extricate myself from their tangled sleeping forms and head downstairs to hit the books. I’ve been reading a lot of medical textbooks and journals lately. Though I’m not nearly smart enough to understand most of the articles, I think I’m slowly gaining a greater understanding of human anatomy and the way the body is supposed to work. I’m hoping that this will improve my healing abilities… if I know where to channel the glamour, maybe I can maximize its effect.

            I just wish I was better at that sort of thing. Oh, I’m good at picking up on social cues and body language, and I have a good enough vocabulary to fake it in conversation, but I know I’ve never been the brightest bulb in the pack. Picking up new skills, retaining difficult information, and the proper application of knowledge have always been difficult for me. I’m pretty sure that’s why things never worked out between me and Kyle. Sometimes I wonder how my life might have gone differently if I’d been just a little smarter…

            But anyway, that’s not important right now. What is important is that I’m giving this rescue preparation my all. It might still be a while before I’m ready to go back for Missy and Jane, but I’m slowly getting there. I’ve even picked up the pace a bit lately by studying all night and morning and then using my Contracts to refresh myself. It’s not like the glamour is hard to come by around here… I find that I really only need to sleep once every four or five days.

            I’ve even had time for a social life. D and I have gone out for dinner and a movie a couple of times, both by ourselves and with Marguerite and Julia. It’s been nice doing regular date stuff with the three of them, instead of just our usual bedroom hijinks. And I can firmly state that I am as much in love with D as ever, and things between us are going great. I’ve made a few more hints that I’d like her to come to church with me soon, but she’s determined not to understand them… so far.

            I’ve also been out with Oleander a few times, and have found myself spending the night at her place on more than one occasion. Since D is pretty much living out of our room at the Dream House these days, it doesn’t seem fair to invite Oleander to stay over at my place. I can’t ask D to go back to her apartment; it wouldn’t feel right. And if she stayed in Marguerite and Julia’s room, there would be too many chances for… awkwardness.

            I don’t know if I’m falling in love with Oleander or not yet. I definitely like her a lot. I really enjoy the time we spend together. She’s smart, she’s funny, and she’s sweet in a way that’s uniquely hers. She’s very passionate about a great many things, but she doesn’t let it control her. And while our lovemaking can be spontaneous and wild, it’s more often the result of careful planning and preparation on her part. Last week, I arrived at her place to find the bedroom lit by candles, heady incense filling the air, champagne on ice, a tray of chocolate truffles, and soft music playing from hidden speakers.

            I love D; there’s no question about that. But I can’t really imagine her doing something like that; it’s not her style. And it was a nice change.

            A few weeks ago, I was contemplating breaking things off with Oleander because of the tension between her and D. I’m glad I didn’t. I’m discovering that our relationship has layers I never suspected, and I look forward to discovering more of them. But the tension is still there. D and Oleander don’t really talk anymore. I mean, neither of them is making aggressive moves toward the other, but I think they’re deliberately avoiding each other. I know that they were never particularly close before, but they used to get along fairly well.

            D’s actually a little depressed about this, I think. She won’t say it in so many words, but whenever she mentions something that came up in the past involving Oleander, she gets this sort of wistful look on her face, like she’s talking about a friend who moved away. While the two of them are very different people, they actually have a lot in common as well, and I feel bad about being the one who’s keeping them apart, however unintentionally.

            In the darkest hours of the night, sometimes I still fantasize about getting both of them in bed at the same time. The scary thing is that I could probably convince them if I really set my mind to it. But as wet as that thought might make me – having two beautiful girls who love me that much pleasing me at the same time – it would almost certainly do more harm than good in the long run.

            Bad Lori. These wonderful girls are willing to let you have the finest steak dinner and the most delicious mousse cake for dessert. It’s petty to complain that you can’t eat them both at the same time.

            In other romantic news, Halley and Monica have decided to call it quits. I feel a little responsible for this, considering that they split up pretty much immediately following my conversation with Monica in the Hedge, but in the end I think it was for the best. Halley is pretty broken up about it, and that’s hard to watch, but it’s better than continuing in a relationship built on false assumptions. The two of them aren’t speaking to each other, and Monica doesn’t seem to want to talk about it at the moment.

            Unfortunately, those aren’t the only tense situations that have intensified over the past few weeks. The ire between Dark Kimberly and the rest of the Freehold’s Chinese population seems to have taken a turn for the worse. Dark Kimberly and Becky both turned up at Glamour Springs the other day, and things got tense. Flores tried to separate the two of them and calm things down, and it seemed to be working at first, but then Becky just… snapped. I still don’t know exactly what happened, but Becky lashed out at Kimberly, and Flores got in the way. She was pretty badly hurt. It’s lucky she was at a Spring Court function, and there were several capable healers close at hand.

            The details of the incident are unclear. Of course, my motley has taken it upon itself to investigate, and get to the bottom of it if we can. Becky says that she doesn’t remember exactly what Kimberly said to set her off; the rage just came across her suddenly (as it sometimes does with us all, I guess). Kimberly says that she didn’t say anything particularly inflammatory; she just asked how White Maria was doing. Since the two of them were speaking Chinese at the time, Flores doesn’t know what was said on either side.

            There were a few other witnesses, but their stories just serve to muddy the waters even more. Davie was there, and he claims that Kimberly asked something completely innocuous… so much so that he can’t remember exactly what it was. But then again, he’s one of Kimberly’s best friends, and he might be covering something up to help her. I don’t want to believe that, but it’s a possibility. Sandoval was there too, and he says that he distinctly heard Kimberly say something biting about White Maria… but he doesn’t speak Chinese, so he might have just heard her name and made assumptions.

            There are more sinister possibilities as well. There are contracts tied to the Summer Court which can cause a person to instantly and irrationally act upon their greatest angry feelings. But someone would have to either be a member of the Summer Court or be in very good standing with them in order to learn those contracts, and no one meeting that description was there at the time. Of course, there are other contracts which can allow people to make themselves practically invisible (just look at Marguerite), and there are certain members of the Freehold who might have both of those powers.

            Gunnar Friday is Autumn Court, but he’s very close to Lucien Torres, and we know that Lucien was tangentially involved with the whole Kimberly-Maria-Sundown debacle years ago. He certainly has the stealth chops to pull it off, too. I don’t have a problem with Gunnar; I remember how helpful he was in dealing with our resident ghosts and Mary’s fetch, but I don’t know that much about him on a personal level, so for the moment he seems like the most likely suspect in my opinion.

            Louis Guo is known to possess high levels of the Contracts of Smoke, and he’s a Summer Courtier in good standing. He used to have ties to the Tong as well from what I hear, back when he was a mortal. But he supposedly cut all ties to them when he found religion and became a Buddhist. And besides, he made it very clear to the entire motley that he was Viscount Augustine’s man through and through, and not one of Lucien’s toadies. Still, by the strength of his capabilities alone, he’s probably number two on the suspect list.

            There are a few wilder theories out there too, and some facts – admittedly gleaned from unreliable witnesses – which hint at something stranger going on. Desiree was there, standing directly behind Kimberly, when Becky attacked. Kimberly says she remembers Desiree screaming in what sounded like fear when Flores went down. Flores herself apparently didn’t make a sound. Could the attack have been intended for Desiree rather than Kimberly? Could Desiree be behind the attack? I know Gawain removed the trigger from her brain, but the conspiracy theorist in me can’t help but wonder… what if she’s really a Loyalist, and the trigger was only there to let her known when to drop her cover personality?

            And what about Flores, for that matter? Kimberly has been attacked twice in the last few months, and on both occasions Flores was present. While I don’t think any of us think Flores could be behind the attacks, could she be their true target? The Rose & Thorn had to shut down for several days after the shooting, after all. And Flores certainly got the worst of things in this encounter as well.

            Annika is not taking the attack on Dark Kimberly well… but then again, Annika isn’t exactly the most stable block in the pyramid, is she? Reading between the lines of Calamity’s report on the two of them, I think there’s trouble in paradise. Annika is trying to be too controlling (surprise, surprise), and Kimberly is chaffing at the overprotective treatment. Kimberly has agreed to travel with a bodyguard for her own protection when she goes out, but it sounds like Annika would rather she never goes out at all.

            Controlling overprotective cradle-robbing psycho bitch.

            Ahem.

            If only that was the only drama slung in our faces yesterday. Alas, ‘twas not so. You see, yesterday was Bastille Day, and that simple fact had Insincere Marguerite feeling more than a little homesick. I confess that I didn’t pick up on this at first, and when she started in with her usual Classier-Than-Thou-Francophile spiel, I snapped at her. I think I said something like “I am in no mood, Marguerite.” It wasn’t until later that I realized she hadn’t corrected me for shortening her name, and she stayed pretty quiet throughout the rest of the discussion of Dark Kimberly’s woes.

            When Calamity went off to visit Annika, D ran off to her job, and I retreated to my boudoir to catch up on my studies. Oh, interesting side note… I think I might actually be a little farsighted, as I’ve found it easier to focus on the books if I wear my fake reading glasses. I dunno, maybe it’s a psychological thing. Girls who wear glasses are smarter, right?

            Anyway, Julia knocked on my door about an hour later, and she was frantic with worry. Marguerite was crying in their room… actually crying! Julia didn’t know what to do, and it was obviously tearing her apart that nothing she said seemed to have any effect on her beloved. She’s a sweet, caring, wonderful girl, and I love her, but this was a situation to which she was not well-suited. She had been trying to reason with Marguerite; to make things better with logical suggestions. That wasn’t what Marguerite needed today.

            I started off by speaking nothing but French to her throughout our conversation. Despite how I sometimes tease Marguerite, my French is anything but perfect, and I’m sure I stumbled and fumbled and generally came out sounding like a backwoods hick to her, but she seemed to appreciate the gesture in any case. I told her that I was there for her, and that Julia was there for her, and that later that day D would be there for her as well. We all know what it’s like to feel homesick, and no one was going to think any less of her for it. I told her to cry if she needed to cry, to let it out, and to purge it if she could.

            We also kinda-sorta make tentative plans to maybe visit Paris next year on Bastille Day. It was Julia’s idea, and what can I say? I’ve never been to Paris, and I’ve always wanted to go. Marguerite pointed out that there might be a few problems with that plan, as she is not well-liked by the Courts there. In point of fact, she fled Paris to escape capture, and was tried and convicted of being a Loyalist in absentia. She said that one of the Changelings she had previously “liberated” from their Keeper (and kept for herself) had testified at the trial, which doesn’t sound hopeful. So either we’ll have to straighten that mess out, or she’ll have to go incognito.

            (Marguerite also said that her “Dearest,” her favorite of those she had stolen, had “come to love her in the end.” I don’t know quite how to respond to that. I suppose it’s good that there’s no bad blood between them, but… well, lots of Changelings come to love their Keepers in the end, and that’s definitely not a good thing.)

            She lived in Montreal after that, but apparently that just made the homesickness worse. She said it was like living in an American town that was pretending to be French; the little things were all wrong and it drove home how much she missed the genuine article. I guess I can understand that.

            Once she had calmed down a bit, I left her to Julia’s tender graces, and went back to my room to resume my studies until it was time for us all to gather for dinner. Gawain had outdone himself when he’d heard about Marguerite’s funk, and had prepared a meal of traditional French cuisine. Banquette de Veau, a light salad, appropriate wines, and a dessert of crepes and cream puffs. It was delightful.

            Jacob had also taken Marguerite “urban exploring” while I was studying, which also seemed to cheer her up a bit. I don’t know what the two of them got up to out there, but it was nice to see her excited about something again. I hope they didn’t break too many of the important laws.

            And then there was movie night. Whatever we had originally planned was summarily preempted by Julia, however, who decreed that we would be watching two films by a director named Tim Burton, based on a conversation from earlier in the day. Both films starred an actor I didn’t know by the name of Johnny Depp, and it was deemed imperative that this should be addressed.

            Sleepy Hollow was a fun little re-telling of the old Washington Irving story… with a lot of elaboration and a hefty dose of creative license. Depp was pretty good as Ichabod Crane, who was presented here as a sort of primitive forensic policeman rather than the nervous and impressionable schoolteacher of the story. His love Katrina was played by a very pretty young woman who could really fill out a peasant top. And there were a few older actors I recognized as well… Christopher Lee and Martin Landau were present in minor roles, and Christopher Walken played the Horseman (in a flashback to when he actually had a head, of course).

            Edward Scissorhands was far stranger, and I’m told it’s more representative of Burton’s early work. Depp played an android in this film, a naïve but kind-hearted young man whose “father” died before he could properly finish his creation. This left the poor creature with a number of sharp blades on the ends of his arms rather than proper hands. Why the old inventor thought scissors would make suitable temporary hands is never explained. Nonetheless, young Edward is taken in by a kindly Avon lady and her family, and he slowly learns how he can fit into society. Though of course there are bumps along the way, many people learn to appreciate Edward for the things that make him different, and his haircuts, hedge trimmings and ice sculptures are big hits. But there are many very dark twists and turns, and the film doesn’t exactly have a happy ending. The love and acceptance this outcast oddball craves simply aren’t in the cards in the end.

            This was a better film overall, in my opinion, and it certainly had a message. On an artistic level, it was visually striking, and I could see why it appealed so much to Julia’s dark Gothic sensibilities (though I still teased her about it a little). Maybe I should look into more of this fellow’s films. Though I have to say, I would not want one of the haircuts Edward gave the neighborhood ladies in that film. Maybe it’s because I skipped the 80s or something, but the styles just looked weird to me… almost as much as Edward’s. His hair stuck out all over the place, and looked sort of like he’d rinsed it with egg whites.

            Flores was there for movie night, and we used the opportunity to question her about the incident between Dark Kimberly and Becky. She mostly confirmed what we had heard from the others, but there’s one thing I should probably mention at this point. When we mentioned the possible connection between this event and the shooting at the Rose & Thorn, she got angry. Really angry. I’ve never seen Flores angry before, and it’s not something I’d like to see again. Her mantle changed, and the little flowers that always seem to be surrounding her grew teeth. Luckily, D and I managed to calm her down.

            As Julia and Marguerite wanted some private time in the wake of the latter’s breakdown, it fell to me and D to keep Flores entertained last night. It was a heavy burden to bear, but I think we managed to show her a good time. Several, in fact. And she eagerly returned the favor in spades.

            In the middle of the night, as I was reading an article on blood coagulation in the American Journal of Medicine, I heard soft strains of music coming from down the hall. I quietly got up, checked that D and Flores were still asleep, and then made my way quietly out into the hallway. The tune resolved into a singing voice, and it was obviously coming from Marguerite and Julia’s room. It was beautiful. The music seemed to get inside my head somehow, and my thoughts became fuzzy and dim.

            I met Jacob in the hall. He was the only other resident of the Dream House who had heard the song. We conferred for a few seconds, and then knocked on the door. The singing stopped, and our heads cleared a bit. Julia opened the door with an embarrassed look on her face. “Yes?” she asked softly.

            “We heard singing,” I whispered back. “Is everything okay?”

            “Yes,” she said again. “I was singing Insincere Marguerite to sleep. Sorry I disturbed you.”

            “No worries,” I replied, “I’ve just never heard you sing before. We wanted to make sure you two were okay.”

            Julia thanked us for our concern, said her goodnights, and went back to the slumbering Marguerite. But the singing didn’t start up again.

            Sex Breakfast came a little earlier than usual this morning, but was doubly appropriate, as most of us were practically humming in the afterglow of something or other. Flores joined us for breakfast before hurrying off to see to the Rose & Thorn. I called Davie up and gleaned some of the information I mentioned above, and promised him that we were looking into the situation and would do everything we could to ensure that it wouldn’t happen again. He’s not happy at the moment, but I think – I hope – he feels a little better knowing that we’re on the case.

            Of course, he might not have been thrilled to learn how we spent the rest of the afternoon. Almost as soon as I hung up the phone, someone – I think it might have been Gawain – suggested that we resurrect the tabled idea of heading out for a few rounds of Laser Tag. The idea had initially been suggested by D as a kind of date for the two of us before she moved in, and the rest of the motley had gotten excited at the prospect. I admit that I had previously sort of manipulated conversations away from that notion, because at the time I was really anxious to spend more time alone with D, so that the two of us could get to know each other better. Now that we’re pretty solidly involved, the idea of playing a few rounds of the game with all of our friends present sounded a lot more appealing.

            We got there, paid for a few games, and suited up. I bemoaned the fact that the glowing red target would be sitting squarely atop my boobs, but it was all in good fun (and while I’m being honest, when have I ever really minded any accessory that calls attention to my assets?). The best part of the experience for me was the fact that we didn’t split up into teams… no “Lovers vs. Fighters” bullshit this time. The entire motley, Luna, D, and even Julia (who practically had to be dragged kicking and screaming into the arena) was on a single side, and we were arrayed against a group of mortals.

            Those poor schmucks never had a chance!

            Okay, so I’ll never be the marksman that some of my motley-mates are. I’m no Annie Oakley, that’s for sure. But I think I gave at least as well as I got out there today. Better, in fact, because while I didn’t rack up a huge amount of “kill shots” like Calamity, at least I was only hit a couple of times myself. I guess all that time I’ve spent jogging and working out with D has paid off a little bit after all.

            Let’s see, about my companions. Gawain spent most of his time shielding the rest of us with his body, which was noble and very… “Gawain” of him. Calamity and D were the queens of the battlefield, cackling like lunatics as they picked off the competition one by one, rarely even bothering to take cover, just dancing through the thick of the fray and dodging this way and that. Jacob was a shadow; his shots were precise, and he was careful about breaking cover. Luna was, if anything, even deadlier than her paramour. Marguerite scored incredibly well, practically disappearing from sight and making straight for the other team’s base to rack up major points. Julia… has many fine qualities, and was a very good sport about being roped into this activity.
