                                                            It just occured to me that a Changeling character I'd thought up a while back is actually not only playable out the gate now, but he's actually far better represented here than he would have been before - Luthor Blackwell, the Beast Artist, with nails like fountain pens, scales tattooed onto his skin, eyes that are pools of ink and ballpoint horns. The Artist kith blessing works incredibly well for him conceptually, given the materials (outside of the paper) would literally be on his body. All he'd be doing is effectively copying them onto stand-in materials. Suddenly I'm really excited to try playing him in a game instead of my Kamen-Rider styled Changelings.
