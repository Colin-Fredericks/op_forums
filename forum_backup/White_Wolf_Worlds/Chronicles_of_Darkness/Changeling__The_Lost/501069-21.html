                Wow, I love changeling, but I don't like this take on Fairest at all. I apologize the writer of the second edition if I am too blunt in the following-- I hope that you can read it with some professional distance despite my strong feelings on the subject.

                One of the problems with WoD is that it tends to repeat the stereotypes of those original vampire clans too closely. Don't get me wrong, I like the Vampire system and set up, but I just don't think every other part of WoD needs to mimic that system too closely. That's one of the wonderful things about Changeling is that with the Courts, Seemings and Kiths, the characters just doesn't fit into that classic Vampire mode of Ventrue, Toreador, Brujah, etc. With the addition of leadership as an attribute, what we are left with is Fairest= Ventrue, and that just bores me to death.

                The wonderful thing about Changeling the Lost was that it drew very knowledgeably from world-wide fairy tales and myths. While some fairest are leaders-- has anyone read the Mary Poppins novels? She's endlessly vain and powerful-- and clearly harvesting glamour from those kids. Not all fairest are leaders: in Snow White we get two Fairest, Snow and the Queen. Perhaps the evil Queen is a leader, but she did marry into the position. Snow definitely not leading the dwarves, since they will keep her if she agrees to:"take care of our house, cook, make the beds, wash, sew, and knit, and if you will keep everything neat and clean."

                I'm surprised when reading other comments that any Changeling campaign would discount Fairest. They are after all, the most pretty, desirable people by definition, and they get bonuses to persuade to boot. Mortals leap up to open doors and buy them drinks (or cars, jewelry, etc.). Changelings are not immune to their charms-- how better to cement your court status than having a Fairest on your arm? How better to move up the ranks in your court than being (or being with) someone who can seduce heartlessly? To be Fairest is to be more free of the memories of durance than any other Changeling (or at least among the freest)-- as a result, they are more like the Fae than anyone. Every Fairest is a diamond designed to cut anyone who tries to hold onto him or her. In my campaigns, most groups love to have a Fairest (despite the prima donna attitude) because he or she is so great at gathering information, getting favors from strangers in emergencies, getting along with people when in a new place, etc.
