    Homebrew Contract: Contracts of the Ruined Tower

    08-14-2014, 11:25 PM
    I had a fun idea for a contract and thought it might be fun to share it and get some commentary on balancing/mechanics. Any input would be great. =D

    Mood-setting story hook:

    Outside of town, just a few miles into the thick of the woods, there's an old castle, or at least there was. The ruins of the castle have stood out in that giant clearing for what's probably been centuries. Most of the ceilings have long since caved in. The stone walls are overgrown with thick vegetation. The trees themselves have encroached on what used to be cobbled streets leading up to the ancient structure. It's a fascinating place, but it lacks the sort of history for any real tourism money to be made by the nearby town. There was a rather wealthy lord that lived there and owned the surrounding countryside. He held onto his serfs with an iron fist and built his little territory into a strong estate complete with a small but well trained military and fields enough to keep everyone fed, One day he suddenly had a bizzare change of heart and decided he wanted to go to war abroad, so he gathered a battalion of men and marched off, never to return. The serfs became free-men and the manor houses became a freehold. As much as the town-folk liked the story, it wasn't anything more than a story to tell. To be honest most have forgotten the very name of the lord that lived there long before,

    Recently though, a strange old man has showed up in town, claiming to be the king of the castle returned from war. He was clearly mad, raving on about fantastical battles and wearing a bizarre mix of what looked like ragged robe and rusty chainmail. Apparently he did have some items of value though, since he garnered enough attention for a small gang of idiots tried to accost him in the street. Eventually he marched off on his own to the old castle, and no one has seen him since. A few people worried he probably died since there was little to eat in that direction. A few that did explore however came back with an impossible report. They saw it in the night, shining palely in the moonlight, tall battlements, banners with a symbol they'd never seen, fires within the windows, the sound of dancing from the balconies, and the roaring laughter of a massive bear of a man.

    Contracts of the Ruined Tower
    • Buried in the Ashes Many treasures are lost to the ages and forgotten, but just as often they may be recovered. With this clause the tower’s stone falls away to more easily unearth secrets.

    Effect: for every success the changeling is alerted to the presence and the general location of a nearby treasure he didn’t know about before. Items that are inherently magical are prioritized, followed by sentimental value, then monetary value.
    Exceptional: the changeling gets an idea of any sort of barriers magical or mundane in the way    		 The changeling mundanely searches for at least 10 minutes (ie. overturning rubble to find symbols in a castle ruin, looking through books to find a rare tome) ● Wits + Wyrd
    •• Ribs of Stone Some things can last over the years. The columns of old castles may last a century. Under the right conditions a blade may outlast the whole dynasty its maker lived in. With this clause, some ancient structures may be preserved a little longer. The changeling must spend a while inscribing the history of her target onto it, whether in etching, chalk, or a mural.
    Ban: The target may be a structure, an object, or a vehicle, but must be at least 25 years old.
    Extended: 1 success per point of structure of the target
    Time per roll: 1 hour
    effect: As the history of the target enwraps it, the object is reinforced with the weight of their own stories. The object gains points of durability = ½ Wyrd rounding up. This is magical in nature and resistant to all but Iron. If the inscription/image is thoroughly defaced the protection fades, but it also gains the benefit of the durability.
    duration: days = total successes in weeks
    Exceptional: The history of the target embeds deeply. Duration is doubled.    		 Tell the story of the target to at least a small group of people while pointing out the inscribed history ●● (○) Expression + Wyrd
    ••• Lessons from the Past “Imagine what they would say if these walls could talk, the knowledge they’d be able to share”
    With this clause of the contract the changeling may inherit the skill and knowledge of someone in the past. In order to use the contract they must know the name (not necessarily true name but one they would answer to) of a former owner of the targeted object.
    Ban: This may be used on an object, a structure, or a vehicle, but must have at one point belonged to someone who is now dead.
    Effect: The changeling gains skill points equal to successes appropriate to the target. A sword will give weaponry and maybe athletics, an old telescope might give investigate or drive, and a kingly court might give politics or expression.

    Duration: until sunset or sunrise, but ends early if contact is ever lost with the target
    Exceptional: The changeling may also manifest 3 merit points relevant to the target. a number of merit points = successes appropriate to the target. This can’t include anything that affects something other than the changeling like resources or status, but may include fighting styles, language dots, or anything that represents inherent ability.    		 The target belonged to a relative ●●● (○) Empathy + Wyrd
    •••• Shadows of Hallowed Halls Places are changed by and are made important by people. The denizens of a place are what give it life and what make it importing ant. With this clause a changeling may resurrect a scene from the past, summoning shades of individuals from a moment that occurred long before. The changeling and any others there may speak to the shades, who react as if the changeling had just walked into the scene from another room and will see the world as it was rather than its broken-down form. The scene recalled is ultimately up to the storyteller, but the character may aim or more vaguely suggest (ie: “the night of the masquerade ball”)
    Ban: the scene to re-play must be from at least 25 years ago
    Effect: a locale is populated by shades and shadows of whatever they interact with. A ballroom fills with dancers, a mage appears in his library over ancient tomes, and a sullen king sits again on his long-broken throne. These shades only interact physically with the world they see.

    Duration: a scene

    Note: This clause summons shades, not the real thing, but that doesn’t mean that the changeling’s words have no consequence. Echoes of conversations may not reach back through time, but if the contract is used to contact the same shades multiple times, they will remember the previous encounters, and if any of these people have left a ghost behind, the ghost will remember the encounters.

    Exceptional: The scene “recalled” is one where the people to be spoken to are in a particularly good mood, alternatively the changeling may choose a specific date and time.    		 The locale is appropriately set up for the occasion to be “recalled” ●●●●(○) Academics + Wyrd
    ••••• Grandeur of Ages Past So long as something is remembered, so it still lives on. So long as the ruins stand and remember, so may they may stand once again. With the final clause of the contract the changeling may restore something to its former glory. Boards of an ancient ship reform, banners of a fallen castle unwind from regrown battlements, a withered old man’s muscles knit together under blushing skin.
    Ban: the target may be a vehicle, structure, or object that is 50 years old or more. The target may also be a living person so long as they are 75 years old or more. May only use be used on a given target once per day.

    Effect: the target is restored to a point of former glory, restoring its shape to a more idealized time. Any formerly possessed properties are restored. Any damage from time, fire, natural erosion, explosives, anything other than true-iron may be undone. While the transformation is blatantly supernatural, the results will register exactly as they would have at the point being restored to under examination.
    Duration: till sunset or sunrise; at which time everything returns to the state it was at the time of casting, unless twilight finds it worse than when the contract was cast, in which case the additional damage is sustained. Similarly if a person dies while in their more youthful form,
    Note: when using on a living being stats and abilities are reset to a previous point in time. This may not actually be useful if the target is more powerful now than they were in the past.
    Exceptional: After the initial clause ends, the target retains some of the strength of what they once were. If targeting something inanimate, may receive the benefits of the second clause for free, if a person, he may gain the benefits of the third clause till the next sunset or sunrise.    		 The changeling activates the clause at midnight atop the highest point of the structure to be restored, or if targeting a living entity must be in physical contact at midnight. ●●●●● (○) Occult + Wyrd (vs. composure + wyrd if resisting)
