    Help please in putting my character to paper

    02-27-2018, 01:34 AM
    It's no secret that I love Warhammer 40k. It's also no secret that I love changeling (and exalted). So why am I here...well allow me to introduce you to my Ogre Razorhand

    Yet he's inspired heavily on Abbadon the Despoiler. This was at first a panicked choice of picture because everyone had already picked what they looked like and I panicked and grabbed this image.


    So now I'm trying to figure out how to make him as a Ogre razor hand in Changeling 2e.
    Here is the story I have so far. My keeper was fascinated by the narrative of these weird games mortals play. So she found the perfect mortal. Someone who she thought could help change the narrative of one game for one faction. Then she grabbed a few others to oppose me, gave us armies of hobs...and I failed. So outraged was she that I failed at her experiment, when she thought I should have won, that she made new armor for me made from spite, anger, and wrath. Then he lined the insides with flesh hooks and super heated all but one piece and welded it to my remade body. The armor was designed to help me win, that combined with the special sword and projectile weapon she grafted into my body.

    The second time around I won because I had help a strategy posed by a friend of mine, and fellow slave of our master and I used my wrath and power to break apart the fake little demi-world I was waging my crusade in. In that moment...a loyalist (to our keeper) playing the part of an angelic saint cut me open and nearly gave me a spine-ectomy. that moment though... as that world crumbled around us, that was all we needed to escape into the hedge.

    So...here is the question...I have 10 merit points...and 3 tokens that I can see.
    1- Sentient Sword that has a chance to harm me instead of attacking.
    2- Black armor (high quality)
    3 - A fey crafted automatic weapon that fires from two barrels at once...

    I would like to get this done with 2 merit points left for anything else like resources, but if all 10 are taken up in what I stole from that manipulative fey witch I'll take it.

              So I know I mentioned the tokens a lot but I was sleepy and those were foremost on my mind. The other thing I should have mentioned with Kith. Luckily I found this. wonderful write up on the Kith update thread by Aquatica1000,
              Razorhand
              The Fae wanted me to be a mindless weapon, to kill whatever they want. Well, no more. This blade is mine, and I'll choose whose blood to spill.
              When the Fae make a Razorhand, they’re looking for someone who can kill with ease, whether as an assassin striking from the shadows or a bodyguard cutting down those who stand in their master’s way. To that end, the Razorhands are taken by forced and altered – oftentimes via the painful implementation of some type of blade into their hands and fingers – only to then be used as living weapons. It is often with these skills that the changelings often slip their captors.
              Once away from Arcadia, though, the Razorhands still must figure out what to do with their newfound freedom. There are plenty who turn to the summer court, eager to turn their combat skill to good use, but there are others still who use their skills to strike fear. And then there are those who, remorseful for those they have slain, turn to pacifism to atone for their sins.
              Beast: He’s got the slitted eyes and striped pelt of a tiger, as well as the cocky attitude befitting one. When the going gets tough, though, he’s quick to demonstrate to his foes that his claws aren’t just meant to look pretty.
              Elemental: She is a representation of metal as a means of violence, with wiry hair and an expression one aggravation away from staring daggers. Sharp blades extend out of her hands almost naturally, as if she were born from them.
              Blessing: When a Razorhand charges at a target and makes an unarmed attack, three successes on the Brawl roll counts as an exceptional success.
              Aim to Maim: The Razorhand can choose to inflict lethal damage with his unarmed weapon (if the Changeling can already inflict lethal damage with unarmed attacks via abilities such as the Beast Seeming, it adds to the base damage by 1). In addition, by spending a point of Glamour and rolling Strength + Weaponry, the Razorhand can choose to automatically inflict one of the following Tilts in the next successful unarmed attack: Arm Wrack, Blinded, Deafened, or Leg Wrack.

              ​Awesome. Okay one step down, still thinking about the tokens I think I have an idea for that ranged weapon...

              Pacifier of Violent Dreams
              Grafted into the flesh of a razor handed servant this weapon turns glamour into solid slugs of deep biting, explosive deadly malice. While it rests this fae crafted weapon rests upon it's symbiotic partners hand and is virtually weightless it's mask causing it to appear as a spiked glove upon it's owners hand. Once fed ammunition it wakes up and becomes visible to those who view it, seeing to cast aside it's mask. In reality the weapon retreats into the hand of it's owner so that only a bulge and a spinned ridge is seen on the back of the hand while it rests. The weapon awakens and readies for battle it rises up out of the body of it's owner.
              Blessing: The weapon is functionally two heavy SMG working together with armor piecing high explosive rounds as ammunition. It has the following stat line..
              Hurt Locker Variant: Damage 3, Range Medium, Capacity High, Iniative -3, Min Strength 3 Size 2, 9 Again AP1
              Core Variant: Damage 3, Range 50/100/200 Clip 30+1, Initiative -3, Min strength 3, Size 2, Burst fire 9 again Armor Piercing 1
              Catch: If the host spends 1 round protecting an individual they have perviously no known connection, and takes at least 1L damage from doing so, with from violence then the weapon may be activated and fed for free.
              Drawback: Any time the host rolls an attack, and at least 1 one appears in the roll they must roll all the 1's that appeared. Successes cause 1B damage to the host as the weapons recoil harms them. If the number of 1's rolled in the attack exceeds the host's stamina they take an arm wrack tilt in addition to any damage from those dice.
              Special: Surgicaly implanted - just possessing this token causes the host 1L damage that cannot be healed as long as they have the implant. If the implant is removed for any reasons other than the loss of the arm it deal 2L additional to the host.

              I'm thinking that's a Level 2 token?

                                          Tenebrous Prison of the Black Crusader
                                          This suit of Black baroque plate armor was forged by the hands of one of the keepers. This armor is black as midnight in it's mien and etched with golden patterns. While terrifyingly beautiful to behold it is also a hell for the one entombed within it. The armor is attached using flesh hooks and sears into the flesh of the wearer. It cannot be removed without risking the life of the one inside, except for 2 pieces. The pelvic plate and helm, for allowing the wearer to tend to the needs of the flesh. Theses are the only pieces not attached by the searing flesh hooks.
                                          This armor causes the wearer to bear 2 points of aggravated damage at all times while the armor is attached to their body. This damage cannot be downgraded or healed until the armor is removed. Removing a piece other than the helm or pelvic plate causes 3L damage to the wearer. or 2L if done by an expert surgeon at a hospital. The armor has the following pieces. x2 Gauntlet, x2 bracer, x2 vambrace , x2 Paldron, Helm, Breast plate, Greaves (with removable pelvic plate), Shin Plates x2, Boots x2, Soft armor gambison. It's mask is the emotional armor of the biker, in their protective leathers.

                                          Blessing of the Black Crusade: While wearing the suit the wearer benefits from a +2 bonus to all strength rolls, and is not subject to the normal minimum bashing damage. This armor cannot be combined with any magic that would normally grant an armor bonus.
                                          The armors stats are as below The strength required is before the bonus it grants.
                                          Teneberus Prison of the Black Crusader
                                          Value: 4/4 Strength: 3 Defense -3 Speed -4
                                          Catch: The wearer swears an oath to an enemy at the beginning of the battle that they will kill them, and during the battle kills them, and claimed their head as a trophy.
                                          Drawback: Removing the armor, provided that didn't kill them, is likely to cause sever withdrawal symptoms in the host. Following the addiction path, until they either willfully return to it or go 1 year without it.
                                          Special: As above the armor causes the host to have a constant 2A damage that cannot be healed or reduced by any means as long as the armor is on. Removal of any of the armor pieces other than the two mentioned above causes sever damage to the body, and removal of the armor is likely to cause death. The armor will reshape it's self for a new host if put on by a new hosts either 1 year after it's removal from the old host, or if the old host dies.
