    [New Hobgoblin] Doctor Humorsworth

    07-03-2017, 10:16 PM


    Deep in the Hedge is a road which cuts through a macabre combination of a graveyard and garden and leads to a Victorian style mansion at the top of a cliff. Despite the eternal night and lightning storms, the stitched together guardians and strange lights that sometimes come from within, people and supernaturals from all walks of life line up to see the brilliant hobgoblin that lives there.

    Known as Doctor Humorsworth (so named for his eccentric jokes and the theory of bodily humors), he makes his living as a psychiatrist and inventor. He listens to anyone who comes to his clinic in goodwill, and shows a profound level of knowledge and emotional insight that often leaves his patients both awed and shaken. More than a few have come into his clinic experiencing horrific problems and doubting his talent, only to leave with clear thoughts and a peaceful spirit. He seems almost human in demeanor during his therapy sessions, and treats everyone with the same diligence and compassion.

    It would seem that he is without psychological cracks, but nothing could be farther from the truth. Doctor Humorsworth has simply managed to turn his morbid passions into a livelihood. What drives him is a powerful desire for knowledge, and he is not afraid to use…unorthodox methods in this pursuit. By mixing fae magic with all kinds of mortal science, he achieves both wonders and horrors, all in the name of progress and being prepared to treat any ailment. And he is all too eager to share his passions and discoveries.

    Storytelling Hints: When asked if he is really a doctor, Humorsworth acts surprised and shows his collection of credentials and awards. Some of these come from mortal institutions, but others mention a Hedge University that certain changelings might recognize as the magnum opus of a legendary Autumn Courtier. In fact, Humorsworth’s Hollow contains a portal to his office in this place, where he teaches aspiring doctors about bizarre but surprisingly effective techniques to treat the ill. The alumni and teachers consist of both humans and supernaturals, but mostly fae. He claims to have been tutored by none other the legendary founder of the University, and he helps to organize competitions and collaborative projects in honor of the Founder. If the PCs manage to convince him to give them passage into this institution, they may join the competitions and projects, but they are warned up front that things work differently here. This is a meritocracy where results are worth more than means, and while incredible rewards await those who have the talent, no one is quite who they seem to be. And rumors have been going around that the Founder is coming back, which has resulted in an all time peak in productivity and intrigue. Can the PCs stand out and hold an audience with the Founder, or will they become pawns in some weird scheme?

    One of the PCs has a therapy session with Humorsworth and it goes marvelously, and sometime after that he invites them to sign a Pledge to become a research assistant. He promises to give samples of the results of his research, but he will not be held responsible for any side effects. The PC will no doubt be very wary, but Humorsworth will show them the various Goblin Fruit and Tokens he has invented, and allow them use a few to see for themselves that this is no scam. And these items always seem to be perfectly suited for whatever problems the PC is facing. Should they accept, Humorsworth will keep his word, but are the risks worth it?

    While exploring his mansion, the PCs come across three little grotesque but very friendly homunculi who meet them like excited children. Humorsworth’s attention is drawn by the commotion, and the homunculi call him “Uncle”. If the PCs ask too many questions, he tells his “nephews” to go play outside with one of the guardians for a moment, and the conversation goes dead quiet. He quickly composes himself and avoids providing any details about his “family”, only that he created them with his own hands. Why is he so evasive about this? Can the homunculi be persuaded to reveal his secrets? Are there more of his creations somewhere? How did the create them? What will he do if he finds out the PCs have been snooping around?

    Virtue: Understanding/Curious
    Vice: Amoral

    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 6, Resolve 4
    Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 5, Stamina 3
    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 4, Composure 5

    Skills: Academics 4, Crafts (Goblin Fruit) 3, Computer 1, Medicine (Unorthodox Methods, Surgery) 6, Occult (Fae) 4, Politics 3, Investigation 3, Science 4, Athletics 1, Firearms 2, Larceny (Security Systems) 3, Stealth 2, Survival (Hedge) 3, Weaponry (Hacksaw) 1, Animal Ken 3, Empathy (Comforting) 5, Expression (Humor) 3, Intimidation 2, Persuasion (Gain Trust) 4, Socialize 4, Streetwise 2, Subterfuge (Careful Omission) 3

    Merits: Area Of Expertise (Unorthodox Methods, Hedge), Common Sense, Eidetic Memory, Empath, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Medicine, Occult), Eye For The Strange, Holistic Awareness, Interdisciplinary Specialty (Unorthodox Methods, Hedge), Investigative Aide (Empathy), Library (Occult 3, Medicine 3, Advanced 1 [Fae]), Professional Training 5 (Fae Doctor: Medicine, Occult, Empathy), Tolerance For Biology, Trained Observer 3, Virtuous, Allies (Fae Doctors 4), Closed Book 3, Contacts (Patients, Goblin Markets), Fame 1, Hobbyist Clique (Fae Doctors: Medicine), Inspiring, Iron Will, Resources 3, Pusher, Retainer (Homunculi “Nephews” 3), Staff 3 (Lab Assistants: Medicine), Sympathetic, Table Turner, Brownie’s Boon, Faerie Healing, Harvest 4 (Goblin Fruit), Master Shaper 5, Hollow (Spooky Mansion-Laboratory: Anything Needed)

    Willpower: 9
    Defense: 6
    Initiative: 10
    Speed: 13
    Health: 8
    Wyrd: 6

    Fae Aspects:

    Trust Me, I’m A Doctor: Doctor Humorsworth has access to the following Contracts, and the Blessings of the Chirurgeon, Whisperwisp, Maker, Muse and Truefriend Kiths. He may use his Muse Blessing on both mortals and supernaturals, and all his patients are protected by his Truefriend Blessing.

    Changeling Fate 2 (Wizened), Pathfinder (Beast, Elemental), Read Lucidity (Fairest), Trivial Reworking (Wizened, Ogre), Dreamsteps (Fairest), Vainglory 3 (Splendor Of The Envoy’s Protection, Words Of Memories Never Lived, Fairest, Wizened), Discreet Conjuration (Wizened), Flickering Hours, Portents And Visions, Shared Burden (Ogre, Wizened), Temporary Lucidity (Fairest), Armored Clarity (Ogre, Wizened), Tatterdemalion’s Workshop (Wizened, Ogre).

    Wyrd Science: Doctor Humorsworth is capable of Hedgespinning, and may roll Medicine instead of Crafts depending on what he intends to create. He is also considered to have Goblin Vow (Unorthodox Medicine).

    Skeleton In The Closet: A number of times per story equal to his Wyrd, Doctor Humorsworth may spend 1 Glamour and roll Manipulation + Empathy + Wyrd (vs Composured + Supernatural Tolerance if the target is unwilling), and offer his Successes as bonus dice to the target(s) next Clarity, Integrity or equivalent roll (maximum +5). He gives +1 even if he fails the roll. This takes the form of a therapy session, during which the target(s) do not suffer from Integrity Breaking Points for facing the supernatural, or Clarity Breaking Points for facing triggers. However, he is considered to have the Leveraged Condition against those who benefit from this Fae Aspect. Furthermore, he may use his Empath Merit to give 8 Again on Breaking Point rolls to both mortals and supernaturals at ST’s discretion.
