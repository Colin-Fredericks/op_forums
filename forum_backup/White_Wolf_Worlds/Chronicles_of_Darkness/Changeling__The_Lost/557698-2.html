                    Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
                    In the Darkling write up it really felt like Background and Escape were describing one specific person. The Ogre write up's background section describes the situation Ogres were in (bully victim) rather than their charachter traits (The Darkling backgrounds talks about them as loyal people). As a result it doesn't feel so much like a single charachter.

                    That's why you have Xenesis and WyrdHampster praising the new format. It's not a new format, but it still feels like it.
                    Yes, sorry for mix up with character concepts numbers. But King Raven's autopsy is correct one - Ogres write-up is centered on things the characters endured, not who they are. It's very important change - Seemings should be about your narrative in Arcadia, not about traits character have. Because I want to be able to make loyal and disloyal Darklings. Or leading and unleading Fairest. Or any other Seeming - Seemings are stories, are choices now.

                    But they are not character psychological traits - or we would end with "always loyal Ventrue" or "morbid Moros" splats. Yes, those are part of character concepts pools, but X-Splats are more about transformation and "common themes" - they are more about stories of character to this day. Especially with Seemings role as "story character choose over one putted by Keeper".

                    So I have a request to developers - please, write 2ED Seemings as stories, not psychological traits. Becuase, like with Ogres, the same stimuli can lead to slightly diffrent results - but those results still are all Ogres characters. They still have one Seeming.
