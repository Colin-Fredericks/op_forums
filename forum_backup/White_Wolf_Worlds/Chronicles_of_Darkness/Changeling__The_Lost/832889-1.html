                                                        Okay. The more 2E Changeling fluff and mechanics I see churned out, the more excited I am for when everything gets out. So on top of wanting to see more stuff on the Contracts, as well as more discussion about this "Embracing the Wyrd" mechanic you mentioned in passing, I'm also interested in seeing how Needle and Thread (the Virtue and Vice analogue for the Lost, I assume) will be handled.

                                                        Moving on to the kiths...

                                                        Cleareyes are definitely my favorite of this batch. I knew they were going to get Trained Observer for free, but the way their trademark senses were handled made it so much better than what I did for my stopgap conversion of the kith. Also, when I read that they weren't affected by any penalties due to impeded perception, does that mean Conditions/Tilts from blindness and deafness don't have as much bite for them as they do for everyone else? Either way, they're very potent spotters when you add in the benefits of (the current draft of) Clarity.

                                                        The Corpsegrinders can make very effective assassins with their blessings, seeing as they have a good chance of killing someone (again, in the case of undead) with one blow. I do agree, though, that maybe they should also benefit from a form of regeneration. Like, maybe, the first [Wyrd] points of damage they inflict in a turn allow them to heal the same amount of bashing/lethal damage? Maybe you can limit it to every instance of a killing blow, if it's too powerful and/or overlaps with the Gristlegrinders and Leechfingers.

                                                        Shadowsouls are also much more interesting in 2E, seeing as they can extend the bonuses of their blessing to anyone they're touching. Add in their ability to shadow walk, and you have a kith that works well for those who are assassins, scouts, and spies in terms of Attributes, Skills, and Specialties.

                                                        One word on Soldiers. Awesome. I was instantly reminded of Taskmaster (and, to a lesser extent, Mokujin and Velvet Scarlatina). And I had this image in my head of a Soldier catching a vampire off-guard when he suddenly begins using the techniques for Kindred Duelists. All in all, another solid conversion of an offense-oriented kith to make them even more dangerous on the battlefield.

                                                        Swimmerskins were handled well, with their conversion. No overlap with the other aquatic-based kiths aside from their common advantage. But seeing as their write-up was made with the assumption that a Swimmerskin has a persistent Leg Wrack, does that mean they can still swim fine even if they get the Tilt? I'm a bit confused there, so maybe a little clarification is in order.

                                                        Like with the Soldiers, Miners are a lot more potent as a kith, now. I kinda was hoping they'd retain their ability to communicate via the 'morse code' of their original blessing, though. Can we expect that to make a reappearance as a Contract?

                                                        For the Flamesirens, maybe their blessing could use a small revision to really reflect how they're "too hot to handle". I don't know. Letting them have their Extreme Environment Tilt be limited to anyone with the Swooning Condition seems a bit restrictive to me. Though I can kind of understand how it could be abused. For an alternative take, though, why not let the Flamesiren be treated as an Extreme Environment of [Wyrd/2] potency, by default? And then apply the full Wyrd rating for those with the Condition? The changeling could even suppress the effect, at will.

                                                        Lurkgliders and Windwings were a surprise addition, seeing as I had assumed they were folded into the Airtouched. But I'm liking them all the same, especially the Windwings. Now, they can really fly (however briefly they can manage it in each attempt).

                                                        Dearhearts are definitely a good kith for designing nuanced characters, whether it be the manipulative Incubi and Succubi or the more genuinely sympathetic guardian angel who'll happily listen to your problems before setting off to ease your burden... one way or another. Of course, with the modular rules for making custom kiths, it should be easy to design kiths that evoke the seduction theme more strongly.

                                                        Steepscramblers reminded me of a number of characters the same way the Solders did. In this case, it was Spiderman, Daredevil, and the Assassins from Assassin's Creed,

                                                        Definitely wasn't expecting the Weisse Frau to become telekinetics. But I'm certainly not arguing about it, either.

                                                        All in all, great work on this batch.


                                                        PS: Is Dual Kith still a thing in 2E?
