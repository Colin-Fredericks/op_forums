                                        Preparing for the ball. Lorelei is Gawain is worried, Lorelei is down, Calamity is stressed, Jacob is melancholy. Just another typical day at the Dream House.

                                        Session XXIV

                                        Gawain’s Memories

                                        June 13th

                                        Well, yesterday kinda took a turn. I think the girls had fun at the shopping trip, but by the time the show came around, everybody was down. To be fair, really it was Lori that was down, but then she sang [a really sad song]. I mean, that’s a pretty sad song, but she made it, you know, really really sad. I don’t think anybody wasn’t crying, at least a little. Calamity started drinking at the bar instead of doing her usual smoozing thing. She was playing with the scar she got from her blood sister thing with Anastasia. I was really worried she had figured out something bad had happened to Stasia, but she was just missing her. I mean, that’s still not good, but it’s not, like, final or anything.

                                        I kept her company for a bit, but then Lori went right upstairs after her set, and Calamity pointed out that De la Roche wasn’t here, and she’s -always- here. We checked up on her, and she was dressed in pajamas, which was kinda weird. She said De la Roche had to work through things, I guess before the Greening and all. It seemed to come out of nowhere, but Calamity seemed like she understood, and Lori said they hadn’t broken up or anything, so I think it’ll be ok? She said once the whole show was over and folks were gone, she’d come down and have ice cream and bourbon and watch a movie with people, which seemed like a good plan. She certainly had the hangover to go with that today.

                                        I had to duck out to go to the Spring Court. I felt bad leaving Lori upset like that, and Calamity not ok either, but I wasn’t gonna be the best at helping out anyway, and helping Desiree was important too. She trusted me, and I needed to do something to make it better. That went pretty well - I think me and Desiree might have had the best nights of anyone. I didn’t find what I needed yet, but it was right there. Tonight I’ll get it, I’m sure. She’s a strong dreamer too - I didn’t have any problems slipping in, but I didn’t run any risk of overwhelming her ‘scape.

                                        Her dream was affected a little by the event of the evening for her part - she and Princess Joli had been making out when I got there. She was in much better spirits, even if Becky was, you know, not happy. Not happy at all. I can’t really blame her... if I didn’t trust Desiree, and was in charge of the Princess’s safety, I’d probably be just as pissy. But it was good to see Desiree less... hopeless. And her dream was upbeat, her in a theatre with a large appreciative audience - I think I saw Joli in there, out of the corner of my eye. The people other than Desiree flickered between being kinda vague and faceless and being regular people though. I didn’t really see anything I’d have pinpointed as control though, like strings, or cue cards or something that fit the scene. I didn’t have a full insight though, so we’ll have to just see, you know?

                                        Anyway, I got back to the Dream House in time to make breakfast. Lori was super hung over, like I mentioned, so she just had aspirin and OJ. I suggested she have something with salt, to help with the water retention, you know, so she put salt in her OJ, which was disgusting, of course. And probably didn’t mix well with the taste of dead skunk plus ashtray she said she already had when she woke up. Jacob made coffee, and looked a little foggy. When I noticed the coffee pot had been used a lot last night, I asked him if everything had gone ok. Then he started telling me about Chalcedony having stayed the night because Becky needed help and so Calamity couldn’t watch over Chalcedony and it was kinda a mess.

                                        That was about the time Calamity showed up from Becky’s, and Chalcedony came up from the guest room in the basement. Once Becky had come to pick Chalcedony up, I asked for clarification and Calamity told me the whole story in order. So, Flores had called Julia and asked her to keep an eye out for Chalcedony if she came back, cause she seemed unwell. When she didn’t come by, Calamity decided to go check up on her. She found Chalcedony agitated and pacing and smoking. See, she’d gotten a weird phone call, with no one talking at the other end, just tones, and since then she felt foggy, wasn’t thinking straight and couldn’t concentrate. She didn’t lose time or anything, but it was probably still the same thing, since Desiree had also gotten a phone call from a Private number just before she called Princess Joli to set up the date.

                                        So Calamity was going to stay the night and make sure nothing kicked in when she went to sleep, but first she called Becky to let her know what was going on, and Becky was really, really not happy. I guess after I went in with Desiree, Becky and Joli had an argument about Desiree coming to the Masquerade (Joli insists) and the two of them spending time together and everything. Princess Joli got mad enough that she told Becky to leave her be and not watch over her while she was at home, and Becky wasn’t taking it well. I think Becky’s in love with her too. Calamity likes Becky, so she went to go make sure Becky was alright, and took Chalcedony to the Dream House so Jacob could watch over her.

                                        So that was all exciting. Things seemed a little calmer after breakfast - Lori went to sleep it off some more, Jacob took a nap too, and me and Calamity headed off to tour the house the Masquerade was going to be in with the rest of the security team. That was Becky, of course, and Davey and Sandeval from the Spring Court. It was a little surprising to see Davey there, but he’s a Mirrorskin, which does sound super useful for security stuff. TJ and a pair of dudes called Louis Guo and Ji Wen Ja were there from the Summer Court. Louis Guo and Ji Wen Ja seemed really cool. They introduced themselves to us later. Well, Louis did, cause Ji Wen Ja doesn’t speak English. He can understand it, just not speak it. He’s got a cool thing where he can make it so that no one can fight around him and he can calm situations, which is pretty handy. Louis was very careful to point out that they looked to the Viscount, specifically. And that TJ looked to Lucien. Politics stuff, but good to know, you know? I like the Summer Court in general, and I like Joan, but Lucien makes me... concerned, sometimes. So it’s good to know there’s another faction, I guess?

                                        The site itself was big. There were three dance rooms - a big ball room and a pair of smaller rooms set up, one for rave type stuff and one for other more modern dances - plus about 30 side rooms for liasons. The hallway between the dance rooms had a surveillance station that we had a key to. We had a key to all the rooms actually, though of course we’re not to barge into a side room without an emergency. But if one of the silent alarms went off that were handed out to folks who wanted, that counted, and of course anything obvious.

                                        Becky also went over specific situations to watch out for, like Desiree and Chalcedony and Mary and whoever shot Dark Kimberly. Sandeval mentioned that the Warlock of Night had just today accepted the traditional invitation. That threw Becky for a loop and I almost felt bad bringing up Felicity, but it seemed important and pertinent with the Night Court having a bigger presence. Apparently she has a particular bad relationship with the Warlock’s Handmaidens. They threatened to eat her, and not only in a good way. So we’re just going to try and keep them apart, as best we can, you know? I gave her a call to warn her about that, and also let her know that it probably wasn’t the Summer Court breaking in. I’m still kinda hoping that her being away at the Masquerade will get whoever it is to make a move, and we can catch them out, either using Insincere Marguerite’s mirror powers, or just regular security.

                                        After the tour, Calamity went off visit Annika. She had called Queen Charlotte to talk about some Court business, and the Queen had pointed out that maybe it wasn’t the Director at all, but someone was targetting people of the Dancer kith. Since Annika is the only other one of those, Calamity offered to go warn her. On the way home though, so she could get her bike, she asked me if I really wanted to do the Greening. The way she said it at first I almost wasn’t sure if she was trying to say she wasn’t sure about it anymore, so I pulled over so I could actually talk. That’s not what she was trying to say, I'm pretty sure now, but she started talking about there being other prettier, nicer, more dangerous girls and that she didn’t want to force me into belonging to her, so I was glad I pulled over anyway. I held as close as I could there in the van, and told her of course I did, cause of course I did. I think all the craziness of not being able to figure something out and *do* something about it is getting to her again. I hope it’s just that and not something I’ve done that makes her think I might want someone else more.

                                        It makes me feel really bad that I’ll be away again tonight, but I was so close... I think she understands. I hope she does. Jacob pulled her aside to spar after the show as I was leaving, which is really good of him, you know? I think that’ll help even her out. The show did good too. Lori was less depressed - she sang a blues song, but it was Weird Al’s ‘Generic Blues’, so it was as fun as anything else. She started out dressed in a long trenchcoat and wide brimmed hat, before throwing it off to reveal a sparkly dress. Captain Jack came by to make more plans, dressed in a long trench coat and wide brimmed hat too, so he could be incognito. He didn’t have a sparkly dress underneath though. Since they were dressed so close to the same they decided to do an impromptu Stand by Your Man performance, which was awesome. Captain Jack is a great singer.

                                        He’s also good at acquiring things - he managed to get a Playboy bunny outfit to fit him, through intimidation I guess. Oh right, I almost forgot to mention. Calamity decided a blue Playboy bunny outfit would be the best match for her Frank mask. I’m not going to argue with that, for sure, though Captain Jack in that is going to be... special. We decided to do the whole scene about nine - late enough that folks would all be there, but not going to interfere with things like the Greening or anything.

                                        We talked some about the Warlock’s Handmaidens too - he called them Thing 2 and Thing 1. They seem to be on good terms actually, though apparently it took a lot to get there. They were displeased that they couldn't seduce him, then failed at pranking them, but then they all three got rip roaring drunk together and now they’re friends. According to their driver’s licenses, they are married to each other, and according to Captain Jack, they are very ticklish. It was a very different conversation about them than I’d had with anyone else, so it was good we talked with him. Also good so I don’t misjudge any teasing that goes on between them.



                                        June 14th

                                        Yes! I figured it out! Well, kinda. It was touch and go a bit, especially at first when I almost shattered her dreamspace hitching a ride. But I managed to keep it together and something kept pulling me backstage. Like, the whole dream was a stage, and I was in among the rigging and the machinery and curtain stuff. One of the control systems had holes around it, like someone strong was flailing around in the dark, and knew there was a switch but not quite where. It was in the off position when I found it, and when I flipped it, the scene completely changed on stage, to what looked like a dinner date, and a mannikin that looked kinda like Joli was on stage with Desiree, who was acting wooden all of the sudden. No, more like clockwork. Anyways, definitely different, you know?

                                        So it wasn’t some kind of mind control directly, but an implanted thing. A piece of her psyche that was waiting to be switched. So, Manchurian candidate was the right idea, I guess. But whoever switched it didn’t know what they were doing, which was why it was so clumsy. Like, they got her to go on a date with Joli but they couldn’t cover it up well, so there was the lost time and the obvious threat. It clearly wasn’t her Keeper, or whoever implanted the switch, who did it, just someone who figured it out.

                                        I even figured out I could get rid of the switch, though it’d probably take a while. One of those Oneirophysics I’d heard about might be able to fix it faster, but there weren’t any of them around, that I knew of. But that means we could get rid of Chalcedony’s switch, if it was the same thing, and anyone else who comes from the Director too, now that we know where to look, which is awesome!


                                        Lorelei’s Diary

                                        I am getting sick of starting these journal entries with sentences or paragraphs about how terrible I’ve been feeling. This one really takes the cake, though. Tomorrow is the masquerade; I should be over the moon with excitement. It’s one of the biggest events of the Spring, and since my Court really knows how to party, I imagine it’s going to be spectacular.

                                        So my life started to go to shit yesterday about an hour before the show. I’d been planning to do an upbeat number; something to really get the crowd clapping their hands and stomping their feet. Then D dropped by and asked to speak with me privately. I said sure and we walked into a currently unoccupied room and closed the door.

                                        “I’m not going to stay here tonight,” she said glumly.

                                        “Okay,” I said, a little disappointed.

                                        “I’m going to get some vodka, a pack of sexy dude playing cards, and… probably something even stronger than the vodka,” she continued. “I’m going to have a long conversation with myself. More of an argument, really. Then, myself and I are going to find my self-image, and my insecurities, and we’re going to hash this whole thing out. I’m probably going to yell, and I might cry. And if I have to, I’m going to beat the shit out of that bitch until she sees the light. It won’t be pretty. I don’t know if I’ll be back tomorrow or not, but I’ll be here on Saturday, and I promise I’ll have this whole thing worked out by then, okay?”

                                        I’d been expecting her to have some sort of crisis/epiphany over her sexuality or her self-image for a few days, so it didn’t really come as much of a surprise (though her wording of the situation threw me for a minute). “D,” I said softly, “I know you need to do this, and I know you need to work through it on your own. But is there anything I can do to help you? Anything at all?”

                                        She shook her head and thought about it for a moment. “I might call you up and ask you to come over to my place tomorrow,” she said eventually, “and it’s cool if you want to. But… don’t have sex with me there, okay? Not even if I ask you to. If I show up here, it’s fine… that means I’ve worked things out already.”

                                        I nodded in understanding. I know that Insincere Marguerite or Calamity’s friend Tommy could be listening in on any conversation we had in the Dream House, so I leaned in, kissed her on the cheek, and whispered in her ear. “I love you, Maria, and I will be waiting for you when you come back, okay?”

                                        She nodded, kissed me back, and turned to leave. “I’ll… I’ll see you Saturday, if not sooner, okay Lori?” she said without turning around. I acknowledged her statement briefly and then she was gone.

                                        I couldn’t let myself fall apart; it was time for makeup and costumes. I talked with some of the girls and managed to arrange substitutions for all of the numbers except my solo near the end of the first set. I really wasn’t in the mood to sing “Copacabana” or anything like that, so I spoke with Marguerite about a substitution. She raised an eyebrow at my selection and asked me if everything was alright, and I assured her that I would be fine, and then made my way backstage again.

                                        When my number came up, I walked out in a sedate blue evening gown. It was pretty enough, but not nearly as sexy as most of my outfits. I had my hair brushed out straight over one shoulder, and kept my eyes mostly lidded through the number to hide the sheen that I knew would show there. As the opening chords of Eric Clapton’s guitar drifted softly from the house speakers, I began to sing.

                                        “Tears In Heaven” came out years after I was taken, but I came across it when researching songs to generate sorrow as a favor for our friends in the Winter Court. It is beautiful, devastatingly sad, and the lyrics have almost nothing to do with my current situation, so it’s not giving away anything personal. It seemed like a perfect choice. I put my all into that number, and infused it with a little glamour to ensure that everyone could feel the sorrow as keenly as I did. I admit it was a little spiteful of me, and I’m kind of ashamed of it now, but they do say that misery loves company.

                                        After my number I retreated upstairs to my bedroom. I dug out a pair of warm, comfy flannel pajamas. They were a gift from D; kind of a gag gift since she of all people knows I almost never wear anything to bed. They were tailored by a Changeling I haven’t met in Santa Monica who specializes in making clothing for those of us with “interesting” physiques, so they have large slits cut in the back to accommodate my wings. They aren’t simple tears in the fabric, either… the edges are doubled up and sewn so that the garment will stand up to use.

                                        They’re a little baggy on me, and they’re this ugly red tartan pattern. D claims that she gave them to me to prove that I would look sexy no matter what I was wearing. I love them.

                                        Calamity and Gawain knocked on my door a little while later. I was a bit surprised, because the show was still going on. Calamity explained that they were worried because it’s not like me to leave the parlor before the end of a show, and Julia had told her that D was acting weird earlier. I told them that I was fine, but of course they didn’t leave it at that. I wouldn’t have left it at that if one of them was acting like I had been acting.

                                        I sniffled and wiped at my eyes. “D is working through a few things. It’s… not easy for her, and there’s nothing I can do to help her through it, and I hate feeling helpless.”

                                        They commiserated, but there wasn’t really a lot they could do to help. I announced my intention to head downstairs after the crowds had dispersed and lay claim to a half-gallon of chocolate ice cream and a bottle of bourbon. Calamity and Gawain both told me that they’d be happy to join me in a movie marathon while I drown my sorrows. I gave them a weak smile and told them that it sounded like a good plan to me.

                                        Didn’t work out that way, unfortunately. Gawain got the call from Glamour Springs that he was welcome to try his oneiromancy on Desiree tonight, and Calamity got word that Chalcedony had been acting strangely at the Rose & Thorn earlier in the evening, and went to check on her. When I came downstairs, only Jacob, Insincere Marguerite, and Julia were anywhere to be found (I have no idea where Ace went). Calamity actually called me to apologize for bailing, but I told her I knew that she had more important things to do.

                                        It was almost funny how quickly she got indignant at that statement, but I was in no mood to appreciate the humor. “Okay, if not more important, then at least more immediate,” I corrected myself. “I understand, Calamity. Don’t worry. You’re off the hook, I promise.” I think she might have been worried that I was still mad over the mudslinging incident. I hope I reassured her on that count.

                                        I don’t remember much of last night. I know Jacob started me off with a mudslide, which was very tasty, but did not deter me from dipping (deep) into the bourbon next. Julia was concerned about me and D, and I did my best to reassure her (and myself) that things would be fine. Insincere Marguerite seemed largely unconcerned, and spent most of her time being fed small slices of banana by Julia.

                                        Julia actually brought me a little pot when I idly asked if anyone had any. Just enough for one joint… just enough to get me a little mellow. What can I say? I just didn’t want to think seriously about anything last night.

                                        I was completely shit-faced by the time Calamity showed up a couple of hours later with Chalcedony in tow. I don’t really remember much of the encounter, but the others have since filled me in on the details. Apparently Chalcedony received a telephone call earlier that day, and heard a series of tones with no associated words. She said she hadn’t felt quite right since; like her thoughts were fuzzy and unfocused, and she didn’t trust her own judgment. It had been Calamity’s intention to stay with Chalcedony at her place, just to ensure that she would be safe and wouldn’t do anything unfortunate. But then Becky had some sort of crisis and needed a friend, so Calamity decided to drop Chalcedony off at the Dream House and go check on her pal.

                                        I’m told I had a somewhat embarrassing conversation with Chalcedony, but I really don’t remember it. She apparently asked if I was alright, and I blurted out all of my insecurities about how the girl I loved was off wrestling with her sexuality and I was afraid that she was going to dump me because I “don’t have a dick,” and how that would utterly destroy me. Wow… I didn’t even know all that was in me. Understandably, Chalcedony chose to just retire to a guest room after that.

                                        Jacob made sure I got to bed okay, and made sure a big glass of cold water was on my nightstand to help with the next morning’s inevitable hangover. I’m pretty sure I didn’t proposition him (or anyone else) last night; I was not in the mood for lovin’ at all. I wanted D, but mostly I just wanted her to be there and to be okay.

                                        I woke up this morning (or early afternoon) with a splitting headache and a terrible taste in my mouth (I later described it as tasting “like a skunk had dropped its ashtray in my mouth before it died”). I staggered down to the kitchen, my hair a mess and drool drying on my chin. Jacob smiled sympathetically when I stumbled in and offered me a glass of orange juice with aspirin ground up in it. He also suggested that I should try to get some salt back in my system, so I shrugged, upended a shaker over the orange juice, and downed the whole glass. It was vile.

                                        I went back to bed for an hour or two. After the headache subsided, I spent the rest of the afternoon researching blues music, trying to come up with an act for the show that would suit my mood and still be club-appropriate. I came across a number of songs which seemed suitable, but I also came across something else which surprised me.

                                        I figured the best place to start looking was with the grand-daddy of all blues musicians, Mr. B. B. King himself. I came across an article online in which he listed what were, in his opinion, the ten best blues songs ever performed. I recognized some of them, and others were completely new to me. The biggest surprise came in the form of a song called “Generic Blues,” by an artist with the unlikely name of Weird Al Yankovic.

                                        Hey, jazz and blues musicians have always had colorful nicknames. I didn’t think anything of it at the time. But it turns out that Mr. Yankovic is a fairly famous parodist, and his “Generic Blues” song was incredibly funny. I think it must have flipped a switch inside me or something, because I couldn’t stop laughing for about five minutes. And that got me thinking… I’d been obsessing over D’s absence for a day, even though I know she’s doing this for us, so that she can devote herself to our relationship wholeheartedly.

                                        I guess I really am as self-absorbed as people say. You have to laugh, right? It sure beats the hell out of being depressed for no good reason. I still miss her, and I know I will continue to miss her until we’re back together for good. But I’m not going to let it take over my whole life any more.

                                        D actually called a little while before the show. She sounded a little choked up. She said that she wouldn’t be at the show tonight, and she wouldn’t be staying here with me tonight either… but she also promised that she would come by the Dream House tomorrow before the masquerade, and that she would be whatever I needed her to be. I tried to be strong for her as I told her again that I’d wait as long as she needed. I… hope she finds what she’s looking for soon, though.

                                        I decided not to share my plans for the show with the rest of the motley, so I just gave Insincere Marguerite a thumb drive with instructions to play the file it contained when my solo came up, and just to throw a single spot on me when the music started. I had been running on willpower and coffee more than anything before we even started. The burn and hum in my muscles started easing into aching and it took more and more effort to keep myself moving full tilt. But I didn't want to stop and I thought I had a bit more in me, a few more passes at least. Jacob asked me as we were circling between clashes if I'd gotten any sleep at all last night. I admitted I had not and he just nodded and kept going. I should have stopped or at least slowed the pace down.

                                            But I wanted to keep going and I wanted it enough that I convinced myself I was still good to keep going even though I knew better. We kept at it a bit longer until I was seriously feeling the strain. And that's when I fumbled. He was coming in fast and low and I started to spin and sidestep. His eyes followed my movement, flicking to my razorhands, one low and one higher. He shifted his weight, preparing to pivot and turn the thrust into a slash as he passed, a move that would force me to step back far enough to avoid it that I wouldn't be in reach to cut him while he was off balance from the lunge. Only my body was two steps behind rather than ahead. Jacob's eyes flickered with surprise as it suddenly became clear that I was actually still going to be there when his knife reached me. He shifted abruptly, twisting his arm and knife away from us both and crashed into me shoulder first.

                                            I was off kilter to begin with and so was he at that point. So down we went in a graceless tumble. I landed hard enough to snap one of my shards off almost at the skin (fuck did that hurt like a bitch... it's still throbbing as I write this.) Jacob came down on top of me and for a moment we just laid there before we started to untangle ourselves and help each other up. As we started to check each other for injuries I managed to break the quiet with a quip about how I generally preferred to be the one on top.

But Jacob still got the worse of it with a couple of long though thankfully shallow cuts from my glass. Thankfully, nothing needed stitches (or rousting Lori for her healing magic). So the first aid kit was brought out and we took turns cleaning and bandaging each other.

                                            I was rapidly moving into sore and a little shaky, clear proof that I'd pushed myself past a reasonable point. Jacob didn't say anything, but I know he noticed. I admitted that I'd pushed too hard and apologized to him for not calling a stop sooner as I worked on his shoulder. I also thanked him for the workout. Even with things flowing back in I'm feeling clearer than I was and I know that once I finish this and stretch out I am going to be dead to the world. Once we were both patched up and sipping some water, I asked him how he was doing, more to remind him that I was available than to try to get him to open up any. I believe he will talk to me at some point, but not until he's ready. And I like to think it helps for him to know I'm willing when that time comes.

                                            But tonight was not the night. He said he was fine and I moved to thanking him again for watching things while I was busy seeing to Becky last night. Then we slipped into a comfortable silence and cleaned up before biding each other goodnight.

                                            Tomorrow is going to be a long night. I have more irons in the fire than I'd like but so far everything looks like it might just go off without a hitch. But if I hope to keep it that way I am going to have to bring my S+ game. And that means I have to stop putting off crawling into this empty bed alone.

                                            Tonight was odd all around. Lorelei seemed a bit down before she went on stage, and then proceeded to sing an utterly depressing song. “Tears of Heaven” I think it was called. Not a dry eye in the house after that number. Great for us Winter courtiers, but not so much for the others. I will admit that I did take advantage of all the sorrow in the room and re-centered myself. I felt a bit more invigorated after that song. The customers not so much. Though it did good things for the drink sales.

Apparently she was not unaffected by the song or the little bit of power that Lori put into it. We talked for a bit, and Gawain came over to join us. After the song was over, Lori walked off stage and headed straight upstairs. It’s not like her to just blow off the rest of her numbers, so we figured that something was wrong. As if the downer of a song wasn’t hint enough. So, I offered to keep an eye on the crowd while Calamity and Gawain checked in on our resident songstress.

I know that De la Rocha has had these issues since she first started dating Lorelei. I thought that she had made peace with herself about the whole same sex lover thing already, but apparently not. I understand that she needs to figure things out, but she probably could have done it in a better way. Lori looked like a wreck when she was going upstairs. Calamity and Gawain said she wasn’t looking too good either. Apparently her plans for after the show included chocolate ice cream and bourbon. I decided then that I would do my part to help Lori feel better, even if she wouldn’t thank me in the morning.

I convinced her that I had a better option for her, so I went about getting the ingredients for a frozen mudslide. Not strictly by the book, but I think it tastes better if you make it with chocolate ice cream. I frosted the glass first then went about making the drink. I think Lori liked it. At least the way she was licking her lips after she drank it said as much. I tried to keep her interested in mixed drinks so I could control how much alcohol she drank, but she went back to the bottle of bourbon. She didn’t even get a glass. I knew that wasn’t going to end well, but there wasn’t much I could do to stop her unless I took the bottle from her. I think she took the rest of the ice cream with her too.

Calamity called me tonight, before Lori absconded with the liquor, to say that she needed to drop Chalcedony off at the dream house so she could take care of Becky. Long story short, Julia approached Calamity to let her know that Chalcedony had been acting odd. Calamity went to Chalcedony’s house to make sure that everything was okay. Turns out that it wasn’t. Chalcedony told Calamity that she had received a strange phone call. No voice, just strange sounds/tones. Then after that she said that her head felt fuzzy and it was really hard to concentrate. Kind of sounded like what happened to Desiree, but didn’t work entirely.

She apparently got an earful of Becky ranting about the Princess and Desiree, and was really upset and feeling marginalized. Calamity then had two young ladies who both needed attention. So, she called me and said that she was going to bring Chalcedony by the Dream House and asked if I could keep an eye on her over night to make sure that she didn’t try to do anything strange, or that nothing happened to her. I said that was fine, and that she should see to her friend.

She said that she was just tired and wanted to go to sleep. That was fine. I showed her to the basement guestroom. I figured that if she tried to leave, or if she sleep walked or anything she’d have to go past me to do it. So, after Lori finally went upstairs to pass out, I stayed downstairs and read or flipped through TV channels.

                                            I will admit that I felt bad for both Desiree and Chalcedony. Something is definitely going on with them and it’s taking away their self-control. That is kind of scary in and of itself, but being made to do stuff that you don’t remember later. That’s not good. Whatever is going on likely has something to do with their Keeper, but I don’t know if the Keeper is the one messing with them now. You’d think that such a canny guy would cover his tracks better. It’s obvious that something is going on with those girls. If I had been the one to make them do stuff for me you better believe I’d be a lot more subtle about it. At least the Lady never messed with my head in that way. Not to say that she didn’t mess with my head. She did, but it was more psychological. She used fear, pain, hunger, all that stuff to get the desired result out of me. It wasn’t exactly subtle. I don’t know if the Lady “does” subtle. It was effective though. Can’t say that it wasn’t.

                                            I don’t know what it is that changed in me when I met Delight Diane and others in her freehold, but something did. I guess the best way to describe it, is that something in me thawed. I felt things again, well, at least stronger than I had in a very long time. Sometimes I miss that numbness that surrounded me when I was in the Lady’s service. I didn’t really think about the consequences of my actions, I just knew that I was doing right and making Her happy. Now….now I can’t stop seeing the faces of those that I wronged or killed over the years. I see them when I close my eyes. I see them in the faces of people in the crowd at the club, or in strangers walking down the street. I feel a pain deep inside me that won’t go away. Sometimes it lessens a bit. Sometimes I can put it out of my mind if I’m focusing on something else, but it never really goes away. Sometimes it is so tempting to go back to her realm, and beg forgiveness so I can get that blessed numbness back. Sometimes.

I had left water and aspirin on her nightstand last night (or early this morning) figuring that she’d need it. I think she did drink it, but brought the aspirin downstairs with her. We got her some juice and she put the pills in it. Then Gawain said something about salt being good for hangovers so she added that too. Not the most appetizing thing, but she drank it down. Hopefully it helped her in some way. I myself have found that equilibrium that lets me get mostly drunk, at least pleasantly buzzed, and not get a hangover in the morning. It works most of the time. Though, I do admit that sometimes I say ‘fuck the equilibrium’ and get trashed out of my head. Sometimes I need it I think. Not the healthiest way to deal with my issues, but it’s working for now.

                                            Gawain guessed that I’d been up all night since it was obvious from the coffee grounds in the trash that the morning pot was not the first one I’d made today. It’s those early morning, getting close to dawn hours that are the hardest for me. Maybe it just makes me realize just how long I’ve been up and my body reminds me that it’s tired. So, coffee was my friend. I admitted that I’d been up all night and explained why. I may not have been the clearest in my explanation of things. It certainly felt complete at the time I was saying it, but looking back I can see the gaps. When she got back, I think Calamity filled in her beau about the details of what happened a little later.

I was tempted to volunteer for security detail, but decided not to at the last minute. Not sure why now that I think about it. Dances can be fun and all that, but I don’t do a whole lot of dancing. I do like people watching though. It’s interesting to see interactions with folks and how it differs from person to person and group to group. I suppose if I’m not busy paying attention to safety issues then I’ll have more time to just watch people. I do want to dance a little bit. Maybe Insincere Marguerite would like to dance?

                                            Early this afternoon I took the opportunity to get a bit of sleep. I figured if I was going to be able to stay awake for the show tonight and mix drinks without dropping bottles, I would need some shut eye. When I got up I did something that has been on my mind for a while now, but I haven’t had the guts to do. I looked for any trace of Brandon Mills. I looked online and in the phone book. He actually has a listing in the book. I found it in L.A. Not somewhere that I can ride my bike to unfortunately. I’ll need to get a car for that trip. I had thought about asking Gawain to borrow his van, but I didn’t really want to take him with me. I need to get my license and my own car. Then I can make the trip myself and not have to explain things to the others. I’ve been saving up some money from my side job, and hopefully will have enough to buy a used car out of the paper. Maybe a jeep. I’ve always wanted a jeep.

                                            I don’t honestly know what I’m going to do when/if I find Brandon. As long has he hasn’t strengthened his mask I should be able to tell by looking if he is a Changeling or the fetch. Of course that is not substantial evidence. If I find a fetch, then I know one of two things, that Brandon is still in Arcadia, or that he’s out and has taken a new name. Still leaves me not knowing where he is. I’m not sure that I could just go around asking local Changeling’s if they’ve seen anyone that sort of looks like Brandon used to, but fuzzier. The last time I saw Mills he looked to be on his way to becoming an ogre or beast. Big, hairy and strong. He could still be in the area, or he could have moved far away. If he’s nearby though, and I start asking after him it might draw a bit more attention my way than I’d like. If someone tells him that a skinny guy with white hair and frost on his face was looking for him, I’m not sure what he’d do. He was a big kind of guy when I met him the first time. It was one of the reasons I chose him. Skinny little me tricking the big, strong jock into the Lady’s realm where the tides could turn. He’d be the weak one and I’d be strong. Yeah, kind of messed up there.

Tonight at the show everything went pretty much like normal, except one number where Lorelei walked out wearing a floppy hat and a trenchcoat. The look was not the most attractive on her and I wondered what she was up to. Then she threw off the outer wear and was wearing a spangly low cut dress underneath I thought it was a nice change up from what she normally did on stage and the audience appreciated the reveal. Yeah, they definitely appreciated the reveal. The thing that kind of tied the whole evening together in a funny kind of way was when Captain Jack showed up at the club wearing a disguise of a floppy hat and a trenchcoat. He was actually here to talk to Gawain about their plans for the ball, but it turned into more than that. Both he and Lori got on stage and did a number together, and to pull it together they both wore their long trenchcoats and floppy hats. For their number they decided to do “Stand by Your Man.” It was good. They make a good duet, but at the same time it was funny. I couldn’t help it. I laughed a bit at the bar while watching them on stage.

Unfortunately Gawain wasn’t going to be able help with this. Calamity needed to be able to enact physical violence on something or someone. I decided to be that someone. I know, I’m such a good friend to let her beat the snot out of me. Well, to try anyway. Each of us gives as good as we get.
