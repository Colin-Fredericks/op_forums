    2 Changeling Conditions

    05-20-2014, 03:00 PM
    I decided to convert some old Changeling ideas into Conditions, just to see if I could strike a good balance. Constructive feedback would be appreciated. =)

    Court Enmity

    Perhaps you switched Courts, failed to fulfill your Court duties, or abandoned the Court system entirely - or maybe you didn't do anything to deserve censure at all. Whatever the case, you now suffer -2 on any Social rolls with members of a specific Court. If using Social maneuvers against members of your enemy Court, you must open an extra Door. Additionally, you cannot access the social benefits of any Mantle or Court Goodwill rating you may have in that Court until the Condition is resolved. Court Enmity can occur with multiple Courts at once.

    Example Skills: Subterfuge, Socialize
    Resolution: The falsehood is openly debunked or your character's name is cleared (often through a challenge such as a major duel, fulfilling a pledgebound quest, or offering an important item).
    Beat: n/a


    Forgotten Promise

    You have entered into a pledge but do not recall doing so, which leaves you unaware of the parties, restrictions, and boons involved (at least temporarily). The Wyrd will eventually reveal the terms of the deal to you or you can seek them out on your own, but you will have to live up to your end of the bargain or face the consequences. Merits like Common Sense or supernatural powers with similar functions can help you sense when you are about to violate a restriction of a forgotten promise.

    This Condition is usually only available at character creation, though it can stem from a memory-related Condition gained during play, like Amnesia. The player should give input into the pledge's terms, at least about its overall severity and the boon(s) their character was seeking. Storytellers should remain open to amending the terms, should the player have a very negative response.

    Resolution: Your character discovers all of the terms of the pledge.
    Beat: A complication arises due to your character's active pursuit of the details of the pledge.

      First, awesome to see someone working on GMC content for Changeling! It's one of my favorite games and I'm happy to see any part of it get a rules update.

      I think Court Enmity could be a Persistent Condition. While Notoriety isn't (which it looks like you modeled this Condition on), I think it'd be neat for players to pick up during character creation. That way, their changelings could have a more significant relationship with Courts they aren't members of/have the Goodwill Merit towards. The Beat could be the usual "whenever it makes you fail a roll."

      Forgotten Promise is a cool idea. Lots of things Storytellers could potentially do with it, and solving mysteries about their characters is just plain interesting for players. Formatting-wise, it should have (Persistent) after its name, since you're okay with picking it up during characer creation.

      Otherwise, would love to see any further Conditions. Blood and Smoke had a million vampire-related ones, and I'm sure just as many could be done up for changelings.

                          I don't think there's a Condition for insomnia anywhere yet, but Asylum had it as a derangement. Converting it to a GMC game should be pretty easy; heck, you don't need to change any mechanics, just the formatting (since it already explains how to recover).

                          Insomnia (mild): Your character has trouble getting the required amount of sleep, and not just occasionally. It happens more often than not, and leaves her feeling tired, irritable and unable to concentrate. Any time the character is engaged in a stressful situation (Storyteller discretion), roll Resolve + Composure. Failure means that your character is unable to sleep properly and suffers a –2 penalty on all rolls the following day. Each day thereafter is considered “stressful” and requires a similar roll until the character succeeds and gets a full night’s or day’s rest.
                          And totally agreed on insomnia coming up in Changeling games. Getting your dreams meddled with by the fae (in all the many ways they can) can't be good for someone's sleep.

                                  Very neat ideas, all! The dreaming Condition in particular seems like it could come up in a lot of games (because really, the dream-shaping aspect of Changeling is just so cool). Here's some stabs at a couple of 'em.


                                  Oathbreaker (Persistent)

                                  Your character made a pledge and broke it. In addition to whatever punishment the oath's sanction carries, your character is shunned by her freehold and suffers a -5 penalty on any Social rolls against changelings who know of her treachery. She will not be granted aid or succor against the True Fae and their servants.

                                  Resolution The oath's sanction expires, and your character performs a great service to the local freehold that clears her name.
                                  Beat Your character fails a roll because of the Condition, or other changelings refuse to help her when she's threatened by the True Fae.


                                  Solipsistic (Persistent)

                                  Your character believes nothing outside her imagination is real. The world is merely a construct for her amusement. She ignores or reacts bizarrely to situations that don't further her current whims, and suffers a -2 penalty to all Perception, Empathy, and Investigation rolls.

                                  Resolution Regain a dot of Clarity, lose another dot of Clarity, or achieve an exceptional success on a breaking point.
                                  Beat Your character does something that causes problems for herself (she plucks daisies in the middle of a gunfight, ignores a police officer asking her to pull over, abandons a friend in need, etc.).

                                  (Somewhat broadened from your original concept of seeing the world as a dream. I can picture it coming up under other circumstances, like prolonged or intimate contact with the True Fae.)


                                  Glamour addiction is already nicely modeled by the Addicted and Deprived Conditions. It's handy how broad a lot of them are.

                                  A few further states of being from the Changeling corebook that seem like potential Conditions me; persistent nightmares, ensorcelled, oathbound, unmasked, the wanderlust and hedge-calling derangements.
