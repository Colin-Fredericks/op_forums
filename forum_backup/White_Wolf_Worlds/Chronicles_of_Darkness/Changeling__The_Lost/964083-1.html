                Anyways, about the actual meat of the freehold. I do love the Moon Princess legend, though I am a bit sad we don't have a Jeweled Branch Court. I do find it a bit odd at the red tape involved - how many changelings must there be to support such a massive beaurocracy? Hundreds? Thousands? A million? More than anything, I feel like I can't quite grasp the scope of this freehold, and that's throwing me off. What made you decide to do Honshu as a whole instead of a single city?

                The Courts... honestly, I'm less than impressed with the Courts. I feel like they're rather shallow - an umbrella group of rebels and malcontents, traditional conservatives, and buddhist ascetics, as if you can paint those with a single brush each. I'm not feeling anything approaching depth to them. They're just kind of... there. They have no relationship to the larger freehold or its three factions, no roles to play, I'm not getting a sense of organization, I don't see how this relates to finding a new home, and I just don't get a feeling of any kind of personality. I get the feeling that they're unnecessary.

                I like the Leadership Council and Envoys, both of which are fun. I find that the best part of this writeup is the descriptions of the Envoys, which are beautifully done.
