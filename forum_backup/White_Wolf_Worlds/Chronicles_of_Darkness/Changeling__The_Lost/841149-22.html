        Stoutfellow


        "Big things come in small packages"


        In Arcadia nothing is ever quite as it seems, as the True Fae have a love for creating mind bending illusions and distorting reality. Changelings who became Stoutfellow were compressed into a fraction of their former size, yet retained all of their physical ability. What resulted was a being who could compete with those several times their size. The Gentry were said to find great amusement in seeing their performance, until even they made the mistake of underestimating their creation.


        Beast: An armored carapace and twitching antennae reveal this Changeling's previous life as an insect.

        Ogre: Nearly as broad as he is tall and with a beard hanging down to his stomach, this man resembles the dwarf of Norse mythology.

        Darkling: Scrawny and timid from years of public humiliation, they found a strength within themselves they never knew they had.


        Blessing: When it comes to the Stoutfellow, size isn't everything. They automatically gain the Small-Framed merit at character creation without suffering the reduced health box and may purchase physical attributes at a cost of x2 instead of x4.


        ----------------------------------

        NOTE: I can't think of a Glamour spending part to their blessing.

        I think that Master Aquatosic's idea of replacing the Experience bonus with the ability to enhance your Strength with Glamour would work well there. That said, this is a pretty interesting kith.

            Wavemaster
            "Do what you want with them. Drown them if you like. I certainly would".

            Anyone who survived a flood knows very well how destructive water can be. Changelings who were remade into Wavemasters spent their durance commanding raging rivers and turbulent seas. More than once, they have used this power to drown any who dared intrude on their Keepers' realms. And they have just as often slain fellow captives who tried to swim for freedom. Those Wavemasters who do escape did so after willing the waters of their prison to hold the Gentry at bay.

            • Beast: An anthropomorphic shark with sharp teeth and webbed hands and feet.
            • Elemental: A being sculpted wholly from living coral.
            • Fairest: A sea witch with blue skin and silvery hair, with a pair of gills at the sides of her neck.

            Blessing: A Wavemaster can breathe underwater, and adds her Wyrd to her Initiative so long as more than half her body is submerged. If she spends Glamour or Willpower, she may use the three-dot version of the Psychokinesis Merit to affect water. All rolls to use the Merit gain the 8-again quality if performed at night.

                Here is another one. I can't remember whether anyone has tried a 'holy person' kith yet, but here is my take on it.

                Bishop

                My friends, we cannot simply hide here and wait for mad gods to snatch us away again! Take up your arms, and let us drive this Huntsman from our door!

                The Others are not often religious beings, but on occasion, it suits their purposes to play at spirituality to instill such feelings in others. The bishops were often extremely religious before they were taken to act as the Gentry's holy men and women. A bishop may have acted as a spiritual adviser to ensure her Keeper's other servants stayed in line, or she may have been sent out into the world to form bizarre cults in her Keeper's honor. After escaping, most struggle to reconcile their experiences in Arcadia with the faith that they had held before.

                Beast: The horned shaman smells of smoke and speaks with a gravelly voice, and there is a vicious scar upon his throat.
                Elemental: A fire and brimstone preacher composed of actual fire and brimstone.
                Grimm: A handsome man clad in ritual robes sown from the pages of assorted holy texts from around the world.
                Ogre: A savage one-eyed monk with blue skin and a charming voice.
                Wizened: A smiling, squat little man with countless holy relics hung from straps of leather that cross and penetrate his skin.

                Blessing: Bishops were intended to move large groups of people to act in the favor of their Keepers. Each bishop gains the Inspiring Merit free of cost, even if she lacks the prerequisite. When using this Merit, instead of the usual penalties, she suffers a -3 penalty to the roll for a single listener, a -2 penalty for a small clique, a -1 penalty for a small crowd, and no penalty for a large crowd. Additionally, by spending a point of Glamour, any listeners who have gained the Inspired Condition from the bishop can temporarily gain either the Virtuous or Vice-Ridden Merit with a Virtue or Vice of the bishop's choice. The temporary Merit persists until it is used to regain Willpower or for a number of days equal to the bishop's Wyrd, whichever comes first. All affected listeners receive the same Virtue or Vice.

                                                            Iceborn
                                                            "Don't worry. A little cold isn't going to stop me"

                                                            Taken by Keepers who made their homes in realms of endless winter, Iceborn were kept as sentries who roamed under the cover of blizzards. Their touch is imbued with biting cold, and many intruders who crossed their paths more often than not ended up as stiff corpses. Yet while most were numbed by the cold they now embodied, some manage to flee when memories of a warmer time thawed their hearts and urged them to flee.

                                                            • Beast: A white wolf whose howl echoes with those of the winter winds.
                                                            • Elemental: A living snowman with glistening patterns of ice instead of veins.
                                                            • Fairest: A platinum-haired woman garbed in a translucent blue gown.
                                                            • Grimm: Snowflakes decorate his flesh, their kaleidoscopic forms shaping lines of text which speak of cold, lonely tales.

                                                            Blessing: Iceborn are immune to any penalties from cold weather, and can walk on ice without difficulty. If they spend a point of Glamour in a scene, they may cause anyone they touch to suffer the Extreme Cold Tilt at a starting penalty equal to their Wyrd. This Tilt lasts for a number of hours equal to successes on a Resolve + Occult roll. And if the victim is already suffering the Tilt, he adds to its existing penalty, and takes any dots in excess of 5 as lethal damage.
