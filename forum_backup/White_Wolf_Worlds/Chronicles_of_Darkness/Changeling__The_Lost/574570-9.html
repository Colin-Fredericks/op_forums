    One thing that seems to be getting forgotten is that Kith is determined by what a character did while in Arcadia, while Seeming is determined by how the character got out of Arcadia. For example: a college girl gets abducted, then turned in a crime-fighting robot, and finally escapes by grossly breaking department regs and being fired. Manikin Kith, Beast Seeming.

                A Wizened Draconic eh?


                Of all the people in the world. Why did it pick an accountant to make a dragon? That's the one question I've never answered.

                Being a dragon isn't as fun as you might expect. You're obsessed with gold, but having it doesn't make you happy. You don't feel powerful when every knight slays you without trouble. I'm a smart man, I thought I'd stay aloft and out of reach, give them the ol firebreath. I'm still not sure how they killed me with a sword at fifty foot up, but they did. Every god damn time.

                The first time I died I thought enough was enough, I could fly so I picked a direction and set off. A knight got me. I tried a few more times but no matter what I did they knew if I was trying to escape. For a while I just gave up, I even started forgetting I used to be human, how to think like a human.

                In the end it was the princess that saved me. Not the first one I'd forced a king to sacrifice, dumb as bricks all of them. Never anything but weeping and swooning over the knight who was sure to rescue them. Well by this point I was so desperate for human conversation that I didn't care, and the knights always took a few days after a kidnapping, I was the end of the quest after all. So after a day I started getting through to her. It turns out that They made me into a dragon and her into an airhead. Once she broke through that she was a normal person again, and together we came up with a plan.

                Ok, she came up with the plan. She's the one who read fantasy, I read accounting, that's why I did most of the work. So this place, I suppose I should count my blessings, the peasents lived knee deep in dung and plague most of the time. Most of the kingdoms' treasuries were in my cave after all. But this time when the knight killed me he rescued a princess with a crash coarse in financial investment. It took a few kidnappings, but before long every knightly order had enough of my horde to run a few Charitable organisations. Note the capital C, it's important.

                Once the knights had gotten their head around the idea that material possessions can help people, she started getting them to think about how they could acquire more gold to help more people. That was the first crack in their oh-so-holy attitude. The princess gave the signal for me to abduct her again, and we waited. When the knight came she walked out stark naked, and our plan worked. He reacted like a human being, not a walking cliche, and a knight without his virtue can't hope to fight of a 60 foot fire breathing armoured lizard with a piece of sharp metal. After he was dead, I hope it wasn't permanent, it never was for me, I took the princess and we flew off. Not one fucking knight to be seen.

                We make an odd pair. A princess who looks like a wounded lion and a dragon who looks like an accountant; but everyone's odd around here.


                BTW, I definitely imagine him looking like a cross between a dragon and an old wise sage type. Something like this. Accountants don't really modify their body in the first place, and even if they could it doesn't fit the story. There are ways to make it fit, maybe his body is covered with accounting ledgers that he scratched on as self therapy to distract himself from the next knight (or that the Wyrd retroactively said he used as therapy even though he didn't). But given his story; the image is obviously hermit-sage with body modification a secondary feature at best.

                The Damsel in Distress kith ended up a lioness because like many Damsels she was royalty and lions are kings of beasts. Wounded animals show up a few times in faerie tales so it seemed like the perfect centre of the royalty, damsel, beast, ven diagram.
