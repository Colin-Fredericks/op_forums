        Shadowcaster
        "What's the matter? Afraid of the dark?"

        The darkness is a common fear, representing the menacing unknown to the mortal world. More specific is mankind's fear of having the light stolen from them, the sudden rush of terror a dropped flashlight or power outage can cause. Shadowcasters are often those who found this fear realized, forced to run through nightmares where the lights always went out, and when they did, terrible things would happen. Others were those who used the darkness to protect themselves, shattering lights or otherwise engendering an absence of illumination.

        Blessing: The Shadowcaster gains three dots in the Psychokinesis Merit, and can spend Glamour or Willpower to manipulate shadows. If he spends a point of Glamour, he may drain all light within a radius of ten yards per dot of Wyrd, inflicting penalties on all visual rolls as if they were made in complete darkness, except those made by the changeling himself.

            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
            This is an interesting concept for a kith, and one not explored by any in the docs at that. The write-up could use some revisions, and I'm not sure if using character Size is a good idea (alternatively, I could make it 5 per dot of Wyrd). But other than that, it looks good.
            It's important to remember a Kith is only a fraction of what a Changeling was able to do in Arcadia, where they were empowered by the Gentry's whims and the raw Wyrd beyond the Hedge. There should be a limit of some kind and I considered Size to be the best qualifier as it's universally used for people and objects. Instead of volumetric math you compare the total Size of a collection to the Changeling's maximum. Their own Size (because the objects are housed within) and Wyrd are a suitable maximum, and Size 0 objects can be stored limitlessly if you wanted to spend the Glamour. It's true that while in their Keeper's realms the Hollowmade could be a tower or a palace, back on earth you need to pick up Potent Kith if you want to be the walking garage with a fleet of vehicles and tools.

            The most I would consider expanding the limit is twice the character Size + Wyrd. Anything greater and it would just be silly as a Kith, chucking cars and dumpsters out of your body, although that would be an entertaining high rating Contract.

            This Kith never had a previous writeup as it was only a musing. My interest in developing the Kith has spiked since the revised Dual Kith reduces your effective Wyrd for multiple Kith benefits. This custom Kith emulates other abilities for a different cost

            Assemblyman
            (from AgeOfRavens blog)

            What if you were never abducted from Earth to begin with? It's true that every Changeling is not the same when they escape Arcadia, with a Fetch in the place of the person that was taken. Although you were never a single person to begin with. Especially bizarre and morbid Keepers that has fashioned themselves as sewers of flesh, cobblers of discorporate parts or swapping minds and bodies of their captives indiscriminately often 'unintentionally' create this Kith pulled together from scraps and failed projects. What emerges from Arcadia as living hodgepodges find themselves a mess of memories, bodies and durances from beings that no longer exist. Or perhaps the Assemblyman is the result of a fierce sense of desperation pulled together from the bodies of several fallen escapees.
            • Fairest: a horrid yet pleasing appearance masking distinctive and contrasting attitudes
            • Grimm: like a bad fan fiction, several characters compiled together
            • Darkling: each of their personalities has retained a frighting feature
            Blessing: The Assemblyman selects 4 Skills to gain the benefits of this Kith. Each day they have a pool of dice that can be spent on any of these skills, although no more than half their Wyrd for a single roll. Additionally the Assemblyman selects three other Kiths that they can emulate, using the lowest of their Wyrd or their lowest Resistance Attribute in place of Wyrd for these Kiths. Unlocking the benefits of these other Kiths requires taking a level of damage resistant to supernatural healing that cannot be mitigated in any way. It costs 1 Bashing for the first Kith, 1 Lethal for the second Kith, and 1 Aggravated damage for the third Kith in the same scene.

                    Here's one I've been working on for a bit. Not sure if this sort of idea has been covered or not.

                    Hearth Heart
                    I know I have some with me. Hold on, let me check my pockets...my sleeves...my shadow maybe...ah, there it is! Weird at how hard it is to keep track of loose change. Worse than socks in that regard.

                    Even with the vast space their realms can grant, even the Gentry need something, or someone, to keep track of things. Whether it's a treasured token, bric a brac or even just themselves, sometimes there's nothing safer than hiding something inside of someone. Hearth Hearts are those who were hollowed out in some way, making their bodies, minds and even their shadows storage. Some Keepers even make their Changelings into fashion statements, transformed into a coat that bares the winter instead of the wearer.

                    Darkling: Her shadows teem with hands that grasp and pull at anything close, and her mouth unzips to reveal a massive space.
                    Elemental: Once an armoire, his front has a split down the middle, and hinges sit at his sides, ready to open at a thought.
                    Fairest: Her sleeves store anything and everything, and her eyes have an impossible sense of depth to them.

                    Blessing: The Changeling always has a space with them they may store items in, invisible and inaccessible to anyone without permission. This space is capable of holding items with a size up and equal to their Wyrd. Time is arrested in this space - anything within will not degrade or age, but anything living will be conscious and able to make a Perception roll (penalized by your character's Wyrd) to listen outside. The Changeling feels the weight of anything within the space, but everything within them is considered half as heavy as it normally is. By expending a Glamour, this space may be tripled for the remainder of the scene at the risk of the Changeling over encumbering themselves.

                                        So that was yours? That link ended up being one of the larger list of Kith concepts I had come across. Lots of fund ideas there.

                                        -----------------------------------------

                                        As mentioned above, Hollowmade was a revision of a previously written custom Kith. This was the blessing of the 1E version to be updated for 2E:

                                        The Hollowmade are Empty Inside; their bodies invariably contain an entrance of some sort that can expand to create a special Hollow usable only by the Hollowmade, into which various objects can be placed for storage at the cost of Glamour. This Hollow is about the size of a large closet (5' by 5' by 10'), and cannot store living beings (or undead). Inserting or removing an object is an instant action; the changeling never has to search for an item, it always comes to hand immediately. When placing an item inside, the Hollowmade's true nature is fully visible for a turn. Note that for every day this Blessing in use (that is, the changeling contains one or more objects), the changeling must spend a point of Glamour to avoid taking a level of aggravated damage. In addition, Hollowmade add a dot to any Hollow which they have already purchased at least a dot of; this dot can be in any category.
                                        Much like your Hearth Heart, Taidragon, there should be some kind of consistent mechanic and limitation to what the Changeling can store. There's also a slight difference of theme, as your kith outlines Changelings used as storage like a chest or a pocket vest and Hollowmade is indicative of structures, buildings, or Hedge border growths. A lot of the crunch in the first half of both Kiths cover similar strata.

                                        Towards the end, I still wanted to retain the Hollowmade notion that they are 'living dwellings' and how that influences their connection with a Hollow. Also, this will very greatly on how the Hollow merit is done in 2E. From the early looks, I was operating under the assumption that the Hollow merit is one that unlocks additional features depending on how many dots you have. Those 2 or 3 dot Escape Routes and Long Roads and such.

                                        Keeping the first half of the Kith writeup the same, here's a new draft for the ancillary blessing:

                                        For the duration of their visit the Hollowmade can enhance a Hollow they own and upkeep, receiving 2 free dots of features. If they are given permission from the owner of another Hollow they visit, they may grant that Hollow an additional dot of features for the duration of their stay. Different features can be selected each visit.
