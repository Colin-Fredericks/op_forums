                                    I've only skimmed here and there but overall I like what I'm seeing. While I liked the concept of your Seeming being how you escaped rather than what your Keeper did to you, I have to admit the new write ups feel more flexible. The previous set felt like a specific charachter rather than a foundation. I think the physical decscriptions of Wizened fit much better than the old cyborg ones. I'm still hopeful that one day there will be a damsel in distress kith who's blessing helps convince others that they are innocnent, harmless, and should be rescued (truthfully or to trick them). The Notary Kith was a pretty awesome addition, I'm hoping at some point there'll be some mechanics for a Notary using the specific pledge they represent against their keeper.

                                    The give and take for the Courts were also a step up from the old Approaches. Approaches gave points to Huntsmen, but nothing stopped a Huntsman taking it's full starting pool and going straight for the capture. Autumn and Winter were particularly good, since they forced the Gentry to play to the Changling Court's strength. Autumn gets plenty of time to hit the books, and since Winter inflicts a horrible disadvantage in open combat the Gentry are all but forced to try and catch changelings alone in dark alleyways - and Winter plays that game well. Spring and Summer didn't capture me the same way. Spring controls when the Gentry/Huntsmen/Loyalists are aloud to attack, but doesn't change the methods they can use after they make their move. Summer feels like it would discourage open conflicts in favour of striking isolated weak targets, but on the other hand we haven't seen the mechanics for Gentry and Huntsman yet. If there's plenty of ways for them to escape then this becomes a useful bonus, even if not specifically a bonus about not being abducted again.

                                    The Hedge seemed solid, but I'd like to see mechanics on that.
