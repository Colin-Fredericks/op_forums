                    I'm not disagreeing with you, but I'm not agreeing with you either. I can see both arguments. So, that said, let's go through this.

                    -1) Feral doesn't fit with the aesthetic of Changeling's wordplay in this particular instance.

                    0) I don't make these calls. Just making that clear. I am responsible for one thing, and one thing only in this book, and that's a city.

                    1) On the one hand, you're not wrong, it is confusing. I created a context for which Goblin is used, and Goblin Vow doesn't quite fit that aspect, though it does fit the idea of 'goblin' being a term for factors that skew "Going Home" instead of "Leaving Arcadia". Still, it doesn't quite fit into the norms.

                    2) On the other hand, there is still that connotation in the fact that you are partaking in an act of psycho-spiritual balance with the freaking world, with finding the humanity in the wild and bringing it to the uncanny. Goblin, as it's culturally used, applies within the context we're using, it fits neatly enough.

                    3) But on the first hand, the words Seeming and Kith are being looked at being changed two, two words that ostensibly have even deeper roots in the cultural consciousness of the game than Goblin Vow. And as a semi-related aside, if one of the two must change, it must be Goblin Vow, both for 'grandfather' reasons as well as the fact that the Contracts have greater claim than Vow by their nature(though point 2 still stands, it's not like it's completely free of it)

                    4) Back on the other hand, it's been called Goblin Vow since early 2008, and it's stuck in most people's head as Goblin Vow. To change the name without significantly changing the power (in a way I don't think it has) is offsetting, particularly since the name is a part of the culture, the mindset, the vein of myth we've established.

                    I could probably go on. I can see reasons why to keep it. I probably would keep it. But I can see why to change it as well, and I could definitely make it's case. I'm not completely moved not to, even though I'd probably keep it the same.

                    And in the end, not my yob man. Even if it were, I don't feel strongly enough either way to make the say.
