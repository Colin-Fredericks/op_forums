          Let's see.

          I said a bunch of times that It pretty much fits all the points of an Idigam, being a shapeshifting horror from the beginning of time that has very clear Ban and Banes. Not the only route, but by far the easiest one. I might actually stat It once I'm done reading the book again (it's been a while, I don't remember all the details about it)

          Randall Flag is difficult, but if we go by the Dark Tower, my personal answer would be, by the time the books happen, Archmaster who acts as agent of some seriously bad guy. Probably Abyssal, with the Crimson King and its brethen being described as abominations from coming from the space between the dimensions. Their thing resonates a bit with the whole good 'ol "tear the world apart and let the Annunaki in"

          Danny Torrance is a Sin-Eater. Has powers related to death (and premonition, I give you that), attracts the attention of ghosts and, in the sequel, helps dying people to pass forward peacefully. He also fight an old cabal of Abmortals that devour the souls of children. Alternatively, a Moros that stumbles upon some nasty Tremere.


          That's some examples for now. I might have more later, but I don't want to spoil the fun for others and write too much.

              RE: The Work of Stephen King

              Much of my approach to Horror as a genre has been informed by Stephen King, so its always a fun exercise to cast his creations through the CofD lense. That being said, I have always been more inclined to rebuild and interpret the idea. here are a few things that come to mind...
              The Weeds
              from Creepshow (Film;1982)- The Lonesome Death of The Lonesome Death of Jordy Verrill

              The lifeform that emerged from the shattered meteor that landed on the property of the unfortunate Mr.Verrill is a plant-like organism capable of growing on anything with even a trace of moisture. In the case of organic lifeforms, they are quickly overwhelmed as the organism grows upon their flesh (which causes a maddening itching,burning sensation). A character exposed to the organism must make a Resolve + Stamina roll with a -2 penalty. Failure inflicts the Weeds Condition.

              Weeds
              Your character has been infected with an aggressively fast-growing form of alien plant life. Drawn to moisture, it quickly covers the body in a bright green grass-like substance.Once per hour, the character must make a Resolve + Stamina roll with a -2 penalty. On a failure the character with this condition suffers a point of Lethal Damage. In addition, the character suffers a -1 penalty to all rolls from the burning itch caused by the growing infection. Once your character has taken 5 or more boxes of damage from this condition, their bodies are almost entirely covered, which may cause additional conditions or tilts.
              Possible Sources: Touching the meteor or any other infected person or thing.
              Resolution: Non known, buy avoiding moisture, to the point of severe dehydration, may be the only way to halt the infection. Defoliant chemicals and other, more exotic means might halt the Weeds.
              The Pet Semetary
              from Pet Semetary (Novel;1983)

              Rather than the innocuous graveyard for beloved pets, the true "Pet Semetary" was a burial ground located several miles away across harsh, rural terrain. Once used by pre-colonial natives, and could very well predate even those people. In the words of local sages, the place has a power... its own evil purpose. Those buried in this spoiled earth return to a semblance of life, but as shadows of themselves. Violent and atavistic, the revenant creatures visit pain and death upon those they once loved,until they are returned to the grave. This uses the Strange Places rules.
              Gauntlet: +2(Loci), Potency: 4, Willpower: 5
              Environmental Traits: Cemetary Gate, Remote,Tainted Area

              Cemetary Gate: Those buried in the rocky soil of the Pet Semetary return to life after a few hours. The returned individual are alive, but "wrong". All of the returned suffer the Soulless Condition, gain a dot in a Physical Attribute, use the Higher of their Dexterity or Wits for Determining Initiative. Those dead for more days than their integrity return confused and unbalanced, and lose a point of Intelligence or Resolve. Sometimes Dead is Better.

              Remote: The hike to the Pet Semetary is across several miles of rugged rocky terrain and skeletal forests.

              Tainted Area: There is a pervasive sense of unease in the Pet Semetary, and those go there know on a gut level that what the land is capable of doing is a sincere blasphemy.

              The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
              Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
              The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
              Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
              Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

                              I've been a fan of King since highschool. And I have been enjoying it's popularity return in media. It goes in cycles you see. It was big in the 80s-90s

                              A few off the top of my Head. Graveyard Shift. It's a story of a guy who runs into The Rat King. A simple rat spirit that had been feeding on the local death spirits.

                              Randall Flag Is a Demon. A Demon that doesn't bing Angels. It's the GM subconscious desire to self terminate.
                              Pulling apart the world Across multiple iterations of reality. He never truly dies, but can be banished. He's an agent of natural entropy that has become manifest and gone 'off the reservation.' Figuring out his ultimate plan is simple, the systematic destruction of humanity and reality. His schemes are the tricky part to puzzle out. Relaying on some bit of anonymity. Figuring out how to banish him is the mystery. He has been chased a few times, which assumes he can be defeated. He may not always be the source of disaster but he is certainly an agent of ruin.

                              The Dark Tower, is a place holder for reality and it's many folded nature of probability and possibility. A physical representation that can present itself during certain situations

                              The langoliers. These are abyssal entities that eat any foolish enough to try that time stop technique for too long. Getting stuck in the past as it were.

                              Salem's lot is the straight forward story of a vampire infestation gone out of control.

                              A better question is The Talisman and Black House. What is The reality flipping Jack Sawer? I almost want to say changeling, because of Arcadia beach, but that doesn't make sense. leaning towards wolf blooded. I miss Wolf. I remember in Blackhouse that world destroyer breaker. I believe that was a bit of GM infrastructure repurposed to take out connecting realities.

                              Blaine the Train: It had its origin as an AI. A self driving train. But when the world committed suicide, he was no longer driving people to towns, but to hollowed out disasters. It became self aware of this fact and took on a spiritual awakening. The Spirit started running the show. Becoming eternally bored and slowly insane. Intelligence spark by thousands of computer banks. Almost limitless processing power and nothing to use it on. -I have no mouth and I must scream- Riddles were the closest it could get to anything interesting. In a journey to prove it was superior to organic brains. Which it did very well, with endless data bases and references. Google on Crack. Until it through a hissy hit and finally crashed itself. Comedy folks it's a killing

                              The Waste Lands. A place where Space Arcana has broken. Roland the gunslinger's Original world. It's King's worst case scenario for man's cruelty to man. The bombs were dropped and the world is pealing itself back at the seems. Advanced tech but no one remembers why it's important. Almost feel like GM Infrastructure lying around with no one attending it.

                                          Counter

                                          IT is a in fact a Changeling that's going way over the edge into TrueFae. IT's creation, knowledge, use and manipulation of fear is unparalleled to the point that it's only weapon. His main target are children. IT has this weird 27 year hibernation cycle. Oddly timeless ageless and Temporal screwy. Everything about IT is an illusion and fake, but has immense consequences upon reality.

                                          It's actual form is insect in origin the clown is a puppet. It originally adopted the puppet because of a subtle clown phobia, access to targets, and the unanimity mundane form. The floating is fucking spider silk!!


                                          IT has turned Derry into a place of learned hopelessness, where people don't even fight back against the fear, they coward and hope the dark shadows passes over them Keeping them isolated in their fear and from the plight of others. Everyone just takes a blind eye to the mundane harassment. IT is only defeated when it's tricks no longer work. And that only happens when the protags stick together and fight for each other. Dispelling the effect of any one fearful illusion. United we're strong divided we fall. That's its entire game plan divide, hide. In fact the groups we do see forming in the narrative are those that spread fear. The bullies.
