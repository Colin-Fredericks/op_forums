                Originally posted by Errol216 View Post
                Does anyone have an ideas for a different Ogre Blessing/Curse that isn't so... combat-focused? Admittedly, combat isn't going to be much extended when the Ogre is walking around applying Beaten Down on everyone, but I'd kinda like an equivalent for Mental or Social situations.
                I'm leery of doing anything, since we're going to be likely seeing a lot of social contracts that do exactly that kind of thing, and Seeming stuff seems to be pretty weak / short lived. Darklings turn to shadow for 3 rounds. Beaten Down lasts three rounds (and even then, can keep fighting by spending willpower). Elementals can psychokinesis elements up to 3 yards. Fairest spend a willpower to boost others for +3 dice. Beasts get +3 speed/init. Notice a lot of threes.

                At best, we're looking at a +3 bonus when forcing doors, and, to be perfectly blunt, that's kind of weak for a changeling that can make a simple goblin vow and get a BETTER bonus without losing out on other things. And there's likely going to be Contracts for social stuff like this that would make having a bonus like this redundant. I'm not a fan of redundant things.

                That said, you can use Beaten Down to get social stuff, after all. Declare having them give you information, or do what you want, as the goal of "combat" and then just poke them. It counts as triggering Beaten Down for a few rounds. Resisting your social-fu requires willpower then, which is an advantage on its own.

                EDIT - doh! I completely overthought this. Why not just assign another appropriate Condition that lasts for three rounds? Scarred, Shaken, Spooked are my personal suggestions
