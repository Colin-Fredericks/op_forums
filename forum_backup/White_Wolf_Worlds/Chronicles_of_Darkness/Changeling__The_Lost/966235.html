    09-09-2016, 11:21 AM
    Hello everyone.

    I was wondering what makes 2.0 changelings stand out compared to other supernaturals in terms of game mechanics. What are their strengths, what are their weaknesses, and which parts of character creation deserve some extra attention when planning a new character concept?

      Well, the book isn't even out yet, just teasers. But their specialty really remains the same - being tricksters. Using the entironment to your advantage, illusions, avoidance, getting in and out of locked rooms. Everything ties back into dreams and fantasy and art. I feel the game also tend to support Finesse attributes over Power, so less raw strength and more coming from the sides.

      Now, compared to vampires, beasts and werewolves, changelings curious have no way of directly boosting their damage modifier. Sure, they can raise effective Strength dots, but everyone else can increase the auto-damage in some way. I'm not sure on changeling enemies, but that kind of ability makes me think that going against something with high defense or healing capabilities is a losing proposition without lots of preparation before hand. I'm highly leery of direct combat as a result. Maybe playing too much Vamp and Wolf, but that's my thoughts.

      When making a character, I tend to find a theme and stick with it when picking abilities. Seeming, kiths, merits, Contracts and Vows are all highly synergetic and work best together when focused on said theme. At least, that's my opinion.

          (Note that what we've seen so far was in a playtest state so some of this may be subject to change. In particular, we know that the dream mechanics have been changed quite a bit.)

          Changelings are slippery. Without cold iron restraints, you can pretty much never count on keeping one captive for long. If they promise some ongoing service to a human, they can blend in with humanity almost flawlessly as long as they hold up their end of the deal. You've got to watch what you say around them too - if something can be interpreted as a statement of intent, they can hold you to it, punishing you if you don't keep to your word.

          Changelings are perceptive. A Changeling that manages to keep their Clarity at a decent level gets some respectable bonuses to perception-related rolls, and they have access to a wide variety of powers related to perception and revelation.

          Changelings are masters of dreams. Where other creatures that can enter dreams generally do so in a special dream form that alters their stats and limits their capabilities, a Changeling can literally just walk bodily into a person's dreams like they're physical spaces. Moreover, once inside a dream they can interact with it in ways rivaled only by the Horrors of the Primordial Dream, sculpting it to their hearts' desires.

          Changelings are master emotional manipulators. They can play you like a fiddle, making you feel however they want you to feel, and then using that emotional energy to fuel their own powers. If they're feeling especially cruel, they can drain your emotions completely dry in an instant, leaving you feeling nothing while they replenish their Glamour pool completely.

          Changelings are downright terrifying when they need to be. A Changeling that throws caution to the wind, Dropping the Mask, Embracing the Wyrd, and Scouring people's emotions wantonly is a goddamn force of nature.

          Changelings are, however, quite subtle most of the time. And breaking that subtlety comes at a huge risk. A significant amount of their capabilities are defensive in nature, dedicated to keeping them hidden from the eyes of the Gentry and their Huntsmen, protecting themselves from their pursuers, and avoiding or escaping capture. Proactivity is not really their strong point, except when it comes to preparing their defenses. And, similarly to Demons, some of their most powerful abilities attract the attention of the enemies that they are so focused on trying to stay hidden and protected from.

              Several different things, but with Changeling they diverge alot depending on Kith, Contracts, Seeming, merits, and Court.

              But the universal one is becoming stronger in the face of things that should break them.

              Take a look at how Changelings deal with breaking points.

              Something causes a breaking point in a Changeling, so either the Changeling wins the roll and gains acuity (and one other benifical condition in the case of an exceptional sucess), which can easily be used to regain clarity or the Changeling fails that roll, and gains a condition, which can be used to gain a beat, which in turn can be traded in for an experience point.

              And aside from certain contracts and few other things, a Changeling's breaking points are based on personal trama and things that challenge their world view.

              The Changeling who seeks these things out makes him or herself more powerful, even if it means Clarity likely bounces all over the place.

              So clarity, including how one loses and gains it and the unique way it works makes Changelings very powerful in a very unique way that is universal to Changelings, it potentially means Changelings can grow in power very fast compared to some other templates, but at some risk and with some consquences.
