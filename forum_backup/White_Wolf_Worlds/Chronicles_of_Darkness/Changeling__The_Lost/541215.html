    2ED games taglines

    06-05-2015, 11:04 AM
    So we know that 2ED corebooks of Vampire, Werewolf and Mage have their own taglines, that were center to the setting changes brought with 2ED. The games have those sentances, that were also names for the books before they become official 2ED corebooks

    Vampire: The Requiem 2ED - "Blood and Smoke"
    Werewolf: The Forsaken 2ED - "The Wolf Must Hunt"
    Mage: The Awakening 2ED - "Addicted to Mysteries"

    Now, I make series of nWoD 2ED settings presentation for our RPG club, and run to this date with those taglines as titles for them. Only, as I know, when rest of annoucned corebooks come to development ( Promethean 2ED, Changeling 2ED ) I do not remember those phrases. Have they been putted to the fandom or public? Were they even made? Also, does other games with Storytelling System 2ED mechanics have them ( i.e. "new" games ) like Demon: The Descent or Beast: The Primordial?

    If the all answers to above question are "No", maybe fans have some cool taglines for them? I look especially now in to Beast one, as I want to make a presentation about it in the few weeks.
