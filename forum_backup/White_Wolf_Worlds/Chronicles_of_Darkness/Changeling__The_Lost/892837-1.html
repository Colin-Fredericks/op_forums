    Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
    So, let's chat.

    As it stands now, Changelings predominately enter dreams physically at all times. In their own sleep, in the mortal world at least, they have the choice of the Gate of Horn or the Gate of Ivory, which basically is the choice of slipping into the dream world and receiving the benefits of sleep, or staying in the mortal world but not regaining Willpower. In the Hedge, they form a Bastion when they sleep.
    That's one of the few things about 2E that I haven't liked so far. The inability to gain the benefits of sleep without disappearing seems like a pretty huge hole in their ability to remain hidden. In my games, I'm going to add a third option. The Gate of Horn shall still allow the full body lucid dreaming effect, while the Gate of Ivory will allow them to dream as a mortal does, but leaves them as vulnerable as anyone else to dream meddling. They will get the ability to turn off their dreams entirely by refusing to go through either gate.

                                                    It's funny, because a lot of my problems with the old dreaming system stemmed from the fact that they copy-pasted randomly from Mage (namely, how to get into other's dreams was a conflicted mess). I'd rather have an independent system that is internally consistent, rather than having some things pulled from other splats halfhazardly or have the devs spend countless more hours trying to make every subsystem play nice together.

                                                    Dream-stuff is hardly as ubiquitous as combat, and isn't something your baseline human can do, so I don't think that's a fair equivalency. Or heck, even like magic powers themselves; Gifts work differently from Embeds work differently from Contracts. They have different systems because the different systems work thematically. Changelings make agreements with scopes of reality, so they have discrete powers of different scale. Making them unscaling (like Demon) or fluid (like Mage) makes them feel different and not fit.

                                                    Same deal with dreams: Changelings are infused bodily with dreams, so they enter bodily. Mages think their way in in, Beasts have a soul there, but neither have a body, so they follow Dream Form rules. Changelings have a body (and can make things get physical), so they don't. I'd assume that anyone using a Dream Form is, in fact, dreaming (it's in the name), and thus a Changeling can go to where they are. Beyond that...IDK.
