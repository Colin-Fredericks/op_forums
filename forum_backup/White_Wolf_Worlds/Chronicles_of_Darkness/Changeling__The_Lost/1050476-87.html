                Originally posted by Wade L View Post
                Does the Mask covers things like Contracts and Kith benefits? Like if I swoop from the sky on someone using my Windwing Kith benefit, swinging a flaming sword conjured with Elemental Weapon, do normal people see a perfectly mundane human sailing through the sky with a flaming sword?                 What about physical changes summoned with magic but that aren't actually part of your Mien, like surrounding yourself with fire with Primal Glory or gaining claws with Wing and Talon?
                Unless otherwise specified (which some Kiths do), any activated powers are not protected by the Mask. It only covers your Mien.
                Actions are also fully visible; if you use Lethal Mien to eviscerate someone, anyone looking will see how your nails or teeth dig much deeper than what they would expect.
                The Mask is powerful in how it's always active, but unlike Quiescence and Lunacy, it does not affect anyone's mental state or cause people to rationalise away anything they see. (Many people still do, but that's the perfectly mundane psychological effect of the shock and the inability to make sense of what they experienced.)

                                                Fae, as in lowercase 'fae', usually mean any fae creature (Gentry, changelings, Hedge Ghosts, Fae-Touched, hobgoblins, et cetera). I would count defending members of the Freehold from harm as 'defending the Freehold', but to me acting as a protector, bodyguard, or champion is a formal role. This is just my take, and generally I assume every group finds their own definitions (these were pretty vaguely defined).

                                                        If you are not suffering some form of fear, you just get +3 Initiative and Speed and can deal lethal damage unarmed, period. Use lethal, use lethal, use lethal, use lethal, use lethal, no Glamour cost.

                                                        If you are afraid of something to the extent that it inflicts a Condition, you can spend a Glamour to get +3 Initiative and Speed and deal lethal damage unarmed for three turns. Use bashing, (glamour) use lethal, use lethal, use lethal, (glamour) use lethal, use lethal, use lethal.
