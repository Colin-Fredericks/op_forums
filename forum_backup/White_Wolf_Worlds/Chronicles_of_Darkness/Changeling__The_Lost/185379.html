    What Kiths are You?

    07-01-2014, 02:06 AM
    So here's how this works. For each of the Changeling Seemings, you choose a Kith from their list, you can even select from one of the many additions we have on the "What's YOUR Custom Kith" topic. These Kiths are ones that you feel you would most likely be if you were made into that seeming. Along with choosing a Kith, also provide a description of why you think you'd be suited to that Kith. If you wish, you can also list which Court you would belong to depending on the Kith.

    With 6 Seemings, that gives you 6 Kiths to fill out. Have fun!


    Ogre - Witchtooth: I generally enjoy mythology, fairy tales, and magic. If I had to be an Ogre, I think I'd be drawn to studying the dark powers of the Fae. Of course, I'd be one of the male Witchtooths. The fact that in many role playing games I prefer to play the mage instead of the warrior also makes me think that black magic would be my calling, as opposed to swinging a club around my head.

    Court - Autumn



    Elemental - Snowskin: While I'm not sure I'd be as particularly intimidating as the Snowskin are described, it does mention that they are skilled at hiding their true emotions and goals. My emotions and desires tend to flicker randomly to the point where even I have difficulty telling what my motives are, and thus I think the Snowskin would be an appropriate choice.

    Court - Change alliances depending on who's in power


    Fairest - Satyr: Taken from the custom Kith topic and one of my own creation. I don't consider myself especially beautiful or terrifying as with many other Fairest, but I do not deny that I'm addicted to pleasure and indulgence. If I had to be a Fairest, it would be the hedonistic Satyr kith, and my time in the Free Holds would likely focus on how to overcome my vices.

    Court - Spring


    Beast - Hunterheart: If I were turned into an animal, I'd like to say it would be a savage one like the Hunterhearts. After my escape, I'd probably be prone to Adamantium Rage moments and leaping upon the Gentry's servants to tear them apart with my bare (or is it bear?) hands. I've also been curious about what it would be like to live in a world without morality or civility, where you're free to give into your savagery.

    Court - Summer



    Darkling - Leechfinger or Mirror Skin: There's something alluring about the power to drain the life out of people by touching them. Whipping that power out in battle against the Gentry's servants and draining them into lifeless husks would be a favored battle strategy of mine. Alternatively, I think I'd fit as a Mirror Skin for a similar reason to the Snowskin. As I mentioned before, I sometimes have trouble determining my true desires and opinions, so having the ability to shift into different identities on a whim would be a perfect manifestation of that!

    Court - Autumn or Winter


    Wizened - Smith or Author: If I had to choose a career in Arcadia, it would probably involve forging weaponry, armor, and trinkets of power. In my opinion, guns removed honor and skill from battle, so I'd like the opportunity to craft blades for my fellow Lost instead of some high powered rifle. Alternatively, I could also be an Author, since I tend to write as a recreational hobby. Of course when it comes to durances, even a hobby can be a back breaking chore.

    Court - Summer or Autumn

          I think my Court allegiances would generally steer my towards Spring and Summer, with a heavier leaning towards Spring-I can't live in hate all the time. I have a soft spot for Autumn though, and would love to play ball with them.

          So, Kiths.

          Beasts: Coyote. I could make a case for a number of the other ones, but it really comes down to the fact that I'm neither predator nor prey, not wholly aggressive and yet not not wholly passive either. A scavenger fits me quite well for a number of reasons. Oh, and as an aside? Never let me talk when the chips are down-I might fight or fly like a demon, but let me talk and I'll spin you around until you don't know which way is up.

          Darkling: Moonborn. It's weird-I'm inherently a Darkling. Like, whether by affinity or by the common reason keeprs make Darlings, I am a Darkling. Even my 'loudness' leans on the fact that I am a Darkling. But kiths? That was a hard one to score. A friend of mine loosely and mostly-jokingly said I was Moonborn. A case might be better made for antiquarians....But we have too many of those, and I think there's an element of truth to this in two directions. One, insanity fascinates me. I am curious how many of these mental symptoms, from the minor to the overplayed hard cases actually happen, how they work out, what it's like. It's not pretty, in fact it's kind of soul crushing to watch be worked with a lot of the times, and yet I still have the urge to push buttons. Second,and more darkly, I know a little of it personally. I'm not unique in having dealt with emotional breakdowns, but the idea of mental fragility is....like a whisper in my life, a barely audible underscore to the soundtrack. I hallucinate, very mildly, quite often. I could see a Stranger being damn curious what would happened if it pushed my buttons, too.

          Elementals: Waterborn. For ages now, I've been told that it's my affinity element, and as I get older and wiser I understand. My emotions tend to be big and swirl with the landscape of the moment, my thoughts and general life are deeper than imagine, and my attitude towards life generally begins with the idea of going with the flow. I think on water a lot, I recover with water more than most, and I rejoice or despair more strongly when water is involved than any other time.

          Fairest: Either Draconic or Bright One. As a rule, the fairest are taken for show pieces, and at the end of the day I'm not a quiet art piece. I am not a lilting fragile thing, nor am I a subtle force-even when I'm not out to make an impression, my presence is marked on entrance. And when I want you to know I'm here, you better damn believe I fill the room with my self. THe bit of debate comes in just how aggressive I think I can get away with. Odds are probably that I'm a Bright One simply because I'm prone to downplaying the effect of aggression, but give me an inch and I'll walk out a Draconic.

          Ogre: Troll. Ogres aren't inherently stupid, nor are they necessarily meatballs. Many are tall and lanky like I am. The brutal side of me can come out in a number of ways, from cannibalism to bloody improv to the magic of fear, and much would be appropriate. I think, though, the key thing about me is that I'm not what you'd expect just looking at me-people who would laugh at me on first meeting me tell me after a while that I'm the last person they'd want to fight-I know no boundaries. In a similar way, I'm a Troll because there you'd see someone who'd break your bones, and thus try to get out of a bad scene by talking. As I discussed with Coyote, you never want to let me talk. I'm both vicious and clever, and I'll have no problem sucking the marrow from your bones when I'm done.

          Wizened: Either Gameplayer or Author. Quite frankly, someone looking to get a Wizened out of me has options-I have a wide variety of skills and pre-dispositions that can translate into roles. I'd make a good Oracle,Solider, a decent Smith given some reminders, a Drudge, even Artists or Fatemakers, and if you make it fun I'd make a hell of a Gremlin. But at the end of the day, I am a storyteller-I think in these terms all the time, and it wouldn't take much to get me started. I have a way with words when I can to bother, and slip out great turns of phrases all the time. That said, while I can't articulate much about it, I can't deny that I'd be a great Gameplayer and would also easily slip into that.
