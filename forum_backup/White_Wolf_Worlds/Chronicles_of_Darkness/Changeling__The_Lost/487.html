    Concepts and Archetypes of the Fae - Ideas for PC's and NPC's

    11-01-2013, 05:23 PM
    I think we should start the forum for the story people by posting our favorite stories we base them on!

    I'm Batman - You remember the rain more than anything, even more than the blood all over the walls. Your girlfriend's face is murky and the reason you even took the long way home is lost. After shoving you down The Hole your keeper forgot about you. Bad move, because you didn't die. You adapted into a predator, a machine, and you're going to make him pay. Through your rage, His fears, or your own sorrow you plan on tearing his kingdom down around him. You remember the rain, and your going to make sure He remembers you.

    Mother May I - You were taken, carved, and remade into something meant to lead. And dammit, they need to listen! It's not your fault they came back deformed, but it is thanks to you that any of you made it out anyway. The deals, the Contracts, you made in there give you an even bigger edge when it comes to being in charge. They were workers and all they know how to do is break shit. And that's great! You guys need that, but you also need a leader. And if they can't see that, can't see that you were made a leader, then maybe it's time to use some of those Contracts to help them see the way things should be.

    Gingerbread House - Some of your kind, the Lost you call yourselves, don't know what they want. They try to drink or fight or hide away all their problems. But you know what you want; you want power. The things you can do now, the incredible magic at your fingertips, fascinates you above anything else. Best of all, the better you get the harder it will be for Her to drag you back. No one's coming for you, not when every rat and pigeon in the neighborhood is watching out for you and not when fire and storms dance to your voice. All you need is more magic, and you're always searching. Now how does it go again? "Hubble bubble, toil and trouble..."

    I'm a filthy hack-fraud with dumb ideas, what are some the concepts you guys have sued for characters? Post 'em!

    But that would be *shudders* fun!

          Here's an Idea for a PC, that I really want to try sometime.

          The Ancient Mariner: You didn't mean to kill the albatross, really, but once you did, it was like the poem all over again. Water, water everywhere and all that shit. The damn boat just sat there for days, and just when you thought you couldn't take it anymore, boom, twin Keepers Death, and Life in Death showed up, offed the crew, and took you on a one way trip to aquatic hell. Your skin grew crusted and dry from dehydration, and your eyes became little more than piercing points of light. But then, oh then, you got out. It took a while sure, but you watched the animals and whatnot in your Keepers Domain, and eventually rallied them. You got out, and if you have your way, you are never going back. You have a mission, though, a pledge to your only friend in that god forsaken hellhole. Tell everyone. You can't let people be taken unaware. And as a dual kith Muse/Stonebones, you've got the power to make sure they know. You scare people, but they listen. Your skin might be like stone, but you can spin a tale. Mortals, other Changelings, doesn't matter, they are all enthralled, and they take the warning to heart. Along side the Summer Court, you just might have the edge you need to make sure that it never happens to anyone under you watch. Ever.

              The Abductee: You never bought into that conspiracy theory bullshit until it found you. Your memory is hazy; you're driving down the interstate, you start to feel sleepy, you saw some lights. You must've fallen asleep, and you only remember a horribly gruesome nightmare. They cut you open, took parts of you out, put something else in. The worst part of it all is, they were honest-to-God Greys, just like you used to see on The X-Files. You woke back up on the side of the hallway, three weeks later. No one had filed a missing persons report, because you apparently never went missing; someone who looks like you and has your name drove your car home that night and picked up your life while you were out. You can't believe any of this is happening; you're schizophrenic, maybe, or hit your head. It doesn't matter. Right now, you can't go back to the life you're used to. You've found a bunch of nutjobs with similar stories, the kind of people that you would never associate with in a millions years. They're all insane enough to believe you, so you're stuck with them until you can figure out just what the hell is going on.

                                      You think you might have seen Him first when you were a kid. You can't *quite* remember, thank the absent gods, but there are snippets, flashes of a friend's
                                      party, tall man, a black suit, and then a fire. After that, nothing - not until college, when you made that stupid project for film class. Then, once you started reviewing your first reel, you saw Him.

                                      He toyed with you, at first. Glimpses and flashes, first on camera and then out of the corner of your eye. Your friends left, or you drove them away, or they disappeared one day and were never seen again. Eventually, you'd awaken late at night, coughing, and find him watching you. Just watching.

                                      It was almost a relief, when he finally took you. That ended when you saw his home place, when he began to alter you to make you more like him. To have you take his prey.

                                      But you climbed, and confused him, and got away. Now it's your arms, supple and long, which reach out. Now it's your face which is hidden. You didn't want to go, you didn't want to kill them, but His persistent silence and outstretched arms horrified and comforted you at the same time...

                                          Duuuudes.... I just had an idea for a character concept that is... radical.

                                          Aaron Holgren was a normal highschool student in the early 90's. Captain of the varsity fencing team and modestly popular. His life was fairly ordinary, to his displeasure. He wished he could find some sort of excitement, something not ordinary. Something extraordinary. One day right during summer vacation he saw this ad in the paper.



                                          Ignited with the possibility of an trip of the (and through) the ages. He equipped his fencing rapier, his dad's revolver, and a burning desire for adventure. He got everything he wanted. Until his partner ditched him "in the thorns of causality." He was forced to make his own way through the "different timelines" his body changing and becoming mirror like as a "Anti-Paradox Counter measure" in case he found other hims. Finally he found his way back to his timeline, but 20 years too late. Reality itself had managed to replace him in order to remove his temporal escapades from the equation. The agents of fate are after him as an anomaly. He travels to a collage town to re-replace him and preserve his existence in this reality.

                                          Seeming/kith: Darkling/Mirrorskin
