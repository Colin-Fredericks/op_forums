                                                          Yeah, part of what makes a Fetch a Fetch is their capacity for humanity. An often unused capacity, unfortunately, but I always did see Fetches as being the living embodiment of a philosophical quandary; at what point does an imitation of such a subjective concept as humanity become an example of humanity. That, and I always embraced the idea that a fetch is actually a "real" person, in a sense-they're the part their changelings are missing. The "losing template" rules (namely, how it requires coldblooded murder of the fetch after turning it from a golem into a biological clone, soul and all) are awesome for this precise reason.

                                                          While it's not strictly needed in the core, I could easily see "souled Fetches") as a playable minor template-by gaining an actual piece of a soul, the Fetch gains agency in the eyes of the Wyrd. They still have Echos, but the difference is that they can now mimic anyone. Not the puppet, but the ventriloquist.
