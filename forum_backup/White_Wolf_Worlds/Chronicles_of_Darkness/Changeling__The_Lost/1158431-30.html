                                            My interpretation is that hobgoblins cannot retreat from the Summer Court's blessing - just as Spring becoming Summer is an intrinsic fact, so too do the blessings of the various Courts establish rules that the Fae must obey.                                             As for if a hobgoblin or another Fae know that a blessing is in effect, I'm not sure, but I'm certain that hobs that have lived around a freehold long enough will catch on to the seasons and what they mean for any hobs abound, enough to be wary about particular things.

But a lot of the Fae, especially Titles that do the Keeper thing a lot and have a lot of goblin servants or spend a lot of time poking around in human dreams, have come up against the Bargains before or heard the stories secondhand.
