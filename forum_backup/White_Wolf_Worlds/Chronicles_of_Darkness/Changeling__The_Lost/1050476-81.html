    Originally posted by Amravanti View Post
    How do the alternative Courts work? When does a Monarch reign?

    It’s pretty easy for the Seasonal Courts, right? Three month cycle, reigns end. But when does the Queen of Coin from the Traders Courts reign?

    Does the Dragon Court (Morning, Day, Night Courts) cycle through monarchs three times during the course of a day? I don’t see any indication but perhaps I’m missing something?
    Every Freehold has its own system for how to transition between Courts. Seasonal Courts have a pretty self-explanatory cycle for determining which Court should be the next, but exactly when the transition happens is still up for the Freehold to decide. It could be at specific dates, or it could be decided by committee or by an election to allow for choosing the time that works best for such a transition.
    Freeholds without cyclical transitions also have to decide in which order the Courts take power, but it's the same thing there; it can be by committee, by general election (either for the Courts at large or for individual candidates for the Crown), through a tournament or other type of contest, by drawing straws or other ways of using random chance, by consulting an oracle (or maybe just whoever is the next to walk through that doorway), etc.
    Edit: Even cyclical Court structures could have some leeway in how they run things. For example, if the rules of a Freehold with the four Seasonal Courts allow it, they could potentially skip a season if that particular Court doesn't have a candidate for being monarch or if the actual season isn't particularly prominent (meteorologically speaking) in that region.

                                    Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post
                                    Gotcha. Hmm... looks like a True Fae can masquerade as the "will of the city" after all...
                                    Worth noting that:

                                    1) The filthy high-rise is reproduced verbatim from the True Fae material in 1e's Equinox Road, where Realm forms are limited to Arcadia and are considered by Fae to be a dull but necessary use of their resources, much like they don't enjoy taking the Prop forms whose 2e equivalent is imprisonment in their Regalia (the Actor is the most commonly-encountered Manifestation for a reason)
                                    2) True Fae in the mortal world suffer Glamour addiction, have frailties and sensory cues that accompany each Title, which makes Realm-equivalent shapeshifting a bit of a poor fit for a mortal city, and
                                    3) the entire deal with the "City Gods" sidebar in Legacies: the Sublime concerns a phenomenon so subtle that many mages doubt it even exists.

                                    If anything, I'd look to Mummy's Lifeweb or Demon's Infrastructure for that particular facet of the House of Ariadne's metaphysics; Changeling's extended cast trends a lot more specifically otherworldly than can be found rooted to a metropolis.
