                The Keeper Nuzlocke. A Changeling once challenged Nuzlocke to a game with a very particular set of rules that involved her consistently releasing Changelings into the head, under the pretense of it simulating permanent death and making her games of torture and blood and war more exciting. Nuzlocke agreed, and game is otherwise a great success by all accounts. More changelings are coming home. The rules related to capturing humans offsets the need for more of them (in theory, numbers still pending). Nuzlocke is having more fun and her peers are paying attention to her "Nuzlocke Runs" with greater fever. Then she learns that the Changelings she's been "releasing" are less than enthusiastic in simulating permanent death when another Keeper recaptures a Changeling she previously let go. Panicing, Nuzlocke's Huntsmen ("Trainers," if you will) venture into the world in untold force to correct this little mistake.
