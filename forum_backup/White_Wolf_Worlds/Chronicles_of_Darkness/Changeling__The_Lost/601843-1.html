                1) The Goblin Market is intended to generate stories. If you really want to generate a story out of this that way, then make it cost appropriately for the player. That said, consider all the ramifications that come with a person switching their sex outside of the legal process, particularly for people already dislocated. Any attempt to get home will now have a speed bump, and any attempt to make a new life has to contend with some of the added layers of difficulties. Upping the price at the market essentially means that whatever's being paid is the conflict you want to explore over the ones that inherently come with sex-change for the Lost.

                2) Also on Goblin Markets, I'm pretty sure a sex change is not just a 'permanent cosmetic(read in my mind as surface) change'. There's some internal re-wiring/plumbing that needs to go on too.

                3) On the Durance as wish fulfillment, it's worth noting that that's actually viable in adjunct with the psychological horror. Remember, one of the things changelings deal with is that the Fae are beautiful as well as terrible, and a large part of their struggle involves learning to appreciate the former when it happens without necessarily tainting it with the latter on instinct.
