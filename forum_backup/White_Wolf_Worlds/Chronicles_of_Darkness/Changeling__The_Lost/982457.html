    [2E] Help me design the Courts of New Orleans

    10-05-2016, 08:29 PM
    Hi!

    I'm presently running a crossover game set in New Orleans, vaguely inspired by The Originals, True Blood and the official VtR material. I'm having a lot of fun devising a multilayered city, but I've got stuck with Changeling. I've read the Court Creation playtest material, but I still struggle to understand exactly how the bulwark and Courts function in-game.

    Now, let me show what I've got so far:
    • The Bulwark and theme of the Courts is, of course, about Mardi Gras.
    • Each Court is called a Krewe here in the city, and each one is led by a Rex or Regina (King or Queen, respectively).
    • There are three Courts: the Court of the Cross is the Court of Faith; its color is green; its emotion is Devotion. The Court of the Coin is the Court of Power; its color is gold; its emotion is Pride. The Indian Court is the Court of Justice; its color is purple; its emotion is Outrage.
    • I imagine the Bulwark having taking place during Mardi Gras, perhaps as an elaborate competition between the masked members of the Courts and the Huntsmen. Maybe the Lost have to chase their Icons hidden among baubbles and throws. Maybe they invite the Gentry to an elaborate Ball, hoping to trick them into accepting a challenge doomed to failure.


    Any help would be greatly appreciated. Thanks in advance!

                      Keep the Bulwark simple, its IS Mardi Gras.

                      So to maintain the Bulwark every year the Freehold maintains and protects the traditions of Mardi Gras with each court having its main area of responsiblity.

                      The Court of Devotion focuses on protecting the religious end of things and particapates in the more religious elements.

                      The Court of Outrage focuses on the Parade and Partying, making sure the parade goes ahead as schuedule, that the police don't interfer with people flashing for beads, that the city doesn't run out of booze.

                      And the Court of Coins makes sure the Tourists visit, that politicians don't fuck things up, and the New Orleans businesses are prepared.

                      So what the Huntsmen and enemy courts do to cause the Bulwark to fail is to cause Mardi Gras to fail, get the parade cancelled, have police crack down on public nudity, cut off the cities supply of booze, stir up hurricanes so no one visits, discourage the religious from observing the spiritual elements of Mardi Gras. In other words they try and sabotage it.
