    Dev appreciation thread.

    11-10-2015, 06:07 PM
    As written in the title.

    So far I really love the courts system, especially Toronto as its a cool city.

    I like that were getting multiple courts and the tools to make more.

    I like the multiple possibly city setting approach, including along other WotC books a strong cross over city in Tokyo. It gives me all kinds of ideas.

    I loooove what they've with the seemings and kiths approach, especially Darklings which really emotional resenated with me. if they do decide to go with dual kith, there will be a huge amount of combos between Seeming/Kith/ second kith/court (and possibly entitlements).

    There approach to contracts will get rid of the weak ones you had to take to get higher ones, I like that.

    I like that changelings can get out of restraints by using magic now.

    I'm curious amd excited about how pledges will work.

    I'm glad goblin vows are in the core book now.

    I love what we know so far about Onieromamcy, and how Changelings can physically enter the dreams via the hedge.

    I like the Fae Touched, a flavourful, yet flexible mortal splat, but I'd give them a normal lifespan.

    I like that they're updating the rules to the 2e rule set.

    I like the huntsmen.

    I like the cult of Mithras

    The idea of Loyalist Seeming. I like the ideas for expanding the Seemings like Grimms and gamblers (or whatever they will be called).

    Did I miss anything else that's awesome?

    I'd like to thank the devs for all thier hard work.

      i agree that I enjoyed pretty much everything I've read thus far.

      The new Seemings allow for a lot more flavor, I think, and capture a concept that wasn't part of 1E - how/why the character escaped, and the effects it has on the character. I loved 1st Ed, of course, but the Seemings were more about why they were taken, which was then specified further in the Kits (along with what role they played in Arcadia). The new Seeming/Kith system, and the separation between them, make more sense to me,and provide more room for exploration.

      But more than that, I think that the new Court system, and making it more flexible from the gate rather than adding in extra courts in later supplements makes the setting feel much more robust and complete from the get-go; beyond that, having a demonstration of court and city construction makes coming up with new systems that much easier.
