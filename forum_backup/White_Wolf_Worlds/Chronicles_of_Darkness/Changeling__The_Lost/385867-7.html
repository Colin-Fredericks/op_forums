    Originally posted by Gaius View Post
    I'm... away from my books and drawing a complete blank. >.> What is Bedlam in Changeling: the Lost 1e, again?
    Bedlam is essentially the ability to inflict a strong emotion from the Seasonal Courts' set of four on a large group of people with increasing frequency at Wyrd 6+, rolling Manipulation+Wyrd+[modifiers which include "does this emotion correspond to your Court, your Court's 'opposite,' or one of the other two?" and "how fitting is this emotion to the context?"].

                                                    04-27-2015, 01:01 PM
                                                    I know I'm late to asking questions here, but since I have the, I thought I'd ask:

                                                    Oneiromancy

                                                    Since my players have loved it over the years but we've had to adjust it a lot, I thought I'd ask about it.

                                                    Can we have groups of maneuvers (like analysis, manipulation, healing) and rolls determined by the category? Something to streamline the rolls but not have it all be Wits + Empathy + Wyrd?

                                                    Will there be an option and rules for for entering dreams as a group, perhaps through skeinwalking?

                                                    Pledges

                                                    Can an option for more freeform pledges be worked into a later supplement, perhaps? Because while the old system took some getting used to, it offers a lot of flexibility that some players really enjoy. Since the new edition seems focused on offering players a lot of options to choose from, it seems natural that pledges could be thus. Perhaps in an advanced form? Will there be a roll for pledge-making, and/or a system for those who try to deceive while setting up pledges?

                                                    Will there be a curse system, perhaps as an advanced pledge variation? A number of fairy tales are about levying curses out of spite, which would make excellent Conditions and stories but would need rules to avoid major abuses.

                                                    Fetches

                                                    Can we have a system for more advanced fetches and/or ways to make them scarier antagonists?

                                                    Misc.

                                                    I saw a section for goblin fruit. Will there still be trifles and oddments, or is it all under the banner of goblin fruit now?

                                                    Similarly, will there be more of a structure for the various types of creatures in the game and building them with some additional guidancee? It seemed like it might be easier to have a basic template for each major type (hedge-warped, Arcadian native, incubi or dream-natives) that could then be added to based upon the Wyrd rating of a particular creature.

                                                    Are there any plans to tackle the Hedge in greater depth and length?

                                                    Kismet's WOD - D&D - C:tL - M:tC
