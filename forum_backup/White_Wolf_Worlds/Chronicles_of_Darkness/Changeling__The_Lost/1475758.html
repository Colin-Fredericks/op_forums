    Asian American Changeling type: Cathayans

    03-03-2022, 09:49 PM
    In an idea for a Changeling chronicle that focuses on Asian American Changelings, I describe that there are three types of Asian American changelings.
    1. The Changelings and Courts of Asia who immigrated to the West, following their families or setting up powerbases in the West.
    2. Changelings who are of Asian descent but born in America.
    And 3) Asians who were made sold into the service of Western Fae.

    I especially want to use the third group to explore the long historical, economic, and political connections the West has had with Asia. I think this type of Asian American or Asian European Changelings was especially common during the era of European Imperial Expansion, China’s century of national humiliation, the mass immigration of Chinese and Japanese to the US during in the mid to late 1800’s, and then the occupation of Japan after WW2.

    I refer to this third group as Cathayans, because I want to explore the bias, bigotry, and stereotyping Asians have had to confront.Yet I also wanted to emphasize that intolerance is not limited to white people. Unfortunately, the peoples of Asia can be hostile to each other and their own peoples.Certain countries would be hostile to Cathayans, especially the more traditional courts.

    For examples of how I envision playing Cathayan characters, here is a description of a Japanese Cathayan character and a general description of Japanese Cathayans in the US. Japanese Cathayans can be found in Brazil, Canada, Europe, and Mexico.

    Cathayan Characters

    Madame Butterfly(Kiku Saito) is a fairest nightsinger and the Spring Queen of Little Tokyo in San Fransisco. In 1883, She was originally a Geisha from Nagasaki, whose voice was so beautiful it drew her a long list of clients. So beautiful was her voice, that it caught the attention of an American sailor, who was also a changeling racketeer. He courted and pretended to want to marry her, but then sold her to Western True Fae, Lover with Crimson Roses. When she escaped, she found herself in San Francisco, California. It was the year 1920, World War 1 had just ended and the jazz age was about to go into full swing. She settled in San Francisco’s Japan Town in the Western Audition and made a living for herself singing in a nightclub. As a Cathayan, she now spoke English better than her native language and quickly built a backstory for herself based on Giacomo Puccini’s Madame Butterfly and was given that title. Her physical beauty her voice, and her ability to quickly adjust to the expectations of her white audience won her the admiration of many Western Changelings and mortals.

    For more than a decade, fear and resentment had been growing among white Americans of Asian Americans taking their jobs, the “yellow peril”. Even after the Immigration Act of 1924, attacks and excluding Asian Americans from Asian jobs continued. Madame Butterfly used her connections in the white police force and among white politicians to buy some protection for the Japanese Americans and Cathayans. When President Roosevelt signed Executive Order 9066, forcibly moving more than 120,000 Japanese Americans to internment camps, used her influence to provide the people in her camp with more goods and services than Japanese Americans in other internment camps enjoyed.

    After the war, Madame Butterfly, was one of the few Japanese American Cathayans to return to Japan Town in the Western Audition. While initially very, very small, during the US occupation of Japan, a new wave of Japanese Cathayans found their way to the Japan Town in the Western District. Madame Butterfly helped build the new freehold in Japan Town. Because of her political connections, she quickly became the Spring Monarch. When the courts of Japan began moving to San Francisco during the 1980s, Madame Butterfly built political ties with these courts and acted as a mediator between them Western Seasonal Courts, and the Cathayan Seasonal Courts.

    In modern nights Madame Butterfly is one of the most influential Changelings in San Francisco, having strong political ties between the different courts and freeholds in the city.

    Appearance

    Despite having lived in San Francisco for more than a hundred years, she still looks like a young woman in her early twenties (Note: I have given her a merit called ageless beauty, which negates the penalties of aging and using the mechanics that at wyrd 7 +150 years to the changeling’s lifespan). Her mask shows a tall, beautiful Japanese woman with jet black hair to her waste. Possessing the ideal of Japanese feminine beauty, her hair is jet black and her skin is very pale.
    Her mien looks how modern fantasy artists view of a sidhe from Asia. Behind the mask, her skin is porcelain white, her hair is a sky blue, and her huge, oblong eyes are a deep purple. She also possesses claws, large pointed, dog-like ears, and a tail.

      Japanese Cathayans: The first Japanese to fall into servitude of Western Fae might have started as far back as the 1540’s, when the Portuguese first came to Japan. The records of the Fae of Portugal and Netherlands, indicate that there were at least 7 changelings of Japanese descent among their court, before Tokugawa Iemitsu closed the country to foreign influence in 1623. However, it did not become common until the Meiji Period, after Commodore Perry’s black ships forcibly opened the country. In Japan the stability and strength of Japan’s courts kept the racketeers of the West from dragging hundreds of their people into slavery as compared to the courts of China and other Asian countries, which had been made into Western colonies, Western racketeers WERE able to net many of its people into the hands of their Gentry. Many more fell into nets, immigrating to Hawaii and then mainland USA. The number of Japanese falling into Western hands began to slow after Theodore Roosevelt’s Gentlemen's Agreement of 1907. Japan agrees to not allow more immigration from Japan to the US and the US agreed to not impose restrictions on Japanese immigrants already in the US. Immigration comes to a halt after the Immigration act of 1924. The Japanese Cathayan community stops growing, begins to shrink, and is then scattered when Japanese Americans are put in internment camps.

      The Japanese Cathayan community started to regrow during the US occupation of Japan. Because the Japanese Imperial Changeling Courts were smashed at the end of World War 2, they are incapable to stopping US racketeers from dragging Japanese men, women (especially Geisha) and children into slavery for the Western Gentry. It would not be until the mid-70’s the Japanese courts would be strong to bring this to a halt. Many more Japanese fell into traps from US loyalists, traveling to study at US universities and do business in the United States.
      In modern nights, large communities can be found in California, Hawaii, Illinois, New York, Ohio, and Washington. Though Japanese Cathayans can be found in unexpected places like Alaska, Alabama, Florida, Georgia, and Virginia.

      Appearance

      Japanese Cathayans are distinct from other changelings from Japan in that their Western Keepers gave them features associated with Westerners like blonde or red hair and blue or green eyes. Some have skin that is as dark as an African American or a Hispanics. High wyrd Japanese Cathayans looks like Japanese versions of Western Fae. It is usually clear to tell which Japanese were spirited away prior to the late 1970’s from those spirited away after 1980. Because of the popularity of manga and anime, their Western Keepers give them eye and hair color like blood red, cotton candy pink, green, purple, and sky blue. Western Keepers like to give their Cathayans a fusion of Western and Asian beauty. Japanese women taken after 1980 have slender forms, but large firm breasts that are too big and too perfect to not be REALLY good plastic surgery or the gift of the gods.

      Demographics

      The Japanese Cathayan community is shockingly large compared to the mortal Japanese American community. This is largely because of two factors; 1) While many Japanese Cathayans have lived in the US for decades or more than a century, they have never officially become US citizens 2) Many Cathayans use the power of the wyrd to live beyond the mortal lifespan and remove the effects of aging.

      While it DOES still happen, the number of Japanese being spirited away by Westerners has dwindled to a small handful. This is due to a combination of the number of Japanese traveling to the US for business and education has shrunk to a small fraction of the height during the 1990s. Also, the graying of Japan’s population means fewer are immigrating to other countries. The extended lifespan the wyrd prevents the Japanese Cathayan population from shrinking the way the mortal Japanese American community is.

      Court System: The Japanese Cathayans in the United States use the Seasonal Court system, BUT one that uses themes and concepts from Japanese belief system.

      Relationship with Western Courts: Like their mortal counterparts, Japanese Cathayans have confronted bias and bigotry through much of their early history in the United States. After 1960’s, this lessened a great deal. HOWEVER, after the arrival of covid and Donald Trump blamed China for its spread in the US, they have been confronting attacks by White changelings and mortals. Western loyalists and racketeers are especially fanning these flames.

      Relationship with Japanese Courts: While the Japanese Courts do not feel the same hostility, suspicion and contempt, the other Asian courts feel for their Cathayan counterparts, they do not regard them as truly Japanese. This a result of Cathayans having Western features and mannerisms. They are treated as a US court distinct from the other Western Courts.

              Okay, so having just realized that this is an obsession that has been going on a while and has roots, I need to step back and ground myself on this, so I have some questions.

              1) Yikes.

              Sorry, not a question, but for all the reasons Loserman has mentioned, it needs to be said, yikes.

              2) Is this for an actual game that's going to be played/is playing/was played but still can't get out of your head, or is this all a hypothetical?

              3) Why is the theme of "the bias, bigotry, and stereotyping Asians have had to confront" a theme you want to particularly explore in a gaming scenario, and...

              4) ...why Changeling in particular for it, as opposed to, say, Deviant, Beast, or Promethean?

              5) Why, in exploration of this idea, is it pertinent to have a particular delineation between the types of Lost you're talking about in the first place? Changeling tends to have lean towards the trauma of Arcadia being more of a unifying subject than something that retains the earthly politics that changelings are snatched from or return to, and in the cases where divisions between freeholds come in, like in Victorian Lost, a lot of those factors relate those back to issues of the Fae itself, or in ways in which the Durance bleeds back into connecting back into human society.

              Like, yes, Asian American changelings are going to have to deal with the fact that white America has a lot of obstacles that makes being one of the Lost even harder, and that Asian communities are going to a) carry over prejudices from their own home countries, and b) are going to have their own metrics of what constitutes a betrayal of roots, but I have trouble of relating that to how Lost Society is going to fracture around and along those same lines into the same granular things when all changelings basically experience trauma at the hands of eldritch abominations who are just as clearly disconnected from the stories they appropriate for form as they crudely follow them without a particularly compelling reason for that to happen.( There are no "'Western' Fae", there are "Fairies who predate and exploit from 'Western' locations." Faerie has no west, let alone a Europe.)

              6) There's also something very confusing about the whataboutism in play in trying to tackle this. Yes. Asian communites had their own struggles and strife with each other that would make creating a cohseive block problematic for decades on end, but much like how I have to ask "What does all this granulation have to do with the protection against the True Fae?", fixating on how that happened feels like it's focusing on molehills versus the mountain of a problem that was American politics against immigrants and immigration. For some pure historical research, that could be interesting to learn and wrap into future planning for, but for a game's purposes, you already have a game that is setup between the tyranny of two major forces (An America in which you are not white, and the True Fae), getting into schisms on Asian American Communities while trying to rationalize that within the context of the fairies feels like it wouldn't be particularly fun as a whole focus (maybe a factor for a character's story it'd work?), and feels like some kind of safe cultural fetish-wanting to get into the plight of Asian Americans while trying to make white players feel okay about it because "They did it to themselves, too."

              Honestly, this entire thing feels squicky to me, and I'm hoping that the answers to these questions does something to resolve that going forward.
