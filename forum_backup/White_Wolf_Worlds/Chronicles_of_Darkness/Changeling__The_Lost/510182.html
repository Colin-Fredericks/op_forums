    Sun and Moon Game - help needed

    05-10-2015, 05:31 PM
    Hello fellow gamers,

    Looking for you help and input here.

    Background on me: I have been a gamer for 27 years, and when D&D 4e came out I switched to Changeling The Lost and never looked back. My game group has asked me to resume story telling and I am aspiring to make this by best story yet.

    Background on World: My city setting is Corpus Christi TX. I have plenty of established NPCs in place already of varying EXP level, ability, orientation, etc.. (the setting is well fleshed out) For reasons and methods unknown to the players, the Seasonal Courts completely fell apart about 3 years ago (both in game and in real world terms) The Entitlements tried to rule but in the end, Sun and Moon Courts have moved into the area and both have flourished in their own ways. Sun Court has developed many contacts throughout the city, is well financed and meticulous in all its actions and they are reasonably active in all aspects of the changeling and mortal realm. They are ruled by a deliberate and calculating queen. Moon Court is succeeding in its depravity by sheer numbers and freedom bestowed on them. (not much else to note on them yet) A 3rd court is on the scene but not a player option, this court exists only to help keep the peace between the Sun and Moon, And should not be a very active facet of the game on a day to day basis.

    Player set up: Players are being permitted to play a character of any experience level of their choosing (up to about 120 exp) 2 have chosen 0 exp to start, 1 has chosen about 70, and the fourth around 100. They all chose to be sun court together. other than that, my only rule was that the character HAD to make sense in all aspects of conception and creation. The characters I have seen thus far have been well put together and very creative. Each character has at least 1 aspect of their back story that the player is unaware of that will come up in future games.

    What I need help with: The overarching story line I am planning on working with is this: The sun court is working hard to be the prevailing court of the area, but also the national bastion of Sun Court in the US. While new to the area, strides are being made. The Queen has decided the best way to succeed in this endeavor is to create a genuinely unique culture of near utopian ideals, and creative efforts. Shes placed her subjects in positions of power in the city/county and/or enschrolled mortals where needed. She helps her subjects get advanced educations, Shes paired local cooks with nationally recognized chefs to create a food culture amongst her people to unite them. Shes Bankrolled a pop music group that plays music and makes videos that are very changeling-esque, and exposes the virtues of the Sun Court of south Texas, to attract changeling tourism and grow the courts numbers, the band is now on a national tour. And anything else she can do to get national recognition. (feel free to make suggestions).

    The Characters will be tasked with becoming the heroes of the court. Not crusaders mind you, less sponsored than that. They are to become heroes of the every man. Like Robin Hood. Figure heads to have stores told about them in distant courts. They are to rise to the top and become folk heroes. On par with John Henry, Pecos Bill, Slinderman or Bloody-Mary. A troupe that Sun Courtiers will talk about over meals, tell bedtime stores about, scour the internet for more information, and fear deep down. In trade for their efforts the court will turn a blind eye to their efforts when they push the boundaries of courtly values, they may from time to time receive assistance from the many contacts around the city, and may receive other favors as they exercise their task, variable on their effectiveness.

    looking for: So I have the overarching story plot, wanting advice for the chapters. Day to day game ideas to keep things running. What more minor plots should I put to them to accomplish? Adventures for them to go on as they go to accomplish their task. Couple of ideas I have had: Rid the local hedge of a major threat well known to the court members. Disrupt Moon court drug flows, Dispel the local vampires. But all this seems so... IDK, normal stuff. Im looking for some more new and epic ideas. Something unique and unexpected. Something worthy of folk hero accomplishments/task. Something worthy of the origonal fairy tales. any ideas? Anyone?

    Thanks for your help,

    Bill

      Here are some suggestions. Hope they help out.

      University:
      (Let's assume that the Queen helps her subjects get degrees through influence at one particular university. When there are problems at this university, the players are asked to investigate.)
      1. Several students are taking the same Anthropology course focused on folklore and superstition. They've been careless, and have dropped enough hints in their papers and class discussions that the teacher has caught on that they know quite a bit about the supernatural world. After watching them for most of a semester, the teacher has figured out a little bit about their true nature. The professor is now stalking and spying on the Changeling students and demanding that they show him more. One of the students even has a crush on the professor and has revealed far more than they should have. Normally, the professor would just be ensorcelled and bound by pledge to keep quiet, but it's a little more complicated than that. You see, the professor is an Acanthus mage. Now that they know about the fae students, their curiosity won't be easily assuaged.
      2. A group of Psychology grad students are performing sleep studies as part of their doctoral work. Everything is going well, until volunteers begin dying in their sleep (seemingly of natural causes). Even though there is nothing to tie the study to the deaths, the project is shut down. But the deaths don't stop. Is the consent form for the sleep study a cleverly worded dream pledge? Has someone involved with the study been planting dream phials on the participants? Or is the cause something less familiar to the Lost?

      Pop Music:
      1. By openly hinting at their Changeling nature, the band becomes the target of a group of hunters (possibly a Malleus Maleficarum offshoot that specializes in fae creatures). The band has already had an encounter with the hunters and escaped relatively unscathed, but they're (rightfully) afraid that they will return and be even better prepared than before. They're too busy with their tour to handle the hunters themselves, and cancelling tour dates would be financially devastating to the Sun Court, so the players are called in. If you wish to make things more morally complicated, the hunters could be making an honest mistake. Perhaps one of the group's fans has been terrorizing people and leaving a calling card that points to the band. Or maybe they notice a number of teens who begin acting strangely (Changelings replacing their fetches) who suddenly become fans, and confuse the symptom for the cause, thinking the band has something to do with their abduction and replacement. Can the players diffuse the situation without hurting the hunters? If they are forced to violence, what will the repercussions be?
      2. The band's latest music video is a little bit darker than their previous offerings, but nothing to overly excite the morality police. Nonetheless, a number of fans go missing after complaining online about nightmares featuring imagery from the video. One fan posts a panicked plea for help after (they claim) being attacked by something horrible that they aren't able to describe, but that definitely isn't human. Has the band accidentally given a creature from the Hedge or, worse, Arcade a link to their fans? Maybe even one of the Gentry? Who's responsible for the music video's unwanted side-effects? Can the players put a stop to it? Can they get back the fans that have been taken?

      Moon Court:
      1. A Moon Court motley, was involved in the drug trade, yes, but that's not all. Now someone in the Sun Court suspects that they're also tied to human trafficking. As the players investigate they find out that the motley is working with Vampires who have deep connections with organized crime. After some more digging, they find that the Moon Court motley had no intention of doing anything more than muling drugs for the Vampires, but a poorly worded pledge has gotten them in far over their heads. Their involvement in human trafficking is eroding their Clarity quickly, and they want to help the players put a stop to what the Vampires are doing, but they need to find a way around the pledge. Can the players do it without starting a war with an undead drug cartel? Can they find a way around the pledge before the Moon Court motley loses their collective grip completely and has to be dealt with?
      2. A petty motley of Moon Court Changeling has taken it upon themselves to sabotage the Queen's favorite chefs. Replacing ingredients with vile substitutes, sneaking vermin into establishments, and even spreading infectious diseases among patrons. They even go so far as to replace meat with human flesh (from teaching cadavers stolen form the medical school). Can the players do damage control before the businesses are ruined? Will they cover things up in the media? How will they catch the culprits and what will they do with them if they do?

      The Sun Queen:
      (This one is, admittedly, not very minor.)
      1. The Sun Queen's plans are extremely ambitious. So ambitious, that she can't accomplish them without help. She's made a deal: an unspeakable bargain with an unthinkable partner. (Maybe even one of the Gentry or a Demon.) A lone Moon Court Changeling has found out and has proof. For whatever reason, they don't want to take it to the Moon Court leaders. Probably because they don't want to start a war. So, instead, they bring it to the players. If they take action against the Queen, she'll likely have to be replaced, much of her philanthropic work would be undone, and without her partner's help the Court's goals will become far more difficult to achieve. Will they act against the Queen? If not, how will they silence the Moon Court Changeling who knows too much? If they do take down the Queen, will one of the players attempt to replace her?

              I've been running a campaign in Miami for 3 years now and the group has successfully over thrown the Summer Courts rule. After they defeat Grandfather Thunder in the final scene atop the Freedom Tower, they had to escape into the Hedge from some highly pissed off Summer courtiers. After finding asylum in the Hedge for what seemed like a few months, they emerge back into Miami a year later. The 4 seasonal courts have been overthrown and are in hiding, save for the Autumn Court, and the Sun and Moon courts have taken up the ruling mantel.
              Just like in your game, I have the Sun court intertwined with the Police and High political figures. While the Moon court has taken up Winter Courts Drug trade as well as taking the strip/night clubs once owned by the Vichy Spring Court.
              We have been playing this setting sense October of last year. The big twist that I have in store for everyone is that the Sun and Moon Court, in order to spread their influence, have made packs with Demons. If you know the lore of both Courts, then you can guess who they've made a pacts with. The Sun court has pledged to Belobog. The Moon Court, Chernobog. Both demons are trying to cross over from Hell by gathering followers. This is an excellent way in introduce demons, (Not fallen angels) into your campaign.
              Now the Motley is trying to establish the seasonal courts to oust the Sun and Moon.

              One story idea could deal with the Moon Court introducing a new drug into the market, Black Ink. This drug is particularly dangerous because it allows mortals to see past the Mask. Have one of the other NPC Motleys tip the players off on a "Treasure trove" of Glamour flooding the Hospital. They don't know why but it seems like people have been coming into the ER speaking of whimsical, and terrible creatures appearing all over the city. They don't know the cause of the sightings but its hard to pass up such easy glamour collection. From here the Motley can find out who's responsible.

              "The land doesn't forget. I'm not prone to, either."

                  In Changeling: The Dreaming, there was a drug called "enchanter" that did this. I liked it so much that I continue to use it to this day in my Lost game. It allows them to see past the mask, and when they dry out, they just chalk it up to vivid psychotropics. On changelings, it hides the wyrd so they can come down off glamour highs and get back in touch with their humanity. ie, its is used as a medicine for changelings taht are loosing their mind to wyrd induced insanities.

                  I keep the drug very rare (on par with cold iron) so when it pops up, its kind of a big deal. Sometimes the Motly has some to distribute, sometimes they are tasked with destroying a shipment or something similar.
