    The Books of Faerie inspirations

    12-24-2014, 01:13 AM
    The Books of Faerie are side series to Books of Magic, centered on almost CtL presentation of Faerie realm and it's rulers monarch - King Auberon and Queen Titania. I want to dedicate this topic to talk about idea that one can stea... use from this series. It's especially striking me as almost perfect show of idea from gameline. We have here callous Fae monarchs, they "not so pure" servants that are bullied by they rulers - and mortals taken by the Others to become lesser fae after they long stay in Faerie. There are even hobgoblins or Goblin Markets. One is painted beautifully here:



    So what do you think about series? Any cool ideas to be found in here for Changeling games?

      Here is my quick look on three most important characters to the series. Could they have Kiths? Or maybe Titles however they work? ( I did not read the Equinox Road now ).

      King Auberon - True Fae of the Seeming of Fairest that only wishing for his wife to get him the Heir to the Faerie Throne. He get here by killing his uncle-regent in honorable battle, and is known for fulfilling his once given word.

      Queen Titania - She is bored monarch of Faerie that most of the time cannot stand here forced husband, also the Fairest. She is also known from her affiar with once mortal falconer of the court, Tamlin. And it wa reveled that
      she was mortal girl before becoming queen of Fae, so she was a changeling before being True Fae
      .

      Amand - Hobgoblin jester and a plotter, he is officially messenger of Titania. Most of the time he is disrespected by Fae, but if they know his real cunning, they treated him much better than he looks like.

      Tamlin - He was a Scottish clansman from XV century, that escorted Titania back to her realm and stayed there for her. He become falconer of her husband, Auberon, and lover of Faerie's queen. After years of being in Arcadia he learned much about realms magic, suppresing in some matters even his masters. He probably is the Beast of Windwing Kith in 1ed even if he does not look like one - but in 2ed I would rather make him a Windwing Fairest, as he is both beautiful, and works in the ways of Fae, more than anything,

      Side question: Would Auberon and Titania have Fairest Seeming in 2ed, even if they are perfect fit for it - we know that Seemings are things you got on the escape from True Fae, not you get for being one?
