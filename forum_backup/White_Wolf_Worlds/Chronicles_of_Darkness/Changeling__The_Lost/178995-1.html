    Fairest Kiths



    Twins: Upon their abduction, these Lost were split into two bodies with one mind. They refer to each other as siblings and can be either Brother / Brother, Sister / Sister, or Brother / Sister relationships. Although they are two bodies, they are essentially the same person, having the same memories, personality, thoughts, skills, and training! While in their durances, their Keepers amused themselves by toying with one of their bodies and watching the other react like some kind of living voodoo doll. The Twins finally managed to escape one day by working together and pulling off a two Changeling job.

    Blessing - Two Places at Once: By separating the Twin's bodies from each other, they can perform two separate actions at the same time, even in completely different locations as each other. Since they share the same thoughts and memories, they can also relay information between others instantly!

    WARNING: The Twins are connected to each other with a form of Sibling Telepathy. Whatever one of them feels, the other one also feels. This means they share each others health boxes. If one of the Twins is killed, they both die!



    White Knight: These Lost were the heroes of their own fairy tales written and directed by their Keepers. They saved the land, defeated the Great Evil, and won the girl at the end. Of course there were countless dangers along the road and the White Knight nearly died a couple dozen times, but that's the price you pay for fame in Arcadia! One day, however, the Keeper overestimated their Changeling's abilities and created a Great Evil that was too powerful to stop. Disgraced in front of the whole kingdom, the White Knight went into self imposed exile, fleeing through the Hedge and awakening to the fact that they were not the invincible hero they were lead to believe. Taking a stand in the Free Hold, the White Knight prepares for the day when the Great Evil might come through the Hedge and seek to conquer their new home.

    Blessing - Hero's Valor: The heroism of a White Knight knows very few equals. By spending a point of Glamour, the White Knight can +2 on Composure and Fortitude saves for the rest of the scene.





    Black Knight: The antithesis of the White Knight, these Lost were turned into villains and given the task of terrorizing the kingdom. Their fame was replace with infamy, and their influence was replaced by iron fisted tyranny. Black Knights became accustomed to earning loyalty through force as opposed to respect. One day they were toppled from their castles by some pure hearted hero, and managed to escape justice by fleeing into The Hedge. During their escape, they awakened to the former identities and became somewhat less prone to violence and mayhem, but still follow the believe that it is better to be feared than loved. While many Fairest are reflections of all that is beautiful and alluring in the realm of Arcadia, the Black Knight is a reflection of what is terrifying and abhorrent.

    Blessing - Tyrannical Ruler: The Black Knight was a figure of terror and dominion in Arcadia, and they continue to remain as intimidating in the mundane world. By spending a point of Glamour, the Black Knight gains a +2 on Intimidation and Attack rolls for the rest of the scene.




    Djinn: A culture themed Kith who have the power to grant wishes. Even in the realm of Arcadia they were highly coveted slaves and it is said only a small number of Gentry knew how to create them. They were able to escape through The Hedge one day when they persuaded (or tricked) someone into wishing their freedom.

    Blessing - Three Wishes: By spending Glamour, the Djinn is able to conjure an item, transport to a location, or bestow an automatic Exceptional Success to another person who wishes for it. However, they can only do this 3 times for each person in their life. When the 3rd wish is granted, they can no longer aid them in such a manner. The Djinn can not grant their own wishes, which greatly restricts their power when not assisting others.

    Items which are created with the aid of a Three Wishes spell can have a maximum level of power that is based upon the Djinn's Wyrd level. Meaning a level 1 Djinn could not produce the dreaded Fae Slayer Claymore, but maybe a level 7 Djinn could. PC's should consult with the Story Teller or the rules in regards to creating items of power.




    Merlin: The personifications of wisdom, scholarship, and lore, Merlins were created by curious Gentry who wanted to see how much of Arcadia's knowledge the human mind was able to withstand. Shockingly, the Merlins thrived in Arcadia without being driven insane, and mastered its magic to a point where they were often sought out to provide advice by other residents of Arcadia. Driven by academic curiosity, the Merlin escaped through The Hedge one day when attempting to discover its deeper mysteries, and awakened to their true identity in the process.

    Blessing - The Scrolls of Merlin: During their time spent in Arcadia, Merlins became privy to many secrets of Fae magic that only the Witchtooths might be able to rival. By spending a point of Glamour, the Merlin is able to conjure a Scroll which may include insight into some kind of magical research. The scroll grants a +3 bonus on any Occult rolls for the person who is using it. The scroll remains for the rest of the scene, but can be dismissed early by spending another Glamour.

        Darkling Kiths


        Rotters: The tenants of Arcadian graveyards and ghost towns during their time in Arcadia. Rotters resemble the walking dead, having absorbed the essence of death in their time spent around Fae corpses.

        Blessing - Embrace of Death: By spending Glamour, the Rotter can attempt to make a grapple attack which instils the subject with the chill of the dead. An afflicted subject is cursed with a -1 on physical rolls equal to the successes rolled (2 successes results in -2) for a duration of rounds equal to the Rotter's Wyrd level. This count down starts after they break the grapple, otherwise it can be prolonged for as long as they continue to hold them.




        Horrors: A creature born straight out of the nightmares of the human imagination, Horrors are such creatures as Jack, the Killer, Slender Man, just to name a couple. They are Lost who's Keepers wanted to push the limits and see just how monstrous they could turn them, resulting in some of the most frightening beings in the mundane world. Homicidal killers possessing Fae magic, those who have escaped to the mundane world have managed to regain enough clarity to begin socializing again without a knife to the face being their traditional greeting. Horrors make legendary assassins in the Free Hold, and it is said that once they choose their target, there is No Escape.

        Blessing - No Escape: By thinking of a person and spending Glamour, the Horror can teleport to their general location (give or take a few yards) from anywhere in the world. Of course this means the Horror actually has to know that persons exists, and likely have a decent memory of what they look like in order for the effect to work.



        Voids: Most Darklings choose to shun the light. A Void repels the light, it recoils at their touch, refusing to come near them. Voids were created by Keepers who held such utter contempt for a mortal, they decided nobody would ever gaze upon them again. After their abduction, they forced Light to enter a contract in which it would never willingly shine upon these Changelings. A Void's appearance is humanoid in shape, but is comprised of fluctuating darkness that renders it impossible to make out any sort of distinguishing features like eyes, nose, or mouth. Despite the fact its body is constantly shifting, the Void does not gain any significant bonus to physical evasion, as it appears the entire darkness is their body now.

        Blessing - Banish the Light: Voids are cursed by their Keepers to be anathema to the Light to the point where it seeks to avoid them if it can. This has the nice little bonus of granting the Void a +3 on defensive rolls against any sort of light based power.



        Trash: Human garbage, these Lost were abandoned by their Keepers and left to rot for eternity in some dungeon in Arcadia. Their masters held such little regard for them, he apparently didn't even notice when a few of them went missing and escaped back through The Hedge! Trash are typically considered to be worthless by others, having no discernible skills that make them stand out above others. However while they may not be specialists, they do have a broader focus in their desperate attempt to prove they have some value.

        Blessing - Diamond in the Rough: The Trash were cast off because they were considered by their Keepers to be worthless, having no discernible skills. In a desperate attempt to prove this isn't true, the Trash begin to study a large number of skills at once. With Diamond in the Rough, a Trash can purchase skills at half their normal experience cost. Unfortunately, they can't raise any skill higher than 2 with the exception of those chosen at character generation!



        Grendel: Hideously deformed, these Lost were forced to avoid all forms of social contact to avoid being killed on sight. Unfortunately they possess extremely sensitive hearing and are prone to violent outbursts when faced with loud noises.

        Blessing - Make It Stop: When they were in Arcadia, a Grendel's hearing could pick up sounds for miles around and it drove them into a killing frenzy in an effort to drown out the noise. When a Grendel is confronted with loud noises, they gain a temporary +2 on combat roles and +1 to Strength as they attempt to silence the cause. Their sensitive hearing is much stronger than a normal human's, possessing 5x the normal distance. Additionally, a Grendel may spend Glamour to hear any conversation that mentions them within the Free Hold for the rest of the scene.

        Unfortunately this amplified hearing leaves them with a vulnerability in the form of a -3 when defending against sound based attacks, and their hideous appearances negate the 10 again rule on social roles.



        Gimps: When a Keeper wants to indulge in some rough sex, but doesn't want to risk breaking their precious Fairest, they turn to a Gimp. Bound and gagged in some Arcadian dungeon where nobody can hear them scream, they are tortured for the pleasure of their Keepers. One day, they managed to wiggle out of their binds and escape through The Hedge before their Masters came back for another session. Having endured physical pain that only the Gentry would dream up, the Gimps are incredibly resilient. It seems that during their bondage, the skin tight leather and latex outfits they were forced to wear became part of their own flesh, rendering them impossible to remove without skinning them!

        Blessing - Adrenaline Rush: Gimps are no strangers to pain, and have actually discovered a way to draw strength from their suffering. Whenever a Gimp would normally acquire a Wound Penalty, they instead turn it into a Bonus for the scene in which they acquired it. After the scene is over, however, their adrenaline wears off and they start to feel the pain of their injuries like normal.

            Lots of clever ideas here, worthy of some rare Kiths to offer Players. However, some of the blessings remain improbably Over Powered.

            suggestions are:

            Horrors - strike instant transport to anywhere in the world, instead allow upon spending a glamour, knowledge of exactly what the Target fears most vulnerable and where that thing/person is located.

            Grendels - strike the Cleareyes advantage of the senses, instead allow no 10 roll again on Socials, combine a rage +2 to combat and +1 die to Strength with the aggravated by loud noises. Range of hearing for loud noises and comments about the Grendel's appearance is 5x normal distances. The Grendel may spend glamour to roll Wyrd dice plus Wits to hear their name being used in any location they know. Keep the -3 vulnerable to sound based attacks.

            Gimps - clarify a bit, let the Wound Penalty stand as is (they indeed feel the Pain and debilitation as that was the whole point of the Durance) but instead once wounded (Bashed or Lethal or Dream), allow 9 and 10 roll again on success Physical rolls for the scene. The penalty is then more often offset by sudden but unexpected successes. I think rolling less dice keeps them as mortal as other Kith but the 20% chance of roll again can be a real boost. I even wonder if you might allow every two wounding attacks, increase the "roll again success" number----meaning you have to take down a Gimp fast or the Attrition will start to work against you as they have more and more successes.

                    Gray Men: Lifting you into the sky by a beam of light is usually the calling sign of these Darklings. Gray Men were biologists under the employ of their Keeper, instructed to study, collect, and experiment with various life forms. One day their curiosity got the better of them and they abandoned their research team to live among the test subjects, leading to their escape through The Hedge. Gray Men typically have gray skin and solid black eyes, from which they are named.

                    Blessing - Alien Message: By spending a point of Glamour, the Gray Man can inscribe a hidden message that only those who are touched by the Fae can read. To anyone else, it comes out looking like a bunch of random symbols and drawings. Additionally, the Gray Man gets a +3 on attempts to translate other languages, even if they do not know the language they are translating!



                    Dustling: Creatures of dust, ash, or soot, these Elementals often leave people with respiratory problems after prolonged contact. They often escaped through The Hedge with the aid of a favorable wind

                    Blessing - Choking Cloud: After making a successful grapple attack, the Dustling can spend a point of Glamour to begin suffocating their prisoner with a choking cloud. They continue to apply suffocation damage for each round the grapple is maintained. If the target is released, the cloud dissipates and they must renew the grapple and spend another Glamour to draw it out again.




                    Boom-Boom: Mysterious Lost who were changed into sound waves, they are Elementals who were used to provide any number of sound effects from speech, to music, to explosions, and even screaming!

                    Blessing - Thunderous Voice: By spending a point of Glamour, the Boom Boom can unleash a scream of such intensity, it can actually damage their environment. Thunderous Voice inflicts 1 Bashing damage equal to the Boom-Boom's Wyrd level, up to 10 bashing damage at level 10. They can only perform this attack once a Scene, however, as their physical bodies are not strong enough to withstand constant abuse on their vocal chords like they could in Arcadia.

                    The range of Thunderous Voice's damage is 3 yards per Wyrd level, up to 30 yards at level 10. Victims who are within half their effective range must roll a fortitude save or be deafened for 3 rounds. Thunder Voice affects everyone except for the Boom-Boom, so the PC should check to make sure none of their allies are close by before using it.



                    Worg: Pitch black wolves with glowing red eyes and reaching the size of a horse, Worgs are Beasts who were used by Keepers that wanted something more intimidating for their war steed. One day they were separated from their rider and managed to escape through the Hedge.

                    Blessing - Grim Howl: The cry of a Worg is often the signal for war and is heard just seconds before the battle commences. By spending a point of Glamour, the Worg can unleash an ominous howl that strikes terror into enemies who are able to hear it. Victims within earshot of the Grim Howling must roll a Composure save, or become terrified and suffer a -2 penalty on action rolls. Every 2 rounds, the victim can attempt another Composure roll to overcome their fear.

                    Grim Howl can only be used once a scene and can only be used if it is the Worg's first action in combat.




                    Ent: Known for their incredible patience, these Elemental-Ogres disguised themselves as trees and guarded their forests from intruders. They were forced to flee their woodland homes when the forest was destroyed, leading to them escaping through the Hedge. Although immensely strong and durable, it takes a lot of patience to deal with them, as Ents are slow to take decisive action.

                    Blessing - Sturdy as Oak: Ents are like sentient trees, and their bark-like skin is much sturdier than ordinary flesh. By spending a point of Glamour, the Ent's skin will become hard and rough, providing bonus armor equal to their Wyrd score for the rest of the scene. However while this power is activated, the Ent's speed drops significantly to half its normal level.

                    Kiths: Elemental (Woodblood), Ogre (Gargantuan)

                        Flat Caps: Feeding off decomposing matter, these Elementals were turned into fungi such as mushrooms and toadstools. Their durances taught them how to survive in toxic environments where others would not be able to do so.

                        Blessing - Festering Fungus: Flat Caps managed to survive toxic environments by feeding upon biological matter. This has granted them a +3 on attempts to resist poisons. Additionally, the Flat Cap may spend a point of Glamour to eat rotting meat or plants and regenerate 3 points of Bashing, 2 points of Lethal, or 1 point of Aggravated damage equal to their Wyrd Score.



                        Dhampir: The mythical "half vampire" with the ability to walk in the sunlight. These Darklings often have a grudge against other creatures of the night, and are understandably feared by them. Whether or not they are truly vampiric in origin or simply the result of a Keeper's mockery is subject for debate by both Lost and Kindred alike.

                        Blessing - Blood Thirsty: Regardless of whether or not they are truly vampires, Dhampir can mimic a Vampire's ability to heal their wounds by feeding upon the blood of others. By making a successful grapple attack, the Dhampir can bite a victim and heal 1 point of Aggravated or Lethal damage or 2 points of Bashing damage each round the grapple is maintained. Each round the grapple inflicts 1 point of Lethal damage to the victim as a result of blood loss.




                        Mimic: Used to lure would be thieves into deadly traps, these Elementals were turned into seemingly inanimate objects, only to spring to life when someone tried to steal them from their Keeper's home. A Mimic is constantly shifting their appearance to attract prey, and so their true appearances are unknown to all but the Mimics themselves. Maybe they don't even have a default form, for all we know?

                        Blessing - Bait and Switch: By spending a point of Glamour, the mimic is able to disguise themselves as an object who's material value is based on their Wyrd score. If an enemy touches the Mimic in this state, the effect is dispelled and the Mimic gets to immediately attack with a +3 Initiative. Enemies who watch the mimic activate the effect are immune to this initiative bonus. The Mimic can also spend another point of Glamour to dispel the effect prematurely.




                        Banshee: Prophetess of death, the wail of a Banshee is enough to send chills down just about anyone's spine. These Darklings are easily recognized by their cold, pale skin, wisp-like hair, and grim dispositions. Keepers entertained themselves by letting a Banshee roam through their villages at night, screaming until sunrise, watching their subjects piss themselves in terror.

                        Blessing - Dark Omen: It is said that when a Banshee wails, someone is going to die soon. By spending a point of Glamour, the Banshee can unleash a low, haunting scream that inflicts her enemies with a -3 on physical defense rolls for a number of rounds equal to her Wyrd score. The enemy must be able to hear the wail for it to effect them and if an afflicted enemy dies, the effect is dispelled for all the others as the Dark Omen has come to pass. Dark Omen can only be used once a scene, so PC's should choose wisely on which target they plan to kill.

                        Enemies can resist the Dark Omen by making a successful Fortitude + Composure roll - the Banshee's Wyrd score.




                        Damsel: Stolen by their Keepers and locked in a tower, the Keepers would amuse themselves by challenging would be heroes to try and rescue her. One day someone succeeded, and she was able to escape through the Hedge before her Keeper could exact their revenge.

                        Blessing - Damsel in Distress: These Fairest are quite skilled at getting others to aid them in a time of need. By spending a point of Glamour, the Damsel can attempt to persuade others to come to her aid by making either a Persuasion or Seduction roll. The Damsel receives a bonus to their dice pool equal to their Wyrd score. If she succeeds, her target will begin to attack her enemies, or attempt to perform a favor that she asks of them. This effect lasts for the rest of the scene, or until the task is completed. Damsel in Distress can effect a total number of people equal to half the Damsel's Wyrd score, minimum of 1 at level 1.

                            These are all ones I created a long time ago, but I may as well share them:

                            BEASTS

                            Burrowborn - These Beasts spent their durance burrowing beneath the soils of Arcadia, producing vast warrens of stone or hunting what they could beneath the sands of a Faerie desert. They take after subterranean animals such as moles, badgers, and earthworms. All of these changelings can take Shelter in the Earth. The Burrowborn gain 3 dice to all attempts at burrowing through natural materials, even if they lack any tools to do so. The burrows formed will last until the following sunrise or sunset (whichever comes first), at which point the burrow will degrade naturally. This can be extended with the expenditure of a point of glamour, which will add the changeling's Wyrd to the number of days that the burrow will last. In addition to this, changelings of this kith benefit from the Direction Sense merit as long as they are underground.

                            Cravenheart - These Beasts are those that were taken to be hunted, resembling prey animals such as mice, rats, and other small creatures that survive by going unnoticed. The constant fear these changelings endured have blessed them with the ability to make a Silent Retreat. Cravenhearts gain the 9-Again effect on all Stealth rolls, and may spend a point of Glamour to re-roll any failed Stealth rolls. Additionally, each of them gain the advantages of the Danger Sense Merit, which stacks with the bonus granted by the Merit itself if they possess it.

                            Shellsheltered - These Beasts are attuned to those animals that rely on sturdy armor to protect them from would-be predators, such as turtles, armadillos, and snails. They are all blessed with an Impregnable Carapace, which grants them a single point of Armor at all times. They can add their Wyrd to the Armor total with the expenditure of a point of Glamour, although this added protection lasts only for a single turn.



                            DARKLINGS

                            Nightcreep - These Darklings embody the unseen terror lurking in the darkness, often having suffered the touch of such things at the hands of their Keeper. All Nightcreep's surround themselves with Whispering Shadows. By spending a point of Glamour, the Nightcreep's voice will seem to come from every shadow within a radius of 10 feet per dot of Wyrd the character possesses. This grants the character a +2 bonus to Intimidation rolls, and allows them to communicate without revealing their location. This blessing can not be used while sunlight shines upon the Nightcreep.


                            ELEMENTALS

                            Clayflesh - Elementals who were constantly shaped and reshaped by their Keepers, from living sculptures on display to simple figures of molten wax or liquid metal, a lack of a stable body their only constant. All Clayflesh have gained a Malleable Form as their blessing from their durance in Faerie. Their unstable shapes allow them to learn the Contracts of Mirror with the same ease that most changelings learn Goblin Contracts (new dots x 3). Additionally, with the expenditure of a point of Glamour, the Clayflesh can reroll a failed roll utilizing these Contracts.

                            Stitchbound - The Elementals were made soft for their Keeper's pleasure, some being made into rag dolls or teddy bears to play with, while others were made into more functional forms, such as comfortable cushions or silken dresses. A Softened Body is the legacy of a Stitchbound's durance, which cushions them against damage from blunt sources. All Stitchbound possess an innate Armor rating of 1 that functions only against bashing damage sources. By spending a point of Glamour, a Stitchbound can increase this protection by an amount equal to their Wyrd for the duration of the scene, but it still protects only against bashing damage.

                                This is fun, Changeling offers so many possibilities it's hard to stop!




                                Mistakes: As much as they would have you believe otherwise, the Gentry aren't perfect, and sometimes their plans fail. These Darklings did not turn out the way their Keeper had envisioned them to be, and seem to cause disaster wherever they go. Writing them off as a failure, their Keepers abandoned them in favor of a replacement.

                                Blessing - Jinx: Mistakes have a tendency to spread bad luck with their mere presence. By spending a point of Glamour, the Mistake can force a subject to reroll their previous action with a penalty to their Dice Pool equal to half the Mistake's Wyrd level, minimum of 1 at level 1.




                                Lookout: Meerkats, Prairie Dogs, and other watchful Beasts, the Lookout functioned as a scout who warned others of approaching danger. They make highly effective spies in the Free Hold who can warn inexperienced Lost about a Keeper's servants roaming the area.

                                Blessing - Warn the Colony: By spending a point of Glamour, the Lookout can transmit knowledge of what they see and hear to all Changelings in a radius of yards equal to their Wyrd Score x 10. This transmission does not interfere nor overwrite the receiver's natural sense of sight and sound, it merely lets them know what the Lookout is spying on.




                                Highlander: One of the Gentry got their hands on some old movies and decided it would be fun to watch a bunch of immortals try to decapitate each other until only one was left standing. These Fairest are master warriors who can withstand extreme levels of punishment without succumbing to death. Rather than hunt each other down and wait for the Quickening, a splinter group of Highlanders managed to escape back through The Hedge.

                                Blessing - Head Hunter: The Highlander is practically immortal save for one weakness, decapitation. When all of a Highlander's health boxes have been drained and their head is still attached, the Highlander falls into a comatose state while they take time to heal from their wounds. The Highlander will remain in such a state until at least half their health has been recovered. Additionally, the Highlander gains a +2 on the use of bladed weaponry.

                                                        Blessing - The Red Rage: By spending a point of Glamour, the Berserker can enter into a killing frenzy in which they gain a bonus of +2 Strength, Stamina, Intimidation, and on all Physical actions for its duration. The Red Rage lasts for a number of rounds equal to their Wyrd score or until they use an act of Willpower to cancel it prematurely, and upon wearing off can not be used again for an equal number of rounds as they need time to recover from the ordeal.

                                                        While under the effects of The Red Rage, the Berserker is so feral that they can not perform any kind of social interactions besides Intimidate (as they lose the ability to speak fluently), as well as losing the ability to perform Mental actions (as they are no longer thinking like a rational being).





                                                        Trappers: Creatures of addiction, these Fairest learned how to control other people by predating upon their lack of self control. A few drugs, a night of sex, a bag of cash, whatever it took to get a subject indebted to them. Once they came to rely upon the Trapper, they would switch their friendly disposition into one of authority and control, using their previous gifts as a form of blackmail and extortion. While in their durances, they were used by their Keepers in order to control the rest of the Lost. After their escape, however, they use their talents to maintain Court loyalty and establish contacts and other resources.

                                                        Blessing - Analyze the Mark: By spending a point of Glamour and observing a person for a round, the Trapper can attempt to analyze their Vice by making a contested Perception roll vs the target's Subterfuge. If they succeed, they know what the target's Vice is and can thus learn what kind of social interactions would be most effective against them. If they fail the roll, their Vice remains a mystery, or they completely misread it on a critical failure. Analyze the Mark can only be performed on a person 3 times a day. If they fail all 3 times, the person's Vice is too difficult to analyze, and the Trapper gives up until tomorrow when they feel confident enough to try again.



                                                        Trashmen: Among the most pitiable Wizened, the Trashmen were forced to live inside garbage mounds and junkyards while they spent time in Arcadia. Their purpose was to clean up the trash and refuse that the Gentry had no need of anymore. Like most junkyard workers, however, they found that many of the stuff their masters threw out was still salvageable and used the spare parts to help them escape.

                                                        Blessing - Jurry Rigged Components: By spending a point of Glamour, the Trashman gains a bonus of +2 on Crafts and is able to create devices with different components from what is normally required, as long as the components are relatively similar to one another or can be used in the same fashion. The created device actually works just as well as a normal version, likely augmented with Fae magic to make it so.



                                                        Cloudwalkers: Taken to live in the flying cities of Arcadia, the Cloudwalkers are Fairest who literally looked down upon the other residents of the land. In order to safely traverse the city, they had to learn to walk upon the clouds themselves, as their name is derived from. Having lived in the sky, they have a new appreciation for just how vast the world truly is, and they find the land dwellers pitiable for their short sighted outlook of the world around them. Their constant reference to the freedom of the skies and living among them has been known to aggravate even their allies over time.

                                                        Blessing - Intangible Road: By spending a point of Glamour, the Cloud Walker is able to stand, walk, and run upon gaseous states (clouds, fog, mist, smoke, est) as if they were solids. This effect lasts until the end of the scene or until they stop spending Glamour to renew it. Intangible Road can be used to walk upon open air, but has a maximum distance of 5 yards equal to the Cloudwalker's Wyrd score (up to 50). Upon leaving the designated radius, the Cloudwalker must spend Glamour to cast Intangible Road again to keep traveling in that direction, and this disables Intangible Road for the location they are moving away from.



                                                        Ice Bloods: From the terrifying Yeti, to the Frost Giants of Norse myth, Ice Bloods are brutish creatures who thrived in the frozen wastes of Arcadia. With hair as white as snow and icy blue skin, these Ogres thrive in extreme conditions. After their escape from Arcadia, they found they are most comfortable living in lands that suit their former homes such as Antarctica, Canada, Russia, or the Himalayas

                                                        Blessing - Ymir's Protection: The Ice Blood gains a natural cold resistance equal to their Wyrd Score. The damage level of the cold must bypass this resistance in order to harm them. At a low Wyrd score, this doesn't take much, but as their power increases, the Ice Blood can become resistant to all but the most inhospitable blizzards!



                                                        Flutterforms: Elementals of paper who's bodies could be folded, crumpled, and cut into a myriad of different shapes and patterns. They were typically used by artistic Keepers who wished to amuse themselves by creating designs using them as materials. Being made of paper, they found they were susceptible to gusts of wind, and many were able to escape by allowing themselves to be blown out a window.

                                                        Blessing - Leaf on the Wind: By spending a point of Glamour, the Flutterform can make themselves extremely light to the point where they can actually glide upon air currents. The Flutterform is capable of gliding a distance of 5 yards equal to their Wyrd score (maximum of 50 at level 10) before they start to lose altitude. At which point, they will lose 1 yard of altitude each round until they finally land. Leaf on the Wind lasts until the Flutterform lands on the ground, but can be activated again by leaping off another gliding point, at the cost of another Glamour. Flutterforms do not suffer fall damage when gliding, regardless of the altitude they dropped from!

                                                        While using Leaf on the Wind, the Flutterform also becomes highly vulnerable to knock back effects such as those caused by wind or concussive force. Unlike other gliding powers, Leaf on the Wind can not be steered, the Flutterform travels in whatever direction the wind happens to be blowing at the time and it is very possible for them to be blown off course at the whim of nature. When the Flutterform is hit by an attack that would knock them away from the point of impact, they go flying an extra yard equal to their Wyrd score while Leaf on the Wind is activated.




                                                        Nemean: The story of Hercules tells of his battle with the Nemean Lion, a creature impervious to any weapon, and finally slaying it by means of strangulation. Nemeans are Beasts with a level of durability quite similar to their Lion forebear. Although they need not be actual lions themselves, many of them are from the cat family. Nemeans were used by their Keepers as a means to test the cleverness of their other warriors, placing them against a monster they seemingly could not kill. Those who found a way past their defenses were said to earn the favor of their masters. Some Nemeans managed to escape before a clever enough servant appeared to them, but their near invulnerability was greatly diminished in the process.

                                                        Blessing - Impenetrable Pelt: For each level of their Wyrd Score, the Nemean Lion gains a natural armor rating of +1, up to a maximum of +10 at level 10. This armor rating applies to weapon based lethal damage (such as blades or claws) and bashing damage (such as bludgeons or fists). However, the Nemean is still vulnerable to Aggravated damage (such as those caused by mystical sources) and is still dependent upon bodily necessities (such as food, drink, and breathing).

                                                        A clever opponent can find a way to bypass their armor by finding less direct methods of attacking them.




                                                        Pond Lurker: Frogs and toads, these Beasts were used to guard the murky waters of Arcadia by hiding in the grass or upon lily pads and attacking those who came too close. Blessed with the Elastic Tongue, they can use this hidden body part like a 5th limb!

                                                        Blessing - Elastic Tongue: By spending a point of Glamour, the Pond Lurker can extend their tongue up to a distance of 1 yard equal to their Wyrd score, maximum of 10 yards. The tongue can be used to perform three different functions.

                                                        1 - It can be used to grapple with a +2 bonus due to the sticky saliva covering it.
                                                        2 - It can be used to retrieve objects weighing up to half the Pond Lurker's maximum weight limit.
                                                        3 - It can be used to attack with a level of bashing damage equal to a standard unarmed attack by the Pond Lurker.

                                                        The tongue remains at this length only for one use and then retracts back to normal again. They must spend another point of Glamour to extend it again, so repeated use can drain their Glamour reserves. The exception to this is when the tongue is used to grapple, during which time the tongue will remain stretched until the prisoner is released from its hold.




                                                        Escape Artists: Master performers who amused their Keepers by finding ways to free themselves of exceedingly risky situations. Although stage magicians in the mortal world put on the illusion of teleportation, the Escape Artists of Arcadia learned the real deal, and now having mastered The Great Escape, they are nearly impossible to detain. The only restraints that were able to hold them were made of iron. They are a type of Fairest, as evident by their usage in providing entertainment for their Keepers.

                                                        Blessing - The Great Escape: By spending a point of Glamour, the Escape Artist is able to instantly transport themselves up to a maximum distance of 10 yards equal to their Wyrd score (maxing out at 100 yards at level 10) once per scene. This transportation is disrupted by the presence of iron being in the path in which they are traveling. They can not use The Great Escape if they are wearing iron bindings, or if they are in a room made of iron (ceiling, floors, and walls).

                                                        The Great Escape will never transport the Escape Artist into a wall or some other hazard, but it does improve the accuracy if they actually know where they're going.





                                                        Deep Ones: Those who follow the path to knowledge claim that there are some secrets that people are not ready to possess. The Deep Ones are those who threw caution to the wind, driven by an all consuming passion for discovering the secrets of the world. Taken to the world of Arcadia, they delved so deeply into the hidden mysteries of the Fae, they became a threat. Locked away in prisons at the bottom of the sea or in dungeons hundreds of miles underground, they could not spread the knowledge they accumulated, until the day when they found a way to escape and crossed back through The Hedge! In appearance, these Darklings bare a similarity to the Lovecraftian horrors for which they are named, such as Cthulhu or Yagoth. Their bodies are often warped into nightmarish amalgamations of man and beast. The presence of facial tentacles is common, but other more gruesome transformations also exist. Some have elongated spines and necks giving them a worm-like appearance, others have the bulbous head and oversize eyes of an octopus, and others are too impossible to describe.

                                                        Blessing - Know the Truth: And the truth shall drive you mad. By spending a point of Glamour, the Deep One can begin to speak in a language that is impossible to decipher. They speak with sounds and vocal patterns that humans can not replicate, and it sounds like a jumbled mess of syllables and multiple languages fused into one. Their voice also takes on a raspy echo and sounds barely above a whisper for those who hear it. The target of Know the Truth must roll a successful Wits + Composure - the Deep One's Wyrd score to avoid contracting a mild Derangement of the Deep One's choosing, or upgrading to a major Derangement if they are already insane. The derangement lasts for a number of days equal to the Deep One's Wyrd score, after which their mind will begin to repress the knowledge, allowing them to heal. If they fail the save, they will begin to understand the garbled speech of the Deep One, and come away with some dark and forbidden secret. If they pass the save, they successfully block out the maddening whispers of the Changeling.

                                                        Know the Truth can effect up to one person at a time, but can be used multiple times a scene to effect more than one person. Know the Truth can not be used on the same person again until after its previous effect wears off, if they failed their save. While under the effects of Know the Truth, victims receive a bonus of +2 on Perception and Occult rolls, so it can prove beneficial if you don't mind the side effects.

                                                            New Fairest Kith: Chevelure
                                                            From the Germanic Rapunzel, to the white-haired witch of Chinese folklore, there have been fairest whose durance was centered around the beauty of hair. Perhaps like sheep, they were forced to grow long locks to be cut by the keepers, made into magical tunics, rope, or pure gold. Because of the magic that was bound into it, the hair became supernaturally strong, able to be commanded by the Chevelure to act like another arm, if needed. Their kith blessing is Witch's hair: By spending 1 Glamour, a Chevelure's mane of hair may be used to reflexively grapple a target in reach of it with Strength equal to Presence. Attempts to use weapons or firearms with this ability suffer a penalty of -2 by default.

                                                            New Elemental Kith: Voidsoul
                                                            Sometimes keepers simply forget about their changeling servants, and sometimes they intentionally leave them completely and utterly alone. Without anything or anyone to connect with, these changelings become consumed by a sense of loneliness, or depersonalized with their environment till only the emptiness of the void is left. Voidsouls, as they are called, are changeling elementals of absence. Their mask makes them appear nonchalant and muted of interesting details. Their true form, however, makes them appear gray-skinned, with hollow eyes, and hair that is stark white, or deep black. Their kith blessing is Rift to Nowhere: Once per day, the changeling can spend 1 point of Glamour to "blink" out of existence and reappear five feet away in a direction of their choice.

                                                            New Wizened Kith: Jockey
                                                            Inspired by Crowbar. Represent.
                                                            In Arcadia, Jockeys were those who worked the ghost-drawn carriages of the Gentry, the pilots of antique fighter planes over hedge-spun battlefields. Horses, motorbikes, airplanes, even starships--all these modes of transportation were manned by Wizened changelings when their Keepers felt such work beneath them. Their kith blessing is Overdrive: All Jockeys get a free Drive specialty, and once per day can spend a point of Glamour to make a vehicle or riding-beast run without wasting fuel or energy. This lasts for one scene.
