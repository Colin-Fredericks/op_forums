    Thoughts on True Fae

    04-25-2016, 04:50 AM
    I felt that Changeling 1e had some great ideas, but never fully developed them. One idea that was mentioned in the spoiler for Changeling the Lost 1e was that the true fae love humans, are fascinated by humans, but manifests in a cruel and alien way. How-ever, I never felt that this was fully developed and could have made the True Fae into fascinating villains. For example; a true fae author lacks creativity so he puts his change-lings/ spirited away humans in circumstances and writes about how they act and react “If I turn this man’s wife into a beast, will he still love her? How far is this woman willing to go to rescue her children? Will the bond of these groups of friends survive the horrors and tragedies I put in front of them?” or a True Fae tries to be a prince charming that takes a plain and lonely girl and turns her into a princess. But the more beautiful and elegant she becomes, the more boring he finds her until he tosses her away to look for a new toy. Children are often stolen, because the true fae want to understand innocence or feel the joy of being a parent, but they are too selfish to really experience this.

    It would be interesting to portray a True Fae that actually tries to help the humans it spirits, away.

      This is actually an old debate that's been thrown around a lot. Can the Gentry be benevolant? The general consensus is that while the motivations for the True Fae can be benevolant, they simply lack the empathy and understanding of the human condition to be able to pull it off in any meaningful fashion. Smothering someone with love and care ends up being overly possessive.

      Furthermore, the True Fae are driven by selfish whims - remember that their Vritues and Vices are inverted. Their minds simply don't understand the feeling of fulfillment of helping others like humans do. To them, its a little rush, a dirty little secret, before they move on to something more fulfilling.

      And lets not forget that, if life really is better for a changeling in Faerie? That's a Loyalist who has no reason to leave and go home. They would never be Lost, and we wouldn't have a game set in reality.

                                              Don't think about the True Fae, it only encourages them. >:\

                                              Blue / Orange morality is the default assumption most people make. In my experiences, people's durances tend towards two extremes, sometimes simultaneously. Either the Fae cares about the changeling and is just really terrifying in how that manifests or the Fae simply doesn't care about the changeling at all. In the former there is usually a narrative between the changeling and the fae. In the latter, the changeling is a human who gets caught up in a play as a prop, and it's less about the narrative and more about being a witness of the Fae and the means by which they affect their glory.
