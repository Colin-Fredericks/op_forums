                                            Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
                                            Rose Bailey Any instructions or guidelines on how to convert 1ed Contracts to 2ed?
                                            The guidelines for creating Contracts in Chapter 7 should cover this. You'd take a clause out of a 1e Contract, decide whether it's Common or Royal (which is more about the type of effect than the dot level), and pick a Regalia. Then you'd add seeming clauses... for a comprehensive conversion, you'd want to do two, but if you just want to update the power for your character, just make one extra benefit for whatever their seeming is.

                                            EDIT: The other thing is that not every 1e Contract is going to be five 2e Contracts. Some of those dot levels can be merged together. And a few might even be better fits as changeling-specific Merits.
