    Fae Prey

    07-03-2015, 07:00 AM
    So, it's established that the True Fae take the weak (for certain definitions of 'weak') and alone. Fair enough. But do they take adults, or just children? Or is it just that children are generally more vulnerable and easier to lure?

    What about the homeless? They're certainly vulnerable, and hell, many of them are half-crazy; does that improve their lure to the Fae, or is it a result of their predations?

    How about other groups that might be targeted by the Fae? Would they be, and why/why not?

          In first edition, at least, regions with a large population of recent immigrants tended to also have a large population of Changelings. The implication (to me, at least) seemed to be that the True Fae often targeted people who had been displaced from their homes and lives, and quite possibly didn't know many people in their new home.

          In my Gold Mountain Freehold, I've tried to build on this idea. The Freehold was founded in the aftermath of the Gold Rush, and maintains a strongly Chinese-influenced culture due to the fact that many of the first Changelings in the region were taken from among the hundreds of thousands of Chinese immigrants who settled in California between 1849 and 1882. These were mostly men, separated from their families in a unfamiliar land where they were surrounded by people belonging to a very different culture, who became increasingly hostile to their presence. Thus, they made tempting targets for the True Fae.

          Homeless people would probably be another highly at-risk population for similar reasons. That said, the Gentry do take people who have a strong network of friends and loved ones and are well connected to their communities. It generally just requires more time and work on the part of the Gentry, since the target is alone less frequently and the Faerie will need to create a decent fetch if it wants to keep the abduction a secret. There are a variety of possible reasons why one of the True Fae might want to keep their activities a secret, but regardless of the particular motive it seems to be a priority for most of them.

          Children do get taken - even infants, but it's been mentioned that, generally speaking, the younger a child is when they're taken, the less likely it is that they'll ever be able to find their way back. Most Changelings were therefore taken as adults or at least older children, contrary to what one might expect from the mythology surrounding the name.

              Well mythologically speaking, Changelings were actually the Fetch and not the abductee. Fairies would abduct a human infant and replace them with a doppelganger either as a prank or with the hopes the human parents would raise their child for them. If the replacement was treated poorly, the fairy would switch them back again.

              If you want to go by the mythology then children could be a primary target. But usually people are taken because they possess some quirk, trait, skill, or quality the Fae is attracted to. This usually denotes maturity and experience, so likely adults and teens would be a better candidate than children.

                          Considering the Fae can create fetches, being a loner is not actually a requirement. In fact, since a Changeling need some kind of strong connection with the normal world to escape, chchances are that they are not loner or people that are not missed. The loners and displaced are unlikely to escape.

                          Of course, having strong connections with others does not mean you are not vulnerable to kidnapping. If you take a look at some of the myths about kidnapping in RL, people at times are kidnapped simply because they did the wrong thing or where at the wrong place at the wrong time. Simularly, people are lured into slavery through trickery, deals or even 'love'. A Fae only needs one moment of weakness to strike and they strike all age categories all depending on what they are looking for. Kids are probably a more common target since they are easier to handle, although you also don't want them too young since than their chances of survival is lower. At the same time, those kidnapped at a young age are much less likely to escape and as such are less common amongst Changelings.

                              As others have said, the True Fae do not target the weak by default. Some might in fact target the most dangerous humans they can find for the challenge or out of admiration for the human's power. But then again, weakness is a relative term. Every human has to drop their guard at some point, or move through an area ripe for ambush. And the insidious thing about the Hedge is the Trods. Doorways into and out of the Hedge can manifest anywhere. Everywhere. Think about your bedroom for a moment. Is there a lock on your door? Your window? How quaint. How about your closet? The space under you bed? How about your bathroom mirror? Any one of these places would make a fine door into the Hedge. Which means any one of them is a fine way for the Gentry to creep into your home wisk you away.
