                Originally posted by atamajakki View Post
                A minor thing; please give us a water-breathing kith with no bullshit around it. No "drowning in air," no Glamour spend, no "hold breath longer," just let us breathe underwater.
                Yeah, that one actually bugged me.

                If Seeming is going to be more in the nature of association why is there so much concern about overlap? Yes, some Beast and Elemental Kiths seem especially exclusive, but they're hardly alone. On the Record: I LOVE the idea of emphasising Kiths over Seemings, especially because I happen to like playing some interesting Kiths, I don't see the point of removing some of the more idiosyncratic Kiths because some player will find a way to make a Brewer an Elemental. But, it does raise an interesting question that has come up in several of my games: Kithless. What happens if you get your kith stripped from you or aspects of your Kith? It works fairly well in 1e, but less so with this proposed model.

                Okay, I know this discussion isn't on contracts, but it's been a bugbear for me as a CtL ST for years, if the usual 5 tier system is going to be abandoned will there be ways to craft Custom Clauses for afinity contracts? I'm not going to lie, I often thought that Ogres and Darklings never got enough contract love.

                    Originally posted by MachineIV View Post

                    Beast and Elemental are actually one of my big challenges conceptually right now. As I keep considering more and more content, I'm finding that the two overlap in a lot of ways. It's a case of something where I could merge them, I could change them, or I could just leave them the same. But just leaving them the same doesn't address the cognitive issues I'm hitting as I play at them.

                    I can see the issue here, those are less coherent Seemings and more just collections of important Kiths. I mean, you play a Beast because you want to play a Cat character, or a Snake character, or whatever. You play an Elemental because you want to play a Water character, or a Fire character, or a Tree character. If kiths are detached from those and you can play your Cat character as a Fairest, your Fire character as a Wizened, or what have you - the whole thing gets turned on its head. Of course the big thing here is the Contracts. You want your associated [insert Animal] Contract, or your [insert Element] Contract... can those be tied to Kith instead of to Seeming?

                            Originally posted by MachineIV View Post
                            I am leaning toward Seemings flavoring Contracts, but not providing overarching advantages and penalties.

                            Contracts will probably not be levels 1-5. They'll probably be individual powers, flavored by your Seeming. So like, Contract of Vainglory means something different to Fairest than it means to Ogres.

                            My current pitch is that every Contract can be used by five Seemings. One Seeming favors it. One Seeming is barred (or distinctly limited like an inferior arcanum) from it.
                            I like this, but because it feels like an elephant in the room, I gotta ask:

                            Compare and contrast this with Werewolf 2e's Gift system, where the Shadow Gifts are an essential theme and each Renown has its own take on that Gift?

                            It's already not a direct copy (some Seemings are barred from the Contract), but it's a pretty obvious comparison to make. I think it'd be worth making sure Changeling does its own thing there.
