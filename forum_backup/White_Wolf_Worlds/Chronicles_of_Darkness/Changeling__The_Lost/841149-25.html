    I like the version you've got in the existing doc a bit better. Dissolving the effect for a point of Glamour before the scene ends brings back the problem of low level spell spamming to recharge the spelldrinker's batteries, without diminishing how many effects could actually be stored when the action gets underway. As for how to handle other effects, I'd say that it would ultimately function as though he was reflecting the power back at the desired moment. For instance, if a mage was to target him with a spell to disorient him and he resisted it, he would be able to disorient someone else. He wouldn't understand the Supernal symbols that made it possible, but he would be able to understand that he had an effect that could disorient at his disposal.
    So perhaps instead of having to roll the power himself, he instead uses an alternative dice pool or even just readily use it? Let me see if this version works better.

    -=-

    Blessing: A Spelldrinker benefits from the 8-again rule on all kenning rolls, which are always free reflexive actions. If he successfully contests or resists a supernatural power used against him, he may retain it for later use within the scene. The Spelldrinker can store a number of such powers equal to his Wyrd, with any unused each granting one point of Glamour. He uses his own dots in Attributes and Skills when rolling those powers, but does not add his Wyrd for Contracts he doesn't know. With the powers from other supernaturals, he instead rolls Wits + Occult.

    -=-

    That way, the kith is still best effective against fae magic, but his ability to manipulate the powers of other supernaturals requires further refinement and training. And that's not counting in the fact that it'd make an effective combination for Chimeras or Mageslayers.

            Revisited my "Psylocke" kith and decided to try a different direction.

            Psyblade
            "The mind can be a delicate thing. But mine is as sharp as any blade."

            Assassins of a subtle bent, those made into Psyblades by their Keepers could use their own minds as weapons. While most did not prove hardy in open conflict, they became true threats once they've scattered their foes and single them out, one after the other. A Psyblades who escapes, however, managed to do so after briefly stunning her Keeper with her gift.

            • Elemental: A vaguely humanoid shadow whose hands light up with ethereal flame when it strikes.
            • Fairest: A dark-haired assassin garbed in form-fitting armor, who slowly weakens her foes with each punch and every palm strike.

            Blessing: A Psyblade can spend Glamour or Willpower to use the Aura Reading Merit without any drawbacks, and is immune to any supernatural powers which would detect, influence, or read her thoughts. Additionally, her unarmed attacks inflict lethal damage, and can inflict the Stunned Tilt for a number of turns equal to her Wyrd on an Exceptional Success. Any victims of her attack must succeed on a Resolve + Composure roll in addition to spending Willpower if they wish to end the Tilt sooner.

                            Here is a new one, up for critique:

                            Coveted

                            Tell me that you don't love me, and I promise that I'll go. Tell me you that you do, and we can figure out what to do about my husband.

                            Often beautiful and always desirable, the coveted were created to be gifts for the Gentry's rivals or to disrupt the unity of changeling freeholds. These living apples of discord were intended to utilize their inherent desirability to sow envy and jealousy among all, causing instability among their victims that their Keepers could then exploit. While one of the coveted may be able to free herself from her Keeper's influence, she will inevitably find herself dragged back into the same petty rivalries that she had hoped to escape.

                            Darkling: The skin of her delicate body is the color of beautifully polished opal, but her eyes glow a sickly green and her smile seems unnatural.
                            Elemental: She is a beautiful and baroque golden statue, with silvered angelic wings and two gleaming emeralds for eyes.
                            Fairest: His penetrating green eyes sit within features that subtly alter to be pleasing to the individual viewer.
                            Grimm: Her skin is composed of illuminated manuscripts, promising ancient and forgotten wisdom, with intricate patterns of silver and gold highlighting her forest green eyes.
                            Wizened: His haggard body is held together with loops of the purest silver and gold, and there is sadness lurking in his weary jade eyes.

                            Blessing: The coveted gains the Apple of Discord Contract for free, and she gains the 8-Again quality when rolling for the Contract's activation. Additionally, the coveted may spend a point of Glamour to add her Wyrd to her Manipulation rolls for the scene.

                            New Contract

                            Apple of Discord (..)

                            The character makes herself or an object that she is carrying into something greatly desirable to all who see it, sowing discord wherever the object of desire goes.

                            Cost: 1 Glamour
                            Dice Pool: Presence + Persuasion + Wyrd vs. Composure + Wyrd
                            Action: Instant and Contested

                            Roll Results
                            Dramatic Failure: Everyone who would have been affected by the power realize that the changeling had sought to turn them against each other.
                            Failure: The object of the Contract is no more desirable than it was before.
                            Success: The object of the Contract becomes highly desirable to everyone who looks at it. For the duration of the scene, anyone who looks at the object of the Contract gains the Obsession Condition related to gaining possession of the object. The Contract's effects automatically end if anyone engages in behavior that would constitute a Breaking Point for the individual in order to gain possession of the object.
                            Exceptional Success: The effects of the Contract last for a number of days equal to the changeling's Wyrd.

                            -Darkling: The Contract's effects will not automatically end if one of the affected targets engages in behavior that constitutes a Breaking Point.
                            -Fairest: By touching another person, the Fairest may make that target the object of the Obsession Condition.
                            -Grimm: By writing down an idea, the Grimm may then use this Contract to make this idea disruptive to a group discussing it, giving the participants the Obsession Condition related to proving the superiority of their own ideas.

                            Catch: Someone has previously killed another person to gain ownership of the object.
