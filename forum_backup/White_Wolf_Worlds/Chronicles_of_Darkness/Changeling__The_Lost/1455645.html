    Autumn Changeling Chronicle

    07-27-2021, 03:33 AM
    I am planning an Autumn court focused Changeling Chronicle that reuses several characters from my Epic Changeling Chronicle, liked Edgar Allen Poe. I have used to Edgar Allen Poe to explore many of our modern holidays to old pagan rituals. In my chronicle, he acts the role of Carapace Clavicle Moundshroud from The Halloween Tree by guilding the motely through the Hedge and Arcadia, even the underworld, while teaching them the history Samhain and Walpurgis Night, and also touching upon old horror stories like The Legend of Sleepy Hollow, The Tailypo, and Jack o' the Lantern . Because the 2e Author Kith hasn’t been revealed yet, Edgar Allen Poe does not use his fae magic directly. He provides them with the tools and hints they need to overcome their struggles, BUT never directly helps them by fighting the True Fae for them. Note: It is his wife Eliza (called Anabell Lee) who convinces him to help the character. Though her husband his difficulty emphasizing with humans and even low wyrd changelings, Annabell still possesses strong empathy for humanity. I want to reuse him again to explore the horror stories and pagan rituals connected to Autumn, but plan to use other characters, as well. I have decided to bring in Ichabod Crane as an Antiquarian Darkling and one of Edgar Allen Poe's few true friends. I plan to introduce the Dullahan/Headless Horeman as the stories huntsmen. Does anyone have suggestion for other fairytale characters they might bring in? If so, why?

          Jack of the Lantern is one of my favorite Halloween figures to use - and can definitely emphasize the Autumn Court’s themes twisting around the Fae’s words to overcome them, as well as turning a Curse into power, the burden of isolation, and trying to use people’s fear of you to help them.

          I’m not sure what setting or cultural backgrounds your drawing on, but Poe makes me think of 19th Century New England… and for some reason that made me think of Jeffery Donner as a mythic Autumn Court figure. While his story happens in winter, I’d argue it resonates a lot more with fear. The movie ‘Ravenous’ would be my recommendation for inspiration.

          And if that’s a setting you’re drawing on, using someone from the Salem witch trials would also make a strong character. Perhaps the prosecutor as a Huntsman, or one of the accused as a Hedge Ghost. The trials might also be interesting to use as a cover for a loyalist plot and/or hunt - the echoes of which might still have lingering consequences, and characters.

                                                      I thought I would describe Anna Belle Lee a bit more as she is an important character in my chronicles.

                                                      Anna Belle Lee (Virginia Eliza Clemm Poe) is an important character in the previous chronicle, because she was the one who convinces her husband to help the characters. Even as an ordinary mortal, Edgar Allan Poe was an outcast and eccentric among society. These qualities became worse after becoming a changeling and living beyond the mortal lifespan. While not as causally cruel as the True Fae, HE IS very manipulative with other characters and the only person he truly cares about is his wife. Anna Belle, on the other hand still possess a great deal of empathy and sympathy for humanity and changelings who come from the current era. While she does love him, she will work against her husband, if she feels what he is doing is wrong.

                                                      Right now, she is a Fairest Nymph, though this might change is we are introduced to another water kith in the Kith and Kin book. For mortals who see only her mask, she is a beautiful southern belle in her early twenties with flame red hair and spring leaf green eyes. Behind the mask, her skin is fresh water blue, with her eyes and hair salt water green. Her hands are webbed and she can turn her legs into fins when she wants to.

                                                          Here is a description of Edgar Allen Poe's mien.

                                                          Mien: Poe’s mien is 8 feet tall with inhumanly long digits and limbs. His skin is jet black and his eyes glow like a jack o’ lantern. His ears are large pointed and catlike, though his face somewhat resembles a ravens.

                                                          These alien features are hidden behind his mask. Because his face is so well known, he has the many mask merit and has created multiple identities for himself in the mortal world.
