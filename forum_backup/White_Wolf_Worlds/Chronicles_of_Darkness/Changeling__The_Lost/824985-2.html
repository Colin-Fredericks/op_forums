                Dual Kith primarily exists as shorthanding crunch and compounding crunch.

                As a shorthand, it's when the idea for a kith would take too much mechanical screwery to make a truly unique Kith Blessing for the purposes of the game. If the idea of the character goes beyond reskinning but for some reason can't warrant a new kith with it's own blessing, a Dual Kith works. Or really, if you just don't want to bother-some people just want to work with what's there and clunk it together regardless of the cost.

                As a compound, it's when the character idea benefits from the synergy or asynchronicity of the two kiths, predominantly in a mechanical way. basically, when helps to have to two seperate beneifts that workon their own but create interesting results in play when bounced off or joined together, even if it's only conceptual play.

                As for it's price, I think the right price'd be about 3 Dots. Bit of an expenditure so as to not just be a round-out choice, particularly some of the other excellent merits in the book, but it's also not so expensive as to be nuts.

                I could see a case for 4 though.
