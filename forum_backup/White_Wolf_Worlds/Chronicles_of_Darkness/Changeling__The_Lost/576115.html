    Changeling Conversion of....

    07-02-2015, 03:16 AM
    Armello


    Anybody else think this has a very good premise for a conversion story? I have some basic ideas of how to go about doing this in regards to the role fulfillment.



    -------------------------------------------------

    Freehold Premise: Realizing that the war is an endless cycle perpetrated by their keeper, some of the inhabitants of Armello flee the kingdom as refugees back to the human world. Once there, they attempt to set aside their differences and work together to establish a new kingdom. This also came with the decision to abolish their species based clans, and instead focus on clans devoted to various roles in society in an effort to promote cooperation and understanding.. But their Keeper is unhappy with them abandoning the field and has convinced the kingdom that they are deserters in the war. Thus the armies of Armello send others to hunt them down and drag them back to face justice for their betrayal.


    When they lived in the Keeper's realm, they were immortal. Those who fell on the battlefield were reconstituted almost immediately back in their clan's territory, and thus the war continued in an endless cycle that nobody had a hope of winning. In the inevitable event that the King was slain, the entire war was set back to the beginning as if it never happened. But now that they have returned to the human world, they are no longer protected by the Keeper's power, and those who fall in battle remain dead. Such is the price they paid in exchange for their freedom and a chance at lasting peace among the clans.


    No longer bound to cultures that revolve around a singular animal, the residents of the New Armello Freehold are able to create a society that promotes cooperation and understanding between its citizens, where previously racial tensions and mistrust had driven them to petty fighting and bloodshed. This new unified kingdom has something that the original Armello did not; diversity. Those still loyal to the Keeper know only a select way of doing things and often come into conflict with those who should be considered allies simply because they hail from other clans. But the Freehold shares its ideas and experiences, allowing them to adapt to multiple situations they would have otherwise been unprepared for. While this gives them an edge against common Loyalists, however, the effectiveness it has against the infamous Huntsmen remains to be seen.


    ------------------------------------------------------

    Player Factions


    Clans = Courts

    Wyld Clan = Priests and sorcerers guiding the freehold on matters of faith and magic. They follow teachings passed down by the deeply spiritual Bear Clan. Their Glamour is primarily harvested from Serenity.


    Defender Clan = The guardians and militia of the freehold, keeping the inhabitants safe from those who would see them harmed. Their tactics and training are derived from the Wolf Clan's warrior culture. Their Glamour is primarily harvested from Dedication.


    Sentinel Clan = Spies and saboteurs, keeping the freehold alert for information and harassing those who prove difficult to handle directly. Their skills with espionage were developed by the Rat Clan, who had a long history as information brokers and assassins. Their Glamour is primarily harvested from Suspicion.


    Merchant Clan = Traders and diplomats, they settle domestic disputes and keep the freehold supplied with the tools and resources it needs to function. The Merchant Clan was originally founded thanks to wealthy investments by the Rabbit Clan. Their Glamour is primarily harvested from Satisfaction.


    Wanderers = Whether they live in exile or have yet to swear fealty, Wanderers are those without a clan or court. Without a unified philosophy or goal to guide them, Wanderers typically follow the mercenary lifestyle, hiring out their services to any of the clans who need them. Prior to seeking refuge from the war, many Wanderers were part of the infamous Bandit Clan, who survived the conflict by resorting to criminal activities. Hopefully they will not need such methods to provide for themselves in this new kingdom.


    --------------------------------------

    Enemies Factions


    The Rot = The Keeper, driving the people of Armello to war and madness seemingly for its own amusement.


    Royal Guard = Loyalist who serve the Rot infected king without question or remorse. Despite the fact the kingdom is being torn apart by civil war, the Royal Guard seem content to let the clans slaughter each other. It is only a possible end to the war is offered that they intervene, often killing those attempting to bring peace and stability. Within their Keeper's realm, the Royal Guard are members of the Dog Clan, appropriately themed for their blind obedience.


    Banes = Huntsmen, physical manifestations of The Rot's corruption, they emerge at night to terrorize the kingdom and bring more poor soul under The Rot's influence.


    Bandit Clan = Criminals and outcasts, members of the Bandit Clan care only for their personal satisfaction and pleasure. They lie, cheat, steal, murder, kidnap and do whatever they feel they can get away with in order to advance their own goals. During the war, the Bandit Clan is a neutral party, owing allegiance to no particular clan and attacking all factions indiscriminately.


    ----------------------------------------

    Contracts



    Wyld Magic = Contracts, by living in harmony with your surroundings, you obtain magical abilities to help you survive.


    Rot Magic = Goblin Contracts, offering incredible power but coming with a terrible price.



    ---------------------------------


    For those who don't know what Armello is, here is a basic run down:

    Armello is a war game played with cards and a table top. The king of Armello has become corrupted by a malevolent presence known as The Rot, and it is slowly killing him. To make things worse, he has fallen into madness and the kingdom is in need of new leadership. This plunges Armello into war with four great clans attempting to overthrow the king and bring order back to the land, while at the same time fighting off servants of The Rot and avoid falling under its influence themselves.


    The inhabitants of Armello are animals, not unlike the story of Redwall, or the Secret of Nimh. They are divided up into groups known as Clans. typically speaking, each of the clans are comprised of a single type of species, with the exception of the Bandit Clan, which has members from nearly all types.



    When your character dies in the game, they go back to their clan territory and lose a spirit stone if they were carrying one at the time. I thought this would be perfectly described as their Keeper's doing, placing its slaves in an endless cycle of death and resurrection to ensure the war goes on for eternity. But now that they're free, they've broken the cycle and death once more has consequences.



    The primary religion in Armello was called The Wyld, and it was based on a philosophy of living in harmony with nature instead of conquering it. Those who followed The Wyld were granted various magical abilities. Likewise, The Rot offered powers of its own, but those who were willing to invoke them were forced deeper into corruption and madness as The Rot took over their minds and bodies.

                      Right, well creating a freehold requires a pretty hefty write up, so I may have to break this up into smaller parts. Right here, I think I've gotten the basic information on the Freeholds, but a more thorough explanation of each Court / Clan will have to come later, once I have more free time.


                      --------------------------------------------



                      New Armello Freehold: Possibly unique among changeling society, the New Armello Freehold consists of Changelings who all share the same Keeper, a malevolent entity known simply as The Rot.

                      Prior to their escape, members of the freehold were forced to take part in a war that played on an endless repeat cycle, starting anew every time it reached its conclusion. Eventually they amassed the willpower to flee the kingdom and its war, seeking refuge in a distant land, the human world. Upon their arrival, they attempted to set aside their grievances and put the terrible events of the war behind them. Thus, they formed New Armello, a kingdom that would not be divided based on species and cultural heritage.

                      Back in Arcadia, everybody was divided up into clans dedicated to a particular species of animal. There were many, many clans such as the badgers, foxes, otters, squirrels, beavers and more. But the four most powerful clans were the rabbit, bear, wolf, and rat. Refugees belonging to these four clans became the founding members of a new clan system. One dedicated to a service or belief rather than race and culture.

                      The Wolf Clan had been militaristic and ferocious, and so they founded the Defender Clan, dedicated to protecting the Freehold from outside threats. They passed their military training onto others who wished to join, eventually forming the New Armello militia.

                      The Rat Clan had been cunning and deceitful, and so they formed the Sentinel Clan, dedicated to protecting the Freehold from internal threats. Once they began to share their secrets of espionage and information brokering to others, it did not take long for them to establish an intelligence network of spies and assassins.

                      The Bear Clan had been deeply rooted in spirituality and magic despite their powerful appearances, and so they formed the Wyld Clan, dedicated to the study and development of magic and philosophy. Those who had suffered heavily under the Keeper's control turned to them to learn the ways of harmony and enlightenment, moving past the pain they suffered and healing themselves.

                      The Rabbit Clan was perhaps the wealthiest of the great clans, becoming the founding members of the Merchant Clan. Before their escape, the rabbits had been dedicated treasure seekers, finding precious gems and minerals underground and trading them to the surface. Under their tutelage the rest of the refugees formed diplomatic ties and established a trade network. They now run the economy of the New Armello Freehold.

                      But not all within the Freehold were able to decide on a role for their new life, or perhaps they did not agree with the choices presented to them. They became the Wanderer Clan, a mercenary organization who assist the freehold wherever it is needed, often in exchange for payment of some kind that the individual wanderer desires or needs.

                      -----------------------------------------

                      Bulwark: The refugees fled the war because they grew tired of a battle that would see no end. The Freehold was constructed around the ideas of peace and prosperity as opposed to endless conflict. To this end, New Armello performs a variety of rituals dedicated to preserving the peace and stability in the Freehold. The boon that those who join the Freehold receive is unity. Those who serve their Keeper are torn by infighting, petty squabbles and racial ethnicity. They can not work together for any great length of time, which leads to chaos and destruction more often than positive results. By working together, the residents of New Armello are more organized and capable, allowing them to plan better defenses in the event of their Keeper trying to reclaim them.

                      One such ritual is called the Day of Tranquility, and it serves as an initiation ceremony for those wishing to join the Freehold. The ceremony lasts for 24 hours, during which time they are forbidden to take any hostile actions against members of the Freehold and likewise the Freehold can not take hostile actions towards them. It is meant to symbolize that they have come to the Freehold to start a life free of conflict, and to display hostility in such a way would be in violation of that desire. If the Changeling successfully manages to pass the ceremony, they are welcomed into the Freehold as a full member.

                      Another ritual for the Freehold is the Festival of Peace, which occurs every year on the anniversary of the Freehold's creation, January 22nd. It is a huge celebration which is meant to honor the signing of a peace treaty and the unification of the people. The day of the festival is dedicated to activities such as drinking, dancing, music, games, and maybe intimate relations depending on how wild things get. Their Keeper who thrived on war and death, is unable to handle friendship and unity on such a scale, and retreats from the Freehold in confusion and disgust. The festival begins with a representative from each species in the Freehold signing a treaty, demonstrating that all animals in New Armello are unified. These representatives are often voted on a week or two before the ceremony and rarely does political squabbling prolong the voting.

                      This next ritual is more frequent than the previous two. It is called the Rite of Amendments. It is held once a week and each member of the freehold performs a good deed for those who they have slighted during that time. It does not have to be a major act, it simply has to show that you are attempting to apologize and willing to forgive, in order that past grievances do not linger and wounds might begin to heal.


                      --------------------------------

                      Ideals and Emotions:


                      Defender = Dedication

                      The Defender Clan is the sword and shield of the New Armello Freehold. The army of their Keeper is constantly at the border, ready to strike when they sense the opportunity. Because of this, the Defenders draw their power from Dedication, those willing to commit themselves to a task even if it proves frustrating at times. They can not waver in their duty to protect the rest of the Freehold, and they encourage others to show similar resolve in their expectations. As the militant branch of the Freehold, the Defender Clan is the most prone to shedding blood, although they do so out of necessity to protect those who want to live in peace. They are honored and respected by the rest of the Freehold because of this sacrifice.


                      Sentinel = Suspicion

                      The Sentinel Clan are perhaps the most controversial of the New Armello societies. In a Freehold that devotes itself to peace, they are constantly on the lookout for betrayal, and so they draw their power from Suspicion, the fear that others are conspiring against you. The clan will work with you, forgive you, even perform favors for you, but they will never trust you completely and they encourage others to be constantly wary as well. As the secret police of New Armello, they protect the Freehold from internal threats that would destabilize its infrastructure.


                      Wyld = Serenity

                      The Wyld Clan are the spiritual leaders of the Freehold and guide its study of magic, philosophy and religion. Drawing their power from Serenity, they seek to live in harmony with their environment, only taking what they need and making sure to replace what they can. They spread their message of peace by tending to the wounds both physical and mental of the Freehold, helping them overcome the traumas inflicted upon them. Their name "Wyld" is derived from a form of magic they discovered in Armello that allowed them to harness the power of nature.


                      Merchant = Satisfaction

                      The Merchant Clan is the economy of New Armello and responsible for keeping it well supplied and settling political arguments. Drawing their power from Satisfaction, they are skilled craftsmen and shrewd negotiators. They strive to make sure as many people are happy with their work as possible, and that means learning how to compromise and pay attention to the fine details of a product or contract. The Merchant Clan has the most experience with Goblin Markets among the Freehold and usually knows how to get you what you need, provided you can come to an equitable arrangement.


                      Wanderer = Uncertainty

                      A more fancy name for "Courtless" in New Armello. The Wanderers do not belong to an official clan either because they can not make up their minds or they do not agree with the current options. It is also possible they may have been kicked out of their clan for various reasons. Left to wander the Freehold, they have become mercenaries with no guiding philosophy save their own moral compass. They feel drawn to others who are similarly confused about their situation, and have learned to harness their power from the confusion of those around them. Once their job is done and their Glamour harvested, they typically leave in search of their next line of work or meal, leaving others to speculate and theorize about these mysterious travelers.
