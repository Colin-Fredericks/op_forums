                            Each setting (not all of the settings are strictly regional) has its own factions, and there's stuff to build your own. But we also present an exciting and accessible group of Courts in 1e-level detail, and those are the seasons.

                            Starting with Vampire, there's been a pattern in 2e of encouraging factions unique to each setting while also presenting a set that are flavorful and easy to use in many settings, and Changeling follows that. As in other games, not all factions follow a single metaphysical model -- the French Market's Courts of exchange have some things in common with the seasons, but Hong Kong's factions are set up in an entirely different way. You'll find that the local Courts or other factions are closely tied in to the history of contact and conflict with faerie beings and phenomena in that particular area.

                            EDIT: Yes, Elizabethan London's Courts are a great example of 2e-style Courts.
