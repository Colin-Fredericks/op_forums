    A New Version of Mobile Hollow

    05-10-2014, 07:58 PM
    I do not care for the canonucal version of Mobile Hollow. I am not interested in going into the specifics of my dislike. However, I am trying to write up a version that does seem balanced for game mechanics. I used Fae Mount's mechanics as a starting point.

    Mobile Hollow (• to •••••)
    Pre-Requisite: Hollow (Size) 1+, Wyrd 3 (or 6+)
    Effect: Your character’s Hollow gains some measure of mobility. Mobile Hollows are well known from many tales and legends, from a hut that walks on enormous chicken legs to ships that plow through the earth or hover across tree tops, to castles that travel on clouds, to ships or shops that appear and disappear at whim or random. How much control you have over your Hollow’s speed and method of movement is dependent on the number of dots spent on this merit.
    The five aspects of mobility for Hollows are mode, navigation, speed, stopping, and tracks—the more dots assigned to this merit grants more of each of the four aspects. Mode determines how (by wheels, insecticidal legs, no visible means, as the wind blows, etc.) and where (along the ground through the air, on sea, underwater, or burrowing) the Hollow travels within the Hedge. If the Hollow runs out of that mode (sea turns into shore or vice versa, for example) and the Hollow cannot be stopped or steered away, then the hollow plows forward sustaining damage (from grinding on rock, or flooding, or what have you) as the ST sees fit—damage may result in temporary or permanent dot loss to Mobility, Amenities, Size, or any other aspects of the Hollow. Navigation allows you to change direction less or more precisely, thus avoiding pitfalls or obstacles as well as heading to specific goals. Speed lets you move slower or faster as the desire arises. Stopping is your ability to halt the mobility of you hollow with more or less effort. Tracks are what your Hollow leaves as an indicator of it’s passing through the Hedge, from an unmistakable trail to no detectable sign.
    Stopping may be especially rigorous, a flying boat may need to be manually moored to tree tops or rock outcroppings, or a walking hut may require a fence of skulls to be erected around it, or a pentagram of salt or blood, and so on. Even then the Hollow is not technically immobile, rather it simply bobs gently against its tether or meanders within it’s containment pen. Your ST may allow you to use the Hobkin Merit to help stop your Mobile Hollow, in which case Hobkin will half any stopping times.
    Hollow Doors that have been linked from elsewhere in the Hedge or the Real World back to the Hollow do not function, unless the Hollow is stopped. Doors cease functioning as soon as the Hollow is set free of it’s moorings, but take at least 15 minutes to reconnect once the Hollow is officially stopped. This process cannot be sped up with Brownies boon or other magical effects.
    Drawback: Steering may only be done by a character that has spent dots on the Hollow, if no such character is in the Hollow it is considered unattended for mobility pirposes. If left unattended AND not properly stopped, or if the moorings or fence are damaged (amount under ST discretion), your hollow will move on without you and need to be tracked down, the Hollow will move at its slowest speed for 1 rd then increase 1 speed increment per round up to it’s maximum speed.
    Special: Characters who share a Hollow can also share Mobility dots, in which case the Wyrd pre-requisite is a total of 6 or more of all contributors added together. Each of these characters receive the full benefits of the Mobile Hollow, including knowledge of how to steer, stop, and otherwise control the mobility. It may happen that the characters suffer a falling-out, in which case one or more might be asked to forfeit their Mobile Hollow privileges by the others. Those who are banned lose whatever dots they contributed. Shared Mobile Hollows should be marked with an asterisk (*) on your character sheet. See the description of the Hollow Merit for details on how to allocate dots.

    • = Choose one mode of travel. Navigation requires a pilot to physically manhandle the Hollow (via rigging, a large wheel, elaborate array of crystal rods, or some other aesthetically appropriate mechanism) and the hollow is only able to move forward or turn up to 45 degrees every round (this includes up and down for flying, swimming, or burrowing Hollows). The Hollow moves at a constant and steady pace (speed = highest Wyrd of Hollow owners + Hollow Amenities – Hollow Size, minimum of 1). Stopping requires more physical effort and takes 15 minutes, while you jump out and lash the Hollow to a secure mooring or set up a pen of some sort (this is considered a mundane task so Brownies Boon or similar effects may be employed to reduce the time taken). Your Hollow leaves a wide, distinct, and easily detectable track (plowed earth, crushed trees, plume of smoke, noxious vapor, a rainbow, an eerie chill or ringing sound, etc.) that take several days to dissipate in the ever-changing Hedge—anyone tracking your hollow gains +5 for the first 24 hrs, +3 for the second day, and +1 on the third.
    •• = Choose one of the following enhancements: one additional mode, navigate up to 90 degrees per round, control speed from 1 up to double base (as listed above), stopping only takes 5 minutes (Anker instead of ropes, a tool to help set out the fence, etc.), or tracks are less obvious and dissipate faster (tracking is at +3 within the first 12 hrs, +2 for the next 12 hrs, and +1 for 12 hrs after that).
    ••• = gain two more of the enhancements from the 2 dot version, or choose to have Doors reconnect after stopping in only 5 minutes as one of the enhancements. Each navigation enhancement adds another 45 degrees of control per round to a maximum of 180 degrees. Each speed enhancement adds another amount equal to the base speed to the Hollow’s tops speed to a maximum of 5 base increments. Each stopping enhancement makes stopping faster as follows: from 15 min to 5 min to 1 min to 1 round to instant. Each tracks enhancement reduces delectability as follows: 1st enhancement = +3/0-12hr, +2/12-24hr, +1/24-36hr, 2nd enhancement = +3/0-1hr, +2/1-2hr, +1/2-3hr, 3rd enhancement = +3/0-10min, +2/10-20min, +1/20-30min, 4th enhancement no tracks left).
    •••• = gain two more enhancements as listed above, or the ability to remotely start and stop the Hollow as one enhancement, or autopilot as one enhancement. Remote on/off requires you to be in the Hedge and could resild in the Hollow heading off unattended if not careful. Autopilot still requires a Hollow Owner to be present in the Hollow, but allows the navigator to set a specific course that the Hollow will follow until a new course of action is indicaded—setting courses takes 1 minute of uninterrupted action. If Doors reconnection is taken again at this rank the Hollow’s Doors reconnect in 1 minute. Door reconnection may only be enhanced once per rank.
    ••••• = gain two more of the above enhancements, or choose teleportation as one enhancement, or remote control as one enhancement. Teleportation allows the hollow to move instantaneously within a range equal to the Hollow’s base speed in miles a number of times per day equal to the Hollow’s owner’s lowest Wyrd—teleporting leaves no tracks. Remote control allows you to summon your Hollow while in the Hedge, it will move at its max speed avoiding obstacles (teleporting if possible), until it reaches the nearest Hollow owner within the Hedge, or you may send it away unattended and it will move at whatever speed you set for up to one day and still avoid obstacles—if the Mobile Hollow does not have remote on/off and is unattened, it will not stop and continue on in a strait line getting faster until found or crashing. Choosing reconnecting doors a 3rd time allows the doors to reconnect as soon as the Hollow stops—teleporting is movement, thus doors will need to reconnect after every teleport.
    Note: navigation never allows for reverse. Chalk it up to one of the many perversities of being in the Hedge and dealing with Wyrd dependent objects.

    I am wondering if I should just make "stopping" a feature of "navigation" or "speed", rather than it's own aspect. I am also concerned that 2 enhancements at 3, 4, and 5 dots is too much.
    Any constryctive or possitive thoughts?

    I hope you will enjoy it.

      Nice write up.
      I would do a couple things to make this even more like Fae Mounts. Stopping should be part of navigation and there is some 'base stopping' for all Mobile Hollows.

      Make a list of enhancements that are minor, major, or supernatural. Allow the group to choose the enhancements then based on your scale of additions, but cost the enhancements as you suggested with some of the extras.

      Minor: extra turning, cover tracks, fast stop, fast anchor (minor enhancements are a single value choice)

      Major: autopilot, extra speed, surf and turf, (major enhancements are a double value choice)

      Supernatural: healing, dream anchor, intrinsic weapon, (unique enhancements are a triple value choice)



      I would drop the idea that turning is based on dots, but provide a base turning for any mobile hollow, then subtract the hollow size (@ 10 degree increments) from the turning speed.
      Examples:
      the hollow is a cloud, base turn is 30 degrees, but subtract 10 degrees for each double size greater than an Ogre, (cloud is the size of 20 Ogres, which is 4 ranks of subtraction, the cloud can turn 10 degrees a turn, mobile hollows always can turn at least 10 degrees a turn)
      the hollow is a British Police Box, base turn is 30 degrees, but subtract 10 degrees for each double size greater than Ogre. (no subtraction for size)
      the hollow is an airship, base turn is 30 degrees, but subtract 10 degrees for each double size greater than Ogre. (airship is the size of 30 Ogres, rank 4 subtraction) And the owners have added extra turning twice (1+1+1 degree base turn = 90 degrees.) Final turning per turn is 50 degrees.

              So, um, yeah, I totally went a variety of mixed nuts with my first draft. I had at least two completely new game mechanics, that just did not look like anything else in White Wolf.
              Here's my redraft, I think it's much smoother and more in line with the published game materials. Although, I'm still not confident about the total possible number of enhancements (6) and I wonder if all the remote access mods should be lumped into one at the 5 dot level.

              Mobile Hollow (• to •••••)
              Pre-Requisite: Hollow (Size) 1+, Wyrd 3 (or 6+)
              Effect: Your character’s Hollow gains some measure of mobility. Mobile Hollows are well known from many tales and legends, from a hut that walks on enormous chicken legs to ships that plow through the earth or hover across tree tops, to castles that travel on clouds, to ships or shops that appear and disappear at whim or random. How much control you have over your Hollow’s speed and method of movement is dependent on the number of dots spent on this merit.
              The three base aspects of Hollow mobility are mode, navigation, and speed. Mode determines how (by wheels, insecticidal legs, no visible means, as the wind blows, etc.) and where (along the ground through the air, on sea, underwater, or burrowing) the Hollow travels within the Hedge. If the Hollow runs out of that mode (sea turns into shore or vice versa, for example) and the Hollow cannot be stopped or steered away, then the hollow plows forward sustaining damage (from grinding on rock, or flooding, or what have you) as the ST sees fit—damage may result in temporary or permanent dot loss to Mobility, Amenities, Size, or any other aspects of the Hollow. Navigation allows you to change direction (and possibly mode) less or more precisely, thus avoiding pitfalls or obstacles as well as heading to specific goals. Navigating may only be done by a character that has spent dots on the Hollow, if no such character is in the Hollow it is considered unattended for mobility purposes. Speed lets you move slower, faster, or stop as the desire arises. More dots allow you to choose enhancements to one or more of the base aspects. Stopping may be especially rigorous, a flying boat may need to be manually moored to tree tops or rock outcroppings, or a walking hut may require a fence of skulls to be erected around it, or a pentagram of salt or blood, and so on. Your ST may allow you to use the Hobkin Merit to help stop your Mobile Hollow, in which case Hobkin will half any stopping times.

              Drawback: If left unattended AND not properly stopped, or if the mechanisms of the stopping are damaged (amount under ST discretion), your hollow will move on without you and need to be tracked down, the Hollow will move at its slowest speed for 1 rd then increase 1 speed increment per round up to it’s maximum speed.
              Drawback: a Mobile Hollows is never technically immobile, even when stopped the hollow still bobs gently against its tether or meanders within it’s containment area. Thus, allowing the possibility of the Hollow moving of unattended.
              Drawback: Hollows by their very nature are not mundane, therefore Brownie’s Boon and other similar merits or abilities can not be used to effect stopping or navigation.
              Drawback: your Mobile Hollow may leave tracks of some kind through the Hedge (plowed earth, crushed trees, plume of smoke, noxious vapor, a rainbow, an eerie chill or ringing sound, etc.) the ever changing Hedge will obscure the tracks after 24 hours. Thus any attempts to locate your Hollow in an area it has passed through within the last day are granted a bonus equal to 1 + Hollow’s Size – Hollow’s Ward—this may result in a negative modifier for the tracker. If the Mobile Hollow has remained stopped for over 24 hours, then it’s full Ward penalty applies as normal to attempt to locate it—including for owner’s of the Hollow that were not present during the last movement/stopping.
              Drawback: Hollow Doors that have been linked from elsewhere in the Hedge or the Real World back to the Hollow do not function, unless the Hollow is stopped. Doors cease functioning as soon as the Hollow is set free of it’s moorings, but take at least 16 minutes to reconnect once the Hollow is officially stopped. Use of Ritual Door will fail if the Hollow is not stopped, or not stopped long enough, but will otherwise grant access—even if you do not know the Hollow’s current location.

Each of these characters receive the full benefits of the Mobile Hollow, including knowledge of how to steer, stop, and otherwise control the mobility. It may happen that the characters suffer a falling-out, in which case one or more might be asked to forfeit their Mobile Hollow privileges by the others. Those who are banned lose whatever dots they contributed. Shared Mobile Hollows should be marked with an asterisk (*) on your character sheet. See the description of the Hollow Merit for details on how to allocate dots.

              • = Choose one mode of travel. Navigation requires a pilot to physically manhandle the Hollow (via rigging, a large wheel, elaborate array of crystal rods, or some other aesthetically appropriate mechanism) and the hollow is only able to move forward or turn up to 45 degrees every round (this includes up and down for flying, swimming, or burrowing Hollows). The Hollow has two speeds, stopped and base movement (= highest Wyrd of Hollow owners + Hollow Amenities – Hollow Size, minimum of 1). Stopping also requires physical effort and takes 16 minutes, while you jump out and lash the Hollow to a secure mooring or set up a pen of some sort.
              •• = Choose one of the following enhancements: one additional mode, navigate up to 90 degrees per round, navigate in reverse, stopping time halved and variable speed (1 up to double base movement).
              ••• = gain one more of the enhancements from the 2 dot version, or choose to have Doors reconnect in half the time. Each steering enhancement adds another 45 degrees of control per round to a maximum of 180 degrees. Each speed enhancement halves stopping time and adds another amount equal to the base movement to the Hollow’s top speed (maximum of 5 base increments).
              •••• = gain two more enhancements as listed above, or the ability to remotely start and stop the Hollow as one enhancement, or autopilot as one enhancement.                               Okay, so after further consideration, I had an epiphany. I had been approaching Mobile Hollow in a reactionary manner based on the original merit and then tried to rebuild it based on Fae Mount (not a Hollow related merit at all). So, I re-read Hollow and it four basic aspects, plus Archive, Workshop, Hobkin, and Ritual Doorway to get into the truly Hollow related mindset. What follows is something that I feel is much more in keeping with the aesthetics of all the other Hollow related merits.

                              Mobile Hollow (•••)
                              [AMRV House Rule]
                              Pre-Requisite: Hollow Amenities (•••••)
                              Effect: Your character’s Hollow gains some measure of mobility. Mobile Hollows are well known from many tales and legends, from a hut that walks on enormous chicken legs to ships that plow through the earth or hover across tree tops, to castles that travel on clouds, to ships or shops that appear and disappear at whim or random. This merit grants your Hollow the ability to move in, or on, one medium—ground travel, sea faring, flying, submarine, or burrowing—while remaining within the Hedge at all times. This merit may be taken multiple times, each time adding one mode of travel. If the Hollow runs out of the appropriate terrain, it stops until you can redirect it. You and the ST should work together to describe how your Hollow’s mobility manifests (i.e. by wheels, insecticidal legs, no visible means, as the wind blows, etc.)
                              Also consult with the ST regarding the manner in which your Hollow’s mobility is activated, steered, and stopped. Starting and stopping may require use of a special key, enacting a particular ritual (setting up/removing a pentagram of salt, fence of skulls, or a ship’s anchor, etc.) Steering your Hollow should also be thematically appropriate for your Hollow (manipulating rigging, a massive wheel, a panel of colored crystals, etc.) and requires at least one character’s full attention while the Hollow is in motion. The ST may require a drive check to steer a Hollow in harrowing situations (being chased, terrible weather, etc.) If the Hollow’s Pilot is distracted or otherwise prevented from actively steering, the ST will determine if the Hollow stops or moves on randomly (and what that would mean in game terms).
                              The speed and maneuverability of your hollow is determined by the pilot’s wyrd, the Hollow’s other statistics, and the ST. Base speed is equal to the pilot’s Wyrd + Hollow Amenities – Hollow Size, with a minimum of 1. If your Hollow has a base speed greater than 1, then the pilot may vary the speed from 1 to the full base speed freely. The pilot may exceed the base speed—up to dubble the base speed—with a successful drive check per minute of faster movement. The ST will determine how easily your Hollow can maneuver turns and any modifiers to navigation due to terrain, weather, etc.
                              Your Mobile Hollow leaves tracks of some kind through the Hedge (plowed earth, crushed trees, plume of smoke, noxious vapor, a rainbow, an eerie chill or ringing sound, etc.) the ever changing Hedge will obscure the tracks after 24 hours. Thus any attempts to locate your Hollow in an area it has passed through within the last day are granted a bonus equal to 1 + Hollow’s Size – Hollow’s Ward—this may result in a negative modifier for the tracker. If the Mobile Hollow has remained stopped for over 24 hours, then it’s full Ward penalty applies as normal to attempt to locate it—including for owner’s of the Hollow that were not present during the last movement/stopping.

                              Note: anyone that has a Wyrd rank and access to your Hollow’s steering mechanism can drive the Hollow. So, consider having a secret ritual or special key for starting the mobility, to reduce ease of access further, and be careful who you let into the pilot’s area.

                              Drawback: Hollow Doors that have been linked from elsewhere in the Hedge or the Real World back to the Hollow do not function, unless the Hollow is stopped. Doors cease functioning as soon as the Hollow is set free of it’s moorings, but take at least 16 minutes to reconnect once the Hollow is officially stopped. Use of Ritual Doorway will fail if the Hollow is not stopped, but will otherwise grant access—even if you do not know the Hollow’s current location.

                              Special: upon taking the Mobile Hollow merit multiple times the ST may agree to let you have some benefit other than an additional terrain movement. Perhaps you can convince your ST to allow your Hollow Doors to remain functional while the Hollow moves, gain greater speed, gain remote control over the Hollow’s mobility, teleportation, or something else. Additional movements (like teleportation) will most likely be based on the pilot’s wyrd for distance and number of daily uses, however it is completely under the ST discretion in order to maintain their narrative for the game.

                              Special: Characters who share a Hollow can also share Mobility dots, in which case the Wyrd pre-requisite is a total of 6 or more of all contributors added together. Each of these characters receive the full benefits of the Mobile Hollow, including knowledge of how to steer, stop, and otherwise control the mobility. It may happen that the characters suffer a falling-out, in which case one or more might be asked to forfeit their Mobile Hollow privileges by the others. Those who are banned lose whatever dots they contributed. Shared Mobile Hollows should be marked with an asterisk (*) on your character sheet. See the description of the Hollow Merit for details on how to allocate dots.

                                      I went more with the Ritual Doorway model of a Hollow related merits. Of the basic four only Size and Amenities have guidlines for teir increases. Wards gives a flat +1 per dot bonus. Doors only give 1 new door per dot, then adds Ritual Dorrway. Similarly Archive and Workshop only offer a single add-on per dot. So, I did not feel the exponential teir mods were all that essencial. And it struck me that mobility was most like a suped-up Amenity and that matched closest to Ritual Doorway as a suped-up Door.

                                      As for the per dot enhancements in general, I really tried that with the two previous versions. I just did not feel happy with setting a specific mod (only speed, or only terrain, etc.), as a player I would want more flexibility. Hense the earlier versions having such complex dot tiers. I felt like my compromise with this third version of allowing the merit to be taken multiple times allowed for the players to enhance if they wanted while not bogging the merit down with too much mechanics. Plus, the "special" note allows enough leway that the player and ST to make any mods to the mobility that are roughly equivelent to adding a new terrain. So, take the merit once get one terrain type of mobility, take it again and see if your ST will allow much greater speed, or remote control, or teleporting, and so on.
