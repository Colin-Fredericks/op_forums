    Biological sex and the Mask

    07-24-2015, 11:31 AM
    Hoooo. Okay, once again, starting a thread here very, very nervously. Different reasons this time - less unfamiliarity (you guys seem pretty cool?) and more... slightly ashamed of the subject matter.

    So it's clearly possible for a Changeling to no longer have a biological sex. Being made entirely of snow, or being a tree, or so on results in a Changeling who in spite of their Mask does not have a sex in any human fashion. This doesn't TEND to bleed through the mask, because... the mask is obviously human. So the mask appears to have the biological sex of the Changeling beforehand.

    As a contrast to that, their fae Mien clearly does affect their mask in some ways - Wizened may appear shorter or 'lessened', and Ogre masks adapt to their new hulking forms, resulting in a Changeling who is noticibly different from themselves pre-abduction.

    I've been wondering (and beating myself up over - as mentioned, ashamed that I'm asking about this) - if a Keeper was to change the sex of one of their Changelings, which is undeniably within their power in Arcadia and seems pretty plausible for someone taken as a Romancer - and they escaped, would their Mask be identical to themselves pre-abduction, or would it be like an Ogre, adapting slightly to fit the new form?

    The Bodhisattvas of the Broken Cage are noted to occasionally slowly change sex. This, however, doesn't affect their mask at all. Is this just a hard-and-fast rule on the Mask always conforming to their gender identity, or is their mask trying to... well, mask the changes?

      Something to be said about the Mask that is mentioned but isn't prominent is that the Mask molds to what makes sense as a human analogue to the faerie under it. So, let's say I got taken and got turned into a woman. Coming back, I would find that my Mask looks like a female version of myself, like I went got a really good sex change operation.

      The Mask is rigid, but it's not so rigid as to be completely contradictory. The example they gave in the book was if someone went and lost a lot of weight in Arcadia, the weight loss would show, just not anything related to the fae. Gender goes the same way.

              This is a situation where I believe the kind of story the player is trying to tell with this character is infinitely more important than anything written in the book. Does the player want to explore the idea of a character who is perceived as a different sex than they actually are? Their Mask can absolutely be different than their Mane, because that's an important thing to be able to experience through roleplay, so I really don't care if the book says it should work that way or not. Does the player want to play a character who was secure in their gender identity prior to being taken but having their sex changed in their Durance has left them questioning their identity? It seems like having the Mask and Mane both match their new sex would fit that narrative better, and again I think that narrative is more important than whether the book says that's how it would work or not.

              EDIT: Also, I don't think there's anything to be ashamed of about asking these kinds of questions. The fact that Changeling allows players to examine issues like the relationship between sex, gender identity, and gender expression, and the experience of those being out-of-synch, is part of what's great about it! So I say good on you for bringing this matter up.

                              I think the answer is perfectly obvious myself: It's different for different Changelings.

                              That said if you want to tell a story about Changelings and gender identity you have an enormous elephant in the room. Goblin Markets. If you don't like your biological sex it seems almost obvious that you can buy yourself a sex change (Mein and/or Mask) since the Goblin Markets PDF lists transformations as a category of goods. And there's probably other ways to do it too.

                              Changeling is a good game to explore identity issues with, but if you do the transgender charachter who knows what they're supposed to be, the opposite sex, then you have to ask why the Character hasn't just applied a magical solution yet. Changeling and identity works best when after the mind-fuck that is Arcadia you don't know who you are anymore. They are called The Lost after all.

                              If you wanted to do a straight forward transgender charachter you'd probably have to be creative. Perhaps the Changeling who's keeper turned him into a woman doesn't want to buy anyone's maleness, he wants his maleness which is unfortunately lying in an Arcadian storage room. Or a Mirrorskin constantly shifted disguises during their Durance but only ever into one sex (long term undercover in a segregated area) and their gender issues isn't that they have the wrong body, since they can shapeshift, it's that they get temporally stuck in the wrong sex whenever they have an Arcadian flashback.
