    Originally posted by Elfive View Post
    So, to be clear, when a Title is empowering a huntsman, it's unavailable for the True Fae to manifest in, right?
    To all available evidence, no — the description of what happens when a Huntsman dies frames the Title as returning to a still-extant actor, and the Gentry's section describes them as sufficiently boundless that it's not difficult to imagine a Keeper's Title serving as both the principal and the source of its Huntsman agent's power, motifs, and agenda.

    The imposition is more like that of a vampire and ghoul than a body-thief and its host save for the implicit limit of one-Huntsman-at-a-time; the Keeper is staking its Title on the verderer's success, not outright possessing it through the shared desire.

    Could make life tricky for single-title keepers.
    Fortunately, if we're drawing from Equinox Roads, most Fae have at least two Titles and having less than that is generally dire straits regardless of what it hopes to accomplish.
