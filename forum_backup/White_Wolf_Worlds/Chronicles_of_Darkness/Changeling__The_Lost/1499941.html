    My Homebrewed Tokens

    12-17-2022, 08:30 AM
    Dripping Skull Ring (●●●●●)

    Mask: A silver and gold ring with a stylized human skull.

    Mien: The skull becomes cyclopean, laughs and drools blood.

    Effect: The user effectively has Striking Looks 2 (Advanced) [Glorious], Fame 3 (Advanced 1) and Status 5 (All Mundane Organizations), which manifests as a bloody halo that makes the user appear to be some form of divinity, cosmic force, greater power or equivalent that is completely beyond reproach. A policeman feels like he is talking to embodiment of the rule of law or might makes right, a businessman feels like he is talking to the father of all CEOs, and a priest feels like he is talking to an Archangel.

    Unlike the usual benefit of Advanced Fame, this Token can affect all characters, not just humans. Furthermore, the resulting Condition can be Social, Mental or Physical, and can be different on each activation. For example: Blind in the sense of being unable to see any coercive or harmful actions the user performs, or Deafness in the sense of being unable to hear the words of anyone except those spoken by the user. Any supernatural attempt to gainsay, suppress or ignore the user's demands, such as by controlling the emotions of those affected by the Token, triggers a Clash Of Wills against the user, with bonus dice equal to the Token's Dot Rating. Mortals use Resolve + 5 for this Clash Of Wills.

    Drawback: The Token harms the user's confidence, self-image, or equivalent. For Changelings, this inflicts 1 Severe Clarity Damage. If this damages one of the three rightmost Clarity Boxes, the Persistent Condition must reflect difficulty with asserting oneself over others or enforcing one's position in society (e.g. Broken), or a false belief that one has no actual identity or personality of one's own (e.g. Delusional). For mortals, they must succeed on a reflexive roll of Resolve + Composure - 5 or suffer similar Persistent Conditions.

    Catch: The user suffers 1 point of Goblin Debt.



    Arachnid Puzzle Box (●●●)

    Mask: A puzzle box akin to a Rubik's Cube.

    Mien: The puzzle box becomes very cold to the touch, turns from plastic to clockwork, and displays a spider-motif. The mechanisms twitch and hiss as if alive.

    Effect: It summons any spider-like hobgoblin whose type the user is aware of, or a random one if the user doesn't know any such creatures, with a Wyrd up to the Token's Dot Rating. This hobgoblin is cooperative and loyal to the user but will not follow commands that are obviously deadly, or go against the hobgoblin's Frailties. If someone else makes a supernatural attempt to seize control of the hobgoblin, the user imposes a Clash Of Wills, with bonus dice equal to the Token's Dot Rating. Mortals use Resolve + 3 for this Clash Of Wills.

    Drawback: The user must stab the Token's mandibles into his wrist, which drains his blood like a spider drinking the liquefied innards of its prey. This inflicts Lethal Damage equal to the Wyrd of the summoned hobgoblin.

    Catch: The hobgoblin summoned is not cooperative nor loyal. It can only be convinced to not betray or abandon the user if defeated in a challenge that involves enigmas, riddles, puzzles or equivalent. This is usually a contested roll of Wits + Empathy or Investigation or Academics VS Manipulation + Academics or Subterfuge or Expression, but the hobgoblin might make this more difficult by using its Contracts and Dread Powers if it expects that losing the challenge will result in its death or worse.



    The Bloodthirsty Engine (••)

    Mask: A non-specific car engine. Very rusty and looks like it'll fall apart if used as is.

    Mien: It roars to life like a metal lion, violently shakes as if about to break the sound barrier, and emits crimson smoke that smells far too much like iron.

    Effect: This Token can adjust the particulars of its shape, size and composition to fit inside various vehicles that make use of combustion for their operation. When inserted into any such vehicle, it can operate normally inside the Hedge without malfunctioning, breaking, transforming or otherwise losing the capacity to perform its intended function. Furthermore, the user is considered to negate any Handling penalties of their vehicle, and any peripheral Equipment such as radio and GPS also work, but do so in strange ways that might require some time and effort to understand. Lastly, the vehicle passively removes Structure Damage to itself equal to the Token's Dot Rating every day, without the need for active Repairs. Any supernatural attempt to control, destroy or alter the vehicle triggers a Clash Of Wills against the user, with bonus dice equal to the Token's Dot Rating. Mortals use Resolve + 2 for this Clash Of Wills.

    Drawback: It uses human blood instead of gasoline. Whenever someone is killed within the immediate vicinity of the Token (including being run over by the vehicle), the blood and gore crawl and slither on their own out of the victim's corpse and squelch and squeeze into the Token. This grants days of normal functioning inside the Hedge equal to [Size + Stamina] of the victim. Without this sacrifice, the Token does not work. Blood from fae entities and other supernaturals may be used instead, but any Failures on Drive rolls become Dramatic Failures. This can have various surreal and terrifying results, depending on the overall type of fae or equivalent from which the blood was taken. At the ST's discretion, this Token can be fueled with Goblin Fruits or Oddments of a sanguine nature, but they should not be trivial to find, buy or cultivate.

    Catch: The Token emits sanguine smoke from the exhaust pipes or equivalent display, which has a tendency to attract blood-drinking Hobgoblins which are individually weak but arrive in swarms. The driver and passengers suffer the Hunted Condition for the rest of the scene.

      These are neat. I have a homebrew Token of my own to share.


      Jar of Stolen Shadows (•••)

      This token appears to be a pickle jar filled with what seems to be a number of black silk ribbons that occasionally flutter about. While activated, the jar allows a changeling to steal a shadow from another creature, which he can either consume to heal himself or store for later use. Capturing a shadow requires a roll of Dexterity + Wits + Wyrd vs. the victim's Resolve + Wyrd. The victim loses a point of Willpower from the shock. The shadow regenerates itself within one scene, or sooner if the victim regains Willpower before the scene is over.

      Once the jar has captured a shadow, the changeling can either consume it or store it. The shadow begins at the same Size rating as the person who cast it, and loses one point of Size per turn unless stored. If stored, the shadow doesn't lose Size. Retrieving a shadow can be done at will as an Instant action while the token is active.

      Whoever holds the shadow can heal damage by consuming it, at a rate of one point of bashing damage every turn, one point of lethal damage every two turns and one point of aggravated damage every three turns. The character heals the worst damage first.

      Alternatively, a changeling that steals the shadow of a fetch can use it as an Icon tied to the original, either reclaiming it (if it's their own Icon) or using it to glean information about another changeling (even one who might still be in Durance in Arcadia). Stealing this Icon from the fetch deals one point of aggravated damage to it, in addition to the lost point of Willpower, and the Fetch cannot regain Willpower from its Shadow until it heals the damage. The Icon can only be taken from a particular fetch once, even if the changeling steals their shadow again once it regenerates.

      If a changeling eats the shadow of his own fetch it helps him regain a sense of himself, reducing the attack pool of his next Clarity breaking point by 3. A Changeling can benefit from this bonus only once, even if he steals his Fetch's shadows multiple times. The bonus lasts until the roll is made.

      The jar can take shadows only from living beings with souls. This means that werewolves, changelings and mages are fair game, but vampires and True Fae are not (fetches have a piece of a soul, so they're fair game; other supernatural creatures are the Storyteller’s call). Stealing a shadow from a supernatural being has a benefit; add the being’s Supernatural Tolerance to the effective Size of the shadow.

      Mien: The scraps of shadows held within whirl and dance. Ghostly faces appear in the shadows, scream silently for an instant, and then vanish.

      Catch: The lid of the jar will open if the opener is willing to trade a bit of their own shadow to it. While active, the opener gains a painful aversion to bright lights, taking one bashing damage if exposed to light brighter than a 40-watt bulb or one lethal damage if exposed to natural daylight (this damage occurs only once during the scene).

      Drawback: Tearing off the shadows of others to eat them is very far from normal, and the opener begins to question their own reality after doing so. The character suffers a 2-dice Clarity attack, or a similar level Breaking Point if not a changeling.

          Wow, I'm surprised there hasn't been a homebrew token thread in like... six years???

          The Everchanging Storybook (••)

          Effect: You may roll an Intelligence + (relevant Mental skill) to find a story that intrigues you. Should the user succeed, he gains cryptic knowledge on what he sought, about any topic he chooses. On an exceptional success, he gains the Informed Condition regarding the subject and learns more details.

          Catch: The user reads the storybook from the hours from 6:00pm to 12:00am.

          Drawback: The user gains the Lethargic Condition.

          Steamer Trunk Key (••)

          Effect: By sticking the key into the air, summon a steamer trunk from mid-air. The contents are the same every time they are summoned, and are stored in a pocket dimension in the meantime. The trunk can be dismissed without spending an additional point of glamour.

          Catch: By sticking the key into an unlocked container of similar size, the contents are replaced with that of the trunk.

          Drawback: The trunk is anxious for attention, and requires an item be taken out of it or put into it every time it is summoned. Summoning the trunk without doing this acts as if the user broke a minor sealing.

          Bat of Blunt Truth (•••)

          Effect: When striking someone with the bat, the user can roll Presence + Intimidation - Composure. For each success, the target must speak the truth for a minute. The user knows when the effect has gone through. When the target attempts to lie or skirt around the truth, they gain the Guilty condition.

          Catch: Use it to interrogate or punish someone who is immobilized or otherwise standing still.

          Drawback: If the target admits the truth, the user suffers a -4 penalty on all further attacks to strike them.

          Messenger Pigeon (•••)

          Effect: Upon activation, the user names the person (or a description of the person) they want to send the letter. The pigeon can accept voice recordings, written letters, or small packages. The pigeon carries the letter to the target with great speed and fate, with any attempts to stop or capture the pigeon taking a -5 penalty. Upon finishing its task, it can either wait for a response, or it can just fly back to the user.

          Catch: Write a message that you mean with sincerity.

          Drawback: The pigeon demands reimbursement, asking for at least one form of payment for every trip.

          The Motivator (•••••)
          Keeper's Token

          Effect: The first enemy struck with The Motivator suffers the Arm Wrack or Leg Wrack Tilt (attacker’s choice) permanently, as the weapon chops the limb clean off. It burns, causing Wound Penalties as if you had wounds in your last three health boxes (-3) until supernaturally healed (Vampire Regen, Werewolf Essence Healing, Changeling Spring Contracts). Mundane healing (waiting, medical care, etc) doesn't work, and each day the enemy takes one bashing.

          Catch: The enemy you're using it against has the Guilty, Frightened, or Shaken/Spooked Condition because of you.

          Drawback: Using this knife adds the Token Rating to Clarity damage pools when used.
