    Alpha Predators: Changelings

    06-28-2016, 05:00 PM
    Challenge: create a Changeling at the heights of Fae power. Start with the basic character creation. You gain 45 Experiences to raise Wyrd to 10. You have 250 Experiences for everything else. You must create an actual character, not a one-dimensional white-room collection of numbers. Describe at least in brief how you might play this character, or her background, fill in some details. Go into as much detail as you like, but give us at least a little context.

    To give credit where credit is due, I'm borrowing Claire Redfields idea (and even some of the wording, altered) for this thread.

    So what kind of Fae can you come up with that would make theTrue Fae trumble.

                                          You've mixed up two optional setting/rules hacks here.

                                          The first one is that if changelings hit wyrd 10 and if they (as in both the player and the character) chooses to do so, they can undergo the transformation into a true fae. But getting to Wyrd 10 is a choice, as is the transformation, so the player and their character has to make at least two choices to get there.

                                          The second one is a variant of the first- Except in this one, wyrd isn't something that changeling buy but risk accumulating like mage's paradox every time they use their powers, and if the changeling pushes themselves to wyrd 10 they automatically start the transformation into a true fae.

                                          But if one is in a game in which you actually pay experience for wyrd, then obviously the game isn't using the second option.
