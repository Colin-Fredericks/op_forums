    Like many things faerie, sometimes the symbolism of an act is closer to the truth of things than the act itself. It's also very much a "Fruit of the Underworld" concept, which you can find in several mythos throughout. Someone ends up in the underworld, throught fortune, design, or the natural way of things. To survive there or be allowed to leave, they must partake of its food, an act which forever binds them to it in some way.

                Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                The metaphysics of Faerie prevent it from providing succor to anything that doesn't have an agreement with it - and the Fae are Faerie. Likewise, agreements aren't always explicitly agreements; to borrow an example, an impromptu fight over a possession is an agreement between two parties - if your opponent abandons the fight, you as the challenger can't actually win.

                Similarly, accepting succor is tacit acceptance of the bonds of hospitality - you can wander in an "abandoned" Fae domain for years scrounging leftovers and never encounter any signatory to the laws that prevent Arcadia from simply withholding sustenance, but the fact that you found food and it didn't not feed you means something was making you an offer and you took it.

                The Shadow and the Lord are Fae archetypes for a reason, too - some of the Strangers will just cut you a deal and turn you loose on their mansion-grounds or toss you in the attic and forget about you for a decade.
                Okay. with having this in mind, how to make a 2ed Kithless and Seemingless work? If Kiths are role you played in Arcadia, even if no Fae made you do his will, what are Kithless? Those that only gone to Faerie to run from it with Contract signed with it and been "free" by Seeming means?

                And the other way around - are those that are Seemingless now, with Seemings being ways you freed yourself from Arcadia, are those that were left behind? You could not now play a Seemingless the Lost, as those are in Faerie for all eternity, working for Gentry?

                To be frank, even if I generally like the Kiths being represented in all Seemings, I have above problems with "Seemings as a way out of Arcadia" interpretation now 2ed is heading. I would more be in favor that Seemings are not "a story of mine return", but rather "a story of mine Durance", like in 1ed they were. Because not every Kithless left Arcadia - with now propositon for 2ed they would be both Kithless and Seemingless, as they were not free.

                Being Darkling is not about "I was ruthless in Fearie and so it free me", it's more with "I like darkness and decivness because mine Durance was that". More a "personality scary" than "way out of Arcadia".

                This would easily solve out Kithless problem - those changelings that are in Arcadia just relive thur Durance still, and Seeming it's not a "story of our escape", but rather "a story of our Durance". The matter that most Fairest use they charms, and Beast their instincts to escape, is just another implication of this, not main tone.
