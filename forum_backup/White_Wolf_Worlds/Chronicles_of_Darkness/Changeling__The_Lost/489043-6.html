                            There are many different legends regarding Fae and monsters than what is portrayed in the past version. I believe the Keeper would turn you into an animal or something, but depending on your Seeming affects how that animal is portrayed.


                            Take for example, a Keeper who wants a pet wolf. A Changeling who becomes a Beast might simply take on the characteristics of a wolf upon their escape. But an Ogre might become something closer to a Dire Wolf or possibly even a Worg (demonic wolves, pretty much). A Fairest who gets turned into a wolf might have a beautiful, shiny coat and be a majestic creature of the forest. Darklings might be predatory hunters or turned into pseudo-lycanthropes. Wizened might be used to perform various tasks such as pulling a sleigh or hunting game. Elementals, I'm not sure of just yet.


                            So to reiterate; yes, your keeper may turn into an animal or a doll or whatever, but your Seeming has some impact on how that transformation manifests itself. It sounds like it offers a lot more freedom and customization than the old "here's a list of options, pick one."



                            Remember that your Kith is the job you were given, and your Seeming is how you took control of your life again. As for the courtesan growing goat horns, maybe they resort to headbutting the guards in order to make an escape? So the courtesan sang, danced and drank, pretty much being a good party planner.
