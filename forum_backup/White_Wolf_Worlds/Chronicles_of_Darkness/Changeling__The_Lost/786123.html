    Seeming for 'Freed' Changelings

    12-14-2015, 03:20 PM
    After reading through the development logs for the seemings, I was wondering what would happen to Changelings that where 'allowed' to leave for whatever reason by their keeper? Do they just not get a seeming and are left only with their kith? If so I hope that the new seeming coming in Dark Era's is built for changelings that where freed since I'm not sure I want to miss out on the blessing and curse mechanic.

      If you're freed, you haven't mutated yourself with a promise to the world you were residing in to be larger than it, in some way. So, freed changelings are Seemingless, and only have their kith.

      No Seeming will ever be awarded to a changeling who doesn't do something to free themselves. Even the idea of the Loyalist involves a changeling freeing themselves by binding themselves to powers larger than themselves. A Seeming is for those who act, who decides to do something to free themselves.

                  You can stretch the definition of Agency a bit to find the closest Seeming. It's practically unheard of for True Fae to simply squander their investment into a captured mortal while they are still useful. Even if you do something so outrageous and insane that even your Keeper starts doubting if it's a good idea to have you around, that could turn you into a Beast. Sure, your Keeper opened the gate and flat out told you to never come back, but they only did so because of something YOU did.

                  That's how I see it, at least.

                  As for the possibility of developing a Seeming after leaving Arcadia? I don't think the rules will support that. Between the 6 standard Seemings and the ones coming up in the Dark Eras, you can certainly find a good enough fit if you put some more thought into it.
