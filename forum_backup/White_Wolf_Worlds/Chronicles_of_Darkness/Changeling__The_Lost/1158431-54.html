                                                #822
                                                12-08-2017, 08:05 AM
                                                Originally posted by LokiRavenSpeak View Post
                                                I find the whole "changeling can escape any bonds by 1 glamour" ability weird. Aside that it makes grapple and grapple relating merit completely useless. It seems weird that are various contracts and kith ability that boost on grapple.
                                                Well, its not as bad as all that. Changelings rarely end up in combat (and thus grappling) with other Lost - usually, we're talking some variation of hobs, dream critters, mirror folk, huntsmen, mortals and the like. And Portaling (which is apparently the term for opening gateways as well as slipping bonds) is unique to changelings. Arguably not apply to Loyalists, as they might not have escaped, and thus haven't "forged a Contract with Faerie itself, so that not even dreams can hold them prisoner." In either case, my point remains - the overwhelming majority of opponents the PCs will face won't have innate, anti-grapple abilities.

                                                So, not really useless. And, in the case of the three contracts (might of the terrible brute, growth of ivy, beast clause of boon of the scuttling spider) and the one kith (gristlegrinder), there's still Clash of Wills in the case of using it against other changelings. And, as the ST, you can throw enough dice on the NPC's clash pool to make it stick if you really want/need to. Or just have them use an iron chain as an assist - that will put an end to portaling real quick. You're probably going to need iron in the first place - all darklings turn intangible as part of their seeming, and there are numerous abilities that have a similar effect. Huntsmen already have powers that specifically act like iron for purposes of capturing changelings, so that's not an issue there.

                                                You're mostly going to see this come into play when changelings deal with mortals that don't know better. Get in trouble with the police or kidnapped? Slip away. That's going to come with story complications of its own, but hardly a powerful ability - escaping mortal captivity is not exactly rocket science for supernaturals. Its a neat, thematic ability that really doesn't have that big of an effect on major threats, but potentially interesting story hooks.
