    2e Seeming Predictions

    05-02-2015, 10:26 AM
    So this thread probably won't be relevant for terribly long, but it popped up in the Darkling thread and I thought it would be best to have a topic specifically for this. Now that we have two examples of 2e Seemings, we can start to establish potential patterns and use them to try to predict what direction the other four might go. So what do you think? What might the pre-durance backgrounds for the remaining Seemings look like? How did they change themselves to escape? What will their Breaking Points be? How will they regain Clarity?

      So I'd like to share my thoughts on what the Fairest might look like. So far with Beasts and Darklings, we've gotten quite a but of background on what characters of each Seeming were like before their Durance. Some people have expressed concerns that these backgrounds make the concepts too specific, but personally I disagree. I don't see it as all that different from 1e specifying why Changelings of each Seeming were usually taken, and I see the background filling a very similar purpose in 2e - to establish what kind of person is likely to end up as this Seeming. In my reading, the backgrounds are also tied in strongly with the abuse metaphor, showing how these characters were vulnerable, what it was about them that the Gentry were able to take advantage of, and what about themselves they had to change in order to escape that cycle of abuse.

      The pattern so far seems to be that the vulnerability is very much the opposite of the creature they had to become. So Beasts were formerly restricted, making them easy prey for their abusers to entrap, and the only way they could escape that was by becoming wild and untamable. The Wyrd took that figurative wildness and turning them into literal beasts. Darklings were formerly loyal, trusting, making them easy prey for their abusers to neglect or betray, and the only way they could escape that was by making themselves colder, darker, more pragmatic. The Wyrd took that figurative darkness and turned them into literal creatures of the dark. So, in that light, how can we apply this pattern to other Seemings?

      This is what lead to my prediction on the Fairest. The Fairest have always been about beauty - in 1e it was often the reason they were taken, but in the 2e paradigm where Seeming is a matter of personal agency, I would assume they had to make themselves beautiful to escape. Again, this was probably not literal, but became so due to the Wyrd. Since the backgrounds so far have been fairlt opposite of what the character became, my prediction is that the Fairest background will be people who had issues of low self-esteem and negative self-image. This ties in very well with the abuse metaphor, as people with low self-esteem are some of the most commonly victimized by abusers. This poor self-image could be what made the character vulnerable to being ovjectified in Arcadia, and their means of escape would be through developing self-worth. They escape by making themselves beautiful to themselves, and the Wyrd in turn makes them beautiful to everyone else.

      Thoughts? Other predictions?

      Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.

          Elementals: Abandon the Self and embrace the World - Clarity through rejection of of the Self - People who felt bound by themselves and let go
          The Zen counterpart to Beasts. Beasts break free, Elementals let go.

          Ogres: Abandon discipline - Clarity through consumption/indulgence - Ascetics who had to indulge themselves

          Wizened: You were never strong enough, fix that - Clarity through ???Craft??? - Strong people who had to accept their imperfections??

          I have a pretty solid idea of where I think/hope they're going to go with Elementals, but the others are a bit more murky. As far as Fairest go, I'm expecting something involving either co-dependency or narcissism. From what we've seen so far it seems like Seemings will be able to map pretty well with emotional issues that can arise from PTSD. Beasts act out, Darklings can't trust, Elementals are emotionally severed, Ogres "consume" which can translate to substance abuse or binging, Wizened look like they'll have a bit of a thing about modifying themselves or things around them so basically people who bury themselves in their work or something along those lines.
