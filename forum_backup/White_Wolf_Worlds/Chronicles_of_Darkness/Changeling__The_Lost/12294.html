    Say a Freehold is the dominant Supernatural force in a Citym

    11-16-2013, 05:21 PM
    So say a Freehold is the dominant Supernatural Force in a city, this could be because its a small town an they are the only Supernatural Entity in large numbers there, or because the others have gotten eradicated or chased away in their own conflicts, or simply because Changelings outnumber the other factions. What are some good ideas for incorporating Non-changelings into a Freehold? In my games I've had large freeholds that have counted enchanted Humans, Fetches, Hobgoblins, Ghost slaves, and a small number of mages among its members. Its a good way to bolster Freehold numbers, particularly when humans/hobgoblins are allowed membership into a freehold you can boost its numbers from a few dozen with only Changelings to a few hundred. This can give a stronger sense of an actual community, particularly if their is a Freehold shared Hallow. And Titles like King carries more of a punch if your a king over hundreds of individuals rather then a couple dozen. One of the best parts is since lost Numbers don't correlate exactly to human population size you could have such a freehold in a smaller city or even a town or island. And Festivals can actually feel like festivals without needing to doop a bunch of drug addled mortals or slum it with only hobgoblins.

    But what are some ideas for how to incorporate others into a Freehold? And what are some interesting rules or customs for such a Freehold? And variations on how it would work with the various Court systems. Personally I think Changeling is uniquely suited to have the potential for this sort of setting, mayhaps because their Culture revolves around tolerance for waywards/vagabonds/homeless/mad, and combined with the sanctity and prevalence of Reliable Pact Magic, and the security/privacy of Hallows.

    So humor me here.
    Though I would be interested if you have something to say why a particular group would be especially dis inclined to join such a community.
    And feel free to suggest the minor Templates, or Fanmades as I am ignorant of a lot of them.


    Oh and I think I'll fold this into here, but another idea I was going to start a thread on was this. What do you think about a group that has method for joining for all/most the Supernaturals through the Z/subsplat? So something that would say count as an Entitlement to Changelings, but has a branch that counts a Lodge for Werewolves, a Legacy for Mages, a Bloodline for Draculers, an Athanor for Prometheans. I guess for Hunters it would count as joining a Conspiracy, or maybe a compact, and for Geists it would be joining the Krewe of the Geists involved. And Mummies I guess would be part of the Cult attached to it, and I have no idea for the Demons I'm still learning about them.

    Once again humor me. :P


    EDIT:

    Okay let me list the major assumptions of this thread. If you read anything read this.

    The 1st point, this Freehold would be the major power in the area, so discussions of other supernaturals not joining and going to war is relatively moot as they would be in small numbers and likely just driven off or killed. Its about an Area where Changeling Culture is dominant and virtually the only game in town.

    The 2nd point is the area doesn’t necessarily have to be a major city, it can even be a small town.

    The 3rd point is you have to make a Pledge of Loyalty to the Freehold to join, the particulars depends on what you want out of the game.

    The 4th point, is everything not under the control of the Freehold/the end of its territory would be a border march brim type area. You could run more of a squatters type population here, like the remaining supernaturals driven out of the premium areas of the city dwell here awaiting a pogrom, or just enjoying the riches that come with controlling an area with more freedom and less security.

    The 5th point is the Freehold would consist mainly of the City/Town itself along with prime areas it considers territory (like a state park or farm), and a communal Hallow with the size of five, hell it could be even larger like multiple hallows or a Rank 5 Hallow for each Court. The nearby Hedge would not be advisable to enter for non Changelings, but there would be various portals into the Hallow guarded by the freehold through the city.

      Just my two cents here.

      1. Changelings are never going to accept other Supernaturals. The whole point of being a Changeling is that you are a paranoid, fearful wreck. Vampires aren't going to want to have any part of a group that literally harnesses the power of the Summer Sun. Mages would only want to study them, (case in point, Acanthus.) I don't really know nWoD Werewolves, but keep in mind that they shapeshift, and are not fully human, in that they are part spirit. Hunters...well maybe, depending on what kind. Prometheans, remember have the Disquiet/Wasteland thing going on, and add to that the fact that they literally feel more comfortable around fetches.

      2. I would argue what you said about Changeling Culture. "their Culture revolves around tolerance for waywards/vagabonds/homeless/mad." I really don't think this is correct at all. Anything not Human could be a spy for the Gentry. Anything supernatural could be a True Fae in exile. Freehold culture is close knit, sure, but also very exclusive. You have a layer of oaths, pledges, and whatnot enforcing peace. Changeling culture isn't tolerance, its xenophobia at its finest. Heck, Freeholds in different cities might not even communicate.

      3. Now, since I'm sure you want a better answer, I'm going to target how I would bring in other Supernatural splats. I think you are most likely to see Mages, Vampires, and Created.

      3a. Mages are students, first and foremost. Unless you get a really, really determined Acanthus, they aren't going to try to brave the Hedge after the first try. (Remember, Mages can't even walk on Trods. The Hedge eats their souls. No soul=No magic.) I would approach it on a Motley to Cabal basis. You aren't going to have totally open peace between the Mages and the Changelings, but you might have Changelings on a decent, cooperative basis with one or two Cabals.

      3b. Vampires have connections. So do Changelings. A large Freehold is going to run into some agent of the Prince eventually. It really depends on how they meet. With a little luck, a business relationship is most likely. Also keep in mind that Changeling blood is addictive, (due to the Glamour) so that might be an issue. I do like the idea of a Beast finding a Gangrel and trying to befriend them. That has potential for Drama.

      3c. Created are never going to be seen in numbers in a Freehold. Once Wasteland starts happening, they are going to be driven out or killed. What you might see, though, is a Changeling sponsored area for Created, staffed by Fetches. I'd call it a joint Spring/Winter operation, and have it focused on finding these "half men" and fixing them. (Side note, that would be a really cool Promethean Chronicle. Have them finish the Pilgrimage under the tutelage of someone who can never be human again. Very tragic.)

      4. No. I don't think you could have an all encompassing Z splat, unless you depowered it immensely. Unless you want to go the route of each founder being a different Supernatural, you won't have the powers available. If you did it that way, I would have it focused on cross-species diplomacy. Give each member of the Embassy of Virtue bonuses to speaking to other Splats.

      Again, just my two cents!

              As it's written, from the start, most supernaturals don't know about other supernaturals except for rumors and what they've seen in movies. Even a Mage who awakens doesn't automatically know there's vampires and werewolves.

              Changelings may be about tolerance (not sure if they are), but they're certainly about wariness. Trust doesn't come easy. A human can be a true fae in disguise, and while a few changelings may openly embrace vampires for some reason, an equal amount would worry that they are minions of the Keepers. Or at least a threat to their hidden lives. Vampires need food, after all, people food, unless you want them doped up on changelings all the time.

              As for others being disinclined, simply put everyone has their own agenda. Werewolves deal with a world that changelings won't understand. Vampires are princes of the night, and while a neonate may certainly bow and pledge before someone who calls themselves a king, older vampires wouldn't be likely to. Mages explore and dissect the world around them. Do you think changelings who've spent years being a slave to some alien being would appreciate being told that it was just some wild spirit or part of the fallen world?
              Even ignoring their own agendas, why would they put themselves beneath something else, something alien to them? It's one thing for a werewolf to accept another as alpha, it's another to accept another being entirely that can never appreciate the werewolf's lot in life and duty as Uratha, let alone a different one each season. And werewolves aren't even the most power hungry of splats. Would changelings agree to serve under a mage? An entire freehold of them? Cause if they didn't, he could just make them. Part of the whole reason the highest title vampires can hold is prince is because it would be so arrogant (and dangerous) for one be labelled king.

              All that said, if you want to run it there's nothing stopping you, I think you'd either have to ignore a lot of/all of the background of the other supernaturals and have a freehold that somehow manages to all be on the same page regarding what they want, but nothing's impossible.

              Also there's an entitlement, Lord Sages of the Unknown Reaches, they are the changelings that look into and make deals with other splats. I believe they can get some talens or some magic items from other splats. So they could be part of your tentpole.

                                          I have a problem with the quotes here so pardon me not using them.

                                          Tolerance: One of the sample freeholds (Hacienda Suenos) takes in new changelings as slaves for five years. For five years after escaping the Hedge, you are forced to harvest and prepare cocaine for the others, who enjoy the profits of their cocaine empire. As written, the game isn't about tolerance.

                                          Join or die: Well this is kind of interesting in that a few of the splats would prefer to be alone, rather than among others. Changelings will never understand things the way Mages do, and now that a Mage is alone he can go and rifle through all their old master's research. Vampires aren't normally prey for some overarching threat, a lone vampire would be more likely to move to a vampire society or just make new vampires, wholly addicted to his power. Of course they could just join and bring the entire freehold under the Vinculum as well.

                                          Personally I don't really see fetches as part of a freehold, either, too many changelings would be way too eager to kill them for a wide variety of reasons. There's an entire entitlement based around killing fetches.

                                          It's clear you really want this scenario, however. So as I said you're going to have to ignore stuff like that. So you don't really need an explanation at all beyond 'everyone who doesn't join dies,' which is good enough, though you may have to flesh out that threat at some point.

                                          So your problem becomes how do I make everyone want to do it. In any group there's going to be people who don't like each other (for instance, some changelings might dislike the vampires who use disciplines and their Vitae to mentally enslave others). You had an idea for bloodlines and legacies. A sort of universal z-splat would be the way to do it. I'd suggest locking it to the area. The Silent Lodge, Silent Bloodline, the Legacy of Silent Hill, etc. It's not really an ancient conspiracy that's lasted across the world for centuries, it's just localized in this place. So you should probably spice up the place, think of some sort of historical precedent that would cause everyone to require the shelter of the freehold and work your way around that.

                                          You'll also have to figure out what the other splats do for the freehold. Even in the nicest of freeholds you have to participate, you have to give them something in exchange for everything they're giving you.

                                                          I think a lot of good things have been said and I'll try and put down what I think and hopefully not repeat too much of what has already said...

                                                          Vampires: As a rule they don't like other splats (unless they can exploit them with impunity) because they are big reminder they are not necessarily on the top of the metaphysical food chain. That being said they're not dumb. If a Prince is confronted with a Freehold that has a significant numbers and said freehold has/is cementing its power/ties in the mortal side of things, they are more likely to try and come to some sort of arrangement. Any conflict of any size could easily breach the Marsquarade.

                                                          Invictus: If they have to they will go for polite, civil and at arms-length. They will make sure everyone else knows the Freeholders are off limits and what establishment they are not allowed to go within 500 feet. And they would make sure every other vamp in town knows anyone trying to make a deal with the Ling's without the Invictus present would be executed.
                                                          Ordo Dracul: The Ordo would be the most likely to actively seek out and establish a relationship. A very tit-for-tat one based on occult knowledge, but some sort of agreement none the less.
                                                          Crone: I think the Crone would have the same interest as the Ordo.
                                                          LS: They would likely have a problem with the Changelings and would tell their flocks to stay away for any number of reasons. Some of their more militant groups might even try killing changelings for being demons or tempters and what not.
                                                          Carthians: Somewhere between the Invictus and Ordo I think? I don't play Carthian all that much so I won't speculate.

                                                          Where do they fit in, in this Freehold? Not a heck of a lot. No one likes being on the menu so Lings wont' want them around all that much. Spies, informants? They definately fill the roll of 'allies' and 'contacts'. Daevas might try partying it up with Springers and Autumn might have some sort of deal with the Ordo but generally the two would stay apart.


                                                          Mages: As many have said the Mages will be very very very..very very interested in Changelings. I can see individual cabals forming agreements with a big Freehold to avoid potential conflicts. Or the Seasonal Heads would meet with the Hierach and say "Here is a list where we don't want to see your people, give us a similar list and we'll stay out of each other's hair." What it comes down to is numbers. If the mages are outnumbered they will eventually toe whatever line the Lings give them. Yes mages are powerful. But if the mages are caught off guard or its 12 on 50, the mages will loose. They'll take a bunch of Ling with them but they will loose eventually.

                                                          Where do mages fit in this freehold? Similar to vampires but because of the sheer power of individual mages Lings would be skittish. If they aren't too pushy or rude you could easily have a situation where tokens and enchanted items could be commissioned back and forth. The more artist and scholarly minded mages and lings could get a long as long as boundaries are not crossed.

                                                          Fetches: All I can say is what everything changeling I've played would say. Ehem. "KIll it. Kill it fire. Kill it with bullets. Kill it trained attack dolphines. Kill it with green eggs and ham."

If he's on a crusade to fight 'the darkness' it probably won't work out. If they are part of the Thule or the Union, an amicable arrangement could be reached. Conspiracies...not so much. What happens if TFV finds out there's a Freehold that is 60+ and is actively trying to assert it's will over all the monsters and hunters in the city? What's to stop TFV from wondering if these Lings aren't trying to do that in city hall or sending people they've influenced to the state capital or DC? Cheiron might decide to send a whole mess of retrieval teams in while the picking is good.
