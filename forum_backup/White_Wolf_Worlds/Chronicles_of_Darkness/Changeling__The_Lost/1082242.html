    Hedge Ghosts

    04-22-2017, 10:04 AM
    I just thought that a focused thread on this topic would be interesting and good for asking questions.

    Like what is the unifying theme of Hedge Ghosts given that they can have very different origins?

    Like they can be made from bits of soul called icons or intense emotions or people/goblins dying, and who knows what else?

    Can dreams produce Hedge Ghosts?

    Can Hedge Ghosts leave the Hedge to visit Arcadia, Dreams, or Ironside, and if they do, do they bleed glamour?

    How do they harvest Glamour, like Changeling via emotions, dreams, and hedge bounty? Weirder ways?

    Can they use the Claimed Manifestation? do Hedge Claimed count as Goblins?

    Do Changelings gain any ablity to create/summon/control Hedge Ghosts on purpose?

    Do Hedge Ghosts have any relation to Underworld Ghosts or Goetics?

    Can Hedge Ghosts steal Glamour from other Fae like underworld Ghosts can steal essence from other underworld ghosts and spirits can steal from other spirits?

    Will their be a retainer feat for Changeling Characters who want a Hedge Ghost pet?

              Speculation can be fun.

              I'll start. There will be a drain numena that drains either willpower or glamour, instead willpower or essence like regular one.

              Hedge Ghosts will bleed glamour outside the hedge.

              Spirits and normal ghosts will slowly transform into Hedge Ghost if stuck in the Hedge too long.

              Hedge Ghosts will be able to harvest glamour from emotions like a Changeling.

              Hedge Ghosts will not have influences or the Claim Manifestation, but might have a Goblin Manifestation or Numina.

              Universal Seeker numena.

              Cause Bedlam Numina.

              A pledge Numina.

              Rank instead of Wyrd.

              Will get access to fetter and regular possession numena.

              Some kind of Dream Numina.

              Will replace Incubi and Succubi Oniero as dream creatures.

              Will be summonable/creatable via a contract.

              They won't be able to go above Rank 5, or if they can they Rank 6+ will be special some how, something even the Gentery don't like or at least respect.
