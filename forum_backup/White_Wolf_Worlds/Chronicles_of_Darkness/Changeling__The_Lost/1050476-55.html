                                                    Originally posted by TheStray7 View Post
                                                    So I'm unclear about how magical abilities interact with Attributes in 2e. Might of the Terrible Brute specifically says it can let you get more Strength than your normal Wyrd limit. Does that mean that Red Revenge's +3 to all Physical Attributes only lifts you to the normal limit?
                                                    Might of the Terrible Brute gives you access to a move that increases your Strength by a variable amount, which is limited in terms of net bonus because a Common Contract allowing you to arm-wrestle your way to Strength 20 is a bit excessive. This in no way suggests that Red Revenge, a Royal Contract with a fixed bonus that inflicts a negative Condition on its user, can't break the cap.

                                                    Generally speaking, if a power boosts a character's actual Trait ratings it will specify if it doesn't​ adhere to the caps.

                                                    Originally posted by DoktorNiemand View Post
                                                    I'm really sorry if this has been asked before, but...
                                                    Are Goblin Vows and Talecrafting getting updated to 2nd edition?
                                                    There has been no indication that this is the case and the basis for Talecrafting working as it did is no longer the assumed framework of Changeling in 2e. The breadth of Goblin Contracts and the greater ease with which a character can strike a Bargain fills some of the niche of Goblin Vows, but I also wouldn't put money on the prospect of a game element so firmly based in 1e's model of pledgecraft returning without heavily reimagining the execution.
