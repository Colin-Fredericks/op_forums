                                                            Two Freeholds, relatively close together, in a large metro area. One Freehold has strict, oath-enforced rules about Tokens of Reprieve. The second Freehold has no such rules, and offers sanctuary to Changelings fleeing the first Freehold for forswearing the pledges forbidding taking or posessing tokens. How would politics and society differ between the Freeholds as a result of these differences? Would the Huntsman treat the Freeholds differently? Play them against each other?                                                             This is the exact premise for how I'll be running the Portland Privateer Freehold of Shanghai Stumptown which will be grossly oppressing and victimizing the surrounding Lost populations for Tokens of Reprieve, while building a retinue of Fetch slaves which they get from their Privateering of mortals (the source of the inflated lost population in the area). This of course to go with the Human Trafficking motif I have in my Chronicle.

                                                            The difference in how the Freehold is run compared to its neighbors is radically different and the Huntsmen would have trouble passing through their Hedge without hearing how cooperative Portland Lost are for claiming bounties on outsiders, and how "wealthy" they are with Tokens of Reprieve. Does that cause some impetuous Huntsmen seek a quick score to get a moment of rest from existing? Possible but unlikely, it'd ruin their chances to get eager help for the more permanent solution of catching their quarry and taking it back to Arcadia, especially if the Huntsmen can short circuit the Token of Reprieve their target may have by having their target captured by Lost who steal all the possessions of their victim, so they have no token of Reprieve to present the Huntsmen.
