    [2ED] Seemings and fairytales

    12-25-2015, 02:07 AM
    I'm watching two version of "Beauty and the Beast" - Disney one from 1991 and French one ( "for adults" ) from 2014 - preparing to run Beast: The Primodrial. Of course, I got some Changeling thoughts and questions and as 2ED Seemings idea always not played completely for me, maybe this topic will clear a bit now. Discussion idea is simple - You take fairytale and make into 2ED Changeling story format. Then we talk implications of this story to the game setting.

    Here is mine...

    Beauty and the Beast

    The prince of a small kingdom in France is known for its narcissism and unworthy behavior. He is cursed in into titular Beast and must be loved for someone to break the spell. After years, young women called Belle is taken hostage by him ...

    Changeling interpretation: The Prince ( is also his Kith ) is becoming of Beast Seeming when he break the rules of the Gentry's Courts and works of his Keeper. As punishment, he will be Beast until someone love him - as the Keeper do not think anyone can love Beasts ( they are wild animals ), his Sorcerers Lady thinks it will be forever. ( In Disney version also whole court of the Princes Palace are changed in to Wizened item-servants ). But, after years of waiting, then comes Belle and she break the spell.

    Implications: So does it mean Seeming can be put on someone against his will? Can it be lift of the changeling by some factors ( like true love of another )? Or that "Beast" is in fact Fairest of the Lionheart Kith in truth? And he's change in to human is only going to maximum of Clarity and his own forgetting he is fae and seeing only his own Mask?
