As a loyal servant of the Mothership, she volunteered to hunt the escapee down, but once she made it back to the world, she realized how wrong she had been, and came back to the side of the angels. Which of course meant I was treated to another of Annika’s fabulous dance lessons. I can’t think of anything I’d rather do than watch one of my best friends go to someone I hate instead of me when she wants to learn to dance. Sarcasm is a wonderful thing, isn’t it?

Might not have been a good idea to drink so much then, huh. Oh well, not much I can do about it now. It’s really late, or early depending on your perspective. We didn’t get back until almost 4:00am, then we took the time to put everything away and wash up. Then I spent a good bit of time writing in this stupid notebook. I need sleep…

                                                        **** **** ****
                                                        Okay…it’s a new day. I’m tired and wired all at once. I got up a bit before the others and made coffee. I drank a good bit of that first pot, so I made some more for the others. Blood loss has an interesting effect with caffeine too. Definitely got to remember that. Anyhoo…I caffeinated the others while Gawain did his usual wonderful job making breakfast. We all tucked in and ate. Then we discussed what kind of boon we wanted to ask for as recompense for taking care of the briar wolf problem in the hedge. I think we all agreed that strengthening our official identities would be the best thing. We all have basic forged identities, but they won’t hold up to any sort of official scrutiny. So, we decided it would be best to remedy that. So, we all piled in the van and headed over to collect our boon. It was straightforward enough. The guy we talked to, Billy, didn’t seem keen on doing that many ID’s, but we turned Lorelei loose on him and it was just a matter of time before the man caved. It’s just not possible to stand up to that woman when she turns on the charm. Hot. Anyhoo…that got worked out quickly enough.

                                                        Then we noticed Joan and decided that we should talk to her about our concerns with Ginny and Desiree. Yeah, that was an interesting “conversation.” It was mostly her going on and on about how she was going to hunt down the pair of them and end the threat. Then this guy Percival kept making pointed comments about her and to her and it really didn’t make things better. I was tempted to deck him just to get him to shut up. I think we managed to talk her out of killing the two outright and instead settled on us keeping an eye on Ginny, since she works for us, and Joan (or someone in the Summer court) watching Desiree. It was a rather stressful time all around. I think we managed to avoid any outright bloodshed for the moment. We’ll see how long that lasts. Personally I think Joan will keep to her word. She seems the sort. I’m not so sure of the other Summer Courtiers, but we’ll see how it goes. Suspicious behavior does not necessarily mean that someone is a Loyalist.

                                                        Hell, a good bit of my behavior has probably been considered to be suspicious and I am most definitely not a Loyalist. I was, but not anymore. Does that distinction mean as much as I hope it does? I hope so. I have no intention of serving The Lady or any of her compatriots. I have in point most definitely acted against them since I’ve arrived here. I guess the only thing I can do is what I have been doing, and that is watching out for my friends and keeping them safe. Most of them are not naïve by any stretch of the imagination, but I don’t think they really understand the other side. That’s not necessarily a bad thing. Some of them think that they are monsters in some regard. I can understand why they, she, thinks that. From a certain point of view that might be true, but it really isn’t. I have seen what it means to be a monster. I have been that monster. Willingly. The only thing I can do now is try to make up for that. I’m not sure if I can, but I have to try. I have to try to make right the sins that I committed. Fuck. I don’t know if I can do that. I don’t know if it’s possible for me to even that out, or even come close to wiping out all the shit that I did. The thing is, I have to try. I know what it is to be the monster. I know what it is to be indifferent. I have to be something else. I have to try to make things better. Goddess let me do some good with this second chance that I have been given.

                                                        The hunt for Briarwolves went fairly well. Mostly. Gawain, Lorelei and Calamity got beat up a bit, but Lorelei heals well and took care of it. Mostly. We ran into Desiree and Ginny Green in the Hedge though, being chased by some sort of strange Mannequins. The Mannequins were personally more dangerous that the Briarwolves, if much much more disorganized. The Director apparently sent them out. They hurt Desiree, but were not trying to kill her. Apparently. Jacob drew a lot of attention in the fight, of course, but seems okay.

                                                        After killing all the enemies, we spoke to the two. People were keen to know why they were in the Hedge. Doubtlessly nefarious reasons, apparently unlike we, who were simply there to murder Briarwolves. If Calamity kills something, it is always murder. She is just the sort. It’s not a matter of justification; it is a matter of resonance. Clearly. Desiree did not say why she was lingering, but could have stopped to rest on the path. Ginny said she was there to meet someone, eventually Flora, but De La Rocha says (and Julia later said) that makes no sense. She was hiding something, which seemed to annoy Calamity. Truthfully, I think Calamity wanted to cut them both down. Is the Autumn Court not the Court of Secrets?

Calamity and the boys went to skin Briarwolves for Hedge-Spinning, and De La Rocha, Lorelei and I returned home. I did some playful flirting with them, and they were intrigued. Obviously. Julia joined us when we returned. De La Rocha and Lorelei decided to not have their entire view of sex turned on its head by me that night. Sadly. Julia was nervous but also interested. Naturally. I think she likes Lorelei. Which is fair, I do as well. De La Rocha clearly is intrigued by me though…our eyes met a couple times. I smirked at her of course. They do not act on their desires, instead choosing to live life less fully. I do not understand that. I mean, I do, I was Catholic, but they should know better by now. Clearly. The Gentry could come at any time, best spend what time we can in a hidden, dark place coiled around someone pleasing, mingling your sweat with theirs. Yes.

I had just seen it, but it was good to watch again. Happily. I don’t think it was De La Rocha’s sort of movie, but she liked it okay. Julia actually had seen it before. Strangely.

It makes sense. Once there, while a Beast named Old Billy made the arrangements, the others talked to Joan and an odd Ogre named Percival. For some reason Gawain decided to tell Joan all about Ginny and Desiree, and seemed surprised when Joan wanted to reap them like bloody wheat. It’s what she does. Surely he has been around Calamity enough by now to know some people are just like that. Americans especially. Before they could all decide to drag Ginny screaming into some underground room I spoke up and suggested maybe we should observe her a bit, since walking through the hedge, fighting the minions of the Gentry, and not wanting to say her precise business are not crimes. I did not say it that way or it would have been taken badly. I don’t really know Desiree, so did not argue with the homicidal maniac about her.
