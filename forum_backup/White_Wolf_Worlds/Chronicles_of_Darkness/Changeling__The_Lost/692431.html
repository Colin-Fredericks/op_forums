    Tarot Card Freehold

    10-07-2015, 10:18 AM
    So I have this idea for a freehold, its courts centered around the 4 Suits (Pentacle, Cup, Sword, Wand), and whose bulwark is either a yearly reading of the cards, which the freehold must either make a reality by next year, or endure therein. Question is: how would governance work? Would there be four kings/queens ruling simultaneously, or only 1? Also, would certain members of the freehold become representatives of particular (major) arcana, or gain boons symbolic of said-cards?

                                                  ( Yes, survive like in Hunger Games - in this take Wyrd is actively hunting Fools to limit the to one only at the end and Gentry is obvious to mark them as their victims. )

                                                  I think we have strong idea for general Bulwark - now, could someone write Arcana Courts in 2ED format?

                                                  I can add from myself that in White Wolf forums times there were fan created Tarot for Willpower rules that I use in mine Mage games to this day - only that I have mine Polish translation, not original document. There were nice, open interpretations for all "Colors" so maybe look in there could be. I will do quick translate for this topic on general sum up from mine files. ( I will also ask openly on general forum for original rules, maybe someone have them in English ).

                                                  Wands/ Sticks/ Clubs/ Fire / Creativity
                                                  Psychic Sphere — Wands represent idea, they are original seeds or ideas and most important - elements of development. When we see Wands in play, they point to the ideas, ambitions and growth.
                                                  Sphere: Mental Skills

                                                  Cups/ Chalices/ Hearts/ Water/ Passion
                                                  Heart Sphere — Cups represent strength and power to achieve by will alone. When we see Cups in play, they point on needs and feelings, and to overcoming strives in obtaining them.
                                                  Sphere: Resolve, Stamina, Composure or Defense

                                                  Swords/ Sabers/ Air/ Actions
                                                  Action Sphere — Swords represent focused will to make physical manifestation. Often Swords means combat; difficulties in making physical ideas in to reality. When we see Swords in play, they point to action and conflict before final resolution.
                                                  Sphere: Physical Skills

                                                  Pentacles/ Coins/ Diamonds/ Earth/ Charm
                                                  Social Sphere – Pentacles represent our material desires and social standing. Coins are manifestation of material plane, body, fruits of our work and gifts, we desire. When we see Pentacles in play, they point not only on wealth and power, but also on social manipulation and actual results.
                                                  Sphere: Social Skills

                                                  So who made those in to proper 2ED Courts?
