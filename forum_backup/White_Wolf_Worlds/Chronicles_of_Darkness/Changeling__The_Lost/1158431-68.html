            Hi Guys, how are you doing?
            After reading all the manuscripts and running a play test chronicle, I'd like to ask Rose Bailey something:

            We have Comatose condition as the only one which is applicable once a character suffers clarity damage on his last box, and I think it works very well when you wish to create some hooks for characters rescuing an ally by entering her dreams, but I think that maybe it's a little limited for being the only applicable condition, because it gives you the understanding that a changeling can never be an evil person, like a serial killer, a torturer, a psychopath, e.g., without the "help" of a contract protecting her clarity.

            This is not the main theme of my chronicles, but the comatose rule applies to anyone in it, including my NPCs. This gives me the impression that Changeling has these defined roles, where the changeling are always the good guys and the huntsmen/gentry are the bad guys (they are). It strikes me as some sort of limitation for conflicts between changelings, humans, and other supernatural beings, where the changeling risks comatose every time he takes drastic measures against an enemy/threat/rival. It's kind of off the Chronicles of Darkness main scenario, IMO.

            So, I would like to know if we'll have other conditions applicable when the changeling fills his last clarity box. Something like, a megalomaniac state in which the changeling starts behaving as he believed he is a true fae, or a state in which he starts denying the existence of magic and believes it's all his imagination? Or will Comatose be the only condition applicable?

            Sorry for the long post, and thanks in advance.

                Originally posted by bardobobs View Post
                We have Comatose condition as the only one which is applicable once a character suffers clarity damage on his last box, and I think it works very well when you wish to create some hooks for characters rescuing an ally by entering her dreams, but I think that maybe it's a little limited for being the only applicable condition, because it gives you the understanding that a changeling can never be an evil person, like a serial killer, a torturer, a psychopath, e.g., without the "help" of a contract protecting her clarity.
                It gives you the understanding that those characters will almost never be emotionally transparent people and/or lack a particularly strong attachment to or frequent associate of their Touchstones, which is why Comatose coming out of nowhere is already uncommon.

                Even in situations where a Clarity attack rolls a particularly high dicepool, the worst outcome is that you may need to spend multiple scenes undoing the damage instead of only one. It's mathematically unlikely for a character to get a full track of mild damage without seriously neglect unless things have gone incredibly wrong already, much less severe damage.

                            ...it's a slow roll out because Backerkit does what they call a "smoke test."
