    Originally posted by Monalfie View Post
    1. Are Changelings always originally humans or can they originate from animals or objects through some weirdness that become self aware?
    Answer: Yes, changelings are always humans. Animals and objects can be come "self-aware" through exposure to the Wyrd, but they become either Hobgoblins (for animals) or Tokens (objects).

    Originally posted by Monalfie View Post
    2. Just in general, do/would a fae take an animal into the Hedge and would it likely affect it in a manner?
    Answer: It depends on the fairy, I guess. Hobgoblins, True Fae and Changelings often leave objects in the Hedge for an extended period of time so they can become tokens. Plenty of hobgoblins are descended from animals that have found themselves in the Hedge for some reason or another.
