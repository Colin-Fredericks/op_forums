                                Insincere Marguerite is not the only one to have spectres in her past. Now Lorelei and Jacob face shadows of the past, even as they celebrate their successes over the week. How do the different fae react when these shadows fall across theirs?

                                Insincere Marguerite is a short story taking place on the evening during the session’s timeframe.

                                Session LXX

                                Lorelei’s Diary

                                Oh my God I can’t even think right now I haven’t slept and the Spring contracts aren’t really helping that much. I just can’t seem to focus my mind on anything. A lot has happened over the past day and some of it was even good so I suppose I’m going to want to remember it and setting it all down here might help to distract me and keep me from completely losing my shit, so here goes.

                                Right, so a little while after I finished writing my previous entry the Night Queen came out and thanked us all for speaking with her about Marguerite. She wanted to confer with Lizette for a while, and told us that the two of them would definitely stay to see the show at the Dream House that night before heading back to Paris. Both of them seemed relatively upbeat about the whole thing, so I guess that’s a good sign.

                                When we got back to the Dream House, Calamity and I sort of fell into rehearsal mode and attempted to hammer out the last few niggling imperfections in the “Bloody Creature Poster Girl” number. We made sure that Marguerite had the lighting cues down, and that Courtney and Teresa were pitch-perfect as the backup singers. When I was sure that we had it as close to perfection as it was likely to get, we put the rehearsals to bed and split up to prepare in our own ways.

                                The result was spectacular. It was easily one of the better performances of my career, and I don’t think I’ve ever seen Calamity dance like that. Gawain and I both gave her a little pep talk before the show. I know I infused some glamour into my words to give her a bit more self-confidence, and I think it helped a little… but she would have killed it even without the magical help from me. Though it goes against my nature, I knew that this number was all about her dance, so I did what I could to draw attention to her rather than myself.

                                People were impressed. Ordinarily I’d be lapping it up and describing the compliments we got, but it doesn’t really seem to matter right now, so I’ll just hit the highlights.

                                Joan and Andromeda were there. I stopped by their table and spoke to them briefly. They were both a little upset because Andromeda’s parents had not accepted their relationship as well as they had hoped. The professed reason is because Joan is not Jewish, though Andromeda is relatively certain that it’s really because she’s seeing a woman. So that sucks, but as far as I can tell they’re not letting it come between them, so I’m sure they’ll survive.

                                I talked to a lot of other people, but the next one I feel like I should mention is Oleander. She was still mildly toxic, so we couldn’t smooch, but it was the first time I’d seen her since she’d disappeared into the Hedge five days earlier, so I made sure to make time to speak with her. I brought up Charlotte’s little visit, of course, and tried to impress upon her that I didn’t want her getting in trouble with her Court just to help me, but she kept insisting that she’d done the right thing both for me and for the Freehold, and that even if that hadn’t been the case, she’d do anything to help me out.

                                God, I hope I can temper that attitude with a bit of reason. The last thing I need on my conscience is for something really awful to happen to her while she’s sticking her neck out for me. I don’t know what I’d do if she’d been seriously hurt last Monday, or if she’d gotten in real trouble with Queen Charlotte.

                                I was still pretty high on life at that point, so I made sure she knew that I appreciated what she was willing to do for me, and then asked for her advice on finding a magical Christmas tree in the Hedge. Of course, she almost immediately volunteered to find something suitable for us herself, and wouldn’t be deterred. Jesus, maybe Charlotte’s right about me… I need to be more careful with the requests I make of my lovers.

                                So after that I pretty much planted myself in D’s lap at the “girlfriend table.” Marguerite and Julia were there, as were Becky and Calamity. We were all being pretty affectionate at that point when we all received a group text from Gawain, telling us that we needed to have a motley meeting after the crowds cleared out. That’s when the first dark shadow fell over the evening, and things have yet to lighten up.

                                Becky hung around downstairs while the rest of us made our way to the media room to hear what Gawain had to tell us. It was actually Jacob’s news, though. During the show, a mysterious woman approached him and introduced herself as Moire. She’s apparently an oracle of the Dusk Court, and she had information for him (Gawain called Captain Jack later, and he vouched for her veracity).

                                “It is about Rook,” Jacob said softly.

                                “You have a lead?” I asked eagerly. I know that Jacob’s been hoping to find and redeem his friend for months now, and all of our investigative avenues dried up long ago.

                                “She said that he knows about my sister,” he deadpanned.

                                “Oh my God,” I said. “Is she okay? Do we need to go to her?” I really need to learn to stop interrupting people.

                                Jacob put a hand on my shoulder. “She also said that he has allied himself with a bird of prey,” he said.

                                D caught the look in my eyes, strode over to me and threw her arms around me. I think, in hindsight, that’s the main reason that I didn’t just bolt once I got over the shock.

                                Hunting Hawk. I think I always knew in the back of my mind that he was still out there somewhere looking for me. It was naïve of me to believe the Empress would abide by the spirit of our agreement and let me live out my life here on Earth; I was her favorite after all. Of course she sent her most trusted hatchet-man to bring me back.

                                It took me a few minutes to process what was being said. The terror was all-encompassing, and there was no room for anything else in my brain. My dreams of becoming a doctor, my improved relationship with my father, even my love for the people surrounding me… all of it scattered like leaves in a strong wind. All that was left was my intense fear that Hawk would find me and take me back to my cage.

                                “I’m here, Jules.”

                                Those words, whispered into my ear, broke through the dread which had essentially seized up my muscles, and I let myself relax a little. Maria is strong, and brave, and utterly devoted to me. She’s more than a match for Hunting Hawk by herself… and when he comes for me, she won’t be by herself. Calamity won’t let him take me. Gawain won’t let him take me. Jacob won’t let him take me. Marguerite and Julia sure as hell won’t let him take me. The rest of the Freehold won’t let him take me.

                                These thoughts allowed me to rejoin the conversation. Besides, there was someone else to think about.

                                “Sorry,” I said, shaking my head in a futile attempt to clear it. “Sorry. Is your sister okay, Jacob? Do we need to go to her?”

                                Jacob shook his head. “Marguerite looked in on her through her mirrors. She is fine. She has a houseguest… another young woman is sleeping on her couch. And Captain Jack said that Moire’s prophecies usually have a little bit of lead time.”

                                “So… how do we deal with this?” I asked, maybe still a teeny bit panicky.

                                Jacob shrugged. “I would still like to find Rook and try to help him, if he is willing,” he said simply.

                                I thought about that, and nodded. “I… don’t expect anything to come of it, but if possible we should offer Hunting Hawk the same chance. It’s only fair.”

                                D stepped forward, keeping an arm around my waist. “One thing I want to make very clear right here and now is that Lori is not going to be used as bait to draw these psychos out of hiding,” she said fervently. “I’m open to just about any other strategy you guys want to try… but that is not happening. End of story.” Julia seconded this, and the rest of the group (myself included) rapidly approved the motion.

                                I excused myself to get some air after that. While it’s true that I was having trouble breathing by that point, that wasn’t the real reason I wanted to get outside. Thinking about Hunting Hawk made me think about Arcadia, which made me think about that damn cage that was all I knew while I was there, and… it just felt like the walls were closing in on me all of a sudden. I had to get outside, to feel the open air on my wings for a few minutes, even if I wasn’t flying.

                                D followed me out there, of course, as did a few of the others. My honey got this sort of thoughtful look on her face after a moment, and then began to speak. “I have an idea,” she said slowly. “It’s a terrible idea, but I think I should mention it anyway.”

                                The rest of us looked at her expectantly.

                                “Well,” she continued after a moment, “Hunting Hawk was the main guard that watched over you in Arcadia, right?” she asked.

                                I nodded. “He was the only guard we ever saw,” I acknowledged.

                                “So if he’s here… who’s watching your two friends?” she asked after a moment.

                                I thought about that. “It… might be the perfect time to rescue them,” I said.

                                “But only if we’re ready,” D cautioned. “It’s something to think about, but for right now we have to make sure that you, Jacob, and his sister are safe.”

                                Julia piped up with a suggestion. “Has anyone told the Winter Court about this yet? I mean, ferreting out Loyalists is what they do, right?”

                                Calamity seconded the suggestion. “I’m pledge-bound to report this to them anyway,” she added after a moment.

                                “And I hate to mention this,” Julia continued, “but we probably need to bring Darke in on this situation. It’s what he does, and he does it very well…”

                                “No,” Jacob and I both said at the same time… but we agreed that the Winter Court as a whole should know about the situation.

                                Jacob called Boss Lowjack and let him know what was going on. He was understandably upset by the news, and offered to help us out however he could. He also wanted to bring Darke in on this, but said he would allow us to deal with it “in-house” if that’s what we wanted to do. However, he needed a definite answer by the following day or else he would be forced to act on his own.

                                We argued the issue back and forth for a while. Jacob didn’t want to let Darke anywhere near his former friend, and I can definitely see why. For my part, I don’t think we can allow ourselves to employ Darke’s methods no matter what the risk. But the others raised some very valid points, even though I could tell that it galled them to even suggest going to Darke for help. Darke knows a lot more about hunting Loyalists than we do. He has resources that we don’t. And he would have an outside perspective on how to deal with the situation… one untainted by our fear and worry.

                                So in the end we decided to at least consult with Darke before taking any action on our own. Ideally we’d still like to handle this within the motley, but it would be foolish not to take advantage of his expertise.

                                “We will call Boss Lowjack after breakfast,” Marguerite suggested, “and tell him that we will consult with Darke, but that he is not to take any action without our consent. But for now, we all need sleep, no?”

                                I did not sleep last night. I knew I wouldn’t. Once we were away from the others, D and Marguerite and Julia swept me into an embrace and held me as I trembled and cried and muttered to myself like a crazy person. I was rambling; nothing I said made any kind of sense. Hell, it probably still doesn’t. I know I told them that Hunting Hawk probably didn’t know me as Lorelei Grace, since I chose that name after I escaped from the Empress. She always called me Rainbow. He usually called me Whore, when he bothered to speak with me at all.

                                Or maybe I talked about that before I went to bed. I don’t know.

                                There was no sex last night. For once I truly wasn’t in the mood.

                                I got up before the others and went to church. If Rook and/or Hunting Hawk are watching us, they probably don’t know that we know about them, and we don’t want to tip them off to that tidbit by varying our routines. D went with me and Marguerite shadowed us, since the group doesn’t want me going off by myself until this situation is resolved. We spoke to Dad briefly, and I’m pretty sure he picked up on the fact that something was bothering me, but I didn’t tell him what’s going on at that point.

                                We had sex breakfast around noon as usual. I had used Spring contracts to pep myself up before church, but even after that I couldn’t muster up any true enthusiasm for the day. I don’t remember what we ate, but Jacob called Lowjack and informed him of our decision, and he invited us to meet with Darke at his bar in half an hour to make our plans. We agreed.

                                I don’t claim to remember much about the plans we made either; talking about this situation feels almost like a dream to me now. I know Darke said they had not been able to locate Rook, but they had eyes on a Loyalist in L.A. they thought was Hunting Hawk. I gave him every bit of information I could remember about the Empress’ jailor, and that seemed to confirm his suspicions.

                                Hawk has been living in L.A., off the grid and under the radar. He hasn’t yet attracted the attention of the Day or Night Courts. He seems to be having difficulty adjusting to this world… the fact that he can’t fly here and the smog in the atmosphere are forcing him to reevaluate his usual hunting tactics. He’s been observing me from a distance – no one really seems to know for how long – but now that he’s got an ally, it seems like he might be ready to make his move at last.

                                I’ll be honest, I don’t really know how we decided to proceed either. There was talk of bringing Jacob’s sister in on the secrets of Changeling society, ensorcelling her… though Jacob still doesn’t want to tell her who he really is, even if we end up doing that. But if she’s aware of the real danger posed by Rook, we might have an easier time protecting her from him.

                                Honestly, we could probably take care of Hunting Hawk pretty easily at this point if we wanted to. The problem is that we have no leads on Rook’s location at the moment, and Darke is hoping that Hawk will lead us to him. If we spring a trap on Hawk, Rook will almost certainly get wind of it, and he’ll go to ground, making it that much harder for us to resolve this situation once and for all.

                                There were a few plans thrown out there. We talked about sending Jacob’s sister (or possibly Davie disguised as her) to an art exhibition in L.A. on Wednesday, to try to lure Rook out of hiding. We also discussed spreading rumors that the Empress had sent another servant after me, and that servant would be taking me back through the Hedge at a particular time and place. That might work… Hunting Hawk was always desperate for the Empress’ approval, and the idea that someone might steal his glory on this particular hunt would probably raise his hackles like nothing else.

                                Darke asked a few questions about my Durance at that point, so that if we go with the latter plan he’ll be able to make the “second hunter” convincing. It seems strange now, but he pointed out something that had never occurred to me before: all of the Empress’ songbirds were girls. Hunting Hawk was the only man I ever saw in that place, and he was also the only guard I ever encountered. It seems logical to assume that the Empress employs only males as guards, and only females as pets.

                                Enough of that. I don’t want to talk about this situation anymore; I just want it to be over. We have a show tonight. I have to perform, and I can’t let anyone who might be watching know that anything is wrong. I pride myself on my acting ability, but this might well stretch me to my limits. I’ll have to draw on every ounce of professional pride I possess to keep from falling apart out there tonight. The show must go on, but… this is Hunting Hawk. He represents everything that I’ve been trying to escape for almost a year now… everything that I endured for three and a half decades. Servitude. Enslavement. Humiliation. The subjugation of my very being.

                                I’ve never been this scared. Not when Mary’s fetch was trying to kill us. Not when the Mothership attacked. Not even when I had to tell my Dad the truth about who and what I am. I know Maria and the others will protect me. I know that I am as safe here with them as I could be anywhere in the world. But what can I say? Fear isn’t rational, and Hunting Hawk is the boogeyman who has haunted my dreams ever since I escaped.

                                God, I will serve Your will in this as in all things, but I beg You to spare me this fate. May Hunting Hawk not take me back to the Empress’ service. May he not harm my dearest Maria, or Marguerite, or Julia, or any of my other friends and loves. May Jacob’s sister Elena be kept from whatever terrible fate Rook plans for her. And please, God… grant me the courage to face Arcadia again, so that I can rescue Missy and Jane at last.

                                Lorelei Grace


                                Calamity’s Little Black Book

                                Sunday, November 28th
                                Nine years, six months, twenty-two days
                                Days without stabbing: 0

                                I am a fucking monster. I almost forgot that. I came so close to forgetting that. I let myself get lost in stupid little girl with dreams of living happily ever after and dancing. This time yesterday I was drunk with happy. The meetings with Nightqueen Skitters and her guard Lizette were over and they seemed satisfied that whatever the situation might have been that it seemed clear that Insincere Marguerite was not a loyalist or Keeper now. The show was shaping up to go off perfectly. Handsome gave both Lori and I a nice inspirational talk and Lori used her magic to inspire us both. I even called upon the goblin vow I made with Blood to ask it to make the crowd ‘hot blooded’ for the show. I promised to bleed or draw blood in the heat of passion before dawn. I figured that throwing myself into that number would have me in the mood for such anyway. And I was certainly in that mood before dawn, just not the way I was expecting to be.

                                The Dream House was packed. I was nervous but we’d really put in the effort on this one. I called on the Vainglory Lori had taught me and once the lights flared and the music started… Fuck it was amazing. I threw myself into it and I fucking nailed it. Everything was perfect. It felt so damn good. I lost myself in it like the best sex or the hardest fight. And the GLAMOUR… Oh the glamour, feeling all of the lust and desire and fear and hunger and need rolling off the crowd. And even afterward, as I worked the room. I could feel the looks and I couldn’t help but strut across the floor with my mien still showing thanks to the Vainglory. I was flirty and feeling the mix of hunger and fear as I did so… I was flying. I got into a long chat with Lizette about chainsaws and incendiary weapons that was way more sexual than it should have been until the Nightqueen elbowed her companion and we were both reminded that there were mortals about. I floated to the girlfriend table and my Dragon. I had thought about trying to avoid Beautiful given the sort of mood I knew I’d be in but despite the prey in the room I wanted to be with her and she made no move to stop me from kissing her hard and hungry and showing her what I wanted.

                                Yeah, I was on top of the fucking world right about then, sitting close and teasing her subtly as she growled and teased back. Lori and DLR were getting a little frisky as well. And I guess that’s why Handsome sent the text out that we needed a motley meeting. I could see that he looked guilty but really worried and I knew something was wrong. I told Beautiful to wait for me and she agreed to. I should have known better and sent her home but in the moment I just couldn’t imagine anything bringing me down.

                                Stupid of me to think that. I dance and I sing about being a monster... about being dangerous. I make light of it. I play at it as if it’s just some fun little game. A little thrill to the fucking. But the monster inside me isn’t fur lined cuffs and a little slap and tickle. It’s nails and razors and blood and screaming. And as soon as it became clear what was going on it hit me like a fucking tidal wave. I thought I was so much better. I thought I had it under control. I thought I was ready to think about playing house and being some sort of fucking happy little girlfriend. Like I could keep it caged with a white picket fence.

                                Rook knows about Jacob’s sister and he’s formed some sort of partnership with Hunting Hawk. Jacob is worried about her. Lori is shaken. I tried to reassure them both but it was so fucking hard to focus. I want them DEAD. I want to hunt them and kill them and see them ended. They’re threatening my motley, my family… But honestly, that part of me doesn’t really care. It’s a rapid dog straining at its leash because it has prey its allowed to hurt and maim and kill. Prey I am allowed to hurt and kill. I want to hurt them. I want to end them. I want to feel their blood on my glass and see the light fade from their eyes. I don’t have to hold back. I don’t have to feel guilty. They deserve to hurt and bleed and die. They deserve the Calamity that is going to befall them.

                                I won’t let them take my motley, my friends. I won’t let them harm them or anyone else. I will do want ever it takes to see that those I swore to protect remain safe. Darke was brought up. The others are leery of working with him. I should be but this is what he does. He hunts loyalists and he’s the best at it. He’s a bigger monster than they are. He’s the monster we need right now.

                                I am afraid that he’s the monster I will be some day. After the meeting I had to go back to face Becky. I walked out and lingered on the balcony, looking at her sitting there playing some silly little game on her phone with one claw-tip as the Christmas lights blinked and turned her white hair into a silken rainbow that swayed and danced impatiently about her shoulders. I had to just watch her for a long moment, and then when I did finally approach, I watched as she turned and her expression went from bright to deathly serious. Even without being a Spring Courtier or a succubus she could tell something was wrong. I wasn’t very coherent. I did manage to explain what had happened, but at the time I couldn’t find the words for how I was feeling. How all those months of therapy and meditation and working at laying this darkness to rest had just evaporated. I hugged her and sent her on her way. I wasn’t about to kiss her and let her feel what I wanted.

                                I turned, planning to head down to the basement and work on the heavy bag until I either broke it or my hand. But Jacob was there and had his knives. He just looked at me. “You need to spar.” It wasn’t a question. I think he needed it too. The usual banter and friendly comments were absent. We fought hard in the dark and in silence, even as we exchanged nicks and cuts with little more than a hiss to acknowledge them. I’m not sure either of us really felt better, but it did wear us both out enough to sleep.

                                Today hasn’t been much better. We met with Lowjack and Darke. We talked to them about the situation and tried to make plans. Darke’s people have been aware of and watching Hunting Hawk though they didn’t know who he was after. It wouldn’t be hard to move on him but we need to draw Rook out. Lots of planning and ideas. I tried to be constructive but I know my focus was off. I want those two dead. I want them to hurt and suffer. I know we need to give Jacob a chance to talk to Rook. Closure if nothing else. He needs to be able to say he at least tired to save him. I know enough of his own turn to doubt that he alone is going to be able to sway Rook. I could be wrong. I want to be wrong. I also want to be right because I want to fight Rook. I’ve heard Jacob talk about his skills. I want to dance with him, not the practice of sparring but the real thing, the life or death Thunderdome two enter one leaves dance. I want to feel his blood on my skin as I run my glass fingers along his bones. I want to taste his razor kisses. I want to know if Hunting Hawk’s feathers are are soft as Lori’s. I can only imagine that he’s as gorgeous as she is and I want to cut that perfect skin and destroy that beauty.

                                I need to focus. This is wrong. This is sick. Even if they do deserve it… And they do. They deserve to be punished and I am made for it. I want.. I need… Why shouldn’t I savor their just desserts? Why should it fall on someone who will regret or anguish over delivering the pain and vengeance they deserve? I want their pain. I’m already broken… why should someone else have to become a monster when I already am one?

                                Is this why the Voices haven’t come looking for me? Is this what They wanted me to become? Are They just waiting for me to return and take my place with Them, punishing the naughty girls and boys in the darkness? Or is it that They really don’t care? Perhaps my escape meant nothing more than needing a new Jane. No… I CUT Them. I HURT Them. I was the only one that struck back at Them. That has to mean something. It has to.

                                But if that were true, why haven’t They come after me for it?

                                Fuck. There is a show tonight. I need to push past all of this and focus. My motley needs my head in the fucking game right now. Lori and Jacob need me. I… I can rise about this. I can keep this in check and be reasonable. This is about protecting them, not destroying Rook and Hunting Hawk. I am not going to give into these urges.

                                I need to go and do my forms. I need to find my balance and my focus.

                                    Jacob’s Journal

                                    I am exhausted. Today has been a very long day for more reasons than one. Before the show I told the motley of my suspicions that Yvette might have left something behind or taken something when she was here spying on us. The lot of us searched the house from top to bottom and didn’t find anything missing or added. So there’s that at least. One good thing to happen today. After that we all went our separate ways to prepare for the show. The ladies did one last run through of their act and started to get dressed. Gawain swept while they practiced, and I stocked up the bar. We went through a good bit of alcohol during the Thanksgiving get together. Considering how many guests we had I’m not surprised, but I still had to restock for the show, so that’s what I was doing.

                                    At the show Lori and Calamity debuted their new act to thunderous applause. It really was very well done. I watched from the bar as they danced and sang to the song “Bloody Creature Poster Girl.” I forgot the band’s name, but I’m not forgetting the name of that song any time soon. It was apropos to Calamity in a lot of ways. When she lets the “monster” out during a fight I can see the similarities.

                                    We had several influential people as our guests tonight at the show. Desiree was there with Jolie and Gloria. Nightqueen Skitters and Lizette were present as well. Apparently after the interviews they had tea with Madame Spidertoes and then came to the show. They seemed to be in good spirits so hopefully they found everything with Insincere Marguerite to be satisfactory. At least I have not heard otherwise. I wonder when or if they are going to reveal their findings to Insincere Marguerite to let her know if she can return to Paris or not? I’m assuming that they will, but it didn’t happen tonight. At least not that I know of.

                                    And speaking of things that we’re not sure of yet. Molly came to the bar, as has become her habit, and started talking to me and Gawain. She has been doing well and is still spending a lot of time with Chalcedony. She even offered to talk to the azure girl and see if she might be willing to come back to the Dream House sometime in the near future. Gawain expressed an interest in this and explained that he wanted to be able to apologize to Chalcedony in person for everything that happened at the club. I can understand that. He wants to make amends. I would apologize, but find that I still don’t trust her. Maybe how things were handled was not the best, but it was the best we could do at the time. I guess for my part I just want Chalcedony to prove that she is not up to something. I’m not sure how she’s supposed to do that, but I am worried about the attraction that so many people feel towards her after they watch her for any length of time. I will be rather upset if she hurts Molly in any way.

                                    The bar is where so much happens. Tonight a Dusk courtier by the name of Moire came up to me and said that the pursuer knows about my sister and that he has acquired a bird of prey. The words made my blood run cold. I questioned the woman a little bit to find out who she was and how she knew this. She told me her name and court and that she is a seer. She said that she saw this and wanted to warn me of what was coming. After she left I told Gawain and he called Jack to verify that Moire is in fact a Dusk courtier and seer. He confirmed that she is both. He also said that her predictions usually have a little bit of lead time so it will not likely come to pass in the very immediate future. So, we have time to prepare. I talked over possible options with Gawain and we came up with a few. I could tell Elena that we overheard something said at the bar that concerned her and wanted to warn her of possible trouble. The question as to why someone would go after her would come up and we don’t have a good reason in mind. On the flip side we could tell Elena what we are and show her our true selves and let her know that she is in danger because she is my friend. I am a target and someone might use her to draw me out. The good thing about this plan is that it is the truth. Nothing that comes up later will refute what we say so we won’t get caught in a lie later on. I just won’t mention that she is my sister. She has been happily believing that the fetch is her Jacob. Who am I to disillusion her? The last option that we came up with was to have Insincere Marguerite check in Elena’s mirrors and make sure that she is okay now. I was wholeheartedly behind that plan for tonight.

                                    So, Gawain looked around the room and saw how things were going and sent out a text message to the motley that we needed to meet after all of the customers had left. Left to their own devices I think most everyone would have split off in pairs or more and headed off to their respective bedrooms. I hated to spoil everyone’s mood, but the information I had was important and the others needed to know about it, especially Lorelei. So, after everyone except Becky had left we had our talk. Becky waited downstairs while we talked upstairs in one of the rooms. The result was pretty much as I expected. Everyone got serious. Calamity looked mad, and Lori was a combination of in shock and terrified. She was often guarded by Hunting Hawk, which is who we believe the “bird of prey” in Moire’s prophecy to be. Lori for her part went out on the balcony to get some fresh air while the rest of us discussed options. Calamity mentioned that since this was a possible threat to the freehold she was oath bound to tell Dahlia about it. I agreed that the Winter king needed to know and called Lowjack. I explained to him what I was told and what we thought it meant. He asked if we wanted to take care of it ourselves, let Darke handle it, or work with Darke to capture Rook and Hunting Hawk. If we handled it ourselves he said that he wanted a pledge from the lot of us that we will do our utmost to capture and/or kill them. I told him that I’d have to talk things over with the motley and he said that he needed to know first thing tomorrow what we’d decided. Makes sense to me. He would have to move fast to put people and/or resources in place. While I was on the phone Insincere Marguerite checked in on Elena’a mirrors and found her to be asleep with a friend sleeping on her couch. So, she is safe. I am glad.

                                    After I got off the phone with Lowjack I told them what he said. We talked things over for a bit. Gawain even brought up De La Rocha’s idea of going into Arcadia to rescue Lori’s friends while Hunting Hawk was in ‘the world.’ It seemed like a good idea, but had one problem. It left things open for Hunting Hawk to come in to Arcadia after us and block us from getting back out. We agreed that we needed to take care of Hunting Hawk before we could make our run on Arcadia to free the two girls still there. After that discussion we talked about what we wanted to do with Rook and Hunting Hawk. I think Calamity was all for just killing them outright, but I explained again that I wanted to try and give Rook the opportunity to leave the Lady’s service of his own free will. He was a friend for many years. I hate to have to fight him. I will if I have to though. Lori expressed a similar wish that Hunting Hawk be given a second chance. We weren’t sure that Hawk would be interested in changing sides. He doesn’t have the same kind of close relationship that Rook and I had. He just saw the girls as a distraction for the Violet Empress’s affections. On more than one occasion he called Lorelei a whore. If he had been a Knight of the Lady’s and said something like that of one of her Treasures he would die a long and painful death.

                                    In fact, there was one man years ago, at least years ago in Arcadia, that had been brought before the Lady so she could decide what she wanted done with him. He had managed to harm one of her Knights in a fight. He was captured and brought before Her and the court. We were all summoned to the court, Knight, servant and Treasured alike. I can’t remember what his initial crime was, but I do remember why and how he died. He was desperate to escape and managed to get away from the Knight restraining him, taking a knife from the man’s belt as well. In a bid to be let out of the palace alive he grabbed one of the Lady’s Treasures and pulled him in front of his body to use as a shield. The doomed man had put the knife to the Treasure’s throat and threatened to kill the man if he was not let out of the palace. I have rarely seen the Lady so angry. Well, maybe when she found out about my betrayal. She was pretty mad about that. Anyway, the escapee started walking backwards pulling the Treasure with him when he suddenly came upon Rook. Rook stepped out of the shadows and swiftly and cleanly disarmed the man before handing off the knife to me and restraining the escapee again. The Treasure was not hurt, but he was rather shaken. The Lady was livid. She ordered that the man be brought to her dungeons and that she would deal with him personally. We could hear the man’s screams for days after that. At the time I wondered what the Lady was doing to him to make him cry out so, but now I have a pretty good idea of what she did. The Lady took attacks or insults to her Treasures as attacks or insults against herself, and dealt with them accordingly. Those that used such crude insults against her Treasured were rarely seen again.

                                    We discussed the situation a little bit longer before all agreeing that we needed to sleep on it before we came up with a plan of action. We were all kind of leaning in the direction where we take care of the situation ourselves with some help from others in the freehold, likely Darke. After everyone started heading to their respective rooms I made a quick stop to my room to grab my knives and stopped Calamity who was on her way to the gym downstairs. She looked like she needed to work out some frustration and anger, and I know I did so I suggested that we spar. It was probably the most intense and quiet sparring match we’ve ever had. Neither of us felt up to banter, or pointing out weaknesses in defense, we just fought hard and brutal. Both of us ended up with multiple cuts and bruises from our sparring match, but we barely made any noise when we got them. Maybe a slight grunt or hiss of pain, but for the most part we just pushed through the discomfort and kept going until we were exhausted. Afterwards we just nodded at each other and went to our respective rooms to clean up and sleep. I think we both needed that.

                                    **** **** ****

                                    We had agreed last night that keeping up appearances was important, so Lorelei despite feeling scared, went to church this morning as is her usual habit. De La Rocha went with her for support, and I believe that Insincere Marguerite followed them to keep an eye on them just in case. It seemed the prudent thing to do. We need Rook and Hunting Hawk to believe that we don’t know that they have plans against us, or that we have plans of our own against them. The more we can predict their moves the better for us, and we’ll be able to do that if we can control what happens and when, with our daily lives. Besides it would be to our benefit to lure them into a false sense of security.

                                    Today’s breakfast was a serious affair as we continued our discussion on Rook and Hunting Hawk. We went back over the ideas brought up last night and decided that we did want to deal with it ourselves, but see about getting Darke’s help in carrying out our plan. I’m not sure any of us were super keen on working with Darke on this, but he is very good at his job, and his job is finding loyalists. I do not like the man, but I will trust him to do his utmost to capture Rook and Hunting Hawk. The others agreed with me, so I called Lowjack and told him that we’d decided on what we wanted to do. I explained our plan, and he said that he could set up a meeting with us and Darke to go over the particulars. He also said that he would be there if we wanted. I said that would be a good idea. Plan of action in hand, we met with Darke and Lowjack thirty minutes later.

                                    The meeting with Darke was actually rather cordial. We were all focused on the situation at hand and did our best to work out a plan of action. Darke said that he knew about Hunting Hawk. He sent Vik to keep an eye on him in L.A. Apparently Hawk is getting used to ‘the world’ and discovering just how different things are. For one, he cannot fly here. His abilities are also somewhat limited as well. I remember the first time the Lady sent me to ‘the world’ to do recon on a potential acquisition. I had to get used to the idea that my powers were muted somewhat from what they’d been in Arcadia, and things just felt weird, different. It took some getting used to. After a couple days of acquainting myself with how things are in ‘the world’ I got on with my mission. Anyway, Darke knows where Hunting Hawk is, but does not know where Rook is, though he knows that Hawk has been in contact with someone in L.A. recently. It is possible that he’s communicating with my former brother in arms.

                                    During our discussion we agreed that we would not go ahead and scoop up Hunting Hawk even though we know exactly where he is. We need him to lead us to Rook, so we will leave him free for the moment. We also talked about ways to lure Rook out and get him to enact his plan. It is unlikely that he will actually hurt Elena. His target is me, and Hunting Hawk’s is Lorelei. So, my theory is that he will use Elena to lure me out so I will go to her rescue. He can then attack me or have Hawk do it. I’m not sure of their plan to lure Lori out, unless they hope to get the lot of us. I know he is a good fighter, but taking on the whole motley, plus Luna, would be suicide. There are at least five seasoned fighters among that group and it would just be the two of them. Unless Rook has gathered allies he would be better served trying to get me alone. We’ll see how it goes though.

                                    One idea that Darke mentioned was that there was an art display in downtown L.A. We could have someone disguise themselves as Elena, maybe Davie, and go to the art show. This would give Rook an opportunity to capture “Elena”, or whatever else he has planned, while keeping my real sister safe. This option would probably necessitate me telling Elena what I actually am and what is going on, but I am willing to do that. I do not think that she would react too badly to the revelation of what we are. I don’t know if I’m going to mention being her brother or not. I don’t know if I should. I suppose I can ask my motley mates what they think I should do. I know that Lori has had success in telling her father what she is, so maybe Elena would be accepting of who I am as well.

                                    Colloquy


                                    Maria de la Rocha leaned on the door to the bathroom in the henhouse. She looked at Insincere Marguerite DeLisle, who stood in the doorway of her own room, and Julia Oldacre, who was pacing in the hall. The sound of the shower was audible, and the pleasant clean smell was emerging, but the three women had no trouble hearing each other.

                                    “She’s scared. We need to protect her. We can be with her all the time. Even in the bathroom if needs be.”

                                    Insincere Marguerite pushed out of the doorway, to lean bonelessly against the wall next to the Latina. “We will. I have personally declared her under my protection.” Blue eyes looked deeply into brown.

                                    “I know, but I am worried. I won’t admit in front of her but…I don’t know anything about this Hunting Hawk. I know Jules is afraid of him. Very afraid.”

                                    “We often irrationally fear the minions of our Keepers. They seem near invulnerable, omniscient. We fear them. But they are like us. Only more pathetic.” Green eyes looked from brown to blue.

                                    “I also need to protect you from Yvette.” This time the brown-eyed women pointed at blue. “We don’t know her goals, but I suspect they are to kill you.”

                                    “She won’t.” Green eyes darkened.

                                    “Julia is absolutely correct.” Insincere Marguerite’s airy French accent was harder to hear over the shower, but both girls managed. “I know Yvette as well as she knows herself. She cannot kill me.”

                                    “I’m not willing to bet on that.” The accent this time was Latin American. “Jules is willing to let me guard her. You need to be too, Mags. I mean, mind yourself of course, but please let me keep a watch.”

                                    “What are you intending?” The artist moved to lean against the door to her room, opposite the Latina and her fiancée.

                                    “I don’t have a precise plan. He is a stalker though. I was a stalker. I know how to watch. Separation from the herd. Fatigue from fear, from running. It was my Durance.” The Latina’s voice was low.

                                    The French woman moved in front of her. Blue eye met brown. “Is this…part of yourself you should be channeling? You have repressed that Maria for a very long time.” The Darkling cocked her head, looking up slightly at the Leechfinger. “I would not want it to…overtake you.”

                                    The Nightsinger’s eyebrow quirked. A small nibble of her lip.

                                    “Ah, you are sweet Mags but…no. I mean, I am not schizophrenic. I do remember my Durance. Unfortunately, as I have gotten stronger I remember it better.” She broke eye contact to look to her right. “I don’t have to feel instincts to remember them.”

                                    “If you say so.” The Lurker seemed unconvinced, but moved from in front of the Latina, sitting in a boneless pile on the floor. Meanwhile the green-eyed Artist paced watching her lovers very carefully.

                                    “Anyway, main thing is, we stick together, we get through this.” De La Rocha sounded confident, and she was. “Lori and I will go to church together tomorrow, but we will all stay close otherwise.”

                                    “That’s a good idea. Let her take some solace and comfort from her faith.” Julia nodded.

                                    “I will come along in show, hein? I can remain mostly out of sight and disappear quickly, but if something does happen I can be a third defender from the shadows.” Blue eyes glanced up at brown.

                                    ‘Or warn the others if we get grabbed and pulled away somehow.” The Leechfinger nodded.

                                    “That’s not going to happen, you said so yourself.” Julia’s voice was challenging.

                                    The Latina looked exasperated. “It’s dumb not to have a plan ready. We all need to stick together and support each other. Just if you tail us remember Yvette is out there-”

                                    “I am not even sure Lorelei noticed Bon-Bon and I are even in the room most of the evening. It’s hard to be supportive when you barely exist to her. There is you, and there is only you.” The green-eyed Artist pointed almost accusingly at Maria, interrupting her. “Bon-Bon and I will protect her, but I am not going to lie to myself and say we have any significance to her right now.”

                                    “I agree that would be nice, but relationships are not about supporting people at their best, Julia.” De La Rocha was calm, her brown eyes steely. “I mean nothing by this except what I say when I say you are new to really being in a relationship, and being in a relationship means supporting the other person even when they are being an absolute shit, as long as they are not abusive, and Jules is being neither an absolute shit nor abusive.” A sigh. “Insensitive sure, but she is afraid for her damn life. Most of us would be.” Brown eyes met green.

                                    Insincere Marguerite rose and put skinny arms around Julia. The Artist put her head on the shorter woman’s shoulder, or approximated it as best she could. Blue eyes gave an appreciative look to brown. Julia turned slightly. “Sorry.”

                                    The Witchtooth was dismissive. “Ah, it’s nothing. Don’t worry. As I say, we are all in a relationship, and we support each other. That means me for you when you get jealous. Just don’t get psycho-jealous.”

                                    Julia chuckled despite herself. “Do I seem psycho-jealous?”

                                    Both other women looked at her. There was an uncomfortable silence.

                                    “Well I am not going to hurt any of us!” She sounded exasperated. De La Rocha and Insincere Marguerite gave each other a look. The Blue eyed one smiled slightly.

                                    The water stopped.

                                    “Okay main thing is, half of us are in potential danger, and we all need to let this make us stronger, not push us apart. We need to be there for each other.” The brown-eyed woman switched back to her more authoritative voice when she heard the shower had ended.

                                    “Of course,” Julia nodded, she looked to her fiancée, who smiled lovingly at her.

                                    The brown-eyed girl looked back. “Trust me, Timebomb, she will notice.”

                                    Before Julia could respond Lorelei Grace emerged, a towel wrapped around her, her black eyes afraid. She was, Insincere Marguerite reminded herself, the most beautiful changeling she had ever…loved.

                                    “How are you?” The green-eyed girl spoke first.

                                    “Been better.” A weak smile. “I don’t think I am up to any…you know.”

                                    “It’s fine.” The colors spoke in unison.

                                    Maria de la Rocha put her arms around Lorelei and Insincere Marguerite. “Don’t worry ladies, as long as I am here, you two have nothing to worry about. Lori leaned against her lover and closed her eyes. Insincere Marguerite squirmed like an unhappy cat in arms to squeeze out of the Latina grip, until she felt the firmness of the bicep against her. Despite herself, she smiled a moment.

                                    Perhaps Maria can be allowed certain little displays, she thought.

Did not get as many successes as the Bloody Creature Poster Girl team, but did okay for herself.

She ha to shed blood in passion after, which with the mood killed lead to the sparring.

                                    This is a slightly difficult story to do given the themes of Malibu Dream House. One thing going on as a recurring idea is that in the Lady by the Sea Freehold, some of those who fight Loyalists are scarier and frankly more evil than the Loyalists themselves. The times Loyalists or potential Loyalists have come up, they tend to be more sympathetic than the Loyalist hunters. Compare Desiree or Chalcedony to Lucian Torrez, Master Darke, Doctor Bright, or Twitch. Even some non-hunters, like Madame Spidertoes, Tommy Rawhead, Leanne, or the Handmaidens seems to be dangerous customers, or generally frightening or offputting somehow. Even some PCs struggle with their demons, be it Insincere Marguerite’s fighting Gentrihood, Calamity fighting becoming an utter monster, and Jacob’s trying to thaw. These internal struggles are more how the claws of Arcadia grasp at the PCs. This is a deliberate deviation from the usual presentation in Changeling: The Lost, though the Loyalists goals are still horrible and must be foiled, and the Gentry are of course truly frightening. But after Loyalists played a huge role in Beyond the Sea, my last Changeling campaign, I wanted something different.

                                    But these Loyalist foes exist, and really, it was time they appeared. They won’t become campaign villains; The Motley would be wise to fear others of their kind more than these hunters, or at the very least, those wolves in sheep’s clothing that can appear thus. Still, no harm in making them sweat a little.

                                                            Lorelei’s Diary

                                                            Okay, so maybe things are starting to look up. I’m still terrified by the fact that Hunting Hawk and Rook are out there, and I can’t claim that I’m pleased with the way my friends handled our most recent crisis… but I think I’m starting to get a handle on the situation, and my panic is beginning to recede.

                                                            Jacob texted his sister Elena during the afternoon, hoping he could meet with her to bring her into the fold regarding his status as a changeling and thus give her some idea of what she’s up against while Rook is stalking her. Unfortunately, she was reluctant to even speak with him, sending terse responses about how it “wasn’t a good time to talk.” Of course this just made him more worried, and he began to insist that they meet. She eventually agreed, so long as he came to meet with her alone.

                                                            This sent up red flags for all of us, of course. Gawain agreed to head to Elena’s place with Jacob. He said he’d stay outside so as not to alarm the girl, but that way he’d be close enough to help if Jacob needed backup. So Jacob walked into his sister’s apartment alone… and into one of the strangest plot twists any of us had ever encountered.

                                                            Elena’s houseguest is a Ukrainian girl named Natasha. Her father is apparently politically important back home, and she was visiting relatives in the States when some sort of trouble broke out in the Mother Country. Now Russian intelligence agents are trying to capture her to use as leverage against her father. She knew she was being followed by at least one such agent, and suspected that there might be more of them. She had taken refuge with Elena because her English is not so good, and Elena was one of the few people she had met in the States who speaks Russian. Now they were pretty sure the apartment was under surveillance, and both girls were afraid to leave.

                                                            Though perhaps not the best time for it, Jacob decided to play his trump card. He told Elena that he was a changeling, and told her that if she would trust him, he could get the two of them out of the building unobserved by any mortal force. Though both girls were hesitant to accept his admittedly crazy-sounding plan, they agreed that they had little choice in the end.
