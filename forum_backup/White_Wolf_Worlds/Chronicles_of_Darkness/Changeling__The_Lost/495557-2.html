                    Originally posted by Arlecchino View Post
                    Though I think the Greys fall outside that thematic frame. While I would agree cryptids and urban legends are modern folklore, working in aliens strikes me as too wild a departure from the timeless magical creatures Changeling otherwise draws from.
                    I believe that is intentional. In the write-up it is mentioned the Kiths change with human culture and the evolution of myth and stories, as in the end the Gentry only have as much imagination as the human collective they draw from for inspiration. So it says something very interesting that one of the Kiths existing in the current day is in fact inspired by the idea of Grey aliens. They are a legend of the modern era, as much as dragons, barghest, wendigos, sidhe and what have you come from folklore that has existed for a long time and proliferated through the human mind.

                    Besides, this is hardly the first mention of the True Fae taking inspiration from alien abduction stories and alleged extraterrestial contacts influencing some of the Others in how they present themselves or treat their captives. Alien abduction is the "spirited away" of 20th and 21st century pop culture. In my opinion, it makes perfect sense for the Greys to be present in this capacity in the 2nd edition. It gives the kiths and the Gentry a real sense of dynamic substance accompanying the progress of real world ideas.
