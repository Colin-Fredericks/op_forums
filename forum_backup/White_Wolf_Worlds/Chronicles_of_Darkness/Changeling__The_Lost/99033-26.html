Lowjack believes that if enough of the non-seasonal courtiers in the area swear allegiance to one of the monarchs – not that monarch’s Court, but the monarch him-or-herself – the crowns won’t appear at all. He says that he wants to craft a pledge to ensure that the monarchs in question will never ask those who swear to them to do anything which goes against their true court’s philosophy, and that they won’t try to trick or manipulate their allies into joining the Seasonal courts either.

Marguerite seemed willing to go along with it (she’s never wanted the Crown of Night anyway), and Gawain was cautiously optimistic, though he made it clear that participation in this program should be entirely voluntary, and that he didn’t intend to swear allegiance to any of the current sitting monarchs. It’s part of a contingency plan… if the crowns materialize despite his best efforts, he would rather Marguerite inherit the position than Salk, who is apparently the runner-up. Lowjack doesn’t trust Salk, and I can only assume that he has cause to feel that way.

Questioning glances were fired back and forth between members of the motley as to whether we should tell him about Gloria… but in the end there didn’t seem to be any other choice. He is the Freehold’s spymaster, and unwilling agents of Hollywood are something he needs to know about. I also mentioned a theory we had kicked around earlier: since Gloria’s directives were so basic and simplistic, it’s possible that she wasn’t even a targeted spy. The Hollywood Courts might have put that sort of basic programming in everyone over there, to cast a wide net.

I wonder if all of the doctors here start with the questions to rule out or confirm PTSD? I guess he confirmed it for me given my answers to his questions. I guess I’m not really surprised. I just hadn’t put that label on it before. Not in any real way.

                            I think there are some things that are going to take a bit longer to “fix” as far as my problems go. He asked if I’d ever harmed myself in any way. I said that I had not done that intentionally. He seemed to think there was more to that and asked what had happened. I explained the situation when I got injured by the wolves in the Hedge, and didn’t let Lori know right away. Dr. Sullivan agreed with the others that I should have sought medical attention, though he seemed to understand when I explained why I had not gone to Lori right away. It wasn’t life threatening so I just dealt with it on my own, like I’d always done. I guess even now I’m learning that there are different rules in “the world” than there are in Arcadia. Overall though I think it went well. The hour seemed to fly by and before I knew it, it was time to leave. Calamity and I set up our appointments for the same days and times so we could carpool to our sessions. I’m glad they were able to work out that kind of schedule for us.

                            On a different note, I think something changed in me recently. Nothing that I can really put my finger on, at least not all of it, but I feel different. I feel closer to what I used to be, but not in the negative detached way that I’ve noticed already. This is more of a confidence in who I am, what I am. I feel closer to my element, which I suppose in California is just going to be uncomfortable. It’s too warm here. I also noticed that the frost on my skin reaches past my shoulders now and goes down a bit of my upper arms. I used to think that the frost on my skin was a sign of my connection to the Lady, but lately I’ve been thinking that it’s just a representation of my element, of my connection to it. If I’ve become stronger in snow and ice it would make sense that it is represented in some way, I suppose. In any case, it did not alarm me the way it might have in the past. After all, change is a good thing, right?


                            Insincere Marguerite’s Whispers

                            A great deal has gone on this past day and a half or so.

                            I am now formally engaged to Julia Oldacre. We got engaged in the nighttime graveyard walk on her birthday. I honestly don’t remember it all clearly; I was too excited and too nervous. But it worked. I do recall her squealing with joy, which was interesting. But she is very happy, and I am very happy. I am glad the proposing is over. Given how obvious her intentions were (she bought the ring, after all) I had little need to be nervous, but I was very much so.

                            We have discussed marriage arrangements a little. I have largely let her plan the ceremony, which she was, well, already planning. I believe she and I will both be in gowns, hers black mine white. My attempts to talk her into blue white and red did not work for me. There is some discussion of bridesmaids: We both want them. Just because I am marrying a woman does not mean I do not get to be a bride! Both having a maid of honor will be interesting, I would say Lorelei and Maria, but I am not sure Maria is willing to dress appropriately for Julia (I am unconcerned. A maid of honor need not wear a gown). We announced it (to Calamity’s annoyance) this morning.

                            I sort of want her to keep her old name, which surprised her. I am not going to take it, but I have little desire to force DeLisle on her. That said, the latter would be upsetting to my family if they ever found out, so it might even be worth it.

                            Of course, there is some issue within my “squad” as Lorelei rather directly boasted I would not be able to disrupt her performing, so I snuck over invisibly and, sadly, she was right. However, despite my never making such boasts, she took it upon herself to distract I performing in front of both my Monarch the Warlock of Night and Queen Charlotte. This despite my being less then completely skilled at what I am doing yet, and only third time out there ever. Unfortunately.

                            I am deeply displeased by this. Me messing with her doing something she does well on an off night and I doing everything I can to impress the Monarchs at something I am less skilled at only to be sabotaged by a loved one is deeply upsetting.

                            That’s two members of this Motley who have sabotaged me in less than a week. A gal could get rather paranoid.

                            It seems, perhaps, she has forgotten that it was I who filled the Lord of Day’s manor house with roaches and sewage. That the Night Court is known for it’s dastardly tricks and frightening pranks. While I considered corrupting, say, Dark Kimberly into causing a wardrobe malfunction as they say in America, I am not sure that would be best. After all, she caused me to misstep at doing something important to me that I have less skill at in front of my Monarchs. I am sure I can find something she is less good at to cause her to foul up in a very, very public way. Yes, I do have a few ideas already. While some say she is self-absorbed, she cannot possibly be so self-absorbed as to have no idea that sabotaging me in my most important performance would not anger me so.

                            My dreams were especially vivid last night. Vivid and intense. And now I feel stronger than ever before. Perhaps it is a reaction to my recent trials. I shall have to see if I can stretch my Wyrd and power a bit to see what happens.

                            Let’s see…I had an encounter with Desiree yesterday. It was fun. We spent a little time together a while back but everything fell apart soon after. It was good to do that again. She is a sweet girl. She asked me if I was trying to steal her from the director. Silly girl, that would be something happening in the future, not the past.

                            But I have a wedding to plan and hi-jinx to scheme. So little more time for writing. I think I am improving though. Perhaps I will add more thoughts later.

                            End Session LVI

                            Everyone is now Wyrd 6! Gawain is compelled to offer protection to strangers, Lorelei must sing if commanded, Calamity is injured by innocent blood, Jacob cannot harm children, Insincere Marguerite cannot walk on bare earth without removing her shoes.

                            Calamity performed Tainted Love as performed by Marilyn Manson, I note it as no journal stated the song.

Lori distracted her, and Insincere Marguerite was penalized on her Manipulation + Expression roll to perform based on Lori beating her resistance roll.
