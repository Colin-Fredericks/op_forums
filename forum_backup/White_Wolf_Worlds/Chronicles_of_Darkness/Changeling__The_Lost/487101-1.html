    Originally posted by branford View Post


    I think the point is that Resources alone may be insufficient to obtain silver ammunition, or that additional merits or skills may be required, such as a few dots in Crafts to make the ammunition on your own (and maintain the firearms ruined by the silver), or Contacts, Retainer or Allies (or Fixer, Esoteric Armory, etc.) for them to make or procure it for you. The cost and availability of silver ammunition exceeds the cost of just the silver due to its scarcity in your nWOD. Silver purchases and sales, capable gunsmiths, etc. may also be watched carefully by werewolves and their spirit and mundane spies and allies. After you procure the silver ammunition, it's entirely possible that characters may also promptly get a visit from local law enforcement, Homeland Security, mental health professionals, Task Force: Valkyrie, etc.

    Outfitting a platoon or other large group with regular sources of esoteric, no less relatively expensive, ammunition such as silver, may be the province of governments and large corporations (and Mages with Matter), and outside the purview of PC's without a very good explanation.

    Dots in Resources alone should not be able to solve all your more unusual procurement needs. It is not always a stand-in for other Merits.
    To add to this, yes, you could likely purchase a cheap box of "silver" ammo online. 90% of the time it'd be useless, or would completely ruin your firearm after the first shot.

                    Originally posted by nofather View Post
                    You can just buy silver coins or silverware at a pawn shop and melt it down, people make their own bullets on the cheap pretty easily as it is.

                    Remember that according to W:tF 2e, p. 101, silver weapons and ammunition must be at least 80% pure to gain the intended benefit against werewolves. Many of the silver coins that may be readily available, such as Kennedy half-dollars, are far below this threshold (usually only about 20-40% silver). Older, ~80% silver coins are much more expensive, rare, and their purchase easily traceable. Shooting a werewolf with "cheap" or impure silver will just get him angrier, and is not recommended!

                    Although normal bullets are relatively easy to make, it still requires Crafts and related skills and/or Merits besides Resources, either to manufacture yourself, or find someone to do it for you, which has its own complications and risks. Additionally, silver is a lousy metal for bullets, and hence requires better than average gunsmithing skills. Gun jams and exploding barrels are quick means of become werewolf kibble.

                                                        I actually did some research on this subject for a novel I wrote and am in the process of attempting to publish. Asking the proprietors of a local gunstore about silver bullets, the only thing they could really come up with is that, because silver is a little bit harder than lead, it might cause the gun barrel to deteriorate a little bit more quickly, to the point of having to replace the barrel after firing 1,000 rounds, instead of 10,000 as with "normal" ammo. Proper maintenance and cleaning will, of course, extend the barrel life of any gun.

                                                        As for actual Resources costs, I would say that functional silver ammo just doesn't exist. It's too expensive and not worth it. Any silver ammo will be hand-crafted, or produced on small-scale for those who find they require it fairly often (like better-funded Hunter groups.) If the PCs can make a contact that supplies them with ready-made silver bullets, add a dot or two to the normal ammo cost for a given gun. Otherwise, they have to obtain silver (use current price guidelines to establish how much silver they can buy with a given amount of Resources funds), and then roll Crafts to make decent bullets.
