                    While I agree that different courts might be a problem, I think the Storytelling section will likely have a juice section. I could tell certain themes among all of the mantle effects, so those won't be hard to homebrew. It shouldn't be hard to pick an Emotion for a given Court, and then a Condition. Mages hafta make their own Aura, so the ST has it slightly easier. Really, the biggest toughie will be Give and Take, Contracts, and to a lesser degree Crown effects. However, I think that they do indeed cover that, I just hope it has the wordcount it needed.

                    My biggest concern so far, besides the beaten dead horse that is the Kiths, which is solved by the Kith and Kin stuff... is the Hobgoblin issue. I like how theyre designed, but I bought the God Machine Chronicle book. I didn't get the Chronicles of Darkness. And if MOST of chronicles of darkness is the same as God Machine Chronicle, AND reprinted in the Changeling book.. Im wincing at the idea I'd have to buy the whole CofD core JUST to be able to interpret and design Hobgoblins. I mean, as it is right now, I might RARELY bust out a hobgoblin, and MORE often just throw Hedge Ghosts at my players. If I already have to buy the CofD core book just for the Dread Powers, why reprint it in the Changeling book? You can easily say "Buy the corebook" like 1e, and then get a LOT of extra wordcount for Changeling.. or edit out something. Like.. the 12 kiths.

                    Its kind of the same situation with them, really. An easy solution would be to take Kiths out of the core book, like what was done with Bloodlines. Mention that they exist, make them a second Z splat that can develop through play and exploring, shift all the kiths into the Supplement, and use that wordcount for the Dread Powers. Cuz those, as it stands, are more handy than the few Kiths we have, when most of us will be waiting for the Kith and Kin stuff to even finish our characters. At least as a Z splat, optional, but powerful, we won't feel so incomplete. We'll just have to get used to the Vampire//Promethean model and define our changeling with a vague X and Y and their powers, and then "double down" on our specific faeness with the Kith.

                    Similarly, if the Example Courts arent gonna have an Emotion, or Contracts, or a Crown etc.. why spend wordcount on the Mantle? That could also be switched into the supplement, if the dread powers need too much word count. Put various Courts in the supplement, with Mantle, Emotion, Contracts, Crown, Give and Take, etc. I don't think anyone would mind, especially since the backers will have access to that info easily enough from the previews, and actual buyers AFTER the kickstarter are gonna be seriously lacking in a lot of setting elements.

                    I really think info on Dread Powers right now is more valuable than the incomplete information we have on the optional Courts and the Kiths, and those things, as it stands, are better explored in supplements than the core. I mean, what are people gonna think if they just have the Core with its 12 kiths, and then LATER discover hundreds or at least dozens of premade, prebalanced, preexplained Kiths?
