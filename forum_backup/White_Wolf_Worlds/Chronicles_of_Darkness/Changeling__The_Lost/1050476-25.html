    Originally posted by Mr.F.I.X. View Post
    Should the 1e goblin market book still function for 2e?
    That book was mostly great for inspiration material so I don't see why not.

    Looking specifically at the book now.
    Chapter 1 should work (if you want it to) as written in 2nd Ed.

    Chapter 2 might need a bit of updating but not much.
    The things I see are:
    - Goblin Contract Price: basically the same but be aware that Goblin Debt should be a part of the process.
    - Actually Goblin Debt should be pretty much everywhere in this chapter (still a very minor update, just determine the Goblin Debt amount and who owns the debt (as an ST)
    - I'd include the merit "Fae Mount" in the "Servants, Pets, and Slaves" portion of the chapter as things you could potentially get.
    - Since Changelings don't use Virtue or Vice, the Vice portion could be adapted to things that have similar effects.
    - Pretty much all goblin contracts can prolly still work they just need a bit of updating (no dot rating, no catch, maybe someother stuff)

    Chapter 3 would need a tad bit of updating mostly for the kinds of contracts the sample characters have. But otherwise should be usable. The big part would be changing out their contracts me thinks.

                                                    Originally posted by lnodiv View Post

                                                    Taking something you made via Hedgespinning out of the Hedge makes it vanish unless you go through one of the processes it takes to make any other kind of token.

                                                    2e manuscript quiestion regarding true fey, are true fey immune to non-bane damage unless its true name is invoked or it breaks an oath?
                                                    True Fae are immune to bashing damage except from banes; they're immune to lethal damage except from banes unless those things happen; the only thing that causes them aggravated damage is cold iron, which works no matter what (as it's a bane).
