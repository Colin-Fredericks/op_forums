    Mastermind

    Update to my Villian Kith. Just a new name.

    Masochists.

    The Masochists and Sadists are the Gentry's attempt at understanding human pain.

    Masochists: The Masochists gain pleasure for pain, but also power. They bounce back from a beating faster then other Changelings.

    Fairest: Golden Piecings, and glowing whip marks in gorgeous, erotic patterns that change often.

    Beast: Feline features, with a collar and cuffs, purrs when hurt.

    Elemental: Skin is one giant scar, cracked and bleeding.

    Blessing: Any time the Masochist takes 3 bashing or 1 lethal damage they gain a point of glammer. By spending a point of Glamour they can regenerate like a werewolf for a scene.

    Sadists: Generally unpopular with other Changeling except those with gruesome Kiths and oddly Masochists who are drawn to Sadists like moths to flames.

    Orgers: Covered in blood splatter that never washes off and surrounded by the screams of their victims at all times.

    Darkling: Skin like supple black leather, eyes that sparkling and glow then they cause pain.

    Wizened: Shaped like a mobile ironmaiden with arms and legs, some always seems to be someone struggling and crying within the Wizened.

    Blessing. You can summon a whip or riding crop by spending a point of Glamour. You have a free speciality in whips and riding crops. A Changeling has to spend twice as much glamour to get free if you tie them up or imprison them.

    Heirophants

    True Fae often loved to be worshipped. All Heirophants have the symbol of their Keepers on their foreheads, but in none Loyalists that Symbol is upside down to express they're rebellion.

    Fairest: WIngs of Light and glowing eyes, with a glowing the symbol of their Keeper on their forheads.

    Darkling: Batwing made of Shadow, red skin, and massive horns, and their Keepers symbol on their foreheads written in shadows and flame.

    Wizened: Flesh like parchment with the holy words of their Keeper written on their skin in blood, and a metal holy symbol embedded in their forehead.

    Blessing: You gain the Lay on Hands Merit, you have a free occult specialty in your Keeper.

    Grim Reapers: Some Changeling refuse to make a deal with death in Arcadia, the True Fae used the Grim Reapers to convince those Changelings to make deals with death.

    Darkling: Fact like a skull and robes of shadows.

    Beasts: Jackle headed Anubis like Grim Reapers, with Heiroglyph tattoos expressing great truths about dying.

    Fairest: Looking like a beautiful corpse straight from the Morturary's makeup artist, they don't appear to breath and when cut, they bleed perservatives, not blood.

    Blessings: Spend a point of glamour and touch a target to block them from magical healing during the scene. If the target dies during that scene they can choose not to come back if they have a resurrection power.

    Slob: Why the True Fae choose some people to become Slobs is unknown.

    Elemental: A pile of debris in human form, they keep dropping random peices of debris all over the place, which disppears when they get to far from it.

    Orger: Overweight with poor hygine, and a noise that always seems to be dripping with snot.

    Beast: A dirty Racoon, Rat, or Pigeon, with animal droppings scatter all around them.

    Blessings: Spend a Glamour and have someone of your choice within 30 feet trip on a discarded piece of your mien. With a touch learn any taboos they might have.

            Paleos
            Time ain't what you think it is, kid. Not in There. I walked an ancient world. All the stuff you ever learned in school is the stripped-down 'kids' movie fairy tale' version of it. Monsters that shook the earth. Screeching not-birds wheeling in the sky. Deserts so vast they are oceans, oceans so vast whole worlds could swim between 'em. I was a monster there, too. And I liked it.

            The True Fae weren't always "the Gentry." They're prehistoric, haunting the dreams of mortals since the days of Paleolithic hunters and perhaps before. The scariest stories tell that the Fae intentionally spurred the dreams that inspired humans to grow, that they might have more interesting playthings. Even in those ancient days the True Fae captured mortals and took them to impossibly vast wildernesses born of primal nightmares. The Paleos are just that: remnants of the creatures and powerful elemental forces that raged so fiercely in this prehistoric paradise. More than most other changelings they return to a time far removed from the world they knew. How they can return when this is the case is a matter of endless debate, but the echoing laughter of now-refined Gentry follows these living fossils out of their lost world.

            • Beast: Armored skin, spikes, fangs, glowing reptilian eyes. A Beast that would give dinosaurs nightmares.
            • Elemental: Craggy skin like igneous rock, emitting noxious volcanic fumes with every breath.
            • Fairest: Tall, Amazonian in build, clad in a raiment of glittering fronds and animal hides that move as though alive.
            • Ogre: A saurian behemoth, its hide pitted and scarred with murals that depict her many battles.
            • Wizened: This creature is swift, short, and covered in fur, with wide yellow eyes that let its clever fingers work in the dark.

            Blessing: Hailing from the savage primordial Faerie, Paleos are rugged in a way that few modern changelings can match. Scaled armor or thick fur grant the changeling 1/1 armor. The character gains a +3 bonus to Speed and perception rolls.

                            Here is my first attempt at a kith. Banshee

                            Don’t ever laugh as a hearse goes by, for you may be the next to die.

                            Being immortals the True Fae are fascinated by death. Not just the concept of being extinguished/ceasing to be, but the emotions surrounding death. All Banshee’s are created from mortals touched by death in some way; the grief a mortal feels when losing a loved one, the guilt a soldier feels when they survived, but their unit did not, the horror of someone who has cancer growing in them. The True Fae took this connection to death and made it part of the changeling’s kith.
                            Banshee’s Lament: Banshee’s can feel death around them. They see the death marks on mortals and feel/experience a death that is currently happening near by. Finally, by spending a point of glamour, they can give a target, usually a mortal, a vision of their death.

                            Darkling: Ghostly apparitions whose wail foretells your coming death.
                            Fairest: Beautiful Maidens of the Sidhe
                            Wizened: The Washer Woman who cleans your blood stained clothes.

                                                        Having discussed Changeling crossover chronicles in another thread, I thought I would try my hand at a crossover kith. I shared some of my thoughts on Changeling vampires so here is my attempt at that.

                                                        Red Stained

                                                        There are many legends that link Fairies to vampires. The Baobhan sith lured unwary travelers with their charming looks to feast on their blood, the Glaistig, and the Red Caps who stained their hat’s in the blood of their victims. The revelation of the existence of vampires has led some of the Lost to believe that these legends are about vampires who were turned into Changelings. This is partly incorrect. Because the Kindred gave up their souls, the Gentry tend to regards vampires the way humans regard rats or cockroaches. Many True, however, are curious about their ghoul servants and half-damned offspring, the Dhampires. The Red Stained are created from former ghouls and dhampires to explore the vampire curse’ connection to life and the underworld.
                                                        Darklings: Red Caps, Venezuelan La Sayona, The One Legged South American Patasola.
                                                        Elementals: Slavic Samodiva
                                                        Fairiest: Scottish Baobahan sith, Indonesian Sundal Bolong, Russian Rusalka,
                                                        The Hunger: The Red Stained blessing mimics the powers of vampires to a certain degree. They can harvest glamor by drinking blood, and have retractable fangs, and a healing saliva to leave no traces of a wound. Their ability to smell blood is as keen as a sharks and they gain +4 to all perception roles in an area where blood has been spilled. Finally, when they taste a victims vitae, they gain a great deal of genetic information on the victim.

                                                            The Daemonic

                                                            Some legends about the Fae claim that they are demons or fallen angels. These changelings and their Keepers are a manifestation of this belief. Ironically, they were not demon worshipers or occult scholars as mortals. They tend to be men and women of great Christian faith or innocence; pious priests, strict and severe nuns, innocent and chaste catholic school girls. The True Fae tried to corrupt/destroy their Christian faith by turning them into demons and forcing them to participate in some of the most depraved acts.
                                                            The Devils Tongue: The Daemonic possess one permanent demonic feature to better represent their infernal masters. They also have the 4 dot fame merit, in that mortals feel comfortable sharing their darkest secrets/desires with them. Finally, by spend a dot a glamor they can force a mortal to give in to their darkest desires.
                                                            Elementals: Namaru, Asharu, Annunaki,and Lammasu.
                                                            Fairiest: Lucifer
                                                            Wiziened: Goblins and imps
