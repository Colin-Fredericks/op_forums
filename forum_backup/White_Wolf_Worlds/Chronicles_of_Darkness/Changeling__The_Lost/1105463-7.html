            You could characterize the Clarity rules as "gain" and "loss," but that's not really how I'd describe them. Clarity isn't CoC's Sanity meter or something. It's the act of defying two worlds trying to gaslight you -- so it can be challenged, it can be hurt, but it can also be healed. And that can happen at different levels -- something can be a mild challenge to your Clarity, or a severe one. And following from that, Clarity, as the right of a thinking being, isn't so much gained as healed.

            You'll see more of this later, but Clarity is a positive personal trait about owning your own perceptions when the True Fae (or goblins, or other magic, or even sometimes mundane things) try to lie to you and get you to doubt that your experiences and values are true.
