                Then let's provide context. Elementals are the Eye of the Storm. Maybe they are the eye because they deeply understand it by being the storm, the wildfire, the thunderstorm, the flash flood.                             Originally posted by shkspr1048 View Post
                            Yes, very clever (as expected of an Adams work), but how does that fit in as an example of reverse causality?
                            The conventional understanding of causality is that effects are justified by their causes, and thus most people envision an effect and then reverse-engineer the course of action needed to cause it.

                            Reverse causality as typified by Clarity of Forces instead justifies causes by their effects, and thus Elementals create a cause which later provides an effect that affirms its necessity.

                            Resident Lore-Hound

                                        But, given the whole "big fish in a small pond" thing, I honestly can't see how the Maker Kith is just inherently wrong for the Elementals either.

                                        The girl who is really big into the Maker/DIY scene, because she's good at it and receives lots of praise for it and knows she's good at it, until a rival comes along and bests her, taking her praise, to the point that she would do anything to get it back. And so she agrees to a strange commission without reading the fine print and ends up in Arcadia, remolded into a Steampunk C&C Machine, creating her masterpieces for her Keeper who holds her until she produces her promised "Ultimate Masterpiece." She creates many things of unsurpassed skill, but still the Keeper is unsatisfied. It is not until she realizes that her own ego and desire for glory are keeping her too focused on the wrong aspects of design, that she can only create great art once she gives herself to it, body and soul, that she is able to break her heart and transcend her skills, to produce something so wonderful that the Keeper has to agree that it is the promised "ultimate Masterpiece" and allowing her to return home.

                                        ....Ok, so, maybe you could argue that works better as a Wizened or something, and I'm not entirely sure you can actually escape a Keeper like that, but I think it fits the described Elemental background and durance reasonably well. In her case, the Element would be something like Craft. And, while the resulting changeling would probably be rather obsessed with making things, to the point of ignoring the emotional (and possibly physical) needs of others, she's not likely to attract extreme sports enthusiasts as hangers on.
