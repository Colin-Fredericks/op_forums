    Vampire the Masquiem

    05-07-2016, 05:44 PM
    Premises
    The world is both old and new. The creatures who infest it are old, they remember times gone by, perhaps not always clearly but sometimes with the uncanny clarity of a senile old man reminiscing his childhood memories. They remember the voivodes in their Carpathian castles, the corpse-kings in ancient Egypt as well than the shadow-binders of Thera. Their Sects are new, be they proud Invicitii whose shadow looms over the mortal world or the mysterious Ordo Dracul seeking to remake vampires into better, more efficient monsters. The interaction between the old and the new is not without conflict but they can resolve themselves with a bit of work.

    Many are the faces of the Damned, many are the stories the human kine tell about them. However, the Kindred, as the vampires named their species, share some traits despite all their differences, some strengths and weaknesses beyond the boundaries of clan and sect. They share their death and the Blood, the Banes and the Beast.

    First and most obvious, each and every Kindred is a blood-drinking corpse. They are dead things, in body as in mind. The transition from living to undead does strange things to human minds and most of the vampire's population suffer from slight mental problems. Their emotions become more sluggish and more violent, more sluggish because they are predators and their minds understand subconsciously this fact and try to shield themselves against guilt and regrets. More violent because of the Beast we will talk about. Physically, vampiric organs will not work without the power of blood, they have no lungs to draw poison from the air and find no nourishment in mortal food.

    Vampires drink blood. The statement is simple but its consequences reach far and wide. Blood is no gruel or stale water to vampires’ lips but it offers a rush of pleasure without peer. When the blood pours down the throat, a vampire feels alive again in all senses of the word. Thanks to blood, the vampire can go on their unlife, thanks to blood; the vampire can fuel their powers. Indeed, vampiric disciplines, powers different for each lineage requires the expenditure of blood to function. Fortunately, they transform the vampire into a more powerful predator needing only few efforts to replenish its reserves.

    With their shared thirst, vampires share some weaknesses. Even if their undead flesh is more resilient than mortal skin, fire burns them more easily. The sun, even the false sun of a winter day, or the first lights of dawn burns them too. In fact, vampires have to consciously spend blood to remain awake in the day, most of the time they slumber in dreamless sleep. That is a weakness too, as a vampire grows older and older; the weight of centuries forces her to sleep more and more. The first torpor spans ten years, the sleep of the ancients is counted in millennia.

    Most dreadful among the Kindred banes is the Beast. Each vampire crawls from the Embrace with a dark thing nested in his or her skin. The name “Beast” is a bit of a misnomer, while this thing wants certainly to destroy a vampire’s humanity, most are more sadistic than an animal would be. The Beast is responsible for “frenzy” a state where the vampire is a mindless predator, tearing apart the thing that angered him or fleeing the fire or the sun. Some clans’ Beasts more powerful than others, Nosferatu and Gangrel come to mind for slightly different reasons.

      The Clans
      Thirteen clans are recognized among the Damned. This number is strangely stable and many intellectuals rave about the sacred meaning of such a number. According to Noddist beliefs more prevalent in Europe, the thirteen clans are descendants of thirteen ancestors, ancient vampires of godlike power. Others places have others legends but generally the thirteen clans recognized are the same they are:


      The Assamites
      Something dead approaches and you are not its prey. It comes with the bearing of a predator, extending its tongue to feel the scent, jumping across rooftops in some inhuman acrobatics for the quarry he seeks is not human. Be thankful for it is quick, quickest than anything dead has any right to be, closing distances in one jump and running the fastest among you through. Be thankful for he can mask his presence to be no one or a single quick forgotten face in the crowd until the moment he strikes. Be thankful for few mortals are the victims of their secret art who drives the blood of their very skin into their throat or change itself into deadly poison in the dead veins of the Kindred. If you are one of these creatures, you will be reviled by those of your kin who can see the true nature of things for you will reek of death and you will know a thirst unabated by mortal blood.


      The Cappadocians:
      Something dead approaches and at the first sight, you know it is dead. Cold and hard are their eyes, pale, paler than the less lifelike of ghosts is their skin. It wants you dead, not just for the blood that runs in your veins but simply because you are alive and to its undead eyes this is an error that must be rectified. You will try to hide but these eyes pierce all disguises and all hideouts to find their prey. You will try to kill it but this flesh is strengthened by the grave and does not give away. You will try to fight but a simple touch will rot your living flesh and turn your entrails to goo. If you are one of these scholars, you will know the true meaning of death each time you gaze upon your mirror and each time you see with indifference the winged soul jump out of a body.


      The Daeva:
      Something dead approaches and it is beautiful beyond compare. It comes to you like a dream of stone made of flesh and you bow to eyes who never laugh or weep. You are enthralled by them, you see them fair and beautiful, creating wonders with their hands and songs and poetry and great works of art. This thing will not hunt you or chase you; you will come to them and bow without treachery or falsehood. You will be their slave and their love, as they will break you for their own pleasure and to forget they are dead while you are alive and breathing. Even if you wanted to, you could not fight them. Their presence is enthralling and addictive, forcing you to attend them in worship. They can see your soul and discern falsehood from truth and so no plots can be hidden from them very long. And even if you engaged them in battle, they are quicker than the last beat of a dying heart. However, of you accept their offer and be one of them, you will be broken for you will understand what you gave up. You will be entranced by beauty even by mad or cruel beauty and stay as a statue, unmoved and speechless when you will encounter it.



      The Gangrel:
      Something dead approaches and you know it is not human. Red as the forbidden sun are their eyes and clawed are their hands. If you are lucky, you will not have to see further deviation but even a single look to the least mutated among them hints at future changes. These things are no better than snarling beasts; they will hunt you for sport and food, making you into an animal before tearing your throat for your warm blood. You will try to hide but birds and beasts will report to their master of your movements. You will try to defend yourself, these things can be mist or bat or snake and pass through all locks and all gates. You will try to kill it but dead flesh is more durable than it appears. If you are one of these animals, you will see how fragile the border between man and beast is. You will see how your actions are reflected on your corpse until each atrocity, every instance of frenzy claim its toil and you are unable to pass as human anymore.


      The Iliesy
      Something dead approaches and you will never know it is dead. How bright they shine in the night, passion flaring in their red eyes, brightening their dead faces like the forbidden sun. It is dangerous. Not because it is strong and, believe me, it is strong enough to break necks and tear limbs out of their sockets. It is not dangerous because it is fast and it is as fast as the last heartbeat of a dying man. It is dangerous for its passion entices and seduces. You will listen to them; you will listen to their songs and speeches and do whatever makes them happy. You will take their causes at their own, bare your throat and drink their blood hoping to find freedom where only slavery lies. Perhaps you will find the will to fight them for their gaze is not an unbreakable chain but in doing so you will face their wrath and know the true meaning of rage. To be one of them is to know the burning need not of the Thirst but of unshackled feelings. You will know how thin the border between sage and ravening beast is and fear this knowledge.


      The Lasombra:
      Something dead approaches and the darkness comes with it. It styles itself a queen or a priest and with each step she takes, the shadow lingers around her like a shrouded crown. Even the nimblest of them is strong enough to break necks and tear hearts out of chests but their strength is not their most frightening. Their eyes have the power to rob you of your will until you applaud at what grieves your soul and bear arms for these creatures but their gaze is not their most frightening weapon. The very shadows obey them as they mold them into openings to some strange abyssal realm where you can only hear the flapping of great wings and the skulking of worms. To be such a creature is to have no reflection in the mirrors, to know with each gaze that you are not human anymore. It is to be frightened at your power and ask yourself where the darkness you command comes from and what its terrible sentience wants.


      The Malkavians:
      Something dead approaches and sanity dies with its steps. You recoil from them instinctively but in your head, you still hear the old admonishments warning that the madman is blessed or touched by the divine. Some speak to the air, some hearken to voices nobody hears, some are joyful a moment and sad the others. None is funny when you meet them in the night. They know things they should not, your deepest held secrets, your true nature; they will know it and they will cry it to the world in riddles and metaphors. When they wish it, their presence dims and they can caper and jeer among the beggars and the poor with none the wiser. Anger them and they will breach the sanctity of your mind and you will know what it is to be like them. You will see as they see and your mind will shatter like a broken mirror under the strain of the truths you will learn. To be one of these wretches is to be honored and reviled for madness may be a curse more painful than deathly pallor or addiction to sin but it may also be a blessing to let you continue through the night innocent of the suffering you cause.


      The Mekhet:
      Something dead approaches and you will know something is off before your mind registers it consciously. That is if you see your tormentor. Most of the time they will be unseen, sticking in the shadows out of even the pale glow of streetlights, but sometime even the spider must leave its web. By their servants you will know them, smelling of copper and shed blood, they will gather in worship to gods and angels they don’t understand, or cater to seers who exact terrifying prices for their insights. They are quick these Shadows and always hidden beneath a borrowed visage or simply the cold mist. However, it is their Sight that makes them welcomed among the Kindred for they see many things without the madness of the Malkavian. To be one of these Shadows is to be one with the darkness, to stalk the world unheard and unseen. It is to feed on strays and on easily forgotten prey while you’ll learn many secrets. Unfortunately to be sheltered by darkness is to fear light until even the flickering flame of a campfire frightens you.


      The Nosferatu:
      Something dead approaches and it is terrifying. Perhaps it is something in its appearance. Doubtful while it can be hideous as a nightmare, it can also seem normal but always off-putting. You will have visions when you look into their eyes, limbs with too many joints or bald elongated faces. Sometimes you will shrug off these fears but if they want you, you will surrender yourself to holy terror. To struggle against them, bat and rats are their spies and their hands during the days. Hunt them? They clad themselves in rags, mists, and foreign faces. Face them? They are fear and nightmares incarnate to mortals or Kindred alike for different reasons. In their eyes, the damned see the Beasts that twist their bodies and urge them to bloody excesses. However, most of them refuse to surrender, to forsake decency and morality and become the monsters others wish them to be. If you are one of these beasts, you will never escape your monstrosity and for that, perhaps you will never be drawn to the atrocities of your kind.


      The Ravnos:
      Something dead approaches and you cannot be sure of what it is. It comes to you dancing and singing, as it was not dead. You cannot help but see it come from outside as owl and wild dogs attend to it. Yet it smiles to you as it tempts you to evil. This thing will not hunt you or stalk you. He could, after all the beasts of the sky and the fields bear him news. No, it lies in wait in its den. You will come as they provide you with every vice under the moon. You will come and marvel at the tables bearing the feasts, the tapestries hanging along the masterworks. Then you will see the curtain rise and die. To fight these liars? They walk during the day under the skin of a lesser beast, the same beasts that will feed them if you harry them out of town. Their skin will harden under your blows. However, their greatest strength and their greater weakness is their capacity to weave the illusion of the worlds in convincing lies. If you are one of them, you will find yourself drawn to sin and degradation as your Beast lies to you as you lie to others.


      The Strigoi:
      Something dead approaches and it reeks of old secrets and strange gods. If you are familiar with the magicians and sorcerers of this world you will see that the dead thing before you claim kinship with them. They strut hidden from all but their eyes yearn for secrets to discover, new magic to master. What are the traits of the witch after all? She sees all, enthralls men by her glance and disappears from the view and wrath of the common people. Therefore, it is with these vampires: Meet their gaze and you will see all your secrets and lies laid bare while a stranger will bears into your skull to reshape you to its liking. Seek them and they are not here or there, or perhaps they are draped in stolen faces and illusions. To fight these beasts, even the rare of the brood who do not delve in blood sorcery is to court madness. You will not find them unless they choose to ensnare you into a trap. Your plans will not stay hidden from their gaze and your friends will become your foes. To be one of these monsters is to know slavery like no other who walks the night. Obsesssion will guide you, obsession for secrets found only in the dark. All your control and power will fade in presence of so raw a need it'll compel you to kneel before blaspehmous idols, compile insidious texts, know more of the things between heaven and earth a sane mind would want to know.


      The Tziminsce:
      Something dead approaches and you are not sure of what it is. Its flesh moves and changes almost under your very eyes announcing proudly the nature of the beasts. They are regal these monsters even if they have no innate means to compel obedience or attract worship. They are the masters of their own bodies though, the masters of their dead flesh and withered bones. One is beautiful beyond all compare, stalking the night like a fallen angel, enthralling all by his looks alone. Another has abandoned all pretenses of humanity. She has reshaped herself in a terrible creature whose bear more resemblance to an alien traveler coming from distant cold stars than to anything else. What are the traits these things emulate? They see everything under the skies, even bearing down the lines of power that crisscross the land. They are kin to the beast of the fields and of the skies. They transform their flesh and those of others. And so are these Fiends. To fight one of them retrenched in her castle is folly. Indeed, he knows you’re coming as you leave your home. Your movements are reported by birds and wolves and animals harass you. You’ll fight demented creations, born from an insane mind and if you fall you’ll join them. To be one of these old murderers is to be rived to one place throughout your own life. The soil of your grave will be necessary for you to rest fitfully and to be weak and irritable if you are deprived of it.


      The Ventrue:
      Something dead approaches and it lifts its head high for it knows nothing can harm it. She wears a crown on her head, of tarnished gold and sunless jewels. He wears robes of royal purple stained with the blood of his feasts. He gazes upon you in hunger but more often than not your blood will be mud on his lips. But she craves your obedience nonetheless, that you kneel and offers great obeisance. What are the traits of the tyrant after all? They are resistant, throning in a sea of enemies without relinquishing their powers. They sway by their speech the masses to applaud to what their hearts detest. They want everyone to kneel before them, acknowledging no equals but only superiors or inferiors. Like all despots these vampires are more enduring than cockroaches, able to shrug off the blade of the enemy, the poison of the assassin, the torch of the rebel with ease. They are charismatic, swaying the masses with false emotion, acquiring fidelity with their raw aura rather than their ideas. And if that falls their eyes carry the most powerful of their disciplines, their gaze enthralls letting them hollow their slaves to talk by their mouths and act by their hands. To be one of them is however to knows the torture of thirst. It is to choose a type of vessels in your first nights and to know only one taste, one nourishment and to know the pain of Tantalus when your chosen chalices are absent.

          The Sects
          Although clans are families and lineage linked by blood, they are generally not the social groups of the Kindred. While shared disciplines and weaknesses bred familiarity and kinship they don’t answer the burning questions every vampire asks at least once in her unlife: What does my life means? What do I want to do with my eternity? And how I meet people interested by the same goals. The Sects are an answer to these questions. Five of them are global conspiracies with all the links and networking undeath’ supernatural powers can forge. Rare is the vampiric domain where they are completely inexistent and rarer is the domain where the local societies and lodges don’t assume the psychological and societal rules of the Great Five. Other Sects exist in the world, some of them are also global but don’t have the influence to really be part of the “default” Kindred society, most of them are only present at the regional level even if they can completely rule it.


          Vampires societies and Networking: Most of the Sects can seem very strange for they fuse a global hierarchy with popes and emperors and councils of respected Elders and at the same time can seem very decentralized. To solve this paradox, look at the state of coordination among mortals before phones, rail transport or the internet were widespread and double the difficulties for creatures unable to travel by day. While hierarchy always existed, each Sect tended and always tend to an extent to have multiples heads acting as supreme authority on national level. Minor Sects being more geographically restrained tend to be more centralized.


          Whenever a society arises some wish to control it. But how to control a society of predators? What laws must you use to chain beasts believing themselves to be human? How can you rule, or at least be safe from, mortals when you are forced to sleep during the day? If you ask these questions, if the need to be part of the elite is as strong as it was when you were alive and if your lust for power is a keener thirst than your need for the blood of the living, then you should knock at the doors of the Invictii, the Unconquered who defined what power means in the Dance Macabre since centuries uncounted. They say they are the heirs to the Camarilla, “the small debating chamber” who ruled the nights of Rome just as the Caesars ruled the days. They escaped the ruin of their first empire brought down by corruption and flame to emerge on the top of the new social order and how they cling to their position. The Traditions are their invention, the roles you’ll consider natural in your unlife such as Prince, Harpy or Priscus were invented by them and enforced by this simple truth: Elders walking among younger vampires are power. Their other gift to the Kindred is the Masquerade and its enforcement. The Conspiracy of Silence as the Sect is often called is determined to hide the existence of vampires from the herd and rule from the shadows. Indeed, despite many an Anarch’s speech most of the concentrated power of these ancient parasites who form the courts of the Sect is bent to the obfuscation of the supernatural from mortal life. He is a blood-slave to the Invictii the renowned author who publishes books detailing inexact vampiric lore and signs. She’s of the Invictii this Prince who use her mortal connections in politics to maintain a sizeable population who won’t be missed in her city. In both cases, the monsters understand they must be hidden if they want to rule. But if they are hidden, they can rule forever and ever.



          The Sanctum Lanceae Sanctae: What does it mean when you rise from your grave as a bloodthirsty monster? What does it mean when you thirst for the blood of the living while every temptation is now slithering in your head with so sweet a voice? What happened to the soul you perhaps never believe in while you were alive? Are you damned now, a parasite subsisting from humanity’s refuse and condemned to wander until Final Death or the End of Days? If you ask these questions, if you think you can be more than the urges given to you by the Embrace and still serve the powers of goodness in this world, then you should attend one of the Sanctum Sanctae Lancea’s masses. The Sanctuary of the Holy Lance is but the Christian face of a heresy present in many religions, some not monotheistic. The main body of the Sect will tell you they were founded by Longinus the centurion who struck Christ the deadly blow and was granted death eternal for his sin. They will tell you that you are Damned by your actions while living for none is innocent. They will tell you of your new role in the cosmos, of the atonement present in every damnation even yours. You are a dread example of the price of sin and it’s your role to demonstrate this price to humanity. Sin has changed definitions for it is not sinful for a demon and a tempter to indulge but only to indulge wrongly. You listen to their sermons, witness the dark miracles of the sorcery they received from heaven, this sorcery that enables the different faces of the Sect to not murder each other over theological differences and then you are sent in the world. Will you haunt lover lanes and campuses providing bloody examples of the danger of teenage fornication? Will you tempt virtuous leaders to sin to assess their worth and witness their holiness? To embrace the Satan’s two urges, temptation and judgement is to belong to the Sanctuary whatever your faith.


          The Circle of the Crone: What does it mean to be dead and yet living? What does it mean to be insensate and yet so hungry? What does it mean to rise from your death and yet be unable to taste the touch of the sun or the taste of food? Are you like the dead now, unchanging and unfeeling save for echoes of your old emotion, chained to the two rocks of Humanity and the Beast? Is there a place for you in the world where you can let loose without losing yourself? A place to feel even if it’s to feel pain and burning and ecstasy? If you ask these questions, if your steps lead you from civilization to the wilderness, then you will find one of the manifold cults of the Circle of the Crone. They are a loose confederation of ancient religions and outlawed cults teaching the only truth of existence: You must change, change and change until change becomes what you are for none, god or mortal, can catch that which is formless. The servants of the Mother of Monsters will teach you to revel, to feel and to be reborn in pain and pleasure. Their theology, as much as they have one, is one of birth and violence, of violent change and the necessity for pain. Most are pagan but some can worship at the altar of Lilith. They will tell you your state is not a curse but a blessing, a gift or a natural evolution. They will encourage you to find your own moral allowing you to reconcile the Beast with ambitions and thought and don’t judge the result. You can become like the beasts, hunting only to feed, without cruelty and birthing monsters like Echidna of old. Or you can, like Lilith cultivate your garden of suffering, seeking ecstasy in the sweet release of agony and teaching to the mortals the true meaning of the “little death”. In any case you will adapt and survive for that is the lot of all creatures of the earth, living and unliving.


          The Anarchs Movement: Does being dead means languish in dismal slavery to those who are older than you? Does not being human anymore means you have to renounce all the benefits of modernity? Are you, as a monster, forced to condone the servitude of your brothers to old monsters older than your nation’s entire existence? Are you doomed to a meaningless descent from humanity until the only things holding your soul together will be oaths you don’t believe in? Can things really change, can the society of the night become a utopia? Are the dreams of a world meant to vampires and not humans only dream? If you ask yourself these questions and that fire burns in your heart, then welcome to the Anarch Movement. Don’t be fooled by the name, some of them are anarchists it’s true but all political systems diverging from the vampiric norm are welcome here from communism to hardcore libertarianism. They will hold rallies to explain their doctrines, they will debate of their respective merits and put them to the votes. At least that what they do when they are allowed to exist openly. When they are suppressed then you will be taken to secret reunions, partake of all things forbidden by the tyrants who rule the nights. Consorting with mortals to fight the elders? Drinking the heart’s blood of your enemies? Bonding your group by chains of blood until you are a hydra with many heads and limbs? All these things are done nightly by the Movement and without a hint of regret. It’s normal. After all freedom is a whore you must force on a bed of corpses and the tree of liberty must be watered with the blood of tyrants. Some of the most ambitious among you dream to reogarnize the very cities to suit your needs rather than humanity’s. You can be one of them, making life worse for the herd to gather a viable food supply or you can be one of the great dreamers who want every mortal of the city offering a drop of their blood to your immortal thirst. Anyway you are sure of one thing: Whoever is against you will meet the sun.


          The Ordo Dracul: Is being a vampire a condemnation to languish in the same state forever and ever? Will you always fear the fire and the sun and the Beast in your breast? Is there not any means to change for the better, perhaps to become human again, or perhaps simply to enjoy the perks of becoming a better type of monster? Are you content to remain as you are, undead subjects to bane, forced to drink blood just to survive and not to fuel your magnificent powers? Are you interested by all things hidden in this world of darkness, from spirit coves to witches covens and the like? If yes, the Ordo Dracul is ready to welcome your research and share with you their own results. Formed from the remains of the Black Hand, a secret society of alchemists and sorcerers, after their taking over by the famous Dracula, the Ordo is determined to unlock the secrets of the vampiric condition. Their ideology revere changes but shackles it to the will of the adept, forcing evolution and adaptation from the undying carcass of the acolyte. Their initiations are grueling even considering how vampire’s sects love pain and fear. To be a Dragon you must be afraid of nothing and learn to cow your bestial urges. To seize a brand of fire despite the instinctive revulsion, to be beaten near unconsciousness to develop supernatural resilience, to force yourself to subsist on a diet of animal blood. These are examples of the Ordo’s teachings. For agonizing they are, they work and those who master the Coils of the Dragon or the Mysteries develop capacities unheard of in the others sects. There are rumors of old Dragons subsisting on the lifeblood of animals, going to sleep well in the day and waking up at twilight. Still it’s not enough, it’s never enough. To be one of them is to be asked to devote yourself to improvement. Of course the most evident way is to chastise and punish your dead body to force the change but you can also explore the world of darkness and experiment on its many denizens. In the end you will spit in the eye of the Almighty and he will allow it.

              Belial’s Brood : The Brood of Belial doesn’t exist. Not officially in any case. Indeed, it’s a name present in accusations throughout Kindred’ history just as “Belial’s synagogue” was an accusation in Judeo-Christian circles. Not all the heretics designed by this slur die the Final Death and enough of them take the name hurled against them as a badge of pride. Thus there are vampires embraced or joining organizations calling themselves Belial’s Brood or a local variant thereof. Some of these cults feed each other doctrines and goals resulting in a web of interconnected societies not dissimilar to the Circle of the Crone but with very different doctrines. Some of them are worshippers of the dark side of the supernatural world. Considering themselves beyond redemption they accept their role and kneel before demons, dark spirits and shadowy owls. They seek to emulate these beings in all their inhumanity and work their goals on earth. Whether that means instituting a sacrificial cult among the kine or summoning their patrons in the flesh, or even surrendering their own corpses to their masters is irrelevant to most of them. Others try to become the Beast, not at the manner of the Crones who hold it as a source of power, but in the goal of becoming a monster not moved by instinct but by diabolical intelligence. Others still are moved by pure instinct and don’t put limits to the flows of their desires. None of them want to become the kind of animal those lost to the Beast often are. So they find ways to anchor themselves to the Beast, to places of evil and tainted artifacts, their attachment to not say obsession with such items help them become monsters and not mere animals. Still they listen to their inner demon more often than the average vampire and that what makes them unwelcome and outcasts among the society of the night. Some revile them because they serve dark entities whose feeding habits make even jaded monsters raise eyebrows. Some revile them because they embody a temptation in being completely free but completely monstrous. Other revile them because they revile all trucking with the Beast.
