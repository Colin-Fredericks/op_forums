    Home-brew Merit: Off Hand Training

    08-19-2016, 04:48 PM
    Off Hand Training (Weaponry or Firearms) •• or •••
    Not everyone is born Ambidextrous, but through dedicated training some may learn to fight as if they were.

    Prerequisite: Resolve •••, Composure •••, and Weaponry or Firearms ••
    Effect: When this Merit is taken choose if it is for Firearms or Weaponry. You may take this Merit Twice, once for Firearms, and again for Weaponry.The •• version of this Merit allows you to reduce the off hand penalty for either Firearms or Weaponry to -1. The ••• Version of this Merit allows you to negate the off hand penalty for either Weaponry or Firearms.

    Notes:
    Ambidextrous is the better merit because it does more for less, but unlike Ambidextrous Off Hand Training can be learned after character creation so long as a character has the discipline (Resolve 3 & Composure 3) and skill (Firearms or Weaponry 2) to work towards overcoming the disadvantages of using their off hand.

    I designed this Merit with the idea of some Age of Sail Character Types potential running amok with a cutlass in one hand and a pistol in the other, though I believe it could also be useful for wild west gunslingers, or any character who often finds themselves using their off-hand. It is not a dual wielding Merit, but simply a better at using your off hand in combat merit.
