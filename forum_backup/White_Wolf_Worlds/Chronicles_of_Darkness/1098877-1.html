For what it's worth, I think the changes there were an improvement. Also Contracts in general seem to have Regalia now, which hints at changes to Contracts and Seemings, though we don't yet know to what extent. Changes to Courts mean changes to Huntsmen, unless the new version somehow incorporates Yearning, though again we don't know to what extent. And it's possible other things might change that we can't infer from the latest previews.

                                            It's all those "we don't know"s that give me anxiety. If I at least knew what not to bother hoping will remain intact, I could switch gears and get ready to hype myself up for Rose's game. But as long as any part of David's game could be next on the chopping block without any warning, I'm not going to be able to "just relax" and be content with the knowledge that the final product will be a good game.

                                            I'm reminded of a joke Mark Hamill told about his surprise at finding out what the extent of his role in Force Awakens would be.

                                            The first ~2 minutes is the relevant bit. Ultimately, the point he seems to be making is that people take big news better when you prepare them for it. Not having been prepared for big changes to the court system or the hedge, many fans, myself included did not take it as well as we might have if we had been better prepared.
