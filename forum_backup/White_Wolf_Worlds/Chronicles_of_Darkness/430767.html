    New to Forsaken 1.0 and totally new to 2.0; but what is 2.0 about?

    03-06-2015, 07:28 PM
    I used to play Werewolf the Apocalypse back in the 90s with my buds I grew up with and have great memories doing so when home from college during summers and extended holiday seasons. Some of those memories lead me to feel like Werewolf : The Forsaken 1.0 was 'too evil' or we thought that the line had a too personal intent and horror which we didn't feel we wanted to have anything to do with... but now, in March 6th 2015 I am reading the PDF for Forsaken 1.0 and I can't believe I didn't give it a chance before now. I totally like it way better then the version back in the 90s and it's no less morally soul staining then any other RPG based on conflict with death as the usual main mechanic for ending the life of anything that gets in your way.

    So, I want to buy the print product of 1.0 Forsaken, but I just got wind that 2.0 came out as a PDF days ago as an 'advance product' and the FAQ says it's 4-6 six weeks for a vetting process before it comes out for Print-On-Demand... I am cool with the wait, but what does 2.0 have that would lure me away from 1.0 story and material. I just like everyone else, like shinny, novel things... but I sort of need to know without dumping $20 on another Forsaken PDF Product even if it is the newest one.

    Can someone who has the 2.0 version give me a basic compare and contrast in a (of course limited and not comprehensive - unless of course you want to lay it all out) summary so I can judge to see if 1.0 is good enough to start with or whether 2.0 is going to be better - it said it 're-imagined' the Tribes and such... how much is their a deviation, do you guys/gals like the change from 1.0 to 2.0 or what would you do in my shoes.

    I am not going to buy both at the start. I might buy 2.0 in a few months or a year later or so, or maybe I will only buy 2.0 as print and just keep my PDF of 1.0. I am a patient Aeternal and can wait on buying... especially since I its been 10 years out from when Forsaken was first released in 2005. What is remarkable is that the Forsaken released 2005 or so still speaks to me even though its definitely dated... that said, I kind of don't want to ruin that novel delight I got with it right now by grabbing 2.0 because 2.0 IS the newest shinny thing that this chattering monkey wants to bite - but since Forsaken 1.0 is only $25.99 Premium Color Print on Demand - it's a tough place to be.

    Thanks in Advance for any advice... insight into the different versions... and any other words that would help me.
    P.S - Where can one find some online games of Werewolf the Forsaken 1.0 or 2.0?

          Well, I don't actually have the full PDF version of 2.0 quite yet, but I did write part of it, so maybe I can help. (Although, obviously, I may be a bit biased, so keep that in mind.)

          First, just a quick background so you know where I'm coming from. Like you, I played Apocalypse back in the day. It was what brought me into the WoD in the first place. You might say it was my first WoD love, so to speak. When Forsaken came around, I got excited all over again, due to the streamlining (five tribes instead of thirteen, for instance, and so on), the focus on the hunt, the change in tone, and the lack of metaplot.

          I ended up getting a copy of the First Edition core book at the Camarilla's Golden Ticket LARP '05 (holy shit that was a decade ago, I just realized). This was a month or two before it came out in general release, so I ate that thing right the hell up. I later served as a Forsaken Storyteller in some capacity at every single level of the club at the time (so, local/domain, regional, national, and global). Twice I held the regional and global positions simultaneously, which is just as exhausting as you'd think it is.

          I only say that so that when I say I love the hell out of this game, you know I mean it. Which brings us to Second Edition.

          It is so much better.

          There is a much tighter focus on the Hunt in this edition. Our werewolves will find you. They will hunt you. And they will kill you. And they won't feel a single bit of doubt about it. Packs are terrifying - with an expansion that includes both wolf-blooded and human members of the extended pack, they could be anywhere, be anyone.

          Your tribe focuses you on a particular prey. Your Auspice dictates how you hunt that prey. A Rahu is certainly more straightforward than his Irraka packmate, but she'll just roll her eyes and hit the prey in its weak spot just when it isn't looking (or, more to the point, paying far more attention to the enraged wolf monster in front of it). Meanwhile, their Ithaeur has already bound spirits into her service to create a fetish to help kill or capture the prey, depending on the pack's goals.

          Speaking of the prey, they have more tools at their disposal. They won't go down without a fight. Happily, the Uratha have a new organization of Gifts and Facets that help alleviate the issues of the previous incarnation. Mechanically, the forms have more tools at their claws, in order to fulfill a variety of roles in the Hunt.

          The idigam themselves have also changed. They are the werewolves' "out of context problem," and they have new rules and new abilities at their disposal. A collection of five sample idigam, complete with story hooks, backgrounds, stats, and more are in the book for your use if you like.

          As much as I loved the 1st Edition, I think 2nd Edition blows it out of the water. But that's just my opinion. I hope you check out the blog, and anything you can read up on it and make an informed decision for you and your group.

          But regardless of which edition you're playing, you're still playing Forsaken, so I think that's fantastic. Howl to the stars because you have prey to chase down!

          The Wolf Must Hunt.
