                                        A few things about the Strix that might appeal to you.
                                        ​First: The Strix are drawn to coming death and destruction, meaning even before the volcano goes off the Birds of Dis are there waiting. It wouldn't be far fetched for them to work against those trying to spoil the coming good time. No, the book never explains how they know what's coming, just that they often appear as heralds of what's to come. Several cultures hold owls to be omens of death. They don't even have to be known allies of your villain, they just want him to succeed. At least far enough to unleash hell on Earth.
                                        Second: Until they reach the highest levels of potency and can solidify their shadow form, the Strix are mostly impotent without a host form. This means you can kill or destroy the host and defeat the owl.
                                        Third: Unless you find a way to trap the Strix (fire or sunlight, two things Vampires aren't likely to use) then once the host is destroyed the Strix will escape (evil cackle fading into the distance). This means your heroes can fight and even defeat the bad guy in battle only to have him rise up again, over and over again. Each time mocking them and making dire promises, then he takes someone they actually care about as host and really amps up the drama.
                                        Last but not least: The stated goal of the Strix is to teach the Vampires that they are supposed to be monsters, not pale imitations of humanity, and if that doesn't fit the Sabbat ideal what does?
