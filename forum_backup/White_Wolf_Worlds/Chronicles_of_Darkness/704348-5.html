                                        On the other hand, a carefully and quietely aimed crossbow shoot from 150 yard on an unaware vampire, right in the back, is reasonably workable.

                                        ( extreme range: -2, aiming to hearth -4, aiming for 3 rounds +3, binocular aimer and laser punctator + 3, good quality crossbow +2 dices to attack and 3 additional dices to damage if the shoot lands )
