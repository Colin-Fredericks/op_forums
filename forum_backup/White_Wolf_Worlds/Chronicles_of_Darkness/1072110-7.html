                    A few cursory opinions on the darkness beyond "aaaa, it's evil, kill it"

                    Ashwood Abbey- a lot of Ashwood Abbey might well be darkened, and in some of the worst chapters full-fledged Mnemosynes might be able to fit in. This can leave the Abbey in the unfortunate situation of having to defend creatures of darkness. Of course, the Abbey isn't populated entirely by monsters, and the risk of civil war if a particularly influential member turns to the dark is enough to ensure that these kind of situations are often "dealt with" before they become a nuisance.

                    The Long Night- There was a perfect world, and then a shadowy force came, manipulated the people there into sin and cursed the world with darkness. Sound familiar? The Darkness is the curse lain on the earth by the fall of eden, still manifesting to this day. Does this mean the compact should leave it to wreck judgement, or fight it as an aspect of sin? Opinions vary. Of course, an alternate reading is it's not the curse, but the Serpent- that is, Satan. The implications of the adversary manifesting and turning people into monsters are... worrying, to say the least.

                    The Cheiron Group- sadly, implants from "creatures of darkness" are not very useful. They're easy to transplant (almost suspiciously so, some of the more paranoid surgeons say) and provide impressive amounts of power, but the people with them tend to go weird. Then murderous. Then their skin falls off and they become knife-monsters. Analysis of the bodies isn't hugely useful- it seems most of the damage is more metaphysical then physical. This goes against everything cheiron believes, and they keep trying in the hope of finding out what is causing the reaction.

                    The Union- The union isn't as opposed to the darkness as many people are. Sure, the skeletal things that eat children's minds, they need to go away. But a lot of the so-called "darkened" are just members of the community. Assholes, sure, but not monsters. Likewise tainted places, which are just as often someone's house as underground torture bunkers. While the union has been told by the nobles that these darkened will become monsters, and many do, they're still a bit leery about massacring people or burning down blocks based only on the word of strange magic ladies. While this hasn't been enough to break any alliances yet, in areas with a lot of tainted places it has started causing significant tensions.

                    The Lucifuge- sin leads to hell. Sin enough in one place, and hell draws closer. To the Lucifuge, tainted places are, if not portals to hell, places where the inferno is a lot closer to earth then normal. The darkspawn are either demons or a strange form of possession. To this end, the Lucifuge work with princesses to erase these areas- although a few of the more fanatical members wander if finding a way to blow these thin areas open might be a way to finally take the fight to the demons. Doing so would presumably require committing horrific sins in an already tainted place. Something no member of the Lucifuge would ever condone, of course.

                                    From least to most powerful:

                                    Darkened- humans who are influenced by the darkness, with soem powers, but have not yet actually become supernatural creatures yet.

                                    Darkspawn- Darkened twisted into feral monsters. They lack any aspect of their human lives and are very rarely sentient.

                                    Mnemosynes- Darkened totally corrupted by the darkness who can can still pass for human. They have magic-style powers and are fully sentient.

                                    Cataphractoi- Copies of people who die filled with despair. They have the physical mutations of the darkspawn and the magic of the mnemosynes, and work to emulate the life of the person they copied in horrifying ways. Can control lesser darkspawn

                                    Dethroned- Princesses who have fallen to the Darkness. Have massive magic powers and twist reality around them into reflections of their broken psyche.

                                            Cataphractoi can also perform Transformations of their own. These vary wildly, but are always bad news.

                                            Just want to give a couple reminders about falling to Belief 0 and the Dethroned:

                                            First of all, while a Princess that hits Belief 0 due to Hunter meddling is likely to be reminded of her former supernatural status and attempt a Transformation, this isn't a certainty. While a Belief 0 Princess almost always invites trouble (apparently, Light and Dark-attuned characters are drawn to them), they can also just be zombie-like versions of themselves, and will default to either doing nothing or making token efforts to uphold her mundane life until her Willpower hits 0. A Dethroned is merely a possibility (though, again, an admittedly likely one with Hunters involved).

                                            Secondly, not all Dethroned are giant monsters. In fact, the example Dethroned from the corebooks has a detailed enough "script" to pass as a normal old woman. Sure, they lash out like monsters without a care in the world and act like fake people when they have enough of a "script" to act, but much like their lesser brethren, they are a varied bunch.

                                            Finally, Dethroned are mostly passive. They spend the majority of their time just sitting in their Fortress of Regrets in the Dark World and moping. Therefore, a Hunter would only be likely to encounter a Dethroned in two situations. The first is at ground zero before a new Dethroned realizes that the Dark World hurts a bit less and retreats there. This is usually the Hunter's fault, but it certainly hasn't done much good for the reputation of the Hopeful. The second occasion is if a Dethroned exits the Dark World through a hijacked Tainted Place, usually at the urging of some desperate Creatures of Darkness. The Vocation Version goes into the different kinds of interactions a Dethroned's abilities can have on reality, but the Dream Version assumes that when a Dethroned strays close to reality, they tend to pop in for a short visit. This… can be disastrous. If Hunters don't make it to the attack in time, they'll probably be called in to help with the cleanup. Some Dethroned are uncanny people, but others are small Kaiju, and they are far past the point of caring who sees them.

                                                    Even in-universe, nobody is sure what exactly the Dark World is, aside from the fact the Darkness came from this place (even though ironically even creatures of the Darkness have trouble staying active inside it when they get too far away from Tainted Areas). What is known about it is that it looks like a dark reflection of the real world (as in everything is destroyed or trying to kill you, buildings are in ruins, the moon is a black hole, the sun is red....), and Tainted Areas are places where it "leaks" into the real world. Because of this, creatures of the Darkness (and Princesses with the right Lacrima Charms) can use Tainted Areas as portals to travel to the Dark World and vice-versa the same way werewolves use Loci to travel to the Shadow.

                                                    Also, since we're at it, I'd like to clarify a few more things regarding the Darkness:

                                                    Darkened, being still human for the most part, aren't systematically evil; many of them don't even realize they have been corrupt until their "Umbrae" (the mutations provoked by their conditions) get too blatant to not notice (many earlier Umbrae actually are pretty subtle). There can even be people who who are looking for a cure or a mean to use these powers well (though the latter rarely goes well). Princesses are aware of this, so with the exception of Stormites, they don't usually shoot Darkened on sight and would rather try to cure the innocent ones when it is still possible. Only Mnemosyne, Darkspawns are Cataphracoi are ireedeemably evil.

                                                    Many Darkened and Mnemosyne organize in Dark Cults intended to recruit and corrupt more people, usually by baiting them first and then by locking them in Tainted Areas if they are too reluctant. These cults rarely last long, however -- most of the time their blatantly evil attitude means the police will end up taking care of them even if a Princess doesn't show up. Only the really smart ones survive.

                                                    When a Darkened reaches Integrity 0 with even one Umbrae (he has a chance of getting one each time his Integrity lowers), he dies in his sleep and rises as a Darkspawn later. Darkened who somehow managed to get to Integrity 0 without a single Umbrae become Mnemosyne instead. Cataphractoi, meanwhile, are born when someone reach a level of dispair where she is convinced there's nothing good in the world and dies in that state (frequently but not always suicide), resulting in her body rising as a Cataphract.
