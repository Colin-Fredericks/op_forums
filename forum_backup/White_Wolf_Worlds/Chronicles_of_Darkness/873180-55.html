                    If Beasts could see invisible portals, that'd be specified in the text. I do think many such gateways could be discovered through Wits+Occult rolls to pick up the spiritual energies in the area (I might misremember, but didn't Book of the Dead use that roll to find Avernian Gates?) but for those who wants a more reliable method I present this homebrew Merit:

                    Gate Sense (• to •••••)
                    Prerequisite: Wits •••, Kinship with being related to the chosen realm(s)
                    Effect: This Merit allows the Beast to innately sense the presence of an otherworldly gateway within her Lair * 10 metres. If it resides in Twilight or is otherwise hidden, the Beast can still see a faint seam shimmering in the air where the portal would be. Each dot only reveals gateways to a specific realm, and requires Kinship with Kin who are metaphysically tied to that realm. For example, Kinship with ghosts and Bound reveals Avernian Gates, Kinship with spirits and werewolves reveals Loci and Verges, Kinship with Strix reveals the shadowy portals to the little known realm sometimes called Dis, and Kinship with Changelings and Hobgoblins reveals Hedgegates. While inside those realms, the Beast also senses any gateways leading back to the material world or their own Lair, but not other realms unless they also are represented by this Merit.
                    Drawback: This Merit does nothing to reveal gateways that are specifically hidden from supernatural senses.

                    Writer for Bloodlines: The Ageless and Fire & Revolution: Carthians on STV
