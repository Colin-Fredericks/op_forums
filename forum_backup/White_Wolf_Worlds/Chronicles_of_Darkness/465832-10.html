        Today we have a birthday, a battle, and a revelation. Not quite in that order. This session covers two days.

        Session XXXII

        Marek’s Thoughts

        27 October 1922

        Today was Lizette’s 22nd birthday. We started the day like any other. Wolfram and I started Fenric with his gun safety and shooting lessons in the back yard. He is doing pretty well for a novice shooter, I will give him that. He just needs to work on the correct positioning of his arms. He needs to make sure that they are in the correct position to be able to absorb the recoil from the gun. He tends to bend his arms a bit too much and the angle of his elbow is too shallow. It must be wider to create the right shape to absorb the shock. He will get it though. I do not doubt his resolve to learn this new skill.

        Tonight we all planned on “luring” Lizette to the chateau. Most of us went back via the gate in the basement earlier and left Wolfram to get Lizette to come through. I think he just said something about needing to come back to the house and Lizette offered to come with him. He accepted and we surprised her as she stepped through the gate. While we were in Germany, Christina was busy having a bonfire set up in the backyard. There were also fireworks. Overall It was a relaxing night. I think Lizette enjoyed herself I know she definitely enjoyed the present that Fenric and Alla got her. It was a lighter shaped like a gun. So appropriate for our fae pyromaniac.


        28 October 1922

        Today started off with Oneida sending a message to us saying that she wants to meet with us to discuss the Claimed problem that they had. One of the Claimed specifically went after Prince Sascha and Oneida is still concerned for her monarch’s safety. They did not succeed in taking out their target and she was worried that they may try and finish the job. So, we all met at an outside café for lunch so we could discuss our issues in public. We did have a couple people watching us. The unsurprising one was a man in Woebegone’s employ. He was hard to miss at seven feet tall and bald. He kept a respectful distance and Oneida said that Woebegone likely sent him to act as security for Oneida herself. Seems likely. The second watcher was not someone that we were expecting. It was William Forsythe from the train. Back then he had wanted help in spying on the demon woman while we were on that trip. It seems that we have made a lasting impression on several people due to our actions on that damned train. Dacian saw the demon man that could turn into bees outside the post office a little while back, and now we have William spying on us in Germany as well.

        Oneida’s main concern was that the men who’d attacked Sascha the first time were in the club last night. She was afraid that they were going to try something else tonight and wanted to ask for our help. We readily agreed and proceeded to make plans on who would be where and when we would arrive. We figured that if Dacian and I arrived early we could take positions up in the rafters where we could both snipe from. Dacian was going to bring his bow and arrow and I my sniper rifle. The idea was also for Tatiana to communicate with the doorman to let him know who was unacceptable to come into the club, and that would be Pure and wolf-blooded. They would be able to sense that we were there and it could blow the whole thing. Greta was even called by Tatiana to see if she wanted to join us to at least take pictures. Alla would be in the crowd and be ready to use on of her gifts to compel all the guests to leave the club once the violence started. The others did not want there to be more collateral damage than there had to be. I was not keen on drawing too much attention to ourselves, since we do not know who the police are allied with in this town. That should be something that we look into in the future. We have a house here now and it would do well to identify any possible allies or enemies.

        We also talked about the problem with killing those that are Claimed in the way that Wolfram is. If he were to die, if he hadn’t been connected to the Golden Eagle, he would eventually come back but someone else nearby would lose their life to provide him with a new one. It is a payment that the Claimed must make to come back from the dead. A stranger dies in their place. We discussed ways that we might incapacitate the Claimed so that there would be no random people dying due to a Claimed man dying. I personally did not have any problem killing the Claimed, but it would be beneficial if we could get them to run away, as was our plan. That way we wouldn’t have to deal with bodies.

        While we were discussing our plans Sabine stopped at the table to talk to Wolfram. She complimented his choice in company, but then proceeded to be exceeding rude to Oneida. Turns out that the two are estranged sisters. Sabine blames Oneida for her death (and resurrection) and the death of her lover. As it turns out Sabine and her lover were planning on getting married. Her lover convincingly passed herself off as a man so Sabine’s father would bless the marriage. The disguise was very good, but unfortunately did not fool Oneida’s fetch, who ratted the couple out to their father. The father got upset and locked Sabine up in a steamer trunk and killed her lover. Sabine unfortunately suffocated while she was in the trunk.

        Apparently when people are taken to the Fae lands, Arcadia, a duplicate is left in their place. This duplicate is called a Fetch. The duplicate does not always act like the original would and unfortunate things can happen. I do not know if my Fae friends have Fetch’s or not. I know that Lizette’s village was bombed off the map. Her fetch died with her family. The others I’m not sure of. I do know that Dearbhail has had to take care that her identity cannot be traced back to her actual family. That makes me think that her duplicate is still there. Not sure about Dacian’s duplicate.

        So, the sisters made it known that they do not get along. I believe that Oneida tried to explain what had happened, that it wasn’t her, but it sounds like Sabine was too upset to really listen and so she is still angry at her “betrayer.” The sister’s estrangement does not directly affect us, but may cause trouble down the line if we’re not too careful. I do not know how Sabine will take it if we associate overly much with Oneida. I do not think that Oneida will get too upset if we keep company with her sister though. It is an awkward situation all around. I would prefer not to get involved in it at all, but I have a feeling that others in my pack will feel otherwise.

        The rest of this evening was both satisfying and extremely painful. We arrived at the club at 4:30pm and got into position. Two hours after the club opened Rudolph, the very tall gentleman that works for Woebegone, came in and told Lizette that there was a group of armed men waiting outside the club. Their description matched that of the original attackers. So, our plans changed. Dacian and I snuck out onto the roof and took up positions up there. Dearbhail came up on the roof with us, planning to provide healing as needed and any other assistance she could from a distance. Lizette, Wolfram, Alla and Tatiana all went outside to deal with the men on the ground. They managed to surround the group in a pincer move. I thought it was fairly well done. Also as they were heading out, as Lizette passed three parked cars they all started up, but stayed idling where they were. I assumed at the time that she had a plan in mind for them. I was right.

        I started things off by shooting one man in the eye, blinding him. I believe that Dearbhail used one of her Fae gifts to make that first shot easier than is would have been usually. Normally it is fairly hard to hit such a small target, but I had no trouble doing it tonight. Dacian shot one of them in the arm in an effort to keep them from firing upon us. It was a good plan, but those men had gifts that didn’t require use of arms, just intention. While we sniped from above Alla attacked one of the men in front of her. I believe that he will well remember that hit. I have sparred with Alla and know that in a fight the nice and polite woman drops away and she becomes terrifying with her strength and ability. Unfortunately for her one of the Claimed realized the very real danger that he was in and immediately set Alla on fire. He could see that she was dead and knew what she was. From my understanding Kindred that go into a frenzy because of fire either go all out and attack their enemy or they flee. Luckily for her attacker, but bad for us, Alla ran. I did not blame her. Fire does worse damage to those of her kind that it does to me or the others, and it hurts us bad enough as it is.

        Then one of the Claimed used some sort of gift on Tatiana. I did not see a physical attack but suddenly she staggered and acted as if she had taken a substantial blow. I saw red and shot that man in the head for hurting my pack mate. He remained standing after the shot. The Claimed are very hard to kill and he was no exception. Then one of his fellows used a nasty gift on Tatiana to make her start bleeding from her eyes, nose, mouth, everywhere. It was not a pleasant sight. It was even less pleasant to experience. Another of the Claimed used that same gift on me. It felt like I was burning from the inside out. It hurt just about as much as getting shot in the chest with silver did, but I think the damage was more extensive. At least I kept bleeding for a little while after that. My natural healing replenished most of the blood I lost but it was starting to take its toll towards the end of the fight.

        Well, I wasn’t going to let them get away what they had done so I aimed for my original target and fired. The man had no chance. For me the whole plan on leaving them alive left and all I could think about was putting down my target. I don’t have a high regard for those that are not pack anyway, and even less of one for those who are actively hurting my pack mates. Needless to say, that man died.

        After getting hit a second time by the devastating gifts of the Claimed Tatiana shifted to Guaru form and started attacking another man. I believe he was engaged fighting with Lizette. At least she was bleeding everywhere like Tatiana and I were. There was more than one of the Claimed that could do that attack and I was determined to make sure they paid in kind. It seemed that Tatiana was of a like mind.

        Just after that a gift was used that I recognized as the one that you can use to spy on an area. I believe Wolfram called it Boneyard. It obviously wasn’t one of the attackers since they were as surprised as I was that it went up. I am assuming that it was Greta who put it up. In the same instance a large wave of water came moving down the alley towards Alla and put out the flames that engulfed her. She seemed to pause a moment and regain control of herself. She acted like normal, but certainly didn’t look like it.

        The fight was not going well for us. Most of us were significantly hurt. They were hurt as well, but seemed to be able to soak up damage and discharge white smoke like it was nothing. I am given to understand that there is a limit to how much damage that can be negated by the smoke, but it was substantial enough. I was getting ready to line up another shot when all of a sudden those three running cars came barreling forward into the group of Claimed. I think they decided that discretion was the better part of valor and decided to run. One man apologized out loud before raising his dead friend as a zombie so they could all flee. We left them an opening so they could leave and we let them. Mostly. I will admit that I was more than a little angry to see my pack mates in such a state. At least two of them had been hit by that horrendous gift and were bleeding quite freely. Tatiana was healing the smaller wounds but was still bleeding. Lizette was in much the same condition, though she does not have the natural healing of my kind. I should have let them run, but I was angry. It is rare that anger takes over my actions but this time it did. I fired a parting shot at one of the men in the back. It hit him and he stumbled. I paid for it though. I second later I was hit by a different gift and set ablaze. I just about fell to my knees in blinding pain. I had to shift to Guaru form to avoid dying from that last hit. I stayed in that form long enough to soak some of the damage and heal some of the lesser wounds. I do not think I would have survived if I had not shifted forms.

        I do not understand Lizette’s fascination with fire. It hurts. A lot. Thankfully Dacian was there to help me put out the flames, and Dearbhail came over and helped stop my bleeding. I was reminded again how remarkable our Fae friend’s gifts are. I was not in a good way before her ministrations, and wasn’t too much better afterwards, but I was at least no longer bleeding out. I have come this close to death twice before and really do not feel like repeating the experience any time soon.

        I watched from the roof as Wolfram scooped Lizette out of the trunk of her car. Apparently she has a tendency to curl up somewhere safe and nurse her wounds when she is badly hurt. I wish she did not have the experience often enough for this to be a habit. But Wolfram carried her into the club through a back entrance, while Dacian and Dearbhail helped me get back into the club though the roof. We all met up with Sascha and he used the same gift that Dearbhail had to heal our wounds. He completely healed Tatiana, and was able to heal most of Lizette’s and my wounds. I thanked the man for his help and looked around to make sure that my pack mates were alright. Everyone seemed okay. Wolfram was doting on Lizette a little more than usual, but I can’t say that I was surprised. She had been badly hurt, and my German friend has feelings for our Fae inventor. I think he always has. Dearbhail was a little clingy with me, but I assured her that I was fine and that that the rest of my wounds would heal in time.

        Tonight was overall a success. We encouraged the Claimed men to run away and we kept Sascha from getting hurt. We fought a group of individuals who were definitely more prepared and capable of dealing out damage than we were, but we held our own and sent them running. That was satisfying. I’m also assuming that Greta got some decent photographs of the incident. I will have to check with her later to see if she got what she wanted from the evenings events.

        Overall, I was not happy with my actions towards the end of the fight. I know that my pack is my weak point, but I was not expecting to get so angry that I’d go against the plan we had in place. We were only supposed to incapacitate them, but I intentionally killed one because he had hurt my pack mates. Granted I had no qualms about some random person dying later, but my pack did and for them I was going to go along with the plan. But, my anger got the better of me. I do not regret what I did. I would likely do it again. I was just a bit surprised by how easily my emotions ruled my actions. It is not usually something that I have a problem with.

        We have definitely made our mark on this town. We will likely begin to see more of an interest in our presence here. It will not be a friendly interest from The Watch. I’m not sure how the Black Company will take our actions. They may not have any problems with us dealing damage to their rivals. It is possible that they will see our actions as an intrusion in their city though. We have definitely disrupted the status quo. I wonder if the Pure in this city will be attracted to us due to our actions. There was no secret that two werewolves were involved in that fight, and it is likely that The Watch will talk about it. Some of the Pure or wolf-blooded may hear about it. In which case we should be cautious from here on out. Definitely no travelling alone regardless of the errand.

        I will have to offer an apology to the rest of the pack for deviating from the set plan that we had come up with. I will explain that I reacted in anger. It is not something that I usually have a problem with. I should have expected others to get hurt. I guess I just did not expect the amount or type of injuries that they were able to do with what looked like relative ease. I will not underestimate the Claimed again. I am sure that there will be future altercations with them. Unless they took tonight’s meeting to be a warning that we can beat them. I wonder if they realized that there should have been more of them dead than just the one. Tatiana switched targets, as did I for the most part, in order to avoid killing some of them. If we had been aiming to kill them there would have been at least two more in that total. If I had not killed that one it would have been more obvious that we were holding back. My actions may have muddled the overall message. Perhaps they will understand it nonetheless. Hopefully they will at least leave Sascha alone. We are prepared for them, Prince Sascha is not. Time will tell.


        Tatiana’s Conversations

        I did not say unharmed, did I? But I stopped the bleeding, and the fairy Prince put my insides back in order. I will be fine in no time. Marek and Lizette are still hurt, you should fuss over them.

        Well it is very tiring to feel like you are carrying the world on your back. And Greta was not fussing over me.

        She hugged me once. You have licked me far more than once.

        No, volchonok, you are not to tell Ocranny. Greta said so.

        Because she was concerned for me, like you, foolish volchonok.

        She did - she brought the big wave that stopped Alla from being on fire. But she was taking photographs before that.

        So that we can get these Watch people in trouble.

        I don’t know. But Greta is smart and Wolfram approves as well. So it is a good plan.

        The bald fairy? He guarded Oneida, who guarded the fairy Prince, so they were all inside. Guarding.

        We did not need him. It would have been nice to see Oneida fight though.

        With knives, like her sister Sabine, the Sin-Eater from the big cabaret. They performed together, with knives, before Oneida became a fairy.

        No, the thing that was left behind who looked like Oneida got Sabine and her fiancée killed. It is a shame - they had a good plan for getting married.

        The fiancée was pretending to be a fiancé, so that the foolish human customs would allow them to be mates.

        Don’t be foolish volchonok. The village knows I am a woman. A Russian man of my size who suddenly appears and begins courting Elena would be obvious.

        I have not decided. Now hush volchonok.

        Wise. Yes, it was a good fight.

        What? Of course we won. I am the best at fighting.

        Yes, we only killed one, but we frightened them and caused them to spend much of their kind of Essence.

        No, volchonok, recall, it is bad to kill them. They will kill a human to return to life.

        I believe Marek was angry with that one. He is a very good shot.

        It is true volchonok. We do have a good pack.

        No, I do not think they want to go running. I believe they all want to rest. There will be time for running later.


        Lizette’s Plans

        28th October, 1922

        I’ve seen better days, but I have seen worse.

        Yesterday was my birthday. I am twenty-two years old. I still look like I did at nineteen. Actually I guess I have not aged much in the six years since my return, but still. Spent the day in the workshop, modifying the Buzzsaw Bow a little, then took Wolfram back to the house to check on Christina, where there was a surprise party waiting. The whole Motley, Christina, Marlene, everyone. Wolfram was cheerful and enthusiastically spent time talking to me. Christina hugged me several times, more affectionate than she has been in awhile. She was huge, of course. Poor dear had trouble hugging me. I got gifts from everyone, even something from Marlene. Heck, I even had something from Dana in the mail. I think the most ironic gift was from Alla and Fenric, an ornate lighter shaped like a gun.

        Marlene showed up at my room that night, said I should not sleep alone on my birthday. It was a good night. The first birthday night since meeting her I did not spend with Christina, but Marlene was…quite well enough of a distraction from that. She was the second other woman besides Dana to sleep in my bed. I went to sleep happy, and certainly so did she.

        I think I heard Mademoiselle Théière sigh when Marlene was slipping inside though. I did not think a teapot could sigh.

        Today was less blissful. We were contacted by Oneida at the house, who wanted to meet at a café. It turns out Prince Sascha was being menaced by what Wolfram was sure was members of Die Wacht. It sounded like them, and they wore the colors of the Imperial German flag. Queen Woebegone sent help in the form of Rudolph Gusser, the tall lanky Darkling. He kept an eye on the meeting. We agreed to come by The Hedge Maze and provide security.

        While we were there, Sabine, the knife dancer, came by, speaking sharply to Wolfram. She and Oneida got in each other’s faces. It turns out they are sisters. Twin sisters. But Oneida was Taken, and when Sabine attempted to leave with a woman disguised as a man to be married, the Fetch saw through it. Their father killed Sabine’s fiancée and stuffed Sabine in a steamer trunk, where she suffocated. I am given to understand he died horribly when she rose as one of the Bound. When Oneida returned, she found Sabine decidedly disinterested in making up. It did not help that she walked in on her in flagrante delicto with another woman, and responded with shock. As one would, I suppose, seeing their twin with anyone. At any rate, the conversation between the two could be best described as “chilly” and at worst “hostile,” though they never came to blows. I would not wish to see Oneida or Sabine hurt, but to see those two lithe, leggy amazing women in a knife fight would be…exhilarating.

        The thought of spinning blades did remind me of the umbrella I had been working on, which would have steel serrated plates which when activated would spin in a deadly fan, carving up ones foe while the umbrella protected you from the gore splatters of whomever you were carving up. And if not carving anyone up, it could provide a nice breeze to passersby, provided they did not fall into it or something. I would have teapot designs on it.

        Given the gore shield, I could have used it tonight. Mayhem!

        We arrived at The Hedge Maze and met up with Greta Hess, whom Tatiana had invited and who managed not to fall into bed with a vampire in the process. Rudolph showed up too. We made a great deal of planning, and had drinks on the house. Marek and Dacian took up positions on the catwalks over the stage, Tatiana near the door, Rudolph, Dearbháil, Oneida and Wolfram near Prince Sascha, and Alla, Fenric Greta and I in the crowd. However, after a bit, Rudolph warned us they were awaiting in ambush outside, so we regrouped and headed out, with Dearbháil, Dacian, and Marek on the roof. Alla stayed near me, and the other moved to attack from different sides. Rudolph stayed with Oneida inside in case it was a ruse.

        I animated several cars en route, but held them in reserve as I, Wolfram (with my Buzzsaw Bow!) Alla and Tatiana charged them directly. They heavily attacked Tatiana, enough to send her into a rage. I did not catch everything, but I know she started coughing and slowed down, and Alla burst into flame, and Tatiana and I both bled heavily from our mouth, eyes, ears, and places not described in polite company. It was agonizing, like my organs were liquefying and running out of my body. I still manage to carve them with my chainsaw, and wound up as much covered in their blood as my own.

        Not to brag, but my chainsaw never got clogged up even with the viscera flying around.

        It was hard to fight; feeling like your entire abdomen was full of razors. I envied Alla; at least she was on fire. Well, until Greta put her out with a wave of water. As the pain was making my head spin I ran the cars into them, and heard several crunches. I could not see it, but apparently Marek and Dacian were tearing them apart from the air, even as Wolfram was sending blades into their body. There was so much fog from their wounds with five of them it was hard to see. That and the eyeball blood.

        Eventually, they fled, taking their temporary dead with them. As is my way, I crawled in our automobile’s trunk to sleep, which is what I did on the battlefield when injured, find a trunk away from the fight to sleep in. Wolfram came and got me however, and took me inside. I remember him carrying me. Despite the pain, that was nice.

        I will need to get a performance assessment of the Buzzsaw Bow from him. It seemed to be carving nicely, but I really did not get a good view of the fight, with blood and sinew flying in my face and the fog and whatnot. Did I mention it hurt, like sliding down a greased razor blade while inside out?

        I wish I set them on fire.

        Dearbháil had already healed me some, and Prince Sascha also used his healing powers. I am still hurt, but it is a more natural kind of hurt, like falling from a plane or getting shot five or six time. Not this liquefying organs thing. Marek is still hurt too. They did not have guns, they must have used some Sin-Eater power I have never seen Wolfram or Greta use. Speaking of the daddy’s girl, Greta got several pictures she was eager to leverage against them. For my part, I was ready to sleep. As much fun as carving screaming Sin-Eaters with a chainsaw was, I needed to recover a bit. I doubt Die Wacht is going to let this lie.

        Speaking of lying, though, I play to lie down on this very soft bed, and sleep a very long time.

        Dream Mayhem….

        End Session XXXII

        Okay, let’s do this. The 27th was a light RP, just what people were up to at the party.

        Each member of the Watch had Rage 4, a Manifestation at 3, and a Manifestation at 2. 3 keys each, most had Passion, Stigmata, and one other…Pyre-Flame and Grave-Dirt was used. They were Psyche 3. Sin-Eaters are tough, and Manifestations are powerful. They were, however, unarmed beyond knives or pistols, and given the firepower incoming Rage, Curse, and such were more useful. Given the PCs were ambushing, for many Manifestations the Sin-Eaters had to weigh activating them and standing there being pounded. They knew they were fighting a Sin-Eater, Werewolf, and Vampire, but did not know what Lizette, Dearbháil or Marek were, and never saw Dacian. The PCs were larguly trying to pile tilts on them, since Sin-Eater killing is temporary at best, so you had blinded, maimened people throwing powers around, hence Wolfram using the Buzzaw Bow.

        Greta used the Tear-Stained Boneyard to put Alla out.

        So, in case you have fallen behind a bit, the reader’s digest.

        The Conspiracy was brought to Berlin by a number of things, Alla to do Constancia (the Maenad Mekhet Princess of Evil) a favor, Wolfram to answer a request by a former soldier named Willie, and Dacian to Skadi, the Winter Queen of Berlin. So far, there was a short meeting with the Circle members for Alla, so far, so good.

        Willie talked of an alliance between the Watch (proto-Nazi racists) and some elements of Black Company (an officially apolitical large Krewe of former soldiers) that he feared would embolden the Watch. It specifically included acquiring weapons from a Krewe called Red October from Russia (weapons are harder to come by due to sanctions) making them think they are destined for Communists in a Fifth Column, but actually going to the Watch. Our Heroes arranged for the meeting to be broken up not by them, but by the Pure, who are overconfidant in their control of the area, and whose members in the Ivory Claws tribe hate Sin-Eaters (and ghosts, and Vampires). They used a photo of Tatiana who had fought the Ivory Claw Ilsa in Vienna to get their attention, and left silver Lizette had made in the packages. The Sin-Eater Greta who they met in Paris, who lives in Berlin, is helping too.

        Skadi fears an alliance between the Spring King Sascha and Autumn Queen Woebegone. She also seeks to keep Germany in mourning so it will not be able to channel it’s anger into another disastrous war with France. She is even murdering some Summer Courtiers in a bid to keep Wrath weakened. She fears the waxing powers of the Courts of Desire and Fear, who combined are her equal, may also strengthen the resolve of Germans too quickly to fight. She gave the Conspiracy no instruction, but implied weakening the courts would please her. Otherwise they are honored guests.

        Alla and Lizette met with Queen Woebegone and it went well. Alla began negotiating for experiments involving film and Vampires. Meeting Prince Sascha revealed that he has been targeted by the Watch due to his being a bisexual Jewish man running a club. They were menacing him so the PCs ambushed a group that came by to beat him, taking damage while utterly taking the Sin-Eaters off guard and routing them.

        Meanwhile, they also saw evidence the largest cabaret is under the control of an unknown Wolf-Blooded and with a number of supernatural denizens, including a singer of unknown nature and haunting voice and powerful Sin-Eater with the Girlfriend’s Krewe.
