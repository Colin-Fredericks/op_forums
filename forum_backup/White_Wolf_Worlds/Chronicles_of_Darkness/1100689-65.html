                                                            Challenge Accepted!

                                                            Considering that the most immediate gamelines which you think about when you hear about Arthur are Changeling and Mage, we can safely rule them out for this Era. The same for Geist and Mummy in Hawara and Deviant and Demon for Sci Fi. Promethean is also too close for Sci Fi, and vampire has historic potential for Arthur (having a future Era in Britain) and there have been suggestions for Hunter in Arthur's Era as Knights of the Round, so let's remove them too for each of the Era's list. Vampire is also ruled out of Hawara, considering that it also works with the theme of "life and death" and that it already got 2 Eras. By that logic, we also remove Geist and Mage, which got Pirates. I want to say Hunter for Hawara, but I already made a compact based around Sobek so I have to remove it as a potential Era following the basic logic of this post. If we go about the last gamelines we would think about for each Era but which would still make sense, I'll say Beast and Demon for Arthur, Promethean and Werewolf for Hawara and Hunter and Changeling for Sci Fi. In case we don't rule out Hunter for Hawara out of simple internal logic, I think it would change places with werewolf for each Era. I must admit that Promethean may make too much sense for Hawara, but it is the only potential gameline left which is not that obvious :P
