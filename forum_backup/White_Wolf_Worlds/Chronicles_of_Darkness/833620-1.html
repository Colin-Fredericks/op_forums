                                                I think there are a few issues here (this is just for clarification for anybody who doesn't understand what's being argued):

                                                1) Some people don't agree that emotional feeding should have a consequence. 1e explained how it's possible to simply sup on the "emotional effluents" of a person and not on their "emotional core". Under this view, Changelings would be like cold people simply basking in the warmth of a fire (which does not actually diminish the fire at all).
                                                2) Some people agree that emotional feeding should have a consequence, but they don't agree Reticent is the right one. Some would rather see different Conditions or effects.
                                                3) Some people agree on something like Reticent being an acceptable consequence for emotional feeding, but find the Condition far too mechanically harsh, or too easily inflicted.
                                                4) Some people would like more nuance and granularity in emotional feeding, so that changelings have more options and it isn't an all-or-nothing affair.
