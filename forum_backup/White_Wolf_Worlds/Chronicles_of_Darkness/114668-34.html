    I kind of can't figure out how much reach you need for remote viewing vs sympathy casting. It SOUNDS like both require +2 reach total? but sometimes it sounds like remote viewing needs 2 reach and then sympathy casting needs just 1?     Both Sympathetic Range and Temporal Sympathy require that the spell be at Sensory but don't have any additional Reach mentioned, so I think using just the text you arrive at:

    Sensory Range: +1 Reach

    That seems a little odd - but I could imagine a Merit that lets Space 2 people spend 1 mana instead of 1 extra reach to cast on remote viewing (maybe requiring a sympathetic yantra) especially if you wanted to make a Free Councilor or Seer that routinely operated via surveillance networks.

                            That seems a little odd - but I could imagine a Merit that lets Space 2 people spend 1 mana instead of 1 extra reach to cast on remote viewing (maybe requiring a sympathetic yantra) especially if you wanted to make a Free Councilor or Seer that routinely operated via surveillance networks.
                            I always viewed that the remote viewing became sympathetic with space 2, as you had a current, visual representation right there in front of you for whatever means you were remotely viewing the target. Thus, why use the remote viewing casting when your skill in space would let you hit the target no matter where he was.

                            I viewed the remote viewing extra reach was so that someone couldn't just tap into all the cameras in a city using a rote 1 dot force spell and then start killing people with lightning blasts without ever needing space or to use up a Yantra slot. Sympathetic Casting, with it's Yantra requirement, does lessen the death from elsewhere aspect with its required XP, as well as the need to lessen the spell potential with the loss of a Yantra slot to cast sympathetically (it also keeps space from becoming useless when Forces exists during the age of the internet.) Note, the Blitz* is still feasible, but more prone to paradox without having more reach available (i.e. higher arcana.)


                            *The aformentioned Camera/Lightning combo so named by my Obrimos player based on London's numerous cameras as well as the attacks that befell said city during WWII, which were named after the German word for lightning. Considering the devastation an Obrimos could achieve with this effect, and over a city wide area of they just wished to target structures or indiscriminately, I find the name to be terrifyingly appropriate.
