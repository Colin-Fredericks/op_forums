                                    Originally posted by Chattnos View Post
                                    I get Cruac rips your humanity more. But I cannot wrap my head around Theban requiring a Humanity equal to the level your casting.
                                    Maybe if it were thought of as something to do with how much personal understanding of the Man or Beast the practitioner has, not just to what degree blood magic blocks or requires Humanity.

                                    IOW, it's possible to practice baseline Cruac at Humanity 9 because even the youngest Acolyte starts with a slobbering monster whose perversions can be turned outward, but which can also (usually) be tucked away when counterproductive. You let the Beast beat its chest for a minute, think bloody, brutal thoughts, and then when you're done you go right back to supressing it.

                                    The same young vampire would find baseline Theban Sorcery a comparative cakewalk, conceptually, because they've been listening to the Man before it was even the Man.
                                    ...an old or degenerate vampire, OTOH, one who's been dead several times longer than they've been alive, who has killed more times than they can remember, has been listening to the Beast so closely that the Man is becoming a reflex memory, would have it the other way around, would have to really scrape to find common ground with the LeS' mission plan of "save and/or punish sinners for their own good/God."

                                    Or, in OTHER other words, Theban Sorcery doesn't require you to be a good person, necessarily, any more than Cruac requires you to be a shameless monster - you just have to understand what a good person would have done and why before it'll work.
