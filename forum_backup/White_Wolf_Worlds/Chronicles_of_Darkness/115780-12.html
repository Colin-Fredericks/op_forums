                                            Protean claws don't do Aggravated any more. They don't even do Lethal to vampires any more until level 4. You can take a Merit to get aggravated claws, but you need Protean 4 as a prerequisite, and then they only deal agg while you're frenzying. Aggravated damage is much more rare now than it was before the update, and the way Vampire's damage reduction works is that mundane sources of lethal deal bashing and mundane sources of Agg deal lethal. Supernatural sources are not downgraded.
