        So I'd established that Jabberwocks were a Wyrd 5 hobgoblin previously in-game. For the situation in question, the Wizened who had Discreet Summons wanted to know if they could summon one as a distraction, to give a bunch of Pandorans that could feed on Glamour (long story) something else to gnaw on and/or get crushed by while she ran for the hills. Discreet Summons can only call Hobgoblins with Wyrd 3 or less, so I ruled that the player could call a "young" Jabberwock for the purpose. It worked.

        Edit: The "Wait, what?" reaction was from the other players, who hadn't realized Discreet Summons could be used to summon bestial Hobgoblins like the Jabberwock or other Hedge Beasts just as well as calling up specialist Hobs.
