        A period during the colonization of the Americas for Mummy would be great, because you get the chance to be among the first Arisen to arrive there.

        I forget who it is who has the "Dark Eras: Electroweak Epoch" sig, but that could actually work for Genius or Mage...the only good thing about the wait for this is that I don't have to rush to get the existing Dark Eras updated for Transgression and TLC (if it's even needed, tbh. DE stuff tends not to need much modification).

        Hmmmm, thinking about those two projects going forward...does anyone have any ideas for a Mage: The Transgression era? The obvious one, though, is to just do The War in the middle of the twentieth century between the Peerage and Lemuria. I might actually do that an include it with the Player's Guide.

        And I'm not seeing many Promethean suggestions, which is a shame but understandable. On the topic of future/alternate history Dark Eras, though, I proposed "Art Deco Dieselpunk Retro-Future Promethean" as an Era based on Metropolis, Janelle Monae, and the Protomen. Speaking of, that's planned for the Lightning Chronicle Guide too. I might stick the updated Handful of Dust there, but I'm not that fond of it.

        atamajakki, would you mind if I wrote up your proposed 1950s Promethean setting for TLC? If yes, I'll credit you for the concept in whatever way you want.

        And this is only somewhat related to the thread topic, but dear god I would be ecstatic to see anything from Mirrors show up again, and having a post-apocalyptic or Infinite Macabre Dark Era would be a super cool way to do that. So there's another question- if DE2 was going to have post-apocalypse, Infinite Macabre, and Bleeding Edge as Eras, which would you give to which gameline? Personally, I'd do Post-Apoc Vampire/Werewolf, Infinite Macabre Beast/Demon, and Bleeding Edge Promethean/Deviant.

        ...argh, if I wasn't writing so much other stuff right now I'd probably be making plans to actually write up those six ideas.

                                        I think I've said this elsewhere, but I think 1930s Shanghai (under Japanese occupation) makes a fantastic "and the kitchen sink" crossover setting. This is a world where Triad gangsters, White Russian expats, Imperial Japanese soldiers, Nazi spies, and Western playboys with more money than sense all fight, mingle, and die under the glittering neon lights; with the mortal situation already so volatile, adding vampires and mummies barely even registers as odd. You could either play it as a glamorous noir story as the last gasp of an era crushed by foreign oppression or as high-flying, bad guy-punching action in the streets.

                                        Remi. she/her. game designer.
