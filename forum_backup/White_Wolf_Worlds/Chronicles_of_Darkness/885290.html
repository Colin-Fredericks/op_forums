    [Pet Peeve] The Earthquake Tilt: Why is this still a thing?

    05-08-2016, 07:33 AM
    [This is a a slightly ranty, but please don't take it too seriously. The Tilt is such a small thing that it hasn't impacted my enjoyment of the game, and I am more amused about it than annoyed. I wouldn't even post about it if didn't find the the Tilt slightly funny and the new Mage book hadn't gone so far as to write new Earthquake rules specifically for a spell just to avoid the Tilt]
    The earthquake tilt. I enjoy the CofD and don't have the problems with the ruleset many others seem to have. So that my biggest Pet Peeve is this insignificant Tilt that doesn't impact my enjoyment of the game at all says a lot IMO. But we just got Mage 2e and it's still there, so... this is today's edition of How Is This Still A Thing: The Earthquake Tilt.

    So you don't have to look it up, here are it's mechanics:

    When the quake’s actually occurring, all Dexterity-based dice
    pools (and Defense) suffer a –1 to –5 penalty depending on the
    quake’s severity. Characters take between one and three points
    of lethal damage per turn of the quake’s duration, though a
    reflexive Stamina + Athletics roll can downgrade that damage
    to bashing — or cancel it entirely on an exceptional success

    That's incredibly deadly, will kill most of living thing in the area and leave buildings entirely unharmed. I don't even know what it's supposed to represent, do earthquakes in the CofD consist of shards of glas getting shot out of the ground? Does poison rain from the sky? I don't know, but whatever it is, that Tilt doesn't look like the effects of an Earthquake. And I just don't understand how it's still a thing. It's not like this is the first comment about this Tilt.

    Hell, at least some writers seem to agree, because whoever wrote the Earthquake spell didn't go the wordcount saving path of letting the spell apply the Earthquake Tilt. No, they seemingly agree that the Tilt is bonkers and wrote up entirely new rules for the effects of an earthquake just for that spell! And yet, despite that, the Tilt itself is still in the back of the book.

    Earthquake Tilt: Why does it remain unchanged four books into the line and what does it even model? Are Earthquake hotspots like Japan in the CofD deserted wastelands where only the structures remain undamaged and not a living thing remains?

      Tilts represent their effects on characters as opposed to objects in the surrounding area. I imagine that the tilt is supposed to represent debris and whatnot raining down on PCs and whatnot from above, or being slammed headfirst into a wall with a particularly violent shift of the ground beneath them. Any effects on buildings and the like are probably down t whatever the ST decides on.

      I mean, you bring up this tilt, but there are other tilts which would have an impact on buildings which aren't represented in the rules. Where's the buildings being torn apart by a hurricane? Where's the damage to electronics from floods? etc.

                      The Earthquake Tilt doesn't represent earthquakes accurately because the only situation where its effects make sense is being inside a collapsing building, or possibly a landslide. Whether an earthquake actually makes a building collapse depends on how well it's constructed as much as the quake's strength; and there are several things that can make a building collapse other than an earthquake. IOW the Tilt is simply misnamed - it should be called Avalanche or Massive Property Damage or Mid-Demolition, and earthquakes should be mentioned only as a possible cause.
