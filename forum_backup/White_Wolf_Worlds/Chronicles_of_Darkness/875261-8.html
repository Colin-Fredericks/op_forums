    Mana is your "Power Point" resource thingy. Characters start the game with their mana bar full; it's a change from 1st Edition, where characters started with Mana based on their Wisdom. How much is "full" depends on your Gnosis - basically, each Gnosis point increases your total maximum by 1. So Gnosis 1 gives you 10 maximum (and starting) Mana, Gnosis 2 gives you 11, and so on. Honestly, I don't think you need to put the mana thing at all, as it seems to be very wordy without actually being useful .

    As for sheet, don't worry, it's not a problem! I'm glad I noticed it before everyone ported their sheets, because they would probably kill me :P. Sorry if I sounded mad or something, I just posted it quickly while trying to give bugsplat information.

                                                        Originally posted by Mr Gone View Post
                                                        Though, I'm not sure why I'm only hearing about Tilts now if there important enough to go on the sheet. It'd have been easier to plan for it when I was first building the sheet. What are Tilts?
                                                        The Nimbus Tilt in particular is the mage equivalent of Vampire's Predatory Aura and Werewolf's Hunter's Aspect; people who witness you casting a spell or flaring your Nimbus are subject to a brief and minor effect that can add or subtract dice from certain Attribute rolls or apply positive or negative dice tricks to certain Skill rolls for a couple of turns. The Tilt is personalized from character to character and can change completely as your Gnosis grows.
