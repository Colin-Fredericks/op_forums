                                10-23-2016, 03:31 PM
                                Mana can't be converted to Essence without Prime+Spirit 4 (Or passing it to your familiar in which case the conversion is automatic). Note that when you channel Essence into yourself it doesn't convert the Essence into Mana, it just makes you a convenient storage place for Essence. If you have Essence in your Pattern you can use Channel Essence to give some or all of that Essence to a spirit. It's pretty easy to create Essence though, for instance, to create Fire Essence, you light a fire and then a spirit that consumes Fire Essence can harvest from it.
