                                                            This is interesting, but I'm curious to see how it plays out.

                                                            It's interesting that they're giving it an "X Chronicles" title, since that follows the model for the standalone lines. My presumption is that, like the Strix or God-Machine or Slashers or whomever, Contagion will represent some sort of existential danger that ties into the book's theme, without superimposing those threats onto the other game lines. So I guess the question is, what sort of overarching theme makes sense for all of the splats (or whichever splats they include, rather. Perhaps this isn't a full-bore crossover?).

                                                            I'd guess, since it's supposed to be a crossover game, it's not going to be standalone in the same way as the 2e cores, but is it possible it could be? A core book that uses a variation of Dread Powers for character building, some specific crunch for monster-on-monster interaction, and simplified versions of the core monsters as splat templates? If the Contagion mutates the various supernaturals, that could provide good reason to get creative with the mixing and matching of power sets.
