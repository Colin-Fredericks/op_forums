                                                  Originally posted by xenoterracide View Post

                                                  I'd be interested in an Infrastructure toolkit, like the core Horror system but for Infrastructure. Also more examples, also maybe examples of building an infrastructure system. Inputs to outputs and all that.

                                                  I’m intrigued by the idea of a toolkit, Horror-like approach, though I think it would be tricky, because Infrastructure is weird and fundamentally it has few traits: what shape it takes, what protection it has, intended outputs and how you could hack it. The coach is, in many ways, a bad example ‘cause it’s a plot device to facilitate a time travel plot. Compare it the library in Flowers of Hell or, well, anything from the Aztec Demon chapter. Those examples have a clear purpose (even if it’s wonderfully crazy, like make scorpions intelligent) and even suggestions on how to hack it. The Aztec examples are really fantastic because without context they’re as odd as the couch, but in context they work very well.

                                                  Actually, here's a quick and dirty explanation for the couch. It's similar, though much smaller, device to the ‘alternate timeline crumple-zone’ Infrastructure beneath the Golden Gate in Istanbul. Using it for time travel is actually a hack, an unintended application.
