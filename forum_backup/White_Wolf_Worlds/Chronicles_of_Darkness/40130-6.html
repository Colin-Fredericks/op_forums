    Originally posted by quantumelody View Post

    The new version of Simulacrum above is (mostly) styled like new Harmony, but there is also a scale of penalties for human interaction and behavior. That part was cool about vampire. What kinds of Breaking points are you guys thinking about?
    Most will provably adhere to the list of existing breaking points in WtF 2e Harmony or be very similar, but there's one or two that should only apply to werewolves so we'll be taking those out. We're thinking of placing the use of some powers as breaking points after certain levels of Harmony. The Projection Siddhi, vital as it is to the Purified condition, will also be affected by Harmony. High enough, you're going to have a hard time getting out of your body or even reforming it if you die. (Death and ressurecting are probably also going to be a breaking point.) With low Harmony, their spiritual weaknesses will become more severe or you'll gain more of them as Werewolves gain Bans.

    And eating human flesh for Essence (and only human flesh) will be an option, as well as a penalized breaking point the same way it is for Werewolves devouring human and wolf flesh.

                    The reason Aiden and I decided to use Harmony as an alternative to Integrity with Octavo's GMC Purified Hack is because we believe there is thematically fitting conflict to be drawn from it. Purified aren't true spirits. But they aren't exactly humans anymore, neither in soul or body. This divide between their humanity and their closeness to spirits, and their need to balance it, live with the fact that they are forcibly part of both worlds, has always been a keystone of their existence far as we're concerned. How they chose to behave, and to what side of their dual life they are drawn towards is even more important for them than for Werewolves in some ways; because for the majority, becoming Purified to begin with was a deliberate, conscious decision. A change they might have dedicated themselves to and pursued for several decades.

                    Their minds are affected by this change as well, forcing them to deal with things human perspective isn't equipped towards. Purified aren't natural. Being one has the logical consequence of causing your mentality to change to compensate; or to ground itself more strongly to retain your self-awareness as a human. Hence, Harmony. Because the balance between the different facets of what they are, or lack thereof, is important to a Purified's identity, and it makes sense that it would influence their state of being and their abilities reliant on thus.

                    The premise we start from for the Purified is different from the one Kaye and Callishka assume in their much more extense mod. I also haven't really read it, so I don't know the details of Simulacrum; but what I remember of its narrative and thematic meaning doesn't really fit with Harmony, in my opinion.

                                    I think that there are some moments of alignment in how we are approaching Purified, even if the core conceptual framework is different. It would be interesting to hear your thoughts after reading it.

                                    Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post
                                    I think my beef with is is that Werewolves are actually hybrids, so Harmony makes sense as keeping the balance between the two. Like Amechra says, if the emphasis is on the Purified not being human anymore, but basically humans transfigurated into spirits, then it's not a question of achieving harmony between your two halves, as you don't have two halves. You're one whole Spirit, clinging to the humanity it once had.
                                    To expand on what I said to amechra above, one of the thought problems that I did not feel great about in the first version of this was actually some of the Simulacrum, and so I decided to leave it as-is until I had a good example of a push-and-pull system versus a degenerative one (although I would argue that mashing them up manages to do both). I had this core thought that Purified resisting the creation of Resonant Conditions, resisting Shadow, &c., could cause a Breaking Point just as much as spiraling down into a humanoid spirit barely hanging onto the physical world, but it was difficult to scale at tier levels in a way that I found elegant.

                                    The Spirit Core of a Purified isn't a separate Beast; it's their own mind, and that's what is terrifying about it. There isn't an Other. There is only the terrifying realization that you cannot talk to someone in the checkout line at the grocery store because the fact that they are not buying pine-scented cleaner makes you feel a deep-rooted existential betrayal because their house will not smell like pine. You have three options: One, you can realize that the thought you just had is not human at all, leave the checkout line, and buy a floral-scented cleaner. Two, you can stay in the checkout lane and decide that humans just might not be that into your Influence (Pine), so you don't want their house to be Resonant with it, anyway. The hater. Third, you can indulge in that deeply inhuman reflex and let the existential betrayal turn into a tantrum of Numina or dirty looks. One side reinforces socially normal behavior and preserves/increases the Simulacrum, and one side does not, but neither leaves you feeling completely easy. (Having a conscience, even an artificial reflection of one, makes denying spirit impulses just as disturbing as overindulging ... until you slip one way or the other.) The middle path keeps things as they are.

                                    That said, I don't think Purified care about processed food or associate it with being more human. That definitely had to go. I mean, for Outer Paths, Purification Juice is the least natural concoction a person can drink. ^____^ And I think the mods could be done either with a Humanity-style Simulacrum or a Harmony-style Simulacrum. The in-game situations will be just as complex and rewarding any way one slices it.
