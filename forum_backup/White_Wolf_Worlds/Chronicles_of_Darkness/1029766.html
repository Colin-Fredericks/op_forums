    CofD TV

    01-10-2017, 05:40 PM
    Right, so this could generate groans just by its presence, but.

    I was watching Bitten with my lovely wife this evening. Despite enjoying the books quite a bit, the show itself left me grinding my teeth a bit and daydreaming.

    So how would CofD manage as a properly funded, Netflix style series? In a completely unlikely world, etc.

    The way I see it, you got a few different routes:

    1. You pick a line, and stick with it for a mini series. This lets you tell a coherent story arc with a solid tone, but you do run into the issue of being tied to a single line.

    2. You do a 10 episode season with each ep focusing on a different race. Lets you showcase everyone, but a bit shallow, realistically. (Bonus points if each episode is happening concurrently, so you glimpse moments of overlap between each ep).

    3. You mash the above two options, and do shorter seasons, like the BBC (hello Sherlock and Luther). So season 1 gives you Requiem, Forsaken and Awakening, with each episode being a solid 3 hours or so.


    Tone could be a challenge of sorts. Certain lines make easy comparisons for TV, but others.... at a basic level, I figure:

    Requiem: Game of Thrones political intrigue
    Forsaken: Sons of Anarchy
    Awakening: some kind of detective show? Really unsure.
    Promethean: ?
    Changeling: court intrigue as well? Unsure.
    Hunter: procedural?
    Geist: no idea, but all over it.
    Demon: Person of Interest
    Mummy: ?

    I mean, yes, this will never happen; the cost alone to have it not look like amateur hour would be eye watering. But a guy can dream.
