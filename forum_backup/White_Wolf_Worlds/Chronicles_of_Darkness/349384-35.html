            So having recently seen it, Kubo and the Two Strings works REMARKABLY well as a Changeling story (spoilers, natch)

            The Moon King is the primary title of a Fae, appearing the guise of an Actor. The two Daughters could be either a different Title or a different one, either way appearing in the guise of wisps. Kubo's mother is a Charlatan, another of the Fae's titles which fell to earth after falling in love.

            The plot of the movie is essentially the Moon King's attempts to reclaim the lost Title by killing his daughter and reclaiming his grandson as part of the court.

            He doesn't hate you. He wants to take your eye so you'll be blind to humanity. Then you can take your place by his side, and be just like him; cold and hard and perfect.
