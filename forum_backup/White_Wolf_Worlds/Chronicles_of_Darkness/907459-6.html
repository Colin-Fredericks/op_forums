                                                        Originally posted by Errol216 View Post
                                                        Sigh.

                                                        I said your read was unfair. "Read". As in "interpretation", not "impression". Not that you didn't feel what you felt.

                                                        Seriously, in a thread about how people frequently misread gamelines completely and possibly intentionally, this is the hill you're choosing to die on?
                                                        My "read?" My interpretation? And here I was thinking that saying how I felt about something was still just an impression. Silly me.

                                                        Seriously, people do this all the time one these forums. "I don't like the Seers - help me change them. Please don't comment if you don't want to offer suggestions!" What happens? People come in to the threads and argue up and down that, no, you should like the Seers as is. No changing. They say its unfair, that you're reading things wrong. Knee jerk reactions against people not liking what you like. I've even seen people admit that - they felt like, because what they liked wasn't liked by others, it was somehow an "attack."

                                                        If you're going to make this about a hill to die on, then actually identify the hill first, Errol. I did nothing more than state my feelings on the matter. I even made sure to use the word "felt" to highlight that it was nothing more than my subjective feelings on the matter. And then, you said my feelings were "unfair." As if these feelings were unreasonable or wrong somehow. That might not have been your intent, but that is what happened. People should be allowed to not like something, for any reason, without it being 'splained to why they are wrong.

                                                        I think that's the heart of these "you're missing the point!" threads. These are subjective genres, but if someone doesn't like the same thing another person did, then that latter person reads into it negatively. Then, there has to be a post that says, no! wrong! Or unfair, or something like that. Somehow, invariably, the opposing opinion needs to be undermined. "If you don't agree with me, then you must be missing the point." Things might get lost when people get insulting or patronizing towards one another, but I think that's the heart of the matter. One person doesn't like an aspect, and wants to change it. Other people like the original, feel threatened, and have to "fight back."

                                                        You see it all the time, but that doesn't make it right. Its nothing more than a knee jerk "Us Versus Them" mentality that doesn't really work in a society. There are a wide variety of opinions, and none of them should be dismissed out of hand. I don't like something, because feelings. You like something, because feelings. Great. Both are fair.
