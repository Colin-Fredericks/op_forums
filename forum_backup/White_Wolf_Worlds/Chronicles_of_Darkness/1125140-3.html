                                                            Enthusiastic Creative Thaumaturgy based on pure speculation? But of course!

                                                            Superlative Luck (Fate Ruling) + (Spirit Weaving)
                                                            For the Duration of this Spell, its target gains a Resonant Condition for Spirits of accidents. This takes place even when the Spell is used to place a Boon, so the target must be planning to engage in acts of sabotage, if they're not to offend the denizens of the Shadow.

                                                            Terrorize (Mind Unraveling) + (Time Patterning)
                                                            In addition to being flooded with fear, the target also experiences a vision of a horrifying future. This Prophecy is accurate unless interfered with, and may depict anything that would terrify the target, whether related to their life or not.

                                                            Control Light (Forces Unveiling) + (Death Fraying)
                                                            Light modified through this Spell has the additional effect of inflicting the Stunned Tilt on Cthonians.

                                                            I've sometimes felt that Spells in Mage are rather sterile, but accepted it as a necessity of the system. Palimpsest Rotes are what I always wanted to have from Awakening, but were afraid to ask. However, if I read the preview correctly, the following Spell couldn't be produced by a Palimpsest, due to doubling down upon the same Arcanum - we'd get an extra Reach instead.

                                                            Purge Illness (Life Shielding) + (Life Weaving)
                                                            Purged pathogens are vomited out by the target and upon touching inert matter are transformed into poisonous fungi for the duration of the Spell.

                                                            There are also a few things I feel are ambiguous:

                                                            + I assumed that each Rote has a fixed blended effect from the moment the Palimpsest is completed, as opposed to making each casting unpredictable. Am I correct?
                                                            + Could a Mage benefit from Arcanum 5 blended effect, if they only have a single dot in the Rote's main Arcanum? The text suggests they can.
                                                            + Could any Mage, whether a Master or not, create Rotes based on echoes from a Palimpsest? The text suggests they can.
                                                            + Could Rotes with extra Reach be learned by a Mage, or do they remain a unique property of the Grimoire? The text suggests the former.

                                                            I also have doubts concerning Daimonomicon as a Merit. A five-Dot Grimoire gives a character access to 10 Rotes they wouldn't otherwise have, and even when fully internalized, the Grimoire still aids spellcasting. A five-Dot Daimonomicon on the other hand doesn't grant any powers until the player spends Experiences on Attainments, and at that point the Daimonomicon becomes mostly useless to it's owner - at least from a mechanical standpoint. With Sanctity of Merits, Daimonomicon is merely a piggy bank for Experiences, with minor use as a sympathetic Yantra. Even Techné is only worth two Dots.
