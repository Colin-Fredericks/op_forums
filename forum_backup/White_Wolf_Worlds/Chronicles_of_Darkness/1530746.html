    The Azure Cranes, a Moros Legacy of healer alchemists

    02-13-2024, 12:11 PM
    Origins

    “The aim of medicine is to prevent disease and prolong life; the ideal of medicine is to eliminate the need of a physician.”
    - William James Mayo

    Nicknames: Furnace lords
    Parentage: Moros, Adamantine Arrow, Free Council, Mysterium

    Background: The Azure Cranes were founded during the Warring States period by a Moros called Bian Que, although information about his life is fragmented at best.

    According to one story, he developed it through extensively studying the methods of his namesake, a legendary physician from the Yellow Emperor's era, creating a synthesis of lost techniques from the bronze uchronia.

    Another telling cast him as an alchemist distressed with how often failed elixirs had lethal results, which lead him to re-examine the fundamentals and create his own alchemical framework, with the legend stating his greatest discoveries still lie in his lost laboratory.

    Some even whisper he was not the first member, but rather that he received the initiation rites from one of the immortals, or perhaps even an archmaster, his work needed for some greater purpose. The truth of the matter is still unclear, with folklore only providing an account of his life after that point.

    What is known is that he and his students were very active healers in multiple states, codifying their findings even as they searched for new techniques.

    With the advent of the silk road, scholars found themselves with an unprecedented ability to visit new lands, leading the Azure Cranes to travel to the far corners of the world in quests to expand their stores of knowledge, from learning herbalism and poisons at the court of Mithridates, to researching the surgical and occult principles of Sushruta's medicine, to uncovering new herbs and tinctures in Atlantean ruins. In time they would find natural partners in the Keepers of the Word and Adamantine Arrow, pursuing new medical insights alongside the former and resonating with the humanitarian relief missions of the latter. In modern times, Libertines have also been drawn to the cranes, seeking to redefine the bleeding edge of medicine by reconciling it with alchemical secrets.

    Appearances: Verdant greens and indigos are commonly found in the clothes of the furnace lords, invoking the color qīng’s association with prosperity, health and harmony. Cranes, often seen as omens of happiness and longevity, are also a common sight in their phone cases, ties, cufflinks and even logos for their medical practices.

    Because they are often called to act as combat medics and as part of the first responders in relief missions, as well as their inclination to search the Tapestry for new medical insights, most members are in surprisingly good shape.

    Driven by their quest to develop the grand theory of alchemy, their Sanctums display pill furnaces, cauldrons and other alchemical apparatus coexisting with cutting edge pharmaceutical equipment. Greenhouses and herbal gardens abound, containing everything from plants whose properties were magically catalyzed, genetically modified trees, herbs transplanted from Emanations, rare flora recovered from Atlantean ruins and even new species cultivated in Verges.

    When attending a formal event of the Legacy or Consilia, wearing a hechang daopao is customary, with a crane motif integrated into the outfit. When a member has achieved a certain level of skill, they normally add stylized depictions of an alchemical pill furnace to the sleeves, the color indicating their level of progress. Violet indicates someone able to mentor others in the Legacy, jade green represents an adept and azure is the sign of a master.

    Doctrine

    “Medicine is not only a science; it is also an art. It does not consist of compounding pills and plasters; it deals with the very processes of life, which must be understood before they may be guided.”
    - Paracelsus

    Prerequisites: Life 2, Medicine 2, two dots in one of the following Skills and with an Alchemy specialty: Crafts, Occult, Science.

    Initiation: The mage prepares an alchemical pill furnace made of imperial jade, inscribed with High Speech runes. Utilizing it, they concoct a medicinal pill whose ingredients include water from the River Lethe (representing drinking the tea of forgetfulness upon rebirth), a peach from a tree that was grown in a Node (symbolizing the peaches of immortality), as well as a plethora of herbs. When the refining process is at its peak, the mage invests it with a portion of their Awakened soul, similar to how they would create a soul stone, allowing it to be shaped by the process. The ceremony is complete when they consume the resulting medicinal pill, reintegrating the transmuted part of their soul.

    Seeking out the ingredients is a crucial part of the initiation, imparting two key lessons to the prospective member. The first is that the grand theory of alchemy must be proactively sought, an alchemist will not make major breakthroughs only in the lab. In seeking out the ingredients, the initiate familiarizes themselves with field work. Perhaps they research locations of a Node which contains peaches, some seek out Sin-Eaters as guides on the journey to the River Lethe or find Goblin Markets where an ingredient may be bartered for.

    The second lesson is meticulousness. The consequences of improperly practiced alchemy can be just as severe as medical malpractice. The scene long ceremony for concocting the pill is more than a celebration of the fruits of the initiate's labor, but it also showcases their methodical focus to the other members of the apothecary.

    Traditionally it is the responsibility of the mentor to provide the pill furnace, as a way to honor the student's diligence.

    Organization: Furnace lords traditionally gather into apothecaries, where they can pool together their knowledge and cooperate in researching the grand theory of alchemy. In Caucuses where they have a greater presence, they often push to create an emergency supply of cures and vaccines to the most notorious supernatural illnesses.

    Theory: Similarly to how a pill furnace refines ingredients into medicine, so too do challenge and adversity refine the self. With each tribulation overcome, the body, mind and soul draw closer to the Supernal. However, illness and suffering can pollute this process, creating bottlenecks to the cultivation of the self.

    The highest goal of alchemy, therefore, is to provide humanity with the health and longevity needed to pursue enlightenment and prosperity. By practicing internal alchemy, one's control of Qi can be refined, allowing the balance between body and soul to be restored and maladies healed.

    Some supernatural afflictions cannot be remedied with those techniques alone. In the same way that Heaven and Earth depend on each other for balance and wholeness, so too must internal alchemy be paired with its external counterpart. By studying the myriad herbs, medical texts and techniques of the healers, both mundane and occult, new cures can be created.

    Seek out knowledge of the healing arts, wield internal and external alchemy together as one. Only then will you be able to create the grand theory of alchemy, and humanity transcend all bodily ills.

    Magic

    “Medicine is the restoration of discordant elements; sickness is the discord of the elements infused into the living body.”
    -Leonardo da Vinci

    Ruling Arcanum: Life
    Yantras: Pharmacies (+2), hospitals (+2), alchemical labs (+2), cranes (+1 for images, +2 for living ones), medical or alchemical documents (+1, +2 if relevant to the spell or written on jade slips), medical implements (+1), remedies (+1, +2 if they contain supernatural ingredients), acupuncture needles (+1, +2 if handmade based on the Lingshu), moxibustion set (+1, +2 if artisanally made based on the Bian Que Neijing), collecting medical ingredients (+1), succeeding in a Medicine or alchemy roll related to the spell (+2), collecting medical/alchemical samples or recording medical/alchemical data relevant to the spell (+1).

    Oblations: Researching new medical techniques, tending a medicinal garden, writing a medical paper, ministering to patients, working at a pharmacy, teaching medicine or alchemy, deciphering alchemical texts, concocting medicine.

    Attainments

    First: Physician's Insight
    Prerequisites: Initiation

    The most fundamental skill for healing a patient is making an accurate diagnosis. The Azure Crane reads the flow of the patient’s Qi circulatory system, allowing them to holistically assess their health and tailor their treatments accordingly.

    This replicates Analyze Life with Reach assigned to instant use.

    Optional: Death 1

    When dealing with deadly poisons or dangerous drugs, time is of the essence, and antitoxins may not always be on hand. Furnace lords have learned to use their Qi to help their patient's bodies fight off and break down these dangerous substances, reducing their toxicity by [Death]. Should the toxicity be reduced to zero, the patient is cured of it. Because of this, it is said no furnace lord has ever lost a drinking contest.

    This is a custom Compelling of Life and Death, with Reach assigned to instant use.

    Second: The Subtle Elixir
    Prerequisites: Life 2, Medicine 3, Area of Expertise (Alchemy)

    The Azure Crane focuses their Qi into a healing pulse, which purifies the twelve main and eight extraordinary meridians, allowing the patient's Qi to flow uninterrupted once more, curing any diseases.

    This replicates Purge Illness. Reach is assigned to instant use.

    Optional: Death 2

    Patients that have lost body parts often feel as if they were still attached. The Azure Cranes know this is caused by the spirit roots of the limb still being connected to their phantom version in Twilight, a fact which can be leveraged to restore them.

    After a scene of practicing qigong, the furnace lord becomes attuned to the flow of Death Twilight, allowing them to summon items from it once more to the world.

    Lost limbs can be returned, taking the appearance they once had and functioning normally. However, this is only a temporary solution, since once the furnace lord loses attunement, the limbs return to Twilight and must be restored once more.

    This replicates Touch of the Grave, with conjunctional Life allowing for the restoration of body parts. Reach is assigned to advanced duration.

    Third: Surgeon's Panoply
    Prerequisites: Life 3, two dots in a second Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills

    Surgery carries several risks, a poorly executed procedure can be lethal, performing it requires specialized equipment, even a successful one leaves the patient weakened during recovery, and infection is always a risk. Utilizing their extensive alchemical knowledge and finely honed control, the Azure Cranes have learned to direct and concentrate the flow of Qi to alter the body's processes, fleshcrafting it and bypassing these risks entirely. Potency can be allocated, on a one to one basis, for any of the following effects:
    • End up to [Potency] physical tilts or conditions, so long as they are not caused by missing limbs or organs.
    • Stabilize a target.
    • Degrade [Potency] wounds, from aggravated to lethal or lethal to bashing, starting with the most serious ones. Wounds can be degraded multiple times.
    • Perform any medical procedure without equipment, gaining [Potency] extra dice to the rolls.
    • Afflict the target with up to [Potency] physical tilts or conditions, such as sealing their mouths by welding bone, mold eyes shut or bind hands together. These effects can be undone by another application of the Attainment. Furnace lords acting as combat medics or field researchers often employ this in self defense.
    This is a Potency primary custom spell, which utilizes a Life version of Shaping, Withstood by Stamina. Reach is assigned to instant use and sensory range.

    Optional: Death 3

    The three dantian are the core of the Qi circulatory system, and as long as they continue to function, the fire of life shall burn brightly. The furnace lord is now able to envelop the dantians with protective Qi, allowing them to shield their allies, safeguard their patients and persevere on dangerous battlefields.

    Whenever a subject under this effect is about to die, it is automatically ended, the subject retains one health, is stabilized and becomes fully conscious.

    However, despite their vitals being stable, superficial signs of damage still remain. Burns, bullet wounds and slashes remain visible, creating a haunting visage.

    This is a custom Shielding of Life and Death, with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.
    ​
    Fourth: Meridian Reconstruction
    Prerequisites: Life 4, Medicine 4, Interdisciplinary Specialty (Alchemy)

    The Azure Crane’s research has expanded their finesse to the point where they can remodel the Qi circulatory system, allowing them to perform reconstructive and plastic surgeries. After spending a point of mana, they manifest Qi infused needles, which are applied to the patient's acupoints over the course of a scene. When these needles enter the body, they shoot out threads of Qi which restructure it. Scars and burns can be healed, poor vision or other senses corrected, and even height and fitness altered.

    The appearance of the patient can be modified to such an extent that they can be endowed with ethereal beauty or made into a visage of horror, allowing the furnace lord to grant them up to [Potency] dots in Striking Looks, which may exceed the merit's dot limit.

    This replicates Many Faces. Reach is assigned to indefinite duration, with a mana point being used for indefinite duration.

    Optional: Death 4

    The furnace lord's Qi can now bring the body in harmony with the soul, granting it great resilience and allowing it to restore itself by wielding the power of shen, the energy of the soul. A patient under this effect does not bleed out or fall unconscious from damage, unless they wish otherwise. In addition, the patient's body is now able to store [Death] points of ectoplasm, which may be used reflexively in any of the following ways:
    • A point can be used to heal a physical Tilt or degrade an injury by one level, from aggravated to lethal, lethal to bashing or bashing to none. Wounds can be degraded multiple times.
    • Two points can heal a non-permanent physical condition.
    • Three points can heal a permanent physical condition and can regrow a missing body part.
    Furnace lords have learned how to disguise ectoplasm as supplements, pills, tinctures, booster shots and more, promoting robust health through enhanced healing.

    This is a custom, Potency primary, Weaving of Life and Death. Reach is assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

    Fifth: The Azure Furnace
    Prerequisites: Life 5, two dots in a third Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills

    The furnace lord's Qi becomes both pill furnace and alchemical flame. When harnessed to refine mana and combined with their alchemical insights, it allows them to elevate Pattern Restoration to new heights. The furnace lord can now use it to heal resistant damage. Additionally, they are able to heal aggravated wounds at a rate of 3 mana per point.

    Lastly, the furnace lord is able to concoct the Qi and refined mana into a remedy they can extrude, which can be applied through contact to use Pattern Restoration on a patient. Alternatively, it can be stored for later use, this medicine never expires and can be administered by all known means (oral, IV, inhalation, subcutaneous injection, etc), although it is usually made into a medical pill. Should the required mana be greater than the furnace lord's mana per turn limit (such as one that heals 8 aggravated damage), they may take multiple turns to synthesize it.

    Optional: Death 5

    The Azure Crane learns to master their alchemical flame further. With a touch they are able to apply its power via indirect moxibustion, its heat burning away pain, healing injury and rejuvenating the patient. The effects of any positive physical changes, such as increased strength or enhanced durability, are not undone, nor are any effects from the other Attainments, as the flame only consumes that which harms the subject.

    This replicates Deny The Reaper with Reach assigned to instant use and conjunctional Life excluding any positive physical changes.
    ​
    What were you thinking design wise ?

    The first Attainment focuses on diagnosis, if the mage cannot cure the patient, then it's likely that it can point them in the direction of a treatment. It's also thematically fitting, since one of the greatest innovations of the historical Bian Que was developing methods of diagnosis.

    The first optional Attainment is a custom spell called Purge Toxin. When looking at Cleanse the Body, it simply adds dice to rolls to resist a toxin, but since mundane poisons can go up to toxicity 8, it means not even a master can guarantee a patient recovers. So by adding conjunctional Death, it helps break down and eliminate the toxins themselves, making it a much more useful ability in an emergency.

    The second Attainment is Purge Illness, guaranteeing that supernatural cures to mundane diseases are always available and free from The Lie.

    The second optional Attainment is based on phantom limbs, with the supernatural truth behind them being imperceptible to mundane senses. By using Touch of the Grave, they can be temporarily recalled. With conjunctional Life, they can be reattached and won't look ghostly. This allows the Azure Cranes to have an alternative comparable to Regeneration, but much faster in practice once the Attainment is active. All the general utility of Touch of the Grave is available, while at the same time providing magical prosthetics when needed.

    The third Attainment is a Life version of Shaping, inspired by the Vicissitude Discipline from Vampire: The Masquerade. It allows them to heal allies by fleshcrafting injuries away, even across a battlefield. It also provides a means of self defense, plus some utility for capturing enemies and a means to be intimidating. Should the medical problem be more complex, it allows them to operate without risk of infection and with a bonus comparable to a well equipped emergency room.

    The third optional Attainment is based on Death Ward from D&D. A combat medic is no good if they can't survive, so this goes one step further and creates a safety net for their allies as well. The visuals of the patient showing superficial wounds was an idea of orathaic, which allows patients to play up the sheer visual shock of seeing it happen.

    The fourth Attainment covers reconstructive and plastic surgery. Since it provides a permanent disguise, which can't be dispelled, it's an excellent way to mask their moves. When combined with Suppress Aura, it can allow them to act anonymously. I thought it would be appropriate for a skilled magical plastic surgeon to be capable of altering the patient's physical appearance beyond what mundane doctors could, so I added the ability to grant dots in Striking Looks. The activation ritual is based on Tohou Fuhai's Human Modification Technique from Rosario + Vampire.

    The fourth optional Attainment grants a version of Plasmic Healing. While not as powerful as a Sin-Eater's, it works as a versatile first aid kit, making it more likely the users make it back to the Azure Crane. It also provides true limb regeneration, which is a major boon. However, the healing is not enough to push a patient out of the range of damage penalties (at least not in lethal combat) and it takes away from the action economy to resupply the first aid kit, either by the patient using their supplies or the Death caster using their turn. However, it gives enough breathing room to aid survival. During downtime, having an Apprentice of Death is more than enough to guarantee a full heal and allow the cabal to forge ahead.

    The capstone Attainment allows them to develop a master level version of Pattern Restoration. The ability is inspired by Meng Hao's East Pill Everburning Flame from I Shall Seal The Heavens, a pill concocting alchemical fire that allows him to perform alchemy anywhere. By utilizing the Contact High spell as a basis, the alchemist can create medicine anywhere, with the cost of indefinite duration being subsumed into the total cost. The ability to heal resistant damage was based on the Crown of Doctrine, while transforming it into a better version of Pattern Restoration is influenced by the updated version of the Perfected Adepts by @21C Hermit, which expanded on Arcana Attainments in fascinating ways. The Attainment is fully compatible with Improved Pattern Restoration and should allow its benefits when healing others.

    Being able to heal resistant damage is a major ability, but it quickly burns through the mage’s mana, making it so it can’t be used often without preparation.

    The optional capstone gives them the ability to restore any recent injury without using resources, as well provide a form of eternal youth, which resonates with the xianxia inspiration of the Legacy.

    Some of the Attainments, such as the third and fourth main ones, sound like they could be very unpleasant to experience. But this is deliberately left ambiguous. The Attainments were written to indicate a carefully honed sense of finesse, so a furnace lord should be able to make the process painless, but those who do not care or have malicious intent could certainly make the experience excruciating.

    The original concept for the Legacy was “Medical care available anytime and anywhere”. So each Attainment focused on a different medical niche, each one contributing a different tool for survival to the kit, this allowed me to avoid problems like most of the significant healing being concentrated in the last two Attainments.

    I also wanted to provide an ancient feel to the Legacy, since using magic to cure what medicine couldn't was probably a very attractive prospect. The Mesopotamian Ašipu were a strong candidate, since they had surprisingly robust medical writings. But because Tome of the Pentacle set their focus as exorcists (historical ones focused on divination, exorcism and medicine), I moved on to other options.

    The Egyptian swnw were another strong contender, mixing naming magic with medicine, but unfortunately I couldn't get all Attainments to fit into their methods.

    Eventually I decided to focus on traditional Chinese medicine, with some xianxia inspirations. Internal alchemy and Qi manipulation would provide a framework for the magic system and external alchemy would handle anything the Attainments couldn't cure.

    The name Azure Cranes has two associated symbols. The first is the color qīng, which means both green and blue (similar to the word aoi in Japanese), with Azure being an approximate translation. Qīng is associated with health, prosperity and longevity. It also represents the Life (green) and Death (blue) Arcana the furnace lords wield with their healing techniques.

    The second is the meaning of cranes in Chinese mythology. They symbolize elegance and longevity, with images of cranes flying up being a symbol of prosperity. They are also associated with the xian/immortals, flying them on their backs to immortal lands like the island of Penglai or mount Kunlun. Sometimes they are depicted as carrying the herb of immortality or the peaches of immortality, which is one of the most auspicious symbols of longevity.

    The origins of Chinese alchemy are nebulous at best. They have been ascribed to the Yellow Emperor, but both this account and the book of medical techniques prescribed by him (since alchemy and medicine were one at the time) are from the Warring States era. Simultaneously, the origin has been attributed to the physician Bian Que, also from the Warring States era, who wrote his own medical book and whose life is surrounded by myth. In one story, for example, he was given diagnostic vision because he consumed medicine gifted by an immortal. Adding to the confusion, there existed a legendary physician in the Yellow Emperor's era, who was also called Bian Que and was also said to be the inventor of alchemy. So I decided to capitalize on this ambiguity to create a potential Mystery to be explored.

    The background also expands on the cosmopolitan outlook of the Legacy. I imagine that after seeing some of the alchemical Truths of Stygia, it would reinforce the idea that no one has the full picture and incentivize them to proactively seek new sources of knowledge. This holistic approach is further reinforced by the Attainment prerequisites. It also creates a source of possible plot hooks, such as investigating the methodologies of the Mesen-Nebu, researching Pyros based alchemy, seeking the Elixir formulas of the Ascending Ones, etc.

    The appearances section reinforces the symbolism of the name. The utilization of a crane Daoist robe, as well having pill furnaces embroidered in their sleeves, is inspired by the Violet Fate Sect, a major alchemy faction in I Shall Seal The Heavens. The nickname furnace lords is a reference to the title of the highest ranked members.

    The colors of the sleeve embroideries have symbolic meaning as well. Violet is associated with Laozi, it is said that an astronomer once saw violet Qi accumulating in the east, which he interpreted as an omen of a sage or enlightened person heading in their direction. When Laozi passed by on his way out of the country, the astronomer persuaded him to write his teachings down, which became the Tao Te Ching. Because of that, violet became a color associated with spiritual awareness, so a furnace lord with purple sleeves understands the philosophy of the Legacy well enough to initiate someone into it. Jade is a symbol of balance and healing, an Azure Crane with jade sleeves is skilled enough to heal your limbs back permanently. Azure sleeves link thematically to the final Attainment and represent a complete understanding of their methodology.

    The organizational structure establishes members as being in regular contact with each other and supporting each other's research.

    Lastly, the yantras and oblations incentivize them being actively involved in utilizing the fruits of their research. The yantra for keeping recipes in a jade slip is an homage to a xianxia trope, where jade slips are used to mystically store information.

    That concludes the notes. I hope you had as much fun reading them as I did writing them, thank you in advance for the feedback. A big thanks to orathaic for all his help with this Legacy.

    New experiences are the font of creativity, when seeking inspiration, break your routine.
