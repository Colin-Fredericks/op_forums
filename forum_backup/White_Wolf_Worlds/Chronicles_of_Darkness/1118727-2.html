            Or people who, you know, just don't like killing. Which would be... the grand majority of the population.

            ---

            Anyway, I'd like to point out the near-perfect partnership between a Conjurer and a Titov.

            Turns out bribery powers go really well with make-stuff powers. Plus, they both have a "your power comes at a cost of sanity" theme, so they'll have some common ground there.

            On to the merits...

            Eldritch Lore/Whispers/Secrets don't feel right, as far as "conjuring stuff" powers go. It kind of dilutes the idea of "summoning" if you can also come up with brilliant plans on-the-spot and can read minds.

            I mean, I can kind of see a way of "refactoring" the merits to make them work.

            Conjurer: By "investing" a dot in Integrity, you can create things whose Size and equipment bonus are limited to your dots in this merit. These things...
            1) Must be independent objects.
            2) Must be inanimate.
            3) Cannot be supernatural.
            4) Cannot contain information that you, yourself, don't know (no perfect audio recordings of the Gettysburg Address unless you've actually heard what Lincoln's voice sounded like, and no maps of places you've never been).
            5) Must be inorganic - steel and plastic are fine, leather isn't.

            Merit #1: Lets you conjure things as part of something else, letting you do things like replacing a fuse by tapping the fusebox or replace the engine of your car. The overall object needs to follow all the other restrictions other than size/equipment bonus (you can repair your supercomputer, even if you only have Conjurer •).

            Merit #2: You can give your creations life/motive force. At the very least, you can do Mickey-Mouse Fantasia garbage.

            Merit #3: You can make supernatural stuff now. Lets your items mimic the effects of some supernatural merits, or even Dread Powers if you're good enough.

            Merit #4: Your creations can know things that you don't. Conjure up a flawless map of somewhere you've never been, pull a phone book out of nowhere, or just conjure up a copy of Doom to play during downtime.

            Merit #5: You guessed it - you can do organic stuff now. At the very least, you can make some fine leather jackets.

            The real fun, of course, being that you can combine those upgrade merits. Merits #1 and #5 could let you replace someone's arm, you can make homunculi with Merits #2, #4, and #5...

            So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.
