    Finnish here, and neither have I ever had problems with first tongue. I think only the more unknown sounds (Z's, X's) are harder, but for example Imnir and Meninna, idigam and so are easy as pie.

    Now for my own takes;

    * Refocus on the wolf: Oh man is it here, I'm loving how much more the focus is on the fact that the characters are hunters, not warriors. And that humanity vs feral creature is now more the focus.

    *New Harmony is not based on the Oath: This was one of my biggest gripes about Forsaken. Any ambiguity of their wars and issues is taken away by looking at the Harmony chart essentially following the oath. So the Pure are of course wrong. Another reason why it stunk was that it made the intrepentation of these laws essentially pointless from tribe to tribe. Now, it is more about actual balance, and punishes going too far on either side.

    *Wolf-Blooded are not kinfolk: Another correction I just love. As a mainly online gamer, I have seen wolf-blooded become essentially kinfolk 2.0 in games. As much as I like kinfolk in certain situations, I much rather not have them in Forsaken. Now, Wolf-Blooded are truly -wolf- blooded, with their changes being random, their nature is no longer so clear. The fact that they can shapeshift, they can be a walking locus,etc. make being one much more a curse/blessing thing. And as noted in other threads, now players who want traditional werewolves..get traditional werewolves.

    *Tribes focus on prey: My most common voiced complaint about Forsaken is how new uratha join tribes, if the game is bottom up. And why? In a simple move, it is explained, tribes have favoured prey and so an uratha joins tribe to focus on that. That takes away so much of the confusion why, because in a society of hunters,specialization is extremely good. Also, Iron Masters being human hunters is awesome, giving their city-ness a new sinister feel.

    *Packs can be whatever the Uratha wish it to be: Bottom up society, right? Well, often in the later books (plus in play) I felt the Uratha society was still there. Now that packs seem to be more what each pack wants it to be, say some members are animals, some WBs, some humans, a pack is less an uratha only club and more a gaggle of friends. NWoD shines in its freedom to have whatever it wishes, without pre-established canon to weight crossover interactions down, this also means crossover just got a lot more fun ^^

    Edit: Wolf-born=Wolf-blooded XP
