        Re: Urban Spirits

        Cool Suggestions - here are the first few that come to mind.
        Counterfeit Shades
        The spirits referred to as “Counterfeit Shades” are spirits of deception and criminal fraud. Once they were associated primarily with counterfeited currency, but have adapted to gain Essence from acts of embezzlement, identity theft, and credit card fraud. The spirits appear as humanoid figures, wearing clothing that constantly shifts from one style of dress to another, and features that are an unrecognizable blur.

        Rank 2[Spirit]
        Power 3, Finesse 5, Resistance 4, Willpower 9, Essence 15
        Corpus 9, Size 5, Initiative 9, Defense 3, Speed 13
        Influence: Deception 2, Money 1
        Ban: Loses all Willpower if deceived or tricked by non-ephemeral being; Bane: Salt
        Manifestations: Discorporate, Materialize, Twilight Form
        Numina: Hallucination, Mortal Mask, Sign
        The Hard Place
        “The Hard Place” is the Spirit of a Maximum Security Prison. In its long years, it has witnessed almost innumerable acts of cruelty, despair, desperation and violence. It now seeks to punish and torment the incarcerated (and has little problem doing the same to the guards and wardens). When manifested, “The Hard Place” appears as a massive face comprised of concrete with teeth of iron pushing forth from the nearest wall. The features of the face are coarse and its expression is angry or cruelly amused.

        Rank 4[Spirit]
        Power 12, Finesse 8, Resistance 12, Willpower 10 Essence 25
        Corpus 20, Size 8, Initiative 20, Defense 12, Speed 25
        Influence: Prison 4, Punishment 2
        Ban: Loses all Willpower if a Prisoner Escapes; Bane: Ink from a pen used to sign an official Pardon
        Manifestation: Fetter, Image, Posses, Reaching, Twilight Form
        Numina: Awe, Dement, Emotional Aura, Essence Thief, Implant Mission, Stalwart
        Lean Fortune
        “Lean Fortune” are spirits of poverty and the bad luck that often plague those of little means. It uses it influence to cause minor misfortunes and financial setbacks to ensure that the impoverished stay that way. They appear most often as thin, shadowy figures, wearing faded and threadbare cast-off clothing.

        Rank 2[Spirit]
        Power 4, Finesse 3, Resistance 5, Willpower 8, Essence 15
        Corpus 10, Size 5, Initiative 8, Defense 3, Speed 12
        Influence: Poverty 2, Misfortune 1
        Ban: Cannot use influence or numina on those wearing the trappings of luxury; Bane: Gold
        Manifestations: Fetter, Twilight Form, Unfetter
        Numina: Aggressive Meme, Entropic Decay, Left-Handed Spanner
        Poison Black Fog
        The entities known as “Poison Black Fog” are Spirits of Smog and are common in cities and environments with a high degree of air pollution. They manifest as wispy clouds of dark, polluted vapor and are frequently found in the company of factory or automotive spirits.

        Rank 2[Spirit]
        Power 3, Finesse 6, Resistance 3, Willpower 9, Essence 15
        Corpus 5, Size 2, Initiative 9, Defense 3, Speed 14
        Influence: Smog 2
        Ban: Becomes weakened in places without readily detectable air pollution; Bane: green wood
        Manifestations: Discorporate, Reaching, Twilight Form
        Numina: Drain, Innocuous, Seek

                Re: More Urban Spirits

                Working my way down the list.
                All Shop
                An “All Shop” is a spirit associated with malls, supermarkets, and other shopping centers. As the local shopping center is often a focal point of a neighborhood, an All Shop can grow powerful as they feed on the lesser spirits that congregate near their physical reflections. The often have personalities that reflect their clientele, All Shops from affluent neighborhoods can be elitist and discriminating, while those from poorer places can seem desperate and opportunistic. The spirits typically appear as large, anthropomorphized versions of their physical reflection, but can materialize as non-descript people in the clothing of a store employee (often a manager or cashier).

                Rank 3[Spirit]
                Power 8, Finesse 6, Resistance 8, Willpower 10 Essence 20
                Corpus 18, Size 10, Initiative 14, Defense 6, Speed 14
                Influence: Commerce 3
                Ban: Goes into hibernation if the store operates at a loss for more than a month; Bane: Bricks taken from a market that has gone out of business and been demolished
                Manifestation: Fetter, Reaching, Materialize, Twilight Form
                Numina: Aggressive Meme, Innocuous, Mortal Mask, Sign, Telekinesis
                Break Beat
                “Break Beat” is a spirit of artistic expression, born in the earliest days of hip-hop from the then-revolutionary hip hop DJs and MC’s. As the techniques have grown and evolved and even spread to other genres, so has Break Beat’s power. Despite its increased influence, the spirit retains a rebellious, counter culture nature, and instinctively fights against “The Establishment” in its myriad forms. The spirit appears a luminescent apparition of a young African American man dressed in contemporary club fashion (but still sports the Kangol hat and heavy gold ropes of earlier hip style).

                Rank 3[Spirit]
                Power 8, Finesse 8, Resistance 8, Willpower 10 Essence 20
                Corpus 13, Size 5, Initiative 16, Defense 8, Speed 21
                Influence: Music 3, Rebellion 1
                Ban: Goes into hibernation if defeated in a DJ or MC Battle; Bane: Magnets from a speaker made prior to 1980
                Manifestation: Discorporate, Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                Numina: Blast (Bass), Emotional Aura, Implant Mission, Mortal Mask, Sign
                Engine Twelve
                The spirit called “Engine Twelve” awoke during a horrific accident, where the subway train that is its physical reflection lost control and slammed into a crowded platform, killing many and maiming others. Since then, the spirit continues to roam the dark reaches of the subways, hunting down and devouring any foolish enough to wander the deeper recesses of the labyrinthine subway network. It takes the appearance of a nightmarishly exaggerated subway train engine, with a grill like crooked teeth and a headlight with a blinding white glare.

                Rank 4[Spirit]
                Power 12, Finesse 6, Resistance 10, Willpower 10 Essence 25
                Corpus 30, Size 20, Initiative 16, Defense 10, Speed 38
                Influence: Accidents 2, Trains 3
                Ban: Can only travel on or near rail lines Bane: glass from the original physical Engine 12
                Manifestation: Gauntlet Breech, Image, Materialize, Shadow Gateway, Twilight Form
                Numina: Awe, Banshee Wail, Phantom Arsenal, Speed, Stalwart, Strike Blind

                Numina – Banshee Wail: The entity creates an earsplitting scream that deafens anyone nearby. Anyone within 20 yards of the entity must succeed on a Resolve + Stamina roll – the number of successes gained or suffer the Stunned and Deafened Tilt. The Numen costs one Essence to activate.

                Numina – Phantom Arsenal: The entity spends 1 Essence point to manifest blades, claws, fangs, hooks or other appropriate weaponry. The modification acts as a +1L weapon. By spending an additional point of essence, this weapon gains the “9-Again” quality.

                Numina – Strike Blind: The entity using this Numen may blind people over an area, whether using darkness or blinding light (chosen when gained and appropriate to the entity in question). Activating the Numen requires 1 Essence and affects an area with a radius of the entity’s Power in yards, causing the Blinded Tilt. Characters may resist with Stamina + Supernatural Tolerance. The effect is magical in origin but can be dealt with in mundane fashion, such as by activating light sources to partially negate darkness or a welder’s mask to help reduce blinding glare. The Tilt lasts until the entity dismisses it, leaves the area, or the scene ends.
                Flickering Usher
                A “Flickering Usher” is a type of spirit that is associated with movie theatres. They are mercurial; shifting through personae’s absorbed from countless of hours of film and the emotional experience of thousands of movie goers. They also know an amazing amount of movie trivia. Most theatres only have a single Flickering Usher (if any), but large or popular venues might support several. They appear as a humanoid form made from flickering light and speak in hushed tones (unless quoting a film, which they do perfectly). They can manifest in human form, typically that of either a theatre employee, or a character from a recent (or particularly popular) film.

                Rank 2[Spirit]
                Power 4, Finesse 6, Resistance 4, Willpower 10 Essence 15
                Corpus 9, Size 5, Initiative 10, Defense 4, Speed 20
                Influence: Movie Theatre 2
                Ban: Can take no action (other than watch) if a film is running. It can defend itself if attacked; Bane: Shard of glass from a television screen.
                Manifestation: Fetter, Materialize, Twilight Form
                Numina: Emotional Aura, Hallucination, Mortal Mask, Sign

                            Re: Mall Spirit

                            I would say the spirit court of a Shopping Center/Mall would be circumstantial. The spirit of a thriving shopping plaza might be powerful enough to rule over the spirits of the constituent stores and attendant commerce spirits. A slowing dying mall kept afloat by one or two popular storefronts might actually be subservient to those spirits, if it exists as a unique entity at all.

                            Re:Ghost of the City

                            After reviewing that thread, I can put together an example Ghost.
                            Councilman Tracy
                            Niles Tracy had always been civic minded. Throughout his youth and well into middle age, Niles had been involved in local politics – from door to door campaigns, to fund raising, to serving on the city council. He made it his personal duty to fight the corruption in government he saw all around him, and eventually realized that the source of much of this corruption were creature that could only be called Vampires. Luckily, he soon was approached by like-minded individuals who aided him in his fight against the influence of these creatures (which was far deeper and more insidious than he ever suspected). His life ended when he was pulled over while driving him by a police officer; the last thing he saw was the flash of the pistol firing into his face. The crime was attributed to known ex con who enthusiastically confessed to the crime. Since then, the ghost of Councilman Tracy lingers on, haunting City Hall and the surrounding neighborhood. He uses his powers to fetter himself to others he know the truth about the undead and aid them however he can.
                            Councilman Tracy appears much as he did in life; a middle–aged man with greying hair and a kind face, wearing a dark grey suit with a gold tie. Where his right eyes would be is a ragged gunshot wound.

                            Rank 2[Ghost]
                            Power 4 Finesse 6, Resistance 4, Willpower 10, Essence 15
                            Corpus 9, Size 5, Initiative 10, Defense 4, Speed 15
                            Virtue Civic Minded, Vice Meddlesome
                            Aspiration: To curb Vampiric Influence in city affairs
                            Key Skills: Persuasion, Politics
                            Key Merits: Professional Training (Politician)
                            Influence: Anchor (City Hall) 2
                            Ban: Can only use Numina and Influence on those who know Vampires exist Bane: Salt
                            Manifestation: Fetter, Twilight Form, Unfetter

                                    Re: Building Spirits

                                    I most cases, the "Spirit of the Store" and the "Spirit of The Building that Houses the Store" are one and the same. Any distinction between the two is so minor as to not have any practical difference. The spirit could still appear as something that lives within the building rather than the building itself, but its powers an influences would be the same as another spirit of the same type but of a more literal manifestation. Of course, all of this is how I conceptualize and run things in my games (YMMV, as always).

                                    Re: More Spirits
                                    Been a while since I put up some content, so lets continue with a few more Spirits of Urban Phenomena.
                                    Academy Principle
                                    The spirits called “Academy Principles” are the awakened spirits of an educational institution. They embody the ideals of the institution and experiences of students, faculty and surrounding community. Because of this, their personalities can vary wildly; one can expect the spirit of a private school for gifted students to be quite different than an inner city public school. But even in light of this variance, Academy Principles are almost always formal, erudite and tradition minded. The Academy Principles appear as tall humanoid figure, dressed in cap and gown (or other ceremonial clothing). Its features are a semi-transparent shadow with brightly glowing eyes and it always carries a heavy tome.
                                    Rank 3[Spirit]
                                    Power 8, Finesse 9, Resistance 8, Willpower 10 Essence 20
                                    Corpus 15, Size 7, Initiative 17, Defense 8, Speed 17
                                    Influence: Educational Institution 3
                                    Ban: Cannot manifest when school is not in session; Bane: ashes of a diploma burned by the graduate
                                    Manifestation: Fetter, Image, Reaching, Twilight Form
                                    Numina: Hallucination, Implant Mission, Innocuous, Sign, Telekinesis
                                    Company Men
                                    The “Company Men” are spirits associated with large businesses or corporations. These entities serve as enforcers of the company’s philosophies and practices – using their influence to promote the culture while quashing dissent. While a given “Company Man” is of relatively limited power, individual spirits (especially those of powerful and well-known companies” can become incredibly potent. Despite their names, “Company Men” appear as blandly attractive male or female humans, dressed in a “business casual” style. This is a façade, as any tear to their flesh or removal of clothing will reveal a completely hollow interior.
                                    Rank 2[Spirit]
                                    Power 4, Finesse 6, Resistance 4, Willpower 10 Essence 15
                                    Corpus 9, Size 5, Initiative 10, Defense 4, Speed 15
                                    Influence: Loyalty 2
                                    Ban: Cannot act outside of Corporate Policy; Bane: A written notice of termination
                                    Manifestation: Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                                    Numina: Aggressive Meme, Innocuous, Mortal Mask, Rapture
                                    Travel Guides
                                    “Travel Guides” are strange spirits that use their influence to convince others to travel. They are spawned from wanderlust and attracted to those who desire to travel but never get the opportunity. They are largely harmless, but ones that grow powerful can compel their targets to act upon their desires –regardless of consequence. The spirits appear as small, somewhat animalistic beings, with the long limbs of a natural runner and large inquisitive eyes.
                                    Rank 1[Spirit]
                                    Power 3, Finesse 3, Resistance 2, Willpower 5 Essence 10
                                    Corpus 5, Size 3, Initiative 5, Defense 3, Speed 14
                                    Influence: Wanderlust 1
                                    Ban: Cannot stay fettered to a single person or place for more than three sunrises; Bane: Marble
                                    Manifestation: Fetter, Unfetter, Twilight Form
                                    Numina: Pathfinder, Speed

                                                        RE: 1403

                                                        At least as I would run it, an Ephemeral Anchored/Fettered to a room would be able to use its Numina on those inside of the room as per usual Manifestation rules (the room would have the Open Condition)
