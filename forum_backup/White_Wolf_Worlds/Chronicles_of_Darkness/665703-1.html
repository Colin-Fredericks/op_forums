    Friggin' sweet! May I ask what your username is on Deviantart and Tumblr?

    As for suggestions, I second the idea of more varied looks for Gauru and the other forms. Some might look Strix-like, all pitch black. billowing smoke, silent feet and glowing eyes. Others have such boiling blood that even their flesh and fur take on a reddish tint, and bony protrusions come out of their spine, elbows and such. A particularly tech-loving Iron Master might even ask spirits of technology to inhabit fetishes bound into his flesh that come out in certain forms, giving him a cyborg look.

    I'd love it if you drew a Ghost Wolf in Gauru form whose Harmony is so deep into the Spirit side that he looks like an abstraction of a werewolf, more of a humanoid, muscular, crimson whirlwind of predatory features. If you would like a reference, look for fan art of the shoggoths from the Cthulhu Mythos, but still roughly human-shaped and wolf-like. He might even be mistaken for an Idigam that hasn't coalesced yet at first sight. Some kind of boogeyman the Tribes tell stories about to warn new Uratha about the dangers of losing oneself to the spirit side. It would be even cooler to have this boogeyman showing up inside the smoke flowing upwards from a bonfire with werewolves gathered around it. I don't know if that would be too hard to draw, so I apologize if it is a tall order.

    Furthermore, how do you envision Spirits and the Shadow Realm as a whole, in visual terms? I like to use the Noise from "The World Ends With You" and the painterly aesthetic from "Okami" as inspiration. I usually imagine the Shadow in tones of sepia and red, with more nuanced palettes showing up in locations and denizens of distinction. As an extension of that, the unclean hybridization of the magath is reflected in their garish and psychedelic combinations of colors. If a magath starts looking like something from an acid trip seen through a rainbow filter, it is probably very bizarre in Influences and behavior.

    Do you have any headcanons for what Father Wolf and Mother Luna might have looked like?

    Bye.
