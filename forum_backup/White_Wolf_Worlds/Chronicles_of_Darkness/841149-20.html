    Here it is:

    Venator

    Shut up! Don't you hear that howl? That's a briarwolf, and they don't travel alone.

    The Hedge is a place of great peril as well as wonder, and the odd creatures called hobgoblins are among the greatest dangers a changeling might face, outside of the Gentry and the Huntsmen themselves. Whether he faced them in arenas surrounded by scores of gaping Fae spectators or patrolled the Hedge borders to rid his Keeper's lands of dangerous pests, it was the venator's task to tame or slay these bizarre beasts. An escaped venator often becomes a valued Hedge guide or guardian for the freehold that earns his loyalty.

    Elemental: A bronze helmet encrusted with verdigris sits atop her body made of thick studded leather.
    Grimm: The pale scars covering the sun-tanned skin of his muscular body recounts his past victories and the legends of the creatures that he's slain.
    Ogre: The skull of a great beast sits upon his head, his own tusks barely visible beneath it.
    Wizened: His oddly formed body has been enhanced with the bones of his previous conquests, with furs stitched over his skin.

    Blessing: Venatores are skilled at the hunting, slaying, and taming of the wild hobgoblins of the Hedge. Every venator gains the Interdisciplinary Specialty (Bestial Hobgoblins) Merit at no cost. By taking a turn to study a hobgoblin in combat, the venator may spend a point of Glamour to add bonus dice equal to his Wyrd to his next attack against the beast. Additionally, if the venator successfully renders a hobgoblin completely helpless without killing it, he may spend a point of Glamour to make the hobgoblin into a temporary Fae Mount or Hedgebeast Companion as per the Merits for a number of days equal to his Wyrd. This can be extended by spending an additional point of Glamour on this blessing for each additional day that he wishes to maintain the bond. If the hobgoblin would fit within the natural limits of one of the two Merits, the venator may spend the necessary Experiences to make the Merit permanent. A venator may only have a number of hobgoblins bonded to him through this blessing equal to the lower of his Presence or Wyrd, although permanent instances of the Merits do not count against this total. No aspect of this blessing functions against the more humanoid hobs of the Hedge.

    Jason Ross Inczauskis, Freelance Writer

            Grendel

            "This is the last time I'm going to ask you to keep it down. You don't want to see what happens when I stop using the broomstick to knock."


            Some Keepers like to enjoy peace and quiet in their realms; absolutely, unbroken silence to the point where even footsteps are muted. To ensure the continuation of this silence, they created the Grendel, tortured Changelings who's sense of hearing was so powerful that they could hear a conversation from miles away. If the Grendel detected any noise, it would descend upon the offenders in a murderous rage. Unable to bare the cacophony of rebellion, the Grendel deafened itself just long enough to flee into the Hedge and make their escape.


            Ogre: a hideously deformed giant who appears to have ripped off its own ears in a fit of agony.

            Wizened: vaguely resembling a pair of horns, a set of brass trumpets have been permanently lodged in their ear canals.

            Beast: An animal who uses sensitive hearing to make up for it's impaired vision


            Blessing: While in Arcadia, the Grendel's sense of hearing was so powerful, it was detrimental to their health. Even in its damaged state, the Grendel can still hear far better than most other people. The Grendel is immune to all penalties on sound based Perception rolls As long as they are within earshot, they can listen to it with perfect clarity, ignoring such things like mufflers, frequency, background noise and distance traveled. Additionally, by spending a point of Glamour, they can multiply their range of hearing by a number of times equal to their Wyrd for the rest of the scene.

                        I also got around to revising the honeysoul, finally. Let me know what you think.

                        Honeysoul

                        Look, I'm no happier about this than you are, but you're wounded, and if we're going to have any chance of getting out of here alive, you're going to need to get over it and start eating.

                        Some Keepers fancy themselves to be gourmets, partaking of exotic fare and hosting great banquets featuring esoteric recipes. It was the sad lot of the honeysouls to provide the substance for these bizarre repasts. Each night, the honeysoul was devoured by her Keeper or his favored guests and servants, and each morning she was born anew from her bones. Even when a honeysoul has escaped this vicious cycle, it is not uncommon to see her weep in sympathy over her own meals.

                        Beast: She is a timid rabbit girl, with a bite taken out of one long chocolate ear.
                        Darkling: Green and red gummy flesh mingle together, covering a visible skeleton of hard black bones.
                        Elemental: His features are lovingly detailed with icing upon his gingerbread flesh.
                        Fairest: Her skin is the color of honey, and it is slightly sticky to the touch and sweet to the taste.
                        Ogre: He is a great boar-like creature with slightly burned yet succulent flesh, a tantalizing aroma filling the air around him.

                        Blessing: Each night when the honeysoul sleeps, she heals all bashing and lethal damage she has suffered, or downgrades a single level of aggravated damage to lethal. Additionally, her flesh and blood is mystically potent and especially nourishing. A honeysoul will never suffer any negative effects due to starvation. Other beings may also benefit from her nourishing flesh by devouring it, regaining a point of Willpower, a point of Glamour, or recovering a single level of bashing or lethal damage for each point of lethal damage inflicted upon the honeysoul. This blessing can only be used a number of times per day equal to the honeysoul's Wyrd.

                                                            Logician By Deionscribe
                                                            I have already gone through all possible courses of action. Believe me when I say that this is the best one.

                                                            Some changelings were shaped to be the living beings of pure reason and thought. These Lost served as calculated advisors to their faerie master, while others were given tasks of great magnitude and order. Their heightened thought processes, however, led them to the solution to their plight, and thus made their escape possible.

                                                            • Beast: An animal well-known for its intellect, like a dolphin or an octopus.
                                                            • Darkling: A cold-hearted demon whose pupiless eyes gleam with inhuman intellect.
                                                            • Elemental: Shifting fractals of light that cluster together in the shape of a man.
                                                            • Wizened:A human computer, with circuit lines and fiber wires running throughout his body, and a crystalline processor for a brain.

                                                            Blessing: A Logician gains a dot of Intelligence, which can take him above the usual limitations imposed by his Wyrd. He also does not suffer any penalties to any rolls which use that Attribute, and he may spend a point of Glamour to halve their target number of successes if they are performed as Extended Actions.
