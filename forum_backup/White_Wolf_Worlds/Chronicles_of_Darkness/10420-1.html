            In answering these, I'm only writing official information, which can be easily learned by the PCs (for obvious reasons).

            Originally posted by figurehead View Post
            As time goes on and the PCs become more a part of the world, I think the game has gotten more and more interesting. I know you have recently had a few PCs become NPCs and a few new PCs join the game. Has this been difficult for you as ST?
            Not as hard as it could have been, since the PCs who dropped were easy to split away from the party IC as well (due to them joining a covenant the rest of the party was not interested in). So moving the characters out was quite easy Moving the new characters in.. wasn't too hard either, since we built their backstories to accommodate it as much as possible( setting up Amir as a friend and the two new characters as old time colleagues).

            Originally posted by figurehead View Post
            Presumably, the older PCs already have some connections in the city and if I recall correctly even have some of the signatures of Primogen which are required to graduate from the kiddie pool in Hartford.
            No, they don't have any signatures, yet. However, as the scale and importance of events is going up, they should be able to start obtaining them quite soon

            Originally posted by figurehead View Post
            Also, it seems the carthians of the kiddie pool have decided to split up the territory for themselves. Can you give us an idea of the current population of the kiddie pool and how many of them are carthians? The reason I am wanting to know is I wonder if they can succeed in setting it up this way.
            Sure. There are currently X coteries in the kiddie pool (all loners have disappeared. Harry - burned in trial, Aiste - turned citizen, Oliver - joined with Tauras, Katerina).
            They are:
            Daniel's coterie. Consists of Daniel, a Carthian ventrue, sibling of Tauras. Kendalas Kyfas, a Ordo Dracul Gangrel and Emersonas Bohas, unaligned Gangrel.
            Erika's coterie. Consists of Erika, Carthian ventrue, Antonijus May, Carthian Mekhet, Zacharajus, unaligned Nosferatu and Donaldas Bankorfas, unaligned Daeva.
            Tina's coterie. Consists of Tina Wats, Ordo Dracul mekhet, Albertas Rydas, Lancea Sanctum nosferatu
            Tauras' coterie. Constis of Tauras, Acolyte Ventrue. Katerina, Acolyte Gangrel. Oliver, unaligned nosferatu.
            Teodoras' coterie. Consists of Teodoras, Acolyte ventrue and Barbara, acolyte Daeva.
            PCs coterie. Consists of all the PCs. To remind you. Markas - member of Ordo Dracul and Carthians nosferatu. Albert - wannabe Invictus, planning to join them, and already creating ties to go in that direction. He's a Ventrue . Rhea - Lancea Sanctum Mekhet. Amir - Unaligned Nosferatu.

            Carthians expect to come out at the top, because 3 out of 6 coteries contain one. One (tina's) is considered quite weak. The only real threat to this arrangement is the Circle of the Crone aligned Coteries. But they are likely to cause trouble only if somebody tries to move against their feeding grounds (The Point and The Spa).

            Does this answer the question?

            Originally posted by figurehead View Post
            Another question: I have noticed that frenzying during feeding is a semi-regular occurrence. Is this because their sires have not taught them well? Is the vitae economy so strict that finding oneself low on blood is common? Do your PCs just have low resolve/composure? etc?
            At the start of the chronicle, I used to overdo the "roll for frenzy when feeding" thing. Nowadays I only ask for it if the vampires have 4 or less vitae. (Albert hunted with 4 or 2.. can't remember right now). So I'd guess it's because I was over strict with the frenzy rules should be better now.
