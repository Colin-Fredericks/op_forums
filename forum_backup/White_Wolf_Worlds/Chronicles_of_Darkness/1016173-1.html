                    Originally posted by Suzuki View Post
                    What are some unusual setting for games you've played, or settings you'd like to play in?

                    For me, I'd like to do a game set in Antarctica, where the players are researchers who stumble across some horror. Think John Carpenter's The Thing.

                    I'd also like to do a Werewolf game set in Las Vegas. The city is a testament to humanity's alienation from nature, and there could be lots of cool spirits as well (gambling spirits, lust spirits, etc.) It's also in the same desert as one of the least hospitable places in the country.
                    As a born and raised Vegas boy, I'd agree that we'd have one weird Shadow. I'd imagine the Strip as this island. A mighty fortress of neon and steel, hoarding all the ambient essence directed at Vegas from afar, ruled over by Jackpot and Lady Luck. Around the strip is a brief warzone of slums, homeless tent villages, shoals, fear, death, and sadness. Then, just past that, its a normal town, if a little sad and desperate, with a few more exciting and liberal spirits.

                    Of course, there is the rumor that some massive, sleeping evil deep underneath the Vegas valley. Who knows what that could be. And of course, the water spirits in the whole area are manmade and domesticated by the casino and resort gods. Still, Redrock canyon would be a great place for some iron masters (who may look coyoteish) to set up and make progress through the suburbs towards the Ivory Claw supported Strip.
