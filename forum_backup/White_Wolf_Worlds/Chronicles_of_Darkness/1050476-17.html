                    Originally posted by Lanic View Post
                    I'm quite sure this has been already discussed somewhere.

                    How does Mask interact with recording devices? Essentially, there apper to be two reasonable rulings:
                    (i) Since Mask is an illusion, a photo of a changeling records only Mien, but the photo itself is only subject to a Mask. In particular, other changelings can just look at a picture of a large group and immediately tell whether some changelings or other Fae creatures are present there.
                    (ii) Mask is not only an illusion, it actually interacts with recording devices so as to produce the image of the Mask. No one can ever tell, whether you are a Changeling or not just by looking at some pictures.

                    In a similar vein, if a changeling has antlers, does he fit through a door? Does he shuffle in sideways? We've house ruled that the antlers are only "there" when the Wyrd finds it thematically relevant.

                        Originally posted by RBAWintrow View Post


                        In a similar vein, if a changeling has antlers, does he fit through a door? Does he shuffle in sideways? We've house ruled that the antlers are only "there" when the Wyrd finds it thematically relevant.

                        To maintain the theme of "you can't go back" and lasting marks, I would actually suggest that yeah, if you've got antlers, you might have to shuffle through doors sideways. The Mask might alter how ordinary humans perceive this: they might see you as taller and having to duck down, or a little clumsy and trip-prone, or something. Small everyday inconveniences are pretty appropriate for certain miens.

                        Bigger inconveniences than that ought to be opt-in from player-side or handwaved. The character's consent isn't necessarily, but the player's is, if you're going to have stuff like a headless horseman who actually has to carry around his head (likely a Persistent Condition that can award beats), and a lightning elemental should be able to get into a pool without shocking everyone who's swimming. But if it comes down to something flavorful that doesn't have be narratively labored over all the time, something where you can just say "yeah he doesn't like doors anymore because it's awkward getting in," I think that's a comfortable space to express the reality of the mien.

                                                Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post
                                                Do tokens lose their Mask once activated in either 1e or 2e?
                                                In 1e, "a token reveals (only to changelings)" its mien (page 201); in 2e, activated tokens "[reveal] their true fae natures to anyone who can see through the Mask."
