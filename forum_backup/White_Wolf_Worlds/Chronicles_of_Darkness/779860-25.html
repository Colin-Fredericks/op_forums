    Originally posted by Reaper Verse View Post
    Hmm...I don't know how I feel about mortals at least being able to survive the Deluge and becoming survivors. I don't know why. I think it's mainly because the other kinds of Realms have their own sort of "native" lifeform and humans are more of guests than anything. I would say at least the humans that aren't Flensers should be significantly altered.     That's indeed the best way to put it. The Song was created by the weeping tears of dead humans, trying to reach back and stop the end from ever occurring. The Survivors however are not dead, or maybe the effort to create the Song simply sucked whatever was left of a soul out of them. That's not to say that they're under the effects of Soul Loss, but that they simply have the most hollow bits of a soul left in them. The consequence isn't totally magical, the Song isn't the only reason why they only have a shred of a soul left. Another reason is that they simply do not have a future left. Sure, if the Sirens succeed, then the Earth is not doomed in the past. But in the Future that is the Celestial Ocean, there's no salvation waiting for the survivors of the Deluge. Just because the past is fixed doesn't mean that their future will change, because they're separate.

    Of course, I'm prolly getting the timelines stuff wrong, and if so, please correct me. This idea adds on some Grimdark/Fridge Horror to Siren, but I can't help but feel like "there should be a way for the survivors to have even a marginally happy ending".
