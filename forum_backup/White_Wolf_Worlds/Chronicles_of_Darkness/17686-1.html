                        Disney has so many characters that you can probably get away with any combination that you want. Though if you limit yourself purely to the official Disney Princess line then you've only got 13 too choose from, and a lot of the earlier ones are kind of similar. So here's my Disney Dream team, using TvTropes five man band as a template.
                        • The Leader — Kevin Flynn (Tron: Legacy). A Seeker of Diamonds, Embassy to the Dreamlands. Specialisms in Acqua and Legno; Learn and Govern. He's found an equivalent of the ISOs in the Dreamlands and put together an elite team to realize the opportunity. He's also partners with the Dreamlands reflection of himself.
                        • The Lancer — Fa Mulan (Mulan). A Champion of Spades. The most all rounded member of the team, always with another trick up her sleeve. Her leadership and level head make a useful balance to Flynn's introverted absentminded visionary. She can, and does, seize control of the team when she thinks Flynn is about to run off with a bad idea.
                        • The Smart Guy — Milo Thatch (Atlantis: The Lost Empire). A Mender of Diamonds, Embassy to Machines. Though a trained linguist, Milo is an absolute natural with machines, able to quickly understand and repair devices from civilizations he's never seen before. Flynn wants to keep technological options, as well as Embassy priviliages, open. Plus the two geeks enjoy talking shop.
                        • The Big Guy — Merida (Brave). A Champion of Swords. Specializes in Fuoco but is beginning to learn Terra. After Blossoming Merida spent her time running wild and free, enjoying her magic but recently she has had a breakthrough in the relationship with her mother (a Grace of Hearts) and decided she needs to compromise between her wishes and her obligations. But her mother compromised too, and has allowed Merida a few more years of adventuring before she settles down.
                        • The Chick — Wendy (Peter Pan). A Mender of Hearts. The youngest of the group, and something of a liability. They keep her because she has an enormous potential for working with the Dreamlands, and because Flynn sees her as an apprentice. What the others don't know, and she doesn't realize the significance of, is that she was taken by a True Fae. As a guest, not a prisoner, and her affinity for dreams is just one of the lasting effects of her trip to Arcadia.


                        And yes, Mickey Mouse could be found in the Dreamlands and could be taken as a Mentor. A Shikigami is debatible. There's a upper limit on how powerful dreamlanders can be before they can become Shikigami (that's mostly there to justify why the Queens haven't done so themselves)
