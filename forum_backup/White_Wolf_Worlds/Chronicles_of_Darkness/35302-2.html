                        Originally posted by MissMaddy View Post
                        I dislike that aspect of Demon more than any other. That should be one proposed explanation or mechanism, but not the only one.

                        The implication of 'Cosmic Karma' and the Universal Equivalence of the God Machine are, in my mind, clashing themes.

                        I prefer to suggest that it is indeed possible that those sort of equivelent exchanges happen (and even that they have occasionally been observed, giving credance to claims that this is 'how it works') but that they cannot or do not account for everything the God Machine does.

                        In the end, the amount of energy that is required for the God Machine to give somebody Resources is literally insiginificant to it, just as we... human beings... are more or less insignificant to its Cthonic nature. Perhaps sometimes it does take from A to give to B, but just as often it takes from C, or D, or E, or even more difficult to understand solutions. Sometimes it pilfers from other realities, other timelines, that are not even in the 'frame of reference' that typical nWoD entities operate.

                        I find that kind of 'mystery Cthulhu' theme much more engaging than the 'In All Thing, Equivalence' stuff that's been kinda done in lots other media before.
                        I can tell you this: the idea that everything must balance and that granted Benefits come from lost Benefits somewhere else was only one idea. It's not necessarily the only explanation.

                        Myself, I like the implications that pacts represent at all. Angels can't make them. All demons can. Maybe it's moving parts and rotating cosmic gears into place in a sort of microcosmic representation of the God-Machine's ability itself. Maybe the reason the Machine specifically demands that angels never betray their mission is because once, an impossibly long time ago, It was an angel who betrayed Its mission....

                        Anyway, feel free to go with whatever theories you like for your games, and indeed have clashing theories. As far as I'm concerned, there isn't one correct theory—perhaps multiple contradictory theories are correct, perhaps none of them scratch the surface. Even the Unchained cannot fully understand the vastness of the God-Machine or Its incomprehensible plans.

                                    Pacts can alter things retroactively in regards to the Demon specifically (the girlfriend example above, for example), but it doesn't alter things in regards to unrelated "Fourth" parties. PlayerA knows he traded his girlfriend away, DemonB now has said girlfriend, and GirlfriendC now thinks DemonB is her boyfriend instead of PlayerA. However, FatherD will be surprised to note PlayerA is no longer GirlfriendC's boyfriend (though will likely justify it instead of rampaging).

                                    By that example, if a mortal Male became a Female, her friends would be under no compulsion to believe (s/he) was always a Female, though they may justify it as a mundane sex change operation.

                                    The real question that should be asked is whether a Demon would actually benefit from making that arrangement, because it's very messy socially. And anything that messy would risk cover exposure or compromise.

                                    Furthermore, while the book does mention that everything comes from somewhere, it's not really a hard and fast "You can't do this in the game!!!!!!" rule, because the world has potentially untapped resources of all sorts. It seems to me this was added more to explain the Top-level Fundaments of "How it Works", and as potential localized karmic story-arcs. If you Pact someone for Resources 5, it doesn't -necessarily- mean your next door neighbors suddenly lose 5 dots split between them, it just means that the world appropriates those funds for you instead of redistributing them elsewhere. People die all over the world in droves. Like in Office Space, you take a few floating integers from all those dying folks, and you suddenly have Resources 5.
