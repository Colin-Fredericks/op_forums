    If we got a book introducing New Courts what would you want?

    11-17-2013, 04:31 PM
    Say we got a book introducing New Courts to the game, what would you want? Personally I'm hungry for any new changeling material, and I find Court systems to be well quite fun, and not only that but as far as optional alternative systems go they are among the best and most usable in the NWOD!

    So what Court Systems would you like to see?

    I would like it if they put in the missing courts that go with Dawn and Dusk, or possibly an explanation for connecting them to the Sun and Moon courts (perhaps sun and moon fit the roles of Day and Night when combined with the Dusk and Dawn Courts.).


    The Books also mention a Buddhist Cycle Court, and an African Predator Prey Foodchain Court. If someone came up with a good pitch for these I wouldn't mind seeing them, but I also wouldn't mind ignoring them as they haven't been introduced as incomplete like the other courts.


    What Court Emotions/Contracts do think would be fun to have? Once Again I wish they finished the ones for the Sun and Moon Court because we already have these courts in the books but they are damned incomplete!

      I think an Ashrama Court based on the Hindu Four Stages of Life would be interesting. Such as

      Brahmacharya
      (student life)

      Grihastha
      (household life)

      Vanaprastha
      (retired life)

      Sannyasa
      (renounced life)


      This would be interesting in how they handled court rule. Perhaps each stage would have one who rules all members of the Freehold of their court, so like the Directional Courts they are not divided by seasons but by membership or age/maturity? Or they could run on the Seasonal Cycle with Brahmacharya starting in Spring, and Sannyasa in Winter. Perhaps the period of the Sannyasa rule is a hands off time where folks are not constrained by Courtly politics? I can't help but feel a number of cool contracts could be spawned from this idea. Particularly Contracts for the Sannyasa.


      This could be say mirrored with a western Norn/Moirae Court of the Maiden Mother Crone Courts. Or perhaps the men of a Freehold take up the Ashrama Court and the Women take up the Norn/Moirae Courts.

          Glamourweaver has a concept for the Arabian Court of Days that I'd like to see.

          I'd very much like a bit of polish on the Moon Court; change it's emotion to Joy and a bit more emphasis on "not really evil" to balance the Sun's "not really good" and both courts knowingly relying on each other to stop them going too far.


          I'd also quite like a Court system specifically for games that focus mostly interaction with the human world. Let's call it the Courts of Flowers. Flowers are cool and pretty.

          One court that's all about family, they want their old family or to start a new one. They are stereotyped as clingy. Emotion: Dependency.

          One court that's all about being accepted for who they are. That means refusing to fix their Changeling related mental issues. Emotion: Stubbornness.

          One court that's about being useful to people while avoiding notice, based on myths of household brownies and similar. Emotion: Contentment.

          One court that's about gaining fame and/or fortune in the human world. Emotion: Ambition.

          1 and 2 are polar opposites: Change everything about yourself to please a boy/girlfriend vs change nothing. 3 and 4 are also opposites: Humility vs loudness.


          No flower court has a king, instead the Wyrd crowns one mortal to rule over the freehold. This mortal is chosen as the person who is most able to:
          1) Know what the other mortals in the Freehold's area would want the Freehold to do; if they knew it existed.
          2) Know how to get the highly dysfunctional (seriously, these guys look worse than the Directional Courts) Freehold to do that.

          The Crown would grant the ability to see faeries, the ability to travel the hedge, and a really good defence - say reduce all successes by 5 - against magic.


          It just needs four symbolically appropriate flowers

                                                  Thanks, I see I'm not the only one who's been thinking up flowers I actually brainstormed this a while back here's the list I came up with.

                                                  1- I actually went for honeysuckle as it's a vine which grows on other plants and can choke them. It symbolises The Bond of Love

                                                  2 - Euphorbia for resolve, the closest I could get to stubbornness. I'm not too keen on this one though, the name just doesn't fit with the others.

                                                  3 - I went for Violets too loyalty, faithfulness and if you look at violets in their shrubs they look kinda humble.

                                                  4 - Hollyhock. It symbolises pride and ambition which I think fits a little better, it's fame or fortune.

                                                  5 - The Crown is white lilies: Virginity, Purity, Majesty, It's Heavenly to be with You, Youth. Perfect symbolism.


                                                  That also gives each court it's own colour which I think is quite fitting: Pink/yellow/bluet/red (hollyhock comes in many colours)/white. I'd hoped to go for a set of spring blooming flowers to riff of their Spring Court but that I gave up.
