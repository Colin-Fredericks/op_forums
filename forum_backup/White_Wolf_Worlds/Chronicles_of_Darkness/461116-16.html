            I've actually run High School nWoD before, but some complications with my lack of knowledge of the latest developments prevent me from throwing my hat into the ring.

            Some storytelling advice for the future St: keep the focus local. Downscale the threats, keep it focused on what the characters care about. IMO the show Teen Wolf did a solid enough job during the first two seasons. It forged connections between the characters and kept the theme focused via the plot.

            I would recommend that the players work out their character connections in advance. You're going to need that whole "how did her character help yours? Why are you mad at his character?" sort of setup.
