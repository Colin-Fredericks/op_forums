            Ok, i got an example

            Axioms
            Axioms are composed of multiple base [powers] that are available at each dot, some of which improve as the Inspired's mastery of the Axiom and Inspiration grows. These powers cannot be integrated into Wonders on their own, but must be molded and focused by Inferences. Most Inferences grant bonuses to the base power while constricting it's targets, some Inferences simply grant new powers wholesale, though these are inevitably narrow in focus.

            Example Axioms
            Exelixi

            Autonomous Repair (•): Wonders made using this [Power] can heal itself as if they were human.
            Mending (•, and ••••): This [Power] allows for the genius the mend both organic and inorganic matter. He rolls Dexterity + Medicine or Crafts. Each success repairs one point of Structure or Lethal damage or two points of Bashing damage.

            Katastrofi

            Basic Damage (• to •••••): The base effect of Katastrofi Wonders is dealing damage. At Katastrofi (•) the Wonder can only deal Bashing damage. Katastrofi (••) can deal Lethal damage and grants the 9-again quality to Bashing damage. Katastrofi (•••) Wonders can deal Aggravated damage, grants the 9-again quality to Lethal damage, and the 8-again quality to Bashing damage. Katastrofi (••••) grants the 9-again quality to Aggravated damage and 8-again to both Lethal and Bashing damage. Katastrofi (•••••) grants 8-again on all damage.
            Trait Reduction (• to •••): This [Power] allows the Wonder to remove dots in a Trait specified by the chosen Inference. This Trait is reduced by one dot per three Health Levels of damage that would have been dealt. At Katastrofi (•) this Trait heals at the rate of Bashing damage, Lethal damage at Katastrofi (••), and Aggravated damage at Katastrofi (•••).

            Skafoi

            Dimensional Travel (••••): This [Power] allows the Wonder to access other dimensions.


            Example Fields
            Necronics [Ghosts]

            This Field is the study and manipulation of spectral beings and the Underworld.
            Required Skill: Occult
            Inferences:
            Plasmic Regenerator (Exelixi •): This Inference uses the Mending [Power] of Exelixi, allowing it to heal the Corpus of ghosts, using Occult instead of Medicine in the roll. If it can only heal ghosts, add the higher of geniuses Exelixi or Inspiration to the Dexterity + Occult roll.
            Ectoplasmic-Disintegrator (Katastrofi •): This Inference uses the Basic Damage [Power] of Katastrofi, allowing it to harm ghosts. If it can only harm ghosts, they treat all attacks as coming from a being with an honorary Rank of the higher of the geniuses Katastrofi or Inspiration.
            Plasmic Reductor (Katastrofi •): This Inference uses the Trait Reduction [Power] of Katastrofi, allowing it to reduce a ghost's Power, Finesse, or Resistance traits. If it can only reduce a ghosts Traits, it can reduce Rank as well, which is reduced by one dot per five Health Levels of damage that would have been dealt. Reducing Rank to 0 would permanently destroy the ghosts as nothing of it remains.
            Keymaster (Skafoi ••••): This Inference uses the Dimensional [Power] of Skafoi, allowing it to open Avernian Gates. If it cannot create new gates, it gains a +5 bonus to open them.


            Edit: So basically, you can choose the regular option and effect the thing as you normally would, in addition to anything else it can do. Or, you can specialize and get bonuses to rolls or entirely new effects

                            Ok, how about this

                            Focused: Focused effects are optional effects that come with each Inference. It would specify a drawback that would narrow down its effect but would in turn grant bonuses to the specified action itself.

                            Axioms
                            Axioms are composed of multiple base [Powers] that are available at each dot, most of which improve as the Inspired mastery of the Axiom and Inspiration grows. These powers cannot be integrated into Wonders on their own, but must be molded and focused by Inferences. Most Inferences grant optional bonuses to the base power while constricting it's targets, some Inferences simply grant new powers wholesale, though these are inevitably narrow in focus.

                            Example Axioms

                            Exelixi

                            Autonomous Repair (•): Wonders made using this [Power] can heal itself as if they were human.
                            Mending (•, and ••••): This [Power] allows for the genius the mend both organic and inorganic matter. He rolls Dexterity + Medicine or Crafts. Each success repairs one point of Structure or Lethal damage or two points of Bashing damage.


                            Katastrofi

                            Basic Damage (• to •••••): The base effect of Katastrofi Wonders is dealing damage.

                            At Katastrofi (•) the Wonder can only deal Bashing damage.

                            Katastrofi (••) can deal Lethal damage and grants the 9-again quality to Bashing damage.

                            Katastrofi (•••) Wonders can deal Aggravated damage, grants the 9-again quality to Lethal damage, and the 8-again quality to Bashing damage.

                            Katastrofi (••••) grants the 9-again quality to Aggravated damage and 8-again to both Lethal and Bashing damage.

                            Katastrofi (•••••) grants 8-again on all damage.

                            Trait Reduction (• to •••): This [Power] allows the Wonder to remove dots in a Trait specified by the chosen Inference. This Trait is reduced by one dot per three Health Levels of damage that would have been dealt.

                            At Katastrofi (•) this Trait heals at the rate of Bashing damage, Lethal damage at Katastrofi (••), and Aggravated damage at Katastrofi (•••).

                            Drain (•• to •••••): This [Power] allows Wonders to drain and disperse Metanormal Energies from the target, be it inflicting an Ephemeral Entity with Essence Bleed, literally making hemophages bleed, or stranger things.

                            At Katastrofi (••) those targeted are involved in a Clash of Wills with the Genius, if the target loses, it gains the Mild Power Bleed Condition. They lose one point of Metanormal Energy (Such as Vitae, Essence, Mana, Glamour, and Pyros) every hour. Once per the Genius' Inspiration in hours, the target repeats the Clash of Wills until it finally wins. The target can also spend a point of Willpower to force a Clash once per hour.

                            Katastrofi (•••) inflicts the Moderate Power Bleed Condition, the target loses a point of Metanormal Energy every half-hour.

                            Katastrofi (••••) inflicts the Serious Power Bleed Condition, the target loses a point of Metanormal Energy every minute.

                            Katastrofi (•••••) inflicts the Fatal Power Bleed Condition, the target loses a point of Metanormanl Energy each turn (3 seconds). At this stage the target may spend Willpower to force a Clash once per turn.


                            Skafoi

                            Dimensional Travel (•• to •••••): This [Power] allows the Wonder to access other dimensions.

                            At Skafoi (••) the Wonder can only open preexisting portals for access.

                            Skafoi (•••) allows the Wonder to open new Dimensional Gates in locations where the barriers between worlds are particularly thin, such as Loci or places particularly attuned to death, for the Shadow and the Underworld respectively.

                            Skafoi (••••) allows the Wonder to open Gates to any Dimension anywhere.

                            Skafoi (•••••) allows the Wonder to simply transport the Genius to another Dimension as an instant action, bypassing the need for Gates entirely.


                            Example Fields

                            Necronics [Ghosts]

                            This Field is the study and manipulation of spectral beings and the Underworld.

                            Required Skill: Occult
                            Inferences:

                            Plasmic Regenerator (Exelixi): This Inference uses the Mending [Power] of Exelixi, allowing it to heal the Corpus of ghosts, using Occult instead of Medicine in the roll.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only heal ghosts, add the higher of geniuses Exelixi or Inspiration to the Dexterity + Occult roll.

                            Ectoplasmic-Disintegrator (Katastrofi): This Inference uses the Basic Damage [Power] of Katastrofi, allowing it to harm ghosts.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only harm ghosts, who treat all attacks as coming from a being with an honorary Rank of the higher of the geniuses Katastrofi or Inspiration.

                            Plasmic Reductor (Katastrofi): This Inference uses the Trait Reduction [Power] of Katastrofi, allowing it to reduce a ghost's Power, Finesse, or Resistance traits.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only reduce a ghosts Traits, it can reduce Rank as well, which is reduced by one dot per five Health Levels of damage that would have been dealt. Reducing a ghost Rank to 0 would permanently destroy it as nothing of it remains.

                            Ecto-Drain (Katastrofi): This Inference uses the Drain [Power] of Katastrofi, allowing it to drain a ghosts Essence.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only drain ghosts, not even other Ephemerals, the ghost loses two points of Essence every interval.

                            Keymaster (Skafoi): This Inference uses the Dimensional [Power] of Skafoi, allowing it to open travel to the Underworld.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only travel to the Underworld, it gains a bonus to the roll equal to the higher of the Genius' Inspiration or Skafoi


                            Faeries [Fae]

                            This Field is the study and manipulation of Fae beings and the Hedge
                            Required Skill: Occult
                            Inferences:

                            Goblin Healing (Exelixi): This Inference uses the Mending [Power] of Exelixi, allowing it to heal the Fae creatures, using Occult instead of Medicine in the roll when appropriate.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only heal Fae, add the higher of geniuses Exelixi or Inspiration to the Dexterity + Skill roll.

                            Wyrd Reduction (Katastrofi): This Inference uses the Trait Reduction [Power] of Katastrofi, allowing it to reduce a Fae's Attributes.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only reduce a Fae's Traits, it can reduce Wyrd as well, which is reduced by one dot per five Health Levels of damage that would have been dealt.

                            Banality (Katastrofi): This Inference uses the Drain [Power] of Katastrofi, allowing it to drain Glamour.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only drain Glamour, the target loses two points of Glamour every interval.

                            Hedge Key (Skafoi): This Inference uses the Dimensional [Power] of Skafoi, allowing it to open travel to the Hedge.
                            Focused: The Wonder can only travel to the Hedge, it gains a bonus to the roll equal to the higher of the Genius' Inspiration or Skafoi

                                                I think the Unmada field could be broken into three parts:
                                                • The "Paradigm Field": This imposes an illusion of the Genius's Paradigm on those around them. A Genius might be able to identify the handiwork of another by the traces of the Paradigm field. Things get a little weird around the Genius, in a very specific way to the Genius, but it's mostly cosmetic.
                                                • The Unmada Field: An Unmada's Paradigm Field actually bends reality around them. Their Wonders explicitly working by means that suit their Paradigm get exceptional success on rolls to use within the field. This may also extend to other paranormal effects (A Narrativist Genius might also bolster certain aspects of the Wyrd). The field also imposes a Tilt on the area to reflect its effects. The field doesn't actually change reality, and when it is removed, things snap back to normal. A Genius can also invoke Deep Inspiration to temporarily invoke this field, risking Unmada status as normal.
                                                • Asserting the Paradigm: Genii with opposing Paradigms interact weirdly, and often violently. A Genius can purposely lash out at an Inspired phenomenon (a Mane, a Wonder, a Bardo, etc.) that directly opposes their Paradigm, invoking a Clash of Wills (Presence + Inspiration vs. target's Composure + Inspiration). If the Genius successfully lashes out, the target suffers Havoc. On failure, the Genius instead gains the Beaten Down Tilt. How the Genius might lash out may vary from simple intimidation, to a projection of psychic energy, to a simple effort to disprove the opponent in a puff of logic.

                                                        Okay! I've been thinking and I do think failure is key ingredient of what it means to be mad scientist. Geniuses are heavily tied into tropes of supervillains and morally questionable heroes, which is why I feel like their Integrity should give leeway for those kind of characters. But there should be reason for players to try to sabotage Genius's own work as if they were doing it subconsciously. Why would Genius need to fail? Do they need to be proved wrong about something? What could separate most self indulgent and basdardly, villainous Genius from cold disconnected Illuminated? Answer to all thesequestions would be Faith. Geniuses need to have Faith in Humanity. Even most misanthropic Neid or transhumanist Hoffnung need to believethat at the end of the day humanity will go on. In the lowest end of the scale this Faith might even lashout similarly to hatred and anger, but Geniuses would be spewing their insults towards mankind while wishing that someone would come by to prove them wrong. Illuminated don't hate humanity, they don't care about it in the slightest beyond as part of their big plan.
                                                        Geniuses loose Faith when they see mortals (or even beholden) raise up to the challange, when they fail to preprogrammed pattern Genius has foreseen them to go. They restore Faith when they see acts of selflessness and genuine kindness (which may be rare in World of Darkness), but also when humans raise against great odds (like destroying wannabe overlord's domination scheme). Taking this in account Hunters would indeed make an amazing source of Faith, but eventually cause it to deplete.
                                                        So, what do you think?
