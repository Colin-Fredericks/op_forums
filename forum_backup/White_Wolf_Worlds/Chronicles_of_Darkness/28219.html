    My Weekly WoD Project Program!

    12-11-2013, 03:46 PM
    EDIT- so this thing is really not updated anymore, so instead I'm making it into proper Homebrew signatures!

    General World of Darkness- Conjurer [fan splat WIP] Mapping the Stars [Cosmology Thread WIP] God Did It! [Abrahamic Cosmology in WoD] Nations in the Dark [Shard, WIP] Carnival of Freaks [simple templates in the style of Mortal Remains, WIP] Sin Song Infection [a variant Infected template] Judges [biblical minor template, working with Kingsraven] Promised [you made a pact, now pay the price] Intruders [spirits from the Outside who create false bodies to walk among mankind unseen], Diaries of Darkness [plot hook in the form of a mysterius book holding hidden knowledge] One Night in Lonseome October [Setting Hack which allows you play the novel's plot in the CofD]
    River of Souls [trapped ghosts which drain the life of those around them] Jackals [scavengers who feast upon the dead in an attempt to maintain their humanity] The Human Combustion Phenomena [Enen no Shouboutai conversion, including a micro template and new antagonists] 3:15 [metaploty thing expanded from Chris Allen's concept]
    Dark Eras
    Book of Judges [rough draft with some ideas which were later developed]
    Spanish Inquisition [including a new "conspiracy"- Los Inocentes. Kingsraven also contributes]
    Age of Azar [crossover setting for the First Turn Egypt, collaborating with Deionscribe and Arcanist]
    Upper Paleolithic [rough draft and a lot of concepts for "primitive templates"]
    First Temple [pitch for how the many gamelines would operate under King Solomon's rule]
    Changing Breeds- Old Ones [needs editing, first draft] Changing Breeds 2nd Edition [first draft, WIP after Werewolf is released]
    Requiem- Veneficia [Variant, Blood Sorcery rules/ 2E rules] Grettir [Lost Clan] Sutina, the Grave Goods [vampiric artifacts who drain their owners into unlife], Akhud and Ahranites [Seven without Seven], Dragolescu [Bloodline conversion] The Keening [setting material, strange song which devours the vampire mind and Humanity] Once Upon a Midnight Darkly [the full story] This is Not For You to Understand [Eschaton expansion] The Bitten [micro template for those who are change by a vampire's Kiss, including an Inamorata template] Ale [Half Damned Expansion] Demon in the Wind [Edimmu Update + Lesser Template + Merges Sorcery] Phanariot [2e update/revision, with a new connection to the shadow realm of the strix]
    Werewolf- Viskthaigilu [the Moth Host] Apep, the Chaos Serpent [new idigam, collaborating with Deionscribe and Arcanist.] Culte de la Raison [MCI merit for the public face of the Temple of Apollo, where debauchery is the norm and money is the only goal] Of Firstborn Less Told [expansion of both existing and homebrew lesser Firstborn and presentation of the Tribes they may establish] Lords of Pangaea- Today[list of both canon and fanon pangaeans and their state in modern times]
    Mage-Ways and Archetypes [a kith like system for mages] Magical Origin [a system for alternative origins for mages without the Watchtowers] Faces of the Exarchs [the shapes the exarch may take when dealing with mortals] The Warden [heretical Iron Seal which governs upon the Lower Depths]

    Hunter-
    Sixth Castigation Rules [alternative mechanics for learning more than 5 Castigations- at great cost]
    Hunters Around the Globe! [mapping the Hunters orgs and similar groups all around the world]
    Core
    Pythian Oracles [prophets and diviners who are willing to kill in the name of a better future]
    Raziel Ltd [Israeli weapon company protecting its land with theurgic secrets]
    Silent Imperative [Salem Era group who believe that not every witch must burn]
    Apollonian Priesthood [a neo-Hellenistic cult of sun worshipers who guides every person to fulfill his potential]
    ZMEI Initiative [a Russian organization of psychics who went underground after the Cold War]
    Tartarus Enterprise [a cancer conspiracy who work with monsters to create terrible wonders of technology]
    Los Inocentes [self proclaimed inquisitors of the vigil who hunt for heretics among hunters]
    Silentium [an agency who search for corrupted hunted and potential slashers and remove their memories of the Vigil]
    Tartaruchi [oath bound demon children of the furies who punish the wicked to give hell its due]
    Domus Sanctus [cleaners of tainted places which try to literally fix the world]
    Moving Up the Tiers 1 / 2 [cause everyone can be a conspiracy!]
    Inspired [a microtemplate meant to make a divine inspired agents of Heaven]
    Civil Wars [see what happens when conspiracies fall.. and how they rise again]
    To Do List [so many ideas to develop, so little time]
    Heirs of the Lie [when the Exrachs themselves stand vigilant]
    Strains of the Cursed Blood [the many demonic bloodlines who stand against Hell]
    Vigilant Times [the history of the Vigil, expanded upon Dark Eras and other historic settings]
    From Monsters to Hunters [reimagining the big bads of the CofD as the good guys.. sort of]
    Slashing Conspiracies [Cabals based around being dark mirrors to the major conspiracies of the world]
    Night Stalkers
    Cage of Shadows [an heretic Jewish sect which hunts after the children of Lilith]
    Schrlach Spinnwebe [a modern slave traders who apply a network of blood dolls in order to enslave vampires]
    Al-Rih al-Asuad [descendants of the mesopotemian wind demons who fight against the spawn of Lamashtu]
    Tecuanimeh [servants of the Smoking Mirror who make sacrifices to hungry relics to survive under the black sun]
    Spirit Slayers
    Aki Ten [modern incarnation of the Onmyodo in Japan who wish to protect their people and reclaim their glory]
    E.V.E Institution [Australian biologists who seek to advance humanity to its next form]
    Nibiru [claims to be the oldest hunters in the world, the maintain the bindings of ancient gods and seal new ones]
    Silver Syndicate [part time investment company, part time criminal organization, full time shapeshifter hunters]
    Followers of Set [modern incarnation of ancient cult which relays on monsters to fight worse horrors]
    Church of the Blood Moon [heretic priesthood who lures the wolves of the devil with mystical songs as shepherds]
    Lycos Agema [spartan werewolf hunters who seek to proof themselves as the greatest warriors]
    Hrt Isfet [bloodline who carries the venom of an ancient demon in their veins and use it to fight chaos's spawns]
    Witch Finders
    Museum Secretorum [an hermetic order who seeks after studying and archiving every magical phenomena]
    Order of the Nameless [an occult society of mostly men who seeks to take over the Abyss]
    A.S.T.R.A.L [a data company who destroy knowledge in order to stop potential witches from awakening]
    Nemontemi [starry eyed lineage who hunts the abyssal gods they once worshiped]
    ​Torch Bearers
    Ashen Collective [alchemists of the old who seek to refine human souls in order to live forever]
    Aga Nritya [Thuggee cultists who dance with the Divine Fire of Kali]
    Custodes Ignis [holy warriors who maintain the harmony of the world]
    Sabaoth [alchemically enhanced soldiers of god who fight the ones that defy the heavens]
    Penyihir Mayat [children of mothers bitten by Leyak which maintain the balance between good and evil]
    Dream Catchers
    Orchestra Fatalibus [singers united through the French Revolution who sing the song of humanity]
    Garden of the Hesperides [healers who enter the mystical garden and harvest its weird plants for remedies]
    Chasseurs de Tetes [cabal turned hunters who carry the old story of revolution from the Terror to modernity]
    Tiansheng [chinese bloodline originated from trickery who hunt the yaogui under the Way and Mandate of Heaven]
    Mori Kage [assassins from the Sengoku era which are bound by oath to the yokai and return them the escaped yosei]
    Grave Robbers
    Ebony Fangs [ancient necromancers committed to return lost soul back to the Underworld]
    Shepherds of Annwn [blood witches who protect sacred sites and cursed places from ancient evil and the dead]
    Pomegranate Deme [orphic cult which collects restless shades to stop them from falling to the Underworld]
    Tatau o te Po [maori bloodline who fights for survival against the forces of Whiro]
    Tomb Raiders
    Temple of Silence [servants of a lost goddess who seek to bring eternal sleep to Irem]
    Maagal HaBaal VehaAshera [an ancient priesthood from the Time of Judges turned into modern artists and idol makers]
    Maw of the River [destroyers of Relics who wish to return their power back to the River]
    Eye of Heaven [advisers and kingmakers dedicated to control the sleeping gods in Heaven's name]
    Avici Sutra [souls reincarnated as lords among the asura who seek to escape to cycle of suffering by defeating demons]
    R.A.M [israeli governmental agency which protects its country from dangerous relics and the cults built around them]
    Hell Binders
    Theater of Tragedies [brothel of cross dressers who make deals with the devil and cheat their way out]
    The Nimrod Movement [former atheists who take over angels to bring down God and free mankind]
    Heirs of the Crown [heretics who banish demons to purify themselves through sin]
    Cult of Moloch [a scattered group of killers who are willing to sacrifice anything to make their wishes come true]
    MourningStar Developments [group of programmers and analysts who reshape their identities for protection]
    Usij [an iranian lineage descendant of Angra Mainyu who use the druj as a weapon and shield]
    Myth Breakers
    Navio de Tolos [dream pirates who sale the Primordial Dream in search for adventure]
    Knights of the Bloody Chalice [blood bathers who seek to become fear incarnated]
    Nightmare Network [shamanistic tradition turned into programmer who use stolen secrets to overcome fear]
    Burya Storozh [dragon people who fight against the storms of the ala to let hope to shine once more]
    Yam VeNahar Factories [chemical company which harvest lairs and other worlds for resources]
    Renegade Chasers
    Bone Collectors [survivalists who plan to become heroes by scavenging the powers of failed experiments]
    Benei Topheth [victims of ancient cult who seek to destroy those to abuse others for selfish gain]

    Promethean- Baalim [infernal Lineage, WIP] The Awful Ice [setting material, antagonistic force which devours the Fire and its Created]
    Geist- New Keys [2e version, draft version]
    Mummy- Cabiri [Lifeless product of a bastardization of the Rite of Return] Visitors [Variant template connecting those elusive immortals to the powers of Fate and alien civilizations] Vaettir [immortal sorcerers who could make sekhem dance to their command]
    Demon- Grigori [the Second Children from the Pain Prophet's Testament] Nine Pieces Mechanism [invoke the curses of sleeping gods- if you dare!], My Dear Grey Men [3 variant Stigmatic Templates based around exorcists, the Third Exorcists and the Noah]
