    Clans as Bloodlines

    02-08-2016, 03:56 PM
    Long story short, after years playing CofD, and gradually developing a setting, I've become increasingly disatisfied with the concept of Clans (and X-spalts in general). Now I've decided to retire them altogether. Or, if you want to think of it another way, I've decided upgrade all Bloodlines to Clan status.

    However, here's where I hit a hitch. I don't want to retire, or radically alter those PCs and NPCs who were without a Bloodline, nor do I want to shuffle them off into the closest fit. Hence the topic, converting the Five clans into Bloodlines. I've already determined the Disciplines spread I want (making certain to keep a balance of 3 each of Animalism, Celerity, Obfuscate, Resilience & Vigor):
    • Daeva: Celerity, Majesty, Resilience & Vigor
    • Gangrel: Animalism, Protean, Obfuscate & Resilience
    • Mehket: Animalism, Auspex, Celerity & Obfuscate
    • Nosferatu: Celerity, Nightmare, Obfuscate, Vigor
    • Ventrue: Animalism, Dominate, Resilience & Vigor
    The Problem comes in determining their Bloodline Gifts, there I am drawing something of a blank. Hence I turn to you all, mighty forumites. What Gifts (2e style) do you think would be appropriate for the five clans?

                      Daeva didn't really get much, just some Merits including the 1e Cacophany Listener and a bloodline.

                      Mekhet probably got the most with the 1e Shadow Cult Initiation Merit, several well-detailed Shadow Cults, some Merits and Devotions that went along with them, two bloodlines, and the rules for Hollow Mekhet which were cool as hell but like I said almost all downside.

                      Nosferatu got some Merits and Devotions, expanded Haven rules for building Necropoli, two Bloodlines, and mechanics for giving Nos characters Skill bonuses and penalties based on the way the character's Clan Bane manifested, which would get more severe as their Blood Potency increased.

                      Ventrue got some Merits and Devotions, and the rules for Malkavia as a disease, which may or may not also be a Bloodline. My homebrew Malkav's Disease write up was an attempt to reproduce these mechanics as closely as possible in 2e.

                              Oooh, here's an idea, building off the Edward Cullen approach: Daeva cause their touchstones to love them, no blood required. If a Daeva is obsessed with you, suddenly you find yourself obsessed with him.

                              Perhaps I should go read through David Hill's psychic vampires again for inspiration...

                              Edit: Okay, here's the outline for Daeva:
                              • Daeva gain a special touchstone whenever they feed on someone more than once (as per their curse). Once this touchstone is gained they cannot gain another in the same way until the first is dead. Unfortunately this will probably come sooner rather than later.
                              • The Daeva's touchstone also falls in love with the Daeva, gaining the swooning condition in his presence and Deprived when he is gone. The Deprived condition can actually kill the Touchstone, so it's not as simple as staying away from them to alleviate the curse.
                              • Finally, the Daeva steal the breath of their lover while in their presence. Effectively feeding a point of Vitae (e.g. one lethal damage) every hour they are together.
                              Thoughts?

                                              They did get a nifty merit that let them feed off encouraging someone else to indulge their vice. Its not as iconic or game changing as others, but I do like it as a signature "daeva" thing
                                              That sounds intriguing. And the best part about this approach is if I can't decide on which Daeva approach to take...I can take both and call them different bloodlines!
                                              Now then, Nosferatu and Mekhet approaches. This is what I'm currently thinking:

                                              Mekhet: Faded
                                              Mekhet don't register strongly in the world, they don't cast shadows or reflections, they don't leave much evidence of their passing and they are difficult for humans to recall.
                                              • Mekhet don't show up on camera or recording (meaning they can't use a phone), they don't cast shadows or reflections, they don't even make floorboards creak when they walk on them.
                                              • Mekhet don't leave minor evidence. Clues of their passing just up and disappear. No finger prints, no stray hairs. Doors and windows shut and lock behind them. Only things like a dropped wallet with a photo ID are big enough to stick around.
                                              • Mekhet are hard to remember, people who run across them have a difficult time recalling their name or description, and victims they feed on forget all about the incident...at least until they meet the vampire again. Supernaturals, Ghouls and victims Blood Bound by the Mekhet are exempt.
                                              Nosferatu: The Curse
                                              When a Nosferatu bites you they pass on a bit of their accursed life. The world turns against their victims, becoming a living nightmare. This curse can be lifted only through time, or by allowing the Nosferatu to bind you with his blood.
                                              • Victims of a Nosferatu become loci of bad luck. People around them fail dramatically more often, and turns of good fortune are few and far between.
                                              • Victims of a Nosferatu are hated by the world. Animals attack them unprompted, the weather turns poor and sickness seems to gather around them.
                                              • Victims of a Nosferatu receive omens when a Nosferatu is near, intensifying the previous two afflictions.
