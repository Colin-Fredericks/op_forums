    Which Hunter group would you join IRL?

    07-02-2021, 10:59 PM
    Here's an entertaining thought experiment. If you were to join up with a Compact and/or Conspiracy in real life which one would it be and why?

    I'll start. Philosophically, and because on the civilian side I'm a lab rat, I'd see myself joining Null Mysteries. I just identify with their world view the most; I'm of the mind that if something's real and observable it can be studied, if it can be studied then its mysteries can be discovered through observation and experimentation. If I can observe something and interact with it, then I can form educated guesses as to how it works, then set up a well planned experiment to test my idea. If my idea turns out to be wrong I can always go back to the drawing board and form a new idea, taking the data from the failed experiment into account. Just have to be careful to survive what can study me back...

    On the Reserve side though, I'm an Entomologist for the US Army (yes the army has bug experts for soldiers, you can thank Walter Reed for that). It's not too much of a leap to think that I'd be recruited to Task Force: Valkyrie because Entomologists are already studying and interacting with strange creatures. Insects; their skin IS their skeleton, their heart is along the back and middle of their butt, their central nervous system runs down the center of their chest, they breath through tiny holes in their exoskeleton, their mouth parts move sideways rather than up and down because those mouth parts used to be the forelimbs of their primordial ancestors, and the legs and eyes...insects are alien and strange compared to us. I could see Task Force: Valkyrie calling me in to help study and deal with supernatural insects or insect-like creatures.

    DISCLAIMER: I know that my world view can clash with others, and that there are Hunter orgs in the game whose outlook I definitely don't share, BUT I'm not trying to start a flame war, nor will I bash other people's beliefs here. I think it's against a community guideline anyway. Even if it wasn't that's not the point of this thread. This is an opportunity to think about what hunter organization(s) YOU would join, why, and share with the group for entertainment purposes. No judgement.

                      Aw geez Rick, that's a tough question. One I will over answer trying to decide which one I'd go within a more complicated way than I should.

                      Hunter 1st Compacts: Uh. Probably either the Loyalists of Thule-the stark reminder of the dangers of powers, acting as a communal support function and subtle judge of their community, the dig for occult secrets and knowing More Than Everyone Else, Fuck Nazis-or Network Zero-very much my people, let's call it there.

                      Hunter 1st Conspiracies: The Ascending Ones or the Lucifuge-deep emphasis on compassion, insight, and diplomacy for both, deeply interesting histories, on the AO's part a emphasis on interconnected communities, on the Lucifuge, power ostensibly graspable that can punch out the larger forms of oppression.

                      Witch Finders Compacts: Is it weird to say The Promethean Brotherhood? We've established not every mage uses their power to their best ends, and while the Promethean Brotherhood is also not all sunshine and rainbows, they still have a lot more punch against some of the more influential enemies in the Chronicles world.

                      Spirit Slayers Compacts: The Talbot Group, hands down. Sure, they have no fucking clue what they're doing, but they're still among the better motivated of the Hunter Organizations and that makes it easy for them to learn better.

                      Night Stalkers Compacts: Uhm. the Night Angels probably need more people, but they wouldn't trust me and also it's more work. Sister's Blood Maidenhood.

                      Slasher/Mortal Remains Compacts: It's a toss up between Habibti Ma and Yuri's Group. Probably Yuri's Group.

                      Supplemental Conspiracies: Is it weird I kind of want to leave my answer at The Cainite Conspiracy? It's not, it's Les Mysteres first, and then from there, barring the Merrick Institute which I would not want by virtue of having been part or and probably couldn't get into as an adult, they're all kind of assholes, soooooooo maybe the Cainites?

                      Dark Eras Compacts: Azusa Miko or Bijin. Which depends on how vain I'm feeling on the day(some days I am sooo pretty, people).

                      Dark Era Conspiracies': What, you mean which of the only four conspiracies that exist across three books? Order of the Black Knight, duh, it has a lot of varying levels of action, and it's highest action has you in a bitching suit of immortality armor that eats your sanity.

                      Hunter 2nd Compacts: Wait, do I even qualify for any of the new ones?

                      Hunter 2nd Conspiracies: Sure, I'll dance for some ghosts-it's not like there's a lot of new options outside of the Council of Bones.

                      Actual Compact Choice: Honestly, the Loyalists are the right layer of action and motivation for me. You can have days off and take the backseat, but you can still also clearly do a lot of good, and even occasionally take to the scene.

                      Actual Conspiracy Choice: The Ascending Ones do not get enough love-these are very much my people (that do not greet you by putting a gun to your head and giving you a very clear choice).

                      And between the two, the grand winner is:

                      The Loyalists of Thule. I mean, c'mon, off days, background work, AND punching Nazis, what's not love.
