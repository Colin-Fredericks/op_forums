    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post

    If we get Mage\Mummy French Revolution, then there is really not being possible to see Beast French Revolution, as OPP do not return with material to their previous written Dark Eras.
    Based on what? All three of the mini-Eras in War Against the Pure (Yellow Turban China, Roman-Parthian War, and Icelandic Settlement) were either featured again (The Wolf and The Raven) or had an era set a few decades before (Forsaken by Roman) or after (Three Kingdoms of Darkness). Hell, the biggest historical setting bar none is Requiem for Rome, and it got not one but two Eras for other gamelines in the DEC. Even the appendix of RfR, Slouching Towards Byzantium, got a semi-sequel in Sothis Ascends (Return to Dust). I'd say there is plenty of evidence that if a setting is compelling, OPP will revisit it, just not in the same Kickstarter and probably not right away.
