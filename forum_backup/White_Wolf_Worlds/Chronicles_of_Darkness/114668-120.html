                        Originally posted by Tessie View Post
                        Veiling generally just hides something rather than changing how it appears, but if Life can make something appear different then all the other Arcana should be able to as well.
                        Worth noting that comparable effects exist for other Arcana:

                        - Corpse Mask (Death 2) changes the apparent cause of death of a corpse.
                        - Fabricate Fortune (Fate 2) can create fake destinies and fake Fate-based trigger events.
                        - Alter Mental Pattern (Mind 2) gives the subject a fake set of thoughts, emotions etc if someone tries to use supernatural powers to read their mind.
                        - Lying Maps (Space 2) alters the subject's sense of direction to direct them down a desired route.

                        So I'd say the use of Veiling to cosmetically alter the subject is well-established.

                                                My reading of that is that the spell exists to tell you about future events caused by the subject, whether the subject is something in the present or something in the past. In the case of walking into the room, for instance, you could cast the spell first to determine whether the room bodes well for you. If someone's waiting inside to shoot you, the answer will probably be "no". Alternatively, if you've already gone in, and someone is about to shoot you from hiding but hasn't done so yet, and you cast the spell with Temporal Sympathy, you can ask "does the fact that I entered the room bode well or ill for me?" and the answer, again, will probably be "ill". In all cases, though, it's you in the present who casts the spell and receives the information.

                                                Does that clarify things for you at all?
