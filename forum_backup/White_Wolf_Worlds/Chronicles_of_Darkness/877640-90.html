    Originally posted by LadyLens View Post
    I don't think you'd need Death to use it on a corpse, and spirits aren't composed of conventional matter.
    I chose Structure because the purpose of the spell is to break down the entire object, and the best way to represent that is with Structure for the Withstand. It's meant primarily as a lab or workshop tool, not an attack spell.
    For Withstand you’re supposed to use the trait that represents resistance, for objects that trait is Durability. Structure is the representation of an object’s health, if this was an attack spell to destroy objects it would just reduce structure by Potency.

                                    Originally posted by Menace View Post
                                    One of my players in my chronicle has a dot of Fate but struggles a bit what he can do with dot. Anyone got some low-dot Fate effects that aren't "You're lucky now" and "Deal with destiny in some way".
                                    Luck and Destiny is basically it for Fate, but they're so wide fields that you can do quite a lot as long as you're just creative about it. Here are some smaller themes I've recognised:
                                    Blessings, Hexes, probabilities, oaths, destinies and conditional effects.
                                    • Blessings are most useful if you use them to grant Steadfast and Charmed to yourself and your cabal. Extra dice and dice tricks can also be nice if you have a turn to prepare yourself. Anyone who dabbles in Fate should get Exceptional Luck as a Rote just because of this.
                                    • Hexes can be used to apply Arm Wracks, Leg Wracks and Blinded to enemies in a combat situation.
                                    • Probabilities can be manipulated to make a fortune in gambling, as mentioned, but also to make you find the person you're looking for or even allow you to correctly guess passwords and phone numbers. With Serendipity you can even stumble upon stuff you didn't even know you needed, but can't be guaranteed to be particularly effective.
                                    • At the first dot you can use oaths to find out whether or not someone has fulfilled or broken an agreement. At the third dot you can supernaturally enforce any stated punishments for breaking the agreement.
                                    • You can recognise or shield an existing destiny at low dot levels, but most interesting effects are at later dots.
                                    • At two dots you get Conditional Duration as an Attainment, which allows spells to end when they're no longer useful. This is particularly useful in combination with Hung Spell from Time 2. Warding Gesture is at the same dot, allowing you to selectively exclude your subjects from AoE spells.

                                                            Calculate odds (Fate 1)
                                                            Practice: Unveiling
                                                            Primary factor: Duration

                                                            The subject of the spell becomes aware of the chance of success of his actions before attempting any action.
