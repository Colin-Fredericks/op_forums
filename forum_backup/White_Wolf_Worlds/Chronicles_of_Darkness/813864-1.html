    Originally posted by JMecha View Post
    As for the First Three Dots being Passive.....

    With the Dragon getting a bonus to detachment checks, will the Dragon begin to believe they have Transcended the need for Touchstones and start burning useless bridges? Will The Dragon who regularly feels remorse for Sins decide to make an effort to climb the Humanity Scale and be less of a Monster? or Will the Bonus Dice to Detachment Checks inspire a bit of reckless carelessness in the Dragon who decides that no matter what they do the Beast can not have them.....what does the all night society do with a member like that?

    A Dragon who is no longer suffering a penalty for interacting with Mortals, or even possibly getting a bonus for such interactions....what doors and opportunities does that create? The wedge that worked to separate the Dragon from the Mortal Population of the World has been removed or at a minimal weakened....how will that change the character's outlook, behavior, and options?

    As for the Torpor One.....that one's Passiveness and or Usefulness really changes table to table. At some tables it will make the difference between a Character in Torpor through some really fun and exciting swaths of time, and at other tables the Interesting Bits will be rearranged so they take place after any unexpected or inconvenient Torpor.

    I do not feel the coils I presented are exceptionally Passive compared to the Three Mysterious in the Core Book, maybe you are seeing something I am not? or hell maybe I am seeing something in my write up because it is mine and I am bias as hell? Either way I will review them again and compare them to the other existing coils to see if there is anything I do to make them less passive.
    Yes, the are all passive. As in, it doesn't encourage people to go out and do things. Its all reactionary.

    Touchstones do more than resist detachment. It talks you down from frenzy as well. Someone with high willpower will eventually feel less need for a Touchstone, sure. Especially the Ventrue, but that's part of the game. Touchstones have a lot of thematic impact on the game, and merely adding a bonus to resist degeneration isn't going to negate that. You'll need more.

    I can not suffer a penalty for interacting with mortals with a simple application of Majesty 1 (for appearance issues) and Auspex 1 (for empathy check issues). What doors and opertunities it creates? That depends entirely on who's running the game. 9 times of 10, you won't be opening more doors than the Daeva will. And it still depends on you reacting to the stimuli the ST provides.

    The Coils are passive because they all depend on the ST doing things, and then coming up in niche cases. Coil of the Wyrm? All about Riding the Wave. Frenzy threatens? You ride it, do something bad ass you choose to do, then snap out of it. A Nightmare/Majesty user threatened with a Pred Aura? Snap into frenzy, Aura back with larger dice pool, shrug off back to normal. Battle? You Ride again. Coil of the Voivode - go out and blood bond, get massive blood sympathy bonuses, create a dynasty from your blood. Coil of the Ascendant? Go out and play in the sun. Each Coil pushes you to do -more-. You don't learn Coils to sit there and shrug off bad things. You learn Coils to gain power and advantage over your rivals and enemies.

    Each of the core book Coils are deliberately provoking you to do -something-. To go out and make stories. Simply resisting detachment is not very story fulfilling.

                        I actually liked the flavor of the Coil, and can easily see it as mainstream in the Ordo, not as a heretical Coil.

                        Yes, Humanity is a weakness for a vampire. But what the Coil do to protect it serves for the vampire to ignore the weakness.

                        That in mind, I think the first dot is good enough. It is strong, but not overpowered in my opinion. Yes, it does hurt a central theme of the game, but this is what every Coil do! They are about going against vampire weaknesses (even Coil of the Wyrm grants more control of frenzy as you start to take it into account instead of avoiding it). It can simply be banned for players when the chronicle demands, as should be any other Coil, but doesn't seem specially bad as default.

                        But the complains about passiveness are very valid. I loved the flavor, but the rules are too much reactive, and mostly positively weak. Let me revise each one, it is easier for me:

                        Sisyphean Fortitude•
                        The Beast is an ever present current of want, eroding away what Humanity remains until nothing remains but want. Those who have mastered this coil have learned to better endure the storm that is the every present threat of the Beast upon their Sanity. Whenever the Dragon rolls for Detachment add their Coils of Humanities Rank to their Detachment Dice Pool to maintain Humanity. In addition to this an Exceptional Success can be achieved with 3 Successes.
                        This seems good enough. If much, it could be turned to another level. It makes possible for the vampire to keep its sanity while being completely atrocious. Another possibility could be to let the vampire develop a Ban to prevent a specific Breaking Point before doing the act, but maybe it is better reserved for a scale. A possibility for putting a little limit on the use of the bonus could be to link it to the Meditation roll.
                        Koinonein • •
                        To Divide and Conquer is a time tested and proven tactic, and the Beast knows it well. One of the more subtle and ruthless attacks upon the Mind of the Man is the Beast's efforts to isolate the Man from others. By making the Man feel removed from Humanity, he is removed from the herd and easier prey for the Beast. Dragons who have Mastered this Coil have learned how to keep their place in the Herd despite the Beast's best efforts. Add the Dragon's ranks in the Coils of Humanities in regards to interacting with Humanity.
                        This one seems a little off even in the flavor, but starts too good to be ignored. Maybe at this stage, the Dragon could start to develop personas to deal with humans, assuming full effect of the idea of Humanity as the ability to mimic the Herd. Thinking quickly about Protean Predatory Aspect, I think similar bonuses could be developed for human interactions as "personality aspects". It would be more interesting, less passive, synergistic with Majesty instead of overlapping, and at the same time more inhuman even being Human.
                        Promethus in the Cave of Hypnos• • •
                        The forced Hibernation of Topor is another of the Beasts threats to the Mind of Man. Through removing the Man from Humanity for vast cycles of time it forces the Man to see himself as an entity separate from the present, separate from the People of Today, always a creature of the past and of the future. In making the Man unfocused upon the present and uncaring for those who live within it, the Beast makes it easier for the Man to give in to the ever present Temptations of the Beast. Dragons who have Mastered this Coil have learned to steel themselves against the Beast's Call to Hibernate as an Animal. Dragons add their ranks in the Coil of Humanities to their Humanity for purposes of determining the length of their Topor.
                        This needs a lot of work. Torpor is something that isolates the vampire, but also something that prepares it for the new times, specially when the Fog of Eternity enters play. But it reduces actual power. And what's worse, it rarely enters play truly. For most games (I know there are a lot of exceptions), a long torpor is as good as death for practical game purposes. And simply reducing its length don't make it more interesting. A Coil that works Torpor should make Torpor something that will enter play a lot, as the Coil of the Wyrm makes with Frenzy (from avoiding at all cost, the PC starts to want it). Maybe this Coil could let the vampire go into Torpor for controlled lengths of time (in a span that is actually playable, weeks at the worst), to reap true benefits from it. As a first draft, I would let each day of Torpor eliminate 1XP in Abilities or other traits approved by the ST, as the character forgets to make room for the new, and this induced freshness of the mind lets it relocate the XP removed to new Abilities at the same rate. It doesn't seem really good to me as written, but something in this direction, I don't have the time to work it better now.
                        Man Beneath Morpheus • • • •
                        The Beast is never truly at rest. Even within the deep hibernation of Topor, or the slumber of Day Sleep the Beast is Hungry for more, it's mind races with the revelry of past and future hunts, always denying the mind of Man the Rest it needs to fully recover. Dragons who have Mastered this Coil of Humanity are no longer the victims of the Beast's hunger when they close their eyes. Dragons who have Mastered this Coil of Humanities may regain 1 point of Willpower from Day Sleep the same as Mortals regain Willpower from a Night's Rest.
                        This is just too weak a benefit even for first level. For fourth it is laughable. But what about bringing Man and Beast to the same level? Or even inverting them? One benefit I think is of the reversing relation of Man and Beast during Daysleep: the vampire actualy let its Beast take control during Day, its most weak of times, while the Man rests, but is still wake enough to get the leash. The vampire enters a state of constant frenzy by day, but the Man can intercede at any moment to force down the Beast spending 1 WP. Being day, it is mostly a self-preserving tactic, with the Beast being able to defend itself with all its superlative strength, but barred everywhere by its own coward nature (because as any savage animal, the Beast is truly a coward facing any real danger. Throw in some more benefits, like bonuses for resisting frenzies during the night when your Beast had all day active, and that is my current idea. This is a tough one.
                        Human Under Helios • • • • •
                        The Beast is a Nocturnal Predator, Man is not. The Light of Day does not Burn Man, it Burns the Beast. Dragons who have Mastered the Fifth and Final Coil of Humanities stand dominate over the Beast, while it cowers in the shadow of Man. Because the Dragon's Beast cowers within the shadow of Man, the Dragon is afforded some protection from the light of day. The Dragon may as Instant action spend Willpower to to increase their Humanity Rating for One Scene. This use of Willpower does count as their use of Willpower for the Round, but for purposes of this Coil the Dragon can spend as many Willpower Points as they desire to increase their effective Humanity Rating for the Scene. The Dragon's modified Humanity for the Scene is the Humanity that is used to determine the penalty or Damage they receive for any and all Banes that use Humanity as part of their formula. This includes the Damage Dealt by Sun Light.
                        This here really is too similar to another Coil, but not beyond hope. The core idea is to keep your Mind under the sun, not the body necessarily. To use the sun against the Beast. I'm thinking about something to get advantages against Degeneration and Frenzy from the sun, or from a sunburn. Or maybe an advantage to other mental tasks or powers.

                                                        Mystery of the Humanities

                                                        Often the Mystery of Humanity is spurned as a Heretical teaching created in effort to escape Vamprism and return to Humanity, instead of Transcending Vamprisim. The teaching of the Coils of Humanities is banned in a great many Ordo Dracul Chapter Houses across the World, and even where it's teachings are not banned out right, the Mystery of Humanities and it's Coils are very rarely studied or practiced. In truth the Ordo of Dracul has found no means of actually measuring the progress of any student of the Mystery of Humanities and so never is any claimed progress in the Mystery allowed to be part of any Dragon's Covenant Rank, Status, or Title.

                                                        The Mystery of Humanity was not created in effort to escape Vampirism and revert back to being Human.The truth of the Mystery though is that it was created to aid the Man in his eternal struggle against the Beast. The creator of the Mystery of Humanity believed that the greatest bane of the Vampric condition was the slow but inevitable erosion of self.
                                                        The lessons learned through exploring the Mystery of Humanity are lessons of the Humanities such as Art, Philosophy, Morality, Ethics, and Logic. The Humanities though are not studied though in effort to force Morality upon the Vampire, or in Effort to once more to become Human. They are studied to better draw the divide the between the Vampire's Mind and the Beast. Dragons who have mastered the Mystery of Humanity and it's Coils have learned to separate their own choices from the Beast's desires and demands, regardless of the morality of the choices they choose to make.

                                                        Coils of the Humanities

                                                        Sisyphean Fortitude•

Those who have mastered this coil have learned to better endure the storm that is the every present threat of the Beast upon their Sanity. Dragons who have Mastered this Coil have learned how to keep thier place in the Herd dispite the Beast's best efforts. Add the Dragon's ranks in the Coils of Humanities in regards to interacting with Humanity.

                                                        Man beneath Morpheus • • •
                                                        The Beast is never truly at rest. Even within the deep hibernation of Torpor, or the slumber of Day Sleep the Beast is Hungry for more, it's mind races with the revelry of past and future hunts, always denying the mind of Man the Rest it needs to fully recover. Dragons who have Mastered this Coil of Humanity are no longer the victims of the beast's unsated hungers when they close their eyes.                                                         Scales of Humanity

                                                        Lessons of Humility
                                                        Prerequisite Coils: Koinonein
                                                        Procedure: Over the course of an hour the Dragon is able to remind his subject of the frailty of Mortal Men and the many hardships they endure through out their existence. This conditioning can when successful aid even the most jaded kindred in remembering how to empathize with others and understand their prey in a way they had forgotten. For a short time the subject may once more remember what it meant to be a mere mortal and once more move among them seeing them for more then mere meals.

                                                        The Dragon needs to roll Presence + Expression.
                                                        Roll Results
                                                        Dramatic Failure: The Dragon has rambled on and on about the weaknesses of humanity and has failed to inspire sympathy for mortals, and instead stirred the Beast within himself and his audience. Both the Dragon and his Subject gain the Wanton Condition.
                                                        Failure: The Dragon's meaningless words fall upon deaf and uncaring ears.
                                                        Success: The Dragon's words once more, if only for a Night, remind the Subject of the plight of Man Kind. The Subject may add the Dragon's Successes to his Humanity for one Night in regards to how Humanity effects interaction with Mortals.
                                                        Exceptional Success: Same as a Success, and the Jaded Condition if possessed is resolved.

                                                        Startling The Slumbering
                                                        Prerequisite Coil: Prometheus in the Cave of Hypnos
                                                        Procedure: The Dragon must spend their Day Sleep along side the Torpored Subject, and upon waking immediately feed a minimal of one point of Vitae to the subject. For every Vitae feed to the subject in this manner a die is rolled, and Successes are added to the Subject's Humanity Score for purposes of determining the length of their Torpor. This procedure can be repeated day after day and night after night with all the successes rolled working towards raising the Subjects Humanity for the purpose of awakening from Torpor.
