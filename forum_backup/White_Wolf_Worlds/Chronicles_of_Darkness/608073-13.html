                                                        I don't think anyone was doing anything that might reasonably create Deviants, although I don't know exactly how they are created. The point is you could fudge any country with the funds for the experimentation and the people with the know how to create Deviants, however that ends up being, to create a setting. Eugenics, and similar thinking, was indeed the lead in, IMO, so you could easily come up with something when we actually know about Deviants.
