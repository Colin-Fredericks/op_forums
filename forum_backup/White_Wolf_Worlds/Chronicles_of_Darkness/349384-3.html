    Alex Mercer (Prototype): a Gangrel scientist hired by a genetic research corporation controlled by the Ordo Dracul. The owners of the corporation seek to take the vampire curse to its full militaristic potential. His research leads him into finding and experimenting with a Strix called "The Blacklight", giving it the power to infect and spread beyond what any other Strix can. When he learned that the employees of the corporation where being killed by Hunters to cover the research up, Alex took the Strix, bound into a vial of blood, and unleashed it into the world.

    Infected Alex Mercer:

    Shadow Potency: 10

    Max Vitae/Per Turn: 75/15

    Vice: Proliferate

    Attributes: Power 10, Finesse 6, Resistance 8

    Host Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 4, Stamina 5

    Skills: Academics 3, Crafts 3, Computer 3, Medicine 3, Occult 3, Politics 1, Investigation 5, Science 5, Athletics 5, Brawl 5, Drive 3, Firearms 3, Larceny 3, Stealth 3, Survival 3, Weaponry 5, Empathy 3, Expression 1, Intimidation 5, Persuasion 3, Streetwise 2, Subterfuge 4

    Corpus: 14 (Size 6)

    Host Health: 10

    Willpower: 10

    Defense: 4 + Athletics/Brawl/Weaponry

    Initiative Mod: 14

    Speed: 15

    Dread Powers:

    Doom – See The Cracks, Web Of Destiny
    Host – Breath Eater, Hollow Bones
    Shadow – Sudden Surprise
    Vitae – Indomitable, Kindred Disciplines x 4

    Disciplines: Protean 5, Resilience 5, Vigor 5, Celerity 5, Obfuscate 4, Auspex 3, Nightmare 2

    Devotions: Body Of Will, Force Of Nature, Juggernaut’s Gait, Hint Of Fear, Shatter The Shroud, Abominable Weaponry, Abominable Armor, One Drop Great Mending (Advanced), Relentless Mauling (Advanced), Quicken Sight, The Blacklight Virus

    Embodiments: Shadow Form, Possess Corpse, Possess Revenant, Possess Kindred, Possess Living, Synthesis, Materialize, Enter Twilight, Shadow Jump, Dark Places

    Banes: up to the ST.

    Abominable Weaponry (Vigor 5, Protean 4)

    The Kindred who possess this Devotion are not to be trifled with. They can manifest truly horrible natural weapons.

    Cost: 10 Vitae
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: Reflexive

    The Kindred grows thrashing, barbed tendrils from all over his body, changes his hands into humongous claws or his arm into a steel piercing sword and so on. He may make Brawl or Weaponry attacks that have a +2A damage bonus and Armor Piercing 4 for the rest of the scene. Resilience offers no protection against this Devotion, but a related Devotion does (see below). This Devotion costs 5 Experiences.

    Abominable Armor (Resilience 5, Protean 4)

    The Kindred who possess this Devotion are hard to take down. They can manifest very persistent natural armor.

    Cost: 10 Vitae
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: Reflexive

    The Kindred applies his Resilience as Armor against all attacks (including the previous Devotion) for the remainder of the scene, but he is covered in some obvious protection: chitin or bone plates, jagged obsidian or crystallized blood. This Devotion costs 5 Experiences.

    One Drop, Great Mending (Vigor 3, Resilience 3, Celerity 3)

    The Kindred’s Vitae is more efficient at regenerating his undead body than usual.

    Cost: None
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: None (always active)

    Every point of Vitae heals 2 lethal or 3 bashing. Healing 1 aggravated only costs 3 Vitae. This Devotion costs 3 Experiences. There is an advanced version which requires Vigor 4, Resilience 4, Celerity 4 and Blood Potency 6. This version makes each Vitae heal 3 lethal, and allows you to heal 1 aggravated with 3 Vitae and no sleeping. The advanced version costs 6 Experiences.

    Relentless Mauling (Vigor 3, Resilience 3, Celerity 3)

    The Kindred can strike multiple foes in one turn.

    Cost: 3 Vitae or 5 Vitae (Advanced)
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: Reflexive

    Upon a successful Brawl or Weaponry attack, double the amount of total points of damage dealt and divide them between two or more targets. Each target may only receive as much damage as your base successes. Example: your vampire grows natural weapons by using Predatory Aspect, Unnatural Aspect, Abominable Aspect or some other power. Let’s say you get 3 successes and your weapon has a damage bonus of 2. [3 + 2] x 2 = 10 points of damage to be distributed, but each target may only receive up to 3 points of damage. This Devotion costs 3 Experiences. The advanced version requires Vigor 5, Resilience 5 and Celerity 5, which quadruples your total success before division. The advanced version costs 6 Experiences.

    The Blacklight Virus (Protean 5, Vigor 5, Resilience 5, Celerity 5, Blacklight)

    The Strix known as Blacklight has been infused into a viral medium that is much faster than anything it could accomplish on its own. The Blacklight Virus spreads through contact with infected blood, turning victims into Draugr, huge Larvae with natural weapons and other blasphemies against nature. Only occasionally does the Blacklight Virus turn a victim into a Kindred.

            The ponies and other creatures from "My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic" can be adapted to the NWOD in two ways. If they are straight up mystical, intelligent anmals, they are hobgoblins living in a massive Hedge Kingdom (NOT an Arcadian Realm). The Princesses and major antagonists are elder hobgoblins with access to Contracts that have Archmage level effects.

            If they are adapted as humans, they'd work nicely as Mages. Their "Cutie Marks" are actually their Nimbuses. Of note is that all major antagonists in the series so far wanted power. An interesting finale for Mage FiM would be the Mane Six teaming up with Discord and the Princesses against a pony equivalent of an Aswadim (Scelestus Archmage). This finale is not likely to happen in the original FiM anytime soon, as it would go against Hasbro's interests to keep the series going on for as long as it can.

                                Initiative: 12
                                Defense: 8
                                Speed: 13

                                    Albert Wesker

                                    Virtue: Ambitious; Wesker set lofty goals for himself, and frequently achieved them.
                                    Vice: Conceited; Wesker is supremely assured of his physical and mental superiority.

                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                                    Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 5, Stamina 5
                                    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 3, Composure 5

                                    Skills: Academics 4(Research, Tactics), Animal Ken 2 (Test Subjects), Athletics 4, Brawl 5, Computers 2, Drive 2 (Helicopter),Firearms 3, Intimidation 4(Superiority), Investigation 3, Persuasion 4 (Blackmail, Negotiation), Politics 3 (Corporate, Military), Science 5 (B.O.W’s, Virology), Socialize 2 (Corporate), Stealth 3, Streetwise 2 (Black Markets), Subterfuge 4(Espionage), Survival 2, Weaponry 2(Improvised)

                                    Merits: Allies 4(TRICELL), Area of Expertise (B.O.W’s), Close Quarters Combat Style 2, Contacts (Many), Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Eidetic Memory, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Science), Fast Reflexes 3, Fire Fight Style 2, Fleet of Foot 3, Hardy 3, Heavy Weapons Style 3, Improvised Weaponry Style 1, Interdisciplinary Specialty (Tactics), Iron Will, Language (Many), Library 5 (Umbrella Archive; Academics, Politics, Science), Martial Arts Style 4, Patient, Professional Training 5 ( Scientist; Academics, Science, Subterfuge), Resources 5, Striking Looks 2, Trained Observer 1, Unarmed Defense Style 5

                                    Integrity: 6
                                    Willpower: 9
                                    Potency: 4 [Mortal Remains, pg. 104-105]
                                    Size: 5
                                    Initiative: 16
                                    Defense: 9
                                    Speed: 18
                                    Health: 10
                                    Armor: 3/3
                                    Condition: Megalomania

                                    Dread Powers

                                    Dead Flesh
                                    From each source of harm, the Wesker downgrades up to 3 levels of damage from lethal to bashing, or from bashing to nothing. This Dread Power has no effect on aggravated damage.

                                    Flicker and Flash
                                    This Dread Power allows Wesker to step across spaces so quickly; he may as well be teleporting.
                                    Cost: 1 Willpower Action: Reflexive Dice Pool: 12 dice
                                    Successful use of this Dread Power adds the number of successes to Wesker’s Defense for the turn, and allows him to reflexively move his Speed +3. If he uses Flicker and Flash reflexively before an attack roll is made, he might move out of its range and avoid it altogether.
                                    Wesker can only activate Flicker and Flash three times in a turn. However, take the highest rolled successes to modify her Defense; the effect is not cumulative.

                                    Immune to Pain
                                    Cost: 2 Willpower Action: Instant
                                    Wound penalties do not apply to Wesker, no matter how badly he is hurt. Stamina rolls to remain conscious aren’t made when a bashing wound is marked in his rightmost Health box. He remains conscious automatically, collapsing only when a lethal or aggravated wound is marked in that box (and he is dying or dead). This power lasts until the end of the scene.

                                    Inhuman Alacrity
                                    Cost: 1 Willpower Action: Reflexive
                                    Wesker has speed, coordination and reflexes above and beyond human limits. Until the end of the turn, Wesker may add his full Defense against ranged attacks. Wesker must still be aware of incoming attacks to defend against them.

                                    Regeneration
                                    Wesker heals at an unnatural rate; Bashing damage heals at one point per turn. Lethal damage disappears at a rate of one point per 15 minutes. Aggravated damage, however, is not affected by this ability.

                                    Sharp Senses
                                    Wesker’s senses are especially keen. He receives +2 to all perception rolls, and may track by scent. Wesker can also ignore up to three dice of distance-related penalties to any perception roll.

                                    Swift Intercession
                                    Cost: 1 Willpower Action: reflexive
                                    This dread power gives Wesker a +3bonus to Initiative. By spending a point of Willpower, he can immediately move to the head of the Initiative queue. This boost in Initiative lasts only one turn, after which all combatants return to acting in their rolled order.

                                    Unholy Dexterity: Wesker may spend 1 Willpower to gain a +2 bonus to dexterity. This bonus lasts until the end of the scene. Recalculate appropriate traits (Speed, Health, Initiative, Defense) using the new Attribute score.

                                    Unholy Stamina: Wesker may spend 1 Willpower to gain a +4 bonus to stamina. This bonus lasts until the end of the scene. Recalculate appropriate traits (Speed, Health, Initiative, Defense) using the new Attribute score.

                                    Unholy Strength: Wesker may spend 1 Willpower to gain a +4 bonus to strength. This bonus lasts until the end of the scene. Recalculate appropriate traits (Speed, Health, Initiative, Defense) using the new Attribute score.

                                    Notes

                                    The above reflects Albert Wesker shortly after the dissolution of the UMBRELLA Corporation.

                                        Merits: Giant, Favored Weapon (Guns Of Arthur Eld), Fleet Of Foot 2, Tolerance for Biology, Quick Healer, Inspiring, Demolisher 2, increase Hardy to 3, Prodigy (homebrewed by Pendragon, this allows you to raise an Attribute/Skill to 6 if you are the best of the best) and his signature Lightning Reload technique.

                                        Lightning Reload: Roland does not need to spend a turn to reload his revolvers. However, every time he uses this Merit, he must roll Stamina + Resolve, with a cumulative -1 penalty. If he fails, he gains the Impaired Condition in relation to his hands. The cumulative penalty is reset at the end of the scene.

                                        Impaired (Persistent): the specific body part is damaged, but still usable. Rolls involving it lose the 10 Again bonus, suffer a -2 penalty and turn into failures if any 1s come up. Beat: you fail a roll due to the Condition. Resolution: proper medical treatment.

                                                    The Executioner (AKA Pyramid Head)

                                                    Quote: (heavy breathing and the high pitched scraping of metal on metal)

                                                    Background: the Executioner, more so than other monsters of Silent Hill, exists to deliver judgement. It represents repressed urges and the desire to be punished, taken to nightmarish and extremely grotesque levels..

                                                    Description: the Executioner manifests as a very tall, vaguely masculine humanoid with the trademark burnt flesh of Silent Hill’s monsters. It either wears minimal clothing made from human skin or is naked. Its head is covered by a contraption of rusted metal and varying shape, from which its hideous vocalizations can sometimes be heard. Whatever is hidden inside its helmet is best left undiscovered. Even when not carrying its massive weapons, fighting the Executioner can mean agonizing injuries that quickly lead to death.

                                                    Storytelling Hints: the Executioner is good for visceral horror, but it represents heavy themes. You should make sure your players are OK with graphic description of extreme violence before including the Executioner. Even then, use it sparingly, so as to not diminish the impact of its appearance or cause unfair deaths. The only way to truly confront it is coming to terms with your guilt. What this means varies according to character, should be carefully discussed and a major plot point.

                                                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 1, Wits 2, Resolve 3
                                                    Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 2, Stamina 4
                                                    Social Attributes: Presence 5, Manipulation 1, Composure 3

                                                    Mental Skills: Investigation (Searching) 2
                                                    Physical Skills: Athletics (Throwing) 3, Brawl (Grappling) 5, Weaponry (Great Knife, Great Spear) 5
                                                    Social Skills: Intimidation 3

                                                    Merits: Giant, Bound To The Town 5, Heavy Weapons 3 (Great Knife, Great Spear), Demolisher 3

                                                    Willpower: 6
                                                    Initiative: 5
                                                    Defense: 2 + Athletics/Brawl/Weaponry
                                                    Speed: 11
                                                    Health: 10
                                                    Static Rating: 10

                                                    Weapons:

                                                    Bare Hands: +0L. Dice Pool: 10. Special: after grappling, spend 1 willpower point to strike a Killing Blow by flaying the skin.
                                                    Great Knife: +4L. Dice Pool: 11. -3 Initiative. -6 Speed.
                                                    Great Spear: +3L. Dice Pool: 7 for throwing, 11 for melee. -2 Initiative. -3 Speed. Range: 20/40/80

                                                    Note: I am using the template included in Mr Gone’s Silent Hill Supplement for the NWOD.
