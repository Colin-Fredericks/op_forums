        It should be noted that manipulating Conspiracies/Compacts would drastically raise both the dangers and benefits of manipulating Hunters. It would probably be less personal and more difficult for player characters to pull of, but canon Task Force: Valkyrie is secretly run by vampires for one example. Having a single monster hunter on a leash has its uses and its risks. Having a cell has more benefits and more risks. Having a large organization as your personal hit-squad can be incredibly useful... if you can keep it from backfiring.

                                                            I have to argue for manipulating a cell rather than coopting them, after all the greatest value of such a group is its role as a deniable asset and a disposable one and the more you tie yourself to them or invest in them the less deniable and disposable they become.
                                                            ​I also don't see any worthwhile 'Fame' being gained from having such a group at your side, yes you want your enemies and friends to be mindful of the possible resources you can call up but having such a group being positively known might instead win you infamy as a threat to the society as a whole, helping your enemies turn even your friends against you.

                                                            ​You don't have to coopt the entire cell to gain control over it, just one influential member (or even just one voice that votes the way you want) can give great influence over the group.

                                                            ​You can also go all 'Citizen Kane' on them, control the flow of information they receive and you control their choice of targets.

                                                            ​I imagine any supernatural would want at least one degree of separation from the cell, if only to give him time to run when things go south.

                                                            ​Lastly I would say that most splats wouldn't use the cell to hunt their own, but the other splats. For example a Vampire wouldn't send the Hunters after other Vampires, but against the Werewolves engaging him in a turf war.

                                                            ​The case of the teenage girl turned Hunter would make for an interesting complication, especially if the supernatural admires her for her willingness to fight back.
