    What If? Overwatch (Blizzard) Canon

    04-22-2018, 11:24 PM
    Because I felt the need to complete the DCU and Marvel reimagining, and I know squat about Image. That, and the game already has a Gothic Horror event that happens every Halloween, so...

    What if the reason Overwatch's technology seems so much like magic was that...it was. Magic and advanced technology draw on the same laws of the universe in the CofD, so the world of Overwatch being what happens after humanity powers through the elementary sections of occult physics isn't that far fetched. That, and if you're someone who crawls through the lore a lot, you quickly realize that the supposed utopia...really isn't. See: Vishkar, and them reacting to discovering no evidence of wrongdoing by a competitor being to blow up their building rather than lose a construction bid.

    So, random theories on what various Overwatch Heroes are through a CofD lens?

    My personal picks:

    Tracer: Hungry Sin-Eater. Yeah, no lucky time-shift here, that accident left her very dead and joined with a geist personifying the risk of fatal accidents in stunts, giving her the distinct easy-going attitude.

    Symmetra: Obrimos Seer of the Throne trying very hard to convince herself she was recruited by the right side despite eying a phone number to the local thearchs' recruitment center.

    Roadhog: Hunter in Darkness Rahu, living embodiment trying to protect what's left of his home somehow. Sometimes not as effectively as he'd like.

    Widowmaker: Centimanus Tammuz made from the remains of Amelie Lacroix, and having some strong Residual Memories. It's not even that she wants to be evil, it's that she has no idea how other to be than an assassin. If she ever leaves Talon she'll be a good Pariah.

    EDIT: Realized that, given what she was made for, Tammuz fits Widowmaker more than Galateid. She is no one's companion, as evinced by that "Under the Mistletoe" highlight (she pulls it out to hang over her head...then punches the cameraman out in disgust).

                                      Also, some of the hero Heroes:

                                      Soldier: 76: A member of VALKYRIE's successor (his genetic treatments weren't enough to push him beyond human - another reason Reaper resents him to no end), turned Pariah, turned "solo operator" (big Swiss quotation marks around that phrase - he gets his tech somewhere).

                                      Jesse McCree: Tempted to make him a Blood Talon after all the fan-concept of him being a werewolf, but I'll go with his lack of any wolf-theming and make him an Inguma Predator who leans a bit into the Ugallu part of his family ("It's high noon" may as well be his hunting cry, the last thing his prey ever hears before feeling the bullets impact).

                                      Mercy: An Obrimos Libertine, actually, though she's a specialist in Life magic. She's all about Techne and turning magical theory into nanotech fact. Which implies that in the CofD, she really is a mad scientist who has a complicated time with ethics, and a genuinely good person. Mages be complicated, yo.

                                      The Shimada Bros: Lineage of Offspring or possibly Proximi; Hanzo relies on his abilities and archery exclusively, while Genji was saved by Mercy hacking the God-Machine's own medical tech to catch his soul before it left his body (as shown in his Blackwatch skin, his mods aren't actually all that extensive).

                                      Mei: The First Unchained Herself come to wreck vengeance upon the world A Lion-Headed Arisen who's gone seriously native, to the point where it's not obvious what her Guild is; she does not care about the Judges any more. Snowball is actually her faithful Sadikh's brain inna jar inna cute exterior.
