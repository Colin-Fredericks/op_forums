                        The most-obvious thing we should do after the Waters would be the Currents and fleshing out what they mean and how they interact. We might also want to push on codifying the lore or at least establishing what events are important when it comes to a Siren. The main thing is the Surfacing and The Song, which are key points that create a Siren. It kinda raises the question of how much does it change the once-mortal's perspective on life, especially considering that the Sirens merge two existences together. How much of Dimension A's memories flow into Dimension B's? There is a lot of questions that are raised and should be answered.

                        I think this version of Siren kinda deviated from what the typical depiction of Sirens are. I'm not saying that it's wrong, but I think some of my ideas are going too far into the "high sci-fi" direction. It's mainly how I justified Sirens, the "time" theme, and the idea of alternate dimensions. It's considerably jarring when the other aspects of Siren revolve around music, nature, and mythology.

                        Taking a step back, the key lore of Siren is that humanity is going to be wiped out by The Deluge, an apocalyptic flood. The last of humanity began a melody known as The Song to express their profound regret and sorrow over the state of the world under The Deluge. This caused Ceto to awaken the Sirens and send them either back in time or to an alternate dimension. Echidna is a running antagonistic force to the Sirens and she controls a sort of stronghold known as Serenia, a great library that holds the history of the world within it. She is also the creator of the Vodianoi, amalgamations of humanity and sea creatures in order to create a life form that can withstand The Deluge. Eventually, the Currents were created from exceptional, mythical Sirens exemplifying a certain way of interacting with humanity. The Celestial Ocean, a realm that exists between realms, allows the Sirens access to their previous life as well as granting them a way to see into alternate realities.

                        I think, as someone has said before, that the multiple realities idea muddles the general feel of Sirens. Considering that Sirens are oracles with the ability to Trace events and are meant to be mediators between humanity and nature, while factoring in that there is a theme of History and a Music-based motif, maybe it would be better to make the "alternate realities" into "Oral History". Rather than being from a bad future, Sirens are actually from the past where The Deluge has already happened. The world has already reset multiple times, meaning that there has been lineages of Sirens that have attempted to stop The Deluge of their time. Some had succeeded, some had failed. However, The Deluge always comes to claim them. When The Deluge comes, it wipes out humanity as well as the Sirens in its waves. Some Sirens accept their failure, passing on their nature to the Sirens of the new world. Some Sirens refuse to accept their failure, disappearing into the depths of the Celestial Ocean, as did Echidna, the Siren of the first world. When a Siren is born into the new world, they hear an echo that ripples in their mind, like a drop of water in a lake. That echo, that sound is the remnants of The Song, the haunting melody sang by the lost humans of the first world. It is The Song that awakens the Sirens. The Sirens of the new world must struggle, the waves of many Deluges surrounding them in a whirlpool of suffering. When a Siren Surfaces from these memories, they are alive.

                        When a Siren emerges after Hearing the Song and Surfacing, they have gained the essence that makes them a Siren. The Song that echoed in their mind were the words of Sirens past, warning them of a disaster that awaits their world. The chorus of voices whisper to them, urging them to take up their mantle in order to stop the coming storm. The many records that have been drowned by The Deluge may be gone, but the struggle of the Sirens will live on as long as one Siren fights.

                        Hopefully, this would remove the complexity of multiple universes without losing on what the core of Sirens is meant to be. It would also allow us to justify the idea of submerged lands that was mentioned a while ago, with Mu, Lemuria, Ys, etc. being lands that existed before The Deluges of the past.

                                I believe it would work like this: Sirens aren't completely immortal. They are long-living at 300 years if they can sequester themselves and avoid any contact with chemicals or the Flensers. The main thing is that Sirens were awakened to stop the next instance of The Deluge at this time. The Sirens that managed to stop one of the instances were able to live a calm life with humanity until the next instance. When The Deluge is not stopped, the Sirens give up their existence in order to empower the next generation of Sirens. Some Sirens who refuse to fully give up their existence are thrown into the Celestial Ocean where they are cursed to watch the lineage of Sirens succeed and fail over time. The oldest of these Sirens is Echidna, who is able to reach out into the material world and create her Vodianoi.

                                With The Deluge, it is Nature's reset button that destroys life above the sea as it attempts to recover from a major disaster. Note that The Deluge is not necessarily always based on water. It could be a natural disaster on a small-scale that allows Nature to repair a damaged landscape without interference. Smaller instances of The Deluge is known as Flooding, the arguably best-known being the flood that Noah experienced. Sirens are meant to deal with Floodings immediately while The Deluge is a sort of disaster that is waiting on the horizon. While the Floodings are meant to "patch things up", The Deluge is meant to "shut everything down and start from scratch". The Deluge is the last resort for Nature as it tries to start again from square one. The implications of this is that humanity has existed multiple times before and have failed to right their wrongs before The Deluge hit, creating this sort of cycle of inevitability.

                                Because The Deluge is a reset, it destroys any evidence of previous life on earth as it returns to the beginning of life. Time and time again, humans rise as a dominant species and continue on their lives as expected. The major difference is the existence of Sirens, who are able to see glimpses of the past existences due to their connection with the Celestial Ocean. This is sort of where the "Oral History" idea comes in. Humanity does not remember their past existences but Sirens are able to sense the past. The memories may be fuzzy the further the memories go into the past, but it grants them some sort of insight that drives them to make changes in the world so that humanity may live, even if it is just for a little bit longer.

                                I also want to say that there are things that managed to escape from The Deluge, considering that The Deluge is meant to affect terrestrial life. Things that manage to hide deep within the oceans or escape into orbit are able to survive The Deluge. However, this means that they will have to experience eras of change and evolution to return to a point where humanity exists as top-dog. If these things are able to survive for that long, they could be drastically different, warped from years of breeding or aged to the point where they decay at a light touch. It's a myth among Sirens, but some believe that deep down in the oceans, past the Hadal Zone, exists a breed of Sirens that have long-since lost their connection to the Celestial Ocean. They are hungry beings who are unable to think, unable to remember The Song. They have grown into titanic creatures that will exist even when The Deluge comes, time and time again.

                                                The Deluge itself, the end-all apocalypse, is global and is meant to occur some time in the future from now. The Floodings, disasters that are in effect miniature Deluges, are regional and occur sporadically. In order to not step on Werewolf's toes, The Deluge and the Floodings are entirely natural events in technicality (as in they are not necessarily influenced by ghosts, spirits, etc. They are technically natural responses made by Nature).
