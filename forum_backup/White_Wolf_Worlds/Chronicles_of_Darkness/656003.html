    Question about True Friend merit and touchstones

    09-08-2015, 12:14 AM
    Applying the True Friend merit to a touchstone: As it effectively makes the touchstone, being a person, safe from harm (meta), Would you allow this, or is it to too cheese?

      I'd allow it. It is sort of 'cheese,' but it's the Storyteller who can't kill the True Friend or make them betray you, and I can easily see a vampire PC doing it themselves.

                                                          Why not? True Friends aren't supposed to be killed off by the GM for the sake of the plot. That doesn't leave a True Friend tagged NPC as off-limits for the GM to use as plot material. Going after a vampire's Touchstones (if you can identify them) is a pretty effective indirect way to chip away at a political rival. If your rival falls into the despair of losing those connections (aka the Languid Condition gets applied) they're going to become weaker and weaker; and eventually hit torpor.

                                                          Even if the NPCs threatening your True Friend have no real intention of killing them, they can easily know that you can't the risk of detachment (even if they don't die as Charlaquin points out). The goal might simply to be manipulation.
