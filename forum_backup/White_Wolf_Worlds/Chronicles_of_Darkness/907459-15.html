                                Here's my 2 cents on what's going on. I'll use D&D 5e as an example, which is a perfectly fine system BTW.
                                1. If you like Fantasy RPG, but don't like D&D 5e, there's a wealth of other alternatives with very active communities and publishers. If you like Horror / Play as supernatural, there are very few alternatives. Yes, there is CoC, Chill, Dresden Files, etc. but they tend to be very narrow games but they don't have very active communities and publishers. If you want to play Vampires that aren't horrible people, it's not like you can play that other super popular Vampire game not made by White Wolf (there isn't one)
                                2. D&D 5e is designed to appeal to the widest possible audience. You like Grim and Gritty, there's all sorts of dials to tweak in the DMG that let you do that. Onyx Path has made the decision with the CoD 2e books to just do whatever they think is the most interesting, challenging, creative take on the material. You want to play Prometheans that are dark superheroes, good for you! They didn't make that game.
                                3. D&D 5e wasn't really designed around a laser like focus on theme/mood/activity. Onyx Path made the decision in the CoD 2e books to design all the mechanics and fluff supporting a single theme/mood/activity. Werewolf is about the hunt, and walking the razor line of balance as a monstrous hunter. Promethean is about becoming human from an unnatural creature. If you change the theme/mood/activity, the mechanics don't fit that.
                                However, 1 is opposed to 2 and 3. It's great that I want to play the Angel TV show as an RPG, but the new CoD 2e wasn't built to do that. I can do it, but the system wasn't designed do whatever anyone felt they wanted to do with whatever theme/mood/activity. WoD isn't a really good alternative because the books are full of metaplot, lore, and assumptions that make them equally not hackable.

                                Now, it's not Onyx Path's responsibility to change 2 and 3 to accomodate 1. It would be cool if they did, and produced new Mirrors / Chronicles books for CoD 2e to support that. Yea, that would be cool HINT HINT HINT HINT.
