            Another observation about the Gold And Silver Caul. At first, I didn't get why Mastigos was the Resonant Path. To me, it would be Obrimos, because ideal heroes like Superman and Captain America fight for a cause they sincerely believe in, come Hell or high water. But then I remembered that most superheroes have a tragic event in their past that spurs them onward. Those who Awaken to the Watchtower of the Iron Gauntlet have to come to terms with a crucial flaw, no matter how much it hurts, hence the the term Scourging. Some heroes do exactly that, but others wear the trappings and use the powers of a Mastigos without embracing the renewal in agony. Batman is a divisive case. You can say he's a real Mastigos because he rejects a safe and ostentatious life in the ivory tower in order to protect the innocent. Or you can say he's a Banisher who cannot accept a misfortune that wasn't his fault. I'd go as far as to say that he would only become a Mastigos if he stopped fighting crime as Batman.

            I might be running my mouth off into superficiality town, but I thought it was worth mentioning.

Indeed, and perhaps I should make this more clear in the doc "Guardian of the Veil" or "Adamant Arrow" are not viable Art choices. Under this hack Orders don't provide Rotes or Yantra just for being part of them, they provide access to Arts unique to the order (which in turn give Rotes and Yantra), Metamagical merits like Masques, Egregore and Adamant Hand, and social advantages like Status, Contacts and Allies. Indeed, in older drafts I had an order affiliation section for each Art, but dropped it for the sake of flexibility (e.g. storytellers get to decide which arts cleave to which orders, and which cross boundaries or stand alone). That all said, certain arts clearly nod towards certain Orders. Shepherds of the Flock, your friendly neighborhood thought police, definitely fall under Guardians or the Silver Ladder, and a martial art Art (heh) like the one you've proposed would likely favor either Arrows or Traditionalists.

                                            Originally posted by GibberingEloquence View Post
                                            I read your Mage house rules again. While I do support the elimination of Ruling and Inferior Arcana, I am fond of the idea that you must seek out teachers of a specific Path to become a Master yourself. What else do you think could encourage them to do that?
                                            Experience is something I've been toying around with recently, and not just for Mage. Like you I don't want to lose that Master-Student relationship Mage provides, but I also don't want to 'favor' certain Arcana or utterly hamstring independent Mages (cutting them off from Arts and Rotes is a pretty heavy hit already). What I'm thinking I'll do, going forward is break experience down by Mundane and Arcane. Mundane can be spent on Attributes, Skills, and Merits, stuff everyone can buy. Arcane Experience is spent on Arcana, Rotes & Attainments, things only Mages get. Then, taking a note from Exalted, Gnosis isn't bought, but rises naturally by 1 for every 5 or10 (I'm undecided on the amount) Arcane Experiences you accrue.

                                            As for what a Mentor can provide: Rotes and Attainments, which are both huge. The hacks make Rotes stupendously powerful, and Attainments should be highly enticing to anyone who wants to spice up their magic. Mentors also provide guidance and direction, which will rapidly translate into Experiences Mundane and Arcane. But as for Arcana, just as you cannot forcibly Awaken a Sleeper, you cannot beat enlightenment into a Mage. That's between him and the Supernal.

                                            (Note: I'm planning to do this for other splats as well. Vampires get Kindred Experience, Werewolves get Spirit Experiences, etc. Which nicely solves the somewhat common issue of players dumping all their Experiences into splat traits, or feeling disappointed that they have to 'waste' exp on things like skills).

                                                I like that idea from Exalted of raising the Powerstat for every 5 or 10 Supernatural Experiences.
