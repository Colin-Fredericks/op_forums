    Question on feeding on animals

    04-20-2016, 09:57 PM
    When a kindred feed on am animal and gain the vitae / size of the animal does this kill the animal? I do belive that is what that section is saying but I am unsure if it would kill the animal because it is not directly stated.

    The alternative way I could read it is that beyond the 1 vitae per size, the beast cannot supply further vitae for a set time. Since the general rule of 1 lethal per vitae drained it is possible for the animal to survive if it is not killed by default.

    The main reason I ask is because if this is the proper reading of it you could truly have a vampire that refuses to feed from humans and refuses to kill animals. Or even just buying a cow for an easy source of vitae.

              I think the intent is that draining all of an animal's blood (which would kill them) yields Vitae equal to the animal's size. It's not explicitly stated this way, but it seems odd to me that you'd be able to drain a point worth of Vitae from a size 1 animal and leave it in relatively fine health. There's not much blood in a rat, but yet you can get a whole Vitae our of one without killing it? That would imply that rats have a greater concentration of Vitae to blood than humans, which doesn't seem right. I would say if you want to drain an animal of less than its full Size in Vitae, it should take a proportional amount of lethal damage to its health. (i.e. taking 1 Vitae from a size 4 animal should deal 1/4 of its health in lethal to it).

                                                          Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post

                                                          Yes, I read your post, and I fail to see how it's a counter-argument. Yes, We are talking about very large quantities of fluid, and it would take a long time to get through it all. In what way does that mean it can't be done?
                                                          We've had this discussion in another thread, though with humans. Your earlier statement kind of contradicts this attitude, too. I don't think you have to drink it all to get all the vitae. Volume of blood in a living thing doesn't really matter in my mind.

                                                          With humans, I don't think drinking all Vitae drains all their blood. It doesn't really make any sense, given you can survive having all your vitae being taken.

                                                          With animals, therefore, there are two interpretations that seem to make sense:

                                                          1. It's the same as humans. Which means drinking all their vitae doesn't drink all their blood and they can potentially survive it (though size 1 critters won't give you much time to stabilize them).

                                                          2. It's not the same, and you do have to drink all their blood to get all their vitae. But this means they'll be dead before you finish drinking, and they probably shouldn't survive half-feedings. It makes less sense to me, but it's valid as anything else.
