                                                    Hi! A situation came up during my biweekly game, and I'd like your opinion in the matter:

                                                    One of my players tried to use Memory Hole (Mind 2) on a Sleeper with the intent that he would forget a compromising scene that happened a minute before. The problem is, the spell doesn't list any Withstand trait, yet the intent was clearly to act against the target's will. I ruled it at the moment that he would need an extra Reach and face the target's Resolve as the Withstand rating.

                                                    Later on, I read again the spells section and I realized that there were a lot of spells without any listed Withstand rating that could be used against a target, while the intent in most cases is to provide some sort of advantage to the target of the spell instead. For example, some Veiling spells don't list any Withstand rating, yet they could be used against a target as a special kind of curse, like Supress Life, or Cloak Nimbus. Do you think I made the right call? Should I consider unintended uses of the spell as creative thaumaturgy (and therefore as a completely different spell? Was the extra Reach a bit too much?
