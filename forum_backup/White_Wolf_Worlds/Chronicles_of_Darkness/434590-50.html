    Bear in mind I haven't really looked at Mummy in ages, but my impression is that they've got a few very powerful tricks like triggering an Earthquake, and an automatic loyal cult, but apart from those they're squarely mid tier when compared to others with the same power stat rating.

    Bear in mind though, that Leviathans are intentionally really really powerful. You are playing Cthulhu after all, that's way more powerful than Count Dracula. Something like Heart of the Swarm or the exceptional size rules is beyond any other splat. I'd say even a Mummy shouldn't be able to take on a Leviathan in a fist fight, but they should be able to pull of rituals that affect as many people as Leviathans, and could probably outdo them in creative effects and have an easier time of organising everything because their Cults are more competent.

    So while a powerful Leviathan doesn't need outside help to create an entire swarm of minions, I'd like to see a Mummy with prep time being able to match him by using a ritual to summon an army of it's own; or convert it's entire tomb into a giant battle relic.

        Originally posted by The Kings Raven View Post
        What sort of mechanics do they use to represent Deborah?
        ammm her power set is called Gifts, which are powered by her faith- she must have a belief in her mission and justice, else her powers simply fail. They cost either a Willpower or a meditation in order to activate them. It includes rising her Attributes through prayer, regeneration, and the ability to enhance the elemental qualities of an object through song. She also have a number of Supernatural Merits to represent her powers (like Laying on Hands). She may also be immune to Blood Bonds, Domination, the Embrace and other vampiric mind manipulation. Some of her powers flow through her blood, and so her descendants may carry them in them (if I'm reading it right, she may choose what Gifts her descendants may have). The principle around her mechanics seems to be more or less what we have worked about- a primal power of divinity, channeled directly through her faith and body instead of delicate rituals and invocations. As she isn't meant to be a PC, there aren't many details about how to make Judges in the game- but maybe it is a sign we'll get Book of Judges in the next Dark Eras book.
