            You can even remove the undertones of corruption from absolute power, what happens to a Mage is change on a level that simply removes them from the human experience. It all starts at the moment of Awakening. They're monsters, make no mistake, even if they can fool themselves into thinking that they're not. Heck, one could argue that one of their weaknesses is that they're able to continue to delude themselves that they're people. Even at their lowest Gnosis they experience inhuman drives, and the cosmos confront them with the reality of their own inhumanity. The "ultimate" destination of a Mage is to be a Symbol of the Supernal where they reign over humanity, as a creation of man gone beyond their control. The gods and their scions walk the earth and become legend. That's Mage.

                                    On the mage matter - mages are still human (pre-archmastery), but that doesn't mean that they're the embodiment of humanity either. I think that ignoring that is rather problematic in this discussion.

                                    On the matter of actually being the embodiment of humanity - Do psychics count? Is becoming a psychic the ultimate expression of humanity, or is that just another deviation?

                                            Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post
                                            Actually, the Demon and Angel comparison isn't quite accurate. The Demon isn't an entirely separate entity, it's an Angel who's achieved conscious will. It didn't splinter off from the Angel, but rather the Angel became the Demon.
                                            Actually... let's look in-universe for a moment. What is the reason some see ghosts and the dead person as the same? Because they share memories and an identity. What is the reason some see ghosts and the dead person as different? Because they do not share anything except memories and identity; their personality changes (flipped Vice & Virtue), their priorities in life change (Aspirations), they no longer have a material body (Corpus), they can do stuff that humans simply can't do (Numina, Manifestations, Influences), and they rely on external factors to stay in the mortal world (the need for Anchors).

                                            What connects a demon to its pre-Fall state? Memories born of service, and an identity born out of it. What separates a demon from its angelic existence? Their changed personality (Vice & Virtue are no longer alien to humans, and are now the inverse of that of humans), their changed life priorities (the Mission traded for an Agenda), their body's composition (Cover bodies are human, and Primum cores are quantum engines), abilities that they simply couldn't do as angels (Exploits, Pact-making, etc), and a reliance on external factors for their continued walking-among-men (the need for Covers).

                                            Yes, we had no developer clarify the issue, but the resemblances are there, enough to make comparisons on an out-of-game narrative level.
