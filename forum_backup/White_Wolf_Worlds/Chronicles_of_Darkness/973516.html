              Yeah, what you're looking for mechanically as the equivalent of "flaws" in other systems is Persistent Conditions and Tilts. On the math level, you take some dice penalty as appropriate, and when the flaw comes up during a session as a hindrance, you take a Beat.

              In terms of roleplaying a character with a disability: ask your group what they're comfortable with. One thing to consider is whether you want the condition to be something that's "fixable" with magic of some sort during play. I can imagine that a blind person might not like the thought that they can only participate fully in this fantastic world if they're "fixed" first, for example, as opposed to imagining their own lived experience with the addition of magic and monsters as a sighted person would. Especially with a long-term disability, the character is probably not going to constantly be saying "woe is me, if only I had my legs, alas, how I miss them." It's just a fact of life, and one that's modified and worked around with the tools available.

It was possible to lose limbs as well and regenerate them, but it's expensive and costs Willpower dots from what I heard.

                                          In the current edition, it plays up the regeneration factor much higher and much easier.. To make a long story short, all physical disabilities are temporary conditions as far as the game treats it, just really long lasting temporary conditions. They're still persistent conditions, just... ones that you can remove once you've healed all the damage. Doesn't matter how you got it, if you heal the damage that caused the Tilt, it's restored. Takes a few weeks or months depending on the injury in question though, when you deal with high stamina and high health, the tilts involved tend to require doing a big beating. I know senses get restored mere days after the First change, but I don't recall what the rules are for crippled or lost limbs lost beforehand. I assume they're also covered since you have a rule that says you remove tilts when you have recovered from the damage that caused them.... or there's probably a line of text that clarifies it.

                                                          I made fan Scar Persistent Conditions for 2E Werewolves that mark your wounds lasting from previous battle. Could aplay them when you end the source Condition of wound.

                                                          Scar (Persistant)
                                                          It is honorable thing to get scar from memorable hunt in Uratha culture and most werewolves take pride in leaving marks of the previous battles on they flesh, even if they bodies wants to heal them. This character made sure her previous fatal wound, lost organ or ripped off limb healed only enough to show that it was lost. Character can leave Scar on herself after getting the Tilt in battle that would left major wounds, like Blindness, Arm or Leg Wrack. She writes name of original Tilt and enemy that was source of it. So werewolf that lost one eye in fight with Pure writes this Condition as Scar (One Eye - Pure) on character sheet.
                                                          Effect: Character takes half of modifiers from normal Tilt (rounded down) all the time from half cured effect. When faced with enemy that made her the Scar, character get's +1 bonus to all action against this particular prey, fueled by the memories of her hunt that left he Scar on her.
                                                          Resolution: Character spend Willpower dot and let her regeneration abilities to heal her scar.
                                                          Beat: Character encounters a limitation or difficulty that inconveniences her, streaming from the Scar. Your character speaks or listen to the story of how she got her Scar.
