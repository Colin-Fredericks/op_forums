                Finally the event you have been waiting for, the history of Poseidon International! " Cue fanfare."
                "Note, the reason this history is in first-person is because this history is being told from the point-of view of a Employee Instruction tape".
                Hello new employee, my name is Dr. Ernst Egarsson, CEO and current chairman of Poseidon Labs International. Let me be the first one to welcome you to your new job in Poseidon and I, along with the rest of the board hope you have a long, happy career with us. The purpose of this tape is to explain the history of our proud corporation. This tape will only consume a little amount of time and when this tape is over you may start your workday.

                Our proud corporation had it's start in 1885 with a more humble origin.It all started with a club under the name " The Oceanography Enthusiasts Club" which was founded by amateur Norwegian oceanographer Roy Petterson. This club was founded by Roy for the purpose of meeting and discussing developments in the world of oceanography with fellow enthusiasts. Even though most clubs back then only admitted the social elite Roy's welcomed in anyone who had in interest in the Ocean or Oceanography. Within 5 years of finding this marvelous club, membership soared from 5 to 50 members and was recognized as a scientific organization by the Swedish government.

                Tragically, Roy died in 1895 while on a expedition with fellow oceanographer, Sir Duncan Smith. The official cause of death was apparently a shipwreck which killed all hands on-board. Unfortunately, Roy's death destabilized the club and half of it's members left upon hearing this bad news. The club's new leader, Sven Lagersson, was extremely unpopular with the club due to his heavy drinking and utter disregard for club rules. In 1900, the club formerly dissolved after Sven died in a bar fight. The final meeting was held on New Year's Eve 1899, what the good men of the club did not know was that among the visitors to the club's final meeting was a youth who would one day form the Modern-day Poseidon Corporation. His name was Daniel Herre.

                Good old Daniel Herre, father of our corporation, was avid an visitor to the club and was upset about it's dissolution. Daniel then started his own study of the ocean with the help of Roy's personal diary, which was published due to a specification in his will that wished for that to happen after he died. As he read through Roy's diary, Daniel became aware of how beneficial the sea could be for humanity. So with a loan from his rich cousin, Daniel went out and started Poseidon's Bounty, the grandfather of our corporation. Unfortunately, technology in the 1900s was not up to snuff with our technology so Poseidon's Bounty mostly sold fish. In 1920, Daniel made a discovery that changed the face of his company. He was supervising the catching of a new bounty of fish when he saw that the net accidentally caught some lose seaweed. Taking this seaweed home with him, he absent-mindidly placed it in a potted plant. When he woke up the next morning, he found that the seaweed had rotted and as a result, it made a great fertilizer for the plant. This was the birth of our first product " Neptune's Fertilizer" one of the first fertilizers made of seaweed.

                Wanting to repeat his success with the seaweed. Daniel spent the next 15 years scouring the ocean looking to make potentially useful products. The products varied from cleaning powder made from crushed-up sea shells all the way to sandpaper made of shark-skin. In 1940, The Nazi's invaded Norway and Daniel was forced to take his company's fortune and flee to America, bankrupting Poseidon's Bounty in the process. After the war, Daniel met and befriended Dr. Emil Grund, a former German scientist who was pardoned from being tried in the Nuremberg Trials due to the fact his experiments never killed anyone. The two realized that they both shared an interest in the ocean and went into business together forming the modern-day Poseidon Labs International.

                During our 61 years in business, we at Poseidon labs have made many discovery's from the ocean which benefited mankind. Of course we still sell " Neptune's Fertilizer" and some of the other products Poseidon's Bounty sold. But, are scientists are making new discovery's everyday and have made wonderful products which have definitely helped mankind. We have two experiments for which you are qualified for. "Project Graft", the study of sea sponges and their resistance to cancer and "Project Shaker", an attempt to find a process that makes Desalination of water possible. Of course we have higher-level experiments but they are for high-level staff and are completely restricted for new employees. And, if i hear about you or any other new employee trying to get into the project or even accessing files about it, i will personally make sure that you are terminated and that you will never work in this business again. I thank you for choosing a career with Poseidon International, now good luck and remember our slogan." The best bounty is Neptune's Bounty."

                                                    Originally posted by SaulottheGentle View Post
                                                    I see...if I may ask, what book covers that? I'm lacking the newest corebook for the CoD and working mostly from the GMC book.
                                                    Chronicles of Darkness (the second edition) has a few new systems alongside Social Maneuvering that had been introduced in GMC. In fact, the second half of the book is the GMC again with a few layout tweaks.

                                                    The main two for consideration are the Investigative and Chase rules. These are semi-optional for Storyteller preference but there are Merits that work with these new systems, Social Maneuvering and Investigative and Chase. They roughly equate to Social, Mental and Physical categories but their primary use is to given more storytelling tools to the players.

                                                    Investigation
                                                    For the Investigative system (which is just titled Investigation, I use the other word for distinction from the skill) it can be a slow burn type of system, used for when there is a case or mystery of some kind that the players are trying to solve can span most of a chronicle. It's best for the Storyteller to not over plan a mystery to leave room for player input. Based on the group's intent a set threshold of Clues are required to complete the investigation.

                                                    ​To uncover a Clue, the player specifies a specific dice pool to discover the clue. These can be of different Attributes, Skills, Merits or special abilities, although each time the same skill is used it confers a cumulative penalty. Each Clue has a number of Elements, disposable die bonuses that can be used on other actions related to the investigation. At default a Clue has 1 Element and gains more from certain criteria:
                                                    • An Exceptional Success when uncovering the Clue
                                                    • The player had a Specialty pertaining to the Clue
                                                    • The player had 4+ dots in the Skill uncovering the Clue
                                                    • Merits that react with Clues
                                                    Clues can also get tagged, which impacts how their Elements are used. Even with the Elements have been used the Clue remains for tracking progress of the investigative effort. To use Elements a character draws from a single Clue, or from a number of Clues equal to their Investigation skill if they have it. The most common end goal, to uncover the truth, is automatic if the players have found the requisite number of Clues and have an equal number of Elements.


                                                    Given that, I may rephrase Spectral Vision like this:
                                                    The Siren has enhanced vision for both infrared and ultraviolet spectrums, being able to see residues and evidence not visible to the naked eye. Their failures to uncover Clues never get the Incomplete tag, and their successfully uncovered Clues gain an additional element.
                                                    However, the Alteration should still have another more commonly used benefit. I like the idea of being able to see more details although a proper mechanic is lacking, since we already had requisite skill boost Alterations.

                                                    Chase
                                                    Chases refer to scenes where the characters quickly need to reach a goal, such a pursuing an opponent, although it can also represent 'chasing' after someone's slippery logic in a social setting.

                                                    At the start, each side is set with a target number they need to accomplish their goal, with various factors like time and other stat differences. The target number indicates how many successful actions they need to reach their goal, not the number of successes resulting from the roll. What rolls are needed are set by whoever has the Edge.

                                                    The Edge indicates which side has the most advantage. The side with the advantage indicates what obstacle they present and thus what dice roll is needed. Success and failure mark off their target numbers, but regardless of results each side needs to estimate their result. If you rolled the number of successes you guessed you can claim the Edge. Using a different dice pool then that set by the Edge confers a cumulative penalty.

                                                    Since there's already several merits pertaining to the Edge, all tied to physical implementation, it would likely be unessecary to bring that into Alterations.


                                                    I like how you worded that. For the most part, the reason I set Malformations to have disadvantages was because that seemed like the theme for the "Dark side" powers. Power at a price and all that. But just how you said that makes me think for once of how it'd be explained in game. Echidna is trying to make the perfect race. A perfect race would have no need to evolve anymore, which is what you said in how evolutionary advantages being lost. Thanks for inspiring that. :3
                                                    Consider that a lucky choice of terminology.


                                                    If it's fine to bring up an idea for Siren, I've been thinking on something. As it stands, I wanted to implement some stuff about the connection of Sirens between themselves and the Song. So far, the echoes of these ideas have resulted in the idea of the Song of the School and Trace (which was more of an expansion of the Psychometry Merit)...and yet these don't feel like they have enough of a connection to Sirens or the Song. Right now, I'm sort of thinking of melding the Song of the School and Trace to being automatic abilities Sirens have access to, making something new that Sirens can make use of out of the old mechanics.

                                                    The start of my ideas is that they would still have Trace like abilities, seeing into the past and whatnot, but with Verses the Siren may affect the past as if she used the verse there at that time. Of course, the idea of "Overtracing" still exists, and some events are nearly impossible to mess with. This is fitting in more of the original symbology of how Sirens could somehow deal with Time. An in game perspective is that the Song has messed with Time that it's managed to be heard in the past, before the Deluge has happened.

                                                    I also imagine Sirens being able to use the echoes of the Song within them, aka their Akashic Record, to be able to pinpoint other Sirens or beings with souls. An automatic ability Sirens would have is the ability to "send" an echo back, sort of like "I heard your message" and stuff. Perhaps the Currents have made special versions of this "Call" that allows them to speak to each other like how the US used the Navajo Native American Language for secret messages in WW2. This could even be wrapped up as different dialects of the Bel Canto Language. :3

                                                    So far, these are just ideas, but what do you guys think of them? The basic ideas I have seem to have a theme of post-cognition, recording, replaying and editing so far...
                                                    Just making sure, if the Sirens can work their Verses into the events they Trace, they aren't actually changing the past? Or we introduce Time Hollow and Ghost Trick levels of shenanigans to the sense of continuity. Unless the Sirens are merely able to 'see' how things could have gone differently by working their influences. And for the particularly insidious, they could rework and event (Overtrace it so much, if you will) that it obscures the true reality of the event if others try to Trace it.

                                                    The communicative aspect sounds like the Siren could leave her commentary on the event for other Sirens to uncover when they Trace.

                                                    Otherwise, I thought that the Deluge Opus handled Trace enhancement with its figurative implementation.

                                                        Anyways, here's my ideas so far on revamping Trace. It has five factors: Time, Range, Clarity, Audience and Verse. When a Siren invokes Trace, they must spend 1 Pneuma and a point of Willpower (if they put any suxx into Verse, it becomes a point of permanent Willpower) and roll Akashic Record + Resolve to see how many suxx they get, then they choose how many suxx they spend on each of the mechanics. The higher suxx, the higher strength the Siren may put into a mechanic. If the Siren uses Verse, then she must pay the Verses cost in the subsequent turn.

                                                        Time allows a Siren to see forwards and backwards through time, seeing past or probable future events. The more suxx put into Time, the farther forwards or backwards she may see. Seeing forwards shows the most likely occurrance, but it's easily disrupted by things such as controlling fate or simple dumb luck. Since Time doesn't take into account for this, Sirens tend to see looking forwards with Trace as unreliable at best.

                                                        Range allows a Siren to choose how far her Trace can pull, allowing her to see elsewhere and even exert force with more suxx invested. She may even speak telepathically to people with this. With no suxx, the Trace can only affect the Siren herself, and with one suxx, she may use the Trace on things within her line of sight. With more suxx invested to this, the Siren may communicate to those at a greater distance. With investment to Time as well, a Siren can look into the past or future of places or people far away from her.

                                                        Clarity allows the Trace to have greater definition when Tracing things. She may read into the composition of objects and see physical and mental traits of people. With investment to Time, the Siren may get her defined answers instead of seeing things through a set timeframe. With Investment to Range, the Siren may speak more than a simple phrase and add more words to her telepathic messages.

                                                        Audience allows the Trace to affect a group of people outside of the siren, and by itself it allows a Siren to try to "listen" into other active traces. With more suxx put into this, the group of people can grow very large. With investment to Time, the Siren shares a Time Trace's Vision with the Audience. With investment to Range, the Siren may hold a group conversation with people. With Investment to Clarity, a Siren may pre-record a message that she wants any specific audience members to hear and leaving others out.

                                                        Verse is simply what allows a Siren to wield a Verse during her Tracing. The amount of Suxx put into this equals the dot level of the Conclusion the Siren may use through the Trace. With Investment to Time, a Siren may use Verses in the past. With Investment to Range, a Siren may use Verses at the places she sees, as if she were actually there (and thus negating the Amour modifier normally required to affect places out of sensory vision). Clarity and Audience have no effect with this.

                                                        There's also the idea of Sirens being able to Trace other Sirens, but due to Song's nature (and thus a Siren's as well) of achronality, if the Siren doesn't wish to be traced, she may reflexively roll Akashic Record + Composure to contest the use of the trace. The Resisting Siren's suxx is subtracted from the Tracing Sirens suxx that they may spend, and if the Resisting Siren's suxx is higher, then she completely blocks the Trace from occurring.

                                                        Any thoughts on this?
