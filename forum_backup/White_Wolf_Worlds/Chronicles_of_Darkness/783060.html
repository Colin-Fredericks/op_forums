                                              12-12-2015, 01:27 PM
                                              Following the guidelines in Equinox Road, almost every Fae has 1-5 additional manifestations. They can be killed individually, but doing so tends to be immensely difficult and dangerous. Granted, Autumn Nightmares has a selection of True Fae that can likely be beaten by a moderately experienced motley, so how tough it is really depends on what source you're using.

                                              For the most common and interactive form that mortals are likely to encounter in the mortal world, the Actor form, they're just especially powerful monsters and can theoretically be killed in combat. Killing an actor seriously hurts the True Fae it belongs to, but will not kill the Fae itself unless all its other manifestations have been destroyed as well. There are example characters, including Grandfather Thunder, who are known or at least rumored to have killed a Keeper.

                                                      The True Fae are composed of a Name and a variable number of Titles. Titles can be spun into various forms: Actors, Props, Wisps and Realms. Actors are the ones that look like people and have normal stats. High, but normal. You can kill an Actor the same way you'd kill anything else. Props are objects; they can do one thing perfectly and irresistibly, and can only be destroyed by one particular means. Expose them to that one individual weakness, they just... cease. Wisps are basically mobs. They can be killed like any mob. Realms are buildings and landscapes. Killing them is very tricky. Finally, if you destroy all the Fae's Titles, you can kill the Name, which always manifests as an Actor. Fae can steal Titles from one another, but I don't think changelings can do that. You can face a fae's Name, even fight it, while it still have Titles available, but you *cannot* kill it or even hurt it without destroying the Titles first. So if one of the Gentry takes the form of an Arabian Nights king, the king could be its Name, the palace a Realm, the slave girls a pack of Wisps, his crown a Prop, and his wazir an Actor. Even if you managed to kill the wazir and the slaves and topple the palace, you'd still have to find the crown's sole weakness before you could harm the king. Perhaps you need to immerse it in liquid darkness, or expose it to the light of the moon at midday (both of these are quite possible: matte black paint would qualify as liquid darkness and the moon shines at midday during fall and winter). That's important: a prop's weakness is always discoverable and always possible, and often fairly simple once learned. No "place it between the sea and the strand" sort of thing.
