    Okay, here are the factions (called Postings) of the Cold Guard. For the Clerks, their thing is that they rely a bit much on precedence and what the Guard already knows to make judgements-good for stability, not so good for speedy decisions or adapting:

    Bondsmen: To inter a prisoner, they must be captured. This is the duty of the Bondsmen, the most traditional hunters of the Cold Guard. In truth, their name is something of a relic to a time before the Queens betrayed them; in the days of the Kingdom, the Bondsmen gave up the safety of civilization out of loyalty to their Nobles, and so were entrusted as their most devoted servants. Now the name is something of a pun-they are both the ones who forge bonds, and when the other Postings fail in keeping their prisoners, it is they who retrieve the escapees. This duty is perhaps the most dangerous, with great bounties and acclaim being offered to those who succeed. After all, the escapees know what's coming for them. Specialty: Athletics (Foot Chase).

    Overseers: The Tenebrous Gaol needs a gaol, it's kind of hinted at by the nickname. And wherever there is a Malebolge, there are guards to keep it. The Overseers are those guards, both from prisoners escaping and from fools who would see them set free. While the Guard isn't numerous enough to post an Overseer to every site where they keep a monster or Dark place sealed, they maintain regular patrols and are the designers of the wards by which the Dark is kept in. In many ways, the Overseers are also the research wing, as it is from their cells that new purified distillations of Dark energies are derived; in seeking lore of the Dark and Malebranches, one could do worse than peruse the writings of the Overseers. Specialty: Occult (Darkness).

    Clerks: Perhaps once, they were attached to an actual justice system, but that was part of the damage the Betrayal did to the fabric of the world. Today, the Cold Guard's Clerks are a combination scribe and psychologist, serving as both the collectors of information needed to pursue their mission, and monitors of their fellows' well-being as part of a broad interpretation of their writ to monitor the internal affairs of the other Postings. It is by the Clerks that the Guard keeps track of its many Malebolges and ensures they are not forgotten in the confusion and secrecy, and it is they who judge whether an edge case is deserving of being locked away in the pits. It's also they alone who possess the right of pardon-if a monster is judged to have sufficiently reformed, then the Clerks have the privilege to order a precautionary release for the sake of neutralizing a greater evil. Specialty: Academics (Cold Guard Convict Lore)

    [Art: A grave-looking man of indeterminate ethnicity is walking by a cell in a prison guard uniform, hitting the bars with a nightstick. Tentacles and claws are reaching out towards him.]

                                    Also, completed the Enemy section for the People's Guard with a help from someone. An opinion, please? Here it is:


                                    The hostility of the People’s Guard against Princesses surprises all but the most militant Hunter organizations.. Most Compacts and Conspiracies don’t necessarily trust or work with Princesses, but they at least feel uncomfortable openly using lethal force against them. But the Egalitarians have nothing but disdain for these so-called Nobles, and do not think twice before using lethal force against them. To them, each kind of Noble represent a facet of the old aristocracy they rose to fight.
                                    The Weeping are elitism and oppression, trying to dominate the people and drain their will to live for their own end.The Selfish are vanity and arrogance, convinced of their own superiority from birth. The Wrathful are the violent knights from medieval times, the ones who go on pointless crusades against a random enemy as an excuse to slaughter innocents in their blood thirst.
                                    As for the younger ones, these so-called “good” Nobles? They may consider themselves a well-meaning aristocracy, but they still consider themselves aristocracy, taught by detached and presumptuous “Queens”, and they have the power and the privilege to impose their views of a better world on the people. The People’s Guard will never allow humanity to submit to such would-be monarchs.
                                    Of course, this struggle against oppression doesn’t limit itself to Nobles, though they are by far the primary target. The People’s Guard has no particular qualm against being supernatural, but their hatred burns for anyone who sees themselves as meant to rule over those they see as beneath them. If magic won’t keep the People’s Guard from warring against the Nobility, neither will it prevent the People’s Guard from striking at Vampires, Beasts, or any others who have turned into tyrants.
                                    Needless to say, you don’t stay in the Vigil that long without learning about the creatures of the Darkness, especially when you focus on fighting Princesses. The Egalitarians have a very specific modus operanti when it comes to these; they fight Spawns out of necessity, but usually restrain from immediately destroying Nests, for they realized such places acted as perfect honey traps for the Hopeful. As such, they typically leave agents patrolling around the Nests, taking down any Noble coming to deal with the Nest; once they stop coming, the Guard destroy their trap and move on.

                                        (finally a moment to myself. Have the beginnings of the Enemy section!)

                                        The Enemy

                                        Let's be clear: The Guard does not hate the target it was meant to contain.

                                        That may sound odd, but let's reiterate: the Cold Guard does not hate the Dark. Fear it, despise its effects, and loathe the fools who drink of its unfiltered power and so become monsters...but they don't hate the Dark itself. Hating the Dark is like hating death; pointless, and often leads one down the path that ends with one becoming its embodiment. Fear the Dark, but recognize it is a part of nature, one that seeks to imbalance nature and create the unnatural by its very existence, but a "natural" humanity would still be a desperate prey animal, not even organized enough to form proper tribes of hunter-gatherers; no tools, no fire, no hope. In practice, this byline isn't as followed as strictly as it is said to be (most of the Tenebrous Gaol came to their grim profession out of lethal encounters with the Dark), but it informs everything they do. They do not hunt, they quarantine. They do not kill, they capture. They do not purge, they filter. In that last case, frequently the difference is purely semantic, but it all ties into their cosmological view of the world. To them, the Dark is a force of chaos and change, frequently entropic, but also the ultimate source of "low magic" (low as in lowly, aka humble); it asks nothing, it repays what you sacrifice, and it leads to the weak becoming the strong. Of course, in its excessive state is seeks to devour that which is associated with the Light-kindness, order, happiness, but in its balanced state it accepts less cruel sacrifices than your soul. The gifts it gives are also less vicious (and frequently less directly powerful-you get what you pay for) in balance. Malebranches aren't quite born from balance, and in time, the Cold Guard accepts, the cancerous part of the Dark will inevitably devour their lives-but that is the point. A successful initiation and Decathexis rite is all about sacrificing the darkest potential of the Empty Court and the belief you, personally, will live forever to the Dark, taming the corruption in your soul and making its hungers a tool of virtue. From the moment she first manifests the Wicked Claws, a Cold Guard has in some sense won; she will never be consumed in total by the Dark and made a body of the Court or one of their Viziers (the same cannot be said of her body, but her soul is safe).

                                        The same cannot be said of the hypocrites that made true balance nigh-impossible and stole the Light. The Cold Guard hates the Nobility, and for all of their recorded history they keep on accumulating new justifications for their vitriol.

                                        (Not nearly complete yet).
