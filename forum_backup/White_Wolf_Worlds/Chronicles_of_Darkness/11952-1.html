                        76) Janos Kardassian is a nice, unassuming man. Unemployed, since recently been laid off. Divorced. Two kids. Werewolf senses would likely notice that he has a little drinking problem; other than that, there is nothing extraordinary about him. In the Flesh, at least.

                        In the Shadow, Janos Kardassian is constantly followed around by a rather huge host of shadowy entities. They read his thoughts. They follow his subconscious's wishes. One of the spirits has recently burned down his former boss's apartment. The spirits are ready and prepared to do anything and everything Janos wants.

                        There is no reason and no explanation. There is no trace of wolf blood in Jano's veins, and he has never knowingly experienced anything remotely supernatural himself.

                                78) (For a Pure chronicle) The city has been a stronghold for the Anshega for the last twelve years, after the three tribes united under the (temporary) leadership of a clever Fire-Touched alpha and very nearly exterminated the Urdaga. But recently, relations between the tribes have become strained, and every indication is that things will only get worse. On the increasingly-rare occasion that the packs have a meeting, old grudges keep coming up without resolution and new grudges appear almost every other week.

                                Your pack is the only one with members from each of the three tribes. You've staked out a territory on the outskirts, where you can watch for activity from Luna's minions. You do this because no one else will. One day, your totem informs you that a Silver Crusade is coming--the Forsaken are preparing to invade and re-conquer the city, and they have the numbers, the weapons and the allies to do this. The Pure have little hope of victory. Your totem goes on to say that you should be looking out for each other now, putting your pack first, and that survival may require you to consider...new ideas.

                                        71) The Refugium is a place that isn't - that is, a location in the Shadow that doesn't have an equivalent in the Flesh. Only very few werewolves know about its existence and how to reach it, and it should take long and hard quests through the deepest reaches of the spirit wilds to find it on one's own.

                                        To werewolf senses, the Refugium appears to be Pangaea resurrected. There is plenty of prey to be hunted, and the Uratha have the opportunity to rule as kings over lesser creatures and animals. The entire place is flowing with Essence, and werewolves should get all kinds of system advantages while in the Refugium, like faster regeneration, more powerful forms and extra gift dice. Werewolves should feel welcome.

                                        So welcome, in fact, that they might never want to leave. The Refugium is, to all intents and purposes, what's left of Pangaea - a lost paradise for the People, a heaven for predators.

                                        Only the most powerful rites will reveal that this place is actually weakly tainted by the essence of Beliar, Maeljin of Sloth.

                                        The PCs should feel tempted to stay in Refugium forever. This place offers respite to the horrors of the World of Darkness. There are no Pure, no Hosts, no creatures that hate them because of what they are. There's no suffering, and it feels good to be in a place that accepts the Uratha.

                                        In the end, the PCs are going to have to decide between their duties as Forsaken werewolves of the Tribes of the Moon, keeping their people and territories safe, or respite from being Forsaken in this heaven specifically designed for them.

                                        Once you leave the Refugium, there is no turning back. You've forsaken paradise in favor of the World of Darkness, and your decision must be final.
