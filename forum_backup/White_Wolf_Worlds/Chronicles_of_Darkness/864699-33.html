                                Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post
                                Can Supernaturals do basic exorcisms and abjurations and other mortal magic and such using their Integrity-equivalent
                                Summoning, Exorcism, Binding and Warding are just rituals using implements relevant to the ephemeral entity. Nothing suggests it has anything to do with Integrity or the soul so it should possible for all supers to do.
                                Abjuration, on the other hand, is described as using the character's soul and is directly modified by Integrity, so those specific rules only applies to those with an Integrity rating. Whether or not supernaturals can carry out an Abjuration is left unstated since they don't have any rules for it.
