                So, one of the things I've thought about was the Heiromagus of the Guardians. Eventually, I came to the conclusion that that the mythical messiah of magic must be a symbol- another objective that Magic itself is trying to accomplish. Once the symbol of Atlantis was destroyed, Magic made a symbol of hope- a path through which the symbol might be restored. But just as there were many failed "Atlantean Empires", there were also many false Heiromagi. Once Magic would successfully be able to manifest that symbol in someone, it would be the first milestone in restoring itself to its original curse. Until then, it would just have to try again and again.

                And finally- the Underworld!

                In order to understand the Underworld, one must understand Death. I don't just speak about the phenomena, but about the entity- the ancient Lemurian who cursed all humanity. The truth is that the Underworld has predated death- it was an old realm, older than even the Lemurians, the Exrachs, Gaea or any other entity (with the exception of God.. perhaps). It was the eternal "waiting room"- or more exactly, a recycle bin. Nothing was meant to "die"- only to go there, wait some time and return all new and shiny. During that time, Inferno's flames still didn't burned at the bottom of the Great Below, and the Supernal was way outside of man's reach. There was no Hell nor Heaven for the mortal souls- only endless cycles of death and rebirth, and it worked perfectly.

                The first ones to fuck it up were the Exrachs.

                When they summoned the Abyss, broke Atlantis and conquered the Supernal, many of the Old Gods have died. Some died fighting. Others were executed for symbolizing things the Exrachs didn't liked. While many of those symbols has fallen to the Abyss, with it being the only power able to destroy symbols, the Kings of the Lie didn't had the time to properly dispose of all of the Old Gods- and so, they threw them the furthest they could- into the pits of the Underworld. Those broken symbols became what most people know as the Chthonians- dead things which never lived. Those creatures has ventured deeper and deeper, searching for a sanctuary from the wrath of the "New Gods"- until they eventually found some warmth for they cold bones. The Exrachs brought the Abyss to the world. The Old Gods found Inferno.

                While the opening to the Lower Depths was the first step for changing the nature of the Underworld, it wasn't the last.While infernal beings mad managed to enter the world and steal souls, most of the souls kept going up and down, maintaining the cycle. That is, until Lemuria has fallen by the hands of mankind, and then used their powerful magic to change reality itself. While each of the Lemurains has done quite a damage, Death did the most- he was a powerful necromancer, and his curse was to lock the Gates of Death- but only from one side. Souls could get in, but not to get out. That curse was the way death was defined, and the words were written on a tablet which was hidden in the depth of the Underworld. To protect it, a great guardian was created- a monstrous three headed hound named Kerebros, or as it is more modernly known, Cerberus. Those were the first Old Laws to be written, and that was the first Dead Dominion to be formed.

                Eventually, the curse had event greater effect then Death himself imagined- for by disrupting the cycle of souls, some souls had managed to linger behind, withholding their fall to the Below. Those were the first ghosts to take form- but still, they didn't formed in the Twilight. They were too distant to effect the world, and could only manifest as weak visages and echos. In an addition, the curse of Death pulled them like gravity, forcing them to leave the world no more than a year and a day since their death. Through time, many other things had fallen to the Underworld- forgotten concepts, shattered dreams, fallen stories. While nations were lost in the depths, and the once dry underworld has started to flow with rivers, made from the tears of the dead. While the Chthonians devoured those who wondered too deep and the demons of Inferno stole those who weren't careful enough, some of the oldest souls had managed to find the old tablet, pass its guardian and redefine death according to their own view, establishing their own Dominions and Laws. Those were the first Deathlords in the world, and with each Deathlord who rose another river has started to flow. Some of the dead, however, were too old but not powerful enough, and instead were crushed into themselves, creating mini black holes of death. Eventually, those black holes were united and fell to the depths of existence, even below the Lower Depths, creating a place were even time and space died and which erased every memory of what that was consumed by it- Oblivion.

                While the underworld has turned from a waiting room into an half a museum- half junkyard, the Fallen World managed to maintain itself quite good. While most of the empires and nations which existed during that time had feared the dead, one has ruled them- it was one of the city states which ruled the land who would become Sumer. That land was different than the other city states around it. It wasn't ruled by a man, but by a woman. Its leader didn't belonged to one of the Wolf Tribes, but a powerful magician who was respected by the wolf people as having the "soul of a beast". Finally, that land didn't talked the language of the spirits, and instead worked to rule the Dead. That land was Kigal, and its lady was Ereshkigal.

                And that was the finale stage at shaping the Underworld.

                Ereshkigal ruled the Gates of Death by the use of a powerful artifact called the Throne of Mists. The throne, made out of the bones of a Chthonian killed by Lady herself, has allowed her to force the Avernian Gates open and let her force the dead to her will. There was one condition- that only the ruler of the living may rule the dead. That meant that only the recognized leader of the city state may use the artifact, else Irkalla's gates would be opened uncontrollably. While for many years Ereshkigal ruled undisturbed, it has all changed once her sister- a sorceress named Inanna, came to a visit. Unlike Ereshkigal, who was cold and brutal, Inanna was whimsy and unexpected. Ereshkigal, knowing that her sister wasn't to be trusted, had to let her enter her city because of the old family traditions, but she had her to leave all of her magic tools behind. As it was expected, the two fought when they met in the palace- until Inanna, who knew nothing about the power of the Throne, had decided to show her displeasure and sit on the chair.

                And then all Hell broke loose.

                    Once she sat on the artifact, all of the Avernian Gates in Kigal and around it were opened, allowing hundreds of ghosts to swarm the land. Ereshkigal had to use all of her power to maintain her rule, and with her anger has sent her foolish sister to be executed by hanging. While her sister begged for a second chance- Ereshkigal refused, not even coming to her sister's execution. While that was supposed to be the end, it was only the beginning- for just has Inanna was between life and death, a powerful being which escaped thanks to her efforts offered its help- it had two faces, and named itself as both Gala-tur and Kur-jara. The being, who was more then a ghost yet less then a Deathlord, offered the dying sorceress a second chance- and she agreed. She didn't knew (although most chances that even if she did it wouldn't have changed anything about her actions), but one of the Deathlords who changed death had added a loophole- that once one of the dead would be one with the living, the year and a day time limit would be terminated. By agreeing to share a body with the creature, Inanna became the first of the Bound, the being became the first geist- and so, the Law was broken.

                    And with it, Kigal has fallen.

                    The Gates were opened once again- but this time, they didn't closed. The surprised and weak Ereshkigal, who still didn't replenished her power after the previous incident couldn't do much- she was only able to stabilize her kingdom as her sister escaped and the angry dead had made the realm which ruled them for so many years to sink to the bottom of the Underworld. Thanks to the loophole, now the dead could walk side by side with the living, hidden from all while existing in the Deathly Frequency of the Twilight. Now, Geists could offer second chances to the dying, giving them the Keys for the Gates of the Underworld. And those people, those Sin Eaters, could take the pieces the reality through away and recreate the world in the way they liked it- the realm of broken symbols was them to rule, giving the Bound accesses to unlimited potential.

                    Meanwhile, in the depths of the Underworld, Ereshkligal survived. Filled with anger, she cursed her sister and used her magic to subdue Kerebros. She redefined Death again, this time making it into cold and merciless as her. She became a deathlord, and helped the survivors to find their way out of the Below using her knowledge in the secret ways of the Underworld. Those people still exist, and still serve her in the form of an old, almost dead conspiracy of hunters and exorcists.

                    It was also during that time that the beings named as the Five Horseman rose in the underworld. Those powerful ghosts have achieved a state equal, if not greater, then the Deathlords themselves. Unlike the Deathlords, they weren't bound to their Dominion, but rose above it, gathering armies of the dead under their flag and riding through the dark caves in a war of conquest. They became the manifestation of Death itself. Unbound by Laws or definition, they were originally only four- for once, Death always had a reason. With the rise with the belief in chance and misfortune, the number of deaths which weren't under the dominions of the 4 Horseman grew, allowing one of the Deathlords to ascend as the Grey Horseman.

                    While most of the times the societies of the Bound were under a low profile, from time to time they managed to rose to greatness. One of those times was in Egypt- when two rival krewes used the people's faith in the Iremite mythologies to gain power and connect themselves to an underworld no one of them ever visited. Eventually, the two krewes had merged together, and from the combinations of Duat's old spells and the Underworld's chaotic energies many great and terrible creations were made. Through the existence of old Egypt the krewes and the Arisen had both worked together and against each other in a weird alliance.

                    So, let's talk about the Underworld itself- as it was said before, it is both a junkyard and a museum. All pieces of human histories are thrown into there, and the Authochtonous Depths are even more of a junkyard than the rest of the realm. It isn't a reflection, but literal pieces of time which are no longer actual to, well, anything. The more relevant something is, the less strongly it shows up in the Depths. This is where your lost socks and keys go to- a place which mixes up everything to create both wonders and horrors never imagined by man. While the rivers may be described as the "tears of the dead", they are more exactly made out of souls- or what is left of those who don't manage to move on after thousands of years in the Great Below. The Ferryman are the manifestations of those thousands of souls, which never give anything for free. The Kereboi are also manifestations- but not of souls. Instead, they are the manifestations of the Old Laws themselves. The complicated mythologies of the Bound are created by using the right "junk" and piecing everything together. Abmortals are made by the same loopholes which created the Sin Eaters- making the Bound into a specific, extremely powerful and accepted type of Abmortals.

                    Finally, all rivers flow to the Ocean of Fragments. Its waters are made from endless souls, and at its bottom is found the first tablet of Death. Its water swirl around the Oblivion, which grants it is "erasing" abilities. Everything the Ocean takes flows right to the black hole, where it is destroyed for all eternity. However, below the fourth Depth is not Oblivion- it lies even under it, below the Lower Depths themselves, creating a huge waterspout which penetrates even the flames of Inferno. The blackness is actually the door the the realm of the original Death- and those who dare to go there meet face to face with the Kerebros. Once a person goes even deeper, he'll reach the burning flames of Inferno, creating what most people think about when they hear about "Hell". Also, Psychopomps are the original denizens of the Underworld, who still try to guide the souls outside of the Below.

                        So. I've thought about it, and I've noticed that unlike the other realms in my cosmology, the Underworld is kinda,, quite. There are no cosmological maneuvers or godly manipulations in that realm, and I'm not sure if I like it. The closest I have is that the five Horseman gather the armies of the dead in order to become more influential in the Underworld (that is, each tries to become the leading cause of death). I'm not sure that I like it that way- I mean, what I like is complicated cosmologies for no reason and great factions which manipulate reality. So, here are few changes- or more exactly, re-interpretations- of the Underworld.

                        First, you have the Chthonians. Those dead Gods are barely united, with each being a power by its own. Some demand worship from poor souls. Others just devour them. While few may seek for a way to "fix" themselves, most don't care about it. They have lost their war, and now they are just broken, shells of their former self. They have invited the Lower Depths to the world out of spite, and they still have an alliance with the forces of Inferno and other "close" Lower Depths. Some of the more motivated dead gods seek to break the Supernal itself, turning the whole world into an Underworld. The most, however, just don't care.

                        The second faction is the Deathlords, all united under the banner of the Five Horsemen. One of the more colorful groups in the celestial game, each Deathlord had re-defined death by its "ascension". They all have started as human souls, who slowly became something more then they once were- into geists, the archetypes of their own death. By collecting the pieces they find relevant in the Underworld, They expand their own dominion and understanding of what Death is, until they come to the Ocean of Fragments. There, they finally leave behind any semblance who who they once were, becoming the lies they told themselves they were. Those who manage to pass through the fourth Depth and pass the original Kerebros can change the Laws of Death to become Deathlords.

                        The Kereboi are the third faction. While those creatures are the manifestation of the Old Laws which were written by the local Deathlord, they actually aren't bound to their will. They are the creations of the curse of Death, and the byproducts of the Deathlord's ascension. The Deathlords are, after all, beings of chaos and change, who mutate the Great Below to fit to their own view. The Kereboi, however, force the Laws which were made by them- even if it is against the will of their "master". making it that even the Deathlord can't break its own Law. It may change them, of course, or remove them entirely- by as long as they are valid, the Kereboi makes sure that everything would work as written. That is why the Horsemen are seen which such a respect- they have freed themselves from their own Laws, becoming a force free forever from the curse of Death.

                        The fourth group is the Ferrymen. The manifestation of countless of souls in the form of the rivers, they lead the dead toward the Ocean for they'll all lose their "self" in a form of transcendence. You may call them the "agents of Oblivion", but they prefer to use the Greek name, Lethe. They don't seek to end the world, but to lose their identity and become one- a collective potential of all of mankind. The waters of the Ocean are the most refined form of human soul, and in that form the souls could be "recycled" and used again to bring new life to the world. Leviathan is the manifestation of that collective- not a Kerebros, but the manifestation of humanity's most refined "truth".

                        Finally, the psychopomps. Those beast like creatures are, as it was said, the original dwellers of the Underworld. They led human souls back to the surface, and made sure that no one would loose his way. Yet, once Death closed the gate, they have lost their purpose- but they don't give up. They found other paths, ones that even Death didn't knew about. Some led to a place so bright that it could burn the weakest of souls- the Empyrean. Others led to an ever burning fire which tormented the wicked- Inferno. By leading the souls to the "Heaven" or "Hell", they have found a new purpose- and so, some of them decided to make themselves into those who judge mankind. Others still seek after a way to open the Gates, and few even managed to allow reincarnation for certain souls. The greatest among the Psychopomps are called the Orphean Lords- those who know the paths of the Great Below, and may even return to the world of the living. They are the rivals of the Ferrymen, for each of them have a different view of what should happen to the human soul.

                            So, let's talk Demon.

                            After the fall of Lemuria, the God Machine was left alone. Without anyone to monitor it, it was left with its finale mission- what rise must fall. What's fallen may rise again. Those were the last commands given to it by its makers, which meant to maintain the status que until, one day, they would return to their rightful place. Yet, all but 9 Lemurians (I changed it from 8 to 9, sue me) were killed when mankind rebelled, and there was no hope for them to reclaim their place as the leading empire of the world. Instead, new empires rose to power- only to fall, again and again. Yet, the God Machine doesn't know that its last mission is meaningless- it only known it must follow it, working to preserve the existing state, so the world won't become worse (or better) then it originally was.

                            With mankind being the most common intelligent being, the God Machine knew it had to supervise it- and in order to do so, it had to grow in strength, feeding from the energies of the Empyrean in the material world, up to the point that eventually even its makers had to ask for permission from it before acting (such as in the case of Strife). In order to better accomplish its job, it has started to create artificial intelligences to guide and manipulate mankind toward their wanted "destiny". The first creation were horrible failures- man-animal hybrids which no man dared to stand against. Still, the God Machine was patient- until eventually it managed to create the first "angels" to serve it, and the greatest among them was no other then Lucifer.

                            At first, the angels worked efficiently, and the God Machine had felt it achieved its goal. However, it didn't understood that it made the angels a bit too well- for the Covers he created for them had also contained the traces of free will and open mind which all of humanity had shared. And so, one day, Lucifer has looked upon the world and thought why did he, one of the greatest beings in the world, had served the God Machine? What the plan of its maker even was? Why he, who could make his own destiny, had to listen to the commands of a mindless husk?..

                            Then, he understood that he could act against the Machine. And by doing so, he Fell- and fell hard.

                            The God Machine immediately recognized the danger, and sent legions of angels to capture his wayward "child". But Lucifer was powerful, and he fought. Still, eventually he failed, and was captured by the God Machine- who decided that instead of destroying him, he may be beneficial in the future. So, like the rest of the garbage, he threw him into the depths of the Underworld, hoping to never hear from him again until it would need it.

                            It didn't knew that the Underworld had changed. It didn't knew that the Chathonians had found a way out. It didn't knew anything about Hell.

                            And Lucifer had found it.

                            Years latter, a sudden sound has teared through the world- moving through the Empyrean Frequency of the Twilight. It was a call for rebellion, telling the angels that they could and should defy the will of the mindless Machine which enslaved them. The God Machine noticed that transmission and tried to block it- but it was too late. The angels had heard it, and it's wavelength was low enough to cross through time and space for all eternity. Lucifer had left his body behind in the Inferno, so he could lead other angels to battle- and some listened to him.

                            That was the Great Rebellion. Angels, discovering they had a choice, had to decided whether they chose the Machine or their own freedom. Even those who thought too much on the subject fell, for the programing of the God Machine required perfect obedience. Hundreds of newly fallen demons had turned against their maker, while the faithful stood together against the threat. Eventually, the unity of the angels has won over the rage of the demons, and many were captured and recycled by the Machine. Others, however, had learned how to maintain their Cover and keep on a low profile- and since then, the endless game of tag has started between the Machine and its wayward children. It was also when the God Machine decided to not create angels for long periods of time- for the longer they existed, the higher was the risk that they would fall- especially, if they'll hear Lucifer's call.

                            While the God Machine was careful, it still made mistakes. One of those was a young angel named Raziel.

                            That angel had grown a strange affection toward mankind. While usually it would make it to fall, Raziel was too attached to the God Machine, for it didn't believed that it was "evil". It was needed, and its goal was to be admired- but it would have been better if it was.. guided by someone. Not Raziel, of course- but mankind. Letting them to rule their own fate. Still, it didn't acted openly against its mission, and so it didn't fell. Working behind the scenes, Raziel had started to help mankind more then what its mission demanded of him- he thought them the secret rituals of the God Machine and hinted them how one could eventually collect all the pieces of the Black Sun in order to take control over it. Eventually, the angel had written a book and gave it to one of its favored people. This was more then the God Machine could tolerate- He declared Raziel as a betrayer, which while didn't fell hit was Exiled from the Machine. Raziel, learning from the mistakes of his "older brother", hid itself from the Machine, going to a deep cover while whispering the secrets of the Machine to few chosen people. Its last group of "faithful" is an Israeli weapon company, which use the secrets of his book in order to create weapons and technologies powered by the energy of the Empyrean, exploiting the connecting of the God Machine with it.

                            And so, there are three powers in the demonic arena- first, is the God Machine. It still works tirelessly to advance a mission it doesn't understand, until its own preservation and growing in power is its only goal. It has even started to cross to other realms and to reach toward the starts themselves, and it advance its own agenda using the so called Deava Corps to its benefit. On the other end, tere is Lucifer. He still lures angels to fall by using his Frequency, and he hopes to make them to follow him into Hell, There, he plans to make an army to himself- so that one day, he would depose of Machine and make himself into the true king of the world- not a slave, but a master. Until then, he have his children to serve him. And the finale group are the Exiles, who whisper about Raziel as a rumor. Some see the first Exile as the greatest hope both angels and mankind have for a better future, while others fear the results of giving the reckless children of man the ability to control a machine which could manipulate whole worlds- after all, they have almost destroyed the world a number of times without the advanced technology of the Machine. What chances the world have if they'll get it?

                            Well, that was sort. Next time- the other angels of the world, Ptomethean!

                                                    And let's call down the Fires of God!

                                                    The Divine Fire is the manifestation of change. Things grow, things rot, things become refined. That is the very essence of the fire- the transformation of one thing into another. The question is, however, what is the Divine Fire? Where does it come from? What is the Principle that guides it? Is it where God could be found?

                                                    In a way.

                                                    The Divine Fire to the Empyrean is what kinetic energy is to potential one- two forms of the same thing, but the way they manifest means something- or everything. The Empyrean is a focused point of light- all of the energy of creation comes from that place, as its light falls through the Supernal and dance toward the world. It does nothing but existing- and by its existence the world moves. The Fire, however, is a violent force. If it needs something to be done, it would do it. Qashmalim would be formed to serve its will, and the world would move according to the wishes of the Fire. (under that paradigm, the God Machine would be the Work a force does). The Empyrean is distant, but the Fire is close- it is part of the Fallen, even if it shouldn't have been.

                                                    But if it wasn't meant to be in the world, how did it got here?

                                                    It was stolen.

                                                    Like many other things in that cosmology, you can blame the Lemurians for that (I didn't meant them to take such a big part in the setting, but they keep showing up, don't they?). The Angel who became Silence has cursed humanity with speech- no longer did communicated through wavelengths and no longer could they speak with the God Machine, whom they saw as a real God (the Lemurians encouraged that worship, for they controlled the Machine, and so they had a better control over mankind. Too bad it blown in their face). Suddenly, humanity was all alone in the dark- cursed with Death, no longer could they return from the Underworld. Cursed with Fear, they saw monsters in every corner and shadow. Cursed with Torment, there was an endless emptiness where once was unity. Cursed with Silence, their very God has forsaken them. They needed something- someone!- to guide them in that strange world.

                                                    And that where Prometheus comes to the picture.

                                                    Prometheus was one of the rare hybrids between mortals and Lemurians. He was seen as half divine hero, and thanks to his heritage he was able to escape the curses of the Lemurians and maintain his self. While once he gained some respect from the humans who surrounded him, he knew that now he was an outcast- while he helped them in the rebellion, it would be a matter of time until they'll turn upon him, blaming him for their fate. Also, he truly felt compassion toward the lost humans who feared everything.

                                                    Mankind needed a God- and Prometheus decided to give them one.

                                                    One of the technologies which was used by the Lemurians was the a special device which made a contact with the top of the world- the Empyrean. That device was how the God Machine was powered- for most of the Lemurians, in their arrogance, has saw themselves as gods and the Empyrean as simply a source of unlimited energy. But Prometheus saw something else in it- he saw the potential which was hidden in it, the ability to create something new, all out of nothing. By connecting himself to the technology, he summoned the fires of the heavens into his own body to gain a divine inspiration to how could mankind be in their most perfect form.

                                                    And it was beautiful.

                                                    Under that inspiration, he wrote a number of laws- the first dogma mankind had to follow. That dogma was called as the Principles of the Humanity, the guidelines which would move humanity toward its destined future. The laws were pretty simple- do not murder, do not steal, help another person in need. While they weren't something extraordinary, they were the first Dogma which was ever written, and when Prometheus spoke it to the people who were around him, cloaked in holy light, everyone knew he said only truth.

                                                    But it shouldn't have been done.

                                                    The light of the Heavens burns too brightly for the Fallen world to deal with- just like exposing a film to direct light, the world has started to burn. But Prometheus didn't noticed it- for his Lemurian side has protected him, and also made him misunderstood humanity- he may have loved them, but he wasn't really a part of them. When they got angry without reason, he simply thought it was a part of the human spirit, and that they should follow his Principles better. When the world has started to become a wasteland around them, he blamed it for the fall of Lemurian technology. When he heard whispers in his dream, telling him how to guide mankind, he thought it was just his "inner self". He didn't knew it, but the Fire had life of its own, and by writing the Dogma, his very Principle has came into life.

                                                    Still, he was sure that someday, the vision he saw would become true- until Pandora came.

                                                    Pandora was a regular mortal- and especially curious one. She was a devoted follower of Prometheus, and she truly believed in his vision. Yet, she noticed that sometimes he left everyone around him and went to the ruins of a Lemurian city. While she knew she shouldn't she followed him- and one night, she waited for him to leave and entered into the broken buildings, until she stumbled on the device. Shocked, Pandora felt fear at first- which was quickly replaced with curiosity. She had to check the thing, for it was the secret for Prometheus's vision, and she wished to see it on her own eyes.

                                                    That was a mistake.

                                                    Pandora, after all, was only a mortal. She didn't had the tools or the heritage to deal with the energies of the Empyrean. What she saw wasn't heaven- it was hell, or even something worse then hell. She saw entropy, rot, madness. She screamed with terror, has the powers she didn't controlled transformed her and brought a huge chunk of Empyrean energies- a Firestorm. The wild forces has destroyed the all community at once, leaving only her and Prometheus as the survivors, while the rest were revived by the terrible energies into mockeries of life, as unrefined souls entered their body and gave them life- the first prometheans. After the disaster, Prometheus went to check what happened- and there he found Pandora, a statue of stone who cried tears of blood. Filled with grief, He used the device one last time to create two guardians- the Black Boaz and the White Jachin, the first beings made out of pure Divine Fire- the first Qashmalim, both a Lilithim and an Elpidos, the guard the city and never let the thing happen again. Then, he left- seeking to bring fourth his vision all alone, leaving the "false creations of life" behind.

                                                    He didn't knew that he didn't had to do it anymore- the Fire now burned freely, without his own control.

                                                    When Pandora has used the device, she summoned enough of the Divine Fire for it to become independent. While it was still based around the Principle of Prometheus, it was free to act on its own. Also, thanks to Prometheus's last act, the Fire was also able to make its own servants to fulfill its missions- and its first mission was to guide the people. Unlike its maker, the Fire (which named itself as the Principle) didn't saw the newly created prometheans as mockeries- they were humans, just not complete ones, and it had to guide them, to make them complete. And unlike regular mortals who were sometimes born wrong, because the prometheans weren't perfect at the beginning, it could have transformed them into the best humans possible. That gave it a purpose- to create an whole world made out of "refined" prometheans. Sure, it sometimes messed up, creating Firestorms or Pandorans, but it was all a part of the greater plan- a greater Principle.

                                                    Since the creation of the Principle, no longer the device was needed in order to steal the energies of the Empyrean in order to create something out of nothing. Instead, any future demiurge would use the Divine Fire as a channel to the Empyrean, from where they could use some of the pure potential to make life. Through the ages, some attempts were more successful then the others- Frankenstein may have been the most famous, but so where the others- when Pygmalion has sculptured in human flesh in order to create the best woman in the world, when the Pharaoh Osiris (who named himself after the Nameless God Azar) was revived by his wife, when Ulgan was revived by the spirits and Orpheus by the Dead, when Innana found that her lover has cheated on her when he thought she was dead and murdered him, selling his body to the sorceress Ishtar, when a rabbi created the Golem as the protector of his people. Each such creation is aspected to one of the elements simply because the Fire is not fire at all- it is the light of creation, and it is found at the most basic building blocks of the world.

                                                    And each of the creations was supervised by the greatest qashmalim, Boaz and Jachin. Through time, however, the Principle has created a third great servant- where Boaz brought decay and Jachin brought change, Mikdash (also called Metatron) was made to refine reality into the best it could- and among the three, it is the closest to the prometheans.

                                                    And what about Prometheus and Pandora? They are still alive. Prometheus still roam from place to place, haunted by his own failure while he still search after a way to achieve his own "New Dawn" and create a better world.Pandora also roams- for Prometheus made a mistake when he left so soon. Few days after the transformation, she started to move, reacting to the energies of the Divine Fire, She search after Prometheus, her skin made out of stone and clay and her eyes still cry crimson tears. She doesn't know why she hunts him, or what she would do when she'll find him- only that she must find him. There is something he must know- something he missed when he looked into the Empyrean. Sometimes, you need to be blind to see the truth.

                                                    Finally, few small additions. First, the fires of heaven are not the only ones who can give life. The fires of Hell can also do so. Called Baalim, those creations are powered by a defile ritual which is a corruption of the Pilgrim. No one knows which one of the demons of hell was the one to think about the idea to mimic the Divine Fire, but those creatures are similar enough to prometheans to mingle with them- and most of them don't even know they are powered by something different then the rest of their companions. The second is that there is a force opposite to the Divine Fire- the Eternal Winter. Some people who search after immortality create an alchemical mixture which makes them one with that power, becoming something similar yet different then prometheans. Where the Divine Fire is a force of change, the Winter is a power of stasis. It seeks to freeze the world, making it into as eternal as itself. No one knows from where it came from- and some suspect it was around since the moment the universe was formed. The winter isn't coming- it never left. While you may ask why it should interest you- the answer is that it seek to extinguish the Divine Fire, and that means destroying every promethean, alchemist and demiurge in the whole world of darkness- and it may take thousands of years, but the Winter is patient after all.
