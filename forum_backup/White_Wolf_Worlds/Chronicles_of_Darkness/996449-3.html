                    Random ideas for splat + splats while I was on the train away from books and laptop:

                    About to awaken + stigmata - instead of your mind opening to the supernal you start to see the god machine everywhere and can see how infrastructure holds up the building blocks of existence. Mage gives partial sight, obsession, gnosis and wisdom (no nimbus), mana. Demon gives incarnation, embeds and exploits for gadget, cipher which is tied with obsession (thesis?), aether bleed out of you into your surroundings, loses oerios, and comprise causes angels to hunt you. Can learn any embed of your incarnation, but can be taught other embeds by demons. Can only get mana from obsession and scouring (scouring takes the form of working so long that your body starts to give)

                    Making a Promethean from a werewolf's body sometimes results in a shapeshifty abomination that has its identity change in a very physical manner, starting blank and gains permanent features as it refines itself. Werewolf gives it regeneration, shape changing, auspice (which defines part of their pilgrimage), death rage becomes merged with torment except the abomination has no defined form, seeing into twilight, and can calcify their essence to regain gifts from their life. Promethean gives it alchemy powers, refinements, pilgrimage, and azoth. Disquiet is mingled with lunacy, driving people insane and not requiring a wasteland to get to high levels if he shapeshifts. Needs essence of a chosen resonance to keep a refinement or they go centimanti

                    Beast trades soul to demon, demon takes on the beasts body but then the horror has no where to go so it goes into the demon. Normally this would result in one of the rampant, but in this case one of the demons covers is rampant with the overflowing horrors ephemera trying to seep into the cracks in the demons other cover infrastructure. This leaves the demon to try and make his covers a fortress as his quantum nature becomes a eldritch lair for the horror, resisted only by the fact that the unchained is not Kin with beasts and so the beast cannot use is normal pathways on it.
