    [2E] Aggravated Damage and Armor

    11-10-2016, 08:54 PM
    I imagine I am just missing something here, or reading too much into it... but what are the rules of aggravated damage and armor? Armor rules on pg94 says that general armor applies to all attacks and reduces the total damage taken. Does this really include Aggravated Damage sources? I ask because of Armor Piercing's affect on Durability and how Agg just ignores Durability?

    Thanks for any insight

      In general (so pulling from a few sources here) Agg damage sources rely on common sense and/or explicit statement on this. Part of the issue is "naturally" Agg, and "bane" Agg. A magical attack that does Agg to everything usually ignores armor. Stabbing a werewolf with a silver blade doesn't ignore armor. This makes sense with the idea that the mystic bolt might not even pass through physical space to be impeded by armor, but the knife only does Agg to the werewolf because of the werewolf's weakness. It has to cut the werewolf to do harm, and isn't going to ignore a sheet of metal in the way.

              Well yes, though 2e downgraded the availability of Agg significantly (Vampires do lethal with their claws without a Merit and even then only in frenzy for example).

              As well, there's still a significant amount of "apply common sense" that's supposed to be applied here over flat rules. Which basically means one ST could rule that Agg dealing claws don't ignore Armor because they still have to cut through stuff before hitting flesh and some of the damage will by ablated, and another ST could say all "natural" Agg ignores Armor unless explicitly disallowed by the power granting Agg damage. Both have enough evidence to be "right" by the rules.

                              Remember that Mage Armors are Reflexive, which means they can be activated at any point of time even outside your turn in response to being attacked. Faster than thought or bullets.

                              Other thing about Aggravated damage is that it can destroy limbs if damage it caused is attached to a Tilt. This can be potentially devastating to characters without regeneration. Vampires and other self-regenerating thingies have it easy, as while it takes time and is pain in the ass, they can restore damaged limbs and organs permanently just fine. Mages have it hard, because lasting magic won't give you your hand back, and regrowing it means having to sustain regeneration spell indefinitely; it being dispelled equals to your limb withering away.
