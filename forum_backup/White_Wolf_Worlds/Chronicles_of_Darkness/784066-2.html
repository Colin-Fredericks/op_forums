        Actually, in real life our ancestors definitely worshipped multiple gods and totem spirits without problems. It's a very Judeo-Christian notion that there's going to be problems.

        If a character pays into tribe and lodge totems (through status) then there is little problem with having a personal totem. They do not get jealous of one another in the manner folks are suggesting. If they did, the big bad tribal totem would simply gobble the smaller ones up. Anyway, that's an understanding from a shaman's point of view.

                                        Here is where the issue with a personal totem comes up. Yes every werewolf has a tribal totem, and their behavior is influenced slightly by their Tribal Oath. Yes you have the pack totem, where things get more hairy. A packs behavior is heavily influenced by their totem. Have a totem of war? You are going to be spending time going into battle and following the pack Bane of picking fights with every tough bastard that comes along. When you get to the subject of a personal totem, how is it going to work? A totem isn't a spirit ally, it is a spirit that the werewolf in question holds at least partial fealty to in return for benefits from said spirit. When you already have a pack totem 2 conflicts arise.

                                        Conflict between the pack and pack mate. The pack has already dedicated a huge amount towards their current pack totem, one that influences their style and behavior. Why would any pack mate stop doing that to form a totem bond with another spirit? The pack, now has to worry about their totem being offended, how this new totems interests may clash with the pack totems, and whether or not the pack mate will have the interest of the pack at heart.

                                        Conflict from the totem. Spirits are not nice buddy buddy kinds of beings. You take a personal totem and your pack totem will have NONE of that shit. It didn't bind itself to a pack of werewolves for shits and giggle, it did so because the pack furthers it's interests and gives it a crap ton of essence in doing so. Now some werewolf is going to take time away from the pack totem to go put effort into another spirit? One of three things is going to happen, most likely two.

                                        1. Pack totem is going to remove his favor from the werewolf in question. This is almost a foregone conclusion. If your pack totem sees you running off forming another totem bond and ignoring it for your new spirit, be prepared to lose the mechanical benefits of the pack totem.

                                        2. Pack totem is going to attack your personal totem and likely destroy it. Your pack totem is not most likely not going to sit by while some low rank spirit comes along and tries to take away from the pack it has been working with to further it's interests.

                                        3. The whole pack will suffer loss of their totems blessing. Less likely but still possible. Some especially proud spirits will blame the ENTIRE pack for not stopping this werewolf from dedicating said amount of time to furthering it as a totem.

                                            Originally posted by Heroofthemists View Post
                                            Conflict between the pack and pack mate. The pack has already dedicated a huge amount towards their current pack totem, one that influences their style and behavior. Why would any pack mate stop doing that to form a totem bond with another spirit? The pack, now has to worry about their totem being offended, how this new totems interests may clash with the pack totems, and whether or not the pack mate will have the interest of the pack at heart.

                                            Conflict from the totem. Spirits are not nice buddy buddy kinds of beings. You take a personal totem and your pack totem will have NONE of that shit. It didn't bind itself to a pack of werewolves for shits and giggle, it did so because the pack furthers it's interests and gives it a crap ton of essence in doing so. Now some werewolf is going to take time away from the pack totem to go put effort into another spirit? One of three things is going to happen, most likely two.
                                            What you are talking about sounds more like a Fire-Touched Pack. Depending on how you spend your merits a totem could be a spirit ally, a true friend, or several other things. You can absolutely have a totem that is intensely jealous about their pack and does not wish them to share any time with others, but that is going to depend entirely on the motivations of the pack and what kind of spirit the totem is.

                                            Pack Perspective: Sometimes totems can be forced upon a pack, but most of the time the pack is going to ask a spirit to become their totem or be approached by a spirit who wants to strike a bargain. This means the power of choice lies with the pack, who are probably either choosing to work with that spirit because it will bring them great power or because they can get along with the spirit. A Forsaken pack might put up with an asshole spirit for the sake of power, but this is going to depend heavily on what the pack needs and how they feel about assholes. Storm Lords might endure a jealous totem because it shows their dedication to the cause. Blood Talons might not accept a totem that refuses to work with others. Iron Masters might tell the spirit to stop with the controlling boyfriend shit.

                                            Since most packs are not going to put up with an asshole, that leaves spirits that are willing to exchange benefits like a business arrangement or spirits they genuinely like. This means that most totems are going to be at least tolerable and possibly even decent. If the spirit starts behaving in a way that is uncool then the pack has the right to ask them to shape up, and if the bad behavior continues then they may drop the totem entirely and seek out some other spirit. Particularly brutal packs might take their totem down a dark alley in the Shadow and beat some sense into the spirit until it agrees to cooperate.

                                            The important thing is that Forsaken packs are going to have actual relationships with their Totems. It's going to be a two way street where both parties may do things that annoy the other, and sometimes there might even be some serious drama. But as long as things generally stay positive then everything works out. That is what friendship is all about.

                                            Spirit Perspective: Maybe a spirit dreams of bossing around a bunch of werewolves, then forcing the pack to serve them and becoming Pure. Good for them. Most spirits are not going to be that lucky. They may be pretty obsessive about what they want, but if they have a couple of ranks under their belt they are going to be smarter than your average mote. The kind of spirits that end up as Forsaken totems are going to understand that werewolves can protect it from other spirits that might want to eat it, and provide it with food and power in exchange for spiritual favor. As long as it gets more out of the bargain than it loses, it is probably not going to want to casually break that pact.

                                            These middle rank spirits are going to be like teenagers and that is okay. They are going to push the boundaries and see how much they can get away with, and if the pack lets that happen then they are going to keep pushing until they dominate the pack like you are suggesting. Most Forsaken packs are not going to let that happen. The spirit is going to be sullen for a while but eventually will learn that it cannot be a total asshole without the risk of losing that protection. Lower rank spirits might even need constant reinforcement of this lesson.

                                            The type of spirit also plays a huge role. A lion totem spirit is probably not going to be cool with someone's personal deer totem, and much like the scorpion that stung the lion, the lion is going to have a hard time resisting the urge to eat the deer. The pack has every reason to wonder why someone is bringing in a deer totem to a lion pack. But that same lion totem is probably not going to have a big issue with someone else having another lion spirit as long as this new personal totem understands the hierarchy. Spirits of herd animals or community spirits are probably going to have the easiest time cooperating with personal totems. A computer spirit might be ecstatic about having someone else to network with, which would be confusing as hell to your personal frog totem. A warfare spirit might welcome personal totems with open arms, while expecting them to behave like good soldiers.

                                            The higher in rank a spirit is, the more likely it is going to be part of a tithing network. It has a boss it needs to send essence to, and as long as something particularly delicious does not come along it should have no problems with lesser spirits hanging out -- provided they are contributing essence to the pack totem as their own tithe.

                                            Storyteller Perspective: At the end of the day, the player and the storyteller should sit down together and discuss what kind of story they want to tell about the personal totem. If both parties think a story about jealousy and power would be interesting, then that is what's going to happen. If both parties think a story about business contracts or friendship would be interesting, then that is how the relationship is going to play out. The personal totem might be put into peril when the story demands it, but the majority of scenes involving the personal totem are going to be about the things the player and storyteller agreed upon.

                                            It's also okay for a storyteller to say no. Much like bringing a paladin into a group full of thieves, a storyteller should let a player know if his personal totem is going to be a problem or not before those experience points are spent.
