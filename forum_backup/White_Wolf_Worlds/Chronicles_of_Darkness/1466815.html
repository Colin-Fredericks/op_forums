    [Crazy ideas] What would you want out of a 2ed of The Descent ?

    11-05-2021, 04:49 PM
    So, Demon's finally been translated in French, which makes it tremendously easier for me to understand it !

    it is, therefore, the best time to ask you folks what you'd want out of an hypothetical 2nd edition of that awesome game.

    I'm still in the middle of reading the corebook (I read it long ago in English but I clearly didn't understood all of it back then and I hadn't as much experience as today when it comes to CoD games, since I'm now a very happy player in a Werewolf campaign !).

    Something I'd like personally would be to incorporate the Analysts wholly into the main book, add a new kind of Embeds and Exploits tailored to them and generally speaking (perhaps because I've read far too much Requiem) makes something a little more distinct with the Incarnations, which, I think should work a little like the Clans, in that they could give out one point in an Attribute or two to represent the way the God-Machines builds its angels.

    Then again, it is true that the corebook emphasis time and again that not all Destroyers are brutes and the like but I still think there could be something more here to mechanically adds more weight to the demon's time as an angel in the player's creation of her character, you know ? It wouldn't be too hard to tie it into the Speciality which can fray the Cover : Mary the gentle housewife actually knows how to wield a sword and the also is far stronger than her husband, but does her best to hide it all, as an exemple.

    I'd definitvely keep the Integrators as an Agenda. In most other splats, they are the "low-level"/evil/twisted version of the players, the one willing to seel other Changelings to the Fae or they would be VII or the Pure... But here, they are playable and it's actually one of the greatest strength of the splat for me. All Agendas would deserve to be expanded and worked upon to be more flesh out but Integrators are the one which needs it the most to me because of their almost bad-guys vibe.

    I also think that, in accordance with the Analysts becoming a base Incarnation, the Tempters would need to be split into two distinct Agendas : the good old hedonistic Tempters and Usurpers. The former would largely be the basic corrupt and self-serving demon which often spring into mind when ones think about the Tempters, whereas the Usurpers would be demons who truly believe that they are embryonic God-Machines in their own rights and who want to overthrow a tyrannic parent who stiffle their growth.

    I'll probably have more ideas as I read the book, but what would you want out of a potential 2ed ?

                  There was an old thread on this subject, from which my views largely haven't changed.
                  - Clearer view from the start on how angelic Covers work, as stated
                  - Possibly draw on Deviant's Web of Pain for rules for higher-level projects and infrastructure?
                  - Streamline pact rules. They're structured with the detailed, customizable shape of 1e Changeling pledges, but not actually customizable and flexible enough to take advantage of that detailed system. There's no need to be weighing pact factors and modifying Willpower costs based on the imbalance.
                  - Reduce Condition bloat. Demon doesn't have it as bad as some other gamelines but they can stand to be shorn down.
                  - Demon, especially the corebook, suffers a little from a bias towards presenting angels and demons in an immediate context, defined by their most recent mission, in a manner that elides angels that have been active for historical events. At its worst, this can send the message that angels are minted for a particular mission and then stripped for parts or wiped of memory. Part of the appeal of a Demon game is in a character who has had some role in crafting the world as it is. More examples, then, of angels who have been reused for multiple projects across long stretches of time, such that you can play a recently fallen demon and say "I appeared as dazzling lights over 12th century France" as a background detail and have it make sense. I may be the one person really carrying this torch, but it matters to me.

                                          So, continuing my reading of Demon, I can tell I like Embeds very much like they are. Some of them may need a rewrite to not be as broken as they seem to be at first glance (Fungible Knowledge and that other one which allows to switch Competences come to mind) but overall I feel they wouldn't need much work in a potential new edition.

                                          The Cypher on the other hand... I like the concept of it, of course, but I feel it has two huge flaws : it takes some agency out of the player character since it's basically designed by the Storyteller and it add a lot of weight on said Storyteller's shoulders because he has to design the Final revelation and the Interlocks. And he needs to do that basically before first session, once each character has been created but isn't really established. And if a player want to make changes/take his character in widly different directions it may invalidate the whole Cypher (I know I did just that with my Irraka, who started a Ghost Wolf and is drifting toward joining the Bone Shadows).

                                          Not losing the deep implications of the Cypher is hard because letting it to the player's appreciation means that min-maxers could very well end being Primum 4 with their whole Cypher super fast, knowing which Embeds to buy to unlock it. It's honestly a conundrum i'm hitting my head on and I can't seem to find a way to make it work better. So, would you guys keep it as such or do you have other ideas ?

                                          Addendum with Pactes : they are great conceptually but quite unwieldy in nature it seems, at least at a first reading, I'll need to return to it. The rules themselves are good for mortals but I find the Cover part to be somewhat too limited because the effects of the normal pacts don't extend far enough. In a future edition i'd like for them to perhaps provide a lesser number of dots but extend further so everyone would believe that something happened to the demon, if you get what I mean. Not sure I've the vocabulary to explain what I mean however.

                                              Thinking on it, I think I'd like to see demon approach the fate mechanics again. Though it could be as simple as taking what made it into the 2e core blue book I can't help but feel itd be different with a more active involvement from the god machine.

                                              Potentially related to that or possibly independent I think itd be interesting to see rules for people being pieces of infrastructure. Not simply people that work in infrastructure but people who are made to be pieces of infrastructure. This could be as simple as implanted routines that act like obsessions to fulfill an occult matrix. As for how much aether could potentially be stored in this unique infrastructure type, I'd probably lean on VtR ghoul mechanics and each human in such a network would only be able to store up to their stamina in aether.
                                              As an interesting side detail, such people could also potentially be made subhorned infrastructure by reprogramming their obsession and disconnecting them from the machine.
