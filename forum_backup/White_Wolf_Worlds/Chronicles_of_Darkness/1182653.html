    Hillfolk: The Desperation-A Fan Game (Open Development Thread)

    02-05-2018, 01:59 PM
    Alright, I have decided I want to make a fan game for the New World of Darkness/Chronicles of Darkness, specifically the First Edition rules set, as I personally do not care for CofD Second Edition (mainly for mechanical reasons) and I am more familiar with 1E anyway so I am going to try and work within those parameters. However, if any of you want to adapt this idea for Second Edition, be my guest.

    The fan game I have in mind is a New World of Darkness take on the "Hixploitation" genre of horror where hapless suburban city-dwellers wander into the wilderness and are attacked by backwoods savages. It's a well-known horror cliche that has been seen in many works such as Fallout 3's Point Lookout DLC, Deliverance, The Hills Have Eyes, Wrong Turn, and too many others to list. The parody horror film Tucker & Dale vs. Evil is an excellent deconstruction of the genre.

    A little bit of background on the concept and why I want to make this into a game: I grew up in the rural hills of Central Appalachia, specifically the coalfields of Southwestern Virginia (Dickenson County, VA and Wise County, VA) and believe me, I am intimately familiar with the stereotypes and cliches of my people, and the Hixploitation genre has not helped at all. However, I want to take the genre and turn it on its head. Essentially I'm taking the tired "backwoods redneck savages" cliche and "taking it back", so to speak. By making the so-called savage backwoods monsters playable and somewhat sympathetic, I am lending an alternate point of view on the genre.

    The main themes of Hillfolk: The Desperation are honor, desperate survivalism, tribalism, distrust of outsiders, and solemn duties of holding back ancient and eldritch evils, even if it means taking extreme and desperate actions. I want to have sort of a cosmic horror bent to it, almost Lovecraftian, as if the Hillfolk are trying to hold back some sort of eldritch monstrosity. The game draws heavily on themes established in the Classic WoD games Werewolf: The Apocalypse and Hunter: The Reckoning.

    The Players are rustic outcasts known as Hillfolk, regardless of where they are from. The Hillfolk are those imbued or cursed with ancient powers and markings to defend mankind from a collection of ancient and unfathomable horrors known as The Outsiders, whose motives are unknowable and generally destructive. Some of the Hillfolk were once normal humans cursed by The Outsiders while others were born into their unfortunate state. Some Hillfolk look outwardly deformed or mutated by these curses, while others are insane. Yet others may appear to be of "normal" mind and body, but are damaged spiritually. These same curses also have left them open to powers granted to them by mysterious beings known as The Enlightened, designating these unfortunate few to go into the far reaches of wilderness and make war against The Outsiders, whether it be to save the world, save their hometown, or just save their own souls.

    I want this game to be more in the open-ended toolkit style of the early First Edition releases, so there will be five main splats and possibly five faction splats as well. This game is in the very early development phases and it is an open development thread where anyone can offer input on it.

    There would be five splats called "Families", equivalent to Vampire Clans or Werewolf Auspices. I have yet to determine the exact names, archetypes, and powers of each Family, but the premise is there. So far, I have the following ideas for Families....

    A Family centered around defending sacred places or keeping outsiders away from lands where no human should dare tread.

    A Family centered around hunting down and slaying the servants of The Outsiders, many of whom are scouts, spies, and trackers.

    A Family centered around preserving ancient rituals and traditions, often scavenging for ancient relics to ward off The Outsiders.

    A Family of rugged survivalists who realize that The Outsiders will rise up, but they will do their best to endure and rebuild from the ashes.

    A Family of warlike and militant hillfolk who want to confront The Outsiders and their servants directly and hopefully win the war.

    Again, this is in the very early stages of development and I just came up with the idea this morning, but I felt it was too good to let go and so I decided to share the idea and from here, develop it further.

                                  While I am all about doing better ChroD/WoD material for Appalachia (its almost becoming a personal brand at this point), I'm not sure if doing an entire splat is the best approach. For one, while "mutant hillbilly" is definitely a well worn trope it's a little hard to translate into a splat (in my opinion at least) because of the difficulty of disentangling the "mutant" part from the "hillbilly" part. Are they a splat because they're mutants? If so then the focus becomes much more about the mutation than about their being from them thar hills. On the other hand if they are a splat because they are hillbillies then while that does preserve the desired focus it does create some awkward and potentially problematic implications. I definitely understand wanting to reclaim stereotypes, but I'm not necessarily all about starting that from the position that these "Hillfolk" are in actuality a species of supernatural beings distinct and apart from humanity.

                                  To be clear: I'm not nay-saying the entire idea. I like several of the concepts presented, but I think that there may be better ways to express them than through a splat-game. Honestly if people wanted to collaborate on some kind of broader Night Horrors or other blue book type supplement focused on Appalachia that is something I'd definitely be down for.

                                  For my own part, background-wise: I spent the first twenty five years of my life (which is to say all but the last five) bouncing around between parts of WV and southwestern PA.

                                                  For me the best way to visualize these things is to look at a character sheet and have it explained piece by piece. So here's my drafts of a character sheet

                                                  Name:
                                                  Dwelling:
                                                  Clan:

                                                  *
                                                  *
                                                  *
                                                  Dwelling would be what type of Hillfolk they are, similar to a bloodline or auspice. Keeping to the model of 5 we have

                                                  Mountain-Men: Living up in isolated mountain ranges, such as the Appalachia area but could also represent something similar to Gurkhas, characterized by being physically powerful typically

                                                  Backwoodsmen: Living out in the woods, hunting, trapping and fishing to live off the land. They are heavily inclined towards the survivalist archetype.

                                                  Swamp-Rats: Whether Hoodoo witch doctors or gator punchers, these are the ones that live in the bayous and swamps. They don't have to be Cajun though, witch doctors living in the swamps are an old stereotype from all over the world

                                                  Bushmen: living out in the deserts and plains, they know the tunnels and caverns inside and out, associated with aboriginal Australians, Native Americans and Steppe Riders, Bushmen are the historical raiders in and out in a flash

                                                  Longshoremen: inspired by the citizens of Dunwich and Innsmouth, these are the citizens of the coastal rural towns that Lovecraft was so critical of. Whether Massachusetts, Maine or even the British Isles, the stereotype of the sea faring locals suspicious of outlanders is common.

                                                  Clans would be the organizations Hillfolk ally themselves with, these would be the families you spoke of, with their goals being what you wrote

                                                  I didn't know exactly what they would use for morality, but in my experience many of these stereotypes take a pride in being one with the land, and Prometheans and Vampires already share humanity so maybe use Harmony for these guys

                                                  Gumption is the power stat, the analogue to Blood Potency or Primal Urge.

                                                  Moxie, I gotta be honest, I just didn't know what their Vitae/Essence analogue would be so I just kind of made it up

                                                  Inheritances would be the special powers, like werewolf Gifts and Vampire Disciplines, the inheritances you get partially determined by Dwelling and Clan.

                                                  Other Notes
                                                  The party name for a group of Hillfolk(like pack for werewolves or cabal for mages) is A Holler

                                                  I'm not sure what kind of special anchors they'd have, and I'm still iffy on the Harmony and Moxie thing, we'd have to come up some base powers but I can't think of any right now
