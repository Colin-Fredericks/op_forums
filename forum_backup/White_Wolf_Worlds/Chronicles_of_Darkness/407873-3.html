        Originally posted by Christian A View Post
        It's important to remember, also, that the Pure truly believe themselves to be victims of a cosmic struggle that started an apocalypse ago. They whine about being martyrs for a holy cause, they think they're the underdogs of the setting who've been wronged more than anyone else - yet they have all the numbers, allies and advantages, and they had them for a long, long while. Their dogma cannot allow for themselves to accept that fact, though.
        This isn't true, though. The books are very explicit that their numbers wax and wane. They are currently at a high point because they took advantage of the Brethren Wars in the 80s and 90s and claimed a lot of territory. Prior to that, though, they were explicitly lurking in the margins with territories in isolated places. And this has been the pattern. While they have their advantages, they don't have all the advantages. Their totems are more powerful and they get along better with spirits, but the Forsaken have Luna on their side, less susceptibility to silver and aren't operating under the crippling bans the Pure are, from both their pack and tribal totems. As for numbers, the Pure are often self-limiting. The Fire-Touched rituals are so abusive that they must have a high mortality rate (many of them set their new recruits on fire, for God's sake). Ivory Claws are incredibly picky in who they will take, refusing to even consider someone who Luna even smiled at. Predator Kings live a lifestyle of incredible violence, even for werewolves, and their problem with human artifacts drives them into the boonies, away from resources and breeding stock. Their culture of paranoia and constant competition to prove themselves Purer-than-thou would lead to a lot of packs imploding, like many extreme religious cults.
