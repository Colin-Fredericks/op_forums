    To the Moooon!

    04-03-2017, 08:03 PM
    Hi all. So I've been running a WoD game for a number of years and, with the changeover to Chronicles of Darkness the chronicle is reaching an epic peak and I need some ideas on the following:

    The players got ahold of the entropy artifact from The Moon Window story seed in The God-Machine Chronicle and, beginning to realize what "Packet Theta" is, are growing desperate to try and put the artifact back where it was taken from.

    So the party needs to get to the moon.

    It's a highly mixed group, all characters with about 52 XP worth of extras (using the old XP rules) and multiple ways of traversing through other realms.

    We have the following to work with, as some of my players created and run 2 characters each.

    Circle of the Crone Daeva Vampire
    Stannum Galatean Promethean
    Ordo Dracul Mekhet Vampire
    Makara Nemesis Beast
    Moros Mysterium Mage
    Beast-Hunterheart Summer Court Changeling
    Wizened Smith Winter Court Changeling

    They also have made allies with the local Pure pack network and are seeking a Demon to ally with.

    Now given what we have to work with, what methods would work to get them to the Crypt of the Butterfly? With this bunch, they're capable of shifting world-to-world and I was hoping to see if anyone had a houseruled Demon Propulsion that might at least get them to low orbit where they can engage the God-Machine's defenses (one of which is the "Black Knight" satellite of urban legend).

    - Yulian

                                                  If the Beast learns the Shadowed Soul Atavism it's theoretically possible to teleport to the shadowed part of the moon, but that doesn't bring anyone else along.

                                                  Some canny enough tricks with Primordial Pathways might be able to open a path?

                                                  Ok I have an idea. The Moros presumably has a decent Matter Arcana rating. Manipulate a room into as close a resemblance of the moon's surface as you can, have the Beast use Nightmares until they get an exceptional success or somebody fails a breaking point and they can turn the room into a Chamber. You now have enough resemblance to attempt to open a pathway to the moon. It's a pretty hefty penalty but with the backing of a whole Brood there ought to be enough ways to add bonus dice to make it happen
