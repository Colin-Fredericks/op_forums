    [2E New Stuff][Help] Blood Sight

    10-05-2016, 01:36 AM
    So, I wrote a thing inspired by some story about a vamp who saw the sunrise. Auspex is cool and all, but I'm a sucker for sweet eyewear and Darth Kreia from Knights of the Old Republic 2, so having blindfolded vampires running around that "See beyond sight" is appealing. Problem is, I'm having some trouble coming up with some specifics. I've got some basic ideas and a short writeup. But I don't have anything substantial.

    “I saw the sun...”
    “It was glorious; beautiful. I hadn’t seen it in decades. It was an accident, of course. I spent the night with my grandson. He was on his deathbed. AIDS left him a shell of his former self. I didn’t trust him to make it to the next night, so I had to...at any rate, by the time I made it back to my Haven, the dawn had started to creep over the horizon, and I looked heavenward at just the right moment. I managed to resist the Beast’s fear, but my eyes burned out of their sockets before I had the chance to retreat to my chambers.

    “The next evening, my eyes were still gone, and I was hungry. A servant gave their lifeblood to me. But I couldn’t concentrate. I decided not to grow my eyes back. I could, if I wanted to. But I choose not to. It’s a reminder of our weakness, but also our strength. I see so much more without them, and I shall never forget my last sunrise.”

    Blood Sight
    This unique form of Cruác was developed by a member of the Circle of the Crone whose eyes were burned out by the sun. It doesn’t require its Acolytes to go to such extremes, but since blinding oneself is required in the ritual, the Acolyte gouging her eyes out is a common practice among older practitioners (though spending a Vitae in any other fashion is acceptable). If a queasy Kindred blindfolds herself, then as soon as the blindfold is removed, the effect ends until she casts the spell again. Otherwise, it lasts until the Kindred’s eyes regrow during daysleep.

    The simplest benefit of Bloodsight is that the Acolyte’s Kindred Senses treat her Blood Potency as 2 higher. In addition, the Acolyte gains Unseen Senses (Ephemeral Entities).

    And after that point I don't really know where to go. Sight beyond sight, enhanced other senses. Makes sense for vampires. Just trying to come up with something more interesting. Seeing through Concealment definitely. But maybe this should be some sort of Merit?

    "Nihhina kalekal-zidu kal masun, kal manudanadu. Nihhina kalekal-zidu nukal shaghu-desasudu — nihhina kalekal-zidu kal innu-desasudu udhkal samm." Arthur Ashe

      I like the idea, so lets run with this!

      New Merit: Bloodsight

      The Bloodsight has it's origins in an Acolyte who was burnt by sunlight, but the Acolytes know that is in darkness their kind belongs. The act of blinding oneself opens the faithful to truths their eyes deny, human senses keeping the Acolyte from achieving true connection with her true nature. The Beast dwells in a shroud of eternal blackness, and by caging herself within that realm with it, the Acolytes believe they can listen to it unlike any other.

      Emboldening the Hunter (•): Your character has ritually blinded herself in hopes of growing closer to her inner darkness, empowering the Beast. She doubles the base range of all her Kindred Senses (see Vampire the Requiem 2nd Edition, p. 91).

      Blood Will Tell (••): Your character's Beast isn't just emboldened, but an experienced hunter of men and monsters, fully capable of replacing her lost sight with something more. If tracking by blood your character suffers a –2 die penalty to rolls that require vision to act against her prey, and if the vampire tastes her victim's blood she suffers no penalty at all. This does not allow the character to see her target.
      Drawback: The Beast has learned to see all as prey, a source of nourishment essential to survival, and this mindset has slowly crept into the character's own instincts. Whenever she tastes a Kindred victim's vitae a vampire with this merit must roll Resolve + Composure, minus one die for each point of vitae taken. Failure means she will feed on her prey to the bitter end, should she successfully subdue it.

      Dark Union (•••): Your character understands her Beast in such a way that it can almost make sense of it, at times. She understands what makes it tick, and in return it has grown overzealous of the vampire. Anytime the character Lashes Out (see Vampire the Requiem 2nd Edition, p. 92) she may add her dots in this merit to the roll.
      Drawback: A Beast unleashed is a dangerous partner. It knows only the need to feed and protect the vampire, and in any scene the character has Lashed Out all her rolls to resist frency (but not ride it) are penalized by this merit's dots.

      Beckoning Shadows (••••): The terrifying truth is that most Kindred believe themselves to be apex predators because they cannot feel their hunters' presence, hiding among them within stolen bodies. A character with this merit was revealed these terrible truths by her Beast, and may sense the Bird of Dis with a Wits + Blood Potency roll, whether the creature is housed inside a body or not.
