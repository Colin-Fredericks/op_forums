    Antagonists By Types Across Gamelines [Just For Fun]

    09-19-2017, 04:28 PM
    While each of the gamelines usually operates on its own little piece of the CofD universe, some trends seems to be dominant in each of the sub-settings, like the different types of Antagonists that each game propose to its players. While the antagonists themselves are different, they seem to occupy certain positions which are meant to reflect the protagonist splat, helping to define the hero by being like them, but different enough to make the protagonist question themselves, their motives and the world they live in. So, just for fun (and because I need a break), I've thought about to divide the different antagonist splats according to their common interest. So- by using the reflection metaphor from earlier- the way I divide the antagonists is as follows-

    My Left Is Your Right- at first glance, they look like you. They have the face as you have, the same eyes and smile and that big nose your mother insists you got from your father. They look so similar to you, like your twin brother, that it only looks natural that you are family, right? However, each family has its differences- and only when you get close enough, you start to understand that your left is their right, and that while on the surface you are the same, in truth your opinions are so different that they create a terrible tear between the two of you, one which can't not be breached. Politics always seems to ruin family reunions.

    Antagonists- VII, the Pure, Seers of the Throne, Changeling Loyalists, Cancer Cells, Sacrosanct, Deceived, Deviant Loyalists

    Shattered Image- everyone knows that you shouldn't break the mirror. It brings 7 years of bad luck, they say. While it may only be a superstition, the truth is that the face reflected from a broken mirror aren't that pleasant to look at. It is you, just as you may have been, but only defiled by something else, something which tore you to shreds and left only a shattered image behind. While it is disturbing to look at, you may learn something from the act- for example, by avoiding the same fall by yourself, so your own image would be kept nice and clean.

    Antagonists- Belial's Brood, Bale Hounds, Scelesti, Centimani, Vacant

    Bloody Shards- while being covered in cracks in not exactly the nicest image you can have of yourself, it is still not the worst. Sometimes, the mirror doesn't simply breaks- it is shattered to pieces and scattered in the wind. Each of those shards shows only a small image of who you once were- not the full image, only a small fraction of reality, broken by madness. Trying to look at yourself using those shards is dangerous- for they are sharp in their corners, and touching them without care may end with spilled blood.

    Antagonists- Draugr, Zi'ir (Obsolete), Mad, Madmen, Wretched, Silvers

    Twisting Surface- not all mirrors show the truth. Some take it, twist it, changing it and showing an whole new bizarre image. Some make you look too big, other too small. Some make you too fat, others too thin. This one makes you head looks huge. That one enlarge your feet and hands. That one shows you standing on your head! As they are mostly used in carnivals and their like, it is easy to not take those reflections too seriously- only tools for your entertainment. But if you look at it long enough, you can see that, in the end, that twisted image of yours is still you, on way or another.

    Antagonists- Hosts, Pandorans, Fetches, Horror, Abmortal (Obsolete), Shuankshen, Exiled, Heroes

    Smoking Mirror- the most dangerous of mirrors are the ones who don't show you what you are looking at. Deep in the smoky surface of the black obsidian, who can truly say what's truth and what's false anymore? Lies and fantasies mix up with reality, and eventually you aren't even sure at what you are looking at. And in the depths of that mad world, behind the shadows and smoke and the movements beneath the surface, you could still see yourself- not clearly, but you are still there, lurking. You become the enemy of yourself, and in that mad war which defies everything you ever knew as truth, how can you ever look again in the mirror and believe what you see in there?

    Antagonists- Strix, Idigam, Huntsmen, Alchemists, Slashers, Angels, Insatiable, Progenitor(?)

    Of course, not all antagonists are that easy to organize. What about ghosts and spirits? Where should one put the Claimed or the Banishers? Or Deva and LDI? In the end, while those categories may help you define your setting, the CofD by their nature defy definition. Some gamelines have more than one enemy of the same type, while others have none. Still, hope it is somehow helpful, and feel free to suggest your own divisions!

          Or, to break things down to a bit less wordy level:

          We're-Evil-Monsters splat (brood, bale, seers*, centimani, Loyalists/Privateers, that one Hellfire compact / cancer cells)
          Hunter splat (VII, pure, banishers, bridgeburners, pretty much every compact & conspiracy)
          Extreme Integrity splat (druagr, forgot werewolf name, the Mad, forgot changeling name, Slashers)
          Main spirit antagonist that's hard to kill (strix, idigam, abyssal, sublimanti, huntsmen, demons)
          Common enemies (other vamps, spirits, everyone not-supernal, pandorans, hobs, other splats)
          Revenge of the Half-Splat (rogue ghouls, hunter wolfbloods, uppity proximus families, no prommie halfspats, fetches)

          The WEM splat is basically just showcasing the worst of the supernatural in question all grouped together.
          Hunter splats are supernaturals that basically got a hate-on for their fellow supernaturals.
          Most supernaturals when they get low Inegrity equialents have issues to show the improtance of not being a cruel bastard.

          And so on. The enemies in 1st edition were just as formulatic as people claiming the 5x5 splats were. 2nd ed has shaken that up a bit, but there's some carry over.
