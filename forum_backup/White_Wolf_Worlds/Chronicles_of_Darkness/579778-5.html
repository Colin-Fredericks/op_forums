                Originally posted by MCN View Post

                EDIT - Also, you missed the theme of "Choosing your own Family." That's a big one in Beast.
                Actually this is a theme (and feature) of RPG's in general. A game of more substance would engage and focus on the bigger themes of commitment and obligation (note you can still choose your family and focus on those themes). Right now the game talks a lot about "family" but it never actually stops to delve into what that word means. Vampire, Demon, and Werewolf all do demonstrably better jobs at engaging each of their themes. It would not be such a bad thing to take the Family notion and re-examine it from the perspective of some permutations. IMO it should be possible to build multiple types of families using the game; if family is a thematic focus of the game, then additional mechanics should support these multiple builds; otherwise every game of Beast is going to look more than a little cookie-cutter.

                One of the best things Werewolf 2nd does is crack open and fully expand the notion of pack. Beast isn't even doing 10% as good of a job at exploring the notion of family.

                        Originally posted by Magnum Opus View Post

                        May I suggest closing topic then? Since visibly my arguments are considered silly or unreasonable by most here and I feel like this thread was a bad idea to start with.

                        I hope that doesn't happen. If this forum isn't grown up enough to debate and discuss game cornerstones then I fear for the future of OnyxPath. Right now this game is sitting at about a Mummy level of quality. I'd like to at least see it get up to the Demon level if not the Vampire level. We should have some expectations for these games. I certainly don't want it to be another Geist 1.0.

                        Honestly, it afforded me an opportunity to voice some of my own (unpopular) opinions. Unlike you, I think that cross-over support is something that should be pursued but not if it's at the expense of overall game quality. My main concern is that the Werewolf notion of pack is, in fact, 100 times more supportive of cross-overs than the current draft's explication of family (and the you choose it) theme. The developers seem to have forgotten that we choose family members in storytelling games anyway (the whole thing is a coop storytelling experience). The notion of family is a good one; I believe that they should keep refining it. They should add whatever mechanics are necessary to support it. And most of all, they should just go ahead and ask -- what is family anyway? Is it the people that you surround yourself with? Is it the people tied to you by the very long lines of lineage? To my mind, we can best explore these questions by focusing on themes of commitment and obligation -- core values of families -- whether they be blood relations or chosen circles of friends and familiars.
