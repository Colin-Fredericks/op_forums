    The rite requires you to have symbolic components that evoke the target resonance. There are more useful resonances to aim for.

    Again: "spirit of ___" does not necessitate "Influence: ___" and it's a gross error to make that assumption. There's Helions with no Sunlight Influence. There's no spirits of drowning. That's geists. There's spirits of watery death.

    Think of Influences as what lets the spirit stay on Earth before you think of them as a powerset.

                                                    Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post

                                                    Do you have any other thoughts on the Purified about other ways to interpret them? It's always interesting to hear that sort of stuff. I like the groundwork for this PDF'd version while other things give me some misgivings. More ideas are always good.
                                                    Part of my problem with that take on them is that it completely removes most of the ambiguity and doubt that comes with the question of what the Purified are. By just outright saying "they're spirits/they've become special spirits". Comparing them to Claimed who have permanently fixed themselves to their original body also rubs me the wrong way, since even special snowflake ones shouldn't work like that. They don't get to just reform their original host body from nothing. Like Purified can.

                                                    I also always thought of their Morality/Integrity as also marking how far from slipping into the spirit side of things they are - an additional indicator of the proximity of their minds to animist ephemeral beings. Because, relevant, Spirits do not in fact have an Integrity equivalent, so a Purified's Integrity falling to 0 could very well mean the same to them as losing human-like mentality entirely in favor of a spirit-like one.

                                                    (Though, as a friend pointed out to me, once Werewolf comes out with its 2e, they might benefit more from a modified Harmony.)

                                                    But without wanting to go on a huge tangent and lacking the brain fuel to go into an extended argument over it at this time, that conflict (their bodies and souls become closer to spirits', but how closer?) and how their unique natures can toss them into the middle of many situations involving ephemeral entities and other long lasting supernaturals, are very important parts of the Purified to me.


                                                    On the topic of mechanics, giving them Influences sounds like a doable thing, but I'd actually take more from Bailey's Kigan (because I love the hell out of it and a few things there are ridiculously suited for this) than anything else. Essentially, import the Spirit Court merit covering the favor they can acquire with a specific court, and then allow them to purchase dots of its Influence no greater than the merit rating. As they aren't actually true spirits (even if becoming Purified most definitely makes them closer, and they aren't plain humans anymore spiritually speaking), they'd require a sucess on a roll to succeed on using Influence, with successes equal to damage if used for those purposes.

                                                    As for their projected ephemeral forms, I like the idea of using the simplified attributes. But with the average of each attribute category to determine Power, Finesse and Resistance instead of adding them (capped by whatever), while they retain their skills.
