                                                        I meant more "are there going to be rules in place for reaching the goal of Emperor" rather than "are there going to be rules in place for playing as an Emperor". And right now, the only thing in your version of Staves that has distances is the last dot, which talks about kilometers. (Personally, I think that the distances should stick to either metric or imperial, for sanity reasons.)

                                                        Originally posted by Vent0 View Post

                                                        Awww. No vortex fun or fiery blasts?

                                                        I just like thinking with Portals. And using abilities in inventive ways in general. Like taking logical conclusions to turn transit powers into weapons of mass destruction.

                                                        For the Vortex, its effectiveness is capped at how long you can keep it open.

                                                        For the Sub-Blast, I'd make sure it is very momentary, and just apply a couple Tilts to the are for 1 Turn. Inferno, Blinding Light, Hurricane, etc. Damage wouldn't be that much, due to the heat not having long to work on things. I'd rule the greater the distance, the longer the Principality takes to activate, too (planetary distances are same turn, orbit might be 1 full turn, the Sun might be 3+ Turns). Can a Monarch Walk to Alpha Centauri? Sure! If they feel like channeling Staves 5 for a year or more.

                                                        More fun about thinking with spatial manipulation.

                                                        Stretching the space around the above Vortex (or a Grenade) beyond the "area-of-effect", effectively neutralizes it, since the blast radius is contained inside the "virtual space" you are adding.

                                                        Compressing space already lets you "move" quicker from A to B, and also lets you "shorten" range bands - long can become medium or short, because there is less distance to traverse along the modified trajectory, making Bows more effective.
                                                        So I'm still against this, working on explaining why. When I create a power, it needs to fit five criteria: cool, thematic, realistic, balanced, simple. Vortexes and sun-blasts are cool, certainly. They fit the theme of bending space, and tie into general Monarch themes of outerspace and science. Where they don't fly is the other three.

                                                        Realism is hard to fit, because this is a science fiction power, we don't know what happens when you open a portal to outer space/the sun. However, based on what I know of physics and what other media has done, I can make a guess, and get a feeling on how it should work. Space portals suck you into them. If you're close enough to create it, you're gonna get sucked in because vacuum. It's going to mess with the pressure of the area, probably causing the earth to lose atmosphere and any not-immune-to-vacuum-beings to get the bends, at best. Sun portals are worse, because you've got a massive outpouring of heat and light (even if open briefly) as well as a massive pull of gravity sucking you in. So even Monarchs with their healing powers are getting blinded and fried while yet again getting sucked in.

                                                        Balance is still rough. One use of the power creates a massive number of damaging Tilts, but no other version does. Simplicity is worse, since if sun-blasts and vortexes are included, what about opening portals at the bottom of the ocean? What about at the earth's core? What about into other splat's realms (Shadow, Hedge, Primordial Dream, etc)? How can you tell what sort of portals can be open for long periods, and what sort are only short-term? That's more stuff to create/remember than I as a creator/ST want to deal with.

                                                        So while the suggestions are better, I side on the scale of miles, not planets.
