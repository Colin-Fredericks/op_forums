                                        It might be worthwhile to examine what creatures and themes/subjects the games have covered, to examine what is there and what could be tapped. I fully admit I am not entirely up to date with all the gamelines, so I may be off here.

                                        Vampire: vampires (doi), blood, parasites, hungry darkness for life within others

                                        Werewolf: werewolves, spirits, unquenchable rage, balance within and spiritually

                                        Mage: mages, magic, pride and ego, control over universal forces and the backlash for using said power, insatiable curiosity, ascending power and hard falls for overreaching

                                        Hunter: humanity, fighting an unending battle for the needs of the many, humanity capable of being as bad as the monsters they face if not worse

                                        Changeling: fae, abuse/trauma and recovering, dreams, emotions, deals, equivalent exchange, self-actualization

                                        Promethean: Mary Shelly’s brainchildren, discovering and understanding humanity and all its foibles, creating monsters while trying to become whole, alchemy, journeys

                                        Geist: the undead/unliving, ghosts, remembrance, celebration, making the world (and underworld) a better place, working together between classes and states of living, second chances

                                        Mummy: mummies, ancient history, modern history, memories/amnesia, gods and religion, cults, immortality and the cost therein

                                        Demon: angels and demons, reality and religion not being the sanitized place we are taught, horrors beyond humanity’s scope, history and humanity being fodder for unknown goals, god being as inscrutable as it is beyond morality, espionage, people not being people underneath, sanctuary

                                        Beast: horrors in human form, literally sustaining off terror, family, monsters, dreams (or rather nightmares), fulfilling and subverting expectations, that heroes and villains are not cut and dry (and how heroes can be monsters in their own right), lineages

                                        Deviant: the exploited and experimented, body horror, conspiracies, loss of humanity and self, degradation of humanity and self, anger

                                        This is far from comprehensive, and there are definitely overlaps between the various splats, so one shouldn’t take these as things that cannot be repeated. However, new themes/subjects will definitely make for games that don’t blur together.

                                        As for me? I think the next splat should be about ideas and concepts - what happens to the ideas, fears and hopes we discard as grow older and die? What happens when things stop being remembered, and when momentary whims take on a life of their own? What if the reality you know and depend on isn’t real?

                                        I dunno, it’s late and I should be sleeping. Looking forward to it all the same.

                                                            I can't see another gameline coming any time in the future (then again I had no way of predicting Beast: The Primordial or Deviant: The Renegades).
                                                            What I can see is a number blue books & supplemental material.

                                                            Zombies: they've covered this before, but it might be nice to get a whole book on the subject.

                                                            Magical Girls: not Princess: The Hopeful. While that gameline is good it's more of a dark fantasy than a horror. Let's have a proper horror magical girls book. But please don't build it off of Puella Magi Madoka Magica, that's been done to death.

                                                            Apocalyptic Scenarios: this was touched on in Mirrors & I can see the possibility for more fleshing out of it.

                                                            Infinite Macabre 2.0: rehash the alien creation rules & develop them some more. Provide extra alien species, maybe including a couple of original ones. Starship, planet, colony construction rules & stuff in various flavours.

                                                            Mirrors Fantasy: Dive into the fantasy worlds in greater depth than they managed in Mirrors.

                                                            Mythological Bestiary: A bestiary for creatures/races pulled from mythologies around the world.

                                                            Alternate Takes: personally this sounds like extra Chronicler's Handbooks to me & I am in full support of such an idea. I'm pretty sure we've only got ones for Vampire, Mage, & Werewolf.

                                                            In hindsight the magical girls one sounds like the start of a new gameline.
