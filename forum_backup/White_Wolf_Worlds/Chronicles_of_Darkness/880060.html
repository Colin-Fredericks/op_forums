    Awakening Second Edition FAQ Thread

    05-02-2016, 08:07 AM
    (Mods, please sticky!)

    So, not to detract from the "Ask a Simple Question" threads, but I was fond of the FAQ Bill (the original Mage Developer) kept up for first edition, and I get pounced on with queries everywhere I go on the internet. So, to keep things organised...

    Ask Dave!

    Some rules:

    1) This thread is for posing rules and setting queries for Mage: The Awakening. I will collect them, and put the answers in a Google Doc linked to from the next post.

    2) By posting in this thread, you agree to let me quote your question.

    3) Please do not supply answers to other posters' questions, even if you think the answer's obvious.

    4) Once the FAQ goes up, please check to see if your query has already been answered before. I will organize it by sections corresponding to the chapters of the 2e core, and if we're still going by the time sourcebooks come out, I'll add them too.

    5) I reserve the right to stop this at any time, as I'm not paid to do it.

    6) If possible, provide page references to support your question.

    Dave Brookshaw
