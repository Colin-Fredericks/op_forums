            So from my understanding this is how this spell would need to work at your Sanctum:
            You need Spirit 1, Space 3, Gnosis 2, Ban Rote, and Scry Rote.
            Ban is a 3 dot Space Spell and is a Rote, so you have 3 Free Reach to play with.
            Scry is a 2 Dot Space Spell and is a Rote, so you have 4 Reach to play with.
            Exceptional Luck is on you as a Spell.

            That is 3 Spells, which means you are going to suffer 1 Reach worth of Paradox due to spell control issues.

            You also need to defeat Gauntlet Strength as the following indicates:
            pg. 179 MtAw2E: Any spell cast on a subject on the other side of the Gauntlet or on the Gauntlet itself is Withstood by the Gauntlet Strength.
            Your target is across the Gauntlet so you need to defeat the strength at the location. (Typically 3/4) Let's say Strength of 4.
            You would need to be able to Scry on the target, which I believe just requires a Sympathetic Connection and Yantra along with Sympathetic Range. (1 Reach for Sensory Range & 1 Mana to Cast Sympathetically)
            The Scrying Spell requires a Potency of 8. (4 Gauntlet Strength + 1 Don't Know True Name + 1 for Strong Sympathetic Connection +1 for Additional Withstand).

            Your die pool is the following for this: 2 (Gnosis) + 3 (Space) + 4 (Rote Mudra) -14 (Potency) + 5 (Ritual Casting) + 3 (Willpower) = 3 Die.

            But let's say you lived in the wilderness (for some reason) and thus only need a Potency of 6. (2 Gauntlet Strength + 1 Don't Know True Name + 1 for Strong Sympathetic Connection +1 for Additional Withstand).

            Your die pool is now the following for this: 2 (Gnosis) + 3 (Space) + 4 (Rote Mudra) -10 (Potency) + 3 (Willpower) + 2 (Ritual Casting) = 4 Die. You may be a little tired, but this is doable.

            Next, once you can see the area where your target is located, you need to cast the Ban on that area, which again requires a Potency of at least 3 in order to take effect since it just needs to beat the Gauntlet Strength.
            You need 1 Reach to Cast with Advanced Duration.
            You need 1 Reach to Cast within Sensory Range.
            You need 1 Reach to Cast while viewing Remotely.
            You need 1 Reach to for Advanced Scale.
            You use 1 Mana for the Conditional Duration Factor of +2 granting a Duration of 1 Year.
            You use 1 Mana to reduce the Paradox Roll to 1 Die.
            Total 4 Reach and 2 Mana

            Your die pool for Ban is the following now: 2 (Gnosis) + 3 (Space) + 4 (Rote Mudra) - 4 (Potency) - 2 (Size) = 3 Die w/ Exceptional Luck.

            This will effectively Ban the Rank 5 Creature for sometime with its Clash of Wills equivalent to 5 vs.10.

            For all intents and purposes, mechanically that Idigam is trapped assuming it is unable to have an ally aid the creature.

            You can't make the spell indefinite since it requires the following without risking some serious paradox plus you need higher gnosis:
            Doing so can increase the Duration of a spell, although the mage must still spend Mana and a Reach if Duration becomes indefinite
