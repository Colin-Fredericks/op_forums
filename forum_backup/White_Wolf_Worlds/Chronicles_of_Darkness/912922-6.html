                        Whew, making me work for this one

                        Samuel Albertine:
                        • Who is the City Treasurer and why do you think they have an interesting secret?
                        The Treasurer was speaking at a commencement ceremony at the university. With Mimir's Wisdom I can tell immediately when someone is lying. Every time he spoke about the city's success and finances I could tell that he was lying. I wasn't able to get close enough to press him for more information.
                        • What is the library you work at like?
                        The Library at the university is three stories, open in the center. Samuel spends more time in the library than he does in his own office, roosting on the third floor so he could see over the edge. The restricted section is nearby in a gated off area, Samuel has keys as both a professor, a researcher and he donated some of the first editions in that section.
                        • What books are missing from the restricted section?
                        Several first editions, largely of literary works. What's making Samuel concerned is not that they're in the restricted section (though that is a problem) but also because there copies that aren't first editions in the normal sections of the library. It's possible whoever it is might need the first editions for a particular reason, or wasn't aware that there were other copies. It just seems suspicious.
                        • Do you intend on Punishing whoever is taking these books?
                        It depends on why. If it's an absent minded college student or someone working on the final stretch of a research paper then he'll understand (but pressure them to return what they don't need). If they've hoarded, damaged or (god forbid) sold/destroyed the books then yes, he'll be seeing red.
                        • Can you tell me about a Sin-Eater with whom you have Family Ties?
                        I'll redact the family ties, I thought I had to designate it before trying to create a relationship with one. Samuel is currently trying to track one down to have some guidance through the underworld. So far he's been casting himself blind into the underworld looking for Libraries, but it's been,.. unreliable. He was lucky to discover the library he did for his first chamber. His other attempts have ended either unsuccessfully or with narrow escapes.
                        • How does your Lair connect with Dorian Long's?
                        Burrow from empty chamber to one of his empty chambers. Pending Arcanist's approval of course.
                        • What's the most interesting secret you've ever discovered?
                        The Library Samuel pulled from the underworld had a breadth of information he wasn't familiar with (though as always, the presence of Omen made much of it familiar). The persistence of the dead in the underworld was shocking, as was the discovery that death is nowhere even remotely near the end of the road. However, it was the sections on the Tenemos he found the most intriguing. Not only was public opinion readily visible there, but it could be altered with ideological attacks. Further study is definitely required.
                        • What's your favorite thing about being a Beast?
                        There will never be a limit to what can be discovered, explored and studied. Ever. Humanity, it's origin, destination and deepest depths are all laid bare for a Beast.
                        • What do you believe to be true about the Underworld?
                        Anything destroyed has a chance to fall into the underworld. The chances of which are increased exponentially based on the emotional weight of the event in question. At the time the Hero invaded Omen's Heart he had a fair number of followers with him. His death, the death of many others and the burning of the library should have created a significant enough emotional resonance to dump it as a realm in the underworld. Samuel is looking for the lost Library, to recover his lost knowledge.

                        If he can't then he'll find copies in other libraries or novel information lost from other places. In truth the persistence of the living dead is a near infinite source of information. Samuel wishes to spend a great deal more time there, but without a guide there are functional problems.
                        • Are you scared of death?
                        Not really, Omen will remember Samuel if he dies and he knows that. In a way that's all the immortality he needs. What he's more afraid of is loss, the loss of knowledge that can never be recovered. Samuel can only tap so much of Omen's knowledge in his lifetime but finding physical copies of the books is a different matter.
                        • Have you ever killed someone?
                        Samuel, no, Omen, yes. Samuel loathes all things physical, but he wouldn't hesitate to electrocute someone or blow them out the window with Storm Ravaged if he needed to. He doesn't have a lot of attachment to human life as valuable, largely it's that no one has given him enough of a reason to put the effort in as of yet. If he was in a situation where it would be advantageous to kill someone with physical means, he probably wouldn't. Too brutish.
                        • What do you normally have for breakfast?
                        Samuel is English on his mothers side so he's inherited some of their mannerisms. Breakfast is normally an eggy in a basket (egg on toast) and a cup of english breakfast tea. Two if it's been a long night.

                                        Alright, so, in order:

                                        Dorian has Eyes of Heaven as one of his first Atavisms; seeing people's weaknesses, mundane or otherwise, comes naturally to him. Little things like Vices make perfect sense to him, but when details like "Cold Iron" or "Unable to tell a lie" come up, that's going to make him think twice. The natural senses of the Begotten that allow them to detect Kin paired with his exceptionally keen eyes as an Ugallu and a dragon enabled him to figure out that a not-inconsiderable proportion of his existing clients were waking up screaming from nightmares that he had absolutely nothing to do with. He has a couple other odd customers, but he gravitated towards Changelings because of their natural inclination towards dreams, and also because of his own specialty in treating trauma. From there, things spread by word of mouth, as he started specializing in serving as a safe place to talk about the kinds of things that would get you committed if you told them to another psychiatrist, jumpstarting the healing process in a way conventional therapy wouldn't. His honesty, willingness to reveal his true self, and rigid adherence to the ethical standards of a practicing therapist make him a trustworthy figure in the eyes of the Lost.

                                        The Chicago Freehold is (because I'm unimaginative right now and would rather not dictate a massive part of the setting) a Seasonal Court (unless you say otherwise). Dreaming Chicago bleeds into the Skein and the Hedge as well, and the Changelings find it rather bothersome. Hobgoblins and more vile fae creatures find plenty of shelter in the structures in the Hedge, and many a cursed bauble emerges from dream Bastions to litter the Hedge. In some groves where Bastions are established the ground is littered with the refuse of torturous dreams. The intense nightmares make it hard to harvest Glamour from dreams, forcing the Freehold to turn to more exploitative means of harvesting it, though they'd rather not. The Autumn Court is highly accepting of Dr. Thorn and what he is, but there is a sizeable (quiet) undercurrent that can't help but notice the similarities between Beast and Gentry. They still come to him, though. He's the best gig in town. Autumn in particular is powerful here because of Dreaming Chicago, and the rest of the Freehold trusts their knowledge. Winter calls for caution and is constantly on high alert, while Summer is intent on patrolling the Hedge and Dreaming Chicago diligently- trying to root out whatever grows there. Spring enjoys the idea of Dreaming Chicago, as it enables them to establish long-standing, expansive Hollows that verge on becoming Hedge Towns. They even have Changeling-specific nightclubs in these Hollows that are built around places where Bastions clump together, enabling groups of Lost to party, gather as much Glamour as they can stomach, and really cut loose. The rest of the Freehold quietly whispers that the rulership of Spring is infested with Loyalists.

                                        Dorian, though he really wants to be a part of the Freehold, distances himself because so many members of the relatively small, tight-knit community come to him for treatment, and the code of ethics prevents him from establishing multiple relationships with patients to avoid jepordizing their healing process. That, and he strongly suspects the existence of the pervasive element of the Freehold that hates and fears him for what he is. That hurts, but he can at least understand. He fears his own Horror and its tastes sometimes.

                                        This one I haven't thought much about, but I imagine several high-ranking members of the Courts discreetly come to him. This is another reason he, for now, avoids becoming an Oathbound member of the Freehold- accusations that he is abusing his position of authority to puppeteer the leadership. Dorian, of course, never would, but he would need to wait at least two years after finishing his treatment before considering entering their lives in a different capacity, and he likely won't finish anytime soon. The Changelings are too good a source of food. Perhaps in a few years he'll move on to Mages.

                                        The Horror would like him to collect mementos of kills, people brought down by their own weaknesses. Dorian himself has determined that all he really needs to subsist off is the shock of having all your weaknesses exposed, and specifically an admission of weakness. He can take the information, but he isn't an Anakim- that doesn't do it for him. He's an Ugallu, he needs his patients to admit that they've been caught and to admit to their own weaknesses. Dorian has absolutely no problem with this, as admission of a problem or a flaw can be cathartic, and it allows for a healthier healing process. This almost certainly triggers Clarity Breaking Points in his patients, but with Dorian's genuine psychiatric treatment and his social finesse in setting up the admission, he hasn't had a patient deteriorate on him, yet. Most come leave with higher Clarity than they did when they came in. To be perfectly clear- he collects admissions of weakness and specifically feeds off of the moment when someone realizes they have been found out and a secret taken from them; they have no choice but to come clean.

                                        Dorian unwinds from therapy by indulging his own hobbies, reading obsessively on the occult and trying to track down ancient legends of the Begotten and possibly trace the Dark Mother throughout the millennia. He tinkers with theories that will likely never see the light of day outside the Leaden Mirror, Samuel's Lair, or a Mysterium Athanaeum, but he intends to write and publish comprehensive papers on the true nature of dreams, the mind, and the soul- even if his only readers will be a privileged, weird, few.

                                        Dorian hasn't really been in any kind of extended romantic relationship, or any seriously close friendships. The altered psychology of the Begotten and the Eyes of Heaven make it hard to connect with his human friends and family. He still sees a couple patients from before his Devouring, some he's seen for over six years, but humans don't see their own massive traumas and changes quite as well as a Changeling might. He wants to connect, he really does, but his nature and his training persuades him to isolate. Samuel is probably his closest friend and he's more a colleague than anything.

                                        Dreaming Chicago is what happens when a city becomes a massive part of someone's identity and even becomes an important character in local folklore. It's Temenos Realm is so close to the Skein that it bleeds through, and even taints the Hedge. He imagines something similar is in place in cities like Detroit or New York.

                                        Dorian lives in a relatively cozy, but small, apartment near the university. It's sparsely furnished and clean in a way only a space that hasn't been lived in can be, with one obvious area of omission. He has turned the small living room into a virtual library, crammed with bookshelves filled to the brim with psychology books, occult texts, and works in mythology and folklore. He especially loves Jung and Campbell, but for very different reasons. Something that might be easier to miss is a little shrine tucked beside his bed, something oddly primal and crude for the apartment of an erudite intellectual. He actually spends a lot of time in his Lair, meditating by walking the Chambers like a Labyrinth and contemplating the much larger shrine to the Dark Mother within the Heart. He occasionally goes to bother Samuel, though only if he's stuck with a problem- Dorian of all people understands the importance of boundaries.

                                        Dorian is certain that Nathaniel hasn't, and likely never will, get over his original traumas. He ended treatment because he believes that continuing wouldn't help, but Dorian is at least relieved that he was able to help Nathaniel come to terms with the reality of his situation. He admires Nathaniel's dedication to teaching Lessons, but is worried that he isn't establishing enough of a boundary between himself and his Horror and that it isn't healthy for him to think of the Horror as his deceased rabbit.

                                        If he met the Dark Mother? Likely fight the urge to abandon his mortal life entirely and learn at her feet. He binds himself to mankind with the rigidity of his ethical code, but the young dragon in his soul yearns to see Mother again. During his Devouring he felt her presence, somewhere far off, a Horror to a Horror, loving and accepting and grim and terrible. He can only describe it as a religious experience. Dorian wants to know the truth, and she's one of the few beings in existence that likely has answers. She's the one that broke him down and revealed his hubris, but she accepted him anyways and adopted him as her own child. That must count for something.

                                                        And those are some peculiar questions. As they say: Never give the Storyteller Ideas. Well screw this, I'm gonna do it either way!
                                                        • Q: Tell me about this hunter cell you lead. Who are its members? A: The cell, at the moment consists of Elias and 5 other members. 2 of them are on the Cheiron Group payroll, other 3 are Union. The Cell originally formed as a pure Union one, but two of their members were later offered job at Cheiron and accepted. The Cheiron employees, equipped with any taumatech you see fit, are: Jorge Dispes, a retired (By the ways of getting too close to going Dirty Harry) cop with experience dealing with organized crime, mainly drug cartels, who got into the Vigil after his last case ended in his partner's death by a Claimed junkie, and Nicholas Lodderberg, sport archer formerly with a passion for mundane hunting, who got into the Vigil after barely escaping alive from a Werewolf Pack whose territory he entered when hunting. The Union Members are: Colette Lynx, a high-school Design & Technology teacher who took up the Vigil after her sister started attending a vampire night club and subsequently became one of the Kindred's walking blood bank and slave (the vampire in question later got "visited" by a very pissed Elias and carefully explained why doing such things is bad for you), Ronald Werrety, an IT geek, freelance programmer and ridiculously skilled hacker whose candle was lit after he found out that the stuff that caused his dad to go bankrupt and ultimately broke his family wasn't exactly 100% human, and last but not least, George Vincent, MD, a surgeon who had waaay too many cases of supernatural injuries at his operating table not to start wondering. The cell's main base of operations and territory is Pill Hill, but they do stuff all over the city.
                                                        • Q:How did you end up heading a cancer cell anyway? A: The situation was actually pretty simple. He lives in the same neighborhood as the rest of the cell, and it's not that secret that he is a former military. Which means that when the Cell needed someone with experience similiar to his, to help with the "Demonic Possession" problem a few years back, they came to him. And he, seeing an opportunity for having some fun while also possibly dealing some collateral damage to sate the Douchelizard Wyvern, accepted. After the matter has been dealt with, he told them to contact him if they're ever doing something like that again. Well, the point of organized monster hunting is to do such things more often, and so after a few such situations, he was officially accepted into the cell.
