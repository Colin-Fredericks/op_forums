                Seriously... An eldritch, unkillable creature with some perifieral connections to the Lovecraft Mythos who has minons steeped in occult lore in various states of not being human anymore hiding away a precious magical item of personal importance in a "Dead Man's Chest" as a result of dealings with a ancient, capricious, and dangerous deity? Can you get a character who is any more like one of the Arisen in a mainstream movie without falling back on the trappings of Ancient Egypt?

                In general, I'm personally disenclined to credit PotC with the popularity of this era for exactly this reason. That mythology is broadly applicable across every game that CofD has produced. No blockbusters needed.

                            Since we are in a holding pattern for those last few dollars before funding, here are some more expansion ideas:

                            1600 - Demon, dramatic changes in concepts of god and science cause angels to fall inadvertently
                            French Revolution - Promethean for panicky mobs, fear everywhere, and senseless disquiet
                            Seven Wonders - Three words... Parthian Zoastrian Hunters
                            Seven Wonders - Werewolf for "minotaurs" aka updated Baal-Hadad
                            1001 Nights - Hunter for Ahl al-Jabal, including the Ismaili disporia after the Mongol Invasions
                            Last Imperials - Demon for 1860s industrialization and the Mandate of Heaven
                            WWI - Innocents in France, Belgium, and Germany for a look at life behind the trench lines
                            WWI - Hurt Locker Microtemplates, particularly the Infected and the Psychic Vampire
                            Pirates - Demon since deals deals with the Devil are a pirate staple
                            Pirates - Inferno for the same reason
                            Pirates - Mummies for relic hunting and buried treasure
                            Pirates - Werewolf for updated Brineborn
                            Pirates - Hunter for non-European compacts and conspiracies

                            Also... please can we have updated Nidmuzug in the WWI chapter? That would be awesome!
