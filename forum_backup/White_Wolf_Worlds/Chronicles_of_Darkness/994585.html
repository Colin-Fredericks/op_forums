                                  Well, if memory serves, the Seers and Diamond mages would be serving together on the same ship because of a Nameless order they joined together to defeat during the golden age of piracy. If I remember the Nameless order in question founded or controlled Nassau and they were doing something super bad wrong fun so the other wizardly lads and ladies came to wreck their day. As such, I could see the majority of the crew being Adamantine Arrow, the Pratorians and the Guardians of the Veil's more combat focused members. The Captain would probably be a mixed group of the Hegemonic, the Paternoster and the Silver Ladder. Meanwhile, the Mysterium, the Panopticon probably both have attaches there to try and manage the allocation of spoils and to screw one another over trying to acquire the spoils of the war.

                                  All in all, the boat probably barely goes anywhere, arrives several months late thanks to the crew arguing over the best courses, and as soon as they finish fighting the locals, immediately explodes as both sides of the orcale/exarch argument immediately begin trying to defeat the other.

                                              Vampire: I homebrewed this a while back

                                              Changeling: Trinica Dracken. And if you haven't read Tales of the Ketty Jay imagine Elizabeth Swan's being traumatised and broken by her time on the Black Perl and transforming herself into something like Blackbeard from Pirates of the Caribbean 4 in order to survive life on the high seas.

                                              Mage: "Pirate society is free and democratic while the empires are autocratic and allow slavery". "But capt'n, our society relies on the power of our guns and cutlass. Has power corrupted us into just another form oppression for the defenceless working classes. Maybe we should use our wealth to rejoin society and become abolitionists". "Quite you, point that philosophising at the Seers!"

                                              [Late Edit:] Hunter - the Royal navy / Spanish navy. Yes they're agents of oppressive empires, but you're pillaging murderous pirates so you don't exactly have the moral high ground and unlike those empires the various monster governments wont get round to outlawing slavery at the end of the age of piracy.

                                              Sin Eaters: "So do we raid a Spanish treasure fleet or shall we sail to China and see the sites", "Well you only live twice, lets go to China. I don't want to get stuck into a routine."

                                              Beast: Long John Silvers from Treasure Island. He takes you under his wing, is genuinely nice to you, but none of that excuses the fact he's a ruthless murdering pirate. [late edit] It also doesn't absolve him of the fact that everything horrible that happened to you is his fault, he did hire the crew of cut-throats.

                                              Princess: A swashbuckling robin hood type of Pirate who wondered in from a Disney pirate story and is determined not to let the fact she's now in an HBO pirate story wear hear down.

                                              Leviathan: The villain. All the other gamelines are busy crewing the Black Perl, while the Leviathan is Davy Jones.
