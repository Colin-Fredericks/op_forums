                    Thanksgiving is over; it is the day of the hike. But not all goes as planned for the Dark Haven Bluffs Krewe.

                    Doc’s player had to miss due to work related emergency, so Sara carries the bulk of the session. Natalie’s journal is a fiction that takes place at the beginning of the session. Alice’s journal is similar, but it covers an actual conversation that happened at the table and her reactions to it.

                    Let’s see what happened.

                    Session CXLV

                    Sara’s Letters

                    Sasha -

                    Even the good times must come to an end. Still, while Thanksgiving has become a fond memory, there is Christmas to look forward to, and the present to live in. And oh what a present. I may have spent the night with a certainly ruggedly handsome storytelling wanderer. And had I, it most certainly would have been wonderful.

                    It also would have been a wonderful ending to a day of truths and goodbyes. It was the day of our hiking trip, since yesterday we got rained out, and so I wasn’t there in person to see Julie and younger Natalie off at the airport. Karen was though, so Natalie wasn’t alone, which was good. I understand it went fine and that plans are in the works for us to visit them for Christmas.

                    The hiking was a lot of fun. Alice, Eris, Noriko, Jacob and I left early from the Bluffs to make the most of the day. We met my competition, Tanya and Deirdre there, but I did get to sit next to Jacob on the crowded back seat all the way there and back. Deidre’s tight shirt and pants had our little Red blushing as soon as she saw her and I think she was red the whole trip, just about. Can’t say I blame her - Deirdre was looking super hot. Tanya was more understated, but was still pretty head turning. I should make sure to check if Jacob plans on taking them up on their request for a goodbye visit on his way out. And if I can come along. I’m sure I could get a ride back home.

                    Of course, it wasn’t all splashing in the river, climbing down cliff faces and flirting. Poor Alice had wanted to keep the fact that she, and nearly all of her friends, were Touched, but an incident came up with one of the Touched aspects of the rangers’ jobs and it kinda wasn’t really an option anymore. He took it well, and I think it’s good for her, in the end. Being truthful and open about who you are usually is. She seemed more relaxed, and even more open to going to visit her parents. Not that she’ll tell them she’s Touched, I imagine. But it kinda puts coming out as a lesbian in a different perspective.

                    Once Alice got done cleaning up and thus gave Noriko and Eris the courage to be in the same room as some serious flirting, we headed home to find the others had their own adventures. Doc and Karen went to visit her brother so she could get to have some blood-family time. It went well, according to Natalie. She and Alexia had dealt with a ‘minor breakdown’ of Clarissa’s. She’s apparently not dealing well with the increasing independence of her sidekick Deanna and blames Natalie for it. I’m glad I was never that silly. Not that I didn’t have my own brand of silly. Come to think of it, I wonder if Emcee’s patented ‘knock their heads together’ technique would come in handy...

                    Natalie was more worried about Tatum and I though. She and Karen are thinking about having a more committed thing with her and she still resents me a little - she was apparently on her way up the relationship ladder when I showed up and ‘cut in line’. As far as I can tell, she doesn’t have a serious problem with me. I mean, when Natalie started asking, I did realize we’d never been just the two of us fooling around, but... eh? Not everyone has to love me. I was mostly just confused, since at first it seemed like Natalie was asking if I had a problem with including her, which of course I don’t. But she was more asking if I thought Tatum would have a problem, which is dangerous, cause while I am pretty empathetic, and can read crowds like a born performer, I just don’t get jealousy. I’m pretty sure it’ll be fine though? Tatum’s gonna be around for a few days, helping keep Natalie stable, so I guess we’ll see.

                    And so the holiday ends and we get back to normal, or as close as we crazy kids get. Only the fates know what madness comes next. Just the way I like it.

                    Sara


                    Alice’s Reflections

                    Jacob and I sit side-by-side on a fallen log in Sleeping Giant Park as Eris, Sara and the others move away a bit, giving us some privacy. My clothes are still splattered in blood from the awful rat spirits Eris, Deirdre, Tanya and I just killed, and Jacob keeps glancing nervously at me. His fingers are drumming a quick beat on his knee, a sure sign he's freaking out inside. Still, all things considered, he's holding up pretty well. I mean, he just saw Sara burst into flames and Noriko turn into a hulking cat beast to save him from another one of the rat-things. Most people would be pretty messed up right now, but Jacob's a lot stronger than most people.

                    We sit awkwardly for several moments. I have no idea what to say. I'm still trying to organize my thoughts, but Jacob speaks first.

                    "So…uh…your friend is a cat monster." There's a tremble to his voice, but he's hiding it well, trying to make light of things.

                    "A werecat. Yeah. But she's all right. I wouldn't have run off and left you like that if she and Sara hadn't been there to protect you. I just…I thought Eris and the others might need my help."

                    "Need your help? What, with more of those…those rat things?"

                    I nod. "Eris, Deirdre and the other rangers protect this park from things like that. I guess, now that I'm with Eris, I'm sort of an honorary member of the pack, so I thought I should help out."

                    "The pack?"

                    "Um…yeah. Eris and the other rangers are werewolves. But not like in the movies. I mean, yes, they can turn into big wolf beasts, but they're good guys."

                    Jacob's eyes widen and he turns to look me in the eyes. "Holy shit. Holy shit Alice. They bit you didn't they? They turned you into a werewolf…"

                    "What? No!" I quickly try to allay his fears. "It's not like that. It doesn't even work like that. I'm not a werewolf."

                    "But…how did you kill those rat-things?"

                    I sheepishly pull the wrappings off the head of my axe, which is strapped onto the side of my pack. "I've gotten pretty good with this thing," I say, cautiously. "All those years chopping firewood on the farm paid off, I guess."

                    Jacob runs a hand across his close-cropped hair. "Holy shit, Alice. I knew something was different about you. You've changed. But if you're not a…a werewolf…what the hell—.."

                    "I died, Jacob," I interrupt my brother. "And then I came back. I was out caving and—."

                    Jacob put up a hand to stop me. "You don't have to tell me. I don't want to know. I can't deal with the image of my kid sister…dead."

                    I bow my head, not really sure what to say next.

                    "But you came back?" Jacob asks. "How? Did you make a deal with the devil or something? Some sort of crossroads thing?"

                    "Uh...sort of? But not with the devil. When I died, a really powerful ghost appeared and offered me a second chance. She was a Navajo witch once. I didn't want to die, so I took her offer and she brought me back. Now she's a part of me, riding along in my head, I guess. But don't worry! She doesn't control me or anything. She just talks to me. And it turns out coming back from the dead with a ghostly patron gives you all sorts of supernatural strength. I can do a lot of things normal people can't now. I can hold my own with a pack of werewolves, anyway."

                    Jacob takes a deep breath and lets it out slowly. He stares at the ground for several long, tense moments before speaking again.

                    "So, the people at the mansion, are they like you?"

                    "Yes. Well, some of them anyway. Sara, Doc, Natalie, Simon and Alexia. They all died too, and they have their own ghosts. We try to help people by dealing with dangerous spirits and other thing like that. Sometimes we help ghosts by righting the wrongs that are keeping them from passing on. We usually tell people we're paranormal investigators."

                    Jacob shakes his head, trying to come to terms with everything I'm telling him. His fingers are still tapping a desperate rhythm on his knee.

                    "I'm trying to do the right thing, Jacob. I know this is all really weird, but you have to believe that."

                    Jacob exhales heavily, then nods his head decisively like he's made up his mind. He stops tapping his fingers and claps a hand on my knee, then looks me straight in the eye. "I do, Freckles. I don't know what to make of all this, but your people here seem like good people. Strange and kinda scary, but good. And you're still my kid sister. I trust you. Nothing's going to change that."

                    My eyes start to tear up. Jacob hates to see me cry, so he folds me in a warm hug. I rest my chin on his shoulder. I wish I could express to my brother just how grateful I am that he can be so understanding.

                    I wonder how understanding he would be if he knew the whole truth? Would your brother still accept you if he knew you were a blood-soaked killer? You and I have forged our path, dear Alice. And it is a solitary one. We walk a grim road, and there's no place for us among family or friends. Tell your brother about the people you've murdered, Alice, and see the truth behind my words.

                    The Huntress' words cut deep and slice away the sense of peace Jacob's acceptance has given me. I start to cry, but they're tears of regret.


                    Natalie’s Tweets

                    Homecoming IV

                    The four women walked through the Tweed New Haven Airport, three in black one in white. They were smiling, but a sadness lingered in the air around them.

                    “I still greatly appreciate he song,” the eldest said as they walked. “I can’t believe you remembered that.”

                    “It was the first song I learned to play by ear, mom,” her daughter said. “For you.”

                    “You were always so sweet when you wanted to be.” The older woman seemed amused by her own comment.

                    As they approached the gate, Julie Lousler turned around to her daughter and her daughter-in-law. “It’s been an amazing trip. Life changing, even. You will pass my best wishes on to Sara won’t you?”

                    “Of course!” Natalie Knight smiled. “Sorry about the change in plans, but the hike was put off till today. Sara sends her love.” Natalie looked from her mother to her daughter, “To both of you.”

                    Julie smiled. “Ah Jacob. That boy in a house full of girls is trouble. Even if mischief isn’t his nature, with so many attractive women around he will find it or it will find him. Either way, I doubt he will be unhappy.”

                    “Yea…well it was good to see everyone,” Natalie Lousler shifted uncomfortably. She was not good at goodbyes in the best of times and really hated leaving her mother behind. Again. Despite her demeanor, she had also enjoyed herself, and she was already thinking of her next trip up. Her grandmother, however, was thinking of something closer.

                    “So, you three will be coming down when?” Julie asked. Her daughter looked at her phone. “The Winter Solstice is on the 21st. I expect we will head your way on the 22nd. If all is well with you, we will stay through the New Year and leave maybe the second or third.”

                    “That sounds good I…would rather have you there when the ball dropped. It had become a…tainted sight to me.” Julie was careful with her words.

                    “I’m sorry.”

                    “You are here now. That’s what matters. Do not dwell on regrets. Regret is something I know well. Take care not to cling to it, to hold it so close that it poisons your soul."

                    “I…of course.”

                    Julie chuckled. “Sorry something I was told long ago. So…that fourth girl, Tatum. Is she coming?” Julie smiled slightly as Natalie’s eyes winded in shock, though her face otherwise betrayed nothing. Her namesake stopped her silent urging to move on to the bag check at that as well. “What?”

                    “You can’t fool me. I see how you and Tatum look at each other. I see how Tatum and Karen interact. I know love when I see it. I know you both love her, and she you.”

                    “I…” Natalie paused, “Tatum has never…committed to a relationship with us. We do care but she…” Natalie looked helplessly at her wife, “I don’t…”

                    Karen Knight cleared her throat, “Tatum has been close to us, and we have had dinner with the Gillises a few times now. There has been talk, but we have not felt…we don’t want to rush into anything, you understand. But you are right, there is something there, and it is not purely physical.”

                    Karen’s wife shifted her foot uncomfortably. Karen looked at her and back to Julie, “Also, Natalie is not great with commitment.” Julie smiled as Natalie looked up sharply.

                    “Well, if you decide to admit what you know and be open about being in love with her, she is welcome too. In fact I would like to know her as I do Karen and Sara,” Julie chuckled again, “I have so many daughters now.”

                    “Yea,” Natalie looked between Karen and Julie. “I think I will call her when we get back. Maybe we should have the conversation she wants to have. Has wanted to have.” Karen put a hand on Natalie’s shoulder, “Only if you are ready sweetheart.”

                    “I will leave that to you, my dear. It’s about time to go. I will go home, and get ready for Christmas. The tree will go up, and I will look forward to having you there around it.”

                    “Okay,” her daughter said smiling slightly, “But leave the choir angel ornaments for me to put up. That’s always been my job, remember?”

                    “I know.” Julie’s eyes twinkled, “They have not gone up since you left.”

                    And the crying started all over again.

                    End Session CXLV

                    Man, all this crying, not cool.

                    The rat spirits fought by the pack and Alice were Beshilu, and despite having superior numbers (roughly 2 to 1) they could not cope with the Uratha or Alice, routinely rolling chance dice or single “real” dice to hit. I expected it would go fine, but I was slightly surprised again at what killing machines 2e Uratha were. Surprised but not in any way bothered, mind. Alice was able to keep up, but Alice is also a very combat oriented Sin-Eater.

                    There is a whole scene not really covered where Natalie confronts Clarissa in the mall lot, with Alexia there to keep things “civil.” It began with Clarissa accusing Natalie of seducing and corrupting Deanna. Natalie used the defense that Deanna had in fact asked her to the party, felt her up at the party of her own accord, invited her back to her place, and initiated sex there. Of course, Natalie is rather manipulative, though Clarissa did not know her MO. Still, the factor that had set Clarissa off was that Deanna, once again, wanted to go out by herself and say little of what she was doing, like she had the weekend before with Natalie, and Clarissa assumed she was going out with Natalie. She wasn’t. It came down to the fact that Deanna and Clarissa had been inseparable for around a decade, down to Deanna being Clarissa’s wingman, and even her lookout when Clarissa was making out, and now she had slept with someone (well, period, it was the first time either had had sex) who was not someone Clarissa had flirted with and gotten tired of, like all Deanna’s previous boyfriends. Still, it was not just Clarissa being selfish…Deanna was her best friend, and she relied on her, and she felt Natalie was taking her away. While there was some yelling and at one point it looked like there may be a fight, Alexia managed to keep things calm. Natalie promised to talk to Deanna about not cutting her friend loose and keeping her as part of her life. She confessed she had no idea what Deanna was up to, and it was not her business; she and Deanna were friends, not really lovers, but she understood how Clarissa felt.

                    This going back and forth with the battle in the park and scenes of Sara, Noriko, and Jacob chatting and eventually their encounter with the Beshilu straggler.

                                            Session CXLVI

                                            Sara’s Letters

                                            Sasha -

                                            Well, a day that promised mischief and fun at breakfast has taken a serious turn. I can’t really complain - I just missed out on watching Noriko and Chandra conspire against Doc, poor Jacob missed out on quality time with the ladies at Sleeping Giant. He’s left town now, headed back out on the road. I hope he’ll be back. I’m sure he will.

                                            And Alice will probably forgive me. Not that she was upset, just a little, well, embarrassed, I think. It was a kinda of awkward breakfast, between that and Tatum coming down before my lovelies. She relaxed once I was there though. Even Doc’s meditative vibe was getting dinged with curiosity, since Noriko and Chandra were whispering about a ‘master plan’, giggling, and glancing at him. All that got pushed aside though, when Zed called Doc just after breakfast.

                                            I haven’t mentioned Zed before - he’s one of the ‘family’ of Touched that Angelique and Eris belong to. Angelique has been recovering nicely, but for safety’s sake had been staying with another pair of the same, Austin and Erin Callahan. And late yesterday, Angelique had discovered they were missing. Maybe not the most alarming thing, but given the circumstances, there was a buddy/check-in system in place, and they had skipped out on that. Plus, they weren’t answering their phone.

                                            The reason Zed called Doc was not the one we might have wanted, that being so that we could help out. See, the two Callahans, Zed, Malard and Angelique are one unit. Eris and the other folks at the Sleeping Giant are another. And there’s a little bit of a rivalry. And Connecticut is kinda chilly this time of year. So... they wanted to talk with Eris, as a potential suspect. Doc too, to a lesser extent, cause there’s specific beef between him and Erin.

                                            So that was tense. And worrisome. Natalie sent Karen and Tatum out. Alice asked Jacob to leave a little early. He was going to today anyway, just this afternoon. He really didn’t want to leave, especially when she said there might be danger - he’s quite the hero - but I managed to convince him she was safe, and that she would be destroyed if something happened to him because of her.

                                            The meeting itself actually went pretty well. Except for Noriko glaring serious daggers at Angelique, which pissed our Little Red off. It was heartwrenching to hear Angelique talking with Alice. The poor dear. Honestly, I’m not entirely sure Malard’s group is going to weather the current storm. I hope we can do something to get her safe haven if they don’t. With the way Noriko’s acting, I don’t know if here will be one though.

                                            Anyway, shit kept getting shoveled into the fan in the here and now. Zed wanted to check up on our story about the hike, and when he did, Deirdre started going off on him. Ildefonso was trying to make a deal to work out a momentary setting aside of the rivalry, and she went off on him. Eris tried to talk her down, and she even went off on her. So Alice and Eris went up to the Sleeping Giant to see what was up there while we checked out the house the Callahan’s disappeared from.

                                            it was quite a party since Chandra, Angelique, Zed, Malard, and all the Touched of the troupe minus Alice and Noriko were there. Noriko had stormed off and not come back after Alice called her on being unkind to Angelique and it seemed best to let it stand at that. At the house we got right down to it. Since my Sight gives me visions of the past, and Alexia’s lets her ask specific questions of an object’s memory, I went first. The fragments of visions I saw were intriguing - especially the one where Erin argued with her brother in the kitchen, naked - but I could only focus on one, and the one that was most recent, the one with no emotional overtones, seemed most likely to give evidence of spiritual tampering. It showed Austin stopping mid-dish-drying, moving purposefully for the door. Erin joined him there and they left together without speaking to each other.

                                            I tried to trace back her steps. The next vision came in her office, where she’d been Googling dietary needs in early pregnancy. It painted a fairly clear, if lurid, picture. They were blood relatives, distant cousins, only adopted siblings, but there was also the taboo against sexual relationships among their mystical ‘family’. And between Alexia’s questions, and the fact that Angelique had been living there a while and they were... not quiet, it was clear who the father was. According to Angelique, they two aren’t a couple, it’s just that they fight a lot, and their fights have something of an aftermath. I, personally, think there are worse ways to end a fight, but...

Those problems go way, way beyond rivalry. I had to hide behind our Little Red when Doc even mentioned Raul - I thought the two groups first united effort might be to tear through Doc and maybe not stop with him. Angelique, however, reminded everyone of ancient pacts (not with each other - the groups hate each other too much for that, but with old spirits) and Zed kept them focused on the real enemy, so everything was peachy.

If one must go to Glenwood, then as part of a caravan of powerful Touched people, in Alice’s car, being held tightly by my lovely Natalie, is probably the best way to go.

Fate willing, we’ll get some soon.
