        Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
        If you want a non-canon answer with some precedence though, it's not completely inconceivable that a supernatural entity could make a Deviant if they can successfully step away from their supernatural side enough for the process, in the same way supernaturals could make Prometheans if they stepped away from their supernatural selves and relief on good ol' fashioned raw human desperation for the entire process. As an example, a mage could conceivably make a Deviant if they didn't rely on magic throughout the process. Since Divergence isn't rooted as strongly in that raw desperation as Promethean's generation is, though, it's debateable how much that would affect it.
        I'm not convinced that this works. Supernaturals can't make Prometheans because the Divine Fire is the only source of Prometheans, and the Fire doesn't play well with other supernatural powers. But the Coactive Clade of Deviants are expressly what happens when you combine Divergence's damage to a soul with esoteric power sources - and ephemera are on the list of sources. It seems to me that supernatural beings experimenting with their powers could perfectly well make a Deviant. It's not by any means the normal result, but it's still possible.

        Which is to say, if bog-standard humans can do something, that's no reason whatsoever to conclude that supernaturals therefore can't do it, or that they have to do it the same way humans do. And the Deviant syndrome is, in principle, something any type of supernatural power can cause, not something that calls for one specific type.

                    09-22-2019, 11:00 PM
                    Originally posted by Primordial newcomer View Post

                    if it's ok with you, may you share how it felt to see your idea be realized in less than a day (referencing the successful kickstarter for Deviant)
                    I've been working on this game in some capacity for more than a year, and my family feels like I've done practically nothing else since December, so saying it was "realized in less than a day" seems a bit weird to me. *laughs*

                    I'm thrilled with how things have been going so far, and I'm really hoping to see the Stretch Goals I helped plan come into reality so that I can add more cool toys to the base game. Like, really, really hoping. With outlines. And a list of freelancers I definitely want to have involved. So I'm really delighted by the positive response Deviant has gotten, so far, and I'm looking forward to having a chance to share some of the crunchier parts of the system, which tends to be where "substance" has to live up to the "style" of those early chapters (at once an exciting and terrifying prospect!).

                    Regarding major templates creating Deviants, I'm not going to slam the door on that possibility, but I'm not doing it in the core. I'm likely to address crossover in some form of a Deviant Storytellers Guide. But there won't be a "Create Deviant" spell for mages or a "Rite of the Broken Soul" for vampires. Deviants are the exceptions, the failures, the accidents, the unexpected (but largely unrepeatable) successes. Even conspiracies who "mass-produce" or "carefully screen" candidates have lots of failed experiments. If you tap into ineffable power or seek to achieve the impossible, most of the time you just fail, and your test subject meets a messy end (and if that test subject is you, you become a cautionary tale told to future generations), but sometimes something unexpected happens. That's a Deviant. Regardless of how it happens or why, the Divergence is never a completely controlled process with predictable results - not even when Masters of Death and Spirit (or the God-Machine) get involved with it.

                    Onyx Path Freelancer and former Deviant line developer.

                        Part of being agnostic is that while you can't confirm the involvement of a particular metaphysics, you can't reject it either. Take the Cainite Heresy: their Endowment is fueled by the metaphysical properties of blood. Is it fueled by the same properties that manifest as Vitae in a vampire? Maybe; maybe not. And then there's the Cheiron Group.

                        I don't think that Dave meant that nothing that empowers Deviants comes from any other gameline's metaphysics; only that Deviant won't explicitly tie it together, and there will always be the (additional?) factor of a cracked soul that sets Deviants apart from other modified humans (ghouls, wolfbloods, Proximi, and so on). In particular, I wouldn't rule out, say, a ghoul-like Deviant that was created by processing Vitae and injecting it into a subject in a way that triggers a Divergence. It just wouldn't be a ghoul; that's a separate thing.

                                                The thing about crossover Divergences in Deviant (as opposed to crossover games, like, where a Remade just shows up in a Mage game or something - those work fine) is that it’s not just a matter of the Variations’ and Scars’ wordcount. You have to balance “my Conspiracy is headed by Vampires” and suchlike in the Conspiracy mechanics as well. That’s why we didn’t do it in the corebook, and why I asked no one do it in Contagion Chronicle.

                                                But it could be done, and nothing stops Eric devoting the space for it in a future book. You could have a Form per major splat, writeups of how much a vampire coterie or Promethean throng are “worth” in Conspiracy-building points, Variations that explicitly link into the ephemeral entity rules (there weren’t any last I saw the game, because the entity rules weren’t in it) that sort of thing.

                                                After all, Eric’s the one IIRC who invented the arrangement between the God Machine and Exarchs. You are in safe hands crossover-wise.
