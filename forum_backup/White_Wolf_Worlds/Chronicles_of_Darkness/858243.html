    Face in the Crowd

    04-03-2016, 05:46 AM
    Hi, I need some clarification about this power. It says:

    "Unless the vampire’s doing something to draw people’s attention — pulling a gun, or screaming at people — or he’s in a place where someone doesn’t expect anyone else to be, everyone around him ignores him".
    So, if the vampire is getting into, for instance, a nunnery where the nuns are not expecting to find any foreign, should they be able to notice her or the vampire mingles among the sisters?
    What are the situation in wich the vampire should be noticed? Just in place where really none should be inside, for example inside a closed museum or an abandoned house, or even in place where common people shouldn't be inside?

                              Face in the Crowd doesn't allow you to be seen as a specific kind of person (that's what Familiar Stranger is for), it just makes you seem like a random passerby of no importance. So, it wouldn't really allow you to mingle with a group of cops. For one thing, the cops wouldn't percieve you as one of them, just as some random person, and once they question what this random person is doing here, that will break the Obfuscate effect. The other thing is, mingling would require you to interact, which would call attention to yourself and break the Obfuscate effect.
