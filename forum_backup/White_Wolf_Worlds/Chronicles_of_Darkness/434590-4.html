                                                            Well, the fact i didn't discovered Leviathan: The Tempest before is certainly unfortunate, but at least i arrive on time to this new expansion.

                                                            I'm currently consuming all its information, so i won't be able to help properly until finished... and given the amounth of work i have, that will take some time still. But i didn't want to wait until finish it to compliment the developers of the game and the unique flavour it creates. Great game, well done (so far)!!

                                                            As player of both WoD and Call of Cthulhu, i absolutely love the Leviathan's vibe. I've been reading through the Introduction and Lexicon, and also The World that Is (and Was). And i couldn't resist and jumped over the rules and stuff to go directly into The Rift. And here is the first thing i wanted to share with you all: definitely a concept so awesome as The Rift needs a lot more love than what i've read so far. The stuff i've read is great, but feels... incomplete, too schematic maybe. Needs much much more flesh in those titanic bones. (And i'd remove the conscient changing form pròhibition).

                                                            You see, i've fallen in love with the idea of the Rift. It seems to me the definitive identity point of the game. I see (and the Old God sectarian in myself cannot help but approve) the Cult direction this new edition is taking, but to me the Rift and its nature is the real core (and maybe even the real enemy!) of a Leviathan. (Yes, sorry, I'm total Rift fanboy). After all, we already have Mummy to focus on Cults, and all the splats have some in-built "Cult". I get perfectly that Leviathan's Cults are totally different thanks to that beautiful Wake mechanic, and they add all the Cthulhu-esque flavour to the game... But the Rift? That's Leviathan stuff only. Have you thought about changing the Rift in any way? I'd love to help on that!!<3
