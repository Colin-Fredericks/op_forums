                                                    08-26-2014, 01:33 PM
                                                    Originally posted by Monghani View Post
                                                    I find it interesting you're using a gift from the Dreaming Moon Gift list for the default Cahalith List. My going theory was that the 3 Aspects of the Auspice, as talked about in the blurb for Cahalith, would correspond to 3 Moon Gift Lists as seen from First Edition's Corebook, Lore of the Forsaken, and Signs of the Moon. Allowing even players of the same Tribe and Auspice to go wildly different in how they operate.
                                                    We only had space for one Moon Gift for each Auspice in the corebook, and I didn't want to leave out the 'dreamer' element of the Cahalith from that core Gift. I can easily see a full-on dream-focused Moon Gift as well (or Shadow Gift, actually).

                                                    Originally posted by nofather View Post
                                                    This edition is doing a great job of making me want to play, like, everything. I love the Crimson Spasm, reminds me of the old Warp Spasm of Cú Chulainn and ye olde Sláine comics.
                                                    It reminds you of those for good reason

                                                    Originally posted by Ursufgutha Storm-Born View Post
                                                    I was going to ask this anyway, but now looking at Crimson Spasm... I suppose it's safe to assume that the old "I shapeshift Gauru MUSCLES into my Hishu form" tomfoolery (and the rest like it) with Partial Change has been effectively done away with? Just making sure...
                                                    The new version of Partial Change, Quicksilver Flesh, works similarly to the old one but offers an initial, defined menu of options for things you can do with it. It also leaves space for some player creativity, but I wanted to make it a little less wishy-washy so that players who were less sure of exploiting the freeform nature of Partial Change had something to grip onto, while also giving the Storyteller a better sense of what power level the Facet's changes should offer.
