    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    I think the desire for them not to just be in books like The Pack or Dark Eras, is that if you just want some neat z-splats, they get locked behind books with lots of other stuff. The big books of them (esp. since few of those are referenced in other books, making them extremely optional stuff) had an appeal to people in that way.

    A different approach are things like some of the later Night Horrors books: be a big book of them, but have clusters of linked groups that can be used as part of a bigger story rather than just a bunch of loosely thematically related ones, and even include groups from other game lines (like a new Hunter group that's linked in with the cluster) to expand things out.

    When executed well you have both a collection of functionally stand-alone z-splats, as well as something more engaging to do with the book.
    Look at it this way: if I don't really care for z-splats and you love them above everything else, a book with a few z-splats along with some other content has stuff both of us want in it, and thus we'll both buy it. If a book consists entirely of z-splats, I'm not gonna buy it, while the hypothetical you here wouldn't buy the inverse. Ideally every book has at least one thing in it that everyone will want, so that the maximum number of people will pick it up.

        I get the idea, but there's two issues with the "some extra z-splats" approach:

        1) "Having something for everyone" doesn't always work, because it can also make people feel like they're only getting "half" of what they want, and having to pay for "half" of stuff they don't want when they could by a book that's almost all stuff they want. Maximizing buyers can be harmed if you try to appeal to everyone and end up appealing to no one.

        2) A lot of the z-splats in the 1e "big book" approach books might have never happened without those books. Z-splat focused don't have to fit in to the themes and goals of a book that's focused on something else. This is why I keep going back to the Night Horrors series that managed to have a few z-splat focused books, that had a lot more story/flavor compared to the bigger books of them.
