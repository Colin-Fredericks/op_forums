            As we reach the penaultimate session in the storyline, it is the raid on the Lake of Tears. The name is appropriate; Not all victories feel good, or even like victories.

            Session LVIII

            Marek’s Thoughts

            22 August 1925

            I want to find Ludovico and kill him slowly with my claws, then have Greta raise him as a zombie and I’ll do it again. When I find him he will know pain. I will give Tatiana the chance to kill him though. She deserves that after all that she’s been through. I will hurt him nonetheless for what his men did to my pack mates. I suppose Wolfram deserves a shot at him as well, considering. We will see how it goes when we eventually find him. I will endeavor to let my pack mates have the kill, despite how much I would like to take it for myself.

            I suppose I should start at the beginning, though it is unlikely that today will fade from my memory. Martinique took us to the headquarters of the Black Swan. To start we were all blindfolded for the drive there. Once we arrived at our destination we found ourselves at the edge of a lake. Martinique said that we’d have to take boats across, but to follow her closely since there were mines in the water. They take their safety seriously. Unfortunately for them their precautions didn’t amount to much in the end.

            We split up and took two boats. I rowed one boat as quickly and quietly as I could. We had seen boats patrolling inside the line of mines and they didn’t appear to be Black Swan. So, Martinique guided my boat, which had Tatiana, Laszlo and Marie as passengers. Lizette rowed the second boat with the others, including Greta. As we were moving closer to the shore we noticed that there were six people on the bank. Three of which were vampires, one was a Claimed and one of the last two was a creature with many mouths on its body. I believe someone later said that it was a Promethean, or possibly something that used to be a Promethean.

            So, Tatiana and I shared a look and then she did what she does best. She charged straight at the Claimed and attacked. While he was still reeling from her blow I followed it up with a shot from my rifle. Between the two of us we hurt him badly and he fled towards the house. After that a vampire attacked Tatiana, hurting her pretty badly. Not enough to make her stop, but enough to make her bleed freely. After that is where it got bad. The monster with all the mouths, the promethean touched Tatiana’s arm. A moment later mouths appeared all over her arm and hand and it began to move of its own accord. The promethean made the arm hit Tatiana and it went right for her throat, killing her within seconds. Wolfram indicated that he was going to spray the area a moment before he opened fire with his, usually vehicle mounted, machine gun. He hit several enemy combatants. A moment later Lizette threw a grenade into the same group. The promethean was bleeding freely and where its blood hit the ground the rocks it hit suddenly sprouted legs and started walking towards us. Wolfram opened up again and the promethean exploded. Two of the vampires were also killed in that attack.

            I could not attack the monster that killed my pack mate so I attacked one of the only people left, the remaining vampire. Then there was more machine gun fire, but it did not come from Wolfram’s weapon. The mounted gun on the beach had been sprayed with promethean blood and began firing at my pack. Wolfram was hit hard and he stumbled. Smoke and blood was pouring off of his body. The vampire hit me after that, but I will admit that I didn’t pay much attention to the wounds in that instant. I was rather mad that Tatiana had been killed and Wolfram wounded so badly. Laszlo retaliated against the vampire and killed him in one hit. I then started to move forward when Lizette threw another grenade into the group of promethean spawn. I think Wolfram called them pandorans. Whatever they were, Tatiana had become one of their number when the monster’s blood hit her body. A moment later Greta sent a huge wave of water towards them and killed them all. In Tatiana’s case she was killed again. I will hurt Ludovico in ways that he’s never thought of for doing that to my packmate. He will know fear before he dies.

            We took a moment to catch our breath and Greta admitted that she could raise Tatiana as a zombie. She would not be cognizant, but she would be animate and be able to attack. I had mixed feelings about my friend being raised from the dead like that, but conceded that we could use all the help we could get. We headed for the house after everything on the beach was dead. At the front doors we paused and let Lizette throw another grenade. Well, she actually just rolled it through an opening in the front doors. After it went off we all rushed in, Tatiana in the fore. What we found inside was wholly unexpected and rather disturbing. We encountered monstrous women, or what used to be women. They were creatures that were the bodies of two or three women all merged together. The sight took many of us by surprise, and really disturbed Martinique. I wasn’t surprised that she was so disturbed by the sight. They were her friends after all. Anyway, the monsters attacked and we reciprocated. I killed one creature that was two women merged with a head where her stomach should be. As I hit her I thought I saw gratitude in her eyes. I realized in that instant that the women were not dead. They were still alive and aware of what had happened to them. I could even hear them trying to speak. It sounded like garbled German. I endeavored after that to kill them as quickly as I could. They did not deserve what happened to them.

            Tatiana followed the Claimed’s blood trail while the rest of us dealt with more of those monsters that came swarming out of the rooms in the hallway. Several attacked us in the foyer. Lizette was badly hit and Wolfram, Laszlo and I made short work of the few that were immediately in front of us. Lizette threw a grenade down the hall to deal with the ones there. A moment later a wave followed and killed the remaining monsters.

            I found out after we got down the hall that Tatiana had killed the Claimed and began attacking the next biggest target in the room. We all joined her soon enough and began attacking a rather large monster that was in the room. It touched Tatiana and made her attack herself, much like the Promethean did outside, but it missed. The rest of us attacked and eventually I got in the killing blow with my claws. There was also an old man with an equally old gun in his hands. He was a vampire. He shot at Tatiana. Laszlo retaliated. The vampire seeing that he was outnumbered turned into a ferret and ran into the walls. Wolfram was insistent that we follow the vampire so I tracked it outside and around to the front of the house. When he realized we had followed him he turned into mist and tried to get away. Wolfram used a gift that he has called Pyre flame and burned the vampire though he was in mist form. The vampire fled under the water to get away from us. I’m pretty sure that we hurt him badly.

            Unfortunately when we followed the vampire out to the front of the house we forgot about the three boats that had been patrolling the waters near the island. They started making their way towards the house when they heard gunfire and explosions. Right after Wolfram used his gift to attack the vampire the boats opened fire on him. He was already pretty badly hurt and could not stand up to too much more injury. He was killed almost instantly. Lizette was also badly hurt by the gunfire. Alla and I pulled Lizette behind cover and did our best to bandage her wounds so she would not bleed out on the beach. We didn’t have much choice after that. We were outnumbered. We made ready to run and Greta pointed Tatiana in the direction of the boats. She could jump easily to the deck of one and distract those that were after us. She was already dead, so logistically it made sense to leave her to attack the boats. Personally, I hated to do it. We were forced to leave Wolfram’s body and Tatiana’s soon to be body behind. We couldn’t all escape and take them with us. So, we gathered up Wolfram and Tatiana’s personal effects and I put them in my pack with my rifle. Our packmates would regenerate at the site of the Eagle, but their personal items would not, so we took those with us. The rest of us ran for the opposite side of the house and took to the water to escape. I helped Laszlo swim to shore, since he does not know how to swim yet. Alla helped Lizette since she was still so badly hurt. I’m not sure if anyone else was hurt outside of my pack. I think Alla was hit at least once, but she shrugged it off. I didn’t notice her acting pained in any way. My own injuries were making themselves known quite emphatically as I helped Laszlo swim to shore, but I gritted my teeth and kept going. We did not have the luxury of resting while we were fleeing the gunboats. We eventually made it to shore, found our cars and drove back to the house.

            At this point all we can do is wait for our friends to come back to us. I will admit that I am very glad that we took Jamal up on his offer of using the Golden Eagle. I have become rather attached to my pack mates and was greatly pained to see them lying on the ground dead or badly hurt. I have seen my friends bleed far more than I’d like, but I know that we live a dangerous life. We get ourselves involved in problems foreign and domestic, whenever a friend calls out to us. I do not begrudge our lifestyle. We chose it. I chose to be an assassin. I know that there is someone out there that wants me dead, and he or she knows how to do it. Their last “envoy” used silver bullets, which I’ll admit is an experience I don’t look forward to any time soon. But this is a life that I chose. My friends chose it too. I am only glad that Dearbhail was not with us on the island. If she had been hurt or killed I don’t know what I’d do. As it is, I want to rip Ludovico and his men limb from limb. And that’s just for killing my pack mates. If he had hurt my special lady I am honestly not sure what I’d do. I do know that it would be violent, bloody and possibly involve a grenade.


            Tatiana’s Conversations

            Volchonok? Volchonok! Where is the pack? Are they alright?

            Oh. Good. Wait - Wolfram woke up yesterday? The others? Martinique? Greta? Marie?

            Just us two died? Good.

            Ah! Volchonok, take care. Uh - bah - stop that. Yes, yes, it is good we are all well.

            I did not bite myself!

            Yes, I scratched at my neck.

            Yes, volchonok, there were teeth in the mouths on my paws where my claws should have been.

            Well, they were not my teeth, so I did not bite myself.

            Just because I died once, volchonok, that does not mean you can contradict me like that.

            Three times? What do you mean 3 times? Explain.

            Perhaps Lizette’s advice to ‘spray the area’ is not so bad. So, the Sin-Eater ran and Wolfram shot down the mouth creature and some dead men. What then?

            Spider rocks? Volchonok, you...

            OK, OK! It is you who is telling the story.

            Let me get this straight. Everything the mouth creature’s blood touched grew mouths and began walking towards the pack, including a tree, a mounted machine, and me? I did not hurt anyone?

            I must thank Greta then. I would not have been easy with the idea that my teeth finished Wolfram off, after the walking machine gun filled him with holes.

            I must really thank her! A zombie? Did I kill the rest?

            Oh right, Lizette’s grenades and Greta’s wave. Surely there was more to fight? The Sin-Eater?

            Excellent! Of course I tracked him down. I am the best at hunting, even after dying twice. Were there others to fight?

            Another monster? Like with the mouths?

            Only one for each head? How many heads...?

            Four. Of course, volchonok, I should have guessed. What the hell were they doing at that place?

            Grrr...

            You cannot blame Martinique. She is not Uratha, but they were her pack, those women you say were melted together, or molded into new shapes. We killed them all, yes?

            I meant the ones who did such things, though I imagine the women-monsters were well put out of their misery.

            All but one? How did we let it get away?

            A vampire you mean?

            Yes, yes, Alla is much better than him. But Pure are still called werewolves, even though I’m better than them, so he is still called a vampire.

            Of course I could track a ferret, even a vampire one!

            Oh. Well, of course Marek could track a ferret, even a vampire one.

            Mist? That is cheating.

            Traps are also cheating. At least Wolfram lit the cheating vampire on fire before he was killed. How did the pack escape, with three boats of riflemen?

            Of course I did, volchonok! Those boats didn’t stand a chance.

            Close enough. The pack escaped, didn’t they?

            So that counts as killing all 12 of them.

            Because I say so, volchonok. Now. Where are they? Not here, but for Fenric, or I would know.

            End Session LVIII

            Thank goodness for the Eagle, eh?

            So, I have to talk about how Tatiana died. Tatiana had taken some aggravated damage from a Vampire with Protean, but what killed her was hitting herself when her arm was, for a single action, infused with Flux from a Centimenti, and attacked her. She rolled twenty dice to attack herself (due to no defense from all out attacking). If she had rolled nineteen successes she would have survived (it was doing bashing because Werewolf). But she rolled twenty-two successes on twenty dice, and died. Poor player is never going to have an attack roll like that again is she?

            So after being a Pandoran for a very brief time before being put down by Lizette and Greta, Greta animated her with the Stillness Marionette. She pumped a fair amount of Plasm into the zombie, and I gave it to Tatiana’s player to run. The resulting Zombie was much tougher, but did notably less severe damage. Still it’s mindless charge after the fleeing Sin-Eater pulled everything in the House, leading to people talking about “Scholomance all over again.” Though the hall full of once-women was wiped out by Lizette’s grenades among other things.

            Speaking of Lizette, she ended the fight with 6/7 of her Health Levels full of aggravated damage, and the last with lethal. She only survived because a medicine roll was made.

            Have I mentioned rifles suck? Even a handful of humans with them is dangerous. What happened at the end was the players forget they rushed inside after the outdoor fight was over because the three boats were moving back toward the island, with riflemen aboard. So when they rushed out after the Vampire, the boats were waiting.

            The members of the Black Swan inside were damaged by Flux experimentation.

            So yeah, two members killed one completely incapacitated and only saved via medicine. Everyone on the island dead or wished they were. Not the best end, but they will deal with Ludovico again.

            Last night was a very short session, Alla’s player being there long as she could before leaving with a migraine. Mostly some hunting for Laszlo and the Vampire social. Then ahead is some hero style archeology in Egypt! I’ll see you then.

                It was a very short session, about an hour of play as we had a scene with Laszlo, had the Salon, and some downtime discussion. Journal from Marek, though. Enjoy.

                Session LXXXIX

                Marek’s Thoughts

                24 August 1925

                We are still waiting on Tatiana and Wolfram to come back to us. We cannot do anything until that happens. So in the meantime Laszlo and I decided to go on a hunt. He needed to strengthen his resolve and to do that he hunts and “teaches.” So, we went out looking for Fascists. It wasn’t hard, they are all over the place in this town. So, we found a group and I stayed back while Laszlo taunted them. I think his goal was to make them feel vulnerable when they were away from home, like they do to others. We ended up chasing them through a maze of streets. Sometimes I was in human form, other times I chased them as a wolf. All they knew was that two or possibly three beings were chasing them, and they were damn scary. I am not the most physically imposing man but I like to think that I make up for it with determination. I have been told before that my gaze can be scary sometimes, especially if I was in a fight or hunting. So, I offered these men my best smile and gave chase through the back streets. They eventually got themselves lost and trapped in a dead end. Laszlo did his thing where his presence feels much more imposing than his physical form would imply. He’s good at that. After he made sure the Fascists had learned their lesson he deferred to me and I smiled at the cowering men and said that it was my turn. I then did my best to emulate my pack mate and started a fight. I had wanted to take out my anger on someone and decided that these guys were the perfect target, and so I did just that. I beat them to a pulp, only sustaining glancing wounds myself, and left them senseless on the ground in the alley.

                After that Laszlo and I made our way back to the house for a celebratory drink. I wanted to share our exploits with the others, but did not feel that they were up for it. Lizette was resting, as were the others. Wolfram and Tatiana were not back yet, and their deaths had caused a good deal of uproar at the house in Paris. Christine was very upset when we first got back and had to be reassured that her husband would be back within a few days. I don’t know how much that actually reassured her. She was still upset. She has spent a good deal of time also tending to Lizette’s wounds. Her other lover’s injuries bothered her just as much. So, I left them too it and resolved myself to share the story with Tatiana as soon as she came back. I figured that she would appreciate it. She loves to fight.

                Volchonok has been restless these past few days. He keeps prowling around the property in a circle, like he is guarding Tatiana. I figured he was doing just that. He knew that I was nearby and keeping an eye out myself, but he could be with her twenty-four hours a day, whereas I could not. I know that my own spirit companion has been trying to reassure the young pup that Tatiana would be back soon. Volchonok seemed to listen, but then went back to circling the house. I will leave him to it. I will not stand in his way of protecting his friend.


                25 August 1925

                Wolfram came back to us today. Christine was overjoyed, as was Lizette. I felt as if a weight had been lifted from my chest when he drew breath once more. I guess the hunt yesterday did not fully alleviate the stress of losing a friend. Today though, everyone’s mood was much improved. Wolfram came back to us, but he was still very worn out and tired. He replenished what he could of his energy from the locus in our house, but he was still not to full strength. He then within a short time expended a good deal of that recovered energy in healing some of Lizette’s wounds. I know that he was upset to see her injured as she was and set out to do what he could to make it better. I believe that he plans on healing more of her wounds after he replenishes his energy once again. We are lucky to have such mystical loci within our property.


                26 August 1925

                Today was the day of the Salon. Alla had been looking forward to this for a while now. Ever since she got involved in the planning for the party she has thought of little else. She has even gotten others of our pack involved in the planning for this event. Lizette, who is much improved since Wolfram started healing her, planned on animating some random objects at the salon. The house that it is was held at is a bombed-out ruin, and the theme was that of ruined finery. Everyone wore clothing to match the theme and the party appeared to be haunted. Wolfram asked some local ghosts to make an appearance and both he and Lizette added to the effects. Lizette animated things such as chairs and candelabras to move and walk around, while Wolfram used his Boneyard gift to make doors open as people approached them, made windows open and close and lights turn on and off at random intervals. I had no really purpose at the salon other than to act as security at the door. That was fine by me. I am not one for social gatherings anyway. I never seem to get it right. It is a failing on my part, but practice doesn’t really seem to help. I am fine with family and with my pack, but strangers are another thing entirely. I’m told that I come across as cold and somewhat uncaring when dealing with strangers. I suppose that is apt. I don’t actually care much what happens with strangers, except where it affects my family and friends. It’s just the way I am and I can live with that.


                2 September 1925

                I have resumed my hunt of Ludovico. He is a wily bastard I’ll give him that. I have found a few places where he was, but not where he is now. I believe that he has friends that help him. Supernatural friends, or those familiar with the supernatural. Given some of his past acquaintances I would believe either one. I have hunted many men and have not had as much trouble finding them as I have with Ludovico. I will find him eventually though. He will pay for what he did to my pack mates. I suppose it would be good to bring him to justice for the others that he’s hurt as well, but I’m mainly after him for killing my pack mates. Maybe I should see if Tatiana or Laszlo wants to help me hunt the bastard.


                10 September 1925

                He definitely has someone working for him, and whoever they are they have gifts of their own. It started out with little things going wrong, but lately as I get closer, I have noticed an increase in the number of unusual occurrences that have happened to me. Today was the worst by far. I was on a boat following a lead when we suddenly struck something under the surface. We were getting close to shore in an area that is normally fairly deep, so I’m assuming we struck something that had sunk just off shore. I have been told that after the war the waters around several countries are more treacherous due to sunken ships or downed planes. Regardless of what we hit, the boat started going down. The small crew and few passengers started making their way to the life boats. I started to join them when I noticed that our plight had not gone unseen. An Italian navy boat was making its way to us in an effort to help. When they arrived they were perhaps more curt than they really needed to be considering most of the passengers were innocent civilians. There were a few ex-military men on board. I could tell from their bearing. So, given the navy’s less than hospitable introduction I opted to allow my backpack to slide off my shoulder and sink with the ship. I did not want to have to explain to any group of military men what I was doing with a sniper rifle in my possession. I was sad to see my bag sink. I liked that rifle. I will have to talk to Hans and see if he can set me up with a replacement.


                15 September 1925

                I was arrested. Again. As soon as the Navy helped the life boats get to shore they started pulling people aside as soon as they’d stepped on land. The Captain and a couple of his crew were grabbed immediately. I was also taken into custody. I think they assumed that I worked with the Captain or something. I tried to explain then and there that I had nothing to do with the Captain’s business, that I was just a passenger, but they didn’t believe me. It didn’t help that I don’t really know much Italian. So, they brought me and the other men to a military police station and locked us up. Thankfully this jail was considerably better than the one I found myself in when I was in Yugoslavia. This place was clean, almost to a fault, and the prisoners well treated. When I requested that any meat be rare they looked at me like I was a bit off, but agreed nonetheless. I mentioned a couple of simple dishes that employ rare meat as an example, but it really doesn’t matter to me. As long as it’s rare, it’s good for me.

                They interrogated me through an interpreter for a while before finally coming to the conclusion that I was not working with the Captain at whatever illegal activities that he was up to. It helped that the Captain and I barely had a language in common. It was a nuisance to be detained, but I’ve been in worse jails so I couldn’t complain too much. When they released me the commanding officer apologized for the mix up and offered me a ride to a train station or another port. I said that I’d had enough of boats for a while and accepted the offer of a ride to the train station. I believe that I may need to postpone my hunt of Ludovico until I can find a way to circumvent his mystical protections. I will likely need the help of my pack mates in this endeavor. Wolfram is an experienced occultist and Tatiana is unparalleled in a fight. Alla has her way with social situations in a way I could never hope to emulate, and Laszlo is good at being underestimated. It’s funny. Laszlo comes across as very imposing, but most people push that feeling aside when they look at his stature. They assume that they are mistaken in their senses and that this slight man is not a threat. They are so wrong.


                16 October 1925

                It’s been two days since Franko. I was hired to kill the Italian businessman a while back, but had not been able to get to him for a while. It was not a high priority. The client just specified that he needed to die before the new year. I researched Franko and found out that he is involved in human trafficking and is an overall asshole to even his legitimate business partners. I thought it would be an easy job. I followed him one night to a dock where he was unloading cargo from a large container. He and his men were just pulling boxes out so I assumed that the only people down there were criminals. I was wrong. I guess I got cocky and was not as careful as I should have been. He must have seen light reflect off my scope or something. I don’t know what gave me away, but something did. Just as I took my shot Franko reached into the container and pulled out a young girl, no more than ten or twelve years old. He pulled the child in front of him and the bullet struck her before going through and grazing Franko.
                I was in shock for a moment before the rage set in. I could not get the image of blood spreading on the young girl’s blue shirt out of my mind. I still can’t. I don’t hurt children. That has been a hard and fast rule for me since day one. I do not harm children. And Franko made me do just that. He made me kill that child. I saw red and dropped my rifle. I shifted forms on my way down to the loading dock. I hadn’t even realized I’d take gauru form until I reached Franko and his men. They tried shooting me. It was pathetic. They were too scared to shoot straight. Franko pissed himself when I got close. Though he did manage to grab a gun from one of his men and shoot me square in the chest. I will admit that hurt, but given my state of mind I just shrugged it off and growled. There was no point in telling him what he was dying for. He knew. He wouldn’t have understood me anyway.

                After I killed Franko I shifted to dalu form and told Franko’s men to run. They did. I then turned to the storage container and saw that there were five more people hiding in the back. I told them that their captor was dead and that they should run to the authorities. They had not seen the person who shot the little girl, they only saw me kill Franko. Most of them were afraid of me, but one woman said “Thank you,” before running off. I don’t blame them. I was covered in blood and not quite human looking.
                I didn’t wait to see if the others left, I just took off and recovered my rifle from where I’d dropped it. I didn’t want to leave this one behind like I did the last one. I managed to stick to the shadows until I could get to the place I was renting to clean up. I got so far as taking off my ruined shirt before I was violently ill. The realization hit me again. I had killed a child. An innocent child. Somehow, I managed to take a shower and wash away the rest of the blood. I packed up my belongings and left town that night. The room was paid up through the following day, but I did not want to spend any more time in that town than I absolutely had to.



                2 November 1925

                I still have bad dreams about that night. Franko. I don’t usually have bad dreams about killing people, but then I’ve never killed a child before. They were all grown men or women, and they had all done despicable things. I had no qualms about killing them. But Franko changed that. He pulled that child in front of him to use as a shield. How low do you have to sink to use a child as a shield? My only regret is that I could only kill him once. I went into a rage and killed him so quick. True, he had time to understand what was about to happen to him. He got to see the monster that killed him full in the face. My other targets never saw me coming. Franko did. I suppose that gives me some satisfaction. It doesn’t help with the dreams though. I wonder if this is how normal people react to having done violence to another person? Do they see the faces of those that they killed? Do they have dreams that relive the event over and over? Dammit.

                6 November 1925

                I feel like I’ve redeemed myself just a little today. I was passing through a fairly big town in Germany, I don’t recall the name, and saw something that bothered me. A young man, more of a boy by his actions, was holding the leash of a snarling dog. The dog itself was barking at another young man, who was trying his best to avoid the animal. The dog was straining against his leash. It was obvious that he wanted to go after the other man.                                             This is long delayed, but here are the most recent Golden Eagle Journals. Enjoy.

                                            Session LXIII

                                            Marek’s Thoughts

                                            28 January 1926

                                            We arrived in Karnak. While Dana, Tatiana and I went to town to get the lay of the land Nathan worked on getting transportation for the party. He got a couple of wallys and camels for the workers that he hired. We hired locals since none of us have a problem with non-Christians and they are actually cheaper to hire. We also figured that they might know more about the land than outsiders would. It seemed a good idea. In any case the three of us went in to town to see what was going on and found ourselves somewhat surprised. We all noticed the sheer number of soldiers in uniform and carrying rifles. There did not seem to be any police officers around, just soldiers. Dana confirmed for us that this was odd. Tatiana also asked around and found out about the local Uratha situation, which turns out to be not much of anything. There are very few Uratha in the area due to the climate and scarcity of resources. She did note that Cairo is once more in Uratha hands after being held by the Pure for one thousand years. While interesting it doesn’t have much influence on our current activities. At least not yet. We headed back to where the others were and Dana overheard some locals saying something about raiders being seen in the area. I guess we will have to keep a weather eye out.

                                            When we got back we told the others what we had found out. Dana explained to the rest of us how it normally works here. In Egypt, or at least this part of Egypt, a group of soldiers can take over an area and if the actual Army doesn’t kick them out then they lay claim to whatever land they are inhabiting. It’s like some sort of tacit agreement on the part of the Army for the mercenaries to take over whatever town or land they want. It doesn’t seem like a good idea to me, but if the Army is not strong enough to push out all would-be invaders it might be the only way that they can handle things. Fighting them would be costly if they don’t have the resources to back up those fighting.

                                            Karnak is also where we said our goodbyes to Khafu. He said that some of his enemies may be active in the area so we might see him in the near future. He didn’t know for sure. We wished him well and he went on his way, and we went on ours. We loaded up the wallys and camels with all the equipment, as well as Alla’s crate. It’s not the most dignified way to travel, but when you’re a Kindred in Egypt then you have to do what you have to do. We hadn’t gotten very far when we came across a checkpoint of sorts manned by men in uniform. They were not regular Army, but they were soldiers. They told us that we could not pass because of Ethiopian bandits in the area. They repeated this several times to Dana and Nathan who had gone up to talk to the guards. Nicolette translated for the rest of us. It seemed that the soldiers thought that we were trouble, and in Arabic apparently that word can translate to mean danger or threat. Negotiations were breaking down and the guards stopped talking to Dana altogether. They weren’t even accepting the customary bribe, which in and of itself was odd. They really didn’t want us to pass. They were hiding something. Tatiana decided that enough was enough and went forward to bring back Nathan and Dana. We all got back into the vehicles and drove around the checkpoint regardless of what the guards said. They didn’t stop us, which was interesting. I was expecting them to try something. My guess is that they would send a runner ahead to warn their compatriots that we were coming. I was proven right a few moments later. A solider took off across the desert on camel so we drove slowly, and after we were out of line of sight Tatiana jumped off the vehicle and changed shape and chased after the messenger.

                                            Tatiana chased after the soldier into the desert quietly. It looked like he was on his way to an old fort. It was currently manned by several armed soldiers and there was a wally parked outside alongside some camels. She transformed into Urshal form and knocked the poor man off his camel in one blow. She then threatened him with gestures since they did not share a spoken language. She told him not to go to the fort or she would kill him. She also told him to go running back to his friends at the checkpoint. Apparently she was rather effective at communicating with the man and he took off as fast as his legs would carry him. She watched him run for a bit before she transformed back to wolf form and headed back to meet up with the rest of us. We agreed that the fort would warrant a look at some point.

                                            We eventually arrived at an old town that had been abandoned some time ago and repurposed recently by archaeologists as a staging point for their digs. The dig that we were looking for, to find Kruger, was nearby. After we set up our tents Nathan got out his maps so Tatiana could mark the location of the fort on it. Turns out that the fort was already marked on the map. Dana also mentioned, when she got back from talking to Kruger, that if there was another mummy in the area it would probably be at that fort. I had a feeling that we hadn’t heard the last of that place. I expected trouble. It would seem likely to come at some point if the checkpoint guard had been heading straight for that place. It stands to reason they would eventually get up the guts to send another guy to the fort to warn them that we had passed them and traveled into the desert. With that in mind I dozed while we drove and planned to stay up tonight and stand guard. When I mentioned my intentions to the pack Wolfram agreed that it was a good idea and said that he would join me. Four eyes are better than two, and even more so when two of those eyes belong to a werewolf.


                                            **** **** ****

                                            29 January 1926

                                            So far today has been fairly exciting. Once everyone was up Dana introduced us to Kruger who she’d talked to last night. We told him about the checkpoint that we’d encountered yesterday and he admitted that he been seeing soldiers in the area lately. Some of them he thought had suspicious blood stains on their clothes. He said that he wasn’t sure if they were real soldiers, as in real Army, or just mercenaries. They had not been bothering him or his workers, so there’s that. Dana suggested that one of us go with Kruger’s workers to get supplied and see if the soldiers try and stop them or not. Or see how they react to the workers rather than a large group of foreigners traveling by wally. Dana also asked Kruger about Kindred activity in the area. He confirmed that the Bak’ra are active in the nearby regions, but didn’t have much more information than that. It basically just confirmed what we already knew.

                                            Once Kruger took a break to get water Dana asked if any of us could get a bird’s eye view of the area so we could see the soldier’s activities as well as anything else that is going on around here. Wolfram admitted that he could do it but would need to be away from the workers when he tried. See, he would have to immolate himself in order to activate his gift. Things like that remind me how glad I am that Uratha gifts are so much more straightforward than Claimed gifts. I would not want to have to set myself alight to use a gift. It apparently doesn’t really hurt Wolfram to use this gift, which is good. I would not like it if my packmate ended up seriously hurting himself just for some reconnaissance.

                                            As a side note, the sun here is very hot. Of course it’s hot, it’s the desert, but it seems a lot hotter than summers at home. It’s also very dry here and very bright. The sun seems to just reflect off the sand and it takes some getting used to. I don’t know if it’s the heat or the climate, but it just seems to sap the strength right out of you. It’s not a pleasant feeling. It could also be that I stayed up all night, but I’ve done that plenty of times so I prefer to think that it’s the sun causing the problems. Lizette has also been similarly affected. She went so far as to build herself a backpack with a fan on it to blow cooler air down on her. It seems to work rather well, and makes more sense than just continuously fanning herself with something. Doing that just uses more energy and works to make you hotter in the long run. It should just be a matter of acclimating to the dry heat though, so hopefully we’ll get over this discomfort soon.

                                            Shortly after our talk with Kruger some of the workers came over to talk to Dana and said that there were ghostly jackals at the dig site that was nearby. We all gathered up any weapons we wanted and headed for the dig site. Sure enough, when we arrived there were five ghostly jackals wandering around the area. They seemed to just be watching the workers there but became a little more interested when we arrived. We immediately determined that they were not ghosts or spirts, they were mortal creatures that just happened to be supernatural in appearance. They also seemed to be reflecting sunlight, which made them hard to look at for too long. Wolfram called them cryptids. Tatiana then howled, like a human pretending to be a wolf, to get Dana’s attention. Said woman arrived shortly after that and the jackals immediately became interested in her. Our theory is that they were drawn to technology like we’ve encountered in the past… specifically the items that are said to be connected to the Black Pharaoh. Presumably Dana had the wās in her possession and they all looked in her direction.

                                            Dana told the workers to leave and they all did. She then tried to use the wās on the jackals, to no effect. Soon after that the jackals all started heading for us, Dana in particular. We moved to stand in front of her, preparing ourselves as we did so. The ensuing fight was quick. One of the jackals tried to attack Dana, but it didn’t succeed. Tatiana taught one a lesson, hurting it badly. I attacked one with my claws. It was not happy with me after that. One of them, presumably in defense of his friend, flashed a bright light in my eyes blinding me. I had to smile at that. I may not have been able to see them but my other senses took up the slack rather well and I had no problems finding my target after that. Lizette shot one with her tommy gun and Wolfram finished it off with his sword. Laszlo killed the one that I had been fighting and I took out another one that had had been injured by someone. There are plenty of details that I am missing since I was paying attention to the things in my own field of perception. Tatiana killed another one in short order and Lizette opened up with that gun of hers at the remaining one. We thought that we were done with the jackals when all of a sudden another one seemed to appear out of nowhere. It headed for Dana’s wās, and grabbed it. Tatiana killed it before it could get away, and I finished off the last of the original group. We discovered after the fight was over that the jackals had come up out of the sand. They had been hiding under there and that’s why the last one seemed to materialize out of thin air.

                                            After the fight, but before the workers came back, Tatiana and I each stuffed one of the jackals in a sack and brought them back to our tents. We figured that they would make for a good source of meat. Food that will do something for us is going to be hard enough to come by in the desert as it is, so I didn’t see a reason to let the meat go to waste. Soon after that we all packed up our gear. We had discussed our options and felt that leaving sooner would keep Kruger and his men from any future harm. These kinds of creatures are likely to continue showing up as we travel, so there’s no need to put the men in danger for no reason. At least that’s how it was presented to me. I agreed that it would be a waste if the men were killed, but didn’t phrase it like that when talking to the pack. I know enough to know how to phrase certain things. I don’t particularly care about the workers. They are not pack, but I can acknowledge that they serve a purpose and it would be a waste of resources if they were killed when we could prevent it.

                                            Our next step is to have Wolfram use his gift to travel as a spirit would so he can get the lay of the land. We will need to do this out of the sight of Kruger and his men. I will leave this for now and record more later, after I have something to report.


                                            Tatiana’s Conversations

                                            Volchonok, leave off pawing at that. It is fine.

                                            It was just a beast, supernatural or no. See, here in the tent it is merely white, not shining, and slightly larger than a usual jackal. It is good meat.

                                            It is good! I was worried it would be metal, since Wolfram said it was something to do with Dana’s was, or the Golden Eagle. But it is meat, and nothing smells wrong with it.

                                            Yes, Dana’s was. Her magic stick. They attacked when they saw it, and tried to steal it, so it was probably that. Or else they just smelled all of the strange technology on all of us and just attacked when they smelled it directly. They didn’t attack any of the workers, anyway.

                                            You must not have been paying attention, volchonok. I am pretty sure the archeologist man would have mated with Dana no matter what she did, if she wanted to. But he would have been upset had his workers been killed because of us. Probably.

                                            We are not here for his workers, or for Dana to mate with him. He was supposed to know about vampires.

                                            He knew what Khofu had already said. Well, I suppose the archeologist did know for sure they were working around here, not just that they are around sometimes.

                                            Oh, the vampires will probably be fun to fight. But yes, the jackals were a good fight. I think the pack did well, though we shall have to keep in mind that they can burrow, should we see them again.

                                            I do not think we can teach Laszlo to smell better, volchonok, though it would have helped after he got blinded. He did well before that though.

                                            Back on that again? Nicolette did not think the meat was bad. She is just a foolish human who doesn’t like to see people eating meat properly.

                                            Remember? They burn it first. And then use forks.

                                            I am not explaining forks again, volchonok.

                                            She did back out very quickly, it is true. Maybe she will stop trying to mate with me. Maybe when we hunt I shall save some for eating just before bed.

                                            Bah. She is a pale shadow of a nursemaid. Wearing that pink nightgown was the most interesting thing she has done since showing up and remembering that we turned into wolves once. Besides, Marek doesn’t trust her.

                                            Probably because she showed up and remembered that we turned into wolves once in her sight. Or because she is compelled to write.

                                            Also, everyone she mates with dies or has something horrible happen.

                                            Yes, volchonok, I will come back, but I do not think mating with her will be worth going through that again.

                                            Usually you are happy when I will not mate with a human.

                                            Do not go changing the subject...

                                            Oh that. It is alright volchonok. Wolfram is doing it on purpose, so he can become like a spirit and fly to check on the fort, the Mountain of the Hawk.

                                            The one from last night? We were stopped by soldiers, and then we went on a run?

                                            I know you enjoyed the run, you silly headed volchonok. I ran so that I could stop the soldier from reporting us.

                                            Yes, the one who I scared into smelling bad.

                                            I didn’t make sounds at him because he would not understand my sounds.

                                            Oh, he understood my hands.

                                            You don’t? Well, you are just a foolish volchonok...

                                            OK, OK, I will tell you. I told him if he went to the fort, I would kill him. And that I would watch him, and if he came back to the fort later, or anyone else did, I would kill them.

                                            Yes, all that, just with my hands.

                                            I do not know how to say let’s go for a run with my hands. And we can’t, not right now. Now we must pack up, and continue towards the temple. If you want, you can run alongside...

                                            End Session LXIII

                                            About two years ago, when The Golden Eagle began, we were looking at a lot of things. A lot at a different, swinging era, but also an era where women had unprecedented rights, queer people were around and more open than ever. Sewing circles, unground gay clubs. An era when PoC’s had…okay, it still blew for them but better than before. Then the rise of the far right, fascism, Nazism. It would bury all this for many years, until the 90s at earliest. It was a paean to that time even as we had a Nazi fighting good time. Two years ago this was easy for us, a majority female majority queer group to do.

                                            2017 came to be a different time. White power, Nazi salutes in the streets. In my own country. I’m scared. Many of us are scared. Wolfram’s player noted he was playing a man watching his country go mad, now he is a man watching his country go mad. Working on the game has become very stressful for me, and there is something of an air for some of us over it.

                                            Unfortunately this makes it hard to run as the spectre of fascism covers Europe when we worry about it at home away from the table. This has substantially decreased the overall fun of the game. I had a highly stressful October, and the game never quite restarted. Unfortunately as of the end of the year we had to call it. . No drama contributed, in fact the same group is starting a new game soon. I apologize. This game had potential, and if real world politics had not become so horrifying, maybe it would have continued. But as is, it only adds to my and some other player’s stress.

                                            Hope you enjoyed it, folks. Sorry for the sudden end. We could do no different.
