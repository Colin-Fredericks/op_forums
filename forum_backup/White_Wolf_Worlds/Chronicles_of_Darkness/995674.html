                              The Arisen only stand to profit from human conflict, and while the chaos of war would likely upset quite a few long-term plans and old cults they're nothing if not opportunists. Fighting in the cities allows overt attacks on rivals and open relic thievery, while the emotional turmoil (aided by Sybaris) and loss of faith in political power structures serves only to drive mortals into the waiting arms of cults. If things get bad enough that governments start to fail, expect the Diasporic Code to go out the window as some bold Arisen seek to rule openly, carving out petty kingdoms where you either serve or are forced to survive out in the lawless wastes.

                              Civilizations crumble as they always do. Mummies have seen countless apocalypses and weathered them all. Mankind will rebuild again and again, and continue to serve the will of the Judges.

                                                      Originally posted by Basic View Post
                                                      How would changelings or werewolves handle this

                                                      With the Hedge reacting to pain, hiding out there might not be a good idea but it depends on where the battles are taking place. But at least in first edition they had ample ability to allow them to survive and flourish while the world was in chaos around them.

                                                      Originally posted by Wormwood View Post
                                                      But I think WW3 would affect the Shadow a tad negatively, so Werewolves would have their hands full with work, wouldn't they?
                                                      Like the Hedge it depends where battles are taking place. Much of World War 2, for instance, was confined to Europe, the Pacific and Northern Africa. But even then, werewolves aren't obligated to keep the Hisil being a peaceful place. That would be impossible, anyways. It can be nice, if you are planning to live there, but just making a big show of dominance can have the same effect. No need to hunt spirits of destruction if they know their place.

                                                      One can look at Basra for a good example of a war-torn locale where werewolves, at least those of a certain tribe, have thrived.

                                                      Many werewolves would probably dive into the fray. With their regeneration, senses, shifting, gifts, and ability to dominate technology they would excel at combat. Unless packs were upset, they would focus on things as they do now. World War 3 wouldn't mean the end of the Claimed or Hosts, for instance, it would just give them opportunity to hide among the chaos. Iron Masters would be able to clearly point out human causes and targets that need to be brought down. Bone Shadows could point out newly formed Wounds and spirits borne of blood. Blood Talons would see it as an opportunity to eliminate long-standing threats and werewolves who they deem to have done wrong.

                                                      The Pure would ironically probably fare the best. Predator Kings are made for that sort of thing, the Ivory Claws are rich and affluent and would likely remain untouched for the most part, the Fire-Touched would have more spirit allies. Bale Hounds would thrive.

                                                      Ghost Wolf population would probably rise, with the chaos allowing other totems to take them into lodges or packs without letting them know about the tribes.

                                                          Just imagine all the squads that would go MIA, assumed to be enemy casualties but in truth killed/captured/used by supernaturals.

                                                          Werewolves have been shown already to occasionally get involved in military operations, both past and present, so they might jump in or be busy trying to protect their territories and mend the Hisil. And it might be decent cover for combatting rivals or enemy factions. Mages... I guess it honestly would depend on the mage themselves. One might be content to try and hide themselves from the world and conflict, while another uses the chaos to cover their own use of magic or experiments on wayward soldiers. Vampires might not be as likely to get openly involved, obviously, but... Again, depends on the individual or group. That being said, the chaos of a city under siege or isolated soldiers would make good targets for hungry blood drinkers. Sin-Eaters might very well find themselves over their heads with unquiet dead, Changelings may be running just as fast as they always have from their pasts in Arcadia, Demons are used to operating under the chaos of mortal action, and so on.

                                                          It might be a fun thing to examine in a Mirrors book if they end up doing any more.
