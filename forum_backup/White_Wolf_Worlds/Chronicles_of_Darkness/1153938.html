    Showcase your Supernal Entities, Goetia, Spirits and Ghosts (Homebrew)

    10-29-2017, 01:21 PM
    I don't yet have anything to contribute to the topic, but I figured that if people could make example spells, then why not homebrew spirits, Goetia, and all of the various things out there. I know that any Storyteller has probably had to build their own for each of these beings and more, so I figure there's probably a lot of untapped potential waiting out there, and especially when it comes to Goetia and Supernal Entities, there's definitely a lot more examples that we could use. (Summoners is great, but it's both not 2e, and clearly meant to provide only a small sample of what you could create.

    I do plan to contribute to this topic myself at some point, I'm currently working on some Supernal Entities, but I don't have anything yet.

    So have at ye?

      Perpetuus: A Rank 6 goetia. Perpetuus is the manifest dream of the concept of causality. He resides in a spiral tower in the Sidereal Wastes, located on the astral reflection of Saturn's moon Phoebe. He was primarily a plot device used for some characters who died (almost, but not quite) get their bodies back by rewriting time. His primary Numen is a Lasting version of the Rewrite History spell. The trick is that he has a price, which is to take part of your own history away. What he does with these collected bits of history is anyone's guess. Mechanically, this is represented by shifting merits and skills around

      The Narrow Path: A Rank 4 Supernal Entity representing the cycle of Life through mass extinction events. Thrown out of the Primal Wilds by the Exarchs long ago, it now lies slumbering somewhere on the edges of the Anima Mundi. When it stirs, ecosystems start to collapse. Ban: The Narrow Path cannot harm anyone who possesses teeth or claws from the 5 greatest predators of each era it ended with a mass extinction event. Bane: A weapon owned by someone who personally killed at least 100 people in a genocide.

          "The Wraith of Eyes": A Rank 2 Supernal Entity. A Demon of Mind, its trial involves viewing sights and sounds designed to make one cry out or break, from the lives of people who died, or who faced great adversity. If one can stand up to it without breaking, one can gain its boon. The thing most Mages summon it for is that it allows them to view the world out of the eyes of someone else for a time... literally, in this case. It can allow you to know what your enemy is up to even if you don't have the skill at Space, or the sympathetic connection, to view him more directly. The peeks it gives are only brief, and relate to what the target is doing at the time, thus making it an entity one summons with great care towards the timing. After all, you wouldn't want to merely see the inside of someone's eyelids, would you?

                      This is the ghost of the infamous judge who presided over the Salem witch trials, but possessed by a diabolic Inferno lower depths demon. In my game the Tremere left him in charge of Danvers Asylum when they abandoned it:

                      Jonathan Hathorne (Possessed by a Larval Diabolic Demon - Infernal Rank 3)

                      Attributes: Power 7, Finesse 5, Resistance 7
                      Willpower: 14
                      Morality: 1 (Megalomania)
                      Virtue: Justice
                      Vice: Greed
                      Initiative: 12
                      Defense: 7
                      Speed: 22 (species factor 10)
                      Size: 5
                      Corpus: 12
                      Essence: 30

                      Numina: Clairvoyance (dice pool 12), Compulsion (dice pool 12), Ghost Sign (dice pool 12), Ghost Speech (dice pool 12), Magnetic Disruption, Phantasm (dice pool 12), Possession (dice pool 12), Telekinesis (dice pool 12), Terrify (dice pool 12), Greater Hellform

                      • Greater Hellform: Unnatural speed and strange limbs allow it to make an additional attack for every dot of Finesse over 3. Storytellers may also divide its Infernal Rank between bonus attack dice and Corpus. Roll Power + Resistance. Each success allows the demon to maintain the Hellform for a turn. This Numen costs 1 Willpower (which does not add 3 dice to any actions) to activate, plus 1 point of Essence for every turn it functions.

                      Arm Ghost: Entropic Shield 5, Mind Shield 4, Magic Shield 3, Restore Corpus, Counterspell

                      Use the full range of his abilities to set up a truly terrifying encounter.

                              I did have another idea, though I'm not sure what the secondary Arcana would be (it's either Fate or Time), or the specifics of how it'd work.

                              Another, the Revealer of Ways: It shows what one was, and what one can be. By challenging a Mage on the choices they made in the past, and the ways they could have been, their trial dares them to try to 'change' things, but those Mages who do fail. Only those who accept that they are themselves can then gain the primary thing that Another can do. While they can serve as a handy guide to the immediate past, and the skein of fate, what they do most of all is show a person who they could have been if they made other choices. Or if others in their life (such as parents) had acted differently. By viewing these different selves, a Mage might obsess over what they did wrong... or learn to accept that this life and these choices are the ones they made.

                              (Probably can serve as a source of Conditions/XP, not sure.)

                              (I just like the idea of showing alternate selves of a person, or the idea of what they'd be like, to taunt and challenge a Mage.)

                                      The Womb: A Rank 4 Entity, The Womb is one of the many Supernal reflections of the Concept of monstrous fertilities. When Summoned, it doesn't take a form on its own: instead, it takes over even the tiniest bits of organical matter inside and around her Summoning Circle, using his Supernal Powers to make them evolve in progressively stranger and more complex organisms, all meant as biological murder machines, full of claws and fangs and unnaturally placed appendages. Said offspring starts fighting against every other living organism they see: at first, that includes one another, but soon enough, an alpha, the most refined predator born of the Womb, takes their lead and organized slaughter ensues. If the summoner manages to tame, kill, or otherwise asserting its dominance over this alpha, he wins the Trial: the Womb stops its production, but its progeny remains under his command for the Duration of the Entity's stay in the Fallen World. The subtelty of the Trial is to wait long enough to let the Womb develop the most evolved (and therefore useful) progeny as possible... while not succumbing against the increasingly lethal horde.

                                      The Harem: A remote cousin of the Womb (if such familial analogy holds when it comes down to Supernal Denizens), the Harem is another Rank 4 Supernal Entity, this time a Wraith stemming from Pandemonium. Called upon by Mastigos well versed in the Mind and Life Arcana, it appears as a group of strikingly beautiful subjects. As soon as the Harem enters the Fallen World, each of its iteration will engage one bystander (including the Summoner) in Social Maneuvering, with the objective “Lead that person to betray her own objectives or ties of loyalty”. The summoner’s gambit is that his Mastigos self-control will be enough to keep the temptation at bay longer than his enemies. Usually, that suffices. Usually.

                                          Autocide: A Rank 3 Moira from Arcadia, wielding Death and Fate (or maybe a Wraith with Death and Mind... not sure yet).

                                          Autocide appears like an extremely cheerful nurse, usually with a mug of coffee in one hand and a joint in the other: when she laughs - which she does often-, her squinted eyes flash with a silvery gleam. Contrarily to many Entities, Autocide doesn't draw a clear line between its Trial and its services: its abilities are both a gift and curse, and simply requiring her to perform them implies that the Summoner is ready to stomach the consequence.

                                          Autocide is only Summoned to one desperate purpose: fake one's death, Whether by using Death to give to the mage's body the appearance of a corpse, or Fate to allow him to survive a lethal event unbeknowst to anyone else. The Catch ? Autocide will also manage to make everyone happy about it. All those who acknowledge the Summoner's death (and particularly those who loved him and were loved by him) will be showered in Boons and other positive Fate effects, meant to make them thinking of that loss as the best thing that ever happened to them: or, at least, make them forget the "deceased" altogether. The Summoner will have to witness that happiness, and choose: either breaking it through resurfacing (proving that he deliberately lied about his death), or cut the ties. Unless he also becomes convinces that everyone would be happier without him, and decides to really kill himself...

                                              No spirits, huh? Let's get a spirit in here.

                                              Watches In Silence - a Rank 5 spirit of a city's university. Originally it was (and for the most part is still) a spirit of information, feeding on the knowledge generated by the students, professors, and researchers that studied and taught at the university. Such institutions contain plenty of books, computers, academic papers stored physically or electronically, and other forms of information.
                                              During the early days of the university, Watches In Silence began as a spirit of a book. As the university grew, the spirit fed on other spirits of the court of knowledge - later becoming the spirit of the library, and later becoming the spirit of the university at rank 4.
                                              However, as Watches In Silence grows to represent the university at large, and as more and more spirits of the courts of emotion and ideology become fat off of the stress and ideological stances of those who study and teach - Watches In Silence has begun to feed off the negative emotions and ideologies, for how easy is it to disguise an ideology as fact - or belief as knowledge?

                                              Rank 5

                                              Attributes: Power 15, Finesse 12, Resistance 14
                                              Willpower: 10 (max for spirits ranked 1-5)
                                              Initiative: 26
                                              Defense: 12
                                              Speed: 27 (species factor 0)
                                              Size: 5
                                              Corpus: 19
                                              Essence: 50

                                              Bane - the original manuscript of an academic paper covering scientific research. It must be authored solely by professors (and their students) who worked at the university during its first 10 years. In modern days, this may refer to the first printout of such a paper that matches the published version - often printed by the professor for proofreading.

                                              Ban - Watches In Silence cannot harm, influence, or otherwise compel a high ranked member of the university's administrative branches - including deans, program directors, presidents, and other administrative branches. It's attacks do not harm any such administrator, and it cannot grapple them either. It is also unable to target them with any of its numina, influences, or manifestations, but it can feed off of their resonance.

                                              Influences - Information 4, Extremism 1, Stress 1

                                              Manifestations - Twilight Form, Discorporate, Reaching, Image, Fetter, Possess

                                              Numina (dice pool 27): Aggressive Meme, Dement, Emotional Aura, Implant Mission, Innocuous, Left-Handed Spanner, Mortal Mask, Pathfinder, Sign, Telekinesis
                                              Last edited by oeruli; 11-15-2017, 09:25 PM. Reason: Modified ban to apply to high ranked administrators instead of tenured STEM professors. Note some professors still count for this ban.

                                                          Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post
                                                          I like it, but it would have to be a really influential university, perhaps even internationally famous to have its own Rank 5 spirit. You have any in mind, real or invented?

                                                          Also, why only STEM professors? Why not historians or music professors?
                                                          I'm glad you like it.

                                                          1. Indeed, it would have to be something along the lines of an ivy league university in the United States, or other well known universities such as Oxford or Cambridge - any internationally famous university as you pointed out. I didn't specify one so that it can be left open for use. It would certainly be appropriate to weaken it to rank 3 or 4 for less well known universities.

                                                          2. The bane and ban could also potentially be expanded to include all professors tenured at the university (I have a different idea I will discuss below). The main idea was to exclude political science professors. A professor of political science could easily be used as a tool to teach extremist ideals to impressionable students. Then again, anyone is open to stress, and anyone is open to extremist ideals. Any professor tenured at the university would make sense - it's hard enough to become a tenured professor as is.

                                                          In retrospect, I think it makes more sense for the ban to apply to high ranked administrators of the university - deans, presidents, heads of administrative departments, etc. The purview of Watches In Silence is information and knowledge, as well as its transfer. The spirit could feed off of the sharing and teaching of information between professors and students, but would be at a loss with how to handle the administration. Professors are included in this, but not all professors - only professors with high ranked administrative rolls such as deans and program directors.
