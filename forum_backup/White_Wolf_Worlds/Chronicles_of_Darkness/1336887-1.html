                                                    Originally posted by Leliel View Post
                                                    Reading the preview:

                                                    Not a criticism, but, uh, how many fans of shonen and seinen are there at Onyx Path? Because this is the most gleefully hot-blooded thing they've ever done. There's even lore justified season progression (the Web of Pain, the realization the evil organization you just crushed has superiors and/or lieutenants that want to reform it, or worse rivals).
                                                    I've been known to indulge in some "anime and wine" nights with my housemate (currently working through My Hero Academia), so the DNA is in there. But in truth, there are so many books and movies and shows that work as "Deviant stories" that it's surprisingly easy to find them.

                                                    Originally posted by Grimmi05 View Post
                                                    So reading the preview and I like what I see so far. One problem.... they didn't put the Six Million Dollar Man under tv shows for inspirational material
                                                    As I allude to above, OMG, you guys, I was already over word count by like 8,000 words! Yeah, the inspirational media list is ridiculously short just because we had to cram so many other things into the core that I fantasized about TTRPG books that use Time Lord technology so they're like a TARDIS, except with words. Dave gave me a list of something like 100 different media characters that inspired him, and I barely recognized a quarter of them, but the more I worked on this game, the more it seemed like I saw Deviant stories everywhere I looked, and I could probably give you a separate list of 100 media examples of Deviant characters or Deviant stories.

                                                    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post

                                                    Nope. Buffy is a Genotypal Deviant, Born with abilities that had been engineered into her lineage.
                                                    A Genotypal Coactive, specifically, yes - starts out Devoted and becomes a Renegade in later seasons. She was one of the ones on Dave's list that I knew well enough to go, "ah yes, of course!"

                                                    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                                                    I'm sure they will be. That said, I'm expecting a “build your own conspiracy” setup, with one or two examples of what a Conspiracy might look like; and if the Chiron Group isn't mentioned, it will be easy enough to use it anyway.
                                                    One of the cardinal rules of CofD is that we don't do crossover in core books (Beast being a notable exception, for obvious reasons), so Cheiron doesn't get a mention, although it is a pretty easy one to tie in. If I get to do a Storytellers Guide (the traditional place for crossover mechanics), the Conspiracies of Hunter are almost universally shining examples of potential Web of Pain conspiracies (as I've gone on about at some length in the large Deviant thread on these forums). And "build your conspiracy" is a part of character creation. The core doesn't have one or two example conspiracies. It has *11* (plus a twelfth that makes a guest appearance). They're in Chapter Six, though, so it will be awhile before the previews reveal them.
