    Thoughts about a crossover with Exalted

    07-26-2022, 01:54 PM
    Howdy there, I've been thinking about crossovers between CofD and Exalted for a while now, I'd like to share my own thoughts then hear yours.
    It occured to me not long ago that there are two ways that I could make a fun crossover. You could say that Creation is one of the many worlds that exist in the CofD universe,and you could have the CofD's own Gods and Godlike beings learn the secret of Exaltation by watching creation with their great power
    Of course,one possible impediment is that the Gods of the CofD already have agents in the world. The Dark Mother has her Children,the Beasts. The God Machine has its Angels and Judges of Duat have their Mummies and so on. However,the Cofd gods are quite ineffable,unfathomable and numinous. Thus, as homebrewers we can say they might want a second set of agents and Chosen. I dare say some of the Gods of the Chronicles of Darkness might even delight on having two sets of Children fighting each other.
    There's also another fact to consider,those familiar with Forsaken metaphysics will for sure know that CofD has its own Sun God,Moon Goddess and even star gods.
    Again,as a homebrewer I can honestly say that I think if Helios and CofD's own Luna would see their twins in the alternate world of Creation,they just might feel something akin to a sense of one-upmanship: Helios might go "the Unconquered sun has it's Solars,I have my Helions,hell with that I'll create Solars room then I'll have two sets of servants!" And so on.
    With that in mind,I'd like to ask you those who are fans of both gamelines and settings . If the many Gods of the CofD would create Exalted,what do you think they'd be like?

                                          The Punisher Exalted

                                          The Judges of Duat are not what one calls the introspective type, they're too self absorbed in their divine sentences to look at themselves and realise what can be done in the way of self improvement.
                                          Improvement of their servants on the other hand... Oh they always think of that.
                                          When they discovered the secret of Exaltation from excavation through the sands of Time until they realised a new and improved servant was ready to be made.
                                          Even as arrogant as they are, the Judge's did take the Law of Diminishment and the possibility of rebellion into account. They Pondered,how to make sure their new toys/weapons/slaves wouldn't turn on them?
                                          They finally realised in a short moment,that the best slaves are the ones who think their master is a sucker they are manipulating. So their Exaltation sought the arrogant and the mad,the foolish and the vengeful.
                                          Plenty of those,these days.
                                          The ones who offer the Exaltation are not Avatars,nor are they the Mummies. The Judge's send their offers for power through tiny and innocent enough looking beings of duat. The messenger .. that duatling …looks like it couldn't be much of a threat to anyone. So when it offers the power of a Chosen,when it speaks with the deep voice of the Judges. The humans are not suspicious they are being manipulated into slavery, they accept.
                                          That sets the tone for the relationship between Judge and Chosen,the master never overtly orders his new weapon or even shows up in his power. He simply leads his servant into situations where realizing the desire of a Judge is the most convenient
                                          It's all in the fine print.

                                          Traits
                                          The Punisher Exalted are intended for Celestial play

                                          Anima:
                                          Their Animas are Black and Golden,often shining with hues of precious jewels
                                          Punisher Exalted can spend one more to force anyone who has committed what they consider a great sin in medium range to bleed in a strange form of stigmata.
                                          For 3 notes they can mimic the judges magnificent visage gaining 3 dice on an inspire roll.
                                          For 5 Notes they can create a small environmental hazard in the shape of a Sandstorm,a swarm of beetles or something even weirder
                                          For 10 Notes they can pinpoint the exact direction of any mummy.

                                          Martial Arts
                                          They don't have the Terrestrial Keyword,nor the Mastery keyword

                                          Sorcery
                                          They can access the celestial circle

                                          Evocations
                                          Resonant with starmetal,dissonant with rest.

                                          Excellencies:
                                          Usually they can only add (Attribute to a roll) but if they are doing something that involves one of their Intimacy,they can gain Intimacy dice.

                                          P.S (Used Ex3 rules for them,will see if a conversion between exalted and CofD exists later)
