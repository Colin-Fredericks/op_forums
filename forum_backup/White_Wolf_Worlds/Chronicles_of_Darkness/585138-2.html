                            Why do you think Obrimos is not fit for the entire "deal with the devil" aspect? For starters, the term angels is just what mage society calls them. They have nothing to do with the biblical angels - unless you want them to.

                            One picture of the aether is an entirely burning world, everything is in flames. This is as close to either the medieval image of hell, as you can get, or it can also be an image for judgement day, where the sinners are all burned.
                            Angels by default don't look like winged humans in white. They can also look like dragons, gryphons, other mystical beasts assotiated with fire/energy/movement...
                            With everything reflecting energy, forces and light, you get an image that could be very close to a biblical heaven (if you focus on the light parts), or hell (if you go for fire), or entirely unrelated to it, e.g. if you consider that whether is also a domain of the realm => storms and rain and lightning come to mind. Also: energy and forces can be quite destructive, regardless if they are vulcanos, earthquakes, explosions, nuclear bombs or evaporizing laser beams, etc....

                            Combining any supernal realm with christian faith is just a question of coming up with the right ideas. Pandemonium can e.g. actually look like the the catacombs under Rome or old temple ruins, Stygia might have sanctified aspects of graveyards, or maybe there's a huge cathedral, where the souls of the dead are waiting for judgement day... the primal wild could be really biblical in the sense of the story of the creation of the world and then:"And God blessed them. And God said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth and subdue it, and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing that moves on the earth.” (Gen 1.28). That doesn sound VERY primal wild to me: Procreate, hunt, conquer. If you look for beasts in the bible, the old testament has enough of them, right now I remember the story with Jonah and the wale. Arcadia is probably the most creative realm, and the fae might give you the clearest image of heaven - if they wish so. And that's just as likely as that they show you a torture chamber or a pagan ceremony.

                                    Watching 2nd season of Penny Dreadful ( especially episode 3 ) I got final inspiration to create truly witch sabbath Nameless Order. So here it is.

                                    Nameless Order - Lovers of Devil
                                    For almost millennium, Church has accused many women and man of contacts with Devil and getting sorcery powers from this. Many Awakened were misunderstood because of it – to Christians what was Supernal has become Hell, spells became curses and High Speech of Atlantis was Devil’s Tounge. Lovers of Devil are Nameless that embraced those accusations and worship Dark One as their patron. More, they really can summon things that anyone could only describe as demons from Hell.

                                    Awakened from Pentacle or Seers alike agree that Lovers have made in compact with some being from Lower Depths that want to spread sin and wickedness. Mysterium scholars call this dimension as Inferno, but any other can simply call it Hell. As now labeled as Left Hand practice, it lived for almost one thousand years on outskirts of Awakened communities. Most of new members were coming from Nameless that were left on their own – when they uncovered cursed Grimoire or relict of previous sabbath, Lovers come in compact with lower demons from this realm and form covens around it. Their patrons give them clues and advices to find other of their kind, forming loose alliance over whole countries or continents.

                                    Order Tools: Blood, sacrifice, orgies.

                                    Initiation
                                    1: Patron – Novice in coven need to make Pact with Patron of it, being able to communicate with Inferno demon by rituals.
                                    2: High Speech ( as Devils Tongue )
                                    3: Vice-Ridden ( CoD Rulebook )
                                    4: Library (Occult) 3
                                    5: Summoning – Highest witch in sabbath can summon it’s Patron as instant action by connection it have with it.
