    Originally posted by Reighnhell View Post
    Personally, out of all the organizations, I hope the AKD gets a pretty big re-write. I like the flavor of Relic hunters, but I find the animosity with Mages and Werewolves to be kind of forced and awkward. A slight shift of flavor to make them hunters of cursed and dangerous bygones, relics and artifacts as a central theme would be welcome.
    That's funny, I found the AKD's issues with Mages and Werewolves as a natural outgrowth of their "artifact-hunting" base premise. Since Mages and Werewolves also have hunting down mystical items (albeit of distinctly different flavors) as a large part of what they do, it naturally places them into conflict with AKD.

    I also think that shifting their focus to specifically "cursed" items, or things that are just too dangerous to be out in the world, a little too narrow. It's already difficult to avoid Warehouse 13 comparisons (not that that's necessarily a bad thing), and the cool thing about the AKD is you might very well have an otherwise insurmountable problem until some old archivist type smiles and says "you know, I have just the thing. . ."

                So, I have a few comments and ideas.
                AKD and mummies make almost too much sense to be ignored, but I don't see an inherent conflict. AKD has been around for a very long time, I think its very likely they know about and have operational information on Mummies. I mean the AKD might even have a few Mummy members, if the Iremite can remember. The AKD also have a unique perspective among hunter groups, they play the super long game. Their goal is to gather more relics and punish the things that broke the world which is similar enough to the Mummy goal that they likely agree on a number of things.

                Endowments and tiers should be tied to one another. I think every compact and every conspiracy should have their own set of endowments. I also believe endowments should scale with tier, with T3 having the biggest, baddest things available. Each tier of endowments includes the lower tier's things. This method ensures that at each level of play every compact/conspiracy means something other than a filled in blank spot. This of course assumes that tiers are rewritten as scope of play and that we abolish compact and conspiracy being tied to tiers and let folks play their chosen group at any level.

                            Well… yes, and no. I’m certainly not opposed to having the Possessed in the ranks of the Lucifuge; but neither do I think that every member of the org that isn’t a Child of the Seventh Generation is instead Possessed — indeed, Possessed members ought to be rare. As well, the whole point of the exercise is to feature the Lucifuge org in chronicles where the protagonists don’t have Supernatural Merits.

                            Fortunately, I also don’t buy the argument that the only valid way to play the Lucifuge org is “struggling with your inner demon”. Yes, that’s the way to play those who have the org’s Endowment; but without it, the focus shifts to “fighting the demonic”, with an emphasis on supporting/monitoring those with demonic tendencies who are fighting said tendencies. If you don’t find that compelling, fine; it’s you prerogative not to feature any Lucifuge who don’t have its Endowment.

                                I don't feel like going hardcore into the Lucifuge despite them being my favorite cause ahm tired. But that said, the themes of the organization are heritage and circumstance versus choice, namely summarized as compassionate determination of monstrous standing(The Denial are redeemers, but most of the Lucifuge has some inclination towards it, cause if they can fight off evil, anyone else can) and fighting fire with fire(The Reconciliation stands as a group representative, but by and large a group that grabs the forces of evil to fight evil has to be considered such).

                                Also Data, while I recognize that character can be a member of these organizations without the endowments, trying to divorce Endowments from purpose is silly, particularly for the Lucifuge. Endowments are a huge part of the culture and the direction of any organization that has them. Humans beings are tool using creatures-we build our entire society around them, and that's going to go double for the Conspiracies. Trying to divorce the Lucifuge from Castigations or Task Force Valkyrie from Advanced Armory as a whole is castration, pure and simple.

                                On a final note, one does not need to summon hellfire to have inner demons. Even if a Lucifuge has no Castigations, the entire idea of the organization is that, as a member of a demonic heritage, you have to deal with abnormaly strong unsavory urges.

                                Individual characters choices do not shape the whole of an organization. Even people by themselves don't wholly do that.

                                                I think the Lucifuge strongly needed the Reconciliation to be written as the fatalistic, mystical monster slayers.The other two branches serve to humanize the organization; they're hunters who are easy to relate to and understand. These groups have lived in shadow and secret, fighting things that shouldn't exist for (in some cases) centuries. They should not be completely understandable or graspable. Killing to redeem the devil is a great example.
