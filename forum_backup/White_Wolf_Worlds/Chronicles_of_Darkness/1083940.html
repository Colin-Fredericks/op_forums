    Is it impossible for mages to work magic in space? If so, why?

    Or is magic just different in space? Is it the same?

                      There's no reason it wouldn't work in 2nd edition. In fact, I've had to (jokingly) house rule in my game that just because you can see the Moon, it doesn't mean that it's in sensory range.

                      Most of the problem with mages and doing crazy things like teleporting to the moon or another planet revolve around how difficult it is to get there and then survive there. Can't teleport to the moon because you don't have any sympathetic connection to the moon. Same for another planet. Where could you find such things? I'd say out in the astral solar system of the Anima Mundi. A mage who wants to go to Mars can start his adventure by going inwards into the Astral and then finding a sympathetic connection lingering on Sidereal Mars.

                          The astral does not have sympathetic connections with the things they represent. Spatial sympathy comes from direct interaction. The best bet to find a sympathetic connection to the Moon or Mars is finding a sample brought back to Earth.

                          Also, you don't have to house rule the moon being out of sensory range since it's impossible to target something of that Size anyway, and the naked eye can't distinguish areas small enough for AoE spells.
