                    I suppose, but those are all a little cliche. Personally, here's what I'd do. I'm just gonna go with whatever gender I happen to choose for each one, so, assume a bi or pan protagonist, I guess?
                    • Vampires: A bored smart-ass who happens to be immortal, and has seen so many neonate edgelords that any time he sees a vamp slinking about in black robes quoting Byron or some shit, he feels compelled to scream "CRAAAAAAAAWLING IN MY SKIIIIIIIIN" at the top of his lungs. He wears pretty simple, plain clothes, with maybe a few very small touches of color here or there. Depending how "alt" he is, he might have his hair dyed a dark-ish red.
                    • Werewolf: At first glance, maybe kind of a "hippie" chick. Then she takes you out hunting for a weekend and you realize that she's also improbably proficient with a crossbow. She's sweet, she's peaceful, she just broke that guy's arm for grabbing her ass. She is, in a word, "scarousing."
                    • Mage: A hard-core Libertine activist, with a restrictive Seer daddy who's unaware of her Order association. Her route will result in the both of you being arrested at some point. She's pretty rich, but she clearly tries to dress like she's not, to the point that it seems like she's ashamed of her wealth, probably because of how her family came by it. Do not discuss economics with her unless you have studied the hell up.
                    • Promethean: Galateid, and absolutely god-damned-drop-dead gorgeous girl. Unfortunately, not everyone recognizes that she is a girl. She's dressed as cutely as possible at all times, partly as a sort of defiance, but mostly because she likes feeling pretty. Her route is arguably the most involved and difficult, due to her pretty justified defensiveness and Disquiet.
                    • Changeling: Part of a really, really tight-knit clique, that the protagonist later finds out is also a support group. He dresses really plainly in public or when his parents are around, but in his route, he'll take you with him to a concert, and he will be wearing the most aggressively punk-rock get-up imaginable.
                    • Demon: She just really likes dressing up. "Clothes horse" describes him to a tee, and the best way to her heart is to go clothes shopping with him.
                    • Geist: The class clown. He pulls pranks that should, quite honestly, kill him. Sometimes he does the whole "wearing black" thing, but even then, he makes it his own. He doesn't ever really have a consistent style, but he just kinda takes whatever doesn't smell too dodgy from his clothes bin and makes it work.


                    Genius: the Transgression 2E is a thing that's being worked on.

                                                        For the hunters, I'll do some of the Conspiracies:

                                                        Aigis Kai Doru: A nerdy, bookish girl who hides behind the mask of an amoral, almost mechanical killer of monsters. In reality, the only time we see her "game face" is when she's pursuing monsters she knows are completely inhuman; she's secretly a pacifist who distrusts her superiors. Her ending involves her either killing you in the bed after being bullied into it (and starting a vengeful rebellion against the AKD) or quitting and provoking a split in the Guardians between traditionalists and reformists who are sick of being dragged around by a myth that their information tells them isn't even true.

                                                        Cheiron Group: The "rich snobby genius" character. He was volunteered for treatment in intelligence-increasing Thaumatech when he was young, and this has led him to be a prodigy and utterly ruthless businessman. Secretly, he's utterly miserable; he isn't sure if he's human, and he displays a far nicer side to his personality once he becomes aware you're dating (after bluntly asking why you're attracted to him when it's clear he's bad news and probably will hurt you-the answer that solidifies a date is "Because you're afraid of being bad news").

                                                        Knights of St. Adrian: Your typical Lad-ette; female and proud of it, but a tomboy's tomboy with her motorcycle and love of big explosions. Her real arc starts up, however, when she meets the demon-and realizes that the Unchained does not match her mental image of the Infernal at all, and leading her to realize who and what she's working for. She ends up shutting herself away in a house for a while to stew in self-loathing and guilt unless you coax her out-and convince her not to go out in a blaze of glory, taking a large amount of Infrastructure with her (mostly by convincing her it'd be more effective to blow it up steathily and safely).

                                                        Merrick Institute: The emo kid. Mostly it's an affectation, but he's genuinely depressed after they experimented on him and stole his life. He doesn't hate Beasts. being a friend of one of the above, and probably one of the nicest LIs. Also the one most loyal to his Conspiracy as a whole.

                                                        Malleus Maleficarum: You guessed it, she's the nun. A very unhappy nun who has lost any respect she had for the Church. She's the most blatantly angry at the core of her Conspiracy, having discovered what Baudolino was and that he hunts vampires to maintain his immortality, after she lost several loved ones to sacrificing themselves for the Shadow Congregation. She is in a very dark place when you meet her, all hiding it under a placid smile; she actually ends up propositioning you (actually earlier if you're a woman) soon after you realize you're no friend of the Inquisition, mostly because she's spiting everything and everyone connected with the Malleus. The darkest LI, but there's two happy endings with her-one where she finally works up the will to be honest about her broken faith and why, and another where she learns who Baudolino is and comes to forgive him-though she's still excommunicated, she retains her Benedictions in that case.

                                                        VASCU: A hunky cop with psychic powers. There's really not much more to explain characterization-wise, he's a typical Noir-ish detective. The other bisexual hunter LI, gets involved due to pursuing the main antagonist.

                                                        The Main Antagonist: A Legend Slasher who went psycho after hunters killed her mage wife. She's "the Hunter of Hunters", someone called to hurt the Vigil back. She is not a good person; while she technically has a route in the hunter-focused game, she's just using you to get close to her targets (as shown by her "attraction" to males; she loves you not at all, it's not hard to fake sexuality). At best, her ending has her grow fond of you...so she takes your heart, literally, and makes your soul into her pet. It's made quite clear this is even worse than her murdering you, because she uses you as a target of her aggression.

                                                        EDIT: The plot for the hunter-focused game, if they have their own, revolves around the theme of revenge; all the LIs have people they hate for understandable reasons, with their hatred consuming them to varied degrees. The bad endings of each route involve the LI becoming an Avenger for some reason or another (naturally, the antagonist has been consumed a long time ago-there's no way to save her). The last scenes all involve them planning their first murder and discovering their MO.
