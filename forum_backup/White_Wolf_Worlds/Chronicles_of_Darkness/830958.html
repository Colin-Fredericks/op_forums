    Ready-Made Antagonists, Heroes and assorted NPCs

    02-16-2016, 07:53 AM
    I figured we could use a thread to post all sorts of NPCs for Beast's chronicles, a place where to quickly find ready characters when the need arises or, why not, even some inspirations. The Dark Mother knows if this sort of thread can prove useful



    Here's a NPC I made. She's one I was talking about in the Character Idea Thread, statted and all

    Doctor Rhiannon Davies
    Background: Rhiannon's childhood could have been described as unremarkable. Her parents loved her, she managed to make some friends, her shyness notwithstanding, and she lived a quite ordinary life overall. And yet, her outstanding intellect always made her feel like she was out of place. She tried really hard to fit and be more like “a normal girl”, someone her parents could understand better but it was an effort doomed to fail. After many years of self-restraint, Rhiannon abruptly rejected the idea that she was the one that had to change and isolated herself from the others in response.

    Rhiannon was already fascinated by the secrets behind what makes someone different from the others and if there's a pattern that could explain why someone is born the way it is, but it was only when her mother was diagnosed with a terrible neurodegenerative disease that she focused her efforts on the study of genetics, terrified at the idea that she could inherit the same illness and not be able to do something about it. Rhiannon soon became a well respected, if reclusive, expert of the sector, but the early onsets of the same disease that plagued her mother had begin to show.

    Then came the Devouring. If you ask Rhiannon, she recounts it as something that happened not because of other Beasts, but because of the Dark Mother herself. Her second mother, her real mother, had come to save her and love her in a way that her mortal parents just could not. The Devouring erased the disease from her brain and gave her a new purpose. A scientific mind at heart, Rhiannon firmly believes that she can find a trail to the Dark Mother encoded in the genes of living creatures, reasoning that the fear she represents has left a mark on our DNA. She soon learned that such effort would require an occult expertise that she was not inclined to learn, but she's working on that. To Rhiannon, finding a common pattern that she could use as a map to what she likes to call “Mitochondrial Eve of Nightmares” is a way not only to show her gratitude to the Dark Mother, but also to finally find an explanation to her nature, a discovery that would shed light on the existance of all the members of the Family.

    Rhiannon's Devouring unlocked her potential even more. The task she's appointed herself to requires a supernatural intellect to even be remotely possible, something she clearly possess. Rhiannon is not asocial, but she's reserved and does not love the company of others. Her collegues dislike her, feeling that she does not socialize, but the few times she bothers to share her results, they're often ground-breaking. Secretly, Rhiannon hoards vials of blood of people she finds interesting. Whether because of a biological anomaly or because of a peculiar attitude, if Rhiannon sees someone as not ordinary and worthy of note, she'll do whatever she can to obtain a sample. Hidden at her home there's a veritable collection of such vials. Sometimes, when taking the blood, she has a taste of it herself. Most of it is to feed her Hunger, but some, especially that of Beasts, Heroes and supernaturals, it's taken to further her research. She also often steals items that she believes tell a lot about someone's identity. The motorcycle of a biker met in a bar, the college thesis of a literature student...in a way Rhiannon is hoarding knowledge and identities.

    Rhiannon's Kinship include a couple of vampires she became friend with. They don't know each other and Rhiannon likes it that way. She finds herself at ease with them and has quickly embraced the idea of Family, but she's afraid that they will bond with each other and discard her if introduced. One of them is a Gangrel barfly of the Carthian Movement that helps her hunt and keep track of who's around, the other is a Nosferatu of the Ordo Dracul she frequently consults during her studies.

    Description: Rhiannon is in her late twenties. She's often described as gaunt and cold, but that's more because of a general impression she gives than her real looks. She rarely bothers to wears something different from her lab coat, even at home, and when she goes out to Feed or with her Kin she usually wears plain and unremarkable clothes. There's a certain degree of alluring aura in her but it's somewhat countered by her clinical attitude. Rhiannon is perfectly able to socialize with others (she often uses this to make people lower their guard before taking their blood) but she's a bit insufferant about what she does not find stimulating. Unless asked, she does not talk much but she gets passionate if the conversation veers towards topics that interest her.

    Rhiannon's Horror resembles a classical siren as much as the creatures from the deepest abyss resemble the fish from above. Slender, vicious and covered in cartilage protrusions, the hairless monstrosity's visage has nothing in common with a human face. No mouth, no nose, just a pair of wide, milky eyes. She usually feed with the lamprey mouths located on her four clawed hands or pierces the body of her victims with the retractable bone proboscis she can grow from her arm and drains the blood.

    Rhiannon's Lair: imagine an ocean of blood, eerily clear enough to see perfectly through the "water". The surface is not in sight, even if there's some source of light coming from above, though it's not clear if that's a sun or something else. Everything is distorted by the blood and assumes a red tint. If you go deep enough, you'll start noticing the skeletons of titanical beasts floating inside the gore, the deeper you go, the more you find. With some attention you'll be able to hear a distant beat, like a heart. By following it, you'll eventually get to a seafloor made of bone. Inside its chasms, you'll find the literal Heart of the Lair.

    Storytelling Hints: Rhiannon is not exactly evil but what she does still is not pleasant. She has no time to teach people lessons right now, feeling that her research will be more beneficial than any trivial lesson other Beasts impart. She will greet other Beasts as Family and be happy to talk with them (without sharing the details of her work, of course, she deserves the credit) but can become detached, even hostile, if she feels they disapproved her methods and threaten her research. She has no love for Heroes and will help anybody who has a problem with them as soon she's assured to get a sample

    Rhiannon can eventually veer to the deep end and start to experiment some theories on living beings, including other Beasts and members of the Family. If that happens, she could be showed that she's gone too far, but it won't be easy to do so without screwing up. Rhiannon's really, really, determined.

    Rhiannon's research is far from obtaining substantial results, but she's gathered all the knowledge about the Dark Mother she could find. Getting a peek at her library or personal notes can prove enlightening.

    In a chronicle, she can be used either as antagonist, showing the detachement some Beast can reach, as a strong ally with almost encyclopedic knowledge about science and the Dark Mother or even just as a bizarre NPC.

    Legend: Relentless
    Life: Curious
    Family: Makara
    Hunger: Hoard
    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 6, Wits 4, Resolve 5
    Physical Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
    Social Attributes: Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 4
    Mental Skills:Academics 3, Computer 2, Craft 4, Investigation 3, Medicine 5 (Hematology), Occult 3 (Dark Mother), Science 5 (Genetics)
    Physical Skills: Brawl 1, Firearms 1, Larceny 2 (Breaking and Entering), Stealth 2
    Social Skills: Intimidation 2, Persuasion 1,Subterfuge 1
    Merits: Area of Expertise (Genetics), Contacts (Researchers, Hospitals, Medical Community) 3, Double Jointed, Double Jointed (Advanced), Eidetic Memory, Epic Potential (Intelligence), Guilty Pleasure (Blood) Library (Medicine) 3, Library (Science) 3, Library (Occult) 1, Killer Instinct 1, Patient, Resources 3, Safe Place 4, Status 2 (Scientific Community)
    Atavisms: Heart of the Ocean, Limb from Limb, Relentless Hunter, Siren's Treacherous Song
    Nightmares: All Your Teeth Are Falling Out, Everything You Do is Worthless, It's In Your Blood, You Are Alone, You Are Infected (Vampire)
    Lair: 4 (Currents, Flooded, Suffocating, Rotting)
    Health: 8
    Willpower: 9
    Satiety: 4
    Size: 5
    Speed: 10
    Defense: 3
    Initiative: 7
    Armor: 0

    Rhiannon comes with a Nightmare included in the package. I'd appreciate feedback about this:

    It's In Your Blood (Oh hello, Mr. Lovecraft, nice to see you here)
    “There's nothing you can do to fight it. The abominable seeds planted generations ago are about to bloom. All the weaknesses and deviances of your ancestors are hiding in your own blood and calling for you. You're doomed to suffer their same fate as your own body and mind will betray you”

    Dice Pool: Intelligence + Satiety vs. Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance

    Normal: the victim gain the Paranoid Condition. She becomes convinced that there's some horrible secret or perversion that plagues her family which has tainted her very own genes. All those who can expose it or that are in the process of learning more about the past of her family and its members are seen as a threat.

    High Satiety: the victim gains the Broken Condition.

    Satiety Expenditure: the victims enter a state of fugue. She attempts to flee the area and will violently attack anybody that tries to stop her even in the slightest. If that happens, she won't stop until her target is dead, at which point she regains her sense of self and won't remember much of the events, aside from a nightmarish recollection that has nothing to do with what really happened

    Exceptional Success: For the duration of the Nightmare, the victim suffers a -2 penalty on breaking point rolls
