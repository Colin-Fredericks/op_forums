    Sell me on Glory

    05-15-2015, 07:41 PM
    With a lot of the Auspices I can picture pretty much exactly what they are supposed to be doing and their mechanics support this role. Rahu are giant blenders that murder everything in their wake. Full Moon gifts grant a bunch of combat buffs, and a lot of the Pure spirit gifts reinforce this with enough outliers that they aren't shoehorned completely. Irraka are ninja. They have gifts that help them ninja people in the kidneys and remain elusive/unseen. Ithaeur have the esoteric magic, information gathering and spirit angle covered. Their Gifts focus on taking down spirits, gathering information, and esoteric magic. Elodoth focus on finding and exploiting the truth / the Prey's support network and have the social angle largely covered. Their moon gift supports this, but their spirit gifts feel a bit all over the place.

    Then comes the Cahalith. In the fluff they are the storytellers and heralds. They rush in first and announce their prey's imminent destruction while bolstering their pack's moral with the stories they've collected. Okay, cool, I actually really like that. Then we turn to their gifts and... they're all over the place. Their Spirit Gifts while individually mostly okay, they don't collectively paint a picture of what the Cahalith is supposed to actually be doing on a hunt or to support their pack. They feel kind of like the pack Mario, a little good in everything without really excelling in anything. Their Pack buffs and mass debuffs (what I pictured them primarily doing) feel pretty underwhelming.

    Putting it in the context of a hunt...
    Against Werewolves
    Rahu fights everything.
    Irraka infiltrates and murders the key thing.
    Ithaeur gathers information and attacks their totem.
    Elodeth levies mortal influence, brokers deals between packs, and erodes their support network.

    Against Spirits
    Rahu fights everything it can.
    Irraka infiltrates and murders the key thing / delivers Bane directly to face.
    Ithaeur puts the spirit into a box the rest of his pack can actually affect.
    Elodeth manipulates essence through mortals, brokers deals with the spirit (courts) and damages cults.

    Against Humans
    Rahu fights everything.
    Irraka keeps the pack from getting caught.
    Ithaeur gathers information through spirit allies.
    Elodeth handles everything social.

    Against Ridden / Claimed
    Some combination of Humans and Spirits, depending on what exactly you are fighting.

    So what am I missing? Are the Cahalith better than they appear or am I completely off base with what they are supposed to be doing?

                  Originally posted by nofather View Post
                  During the hunt, they make the hunt as epic as possible. After the hunt, they tell stories of the hunt to to give a reason for things like renown to exist.
                  I appologize in advance for the number of f-bombs in this post. I mean no aggression or offense.
                  I just looked back through the Glory-based Shadow Gifts. I saw three main threads which follow the Cahalith's stated role of "know the story and tell it well" pretty closely.

                  1) Know what's going on - Read the World's Loom was the best example of this, and it has great synergy with the Cahalith's in-born ability to have visions and know what's up before anyone else.

                  2) Inspire the Pack - This Story is True and Fearless Hunter go really well with the Glory-based Merit of Song in Your Heart to make Cahaliths an inspiration that keeps the pack driving hard. If you didn't perform the Moon's Mad Love Rite on the human elements of your Pack, Glorious Lunacy is a great inspirational tool, too!

                  3) Make it fucking awesome - Pretty much every other Glory Gift seemed like it was about going balls-to-the-wall. With Barghest, even if it's dead you can still kill it. With Catastrophe you unleash a fire-storm across at least two miles. With Fog of War you send the bullet from one enemy to the other. With Black Earth, Red Hunger you get to say "and then the earth itself hungered for their blood. Vines and shoots cracked through the pavement and covered every inch of the alleyway, consuming their corpses in the process. No trace of them remained." With Unmake and a Glory of 3 (I imagine most Cahilith start at 2), you can stare down an oncoming pick-up truck, say "Fuck you" to it, and it will obligingly fall to pieces with the driver still going 65mph through the air. With Glory 5, you could do it to a tank and let the drivers get crushed by the rubble and exploding unspend munitions.

                  Cahilith are all about the response. To them it matters more than the effect; if you can make your team go "Fuck yeah!" and your enemies go "Holy shit!", you're 80% of the way there.

                                          I see the Cahalith as being research experts. They're not just meant to tell the stories of the Pack's exploits, but learn and share the stories of other packs stories. This helps provide important information such as tactics, personalities, how people could respond when the pack learns they need to deal with particular parties.

                                          The focus on learning stories also extends to delving into the histories of a location and spirits to learn important details. The lore of a region that a Cahalith can dig up can help pinpoint where important loci could be (and help the Irraka get where they need to be to actually find it). The Cahalith helps learn lore for the Itheaur to construct effective rituals or find appropriate offerings to appease spirits or powerful weapons in a spirits ban or bane. The Cahalith similarly helps the Elodoth with the research and person skills. Finally, the Cahalith can stand as a powerful combatant that can support and strengthen the Rahu.

                                          Everyone in the pack has a role. Each of the other auspices have important individual goals, and it can be hard to see where the Cahalith fits precisely, but that's because they don't. The Cahalith is woven to interact with each of those roles, providing support, intelligence and foresight to make the individuals in those roles even more effective.

                                          The Cahalith is a pillar than upholds the pack, makes it stronger, makes it able to withstand all sorts of blows and at the end of the day, keeps the pack focused on what they can and could accomplish so they stay unbowed and unbroken.

                                                  I pretty much just play Cahalith, so I'll add my insight. The Cahalith exists to push the pack to excel in every way, and then tell the stories of how badass the pack is. You push the pack to be the strongest, most powerful pack they can be, with things like Fearless Hunterand The Pack Triumphs Together. You tell the war stories, the hunting tales to the pups, so they do better; This Story is True and Know Thy Prey. When a different pack enters your territory, a Cahalith steps up and howls out the savage, mighty deeds of the pack, daring the intruders to act; War Howl and Voice of Glory.

                                                  Alternatively, a Cahalith can stand on their own, and operate without the Pack at their back. Prey will have no respite, can find no friend, and stop the Cahalith; Dream Hunter, Know Thy Prey and Fearless Hunter. A Cahalith is informed, persuasive, and can deny their prey those advantages, Needle, Sift the Sand, Know thy Prey, and Voice of Glory.
