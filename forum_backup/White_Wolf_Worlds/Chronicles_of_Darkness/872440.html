              What do you want to happen? Vampires are cliquish, catty, and callous, but are quick to recognize most existential threats. If you want your players at war with the vampires, the Prince might get pissed, or her Covenant will. If you want your players engaged in their politics, some rival of your dead vamp might try to sic the pack on another upstart. Plenty of Kindred just want to keep their hunting grounds in peace, and a pack of werewolves can threaten a territory, or protect it in the right circumstances.

              The quick and easy thing is to use the Chicago source book for the setting. Figure out what the repercussions should be for killing this vampire, identify or create the interested parties, and then make life hell for your pack.

              LFP: Foreign Bodies (Deviant: the Renegades)

                                  Ah, there is trouble brewing all around your players! Times of opportunity as well, it seems. My Chicago campaign is presently on ice (we're doing a bit of Mage, and then there's loads of good material in Dark Eras I'm dying to try out) so let's focus on your story for a while, from the vampire perspective.

                                  The South Side is currently a political battleground for the Kindred: for many years, discontent has been brewing in the Carthian Movement, targeted against the leadership of Barowski. The South Side holds a sizeable and organized Carthian population revolving around three assemblies (one of which is controlled by my players' coterie). Prince Max has long been looking for an opportunity to undermine the Carthian unity, and after a recent bloody incident involving a Belial's Brood infestation, he finally managed to turn the South Side Carthians (SSC) openly against the faction leadership. As a result, a large area to the south of Washington Park has been recently granted to the SSC assemblies to run a political egalitarian experiment (the "Experiment"), highly autonomous, completely free of their faction's influence and with the prince's backing.

                                  This new turn of events has produced considerable shifts in population, power and territory. Many neonates and low-influence vampires started settling in the SSC Experiment zone, considering it "virgin territory" in a city with otherwise well-established power structures. A wild-west attitude has sprung up, aided by the fact that the SSC have not yet refined their patrol and vigilance routines. Other supernaturals enjoy greater liberties in the Experiment, at least until someone begins fucking things up seriously. The mainstream Carthians are hostile against the Experiment and try to undermine it covertly at any opportunity, actively employing all sorts of agents (werewolves included). Meanwhile, Prince Max sits back and enjoys the spectacle.

                                  The vampire child may well have been under the protection of some SSC vampire, say the Gangrel Julia, an ex-prostitute who now serves as the protector of sex workers in the local red light district and has always had a big heart and compassionate attitude to outcasts. Julia is an elder and well respected in the SSC assemblies but holds no office. Perhaps she's looking to exact vengeance/justice for the child's death using the SSC, but she could also be convinced to turn her attention to those truly behind the killing and not the hitmen.

                                  A great place to stage werewolf-vampire encounters in the south side is the Zero Club, the new Elysium of the SSC Experiment. No supernatural powers, no feeding, no guns allowed. A three'story venue with private basement and emergency safe havens to those on Zero's (the Daeva owner's) good graces. Main theme: unattainable desire. I could go on and on about this club and the things that happen in there, I should even have a floor plan around somewhere.

                                  Do your PCs or their adversaries do/deal drugs? There's big commotion lately in west Washington Park, where gang wars have erupted. The Nosferatu Scratch and his mafiosi have tipped the balance by offing a high-ranking trafficker, and the are lots of corpses falling lately and lots of influence up for grabs. The Boars are presumed to be actively involved in the fight, perhaps joining Scratch's Machine, or fighting the mob boss for a big slice of the pie.
