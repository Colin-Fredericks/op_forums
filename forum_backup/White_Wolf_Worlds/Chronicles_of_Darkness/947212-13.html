                            Originally posted by Menace View Post
                            A question: how "Read In" heavy is deviant ? I mean how much does a player need to read to enjoy playing the game. More on the heavy part (like Werewolf/Mage - those have a ton of setting material that PC are somewhat assumed to know and there's a lot of mechanics) or the less heavy (Hunter, where it's possible to play on the "there are monsters and you hunt them" lvl).
                            From what I've read so far and from the basic concept of the game, I'd say it's probably more of the latter. Think of shows like Stranger Things or movies like Firestarter. The "experiment" doesn't necessarily know much about the organization that created them. They may not know much about the world at all. Trying to find out who is trying to hunt you down is usually part of the challenge of keeping your freedom (or exacting your revenge). So I'd say the players could go into the game almost blind to anything beyond the basic elements needed to play that specific character.

                            "All you know is the Farm. The Farm raised you. You never knew there was anything other than the Farm. And then they made you kill the kitten, and something broke inside you. You don't remember how you escaped. You don't know where you are. You don't know why they're hunting you. All you know is that you have to run... and that you can kill with your mind."
