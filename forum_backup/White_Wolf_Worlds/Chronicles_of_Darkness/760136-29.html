    New concept:

    Depth A Measurement of the Province’s overall separation of not just space, but from the reality, from the Surface world. As Depth increases, so does the chance of physics breaking….

    It goes all the way up to 10. Provinces that can have surface access directly go to 3. Most Monarchs don't venture below 5.


    The idea of Depth is to emphasize "You dug too deep" as a concept found in games like Dwarf Fortress or Mine Craft. The more valuable things are found as you get deeper in to the Earth. Coincidentally, so are the dangerous things.






    Also, more traits.
    Vitality Spring: This Province possesses special waters that improve healing and longevity with regular treatment. Those who drink from these waters heal at an accelerated rate and resist illness better for a week. Aggravated damage recovers in a single day, lethal in 10 minutes, and bashing every round.

    Watery: This Province in the Beneath is either flooded with water, is actually a subterranean sea, or even a normal sea unto itself. Aquatic movement for the Colony must be enabled to do actions here.

    Airy: This Province defies physics, being mostly an empty space or even possessing floating landmasses. There might even be a “sky” that stretches for miles. Regardless, airborne movement must be enabled for the Colony to act here.

    High Yield, Resource: Though this Province doesn’t produce more of a particular resource than other Provinces its size and industrial output, those that it does produce are of a higher quality. This particular Resource, when converted to Wealth and spent on Colony actions are worth 3 dice or triple the usual value.

    Surface Access: This Province has a route to the Surface or the Beneath. Normally, due to the esotic nature of the Beneath, it is near impossible to access it through purely physical means as those attempting to enter may not approach the right frequencies. This special shaft is one of the ways in, a tunnel of both real and fluxed space.The Kingmakers create these access points “above” new Colonies so that they might have freedom to explore a realspace environment while being safely entrenched in the Beneath.
    Stranger still is that a Surface Access does not require that a Province conform to or be close to the surface in a “objective sense. A Surface Access can seemingly tunnel through another Province and the ones using it would never know about the Province above… There is a requirement however that the Province be somewhat “close” to Realspace; any Province below Depth 3 cannot have a Surface Access.

    Gateway: This Province possesses a Gateway to somewhere else, a point of warped Space usually a tunnel or portal that leads to somewhere else. Usually this is another, but distant Province that also has a Gateway that links to the previous. This can lead to interesting or modified travel or expansion arrangements.






    I'm looking at the Geodics again since I figure I might as well work on them. I realized something as I come to understand what I wanted the Beneath to be.
    Maybe the reason that the Geodics did not take over everything in the interm over millions is a simple fact that... they can't.

    Like the Kingmakers, the Geodics cannot exist on the surface or else reality would reject their existence and destroy them. The Beneath is a place designed to help create stable reality bubbles that allow them to exist, but it's not perfect and it's not stable. It's the result of eons of failed reality warping experiments and the life forms that moved in... and evolved to these conditions. It's only been up until now that they've actually been able to make solid progress in that field...




    Geodics are also likely going to ironically be more "Hivemind" than even Monarchs. With a central intelligence core that manipulates and commands "fragments" or "splinters" of its overall self.

            Okay, so the rough relative timeline for Geodics looks like:
            1. Decide to colonize Earth.
            2. Find out there are compatibility issues.
            3. Start crafting stable realms (the Beneath, likely of the "deeper" depth).
            4. [Fight with other aliens?]
            5. Reach the point where they can abduct people (see Guardians/HCP backstory).
            6. Abduct people (why?).
            7. Abductees fight back and blow their operations to hell.
            8. Endless arrive.
            9. Advances made in colonization efforts.
            Thoughts/Suggestions: What if Geodics operate at the "lower" depths. They can have weaker versions or scouts that can operate in higher depths. They need to establish "Anchors/Stabilizers" to bring "full" Geodics up to higher Depths. The "Human Abduction Experiment" was an attempt to speed up the process, using human slaves to construct the Anchor/Stabilizer in "Realspace", so that the full Geodic force could operate there. The experiment... failed (probably should have never designed those suits for them, no matter how quickly it would have spread things up). When the Endless arrive, and the Kingmakers make their first Monarchs and establish Access points of their own, the Geodics see that they can exploit the paths already paved to speed up their invasion as well.

            Some Game-y ideas: Keep the Anchor/Stabilizer scheme. This means that one constructed in or near one of your Provinces means you have big mean Geodics to deal with, but if you knock out the Stabilizer, you can reduce the attacks to mere scout skirmishes (until they get another one up somewhere). On the other hand, a Stabilizer likely makes it easier for you to use your own physics breaking powers.

                                                        Beneath the World
                                                        The Beneath is the hidden world co-opted by the Endless to hide their Colonies in, so that they might grow and become numerous without interfering… or being intruded upon. However, this realm is not a creation of the Endless, it having existed for long before their arrival. The Kingmakers and many other beings are powerful enough to create points in and out of it, but they are not the true rulers. In fact, one might argue that in its current state, the Beneath does not have any “real ruler”.
                                                        The Geodics created it as an attempt to Crysiform an environment that could support themselves, a slow process that took billions of years, however they failed to do so in the initial setup phase, creating of a world that was beholden to no singular life form. Even worse, before they could rectify this, others moved in, carbon life and spirits, primitive beings that bent their world in different directions. And the more of them that are or the closer a region in the Beneath to the Surface, the more it confirmed to Terran standards of life.
                                                        The Beneath’s unique make-up is such that the dimension cannot truly decide what it is or what kind of physical model it is meant to emulate. As long as a life form can conform to the barest essentials of Euclidean physics are upheld, such as existing in a 3 Dimensional space and operating on Linear time as opposed to acasual, the lifeform in question can exist in the Beneath, treating it for the most part as their home dimension. This allows Terran Endless and humans to exist within it and Spirits when in its Realm are forced to manifest as in their home dimension.
                                                        However, many beings such as True Endless like the Kingmakers do not conform to Linear time or 3D space and thus cannot exist within the Beneath without special means. Unfortunately for the Geodics, the physics they exist in is still too different than that of most carbon life; because of its carbon centric domination close to the surface, more powerful Geodics cannot access the Surface without rapidly dying to the slightly altered physics or means to protect themselves.
                                                        One other trait of the Beneath’s geography is that… occasionally, the place warps and twists, tunnels leading to different sections that previously were located elsewhere… or never existed before. Surface Access points never lose their connections to the above ground, but the areas around those Provinces are often very different once you stop and notice it.


                                                        Denizens of the Beneath
                                                        Though the Beneath is primarily held under the control of either the Monarch Colonies or Geodic Coreminds, they are not the sole denizens of the Beneath. The life forms here have adjusted to life without the Sun’s light, changed by generations of gradual mutations due to altered physics; the Beneath allows many life forms to exist similar to their native environments, but it isn’t perfect, especially with multiple generations slowly adapting to Beneath life over their native environments.
                                                        The Beneath is home to a flourishing ecosystem of subterranean life. Mosses and lichens form the bases, with massive spires of fungi taking the role trees normally take. The Beneath though separated from the Sun’s light still possesses a sort of… ambient energy that stimulates photosynthesis or equivalent systems in plant life; again due to its altered physics.
                                                        Fauna varies, but most of it is recognizably descended from the surface world, though obviously… warped and more vicious than normal. Bioluminescence is a common adaptation with many species possessing some form of illumination; as a result, it’s rare to find patches of complete darkness.


                                                        Humans The most common indigenous sentients. Most humans live in primarily in a primitive hunter-gatherer state with some occasional pockets of rudimentary civilization in some areas. Most settlements are likely descended from ancient migrations that somehow ended up in the Beneath.
                                                        Generally, humans who have lived entire generations in the Beneath are pale skinned and require serious acclimatization to function in the Surface world, in both physicality and socially, especially those with unique languages or lacking any formal education.
                                                        For Monarchs, Beneath humans are ill-suited for warfare outside of command positions due to low replenishment rates, but as aides and companions, they have some uses, especially for Monarchs who want a touch of “home” in their “new” home… or even potential recruiting grounds for new Monarchs.



                                                        Mechanoids Not a race per say, but rather a collective designation referring to any multitude of constructs or machine entities ranging from “dumb” drones, self aware consciousnesses, and posthuman data constructs. Most Mechanoids are the remnants of mad experiments, leftovers from long dead alien races, or tools from surprisingly advanced civilizations that faded away from collapse. Many of these machines still function, either carrying out their final orders until disrupted or replicating biological cycles in metal frames, harvesting materials to bring back to their factory-nests. Others lie dormant, sleeping and waiting for someone to program directives.
                                                        For the most part, Monarchs do not receive as much benefits from machines as they would be to other factions. They already possess a self managing army and workforce full of disposable and replenishable assets that were theirs to control? What need would they have for hordes of metal? Still, if a force can be turned toward’s a Monarch’s uses, why not co-opt them?








                                                        A part of me really wants to write about potential alien/alternate evolved sentient races found in the Beneath, mostly other aliens that settled her away from human eyes or... evolutionary wonders that were first "born" there and never knew the touch of sunlight, like alternative sentients. Also could be used to justify things better with HCP's tech access. Not all of it was Geodic. But eh... maybe not. Thoughts?
