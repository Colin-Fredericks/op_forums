                                            And... I deleted my post just as I was about to publish it. Bleh, redo.


                                            Okay, summary for changes I would like to make.
                                            Some of you are potentially disappointed, but I don't want to "nerf" the Principalities especially since my thoughts are that as they were, they were too weak and needed more in general in addition to what they already had.


                                            Aspects: Both Bows and Blades will have selectable Aspects to Swarms. The first ones will be damage or new attacks. Bows will be slower than Blades here by 1 point. All Principalities will have Aspects similar to these.
                                            Potential for a "Burrowing" aspect?


                                            For Monarchs:
                                            Damage scales directly with dots. This isn't changing.
                                            At every odd level, you get bonus Attribute dots corresponding to what you're taking. At 2nd level, this may be given to your human form by spending 1 Psi.


                                            Also at 2nd, Blades gets a burrow move that also scales with level. Meanwhile, Bows gets the ability to do more range via categories like "Short", "Medium", or "Long". Bows cannot be used in close/melee range at all.


                                            At 3rd, both get the ability to inflict a basic tilt by spending 1 Psi before an attack.

                                            4th level. Blades ignores Armor or Durability with attacks. Bows does not allow targets their defense unless they can dodge firearms.


                                            At 5th level, Bows can go up to Extreme range?



                                            Other things like specific tilts or conditions. will be covered by Cross Principalities.




                                            My reasoning for keeping the damage scaling with dots is because it's consistent with a bunch of other powers that do the same thing, which are often even better than the Principalities because they can be applied to preexisting weapons for overall higher net damage: Vigor applies its dots to all melee and bow attacks, Werewolf Slaughterer adds Purity to anything melee, which includes Werewolf claws for a single a attack.
