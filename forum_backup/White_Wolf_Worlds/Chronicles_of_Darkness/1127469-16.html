                        Okay, I did a little bit of a facelift on Project AMMUT.
                        9999999999999

                        With the close of World War II, the nations of the world realized that to prevent such a war from ever occurring again they needed a mediating body. The United Nations was formed out of these pains, and their purpose was initially simple; provide a forum for nations to discuss their issues, and act as an impartial body to prevent another world war. Sadly, over time the United Nations has become a complicated body, seen by the USSR as a tool of the bourgeois West, and many Americans as an initial step to a “one-world government” that would consume the United States.
                        The United Nations realizes that it is a laughing stock at this point, but the nations of the world won’t pull out of it, and still fund its programs in the impoverished nations of the world and devote forces to be deployed on peacekeeping operations. Some of these troops are considered invaluable to the actions of the United Nations, and requests are made to extend their service with the U.N. for an extended period. Simultaneously, many of the UN’s missions take them to Africa and the Middle East, supported by teams of doctors, engineers, and dozens of other specialists.

                        This is the cover for Project THOTH.

                        History

                        Project THOTH officially is a project devoted to the preservation of archaeological sites threatened by conflict in regions under the authority of UN Peacekeepers. Following up on the Nazi efforts to search across the world to try and figure out why and what they were searching for, these UN efforts were quickly attacked by multiple strange organizations and cults. Unable to find a rational source connected to these attacks, the UN organized several Peacekeeping companies and had them accompany the academics to see if the additional protection would keep the cultists away. It didn’t, but the additional protection aided the archaeologists and historians in feeling safe enough to continue their work. What they uncovered was astounding. In multiple sites across the regions they were investigating there was evidence of a single unified group, stretching far into history, centered around the worship of the scorpion as a kind of holy animal. At the same time the commanders of the Peacekeepers were starting to realize that the attacks on their camps were too organized to be the work of local rebels and the occasional mercenary band.

                        Conferring with the scientists, the commanders and archaeologists worked through all possibilities only to arrive at one incredible and disturbing conclusion: The cults were still operating. It wasn’t odd, but their outright violence kept casualties mounting. Shaking off the hits, the Peacekeeper commanders formed a plan. On the next attacks on UN forces in Egypt, commanders pursued the cult back to their hideout. To a man they all committed suicide, trying to seal off their temple from the Peacekeepers. Blasting the doors open, they were faced with an incredible sight of hundreds of ancient relics and strange pictograms. Sending for the closest archaeologist teams, the Peacekeepers and academics were astounded by the sights before them. Not only was this scorpion cult still present, it was possibly the best source of knowledge about the ancient world available.

                        That was when the tomb was found.

                        Several of the Peacekeepers ate their guns then and there, and two of the archaeologists went mad ranting about something called “Irem”. As the officers ordered a retreat to figure out just what they’d stumbled into, the archaeologists made a quick catalogue and took several relics with them.

                        That night, as the Peacekeepers and academics tried to figure out what the hell had just happened, pickets outside the camp reported coming under attack by a lone figure. In the long and bloody battle that followed, the Peacekeepers fought tooth and nail despite the terror of the situation. They ran down the thing with their jeeps, poured on rifle fire point blank, soaked it in gasoline and lit it afire. Nothing seemed capable of stopping the damn thing, until one of the archaeologists realized that it wasn’t there for them. Grabbing the commander, the doctor told him his men needed to stand down, and let the creature march through to the relics. The commander of the Peacekeepers must have realized that this was an insane situation, and so issued the insane order.

                        As the monster returned to the tomb it had come from, the Peacekeepers and archaeologists hesitantly confronted Secretary-General Dag Hammarskjöld on what they’d encountered. Fearing that this new threat could destabilize an already tenuous balance of power between the Soviet Bloc and NATO, Hammarskjöld ordered a task force be created to investigate and, if necessary, eliminate these creatures.

                        That was the birth of Project: THOTH.

                        A Thousand Centuries Ago

                        From the start THOTH has faced an uphill struggle to figure out just what they face. A civilization as ancient as the “Irem” would have few if any remaining artifacts or even oral histories, and the cults would reasonably be expected to react violently to any attempted investigation. So THOTH embedded themselves with several UN operations, travelling the world looking for evidence of these creatures through mankind’s history.

                        Overall, their missions were confused messes. It wasn’t that the Peacekeepers were unprofessional or ineffective. It wasn’t that the archaeologists were cranks or fools. It was that their enemies were too embedded in the regions they operated in, using ancient arcane powers in their efforts to keep their secrets safe. It wasn’t until the placement of UN forces in the Congo that the project found its first true break. Utilizing Irish Peacekeeper forces during the infamous Siege for Jadotville, THOTH forces were able to break the back of a dangerous cult working under the guise of fighting as mercenaries for Katanga. Breaking into their compound, there were no more half-measures. Overwhelming firepower eventually destroyed the creature even as it tried to push through the hail of gunfire, even though there were casualties. Capturing several cultists alive, they learned just that little bit more about their enemies, and about a wider world behind the shadows.

                        Despite this intelligence coup, THOTH operatives realized that these cults and the mummies leading them were the greatest threat to world stability. Existing for centuries and hidden behind legitimate organizations and fronts, the United Nations may well have been the only group capable of the global reach to truly stop them. World Health Organization doctors carried reports from across the Third World of groups of civilians acting in strange manners, refusing to see WHO specialists in their communities and even driving them out. Reports from the International Labor Organization stated that several nations had cities filled with outright hostility to any kind of labor reform or action, attempting to stone ILO observers. Secretary-General Hammarskjöld had promised action on these matters, until the cults had their revenge.

                        With U Thant now leading the UN, Project THOTH feared that they would be disbanded and their mission left unfinished. Taking a desperate shot in the dark, the leadership of THOTH presented everything they’d compiled to Thant. To their welcome shock, Thant ordered them to continue their operations.

                        THOTH is now only a word spoken by trusted UN leaders in the halls of UN offices around the world. The best of the member-nations of the UN are selected from their duties should they be seen as valuable to the continued operations of THOTH. Through the years THOTH operations have been carried by Peacekeeping operations from Central Africa to the Caribbean, from the humid jungles of Cambodia to the scorched sands of the Middle East. They’re currently present in Central Africa, the Middle East, and scattered between the Balkans, Kashmir, and Haiti.

                        They have met the enemy, and he will not die easily.

                        The Nuclear Age

                        Coming into existence during one of mankind’s most tense periods, THOTH operations were balanced on a knife’s edge. With NATO and the USSR locked in a nuclear tango and the rest of the world struggling under the weight of increasingly forceful calls for independence, democracy, and dictatorship, any THOTH operations had the potential to create such massive international problems that the utmost secrecy was required. At first things went well, or at least went well enough to avoid serious scrutiny.

                        That was when THOTH was contacted by an American organization. They had learned of THOTH during operations in the Dominican Republic, and stepped in to ask them for “mediation”. As it turned out, there were many more groups like THOTH in the world, and the American government sponsored one of them. The problem was that the Soviets did too, and so THOTH had to bring in several diplomats to negotiate with the two groups to keep territories marked and separated. It wasn’t perfect, but THOTH kept a violent secret conflict from spilling onto the streets, and into civilian homes.

                        This was the first time THOTH was called on to serve as peacemakers between what they soon learned were the conspiracies, the forces of power that shaped governments and ruled the shadows. They found out that creatures of all kinds swarmed the night, from honest-to-God vampires to werewolves to witches. Fairies hid behind masks as undead beings wandered the planet. Nightmares made flesh and dozens of even stranger beings.

                        This ignored the dozens of regional conflicts that THOTH leadership had to factor in to their planning when they sought out the cults of the mummies. Complicating the situation was that there were dozens, even hundreds of cults operating at varying levels. Some did follow the mummies of Irem, but others followed demons. Some were devoted to a cause taken to insanity by their leaders. Still more assembled around all kinds of creatures, so numerous that THOTH had to set their analysts and “specialists” in the occult on the task of compiling the information for months. Trading what favors they had and placing what few agents they trusted to infiltrate the conspiracies, they managed to compile a roughly-working series of dossiers on the threats they faced, combined with the increasing tensions between the Soviets and NATO over Southeastern Asia, forced the project to go to ground for some time and focus on keeping some semblance of peace in the regions under Peacekeeper control.

                        Re-organizing, THOTH sent feelers into the national conspiracies and issued an offer. While recognizing their international reach, THOTH officials forced the point that only they had been compiling information on a threat that stretched far back into history, and that none of these groups had the ability to move between national borders like members of the United Nations. They could have the petty vampire power games, the witches grabbing at arcane power, the werewolves roaming the wilderness. THOTH had the power to seek out and fight the Iremite cults and other massive organizations threatening the world. Proverbially slapped in the face but realizing that they all had their own problems to deal with, the representatives agreed. The only ones that didn’t were either so old that THOTH had no real information on them, or more importantly, the Catholic Inquisition. Where the other groups had been operating long before mankind had even finished creating city-states, the Inquisition had a support network stretching from every Catholic Church from Scotland to Swaziland. Despite this, most of the conspiracies agreed to the terms, and THOTH was cleared to finally start operating worldwide.

                        The hunt was on.

                        The New Millenium

                        Over the years Project THOTH has slowly managed to compile one of the most impressive databases of the world’s paranormal beings. Though far from “complete”, THOTH continues to pursue their mission of compiling and, if necessary, eliminating the Iremite threat to the present.

                        As time has gone on, THOTH has come to slowly piece together where the actual dangers are. Negotiations are never entirely off the table, not since an operation under the aegis of United Nations Protection Force in the former Yugoslavia proved that sometimes witches can be negotiated with to aid in a task. In the years since Project THOTH made this revelation, they have kept the monsters at arm’s length while still trying to figure out who is who in their operations areas.

                        After the attacks on 9/11, THOTH went into overdrive. All the information pointed to a completely mundane (and terrifying) terrorist attack, but the mummies in the third world nations where THOTH primarily operated could seize on the coming chaos and launch dangerous moves on regional powers and weak governments. THOTH operations were placed to observe and, if necessary, engage. Unfortunately the fast-pace of the War on Terror has stretched THOTH resources thin. Being called on as observers around the world without the Peacekeepers being brought to aid in protecting those put in danger by conflict, THOTH members have been forced to face the reality that their best hope to remain viable in the Vigil is to continue to gather up intelligence.

                        Re-evaluating their situation again, THOTH has become the Vigil’s premier intelligence broker. The reach of the UN is vast, far greater than many would give it credit for. Despite being unable to act against smaller monsters, THOTH holds itself as the bulwark against threats that could potentially end the world as we know it. When a mummy has gone so far that they’re attempting to resurrect some long-dormant creation of Irem to seize territory in the Middle East, THOTH has the word out to embedded TFV operatives in the US military to call in the airstrikes. If a demonic infestation reaches a critical mass in the Caucus Mountains, Russia’s Section Nine is alerted and brought in to handle the problem. It’s not a secret that none of the conspiracies like weaker organization essentially telling them where the threats are, but can’t deny that after so many years focusing on the most dangerous of threats and managing to get the word to the people with the power to keep the disastrous from occurring that they’ve become an invaluable piece of the Vigil.

                        (A UN diplomat wearing a blue Kevlar vest and helmet over a sharp suit carries a series of folders marked with Iremite symbols. He is looking off into the distance, standing proudly despite being covered in dirt and grime)

                        “I have the Americans waiting for our broadcast, the local vampires have been convinced to stay away, and the Chinese will provide for patrolling the region after the cult falls. You have less than three days to stop them.”

                        The Enemy

                        THOTH doesn’t give a damn about local haunted houses or cursed burial grounds. A vampire pack roving the streets isn’t something they fight against unless their people are under attack. Witches fighting over a city isn’t even important enough to get them to move into an area. THOTH is concerned with the biggest threats to mankind anyone can imagine. Ancient Hebrew chambers sealed for millennia being broken open. Great monsters rising out of lost islands in the Pacific Ocean. Awesome monsters descending from the Arctic wastes to destroy European cities. THOTH watches any and all lines of communication observing for such threats.

                        The project considers the remains of Irem the greatest threats to world security. Unlike the majority of monsters, Iremite mummies seem to labor under the constant idea that their long-dead empire should be returned to the modern world, and the worst part is that they have the powers to do it. Yet even when they hear a mummy making points that the modern age is little different from their ancient eras, the forces of THOTH counter by undercutting their efforts. Instead of relying on Iremite rituals to save their families from disease, THOTH will direct WHO doctors and teams to distribute vaccines and medicines. Rather than killing to ensure the crops and livestock survive one more season, the Food and Agriculture Organization helps the local population to develop better sustainable agriculture that can feed far more than the Iremite sacrifices ever could. When corrupt officials try to protect their piggy bank, auditors from the World Bank quickly arrive to double-check how, exactly, the loans they’ve authorized are being used. Irem might have magic, but THOTH has a far more powerful and dangerous tool; bureaucracy.

                        Demons also have become an object of interest to THOTH, as demonic activity has been found in several faiths to be a portent to a dangerous shift in humankind. Demonic cults have existed since the first city-states, and the ability of demons to possess and spread without being noticed at first terrifies the project leadership. Unable to effectively fight back against the demons due to lacking knowledge on exorcisms, large-scale demonic incursions are handed to groups like the Catholic Church with THOTH forces acting as support. It’s one thing to exorcise a demon and save a soul, but the exorcist can’t do much if they’re taking active fire from a knock-off AK.

                        Aliens give THOTH no end of trouble, primarily because no one knows if the aliens are peaceful and just working off a truly alien logic, or if Earth is about to get rolled over like they’re the Aztecs. The diplomats rule this realm, having handled several incidents involving alien visitors being warned that even if they did manage to rule Earth, they’d have to deal with everything that came with it, including natural disasters, the paranormal, and normal humans resisting their rule by any means necessary. So far it’s worked, but THOTH knows that they need to make a more secure means to make sure that any aliens with the ability to destroy the world don’t.

                        All other duties are devoted to stopping the end of the world, which comes up more often than the normal people, and even many hunters, like to think about. They stopped the cabal of mad geniuses that would have unleashed the Y2K bug. THOTH operatives have successfully kept the angels of God from launching Armageddon, and stopped massive cryptids from rampaging across the globe with a combination of “nuclear testing” and “military exercises”. Ancient gods slumbering in lost cities are buried even deeper, fought until they once more fade into their deathless dreams.

                        Secretly, THOTH considers the other conspiracies the enemy as well, at least in the future. Their actions have prevent catastrophe yes, but they’ve also led to incalculable pain and suffering for men and women worldwide. Children taken in the night, familes ripped asunder, experimentation of occult weapons leading to catastrophe. THOTH feels that one day, these conspiracies will be taken to task over their abuses, and THOTH’s information has been slowly building to a critical mass. Blacksites, occult caches, names and locations. THOTH leaders even feel they know than members of these conspiracies do about their own organizations. THOTH has pictures of the same priests in a small group circling Vatican City from 1964 to the present. Members of the Greek mystery cult hoarding freshly-severed heads in multiple vaults and bunkers. America’s men in black meeting with the ghouls of powerful vampires in the nation’s cities. With this information, THOTH may one day very well be able to collapse these conspiracies from under themselves, destroying the network of support that keeps them all afloat. Darker rumors whisper that once THOTH accomplishes this, they will take the place of them all and become the world’s sole conspiracy.
                        Of course, it’s only a theory involving conspiracies.

                        Hunters

                        You were assigned by your home government to assist UN operations in Darfur, doing your best to protect the refugees there from being slaughtered by madmen intent on “cleansing” their lands. It happened one night while you were on perimeter, some kind of monster mishmash, but thank God you were on the M2 that night. After pumping the creature full of so much lead you were wondering if you caused another American ammunition shortage, you were taken to a stern looking diplomat. You feared that you killed your military career, until the man slid several pictures of similar creatures across his desk.

                        You were a part of the WHO, tracking the spread of what could only be described as an intelligent disease. You tried to push those ideas out of your mind, but the contagion kept moving through Thailand’s sex workers at a rate of nearly one a night. Tracking the outbreak back from the first patient, you traced the most probably source, Patient Zero. After dousing the creature you found with heating oil and scrubbing yourself for about a day, you were met at your door by a pair of men in black holding UN credentials. “Pandemic” was the only word you needed to hear.

                        You’d never held a gun in your life, but you were deadly when it came to managing funds and rooting out fraud. That’s why you took the posting in Columbia. Someone was siphoning away money meant to fund rural farming programs, and in doing so try to cut FARC out at the knees. With the local authorities in the pockets of the cartels and FARC guarding the cocoa fields, it was a hard enough fight before the men with the “potions” got involved. When you managed to get back to the safety of you home embassy and file your report, you were contacted a day later asking for the location of the fields and a pay raise to find more money problems like this.

                        Politics comes naturally, which is why you spend fifteen years glad handing delegates and ambassadors to continue funding UN programs around the globe. You’ve kept food distribution networks stable, and helped build dozens of hospitals and clinics without ever having had your name mentioned. You’re also the best man for bringing the conspiracies to heel. Catholic abuses in the Congo, strange rituals in English abbeys, corporate paper trails with bizarre endings. No one dares to kill you, because no one knows when they’ll need to call on you for information.

                        Stereotypes

                        Ascending Ones: The UN is committed to stamping out the illegal drug trade wherever we can find it. Whether that involves addiction programs, legalization studies, or the formation of drug task forces, we will combat this plague and those who facilitate it.

                        Aegis Kai Doru: Whatever this group is, they’re too dangerous to ignore. These objects, their ‘relics’? They do far too much damage to simply let pass by as a harmless group. They know a lot about the same sites we seek though, so perhaps we can work with them on what we’re looking for.

                        Cheiron Group: The Nix Group, Hestia Pharmaceuticals, it’s all the same tune. We’ve given up on trying to get information to INTERPOL, but we have found several regions that their company has preserved. Maybe a UNESCO investigation can get some light on the subject.

                        Task Force: VALKYRIE: You know, maybe we could be more effective if you would start actually lending forces to help us solve these problems.

                        Operations

                        THOTH is a relatively large compact, but has several division that are organized into “Operations” to streamline the duties of each to ensure a smooth and fast operational culture.

                        Operation: SEKHMET are the UN Peacekeepers dedicated to protecting THOTH operations, guiding the conspiracies to where the monsters are, and providing personnel and materiel support during the inevitable fighting.

                        Operation: RENENUTET are the aid workers, scientists, and specialists who try to rebuild the lives affected by the specific enemies THOTH operates against. They also bring to the fore decades of combined biological, technical, and medical knowledge used to stand against their enemies and find potential weaknesses.

                        Operation: SOBEK are the diplomats and political players who keep the funding and personnel flowing into the operation. Men and women who have lived their entire careers on the worst of political battlefields, they are the ones who have kept THOTH from becoming a tool of both the monsters and more powerful groups.

                        Status

                        THOTH is focused on gathering knowledge, hunting is best left to the people who can actually make a dent. Gathering information on potential threats to the world gives you a good reputation. The better the information, the more likely your superiors will recognize your skills and move you up in the organization.

                        0: You’re a small fish for now, but you’re in a school of small fish and you all have the brains to make things happen. Allies 0: Project: THOTH

                        000: The threats are bigger than you first realized, but your work keeps them from becoming reality. You’re practically an expert in what could destroy the planet that science hasn’t yet learned. Academics: End of the World

                        00000: You’ve spent years working at making sure the world hasn’t gone to hell, and even earned the begrudging respect of several powerful figures in the Vigil. Allies 00: Conspiracies
