    The Vigilant [Hunters as Superheroes]

    10-13-2022, 07:52 PM
    The idea of "superheroes in the CofD" was first introduced in the Mage Chronicler Guide, where it described how to run a Chronicle with the Awakened serving the role of pop culture comic book heroes. As hacking the setting of the CofD is one of my favorite hobbies, I have long considered how the CofD would look like in a superhero like shard- only that instead of using Hunters as the main cast instead of using the Awakened. There are numerous reasons for that- first, hunters are the characters which fight against the darkness in the core setting, making it thematic fitting for them to serve as heroes who fight to protect innocent lives from monstrous villains. Second, many hunters are related to some larger organization, which could fit agencies such as SHIELD and others into the setting. Third, by using Endowments, each conspiracy has its own set of specific "superpowers" which could be replicated and passed on to next bearers of the mantle or sidekicks (or even "dark mirror villains") while keeping thematic of the powers, which is a recurring theme in comic books (like the Bat Family for example). Fourth, some superheroes are just regular humans at the "peak of humanity" or assisted by technology/magical relics/divine blessings/etc, or even just pure skill. Finally, as many times hunters are expected to come all from the same organization and tier, there is a feeling of "crossover" when mixed org games take place, which is always a feature of comic book subplots.

    Taking it all into account, in this thread I am going to try and explore this idea, in terms of making up superheroes, villains, subplots, events and other things which could be of assistance for such a Chronicle, starting from the main cast. As hunters are known to be on the weaker edge of the spectrum of CofD protagonists, I am also plan on suggesting different mechanics to deal with it, even though I think that harvesting a lot of the material in Mirrors for stronger mortal characters could be of great use here. As I said, I am going to start first with the characters- specifically with the ones representing the core conspiracies, and from there we'll see how things go. Feel free to make your own suggestions!

    Anyway, here are the protagonists I had in mind-

    Guardian of the Labyrinth- often shorted to "Guardian", Alexander Kostis Dimas is a scion for a ancient dynasty tracking its origin all the way back to the legendary hero who slayed the Minotaur. Serving the secretive organization Labyrinth, Alexander took the ancestral vow upon the Oath Stone, which granted him access to the vast treasury his family holds- one which contains countless relics once used by the heroes of old age. Bearing the mantle of ancient Greek hero in modern times, Guardian seeks after the treasures of humanity for safekeeping, all while fighting the forces of evil which try to abuse them for their own uses. His greatest enemies is Xania the Witch and her occultist followers, Bullman who claims to be a kin to the Minotaur and the Tomb King that constantly sends his cultists to try and steal the treasures of his family.

    Ascended- a child of faith and seeker of enlightenment, Ascended has left her former name in the past, together with her former lives- for she was reborn. As part of the ceremonial rites of the Cult of the Phoenix, she drank the elixir of poison, died, and lived again, purified and burning with the power of the Phoenix within her. As the messenger of the Phoenix, Ascended seeks to bring light into the darkness, using strange potions concocted by the sacred alchemists of the Cult in order to defeat the enemies of faith and protect the world from harm. However, each such elixir takes its toll from her- as she is only as strong as her faith, and if it were to flatter the multitude of poisons would consume her. She commonly fights against the dread Desert Storm and the dangerous blood cultists who claim her hypocrite, the rogue alchemists of Amalgama who are lead b y the Dr. Gold and the tragic anti hero Dawnchaser.

    Centaur- suffering from a terminal illness, Mike Mason was desperate for nay treatment which may save his life- a path which lead him to take an offer made by the Cheiron Group, a leading pharmaceutical company which promised they have the cure for his condition. The treatment, however, required organ transplant, replacing his heart, eyes, and liver among others. Little did he know at the time that the replacement organs were, in fact, belonging to beasts of myth, and now, in order to keep his illness under control, he must hunt down other monsters and harvest their organs for himself (and other uses Cheiron has in mind). Thankfully, each of those organs grants him mystical power, allowing him to fight the dread monsters, all while questioning "am I still human?". A reclusive superhero (he doesn't refer to himself as such), his only true enemy is the Plaguebearer who infected him with his initial condition, even though Cheiron often calls for his services against his former in the role, the Harvester, as well as the self proclaimed "hero" Dark Knight.

    Councilman- the latest bearer of the Mark of the Scythe, Jonathan Smith was chosen by the ghost of the previous Councilman due to his duty for his dead mother and his commitment to avenge her murder so her spirit may find peace. Assisted by the spirits of the previous Marked, the Councilman is commonly visited by Death, who calls upon him to serve his duty towards the dead, guarding them from the Below and fighting both those who abuse them and those who cause them linger instead of going on to His domain. As parts of his gifts from the Mark, Jonathan can both see, commune and fight the wicked dead, yet he is never to reveal his true identity for anyone as other dark people who try to steal the Mark from him. Such villains include the Grave Tyrant who sees ghosts as nothing but his plaything and the Masked Reaper who steal ghosts in order to feed the eldritch gods it claims to serve.

    Lady Lucifuge- born to a family of devil worshippers claiming to descend from Lucifer himself, Clarissa Blake was just about to go through the ceremonial baptism rite of her family, yet she couldn't handle the act of murdering an innocent person and bathing in their blood. Caught after helping them to escape, she was about to be "punished" by her parents just when a mysterious savior came to her, killing them both and brought her to their place. That was how she met Aunt Lucy, the sister of her mother who also found the ways of her family horrifying and escaped years ago, and she came to train Clarissa to use her demonic powers to both fight evil and protect herself from her family. As such, she was granted the mantle of Lady Lucifuge, the One Who Ran From the Light, starting her carrier at hunting demons and their spawn. While she saved many lives through the years, her past still haunts her- her brother, Jacob the Heretic, have became the new head of her family, the demon Madalai keeps showing in her dreams in an attempt to tempt her to embrace her lineage, and no matter how much she wants to believe, some thing just don't add up in Aunt Lucy's tales, especially as sometimes she acts much older than she seems.

    Malleus- a devoted soul, Sister Caterina has her monastery raided by the powers of evil- and yet she stood against the demons who tried to defile the sacred places with the last of her power. Finding her on the verge of death, Pastor Baudolino found her and helped her recover, claiming it was the act of God that he found and saved her. Seeing her devotion to the Almighty, he brought her before the Pope, which blessed her in the name of the Lord made her into the Hammer of the Church against evil in all of its forms. After being trained by Baudolino and armed with the sacred blessings of the Church, she now hunts evil wherever it may be, showing her worth as a talented exorcist and warrior of God. She keeps seeking after the demons who raided her home, the dead followers of the Lance Bearer, in an attempt to recover the ancient relic which was stolen from the monastery, even though she would not skip any opportunity to fight evil and save innocent lives from the forces of Satan.

    Valkyrie- a secret agent in the service of Project: Einherjar, Victoria Bell is the greatest asset that the America has against paranormal threats. Armed with the finest technology which the scientists of the project can produce, she is a patriot fighting to keep her country safe and subdue any enemy which seeks to destabilize her homeland. That includes the agents of other countries, such as the Iron Comrade of ZMEI or the secretive Guest of the Chinese criminal militia, but most of the time refers to other forces of chaos, be them minor villains or terrible monsters. Her greatest enemy remains the Midgard Serpent, yet no matter how many times she is on the verge of defeating the ancient evil, the vampire manages to escape her again and again- mostly due to flawed information or contradicting commands from headquarters. Lately, she starts to suspect that something wrong is going with Project: Einherjar, and perhaps it is the time to use the same talents cultivated by the project to protect America from itself.

    Vanguard- a private investigator by day and superhero by night, James Samuel is known for his strange intuition and ability to almost hack into the minds of his targets. His secrets is that that's what he actually does- a product of a strange experiment done by an unknown agency which opened his mind to those of others, allowing him to collect their thoughts like how a spider catches flies in its web. James, however, uses his powers only for good, working hard to capture murderers and criminals and bring them to justice. As Vanguard, however, he takes a more bloody approach, dealing with those the law is blind to, so justice will be achieved. Sometimes, his services are hired by the FBI, especially in cases which require to capture "difficult" targets. The most problematic of those is his archnemesis, the Slasher, which always manages to slip through the cracks and hide from his mind piercing powers. Another target is much more personal- Dr. Wintergreen is the scientist who performed the experiment upon him, and which vanished without trace or explaining why he was chosen as a target. The FBI supports his chase after both, especially Agent Martha, and sometimes he wonders if she is too invested in those cases than she should be.

    Well's that's it for a start. Let me know what you think!

          So moving to the next stage- compacts.

          Conspiracies are relatively easy to convert to "dark superheroes" considering that, well, they have superpowers. Compacts are more complex entities in that respect as their members lack Endowments, and their premise is that they are just "everyday people with a social support network". However, such people do happen to end up as superheroes from time to time, such as the Punisher, Hawkeye or Nick Fury (even though some do have some technological support- but it is not the "core" of their abilities). Many sidekicks, however, do answer that criteria (like Robin), and as such it makes sense that in a superhero world compact representatives would take the more supportive roles in the setting. Still, sidekicks and supporting characters are important to every story, and could easily be the main heroes of their own arcs- as the famous "Alfred kicking Superman in the face" scene reminds us.

          Anyway, this is what I have in mind for the "core compacts as superhero characters"-

          Bacchae- the heir for the wealthy Ashwood family, Bianca Ashwood is the leader of the insidious Hellfire Club, where no pleasure is denied. Under her leadership, the Club has became a prominent organization in the criminal underworld, offering services for both superheroes and supervillains alike. Her only rule is that no fights are to be conducted within the Club's territory and that all conflicts are to be solved through the system of "games"- even though many of those games are just as deadly as battles. While lacking superpowers on her own, her wealth and influence has made her a powerful figure among the superhuman society, through the collection of favors she is owed and the blackmail material she holds over the movers and shakers of society by using the services of her Club. Her main nemesis is Marble Prestige, who leads a similar organization yet one far darker and villainous in nature, as well as the Slasher which is claimed to hold sinister connections to the Hellfire Club. As powerful as she may be, her lacking of powers is a sore point which constantly makes her fear for her life. It seems that there are some things that money can't buy- thankfully, money is not the only currency she has access too...

          Father Faith- Father Joshua was a humble priest in service of his congregation until the day one of his followers was possessed by a demon. Trying the best he could to exorcise the devil, he found his methods insufficient- until she came in. Using the powers of the Lord himself, she banished the demon from the heart of the innocent, restoring them to the grace of God. That's when he first met Malleus, who inspired him to join the battle against evil under his own mantle. While both work in the service of God, their interpretations and methods of actions are very different, for where Malleus seeks to destroy evil, Father Faith works to redeem the poor souls infected by the Devil. As such, many times the two come into conflict about whether or not a villain can be saved, as Father Faith always tends to show mercy to his enemies. Those include the followers of Xania the Witch and Ghostspeaker who escaped the judgement of God by cheating death- yet even he knows that some evil can not be redeemed. The demon Madalai have corrupted countless innocent souls, and there will never be rest until the ancient horror will be banished.

          Indebted- even among the superhero community, few know of Klaus Schneider's true identity. This secret is well kept due to family shame- for he is the descendent of the terrible supervillain Rmoahal, a agent of the Nazi forces who almost forced his tyrannical vision upon the world in the name of the Third Reich. While Rmoahal was killed at the end of WWII, his child escaped to the Switzerland under cover, carrying with them the secrets which made granted Rmoahal his superpowers. After discovering the truth behind his family, Klaus vowed to redeem his lineage from the shadow of his ancestor, working to fight evil at any cost. He often clashes with the followers of his grandfather's ideals, especially with those goons who claim the mantle and legacy for themselves with pride, as well as the vampire known as Midgard Serpent and the strange Angel Machine who seem to have been allies of the supervillain. Lately, however, he starts wondering if the means he has are not enough- especially as the sign seems to point that the ghost of Rmoahal lingers to this world. If that's the case, he may be forced to open the old casket and use the same powers which allowed his grandfather to become a villain- even though it may mean to stain himself with the sins of his ancestors.

          Veritas- known only as an online personality, Veritas is the alias used by Clara Miller when she manages the Secret Frequency. This underground social media is dedicated for tracking down both villains and superheroes, sharing information about they actions and making sure they will be held accountable for their misdeeds. Veritas' top priority is making sure that innocent lives will be protected, and reduce collateral damage as a result of the battle between superheroes and supervillains. While Clara was the one to create the alias, she is sure that Veritas could live long after her, and has made precautions in case of her early demise. Her top priority is to reveal the true identities of both heroes and villains to the public, to make sure they won't be able to hide from the consequences of their actions under the protection of their masks. That puts her in a constant conflict with Project: Einherjar and its supersoldier, Valkyrie, as well as with Ivory Mask which seeks to maintain her secret society of dancing corpses, Exarch which uses his magical abilities to erase the minds of witnesses and the demon Faceless who keeps replacing identities on a daily basis. While she does her best, there is limits to how far one could get without superpowers. If she could finally get her satellite working, the playing field would be much more even.

          Ninth Star- Valerie Chu is a police officer first and foremost, dedicated for solving the crimes plaguing her hometown. While she works hard to bring justice, her city grows corrupted by every passing day, as more heinous criminals try to take over the underworld and put innocent lives in risk, all while her division is both underfunded and growing corrupted by each passing day. In her despair, she turned to ask for the help of a private investigator, which is how she met Vanguard for the first time. While she still works in the police, she knows that sometimes she has to wear her suit and fight crime heads on. A Ninth Star, she managed to put the evil in her city under control- yet the police has started to hunt her down as vigilante and due to the influence of criminal organizations on high ranking officers. She mostly fights the influence of Marble Prestige, but there are other enemies to her cause, such as Swordfish that is abscessed with the flesh of their victims, the ever hungry Sandfeast and the foul werewolf known as Name Eaten.

          Professor Lightning- considered as a joke by his academic peers, Josh Spenser has devoted his time and research to one goal and one goal at all- the creation and categorization of superpowers. The head of the small department for superhuman studies, he works tirelessly to study, develop and recreate the different abilities that both superheroes and supervillains show in battle. The problem is that there aren't that many superhumans to begin with, and most of them live under a alias and generally don't like answering science related questions about their abilities, forcing Professor Lightning to go out and experience those powers by himself in an attempt to get some test results. He works closely with Valkyrie, who does appreciate his innovative and information he could provide about both her enemies and her own equipment, as well as being a common user of the Secret Frequency made by Veritas. His enemies are those who try to keep his scientific progress at bay, which includes many heroes and villains but mostly the Exarch and his sweet lies, Ivory Mask who destroyed his research numerous times and Angel Machine which keeps stopping the publication of his papers in an attempt to maintain secrecy.

          Sworn- Kai Denton grew on the old stories of her family, telling her about the time when they worked as the shamans and protectors of their tribes against supernatural evil. Yet, after the White Men came across the ocean, they destroyed their culture and old stories, all while bringing their own terrible horrors which abused the people of the land. While many may have let that dream to die out, Kai was not one of them, making it her life's mission to recover the secrets her family lost and fight off those who may try and prey upon her community. In order to do so, she seeks to recover the old tales of her people, and which of those she learns something new which may lead her to become the hero she always dreamed to be. Those who stand in her quest are the Guardian who keeps many of her family treasures in his Labyrinth, Ivory Mask who represents all woes which befall upon her family and Nightmare which feasts upon the fears and suffering of her community and that could only be defeated by her discovering the secret stories which give them, power.

          Common Man- Ben Jones was a simple man, working ten hours a day in an attempt to provide to his family. His life changes when one night, a supervillain destroyed a bus filled with innocents in it- his daughter among them. Since that day, he devoted his life to do everything he can in order to fight evil in all of its forms, regardless of the means he had available. From his point of view, he is nothing special- just a common person willing to do the right thing even at the cost of his life, and his devotion have kept him going where other superheroes have fallen, against all chances. While lacking superpowers, Common Man has fought against Ivory Mask, Marble Prestige and even the agents of the demon Madalai and the dead pharaoh Tomb King. As such, he is a great asset for any superhero in the battle against evil, his simple devotion inspiring many to take up whatever they have in hand and never surrender to evil, making sure than when he would finally fall to the clutches of his enemies there would be many other common people walking in his footsteps and fighting the good fight.

                          So let's write some characters based around the Supplementary Conspiracies, shall we?

                          Bloodwrath- lying in the street as he was bleeding out, Cain Hill had nothing in his mind except of the desire to exact vengeance against the evil who almost drained him to death. The next thing he knew, he woke up in his bed, his wounds treated and on a phone number written on his right hand. That was how he first came to know the Source- strange, unknown personality with which he communicated only through phone and mail. The Source told him that they saved his life as they recognized his desire for retribution, and there are there to help him in his crusade against the dark conspiracy that almost ended his life. As Bloodwrath, Cain fights against the secret societies of the dead, using the blood magic which was granted to him by Source to destroy the minions of Ivory Mask, the zealots of the Lance Bearer or the hired dupes of Midgard Serpent. In his quest to get rid of the undead, Bloodwrath shows no mercy, believing truly that anyone touched by vampires is already accursed, making him a anti hero that often clashes with other superheroes. Still, sometimes even he has to cooperate- some threats, such as the Tomb King's armies or the ever shifting Shadow are more than even he can take down alone.

                          Voracity- Layla is the chosen of her temple, a holy sanctuary for the believers of the oldest religion- the God Eaters. Being a sacred vessel, she was granted the flesh of the alien divinities as a gift, turning her into a hero meant to be the world's new god. In her quest, she faces eldritch horrors, and her power grows with every enemy she defeats as she consumes their power. Many superheroes, however, view Voracity's quest as a abomination, even though she is one of the few heroes able to fight such horrifying horrors, and is able to stand even for the terrible Tomb King or the alien demons summoned by Xania the Witch. As such, Voracity rarely involves herself with street level threats, focusing on the greater picture in her quest for godhood. A common nemesis for her in Sandfeast, as the vicious immortal claims to have been the true god who gave the sacred rites to the first God Eaters, yet even if it is true, she will eat him all the same, and she also comes into clashes with Behir the Judge who claims to represent the old gods she preys upon.

                          Cuckoo- known for his guile and crafty mind, Sato Akira is a shrewd businessman who reads his clients like an open book. He does not deal with everyday commodity, however- instead, through a bargain he had with a stranger, he gained the Gift of the Cuckoo, which allows him to steal the powers of superhumans and use them for his advantage. Since then, he used the moniker of the Cuckoo in order to get into the circles of superheroes and supervillains alike, buying, selling and stealing powers as he sees right, making him a literal power broker and a force to be reckon with in Tokyo's superhuman community. In general, Cuckoo despise violence, preferring to solve situations through his wits while manipulating his enemies into his service or steal their powers all together. His main adversaries are Ivory Mask, which he cheated a number of times, Faceless and his tendency to steal the lives of those Akira already made contract with, and Fairest Green which always finds loopholes even in the most carefully worded agreements. Still, his greatest concern is the stranger who once granted him the contract- for who knows when he would come to claim his own due?

                          Ex Machina- a self proclaimed angel of God, Adriana was a simply bounty hunter before one of her targets revealed itself to be a demonic beast. A mere mortal, she had no chance fighting against the horror, when a luminous being in the service of the Lord came to fight the demon and saved her life. While it managed to fend off the demon, the angel was gravely wounded, and in its last moment asked Adriana to take a oath to hunt down evil and serve God. As she agreed, it marked her with the signs of God's might, infusing her with divine power and a new purpose to hunt down those demons who try to escape God's grasp. Armed with divine marks, she is protected from the touch of the Devil and is able to force demons out of their false bodies. Her most elusive enemy is Faceless who keeps shifting identities to escape God's judgement, but she also had common clashes against the agents of Madalai and his demon spawn, but she also happens to come into conflict with Angel Machine as it is willing to sacrifice innocent lives in order to capture demons or serve the Lord- even though most of those conflicts are being solves rhetorically. Usually.

                          Dragonslayer- descendant to a secret lineage going all the way back to Richard the Lionheart, William Lewis was blessed by the Secret Patriarchs at the age of 16 with the Spirit of St George, granting him the power to command demons and slay dragons. By summoning the dread demons of Ars Goetia, Dragonslayer fights against the messengers of the Dragons- the eldritch patrons of witches and magic users of all kinds. In his quest to save innocents from evil magics, he clashes against Xania the Witch and Exarch who maneuvers both magic and political power to his advantage, while also trying to fight the influence of Madalai and its infernal cultists. While Dragonslayer always stands triumph due to the power of St George, he suspects sometimes that things are not as clear as they may seem, as George Harrison, the leader of the Secret Patriarchs, seems to hold secrets about the true nature of his abilities and lineage.

                          Shaman Queen- Julia Jean Baptiste is a blessed child. Born with the gift to see the unseen and speak with spirits, she was raised among gods and mortals as one, serving as a bridge between flesh and spirit. Working as a channel between worlds, she worked to guide both her community and the spirit world into harmony- until a pack of werewolves broke the peace, slaying spirits and mediums alike. Seeing the destruction they brought, she called upon the spirits and let them to ride her flesh, granting her the mantle of the Shaman Queen as she fought the shapeshifters, vowing to protect the natural order from her claws. Most of the time, she works in order to mediate between spirits and men, yet her mission often puts her against the foul Wolfhide that disrupts her precious efforts, the ancient god Megalaman who mutilates all that is sacred and make it in its image, and Gargoth of the Wounds, and ancient demon god that feeds upon the chaos of the shadow realms.

                          Dreamwalker- Dreamwalker have no memories of who they were. Their first childhood memories are being called Experiment 008, suffering everyday trauma due to invasive experiments meant to "unleash their psychic potential". While many other "test subjects" have died, Experiment 008 have not only survived, but have also succeeded in harnessing the psychic abilities- for the eternal woe of the scientists who performed the terrible experiments. Using their ability to enter the minds of others, Dreamwalker turned the worlst fears of their tormentors against them, freeing themselves and escaping the facility while it went down in flames. Now, Dreamwalker use their abilities to pierce minds and manipulate dreams to bring good to the world, fighting the constant fear that Nightmare spreads around itself and clashes against the false heroic acts of Dark Knight. Still, Dreamwalker has to constantly be on the move- the agents of the foormer facility seek to capture them and bring them back to the operation room, forcing them to fight for their lives as they uncover the strange conspiracies which surround Spinner, the one who rules over the webs of pain across the world.
