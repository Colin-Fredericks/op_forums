                                Originally posted by strider1276 View Post

                                That's a really good idea. So good in fact...look over there, at that shiny distracting thing! *Yoink!*

                                It just seemed the best way to handle things given the sheer cost of raising a totem's stats in 1e, and the fact that I really wanted to emphasise the role of the totem within the pack.

                                Bound-and-Chained-Lightning, later Unchained Lightning, was one of my favourite NPCs, and its death via suicide-run that killed a dozen Pure during a battle was a huge shock for the pack; firstly that the totem they had been with for a year had become so intertwined with them, and who they were, that it was capable of the kind of feeling and selflessness to commit such an act, and secondly that they had lost something that had become more than just a spiritual source of power and truly become a pack-mate and friend (albeit a really weird friend that liked to talk to the PCs via ringing their mobiles or hijacking the television screen).

                                I love totems.

                                - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer
