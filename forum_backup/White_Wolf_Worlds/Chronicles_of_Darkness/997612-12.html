                                            Let me make my point crystal clear:

                                            In the Genius the Transgression 1st Edition document there is a metric listed early on that details the in-world rate at which people regularly become Genii. It specifies the most likely conditions that allow for a Genii to gain their first Inspiration: Academia and other cerebral occupations. It also lists numbered percentages for both men and women in academia as a base line.

                                            If you add both of those numbers together, it doesn't equal 100%.

                                            Meaning.

                                            That the creator either accidentally forgot to make the statistic equal up to 100%.

                                            Or.

                                            That the creator intentionally made a metric that has hard in-world data to suggest that gender neutral individuals have a below 10% rate of becoming Genii even in the ideal circumstances.

                                            After spending months shadowing the progress of Genius the Transgression 2nd Edition I have come to the opinion that the creator intention's were to showcase that real world academia is so toxic to gender non-binary individuals that tragically many must keep their true selves a secret from the world or else face expulsion or worse, thus why the in-game metric is so low. Thus, the creator unintentionally gave their work a bigoted undertone. Most fans who I've observed either look at this gross sentiment that way, or ignore the statistics all together in favor of a more traditional CofD theme of "Anyone can become Splat X given the right circumstances."

                                            Ignoring it's existence though will do no service to anyone. Acknowledging it and making sure that a creator's intent isn't heinous is never a bad thing. That is critical examination.

                                            I choose to give this game's creator the benefit of the doubt because I believe in GtT 2E's themes and intent. But I acknowledge that this is my own personal opinion and that I am giving them the benefit of the doubt despite what is literally written in the 1st Edition document's opening chapter.
