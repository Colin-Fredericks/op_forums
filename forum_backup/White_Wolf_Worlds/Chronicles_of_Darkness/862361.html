    3D printed CofD

    04-08-2016, 10:19 PM
    I'm lucky enough to have a job where I get to play around with a 3D printer and design random models to help push the limits of what we can do with the technology. That means some of the stuff I create are the bizarre creatures of the Chronicles of Darkness. If you have any .stl, .obj, or other files on Thingiverse or similar sites, please share them here and I will index them in the first post. I'm hoping that as printers get more common, there will be more people who will be willing sharing their designs so everyone can use them in their games.

          One last model for today: a Greater HerasuSabexnt. I had been planning to create more Amkhata, as well as a lesser version of this to post today, but my internet connection is being too laggy right now.

          Also, if anyone else wants to take a shot at this, you don't need lots fo complicated software or much in the way of digital skills. I use tinkercad, which is a free 3D CAD software that lets you create objects for 3D printing. I mostly combine parts of models together that I find on thingiverse which are distributed under a Creative Commons license that allows for remixing. With a bit of practice, you'll be surprised what you can do!

                                  It has been quite a while, but I have some more models to post. First is a generalized chainsaw spirit, the Kar Kar Sum from the Book of Spirits. My design is more of a caricature than the Kar Kar Sum illustration, but I like the dissonance that creates. I'll keep my eye out for other base models that may more and will likely post more versions of this spirit.
                                  Turning our attention to The Pack, one of the featured totems in that book is Glabna, the Unyielding Stone. I am very happy with this miniature!

                                  Finally, we have one of the most bizarre and abstract of the Lodge Totems. There are many possible ways to interpret the description of Zakinsuzi, the Tyrant Over Fear. My design was inspired by the Avatar State used in the final episodes of Avatar: The Last Airbender. Instead of elemental rings, I used a ring of eyes and a ring of masks, as well as a snowflake and circular flame to represent Zakinzuzi's strange form in something that could easily be printed. There are certainly many other design alternative you could use, indeed this was the third iteration of my Zakinsuzi design.
