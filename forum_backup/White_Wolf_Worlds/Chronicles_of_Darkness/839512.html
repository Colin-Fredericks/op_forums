    Other shapeshifters and skinchangers in 2e?

    02-29-2016, 07:26 PM
    Is there any hinting that they're still part of the setting or will be included in any future supplements? How much of Skinchangers still works, and how much needs tweaking for the 2e rules?

    If there's no chance of anything official showing up, any interest in homebrewing some types of alternate shifters for 2e? The lack of them is literally my least favorite thing about the setting, which has plenty of interesting aspects that I feel are kind of wasted by limiting the players to just being wolves.

    Personally, I'm really hoping OP takes another shot at other shapeshifters in a future book. 2e is a perfect chance to make up for The Book That Must Not Be Named.
