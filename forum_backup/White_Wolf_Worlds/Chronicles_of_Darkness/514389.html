    Cross-covenant benefits: Merits, Oaths, Laws, Coils/Scales, Crúac, Theban Sorceries

    05-14-2015, 03:34 PM
    Hi !

    I'm not sure I understand the rules for Covenants properly, especially when you start to mix things up.

    So, a starting character needs to purchase Status ([Covenant]) 1 to start gaining benefits as par «Covenants offer certain advantages to their members. In order to purchase these advantages, your character must have at least one dot in the Covenant Status Merit» (p.80)

    Within the bounds of not having more than five dots total of Covenant Status, can you mix and match as you want? The rules talk about "affiliated group" and "group to which she belongs":

    Originally posted by VtR, p.113
    A character could theoretically gain Status within multiple covenants. Most of the covenants will allow members to dabble in other organizations, so long as they don’t share secrets. The Ordo Dracul, for example, will not stop a member from attending Sanctified mass. But the moment she shares her knowledge of the Coils, she’ll find her Requiem cut abruptly short. Her combined dots in one type of Status (City, Clan, or Covenant) cannot exceed five, and she may never have as many dots in her affiliated group as she does to the group to which she belongs.
    So... can I be a Ordo Dracul 3, Invictus 2 member? What about Ordo Dracul 2, Invictus 1, Circle of the Crone 1, Lancea et Sanctum 1 ? Is it possible, theoretically, to have one dot in each of the five covenants, or is it forbidden as per the aforementioned quote?

    Depending on the legality of the above, could someone thus learn Oaths, Laws, Coils/Scales, Crúac, and Theban Sorceries?

    As long as one doesn't "share secrets", it seems one could keep all merits (and Coils/Crúac/Theban Sorceries, I'd guess), given that «If the character changes or abandons their covenant, the Merit does not go away» (p.109).

    The whole point of my question is that I'm making a city Prince, and I want him to be both Invictus (primarily) and Ordo Dracul (secondarily). I also want my players to know their options.

      Ordo Dracul 3 and Invictus 2 is fine. Circle of the Crone and Lancea et Sanctum seems a bit weird. Technically, you can't get all five unless you are a Carthian with Right of Return p.116. You have to pick a main convenant which must have 2 before you can pick up others. That limits you to 3 covenants with 1 aside from the main one you chose. You could switch your main within reason, but you might lose status in other covenants to make it work out. It would be easier to get status with a covenant, learn what you want and leave if you want some of everything. The Prince needs to have at least one more dot in Invictus than Ordo Dracul, so 3:2 is your max.

                                  In theory, you could pull it off. Realistically, you'd become a hunted person as you piss off your covenants. No one would accept someone renowned for continually jumping ship. No Accolyte would teach deep mysteries of Cruac, nor the advanced Sanctified Theban mysteries, to someone so dedicated to the cause. A lot of Carthian Law effects rely on Carthian status. Invictus Oaths tend to require being sworn to others, giving obvious fealty to another, and following their orders.

                                  The thing with vampire is that, while mechanically you can do a lot of things, the consequences are almost always social. Which makes sense - Vampire is a primarily social game. You have to keep that in mind whenever you try to do something. Mostly, books won't say specifically "You can't have Invictus and Carthian status at once!" because in some games, the Estate and Movement are allies. But its something that you have to keep in mind in individual settings.
