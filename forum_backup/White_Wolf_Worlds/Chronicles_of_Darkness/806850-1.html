                            Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post
                            Acceleration? No, it's Time 3 spell.

                            If the mage knows she's fighting werewolves and has Spirit 2, she can Clash of Wills whenever someone uses a Gift on her, as well.

                            Now, if the mage in our increasingly-unlikely scenario is a master (of any Arcanum)... Well... That's like the aggravated-damage adept scenario only more so. Instead of doing levels of damage, a master can Unmake or Make her Arcanum in the subject's pattern in fatal ways - if it's a Forces mage you're after, we'll say Unmaking Forces to remove all electrical activity from the poor sob that pissed off someone who lists "mastery of the universes' fundamental energies" on her resume.

                            Unlike doing health boxes of damage, this one's actually withstood. Her Potency will be reduced, assuming she casts successfully to start with, by the subject's Stamina. If she has any successes left, the subject dies. Instantly. No matter how much health he has. Her Potency starts at 5, so she would instantly slay all but Olympic athletes, but we'll be generous and assume the person fighting her knows what she is so is going to spend willpower and raise his passive resistance to, say, 6 (assuming he had stamina 4). That's still only -2 dice, though, against a character who has a base dice pool of at least 10, and will probably be spending willpower herself on top of whatever the one yantra she's allowed in a single-turn spell uses. The Paradox will be about the same as the adept, but her foes' chance of survival is a lot slimmer. As with all passive resistances, Withstood spells do not give you more resistance for having a Supernatural Tolerance Trait. All that matters is your Stamina, give or take a +2 for burning Willpower.

                            As before, however, if the werewolf's in war form he's probably got a superhuman Stamina, and like the adept she'll be bleeding dice to account for his massive size. If so, and the master casts her death spell but didn't build a potency high enough... Nothing happens. And then the werewolf kills her.
