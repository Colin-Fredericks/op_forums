        So, I was considering waiting until I had them done, but that could take a while, so I'll just go ahead and post the two things I'm working on right now:
        Mechanical Bloodline Updates: I've realized it's sort of redundant providing fluff that's already mostly been covered by existing sources; most bloodlines just need you to update the discipline list (sometimes), the bloodline bane, and the bloodline gift. So, this is a project wherein I go through a crapton of bloodlines and update them all mechanically, without adding any extraneous fluff (past a sentence or two to describe the bloodline gift in some cases). So far I've done the 1e core book's bloodlines, and a few from Bloodlines The Hidden.
        Kindred Necromancy: This goes hand in hand with the above, but will also be a bit of its own thing. Essentially, instead of having ten different bloodline disciplines that do some sort of necromantic thing, there will be a new blood sorcery that anyone can use, that covers all sorts of necromancy, and then each bloodline will have easier access to it, will make better use of it, and may have other nifty effects in using it based on their bloodline. I'll be filling rituals in as I convert necromantic bloodlines.

        Feel free to offer suggestions for some of the mechanics that are pretty directly converted, if you have an idea for making something fit the more monstrous bent of 2nd edition. I've done what I can, but it's not always obvious how these devotions and other capabilities can be changed.

                                    Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post
                                    Hey,do you happen to have/know the guide for making Horrors from the Chronicles of Darkness core book? I'm kind of interested in knowing the mechanics behind creating one so I can do my take on the Children of the Seventh Generation (aka Lucifuge hunters) as a minor template.                                     Generally they have Potency, which is a power stat of its own, which decides the number of dots they get in attributes, skills, merits, and dread powers; dread powers are very much like what they had in hunter, just capabilities monsters can have, and are very mix and match to make the creature you need. Said dread powers would likely work well as forbidden lore, but otherwise there isn't any systems for experience costs or the like for them, since they're designed for NPCs.
