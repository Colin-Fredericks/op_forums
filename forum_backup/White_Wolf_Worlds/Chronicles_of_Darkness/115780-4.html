    I have a few things I need to get off of my chest.

    First off. Awesome idea. The general idea of how Gifts are supposed to work now is something I love. It's just a bit unclear. Can you buy into Gifts not given by Tribe or Totem? Or are you stuck with those that you start with? And will Lodges grant more Gifts? Because Lodges having esoteric Gifts was one of the fun things.

    Secondly. The Shadow Gifts seem a bit... Weak. Not bad, but just a bit underpowered. They feel like they are on the level with Demon Embeds, but they all cost Essence. And several of them cost multiple Essence (Essences?). And none of them come even close to the higher level Disciplines. They are about two dot Disciplines. And the gifts doesn't at ALL close the gap that stuff like Vigor and Resilience put between Uratha and Kindred. But I hope (oh gods how I hope) that this gets handled by Werewolf shape shifting. A werewolf shouldn't have to be afraid to face a young vampire in one-on-one. At least it should be equal.
    The high level gifts were one of the few things that made Uratha terrifying monsters. Things like Savage Might, Thunderbolt, Vanish and Primal Form were pure terror. Sure, there were many bad 5 dot powers (Most, in fact.), but the solution to that should be fixing the bad ones, not remove them all, should it?

    Thirdly. Are the Wolf Gifts ONLY the old Father Wolf and Mother Luna gifts? Or do they involve things like Strength and Stealth as well? Since they don't really feel magical in the same way, they are just enhancements of the Uratha in question after all.

    Fourthly (Is that a word?), The wording for the Unmake Tech Gift is kinda weird. I don't know about you, but I'd classify an axe as something simple without moving parts. Should it have a -2 or -4 penalty? More examples are needed!
