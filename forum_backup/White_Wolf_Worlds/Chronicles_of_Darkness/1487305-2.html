    Here’s some more.

    A Bloodbrute Fairest. The Ever-Victorious Hero loved having beautiful changelings fight to the death for his amusement. This violence became part of them, and they’re just as deadly as they are attractive.

    A Corpsegrinder Wizened. In the Year of the Plague, some changelings were faced with starvation. Some died, but others adapted, eating carrion. Slowly, they became rats, but were set ablaze every time the cycle reset. This brave little rat, burnt beyond recognition, escapes Ironside.

    A Fireheart Beast. The Hunter of the Perfect Rose loved falconry, but no ordinary bird would suffice. He captured this poor woman and allowed a phoenix Wisp to devour her. The Wisp then laid an egg, and a new phoenix, once human, emerged from the shell.

    A Gargantuan Grimm. He had to write a book in a single night, or else the Merciless Abbot would put him through horrifying ordeals. Again and again he failed, and each attempted book was destroyed in the monastery’s crematoria. Eventually, the author fell upon the secret of it, and wove himself into the book, creating a massive tome that told the man’s story from start to finish, leaving out. Impressed, the Merciless Abbot let him go?

    A Gravewight Elemental. A surgeon was taken by the Hunter of the Perfect Rose, then buried under the earth almost immediately. Slowly, he spread himself thin, the surgeon did, and was then used by the Hunter as a graveyard for past hunts. Eventually, the surgeon remembered who he was, vomited up all the Hedge ghosts slumbering within himself and made a run for it.

    A Larcenist Elemental. Swept up after wishes he could fantastical adventures, this had his hands and feet chopped off and replaced with mercury prosthetics. He would burgle the houses of the Rakshasa’s fellow Gentry, using his hands to become whatever lock he needed at that moment.

    A Lurkglider Wizened. The Queen of Hell, a True Fae obsessed with Western depictions of demons, sometimes kidnap mortals to turn them into replicas or such things. This poor boy became an imp.

    A Nightsinger Ogre. The Hag made a mortal man into a stone idol of some nameless pagan god, with a hole straight through his head where he mouth should be. The wind blew through him, and he howled. Over time, he learned to modulate his pitch. Even now, in Ironside, his stone body belies his angelic singing.

    An Oracle Elemental. The Ever-Seeing Eye, a gigantic Gentry that fills the entire sky of its Arcadian Realm, wishes to behold all things at once. This poor woman, once quite elderly, was transformed into a prop for divination—-a crystal ball. Eventually, her powers allowed her to remember her past and go Ironside.

    A Roteater Elemental. The Architect of Blurred Dreams needed fungi to break down the ample rot and carrion his experiments inevitably left behind, so, this stoner was given a new kind of high. Even after escape, the scent of carrion gets his mouth watering, and bright lights are painful to endure!

    A Roteater Fairest. Known as ‘black angels’, these Fairest are the beautiful, obsidian, white haired heralds of the Hag, and feast on the flesh of the fallen.

    A Skitterskulk Fairest. She’s the queen, and the hive loved her. But the Great Egg couldn’t hatch, and vented its fury on its adopted mother. Eventually, the queen deserted the hive, and found her way Ironside.

    A Steepscrambler Elemental. An infinitely tall chimney needed a fire to light it up. A poor boy from Ironside was turned into chimney fire and smoke, til he escaped through the opening at the top.

    A Windwing Fairest. The Butterfly Queen demands her paramours be able to flit about with her, so, the men she takes have Arcadian butterflies of enormous size implanted into their backs, following the spinal cord. Over time, symbionts and host become one.

        Here’s some more.

        An Antiquarian Ogre. Bloated but obese, every inch of this gargantuan changeling, even folds in the flesh hidden by blubber, is covered in newspaper print, from all over Ironside.

        A Brewer Elemental. The Hall of Alcoholic History is an Arcadian Realm where the party never stopped, where rest was impossible, where happiness was mandatory and horrible things happened just out of the revelers’ sight. This poor man was one of several living refreshments, in his case, cider. When he burst from his keg and made a run for it, the other revelers cheered him on...

        A Daitya Beast. Mommy was sometimes worried about intruders, so she made a guard dog from a man who hated dogs. Trained brutally to attack and devour anything that remotely threatened Mommy, this changeling made his escape as Mommy dealt with a Larcenist sent by a rival Gentry.

        A Di-Cang Beast. He was the Sacred Bull, taken from a mundane life to serve the Stag King. The Sacred Bull’s blood healed wounded forest creatures, and his tears raised the dead. But, on the most blisteringly hot day of Summer, the Sacred Bull was sacrificed to something old, nameless and evil. Even after escaping Ironside the Sacred Bull finds it difficult to turn away those in need.

        A Di-Cang Wizened. The Merciless Abbot had an apothecary to tend to monks and nuns who failed to show sufficient piety, but this apothecary was punished for even the least mistake in her brewing of salves, elixirs and draughts.

        A Drudge Beast. Ironside, he was a brutal taskmaster in his office, running his interns ragged. Then, the Man in the Black Suit came and took the office manager away. Put on a farm the Man in the Black Suit owned, he became a literal workhorse. Even after his escape, this changeling never shirks hard work.

        A Drudge Fairest. To the machine lord Wisps that ruled the robot city, only the very best technology would suffice. Even worker-drones had to be perfect, and sometimes, perfection meant beauty. This woman, a robot so perfectly human it appeared alive, was just such a worker-drone.

        A Fireheart Darkling. This fire burns, but dimly. An ember that refuses to go out, he escaped a Gentry named Heart of the Ocean, who used him as a lamp aboard the Other’s galleon. He’s a bitter, sour, but dependable member of the Day Court!

        A Gandharva Elemental. Our Father Who Art in the City, modeling himself after the greatest propagandists Ironside, took this radio DJ and turned him into a propaganda piece aired 24/7 on the City’s only radio channel. Every now and again, another propaganda piece would be broadcast. The message was simple: Obey your leader. Your leader is divine. Give yourself over to the leader. Somehow, this particular radio broadcast, made to appeal to anyone, found a signal leading to Earth. He took it.

        A Goblin Ogre. In the court of the Glacial Giant King, some giants were larger than others. This particular Ogre, was still towering over most changelings, was a rat scurrying underfoot to his betters. Put to work in cavernous duct systems too small for other giants, this Goblin Ogre learned a great many things, and used that knowledge to go back Ironside.

        A Pishacha Beast. Is it male? Is it female? It no longer remembers. Resembling a giant diseased rat, this changeling was used by Obese Abyss to clean up her dishes after they were eaten. It’s sanity shattered under the weight of the horrifying....material...it was forced to devour.

        A Sandharrowed Ogre.

        A Skogsra Wizened. A Game Warden of the Stag King, this poor woman could simply never do her job right. Her punishments were many and varied, and finally, unable to live like that any longer, she escaped to Earth.

        A Smith Fairest. Sometimes, the Brute desires a paramour, but blacksmithing is inextricably linked to his nature, and is inescapable even here. She was captured and brought to Arcadia to work the Brute’s forge.

        A Stonebones Fairest. Made of living marble, he was taken by the Ever-Victorious Hero and turned into a ‘pretty thing’ chained to the Gentry’s throne. He hasn’t spoken since his escape; the freehold worries for his Clarity...

        A Thusser Beast. After a successful hunt, the Hunter of the Perfect Rose desired a choir of songbirds sing for him. This poor lad was one such songbird.

            Here’s some more.

            An Ask-wee-da-eed Ogre. The Great Hero Yi shot down nine suns, but it was the Monkey King who caught one as it fell and kept it as a pet. As the sun died, it whispered to him the secret of making more. All it would take ways an honorable man, and an incantation to ignite him. Thus, down the millennia, the Monkey King has had nine suns in his sky, hidden from the rest of Heaven.

            A Cleareyes Elemental. Found on Deep Space Station #35, a man who was once an astronomer became an insanely advanced telescope, that the other captive changelings on the station might oooh and aaaah at the majesty of Apollo Alpha, a Gentry which shaped itself into an entire galaxy. Even after escaping, this changeling’s eyesight remains extremely clean.

            A Coldscale Grimm. The Hag turned this poor man into a snake, then skinned him. Too afraid to die, his soul clung to its skin. Upon this skin the Hag wrote spells and Contracts and used him as part of her strange grimoire. Eventually, he regained the will to move, and slithered to his escape.

            A Cyclops Fairest. The Eternal Princess must have her gallant knight, and this man fulfilled that role before he went Ironside. Wounded in a joust, he’s only one good eye left, but constant jousting made him incredibly strong.

            A Di-Cang Ogre.

            A Flamesiren Beast. Caught in eternal darkness streaked with neon glowing colors allowing some degree of sight, this young man resembles an obsidian wolf covered in mass amounts of fiery glowing neon red tattoos. Other creatures followed him, but the King of Colors could never stand competition. After a time, this changeling fled and his Arcadian adherents were massacred.

            An Illes Fairest. Prom was coming up! (Prom is always coming up!) The ‘students’ must wear their best, for the Prom Queen will tolerate nothing less. This girl, snatched on the night of her mundane prom, was turned into a hideous hag, then given enchanted clothing that made her more beautiful than any other ‘student’. At midnight, the Prom Queen would unmask this poor girl and everyone would make fun of her. But, hey! Prom’s coming up! Maybe next time will be better.

            A Moonborn Elemental. The idol of the Mother of the Moon screamed silently as atrocities disguised as shamanic rites took place before her frozen eyes each night. Eventually, the idol could bear no more, and made a mad daytime escape Ironside, while the Mother of the Moon slept beneath the earth.

            An Oni Beast. Mommy had very specific rules visitors to her estate must obey. This man became a snarling hound that was set loose on rules-breakers. Even now, having escaped Ironside, this changeling can’t abide those who lie or cheat.

            An Oracle Ogre. The Hag sometimes needed assistance in her witchery, and this poor woman, once a Wiccan author, was transformed into a lesser hag. She divines for her mistress, and fears whenever he prophecies go wrong...as they inevitably eventually will.

            A Telluric Beast. Caught in eternal darkness streaked with neon glowing colors allowing some degree of sight, this poor man, once a budding fashion icon, was made into a hunter. If he he ate a hundred other changelings, he’d be free. A sleek, cat-like Beast with rainbow neon glowing colors, he is now a Loyalist on Earth.

            A Treasured Beast. A red minotaur adorned with unremovable and copious gold ornaments, he was kept as a showpiece by the Ever-Victorious Hero. Even after escaping, this changeling is obsessed with accumulating as much wealth as he possibly can.

            A Treasured Darkling.

            A Tunnelgrub Ogre. Sometimes a giant displeased the Glacial Giant King, so he would exile these changelings into the tunnels beneath his palace, to fend for themselves. Of course, he never bothers to tell them of gigantic rat hobs that hunt by scent in the freezing dark...

            A Weisse Frau Darkling. She was made into a spider, but escaped her first Keeper, only to end up on the Arcadian farm of the Man in the Black Suit. She was horrified, as pigs were led to slaughter, so, she wove words into her webs, to dazzle and amaze. But she couldn’t save them all, and, one stormy night, was flushed down drainpipe Henge back Ironside.

                Here’s some more.

                An Airtouched Ogre. The Sea Storm Eternal raged forever, venting its fury even on itself. This man, a sea-faring explorer, was caught up in an unnatural wind and transformed into a hurricane, to rage along with his Keeper. Even after escaping, this changeling’s fury knows no end.

This woman was no different, and became an anglerfish to survive. She saw many wonders and horrors in the deeps, before she found a grotto that led back to Ironside shore. Now, strange and powerful fish-hobs able to assume human form court her her with fine gifts.

                A Larcenist Beast. She was a homeless youth, once, before she was taken away by the Primeval Forest. Denied food, denied water, denied shelter, she had to steal just to survive, until finally she became the forest’s greatest thief—-a raccoon. Even after escaping to Earth, she has a phobia of starvation.

                A Di-Cang Darkling. In the Arcadian nuclear wasteland just beyond the robot city, mutants, the war’s only survivors, only kept surviving because of the Survey Corps. Wearing an environment suit with no flesh showing anywhere, Survey Corps doctors became interchangeable.

                A Drudge Darkling. The Master of the Manor demanded his lowest house staff remain unseen—-even being spotted by another changeling was enough to encourage hideous punishments. BUT, they still must complete their tasks on time. This changeling figured out how to do both.

                A Lurkglider Beast. The Hunter of the Perfect Rose turned this man into a manticore, then set him loose. The manticore would be killed then resurrected to be hunted all over again. Eventually, the manticore made a successful bid for freedom!

                A Moonborn Wizened. He’d done it! He’d joined the Fraternal Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawning of a New Age For the Betterment of All Mankind (aka, FHOGDNAFBAM, for short). At last he’d learnt genuine occult truths! The Wizard demanded this man perform the lunar rites, but no matter how he tried, proper enunciation eluded him. The rituals were simply too complex. The Wizard would lock this changeling into the Box of Deletrious Agony and Uncountable Sigils of Pain For the Unworthy (aka, the BDAUSPFU, for short). But he could never learn the rites properly. Escaping Ironside, he is an obsessive student of Contracts and all things of the Wyrd.

                A Muse Ogre. A titan of living sound and congealed noise, Our Father Who Art in the City is propaganda, personified. He broadcasts through all media to the captive changelings in his city-Realm, delighting as their wills collapse, the urge to resist slowly dying inside them. This Ogre was transformed into a biomechanical antenna array, much akin to the creepypasta character Sirenhead.

                A Palewraith Wizened. If she was spotted, the ghostling was dragged with iron hooks into the cellar, where hot bronze knives awaited. So, to this avoid this punishment, she figured out how to cease to exist.

                A Polychromatic Beast. Caught in eternal darkness streaked with neon glowing colors allowing some degree of sight, this lonely young woman was transformed into an obsidian owl covered in glowing lines of neon purple, and hunted with cruel abandon. Even after escaping, her low Clarity score is quite indicative of her unquenchable need to hurt people...

                A Soldier Beast. Used as a literal warhorse in the never-ending Arcadian campaign of the Tireless General, this changeling fled when his unit routed, and galloped like mad all the way back to Earth. Even now, he isn’t afraid of a fight.

                A Water-Born Fairest. A mermaid. What else could she be?

                                                    Fairest Hunterheart -- Princess Twilight Sparkle of The Rainbow Brigade

                                                    The Crystal Kingdom was a utopia that had lasted for a thousand years, ruled by Her Benevolent Eminence, The Kaleidoscope Queen. And defenders of the realm, The Knights of The Rainbow Brigade, were her hand-picked guardians of Truth, Love, and Happiness. Together they rode forth on their Apocalypse Ponies to banish The False, reclaim Those Who Wandered, and who enforced The Decree of Joy, the edict stating that the citizens of The Crystal Kingdom should know nothing but contentment and love and bliss. Her Beloved Eminence scoured the world for the best, the prettiest, the most capable children to knight, sweeping them away from their miserable mortal lives to a realm of wonders and protect their happy, shiny, perfect world.
