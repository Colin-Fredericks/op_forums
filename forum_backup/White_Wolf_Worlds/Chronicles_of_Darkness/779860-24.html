        Hmm...The main thing that would make an Extended Action undesirable is that Extended Actions are meant to eventually "succeed". They open a lock or uncover a buried treasure; there is some sort of end goal. With Tracing, it's more of an interpretation. There isn't really an end goal other than "What is the answer?" and there isn't an exact answer to an interpretation. The end goal is also set as a number of successes that you're supposed to reach over a period of time. That number, as written in the CofD, goes from 5 to 20. I think it may be better to have Tracing function as an Instant Action so that Sirens can't just wait out a solution. If it becomes an Extended Action, it could just be used as a "Solve this Plot Point" ability, which is useful but kinda boring.

        But as a way to incorporate this, I would suggest the idea that a scene can be Traced several times before becoming "Overexposed" and untraceable. Each additional Trace on a scene adds a -2 penalty to the Dice Pool and a Failure causes the scene to become Overexposed. Given that the maximum possible feasible Dice Pool is 10 (if you put all your points in Int and Investigation), I think that should be a fair amount. Beginning Sirens who are more well-rounded would probably have a Dice Pool between 4-6, which means that a scene can be Traced maybe once or twice over before becoming Overexposed.

        As well, where should I put it in the documents? Should it be in Systems and Advantages?
        I think it should go in Systems and Advantages. It's something that is pretty much a core aspect to Sirens.

For example, 1e Changeling Contracts that caused some weather effect that was only made greater by going past the required successes. Verdant Spring is the best example. The second Clause's base effects is to create rain. 5 Suxx creates a light rain, but a Changeling can go further up to 25 suxx, which makes a deluge that causes flooding. One rule of 1e Spellcasting in Mage is that most of the spells can be done as Extended and roll past it even if it hits the threshold. Mage in particular is a good example of where the successes can be spent on different things, kind of like how Tracing is done.

            I'd say that a Siren is limited by their dicepools, the location they're Tracing at and how much Pneuma they're wanting to spend to make the rolls. They would need to spend their successes if they desired to get greater odds in seeing the past as it truly was or to get as much as they desire to know about. Granted, making it extended could easily solve problems, might I perhaps suggest that, if Tracing is done as an Extended action, a small precaution we can take is making each additional roll beyond the first requiring 1 Pneuma to do?

                Although a Siren cannot Trace into the future (within the Celestial Ocean or not), she may be able to have visions of the future while she is residing within the Celestial Ocean. I would say that the Storyteller should secretly roll a Siren's Lucidity in an Extended Roll. The downside to this process is that the Siren's presence within the Celestial Ocean becomes known to the patrolling Echidnal Sirens. It's a risk vs. reward system of sorts.

                                    Hmm...

                                    In Shin Megami Tensei 4, there's a sort of dichotomy between Law and Chaos, asking the question of "Should the world exist in Order where everything is regulated and everyone is equal or in Anarchy where everyone gets what they deserve and those who seek are rewarded". There is a middle ground of Neutral but there is also a separate middle alignment. There is a group called the White. They have lived a long time. A LONG time. As in, they have seen the cycle go on over and over, seeing the world under complete Order or complete Anarchy. They have seen people rise to change the world so that it could be a nice neutral. The cycle goes on. People live. People die. People go on to change the world. People go on to destroy it. It isn't just centuries. It isn't just millennia. It's epochs upon epochs watching this constant cycle going on and on causing people to suffer and beg for their lives. The White want to end this cycle. They want to end this cycle and make sure that it can never come again.

                                    They want to bring the world back into the Void, turning into nothingness so that no one must go through this cycle of pain ever again.

                                    Maybe it would be easier to paint the Abyssal Currents as not antagonists and rather champions who act contrary to what Ceto wished. The Current of Dark Passions seek to delve into the negative aspects of humanity and unleash the pain as a means of cathartic healing. The Current of Dark Knowledge want to seek out answers by abandoning their connections to morality. The Current of Mankind's Victory wants to ride out humanity's destructive path rather than direct it into something more acceptable. The Current of Primordial Darkness have all but turned on humanity in general to appease the world.

                                    With the Current of the Void, I feel that they could be written so that they are attempting to avert the belief that the Deluge is the end. They want it to come so that the world can be reset. They want to end all of life in order to begin again. Or perhaps they feel that it is the best for the world to begin again from scratch. Perhaps Sirens who follow the Current of the Void have gone too deep in their Trace, discovering secrets that they should not have known. Maybe the Sirens have lived too long in the world and have yet to see anything change. Or they have seen the path of the world within the Celestial Ocean and feel that there is no way to escape. For these Sirens, it's simply easier and more peaceful to end everything. Wipe out humanity. Make sure none of this will ever occur again.

                                    Their animal could be the coelacanth, a creature thought to have gone extinct but has managed to live for quite a bit of time. Or it could be a horseshoe crab, a creature that has not evolved into something more complicated for several million years.
