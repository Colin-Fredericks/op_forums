    Session 45

    Akenakhi knew well that he would need help if he ever hoped to kill Sekhet Bastet, and it seemed unlikely that an elderly doctor would be enough. After the events at the night club, he thought he knew of one, at least, among his meret who might help to put an end to her rampage. He met with Aquila in private, and asked her for her help. The Mesen-Nebu Guildmaster agreed that Bast was like a mad dog and that it would be safer, by far, if she were returned to henet as quickly as possible. Having her awake on a Sothic Turn was far too unpredictable, at least in future awakenings she would have a purpose to keep her attention away from causing indiscriminate harm.

    On the other side of the city, Sesu Ra visited Rekhenaten to deposit the Vestiges Akenakhi had discovered. He knew from observing Tobi fight that Rekhenaten had mastered the Dust Beneath Feet Utterance, and asked that Rekhenaten teach it to him. Rekhenaten agreed easily, and they began their studies.

    Bast remained in her tomb, meditating. She had organised a meeting with Yarikh to take place, along with Akenakhi. It was then that she found her mind moving back through the ages, to a strange vision of the world. She remembered walking through a thick fog, surrounded by a crowd of people whose features were blurred and impossible to make out. Words reached her mind. "Do you fear the silence?"

    And it was silent, and it was terrible. It roared in her head, a raw and enormous void where sound should be. Neither the people nor her own footsteps made any noise. Even the voice had appeared in her head without passing through her She knew she was in a city, but it was impossible to tell which one. The silence was more than just the lack of noise, it was a lack of substance that permeated every aspect of this vision.

    As she walked, bright lights appeared in the fog around her, rising higher and higher until they met as arches above her head. Objects fractured and changed the closer she came, exploding into bizarre fractal patterns and shapes. Living people came apart, their skin peeling back and allowing her to see the five pillars of their souls woven into their flesh.

    Bast came to realise that she could not even perceive her own body. She seemed to be a disembodied consciousness, floating through the shadows. And then a man emerged from the darkness. He was a tall man, lean, with hard muscles visible beneath his skin. He had long red hair, although the shifting patterns of light in this place made it seem from time to time as if his skin were scaled, his hair a mane, his eyes yellow and slit like a serpent's. He approached Bast and gripped her chin, despite her lack of the body, to tilt her head back and stare into her eyes.

    The voice resounded in her head again, and Bast knew what she wanted to know. She asked about the Shah, and what she needed to do to return them to this world. The red-haired man smiled sadly, and told her that his beasts died a long time past, even their remains turned to dust on the wind. They had been stolen from the world by time. But they could yet be stolen back. There would be a time in this Sothic Turn when the stars above Jakarta would converge in such a way that the weave of time could be undone.

    The vision ended, and Bast awoke invigorated. When the time came, she went to meet Yarikh at the inner city cafe he had nominated as a meeting spot. She showed up early, before Akenakhi, and found Yarikh was already there. Eager to talk to him without the other Su-Menent looming over her shoulder, she sat down and launched right in. Her discussions with Yama-Het had suggested that her true purpose was to raise one of the Shah from the dead, and she wanted to know if Yarikh knew anything more about them, or about the process by which she could raise one. Specifically, she was hoping he could tell her about the stars, Sutek and if he could recall anything about the remains of a dead Shah.

    Yarikh's responses were careful and measured, and he told her that there wasn't much he could reveal beyond myths and rumours. The stars had been the province of the Lost Guild, according to legend, but the Artists were lost to history and he had never seen any evidence they existed in all his long descents. Sutek was the Nameless Serpent, responsible for Azar's death in Iremite's mythology, and had helped complete the ladder to Heaven. As for the Shah, he only knew that they were rumoured to have existed in the first Sothic Turn. He didn't know of any remains in Jakarta.

    Akenakhi, accompanied by Aquila, showed up just as Yarikh was finishing, and Sekhet Bastet excused herself and left. The three remaining Arisen sat down, and Aquila began to chat amiably with Yarikh while Akenakhi struggled to work out how to get the information he wanted out of the other Mummy. He had just been informed that the cult had purchased the lease for the building where the textile market was. He felt reasonably sure that Yarikh's tomb was in there. He casually mentioned how impressed he'd been with the market during a lull in the conversation, but Yarikh's response was rather noncommittal. Akenakhi tried to provoke Yarikh in giving something away that would help his ambitions in undermining the other Su-Menent's influence in the area, but nothing much was forthcoming. They ended up parting ways without Akenakhi getting the information he was hoping for.

    Once they were alone again, Akenakhi briefed Aquila on how he had been progressing with his purpose. He explained that he was attempting to take control of Yarikh's territory and grow the influence of the cult there. Aquila didn't object to the plan, precisely, but she did suggest they try somewhere with less resistance first. They caught up to Sekhet Bastet and suggested they all head to the slum where Aquila had once worked as a volunteer for a health clinic. There had been rumours of fire spirits and other malevolent forces when she had last been there, and she was interested in investigating to see if she could enable Asa Corp to help the area against the frequent fires that caused so many fatalities in the area. On the mortal side, she began a long-term investment in establishing a group of volunteer firefighters while simultaneously trying to improve infrastructure, particularly electrical infrastructure, so that the spontaneous fires would be less frequent. On the mystical side of things, the three Arisen began to wander the streets looking for signs of the fire spirits they had heard of.

    Akenakhi chose to learn the Enlightened Senses affinity during this process, enabling him to see into Twilight and keep an eye out for the attackers. The group proceeded to the ruins left behind by the most recent fire. They found the large building was almost completely gutted, with several families still living in the ruins under makeshift shelters. Aquila had brought her Sadikh along, and it was Quil who found and alerted her to a strange patch of clean ground in the midst of an ash-filled room. This patch surrounded a small stone dog statue, something from a low quality ornamental garden. When Aquila touched it, flame leapt out and across her arm, burning her.

    Bast and Akenakhi caught up, and Akenakhi informed them that, to his new senses, there was clearly something inside the statue. Aquila needed no further prompting. She unleashed Harvest the Divine Flesh Tier 3, and struck it with the golden flail that Utterance provided. Flame struck back, struggling to break through the golden armour. When the statue finally shattered, corpus spiralled up to take the form of a girl of six or seven with a badly burned face. Aquila didn't hesitate. Before Akenakhi could protest, she seized the ghost and tore it apart.

    The two had a row over whether the ghost should have been reasoned with. Akenakhi pointed out that, had they waited for Sesu Ra, he could have used Chthonic Dominion to grant the Ghost Speech Numen. He was quite angry that Aquila hadn't even tried to avoid violence. Aquila replied that, had the ghost been the product of Words of Dead Hunger, which she thought likely, then it was a tortured soul and she had simply put it out of its misery. She said she and Quil would stay and stake out the place, in case someone came to retrieve the dog statue. If there was a sinister organisation behind this, it was possible that they had been using the ghost to burn down buildings, but they would need to come back for their tool now that the work was done. She sank into the earth using Dust Beneath Feet, while Quil set up a more mundane ambush.

    Akenakhi and Sekhet Bastet left, Akenakhi returning to his tomb, Bast heading out of the city as the sun set. She used the third tier of Secrets Ripped From Skies to ask the stars what her next step should be, but the stars were silent. Finally, she heard a discreet cough behind her. She turned to find a strange man standing behind her. His head was shaved bald, although patches of dark stubble were regrowing in a haphazard manner. He wore a fine silk shirt, white with golden chrysanthemums, that contrasted with his ragged jeans, poor quality sandals, and mud-stained toes. He wore a large satchel around his neck. The man introduced himself as the Prophet of Java, a servant of Fate. Bast asked him if he knew when the convergence of stars would be, the one spoken of in her vision that would allow her to step back in time to retrieve the body of one of the Shah. The Prophet sat down in the field and opened his satchel, pulling out a state-of-the-art laptop. He bade her sit next to him as he scanned through a variety of star maps, visions of the skies above Jakarta in the months yet to come. At last he stopped.

    "This I can do for you," he told her. "When the time comes, I will appear to you and we can undertake this journey. But there is something you must do for me, for the dictates of Fate itself. Retrieve the head of Apophis. Then this work can be done." And then the Prophet vanished.

    Sesu Ra, meanwhile, decided to explore the tunnel he remembered creating in one of his older memories, the one leading to the resting place of Thothekmet. Its entrance was close to the National Monument, a long-sealed concrete tunnel. Dusty light bulbs lined the walls, but there was no electricity to power them. He lit his way with Chthonic Dominion, and came to a secret chamber buried beneath the monument. It was circular, with channels and grooves carved into the floor. These led into pipes that disappeared into the earth on every side. Suspended above the ground was Thothekmet himself, or at least his remains. He was pierced through by long spears, fixed to keep his limbs spreadeagled. Buried in his forehead was the golden chisel Sesu Ra remembered from his vision. As he watched, a single gleaming drop of honey-like liquid ran down the chisel and dripped into the bowl-like depression in the ground beneath him. It was filled with the same liquid, a rarefied form of Sekhem, and a slow, viscous trickle of the liquid ran down each channel to disappear into the pipes beyond. This, Sesu Ra recognised, was how he had created the foundation for his geometric network. Thothekmet seemed to be unaware and frozen in a state of limbo, if he was even still active. Sesu Ra left him there, idly wondering if the World's Wound could take the Mummy's place. He believed it would, but the ramifications for the Arisen and Shuankhsen of Jakarta were probably not worth contemplating.

    Sekhet Bastet remembered that the creature calling itself the Coils of Apophis had made some cryptic comments about stars and light, and decided that the best place to go for advice regarding such things was the strange ghost in the Vault of Stars. She headed off to that abandoned slum, stepping between the pillars in the once-flooded hotel. As before, the world melted and ran like wax, and she found herself standing in a transparent sphere suspended in the void of space where the Earth should be. There was no sign of the ghost until Bast unleashed the first tier of Ancestry of Forgotten Stars. There she could see the ghost of a young woman, hard to discern against the backdrop of stars, whose body seemed to be made of the black vacuum of space filled with tiny pinpricks of light. The woman was curled up into a ball, hugging her knees, and did not respond at first to Bast's questions. When the name "Apophis" was mentioned, however, it caught her attention.

    "It is one of the light's children," she said. "I saw it come here, rippling through the void. It seeks to consume the light of the stars for its father, to feed until the Radiance overwhelms us all."

    At the same time, Akenakhi had decided he needed to assuage his conscience by making another attempt to apologise to the one he had sacrificed. He approached the Vault of Stars himself, not knowing of Bast's presence inside. He stepped across the threshold and froze. The two Arisen each demanded to know why the other was there, and neither of them were terribly forthcoming in their intentions. They were distracted, however, by the ghost speaking. It told them that if they wanted a glimpse of what was coming, they could step through the gates and see.

    This confused them at first. There were no features in the sphere in which they stood, let alone any gates. But Akenakhi, with the knowledge of Palace Knows Its Pharaoh in his mind, realised he could sense openings all around him. He unleashed the Utterance, leaving the destination up to Fate. Both of them felt the epic unleashing, and there was a moment of trepidation as they stood there. The portal was invisible physically, but they could each sense its outline. Akenakhi went first, stepping through. Bast hesitated on the threshold, but in the end her pride forced her across.

    They were somewhere very dark and very quiet. Their bodies moved strangely, not weightless, but certainly not as heavy as they were used to. They moved in the only direction that was distinct from any other, away from the portal and towards a soft greyish suggestion that light could be found on the other end. They could not see one another at first, and they found that any attempt to speak did not carry. It was about this point that they realised there was no air to breathe.

    Around the corner and they found themselves at the mouth of a cave. The vista beyond was not something they had ever seen, not in the six thousand years of their existence. It was a flat, dead world, pockmarked with craters. There were almost no shadows. It was hard to see. When they looked up they were blinded, and needed to seal the flesh to recover the use of their eyes. The sky itself seemed to be made of light. Only the very horizons seemed clear of it. Everywhere else they looked was an immense pillar, impossibly vast, like a star stretched out until it blotted out everything else.

    As they watched, the light seemed to... react. It pulsed and moved, and tendrils split away from its bulk and began to descend. These wisps of light or energy moved slowly, but deliberately. They began to converge on the cave where the Arisen were standing. Bast reacted first, summoning Secrets Ripped From Skies and hurling a meteor at the light. It was a powerful unleashing, but it was impossible for them to tell if it had any impact. At least, until the light expanded. A beam of energy tore through the planet they were standing on, and they were hurled violently as the ground came apart beneath their feet. Blasted, burned, and on the cusp of death, Akenakhi unleashed the second tier of Revelations of Smoke and Flame to fly back to the portal. Bast, in a similar situation, chose to use the second tier of Ancestry of Forgotten Stars. Akenakhi launched himself back into the cavern, dodging falling rocks and reaching the portal only moments ahead of a cave-in. Bast didn't even try to regain her footing. She stepped through broken angles to the nearest site of attuned geometry.

    Akenakhi landed back in the Vault of Stars. The flesh of his face was burned away, and it would be a long time before it healed. He waited for Bast to emerge, but she didn't. A small smile spread across his face as he contemplated the possibility that she was lost somewhere on the other side of the galaxy, trapped far away from Earth. But there was something more pressing. The ghost in here had attacked him the last time they met. If she did so now, he would die. He spread his arms wide and turned to her.

    "I am at your mercy," he said.

    Bast, meanwhile, opened her eyes to a strange sight indeed. Her attempt to leap straight back to Earth had gone awry. She was lying on a bridge arching across a deep chasm. Two suns burned in a violet sky above. All around her was a city, but not one built by human hands. It stretched as far as her eyes could see, impossible architecture bending and twisting in complete disregard to the laws of physics. Cyclopean monoliths towered around her. She could feel the power contained within them. This was Irem writ large, she thought in awe.

    She followed the path she was standing upon, and descended into the depths of these aliens ruins. The city was still, without any sign of life. Whatever had created it seemed to have abandoned it, long ago. She walked through shadowed halls until she came to a great temple plaza, or something that greatly resembled one. There was still the matter of returning home, and she had no idea how to achieve this.

    The answer came to her. "The Herald of Forgotten Stars". She wasn't sure where the thought had come from. It seemed nonsensical. It required help, other Arisen to create the Herald's body. Without a chorus it would be impossible to pronounce the being's true name to summon it, and even if she did, she was not sure how it could get her home. And yet the thought was insistent, and there was little else she could do. Within the shadows of that alien temple, she knelt and began to chant. Her voice echoed among the pillars, returning to her with distorted timbres and strange inflections. The sound swelled the longer she spoke, welling until it sounded like a thousand inhuman voices spoke with her in unison.

    And then the ritual was complete. Her body broke and twisted, and shadows welled from every side until the Herald rose up from among the peaks and spires of the strange city. Bast's consciousness was subsumed, but she felt the Herald step across the galaxy in a single fractured instance, tearing through time and space at unnatural angles. And then there was heat, and her memory dissipated.

    Akenakhi, still in the Vault of Stars, witnessed her approach. A shifting monster of inhuman proportions appeared in the skies over Jakarta, becoming a burning star as it descended through the atmosphere. It flashed across in the city in a flaming arc, and struck the ground some miles away. Every Arisen in the city felt its presence and the waves of Sybaris that cascaded down from it.

    Bast regained consciousness alone in the middle of a large crater. She was near death, and had to force herself to her feet and return to the city on foot. She had no memory of her last moments as the Herald, but her time in the strange city was burned into her mind. She wondered if even the time's toll on her memory would be enough to erase it entirely.

    Back in the Vault of Stars, Akenakhi asked the ghost if there was anything he could do for her. Her response was a long time in coming.

    "Stop the light. Stop it before it reaches us."

    A week passed as Akenakhi and Sekhet Bastet recovered from their injuries. Bast sacrificed a dot of Sekhem to speed up her healing, dropping to Sekhem 7, while the others all dropped to Sekhem 6. Aquila sent Tetasu Xex to dwell among Sem-Huf's cultists, materialising every now and then for momentary glimpses until their Willpower was gone. Towards the end of the week, she finally gave in to the urges of her purpose, and went to meet with Yama-Het to try and find out if there was a way the Sesha-Hebsu could be persuaded to give up the necklace. They largely rehashed the arguments from their last conversation, until Aquila asked Yama-Het's permission to deal with the organised crime problem in her area. If there was nothing for Rekhenaten to invest in, after all, there would be no point in continuing the conflict. Yama-Het gave her permission, and Aquila put her plan into action.

    She and Quil spent some time determining where the Preman in the area could be found. There was a local curry store that was quite popular with many of the higher ranking bosses, but Aquila didn't like the idea of striking somewhere so public. She also found that a building under construction in the area was frequently used by them for a variety of illicit activities. More specifically, it was the building Sesu Ra had invested in heavily when they had been hunting the Pelamat. She sent Tetasu Xex to haunt the place for a day, and then launched her attack close to midnight. There were only four men in the building. Alongside Quil, she emerged from the walls and drained the last of their Willpower. Allowing her darker self to take over, she used Rouse the Khaibit and then, methodically, slit the throats of each of the men. Quil watched, not having seen this part of his mistress before. When she stood up from her work she was smiling, the smile of a predator.

        Session 46

        I know a couple of my players do visit this thread, so this first bit of the write-up is spoilered for those not involved.

        As the days passed, Akenakhi felt the pressure on his mind growing. The strange vision of constellations and celestial movements he had received in the Vault of Stars weighed on him. He pursued studies of the night sky as his body recovered, noting how closely it mimicked the sight in his vision. At last, he knew where he needed to go.

        He wandered outside of the city one evening, following the path laid out by his vision. It led him to a rice paddy, an open field miles from Jakarta's outskirts. It was very quiet, and the stars shone bright in the clear sky.

        The moment arrived. He could sense nascent power in the air. Sekhem, like the presence of a relic, beat like a heartbeat throughout the surroundings. He could see no vessel, however, and had no idea how to proceed.

        "Your kind are blind," came a voice from behind him.

        Akenakhi turned around to find a man seated cross-legged on a nearby rock. He wore a white silk shirt decorated with golden flowers alongside ragged jeans.

        "That may be true," Akenakhi allowed. "What am I missing here?"

        The strange man gestured towards the stars. "There are wonders in this world that have been hidden from the servants of the Shan'iatu. Powers beyond that of text, amulet, uter, regia, and effigy. They are Fate's own wonders, hidden from those who have not possessed them. The Judges must not come by this power."

        Akenakhi swore that the Judges would not get any power from him, and the strange man showed him what he needed to do, gave him a word he needed to pronounce. And then the man was gone, vanished into thin air.

        Akenakhi did as he was told. For a moment, as the last of the word passed his lips, he saw a glowing ball of hieroglyphs suspended in the air above his head. It broke apart and flowed into him, joining to his name. The sensation was not like anything he had ever experienced, and as he turned his eyes towards the heavens he felt a veil lifting from his sight. Were there more stars there now than there had been before?

        He had claimed his first Seba.


        Meanwhile, Bast and Sesu Ra came to an agreement to help Bast's burgeoning cult claim control of their old territory in the Thousand Islands. Since Last Dynasty International had apparently retreated from Jakarta, they did not expect the opposition to be fierce. With Kudama Jav in tow, they travelled out to the resort where they had first encountered the smuggling groups. Shortly after arriving, however, Bast recognised faces among the people at the resort. They had been employees with LDI. The two Arisen withdrew, apparently unnoticed, and sent Kudama to scout out the area and speak with the resort's owner.

        Meanwhile, the Arisen took their boat around the island to find the smuggling dock they remembered being used nearby. They saw a much larger, sleeker motorboat heading in the same direction though, and stopped. The Arisen leapt into the ocean and proceeded along the ocean's bottom. What they found was a large operation, maybe twenty people in total, loading and offloading crates from the ship. They had their suspicions about the culprit even before Kudama, with a bit of surreptitious bribery, found out that a corporate sponsor had requested the discrete use of the land from the resort's owner. It looked like they had found an LDI base just outside of the city limits.

        The pair sat down to wait until nightfall, settling into the sand on the ocean floor. Towards the middle of the afternoon a second ship arrived at the dock, and the two Arisen sensed the presence of a nearby vessel. Sesu Ra made Bast wait long enough for the crew to disembark, and then they acted. Bast unleashed Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, while Sesu Ra became a titan in stone with a combination of Blessed Is The God-King and Dust Beneath Feet. He loomed over the docks and summoned a blade of light and darkness to his hands.

        There were five men in total, one of them handcuffed to a long case which presumably contained the relic. Bast went straight for this man before anyone else could react, plunging her knife through his chest. He crumpled to the ground, and she had cut open a second man before anyone could react. The remaining men went for their guns. Sesu Ra brought his blade down, but he was in no rush to endanger his newly rebuilt Memory. The men watched in astonishment as their guns were cut apart. One man, out of Sesu Ra's reach, was able to open fire. Bast felt her body burn as the incendiary rounds ripped through her, spared from truly mortal wounds only by being slightly out of phase with reality. The two unarmed men fled, and she quickly killed the one who had the temerity to attack her. She passed her Degeneration check, putting her faith in Her-Uru, and it was rewarded with both success and a Descent reset.

        Sesu Ra tore open the case. Inside they found an old, bronze gladius. Using the Model Lifeweb, Sesu Ra was able to conclusively identify it as Aquila's blade. He claimed it as a reward for helping Bast secure the area, which she willingly accepted. The two then retreated before reinforcements could converge on their position, giving themselves the breathing space needed to figure out their next move.

        They arrived back in Jakarta in time to receive an urgent message from Rom Mayehi, telling them that Asa Corp had been attacked. When they arrived they found that Reskan Lehn, a young cultist in the Lehn family, had been badly injured. He'd been standing guard outside Daryat's cell when he heard strange sounds coming from inside. When he went in to investigate, he'd found a tunnel running from the room and into the sewers. There were two women there helping Daryat escape. One of them he recognised as Yama-Het, the other he didn't know. She shot him in the leg and the three of them ran, the tunnel collapsing behind them.

        The meret weren't sure how to take this. Akenakhi argued that it didn't sound like Yama-Het's usual method, but Sesu Ra pointed out that it wasn't actually that far apart. No one had been killed, and she'd hardly been squeamish about violence in the past. The identity of the second woman was a puzzle. They retrieved a picture of her from security footage, but they couldn't match the glimpse they had of her face to anyone they knew. Bast idly floated the sensation that it might have been Aquila in disguise, having decided to whisk Daryat away and out of the reach of the rest of her meret. Although this idea wasn't given much serious consideration, it did catch the imagination of the others.

        Regardless, they needed to speak to Yama-Het about this. When they arrived, however, her cultists refused to allow them to see her. Bast grew quite angry at this, and loomed over the poor cultist on guard. In the end, he confessed that Yama-Het had returned to her tomb. It seemed that her descent had ended only hours before, and she now lay in the repose of henet. Sesu Ra shot down any suggestion that they wake her up purely to investigate, and the group returned to their respective tombs to meditate and think on events.

        Bast, at Sesu Ra's request, made a Kepher roll to try and help him track down a random Vestige somewhere in the city to help with the construction of his Geometric Network. She received a vision of a man named Idih, who looked like nothing more than a square brick of muscle, and the impression that the vessel she sought was somewhere in the fire-prone slum where Aquila did most of her charity work.

        Akenakhi, meanwhile, chose to follow up on Aulia. The damaged woman with a penchant for speaking in tongues was still being held by the cultists at Asa Corp, although a year's worth of help from psychologists had been unable to break through to her. When Akenakhi walked into her room, she greeted him happily in a babble of languages he didn't understand. When he asked after her health, she replied in a sentence that flowed from French to Iremite to Indonesian. "Pretty bright lights in your eyes."

        Akenakhi had a moment of inspiration then. Perhaps, with the help of one of his powers, he could free her mind and grant her a moment of clarity. He blessed her with the use of his Affinity.

        "The Missionaries," she said, quivering with fear. "The Missionaries of the Radiance, they're going to hurt us all."

        Akenakhi wasted no time after that. He called his meret and requested they help him in tracking down the "Missionaries of the Radiance", figuring that they must have something to do with the light they had been warned of. Bast and Sesu Ra had returned from their excursion into the Thousand Islands, and Bast remembered that Aulia had first been encountered in the area around her tomb. Akenakhi pursued legal channels, while Bast leveraged her cult's help in gathering word of mouth. Between them they found evidence of four others who had been afflicted in a similar way to Aulia, and all of them had something to do with a woman named Lia living on the edge of the slum.

        The Arisen decided to immediately confront this woman. They found Lia washing clothes in a tub in front of the hovel she had for a house. She was a petite old woman, but regarded them with confidence when they approached. They told her they knew she worshipped the Radiance, but Lia simply asked them mildly to leave. Akenakhi was able to persuade her to reveal a little of her allegiance, however. She confessed that yes, she did belong to the cult who worshipped the Radiance. She did so because its approach was inevitable, its hunger unquenchable, and that the Earth would be consumed in the end. She only wished to help feed it, sate it enough to delay the doom of this world long enough for her grandchildren to live and die. She did not say anything regarding the curse upon Aulia, and flat out refused to speak of any of the other members of her cult. The Arisen boasted that, between them, they had the strength to stop the Radiance, but Lia clearly didn't believe them. She gathered her washing and went inside, and Bast had to be restrained from going after her and beginning an interrogation. Instead, the Arisen decided to let Lia be, and see if they could come up with another way to get through to her.

            Also, Akenakhi's new thing:

            The Sickle of Eternity (Cosmic Seba, 3 dot)

            Power: Decay is the order of the universe, yet Fate dictates that some things must remain inviolable. The Deathless themselves are examples of this, an act of sorcery preserving a fleeting mortal against the rigours of time.             This Seba can be activated at any time as an instant action. Stars appear in the sky behind the bearer, visible to them and those nearby. The light of these stars forms a mantle or halo behind the bearer, and collects in their hands in the form of a sickle.
            This blade is guided by the mind, not the body. Attacks roll Intelligence + Occult + 2, ignoring the target’s armour. Attacks against Mummies do Aggravated damage as if they were made with a relic weapon, and the bearer is unaffected by target number increases. Attacks against enemies of Fate score successes on a 6 or more.

            Curse: Fate is inscrutable, and the bearer must struggle to work against its agenda. The bearer of this Seba gains successes on 10s when working towards an endeavour Fate does not approve of, whatever that may be. This occurs whether the Sickle is active or not.

                Session 47

                Sekhet Bastet launched her assault on LDI's island hideaway. She had gathered several of her slum-dwelling cultists and her Sadikh, and Sesu Ra dispatched Luca to help her. They attacked in broad daylight, beginning with a meteor strike that blasted apart the boats and wharf on the island. There was a terrified exodus as unarmed men and women fled the small nest of buildings into the jungle, away from the attackers. The attackers were in a single small ship, driven by Kudama Jav, and were packed tightly together. When LDI did return fire, security men raking their boat with bullets, everyone aboard was caught in the crossfire. Riflemen opened up, concentrating their efforts on Luca and Sekhet Bastet, even as they each unleashed their Utterances. Luca grew to an enormous giant, while Sekhet Bastet revealed her demonic visage.

                Under the effects of Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, Bast no longer had any need to worry about the bullets being poured into her body. She could, however, see into Twilight, and immediately noticed the two Greater Amkhata there. One, aloft in the skies above on falcon wings with a snarling lion's head, dove to attack her. In the moments before it struck she lashed out with her blessed knife, carving it apart in a single blow. Pieces of the Lifeless thing fell into the ocean in their wake. Seconds later she hurled a second meteor at one of the structures on the shore, silencing the riflemen there.

                They hit the shore. There were more than half a dozen LDI security guards, their bodies augmented and rendered immune to Sybaris. Some switched to flareguns and other incendiary ammunition in an effort to harm the spectral Bast, but it wasn't enough. She dispatched the second Amkhat in moments and tore into the men, tossing their bodies from side to side. Luca concentrated his fire on one particularly intransigent soldier camped behind the ruins of the wharf. In the end, Kudama Jav vaulted the barrier and laid the man out. Meanwhile, Bast's cultists charged into the fray with their shotguns and brought down two of the defenders.

                When the last man fell, the attackers were battered but alive. The encampment was on fire, and the bodies of the dead were strewn about. Bast took a moment to exult in her victory, and her faith in her Judge protected her Memory from harm. She ordered Kudama Jav to make preparations to secure the area, and was about to begin exploring for anything of interest, when she felt something terrible. Her Talisman had been removed from her tomb, robbing her of its strength.

                Back in Jakarta, Akenakhi and Jameela had been keeping a close eye on Bast's tomb. They waited for several hours after she left before making their move. Akenakhi carried several bags of sand into her tomb, while Imelda remained outside on watch. He scattered the sand around, and then transmuted it into gunpowder. He detected the presence of her many nearby relics and gathered them all into a bag, including Bast's Talisman, the Pale Brood.

                In the last few months, Bast's tomb had seen many new additions, expanding it and increasing its geometry. It was now possible for her to step across the intervening miles in a single moment. Unleashing Ancestry of Forgotten Stars again, she did so and appeared in her tomb, face to face with Akenakhi.

                Bast did not wait for explanations. She saw the desecration of her tomb, the theft of her Judge's property, and hurled herself at Akenakhi with a scream of rage. He burned his pillars to seal his flesh and reinforce his body, but her first blow almost tore him asunder despite his efforts. Scrambling backwards, Akenakhi summoned a sickle made of starlight to his hand. Bast was injured from her attack on LDI, he noted, and he hoped this blade would be enough to put her down. His first blow did leave a burning wound in her flesh, until Bast bulled into him and knocked him to the ground. With a single-minded focus she worked her knife through the flesh of his shoulder and tore his left arm, still holding the bag of relics, from his body.

                As Bast became aware of her surroundings, the smell of gunpowder filled her nostrils and almost overwhelmed her. It reminded her of previous lives in which she had seen the blasting powder in action, and hazy memories threatened to distract her. She left Akenakhi's body, damaged past the point of repair, and retreated with the precious relics a safe distance from the tomb. That was where she spotted Jameela.

                Akenakhi's body stirred. Still mobile thanks to the magic binding his body, he rose slowly to his feet and drew a lighter from his pocket. He dropped it at his feet and Bast's tomb was consumed in fire. A part of the tomb shuddered and collapsed, and every wall and edifice was scarred by the explosion. It wasn't enough to drop Akenakhi entirely, however. He walked burning from the tomb and stopped, staring at the tableau before him. Bast stood over Jameela's gutted body.

                The sickle returned to his hands, angel wings spread from his back, and Akenakhi descended on his fellow Arisen. Bast herself was forced to seal her flesh as Ancestry of Forgotten Stars flickered and expired. Akenakhi had nothing left to lose, doomed to a death cycle no matter the outcome of this fight. Bast was forced on the defensive, trying to stay alive until his magic failed. Nothing she tried seemed to stop the sickle in his hands. The blade fell with the inexorable force of Fate no matter how much strength she devoted to keeping it at bay, no matter what unleashings she used to protect herself from its edge.

                But it wasn't quite enough. Akenakhi's last dregs of magic fled his body and he crumpled to the ground. Bast still stood, badly wounded, but her body capable of repairing itself. She slowly placed her relics on the ground and looked towards her ravaged tomb. Her dazed mind slowly took in the scope of Akenakhi's treachery. Her canopic jars had been inside. Every single one had now been reduced to dust.
