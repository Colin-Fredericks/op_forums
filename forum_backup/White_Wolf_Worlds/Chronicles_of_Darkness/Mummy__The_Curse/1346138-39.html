                    The thing that confuses me is that we get this long sidebar telling us that Apotheosis is bad and wrong because it's anticipated that mummies seeking power for themselves (not what apotheosis was in 1e) is part of the cosmic cycle, just as the Shaniatu sought power beyond their station, but then there's no support for this notion later in the book.

                    I think the writers missed what Apotheosis was in 1e. It wasn't accumulating power or "tearing down the master's house with the master's tools." Apotheosis was about an artistic class abandoning the cycle of exploitation and the power it gave them. They rebuild their identity and have to refrain from hurting their cultists (since that threatens memory in 1e) and in return are freed from the Judges' control and gain control of their own labor, represented (imo) by their ability to create relics again. They lose access to top tier Utterances, and their activities are heavily circumscribed by the fact that losing memory sends them immediately into a death cycle.

                    The change I would suggest would be to make the book's perspective on Apotheosis to be a thing that's debated among arisen, instead of the reader being told what is the right or wrong way to rebel by the authorial voice in the sidebar.

                        My thoughts.

                        Chapter Six
                        ------------

                        The intro paragraph is pretty epic. I especially like the sentence that starts the second paragraph, the one which begins with "They crawl from tombs an crypts.." Its very poetic.

                        Historical Adventurers is a nice sidebar, thumbs up on what it represents.

                        Cuzco, Peru - Oooh, how interesting. I mean the fact that there are Iremite mummies here from the first sothic turn is nifty. Lots of potential.

                        Denmark - I wouldn't have thought of this place as a central to the mummy myth, so the information in this part is fascianting. So thumbs up.

                        Halifax, Canada - For some reason I misstyped this as Hekufax, which amused me considering Heku is magic. Anyway, having Canada be the location for mummy intrigue is kind of brilliant. Especially the whole "no one knows who you can trust, maybe not even yourself!" I like that.

                        Necromancer - In terms of the mummy setting and rules what exactly is a necromancer?

                        Also yay on the Saboteur saving relics from the evi judges.

                        That said I love hearing things like "Relic architecture" and "Sekhem-collecting network".

                        Antartica - Oh this is cool. A part of Irem in a land of snow and ice, so cool. I also really like how Antartica is a place in a lot of CoD splats that has some mythic potential, its really cool.

                        I LOVE how Irem came to this continent and established a base, outpost, or even city. Its really cool.

                        South Africa - The whole rebel movement idea related to Irem interests me. Especially as what might have been 'bad' from Irem's perspective might honestly be 'good' from our own. And that leads to potential campaign seeds.

                        New Zealand - Some interesting material here.

                        Hong Kong - One one hand I am not sure I would use this, but on the other haand it makes PERFECT sense as a potential mummy campaign location. So for that reason I am glad its here.

                        All in all Chapter Six is a good one. The way its organized, the location it covers, and the way things are presented are fun. I don't need the mateiral now, but I do think come certain campaign ideas the material present would be very very useful.

                        -----

                        Chapter Seven
                        --------------

                        I have been looking forward to this chapter quite a bit.

                        The intro is well done. Nice advice. Everything from Tips to Sharing is informative.

                        I'm not sure if the safety guidelines sidebar is necessary but its not bad that it is there.

                        Troupes and Pyramids - The the idea of using something world epic as a starting point for group unity, and then having that resonate through history due to the non-linear nature of things sounds interesting to me.

                        But even more so does the whole idea of a number of mummy Cults coming together over the years. The nifty thing about this is that the new united cult is going to be different then the sum of its parts. Which might also explain certain differences and variaties in terms or departments or whatnot. I like that. Its cool.

                        Heck, there is nothing that says certain parts of the old cults might not have been abosrbed or when they were there were elemenets kept secret. Which is fun.

                        The whole thing about non-mummies in a game is amusing when it is mentioned that a mortal could be the grandson or grandfather of a character. I also like the element of letting the player keep the XP gained from other characters, even as things get rearranged and such. In this I can easily see the Reborn coming into play, and that could be fun.

                        The Truly Epic Chronicle sounds fun. hehe

                        Neter-Khertet

                        This is a very interesting place. I like the elements of it - the two mountains, the storm, the caves, the nature of the land. Its very eerie but its also interesting and has some potential.

                        I wonder if something could be 'built' here by a mummy, leading them to have some sort of base of operations in the space between. hmm

                        Death Cycles

                        Okay so technically the above doesn't work, but, nothing says that they can't find some other way to get into twilight to allow such a thing.

                        That said the rules for resurrection are interesting and I like how 'simple' it is. This is especially useful as it allows for a relatively quick return to the adventure going on in the land of the living, and that is important.

                        Duat

                        I like the idea thought I have to admit the whole Law of Suffering has me going "yeah no." So there is that.

                        Fiends are interesting. I like how they are the native 'life' of Duat. Very fun. As is the fact that they can find a wya to enter the world, which leads them to becoming Amkhata.

                        The Seven Gates

                        This stuff is cool. I am not entirely sure how I would want to use it, but its cool, that I am very sure on. I like the potential of it.

                        Sooo don't trink from the cup of Anpu, gotcha.

                        Okay. So upon ending every Descent they descend into Duat where they face the Trials. Upon success they wait a bit in the timeless place that is there before arising again for the next Descent.

                        Here is what I don't get, if the potential for failure is really there then that means mummies aren't truly forever, which makes no sense to me. Which is why I think I might go with the idea that failure is not really real in the whole "you will be permanently removed" sense but rather failure leaves the Judges or the Devourer with an easier time modifying the mummy. Which is why you don't want to fail.

                        Oh wait, just got to Penalties. lol. I see we actually were thinking the same thing. So yeah nice.

                        Escaping the Cycle

                        First, as a random thought, are the blessed fields of starry A'aru real. Like I mean are they somewhere that someone could actually go to. Or are they a promise that has no true bearing. I'm just curious.

                        Apotheois is cool and so is Reward. Now I want to find a way for my mummy to get those stats.

                        Secret Name. Woah, what would shifting allegiance to Anpu actually entail. Hmm

                        As random thing, if one stops having to go through Duat and instead only has to face a death cycle does that mean they never get to city the black and red city? Which is probably a good thing but I am curious.

                        Storytelling Timelesseness

                        Nice start, but this is particularly potent "None
                        but the Judges can permanently alter the Arisen, and even they face limitations."

                        Adrift in Endless Waters - I love this section. So cool. Time as a river and time as a wheel is both really cool. hehe

                        The Churning Maelstrom - If I thought I liked the previous section this one had me grinning and going "f-in awesome!!" So yeah this is brilliant. Love it.

                        Different Flows - AWESOME stuff. This is some great material, some great options.

                        So yeah, this stuff is cool. Very very cool.

                        Tales through the Sandglass

                        Nice start. Good information.

                        Biker Fiends from Duat - I don't mind the idea though turning a good biker gang bad is not fun for me, especially as there is nothing that can be done to save them. So yeah not for me.

                        The Shadow of Sothis - This is cool. I also like the whole mystery behidn the relic and why it works and how it works and how it keeps on coming back even though it should be gone. So yeah that's cool.

                        Far From Home - Woah. Wow. Just wow. So much fun potential for a campaign start. That said, I really hope that a mummy could wake up and act in other ways than simply bloodshed. Imagine a mummy waking up and immediately making those around them obedient or something. That could be fun. Still, as it is written, tjhis is brilliant.

                        Children of Irem - The idea of the Deathless having a child is kind of intriguing. Its also a potential fun campaign variant.

                        The Darkest Turn - There can be only one! Also, while nicely written, this one ain't for me.

                        Memory Broker - I don't get this. It eats memory, but so what as far as the mummies are concerned since they don't have much they remember. Eating memory is more useful as a curse for a being that remembers everything. Honestly, I think having the 'monster' unlock the mummy's memories would be more interesting, especially if the horror of the bargain was great. That said as the horror of being a monster is not something I care to dwell on in roleplaying this eleement is not gonna be really dwelled on by me.

                        Mortal Power - This could be interesting. Lots of potential here.

                        First and Foremost - I honestly reject the claim of this section (as related to the horror of memory) and so don't really see it as a potential campaign point. BUT, that said, the idea of the 'failures' and the 'testers' of what would be the Rite of Return are still around is an intriguing idea, one that has potential to be used and played around with.

                        Build-a-Beast - This is cool. This is a nifty campaign idea. This could be something a saga is built around.

                        The More You Know - This is a perfectly designed campaign idea. Which side do you support, which side do you fight, what happens if both are right or wrong and something else goes on. So yeah, this is very cool.

                        Mummies on Mars - Firstly, love the name. This is cool. What a nice set up. Its also quite believable in terms of design. I also like how there is something for those both on Mars and on Earth, so that is cool.

                        Duat Falls - Everything about this except that the Rite of Return might fail (to me its a one time act that is now foundational with no chance of being stopped) is brilliant. Stop the Judges. Free yourself by just simply not listening to them. Over power them and take the city and make it yours. So yeah, goo stuff. I also like the whole meret thing going to Duat together and facing the judges as one. So yeah good stuff.

                        A Glimpse of the End - See this is cool in the sense that if you have it be a Descent early in a chronicle then you can have the players seek to fight against it over its length and span. So while I don't consider it the true future it would be a perfect "Oh shit, things are bad, lets fix it" element.

                        Begins Again - Oooh yes, this is cool. Very very cool. So cool in fact I want to think more on it, to analyze the potential, to dwell on the interesting saga it can have. So yeah, very cool.

                        This is a cool part of the chapter. It has a lot of potential interesting elements and story notes. I like the format, a few paragraphs and then bulleted ideas. I like the nature of it.

                        Good stuff.

Which is, in fact, what 1E Apotheosis explicitly was; Dreams of Avarice made it explicit that you can chose the Judges' power or you can chose eternal humanity, not both.
