        A lot of the flavour of the Guilds comes through in the Affinities in Guildhalls because the ones in the core book are 'generic' rather than Affinities specialised for doing 'Guild Business'.

        Mesen-Nebu alchemy and transforming Dedwen are in Guildhalls, Tef-Aabhi manipulating Heka is in Guildhalls, Scribes with writing based powers are in Guildhalls etc.

        Talismans are a whole extra layer of complexity so I'd recommend not using them straight away, they are basically a way of ameliorating the effects of losing Sekhem to the Descent but I think that would be distracting early on as the ticking clock element is a big part of Mummy and countering it with Talismans might dull the impact.

        Remember, Apotheosis is Heresy!

        I'm kind of biased in favour of Guildhalls as I wrote about half the errata thread on the old WW forums so I know it fine toothed comb style :P
