                                        The Unspeakable Name Affinity is probably one of the very best demonstrations of the "master of the True Name" thing. "You think knowing my name gives you power over me? Oh no, it means part of me is inside you, and thus you'll never escape my reach."

                                        Regarding the Judges of Duat... according to the opening chapter the Shan'iatu definitely wanted to become Judges, but we have CAS's word that they didn't, let alone any evidence like number discrepancies. But the big rites at the end of Irem "violated time" - and the Shan'iatu lost a ton of their power over the Empire's course, enough that they had to take deliberate steps to prevent their human students from equalling them. So what if they misinterpreted things (or just fucked them up), and "became" the Judges in the same way that a human "becomes" a Promethean - ie being stripped for parts that are then made into something new. They made the Judges at the Empire's end - but that act of making reached back along their world-lines and leeched their power away for itself.
                                        Or maybe they were going to be the Judges, but squabbling over who'd be the bestest of the Judges annoyed Azar enough that he took the power and gave it to 42 others to be Judges instead (maybe 42 of the potential Arisen who were later thought not have made it through the Rite were actually given more important jobs).
