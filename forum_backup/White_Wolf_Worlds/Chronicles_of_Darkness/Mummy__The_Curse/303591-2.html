                        This book has a great mood to it. The whole imagery of 'dark starlight' really sells it to me. This book isn't about stars burning brightly in the night sky - it's about the dark shadows swirling in between them. The text is very evocative, and the art is fucking terrifying - nightmare stuff.

                        In case anyone was wondering: the Deceived are assholes.

                        They're just jealous. They found out something was going to happen and they were going to be short-handed, so they planned on exploiting the situation and screw everyone else over, but then they got called out on it so they're just pissed. They don't hate the Arisen so much as they hate those who created them.

                        It's like if you and your friends were planning to prank a pizza shop into delivering you a ton of pizza for the price of one. But you, the guy soon to be Deceived, were conspiring to eat all of that pizza before your friends. Then they found out and made sure you got no pizza at all. So now you're planning to burn down the pizza shop.

                        Like...what? What the hell kind of asshole are you?!
