      Purified, but not as presented in immortals (not sure what the 2e treatment is like), as loners and rare, unusual individuals.

      As families, only 'purifying' new members (if at all) after they have produced the next generation - or if the ritual is particularly risky on their deathbeds - though the risk is minimised by repeated attempts, and the scale at which these families operate.

      These families would act as partly patriarchal/matriarchal organisations, part ancestor worship Cult, part corporations adopting new members into the family, depending on the particular group and their founders.

      They might compete with vampires (in an Immortal power game, reminiscent of VtM) but more likely have deals with the forsaken. Learning how to do rituals, sharing knowledge of spirits, and likely getting into conflict with some particularly forsaken pack who decide they should not be allowed to exist (being a mixture of spirit/human, which the forsaken typically think want to keep seperate). I don't know about pack totems, and whether they would need one or gain what they need for the head of the family to be one...

      I think there is room for an entire game there, mortal sorcerers recruited over the years, centuries of occult knowledge accumulated in some libraries, maybe some ghosts (of those who died in the purification gone wrong) left behind are kept well protected and remembered and safely anchored where they are most needed...

      Cross-overs with Mage Liches, Mummys, werewolves, Sin-eaters, and vampires. Oh my.

                                                      I think the Purified have a great balance of useful powers mixed with the ever looming risk of being a snack to a spirit. They are great for NPCs. How they got their ritual and the components in itself can tell a story, as well as what made them want to take the risks on board or what placed in the right situation for it to happen spontaneously.

                                                      Second favorite are the Blood Bathers, who epitomize the "power at a price" aspect of immortality. The more perks they get, the more hoops they need to jump through. They may become a future Geist as they try to cling to life, they may inspire the Legend of a Slasher, so they are antagonists with a far reaching legacy.

                                                      Third favorite are the Psychic Vampires. They are on a time limit, needing to harness ever more Essence as the years go by, being able to burn through resources quickly. They can be mistaken for vampires because they can manipulate minds and cause damage while feeding. They can be mistaken for changelings because they can feed on will (rather than emotions) and the ones with Soul Eater can be mistaken for Eaters of the Dead.

                                                      In short, I like the balance between risk and reward of the Purified and my other favorites are the ones with powers that push them to cross paths with the players.​
