                Session 8

                This session was rather skewed. By which I mean was that this session saw Sesu Ra be declared the game's protagonist, with everyone else just filling supporting roles. His decision to exclude the others from Tef-Aabhi Guild Business meant he was off by himself doing active and exciting things for most of the session, which took up a lot of time. Just thought I would preface this with that. It was a seriously big session for him, less so for everyone else.

                We started with the session with Aquila returning to the mainland after her encounter with the jackal-headed beast. She went to the Temple, and found Mot-Tan meditating there. The reclusive Maa-Kep revealed he had left his territory to meet with Sem-Huf to discuss an issue arising in both their lands. After a bit of questioning, Aquila discovered that someone had killed one of Mot-Tan's cultists and implicated the other Mesen-Nebu in the act. However, upon further investigation, Mot-Tan found it was a lie. The actual perpetrator had been a third party, someone unknown, trying to provoke a war between Mot-Tan and Sem-Huf.

                At this, Aquila decided to recount the story of her conflict with Sem-Huf. When she reached the part about one of her cultists, a man named Ahumara and one of Ligo's uncles, being found staked outside her tomb, Mot-Tan interrupted her to ask if she were sure Sem-Huf was responsible. Aquila realised that she hadn't ever had any definitive proof of guilt, just having assumed it was Sem-Huf due to their conflict. Troubled by this, she asked Mot-Tan to question Sem-Huf about it, and then left quickly before her enemy showed up.

                Akenakhi, meanwhile, was trying to proselytise to the mortals running the restaurant above his tomb. When the owner, a Mrs Dartlan, didn't seem to be taking him seriously, he unleashed the second tier of Revelations of Smoke and Flame. The resulting Terror Sybaris sent her and all of her staff fleeing the building. Not the greatest start. He then met up with Aquila, and they decided to try and work out a plan for the bank heist they'd been planning.

                Sesu Ra waited through the day for nightfall. He was informed by Banek, the leader of the local cabal, that the Pelamat had been spotted entering the district with a lorry and a few motorbikes, presumably preparing for one last attempt at ending the ritual. Banek mentioned something about "the guardians" and how the cabal would bring them over to help with the defence. With Luca and Tobi besides him, Sesu Ra prepared for a fight inside the ritual chamber. Sure enough, just as the ritual was due to begin, the Pelamat attacked.

                There was the sound of fighting in the house above. It dragged on for quite some time. Finally, there were footsteps on the stairs. Banek came into view, saying that the guardians couldn't hold them off, and then a group of blank-faced construction workers, squatters, and other assorted people surged down the stairs with makeshift weapons in their hands. Luca picked up his rifle and gunned down three in a spray of automatic weapon fire, but the survivors seemed unfazed and threw themselves at the defenders. With the others distracted, the rotting, disfigured man from the previous attack appeared and charged Sesu Ra.

                The fight was brief and exceptionally deadly. Everyone was rolling ludicrously well. Sesu Ra found himself nearly maxed out on lethal damage despite sealing the flesh, and largely survived his fight by using Dominating Might to keep his disfigured opponent knocked to the ground. Luca shot his opponents dead and came over to help. Tobi and Banek each killed a man. Finally, there was only the disfigured attacker left. Blow after blow rained down on him, but he kept struggling back to his feet. Luca picked up the sacral blade used to perform the sacrifices and lopped off one of the man's arms, but he continued unflinching. Finally, Sesu Ra pinned the creature to the ground and Luca drove the blade through its chest, and it lay still.

                At that point, Manfred possessed his corpse and walked towards the weapon. Sesu Ra decided that he couldn't risk the ritual being corrupted, and tried to ask Manfred to stop. The ghost pointed out that he had been given a command that he could not disobey, a command to perform the ritual. With that in mind, Sesu Ra punched Manfred and knocked him to the ground (Dominating Might is just amazing), tossed the sacral blade to Luca, and commanded him to execute one of the survivors of the attack. Luca did so.

                Luca lost two points of Morality and gained a derangement as a result of all this, I would like to add.

                As the blade claimed a mortal's life, Sekhem roared through the whole of the Old Dutch Quarter. Sesu Ra was transported back in time for a fleeting instant, to when he was standing in a temple in lost Irem with darkness clouding his eyes, a voice somewhere pronouncing that this would be the day he died. When his vision cleared, he was still in the present and there were animated corpses rising from the ground and bursting from the walls of the basement. Manfred declared that he had free will once more, and turned to Sesu Ra.

                "I did as you commanded, oh Unbending Spine. I claim this land as my reward. Be gone."

                With a veritable army of undead emerging from the woodwork, Sesu Ra decided to leave. As they left, they passed the carnage on the building's first floor. A dozen dead men, and in their midst two corpses that had taken an unbelievable amount of punishment. As they watched, these two corpses started to twitch and show signs of life. With Manfred's ominous words preying on them though, Sesu Ra decided it would be best if they left.

                He wanted to see what the ritual had wrought, however, and travelled first to the building the cabal had called "the Gateway". In the darkness beneath the building, he found that the ground between the inverted obelisks had fallen away. A hole in the Earth stood there now. Standing at its top, Sesu Ra could hear the sounds of waves far below, but he couldn't see anything. Sekhem surged around him in waves, and he gained a descent reset as a result. Analysing the result of the ritual, he realised he could slow the descents of every Mummy in the city, if he used the National Monument as a linchpin and carried out some of the same designs on a larger scale. Sesu Ra told Tobi that he could return to Rekhenaten, his mission fulfilled, with the knowledge that the experiment had been a success.

                He had one final objective before he left the Dutch Quarter. He went to the Museum of the Dutch occupation, the place where the Effigy the cabal protected was being stored. It was late at night and the building looked abandoned, but for some reason the front door was unlocked. Sesu Ra and Luca went inside, found that many of the exhibits away from the main entrance were in poor condition, and eventually ended up in a room undergoing renovations. In the plaster dust on the floor, they spotted footprints. Sesu Ra cut his way through the doorway (Dominating Might again), and they found a ritual room with a few ominous bloodstains, mostly cleaned away and long-since dried. A trapdoor led down below.

                They descended, found an ancient stone door covered in Iremite hieroglyphs and three stone sarcophagi. Two were empty. When Sesu Ra tried to cut through the stone doorway, a piercing scream froze him in place. A European man with a noose around his neck and a young Indonesian woman with bones piercing through her skin appeared. When his khopesh passed straight through the man, Sesu Ra realised he was facing ghosts. With the man wielding telekinetic attacks and the woman stunning them with her cries, they were a challenge even before the third sarcophagi opened. A second woman, this one looking middle-aged and covered in scars, wearing clothes stained in places by dried blood, attacked them with an axe.

                Finally, Luca managed to use Chthonic Dominion to turn the European ghost on the corporeal attacker. She eventually went down after taking an unholy amount of punishment. The female ghost eventually stopped screaming and vanished. With the threats dealt with, Sesu Ra broke open the vault (so much use out of Dominating Might this session) and found a strange pedestal within. It was shaped like a man on his knees, with a cage made of rib bones placed on his back. Inside the cage was a single small item covered in a silk shroud, surrounded by a palpable aura that something here was not meant for mortal, or even immortal, eyes.

                Sesu Ra took it and went back to his tomb. He noted that the woman he had killed was already beginning to heal, just like the men back at the cabal's temple. He went past her without trying to make sure she stayed dead. Once he reached his tomb, he removed the shroud to discover his prize. His natural affinity for Effigies enabled him to determine it was the Blasphemous Depiction, even as its curse struck him with the Vocalisation derangement. It was a maddening sculpture of the Judge Arem-Abfu, a horrifically blasphemous creation that bestowed the ability to perform a primitive version of the Rite of Return. By using it to bind ghosts into corpses, he could create near-indestructible undead beings and give them a single command, a command they would try to complete. The creatures were virtually feral, resorting to violence at the slightest provocation and without much in the way of intelligence, but they were also nigh impossible to destroy. Of course, the curse was that the sight of this relic was enough to shatter the psyche of those who looked upon it. All things considered, it was probably good Aquila and Akenakhi were not around, or else they would be suffering from Schizophrenia and Irrationality.

                He also ordered Luca to scope out the Dutch Quarter, keep the cabal under observation, and to find a way to kidnap Banek for leverage later. And that was about it for him this session.

                Akenakhi decided to camp out at the Arisen Temple to wait for Kotira. He felt it was probably best if he alerted his Guild's Guildmaster that he was in the city, even if it was a century late. Alerted by the Tomb Guardian, Kotira showed up a few hours later and formally welcomed him. She told him there was much work for the Su-Menent in Jakarta, blasphemy was everywhere and it needed to be rooted out. Many mortals were dabbling in things they should not, and she hoped he would have much success in serving the Judges.

                Aquila, in the meantime, went to talk to Mot-Tan to find out how his discussion with Sem-Huf had gone. The big revelation was that Sem-Huf denied any responsibility for killing Ahumara, which had been a serious driving force behind Aquila's desire for revenge. Mot-Tan felt it was pretty clear that something else was involved, and that by fighting the two Mesen-Nebu were playing right into its hands.

                Aquila went to Sem-Huf's tomb, that thought preying on her mind. She stood outside the main residential building and waited for his cultists to alert him to her presence. He came out to meet her alone. After a long, awkward silence, they talked through the situation. Although they each made it clear that they would be more than happy to kill the other one more time, there was clearly something bigger at stake. Until the mystery of these cultists' murders was resolved, they would cease their vendetta.

                With that ending the session, it was time for some descent checks. Sesu Ra has managed to score one every session for the past three sessions, so he was still at Sekhem 5 (functionally 6 thanks to the Talisman) when this one wrapped up. Akenakhi dropped from Sekhem 7 to Sekhem 6. Aquila dropped from Sekhem 4 to Sekhem 3.

                    Session 9

                    Sesu Ra managed to get his Ren up to 5 last session, which means he can now chat with Luca telepathically. As a result, when Luca swung by the cult's island the next day and found every single one of them in a coma, he was able to point out this interesting development to his master. Sesu Ra gathered up Akenakhi and Aquila, Aquila said it was the jackal-headed creature doing it, and they all set off for the island.

                    It took a while, but they eventually managed to lure the creatures in with the smell of their Pillars. They didn't seem prepared to take on three Mummies at once, but when Akenakhi split from the group they were more than happy to gang up on him. Both the small lion-headed bull and the jackal-headed snake with bird wings attacked him. He used a pistol borrowed from Luca, and killed the jackal-snake (sending centipedes everywhere), but proceeded to get thoroughly mauled by the lion/bull until the others showed up and beat it to death. Luca turned out to be fairly ineffectual in the fight for once, his attacks not seeming to do much to the hybrid monster, but the others were able to finish it off without much trouble. The one minor complication is that the blows sent up clouds of some kind of fungal spore, infecting the entire group.

                    They decided, finally, that they should probably get some outside help. The infected Mummies were already starting to smell pretty rank. Aquila stayed behind to nurse the comatose cultists, while Akenakhi and Sesu Ra travelled back to the Temple. They waited there until dawn the next day, when Kotira Nahan and Yarikh arrived for a dawn prayer that, it seemed, was some kind of daily ritual for them. Akenakhi meditated alongside them, and when that was done Kotira left and they asked Yarikh if he knew how to deal with their fungal infection and how to wake their cultists. Yarikh finally gave them a name for the creatures that had been plaguing them, "Amkhata", and told them they had been fighting relic-spawn (well, they knew that). He revealed that spending two points of Pillars and focussing on their own Sahu would burn out the infection, and that the cultists would wake over the course of the next few days. In the meantime they should keep them hydrated and fed as best they could.

                    Sesu Ra and Akenakhi sent Luca off to relay this information to Aquila, and then went to Akenakhi's tomb. Akenakhi's player got sick of having a Haunted tomb really quickly and had spent his accumulated experience buying back the Haunted drawback, so Sesu Ra descended into the tomb and used Chthonic Dominion to find a composite ghost made of the limbs, faces, and mutilated bodies of at least a dozen individuals nesting in Akenakhi's tomb. Every broken and bloody face in the creature's mass turned towards Akenakhi and screamed "Murderer!" For a moment, he had a vision of standing atop a pile of bodies in an underground chamber, perhaps his old tomb while it was being built. Before it could act though, Sesu Ra used the second tier of his Utterance to bind it with the command "Protect me from harm", and then used the third tier to shift its anchor from Akenakhi's tomb to Sesu Ra's own body. Not only that, he erased the creature's memory of how it/they died.

                    The next few days went by. Akenakhi went to Mrs Dartlan, owner of the restaurant above his tomb, and apologised for his earlier display. It turned out she had rationalised the experience of a flaming angel manifesting as the building catching fire, possibly from a thrown firebomb of some kind. Between that and one of her staff found beaten to a pulp in a nearby alley, she was quite convinced her business was under siege. He told her an ancestor of his lay buried beneath her restaurant, and had been responsible for originally constructing it. She seemed interested, and agreed to work with him on finding out more.

                    Aquila burnt out her own infection, and spent the next few days caring for the cult. When Phatri woke, she used Healing Counsel to make sure the old woman was still in good health (at this point, we're pretty certain Phatri is being maintained at an effective Morality 10). Phatri seemed a little disorientated or delirious, and called Aquila "Mother". A short chat teased out the fact that Aquila had raised Phatri from infant through a lot of her childhood.

                    Once all that was sorted, Aquila and Akenakhi went off to put their bank robbing plan into motion. Akenakhi sat in the lobby with a newspaper and used the first tier of Palace Knows Its Pharoah to explore the structure and the bank vault. They found some gold they could steal and a few lock boxes with valuables in them hidden below the building, although Akenakhi failed a few Wits + Composure rolls and couldn't work out what a few of the strange electronic gizmos did. Just as he went to leave the bank, he was accosted by an energetic young woman who tried to persuade him to donate to wildlife charity battling to save many of Indonesia's endangered animals. He refused to subscribe to the newsletter, but swore he'd be back to donate money to the cause later.

                    Aquila tried to work out a deal with a local charity to begin a massive campaign in one of the city's slums to provide food and support. The group was interested, but said they would like to get their lawyer to look at any such agreement. Aquila donated what money she had on her, and left.

                    Sesu Ra spent this time examining Jakarta's National Monument. He's tried to use it to reverse engineer a complete ritual, the second part that was meant to synergise with the experiment he found in the Dutch Quarter, but he all he could really tell was that the sacred geometries of the structure had something resembling his own signature style. He realised he must have known the end plan when he added his designs to it. The key must still be in his own memories.

                    And that was really it for this session. Aquila picked up the Refined Purity of Purpose Affinity, allowing the rest of the group the enticing prospect of trading Memory for Resources. No one has taken her up on that offer, but in the meantime lots of unscrupulous individuals in the city are going to start getting a bit wealthier whenever she's in the area. And I forgot to get Akenakhi to make a Descent check. Have to remember to get him to do that next time.

                        Session 10

                        Aquila called in the favour Sesu Ra owed her for guarding the national monument a few nights ago. She'd realised that her Haunted tomb was probably haunted. Up until this point her player had been acting as if the visions and visitations were largely in Aquila's head, with the assumption being that it was a sign of her going mad. Recent events, particularly the discovery of a ghost living in Akenakhi's tomb, made her suspect that there might be a ghost in hers. So they all went down to Aquila's tomb to toss Chthonic Dominion around and see what turned up.

                        Sure enough, under the light of the first tier the ghost of a young Indonesian woman was revealed. She promptly through herself at Aquila and dragged her into Neter-Khetet. At first the ghost then fled from Aquila (following success on an Intimidation attempt), but when Aquila tried to be compassionate and understanding the spectre became furious. It screamed that Aquila was a child-taker, kidnapper, and murderer, and started attacking her. Eventually, Aquila was able to drain the ghost's Willpower to render it unconscious, but she took another point of aggravated damage from the accumulated attacks in the process. Sesu Ra was able to cast Chthonic Dominion Tier 2, giving it the command "Make the bank vault appear full" as he'd been asked by Aquila. He then turned Akenakhi's tie into an anchor.

                        Sesu Ra and Akenakhi then went off to chat with Yama-Het, apparently not terribly perturbed by Aquila's Twilight-bound state. Aquila, in turn, decided to make the most out of being stuck in Twilight and went to check out the building where she had first met Sem-Huf. She found a few interesting things, namely a false wall masquerading as an exit route in one of the apartments, but being out of phase with the world didn't really help her perception skills. After about an hour she returned to the real world. In the process though she witnessed two great mountains, one in the West and one in the East, and recalled a few myths from Irem about their presence and significance in Neter-Khetet.

                        Meanwhile, with Yama-Het, Sesu Ra announced that he was planning on instigating some Tef-Aabhi projects in her land in West Jakarta. The suburb was undergoing an urban renewal project, making it perfect for her purposes. He warned her that the Pelamat would no doubt try to intervene. She was reasonably accommodating, but in turn asked for his help dealing with some local Preman, an organised crime mob that she believed had kidnapped one of her cultists. She wanted him rescued with minimal bloodshed, and her associates were all known to the mob making it hard to gather information. The cultist, a young man named Ari, used to work fro the Preman in the district, and they'd tried several times to lure him back. This time, it looked like they were giving force a try.

                        Aquila and Akenakhi then reunited and visited Rawa Bening, one of the largest gem markets in South-East Asia. Aquila revealed that she planned on using Equivalency Principle in conjunction with Refined Purity of Purpose in order to replace the gold she intended to steal in the bank heist. She would steal the gold, use it to gain Resources 5 with Refined Purity of Purpose, then spend 5 Resources to buy gems of a high enough value to be transmuted into gold bars, which she could then use to replace those she took from the bank vault. If she took less than a day, and Akenakhi kept the cameras from functioning, and the ghost maintained the illusion that the gold was still there, she could hopefully carry out the heist without anyone realising anything had ever gone missing. Then it would only cost her a single point of Willpower to replenish her Resources.

                        In the process, they ran into Hague, Mot-Tan's European henchman. A brief chat revealed that there were a few vessels scattered through Rawa Bening, but that several undisclosed supernatural forces were vying for them in some kind of cold war. He cautioned them not to use magic in or near the building, and to steer clear of the vessels themselves. Most were likely Amulets, and thus his master's property, but it was proving hard to transport them back to Mot-Tan's territory without the courier mysteriously disappearing.

                        And then it was heist time. Aquila, Akenakhi, and his tie (and companion ghost) travelled to the bank, went underground using Dust Beneath Feet from a few blocks away, and headed for the vault. Aquila anticipated that the ghost might try to be difficult, and left the tie wrapped around a bit of plumbing several hundred yards from the bank itself. Or at least, she thought she did...

                        What followed was a confusing sequence of events, and none of the players were truly able to figure out what was real and what wasn't. Akenakhi shut off power to the vault, and kept an eye on the guards as they tried to work out what was going on. When in the lockbox room, Akenakhi and Aquila heard gunshots and felt bullets pierce their flesh, but there was no visible shooter and the guards outside the room didn't seem to react (Akenakhi was watching them through the walls thanks to Dust Beneath Feet). Aquila left Akenakhi behind to watch them, and entered the vault. She picked up a bar of gold, and then realised it was an illusion. A man-sized snake with the head of a falcon appeared in front of her, but she ignored it and grabbed some gold. Real gold, as far as she could tell. The enormous Amkhat then wrapped itself around her, and sucked out a point of Sekhem. Aquila tried to break free, but three failed attempts later and she was on the verge of death. Then she realised that the tie, with the ghost anchored to it, was still in her pocket and not tied to a bit of plumbing as she'd believed. She decided not to Seal the Flesh, figuring that if it was an illusion her damage would be gone and the creature would disappear. Instead she died.

                        Unaware of what was happening, Akenakhi saw a squad of black-armoured troops rush down the stairs, haul the security guards away, and stick something square to the door. Then the falcon-snake Amkhat materialised behind him and grabbed him. He was able to resist the drain on his Sekhem, break free from the grapple, and slide away underground just in time to hear a muffled explosion. He escaped.

                        Somewhere entirely removed from that debacle, Sesu Ra and Luca decided to interrogate the prisoners they'd taken from the Pelamat warehouse where they'd met Rangarni (or at least, the man they were calling Rangarni) all those sessions ago. They travelled to the Families' island and had the prisoners hauled out before them. When the first man refused to answer his polite questions, Sesu Ra killed him rather than even attempt an interrogation, then turned Luca loose on the others. This earned him a descent reset, and by putting his faith in Set-Qesu he avoided degeneration once again. The other men broke quite, quite quickly after that display.

                        And that was it. Akenakhi dropped to Sekhem 5, joining Sesu Ra who is still Sekhem 5, not counting his Talisman. And Aquila is Sekhem 2, and that's before we even find out what happens in her Death Cycle. Which is another thing I have to write up. I... can't say I was expecting another from Aquila so soon, so we'll see what I come up with over the course of the next week.

                                Well, Aquila is trying to get the "free Resources" aspect of Refined Purity of Purpose to trigger, and decided a gold bar would be the easiest way to do it. And in all fairness, all she really had to do was sidle into a bank and take one.

                                Ten sessions actually feels like an incredible amount of time for Sesu Ra to still be Sekhem 5. From the top of my head, I'd expect most players to be Sekhem 6 after two sessions, Sekhem 5 after three. They are then making Descent checks every two sessions, with reasonable odds of success. We've found that players proceed through the early part of the descent at a rapid rate, but the lower levels (particularly 1-3) are far more forgiving. Dying doesn't help though.

                                As for the player returning, they do need something to fuel the return. At present, Sesu Ra has two relics (he decided to bind the Blasphemous Depiction into his Lifeweb alongside his Talisman) that can be used to resurrect himself, so he has it easy. Neither Akenakhi nor Aquila have relics or vestiges on hand to use in that manner, and they're both currently on their Sothic Turns. If they end their descents, coming back could prove a problem.

                                Even if they had relics though, I would likely rule that they had to stay in henet for a few weeks at least. A lot of the plans the group has, particularly Sesu Ra's, need time to come into fruition, and it would be a good excuse to fast-forward a little bit (although ideally not so far and fast that it triggers descent checks for everyone, at the very least I'd let those players play out attempts to stave off the descent). Ultimately, their cult will re-summon them, but it will cost Aquila the Memory dot she purchased and someone will need to acquire a relic or vestige to be consumed in the process. I don't want it to be something done lightly.

                                And of course, if she does begin a new descent, that would be it for her Sothic Turn. She would come back with a purpose, and all the additional complications that imposes.

                                        Page 76 of the Mummy corebook, "falling into Duat after a descent automatically lowers the Mummy's Memory by one dot (to a minimum of Memory 3) unless he rose in response to a disturbed tomb". Just a thing that happens, a way for the Judges to stamp down on any pesky individuality or something.

                                        As for the speed of the Descent, it's pretty clear that Mummies are meant to spend more time at the 1-3 range than, well, pretty much every preceding level put together. On a Sothic Turn a Mummy can last at Sekhem 1 right up until something kills it, and even when they are on the clock the "story" time frame they last for is "an entire Chronicle". Depending on the length of your game, that could easily mean more time at Sekhem 1 than 2-10 combined. And it does make relic draining an increasingly tempting option.

                                        As I remember the first session, everyone was Sekhem 8 by the time the first session ended, and were making descent checks to fall from Sekhem 7 by the end of the second session. Sesu Ra did make his Talisman in the second session, so he was still effectively Sekhem 7, but everyone else was Sekhem 6 going into Session number 3. It's fast, but it actually informs play in a really beautiful manner. At high Sekhem, Mummies drive straight for their objectives as fast as they can with one eye constantly on the ticking clock. Once the initial surge of power has fallen away, they start to relax a bit and actually explore the world and the consequences of their previous actions.

                                                        I'd usually just assume a single Talisman, as counting on being able to grab and hold onto more than one is a risky prospect. It makes it hard to build a character for. One is easily achievable though, and does allow you to maintain an effective Sekhem 7 when necessary. Choir unleashings are also quite useful once you start to hit the low levels for the same reasons.

                                                        There's also the Jar of Clay Utterance from Guildhalls of the Deathless. As far as I can tell, it basically allows you to decide what Sekhem you want to be and to maintain that indefinitely for a slight Pillar cost. Heck, it's a first tier unleashing, so you could basically do it for free once a scene if you have a Talisman on hand and spend from your Defining Pillar. Every time you place a point of Sekhem in your organs you fall to a new Sekhem rating, starting the descent clock afresh at the new rating. But then you can suck the point of Sekhem back and be at the level you were before, except now you've only just reached that level, effectively earning you a descent reset. It's hard to see how a Mummy with Jar of Clay could ever go below Sekhem 7. Sekhem 8 might be hard to maintain, since you have to go to a canopic jar and do the ritual every three scenes (but not impossible, particularly if you carry a jar around with you), but it's tricky to imagine a session ever going past without a player finding time to do this.

                                                        Anyway, yeah, you're pretty much correct about Aquila. She was last awake in the 1990s, from memory, and her activity over the last century has been pretty rare (three separate risings in the last 100 years, if I remember right). Sesu Ra, meanwhile, has been active every 3-5 years, sometimes for more than a year at a time. And Akenakhi was last awake in the 1940s. Access to a relic allows for a pretty big step up in activity, but Sesu Ra couples that with a personality and goals that require frequent activity. Sekhet Bastet also has a relic, but she hadn't been seen since the 1970s. As a Sheut Mummy, she believes her place is in Duat. And from the cult's perspective... Bast isn't necessarily a person you actually want to have around. Great if you want a whole lot of people dead, but otherwise probably best to leave her where she is.
