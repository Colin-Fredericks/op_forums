    The relic Kudama brought with him was the following, as far as Bast could determine:

    The Book of Dead Names (3 dot Text)
    Size 3, Durability 2, Structure 5

    The Book of Dead Names looks like a large, heavy book with iron clasps on its covers. When opened, its pages are filled with tiny, cramped scrawl that appears to have been written in the native tongue of the reader.

    Power: The bearer of the Book of Dead Names may take an instant action to call a ghost to their presence, so long as they are in proximity to one of the ghost's anchors. If in the presence of someone who has died since the last sunrise or sunset, the bearer may spend a point of Willpower to turn them into a ghost anchored either to the immediate area or the bearer themselves. This ghost always appears wearing a cowled rob and carrying a scroll in their left hand, and always has a compulsion to protect the bearer.

    Curse: The bearer develops a fascination with death and the book's own powers. Any time the bearer is in the presence of the recently deceased, they must roll Wits + Composure. If they fail, they must spend a point of Willpower to use this relic's power. If they do not have any Willpower, they develop a new derangement (normally Fixation or something similar appropriate). Mummies of the Sesha-Hebsu do not suffer this Curse.

        Session 39

        Rom Mayehi must really hate his job sometimes. Today he had the unenviable task of calling Sekhet Bastet and Sesu Ra and informing them that Aquila's Sadikh and Akenakhi were holed up in a room at Asa Corp, with Akenakhi unconscious and Quil apparently keeping him that way. That job done, he very quickly removed himself from the situation.

        The two Arisen arrived and ordered Quil to stop what he was doing. Under questioning, he revealed that Aquila had instructed him to keep Akenakhi unconscious until her return, but that she should have returned hours ago. The Arisen ordered him to stop what he was doing, so that Akenakhi could reveal what he knew of the events.

        It would be a few hours until Akenakhi regained consciousness. Sesu Ra had Vania, the cult's historian, dig through her records to find out if Aquila had any history of acting erratically. He plumbed his own memories while he waited, probing at the hole in his mind.

        He remembered the sound of the doctor's hammer, driving the chisel deeper into his eye. He considered the tools being used, comparing them to the ones he used in his own trade as a mason. Another blow, and the blade shifted deeper. The doctor sat back, a self-satisfied smirk on his face. Behind him, Sesu Ra could see his new Sadikh, Luca, watching. He waited. He should have felt something by now, but it hadn't worked. He stood up. The doctor's expression turned to one of shock. Deaf to the admonishments that he should lie down, be still, continue the pretence of being an anaesthetised patient, Sesu Ra reached out and grabbed the man's head, twisting it right around. There was a sense of resignation as he left the room. If he couldn't physically purge the memories from his mind, he had only one other choice.

        The memory broke, reforming some time later. A bloody khopesh was in his hand. The boats from Ellis Island had stopped flowing. It had worked, although he no longer had a clear grasp of what "it" was. He had to leave now, this city was too familiar, too liable to provoke memories better left buried. Passage had been booked to a new city. A city without history, he had told Luca, and so a Dutch trading post in the Pacific had been chosen. The voyage would begin this very night.

        Another break. He remembered the dark and the pain. Somewhere, a woman was crying, great heaving sobs. He heard a door open in the distance, letting in the sound of jazz music for just a brief moment.

        "They're coming for you again." His modern self recognised Iwseck's voice. "Do you think you'll break this time?"

        Sesu Ra gritted his teeth. "I will never break."

        Soft hands removed his blindfold. Iwseck looked different in this life, although some of her features shone through the flesh of the poor white girl she was wearing. The room was lit by candlelight. One of his eyes was crusted shut with blood. On a chair on the other side of the room, a small wrapped bundle could be seen. Iwseck smiled down at him. "So strong," she murmured. "Let me help."

        His pain ended. The vision faded shortly afterwards.

        Vania's records, about a century's worth of Aquila's activities, did not seem to provide any explanation for her current behaviour. Of course, Sesu Ra was aware that a mere hundred years was an insufficient time scale on which to get a gauge of an Arisen's character.

        He returned in time to witness Akenakhi regaining consciousness, and the explosive swearing that followed. When the Fog of Torment finally calmed down, he revealed that Aquila had taken nearly all of his power. He told the others that he had offered to allow her to take a small measure of his Sekhem to help her, but that she had repaid his kindness by stealing everything he had. He didn't know how she had taken his Sekhem, but he did know why. She had gone to fight Gator.

        On Akenakhi's information, the meret travelled to investigate the bus terminal where Gator had made his lair. They found signs of a fight, cracked earth indicative of a fight between two creatures the size of elephants and armoured in stone. Luca found evidence of a shattered statue that could have been Aquila, but there was no sign of an equivalent one of Gator. He proffered the opinion that Gator had walked away from the fight. Aquila had not.

        The meret had rumours of murders in nearby slums that matched Gator's heart-devouring MO. The squatter settlement that hid Sekhet Bastet's tomb was not far away, so they started there. Bast retreated to her tomb to make sure everything was in order, and then began to ritually cut her flesh with a bone knife. The pain honed her Kepher visions to a startling accuracy, finally allowing her to locate the Uter relic she had been hunting for so long. She saw visions of rusting metal drums, a long neglected chemical plant, and a red sky burning beneath the heat of a black sun. A pressure in her mind told her where she needed to go to find this place, hidden up the river from her tomb.

        She told the others, but they decided they were too weak to mount an assault on the lair of the Shuankhsen. Sesu Ra in particular was cautious, asking that they wait until his current descent had finished. As he pointed out, his Sothic Turn was imminent. With that power, he would be of far greater help in any attack. The rest of the Nome should be alerted and their aid sought, although Sesu Ra was of the opinion that only Aqhat could be counted upon to help them.

        In the meantime, Sesu Ra realised that the final part of his geometric network should be in the area. Following his directions, they were driven downriver until they reached a swathe of long-abandoned towers and apartments. The entire area was abandoned, the buildings clearly damaged by flooding. There weren't even any squatters to be seen.

        Sesu Ra led them through the area to a crumbling hotel, built in the classic colonial style. The windows were clouded with filth, some of them damaged and missing. Inside, the room was entirely bereft of furniture, and there was a build-up of detritus against the walls. In the centre of the room were four pillars, looking entirely out of place.

        This was the heart of the area's geometry. They explored the room, noting hieroglyphs cunningly hidden among the scrollwork and finer details of the hotel's greeting hall. Then Sekhet Bastet stepped between two of the pillars in the room's centre, and vanished. Sesu Ra rushed over, and followed immediately.

        They found themselves in a place outside of the world, in the void of space. Above, below, and all around them were the stars, constellations that they recognised, mixed with glowing symbols and hieroglyphs marked out in golden light. They were standing where the Earth should be, but the Earth itself could not be seen. The room was like a transparent sphere, suspended in space, and no matter where they stood gravity always seemed to make "down" beneath their feet. Surrounded by the stars on all sides, Sekhet Bastet unleashed the third tier of Secrets Ripped From Skies, asking the heavens for help.

        "The Radiance would end Solar's reign and break the ties of Fate. The Restless Stars must stand, lest the world fall."

        Akenakhi followed the other two a few minutes later. As he appeared within the sphere, another being appeared alongside him. It had the shape of a woman, her features picked out in tiny motes of glowing starlight. She looked like a moving constellation, impossibly vast even in human form. Akenakhi recognised her. He had cut her throat, more than four thousand years before.

        "Was it worth it?" She spoke a dialect of Ancient Egyptian, sounding like a badly butchered version of Iremite barely understandable to the Arisen.

        Akenakhi was shocked, and shook his head. "No," he said, on the verge of tears, "no, it wasn't. I am sorry."

        Sesu Ra was eyeing the apparition, trying to come to a decision. Satisfied that what he was looking at was, indeed, a ghost, he decided to unleash Chthonic Dominion Tier 2, commanding the ghost to tell him everything she knew of the magic of this place.

        The command settled upon the ghost, binding her like iron. She screamed with rage and turned on Sesu Ra, appearing in front of him and plunging her hands into his chest. Although he felt no physical pain, his pillars began to spiral rapidly out of him, drained from him by her magic. His Ren disappeared first, and then he began to feel his flesh wither under the assault.

        Yet, despite her anger, the ghost could not stop herself from speaking. She spoke of the magic she had been killed to reveal, telling them that there was a magic in the name of stars they could not even comprehend. This magic had long been hidden from the world, and could only now be seen by those who had already wielded it. She spoke of the eternity she had endured, bound here beyond the world, bare and open to the gaze of the malevolent stars.

        Worming her hands ever deeper, now stealing Sesu Ra's Sheut, she spoke of a light she had seen. Scientists called it a quasar and knew it to be the brightest thing in the universe, but she knew better. It was like a living thing, coursing through the stars. It would devour all other light, and feast upon the hidden magic.

        Those final words said, she sealed her mouth tightly shut. Sesu Ra endured her onslaught a little longer, but when it was apparent there was no more information forthcoming, the meret found they could return to the world simply by willing themselves away.

        The meret decided they needed to tie up any loose ends regarding the ghost cult they had encountered. Akenakhi and Sesu Ra went to meet with Adelaide to see if she had any help she could offer, while Luca and Sekhet Bastet found that the cult's meeting place now had a sign declaring it Condemned. They were able to track down the home of Abilo, the cult leader, and were met at the door by a pretty young woman who introduced herself as Calisto. She told them that her father didn't want to see them, and when they tried to push past her she stabbed Luca in the neck with a knife.

        More men, including Abilo, came rushing from the house. Ghosts began to materialise around them. Bast unleashed Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, revealing her demonic form. The men were overwhelmed with Sybaris, flinging themselves to the ground. Only Calisto was entirely unaffected, shrugging off the supernatural terror with a supreme act of will. Luca bludgeoned her unconscious, while Abilo went to flee. Bast walked after him, flitting through walls and dancing across the impossible angles until she was standing in front of him. Before he could react, she reached out seized him by the throat. His flesh withered away and he was mummified, a frozen scream distending his face.

        Luca stayed on watch to see if the brief struggle had alerted the authorities, but it seemed that no one in the neighbourhood had raised the alarm. The cultists were tied up, along with Calisto. Exploring, Bast found the iron box she had seen Abilo carrying in their last encounter. It contained a human skull, missing its lower jaw. When she touched it a creature appeared in the fog of Twilight, a ghost consisting of a floating skull and a writhing mass of tentacles that appeared to be made from vertebrae. It was unable to harm her, however, so she ignored it until it disappeared.

        Calisto surged to her feet then, rushing at Luca. She snatched his pistol from his waistband. When he seized her, she put the gun to her own head and fired. In the struggle, however, the shot did little more than graze her. They subdued her again, struggling to understand how she had escaped her bonds.

        Back at Adelaide's home, Sesu Ra and Akenakhi had managed to get through the door and were now waiting for Adelaide to get home. She did, saw them, and went pale. She demanded that they leave. Akenakhi tried to talk her down, saying that they were here to help, but she pointed out that she had made her feelings towards the undead very clear the last time they spoke. The two Arisen left. Sesu Ra, being able to witness the events playing out through his Sadikh's eyes, didn't mind overly much. It seemed the situation was under control.

        Akenakhi returned to the abandoned suburb. He remembered the priests of Iunu calling this place the Vault, and stepped through to speak with the girl he had killed. She appeared and attacked without speaking, taking from him his Ab. He stayed as long as he could, begging for forgiveness, and left before he was destroyed.

            Session 40

            Aquila fell ever deeper into Neter-Khetet. She remembered standing on a hill on the day the city fell. The gates were opened from within by treachery and starving citizens, trapped between a desperate warlord and a mad emperor. It was the end of the only empire since the days of Irem she considered worthy of the title. She wondered if this was what it would have been like to watch Irem die.

            The barbarian horde swept through the city. They had cut her off from her cult, her temple. She was making her way back to her people when she was struck from behind. The blow sent her sprawling. It was the first time in more than two hundred years that anyone had dared raise a hand to her, and she rose brimming with fury. The man gave her an insolent smirk, wearing the armour of the invaders. She had seen how men behaved in the aftermath of conquest, and Qerrti compelled her to act.

            She unleashed Harvest the Divine Flesh, unveiling her true glory. Rather than cower from the Sybaris, however, the man's smile only widened. His comrades formed a loose semi-circle around them as he levelled his spear at her and thrust, driving the blade deep into her body. She raised a hand to strike back, and heard a voice shout out, "Stop this madness!"

            An old man interposed himself between the fighters. He moved with the vigour of youth, but his hair was grey and his face lined. He apologised to Aquila profusely, speaking Latin with a thick Gothic accent. His men had heard stories, he said, and were much too eager to test themselves against one of the gods. Aquila sneered, expressing her surprise at hearing a barbarian speak the civilised tongue, but said that he spoke well for what he was. The man's effusive thanks for her compliment mollified her, and she Sealed her flesh to knit the wounds she had suffered back together.

            The man explained that he had come with a message, not for her but for the one who would one day remember these words. He locked eyes with her, and she fancied she saw bright lights in the depths of his gaze. "Fate hates you," he said. "It loathes you. It twists the world against you and brings ruin to all your endeavours for no reason other than that you are what you are. But Fate can be broken, its chains smashed, and what must be need not come to pass." In every Sothic Turn, he explained, Fate could become mortal. If they destroyed the bonds of Fate, then, at last, they could know peace.

            The vision ended, and Aquila awoke in her tomb. Her last canopic jar was gone, her body reformed around her talismanic spirit organ. She had only the last dregs of Sekhem remaining, and she realised what that meant. She had nothing to give back to Akenakhi, no price to pay that would lessen her betrayal. Overcome with shame at her hubris, she sank to the depths of her tomb and nursed the growing hatred for the hand Fate had dealt her.

            On the surface, the Arisen were being stirred to a frenzy of activity. Sekhet Bastet had rallied the Nome. With news that she had located the nest of the Shuankhsen, she had arranged for every active Arisen in the city to gather at Rekhenaten's tomb. The Master of the Pit was not terribly happy with this arrangement, and made it clear that he expected neutral territory to be used for all future gatherings of this nature, but he was happy to play host this one time. He had no intention of becoming the next Temple, however.

            The Arisen began to arrive. Only Aquila, hiding in her tomb, and Mot-Tan and Kotira, currently in henet, did not make an appearance. Rekhenaten had arranged for a lounge room on his estate to be used, a large room filled with armchairs and lavish carpets. Yarikh joined them first, but chose a corner of the room to stand in. The siblings Mekshonat and Benerib were next, escorted by Rekhenaten's cultists who had been helping to arrange new tombs for them in Jakarta. Hetenatra arrived seated in a wheelchair, pushed by her cultist Imeni. Sem-Huf chose a chair close to where Rekhenaten himself was seated, while Yama-Het and Aqhat chose to stand.

            Rekhenaten dismissed his cultists before the meeting began, and ceded the floor to Bast. She spoke of the vile nature of the Shuankhsen, of their parasitic lust for Sekhem and the need to destroy them like the rabid dogs they were. If left to fester, there was the danger they may create more of their kind, maybe even from among those Arisen in this chamber. She had tracked a relic they had in their position, and believed she knew the location of their primary stronghold within Jakarta. She called upon the Nome to put together a combined crusade to destroy these creatures.

            Aqhat stepped forward immediately, before any others could react, to offer his services. Mekshonat was next, volunteering his sister's help in destroying these creatures. The others were more measured, but every Arisen present offered some kind of support. Sem-Huf, though too weak to fight, said he would dispatch as many of his cult's soldiers as he could spare. Yama-Het would send her chosen warriors, augmented with her magic, but cautioned that they should be returned to her the minute the fighting was done lest the magic rend their bodies asunder. Yarikh offered as many Awakened Servants as he could create on short notice, while Hetenatra offered to come in person and ensure that judgement was brought down upon the wicked. Finally, Rekhenaten said he would send his Sadikh, Tobi, a capable fighter with the power to call upon Rekhenaten's own magic.

            Decision made, the Arisen moved quickly. Asa Corp arranged for vans to transport the Nome's forces, sending them around the city and to gather the army at Asa Corp. Sem-Huf's mortal cultists were chosen as drivers, as they would be of little use in actual combat in the face of so many sources of Sybaris. Sesu Ra chose to retire to his tomb and send Luca in his place. He had no interest in fighting the Shuankhsen in person while his Sekhem was so low.

            Below the city, Quil alerted Aquila to the cult's movements. The interest drew her out of the depths. A strike against the Shuankhsen interested her greatly. A chance for revenge, perhaps. However, she dared not show her face openly, not until she could repay Akenakhi. She summoned Tetasu Xex, knowing that the ability to drain Sekhem would be vital if they hoped to destroy the Shuankhsen with any kind of permanency.

            That was when she found that she had a message from Mike Thornton. She called him, and after he finished berating her for being out of contact for so long, he told her that LDI had come to a decision. The attacks on their port facilities had done a lot of damage, and they were retreating from the city. Only a few projects would remain in place, which would be folded into Asa Corp as "research divisions" of the smaller company. They were going to trial a less intrusive presence, to see if the donations of Sekhem from willing Arisen could make up the potential shortfall. Aquila contacted loyal members of her cult, arranging for this merger to take place without her direct supervision, hiding LDI's presence within the cult from the rest of her meret.

            Mechanically, this gave the cult the Compartmentalised advantage.

            The vans began to move out, the Nome's army inside. Aquila, Quil, and Tetasu followed in a van of their own at a discrete distance. They wound through the city, following the kepher trail illuminated in Sekhet Bastet's mind.

            The chemical plant was located in the industrial quarter of the city, along the banks of the river. As they arrived, Bast realised why a mundane search had never found the place. There was a distortion of the air lying across the entire property, giving the impression of a mirage. Any attempt to notice it, to see it as something significant, failed. The eye simply drifted away from the plant, finding other, more important things to focus upon. Without the scarab's path telling her that this was where the relic lay, she would never have paid it any attention.

            The front gates were padlocked shut, but a blow from Aqhat tore the very gates themselves from their hinges and sent them crashing to the ground. Beyond was a long gravel driveway, littered with construction equipment and rusting steel drums. At its end was the plant, two long wings extending up and downstream on either side of a central tower. Bast's kepher unerringly pointed forward, to the top of the tower.

            The vans disgorged their contents. Kudama Jav, his book in hand, summoned forth his spirits to aid his master. Awakened Servants shambled out to form a vanguard of undead flesh. Then came Yama-Het's warriors, a man and a woman who had been greatly altered by her magic. The man had the head and claws of a lion, while the woman had a falcon's head and a great pair of wings spreading out behind her. The skin of both almost seemed to shimmer with a golden aura, a palpable manifestation of the magic inside them. Tobi, bearing a Japanese officer's sword from the second World War, summoned Rekhenaten's magic to armour himself in earth. They each crossed through the gates, the Arisen in their midst.

            Stepping across that threshold filled even the mightiest of the assembled Mummies with a sense of nausea. The world beyond was broken. A black sun burned in the midst of a sky mottled like a purple bruise, surrounded by dark stars. The ground cracked underfoot, and through the cracks could be seen flesh oozing like an open wound. Bast, looking into the world beyond with Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, realised something else. Twilight did not exist here, Neter-Khetet had been folded into the physical world. Kudama's ghosts manifested physically, every bit as real as the creatures around them.

            The group advanced on the factory. That was when Akenakhi spotted the lesser Amkhata. A host of them, almost all with the heads of falcons or jackals, descended on the group. During the fighting, several could be seen fleeing back towards the main building. The Arisen army overwhelmed their attackers easily enough, having more trouble from the scorpions, bees, and centipedes that spilled from the creatures' wounds than from the Amkhata themselves.

            Hetenatra announced that she would remain outside, to summon her magic as needed. Akenakhi muttered something snide beneath his breath at that, about avoiding the danger and claiming all the glory. Bast had Kudama stay with Hetenatra, while the rest of the group advanced on the main tower. Aqhat smashed down the front door, and they all filed inside.

            There was a large circular room at the base of the tower. Three doors led off in different directions. Bast's kepher only told her that she needed to go up, and there was no way to tell which door was the correct one. Akenakhi unleashed Palace Knows Its Pharaoh to try and discover the correct route, and had to bite back a scream as the information flooded his mind. The interior of this building did not conform to normal physics. His mind could not make sense of the barrage of information, of the way rooms lay within smaller rooms and down became the same as up. He had a sense that the central door was the one they wanted, but more than that he could not determine.

            The door led to a staircase, which wound up and up and up for an interminably long length of time. As they grew weary of the climb, they smelled something foul in the air. A moment later a hissing, toxic liquid came pouring down the walls and stairs. The more nimble of the invaders were able to avoid being hurt too badly, but the Awakened Servants lost much of their flesh to the corrosive fluid.

            They rushed to the top of the tower to confront the source of the attack, but as they charged the stairs began to change. The stairwell's walls became craggy stone, and one by one the other members of the group disappeared from view. Bast and Akenakhi each found themselves alone in a canyon they recognised. The shadows formed jackal-headed demons carrying bone knives, and oversized scorpions emerged from the sands. They realised slowly what had happened. Somehow, they had fallen into Duat. There was nothing they could do except run.

            The canyon maze was hard to navigate. Bast unleashed Ancestry of Forgotten Stars to make herself faster, easily outpacing her pursuers. Akenakhi was not so fortunate, suffering cuts and stings from the demons who dogged him. Neither of them fell, however, and they emerged into a burning sky for a moment before they fell into a chamber beneath the earth. Like the first chamber they had entered, it was round, but this one had a sloping floor that fell towards a large grill in the middle. They were back on Earth and in what looked to be a waste disposal chamber.

            Aqhat was there already. The lion-headed warrior emerged from the walls seconds after they did, and they were joined by Tobi moments after that. There was no sign of Luca, nor any of the others.

            A rumbling came from the pipes leading into the chamber, along with a high-pitched wailing. The group stood back to back as a host of the dead emerged. They mostly appeared to be workers from the chemical plant, badly scorched and broken by whatever had killed them. They crawled on shattered limbs as they spilled like a flood of flesh towards the intruders. The lion warrior tore into them in a frenzy, joined by Bast and Tobi. Akenakhi and Aqhat stayed back, noting with dismay what little impact the attack was having on the enemy's numbers. Aqhat strode to the central drain and smashed it open, calling out for the others to follow him. Then he leapt down into the depths.

            When Bast pulled her way out of the horde to follow, she experienced the disconcerting knowledge that she was getting closer to the relic above her despite the fact that she was going down and ever sense told her that she needed to go up. The Arisen and their allies emerged in a neat, grey corridor. The walls were cracked like the ground outside, the flesh within festering with nests of maggots. Akenakhi poked one with his spear, and grimaced when it burst.

            They heard the sound of fighting and rushed to the nearest door. It flew open at a touch and they piled into the room. Benerib was there, one arm missing and her body shattered beyond any Arisen's ability to heal. As they watched, a being with the head of a diseased lion, who Akenakhi recognised as Iwseck, tore her apart and swallowed the last few chunks. With her was Gator, armoured in stone with his flesh coloured gold and his eyes made of jewels. Behind them, Nenheta lurked near the back of the room. An Amkhat with the head of a lion and the body of a serpent roared a challenge, and a group of Gator's Fasad, led by the one the meret knew as Rotlung, rallied at their master's side.

            There were no speeches or declarations. The two sides charged. Aqhat rushed the Amkhat, severing its head from its body in a single blow. Scorpions flooded the room, spilling from the thrashing body as it died. Rotlung charged Bast, tearing at her with his oversized teeth. She seized him by the throat and cut him apart, her blessed bone blade easily parting his Lifeless flesh. Before she could recover, however, wounds began to appear on her body as if from nowhere. The assault came from Nenheta, her hand outstretched to strike at Bast's shadow. To her left Akenakhi battled one of Gator's Fasad, keeping the savage creature at bay with his spear. The third Fasad vanished under the lion warrior's claws, limbs and chunks of flesh flying as Yama-Het's soldier demonstrated the strength of his blessings.

            Aqhat went to the defence of his fellow Su-Menent, knocking Nenheta to the ground and embedding his axe in her chest. She spat out the power of the Kiss of Apep as her death curse, infecting his body with a mystical poison and robbing him of his ability to heal. Then Iwseck leapt on him from behind. Her lion jaws sank deep, ripping him apart. The great Shemsu of the Su-Menent fell. As he died, the entire room shook, and shook again. The roof collapsed, bringing with it fire and the wrath of the Judges. Meteors struck at the Shuankhsen, and Sekhet Bastet realise it must be the work of Hetenatra. The attacks were carefully placed, hitting only those enemies who were not so close to the Arisen as to risk harming them. Bast took advantage of Iwseck's sudden injuries to revenge Aqhat's death, striking a blow that almost ripped Iwseck's jaw from her head. The Shuankhsen rounded on her in a fury, her jaws biting out chunks of the of the Arisen's flesh. Ancestry of Forgotten Stars saved her from losing Sekhem to the creature, but she could not maintain the spell for long.

            Gator had seen the earth-clad Tobi and, seeing him as the closest thing to Aquila, had bulled his way through the melee to attack the Sadikh. His massive jaws kept Tobi staggered and on the defensive, doing horrific damage to the Sadikh despite his armour. Akenakhi, trying to help, rushed over and laid a hand on Gator's body.                                         Sesu Ra's exploration of his own memories took him back to the days of Irem, to his mortal life, and the years he spent working in his Guildhall. It was the happiest time of his mortal life. He lived with Iwseck and worked under the tutelage of a Shan'iatu Guildmaster, among the finest masons of Irem. His skill was such that his master chose him, alone, to create an effigy of unsurpassed power. It would be a mighty statue, a representation of the master himself, and so long as it stood the Shan'iatu's power would be beyond any challenger.

                                        He worked for many days and nights. The heka of Irem flowed into his work chamber, and even to his mortal senses the statue's power became clear. He began to carve, creating the form of a Shan'iatu in full regalia. He worked upon it in secret, accepting no help from the Amulet Bearers of the Maa-Kep, spurning even his fellow masons.

                                        There was not enough power in the city's energies alone for what was needed. The Shan'iatu sent him slaves, and they died by Sesu Ra's hands. Their blood stained the statue's feet red, even as their Sekhem infused the effigy with untold majesty. It was his finest achievement, his life's work. But something was wrong.

                                        He recalled a night in his chambers, Iwseck by his side. She was worried about him. All he could think about was the feeling of the knife in his hands, watching today's slave die at the statue's feet. It had been a young man today. The day before, a woman, Iwseck's age. He remembered wondering how long it would be before Iwseck suffered such a fate. Irem was not kind to its slaves.

                                        Sesu Ra made his choice. He returned to work on the statue. The head had yet to be carved, and required only the slightest modifications for what he intended. Form was power, and he knew best how to change the form to garner the power he needed.

                                        The day of the unveiling came. He stood proud among the crowd, knowing what was to come. As the statue was bared to the assembled masses, a sudden stillness came across the square. The statue wore the regalia of a Shan'iatu Guildmaster. It contained all the power Sesu Ra had promised. But rather than the mask of a Shan'iatu Guildmaster, its face was Iwseck's.

                                        They were in the temple. The Shan'iatu's guards kept Sesu Ra on his knees.

                                        "For your skill, you will become one of our immortal servants," the Shan'iatu said. The master spoke of the thousand lifetimes he would live in glorious service to the Judges. A great honour, worthy of a craftsman of his talent.

                                        "For your treachery, a price must be paid." That was when Iwseck was dragged, struggling, into the chamber, and the Shan'iatu spoke of the need of lives to power the Rite. He gave Iwseck a golden cup and whispered something to her, too low for Sesu Ra to hear. Her eyes fixed firmly on her husband, Iwseck drank, and he watched helplessly as black veins rose up her neck.

                                        The last thing Sesu Ra remembered, watching Iwseck die, was the order being given to have him prepared for the Rite of Return.

                                        Back in present day Jakarta, Sekhet Bastet and Akenakhi each made the effort to reconnect with their cultists in Asa Corp. Through them they discovered the others had returned from henet. Although the Sphinx of Rome was absent, the other three gathered together in the one place. Akenakhi had managed to discover a second vestige in his exploration of the textile market in Yarikh's neighbourhood, although the location of the rival Arisen's tomb still eluded him. Sesu Ra noticed the vestige and became extremely interested. He asked Akenakhi if there was anything he could offer in return for the items, as he needed them for a great ritual for his guild. Akenakhi magnanimously declared that he would offer them freely, a gift to his meret. Sesu Ra accepted happily, and began to share the information he had gathered on the state of Jakarta in 2013. Most importantly, he mentioned that Yama-Het had somehow got her hands upon a relic he had made in his mortal days, and that he intended to get it back. Aquila had been raised for this purpose, but he was growing quickly impatient.

                                        The three Arisen set off then to speak to the Sesha-Hebsu guildmaster. Akenakhi and Sesu Ra had lost power enough that they could pass for human, but Sekhet Bastet was asked to keep to the back of the van where no one could see her. Luca drove them to Yama-Het's community centre, where they found her cultists. Sesu Ra was refused access by her cultists, who said their master did not want to have a Tef-Aabhi within her quarters while the tensions were so high.

                                        Akenakhi and Bast went in without him, Bast grudgingly agreeing to be concealed in heavy clothing to prevent her from driving Yama-Het's cultist mad with fear. Akenakhi asked Yama-Het about the relic, and she confirmed that it was a trophy she took from defeating a Shuankhsen in battle many years ago. She did not see any reason why she should give it up. When Akenakhi suggested that she part with it so that Sesu Ra could make peace between her and Rekhenaten, she grew angry. Rekhenaten attacked her. She was not going to give up her property to buy peace. If anything, he should lend his resources to helping her rebuild. Neither of them had any significant advantage over the other, and he was in the wrong.

                                        Akenakhi left to report this to Sesu Ra, but Bast remained behind to ask Yama-Het some unrelated questions. She wanted to know about the "square-eared beast" and the significance of red-haired men. While Yama-Het wasn't able to tell her anything about the latter, she did mention what she knew about the shah, Sutek's Beast, a group of relic-creatures created with the components of relics from each of the five Guilds. These creatures were destroyed during the first Sothic Turn, either by the Shuankhsen or simply over time as their power drained away. Bast mentioned her belief that her true purpose was related to one, and that she felt she needed to return a shah to life. She asked if Yama-Het knew where she could find the remains of one, but the Sesha-Hebsu Guildmaster was unable to help.

                                        Bast left, but she wasn't the last of Yama-Het's visitors. Luca had stolen past her guards and entered her chamber. She looked up when he entered, and Luca informed her that his master wanted the return of his possession. The relic she had taken as a trophy was his. Yama-Het merely folded her hands and said that she would not be making any deals until the conflict with Rekhenaten was over and reparations had been made. Sesu Ra chose that moment to step into his servant's place, swapping positions. They each knew that, in a fight, the drained Yama-Het wouldn't have a chance against the newly risen Tef-Aabhi Guildmaster. Sesu Ra made some veiled threats about entering the conflict on his subordinate's side, and Yama-Het told him she did not think he wanted to make this a guild affair. If he left, however, she would make sure to tell her cultists not to shoot him on the way out.

                                        Sesu Ra decided to leave, although he was sure he had no intention of order Rekhenaten to make peace with the Sesha-Hebsu. The Arisen took a moment from there to go and speak to Aqhat's cultists in charge of monitoring the siege of the Shuankhsen to find out what had happened to Iwseck, Nenheta, and Gator. Nenheta had been killed by Hetenatra early in the siege during a breakout attempt. She hadn't been seen since then. Gator had made several attempts to escape, even fleeing underground using Dust Beneath Feet, but the moment he left the protection of the World's Wound Hetenatra's flaming ghosts converged and burned him until he retreated. As for Iwseck, she hadn't been seen once. It was entirely possible that she had been trapped on the outside when the siege began.

                                        Without any clear leads, Sekhet Bastet asked her meret to help her track an uter she had discovered simply through allowing her kepher senses to roam. The Kepher led them to a Doctor Jameela, a GP working in an affluent neighbourhood not far from Akenakhi's tomb. Akenakhi was able to talk them past receipt to grab fifteen minutes with the doctor, who took one look at the Arisen and told them all that they should be dead. Bast responded to this by slamming the human against the wall and demanding everything she knew of a withered, mummified heart. With the help of her Fearsome Soul affinity, she broke the doctor quickly. Jameela confessed between terrified sobs that she had discovered the heart when she was young and travelling in the Middle East, and had brought it back to study. She had some passing affinity for the occult, and when her cousin Imelda started to die from a heart condition, she realised she could use the heart to save her. She transplanted it into Imelda's chest, and her cousin had been fine ever since.

                                        Akenakhi realised what Bast was about to do then, and tried to stop her. He wasn't quite fast enough. She pressed her hands against Jameela's throat and subjected her to the Withering Judgement, reducing Jameela to a mummified corpse. In response to Akenakhi's horrified protest, Bast pointed out that this would wear off in a few hours. It was just to stop the doctor from warning her cousin.

                                        The meret walked out before anyone discovered the body and went immediately to the address Jameela had given them. It was an old, stately colonial mansion, with a garden full of wildflowers, orchids, and weeds in equal measure. The facade was stained an off-white by exhaust fumes, but other than that appeared to be in good repair. Bast walked up to the front door. When no one replied to her knock she asked Sesu Ra to knock the door down. He declined. Akenakhi noted that there was a tall hedge wall around the property that made the building proper hard to spot from the road. He was forced to repair his damaged pillars, torn apart by the Shuankhsen, on the spot, and then unleashed Revelations of Smoke and Flame Tier 2. He flew up to the second story and punched a small hole in one window, allowing him to reach inside and open it.

                                        Bast narrowed her eyes at this, and kicked the door hard enough to break the lock. She didn't trust the other Su-Menent not to do something to spoil her efforts in retrieving the uter. She ran through the house and up the stairs, while Akenakhi raced along the upstairs corridor towards the only door with light coming from underneath it. They reached the top of the stairs at the same time, neck and neck, but Akenakhi was the faster one. He reached the door to the room and stepped inside, slamming it in Sekhet Bastet's face. She snarled and unleashed the Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, allowing her to phase straight through the door and appear in Akenakhi's face in her demonic form. She spat at him that she would not be denied her prize, at which point the two Arisen took stock of their surroundings.

                                        They were in an old woman's bedroom. The air was a little stale, but otherwise it was clean and respectable. Imelda was sitting in the bed with a pair of reading glasses on, looking only a little terrified at the flaming angel and ghostly demon that had appeared in her bedroom, but apparently unaffected by Sybaris. Bast demanded that she give up the uter, her heart, and Imelda told her that she had no wish to do so. Bast rushed her and Akenakhi threw himself at the other Su-Menent, yelling at the old woman to run. Imelda went for her bedside table, moving too slowly for Akenakhi's comfort, and to his dismay pulled out a gun. She fired at Bast, but the bullets passed straight through the demon form without hurting their target. Laughing, Bast called upon the Jar of Teeth. Red smoke poured from her mouth and tore at Imelda, ripping the flesh from her bones. Akenakhi struggled in vain to subdue the stronger Mummy, and Imelda tried to run past them, firing the gun at the window, just as Sesu Ra walked into the room.

                                        "Stop her!" Bast screamed at him. There was a moment as everyone waited to see what the Tef-Aabhi would do, and then he struck Imelda with a casual, almost lazy blow that sent her sprawling to the ground. That was enough. Bast called up the Jar of Teeth one more time, and the power flayed Imelda alive.

                                        Akenakhi released her then. Not once looking at her fellow Su-Menent, Bast gathered the heart from Imelda's remains and identified it as the Heart of Sek-Bet, a relic with the power to turn mortals into immortal undead when placed within their chests. Her-Uru rewarded her with a descent reset for her achievement, and she left in triumph.

                                        Akenakhi returned to Jameela's office alone, and found it closed to the day. He was able to go to the nearest hospital and masquerade as a police officer, finding that Jameela had woken screaming in the morgue and that the desiccated corpse found in her practice had mysteriously disappeared. She appeared to be hysterical, but the hospital suspected some kind of hoax. Akenakhi was able to get access to her in a private ward, and managed, eventually, to persuade her to stop screaming at the sight of him. He told her, in broken tones, that he hadn't been able to prevent Imelda from being killed. His companion had wanted her heart.

                                        Jameela asked him if there was any way Bast could be killed. Akenakhi told her that it was possible, but it would be very, very hard. She asked for his help, and he promised to give it. Too many lives had ended at the hands of She Who Is Power, he told her. If he could put a stop to it, he would.

                                            I keep forgetting to post these. Anyway, below is Bast's latest prize:

                                            The Heart of Sek-Bet (4 dot Uter)
                                            Size 1, Durability 2, Structure 3

                                            This dry and withered heart fits easily into the palm of one's hand. It is clearly dead and inert, but from time to time a dark, red liquid will seep from its closed valves.

                                            Power: The Heart requires a newly dead body to be used. It must be placed within the chest cavity of a dead mortal. This is a surgical operation, and requires a successful Intelligence + Medicine roll to properly stitch the heart in place. Once the wound has been closed over the heart will begin to beat, returning the dead mortal to life. They gain the following benefits:

                                            -Their Stamina rises to 6, and their Strength to 4 (provided it was not 4 or 5 already). They gain Iron Stamina 3.
                                            -They become undead. They no longer need to eat, sleep, or breathe. Their health must be completely filled with Aggravated damage before they die, and they do not suffer any loss of consciousness from bashing or lethal damage.
                                            -They no longer heal damage naturally. Instead, they must spend a Willpower to downgrade an Aggravated Wound to lethal, a lethal to a bashing, or a bashing wound to nothing.

                                            Curse: In order to give life, the Heart takes the vital energies of the one who performs the operation. This causes the one who uses the Heart to lose a permanent dot of Stamina. This is more temporary for the Su-Menent, who merely suffer a point of Aggravated damage.

                                                        Session 44

                                                        It was Sesu Ra's turn to let his senses loose along the Scarab's Path, searching for anything that might lead to an Effigy or Vestige to help with his geometric network. Fate was with him and led him unerringly to a large shopping mall in the north of the city. The beacon of the Effigy was so great that it allowed him to locate it precisely, hidden in the desk of a manager's office on the fourth floor. Sesu Ra forced the door and smashed the lock on the desk, finding a small scorpion figurine carved of dark stone within. The rest of the meret had been given enough time to gather for this expedition, and Akenakhi was able to distract a large, heavily tattooed man who came to investigate long enough for the group to get clear.

                                                        They didn't face any further opposition as they left the shopping centre and returned to Akenakhi's tomb, or rather the restaurant above it. Aquila met the others there, and an argument immediately unfolded between her and Bast over the use of an Amkhat in the attack on the Shuankhsen's stronghold. Aquila pretended she had merely tamed the creature, rather than created it, and pointed out that Bast had once done the same when faced with an Amkhat she couldn't destroy. Bast rather resented that, pointing out that she had done so in order to find a way to destroy the creature, not to use it, but it did take the wind out of her sails.

                                                        Sesu Ra tried to reign things in then. With the Shuankhsen apparently gone, the meret needed to work out their new priorities. LDI was apparently scattered to the winds, so their largest enemies no longer appeared to be in a position to hurt them. Of course, Sesu Ra's preference was for his meret to help him deal with Yama-Het. He didn't particularly care one way or the other if it was a violent or peaceful resolution, but he wanted his Effigy back immediately. Retrieving it was Aquila's purpose, so she didn't have much choice in the matter, but the others didn't have any interest in making another attempt.

                                                        In the end, Aquila left on her own to speak with Yama-Het. The Sesha-Hebsu's cultists were welcoming enough, although Aquila could see at a glance that they were on guard. Her first sight of Yama-Het after a year showed just how tired and rundown the other Guildmaster had become. The two talked about the situation, and Aquila was worried at the way Yama-Het's version of events didn't seem to match up with Rekhenaten's. Each seemed to think the other had attacked them unprovoked, although Yama-Het admitted there was a chance she had killed some of Rekhenaten's people during her crusade against organised crime. Aquila's attempts to persuade Yama-Het to give up the Effigy she held were similar to Akenakhi's, and again Yama-Het resented the idea that she should give away her relics to stop a war she didn't start. She repeated her demand that Sesu Ra call off Rekhenaten. Only then would they talk.

It seemed he had been transferred to an overseas posting, forcing her to dictate an email to an Asa Corp cultist requesting information on the disposition of her stolen sword. After that, she returned to report the news to Sesu Ra.

                                                        When Aquila returned from her meeting Yama-Het without success, Sesu Ra began contemplating other options. His expanded memory offered up one possible option. He could remember being captured by a group of fanatical cultists once, men and women who had scarred themselves and painted spider-like runes across their bodies in blood. He had been held down as he watched a blasphemous perversion of the Call being performed. The memory ended with Iwseck waking, rising clothed in the flesh of a dead sacrifice. He studied the memory, and believed he could repeat the ceremony if need be. He dispatched Luca to find for him a recently deceased female body. If he had to, he intended to summon Iwseck and set her upon the weakened Yama-Het.

They recalled rumours of a group that had taken advantage of the destruction of the Pelamat to secure the drug trade in the nightclub district close to Sesu Ra's tomb, back before they were even aware of the Pelamat's nature as a Shuankhsen cult. When coupled with the stories that the south part of the city, which included this area, had become increasingly hostile to the Arisen, the meret decided to investigate and see if the matters were linked.

Aquila's good looks and money got them through the door without a problem. Bast and Sesu Ra tried to mingle with the people on the first floor. Aquila and Akenakhi wormed their way onto the top level of the building. Rather more adept in this environment than their meret mates on the ground floor, they quickly learned that the building had come under new management over the course of the last year. From Aquila's many admirers they learned that the former owner, a man named Benjy, had disappeared mysteriously at the time of the change. It had caused a bit of the stir in the area, but it had actually drummed up a fair bit of business for the nightclub just from sheer curiosity. Probing further, they found that no one was really sure who the new owners were. They were very fond of live music, however, and had ensured a regular roster of performers were always available for the VIP lounge. One of the crowd that had formed around them mentioned that Daryat, the one-armed man in the bar below, was the manager and the best person to talk to. He was notoriously tight-lipped on the subject of the owners, however.

They located Daryat quickly enough, and eventually managed to catch his attention. Aquila bluntly tried to worm the names of the building's owners out of him, suggesting she had a lucrative business offer. Daryat steadfastly refused, but did agree to talk further in his office away from the music and the crowds.

They returned Daryat to life, Aquila drained away most of his Willpower, and Bast immediately got to work using her Fearsome Soul Affinity to break him completely. To their absolute bafflement, however, Daryat remained resolute and refused to give up any information at all. Outraged at his defiance, Bast drew her knife and began to carve apart his remaining hand before Aquila dragged her off the man. She rendered Daryat unconscious with a light touch and used her powers to heal his wounds, then angrily told the other Arisen that she would take responsibility for the prisoner. In return, she demanded that Bast never touch him again. Bast wasn't happy about it, but she backed down.

                                                            The Black Scorpion (2 dot Effigy)
                                                            Size 1, Durability 3, Structure 4

                                                            This is a small carved figurine of a scorpion, made from an unknown kind of black stone. Those who hold it find it warm to the touch, no matter what the temperature of the room might be. Everything within range takes 1L damage a turn as a consequence of their attacks.
