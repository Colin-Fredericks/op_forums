    Session 20

    "Let's see who's been paying attention."

    Akenakhi has reached Memory 5, so we ran a flashback for him. It seemed to be taking place during at the time he first arrived in the city, believed to be 1916/1917. Unlike most of his flashbacks, this one was particularly abstract and vague. He remembered an item, the Rod of Tjesh, that he had made in life and passed to another. He remembered walking through the city streets, but they were shrouded in mist and seemed to curve in on themselves. Everyone he spoke to was faceless and spoke in a seemingly androgynous murmur, their exact words a mystery. And always, in the distance, was a glow that he knew indicated the place of the Rod. He wandered aimlessly, until he heard a snarl.

    He was attacked by a hulking, cat-like creature with the head of a worm. Rings of teeth glinted in the dull light. It leapt at him, and he dove out of the way. It faded into the mist.

    Then he heard Aquila's voice calling. He stood torn. He heard her promise oblivion, safety, and a chance to mourn. But the glow of the relic beckoned stronger still. He chose the relic over safe oblivion. The vision faded, to be replaced with the sound of a storm. Waves as high as castle walls were accompanied by the sound of splintering wood. The relic had been lost at sea.

    We cut back then to the group on the verge of storming Manfred von Schuss's hideout. Aquila scouted out the area first, and alerted everyone that the basement walls were packed with corpses, probably for use as zombies. They figured that Manfred's corpse was likely his anchor, and that they could just go in via Dust Beneath Feet, drag it underground, and pulp it. Akenakhi and Reza waited outside while Aquila, Luca, and Sesu Ra went in underground.

    The plan didn't go as expected. Luca was detected trying to grab the corpse, and the ground seemed to come alive with grasping hands. He leapt out of the earth and triggered Blessed is the God King, transforming into the form of an immense bull-headed being just as the zombies surged from the walls. Sesu Ra joined his Sadikh a moment later and started to battle the zombies, while Aquila rolled her eyes and kept firmly underground.

    Akenakhi, meanwhile, felt the surge of Sekhem from the unleashing and charged inside, to be confronted by a group of four cultists. He unleashed the second tier of Revelations of Smoke and Flame, driving three of the cultists away in the grip of Terror Sybaris and inciting the remaining one to attack him. In the frenzied fighting that followed he nearly killed the man by flaying him with the Utterance's first tier. He struggled to decide between helping his meret mate and staying behind to treat the man, but ultimately decided that retrieving Aquila would give him better odds of keeping the man alive.

    The fight against the zombies was going slowly. The grasping hands fell inert, and Sesu Ra unleashed the first tier of Chthonic Dominion to reveal Twilight dwellers locked in combat. Sesu Ra's ghostly guardian was grappling with Manfred, a battle that mirrored the fight against the zombies. Knowing that the animate corpses wouldn't be slowed terribly by mere lethal damage, Sesu Ra and Luca both chose to fought with manifested weapons of light and darkness granted by their God King status. They drove poisoned rot into the flesh of their undead enemies as the ghostly battle raged on.

    Finally, Manfred gained the upper hand. He seemed about to overwhelm Sesu Ra's guardian when Sesu Ra turned away from the zombies he was fighting. The Arisen raised a hand and called on the third tier of Chthonic Dominion to strip away Manfred's Anchors. Manfred was an old, old ghost, almost impossibly hard to effect with the Utterance, but by a stroke of luck it worked. Anchors severed, Manfred vanished. The zombies crumpled the moment he was gone.

    Aquila and Akenakhi went upstairs, where Aquila used Healing Counsel to restore the injured man. Sesu Ra wandered across the road, after returning to his more mortal-seeming, to speak with the old man Jonah who had once been a follower of his. He told Jonah that the Families of the West would be moving into the area, and they expected his help in securing the architecturally important sites like the district's museum. Jonah was happy to help.

    The player cult went up a point of Reach from this acquisition, bringing them to Reach 2, Grasp 2.

    After the fight, the meret left to restore their pillars and rest. Aquila had noticed something was up with Rom, however. He hadn't been sleeping well, and there had been more than a few instances of strange behaviour. After a period of intense semi-interrogation on her part, she managed to persuade him to cough up some unpleasant details about his involvement in anti-Chinese race riots in his youth. He said he'd been dreaming about the event a lot recently, it seemed to be plaguing him. While more than a little discomforted by the knowledge that her hierophant was so prejudiced towards others, Aquila was also convinced that he was being literally haunted by a spectre from his past. She called in Sesu Ra for some help with the matter.

    The two Arisen spent the night with Rom, probing his room with the werelight from the first tier of Chthonic Dominion, but no spirits were in evidence. At last, as dawn broke, Rom woke screaming. Sesu Ra mulled over the situation a little, and pointed out that the symptoms could be explained by the use of Dreams of Dead Gods. Or perfectly mundane trauma, but the two Arisen dismissed that option more or less out of hand. They didn't know of any Mummy in the city with the Utterance in question, but Aquila was willing to venture a guess. There was one Mummy in Jakarta known to dwell in the ethnically Chinese sections of the city, who might have been wronged by Rom's actions in the past. Hetenatra, the Lady of the Broken Stele.

    When they gathered the next day, Akenakhi had exciting news that temporarily drove the matter from their minds. He'd located a shipwreck using Kepher, the resting place of a relic just off the coast of Jakarta and somewhere in the Thousand Isles. The group took a boat and headed out.

    They found the island, a large, uninhabited place covered in jungle, without too many difficulties. A bit of digging on the beach found the ship's figurehead buried in a mound of sand, but the rest of the ship was beneath the waves. Not much of an obstacle to undead beings. They went for a walk.

    That was when it became apparent that it was going to be a bit more difficult than a mere walk. A small group of reef sharks set upon them as the light began to fade. As the group fought them, they saw something huge approaching. Already in danger of being overwhelmed, they slipped beneath the sand one by one as Aquila passed on the blessing from Dust Beneath the Feet. Sesu Ra was the last to go, and saw what the approaching monstrosity actually looked like. It was a great swarm of fish, an impossibly great school of hundreds of different species. The host bore down on the group, but they were under the sand and away before they could be reached.

    They came to the shipwreck before long, guided by Akenakhi's Kepher. Locating the relic among the collapsing ruin was more difficult than expected however, especially when the fish school arrived. It swept through the shipwreck, battering the group into walls. They raced for what was left of the hold, and barred themselves in. Sesu Ra held the door shut against the fish, who were busy battering themselves senseless on the doorway, and Akenakhi found the relic lying in what remained of an old chest, long since rotted. The group smashed their way out of the shipwreck as the fish, joined by the sharks, came through the door. They slipped beneath the ground and got away, heading back to the island.

    When they arrived, they found crocodiles draped across their boat. A growl came from the jungle. A swarm of seabirds circled in the sky. As the animals closed in, they heard a voice speaking Iremite.

    "Who dares trespass?"

    Akenakhi had already slipped beneath the ground at the first sign of danger, and missed the words. Sesu Ra strode forward and declared himself, "the Unbending Spine, Guildmaster of the Tef-Aabhi." There was a pause, and the animals dispersed. Then, from the jungle, came a human figure and a large tiger.

    The woman looked like a pale Chinese woman in her early thirties, but it wasn't long before the group noticed the signs of unlife that marked her as a Mummy. She introduced herself as Nenheta, a hermit of the Su-Menent. The Rod of Tjesh was hers, she said, and they had no right to rob her.

    That was when Akenakhi emerged from the ground. The two stared at one another, stunned, for several long moments. Akenakhi recognised her, despite her changed features. A frenzied conversation in French followed, each expressing their amazement at seeing the other after so many centuries.

    Akenakhi made things different, Nenheta said addressing the group at large. The Rod was his, after all. The other two returned to Jakarta, saying they would send a cultist with a ship back to pick up Akenakhi. The two spoke of earlier times, and of their respective hunts for Apotheosis. Akenakhi shared that he was sure Yarikh knew something that they didn't, and Nenheta cautioned him. Su-Menent collecting information on Apotheosis could be dangerous, themselves notwithstanding. It was all too possible that he was using the information to seek out heretics, a fanatic ready to destroy them at a moment's notice.

    During this conversation, she revealed that her plan had been to live as a hermit as long as she could out in these islands. No other Arisen in Jakarta knew she was out here. She begged Akenakhi to not let his friends reveal her presence to any others.

    After he agreed, something seemed to startle her. She left with the words, "Goodbye my love. I could not be more pleased that you are here."

    Back on the mainland, Aquila was meditating again at the temple. She'd chosen to abandon her tomb near Sem-Huf's territory, planning on building a new one, but she didn't want to abandon the people to his rule. She'd heard of a woman who was leading some kind of resistance to the Sons, Sem-Huf's cultists, and resolved to meet her.

    A search of the slum led her to the doorstep of the old widow Wuldanri. Her grandson, a big, burly man named Pukta, answered the door. He didn't want to let her in, immediately suspicious of Aquila based on her look and fluent grasp of Indonesian, but she talked her way past him. Wuldanri, in turn, proved to be an old, hunched over woman who eyed Aquila with hostility from the very start. She didn't know what Aquila was, but she had no doubt that she was related in some way to the unnatural thing that lurked at the top of the tower the Sons called home.

    Aquila didn't deny this. Instead, she offered knowledge and help. She would provide funding, and weapons, to anyone Wuldanri could gather to fight this creature. She would even provide her own powers to heal the injured. She revealed that fire would help vanquish it. Meanwhile, the Sons could be broken by building new homes in the area, she thought, stealing away their grip on shelter and employment in the region. If they tried to fight back, automatic weapons and body armour could be made available to the locals.

    A good deal, Wuldanri called it. Ever since Pukta had returned to her after working for the Sons, telling stories of sorcerers and spirits, she had been afraid for her friends and family. For the first time, she had hope that she could vanquish the monster. With Aquila's backing, the Arisen would be driven from this corner of Jakarta...

        And for those interested, the following is the full write-up of the relic they discovered:

        The Rod of Tjesh (3 dot Uter)
        Size 3, Durability 3, Structure 6

        The Rod of Tjesh was wielded by those of the Su-Menent who needed to pass unnoticed. It appears to be a slim bone rod, about four feet long. Its head is wrapped in golden wire and topped with a stylised scarab. Beneath this ornamentation it is flat and featureless, except for the very bottom of the rod. It has been capped with mother of pearl, and impressed with a single hieroglyph that no one can read. Looking it it too long gives the unpleasant sensation that something is crawling behind one's eyes.

        Power: The Rod of Tjesh bores into the mind of the one that wields it. It feeds on everything that makes the wielder who they are, leaving them faceless, nameless, and almost impossible to notice. When its power ends, the wieldder recovers their identity... but not always entirely intact.
        Activating the Rod requires the user to forfeit all their current Willpower points. They must have at least one point to sacrifice, the Rod will not work for those who have nothing to give. So long as it is active, the bearer has no Virtue or Vice.
        In return, the bearer gains anonymity. They will pass unnoticed in any crowd. Guards will not notice them on their rounds. Sentries will let them pass unchallenged. This works only as long as another "group" is allowed in the area, a category of people outside of those assigned to protect it. Any guards will assume the bearer belongs to that group. Witnesses and magical beings are immune to this effect.
        The effect lasts one scene.

        Curse: At the end of the scene, the Storyteller rolls a Chance Die. On a success, the bearer regains a Willpower point. On a Failure, nothing happens. On a Dramatic Failure, the bearer loses a Willpower Dot.
        Su-Menent roll an ordinary die instead of a Chance Die.

            Session 21

            Aquila rose to Memory 5, and so began her next flashback. She was back in Ancient Rome, in a slave’s quarters, and discussing the upcoming rebellion with a young gladiator. For a moment, the version of her that was watching recognised the young man. She’d seen him spitted on Roman blades in one of her earlier flashbacks. Both of them were excited, tonight would be the night they slipped their chains.

            After the gladiator had left, Yarikh spoke from somewhere in the room behind her. He warned her that this was doomed to fail. Aquila shrugged. She had to try. With a heavy sigh, Yarikh told her that she knew where he stood, and left.

            The vision skipped forward. In the distance, Aquila could hear the escaped slaves praising Spartacus’s name. She was ambushed by a patrol of Roman soldiers, and unleashed her powers to drive them off with Sybaris. She was seen by a slave, however, who fled back to camp screaming of monsters and magic in the woods.

            The vision moved forward again. Alongside her followers, Aquila had been almost entirely ostracised from the slave army’s camp. Wherever she walked, she was regarded with suspicion and whispers of magic. The rumours were enough to see her seen as bad luck at best, a curse on the rebels at worst.

            Aquila’s mind returned to the present. She gritted her teeth. “This time will be different.”

            Meanwhile, Sekhet Bastet had been chewing her way through Gator’s cultists. After tracking down and killing another half-dozen, she finally ran out of leads. The only thing left to her were cryptic comments about a rot in Aquila’s heart, put there by Gator, and the corruption that would inevitably come.

            She decided to turn to the skies for help. Leaving the city, she performed Tier 3 of Secrets Ripped from Skies to ask for guidance. She received this message:

            “Three wolves circle the flock. The White Worm, the Gold Crocodile, and the Beast-Who-Is-Fire. They appear apart, but are together. Find one, find them all.”

            Concluding quickly that this referred to Gator as the Gold Crocodile, and that the others must thus by Shuankhsen, she went to Sesu Ra’s tomb to deliver this message to him and the rest of the meret. Sesu Ra greeted her happily, and handed her the Dragon’s Claw. The Uter, he said, was rightfully hers.

            They discussed what had happened since they last met. Bast shared her prophecy, and Sesu Ra stated somewhat cryptically that he thought he knew the identity of the Beast-Who-Is-Fire. As for the other, the White Worm, he didn’t know. The message was clear though. The city’s Shuankhsen were coordinating, working together.

            Then Bast brought up her concerns, beginning with Aquila. She wanted to know if anything strange had been happening to the other Mummy, anything that might indicate that she was slipping towards the Shuankhsen. Sesu Ra didn’t think so, however.

            And then it was Sesu Ra’s turn to ask the questions. Seeing as Nenheta had introduced herself as one of the Su-Menent, he asked Bast if she knew anything about her. At that, Bast froze and demanded to know where he had heard that name. The whole story of Akenakhi’s relic and Nenheta on the island came out quickly. Bast declared that they had to find the others, and they went to the Temple.

            Both Akenakhi and Aquila were in attendance, replenishing their pillars. Bast stormed in and demanded to know where Nenheta was. Akenakhi was quite upset. Nenheta had specifically asked that her presence go unknown, but Sesu Ra replied simply that he trusted Bast a helluva lot more than Nenheta. Or Akenakhi, for that matter.

            Sekhet Bastet decided she needed to meet with Nenheta. She wasn’t sure, herself, how reliable her own memory was. The news that Nenheta was apparently Twice-Arisen was concerning, but not completely damning in its own right. It was possible that she hadn’t been entirely corrupted in their last encounter, and that the other Su-Menent had been cured of the affliction by the fire Bast had wielded to end her.

            Sesu Ra offered to accompany her, and Akenakhi demanded that he come as well. Aquila chose to remain behind, wanting to visit Yama-Het instead.

            As the others set off, Aquila went to West Jakarta to speak with her friend. When she walked into Yama-Het’s private chambers, the other Mummy greeted her by name. She was sitting at a writing desk, her back to the door, and writing with a brilliantly coloured red and orange quill. Before Aquila could say anything, Yama-Het confessed that she was worried. There were too many attacks on the Arisen cults, too many strange magics at play. The gem market in East Jakarta had seen several cultist guards killed, and the potential relics therein were left entirely at the mercy of the perpetrator. No one could investigate, the police were refusing to allow anyone in. Mot-Tan and Sem-Huf’s cultists, responsible for the protection of the site, were forced to watch and wait.

            Aquila chose to add to these concerns. Her meret balanced on a knife edge, she said, and her hierophant was afflicted by a curse of dreams. She wanted to know how to remove it. Yama-Het’s advice was simply that a great show of will on his part would be the cure, or the use of an Utterance such as Rebuke the Vizier. Unfortunately, she lacked that particular knowledge herself.

            Out in the Thousand Isles, the other three Arisen were hauling their boat up and onto the land. They could see no sign of Nenheta, and so they entered the jungle. Once inside, Akenakhi got to work. The only one with any knowledge of tracking was Sekhet Bastet, but Akenakhi did his best to use lies and misdirection to prevent her from noticing the right way to go. The others put it down to incompetence, and it was ultimately unsuccessful. They made their way through the jungle until they came across a crude wooden hut.

            Nenheta emerged from the jungle. Bast stepped up to confront her, demanding to know if Nenheta remembered her. Nenheta looked from her to Akenakhi and back, and said in a broken voice, “I said I wanted to be left alone.”

            Akenakhi stepped up to her and put his hand on her shoulder. He glanced back at the rest of the meret. Then he put the Rod of Tjesh in her hands and told her, “Run.”

            Nenheta took off. Bast unleashed the second tier of Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, letting her slip through the cracks in reality. Akenakhi hurled himself at Bast. Sesu Ra whirled around and struck Akenakhi, throwing him to the ground. Bast gave chase, and Akenakhi and Sesu Ra grappled with another. When Sesu Ra unleashed Blessed is the God-King, Akenakhi conceded and slumped to the ground.

            Meanwhile, in the jungle, Bast dogged Nenheta’s heels. The other Mummy knew the terrain far better than her pursuer, however, and managed to slip out of sight. Having lost her quarry, a furious Bast returned to where Akenakhi and Sesu Ra were waiting.

            They explored the hut, and found it full of small animal bones. Food for the tiger, presumably. They demanded to know why Akenakhi had done what he did, and he said simply that he didn’t want to see his love killed by them. They protested that they just wanted to talk, but Akenakhi raised an eyebrow and pointed to their respective track records.

            When the pair escorted Akenakhi out of the forest, they found their boat was missing. Cursing in frustration, Sesu Ra called for a new one while Sekhet Bastet tried to intimidate Akenakhi into giving up Nenheta’s location. They knew that with Blessed Panoply, Akenakhi should have no problem tracking the Rod of Tjesh.

            When Akenakhi refused, Sekhet Bastet took matters into her own hands. A Kepher search located the relic, in Nenheta’s hands and descending into the bowels of an old Dutch fort. Sekhet Bastet interrogated Akenakhi, stripping away his willpower and breaking him, to milk him for all the information he had on Nenheta. All she really learned was that they had been together for thousands of years.

            The three, ferried by the cultist who had come out to retrieve them, returned to Jakarta. There Bast took off, hunting Nenheta. Sesu Ra and Akenakhi followed close behind. They came to the Dutch fort, and one by one they slipped past the security and descended into the fort’s tunnels. Bast reached Nenheta and her tiger before the others were even in sight.

            The brief conversation was bitter. Bast pointed out that, once, she and Nenheta had been friends. She had tried to save Nenheta from her fate. She just wanted answers to her questions, about Nenheta’s nature. Nenheta’s response was scathing. “You tried to save me? You failed.”

            The next few moments passed in a frenzy. Bast unleashed Ancestry of Forgotten Stars Tier 2. Nenheta and her tiger lunged forwards, grappling with Bast. Bast, the Dragon’s Claw in hand, nearly tore Nenheta in half. Sending her tiger away, Nenheta threw her head back and a many-toothed worm tore from her throat and dove into Bast’s flesh. It burrowed in deep, but not deep enough to save its creator. Bast pinned Nenheta’s arms down and waited. When she ran out of Pillars available to Seal the Flesh, Nenheta died.

            There was movement in the approaching passages. Sesu Ra and Akenakhi were approaching. Bast barely noticed though. To her absolute horror and disgust, the worm was inside her. She was infected. The Bane Affinity Wormheart had forced its way into her soul.

            The others arrived and demanded to know what had happened. Bast replied that she had found the third Shuankhsen, the White Worm. Akenakhi seemed to go into shock. He couldn’t believe it, didn’t want to believe it. He and Bast just stared down at the body on the floor, in silence.

            Sesu Ra, on the other hand, began to look around the tunnel. It was a dead end, but it didn’t take long before he found the switch at the base of the wall that opened a secret passage. He took one step inside… and stepped on a pressure plate. The resulting spears ran him through, but he sealed the flesh and continued on. Inside, he discovered a simple little tomb. The plain stone sarcophagus faced a wall where Iremite hieroglyphs were written in a cipher he and Bast recognised. It wasn’t long before he translated them, and read out the following pledge:

            “We three, by the source of the river’s curse, do swear on the Devourer’s Jaws that the works of Irem shall fail.”

            Bast and Sesu Ra waited there by the tomb in silence, to see if Nenheta returned. Akenakhi went back to his own tomb, having collected the Rod of Tjesh from Nenheta’s corpse. He poured his being into the item, taking his mind from events with the creation of a Talisman. On the other side of the city, Aquila showed up to her volunteer shift at a slum’s medical clinic, working through the night to keep mortal lives from fading.

            None of them felt like the dawn would bring anything better.

                Session 22

                Bad habits involving an upcoming election and arguing over politics sort of stopped us from making too much progress this session. Ah well, once we got distracted by the game it all went well.

                The day after their confrontation with Nenheta, the group pottered off to pursue their own interests. Sesu Ra eventually left Bast to guard Nenheta's tomb, and journeyed back to meditate. Aquila made the rounds among her cultists, sounding people out as they made the gradual transition into becoming an Enterprise cult. She spent a while speaking with the young Quil Mayehi, both about his lost father Richard and his education as a fisherman. Akenakhi... mostly just settled in for a long day of non-stop drinking. They each whiled away the day, before Aquila called them all together to meet at the temple the next morning, before dawn.

                That morning, they assembled there. They weren't the only ones. Kotira Nahan, Yarikh, Sem-Huf, and Yama-Het were all in attendance. Kotira was quite pleased by this apparent show of piety from every waking Mummy in the city, save for Sekhet Bastet and Mot-Tan. Bast's own piety wasn't really in doubt, and Mot-Tan had long been considered a lost cause.

                After the morning's prayers and meditations, the group began to talk. Sem-Huf and Aquila circled the gathering, staying on opposite sides of the crowd to one another. Yama-Het was truly perturbed, and extremely interested, in some of the stories about Shuankhsen that the group had encountered. Aquila quizzed Yarikh and Kotira about anyone they might know with the Rebuke the Vizier Utterance, so that she could have the curse from Dream of Dead Gods removed from Rom. Neither of them knew, however. Akenakhi and Sesu Ra explained most of the information they had gathered on the city's three Shuankhsen, but it seemed most of the others had been lucky enough to avoid any direct contact with the beasts. Aquila, still feeling the gnawing desire for human hearts, kept a low profile at the mutterings about "vile beasts" and the need to cleanse the city of their ilk.

                After the meeting, Aquila turned to Vania, the historian of their cult, for any information she might have on a Mummy with access to Rebuke the Vizier. Vania turned up a blank, although she did mention in passing that some documents might have been left behind on the island during LDI's attack. When Aquila wondered aloud how such a thing might be possible, Vania mentioned, a little reproachfully, that many documents had been hard to catalog because they had been written in languages Vania herself couldn't read. However, the conversation did quickly distract Aquila from that subject, as it turned out many of the older men of the cult were suffering similar nightmares to those suffered by Rom.

                Aquila gathered Sesu Ra and they set out for West Jakarta, the place they had heard was home to Hetenatra. The district was filled with examples of Chinese architecture, but it was also terrifically poor. It seemed clean and respectable at first, but the alleys were full of racist graffiti sporting anti-Chinese slurs and filled with rubbish and makeshift homes of the homeless. It was a place of desperation and no hope of improvement. They found, by asking a homeless man named Suharno who appeared to be suffering from polio, that there were a variety of places in the area regarded as supernatural. The old bridge was meant to be lucky, the community library was popularly considered spooky, many streets were thought to be haunted by the wandering ghosts of those killed in the Communist Party Massacre, and occasionally a mysteriously vigilante would kill Indonesian youths who had come to stir up trouble among the district's Chinese population.

                Eventually, they arrived at the community library. A wizened old man with thinning hair, a white moustache, and a cane introduced himself as Imeni, and almost immediately addressed them as Arisen. He asked if they had come to visit the Lady, and when they said they had he shuffled out the door and beckoned for them to follow. Carefully locking up the library behind him, he led them only a few doors down to a grocery store, where he introduced them to Yanar, the high priest of Hetenetra.

                Introductions took a little while. Yanar seemed awed, she had never seen an Arisen, while Imeni seemed a bit more at ease. When they were asked if they could raise Hetenatra so that the characters could speak with her, however, she got quite flustered. Eventually, Aquila coaxed the story out of her.

                Imeni, Yanar, and two others were the only ones left of Hetenatra's cult. Yanar had only been an infant when the Communist Party Massacre shattered the lives of Hetenetra's followers. Imeni helped her mother escape with her, along with a few others. None of them had been truly well versed in the ancient rites, however. They had only a few vague ideas of how to summon their Lady, and none of them had panned out. They had been told to never provoke the wrath from beyond the grave, so disturbing remains were out. And to cap it all, they had no relic or vestige by which to summon their mistress.

                The group was baffled. They had never seen an Arisen's followers reduced to such a state. Agreeing to help the broken cult, they resolved to begin a hunt for a suitable vessel to restore the Lady of the Broken Stele to life.

                    Session 23

                                                        Aquila was now down to Sekhem 3.

                                                        She was then invited to join Mike Thornton in his office. Aquila posited that the two of them were not so different, that LDI's generous works in places like Africa replicated the kind of results that she herself wanted to achieve. She just wished that the group would forego their barbarous ways. In response, Mike pointed out the shocking bloodbath caused by the Arisen assault on LDI's corporate headquarters. Most Arisen were immortal brutes, he said, rushing through their lives with no thought for the mortals about them, their only thoughts for magical artefacts that were not theirs to steal anyway. They acted with violence and attitudes appropriate to ages long past. LDI couldn't deal with such monsters, they had to subdue them with force so that the magic within them could be channelled to produce products capable of healing even the most dire of diseases. And in the end, all of LDI's actions represented a mere minor inconvenience to the Arisen, a slight hastening of a Descent that caused no permanent harm to the Arisen.

                                                        After some discussion, Aquila said she hoped a more peaceful arrangement could be made with LDI. She would reach out to the Arisen of the city, trying to persuade them to stop working against the group. She hoped to even rally some volunteers to offer their Sekhem, in return for the material aid from the company. But, as a show of good faith, she expected Mike to release any Arisen he currently held captive, as well as any relics he might currently be holding. Mike said it wasn't his decision to make, and in any case he needed an exceedingly strong demonstration that the Arisen would cease their violence before he undertook any such actions. He didn't want to be the target of a revenge-fuelled killing spree so soon after the last one.

                                                        Then, as agreed, Aquila departed with a written guarantee that LDI would support her efforts in Jakarta's slums. She was blindfolded on the way out, but she made her way back to the Temple to meet with her meret without any major difficulties. She then decided not to divulge what had happened, merely saying that she had been drained of some of her power and then criticising the group for letting her get taken so easily.

                                                        Sekhet Bastet had spent the last twelve hours watching over their trapped Amkhat. In the final hours before morning, it began to fade. Cursing her in Ammut's name, it swore to her that Ammut's triumph was inevitable. No matter how many centuries it took, the Devourer would claim her. And then it died, collapsing into piles of rotting animal flesh... and the skeleton of a young child.

                                                        The meret decided to chase down Akenakhi's missing canopic jars. Blessed Panoply led them out to the Thousand Islands, straight back to Nenheta's island. Sesu Ra activated Model Lifeweb to reveal anything that was pretending to be something it wasn't, and as Akenakhi led them through the jungle Sesu Ra's vision detected a tree stump that was truly a hiding spot for valuables. Tearing it apart, he revealed both Akenakhi's canopic jar and a book entitled "Sutek's Ladder". Everyone present recognised the title as an allusion to Apotheosis, and Akenakhi snatched it up. He had brief, scattered visions that this book should have been lost while being transported to Batavia, back when he was following Nenheta's trail. The book was a chronicle of Akenakhi and Nenheta's joint efforts to pursue Apotheosis. The others, not knowing the contents, said that they wished to read the book the moment Akenakhi was finished.

                                                        Frustrated at the lack of any Shuankhsen to fight, Sekhet Bastet used Secrets Ripped From Skies Tier 3 to ask for guidance. She received the following riddle:

                                                        Beneath the floor of the Subtle Bazaar, the husks of souls who once were prey upon the sons of gold and the bearers of flame in the night. Shadows cloud glory and blind eyes until only foes remain.

                                                        (Note: I had to make this up on the spot, so forgive its general clunkiness)

                                                        Sharing this with the meret, Aquila mentioned that the Subtle Bazaar was a Mesen-Nebu concept and Sesu Ra pointed out that the Sons of Gold Dust were Sem-Huf's followers. They promptly decided to pay Sem-Huf a visit.

                                                        And we finished there. With the experience from the session, Akenakhi bought his Memory to 7.

                                                            Session 31

                                                            Akenakhi retreated to his tomb to examine the words of "Sutek's Ladder", the book he had written about his quest for Apotheosis. He turned to the opening page, and found the following prayer inside:

                                                            I pay homage to thee, O divine ladder. Homage to thee, O Ladder of Sutek.

                                                            Stand thou upright, O divine ladder, whereby Azar came forth to the Light Land.

                                                            For I am thy son, Azar of Old, and thou shall give to me the Ladder of Sutek.

                                                            I need plow no Earth, nor collect any offering, for that which I see and hear feed and nourish me when I appear in the Light Land by the Ladder of Sutek.

                                                            I have gathered together my bones, I have collected my flesh, and I shall go quickly to the Light Land by the Two Fingers of great Sutek, god of the ladder.

                                                            As he read the words, his vision began to swim. He remembered the streets of Constantinople, back during the Third Sothic Turn. The ravages of the Plague of Justinian. He was on his way to a gathering of local Arisen... but something changed. This wasn't a normal memory. He wasn't a passive observer inside his own skull. He was here. He could remember the future, Jakarta, the fight against the Shuankhsen that had plagued most of his fourth Sothic Turn. As his memory cleared, he realised he could remember many of the Descents between now and then, nearly every Descent he had ever had was open to him. The long course of history, his long quest for freedom, it was all clear. And he was here, in the past, in the streets of Constantinople.

                                                            A cultist by his side, a well-dressed young man who he knew was the son of his high priest, asked if anything was wrong. Akenakhi avoided the question, and instead had them continue onwards to the gathering. He recalled that there were rumours of a time when the sky had been darkened and the reach of the Judges had been kept from this world. He had been in henet during this period, but he hoped to speak to Arisen who had been active to see if there were any clues he could ascertain about the time.

                                                            They walk through streets largely devoid of travellers. The bustling streets have been cleared, and those mortals they see hurry past or cross the street to avoid them. The plague has made everyone wary of contact with strangers. Akenakhi recalls that the Su-Menent had a relic at this time which had been shared among the guild's cults, his own included, to protect them from the sickness.

                                                            The pair arrived at the estate of a wealthy landowner. The mortal cultists were hosting a party among those of Constantinople's elite willing to risk such a large social gathering, but Akenakhi was shown down to the wine cellar by a poor, deformed servant who hissed that he should take a torch, head to the back, and press on the ibis to reveal the secret chamber. Sure enough, at the back of the chamber there was a mosaic depicting the pagan gods of Egypt, and a stylised ibis to represent Thoth. When it swung back, Akenakhi entered the hidden chamber where the other Arisen were gathered.

                                                            Including himself, there were five others in attendance. Mekshonat, the Seal of Babylon, was a highly placed Maa-Kep and the host of the evening's gathering. His sister, Benerib the Penitent Falcon, was a Flail Bearer and warrior who kept to her brother's side at all times. Kor-Et-Udar, He of the Consecrated Flesh, was a badly scarred Sesha-Hebsu in close discussion with Ikapushet, Re's Hunger, a golden skinned Mesen-Nebu. Finally, at the back of the chamber, a mysterious Arisen known to the others only as the Tribune seemed content to watch from afar.

                                                            Akenakhi chose to mingle. He knew from the rumours he had heard that Kor-Et-Udar and Mekshonat had been active during the time of darkness. He was able to lure some information out of Kor-Et-Udar by suggesting that the Emperor Justinian could have been involved. Kor-Et-Udar said that all information indicated that a calamity in the east was responsible, but that none of the searchers his guild had sent had returned.

                                                            Then they heard the grinding stone that meant someone else had entered the chamber, and Sekhet Bastet walked in. She announced that she had run her quarry to ground and destroyed it, and Akenakhi recalled that a priest of some note had ordered Nenheta woken to pursue a dangerous quarry before he was woken by his own Sothic Turn. That must have been Sekhet Bastet. He offered her a glass of wine, which she quietly accepted but did not drink. Any attempts to lure out the location of Nenheta were met with cold silence, but with the knowledge of the future in his mind Akenakhi was quite sure he knew what had happened.

                                                            As the party began to separate and leave, Akenakhi had the sudden realisation that he could recall every relic he had ever made in his mortal life. He knew, with equal certainty, that when he had first lived these events his Memory had not been clear enough to do so. His kepher sought out the relics. Most of his arts had been destroyed, consumed, or sent to Duat in the intervening years, but three still remained. The Rod of Tjesh, even now kept in the tomb he shared with Nenheta. The Withering Venom, currently somewhere in south Gaul. And the Serpent Named, in this very city. His kepher senses told him to seek out a ship's captain named Clodius for more information.

                                                            But before he left, he took Mekshonat to one side and told the Maa-Kep about the Su-Menent's relic, and the cure to the plague it represented. He said his conscience had been weighing him down so far, but that he could no longer countenance the suffering of other guilds' cultists to protect his own guild's secrets. When asked where it was now, he speculated that Sekhet Bastet, as an active Su-Menent, could well be the one currently keeping it. Mekshonat gravely promised to investigate the allegations.

                                                            Akenakhi knew that, when he had first lived these events, he had pursued Sekhet Bastet to find out more about Nenheta's fate. But he knew that now, as he knew of the existence of the relic known as the Serpent Named. He hurried towards the docks, ordering his cultists to assemble some warriors and follow him. They were able to locate Clodius as his men, preparing a ship to sail. Although the captain seemed initially reluctant to tell what he knew, Akenakhi spotted that one of his men was beginning to show symptoms of the plague. In return for ridding his ship of the disease, Clodius pointed out a strange galley at the end of the docks. A stranger from another land, a man much like Akenakhi himself, had been taking aboard plague victims with the claim that he could take them to a medicine man who could cure them. The ship was crawling with the diseased.

                                                            Akenakhi and his men hurried towards the galley and boarded it. The putrefying smell emerging from the ship's hold almost overwhelmed the mortals, and as Akenakhi crossed the threshold to the stairwell, a surge of Sybaris sent his mortal followers fleeing into the night.

                                                            The hold was lit by torches, illuminating the piles of bodies. Slight movement and groans indicated that some among the piles were still alive, but telling the blank and staring gazes of the living and dead apart was nearly impossible. At the back of the hold stood the Tribune, who ordered Akenakhi to begone. "You shouldn't be here... you were never here," he said, sounding more than a little confused.

                                                            Akenakhi said that he was here for the Serpent Named, and the Tribune removed the relic from the folds of his robes. It was a ceremonial flail, its haft wrapped around with a mummified snake. He couldn't take it, however. The Tribune said he had need of it still, to help those suffering from the plague. Spirits began to emerge from the shadows as he spoke, creatures of broken bone and torn corpus, the ghosts of the dead and dying. He had failed to heal their bodies, but he could at least remove their souls from beyond the corruption of the disease. And once his work was done, he would return the relic to his Judge.

                                                            Obviously, that didn't meet with Akenakhi's hopes. He persuaded the Tribune that the Su-Menent had a relic that could cure the plague. If they worked together, they could retrieve it for him. In return, Akenakhi just wanted the Serpent Named. The Tribune agreed, and the pair headed immediately for the neighbourhood where Sekhet Bastet's tomb was kept.

                                                            The neighbourhood was on fire when they arrived. Dead mortals dotted the streets, badly burned. They found Sekhet Bastet protecting a building near the centre of the district, armed with spear and shield and surrounded by the bodies of the dead. Akenakhi demanded that she allow the Tribune to enter and use the relic, but she replied that the First Prophet had ordered that only the Su-Menent should be allowed access. When Akenakhi suggested that the First Prophet be ignored, she attacked. Akenakhi threw himself onto her in an effort to restrain her, but she called on the Secrets Ripped From Skies to strike all three of them with fire from the heavens. The Tribune was hurled back into a building, body shattered, and struck a wall. In that moment the Serpent Named fell from his hands, and the relic mask he wore fell from his face. And Akenakhi realised that the Tribune was Aquila.

                                                            Nearly consumed by the fire, Akenakhi sued for peace. Sekhet Bastet seemed torn as to whether she should accept or not, when the sound of stone breaking came from the building behind her. Without another word, she whirled around and charged inside.

                                                            Akenakhi hastily gathered up the relics and ran. He made his way back to his tomb. As he entered, his vision swam, and he found himself back in the fourth Sothic Turn, seated at the table where he had been reading Sutek's Ladder. Except now, in his hands, he held the relics he had stolen from the past.

                                                            On the other side of the city, Sesu Ra and Sekhet Bastet arrived at Sem-Huf's tomb to find a mob brawling with his cultists in the streets. There were brief glimpses of men with automatic weapons, but for the moment the fighting appeared to be non-lethal. The Arisen pushed their way through the mob, found a cultist to speak to, and ascertained that Sem-Huf was in his tower putting out fires that had been started. They headed there, bulled there way past the guards at the building who quickly caved to pressure from the Arisen, and walked up to speak to Sem-Huf.

                                                            The Mesen-Nebu was wielding the Power of Re to put out fires in danger of consuming his estate. He told them that the fighting had started only a short time ago, apparently provoked by the deaths of some insignificant locals. They had blamed Sem-Huf for the deaths, although he'd had nothing to do with it, and attacked his tomb. His cultists had forced them back. Sesu Ra and Sekhet Bastet offered to finish off the mortal problem for him, and he accepted the offer gladly.

                                                            With Luca by their side, the two Mummies walked out to where the two mobs faced one another. Sekhet Bastet smiled at Sem-Huf's cultists, and in a quiet voice told them to run and not look back. As the cultists fled, Sekhet Bastet struck the largest concentration of attackers with Secrets Ripped From Skies Tier 2. The Sybaris left nearly everyone who wasn't struck dead by the meteor begging for their lives, and they quickly crushed the last bit of resistance. Then they felt Sekhem flare in the tower behind them, someone unleashing an Epic Utterance. They left Luca to guard the prisoners and raced back to Sem-Huf's side. They passed the guards, torn apart by the teeth of a Shuankhsen, and raced into the chamber in time to witness Gator consuming the last of Sem-Huf's body.

                                                            Sesu Ra unleashed Blessed is the God-King, Sekhet Bastet unleashed Ancestry of Forgotten Stars Tier 2, and Gator unleashed Dust Beneath Feet Tier 2. That took the Arisen off-guard for a moment, as it wasn't something they'd ever seen the Shuankhsen do before. As they squared off, the Shuankhsen demanded to know where Emerset could be found. Sesu Ra snarled out "Silence beast, and die like a dog," and the two giants slammed into one another.

                                                            Sesu Ra fought with a khopesh made of light and shadow, while Gator had only his teeth and stone form. He tore massive chunks out of the larger Mummy, before Bast distracted him by digging her relic knife into his back. Given an opening, Sesu Ra landed several powerful blows with his blade, unleashing a mystic rot that ravaged Gator's form. Although he severely injured the two Arisen, Sesu Ra's attacks were too much. He said to tell Emerset he would be coming for her, and walked through the nearest wall to escape.

                                                            The Mummies went back to their prisoners. Bast discovered both the location of the original killing, and the fact that their weapons and the knowledge that fire would harm the Arisen had come from an old local woman named Wuldanri. However, this attack had been a spur of the moment thing organised without her consent, a furious retaliation by the men of the slum against the deaths of their own. Bast left to speak to Wuldanri, and Sesu Ra and Luca executed the prisoners and left to fine the scene of the murders.

                                                            Bast found Wuldanri's home guarded by a giant, heavily muscled man. When he refused to let her past, she unleashed Terror Sybaris and left him begging for his life on the ground. She walked in to confront Wuldanri, who demanded to know what she had done to her son. "Put him on the ground, where he belongs," Bast said, before beginning the interrogation. Wuldanri was hard to break, but Bast's Fearsome Soul eventually broke her and she extracted Aquila's description and a fairly detailed knowledge of their intended plans for the district. That done, Bast spoke Wuldanri's name into the Pale Brood and left.

                                                            Sesu Ra and Luca, meanwhile, had found the site of the original killings. Someone had written the words "No one is safe" in the blood, using the hieroglyphs of Ancient Irem. It mimicked the site of Mot-Tan's death directly, suggesting that the killings might be connected. Luca did find that the "handwriting" was different, but they couldn't definitively determine anything from that. The style certainly seemed to indicate someone who had used Iremite in life, rather than a cultist who had discovered it at a later date, however.

                                                            The meret assembled at the city Temple, where Aquila was waiting. Akenakhi had come to tell the others about his strange voyages through time, but he was pushed to the side by Sesu Ra and Sekhet Bastet who demanded a full explanation of Aquila's actions. She had set mortals against another of her guild, and allowed a Shuankhsen to consume one of the Arisen. Combined with the fact that she had refused to tell them much about what had happened when LDI kidnapped her, they were extremely suspicious. Akenakhi handed Aquila her mask, a one dot relic which mystically shielded the bearer's identity while worn, and the sight provoked a brief flash of memory in Bast who recalled fighting someone wearing that mask. That didn't help soothe tensions either.

                                                            The argument raged for a while, Bast and Aquila hurling furious accusations at one another. Bast had no real evidence for her suspicions, and Sesu Ra seemed inclined to put the events down to Aquila's incompetence, which angered Aquila even more. Eventually, Sesu Ra grew sick of watching and walked out. Bast followed a little bit later, warning Aquila not to slip again. Aquila spat out that she was a Guildmaster of the Mesen-Nebu, and not accountable to either the Tef-Aabhi or the Su-Menent.

                                                            Once alone with Akenakhi, the two swapped their stories in full. Akenakhi elaborated on his tale of being cast into the past, and told her the story of Constantinople and what had transpired there. Aquila shared what had happened when she had been taken by LDI, and how she thought they could align their interests with that of the organisation to break the hold of the more brutal Arisen on the city. Akenakhi, always happy to cause trouble to the guilds, agreed that it seemed like a good course of action. They didn't want to see any more of the kind of bloodshed their own meret brought in their wake.
