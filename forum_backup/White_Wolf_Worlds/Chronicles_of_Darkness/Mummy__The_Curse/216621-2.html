    Yeah, only a Status 4 Su-Menent could pull it off (Sekhet Bastet could buy the Utterance, but she ultimately decided it didn't suit a character who actually wanted to be spending time in henet). With a Talisman you could be Sekhem 8 pretty easily, since you descend as if you were Sekhem 7.

    One minor thing though. If you have a Talisman , you start the descent at Sekhem 9 instead of 10. You put a permanent sliver of your power into it. Any Mummy who loses a Talisman is going to be permanently disadvantaged, but also have a permanent connection to that Talisman. Hence why it's very, very hard to ever get your hands on more than one. You can only ever make one, the others have to be stolen from people highly capable and motivated at getting it back. It's possible (see Sabola in Cursed Necropolis D.C.), but exceptionally difficult.

        Speaking of Sekhet Bastet...

        Interlude 1

        In spite of Bast's player being out of the country, we've been staying in contact and working through some things Sekhet Bastet has been up to since that departure. We recently concluded a flashback sequence.

        Bast found herself on a rainy island, carrying a spear and shield and hunting some kind of unspecified quarry. She was there alongside a fellow Su-Menent, a woman named Nenheta. After some discussion, they decided that Bast would explore some nearby caverns near the ocean, while Nenheta and her leopard Beast Companion explored the abandoned village that sat upon a nearby hill.

        Exploring the caverns took a while. The rain hadn't let up in more than a week, and Bast found that the caverns actually connected to the ocean. It was high tide, and a lot of the area was flooded. Not a big problem for a Mummy, mind you. The darkness underwater was more of an issue than any other potential problem, like not being able to breathe. As she delved deeper into the caverns she discovered a place with a palpable aura of occult magic, and then was attacked by a Chthonic Being. The ensuing fight was long and drawn out, as Bast and the creature burned through nearly all their power regenerating damage. When Bast finally struck the killing blow, she only had one Pillar left (and couldn't use it to seal the flesh, as she'd already spent one from that pillar earlier), and only two points of health not filled with Aggravated damage. Those two points were filled with lethal instead. On the Chthonic Being's part, it had been forced to use every spot of Essence it had, but ultimately it just wasn't quite enough to stop Bast from killing it.

        Exhausted and drained, Bast retreated to the cavern's entrance. A bit of exploration in the upper levels discovered a cache containing several vestiges. To the present day Bast's distaste, her past self drained one of the vestiges to regenerate a few Pillar points. She decided to rest for several hours to recover from the fight, and then set off to try and find Nenheta. The sun was setting at this point, and the fellow Su-Menent was not at the agreed upon meeting place. Bast trekked across the island and reached the village. In the middle of the street she found the gutted remains of Nenheta's leopard, and saw a bonfire burning in the distance. She reached it, and found herself on the steps leading to the temple where the mortal's worshipped their false god. She lit the tip of her spear on fire and went inside, and found a sobbing Nenheta within.

        The scent of Bast's Sekhem drove Nenheta into a frenzy. She attacked Sekhet Bastet, who realised her fellow Su-Menent had become a Shuankhsen. Their quarry had trapped her and turned her. Luckily for the badly injured Bast, the process had not left Nenheta in a good state either. The very first blow "killed" Nenheta, and then it was just a matter of beating her down into the ground repeatedly until she could no longer Seal the Flesh.

        The memory ended with Bast staring at the crumpled, burning corpse of her ally. When she woke in the present, she decided the vision was a sign. The Shuankhsen must be at large in Jakarta, and she was going to find them.

            Session 11

            Aquila's latest Death Cycle took her back to a gully surrounded by injured soldiers and damaged equipment. She was doing her best to care for the men, when a messenger arrived to announce that Yarikh's men were coming. He had only just got the words out when an arrow struck him and a host of armoured soldiers charged down the slopes to overwhelm Aquila's men. She found that in this prior life she didn't know the Dust Beneath Feet Utterance, but that she was familiar with Harvest the Divine Flesh. In the end, Aquila managed to kill one of her attacks before the rest bore her to the ground. Alongside most of her soldiers, she was bound and led away in chains.

            It took four days for Aquila to complete that Death Cycle. In the intervening time Sesu Ra and Luca managed to map out the everyday routine of Banek, the leader of the death cult in the Old Dutch Quarter. Luca was dispatched to kidnap him, and the next day told his master that Banek was locked away in the increasingly permanent holding pens on their cultists' island.

            Akenakhi spent the time recovering his pillars following the encounter with the Greater Amkhat, and drinking a lot at Darnton's.

            After Aquila returned to life she sought out and gathered her meret. They decided they needed more information on the larger breed of Amkhata before they tried to hunt it down and figure out what it was doing in a bank vault, so they sent cultists to request Yarikh meet the lot of them at the Temple. They ended up meeting just before the Su-Menent dawn service. Yarikh told them that Greater Amkhata were made, not born from broken relics, in a perverse ritual that required access to Sekhem and liberal amounts of human blood and animal body parts. Sesu Ra immediately suspected a fellow Arisen, pointing out that they had the easiest access to the Sekhem requirement. Yarikh suggested he speak to Kotira instead, since she knew of matters he was not at liberty to discuss himself.

            Aquila then dragged Yarikh off to one side to discuss her Death Cycle. After being a little cagey for a while, Yarikh added a few details to the story. They'd been fighting in the Third Servile War, side by side, each of them moved by their empathy for the slaves at the time. As the tide turned against the rebellion, Aquila and her followers were cornered. Yarikh was marching as fast as he could to help her, but by the time he arrived she was gone and most of her men were dead.

            The group approached Kotira after the morning prayers, and she revealed that there had been a marked increase in Greater Amkhata sightings in Jakarta in the last two decades. She had been trying to get to the root of the problem on and off, but the revenants she created for that purpose kept killing the leads they discovered. The men and women they killed had been employed at a variety of different multinational corporations, but she hadn't been able to draw a convincing link between them to give herself a single suspect. What she did know was that after each assassination, a cultist of hers would be slain. She had enough evidence to suggest it was the Amkhata doing this each time.

            When asked about why such a creature might be found in a bank vault, Kotira revealed that she suspected whoever was behind it might be able to detect the use of Utterances and other obvious manifestations of Sekhem. She didn't know if it was something magical in nature or not, but she did know that the coverage was not total and that it instead tended to be around locations where an Arisen might logically find something of interest. A bank vault would be a prime example.

            The party then decided to rob the bank again. This time they did things a lot more simply. Aquila put Dust Beneath Feet on all of them, they burrowed into the vault around midnight, cut off the power and turned the gold into an enormous diamond for transport. They then fled as fast as they could, without spotting any sign of trouble. Luca drove them away, and thanks to a multitude of poor Wits + Composure rolls they were unaware that they were being followed.

            When they stopped at a desert part of the docks they were attacked by the black armoured men Akenakhi had seen during the last robbery attempt. Aquila nearly died again, she was still badly damaged from her most recent death, and had to flee into the ocean with only one health not filled with aggravated damage. The ghost from Akenakhi's tomb obeyed Sesu Ra's standing command to protect him, and possessed one of the attackers and turned him on the others. The soldiers began to retreat, but Luca and Sesu Ra ran them down. One man fired a flare gun into Sesu Ra's stomach. Two attackers escaped in the end, one was left in the meret's hands but on the verge of death, and the other three died. We ended with the group saving the life of their new prisoner just before he bled out from his wounds.

            It was one of those sessions where a decent amount of stuff actually happened, but it doesn't look like much when I type it up like this. We had a lot of long roleplaying discussions, and then the fight against the men in black dragged on for quite a while. It's left the group pretty beat up though, and the trap Sesu Ra was setting for the Pelamat is about to spring, so it'll be interesting to see how they decide to run things next time.

                Session 12

                In the aftermath of their fight, Akenakhi and Sesu Ra bundled their new captive aboard a stolen boat, along with the corpses of the dead men. Aquila hunted through a fish gutting station near the docks, found a suitable bowl and water, and absorbed the wealth of the gold she'd stolen through an alchemical mixture to grant herself Resources 5. As her meret mates headed inland, Aquila went to check out the gem market. At this point it was very early morning, and the place was still closed. Frustrated, she realised that a cheque made out using her new resources would actually serve to transmute back into gold to replace that which she'd stolen. She hurried back to the bank, waited for it to open, went through a rather lengthy procedure to have a cheque written for 5 Resources worth of wealth. She negotiated the hurdles easily enough, withdrew the money from the large account she suddenly had (or had always had) with the bank, travelled a short distance to transmute it into gold, and then placed it in the vault. The bank would likely find some issues with the video camera later, namely the discovery that dirt had somehow gotten into its wiring. With any luck, she hoped, that would be enough to stop the theft being noticed.

                Sesu Ra had the corpses embalmed, so that he could use them with his Blasphemous Depiction later, and had Luca transport one of them to his tomb. He then went to the graveyard outside of Sekhet Bastet's tomb, dominated one of the ghosts there, and took it back with him (using Chthonic Dominion to turn himself into its anchor). He then created a proto-mummy creature with the task to defend his infrastructure in Yama-Het's territory, and the whole group proceeded to rest for a few days. Aquila camped out in the Temple, the others mostly stayed in their tombs.

                Akenakhi had a brief conversation with their new, impassive captive. The man looked Caucasian, was fit and well-built, and remained completely silent in the face of questioning. Conceding that he wasn't all that good at milking information from suspects, Akenakhi went and joined Sesu Ra and Luca in staking out the new projects that were being started in West Jakarta. Not too much time later, three Pelamat attacked. The fight was brief, the group drove past just long enough to hurl molotovs at the group and at the scaffolding put up in preparations for modifications to an existing building's facade, then drove away. As they were fleeing though, Akenakhi used Obedient Clay to turn one of their motorbikes into liquid. They grabbed the man and hustled him off to Yama-Het's community centre for interrogation. The Sesha-Hebsu Guildmaster was not in attendance, but the cultists let them take a room upstairs.

                After some interrogation from Luca, the man revealed he'd been sent by Rangarni. The compartmentalised nature of the Pelamat continued to frustrate the players, as he didn't have any information about how to track down the leader. They did find that the guy had a phone with Rangarni's phone number, which could prove useful. And they did learn that the Pelamat were apparently purchasing real estate in South Jakarta. After that, Sesu Ra killed him and earned yet another Descent Reset. This time though, putting his faith in Set-Qesu was not enough. He dropped to Memory 2 and gained the False Incarnation derangement (we wanted to play around with one of the new Mummy-specific derangements).

                This session was really quite short, so we had to end there. I was feeling pretty wrecked, and I think most of the others were kind of out of it as well. Hopefully things will have improved next week, but it still had a few fun scenes. Aquila healed enough so that she now "only" has five aggravated damage, and Akenakhi dropped to Sekhem 4.

                                                Yeah, sure. Akenakhi and Aquila are both relic-less, although Aquila knows the location of a relic sword that was once hers.

                                                Sesu Ra has two relics. One, filling the "from Ancient Irem" slot in his lifeweb, is the Blasphemous Depiction. As I mentioned earlier, it allows the wielder to perform a mummification ritual on a corpse, bind a ghost into the body, and create an Abmortal with a single driving Purpose. Once the Purpose is complete the Abmortal stops healing damage. As a rule the creature acts like a Memory 0 Mummy, defaulting to violence to solve its problems and largely bereft of personality. I do love the Depiction's rules in the core book and the options it gives you. You could have it make vampires, sin-eaters, prometheans, revenants, restore humans to life, Shuankhsen, generic zombies, or whatever you want your chronicle to have. It's a brilliant piece of work. And as Sesu Ra's player pointed out, once you're suffering from the curse you could quite easily use the thing as a weapon. Your curse can't get worse, and it could be fun to inflict Schizophrenia and Irrationality on your enemies.

                                                The other, in his lifeweb under the "Any number of relics made by your own hand" category, is a three-storey obelisk called the Strength of Anut. Anyone who deals with him while he is in the "seat" of his power treats him as though he were Status 5 in an appropriate organisation. It's incorporated into the structure of his tomb, and its effects count the entire skyscraper as the seat of his power. He has turned the obelisk into a Talisman with two Manifestation dots in Blessed is the God King and one in Chthonic Dominion.

                                                Sekhet Bastet has the Pale Brood uter, taking up the "from Ancient Irem" slot in her lifeweb. She has also turned it into a Talisman, containing one Manifestation dot of Secrets Ripped from Skies, one dot from Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, and two dots from Jar of Teeth. She also has a vestige (Hope and Justice fetters, I think) in the form of the cowboy hat being worn by one of the Faithful leaders.

                                                As for Aquila, it turns out getting killed is a very efficient way to speed through a Descent. She could reclaim her Sekhem point if she could track down and kill the Amkhat that stole it, unless the creature's master has already used the Sekhem for some other nefarious purpose. If it is gone, and she does decide to try and make a Talisman, she's going to have to drain a relic or something to boost her Sekhem back up a little.

                                                    Session 13

                                                    This session started out with a bit of confusion. Sesu Ra and Akenakhi tried to track down Yama-Het's missing cultist, Ari, but weren't sure how to go about it. They ultimately ended up heading to Ari's apartment, where they'd heard he was kidnapped from. One neighbour suggested that she heard a visitor of his say words that sounded like "taken to the country". So they reported back, asking if Yama-Het knew whether any of the local Preman owned land out in the countryside. They did some research, digging through all the information Yama-Het's cult could gather while trying to ignore the party going on in the downstairs room of the community centre. Akenakhi narrowed the field down through Muhammad's lieutenants to find a man named Awei Mohallem Wasi, who seemed to be the most likely culprit to actually have committed the kidnapping. More cultists were dispatched to find out if he had a house outside Jakarta.

                                                    After that, they toyed with the idea of bribing some police to try and track down the Pelamat, but ultimately decided against it (informed by the fact that Aquila was the only one with money to spend). Instead, they returned to the island to interrogate the man they captured from the soldiers who attacked them. They found him inside a hut, strung up and being beaten by a younger cultist under the supervision of Pama Lehn. Apparently, the man had tried to escape. Sending the cultists outside of the room, Sesu Ra settled in for several hours of near-torture and interrogation.

                                                    Finally, they got some details. The man worked for "L41Secure", a security firm owned by a pharmaceutical giant called LDI. They were an acquisitions team sent out to capture "targets", powerful immortal beings for study in LDI labs, but apparently surveillance had screwed up big time on this op. They hadn't realised there were three priority targets all working together, and had come expecting only a single supernatural foe. They found out that he had used to work for a PMC, before being recruited. He'd taken part in two previous acquisition missions, one in Afghanistan and another in Peru. The players were outraged, and were quick to make sure that he had no idea who the Faithful were.

                                                    As they went to leave, however, the man mentioned that the full weight of LDI and its security forces would come down on the group. He seemed confident that they would have tracked down the cult, and that the villagers here would soon be massacred.

                                                    The group's preparations for conflict lasted the rest of the session. Three days passed in which Akenakhi had cultists dig trenches and foxholes. He seemed a little confused by the suggestion that World War I tactics were no longer applicable. He did have a brief flashback to the Great War in the process, earning him a point of Sebayt experience.

                                                    The others took it in shifts to go back to the mainland to mediate at their tomb (or the temple in Aquila's case), while debating a lot over how best to prepare for a fight. Aquila set Resa to track down one of his smuggling contacts, a man called Kudava Jav, to obtain a set of riot gear. A few arguments were had about the viability of explosives, of turning the entire cult into zombie-monsters using the Blasphemous Depiction and the small host of ghosts outside of Bast's tomb, and whether it would just be simpler to move to a different island. Ultimately, women and children were evacuated to the smuggler island Resa had once used as his base. Many, many ideas were offered up, and this sequence easily took far more time than anything else this session. In the end Sesu Ra did create a zombie using a body he'd embalmed earlier and a ghost from near Bast's tomb, commanding it to "Protect the cult's island", but I don't believe any other grand strategy arose outside of it. Luca and a few men did take the accumulated prisoners, particularly Banek and the interrogated soldier, to an entirely uninhabited island, where they sheltered themselves from aerial observation..

                                                    During this time, Akenakhi made a few trips to Dartlan's. Mrs Dartlan's husband had apparently just returned from France, but was quite sick. He tried to offer to buy the restaurant. She told him that her husband owned the restaurant, but was too ill from all his travelling to receive visitors just yet.

                                                    On one shore trip, Aquila found one of Yama-Het's cultists waiting for her. He told her that Awei had a brother who owned a farm in the countryside. They decided that they would finally set off and rescue Ari, hoping no attack would happen in the meantime and leaving Luca behind on guard. Aquila used Dust Beneath Feet once they came within sight of the farm in order to take herself and Resa along. Resa, with no Ka or Sekhem rating, was completely blind underground. At Sekhem 1 and Ka 1, Aquila wasn't a whole lot better. They managed to navigate their way into the basement and found Ari inside, but their emergence provoked Terror Sybaris. Aquila desperately intimidated him while wishing she'd brought Akenakhi along with his ability to bestow the Witness merit. They managed to shepherd him underground, using Dust Beneath Feet Tier 1 on him as well, and get back to where the others were, before they were discovered. They headed back to town by hitching a lift in the back of a passing truck.

                                                    And there it ended. An extra point of experience was awarded, because it was the first time in quite a while that the players had actually succeeded in wrapping up a story, albeit it a pretty minor one.
