                            Originally posted by reseru View Post

                            Yeah, Affinities are a possibility (aspects of the soul and everything). However, all the information we have indicates that Utterances are manifestations of "the Word", to use the Sesha-Hebsu term. The Sesha-Hebsu are also quite sure that the Sixth Guild dealt with "the Will", which gave them the dangerous knowledge of how to create rituals and spells of their own. Utterances, stolen though they may be, are still just rote magic. There's no experimentation to be done with them. You utter the spell, the spell has an effect, and it will always have that same effect (barring the occasional Utterance like Dream of Dead Gods that lets you pick from a few different ones). The Will is described in very different terms. From what I've read, having access to the Will is like a grounding in something like Chemistry. You understand the underlying laws of magic, why it works the way it does, and that means you can experiment with a reasonable idea of what the effects will be. It sounds more like Mage magic than the rote-powers of the Arisen.

                            As Deathless though, I fully expect Deceived to follow many of the other Mummy rules. I'm sure they'll have Pillars and Sekhem for example, although I imagine they'll have their own spin on the Sekhem rules. Maybe even use Sekhem more like a normal power stat like the other splats. The Deceived were destroyed before Irem fell, after all. Since the other Mummies are aware of the betrayal, that means that it was before even the Rite of Return. Which means that any Deceived that still exist must be around due to some spell of their own devising, rather than the work of the Shan'iatu, which likely means they'll be operating on a very different wavelength to Mummies (I doubt they'll have bound themselves to the service of a Judge, for example, so no dropping Sekhem for not following your purpose).

                                    I'm with Azahul on this one. Particularly in regard to the different template aspects the Deceived might have in relation to the Arisen. Since all they really have in common is that they are both Deathless creatures.

                                    Affinities -

                                    The Deceived MIGHT have affinities, but that's a big maybe.

                                    Considering that affinities were powers designed into the Arisen through the Rite of Return by the Shan'Iatu. As Azahul mentioned, the Deceived were purged before the Rite of Return was starting to be enacted. So they never underwent the Rite. Its implied that their ability to manipulate Names allowed them to make their own method of becoming Deathless. So there is in-universe absolutely no reason for them to have access to affinities. On a more pragmatic out-of-game look, I'd bet they have something similar/equivalent if they just don't have outright access to them in some sort of "universal truth/backdoor" way.

                                    Utterances -

                                    I really hope the Deceived actually don't have access to Utterances. Mainly because Utterances are perfect examples of "rote magical effects" that very much fit with the idea that the Arisen only have the "word" part of the magic puzzle. The Deceived will, once again, likely have something that is equivalent, but I really hope the big reveal about Name/Will magic is not just a set of unique Utterances, that would be a massive let down.

                                    Pillars -

                                    I'll be interested to see if the Deceived have a favored pillar/decree. Since that aspect of the Arisen seems to be tied into their relationship with the Judges. It would make sense given the metaphysical underpinning of Mummy that they have Pillars and a five-fold soul, but with the information we have I can't see them having decrees. Then again, one rumour about them talks about them all being Ren decree. So maybe they do only have one pillar but its not capped at 5?

                                    ​Then again the whole Ren thing stinks to me of the Arisen trying to rationalize something they know basically nothing about in terms they understand.

                                    Sekhem -

                                    Goes without saying they will have this. Though I will be interested to see how their Sekhem system works. Does it behave more like traditional power stats and climb? Does it rise and fall? Does it every drop to 0 like with the Arisen? I guess this all links into the idea of if the Deceived are continuous immortals in contrast to the Arisens bursts of activity.

                                    Relics -

                                    Seems Deceived interact with the abstract matrix of magic that forms different relics depending on its shape rather then the physical aspect of a relic. So I'd say they can and do interact with all the relic types we are familiar with. Likely as well or better then Arisen of the Relics's corresponding Guild at least. They also likely have some unique "abstract" relic aspect that is likely part of their magic system.

                                    Misc -

                                    I am wondering how much like the Arisen the Deceived are physically as well. Are they as tough and unkillable? Considering a lot of the Arisen's template's perks are designed to keep them going to do the Judges will the Deceived could be very different in this regard as well. Perhaps something more like the "Reborn" type immortals in the Immortals splat book for core WoD.

                                    Originally posted by reseru View Post
                                    While the Deceived are a unique breed and warrant a different play experience, on a pragmatic level I don't think they would write a book and say, "Here's a new type of character, but they can't use any of the powers in the core book"
                                    Weirdly, I'm taking the opposite view. Even with the idea that the Shaunkshen have Affinities and can steal Utterances comes from the fluff relating them to the Rite and the similar relationship they have with their patron that the Arisen have with the Judges. The Deceived meanwhile, seem to be working on a completely different paradigm to achieve their Deathless state.

                                    I really hope the Deceived are such a different type of Deathless that the books about the Arisen basically don't apply to them in terms of mechanics except for when it comes to Deceived magic effecting Arisen and Arisen magic effecting Deceived. Sure common names such as Sekhem and Pillars can and probably should apply, but the systems for those traits should behave differently.

                                    Though for me the perfect Deceived would be a variation on the Arisen template with true free-form Mage style magic running along the themes of - Names, Matter, Energy, Death and Astronomy. Similar to the expanded Blood Magic rules for Vampire found in Blasphemies and Sacraments.

                                    Just my 2p on the issue.
