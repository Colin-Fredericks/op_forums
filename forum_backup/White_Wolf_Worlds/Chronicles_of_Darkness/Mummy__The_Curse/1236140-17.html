                                            Originally posted by Niknokk View Post


                                            ​Oh, and I thought of another question: Mummies get Iremic language at Memory 1: is that the language that they start with or is that considered extra because it's technically tied to Memory? So do new Mummy characters start with a language of the players choice AND Iremic at Memory 1, or just Iremic because you can't really communicate at Memory 0 anyway?
                                            It's considered extra, though it's easier to understand if you flip the script a little, that the level of Memory 1 is rooted to the language because so much of what we understand about cognition is wrapped up in linguistics, and particularly the dominant linguistic of your every day life. It's effectively saying that if you don't remember a whole lot about who you are, you're still going to remember your most potent way of interacting with the abstract elements of reality in the same way you'll rely on your senses.

                                            So at character creation, it goes something like "Everyone knows Iremite, plus one language that's going to be relevant to the story."*

                                            *unless your have a story where the opening, at least, is rooted in not being able to easily communicate with the NPC's, but that's a niche.

                                                        Originally posted by evtrax View Post
                                                        I don't know if I am doing this right, but are the judges of mummy the curse based upon the 42 judges of the dead from Egyptian mythology? I feel like they are, but I would like confirmation from someone who knows for sure
                                                        Yes and no. The inspiration is absolutely the 42 Assessors of Maat that judged a particular sin during the 'weighing of the heart' scenario in Ancient Egyptian mythology. Their specific names are in fact based on a particular scholar's translations of the Negative confessions (I want to say Budge, but don't quote me on that).

                                                        That said, there really isn't much in common between the original mythology and Mummy beyond sharing a name and the sins they judge being somewhat based on the negative confessions from the Book of the Dead (I say somewhat because a lot of those sins are heavily extrapolated and treated with lots of poetic license). I speak from personal experience- doing research on the topic and comparing the two tends be get frustrating. The write ups do what they need to do in the context of the game, but there were some missed opportunities. Most of the judges are obscure and we don't know much about their Egyptian mythology beyond their names. But the ones we do know a lot about are often completely different from their in game counterparts.
