For some reason, when the book spoke of the Shan'Iatu uncovering the great black stone in the Sahara, I got three images immediately: a) the obelisk in Space Odyssey, b) a meteorite, and c) the Kaaba.               Its a touchy subject, but no worse then someone putting a Relic in the Vatican Library ("Oh god...YOU KILLED TOM HANKS!") or something similar. It wouldn't be hard for an Imam to mistake a fantastic object of Irem for a holy item. Hell, it technically IS a holy item, just not something Allah had anything to do with. That said, if you DO run something like this, it's best done carefully. The Kabaa is one of the most sacred objects in Islam, and its not to be taken lightly (I'm not a Muslim, by the way. And I'd be doing this with any other faith that popped up on here.)

              If you want to take it further, you could claim Tunguska was a Mummy waking up, and while still at Sekhem 10, nuking the area with a powerful Utterance (Although I think a Quashmalilim was responsible for that in canon. I've always liked the idea that it WAS a Meteor...that promptly crashed into a True Fae hunting down some Russian Changelings!). Or claiming that some of the Judges of Duat later inspired the Ars Goetia and the Keys of Solomon (Maybe similar rites where used by the Priests...And the existing ones might just be a little TOO similar...)
