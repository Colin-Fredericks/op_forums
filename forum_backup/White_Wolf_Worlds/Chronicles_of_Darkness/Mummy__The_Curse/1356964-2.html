        I want to add a little to the discussion about ghosts:
        While there's no reason to think they aren't related to the Sheut pillar (especially considering the text from Dreams of Avarice above), a lot of stuff regarding the formation of ghosts concerns strong emotions and Integrity breaking points. (The strength of human ghosts and thr likelyhood that they are formed at all are tied to their Integrity, while the strength of non-human ghosts are tied to how traumatic or important their death or destruction was to humans.) To me, that aspect seems more like it's tied to the Ba pillar. The Spirit is also what's said to create undead beings, which could refer to revenants (the kind that are ghosts possessing their own corpses; not the revenants from 2e VtR).
        On top of that ghosts gain Essence from being remembered. I think that implies a relationship with the Ren pillar as well.

        I absolutely believe that ghosts aren't "a pillar", or even made from the parts of a pillar (since the formation of ghosts seems to have zero effect on the soul, given the existence of Doppelgänger and how Mage makes no distinction between souls taken from the living and souls trapped after death), but I do think that there's still a connection. Rather than ghosts being detached parts of the soul, I would consider the soul to be the creator of ghosts, possibly the Spirit being what powers the process and the Shadow being the "template" around which a ghost is formed, or something like that. How the relationship would look is something I'd rather leave to someone more confident in their MtC lore to describe.


        Anyway, has anyone done a comparison between the various topics of this thread (ghosts, shades, the five pillars of the soul) with the Hollow Mekhet?
        For those who don't know, Mekhet is a Clan of vampires, and the Hollow Mekhet is the original variation of them, later replaced by regular Mekhet upon contact with other vampires. The Hollow Mekhet dates back to ancient Egypt (though in their Clanbook I think they're implied to be older still, quite possibly contemporary with the Nameless Empire) and are for all purposes vampires haunted by a "ghost" of their own. It is named the "Ka" and is said to be envious of the corporeal vampire. Ruleswise they're ghosts but instead of being in Twilight and being able to use Manifestations, they only appears in mirrors, phones, TVs, and such. Basically anything that can "reflect" a person somehow.
        I almost forgot: The other part that sets Hollow Mekhet aside from regular Mekhet (as well as all other vampires) is that they don't have an echo, a shadow or a reflection in the mirror, and never appears on any recordings. Basically, anything the vampire should be reflected in (except the echo and the shadow) it is the Ka that can take its place if it so chooses.
        Any thoughts about that?

            All that talk about how ghosts are connected to Integrity, trauma and memories made me think about something- dust at home is said to be around 50% dead human skin.

            And not only that- we cut our nails. Our hair drops naturally. Our spit is being carried away with every breath. Our oils leave fingerprints on whatever we touch. When we are wounded, we bleed. All of those pieces- skin, hair, nails, blood - all of those dead pieces, they aren't us. They are what remains of us. Our body constantly dies and being reborn, yet we are still we- all that dead matter, that's not us. Those are just pieces of us- and yet, if we were to examine those leftovers, we'll see that they still carry a trace to us. Our DNA, our protean- our own body keeps falling apart and fixing itself, leaving incomplete replicas of "us" everywhere. Ghosts could very well be basically that- they are not souls, but they are of the soul. Breaking points target our mind, by healthy Integrity and a healthy soul generally go together (for example, losing a Soul generally means that your Integrity is going to spiral down). Every time that someone goes through a breaking point, their soul is wounded- and we do know that soul can and do break, according to Deviant. Still, not every small scratch breaks the soul, and it is not impossible that the soul has some sort of an "healing mechanism" to fix itself after getting wounded- but some "soul stuff" still oozes out. Incomplete. Unbalanced. Broken. Pieces of ourselves which are not us. Dead remains which are of us.

            Ghosts may not be souls, but they very well be made from the same raw materials- only dead, and slowly rotting away. And just like how once people didn't believed that we could make organic material without life, it could be that it is only a matter of time before people could start making "ghosts" through artificial methods- in the Cyberpunk Shard, for example, AIs were presented as ghosts, after all. So yeah- ghosts may very well be us and be made from Pillars, yet still not be souls. And just like we fail at making life out of scratch, it is currently impossible to make souls out of the raw material... for now, at least.

            As for the Hollow Mekhet- IIRC, the Mekhet in their Clan Book were very tied to "ghosts", especially their Hollow version, and it is a common theory that the first Hollow may have been the result of an experiment to recreate the Rite of Return (even if their Clan Book suggests that they may be older than humanity), but it could very well be that their creation is based around a manipulation of the dead person's Pillars in a way that relates to ghosts. IIRC, Hollows are created from desecrated burial, which could break the soul into its components- leaving their Reflection and Shadow fleeing the body to torment the living, while the other Pillars drive themselves into the dead body to feed upon the living. Yeah, the Hollow situation fits really well to the end result of "what happens when the soul's Pillars get broken and scattered".

                                So with 2e being out and the shade rules being official, here is something that I felt that we should take into notice- we all know that ghosts are soulless because that like soulless mortals, their "mentality" is reversed- they gain the most from their Vice and the least from their Virtue, as following their lower nature feels just far more satisfying than following their ideals.

                                And here is the thing- shades also have Virtue and Vice, and they are also reversed.

                                That means that shades can't have a soul- if they had, then their Virtue and Vice should have acted normally (as again, the reversed situation was the main argument for ghosts being soulless). So in that case, they can't really "carry the soul" (well, unless they carry it like its their baby- they don't have it, but they physically hold it), and if they are just one Pillar, that supports the notion that being a part of a soul does not mean you have a soul (which supports the ghosts are also made of "soulstuff", even if we can argue how exactly it happens).

                                Just something to ponder about.

                                                Mages defines souls as being governed by five Arcana. This doesn't really mean five parts, but it does mean that souls according to Mage has a fivefold nature of sorts. Furthermore, each of these Arcana can be used to manipulate points of a corresponding Pillar (as described in one of the Dark Eras books) which confirms that when Mage talks about souls it most definitely means the entire thing, five Pillars and all. And since soul loss in Mage is the same soul loss in all other games (that feature soul loss), each of them also refers to the entire soul.
                                                Basically, if the soul as known in other games was just the Ka, then mages would only be able to use the corresponding Arcanum to interact with the soul rather than all five. (Muddying things a bit is that mages still primarily only uses the Death Arcanum to manipulate souls, and Death corresponds to the Sheut Pillar. (Meaning Ka is definitely not the correct Pillar.) They can still see souls with all five related Arcana and would in theory need all five Arcana to create souls if that is possible with mages' magics.)

                                                I think the soul can be likened to a garlic. When Mage and most other games refers to the soul they consider the entire bulb. But when Mummy refers to the soul, it goes in depth and refers to the (in this case) five different cloves.
