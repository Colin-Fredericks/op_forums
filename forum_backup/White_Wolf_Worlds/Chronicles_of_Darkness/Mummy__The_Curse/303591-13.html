                Originally posted by TyrannicalRabbit View Post

                The impression I got from both Mummy and Mage is that getting True Names is far more research, occult knowledge, insight into the entity,etc research work rather than a matter of magic.
                I agree with you that 9 times out of 10 research, questing and all that should be a thing in a game to learn a beings True Name. In a mortals game involving Demons (Inferno type) having to track down all the literature and dodgy cults to find out the True Name of the demon troubling your loved ones. In a Mage game the whole investigation thing fits very nicely with the "uncover the mysteries of the world" theme that Mage has going for it.

                Mummy's a different creature I feel though. Considering that Mummy's already set the president for being able to use Iremite/Arisen magic to find True Names in Guildhalls of the Deathless. As I mentioned in my previous posts the Scribes Level 5 Guild Affinity "Erudite Bastion of Perfection" (Guildhalls of the Deathless p.111) allows them to piece together the True Name of objects and entities.

                At current, the Deceived, who are supposed to be masters of Name Magic have no innate or even optional magical method of doing the same thing a Scribe Guildmaster can do. This just feels kinda...off to me. A load of their abilities mention IF you have a targets True Name (some just need any name and that's kinda cool), but at current the Deceived have to go through the same basically "mortal" process of occult research and "insight" as everyone else. Once again, this just feels kinda...weird in regard to the master of name magic.

                Hopefully the update CAS mentioned in his last post will go into this a bit more (namely what is a true name, how to get hold of one and how the Deceived are better at doing so then everyone else). I'm hoping its an addenda to Book of the Deceived (like the mirage rules added to DC) and not material in a later book in the line.

                                        Originally posted by Felipe View Post

                                        They are much better at exploring True Names, that's their magic, but yes a bit about uncovering them would have been nice too.
                                        In the fluff yes, they are supposed to be the only people who have any proper grasp of True Name magic.

                                        Mechanically at current (as the errataed version might change this from what CAS seems to be hinting at) they are absolutely no better then anyone else at finding out True Names.

                                        The other issue I've noticed is their ability to stay hidden and "a myth" from the Arisen.

                                        Their level 1 Guild Affinity makes them pretty much perfect disguise artists from mundane observation (as does one of their Soul Affinities). Their level 2 Guild Affinity protects them from most forms of Mummy specific magical sights (the Soul Sight affinity in particular). The general fact they have "super" Engima as a extra merit thingy is pretty good for getting rid of any mundane traces they leave and can stack with several Affinities that let them hide mundane evidence of their existence very well.

                                        Thing is, they don't have any obvious protection from Godsight, an Affinity that a fair few Arisen have, indeed its mentioned that their level 1 Guild Affinity is pierced by Godsight. The shape-shifting Soul Affinity makes no mention of it, but its basically a passive magic effect on them all the time, exactly the sort of thing Godsight can see (though in this case the Deceived's true form would not be revealed only that they had a shape-shifting power active). The level 2 Guild Affinity does not apply.

                                        They almost certainly cause a -5 to the WIts+Occult roll Godsight grants to spot supernatural creatures/active magics I would imagine due to their rarity and their "myth" status. Other then that Enigma might add another -5 only if they were in a crowd when it was active. Even then the Arisen could spend a Willpower for extra dice and all he/she needs is 1 success. Given how good Godsight is at identifying magic and supernatural creatures, this would mean an Arisen would pretty much (with a more evocative description in game) get the answer from the ST of "a Deceived" if they got a success.

                                        Once again, maybe something the errata will deal with. Pretty easy to fix by removing Godsights ability to pierce the Level 1 Guild Affinity. Or maybe some text can be added to the Level 2 Guild Affinity to say it also negates general magic sights like Godsight's ability to detect the Deceived as anything other then an Arisen.

                                                Godsight doesn't strike me as being particularly common, though. The followers of Uatch-Rekhet are guaranteed to have it, as are a fifth of Usekh-Nemtet's followers. Given how many Affinities there are, and that Mummies start with 2-3 non-Guild Affinities, I can't see it being particularly likely that anyone outside of those two will have the Affinity. But let's be generous and assume that one in twenty Arisen do.

                                                So we couple scarcity with two other important elements. The first is Memory, wherein a Mummy that actually encounters and knows they encountered a Deceived will inevitably forget the encounter. Supporting evidence, like written documents and first-hand accounts of witnesses, are not impossible to destroy. The second is that the Deceived seem to tend towards "from the shadows" kind of plots rather than direct confrontations, severely limiting exposure.

                                                With all this, it's not surprising that the Deceived are little more than rumours, at least to me. Godsight, if anything, just provides an explanation for why there even are rumours.
