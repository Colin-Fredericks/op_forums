    Interlude 2

    While all this has been going on, Sekhet Bastet has been on the hunt for Shuankhsen. After waking from her memories, she gathered a few cultists and told them to look for signs of the creatures' presence. She knew enough about Shuankhsen from her memories now to say that large numbers of missing people were the most likely sign of such a monster's presence. After deciding that it would take too long to trawl through each of Jakarta's many slums in search of vulnerable people, she chose instead to look into places with transient populations. A combination of Sybaritic Omens and mundane investigation led her to an enormous bus terminal in East Jakarta. This place had come up on the players' radar before, not being all that far from the old warehouse where they first encountered Rangarni, and because it apparently had a problem with missing persons. Dragging a cultist along to translate for her, she went in to investigate.

    The terminal was enormous, resembling an airport in scale and layout. People thronged its corridors, including a sizable number of foreign backpackers. Bast wandered up and down for a while, getting a feel for the place and trying to find something out of the ordinary. In the end, it took about a week of patrols before she ran into something of interest. Most of the departure lounges were packed, the hard plastic seats perpetually full. Today, one of them was completely empty except for a single woman with red hair and pale, badly sun-burnt skin. The glass walls of the lounge made the absence of anyone else quite obvious, and the fact that the woman was arguing with a security guard piqued her curiosity. The man was clearly trying to keep the woman inside. He said something, and the woman backed away and took a seat.

    Bast wandered over. She told her cultist to tell the guard that she was some kind of investigator and just strode past him, ignoring the attempts he made to stop her. She sat down next to the woman, who was looking at her in surprise, and waited for her cultist to catch up. Then, through her translator, she asked what was going on.

    The woman, named Lauren, turned out to be an English backpacker on a solo trip across the country. She was meant to be heading to Bali later in the week, but the bus to take her out of the city hadn't materialised. Instead, she was told there was a problem and to wait here, even when the rest of the passengers were cleared out of the room. That was a while ago now, and she's been getting more and more worried.

    When Bast looked away, she saw that there were now three guards outside all looking at her with unfriendly expressions. Past them, she could see a fourth man watching the proceedings with clear interest. She got up, told her cultist to tell Lauren to follow, and more or less bulled her way past the guards. They seemed largely helpless to prevent it. The watcher, meanwhile, had started to walk away, but Bast hurried to catch up with her gaggle of mortals in tow. When they did catch the man, he was asking a ticket seller about someone who had previously bought a ticket to Semarang. Bast, her cultist, and Lauren followed the man outside, with Lauren at this point looking more worried than she had when she was being held in the room.

    In the smoke-filled passageway between two of the terminal's buildings, the man they were following turned around and addressed to ask why they were following him. Bast, instead, demanded he say why he was watching them. Eventually, she learned that he was a guide, named Keiichi, and was looking for two of his clients. They were meant to travel to Semarang together, but as far as he could tell the pair had left a day early without telling him. He was watching because he'd wondered at first if Lauren was one of his clients, she looked a little similar. He's been getting a bit worried, a few other people in the business have told him that there have been a few tourists vanish rather than meet with their local guides.

    He does give Bast her next lead, however. Apparently, the guard keeping Lauren in the room, Ali, is a little bit notorious. He has a criminal background and martial arts training, a combination that means he's probably linked to organised crime. Bast leaves Lauren with Keiichi and heads back, eventually tracking down Ali and shadowing him from a distance. When he goes to head through a "Staff Only" door, Bast slips in behind him, closes the door, and grabs him. A brief struggle ends with Ali on the floor. Bast sends her cultist to find somewhere secure to interrogate the man, and he comes back to tell her that there's a security terminal manned by a single guard just around the bend. Bast strides in, grabs the guard before he can speak (silencing him with Grip of Death), and then beats his head against every available surface until he stops moving.

    Ali is then dragged in, and Bast waits for him to regain consciousness. A short interrogation breaks him, and Bast finds exactly what she is looking for. Ali is a cultist, a member of a family that has served a creature called "Gator" for generations. He, and a number of other staff in the building, all worship the creature. They conspire to send it regular meals, generally picking on tourists without a set schedule, people who could go missing at any point in their trip and no one would really know where to start looking. Lauren was intended to be its latest.

    Bast demands that Ali take her to the entrance to the creature's lair. A door leading to an underground maintenance system turns out to be the answer. When it opens, however, a pair of human corpses can be seen lying at the base of the stairs. They struggle to their feet when Bast enters, and in the dim light she can see that they have bloody holes in their chest, right where their hearts should be.

    Bast's response to the walking dead is to throw Ali at them. The creatures ignore him, and instead charge at Bast. The fight takes a long, long while. The creatures are incredibly strong, bolstered by lurking near their master's tomb, and won't go down until their health is full with aggravated damage, and Bast has no way of doing anything more than bashing. When she pauses to Seal the Flesh, the Terror Sybaris infects Ali and he starts to beg for mercy in the background. When she finally does finish off the Awakened Servants, tearing off their limbs one by one, she looks down at Ali and decides he's useless to her. She kills him, earning a Descent reset and passing her Degeneration check.

    Leaving her cultist behind, Bast explores and locates an altar room. A variety of bodies, in varying states of decay, lie on the ground around a central plinth. Some look quite recent, others have been there for years. Every single one has a hole where their heart should be. The place stinks of decay. There is a door on the other side of the room, a large stone door that looks distinctly out of place after the far more modern environs of the maintenance tunnels.

    Then, one by one, the bodies start to animate and get to their feet. Bast makes a break for the stone door, slamming into it with her shoulder. She rolls really, really high, and something breaks inside the door. She forces it open, and finds herself in a much smaller room. There is an opening in the middle of the floor. Although she can't read them beyond recognising the picture of a raindrop, there are signs around saying that there's a large water tank here. Bast closes the door behind her, walks over to the tank, and leaps down.

    It's quite a long drop. She takes a few points of bashing damage and is knocked to the ground, but Grip of Death pulls her to her feet a moment later. The only lighting at the bottom of the tank comes from above, putting Bast in the middle of a shaft of illumination. She hears something moving, and then a voice from the darkness speaks to her. She sees a huge shape in the darkness. It looks like a man, but then its head changes. The jaws grow longer and the head splits, until it looks like the jaws of a crocodile. Backing into the darkness, Bast triggers Ancestry of Forgotten Stars to assume her demon form just before the creature charges.

    When it comes through the light to attack her, she gets her first good look at the Shuankhsen. He's an enormous man, absolutely dwarfing her. He has dark brown skin, discoloured a reddish-bronze in places, and covered with tattoos of blue hieroglyphs in others. The hieroglyphs on his neck and head are impossible to read, stretched and elongated by his crocodile jaws, and she doesn't get a chance to look at the others.

    Then the Shuankhsen is on top of her. Even with Ancestry of Forgotten Stars and Seal the Flesh, she starts taking a lot of bashing damage. Her own attacks barely seem to phase her attacker. She grapples him, and the fight from that point is eerily quiet (both participants have Grip of Death). The Shuankhsen goes into a frenzy. Every strike that does 5+ damage stuns Bast for a round. She gets torn to pieces, the creature biting out huge chunks of her body and swallowing them, taking a point of Sekhem with it. She breaks free, recasts Ancestry of Forgotten Stars (it expired a round or two back), and, with only one health not filled with Aggravated damage, teleports to the nearest Geometry 4+ location. Luckily, that happens to be Sesu Ra's tomb.

        Session 14

        Sesu Ra was partway through meditating to restore his Pillars when Sekhet Bastet appeared in the room, missing parts of her head, neck, breast, arms, and clearly on the verge of entering a Death Cycle. After a stunned pause, he demanded to know what had happened. "Shuankhsen," she said. She demanded the meret be gathered to mount an immediate assault on the place. Not hugely in favour of rest and recuperation, Bast.

        Luca was summoned telepathically, and ordered to bring the rest of the group with him. Aquila and Akenakhi were returning after delivering Ari to Yama-Het. Aquila had tried to get some assistance for her cultists against the inevitable LDI attack, but Yama-Het was reluctant to send her cultists out without her blessing, a blessing that would require them to return to her within a few hours. That wasn't exactly feasible given the several hour boat trip required to get to the island where the Families lived, but she did promise to help if they needed help mounting their own offensive. Luca ran into them on their way back to the cult, and told them Sesu Ra was summoning them all on an urgent matter.

        Although Aquila and Akenakhi were reluctant to leave the cult undefended, they decided that a Shuankhsen close to one of their own bases of operation needed to be removed immediately. They headed for the bus terminal, pausing long enough for Sesu Ra to call up an impressive number of cultists to help them with the attack. Aquila sunk beneath the ground upon their arrival, carrying the party's weapons so they could move through the crowds without suspicion. Bast was wearing heavy clothing, both to disguise her injuries and prevent recognition. The group walked through the bus terminal following Bast's lead, reaching the "Staff Only" door. Aquila was able to open it from the inside. The door had just closed behind them when a pair of security guards confronted them. Sesu Ra's response was to use Chthonic Dominion to reveal the ghostly bodyguard following him, the composite spectre made up of dozens of dead men, women, and children. The guards promptly fled.

        They reached the maintenance tunnel that Bast had found, and Aquila handed Luca and Sesu Ra their weapons. Akenakhi used Palace Knows Its Pharoah to scout ahead as they walked, making sure they weren't ambushed. At around this point, Sekhet Bastet's player was able to skype in and take over for the rest of the session.

        When they reached the altar room, it was occupied by three cultists. They attacked the meret, and the corpses around the room started to rise. In all, six Awakened Servants and three cultists beset the party. Aquila and Bast both held back at first, Aquila hiding underground, while Luca and Sesu Ra both unleashed Blessed is the God King (Luca first, then Sesu Ra the next turn) and started boosting their Attributes right to the upper limits.The tight corridor meant that their own cultists couldn't join the fight at first, and the party had to actually push through the zombies and into the room before everyone could pitch in.

        When Sesu Ra's ghost possessed one of the enemy cultists and Luca gunned down a second one, the sole surviving man bolted for the next room and screamed for his master. Aquila scooted forward, still underground, to watch what happened. First came a hollow booming sound, as something climbed up from the pit. Aquila couldn't see what, as the water tank's walls were made of plastic or similar material. Until, that is, the Shuankhsen emerged. Aquila felt a sense of deja vu looking at the reddish-bronze discolourations on his flesh, and decided not to attack him.

        Back in the altar room, the fight was getting pretty bogged down. The immensely strong Awakened Servants were tearing Akenakhi apart, but were having a hard time touching Sesu Ra and Luca due to their high strength and added protection from Blessed is the God King. Bast, along with the three cultists, put one of the zombies in the dust. The ghost spun its mortal body's form into something that resembled an enormous, fleshy praying mantis with rib bones serving as scythe blades. It dismembered one of the Servants attacking Sesu Ra.

        The Shuankhsen stormed into the chamber. It saw its tomb under attack by Arisen and roared out a challenge, as its head deformed into crocodile jaws. Sesu Ra answered, storming up and nearly cutting the creature in half with a single blow. Then the Shuankhsen threw itself into Sesu Ra, biting deep and keeping the larger attacker off-balance as it bit again and again, each strike doing enough to deny Sesu Ra his next action. Between Stamina 10 and Living Monolith (bound as part of Blessed is the God King thanks to Ren 5), Sesu Ra was still feeling reasonably confident, until his health filled with lethal damage and the Shuankhsen bit out a chunk of his Sekhem. Sesu Ra fell to Sekhem 4 (effectively 5).

        The rest of the party slowly finished off the Awakened Servants. Bast walked up to the only living enemy cultist and mummified him on the spot. Akenakhi and the allied cultists made sure that the non-zombie corpses around the room were decapitated, preventing them from rising to help. Aquila sung out with Healing Counsel to keep Sesu Ra on his feet. At the sound of her voice, however, the Shuankhsen stiffened. It turned its head back and forth wildly, and roared out a name. "Emerset!" For a moment, Aquila was back in Irem. She was being handed a rod of office, and being congratulated by a priest on her new station. "Guildmaster Emerset," he called her. Aquila made sure she stuck far underground after that.

        With only the Shuankhsen and one of its servants still standing, the group focussed all their attentions on the Shuankhsen. Luca and the ghost grappled it. Sesu Ra and Bast began stabbing it repeatedly. Akenakhi, who had been using Revelations of Smoke and Flame to attack for most of the fight, switched to a pistol and threw a couple of shots in. With the creature weighed down by so many allies and clearly on the verge of death, Aquila rose from the ground and punched it. Her blow failed to connect, however. Sekhet Bastet struck what should have been a killing blow, but the creature's animating magic had enough juice in it for one more strike.

        Ignoring Luca and the ghost, the Shuankhsen lunged at Aquila screaming "Emerset! Give back what you took from me!". It bit down hard, before the others hauled it off her. And then it collapsed, having taken too much damage for it to sustain itself any longer.

        The group quickly destroyed the final Awakened Servant. The Shuankhsen's cultists were out, one mummified, another dying from gunshot wounds, and the third having taken horrific damage while ridden by Sesu Ra's pet ghost. The group took stock, and Aquila felt something foul taking root in her soul. That last, final bite had infected her with something. A craving for human hearts.

            Session 15

            Akenakhi purchased a dot of Memory at the end of last session, so it was flashback time. He found himself standing in the ice and snow, staring mournfully out into the blizzard. Then a door opened somewhere behind him, he felt the warmth of a fire, and another Arisen joined him. He remembered her name, Nenheta, and that she was an acolyte of the Su-Menent. Like him, she was a seeker of knowledge, looking for Apotheosis.

            They were both bitter about the storm. It was going to ruin the ritual they were set to perform, they were sure of it. In spite of that, Nenheta told Akenakhi that he had best head inside before he froze over completely. They entered the crude, wooden lodge where their cultists waited, and tried to call on the wisdom of the stars to reveal their path to freedom. For a moment they thought they saw sand, stone temples, and white cotton robes. Then it was gone.

            Thinking it had failed, the pair slumped into the carved, stone seats, thrones that seemed quite out of place surrounded by the wooden hut and fur-garbed cultists. Then the fire surged, and spoke. Eyes opened in the fire, and lips of ash parted to reveal glowing coals for teeth. It told them that it knew the knowledge they sought, the path to Apotheosis, but that it would only do so for a price. There were sixteen youths who had not yet seen fourteen winters living in the village. Feed them to the fire, and it would tell them how to break their chains.

            Akenakhi agreed, despite Nenheta expressing doubts that the creature could be trusted. She only watched as he fed the children and infants to the fire, a sequence he remembered only as a frenzied procession of tears and screaming and heat. When it was done, the flame spoke again.

            "You must sunder your soul to break the Judges' hold. Let Devourer's Banes claim heart and shadow, so that you may be free of the Rite. With your master gone, restore your spirit to climb Sutek's ladder."

            Back in the present, Bast teleported out of the Shuankhsen's altar room carrying the mummified body of one of the creature's cultists. She interrogated him to extract as much information as she could, getting a list of fourteen names of fellow cultists employed at the bus terminal, the location of a safe house near Aquila's tomb, and a few facts about Shuankhsen and the way they resurrect. Namely, their enemy would be up and walking about in a day or two, probably inside someone else's body.

            The rest of the group walked out of the terminal without any problems, Aquila carrying their weapons underground once again. Luca and Sesu Ra went back to Sesu Ra's tomb, where they found Bast during her interrogation and pitched. in. Sesu Ra was interested in the enemy cult's presence around the Aquila/Sem-Huf territory, and they quickly discovered that the Shuankhsen had been responsible for fostering tensions between Aquila, Sem-Huf, and Mot-Tan by killing their cultists in ways that implicated one another (that cultist staked outside Aquila's tomb, which she blamed on Sem-Huf, was one prime example). Sesu Ra also found out that the Shuankhsen's followers called it Gator, something Sekhet Bastet had known for a while but I don't think anyone else in the party had discovered.

            The pair then discussed the situation with LDI, and how the cultists' island was likely in danger. Words that proved to be prophetic, because meanwhile...

            Aquila and Akenakhi reached the docks to find that their cultist there was in a panic. Several helicopters had landed on the cultists' island. While most people had been evacuated to Reza's old smuggling island, four, including Quil Mayehi (the man Aquila had resurrected when he was a stillborn infant) and Vania, the cult's historian and one of Phatri's daughters, had been taken prisoner.

            The two headed out immediately. They met up with Reza on the smuggling island, and quickly formed a plan. They'd walk in underground, locate the cultists, inoculate them against Sybaris using Akenakhi's make-a-Witness power, and then help them escape using Dust Beneath Feet.

            They meditated to restore some pillars as they waited for dusk, and then went in under cover of darkness. The few glimpses they saw of the island from underground were daunting, the place was absolutely swarming with black-clad soldiers, and there were at least three helicopters. They got lost trying to locate the cultists, but eventually found them in one of the larger buildings. Akenakhi darted off and caused a distraction, luring their guard away, and then hurried back to render them witnesses (Quil, thanks to one of the perks of resurrection via Gift of the Golden Ankh, already was one). However, he only had enough Willpower for two of them. The third would be a problem.

            Aquila used her Utterance on two in a quick succession, but just as she was moving up to bless the third a Greater Amkhat appeared in the room. A hulking lion with a jackal's head, bees crawling in and out of its nose and ears. Aquila and Akenakhi reacted first. Aquila blessed the third cultist, who scored a whopping six successes to resist Sybaris and so could function normally. They all threw themselves into the walls of the building, then down underground. They continued underground until they reached a neighbouring island, where they popped up again to board the waiting boat. Izman Lehn was the driver, and they were just getting aboard when he shouted and pointed behind them. The Amkhata emerged from the jungle, only metres away, and a helicopter roared overhead seconds later.

            Neither Akenakhi nor Aquila were any good at driving, so they couldn't do much more than hold on for dear life as they went on a hair-rising chase, weaving between islands in an attempt to lose the closely pursuing helicopter. After some truly stellar work from Izman, they were able to hide up an overhung stream while the helicopter passed them, then set off for the smuggler's island to rejoin the cult.

            They arrived victorious, all of the captives safe from harm. Immediately, Aquila ordered this island evacuated as well, everyone to be ferried off to that warehouse the party seized from the Pelamat in eastern Jakarta. She was worried that they might be followed. There was no sign of any LDI forces by the time the move was completed though. Aquila rode with Phatri, finding out that she'd raised the old woman from infant until she was seventeen, preventing her from marrying the whole while (Phatri made a snide joke about making up for lost time since then). Phatri never knew her birth parents, and Aquila had never told her about them.

            With the cultists all set up, Aquila travelled to the nearby bus terminal. After a bit of political jockeying and being passed from secretary to secretary, she eventually used her power as a Mesen-Nebu Guildmaster to simultaneously coerce and bribe the terminal's management into replacing a lot of their staff with loyal cultists. As she hadn't got her hands on that list of names Sesu Ra and Bast had discovered, she didn't know if there were still any of Gator's people on the payroll. It was enough to secure the district as under their control though, so the Families of the West edged a bit closer to gaining their next dot of Reach.

            Sesu Ra went to the Temple in the meantime, and had a long discussion with Kotira Nahan about LDI. She'd been fighting them for a while now, ever since they forced her to pull out of the crusade against the Faithful. It turned out the rash of murders in the Golden Triangle had been down to this conflict. She sent ghosts and revenants to take out any high status, executives and public allies of the corporation that she could find, without arousing untoward suspicion, and they retaliated in kind by siccing Amkhata on her most visible cultists. The police hadn't quite put the conflict together, in part due to the supernatural nature of the crimes, and in part because Kotira's banking interests didn't seem to have any logical conflict with LDI's pharmaceutical works. She was able to tell Sesu that the best way to get rid of them was to make staying uncomfortably expensive for them. In that respect, targetting the shipping quarter down near the docks would be the best route. LDI owned their own ships, docks, and warehouses down there. Finding some way to remove them would go a long way to rendering the corporation's existence in Jakarta unsustainable. Kotira and Sesu Ra agreed to coordinate their efforts against LDI in future, before Sesu Ra moved out.

            Sesu Ra and Bast rejoined, alongside Luca, to mount an attack on Gator's cult's safe house near Aquila's tomb. There was only a single cultist on guard, but Luca gunned him down. Inside they found evidence of the group's attacks on Aquila, including signs that they were planning to do another one in the near future (there was at least one pole, perfect for impaling a dead cultist, that was probably going to be used to try and incite violence between her and Sem-Huf again). There was a large amount of cash lying around, and a letter written in hieroglyphs neither of them could read. Sesu Ra grabbed the pole and impaled the dead cultist on it, sticking him up outside. The trio then scarpered before a mob could form.

            We skipped a couple of days, allowing each player to get back a decent amount of pillars and heal a point of aggravated damage. Reza returned with two suits of riot gear, procured through his smuggler contacts and purchased using Aquila's wealth. Aquila decided to take on Ligo as her personal assistant, having learned about this kind of "modern slave" concept during her political bribery efforts earlier in the session.

            Sesu Ra and Bast ended the game sitting down at the table in the city's Temple, resolving to try and decode the strange piece of paper they'd recovered.

                Session 16

                The meret mucked around for the first half of the session, making sure their relocated cultists were safe and doing some of their own projects. Aquila waited in her tomb for its ghost to show up. Akenakhi went to chat with the Dartlan's, but found the restaurant closed. Bast decoded the message hidden in the piece of paper they recovered, and handed it to Sesu Ra.

                "Rotlung grows impatient. I am not so sure I can restrain him without your supervision, lord. He is like an animal, and every day I catch him making more of the walking dead.

                As for the men of Chika, the other bosses are yet to come around to my way of thinking. I fear I may need to show them a miracle."

                Sesu Ra began trying to track down Shuankhsen's cultists, using a list of names provided by the captured cultist Bast interrogated. Unfortunately, it quickly became apparent that they had been forewarned that he might be coming for them. A message left on the phone of one such vanished cultist revealed that Gator had already returned to life, and ordered his servants to abandon the area.

                Aquila called on Sesu Ra to help her with her ghost. It hadn't shown itself, even after 24 hours of sitting in the tomb. Sesu Ra used Chthonic Dominion destroy the extra anchor they gave to the ghost during the bank robbery, forcing it back to the tomb. Then he used the first tier to have it reveal itself.

                When the ghost flew at Aquila, screaming its usual babble about murder and kidnapping, she silenced it with a declaration and a promise. She would take it to see its daughter, Phatri, so long as it calmed down and stopped trying to kill her. Boosting her Presence and spending Willpower, Aquila was able to completely dominate it. When it agreed, she asked to hear its story, to explain what had happened.

                Phatri's mother revealed that Aquila killed her, and her husband, and most of their village. They were living out in the islands, and had no ties to the supernatural that she knew. They were innocent, when she appeared in the village and began to kill. As the ghost described the events of her death, a series of rapid flashbacks ran through Aquila's head. She saw herself standing over dismembered bodies, dispatching pleading injured victims as they tried to crawl away, decapitating Phatri's father and running through her mother, until she heard the sound of an infant crying. She remembered her gladius dropping from her hand, the sound of it striking the floor, and the vision ended.

                Shocked and disturbed, and more than a little confused at her own actions, Aquila had Sesu Ra bind the ghost into a knife so she could carry it to Phatri. By this point, Sesu Ra was getting a bit grumpy at the constant drain on his pillars, so Aquila offered him something in exchange. A painting depicting events of his prior life, that she had made long ago.

                (For those in need of a reminder, this was a reproduction of the stele Aquila remembered tracking down in order to give to Sesu Ra so that he could destroy it, back during the period when he was actively seeking to annihilate his own memory.)

                They took the knife to Phatri and told her that the spirit of her mother wished to speak to her. However, the group wasn't entirely abreast on ghost mechanics, so they failed to account for the fact that the presence of all the other cultists would mean that the ghost would be functionally unable to manifest. After nothing happened for a while, Aquila left to arrange for more permanent accommodation for the cultists, since the warehouse was increasingly proving unsuitable.

                Before she left, she had the time to locate the painted image for Sesu Ra. He looked upon an stylised depiction of himself, according to the accompanying Iremite script, working on the completion of an immense statue. It triggered a very brief flashback (truncated by Sesu Ra's Memory 2) of standing among a crowd in ancient Irem, slaves and fellow craftsmen all around. The sky above shimmered with magic, as the walls fell away to reveal the masterpiece he had toiled upon for so many years. The vision ended before he could get a proper look at the statue, however.

                At the end of the session, Akenakhi managed to avoid successes on his Descent check. The session did have to be cut a bit short, and we've ended up rescheduling our weekly game to the weekend. It'll be a bit longer than usual until the next write-up, as a result. On the plus side, I hope to have something interesting to post up between now and then, but it will probably deserve its own topic.

                                    Session 17

                                    Sesu Ra accessed the computers in his tower (his relic making it easy) in order to discover the significance of the phrase "men of Chika" from the last session. He quickly found one, Chika Productions, a motorbike, autorickshaw, and car manufacturing plant in East Jakarta. He called up Luca and sent cultists to find the others, certain that this business must be in some way tied to Gator.

                                    Aquila, meanwhile, was checking in with the cult. She'd arranged for most of them to move in to new apartments in the area surrounding the warehouse base. Phatri still hadn't received any ghostly communication from her mother, but Rom Mayehi reported that he'd been suffering from terrible dreams. He was reluctant to speak about them, and Aquila virtually had to interrogate him to find out what they concerned. It became apparent that Rom had been involved in some anti-Chinese riots/pogroms some years before. He'd killed one or two people, but the event hadn't ever bothered his conscience before. Now though, every night for the past week he'd been plagued by nightmares. Aquila was appalled to hear of the behaviour of her cult's hierophant, but calmed her initial rage by remembering that she herself had done some terrible things.

                                    Akenakhi arranged for some cultists to form contacts and allies among the police, justification for spending experience on those merits.

                                    And then everyone gathered at Sesu Ra's tower for a briefing on the situation at hand. He explained that the note led them to Chika Productions, and the group, along with Luca, headed there immediately. They were initially treated with a mixture of puzzlement and suspicion by the men there, who weren't used to seeing well-dressed foreigners walking around their rather filthy and dangerous production plant. The closest thing to authorities on sight were the work crew bosses, so the group persuaded a man, Tega, to take them to meet one of those bosses. Aquila became furious when Tega laughed off her initial intimidation attempt, especially at the patronising way he treated her after that, and fumed quietly when Sesu Ra was able to persuade him quite easily to let them inside.

                                    They met two of the plant's bosses in a quick succession, Donny and Dimah, both middle-aged men in charge of shifts at the plant. The plant itself was a mess, ancient, dangerous machinery, terrible working conditions, no real safety clothing for anyone but the bosses themselves, and with many of the workers showing signs of exhaustion and previous injury. Donny wasn't much help, but Dimah implicitly confirmed some information when he went on the defensive and swore that he'd never met Rotlung. Sesu Ra waited until his shift ended, tailed Dimah home, and ambushed him in his house alongside Luca. They were able to intimidate him easily, helped by Sesu Ra's use of Dominating Might to smash down the front door with his bare fists, tie him to a chair, and interrogate him. A third boss, Ratger, had told Dimah that there was a lot of money to be made if they followed the instructions of a mysterious benefactor. In addition, a man named Rotlung would be around to handle any problems that might arise. Sesu Ra decided to gag Dimah and leave him tied to his chair, figuring that he'd eventually be rescued, and walked out.

                                    Aquila was mucking about trying to find the plant's fourth boss, Yayian Masari. She'd heard he was Tega's immediate superior and was hoping to get him fired. A few failed rolls though, and she was forced to concede defeat in the face of stubborn and patronising workers who refused to tell her where to find him.

                                    Eventually, Sesu Ra and Luca returned to tell the group that Ratger was manning the late shift and wouldn't clock out until midnight. They camped within sight of the compound's entrance, but by 1am he still hadn't emerged. The group went to investigate. The compound seemed abandoned, no security or anything, until they entered the main plant. Akenakhi was scouting ahead with Palace Knows Its Pharaoh and spotted an Awakened Servant walking a patrol. Luca charged in and beat the thing to death, ripping off limbs with his bare hands, much to the admiration of the watching Arisen.

                                    There were two warehouses left to explore, so the group split. Luca and and Aquila headed for the closest, Akenakhi and Sesu Ra went towards the other. However, as Aquila and Luca were halfway to the second warehouse when motion sensors kicked in and lit them up with floodlights. Then, the door of the warehouse opened and a huge horde of zombies spilled out. The group counted about twenty in all, too many for them to easily fight. Aquila dropped beneath the ground. Sesu Ra and Akenakhi hid off to one side, and Sesu Ra telepathically ordered Luca to draw the zombies away. Luca ran for it, running back into the manufacturing plant and using the overhead walkways to keep out of reach of the zombies as they chased him. He finally leapt out through a second story window to escape, made it to the car, and drove a safe distance away to wait for the others.

                                    With the zombie horde busy, the rest of the group headed for the warehouse. Aquila was ahead of the others, not having to wait for the zombies to move, so she snuck in and found two men inside. One matched their description of Ratger, a man wearing a doctor's mask, hard hat, long-sleeved clothing and pants, and a set of gloves. Only the skin around his eyes was easily visible. Dimah had said under interrogation that he started wearing the heavier clothing and mask around the plant a month or two ago, complaining about a chest infection from the fumes. The other she assumed was Rotlung, a disgusting creature that was barely recognisable as human. Every inch of skin seemed to be cracked, weeping, oozing, or coated in scar tissue. When he spoke, his words were slurred as he hissed through broken teeth. He was wearing nothing but a pair of tattered shorts. The two were arguing, Ratger saying their instructions were to flee when someone matching the description of the Arisen showed up. Rotlung said he would rather bring their heads to his master.

                                    Sesu Ra hadn't brought his khopesh, so he made do with a massive wrench he found in the main plant. He walked up to the warehouse, banging the wrench against the wall as he went. The sound clearly worried Ratger, but it gave Rotlung time to get his knife out. When Sesu Ra and Akenakhi entered through the front entrance, Rotlung smiled at them, licked his curved blade, and said, "I have waited long to taste Arisen flesh."

                                    The group started combat. Aquila failed to drain Rotlung's Willpower, Rotlung successfully gave Phobia (Rotlung) to Sesu Ra, Sesu Ra was able to resist the Phobia triggering and unleashed Blessed Is The God King, and Akenakhi used the second tier of Palace Knows Its Pharoah to throw a moped into Rotlung. Ratger found a hammer lying around and watched nervously. Then Aquila rose from the ground and struck Rotlung from behind. He spun around, saw her, and seemed both surprised and pleased. He hissed something about taking her head back to his master and stabbed her with his knife. When it did aggravated damage, the group realised that all the rolls I'd been making them do (that they'd failed) were Kepher rolls, and that they were up against a relic.

                                    Ratger charged at Sesu Ra to try and slow him down. Aquila, not wanting to die again, hid beneath the ground to avoid further aggravated damage. Rotlung responded by charging Akenakhi, who had dealt a considerable amount of damage by throwing first the moped, and then a long javelin-esque pole of metal at him. In a single blow, Rotlung carved into Akenakhi and filled his health with aggravated damage. It was at this point that Sesu Ra's player got a bit exasperated at the fact that he was the only character who ever bothered to boost Stamina before a fight.

                                    Since he was the only one standing against the two combatants, and his ghost protector was apparently not intervening, Sesu Ra put his priorities on Rotlung. Akenakhi was able to stay functional by Sealing the Flesh, using Revelations of Smoke and Flame to scour Rotlung with blasts of smoke and ash. Mostly though, Sesu Ra kept Rotlung knocked to the ground with repeated blows from his massive wrench. Rotlung's retaliation was to scream obscenities and stab Sesu Ra repeatedly in the leg and foot from where he lay, while Ratger tried to club Sesu Ra from behind. They weren't able to make it through all of Sesu Ra's temporary health, however, before Sesu Ra leapt onto Rotlung's back, overpowered him, and turned the relic knife against its wielder to slit Rotlung's own throat. Seconds later, Akenakhi's attempt to Seal the Flesh expired and he died.

                                    Rather than face Sesu Ra alone, Ratger ran for it. He bolted out the front door the warehouse, but Sesu Ra ran him down. The motion sensitive floodlights came on again, providing stark lighting for the tableau that unfolded. Despite his sobs and screams and fighting like a cornered animal, Ratger wasn't able to stop Sesu Ra from knocking him to the ground and beating him with the wrench until his skull caved in and he lay still.

                                    Aquila was a bit annoyed at Sesu Ra for not leaving anyone alive to interrogate. Sesu Ra shrugged off the criticism. Aquila briefly explored the other warehouse, found it contained two more Awakened Servants, and backed out quickly. She made it back to the first warehouse to find Sesu Ra examining the relic knife. Thanks to his Model Lifeweb Affinity, Sesu Ra determined that it was a weak Uter, a komodo dragon's claw set on a haft of wood. Those struck by it started bleeding out, just as if their health was filled with lethal damage, causing them to take a point of bashing damage every minute until treatment. Its curse was something Sesu Ra had already experienced. The claw thirsted for blood, granting a bonus to anyone who grappled its wielder and used it to turn the blade back on the user. Sesu Ra pocketed it, intending to give it to Sekhet Bastet at the earliest opportunity.

                                    The zombies that had chased Luca were returning. Sesu Ra made some hasty preparations. He found everything flammable he could, rigged the warehouse to burn down, and grabbed Ratger and Akenakhi's bodies. Rotlung's he drenched in flammable liquids, hoping it would prevent the creature from returning to life. They waited then until the zombies entered the warehouse, Aquila blessed Sesu Ra with Dust Beneath Feet, and they dove underground as the building, with all the zombies inside, began to burn.

                                    They met up with Luca, Sesu Ra sending a telepathic order to come and pick them up. Searching Ratger's body, Sesu Ra found a reply to the earlier message he'd discovered. "Rotlung is your elder, slave. I expect you to follow his commands, not question them. As for the miracle, I expect my Fasad to succeed in their tasks without my help. Fail, and I shall replace you."

                                    And on that note (heh), we wrapped up. First thing next session, another Death Cycle!

                                                Session 18

                                                Aquila's player couldn't make it to this session. We started out with Akenakhi's Death Cycle. After meeting with Anpu, he found himself in France, reading a libellous pamphlet slurring a member of the aristocracy. It wasn't the content that interested him, however, but rather the code he recognised. It was from someone he had known, someone he had loved, but all he could glean from the work was that it was telling him to seek out more books of the same type.

                                                So he did so. He wandered to a nearby back alley bookstore he knew, where he found a Dutch man arguing with the owner. They were speaking a language he didn't understand, but he noticed an Iremite symbol tattooed on the neck of the Dutch man. Intrigued, Akenakhi slipped a knuckle bone he owned into the Dutch man's pocket. He would be able to track the item, so he waited for the Dutch man to leave and found the book he was looking for, "Scandals of the Scarron Dynasty". Then he took off, tracking his mark.

                                                He followed the man through the streets until they reached the large opening to a man-made cavern. Black-shrouded caravans were rolling through the door, and the Dutch man wandered in. Akenakhi snuck in after him and observed him talking to someone else in the same language from before, one he didn't know. He accosted the target after the meeting, demanding to know the meaning of the tattoo on his neck. The huge blonde man seemed a bit bemused by all this. He said some friends had given him the tattoo when he served aboard a naval vessel, but he was willing enough to show Akenakhi the tattoo in more detail. It turned out that it read "Heart of Nenheta". Akenakhi discovered that the man now served with the East India Company, and asked about Nenheta. For a moment the man looked stricken, and then the memory dissolved.

                                                The next part of the memory took place some days later. Akenakhi managed to decode the message within the book he bought. It listed three places. The first, the catacombs. The second, he couldn't remember, the word appeared blurry in his memory. The third, Batavia.

                                                He woke in the present to find that Sesu Ra had wrapped his body and placed it within his sarcophagus, shortening his Death Cycle to just half an hour. He immediately meditated to regenerate some lost pillars.

                                                Sesu Ra had been doing the same. When he emerged from his tomb, he sent a cultist to ask Yarikh to meet him at the temple to discuss the meaning of the word "Fasad". His own attempts to research the word, at least beyond its modern meaning, frustrated him. As he was leaving, his False Incarnation derangement triggered, and he found himself pursuing a man on motorbike by jumping in a nearby taxi. He ended up outside a large, closed down building, watching the man duck beneath the tape closing off the entrance, and got out to follow.

                                                Inside there was a large squatter settlement. Sesu Ra seized the man he was chasing and dragged him back outside. He was convinced that the man had been a slave of his back in Irem, working on the construction of the monument he had remembered unveiling. He was demanding to know what the "slave" knew of his projects, when the man started sobbing and said that he'd just been paid to keep an eye on Sesu Ra by a woman concerned for his safety. Apparently this woman was just upstairs, so Sesu Ra dragged his new slave up two flights of stairs and...

                                                Found himself staring at a dozen Pelamat, some of whom he recognised from previous encounters. Both sides were somewhat stunned. A long moment passed, and then the Pelamat snatched up their weapons and Sesu Ra unleashed Blessed Is The God King.

                                                The following fight was absolutely gruelling. Sesu Ra strode through the horde of attackers like a Bronze Age hero made manifest, cutting down a man with nearly every blow. His ghostly protector possessed one of the gun armed Pelamat, and distracted two other men in a fight that ended in the death of both the possessed character and one of his opponents. Sybaris took four of the Pelamat out of the fight, but the remaining eight were nearly enough to kill Sesu Ra. Despite being Size 7, Stamina 10, and temporarily manifesting Living Monolith with Ka 5, he ended severely damaged by the time he cut down his final opponent. He'd taken 17 aggravated damage. The gore-splattered room was quiet for a moment, and then he quickly dispatched the cowering, Sybaris-infected survivors. The man that had been the subject of his False Incarnation derangement had long since fled. By some Judge-given miracle, Sesu Ra was able to avoid failing his degeneration check for this act of mass murder by putting his faith in Set-Qesu.

                                                There was a door to the next room. From behind it, Sesu Ra could hear someone playing music, jazz, loud enough to drown out the sound of the fight. At least, that's what he hoped. Still resplendent in God King form, he threw open the doorway and strode in.

                                                The room he found himself in was filled with contrasts. It had all the hallmarks of an abandoned building, from exposed maintenance work, a lack of wallpaper, and a general sense of incompleteness. Someone had chosen to decorate it with luxury furniture, including a truly opulent lounge. Curled up like cats at the base of this lounge were a pair of naked men. Reclining above them was what appeared to be a young Indonesian woman, tapping her foot in time to the music.

                                                Then the record ended, Sesu Ra moved, and the woman looked up and met his eyes. In that moment, Sesu Ra felt a rush of emotions. Hatred, love, shame, and a horrible, aching sense of loss. The woman flinched back, looked out towards the room's balcony, and screamed, "No. No, no, no, no, no! Rangarni!"

                                                Sesu Ra demanded to know who she was. He spotted the tell-tale flickering of a khat moving beneath the sahu, a sure sign that she was some form of Mummy. She refused to answer him, babbling instead that he wasn't meant to be here and studiously avoiding further eye contact. Then Rangarni walked in from the balcony. He looked different from the last time Sesu Ra had seen him, sporting open, weeping sores on his face and hands, and a definite discolouration of his skin. He looked nervously from Sesu Ra to the woman and back.

                                                To Sesu Ra's frustration, no one seemed interested in answering his question. The woman demanded that Rangarni remove Sesu Ra. Rangarni quietly asked the woman, calling her Iwseck, to follow him out of the room. The two naked men seemed oblivious to the proceedings, watching on with blank, staring eyes.

                                                Eventually, Iwseck and Rangarni backed out to the balcony. Sesu Ra made one last demand that they tell him why the Pelamat had been so determined to kill them. The woman looked straight at him, and he saw she was crying. She spoke an Utterance, manifesting great wings of smoke and flame. Rangarni followed suit a second later. Then the pair leapt over the balcony and disappeared. Blessed Is The God King ended moments later, and Sesu Ra felt fate's curse settle on him for squandering its majesty. He sent a mental message for Luca to come and find him, and stood among the wreckage as he waited.

                                                            Session 19

                                                            Sesu Ra waited for Luca to show up at the squatter's building where he had confronted the Pelamat. They searched Iwseck's room together, and found a collection of photographs inside her dresser. They ranged from black-and-white to digital printouts, all of Sesu Ra in a variety of locations scattered across the past century. They searched a little more, didn't find anything else of interest, and then caught a ride back to the Temple. Sesu Ra met Akenakhi there, and shared the story of his fight and the strange conversation he'd had with Iwseck. A short while later Aquila showed up, and together they all waited for Yarikh.

                                                            The Speaker to the Thrice-Dead showed up at the appointed time, and the group started bombarding him with questions about the various supernatural terms they had uncovered over the last little while. Sesu Ra discovered that Fasad were the Shuankhsen equivalent to Sadikh, but that Yarikh had heard rumours that a single Shuankhsen could manage multiple Fasad at a time. Aquila learnt a few interesting facts about ghosts, their corpus, and ways they could be destroyed/persuaded to pass on. And then Akenakhi asked about the phrase "climb Sutek's ladder". Yarikh shut the conversation down. He curtly informed them that he hadn't ever heard the phrase, but that Sutek was held, according to mythology, to be the deity responsible for Azar's death and subsequent ascension to the throne of Duat. Akenakhi seemed satisfied by this response, but when Yarikh wasn't looking he cut off part of one of his own fingers and dropped it into Yarikh's pocket, letting him track where the other Mummy went.

                                                            When Yarikh had left, Aquila asked Sesu Ra to use Chthonic Dominion so that she could converse with her ghost. She was perplexed about why it hadn't yet appeared to Phatri. When they did so, the exasperated ghost explained that she hadn't been able to touch manifest in the presence of so many mortals. Since Phatri had been moved out of the warehouse and into a new apartment, they took the ghost there immediately. The three Mummies waited outside while Phatri and her mother spoke, leaving the ghost in the room along with the ghost-light from Chthonic Dominion. After about an hour, they heard Phatri called to them and walked inside. The light was still there, but the ghost was gone. Phatri said that she had simply faded, and asked that they leave her alone for a little while. She had a lot to think about.

                                                            The group went back to their warehouse to discuss what to do next. Aquila wanted the cult to move away from its tribal foundation, to become a legitimate enterprise dedicated to improving the lives of the lower classes. The others had no particular objections to this, neither caring all that much about what activities their cultists were engaged in. Akenakhi couldn't trust any of them due to his personal pursuit of Apotheosis, and Sesu Ra generally considered his cultists to be rather beneath his notice. Ligo was dispatched to see about laying the groundwork for the shift.

                                                            They waited a bit over a week, allowing their bodies to heal a little from the confrontation with Gator/Rotlung and to gather some information on their next move. Aquila began working as a volunteer at a medical clinic in a West Jakarta slum, the opposite side of the city to the slums ruled by Mot-Tan and Sem-Huf. She had a few initial problems with local medical personnel noticing strange quirks about her undying state, but she gradually got better at emulating vital signs. Sesu Ra, meanwhile, was using Luca's connections and spying skills to find out about two potential targets, an LDI owned section of the docks and the ghost-worshipped cult in the Old Dutch Quarter of Kota. He'd decided that the threat posed by each of them needed to be removed, but wasn't sure which one to strike at first.

                                                            He discovered that a massive tanker, the Sethis, was due into port in the next few weeks. It was owned by LDI, and would be carrying a cargo of medical supplies alongside certain items that the company had secured permission to bring into the country without first clearing customs. It presented ample opportunity to demonstrate to the company that continued operations in Jakarta would not be a profitable endeavour. Security would be exceptionally tight, of course, and they would have to be careful about how they went about it if they wanted to avoid bringing a city-wide manhunt down on their own heads.

                                                            In Kota, the Temple the cultists used to worship Mannfred was obviously the main target. It was a symbol of their faith and presumably either was, or contained, Mannfred's principle anchor. Destroying it would be a great blow to the ghost, and killing the ghost himself would rob them of their supernatural backing. They'd already lost the Blasphemous Depiction, so they wouldn't be able to replace any zombies that they lost either.

                                                            After a bit of debate, Sesu Ra declared that Kota would be the target. It seemed like the softer option, they all still had a bit of damage from their trials, and it presented the opportunity to add a few more ghost servants to Sesu Ra's menagerie in preparation for the larger confrontation.

                                                            They went to back to the museum first. Sesu Ra recalled dominating one of the ghosts there on his last visit, and was interested in recruiting it. The group entered the museum in daylight hours, snuck into the rooms under renovation, and broke into the ceremonial room where the Blasphemous Depiction once was used. Sesu Ra sent his ghost-light into the chamber beneath, and saw the female ghost (the one he hadn't dominated) staring back. He immediately guessed that it must have destroyed the ghost he had controlled, it wasn't likely that the pair could be co-existing after he commanded the male one to turn on its allies. On the plus side, that meant he would be able to seize the creature with the banshee wail.

                                                            There was a surge of movement, and the zombie he fought last time lurched out of the hole to attack. Sesu Ra kept it knocked to the ground, although the wailing spirit proved to be a bit of a pest as it caused each of the characters involved to lose at least a single turn of combat with its banshee screams. When the zombie finally stopped moving, they bundled it up in sheets from the renovation equipment and crept out, after Sesu Ra was able to bind the ghost to himself and force it to protect him from harm.

                                                            The others had their cultists take the zombie's remains to be burnt continuously for the next 24 hours, while Sesu Ra returned to his tomb. Selecting one of the corpses he'd had preserved, the corpse of a member of the LDI acquisition's team, he used the Blasphemous Depiction to bind the banshee into it. He commanded it to guard his tomb and protect it from intruders, thinking that its scream Numen would help it against larger numbers.

                                                            That night, the meret assembled near the temple for its assault. Aquila and Reza showed up wearing the riot gear they'd had smuggled to them. Sesu Ra and Luca carried the best weapons they could bring from their selection. Akenakhi had spent some time regenerating his pillars in preparation for the confrontation, and temporarily made Reza a witness.

                                                            We called an end to the session just before they began their attack. It was good to have a session to plan, heal, and take stock, and we decided we could easily leave the next big fight until later. When "later" will be, however, is another matter. We'll all be pretty busy for the next two weeks, but hopefully there'll be time before the new year to have one more game. If not, I guess we're back in January.
