                Originally posted by LordHeru View Post
                I do have one thematic and mechanical question for you. I'm interested in hearing why you choose to remove raising Sekhem with experience and made it based on sacrifice, Vessel or person.
                Three main reasons:

                One, because the previous version I tried with a 'permanent' power splat (it was called Puissance) felt like it didn't have any 'catch' or 'issue'. Mages have Obsessions, werewolves have their rage, Prometheans have being a Promethean, but Wrights just... had powers.

                Two, one of the central themes of Mummy is a choice between power and slavery, or freedom and weakness. I decided to take this and tweak it a little with Wrights, such that if you want to be powerful you need to accept one of two strictures: either husband your power carefully, using it only when you really need to, or face the issue of upkeep.

                Three, Wrights are thematically based around building themselves up, so it made sense to me to gamify that aspect of them; spending XP on a power stat isn't a very interesting thing, but having to choose between unleashing your full might and being safer (via Supernatural Tolerance) makes for an interesting dynamic, I think, as does the fact that the very way Sekhem functions asks both Wright and player the question "What are you willing to do for power?" Are you willing to sacrifice a few people to gain the power to banish that monster with a meteor? Are you willing to sacrifice your magical panoply? Is the short-term gain worth the long-term loss?

                                                        I've actually made a start on that, swapping out the soul-parts for elements (wood, metal, earth, water, fire).

                                                        Wuxia

                                                        Changes:
                                                        • Replace ‘Wright’ with ‘Fangshi’
                                                        • Replace ‘Sekhem’ with ‘Qi’.
                                                        • Replace ‘Pillars’ with ‘Elements’
                                                        • Replace ‘Pillar Point(s)’ with ‘[Elemental] Breath’ (e.g. Fire Breath, Metal Breath etc)
                                                        • Replace the descriptions of the Pillars with the following:
                                                        Fangshi differ from ordinary residents of the Middle Kingdom in that they have cultivated a mystical connection to the five elements which compose all things. This applies not only to the physical representations of those elements, but to the spiritual principles they represent.
                                                        Determine your defining Element based on what best fits the core of your identity and personality. You can distribute effective Element dots equal to your Willpower among the five Elements, allocating new dots whenever your permanent Willpower increases, and choosing which become dormant whenever your Willpower falls. No other Element can be rated higher than you defining Element at any time. Only one Element may have no dots.
                                                        The Elements are as follows:
                                                        • Fire: The element of Fire represents the emotional and social domains of reality; words burn in the minds of those who hear them, and rebellion spreads like wildfire across the land. Those defined by Fire heart are loving and beloved, a radiant presence who cannot help but live according to the dictates of passion. Among Fansghi, they are sometimes called the Ardent.
                                                          • A Fangshi defined by Fire gains the following abilities:
                                                            • The character regains one Breath point of their choice after any scene in which they are able to revel in victory over a rival, or overcome challenges by appealing to others’ emotions or through improvisation.
                                                            • Once per chapter, the Ardent may fully restore their Fire Breath through an intense emotional experience, provided the event seems novel to them.
                                                            • When the Fangshi improves a character’s impression of her during Social Maneuvering, she learns one of that character’s Aspirations, feeling his desires like the warmth of a flame upon her flesh.
                                                            • All flames are alike, and so the Fangshi may take into herself the emotions of others. Spend a Breath point while touching another character to make a (Resolve + Empathy) roll. If successful, choose an emotion-based Condition affecting the target. The character loses the Condition without resolving it, and the Fangshi gains the Condition.
                                                        • Wood: Fire consumes forests, floods drown grasslands and typhoons flatten trees - and yet, life arises once more with near-infinite vitality. This is the truth of Wood:to live is to grow. Those defined by Wood are tireless idealists, forever seeking the future and whatever it may bring. They thrive on challenge and adversity, only growing stronger in response to difficulty.
                                                          • A Fangshi defined by Wood gains the following abilities:
                                                            • The Fangshi regains one Breath of her choice after any scene in which she chooses to mentally or physically challenge herself. The challenge must contain some risk of failure, but she regains the Breath point even if she fails.
                                                            • Once per chapter, the Fangshi’s Wood Breath may be fully restored by learning or creating something unique, new or novel, whether a technique, a house or a weapon.
                                                            • Once per chapter per dot of Wood, the Fangshi may gain the Inspired Condition when she makes a contested action against an enemy, regardless of whether she fails or succeeds.
                                                            • When helping another character fulfill an Aspiration, the Fangshi achieves an exceptional success on rolls with three success.
                                                        • Metal: Metal is the element of civilization, determination, rigidity, persistence and strength. Fangshi who define themselves with metal align themselves with these ideals; they are stubborn, tough and often quick to take the simplest and most direct route to a goal.
                                                          • A Fangshi attuned to Metal gains the following abilities:
                                                            • After any scene in which the Fangshi overcomes a challenge by tearing away extraneous details and breaking the issue down to its basic essence, they regain one Breath of their choosing.
                                                            • Once per chapter, the Fangshi’s Metal Breath may be fully restored by triumphing over a challenge that entails severe consequences if she fails.
                                                            • When the Fangshi successfully opens Doors during Social Maneuvering using intimidation or violence, she gains the Steadfast Condition.
                                                            • The Fangshi benefits from 1/0 armor, which stacks with any other armor she possesses.
                                                        • Water: Water is the binding essence of the world; it flows in its veins, encircles it in the ocean and falls from the heavens as rain. It is the element of wisdom, wit, flexibility and connection, for it unifies all living things. Nevertheless, when it flows in power it is among the most terrifying of the elements.
                                                          • A Fangshi defined by Water gains the following abilities:
                                                            • The Fangshi regains one Breath point of her choice after any scene in which she overcomes a serious problem by devising an unconventional solution or otherwise taking the path of least resistance.
                                                            • Once per chapter, the Fangshi may fully restore her Water Element by making common cause with an erstwhile enemy or by persuading others to do the same with one another.
                                                            • When engaging in Social Maneuvering, if the Fangshi’s goal would be a breaking point for the character she is trying to influence, add one Door instead of two.
                                                            • When the Fangshi improves a character’s impression of her during Social Maneuvering, she gains the Informed Condition regarding that character.
                                                        • Earth: When hope falls into darkness, passion is met with denial and ambition with failure, it is the earth that endures. Earth represents not only the physical earth, but also the spiritual side of existence - all things are part of the earth, and will return to it in time. Those Fangshi who define themselves by the Earth have a special connection with both natural spirits and the spirits of the dead.
                                                          • A Fangshi defined by Earth gains the following abilities:
                                                            • She regains one Breath of her choice after any scene in which her wisdom helps another character confront their fears, or in which she shows no fear in the face of possible destruction.
                                                            • Once per chapter, the Fangshi may fully restore her Earth Breath by bringing peace, accord or resolution to the living and the dead.
                                                            • She may spend a point of Breath to make a (Composure + Occult) roll. On a success, her spirit leaves her body, gaining the Disembodied Condition. Touching her body again resolves the Condition. If something destroys the Fangshi’s body while she still possesses the Disembodied Condition, she immediately dies and leaves a ghost of Rank equal to (Defining Element - 1), minimum 1.
                                                            • When touching an object or person, the Wright knows if it is a ghost’s anchor.
                                                        • Replace ‘seba’ with ‘zhouyu’.
                                                        • Replace ‘Keshina’ with ‘Yuanying’.
                                                        • Replace ‘Sybaris’ with ‘’.
                                                        • Replace ‘Seal the Flesh’ with ‘Vital Breath’.
                                                        • Replace ‘Arising in Might’ with ‘Elemental Prowess’
                                                          • Elemental Prowess may be used to increase the following Attributes based on defining Element:
                                                            • Fire: Presence
                                                            • Wood: Intelligence
                                                            • Metal: Resolve
                                                            • Water: Wits
                                                            • Earth: Composure
                                                        • Companions who benefit from the Fangshi’s Rite of the Faithful Heart gain the following benefits based on Elements:
                                                          • Burning Spirit: A Companion whose master has Fire 5 gains +2 dice to resist or overpower a character who attempts to sway him servant from his master. The Companion may give his master any Willpower he recovers from fulfilling his Virtue or Vice, instead of gaining the benefit himself.
                                                          • Verdant Self: A Companion whose master has Wood 5 heals at twice the normal rate. When acting as a secondary actor in a teamwork action supporting his master, he adds 2 dice to his pool and negates the effect of any Dramatic Failure by himself or other secondary actors upon the Fangshi’s pool.
                                                          • Perfected Body: A Companion whose master has Metal 5 may exchange up to 4 bashing wounds or 2 lethal wounds to or from his master, with permission from the Fangshi, as a Reflexive action. The Companion cannot accept further wounds if he has any aggravated wounds. This power is effective irrespective of distance.
                                                          • Flowing Mind: A Companion whose master has Water 5 shares a true telepathic bond with his master and can mentally communicate in whole sentences with her. The Companion cannot communicate in this way if he has suffered any aggravated wounds. This power is effective irrespective of distance.
                                                          • Harmonic Soul: A Companion whose master has Earth 5 exists in harmony with creation, gaining 2 dice to Clash of Wills rolls, whether resisting or attacking.
