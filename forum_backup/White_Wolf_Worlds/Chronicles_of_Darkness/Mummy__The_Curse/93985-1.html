            Originally posted by Rose Bailey View Post
            Add to that schedule slippage. We have only two or three writers or developers who aren't juggling a day job, and most of them have families, too. I'm one of relatively RPG workers I know who's both full time and has no kids. We have been tightening up the schedule and improving our planning -- that's one reason Demon is moving along well. It's still got slips, though -- Vampire, Kickstarters, and The Demon Seed Collection all pushed back the Translation Guide. Even within that, we lose time when somebody's child is sick, or development gets pushed back because another book needs attention, and so on. We don't ruthlessly drop contributors as soon as they ask for an extension -- there were times at other jobs when I've had to do that and I don't like it. All of that goes for artists as well as authors.

            And just to be clear, we haven't moved any resources from Mummy to other lines. The only place Mummy books bump up against other books is in the art queue, and so far I don't think Mummy books have waited any longer for art direction and layout than anything else. (I'd have to double check that with Mike; he schedules and manages all of that after my people have handed off the book.)
            I've highlighted because I feel that point is an important one. Mummy was my first experience with the Onyx Path production model. As such I kinda just presumed that the pace which its development was pretty much the norm. Same with the style of the development.

            Demon kinda changed all that for me. I backed Demon and you guys have been pretty amazing ever since with it: -
            - Regular updates on the Monday Meeting blog,
            - Separate articles about Demon in general on the website.
            - Lots of interaction with the fans of the forums (and not only the Demon forum)
            - Just now at the 2/3 month mark when all communication dropped off with Mummy you guys did an special update e-mail for Kickstarter backers explain each project's development.
            - Then you did a special post about the next non-Kickstarter Demon book.

            The level of attention to the community and transparency from Demon as a project has frankly, been amazing. I can google the writers for any upcoming Demon book if I wanted to. Its not something I'd normally do in the process of a books development, but the point is you guys have given that much info that I could if I felt like it.

            Yes as you say Rose the Translation Guide got bumped. However, that's one slippage that relatively small and the product in question is not vital to playing the game nor does it add a new dynamic to the game. Its a toolkit for backwards compatibility with Fallen.

            With all the above recently opening my eyes to what a line can be in both its well organised production and its level of community forethought and saturation I look at the same things in Mummy and I'm just disappointed frankly. Which makes me frustrated, because I love this game and I really feel it gets at some important ideas within its context. I feel like I'm in a niche crowd by even following/buying/supporting Mummy (because lets face it its about as niche as Promethean really) and as such within this smaller niche community their should be a dialogue between the fans and the creators. Much like their is in the more widely liked Demon.

            I've already mentioned release pace between lines as an example. Another is community involvement. Rose, by simply being on this thread and posting a few times about the production method has been more involved and transparent about the creative part of the game then CAS has been since the game was released. Likewise, the last time Kickstarter backers got any update or news about further Kickstarter related products for Mummy was in September 2013, six months ago. There have been no article posts outside the Monday Meeting blog about Mummy since the teaser for Guildhalls in July (except to announce things like the pdf of the Ready Made characters and ST Screen for general sale). Demon's suffered minor slippage on one project, every single Mummy product has been delayed by at least a month and these delays are only growing bigger and bigger as the line goes on.

            This is only compounded by the fact that a lot of these upcoming products add whole new aspects to the game. Guildhalls allows for actual guild politics to be a thing on a global or regional scale. Good luck running Deceived plot with the resources we currently have for them. Or doing city based Mummy politics and Guild Structure without DC.

            The truly sad thing is that all the above things I've listed could be resolved or at least explained with a couple of forum posts an e-mail and a general news article on the Onyx Path site. It does not take a lot of effort to do that sort of thing, it might take a few hours to write such things. It would go a very, very long way with a community that (judging by the activity on this forum at least) is growing increasingly detached.

            ​OK, that's my vent out of the way. Rose, if you can poke Colin and get him to talk to us in same fashion that would be great.

            "There is no God in the Badlands." - Sunny

                Originally posted by Rose Bailey View Post
                reseru, I'm glad to stop in. Since Mummy is CAS's baby, I don't usually have a lot to contribute. But being the producer, I can always talk about production
                Right, I know you aren't involved with Mummy but it's good that someone is on here that's developed or written for Onyx Path!

                Hawthorn brought up some good points (especially how essential the supplements are to run different aspects of the game), and I don't want this thread to turn into what the 2014 Mummy Previews & Releases thread was/is, but I do want to say this one bit: more than anything else, I think all of our concerns aren't necessarily that the books have been delayed but rather that there's so little communication about them.

                CAS is a judge for the...Ennies? Or something, so he has a lot of RPG books to get through at the moment, and everyone pretty much has a day job, too. So, I understand books being delayed. But what makes us feel like the line has already been forsaken is that there's so little word about what's going on. Book of the Deceived was estimated to be released in Novemeber, and five months later (before we were finally told a writer dropped) all we knew was that it was being redlined. When we ask, "What's going on?" it's not, "I'm so angry why is this delayed how could they!" It's, "What happened, because I'm curious about a game I'm interested in and redlining for five months after estimated release seems odd."

                The Fallen World Chronicles isn't even on an OP schedule (though we know it's shooting for November), and I know more about it than a book that was estimated to be released five months ago! Between this forum and the old one there's been half a dozen threads about people wondering how to implement the Deceived into their Mummy game, and the best answer anyone has it, "Wait for Book of the Deceived."
