    Session 53

    The new temple rose quickly on the secluded island, built with magic and the labour of undead slaves. Sem-Huf and Yarikh continued to make the trip to and from Jakarta, monitoring their cults and tombs, the rest of the city's Arisen had relocated to the island.

    On the mainland, Sesu Ra had used his understanding of the city's geometric webs to locate Hetenatra's and Yarikh's tombs. Since Yarikh had been active for more than a year, he believed the latter would be an easy target with little Sekhem to protect his pillars. He set his Fasad in place to ambush the Arisen. Days passed, but there was no sign of the Su-Menent.

    As the others made ready for the Radiance's onslaught and prepared their allies, Aquila sat despondent in self-imposed exile. Her every plan had failed. She could feel her mastery of her Guild's powers weaken as Sem-Huf made contact with Mesen-Nebu in other parts of the world and arranged for her to be removed as a Guildmaster. Everything she had touched had fallen into ruin.

    Sesu Ra encountered another new Shuankhsen during his exploration of the area around Hetenatra's tomb. The creature was a nervous, twitchy young man named Kuuruuk, who proved to be extremely servile and offered himself up as Sesu Ra's slave at the earliest possible moment. Sesu Ra was well pleased with this, and ordered the newly arisen Shuankhsen to stand ready for the moment of the Radiance's attack. He intended to conscript all of the city's Shuankhsen to the defence of Jakarta.

    He then ordered his Fasad to return to him. He wielded Inscriptions Upon Flesh, gifting them with different Amkhata heads to better aid their efforts. Two were given falcon heads, to pierce magical disguises. Two were given jackal heads, so that they could track his enemies to their lairs. And Mark, the leader, was given the head of a lion to lead them into battle.

    This time, the hunt for Yarikh went a lot more smoothly. He had been shapeshifting whenever he entered or left his tomb, and the Fasad had been unable to recognise him as he walked through the streets around them. Now able to see his true form, they fell upon him the moment they saw that he was alone. As Sesu Ra feasted upon the Su-Menent's Sekhem, he was shocked to discover that Yarikh was not at the very end of his Descent. In fact, he still had plenty of Sekhem, more than enough to shield his pillars and deny Sesu Ra the prize he truly craved.

    Aquila's solitude was breached by Lia, the old woman they had encountered who served with the Radiance's cult. Lia said that, though their chance to destroy Fate itself had been lost, it was still possible to escape its grasp. The Radiance's influence could disrupt Fate's weaves and let them avoid the doom promised to each of them. She told Aquila that her eyes had been opened by the Radiance's new prophet. There was no need for it to destroy the world, it simply wanted to feed upon "the light". Humanity could continue, oblivious, bathing in the Radiance's glow without ever realising that it had replaced the sun. If Aquila helped them, they could be free from fate. When Aquila appeared unmoved, Lia mentioned that Hakef, the creature she called Gator, was corralled and in a place where the Radiance's servants could easily manipulate him. If Aquila helped them, she could have control of her own enemy, bending his power towards her own objectives and whims. Aquila wavered, then snarled at Lia to leave her be.

    Bast had tried, repeatedly, to contact Aquila. The other Arisen refused to answer her phone, and there appeared to be no way to reach her or her Sadikh. In the end she gave up, and called upon Secrets Ripped From Skies to ask for a final piece of advice before the Radiance arrived. Fate warned her that the power that ran like veins beneath Jakarta's streets would be all the Radiance needed to reach through the void to the world.

    When the vision ended, leaving Bast feeling vaguely confused, she found that the Prophet was standing next to her. He took her by the hand, and a moment later they were standing next to Aquila. For a moment it looked as if Aquila were about to attack the Prophet, but then she seemed to shrug and turned back to her brooding. Bast seized her shoulders and tried to talk her out of the fugue, but to no avail. Aquila told Bast that they would be better off without her.

    At this, the Prophet spoke. He told Aquila that she was indeed doomed, as she had been told, but not by any higher cosmic order. Her fate was preordained because of who she was. She strove for good and worthy goals, time and again, but she would always fail because she lacked the strength of character to succeed. He told her that, time and again, she would always choose violence, becoming worse than the monsters she fought, never considering that her failures might be due to her own actions rather than that of a hostile universe. She was her own fate, he told her, and it was an ugly one.

    "You're right," Aquila said, and she unleashed Dust Beneath Feet and fled deep into the earth. Down she plummeted, shedding her Sekhem as she did, until her body stopped frozen and inert in the darkness of the world.

    Bast stared down where her friend had once stood. She knew that Aquila would not be coming back.

        Session 54

        The temple was under attack. Kotira ordered the Su-Menent and Maa-Kep to join her in the central chamber. Bast joined her there, while the temple emptied of its defenders. The sounds of fighting carried to them, as Sem-Huf and Hetenatra lent their powers to aid the undead servants, ghosts, and mortal cultists who went forth to fight the Radiance's warriors.

        Standing in the chamber as the battle raged, Kotira began a low, dire chant. The name of the Herald of Forgotten Stars. Aqhat and Sekhet Bastet joined her as hierophants, Benerib and Mekshonat adding their strength as acolytes. The assembled Arisen spoke as the sounds of fighting grew closer and closer with every passing minute. And then it was done.

        The Herald's form left the Radiance's mortal followers dead and bleeding with nothing more than the sight of its fragmented, crawling form. They died in swathes and it fed upon their lives. Kudama Jav dragged an old woman, Lia, before the Herald and cut her throat, asking it what they needed to do to drive the Radiance back and keep it from the Earth.

        "Seek the help of Irem's lost rulers. Aid the betrayed and betrayers in this fight."

        At that moment, the Radiance reached out and took Jakarta in its grasp. Sesu Ra was the first to realise that something had gone wrong. He felt something wrench and tear inside him as the geometric network was wrenched from his control and funnelled into the void. The power of Jakarta, the life and energy of tens of millions of souls and the city in which they lived, was bent towards its centre. Outside the city, the Herald turned its manifold gaze and could see the corruption leaking forth as light was twisted and changed, consumed by an unseen void in the midst of the city.

        Sesu Ra stepped through fractured space to arrive at the heart of his geometric web, using the power of Ancestry of Forgotten Stars consumed from Yarikh's corpse. The Herald joined him moments later, its crawling chaos overwhelming the senses. They were standing at the foot of the national monument. Space was folded here with somewhere distant, somewhere impossibly cold. In the midst of this folded space was Nenheta.

        She looked at the horrors before her sadly. "The Radiance will set me free," she told them. "It will give me oblivion." And then she vanished into the cold and the dark.

        Sesu Ra charged after her without hesitation. The Herald though, seemed to pause on the threshold. Its eyes could pierce the frail dirt and concrete to see the chambers below, the chambers where the World's Wound pulsed in the midst of arcane geometry. But then the Herald wrenched itself away and followed the others through.

        They stood on the surface of a world of ice, its atmosphere frail and thin, cold beyond any comprehension. The pale light of the star they knew to be their sun gleamed in the sky, but so small that it was hardly bigger than any of the other stars in the cosmos. A part of Bast, buried deep within the Herald, knew this to be a planet on the outer edge of the solar system, the one called Pluto by modern men.

        The plain lay flat and bare around them, but Nenheta was nowhere in sight. In the distance they could see mountains of ice and nitrogen glaciers, while on their left rose a forest of dark spires. The spires were too regular to be natural, concentric rings rising to a peak at the very centre. It was the only way Nenheta could have gone. The Old Gods attacked.

        Light bloomed in the sky, throwing the shadowed plain into stark relief. A coruscating nimbus of colour marked the Radiance's arrival above their heads. It was too bright to look at, but even from the corners of their eyes they could see creatures moving amongst the light, descending towards them. The Radiance's children came to wipe these troubling undead from their parent's path. There were blind worms from the outer dark, creatures of light and shadow, horrors that defied all logic with their physiology and form.

        One such worm slammed into Sesu Ra. He fought back with tooth and fist. The two struggled back and forth, but fuelled by all the Sekhem he had consumed there was little hope for the alien being. He tore it apart and looked up, to find that the Herald had carved a terrible swathe through the attacking demons with little pause. The mixed forms of Bast and Aqhat, empowered by their union with so many other Arisen, could not be stopped. The children of the Radiance met their doom at the broken limbs of the Herald in amongst the spires.

        But the waves of creatures were never-ending, and the Herald would not be long for this world. The worms poured down in waves. Then they felt Sekhem flare around them, and other Arisen began to join the battle. Sesu Ra watched as Sem-Huf charged a creature moments before it could cut him off from a path to the central spire, pinning the creature to the ground with a flaming spear. Meteors began to run down, smiting horrors from the sky before they could reach the Arisen, as Hetenatra added her might to the attack. And Sesu Ra watched with pleasure as Kuuruuk and Shabtisk joined the fight, watched over by Iwseck, her identity disguised with a relic stolen from Aquila's tomb.

        The Herald reached the central spire, rending apart the creatures that assailed it. There was still no sign of Nenheta. As its eyes surveyed the battle, it saw a man it recognised approaching. One of the Deceived. The one who bore the Shan'iatu called Siranuthis made his way through the spires from the opposite direction to the way the Arisen had come, and began to ascend the spire. Demons attacked him, but the Herald recognised an ally and destroyed them. He reached the top and looked up towards the light, and began to sing. Despite the poorly lacking atmosphere, every being present could hear the song. The Radiance certainly did, and it struck.

        Nenheta flickered in and out among the spires, striking at the Arisen and Deceived in equal measure, gone before they could attack back. The Shan'iatu named Tutkepertanu appeared among the pillars and moved to defend the other Deceived. An enormous creature like a fleshy snake struck at Sesu Ra, splaying its body open like a cobra's hood to reveal dozens of milky white eyes, trying to compel him to turn on his allies. The Unbending Spine refused to give in to the creature's overwhelming power and struck back, his fists tearing gaping wounds in its form.

        Above the battlefield, a woman with a snake's head, composed entirely from stars, tore at a winged being with flaming wings. The Herald recognised Akenakhi writing in the woman's grip. As tempting as it was to let him die, the Herald struck and tore the woman apart before she could finish him off.

        A great tower of basalt began to grow from the snow, Chthonic Beings appearing en masse in its shadow. They raged across the battlefield, striking mostly at the far more numerous demons, the various mummies scrambling to stay out of their way.

        There was a surge of power in the centre of the battlefield, where the will of the Deceived was pitted against that of a star god. A creature of towering flame appeared, bow in hand, and began to smite any creatures that dared to try and approach those on the central spire. Flaming bolts rained down upon the battlefield, destroying a demon with every shot.

        Slowly, the Deathless and Lifeless fighters alike began to form a circle around the central spire. Back to back, they fought off waves of the Radiance's children. Tutkepertanu hurled a meteor and obliterated the blind-eyed snake. Nenheta threw herself at the Deceived, seeking to break their concentration and disrupt their ritual, but as she charged towards the singing Deathless she was seized by the throat by the being of fire that stood by their side. For a moment she struggled in its grip, and then she was gone in a flash of flame.

        There was a moment of pause as the light blazed brighter and brighter in the sky. The Sesu Ra felt his eyeballs began to boil in his skull. And then the moment passed, and the Radiance began to pour down out of the sky and into the chest of the Deceived. And then everything was dark and still.

        Everything, that is, save for the glorious creature of fire and magic. It turned its bow upon the Shuankhsen without a moment's pause. Kuuruuk was blasted to pieces before he even realised the fighting was not yet done. Shabtisk turned to flee, but the Herald was upon him before he could reach the portal. Sesu Ra went to run, only to watch the Herald tear across space towards the earth. He and Iwseck followed its path through Twilight, appearing back on Earth in Bast's tomb, staring at the Herald.

        The creature only had moments left before it separated into its component parts, but those moments would be more than enough to destroy him. Sesu Ra knew that if he fought, Iwseck would fight too, and they both would die. With the mask she was wearing though, there was a strong chance she would be mistaken for Aquila. He fell to his knees before the Herald.

        "I accept your judgement."

        The Herald tore him apart, and the spell ended. Bast took Yama-Het's Talisman from his corpse. She spared a moment for Iwseck, thanking the woman she thought was Aquila for coming to her senses, and then rushed back to the national monument. She remembered the chamber she had seen as the Herald, and found its entrance. There, at its centre, was the World's Wound. This time, she would not make the mistake of trying to keep it safe. She summoned the bladed tail of the Exalted Sha, and destroyed the Bane Uter where it lay.

        By the time she returned to her tomb, Sesu Ra's corpse had vanished. She made her way back to the island temple, took a seat at its centre, and began to meditate. She was not left to her solitude for long. An explosion rocked the temple, and she dragged her weary self up to do battle once more.

        A helicopter hovered over the island, raining down fire. The Shan'iatu Siranuthis stood aboard, howling fire and blasphemy as he swore that he would drive the last Arisen from Jakarta. Bast summoned a meteor to strike him from the sky, but he dispelled the Utterance with a lazy gesture. Then Aqhat appeared beside her, vomiting forth a horde of scarabs. They flew into the helicopter and devoured its pilot's eyes, and the howling Shan'iatu descend in flames. It strode from the wreckage unharmed, hurling fire and spells. Sekhem flared as the two warriors of the Su-Menent charged into battle. They fought magic with axe and scorpion tail, and for the first time Siranuthis found himself wounded and bleeding and on the verge of defeat. He summoned flaming wings and ascended out of their reach, still hurling spells down upon their heads. Bast joined him in the air with wings of her own summoned through the Exalted Sha. As the two angels warred, immortal blood raining down on the beach below, the Shan'iatu realised he could not win this fight. His body scattered into sand, taking himself beyond Bast's reach.

        The temple still stood, damaged but not broken, not defiled by a heretic's presence. Only those Arisen who had been part of the Herald had returned from Pluto's surface. Mekshonat, Benerib, Kotira Nahan, Aqhat, and Sekhet Bastet. Where the other guilds had fallen or failed, the Su-Menent and their closest allies stood triumphant.

            Epilogue

            By the end of 2013, the nome of Jakarta lay in ruins. The city itself lay in the grip of the Deceived, the dark empire of their cult spreading through the halls of power and driving the Arisen away from any power or influence. The mummies of the city had seen their cults shattered, and those that remained existed only among the poor and deprived, the criminals and outcasts. They had fallen far.

            The Sesha-Hebsu and Mesen-Nebu have no presence in the city. Hetenatra and Sem-Huf were trapped on the frozen surface of a distant planet at the battle's end. When their descents ended, there was a chance that their cults could return them to Earth by raising them through their canopic jars. Nevertheless, they have not been seen since the Radiance was vanquished.

            Of the Tef-Aabhi, only Sesu Ra remains. He still plays the role of Guildmaster, although he has little influence among the other Arisen. Bast refuses to trust him and takes efforts to stymie his efforts to return himself to influence. For his part, he chooses to endure this exile, knowing that in time the others' memories will fail and that he will have his chance to move among them once more, a wolf in disguise. The Deceived remain his focus, and he does what he can to coordinate both Shuankhsen and Arisen against them. Thus far, this has had little impact.

            Benerib and Mekshonat have declared the Maa-Kep's support for the Su-Menent cause. Like the other Arisen of the Nome, they have made new tombs around the island temple, and have begun to build local cults to support them in future descents.

            Kotira, Aqhat, and Bast of the Su-Menent rule from their temple island. They have been forced from their city, but their fortress has stood against the might of the Deceived and their mystical might is singularly capable of defeating their opponents in open battle. While the Deceived rule their empire, the Su-Menent plot in their temple and lay the foundations for a great crusade, a bloody campaign to take back what they have lost.

            In the alleys and slums of the city, feral packs of Shuankhsen and Fasad hunt those Mummies they can find. Drawn to the city by the promised power of Sesu Ra's corrupted geometric network, they are now bereft of that strength and forced into the shadows by the Deceived. Reduced from a ravening horde to a pack of scavengers, they watch the warring deathless from the darkness and wait for their chance in the conflict that is bound to come.

            And somewhere in the dark of the world, a new Shuankhsen is being born. One without memory, with no knowledge of who she was, just a hunger in her soul and the barest memories of a name. "Aquila".

                            Actually getting to use the Herald in a fight for the first time in the chronicle (remember that Bast has been carrying the Utterance around all campaign) was incredible It's so logistically difficult to pull off, but it was great to get that payoff at last.

                                    It's less the mystical power/armies of undead that the Su-Menent might have, and more that Aqhat and Sekhet Bastet are the only two Arisen capable of interacting with Twilight (and thus hurting the Temakh in Blessed Is The God-King mode) without relying on some kind of Utterance-based "buff".
