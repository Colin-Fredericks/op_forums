    Sutek is the guy that everyone SHOULDVE been worshipping, had he not been horrifically maimed to create other Gods. It seems that the Deceived weren't the only mummies who identify with Sutek--everyone did, and thus is possible ALL the shaniatu took part in Azar's murder. Also, Aset is talked about, but the Namesless Falcon in this book was Heru. So, the idea of the five nameless gods being all equal, and transcendent, has now been rather... muddied. We finally solve the shaniatu/Judge problem, and now we run into the Nameless Gods problem. Besides Anpu and Sutek, the nameless gods are a bit confusing now. Especially if, like Mal said am-hotep, some gods are named after other gods (Aset).
