                            Originally posted by Gabby View Post
                            So wait, where are the rules for vestiges dampening or overwriting unease sybaris, because I don't know that I've seen them anywhere. Yet they're referenced in the mirage sidebar and referred to like they're a known thing.
                            Yep in the updated version (released yesterday 9th) of DC the rules relating to Vestiges negating Unease Sybaris are detailed in a large sidebar on the last page of the Storytelling chapter (Chapter 4).

                            To address the point reseru brings up -

                            Controlled Sybaris is useful, because it allows the Arisen (and their Cult) to tailor the mindset and disposition of an area to something useful for the Mummy e.g. Cults against Death or Signs and Omens. As you say, such things are useful to make sure a Cult is maintained and finds easy recruits that it can grow by inducing into its ranks.

                            The problem is uncontrolled Sybaris in an area. If people keep on having the Flood of Duat result a lot of "odd stuff" is going to start happening in an area. This draws mortal attention in a not-so-useful way. Cults are at their most effective when they are secret after all.

                            Worse, anyone who knows about Mummy's such as Last Dynasty Inc (or several other Hunter compacts like the Ascended Ones or AKD) can be alerted and drawn to the area. Bringing real peril to the Arisen and their cult in a more immediate fashion.

                            Its also worth noting that while its Sybaris is good for the recruitment drive and omens it might produce in mortal followers it also has a notably detrimental effect on sanity. Gibbering Cultists have their uses but you don't want your whole cult made up of them.

                            For Nomes controlling Sybaris has to be an issue. If only one Arisen is around then the point you make about Sothic Turns and descents does apply, enough Arisen are not awakening close enough to one another most of the time for it to be an issue. However, in a Nome like DC with 24 Arisen who seem to be awaken fairly often even outside Sothic turns to guide their Cults, Sybaris would be happening more often then is healthy for the Nomes mortal population. Hence the need for things like a steady flow of Vestiges in the city to anchor to Lifewebs and monuments to bleed away Sybaris.

                            "There is no God in the Badlands." - Sunny

                                                        I reread the sybaris rules, and really I don't think unease sybaris is that bad.

                                                        If you've never been in the Mummy's tomb, never interacted with the Mummy or its relics itself, or never encounter one of its cultists while the Mummy is awake then the effects are one step lower (meaning, less intense/powerful). Considering that unease sybaris is directly correlated to the Mummy's sekhem, the degree of sybaris will go down an entire step after the first day, more or less. Then over the course of its descent, the sybaris will not only become less intense but the radius will decrease accordingly, too.

                                                        So imagine it. The Mummy awakens, some people near its tomb get depressed for a couple days, maybe someone scrawls some Iremite gibberish in a back alley, and then things return to relative normalcy. In a hypothetical person's diary, it's just a couple entries where they say, "Dear Diary, today I felt like crap. What's the point of living because life doesn't last forever?" Then a few days later they reread their diary and think, "Yeah, I don't know what got into me. I was just in a weird mood"
