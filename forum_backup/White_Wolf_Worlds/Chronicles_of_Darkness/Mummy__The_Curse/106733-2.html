            Ever the optimist Reseru.

            I would not call Book of the Deceived as in its "home stretch" until its in layout with all the art in.

            Considering that Deceived has been "in Editing" for 12 weeks, it seems to have fallen prey the development slippage that the whole line seems to suffer from endemically.

            To give you an idea the estimated "Editing Time" for M20, the largest book that White Wolf/Onyx Path has ever produced is estimated at around 8 weeks from now (having been in editing for about 2/3 before that announcement was made). Rich even mentioned that on average a splat book should be about 4 weeks in editing, with new core books being around 6ish.

            So Deceived seems to have already taken 3 times as long in editing as is normal for a book of its kind. Really the development cycle for this book has been nothing but "cursed" you might say. 8 months in redlines and now 3+ in editing with art only going out to artists this week for pieces beyond the chapter starting art full-page spreads.

            Thank the Judges I'm basically beyond caring about the development issues that wrack the Mummy line. Though apparently not enough to occasionally post semi-whining forum comments it seems :P

                        Its just a person thing for me, but I consider Mummy the most neglected line after Geist.

                        I consider Promethean as "more loved" simply because its got its entire book run out and has all the subject matter the game engages with covered by its book line. In addition, its getting a Second Edition which, if its anything like Strix will consolidate all the best bits from the various books and add new, interesting and streamlined stuff. I actually really like that post Second Edition, its entirely likely that Promethean will suffer not one bit for being run out of a single book.

                        Mummy on the other hand, well, I've made my view about the way the lines development has been handled known at length on several threads. To sum up, Mummy's been out 18 months and the line is no where near finished its original book run and the games content is still incomplete.

                        Compare this to Demon (which is a valid comparison I feel, as they were both "new line" Kickstarted books in the new world of darkness). In the 8 months since its preview release to the Kickstarter backers (and 6 months to the general public) Demon has managed to get all its Kickstarter books out. The Demon lines final book going into production as we speak for likely a January-March release next year. Meaning the entire line is going to be put to likely to be put to bed inside 12 months.

                        Meanwhile, Mummy is only about half way through its Kickstarter reward books (not even that really) and every thing book in the line seems to undergo at least a 6 month delay. At this point I seriously would not be surprised if we see the Beast core book preview pdf for Kickstarter backers get out before the Mummy line has finished. At its current rate Sothis will be released around July next year. Rio around April maybe. Dreams might as well be this time next year.

                        I need to stop now, thinking about this stuff again is actually making me angry...
