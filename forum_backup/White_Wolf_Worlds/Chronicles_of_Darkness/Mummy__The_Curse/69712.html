It's February now and I was hoping if there's any more word on anything? I love this game and there's a number of AP threads out there now, but I'd love some developers and writers to talk up how great it is!

                                  I feel Demon: the Descent was successful for a lot of reasons, not the least of which is that Matt and Rose and some writers were very engaged with its fans. I guess now, for me, it's just coming down to simple jealousy
                                  To my knowledge the following Mummy releases can be expected in the next 10 months or so for sure:

                                  Cursed Necropolis: DC (its in editing, earliest we are going to see it is end of March)
                                  Book of the Deceived (in second phase of redlining after rewrites)

                                  It's highly likely (but by no means guaranteed) that the following will also be released in 2014:

                                  Cursed Necropolis: Rio (Don't know the status of this one)
                                  Dreams of Avarice (A writer was decided upon on contracted in November last year according to Rich T's e-mail updates for Mummy Kickstarter backers, nothing heard since. I really wish they would update us on this via an e-mail at least.)

                                  The following are a big MAYBE for this year and very likely going to be out in 2015:

                                  Sothis Ascends
                                  Mummy Novel
                                  Fiction Anthology

                                  The other books have been discussed or teased, but we don't even know if they are on the schedule for next year yet:

                                  Book of Going Westward
                                  Book about Apothesis

                                  The former of these two was talked about at the end of the Kickstarter (as something the Dev's might do if Mummy sold well enough).
                                  The latter was something that CAS talked about in an interview on the Onyx Path website. Though to be clear he may have possibly been referring to Book of the Deceived or Sothis Ascends.

                                  In a previous thread we discussed if Mummy actually NEEDED any more books after these ones. The general thought was that a book to expand the Cult rules would be good. I believe there was also some talk about doing a sorta "Antagonists Book" to expand on the Lifeless a bit. Since at the moment the Shaukshan (spelling...gah) are a bit...one note as villains.

                                  I strongly agree with Reseru about the handling of Mummy following its initial release. Namely the lack of communication between CAS and the fans

                                  Rose and Matt have been supporting the Demon fanbase excellently by regularly staying in contact, answering questions and even incorporating fan feedback into their revisions and future products. OK maybe they have spoiled those of us that have been backing Demon as well, but I've found myself infinitely more well endeared to Demon due to the fact that I know there is a dialogue between the creators and fans.

                                  CAS on the other hand popped in to say "Hi I'm on the forums." and then answered all of about 3 fan queries (one of which was posted by me as a hypothesis about the upcoming Book of the Deceived) and has been absent ever since (going on about 5 months).

                                  In the meantime the preview for Deceived not only failed to materialize in November but failed to appear in 2013 at all. Likewise, the DC preview mentioned on this very thread for "the next week or so" (made about 2 weeks ago) has once again failed to happen.

                                  Now, I might be being completely unreasonable, CAS might just be a very busy guy, he might not have the sort of time free that Rose or Matt spend on various forums chatting with fans. If so fair enough, I don't know his circumstances and it would be wrong of me to draw any conclusions without having all the facts.

                                  Not to flog a dead horse but there is the matter of product release issues as well.

                                  I know Onyx Path has a strict policy of: "Every date is just an estimate/guideline and we want to give our developers enough time to get every product perfect because they are free-lancers and are doing this out of love."

                                  Still even with that in mind every single Mummy release has been delayed horribly. The Core book (not counting Kickstarter copies) was about 3 months delayed. Guildhalls was about 6 months late. Ready Made characters took forever to get to pdf let alone print. DC was originally estimated for October, Deceived for November. I thought adding 4 months to these release dates would practical on my part. At current DC may be as late as April in release (6 months delayed, its been in "editing" for 6 weeks, what are they using to edit it an old fashion tip-ex machine) and Deceived is looking to be an April release at earliest (more likely May, making it 7 months delayed).

                                  Now, I'm fully aware other lines in the NWoD have also suffered delays Blood and Smoke was 4 months delayed after all. Likewise, the first Demon supplement has already been pushed back by 2/3 months. However, all this stuff was mentioned at least a month before the original release estimate by the Devs in question. They gave explanations for these things, talked about what was being polished and improved and changed.

                                  With Mummy its just been the case that Rich T's Monday Morning blog has a single sentence (in some cases a single word) saying where the Mummy project is at current. No communication to the fans, no talk about how the projects developing, no explanation. Just that brief update. That's it.

                                  I'm a fan of Mummy, I'm running a game of it at current and have been for damn near a year. Hand's down I've been looking forward to Book of the Deceived since it came on the docket a year and a half ago. Now I'm just fed up waiting for it. It was in the first round of redlining for 6 weeks and in rewrites for another 6 weeks (and I see no indication it won't spend the best part of the next two months in redlining again), I appreciate that CAS is more involved with this book and maybe wants it "perfect" but at this point I', afraid if its anything less then "prefect" then I'm going to be so embittered by the prolonged wait that I can't be anything but disappointed in it.

                                  However, all that enthusiasm I had that's got piled into frustration could have easily been stoked by much higher heights if CAS could have been a little more fan-friendly and post previews, stoked some fan conversation and hinted at some stuff.

                                  OK, I've cast all my stones, had my gump and kicked all hell out of these old issues. You may all now commence retaliatory strikes as you see fit. I'll take my lumps.

                                  "There is no God in the Badlands." - Sunny

                                              I've said it before and I'll say it again: Mummy is a good game. Its just not a complete game.

                                              I honestly believe its head and shoulders above some other games in terms of setting and themes (Werewolf springs to mind but I've never been a fan of "shaman-the-spirit-cop" theme anyway).

                                              You can't really understand how the Guilds work without Guildhalls (not to mention Bane relics, they should have been in the core), you can't really know how Nomes work without the DC book, good luck running a Decieved NPC (and not just making their rules up) without Book of the Deceived.

                                              Basically, it a very incomplete game out of the core. As such, more then maybe any other game line it REQUIRES splat books for its full potential to be reached. Which is very annoying when every single release is at least 4 months delayed.

                                              On the matter of Mummy being a "failure". If were talking about sales I'd bet that its the lowest selling NWoD game after Giest and maybe Promethean, I won't be expecting many Mummy stretch goals in the upcoming Dark Era's kickstarter lets put it like that.

                                              We can take heart that thanks the Kickstarter it is getting decent line support, in so far as its guaranteed 6 splat books (Guildhalls, Ready Made Characters, DC, Deceived, Rio, Avarice) which is more then Giest or Promethean got (admittedly 4 of those are only due to the kickstarter). Plus its getting two fiction books (Novel and Fiction Anthology) which I will likely end up mining for plot and story, so kinda splat books for me at least. Also two other splats...sorta: Mortal Remains (for Hunter) and Dark Eras.

                                              If we are going for more abstract measures of "failure" well there are a few we could point to:

                                              1) The game suffers for being released in NWoD 1.0 rules. Simply the old rules are weaker then the GM update ones. Likewise, GM was announced around the same time as Mummy and a lot of people "wrote it off" when they found out it was not going to be in the GM rules (about three quarters of my local gaming community in my case). Mummy is a game that would have benefited from the Condition rules a lot.

                                              2) The Kickstarter ran 23 days instead of the usual 30 and took place over Christmas (should have waited until January). It was mishandled terribly in the last week (new years) due to basically real-life getting in the way of the people running it. As a result it did not get the sort of money it could have.

                                              3) It got very little hype. Once again this ties in with the first point of people going off it before it even came out on rules basis. CAS did basically nothing to plug the game on various interactive forums and by the time of release even the Mummy Blog (which started out with regular updates) had pretty much gone silent. Contrast this with Demon, where Rose and Matt were EVERYWHERE on gaming websites plugging the game, not to mention running regular updates on Demons content and giving us a free pdf rough draft when the Kickstarter started.

                                              4) Mummy tried to do something different. People mostly don't like change (could I be more general?). The whole player/storyteller divide in the middle of the book, the use of a hard cannon for the game and the reversal of the traditional power mechanic (Sekhem goes down) also turned a lot of people off it. On top of this Mummy made no effort to include itself in the wider NWoD. It very much feels like Mummy is in its own parallel universe to the rest of the games. Once again to contrast Demon, which dedicated a couple of pages to just how Demon's interact with all the other supernaturals of the NWoD except...Mummy's. Which pretty much reinforces the idea that Mummy is off in its own sandbox playing alone.

                                              5) Not sure if this one extends beyond my local gaming community but I'll list it anyway. Mummy is seen as a bit...OTT by many. Its Dark Plup for sure and you can get into some really meaty character stuff with memory (which has been the focus of my game). However, most people seem to look at it, see how powerful Mummy's can be with a bit of XP and how powerful a lot of their antagonists are and get turned away. This is even more apparent when you realize that Mummy's basically have no Masquerade equivalent, make people believe in the supernatural and have powers that are so flashy and epic that people could not help but notice them. All of which I will admit does break away from the "stay hidden" aspect of all the other game lines.

                                              6) The supplements have been so-so. Ready Made Characters read like a Vampire splat (at least to me) and did nothing to reinforce the themes of the game (save for the Maa-Keep character). Guildhalls had a load of good info in it that could have done with being in Core but it was a mish-mash of tone and mechanics. It suffered from having a different writer for each chapter. Worse it was obvious the authors had not tired to really talk to each other to see what other people were doing. Hence a sever lack of uniformity and some guilds getting more then others e.g. Sesha Hesbu vs Tef Aahebi anyone?

                                              The way I have come to look at is is that each new release makes Mummy a more complete game with more stuff for ST's to entice players with and widen their games plots and themes with. However, I'm fully expecting that we will get our 6 splat books and 2 fiction books and then Mummy will be expected to go live in a corner (much like Giest and Promethean) and get the of couple of page pdf thing now and then after 2015. IF we are really lucky they will update Mummy to GM rules as a "Chronicle" book around 2017 (keeping with the idea that they are doing 2 Chronicle books a year from this year forward), which I might add I would buy in a heartbeat.

                                              I mean honestly, compare the enthusiasm, hype, sales, communication, kickstarters and forum activity between Mummy and Demon, you'll notice a BIG difference. I'm pretty sure Demon's been the most successful game for NWoD since Changeling to give you an idea. Only Werewolf the Apoc and Exalted outrank it for Kickstarter earnings. It was only planned to have 3 splats (Translation Guide, Player Guide and Storyteller Guide) but the kickstarter alone added another 4 to that (Story Matrix, Heirs to Hell, Seattle City Book, Demon Seed Collection). Mortal Remains (for Hunter) and Dark Era's also providing an additional 2 splats on top of that. I will not be surprised if the 2015 release schedule adds at least another 2 (Antagonists Book + ?) splat books to the game line. Meanwhile I think any Mummy releases we see on the new 2015 schedule will be simply delayed products from this year or things like the fiction books.

                                              Sorry, did not mean this to be a massive downer. Just spelling out my take on the situation and reinforcing my belief that the game has made a few mistakes (both pre and post release) and has been mishandled.

                                              Once again feel free to throw rocks at this grumbling bitter old man.

                                              "There is no God in the Badlands." - Sunny
