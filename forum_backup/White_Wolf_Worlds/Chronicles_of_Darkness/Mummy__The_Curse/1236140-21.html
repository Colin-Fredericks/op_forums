                The Mekharr guard things from all trespassers. Duat is, at least nominally, the domain of gods and greater beings. Its Guardians will attack anyone who gets too close to the thing they're protecting, and it's only through the invocation of Anpu that the Arisen get an exception.

                Shades aren't explicitly souls, they're drawn toward Duat (a storehouse of Sekhem, which they consume for power), and they don't have a free pass for the guards like mummies do; whether they're supposed to be there or not, they don't get the VIP treatment that would allow them to get through the gates unscathed.

                It's up to you what Duat's intentions are as regards shades in your game, but they have no top-level purpose in its functioning that would give the Mekharr at its gates an overarching reason not to treat them like any other intruders.

                        Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                        I m searching the core book, but i can t find any source of bashing damage for Cults (the same for Krewes of Geist).
                        What events could cause this kind of damage?
                        As described, bashing damage to an organization's health represents its membership being distracted from its principles and unity, in contrast to the direct prelude to mutiny that lethal damage represents or aggravated damage representing the group starting to lose membership (whether directly by them running away or spiritually by their having lost enough faith in the cause that they're only nominally part of the cult).

                        Deviant's conspiracy systems put bashing damage as being caused by other conspiracies engaging in sabotage (which also consumes one of the conspiracy's conspiracy actions and is mainly contrasted against the lethal damage of direct war), with conspiracies attached to PC Devoted adding basic success on conspiracy surveillance rolls or a Devoted with the Devoted Authority or Devoted Control Condition blocking a conspiracy action relating to a Node they can influence.

                        Basically, if it takes the cult's attention away from its Doctrines but doesn't actually make them doubt the cause or the commitment of its leadership, it's bashing damage.

                                Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                                I can t find in the core if Duat s fiends suffer from Essence bleed.
                                They function like Amkhata except where noted, meaning they lose their Rank in Essence per day that they're manifested, then start losing Corpus, then collapse back into Twilight with full Essence, whereupon they can gather Sekhem for another full-body emergence or use their Manifestations to act less dramatically.
                                Also, how do you can banish them back to Duat?
                                Same ways as normal ephemeral beings: deprive them of their sustaining Conditions until they run out of Essence and then destroy them, or use magic that banishes them (either them specifically or a category of being that includes them).

                                Since every use of the Idol Manifestation creates the Open Condition at the depiction it transports them to and the fiend's other means of gaining Essence require its presence to work, this largely means destroying any depiction of the fiend, beating it down, and kicking it the rest of the way back with rituals or means of attack that hit beings in Twilight to kill it again.
