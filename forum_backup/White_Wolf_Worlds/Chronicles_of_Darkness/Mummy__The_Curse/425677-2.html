    Originally posted by Azahul View Post
    To be clear, it's not the phrase "new Lifeless" that is getting my hackles up. It's "new Mummies". There's plenty of room for new kinds of undead being in the world of Mummy, let's bear in mind that the term "Lifeless" includes the Amkhat, not just humanoid mummified creatures. I keep thinking of guardian statues and the permutations of the demons of Duat, for example. But when it comes to Mummies, I can't see what non-Iremite versions could possibly add to the game.

    Although in your example, yes, I would say that's what the Fasad are for. If only because the phrase "Hound of the Shuankhsen" is almost word for word the description given of the core book's sample Fasad.

    Also, I think TyrannicalRabbit is referring to the fact that the default position of an Arisen towards any form of Lifeless is condescending hostility, not faith-shattering awe.
    Exactly this. Also while there are elements of wonder and awe with the accomplishments of Irem, even with consideration that it was a far far more brutal time than we the modern person and modern morality has to deal with. The setting is often a rumination on the trap of power and power vs identity,etc. It's simply not a setting built on a necessity to hammer into a player's head the idea that Irem wasn't anything unique and just badwrong. There are plenty of space for modern visions to look upon the necromancy, the brutality, the lies, the deceptions and conquests of Irem without bashing people over the head with Deathless of other civilizations. That the developer and co. stuck to their guns on this has been a relief. And I say that as someone who played Mummy, Mummy 2nd edition and Mummy: the Resurrection.

                                                    As Christian A mentioned, we have a rough blurb of this book and what to expect.

                                                    Other then that, Reseru referenced Gentlemen Gamer mentioning Mummy eating antagonists in the Third Turn.

                                                    We also have mention of Relic Architecture in DC and that it would be expanded in a future supplement. Considering Sothis seems a better place for that then Rio I'm hedging my bets that it will contain said rules expansion. Also from DC the White Jackal has an Utterance that still has not been published in any Mummy book, so I think that's likely to be in Sothis as well.

                                                    I also remember an interview with CAS (might actually be under the Mummy tab on the Onyx Path website) that mentioned about adding new Lifeless who were knock-offs of the Rite of Return in Sothis.

                                                    That's all that's been discussed or teased by the books or the devs I think.

                                                    Now, with that out of the way the question is what would I like to see/hope to see?

                                                    1) Bane relics.
                                                    2) Demons of Duat as antagonists.
                                                    3) Sorcerers (need someone to summon said demons)
                                                    4) More relics
                                                    5) Some wordcount (not a huge amount, half a page sidebar would do) about running into other immortal characters across the turns/descents who are not Mummy's. Vampires and the three main types of Immortal from WoD:Immortals mainly.

                                                    For that last point it mainly comes out of a comment on the old forums (or possibly an RPG.net) thread that was running while Mummy was in development that had a dev mention that the Purified from Immortals got a little quote or something about the Arisen and it was a statement of abject horror. Also, though I am less sure of this, I think before wordcount got cut down (so first draft of the book likely before redlines) there was going to be a sidebar or something on other immortals. So ever since I heard that got cut from the core I've kinda been hoping to see it get put somewhere and Sothis seems like the last place it could go.
