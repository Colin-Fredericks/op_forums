And any living soul who witnessed it was either on the receiving end or consigned directly to Duat. Then there's the implication from Book of the Deceived that the world was inherently more magical in the time of Irem, that rituals on the scale of the Rite may not actually be possible in the modern magic climate. It's possible that a change to the fundamental laws of physics in the intervening time may render it impossible for a later civilisation to replicate a past one's technological achievements.
