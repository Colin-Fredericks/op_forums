    Oh, and the relics.

    The Serpent Named (4 dot Uter)
    Size 2, Durability 2, Structure 4

    The Serpent Named appears to be a mummified snake, carefully coiled around the haft of a ceremonial flail. Its head, mouth agape and eye sockets staring, rests at the base of the haft. The presence of the delicate animal remains makes it impossible to wield the flail as a weapon, and it must be carried with care. It looks as if the bandages that bind it were once dyed a brilliant array of colours, but they have all but faded.

    Power: The bearer of the Serpent Named may project their soul through the relic, taking an instant action and a point of Willpower. It emerges like a whisper, a sliver of consciousness cast into the void of Neter-Khetet. They manifest in their soul-form, a mystic representation of their Decree animal. This spirit form is represented by a ghost. This ghost has a Power, Finesse, and Resistance of 1, an Essence Pool equal to their Decree Pillar (mortals get 0), and the Spirit Prowess Numen.
    If the bearer knows someone’s true name, however, the powers become even greater. They can call out the soul of anyone whose true name they know as an instant action. A true named soul has its attributes determined in the normal fashion (Power equals highest of Strength, Intelligence, or Presence, Finesse equals highest of Wits, Manipulation, or Dexterity, Resistance is highest of Stamina, Resolve, or Composure). They get 10 Essence, the Spirit Prowess, Regeneration, and Old Age (always 10 for Arisen, partly because they’re actually that old and partly because added protections layered onto their soul).
    In either case, if destroyed, the bearer is forcibly returned to their body and lose all Willpower and Pillars.
    As long as someone's spirit is absent from their body, their body cannot take any actions. Arisen seem to return to henet, their sahu vanishing and leaving behind their dead khat. No Sekhem can be detected in the corpse, to all extents and purposes they appear to have finished their Descent. Mortals appear to have fallen into a coma, and it is impossible to detect brain activity until their spirit returns. The spirit remains separate from the body until an akhet can be found in Neter-Khetet that will return the spirit to the corporeal world.

    Curse: The bearer’s true name becomes vulnerable to other occultists. All attempts to discover it gain a +3 bonus.

    Note: Aquila realised that, based on Akenakhi's description of her actions as the Tribune and his description of what the Serpent Named could do, she must have at one point known enough about true name to determine the true names of the plague victims aboard her ship.

    Face of Deception (1 dot Regia)
    Size 2, Durability 4, Structure 6

    This mask appears to have been cast from a gold and bronze alloy in the style of a Greek theatre mask. Beyond the wide, open eyes and mouth, there are no details that give any indication as to what the mask is meant to represent.

    Powers: While wearing the mask, the bearer has no recognisable features. Any who try to describe them will be unable to recall any details about their person that could lead to determining the wearer's identity, whether that be their height, skin colour, what they were wearing, even their sex or gender. It is still easy enough to remember the actions they take however, and nothing prevents someone who knows the wearer carries the mask from putting two and two together to figure out who this mysterious stranger might be.

    Curse: The bearer suffers a -1 penalty on all Memory checks, as it becomes harder to maintain a hold on their identity while hidden in such a fashion. Mesen-Nebu are exempt.

        Session 32

        After the meeting with Aquila, Akenakhi returned to his tomb. As he turned into the alley approaching his tomb's entrance, he saw a woman sitting in the middle of the street in a pink sleeveless dress. She smiled up at him, and said "Hello Akenakhi. I've been waiting to meet you for some time. My name is Iwseck."

        Akenakhi realised that she was holding something in her lap. With an impish smile, Iwseck turned it around so he could see that it was Nenheta's head. Her features were twisted in a rictus of pain and her hair matted with her own blood. "She promised me something, Akenakhi. Something she couldn't deliver on. Now, I don't like violence, but I think she was protecting you. And I don't like my friends to lie to me like that."

        She climbed to her feet and tossed Nenheta's head into the nearby gutter. She explained that Akenakhi had failed her as well, but she wasn't angry. He couldn't have known he was working for her. But now, in no uncertain terms, she expected him to deliver Sesu Ra's canopic jars to her immediately. If he didn't, she would kill every mortal close to him and desecrate his tomb, and that would just be to make sure she had his attention. She wouldn't go as easy on him as Nenheta had. But, on the other hand, Akenakhi could use her help for his little experiment in freedom. Sesu Ra was part of the very establishment he hated, and she could always resurrect Nenheta to take the fall for him once it was done. Just so long as her own name stayed out of it, she would be happy. Akenakhi promised to deliver the canopic jars to her, and she walked away smiling.

        After that disturbing experience, Akenakhi turned his attention back to Sutek's Ladder. It didn't have much more to tell him than what he had already discovered through his own memories, thanks to his heavy focus on restoring his memories. What it did tell him was that he and Nenheta had never been lovers, that tidbit must have simply been a lie she thought would make him easier to manipulate.

        Meanwhile, Sesu Ra did some digging around the docks and discovered that a company called Integrated Transport Solutions dealt with the transportation of sea and air freight for most of LDI's transport needs. He remembered the name from the attack on LDI's headquarters, where they had walked through the offices of a company by that name. He contacted them under the guise of a representative of Asa Corp, and was surprised to discover that they were quite genial towards him. Thanks to his familiarity with corporate structures, a combination of successful Politics and Persuasion rolls allowed him to secure a copy of the manifest of the next ocean-bound shipment.

        Sekhet Bastet and Luca were spending the time trying to discover anything they could about local pirate activity. Their plan for disrupting all LDI's future shipment relied upon finding a group willing to seize any LDI ships that entered Indonesian waters. Their exploration led them to Gorijo, the man who had first put them in contact with Reza's smugglers. The middleman was still working near the resort where they had first met him, and after a brief discussion with Bast he caved and told them that Kudama Jav was the main underground figure engaging in maritime activity. He seemed quite terrified of the man, although he'd only met with him in person a few times. Stories said his enemies died under mysterious circumstances, found without a mark on their bodies but expressions of horror etched into their faces.

        They met up with Luca. Gorijo told them how to go about meeting with Kudama. He had tunnels beneath the fishing dock districts of North Jakarta, their entrances hidden as seemingly normal maintenance tunnels and sewer access points. There were buildings near each one with Kudama's men, who would watch these points and help potential business partners to enter them. They found these men without a problem, and Sesu Ra quickly convinced them that they were here to discuss business. They ended up in the tunnels, which started out looking very modern in appearance. Quite quickly, however, the pipes and lighting fixtures began to fade into the wall, and the concrete walls began to change colour to a yellowish sandstone of far more ancient origins. They realised that etched into the walls were Iremite hieroglyphs, telling the story of an Arisen called Thothekmet. He had pursued an undead abomination, the product of a failed experiment, to Batavia after the creature sought refuge with the Dutch. The first tunnel seemed to describe the journey to Indonesia. At that point, Luca draw their gaze to the shadows cast by the flaming torches placed at even intervals along the tunnel. The shadows thrown by the light seemed to suggest a lot more people were in this tunnel than just the three of them. When Sesu Ra unleashed Tier 1 Chthonic Dominion, it revealed that next to each torch was a figure in a dark robe, a scroll clutched in their left hand. The hoods of these figures turned to watch the three as they continued deeper into the maze.

        Sesu Ra noticed a few other things as they continued. He realised that the maze wasn't conforming to regular geography, but was rather bending and twisting according to the dictates of magic. They would have been lost without his occult senses to guide them. On top of that, a brief study shared the sudden insight that the knowledge used to build these tunnels could be repurposed in his geometric network. Instead of creating a city in which Arisen descend more slowly, or one in which Sebayt experience allowed them to fight the grind of ages on their memories, he could create a Lifeweb spanning the entire city that would allow him to treat all of Jakarta as his tomb.

        He didn't share this revelation with the others. Instead, he led them to the centre of the maze, to the lair of Kudama Jav. They found smugglers, and a great deal of illicit cargo, in the central chamber. Kudama, a rake-thin man with wild eyes, seemed startled by their appearance and demanded to know how they had arrived. The Arisen avoided the question. They proposed a deal, agreeing to supply his group with weapons in return for their help attacking LDI's freighters and a promise to give them 80% of the loot. Both Bast and Sesu Ra realised during this conversation that the book Kudama was clutching was a relic. Bast queried whether he understood the writing in the tunnels. It had stopped three turns before reaching the maze's centre, ending suddenly with the words that Thothekmet was closing in on his quarry. Kudama appeared to have no idea what the words said, and was greatly interested to hear the story. The group left soon after that, finding their way out easily enough. Sesu Ra theorised that the tunnel network would be the perfect tool for smugglers. Mortal attention largely avoided it, as it was functionally a tomb with the Obscure merit. Even if police investigated, they would need knowledge of the occult to locate the "stable" rooms where the products were kept. The rest of the maze was constantly shifting, and they could be lost for all time if they entered without any idea of how to navigate. Meanwhile, the smugglers could use the tunnels to get anywhere quickly, as the maze could cut through seemingly impassible obstacles.

        With this agreement struck, the group decided to wait and focus on healing their wounds. Bast and Sesu Ra used the down-time to spend on Sesu Ra teaching Bast the Utterance Blessed is the God-King. They also gathered Akenakhi to mount another expedition to try and find the Source of the River's Curse from Sekhet Bastet's visions, but it was no more forthcoming than before. It was at this point that Akenakhi decided to share the information that one of the Shuankhsen was trying to get him to steal Sesu Ra's canopic jars. He refused to name the Shuankhsen, afraid she might have mystic senses that would detect him breaking his promise in that way, which made Bast somewhat suspicious. That, and the knowledge that the Shuankhsen might have some kind of leverage on him. He shared the true secrets in private with Sesu Ra sometime later, when they met together at the temple. He told the full story, saying that it was Iwseck behind it all, and that she knew he was a heretic. He explained that he was a student of Apotheosis and that she sought to use his studies against him. Sesu Ra wasn't particularly interested in the revelation of heresy, as it had little to do with his Guild's own projects. However, as part of his agreement to stay silent on the matter, he did ask to read Sutek's Ladder. Akenakhi delivered a copy of the book, after making a show of burning it before the meret so that Bast would not seek the same thing.

        Days passed. The group waited for word of Sem-Huf's awakening, but there was none. However, a messenger did arrive from Rekhenaten's cultists. Rekhenaten had awoken for his Sothic Turn, and had asked to speak with his Guildmaster. Sesu Ra was escorted to the Flesh Mason's lavish living quarters, and there the two Tef-Aabhi discussed their plans for the city. Rekhenaten said he had collected many vestiges to help anchor the arcane geometries Sesu Ra had spent decades constructing. They weren't far from completion, they just needed the missing head from Sesu Ra's memories and they would be on the verge of completion. That, and to discover what revelations the site in the East held. There had been one in North, South, West, and Central Jakarta, so there must be one last secret to discover. Having warned Rekhenaten to step up security against the Shuankhsen, Sesu Ra returned to his tomb.

        Bast spent some time with the personal cult she was growing in Mot-Tan's former district, a new attempt to create a cult dedicated wholly to the Judges. She met with Tio, a middle aged man with a milky white eye who seemed to have taken the role of policing the cult upon himself, Alex, a fifteen year old boy who was one of the many cultists showing signs of the same wasting sickness she remembered afflicting her old jungle cult, and Julie, a ragged, malnourished woman with a raw wound where her left ear should be. After complimenting Tio's willingness to cut open his own scars during a ritual devoted to her and dealing with Julie's request for retribution upon her husband, quickly accomplished by the use of the Pale Brood, she did some cursory investigation of the disease. It was afflicting those who drank from the river most strongly, and, oddly, her cultists were disproportionately affected even among the other slum dwellers.

        Iwseck, meanwhile, had grown impatient. She confronted Akenakhi at his tomb again, this time wearing a floral green dress. Akenakhi appeared unflustered by her sudden appearance, asking after the brand of clothing she was wearing. Iwseck told him to ask the woman who had worn it before her, and then asked if they could speak in his tomb, somewhere they wouldn't disturb any onlookers. Akenakhi agreed. Keeping her voice measured, she asked why he hadn't provided the jars yet. It had been several days, after all. Akenakhi tried to stall for more time, saying that he was on the verge of being able to enact his plan and that killing him would hurt her plans, not help them. It didn't work. Iwseck unleashed Blessed is the God-King Tier 3, turning into an enormous being sporting a desiccated lion's head. She picked Akenakhi and slammed him into the wall. He tried one more time to delay her, to convince her that this was a mistake. When they didn't work, he wriggled free and bolted for the exit. She was on top of him before he could make it, and tore him apart. He felt her teeth sink deep into his body and soul as he collapsed. As the last shreds of his Sekhem were torn away, his pillars were left vulnerable. Akenakhi lost not only his final dot of Sekhem, but also a dot of Ab to Iwseck's feasting. It was the end of his Descent, the last moments of his Sothic Turn.

        Sesu Ra was the first to discover the carnage. Days later, he entered Akenakhi's tomb to return Sutek's Ladder. He found the shredded remains of Akenakhi, left to lie in his tomb rather than be consumed, and a second copy of Sutek's Ladder lying beside the corpse.

        And that's where we ended it. Aquila's player couldn't make the session, but I have been informed that Aquila will be going to Memory 5. Sekhet Bastet is planning on getting Memory 4. And Akenakhi, despite dropping to Memory 6, should get a final flashback before his soul returns to Duat. So next session should involve a lot of time in the past...

                Session 33

                Akenakhi's death sent him plunging into the final Death Cycle of this Descent. He descended again into the swirling darkness of Neter-Khetet, a path beneath his feet leading him to an enormous vaulted arch. Hieroglyphs ran up the monument's sides, saying "The Akhet Betwixt Two Titans". In the shadow of the gate, Anpu waited. He held a staff in his left hand, and gestured for Akenakhi to follow.

                Before long, the ground began to crunch under Akenakhi's feet. The darkness gave way to light, and he found himself standing atop a sand dune with Anpu by his side. The god handed him the staff, and a glimmer of starlight atop the object seemed to light a path for Akenakhi to follow. An impression of water and safety filled his mind, and he knew where he had to go.

                He never made it, however. The oasis promised in Anpu's vision never appeared. The red sun in the black sky above began to burn, and Akenakhi realised he was being hunted. Massive, bloated scorpions began to emerge from the sands, rising from his own footprints. The slaughterers converged on him, and poison filled his veins even as their mandibles sank into his flesh. He staggered and collapsed, and the pain seemed to last an eternity.

                When Akenakhi regained his sight, he found himself lost beneath the stars. In the far East was an enormous mountain, crested with sunlight. To the West was its twin, guarding the sun's resting place. They were the only landmarks he could rely upon. He was standing on a flat, desolate plain, but ruins covered the ground.They moved and changed constantly, like a mirage, the architecture changing through various cities he had known in his long life. He saw buildings from Jakarta, Constantinople, Paris, Irem, and others, all ruined by time and some kind of apocalyptic devastation.

                Then he heard the sound of marching feet. From the West approached an army, a legion of Irem's dead. Ghosts of slave-shoulders armed and armoured in the regalia he knew in life, their flesh peeling and rotting as they walked. Despite their ephemeral appearance, Akenakhi knew their blades would cut as surely as any mortal blade.

                He sensed a presence beside him, and looked up to see Anpu watching the scene impassively. Following the god's gaze, he saw in the East a howling mass rising from the ruins of the shattered city. Flaming spirits, the broken remnants of mortal souls, created and empowered to fight the intruders. They plunged down and crashed into the ranks of the legion of the dead. Fire turned the sand to glass, and Akenakhi felt himself drop through the glass into the darkness of Duat.

                Elsewhere, Aquila found herself meditating on new memories of her life just after coming to Jakarta. Her cult was small, just three mortals, but they had secured the gem she requested. They brought it to her, and she drained the Sekhem eagerly. For the first time in months, the world seemed to have colour again. She was invigorated, and rose to thank her servants for the duty they had performed.

                Then, heavy footsteps sounded, and a large man entered her tomb. In his arms he carried Hetenatra, the Lady of the Broken Stele. Aquila saw the judgement in her glare, and realised with a sinking feeling that the other Arisen knew what she had been doing to stay awake.

                "Sphinx of Rome," Hetenatra addressed her, "You have sinned most grievously against the Judges' will. Six years of heresy lie behind you. For the sake of a mortal child, you have forsaken your purpose, abandoned your fellow Deathless, and, most grievously of all, you have committed the heresy of despoiling relics to ensure your longevity. Do you contest these allegations?"

                Aquila gritted her teeth. "No."

                Hetenatra ordered her cultists to bring in the child. Phatri was a beautiful child at six years of age, wearing a simple brown shift that Aquila had made with her own hands. The hem was stained dark, a sign that she must have been playing near the ocean.

                Quietly, Hetenatra explained that she could kill the child, and not a single Arisen in the city would say it had been the wrong choice. The very guild Aquila claimed to master would applaud the decision, once the full scope of her crimes became known. But, Hetenatra was willing to show leniency. She did not think Aquila was entirely given over to heresy. She could use a highly placed servant, and if Aquila swore to aid her in all her efforts to bring justice to heretics then judgement for her indiscretions could be postponed indefinitely. However, if she ever found that Aquila had devoured another relic, her doom would fly on wings of fire.

                Aquila's pride battled against her desire to see Phatri safe, but her love for Phatri won out. She accepted the offer.

                Hours later, she was sitting by the beach watching the sun set, Phatri curled against her side.

                "Mother?"
                "Yes?"
                "Are you going to go away soon?"
                "Soon, my love, but not just yet. And I will always return to protect you."

                In the darkness of her own tomb, Sekhet Bastet felt her own mind expand. She remembered another dark temple, long ago.

                Fear the red-haired man. Exalt the square-eared beast.

                The cryptic phrase has dogged Sekhet Bastet through the centuries. It is, she believes, a clue to her True Purpose.

                She remembered the Crusades, and the end of a bloody campaign. A long lost temple to the Judges, hidden away in the mountains, was finally reclaimed. Even as the hymns of Christian warriors rose from the camp outside, the cults within the army had ensured that the holy ground remained sacrosanct and safe from the monotheists. The Su-Menent had gathered in the old temple, and each was taking their time to present a sermon. Bast remembered speaking herself.

                "It is right to worship what one fears. Witness the Judges, in all their terrible glory. Their splendour is more than the mortal eye can comprehend, and their forms inspire emotions humans cannot readily accept. Terror and awe are simply signs that what we perceive is beyond our ken."

                Her words on the nature of worship, the necessity of fear, and humanity's need for an impending judgement are well received. As she descends once more to take her place among the black-robed masses, she spots something that makes her freeze in her tracks. Beneath layers of dust and the detritus of ages is a statue of a square-eared beast. As she peers closer to examine it, its eyes peel open and she finds herself staring into a gulf of stars.

                The statue doesn't move, but a voice appears to pour from the vista and sink deep into her mind. "Your choice, She Who Is Power, remains the same as ever. You may choose victory in the arms of your enemies, or a violent death as you stand alone. The stars see the passage of your fate. You fight on so many fronts, against the heresy within, the Devourer's slaves, mortal treachery, and abominations that exist outside the natural order. And yet, your greatest victories will come when you allow one enemy to live so that you may hasten the death of another."

                The temple melts away. Bast finds herself on a barren rock in the midst of the void. Stars burn close, bolts of golden fire ripple across the cosmos, and she notes without surprise that her body has become that of a colossal jackal.

                Beyond the light, she senses her enemies on the move. Shuankhsen move like sharks, testing the water for the scent of Sekhem. Amkhata flock like carrion birds in their wake. The tiny motes of humanity are a constant irritant, needles against her skin. The stars themselves seem hostile, turning their malevolent gaze upon her, and she feels their will as a curse that seems to sap her energy and turn fate itself against her. Beyond even the stars, stranger things crawl, creatures that make her soul rebel even to consider the nature of their existence.

                All these foes descend, and Bast finds herself overwhelmed. Her skin tears beneath the onslaught, her flesh unravels, her very existence becomes a raw wound pulsing with agony. Against any single one of these enemies, she could prevail. But against all of them...

                In desperation, she remembers the words. She exerts her will, and her enemies turn upon themselves. And for a moment, Bast glimpses the Ladder of Sutek. With a wrench, she realises she has seen the path heretics walk towards Apotheosis. When she looks down, she sees her own feet upon it as well.

                The vision ends suddenly. Thoroughly disturbed, Bast tries to focus her thoughts on the Shuankhsen. Cutting herself to improve her visions, she seeks out the cursed Uter she has been hunting. Yet again, however, her visions lead to nothing. Just the same images of pipes pumping vile substances into the river.

                Sesu Ra orders the meret to gather, and reveals the destruction of Akenakhi. Troubled by the death of their ally, they continue their hunt for the Shuankhsen. Bast and Aquila seek out Yama-Het, hoping that she might have more on the actions of the Shuankhsen. The Sesha-Hebsu Guildmaster is disturbed, but has nothing of substance to offer. They ask her advice on the bloody writing found at the site of Mot-Tan and Sem-Huf's deaths, and point out that some force is preying on the isolated Arisen in the city. Unfortunately, she isn't able to make any sense of the events.

                The meret oversees dredging operations they set in motion, in the hopes that finding the cause of the river's taint will lure out the Shuankhsen. They find that some of the dredge contains a thick reddish sludge, with a tendency to stick to itself in lumps resembling flesh. From what they observe, no matter where in the river they dredge, those dredging operations manned largely or entirely by cultists discover this substance while those involving outside contractors don't seem to encounter any.

                A week passes largely without event, allowing Aquila to recover from her wounds. She spends some time away from the others, preying on the city's criminal population by targeting jails in order to gather the hearts she needs to heal. Dust Beneath Feet makes her a consummate predator, able to come and go without notice. Sekhet Bastet and Aquila each spend the time mastering a new Utterance. Bast draws on Sesu Ra's tutelage to learn Blessed Is The God-King, while Aquila mines her own memories to once again wield Harvest The Divine Flesh.

                At the end of the week, Bast and Aquila meet at the temple and find Aqhat there. With his flesh restored, he takes the form a blockish Persian man with light olive skin, a thick, curly beard, and a shaved head. He is wearing a simple linen wrap around his waist, dark kohl around his eyes, and has his axe in hand. They have caught him mid-practice. The weapon moves impossible fast as he goes through the movements, displaying a skill and speed no mortal man could ever match.

                The three have a quick conversation. Aqhat reveals that he has turned to outside help to locate Kotira. Allies are due to arrive at the airport tomorrow. He is glad to see the pair, because their meret proved themselves allies in their fight together. He asks their help. The newcomers will be vulnerable when they arrive, and he suspects LDI will be one step ahead. If they could secure the airport for the newcomers, he would be most appreciative.

                The pair agree. Bast asks who it is that Aqhat has summoned, and he says that the newcomers will be of the Maa-Kep. Their names are Benerib, the Penitent Falcon, and Mekshonat, the Seal of Babylon. Benerib has been a close friend of the Su-Menent for centuries, and long ago she and Kotira marked a mystic pact that should allow her to locate Kotira immediately.

                They inform Sesu Ra of the proceedings, and decide to wake Akenakhi. A Sekhem 10 Mummy would be good back-up if LDI does mount a serious attack. Investigating his tomb, they find that he has sealed his relics within his tomb using Palace Knows Its Pharaoh. There are two entrances to his treasury, and they are connected in such a way that entering the treasury through one causes the walker to exit the treasury through the other in the same instant. After some consideration, Sekhet Bastet uses Ancestry of Forgotten Stars Tier 2 to pass through the walls and snatch the Rod of Tjesh.

                The cultists, under Aquila's direction, then raise Akenakhi back to life. The Rod of Tjesh is drained in the process, becoming inactive for the next 14 weeks. He rises, and they give him the command "Destroy the Shuankhsen".

                First, however, a quick detour to the airport (with Akenakhi carefully swathed in layers of clothes to avoid invoking widespread Sybaris). The meret, and Luca, arrived several hours before the plane was due and began scouting out the area surrounding the private hanger chartered for the use of the arriving Arisen. They discovered several signs in a quick secession that something was amiss. Luca noted the baggage handlers are not the ones listed on the documentation of those chosen for the roster that day. Bast found caltrops had been scattered across the driveway leading away from the hanger. Aquila put two and two together, and quietly excused herself. She called Mike Thornton, and warned him that he should call off any operations LDI was running near the airport that day. They were facing down an entire meret, at least. Mike quietly thanked her for the information, and hung up. Over the next few minutes, each one of the suspicious baggage handlers slipped away. Luca followed them undetected.

                The private plane arrived without incident. Benerib emerged, a handsome woman of clear Iremite origins wearing a formal suit and high heels. She greeted each of the group, although when Akenakhi asked if she remembered him she politely indicated that she didn't, to his disappointment. She asked that they wait until her brother could be off-loaded from the plane, and they watched as a large packing crate was loaded into a truck. Passing through customs was smooth, the group got the impression that Kotira's cultists must be at work, and before long they were heading for the temple. Benerib wanted to leave her brother there, and then immediately head for Kotira's current location.

                They picked up Aqhat at the temple, who was wearing his full battle armour, and piled into the truck again. Benerib's sense for Kotira led them straight to a hospital in West Jakarta. They asked Aqhat to wait while they investigated, and went inside. The topmost levels were where Kotira was. So, acting like they belonged there, the meret and Benerib walked straight for the elevator and headed straight for the top floor. The doors started to open...

                And we ran out of time, so I called the session there.

He was collected by Suyifa, who had him sign some documents to the effect that LDI wasn't liable if anything happened to him, and had him blindfolded and driven to a facility where he was tied down and cut open. He had emptied almost all of his pillars, leaving only his Ren filled, at Aquila's suggestion. The power that flowed from him was little more than a trickle. All around, the LDI scientists recited their blasphemous chant decrying the Judges and all they held dear, much to Akenakhi's interest. When the power was done they helped him patch his wounds, and then he was escorted out the way he had come. Akenakhi fell to Sekhem 6.

                                Bast and Sesu Ra were with Kudama Jav again, preparing his men for the attack on LDI's transport ship. The pirates had the weapons provided by the Arisen, and Sesu Ra offered to give material bodies to some of Kudama Jav's ghosts so that they could be the front line of the attack. After some consideration, the smuggler leader agreed. Sesu Ra took three of the hooded ghosts back to his tomb, and bound them into embalmed corpses using the Blasphemous Depiction. "Destroy LDI's shipping" was the instruction he gave them.

                                Across the city, Aquila had cut apart a snake and bull. The LDI lab had provided her with a fungal heart for the False Ka. She stitched the bull's head to the snake's body with a needle of bone and sutures of sinew, and sacrificed her own Sekhem to the pit. The ritual took a day and a night, but there among the stench of rotting animal parts she brought forth a foul new life. Tetasu Xex was born, and for her blasphemy the Judge Qerrti struck at her Sekhem.

                                Aquila was now Sekhem 2, but she felt she had nearly everything she needed to fight Gator. A Talisman would be the final piece of the puzzle.

                                                        Session 37

                                                        Sekhet Bastet and Sesu Ra boarded Kudama Jav's ships, which took them out to the Thousand Islands to transfer to the open-topped longboats that would be used to board the LDI freighter. It would take nearly 24 hours to get far enough away from Jakarta to carry out the operation safely.

They found themselves in an old Red Light district, a place where the gap between worlds seemed remarkably thin. Although in recent years the brothels had been driven out of business, signs of the what the neighbourhood had once been were everywhere. Among the evidence of vice they were able to track down a woman from Aquila's named Adelaide Baron, a self-proclaimed spiritualist. Adelaide was a serious middle-aged woman who worked out of her home. She welcomed them in when they arrived, claiming she could sense a dark presence hanging over them. Aquila thought guiltily about Tetasu Xex, and decided to have him manifest at the earliest opportunity.

                                                        A conversation followed in which Adelaide revealed that there were many among the people here who worshipped false gods and dark spirits. She had banished some of their so-called gods herself with her knowledge of exorcism, and said that her greatest battle was yet to come. The Arisen offered their help, thinking it might lead them to the relic they sought, and she accepted gladly. They were to head directly to the evil spirit's resting place, but Aquila begged to excuse herself. Once somewhere no one could see, she summoned Tetasu Xex.

                                                        To her horror, her Amkhat appeared with great wounds rent in its body, fungus oozing from a hundred cuts. The creature was clearly near death. As the Arisen wandered oblivious through the streets, there had apparently been a desperate battle taking place in Neter-Khetet that her newborn pet had only just survived. She ordered the creature to return to Asa Corp headquarters, where it was to remain in Twilight and lick its wounds.

                                                        She then accompanied Akenakhi and Adelaide to an old, dilapidated home. It was clearly long-abandoned and dilapidated, its walls riddled with rot and paint peeling from every surface. They came upon a strange iron door that was at odds with the rest of the building, clearly new and well-furbished. It was carved with symbols that seemed to denote human sacrifice, and written in Indonesian across the top of the door were the words "The Mindless Body".

                                                        They opened the door and stared into the darkness beyond. Just as they were about to step across the threshold, however, coils of undulating corpus began to spill out of the room. The Arisen realised quickly what was happening and turned to run. They weren't quite fast enough, as snapping jaws took chunks out of Aquila. They each spent pillars to boost their Physical attributes as they raced towards the exit, and Adelaide saw their true form. The Sybaris overwhelmed her and she fled, barely avoiding being caught by the Chthonic Being that chased them. They reached the street when they noticed that the Chthonic Being wasn't pursuing them.

                                                        Adelaide demanded that they go away and leave her alone. She had seen them for the dead things they really were, and wanted nothing more to do with them. The two Arisen weren't willing to fight a Chthonic Being without the rest of the meret, so they retreated to nurse their wounds.

A blaring siren made it very clear their presence had been noticed. Boarding ladders were raised, while Sesu Ra drew his khopesh and cut through the steel hull like butter. At least one of the undying monsters he had created entered through the gap, while Luca, Bast, and he all followed suit.

                                                        Once inside they could hear occasional gunshots up above. They heard movement coming towards them and hid quickly. When half a dozen crew emerged, Luca opened fire and gunned them down. That was when they realised the men were unarmed. By the time they reached the top deck they found most of the crew alive and held prisoner by Kudama Jav's men. There had been no armed resistance, no Amkhata, just a civilian crew without any defences. Kudama's men started stripping the ship of anything of value while the Arisen interrogated the ship's captain to find out if he knew anything about LDI operations. There wasn't much information forthcoming.

Sesu Ra set a course that would take it into the damaged section of the LDI docks, and once it had run aground, Sekhet Bastet called down a meteor that blasted a massive hole in it. The burning ship should remove most useful evidence for them, and send a remarkably good message to their enemies.

With a relic on the table and Sesu Ra's normal interest in ghosts, they decided to destroy the Chthonic Being immediately. Before they left, Aquila gathered some cultists together to feed on their Willpower and restore her own. She found, however, that her cultists were already drained. Every attempt she made simply rendered them unconscious without any benefit to herself. She wondered at first if it was due to the dreams being inflicted upon them by Hetenatra, but it turned out even cultists unaffected by the dreams were feeling drained and demoralised. She deduced then that it must be due to the presence of Tetasu Xex. The cultists were being weakened by his presence.

When they arrived at the residence they found an old man waiting for them. He demanded that they leave. They were trespassing on sacred ground, on a resting place of the God-In-Three-Parts. The Arisen brushed past him, Aquila pausing to drain his Willpower until he was rendered unconscious.

Aquila tried to seize the creature and turn a chunk of it to gold, but it shrugged off the magic easily. Bast, wielding bone knives made for her by her cultists, began to carve into its essence doing terrific damage to the creature. It roared and struggled, to little avail. Sesu Ra tried to will the power of Blessed Is The God-King forth from his Talisman, but his Sekhem was so low that, by the time he completed the Unleashing, the Chthonic Being had been torn apart by Bast's attacks.

After a brief consultation they decided to check out the church from the original kepher vision. It was an old, dilapidated building that had a greater resemblance to local architecture than any classical church imagery. They asked the priest, who assumed they were tourists, for a brief tour of the building. They managed to milk him for information in the process, finding out that there was a local preacher who had been poaching from his congregation to gather people in the worship of a strange God. Outwardly, this preacher seemed to adopt the trappings of Christianity, but the priest had visited his temples and seen some very strange and worrying things.

The building was one of the more modern buildings in the area, a shining example of the progress the district was making. They walked in, and that was when the ghosts began to materialise around them.

                                                        There were close to a dozen of them. Terrible, twisted things of shredded corpus, many eyes, and unnatural physiology. Many were close enough to human to tell their origins, but others looked almost entirely alien. A mortal man walked towards them through the spirits, clutching a metal box under his arm. He said his name was Ancilo, the leader of this congregation, and told them to leave immediately.

It was the relic they'd come here seeking. Finally, the meret decided that conversation wasn't getting them anywhere. They attacked.

Bast waded into the ghosts, cutting them apart with her bone knives, but they singled her out as the greatest threat. Many of the creatures were able to become physically manifest, and they tore into her with fangs and talons of corpus. Sesu Ra used Chthonic Dominion Tier 2 to dominate one of the spirits, turning it against the others, while his ghostly protector joined the fight to attack the ghosts that were striking at its master. Aquila summoned up armour from the earth with Dust Beneath Feet and rushed towards the pillars, two of the spirits clinging to her back and tearing out chunks of flesh. She reached it, and used Name the Thing to determine its power and if it was in any way responsible for the attackers.

Akenakhi was nowhere to be seen, having pursued Ancilo down the street but failing to catch the man. And then, forming from a black cloud that seemed to spill out of the altar. It was a tall, insectile thing, with a torso nearly seven feet long, eight bladed limbs, and a head that was nothing but eyes. It speared Aquila with one limb. Bast, on the verge of death, decided to rush the creature and put it in the ground. Her blades tore into it, causing it to screech and strike at her. The blow should have killed her, but instead it opened her eyes.

Of the many spirits attacking them, only three appeared to be real. Most were illusions, causing her allies to flail at the air while their real enemies struck at them in relative safety. She redoubled her attacks on the strange monster, killing it, and directing her allies against the real foes. They were soon alone in the chamber, torn corpus gently dissipating into the air around them.

Aquila had determined that it was a Minor Djed, and was almost certainly responsible for the corrupted nature of the ghosts. Sesu Ra used Chthonic Dominion to set himself as an anchor for his newly enslaved servant, having given the creature the command "Protect the Tef-Aabhi". Even the brief exposure to the relic had caused visible effects on his existing protector.

                                                        Although many of their injuries were phantasmal, Bast and Aquila were happy to retreat and lick their wounds. They had retrieved what they had come for anyway. The meret returned to their tombs (the still tombless Aquila returned to Asa Corp).

                                                        When Sesu Ra reached the lobby of the skyscraper where his tomb was concealed, a heavily shrouded figure asked him to come with him. He chose to send Luca after the man instead, and returned to his tomb. The man flatly refused to speak to Luca, even when Sesu Ra seized control of Luca's voice and spoke through him as a puppet. He demanded they meet in person, somewhere quiet where they could be alone.

                                                        Sesu Ra gave in. Leaving Luca behind, he met with the figure in an empty corridor of the building. The man removed the hood and shrouds that concealed his identity. Beneath the rot, the diseased skin stretched tight across a malformed skull, the wounds and sores visible in his flesh, Sesu Ra recognised Rangarni, Iwseck's Fasad.

                                                        "I need you to help me die," he said.
