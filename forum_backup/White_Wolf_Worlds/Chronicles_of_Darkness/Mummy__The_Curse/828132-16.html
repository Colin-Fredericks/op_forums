        To add to thr Seats of the Gods doc as a sidebar for Per-Wadjet. And one additional point for Nekhen and Nekheb's.

        -=-

        Poison of Vision: As the oracles of Lower Kemet, the Cult of Wadjet commands a great deal of influence, and this has only been reinforced since Khenut was made Unas' second wife. They have seen that Khenut's child will birth next line of kings, and thus work to ensure that mother and child live through the chaos engulfing the Black Land.

        Lake of Dreams: Of all the people who returned from the Sea of Reeds, those of Per-Wadjet are perhaps the most prosperous. These fae beings have built a city which mirrors Per-Wadjet in grandeur, and their courts enjoy access not only to a successful Market, but also a cornucopia of enchanted fruits grown in fertile soil.

        -=-

        Foresight of the Mothers: Though they continue to give their services to the Great House, the Cult of Nekhbet holds no real loyalty to Unas.Their divinations have exposed to them the truth of the mad king, and that his actions threaten to drown Kemet in a storm of chaos and destruction. Armed with this knowledge, the Mothers secretly plot to avert the coming crisis before it is too late.
