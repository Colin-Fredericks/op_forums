                  The POD quality is, in my experience, equal to traditional printing at this point. There were some hiccups at start, but they seem to have been hammered out. The only issue I have with POD products at this point is an issue with how the digital file fills the page, leading to the books being slightly larger to maintain the same internal dimensions on the page. This is an incredibly minor thing, but I have a shelf I made specifically for my WOD collection that holds the traditional run books just right, but the POD books hit the bottom of the shelf above them.

                  So yeah, if that's my biggest complaint with the POD service, I don't think you've got anything to worry about.

                              Originally posted by Varsovian View Post
                              What about book covers? Traditional White Wolf books had these nice, hard covers with shiny elements etc. I guess POD versions don't look that good, right?
                              Traditional WW books were a mixture of covers. There are deluxe hardcovers, hardcovers, and softcovers. The deluxe hardcovers are not available POD, so that's somewhat irrelevant (they're being reserved for Kickstarter drives due to the fact that they can't be adequately handled by the POD service). The regular POD hardcovers and softcovers are just as good as the traditional ones.

                              As far as I can tell, the only difference is that the POD service doesn't seem to be able to do mixed glossy and matte covers like some of the nWOD traditional run books have. But they look great even without a bit of contrasting texture.

                              Originally posted by Phaolan
                              And the size thing's only been an issue for me with the hardcover books. The paperpack ones aren't noticeably taller on my shelves.
                              To my knowledge this isn't an issue of cover, but of B&W vs. color books. So a hardcover color POD copy of Mummy should be the same size as a traditional WW hardcover, where a hardcover B&W copy of Mummy would be a tad taller.

                              I'm not sure anyone knows why, exactly, the B&W printing process has trouble here where the color one does not. But the B&W process has been stated to be the source of it.

                                      Correct. B&W & color PODs, whether standard or premium are the same size, or about ¼ inch taller than the traditional printed hardcovers. The only difference is the quality of paper and vibrancy of color. In my experience the binding is well made (although glued, not sewn like the deluxe). The POD hardcovers are really sturdy and well made and are more akin to the classic WOD hardcovers than the new WOD hardcovers (or glossy vs matte/metallic). My mummy deluxe (no pun intended) is the same size as my Vampire the Requiem & Mage the Awakening hardcovers, as well as having the same 2 color treatment as the others. Although I hear that the newest deluxe for Demon will be in the style of the metallic sheen that Requiem, Awakening, Lost and Forsaken, which is nice that they are bringing that back.

                                      The Mummy Deluxe hardcover sold for $40 ($5.00 shipping)
                                      The Mummy premium hardcover POD sells for $60 (without shipping)

                                      I imagine the same will be for Demon, which is going for $50 (shipping included)

                                      I think its fair to say that those who pledge in for the deluxe books get the better deal :-)
