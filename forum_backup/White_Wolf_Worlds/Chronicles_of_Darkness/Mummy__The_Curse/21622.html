          I've recently set a mummy game during 1942 as the German forces led by Erwin Rommel pushed into north africa and the Nazi occultists followed in their wake pillaging tombs. Its still within living memory, well documented and so easy to research. It was one of the first things that popped into my head as we were sitting around wondering what to do one sunday and there is a simple uncomplicated fun to be had apparently in unleashing tier 3 utterances against the third reich. seemed easy to run a session that explored the more superficial and obvious aspects of (un)life as an arisen without having to worry about the underlying themes and mood to much (which can wait untill ive written a much better story for the current sothic turn in istanbul)

                              12-05-2013, 01:21 PM
                              I'm in the development stages of a French Revolution Mummy game. I think this era could work well as either a stand alone chronicle, or as a prequel to a present day game.

                              The eternal servant wakes up, to discover that the lower castes are rising up against their leaders. Apart from the direct challenges of dodging revolutionaries and protecting ones cult in a world where the aristos are pariah, on an existential level how does the mummy deal with the revolution? Is she appalled by the actions of the revolutionaries? How dare they rise up againt their betters?! But then again, if mere mortals can do it, can the mummy not do the same? "The secret of freedom lies in educating people, whereas the secret of tyranny is in keeping them ignorant" Robspierre said. Are the Shan'iatu and the Judges deliberately keeping their servants ignorant? Can they in turn be overthrown? Can the mummy decide its own destiny?

                              Those are the questions that makes me believe French Revolution Mummy could be an awesome setting.

                                                      It may or may not contradict existing canon, but in my Mummyverse, the Heretic (rather like Saulot in owod) learned his secrets in the far east. The way I run my secret history ********SPOILERS********** is that sometime before/during the first Turn, many Mummies were appaled by their condition and traveled East, seeking freedom. In my chronicle, the Heretic is not the first Mummy to attain Apotheiosis, merely the most famous and outspoken. I'm using the Eight Immortals myth to create eight Mummies of various sorts and mindsets who acheived apotheiosis before Buddha was born. Thus, in addition to the Dreams of Avarice, mummies seeking aptheiosis in my game often travel to India, Tibet, Nepal, and other north and far eastern areas instinctually.
