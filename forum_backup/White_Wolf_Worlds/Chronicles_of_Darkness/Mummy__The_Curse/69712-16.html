    Originally posted by The Gentleman Gamer View Post
    Additionally, to echo Dave, it's poor form to say "I wish X author / developer was writing this instead of X author / developer". That doesn't sound like the kind of thing you'd say to X author's / developer's face, as it's pretty insulting.
    Please, please, please spare me the hypothetical character judgement. Considering this is a forum discussion and like all anonymous/semi-anonymous communication its basically impossible judge anything about anyone beyond what they say and how they say it (by which you can ascertain that I am frustrated by some of the stuff involved in the Mummy lines development and I'm not afraid of being "rood" to voice my opinion on a public forum that allows for freedom of opinion to be voiced) this sort of statement is pointless. Any statement I could make is response to such a comment would likewise, be pointless. So I'm just going to leave that whole can of worms alone now.

    As for the other topics raised, because this thread got really busy in the day I've been away.

    1) I think CAS has mentioned it before and I've agreed with him in the past about the page count issue for the Mummy Corebook. Nice to see that refreshed, at the same time, its an old point I'll leave alone from now on. No point beating a dead horse and everyone on the thread pretty much seems to wish we had had more pages but as CAS said the books page issue was just something that happened. End of story. I won't be bringing it up again.

    2) True Malcolm sometimes all one needs to do is go back and refresh ones Memory of the rules in the books. I find a lot of problems are solved this way. Sometimes people go back and reread the books and don't find the explanation or info they are looking for. Sometimes they find the language unclear or confusing or that its lacking a clear defined meaning them.

    I've seen both happen to my players in Mummy's case and I've had that same thing happen to me. Its not like its endemic to the book, its just that some things (like Memory levels) could have been helped with examples and a little more word count. Once again though this is just mine and my players experience (though several other people on the forum have mentioned it I think, if Memory serves)

    In addenda, the idea that the Mummy lines age removes it from the possibility of open-dev is one I find a bit strange. V20's been going for a while too, each one of its supplements has had open dev? Blood and Smoke is currently running open dev for its first splat-book. Obviously this sort of thing might have seen more wide-spread implementation after Mummy's initial release. Likewise, I appreciate that the final decision on stuff like this rests with the devs and its their choice to do open-dev or not.

    3) Yes I agree that Mummy is probably the best game at getting its "mood" across in actual play. Particularly I've found in the distinction it draws between its magic (which in general is more grotesque and visceral) and several other types in the WoD (my players were veteran Mage players).

    4) I'd like to clarify I never asked for additional info on future releases that are past Deceived in the release chain while Deceived is still not out. That's insane in a game as interlinked as Mummy and in general not something I've seen any developers for the various WoD lines do.

    5) I've touched on this previously in this post. However, to expand.

    I mentioned possible devs for Mummy 2nd ed for a number of reasons. Firstly, because every other second edition so fare (save Awakening) has had a different lead developer then the previous edition. Secondly, this has allowed the second editions to go in different directions from their first edition counterparts (I like change between editions, I know that's not to everyone's taste), as it goes without saying that different devs have different ideas. Thirdly, I listed two Devs that I find genuinely engaging and interesting in their take (which takes material in a different direction while being loyal to core themes and mood established by said material) on pre-established material (Dark Ages is Daves case, Promethean in Matts) and felt could likewise do something interesting with Mummy. Fourthly, I listed two devs I enjoy the involvement of in the Onyx Path community (and obvious commitment to that community and Onyx Path as major priorities) and yes, like the open-dev process of.

    Fifthly, it was not my intent to cause offense to any other Devs currently working on the Mummy line (edit - As I know I have in the past, around page 7 of this thread where I launched a semi-personal attack on CAS's handling of the game. Not something I'm proud of and something that CAS and I addressed shortly after). Surprise, surprise I'm a big fan of the game, Mummy's in my top 4 NWoD games. I've praised its setting and feel to high heavens in the past (not to mention try to sell the game to a group of massively skeptical players in the first place). Content wise its second to none in the NWoD, no doubt about that. I'm sure Mummy would not be the game it is without CAS and is meticulous attention to the setting and his love of the history involved.

    6) I'll add my voice to Azahhul in saying that I have very few criticism of how Mummy has been written and how the world of Mummy has been created and presented in the books. Likewise, (because I get the feeling rotten fruit and vegetables are being prepared) I have no huge criticisms of any of the writing teams content on the line.

    My criticism are and always have been based on the implementation of the Mummy line in regards to its schedule (especially in relation to other Onyx Path lines) and the actual structure of the game itself e.g. the idea of having a big in-game "meta-secret" that the players should not know about in the age of the internet and its various online achieves for this stuff.

    However, I simultaneously recognize that these are largely criticism that I myself view in a different manner to many of the other fans on the forums who may have similar criticisms. As such I've made an attempt to not really talk about them. Since most of the argument and discussion about them has already occurred in this thread and I don't like going around in circles. I've had a little slip obviously judging by the last 2 pages of this thread.

    With all that said lets just hold out till Deceived and then we can all talk about how awesome that book is.

                                            Can someone remind me again what is Dreams of Avarice again. Sorry, I forgot.

                                            For myself, as far as the investment into Mummy it's sort of an odd tale. I've always been an Egyptophile so with the CWoD days it was things like the first W:tA purchase was the first edition of the Silent Striders tribebook, though never took to the Setites as much. I wish I still had my copies of World of Darkness:Mummy, Mummy 2nd edition and the two books I had for Mummy:the Resurrection. Always too hard to get into them with most people running standard Camarilla affairs. The excitement geared up not simply just hearing the news shortly after perusing a thread on people trying to make Mummy rules for NWoD, but finding out CAS was involved.(which was funny as a few people on that thread were essentially trying to hush people up saying NWoD would never get a Mummy game), with even more excitement after finding out Nephiphal was involved after enjoying his work from Exalted. That was a big deal as fond memories of CAS's work from CWoD still resonated. It was somewhat tough in reading the spoilers as a small part of me initially pined for multi-cultural Mummies and the heroic angle of CWoD Mummy.

                                            Given how much was tied to the metaplot though, it was easy to understand that a reiteration was not in the works. The surprising part was the way it meshed with the NWoD's style of horror. I don't know what exact would use for how the general flavour of horror I would use for the NWoD overall:eldritch, insidious perhaps. It has a sort of creeping quality, like awful things Man Was Not Meant to Know™ lurks in every alley and rural hamlet of the NWoD just waiting for unsuspecting people to fall into. The blue book caught me by surprise with that sense and Mummy captured and expanded upon it, to me, more so than I had felt with other lines. It, along with the NWoD in general has been a pleasant surprise that I hope to eventually get my CWoD friends to finally try.

                                                Originally posted by TyrannicalRabbit View Post
                                                Can someone remind me again what is Dreams of Avarice again. Sorry, I forgot.
                                                In Game:

                                                Its the "holy book" for those Arisen pursuing Apotheosis. Its written by "The Heretic" the only Arisen known to achieve this much vaunted/maligned (depending on who you ask) state.

                                                Other then then that very little is known about the Heretic. Lots of tid-bits in the books hint at some aspects of his life, but since he achieved Apotheosis he's been one big mystery, even how long ago he achieved Apotheosis is debated. Some put it in the last century, others some time in the last millennium.

                                                Supposedly studying the book gives one insight into achieving Apotheosis. The ongoing chronicle for the Mummy books details the results of this text becoming a little more commonly distributed among the Arisen community in the current Sothic Turn.

                                                Out of Game:

                                                Its a "fluff" book for the Mummy line. A bit like The Book of Nod or the Lilith Cycle was for Vampire the Masquerade back in the day. So lots of in-setting information with a lot of pretty art (I hope they get the guy who did the Deceived splats to illustrate the whole thing). Like Deceived I think its supposed to "blow the doors off" a few ideas the fanbase has been kicking around since the core book was launched.

                                                Its likely to detail not only more of the "truth" of Irem, but also what's really going on in Duat with the Judges, since Apotheosis lets an Arisen remember what happens to them in Duat and what the Judges might be up too. I'm looking forward to it massively, as while I'm sure Sothis Ascends will have a few more pieces of the Mummy puzzle I think Dreams is more likely to give us possible answers to the last couple of big questions hagging over the setting.

                                                Also fluffy art books that can be used as in-game props are pretty much my favourite sorta book in general.
