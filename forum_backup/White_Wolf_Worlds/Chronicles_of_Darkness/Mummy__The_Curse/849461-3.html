                                            Let me see if I can take a crack at my hypothetical ultimate setting list for Mummy 2E:

                                            Cairo, Egypt
                                            Honestly, even though the Arisen have spread all over the world, I couldn't imagine leaving Egypt out of the setting chapter. The Cairo area has been a center of Egyptian civilization since there has been an Egypt, and it's continuing importance in the modern era is reflected in the fact that many Egyptians refer to it simply as Maṣr, the Egyptian Arabic word for Egypt itself.

                                            Florence, Italy
                                            Both the Arisen and the Deceived have been present in what is now Tuscany since the early days of Etruscan civilization, and there are some who have never left. During the late middle ages and the Italian Renaissance, Florence seemingly had something to offer all of the Deathless, becoming a center of art, architecture, banking, engineering, literature, philosophy, theater, and occult thought. The centuries since have not always been kind to the city, but many Arisen remain, as do their eternal, half-remembered foes .

                                            Istanbul, Turkey
                                            The center of Mediterranean civilization during the third Sothic Turn may find itself practically flooded with Arisen during the fourth, exacerbating tensions in the capital of a nation already experiencing troubled times.

                                            Lagos, Nigeria
                                            It's the biggest city in Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa never seems to get it's fair share of attention in fantasy fiction and games. Mummy is the game line that seems to have dealt with Africa the most, so it's only natural that a major African city outside of Egypt should show up in Mummy 2E. Lagos itself is well outside of the ancient Iremite cultural sphere, and offers a great opportunity to explore some of the diverse cultures of west Africa.

                                            London, UK
                                            Sort of a follow-up to Ruins of Empire, Mummies could have a unique and interesting perspective on the troubled legacy of the British Empire and colonial Egyptology. The Tef-Aabhi may mourn the loss of yet another promising world-empire, while the Mesen-Nebu appreciate London's continuing importance as a center of global trade and finance.

                                            Mexico City, Mexico
                                            Is this already a setting in one of the CofD books? If not, why not? There are great opportunities for cults adapting Iremite dogma to Mesoamerican trappings, and it could make a great setting for stories about the conflict between the ancient and the modern, and between democracy and authoritarianism.

                                            Santa Clara Valley, California
                                            "Silicon Valley" is, in some ways, the antithesis of everything the Arisen stand for, priding itself on its culture of "disruptive innovation" and aggressively promoting the virtues of the new and the temporary over the stable and traditional. And yet, an increasing number of Arisen who have seen the value of modern information technology - as a tool of trade, scholarship, surveillance, or control - have been drawn to the birthplace of the internet age.

                                            Xi'an, China
                                            Formerly Chang'an, an ancient capital of China, it was the most important starting point of the silk road, which brought a trickle of Awakened into China over the centuries. It's glories have faded since it was the largest and wealthiest city in the world, but in the last few decades it has re-emerged as a cultural and economic center, and has begun to grow into one of China's newest megacities.

                                            Tokyo, Japan
                                            Because there's always a Tokyo chapter
