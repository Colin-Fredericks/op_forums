                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                    In fact the Amkhata twilight condition confuse me

                    They have the dread power Ghost touch to interact with ghosts, but does this mean that they are already able to see them?
                    What kind of Affinity could use a Mummy to see the Amkhata?
                    My best suggestion is to ignore Ghost Touch sitting there. That's just where Mummy puts the list of Dread Powers, which are a semi-universal list of supernatural powers CofD uses for various miscellaneous beings. Some things that have Dread Powers would need Ghost Touch to touch ghosts. Amkhata do not.
                    All of the ephemeral entities introduced in the Mummy corebook share the same "frequency" of Twilight, which Mummy calls Neter-Khertet. This is the same Twilight "frequency" used by ghosts, the one containing Avernian Gates which open to Geist's Underworld.

                    Both the Deathsight and Enlightened Senses Soul Affinities can be used to see and touch Amkhata in Twilight (as well as any other inhabitants of Neter-Khertet). The Jackal's Shade Affinity conferred by the Ashem decree can be used to enter Neter-Khertet yourself, where you can then see and touch Amkhata in Twilight.

                    Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                    About attributes and Sekhem
                    When you boost Favored attributes, each point boost one attribute dot or both?
                    Each point boosts both Attributes. If your decree is Ashem, you favor Composure and Stamina, and thus spending two points of Sheut gives you +2 Composure and +2 Stamina for the scene.​

                        Originally posted by Neos01 View Post
                        About attributes and Sekhem
                        Trait Maximum column indicates the maximum limit that you can buy with experience, and you lose 6+ trait levels only when you hit Sekhem 5 right?
                        If you have an Attribute above 5 and your current Sekhem is 5 or lower, for all intents and purposes your rating in that Attribute is 5 until your Sekhem rating equals or exceeds the Attribute rating on your sheet.

                        About using Pillar points, you can boost an Attribute with ONLY one pillar point per scene, gaining (Sekhem determined) dots? Can you spend a second point on a different physical attribute on the same scene?
                        Once per scene you can boost a single Physical Attribute by the amount determined by your Sekhem rating. That means one Attribute per scene, no splitting; presumably the difference from 1e is related to the fact that the boosted Attributes aren't explicitly subject to your Sekhem-based Trait limit (and also implicitly aren't subject to it either, because otherwise there'd be no need to specify that you calculate boosts starting from 5 if you have a natural 6+ rating you can't currently access).
