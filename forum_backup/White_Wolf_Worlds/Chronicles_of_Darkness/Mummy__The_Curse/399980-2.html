                    Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                    Somehow I think this reference to the Ochre-Masked King means more to you than to me.
                    Mummy now has two "totally not from the Cthulhu Mythos, guys" creatures mentioned offhandedly in Utterances. The King in Yellow is a masked figure notorious for either wearing a dreadful mask or wearing no mask at all (i.e. the horror is his true face); he primarily acts through a play that shares his name, which drives all who see it insane and drags the area around it into an extradimensional city known as Carcosa. Ochre happens to be a yellow tone, and there's already Mythos precedent in Mummy through the Ancestry of Forgotten Stars Affinity, which calls the Herald of Forgotten Stars a "crawling chaos" (Nyarlathotep's title) and has a "tall, cruel-looking man dressed as ancient royalty" arrive alongside it, who matches Gnarly Nyarly's frequent depiction as a pharaoh with jet-black skin.

                    The overall fascination with stars and things from beyond time fit the cosmic horror genre well, and these little nods to its progenitor make me giddy.
