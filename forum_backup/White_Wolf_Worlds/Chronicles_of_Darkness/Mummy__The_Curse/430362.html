    Some rules questions (Merits, Blood Bonds, Predatory Aura, Lashing Out, etc)

    03-06-2015, 12:05 PM
    Blood Bonds Do supernatural creatures other than Vampires get a chance to resist the Blood Bond? It isn't Resist Trait + Supernatural Advantage, so I'm not sure. Does the Increased Duration bonus of Bond 3 also apply to Devotions? Can you spend Willpower when resisting the Blood Bond?

    Lashing Out Do supernatural creatures other than Vampires add their Supernatural Advantage if they opt to Fight? Do other forms of Supernatural Advantage count as "Blood Potency" or qualify the other creature as "Counting as a Vampire" for purposes of needing to spend Willpower? If a supernatural creature other than a Vampire wins a Fight contest, they get the same +2 bonus that Mortals get, correct?

    Merits
    - Swarm Form (114). Does taking this Merit expand the list of things you can get with Beast's Skin to include Size 0 creatures? If yes, that means like bugs, right? Does Swarm Form replace Beast's Skin's normal advantages or augment them? I.E. Does the Strength + Brawl roll use your normal traits, or the trait of the individual animals you create? If you use Vigor to add damage levels to your attack, how does that apply? To one target? Once per target? Or extra successes you can spread around? If you activate Swarm Form and Obfuscate 3 at one time and attack someone, does that reveal your entire swarm or just the ones that attacked that person?
    - Does Indomitable (page 121) apply to resisting the blood bond? If you choose to Fight when someone Lashes Out at you, does Indomitable apply? If yes, to one or both sides (since both are the "aggressors," arguably).
    - Does Iron Will (page 121) ever apply against supernatural powers?
    - Parkour 5 (Page 122). How long does Freeflow last?

    Animalism
    - Summon the Hunt (Page 127). How would you handle gathering together a sizable swarm of, say, Rats and having them attack some dudes?

    Protean
    - Beast's Skin (Page 141). Can you eat multiple forms in the span of a few hours and then sleep for one day and night to incorporate all of them, or must you sleep for one day and night per form?
    - Primeval Miasma (Page 141). Are the Vampire's actions in any way limited while in this form? Can they still physically interact with the world around them? Can they use Disciplines? Does feeding still produce The Kiss or The Assault? Does stealing someone's breath leave a discernible cause of death? If someone is using Primeval Miasma at the same time as Obfuscate, and someone walks in the cloud, does that break Obfuscate or does the Vampire need to actually do something to them?

    Devotions
    - Quicken Sight (Page 145). What does "and can benefit from aiming instantly." mean?
    - Stalwart Servant (Page 146). If you use it on a Vampire or a Ghoul, can they spend Vitae to use the active effects of the Discipline?

    Cuac
    - With Touch of Morrigan, do you hold the "charge" to touch someone later, or do you have to do it as part of the ritual?

    Theban
    - With "Gift of Lazarus," can you re-use the rite to extend the zombie's lifespan?

    Coils and Scales
    - Can you store prepared skin from Flesh Graft Treatment? (Page 156)
    - For Beast's Hunger, what is "intensity of the Kindred's predatory aura"? Does that mean it adds to Lashing Out rolls? (Page 157)
    - With Augmented Vitae Drought, can you leave the needles and tubing in, under your clothes to create a drought on the spot? (Page 157)
    - For Surgical Heart Removal, the part saying it takes "about six hours" clashes with the extended action portion, unless one takes that as fluff. What's correct? (Page 158)
    - What happens when someone who is already addicted to Vitae tastes someone who has Taste of Fealty's blood? (Page 159)
    - Can you use the Blood Cleansing Ritual to remove your own Blood Bond? (Page 160)'

      (Note: I'm not a developer or anything like that so this is all personal interpretation and what I've heard before)

      Originally posted by SoulGambit View Post
      Blood Bonds Do supernatural creatures other than Vampires get a chance to resist the Blood Bond? It isn't Resist Trait + Supernatural Advantage, so I'm not sure. Does the Increased Duration bonus of Bond 3 also apply to Devotions? Can you spend Willpower when resisting the Blood Bond?
      Other supernatural creatures don't by default, as it's not resisting a power or a Clash of Wills, by RAW, but the developers might have intended otherwise, and it's a very reasonable house rule to allow it.

      Generally, things that enhance disciplines in general will also enhance devotions, I believe.

      No, you can't spend willpower for a bonus because you need to spend willpower to even make the resistance roll in the first place.

      Lashing Out Do supernatural creatures other than Vampires add their Supernatural Advantage if they opt to Fight? Do other forms of Supernatural Advantage count as "Blood Potency" or qualify the other creature as "Counting as a Vampire" for purposes of needing to spend Willpower? If a supernatural creature other than a Vampire wins a Fight contest, they get the same +2 bonus that Mortals get, correct?
      Other supernatural creatures will usually have their own Aura mechanic; a Werewolf would use their Hunter's Aspect instead of using the pool listed for vampires, for instance. If they don't, for instance like with Demons or with something like Spirits, then I believe they would add their Supernatural Advantage, as that seems a bit more like resistance, even if using a power attribute.

      As for willpower, I have no idea there, but my personal call would be if they have an Aura mechanic then yes, it requires Willpower, since they can push back with the same force, but otherwise no.

      Anyone that wins against the Vampire while using Fight gets the +2, so yes, a Supernatural would too.

      Merits
      - Swarm Form (114). Does taking this Merit expand the list of things you can get with Beast's Skin to include Size 0 creatures? If yes, that means like bugs, right? Does Swarm Form replace Beast's Skin's normal advantages or augment them? I.E. Does the Strength + Brawl roll use your normal traits, or the trait of the individual animals you create? If you use Vigor to add damage levels to your attack, how does that apply? To one target? Once per target? Or extra successes you can spread around? If you activate Swarm Form and Obfuscate 3 at one time and attack someone, does that reveal your entire swarm or just the ones that attacked that person?
      - Does Indomitable (page 121) apply to resisting the blood bond? If you choose to Fight when someone Lashes Out at you, does Indomitable apply? If yes, to one or both sides (since both are the "aggressors," arguably).
      - Does Iron Will (page 121) ever apply against supernatural powers?
      - Parkour 5 (Page 122). How long does Freeflow last?
      The swarm form replaces what you get with Beast's skin; you can become a swarm of size 0 creatures (like bugs) but not a single one of them, only a swarm.You use your normal Strength + Brawl, Vigor would add to the damage pool that you get to spread around before the fact. Using Obfuscate 3 with it would reveal the whole swam because the swarm is all 'you', so if even one attacks, 'you' attacked, so 'you' are revealed.

      Indomitable would not apply to resisting the blood bond, as that's not a power nor is it contested. As for lashing out, I'm not entirely certain, I could see that being a bit easier to argue either way.

      As for Iron Will... probably not directly, but likely it would apply indirectly if they used a power that affected the Social system and then tried to make use of it.

      Freeflow lasts until you stop your free running for one reason or another.

      Animalism
      - Summon the Hunt (Page 127). How would you handle gathering together a sizable swarm of, say, Rats and having them attack some dudes?
      It works as written; you use Summon the Hunt, and if successful, you summon the rats and then can give all of them the command of 'attack those dudes'. If you mean, like, how many rats come, I'd personally say to just treat it like (successes) rat swarms that individually attack using swarm mechanics.

      Protean
      - Beast's Skin (Page 141). Can you eat multiple forms in the span of a few hours and then sleep for one day and night to incorporate all of them, or must you sleep for one day and night per form?
      - Primeval Miasma (Page 141). Are the Vampire's actions in any way limited while in this form? Can they still physically interact with the world around them? Can they use Disciplines? Does feeding still produce The Kiss or The Assault? Does stealing someone's breath leave a discernible cause of death? If someone is using Primeval Miasma at the same time as Obfuscate, and someone walks in the cloud, does that break Obfuscate or does the Vampire need to actually do something to them?
      For Beast's Skin, I'd personally say you could, but I could see that argued either way.

      For Primeval Miasma, they're only as physical as smoke would ever be; they can touch things, but can't really manipulate them. For disciplines, since it doesn't prevent it, you can probably use anything that suits the form (Vigor won't do squat for you, but Celerity would work fine). Feeding would provide the normal effects. Killing someone that way likely looks like asphyxiation of some sort. People won't walk into your cloud when you use Obfuscate for the same reason they won't walk into a physical Obfuscated vampire, but it'd break if you decided to 'engulf' them, I'd imagine.

      Devotions
      - Quicken Sight (Page 145). What does "and can benefit from aiming instantly." mean?
      - Stalwart Servant (Page 146). If you use it on a Vampire or a Ghoul, can they spend Vitae to use the active effects of the Discipline?
      My guess is it means that you can aim on the same turn you fire, meaning you essentially get a +1 bonus to ranged attacks, but I'm not entirely sure there.

      Yes, they can use their own sources of Vitae normally.

      Cuac
      - With Touch of Morrigan, do you hold the "charge" to touch someone later, or do you have to do it as part of the ritual?
      You hold the charge.

      Theban
      - With "Gift of Lazarus," can you re-use the rite to extend the zombie's lifespan?
      No, but you can extend the ritual duration (with ST permission) using the chart in Blood Sorcery S&B; the developers have commented those charts were used to create the success ranges for the new versions of rituals, and can be used to adjust the target.

      Coils and Scales
      - Can you store prepared skin from Flesh Graft Treatment? (Page 156)
      - For Beast's Hunger, what is "intensity of the Kindred's predatory aura"? Does that mean it adds to Lashing Out rolls? (Page 157)
      - With Augmented Vitae Drought, can you leave the needles and tubing in, under your clothes to create a drought on the spot? (Page 157)
      - For Surgical Heart Removal, the part saying it takes "about six hours" clashes with the extended action portion, unless one takes that as fluff. What's correct? (Page 158)
      - What happens when someone who is already addicted to Vitae tastes someone who has Taste of Fealty's blood? (Page 159)
      - Can you use the Blood Cleansing Ritual to remove your own Blood Bond? (Page 160)'
      Probably not; the 24 hour mark seems to be pretty specific, you use it at that point or it loses potency.

      Yes, you'd add it to Lashing out or Fighting another vampire that's lashing out.

      No, you need to do the entire Procedure every single time, which includes cleaning them in the alchemical solution beforehand.

      The six hours is fluff.

      It just counts as two more drinks on top of what they've had before, with the other effects working normally.

      It would likely be up to an ST if the Dragon could even manage the process on themselves to begin with, likely requiring at the very least some heavily penalized Res + Comp rolls, but if they somehow could it probably would work.

          << If a supernatural creature other than a Vampire wins a Fight contest, they get the same +2 bonus that Mortals get, correct? >>

          They would get whatever bonus is provided by successfully Lashing Out using their respective aura.

          << - For Beast's Hunger, what is "intensity of the Kindred's predatory aura"? Does that mean it adds to Lashing Out rolls? (Page 157) >>

          It means your effective Blood Potency for purposes of how far you can detect something using Kindred Senses or whether or not you have to spend willpower to Lash Out in retaliation. The rules for Lashing Out expressly say other powers do not add to that roll, unless the power's description says they add dice to Lashing Out.

          << - Does Indomitable (page 121) apply to resisting the blood bond? >>

          You're only entitled to resist the blood bond if you're a vampire in the first place. I would say no, it does not affect the blood bond itself (there are other advantages to employing it), only attempts to actively use your thoughts or emotions that have been warped against you.

          << If you choose to Fight when someone Lashes Out at you, does Indomitable apply? If yes, to one or both sides (since both are the "aggressors," arguably). >>

          The aggressor is the one who initiated the encounter. I would say no. 1. The mechanics for Lashing Out are prohibitive as to when a power adds to the dice roll. 2. It would protect against a kindred seeing to exploit the attendant Conditions Lashing Out would leverage, not placing them in the first place. (Note: Indomitable automatically cancels out the +2 they would normally get if they successfully invoked a Condition from Lashing Out.)

          << - Does Iron Will (page 121) ever apply against supernatural powers? >>

          If those powers entail facilitating a Social interaction for the user or are affected by your Willpower or Resolve to compel you into doing something, yes.

          << For Surgical Heart Removal, the part saying it takes "about six hours" clashes with the extended action portion, unless one takes that as fluff. What's correct? (Page 158) >>

          There's no conflict, by default, you have six hours to accumulate 10 successes on a series of Intelligence + Medicine rolls, each roll represenets 1 hour of work. if you don't the procedure fails. You don't get to keep rolling a number of times equal to your Intelligence + Medicine score beyond six hours until you succeed because the patient dies. But if you have merits like Patient or Good Time Management you can get additional rolls.

          << Can you use the Blood Cleansing Ritual to remove your own Blood Bond? >>

          No you can't waterboard yourself with a mixture of animal blood, entrails, and your own contaminated vitae to purge yourself of a blood bond.
