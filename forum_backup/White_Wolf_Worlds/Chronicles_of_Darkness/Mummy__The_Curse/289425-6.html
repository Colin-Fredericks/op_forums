                                Holic in general is kind of a grab bag for the whole World of Darkness. Yuuko herself fits in as a Mage, a fae, a ghost, a mummy, etc. The spirits and ghosts can be construed in any number of ways, from the afore mentioned to fae to infernal demons to supernal and astral entitites-often within the same creature. It's flexible and doesn't offer answers easily, fitting into the toolbox mystery nature of the setting beautifully.

                                If I ever get around to finishing it(I know spoilers, no fear), I might just declare it one of the perfect sources for WoD period.

                                --------

                                So prior to finding out about the Temakhs, I had been devising a character for Idigam, a girl who's played it safe her entire life, but with the first change has started to try sloughing that off, including pursuing her desire to be a DJ. I dunno if I could get a group who could do a whole music theme thing, but that was in my head.

                                Then we found out about the Temakhs, and now I want a battle between a scrappy electronic music pack up against a Deceived performance hall. Classic and more established music against the chaotic experimentality and less validated of the younger genres, an allegory I suppose.

                                I also want an excuse to play Les Friction's Louder Than Words, but it doesn't have to be in that context.
