                                                Ok, so, I've been reading the next chapter of the Avarice Chronicle and... it's a bit strange. The bit that really stands out to me is the finality with which it seems to treat an Arisen's death during the scenes at Sybaris. First, it was in regards to Akhomet's death by the Roller. Given the way Mummies work, the Roller could have killed her, consumed her body, and she would have simply undergone a Death Cycle and reformed around a canopic jar and/or been resummoned by her cult at a later date, rather than the the kind of destruction the story seems to imply has occurred. Death by Shuankhsen can certainly be hazardous, but that's due to the risk of returning as a Shuankhsen rather than the permanent ending implied in the text.

                                                On its own, I would simply put that down to some kind of power peculiar to the Roller. But in the next scene the Mummies are confronted by a chorus of seven Deceived, and it seems to think that if the Mummies don't behave in a civil manner and restrain themselves from fighting the Deceived then "they will never be heard from again". Except... again, even the total destruction of their present bodies doesn't prevent them from just reforming around a canopic jar (probably on the other side of the planet) a few hours or days later.

                                                Anyone else a bit perplexed by this sample adventure?

                                                        "The characters must negotiate with the Deceived, or they will never be heard from again."

                                                        That wording doesn't exactly made me think, "The characters must negotiate with the Deceived, or they will wake up somewhere else a few hours later. If they are close to the end of their Descents and perform particularly badly on their Death Cycles, they might even need to wait years."

                                                        It's just really strange. The story seems to put a whole lot more gravitas on a Mummy dying than the mechanics do. Even at Sekhem 2, you have to perform badly on your Death Cycle for that to end your Descent. With Akhomat, she's Sekhem 3 and Memory 8. The Roller would have had to strip 2 Sekhem from her with his attacks (two separate attacks that each deal 3+ Aggravated damage) for her to be forced to the end of the Descent by that encounter.
