                            Sorry about the double post. I wanted to separate out the questions I had from any other forum post I might make on here.

                            With CAS being around now I thought I better capitalize on the chance to ask him some questions about the planned products for the Mummy game line. Since we've got a chance to increase the transparency of the lines development now.

                            I've listed the various products in the order that they were 50th and currently final Kickstarter backers e-mail for Mummy the Curse on the 3rd of December 2013. For the sake of completeness I've included products in the line that are already out.

                            In general, even if you can't answer the questions I pose CAS for spoiler reasons a more detailed update on where each of these projects is at would be great.

                            - MtC Ready Made Characters: Already released as of December 23rd 2013

                            - Guildhalls of the Deathless: Already released as of October 30th 2013

                            - Cursed Necropolis: D.C.: As of 1st of April its has all Art for it completed and is currently in layout. Written by Harry Heckel, (who did the classic Vampire the the Masquerade book DC By Night) and Neall Raemonn Price (who wrote on Exalted's Manual of Exalted Power: Alchemists)are writing this.

                            Questions:

                            1) To reiterate Cleverest of Things is this book going to contain some new content material beyond being a setting book e.g. antagonists, relics and such?

                            2) Is the aim to get this out before the end of April?

                            - Book of the Deceived: As of 1st of April Redlines (on second drafts I believe) one writer had to drop his section so a new one is being located.

                            Questions:

                            1) Who are the writers on this one? I know you mentioned CAS that you wanted very particular people working on this book, also who is the new writer?

                            2) Can you explain some of the stuff that made this book such a difficult one to develop? Obviously this might be nothing to do with the book itself and everything to do with everything else going on and distracting you from working on it. Still, I got the impression from your previous comments about this book that you wanted it to be a particular way due to it being the "spoiler" book for the whole line basically.

                            3) Can you give you any new info/teases about the Deceived? Mainly I' interested in knowing how they play differently from the Arisen while still being Mummy's and still being PC characters. Obviously without spoilers, can you give any info on how they might "play differently" from the Arisen?

                            - Mummy Fiction Anthology: In December this was in the "Writing" stage.

                            Questions:

                            1) Who are the writers for this? Can you give us any hints as to if the story's are interlinked or stand alone?

                            2) Is this still being written or has it moved onto editing? (Do fiction anthology's have a redline stage?)

                            - Dreams of Avarice: In December a writer had been brought onboard and was being contracted.

                            Questions:

                            1) Did you get the writer you wanted?

                            2) Is this in the writing stage or has it moved any further?

                            - Mummy: The Curse Novel: As of the first week of December Rich T and Colin were discussing this.

                            Questions:

                            1) Has a writer been contacted? If so can you tease where the novel will be set (or when for that matter)?

                            - Sothis Ascends: This was not actually included in the December update because its the last book on the official release line for Mummy that we know of.

                            Questions:

                            1) Is this still in concept?

                            2) Do you know which writers you want for this book? Are any of them working on Deceived?

                            3) When you think writers might be able to make a start on drafts for this one?

                            Apart from these projects (yes I'm aware I missed out Cursed Necropolis: Rio, I could not think of any questions for it) there are two other projects relating to Mummy currently in the works:

                            - Hunter the Vigil: Mortal Remains: As of the 1st of April this has had to go back into editing and has art coming in for it.

                            Questions:

                            1) How much involvement did you have with the Mummy chapter of the book? Your comment earlier in the thread implies that you did not get first pass or something.

                            2) What are the themes you think putting a Mummy into a Hunter game/setting can invoke? Human fragility in the face of the timeless/immortal is one I think can be focused on, but I'm very interested to hear your take on this issue.

                            - World of Darkness: Dark Eras: As of the 1st of April this is in mostly second drafts with one exception.

                            Questions:

                            1) We know the Mummy setting is sorta 1880-1920's British Empire, which makes sense with the Egyptian craze that swept through English society at the time. Do you know who's writing the Mummy Chapter for Dark Era's?

                            2) Is there anything in particular that you want to do with/in the Dark Era's Mummy expansion? Considering that Sothis Ascends is going to address the issue of playing Mummy in other time periods in general. Do you think you could use the Dark Era's setting to "focus in" on that idea with a unique example?

                            I know there is a lot to cover here. If you can answer even a fraction of these questions CAS I'll be a happy man.

                            All my frustrations and negativity were pretty much coming out of the lack of communication and transparency between the devs and fans over the last sixth months really. So I'm happy that your back and around and really, really hoping this can be the start of a new phase of Mummy development that leaves the issues that have previously plagued the line in the past at last.

                            Many Thanks,

                                            Hiya,

                                            We're definitely hoping to get at least the beta-proof on D.C. out by month's end, yep.

                                            The writers on Book of the Deceived include Malcolm Sheppard, George Holochwost, Michael Goodwin, Heather Curatola, and myself. The Deceived are Deathless, like the Arisen, but are ultimately more different from the Arisen than, say, the vampires of Belial's Brood are from the vampires of other covenants. A different breed.

                                            The anthology will be called Curse of the Blue Nile. It has been contracted and is being written. One of the authors is a six-time Bram Stoker Award-winner.

                                            Likewise, both Dreams of Avarice and the tie-in novel have their writers and things are progressing apace. The former is being written by Malcolm Sheppard.

                                            Sothis Ascends is coming due for redlines, and I've already seen some text. Good stuff. As mentioned, the Gentleman Gamer is one of the writers.

                                            The Mummy chapter of Dark Eras has been turned in and is now in development. It was written by Jesse Heinig.

                                            I've had no involvement in any Hunter books.

                                                            Hiya folks. I was super-stoked to work on the Dark Eras material for Mummy at the behest of CAS. I can't go into a lot of detail about it as it's in development hands now, but it was a great honor to work on such a striking game and I hope that I did justice to the important themes and topics of Mummy.

                                                            My goal was to cover the era in a way that would give inspirations and story hooks to Mummy players who want to use that period of history. In this information age, anyone can look up the historical facts of the Boer War or the fall of the Ottoman Empire or the formation of Turkey. What's useful is giving people ideas on how to place those elements into their chronicles.

                                                            The Mummy segment of Dark Eras is also useful even to people who don't set a chronicle in that period, in that it can serve as a flashback device. Mummies might acquire lost memories of time spent in that era. Plus, that time period is recent enough that cultists might be grandchildren of people who lived through some of those events, which allows groups to play with narrative threads that follow the familial and cultural ties of various mummy cultists and their mortal associates.

                                                            Naturally, I also included a few new Merits and powers that are pertinent to the time period.

                                                            I'm sure CAS will let you know more as the book gets closer to release!
