    Need a little help with my campaign...

    12-10-2013, 08:09 PM
    Hi all,

    So I need a little help with my ongoing Mummy game.

    To give some perspective on the game: -

    Its a Changeling/Mummy crossover (we've been calling it Chummy) that's centered around the theme of Identity (as its the theme that crosses over between the two games most obviously).

    The games been running since April of this year and is at current about a third of the way through the "second act" (its got 3 acts in total, with act 1 being the largest).

    The setting is the City of York and the play-style was very "slice of life" (literally every day has been played through from April 9th to April 16th before IC events meant the group "time-skipped" to June 23rd and played until June 26th) with hidden truths. Some major "behind the scenes" manipulation is going on and due to both the PC Mummy's have been running into plots that their past selves kicked off centuries and decades ago. Likewise, the Changelings memories of her durance have been proven to be faulty a few times and simply absent in others.

    Recently the main player group (two high Memory Mummy's (they both have worked their way up to a rating of 7 from 3) and a Changeling) have gone off to do some "memory" hunting in places around the world they remember as important in past descents. They've visited Budapest, Istanbul (which they plan on returning too, as they have unfinished business there) and have just arrived in Rome. The tone of the game has also changed, taking on a more "episodic" feel (not my usual ST style) with "downtimes" of a couple of days as the group travels from city to city.

    I've got a lot of the plot I wanted to run for them sorted in Rome. However I wrote it all about 4 weeks ago (real life has gotten in the way of me running the session since then) and looking back on it I'm not warming to my own stuff so much.

    To give some perspective on the OC circumstances: -

    As I mentioned, the game-style has changed to one that is not typical style. However, while I'm trying to engage with it I don't find it inherently troublesome. Good to broaden play styles and all that. There are a number of contextual points that I feel bear some import on the campaigns continuation:

    1) I usually run Campaigns for about a year to a year and a half, so running anything is massive commitment for me.

    2) This particular Campaign has suffered from thematic issues between Mummy and Changeling and the choices I have made in my design of the metaphysics and metaplot of my world. This has led to group issues (the Changeling was getting carried around like a "backpack" for a while and was having trouble contributing).

    3) I tried to ad-lib some stuff when it came to the Changeling part of the game (as I had not re-read the books for about a year when writing this campaign), two of my players are ST and rules ST for our local Changeling Live game. They picked up on my less then complete games mastery and slight unease with Changeling in general (I like the game, but I don't completely "get it" and have issue with the amount its content gets "fanboyed" into other gamelines sometimes) so I since have re-read the books.

    4) I'm writing a new Vampire setting (using Blood and Smoke) for a local Vampire Live game which is going to be eating my time once that book is out. So I'm not likely to have so much time or Chummy at that point.

    5) I have a habit of getting board about 9 months into my campaigns and get excited about Onyx Path new gamelines. I'm part of the Demon kickstarter and really like the look of the game, so I'll likely be reading the pdf soon. Not constructive to Chummy writing (I don't want to include Demons in my setting).

    6) I'm currently working 2 jobs (which I was not when I started running this campaign) so I have even less time to write then I did (or would like to).

    So to finally get the point, with all the above being true and very general can you helpful, wonderful people here on the Onyx Path forums help me out?

    Basically, considering all the above, I'm very interested in peoples general thoughts for plot ideas for the campaign (Roman themed, working with the theme of Identity, cool possible points of crossover for Changeling and Mummy's etc). As I'm hoping a bit of a brain storm and discussion might spark something in my mind and get me over my current block.

    Thanks beforehand to everyone who takes the time to contribute to this thread.

    "There is no God in the Badlands." - Sunny

              Well you should give some spotlight to the Changeling soon, I would think.

              Perhaps the Changeling's Keeper has been stealing people from all over the world, or at least from the contemporary nations of the ancient Roman empire. Perhaps It fancies itself as a Caesar-style Emperor, or it abducts those with imperial blood in them. As the Mummies travel over old Rome and re-discover their Memories, so the Changeling should be piecing together who their Keeper was, why It abducted him, and how it can prevent more abductions from occurring.

              What level of Sekhem are your Mummies at right now? The Descent chart is easy to calculate an estimate of how long the rest of their Descent will be. Plan your chronicle ending roughly around that time. The Descent is finite, and so, apparently, is your own time, energy, and interest so it's a very good time-frame to use, and it's best to give your story a bang at the finish rather than a drawn out whimper.

              There's two goals here: for the Mummies, they're reclaiming their Memory; for the Changeling, it's re-integrating themselves into society and, at least one day, ending their Keeper. Will the Keeper die? Probably not, if your time is on a budget. The same goes for Mummies claiming Apotheosis. So keep the stakes on their scale

                          well, ive got a couple of ideas to consider. the first one involves an area that is an overlap between the two games--Fate. Mummies interact with fate in various ways, and at the very least believe in it. changeling, however, is directly intwined into fate. to bring in more changeling esque elements, plllay up on that. for example, maybe one of the mummies begins to realize that Fate drives him to repeat certain patterns after a certain number of years pass, or play a certain role. he may be drawn to fulfil these actions, and the changelings are the only ones who know fate's mechanisms well enough to stop it. or, perhaps, play up on the storyline of the changeling by showing that the mummies have been remembering this prophecy, and bits of it, over the ages. as one of the mummies was called for that purpose, it resonates. perhaps the bad thing happening to her wasnt arcadia, but something else. bringing in the fate mechanics from dancers in the dusk would highlight the themes. perhaps this changeling, like her "keeper", is true fae? if that is the case, she could have past life memories of her own, alongside the mummies. as for Rome specifically, you can bring in some colorful changeling stuff by playing up on the idea that the seasonal courts may have possibly been born in rome, potentially around the time of the third sothic cycle. it could be possible that the mummies experienced the birth and spread of these courts to the areas outside of rome. playing up on the idea that fate and the fae sometimes chose the same roles to be played by people over time, you could allow the changeling player to take part in those flashbacks as a changeling very much like her from the same keeper. You could even introduce a relic or token that somehow lets the modern changeling remember those events as though she lived them (letting her player keep the xp and not have to act ignorant of the events).
