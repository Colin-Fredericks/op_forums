      Arisen: The Arisen are mummies following the Judges, ancient pseudo-gods of a lost empire. Their purpose is to retrieve relics imbued with divine life-force, and send them back to the underworld. All Arisen (the Deathless) belong to one of five Guilds, organisations that reflect ancient trade guilds that helped build the Nameless Empire. To aid in their divine task, they form cults around themselves and their Judges using mortals. Each mummy makes a decree to one of the parts of the Egyptian five-part soul (this is the equivalent of Clan or Path in other NWoD games).

      Shuanksen: The Lifeless are mummies that were created when the the guildmasters of the Nameless Empire sacrificed countless slaves to develop the Rite of Return, the powerful spell that created the Arisen. The Shuanksen, rather than serving the Judges, follow the Devourer. Some Shuanksen were once Arisen, but through a variety of ways, they fell from grace.

      Deceived: Members of the lost sixth guild, whose members were artists. Little is known about them, only that they were betrayed by the other five guilds, and none of the Arisen remember why. (Not a lot has been released on the Deceived; their book is coming out soon, though).

      That's all off the top of my head, so people can correct me if I've made any vital errors, or left out anything really important. The core rule book lays things out pretty well, though you have to filter through some of the mythos to get at the basics. Also, depending on if you're a player or an ST, the second half of the core book has background details meant for the Storyteller only. That said, knowing the stuff in the second half hasn't strongly affected my view of the game as a player, so take that with a grain of salt.

          I'm not sure whether to spoiler this or not, so in deference to the player-storyteller information divide, I'll err on the side of spoiler conservation.


          The Arisen are the dead-and-living eternal servants of the Judges of Duat, who labor to secure the holy relics of their people and deliver them in honor of the Duat. Their memory weakens with the weight of ages, due to some quality of the Rite of Return that binds them to service.

          The Shuankhsen are the price paid to create the Arisen, a tithe of eternal slaves paid to the cosmic devourer. Her rite does not weaken their memory, but wounds their eternal soul, leaving them wretched and spiritually starving.

          The Deceived are... well, their book isn't out yet. We know that they are much like the Arisen, that they seem to maintain a greater memory of the ages that birthed them, and that they nurse a vicious grudge which fuels them to turn blasphemously against the rightful Judges of Duat recognized by the Arisen.

                      It's kind of important to note, I feel, that in the player section the information on the Shuankhsen and the Deceived (mostly the Shuankhsen) is a bit different to that in the GM section.

                      From the perspective of a player, a Shuankhsen is some kind of feral undead abomination. It's clearly animated by a power similar to the Rite of Return. Most Mummies put their existence down to blasphemous attempts by lesser cultures to mimic the Rite of Return, although it is known that a few Shuankhsen are corrupted Arisen.

                      The Deceived, meanwhile, are more like a myth or legend. They're heretical masters of magic, as far as the other guilds are concerned, and may or may not have survived Irem's fall. Some Mummies don't even believe there was a sixth guild. If they do exist though, they are clearly working against the will of the Judges.

                          The Shuankhsen are monsters. They're immortal just like you, but wrong. Where other Arisen are your allies and rivals, Shuankhsen cannot ever be anything other than enemies. They derive power from eating your body. They're less powerful than you, individually, but still immortal. Plus, they know some nasty tricks - claws and jaws and things that make it easier for them to eat you.
                          The Judges like it if you put them down like the rabid dogs they are.

                          The Deceived don't exist. They're a footnote in the history of the Nameless Empire. Just because some Closed Books have a few scrolls on them doesn't make it less of an affront against the will of the Judges of Duat to discuss matters pertaining to the Lost Guild, so you and I should stop talking about them - *thinking* about them - right now.
