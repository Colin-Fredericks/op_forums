                                                    Personally, I have very few criticisms about the way Mummy has been written. It honestly feels better "balanced", for want of a better word, than many of the other games I've played. Changeling, as the most recent one, springs to mind. There are far fewer dud Affinities and almost no dud Utterances (Waters of Life and Death is the only one where I can't conceive of any reason to actually want it) than there were clauses and contracts, for example, and generally the mechanics of the game are a lot more clear. I don't find that there are any major ambiguities in central game mechanics, and the vestige thing just struck me as odd in that a core part of the mechanics of an entire category of vessels was relegated to a city book supplement, rather than the core rules where they discuss how vestiges function.

                                                    Mummy is my favourite RPG, bar none. It is a beautifully written book, and as much as the slow pace is killing me, I wouldn't wish the writing team to change in the slightest. You guys have already produced the best roleplaying product I have ever had the pleasure to spend money on, and once the books are all out I won't even have my own impatience detracting from the affection I have for the line.
