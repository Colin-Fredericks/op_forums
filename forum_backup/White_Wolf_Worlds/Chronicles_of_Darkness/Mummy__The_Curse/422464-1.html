                                                        Maybe things went horribly wrong to begin with. Let's see some unexpected side-effects of the Rite of Return:
                                                        • Mummies are not perfectly obedient, and have the Memory trait
                                                        • They awaken seemingly by Fate's decree during Sothic turns with no first purpose - surely something unintended by the Ritualists
                                                        • Cults may summon them and give a Mummy their own purpose
                                                        • They can rebuild their souls (Pillars), making Apotheosis is possible
                                                        • The Shuankhsen were the price they paid to Ammut, who taught them the Rite anyway and is the reason why the Arisen can devolve into Shuankhsen to begin with
                                                        • In the eyes of the Sixth Guild's temakhs, the whole betrayal thing was seen as an unexpected side-effect
                                                        • They can learn Utterances, a secret ripped from the fabric of the cosmos
                                                        Of course, maybe some of this was intended but the core book implies some of these things were definitely unforeseen. So let's play pretend for a moment and think, "Damn, if all this stuff was the Rite gone horribly wrong then what would it be like had it gone horribly right?"
