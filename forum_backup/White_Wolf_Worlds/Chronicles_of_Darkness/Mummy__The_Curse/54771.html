    Need help understanding the Judges

    01-21-2014, 02:17 PM
    Hello all. I'm about to run my first game of Mummy and I'm currently helping players through character creation, and I've run into something of a puzzle. It concerns the Judges.

    When the guildmember dies and enters Duat he is brought before the 42 Judges, who each test his soul before passing him on to the final Judge. The mummy to be makes his decree before this Judge. However, after that things become a bit unclear. What are the reasons for the Judge choosing the mummy to be? What does their relationship represent?

    What I'm wondering is this - Does the bond with the Judge represent the one sin the Arisen was most guilty of, or is it the sin he is supposed to guard against to honor his Judge?

    For example: Tubaal the Thrice Anointed's Judge is Ser-Kheru. Ser-Kehru is the Disposer of Speech, the Judge to all those who stir up strife among their own kind. Does Tubaal follow Ser-Kheru because he in life stirred up strife amongst the peoples of Irem and the Judge is punishing him, or did Ser-Kehru choose Tubaal because in life Tubaal fucking hated those who tried to stir up strife?
