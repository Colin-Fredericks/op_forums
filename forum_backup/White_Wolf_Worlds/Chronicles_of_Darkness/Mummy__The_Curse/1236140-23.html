                Can Sadikhs take Supernatural merits or are they considered as having a supernatural template and therefore those merits are off for them?

                EDIT/UPDATE: the Timeless Merit Supernatural Resistance (MtC2e, p.108) seems to suggest Sadikhs can use Supernatural Merits, just like Immortals.

                If creating a Sadikh character, do we follow the Arisen creation rules as a basis, like we do for Immortals, which means getting 10 Experience, or do we follow the mortal creation rules from Chronicles of Darkness, namely no Experience at start? Same question for mortal sorcerers and Witnesses in a cult.

                EDIT/UPDATE: apparently it's up to the ST, since MtC2e says one can create cultists from either that book (which appears to give 10 Experience to new characters) or the Chronicles of Darkness Rulebook (which doesn't give Experience). Ephemeral cultists get no Experience at start, as per BoLD pp.46-47. On the one side, it makes sense for Arisen, Sadikhs and Immortals to start with more Experience given their longevity. On the other, given the already large power gap before these character types, I fear a mixed meret would be heavily imbalanced if mortal and ephemeral cultists had zero Experience while Arisen and Immortals had 10 on top of their other advantages. Add to that that mortals created from the Chronicles of Darkness Rulebook also start with 7 dots of Merits instead of 10. I think it's better for balance's sake to give all cultists 10 dots of Merits and 10 Experience, to show that MtC is a game on a higher power level than others anyway.

                Is there any mechanical difference I may have missed between Balance/Burden and Virtue/Vice or have the Anchors just been renamed for thematic reasons?

                EDIT/UPDATE: apparently it's mostly a thematic change, as cultists can choose Virtue/Vice from the Balance/Burden list.

                    Originally posted by Hermeticus View Post
                    Is there any mechanical difference I may have missed between Balance/Burden and Virtue/Vice or have the Anchors just been renamed for thematic reasons?
                    You can recover one Willpower once per scene by indulging your Vice in a minor way. You can recover all Willpower once per session by acting out your Virtue in a major, dramatic way.

                    Both Balance and Burden can recover Willpower in both ways, one Willpower for a minor invocation or all Willpower through a major, dramatic embodiment.
