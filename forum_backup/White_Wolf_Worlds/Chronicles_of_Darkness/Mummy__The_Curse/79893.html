    Mummy Crossover - The "Necropolis: Chicago" Chronicle

    02-25-2014, 11:01 PM
    Hi folks!

    For some time, i've been looking online for opinions regarding Mummy crossovers. Im currently running three chronicles on the same Chicago setting, with interrelated stories. A Changeling: The Lost chronicle, a Vampire: The Requiem & Geist: The Sin Eaters crossover chronicle, and this.

    "Necropolis Chicago" is a Mummy, Geist and Mage crossover. The chronicle develops in the XXI century. at the start of a Sothic Turn.

    We have 5 characters in the Chronicle;

    Lily, a Sin Eater that died roughly 2 months ago in a plane accident. Her geist is a spooky pilot spectre that speaks to her through electric sounds, alarms, pre-recorded security messages, etc, and also directly with a voice that sounds like coming from an intercom. She is a very curious and good looking girl, always taking notes on the strange things she witnesses.

    Anaximandro, a priest in the local church, is a Sin Eater that died falling from a laurel tree while he gathered leaves. His geist is a red eyed crow that talks a lot, usually questioning his faith in God. Caring and helpful, he tries to help others but usually becomes angered by those who, in his eyes (therefore the eyes of God), misbehave.

    Both Sin Eaters were contacted after their return to life by Peter Pracownik, a secretive man with deep rooted connections in the police and with private investigators. Mr. Pracownik is the owner of a small paranormal investigation project called "Sleeper Cell INC", devoted to finding Sin Eaters in Chicago and creating a network that provides opportunities for the explotation of their newfound capabilities. Also, Sleeper Cell INC members have access to an encrypted online database, of the likes of Wikipedia, called ALICE, which they use to expand their knowledge of the occult.

    Then we have our Mage character; Kebechet (her shadowname) is an investigator for Mysterium. She bears the weight of a dark and violent past, which ultimately led her to becoming a hit-man (or hit-woman) for a local Mafia ran by Frank Herbert (yes, i use some writers/artists names in my chronicles :P). A couple of months before her awakening, Kebechet found out that the Mafia was actually a cult for an ancient god, and soon began asking questions about it. She then found out that the Mafia was actually a cult for a very old pseudo-egyptian deity (one of the Mummy PCs). After her awakening, Kebechet offered her new capabilities to the service of the Herberts in exchange of information, knowledge, and the chance of someday seeing the Mummy and learning from her directly.

    Our Mummies are;

    Khalid, a Tef-Aabhi that rests beneath the Herbert manor, slowly regaining consciousnes and finding himself to be an aesthete that is amazed (and derailed) by the wonders of the new century. Soon enough, Qerrti puts him back on track, and begins finding his darker nature, as he locates a former owner of the relic he located with his kepher. He gets the information needed from this person thanks to his cult's connections (a Conspiracy) and his regained skills for torture. Soon, he finds out he is not alone...

    Blakanis is a Su-Menent mummy who wakes up in the middle of a turmoil caused by a hostile spirit that has taken the tomb. The cultists (Cult is Tribal) praise their newly awoken deity and John Smith, the cult leader, notifies Blakanis about the presence of this ghost. Blakanis is a determined and ruthless follower of his Judge. He is proud and efficient, and expects to see this values spread through the ranks of the cult. His tomb is a complex underground fortress that is build below a park near the heart of the city.

    ------------------

    The Story So Far:

    //////SPOILER ALERT//////

    John Smith, Blakanis's cult leader, tells his master that he made contact with a group of people with special qualities through a PI friend, and that they are soon to arrive to the tomb. This group of people are, of course, Lily and Anaximandro, members of Sleeper Cell INC, who have no idea that they are going for a ghost-busting job to the ancient lair of a 6.000 years old entity.
    After making first contact with the mummy, which the Sin Eaters observe as a both dead-and-alive entity, they start moving carefully through the tomb hallways, and lastly identify the ghostly threat. They dispose of it, and Father Anaximandro lets the ghostly remnants of the spirit to fade, unwilling to terminate it. The Mummy insists on them finishing the job of destroying the ghost, and the Sin Eaters contact Pracownik, telling him about the mummy. Peter Pracownik bids Lily and Father Anaximandros to stay close to the mummy as their assistants, and to take notes that would prove invaluable for the enrichment of the ALICE database.

    After this is done, the Mummy sees a relic through kepher; a statue of Qerrti, who is somewhere in Chicago. He doesn't know though, that the relic is owned by Khalid, another mummy that has woken up due to the Sothic Turn. Both mummies experience some flashbacks of their last descent, in which they fought side by side, although no one remembers who the other Arisen is.

    ////////////////////////

    This is, more or less, the basis of how the chronicle is developed. Just wanted to share it so we could fight off those who usually say that Mummy can't be run as a Crossover. I'll be reporting from time to time to tell you guys how the thing goes -not going to post much about the story thou, my players check this forums too!

      Interesting set up. I think having the Sin Eaters be part of what is basically a tier 2 Crewe is a good way of giving them a bit more order and direction.

      I'm of the opinion that Mummy (along with Promethean) is the hardest game to crossover. Mainly because Mummy (once again like Promethean) is very, very self involved as a game and is very caught up in its own history and setting. The fact that is offers no support for crossover in the core book nor has it been talked about in any of the other Core lines for the purposes of crossover do not help its cause.

      However, I just yesterday finished running a Mummy/Changeling Campaign that's been going on for about 9 months. So it can be done. I learned a lot of the pitfalls of trying to crossover Mummy in that time. Namely, if you include the Mummy backstory stuff, its very easy to make that the focus of the game and to have a great deal of the metaplot run off that. Meaning that your other supernaturals can end up being there simply to facilitate and expansion of Mummy plot. This was a massive problem for me at the start of my campaign and nearly broke it up about 4 months in.

      Of course, if your running a game that is not going to delve into the more meta (plot or physics) then I don't think this issue will arise (no pun intended). Certainly if I ran a Mummy Crossover again I would stay away from those aspects.

      For what its worth I think Sin Eaters cross over much better with Mummy then any other line save Vampire. Due to the amount of Necromantic themes that are present in both games. Mage is an interesting one to include, as it can (particularly as characters get more XP) come to dominate a game. Out of interest what path is the Mage Character? Moros?

      Overall, very good set up, look forward to more play reports.

              Yes indeed, the Mage is a Moros .

              The alternatives for crossover are several. You can make them part of the cult, you can give them a common purpose or have them struck a deal to work together for mutual benefit. You can also link a mummy and another inmortal/mature or old/long living supernatural by making them part of the past experiences of the mummy.

              On a more general note, crossovers in my opinion need certain things to work. The thing you usually dont have for granted with crossovers is the sense of community among individuals of the same species. To make a crossover work, each kind of supernatural has to be backed up or be part of a fully fledged organisation. What im talking about is; a Krewe in a Geist chronicle is made a living organism by the players, that shape the organisation with their decisions and personalities. When you only have one or two Sin Eaters, combined with other supernaturals, you have to create a solid organisation by yourself, feed it into life with interesting NPCs, and if possible, making the whole "dealing with other supernaturals" thing something they are pretty involved into -and possibly, one of the central pillars of what the Chronicle is about.

              Even if Mummy is usually a self centered game, i think the reason it has good potential for crossover is the fact that each mummy comes with his own independent community (the cult) that not only does not need other PCs involvement for it to become alive, but it also serves as a great tool to integrate other supernaturals with the mummy. And yes, the sinergy with Geist that mummy has is pretty cool. They share lots of powers that deal with ghosts, and mummies even get an affinity that allows them to see Twilight beings.
