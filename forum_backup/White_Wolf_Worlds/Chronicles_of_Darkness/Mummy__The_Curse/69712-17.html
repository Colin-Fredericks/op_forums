                        Once again, to be the pessimist in a group of optimists...

                        Any additional books for Mummy beyond the ones we know about is at current pure speculation.

                        Considering Mummy is a "limited line" like Promethean, Changeling, Geist and Demon it will have a set number of "releases" in its initial run. Usually these lines then get some minor support in general supplements such as Glimpses of the Unknown and the upcoming Dark Eras. How many books each line gets is does depend on the line. Promethean only got 4 books (which is frankly all it really needed), Geist only got 1 (due to troubles at White Wolf at the time), Changeling and Hunter were popular enough they both got "expanded" lines up to around 8 books during their initial run (and a couple extra just focused on them after the fact).

                        Mummy is in a bit of a strange situation. We know that it was supposed to have the core book plus 3 splat books (Guildhalls, Deceived, Sothis). The Kickstarter added city books in DC and Rio, a prop book in Dreams and to fiction books (Curse of the Blue Nile and the yet unnamed novel). As I mentioned previously Mummy is also getting a chapter in the Dark Era's book and might get additional content depending on how that Kickstarter goes (and its format, since Rich has suggested some interesting things). Anything else, is as I mentioned pure speculation at this point.

                        As Reseru pointed out Mummy is likely to see a 2E in 2017, likely towards the end of that year, so about three years from now. In that time all the other pending releases for Mummy are going to make it out.

                        For me its that final factor that's the deciding one. Mummy will soon (in the next 18 months) be in the minority of NWoD's still in 1E (Hunter and Geist will likely have announced 2E by then and everything from Vampire-Changeling will likely be out) and it will be the only one getting new content with the 1E rule set by that point. Considering the development time for these books it could take that 18 months (or longer) to get Dark Eras,Sothis, Rio, Dreams, (possible Dark Era's bonus content), Curse of the Blue Nile and the Novel out to the fans. Its likely that only after all these planned books are out that Onyx Path will be able to take a look at the sales numbers for the line that they will then decide doing more with the line.

                        Considering that this possible takes us into early-mid 2016 remember to get all this stuff out (once again, I'm presuming a consistent pace of development with realistic delays here). Not that big a deal on face value. However, I'm sure their will be substantial lead in time to Second Edition Mummy's development as it seems to be the case with the other Second Edition games. For example, BlackHatMatt mentioned that Promethean 2E has been in development for about 6 months before it was announced. Likewise 2E Mage had a fair bit of planning and writing done on it before it was announced I think. Which means at least a years with of development before release. Meaning work on Mummy 2E could start in mid-late 2016. Once the development of the new 2E core has started I doubt the 1E version of the line will see any updates.

                        I'm sure if their is time and the sales are going on the books we know about they might try and squeeze another book it. Book of Going Westward would be a good choice for sure. It was a possible Kickstarter goal and people seemed to want it pretty bad (the fact its still talked about now, nearly two years later, is a good clue to its potential popularity). Ultimately, its a matter of time and timing.

                        Considering my situation (my local group not touching Mummy with a 10ft pole until it hits 2E) I'd be happy for them to finish all the books they have planned for now by the end of 2015 and then have a year to do development on 2E before its even announced. Obviously, more content is good and I hope Mummy gets more, but I'm trying to be a realist in terms of both time and timing, development cycle for books in the line, business of Onyx Path in general (they are getting more popular each year) and the general popularity/sales of the line (which I cannot directly comment on, but I have heard Mummy compared to Promethean in that area, excellent game, but not exactly the easiest to get into or sell to most roleplayers).

                        "There is no God in the Badlands." - Sunny
