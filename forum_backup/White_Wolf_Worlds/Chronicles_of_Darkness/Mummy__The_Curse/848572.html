    Index of Mummies

    03-16-2016, 11:03 PM
    Mummy, by its very nature, is a story of history, so sometimes you end up seeing different characters in different stages of their lives. Also by that same nature, its not an easy game to wrap you head around the names all the time. I've found it difficult to keep track to characters across multiple books and I figure others have done the same. To help us out, I'm going to start listing the named Mummies we've encountered in different books with page references so I can find them easily again and so that I can recognize any that show up in Dark Eras. This will very much be a work in progress, so if you want to post something that I've missed or haven't got to, feel free and I will add it to the list.

    *******
    Unaligned
    • The Heretic (former Sesha-Hebsu, former Su-Menent) - Core: Pg 30, 174; Chronicles (all of it); Guildhalls: Pg 116
    • Sharifa, the Forgotten - Rio
    Maa-Kep
    • Aapep Medes, the Moon Snake (Second personality: The Viridian Vizier) - Rio
    • Bes-Mat - Guildhalls: pg 140
    • Beset Khered, the Progenitor of Duality (Worshipped as Oxumare) - Rio
    • Chatuluka, the Drowned Princess - Rio
    • The Diarist (former Sesha-Hebsu, possibly returned to that guild) - Guildhalls: Pg 11
    • Khyan, The Unsurpassed (former guildmaster of Europe) - Guildhalls Pg 100-101
    • Nekhakha, the Flail of Eternity - Rio
    • Osorehe, the Basalt Tower of Forgotten Dreams (guildmaster, worshiped as Ogoun) - Rio
    • Pestem-Aah, A Senhora de Pesar (Lady of Regret, guildmaster of SA) - Guildhalls: Pg 16, 101
    • Sabola (Khent-henu) - DC: pg 24
    • Sefet-Qam (Signature Maa-Kep, former guildmaster of Africa) - Core: pp. 2-5, 12, 33 (illustration); Guildhalls: Pg 14
    • Ta-Em-Sertu (guildmaster of NA) - Guildhalls: pg 15-16
    • Tausret, Who Binds the Gaze of Men (guildmaster of Europe) - Guildhalls: pg 99-100
    Mesen-Nebu
    • Kanika - DC: pg 24
    • Khons-Neb-Ankh, Master of the Paths of Life - Rio
    • Merew-Tjaw, The Anointed One (Signature Mesen-Nebu) - Core: pg 140; Deceived: 2-3, 5; Guildhalls: Pg 104-106
    • Tarikh-Sethos (Khent-henu) - DC: pg 24
    • Tashakti - Guildhalls: pg 140
    • Teshra-Gemet, O Pardal Vermelho (Guildmaster) - Core: pg 256-257, Rio
    Sesha-Hebsu
    • Akhomet - Deceived: pg 157-158
    • Antu-Herap, Prince of Glass (Signature Sesha-Hebsu) - Guildhalls: Pg 115-117
    • Bantanath, the Artificer of Secrets (Guildmaster, second personality: The Scarlet Queen) - Rio
    • Hes-Sedjet, the Empress of Azure Waves (Worshiped as Iemanja) - Rio
    • Sa-Khonsu, the Oubliette of Flowers (Second personality: The Ebon Princess) - Rio
    • Su-Thoth - Deceived: pp. 139-140
    • Tarset, Hand of Antu-Herap - Guildhalls: Pg 117-119
    • Tawret - Guildhalls: Pg 140, 143-144
    • Ur-Qeb (Khent-henu) - DC: pg 11, 24; Sothis: pg 67
    Su-Menent
    • Ankh-Nephris, Hand of Wisdom (Signature Su-Menent) - Core: pp. 15, 48 (illustration), 272-276
    • Husani, Reviver of Dead Things (Guildmaster and South American First Prophet) - Rio
    • Kekewey, the Hunter of Divinity (Worshipped as Oxossi) - Rio
    • Lisimba - Guildhalls: pg 135-136, 140
    • Maahes (burned, may now be Shuankhsen) - Guildhalls: Pg 87
    • Menmaatre, First Prophet of New York - Guildhalls: pg 140
    • Mosret - Guildhalls: pg 135-136
    • Nefernewet - Dreams: pp. 46-48, 52-56
    • Oba-Heshef (Khent-hunu) - DC: pg 24
    • Sacmist, the Prince of Storms (Worshipped as Xango) - Rio
    • Tef-Re (former First Prophet, dissappeared in 391 CE) - Guildhalls: pg 54
    • Tepemkau - Rio
    Tef-Aabhi
    • Amun-Rubi, the Granite Sphinx (Guildmaster) - Rio
    • Anak-Em-Tanen, Inciter of Forbidden Visions (Worshiped as Exu) - Rio
    • Aziza, The Virtue of Stone - DC: pg 42-44
    • Buikhuwati - DC: pg 44-45
    • Hamset, the Hand of Azar (Guildmaster) - Core, Rio
    • Ib-Sheta, the Guillotine of Innocence (Second personality: the Golden King) - Rio
    • Kasmut - Core: pg 255-256
    • Menefes, the Crimson Lion (Worshipped as Erinle) - Rio
    • Seb-Hetchet, The White Jackel (Signature Tef-Aabhi, Khent-henu) - DC: Pg 2-5, 11-21, 23-29, 31-33, 41-49, 51, 53, 55-56, 58, 60-62, 64-65, 71, 81, 86, 89-92, 95-100, ; Sothis: Pg 67
    • Salidji, Life of the City - Guildhalls: Pg 76-77
    • Sekhimib, The Body Canvas - DC: pg 45-46
    • Ubaid, The Right Hand of Glory - DC: pg 46-48
    • Udjat - Guildhalls: pg 140
    Sadikh
    • Nidal Alhindi, Sadikh of Uhm Bennu - Core: pg 191-192
    • Oznur Murad, Sadikh of Sekhimib - DC: pg 47
    The Deceived
    • Awa (Keeper's Temakh, leader of the Rotting Temple) - Deceived: pp. 70-71
    • Aziza (Painter's Temakh) - Deceived: pg 2-7
    • Hab-Utet, The Hidden Begotten - DC: pg 99
    • Kefershaitan, Stained Hands (Keeper's Temakh) - Deceived: pp. 124-125
    • Kephermenes (Dancer's Temakh) - Deceived: pg 2-7
    • The Mother of Claws (Keeper's Temakh, leader of the Scions of the Emerald Ankh; may not actually be an individual mummy) - Deceived: p. 73
    • Najaulla-Sen, The Merciful One (Keeper's Temakh) - Deceived: pg 123-124
    • Ngozi (Poet's Temakh, foremost of the founders of Le Troupe Noir) - Deceived: p.82
    • Obarous-Tet (Keeper's Temakh) - Deceived: pp. 125-126
    • Sakhet (Dancer's Temakh, leader of the Tomb Legion) - Deceived: p. 77
    • The Star Pharaoh (Painter's Temakh, founder of Al-Runihura) - Deceived: pp. 79-80
    Shuankhsen
    • Huahra - Core: pg 202
    • Mekaw - Guildhalls: Pg 105
    • Mesedikereh (formerly Taset) - Sothis pg 30-31
    • Nefersobk, The Crocodile Queen - Core pg 198-200
    • Sacmis (former Su-Menent First Prophet of Tokyo, missing and presumed destroyed) - Guildhalls: pg 52, 54, 140
    • Sefetjaw Saret, the Butcher of Wisdom - Rio
    • The Roller (former Tef-Aabhi) - Deceived: pg 156-157, Guildhalls: Pg 140
    • The Twins - Guildhalls: Pg 140, 144-145
    Fasad
    • Wormflesh, Fasad of Huahra - Core: pg 201-202
    Unknown
    • Rabi Levi - Mortal Remains: pg 101
    • The Crimson Pharaoh: Sothis: Pg 68-69
    • "Santa Muerte" - Mortal Remains: pg 100
    • "Toshi Densetsu" - Mortal Remains: pg 101
    • Uhm Bennu - Core: pg 191
    Other Beings
    • The Beast - DC: 99-101
    • Herald of Forgotten Stars, the Crawling Chaos - Guildhalls: pg 122
    • The Iron Bull - Rio
    • Ochre-Masked King - Lore: pg 12
    Books completed: Core, Guildhalls of the Deathless, Lore of the Deceived
