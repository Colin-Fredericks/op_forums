                                So upon even further thinking I can't help but think to myself it is the other true immortals, especially the Reborn, that I want to make a campaign about. The idea of them being basically normal humans but with special time traveling abilities really interests me.

                                That said, i would add certain things. Like them having Sekhem points that they can use to activate their supernatural powers, in addition to still being able to use Willpower. Additionally, Sekhem (as a power stat) gets added to supernatural power dice pools, increasing their potency.

                                I would also add the ability to learn or participate in a ceremonial ritual that would allow the Reborn to basically move themselves between the other lives, be they past or future or even a life in a sideways timeline. As part of this I would make this, modern, life something special - maybe having it so they were part of something - either by choice, force, or accident - that supercharged their soul.

                                As an additional note, I could totally see taking some of the themes and campaign advice from Mummy and using it in a much less dark and horror nature in a variant Trinity Continuum game.

                                All that said, basically, upon further analysis of Mummy the Curse 2e, I am glad I backed it. While some of the elements I disliked intensely from 1e are still there the changes, additions, and options that are presented make those elements a much lesser force, which I totally approve of. So yeah, Mummy the Curse 2e is pretty awesome.

                                                        So, the writer shed some extra light on the intent of this in Discord, which made this click for me in a way that zeroing in on a blanket ban on specific practices did not. Paraphrasing - attempts to affect the Time, Fate, or Memory of a mummy will always fail; this totally makes sense to me and is super thematic. In light of that, the only real issue I have with the sidebar is the way it addresses the practices, since it creates edge cases that don't follow the intent.

                                                        For example:
                                                        1) As written, an Acanthus can go back in time with Shifting Sands by a scene or so and 'kill' a Mummy. They would remain 'dead' in the present. This should not be possible, because that's messing with the personal Time of the Deathless.
                                                        2) As written, an Acanthus cannot use Warding Gesture to shield a Mummy ally from her own hostile AOE spells, because Shielding is a forbidden practice. This should be possible, because it's not messing with the personal Fate of the Deathless.
