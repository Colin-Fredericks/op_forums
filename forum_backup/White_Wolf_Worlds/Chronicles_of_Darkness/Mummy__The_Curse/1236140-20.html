                        Originally posted by Psicose View Post
                        What would a group of hunters specialized in fighting Arisens have?
                        Depends what you mean by "fighting."

                        Weird sorcery that can suspend the font of vitality giving a mummy their supercharged regenerative powers helps a team plink down the Health of the Arisen in a direct or running battle, as to other ways of bringing them closer to mortality.

                        A way with people and the means to ensure people's needs are being met will serve a crew of cult deprogrammers much better in helping cut down on the likelihood that a mummy is summoned in the first place, as will keeping track of occult objects in one's area.

                        The Arisen are encountered by hunters as part of a big-picture problem, because they've got infrastructure in place that causes them to become active, and those cults and tombs and collections of relics can be trouble on their own even before the walking corpse wakes up.

                                                Originally posted by TyrannicalRabbit
                                                Voice of Conscience and it's Vice counterpart Affinity-I haven't used the system that involves Doors too much in my games, but at face value these Affinities seem rather strong, especially when looking at the rest of the mummy power suite involving having better impression levels etc. What has been your experiences with them?
                                                Worth remembering that most normal characters will only have two or maybe three Doors before adjusting for Anchors, Aspirations, and breaking points, that the ability to spend Willpower to open all Doors on a successful Social Maneuver is in the specific context of appealing to the target's Virtue or Vice, and that assisting a Touchstone in fulfilling their Virtue or indulging their Vice is a source of Reminisce Beats.

                                                Tangent of Scorpion Veins-given the book kind of poorly has the information that specifies that mummies are not susceptible to non supernatural diseases, how do you run that with toxins? Given they're undead nature and lack of functional physiology like the living, I tend to have them also unaffected by non supernatural poisons by default. What do you usually do in your games?
                                                1e didn't have the Arisen immune to poisons (there's a Bane Affinity whose effects include offloading the effects of poison and intoxication onto) and did have them universally immune to disease (and the same paragraph where this is mentioned says that some injuries that logically shouldn't affect them still do).

                                                Given this, 2e's lack of statement that the Arisen are immune to poison on the same page where it specifies their partial immunity to disease, and the fact that Scorpion Veins leads with "The Arisen is immune to poisons," plus the practical concerns of not no-selling the main threat of Scorpion-Limbed Amkhata (and the thematic appropriateness of the servants of the Scorpion Empire not having any extra resistance to the main weapon of its symbolic creature), I'd run mummies as not being immune or resistant to poisons by default; their main mechanical effect is mitigated by Arisen regeneration rates anyway.

                                                Fated Soul- Anyone made any house rules in regards to this and Beast Nightmares?
                                                The +2 to contested resistance applies, the immunity doesn't; that's pretty much exactly how Beast's section on Nightmares says it would work: "Nightmares function on supernatural creatures just as they do on normal human beings. Even creatures that don’t dream or feel fear can tremble before the power of the Dark Mother. Such beings are, however, better able to resist than human beings."

                                                Blessed is the God King- Do or would you allow tier 2 to be used on supernatural beings?
                                                Given that it specifies "mortal," I'd say no.

                                                Dreams of Dead Gods-Do or would you allow tier 3 to be used on supernatural beings?
                                                See previous.

                                                Invoking Tempest's Fury - Is tier 3 in conjunction with other tiers meant to cap at Extreme Environment 3 or 4?
                                                3. This is an Utterance themed around wind and thunderstorms, and combining either of the two preceding effects (to say nothing of combining both) already means anyone the mummy might wish to damage can be pulverized by per-turn damage for the rest of the day/night.

                                                Wrathful Desert Power- Is tier 3 when invoking the power by taking a scene meant to create an Extreme Environment, and is the bonus effect meant to make you intangible in the storm?
                                                Being substantially concealed applies a -3 penalty to ranged attacks against the mummy, the Sandstorm Tilt penalizes Physical rolls like attacks by -3 along with a scaling penalty to rolls to see anything as well as damaging living characters each turn due to lung damage, and the preceding Tier's bonus effect allows you to reflexively teleport. I would say it's not an Extreme Environment at extended length and the bonus effect doesn't make it impossible to hit you so much as the base Tier already makes you very hard to hit.
