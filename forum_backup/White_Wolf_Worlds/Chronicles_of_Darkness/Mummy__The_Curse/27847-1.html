        Originally posted by reseru View Post
        How have the Arisen not returned all vessels to Duat over 6,000 years, you ask? It's because they haven't had 6,000 years. In terms of actual walking activity, they've probably had a few centuries. . . They mostly wake up because of disturbed tombs or their cult summons them, which means their first purpose takes precedence over general relic-hunting. They also hate each other, scheming across millennia to spite one another, or try to indulge in some mortal life occasionally. There's an instinct for Mummies to hunt relics, as we humans have an instinct to eat food, but it's not what they do with all of their time, no more than we sit around and...um...well...you get the idea.
        So, you're proposing that:
        1) They're not awake that much
        2) They don't care that much
        3) They get in each other's way a lot

        It seems they should be awake a fair bit, though: most Mummy's have some vessel to help return them (as I recall), and with a vessel their cult can return them fairly frequently - on the order of months, if they went at the soonest. Even if they do complete their first purpose immediately, they have the better part of a year to stay awake.

        Stated demographics establish at least a "Few thousand" active Mummies at present, yes? Enough to have a critical mass for a worldwide culture of Guilds. Thousands of Mummies, active for one year out of every - what? 5? 10? An average as high as 25 would mean you'd start losing cult continuity as nobody actually knew how to wake their 'protector' anymore. While that surely happens, it seems unlikely that it would be the norm. Plus the general Sothic awakenings.

        Plus, recovery of a Relic generally counts, if not as their first purpose, at least a power bump for having pleased the Judges - since it was established that they really do want their relics back a whole lot, 'like parents would want lost children'. Which seems pretty motivating to me. And even if they schemed to deny the other guy the chance to recover some relic, they'd still want to do it themselves, since it gets them credit with their own boss. While that may end up in lost opportunities sometimes, given the Judges really REALLY want those relics, I doubt it could be too often, or else somebody would get smacked down.

        All of which leads to the conclusion of thousands of Mummy-work-years active and motivated to recover artifacts.

        All of which leads to some mixture of the following:
        1) Quite a lot of relics have been made over time
        2) Quite a lot of relics have been returned over time
        3) Mummies aren't very good at getting / keeping relics.

        Since #3 doesn't seem supported by the rules or story, it would seem some combination of 1 and 2 are the most likely results.

        .... Which brings me back to my starting question: WHY do the Judges want to eat the relics so much? If the relics are chess pieces, why take them off the board? Are they capturing the other guy's pieces, in order to send back a relic to their advantage? Does that mean the sum of relics in the world is a zero sum game, which is why the Judges think of /all/ relics as their property, because even if they're not from Irem, they're powered by legacy sekhem which has been recycled? Or is the world constantly creating new relics on its own, and the Judges want them sent to Duat to feed their own appetites?


        Mummies that, frankly and I apologize for the meta-gameyness here, aren't PCs (or aren't NPCs in a campaign of PCs) probably don't wake up as often or engage in as much activity.
        Sure, but the PCs haven't even been PCs for their whole lives. If they're expected to believe that relic-acquisition is a thing they should do as a Mummy, there should be some internally consistent, believable reason (even if their characters aren't immediately privy to it) why that's a problem which keeps popping up and needs solving.
