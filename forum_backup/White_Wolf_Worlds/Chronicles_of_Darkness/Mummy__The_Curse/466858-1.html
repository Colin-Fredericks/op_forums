    Originally posted by DesolateDolly View Post
    The complete picture I was referring to is not "soul stuff" or a ghost that has been divided into five pieces, or many, but rather the True Name. As a True Name is more than just a word, it is the perfect heavenly expression of everything you are so much that it's utterance gives the utterer absolute power over you.

    The separation of "soul stuff" was in reference to the concept that there are physical remnants of the soul in the Arisen's body that would allow, say, a mage to manipulate the Mummy's soul. The only parts left are the Pillars, the core pillars belong to the Judge that the Arisen has in their Decree, all else are gravy.

    Basically, killing a Mummy until the next Sothic Turn is about as good as it gets. I mean, it's the Rite of Return, anything that so simply disrupts it just because one did some research would neuter the Mummy's place in the World of Darkness. Not even the Arisen understand the Rite, and I would say that understanding the Rite is the only way you could disrupt it, and I would also remind that knowledge of the Rite was lost with Irem. So short of finding the Lost City, talking to the Shaniatu, or the Judges, the best way to "kill" a Mummy is to destroy his tomb, his body and his cult. Feel free to change that in your stories all you care to, but I think putting him down until Sothis ascends again is a fairly effective enough death for any antagonist in a chronicle.

    Only as long as you aren't talking Rank 6+ or Arcanum 6+ entities, then we are entirely agreed.

        You mean like the Shaniatu or the Judges? 'cause while Mage the Awakening might have an old 1e supplement (and indeed Mummy is still 1e) that detailed in the statistical abilities of Rank 6+ entities (entities that everywhere else say pointedly do not require statistics because they are plot devices and effectively insurmountable unless you want them to be), this is Mummy we're talking about so I'll answer from the perspective that this is a "Mummy the Curse" game and not a "Mage the Awakening" game since they are going to have different themes and quote this:

        Originally posted by Nephilpal View Post

        All of this is correct. Expanding a bit on #3, each of the game lines have an intended "specialness" to them in which one line does not co-opt another. It would be as inappropriate for mages to make mummies as it would be for mummies to have an utterance that awakens mortals as mages. Even more importantly, it is a key part of mummy that the Rite is something miraculous from a bygone age of epic sorcery, something that happened once and transformed the world and then has never (and could never) be duplicated without very extensive changes to the setting. Mummies are living relics (literally) of antiquity, rooted in a glorious past that has decayed until its traces barely remain. This is part of their tragedy, especially since the decline of Memory means they have personally decayed and lost their past even right along with the other legacies of Irem. They must struggle to hold onto the pieces of who they were and expend enormous effort to probe the mysteries of where they came from.

        This is not to say that you are having badwrong fun if you want to tell a story of modern mystics making their own mummies, only that canon has strong reasons not to permit that within official material.
        If a mage is not intended to create a Mummy, or to be capable of replicating the Rite of Return, I suspect that disrupting the Rite of Return would be equally suspect regardless of a Mage's Arcanum score. If we're talking about what is official material, it would seem, that you can only kill a Mummy in the ways described.

        If we're talking about what you want to do in your own game? Again, that's whatever you want to do. It seems now we're getting into an argument of whether or not the RAW supports it or the RAI supports it and I'll always favour the RAI in an abstract set of rules.

        (( The thread the quote is taken from can be found here: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...wer-if-you-can ))
