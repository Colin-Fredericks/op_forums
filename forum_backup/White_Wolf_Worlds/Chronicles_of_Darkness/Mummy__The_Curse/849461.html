                      Good to hear. So, in the meantime let's do a little roleplaying. Onyx has contracted YOU to write the main book for Mummy 2.0. What do you want to add to the new edition?                           Mummy has some of my favorite fluff, so I would probably change exactly none of it (only expand things 1e hasn't detailed or explored much) and just clean up mechanics. Maybe tidy up the presentation a bit, though I still think the Player/Storyteller division in the core works very well for the kind of game Mummy is.

                          Decree is a fairly vestigial x-splat all things considered and could be made more distinct - or just removed. The Mummy template should probably get a power boost to bring some of its original strengths in line with 2e. Pillar attribute boosting has always been needlessly complicated and could use being simplified and made more reliable. I'd also rework the structure of Utterances, as to avoid the annoyingly repeated instances of going back and adjusting Pillar distribution during character creation to make sure you could actually use all three tiers of your chosen spells and didn't end up with fillers.

                          I'm not sure how I'd change or adjust the progression of the Descent, but there are a lot of interesting suggestions and additions floating around 2e hacks to consider.

                                      Definitely keep Mummy firmly Iremite/Egyptian. M:tR exists if you're desperate for Chinese mummies and becoming Twice-Arisen doesn't satisfy you.

                                      Expand the Decree into something meaningful beyond just your choice of Judge and defining Pillar. Add in that unique form of Sybaris from Ruins of Empire as a default option, and turn Sybaris into a chain of Conditions. Give rules for building your own Slaughterers and other demons, so that the Arisen have foes other than Amkhat and themselves to fight. Cults and Pyramid play get an entire chapter to themselves. Add in something similar to Monuments or my previously discussed Flashpoints, some sort of Touchstone-equivalent firmly rooted in your personal history. Toss in a list of easy to assemble Egyptian name parts. Players don't like doing that homework and I don't want Nephren-Ka and Imhotep at every table.

                                      I wouldn't change a damn thing, just add on. Mummy is alread a masterpiece in my eyes.
