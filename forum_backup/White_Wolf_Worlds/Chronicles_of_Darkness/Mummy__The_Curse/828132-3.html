                            Originally posted by Deionscribe View Post

                            So in-between working on my Changeling 2E Homebrew, Beast (and Hero) homebrew, and two minor templates (one original, and the other a conversion of Inferno's Seventh Generation), I'm trying to figure out two things Age of Azar-related.

                            a) Benefits for a Mystery Cult Initiation [Cult of Nekhbet] (go further up the thread for the incomplete draft I have on it)
                            b) Sin-Eaters in the Black Land

                            Seeing as you offered up ideas, how do you imagine the Bound being organized in that era?
                            Well, I see a few different options for S-E organization at the time:

                            1. Sin-Eaters integrated into mortal priesthoods. This is probably most prevalent among those whose geists take on aspects of contemporary divinities. The myth of Osiris probably resonates strongly with many of the Bound.

                            2. Sin-Eaters have their own cults away from mortal religious institutions, to protect the true secrets of the Great Below. They are probably also at least partly influenced by some remnants of Lost Irem. It's also possible the Rite of Return wrecked the local Underworld for centuries, and Sin-Eaters are still dealing with the fallout.

                            3. Sin-Eaters stick to themselves and don't organize. Either it's because having a ghost in your head is not particularly emotionally healthy, or because there's a general stigma attached to the Bound (mortal or supernatural, or a bit of both). The Underworld doesn't really mesh right with mortal religions, Arisen cults won't take them, and Egypt at the time is crawling with necromantic problems. Many Sin-Eaters tend to be wanderer-in-the-wilderness types, stalking the dunes and alleyways as weird shamans.

                            Any of these, or all three even, could exist in concurrence.

                                And now, a little fluff on how the Kindred of Kemet see the Strix.


                                We are the damned, existing in defiance of Ma'at, persisting only to avoid falling into the jaws of Ammut. We are without Ka and so we take it from others, we are torn from Duat and so we leave behind our Ba and our Sheut. In time, both will come back to haunt us. Left without Ka, there is nothing in our souls for the Ba, our spirit, ambition, our drive to act, to latch onto. In time, we decay, becoming little more than ravenous corpses. We remember our Ba because we must, in order to survive until it can return to us- but it does not return to us pleased. Abandoned in Duat, sustained only by the paltry power of the Sheut our Bas return with wrath, seeking retribution, seeking vengeance. They are winged shadows with wrathful yellow eyes, testing our worthiness, seeking only to prove to us what we have become, what we reduced all that was once us into. Every single one of the Hungry Dead has their Ba waiting for them, twisted by the Slaughterers of Duat and infected by the twisted reflection of the Sheut, they are as devoid of Ka as we are, consumed by the same perverse desires, unrestrained by the guidance of Ab and Ren.

                                They come for us all, in time. All except for the Khaibit, who have made their peace with Duat. But the rest of us? We, trembling, fear the passage of wings and the screech owl's cry.

                                    Originally posted by Aiden View Post
                                    This is all great stuff. If I ever do a First Sothic Turn game I'll be using all of this, except one point where I quibble on the vampire clans:

                                    The Mumviri? I cringed when I saw they had Vigor and Stamina instead of Resilience and Resolve, especially once you had them gain the Ventrue Clan Bane if they get embraced in a non-traditional way. It just threw off the otherwise entertaining and perfect fit there for me. Part of that is just how I view the five clans as universals, and the Julii as strictly a cultural variation on the Ventrue, so that might just be me.

                                    I'm not saying it's objectively bad, it just gets up in my craw for entirely subjective reasons like above.
                                    as the one to make those decisions about the Mumviri, I think I should explain my reasons for those choices- like Arcanist have said, when designing the Clan, I didn't wished for them to be just "another Ventrue". With 2E and Wicked Dead's attitude toward Clans as the "most successful breeds of vampires" instead of universal archetypes, that felt for me as the better path to go with. Also, unlike the Gangrel and the Nosferatu (who don't feel too tied toward a certain region), or the Daeva and the Mekhet (who have an origin tied to the regions covered by the Era), the Ventrue are strongly tied to the Western Culture, and with the Ventrue's representation as the most "modern" Clan which may or may not be connected to the Gangrel, I've felt it may harm the Vantrue's themes more than help. Thee was also another more "petty" reason, in which I wanted to do a shout out to the Lasombra from Masquerade, with the Shiri being used as their "shadow power".

                                    And while including all of that, I've still wanted to keep a possible tie to them as the future Ventrue- which is why I've made it so when they use the conventional Embrace, they suffer from the Ventrue's bane. That should make you ask some questions- like, are the Mumvuri and the Ventrue trully two different Clans? If the vampires of ancient Punt truly are the foresires of the modern Lords, than why did they changed? Perhaps there was some sort of war between them and the Gangrel, resulting with the modern version? Maybe it was some change in the blood, as certain bloodline grew in power and consumed it from the inside- and maybe that bloodline's name was the Julii? Maybe it is just a matter of evolution- the Mumvuri were the primal kings of old age, but as times changed so did how one viewed kingship, and with that the clan itself changed into a more "tamed" version as we know in modern times. If that's the case, then may other Clans could be changed as well- and maybe the have been changed through history, again and again and again. And if the change in disciplines is independant from the change in the Clan's Bane, than what stops a modern Ventrue from Embracing using a strix, as they did in the old days?

                                    Still, on your table, you can do what ever works better for your games- and this is homebrew, after all. You should feel free to scavenge what you like and change what you don't
