    Rumors: Mummies, Irem, and Lost History

    12-02-2014, 01:53 AM
    http://www.mediafire.com/download/2l...ess+Rumors.pdf

    Based on this file, I'm spearheading a 2.0 Rumors project to create even more awesome plot hooks, story ideas, and paradigm shifts that anyone can use at their table. If you have an awesome idea for a rumor, story, or legend, post it here, and by next February we'll decide who's story passed the bar to be included.

    This thread is focused on mummies, their lost pasts, and what's happened since they last remembered anything; ideas not pertaining to these in some form will automatically not be included.

      "I know that only the Shaniatu were the only ones to speak in the name of the gods. I know that they were the only priests and prophets, but.. I remeber something. Some memory which haunts me. A young woman, carrying a white flower. A blue flag with the same flower, risen by many followers of that woman. A great rebellion between the Empire and the woman, and the woman sings- it is a lullaby, about sleep and freedom. I remember the woman as she was executed, and the statue of her goddess being destroyed and her name was stolen and forbidden to be pronounced. I also have another memory, years after Irem, of the same woman walking, offering me to return to my sleep- forever and ever. Apparently, I refused, or else what am I doing here? But I wonder if she is still around there, singing her song"

          "In the days of Irem, the Nameless Empire was the fortress of humanity against the dog-men, blood drinking corpses, harpies, nightmare gods, and sea-born horrors that preyed on humanity throughout the barbarous world beyond. As the Maa-Kep, it was our duty to keep watch against such shades and freaks that prowled the night, and toward this end we were entrusted by the Shan'iature with one of the Empire's greatest secrets: the Nameless. Slaves scourged of their very ren, kept in the possession of the sorcerer-priests, with whose voice we commanded these vassals of their will to fight the monsters of Ammut who dared to arise within the borders of the Empire, or even Irem itself. With no name of their own, they could not be touched by any of their enemy's powers to command, warp, harm, or even sense them.

          I wonder though what became of them. With no names of their own, they'd make no fit sacrifice for the Rite of Return. And with the Arisen in Henet and the Shan'iatu gone, there would have no one left to command them. Did they wither away without purpose to even feed themselves? Could death even claim that which had no Name to assign and ending to? Do their last commands still stand? What might we look like to them now?"

                                  Have you ever heard of the Flesh Masons, my friend? I'm not surprised, my Guild keeps quiet when it comes to them. They work with people instead of stone, breeding bloodlines, even entire populations, in ways that channel and refine Sekhem in ever greater quantities. But that's not what's important here.

                                  See, I just remembered a conversation I had with one of the Su-Menent, a long, long time ago. He was explaining something to me, something about the demonic heritage of mankind. He claimed his Guild had developed powers that drew on the legacy of unholy unions that lay dormant within their own flesh and bone. They can grow extra limbs, armour themselves in scales, climb walls, run on water, phase through doors, even reach into Neter-Khetet and walk with the creatures that lie there. There's enough demon inside us, inside all humans, to make some pretty scary stuff possible.

                                  It makes you think. What might a Flesh Mason do with that knowledge? What if they thought to breed the mortals so that, with every generation, that demon inside all of us got a little stronger?

                                  What if they already know? What if they've been doing it for centuries?

                                      A papyrus scroll discovered in the vacant tomb of Anok Sabe, Guildmaster of the Sesha-Hebsu, in 2013:

                                      Hearken, Arisen and attend to the voice of the Law.

                                      I, Anok Sabe, of Subtle Judgments, guildmaster and Kenbet of the Sesha-Hebsu in the nome of Dubai, give this decree 1,456 years after the third rising of Sothis:

                                      I declare that the Rose Tower is the abode of iniquity.

                                      It is forbidden to the Arisen, or the servants of the Arisen, to enter the Rose Tower. It is forbidden to the Arisen, or the servants of the Arisen, to pass within 15 cubits and 1 palm of the Rose Tower. None may pass over it, or under it, or approach within 15 cubits and 1 palm of the Rose Tower. It is forbidden to the Arisen, or the servants of the Arisen, to know the inside of the Rose Tower, either by artifice or sorcery.

                                      The recompense for transgressions against this decree are these: The Arisen shall suffer the blow of the lash 42 times. If he possesses canopic jars, one is forfeited. If he does not, one of his vessels is forfeited. If neither of these is possessed, he shall suffer the blow of the lash another 42 times. Then he shall be dispatched to the death cycle. Any mortal servants of the Arisen who share in his lawbreaking, or who otherwise learn of the interior of the Rose Tower, forfeit their lives.

                                      In the name of Unem-Sef, the Eater of Blood, I certify that this decree was given by my own hand 1,456 years after the third rising of Sothis.

                                      Praise the Shan'iatu, Praise the Judges, Praise the Gods. Glory to Irem.
