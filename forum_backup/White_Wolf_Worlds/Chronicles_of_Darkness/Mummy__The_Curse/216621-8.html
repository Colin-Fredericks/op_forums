                        Session 48

                        Sesu Ra had been spending this time divining the location of Yama-Het's tomb. It was near one of the oldest buildings constructed under the area's gentrification area, in a small park containing a stone maze. Inside he found a staircase leading to a locked door. He ripped the lock out of the door and went inside.

                        He was immediately assailed by a sense of dread, and had to force himself through. The curse lying on Yama-Het's resting place very nearly overwhelmed him, but he had just enough willpower to carry over the threshold. Once inside he found himself in a spartan room. In its centre was a tall stone pillar, covered in Iremite hieroglyphs. On the right were rows of locked safes. Sesu Ra activated Dust Beneath Feet Tier 1 in order to peer through the metal, and found them full of books. These books appeared to be centuries old in nature. Closest to the door was a safe containing video tapes, cassettes, and CDs. He figured they must be recordings of Yama-Het's descents.

                        The pillar, meanwhile, turned out to be a description of Yama-Het's long crusade against the Shuankhsen. Nine separate hunts had been successfully concluded, each ending in the destruction of one of the Lifeless and the dissolution of their perverted cultists. Yama-Het's strategy was to transform her cultists into animal-headed warriors, allowing them to do the fighting without running the risk of feeding the Shuankhsen with her Sekhem. There seemed to have been five individual Shuankhsen, some of them had been destroyed multiple times. Sesu Ra noticed a mention of "the Beast Who Is Fire", Iwseck, among the hieroglyphs.

                        He continued through her tomb carefully, making sure not to disturb anything. The next room contained Yama-Het's vessels, each in an alcove positioned at a cardinal point in the room. The southernmost one was empty. He found the necklace he had come seeking there, as well as a quill he had seen Yama-Het use many times before, and a stone seal. Using the Model Lifeweb Affinity, he determined that the latter was a powerful Vestige with the Fetters of Temperance and Wrath, presumably conforming to Yama-Het's own Virtue and Vice. The necklace was called the Shield of Ptah and contained powerful protective magic, while Yama-Het's quill aided those who wielded in determining true names, and used that knowledge to spy upon them.

                        Since Sesu Ra had no interest in facing off against a Sekhem 10 Yama-Het in the middle of her tomb, he withdrew before he accidentally triggered her awakening. He decided to pay a visit to Rekhenaten, and offered him a plan. He could guarantee that the local Preman would help against Yama-Het's cultists, the Sutra Watch, and he could make sure that they were unable to wake their mistress to defend them. Rekhenaten just needed to leverage his cult's formidable resources to wipe out Yama-Het's cult completely. The Master of the Pit, eager to see this stalemate end before his Descent finished, agreed eagerly.

                        It didn't take long for the necessary preparations to be put into place. Rekhenaten used his considerable connections to ensure that the mortal police would be no obstacle. Sesu Ra sent Luca to persuade the Preman in the area that now was there time to take action. The two Tef-Aabhi sent their Sadikh, Tobi having been resurrected recently and in secret by his master, to the two most critical points. With the help of Dust Beneath Feet, Tobi hid in the basement of the Sutra Watch's community centre, their headquarters, to guard the secret tunnels there. Luca, meanwhile, hid within the maze leading to Yama-Het's tomb, using Dust Beneath Feet himself to spy through the walls and intercept anyone who came to awaken their mistress.

                        According to official reports, a wave of criminal violence and rioting took place over the course of the day. The community centre was burned to the ground, and found to have failed to meet the fire safety code. Tragically, many of those inside, attending a meeting for a local community watch group, died in the flame. At the same time, several fatal muggings and assaults occurred, scattered over the course of the day. By the time night fell, the Sutra Watch was no more.

                        Rekhenaten moved to consolidate his hold in the area. Sesu Ra had no problem with this. He sent Luca to retrieve a female body for him, and travelled out to the Thousand Islands with it. Luca had conspicuously little difficulty with the task assigned for him, and met his master there soon with the body of a beautiful local woman. Using the ritual he had learned from his memories, and sacrificing the Black Scorpion, Sesu Ra performed the Call to summon Iwseck back from Duat. He was nervous, wondering if she would return without memory and seek to devour him in her hunger, but that fear disappeared when he saw a brilliant smile spread across the dead woman's face.

                        The two embraced, and Sesu Ra explained that he had delivered Yama-Het to her. He told Iwseck where to find the tomb of the Sesha-Hebsu Guildmaster. He bade her go there and reclaim the Shield of Ptah for herself, kill Yama-Het, and consume her Canopic Jars. Leave her khat, however, so that Iwseck could devour her again and again for her Sekhem and pillars, and leave the other vessels for him. Iwseck had no problems with this, and after a quick kiss she was off to take her revenge on the Arisen who had hunted her down centuries before. Sesu Ra dropped to Sekhem 6 (effectively 7) due to his interaction with the Shuankhsen.

                        Luca was sent to watch how events panned out. He saw Iwseck enter Yama-Het's tomb, and then leave again a few minutes later. She looked bruised and injured, but clearly proud of herself. She carried a body presumabling containing what was left of Yama-Het's khat.

                        Once the coast was clear, Sesu Ra entered the tomb. He found that the Shield of Ptah had been taken, Yama-Het's canopic jars and khat were gone, and that the safes full of her records had been melted to slag and ash. He found and took Yama-Het's quill, which he discovered was also a Talisman containing the first two tiers of a Sesha-Hebsu Guild Utterance, Inscriptions Upon Flesh. He also retrieved the Seal, which almost brought him to the total number of vestiges he needed to complete his Geomantic Network. He took the Seal to Rekhenaten and returned the Talisman to his tomb.

                        Sekhet Bastset remained near her tomb. She summoned her cultists and ordered them to set a watch on the surrounding slum. They were to prevent her meret, or any other Arisen, from entering the area without her express permission. Before they left to carry out their duties, she had them bring wood and oil, and burned Akenakhi's body before them.

                        Aquila, meanwhile, was still haunting the building Sesu Ra had renovated for use by the Preman. Every time a group entered she would drop down on them, Harvest the Divine Flesh Tier 3 active, and slaughter them to the man. Those who tried to fight her were unable to harm her through the armour and their own fear. However, when one particularly large group filtered in, she overheard them talking about the destruction of the Sutra Watch's community centre. She wasn't able to get to all of the men as they fled, but she turned one man's legs to gold and interrogated him. He confessed that they had been given a list of names and addresses, and someone with a whole lot more influence than they had called off the police. The Sutra Watch was dead.

                        Bast returned to Lia to find out more about the Radiance. Without Akenakhi to hold her back, this time she chose to go straight to the interrogation. She struck Lia across the face, but the woman seemed to melt away, fading out of reality. Even with the help of Ancestry of Forgotten Stars, Bast found that she couldn't touch or harm the woman. Frustrated, she went to leave and found the Prophet of Java waiting for her.

                        "The Radiance bestows upon its followers blasphemous rites with which to sunder Fate's hold," the Prophet said gravely. When Bast asked what he was doing there, he told her that he had come to prepare her. She was one of the few willing to lead the fight against the light. There was a day coming where Fate's hold on the world would be weak, and the Radiance's servants would seek to tear reality asunder. The Coils of Apophis would be at their most arrogant on that day. She would have her chance to destroy it then.

                        After this encounter, Bast decided that she needed to inform her meret of what had happened. Sesu Ra could not be reached, but she called Aquila and explained over the phone what had happened. Aquila pretended to be sympathetic. She suggested summoning Hetenatra to lay a judgement down on Akenakhi's actions, but privately she wanted the Lady of the Broken Stele awake to help her locate Yama-Het's tomb and return the Sesha-Hebsu Guildmaster to life so that they could deal with the attack on the Sutra Watch.

                        On the way, Bast explained to Aquila what they had discovered about the Radiance, and the impending doom it represented for the world. Her mention of the Prophet of Java, an agent of Fate, greatly interested Aquila. She recalled what she had been told, long ago, by an old man. Fate would become mortal, and she would have a chance to break the never-ending cycle of failed revolution she had been condemned to.

                        The two Arisen approached Imeni, one of Hetenatra's old cultists, and explained that they required Hetenatra to sit in judgement. She had set provisions in place for this occurrence, and he went immediately to perform the Call.

                        Hetenatra emerged a short while later, her sahu withered from the strength of her Sekhem. Bast put forward her case against Akenakhi. By her count, he had broken two of the laws of the Diasporic Code, and may be guilty of a third. By striking at her tomb he had broken the Essence Law, and doing so without first seeking Sesha-Hebsu arbitration meant that he had broken the Spirit Law. He had also made her dangerously fragile in the process, close to becoming a Shuankhsen, so she asked Hetenatra consider him guilty of breaking the Shadow Law as well. Hetenatra gave these points due consideration, and agreed. By her judgement, Bast had the right, nay, the duty, to destroy Akenakhi's tomb in retaliation. In addition, every relic he possessed was now hers by right, and his right hand, the hand with which he had lifted a blade against her, was forfeit for the rest of his descent.

                        Bast was ready to leave at this point, but Aquila brought up the attack on Yama-Het as needing Hetenatra's attention. Personally, Aquila believed Rekhenaten was responsible. His cult was by far the most powerful, and possibly the only one in the city with enough influence capable of ensuring the police did not get involved in such blatant assassinations. He was clearly taking advantage of Yama-Het's return to henet by breaking the stalemate. Hetenatra pointed out that there wasn't much to go on in that respect, and that in any case by acting through the Preman he had not clearly broken any laws of the Diasporic Code. Nevertheless, the reckless endangerment of her Guildmaster's ability to act in Jakarta was a problem. Without anyone to perform the Call, Yama-Het would not return until her tomb was disturbed or the next Sothic Turn occurred in some 1500 years. Aquila suggested she kepher Yama-Het's relic, the quill she was often seen carrying. Hetenatra had seen the relic before and, with her Sekhem as high as it was, bent her will towards locating it.

                        The scarab's path was clear to her. She rolled an exceptional success. When she looked back at Bast and Aquila, her voice was subdued. "This is disturbing," she said. "The Unbending Spine has the quill."

                        Bast's expression was first shocked, then devastated as the implications sunk in. Sesu Ra must have done to Yama-Het what Akenakhi had tried to do to her. Aquila felt something akin to a sense of triumph. She had always resented the Tef-Aabhi's Guildmaster's assumption of authority whenever the two were at odds, and now she had Bast on her side. She told Bast that they had to work together, they could only trust one another now. Bast asked that they wait before assaulting Sesu Ra’s tomb, that they demand an explanation from him first, and then left to think on the matter.

                        Aquila decided that she needed to prepare for the possibility of a fight against Sesu Ra. She used Silenced Utterance Vessel to store several of her combat Utterances, but she didn’t feel it was enough. She had considered learning the Kiss of Apep for a while, but it seemed that of all the Arisen in the city, only Mot-Tan knew the Utterance to teach her. She decided to take advantage of all the confusion around Akenakhi’s attack on Bast to send Quil into the area, intending to locate one of Mot-Tan’s former cultists and extract knowledge of his tomb and how to summon him. Instead, Quil returned reporting his every effort had been blocked or intercepted by Bast’s suddenly hyper-paranoid cultists.

                        The only option appeared to be to fight fire with fire. Aquila called on those members of Asa Corp loyal to her personally, and dispatched them to do the task Quil had failed to perform. Unfortunately, with Bast’s tomb nearby, her cultists proved too much of an obstacle for Asa Corp. It was worse than simple failure, however. A confrontation between the two groups spiralled into disaster, into violence, and the shocks sent reverberations back through the whole of Asa Corp. Its influence shrunk, dropping to Reach 2.

                        Aquila knew how Bast’s mind worked. She would take this event in the worst possible way. She rushed straight to Bast’s tomb, only to be stopped at the edge of the slum by Bast’s cultists. She was forced to wait there while a runner was sent to ask Bast what they should do with her. When he returned, close to an hour later, he instructed that Aquila be brought under escort to Bast’s tomb.

                        Bast was waiting outside, sitting cross-legged among the bodies of the men and women Asa Corp had sent to the area. Any hope that Bast might not have recognised them died at the look on her face. Aquila seated herself opposite and tried to explain her actions. She needed Mot-Tan’s help, his knowledge, if she had any hope of overcoming Sesu Ra. Bast replied that the Maa-Kep would be a distraction they could ill-afford. At that, Aquila decided to take a risk.

                        “What if he wasn’t? What if I could make sure his descent was over quickly, and that the power that would have fuelled him could come to us instead?”

                        Bast’s eyes narrowed as she considered what Aquila was suggesting. Aquila suggested that she might be able to locate an Amkhat, and use it to drain Mot-Tan to nothing once he had taught her what she needed to know. That Sekhem could be made to make the both of them stronger, which they might well need if the situation with Sesu Ra came to violence.

                        Bast was horrified. Taming an Amkhat was the worst thing she had ever done, a blasphemy that still made her sick to contemplate. She had only done it because no other way to stop the creature could be found. What Aquila was suggesting was beyond the pale. She demanded that Aquila leave, immediately. The Mesen-Nebu Guildmaster did so, cursing the other Arisen for her stubbornness.

                        Every member of her meret had betrayed her. Bast felt cold and very, very alone.

                        She set her mind to the task she had been working on before all these events occurred. Her kepher had told her of a vessel on the other side of the city, in the slum where Aquila occasionally did medical work. She summoned Kudama Jav and went there on her own, seeking out the man Idrih she had witnessed in her vision.

                        Her search led her to Block 94, a tenement building in the midst of the slum. It was well known among the locals as being a local Preman hideout, and Idrih appeared to live on the third floor. She located his room and knocked. He answered the door, a man who looked more like a cube of muscle wearing a singlet and cargo shorts. He stared at her for a long moment, and Bast demanded that he let her inside. She knew he had knowledge of an item of power, one not meant to be in the hands of mortals. At the word “mortals” Idrih gave a savage kind of smile, and welcomed her inside.

                        There was a large slab of meat on his kitchen bend, beside an enormous meat cleaver. Bast had clearly interrupted him in the middle of something. Idrih said, in tones a lot more erudite than she would have expected from a man of his appearance, that he had heard of an item like the one she sought. However, he wanted something in return before he shared what he knew. A taste, just a morsel, of her divine flesh.

                        Bast narrowed her eyes. She asked if Idrih knew of a group called the Faithful. He gave her an innocent smile and said that he had belonged to them once, but had left after a dispute involving his consumption of the hierarch. He had learned of the gods and spirits that walked among men from them, however, and he had partaken of the flesh of many beings. Never another Arisen, but he had consumed a chimera and many of those claimed by the souls of dead things who plagued this area.

                        After some consideration, Bast sliced off a sliver of flesh from the ball of her thumb. She offered to him, saying she hoped for his help in the future. Idrih was amenable enough and quickly swallowed what she offered. He then explained that the Preman boss in this area, a man named Rahma, was known to wear a yellow pendant around his neck with strange properties. Rahma lived in an apartment at the top of this building.

                        The three of them went there immediately. When they knocked on Rahma’s door, they had a brief encounter with a guard. Bast’s attempts to intimidate the man were unsuccessful, until a woman called him away to let her deal with the matter. The woman was beautiful, and introduced herself as Amia. She extracted Bast’s name from her, but the Arisen steadfastly refused to discuss her business with anyone except Rahma. Finally, apparently from exasperation, Amia invited her inside and took her to see the Preman boss.

                        They found Rahma passed out unconscious in a chair in a small living room. Track marks ran all the way up his arm. Bast saw the vessel, a vestige, gleaming on his chest, and realised that Amia was the real authority here. Amia said that the pendant had been very useful for her, making men extremely amenable to her preferred form of suggestions, and asked Bast what she could offer in return for what clearly interested her. Bast said that she had the power to make Amia the unquestioned leader here, without the need to rule through subjugated men. A master in her own right. Amia handed over the vestige with a smile.

                        Bast’s cult soon spread its influence into the area, rising to Grasp 3 in the process.

                        Aquila had left Bast’s presence feeling furious. She was approached on the edge of the slum by an old woman, sporting yellowing bruises on her face. The woman told her to stay close to her meret. Fate’s time was ending, and the chance would come soon. There would be a day when the edges of reality would fray and the light’s glory would spill into that world. On that day, they, the missionaries of the Radiance, would help Aquila claim the Prophet’s head.

                            The Shield of Ptah (2 dot Effigy)
                            Size 1, Durability 3, Structure 4

                            The power of this necklace lies entirely within the small bronze representation of a shield.                             Power: Once a scene, the bearer may invoke Ptah’s protection as a reflexive action. The bearer rolls their Stamina + Composure. They are protected for a number of turns equal to their successes. This protection renders them completely immune to non-magical sources of damage for that many turns. Only attacks made with relics or Utterances may still harm the bearer.

                            Curse: The Shield turns the damage aside, to the detriment of those around the bearer. For every attack that should have hurt the bearer, an ally or friend in the same area suffers the damage instead at the moment the damage is done.
                            The Tef-Aabhi may pay a pillar point per attack to absorb these energies with the strength of their soul. By paying the pillar point they ensure that no harm comes to those around them.


                            And:

                            The Dark Truths – 4 dot Text

                            Powers: This quill has existed for centuries, passed down among occult sorcerers with an interest in Name Magic. Its creators have long since passed into myth. The bearer may learn many great secrets about the nature of the world with the use of this cursed quill, but the scales must always be balanced and they are doomed to lose as much knowledge as they gain. The bearer may ignore 3 different penalties when determining the target’s true name. If the target is a Mummy, ignore the -5 penalty for that instead.
                            If you know someone’s true name, you can spend a point of Willpower to allow the quill to write on its own, held by your hand. It will describe what the target is doing.

                            Curse: Whenever the text is used, the bearer loses their highest Mental Skill dot until the end of the next Chapter. Sesha-Hebsu are immune to this Curse.

                                            Session 49

                                            Sesu Ra now had everything he needed to complete his Geometric Network. The Vestige stolen from Yama-Het's tomb was the last piece he required, save for the head of the statue in Iwseck's possession. She called him shortly after their joint raid had been completed. She was so, so very pleased he had kept his side of the bargain, growing his Memory until he could recall the time they had spent together. Now, of course, she was going to follow through with her own side. She gave him an address, quite close to a local gem market, and asked him to meet her there.

A small collection of huts and tents marked the site of the army's encampment, just a short march further up the road from the site of the battle. As Bast approached, she spotted a figure standing atop one of the taller sand dunes. It looked like a large canine, twin-scorpion tails rising above its head. A moment later, however, and the figure shimmered like a mirage and turned into a tall, North African man with reddish hair. She stiffened at the sight, and the words of her true purpose ran through her mind.

                                                    Fear the red-headed man. Exalt the Square-Eared Beast.

                                                    The Arisen made a beeline straight for the red-headed man. As they grew nearer he looked out over the group and spoke in a soft, lilting voice, speaking Iremite with a strange accent. "The battle is won. The Devourer's Hounds are at bay. Our lesson resumes."

                                                    Bast felt a sudden pressure from her Judge, and knew with a sudden certainty that her purpose was not to learn this lesson. She withdrew instead to a tent set aside for her among the soldiers, asking that the Sha join her there when the lesson was done.

                                                    Later that night, the Sha entered the tent. It was in its canine form, watching her with eyes of lapis lazuli that left her feeling bare and naked. She asked its purpose, and it said that it had come out of the desert to teach the secret arts to Irem's diaspora. The Utterances, the words by which their cultists might raise them from henet, and other such secrets. Bast listened and felt regret, for she could remember the words of a strange version of one of Irem's oldest myths. It was not a version of the myth she could ever remember hearing before, but it seemed right for what she had to do. The myth spoke of Azar's exaltation by the mortal Aset, in which he was carved into 42 pieces and devoured over the course of 42 days and nights. She knew what she had to do.

                                                    Bast lunged at the Sha before the creature could react, seizing it and silencing it before it could react. She plunged her knife into its chest and the blade lodged firm, the light died from the Sha's eyes. And then, slowly and carefully, Bast carved it into 42 pieces and devoured them.

                                                    When she looked up, the Prophet was standing before her. His face was carefully neutral, no hint of his feelings apparent as he held out one hand to her. She reached up and grabbed it, and a moment later she was standing alone and in her tomb.

                                                    Across the city, Sesu Ra had stumbled back into the world to find that time had resumed its normal course. He was badly injured, his flesh burned and torn and his skull showing through in places. That was almost as nothing next to the state of his mind. The presence of the Shan'iatu in Jakarta left him with only a single recourse.

                                                    He'd returned to his tomb and retrieved his personal affects there when he received a call from Rom. The man sounded as if he was in pain. Asa Corp had been attacked. Rom wasn't sure who had done it or what had happened. One moment everything had been fine, and the next there was blood on the walls and dead bodies all around. He'd found himself lying against a wall, his ribs broken and blood matting his face. Sesu Ra ordered him to gather any surviving cultists and come to his tomb.

                                                    There was another incoming call before he hung up. Rekhenaten. The Master of the Pit informed in a quiet, even voice that his cultists had been slaughtered. A creature like a strange, fleshy snake covered in blind eyes had rampaged through his home, slaughtering his people. Sesu Ra realised that this had been a city-wide attack on Arisen cults. He ordered Rekhenaten to contact as many of the others as he could, and then placed his head in his hands. A moment later, he called Iwseck.

                                                    The phone call was short. They needed to meet. He had preparations to make. He gathered Yama-Het's talisman and his remaining vestige and brought them with him. When Iwseck joined him he ignored her shocked gasp at the state of him, cupped her head in his hands, and quickly told her everything she needed to know to raise him from henet. He handed her the vessels he was carrying to safeguard for him. Then he told her the location of his tomb, handed her Luca's key to work the lift, and left. He dropped to Sekhem 4, punished by his Judge for his continued failure to exact destruction upon the Lifeless.

                                                    Finally, he went to Bast's tomb. Her cultists allowed him to pass through, Luca by his side. He told her what had happened and asked her to come with him to his tomb. Rom and the surviving members of Asa Corp would need to be assimilated with her cultists, one of the only functioning cults left in the city. Bast agreed, and didn't bat an eyelid when he said that Luca wouldn't be coming with them.

                                                    The two Deathless went back to the city central. It was late at night when they pulled up near Sesu Ra's tomb. Besides Rom, there were fewer than a dozen cultists left alive. Ligo, Ami, and two of Pama Lehn's children were among the survivors. Bast began to make preparations for them, but Sesu Ra seemed distracted.

                                                    Back at Bast's tomb, Luca was using Dust Beneath Feet and his prodigious skill at silent movement to evade notice as he crept towards her tomb. He gained entrance without anyone noticing, and quietly collected the World's Wound and the Heart of Sek-Bet from their respective alcoves, awaiting dispatch to Her-Uru. He fled the same way he had come.

                                                    The business with their cultists resolved, Sesu Ra told Bast that he would be going into seclusion. Once she was gone, he went straight to the national monument, the heart of his geometric network. Luca met him there. They descended into the darkness beneath the monument, and Sesu Ra removed Thothekmet from his place in the heart of power. The other Mummy's corpse thudded to the ground and lay there, an inert khat. Sesu Ra then raised the World's Wound in its place. The bane relic's influence flooded the chamber, empowering Shuankhsen throughout Jakarta. The golden flow of Sekhem ended, replaced by viscous gore dripping from cracks and wounds in the ceiling. Sesu Ra left, his work complete.

                                                    Once they had left the chamber, he turned to Luca and asked for the Heart of Sek-Bet. Once Sesu Ra had it in his hands, he released Luca from his service. The Sadikh dropped to the ground, his long-prevented death overwhelming him. As he died, however, Sesu Ra took the Heart and cracked open Luca's chest. The heart was then nestled inside. Luca gasped and his eyes opened. He was still undead, still immortal, but now he was free of Sesu Ra's service.

                                                    "Please sir, don't do this," Luca said. They both knew they weren't talking about Luca's position as Sadikh. He knew what Sesu Ra's plan was.

                                                    Sesu Ra ignored the request. Bast would be coming for her relics soon, and it would be in Luca's best interests if he was out of the country by then. Sesu Ra ordered Luca to leave him, to return to America or go wherever he wanted. Luca seemed on the verge of refusing, but Sesu Ra's final order seemed to have some remaining compulsion for him. And so the Unbending Spine watched the only man he had trusted in all his time in Jakarta walk away, into the dark and silent streets.

                                                    That done, Sesu Ra returned to his tomb. He burned his remaining Sekhem, returning to the repose of henet for the last time.

                                                    Across the city, Bast had noticed her missing relics. She made an immediate kepher roll and located Luca, heading towards the airport. She felt a wave of nausea. Had everyone betrayed her?

                                                    Luca and Bast met in the streets near the airport. They saw one another at a distance and stopped, staring at one another. And then Luca continued on, walking towards her. He stopped when she ordered him to, and she asked him what his master had done.

                                                    "Just what he had to," was all Luca would say. "He set me free."

                                                    Bast stared at him. She knew the significance now of the Heart of Sek-Bet, which she could clearly sense inside his chest. The former Sadikh was evidently devastated by what had happened. She ordered him to leave, her voice cold. He would live on her time from now on. One day, she told him, she would come back for what he had stolen from her.

                                                    Sesu Ra felt his soul being called from his Judge's grip. He rose towards the living world, as he had so many times before. But something was wrong. There was a discordant note to the ritual. He felt his soul tremble, and then fall. He plunged into the darkest depths of Duat, into Ammut's own jaws. For a single moment he experienced darkness and pain, a moment that dragged on for an eternity. His five-fold soul was shattered and broken, torn apart by the Devourer's teeth. His mind buckled under the flood of pain, and he clung to the only thing he had left. Iwseck, and his need to keep her safe, to prevent the Shan'iatu from ever hurting her again.

                                                    His eyes opened. He lifted his hands. Everything felt wrong, like a broken shadow of his previous descents. His body roared with power, the World's Wound's influence empowering him beyond what he had expected, but it was not enough to fill the gaping holes in the pillars of his soul. But there was a smell, a scent that he recognised. Iwseck.

                                                    Sesu Ra stepped from his sarcophagus as a newborn Shuankhsen, and into the arms of his lover.
