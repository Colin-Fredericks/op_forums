    Originally posted by LostLight View Post
    I don't think there is any problem with Duat being classified as a "Lower Depth" by mages. The "Lower Depths" is simply a term to define any realm which is "metaphysically distant" from the Supernal Realms, and as such it is harder for mages to effect it. Dave once said that the reason mages give such shiny and big names to things it is in order to claim that they understand them- even if they don't. In short, the Lower Depths is simply a term for realms that mages don't know much about them, and don't really have power upon them- and Duat seems to answer that definition.
    This guy put what I was trying to say a little better. In-universe, at the very least, Mages aren't even sure there is a lower, emptier, more distant, hungrier realm. They just see malicious, hungry creatures and say, "Wow, I wonder where all these hungry things come from. Maybe there's a Hungry Realm? Maybe we'll be pretentious and call it, 'Lower Depths!'" When they see Mummies they aren't saying, "Ah, yes. We know they're from the Lower Depths because we're Mages and know everything." They're saying, "What the hell is this thing talking about?! It's giving me the heeby jeebies, just like things from the Hungry Realm do."

    But correlation-oriented conclusions are shaky at best
