                                    Huh. People had a problem with Target Numbers changing? That surprises me. It wasn't like World of Darkness, where it was a core part of the mechanics. It was an "only if you had a magic ability" sort of deal. The issue with the World of Darkness variant was that it meant there were two different metrics of task difficulty on basic rolls, which was redundant.

                                    And Tier 1 of Blessed is the God King was a favourite at my table. As a way of showing Fate in action, looking at a dice roll and being able to say, "Well, no successes, but I trigger my ability to retroactively succeed on 6s and now I have four" always felt fantastic to my players. Always accompanied by some narrative description of Fate swinging into action.

                                    But whatever. It's a mechanic. I always felt Mummy had the best mechanics of any Chronicles of Darkness game I ever played, better than the hot mess that was Demon and a lot less finicky than Changeling for example. I hope that it stays that way going into second edition. But this specific mechanic isn't a hill I'm going to die on. It always felt like it did its job of representing Fate well, but so long as they can find a new way of representing that I have no particular problem.

                                                Necro-ing this since it asked what I wished to see in the 2ed. Which is.

                                                1. Refocus the guilds. It's not that I have a problem with them, just with how it is potrayed. In 1e it felt that the guilds(mostly Maa-kep especially in Guildhalls) were still a thing in modern day but that doesn't make sense given how mummies work. Like mummies having a status for the guild and that they could switch if they want. I don't know for splat that is so spread out and rare that's just feel like it's to much structure. I would like to see Guilds more as forgotten relics of an Arisen's past that he uses to interact and experience the world around them, which fits with mummies theme of reclaiming Memory and defining who you are from that. Originally Guilds would have been factions like Covenants and Orders during Irem, but now that the City of Pillars are gone they should be more like Agendas that is philosophies Arisen hold on to as a pillar of identity and support for those who fight to keep what memories they have. Whether it will be like that I don't know. We will have to see.

                                                2. Really interested to see how Memory would work with Morality out of the picture. I'm really excited on that front.

                                                3. I want to see the Restless Stars as a playable option like how Seers were in Mage, that be really fun. As much as I would love that I have to admit that it is unlikely given that for them to that they all so have to included the Temahk's also. So I wonder if that would be to complicated from the get go.

                                                4. More focus on the Apotheosis. Also more variations on options. Because what the Apotheosis is you gaining memories, defining who you are and coming up with your own answer yeah? If so that means that not necessarily mean an Arisen would just choose to rebel against the Judges and Irem because of it, because those are important charastics for an Arisen and for one like the Maa-Kep whose whole point of existence is loyalty and reverence for the Judges, abandoning them is like saying everything he's ever done is meaningless. What I want Apotheosis to do is provide a choice, a choice in allowing the Arisen to do anything that he wishes to do, freedom but not freedom in going against Dust but freedom to decide what they want, no matter what it is. I just want them to focus on that part.

                                                5. Also give more insight on Decrees. That be fun.

                                                Well those are my thoughts on it anyhow.
