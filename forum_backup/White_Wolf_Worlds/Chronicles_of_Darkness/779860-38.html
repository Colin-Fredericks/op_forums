        Alrighties then, the Survivors and Breathless have been merged into one Splat! :3

        As well, I've been also thinking. Almost all of the Siren Currents are based on dealing with the Deluge in a specific way...save for two Currents. Cocytus and Phlegethon.         As for Phlegethon, I imagine that upon seeing the Deluge happen, they decide it's too much work or it's insurmountable. In short, they decide that given the apocalypse is happening that it's better to spend the rest of your days happy and indulging in some good old "End of the world debauchery" than trying to fix what isn't fixable. It also gives them abit more sympathetic points than their current "For the Evulz" take.

                Minor Templates
                Making Survivors and Breathless the same is really brilliant. It shows that humanity is going to change from the Cataclysms that enact the Deluge, let alone the exposure to the Song that has not made them Sirens. It easily explains the difference of post-Deluge beings, as they won't have any benefits normally seen amongst Sirens that Therons will have but aren't too vulnerable to a Siren's verses either. It makes any modern day or far future flung Breathless/Survivors companions the same for ease of game mechanics.

                Choruses
                So seeing that Choruses are the attempted prestige class or z-splat option for Sirens, the first question to ask is what is their THEME? What is it that binds them together and how does that provide them benefits not common amongst everyday Sirens?

                Each Chronicles system that has a z-splat option does so to provide new opportunities for the character, as any prestige option should. To generalize some examples: Changeling's Entitlements bring the Lost into new social groups, titles, new stories and roles and the power begotten from the Wyrd that fits the Entitlement. In Princess the Embassies give the Hopeful expertise in different avenues beyond the battle of Light vs the Darkness, a new motif to their Regalia and exclusive privileges or powers, and a focus towards different facets of the word both mundane and supernatural. And for Genius (ideally with 2E readdressing the structure) a Fellowship is a niche application, project or field of Mad Science. It is tiered towards one particular Wonder or theorem that is nigh-perfected and avoids the pitfalls lesser Wonders of the same type would have.

                So from how a Chorus should be outlined, they usually have a few prerequisites. Usually Supernatural Tolerance at a certain level, certain Attribute or Skill minimums and some require belonging to a certain faction. Although I would suggest only a minority of Chorus have a faction or Current based prerequisite.

                If the Water represents where the Siren comes from and what she embodies, and the Current is both her outlook, methodology and like mindedness with other Sirens, what niche does the Chorus expand or fulfill?

                From what is initially presented, teamwork looks to be a central point of Choruses. You cannot form a Chorus from an individual. Perhaps besides benefits for herself the Siren can provide a small boon to her fellows (what is a group of Sirens called again, I've forgotten), and the boon is better when performed with more members of the same Chorus.

                As for the unifying themes of each Chorus several have been presented. One way is to allow the Siren more formats to enact the Song, the music and theatrical connotations. Another is a Chorus formed to deal with specific kinds of disasters, conflicts or other issues. The Chorus could even be the background of a certain kind of story, giving the Siren an 'in' with different facets of life while remaining inconspicuous. A Chorus is also a soft way of inducting Crossover themes (like the way Princess does it) to represent the Siren approach to material not found in the typical Siren game.

                    Apologies for the double post, I have been working on drafting some Siren NPC characters and wanted to share the first of these characters. I plan to use her to introduce Siren to my mega crossover game that is Princess centered, so the Crossover material is set aside in it's own tab. Nonetheless I wanted to provide basis for the character without Crossover in mind, as she would serve as a model for the type of Siren I would want to play as my first character in a Siren: the Drowning game.

                    Character Draft: Ixcuina
                    Concept: snarky ex-Echidnal filth eater

                    Water: Discordia (Gardenia)
                    Current: Buoyancy

                    Favored Opuses:Beauty, Sound, Water, Taint, Blood, Chaos
                    Favored Conclusion:Travel

                    Attributes:
                    Intelligence                     2, Wits 4, Resolve 3
                    Strength 2, Dexterity 5, Stamina 3
                    Presence 2, Manipulation 2, Composure 2
                    Mental Skills: Computer 1, Crafts 1, Medicine 3, Occult 4, Science 2
                    Physical Skills: Athletics 1, Brawl 1, Drive 2, Larceny 3, Stealth 2, Survival 4
                    Social Skills: Animal Ken 3, Empathy 4, Intimidation 2, Socialize 3, Streetwise 4, Subterfuge 1

                    Specialties:
                    Crafts: Destruction
                    Survival: Hazardous Areas
                    Streetwise: Carnal Pleasures

                    Flow: Disruptive
                    Beaching: Dirty

                    Size: 4
                    Perception: 6
                    Willpower: 5

                    Akashic Record: 4
                    Pneuma: 13/4
                    Refraction: -4 (Song +1)

                    Introductions: Location, Sentient, Self, Insensate
                    Opuses: Beauty, Sound, Water, Blood, Taint, Chaos, Earth, Growth, Darkness
                    Conclusions: Travel 4, Invoke 3, Disturb 5, Calm 2

                    Trace: Bonus for the Time aspect

                    Diluvian Form: max 7 Alterations
                    Common: Boneless Imitation
                    Gardenia: Fetid Touch
                    Tropicana: Poison Immunity
                    Malformations: Poisonous Blood, Slimy Physique, Acidic Excretion

                    Knowledges: 4 total from Double Dipping merit
                    Gardenia: History Repeats Itself, You Get As Much As You Give
                    Discordia: Water Changes Land Does Not, Conflict Is Life

                    Mental Merits: Tolerance for Biology, Language (English), Multilingual (Nahuatl, Korean); Spanish is her primary language
                    Physical Merits: Small Framed, Double Jointed, Demolisher (1 dot)
                    Social Merits: Fixer, Fast Talking (Style; 3 dots), Striking Looks (Unseemly, 1 dot)
                    Siren Merits: Double Dipping, Bel Canto

                    Appearance: Ixcuina has dark skin befitting south Mexican Aztec descended features. Short and quite petite, her body is quite flexible making her a natural contortionist. She has a scar from a cleft lip. She's been letting her hair out but doesn't style it much, having thick dreads as a mass of snarling strands.

                    Given her Malformations there's a sickly bruised choler to her skin and her skin's slime is glossy, like tanning lotion, so she has to avoid whites and light colors.

                    Her Diluvian form is as blue as window cleaner, with rubbery skin and wet clinging blue hair smoothed down the back of her neck. Her tail is long and sinuous, something like an eel’s, with the skeletal frame of cartilage branching out with vestigial fins stretched between. Her green blood is more prevalent in areas like her hands and mouth, her eyes a green-yellow.

                    Most noticeable in her Diluvian form is the smudge of questionable whatsit around her mouth and nose, a deep brown covering her cleft lip that forms a pattern of strands along the rest of her face and neck.

                    Personality: Ixcuina is a women well accustomed to the worst the world has to offer, one who remains defiant in response to these troubles. While appearing to have a languid, resigned and almost slothful personality her signature brand of black comedy is the tip of a feisty avenger. She frequently thinks outside the box and relishes in using taboos to catch opponents off guard. A frequent trick of hers is to catch others at their most vulnerable in public bathrooms. Although quite driven as a Siren she enjoys meeting simple, basic desires and doing as she pleases.

                    Backstory: Ixcuina, as she now calls herself, grew up in the region south of the Gulf of Mexico, a land of highs and lows. Lows included the mingling trade of Colombian drugs and Mexican cartels. As she grew older her hometown grew more corrupt with crime and pollution. The dregs of society have no hope when both neighboring sets of Civilization reject them, the very land itself refusing to aid as Nature worsens at maltreatment.

                    Her full despairing event was being in the midst of a shootout between two gangs, her travel through the Deluge sympathetic to another event far across the Gulf where oil corporations really screwed up just outside of American waters. The Deepwater Horizon oil spill.

                    In the Deluge she saw an earth where such pollution had reached its penultimate turmoil. The flooded seas a mire of crude sludge, stricken wildlife dead in the oceans and flaming spills blocking out the skies with smoke.

                    Her survival instincts were ones of desperation, needing any way out of this cataclysmic destruction humanity had reeked on itself in pursuit of energy and greed. It brought Ixcuina into the depths of Echidna's domain, the dark mother welcome to new recruits that would agree to help her help humanity. Fixing Ixcuina with the means to survive this massive oil spill, Echidna joined her with another group of her Sirens and Vodianoi sent to punish those that failed to remedy their crimes against nature that threatened humanity so.

                    She was a capable agent amongst the Echidnal Sirens, going off to enact eco-terrorism and returning with more human resources for Vodianoi. Her resolve to follow Echidna's aims, enforced by the sheer number of malformations she had at one time, soon began to waver as her Akashic Record increased. First it was the fights amongst other Abyssal Currents, which drew attention of the Celestial Currents and other foes. And from each defeat or pyrrhic victory, Ixcuina had more questions brought on from the realization that there are more Sirens in the sea than she imagined, each with very different viewpoints.

                    Questions her fellow Echidnal Sirens didn’t appreciate, especially the thought that Echidna’s deprived mutilations would never produce the next stage of humanity that could survive the Deluge. After leaving anonymous tip offs to Sirens from the Celestial Currents about Echidna’s recent aims, she effectively defected from the sea monster’s faction in the midst of an ambush (more of a slaughter, really) of her former comrades.

                    Since then Ixcunia has been enjoying newfound freedom, her independent streak leading her along the Current of Buoyancy, rarely keeping close ties with other Sirens save for the original Sirens she developed rapport with as an Echidnal agent. Still, the oil spoiled Deluge she experienced strives her to prevent or punish widescale pollution, be it actual or thematic corruption of life.

                    Crossover:
                    After distancing herself from Echidna, Ixcunia once came across the site of a Tainted Place that she mistook for a Quagmire. After some trial and error she found that the wounds left of the world from the Darkness could be impacted by similar Verses she used combating mundane pollution. Although Darkspawn were just mindless monsters and easily considered foes, she was more sympathetic towards Darkened humans if unsure what to do about them. From her spective the tainted Darkness was a potent stain not easily cleaned, the unfortunate Darkened could only be prevented from getting worse rather than better.

                    Developing a specialty towards Tainted Areas, investigation of a location in the MidWest got Ixcuina in over her head. Low on Pneuma when she needed to escape, the Mnemosyne that tended the Taint managing to capture her and developing an ingenious way to imprison the Siren where she would be unable gain Pneuma or form a rhythm to enact even the most basic verse.

                    Given that the Light forms the natural enemy of the Darkness, perhaps some Princesses will manage to discover her in time when dealing with the Mnemosyne foe.

                                    Choruses
                                    When it comes to finding purpose for Choruses that finds ground between the themes, mechanics and various ways you can take it, there should be one foundation from which each Chorus is built.

                                    A Chorus is made in support of something.

                                    If Water is their origin and Current their motivation, the Chorus is what brings various Sirens together for the support of something greater. No matter how small or background their part, the Sirens within the Chorus are vital for dynamic and continued support of this ideal. Sometimes this requires joining together with others of the Chorus. Sometimes this requires knowing when to take center stage and when to stay in the wings so another can act. Sometimes it can be a grueling obligation, for the Chorus is also the refrain that occurs again and again in the Song. Some Sirens take comfort in this, knowing that even if the point of a Chorus disappears it will one day become important again.

                                    You could label the 'what' that a Chorus supports based on category although I think this is unnecessary. With a core of 'support' you can have Sirens dedicated to various goals and the enhanced abilities to match. This could be providing the support of a story or sequence to come to pass, much like the roles Changelings and Prometheans put themselves in. It could be support of a specific culture, genre or style for how it can be applied to the Siren's woven magic. Choruses can also be proactive or preventive towards various kinds of disasters, both social and natural, all the way up to insuring a specific version of the Deluge comes to pass for more ambiguously nefarious takes on Choruses.

                                    In this way Choruses are much like Embassies and Protectorates from Princess, with a side of Changeling Entitlements. I do like the Princess variety of z-splat where you have tiers of effects for that sense of achievement and progression, although that isn't to say it should carbon copy that method of a prestige option.

                                    I'm waffling about what else to cast as concrete for the origination of a Chorus. At the same time it provides new avenues and benefits you don't what it to replace other parts of the system. Perhaps a Siren can join and leave Choruses easily, or even have multiple in effect if she can manage it. Yet her advancement in multiple won't be as significant as a single or primary Chorus. I'm not sure what determines that limit. You also don't what the Chorus benefits to be redundant of Verse or Trace mechanics, as there is already a lot of freeform in those systems. On one hand using an Opus as a prerequisite for a Chorus would represent both the niche and variety of Sirens within it, although you shouldn't have to belong in a certain Chorus to use an Opus (& Introduction/Conclusion) in any particular way.

                                    Character Draft: Roe Lannister
                                    Concept: self sustaining acoustic musician

                                    Water: Freshwater
                                    Current: Harmony

                                    Favored Opuses: Beauty, Sound, Water, Human, Technology, Fate
                                    Favored Conclusion: Calm

                                    Attributes:
                                    Intelligence 3, Wits 3, Resolve 4
                                    Strength 3, Dexterity 1, Stamina 2
                                    Presence 2, Manipulation 4, Composure 3

                                    Mental Skills: Academics 4, Computer 2, Investigation 2, Occult 2, Politics 3
                                    Physical Skills: Athletics 2, Survival 3
                                    Social Skills: Animal Ken 4, Empathy 2, Expression 4, Persuasion 2, Socialize 4

                                    Specialties: Computer (Sound-mixing, Digital Distribution), Expression (Folk Music), Socialize (Concerts)

                                    Size: 5
                                    Perception: 6
                                    Willpower: 7

                                    Flow: Spontaneity
                                    Beaching: Seclusion

                                    Akashic Record: 3
                                    Pneuma: 12/3
                                    Refraction: -2

                                    Introductions: Sentient, Location, Society
                                    Opuses: Beauty, Sound, Human, Technology, Fate, Animal, Growth, Dream, Passion
                                    Conclusions: Calm 4, Restore 3, Protect 2, Sense 4

                                    Trace: bonus to Audience

                                    Diluvian Form: max 6 Alterations
                                    Common: Whalesong
                                    Polaria: Ice Walker
                                    Freshwater: Anuran Breath, Amphibious Legs
                                    Discordia: The Living Fossil Coelacanth

                                    Knowledges: The Future is Now, Life is Always Changing

                                    Mental Merits: Common Sense, Direction Sense, Indomitable, Meditative Mind (4 dots)
                                    Physical Merits: Ambidextrous
                                    Social Merits: Fame (2 dots; musician), Barfly, Inspiring, Safe Place (2 dots; cabin)
                                    Siren Merits: Enchanting Voice (2 dots), Theronic Companion (3 dots)

                                    Companion: Matthias the “Marsh Hound”
                                    Taking the mundane appearance of a well trained husky and lab mix, the hound is more like an aquatic wolf with flipper appendages, a seal-like pelt and coloration matching the frozen marshlands. Friends know Matthias to accompany Roe most anywhere and for being notoriously thirsty.
                                    Size: 4; Alterations: Thalassic Movement, Lamian Senses, Amphibious Legs

                                    Appearance: Roe is an aged looking man approaching his seventies. He has an angular face, long grey/white hair and an unshaven face. He favors casual yet durable wear including down jackets, pullovers and tan pants. He has a slim build with strong legs.

                                    In his Diluvian form Roe looks mostly the same, with wrinkly waterlogged skin and webbed digits. If anything his hair becomes extra shaggy, almost like thatch. His amphibus features and retained humanoid frame, along with hometown location, are indicative of legends about swamp or marsh men. Particularly like the character of ‘Puddleglum, the Marshwiggle’ from the Chronicles of Narnia.

                                    Personality: Roe is a friendly and outgoing man, very sensitive towards changes in society and the stretches of nature along his northwoods hometown along Lake Superior. He has earned some modest fame as a folk musician, developing a signature style where he ‘records’ his music live by layering vocal and guitar tracks with a pedal operated sound mixer.

                                    Although very comfortable onstage and other public areas, Roe remains a private man who favors stretches of solitude. People interest him, because his music is derived from the human condition. It is within nature, the deep forests and cold lakes bordering Minnesota and Canada, that he finds solace. Roe is very much a slow and steady type of guy, often eschewing contact entirely between tours when he is at work at new music. His love is to bring nature, technology and music together, a desire that has grown stronger since his Drowning.

                                    Backstory: Monroe Lannister, to use his full name, did not experience his Drowning until he was past middle age. For the first half of his life he was a curious and passionate if wayward individual, unsure of where he wanted to be in the world. Feeling that his hometown was small and too confining he had many false starts as an adult trying to find a new niche.

                                    He traveled extensively, tried his hands at the fast paced world of business, studied new technologies and rarely stayed in a career for long. He did hone his skills as a musician at this time but never did anything with it beyond busking at street corners. Roe had no ambitions, no direction and no family. Ultimately he had no ties of his own.

                                    Roe recounts his Drowning as a bitter soul wracking pang of nostalgia and homesickness. He remembered his hometown, the one that felt so confining when he was in his youth. He remembered exploring the woods behind his home, of endless gazing across the lapping waves of the great lake. He remembered the simple, rural people and the qualities of the small town that rooted them within family and community, along with others like him who had spurned their early life the first chance they got.

                                    He experienced the both widespread and personal horror of the Deluge. Of people who forgot their roots, of humanity forgetting what it owes to the geography and planet that sustains them. Of a world deaded, the lakes destroyed and the woods cut down from endless urban sprawl. Of the dangers when Civilization is left unchecked, not only is Nature destroyed but humanity and its descendants also get the raw end of the deal. Domed to futile lives under the trappings of Civilization, its concrete and metal and technology that is uncentered from the core of community, protection and love for one’s home. A sorrow that says 'if only there was more that had been done.'

                                    Roe was reminded that change did not need to be big. Small changes, even as simple as a smile at the right time or a simple comfort for a troubled soul, can add up to make all the difference. Another man of his age might have languished that he had wasted his life for so long but for Roe it was a new beginning. If his only legacy was going to be the music that reminds people to find their family and community, bridging the needs of both Nature and Civilization, then that was enough for him.

                                    He returned to the hometown he left behind, taking home in a cabin that had no plumbing or modern comforts until he installed green technology only for his basic needs. The majority of the power was dedicated to his recording studio, his music all self produced. He became a prolific local musician in a relatively short time, reintegrating himself in his community and gaining a modicum of recognition across the MidWest. Alongside his career he invested in green technologies, joined or encouraged grassroots movements, and becoming a fixture in an annual festival along the shores of Superior. The great lake served as the testing ground to explore his new abilities. He is a musician of two musics, the sound of his guitar and the twisting tale of the great Song.

                                    Roe prefers to work indirectly as a Siren, not only encouraging humanity to rethink their place in the world but to inspire and support his fellow Sirens as well. If it is within his power he would travel to assist another Celestial Siren, especially in his own Current. Otherwise he remains in his semi-reclusive lifestyle in the heart of the northwoods. A life that is punctuated by sojourns into the Deluge, observing the flux of its cataclysms and the initial breakdowns that cause its ripples. From this he creates new music or fosters communication across Sirens continental and international that are striving for a positive change. It was from such a journey that he trained the Theron he named Matthias.

                                            Today at work (as I frequently let my mind percolate ideas in the background) I had more ideas about how a Chorus could be structured. I'm not sure if the mechanics 'sound' proper for Siren but it would be a unique z-splat option to expand upon Siren further.

                                            Continuing from the idea that a Chorus is formed in support of something, the tier of benefit the Siren receives from a Chorus is factored by how much effort she puts into it. There is also a timely aspect to the Chorus, membership 'renewing' every month which hereafter will be referred to as a Cycle. The length of a Cycle is the personal timeline the Siren experiences. For example, if she spent a day in the Deluge but returned to earth a week after she departed, her Cycle only would have advanced a day.

                                            Membership in a Chorus provides 3-4 benefits with permanency in the following categories: Passive, Active and Invested.

                                            A Siren receives the Passive benefit the moment she joins a Chorus she meets the prerequisites for. Prerequisites would be Akashic Record 3 and any Attribute/Skill etc ratings. The Siren always receives the Passive benefit from a Chorus she has joined until she decides to leave it. A Passive benefit should provide, at most, a +1 bonus, automatic Trace success or a simple effect normally conveyed by a Conclusion 1 Verse.

                                            The Active benefit shows the Siren is not passively sitting in her Chorus, that she communicates and helps others in her Chorus frequently. Each Cycle, the Siren can have only one Active Benefit from a single Chorus. For Sirens only in one Chorus they receive that Active benefit. For Sirens in two or more Choruses, they must select which Chorus they are Active in each Cycle, trading off one Active Benefit for another. To change Active Benefits early the Siren must invest Pneuma, covered below. The Active benefit is either an increased version of the Passive benefit or a new, slightly more widespread effect.

                                            A Siren receives the Invested benefit by dedicating her full capabilities and energy to supporting her Chorus, above and beyond the duty of Active membership. Being Invested means you have both the Passive and Active benefits, so the Siren needed to have selected the Active benefit in the Chorus for this Cycle. A Siren is Invested in her Chorus by giving most of her voice into the snippet of the Song her Chorus represents. This is done by spending a hefty cost of Pneuma, the precise tier of the Invested benefit being scalable for a gross amount of Pneuma for either a factored dice bonus or widespread and nuanced effect. The base Investment cost is also the price for the Siren to become Active in a Chorus before the end of her Cycle.


                                            Although this analogy has nothing to do with the themes of Siren: the Drowning, where a Siren sits in her Chorus(es) in this model is very much like a game of Monopoly. Initially joining a Chorus and being Passive is like owning one property in Monopoly. You get a little perk but don't receive much until you collect the whole set (become Active). Once a Siren is Active if she has the Pneuma to spend each Cycle she can become Invested, which in the Monopoly analogy is when you start putting down houses and hotels. Normally only Sirens with a high Akashic Record will be able to be Invested in the Choruses. A Siren could remain in a single Chorus and be able to use its benefits more consistently. Or the Siren could be a member of different Choruses, receiving all the Passive benefits but only a sole Active and chance for Invested benefits at one time each Cycle. Leaving a Chorus completely is when you no longer own any Monopoly properties in the set.

                                            While that's a draft for the general layout of a Chorus, I admit I don't have any full example Choruses with theme and mechanics to help demonstrate the idea at the time of this post.

Or the Siren could be a member of different Choruses, receiving all the Passive benefits but only a sole Active and chance for Invested benefits at one time each Cycle. Leaving a Chorus completely is when you no longer own any Monopoly properties in the set.

                                                While that's a draft for the general layout of a Chorus, I admit I don't have any full example Choruses with theme and mechanics to help demonstrate the idea at the time of this post.
                                                I'm definitely putting you in the Special Thanks for making the Chorus outline. I really like how this looks, it's a good system for Siren and it fits the ideas presented above as well as one I haven't presented. Namely, the ideas of how Fellowships were in Genius and how Geniuses could temporarily join one and being called a "Scholarship". The idea of having a passive Benefit and that Sirens could belong to one temporarily.

                                                I wonder at the possibility of Choruses (as a Passive Effect) also being able to favor Opuses or Conclusions, making it easy to gain experience in a Conclusion or to form up Stanzas for Opuses.

                                                The Order of the Black Huntsmen
                                                Now now, don't fret about our deal. It was clearly spelt out, and you put your name on the dotted line. Maybe next time you'll actually look over the fine print and know that I always get my due.
                                                Aka: The Order of Strangers, The Order of Old Scratch
                                                Nicknames: Samiels, Dealers, Strangers, Freeshooters

                                                Throughout history, there have been many stories of people who have made deals with the supernatural for various boons. Sometimes it's to extend lives, sometimes it's to make a simple fortune, sometimes it's to win their beloved's heart. But in all cases, there's always a price, almost always equal to the boon gained. The Sirens have taken note of these events, forging a Chorus based on the simple support of the concept of equivalent exchange: gaining one thing and something of equal value must be lost.

                                                The Order of the Black Huntsmen are therefore happy to play the role as a mysterious benefactor that treats their targets to whatever their hearts desire, until the day comes that they eventually take something of equal value away. Sometimes it helps against the deluge, but those within this Chorus can sometimes care less and focus more on the present.

                                                The Huntsmen and the Requirements: The Huntsmen, or Huntswomen, are usually made up from the ranks of the Vaporia or Polaria Waters, given the formers knowledge of choice and consequence and the latter's knowledge that eventually everything must end. The Currents of Buoyancy and Cocytus are also enamored with this Chorus, as the Winding Current is usually good enough to carry out manipulations to grant boons and take them away, and Cocytus either has things of great power to offer to potential "signatories" or are willing to dig up dirty secrets to show the world of their target as their due.

                                                In order to join the Order, the Siren must not just display the prerequisite for her connection to the Song, but also through her ability to manipulate others and get them willing to sign for what they dream of. Their Persuasion Skill must be at 3 or above.

                                                Passive: The Samiel gains a +1 Bonus to all Traces and Verses that deal with affecting someone she currently has an outstanding deal with.
                                                Active: The Samiel may add their Akashic Record to all Traces and verses that deal with affecting someone she currently has an outstanding deal with.
                                                Invested: The Samiel can at any time make a deal with a mortal, in which the mortal is entitled to receive a number of boons equal to the Siren's Akashic Record. They may choose whether the boon is small (like using a verse for a Mortal's devices), medium (the mortal desires Resources 2 and above) or even large (the mortal wishes to start a new life elsewhere). The number of beginning boons are small boons, but a mortal can give up two small boons to gain a medium boon, or three small boons to get one large boon, or vice versa.

                                                Then, within the time agreed upon to grant the boons, the Huntsman must deliver upon them whenever the mortal calls upon them. One catch is that the Huntsman is free to twist any one of the boons to whatever they desire, usually equivalent to the boons already spent or used. A Hunstman can choose to do the twist at the first boon called in, meaning the mortal will not suffer from any further twists but also denying any equal exchange, turning the Huntsman's bonuses from this current into penalties (effectively, this means the Siren takes the bad luck on herself).

                                                However, a Huntsman may choose to hold back committing their twists until after doing a few boons already. Doing so in this manner grants a +1 for each small boon completed beforehand, +2 for each medium boon, and +3 for each large boon. This bonus stacks with the Passive and Active bonuses from this Chorus, and the bonuses apply towards any roll that would help set up or act out the Twist, so a Huntsmen who waits to twist a boon until it's the very last boon usually find that their efforts succeed without fail. Huntsmen who do not commit a Twist find that the boons are taken out of their own lives instead, which is a harsh warning to those in the Order to take it out on those who desire favors.
