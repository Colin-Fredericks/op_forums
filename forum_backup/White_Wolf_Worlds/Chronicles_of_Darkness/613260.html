    Nurglings?!

    08-03-2015, 09:09 AM
    So after writing a thread asking for how to develop Nyarlathotep from the Cthulhu Mythos, I felt tempted to try another ancient evil as a demon. His name is Papa Nurgle. Thing is, if you're doing a conversion of the Warhammer Chaos God of Entropy and Disease, you have to realize that he's got a lot of minions. A LOT. Any ideas on a Cipher that'd allow for cryptids akin to nurglings, Plaguebearers, and/or Rotted Beasts? Or maybe it'd be part of a Mystery Cult merit...

      Personally, I think you could do a passable job using Exploits (Affliction, Hellhounds and Swarm are really all you need, the only downside being that latter two aren't permanent). A Cipher to create/command supernatural disease-monsters is a tricky one. I might have a go - Plaguebearers are the most interesting to me, what with the whole 'used to be human' angle. Nurglings and Rotted Beasts just feel they should be modeled with aforementioned Exploits (Swarm and Hellhounds, respectively) - just a reskin of the powers from biomechanical to filthy, disease-ridden rotting horror would do it.

      What else would a Nurglite Cipher contain? Obviously some disease spreading/manipulating powers. Possibly some ability to gain endurance when diseased, either for oneself or others. Hmmm...

              To be frank, I don't think his Cipher should focus of making lots of minions. The literal disease powers can be represented by the aforementioned Exploits. If you feel it is crucial, you could give him a high Primum Merit that greatly extends the duration of any effect he inflicts upon living tissue. However, Nurgle does not force his gifts upon others, he has to feel like they are being accepted. That might make Hellhounds out of character. You could give him some kind of "Cryptid Whisperer" Merit, which is more "friendly". Said Merit might let him train Cryptids with greater ease and sustain them with less Aether/Essence. However, I could say that Cryptids who bond with an Unchained master this way starve faster than usual when separated from him and/or manifest his Glitches (which would make the pet show sign of disease, in this case). But let's not forget the Living Installation Exploit, which explicitly lets you make Gadgets out of living beings. These bio-Gadgets can only hold Embeds and last up to a month, though.

              You could also give him another special Merit that lets him build biological Gadgets with Medicine instead of Crafts. It IS a game rule that Gadget Hardware can only consist of objects without supernatural properties. Biological parts that have been separated from their former owners make quite a mess, even when removed with free surgeries. Then again, he probably lets said body parts decompose before using them as Hardware. It's pretty much brute matter by then, so there is less risk of Hardware rejection.

              Most importantly, the plagues of body and mind are tangible manifestations of a more abstract yet primal fear: everything and everyone will become dust eventually. That leads me to believe that his Cipher breaks down normal objects, then Gadgets, then whole Infrastructures and finally the wills of Demon and Angel alike. So this Cipher probably has Cacophony, Instrumental and Vocal Embeds. Mundane Embeds seem very unlikely.

              I'll see if I can make a convincing Cipher for our Papa, and maybe a full character sheet.

              EDIT: my current plan is to make it a Cipher with only Cacophony Embeds. The Keys are, in order: Bystander Effect, Shifty Eyes, Anarchism and Breakdown. The first Interlock causes sluggish and conformist views to gain more influence on the the local Agencies, resulting in a snowball of failed missions. The second Interlock increases tensions to the point of causing direct conflict with no regard for collateral damage until it is too late. The third and final Interlock petrifies the collective despair into hopeless inaction and self-destructive behavior on a wide scale. Mechanics coming soon.

              EDIT 2: actually, scratch that comment about Nurgle only giving plagues with acceptance from others. He spreads plagues wherever and whenever he wants. What he offers is the blessing of not being about to care about the plagues that have warped you beyond recognition.

                  Behold, Nurgle's Cipher! Admittedly, the Interlocks sort of clash with the game's themes and rules, but I feel like it captured Nurgle's essence pretty well. All feedback is welcome.

                  Grown From Idleness

                  Nurgle does not alter his discourse when alliances shift. No one will ever love and care for you like he will. If only you’d listen.

                  Prerequisites: Bystander Effect and Shifty Eyes

                  Dice pool: Presence + Persuasion

                  Dramatic Failure: The children throw a tantrum, delaying their release. For the rest of the scene, suffer a -2 penalty to all Social rolls not related to Intimidation.

                  Failure: No effect. Try again with a cumulative -1 penalty.

                  Success: Nurgle may give a warning, speech or similar pronouncement that implants a suggestion of generalized distrust in those who can hear it in person. The only limitation to the spread of this Interlock and the next ones is that recordings, transmissions and documents of Nurgle’s words don’t work. He may use technology to increase the reach of his voice, but he prefers it to be up close.

                  Anyone with even a small reason to place blame on others and do nothing about it feels more justified. For the rest of the scene, any attempts to help others with extended actions that might risk one’s Cover require a Resolve + Composure roll before each interval, penalized by Nurgle’s successes. They may still help if they fail the Resolve + Composure roll, but must spend 1 willpower point.

                  Exceptional Success: As before, but now helping with even instant actions requires a Resolve + Composure roll and possibly Willpower points.

                  Ruination From Within

                  It makes him laugh. No offense. Cold and stiff dreams are as predictable to him as the sun rising, and yet, it hurts his bloated heart to see family on each other’s throats. His arms are forever open, and his embrace closes you forevermore.

                  Prerequisites: Shifty Eyes and Anarchism

                  Dice pool: Manipulation + Subterfuge - Resolve

                  Dramatic Failure: The little ones lie to themselves, saying that adversity builds character. They gain the Steadfast Condition.

                  Failure: No effect. Try again with a cumulative -1 penalty.

                  Success: Those who lost all willpower points as a result of the previous Interlock within the scene may be targeted by this one, even if they regained 1 or more willpower points. They may have done their best, but accusations of not doing enough may be thrown from any side. All rolls made with the intent of remembering what really went wrong and finding out what can be fixed don’t just sound futile and dangerous. Their very Covers are infected with malware, making said rolls require Compromise checks at a -2 penalty. The Integrity of mortal allies and servants is likewise corrupted.

                  Exceptional Success: The penalty is increased to -4. In addition, Exceptional Success cannot be reached and Failure turns into Dramatic Failure automatically, with no Beats earned.

                  Come To Your Papa

                  Now you see the truth. But he will not hold it against you. He sees your pain, no matter how well you think you are hiding it. He can make it all better. Come with him and let all hope be undone. You are home.

                  Prerequisites: Anarchism and Breakdown

                  Dice pool: Wits + Empathy - Composure

                  Dramatic Failure: They shun him, they shun him loudly. He is the cause of their misery, but they will not be defeated. He can wait. The Inspired Condition may be given to and used by any of them for regaining some sense of trust among themselves.

                  Failure: No effect. Try again with a cumulative -1 penalty.

                  Success: Any Unchained who received the Blown, Betrayed, or Hunted Condition as a result of the previous Interlock may end them by accepting Nurgle’s gift. Doing so results in something normally impossible: the Demon is now indefinitely one of the Burned (NOT Gone Loud), but no negative or positive Conditions related to the God-Machine and its agents can apply to him anymore. It is as if the Demon was completely deleted from all the radars and data banks of the God-Machine. His Demonic Form, no matter how glorious it might have been, is now covered and overflowing with all that is filthy, for flesh and metal. The Burned can receive one of the following Exploits for free, disregarding Prerequisites: Affliction, Rain Of Blood or Swarm. Nothing short of a true miracle can restore the illusion of normalcy, but this Burned cannot take any action of his own will to try and change his situation. It goes without saying that this may shock and terrify both Demons and mortals, even loved ones. He simply no longer cares.

                  Exceptional Success: Nurgle may further bless this Burned with the Demonic Rage Condition. The resulting shell is filled with something closer to a serene despair with a life of its own and a will to spread. He gains all the 3 Exploits mentioned above.

                          Originally posted by MrParaduo View Post
                          Very interesting. I never considered an interlock that negated the drawbacks of being out in the open. Granted, that might prove OP, but then, it's an endgame Cipher.
                          I know it might sound overpowered, but let me emphasize a few of its aspects.

                          When Nurgle says "you are wonderful just the way you are" (that is, being out in the open), the message he sends is "nothing will ever get better, just give up".

                          The final Interlock removes both negative and positive Conditions related to the God-Machine. The Demon effectively vanished from the cosmos, as far as the God-Machine can tell. What if an Integrator was tricked or blackmailed into accepting the gift? His vision of Hell is but a suffocated gurgle in the back of his mind now. His former allies see him as a terminally ill patient, discussing his elimination like he is junk code. The few mortals he cared about as something other than pawns will run away at the sight of his rotting form, or break down and cry if they figure out what happened. He won't be able to tell why. Everything is so...so calm. They'll understand, he just has to show them what real love means. He doesn't go into perma-Loud mode, and even with an Exceptional Success on the last Interlock, Nurgle can choose to not make him a bigger threat. But why would Nurgle want that? That's selfish.

                          And remember, the Integrator only ended up like that because, at one point, he insisted on making a difference. He could have simply remained isolated in his ulterior goals and secret plans, but he just had to feel altruistic and keep spending Willpower points, didn't he? That's the kind of fucked up lesson that the Chaos Gods teach, willingly or not. No good deed ever goes unpunished, no evil deed ever goes unrewarded. And the rewards of evil, in the end, are not worthy of the name.

                          Besides, one Burned with a few extra Exploits is no threat to Unchained with minimal teamwork, especially since this Burned doesn't draw the God-Machine's attention by himself. It's a psychological horror deal, like dropping to Integrity 0.

                                  I'm not a big fan of Come To Your Papa, mostly because it appears to be permanent, rather than a super-Embed. You'd use it once (per target). It feels like those rules should be our Nurgle-demon's Hell, not their final Interlock.

                                  Here's a quick and dirty take of my own.

                                  Plaguebearer

                                  This vile curse infects a person with a disease both physical and spiritual, isolating them from the rest of humankind and leaving them open to despair. The demon must touch the target to use this power.

                                  Keys: Unperson and Shift Consequence

                                  Dice pool: Presence + Occult - Resolve

                                  Dramatic Failure: The target is immune to this Interlock for the rest of the month.

                                  Failure: No effect. The demon may try again (suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt).

                                  Success: Too all observers, including themselves, the target appears to be suffering from a horrible, disfiguring disease. They will find themselves shunned and ignored by those they meet, a modern-day leper, rejected and desperate, for a number of days equal to the successes rolled. They are likely to suffer Breaking Points due to the rejection. Once the Interlock wears off, all symptoms disappear entirely.

                                  Exceptional Success: In addition to the above, the target cannot regain Willpower by any means while affected by this Interlock.

                                  Master of Pestilence


                                  All disease are but the tools of the Great Unclean One. They may spread plague with a touch, suppressing the immune system and inflaming pre-existing conditions. The demon must touch the target, or cough/sneeze in their face, to use this power.

                                  Keys: Shift Consequence and Special Someone

                                  Dice pool: Manipulation + Medicine - Stamina

                                  Dramatic Failure: The demon fall victim to their plague and takes an appropriate Condition for a number of days equal to their Primum.

                                  Failure: No effect. The demon may try again (suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt).

                                  Success: The target receives as appropriate Condition to reflect their new, or newly strengthened, illness. Use the Sick Tilt when in combat. This Condition lasts a number of days equal to the demon’s Primum.

                                  Exceptional Success: The Condition is now permanent.
                                  Mark of Nurgle


                                  The Grandfather of Plagues offers the strength to endure his pestilential gifts; give in. Accept it. In despair, in surrender, there is strength. Thus Interlock allows a demon to gift such strength to their victims. All targets must suffer from an illness of some sort.

                                  Keys: Special Someone and Just Bruised

                                  Dice pool: Manipulation + Occult (-Stamina)

                                  Dramatic Failure: The disease accelerates without the demon’s careful control and debilitates the host. They receive the Disabled Condition.

                                  Failure: No effect. The demon may try again (suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt).

                                  Success: This power is twofold. The target no longer suffers any dice penalties from any Conditions relating to disease or wound penalties. Their disease is not cured, however. Far from it. It worsens, becoming obvious at a glance. This Interlock lasts a number of days equal to the successes rolled.

                                  Exceptional Success: In addition to any physical bonuses, the target is able to draw mental strength from their physical state. They receive a +2 to all Breaking Points while this Interlock persists.
