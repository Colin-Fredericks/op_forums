    [Werewolf/Exalted 2e Terrible Idea] Yozi Gifts

    03-09-2016, 12:57 PM
    So, I was musing about stuff earlier this week, and it hit me that you can totally draw mental parallels between Infernals and Werewolves, in terms of "get their powers from elsewhere, embedded in their souls, themed around spiritual entities". So I thought that, hey, maybe some Gifts themed around the Yozi wouldn't be terribly amiss.

    It'd make a neat side point - maybe they're Gifts that you get from an Idigam or an Abyssal Entity.

    Unfortunately... I'm a bit stuck. I have a couple of ideas for a few Gifts... but I'm too unfamiliar with the Gift list to ensure that I'm not coming up with overlaps. Also, I just used Charm names from Exalted as reminders to myself of what stuff does - there are more wolf-y names I could use when it comes time.

    But hey, here's what I've thought of so far:

    Gift of Adorjan
    • Wretched Hateful Noise (Cunning) :- Enhances hearing to a terrifying degree; sound becomes another Kuruth trigger.
    • Self As Cyclone Stance (Glory) :- Autofire with your Brawl attacks- 1 Essence for a Medium burst, 5 Essence for a long burst. If you split the attacks, you can move between them.
    • Tragic Love Amusement (Honor) :- Lets you add another character to the pack, and then immediately calls the Sacred Hunt on them.
    • Wind Borne Stride (Purity) :- Multiply your speed for the purposes of movement by [Purity + 1] on any turn where you Dodge.
    • Broken Souls Wisdom (Wisdom) :- Give someone a Ban - mortals that break the Ban are subject to Lunacy.

    Gift of Cecelyne
    • Sublimation of Ordained Purpose (Cunning) :- Requests that would fulfil another character's Aspirations open doors equal to Cunning instead of just one.
    • Demonic Primacy of Essence (Purity) :- Your Purity counts twice when determining your effective Rank. If your effective Rank exceeds a Spirit's by 2 (3?), you can engage in Social Maneuvering without giving gathra.

    Gift of She Who Lives In Her Name
    • Cosmic Transcendence of (Renown) (Purity):- Boosts your Auspice Renown like woah. You get a Kuruth trigger and Blood Archetype appropriate to the Renown, and... stuff.

    I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

    So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.

                          Gift of Adorjan
                          • Wretched Hateful Noise (Cunning) :-
                          As the embodiment of silence, Adorjan senses her antithesis with unparalleled sensitivity.
                          Cost: None
                          Duration: Permanent
                          The Uratha's hearing becomes extremely enhanced, she may completely ignore the Blinded Tilt, and can hear any noise higher than a whisper within ([Cunning] * 100) yards of herself, however sound higher than normal conversation, becomes incredibly painful to her, and counts as a Kuruth Trigger. Any time she suffers the Deafened Tilt she also takes a point of bashing damage.

                          • Self As Cyclone Stance (Glory) :-
                          There is stillness. And then there is storm.
                          Cost: 1 Essence or 5 Essence
                          Action: Instant
                          Duration: Instant
                          The Uratha is hastened by the swiftness of Adorjan, allowing her to Autofire with her Brawl attacks, this Gift causes her to make a Medium burst for 1 Essence, or a Long burst for 5 Essence. If she splits the attacks between different enemies, she may move up to her Speed between them.

                          • Tragic Love Amusement (Honor) :-
                          Sometimes, Adorjan falls in love. Her hate is safer.
                          Cost: 1 Essence
                          Action: Reflexive
                          Duration: Scene
                          This Gift immediately adds a target that the Uratha can see to her pack, and then immediately grants the Siskur-Dah Condition with regards to this Gift's target to all other members of her pack, including herself, but not the target of this Gift. For the duration of this Gift, it is no longer a Harmony Breaking Point to kill a packmember, but only if it is this Gift's target that is killed.

                          • Wind Borne Stride (Purity) :-
                          Where Adorjan blows, few can run fast or far enough to escape her touch.
                          Cost: None
                          Duration: Permanent
                          This Gift multiplies the Uratha's speed by (Purity + 1) on any turn where she dodges, as well as allow her to apply her Defense against firearms for that single turn.

                          • Broken Souls Wisdom (Wisdom) :-
                          Tragedy inspires the best in people. The weak graciously perish to make way for the strong, who rise above opposition and adversity to achieve greatness and ultimately perpetuate that hardship upon their enemies.
                          Cost: 1 Essence
                          Dice Pool: Presence + Intimidation + Wisdom versus Composure + Primal Urge
                          Action: Contested
                          Duration: One month per success in excess of the target's successes.
                          This Gift forces a character to gain a Ban as defined by the Uratha, such as "Do not eat meat" or "Jump three times and spin around when you go through a door." If the target is mortal, they suffer Lunacy if they break their Ban.
                          _

                          Note, I am really bad at writing Gifts.
