                        An incomplete writeup for the Perfected Adepts, posted before completion because I figured people will be more interested in the parts that I did complete.



                        PERFECTED ADEPTS

                        Origins

                        Parentage: Obrimos, Adamantine Arrows

                        Doctrine

                        Prerequisites: Life 2, Athletics 2, two dots in one of the following Skills; Brawl, Medicine, Survival, Academics, Weaponry

                        Magic

                        Ruling Arcanum: Life
                        Yantras: Places prepared for meditation (+1); succeeding on an Athletics roll relevant to the spell (+2); martial arts equipment (+2); acting in service of another (+1, +2 if acting with direct orders from the served)
                        Oblations: Meditation after physical exertion; rigorous martial arts practice; feats of endurance; exhibiting humility via willingly serving another

                        Attainments

                        First: Qi-Balance Technique
                        Prerequisites: Initiation
                        As the Monk balances her inner energies, the general balance (or imbalance) of others become apparent to her. This Attainment replicates the "Analyze Life" spell, with Reach assigned to instant use.
                        Optional: Forces 1
                        The Adept's inner peace spreads to his outer form. After a scene of quiet meditation, his movements are refined as per the "Kinetic Efficiency" spell. Reach is assigned to Advanced Duration.

                        Second: Flowing Chakra Method
                        Prerequisites: Life 2, Athletics 3
                        The Monk releases life-force through her own chakra points and into those of another. She can use Mana to heal others as per Pattern Restoration. Spending a point of Willpower while doing so allows her to employ Improved Pattern Restoration.
                        Optional: Forces 2
                        When the Adept charges energy to release it, he does so with agility born from discipline. By spending a point of Mana when using Precise Force, the Monk can target subjects moving faster than a casual walk.

                        Third: Body-as-Temple Focus
                        Prerequisites: Life 3, two dots in a second Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills
                        If the body is a temple, the Monk is both its god and high priest. This Attainment replicates the "Body Control" spell, with Reach assigned to instant use and Advanced Duration.
                        Optional: Forces 3
                        A temple does not stand isolated, and the Adept's focus spreads beyond his own form. This replicates the "Telekinetic Strike" spell, with Reach assigned to instant use.

                        Fourth: Unmarred Body Discipline
                        Prerequisites: Life 4, Athletics 4
                        The Self is Supernal, and the Supernal ought to be unmarred by the Fallen. The Monk may also use Body Autonomy under the following circumstances:
                        • She is targeted by supernatural powers that would reduce or deny her Initiative, Defense, or Speed traits.
                        • She is targeted by supernatural powers that would impair her Perception rolls.
                        • She is targeted by attacks that would inflict a Physical Condition on her.
                        Optional: Forces 4
                        Mastery over the Self is mastery over the World and its crude powers. When he uses Environmental Immunity, the Monk may spend one more point of Mana to invert any dice penalties the nullified environments into dice bonuses. The bonuses cannot exceed his own Forces dots.

                        Fifth: Supernal Prana Form
                        Prerequisites: Life 5, two dots in a third Skill among the optional prerequisite Skills
                        The Monk's body is finally perfected enough to be open to the cosmic flow, her enlightenment exalting her form beyond the undisciplined. By spending a point of Mana, she may boost one of her Physical Attributes as per the "Honing the Form" spell. Reach is assigned to instant use, Advanced Duration, and going beyond her natural maximum rating.
                        Optional: Forces 5
                        The Adept is a living nexus of the world's pulse, and he has mastered its torrents and currents. He can redirect and deflect moving objects as per the "Turn Momentum" spell. Reach is assigned to switching the primary factor to Duration, reflexive use, and controlling where the objects are turned to.

                            Very nice on the Perfected Adepts 21C Hermit!

                            Is there a reason you went with Analyze Life over Cleanse the Body?

                            I like the way you are extending the Mage template abilities and Arcanum Attainments, like with Pattern Restoration, in Flowing Chakra Method. Definitely more interesting than yet-another-spell.

                            For Body-as-Temple Focus, why the Reach on Instant Casting and Advanced Duration? Instant Casting, I can see for the immediate need of toxin slowing and breath control, such effects could be reapplied as needed, right? What about the 1/0 Armor Reach option? Or maybe the +2 Reach option for halving injuries, with the additional limitation of affecting only the Adept themselves? Or was part of the purpose behind both Instant Casting and Advanced Duration to make it so they could enhance all their fellows as well?

                            Telekinetic Strike at-will - ranged punching, then?

                            So, for the Optional Forces effect in Unmarred Body Discipline - inverting Environmental Tilts - how is this supposed to operate? They perform better in extreme heat and hurricanes?

                            So, for Supernal Prana Form's Turn Momentum: Advanced is to offer the Dodge effect, yes? But if it is Reflexive, wouldn't you just be able to apply it whenever needed anyways? If the Perfected Adept has Time 1+, could they redirect bullets?

                            On the subject of modifying how Attainments and template abilities work, what about Scouring others (some spells already do this, like Devouring the Slain) or objects? Or applying Mage Armor to an area (or inverting it upon another)?
