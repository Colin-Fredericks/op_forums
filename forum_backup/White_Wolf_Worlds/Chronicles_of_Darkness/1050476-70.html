    Or to put it another way, the Bastion is the shell or the skin of the dream.

    When you are within a dream, "the Bastion" simply refers to the dream as a whole, and there aren't necessarily any visible walls that would indicate where "the edge of the dream" is. Everything perceived within a dream is on the inside of the Bastion. There is, however, at least one defined exit from the dream, whatever form it takes (a road leading over the horizon, leaping down a chasm, an Employees Only door). Finding the exit and passing through will put you somewhere in the Dreaming Roads stepping out of the Bastion's entrance, or if you are in dream form, you can instead choose to wake up and return to your body.

    When you are in the Dreaming Roads, a Bastion appears as a symbolic representation of the events and motifs of the dream it contains: a dream of a night on the town might manifest as a nightclub with a line and a bouncer, or a dream of being chased might appear as a dark thicket or a vast wall featureless except for doors that open into snaking hallways. Bastions have exterior defenses which an uninvited guest must circumnavigate to reach an entrance and enter the dream within, represented either by eidolons of the dream standing guard outside, or by hazards and obstacles represented by the dream's Fortification rating.

                                    The Durance is heavily associated with memory loss (both in the way that trauma often can cause memory loss, and by memories literally ripped out of you in the form of Icons), so while a Changeling with Eidetic Memory could potentially remember large swathes of their Durance they would not remember all of it. It's even fully possible that the memory of the entire Durance is lost through Icons, meaning she wouldn't remember a thing even with Eidetic Memory.

                                    While remembering trauma can be traumatising in its own right (especially if it happens unexpectedly) it doesn't have to be, and there's no reason why a changeling's mind would "explode" from it unless the memory is unusually painful and previously repressed. Reenacting/reliving a memory from your Durance is a Clarity breaking point, but it's not one to just remember it.
                                    The Durance is universally described as traumatic, but it explicitly does not have to be (overtly) painful. Some changelings are stated to heavily regret their escape and want to get back to their Keeper, if possible.
