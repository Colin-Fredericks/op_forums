                                                    “And why did you do a thing like that?”

                                                    Sara paused. “I didn’t want to kiss him. I told him no but he wouldn’t leave me alone.”

                                                    “Ah, I see.” The old woman stood up. “A leanbh, come on out please? My knees are not what they once were.”

                                                    Sara crawled out from beneath the trailer, covered in dirt. She ran a hand across her face, smearing more mud on it. She looked down, nudging a rock with her foot. “I’m not in trouble then?”

                                                    Grainne pulled out a scrap of cloth and wiped Sara’s face for her. “No, Sara of mine. As far as I am concerned, you will never be in trouble for defending yourself and you will certainly never have to kiss anyone you don’t want to. Remember that, mo chroi. You, and everyone else, are your own selves and not beholden to other’s pleasures.” She took Sara’s hand. “And if some foolish country boy tries to tell you differently, knee him in the balls and let his loud mother be damned.”

                                                    Sara smiled up at her grandmother and squeezed her hand. “Thanks Maimeo. I love you.”

                                                    “And I you, mo chroi. Always remember that, as well.”

                                                    Sara’s eye fluttered open as the last notes fell away. Natalie let the silence sit for a few minutes, then began another song and Sara let herself get lost in the music from beyond the door as it mixed with the sounds of the bubbles.

                                                    After a few more songs and pauses, Natalie fell quiet. She listened for a few minutes before calling out. “Sara?”

                                                    “Mmm?”

                                                    “You know I love you, right?”

                                                    “Um.” Sara took a moment to gather her bearings. “Yeah, I know. I love you too, Natalie.”

                                                    Natalie moved closer to the door. “I don’t know what you’re planning to do tonight, dear, but... I need you. I need you right now, with... with everything.” Natalie could hear Sara getting out of the bath and drying off. “Everyone’s gone until tomorrow afternoon, it will be just us two.”

                                                    Sara came out, wrapped up in a couple of towels. Natalie’s brow knit - she’d never seen Sara wear a towel in the entire time she’d been there - but Sara didn’t meet her eyes, just moved past her, grabbing an oversized sweatshirt and a pair of pajama pants and putting them on, her back to Natalie. She stayed turned away from her wife, her head down. “Natalie, I...”

                                                    Natalie watched her carefully. Guilt was etched into every line of her body, and fear, fear that she wouldn’t be able to see the line as she crossed it, fear that she would push too far. Again. Sara eventually turned around, hunching her shoulders and retreating into herself again. Natalie noticed the bright spot on her lip where she was not quite still bleeding and searched her lover’s eyes, afraid to find familiar self destructive impulses.

                                                    She relaxed, slightly, when she didn’t find them, just an unrelenting desire to put herself somewhere outside her own control. She considered calling Karen, but she knew the two of them well enough to know that their play was not the kind of thing Sara was looking for. And that if it did reach that point, it would deal serious damage to their relationship.

                                                    “I know, love.” Natalie looked vulnerable and lonely. “Please, I need you to be here, I need to not be alone.”

                                                    “I need some time, Natalie. Love. I just need...” Sara was torn and the destruction of the certainty she’d felt these past few days, that aside from some timing issues, she was immune to the Balehound’s influence, that the idea of sexual ‘transgression’ was for other, less enlightened people left her doubting everything about herself. She wanted to be with someone who she could not possibly coerce, someone she had no control over. But Natalie looked at her pleadingly. “I’ll be fine, Elizabeth won’t hurt me, not really. And I can’t...” She looked away.

                                                    Natalie’s face fell. “It’s not safe, Sara. This influence is infectious. What would it do to Elizabeth? Please. I love you.” Natalie stepped forward. Sara moved back again. “We can do things separately, I just need you here.” Her voice sounded small.

                                                    Sara’s shoulder slumped. “Natalie...” She took a deep breath. “How about a movie? Or some TV show?”

                                                    A grateful smile came over Natalie’s face and she gestured for Sara to lead the way. Sara curled up in an armchair, while her wife sat on the couch. They eventually decided on Spartacus. Natalie even got a smile out of Sara when she promised to introduce her to Lucy Lawless, the next time she was anywhere nearby.

                                                    After going to get popcorn during a break, Sara sat back down on the couch, on the opposite end from Natalie. She smiled, but hid it from Sara by looking down at her phone. As the afternoon went on, the two moved closer together, with a few false starts and moments of backtracking when Sara realized that was happening.

                                                    Eventually, Natalie’s head lay on Sara’s lap while she rested her arm on Natalie’s side, skin to skin. Natalie was afraid to break the moment and send her love running away again. So she even pretended not to notice when Sara started to cry.
