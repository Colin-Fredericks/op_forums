                            Originally posted by MalkSam View Post
                            So is there anything really mechanically stopping a Geist from just telling their Bound who they are?
                            They cannot meaningfully communicate or be communicated with in a complex manner and "who they are" is frequently going to be a complete stranger to their Bound in the first place. It's a metaphysically scarred super-ghost whose primary means of being stopped from acting out on its own is a roll heavily dependent upon its host's core-level relation to it and/or placating bribes. Remembrance tableaus are the most coherent clues you're likely to get from it in the normal course of play.

                            They had to drink fire/scorpions/acid/raw hatred to become what they are, their voice isn't in the best of shape for self-introductions.
