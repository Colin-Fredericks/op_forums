    Session LI

    Doc’s Log

    Doc’s Log: 1030 Hours, Sunday, July 25, 2010

    Today has been pretty helpful in unwinding. Last night really took it out of us all the way around. I got up around 0800 Hours. Meditation took no time at all and I was down for breakfast already showered, dressed, and aware.

    Karen was there for breakfast, but no Nightshade as usual. I could hear the whole AM/PM conversation in my head just thinking about it. We talked about whom to go after next, since no matter how much I dislike Rose’s constant push to get everything done, we really are on a short time table. We had to choose between Alonia or Denise, keeping in mind that we’d have to at least briefly deal with both of them. Rose and I are two of the best at resisting possession but we’d like to actually resolve Denise. After dealing with Adolphe’s “Dead Man Switch,” the Krewe was fairly depleted, as far as using our abilities went so another big fight was not a happy thought. Karen spoke up when I discussed being a distraction for Alonia. She reminded us that Nightshade knew the ceremony to trap ghosts in a bottle. It was also fairly quick. That did help the planning stages, although I think I visibly tensed when Karen entered the conversation. The battle plan at the end of breakfast stood with us planning to trap Denise in a bottle then bind Alonia into her locket.

    Now I need to go and find Karen. She needs to understand my issues and I want to ask her something anyway.


    Doc’s Log: 1620 Hours, Sunday, July 25, 2010

    That went better than I expected.

    I found Karen and asked her if she had time to talk. Something had been bothering me since Karen moved in and I kept my mouth shut because I couldn’t figure out what it was at first...plus it would look bad on my part to randomly have unexplained problems with my girlfriend’s girlfriend. Especially since I freely agreed to the arrangement. When Karen entered the discussion this morning, I finally figured out exactly what it was that bugged me so much. I’m probably the worst Gatekeeper that ever took the Bargain but I understand now. My segregationism comes from trying to keep people safe, ghosts too but in this town they don’t need much help. People normally don’t have any way to deal with ghosts that are bothering them and are normally scarred by trying if they do find some way to deal with them. Thus I try to get involved to keep that from happening. The living stay living, the dead stay dead, and everyone is happy. Theoretically. But Karen got thrown into this. Yes she agreed to find out and yes I believe Nightshade tried very hard to make sure Karen knew what she was getting into. But she is still just a normal, if fairly intelligent, mortal. Normal men and women don’t have ways to deal with the supernatural if they are unlucky enough to get involved. And I had no option to keep Karen from getting involved. Now that she is, she is trying to learn as much as she can so she can keep up. I feel that way with the vampires we know, so I understand the feeling. And she’s done a pretty good job for someone that’s mostly been forced to stay on the sidelines. Tavia isn’t just a normal person so she’s into everything that isn’t combat-related. Karen has nothing. She carries a target on her back with nothing to cover it or shield herself. I don’t like that. It’s not fair and eventually it won’t be safe. So once we moved into the sitting room, I explained all that. I didn’t try to be condescending or anything, because she shouldn’t be able to deal with these things. I think (and hope) she understood why I’m concerned. Now this is the part that proves I’m a terrible Gatekeeper. Once I got all that out and off my chest, I asked her what she thought about the magic that Snowflake and I use. She said that she was normally a skeptic about anything like that, but being around us made it harder to discount out right. Then I asked her if she would be interested in learning something like that? She was a little taken aback as I don’t think she was expecting me to offer to teach her, but that’s basically what I was doing. I don’t like that she’s been thrown naked into a minefield. At the very least, I want to give her some clothes and maybe a better shot at seeing them, if not dodging them altogether. So Snowflake and I will soon have a new apprentice magician. Then it was Karen’s turn. She asked if any of us dealt with many normal people very often. I felt bad that she needed to make the distinction but I guess it’s fair. It was a little amusing to have to defend Rose’s taskmaster approach, but really we don’t have time to take off. I told her about the people at the shop for me and Cupcake, the students and coffee friends with Rose, Ronin has her clients and old police friends, and Nightshade has her and the people at the clubs. Even Snowflake has his group of normal friends up in Rhode Island. Karen told me that Nightshade really doesn’t go out that much anymore and she’s worried that never being around regular people will mess with her head. I think there’s some merit in the thought, but Nightshade goes out to the places when she’s low on Plasm...still, she may move around people less than any of the rest of us at this point. That bit of information was kind of surprising.

    About that time, Nightshade came in and informed me I needed a bidet in my shop, as she laid across both me and Karen. She gave Karen some kind of expectant look, the my new apprentice started immediately massaging Nightshade’s feet. I poked Nightshade for being rude then gave Karen some pointers to keep her hands from getting sore as quickly. A small part of me wanted to show her how it was supposed to be done, but I don’t need to start getting competitive now. We also covered a very bizarre tale of Nightshade’s first and last acid trip. Tavia and Chandra wanted to know if anyone was interested in playing paintball with them, but we needed to rest up. Tonight should be another major ordeal. We also realized about then that Cupcake, Rose, and Ronin were missing. There was a mess in the kitchen like Cupcake had been working on something but no other signs of any of them. I immediately called Ronin, fighting off the sudden panic urge that something had managed to get into the House. She answered her phone but told me they were fine. Said they were at the spa doing “girl stuff” and would be back later. I asked her why the hell she was there if they were doing girl stuff but she hung up on me.

    We all grabbed lunch after that and Snowflake followed us back into the sitting room. After continuing some of our previous conversations, I filled him in on the new magic partner. He was surprised but seemed to enjoy the thought. Anytime we can bring someone else in that can share something intellectual with him, Snowflake tends to like it. After a bit of that, it was my turn to be a little rude. Snowflake needed to talk about relationships, so I excused us to go have a “man talk.” Yes that was probably the worst thing I could have said, but Snowflake needed to talk and I didn’t know how comfortable he would have been admitting or displaying how naive he really is to Karen or Nightshade. Once we moved off into the gallery, Snowflake confessed that he really doesn’t want to be with Tavia in any capacity. We both knew that this would not go well and that Andrea would officially be in danger when it came out. I asked, even though I didn’t think it was the right option, if Tavia would accept being told “Master of the House”-style that he was going to have a mistress? We could never really be sure of what might happen there. He wants to go out and be normal, screw up, make mistakes, basically live...all on his own terms. I usually forget that he’s only 20 so this really does make sense. I told Snowflake he needed to decide if he has permanently lost interest in Tavia, because that may also have a great deal to do with how things need to be handled. Now that I think about all this together, it’s more than a little terrifying that Tavia and Chandra are out playing soldier together.

    Rose, Ronin, and Cupcake came back then and Rose seemed pretty happy. When she used the phrase “Va Va Voom,” I was pretty sure the End Times were upon us. Cupcake’s mad cackling didn’t help that thought any either. With that, I dragged Snowflake down into the Library. Since we’ve got designs on setting up an alchemy lab, I figured it was about time we started to learn about it. I was surprised when he offered to help me do the research. I thought it was just understood that he was the smart one, but apparently he handed the lead to me now. I was honored and floored, but we did well working together. In about twenty minutes I managed to put together what we needed to work out the kind of alchemy that would affect us, not stuff. Stuff will be next, but one step at a time.

    Now I think it’s time to really rest up and coordinate how we’re going to deal with tonight. I’m pretty sure Nightshade has already woken Clyde up from his not so eternal slumber.


    Jasmine’s Diary

    Sunday, July 25, 2010, Morning

    During my meditation just now, I had an insight. Well, really just a confirmation on something I’ve been thinking about for awhile now. I think the Bride and I need to find a way to grasp the Phantasmal Key and learn how to unlock a manifestation of the Oracle. Gwen and Alexia are already quite proficient with the Oracle, but as I’ve become the definitive scout for the krewe, I think it would be useful if I could see past illusions and invisibility. After all, I know how potent one can be when one is unseen, and I don’t want to overlook something hidden when I’m supposed to be preparing the way for my krewe. Certainly in many cases Simon can support me with his Phantasmal Boneyard, but it would be nice to see things on my own, especially for cases when I’m sneaking into places and Simon is not around.

    So I’ve resolved to ask Gwen to help me figure out the Oracle, and to ask Simon for help finding my own Phantasmal Key. The Bride seems pleased, and I think she knows something about the trials of acquiring the Phantasmal Key, something that she thinks will be good for me. She’s remaining surprisingly quiet about it.


    Sunday, July 25, 2010, Morning

    Breakfast was constructive this morning. Natalie was not there, but everyone else attended and we had some delightful crepes with fruit and creme. They reminded me of the ones my mother used to make occasionally.

    We’ve laid plans for our next move against the Decastros. We will eliminate Alonia next. Her only remaining anchor is the locket buried with Denise James, and once we’ve dug it up, I’m going to bind Alonia into the locket with the Fetter’s Binding ceremony. It’s not going to be easy, as we’ll have to keep Alonia at bay for several minutes while I do so. Meanwhile, Denise’s ghost will be a problem, but we plan to have Natalie imprison her temporarily with another ceremony. Once Alonia has been trapped, we can resolve Denise at our leisure.

    I’m worried about Alonia. Doc assures us that he can hold her at bay, but I’m still anxious. She has a potent ability to possess people, and I worry what might happen if she possesses Doc. He is confident that won’t happen, and I think I can trust his confidence.

    We will go to the cemetery to deal with Alonia and Denise tonight.


    Sunday, July 25, 2010, Late Morning

    I’ve just had a very troubling experience, and I need to sort it all out in my head. I understand why the Bride was so eager for me to experience a vision from my subconscious.

    I asked Simon to help me find the Phantasmal Key. He assured me that I was already in tune with the sort of illusory qualities necessary to learn the key, and all that remained was for me to have an experience in which my perceptions were altered by a potent, mind-bending, illusory encounter or hallucination, one in which my understanding of reality is temporarily shifted far enough to provide me with some sort of unique insight into my own subconscious. At least, that’s how Simon explained it, and to me it sounded a bit like the more misguided practices of certain Taoist mystics.

    Simon offered to help by inflicting an illusory reality upon me, one drawn from my own subconscious. We went down to the ritual chamber in the cellars for some privacy, and Simon use his phantasmal powers on me. I experienced a powerful vision which I believe tells me a lot about my own wishes, desires, and frailties, at least in regard to my current romantic prospects. But…he vision has left me feeling extraordinarily guilty, and still confused.

    In my vision, I thought I was alone in a nice, romantic parlour with both Julian and Karl. The entire atmosphere was cozy and sensual, with a subtle yet comforting aroma and soft candlelight. I somehow knew that both Julian and Karl were trying to win my affections. Karl set next to me on a sumptuous sofa and gently placed a book of the most beautiful poetry in my hands. He looked at me with his awkward face and told me he loved me, and that the poetry expressed just a fraction of what he felt. My heart soared, and I was about to respond, when I felt Julian’s hand caress my cheek and then brush a lock of hair over my ear. I turned to him and he flashed a charming smile that made me melt. Before I even realized what was happening we kissed. It was such a luxurious, wonderful kiss.

    Remembering Karl, I turned back to him, and I could see the heartbreak on his face, and that’s when I began to notice that the candles had grown cold on his side of the room, and they flickered not in a gentle breeze, but in a watery current. Despite the cold, or maybe because of it, I felt at home there, yet the primeval fishes that swam by reminded me of Karl’s lineage, and I felt a sense of revulsion, not toward Karl, but toward his family. To temper my guilt, I turned away from him and back to Julian, and doing so was like emerging from the cold depths onto a sunlit beach. He wrapped his arms around me and we kissed and caressed and soon I was laying on the sofa as he slowly, sensually removed my clothing. I remember sensing Karl slowly, sadly walk away, shoulders slumped and spirit broken, but I was far too enamoured of Julian’s affections to care. It has been so long sense I’ve made love and it felt so good to surrender to Julian’s charms, even though, somewhere deep inside, I felt as though somehow it was all wrong.

    And then the vision ended, and I came back to myself with my clothes in disarray and Simon sitting across the room from me, trying desperately not to look at me. I admit I was a bit embarrassed, but I tried to put that aside and focus on what the vision meant. I told Simon briefly what had transpired, but I have a lot to think about now.

    Fortunately, I already talked to Gwen about learning Oracle and told her what I was doing with Simon, and I think she’s booked us a spa appointment for this afternoon. She’s such a wonderful friend. I’m pretty sure she assumed my vision would have to do with Richard, and I think that may have devastated me. Perhaps it’s a good thing that my subconscious decided to focus on Karl and Julian. Maybe it means I’m finally moving on. Then again, maybe that’s one reason I feel so guilty.


    Sunday, July 25, 2010, Afternoon

    Gwen, Alexia and I have returned from a nice afternoon at the spa. The massages and facials were wonderful. And during it all, I talked to them about my vision and what it might mean, which led to a long discussion about what I really wanted in romance and whether Karl or Julian could give me what I need.

    Gwen was a bit confused by my affection for Karl. She seemed to think that Julian was perfect. As she says, I “light up” around him, which I suppose is true. But I worry that my interest is based purely on his charms, and I don’t know how sincere those are. He certainly hasn’t made any effort to ask me out or see me aside from the times we’ve invited him to dinner. I suppose it’s always possible that he doesn’t know I’m interested in him. I do have a tendency to be a bit cold toward people, especially when I’m anxious around them. Gwen didn’t seem to think I’d been cold to Julian at all though. And if that’s the case, I fear Julian simply isn’t interested in me. I suppose he must have many less awkward girls to choose from in Providence. Of course, Gwen and Alexia would hear none of that sort of talk.

    Eventually, I think Gwen began to understand a bit of what I see in Karl. She’s seen me with Julian, but she never saw any of the long and deep conversations I shared with Karl as we discussed poetry and literature. Gwen was impressed to learn that Karl and I regularly send each other books to read. She’s still put off by his appearance, but I told her that I don’t feel it is fair to judge Karl or discount him as a romantic prospect based on his looks. She asked me whether I could see myself making out with Karl, and I confess that I hesitated a bit, which only deepened my confusion and guilt. At the same time, I do think Karl would be very romantic if given the chance, and as Gwen explained to Alexia, the Bride and I are the sorts of girls who need poetry and roses in our romance. I’m certain Julian is good at being romantic too. I just wonder if he would be as sincere as Karl.

    I still don’t know what to think or do. But Gwen and Alexia made me feel better. And Gwen even admitted that even though Karl might not have Julian’s “va-va-voom” (which make Alexia laugh every time we said it), he seemed to be sincere and charming in his own way. But the matter of his family is not such a trivial thing, and Gwen pointed out that I’m not the only one who has concerns about the Fishbornes. Even if I were to accept becoming a part of the family, Gwen doesn’t think the Fishbornes would accept me. She compared it to Romeo and Juliet, only realizing after she said it how much I love that play, and for a moment I think she feared I might throw myself into a doomed romance just because it’s tragic. But I’m far more prudent than that, which I suppose is one reason this whole issue is giving me such difficult time.

    At any rate, Gwen and Alexia are definitely supportive, and it’s good to have friends I can rely on to keep me from doing anything terribly foolish. And besides, I’m probably over thinking all of this. I’m honestly surprised Gwen didn’t chide me about that, but maybe she realizes I need to work this out somehow.

    We returned home after our spa day to find Natalie, Simon, and Doc deeply involved in a conversation of their own. I have no idea what it was about, but they seemed amused that Gwen, Alexia and I had been doing “girl stuff”. Chandra and Tavia apparently went out to play paint ball, so I’m sure Tavia is going to be in no shape to move about later. And Alexia needs help cleaning up the kitchen, which she left in terribly disarray when we left for the spa, so I think I’ll go help her.


    Sunday, July 25, 2010, Late Afternoon

    Gwen, Alexia and I spent the rest of the afternoon (after cleaning up the kitchen) discussing the Oracle more. It seems my regular meditations have prepared me well for opening my eyes to spiritual perception, and I caught on to the concepts very easily. Since Alexia also masters the Phantasmal Key, she was able to help me understand how to unlock the Oracle with Phantasm. I’m still learning, but I think I’m beginning to understand the basics. I spent awhile wandering through the unused wings of Darkhaven Bluffs to practice. The rats watched me, but I don’t think they were upset by my trespass.
    I suppose I should start preparing for tonight.

We also took the body with us to burn at the House. Snowflake took care of returning the grave to a more normal and less robbed state then we left.

Cupcake, who had been in a completely different form during the fight so not necessarily involved, talked her down enough to get her to listen.         Doc’s Log: 1000 Hours, Monday, July 26, 2010

        Sleeping with Nightshade is great but I was a little out of sorts this morning. I slipped out of bed to go meditate about 0730 Hours and I knew it would take a little bit. About an hour later, I was finally centered and got ready for the day.

        I came down for breakfast to find Nightshade awake and anxious. I asked her why she was up, since I did promise to ask the Krewe. She was too nervous though and I thought it would mean more anyway, so she stayed up and asked about baby Nightshade. All it cost was a bit of plasm or a bauble from the mortal world; I paid with the little panda trinket attached to my cell phone.

                                                            We also met a ghost named Janice in the diner. She was clad in a luxurious fur coat. She seemed to know something about the prior krewe of sin-eaters, and Natalie jumped on the opportunity to learn more. However, Janice was only willing to tell us what she knew in exchange for a service, and she gave us several options:

                                                            • We can get her information on her descendent (daughter? granddaughter?), Alicia Walstone, and find out what happened to her fur coat, the one in the mortal world. If we find it, she wants Clarissa, her great granddaughter (our short-tempered psychic) to have it.

                                                            • Alternately, she might simply want to visit the world. She has no anchor there anymore (which implies that her coat may not even exist now).

According to her, if you follows the coast far enough, you will reach a river that leads deeper into the Underworld, or maybe one of the rivers that divides the Underworld (I wasn’t quite clear on the details). This is good news for us as it might make finding the Athenaeum easier when we finally journey there.

                                                            Janice also told us that above the library, if one goes high enough, is the Manor. The manor is a mysterious place. Most never enter it, for it can only be entered by invitation. Those who are invited in are never seen again, though Janice wondered if this wasn’t because they were simply too happy there to deign to return to the lower Underworld. I suspect the truth is rather more unpleasant.

                                                            After spending an hour in the diner, we said our goodbyes to Janice and headed home to find that Doc and Gwen had returned. Tomorrow, we will go to the Ivory Coast. I called Jean a short while ago to tell him I would be unavailable for his training sessions for the next few days because something important has come up and I have to go out of town suddenly. I encouraged him to continue his training regimen without me. I know he probably feels a bit neglected right now, but that doesn’t matter if I can help save his family.
