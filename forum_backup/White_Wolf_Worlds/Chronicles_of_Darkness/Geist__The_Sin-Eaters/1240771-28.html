                                            Originally posted by Yig1015w View Post
                                            Do Geists get Willpower from sleeping?
                                            Bound geists share Willpower with their Sin-Eater. As for Sin-Eaters, per the FAQ in the Player's Guide to the Contagion Chronicle:

                                            Originally posted by Do characters regain Willpower from sleeping?
                                            As described in the Chronicles of Darkness Rulebook, humans regain Willpower when they get a good night’s rest, and so do most monsters.

                                            Not everyone does, though. Complicating that, not every core book says whether a monster does or not, nor draws the line clearly between a given monster type and humans.

                                            The general rule is: “Is sleeping truly restful for this creature? Does it help connect them to themself and affirm their identity, or does it erode it?” Even a sleep filled with nightmares can be inherently fulfilling, allowing a character to see their fears clearly in a way they can’t in the waking world.

                                                            12-27-2021, 08:19 AM
                                                            Originally posted by Newb95 View Post
                                                            Hello everyone, I'm going to join a geist game soon and the corebook so far seems pretty straigthforward compared to the other splats, the only things I don't get is what a geist rank effectively does in game terms and what does it mean when it says that the geist treat its key as influence, does it add a dice bonus or something?
                                                            A Bound geist's Sin-Eater treats their effective Rank as the lower of their geist's Rank and their Synergy, which allows them to treat their touch as a bane against a ghost they outrank by at least 2 Ranks; an Unleashed geist is treated as having Influences in any innate Keys it knows with ratings equal to its Rank, allowing it to Strengthen, Manipulate, and Control instances of those forms of death at the bare minimum, with more powerful geists being able to Create or Mass Create those phenomena as well, as per the rules for ghosts on pages 190-191.
