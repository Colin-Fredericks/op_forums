                        Originally posted by Death is Optional
                        Sin-Eaters present unique challenges to the Unchained. Unlike other supernatural entities, Sin-Eaters’ paranormal concerns rest squarely with the dead. Unless a demon is willing to brave the Underworld, the Bound must be dealt with in human terms. That has its own challenges. With two minds in one body, “troublesome” doesn’t come close to defining how hard it is to pull one over on a Sin-Eater. Their only common goal, as a people, seems to be helping ghosts move on.

                        The ethos of modern Sin-Eaters revolves around unpredictability and celebrating life. Older-generation Bound are generally more serious. Their grim outlook is not simply a matter of age, however. Something runs deep in the bones and plasm of the old guard, something that the Unchained don’t yet understand. The current generation, meanwhile, uses their second chance at life to celebrate it, resulting commonly in a carefree attitude and an irreverent take on the supernatural. The one time all Sin-Eaters are in lockstep is when someone or something interferes with ghosts, or the Underworld they’ve been charged to protect. In those cases, the full extent of a Sin-Eater’s powers are on display; the only recourse is to run or redirect blame onto someone else, ideally a mutual enemy.

                                    Originally posted by Aspel View Post
                                    I'm reading over the Geist crossover section in the Demon Storyteller's Guide. Guest your name isn't on it, but did you have any hand in it? Is anything in it a potential hint of what's to come? Specifically this bit about the difference between modern sin-eaters and the &quot;old guard&quot;.
                                    I offered some feedback on it, but that was written before there was officially a Geist 2e and before I was officially the developer on it. So it's... close-ish in some respects, but it's also very much an outsider's view.

                                    Originally posted by Runcible View Post
                                    Travis, what is the plan for the power set of a Sin-Eater for Geist 2e? Would it be more of a rebalance of the pre-existing set of powers or a new slew of powers all together? Perhaps half and half? I know it's been said that Manifestations will be renamed but how about Keys?
                                    Keys are still in, but the powers are getting a major overhaul. It's still too early for me to want to talk about mechanics, but it's not going to be the same system as 1e. For one thing, trying to design and balance a full set of powers for every Key/Haunt* combo is an incredibly difficult task, and leads to a lot of weird, athematic powers. For another, space is a concern for PoD books. Geist 1e spent 37,000 words in Manifestations and Keys; we have about 25k for the topic. And finally, I'd like Geist to get some actual supplements this time around, and the logistics of trying to add new powers to a grid like that is a nightmare.

                                    So expect a tighter, more thematic power set this time around. We're experimenting with some pretty cool stuff, though comma that I think will make them feel pretty mechanically distinct from the other spots.

                                    * Spoiler. :-)

                                    Travis Stout
                                    Geist 2e Developer

                                            So how do Haunts work if there isn't a full thing? I hate to keep harping on "well what I was doing", but my main ideas were to either use a system like Werewolf's Renown/Gifts system. Are Keys likely to be as limiting (and yet so very random) as they were in 1e? In thinking of how to unofficially redo Geist, I realized that a lot of the Manifestations tended to do things that were, as you said, athematic (and that barely had supporting mechanics to begin with). Another one was to do something similar to the Princess: The Hopeful homebrew and have generic Manifestations--Haunts, in this case--that could then have Keys applied to them for additional effect. Come to think of it, that's sort of how Seeming Contracts work in Changeling. I had to essentially redefine half the keys to make them more reasonable. The overlap between Stygian/Silent and how Stigmatic makes any interactions with ghosts double-easy were of concern, as well as the fact that half the Elemental Keys are "that, but with Water/Air/Fire/Earth instead!" Not to mention the overlap between Caul and Shroud, and the way that so many of both grant Armour and/or Defense, making Sin-eaters impossible to actually hit, even in 1e where Defense is generally meaningless.
                                            I love Geist conceptually, but I think half my posts on the OPP forums are criticizing its actual mechanics... And the themes of things like Krewes. Or the way Krewe merits don't even work with how krewes work. Seriously how do you become a Tier 2 krewe, there is no actual explanation for what happens when you join an existing Krewe despite "your Geist changes and you all have a revelation" being a major aspect of krewes.

                                            Moving on, will we be likely to see anything about the Church of the Black Gate? They're a Claimed/Geist obsessed cult that sees the merger of flesh and ephemera as a state of godliness, and the focus of an SAS (Through The Ebon Gate) and one of the aspects of Geist I've always felt would be good as a major antagonist. Actually, I've always felt in general that with the way Geister can often take on mythic forms that giving Sin-eaters some mythical history would be completely reasonable (and that it's absence is frustrating). I had actually written up some stuff comparing Aztlan with the Underworld, and creating a mythic history where Geister were tied to several aspects of Mayaztec culture, with many of the Gods being Sin-eaters or at least their Geister, and some stuff with Cortez' arrival.
                                            But really now I'm just rambling about my homebrew ideas, which isn't that useful to the thread.
