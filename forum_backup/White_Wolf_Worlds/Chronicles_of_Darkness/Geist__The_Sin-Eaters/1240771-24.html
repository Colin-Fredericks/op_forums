        Using Marionette on yourself means you're pulling on yourself. Not with your physical strength or anything, but it's still shaky. If it was physical, then it would be impossible because you'd literally be unable to have any leverage. Since it works mystically it's kinda impossible to say either way, but both ways are possible.
        Since that line of reasoning isn't conclusive, I think it's better to choose the option that is most thematically appropriate given the context.
        Two examples for what I mean: In Mage this would totally be possible according to the wide utility of the Arcana, but it Changeling it wouldn't because "puppeteering" yourself is not actually puppeteering in a literal/narrative sense even if you apply the same physical forces so that would be excluded from the Contract.
        I don't know Geist that well, but given that the focus of Marionette is largely to manipulate things on a distance I'm definitely leaning towards disallowing use on the self (as well as your clothes when used in this way since you still have the same "leverage" as when attempting it on the self).

                                                        It's pretty clear it's 2 XP per dot, but I agree that it's unreasonably expensive for characters. A five dot Ceremony costs 10 XP! You can also exchange starting Merit dots for Ceremonies, seemingly on a dot for dot basis.
