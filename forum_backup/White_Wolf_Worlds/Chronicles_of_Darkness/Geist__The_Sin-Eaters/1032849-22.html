                    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
                    Interesting...

                    So to break it down:
                    A Haunt is a discrete power, rated 1-5. The base effect applies a Condition/Tilt to a subject or area (Resolution is linked to the source Geist's Ban/Bane). Dots 2-5 offer additional options (and probably help/are part of the activation roll?). More power requires more Plasm, however.
                    Correct. The activation roll for Haunts is Synergy + Haunt (+ Key Attribute, if you use a Key).

                    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
                    A Key is sourced from either the Bound, or from Mementos they possess. They are linked to the 9 Attributes. Activating a Key gives you [Attribute] in "free" Plasm to spend, lets you add [Attribute] to Haunt activation rolls, and gives you Exceptional Success on 3 for thematically resonant circumstances. But... you get that Key's Doom. While under a Doom, you cannot use that particular Key again - but if you have more than one Memento source, you can use different instances.

                    This means that a cautious Sin-Eater could avoid Dooms by not using Keys... if they are okay with weaker activation rolls and spending gobs of Plasm. Keys also do not have to be used with Haunts - they offer Plasm-for-Doom regardless - but still augment them and make their use easier. I think if you wanted to replicate some of the 1E powers, you could, and for ensuring the Haunt is in Theme means you can use the ES-on-3 benefit. Sin-Eaters also seem encouraged to enhance their Attributes more than normal, since they now also boost the Keys' effects.
                    Also correct. Keys don't fundamentally change what Haunts do, but they absolutely influence how they look. The Primeval Rage might manifest as a flock of phantasmal crows that swarm the target pecking and scratching at them, while the Tear-Stained Rage fills their lungs with Plasmic seawater, even if the mechanics of those powers remains "an attack with +(Plasm spent) damage."

                    Originally posted by Vent0 View Post
                    Since it references Mementos-as-Avernian-Gate-Keys, I wonder if mortal Occultists could make use of Keys for their power as well (obviously when also at an Avernian Gate, since unlike Bound, they aren't carrying one with them).

                                                    The first thing that ever drew me to Geist was the concept of mixing and matching a lot of interesting, diverse powers that each seemed to have their own flavors. There was so much individual choice and personality that could go into each character. The next thing I loved about Geist was the joie de vivre and constant paradoxes, not only in Geist culture and abilities but in the tone of the overall game: power and responsibility side by side with celebration - work hard and play harder. I loved the concept of a living soul and a deathly soul bound together, standing between life and death. Like if a vampire's Beast is "dormant" as a mortal and "awakened" by the Embrace, a Sin-Eater's Beast is replaced entirely by their geist and its drives.

                                                    2e so far has some interesting aspects, I suppose. There's clearly a lot of thought put into it and putting the systems in line with the rest of the 2e gamelines, but so much is altered and shifted entirely. It kind of feels like the color and life I loved about 1e are gone. Nonetheless, I'm still curious to see more about the 2e systems and lore. Given all the community interaction, the devs really seem to love the game and the work going into it, which always helps make things great.
