        The game is set in New Haven, Connecticut, most famous for Yale, which is real. Dark Haven Bluffs is a fictional location in New Haven where the Fishborn house (also made up, obviously) stands.

        Most clubs/bars mentioned in the game are real. Gotham Citi is real, Alchemy is real. Lulu's is real. The exception is Murder Ballads, which is not real. The name was taken from a club in Providence I think (which is no longer wherever it was), though I could be misremembering, but the Club itself takes inspiration from a lot of sources, including the classic Succubus Club from Chicago by Night and the standard "Dark Cathedral Nightclub" stuff, such as Confession in Bloodlines (though Confession itself did not inspire it initially as I created it about 3 years before I played Bloodlines the first time). Of course The 27 Club is not real, but somewhat inspired, both IC and OOC, by CBGB. All the parks (like Sleeping Giant) are real. All the Universities (Yale, Albertus Magnus) are real and the dorms mentioned at Albertus Magnus are real. Hopefully less haunted though. The history of how some Dorms were sort of absorbed from old buildings in the area is true. The mentioned lighthouses are real.

        I can't promise how accurate my portrayal of New Haven is. Only one of all 7 of us have been to New Haven (Simon Fishborne's player) and he stopped by a place or two while driving through a few times. One other player has been to Conneticut once for a wedding (Jasmine's player) but did not stop in or drive though New Haven as far as I know, and it was well over a decade before the game regardless. I consulted with another player not in this game (but in Malibu Dream House) who is from Connecticut (but not New Haven) when I was first working on the game, but it was mostly a "does this sound like stupid bullshit to you" conversation. I used This Website when researching the game in 2010 but it looks different now, some clubs added some gone. Also sites for state parks and the universities, and Wikipedia.

        Hope that is helpful

                It’s time to return to our group in New Haven. This session’s journals will be strange, as only Sara will really be describing events that took place in play. Doc’s player had to miss due to a last minute family emergency (everyone is fine now) so his log deals with what Doc was doing during the game time, and Natalie’s journal is a vignette showing events which took place off camera during the session. As a reminder in that, Natalie’s birth name (Allison) is used in the vignette. Anyway, here we go.

                Session CXIII

                Doc’s Log

                Doc’s Log: 1300 Hours, Tuesday, November 2, 2010

                Before I could talk with Nightshade about helping me with driving, the Krewe met for what we might as well call a Working Lunch. All the cards were put on the table so we could figure out the best way to handle things. Bright Eyes still wanted to “go in” as bait to get close Jenny and Mariah. This was thankfully shouted down by more people than just me, but I’m not sure I like the alternative any better. I’m not sure when we found out there would be another party on Friday, but Nightshade and Bright Eyes are going as a couple since Nightshade feels sure she can use her status as a local sex symbol to get in. For what is ostensibly a sex party, that should do it. Rose, Cupcake, and I will be staked out nearby for if and when the SNAFU hits.

                Carol was apparently bringing the ring to Bright Eyes after lunch so she and Cupcake could give it a full object read and occult inspection. I’m pretty sure we could charge $999.95 for that and make a killing, but anyway. Once we know their results, we’ll have a better idea what can be done with Jenny.

                Since I’m not useful in any of this, I’m going to snatch Nightshade away to make sure I know what I’m doing behind the wheel.

                Doc’s Log: 1700 Hours, Tuesday, November 2, 2010

                Driving is harder than it looks. I mean I think I have a pretty good handle on it, but I was surprised at how much you have to constantly consider. I have no idea how Ronin could do the things she did while driving. Nightshade gave me a good lesson on three point turns and parallel parking. Apparently they are very particular about getting that right on the test down in North Carolina. And I have to admit, it took a few tries to get either one right. I’m just glad I didn’t scrape the curb or scratch Nightshade’s wheels. I think the extra height I’ll have in the van will help, but not having a rear view mirror will not. If I have too many problems with it, I’ll see about getting one of those rear view backup monitors. I’m sure Snowflake and I can figure out how that is supposed to work.

                I got the Cliff Notes version of what happened with the ring after Nightshade and I came back around 1600 Hours. I wasn’t too far off from my theory about the Blood Stone rings. The ring didn’t make you love someone, like Jenny hoped. Instead it makes the wearer easier to control. And there was something that I didn’t get all the details on that would allow Mariah to suck away the wearer’s life force. I assume this is to somehow keep her alive or healthy or amused. Something. Either way, they wouldn’t let me smash the rings nor were they ready to try to use sympathetic magic to strike at Mariah.

                They were ready to visit Rose’s dad in Taiwan however. Yeah, that felt like a jump to me too, but I wasn’t there for all the details. Apparently there was blood in the ring Cupcake and Bright Eyes studied. Because we’re dealing with Blood Bathers, this is even more important to get. Rose’s dad had the right kind of jeweler’s tools at his antique shop to get to the blood without ruining the ring. So Nightshade offered to pop her over for a visit. They plan to head over sometime after dinner so that the times line up a little better. Given we aren’t pressed for time (yet), I think Rose is hoping to spend time with her dad while there. Also probably have more of those awesome buns and potstickers without having to share. Maybe I’m just hungry. Lucky for me Cupcake will have dinner ready soon.


                Doc’s Log: 2100 Hours, Tuesday, November 2, 2010

                Nightshade and Rose got off without any problems about an hour ago. With them gone, I caught up with Karl a little bit until Chandra announced that she and Cupcake were going to watch a movie. We knew what that meant, but Bright Eyes didn’t. She and Karen were apparently having their own date night. Karl and I moved out of the TV room to continue our discussion of the effects shifting forms has on hand-to-hand combat. He gave me some examples of how your center of gravity has to shift when your opponents are smaller than you that I would have never considered. About fifteen minutes into our happily uninterrupted conversation, Bright Eyes and Karen walked out of the TV room. I figured the movie must have been interesting if it took Chandra that long. Karl smirked at me but was too classy to comment. I’ve missed getting to hang out with the big guy.

                Eventually I left Karl to a book he was looking forward to reading. I started to work on a few of my katas, but then remembered that I haven’t contacted Sebastian in some time. When I called so early, Sebastian answered quickly and seemed surprised it was me. I apologized for not staying in touch and wondered if he was still up for teaching me more dance steps. I figured why stop now? Sebastian was little reluctant to commit this time though. I asked if I could make it up to him as I thought he was pissed that I just stopped coming to practice. Instead he admitted he was little afraid to stick around so late as there had been an armed robbery down the block from the studio. So I thought if a different reason for us to meet. I asked if Sebastian would like to have coffee and talk about a different proposal. I really hope he doesn’t think I’m asking him out. I want to ask if he’d like to train in martial arts, given how I’ve started getting back into it. Training someone would only help to get me back into the mindset faster. We’ll see what he thinks.


                Doc’s Log: 0230 Hours, Wednesday, November 3, 2010

                I got really lucky when I found Sebastian. I doubt many dance instructors would be nearly as open to my bizarre ideas.

                I met him at the studio just as they were closing up and he took me to a coffee shop many of the dancers frequented. It wasn’t as friendly as Lulu’s but they didn’t kick me out or anything. The robbery had shaken many at the studio because it was one of the other teachers that got robbed. She was okay, but the whole experience left her feeling very vulnerable and violated. Sebastian pointed her out but said she was really not dealing well with strangers right now. I thought was fair. I asked if any of the instructors had ever studied martial arts, since I knew some routines utilized similar movements. He smiled at my question but said he didn’t think so. Then has asked if I wanted to trade. I laughed but that wasn’t my idea. I told him that I would be happy to teach him as it would also help me get back into practice. However I wouldn’t do it instead of paying his fee. Then if he wanted to teach the others when I thought it was safe, he could help them out. I told him that he had to credit me if he worked any of our moves into a routine though. We both laughed and finished our coffee. I walked several of the instructors back to their cars, before they left for the night. Sebastian agreed to meet me again tomorrow night.

                But I was just getting started for the evening. Once the studio instructors had all gone home and the coffee shop closed, I started my patrol. There were only a few people out after 2200 Hours and even less that were obviously sticking around. Sometime after 2300 Hours, a couple of guys that might have been 18 “stumbled” into me but they found they couldn’t pick empty pockets. When I didn’t actually fall from them running into me, I held up a five and asked if that was what they were looking for? I looked back and forth between them as they tried to decide what to do next. Before things turned ugly, a light shined on us. I lowered my hand so it didn’t look like a drug deal. The police asked if everything was alright. I smiled and told the faceless light that we were fine. The boys looked less calm but nodded along with me. I heard the driver say something about that being Doc and they chuckled before wishing us a good night. I turned back to the boys as the light switched off and the cruiser pulled away. We had a very frank discussion about how I would be watching these streets at night and they should probably let their friends know that people in their line of work were going to have it a lot harder. There was another calculated silence. I heard the cruiser turn the corner behind me when the boy on the left decided they could take me. The punch was telegraphed but it would have shattered my nose if I hadn’t stopped it short by catching his fist. When the other boy swung at my open ribs, I threw a left to counter and drove my knuckles into his inner forearm. The force of movement pulled the first boy into the other and they fell into a heap on the curb. I stood over them and repeated that I would be watching these streets. It took a few seconds for them to stop stepping on each up and scramble away. The rest of the evening went smoothly. Tomorrow night should be interesting.


                Doc’s Log: 0930 Hours, Wednesday, November 3, 2010

                Meditation took a little longer than normal this morning. I’m sure it’s because I’m nervous. Hopefully I’ll get my license today.

                Nightshade and Rose got back this morning so they talked about visiting the pawn shop owner over breakfast. I really wanted to go along for that, but I knew Bright Eyes and Nightshade could get things done. Besides, I needed to ask Karl or Tavia to take me to the DMV so I could take my test. Hell maybe even Snowflake, but I don’t know if he’s up yet. Wish me luck.


                Doc’s Log: 1630 Hours, Wednesday, November 3, 2010

                I had to wait a little while before I could get a ride as Tavia had to remove a few things from her car. Which really wasn’t so bad considering the Three hours! Three! To wait in line so I could take my test.

                I passed! But everything is not yet happy. They let me take the written test but said I needed some form to show I had insurance before I could take the driving test. I have no idea why my insurance company didn’t think to tell me that when I added the van before. And given the time, there is no way I can get the form and get back in time for anything to happen. Damn it!

                Doc’s Log: 0330 Hours, Thursday, November 4, 2010

                I stalked around the House for a while trying not to be grumpy at anyone. I did learn that Nightshade only had to sniffle a few times before the pawn shop owner gave up the name of the guy who bought Sarah’s comb. I may stop by at some point to get the name of the kid who stole it in the first place, but that will be for later.

                The rest of the time, I spent trying to stay loose and well hydrated for tonight. I got the studio about 2100 Hours, which is still late but not past the time several people are around. Sebastian had a smaller room prepared and was happy to see me so early. We worked on the basics for about two and half hours. Then I decided to break so he could train me. We worked on a tango until about 0100 Hours. Then I walked him to his car. He laughed and said people really would think we were a couple if I kept doing that. I told him I wasn’t worried about what people thought, but I was worried about keeping people safe. He shook my hand and then went home. I spent another two hours walking the alleys around the studio. Nothing happening tonight so now I’m going to bed and hopefully I’ll have my license tomorrow.


                Doc’s Log: 1030 Hours, Thursday, November 4, 2010

                Meditation went fine today but I’m still nervous. I still haven’t told the Krewe what I’m up to because I want to surprise them with the news. That made breakfast a little harder, but I think we’re just getting ready for tomorrow. Sounds good to me.

                I was going to ask Karl if he could take me to the insurance company and then to the DMV, but when I came down Tavia was already standing with keys in hand. Karl was there too. He told me that Tavia insisted he come with me to make sure everything went smoothly. I had to laugh at that, given I wasn’t really looking to intimidate the DMV. I went back up to get my things and write this

                Here’s to hoping.


                Doc’s Log: 1715 Hours, Thursday, November 4, 2010

                Today was a test of my patience more than my driving skills. I had to fill out so much paperwork at the insurance company. I thought they had already taken care of it.

                An hour and a half later, I had to wait in line at the DMV again. How many damn people drive in New Haven? This time I had my form from the insurance company, all my proof of ID, and the paper from yesterday saying I had passed the written test. That was a problem because the people working today didn’t know they could do that. After another thirty minutes of that, I just told the lady to give me the written test again. Then I had to wait for them to grade it and find someone that could go out with me for the driving test. Karl kept shaking his head at Tavia when another delay came up. That was keeping me focused as I couldn’t let myself rage out if I was afraid she would do something we’d all regret later. Finally, a very unimpressed looking lady came up to me and asked if I was ready. Tavia handled me her keys and wished me luck. The DMV lady blinked at Tavia and Karl for a moment then seemed happy to walk a little faster outside.

                But I did it! I have my license and all the bullshit paperwork complete to make me and van a legally matched pair. I swear it’s like getting married. But I did it! And I think Tavia is as proud of me as my Mom would be. Now I get to share the news.


                Sara’s Letters

                Well, we certainly have a plan now and the more specific we get about it, the less copacetic I get. It is a good plan, don’t get me wrong. And It’s much less dangerous than my bait idea. But with that one, I just have to submit and keep my wits about me to help fate along. This new plan is much more, well, active, I guess? I’m backup! In case a fight breaks out! I’m much better at running away than helping out, though I do have a few tricks that work well with the troupe here. I just have to keep reminding myself that it is highly unlikely to come to a fight and Doc, Jasmine and Alexia will be right outside if it does. Plus, it does mean being the personal guest of a sex symbol/internet personality at a hoity-toity sex party. What’s not to love, aside from rumors of certain ‘guests’ being disposable so that no one can out any of the rich bastards as gay.

                Anyways, the ring Jenny gave Carol was super helpful, especially since Jenny has one just like it, from the cult leader. Alexia’s got a slightly different slant to her psychic abilities and we complement each other nicely. With our powers combined, we found out that Jenny thought the cult leader had placed a love spell on it, using both her blood and Jenny’s, so that Carol would come back to her. Actually, it makes the wearer... suggestible, magically speaking, to the cult leader herself. Also, over time, it would allow the cult leader to use the wearer’s life energy in an incredibly destructive way. Way bad juju.

                What we learned did give us some level of hope of saving Jenny for Carol. She’s not quite as deep in it as we might have thought - she didn’t understand the ritual taking place in front of her and didn’t seem to be in any sort of romantic relationship with the cult leader, much less in her confidence. Natalie and I have a goal for the aforementioned sex party on Friday - to get Jenny’s copy of the ring back and hopefully remove the mystical influence the cult leader has over her. Whether or not that will truly redeem her is still questionable, as the picture Carol’s painted of her is not very nice, despite her assuring us that Jenny’s not that bad. But ‘not very nice’ and ‘evil blood magic’ aren’t really the same thing, and if it’s just back to ‘not very nice’ that’s Carol’s lookout.

                So we’ve had ourselves a bit of downtime, since we don’t want to do anything on that case to scare folks off before Friday. Jasmine and Natalie took a day trip to visit Jasmine’s dad. He runs an antique shop and had the kind of gem cutting equipment we needed to really deal with the enspelled rings. So Karen and I had something like a date night. It started out being a double with Alexia and Chandra, watching a movie at the house. Apparently, to those two, “watching a movie” means something slightly different, so Karen and I wandered off. I wound up telling stories about life with the old troupe until we “watched a movie” ourselves.

                We also got some foot work done on the heirloom comb case while we were waiting, once Natalie and Jasmine were back. The pawn shop guy gave us a name for the guy who bought it from him, saying he was a collector of some kind, and a regular there. It nearly took Natalie bursting into tears at the thought of losing ‘the comb her gran had used when she was a young girl’, but he gave in after only a sniffle or two.

                Speaking of Natalie’s ‘gran’, she’s reconciled at least somewhat with her mom, which is awesome. I’ve felt a little bad here as she works through it, cause even though Natalie’s probably the country person I understand the most, of all I’ve met, I can’t really wrap my head around her family situation. I mean, intellectually, sure, I know that my family situation is not the norm or anything, but in my heart, it is difficult to consider. Some of the stuff Natalie’s figured out recently, along with what she’s found out talking to her mom, makes things make more sense at least. For example, Natalie’s mom is touched, a little, like the rest of us, and she had thought she’d seen Natalie die, on one of her close calls. That explains why her mom was so against Natalie’s daughter coming to visit - she thought Natalie had to be an impostor. Also, it explains why she never checked up on her. Dad would have moved heaven and earth to find me, no matter how we’d left things, but if he thought I was dead, well, that would have been different. But anyway, we might get to meet Natalie’s mom, which will be awesome. From what Natalie said, her mom’s a lot like her, more even that Natalie realized. We just have to deal with an evil blood cult first. Yay?

                                                            Alexia isn’t quite as inviting, but she still seems nice, just cautious. I don’t think she likes me very much, but I can’t blame her. I doubt she cares for the whole killing thing. I gather the krewe hasn’t had a Reaper before, and I’m not sure Alexia’s comfortable with what I do, even when it’s for the right reasons. Still, that could be a good thing. If I’m right about her, then she could be exactly the kind of person I need to help me regain my perspective and keep the Huntress at bay.
