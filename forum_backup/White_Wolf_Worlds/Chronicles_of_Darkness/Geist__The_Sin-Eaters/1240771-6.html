                                        Originally posted by Doryna View Post
                                        Stupid question time: Where do Touchstones go on the character sheet? Are they supposed to be under Remembrance Traits?

                                        Edit: To be honest, I'm still not a 100% sure what a Touchstone is. I'm guessing it's supposed to be akin to a Passion or Fetter in Wraith?
                                        A Sin-Eater's Touchstones are persons, places, or things which are the focus of their Burden, Remembrance, and Cause (as Synergy increases; at starting Synergy 1 you only have a Burden Touchstone). It's the subject of a loose thread that will take some narrative effort to finally tie up, an outstanding, longer-term conflict you need to resolve to lay your dying Burden to rest, bring your geist peace, or satisfy yourself that you've done something to correct whatever it is you see as wrong with the cycle of death. Something you find yourself in repeated contact with, one of your personal plot hooks important to you.

                                        If you were murdered unjustly, your Burden Touchstone might be the criminal conspiracy responsible for it. If your geist's memories are the ashes and smoke of a great historical fire, your Remembrance Touchstone might be the site where it all burned down, or the neighborhood living in the wake of that destruction. If you find yourself attached to the cause of preserving the community of the dead so that they have the power to forge their own destiny even in the Underworld, your Cause Touchstone might be a river city or dead dominion where ghosts have banded together and you lend your aid to help keep them afloat.

                                        Touchstones are a mechanical thing in several Chronicles of Darkness 2e games, and Geist uses them unconventionally and a little obliquely. They kind of overlap with aspirations, in that each one is an ongoing conflict that you're working towards some kind of satisfying resolution – bringing an end to the conspiracy and the violence, healing the longstanding wounds of the neighborhood, making a dominion stable and self-sufficient. They're simpler in other games, where they tend to mostly represent relationships with particular people you're attached to, whose emotional support keeps you grounded.
