                                                        Originally posted by Elythia View Post
                                                        1) a) What Rank does a Ghost need to use “Host Jump” -without help- to “claim“ a victim?
                                                        2, with the caveat that most ghosts liable to run a Possess/Host Jump combination are more likely to be geists or Reapers, since Claim requires both Possess and Fetter, ghosts only get so many Manifestation effects for free, and every aspect of this set of effects is very expensive Essence-wise (which means being immune to Essence bleed helps a lot).
                                                        b) What does he need to do? (Is there more than one way?)
                                                        With Plasm or Influence 3, a ghost can temporarily create the Open Condition on a subject without the underlying Anchor Condition; with Influence 3 or repeated Possession, a ghost can step the Open Condition up to Controlled, whereupon the subject can be Claimed, which halts the countdown on any of the supporting Conditions until/unless Claimed is resolved. As mentioned, this is an expensive process.

                                                        A geist with a stock of corpses dead of its innate Key effectively has several no-strings-attached Anchors in the same way that most ghosts treat their own remains as an Anchor, which in turn allows it to Strengthen that facet of death into an Open Condition that can be leveraged for Manifestations like normal.

                                                        2) Why can’t a Ghost use “Influence - Create” to make a person an anchor? (Page in the book?)
                                                        They can, but the problem is that the vast majority of the ways a ghost can reach the Rank necessary to have a Create Influence of any kind don't allow them to assign dots to Influence: Anchors.

                                                        Practically every geist of a Rank higher than 3 can use the Influence of their innate Key to mark a person as an "Anchor," but the way that works is through geists being "Anchored" to a form of death rather than anything relating to them on a personal level. Meanwhile, Reapers have the stats of a Rank 2 ghost when they haven't got the mask on, and putting the mask on adds dots in Influences they probably don't already have, which is unlikely to put them at Influence 4 in anything.

                                                        Influence: Anchors is mostly important to low-Rank ghosts because it allows them to affect all their Anchors with that Influence even if they aren't the same type of thing otherwise. It's no more essential to the Manifestation system than it would be for angels to have Influence: Infrastructure.

                                                        3) Why doesn’t a GhostGeist need an anchor, if it’s not the Rank? (They don’t even need anchors without the Bargain.)
                                                        Because they drank from the Rivers and got Broken in such a fashion that they specifically don't have their one-to-three-average personal Anchors anymore — the narrative role their Anchors played for them is now handled by their Remembrances and the metaphysical role their Anchors played for them is now handled by their innate Keys.

                                                        Geists on their own and Reapers working to plan are ludonarratively set up to spread death in the living world, albeit in different ways; their freedom to leave the Underworld and safely venture beyond any Anchors they have is there in part to allow Reapers to be subtle threats and in part to allow geists to not be hemmed in geographically by their Keys the way ghosts are by their Anchors.

                                                        4) What would you recommend for the story, if you don’t want to change the personality of the Ghost too much?
                                                        (He needs to be able to leave the Underworld, have or make time to find a host, and be able to do a „Host Jump“ and „Claim“ his victims.)
                                                        Make him a geist whose innate Key is the Key of Blood (or whatever other Key is appropriate to his methods) and give him a Remembrance relating to the demon; it's already textual that necromancers can jump straight to geisthood when they die and "finish what I started" is a pretty definite goal to work towards.

                                                        Why are Claim and Host Jump necessary for this concept?

                                                            Originally posted by Elythia View Post
                                                            3) Why doesn’t a [geist] need an anchor, if it’s not the Rank? (They don’t even need anchors without the Bargain.)
                                                            Originally posted by Satchel View Post
                                                            Because they drank from the Rivers and got Broken in such a fashion that they specifically don't have their one-to-three-average personal Anchors anymore — the narrative role their Anchors played for them is now handled by their Remembrances and the metaphysical role their Anchors played for them is now handled by their innate Keys.

                                                            Geists on their own and Reapers working to plan are ludonarratively set up to spread death in the living world, albeit in different ways; their freedom to leave the Underworld and safely venture beyond any Anchors they have is there in part to allow Reapers to be subtle threats and in part to allow geists to not be hemmed in geographically by their Keys the way ghosts are by their Anchors.
                                                            Here we have a potentially jarring gameplay and story dissonance. Narratively speaking a ghost becomes a Geist by drinking from one of the Rivers of the Underworld until they are changed and gain a Rank. Mechanically speaking a ghost becomes a Geist by reaching Rank 3.
                                                            The dissonance comes from the possibility for ghosts to drink from a River without reaching Rank 3 (and thus not becoming a Geist), as well for ghosts to reach Rank 3 through means other than drinking from a River (thus becoming Geister despite not having gone through the process that is supposed to turn them into Geister).

                                                            Narratively speaking, the need for Anchors doesn't necessarily correspond to the ghost's Rank, but mechanically speaking it definitely is tied because Rank is used to determine if a ghost is a Geist or not.
