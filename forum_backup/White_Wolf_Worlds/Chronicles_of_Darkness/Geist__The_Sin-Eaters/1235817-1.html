                        Now that I am properly updated with the Manuscript, I have revised a few of my ghosts!

                        The Drowned Woman
                        She remembers drowning. It happened so long ago that she has forgotten her name or exactly how she ended up on that ship. But she remembers the storm; months too early in the season and with rain like blades and waves like the fists of an angry god. She remembers the crack of the vessel being torn in half and the cold of the water filling her lungs. She haunted that stretch of coast for decades –tied to a locket given to her by someone she loved (perhaps a husband or fiancé). But eventually that was worn away by time and tide as well. In life she was a willful woman, and she has continued on in the underworld, becoming a traveler and guide well versed in the many rivers, lakes, and seas that exist within the underworlds winding caverns.
                        The Drowned Woman appears as a slender woman with bright red hair that stands out against her deathly pale skin and has eyes the color of seawater. Her age is indeterminate, seeming middle aged or youthful by turns. She is dressed in a simple white gown and is perpetually drenched in water as if she just emerged from the waves. She has a lovely voice and sings sea shanties with gusto and skill.

                        Rank 3[Geist]
                        Power 8 Finesse 8, Resistance 6, Willpower 10, Essence 20
                        Corpus 11, Size 5, Initiative 14, Defense 8, Speed 21
                        Virtue Kind, Vice Headstrong
                        Aspiration: To help those that travel by waterway
                        Remembrance: Expression
                        Innate Key: Key of Deep Waters
                        Ban: Cannot board a vessel without offering payment to the Captain Bane: Tokens of Romantic Affection
                        Manifestation: Discorporate, Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                        Numina: Banshee Wail*, Emotional Aura (Loss), Pathfinder, Seek, Telekinesis
                        Numina – Banshee Wail: The entity creates an earsplitting scream that deafens anyone nearby. Anyone within 20 yards of the entity must succeed on a Resolve + Stamina roll – the number of successes gained or suffer the Stunned and Deafened Tilt. The Numen costs one Essence to activate.

                        The Lost Ronin
                        The Lost Ronin lived during the waning days of Tokugawa era Japan, and was a Ronin Samurai known for his love of drinking and gambling, and for his entitled attitude towards the peasantry (or anyone else he considered beneath him). After a particularly ugly incident at a gambling hall, he was ambushed by a group of assailants while too drunk to defend himself, as was murdered. His corpse was buried deep in a field and the murderers never spoke of the incident again. His angry spirit lingers on, enraged that he was slain by peasants while drunk, and denied a death befitting a Samurai. Quenching his thirst in the Rivers of Meido have made the Ronin strong, and he longs for re-entry to the world of the living, for only there can he earn a true, worthy, death.
                        The Lost Ronin has forgotten many details about his mortal life and his appearance has taken on an indistinct quality – a straw hat that obscures his face and a tattered dark blue kimono are the most striking elements, all other details are a water-color blur.

                        Rank 3[Geist]
                        Power 8 Finesse 8, Resistance 8, Willpower 10, Essence 20
                        Corpus 13, Size 5, Initiative 16, Defense 8, Speed 21
                        Virtue Proud, Vice Entitled
                        Aspiration: to be put to rest in a manner befitting a samurai
                        Remembrance: Weaponry
                        Innate Key: Key of Blood
                        Ban: Cannot refuse a challenge to a Duel Bane: A katana blade wielded by a Samurai (or member of a similar warrior elite)
                        Manifestation: Avernian Gateway, Fetter, Materialize, Twilight Form
                        Numina: Awe, Phantom Arsenal (Sword)*, Regenerate, Seek, Speed, Telekinesis
                        Numina – Phantom Arsenal: The entity spends 1 Essence point to manifest blades, claws, fangs, hooks or other appropriate weaponry. The modification acts as a +1L weapon. By spending an additional point of essence, this weapon gains the “9-Again” quality.

                                                    Re: Dullahan

                                                    I have the done Dullahan as a Horror and a Hobgoblin, so here is the Reaper version. I also added another Geist just for fun.
                                                    The Dullahan
                                                    The Reaper known as “The Dullahan” is a nightmarish vision of a headless horseman clad in black riding leathers and wrapped in a dark cloak. A relentless hunter, once its prey is chosen, few things can deter it. Not satisfied with just the dead, it is known to strike at the living fated to die, dragging their their freshly manifested ghosts back to the Underworld. Those wearing the Black Mantle of the Dullahan appear as a tall man (or woman) with lean, athletic frames, and carry their severed heads with them or keep them nearby. The Dullahan is always accompanied by a spectral mount or vehicle of some sort, typically a midnight black stallion, but occasionally they appear with horse drawn coach, motorcycle or car. As The Dullahan, the Reaper carries a sword and a whip. Victims are ensnared by the whip and engulf into the folds of the Black Mantle. Where the captured ghosts are taken is a mystery to even other Reapers, but none are ever seen again. The current Dullahan is a ghost named Christoph, who in life was a notoriously blood thirsty Hessian mercenary. These days he rarely sheds his Deathmask and spends nearly every moment as The Dullahan.

                                                    Rank 2/4 [Reaper]
                                                    Power 7/12 Finesse 3/9, Resistance 4/12, Willpower 7/10, Essence 15/25
                                                    Corpus 9/17, Size 5, Initiative 7/21, Defense 3/9, Speed 15/26
                                                    Virtue Determined, Vice Ominous
                                                    Aspiration: To herald impending death
                                                    Influence: Anchor (Skull) 2, Doom 2
                                                    Ban (Christoph): Cannot approach anyone carrying his mortal remain Bane (Christoph): Gold
                                                    Ban (Dullahan): Cannot enter a House of Worship Bane (Dullahan): Religious icons made of Gold
                                                    Manifestation: Avernian Gate, Discorporate, Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                                                    Numina: Awe, Descend, Engulf, Pathfinder, Phantom Arsenal**, Regeneration, Snare**, Speed, Telekinesis

                                                    Numina- Snare: As per the Dread Power (CofD, pg. 147)
                                                    Numina – Phantom Arsenal: The entity spends 1 Essence point to manifest blades, claws, fangs, hooks or other appropriate weaponry. The modification acts as a +1L weapon. By spending an additional point of essence, this weapon gains the “9-Again” quality.
                                                    The Bleak Cardinal
                                                    The Geist named the Bleak Cardinal can recall only fragments of its mortal life. Once, it was a man of the cloth and respected figure within his community. But he had long ago lost his faith in both man and God and every confession taken only increased his despair. Cast into Purgatory upon his death, he sought redemption, eventually enduring a second baptism in the waters of the Styx. The Bleak Cardinal now seeks redemption and salvation, hoping to help lost souls move beyond purgatory and earn their final rest.
                                                    The Bleak Cardinal appears as a bearded, skeletal corpse, dressed in red vestments of a catholic Cardinal His eyes blaze with a bright light and he speak in a quite, whispering voice with an educated eloquent manner of speaking.

                                                    Rank 3[Geist]
                                                    Power 9 Finesse 8, Resistance 7, Willpower 10, Essence 20
                                                    Corpus 12, Size 5, Initiative 15, Defense 8, Speed 22
                                                    Virtue Compassion, Vice Doubt
                                                    Aspiration: To Regain his Faith
                                                    Remembrance: Persuasion
                                                    Innate Key: Key of Cold Wind
                                                    Ban: Cannot harm members of the Clergy Bane: Communion Wine
                                                    Manifestation: Bargain, Discorporate., Image, Twilight Form
                                                    Numina: Blast, Drain, Emotional Aura, Rapture, Sign, Telekinesis
