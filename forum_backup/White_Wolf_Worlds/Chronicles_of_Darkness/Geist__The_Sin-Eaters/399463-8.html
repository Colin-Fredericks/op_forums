You want to make sure the dead and the living stay seperate? Gatekeepers. Pilgrims, Advocates, Necromancers and Bonepickers.

                                    The game isn't about hedonism as it is about enjoying what life has to offer. What I love about Geist is just that - there's no angst demanded from the players for our characters having died, nor does it say they have to be at odds with the voice in their heads 24/7. If we want to be, go for it, but if we want voices that are benign, if odd, then go for it.
