        Not all that die create a ghost, Just so that I know that I have this correct let me lay this out. A person dies and they become a ghost. The Geist makes a Bargain with the Body of the dead, How recently dead, moment, Hours, Days, Weeks? How long does it take a rank 2 ghost to become a rank 3 Geist once in the Underworld.         A person dies and they have no anchors then they immediately go to the underworld, become a Geist upon arrival and then make a deal with themselves to return a Sin-Eater. Their Ghost become Geist makes a deal with the Their dead body. The Bargain is Made and the Geist is no easier on his ex-body then he would be on a different body. Just a hypothetical and a possible character concept.
