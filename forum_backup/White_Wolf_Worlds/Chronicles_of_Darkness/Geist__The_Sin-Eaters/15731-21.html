    The battle with the Bale Hound ends, and does the twisting of the Krewe’s Taboos. Nuff said.

    Session CLIII

    Sara’s Letters

    When Sara heard Natalie approach, she looked at her watch and realized it had been over an hour since her wife had come by to let her know the others were back safely from the underworld. The books on the table hadn’t changed since then. Neither had the one in front of her. Or the page for that matter. She looked up at Natalie. The strawberry scented woman smiled at her and nodded, letting her know it was time. Sara wrapped her arms around herself and took a deep breath, preparing to face the others, before she stood and followed.

    The others were already there, waiting with their drink offerings. Sara added her whiskey to Natalie’s Cognac, Alexia’s red wine, Alice’s Corona and Doc’s laudanum. She joined the circle, avoiding Alice’s eyes and sitting with Natalie between her and the redhead. In the center was a glass painted with a collage of the krewe’s faces, placed so the light streamed through it. Natalie picked up a chalice with the drink offerings poured into it and pricked her finger with a silver knife, letting a drop of blood fall into the chalice before passing it to Alice.

    “Efforts have been made to drive us apart and they have failed...”

    Natalie’s speech was impassioned and stirring as the chalice and knife made their way around the circle. When it got to Sara, she cut deep, curling the blade carefully around the ball of her thumb. She let a few drops fall into the chalice before cupping her hand and passing the chalice along so she could put pressure on her wound.

    Sara looked down at her hand as the chalice was passed around again, this time with each krewe member taking a sip. When it got back to her, Natalie concluded the ritual. She laid a hand on Sara’s shoulder. She smiled and squeezed Natalie’s hand before leaving to wait in the entrance hall while Natalie gave the blessing of the krewe to Alexia and Alice.

    Tatum found her there and approached cautiously, holding out a silver knife hilt first. Sara closed her hand around her cut, holding it out of sight.

    “I got a text from Karen. She wants you to stab her - not Karen-her, but the bad guy-her.”

    “Thanks love.”

    “You can’t die, right? You’re going to come back safe?”

    Sara nodded and stood up, moving to kiss Tatum on the forehead. Tatum smiled back and handed over the knife.
    ----------------------------------------------------------
    The ride was long. Doc and Alice drove, and Sara was in Doc’s van. She tended to her wound, stopping the bleeding and irritating the edges before covering it in plastic. It took her most of the ride, since her thoughts kept wandering and her eyes kept straying towards Angelique, or Alice’s jeep when it came into view.

    They grouped up once they got there, some ways away from the ritual site. The werewolves were peering intently in that direction. Angelique made a worried noise. When the others looked at her, it was Deirdre who explained.

    “There’s a lot of spirits over there. 17, it looks like? Passion spirits - we’re going to need to do something about them or they will be messing with our heads the whole time.”

    Sara glanced over at Alice, and back down. She ran a hand over her face and wrapped her arms around herself again. Deirdre continued.

    “I can make a, well, a ‘scary noise’ and make probably about half of them run off. We’ll probably have to deal with them later, but it will keep us from being swarmed.”

    “No, no, that could work. But we set an ambush for the ones you scare away and end them before we circle around for the rest.”

    Alice punctuated her plan with a brandish of her claws. Sara possessed her geist, revealing 17 attractive looking spirits. The group circled around the spirits to get into position, following Alice’s direction to set up for an ambush.

    The krewe and its werewolf allies tore through the spirits, but not before Sara felt their influence on her emotions. She was hit with a crushing wave of guilt. With the absolute knowledge that what she had done to Alice was unforgivable and had ruined everything. She knew there was no way she could make up for that breach of trust.

    “A blood sacrifice can wash away any transgression. You know what will bring forgiveness.”

    The Sacrifice’s words rang in her head. Anger washed over Sara and she snarled, swiping at the air. The wild blow took out Alice’s target and she glared at Sara, cowing her. Alice and Eris finished off the last of the fleeing spirits and when Deirdre rejoined them, the group made short work of the remaining ones, with twenty minutes left until sunset. Sara stopped to take a breath as a flood of hope swept through her. She sat on a window ledge, pulling her knees up to her chest. Others followed suit, except Alice who paced, only stopping to check on the others.

    She stopped in front of Sara, her mask pushed up to her forehead, her face hard and feral.

    “And you?”

    Sara pulled back, looking up at her.

    “Are you ok?”

    Sara paused, the hope of forgiveness prompting honestly.

    “No. But, we will be. Right?”

    Alice’s expression softened and she returned Sara’s pleading smile before she turned away to resume pacing. Sara gripped Karen’s silver knife tightly, watching Angelique prepare the ritual. Deirdre watched too, suspiciously, muttering.

    “Do we even know what the ritual she’s setting up does?”

    “It forces open doors.”

    Sara answered first, commenting on the various aspects she saw. Deirdre didn’t like it, but didn’t move to stop Angelique either. Once she was done, the Bone Shadow turned to the group.

    “She’s a mystic, like me. I think sending her into Kuruth should be a priority.”

    “You want us to try and force a solo enemy werewolf into Kuruth?”

    “I’m not saying it won’t be dangerous, but I do think it will be less dangerous. I would be.”

    Deirdre glared at Angelique but didn’t contradict her. After a moment she stopped, looking at the ritual site. Noriko looked up from getting Natalie a blanket as Deirdre and Eris both shifted.

    “She peeked.”

    Angelique completed the ritual, tearing open a door made of bright silver light. Natalie stood up to move in and Sara felt the hope she’d had fade away. Her resolve hardened and she moved quickly to the door, ignoring her geist pleasure. She met Alice there.

    “We need to enrage her. My silver will do that where you claws won’t.”

    “It’s not safe.”

    “I’m very hard to hit.”

    “Well, I’m very hard to hurt.”

    While the two argued, Deirdre pushed past them. Alice took a deep breath and a step back, letting Sara go before her.

    Inside the pocket realm, all Sara found was a pair of spirits. Deirdre tore into one, but Sara waited for Bright Shadows to show herself. Alice got in and saw through her mystical invisibility first however, and Sara followed her lead.

    The silver enraged the Balehound and she shifted into Gauru form, abandoning her invisibility. While Eris and Noriko finished off the accompanying spirits, Bright Shadows lunged at Alice. Sara cried out and moved towards her, but the wound bled nothing but smoke and Alice looked unphased

    Looking resolute, Angelique drew a silver knife and thrust it into her own stomach. A red line flared between her and Bright Shadows, and a mirroring wound appeared on the Balehound. Alexia capitalized on her distraction by slamming a pair of fists into the side of Bright Shadow’s head, her silver knuckles burning into the werewolf’s skin. The Balehound went down.

    Noriko and Sara both converged on Angelique. The werecat wrestled the knife from her while Sara laid a hand on her shoulder. Angelique’s eyes flashed with rage but she choked it down, turning to leave. Alice followed, after digging her claws into the body and tossing it out the door. Alexia encouraged them to continue, to move to the cars, eventually dragging the body there herself since Alice was ignoring her. Sara faded back as Alice approached her and Angelique. Alice ignored her too.

    When Angelique said the effects would end after they slept, Sara winced away and moved towards the car. She hadn’t really expected the Balehound’s influence to end immediately upon her death, but it seemed like one more hope dashed away. The others joined Sara and Alexia at their respective cars eventually and the krewe, victorious, drove home.
    ----------------------------------------------------
    Sara walked quietly into the house, still clutching the silver knife. She watched Natalie go upstairs, announcing that only her wives and Tatum were welcome to join her. She watched Alexia and Tavia go on at length about the people oven to a disturbed Alice before they too left, with the body. Then she followed her wife, wanting to ask her for another moment of hope, so she could believe that Alice and Angelique would forgive her, but settling for a hot shower.

            The storm has passed, but our heroes must recover. Let’s see how that whole healing thing goes. Alice’s player had to miss.

            Session CLIV

            Sara’s Letters

            Sara rolled over, a pleasantly drifty smile on her face. When all she found were warm blankets, she frowned a little, waking up the rest of the way. As she sat up and looked around, she found Karen and relaxed. A hopeful smile spread over her face. Karen returned it.

            The younger woman sat down on the bed and Sara leaned in for a kiss. As they pulled apart, Karen held her gaze, a serious look on her face.

            “I’m going to be violently purging Natalie’s guilt. Am I going to need to do the same for you?”

            “I plan on doing that the old fashioned way, by apologizing.”

            Sara looked at Karen with concern in her eyes and laid a hand lightly on her neck. Karen raised an eyebrow and quirked her mouth wryly.

            “You’ll let me know if you need anything, after you help Natalie? Or during.”

            “I will. I might just drink a lot when done.”

            Tumbling off the bed and to her feet, Sara gave Karen a wink and a hand up with another kiss and a reminder to call on her if she was need. Sara nuzzled her lover’s neck, eventually letting her wander off to check on Natalie while Sara herself headed to the shower.

            ---------------------------------------------------------------------

            Breakfast had already gotten started when Sara got down to the dining room. She walked in with her shoulders squared but after one glance around, they slumped slightly and she looked both relieved and concerned. Natalie looked up at her, as Karen stole the strawberry off her place, and passed Sara the note Alice had left before slipping out earlier that morning.

            'Eris and I are spending some time with her pack. Let us know if you need us.'

            Sara thanked her wife, hiding a grin as she noticed Alexia sneaking Natalie a strawberry under the table. As she sat down, a strong smell of alcohol washed over her. She looked down the table, her eyes landing on Doc From the smell, he’d tried getting past a Sin-eater’s high stamina by drinking a few bottle of whiskey in the last few hours. He was eating mechanically, staring dead eyed straight ahead.

            Her brow furrowed with worry, but she didn’t want to push him in front of everyone. Instead, she turned to Angelique, surprisingly dressed for a New England winter day, and asked about her research into the Balehound’s ritual. She seemed almost guilty when she admitted she hadn’t been able to concentrate much, and had gone straight to bed last night. Sara nodded understandingly and offered to help.

            Doc looked up at the mention of the ritual, the first movement not involving a fork going from plate to mouth. He offered to help with a rough voice.

            “It would be good to have someone who knows the library. But... only if you would like. If you need to get some sleep...”

            “I slept. I’m g...”

            Doc trailed off.

            ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

            Angelique knew right where to start back up, moving directly there. Doc set up his bottle of whiskey, along with a tumbler on the table nearby. Sara pointedly put a water bottle near the tumbler as well. In between drinks and attempts to discourage the same, Doc and Sara helped Angelique figure out where in the restricted section she needed next.

            Angelique came back from a run on the stacks with a heavy book in her hands.

            “Doc, you shouldn’t drink so much.”

            He blinked a few times.

            “What are we looking at?”

            “Names Between Worlds. Do either of you know what kind of blood we need in the receptacle to make it open? In this house, I hope not virgin.”

            Sara grinned at Angelique as Doc looked it over and identified it as needing Fishbourne blood. When he went to go get Tavia, Sara seized her change, setting her book down and taking a deep breath.

            “I need to apologize Angelique.”

            Angelique looked up from her reading, raising an eyebrow as Sara continued.

            “I didn’t resist the Balehound’s influence like I should have. I... I’m afraid I may have pushed you too hard into something... well, something you don’t want.”

            “You didn’t push me into anything. We had a good time together - let’s not make it into an unpleasant memory.”

            Sara gave her a wide smile and looked back down at her book, waiting for Doc.

            ---------------------------------------------

            Doc and Tavia were back quickly and Tavia was happy to help. She warned Angelique not to listen to anything the book said, and to avoid the chapter on Abyssal subjects, but otherwise merely observed.

            It was almost dinnertime when Doc’s bottle ran empty. Sara moved to intercept him as he left to get a new one, taking his hand. He paled, and she stepped back, apology on her face. She didn’t move out of the way though and after a short debate, convinced him to go with her looking for food instead.

            They found Karen fist, staring at her own bottle of scotch. She gave Sara a weak smile.

            “I have my limits on what I like doing to people.”

            “You and me both.”

            Doc sat down heavily as he spoke and resumed the blank stare he had on at breakfast. Sara took a seat next to her wife, laying a hand on hers and offering to trade charges, if Natalie still needed more looking after the next day. When not even Karen’s joking rejoinder about babysitting got a rise out of Doc, Sara raised an eyebrow.

            “So, what’s up Doc?”

            He made a face.

            “No really, what’s going on? This isn’t like you.”

            There was a long pause. Doc looked a little green. Sara let it stretch, waiting until he broke, at least a little. He did, with a deep shuddering breath.

            “I... I did some bad things. I think I was affected by Bright Shadows.”

            “We all were.”

            “Even me.”

            Sara squeezed Karen’s hand as she waited for Doc to continue. He looked like he was fighting being sick, but managed to choke it back.

            “I slept with two women I had no intention of being with. It would have been more but... I know I wouldn’t get anywhere with you, Karen, and, well, I never made it back to the house.”

            Sara smirked at him as he glanced up at her, then away.

            “That might have gone somewhere. Yesterday.”

            “Behind Noriko’s back.”

            “Oh. Yeah. Not so much that.”

            Doc stared at the table, his face blank. The two women eventually got through to him the extent of Bright Shadow’s effect. Largely by confessing what they had done - Sara raised a mock-envious eyebrow at Karen’s confession that she’d slept with Natalie’s ‘rival’, Moiré - and the fact that they’d been forgiven, at least by those around to do so. Sara suggested he apologize and get it off his chest before it festered any more than it already had.

            “I guess this serves as an apology, to you two.”

            “Of course you’re forgiven. I am ridiculously attractive after all.”

            Karen’s casual shrug to go along with her claim got an actual smile from Doc. Their conversation was interrupted by Tatum and Noriko wandering in, Tatum gloating over her win on Rainbow Road. Sara eyed her, considering that Tatum had never informed the rest of them that Karen hadn’t been in the townhouse like Karen had just confessed. Doc paled at the sight of Noriko, and shrank down into his seat.

            Sara was not about to let that stand and tapped his elbow until he looked at her, at which point she looked at Noriko significantly. After an awkward exchange of hellos, Doc did ask her to talk and they left the room for privacy.

            -------------------------------

            Tatum had returned from checking on Natalie, and Sara had almost finished giving Karen a neck rub when Doc and Noriko returned, hand in hand. Sara beamed at the pair of them. Chandra, Alexia and Catherine were close behind, with Catherine in Chandra’s clothes. Seeing all of us there, Alexia looked pleadingly at Tatum then dived into the kitchen, Tatum following behind. Karen watched them go, a dubious look on her face.

            “When did she learn to cook?”

            “I think...”

            Sara stopped when Doc shushed her, keeping Tatum’s mid morning Youtube tutorials secret, for now. She kissed Karen on the top of her head and sat down, smiling mysteriously. Conversation stayed light until Alexia and Tatum came back from the kitchen with burgers.

            Over dinner, Noriko asked Angelique about her research. What she and Tavia had found didn’t give the Bone Shadow much hope for reverse-engineering anything, so they discussed brute forcing their way through it. After some cajoling from Sara, she admitted she was from a suburb of Cincinnati, Ohio. She also agreed to let Catherine sift through her mind, the next time the krewe visited the Underworld, to maximize the peacefulness of the environment.

            Natalie came down eventually, a touch paler than usual and without her makeup. She agreed to let the other krewe know we were no longer infected, at Alexia’s urging, since she and Alice had run across them in the Underworld. Sara kept a worried eye on her strawberry smelling wife over dinner, but she perked up as the evening went on and by the time dinner was over, everyone was happy with Tavia’s suggestion of a game night.

            Everyone except for Angelique, that is, but she agreed to bring the book she was studying to the game room. There were competing suggestions of Candy-land, from Alexia, and cutthroat pool, from Doc, so they split into two groups, Natalie, Sara, Doc, and Noriko with her Hello Kitty pool cue at the pool table, with everyone else at the Candy-land board.

            The mostly unpleasant memories of her last pool game pushed Sara to keep things light, so she played casually and wound up dropping out first. Natalie fell next, as Noriko arranged things to keep Doc in the running. The werecat was strutting around the table by then, and schooled him handily once it was just the two of them.

            The Candy-land game was hardly half done after that first game, so Sara tagged out with Karen and let her get a turn behind a cue. She managed to maintain Karen’s lead and tied with Tavia to become co-rulers of Candy Castle.

            The strutting was in from the start of game 2, with Noriko beginning the game by blowing on Doc’s cue for luck. She told the others, nodding when Alice suggested that might be an adventure for another day.

                                                    Sara smiled at the old ghost as she asked after the last path, the one that led to the Chamber. She got the idea of a trap, a cage, that psychopomps like her had used to capture something dangerous. Alice’s eyes gleamed excitedly when Sara relayed that. Doc tried to talk her out of charging down that path, making reference to the infamous napalm recon mission.

                                                    “We are the only group of people I know who need napalm for recon, and even we didn’t bring any today, understand?”

                                                    “No, I don’t, cause you’ve never actually told me the story, but I get your meaning.”

                                                    Alice looked unhappy, but after the krewe left their offerings, she turned towards the tunnel they’d come from. Sara took her arm as they walked, telling the full napalm reconnaissance story. Alice smiled at her as they reached the mouth of the cave and helped her start her climb.

                                                    The group decided to check out the tugboat first, since it was much easier to get to. Alice hopped on as soon as she gave it a once over, the rest of the group following once nothing untoward happened right off. Nothing happened at all, until Natalie opened the door to the helm.

                                                    “Boat’s not going anywhere today, the Captain’s not feeling well.”

                                                    A ghost in a ratty maid's uniform spoke. Her stringy hair covered her face as she gestured at the captain’s chair. In it was a moss covered skeleton, skull lolling to the side. Doc moved into the small room, addressing the ghost.

                                                    “He’s not been feeling well for awhile, huh?”

                                                    “Nope. I think ‘e’s got the gout.”

                                                    Doc tried to question the ghost about the boat and the rope tied off to nothing, but she claimed to not know much, suggesting that the Captain might, in between trying to get the skeleton to help her tidy up the place. When she prodded him to tidy up the maps, Doc cleared his throat and asked to see them.

                                                    “Well, they’re the Captain’s! Only if he says so.”

                                                    Sara smiled mischievously and a tenor voice came from the captain’s chair, telling the ghost to let Doc have a look. The ghost shrugged and rummaged around before finding and handing them over. Doc looked them over carefully, noting the handful of nearby islands, the red X marking the boat of regrets and the hand-labeled ‘Pup Pid’s Wreck’.

                                                    Alice blinked a little, then moved to stand near the mossy skeleton, looking him over while Doc kept talking to the ghost.

                                                    “Have you ever seen Pup Pid?”

                                                    “Nope, I’m still here.”

                                                    Natalie frowned, drawing her gun. Doc paused in his interrogation, looking over at her.

                                                    “She’s lying.”

                                                    “About?”

                                                    “Seeing Pup Pid.”

                                                    Doc and Alice shifted into a fighting stance and Sara summoned her keystone knife, as the maid threw her hair back, revealing a skull with glowing yellow eyes and golden teeth. The ghost drew a sword and a pistol made of blood. Natalie leveled her gun while the others waited outside the helm.

                                                    The three in the cabin, and Natalie with her gun, made short work of the ghost, but not before she scored a strong hit on Alice. She bulwarked the physical damage, but looked drawn. She shivered once the ghost splashed into warm salt water, sizzling like acid on the deck.

                                                    “I think that hit me in my soul.”

                                                    Eris wormed her way into the helm to put an arm around her mate. Natalie put her gun away, looking stricken as she laid a hand on Alice’s shoulder.

                                                    “I’m sorry. I didn’t... I expected her to back down when I called her out, not attack a whole group of us.”

                                                    Alice squeezed her hand, smiling and waving off her apologies. She let the rest of them know what she had read off the dead captain. He had been a ghost in the area for awhile before being killed, again, by a member of Pup Pid’s crew. He did, in fact, know something about the other rope tied off at the quays. A ship had come in, sometime before this place was in the Underworld. The crew was terrified and they scuttled their own ship as soon as they landed.

                                                    Natalie and Doc began looking around the helm now that the guardian was no longer there. Once Alice finished explaining what she’d learned, Natalie looked up, frowning.

                                                    “Something is off about this boat. I can’t tell if it’s because it’s older than the ones I’m used to, or if something is wrong.”

                                                    “Do we wanna risk unmooring it then? Just in case?”

                                                    Sara looked nervously at the dock, beginning to feel a little queasy. Alice shrugged.

                                                    “We don’t need it, unless we’re going past the islands.”

                                                    “You don’t.”

                                                    “I’ll make sure you get across.”

                                                    “So, Doc throws me across and you catch?”

                                                    Alice smiled at the tattooed woman, rolling her eyes good naturedly. Sara grinned back wickedly, her nervousness forgotten for the moment. Her queasiness came back full blast though, as she looked over at Natalie and saw her poring over some paperwork.

                                                    When Natalie mentioned cargo manifests, Sara joined Doc in looking for a cargo hold, an unusual thing for a tugboat to have. They didn’t find one. Still, it distracted Sara from the rocking of the boat. Alice dealt with her own seasickness by hopping out onto the quay with Eris.

                                                    Doc and Natalie consulted on the paperwork she had found. All of it was for a ship called the Mermaid’s Carriage. The manifests’ last entry was a load of gold, dated 1840, with no receiving signature. They stashed the papers away for a more detailed perusal later, especially a wax-sealed letter addressed to a Captain Namoth. The seal on the wax was two horses pulling a carriage with a lion rampant. Neither of the more local Sin-eaters recognized it, and with a look at the greenish Sara, they put it in the pack with the rest, without breaking it open.

                                                    When the three of them left the helm, Sara’s brow knit, looking for Alice. She grinned and waved when she found her, standing braced on the rocky shore of the third island. Doc followed Sara’s eyeline and growled.

                                                    “I need a harpoon.”

                                                    Sara looked at him sternly, while swaying her way over to the rope bridges Alice had rigged up while they searched the helm. She walked gracefully over the first two, 'stumbling' at the end of the third bridge as Alice reached for her. She wound up falling into the redhead’s arms and winked up at her.

                                                    Doc rolled his eyes as he climbed up onto the island, accepting Alice’s helping hand once she’d set Sara upright further up the beach. He looked over the hastily built shack, taking a deep breath and blowing hard in its direction, a smile playing over his face. Sara grinned at him.

                                                    “OK Mr Big Bad Wolf!”

                                                    “I can’t even begin to claim that title.”

                                                    Doc gestured towards Eris, who was shifting out of wolf form. The werewolf snickered as Alice turned bright red. She turned to hide her blushing face and focused on the shack.

                                                    After a moment she signaled people to come up. They all walked into the one room shack, and as soon as they crossed the threshold, the weres, Alice and Natalie all perked up, looking around uneasily. Natalie drew her pistol. Alice looked at Doc and Sara.

                                                    “Something’s watching us.”

                                                    The group moved carefully, looking over the small room. The furniture was minimal - a bureau, a small writing desk and a chair sitting by the window. A rifle sat on top of the bureau. On the window sill next to the chair was an ornate wooden pipe and a bottle of scotch. Below them was a pair of boots.

                                                    Sara spoke to the air, greeting whoever was watching. If they heard, they didn’t respond to her, or to Doc’s written note. Natalie had found a locked drawer in the bureau and hunkered down next to it, pulling out a hairpin. A few moments of jimmying got the drawer open. Inside was an old flintlock pistol and a map of an unnamed island. Natalie handed the gun over to Doc.

                                                    “It has a resonance like a ghost. I don’t know how it is significant, though it’s maybe something’s weakness.”

                                                    “I guess it’ll take research, huh?”

                                                    Alice smiled resignedly at Doc. Sara grinned encouragingly at her. They gave the room another look over as Alice commented on how homey the shack was. Sara nodded excitedly.

                                                    “We were trying to find places for each of us, right? Maybe we could just get this stretch of the beach - Natalie seemed to feel at home in the boat, you like the feel of the shack, Alice, and surely Doc could dock the pirate ship around here. I just want to set up a camped caravan somewhere, so that’d work on this beach. We need to find a place for Alexia, but it would mean safety in numbers, right?”

                                                    Natalie’s face fell.

                                                    “But... I thought...”

                                                    Sara looked at her, concerned, but Natalie shook her head.

                                                    “First we have to deal with the pirate ship.”

                                                    Sara nodded and suggested Doc step outside with the gun and see if that drew the watcher’s eye. It didn’t, but after a couple of minutes, the unseen presence disappeared completely. Just as those who could detect it began to relax, Alice and Eris looked outside.

                                                    “Something’s moving in the water.”

                                                    “A lot of somethings!”

                                                    Doc started yelling, counting around twenty skeletal sailors climbing up onto the island, all armed with pistols and swords made of blood. Alice rushed outside, the others close behind.

                                                    Natalie put a hand on Sara’s shoulder and asked the other woman to catch her. She did, quickly, as the strawberry scented woman collapsed into a trance. As soon as her eyes closed, the skeletons already on the island froze in place. Natalie muttered into Sara’s ear.

                                                    “There’s about fifty of them. We have thirty yards of wards.”

                                                    Sara let the rest know, wincing at the gleam in Alice’s eyes as the redhead set to smashing skulls, counting down from fifty giddily. Sara made her way carefully across the rope bridges, cradling Natalie close. After a few moments, Doc managed to get Alice to follow and the group made good time back home.

                                                    Once they got home, they stashed the gun in the war room at the Bluffs and headed back in. They agreed to explore away from the beach, to let the heat die down. Alice was interested in checking out the noose orchard that she, Eris, Alexia, and Chandra had passed over last time. They decided to check in at the diner first. As they started down the path towards the diner, Sara hung back, taking Natalie’s arm.

                                                    “What’s wrong love?”

                                                    “I... I assumed we would have the same place. Because we’re soulmates.”

                                                    Sara’s shoulders slumped and she bit her lip.

                                                    “I think we all need a place that resonates with our own Selves. I do want to be close to you though. And not just for safety in numbers.”

                                                    “I want to be close to you too.”

                                                    Natalie pulled Sara close, clinging tightly. Sara kissed her gently, holding her protectively. The strawberry scented woman nuzzled her neck, whispering in her ear.

                                                    “We should be close. Our souls are bound together.”

                                                    Sara’s breath hitched. She managed a smile for her love, kissing her again and putting an arm around her waist. Natalie leaned against her wife, reaching up to play with the hair at the nape of her neck.

                                                    The walk to the diner wasn’t long. Once there, they settled into a booth, ordering milkshakes from the Matron. Janice wandered over after a moment and took a seat after Doc offered. They chatted for awhile before Doc brought up Pup Pid.

                                                    “He’s a pirate? And a really powerful ghost. Probably cause he can’t leave the coast. I don’t know much. There are stories about things being left on the beach, so that if you touch them, you get stuck and pulled underwater to become one of his crew.”

                                                    “We did meet some of his crew.”

                                                    “I’m guessing they didn’t want to exchange bottles of scotch.”

                                                    “No, more like attack our souls.”

                                                    Doc looked to Alice, who nodded.

                                                    “Their skulls split like anything else though.”

                                                    The subject wandered for awhile after that, until Janice asked if they knew about the living redhead woman that had shown up around the area. Natalie tensed a little until Janice clarified that she wasn’t a Sin-eater.

                                                    Alice realized it was Dulcinea, Doc nodding along with her.

                                                    “Our vampire friends said she was a cousin, and she controls bees.”

                                                    “Like, Draculas? Really? I guess I can’t throw stones, huh?”

                                                    Once they finished their milkshakes, they left payment in the form of smokes, ribbon, twist caps and bottle of Corona. Alice took the lead again, directing them towards the noose orchard.
