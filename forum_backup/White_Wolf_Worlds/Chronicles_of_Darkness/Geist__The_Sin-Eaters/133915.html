    Sin-eater History and Background Expansion ("Fluff Houserules")

    05-12-2014, 12:56 PM
    The PDF

    Much as I love Geist, it has a lot of problems. Some--like the way the Krewe Channel completely remakes your Geist and you believe it was always secretly Santa Claus, as one person put it--are handwaved or ignored, the same way that Predator's Taint is often ignored. Other parts, unfortunately, are less about too much information and more about completely missing information. Sin-eaters have little in common with each other beyond being Sin-eaters, and they have no real antagonists to speak of. A lot of players don't even know about Abmortals because they've only been given a few pages. This is compared to groups like VII and the Seers, who have quite a bit of information given about them.

    Since other than the Ready Made Player Characters and Geist's as of yet unspoiled entry in Dark Eras there's little in the foreseeable future for the Bound, I want to try to expand on them myself. Geist, despite being a rather unique kind of character to the World of Darkness and an interesting one at that, gets a lot of flack, and many of the complaints are valid. So let's hear what you don't like about Geist. Whether you're a fan and love the game, or you hate it and dislike the presentation, I want to hear what we the players can do to make Geist better.

    I don't plan on creating a whole new thing, though I do have plans for providing houserules to make the game work with the God-Machine Chronicle rules updates. This won't be a Rum and Bones: The Kerberoi Chronicle or anything, but if all goes well, it will reinvigorate interest in *Geist* and encourage Onyx Path to make more supplements for it.

    Here's a quick list of things I want to address

    ● Plasm generation (one plasm a scene from ofrendas and one per week for a Haunt is ridiculous)

    ● De-emphasizing krewe channels and Tier 3 krewes (the Tier system from Hunter feels very unnecessary here, and goes against much of the rest of the fluff, but it gets a long series of rules and merits anyway)

    ● More about Abmortals, and why Sin-eaters would come into conflict with them.

    ● More reasons for Sin-eaters to go into the Underworld beyond it being cool (even though it totally is)

    ● Meaningful emphasis on Archetypes as diverse groups and their importance in Plasm generation

    ● Meaningful emphasis on Thresholds as more than just starting Key and fluff of the Sixth Sense

    ● Giving krewes a reason to be together

    ● Fleshing out the Sin-eater antagonists, in particular Abmortals, the Sacrosanct, Cthonians, the Vacant, and the Church of the Unified Spirit, a cult that worships the Bound as the perfect expression of humanity.

    ● Power level fix concerning Manifestations (every sin-eater gets at least +3 to their starting Manifestation/Key combo as is, and then can get Mementos)

    ● Discussion of Sin-eater culture around the world, including Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and the pre-Colombian Americas.

    ● Various bits and bobs concerning the Twilight Network, Gravedigger's Cant/Old Tongue, and the Horsemen, adding to the culture of Death and giving it something more concrete.

    Any suggestions are more than welcome!
