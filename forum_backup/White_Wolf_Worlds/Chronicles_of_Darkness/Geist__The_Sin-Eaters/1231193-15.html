                                            Hey, early preview of the Legions of the Dead.

                                            Quick summation:

                                            1) The living can have ghosts of their past come to life; Phantom Limbs are a symbiotic version, but it's just as common, if not more so, for a Doppleganger to be a crazed, incomplete version of a person who desperately wants their living self to become who they left behind. And they merge.

                                            2) Animals do not normally leave ghosts, but only because nobody sapient remembers them. Anyone who has owned a pet, or has developed a healthy fear or awe of a specific beast, can tell you that's not exactly impossible to thwart. Barghests are normally eaten by the Underworld when they move on, but ghosts with Animal Control can and do train them. Also, I have an excuse to post this:


                                            3) Castoffs, the ghosts of objects, can and do get Numina, but they're robots. If it's not part of their "program", they literally cannot do it.
