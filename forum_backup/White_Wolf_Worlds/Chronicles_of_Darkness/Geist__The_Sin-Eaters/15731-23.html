            The Middle Shadows Confront Pup Pid for the Underworld Oceanfront territory. Even bringing in Jasmine for back up. They battle the powerful Geist and their own power grows.

            This is the last session before a time skip. More after the session.

            Session CLX

            Sara’s Letters

            Sara woke up to her wives arguing. Tensions had gotten a little high the night before, after Karen refused to serve as a scourge to Natalie’s guilt, but Natalie had sublimated her frustration into the scene. But it hadn’t faded away. Sara gave Karen an apologetic look and curled around Natalie, holding her tightly as she tried to sooth the strawberry scented woman with words instead of blows. It didn’t address the underlying problem, but Sara was able to assuage Natalie’s guilt, somewhat, and calm the crisis.

            Calm it enough at least that the trio joined the others for lunch. The ladies - Doc having eaten quickly and gone back to his Ceremony - were hanging out after and remarking amusedly on the many garlands of mistletoe that seemed to have popped up overnight when someone knocked on the door frame. Natalie perked up and rushed over to Jasmine, pulling her into a big hug.

            “Hello everyone. I am certainly glad to see you all again. And to know that you realize you can always call.”

            Lily waved broadly at everyone, standing on tiptoes on Jasmine’s shoulder. Karl filed in behind her, returning everyone’s hugs in his awkward way. He smiled as they moved into the living room, commenting on the extent of his cousin’s decorating.

            After a bit, Doc wandered in, carrying a small bag. As soon as she saw him, Lily hopped off the couch arm and tackled his leg. Doc patted her gently and picked her up, depositing her on his shoulder. Jasmine smiled slightly at the pair of them.

            “Lily has missed you.”

            The reunited crew shared their various adventures, Jasmine beginning by telling the others the progress she’d made concerning Bone Dry. Natalie sat close to her, a large smile on her face when Jasmine’s story didn’t call for some other expression. When she was done, Jasmine looked around, frowning slightly.

            “Where’s the new girl?”

            “On her way back from Sleeping Giant, I imagine.”

            “There’s been no... trouble.”

            “Well, if there is, do let me know.”

            Alice arrived not too long later, with Eris in tow, a stubborn look on her face. Natalie grinned at her when she found them in the living room.

            “Spooky’s here!”

            Jasmine and Alice were still wary of one another, but they nodded in greeting, and with everyone there, hashed out the final details of the plan.

            At some point, Troy had overheard them discussing the situation and asked if he could help. The shifted significant others had no intention of allowing their partners to face a ghost pirate without them, so with the six Sin-Eaters, their group numbered 10. The mood was serious as they moved down the path to the beach together. Sara avoided looking at the intense and violent expression on Troy’s face, focusing on gathering suitable pieces of wood for Natalie’s circle. She did manage a bright smile as she quietly teased Alice on her own, much better, gathering efforts.

            At the beach, the Sin-Eaters powered up and the were-creatures shifted as Natalie drew the circle in chalk on wood. As soon as she’d activated her Manifestations, Jasmine slipped quietly into the water, getting in position to do her part as soon as the time came.

            Shortly after the warding circle flared into life, a half dozen skeletons walked up out of the ocean, looking towards the disturbance. Sara took up a defensive stance over Natalie and Doc’s unconscious forms, Alexia next to her searching for a glimmer from Jasmine’s light. The other 6, and Doc as the Dog of War, moved towards the skeletons.

            The two battle lines clashed right as the strong diver’s light turned on. Alexia glanced at the fight, but managed to pull her attention away from the skeletons by force of will and began to raise the boat. The first clash ended with one less skeleton, it having fallen to Troy’s axe as he glowed a blood red. The other skeletons were all worse for the wear, while the krewe was unharmed.

            The next clash saw an end to the other five, with two more newly emerging from the ocean’s spray. They were distracted, marveling at the sight of the galleon’s arrival into the shallow waters. They never noticed Alice leap forward, landing with a splash, her axe biting deep into one of their skulls. Her axe trailed a salty spray as she followed up with a swing towards the second. By that point, Troy had arrived and finished the skeleton off, the red light fading around him.

            Jasmine beat the boat to shore, joining the group as they retreated further up the beach, staying within Natalie’s circle. She looked at them seriously.

            “There’s a really big fish following the boat. I mean, bigger than the boat.”

            Sara’s eyes widened as she looked the sizable galleon over, swallowing as she noticed the swarm of skeletons pouring out of it. Doc began passing out explosives, instructing the combat group how to set them up. Jasmine raised an eyebrow, a slight smile playing over her lips.

            “Sorry, Doc, I didn’t realize this was a reconnaissance mission.”

            He glared at her, but it was softened by his own smile. Natalie sprung the trap as soon as the bulk of the skeletons were a suitable distance from the boat, freezing all of them in place. The others set the explosives quickly and with a bang, evened the odds.

            Natalie shifted closer to Sara as the Dog of War turned to her.

            “There’s 8 skeletons and Pup Pid, focusing on something, waiting in ambush. Doc - the Dog won’t fit.”

            He nodded and returned to his usual body. Sara stayed with Natalie as the rest of them boarded the ship in true pirate style. Even Alexia, though she lagged behind, staying out of sight until it came time for her to do her part with Pup Pid’s Bane.

            They found two skeletons bodily blocking the doorway into the room Natalie had directed them to. Jasmine and Alice were in the lead and Jasmine reacted first, elbowing the one in front of her and throwing it to the ground. Noriko struck around Alice to get at the other, dodging out of the way as Doc lit it on fire. As the flames smouldered, Alice shouldered it further into the room, finishing it off with a solid swing once she had space.

            She stumbled at the end of the swing, coughing up brackish water. She fell to her hands and knees, trying to get rid of the water that had replaced the air in her lungs. Eris bounded in after her and howled, shifting into her Gauru form and clawing at Pup Pid.

            The rest of the skeletons focused on Eris, whose wounds knit themselves with startling quickness. The krewe focused on Pup Pid, with Troy drawing on the mystical powers of the axe to hit the supernaturally evasive geist, Natalie using her Boneyard to directly consume his corpus and Jasmine disappearing and striking unexpectedly to great effect.

            Sara stood close to Natalie, knuckles white around her stone knife as Natalie described the fight in mumbles. The tattooed girl gasped when a large shadow approached the boat with startling quickness and nudged it, causing it to rock on the sandy seabed. Natalie quickly reassured her that while Troy and Karl had lost their footing momentarily, it did not have the effect that Pup Pid had likely wanted.

            Jasmine had not stumbled, and went in for her second blow, wrenching the pirate ghost’s neck around. She felt his corpus fade, and called out.

            “Alexia! Now!”

            Alexia stood and raised the old fashioned pistol, screwing her eyes almost shut. But her aim was true and she fired quickly. The geist dissipated, leaving his hat to fall to the ground. Jasmine reappeared, dusting off her hands.

            As soon as Natalie told her the fight was over and dropped the Boneyard, Sara pulled her to her feet, then along the beach towards the boat. She watched the shadow under the water move away, almost as if fleeing their approach. She ignored it and they climbed quickly, bursting into the inner room.

            They found the walls covered in claw marks and Noriko screaming at Doc in Japanese, as he coughed out the last of the water in his own lungs. Eris appeared clam, sitting close to Alice who was breathing deeply. Sara looked around, confirming that no one seemed seriously hurt, and sighed in relief.

            “Doubloons!”

            Alexia took off, leaving behind the remaining scattered bone pieces to search the ship. Doc got up carefully, joining her with Noriko and Natalie right behind.

            They returned after not too long, without any pirate treasure. They did have a pair of statues they’d found in Pup Pid’s room, a slick black model of a heart and an obsidian statue of a tropical fish. Natalie explained that she thought they were what Pup Pid had used to control the large fish. Alexia’s eyes lit up as the strawberry scented woman went on to point out that the fish had not been some kind of ghost, and she asked if it could be Fishbourne related. Jasmine shook her head.

            “It didn’t look anything like Tavia or Karl’s fish form.”

            Alexia made a face, while Sara’s eyebrows went up at the mention of Tavia’s fish form. The krewe did decide to take the statues home anyway. As they left, Doc’s eyes lit on the hat the captain had left behind. Jasmine frowned as he picked it up.

            “I don’t think it’s a good idea to keep that.”

            Doc dropped in onto his head, pushing it back as it fell over his eyes.

            “Suit yourself.”

            The krewe tied the boat up to one of the quays and began for him. Jasmine, Karl and Natalie split off to check in at the lighthouse before they left the beach. As the others stepped into the real world, Karen scanned the group, jumping to her feet, her eyes wide.

            “Someone’s missing...”

            “Who?! Oh, right. Natalie, Jasmine and Karl went to the lighthouse.”

            “Fine.”

            She sat back down, eyes fixed on the portal. After Karen shut down Doc’s attempts to reassure her, Sara shooed everyone along, giving Karen a reassuring squeeze as they left her in peace.

            The krewe followed Alexia into the kitchen, pitching in to prep for celebratory homemade pizza. Alice smiled mischievously at Doc as she sliced pepperoni.

            “So, Doc, what’s a pirate's favorite letter?”

            “...arrr?”

            “No, it’s the sea!”

            Doc rolled his eyes at her twice, once for the question and once for the answer. They waited for the others to show up to start cooking, but as soon as Natalie, Karen, Jasmine and Karl walked up from the Gate room, Alexia put the pizzas in the over. Karen stood close to Natalie, she squeezed her waist gently.

            “It’s important for you all to know that Karen was definitely not crying.”

            Sara smiled tenderly at the two of them, kissing Karen softly on the forehead. The krewe chatted comfortably until and through dinner, sharing stories, Christmas plans and laughs until it came time for Jasmine and Karl to return home, for awhile.

            Home for Christmas

            Natalie Lousler sat in the sun room, watching the water outside. Her mother would arrive soon, and not be happy with her. Sure enough, she heard boots coming down the hallway, away from the chorus of women’s voices.

            “Natalie….”

            “Oh, hi Mom. How are you,” The younger girl put her phone down.

            The monochromatic elder stepped in. “You got suspended. What happened?”

            “So…did I mention I had a girlfriend?” The younger girl smiled brightly. “Well, had, till she also got suspended.”

            Natalie Knight raised an eyebrow. “Without telling me? For how long?”

            “Oh, most of lunch at least.”

            The older women walked around where the younger girl was sitting. “You got caught kissing?”

            “Yeah.”

            The Mother turned around, “I am very sorry, that is awful. You will be out of this backwards state soon enough and…”

            Younger Natalie sighed. “Okay. Maybe not just kissing.”

            A raised eyebrow.

            ‘I was helping her find something that dropped into her skirt!”

            “Oh son of a bitch!”

            “Daughter.”

            --

            “I can’t believe you were okay with your mother saying grace for us. It’s like you are growing as a person.”

            Karen Knight grinned as she looked out the window. She had always wondered what Natalie’s bedroom that she had grown up in was like. In truth, she had been looking forward to this. Natalie had once lingered in hers, after all, when she lived at home.

            “I permitted the owner of the residence we are staying in to choose the deity to whom we dedicate our breaking of bread,” Natalie was drying her hair with one towel, a second wrapped around her.

            “Well she was okay with us sharing a room,” Karen smiled. She could see a lighthouse in the distance, light sweeping across the sea. It was neat, like something out of a portrait.

            “We are married.” Natalie said as if correcting her.

            “Well, Sara is not legally married to us, and we haven’t had a ceremony yet. But she still let the three of us share a room.”

            “You will get your public ceremony, I promised you,” Natalie ran a brush through her hair.

            “I think when Tatum gets here after Boxing Day she is staying in the spare room. It is all prepped.,” Karen smiled as she heard the water turn off; Sara was done with her shower. She looked at a cross on the wall Natalie had made when she was six.

            “Unfortunately. She is still a bit old fashioned.” Natalie frowned slightly at Karen looking at her cross.

            “Or she plans to make it easier to seduce her herself.”

            Natalie whirled around, “She wouldn’t dare!”

            Sara was standing in the doorway wearing a towel around her waist and hair, a confused look on her face, “What wouldn’t I dare?”

            --

            Natalie Knight opened her eyes to see Sara looking right at her. She was smiling.

            “Tatum gets here today!”

            Natalie sat up in bed once Sara moved her head. She was still unused to waking in her old room. It was strange. She looked at her wife.

            “When is she getting here?”

            “We are picking her up about three. It will be something of a drive to the airport so…”

            Natalie stretched, looking over at Sara, “Yeah, we need to get off our asses and find a good haunt shortcut.”

            Sara smirked at her wife. “I may have already found one, out walking on the beach.”

            “Really?” Natalie’s eyes widened, blue and green looked at her wife.

            “Well yea, I was out walking when that rain shower hit, and a cute couple I was talking to took shelter for a bit with me. That unused beach house a couple blocks down has a very weak haunt in it.”

            Natalie was smirking slightly now, her eyebrow raised

            “…And very squeaky floors.”

            “Don’t ever change, Sara, “ The Goth looked down, then up. “Wait how did you get in, and know it was empty?”

            Sara’s eyes were dancing.

            “Natalie told me. She had been there before.”

            Natalie rested her face in her hands. “Oh apple, if you were not going to fall far, could you have at least rolled a bit?”

            --

            “This is wonderful. I love having the house full of people again.”

            Julie smiled at her daughter. Natalie smirked back.

            “But we don’t have a bunch of guys drinking beer and watching football loudly in the living room.”

            “It’s true, and that is my favorite part of your orientation,” Now it was Julie’s turn to smirk.

            “And the fact they are gorgeous women probably does not bother you either.”

            Julie chuckled. “I am much more pleased there is no fighting, even with Tatum here. I am not sure you were aware of how much arguing went on. It was ugly at times, and it was why I stopped hosting here, to be honest with you.”

            “Ah, I wondered.” Natalie looked at her sympathetically. “Bill and John?”

            Julie rolled her eyes. “They were part of the problem. Lord give me patience they were obnoxious when together. But also your nieces when adolescence hit. They would not stop trying to outdo each other, and snapped at each other constantly.”

            “Oh that’s right,” A manicured nail tapped Natalie’s check. “I remember avoiding them.”

            “I thought at first that was a shame, “ Julie frowned. But then she started to grin. “Now I think that might have been for the best…”

            “MOM!”

            --

            Natalie Knight sat in her tankini top, which had an appearance of lace, on the beach near the house. It was black of course, like everything else she wore; the sun was setting and the high tide meant the water surrounded her with each wave’s arrival. Pretty much everywhere Natalie had lived was near the ocean. She liked it that way. Behind her another woman with shorter hair walked up. “Nat, you okay?”

            Natalie looked behind her at Tatum. “Yea, come over and sit down! “

            Tatum found a rock a few feet back. She was less eager to have the sea crash over her than Natalie was. “I wanted to thank you for bringing me down here. Your mom is like, super nice, and your house looks like something out of a painting.

            Natalie turned and gave her a thin-lipped smile, “Thanks. Glad you are enjoying it.”

            Tatum crouched forward. “You’re not.”

            Natalie turned back to look at the sea. “I have complicated feelings about this place. I left twelve years ago vowing to never return. One more broken promise.”

            “Like shooting yourself? That’s not a bad thing, Natalie. That is a sign you are not the same girl you were then. You are not the same girl you were this time last year, from what I am told.”

            “No, I suppose I am not.” She did not look back. “It’s a good thing. Mostly. A lot has happened.”

            “So…are you okay?”

            “Yes…seeing Mom and Natalie again…it’s unreal. It’s like I can’t believe it is happening. Being here is like a dream. But…not like, a dream romance, or like a nightmare. More…,” she looked back then forward. “Surreal.”

            “As you say.”

            There was a long silence, and then Tatum rose.

            “I am gonna head back to the house. I was promised more chocolate chip cookies.”

            Natalie smiled. “Okay, be careful, it’s easy to trip. Love you.”

            “Love you too!”

            Natalie looked back out to sea. The sun had nearly finished setting. An hour after darkness fell, she rose and returned, one more key on her belt.

                                    Sara’s Stories

                                    It was a sunny Monday afternoon when Sara, Karen, Natalie and Tatum’s plane touched down. Noriko and Tavia saw the group first and hurried over to greet them. After the initial warm greetings, Tatum caught sight of her parents. Sara went with her when she stepped away to wave them over.

                                    Sara introduced herself, flirting shamelessly with the pair of them. Tatum hid her face.

                                    “We should have the three of you over for dinner sometime.”

                                    “And perhaps after dinner...”

                                    Sara grinned wickedly as Tatum linked arms with her parents and all but dragged them away from the tattooed girl.

                                    -----------------------------------------

                                    “I know you just got back, but the kids asked to meet with us. I thought you guys might want to come along.”

                                    They had, so it was the whole krewe who met with Bart and the students in one of the classrooms at Albertus Magnus. The new arrivals caused something of a splash - Deanna immediately ran up to give Natalie a big hug, earning her a dark look from Sarah, and the twins both gave Sara a shy smile, studiously ignoring each other. Sara smiled broadly back at them, then spared a glance for Marie. Alexia had been giving the krewe updates, and had commented that the witch had a serious crush on Tracey. Marie was absorbed in her notes, a serious look on her have, and hadn’t seemed to notice the other girl’s interest in Sara.

                                    After the general chatter of greetings died down, Marie stood and cleared her throat.

                                    “To start this off, this is a serious situation. Not a drill, alright? You guys that go here might have heard that a student disappeared. I’ve been looking into it, and I think it will be a way to use our powers for good.”

                                    The missing girl’s name was Katie Hong. She had a K-pop radio show, Saturday nights at 1 AM and the Saturday before, a different girl had shown up, saying that Katie was feeling sick and had asked her to run the show for her. She hadn’t been seen since, Katie or this mystery substitute.

                                    To be fair, no one knew who the substitute was. The ‘goober’ who had the slot just before her said it was ‘some other Japanese girl who played Japanese music’, despite Katie and her music both being Korean. Susan, a friend of Katie’s that Marie had been talking to, had called into the show to ask after her and been brushed off with a story about how they’d met at a party that Susan hadn’t attended.

                                                        What happens when you combine two awful things? It is not a joke I just want to be prepared.

                                                        So a group of Pure have been corrupted by one of the Gentry, The Handmaiden to the Briar Rose. Rawl’s pack of Predator Kings specifically. He is being held as his distinctly anti-authoritarian nature means he never swore fealty, and they do not want the other pack to know. They were acting rather eccentric. Doc contacted Grin, and he will be contacting the “Autumn King.” With little else to do, we returned. Karen was waiting for us impatiently. As she always does when we go to Glenwood.

                                                        Karen, Sara and I wound up playing “Strip Uno” with Alexia, Chandra and Catherine. Catherine did poorly, and I admit perhaps salivating a bit when she fully undressed. Karen did not fare great but kept her more personal dignity, and Sara ironically did not lose anything. Chandra made sure I knew she regretted that I did not lose more often. I just told her she would have to work a little harder if she wanted to see more of me.

                                                        And then I sated the appetite I had worked up with my lovelies in my room. I presume the other triad did as well. I wonder if they have decided to admit Catherine is “official” yet. I sadly know no non-awkward way to ask.


                                                        April 6th, 2011

                                                        Quiet day, busy night.

                                                        Most of the day was relaxing. Posted a Vlog, shined some of Karen’s boots. Then I worked on some music for my album for a few hours.

                                                        Meeting with the kids went well. Tracy is incorrigible. Totally struck out with Katie’s friend Susan though. Timothy has been playing with magic. We gave out new assignments, which largely consisted of pairs listening to the show…Deanna and Clarissa (and an unnamed Korean friend of Deanna) and Marie and Sarah. Timothy wound up working on a spell, his sister was going to seduce Rebecca Stein again for access to Katie’s room to get sympathetic links. Deanna and Tracy were also going to track down the party.

                                                        Sarah confronted me outside. She wanted to spend more time with me. Specifically that night making love. I had talked to her last time and when we got together about how I worked, and her being important to me, but my relationships being what they are. She is very immature compared to Tatum, Sara or Karen…not ready to deal with my life, Banshee or no. I so did not expect this. I figured if anyone would get too attached it might be Deanna; truthfully, though, I am almost hurt at how she characterized bedding me as “life-changing” but seemed to lose all sexual interest in me after. That never happens, except straight girls, and if Deanna is totally straight, I will eat my El Dorado.

                                                        We got back and saw Grin (and Gretchen, who more or less lives with him now. She came to visit Tavia.) Grin enchanted us (he is as terrifying as Spooky implied) and confirmed that, yes, Gentry corrupted Pure was bad. He spoke a lot of the Changeling politics in the area, but the short version is his Court wants to keep things quiet out of concern how the other Courts will react. Made sense when he said it, but I am not sure how much was spin; Grin is after all somewhat contemptuous of authority figures. Not on the level Rawl is, but still.

                                                        I did some more playing and composing to round out the night. Sara reported overall the shows (by “Li-Hyun” who was not a student.) seemed normal, but she sounded young, and had a thicker accent than a lot of the Korean girls at the school. She also parroted a lot of the comments Katie made, such as comments about boys and bands. Same words too. Sara speculated that Katie was a fetch and “Li-Hyun” the actual Changeling replacing her. But there was more oddity. Sarah reported being utterly creeped out by it, to the point of being unable to listen, and Catherine found it off-putting, but not as seriously. She admitted it could be her social issues, and the fact our “Li-Hyun” could be faking the voice and accent, as it sounds distinct.

                                                        Eventually we settled in, no stip games this time. My goodness, we should at least do strip poker. Strip Canasta? Maybe they would like Canasta.

                                                        Regardless, this little Goth needs her beauty sleep. Night!

                                                        Session CLXI

                                                        Well, that is a problem. Gentry corrupted Pure are a bad deal. As if Glenwood didn’t have enough troubles.

                                                        Hope the Story Weavers story is interesting. The group seems to be getting a kick out of it.
