                        "There were many other heirlooms heaped inside the earth-house, because long ago, with deliberate care, somebody now forgotten had buried the riches of a high-born race in this ancient cache. Death had come and taken them all in times gone by and the only one left to tell their tale, the last of their line, could look forward to nothing but the same fate for himself: he foresaw that his joy in the treasure would be brief." - Beowulf

                        So, Mementos. A few things that we've learned. Mementos arise from death, but the pattern of what becomes a Memento and what doesn't is erratic. Objects brought back from the Underworld can become Mementos. The Bound can create Mementos. Deathmasks are just Mementos made from the permanent death of a Geist. And Ceremonies can create Mementos by binding ghosts into their own Anchors (like a Fetish). Mementos also are equally solid to things in ghostly Twilight as they are to physical beings, which is nice. In addition to activating them for some Plasm from their tied Key, you can also feed it to your Geist, permanently destroying it, for a complete Plasm refresher. There's also "Greater Mementos" that do insanely powerful things that are mostly just rumours, it would seem. The knives that murdered Julius Caesar. The skull of the first murder victim. Things that are famous, or old, or just in the right place at the right time.

                        Additionally, Mementos get some minor powers in addition to providing Keys that feel a lot more like Tokens than, say, Fetishes or Artifacts. It seems like these powers are "always on" and entirely unrelated to activating the Key.

                        And now, we've got a choice. Ghosts (which we know are going to be made into a playable Minor Template), or Reapers (the brand new mysterious antagonists that have been teased a lot)?
