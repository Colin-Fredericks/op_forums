    Choice: become a Vampire or a Geist

    06-29-2014, 08:06 PM
    I thought this was a fun enough idea from the old forums to repost.

    sikker said:
    Being bitten isn't enough to become a vampire, you also have to recieve blood and a dot of willpower from the vampire to seal the deal. It is theoratically possible to be contacted by a Geist at that exact second and be offered to return to life as a Bound instead of a Vampire. But it is one or the other. Accepting the Bargain makes the Embrace fail, and refusing the Bargain simply leaves the Embrace to work as normal.

    It *would* be an interesting story, though, to have a vampire Embrace a childe, and have it seemingly work, bringing the person back to life and able to perform magical tricks... But somehow that childe is able to eat food, age and walk in daylight. How long until it is discovered that the childe is not a vampire at all? That would be an interesting game.
    What I'm thinking though that it'd be pretty cool in a campaign to offer the player that choice.

    You get jumped by vampires, and just at that moment when you are floating between life and death that is needed before you receive that drop of Vitae, this figure comes to you and says, basically: "Look, those guys up there are going to make you one of them. You'll be forced to prey on others like they do and you'll be forever cursed. Why not take on my offer instead? I can bring you back same as you were."

    A pretty good backstory for a Sin-Eater, too. Brought back by a force greater than your assailants to take revenge on them, expecting to get a new childe and receiving a vengeful spirit who can roast their asses. And you are now the great protector of the spirit world, who makes the undead creatures of darkness flee and send them back to the underworld that they were seeking to escape.

    The challenge of course is to make this encounter meaningful if the player picks the vampire side, too. What is this mysterious otherworldy being messing with the Embraces? Is he perhaps a vengeful embodiment of failed embraces, or something even stranger? Did he also make that offer to others in the past, and what motivated them to refuse? Or maybe there are others like him too - an entire congregate of spirits opposing the vampiric race. Or maybe he is something personal to the character, a gatekeeper between life and death who shall appear again when the vampire reaches the end of their unlife, perhaps even a judge of their otherworldy judgement or a guide to Golconda?

Sometimes the shit might not smell that bad, and eventually you might even get used to it. But that dosen't the fact that you're covered in shit, and theres more to come.

                                          I figure I'd add my (somewhat crude) attempt at a Geist for just such a character concept.

                                          FATHER BLOOD
                                          Quote: (Vaguely latin gibberish)
                                          Background: It is said by some Geists do not remember their human lives. And Blood does have trouble remembering the day to day living of his mortal self. But the important parts are all too clear. He was a priest, in a small village where no authority besides the church had authority in anything but name since the Romans left. He was a good man(or so his memory says), and gave guidance and aid to his flock. Then She came. She claimed to seek the wisdom of God, but the Leech-Woman only wanted power. With her wicked blood-magics(and great beauty, though he refuses to acknowledge such failings on his part), she twisted his mind and heart, and gave him blood thrice to bind him as her eternal Ghoul. She used him to cover up her indiscretions and find her food, but mostly used her "lover" for her enjoyment. Again and again, she forced him to break every code and stricture he held dear, laughing as he screamed and prayed for mercy. And one day, she pushed too far, and he screamed not in pain, but rage, and used the blood-powered strength she gave him to pin her to the floor of the old wooden church house with a sword(a trophy she took from the corpse of her last "lover" once she tired of him). He set alight the structure, turned, and bit hard upon her neck, giddy with vengeance and Vitae even as his flesh burned black. But he could not stay dead, for she had oft complaint of her fellows, and so long as one of her blighted kin stalked the faithful, he could never stay his undying hatred. And now, centuries later, he has found a proper ally in his fight against the Leaches( "Daeva" they call themselves), a young man drained dry by one in the blasphemous ritual required to create more of their kind. With Blood's aid, he slaughtered the unprepared abomination, and now lives to protect others from his fate. He is young and weak, so he is reluctant to kill, but Blood knows he can bring him around with just a little more time...
                                          Apperance: Blood appears as a corpse wearing the uniform of a catholic priest from the dark ages, torn and filthy in a way that exposes his limbs and torso, covered in puncture wounds like that of a snake bite, each oozing blood at a steady rate. His face, however, is a charred and blackened skull, its teeth sharpened and lengthened into a parody of a serpent's fangs.
                                          Storytelling hints: Father Blood is presented as a theoretical antagonist, though he could easily be a player's Geist. As an antagonist, he is quite willing to drive his partner insane if it means he'll be willing to kill without question, and doesn't care who gets caught in the crossfire.
                                          Threshold: Torn
                                          Virtue: Justice- The Vampires will pay for the lives they've ruined.
                                          Vice: Wrath-And he doesn't care how many innocents have to burn for his vendetta...
                                          Keys: Pyre-Flame(He killed and died by flame), Stigmatica(He was a Ghoul, and has become obsessed with the "Leaches")

                                          I don't have a good enough grasp of the mechanics to go on, but I hope I've helped someone out.
