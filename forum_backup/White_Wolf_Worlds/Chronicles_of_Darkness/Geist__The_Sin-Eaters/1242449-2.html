        You know what? All we really have to go by at this point concerning Tyranny is its name and how to replace Synergy with it. Everything else is guesswork. We don't know that it's like treating every misdemeanor as a felony. It is a ten-level trait, implying that it comes in degrees, with some Tyrants being bossier than others. And it does seem reasonable that one you reach the apex, it truly lives up to the name. But at the opposite end? For all we know, it might be hard to tell the difference between Synergy 1 and Tyranny 1, because both of their natures are so… mild.

        And yes, Geist makes the assumption that switching to Tyranny is a Bad Thing. But it also makes the assumption that one's Geist isn't totally repugnant to the person to whom it is bound — that while they have differences of opinion, there's enough common ground there to build on to establish a cooperative relationship. We're challenging the latter assumption here; and with it comes the possibility that the former isn't always a cut-and-dry always-right vs. always-wrong contrast, either. Taking an extreme example, what if you learned that your Geist started out as the ghost of Adolph Hitler and has only gotten worse since then? Remember, he's bound to you in the most intimate way possible; you can't just walk away. Staying at Synergy 0 isn't wise, either; and building up Synergy may well prove to be impossible. You have no good options. So the question is whether or not Tyranny might in fact be the least bad option, as bad as it is.

        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                            Re: Sympathy for the Devil

                                            I am enjoying this debate. Hopefully sometime between the backer draft and the final they can fit in at least a little text on this subject. I agree that drawing a firm line under "What to Do when a Ghost is a Bastard" can clear up further debate, even if the answer is a personal choice that changes from Sin Eater to Sin Eater. The path of Tyranny, even at its gentlest, is still enslaving an entity to your whims (at least from what is said thus far). A little more "Why" of Tyranny to go with the "What is Tyranny" would be helpful. We can probably save the "How" of Tyranny for a later book - the 2nd Edition core books don't tend to present antagonistic factions as playable options.

                                            At least the way I am tending to run at my table is that the Sin-Eater faction of the Bound operate from a position of Empathy - that even the most violent, destructive, sadistic shade of a mass murderer has an issue,or trauma, or unresolved pain that causes them to act out, rather than being simply "Bad". Of course, it is difficult to help resolve a Ghosts root trauma and help them move on when they are trying their level best to eat your skin, but that is what makes the game fun.
