            Geist has a lot of good ideas. These ideas don't seem to be working together towards any one feel or type of campaign. I'd like to see Geist tightened up, focused on some particular playstyles, and then have the book reinforce its themes dutifully.

            Is this a game about second chances? Then the reason you came back, your Fetters (replace name?) need to be a major part of character creation, like Touchstones for Vampire or the Durance for Changeling.

            Is the game meant to focus on the tension of the Geist's will and the character's will? Then defining that will needs to be a part of character creation and there needs to be rules to represent that struggle.

            Is the game about enjoying life? Then enjoying life needs to be represented in the mechanics.

            Et cetera, et cetera, et cetera. Really, Geist isn't a bad game, just needs some polish and some focusing.

            Oh, and "Murder Mystery" needs to be a normal mode of play, and the powers / etc. need to support that. Three to five STs I know picked up Geist and said "Oh hey. I'll run a Ghost Stories / Murder Mystery game." Then they player picked boneyard and the ST cried, usually never picking up the game again.

                    You can still run a murder mystery, it just needs a supernatural hook to be effective. Most of a Sin-Eaters powers of murder-solving play around with the ghost and body. What if the body is in a morque or a forensic lab, being studied by the actual police? What if there is no ghost, that the ghost that is, has been so throughly brain-blasted by whatever horror comitted the crime, as to render any communication with it neigh useless?
                    And to be fair, which system really allows an effective murder mystery, outside of Hunter? As a Mage you run into much the same problems as with Sin-Eaters, a Vampire could possibly do something like leech memory out of the corpses blood and both Wereworlf and Vampire could have some ritual shit that allowed to fuck around the ghost too. Perhaps a Promethean or Changeling might be slightly more stunted, but I would be surprised if one could not create a Transmutation or Contract that allowed them to play around with things.

                    I think what Geist needs, are story hooks to make suggestions as to how one might concievably run a murder mystery, given The Bounds bucket of murder-solving abillities without having the entire thing turn into a; "The Storyteller has convieniently shot down your powers, for the sake of the story. Just go with it!".

                    I agree with SoulGambit. The game has alot of disconnected (though very cool) thematic sparks strewn throughout the book, but with little to no mechanical impact to back them up. To be frank, at this point, I think throwing any of them out the door would be a shame. Perhaps a set of comprehensive mechanics could be pressented for each, and then the storyteller could deside for himself what his chronicle would be about?
                    Is his particular chronicle about second chances? Then he uses the mechanics that emphasize the theme of second chances.
                    Is his particular chronicle about the tension of the Geists will and the characters will? Then he uses the mechanics that emphazise the theme of mental dissonance.
                    This way, everyone gets a slice of the Geist-cake, and the games thematic focus isn't shifted for anyone who doesn't specifically desire the focus to be shifted. Of course this might be a pipe-dream, as I could easily imagine a book that encompasses all of these mechanical elements, to take up something like 10000 pages, and nobody wants to write nor buy something like that, outside of a physics study.

                        You can address the issue by adjusting the powers, and/or putting a "so you want to run a murder mystery" section in the ST portion of the book. I suspect the best results will actually come from some combination of the two. I personally have no problems with boneyard, but that's -only- because I participated in / ran a Naruto Persistant World for some time and have oodles of experience with pesky Hyuuga. Whether or not other gamelines allow for an effective murder mystery doesn't really matter. New STs to the line consistently expect Geist to do murder mystery well, so it needs to do it if it wants to retain that population.

                        Generally speaking, I follow the philosophy that a core book should set a tone and do a small number of types of games really well, with supplements introducing material to explore those types more and/or introduce new types. This is mostly personal preference, but I find it makes for an over-all better game.

                        Mechanics are actually pretty easy once you decide on a theme and stick with it.

                                                        I did not write any of the Corebook or Book of the Dead. I'd love to work on a 2e though, and worked on the RMC as well as Mortal Remains.

                                                        One thing with the Geist and the Sin-Eater is we have had a lot of games (Wraith, Dark Reflections, Risen, Kindred of the East, and outside WW/OPP, Deadlands) with a dark force (or light in Spectres case) that is struggled with, with possession systems and what not. To me, that is not BAD, not at all, but it has been done, and done well. While the merged psyches has been somewhat done in Mummy: The Resurrection, Geist feels like a very different game. I would not mind some system for sorting out stuff between Geist and Sin-Eater, but would not want it to be a major focus as really if anything Geist is somewhat more novel, with it not being an innately good or innately bad thing. Not wholly original, but not what has been done as much, at least by OPP.

                                                        Now, something for that if your Synergy gets especially low (assuming a 2e uses Synergy or something like it) might work pretty well, but it would be a consequence of that. I don't want it to be totally off the table, but I don't want to retred ground tread by Wraith or other games.
