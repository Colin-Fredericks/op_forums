                    Omenseer: Not really, beyond don't write it at once and, if possible, have other pairs of eyes go over it behind you.

                    Quiet session. Jasmine’s player was under the weather, so Doc’s log has the actual events of the session. It was short regardless, Jasmine and Simon both being missing and Doc’s player was delayed getting there. Still, enjoy.

                    Session CIV

                    Doc’s Log

                    Doc’s Log: 0145 Hours, Sunday, October 24, 2010

                    We canvassed the rest of the hotel before finally deeming it safe. Then everything was loaded up, including our three new friends, and we headed back to the Bluffs. We got here around 0100 Hours and the whole house emptied to help us unload.

                    Gretchen managed to ambush me as I came in with my last load. I hadn’t forgotten she wanted to ask me something, but this didn’t bode well. And as usual, Gretchen lived up to her reputation. Her question, cleaned up enough that I will dain to write it, regarded if Asian women were...situated differently than caucasian women. I blinked for longer than usual trying to keep from laughing or getting pissed off. I opted for a different approach and asked if she had ever been with another woman before? She hadn’t, which honestly surprised me a little, so I told her to get back to me after she had. Then I walked away.

                    While Rose gave our new tenants the grand tour, I went to my room and called Noriko. She should be here soon, which will be very nice. Assuming Gretchen doesn’t say something incredibly unfortunate.

                    Doc’s Log: 1000 Hours, Sunday, October 24, 2010

                    It’s incredibly relaxing to be home again. I left Noriko in bed around 0800 Hours and went to meditate. That was long overdue. After several false starts, I was finally able to center myself after about an hour. Then I went back to coax the sleepy purring woman out of bed and down to breakfast.

                    Hopefully today continues on this way. I don’t know of anything we need to do before we talk with Becky later. Plus I get to hang out with Noriko and be lazy all morning.

                    Doc’s Log: 1500 Hours, Sunday, October 24, 2010

                    Around 1400 Hours, Bright Eyes came to ask me about getting some tattoos. Noriko didn’t really know I could do that, so we all sat down and talked through the process. I knew that Bright Eyes used tattoos and body modification to mark major events in her life so I was interested in helping. I was a little surprised at what she told me however.

                    My acceptance of the split between Nightshade and I removed a huge weight I didn’t realize I was carrying. And after we killed the creature that tried to wield my anger like a weapon, I noticed how much easier it was to keep the edge off of my rage. I also realized how dramatic I get when I’m writing about a fight after reading over my entries from the hotel...but that’s an issue to work on for another time. The point is, I don’t feel the constant blade of white hot rage searing against my nerves all the time now. So when Bright Eyes came to me with the idea to use the Mpatapo to represent the events at the hotel, I was pretty impressed and a little moved. The African knot was a symbol of reconciliation, of peacemaking after strife. This she wanted on her forearm. The other request came with pictures for reference. She wanted three rings, all linked. These she wanted on her upper left arm. The forearm was where she remembered important ghostly events, but the upper arm was for major personal life events. It was then that I realized one of the rings was hers. She admitted to me that she, Nightshade, and Karen had handfasted last night and planned to have a formal marriage ceremony sometime in the future. That was a very big deal and I was honored both that she told me and that she wanted me to ink the tattoo. I immediately got my sketch pad out and marked off a space to size my template. Once I was satisfied with my sketches, I showed them to Bright Eyes. Noriko took the whole process in with interest but said very little. I’m a little bit of a perfectionist so it took a little longer than it could have, but Bright Eyes was very happy with my work. I don’t think Noriko realized I could draw.

                    Doc’s Log: 1945 Hours, Sunday, October 24, 2010

                    Nightshade called Becky, much to Karen’s irritation, around 1500 Hours to setup a meeting at her townhouse. We met at 1800 Hours and confirmed that there were no more ghosts in the Hidden Hillside Hotel. She said that she would talk to her aunt and that after some time went by with no more hauntings, she would pay us. Six months sounds like forever for payment, but I can understand the desire to be sure.

                    We came back and I informed the Krewe that I had a plan for a new picture, one that I wanted to give to the hotel. I plan to sketch each of the ghosts which had been trapped there separately with room to include their names under the image. The images would go from left to right with the ‘20s first, then ‘40s, and finally the ‘60s. Of course, Troy would be in the center of all the images. In this way, the ghosts that were the most closely tied, by choice or otherwise, could always be remembered. They could keep the picture at the front desk so everyone could see it at check in and check out. Everyone thought this was a great idea so I’ll need to look into getting a proper drafting board and any other equipment I might need to do this the right way. Then I’ll ask Snowflake for a custom frame before I donate it.


                    Jasmine’s Diary

                    Sunday, October 24, 2010, Afternoon

                    It is wonderful to be back home again. I’m pleased that we were able to resolve things there so handily, though I do still worry that the seed pod the outsider planted in that woman is germinating somewhere even now. I suppose we will have to try and track her down somehow, though I haven’t the foggiest notion how we might go about that. Perhaps Becky might be able to help. Sadly, Karl is not back from Cold Harbor yet. He should be getting back in a few hours, and after everything I’ve gone through over the past few days, I’m looking forward to a good night’s sleep with him beside me. I suppose I am feeling a bit lonesome right now, but aside from that I’m simply elated over helping all of the ghosts who were trapped in the hotel. It’s not often I feel this content and…happy…though the feeling is becoming more common now that I’m in love with Karl.

                    We did not return to Dark Haven Bluffs alone. Troy, Sandra and Wynona all returned with us, as they were the only ghosts who did not pass on. We couldn’t exactly leave them at the hotel since we had promised to rid the place of its haunting, but I confess that I am unhappy about adopting them all. Despite that, it somehow fell to me to show them around the house and get them situated in their new home.

                    I don’t really mind Troy. He is earnest and charming, and I like him immensely despite myself. It would be best if he would move on and return to the karmic cycle, but his company is welcome, and I think it will be nice to have another man in the household.

                    Sandra and Wynona are another matter. Wynona is eccentric and amusing, but she easily annoys me. Even a large house like Dark Haven Bluffs can become crowded, especially when it is full of so many bewildering personalities. Thanks to Tavia, Gretchen, and Chandra, things are already frustratingly chaotic and distracting at times, and I fear…no, I know…that Wynona will only make things worse.

                    Sandra is a completely different matter. I’m upset that she is moving in with us because she should have passed on already when we destroyed the outsider. Unfortunately, her blossoming interest in Natalie has anchored her here, or so it seems. I know that Natalie did not intend to awaken such feelings in Sandra, but she did, and now Sandra’s lingering on pointlessly. I feel sorry for her, and I am annoyed with the situation, but since Sandra is drawn to Natalie, there’s very little I can do about it. I can only hope that Natalie takes responsibility and helps resolve Sandra soon.

                    I prepared rooms for our new guests and helped them settle in, a process that included all the standard warnings and an extensive explanation of the rules, which I’m sure Wynona found quite tiresome. If she ignores what I said and wanders into something dangerous, she only has herself to blame. Anyway, I put all the ghosts in rooms on the fourth floor. Troy occupies a room on the west wing, across the hall from the Foreigner Eating Bedroom. Sandra and Wynona are in the east wing, Sandra in the back of the house with a room that opens onto the small widow’s walk there, and Wynona across the hall in the front of the house. Hopefully they will be comfortable there.


                    Natalie’s Tweets

                    -I am no longer alone, physically, spiritually, or emotionally.

                    -I am happy.

                    -<3

                    End Session CIV

                    Not much to add here. Should have Jazzy back tonight. Sadly still no Simon for awhile but he still plans to be back when he can.

                        Back on track with Geist. This session was largely planning, but still, let’s check in on the Middle Shadows.

                        Session CV

                        Doc’s Log

                        Doc’s Log: 1130 Hours, Monday, October 25, 2010

                        Meditation went well this morning and I was on my way only half an hour later. After breakfast, I went into the shop to confirm everything was on schedule for Nightshade’s birthday present. I guess with everything that’s been going on I forgot to mention it. I located a foundry that was willing to custom forge to my specs. So I have a silver-plated athame with a poison ring well in the pommel. Shifting the cross guard will allow whatever is in the pommel to reach the blade through channels in the hilt.

                        Now that I’m satisfied the shipping dates are accurate, I’m going to clean up the tattoo stations for Bright Eyes.


                        Doc’s Log: 1950 Hours, Monday, October 25, 2010

                        Cupcake found something called Craig’s List today. She was trying to find ways online to track the mysterious Cindy. I’ve heard of Craig’s List, but it’s not something I would have any reason to deal with. Nightshade was working on getting some leads as well, but she is more accomplished online so she has gotten a little farther.

                        I brought up the Underworld trip that needs to be pending. I think I surprised Rose with that. But after all that’s happened at the hotel, The Dog and I are feeling closer. Maybe not much more in sync, but closer. I’m very interested in checking out this pirate ship in the Underworld too. Rose mentioned that she needed to talk with Mr. Ox. I think she plans to burn some Hell Money with a message for him tonight. Then tomorrow she is going to purchase our scuba gear. I suggested that she get 10 sets. She looked at me like I was nuts, but I pointed out that we could possibly want to take friends with us for non-mission based events. Things that involve fun. She took the joke quietly but agreed to arrange for 10.

                        Chandra approached me just after dinner. She told me that she wanted a navel ring and asked if I could do the piercing. I told her she just needed to pick out the stud and whatever she wanted to use once the piercing healed.

                        With scuba gear coming and Nightshade’s birthday in two days, I realized that I need to get the van together ASAP.


                        Doc’s Log: 0930 Hours, Tuesday, October 26, 2010

                        I’m really not sure where the night went. I guess I just fell asleep like normal people do.

                        I can tell we’re back home again though. Meditation took an hour this morning. Complacency breeds contempt, I guess. Everyone is falling back into their routines now too. It really feels like we were away for weeks, but it was only a weekend.

                        Chandra found me after breakfast and asked about the navel ring. We agreed to meet here after she can Cupcake get back from shopping for the stud and final piece. Until then, I’m going to be getting some detailing and fine tuning down on the van.


                        Doc’s Log: 1745 Hours, Tuesday, October 26, 2010

                        I was crawling over, around, under, and through the van until late this afternoon before Cupcake and Chandra got home. I got cleaned up then we headed over to the shop. She was surprisingly nervous for someone that can remove her own head. Since they were out all day, we brought home take out for dinner.

                        Rose had more news and it wasn’t good. Mr. Ox is looking out for us again. He let us know that Bone Dry has summoned four very powerful spirits of vice and bound them into bodies. After what we just finished, more vice creatures almost gave me flashbacks. To make it worse, these are made more dangerous for having bodies. These aren’t normal spirits and Mr. Ox said that it was both difficult and risky for Bone Dry to deal with them. That suggests that he is no longer underestimating us and is looking for a fight. They also have magic summoning circles in several locations to which they can simply teleport if contacted. The four spirits don’t know everything, so if we kill communications first, then damage the circles, they will have to learn something is wrong, then travel like everyone else. Not perfect, but it buys us some time to wipe out the book shop. That has easily taken priority over the Underworld trip for now.

                        We’ll need to send Rose in invisibly to damage the circle, while we cut any other forms of communication, then level the place. I definitely want one of the books to study before we torch everything, but then we have to get the hell out

                        Another interesting note from Rose today is that she found a little girl that can see the truth, or at least, can see what people are. She ran up to Rose after Mr. Ox left and told her not to go near that man as he was made of people. That was pretty terrifying and I’m glad I didn’t look any harder. Still, I know how hard it is to be a kid and see things that no one else can. Her mother is a student at AMC and apologized for her daughter’s imagination. Rose tried to convince her that she should listen to her child when she says things like that. Unsurprisingly, the mother led her child away from the woman suggesting monsters were real. Rose really has no business speaking to normal people.

                        Anyway, so our week looks mostly full. Today was get the scuba gear. Tomorrow is Nightshade’s birthday so we will be celebrating her life for most of the day, as well as some basic scuba training. Then once everyone has recovered, we’ll have more in depth training and a book burning of sorts on Thursday. But it will probably be Friday in Hong Kong so that just jumbles everything up. Now I need to see what everyone else is doing tonight.


                        Jasmine’s Diary

                        Monday, October 25, 2010, Afternoon

                        I called Rita last night while I was waiting for Karl to get home. She and Jean are doing fine, and are positively giddy about being in love. She seems to be in control of her urges now, which is good, but I suggested that we continue meditating with tai chi in the mornings.

                        Karl was surprised to see me home already, but very pleased. It was nice to spend a night at home snuggled up with him in our room after all we’ve been through lately. I think it’s the soundest sleep I’ve had in weeks.

                        When I went in for Jean’s training session this morning, I found that he was just as happy as Rita about their relationship, though there is one thing that worries him. Rita keeps trying to work up the courage to tell him what she really is, and she’s hinted that she has something to talk to him about, but never gets around to it. I think it’s probably good for her the ease into things, but Jean’s becoming anxious about whatever it is she has to tell him. I told him not to worry. Rita’s a wonderful girl, and she’ll tell him whenever she’s ready. I don’t think it helped, but maybe he’ll at least be patient with her.

                        Since Natalie’s birthday is on Wednesday, I went out after work to pick up a nice collection of sheet music from the 20’s and 30’s that I think she will like. She enjoys playing piano pieces from that era, so I hope she will find it comforting. Afterward, I returned to campus to see if I couldn’t research Cindy, the hunter in whom the tree outsider from the hotel implanted a seed pod. She seemed to be ex-military, but I didn’t have any luck searching through recent newspapers. Perhaps the others will know of a better approach.


                        Monday, October 25, 2010, Late Night

                        Karl and I went out swimming this evening. It was wonderful. We ended up in our secret undersea grotto and spent some time there enjoying one another’s company and making love. We were a bit late getting back for dinner, but Alexia wasn’t cross with us.

                        Over dinner, the krewe discussed our next plans. Doc is eager to sort things with Pup Pid in the Underworld. I already have my arrangement with the Lighthouse Keeper, but the others need places to stay as well. Alexia seemed to assume she could stay at my place in the lighthouse whenever she reaches the point when she needs to spend time in the Underworld, and I certainly don’t have a problem sharing with her. But I can’t really host everyone, particularly since my deal with the Lighthouse Keeper applies only to me. Doc seems very eager to resurrect Pup Pid’s sunken pirate ship and turn it into a haven, and I would like to rid the seas of the pirate creature, if only to make things easier on the Lighthouse Keeper.

                        To that end, I’ll be purchasing SCUBA gear for all of us tomorrow. I have experience SCUBA diving, so I’ll need to give the others a bit of training if they are going to use the equipment to explore the Underworld shipwreck. We will take care of that this week.

                        I also needed to get in touch with Uncle Ox, so I performed an ancestor honoring ritual and burned some hell money in order to summon him. I suspect he will show up some time tomorrow.


                        Tuesday, October 26, 2010, Afternoon

                        If I didn’t know any better, I’d think Uncle Ox likes seeing me in my swimsuit. He showed up at the Albertus Magnus swimming pool again today, patiently waiting for me to finish working with Jean. I went to speak with him, and he shared some very important information with me.

                        Bone Dry has summoned four powerful spirits to defend his assets. They represent various sins, and are violent and dangerous. They can kill people to become more powerful for a time, so if they know we are coming, they will murder as many people as they can to prepare. We cannot allow that. Fortunately, the spirits are not omnipresent. They move between Bone Dry’s various operations, so if we are careful, we can avoid them, at least for the time being. However, Uncle Ox warned me that there is a mystic circle in the warehouse in Hong Kong where Bone Dry is publishing his false immortality manuals, and the four spirits can transport themselves to the circle very quickly if there is trouble there. That means we will need to damage and deactivate the circle before we attack and find a way to interfere with or cut off any phones that are there so no one can call for help.

                        If the four spirits are beaten, they will return to shadow and must be summoned and bound again if Bone Dry wishes to retain their services, and Uncle Ox assures me that is not something that is easy for Bone Dry to accomplish. The fact that he summoned them in the first place implies that he is scared and desperate. And while it is possible to destroy Bone Dry’s control over them, that would free them to rampage throughout the world, and we cannot allow that either.

                        I asked how we would know the spirits when we saw them, and Uncle Ox simply told me that we would know. They reek of death and though they look mostly human, they will be unmistakable.

                        After Uncle Ox left, a little girl approached me. Her name was Lucy Johnson. She warned me that the man I had been talking to was a really a scary monster. I was shocked that she could see him as he really was, or at least something close to it, and even more shocked that the girl was handling it so well. I suspected that she must see things like this from time to time, and my suspicion was confirmed when her mother, Alice, arrived and apologized for her daughter’s vivid imagination. I tried to tell Alice that some people see things others do not, and that she should listen to her daughter when she speaks of the things she sees. That really wasn’t the right thing to say, and Alice hurried Lucy away. At least Lucy seemed to trust me. If she ever sees anything really scary, I hope she’ll find a way to let me know.

                        I discussed Uncle Ox’s information with the others over dinner, and I think we’ve decided to eliminate Bone Dry’s publishing house in Hong Kong before we go to the Underworld.


                        Footnotes


                        Surfboards

                        Sara and I went to the SCUBA and surf shop to pick up our order of SCUBA gear this afternoon after I returned from work. We took her station wagon since my Beetle doesn’t have room for all the gear.

                        While there, I talked to the clerk about surf boards. I used to surf quite a bit in Taiwan, often with Richard. Both my board and his are in storage at my father’s home, but I thought it would be nice to get Karl a board so that he and I can go surfing at Green Bay or Daxi or any of the other good surf beaches in Taiwan. I spent about an hour discussing the details and placed an order before I left. I told Sara to keep it a secret; I plan to surprise Karl with the surfboard as a gift! I know he has surfed before, but only a little. He seemed eager to give it a try when I suggested we should go surfing some time, though, so I think he will appreciate having a board of his own.

                                        We return to our Krewe, in which they pause to celebrate a birthday and deal with fallout from matchmaking..

                                        Session CVI

                                        Doc's Log

                                        Doc’s Log: 1000 Hours, Wednesday, October 27, 2010

                                        I didn’t go out on patrol last night and I didn’t call Noriko over because I needed to be available at midnight just in case. Today is Nightshade’s 30th birthday. Though things got a little tense, nothing happened that they needed medical attention to correct, so I call it a win.

                                        Once I felt things were as normalized as it could be, I went to bed. Today is a big day after all. I was up by 0700 Hours and meditation was a breeze. By 0800 Hours, I was downstairs to make sure no one needed help with anything. We were all assembled by 0900 Hours and the birthday party started. It was surreal that Nightshade was willfully awake at that time of day on her birthday, but she seemed in good spirits.

                                        Cupcake made the Strawberry Birthday Breakfast as part of her present. Tons of crepes and strawberries with everything you can think of to add if we wanted. I even “invented” a new Strawberry Shortcake character. The happy morning conversation was mostly directed to and about Nightshade and Strawberry Deathcake came up. Someone mentioned a doll with batwings. I declared that the gothic character would have to be Blackberry Tart, in full “traditional” goth gear with the batwings and all. This I think set the tone for the day.

                                        My present is in a black faux wood box with a crimson bow tied around it. I think everyone else is getting their presents and bathing suits together. We’ll be shoving off in about an hour so I guess I should gather up my own clothes for the party.


                                        Doc’s Log: 2245 Hours, Wednesday, October 27, 2010

                                        The party’s being going all day and everybody is happy. Hopefully things continue that way, but the phone call Rose got has kind of threatened that. But that happened last so I’ll get to it.

                                        Once we got out on the yacht, the party actually started. Before the dancing and the drinking started too much, Cupcake handed everyone a tall, fluted champagne glass. Inside was a blood red mixed drink that smelled of strawberries. We learned that she had made a Chocolate Strawberry margarita. So long as Nightshade approved, it would be named after her and sold in Cupcake’s shop. That was pretty awesome and Nightshade happily approved after drinking it. The rest of the presents and several of the bathing suits came out after that. I gave my present and Nightshade’s eyes got wide. I’m pretty sure she liked it. Also pretty sure Karen wanted to steal it. That just means I did good. Tavia and family got her some custom made black leather thigh high boots with stylized symbols running down the side. Bright Eyes had been working on several herbal teas recently as she’d gotten several things from my shop. Different teas were for different things. That and teas are classy. Rose found some ‘20s sheet music on piano. I honestly don’t remember the titles but it made Nightshade happy, so that’s all that matters.

                                        Presents opened, everyone who hadn’t changed into their bathing suits already, did so. Scuba practice began with Rose fighting not to chastise the alcohol consumption as she helped everyone understand how the gear worked. We learned how to wear the equipment as well as how to maintain it. I quietly noted that Rose made sure it took a little more than half an hour to explain all this. I didn’t mention it, but I was amused that she still followed the 30 minutes after food before swimming rule. Once she was satisfied with our understanding of the tanks, we hit the water. Rose was being overly cautious, sending us down in pairs, but it did highlight that she was a good teacher and paid attention to details. Screwing this up for normal people can kill them. I had some very rudimentary scuba experience from the Navy, but it had been years and I never got the chance to practice. This was very helpful and we were glad for Rose to show us how it all worked. The party continued as different people took turns going back down and others just partied on the boat. No one got more than tipsy since Cupcake planned to take us out for dinner as well.

                                        We got back to Dark Haven Bluffs around 1700 Hours to clean up. Our reservations were for 1900 Hours and it was Italian that was Cupcake approved. I called Noriko over and let her know that we were getting a little dressed up for dinner. Having another beautiful woman in the party didn’t upset anyone, and we had enough room so I think Cupcake assumed she was coming anyway. The food was great and the company excellent. About midway through the third course, Rose got a call from Jean. She was trying to be nonchalant about it, but we all realized that something was wrong. When she got off the phone, Rose apologized and said she had to leave. I knew she was going to check on Jean, so I assumed it had something to do with Rita. I asked if they needed any help. Rose knew immediately I was offering first aid, but said she’d call if things were bad. I haven’t heard anything but Rose still isn’t bad either. I’m going to get back to the party now, but hopefully I’ll know more soon.


                                        Doc’s Log: 0100 Hours, Thursday, October 28, 2010

                                        Well in good news, Nightshade made it through her birthday without any overt breakdowns or suicide attempts. Her party was fun and she was surrounded by what she calls her family. She even talked to Belladonna at one point. In that respect, today was a smash hit.

                                        The bad news is that Rita went all Jaws on Jean. If that were the only problem, then things would just be awkward. Jean is alive and will even keep his leg, thanks to some kind of healing bear from the Spring Court. The problem is, he’s in shock and from all the descriptions I’m hearing, is too terrified of the situation to run. I mean to learn that your girlfriend is a shark monster while having sex, have most of your leg eaten off, have your Olympic swim coach come and tell you everything will be okay, then have a bear some and use magic to heal your leg. Rita and Rose were worried that he would want to break up with her. I don’t understand why he hasn’t run screaming (literally or figuratively) into the night. I mean, okay. There is a chance Noriko could snap and do something like that, but I knew that going in. Rita did this because she had sex. She told us that was her concern from being a Siren back in the Hedge. And Jean was just a normal guy, not a Sin-Eater, not a vampire, not a nothing. Jean Q. Mortal. This is why we shouldn’t let ghosts and humans mix. If she had told him beforehand and enchanted him like Grin did to Rose, that would be on him for still going along with it. Karen knew what she was getting into when she came into the house, but we at least taught her to use magic. I was (and still am) not happy about all this, so I called Grin to get an opinion from someone that’s dealt with it before. He told me that Jean was strong willed and that was a good sign he’d be fine as long as Rita didn’t eat him. I just stared at the phone for a second before handing it to Gretchen while she and Tavia were banging pans.

                                        I had to walk away after that. Noriko came with me so I asked her if I was the one who was crazy. She knew I wasn’t joking or happy so she thought about what to say for a few seconds. Finally she said I was crazy but as someone who goes crazier the longer they go without killing bad people, she might not be the best person to ask. I sighed and hugged her. That was fair. I just have to remember that every single Changeling is completely insane and work from that baseline. After that, I found some string and decided I’d had enough bullshit for the night. Now I’m going to use that string and not think about Changelings or anything else other than Noriko for a while.


                                        Doc’s Log: 1145 Hours, Thursday, October 28, 2010

                                        When she finally opened one eye the morning, Noriko purred good morning. I was already up and about to go meditate. She opened the other eye and asked me how meditation worked. I wasn’t expecting that, so I took her to the Meditation Room with me. I’d never led a meditation before so that was I a little awkward but after about an hour, we both found our center.

                                        We got cleaned up and came down for breakfast. We were later than usual, but no one was upset. I learned from Rose that Jean was still alive, even after he spent the night with Rita. Now I’m going to spend the rest of the morning and early afternoon waxing and polishing the van, then stocking the minibar. Once everyone else is home or awake, we’ll go back out for more intense scuba training.

                                        Rose stopped out in the carriage house while I was working to let me know Jean was “better.” I don’t think she understands why I’m not pleased by this. Especially since she said now that Jean knows about Rita, he wants to protect her from her Keeper. That boy has completely snapped and has no idea what he’s suggesting. Rose is just thrilled that true love has prevailed. She was not at all amused when I mentioned Stockholm Syndrome however.


                                        Jasmine’s Diary

                                        Wednesday, October 27, 2010, Evening

                                        Natalie’s birthday was today, and we all went out to celebrate with a party on the yacht. Since Natalie likes strawberry things, Alexia made her a special alcoholic strawberry cocktail, which she plans to serve in her restaurant as a regular item named the Natalie Knight. Alexia is very sweet and thoughtful, though I didn’t know her pastry shop had a liquor license. Doc gave her a beautiful custom-made ceremonial dagger that he had a hand in designing. Sara gave her a collection of hand-made herbals teas. Compared to all of those gifts crafted with loving care, my own gift of sheet music seemed rather pathetic in comparison.

                                        We spent some time practicing SCUBA diving while we were out on the ocean. Karen was quite good at it. We are getting ready to visit one of Alexia’s upscale Italian restaurants for dinner. Karl and I are good to go, but of course, Natalie and Karen are taking a very long time getting ready, so I suppose we will have to wait a while longer.


                                        Wednesday, October 27, 2010, Late Night

                                        At dinner, I got a call from Jean. He sounded shaky, and told me that he had injured himself and wouldn’t be able to train for a couple of days. Of course I immediately suspected that something had gone wrong with Rita, and my fears were confirmed when I got her on the phone and she explained that she had lost control and bitten Jean’s leg rather badly. I excused myself from dinner with a quick word of explanation to the others and rushed to Rita’s flat.

                                        Rita had not just bitten Jean, she had eaten a large chunk of his thigh. She was quite apologetic and was doing everything she could to make Jean feel comfortable and to keep him from sliding further into shock. I simply wondered how she had managed to eat so much of his leg without Jean beating her off. I did what I could for Jean’s injury, and Rita assured me that she had called other help, a changeling who could perform miracles of healing. I asked if the damage could be completely repaired, because it was so extensive that any normal healing would leave severe muscle damage that would ruin Jean’s professional career as a swimmer. Rita thought it would be as good as new.

                                        With that out of the way, the matter of their relationship remained. Jean understandably frightened of Rita, but he didn’t want to leave her, and she certainly didn’t want him to leave. I think she felt that if he left and was able to think through things without talking them over with her, he would become even more frightened and leave her. I wanted to point out the fact that she had nearly killed him. It all seemed like a terrible situation and I worried that I had made the wrong decision by introducing them to one another.

                                        But I chose not to say anything at the moment. I always seem to muddle things up when I start talking to people who aren’t ghosts, and I didn’t want to ruin things between Jean and Rita if they weren’t ruined already. After all, Jean was obviously ensorcelled and could see Rita as she really was, and yet he still seemed attracted to her, so my geist and I felt we had no choice but to put our faith in true love, which was much easier for the Bride than it was for me.

                                        Once the healer arrived and all seemed well, I left with a few words of warning to Rita. However, I was concerned about Jean. I worried that he was only staying because he was frightened that Rita wouldn’t let him leave. I returned to the manor, where I was greeted by Doc and Sara, both of whom were quite worried about the situation. I explained what had happened, and immediately realized that was a bad idea. Doc was furious, and made it quite clear that he was inclined to kill Rita because she was too dangerous. I tried to tell him that she has been struggling very hard to overcome her problems and has been making very good progress, the night’s setback notwithstanding. Perhaps my faith in true love is misguided, but I wanted to give Rita and Jean a chance. But, Doc was rightly concerned that we might be gambling with Jean’s life, so I decided to return to Rita’s flat unseen to spy on her and Jean.

                                        It seems I needn’t have been worried. Jean was healed as good as new, and he and Rita were in bed together, so I quickly left them to it. The Bride chided me a bit, all but saying, “I told you so” as we drove home.


                                        Thursday, October 28, 2010, Morning

                                        I called Jean this morning to make sure everything was okay, and he sounded fine. He was still a bit dazed and confused, though I got the distinct impression that had more to do with an amazing night of passion than any further mishaps. I suppose I should trust Rita. She is my friend, and I need to be supportive of her now even more than before. She’s finally happy, which means a lot, so long as Jean is safe. And Jean seems to have accepted what Rita is and all that entails. Indeed, Rita says that it’s all she can do to convince him not to go marching into the Hedge to protect her from her Keeper. He’s so gallant!

                                        I reported everything to the others over breakfast. Doc still thinks Rita’s too dangerous, but he’s not planning anything drastic, thank goodness. It did occur to me, however, that Doc and I are similar in a way. We both have triggers that make us willing to kill. They may be different—he is more likely to kill for the living, and I for the dead—but we are more alike in that resolution than I think either of us would like to admit.

                                        Which reminds me, of course, that I need to set up a meeting with Stanley’s aunt soon.

                                            Session CVII

                                            Doc’s Log

                                            Doc’s Log: 0015 Hours, Friday, October 29, 2010 (Hong Kong Time)

                                            The extended scuba lessons were put on hold in favor of shutting down Ye Olde Book Shoppe of Damnation. We didn’t want to let Bone Dry get any more occultists tricked into becoming Hopping Vampire slaves before we could get things moving. The revelation of the vice spirits coming in as backup left a bad taste in everyone’s mouth. As such, we left around 1130 Hours New Haven time. I brought a cheap plastic gas can as we all walked out from the wall of a very detailed mausoleum wall. An emaciated blonde vampire was reading in the solitude before we arrived. After watching people walk through a burial wall, she bolted. I tried to call after her in Chinese, but I realized that my limited Mandarin was really unhelpful in a place where they normally speak Cantonese.

                                            Once we walked out of the mausoleum, we could see the factory used for the book shop just down the street. Before we could walk very far, Rose stopped short and looked up into a tree on the sidewalk. She took a pair of keys from the lower branches. I couldn’t read the script, but she translated for us. Bad Luck Farmer. Mr. Ox’s handiwork. At least we know we can count on him.

                                            Nightshade scanned as much of the factory as she could through her Boneyard. There were four Jiang Shi and what looked like a prostitute with a cell phone spread between the two main rooms. The prostitute actually posed the greatest risk because we couldn’t cut a line for her cell phone. Rose left us the keys then phased through the wall invisibly to finish scouting the shop. In a basement concealed by trapdoor, was the summoning circle. While she was looking, Bright Eyes and I realized we could solve all our problems with Pyre-Flame. We couldn’t curse the prostitute until we saw her and that was too long to wait, but we could curse one of us. That would kill the lights, the cell phone, and any security systems we couldn’t see. My Pyre-Flame Shroud would still allow us to see in the blackout and we could move from there. The major downside to this was that the ear curls would also be cut out. Rose also noted that the prostitute had two Deathmarks. They were both signs that she had been drained of blood but that was odd. Either way, we all made sure we knew the plan before our communication channel went down. Bright Eyes would curse Cupcake just before we entered while Rose would tackle the vampire with the cell phone. We could then come in the front door and deal with the Jiang Shi with our full group. Rose got in position in the basement so we could cut the phone line as she disrupted the circle. Then Bright Eyes cursed Cupcake and we rushed the door.

                                            The sounds of crashing water greeted us as we moved. When we got in, there was no prostitute but a purple winged monster flapping in the air. Three of the Jiang Shi were wet and on the far side of the room. I announced in my broken Mandarin to surrender or die, as we were still hoping to save as many of the Jiang Shi from slavery as possible. With that, I hurled a ghost flame fireball into a wooden chair. It sparked but fizzled due to the curse. That was enough for the three Jiang Shi though. The look of terror in their eyes told me they were in full frenzy as they bolted for the door. The purple thing turned out to be a shape changing Filipino vampire called an Aswang. I must have read about them while researching Chandra or something. Anyway, she didn’t seem impressed with my little spark as she literally shrugged. The fourth Jiang Shi arrived but wasn’t able to get to us. The Aswang decided that I was annoying or a threat or just didn’t like my aftershave because she swooped in and hit me with some wicked claws. It stung a little, but I returned the favor by lighting her hand on fire for touching me. She shrieked as the Jiang Shi missed Bright Eyes. Rose rushed in and turned into her Shark Woman form. Thankfully Cupcake had grabbed the cell phone as Bright Eyes ended the curse. Nightshade immediately reported over the ear curl that someone was coming. Bright Eyes turned and ran for the door. I missed the Aswang with my hatefire just as a woman screamed something in Cantonese from outside. Rose translated that someone was rigged to explode. After that the ear curls went dead again but a rough and disturbing voice came to all of us that we could understand anyway.

                                            “Blowing the place up? Let me help!”

                                            Nothing happened immediately but the Aswang looked incredibly pissed off and flew through a window to escape. I flared up another spark to make the last Jiang Shi frenzy, then I headed outside. Rose was talking to the blonde vampire. Apparently the vice spirits had somehow controlled a random patsy to come here and kill us. The man wore a long coat to conceal 26 sticks of dynamite and a detonator. According to Nightshade, he hit the button after the voice came from his oddly open mouth but when nothing happened, the patsy was released. The poor man ran screaming down the street, ripping dynamite off as he went. I have since gathered that up for later use. I think I know of a great housewarming present for Bone Dry now coincidentally. I walked back inside and shoved the TNT into a duffle bag before joining them again.

                                            The vampire that helped us seemed just a little off and I suspected she was Nosferatu. Her name was Rotten Lily, which added to my suspicion. She told us that someone that sounded like one of the vice spirits killed all twelve Elders in a meeting he arrived, then named some nobody from Taiwan as Prince. I asked in my bad Chinese where the Sheriff was for all this, but she said he was one of the ones killed. It occurred to me that we could further weaken the vice spirits and Bone Dry by removing their hold on the local vampires. We agreed to go to Elysium and check it out, but not until we were finished here. I went back in and investigated the shop to make sure we weren’t going to miss anything. I found three complete books and a box of various children’s clothes. Looking at the box a moment longer, I realized that all the children were killed while they still wore the clothes. Downstairs, there were 16-30 round clips for an AK-47 waiting on a table next to the summoning circle. Those also went into the duffle bag. Then I told everyone to get out.

                                            I threw gas on all the equipment and all the walls before pouring a trail to the center of the floor. Then I turned the can over and let the gas pool. As I walked to the door, I struck a match and dropped it. Rotten Lily’s eyes grew wide when my shadow came through the door with a wall of flame rising behind me. I’m not sure if anyone else was aware, but my buttons had been pushed. The vice spirit double crossed the Aswang as she was obviously an outside merc, but more importantly, it tried to blow us up. I have died that way once. That was a very special moment, and not one that I will allow to be repeated. Now it’s personal.

                                            Doc’s Log: 0200 Hours, Friday, October 29, 2010 (Hong Kong Time)

                                            We stashed the duffle bag back at the mausoleum then went with Lily to Elysium. That was an interesting word to translate. I wonder which dialect of Chinese the Sheriff back home speaks? Anyway, we spotted a woman in a similar trench coat as the man at the shop. She was standing in front of the main door trembling with a look of terror in her eyes. We decided that Lily should hide until we came back for her. With that taken care of (and her not responsible for what we were about to do with the Masquerade), we decided that play time was over. Rose changed back into Shark Woman, Bright Eyes ignited her Pyre-Flame Caul before cursing herself, then I armored up. The lights, elevator, even cars in what looked like about a 60 yard radius just stopped. We walked up and ripped the dynamite off the woman who screamed and ran away. Then up to the 7th floor. Cupcake moved to the heavy steel door and spoke to it with her Industrial Marionette. Just as the massive lock slid back, we heard a window break inside. Rose moved so fast I almost didn’t see it. In what was almost one fluid motion, she dove through the window on our side of the door. As she floated amid the shards of broken glass, she shifted into a murder of crows that then spiraled around the building. I didn’t say anything at the time, but that might have been the most bad ass and gothic thing I have ever seen. About 10 minutes later, she came back up the stairs with a vampire in a arm bar. Bright Eyes dropped the curse again so we were all aware that we would be playing Good Cop, Bad Cop, Worse Cop. Guess which one I got. Apparently Rose changed back into her human form while in the air and landed on him like something out of the Crow. I could hear Nightshade’s pulse quicken. Rose and I could both speak to him while Bright Eyes was basically just trying to be intimidating. We didn’t ask for a name at first, but we did learn that he was only about 40 years old. I may have made a comment about him living beyond tonight would depend on how useful he was. Definitely on the weak end of the scale for Prince, given that worked. I think Lynne and Truman called them Neonates. Anyway, he told us the vice spirit he answered to called himself Mr. Smile but there was another one that always wore a red suit. We got the phone number for Mr. Smile, but then I barked enough Mandarin to get a list of all the businesses owned by Bone Dry in Hong Kong. Yang Gong seemed more than ready to do anything we asked at that point. Before we could capitalize on that realization however, he burst into flames in the chair which Rose planted him. Bright Eyes and I almost cursed him to put the flames out but realized it had to be Bone Dry’s supernatural powers, so Rose just grew her claws to put him out of his misery. There was a very pitiful look on her face as she ripped out the flaming vampire’s throat. We’re not very accustomed to mercy killings. But we didn’t have time to think much more about it either. Nightshade got our attention after we made sure the vampire’s corpse didn’t set the rest of the building on fire. From her vantage point at the broken window, she could see the Road to Hell was now one of the shops on the street below. Now that I’m done writing, we’re all going to go down and see what Mr. Ox has to say.

                                            End Session CVII

                                            I am not sure what the situation will be with Jasmine journals for a bit. The player is burned out on doing them right now. We should have some Natalie journals picking up some of the slack, and maybe something from Sara, her player is considering options.

                                            I was struck while double-checking rules how incredibly little damage falling and explosives do in New WoD. The bomber originally had a couple sticks of dynamite, which would have done nothing to the PCs if they went off. It’s not a complaint really, but the very rapid healing times (moreso than even much more gonzo games like Scion) and low damage from falls and explosions is somewhat boggling. But then, I am no expert on how damaging explosives really are in the real world.

                                            I was working with the Aswang from Wicked Dead, which seemed more sophisticated than the Antagonists ones.
