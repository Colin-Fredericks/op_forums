                                                            Originally posted by espritdecalmar
                                                            On the topic of reminiscing about Geist's past, I vaguely recall a post (by I want to say Dave Brookshaw) claiming that the game's original pitch looked quite different from the end result. Something about playing as angels maybe? Does anyone remember this?
                                                            I had only just started out as a freelancer when Geist 1e was written, and didn't work on it. So this is a second-hand anecdote. But having seen subsequent new game pitches, from Demon, to Beast (which ended up very different to the idea Matt McFarland originally pitched), to Deviant (which to this day I don't know whose idea it was - Iwas handed the advert and told "turn that into a game")...

                                                            I digress. But point is these games are only very rarely recognisable as the early sparks of ideas they grow from.

                                                            Anyhoos. The story is that Geist 1e was a mashup of several not-quite-accepted game ideas. One of which, the one that lead to Synergy, the bargain, etc, was being possessed by an entity which had saved you from death. That was first pitched as an angel, but when the game took a turn for the ghostly they became Geists.

                                                            To hear Travis tell it, the game had a lot more anachrotech / racing ghostly muscle cars in it in an early form, too.

                                                            Dave Brookshaw
