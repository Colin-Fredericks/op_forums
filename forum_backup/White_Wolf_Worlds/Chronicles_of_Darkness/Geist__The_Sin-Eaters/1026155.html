                              As noted in the Changeling kickstarter thread, it's a little dangerous to make stretch goals for a core rulebook, and here's why:

                              Originally posted by atamajakki View Post
                              The important thing is that as little of the stretch goals as possible needs to go into core, so as to delay it ideally not at all. It hurts me that it's the case, but most RPG companies have learned that lesson the hard way.
                              So, to avoid delaying the core, what kinds of things would you guys want in supplements of the game? Stuff that you'd like to be in the core, but the game could probably go out without?

                              For me, if the core game doesn't include them (and even if it did), I'd like a supplement with sample Krewes and their ideologies. We only really got one with Geist 1e, via the premade characters, and it was hard to work a new one without more examples. The rules were fine and all, but overall...it was confusing for me.

                              Also more Keys and Haunts, because options are fun.
