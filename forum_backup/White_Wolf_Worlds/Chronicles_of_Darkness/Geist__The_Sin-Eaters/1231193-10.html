                                Well, that was a really nice preview. Keys are creepy, Deathmasks are scary, Haunts are horrifying. I think In like the Geist Creation and Unleashing process, as it actually treat it as a distinct being, with wishes and needs of its own, while still bound to its Sin Eater. I do like the fact that I've predicted the "memento collection" trend that Sin Eater society has (I mean, it just looked so appropriate!), and that for Sin Eaters, being "Dead" is a Condition :P

                                I'm still not sure how I feel toward the fact that Sin Eaters don't actually have any "karma stat"- they can cheat, steal, murder and even eat other humans- when one of their Haunts actually require the last one- but as long as they don't eat other ghosts, no worries! Sure, it may cause Crises Points to their geist, but again, it only puts a greater emphasis that the responsible character in the due in an half mad ghost who can't even recall its own name, which means something. On the other hand, I assume that it does works well with the whole "the Bound are dead and soulless, inside and out", and that "death is freedom and celebration unbound by human soul" things which goes on.

                                Also- as any Deathmask can bestow a Reaper status upon a ghost, it means that Sin Eaters must be very careful about killing other Bound and their geists, or else an unsuspecting ghost may become a servant of the Chthonic Gods. Also, doesn't it give you the feeling of a non Sin Eater Krew which serves the gods of the Bellow and kills Sin Eaters in order to create more Reapers? Yikes.

                                Now I'm really eager to see the "non Sin Eater" Archetypes, and how they operate in the Bound society :P

                                                            It makes the books easier to use as references after the first time you read them; splats are at the front, Conditions are at the back.

                                                            The 2e book template goes Intro - splats - setting - rules for the supernatural beings in question - core storytelling system - antagonists - sample settings - storytelling - minor template - conditions
