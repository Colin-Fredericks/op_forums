            Bashing damage indicates a general slipping of religious attitudes, observant but distracted from the faith by material concerns.
            I think that the way to handle Bashing for Krewes is less specific sources of deliberate damage and more as a "Shit happened this week. Take 1B to the Krewe." Or one of your celebrants is having a really bad time with life things. If they don't get help, you'll take 1B, so it's up to you if you want to spend a scene on figuring that out.

            If I'm right (I'm just guessing), then I'd expect this to come up in the Storytelling chapter. It feels loosely equivalent to a scene where your vampire goes feeding at the local nightclub. The analogue for a vampire feeding or a werewolf hunting, in Geist, is "giving a shit about your congregation".

            The problem with my theory, of course, is that that damage goes away in two days so it doesn't really make sense as a penalty for failure.
