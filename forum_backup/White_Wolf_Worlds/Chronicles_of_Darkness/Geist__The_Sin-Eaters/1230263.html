                                      They've released a fiction anthology with each core book thus far, and I doubt they'll stop now so that's an add on instead of a stretch goal. Other fiction stretch goals will likely come in the form of separately released novellas rather than a second anthology.

                                      Jumpstart and companion are two extremely likely stretch goals. I'd be interested in the companion depending on what they put in it.

                                      I personally hope for a Guide to the Underworld book, but whether it's even a feasible source book depends both on how much of the core book is focused on the Underworld, and how much the Underworld has changed between editions. The Onyx Path will likely not just rerelease a new Book of the Dead if the old one is 95% compatible with second edition. But if they do release one, I hope it will contain at least half a chapter (or an equivalent amount of text spread out in the whole book) dedicated to how the other splats interacts with the Underworld.

                                      SNESferatu mentioned an Ancient Mysteries-type book. While I think that concept has been replaced with Dark Eras, I wouldn't mind a potential Guide to the Underworld to contain historical information about the Underworld. Dead Dominions change all the time and are not particularly interesting other than as an ST resource, but the Sundered World revealed a pretty dramatic change in the Underworld's geography and I'd like to see that explored more, including whatever changes the Underworld might currently be undergoing.

                                      A book focused on template variations and/or minor templates like Half-Damned and Heirs to Hell could be interesting. Depends on what they would include in it, and how well they handled it. My opinions about Half-Damned and Heirs to Hell are quite different so I'm not going to hype this kind of book like I would with a potential Guide to the Underworld book.

                                      Did I mention that I want a Guide to the Underworld book yet?
