                        Supernatural Angst is Angst about being a supernatural creature. Dem Bone's host has it, and that one week willed Sin-Eater whose geist bullies him into doing what it wants, probably has supernatural angst.

                        Any of the wretched who are still conscious? They've got supernatural angst.

                        A Sin-Eater who lives in a big city with a lot of murders and a lot of death, and who doesn't know the ceremonies to keep ghosts from bugging him at home, he might have a little angst until he learns the ceremony and picks up the skills to cope with occasionally stumbling across a place where somebody was murdered, but compared to the angst that a Vampire or a Werewolf or Promeathean might feel, that's a much easier thing to cope with.

                        The Sin-Eater who keeps dying and getting innocent people killed? He's got legitimate reason to angst

                        The vast majority of Sin-Eaters, however,do not have much legitimate reason to angst.                         There are already games about how much it sucks to be X-Supernatural creature. Leave Geist to be about accepting that death happens but that life is worth celebrating.

I can fix that, but you've got to let me tag along with you... Oh, I'll even give you some sweet super powers... Yeah, and you just have to go out and live... Maybe watch stuff burn every so often... Some ghosts might want you to help them, but there are ways to get out of that if you really don't want to, and there are benefits to helping them pass on. So, do we have a deal?"
