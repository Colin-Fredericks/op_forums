                    Originally posted by Aspel View Post

                    Roleplaying ignoring something is still roleplaying. You also don't lose Synergy for not doing what your Geist wants. It's also important to note that Geists themselves don't even have a reason to care about the strange newcomer.

I ignore it' when the Geist is harping about it constantly is kind of like saying 'oh, that need to eat thing I do to survive? I ignore it' - at a certain point it stops being a request and starts becoming a drive, a need in order to get some peace and quiet. This isn't your little brother asking for candy here, this is a voice in your head going 24/7 - there's only so much you can ignore it before it starts making it hard to focus.

Most synergy degeneration rolls are for doing things that go against the nature of your Geist - just like a mortal degeneration roll are for doing things (or seeing things) that go against the assumed moral nature of humans.

                    Don't get me wrong, I don't think it should be for just anything: maybe have a Geist become obsessed with protecting children and have Synergy loses for if their character were to find out that a child they ignored was hurt.

                        Here's a bullet list of what I want from a Geist Chronicle book/ Geist NWoD 2nd ed.
                        - 9 Manifestations, with one based off each stat. If your gonna do powers based off stats and hit seven of them, you might as well hit the other two.
                        - Archetypes and Thresholds having more game rules associated with them, beyond just roleplaying cues. Threshold gives you A Key. I'd like your achetype choice and threshold to do things like give you favored skills or attributes, keys, favored Manifestations, and a unique power and weakness.
                        - More done with the concepts of the dead Grande Krewes, perhaps something analogous to Mage Legacies, Vampire Bloodlines, and/or Promethean Athanors.
                        - More detail in the Antagonist chapters. Actual details on Chthonians, more details on Abmortals and Sacrosanct.
                        - THE ANTAGONISTS TO BE WRITTEN USING THE FINAL RULES SUCH THAT THEIR STAT BLOCKS MAKE SENSE AND CAN BE USED IN THE GAME (lol teh kerberoi can haz up to 5 dots of keys in three different keys KEYS DON'T HAVE DOT RATINGS RABBLE RABBLE RABBLE)
                        - A players Guide with additional options.
                        - A Storytellers Guide with expanded rules for antagonists and setting secrets.
                        - Actual rules for tier 2 and 3 Krewes. Actual rules for Tier 3 Krewe Benefits. Some Examples.
                        - At least three support books beyond PG and StG, one focusing on the Underworld (a better version of WOD:Book of the Dead an actually imprinted as a Geist book), one focusing on a major antagonist group (I don't care which one, just a full book on a major antagonist category like Sacrosanct or Abmortals) and a third book that just expands some aspect of the game, like a guide to Krewes, or a Setting Book (Pick a city, I don't know, have we used Boston? How about Miami? Whatever).
                        - Did I mention having the rules set be consistent through out the entire book?
                        - Manifestations that are useful for the Social Maneuvering rules. Like the Passion Key. Passion Shroud and Passion Caul offering various protections from social attacks, Passion Rage not dealing damage but breaking down doors like Hard Leverage on steroids.
                        - Mementos to get close to the same level of treatment as Gadgets in Demon (especially in the Players Guide).

                                                        Originally posted by Aspel View Post
                                                        Geists don't actually HAVE Anchors or Influences. There's really barely a bit of lip service paid to "they represent a specific kind of death".
                                                        You're confused, which is understandable.

                                                        Writers (mostly Dave) have discussed on prior occasions on the forums the way that Geist's notable ephemeral beings are tentatively modeled in the 2e system now that the ghost rules aren't a skeleton of a skeleton papered over with kludges. Part of that is that Kerberoi and Geister are modeled as higher-Ranking ghosts whose Influences are based on their Dominions (for the former) and particular forms of death like Death By Fire (for the latter), largely replacing the Influences that ghosts draw upon to manifest via the Anchor tag.

                                                        That, and the basic common sense extrapolation from 2e giving ghosts Influences into what is already known about Geist's metaphysics, is what people are talking about when they refer to these aspects of the system.

                                                        Also, our differences aside, I really must implore you to ease back from approaching the game as a mine of what-can-I-find-that-bugs-me-and-where-do-the-mechanics-contradict. It's not terribly productive or conducive to developing a feel for what the intended baseline milieu of the setting is, which is where 2e is liable to aim for.
