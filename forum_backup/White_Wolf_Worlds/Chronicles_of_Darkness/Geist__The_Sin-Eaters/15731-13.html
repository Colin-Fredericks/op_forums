    Doc’s Log

    Doc’s Log: 1600 Hours, Tuesday, November 9, 2010

    I did not see today going the way it has. Nothing says it can’t go nuclear tonight, but for the moment we feel pretty satisfied.

    Before we left to confront Star/rescue The Hair, Cupcake insisted that we bring sweets to the old women. Her thought was that it worked with Mallard so it should at least win us points. I reminded her that we were about to leave so we didn’t really have time to bake a cake. I went to ask Tavia is she could look after the twins while we were gone, but Karen seemed to be perfectly happen to keep them company. When I came back, the decision had been made. We had to stop at Sweet Surrender before heading for The Hair’s grandmother’s house. We were also taking Jaggling with us as she actually knew the way. I realized as we were heading out, and looking at my van, that I still needed to call Rawl. That also reminded me I needed to be a better shot before my next trip to Glenwood.

    We didn’t lose too much time waiting for Cupcake at her shop before we were back on the road, cookies and various pastries in tow. The Hair’s family home was very unassuming, though it was definitely well maintained. The group of us went to the door expecting to meet an elderly woman. Thus when a woman that may have looked like an exquisite 35 answered the door knowing that we had come to speak with The Hair before we admitted it, we were surprised. When we learned this woman of incredible beauty was actually Star, we were all silent for a moment. Whether that moment was spent in appreciation or not, depended on the Krewe member in question. Which is to say, FUCK Star is hot!

    She invited us in and seemed very grateful for the sweets. Apparently The Hair’s mother is Star’s daughter. She said this daughter was actually taking a nap, so I offered for us to keep our voices down so as not to wake her. Star made some amused joke about offering us doilies before leading us into a sitting room. She was going by Rhiannon these days. Thankfully, she both had an idea of what we were and had no interest in conflict. She told us up front that The Hair was having trouble returning to human form and thus did not want to be seen, but was absolutely there. She also apologized to Jaggling and noted that she probably suffered from shock after seeing The Hair while unprepared. Her opinion was that The Hair was doing fine but that the stress of frightening Jaggling was distracting her. We asked how she knew what we were as she too called us Ghostwalkers. Rhiannon told us as far as she knew, Banshee actually came from the Lower Depths. Her understanding was that perhaps a Geist or Kerberoi managed to return to the living world and spawned children. She even mentioned she knew two of our Keys, though she demured when we asked which ones. I think Nightshade almost swooned given the situation. Of course, that could also be because of the many sidelong glances Rhiannon sent her way.

    While we were all taken with Rhiannon’s beauty, at least a few of us were still focused enough to want to see The Hair. Since Nightshade and Bright Eyes were specifically concerning to The Hair, Rhiannon allowed me to go back and see her. Given her current image problem, I intentionally waited around the corner and spoke to The Hair until she agreed it was okay for me to look at her. My medical background seemed to calm her fears of rejection. The Hair’s features had become gaunt, almost skeletal with a deathly pallor, but she was still clearly the same person I knew before. If there was any question, her personality guaranteed her authenticity. I assured The Hair that neither Nightshade nor Bright Eyes would shun her for her appearance, and that she should know that all of us could look much worse depending on our powers. Rhiannon made some comment suggesting that Nightshade would possibly enjoy it. While I agreed, I kept that to myself. She was very concerned for what happened with Jaggling. I assured her that things would be okay. We also included her in the invitation made to Rhiannon to meet with the twins to help solve this unintended curse complication. After that, I confirmed to the Krewe that The Hair was alright and that we would see her when she was ready. We thanked Rhiannon for being so willing to help and make sure we could all keep in contact. We needed to speak with the twins before a meeting could be arranged.

    Before sending her on her way, I stopped Jaggling and showed her the Library. Then I asked if she had any questions. I could see volumes of queries flow over her blank eyes before she admitted she didn’t know where to start. I gave her an incredibly short crash course on what a Sin Eater is, so she would have some context and that seemed to satisfy her, or overwhelm her more, I’m not sure which. But either way, I think Jaggling, The Hair, and possibly Rhiannon could make great contacts for our Krewe. Finally, I sent Jaggling home after promising to let her know about any meeting that took place.

    By the time I joined the rest of the Krewe, they were explaining the situation to the twins. I drew a sketch of Rhiannon while we discussed the situation. Tracy was probably more interested than she should have been. Around 1500 Hours, the twins confirmed that they were willing to meet the Banshee Queen and try to work out at least a compromise. Neither of them were interested in killing people for power, even if they did awaken their inheritance. Since the Krewe was mediating, I suggested we meet at the Firehouse Church. Everyone agreed and the time was set for 2030 Hours.

    Once that was settled, I called Jaggling per my promise. Then I asked Timothy to walk with me. The look I got from Nightshade suggested she thought I was up to something, but I didn’t have time to consider that. If one or, more likely both, twins would awaken as mages, I wanted to speak with the more thoughtful and informed of the two. Timothy was duly impressed with the implications of what lay before him, but I couldn’t help but feel like he knew than he was letting on. I gave him what I think can be summed up as the “Just because you can, doesn’t mean you should” talk. Given the level of his potential powers, I noted that I couldn’t stand against him directly and that seemed to surprise him. Almost like he knew he would have powers but not on a level that could affect me. That at least assured me that he wasn’t making too many contingencies. I hate myself for this, but I’ve already thought of several ways to take both twins out should it become necessary. Hopefully it will never come to that and they will become the first mages we’ve encountered that are neither insane nor need to be put down.

    It’s nice to hope.

With any luck, there will be a decidedly non-business meeting later.

I am more inclined to trust the bean sidhes at this point, quite honestly, so perhaps we can get some new strictures set in place, fully laid out so there’s no accidental triggering. I think Israel is likely to be as much a danger to the twins as the rest of their dead relatives, should they come into their power. But we need time to confirm that and learn more about him. Hopefully, Rhiannon can help us with that.

                Doc’s Log: 2145 Hours, Tuesday, November 9, 2010

                WOOHOO! Mark one point for Hope!

                We just got back from what I think was the most productive meeting of supernaturals we’ve ever attended. The twins came with us and then we went to work. They had never seen the Firehouse Church and since we had to prepare, they got the grand tour. Rhiannon accompanied The cloaked and cowled Hair as they arrived right on time. Cupcake set up her refreshments table while I organized the most fair wait to have the seating arrangements.

                Ironically, when I was finished we had what looked like the last supper with the Krewe around me in the middle. We were meeting in the sanctuary and once together, I made introductions. Tracy looked terrified and refused to let go of Bright Eyes. I was afraid I’d have to amputate later. Timothy was as impressed as everyone else when he saw Rhiannon, but otherwise remained fairly calm. For her part, Rhiannon was very gracious. Jaggling sat next to The Hair. She tried to make up for panicking and going into shock earlier by asking The Hair if there was anything she needed, about every 15 seconds. The Hair was there, even though she couldn’t rebuild her face yet, to show how important this was and I think everyone appreciated the effort.

                I intended to act as moderator, though it turned out that, other than prompting Tracy a few times, everyone was happy to speak in turn. The Banshee Queen made it clear she had no desire to harm the twins, so long as they were not following in Israel’s footsteps. While thankful not to be marked for death, Timothy did voice his frustration at having a sword hanging over his head without a clear idea of what would cause it to drop. The problem here is that the original curse was met for Israel, therefore very little effort was put into clear boundaries, as mages will find ways around them. However, this is now a more delicate situation. Possibly more pressing is that Rhiannon is very concerned that awakening the twins’ powers will actually summon Israel, who may simply absorb them before unleashing his plans all over again. I planted the seed with the Krewe that maybe we could summon him. It’s not like we’ve never had to bind an incredibly powerful ghost before. Or dealt with mages for that matter. Rhiannon warned us that her attempts to summon Israel have always failed. We’re hoping that we have stronger methods we can bring to bear.

Tavia and Gretchen were offered as stewards of the library for either side to try and find more information. I was a little confused as to why Nightshade left me out of the equation, but also suggested that we all keep in contact and share information as we find it. Everyone left happy.

                Tracy and Timothy took Bright Eyes home, while The Hair went back to her apartment with Jaggling. I came back to The Bluffs to write this. Now I think I’m going online to look for van parts.

                Doc’s Log: 0300 Hours, Wednesday, November 10, 2010

                I started finding what I needed for the van pretty quickly, everything being on the Internet like they say. But before I clicked the Checkout button, I realized I was doing it wrong. Several thousand dollars worth of parts were exactly what I needed, but the chance something else would rend my van next week, gave me pause. I needed to find the cheapest replacement parts I could for the van. Then put my alchemy to good use. First I’ll greatly improve the quality of the cheap parts, then I’ll lace the panels with silver. Let those bastard Predator Kings fuck with my van again. Surprise asshole!

It occurred to me that when it comes to spirits and most anything else in the Shadow, we were almost fighting blind. With two tribes of Pure putting us on their list as well as the random spirits we may have to deal with in relation to Bone Dry, I felt it was time I ripped off the blindfold. I hit the Library like Titan and came out three and a half hours later with a spell that will let me summon and command spirits. I’m not thrilled about the fall out I could have for commanding spirits, but my naive assumption is that if I’m issuing commands that tie to their influences, it shouldn’t have as bad of a backlash later.

                I even have my first target, but I think it’s probably better not to summon a spirit at 0300 Hours and order it to teach me things. Bed for now.

                Now I’m going to mind my own business and try to salvage as much as I can on the van before I get the parts in.

                Doc’s Log: 1445 Hours, Wednesday, November 10, 2010

                So much for the warm fuzzies Nightshade and I were sharing. Wait...I’m jumping all over. Damn werewolves are getting me all out of sorts.

                Okay so I spent about three hours tearing out ruined sections of the van and making sure the good pieces weren’t going to be damaged from exposure before I could put the new parts in. That was a decent exercise for not forgetting everything Snowflake and I learned while we put the van together. But when I got finished, I was still thinking about what happened and realized I’d never tried my only contact in Glenwood.

                About 1330 Hours, I called Rawl with a little hesitation. I didn’t think he was the type that would lead a war party into New Haven, but I honestly didn’t know him that well. He figured out who I was fairly quickly and seemed as eager to talk about things as I was. I told him what happened from our viewpoint, which must have been fairly amusing. He told me that he wasn’t there, like I expected, though that he was with his nephew playing mini golf was not what I expected. I asked why we were attacked when we had only come to do research on something else as horrible as the last thing we came to Glenwood about? That was a long, very informative story that pissed me the fuck off.

                Rawl explained that there are people werewolves call Claimed. They sound like Shadow versions of Sin-Eaters, except the people are subsumed by the spirit. The Pure don’t really mind these creatures but others, like our friends around here put them down. Something about spirits keeping to their duties and not interfering with humans. I’m just a Gatekeeper so I don’t know anything about that. But this time, a nasty group of these Claimed were working for a Balehound. More explanations got me to up to speed. Werewolves have a name for vice spirits, not just the little ones, but the Vice Lords. Those more powerful vice spirits are called Majin. These are the spirits that some humans refer to as demons. So a Balehound is a werewolf that actively works for these Majin. The Claimed work for the Balehound but may also be in league with the Majin, something about being vice-ridden and pledging to a Vice Lord. Either way, given our experience with vice spirits during our stay at the Hidden Hillside Hotel, I was not pleased to be compared to such creatures. To make it worse, this particular group of vice-ridden were targeting the Predator Kings kin.

                This all ties back in to us, but not really. Apparently when Xavier and his Ivory Claws got their asses handed to them, he very loudly announced that a group of Claimed with strange powers were in New Haven. It was after our run in with the Ivory Claws that this group of Claimed started their rampage through Glenwood. So when it comes right down to it, the Predator Kings were acting on an honest case of mistaken identity. That still doesn’t excuse that Mustachioed fuckwit Xavier from, well anything, as not even the Predator Kings like him. Rawl did clue me in on some of the Ivory Claws issues as Pure though. They don’t like us because they believe that death is the end of pain; that your suffering stops once you die. And yet, he ran away instead of ending his pain when he fought with us. Anyway, Rawl also said that the Predator Kings haven’t had a fight like we gave them in a long time. They revelled in it as they are hunters and we gave them a challenge. This came up as I tried to gather a concept of truce. These werewolves may or may not believe that we are not vice-ridden, but ultimately they may not care. Rawl believes me, but like he said before these Predator Kings are crazy, even for Predator Kings. Fucking Glenwood.

                After that enlightening conversation, I called Zed. Rawl heard rumors that the Balehound was operating out of New Haven and that made me think about Malard and Angelique. I apologized even as I suggested it, but Zed let me know very quickly that it was something they thought of too. He said that couldn’t prove anything and Angelique got incredibly pissy if people tried to go through her personal stuff. Funny had that works. But while Zed told us we should go for it if we wanted to investigate, there wouldn’t be anything to keep either Malard or Angelique from ripping us to shreds if we were caught.

                So now I know that because the Ivory Claws are cowards, an entire tribe of werewolves in Glenwood attacked us. Because we are fairly competent, those same morons want another piece of us. Also because of Xavier I had to learn about something else in werewolf culture that makes me suspicious of one of our most powerful allies. And all this led me to glance outside where my devastated van sits like a wounded soldier laying in a hospital bed. There will be no quarter for these vice-ridden. And there will be no mercy given to the Ivory Claws ever again. Now I have to tell the others what I found out and try not to smash anything while I tell them.
