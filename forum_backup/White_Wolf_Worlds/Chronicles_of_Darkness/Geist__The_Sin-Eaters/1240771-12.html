                                        Technically the Int+Occult roll is for researching the entity which is an extended action described earlier in the same section. That's the first part of Summoning/Exorcism, but the second part is not described in any rolls.
                                        Going by how Warding and Binding works, it's neither resisted nor contested (Rank as penalty applies specifically when binding the entity as per the Rank table footnote; if the roll for Warding/Binding was resisted it would've also been penalised with Resistance). I believe Intelligence + Occult is the most fitting dice pool (Presence is used for Warding/Binding but that's because it's using the character's force of will, not occult knowledge) and you should probably have a penalty depending on which Manifestation Condition you're trying to apply/remove.

                                        Any rolls can be Teamworked if it makes sense that multiple characters increase the chance of success.
