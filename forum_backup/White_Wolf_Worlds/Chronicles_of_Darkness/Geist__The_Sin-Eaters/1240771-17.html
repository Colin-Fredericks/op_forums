                Do final breaking points involved in a character's death impact their ghost-Integrity (thus not impacting Rank) or their pre-death Integrity (thus impacting Rank)?

                Starting Rank: Most ghosts are created at Rank 1 (if they died with an Integrity of 6 or lower) or Rank 2 (if they died with an Integrity of 7-9).
                Fixed at Death: A ghost’s Integrity is a holdover from her living self, set at the level she had before death, barring any final breaking point
                A character with 7 Integrity kills themselves. As part of that final act, they suffer a breaking point, which they fail. Is the result:

                1) A Rank 2 Ghost with 6 Integrity
                2) A Rank 2 Ghost with 7 Integrity
                3) A Rank 1 Ghost with 6 Integrity
                4) A Rank 1 Ghost with 7 Integrity

                    Originally posted by lnodiv View Post
                    Do final breaking points involved in a character's death impact their ghost-Integrity (thus not impacting Rank) or their pre-death Integrity (thus impacting Rank)?
                    I think it'd have to be pre-death, because ghost-Integrity has its own breaking points to deal with and the roll for that involves a different stat that living characters with Integrity don't have.

                    Considering the likely causes of a "final breaking point" for a character in the immediate process of dying, I feel it's germane to point out that "this is the ghost of someone who died in a traumatic way and didn't take it well" fits much better with Rank 1 ghosts than Rank 2.
