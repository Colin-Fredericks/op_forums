                  And that is more than enough for this test run! Thank you.

                  Since this is not intended for an actual game, we don't need to conform our concepts to a premise (unless we want). Bad news; we don't have a starting premise. So the first hurdle will be character concepts. (I look forward to getting to the Geist creation section to see how Second Chances defines the Titanic friend).

                  So, who were our actors before they died? How did they die and how did it change them?
                  Strictly speaking, this is also where we should pick our Aspirations (but not our Burden one; wrath upon your murderer comes later if you are Vengeful). While I have a tendancy to refine what these are as I go along the process, we should come up with at least one each, right?
