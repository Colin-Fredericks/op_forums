          Originally posted by Bernese View Post
          how deep is the underworld, and where does it lead to? the lower depths? maybe there are beings similar to the neverborn in the deepest levels?
          The deepest levels have an ocean that dissolves identity in it. Whatever's below that, beyond speculation about the Chthonic Gods and chthonians bigger than the ones capable of emerging from the Rivers, is down to speculation.

          The particular nature of the Underworld is such that it's not hard to speculate on there being doors to or entities from the Lower Depths down there, but by and large the Lower Mysteries are the islands of stability between the shores of the Ocean of Fragments and the River Cities at the edge of the Upper Reaches. Chthonians are dead and were never alive, and the deepest Dead Dominions that border the Ocean serve as the main navigational aids for anyone trying to brave the territories of the Admiral and the Leviathan to travel from one shore to another.

          If you want a depth in units of measurement, the place is off-kilter enough that even if getting into the bottom half of the Underworld didn't necessitate travel on the Rivers you're unlikely to get instrumentation all the way down to the Black Beach.

                                                          So, that Island:
                                                          The Kerberos is pretty much D&D Tiamat, but all in lunar hues. It's generally sleeping, but will wake up fast if anyone breaks a Law. It might actually be Supernal. It's a Mystery. It doesn't impose Conditions, it just eats people.
                                                          If you strike a coin - one to a customer, please, and if explorers haven't figured out that "Each may strike once only" refers to striking coins, it would be kind to give them a hint - it has the following effects:
                                                          It can be lost, stolen, traded, lent, or given. It's a coin, those are what they're for.
                                                          It is a "rare, expensive, or dangerous" prop in any Ceremony where you can work it in. (Try baking into a cake for a Dumb Supper! It's made of minimally-reactive apeiron, it's not that poisonous...)
                                                          It is an acceptable deposit at any Irkalla Gate, in lieu of the toll. You'll get it back when you leave, if you don't leave as a fugitive.
                                                          It is a Moros Coin tool, but better. Its tool yantra is +2. It can be dedicated, and likely will be. It's likely to be the thing a Moros who despises possessions will be most concerned about.
                                                          It is all about legacies, and Legacies. Its tool yantra goes up to +3 if it's used to enhance or reinforce a physical object that has outlasted, or might plausibly outlast, a typical human lifetime. It's also a Coin tool for any Legacy that grants Death as an extra Ruling Arcanum.
