                            Session CLXVIII

                            Sara’s Stories

                            Sasha -

                            Well, it has been a satisfying few days, and we even got a few leads out of it. On James, the pyromaniac I’ve mentioned. Natalie worked her magic on the computer, with Doc’s help, and managed to find the guy’s dad. He was in the government, like the file said, in the FBI specifically. Nowadays he works as a consultant for a venture capitalist group.

                            Thankfully, we know some people in that world, on a couple sides of the fence. An associate of Chandra’s that I think I’ve mentioned before, Rebecca, is something of a rival to the group he’s working for. That meeting was exciting - I’d forgotten that Neon and she had hooked up in the past, with drama involved. In fact, in retrospect, I recall that their drama was precisely why Doc was all growly about me getting with her. Nothing I can’t smooth over though, I’m sure. Anyhow, she was familiar with James’ dad and clued us into some of his connections. She hadn’t heard of James, but took the warning to heart. One of our big fears is that if James does have a grudge against his dad, who knows who else might get caught in the crossfire. Even if he doesn’t have a grudge, his dad put him in a scientific facility to have unethical experimenting done on him. I wouldn’t put using his son as a weapon past him.

                            The meeting with my lovely paramour Elizabeth was much more fun, if less immediately helpful. She’s not in the same ‘clique’ as James’ father and his boss, but she does have business connections to them. She knew a little more about them because of that, the ones in charge that is, and thought it was unlikely they would use James as a weapon, but not much beyond that. She did promise to look into it though. Best of all, after we took so long consulting, the roads were far too treacherous in the dark. So I stayed the night. For safety, of course.

                            On our other front, Miranda, I did some work while Natalie and Doc did their research. Grin had found out that Miranda had been suspended for ‘breaking the morality code’ with a comment that read ‘Do you want Carrie? Cause this is how you get Carrie’. There was nothing more to be found at Grin’s level of access, so I went to go talk to the teach that made the complaint (as well as a few others), posing as a potential employer of Miranda’s.

                            All in all, what we found looks... ok for out escaped sociopath. Driven was the most frequent word used, along with highly intelligent. Even the teacher who registered the complaint did her best to soften whatever happened. Or maybe she just thought I wouldn’t believe her. According to her story, Miranda has a ‘low frustration threshold’ and could be ‘intensely disruptive’ if it was exceeded. Apparently this was usually catalyzed by unkindness on the part of her peers. She was very careful to skate around the fact Miranda’d used weird psychic stuff to be disruptive.

                            The official report didn’t skate around it though. Karen managed to hack her way in and found out that Miranda had been suspended for witchcraft, specifically. The final straw of ‘difficult to prove things she was the epicenter of’ was when she Darth Vadered her classmates and then threw everything in the room, including the other students, against the wall.

                            I gotta admit, I was a little surprised that witchcraft merited only a suspension. Karen pointed that she didn’t use it to cheat. Still, gay sex is apparently an automatic expulsion, sex for grades trade or not. They’re letting karen’s marriage pass without comment, thankfully, though I imagine any of us making passes at her fellow students or her making passes at Ms. Hollis, cute little blonde thing or no, would go less well.

                            There was one anomaly among all the stories. One of the teachers I talked to mentioned she got along really well with her classmates. By an interesting coincidence, he was the only one who had her in his class after her suspension. It seems she learned some new tricks. Some new, unfortunate tricks.

                            On a more pleasant note, we had a nice dinner with Rita yesterday. Coffee managed not to bring up any references to Rita’s unfortunate past and Alexia managed to swerve the conversation away from Natalie’s own uncomfortable past into discussion of favorite books and movies. Coffee’s, apparently, is Red Badge of Courage. Unfortunately, Karen was not to be moved from poking at Rita’s shock that Natalie was old enough to have young Natalie. At least, not until I tickled it out of her.

                            Rita is doing very well though. She had a rocky bit when she moved here, but she seems to have gotten her feet under her. She’s still dating Jasmine’s student, Jean, and is even getting on well with his parents. There was some drama among her social group, but she’s been able to stay out of it so far. All in all, it was good to see her again and catch up.

                            Today has been a relaxation day, since all our projects are in the wait until word gets back/clubs open up stage. Of course it started a little later than usual, at least for me. I was up late consulting with Elizabeth, after all. When I got back, the troupe had decided to go Laser Tagging with the Underwater Story Weavers Club.

                            It was a fun time. We were enough for our own team, going up against another group who clearly did this a lot. They were better than most of us. But Alexia and Doc blew them out of the water and we pulled ahead in the end. Clarissa did well too, more surprisingly. I think she had an ace or two up her sleeve.

                            I did pretty badly - I can think of better things to do in a dark room than sneak around and shoot light guns. Which is certainly not to say that I didn’t have an awesome time but... even Deanna did better than me, and she was humming the Batman theme almost the whole time. She was in fine form, really, almost losing cupcake privileges over the way she’s taken to bossing Natalie around. Alexia really doesn’t care for it.

                            We’ve been relaxing in the music room since we got back, Karen on her guitar, Natalie on the piano. I sang for a couple of songs, as did Natalie and Doc even joined in for Riders on the Storm. It was a pleasant end to a pleasant day.

                            Tomorrow we go clubbing in pursuit of a pyromaniac or his friend. Also, we introduce Coffee and Tatum to each other. That should be a hell of an evening.

                            Sara


                            Natalie’s Diary

                            Full disclosure: In retrospect, I probably shouldn’t have done that. Fucked Doc and Noriko I mean.

                            But I needed it. Sexual tension was escalating. And hell, I needed to know if I was still capable of it. Noriko being there helped. And I was mostly able to do it without freaking out at all. I did not get pregnant; that could not have happened this time. But I know I can, if it needs to.

                            And honestly, it was pretty fun. I also wanted to get with Noriko again, and enjoyed that. I am having a pretty wild, awesome few days.

                            We had dinner with Rita last night. It went well, she seems good. Thanks to my coaching, Coffee did not ask any inappropriate questions. Rita is still with Jean, and getting along with his parents. Speaking of parentage, Natalie Lousler came up, and Rita was shocked I had a seventeen year old. Part of that was having her young, but I also credit my good looks. Of course, Karen had to push her luck there. Fortunately Sara swooped in in defense of my tattered virtue.

                            Afterwards we visited Rebecca Jones, the Carthian. She knew the father, Peter but not the son, James; working for a Micah Carter, Peter is ex-FBI. She said she did not know Micah well. Rebecca also menaced Sara a bit about Neon. I hope I don’t need to have a word with her; threatening my wife will not do.

                            Doc and I had done some digging, so we were on his trail. Mostly web research. I was fairly pleased with myself to find the FBI connection. Meanwhile Grin had figured out Miranda had some issues as a student at Albertus Magnus. She had broken the honor code and been suspended at one point, and in with the teacher comments was “Do you want Carrie? Because this is how you get Carrie.”

                            We came back, and Sara went to talk to Elizabeth, her vampire friend. Alexia retired early, as is her way. I went back and found Doc and Noriko, and things progressed. He sent me to find Noriko myself. Once she jumped into my hands, we spent a quiet night bonding.

                            Breakfast was fun, but no Sara. Coffee had finally gotten some rest and her eyes were bright. While going over things to do, because gods forbid we have a day off, the story weavers came up, and the bright idea happened to do laser tag. Actually, not a bad idea. The girls need some self-defense. Doc and Alexia did outstanding, and of the weavers, Clarissa did very well. I mean, cheated, but in the real world that is what you do.

                            Before that Sara had done some legwork and talked to Miranda’s teachers. They described her as driven. And had a low frustration tolerance with people. At one point she started choking people with her powers. Unlike at the research center, no one died. However, the whole thing had been covered up. Karen’s hacking revealed just what she had done to break the honor code: witchcraft. I guess it is an improvement when that just gets you kicked out of school as opposed to murdered.

                            Anyway, We went out with the weavers to eat as a group, and then the Krewe came home to jam. Karen rocked some Für Elise and Lux Aeterna. Doc wanted to do Riders on the Storm, which mean he sang briefly while I tore up the keys. We had a good time though. Love and laughter.

                            Maybe we deserve some time to relax, eh? Ah, probably not.

                            End Session CLXVIII

                            Fun little session. Only real mechanics to discuss is for Laser Tag, had people roll Attribute + Skill combinations to represent how they were contributing, usually Dexterity or Wits plus Stealth, Firearms or Athletics.

                            Rita and Jean are names we have not heard in awhile. Was fun to revisit some stuff from earlier sessions.

                            Was a fun session, hopefully tonight will be. Comments and Questions welcome

                                The Krewe meet the mysterious Miranda. Coffee and Tatum finally meet. A family revelation.

                                Session CLXIX

                                Sara’s Stories

                                Sara let out a deep breath as she turned on her shower, stepping in as soon as the water had warmed up. She’d decided to talk to Doc about James today, at breakfast, before she could procrastinate her way out of it again. She wasn’t looking forward to it.

                                Partially it was because she couldn’t really argue against his main point. James had done some awful things. Things that he had no justification for, inexcusable things. And no, the horrors done to him didn’t absolve him. To her mind though, they did make him all the more deserving of a second chance. But Doc professed less of a belief in second chances than she did. He’d tended to roll his eyes and blame her libido, so far.

                                She’d been putting off talking to Doc about it in a serious way, figuring on leaving it for once they were closer. It all honesty, she knew he had a softer heart than he let on sometimes and had hoped that, confronted with the guy in person, he might let her try and convince James of the error of his ways. But given what Natalie had found out, ‘hopefully’ and ‘later’ didn’t seem like enough.

                                The strawberry scented woman had certainly drawn out the drama of the reveal that Rawl was short for William Rollin, James’ older brother, Sara thought with a fond smile as she let the water run through her long hair. Sara had called her werewolf friend right away. It all came as a surprise to him, though the fact that James was a pyrokinetic apparently explained some things.

                                Rawl couldn’t give any insight as to whether James might be aiming for or working with their father. From what he remembered, there was no antagonism between them, but from what he remembered, James wouldn’t go around murdering homeless people. He’d had very little contact with his family since he changed - that first time he’d come back his parents told him James had been institutionalized, and nothing more, and that only after Rawl had asked. Sara frowned sadly. ‘Coldly caring’ or not, she didn’t approve of Peter Rollin’s fathering.

                                Rawl did promise to come help if he could, with the idea that James would be less inclined to attack him right off. He did warn though, that if a fight broke out, he’d be joining in on his little brother’s side. Which was only fair, in Sara’s mind. When she talked about it with Natalie, her wife had pointed out that Doc’s healing ceremony could potentially restore the excised portion of his brain, leaving them with an option in between killing him and letting him go with a stern talking to. So Sara had figured on talking Doc into it, then making sure Rawl would be copasetic with a fight that had his brother being healed as an end goal.

                                Sara sighed, rinsing the conditioner out of her hair. She’d procrastinated yesterday afternoon, ostensibly not wanting to interrupt Doc’s research. And then it was time for dinner with Tatum, which was an excellent distraction. Sara smiled indulgently. Time with Tatum usually was. If only she could get over seeing every object of Natalie’s affections as a rival. Coffee had managed to deflect some of that, at least, into awe over how fast she talked. They’d certainly got along well enough last night at Murder Ballads.

                                Which was the other thing that got in the way. After dinner was prep for clubbing, and then clubbing, so no unpleasant conversation then either. They’d lucked out at Murder Ballads, since Miranda had been there when the krewe got there. Of course, she was talking to Maria and Sara really didn’t want to sabotage the Immortal’s plans. Or have it look that way. Sara shook her head. She felt for the woman. Maria had a raw deal, and Sara could understand why she was so paranoid, even as the Sin-Eater wished she could reconcile her and Barry.

                                Regardless, Sara was not one to interrupt anyone’s good time, so she waited them out. It gave her plenty of time to help Tatum and Coffee keep getting to know one another. Not that she shirked her voyeuristic duties. Miranda and Maria were both good at the game, exceptionally good. Maria was a little less sincere in her intentions, but they were both pouring on the charm.

                                When they came down off the catwalks, Miranda looked like she was heading home so Sara had moved to intercept her. The poor dear was a bit frazzled it seemed, about as frazzled as she left Maria, though the Immortal didn’t have to deal with learning about a new type of supernatural. To be fair, Maria just didn’t know who she’d met. Sara smirked. It was only fair really - she’d protected Maria’s secrets from Miranda, so she couldn’t very well go blabbing the opposite way. Miranda had taken to asking questions, and learning about Sin-Eaters well, for her part, even if her first instinct had been to try and root through Sara’s brain.

                                That conversation was the other thing that had pushed Sara to promising herself to have that talk with Doc. The psychic woman clearly cared about James, even if he had gotten temperamental before finally leaving her last fall. Sara wanted to talk to her again, and wanted to be able to promise her that they wouldn’t hurt James. Natalie was right, she probably had a way to contact the pyrokinetic, even if she had chosen not to since they broke up.

                                Sara stretched, reveling in the house’s prodigious water heater. She did also want to talk to the psychic woman for other reasons, namely to discuss healing her again. Sara hadn’t wanted to push - like the other woman had said, she’d had a weird night - but Sara was intent on convincing her that stronger was not always better, was not always worth the price.

                                It wasn’t as if Miranda wasn’t clearly a kind person. The concern she showed for Eris and the fact she waved those she could from the Team Tomorrow facility showed that. That honestly made for a harder sell of the ethical angle, since she did those things after the surgery, and it was a hard sell to begin with, considering the details of the surgery. Sara was holding out hope for a conscious sense of self preservation to win out.

                                With another sigh, Sara turned the water off and grabbed for a towel. The whole situation unsettled her. She felt for the awkward angry girl Miranda had been and regretted the things done to her. They’d taken away her choice in how to cope with a lonely world, and on top of that, made it difficult for her to even regain that choice. And yet... how could Sara just let her continue controlling people without regard to their agency or her own safety?

                                Sara wrapped her hair up in the towel and padded out to her room to get some clothes. That was mostly a question for later - she’d hardly had tome to try and talk her around. Maybe it would work out. But before she tried that again, she had to talk Doc out of enacting bloody vengeance against a pyrokinetic murderer. She ran her fingers through her wet hair, and flicked the droplets behind her for luck.

I love Tatum. If I had any idea…

She is clever, she is cunning, and I love her very much. Anyway, she found Miranda talking to Maria Smith. Maria turned on the charm; she is quite good at getting men around her finger. Miranda however gave as good as she got, and soon they had thoroughly seduced each other. A good snogging later they went their separate ways, and Sara swooped in to talk to Miranda. She befriended the girl, and found out she and James dated but broke up. She got no direct leads on James, but she suspects she can learn more later. They broke up, but Miranda probably knows where he lives.

I wonder how that will go with her meeting with Miranda’s powerful mind. It is odd she swooped in on her so quickly after we discovered her. Is she spying on us?

I know Sara doesn’t like to think it, love us as she very clearly does, but I deeply feel like it is true. It’s what’s given me such hope. I wonder how easily everyone else has seen the change in me? I know Sara and Karen clearly have.

                                It has been good to see Tatum. She needed some family time after wintering with us, but hopefully will be spending some time around. I think she will like Coffee. Most of us do.

                                We tease Sara, but it is good to see her desire to help James and Miranda. Miranda is disinclined to have her mind healed but could be convinced. Maybe Sara or I can.

                                Feeling pretty good. It is a good day.

                                The girls cover the session pretty well. Neither mentioned, as neither was conversing with her, that when Maria and Miranda began flirting, Catherine felt somewhat drawn to her too, feeling like she was in the presence of something powerful and awesome, and it made her weak in the knees. None of the PCs felt it though.

                                The healing Miranda and James thing came from healing aggravated damage and regrowing limbs and such. This would sort of be the same thing on a smaller scale.

                                                Session CLXX

                                                Sara’s Stories

                                                'Seanchairde'

                                                Tommy pushed his way gently through the beaded curtains that separated Sasha’s office from the rest of the family’s trailer. He heard her chair scrape on the floor as she turned to see who it was. He smiled as he rounded the bookshelf. “Hello Sasha.”

                                                “Tommy.” She nodded him towards the bench across from her. “I was about to come looking for you. I just heard from Sara.” His eyes lit up and she frowned slightly, reserved concern written on her face. “Tommy... I think it is a wonderful idea for you to go see her. As long as you don’t set yourself up for heartbreak - she is getting married.”

                                                He laid a hand on Sasha’s, smiling his thanks. “I know, I know. I love her, there’s no denying that. And she loves me, I believe that. But I don’t have any plans to swoop in and steal her back for myself. She loves a lot of people, and I wouldn’t have it any other way.” He shook his head, chuckling. “I might try and figure out how those two ladies talked her into a wedding ring though.”

                                                The young man watched his adoptive mom tuck a strand of long red hair behind her ear and saw the love in her small smile back at him. “From what I hear, they are very persuasive. Natalie, in particular, could talk Emcee out of performing, if Sara’s stories are to be believed.”

                                                “They always are. Natalie’s the one that smells like strawberries, right?” He held back a small smirk, thinking about Sara’s description of her wives.

                                                “She is.” Sasha looked at him gaugingly. “Also the one who likes men as well.”

                                                Tommy blushed, shifting in his seat. “Right. Uh... you wanted to talk to me about something Sara said in her email?” It didn’t sound like Sasha thought it wasn’t going to work out, but she did look worried.

                                                Sasha sat back in her chair. “You know, you could email her as well. I’m not saying I have a problem being a go-between - I agreed to for Sara’s sake and she needed that for awhile. But I’m sure she would love hearing from you, as much as she will love seeing you.”

                                                “I...” Tommy drew away into himself. So many things had changed recently and he had no idea how he’d even start going into them over email. He needed to be there with her, and to know whether or not his suspicions were correct.

But at the same time, I expected it would utterly shatter me. It didn’t. Now, this was probably because I knew she would return, but…at the same time, I know I am not the woman that first walked into the house. Even with one of the women I love most in the world so badly incapacitated, I was strong.

                                                            But after I thought about it, I knew why. I needed to be strong for her. When I realized that, when I realized she was going to need me, like she had never needed anyone outside her first death, I drew on strength. I know I can be strong for the women I love. Sara Rowan, Karen Knight…. even my dear Tatum.

                                                            Sara, Coffee, Noriko, Alexia, Doc and I destroyed a horrible, mechanical creature. Coffee, Noriko, and my wife suffered horribly to do it. The second death for Coffee and Sara, the first for Noriko. Now Sara needs me.

                                                            I will be there for her.

                                                            I love her so much.
