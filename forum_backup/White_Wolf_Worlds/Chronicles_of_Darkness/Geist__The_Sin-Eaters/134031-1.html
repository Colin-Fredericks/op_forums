                        It bears mention, that the works of H.P Lovecraft are filled with all sorts of nice storyhooks readily available for storytellers to implement, almost copy-paste.
                        The Evil Clergyman, The Terrible Old Man, and The Case of Charles Dexter Ward are all fine stories of mortal Necromancers with whom the Sin-Eaters would likely take issue.
                        Pickmans Model would pose a strange, yet relatively benign if exceedingly morbid, npc that might be interesting.
                        In particular, the story Herbert West - The Reanimator posits not only the titular Herbert West himself, which could be an antagonist or tragic anti-hero depending on how you wish to portray him, but also the Tomb Legions which show up towards the end. They would certainly make for a fine antagonist group, led by the headless doctor with whom West shared his methods of reanimation.

                                        A new drug has hit the streets - they call it Dust, which dealers claim allows a person to undergo an out-of-body experience. When a mortal takes it, their body grows rigid and they seem to become truly dead - but from their body comes the ghosts, the shades of the person that died. When the drugs effects end, the ghost dissipates, and the individual returns. It's had an effect on the local street - overdoses always leave a ghost, stuck in a repetitive loop of the emotions they felt while taking the drug - the Sin-Eaters do not seem to be able to draw these ghosts back to sense with plasm, because they are already 'whole'. Long term effects are... unknown. But more to the point is when the characters go to a place where everyone's overdosed on this drug... and recognize that one of the individuals who've overdosed on the drug are another known Sin-Eater. Thing is, his ghost has been left as well, and his Geist is no where to be found - and the Sin-Eater is left in an eternal loop of the emotion he felt when taking the drug: absolute, maddening Horror.

                                                        Lately, the Autochthonous Depths are busy -- refugee camps are forming in the closest parts of the Underworld. Sin-Eaters are badgered the minute they cross the threshold. Venturing further into the Underworld turns up a strange scene: the great rivers of the Underworld are flooding. And not cyclically -- they keep flooding, overflowing their banks every, constantly, steadily. The further down the Sin-Eaters go, the worse it gets. Whole Dominions are absent population, save the Kerberoi, who pace at the borders, unable to leave.

                                                        The grapevine comes alive with news of a geist, not bound to any Sin-Eaters, who calls himself Pontifactor. He is hiring mercenaries -- preferably ghosts and other hoary things of the Underworld, but anyone who will accept the pay he offers (ancient golden Iranian coins, Roman sestercii, Greek oboli, any ancient coins, really) can fight for him. He and his rag-tag army are hunting the psychopomps. When they kill one, Pontifactor consumes the psychopomp and then builds a bridge from the stone of the underworld and the vital essence of the psychopomp -- a bridge across the river that the psychopomp used to ferry people across. Word reaches the PCs that one of their allies amongst the ferrymen is the Pontifactor's next target.

                                                        A lone Sin-Eater comes to town, selling something amazing -- artifacts of a bygone age which can increase Synergy. They are simple affairs, and incredibly easy to use. They are expensive however, and the vendor does not part with them easily. Once his price is paid, however, these strange feathers do precisely what they're advertised to do: they raise a Sin-Eater's Synergy by three dots (max 10). There's no immediate catch...key word 'immediate.' What do the PCs do with these feathers, if they decide to acquire them? Keep them for themselves? Use them on Sin-eaters who have lost their way? And what will they do when the feathers' former owner (a tremendous raptor known as 'Ma'at') comes looking for them?

                                                            Chthonians, the Never-born beasts of the Lower Mysteries, gain a semblance of coherency after devouring a ghost. Some ghosts took cues from this, and started cannibalizing other ghosts- coherency and power tend to be equal for ephemerals, after all. And yes, it worked. These cannibal ghosts, called Specters by some sin-eaters, continue to eat other ghosts, even other specters, to gain mystic power and new memories. Normally, the most powerful specters, the hive-mind of devouring and devoured ghosts called the Once-born, stalk the Lower Mysteries together with Chthonians. Too bad that someone or something summoned one of those things straight to your neighborhood. Time for the philosophically bickering krewes to unite and drive back the thing before it devours the Skinlands.

                                                            A crazy yet powerful sin-eater with potent Ceremonies performed an experiment; he split a ghost off from a living guy, boosted its Rank to exalt it to geist-hood, then shoved the confused spirit back to the mortal. The result, for all purposes and outside observation, looks just like a normal sin-eater. Did this mad sorcerer just invent a way for sin-eaters to 'reproduce'? Is it legit? The original sin-eater got murdered by someone (or something), so something's certainly up......
