                            Originally posted by Hardwire99 View Post
                            Ok forgive me for asking, I am an Old World of Darkness player and I have started to look at the New Chronicles of Darkness material. Mage: the Ascension converted to Mage: the Awakening, Vampire: the Masquerade converted to Vampire: the Requiem, Werewolf the Apocalypse converted to Werewolf: the Forsaken and Changeling: the Dreaming converted to Changeling: the Lost, Now I am looking for the CoD equivalent to Wraith: the Oblivion would the be Geist: the Sin-Eaters? And from the sounds of it the Players are the equivalent to the Risen or are they closer to the Revenants mentioned in the Mage: the Awakening Death Arcanum?
                            I think it might be more accurate to say that Geist has elements of both Wraith, and Orpheus but it's a fairly different beast than either of them.

                            The players are still-living humans with the ability to muck about in the ghostly plane like in Orpheus, but they have their Geist, which has a few similarities to the Shadow from Wraith, but isn't as strictly malevolent or destructive, so much as alien and weird. The Underworld also works very differently, although certain Dead Dominions could easily resemble the Shadowlands(I think they're called? I'm note certain) from Wraith.

                            Ironically, Wraith's semi-replacement is one of the most optimistic and upbeat games in ChroD.

                            Genius: the Transgression 2E is a thing that's being worked on.
