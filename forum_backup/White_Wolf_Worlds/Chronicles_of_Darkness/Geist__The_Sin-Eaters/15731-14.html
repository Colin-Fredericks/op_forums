                    Session CXXIX

                    Doc’s Log

                    Doc’s Log: 0930 Hours, Sunday, November 14, 2010

                    Finally learning how Noriko “joined” the Colony and helping her make peace a little more with her situation did good things for me. When I woke up, things just felt more in line. Meditation was both incredibly relaxing and quick.

                    I didn’t expect to see Cupcake for a few hours, so I headed downstairs planning to make food. Rose had beat me to it so we had eggs and fruit instead. We that actually made it home or stayed in last night were just finishing up breakfast when Chandra and Cupcake arrived. Rose gave Cupcake the look while Chandra pulled me to the side.

                    While they certainly seemed to have enjoyed themselves with Mouse, Chandra had been thinking about her meeting with the Prince. She wanted me to set up an interview with Rebecca. The honest purpose was to find leverage on the Prince, but I warned her Rebecca wasn’t likely to betray him. I knew she didn’t expect Rebecca to tell her dark secrets but I pointed out that she wasn’t likely going to do anything to further strain their relationship. We sat down and I told her everything I knew about Rebecca and her relationship with her Sire and as much as I could about Vampires in general. The consensus was that she should play the “innocent and ignorant foreigner” for all it was worth but not to push too hard. We were both a little amused that I was the one to tell her these things. At least I hope she was amused and not embarrassed. I agreed to call Rebecca and try to set up a meeting later tonight.

                    Doc’s Log: 1330 Hours, Sunday, November 14, 2010

                    I don’t understand how Axe can find the most unlikely actions to irritate me like no one else. I was happy this morning, after last night especially. I wasn’t grumpy, I’d had breakfast, I was helpful to Chandra. I even stepped back and watched while Karen decreed to Noriko that she planned to “goth you up.” I wasn’t at all upset with that idea, but it was amusing to watch Noriko decline in a polite but eventually firm way.

                    And then, BAM! Axe runs into the TV Room obviously just in from a morning run and announces to everyone, including Tantrum, Mouse, and Karen that she just free climbed the Bluffs “in five minutes!” Everyone was speechless. Yes, it was an incredible feat and not one I think I could manage. But the Bluffs are at least 100 feet up, and she had no equipment. Worse, we had no idea she was going to do it. The peacock’s aura of pride that surrounded her stabbed a needle into my nerves. I apparently became Mr. Buzzkill when I asked which one of the three mortals present she wanted to kill? I probably could have gone about discussing it with Axe differently, probably should have actually. But she acts like a teenager with no concept of judgement sometimes, and she can’t always blame The Huntress. She tried to “reassure” me that she was safe because of all her various powers. Still in front of Tantrum. I get that she is skilled and at least as powerful as any of us, but she takes unnecessary risks with no backup or even a plan B. And she doesn’t given any thoughts to what she says around who. And now I just feel like a grouchy old man.

                    Karen led Tantrum out of the room so we could discuss Blood Bather issue after that. Axe just sat next to Bright Eyes (who gave me a dirty look) and looked restless. I want to find a way to connect with Axe but she has no context for where I’m coming from most of the time, and apparently I’m in the same boat. Also, I’m off topic. Mouse has been getting worried about the two turncoat Bathers, so we decided it was not wise to doubt the Psychic’s intuition. We are all in agreement that we will have to do things to Mariah that none of us are normally comfortable with. Bright Eyes shot down my idea of taking the cut up pieces sealed in concrete into the Underworld. Her objection to having to then perform a Hero’s Quest once we figured out how to permanently kill the Blood Bather was not without merit. The Underworld or any other realm is unstable enough that we might lose a piece or have it stolen and we never know until it’s too late. That prompted the conversation about needing to plant a flag in the Underworld. Axe was unaware we were planning anything other than a long visit, so she perked back up again at that. We’ll have to spend some time on cleanup and clear out duty soon, but the Blood Bathers are on the front burner for the moment. I didn’t want to move on Mariah until we had some clue as to what this Soul Destruction power she came up with was all about. Bright Eyes and Gretchen came with me to the Library to figure out anything we could about a source for this power, any defense, or really anything actionable we could find.

                    It was slow going at first, not least because we had to keep Gretchen from asking questions she already knew would get her cut to ribbons by my girlfriend. Then we decided to change course since we weren’t finding anything about Blood Bathers and their spells that suggested this was even possible. That was about the time Nightshade came in to let us know she was sending Axe to spy on Mariah. I was far more jaded than I should have been but I was still annoyed with Axe from earlier. Nightshade tried to calm me down by reminding me that she needed to find her place in the Krewe and we didn’t know what she could do. I resisted the urge to bring up the powers she listed off like groceries and agreed that Axe should be allowed to prove herself useful. I really hope whatever I said didn’t come out sounded as much like an asshole as it looks written down here. I asked that she get an ear curl so we could help if she needed it. I didn’t want Axe to learn the hard way that we were dealing with shit not even we can come back from just to prove herself worthy to me. I didn’t say that, but given the looks I’d gotten earlier and the dejected face Axe wore after I scolded her, I’m pretty sure that’s what she feels she has to do. I don’t like being the old man on the mountain.

                    Getting back to our research, we realized that the power Mariah is using likely comes from an artifact as thankfully this seems to be something she can’t do without all the prep work. After about an hour and a half, the best thing we’d come up with is that we needed an expert. I called The Collector to see if he could discuss possible objects that could create such a situation. Bright Eyes was very interested in hearing from The Collector and also feeling silly. Just as our expert answered the phone, she licked the back of my neck. I shot her a look before inquiring about a meeting. The Collector was actually in New York CIty and wouldn’t be back until tomorrow, but agreed to meet sometime between 1400 and 2100 Hours. To be fair, it almost sounded like he was planning a museum heist, so I didn’t ask for any details over the phone.

                    Doc’s Log: 2000 Hours, Sunday, November 14, 2010

                    Axe has been busy and it sounds like she’s dancing on the razor’s edge. Mariah has some kind of pheromones that are both sexual and apparently almost hypnotic. Axe can’t be in the same room with her target without wanting to forget the mission and throw herself at Mariah. She has been watching from a distance, but Mariah is about to leave and Axe is going to follow her. I don’t like it, but I wasn’t given a say in what Axe was doing to fulfill her duty to the Krewe. I should be there backing her up. Not because she can’t do it alone but because she shouldn’t have to. We’re going to have to have a talk when this is over. Anyway, Axe wants us to investigate the vanity mirror at Mariah’s house. She said it didn’t show Mariah’s reflection when all the other mirrors did. She’s going to text us the security code as soon as she gets it and we’ll head out.

                    If anything happens to Axe, I may be more enthusiastic with Mariah than I have any right to be.


                    Sara’s Letters

                    Sasha -

                    My wives, in case it is not already clear, are silly ducks. Karen, the sweetling, has a deathly fear of being tossed aside in favor of someone with ‘boy parts’. So I made much of her, and them, when I got back and they got downstairs. It was a nice relaxing afternoon to follow up a nice relaxing morning and evening. But that’s not the silly part.

                    It seems in my absence that they and Tatum decided that Goth Train (a Goth version of Soul Train you see) needed to be a Thing. What kind of thing - TV show/cartoon/band/album name - was unclear, but we were required to pick instruments. Ocarina is apparently not goth enough (triangle and cowbell were also discluded before it got to me) and just dancing on stage doesn’t suffice, but I think I could handle bass guitar. It’s been awhile since I’ve played, but it’s like riding a bike, right? We’ll be too damn hot for anyone to care anyway. So far I think it’s the four of us - everyone else turned them down, despite Natalie’s sultan clap and Tatum’s puppy dog eyes. We couldn’t even get Noriko to play drums for us, and everyone loves playing drums. Maybe I’ll claim them instead if she doesn’t want them.

                    We did gather quite a crowd, so everyone but Alexia and Chandra were in the TV room when Alice burst in super excited to tell us it only took her five minutes to scale the 10-story cliff to the beach! I was both impressed and happy - that sort of thing was really her element and she looked revitalized. That is, until Doc got grumpy at her for doing it alone. He had a point - I certainly don’t want anything to happen to her - but the way her excitement just sort of drained out of her and left her kinda slumped was heart-breaking. I pulled her over to sit by me and promised I’d go climb it with her next time, which is kinda terrifying, but I have to now, and not just because I said I would. If I go, I bet she’ll promise to keep me safe again,and that’s worth 20 storys of rock-climbing.

                    She seemed a little better after that, between me promising Doc didn’t think she was an idiot anymore than he did me, and the idea of rock climbing again and her date with Angelique tomorrow. Not that the last is pure happiness. She’s planning on telling Angelique some about her past and is worried how that’ll go over. I hope it goes well for her sake. A rejection for that would deal with her dating dilemma, at the cost of a big chunk of self-respect and confidence, I imagine. It’s hard to grow past you old failings when they continue to control your fate. I also hope she doesn’t get lured in either direction by simply being needed. It’s a heady thing but not often enough to sustain someone. I think she gets that though.

                    After a late lunch (bao ban! They were delicious. I think Alexia felt guilty about... something? re: Jasmine and so made her favorite), it was time to get back to work. Catherine,with her strong foreSight, was getting fretty about the lovebirds Carol and Jenny, which lights something of a fire for certain. We still don’t know how to counter her personal blood magic thing, so containment and separation of certain... focal points of power is the plan. I argued against extreme separation, on the grounds of not wanting to have to go through a Hero’s Journey once we did find the counter that would return her to the cycle of fate. That definitely rules out the Underworld, though other things do too. Bringing it up did lead to discussing our plans concerning answering its pull – all of us need to spend sometime there, with how connected we are with death. So we need to make it safe. I'm hoping to set up my own little beachside campsite – the RV might be a bit rough to get down there though. With any luck, we might even make it safe enough for visitors. I'm sure Karen will appreciate that. Just gotta convince Doc and Natalie...

                    But, back on topic - Doc did want to figure out how to deal with the ring, so he and I hit the books, with Gretchen's help and undying interest in everyone’s sex life. Alice decided, with Natalie’s encouragement, to do some surveillance of the cult leader and make sure she’s not up to anything involving the girls. It was a little distracting, but Natalie’s right - she needs to feel like she’s a proper part of things. Besides, like with the cliff, she’s a big girl who knows the risks and is really good at that sort of thing. We even got a call a bit ago, after we spoke with Janus, to let us know the leader was going out and she planned to follow and there was something we should maybe follow up on at the house while she’s out. We’re waiting for the text that will include the security code and let us know she’s gone now.

                    In happier news, we did in fact get to call Janus (well, Doc did. I just listened over his shoulder). See, our research found that the effect on the rings likely came from an artifact and he’s kinda our guy for that sort of thing. At least, he is if I have anything to say about it. We’re even getting to meet with him again, but not until tomorrow afternoon when he gets back in town. The final conclusion of the call, other than the meeting, was that one should never touch or lick evil artifacts, the back of Doc’s neck tastes funny, and Gretchen is not allowed to make out in the library. I think I heard Doc’s phone beep though, so I’m going to go poke him until he tells me our Little Red is safe.

                    Sara


                    Alice’s Reflections

                    Now that Sara and I are back home at the mansion, I’m trying to figure out what to do with myself.

                    Back home at the mansion. I never thought I’d say that. It still feels odd, and my room still feels too big and too empty. Eris’ room was more to my liking, honestly. Still, if I’m going to become a member of this krewe, I’d better get used to living in a drafty, haunted mansion.

                    Don’t become too cozy. You mustn’t lose your predator’s edge.

                    I don’t plan to. If we can hunt down this Balehound, or those blood-bathers we’ve been talking about, I’ll have work to do.

                    Don’t try to sound so reluctant. I know you want to hunt. And kill. How long has it been since our last? A month? Two? The craving is there. I can sense it.

                    I take a deep breath to try and clear my head. Sure, I’ve been distracted over the past couple of days, but I am getting impatient for a new kill. Someone who deserves it, of course.

                    Of course.

                    The Huntress is being patronizing. I don’t actually crave the kill as much as she makes out. At least, I keep telling myself that. Still, I would really like to take action against…something. Something meaningful. Someone who really deserves my axe.

                    I decide to deal with the tension by taking a nice, long jog. Even in the winter, the beach below the cliffs make for a nice run. By the time I’m finished, my head feels clearer even if my lungs are burning from the frigid air. I’m in better spirits. I stand on the beach and catch my breath. Now that I’ve stopped running, the chill starts to sink. Even so, I don’t want to go inside just yet.

                    Without even realizing it, I keep staring at the cliff leading up to the mansion’s yard. There’s a stairway cut into it leading down the pier and dock, but I’m studying the cliff itself. It’s a rough, dark rock wall. It wouldn’t be too hard to climb. There are plenty of handholds, plenty of little ledges where my foot could find purchase. I take a few moments to pick out a likely route up to the top. I’m feeling energized from my run and it’s been too long since I’ve had a good climb.

                    I run toward the cliff and leap for some low handholds. It’s not hard to get a good grip even though my fingers are freezing, and the rest is easy. It doesn’t take me long to scale it. It feels good. Now I’m ready to head back in.

                    I feel good. Flush with the thrill of the climb, I find the others hanging out in the TV room. As soon as I enter, I can’t help but tell everyone that I scaled the cliff in under five minutes. I don’t know why I expect them to be impressed, or to at least share in my triumph. Not that it was really much of a climb, but you know. It’s the little things.

                    Instead, Doc get mad. He starts scolding me. He asks me which of the people in the room might have died to resurrect me if I’d fallen and killed myself. I try to tell him that I’m an expert climber. I wasn’t going to fall. He doesn’t care, even when I point out that I can turn into a flock of birds just by thinking about it. I was never in any real danger.

                    All I can do in the face of Doc’s disappointment, though, is admit that I was being careless, even if I don’t agree. What the heck is Doc’s problem, anyway?

                    I’m crestfallen. I feel like an idiot again, and the only thing that makes it better is Sara’s sympathetic hand on my arm and her promises that she’ll scale the cliff with me again sometime, with the proper equipment. That’s something to look forward to, at least. It’s always fun sharing my passions for rock climbing. Sara looks a bit anxious about it, though, so I promise that I won’t let her fall. Then she gives me a wanton look that makes me blush. I think she likes it when I get all protective.

                    I want to stalk off to the shower and just forget about it, but it seems like I interrupted a serious talk about the blood-bathers. It seems like Alexia’s new girlfriend, Catherine, is worried, so the krewe is discussing our next steps in dealing with them. Doc wants to do more research. Big surprise there. Sounds like he hopes to find a way to prevent the queen blood-bather from destroying us with some sort of magic. Fair enough, but how much does he expect to learn from the library? It’s not like some sort of magic secret weapon is going to jump out of a book and up his butt, though if it did, maybe it would dislodge the bug stuck there.

                    After a nice, hot shower, I flop onto my bed and decide to call Angelique. Maybe she’ll be up for that date I asked for days ago. I feel a little guilty about it, have just spent the night with another woman, but I guess I’ve been as honest as I can be without being hurtful. As the phone rings, I wonder if Angelique will even want to talk to me. She was pretty upset the other day. I she doesn’t want a date, I guess that’ll pretty much decide things for me. A part of me almost hopes she WILL make the decision for me, but that’s not really what I want. Or is it? Maybe I’ve already made a decision and I’m just looking for justification.

                    If you deny Angelique now, it will make it much easier when we decide that she needs to die. It’s inevitable, after all. You know the evil that gnaws at her heart. Her lust for dark power. She might not be a balehound now, but she’s destined to explore that path eventually.

                    You don’t know that. She’s trying to escape her past. Her…lusts.

                    Ah yes, like you. But you aren’t really escaping either, are you? You’re simply stalling. Pretending. Looking for justification when all you really want is to hunt and kill.

                    “Alice?” Angelique picks up the phone. She sounds hopeful.

                    “Umm. Hi, Angelique,” I begin, uncertainly. “So, I’m trying to figure out what my heart wants, like you asked. But I can’t do that unless I get to know you better. I mean, we only just met. I can’t really commit if I don’t really know you. So, I was wondering if you’re still up for that date?”

                    “Yes. I’d like that very much,” she replies readily. “I’m busy this evening though. Pack business.”

                    “Tomorrow then?”

                    “Yeah, that would be good.”

                    I can tell from the sound of her voice that she’s smiling, and even though I can’t see it, it makes me happy.

                    * * * * *

                    I move carefully to the blood-bather’s house. Her name is Moriah, and she’s going to feel the bite of my axe, but not quite yet.

                    I’m still kind of amazed that Doc gave me the go ahead. I didn’t even want to talk to him about this. That’s why I went to Natalie first. She was hesitant, but she agreed with me. We have to find out more about what Moriah’s doing. And I’d like to know more about this particular victim before we try to kill her. There are plenty of things you can’t learn from the library, like the layout of her house, whether she keeps any sort of schedule, or what sort of security she has.

                    I become invisible well before reaching the house. Natalie dropped me off, but she’s long gone now. I’m planning to stick with Moriah as long as I can; I probably won’t call for pick-up until tomorrow.

                    Getting inside is easy. She has a nice security system, but I just phase through the back door. The house is nice. Upper middle-class, two stories. Clean and tidy. The kind of house that never looks lived in. It reminds me a bit of Kitty’s house.

                    Moriah is upstairs. She’s taking her time getting ready to go out. She meticulously applies her make-up, styles her hair, and selects a ravishing dress. She has beautiful eyes and full, red lips. I want to be kissed by those lips. I want to get lost in those eyes. Why the heck am I trying to choose between Angelique and Eris when I could belong to this stunning beauty?

                    Your heart is not so fickle, Alice. You know better than to succumb to this witchcraft.

                    Oh my god. I can’t believe what I was just thinking! This is bad. Just being around this woman is intoxicating. Is this some sort of pheromone thing? And if it is, why is it triggering? She can’t possibly have this going on all the time or she’d cause riots wherever she goes. Maybe this only works on sin-eaters? Whatever it is, at least it hasn’t tipped her off to my presence. She doesn’t seem to know I’m here.

                    Even though I know it’s unwise, I can’t help but watch as she changes into her elegant dress for the evening. Her body is gorgeous. Her bed is right there. I’m sure she would invite me into it if I revealed myself…

                    No! I have Angelique and Eris to worry about, and I’m here to gather information, not get laid. We’re going to kill this woman. I need to focus on that.

I didn’t really get what he was talking about, but his words carried the tone of a good-natured ribbing, and that’s when I realized that they were ready to bring me into the krewe.

                                                        So now I’m kneeling with the others in the cemetery behind the house. The sky is overcast and gray, and it is bitterly cold out here. Even bundled up in a couple of shirts, a thick sweater, a warm scarf, a knit cap and my jacket, I’m freezing. I hate cold weather, but it’s worth it. Natalie is kneeling to my left, and Jasmine to my right. Doc’s directly across from me. The others—Sara, Alexia, and Simon—complete the circle.

                                                        Doc starts things off by slamming a bottle of rum down in the middle of our circle, and we all take a drink, all except for Jasmine, who is leading the ceremony. I gather she’s got some sort of Chinese religious thing that keeps her from imbibing. Then Natalie solemnly recounts how I came to join the krewe and the things I’ve done for them since I arrived. She says that she feels it’s time for me to join the Middle Shadows, and then asks the others if anyone has a problem with me joining. Even though I know he supports all of this, I still halfway expect Doc to object. Instead, he just looks at me with his stern but accepting gaze and says, “Hell, as far as I’m concerned, you’re already a part of the krewe.” I’m touched, and it’s a struggle to keep the tears at bay.

                                                        * * * * *

                                                        Memories of my first krewe binding flood my mind. The Huntress was content then. She was getting what she wanted. Jon was so serious as he performed the ceremony, and Randy was eager to get it done so we could go out drinking to celebrate afterward. I think he wanted to get in my pants back then, before he learned I wasn’t into guys. Not that Randy wasn’t a good guy. He knew more about what we were getting into than the rest of us did, after all, and he taught me a ton about investigation.

                                                        I remember Cassidy watching me warily during the ceremony. She still wasn’t sure about me. She didn’t think I would be a good fit. Maybe she sensed the darkness inside of me. But, like the Middle Shadows, she gave me the benefit of the doubt. And eventually we fell in love. I miss her. I probably always will. I guess I’ll always be haunted by the knowledge that I betrayed her faith in me, and by the pain I caused her. I’ll never be able to make that up to her, but I can still learn from my mistakes.

                                                        * * * * *

                                                        Jasmine takes over the ceremony. Even though Natalie is basically the krewe witch, Jasmine seems to run a lot of the krewe’s ceremonial maintenance: krewe binding, making charms, that sort of thing. Maybe that’s why she doesn’t get out in the field that much. I guess she’s more about support than engagement. She runs us through a short litany that reminds us all of the krewe’s mythos and responsibilities. Then each member of the krewe cuts his or her hand and spills blood upon the cemetery ground. Jasmine takes my hand and cuts it, then has me do the same to hers. Then she takes my hand so that our blood mingles before falling on the graves. I get the distinct impression that she’s passing the torch, but I don’t know why. Not at first, anyway.

                                                        * * * * *

                                                        We enter a trance, and then each of us takes a back seat as our geists gather in a spectral graveyard of their own to welcome the Grasping Huntress. The meeting is tense. The Dog of War despises the Grasping Huntress, and makes it very clear that he will not put up with her blood lust if it threatens the krewe. The Virgin Sacrifice, normally reticent and submissive, similarly warns the Huntress to behave. The Huntress is arrogant and angry that she is being forced into the krewe, but she decides to make the best of things. She tells her fellow geists that she will serve the krewe well, but reminds them that she is Death and Resolve, and that her sin-eater is a Reaper. She tells them that we will do our job, and do it well. Though she doesn’t say it outright, her words imply that the krewe needs us much more than we need them. But she’s wrong. It’s the other way around, and I just wish she would shut up and play nice.

                                                        And then something strange happens. Jasmine’s geist, the Drowned Bride, withdraws into the shadows and mist behind the Huntress. Barely visible, she whispers in a gentle voice full of longing and tells the assembled geists that she will leave the Resolution of Death in the capable, and perhaps more fitting hands of a killer while she and Jasmine move on to other things. And that’s when I realize that the enclave of geists has shifted: the Huntress now occupies the Bride’s place.

                                                        * * * * *

                                                        The ceremony ends. I’m a part of the krewe. It feels good.

                                                        But there’s more to it than that. Everyone looks to Jasmine for some explanation of her geist’s behavior, and that’s when Jasmine explains that she’s going to be leaving the krewe. Well, not leaving it, exactly. She’s a founding member of the Middle Shadows, and she’s still a part of the krewe. But she’s going back to Taiwan with her boyfriend, Karl, to establish a new branch of the Middle Shadows. She’s going to be working with some sort of Chinese demon she calls Uncle Ox to make sure that karmic debts are paid. I don’t really get it, but she seems sure of herself, and the rest of the krewe is supportive. We’re all sad to see her go, though.

                                                        * * * * *

                                                        Later in the afternoon, after celebrating, Deanna comes up in conversation. She’s been flirting with me, and I finally had to tell her I’m seeing someone else. Natalie, on the other hand, has been toying with the idea of a fling with the girl. That worries me, so I ask Natalie if she’s sure she’s doing the right thing. She assures me that she’s made it very clear to Deanna that she’s not interested in a relationship, and that if anything happens between them, it’s merely fun. That’s all well and good, but I’m not sure Deanna really understands that. I mean, it’s one thing to say it, but I know from experience how much it can hurt if two people don’t have the same expectations in a relationship.

                                                        I tell Natalie that I’m worried because I was in relationship with someone who wasn’t really in love with me and just wanted to be with me because she liked the attention. I tell her a little about Kitty, and how she used me. But even after hearing me out, Natalie still assures me that Deanna understands how things are. She also points out that she’s not like Kitty; she’s not trying to deceive Deanna. And she’s right, that’s a big difference. On the other hand, I don’t think Kitty was maliciously trying to deceive me either. Maybe I’m just being generous because I murdered her, but I think she just got carried away with herself and didn’t think about how much it would hurt ME.

                                                        Natalie still doesn’t seem to understand that I’m afraid the same thing might happen with Deanna. Natalie likes attention, and Deanna’s giving her plenty of it. It’s affecting Natalie’s judgment, and I know from personal experience how easy it is to get swept up in a relationship and let hopes change your expectations. Deanna’s very impressionable, and people tend to become VERY enamored of Natalie. I know Natalie doesn’t mean any harm, but I think this is going to end in tears—Deanna’s tears, to be exact. Natalie says that a little heartbreak might help make Deanna less naive, and while that may be true, it still seems a bit cold.

                                                        Still, I’ve said my piece, so now I’ll leave it well enough alone.

                                                        * * * * *

                                                        Angelique and I are finally on our first date, chatting over a meal at a fancy restaurant. It’s been nice so far. Angelique is charming and sexy, and she is clearly in love with me. The intensity of her feelings is a bit frightening, actually. It makes what I’m about to do all that much harder.

                                                        “So, Angelique,” I open hesitantly as I finish my salad, “you’ve been up front with me about your past, so I think you should know a little about mine. There’s a reason I understand you so well, and I think I should be open with you too.”

                                                        I take a deep breath before continuing. “In our krewe, I’m a Reaper. That means it’s my job to kill things that need to die, even people, if it helps resolve a ghost. The problem is, I used to be a part of a different krewe when I lived in Portland. And my geist—the spirit inside me—is a vengeful psychopath.”

                                                        And you are a “just” psychopath. Is there much of a difference?

                                                        I pause to try and quiet the Huntress, reminding myself that I am NOT a psychopath. Once I gather my thoughts again, I continue.

                                                        “It’s all too easy to listen to her whispers and doubt myself, and when I was in Portland, I started to lose my footing. I went too far, and killed some people who didn’t deserve it. I was well on my way to becoming a serial killer when my old krewe kicked me out. That made me realize I was heading down a dark path, so I came here looking for help from the Middle Shadows. I knew that if I tried to go it alone, I wouldn’t be able to resist the Huntress’s influence.

                                                        “The krewe has helped me a lot, and they’re still helping me. But the Huntress is always there, in the back of my mind, and she wants to hunt and kill. It’s something I’ll probably always struggle with.”

                                                        I take another deep breath and then look timidly at Angelique, who fixes me with her elegant eyes. I don’t know whether to expect caution and revulsion or sympathy and compassion in her eyes, but I don’t see any of those things. Instead, I see sadness and a hint of resignation. After a moment, she looks down at her plate as though embarrassed to meet my gaze.

                                                        “None of that bothers me,” she says. I’m both relieved and worried with how easily she took the dark secrets of my past until I realize that something else is bothering her.

                                                        “What’s wrong?” I ask.

                                                        “My demonic patron has contacted me in my dreams,” she whispers, her expression a mask of despair and hopelessness. “She wants me to work with the Balehound. He’s called Bright Shadow because, like a fire, he transforms things into useless dust and ashes. Figuratively, anyway.”

                                                        “Well, maybe we can use that to help us hunt and kill this Bright Shadow,” I offer.

                                                        Angelique shakes her head, “I’m just…I don’t know if I can resist my patron. I don’t know how much of a hold she has on me. I don’t really remember what my mentor did to bind me to her, and if I contact the Balehound, I’m worried my connection to the demon will become even stronger. I don’t know if I can resist.”

                                                        “Don’t say that. You don’t belong to her. She’s not your master. If I can resist the geist in my head, you can resist this demon.”

                                                        “I wish I could be so sure.”

                                                        “Nonsense. You know I’ve got your back, right?”

                                                        Angelique raises her gaze to look at me with a glimmer of hope. The intensity of her gaze is startling. “If it weren’t for you, I wouldn’t know what to do right now. It’s because of you that I was able to resist my patron when she spoke to me in my dreams.”

                                                        Oh boy. Angelique is really serious about us. It’s scary, bordering on obsessive, and I’m not sure I’m comfortable with it.

                                                        “Well listen, no matter what happens between us, I’m your friend,” I try to cool things off a bit. “I’m here for you—the krewe is here for you—and we’ll get through this, okay? We’ll find out what to do about this demon and help protect you from her. I don’t really know how right now, but we’ll figure something out. I guess all Doc’s research might come in handy.”

                                                        Angelique smile softly and nods.

                                                        “Promise me one thing, Alice.”

                                                        “What do you need?” I answer warily.

                                                        “Promise me that if I become dangerous to others that you will kill me rather than let me hurt anyone.”

                                                        Somehow I knew that’s what she was going to ask. I stop and stare down at my plate for several moments, thinking and gathering my thoughts. When I look up again, it’s Alice the Reaper talking.

                                                        “Understand that if you ask me that, and I say yes, then I mean it,” I say.

                                                        “Then you promise?” Angelique insists.

                                                        “Yes, I do,” I say with a slight nod.

                                                        She wouldn’t be the first girlfriend you’ve killed.

                                                        Without thinking, I give voice to the Huntress’s thoughts in a terribly misguided attempt to lighten the mood. “You wouldn’t be the first girlfriend I’ve killed,” I say quietly.

                                                        There’s an awkward silence after that.

                                                        * * * * *

                                                        The rest of our dinner goes much better once we put dark talk of demons and murder aside, and then we go to see a punk band performing at a local club. I have a lot of fun even though I’m not really into punk. Angelique is, though, and she really likes the show. She also likes being with me, and seems to go out of her way to make it clear that we’re a couple. She likes it when I drape my arm around her waist, but I get the feeling there’s something more. Like she’s trying to prove that she’s mine and not the demon’s. The problem is that she’s not really mine. We’re just dating, and I might be even less sure of how I feel about her now than I was before tonight.

                                                        That doesn’t stop me from inviting her home with me. I know I shouldn’t sleep with her again, not when I’m not really sure we’re going to work out as anything other than friends. But she’s gorgeous, and seductive, and she gives the strong impression that she NEEDS me.

                                                        Why aren’t you this good at justifying your actions when there’s a kill at stake? I don’t know why you bother. You’ve slept with that feral tramp Eris twice. It’s only fair to Angelique to sleep with her again too.

                                                        The sarcasm in the Huntress’s cloying voice is so thick I could cut it with a knife, but it doesn’t get in the way of my lovemaking. Angelique is an amazing partner, more confident and comfortable than she was last time. I lose myself in her. And then, during one incredibly intense moment, she breathlessly whispers, “I don’t belong to her. I belong to you.”

                                                        Later, as we’re drifting off to sleep, I realize that it’s not going to work with Angelique. She wants too much too quickly. The intensity of her feelings for me is frightening, the kind of thing I’d expect from a stalker. I mean, I get it. She’s desperate to escape her demonic patron, and I’m the only thing she has to grab on to. But I clearly don’t share her obsession with this relationship, and it’s frankly making me uncomfortable.

                                                        I wake up in the middle of the night to find Angelique standing on my bed in the form of a huge black wolf. She’s staring at the wall as though she sees something I don’t. I invoke my Oracle to see if I can sense anything more, but there’s nothing. My heightened senses tell me that Angelique’s heart is beating way too fast, though. I’m wary of touching her, unsure whether she’ll attack or not, but I gently call out to her.

                                                        At the sound of her name, she begins to shudder and transform back into her human form. She collapses and I catch her, holding her gently. Her eyes flutter open and closed and her heartbeat begins to slow, but she’s exhausted.

                                                        “What happened?” I ask.

                                                        “My patron…she visited my dreams again. She hates you. You’re the only thing giving me the strength to defy her. She told me to kill you, but I resisted. I stood up to her…because of…you.”

                                                        I don’t think Angelique is TRYING to blackmail me into a relationship, but she’s doing a darn good job of it.

                                                        Then Angelique succumbs to her exhaustion and sinks into a deep sleep. I lay holding her, stroking her hair to comfort her until I drift off to sleep, wondering what the heck I’m going to do about this.
