    Oh man you guys, this has been a shitty week. I have barely slept until last night and I am still sore. Hopefully I will sleep tonight. But because my level of comfort rates just higher than sliding naked down a 10’ greased razorblade, I am late with journals, and commentary will be low.

    This time around the fantasic vistas of the Underworld clash with the cold realities of a society all too often still homophobic, and remember this is almost six years ago. Note Doc had to miss this session.

    Session CLVIII

    Sara’s Letters

    The group traveled through a few chambers, including one with a decent sized gazebo as a centerpiece and another overlooked by the sinister Manor. Sara shuddered as they passed by, moving closer to Natalie, keeping Alice between them and the house with its red lit windows. They moved at a casual pace through every chamber but that one, enjoying themselves and each other’s company.

    The three-way crossroad was empty this time, and Alice made a beeline for the wall beneath the cavernous hole near the ceiling. She climbed quickly, stepping up the hand and foot holds like there was a wide ladder. Once up there she secured a rope and dropped it down. Sara followed next, shimmying quickly up and accepting the hand Alice extended with a sparkling smile. Alice smiled back before returning to the rope.

    When they came to a two pronged branch of the tunnel, Alice pointed left and the group followed her lead. In short order, the tunnel opened up to a hollowed out chamber, the ground covered with scattered bones, looking all the world like some mythical Beast’s lair from one of Sara’s fairy tale stories. At Sara’s interested look, Alice explained that this is where they had met Dulcinea, before.

    They continued down the path, reaching the large gaping hole in the floor. The ropes and pitons Alice had placed to let the smaller group continue past were still in place. Alice took stock of things, her heightened senses piercing the dark orchard, the vague luminescence enough to let her make out a group of five young girls, carrying baskets and gathering fruit along a dark river.

    The nearest tree was four or five yards below the lip of the hole. Alice secured a rope and dropped down it, swinging to grab hold of the top branches of the tree. She attached the rope to a sturdy branch and noticed that a couple of the young women had taken note of her. A closer look revealed them to be eerily similar in silhouette - the same height, body shape, and hair style, though the colors, such as they were in the tenebrous Underworld, differed.

    “Greetings. Do you mind if we joined you?”

    “No? Come on down.”

    The girls looked at each other, clustering near the base of the tree, muttering amongst themselves. Alice was able to catch them deciding that the group couldn’t be highwaymen, since they were all women, before her attention was wrapped up in making sure everyone climbed down safely.

    Once they were all down, the girls inched closer, one stepping forward and introducing herself as Marcella. She explained they were gathering fruit for their group. Eventually, she drew up a bit of courage, glancing back at the other girls.

    “You are... very different than anyone who has crossed here before.”

    Alice’s revelation that the group was all alive started a twitter among the girls. The light and close quarters brought forward more similarities - a nose, the shape of a pair of lips, or an eyebrow line shared in precise detail by two or three of them. After a moment, Marcella looked back at her group.

    “Are you here to lead us out? To where we’d be alive?”

    The five girls nodded in unison, unblinking. Natalie’s eyes said trouble and Sara’s brow knit. Alexia glanced at them, and Alice, as Marcella said there were around 30 in their community. Alexia suggested research, with a significant look at the red head. Alice nodded, smiling coldly and shifting so her axe was closer at hand as she pointed out a visit would be excellent research.

    So the krewe followed the five girls further into the chambers. Alice dropped back to speak quietly with the others.

    “This screams Kool-Aid cult to me.”

    “They do kinda remind me of puppets. And Pirate Percy.”

    Alice looked questioningly at Alexia who explained while they approached the small, vaguely German town that was shrouded in mists. Fireflies lit with a green luminescence flitted around the streets, providing the soft lighting. Sara relaxed as the mist curled around her feet, smiling softly as she looked around the quaint town.

    Sara looked up at Alice sleepily, smiling, as the redhead put a hand on her shoulder. She frowned at Alice’s stern look, then shook her head, looking sheepish. Natalie chuckled at her as she made a show of alertness. Alexia covered up the exchange, asking the ghosts why they didn’t blink, getting nothing but blank stares in response.

    The girls led them to one of the larger houses, asking the krewe to wait outside while they spoke with the mother. After just a moment, they left, gesturing for them to go inside. The Mother sat on a comfortable chair, her long dark hair piled on top of her head. The black velvet of her choker stood out against her pale skin, and her gray and dark red dress suited her thin frame. The powerful geist gestured at the other chairs, inviting them to sit.

    “I see you have stumbled on my little paradise.”

    “Paradise? Then why are you trying to leave?”

    “I’m not actually taking them into the world, however, everyone feels better with something to work towards.”

    The Mother explained her vision, extolling the virtues of her little paradise. Sara’s discomfort faded somewhat, as the Mother explained how she accepted those who were afraid, giving them a safe place to rest, sealed off from the rest of the Underworld and its dangers, until an enemy caused the breach in the ceiling. She even explained the similarities:

    “My changed men are well muscled, my changed women unblemished. Eventually one gets tired of everyone being prettier than them.”

    It did not fade completely, as her Passion Oracle left a distinct impression of something being withheld. She kept an eye on Alice as well. The redhead’s hackles were up and distrust screamed from her every line.

    Keeping an eye on all of them, the Mother offered a deal. Raw materials, food and perhaps some finery in exchange for reshaping - ghosts, places, whatever they wanted. Sara’s eyes lit up when the Mother mentioned reshaping places. Alice frowned at her. Sara caught the redhead’s eyes and gave her a half smile.

    “If there’s a deal - and I won’t lie and say it’s not tempting - we need to be open here. I know you’re hiding something... if it’s a deal breaker, we’ll walk out, no harm, no foul. If not, maybe we can arrange some things.”

    “A lady needs her secrets.”

    She looked at Sara and Natalie significantly, as if to say she knew theirs. Sara linked her fingers with Natalie’s, leaning in intimately, raising an eyebrow at the Mother. She looked away.

    “Nothing here in the Underworld is truly pleasant. But I am not harming my people. They sustain me. The only ghosts I have destroyed are those who have committed crimes. All those here are here of their own will.”

    “People can be persuaded to do many things...”

    The Mother chuckled lightly at Alice.

    “With this charm and personality? I assure you, this is as friendly as I get.”

    Some verbal dancing commenced and in the end, the only deal made was for free passage in exchange for not attempting to convince away any of the Mother’s flock. The Mother nodded as they left, Alice taking up the rear and keeping an eye on the geist.

    As they left the house Sara looked around, noticing many more ghosts in the immediate area than there had been when they went in. A couple spoke quietly at the corner. A group of men were lugging firewood to a nearby house. A few of the girls were playing hopscotch and a few boys about their age played marbles a few feet away. That was just nearby; the fog was too thick and obscuring for Sara to make out more. Alice peered into it, her eyes narrowed.

    The ghosts followed them, far enough back in the fog that only Alice could make them out. They faded back and out of even her sight once the krewe started climbing the tree out. The group gathered in the tunnel above the orchard. Natalie immediately began writing subtle Sin-eater marks around the edge of the tunnel, warning others off, and explaining to those there what she had learned from observing the geist.

    “She can eat ghosts and spiritual creatures and gain their powers. She used to be much more predatory. I’d wager she was sealed off by someone else, not her own hand, and has shifted into what we saw because of it.”

    “I could see that side of her trying to come out. She’s bad news. I know better than anyone what the predator inside you can push you to do.”

    Alice looked pensive, and pushed for taking the geist out. Natalie, Alexia and Eris nodded even as Natalie continued marking the cave, having moved on to silver spray painted warnings on the walls. Sara bit her lip and Alice softened.

    “If the krewe doesn’t want to do that, it’s fine. It’s fine! But I don’t want anyone else wandering down - or her getting out.”

    Sara recognized the tone Alice got when talking to the Grasping Huntress. She laid on a hand on Alice’s arm.

    “One’s nature is often not fully their own doing. She protects those people now, and tends to them, whatever she may have done once. And... if they chose uniformity, that is, in the end, their choice. But. The lure is strong, both ghosts to beauty and her to her old ways. I won’t object to resealing her paradise.”

    They left it at that, since they had no way of sealing it, nor were they prepared to make an assault. Since Alice and Alexia had explored beyond the hole, they doubled back to the fork in the tunnels, choosing the last path. They spoke softly, bringing back the quiet cheer and camaraderie that had been lost in the Mother’s domain.

    Topics wandered from Pokemon to Barbies to softball as they walked through the tunnels. They paused as the cave opened up into a cavernous room. A wide river cut through it, a small boat bobbing gently against the near bank. Near the boat a figure stood, cloaked but uncowled, his gray bulbous head uncovered. His eyes were bulging orbs and lit with a green luminescent light, similar to that the fireflies in the mother’s domain gave off. A single tree, bare of leaves, grew near the bank, a large owl resting on its branches. The cloaked figure called out to them as they approached, striding towards them.

    “Are you traveling to Cradlemoss? I hail from there, it is a pleasant place. I can provide ferry service, if you wish to cross to there. Or back, but no one ever wishes to leave Cradlemoss.”

    The owl watched them interestedly, his eyes expressing skepticism at the figure’s claims. The owl named himself a Psychopomp, when asked, and countered the cloaked man, calling Cradlemoss a strange place, saying it had suffered under its new Kerberoi. Alice nodded.

    “This is the Underworld. Good places rarely go to so much trouble to draw people in.”

    The two native creatures verbally sniped at each other, revealing that someone had eaten the old Kerberoi, and, at Natalie’s questioning, they confirmed that it was the Mother, or at least who the Mother had been. The cloaked man warned her not to trust the cannibalistic spirit and continued more happily.

    “The new Kerberoi is the Laughing Darkness. He is much more jovial. Fear cannot exist without laughter - he tried to bring both.”

    “He has put people into lockdown in order to get control.”

    “It is what Kerberoi do. It is in their nature. Like yours is to be an annoying prick.”

    The two glared at each other, only stopping to wish the group well as they took their leave. The krewe made their way back, since it was almost when they were due home. They made a quick stop at the diner at Alice’s insistence, to warn the ghosts there about the Mother.

    As they walked home, they discussed plans to deal with Pup Pid. Natalie was fixated on exploding his ship to draw him out, though she did suggest making a deal with the Mother to eat him, with a mischievous look towards Alice who frowned at her. In the end, they decided to try and raise the pirate ship using the tugboat, since an aquatic assault was inadvisable, and Doc might still want the ship. Jasmine featured heavily in their plans, so they certainly weren’t an immediate concern.

    When they got back to the living world, Alice stretched and smiled at the others.

    “Thanks guys. That was a fun time, aside from the Kool-Aid cult.”

    “Kool-Aid! That’s it!”

    Alexia took off for the kitchen, stopping before she got there when she ran into Karen. The younger woman had a tense look on her face and moved to stand next to Natalie and Sara, looking between the two.

    “About fifteen members of my parent’s church came to pray me home. We made them leave the property, so they crossed the street. Tavia called Mr. Malard, and... there’s a lot of cops.”

    Alice’s eyes widened and she took a step back. Karen explained that no one had been hurt - he shot over their heads - but she was worried about the situation in general. Alexia started back off towards the kitchen, muttering about her emergency Malard cookies.

    Alice looked apologetically at the trio.

    “I don’t think I’ll be helpful. In fact, I might make it worse. I think Eris and I will go home, to the park.”

    Karen and Natalie nodded distractedly. Sara smiled up at her, wishing them well. On their way out, Alice and Eris ran into Officer Norris just as he was about to knock. Alice called back for Karen and the trio met him in the foyer.

    “Mrs. Knight. Ladies. I have some paperwork for you, to file a restraining order. Two sets - one for your parents and one for the priest, since he was the one leading them today. There's a pair for your wife, as well. I can’t promise anything, but if you fill them out and bring them to me, I’ll do my best to find a sympathetic judge.”

    The women thanked him and moved to sit at the dining room table. The noise of Alexia rattling around in the kitchen, warming up frozen cookies, contrasted with their grim faces. Karen let out a long breath, looking at the paperwork. Sara stared intently at her until she began to fill it out.

    “I’m sorry about this. I didn’t tell them where we live...”

    Natalie put an arm around Karen, pulling her close as Sara laid a hand on the younger woman’s hand. They let her vent, telling them what had happened, including the threats Tavia used while holding a carving knife. After a few minutes, Alexia came in with a tin of cookies, checking if any of them wanted to go with her. Sara glanced at Natalie who nodded and pulled Karen closer. The tattooed woman patted Karen’s shoulder and left with Alexia.

    Noriko was lurking around the door, watching Malard’s house. She came along, an intent look on her face. Officer Tart stopped them at Malard’s door, barring their way into the ongoing interview. Noriko stared at him, eyes narrowed.

    Sara smiled playfully up at him, laying a hand on his bicep. It didn’t take long for her to sweet talk Tart into delivering the cookies to Malard and the other officer. There were no raised voices from inside, so Sara gently touched Noriko’s shoulder and guided her away.

    “We’re trying to stop a bloodbath, remember?”

    “He’s not dead, is he?”

    Noriko turned her unnerving gaze on Sara, who nodded noncommittally. When they got back, Noriko took up her perch at the window overlooking Malard’s house again, eyes fixed. Sara rejoined her wives, settling in next to Karen, encouraging her to keep filling out the paperwork as she relayed what had happened.

    As soon as the police left, Noriko was out of the house and on her way over there. Karen looked over at Sara, a hangdog expression on her face as she rubbed the heel of her hand. Sara grinned and suggested a movie. Karen’s eyes brightened and she suggested Black Swan.

    The trio wandered the house, trying to find the others to invite them along. They found Chandra, Catherine and Alexia together, with Angelique nearby. Sara asked about the memory retrieval.

    “I found a whole bunch of suppressed memories. It was starting to really hurt. I could maybe push through, with some coffee or something.”

    Sara looked concerned, encouraging her to be careful. Angelique gave her a wry smile.

    “I suggested she avoid hurting herself.”

    “It’s just an out of practice muscle. I could...”

    Sara gave her a look and suggested that she practice rather than push. Catherine conceded the point, especially since she wasn’t sure how much of Angelique’s more recent psyche would be intact if she burst through the walls blocking off those suppressed memories.

    On the way to the cars, Catherine looked apologetically at Karen.

    “I’m sorry I wasn’t more help, earlier. Large hostile crowds make me nervous. No one’s called me a witch yet, but... they were very angry. “

    Catherine bit her lip nervously, glancing at Natalie.

    “And Natalie – you'll be careful?”

    Sara patted the psychic's shoulder and put an arm around Natalie’s waist, telling the amusing story of a time she had been accused of being a witch. She smiled hopefully at Natalie, who was still tense and unhappy. Natalie smiled wanly back, linking arms with her behind Karen and shifting all three of them closer together.


    Natalie’s Tweets

    December 8th, 2010

    I am in danger.

    Karen’s parents came to protest outside the house, with about 15 people from our church, praying loudly. Tavia menaced them into going across the street to Malard’s house, and he ended up firing on them. The cops were called, and when we returned from irritating a little god we wound up dealing with Gwen’s old friend Officer Norris. That was awkward. He is not quite sure about us. About queer people in general. He seems to be doing his job, though.

    Norris told me he was worried about me. These people think I am an evil manipulator who has tricked Karen into turning her back on God. Even if true, which is not, She made the choice. She was, however, quite gay and quite Pagan before we ever met. But it doesn’t matter now. These people might hurt me. They haven’t…yet…tried, but when an Officer is worried, I suppose I should pay attention. It is so hard, as a queer woman, as a drug addict, as an ex-call girl, to not see him as my enemy. He sees how I look at him. Karen sees a hero. I see someone who has hurt me, confined me, hit me. Not Norris himself, but cops.

    I listened to Norris’s advice, and Karen and I talked, and made some decisions. She is pretty much done with school short exams, and I am going to take her to the exams with at least one other person. We won’t go out by ourselves. This goes double for me; I doubt they want to hurt her. I doubt they would have any qualms about hurting me.

    The self-righteous make my blood boil. Not simply Christians, I have many Christian friends. But these people who think by virtue of belonging to this sect and following their god’s rules they are my betters. I hate them because I was one, once. I suffered under their rules, they are not for me. I have become a better person than I was then, looking imperiously down my nose. But I leave them to their master, it is not my place to try to remove them from YHVH’s influence any more than they have any right to try to convert me. But when they mess with me and mine, that is when shit gets real.

    In less than two weeks I go away for a matter of months. Hopefully they will grow bored. I just need to keep a low profile until then. If they want to menace me on return then, well….

    I will show them a witch. And they will find me hard to tie to the pyre. They will, for years, regret menacing me. I’ll teach them fear.

    I’ll show them power.

    End Session CLVIII

    Let’s not channel too much Margaret Hamilton there Natalie.

    Okay the Mother/Matron is a Geist, and a powerful one. I had her set at a Rank of PC Psyche +1, so 7 at that writing. Serious power. Enough to reshape ghosts and the local landscape to a degree. This leaves a lot of questions for how the PCs should handle it.

    Similarly, the homophobia Natalie and Karen face is as dangerously real a problem as the Mother’s problem is fantastical. I am too grumpy and sore to wax rhapsodic, so just pretend I did with notes most LGBTQIA youth don’t have wealthy girlfriends with superpowers to protect them. They still all too often need our help.

    Okay, I am going to find somewhere and curl up and sleep now. I was just too sick to run tonight, so in two weeks stat updates for some of the Krewe. A lot of crazy scheduling stuff is happening but somehow Geist avoids all of it, so happening hopefully without further interruption.

                                                Now that they have targets, the Middle Shadows decide how to move against them. Most immediately, but determining how to unravel the secrets of Pup Pid

                                                Session CLIX

                                                Sara’s Letters

                                                Doc came down for breakfast the next morning with a big smile on his face and greeted everyone brightly. His smile faded as he took in everyone else’s expressions. The woman all looked at each other when he asked what was up, until Karen shrugged and answered.

                                                “It started, I’ve discovered from talking to the police, with my parents protesting outside of our townhouse for a week.”

                                                “The police? Protesting?”

                                                “They and thirty members of their church want to pray my gay away.”

                                                Doc was sputtering mad throughout the rest of the explanation. He did smile tightly and kiss Tavia on the top of her head when Sara described how the slight Fishbourne had shooed off the protestors. Discussion quickly turned to the triad’s safety. Sara pouted teasingly when Karen asserted there would be less PDAs with the tattooed girl outside the house, since so far, Sara hadn’t come under scrutiny by them. She frowned more seriously at Doc’s suggestion she get a pistol.

                                                “I don’t need a gun to escape from harm. I wouldn’t shoot them anyway.”

                                                “Even with a gun, Natalie probably shouldn’t go out alone, or without someone menacing in particular. I mean, Noriko could eviscerate anyone causing problems but she’s generally... not... m-”

                                                Catherine swallowed hard as Noriko stared at her.

                                                “Uh, maybe Tatum’s a better example.”

                                                “I’m an example!”

                                                Doc pushed a little further on the gun issue, but since Natalie already had one and Karen wasn’t old enough to own her pistol legally, it didn’t go far. The conversation drifted towards more active solutions and Alexia spoke up.

                                                “What if they had a bigger problem to deal with?”

                                                “Oh, I can give them a bigger problem.”

                                                Doc and Natalie growled that out simultaneously. Alexia’s eyes widened and she waved her hands, explaining she meant trying to encourage them to get involved with the new Reverend. Karen frowned.

                                                “There are some good, if stupid people in that group. Some of them helped raise me. They think Natalie’s a cult leader and are just trying to look out for me. I’d rather they not get hurt either.”

                                                “So, Natalie could convince them she’s not! She could dress conservatively, with no makeup and deliver homemade cookies to sick kids, or something.”

                                                Sara bit back a smile, eying Natalie with amusement. The strawberry scented woman gave her a stern look, before counter suggesting that she could disperse the protestors with a thunderstorm every time they showed up. Karen’s suggestion that this wouldn’t change their belief that Natalie was a witch got a shrug.

                                                “We could bug the church.”

                                                Karen stared at Chandra dumbfounded, then turned the look on Doc when he pointed out he was planning on learning how to scry for that same purpose. Sara took her wife’s hand, an apologetic look on her face.

                                                “Knowing what they plan to do ahead of time can let us avoid conflict and make sure no one gets hurt.”

                                                “So would hacking their emails! And that wouldn’t violate the privacy of anyone that’s not harassing us.”

                                                Karen turned her frown on Sara, who nodded and shrugged. Doc, who had been looking intently thoughtful for a while, suggested getting Tracey, as a dream mage, to meddle with the protestors. It was Sara’s turn to look dumbfounded.

                                                “The girl with the absolute power that you are so sure will absolutely corrupt her? You want to ask her to submit other people to her potentially mentally traumatic whimsy because they did something stupid that we disapprove of? The one who’s the most in danger of becoming power mad?”

                                                “OK, maybe we could ask the Changelings, then.”

                                                With tensions getting high, the conversation was steered towards Pup Pid for a time. They reached a consensus on the first step in that direction easily - Doc had the ability to summon the Pup Pid in the living world, since it was a spirit. Then they could acquire some sort of sympathetic link to the ghostly Pup Pid, at worst, and possibly also garner some hint as to the nature of the being.

                                                The rest was more complicated, since Natalie continued to be fixated on sending explosives at the ship. There was some argument as to how to safely tow the boat to shore, since Natalie’s ward was stationary and the tug boat would, by necessity, be in motion. Somewhere into it, Alexia’s eyes lit up.

                                                “I could raise it using Industrial Marionette.”

                                                “As long as you promise to go all Margaret Hamilton for it. Or a little ‘Treguna Mekoides, Trecorum Satis Dee’.”

                                                “I’m thinking Yoda.”

                                                Natalie and Karen bickered goodnaturedly on the merits of their approaches. Meanwhile, Tatum looked unsurely at Natalie.

                                                “That chant is from a movie, right?”

                                                Natalie started gently tapping her forehead to the table.

                                                They went over the details in depth, with Sara continuously having to steer Natalie away from Plans whose first step was ‘fire an explosive at the ship’. Their final plan, allowing for modifications that interrogating Pup Pid might lead to, was to have Alexia carry the bane gun inside her until it was needed, and after Jasmine illuminated the ship itself with Doc’s ghost flashlights, raise it to a shallow enough area that the group could fight in. With any luck, Pup Pid would send a good sized contingent to stop the ship being dragged up, which Natalie could trap in a ward and the group could then blow them up, safely away from the ship. Hopefully then, the remaining crew would be manageable.

                                                That settled, talk turned back to living concerns. Tatum asked whether Karen and Natalie should carry GPS trackers, in case they were snatched. That brought up the Finding ritual, and prompted Doc to offer to teach Tatum his style of ritual magic. They discussed a couple of differences between Doc and Natalie’s magics, with Karen pointing out she followed Doc’s path when Tatum looked torn.

                                                “Of course I went with luck magic. You’ll notice I’m sleeping with all these beautiful women.”

                                                “You were sleeping with some of them before you learned luck magic.”

                                                Natalie raised an eyebrow at Karen and smiled. They went back over the comfortable debate of sorcerer versus witch, with Karen giving over another bulletproof bit of logic.

                                                “Well, I’m not a Witch, and besides, I like putting on my robe and wizard hat...”

                                                “Like Mickey?”

                                                Karen gave Alexia a big smile while Sara tried not to laugh and Chandra looked slightly confused. Doc shook his head at all of them and Tavia offered to make sandwiches for lunch.

                                                -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

                                                The triad was lounging around together while Natalie prepared her ritual chalk with a few drops of human blood to help with the warding circles that would protect Alexia as she raised the ship. At some point, Doc and Alexia headed out to go shopping and shortly after, Natalie got a text from Doc, warning her that two men sitting in a car a few blocks down had binoculars trained on the road. Rage flashed over Natalie’s face. Sara laid a hand on her arm and looked over at Karen.

                                                “Why don’t you call Officer Norris? He can’t arrest them for sitting in a parking lot, but he can encourage them to move along and it will go on the record and support your restraining orders.”

                                                Karen nodded and made the call. Natalie took a deep breath and got back to what she was doing, albeit more distractedly. A little while later, once Karen was back, Sara’s smile grew into a wicked grin.

                                                “So, Natalie, I know you disapprove of mind control, but... what about encouraging a fanatic’s confidence? TO the point where he explains, honestly and with exacting detail, what his misguided campaign is about and what they are doing there on that corner to the police officer that comes to suggest he leave?”

                                                Natalie began grinning to match Sara.


                                                Shortly before evening hit, the krewe gathered for Natalie’s ritual of blessing. Once eventide, Pup Pid’s usual domain, had arrived, Doc began the summoning. Natalie had sent Tatum out of the house for her safety, and Alexia had bowed out, given her fear of skeletons, but the rest of the krewe, as well as Angelique, Karen and Noriko, were all present.

                                                Natalie sat tailor style on the floor, her was in her lap, with Sara and Karen close. Sara glowed brightly with the light of her Pyre Flame Caul and Noriko was a small cat tucked away in a corner. Doc himself was in his canid form, towering over the ritual circle.

                                                After twenty minutes of summoning, and five of waiting, the skeletal pirate Pup Pid appeared, an ornate sword and pistol pair on his belt and scraps of rotted leather and cloth draped over his bones. He spoke in a low booming voice.

                                                “Who summons the terror of the deep?”

                                                “I do.”

                                                Doc stepped up and gestured towards the offering of rum. Pup Pid crouched down and picked it up, downing the bottle. He wiped his mouth on his arm and asked why.

                                                “Information. I need to know about Pup Pid.”

                                                “Pup Pid is the most dangerous pirate to ever sail the 7 Seas. His ship sank here, in a storm - only the Almighty himself could take Pup Pid. And now in death, only his own weapons can harm him. He hides in the dark waters, rising on the full moon to find his lost gold.”

                                                “What about his ship?”

                                                “It is a powerful galleon, with four cannon and a high crow’s nest. It has carried much booty.”

                                                Sara looked closely and noted the pistol on the spirit’s belt did resemble the pistol they’d found. It was simply embellished, as the story the spirit was telling them surely was as well. She also noted that it had shown no sign of recognition of any of them, nor of Karen - Doc had theorized that perhaps Pup Pid was behind her feelings of being watched, but that didn’t seem to be the case, especially as the spirit seemed uncertain it had ever been at the house before.

                                                Doc convinced the spirit to leave a token behind, ostensibly to make it easier to summon him for a golden reward. The ghost removed one of the tattered ribbons ending in a charred hunk of metal that had once been a medal plundered from an enemy.

                                                The spirit left quietly and once he’d gone, the group relaxed. Karen grinned.

                                                “So now that we’re done dealing with a ghost pirate, is it on to the plundering booty part of the evening?”

                                                “I like that part of the evening.”

                                                Sara smirked over at her.


                                                Natalie’s Tweets

                                                December 9th, 2010

                                                Today was mostly planning. We got some things worked out.

                                                As far as Karen’s parents, there was a sort of “buddy system” suggested where I or Karen have someone with me when we go out. At first it was just someone intimidating, but there is no consensus on who is intimidating or to who, so I expect the “buddy system” to fall apart in no time. I mean, who is going to go to exams with Karen and wait for hours? It is a good thought, but I doubt it will happen. I am frankly disgusted to think I need someone with me. I don’t appreciate having my freedom curtailed.

                                                Some of the churchgoers, not Karen’s parents, were lying in wake at the light off the highway to follow us, with binoculars and everything. Doc saw them and called back to the house, so Karen called the cops (not that they can do much) and Sara and I schemed to have a little fun with them. We wound up slipping up there and having me use Passion Marionette to embolden them a bit too much when the cops showed. No one would be hurt and they might get arrested and give us fodder for restraining orders.

                                                I only nudged them a bit, not made them do what they did not wish, that is not mind control, is it?

                                                I don’t like what this is doing from me, mentally, morally or emotionally.

Well, control it to sail to shore. To see it, Jasmine will be moving underwater with a light. She will get skeletons attention, but only briefly if Alexia is quick when the boat gets moving. I hope she is careful, Jasmine is special to me.

                                                Whatever she thinks of me.

                                                I will use my Boneyard to trap incoming skeletons. It may take a lot of Plasm, but I am sure we can do it. Then we will use explosives to destroy most of them. Probably. Unless they decide Doc probably can control them. I am skeptical. They would not let me have my army of the dead anyway.

                                                Alexia has the gun within herself, and I think the plan is she will draw it for the Coup De Grace on Pup Pid, so he is unprepared. If he flees into the water, I am sure Jasmine can catch him, but hopefully it won’t come to that.

                                                Should be an exciting battle, but I am super nervous about it.

He could be a sympathetic link to use our powers on Pup Pid from a safe distance. We may can inhibit him significantly.

We still don’t know precisely who Pup Pid is, or was…but if it is a Geist, it may not matter; like the Spirit, it may have become the legend more than the person it actually was.

Perhaps my time with Angelique has caused me to slip a little and become more laissiez-faire with that. That’s not good; in fact that is two lines I have crossed today.
