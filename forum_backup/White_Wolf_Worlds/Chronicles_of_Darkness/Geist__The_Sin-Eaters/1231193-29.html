    And the last preview is here!

    Tyranny rules have officially been forgotten in the mess, but everything else looks great! I love the Absent, whose hunger for Memories make them both terrifying monsters who often have sacrificed their own selves in the name of surviving another day and thieves of the most intimate, and pitiable, sympathetic protagonists because the alternative is just that bad. Also love the internal link they have to gaki (many Absent have learned how to eat corpses to absorb the last Memories of the recently deceased) and their core mechanic of Skill adaptation (an Absent's Skills are bound in their Memories; change Memories, change Skills. One coming back from a market is completely unpredictable, because you don't know what she's loaded today).

    The endgame options are all pretty cool too; I feel there's a point to be made about Catharsis being a part of Catabasis, becoming proof that there is a better way than the mad greed of the Chthonic, and it always ends with a pretty awesome final boss in the form of their Avatar, forced into being to engage a krewe in Titanomachy. Also, becoming a Chthonic God comes off as horrifying, easy, and...ultimately pointless, because what's it matter if the Reapers have visions of one more mad old tyrant? The Underworld just becomes more Underworld-y, at most. Still, if I ever get around to updating the Reality-Bending Horrors section of Second Sight, I am already planning on upholding the spirit of "focus on the human!" while breaking the letter; the main villain is indeed the Chthonic God the cult worships, because previously, the Chthonic God was its high priest. He has not changed in any way that matters; he's just more aloof and needs rituals to directly lead the cult now.

                Cabeiros is indeed terrifying- the fact that geists would always object to use it comes from the fact that it kinda destroys them, but I wonder why aren't there geists who, from their own will, try to rise to Rank 5+ and become Chthonic Gods by their own right.. I assume that such geists are simply not likely to offer a Bargain, and as such are not detailed.

                It just occurred to me that just the book is focused around those Bound which "care for the dead", it also focus around those geists which "care for the living". I assume that just as there are many Bound who exploit and torment the dead and their own geists for profit, there are geists which abuse and torment the living. A geist which offers the Bargain drank at least one time from the Rivers of the Underworld and said "I had enough from this ***". A geist which wants to become a god can do so by forcing itself to swallow the dread water of the Underworld without reaching to the living, and would probably be an ancient and terrible monster which would defile the living in countless different ways. You want to know who the Chthonic Gods are? They are us, countless of selfish souls which devour others of their kind simply because they are too afraid of moving on, and prefer raw power over going to the next stage. The Chthonic Gods are afraid- and they keep on clinging to the world with tainted hands, leeching everything they can and sacrifice their own memories so they won't be forgotten by a world which cast them away. They are not the gods of death- they are dead gods, who wear masks in order to hide their own, terrified faces from themselves.

                Now, can we please, please get a Night Horrors book which details Tyranny based Bound, non friendly geists and unconventional Bargains? I mean, please...
