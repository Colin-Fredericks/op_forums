    Absent and influence

    06-16-2025, 06:24 AM
    Ok, the rules for Absent are great, I love the idea of palyign a ghost (possibly even more than playing an Ephemeral Cultist in the Mummy rules for playable Ephemerals).

    How and ever, apart from all the fun things you can do (join a mummy cult and get a mission which makes following the Mummy's orders giving the Impassioned Shade condition, or become a Familiar to some Mage character...) there are lots of things that a character in a Geist game can do.

    Gain effective rank by donning the Krewe's Regalia of Eminence (does this change the ban/bane to the equivalent of a rank 3 entity? Or is it not an issue since rank 2 and 3 ghosts have the same kinds or bans&names).

    Resolve anchors and gain protection from essence bleed - but losing their influence, and thus being more detached from the world (for a player character would this be an endgame scenario, rather than allowing the character to continue in play with no anchors? And thus no influence).

    Gain rank by working on their memory (it is clear there are two main ways which Ghost gain rank, by being infused with the energies of the Underworld - death masks/drinking from the rivers - Or by working with memories of themselves, rank 1 to 2 when resolving the Actor condition with the help of the Break the Cycle haunt, or consuming a doppleganger of yourself - which presumably has memories from a different time in the ghost's life). However the rules assume you must drink from the Waters of the Underworld *only*, and that becoming a Geist makes a character unplayable (which is fine, but if I want a rank 3-5 playable ghost for high levels play, not so much fun).

    So I'm going to assume that (by the book) an Absent character with a Krewe (with the Regalia to make it rank 3) has to buy up all the numina/manifestations/attributes/influence it needs to act as an effective rank 3 entity. And further that resolving Anchors is a double sided blade (losing the ability to use influence without replacing them or getting sanctity of merits type benefits, while also being protected from Essence bleed, and being able to roam freely... You still need to spend 1 essence to remain active for a day - which might be easy to find if you take over a slaughter house and eat the rank 0 Barghest which are created there daily... Or a rubbish yard and consuming castoffs).

    Ideally you want your Krewe to be something of an Ancestory worshiping cult and make it up of people who remember you when you were alive. With a weekly ritual aimed at boosting your essence (or at least ritual sacrifice of some objects/animals to allow the cast off/Barghest being consumed for essence).

    Family members, friends, ideally a few of them trained as necromancers... Does a Krewe require any Bound? Or is it just easier to form one? (Maybe a Krewe whose bound have all passed on...) yeah, this would be becoming a NPC cult/antagonist...

    At which point, apart from the obvious (consuming Dopplegangers, Shades or goetia of the same person, and literal magic) is there anything a Ghost can do to increase their power further - without becoming a Geist/Reaper?

      Gain rank by working on their memory (it is clear there are two main ways which Ghost gain rank, by being infused with the energies of the Underworld - death masks/drinking from the rivers - Or by working with memories of themselves, rank 1 to 2 when resolving the Actor condition with the help of the Break the Cycle haunt, or consuming a doppleganger of yourself - which presumably has memories from a different time in the ghost's life).       So I'm going to assume that (by the book) an Absent character with a Krewe (with the Regalia to make it rank 3) has to buy up all the numina/manifestations/attributes/influence it needs to act as an effective rank 3 entity.
      Yes, you need to spend at least 19 Experiences to get from the maximum stats for a Rank 2 ghost to the minimum stats for a Rank 3 ghost, which still requires you to drink from a River per Rank.

      Ideally you want your Krewe to be something of an Ancestory worshiping cult and make it up of people who remember you when you were alive. With a weekly ritual aimed at boosting your essence (or at least ritual sacrifice of some objects/animals to allow the cast off/Barghest being consumed for essence).
      If a ghost belongs to a krewe it can gain Essence in place of Willpower from participating in actions that collectively fulfill its Virtue and/or Creed.

      Family members, friends, ideally a few of them trained as necromancers... Does a Krewe require any Bound? Or is it just easier to form one? (Maybe a Krewe whose bound have all passed on...) yeah, this would be becoming a NPC cult/antagonist...
      The Krewe Binding Ceremony does not require a Bound ritualist to perform, nor does an active krewe require its leadership to have the unique partial deathproofing of the Bargain.

      At which point, apart from the obvious (consuming Dopplegangers, Shades or goetia of the same person, and literal magic) is there anything a Ghost can do to increase their power further - without becoming a Geist/Reaper?
      Unique magical bullshit dependent upon your game and setting, as well as more conventional stuff like the Quicken Ghost spell from Mage or the third Tier of the Words of Dead Dominion Utterance from Mummy, same as how the Rank 5 haunted smog from the "Bad Air Day" Story Seed exists without any more mechanics than "this coalescence happens within a week if the weather doesn't change." There are deliberately no mechanics in Geist that are unambiguously designed for a ghost to consistently gain access to massive supernatural power without forsaking their humanity or compromising their morals by drinking from the Rivers or taking up a Deathmask; the +1 Rank from the Regalia of Eminence is as close as it gets.

                      Originally posted by orathaic View Post
                      I'm not sure why you mention the rank 2 doppleganger. Since a ghost of whatever rank can consume any rank of doppleganger to gain rank or the doppleganger rules, iirc.
                      I mention it because a character who never forms a ghost above Integrity 6 needs to form one extra ghost (and/or successful assistance from a master of the Memoria) in order to produce a Doppelgänger capable of reaching the same level as one who creates a ghost with a starting Rank of 2.

                      There are rumour of packs without werewolves, just humans and spirits (possibly shadow Occultist) in one of the antagonist books for WtF.
                      Shadow Occultists can learn and perform Pack Rites without a pack via their connection to their spiritual patrons, and otherwise at least an appropriate form of Wolf-Blooded Tell is needed to perform a Pack Rite if the pack doesn't have the Moon's Grace Merit that specifically can only be taken by packs with no werewolves in them.

                      Mummy has Ephemeral Cultists, though the rules for Absent seem more comprehensive, and I'm still confused by the mechanics of how Mummy works.
                      The base Ephemeral Cultists mechanics are that any type of ephemeral cultist can hold Pillars through the Rite of Investment and treats gatherings of their mummy's cult as Open, with all other effects being particular to the type of entity; ghost cultists aren't forced into the Underworld and thus presumably treat their initiation into a Scorpion Cult as an Anchor in only that sense, still needing actual Anchors and/or living memory and/or ectophagia to gain Essence on top of requiring the relevant Conditions to Manifest.

                      Broadly speaking, it is a thematically enforced point of ghosts in Geist that without very unusual circumstances, you need to step outside of humanity to get greater preternatural power than a handful of Numina, a PFR Attribute spread, and a Trait cap of 7. If you want Big Magic Powers as a ghost, your options amount to drinking deep of necromantic power, getting a supernatural patron, and/or engaging with an unpublished plot device (or homebrew Merits) in or beyond the same general vein as "dedicated supernatural or supernatural-adjacent organizations can tweak the rules of magic."
