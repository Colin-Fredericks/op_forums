                            Originally posted by lnodiv View Post
                            Previous rules simply had the functions of Virtue and Vice reversed (Virtue for a quick hit of a single WP, Vice for everything once per chapter). That also doesn't seem that bad to me, but I think might be the intended verbiage here, too. Should probably be flagged as errata just to be sure?
                            I think so.

                            Personally, to fit the statement of "Everything is stacked against the dead it seems like", the wording should have it be that:
                            Virtue:
                            - The ghost's ideal state or what they "hold in high regard" (AKA identical to Mortals)
                            - Must be detrimental to the character in someway.
                            - Refills all Willpower
                            - Once per scene
                            Vice:
                            - The "go to" behavior for the ghost (identical to Mortals)
                            - Can be easily fulfilled
                            - Refills only one willpower point
                            - Only once per chapter


                            I feel like Vice still being easy but only helps once per chapter is in line with how hard things stacked against them feels.
                            And I feel like Virtue still being hard to do (must be detrimental) jives with the theme of "hustle" that is prevalent in that chapter (balanced out somewhat that you can do it once a scene, but that's still a detrimental thing each scene which can have lots of consequences).

                            If I were to run a game with the manuscript, the above is likely what I'd do.
