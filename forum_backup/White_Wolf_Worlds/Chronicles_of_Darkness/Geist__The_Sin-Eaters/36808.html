    Mementos and modern events

    12-25-2013, 03:25 AM
    Listed here are a few ideas for Mementos for Geist the Sin Eater that don't quite fit the standby of being pieces of Hitler or Pol Pot's toilet, has you'll see below, death takes on many forms, and can touch in unusual ways. After all, everybody's seen Mementos featuring Hitlers shaved moustashe, or Stalin's bedsheets...these touch more modern events, however, and might be a bit more raw.

    Ryan White's Crown: Ryan White was a small child with Hemophilia, by all counts, a sweet boy with his entire life ahead of him. But fate has a funny way of working, the wrong bag of blood, improperly checked, gave him a horrible disease, causing him to fade away from this world at the age of Eighteen from a Respiratory Infection caused by AIDS. Ryan's death touched thousands of people, which gave this item its unusual power. Nobody quite knows WHO pulled out the dental filling from the body of Ryan White, but it exists nonetheless, a nondescript metal dental crown hanging from a necklace made of thread harvested from his hospital gown.

    Ryan White's Crown gives the following benefits

    * +3 empathy: Mr White touched the lives of the entire country, teaching people not to fear and hate the diseased, but rather hate the sickness.
    * Stigmata Key: By blood he was infected, by blood he died, and blood the Sin Eater can control. Holding this object gives the Sin Eater a sanguine sympathy.
    * Death Bling: The standard benefit applies, other then being a fairly prominent death, his funeral was attended by a number of celebrities. Which begs the question of how the crown was harvested, nobody knows, but the sheer balls of whoever did it is more then enough to give the holding Sin Eater a little more respect. After all, if they carry it, and they didn't snatch it up themselves, then they must of taken it from whoever did...And since they got away with it scot-free, it would presumably be rather difficult to do.





    A Notepad of a nameless girl: In 2012, there was a woman in India. Training to be a psychical therapist, she took a ride home one on a bus...she did not arrive at home. The men driving it took her, violated her, beat her, and threw her into the street. She fought bravely to try to drive them off, but in the end, succumbed to the brutal assault, and was left alone on the street, with only tattered clothes, and a single notebook, stained with blood from the assault.

    She did not survive this encounter, and her death gave her notebook power. A Sin Eater found the notebook, and the dead girl, just before police did, and took it up before alerting his Krewe to what happened. One of the perpetrators of the assault was later found dead in his cell after being arrested. Word in the New Delhi Twilight Network is that the aforementioned Krewe of Indian Reapers made a point to track down the rapists, and destroy them utterly. Slaying them, and then binding their ghosts into Fetters where they can never do such vicious harm. So far, only one has been tracked down and slaughtered, but they intend to bring their own brand of justice to the other rapists.

    The Nameless Girl's Notepad gives the following benefits

    * +3 presence: The indignation and passion of the girl's attempts to fight back was recorded in the form of bite marks on her rapists. Her fury is trapped within the notebook, to be tapped by any Sin Eater who bears it
    * Passion Key: While brutally violent, the rape was a crime of passion, and has such, it resonates with the Passion Key. Those who bear the notebook might find themselves feeling a strong sense of indignant justice, the urge to punish those who commit atrocities
    * Death Bling: The death of the Nameless Girl (Going by a number of pseudonyms in the media) touched millions of people world wide, its no small wonder that the bearer would be seen has hardcore for carrying this. But the meat and potatoes of the benefit comes from the Twilight Network rumor about the Krewe hunting down her rapists, its entirely possible that if the bearer of the Memento isn't part of the Krewe, he would be assumed to be





    Sword of the Arab Spring: Despite the name, the 'sword' is little more then a viciously sharp butchers knife tied to a short length of wood, but despite its seemingly fragile construction, it's quite strong and more then capable of dealing severe damage. Carried by a sixty five year old butcher during the revolution that saw the overthrow of Hosni Mubarak, he brought it with him during a protest, more a means of defending himself and deterring muggers then striking out against the government, he was far too old to be a serious threat to anybody. However, despite this, the Mubarak regime saw him has a threat, and when the president ordered the army to fire on the protestors, he was caught in the middle on his way home from church.

    Twenty four people died in that volley, inciting the protestors to even greater degrees of fervor, in a matter of months, Mubarak was overthrown. Nobody payed the dead man much mind, he had no family, and in the chaos of the revolution, he was forgotten...save by a single man who had been standing next to him when the shots where fired. He died, but unlike the Butcher, he came back has a Sin Eater, a new ally lurking in his skull. The Sin Eater picked up the old man's weapon, and still carries it with him today has the Egyptian turmoil continues.

    In a very real way, the dead have controlled Egypt for centuries, the vast amount of tombs and mummies leading to myriad ghosts with connections yet tying them to the mortal coil (And more then a few are a bit miffed about their bodies being desecrated for medicine and paint pigments). Throw in those who have died in the protests, and there is a very good reason why the Sin Eater who carries the Sword is unwilling to part with it.

    The Sword of the Arab Spring gives the following benefits

    * Stillness Key: The old man died quietly, his lungs flooding with blood from the gunshot wounds, preventing him from making so much as a sound.
    * +5 equipment bonus (Being an improvised weapon, it does a base of 0 L. Merits and other abilities that enhance or work with improvised weapons still effect the sword)
    * Death Bling: Really, how hardcore is it to be shot down at the same time has someone, get raised a few moments later by a Geist, and then steal the guy's weapon? That's the sort of thing Heavy Metal songs feature. Throw in the chaos of Egypt at the moment, and the increased number of ghosts, and anybody coming out of Cairo these days with a Geist is probably seen has one bad mother.
