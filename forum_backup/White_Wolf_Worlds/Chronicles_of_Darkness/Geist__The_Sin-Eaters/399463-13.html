                                                    Speaking of ghosts with masks, are Deathmasks, charms and Sin-Eater culture getting an overhaul? Always thought it was a bit weird to have Deathmasks as a corpse of a Geist and that other Sin-Eaters see that as a positive thing. As a houserule in the Geist game I was in, Deathmasks were taken a bit more literally as something that the Sin-Eater could wear that their Geist would materialize to symbolize their bond and would give a bonus to some specific rolls if you kept on appeasing its desires. It seemed to me to be something more fitting as a status symbol among other Sin-Eaters to demonstrate the power of the bond between the pair.

                                                    We felt that it was better for antagonists Sin-Eater Krewes to collect the corpses of other Geists, and consumed them and other ghosts to empower their Krewer Avatar/Aspect. As something along the lines of a way of trying to bully others into joining them and their believes or suffer the consequences.

                                                    Unrelated question, would you list Beetlejuice as one of the possible inspirations to keep in mind for 2e?
