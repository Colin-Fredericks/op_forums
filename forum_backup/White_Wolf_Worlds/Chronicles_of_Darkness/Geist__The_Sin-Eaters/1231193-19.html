    Originally posted by HelmsDerp View Post
    it seems like it would be faster to just repeatedly use Keys -1 bonus Plasm until you had a full pool and that feels... off
    Using a Key without getting the Doomed Condition requires that you:
    1. Be using it with a Haunt, a power which itself costs at least 1 Plasm and does not end until its baseline Condition does (which is easier/less prone to complication with some Haunts than others but still mostly requires you to have a target), and
    2. Spend an additional 1 Plasm on the Haunt, thus requiring that you use a Key whose Unlock Attribute is 3 or higher in order to receive a net gain in your Plasm total.

    For contrast, you can instead use that Key in a resonant situation for heightened odds of exceptional success, which gives you back half the Plasm you spent on the Haunt after it ends without difficulty in addition to exempting you from the Doom.

    So, in short, you can't get the free Plasm without the Doom unless you use a Haunt and you stand to get better returns from just using the Key normally — and in cases like "what if I Extrude the Caul over and over," you have the fact that annoying your geist can give you a Condition that makes dealing with it harder to manage.

        • When this Condition resolves, the Sin-Eater who created it also takes a Beat.

        Doom: The next time your character speaks even a single word, resolve this Doom and the Condition from the Haunt she unlocked with the Key of Stillness.

        1. Activate Stillness Curse on random target. Reap Composure - 1 Plasm. Accept Doom.

        4. Being Doom-free and up Composure - 1 Plasm, repeat from step 1.

                                            Fingers crossed for the Stygian Key and the Pit making a comeback.

                                            re the "Refueling plasm exploit", as ST I would put serious repercussions on such a thing: For one, they are repeatedly opening a door into the Underworld, and two, the Doomed condition is something like getting the Underworld's attention. The fluff literally says they find themselves controlled by the Old Laws that created the key. I imagine that doing this repeatedly, within a short time frame to boot, is not going to not have consequences. This is more than enough of an excuse for me as ST to allow this behaviour, but abuse the heck out of it to throw out repercussions to hell and back. Imagine the player's realization that the 'exploit' comes back to bite him in the ass severely when e.g. the Doom somehow gets 'stuck' (cannot be resolved for a few scenes) or he cannot stop oozing plasm, attracting more and more ghosts and with them, Reapers. Or have the Key 'break' under the stress of being used so often, and he has to do x to fix it. X could be something like going a night/day/week without spending Plasm, helping a certain number of ghosts pass on (preferably thematically appropriate to the Key), OR eat a certain ghost / sacrifice a ghost to the Key.
                                            You could also just have the Geist be pissed at having to do this stupid act over and over again. Especially Shroud and Coil could end with the Geist going "nope, imma stay in control now, you've pushed me around enough'.
                                            All for a good story and good time at the table, of course.

                                            Regarding the Key of Stillness, I am more than certain that there will be an addition, since breaking the doom by just talking and no other drawback is too easily done. I can imagine that the Mute condition will instead be forced upon the player, or alternatively Deafened.
