      1) a soul that never produces a ghost is not in any place Sin-Eaters know where they go. Usual afterlife beliefs come along here.
      2) Depends. If the anchors resolution is such that they are able to move on, they do not go to the Underworld. Otherwise they do.
      3) Any ghost can move on. Underworld ghosts are less likley to without outside intervention.
      4) More ghosts are in the Underworld than Twilight, probably, just because most ghosts who do not move on end up there eventually. There is no answer in the setting beyond that to any of this.
      5) I cannot parse this question, can you rephrase?
      6) Most Sin-Eaters assume ghosts are people. Not all of them do. Remember Sin-Eater beliefs vary Krewe by Krewe. People abusing ghosts assuming they are not people can certainly cause conflict, but remember some Sin-Eaters do that. Sin-Eaters do know helping them move on infuses them with plasm and something clearly happens, so more are inclined to belive they are people than not.
      7) Varies Krewe by Krewe, remember beliefs vary by Krewe. They would probably view them as really powerful ghosts, though Sin-Eaters are good at handling even really powerful ghosts.

                                                          Here's what I've learned on the subject, being particularly interested in this part of the cosmology and watching these discussions for years. I think there's a dissonance between what the readers think of when they use 'soul' and what it means in the gamelines - particularly mage. It's a very fuzzy term to begin with, and probably a poor word to have these discussions with. What we probably should be asking is: are ghosts people?

                                                          The Word of Dave (from the other thread I linked) is that mages are able to sense a... thing inside of humans and it is related to the subtle arcana. They call this a soul. Their magic for severing souls, creating soulstones, etc interacts with this soul thing; they have the ability to sense and manipulate it. The ones that do can say for certain that mages have it, and its brighter/more potent, or something; ghosts do not have it. Also, werewolves do not have it, nor changelings, demons, or a lot of other supernatural things.

                                                          Werewolves don't have souls. Are they still people though - in a respect for life, sentience, or whatever sense of the word?
                                                          Ghosts don't have souls. Are they still people?

                                                          The philosophy of Sin-Eaters varies between individuals and krewes, but the base assumption of the game is that most Sin-Eaters still see ghosts as being people, and most believe that what happens to a ghost matters. The how gets a lot fuzzier. But they can't not see and hear ghosts - and ghosts recognize what they are, and pester them a lot; they live their lives with these being. They probably get to know them better than living people, because in a big city, the living can all notice each others and interact, so there's no urgency to do so, but ghosts need things done, and the vast majority of the people they see will never know - the effect is probably like living in a very small town or hobbiest club. All Sin-Eaters eventually see a ghost pass on: they know that when their anchors are resolved, relief and joy fill them up and they drift peacefully into the mysterious beyond; those that are violently destroyed vanish in agony and despair. Most decent human beings would decide at that point that ghosts are still people, and one of these outcomes are better than the other, and that there's probably some kind of cosmic reason that occurs.

                                                          Mages, on the other hand, can cast a spell and say with a certainty that humans have a thing inside them, and ghosts do not; the next logical step is that humans are better than ghosts - at least in a way similar to humans being better than other animals. At that point, it's a lot harder to ignore the political pressures of Necromancers who can do really useful (but morally shady) things with ghosts. Besides, the supernal is the only truth that matters, right? and ghosts have clearly lost their connection to that, so... At best, it's a Mystery. At worst, its justification to use and abuse them however you damn well please.

                                                          Obviously this is a sweeping generalization. Not all mages or sin-eaters will think this way - but enough of them might to perpetuate a stereotype. I see a basic difference in their lifestyles and abilities which lead them to different answers, and which probably propel them into conflict. Sin-Eaters are likely to hate them for slavery and abuse; Mages are likely to be perplexed at why people care so much about what amounts to video tapes.


                                                          That's the perspective I've come to anyway.

                                                          [TLDR: ghosts aren't souls, but they're probably people - and yeah, mages and sin-eaters would probably start fights over that.]
