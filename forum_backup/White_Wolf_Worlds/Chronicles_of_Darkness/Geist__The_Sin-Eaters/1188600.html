    New Keys for 2e

    02-22-2018, 03:07 PM
    One of the changes in 2e seems to be a change in the conceptual places of Keys in the setting. While in 1e they were a set of abilities meant to manipulate the existing powers of the Bound, now they are more of thematic representation of the character's death, which seems to me like more of a Kith/Judge like splats than actual powers. As presented, each of the Keys covers a certain death, and is connected to a certain attribute. While the presented nine Keys present a complete set, it does not mean, however, that we can't potentially have more than one such "set", that is, having new Keys separated by their own themes and Doom, but which share an attribute with one of the existing Keys. As we do not yet know how Dooms actually work, we can't homebrew them right now- but we can already start thinking about what new, potential Keys may exist, and to which attributes they may be connected.

    After thinking about it for a bit, here is what I've got-

    The Key of Winter Night - the Frozen Key, the Key of Snow and Sleep, the Key of Argent Solitude. Represents death by cold, and connected to Stamina.

    The Key of Poisons- the Venomous Key, the Key of Sting and Toxin, the Key of Prismatic Pain. Represents death by poisons, venom, acids, drugs and all other nasty chemicals, and connected to Manipulation.

    The Key of Lightning Hit- the Thunder Key, the Key of Storm and Wire, the Key of Indigo Consumption. Represents death by electricity, and connected to Dexterity.

    The Key of Sun Burn- the Nuclear Key, the Key of Boil and Radiance, the Key of Vermilion Suffering. Represents death by radiation, both sunlight and nuclear, and connected to Composure.

    The Key of Sorcery- the Occult Key, the Key of Curse and Spell, the Key of Unseen Damnation. Represents death by supernatural intervention, and is connected to Intelligence.

    The Key of Sacrifice- the Martyred Key, the Key of Blade and Altar, the Key of Platinum Offering. Represents death by self sacrifice or faith, and is connected to Resolve.

    The Key of Industry- the Clockwork Key, the Key of Time and Order, the Key of Smokey Exhaustion. Represents death by work overload, and is connected to Wits.

    The Key of Hunger- the Hollow Key, the Key of Void and Need, the Key of Rusting Passion. Represents death by hunger, and is connected to Presence.

    The Key of Punishment- the Vengeful Key, the Key of Law and Execution, the Key of Iron Tyranny. Represents death by execution, and is connected to Strength.

    Of course, one may find certain overlapping themes between Keys- where is the line between poison and sickness? A person who dies alone in the desert or from hunger in the basement of a madman may be Silent, but may also be Sun Burned/Hungry. Being hit by a lightning is death by electricity or chance? And what about getting killed by a supernatural curse? And when does a death by sacrifice or punishment becomes different enough from the way of the execution? I assume that such overlap is only natural, and I expect some reference to it in the book, but I assume that the chosen Key would be the ones with the strongest association, and that there is a reason why the character actually starts with 2 Keys (the ones of her own death, and the ones represented by the Geist).

    So what do you think? Do you have ideas of your own for new Keys?

      The Key of Haze – The Torpid Key, The Key of Sheets and Succor, the Key of Pearlaceous Lethargy.
      Unlock Attribute: Resolve
      Resonance:
      Doom:
      Deaths occurred through a sleeping disorder: either fatal hazard due to somnambulism, sleep deprivation, or a supernatural, lethal oniric encounter.

      The Key of Bliss – The Rousing Key, the Key of Sighs and Moans, the Key of Scarlet Excitement.
      Unlock Attribute: Presence
      Resonance:
      ​Doom:
      Death occurred directly because of sexual or autoerotic activities. Tied to Presence.

      The Key of Labor – The Barren Key, the Key of Toys and Tenderness, Key of Lavender Innocence.
      Unlock Attribute: Stamina
      Resonance: The Key of Labor is Resonant whenever the life of a sentient being that clearly can't fend off for themselves due to its immaturity is in line. That includes children and Rank 1 Ghosts still unaware of their death.
      ​Doom:
      Mothers (or, exceedingly rarely, infants) dead during labor.

      The Key of Faded Trail – The Vagrant Key, the Key of Path and Compass, the Key of Ebon Nostalgy.
      Unlock Attribute: Intelligence
      Resonance: The Key of Faded Trail is considered resonant when targeting a being with the ability to access and leave Twilight, but who is not attuned to Death-Twilight.
      ​Doom:
      Mortals dead after having unknowingly - or unwittingly -crossed a portal leading to one of the liminal realms beyond mundane reality: a Twilight Verge, a Hedge Gateway, a Beast's Primordial Pathway, etc.

      (NB. This Key obviously doesn't account for places too removed from our plane that even supernatural denizens have little chance to reach them, such as the Supernal Realms, Arcadia or the Duat. It is unknown if a mortal having died in the Underworld could form a Bound displaying this Key.)

      The Key of Myrtle Crown – The Glittering Key, the Key of Fame and Chatter, the Key of Purple Ingratitude.
      Unlock Attribute: Manipulation
      Resonance: The Key of Myrtle Crown is resonant whenever the Sin-Eater is socially put on the spot, and submitted to the judgment and scrutiny of a critical audience.
      ​Doom:
      Deaths tied to fame, gossips. rumors and slander. Tied to Manipulation.

      The Key of Alliance – The Twining Key, The Key of Awe and Mirror, The Key of Coral Resolution.
      Unlock Attribute: Composure
      Resonance:
      ​Doom:
      Death to imitate or join people, or to conform to a trend (Werther Effect, suicid pacts, blue whale challenge, etc). Tied to Composure.

      The Key of Evasion – The Slippery Key, the Key of Maze and Minefield, the Key of Fulvous Defiance.
      Unlock Attribute: Dexterity
      Resonance:
      ​Doom:
      Death while trying to cross a spatial boundary one's not supposed to cross (trying to break in or out of a bank, trying to escape from a prison, being shot while trying to cross a no man's land, etc.)

      The Key of Crowds – The Coiling Key, the Key of Rush and Noise, the Key of Ochre Panic.
      Unlock Attribute: Wits
      Resonance: Whenever the Sin-Eater attempts to get closer to a thing or being, in spite of it frightening enough people to makes them run away in the opposite direction, it is considered resonant to the Key of Crowds.
      ​Doom:
      Death due to crowds (stampede, riots).

      --------------------------------------------

      The Key of Coconut - The Staggering Key, The Key of Palm and Beach, the Key of Brownish Bowling Balls. Death due to coconut falls. Tied to Dexterity.

      ('k I'm joking with the last one. Still, it would be a cool character.)
