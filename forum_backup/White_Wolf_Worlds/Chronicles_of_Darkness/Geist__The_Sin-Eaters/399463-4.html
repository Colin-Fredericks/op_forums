                    Originally posted by Claire Redfield View Post
                    I think if I was developing Geist 2E, I'd mostly just refine what was there. The core premise of the game seems great, and the presentation works well for most things. Bring the Underworld into the core book as much as possible. It's been a while since I read the core book, so I don't remember how often the Sin-Eaters go therein, but I'd make the transition about as integral as the Shadow Realm is to werewolves. Kingdoms of bone and ash, rivers of blood and grave-water, etc. Whole realms lost to the Underworld, histories etched in gravestones and black towers. The Underworld is a really fantastic place (in the classical sense of the word), and I'd want to capitalize on its use as much as possible. Sin-Eaters are both at home and foreign there, one part living mortal and one part deathly spirit from the Underworld. Walk in both worlds, the duality of life and death, of forgotten remnants that linger on.
                    I do think the underworld is tricky with Geist, I mean... you have to keep it disturbing and chthonic, despite the games positive attitude and themes of living life to it's fullest and moving on, and give the different domains strange esoteric rules, without making it just seem like the hedge with less plants and more skulls.

                    Oh, and in the same way that packmembers can be Wolf-Blooded and even mortal now, fully playable, I'd have ghosts be part of the extended Sin-Eater "family." Including being playable. They have some advantages and disadvantages over full Sin-Eaters, but it makes them unique.
                    Well, ghosts were made playable in the last breaths of 1e.... but those don't exactly work with 2e's ghost mechanics, so having them as Weaker-Version of Splat would be cool.

                    Personally, I hope they use influences in some way, I mean. Ghosts can be so varied, but the normal keys are rather limited to just elemental stuff and blood.

                        Originally posted by milo v3 View Post

                        Well, ghosts were made playable in the last breaths of 1e.... but those don't exactly work with 2e's ghost mechanics, so having them as Weaker-Version of Splat would be cool.

                        Personally, I hope they use influences in some way, I mean. Ghosts can be so varied, but the normal keys are rather limited to just elemental stuff and blood.
                        Amusingly enough, I've started planning out a dead dominion where it's mostly covered with plants that can serve as mementos (including fruit trees containing the spirits of those who broke the rules, the fruit from which can act as fetters), though the plants tend to look more like glass than normal green-matter. The rules are mostly themed around Don't Step On The Grass and Be Quiet But Not Sneaky.

                        On track though, I think the underworld is kind of required for a game like this. Aside from Sin Eaters inherently having powers to open avernian gates, how do you have a game which deals with humans who can see and interact with ghosts as much as they do and not have a way to access the underworld detailed in the core book? It just fits thematically. Furthermore, I never really saw the Underworld as a different version of the Hedge. Both are threatening, dangerous and so huge one could easily get lost in them and never re-emerge, but you could say that about any shady neighborhood or subway. That doesn't make them the same. Even if not all games enter the underworld, I could probably bet that not every game of Changeling has them ever entering the Hedge again, or any supernatural going into their own inherent otherworld. Just because one group doesn't use it doesn't mean others won't, and get interesting ideas from it.

                        As for keys, I've found them to be varied enough (in spite of my eagerness to make others). We have four elementals, a couple that deal with the human mind (passion and phantasmal), one for animals, one for manmade objects, one for ghosts or blood, and one for the darkness (stillness). Honestly, I see that as variance enough for the basics. I would like a few guidelines for making ones own keys though, or even manifestations - it's a lot of work to try and make manifestations for keys that aren't "X but with a different theme".
