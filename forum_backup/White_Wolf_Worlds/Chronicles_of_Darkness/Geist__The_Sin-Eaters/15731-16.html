                Thanksgiving day has arrived, and the group has balancing to do. Let’s check in. For related reasons to before, Sara’s player could not make. They will return when the immediate Thanksgiving plot is over.

                Natalie’s journal like most of the last few is a short fiction dealing with her family. It did not happen at the table.

                Session CXLIII

                Doc’s Log

                Doc’s Log: 2000 Hours, Thursday, November 25, 2010

                I have a minute to write before I have to go “cook” dinner again so I wanted to get this down if I can.

                I had to rescue Karen from Matron X around 1700 Hours both to get Karen a break and to talk with Matron X. I know where Nightshade got her mischievous streak from. Anyway, once we got to the Game Room and away from the others, I apologized. She seemed annoyed that I knew she had seen me more than anything but told me I had nothing to apologize for. We talked about things openly (without me outing anyone or any other secrets not Sin-Eater related) for a while. Matron X is actually very content with her powers, which is not how I expected it. I also mentioned how I was trying very hard not to let my reflection get near Belladonna. She was perhaps more appreciative of that than if she hadn’t seen my death wounds. She can also see ghosts in reflections it seems. Naomi bothered her a little, but she hadn’t seen the fish so it could have been worse. I told her about the dinner later and that Nightshade had organized it. She might not have intended to, but Matron X was very proud of her daughter for caring for those that couldn’t care for themselves. Especially this way.

                Once I felt like I had given Matron X as much info as she could safely understand and just talk without a wall of secrets, I let her get back to her vacation. Sadly, that meant going straight for Karen again. I would have tried to help a little more, but Noriko came back in from her walk with Axe, Eris, and Jacob. She immediately jumped up on my shoulders, which was funny for a lot of reasons, not the least of which was that she realized she was still wearing the kimono about half way through the air. We talked together for a while before everyone gathered just to wait for food. That became a little more uncomfortable as everyone watched Karen squirm form Matron X’s line of questions. Eventually, I stepped in with the only helpful thing I could think of.

                Dancing.

                I led as many as were interested into the Music Room and put on Sarah Vaughan’s Summertime. Then I offered my hand to Matron X, both to keep her involved and to put her in the spotlight for a little bit. The song works for a foxtrot and it’s slow enough that I could lead without dragging Matron X around the floor. When we were done, we got an ovation and Matron X looked surprised, happy but surprised.

                Cupcake came in and announced dinner was ready. We all fell into the Dining Room to find enough food for a week. Mom made enough food to feed the entire gathering but it still paled in comparison to what Cupcake presented. She mentioned there were two types of everything when I realized that she and the Cracked Cook were competing again. I knew this was my chance to figure out which one was which. I got a small portion of everything, except Mom’s cooking. The tamales and elote are not something I can get anywhere nearby, plus it was homemade! And I wanted to weep when I had some of the frijoles borrachos. It had been too long. But, I did get a taste of everything Cupcake had to offer and this gave me a good idea of both styles. Given I’ve eaten Cupcake’s meals alot, I was able to pick out hers from the Cook’s. I made sure to compliment everything, but looked at Cupcake directly when I spoke of her dishes. All in all, I think Thanksgiving Dinner went very smoothly. I was a little surprised when Matron X announced that she was going to 27 Club tomorrow night, but that should be fine. Not sure what Mom thought about it, but should be okay.

                Now I have my own cooking to do.


                Doc’s Log: 1130 Hours, Thursday, November 25, 2010

                I am truly not a chef. Fortunately for our spectral guests, the food was made by an excellent one. I just had to bring it across. It took a half hour before I could get the ceremony to work, but it finally did and I was able to go up with Nightshade. We brought drinks and sat with our gathered deceased. Troy, Naomi, Marjorie, and especially Marcy had not seen a spread like we brought in a really long time, if ever. Having tasted of everything already, I could have normal conversation about it and not just awkwardly sit without eating. If anyone would have looked into the room, they would have seen Nightshade and I casually chatting with different people that weren’t there in front of a table with nothing on it. Lucky for them, no one did.

                After both dinners were finished, everyone wandered into the TV Room. When we joined them, Axe convinced Jacob to tell “The Bigfoot Story.” If someone else had been telling the story, I think Belladonna would have been less attentive, but she did a good job of not oooing and ahhing. I wonder if I can talk him into telling it again with Barrett around. Once he was done, I saw Matron X look toward Karen again and decided something else needed to keep everyone’s attention. I invited everyone into the Music Room for dancing. Then I asked Mom to dance.

                I think she must have taken it more as a challenge than an invitation. I didn’t know if Mom knew how to ballroom dance or not, but she kept up with me in a waltz easily. Nightshade and Karen followed with a pair of dances, strictly for showing off. A tango to the Addams Family theme and then a stylish recreation of the contest dance from Pulp Fiction. Mom was looking through the vinyl after they were done. She frowned and asked me why we didn’t have any proper dancing records in Spanish. I knew she wanted to Flamenco, so I hit the laptop Nightshade wired into the sound system. It took a moment, but I found a decent quality traditional guitar and clap meant for Flamenco. Mom was apparently looking to prove that I wasn’t the only one that could dance. She set fire to the floor in a way that I couldn’t have imagined. The applause was fitting for the number in attendance. The smirk Mom gave me as she walked back told me she had won whatever challenge she thought was issued. Jacob asked Belladonna to dance and though she was obviously terrified, she accepted. He made her look good for not knowing a step and I think I saw Matron X pointing something out to Nightshade from the corner of my eye, but I’m not sure what was happening.

                When he came back, Axe asked Jacob if he could show her and Eris how to dance. She looked very nervous but he agreed. What that went on, Nightshade and Karen danced more normally for them, and then it was a group dance. Everyone started pairing off, though I did start a Conga Line at one point. Once I was off the floor but everyone else was still dancing to whatever they could, Jacob appeared beside me as if on cue. I think it’s the power of storytellers to always be in the right place at the right time. Anyway, he mentioned that no one was dancing with Matron X. I looked around and found her alone to one side. I think she was just watching the event but I took the hint. When I walked up, I asked if she’d like to dance again. She smiled and asked if we could Shag. I’m very glad I knew she was from the Carolina coast. I was not very good at that style dance, but she made it look simple. I can attest, it was not. Still, it got her confidence and maybe a little of that mischief up. She then marched over and called Karen out. That might have been the most awkward I’ve ever seen Karen on the dance floor.

                As their set was winding down, I asked Nightshade if she’d like to do something special. It was partly for me, partly for Mom, and if I’m honest, partly to show off. I knew Noriko didn’t know how to ballroom dance and was already nervous around Mom. So I asked if we could do a Pasodoble. I again used the internet for music and Nightshade as my cape. If Mom wasn’t sure I could be Mexican enough, I think I proved my roots.


                Natalie’s Tweets

                Homecoming III

                “I feel wonderful, you have no idea,” the older woman said. Julie Lousler was sitting on the couch with her daughter Allison. Allison’s daughter Natalie, Julie’s granddaughter, lay across their laps, fast asleep. Her mother played idly with her hair.

                “I am glad mom. It’s important to me you feel better. I do seem to have that effect on people,” said Allison, smiling.

                “Well, it’s you, it’s the whole group. Karen, Sara, Doc…they make me feel so welcome, and we have such interesting conversations. The older woman chuckled. “I am almost afraid I will have to remind them I am not their age.”

                “It doesn’t matter, Mom. You are you, that’s what matters. You fit in here.” She paused. “You know, you could come here more often. I mean, I will see you on Christmas, but we should arrange to come here more times. You should come in what passes for warm here in the summer,” the younger woman said, smirking at the last part.

                “I do feel almost like I have been sleepwalking for quite awhile, I admit. Stepping away from work, housework, all that…it’s unreal,” Julie looked a bit distant. “Sitting here with you…I wonder if I am dreaming.”

                “You aren’t. I’m here. You know…if you have been ‘sleepwalking’…maybe…you should date again. Live life again,” Allison chose her words carefully.

                “I don’t know, I am a bit old, and I don’t know if anyone would be interested.”

                Allison pointed at her sleeping daughter, “Someone has suggested you get quite a bit of attention from men at church,” Allison leaned closer, bringing with her the scent of strawberries, “And you are related to me, so I know you notice.”

                “I do, but moving on is hard,” The older woman sighed.

                “That could be the title of my biography,” Allison said. “Just an idea. Not pushing.”

                “It WOULD be a man, you know,” the older woman smirked at her daughter.

                “So? I am obviously not against such arrangements. Some of my best friends are in straight relationships,” Allison gave an exaggerated grin.

                “Right. That reminds me…thanks for being okay with me saying grace I remember it was…a point of contention before you left,” her mother looked seriously at her.

                “I was rebelling. I don’t mind anymore,” The younger girl looked serious as well. “Those demons have been put to bed.”

                “Well, I appreciate it anyway.”

                ‘Thanks for giving me the option too. I demurred mostly for Sunny’s sake. She seems to feel out of place as is.”

                The older woman nodded. “That was good of you.”

                There was a pause, as Allison played with her sleeping daughters hair while Julie silently watched her. After a short time, Allison spoke again.

                “So besides Thanksgiving and Christmas, maybe you could come up summer, say July or something. You are off from teaching, and we can ride on the yacht and walk in the park without freezing anything off. Probably a little earlier since the other two are so close together.”

                “My birthday is in June. How about that?”

                “It could work. I would be very happy to see you again,” Allison smiled warmly. Her mother smiled back then looked thoughtful.

                “This is surreal. I was so sure I had lost you, and even if I hadn’t, we weren’t speaking. Just this Easter I would never have believed we would talk again. Yet here we are, full of turkey, sitting and talking like old friends. I’m…happy.”

                There was another pause, but this time both women were smiling. After a moment, Allison spoke again.

                “So what do you think of the others?”

                Julia looked thoughtful, “I really like Karen. She is a sweet girl, and I see in her eyes how she worries about you. Be good to her. She watches you the way a woman should watch her spouse. Sara is fun, and I am conflicted about whether I wish I knew her as a younger woman or not. I don’t dislike her, but I am still making up my mind a little. Doc is very nice, and like Karen he cares about you. Noriko, Eris, and Alice I don’t know what to make of. If you were not…you…I would be worried.,” She looked down at her granddaughter, “I trust you to keep Natalie safe with them around.”

                “Of course”

                “Alexia and Chandra are a cute couple. I met some Christian Indians before but she is the first Hindu…she is Hindu right?”

                “Honestly? No clue. We have not talked about it. She has said she is not Muslim.”

                “She wears Hindu clothes. Anyway, Simon and Tavia are reclusive but nice. Much like Alice and company, I would worry more if…well, you were not you,” Now the older woman was choosing her words carefully.

                “I guess Jacob and Sunny are new to both of us. I like Sunny, she seems to be trying to keep her bearings…working with young people it’s been easier for me. Jacob is rather handsome, of course. Maybe you have noticed”

                Her daughter smiled. “Maybe,” Then after a moment, “I meant what I said, Mom. I really do want to keep seeing you.”

                “I’m glad,” Julie smiled. “And while it may be cliché to say…I am proud of you. You have good friends. You are taking care of a good woman, and from what I hear, you are doing good things. You have grown into a beautiful and confidant young woman, and you care for your daughter. Whatever was between us, I am proud of the woman you have become.”

                Allison sat speechless. “Th-thanks Mom,” she said eventually. I don’t know how to respond.”

                The older woman smiled. “Then don’t. Relax and enjoy a compliment well earned.”

                The still form laying across their laps stirred. “I should go to bed, and take her upstairs too. I leave you to your night, Allison,” Julie helped Natalie up, turned and smiled, then headed upstairs.

                Allison, Natalie to all save two, stood up and got out her phone, sending a text to Doc, Noriko, Karen, and Sara: “Time to work on Mom’s present.”

                Then she smiled, and left to room to stillness, and a lingering scent of strawberries that soon faded with her passing.

                End Session CXLIII

                Pretty much an RP session. These stories are helping PCs flesh out their backstories and advance their character somewhat. I thus don’t have a lot of commentary. There have been some genuinely emotional moments, well played out, and more than one comment of “I am going go and hug my [Parent/Sibling] now.”

                It's worth noting after Doc's initial Dance Natalie and Karen swept across the floor to the Addam's family movie theme.

                Smaller cast than usual next time as we finish up Julie, Natalie and Sunny’s stay. Please join us. In the meantime comments and questions welcome.

                                        11-01-2015, 04:55 AM
                                        Somewhat strange session. Sara’s player had to miss for personal reasons, and Alice’s was under the weather. Doc’s promised to be there, so I had it anyway, and for more than half we just had Natalie and Alexia. Doc skyped in a bit over halfway though, so we got the moms their last evening there so we could move on to getting dear Sara back.

                                        Because of the nature of the session and Doc missing half, Natalie’s journal is just a recap of the session from an OOC perspective. Doc’s Log covers events while he was there. We have an Alice’s journal, but it actually records events from a couple sessions ago her player had been intending to record. So it is not covering the same session Doc and Natalie are. Nonetheless, strange as it may be enjoy the reminder of the Julie and Sunny appearance for their thanksgiving visit.

                                        Session CXLIV

                                        Doc’s Log

                                        Doc’s Log: 1300 Hours, Friday, November 26, 2010

                                        Last night worked out at least as well as everyone hoped, as best I can tell. Which is good because today is rainy as hell so we’re all still stuck together. I don’t think we’ve ever had a night in of dancing. So that’s something to remember as well.

                                        I rolled out of bed about 0700 Hours and enjoyed a simple meditation before stretches and several hours of katas. I don’t think anyone planned to fight with Black Friday madness and my shop is closed so the girls can get a real break. I feel like I might be back to where my muscle memory was at this point. I don’t know how my training would translate to other styles or training methods. I’d like to continue so I’m planning to look online to see if I can find anything. If nothing else, I guess I could call the Naval bases over in New London to see if they have any leads.

                                        I came down after getting cleaned up around 1100 Hours to grab a snack. The rain apparently postponed the hiking trip to Sleeping Giant so Jacob was trying to relax. I don’t think he was doing very well with that.

                                        After lunch, there was talk of some board game that sounded ridiculous to me, so Noriko and I are going to work on teaching each other Spanish and Japanese.

                                        Doc’s Log: 0045 Hours, Saturday, November 27, 2010

                                        That went better than I was afraid it might. Most all the family are heading out later today but Matron X declared her intention to visit the 27 Club while it was open so we all put on the glitz.

                                        Everyone dressed up somehow. Cupcake went very feminine with a mermaid dress and Chandra matched with a top hat and tails. Nightshade and Belladonna dressed like goth twins, which was a little disturbing for me. Matron X dressed in a formal evening gown which got everyone’s attention. Noriko put some extensions in her hair and wore a leather vest that showed off her navel piercing. I didn’t go too crazy since I didn’t want to freak Mom out. I wore a pair of black leather pants and a crimson silk shirt with some wingtips I could dance in, just in case.

                                        Speaking of, Mom seemed entirely too familiar and comfortable in that kind of setting for my brain to process. She didn’t have anything particularly fancy to wear but at one point I glanced over and she looked like still shot from Casablanca or something. Posed, confident, relaxed; it was all very odd. I don’t think I’d ever seen Mom like that before. And I’m not sure what I think about it either. I don’t think anyone expected Matron X to be so fond of Tantrum either. She walked up in a full suit and immediately had Matron X’s attention until she walked away.

                                        We had a fair amount of standard bar talk, which also included Belladonna trying to order whiskey. Then there was the sudden realization that I had made no plans with or even mentioned Christmas to Mom. This was followed by a sense of failure and panic that I was not expecting or prepared for. Honestly I thought I was going to be sick. Matron X realized something was up and tried to smooth things over, but it was for Mom and I to work out. After a few acts and a few hours, Tantrum came to our table and asked for Karen, Noriko, and I to meet with Nightshade. It was time for Matron X’s surprise. We gathered on stage to more than a few catcalls and wolf whistles. But the performance was very well received. I moved heavy things and hit a tambourine very well. As a group however, we sounded way better than a few nights of practice should have. The others are very talented. Matron X smiled broadly as we started the song and managed to only tear up slightly when Nightshade finished with, “I love you, Mom.” That also drove the place nuts with applause.

                                        Now to figure out what or if I need to work out with Mom for Christmas. Talking to her should not be this stressful. I just feel like I should already know and because I don’t, I’m ...less attentive? I don’t know. I should probably just sit down and talk with her on the way to the airport like a grown up. We’ll see how well I pull that off.


                                        Alice’s Reflections

                                        It's the night before Thanksgiving, and I've brought Jacob to meet my werewolf friends at Sleeping Giant. He's a big hit. Jacob's always had a way with people. He's outgoing and charismatic. I kind of envy that about him. Even though he's just met Ildefonso, Deirdre, Barrett and Tanya, he's already joking around and telling stories like they're old friends. Tanya's flirting shyly with him, and Deirdre's encouraging them. I think Tanya and Jacob would get on well together.

                                        Meanwhile, Eris and I are sitting together on the couch, watching the spectacle. I don't mind. I've always been more reserved than my brother in large gatherings, and besides, it's nice to snuggle up with my girlfriend near the fire place in the pack's cozy lodge.

                                        Don't get too cozy. You sense it, don't you? The barely perceptible scent of your brother's fear?

                                        The Huntress has been blessedly quiet lately, and I grimace inwardly as her voice echoes in the back of my mind. Then I realize that she's not trying to start an argument or give me a hard time. She's merely curious. She's noticed something I should have picked up on sooner. Jacob is nervous. Not much, and he's hiding it well, but something is making him uneasy.

                                        As if to punctuate that thought, Jacob asks Ildefonso and the others, "So, are you guys ex-military?" They look at one another and shake their heads.

                                        "No," answers Ildefonso. "I mean, we're park rangers, of course, so we have law enforcement training, and we're experienced hunters. But we've never been military."

                                        Jacob doesn't probe any further, but now that the Huntress has drawn my attention to it, I can tell what he's thinking. He senses something predatory and dangerous about my werewolf friends. And I think he's realized, on a subconscious level, that he's more prey here than predator.

                                        * * * * *

                                        When we arrive back at the mansion it's late. I tell Eris that I'd like some time with my brother, and she scurries off to bed ahead of me. Jacob and I head to the kitchen and I grab us a couple of Coronas from the fridge.

                                        "I'm glad you came, Jacob. It's really brightened my spirits."

                                        "Happy to oblige," he replies as we raise our beers in a lazy toast. "I'll say this. You run with a strange crowd these days."

                                        "Stranger than my friends in Portland?"

                                        "Are you kidding? Those guys were positively vanilla compared to the people here. It's good, though. They seem good."

                                        I can sense the tension hanging on the end of his words, like there's something else he wants to say but doesn't quite know if he should say it. I know what it is, so I give him a little push.

                                        "What did you think of my ranger friends. They're a fun bunch, huh?"

                                        "Oh yeah," Jacob smiles reflexively. A moment later, the smile fades. "But are they okay? I mean, they seemed…I don't know…"

                                        "Dangerous?"

                                        "Well, I didn't want to put it that way, but yeah. I guess so." Jacob sighs and takes another swig of beer. "Look, Freckles, when you're on the road as much as I am, you get good at reading people, and you get pretty good at sensing when someone is dangerous. It's a kind of survival mechanism, I guess. Your friends back there tripped all the triggers." As he says this Jacob looks at me intently. I can tell he's worried about me, but is there more to it than that? Am I triggering his 'danger sense' too?

                                        "I won't lie to you, Jacob. The guys at Sleeping Giant are dangerous, but not to you or me. They…look out for people like us, I guess. Kind of like the police, in a way."

                                        Jacob gives me a dubious look. "I'm just worried about you, Freckles. I know you've had it rough lately, and I don't want you running with a bad crowd."

                                        "They aren't bad, Jacob. They're good people, and I'm not just saying that. I'd trust them with my life."

                                        "Just so long as they aren't going to attack me or anything."

                                        It's Thanksgiving day, and the family drama is running high in the mansion. Doc's mother is a sweetheart, but Natalie's mom is even more manipulative than her daughter. I think she means well, but she really rubs me the wrong way. I'm trying to stay out of her way as much as possible.

                                        I find them both sitting in a tree, talking. Now that they've been formally introduced, the two of them are becoming fast friends, and Noriko helps bring Eris out of her shell, which makes me happy. As I approach, they wave.

                                        "What the heck are you guys doing in that tree? It's freezing out here!"

                                        "Oh, it's not that bad," says Noriko. "And besides, aren't we planning on going out hiking Saturday? It's still going to be cold then."

                                        "Well, yeah," I respond defensively, "but that's different. We'll be doing something. Staying active will keep the chill off."

                                        "We're doing something, " Eris says brightly. "Come up and sit with us!"

                                        I only hesitate a moment. Sure, I'm shivering from the cold, and sitting in the wind-blown branches of a tree does not sound the least bit fun to me right now. But Eris really looks like she wants me to join them, and if I'm up there, I'm with her. So I climb up and snuggle up next to her for warmth. We stay there, chatting, laughing, and freezing our butts off for about thirty minutes.

                                        Then Eris says, "We should go in so Alice can thaw out." She turns to me and leans in close to nibble my ear lobe. It sends pleasant shivers down my spine. "Thank you," she whispers. "This meant a lot to me. How about we go in and warm up together in the hot tub?" As she pulls away, her smile is bright and genuine (and a little bit sultry), and it warms me up enough to stay out here another few hours if I have to. I'm not going to turn down the hot tub, though, so we drop from the tree and head indoors, arm in arm.

                                        It's amazing how little things can make you fall in love with someone all over again.

                                            Some of the feedback I get on the forums are from PMs or discussions outside the forum threads where the games are discussed. A number of them can be applied pretty broadly to my games, so I paraphrased some and put some info down here to “draw back the curtain” as it were as I thought some would be of interest to some readers. Add your own if you wish, as always.

                                            You have a lot of female characters in your games. Like, most of them. Is this on purpose?

                                            Yes, yes I do. Some of it is deliberate. Media is all about men, and many TV shows, movies, books and, honestly, role-playing campaigns have few females that are often lovers, prizes, victims or relatives. I try to make women who fit every conceivable role, but have many male characters too.

                                            At the same time, I just come up with female characters more easily, and since I cull pictures from the internet which provide inspiration for PCs, the fact that I have way more female pictures means I get inspired for female characters more often. That is not deliberate, just the way it turns out.

                                            How about LGBTQ characters? Same?

                                            Most NPCs are actually Herosexual. With a few exceptions such as Tavia Fishborn or Karen Smith/Knight most NPCs orientation is such that they would be interested in PCs interested in them. Those with exceptions are NPCs whose concept defines their sexuality, and they are rare. Then sometimes as I see them in action NPCs seem to make good couples or drift together. I have no bias to create gay or straight couples this way. It just happens. However, see above that women outnumber men. I am proud, however, of the queer representation in my games.

                                            Of course, just because an NPC could fall for a PC of either sex does not mean they are equally easy to woo. Tatum Gillis could go either way but is easier for women, for example.

                                            No gay male relationships with PCs, but there are several lesbians…

                                            I don’t tell my players what orientation to make their PCs. If a male character wants to have a relationship with a male NPC that’s fine (it even happened in my Masquerade game) but it is what the players want.

                                            You have less Trans characters than I expected.

                                            Yea, not so impressive regarding those numbers. Raindancer, and informant from the Blood Bather story, is the only trans person in the game so far.

                                            You have a lot of, um, sex in your game. Is everyone okay with it?

                                            Yes, yes I do. I will speak plainly. Sex for me is way more interesting than violence. I’d be more interested in seeing a PC having a torrid affair than a gunfight. This does not mean my games are without action; The Golden Eagle has a setting conceit allowing for more danger and daring-do since the conceit prevents a PC revolving door. There is more action there than other games. Night Train Detective Agency has more action than Balance of Shadows or Malibu Dream House, because it is spy-noir, while The Golden Eagle is part noir, part mystery, part pulp. But yes, relationships are a huge thing.

                                            My players know this, and new players are told before they join. No player has to play romantic relationships, straight or queer.

                                            For the record, we almost always fade to black unless there is a good reason not to, and sex is only rolled if something besides having a good time is going on, such as getting a foe vulnerable, staying awake so the PC can rummage through their partners stuff, staying awake so their partner can’t rummage through theirs, impressing a countess, etc.

                                            So everyone has pictures, can I see a picture of [NPC]?

                                            Probably. If it is a picture culled from the internet and you want to see an NPC, I will show it unless I am using the picture with particular permission of the photographer or model, in which case no because they have not given me permission for that.

                                            How many of your players are women/lesbian/bisexual/giraffes/etc

                                            I am not going to give out too much information on my players. I will say every game linked in my sig has a mix of male and female and straight and LGBTQ players. Most games hover somewhere close to 50/50 male female, or one more of one or the other. The Golden Eagle is the exception, with many more women than men. . I will tell you, if you have read about a female PC having relationships with female NPCs in these games and assumed they are being played by a guy you will be wrong much more often than not.

                                            Do Players plan their PCs relationships before the game starts?

                                            Not usually. Sometimes they have an idea getting together is likely. We had some idea Doc and Natalie would have romantic tension, for example.

                                            What genre would you call your games

                                            Mystery/Supernatural Drama. Balance of Shadows has ghost story elements. Night Train Detective Agency has noir and spy elements, and The Golden Eagle has noir, pulp, and mystery. Malibu Dream House is more pure mystery-drama.

                                            Can I play in your game?

                                            Probably not. If you live in North Carolina we can discuss it, but even then probably not. My games are almost all full.
