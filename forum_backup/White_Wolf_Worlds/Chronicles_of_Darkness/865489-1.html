                        Worker Drone 23546-CE5b aka Bill (Alien, Cimex, Grassātōrēs)

                        To most the homeless man known as Bill is just another figure on the street, sifting through dumpsters and begging for food. A handful of others know that he has an unusually good sense of what's going on in the criminal underworld. Those in the know among the IE Detectives know that's because he understands criminal systems better than anyone else. Give him some names, an address and some sugary snacks for his time and Bill will find out just about anything about any criminal activity in the city.

                        See Bill is, like many homeless, from out of town. Unlike many homeless he's from a small star system in the Andromeda Galaxy.

                        His species formed into an impossibly rigid caste system of generally known as 'The Hive' where every individual was born into their station and role and everyone is provided with what they will need to live. From the moment he came into this universe Bill knew he was never meant to be anything great, he was a lowly worker drone, unworthy of even a name. He did have an unusual gift for subverting the system, twisting requisitions just enough to get access to things he wasn't supposed to, being in the right place at the wrong time to overhear the education of higher ranked drones, etc. Using his clever tricks he managed to live a life well beyond his approved means. Eventually the Hive grew wary of Bill's antics and he was forced to flee. He made a living doing odd jobs for various crime syndicates across the stars, gaining an understanding that his tricks were not unique to him, every world had criminals that sought to break the rules to better their own lives.

                        A little over a decade ago he got struck by a solar flare and crash landed on Earth. On this primitive planet Bill found a society not entirely unlike the Hive. Here the people fumbled around for meaning and direction for most of their lives but when they managed to stumble into their roles they took them with unwavering faith. Those that don't find their fulfilling roles twist and break the rules in ways even Bill hadn't imagined before. He felt horrid pains of nostalgia for his old life and did not want to see this system corrupted and twisted as he had twisted the Hive. He regularly takes on jobs exposing criminal activity for the IE Detective Agency but isn't an official employee for two reasons, one he doesn't legally exist on Earth, two he is genuinely uninterested in money, he's all too happy to be paid in food but does not seem to make the connection that money can be used to purchase food.

                            Dr. Jeremiah Jordan Jiller, Consultant Psychologist (Genius, Staunen Scholastic)
                            Jeremiah was always fascinated by the depths of a human mind. People do all sorts of things. People have ambitions. People fall in love. People fall into depression. People commit acts of altruism and good will. People commit crimes. What caused them to this behaviour, though. What made people who they are? What made criminals who they are? How did they reason, how did they think of themselves, what were they driven by, what caused one man to be a vicious murdered and the other to be a paragon of all good and christian values? All of the answers he recieved never satisfied him. He felt like there was more. He decided to, instead of listening to others, find his own answers. And so, after years of meticulous study, he became a crime psychologist. Throught his career, he talked to many various criminals, murderers, thieves, smugglers, forgers. He analyzed the deeds of many more. Over the years, he created many psychological profiles, meticulously analyzing every last bit of them, trying to extract a singular common value. It was not enough. He decided he needed much more information than this, much more than he had. He began to deal with the matters of his criminologist colleagues, to take note of every single detail of the crime scene, compare it with the psychological profile of the perpetrator, and make connections. Bizarre and unjustified, but connections nonetheless. Somewhere in the middle of this, he catalyzed,and didn't even notice until a bit later, when he realized he's took up to engineering and inventing. Some time afterwards, he was privately approached by a worker of the Illuminated Eye, with an offer. Help them as a consultant with whatever they have for him, as a little profitable sidejob to utilize his workaholism for something better, and in exchange, he gets to do anything he wants to before the stuff is turned to the police. He accepted. He'd be a fool not to.

                            Concepts and Ideas: Workaholic Mad Psychologist, Taking all he can get to find the defining element of human psyche responsible for criminal behaviour(And, as a matter of fact, any higher behaviour) . I'd imagine he'd use all Apokalypsi he can get, and maybe a bit of Epikrato to make people he talks to be more open when he needs them to.

                            Dr. Jiller on other people in the agency:
                            Ishaq: Tell me, how different were the criminals back in your day? Oh? You don't remember? That's fine, I'll guess I'll just wait...
                            Ms. Friday: A great Machine... That influences the minds of people all around the world, to get what it needs, with no moral qualms whatsoever.. Lead the way!
                            Gabrielle: You're there, always, to tear down the layers of their lies... You wouldn't mind, if I took a look at what's left when you're done?
                            Maria: You strive to deal punishment, to teach the crooks that crime does not pay off... But how often do they learn? Have you ever wandered how much you change people's lives, people's minds?
                            Wash: You're not looking for truth. You're looking for facts. There's a difference.
                            Ms. Morte: The body tells you nothing about the rest. About the mind, and soul. About the driving force. I wonder how much of you is what you saw up there and how much is actual you?
                            Anton: Are you suuuure you couldn't arrange a talk with Jack the Ripper? No? What about the Scorpio? No? Okay, fine, I'll settle down for John Wilkes Booth.
                            Bill: Everyone's role, assigned to them, from the very beginning... I wonder how that influences your mindset?

                                Norwood Edward Hart (Mortal, Atoll)
                                Coming from a distinguished, wealthy (if currently at risk) British family, Norwood has seldom wanted for much. And considering the accomplishments of his forebears, Norwood understands that with these benefits comes certain expectations. He has honed his mind towards the uncovering of secrets, and fancies himself a modern day Sherlock Holmes. Something his short, but impressive record seems to substantiate. Though he is in fairly high demand, he's refused position or employment at most agencies who've contacted him, due to their disrespectful attitude towards his disheveled, but trustworthy assistant Warner Roach. He has accepted tentative employment at the IE Detective Agency due to their tolerance, nay, acceptance of his assistant's unfortunate demeanor.

                                In truth, though Norwood is a passably good detective (mostly for his abilities to put clues together once presented with them, and talk people into trusting him with details), he wouldn't be nearly as good as he thinks he is without Warner. Though a staunch defender of Warner and others judged more upon appearance than ability, he is blinded by his own ego and romanticized worldview. He's as unaware of Warner's supernatural nature (or the supernatural in general) as he is of the fact that the Agency wouldn't even have hired him if not for his "assistant". Despite that, the two do make a good team, especially for cases where Norwood's superlative ability to come up with non-Supernatural explanations and draw attention are required.

                                Concepts and Ideas: Self-styled Sherlock Holmes. Self-appointed "White Savior" to the downtrodden. Glory-hound with a good heart. Blinded rationalist.

                                Norwood on others:
                                Ishaq: Fine chap. Knows how to respect his subordinates, and has a grandiosely embellished tale to tell or two. I can see us working well together.
                                Friday: Charming woman. Always knows the right thing to say, and keeps the rest of the office running smoothly. But she treats Warner with respect, which is her true virtue.
                                Gabrielle: An energetic girl, if a tad verbose. She's still one moment, then digging into her target like a terrier. She could be a great detective herself, if she'd listen to some of my advice.
                                Maria: Something about her seems a bit... off. But I respect the work she does to protect children and others who can't protect themselves. I suppose seeing what she has would give her an odd edge.
                                Wash: A man with a fine dedication to his craft (if not his appearance). Committed to the truth, despite how much the listeners might not want to hear it. At least, professionally. He has a dry sense of humour and implies the wildest things about Warner with a straight face.
                                Ms Morte: A very... direct woman. Certainly gifted in her field, the way she can tell cause of death seemingly just by looking at a body. I get the feeling she doesn't think too highly of me, though. But then, not everyone thinks my reputation is earned - I'll just have to prove it to her.
                                Anton: Currently upstanding member of the community. Runs a fine casino. I hear he used to be in the criminal underworld, but nothing was ever proven. Still, he seems very concerned about these Cold Cases he keeps bringing up. I wonder what his sources are?
                                Bill: A very odd fellow with his ear to the ground. He delivers far more than he asks for, which is rare for people in his position. I do wish he'd accept at least a little charity from time to time, but he truly seems to not care about wealth. Astonishing.
                                Dr. Jiller: A fellow scholar and exemplar of the mind, though in a different, denser field. We've had a few conversations about the criminal mind over drinks from time to time, but the liquor seems to hit him harder - it isn't long before he veers off into nonsensibility.

                                Warner Roach (Leviathan, Lahamin, School of the Reef)
                                Warner Roach grew up in fairly depressing circumstances - broken home, poverty, alienated by others. He never let that keep him down though, and always dreamed of doing better and making something of himself. He just... didn't think that something would be some primordial horror. While stumbling around in turmoil, trying to adapt to his new life, he had a chance encounter with Norwood Hart and helped him solve a high profile case. Feeling truly at peace for the first time, and encouraged by Norwood's appreciation for his help, Warner signed on with his hero-figure and used his "abilities" to help him solve many more mysteries. He doesn't mind not getting as much credit - he finally has a place he feels he belongs and can do some good.

                                Chance encounters brought Warner into contact with other members of his "family", who educated him on certain facts of life for the Wicked Tribe. He's taken up the School of the Reef, but has decided to hone his investigatory abilities in the service of Norwood, rather than physical augmentation. He knows what effect the Wake might have when he is separated from Norwood's suppressing influence, though considering Warner is around helping his hero at all hours, this doesn't really change things for Warner much.

                                Concepts and Ideas: Humble helper secretly pulling the weight. Kato to Norwood's Green Hornet. Super-effective man-servant and sidekick.

                                Warner on others:
                                Ishaq: Nice guy, especially for someone dead most of the time. He knows what it's like to both have power and still serve.
                                Friday: Nice lady. Talks to me, not at me. Makes sure the checks come on time, too, which helps.
                                Gabrielle: I... don't really know how to talk to her. She does enough for both of us (or anyone), I guess. I just try to smile and nod. I think it's nice, though, that she doesn't say anything about the... you know... smell.
                                Maria: Some people are big on the inside - Maria's both. I like her, but I get the feeling she doesn't think much of Mr. Hart. Guess she doesn't see what I see, then.
                                Wash: I like the stories he tells. Not all the endings, maybe, but he tells it plain. Still, when he asks me if I want to know the Truth about me, I say "no". I already know some truths I'm not comfortable with.
                                Ms Morte: She seems... nice? Very direct, but kinda bored. I mostly just try to stay out of her way.
                                Anton: I heard he came from the gutter too. I hope I become as successful too some day. Just without all the death. And crime, I think. Except solving it I mean.
                                Bill: Uh, wow. Other planets? But everyone is locked into their role? That doesn't seem right. Anyways, here's a spare candy bar.
                                Dr. Jiller: He asks an awful lot of questions. Said he even wants to peer into my head. I'm not sure that's such a good idea...

                                            Alejandro Tejera (Gangrel, Les Gens Libres, Former Carthian)
                                            Look. You're more than welcome to follow the trail of clues wherever it leads you. Just don't get in my way, and I might just decide to hand whatever I find to you.

                                            Rather atypical for Kindred of his Bloodline, Aldo is a former police officer originally from the Philippines, He was Embraced in 1974 by his sire after she found him near death from prolonged torture. He had been left to die by several of his more corrupt colleagues to keep him from exposing the truth behind several cases of forced disappearances.

                                            Aldo tried to make the most of his new condition, using the abilities he came to develop in order to moonlight as a vigilante. He soon hunted down the men responsible for his death, and then tried to do what he could to exact justice for other crimes. The subsequent regime changes, though, did little to lower the corruption in his eyes, and he was soon forced to leave the country altogether after an altercation with the local Invictus forced him to be hunted by even his fellow Carthians.

                                            The Freedman eventually came to the notice of the Association of the Illuminated Eye during his years as a nomadic vampire. He had been picking off members of a murder cult with ties to supernatural patrons, and had been in the middle of another attack when members of the Agency crossed paths with him. While the initial confrontation led to serious misunderstandings, things eventually smoothened over once all realized they had shared interests. At the investigation's conclusion, Aldo had declined an offer to join the organization, but maintains regular correspondence with several of its staff.

                                            Aldo is a five-foot-ten man of Filipino ethnicity, with tan skin that had lightened slightly in the years after his Embrace. He is easygoing and witty around those who know him, but there is a predatory note to his movements that always hints at his nature as a Savage. And to those who exploit others by depriving them of their freedoms and rights, he does not hesitate to unleash the hunger of his Beast with full abandon. These days, however, he's making the effort to keep a low profile with the other vampires in the area. Some have associates in his home country, and he knows they are aware of his reputation well enough to keep eyes on him.

                                            Concepts and Ideas: Police officer turned Vigilante, Wears his heart on his sleeve, Strongly respects the rights of others, Hard to catch (has five dots in Celerity), Also has at least three dots in Prothean
