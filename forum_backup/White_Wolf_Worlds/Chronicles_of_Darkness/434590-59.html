                    Here is a splat for ritual blessings dots depend on the mutation.

                    Wakening of the mind (OO-OOOOOO) (See mutations)
                    This useful ritual blesses the target with grant psychic powers. Many leviathans find this useful for granting their worshipers powers they would not otherwise have and wish to use more human actors instead of relying on Lahmasu or other hybrids. The ritual itself is very popular with Leviathans who wish to create their own psychic network working in tandem with the Besieging the Tower of will channel and other similar channels. Often though this ritual is used to waken the mind of individuals rather than large groups considering it requires more Ardour for large groups.

                    Mutations Taken from Appendix Lahmasu and Hybrids
                    Awareness Mutations: Aura Reading (OOO), Clairvoyance (OOOO), Omen Sensitivity (OOOO), Psychometry (OOOO)
                    Elements Mutations: Psychokinesis (OOOO-OOOOOO) (Cannot Apply Electricity or Fire) (Can apply Fire if Burning Lamps channel is fire in nature or has a Fire element Channel from Elements)
                    Sanctity Mutations: Medium (OOOO), Telepathy (OOOO-OOOOOO)
                    Vitality Mutations: Biokinesis (OO-OOOOOO)

                    Though what I am confused on is does a ritual that grants a blessing have to be one dot more than the merit itself? otherwise these rituals are a dot less.

                                Hello everyone, I've been looking over the Leviathan documents, and there are some things that seem to be missing. In particular, there seem to be a lot of references to the Rift, but no explanation of exactly what it is, how to enter or navigate it, or what might be found in it. Also, the book says that if a Leviathan falls to Ophion status, then unless its Cult is strong enough to anchor it to this world it will flee into the Rift. But if it does that, then unless it has the Channels to communicate at range it can no longer affect the real world, so how are Ophions at the bottom of the Rift supposed to be a threat? Or am I misunderstanding the purpose they serve?

                                Also, I had a Merit idea I wanted to share:

                                Focused Tranquility (O)

                                Whether through training and practice or a quirk of your particular psychology, your restraint of one of your two natures is unusually secure. Choose your divine or your bestial nature and gain a bonus die on all Erosion rolls for that nature.

                                Drawback: The twin natures of the Tribe are Scylla and Charybdis, to stay clear of one is to draw near to the other. You lose one die on all Erosion rolls related to the nature you did not choose.
