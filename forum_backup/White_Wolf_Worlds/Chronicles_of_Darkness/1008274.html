    Exalted Darkness

    11-25-2016, 05:34 PM
    while I do know that there are already some mapping between the different Exalted types and the gamelines of the Word/Chronicles of Darkness, for some time I've wondered about perhaps making a more direct "translations" of the said splats in the setting. You know, something that is directly inspired from those splats and incarnate them into the CofD. There would be some deviations from the source material, of course (especially in terms of power level and setting specific things)- but anyway, here is the first one! hope you'll like it!

    Saints

    You have been called by the divine. The Powers that Be found you worthy to carry their power, and blessed you with holy light. Driven by heavenly inspiration, you harness the powers of the gods to bring justice and enlightenment to a world long engulfed by darkness. Your hands would be the ones to purify the taint of the world- but you must be careful. The light of heaven blinds those who watch it for too long, and its madness consumes even the mightiest souls.

    Supernal Talent- the blessings of heaven manifest themselves through the mundane. By invoking the divine light, you may drive yourself to new heights and transcend human limitations. Each Supernal Talent is a 1-5 Arcana, based around a certain Skill instead of an aspect of reality. Each time you wish to preform an action which resonate with your Talent, you may roll Skill+Talent to preform it. From shooting a person with nothing but pebbles and bare arms, through riding the wind as if it was a horse and up to breaking into minds using a computer, heaven has blessed you with the mandate to bend reality through your skill and understanding. While Sleepers do not effect your divine abilities, and they do not forget the miracles they see, in case you should have got a Paradox you instead suffer from a Divine Madness Condition. In order to power your Talents you must invest one Willpower point per required Talent.

    Evocations- saints sanctify the world around them, finding the divine in the everyday world and invoking their own divinity through them. Relics are left where they walk, and those with holy sight may call upon those mystical powers to their aid. Each saint has a the ability to create Relics which their total rating is no higher then their Willpower score. Creating a Relic requires 8 hours of meditation with the desired object. Each such Relic has a rating no higher then 5 dots, and it holds a single Influence of the same rating. In order to Evoke a Relic, the saint must hold it and spend a Willpower point, and then roll for appropriate Talent+Attribute. A saint may allow other peoples to use her Relics by blessing them, which lasts for 24 hours. A saint by herself may use any Relic made by any saint. She may also deactivate any Relic at any given time, destroying the power inside of it. Recreating it would require the regular process.

    Virtuous- the people of heaven know no sin, as their lower side is devoured by empyrean light. Saints have two Virtues and no Vice. They may take the Virtuous merit to get three Virtues. However, refusing to fulfill one's Virtue forces a Divine Madness Condition.

    Shining Aura- the touch of the Divine leaves its mark upon you, shinning through the darkness like a daybreak. Any being with a major template would recognize the saint for what she is, even if she can't explain her nature. Beings with Integrity or equivalent of 5 and below would fill an instinctive repulse toward her, granting the saint -2 modifier for all Social Rolls (beings with 8 and above give the character a +2 modifier, however). Likewise, unless a certain power actively tries to hide a creature's supernatural nature, the saint's Aura would instinctively reveal it to her. In case there is such a power, call to a Clash of Wills using the saint's Willpower+highest rating Talent. That effect does no reveal the creature's exact nature, only that it is supernatural. The saint may also flare his Aura, causing any supernatural creature or a mortal with Integrity of 4 and bellow to suffer the Shaken Condition.

    Divine Madness- you can't get near the sun without getting burned, and the powers of heaven drive all but the strongest souls to madness. Choose a five negative Conditions which effects the character's mind. Every time the character suffers from a Breaking Point, refuse her Virtues or mess with her Talents, she gains one of those Conditions to the Storyteller's decision. The said Condition may not be resolved until the saint repents, which requires a certain mission granted by Heaven which resonates with her Virtues. Succeeding in the test grants a single Holy Beat. Failing such a test would immediately grant another Divine Madness Condition. Each such Condition has to be resolved independently, although the Storyteller may choose a certain test which includes penance to all different "transgressions". In case the saint gets 5 Divine Madness Conditions before finishing the tasks, she goes through a Breakdown, in which she fulfills all of her Virtues in a way which is disastrous to her life and surroundings. That Breakdown lasts for a Scene, and ends up with a single Holy Experience, although she would lose a single merit as the consequences of the Breakdown (the Sanctity of Merit stills applies). Afterwards, all the Divine Madness Conditions are resolved. The saint may also take a Divine Madness Condition at any moment in order to get a free Willpower point.

      Chimera

      You were driven in the edge. you fell to the bottom. You were beaten, starving, and tortured. Perhaps you were lost in the wilderness, without anyone to save you. Perhaps you were trapped in a basement of a human monster. Whatever the case, at your lowest moment, you had a choice- evolve or die. Something revealed itself to you- an animal, a great beast driven by primal instinct. It was either to be eat or be eaten. You fought and devoured the beast's flesh, and by doing so you ate a part of your own soul. You survived, you were changed, and you would never be broken again.

      Heart Library- all chimeras were touched by their primal self as they devoured their spirit animal, and that even has turned their flesh into a mercurial entity. The Heart Library is a 1-5 merit, and all chimeras start with 1 dot in it. The Library means to number of forms the character may take- all begin with at least one, that of their own spirit animal. The character may gain new forms by tracking down and devouring a new target's heart. She may not have more forms in her Library then 5 times her merit's rating. She may replace new forms with old ones, but forms lost that way may not be returned. The transformation is not to a general form of the being, but to the specific creature which was hunted. Humans are not an exception for that power.

      Mercurial Flesh- the chimeras are beings of change, and are the masters of their own body. Any ability which is meant to transform the flesh, mind or soul of the chimera (other hen her own abilities) lasts for no more then one minute per dot of Heart Library. The chimera, if desired, may wish to continue that transformation in a price of 1 Willpower per time unit, or fasten its degradation by expanding a Willpower point.

      Lick of Blood- while long term access to forms requires the hearts of others, sometimes only a quick shapeshifting is required. By liking the blood of the target, the chimera may transform to her freely by expanding a Willpower dot. She may access that form for one hour per Willpower dot since she consumed the blood. While returning to her old form (or changing to any of her Heart Library forms) could be done for free, returning to the temporary form requires expanding another Willpower point each time she does so in that time frame.

      Reach Beyond the Veil- nothing can bind the chimera, not even the boundaries of space and time. By expanding a Willpower point, the chimera may see and reach into any Twilight frequency she wish for. She enjoys an Honorary Rank equal to her Library, and may attack beings in Twilight form normally.

      Tell- the inner beast inside the chimera has marked her with her sign, both as a curse and as a power. Choose a single Wolf Blooded Tell at character creation, which influence the chimera in any of her forms.

      Deadly Beastman Transformation- the very height of the chimera's shapeshifting, she may mix her human flesh with the terrible form of her primal spirit. By expanding a Willpower point, the character may transform into an hybrid form, which contains different aspects made from her Heart Library forms. During that form, she regenerate wounds as if she was a werewolf, and gain 2 dots per Heart Library to spread among her Attributes (she is not vulnerable to silver, however). Any being who sees her must roll for Resolve+Composure or suffer from the Shaken Condition. To design that form, choose one Aspect per Heart Library rating from War Against The Pure's Shapeshifter template. Those Aspects may be changed later through a "sacred hunt" of the animal which symbolize the desired Aspect.

          Astrologer

          You were birthed beneath strange stars. The patterns of heaven entangled your fate, creating a path written in the stars. The song of the spheres filled your hears since you remember yourself, and the whispers of the darkness beyond the stars has reveled you secrets known to no other- in the price of your sanity. When you look at the world, you see the fate which was chosen for them by the distant, uncaring lights- yours to shape, and to be shaped by.

          Astrology: your eyes can read the omens of Fate, and by listening to its eternal murmur enlightenment may be achieved. At any moment, after making a detailed astrological map relevant to your Investigation, you may go through an Integrity Breaking Point to gain one free Clue. You don't need to role for the map- its credibility doesn't influence your understanding of Fate's whispers, and anyone with understanding of the subject would understand nothing of it, as it notes stars which do not exist or astronomical events which never happened.. yet, at least.

          Broken Mask: the stars of your birth were never meant to be, and as such you were never meant to be. While you do exist, that intuitive truth is understood by anyone who comes in contact with you. If you meet with someone who lacks a long term relationship with you, they must roll for Resolve+Composure at the end of the contact just in order to remember you. Anyone with whom you keep a steady contact (like Allies or Contacts), yet haven't saw you for a number of days equal to your Integrity must roll the same, with Failure erasing any memory from the last "time unit" and Dramatic Failure erase all memories of your existence.

          Weave Destiny: while your face are easily forgotten, there are some benefits for that constant curse- that is, by stealing another's fate and life. By expanding a Willpower and rolling for Manipulation+Resolve, you may create a fake personality that anyone who meets you believes to be true. Only other Astrologers or beings who are "outside of Fate" (like ghosts, abyssal beings, messengers of Fate, high level spirits, archmages, etc) would recognize the fraud. The fake destiny lasts for a number of hours equal to your Integrity.

          Doomspeaker: The beings from beyond the stars know the names of all beings, and by speaking those names dread curses fall on poor souls. As a child of stars, you inherit that power to yourself- by going through an Integrity Breaking Point, you may force upon someone else a Doom, as detailed in the Destiny merit. Supernatural beings may resist that power using their Supernatural Tolerance against the astrologer's Integrity. However, just as you may curse others, you by yourself is cursed, as your very birth is a sin against the natural order of the world. You suffer from the constant effect of a Doom.

          Prayer Strips: the words of heaven are written in the stars, and your wishes may be granted by writing them in the same language. In order to activate a prayer strip, the character has to make them from long pieces of cloths and decorate them with the movements of celestial bodies meant to represent her wishes. Once per Chapter, the character may go through an Integrity Breaking Point in order to temporary gain the benefit of any single merit to your choosing. The effect lasts to the rest of the chapter.

          Star Crossing: Fate's hold crosses the worlds and it touch burns the flesh. Once per Chapter, you may go through an Integrity Breaking Point in order to either move to another world or change your state into Twilight. Returning to regular state or world could be done at any given time without any consequences. In an addition, you enjoy an Honorary Rank against all Ephemeral Beings equal to half of your Integrity.

          Sidereal Arts: you are so fine tuned to the whispers of Fate, that your body dance to the sounds of its celestial music by instinct. Once per a Scene, you may change the target number for a Success by half of your Integrity for a Skill roll. In an addition, you may buy Numina and tie them to a certain Skill. By rolling that Skill+Integrity, you may activate those Numina without needing to pay any price. However, for each 3 Numina you buy your maximal Integrity drops by 1. You may replace a certain Numen with a new one by going through an Integrity Breaking Point.

                                      Hmmmm... say, how about this?

                                      Saints
                                      • Divine Potency: This advantage is ranked from 1 to 5, and functions as Supernatural Tolerance against powers that call for it. Also, add Divine Potency to the Saint's total Willpower dots. Lastly, add half of Divine Potency, rounded down, to the maximum limit of Attributes and Skills that the Saint can acquire.
                                      • Supernal Skill: Choose one Skill for the Saint, defined as his Supernal Skill. He does not receive environmental or personal penalties on using his Supernal Skill. Active attempts to penalize Supernal Skill rolls provoke a Clash of Wills, with the Saint rolling Resolve + Composure. The opponent also rolls Resolve + Composure if he is not using a supernatural power. Once per scene, the Saint may gain an automatic success on a Supernal Skill roll, scoring a number of success equal to his dots in the Supernal Skill if any is required. Once per chapter, the Saint may ignore all equipment and other such restrictions needed to use his Talent. Lastly, if the Saint rolls an exceptional success using his Supernal Skill without relying on this ability's superhuman boons, he regains a point of Willpower.
                                      • Artifact Crafting: The Saint may create an Artifact. Creating the Artifact is an extended action, rolling an appropriate Attribute + Skill and requiring five successes per every Merit dot the Artifact would have, and the Saint to spend a dot of Willpower as the final cost. This does not benefit from Supernal Skill's normal boons, but the Saint need not pay the Willpower dot if the Artifact creation involved his Supernal Skill. Each Relic contains an Influence over a Skill Specialty that fits in the Skill used to create it, with a rating equal to its Merit dots. Spending the Influence rolls the same dice pool as its creation, and costs Willpower instead of Essence. The Saint may choose not to pay the Artifact's Experience cost after creating it, which leaves it free to be used and destroyed by anyone. If he pays Experience, however, it becomes 'dedicated' to him and can only be used by the Saint himself, or those who have received his express permission. If he loses the dedicated Artifact, it miraculously finds its way back to him through a series of consequences. If the dedicated Artifact is destroyed by unwanted forces, the Saint may recreate it by spending a dot of Willpower and using similar materials for a similar length of time, but without actually rolling dice again. The Saint himself may manually destroy dedicated Artifacts himself anytime he wishes. Lastly the Saint may dedicate a number of Artifacts equal to his Divine Potency.
                                      • Grand Ego: A Saint may regain one point of Willpower from casual, albeit conscious and effort-consuming, acts of Virtue. Conversely, a Saint may regain all of Willpower points by indulging his Vice in a manner that sacrifices other people for his own gain.
                                      • Awesom Aura: Supernatural creatures without the Integrity advantage are automatically aware of the Saint's superhuman nature, despite not knowing clearly just what it is that makes him so special, and will act accordingly. Also, the Saint may actively flare his aura by spending a point of Willpower and rolling Divine Potency + Supernal Skill. The Saint must be doing something at least tangentially related to the Supernal Skill when doing so. Those who subject to the aura lose one point of Willpower and are struck with the Shaken Condition for the scene. The Awesome Aura also works as a counter against other 'aura' powers used by some supernatural creatures. This usage is a reflexive action and need not involve the Supernal Skill, but still costs a point of Willpower.
                                      • Divine Madness: The Saint has a Virtuous Aspiration, defined as "Act in accordance with (a Virtue)." This Virtue need not be the Saint's own. However, if the Saint passes on an opportunity to follow his Virtuous Aspiration, he suffers a breaking point, with a penalty as if he willingly inflicted grave violence on someone. If the Saint is actively restained from fulfilling his Virtuous Aspiration, he gains the Madness Condition for a number of days equal to his Divine Potency.
                                      No thoughts yet about others.

                                              so, some proto-ideals for future translations-

                                              Dragons- descendants to primordial gods of the world who carry the powers of the elements themselves in their blood.

                                              Revenants- dead souls who swear fealty to the Kereboi in exchange for life and enforce their Laws both in and outside the Underworld.

                                              Androids- souls uploaded into mechanical bodies using strange science in order to support and protect their society.

                                              Titans- cultists who made a pact with bound infernal beings from before the gods who channel their image through their flesh.

                                              Homunculi- pieces of flesh and memory weaved from dead souls and bound in the service of their maker.
