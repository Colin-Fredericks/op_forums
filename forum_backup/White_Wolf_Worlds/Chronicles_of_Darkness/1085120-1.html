                    • Meiji Restoration ( circa 1868 CE ) for Werewolf
                    • Spanish Inquisition ( 1478 to 1502 CE ) for Hunter

                    • Bronze Age Collapse (1200 - 1100 BCE) for Werewolf - to pick up from teasers in Forsaken by Rome) 😃

                                                    As someone with a deep background in history, all I can say is that there are so many eras simply rife with possibilities. 1920s/30s Shanghai with its international vibe playing against nascent nationalism ... 1730s-80s Europe with the Age of Enlightenment crashing into secret societies and the Cult of Sensibility ... Norman Sicily with its wash of cultures ... basically any era where you suddenly get a melding or clashing of wildly different cultures ... simply add one or mire aspects of Chronicles of Darkness and stir liberally. And, yes, I think some of the "newer" lines (Beast, Demon, Mummy) need to be emphasized.

                                                    I will admit that I am always a bit hesitant abiut Arthurian settings, simply because it is more of a concept than a true time period. Depending on one's vision of King Arthur, this could be anything from 4th century Britain as a whole, subsections such as the West Country, Wales, or even southern Scotland, or even Brittany (Less Britain) as late as the 10th century, possibly with overlays of the 15th. OTOH, there was a strong tradition of interaction between the tales as written (beginning in the 12th century) and courtly culture. Characters of the tales in contemporary clothing and armour ... then courtiers ape the actions of the books, inclufing costume revels and tournaments... leading to a new round of tales in the now-contemporary costumes and armour ... lather, rinse, repeat. Hmm ... maybe this is involvement by the Fae?

                                                            Originally posted by MhicWombat View Post
                                                            I will admit that I am always a bit hesitant abiut Arthurian settings, simply because it is more of a concept than a true time period.
                                                            Arthurian 'Camelot' in Mage is canoniclly name for Britain's local Time Before places and items, just like in Mediterranean Sea area ( Greece, Rome and Egypt ) they where called 'Atlantis'. Merlin is canonical Silver Ladder Acanthus that used myths and those connection to Ascended and show middle finger to Exarchs. But 'Camelot' is only as much viable as 'Atlantis' is - it can be local variant of Awakened Society, or they both could be pieces of the same UrCivilization, from the dawn of times. DaveB even wrote he wanted to write some alternative setting with Camelot being real place character could be possible to enter - but changed mind as it would not work in CoD universe as it is.

                                                            Whole topic on Camelot in Mage. And most important quotes from DaveB on matter:

                                                            Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post
                                                            Merlin created a Utopia using long-term manipulations of Fate and Destiny as his Omens - his Noumenon was to establish a destined link between a king and the land, and he Ascended (his body turning into the crystal cave) when the Bane of all those destinies came true.

                                                            At which point, the whole damn thing was promptly erased from the timeline by the Ruin. But no matter what the Exarchs do, Arthurian symbols resonate with the Acanthus, and the Aeon of Fate transformed from the Greek Triple-Fates into Mordred.
                                                            Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post
                                                            In fact, and I feel okay with telling you this, but that setting was the one of the two I came up with for Dark Eras that we didn't end up writing.

                                                            Dark Ages Britain, replacing "Atlantis" with "Arthurian Britain" as the origin for the inexplicable mysteries and stripping all the Romantic era additions from the myths. It'd be Arthurian as in "grinning blood-soaked warlord" Arthur, stone circles built by giants, probably-not-allegorical dragons, remnants of the old religion, pugnacious Christian saints and all.

                                                            Just like Atlantis never happened in the modern-day setting but mages encounter evidence of it, the setting would be the real Dark Ages - and unlike other games we've made in the past would actually be set in the Dark Ages, not several centuries later - but with the ahistorical magical remnants of Arthurian Britain poking up from the seams.

                                                            Naturally, a few days after I pitched it I found a FATE game called Age of Arthur that hits the same "anti-Pendragon" spot I was aiming for. C'est La Vie. The setting that won out for Dark Eras is more "a very interesting period from Awakening's history that highlights its themes" than "Awakening's themes transplanted to an alien time, largely disconnected from the gameline", so I believe we've made the right decision.

                                                            Camelot will just go on being an occassionally-mentioned cool bit in Awakening's setting lore.

                                                            It's cropped up recently in our discussions for Fallen World Chronicle, actually. We were talking about how we want the Acanthus to shift in presentation from "effortless and unconcerned" to "schemers who see possible futures and then manipulate events to go down their preferred ones" - passive characters to proactive ones. The first two exemples of characters we're thinking of for the nuAcanthus? Paul Mau'dib from Dune... And Merlin.
