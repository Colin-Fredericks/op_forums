    Questions about Pacts

    12-06-2013, 11:48 AM
    So after reading the rules on pacts there are a few things I'm unclear on that I'd love to get some answers to.

    First, what exactly is the downside of a pact to a demon other than the willpower cost? The rules talk about how demons make sure to make pacts unfair towards the person but I don't understand why? Unless I'm misreading it the stuff a person gets out of a deal isn't provided by the demon at all so there's no incentive to cheat. Like if I make pact with a person to get their spouse I can offer the person 1 dot of resources or 3 dots of resources, 4 dots of striking looks, and anything else I can think of and its actually better for me to make the one that helps them as it costs me less willpower and the universe gives them these things not me. Why bother cheating people when unbalancing it in their favor costs you nothing, makes the pact harder for you to make(greater willpower cost) makes it easier to get a signature and makes them positively inclined towards you? It seems like a really smart idea to just make incredibly unbalanced pacts in the humans favor towards some friends then reap the rewards of powerful allies, make the pacts only last a year and you're pretty much guaranteed they will treat you well to keep the pact renewed.

    Second how much extra stuff can be added in? The big thing I saw nothing about was a time limit before a soul pact can be activated. A lot of demon fiction(Supernatural is what sticks out to me) has the deal with a demon be that you get stuff but that in a year or a decade or so the demon shows up to collect. It seems like a way better way to get someone to sign a soul pact, if they know for sure they get at least certain amount of time to enjoy the benefits. Is it possible to write something like that in, or other conditions like after cashing in a soul pact the demon has to take care of the persons children, or after taking the persons pet as a piece of cover the demon has to treat it well?

                                              GMC's Supernatural Merits? Maybe. I think that, generally, a Demon (i.e. Unchained) needs to have a supernatural ability in order to grant it to another (see also: Gadget creation). I could also see an Exploit or Interlock based on the metaphysics behind Inflict Stigmata or Hellhound that could grant supernatural merits. Hunter Endowments? Almost certainly not, but see next paragraph. Anything else? No. There are demons who bear no relation to the Unchained, after all[1].

                                              I wouldn't allow a Demon to grant Endowments immediately, but—by granting Social Merits—they'd help with the social engineering to get access to them. I don't know Hunter, but the impression I've gotten is that most Endowments represent organizational resources, which means that a Demon could speed up becoming a Hunter in that Conspiracy. Once you're in the Conspiracy and have at least one dot of the Endowment, I might allow a Demon to improve it using Pacts. I need to learn more about Endowments.

                                              [1] For Mages making deals with demons, see Summoners, Inferno, or other sources whose names escape me.
