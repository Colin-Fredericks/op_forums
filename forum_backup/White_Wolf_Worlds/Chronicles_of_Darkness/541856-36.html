                                    Just to once again show how much damage even a Begotten fresh out of Character creation can cause:

                                    Rockie
                                    Family: Namtaru
                                    Hunger: Ruin
                                    Concept: Vandalizing Anarchistic Punk
                                    Lair Traits: Tornado, Unstable
                                    Atavisms: Storm-Lashed, Plague Bearer
                                    Nightmares: Your Rage Consumes You, Fear is Contagious
                                    Lesson: Society is just a flimsy cover - you are no better than your neolithic ancestors
                                    Life: Rebellious
                                    Legend: Vicious

                                    Rockie is a prick, and becoming a Begotten didn't make it any better. High on a power trip, disregarding of any semblance of order, he just does whatever he feels like.
                                    He loves showing people how they are no more than (arguably) smart animals by causing riots or making them worse, drinking up the fear of people realizing not the cops nor the law keeps them safe.
                                    However, his latest idea is more of a threat than any before: He plans on gorging himself, then expending all that fear into violent Tornadoes he sends into various directions, followed by massive plagues. The meals from that he plans to invest into violent riots to kick-off a "us vs. them" mentality.
                                    In essence, his goal is to tear down a city - all that fear should do the trick of Incarnating, right?
                                    Imagine his surprise when he will be beset from all sides by Siblings, Cousins, Hunters and Heroes for his activities.



                                    Fun thing: with this setup, he has a reasonable chance of causing immense damage. Expending Satiety on creating a Tornado with Storm-Lashed, using that to also invoke Unstable to better crumble down buildings, then follow up with riots and plagues is a nightmare come true for all residents of the area, mortals and supernaturals alike.
                                    If he had 3 Broodmates (not too unusual a size for a player brood) with Titanic Blow, Heart of the Ocean and something like Dragonfire, and maybe Lair traits like Exposed, Burning, Decayed, Slick, Thin Air, and Thunderous, they could really tear down whole blocks of areas like New York - with no warning at all.

                                                    Garret Blyushtein
                                                    Legend: Relentless. If Garret wants something, he is going to come for it, and nothing's gonna stop him.
                                                    Life: Improvisational. Garret doesn't plan so much as act and react. He feels most at ease when he can just go with the flow, rather than follow a plan.
                                                    Family: Eshmaki
                                                    Hunger: Hoard
                                                    Horror: Scaly fox made from Shadows
                                                    Lair: Overstuffed Attic
                                                    Lair Traits: Hoard, Fog, Maze, Murmurs, Cramped
                                                    Atavisms: Death of Light, Illusion of Safety, Shadowed Soul, From the Shadows, Ravenous Maw
                                                    Nightmares: Tabula Rasa, You Cannot Wake up, You cannot Rest, You Cannot move, You are not alone,
                                                    Merits: Walk Lightly, Sleight of Hand, Fast Reflexes, Fleet of Food 3, Ressources 4, Parkour 5, Confounding Sleight, Insuppressible Parkour, Light Footed, Lightning Reflexes, Scurry, Without Trace, Fighting Finesse (knives), Killing Instinct, Advanced Killing Instinct, Language (Russian)


                                                    Garret is a master thief - not just out of passion, but a professional one. You want something stolen from (or placed at) a place? He's the guy. Unlike his contractors, he doesn't care about the financial value, but the emotional one: an old clock that belonged to someone's great grand-father, the young girl's first place diploma in some sports competition, a winning lottery ticket, the wedding ring of their late spouse. Even their supernatural cousins are not safe from him, though: a Created's Azothic Object, another Created's self-made pottery, a Defiant's lab log, a werewolf's last memento of his time before the Change (a basketball tricot), he even went so far as to steal from a Mummy, and managed to keep it.
                                                    Having made friends with a Krewe as well as several Ghosts (getting people the heirloom back they need to pass on makes him a great help), he has a someone to make up for whatever he lacks in and a Family to rely on.
                                                    Garret is very anti-confrontational and prefers to not be caught if he cannot manage to not be seen at all. He deals with Heroes in a comparatively unique way, therefore - he simply runs away, lures them into a Chamber, and leaves through the Underworld. All he has to do is lose them there or wait for his friends to get them.
                                                    This does not mean he isn't dangerous in a fight at all, though: when it comes down to it, he relies on his nimble build and preciseness to cripple his enemy and leave them for dead.

                                                            "Why are you screaming? It's just like before isn't it? Then again...you're not in control this time around."

                                                            Name: Unknown, but a very lucky few know them by the name of Pan'sWrath.

                                                            Family: Talassii

                                                            Hunger: Punishment

                                                            Concept: Satyr flavored Hard Candy

                                                            Lair Traits: Darkness, Undergrowth

                                                            Atavisms: Caught in the Webs, Enemy Within

                                                            Nightmares: You Deserve This, They are All Around You

                                                            Life: Methodical

                                                            Legend: Vengeance


                                                            Among forums for victims of sexual assault, there's a rumor. If you mention anywhere on the site that your attacker won't be going to jail, you may find yourself getting a PM from someone with a blank username with a ram's skull for their avatar.

                                                            If you can offer a picture of yourself, details about the attack and 20 dollars, they promise to make your attacker suffer.


                                                            Perhaps they were victimized in the past, or perhaps they could do nothing but watch as a loved one was blamed for the vile act as the attacker walked away with their hands clean. While the beginning is shrouded in mystery, the Talassi known as Panswrath is a Nemesis with a desire for a certain kind of victim.

                                                            Pan targets rapists and other sexual offenders, focusing on those who get away with their crimes. Their punishments are elaborate and methodical. Using the details supplied by the victim, Pan recreates the scenery where the attack happened within their Lair and drags the offender into the Lair.

                                                            There, Pan brutally picks apart the offender's mind by talking about the attack and its aftermath, all from the victim's point of view. Through the use of their Atavisms and Nightmares, Pan forces the offender to face the victim and their crimes once more.

                                                            The screaming is oh so satisfying, but even more satisfying is the relief from the victims after the offender has been punished. Pan will admit though, that their favorite cases ended with the offender killing themselves.
