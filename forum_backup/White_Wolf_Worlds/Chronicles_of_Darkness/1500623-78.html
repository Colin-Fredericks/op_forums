            I think this is one of those areas where "something completely different" is a bit of a vague statement that needs to be taken somewhat artistically and maybe used as a justification for when one thinks it's just plain an effect that should require a higher Arcanum.

            Like, is a cup made of glass "completely different" from a cup made of plastic? One might think that's only a minor characteristic and the essential substance of the thing is being a cup, and yet the spell we're given for changing materials is Patterning on the apparent logic that the material is actually the essential thing.

            The same might go for Resonance, particularly if one interprets Resonance as a thing that doesn't really have any substance beyond the thing that it Resonates with.

            (Although I suppose that raises questions of if Resonance is a thing in and of itself with properties, or is just a property of other things. I suppose even in that interpretation, a Weaving spell might alter the current Resonance of a subject into another thing it could be plausibly resonant with, while Patterning is how to bestow Resonance that makes no innate sense to the spell target.)

                                                            Originally posted by orathaic View Post

                                                            You can scry on a subject which is able to fit through an iris, a stone perhaps which you throw.
                                                            Yeah, I think the answer to that of "the spell just ends when its subject is obliterated" not only makes sense, but seems quite elementary.

                                                            Mind, given how scrying on such a subject will only ever show you the subject, even if the stone wasn't rendered non-existent, all you'd get is continuing to scry on a stone without any context as to its surroundings.

                                                            Also, I feel that this is a point worth reminding that while Irises often align along something with the symbolism of a passageway, they're very rarely just lying open. The Key for them is both typically a wilful act, and something that coincides with the act of crossing them to their destination. You probably shouldn't really have a situation in which it's possible to get the passage to the Supernal open and then toss something through it, and I think any additional questions of trying to tease out implications to the concept are readily resolved with "every single Supernal Iris requires a Key, and the Key would coincide with the act of crossing through one".

                                                            So the only thing that you can do with a Supernal Iris is either pass through it to your doom or wait for an entity to emerge from it.
