                                            One Weird Trick to Never Get Jumped by Another Vampire Again! (Sheriffs Hate Him!)

                                            Blood Potency 2+, Natural Born Killer, and Quickdraw (Crossbow). You can't be surprised, you can always have your crossbow at the ready on the first turn of combat, and on the first turn you can stake in one crossbow hit. If your opponent has higher Initiative, you can use Celerity to interrupt.
                                            Optional: Firefight 1, Fast Reflexes up to 3. Increase your Initiative so you have a better chance of going first. If you don't need to spend Vitae to interrupt with Celerity, you can instead spend it on multiplying your speed to launch a surprise attack (somewhat ironic for anyone who thought they'd get the jump on you.)
                                            Optional: Powered Projectile 3, Straight Up Fucking Murderer. Add armor piercing 2 to your crossbow attack to insure a successful staking hit even against most armored opponents. With Murderer, you don't even have to give up your Defense to do it.
                                            Optional: Powered Projectile 4, Quicken Sight. Reduce the penalty for targeting the heart to -1. With Quicken Sight, completely offset the penalty by Aiming (note: text is unclear on what exactly "benefit from aiming instantly" means, so ask your ST before trying this).

                                                Oh. God.

                                                1. Be a Vampire with Knight Commander
                                                2, Have a thrall with Tactical Telepathy •••••
                                                3. Ghoul them, giving them a dot or two of Celerity.
                                                4. You have HOW MANY bonus dice on that roll?

                                                One of the benefits of the higher levels of Tactical Telepathy is that your telepathic "network" can spend Willpower on other people's rolls, which explicitly stacks. One of Knight Commander's benefits is that you can let your Enthralled minions spend one Willpower per turn without actually spending Willpower.

                                                There's no explicit upward limit on how many targets can be subject to either effect. The only limiting factor of Tactical Telepathy is that you take a -1 penalty to your roll for each person past the first you try to include in your network... which is nothing when you can get such cheap, high bonuses.
