                                                            Much like Wyrdhamster, I like looking at Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood and its homunculi for ideas about prometheans and their capabilities...

                                                            But--and I'm surprised no one has mentioned this--but there's a game that was developed by Capcom that serves not only as a great inspiration for the first edition of Promethean, but I believe also would serve as an excellent source of ideas for the second edition coming out (not just for characters, but also for settings and storyteller ideas).

                                                            The game is called Haunting Ground, and follows the story of a young girl called Fiona who gets stranded at a large castle occupied by a number of occupants wishing to do her harm. Why you ask? Because despite being completely human she possesses an Azoth, which pretty much all the characters in the castle wish to utilize in some fashion (save for one)--

                                                            Besides any number of deformed homunculi stranded throughout the castle, along with a few golems you have to take control of, and a list of references to alchemical procedures and famous alchemists throughout history--you have to contend with the stalkers of the game. Two characters are dead ringers for prometheans, both of which were created by alchemical means in-game--one, named Debilitas, is a superlatively strong yet mentally deficient man; and two, Daniella, a mentally unstable woman of unearthly beauty who lacks any sense of touch, taste, and smell.

                                                            Then two other characters are dead ringers for the antagonists in the second edition coming up (Alchemists)--Lorenzo, the aging alchemist who's behind all the workings at the castle; and Riccardo, Lorenzo's clone. Both characters want to use Fiona's Azoth in order to gain immortality.
