        As a member of the SCP Foundation wiki, I am eagerly anticipating the chance to one day play a Deviant Chronicle that flips the traditional perspective of said site: you are not an Agent of the Foundation or another Group Of Interest who hunts anomalies, you ARE the anomaly on the run from these ruthless organizations. Here's a list of the factions that could be adapted to CofD and Deviant. Imagine what it must be like to be taken in by Dr. Wondertainment (TM) and be turned into a trademarked mutant with gimmicks meant to entertain as if you were a living toy, but said Doctor (if they are even a human being) has a very reckless and sometimes downright alien definition of what is fun. Or imagine that you are an orphan living in the streets, but you have a potentially spectacle-worthy talent or supernatural power, and you are approached by Hermann Fuller's Circus Of The Disquieting. The more you define your life and identity around the Circus, the more you find your gift seeping into you and changing you.

        There's a mountain of excellent inspirational material there, but I wish you perseverance to overcome the archive panic you will feel when the extent of the setting becomes apparent.                                                 It's perfectly possible to have large organizations on a global scale without a traditional hierarchical chain of command. A large organization can be sustained by informal means, its leaders are chosen by reputation, and orders are given and obeyed by social influence. Just look at how the ascending ones conspiracy in hunter organizes themselves. It's the best example I can imagine, and the one that makes the most sense with the deviants. There are many other examples: the majority of compacts, and the way demons organize in ideologies. . Another great example is the organization firewall, from the game eclipse phase, take a look at it too. My point is that there are options to make it work and make sense.

                                                        Personally, I feel like that very reason supports a more informal global organisation, rather than an actual society with philosophies and the like. You can have global organisations, as Firanai proposed, but you can also have a global network that merely offers information and facilitates communication, like Geist's Twilight Network. That way, Deviants can team up for short periods of time, so they don't have to agree with each other, and you can keep the "local" play in-between team-ups fairly isolated and Promethean-style.

                                                        Sorta like the Avengers in the movies, if you think of it that way. The chronicles have some stories where it's just the local Deviant group taking care of business in their city or just travelling around, and special stories where they team up with other Deviants from around the world to strike a massive blow at a shady organisation. And then once that's dealt with, everyone goes back to dealing with their local problems. Hell, if your group is up for it (or you play via PbP), you can further refine each local story into a Deviant's solo adventure.
