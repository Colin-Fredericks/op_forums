        Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
        There is no difference between Prime and Spirit Resonance. Prime manipulates Resonance directly, but other arcana manipulate it via reverse engineering. Spirit for instance can slap a spirit into an area where it wouldn’t normally be able to comfortably exist, it can then exert its Influences to make the environment more amenable to it.
        I understand that "Resonance", as a concept, means the same thing whether it's being created by a convergence of ley lines at a Node or by the simple nature of an area, but that doesn't necessarily mean that the Arcana aren't able to distinguish between "Resonance as a result of Nodes, Hallows, ley lines and other sources of Mana" and more general sources of Resonance.

        In fact, there's a lot of evidence to suggest that the opposite is true:
        • The Resonant Manifestation Condition explicitly mentions Spirit as being able to "manipulate Resonance", but does not mention Prime. Your suggestion that Spirit only does this as a side-effect of summoning spirits is akin to saying that Life can manipulate Resonance by growing a bunch of trees in an area. While this is true, the specific mention of Spirit being able to manipulate Resonance seems to suggest a more direct connection.
        • The only Prime spells that influence Resonance are explicitly restricted to the manipulation of Nodes, Hallows and ley lines: in other words, Resonance as it relates to Mana.
        • Spirit Mage Sight explicitly reveals Resonance ("the nature of the Resonance Condition"), whereas Prime Mage Sight does not mention Resonance at all.
        • Of all of the spells that influence Resonance Keywords, the most versatile is the Spirit 5 spell Essence Fountain [p.185], which permits generating Essence out of nothing with any Resonance Keyword, including multiple Resonance Keywords. Prime spells of the same level are more limited (see, e.g. Create Truth [p. 170], which generates a Hallow with Resonance "appropriate to the location and to the caster's Path and Nimbus"). Comparing the two spells suggests that it's the Spirit mage who has greater command over Resonance.
        • On p. 242: "Prime spells manipulate ley lines by moving them, creating them, or altering their Resonance. Certain Spirit spells manipulate Resonance as well." (Again restricting Prime's manipulation of Resonance to sources of Mana, with a less specific nod towards Spirit's ability to manipulate Resonance in general.)

        So, in short... I remain confused. The rules on which Arcana, if any, can directly influence the Resonance Keywords of a person, place or thing seem to all agree that "general" Resonance is a Spirit Arcanum thing, except for the fact that there aren't any listed Spirit spells that actually do this.

        Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post
        As for Spirit Summoms it wraps several effects into one spell, including Opener of the Way, Command Spirit, and Reaching. Essentially, it’s a good spell for conserving spell control skits.
        This seems... weak to me. A whole dot more for a weaker version of Command Spirit whose only advantage is to combine other spells of arguable value, and only then by spending Reach? It doesn't even permit a spirit to cross the Gauntlet, and with the extra commands you get from Spirit 2's Command Spirit, it's hardly difficult to simply command the spirit to use its Influence to create the Open Condition on its own (after all, if the spirit is in sensory range, then it's probably already in an area with the appropriate Resonant Condition).

        On one hand, I kind of like the idea of a Spirit Summons spell that requires you to go hunting for spirits in areas that are likely to attract them... but at the table, this requires a lot of one-on-one time between spirit mage and Storyteller. And how does a spirit mage take advantage of the court they've cultivated during play, without the ability to summon them from afar? Do we assume friendly spirits remain in sensory range of the mage at all times, like Twilight groupies?

                                        Originally posted by PixelPuzzler View Post
                                        So regarding resonance and Prime/Spirit. Seeing as one of the explicit purviews of prime is resonance, is there any particular reason it should be limited on the resonance it can affect?
                                        The problem is that this is only one relevant section in the rules, and it's contradicted in various other places in the book (I've listed them all in bullet points a few posts up). One of the most telling, in my opinion, is in the write-ups for Mage Sight: Prime does not reveal Resonance, while Spirit explicitly does. There's also the fact that the Spirit 5 Essence-creation spell gives much more flexibility in choosing multiple Resonance Keywords, while the equivalent Prime 5 spell for creating Hallows specifically prohibits the mage from choosing the Resonance at all (it's determined by the mage's nimbus and path).

                                        Likewise, literally all references to, or examples of, Prime affecting Resonance are explicitly restricted to Nodes, Hallows and ley lines---there is literally no mention, nor even a hint, that Prime can affect Resonance outside of that restriction. On the other hand, there are numerous examples of Spirit being said to affect Resonance without any such limitations, sometimes in the same sentence as a more-restricted mention of Prime (e.g. "Prime spells manipulate ley lines by moving them, creating them, or altering their Resonance. Certain Spirit spells manipulate Resonance as well.") The Resonant Condition, which is really just a placeholder for "matching Resonance Keyword", lists Spirit and Mind as the only two Arcana that can affect Resonance (presumably, Mind does so for Goetia, while Spirit does so for spirits).

                                        In short, the rules are contradictory and completely unclear on this subject.

                                            Probably has to do with how the two Arcana have different perspectives on Resonance.

                                            To Prime, Resonance is Supernal energy seeping out through the cracks of Fallen reality. So to Prime, Resonance looks like rivers and geographical faults, and that's pretty much what ley lines and nodes are. So Prime gets to have "Resonance" as its explicit purview, for all things Supernal are of Prime, but actual Prime spells that manipulate Resonance do so in a context that fits in with how the Supernal World interacts with Resonance - as ley lines and nodes.

                                            To Spirit, Resonance is still Supernal bleed-over, but more importantly it's also the life-stuff of spirits and the breath of the Shadow. So to Spirit, Resonance is not an environmental feature, but the environment itself. So Spirit doesn't get "Resonance" as its explicit purview, but it still gets to affect Resonance for the same reason you need to control the fuel to control the fire. Thus Spirit spells that manipulate Resonance do so in a context that fits in with how the Shadow interacts with Resonance - as Manifestation Conditions and Essence.

                                            Or at least, that's the message that I got. Dunno if that's what the writers intended, but it made sense to me.
