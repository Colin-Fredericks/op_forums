                                        This one is vague in my head right now, but here goes. An old and powerful Beast (maybe even an Incarnate) that enters the mortal realm once every [insert mythical cycle here], when the proper ritual is performed or just when something unusual catches their attention. Their Brood consists of those monsters that even other monsters fear: Centimani, Scelesti, Bale Hounds and so on. Whether they do this out of sympathy, a sick sense of humor, too much curiosity or something else is unknown, and if asked, they tell a different story every time.

                                        They would be a major antagonist who takes in those other NPCs that the players wronged somehow or thought were defeated. Their Family is probably Namtaru (for consorting with scum and loving it) or Ugallu (for always knowing when someone has the potential to become a true monster). I'm not sure what their Hunger(s) would be, but they would probably favor High Satiety through Hunger Management 3, lots of Family Dinners and Feedings that are theatrical, bizarre and gruesome.

                                        As for their personality, I don't think they would have a tragic past that motivates them or anything like that. They'd be more like Bill Cipher from Gravity Falls.
