            Originally posted by HelmsDerp View Post
            Again, claiming that replacing normal human hands with claws prevents you from using the ability makes zero sense when applied to numerous maneuvers from those styles. Tripping somebody with unarmed defense isn't using your claws, it's using your legs. "Bobbing, weaving and angling away from blows" is not impacted by having claws.
            Oh, as someone else said, you can use them while having claws, you just can't make attacks and deal damage using the claws at the same time. You're not cutting people with claws when attacking with your normal human limbs. I don't forget how to use Heavy Weapon techniques when I'm only carrying around a knife either, but that doesn't mean I can use them.

            The rule seems pretty clear. You don't want to use the rule, sure. There's no badwrongfun police. But its still the default rule.

            Originally posted by nofather
            Has anyone found any of the 'worst fighting styles'?
            I'm going to go with Berserker. While there's a good combination with Improvised Weaponry at the third dot, the other two levels are generally regarded as crap, and that combo is the only way I've found to make use of any of Berserker.

            Kindred Dueling gets a lot of flak in my ciricles; people buy up to the third dot, and use only that dot and no other. And, even then that's often ignored if there's access to stakes, claws or a blood whip. Or just outright grappling. Or discipline use. Or.. you know, I think you get the idea.

            Close Quarters Combat seems to get a lot of aversion as well. Now, this could be the result of our playstyle, but the first three dots generally end up as dead levels. They seem cool, but I've rarely said that cover was outside of someone's range to reach, rarely seen someone willingly end a grapple when they could control the person for more advantage, and non-magical armor is rare enough that its not relevant. The last two dots sound nice, but then none of my players were interested in reaching that high when they could invest in other, more relevant Styles.

            Light Weapon also gets a lot of grief from my group. The first two dots are, on average, actually detriments than advatnages. Feint, the third dot, seems pretty good if not for the fact that Cheap Shot is generally considered better, though more expensive. Fourth dot seems solid. Fifth dot... sacrifices your defense in exchange for an extra point of agg. Doing so negates half the earlier levels of the FS, including the extra damage from the fourth dot, actually leading to a net loss of damage output/recieved.

            Chain Weapons is pretty much frowned on. First dot considered too weak, second too situational.

            Throwing is missing the tilt, something rather critical to the FS as a whole. In either case, only two levels and no synergy with anything else, plus throwing has to compete with guns AND has to worry about Defense.

            Originally posted by lnodiv View Post
            Based on the discussion so far - Striking styles if you're a claw maniac are literally just wasted XP.
            Claws with Street Fighting is pretty badass. I highly recommend that. Relentless Assault also works wonders with claws, and was originally designed for werewolves in their various forms. Grappling still works, and you can ask your ST if claws count as a grapple aid, like a chain would. As I was wondering above, how the ST handles movement in combat could lend a LOT of power to movement Styles, which includes Parkour and Systema. You could try learning Berserker, but, well.

                People don't like Thrust? It's a flat better alternative to All Out Attack (which is admittedly not a terribly high bar). Assuming your ST allows it Thrust combines great with Relentless Assault 2.

                Heavy Weapons 1 is at best situationally useful, and Relentless Assault has a very similar maneuver except that one is -1/+1 instead of -3/+1
