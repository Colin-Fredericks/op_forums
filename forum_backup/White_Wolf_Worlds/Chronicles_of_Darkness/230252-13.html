                        I don't think hoarding of secrets is very big in Promethean. There's many concepts in it that oppose the idea: Pilgrim signs, Pilgrim locations, the Shepherd and Pilgrim merits, and the Scrutiny bestowment, and throng-branding as well - all of these are mechanics which foster communal generosity among the Created. They're really all trying to help each other out.

                        I don't think clandestine societies of political backstabbing works either. Even if 2e does help Prometheans be a little less mobile from Wasteland there's still not going to be more than a dozen in a given city. I mean...their Y-splats aren't really even groups of societies so much as schools of philosophy. Like someone above said, it's the throng/pack that matters most (throng-branding), which again just means that they care about community more than other splats. It's just that their communities aren't very big.

                        Like Matt said above, Prometheans are alone. They only have each other. That means they need to stick together. Vampires, Mages, Mummies, and Demons all have choices to screw each other over. Beasts might just love being around everybody. Prometheans, though? They're a big happy, insular family.

                        That doesn't mean conflict doesn't arise between them - of course! - but Kindred-style politics aren't the name of the game

                                            Second part of the line's ongoing chronicle, found in Pandora's Book. About half-again-ten resident Prometheans and a load more visiting Created all in the same place.                                             Large physical congregations of Created have been covered by the line, as has using the Internet and other long-distance communication technology as a workaround that nevertheless produces a different feel to having a local court of flesh-golems in every thirteenth city.
