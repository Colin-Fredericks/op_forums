One of the comments I’d seen a few times on our feedback cards was about how I rarely sing recent or current music, so I thought I’d fix that. Calamity and Jacob took Molly and Chalcedony upstairs for the dream healing, and the rest of us just sort of sat around mingling, dancing, and trying hard not to think about how that little exercise could go horribly wrong.

Molly was obviously shaken, and I think Chalcedony was a little disturbed by the changes in her friend too (not to mention the fact that she’s still uncomfortable in the Dream House), but I didn’t sense any real hostility from either of them. Molly said that she wanted to be alone to think about all of her restored memories, and Chalcedony offered to escort her home.

I didn’t really get all of the specifics, but to hear Calamity tell it, Molly had to navigate the dreamscape, opening doors and unlocking information while her previous personality stalked her. This previous personality waited to strike; it wanted Molly to remember who she used to be. Calamity warded it off for a while, and was able to weaken it significantly… but in the end Molly had to defeat it herself, which makes sense. She did defeat it, which according to Calamity means that her current personality should still be intact. Now we just have to wait and see how that personality incorporates the knowledge of who she used to be and what was done to her.

That’s why times like last night are so vital to our relationship.

But I think that every once in a while it’s important for us to spend an evening together, to remind ourselves of why we’re getting married next month.

                Oh my God. We’re getting married. Next month. Oh my God.

I may have cried a little, and I’m not ashamed to admit it. I guess I was crying because what we felt for each other was too big to express any other way. Or something. You know.

As usual, I sat through the sermon in the choir loft, sang one solo, spoke briefly with Dad about the wedding, and left the sanctuary feeling refreshed, renewed, and ready to face the day. No mail on Sundays means I didn’t have that particular stress to worry about either, which was a relief. Still, I wish at least one of the colleges I applied to would send me something. It’s getting pretty late in the game here!

Okay, in fairness to her, she was making breakfasts to order, but she was always happy to recommend her favorite dish in that odd perky French of hers. Yvette looked as mystified as I did, which was gratifying.

                So apparently the two of them – Julia and Yvette, that is – are… an item now? Dating? Friends with benefits? I don’t know quite how to describe it, and I have to say I’m more than a little surprised by this development, considering Julia’s attitude of just a few days ago. Apparently Yvette and Marguerite are rekindling something between them, and Yvette has expressed an interest in being with Julia as well. I’m not sure if Julia genuinely reciprocates the other girl’s interest, or if she’s just going along with it to make Marguerite happy, but she’s agreed to give it a try. Oh, and both Marguerite and Yvette herself were quick to assure everyone that Yvette has no intention of causing trouble with any of our established “family.” So that’s nice.

                So the five of us decided to spend the day together on the pier, hanging out and just generally enjoying ourselves. I… honestly, I sort of agonized over that, as there are about ten billion things I should have been doing today: studying my medical journals, training with Jacob and Calamity, working on wedding preparations… the list goes on and on. But, well, those girls are my family now, and I need to be a part of their activities, or else what’s the point of all the other stuff? I mean, what am I working so hard for if not to build a life with them?

                So I tried not to fret about everything on the horizon as I strolled or sat with the three girls I’m closest to, talking and laughing about nothing much at all. I did my best to keep Yvette engaged, too… you know how I hate those awkward silences. I think – I hope – bringing her in to the group is a positive thing. I know that she reminds Marguerite of a time that might be best forgotten, but… but I trust Marguerite. I’ll be trusting her with my life and my freedom and that of my friends in a few weeks, so I have to trust her judgment when it comes to Yvette as well.

                Calamity’s Little Black Book

Because of me, that self is no longer lurking and hiding inside her waiting for a chance to resurface and Molly has her memories back for better and worse. But I should sort this into logical order and begin at the beginning shouldn’t I?

But before it reached the point of someone going up to check on them Julia appeared and explained that Yvette and Marguerite were in deep conversation about what this meant and collected some food for the three of them before returning. I guess it’s a good thing they’re not rushing into whatever they’re doing but it still strikes me as a bad idea. But it’s not my place to tell Marguerite who she can or can’t fuck and I doubt either she or Julia really care about my opinion on the situation so I’ll just keep my thoughts to myself and hope that it doesn’t blow up too spectacularly.

I spent the afternoon working with Juan Carlos on learning more about dream prisons and how to make them. In what I am sure will surprise no one I am planning to use my glassworks as the vessels much the same as I tend to do for my dream crafting. And having gone over it, there are many similarities to the physical component in each case. All in all, it was a well spent afternoon, definitely the sort of focus to keep me from fretting too much over the night’s task. I know Molly was antsy and I did feel bad for making her wait till after the show but given the situation I didn’t want to rush the contingency plans Lowjack had set up. That and I wanted to make sure I was brimming with Glamour from the show.

I can hardly imagine how good it must make Lori feel. It’s almost assuredly the vast amounts of Glamour we deal in here that have fueled our rapid gains in power and having such a ready and ample supply is something most Lost would envy but I can’t help but feel a nagging thirst for that darker spike of fear sometimes. It’s just in my nature to crave something more savory than sweet I guess.

I took a few minutes to speak to White Maria, both to thank her for teaching me since the Tai Chi really has proven useful to me and to let her know that things were on the mend between myself and Becky. She did seem pleased by that since we are among the few she considers friends. And she was pleased when I said that I had no intention of ending my meditations with her. For a moment I was afraid I’d overdone it and cost Molly her chance to regain the lost part of herself but no, Shayna wasn’t defeated.

Molly was still unaware as she roared and charged, slashing the woman deeply with her claws and sending her staggering to land face down in the sand. Shayna roared and shook off the broken spike still dangling from her side, then charged toward the prone figure. I sprinted closer, preparing to hurl myself between them to try to protect Molly but the woman rolled over and brought up her gun, sighting down on the charging tiger and firing calmly. The lone gunshot rang out and echoed as Shayna dropped. Molly pulled herself up and moved closer, kneeling down as Shayna began to dissipate and dissolve into an orange mist. Molly’s eyes flickered brightly as she inhaled, breathing in the mist until it was gone.

I then added a very brief explanation of what had happened (though I downplayed just how I am absolutely sure that Shayna would have devoured Molly without my help)

She said she needed to be alone and wanted to return to her apartment, to a place more familiar while sorting everything out. The others were a bit unsure but I assured them it sounded like a good plan and Molly departed promptly. Chalcedony followed after, wanting to follow and insure she made it home alright, which I did agree with since Molly was a bit out of it.

That part was a bit of a blur as I was feeling downright hollowed out from the rush of glamour I’d thrown behind smashing through the barriers and then fighting Shayna. They shared a table and seemed to enjoy themselves during the show. There was also Rowan, Neon and Veronica present for the show. From the way they were sitting it looked like Rowan and Neon were on a date and Veronica was there as a friend, or in some similar capacity. They all seemed to be enjoying themselves as well. It was good to see Rowan there. She has been absent for a while and only recently started coming back to the club. I think she took the break up with Lori hard. She seems to be bouncing back though, which is good. She is a sweet person.

                                                            After the show Lori and Calamity made their rounds through the crowd. Pretty early on Lori made her way over to the vampire table to talk to Viktor and Serena. They seemed to get along well. At least their conversation was amicable and they smiled a lot. At one point Lori gestured to the bar, so I assumed that she was letting them know that I’d take good care of them if they needed a drink. I wouldn’t be surprised if she comped them a couple of drinks too. The pair didn’t come up to the bar after that so I’m unsure, but I’m sure I’ll find out soon. Calamity also came over to the vampire’s table and the four of them continued the conversation. I’ll have to ask what they think of our newest guests.

                                                            Later Lori made her way over to the table with Rowan, Neon and Veronica and they all chatted for a bit. Lori seemed really happy for Rowan and Neon, which backed up my theory that they were on a date. They all seemed to be having fun, including Veronica. She was there as a friend I guess, but didn’t seem to feel left out of things.

                                                            As far as my evening goes I talked to Natasha for a while. She came to the bar and ordered a drink. I carded her and she produced an ID that said she was twenty-one. I’m not sure that it’s real, but I didn’t quibble over it. I gave her the drink she ordered and she began to vent. Apparently, she is feeling like a third wheel when it comes to Stasia and Elena. It seems that all three of the were supposed to go out to see Fury Road again, but the other two were completely focused on the other and ended up leaving Natasha out of things. I don’t think the movie thing happened. So, Natasha is feeling left out while my sister and Stasia make “goo-goo eyes” at one another. I tried to be sympathetic but wasn’t sure how to really “fix” that situation. I suggested that perhaps once the newness of the relationship (or not a relationship) wears off the pair of them will not leave Natasha out of the dynamic quite as much. After Natasha ordered her second drink I asked her if she had a way home other than driving herself. I was concerned that she was planning on drinking more than was safe. She said that she hadn’t thought of that and asked if she could stay at the Dream House for the night. I said that was fine and let her have her drinks. I did cut her off before she got too drunk. I didn’t want her to suffer a monster hangover in the morning.

She had been assigned by Lowjack to follow someone the other night. I was curious but didn’t ask who she was watching. She’d probably tell me, but I wasn’t sure if that was information that should be known. In retrospect I wish I had asked her. My curiosity is piqued and I can’t exactly go and ask her now. Maybe the next time I see her at the bar I’ll ask and see if she answers. I talked to Hailey too. Again, nothing important. Just general life stuff. I didn’t tell either woman about the errand that Calamity, Luna and I took the other night. Torrez came to Calamity in confidence. It seemed like it would be unwise to let anyone outside the motley know that there was any connection between the Summer Courtier and us. I think he asked that it stay between the motley in any case, so I didn’t mention it.

This time it was Becky, who had stayed with Calamity, and Natasha who had slept off having drunk too much last night. Food was good and appreciated by all. Conversation ran the gamut from sex with Julia’s parents to vampires. I think the earlier conversation started up because Julia wanted to make sure that no one in the motley was having relations with her parents. I think that she thought it would make things awkward later, and she is probably right. We also talked about our out of town guests Viktor and Serena. Natasha for her part was surprised that vampires even exist. It was confirmed that they do, and that they have much the same restrictions on revealing themselves to mortals as we do. They also don’t need to kill a human when they feed on them. They can just take a little bit and it would be like the person had just donated some blood. I hadn’t thought of it, but it makes sense. Otherwise everyone would know about them due to the bodies everywhere.

Apparently they had made plans to meet up and go out to a steak house for lunch to celebrate Lori having been accepted into UCLA. I am happy for Lori. I’m glad that she was able to reconnect with her father and to get accepted into such a good school. She has aspirations to becoming a doctor, and I for one think that she will do great at it. She is a lot smarter than she gives herself credit for, plus she has a genuine desire to help other people. I don’t really remember if I had picked out something that I wanted to study if I got to go to college.
