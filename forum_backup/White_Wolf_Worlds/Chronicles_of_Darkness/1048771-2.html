                    Originally posted by Mrmdubois View Post

                    Gravity forces inside an area are being exerted at a constant rate from the earth, you're manipulating that. I'm not talking about mixing physics and magic except in the most abstract sense.
                    Well, yes. But in a way I want to elaborate more on

                    Gravity is attraction between two objects, depends on both of their mass and the distance between them.
                    So any given two objects attract one another by means of gravity. These forces are often negligible, so we only count the attraction of Earth to everything else when saying "gravity" in everyday speech.

                    Depending on they way you want to look at this, it can be either 1) "the Earth attracts everything, and in this spell's area that force changes direction " or it can be 2) "the Earth attracts this object and this spell changes the direction of that force"

                    Dante90 As far as Gravitic Supremacy goes, the lack of a Withstand is enough of an implication that it's meant to affect an area.
