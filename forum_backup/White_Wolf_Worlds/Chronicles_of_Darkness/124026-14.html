                                The problem is that while in gamer parlance we use "tank" to mean "low DPS, high aggro management, high survivability," in the actual function of a tank as a military weapon is to be a fast moving, high powered weapon that's got so much armor on it you need highly specialized weapons and/or tactics to avoid it leveling your forces.

                                The Gauru form, as it stands, is a tank in the military sense. Having a lot of survivability aids in being a front-line offensive weapon because you can fire more than once before return fire reduces your ability to apply your offensive weapons (snipers, mortars, unarmored artillery).
