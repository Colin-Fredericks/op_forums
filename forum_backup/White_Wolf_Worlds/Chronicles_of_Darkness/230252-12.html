                                        Notice: I’m mostly Mage ST and have read corebook only, so if make silly assumptions on gameline, just tell me.

                                        We see that Promethean 1e is not fun enough for most possible players to like it. Let’s look then on it’s base premise and see what can be done with that, just like it was shown in Mage Chronicle Guide.

                                        Prometheans are lonely creatures, because they are few in the world and they own existence make Disquiet in people and Wasteland in whole nature. Almost all of them seek to become humans after alchemical processes inside their once mortal body. But they don’t have recollection to life of previous host that gave them their body. They make great journeys, because they are hunted by humans driven by Disquiet and they own, mocked creations – Pandorans.

                                        So here are my proposition to make game more fun to play by changing those things a bit.
                                        1. Prometheans are lonely creatures, because they are few in the world
                                        Parliament of Created: One of major problems from RPG game design with PtC 1e is that there are only very small population of Prometheans in the world. You just can’t make local political, world political or comic scale games. I would propose to make in PtC 2e corebook Tiers of Play guidelines, with at least small support for Tiers 2 ( local ) and Tiers 3 ( global games ). Those “modes” of play should be as much visable, as typical Pligrimage mode of 1e.

                                        Throng is good for you: There should be some mechanics and fluff hints that by working in throngs, Created are more human like, beside Generative Acts – maybe some Pyros discount on Transmutations? Something to be felt on each game session. Idea is that other player characters are – maybe not needed – but very good for your own.

                                        More of us, more the fun: Refinements maybe are personal take on Pilgrimage of Created, but there should be obvious mechanics that working in tandem with other practitioners you can achieve your goals quicker. Basically, it should be rather easily to make “alchemical guild” of your Refinement in the city if there are enough practitioners of it – this way we cold get quasi-Order or Covenant local structure for more political oriented games.

                                        Titles, titles and titles: To make possible political games, there need to be positions to strive to, plot for or even kill for inside they community. I’m not saying game need title level of Changling, or even Mage, but one position per Refinement for big “guilds” should make at least in-setting possible for more intrigue advanced game.

                                        2. (…)they own existence make Disquiet in people and Wasteland in whole nature.

                                        Weakening Disquiet and Wasteland: Those mechanics are very interesting and fitting for Created, but are large turn off from players. “What, I can’t have any real relationship with people on around me, and I will make my local in to living hell in time of weeks? Bye bye, local game.” Some middle ground for those harsh mechanics should be found for 2e.

                                        3. Almost all of them seek to become humans

                                        Many end goals: This is my second biggest issue with 1e and I think it’s one of many possible players. Quest to become human is very noble, but many people thinking “I’am artificial man” think “wow, it can be awesome to become MORE than human”. And contrary to developers main line, I think that idea of some Created not wanting to become mortals in the end is much strongly highlighting the Humanity of those that end mortal, than when almost all of them want to become one. It’s like in Mage – Hubris is best shown when you confront characters that fall for it, with those that become Wise.

                                        So idea is basically this – there are many New Dawns. One of them is to become human. Other is to become in reality uberPandoran ( it’s mine Centimanii character idea ). And there is rest, like Created that want to become RoboCop-like entity ( Ferrum guys, mostly ). And many, many others take on Final Goal. Yeah, most of players will probably will lean to proper Humanity. But cool NPCs and players character are made with diversion from norm. And New Dawn is exactly what is heart of Promethean character.

                                        As to mechanics, I think that something like connection of your Refinement and Athanor should point to this. Even more second than the first. If Refinement point you HOW you want to make to end of journey, your Athanor should point to WHAT you strive.

                                        For our RoboCop guy it would be Refinement of Steel ( be honing his physical and warrior code ) and Athanor of Perfection ( for ending as inhuman perfect being ). This model would open game for much, much, much more possible gameplay concepts.

                                        4. (…)alchemical processes inside their once mortal body.

                                        Flesh and Unflesh equal: I very like idea in 1e that Unfleshed were members of normal Linages, only made from “sacred materials” than human bodies. It lead to very interesting characters, like the Pygmalion Acanthus mage in mine LARP that made Galateid being from perfect statue he was obsessed with ( mage player is also great fan of Promethean ). Once more – opening game for more basic options will only serve it good. And contrasting Unfleshed Galateid that so much want to become mortal with Fleshed one that want to forsake it in road to become inhuman being would nicely support themes of game, don’t you think?

                                        5. But they don’t have recollection to life of previous host that gave them their body.

                                        Sometimes they remember: I know Created are new beings based on bodies of previous lives, and generally shouldn’t have memoires of body they inhabit. But nice would be diversion from norm on this from time to time, maybe with Merit, but something more than your muscle memory. What would do character that is practicing Aurum if she will get flashbacks that her body was serial killer? Will it take as proper road for New Dawn? Or Ulgan, that is now inhuman being made from spirits get’s vision of his previous life as happy husband with wife and two children – would he envious them?

                                        6. They make great journeys

                                        Settle for once: Creating WoD chronicle minisetting – with proper locations, bunch of NPCs and subplots – it’s tedious job, that repay in long term, when after couple of session game flows on it’s own. But running Pilgrimage chronicle, with new, little settings every few session when Created are shunted from they locations – it’s tedious to extreme, and only most dedicated Storytellers will go with them. ( This is one of reasons why I’m not running the game, right now )

                                        It is needed that short chronicle ( up to 20 game session ) should be possible to be run in one location. Yes, Prometheans will have reasons to travel often, and many games will still be Pilgrimages. But there need to be more settled style possible to play, especially for political games.

                                        7. (...) hunted by humans driven by Disquiet and they own, mocked creations – Pandorans

                                        More human antagonists: Inhuman enemies of Created are fine and good. But mortal antagonists should not only by raging mob from Disquiet. Alchemists and Hunters are especially groups that should be putted in this light. They are mortals that “knows” what Prometheans really are and they fight with them not only because they ha Disfigurements.

                                        It's just my ideas, but I think this would open game for many, many, many new players. What do you think - especially developers - on those?

                                            @wyrdhamster: I almost exclusively agree with your point about having different end-goals. To open the game up for political plays, means making it so that there are more of The Created. Making it so that there are more of The Created, means that you take away the aspect of lonelyness. Thats one of the major cornerstones of Promethean being taken away right there. It would be the equivalent to taking the mages need for secrecy and shadow-play or the vampires political intrigue.
                                            Promethean dont need the political play. Most other game lines has that in one fashion or another. There is no need to shoe-horn something into Promethean that wont add anything to the core thematic.

                                            I wouldn't characterize Promethean as a "fun" system. But thats fine. Not everything needs to be "fun". As long as it is interesting and provides an enjoyable experience for the people involved, thats enough for me and I feel that Promethean delivers in spades on the "interesting and enjoyable" part.

                                            But yes, a few suggestions for different end-goals seems like something that would add to the system. Especially because some Prometheans dont know their creator, and then who is to tell them of the possibillity of The New Dawn? Other Prometheans? What if they never encounter any? Or the ones they encounter never got told of The New Dawn either?
                                            I also rather liked your idea of "Sometimes they remember". I think that would open up for some decent gameplay with the previous relatives and aquintances of the body. Even though this was mentioned throughout Promethean 1E throughout the books, the storyhooks were shatter-shot and dropped into the books with inconsistency, so it was hard to look at it and really answer to the question "How often is this?". WIthout a merit or flaw to repressent it, it made it slide into a nebulous space even more so.
                                            Personally, I flavored Residual Memory - if applied to mental or social skills - in this fashion, but that more of a houserule than anything else, I think.

FWC is working with what’s already there, emphasizing some elements more than they used to be emphasized and downplaying others, but not introducing wholly new elements or excising anything from the game: refine and elaborate, not wholesale alteration.
