    I was just wrapping up writing my own version of the Gift of Agony, as well as a Gift of Corruption, thought you guys might want to take a looks and share your own ideas and criticisms.
    Gift of Agony
    Saturday, April 04, 2015
    3:46 PM
    Feed Back(Purity)
    The Uratha is so familiar with pain, that he wishes to share it with his prey, especially when the prey has the gall to inflict it upon their natural predator.
    Cost: 1 essence
    Action: Reflexive
    Duration: Instant
    The Uratha may activate this facet when he is injured by a melee attack, he harnesses the pain suffered by the injury, and inflicts it back on the prey several times over, the prey suffers a penalty to their defense equal to the damage dealt to the Uratha, if this reduces his defense to zero, any remaining penalty applies as an injury penalty in addition to those normally suffered by taking damage.
    Conditioning (Honor)
    Pain is nature's greatest teacher. Uratha sometimes need to teach beings of both flesh and shadow their proper place in the natural order.
    Cost: 5 essence
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Intimidate + Honor
    Action: Instant
    Duration: 1 month
    Success: The Uratha strikes the prey, whether with their claws or fists, or a lashing whip. They impress a lesson into the prey's flesh that it will not forget. The prey gains the "Disciplined" condition. Declare a behavior or course of action which is the focus of the discipline. If the prey violates the "rule" dictated by the Uratha, they suffer crippling pain, they are stunned, and suffer a -3 integrity breaking point roll, or a breaking point toward spirit with the same penalty if they are Uratha. Any conditions or bans gained as a result of the breaking point should reflect the unpleasant consequences of their actions, encouraging the prey to avoid such punishments in the future.
    Exceptional Success: The disciplining is so strong in the first place, that they prey suffers a penalty equal to the Uratha's Honor renown on any action which would violate the discipline.
    Crucible of Pain (Wisdom)
    A hunter can know no greater pain than failure, worse even that death the humiliation of failing in the presence of their peers, the Uratha steels themselves to never suffer that fate again.
    Cost: 2 essence
    Dice Pool: Composure + Empathy + Wisdom
    Action: reflexive
    Success: When the uratha suffers a dramatic failure, she may activate this facet to gain the tempered condition in relation to the dramatically failed skill.
    Exceptional Success: The Uratha takes solace in the lesson that they have learned, and restores a point of willpower.
    Jaws on Their Throat (Cunning)
    A cunning hunter is aware of exactly where pain and death come from, the user of this facet knows how best to exploit their enemies most vulnerable parts.
    Cost: 1 essence
    Action: Reflexive
    Duration: Instant
    The uratha may activate this facet when they make a called shot with a melee attack, ignore up to their cunning renown in penalties to hit, also increase the damage dealt for the purposes of inflicting called shot based consequences by their cunning renown (Note: not the actual damage dealt, only the amount needed to inflict an appropriate tilt).
    Stalwart Flesh (Glory)
    Pain and hurt are no reason not to take down your prey
    Cost: 1 essence
    Action: Reflexive
    Duration: Instant
    When the werewolf takes damage from a blow, he may spend and essence to downgrade the damage one step. From aggravated to lethal, or lethal to bashing, it has no effect on bashing wounds, and cannot be used to downgrade damage from silver. It may be used up to a number of times per scene equal to the werewolf's honor renown. This facet may only be used in Dalu, Gauru, or Urshul forms.

    Tempered: You have steeled yourself to succeed where you once failed, you gain a +2 bonus to the skill in question

    Resolution: The uratha may choose to resolve this condition just before he rolls the dice to attempt an action which uses the skill in a dice pool. The Uratha gains a number of successes equal to the Uratha's Wisdom Renown.

    Gift of Corruption
    Tuesday, March 10, 2015
    11:34 PM
    Gift of Corruption
    Trail of Pestilence (Cunning):
    The Uratha marks its prey with a wasting disease Causing damage and making it easier to track.
    The Uratha must poison the prey somehow, placing some of its body fluid into the target's drink, stabbing them with knives or claws coated in the Uratha's own blood, or any other vector in which a piece of the Uratha's fluids makes its way into the prey's body will suffice.
    Cost: 3 essence
    Dice Pool: Stamina + Survival+ Cunning vs. Stamina + Primal Urge
    Action: 1 Simple Action, Contested
    Duration: Permanent, or until the target fights off the disease
    Dramatic failure: The Uratha falls sick, gaining the sickened condition
    Failure: The facet fails
    Success: The prey is afflicted with a terrible disease, every day the prey must make a Stamina +Resolve with a penalty equal to the Uratha's Cunning. Failure causes the Uratha's Cunning Renown in Lethal Damage, while success only causes bashing damage, an exceptional success means they take no damage. The disease is removed after succeeding on the Stamina+ Resolve roll a number of days equal to the Uratha's Cunning. Any damage caused by the disease cannot be healed until the disease has passed. Any day in which the prey has suffered damage from the disease, they leave behind obvious marks of their passing, their body creates a sickening scent that clings to the environment, and they have to frequently void their bowels, and vomit the contents of their stomach. A failure results in these excretions being mixed with blood. Any hunter engaging in contested tracking against the prey starts with the Uratha's cunning added to their spoor, and the prey suffers a penalty on the opposed tests equal to the Uratha's Cunning as well.
    Exceptional Success: The victim has to roll to resist every twelve hours, this may cause the disease to run its course more quickly.
    Traitorous Whispers (Wisdom):
    The Uratha poisons the prey's mind. They suffer from delusional voices and phantom sounds, causing them to jump at things that aren't there.
    Cost: 2 essence
    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Subterfuge + Wisdom Versus Resolve + Primal Urge
    Action: 1 Simple Action, Contested
    Duration: 1 week
    Dramatic Failure: The poison infects the Uratha's own body, they suffer the paranoid condition
    Failure: the facet fails
    Success: The prey gains the paranoid condition. For the duration of the facet, the Uratha may spend one essence to afflict the prey with the delusion condition, altering its senses to conform with the delusion. The delusion must be subtle, making them believe that things are not as they seem. Making a loved one's eyes a different color and believing that they are a skin thief is an appropriate use. Seeing a massive monster in front of them that isn't there and that it is going to eat them is not.
    Exceptional Success: The prey is so distraught with paranoia that they lose a point of willpower.
    Tainted Friendships (Honor):
    The Uratha isolates its prey by poisoning its relationships with others
    Cost: 2 essence
    Dice pool: Manipulation + Subterfuge + Honor-composure
    Action: 1 minute, resisted
    Duration: 1 week
    The Uratha must have an approximate minute of social interaction with the prey. This allows the Uratha to poison the prey's mien with her corrupted words, marking her as unclean to those they care about.
    Dramatic Failure: The corruptive influences infects the Uratha's own networks lose two dots worth of social merits.
    Success: The Prey's sococial networks and personal connections are turned against the prey. Normally caring lovers turn callous, coworkers no longer trust the prey to perform their tasks. Everyone regards the prey as unclean and undesirable. The prey loses a number of social merits equal to the successes rolled on the power. The sudden loss of people that the Uratha thought they could trust also damages their self-esteem, they suffer a -2 penalty to all composure rolls.
    Exceptional Success: Not only do the Prey's social connections deteriorate, they turn against them. Any lost merit points can be utilized by the Uratha against the prey. This doesn't mean that the former allies now collude with the uratha, however they happen to mistreat and betray the prey in ways that leave them vulnerable to the Uratha's hunt.
    Violation of the Spirit (Purity):
    The Uratha violates the very spirit of a prey that it can perceive, making it's mental well-being much more vulnerable , while also tempting to take actions that will throw them out of balance.
    Cost: 2 essence
    Dice Pool: Presence + Empathy + Purity versus Composure + Primal Urge
    Action: 1 action, contested
    Duration: 1 week
    Dramatic Failure: The Uratha gains the ban condition. It cannot resist the opportunity to degrade his own harmony out of balance. Any time he is presented with an opportunity take an action that would be a breaking point towards whichever side of Harmony that she currently favors, she must take it. If she Is harmony 5 she is tempted toward spirit.
    Failure: The facet fails.
    Success: The target of the facet suffers the Uratha's Purity renown as an additional penalty on all breaking point rolls. In the case of Uratha, this only applies to whichever side of Harmony she is currently closer to, or spirit if she is balanced. They also gain a bonus equal to the Uratha's harmony on any breaking point roll that would bring them closer to balance, and may not voluntarily fail said breaking point rolls.
    Exceptional Success: The target gains the Persistent Ban condition for the duration of the facet. As the ban caused by the dramatic failure of this facet.
    Corrupted Majesty (Glory):
    The Uratha reveals the true putrid nature of their soul.
    Cost: 1 essence
    Action: Reflexive
    Duration: 1 scene
    The Uratha's form twists and cracks, as its flesh pulls away to reveal the putrid nature within his tainted soul. He gains a series of mutations to reflect this. He gains a number of points to be distributed amongst several benefits from the following table, additional benefits are possible per the Storyteller's agreement. Any-one who sees the monster's terrible visage is immediately aware that they are in the presence of a depraved and vile soul. Treat the Uratha's influence rating with anyone not associate with wounds, wounded spirits, or other nihilistic forces as hostile.
    1 point: Swim speed, Extra Sense
    2points: +1 to claws or bite attacks,
    4points: Flight, Tunneling,: Tentacles: extend melee reach by 5 yards, enemies in melee range take two points of lethal damage during their turn. Gain +2 to grapple rolls. May maintain grapples with up to strength enemies while acting normally.

      This one needs review; I just threw it together as part of another project.

      Gift of Ambush
      Return to shadow (Honor)
      A single, swift strike is delievered with such grace that the prey does even notice it's attacker,
      Action: Instant
      Cost: 1 Essence
      Dice Pool: Wits+Stealth+Honor vs. Wit+Compsure
      Effect: On the turn following a surprise attack, the Bastet may attempt to return to it's hiding place unnoticed. If successful the Bastet appears to simply faded away like a dream, as it they never existed.

      Cat's Paw (Cunning)
      Misdirection is a powerful tool. By using this Facet a Bastet can cause it's prey to see ghostly strikes and shadows were non-exist, leaving them open to the Bastet's true attack.
      Action: Instant
      Cost: 1 Essence
      Dice Pool: Wits + Larceny + Cunning vs. Composure + Primal Urge
      Effect: The Bastet reduces it's prey's Defense by it's Cunning Renown for a number of rounds equal to successes rolled.

      Pounce (Purity)
      The true expession of the ambush is the pounce. Springing from surprise the Bastet sinks teeth, claws, or both into their prey. Each breed has a variant on this Facet. Lions claw the gut while Tigers always bite the neck.
      Action: Reflexive
      Cost: 1 Essence
      Effect: The Bastet gains a it's Purity Renown as a Weapon Bonus on it's attack in addition to the normal advantages of attacking from surprise.

      Know the ground (Wisdom)
      Preparing a site for ambush is an ancient tradition and the Bastet make good use of it. By walking the grounds of a future battle, they can find places to hide that no one will expect.
      Action: Instant
      Cost: 1 Essence
      Dice Pool: Wits + Survival + Wisdom
      Effect: This Facet effects one environment roughly one hundred yards per Primal Urge in area. This can be a nature area such as a park or an urban area like a housing project. Any attempts to hide in the area for the next week have one of two effects. For the Bastet and it's allies, they gain a Equipment Bonus equal to it's Wisdom Renown. Prey of the Bastet, even unknowing prey, suffers a penalty equal to the Bastet's Wisdom on all attempts to hide or to ambush the Bastet and it's allies.

      Deadly Play (Glory)
      A glorious Bastet strikes with dazing speed, leaving prey reeling well after the surprise blow has landed.
      Action: Reflexive
      Cost: 1 Essence
      Dice Pool: Presence + Intimidation + Glory vs. Composure + Primal Urge
      Effect: The Bastet continues the benefits of striking from ambush into the next round of combat, effectively ambushing the prey a second time.
