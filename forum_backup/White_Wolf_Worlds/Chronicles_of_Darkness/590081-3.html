                        Sadly the timeline is coming up blurry, but as far as I see, the only details to be added is that right before the Rite of Return and Fall of Irem is that slave rebellion briefly mentioned before. This leads directly into each other, as the Shaniatu justified the mass sacrifices to Ammut as being a punishment for the rebellion. Also notable is that the Sphinx is Iremite, and was originally a Sha-beast. It was created as monument to the power of the Temakh (which is synonymous with Shaniatu, now), and was built on the spot where Azar was killed, and was at the edge of the City of Pillars.

                                            Some texts refer to the Weighing of the Heart from historical Egyp-tian theology, but without the feather of Ma’at as a counterweight. Instead, Azar places his hand on the scale, cheating it on the petitioner’s behalf. It’s not clear whether these additions are in keeping with the oldest texts or are later interpolations.
                                            I'm in love with this little detail; for as heady and mythic as Mummy can be, the things like these show a very human underside to it all.
