    Damaging spells and Explosions

    09-08-2016, 08:50 AM
    Let's say I want to take a spell like Celestial Fire, and instead of targeting 1 person with the full effect of heavenly fire I want to use it like a grenade from the Hurt Locker. Can I do that? How do I do that? After all explosions have 3 primary factors. Damage the amount of force suffered by someone at the epicenter of the blast, the primary target of the blast. Then there is force, the damage applied to everyone else. Blast radius the is number of yards for the primary force of the blast, the total distance is equal to double that amount.

    So Umm...First my own theory. Converting any spell to a blast first costs 1 mana. Why? Because I feel that helps balance things.
    So damage and Force let's start with those. Those are derived from potency. Divide potency equally by these any remainder goes into force.

    Blast radius = the number of steps in scale.

    So is this a good method? What do you all think?

      There's nothing that requires it to cost Mana. Just cast the spell on an area instead of a single target. The downside is that you hit everything in the area. Turning an already Wisdom-risking attack spell into a dangerous area-of-effect is a price unto itself. You also reduce your dice pool a bit to target an area, reducing the potential damage you could inflict.

      In other words, yeah, you can do this, and no, you don't need to worry about it too much.

                          I think we're going to have many fun discussions, Hapless.

                          I'm not sure why you're designing the spell like that, though. With a damaging spell in an area, it'd affect all targets within the area equally; that is, they all take the same damage. You could make it try and use the traditional explosive rules, but that makes the spell weaker. Furthermore, without Reach and Mana, it can't inflict Aggravated damage.

                          With all the factors of the spell you've described and included, everyone inside the radius is burned for four damage (Bashing or Lethal depending on if it's Fraying or Unraveling).

                          So, using the factors you've described, Ammon would stick his blade in the ground and smite the bastards with heavenly flame for four damage within an area the size of a small room. No hope of evasion.

                          Given that he's acting in keeping with his Persona and acting in keeping with his Virtue (I imagine), I'd call for a Wisdom roll at Understanding (three dice) with a +2 bonus. He acted in keeping with his Virtue and the nature of his soul, but he still used magic to injure others. If he planned this out beforehand and weighed consequences, I'd give him an extra +1.
