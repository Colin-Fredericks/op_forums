    Well the city of the setting is in Utah, and there was a minor magical nuclear meltdown a few years back between the Seers and the Pentacle. Almost every supernal element in the city was temporarily exposed. Very quickly afterwards, the highest ranking members of each faction, with the exceptions of Banishers (for obvious reasons), the Forsaken and The Predator Kings, signed a treaty. This treaty is upheld by the Changeling Freehold, and sealed in the Wyrd, effectively making the place a very loose freehold in it's own right.

    The Brotherhood would have been attracted to the area in the aftermath of this event, and the Bubbleheads are going to be having the time of their life trying to understand ALL THE THINGS. Specifics from there are hard for me to nail down- there are three other storytellers, running Vampire, Changeling, and Mage, while I run Beast.
