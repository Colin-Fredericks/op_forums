            118) A Free Council cabal has just opened up a next generation VR lounge. They're not doing a lot of publicity stunts, but the people who have gone rave about just how -real- it all seems. What's actually going on is that the cabal is sponsoring trips into the Astral, paying the mana entrance costs for others and doing some finely tuned mental manipulations to make the transition easier. They have at least one Dreamspeaker who helps to monitor things, and one of the Mages is apparently creating a brand new legacy to further aid in their endeavors. They hope that introducing Sleepers to the Astral will help enlighten and encourage them to explore new possibilities. Something inevitably goes horribly wrong.

                (@Menace: I hate to explain jokes, but... when a person ascends, their body goes with them. They often leave behind a sort of mystically significant and magical echo of their body called a Sarira.                 119) A Guardian returned from a mission to an Atlantean site oddly quiet. His demeanor prompted Mysterium investigation, but they barely manage to rescue one or two artifacts before the Guardians destroy the site. The site depicted an Ascended King, who ruled in benevolence and wisdom, but also held the body of a traitorous former Mysterium mage who joined the Seers. They wish to understand why the site was so important to obliterate.

                    [Casts Time and/or Death spell on thread]

                    120) The Cabal encounters a decent sized city that seems to have lost connection with the larger Awakened community. When Death Mage Sight is used, they learn that Ghosts are formed far more often than normal. But the city isn't unduly overrun with the spectral dead. Further Investigations yields that the leylines have been modified - any ghost that enters/passes one is drawn along it to the nearest node, which also happens to be a Hallow. Once there, the Ghost's Essence is leeched from them into the Hallow (where it becomes Mana), then they seem to disappear as an Avernian Gate forms. The few ghosts who remain are often far more powerful than normal, though, and use Sleeper proxies to wage a quiet war against their peers in other "territories". Who modified the leylines this way and why?

                    121) The Cabal gets a lead on an odd lingering mystery from their Order. Upon investigating a small city they make an astonishing discovery - everyone in it has Awakened, at least partially. While there are normal Awakened and Proximi, everyone else has some command over one of the Arcana (treat as Arcana 1, Gnosis 1). However, the Lie seems distorted and strengthened as well - anyone, or indeed, anything (including information) that leaves the city is subject to alterations of memory and content as per Dissonance, regarding this phenomena in the city. Even the Awakened. Messages sent to Order superiors are altered into revealing nothing about this mass Awakening, local news reports have mundane explanations for magical incidents edited in for viewers outside the city, and even commuters forget their powers upon leaving the city bounds, only remembering them when they re-enter.

                    122) A powerful pulse of Mana pings the Mage Sight of everyone in the Consilium/Assembly in what Mages later call The Imbued Phenomenon. Random people in the area gain "super powers" (A Spell, as if they were an Imbued Item). While these powers seem to provoke the memory altering effects of Quiescence as normal for Supernal Magic, they don't harm Sleepers' souls (no Breaking Point), nor risk Paradox due to witnesses. These Imbued also seem to behave as if they are still Sleepers, being still subject to the Lie to any non-Imbued Supernal magic.
                    [An excuse to have "superheroes", with the Cabal cleaning up after their messes.]

                        123) There's a new Awakened in the city that has everybody (who's in the know) biting their nails in anxiety. Apparently the forces that be went to great lengths to ensure that this mage was kept as docile and ignorant as possible. Projecting subconscious interference that causes them to lose focus of their surroundings, suffer memory lapses, clinical depression, self loathing, doubt, and a bunch of other stuff that might generally hinder somebody's ability to function in society. So when they finally did awaken by accident, all that repressed energy was released like a bomb throughout the city district. Every supernaturally attuned creature instantly felt the power radiating off this guy before it settled down again. Most of the time, he seems calm and collected, but when he feels threatened or provoked, his power erupts into violent outbursts that he has only minor control over.

                        You decide to figure out just who this mage is, as well as the source behind his astounding font of power. And if in the process you can figure out a way to tap it for your own, all the better. You'd better hurry, though. Somebody did not want this guy to become a mage, and they most likely aren't going to sit around and let his powers develop any further than they already have!

                            124) An incident of mass soul-distortion has the Consilium/Assembly on edge for Reapers. It seems like a staggering number of people had their souls tampered with almost overnight. Some fear an undetected infiltration of Reapers on an unprecedented scale. It doesn't help when investigators detect the Nimbi of a number of new Mages upon the Sleepers affected. The neophyte Mages claim they were investigating as well, and just fouled up and contaminated the Mystery in their own attempts at Revelation.

                            [The Idea I had behind this (which you can take or leave, in whole or in part) was: A Mad One lurks in the sewers of the city, having enchanted the pipes to preserve all biological material that enters them until it passes into their care. Using the material, they've forged a staggering network of sympathetic links to everyone who has ever used a toilet in that section of the sewer system. But pursuing their Obsession led them to where they needed the help of an Echo Walker. They bartered for what they needed by allowing the Echo Walker the use of their network, which is what triggered the incident.]

                                125) The place the Cabal has traveled to is home to a great - if frustrating - Mystery. Every day, with the tolling of the town clock tower, a powerful wave of magic surges over the area, dispelling most active spells (treat it as a high, though not unbeatable, dispel/Clash of Wills). Only a few, precious, coveted Hallows are unaffected by the wave. Local mages have reconciled themselves to dealing with short term spells, and many have exploited the "free cleanup" by unsafely Relinquishing spells with little care. But what is the source of this recurring dispel? An Artifact? The workings of an Archmaster? Something stranger? And what would happen if it suddenly stopped?

                                126) An old, powerful Master recently died. As quickly becomes evident, they were quietly keeping a number of monsters and dangerous phenomena contained. Thankfully, not all of their spells are undone - they cultivated a large group of Sleepwalkers to carry many of the spells responsible. Except someone is tracking them down and murdering them, one by one. As each falls, more and more binding spells become undone.

                                    127) Something's drawing some of the spirits of the city to an abandoned warehouse used as a meeting space by a local cult. Once more than one spirit is within the building's walls, none of them can leave — whether physically or by discorporation — until one of them has consumed all of the others. It's not clear what attracts a spirit to the warehouse to begin with, as the cult seems as Shadow-blind as the average Sleeper and the building itself is spiritually barren, but spirits related to the cult's members and the "winners" of previous bouts seem to be the most common victims of the process. Stranger still, the latest victor — a greater Jaggling of unpalatable resonance — has neither left the warehouse nor been engaged by subsequent challengers; the Shadow of the building is piled high with the unmoving Corpus of starving spirits. What's at work here? Has the champion disrupted the Mystery, or has a new phase of the process been set into motion?

                                        128) While tracking a Sleeper they think is close to Awakening the Cabal is drawn into his Mystery Play as actors. The Mastigos has to make him fall head over heels in love with him, only to utterly dash his hopes and tear up his illusions. The Moros comes last, he has to to remind him of his greatest fear, then give him the choice of mastering it or dying. If all three succeed, he awakens, if one fails he doesnt make it and settles as a Sleepwalker forever. The players could use magic to assist, but know that throwing spells directly at his pattern in his current state will screw everything up. They have 12 hours to make this happen.

                                                    129) The Consilium/Assembly has always been home to an usually large number of Sleepwalkers. They fill the lower (and even middle) ranks of each Orders' numbers, manage projects, hold Spells, and keep the Awakened world running smoothly.                                                         Here's something I wrote with /tg/'s help.

                                                        130) A strange Golem was discovered in the basement of an unpopular Mystagogue who finally passed at the age of 113.

                                                        The Golem is in fact a relic of the Time Before. It is full of faulty, contradictory, incomplete or just outright wrong information, as is typical of pre-Fall relics. It can still teach the Rotes that were used in its creation, and it might have actually accurate insight sometimes.

                                                        However, it is, at the end of the day, a malfunctioning, menial servant. Not a death machine or keeper of great knowledge.

                                                        Conflict over its fate could kick off a storyline. Should it be scrapped or fixed? Can it be scrapped or fixed safely? And for the love of God, can someone keep it away from those damn Sleeper kids?
