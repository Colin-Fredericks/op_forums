    Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post

    That was the idea.

    Increasing the importance of Bardos and Time Fractures is definitely something I want to do, as well as changing how time travel works.
    • For starters, the Guardians of Forever as they exist in 1E can burn in Hell.
    • Screwing with time will be easier. No need to spend Mania, you can just do it.
    • Timeline changes are unpredictable. You might change the "real" timeline, you might get dumped in a Time Splinter, you might create a Time Splinter, or something crazy.
    Well, I think the Guardians could still exist. Sometimes, as the ST needs. With their existence varying even inside the same game. Time Travel being funny like that. Need Time Police? They exist! Need there to not be Time Police? They never existed (yes, even when they did before)!

    Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post
    But Genii definitely do need more mysteries and a few clear adversaries. We do have the Illuminated, who are neat because they are totally amoral, but can blend in with more scrupulous Genii. On top of that, there are plenty of Bardos that might have an interest in forcing themselves to be real or straight-up invading reality (and a few Inspired might think that's a good thing). I think we also should make Inspiration a more active force in the universe as well. Inspiration is supposed to be a force of creation, chaos and, well, inspiration, but except for the Genii and Mania storms, we don't see a lot of Inspiration directly acting upon people.

    What if places of creativity and innovation acted like natural Mania generators? And what if you could occasionally find a "naturally-occuring" Wonder, like how Mummies can sometimes find unintentionally created Relics? Maybe even a Wonder that has rare or valuable capabilities that other Genii may want to study and adapt for themselves? Making Inspiration part of the world, and worth studying directly, would help give Genii things to do, and help make the Genii themselves look less like the enlightened holders of divine inspiration, and more like people who were lucky enough to figure out more than the rest of the world.
    So, places where the fast flow of thoughts and possible ideas generate Mania? Or moments in time and space that empower an individual into a temporary Genius (able to build one Wonder and then Inspiration fades)? Or a Wonder than wants to be - anyone working on it gains the ability to do so, losing Inspired abilities once they are no longer focused (obsessed) with that?

Ancient Wonders resemble modern technology; future technology resembles modern Wonders, only mundane. Inspiration creates mundane insight, and mundane insight creates Inspiration; they're two sides of the same coin.

                This stuff has limited mechanical representation in 1st ed, but I really like the idea of "genius" as ambiguously supernatural, a kind of God-Machine maybe-magic-maybe-mundane vibe.

                                    I think I am person who complains most about Obligation/Integrity.
