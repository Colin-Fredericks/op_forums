    Druids (Lodge of Seasons 2e conversion)

    05-20-2022, 01:19 PM
    Lodge of Seasons
    Druids
    Pages 91-93 of Lodges the Faithful for lore

    Bonds

    Blessing: Your impression is considered one higher with nature and elemental spirits. During your favored season, the impression is considered two higher. As well, during her chosen season, count her renown as one higher for the purposes of flaring her spirit brands, and add her associated renown to any rolls to interact with nature spirits, cross the gauntlet, manipulate spiritual resonance, and track in the wilderness. You also gain the symbolic focus condition (persistent) when you have the Siskur-Dah condition.

    Aspiration: To live according to the seasons (see below).

    Ban: The lodge’s ban changes with the seasons (see below).

    Sacred Hunt
    The Lodge Sacred Hunt changes with the seasons. During Spring all social rolls made during a hunt earn an exceptional success on three successes instead of five. In Summer, when making an all out attack while on the hunt you retain your defense. In Autumn any roll made to keep the prey of the hunt unaware of you gains three extra dice. In Winter your prey cannot spend willpower against you.

    Lodge Secrets
    • The members of the Lodge of Seasons revere the natural cycles of the world, in particular the changing of one season to the next, and are compelled through their bond to their totem to live according to the current season. In Spring Uratha aspire to find peaceful solutions to the hunt whenever possible and gain the ban “Give new life a chance to grow”. In summer, the members aspire to right all wrongs done to them in Spring and gain the ban “See each conflict to its end”. During Autumn those of the lodge aspire to settle all debts and gain the ban “Strike down no prey without first understanding their tools”. Finally, in Winter, the Uratha aspire to learn all they can from Hisil and its inhabitants and gain the ban “Be introspective in your hunts”.
    • Spring is a time of new life and of growth, and old pacts made with the spirits of Spring must be honored. The druids gather on the first full moon after the Spring equinox to engage in a ritualistic oragy as they honor the season of fertility. If not enough druids are able to gather it is acceptable to find human or wolf blooded partners for the night, although this is seen by the lodge as less pure an expression of the ritual. There are also rumors of druids, far from any other human life, who spend this night with the local wolf population.
    • Summer is a season of heat and rage, the druids of the Lodge of Seasons honor their pacts with the spirits of summer on the night following the Summer Solstice by building bonfires in the night. Around these fires they tell others stories of Spring, of those wrongs they let go because it was not in season to pursue. After all have shared they stand and place part of themselves into the bonfire, allowing the pain in their body and the smell of cooking flesh fuel their rage for the rest of the season as the right the wrongs done to them.
    • The druids honor their pacts with the spirits of Autumn by gathering together for a great feast. The Autumn feast consists of the most recent hunt of each lodge member. Because the lodge focuses on damage to the gauntlet many members prepare for this feast by hunting hosts, the lodge consumes the corpses of the host hybrids with the intent to gain strength from their bodies. Sometimes though a host isn’t the most recent hunt, adding variety to their meal.
    • When Winter comes the druid must be ready to die. This is the one season where druids do not make an attempt to gather together at the turning of the season. The druids say forwell to their packs and enter the shadow to honor their old pacts with the spirits of winter alone. They seek out spirits and make offerings of essence in order to convince the spirit to watch their death. Then they dig themselves a grave in and lay in it, unmoving, throughout the night of the Winter Solstice. When the first sunrise of Winter happens the druids rise with it, now dead in the eyes of the shadow for the remainder of the season.
    Tools
    The Lodge of Seasons has access to the Lodge Sorcery merit and can choose influence in wind, change, and their chosen season.

    Gauntlet Membrane (Wolf Rite •••)

    Beshilu are able to reduce the gauntlet to nothing, allowing for spirits to come into the flesh as they see fit, and the herd can find themselves wandering the shadow. While the two worlds influence each other, they were never meant to touch on the scale the rat host can cause. This rite is used by druids to create a pseudo-gauntlet where large-scale damage has been done in order to keep the worlds separate while the gauntlet heals.
    Symbols: The Gauntlet, growth, separation
    Sample Rite: An Irrakka stalks along the borders of the old Beshilu territory where the gauntlet no longer exists. As he goes he plants small saplings to separate the area between healthy gauntlet and gnawed gauntlet. When he has finished he travels to the center of the verge to plant the final sapling. His work here has only just begun.
    Action: Extended (25 successes: each roll represent 30 minutes)
    Success: If there was no gauntlet here, the gauntlet’s strength increases to 1. This acts as a temporary gauntlet for a lunar month, and can still be gnawed away by Beshilu and interacted with normally, however, no loci will form within this area until the gauntlet’s strength is at least 1 without the use of this rite.

    Resonant Winds (Wolf Rite •••)

    A lone Azlu disrupts the relationship between flesh and shadow when it tampers with the gauntlet, when the Azlu have completely walled the two worlds off from each other and then keep spinning their webs, spirits die and those in the flesh aren’t even able to keep going through the motions. When the damage done to the gauntlet is too much and the shadow is empty of resonance, druids use this rite to jumpstart the healing process.
    Symbols: Restoration, The Gauntlet, Wind, The current season
    Sample Rite: An Ithauer gathers the fallen leaves of the Autumn trees, along with its seeds, and takes them into the shadow. There, she buries the seeds and places the leaves like a barrier over them as she moves through the shadow. Finally, she walks through the rite area again and scatters the leaves to the wind as she finishes the rite.
    Actions: Extended (25 successes: each roll represent 30 minutes)
    Success: A successful performing of this rite causes essence to enter the area like a power air front, blowing in from outside of the area cut off from the flesh. The essence is resonant with the current season and to the element of air, and is enough for new spirits to not only grow, but sustain themselves on it as well. The current of essence will blow in for a lunar month. If the damage to the gauntlet has not been repaired by then the druids may have to perform this rite again in order to keep the spirits alive. The exact amount of essence entering the area is equal to a 3 dot locus condensing in areas marked by the rite master.
