                                        12-20-2014, 01:00 PM
                                        Originally posted by Enokh View Post
                                        So, in my little group, we had been saying that called shots to the head and various things like exceptional successes with longsword attacks and whathaveyou bypass the "lethal damage does bashing" rule and just does lethal. However, last night I happened upon the rules for damage again, and didn't see anything like this.

                                        Do Vampires truly only take bashing from lethal sources except for specific exceptions (kindred dueling 3 and protean claws) or are there rules for general exceptions that I'm missing? Thanks!
                                        Vampires only take bashing damage from mundane sources that usually deal lethal damage, like guns or knives. The exception is massive trauma, like getting hit by a truck.

                                        I've never tried dealing lethal damage on exceptional successes, and that would shift around the balance of combat beyond what we playtested. When the new edition was in development, I considered having certain called shots be exceptions to damage downgrading, but it seemed too fiddly to make the default. I also didn't want to make called shots seem mandatory.

                                        That said, sometimes I consider head wounds and such massive trauma. If a vampire gets a sword through an eye or something and they're not using Resilience, it'd feel off to me for it not to be lethal. But that's something I do pretty occasionally and depends heavily on the narrative part of combat.
