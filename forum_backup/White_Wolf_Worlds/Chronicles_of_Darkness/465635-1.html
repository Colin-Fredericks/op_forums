                            Originally posted by revanauditore92 View Post
                            Descent feels too much like the Matrix to me. I prefer my angels and demons to be rooted in the spirtual and mystical.
                            I think it can be done.

                            First- the God Machine. Let's just drop the Machine part. Instead, treat as as what it is- a distant, cold, logical divinity who manipulates thousands of people to proceed with its projects. Infrastructures don't has to be mechanical- they are forms of sacred geometry, like Ley Lines or any other similar ideas. Instead of saying Gears, you can see it as God's Hands, Ears and Eyes- for God see all, hears all and acts everywhere. The trick is that God is not as strong as the stories claim- maybe he never was omnipotent, just a single powerful divinity who rose higher then the rest. Maybe He was more powerful in the past, but has lost some of His power through a "failed project" of some kind. What ever the truth is, God has became much more dependent on his Angels and cultists.

                            As for Angels, it is easy to just through away the machine-like visage and replace it with plane weird aesthetics. Many eyes, beast heads, alien bodies, perfect and terrible beauty. Each is devoted to a single mission- there is not need to change it. Angel in Hebrew (or Malakh) means "messenger", after all. They are inhumane beings who manifest in the world, searching to fulfill God's will. However, taking a human form also means being subject to human emotions- and while usually walking on God's path would protect them, defying their mission would make them stray too much from the Great Plan and fall, turning their false lives into truth- at least, a form of truth. Angels can sin in human body- and that sin turns them into Demons.

                            Once they are Unchained, they lose touch with God- and he starts searching after them. Demons shouldn't exist, and so they mess up with His Great Design. As long as they disguise themselves as humans, the Angels can't find their Divine Aura. They no longer have Rank, instead gaining power from their own, personal Divinity (instead of Primum). The also no longer consume Essence, finding energy in the Shell that are left behind after God's projects, or Qlipoth (instead of Aether). Being creatures of divine power, they can manipulate the souls of humans and rip the divine structure of reality for their own benefit- in the forms of Pacts. That process allows them taking parts of the human's place in reality in order to create lives of their own, and they can even possess the whole human and through his soul to Hell. Demon, however, can't enter there- God needs all the angels He has, and so he captures them and break their essence in order to make a fresh start. Hell is the only place He can't reach- and so they seek after a way to get in. Until then, a human body would be enough. Sometimes, they can even meld their essence into God's creation in order to take one of his forms as Cover- but it is a risky process which allows Him to notice you.

                            Finally, about their powers- Embeds could be called "Jewellery", or even Hoshen. (the biblical stone decorated breastplate of the high priest). Exploits are now Invocations. Demonic Form abilities are called Transformations for Modifications, Symbols for Technologies, Movement for Propulsions and Visages for Processes. Finally, Ciphers would be Keys.

                            As for antagonists- Angels are as described. Exiles are angels who still walk on God's Path, but didn't returned to him when the mission was over- or simply screwed up too much. Cryptids could be called as Haiot HaKodesh ("the holy beasts"). Stigmatics are people touched by God- you could either keep their name or call them Prophets, both could work. They can see his divine projects everywhere, so they can get a bit mad.

                            That's it, I think. Hope it helped with something :P

                                        Originally posted by revanauditore92 View Post
                                        Descent feels too much like the Matrix to me. I prefer my angels and demons to be rooted in the spirtual and mystical.
                                        The irony is that the God Machine is rooted very strongly in the spiritual and mystical. If anything, it draws from far more mystical and spiritual traditions than Demon the Fallen.

                                        Like others have said, its simplicity within itself to just ignore the machine bits. There's nothing in the game that depends on God being a literal machine. Infrastructure, occult matrix? These are just fancy words for ritual components and the actual ritual itself. God needs the right person (divine-chosen bloodlines) in the right place (geomancy) at the right time (when the stars align) to work its miracles. You can play a red skinned devil with horns, goat legs, a tail, and hellfire powers, or a manipulative, hedonistic, addictive succubus, or an imp, or even a lion headed wheel with spinning legs of fire. The spy thing is just the continuing war between the forces of Heaven and Hell in a way that's hidden from human eyes.


                                        Playing the game without the machine bits is insanely easy. Everything you could want for making a classical-Christian-themed demon is there, just under different names. Even demon summoning is possible. Now, if you don't like the idea of a covert war between Angels and Demons, that's another matter, and one that has nothing to do with "replacing the God Machine." That's kind of the premise of the entire game, where the struggle between Heaven and Hell is the central driving force behind DtD stories. You want demon on demon action? Where God is the absolute Good Guy? You'll have to play Demon the Fallen, or a different game entirely.
