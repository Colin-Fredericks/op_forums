    DtD, What are your House Rules?

    12-06-2021, 08:06 AM
    This gameline has been out for a good amount of time and I find myself curious what twists or tweaks others have made to the game to fit their table best. What small, or not so small, changes have you implemented or seen others implement and how did it affect the game? Of course though, if I'm asking I should also provide what I have done to get the ball rolling.

    The following are a few small changes I've made for my own game out of personal preference:

    1) Investigating a demon uses Investigation rather than the occult skill, as you investigate the inconsistencies that may surround a cover. Be it the limitations of whose memories are altered by pacts, the effects of low cover or other things someone looking close enough may notice.

    2) As I've seen others do as well, I took a stab at changing the show of power exploit. First off it can only "hold" a number of abilities up to your primum. When you see an ability you may activate the exploit to "record" the effect rather than produce it. When recording an ability if you have no available capacity you may "overwrite" one of the previously recorded abilities. Then finally, if mimicking a mage's spell the mimicked effect is at minimum spell factors though the primary spell factor still applies its bonus, treating the demon as having arcana equal to the spell's rating. This is to enable the intent of the exploit only affectively achieving a single success on a powers activation.

    3) Pacts that are unbalanced in favor of a mortal may attract angelic notice. For each point it is unbalanced in favor of the mortal the storyteller may roll one dice. On a success, the God-Macine takes notice and an angel or other agent of the god machine will investigate sometime in the next session/chapter. On an exceptional success the pactbound gains the surveilled condition. When the condition refers to cover, use the pactbound's integrity score instead. If the surveilled condition progresses to hunted, those hunting the pactbound are not seeking to kill them but rather kidnap and extract information regarding the unchained.

    4) In regards to "Pacts are usually permanent, lasting for as long as the contract exists and both parties live." Change [Both] to [Either]

    5) Exiled may participate in "Cover trading" as detailed in Interface as though they were one of the unchained. Any details they purchase must immediately incorporated into their cover. It is called out in the story teller's guide that exiles lack the support of the god machine to restore their own cover as well as do not have access to pacts as demons do to maintain it themselves. This is primarily to give exiles clear motivation or benifit for cooperating with the unchained.
