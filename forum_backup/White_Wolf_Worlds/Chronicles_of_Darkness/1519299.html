    My Contagion Chronicle Idea

    07-31-2023, 09:13 PM
    (NOTE: This idea was originally posted on a discord server so the formatting will look weird)


    I’ve finally made a contagion chronicles outline! Mainly for a group of a human, vampire, Deviant and werewolf (with a Demon cell of 4 as supporting characters)

    so yeah, let me know how you all like this idea.

    edit: (Also…. My original idea is using this for a fanfic-story, but I worked hard on it and thought I’d share)

    IDEA: The GodMachine creates infected Elimination infrastructure. Like a big factory or a power plant. (Going to go with power plant) It creates a thick fog (use *Heavy Rain* or *Blizzard* environmental conditions for sight and perception penalties). It takes dead or near-dead bodies from the hospital and transports them to the structure. There, they get tossed into an incinerator (the lynchpin) and it bellows out the fog. Problem is, it’s STUCK on ‘make fog’. Maybe the fog is combustible via something in the power plant, or another infrastructure… point is, the fog is continuing to spread. What happens when the right ‘match’ is struck in this theoretically-flammable fog? (That’s up to you)


    SYMPTOMS OF CONTAGION:

    1. Fog continues to billow into town and surrounding areas in a perfect radius. And it blocks out the sun to the point of being stuck at being no brighter than ‘dusk’.

    2. The fog has a red tint and carries Vitae and Essence (and maybe Aether). *Provides a +1 temporary point. Once a day*

    3. The edge of the fog is where it’s the thickest. Driving and hiking through it is a chance-die (at least for the NPCs) to get through. Failure means you end up coming out a totally different inside edge. *(ex: head out north, fail roll, you come into the edge on the south.)*. Dramatic fail could mean a loss of integrity or kidnapped by angels (see **characters** 3a) or something else.

    CONSEQUENCES

    1a. Spirits and monsters become more common. They start stalking the streets.

    1B. Further out past the city limits, the fog gets thicker. Easier to get lost and better to hide illicit activities.

    2A. Something in the Vitae in the air is *off.*. Vampires are easier to enrage and harder to calm down.
    (+/-1 to appropriate rolls)

    2b. Harmony is disrupted for werewolves. The fog can boost it or drain it each day. *(At beginning of ‘day’ roll 1d10: 1-4: -1 harmony, 5-6: no change, 7-10: +1 Harmony)*


    3a. There’s ONE thing that comes through. A train. A large cargo train. People hear it come in, goods show up, and it leaves. NOBODY remembers it having a loading crew or anything. Just train shows up, delivery trucks mysteriously filled, ready for humans or undercover angels or others to drive to the stores or warehouse/s. But the train station runs close to the power plant, doubling as a Defense Infrastructure for the power plant. (Only way I could think of to keep the mortals from just up and leaving the town while still keeping them healthy)


    CHARACTERS
    1a. Cultists of the GM, or another organization, start making problems. But the chief of police can’t do much because he’s running a bit thin, especially with other problems.

    1B. A friend of the Deviant has tracked the conspiracy (the WF) to the outskirts of town, where some civilians (or supernaturals) are kidnapped or lured for experiments or to boost the new militia. (See **COMPLICATIONS** 1a and 1B)

    2A. Due to the frenzy and/or drunk on the bonus vitae (and being able to walk in ‘the day’), some vampires have battled on others territories. Less secrecy and planning, more slaughter.

    2b. The werewolves can get a bit more savage, also thinking themselves more powerful. Some succumb to their wolves, starting fights with other tribes (or even with the vampires). Others try to retreat deeper into the nearby woods or hide in the city.

    3a. Due to it being defensive infrastructure, Angels and their kind won’t allow anyone unauthorized (ie; the delivery/cargo truck drivers) onto the property. The drivers themselves are even limited to just the area with their trucks.

    **COMPLICATIONS:** (this is where my ideas start blending in with one another)

    1a. Contacts and allies are less helpful in the police department. Chief is even ORDERED to relax on missing persons cases by the deviant’s conspiracy.
    *(-1 to rolls dealing with most police and aid social rolls)*

    1B. Deviant’s conspiracy has taken up arms as a ‘heroic militia’ or ‘PMC of the people’. While also going to eliminate the supernatural, they also try to find the escaped Deviant. And to do experiments on other people.
    *(Conspiracy agents patrolling the streets and will shoot first if the heroes are too close to some places they shouldn’t be. Maybe some are corrupted god machine agents?)*

    2A. The vampires target the homeless, people they secretly hate and even criminals. Maybe some of the vampires work with/for criminals? The black market and underground networks, even the bars and clubs are paranoid or closed. What vampires are still sane retreat into dens or homes
    *(Vampires could ambush the PCs, and/or limit the contact they have with shady individuals)*

    2B. Similar to the vampires. Hunt people they hate, cause terror for hikers and campers, stories of ‘mad dog things’. Those that HAVENT gone insane in frenzy are starting to go insane from being confined to human and near-human forms. If someone sees them, they’ll get shot or reported.
    *(Werewolf contacts and tribes are wary of the pack member)*

    3a. The corrupted angels will not allow anyone into the power plant that’s causing this whole mess. Some angels could even HIDE *IN* the fog, kidnapping people and other supernaturals to add to the fog machine. And since the train station and train are defense infrastructures, that’s just ANOTHER level of ‘oh shit’ to deal with.
    *(Very difficult to assault the power plant. No plans exist of it, or at least none that can be understood)*

    3Ai. If anyone does manage to get on the train, when it reaches the edge of the fog, they’ll just ‘emerge’ in the power plant.
    (Basically an easy way to A: keep the NPCs from leaving and B: keep fueling the broken machine)


    Oh, and the 4 demons? They’re sent to stop the infrastructure, but they MIGHT get spotted unusually easy if they tried to do this themselves. (Idk)


    So yeah. I guess what just spawned is a ME3 “army builder” of sorts. At least from where I’m looking. Either assist, intimidate and/or eliminate the various groups in order to get factions to help you make this INSANE attack on two infrastructure in a single ‘chapter’/‘day’.


    Probably some few ‘down and dirty’ rolls needed. Idk. Or maybe there’s “half-angel supernaturals”. Like a werewolf with angel-powers?


    Assuming everyone can get inside and destroy the linchpin of the power plant, time kinda ‘rewinds’ a bit. Maybe the fog gets sucked back into the structure and with it the various things that have occurred. People sacrificed to the infrastructure will remain dead of course, or maybe everyone will wake up like it was a very weird dream.

              I have two pieces of advice on this idea.
              First, create a degree of seperation between the plant and the fog. Instead of having it spew the fog from its smokestakes have it secretly dumping contaminants into a large swamp which then produces the fog or place natural caverns or abandoned mine tunnels underneath the area with polutants dumped within slowly seeping out through the ground. This slows any actaul action against the plant. Sure some people will immediately blame it but the powers that be will seek evidence before rocking that boat.
              As for the perfect circle idea, use local geography to control the fogs spread while hidding its origin. Fog settles in low areas so place your community in a valley or series of hollows.

              Second, as far as keeping people from fleeing the area, don't. Unless this goes on for years few people will abandon their homes and jobs to move away. Let life go on as normal for most people, at least for a while, they just have to look away occasionaly.
