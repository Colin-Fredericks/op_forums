    Originally posted by Ventrue Life View Post
    1. When a touchstone gets detached because of humanity loss, does the vampire still care about that touchstone? Would a vampire of humanity 4 still interact with the detached touchstones next to his humanity 6 and 5 dots,.or only his newest touchstone attached to his humanity 4 dot?
    Touchstones having the relation they do with detachment, I think it's a safe bet that a Touchstone ceasing to be attached just means the relationship is no longer strong enough to do more than justify the vampire's continued activity — they still care about them (they're still an Anchor), but they've stopped being able to draw upon that connection to reinforce their ability to keep up the practice of relating to humans as more than food.

    3. When my humanity 6 touchstone dies, do I have to replace it before I can buy new touchstones for my lower humanity dots? Why would a Kindred replace a detached touchstone (e.g. getting a new touchstone next to humanity 6 while his current humanity is 4) from a narrative perspective?
    The Touchstone Merit represents additional things that your character treats as Anchors and can use to vent troublesome frenzy-related Conditions — it doesn't matter that having dinner your high school sweetheart twice a month stopped helping you keep from backsliding into monstrosity years ago, she's still a confidante and a source of comfort when you actually spend that time with her.

        1. Yes. But from a mechanical standpoint they don't get any benefit for doing so. Narratively they may see each other just as frequently but the vampire doesn't quite view the relationship the same way. Or maybe they just distance themselves a bit more to avoid hurting the touchstone. Or maybe after some time and xp expenditure, the detached touchstone attaches at a lower Humanity level.

        2. Depends on the ST. It may invoke a Humanity check the same as an attached touchstone death or destruction. Would they care? Maybe? It depends on their relationship at the time. I tend to take a hands off approach in telling players how their character should feel and react unless it's a complete 180 or stupidly inappropriate.

        3. That's up to your ST how that plays out mechanically. (I recommend you have your character prepare for a stint in torpor unless you have a backup touchstone lined up to replace it.) I personally find the notion of vampires having more than two active touchstones unless they're family members to stretch credibility because of the time investiture required to maintain them plus hunting plus whatever vampire centric clan/city/covenant melodrama they're pursuing at the time.

            Originally posted by tsusasi View Post
            1. Yes. But from a mechanical standpoint they don't get any benefit for doing so.
            I mean, they do, it's just that the mechanical benefits are entirely separate from bolstering your Detachment rolls — you still get Willpower for fighting to keep up the relationship with them, they can still talk you down from frenzy, making meaningful connections with them still helps you resolve Conditions that mess with your ability to resist frenzy, and they still count as your Touchstones in all the ways your clan banes care about. They just aren't giving you a bonus die or two on a specific type of roll.

                        Maintaining ANY meaningful relationship takes time and work. Showing up to binge watch television isn’t meaningful. Add to the fact that most people are not up at all hours of the night, while vampires are only out at night. That means the vampire will never be there to pick them up after they blew a tire on the way to work, or meet for lunch, attend the kids’ soccer games etc. help maintain the lawn ... it can quickly become a one-sided relationship and when that happens the touchstone is going to find other things to do.
                        That’s up to you how much game time and how you want to devote to it but if my player are investing in the merit, that means game time is going to include it. I personally don’t use players’ touchstones as vengeance/victim levers unless they specifically ask or they target others’ touchstones. There’s allies, mentors, proteges, sires, ghouls contacts etc. for rivals to target in retaliation. I use touchstone interactions for when players ‘go solo’ or split up or along with hunting scenes. Sometimes a session will be nothing but the players interacting with their respective touchstones, or training or studying with a mentor or attending a clan or covenant meeting the others can’t attend.
