    Originally posted by Satchel View Post
    Slightly less boring answer: Mastigos at high Satiety, Thyrsus at low Satiety.


    To check my latest understanding of a particular set of Lair mechanics that I've been sporadically grappling with over the years:
    • Hold the Door operates under the assumption that the space you've used to open a Primordial Pathway is "an entrance to your Lair," and is the only means by which visitors to your Lair may receive the protections afforded to a guest (aside from Be My Guest, which works ).
    • Separate from this, anyone attempting to leave the while a Primordial Pathway is open finds themselves in the Beast's Lair, moving through one of their Burrows to another Chamber (presumably regardless of what the normal route to access that Burrow is within the open Chamber).
    • This isn't usually a huge problem because (besides the usual exercises of common sense that mean most Beasts aren't going to have unintended visitors slipping into their souls on a frequent basis) every Beast has the capacity to sustain at least two Chambers whose collapse does not end their lives, a single free pass through the front door doesn't make any other transition less difficult, Chambers are the Horror's favored hunting grounds (and the Horror is immune to its own Lair Traits and doesn't have to struggle to move between Chambers), and it's textual that most Beasts structure their Lairs in such a way that the Heart is the most distant Chamber from any entrances.
    • Be My Guest grants a character the ability to open Primordial Pathways as though they were the Beast whose permission they received, rolling Wits + Resolve along with any modifiers as normal.

    Does that sound about right?
    To be perfectly honest, I'm really not sure how Hold The Door and Be My Guest are supposed to interact with Primordial Pathways or the Lair.

    Re-reading Hold The Door, it seems like a valid interpretation that if a Beast opens a Primordial Pathway and people flee the area, then they don't run into the Lair unless the Beast explicitly Holds The Door for them to do it. That seems to counter what Primordial Pathways says, though, and I'm not sure that's the right interpretation.

Because I want my Beasts to be confronted eventually or when they're not being careful. I want that conflict. And that's why my answer of "Why?" might be different.

                                            Be My Guest allows the recipient to "enter the Lair just as the Beast does." The exact intent could be clearer, but I think the most natural reading is that it gives the person the ability to spend a willpower and roll Wits + Resolve +/- appropriate modifiers.

The main difference is that without Hold The Door they won't be protected from the dangers of the Lair and they can't leave by their own choosing.

                                                        Originally posted by Paradim View Post
                                                        Re-reading Hold The Door, it seems like a valid interpretation that if a Beast opens a Primordial Pathway and people flee the area, then they don't run into the Lair unless the Beast explicitly Holds The Door for them to do it.
                                                        My read of the two "hospitality" abilities is, as both nalak and Helms seem to agree, that their main function is to let you host people in the Lair without turning off deterrent Traits rather than to let them get into the Lair at all — it's textually not an impenetrable fortress, after all.

                                                        At the moment the thing I'm most uncertain of boils down to a combination of whether the Uninvited Guests text on how long it takes to get through a Burrow applies to people attempting to leave and whether a Burrow counts as a separate space for the purposes of being in the Chamber when the Pathway closes; presently my read is "yes" to both, because the default state of being stuck in a secret passage that doesn't exist until another Pathway opens or you luck into brute-forcing your way into the next Chamber feels both secure enough and low-key enough to be run with.

                                                        So trying to leave a Beast's hospital Chamber while a Primordial Pathway to it is open dumps you into the road Burrow linking it to their factory Chamber right away, but you can't proceed to the factory Chamber without spending the Willpower and beating the Horror's contested roll, which takes turns that may exceed the turns that the Pathway is open; once the Pathway's closed, you're on the road (unless you went back to the Chamber in time and "stayed" in the real hospital) and can either continue to try and force your way into the factory or go back to the hospital Chamber and contend with the dangers therein while you wait for the hospital to link back up with reality. Notably, as long as you're on the road it's consistently going to link the hospital and the factory in the way it was designed to do, which may force the issue for the Beast if their Lair can't kill you directly and the Horror somehow can't get at you in its own territory.
