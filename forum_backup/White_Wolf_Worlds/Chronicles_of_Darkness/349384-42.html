        I updated my sheet for Nyarlathotep. The Cipher is courtesy of Eremite.

        Virtue: Cunning
        Vice: Sadistic/Manipulative

        Incarnation: Messenger
        Agenda: Tempter/Inquisitor
        Catalyst: Causality

        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 8, Wits 6, Resolve 8
        Physical Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 5, Stamina 8
        Social Attributes: Presence 8, Manipulation 6, Composure 8

        Mental Skills: Academics (Psychology) 5, Crafts (Machines) 6, Computer 4, Medicine 3, Occult 6, Politics (Secret Societies) 4, Investigation 5, Science 6

        Physical Skills: Athletics 3, Brawl (No Mercy At All) 4, Drive 1, Firearms 2, Larceny 3, Stealth (Plain Sight) 4, Survival 2, Weaponry 3

        Social Skills: Animal Ken 2, Empathy (Body Language) 3, Expression (Speeches) 4, Intimidation (Sudden Seriousness) 7, Persuasion 6, Socialize 2, Streetwise 3, Subterfuge (Propaganda) 6

        Merits: Area Of Expertise (Psychology, Machines, Speeches), Common Sense, Encyclopedic Knowlegde (Crafts, Science, Occult), Indomitable (Epic), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Psychology, Machines), Professional Training 5 (Persuasion, Subterfuge, Occult), Tolerance For Biology, Trained Observer 3, Vice-Ridden (Advanced 3), Ambidextrous, Iron Stamina 3, Sleight Of Hand, Allies (Anything Needed), Closed Book 5, Contacts (Anything Needed), Fixer, Inspiring, Iron Will, Resources 5, Pusher, Status (Anything Needed), Striking Looks 2 (Sinister), Table Turner, Untouchable, Untouchable Style 4, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Killer Instinct 3 (Advanced 3), Light Weapons 4 (Natural Weaponry), Advanced Form, Advanced Self-Repair System 5, Bolthole (Anything Needed), Consummate Professional (Tempter, Inquisitor), Cultists 5, Efficient Dealer, Electromagnetic Linguistics, Ephemeral Integration 5, Lambda Form 4 + Truly Unchained, Living The Lie, Monkeywrencher, Multiple Agendas, Resonance Sensitivity, Suborned Facility (Anything Needed), Subborned Infrastructure 3, Supernatural Integration 5, Terrible Form 4, Transcend Incarnation (Analyst), Supernatural Infiltration 4, Versatile Transformation

        Embeds

        Vocal: All of them, maybe take out one or two

        Instrumental: Efficiency, Ellipses, Fungible Knowledge, High Of Birth, Open Sesame, Read Hostility, Right Tools Right Job, Wasted Time

        Cacophony: Cause And Effect, Cool Heads Prevail, Devil’s Advocate, Lucky Break, Just Bruised, On The Mend, Raw Materials, Shatter, Shifty Eyes, Special Someone, Tag & Release

        Mundane: Alibi, Associate And Integrate, Authorized, Blink Of The Eye, Deep Cover, Face Off, Going Native, Idle Conversation, Identity Theft, In My Pocket, Lost In The Crowd, Never Here, Persistent Legend, Quick Change, Without A Trace, You Can Tell Me

        Exploits

        Affliction, Behind The Curtain, Context Matters, Decoy, Deep Pockets, Demonic Might, Ephemeral Cover, Form Of The Beast, Hellfire, Hellhounds, Incendiary, Inflict Stigmata, Living Shadow, Rain Of Blood, Rip The Gates, Sermon, Show Of Power, Summon, Swarm, Terrible Avatar, Two Places At Once, Urban Legend, Visions Of Heaven And Hell

        Demonic Form

        Modifications: Claws And Fangs, Huge Size, Sonic Acuity, Tough As Stone

        Technologies: Aura Sight, Clairvoyant Sight, Glory And Terror, Mind Reading

        Propulsions: Phasing, Teleportation

        Processes: Amorphous, Cavernous Maw, Nanobot Composition, Wound Healing,

        Lambda Form: ST Fiat

        Defense: 9
        Initiative: 13
        Speed: 16
        Willpower: 10
        Health: 13
        Primum: 10
        Cipher
        A Thousand Masks

        The Crawling Chaos has many faces, and is not bound to only appear in one place at once. By not manifesting its true power through any of its Covers, Nyarlathotep’s masks can act independently. Attacking one of its masks tends to draw its full, terrible attention

        Prerequisites: Alibi and Persistent Legend

        Dice pool: Presence + Subterfuge

        Dramatic Failure: The Crawling Chaos’s attempt to split its Covers apart draws the attention of the God-Machine. Take the Flagged Condition.

        Failure: No effect. The demon may try again (suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt).

        Success: Nyarlathotep may manifest one of its Cover as separate entities. They can interact as normal. If the Crawling Chaos manifests Demonic Form abilities or any of its Covers switch into demonic form the waveform collapses and all the Covers fold back into the demon. By spending a point of Aether, another Cover can be manifested (no upper limit).
        These masks are advised to stay apart – in one another’s presence each exhibits glitches.
        Any damage to the Cover rating of any mask ends this power – that mask is considered Nyarlathotep’s true body, all other masks collapsing into it. The Wave Function Collapse Embed automatically deactivates this power in addition to any other effects, again collapsing all masks into the targeted body.

        Exceptional Success: All Aether costs are waived.

        Dreams Of What Is To Come

        Sometimes, Nyarlathotep leaves its victims with dreams of the nightmare future that is to come. These visions gradually erode their sanity and open them to the Crawling Chaos’ manipulation.

        Prerequisites: Persistent Legend and Recurring Hallucinations

        Dice pool: Manipulation + Persuasion - Composure

        Dramatic Failure: The target’s dreams are protected, somehow, perhaps due to their strong will or some strange benefactor. They are immune to this Interlock for a lunar month.

        Failure: No effect. The demon may try again (suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt).

        Success: The power affects targets for a number of days equal to Crawling Chaos’ Primum. The target no longer recovers Willpower when sleeping. The glimpses of madness each night cause a Breaking Point penalised by the number of successes on the demon’s activation roll.

        Exceptional Success: The target’s will is bent to serve Nyarlathotep. The demon may impose the Obsession Condition on the target, setting whatever vile task it wishes as their Obsession. They must spend a point of Willpower each scene to resist their alien compulsions.

        Behind The Mask

        The Crawling Chaos is merely the herald of incomprehensible forces. A glimpse of its true nature can shred a person’s sanity. Simply looking upon any of its masks can, if Nyarlathotep wishes, shatter a mind.

        Prerequisites: Recurring Hallucinations and Sum of all Fears

        Dice pool: Presence + Intimidation - Composure

        Dramatic Failure: The Crawling Chaos creates an imperfect vision of horror that only strengthens its target’s will. They receive the Steadfast Condition.

        Failure: No effect. The demon may try again (suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt).

        Success: The target takes the Broken Condition and rolls for a Breaking Point penalised by the number of successes on the demon’s activation roll.

        Exceptional Success: In addition to the above, the target takes the Madness Condition.

                            Been a while since I have done one of these.
                            Blade
                            Virtue: Justice; Blade is driven to stop the undead so they can no longer harm mortal society.
                            Vice: Aloof; Blade is a chronic loner, and is frequently cold, surly and distrusting – even to proven allies.

                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                            Physical Attributes: Strength 5[8], Dexterity 4, Stamina 4[6]
                            Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 2, Composure 4
                            Skills: Academics 2, Athletics 4, Brawl 5, Crafts 2(Weaponry), Drive 2, Empathy 2 (Detect Lies), Firearms 3, Investigation 3 (Vampires), Intimidation 4, Larceny 3, Medicine 2 (Blood), Occult 2, Science 2, Stealth 4, Streetwise 4 (Black Markets), Survival 2 (Urban), Weaponry 5 (Blades)
                            Skill Tricks: Athletics (Deadly Accuracy, Size Up), Stealth (Know the Shadows), Weaponry (Cripple, Wearing Weapons)

                            Merits: Air of Menace*,Allies 3 (Nightstalkers), Ambidextrous, Anonymity 2,Area of Expertise (blades, detect lies, vampires), Contacts 2 (Medical, Hunters), Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Weaponry), Encyclopedic Knowledge (vampires), Esoteric Armory 3, Fast Reflexes 2, Fixer, Hardy 2, Indomitable, Interdisciplinary Specialty (vampires), Iron Skin 2, Iron Stamina 2, Iron Will, Language (English; French, Spanish, Russian, likely others), Meditative Mind 2, Quick Draw (Blades), Resources 2, Safe Place 2, Scarred*, Striking Looks 1 (Bad Ass), Style: Armed Defense 5, Style: Close Quarters Combat 4, Style: Heavy Weapons 5, Style: Light Weapons 3, Style: Martial Arts 4, Style: Thrown Weapons 2*, Style: Unarmed Defense 4, Supernatural Resistance 3*, Trained Observer 3, Unseen Sense (Vampires)
                            *From Hurt Locker

                            Integrity: 5
                            Willpower: 8
                            Blood Potency: 0
                            Size: 5
                            Initiative: 10
                            Armor: Kevlar Vest (1/3), Iron Skin (1bashing)
                            Defense: 9 [11 Celerity]
                            Speed: 17 [51 Celerity]
                            Health: 9 [11 Resilience]
                            Disciplines: Auspex 1, Celerity 2, Resilience 2, Vigor 3

                            Special Template – Daywalker
                            Blade is a “Daywalker” – exposed to the Vampire’s curse at birth, he has many of the strengths of the undead without suffering their flaws. Like a member of a Ghoul Family, Daywalkers age slower than a normal human, gain the Unseen Sense (Vampires) Merit, and retain the use of their supernatural powers even without any vitae in his body (and may activate Disciplines using Willpower if they so choose).Daywalkers naturally generate vitae at a rate of 1point every 3 days. In addition, a Daywalker can ingest human blood and turn it into Vitae (every 3 points ingested become one point of Vitae). Despite retaining their powers Daywalkers still gain the Deprived Condition when their Vitae pool is empty and gain the Bestial Condition as well. In all other respects a Daywalker is treated mechanically as a Ghoul. A Daywalker’s vitae is sterile, and cannot be used to embrace, ghoul, form vinculum or cause blood addiction.

                            Equipment: Blade employs an arsenal of weapons in his hunt. His standard gear consists of a sword (Daywalker’s Sword), 2 heavy pistols, and assorted knives, throwing blades and wooden stakes. He also carries any number of unique and specialized weapons as the situation may demand. For protection he sports a Kevlar vest that protects the chest, back, abdomen and groin as well as a long leather trench coat.

                            Daywalkers Sword ●●
                            The Daywalkers Sword is an acid etched titanium ninjato style sword. It is laced with silver and has been consecrated to deal deadly wounds to supernatural creatures.
                            Damage: +3 Initiative: -3 Strength: 2 Size: 3 Durability: 4(6) Availability: 5
                            Powers
                            Damage dealt by the Daywalkers Sword is considered supernatural damage.
                            Trapped Grip: The grip of the sword is set with a trap designed to maul the unwary. The trap may be armed or deactivated with a simple switch. While the weapon is armed, anyone that grips the hilt makes a Wit+ Larceny roll at a -2 penalty. If successful they can disarm the trap or discard the sword. Failing the roll inflicts 2 points of lethal damage and the Arm Wrack tilt.

                            Notes: This version of Blade is a blend of the character as depicted in the films, with some of the comic’s version and VtR style vampire mechanics.

                            The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
                            Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
                            The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
                            Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
                            Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities
