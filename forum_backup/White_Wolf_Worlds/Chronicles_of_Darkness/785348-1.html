    Originally posted by Ephsy View Post
    It's not a question about how useful it is: It's a question about if they have the right to post that.
    And I'm most certain they don't.
    And I dont disagree on that point. Never argued that I did.

    I would argue that this is comparable to music downloads or film downloads or heck, even rpg pdf downloads. A new and better way of doing things have been created in the digital format and the inherant pressure of the user base will push to fulfil that need.

    Tv and films? Netflix, Amazon, iPlayer
    music? iTunes, Spotify, pandora
    rpgs? DrivethruRPG
    books? Amazon
    Stuff? Amazon

    Barnes and noble, blockbuster, Boarders and dozens of other big names failed to change their business models. TV, film, music, books all experienced heavy piracy not because people are inherently thieving meanies but because digital solutions were better than traditional ones. I would argue that assuming subnet has been taken down, other resources will take its place, probably more direct PDF downloads. anyone can download the entire white wolf/onyx path back catalogue without repercussion with a half dozen clicks.

    If I were so bold as to offer a solution; onyx path should go to a cloud based subscription model. Integral search functionality, hyperlinks, cross referencing capabilities, additional audio-visual content, integrate blogs, forums and periodicals. these things would add value and allow for greater creator content to be shared. you wouldnt have to give up your dead tree format or even your pdfs if you didnt want to you could certainly still buy them, but they wouldnt be the core of the model. It would result in lower entry cost and I belive would even increase revenues.

                It would be hard to divvy up the cash and gauge interest, considering most of the employees are developers and freelancers. Some of the freelancers don't mind doing stuff for free, some are adamantly against it. If you subscribe to 'Chronicles of Darkness' and they start putting up, say, Promethean and Geist stuff when you just subscribed for Vampire and Mage, you're not getting your money's worth. Plus it looks as if old World of Darkness gamelines will be taken care of by Paradox's new White Wolf, not Onyx Path.

                I suggested a gameline-oriented Patreon, where fans could subscribe to their gameline, and get weekly or monthly articles, perhaps have a voting system, where they could vote for z-splats and NPCs or new powers and other things that wouldn't necessarily fit into a book. Every year or so you could even compile all the articles into a book for those who didn't subscribe or want things in hardcopy.

                All that said, while game mechanics can't be protected by copyright, the fluff can and is. That's why the canon index is more or less okay, it offers page numbers and book references and an outline or occasionally dicepools, without just copying the book and pasting it on a website.

                The subnet is the one with the issue of not changing their model. They should be contacting the owners of the texts they want to plaster all over their website for rights, or toning down the information given.
