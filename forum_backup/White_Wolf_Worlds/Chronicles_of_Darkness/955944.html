    Flash, Glitter and Scream - What are the most impressive powers?

    08-22-2016, 06:53 PM
    ...I should probably apologize in advance for the title, shouldn't I? Anyway, though the Chronicles of Darkness go out of their way to avoid allowing for the "monstrous superhero" playstyle associated with the World of Darkness, it's not as if every supernatural power is a something that can be easily explained as smoke, mirrors, sleight of hand and tricks of the light. But, I recently found myself curious about just how flashy the supernaturals of the Chronicles can be when they really cut loose - the sorts of things that they can do which can spawn an "oh crap!" from the mortals across the room.

    So, being that my own collection is rather sporadic, I thought I'd turn to the forum for assistance. I want your opinions on the flashiest, lethalist, most grandiose and potentially nightmarish displays of occult power and supernatural might that exist in the arsenals of the player splats of the Chronicles of Darkness.

    Just to be clear, I'm not looking to get into any sort of "whose powers are better" argument here. Everyone knows that there's a difference between a vampire using Protean 5 to become a nightmarish inhuman predator and a Mummy using Tier 3 of... whatever Utterance that is which calls down a meteor strike. But both are suitably crap-your-pants scary that I'd consider them perfect examples of "flashy powers" for the sake of this topic.

      With the Gift of the Elementals, a werewolf can expand the area of effect of their Influence to a radius of miles equal to twice their Glory. So with just the Gift of the Elementals you can create fire, water, earth or air within that area and cause it to do, basically, whatever you want through the use of Manipulation, allowing for earthquakes and tornadoes.

      However, fetishes exist as an open-ended method of altering Gift Facets. So with the right fetish you can mix them together and create bizarre things, like mixing Influences of Air and Sharks to create sharknados or Fire and Spiders to create Lavalantulas. Or go conceptual and create a Silencequake or have it rain sunlight.

      EDIT: Had to go when first posting.

      One of my favorites and certainly obvious, though arguably no more obvious than a werewolf just changing into a werewolf, is the Temple of Apollo and their ability to, when on a sacred hunt, become supercharged against certain prey and have Apollo's sunlight pour from their eyes and mouth. Which is the type of eerily horrifying imagery I find all too rare.

                  Honestly, almost every Tier 3 Utterance is great for just showing off. The most useless Utterance (Waters of Life and Death), the only one my players and I could see no reason to take, still lets you turn all water in the area into blood or even just part a body of water for your Moses impression (complete with Burning Bush courtesy of Power of Re).

                  When it comes to flashy though, I don't think much tops Secrets Ripped From Skies as even the OP has pointed out. My personal favourite runner up is one of the "Words of Dead something" Utterances (Words of Dead Hunger?) which summons something that is a shoggoth in what is basically all but name.

                  If anything were to top these Utterances though, I would say it would be a five dot Seba. Those that exist are of truly cosmic significance. If one can wake every Mummy in the world at once at Sekhem 10, how spectacular a display could something of similar power conjure up were it to have a more directly visual impact?
