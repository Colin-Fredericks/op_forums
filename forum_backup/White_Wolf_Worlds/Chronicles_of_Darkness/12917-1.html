                                                        I'was actually working out on this system of courts, but i'm a little bit stuck in fleshing out holydays and more of theirs philosophy.
                                                        (And a better english translation, possibly.)

                                                        In Kansas, the Freehold of the Yellow Smile holds the reins of a famous fast-food franchise.
                                                        The courts are modeled after food, but some believe there is a hidden layer of truth - ancient pacts which have been re-modernized after these symbols.

                                                        The main difference between "temporal" (day / night and seasonal) freeholds and the freehold of the Yellow Smile is that the power does not pass from court to court, but it is firmly held by one, dingle court.

                                                        On the other hand, all the changelings change periodically court - at the beginning they enroll in the freehold as Coke courtiers, then Fries courtier, then Burger. Afterwards, when the Burger courtier Breaks the oath of truthfulness (see later), they find again themselves in the Coke court.

                                                        Some courtiers prefer to remain for a longer time in the same court, but everyone is expected to change mantle, sooner or later.
                                                        Even if, for someone, the "later" still has to come.


                                                        The court of Burger
                                                        The transubstantiated logo, The Heart Court.

                                                        Emotion: Pride.
                                                        Nickname: Ketchup-mantled , Branded Messiah , Phoenixes

                                                        The courtiers of Burger feels to be the rightful Kings (terrible pun intended) of the Yellow Smile freehold. They are the essence of the brand which is made flesh, material, and coated with sauce to be glorified in their incarnation.

                                                        Their philosophy is that, after the discover of their true self during their exploration as Friers, they have to reveal it with no refrain, even if it may seem terrible to themselves or others. The time of research is now finished, they have to front with courage and honesty what lies inside themselves, independently of its goodness.

                                                        And, on the same time, because of their imposed sincerity, they have to drive the freehold to fabulous deeds in the mortal and faerie world: even if not leaders, they have to set the foundations for the freehold's tomorrow.

                                                        All the members of the burger court have to take the oath to never lie (to others - and expecially themselves), or to be banned from the Burger court. The Oath is not codified - the precise wording is usually chosen from the applicant himself, in order to prove his capability to "express" himself in the world.

                                                        Because of their forced sincerity, Burger courtiers are assumed as true chiefs of the freehold, and is expected form them to guide the other changelings to epic deeds in the faeric and mortal world. Burger courtesans have to set (or shift) the foundations of the Yellow smile, even in roles different from leadership, as with constructive criticism (it is known that they CAN NOT lie), planning buildings for the future, or every other long term project they feel they have to complete.

                                                        Life as phoenix is stressful and hard, and many changeling voluntarily break their sincerity oath, or remain for a long time in the Fries Court in order to find the resolve necessary to become a Phoenix.

                                                        Heraldry: bodily humors, visible stains, spices and strong smells, aureolas, round wafers, crowns.

                                                        Concepts: grandma with no shame about intimate details of her life, valorous knight of carnal abandon, illuminate dreamer-tyrant, politician with no secret about his faults, notekeeper of truths, builder of new franchising, mystical logo designer, quirky teacher.with obscene language.

                                                        Mantle: the courtiers of Burger often have subtle mantles, which reveal the metaphorical truth of the courtier itself - in particular, their vice - as if this were something glorious and sublime. Weak mantles mantle reveals as a smell or a tactile emotion, as the odor of sweat or sex, the sensation of a perfect skin, the taste of blood in the mouth. Strong mantles makes the courtier appear as a saint or a god, with fiery halos and barbecue stigmata.
                                                        At the first dot of mantle, the sincerity emanated from the Ketchup-mantled is felt strongly in his words, giving him a +1 on Persuasion rolls.
                                                        The third dot strengthens the force of his leadership, giving him the 9-again rule when he is the main actor of a teamwork roll.
                                                        At the fifth dot, the Branded Messiah gains mystical power from his complete transparency: once per session, when indulging in his Vice in public, he refills completely his Glamour pool in addition to gain a willpower point.


                                                        The court of Fries

                                                        (The Soul Oil, The Golden Court)

                                                        Emotion: challenge.
                                                        Nicknames : Kristòs, Friars, Doves

                                                        As the potatoes are just pallid matter grown in the earth with no sight of the light, and then purified and fried in the fire of oil, and through this terrible ordeal, transformed in edible gold…

                                                        …so the courtiers of Fries believe that, after having abandoned what was useless through their permanence in the court of coke, now, enduring hardship, they can find their true self, their place in the world and the house they have to build to themselves.

                                                        During this period, the Friers are encouraged to share experiences with others of their Court, and outside it, asking for teachings, exploring the edge, and, in general, try new things in their life.

                                                        After an initial period of accustoming, they're, then, asked to step outer and outer from thir security, in order to hone their skills.

                                                        (OK. HERE I NEED A LITTLE MORE FLUFF. IDEAS?)

                                                        Heraldry: light, thorns, white or golden color, horizon, the dawn, tools for sculpting, friers, knifes.

                                                        Concepts: Philosophic rake, scientist with attention disorder, social hedge backpacker, third-time Frier that doesn't sincerely know what to try next.

                                                        Mantle: the mantle of the Friers is deep with possibility, exaltation and discovery just out a step from your comfort zone. A weak mantle may be made of whispers between a word and the other, objects may seem harder or assume a polished sheen. A high mantle is changing, and well manifest, giving the Dove a "hardened" appearance.
                                                        At the first dot of mantle, the Friar becomes adamantine in her purpose, gaining a +1 bonus on all resolve rolls.
                                                        The third dot widens the horizon of possibilities, canceling a point of penalty for the use of untrained skills (so, in general, no penalty for untrained physical or social, -2 penalty for untrained mental skills.)
                                                        At the fifth dot, the Kristòs takes force from the hardships she endures - spending a point of willpower grants a +4 bonus on rolls instead than a +3.

                                                        The Court of Coke
                                                        The Fizzing Nepenthe, The Sour-Sweet Court

                                                        Emotion: Reproach
                                                        Nicknames: Bubbleheads, Past-solvers, Crows

                                                        The court of Coke happily greets all the changelings coming out from the edge. The teaching of the coke Courtiers is the following - as the coke melts the food in your stomach, allowing you to take the only the essential nutrients and eliminating the waste; to decide your future, to discover your true self, you have to know your past, and only keep what is essential to you.
                                                        The courtiers of Coke are encouraged to observe their fetch, try to take again posses of their past lives, greet their old friends as a new person and remembering the beautiful and horrible things of their mortal life. Those who comes back here after a permanence in the Burger Court are encouraged to think about the effects of their actions, the improvements they created for the society and the material bonds attached to themselves. And then, destroying or abandoning forever everything they do not feel anymore to be useful, so that this can not be anymore a distraction - or used agains them.

                                                        Obvious to say, sometimes a Courtier makes a big mistake, and cancels or abandons something she did not really meant to, then tries to repair the damages caused by this choiche. The Court smiles quietly - it is the absence of something which weights its importance.

                                                        Often, courtiers of Coke try to shepherd humans or others of their Court in this process, but never allowing themselves to take decision for them - strange dichotomy indeed, given that the killings and arsons and farewell used by the courtiers of Coke to cancel their past have often strong repercussion on others.

                                                        The Murder:
                                                        Once a Crow feels that she has no more useless bound to detach herself from, it is customary to gather the other Crows and announce her intention of passing on, to the Golden Court.
                                                        The other members, then, come one at the time nearby and, if they think she's ready, wish her some words of advice and farewell. Otherwise, they stab her : usually it is just a touch of the edge of the blade, but it is not unheard of a person dying because of a failed attempt of "Murder."
                                                        At the end of the Murder, it is left to the Crow herself and to her scars to decide whether she's really ready to pass on.

                                                        Symbols: trash bins, broken mirrors, closets, fires, broken chains, poppies, the color black , acid.

                                                        Concept: Broker of memories, gentle divorce-specialist lawyer, Human resources manager which hires and fires her old friends, classy scrap-merchant.

                                                        Mantle: the mantle of Coke is at the same time exciting and soothing, as the noise of childhood friends, flickers of black and white photos, a smell of industrial acid.
                                                        Stronger mantles of Coke seems to impose themselves as a black coat around the scene, making some details shill and canceling the rest.

                                                        At the first dot, the Past-Solver can take on the force of his memories: once per scene, if she has no point of willpower, she can gain one.
                                                        At the third dot, her capacity to discern between important and useless things make her gain to spend, once per scene, a point of glamour in order to re-roll a perception roll.
                                                        At the fifth dot, the wyrd gives back payment for everything to which the Crow renounces: once per story she can renounce to all the dots in a merit in order to take another merit with equal or lower cost.
                                                        (The renounce has to be as concrete as possible: burning your Library, breaking a friendship with your Ally, giving your Resources to the poors)
