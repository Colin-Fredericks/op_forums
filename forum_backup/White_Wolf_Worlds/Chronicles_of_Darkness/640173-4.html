    Idea for Settings:

    A "Middle/High School" that's actually in the Hedge and all the characters are locked into their behaviors by the Headmaster/Mistress (who would also be one of the potential Romances and an Antagonist) who is a True Fae that has Kidnapped and manipulated all the Supernaturals into a Game.... If they can find their true Love they can go free with the True Love. ALL the other characters (Teachers, Pupils etc) that are not one of the Romancable routes are Either Changelings who have Failed to break out (who may or may not have been in the position of the Protagonist previously) or Fae "Actors" cast as the uninteresting parts.

    A Winter Retreat for the various Supernaturals that is ran by a Neutral 3rd party, That arranges for the various needs of the Different Supernaturals (Arranging a New Mystery every Year, Ensuring there are enough Blood dolls to go around Etc) and tries to keep the Violence down to a Minimum. (The Owner is likely a Beast and has several Other Begotten on Staff to help with the Management.) Occasionally they deal with Special requests and/or Deals/Bets between the various Supernaturals, these in the past have included "Arranging" a breakdown and Blizzard to strand a Family in the Resort and Forcing them to Ally with the Supernatural Elements at the Resort so they can survive. The object of these Bets usually are let into the Bet after the bet has been won and/or they have became one of the Supernaturals. (Not all the Factions/Splats can forcibly transform people, but that just means the bet is Over if they Willingly Join that faction for protection. Atleast one of the Staff is a Mage with the Mind Arcana and can erase the memories of those involved if that is necessary)

    Light and Dark are two Sides of a Coin... Humanity is What Happens when it Lands on it's Edge.

                        Originally posted by ArcaneArts View Post
                        What sort of romance scenarios would you actually want to see in the Chronicles of Darkness?
                        In the game I'm currently running, one of the players has a pretty interesting love story going on with an NPC that I think fits CofD very well.

                        He met the girl a couple of weeks before the story started, and they hit it off super well. He was a socially awkward rookie at the police academy and she was an amateur singer with tons of charisma. They bonded over their love for cheesy action flicks. One week before the story started, she left him a note, asking him to meet her by the old oak tree (it was a meaningful landmark in their relationship). when he arrived, he saw her waiting for him, worried. Before she could say anything, long, gnarled hands grabbed her and dragged her deep into the woods.

                        Now, here is where Fate was altered. Normally, he would've chased after her, both shouting each other's name. She would've been taken to Arcadia, and he would've become a Fae-Touched Hunter, specialised in Fae lore and always holding out hope that he'd find her, or that she'd return.

                        But his Fate was tampered with. He was marked for death in a ritual, and one of the old trees splintered when he was running past it, dropping a heavy branch on his head and killing him instantly. When he returned as one of the Bound, he was amnesic. He had her note, but he didn't remember her at all. The Keeper that took the girl left a Fetch behind. Every Fetch has a flaw, something they're missing. The flaw on the girl's Fetch is that she doesn't remember him either.

                        Now, as he struggles to piece his past together and solve the mysteries left behind by the person who marked him for Geist-dom, he has to deal with the outcome of the events that transpired that day. Neither of them are mortal any longer.

                        I look forward to seeing what happens with that relationship, personally.

                        My homebrew hub.

                                        I always liked werewolf and vampires. They are the two big 'classic' monsters, and both are likely to run into one another. While they're just stereotypes, the vampire tends to be seen as more cool, what with being undead, with the werewolf more hot-blooded, what with animalistic passion. Then there's the fact that they can, basically, keep pumping out Vitae if they need, and at least in first edition werewolf blood sort of inflamed the vampire's passions, all leading to an interesting dynamic, especially when factoring in the individual's taboos and concerns. That pairing has probably popped up the most in our games outside of 'supernatural x mortal' pairings.
