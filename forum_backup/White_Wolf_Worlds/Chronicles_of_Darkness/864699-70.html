    I generally ask players for reasons why their character would have a stat or skill to an exceptionally high level, particularly if it doesn't match their stated concept.

    That said, there's no reason not to let characters be good at what their player wants to be good at. It helps you to know what sort of content they want to engage in, and if you want to challenge them, you can challenge them from an angle they aren't so good at. There is a lingering sentiment among some STs that players should be knocked down a peg or not be allowed to be exceptional at things. There's no need for this, for the reasons listed above.

                                                    Originally posted by KieranMullen View Post
                                                    Moreover, you can't let them know if they set off the ward. That's meta-knowledge.
                                                    This is a system where circumstances can allow you to choose to deliberately fail rolls or do inadvisable stuff and the base system lets you make regular failures worse for you, and both of these things are rewarded with character-development resources.

                                                    There are systems where player knowledge and character knowledge are expected to maintain parity, but ChroD is mostly not one of them. Talk to your players.
