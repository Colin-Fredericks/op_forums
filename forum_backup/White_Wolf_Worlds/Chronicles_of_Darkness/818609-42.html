    Originally posted by malonkey1 View Post

    I think we may want to move away from failure in a direct sense. I think &quot;Gone Horribly Right&quot; is a much more interesting angle to take, and there are already plenty of ways for the Genii to screw themselves (Havoc and Jabir, for example).

    The Integrity replacement I've been working on is Restraint, representing how well the Genius can control themselves and their Inspiration, as well as their general ability to fit in. At low levels, you have your Dr. Venture and your Victor Von Dooms, at high levels, you have your Lex Luthor and your Elon Musk-types. Renegades at the low end are more proactive, but have a harder time fitting in and are more likely to Illuminate. Paragons at the high end are more restrained and can hide their Inspiration better, but tend to choke up when shit gets real, and at extremely high Restraint, become nearly suicidally (or Genius-cidally) aware of how dangerous Inspiration is.

    Guys in the middle are good at laying low (they're not blowing up banks with plasma cannons, but they're also not building utopic arcology projects in downtown Detroit).

    Being at any point on the bar can have advantages and drawbacks.

                            Fair enough, but before I slink back off to the Bolthole let me make one last sales pitch for Transgression.

                            With Transgression, the player's actions and consiquences become the forefront of Genius' Integrity system. An Inspired's Transgression would rise without roll every time they cross a greater threshold between what is possible and shouldn't be possible, and all instances that the line is crossed accidentally or unintentionally would result in a threshold roll. Rising in Transgression would give scientific beats (if you folks are still incorporating something like arcane beats) and bestow conditions relative to the consiquences of the Genii's Transgression (becoming noticed by Hunters or the God Machine, creating time paradoxes, instilling a panic amongst the city's residents, even Unmada.) Which in turn generates more beats and incentivizes the Genii to Transgress further until they run the risk of Transgressing so far that they either make a powerful enough enemy to destroy themselves, have a large enough miscalculation to destroy themselves (and maybe erase a city from known history), or even most horrifying of all...actually succeed in their wildest dreams.

                            If nothing else at least make certain actions and character decisions automatic Restraint decreases. That way it truly feels like the gauge is an honest representation of just how far a Genius is willing to go for their Vision.

                            That is all I have for now.

                                                The biggest elephant in the room when it comes to genius, at least for me, has always been how their superscience hasn't changed the world or how they manage to remain hidden from the rest of society.
                                                Restraint is a very good way to explain why they censor themselves and try not to draw attention, I love it, but even then I think we need more. It doesn't need to be one thing, it can be a combination of factors on top of restraint. Maybe society rejects their discoveries because it's no ready for them, maybe there are powerful people who don't want society to change, etc...
                                                Even if their wonders can't be reproduced or tend to be very unstable, just the fact that they're technological devices will make regular scientists try to reproduce their effects somehow, and that pushes technological development, even if it's just a little bit. A dedicated scientist doesn't need to understand it, just knowing that it's possible will make him try and push forward. In a certain way it's the whole point of being a genius, not only are they inspired, their mere existence inspires people around them.
                                                This are just some general observations, although I haven't had time to go through all the posts I like the changes that are being introduced, I just would like a bit more development in these two areas of the setting, that's all. Good luck with it.

                                                        Edit: DArchon5 Yeah, we were going to have that. You can reverse-engineer Wonders, Manes, and paranormal phenomena to open up new Fields and act as Theses for increasing Inspiration or Axiom ratings.

                                                        The "masquerade" is a combination of:
                                                        • Havoc breaking Wonders
                                                        • Many Wonders being impossible for most people to replicate, and those that can replicate it are either Edisons* or working with a Wonder that's within the realm of mortal theoretical knowledge anyway.
                                                        • The Peerage and Lemuria keeping things quiet for their respective reasons ("mankind's not ready" and "We're called secret masters for a reason, guys!")
                                                        • the Restraint of many Genii
                                                        • People being willing to rationalize things away, like with a lot of the weird stuff
                                                        • The sheer expense of a lot of bigger projects makes them rare and slow-going
                                                        • and finally, Genii voluntarily hiding for their own benefit.

                                                        There's not a lot enforcing the masquerade unless you want it to. I wouldn't be surprised to see a few Genii that have to act as cleaners, mopping up the nonsense other Genii leave and possibly removing Genii who get too loud or rowdy. If a person sees a giant mecha in the streets, that might out a specific mad scientist, who might have to answer to the Cleaners, the Peerage or Lemuria at large, their close colleagues who aren't interested in being outed, as well as Edisons* who might want to get their hands on a mech to sell to the military.

                                                        *As a side note, Edisons are intended to be sort of semi-Inspired, analogous to Wolf-Blooded, Proximi or Fae-Touched. Their deal is that they have a limited capacity to store and use Mania, and can reproduce Wonders that they've analyzed, but they can't make their own and depend on Genii for what they do. On the flip side, they're much more magnetic and able to fit in socially, making them a tempting proxy for Genii who want to make a buck off their Wonders. However, if you know the story of Tesla and Edison, you'll probably know why working with an Edison might not be a great idea.
