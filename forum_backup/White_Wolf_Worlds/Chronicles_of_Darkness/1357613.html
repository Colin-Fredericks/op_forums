    [VtR 2e / V5] Hunger Dice and Rouse in VtR

    12-29-2019, 06:18 PM
    Hey all!

    I'm looking to mix things up for my players and how they relate to Blood in VtR, so I'm interested in trying to import the Hunger mechanics from V5. I like the lack of certainty the Hunger mechanic provides concerning how full you are and how quickly the Hunger will grow and threaten to overtake you or taint your actions.

    However, as usual I'm worried about some of the differences between editions and the impact that can have on the systems, especially the TN differences.

    I want to tie blood power growth to Potency. Right now I'm considering this:
    Blood Potency
    (Rouse / Turn)
    Rouse Dice
    Feeding
    1
    1
    		 Blood Bags, Animals, Humans
    2
    2
    		 Animals, Humans
    3
    2
    		 Humans
    4
    3
    		 Humans slake 1 less Hunger
    5
    3
    		 Must drain & kill a human to reduce Hunger < 2
    6 4 Humans slake 2 less Hunger
    etc. etc. etc.
    My reasoning is as follows:
    • V5 scales healing ability and blood surge with Potency. I think that's a bit too much for me to convert across editions, and I prefer VtR's simpler way of keeping the ability static but increasing how much blood you can spend per turn. I think in V5 you can Rouse as much as you want per turn, whereas here I'm tying how much you can Rouse per turn to BP, just as it was in the old Blood Pool system.
    • Since there's no blood pool, I need a way to simulate the greater pool or efficacy of the blood as Potency rises. I think the best way to do that is to increase the Rouse Dice so they have a greater chance of not increasing their Hunger when they rouse the blood. If I did this right, at low BP they'll become hungry a little more frequently than one Rouse die in V5, then at 2 and 3 they'll match one Rouse die in V5, then at 4 and 5 they'll be ~10% under the equivalent of two TN6 Rouse Dice.
    • Getting more Rouse dice in V5 is tied to BP and only really for Disciplines at certain levels. Given the differences in the systems, and not wanting to overly track things like that, I broadened it to all Rouse checks, hoping this will help offset some of the percentage differences due to TN.
    • I could keep extrapolating up to BP10, but it's not likely we'd ever reach those heights in game. In fact, I prefer a world where BP is capped at 5, with 6 reserved for diablerists only.
    • Attribute/Skill max scaling is the same. It's just not really relevant to this discussion.
    • When do Vampires have to feed from other Vamps. I've mulled it over, and I actually prefer V5's scale. It keeps the focus on the Vamp/Human dynamic. There's nothing STOPPING an Elder, or anyone, feeding on Kindred. In fact, the option becomes all the more appealing as it becomes less and less satisfying to feed on Humans, which I think increases the horror because it remains a choice made to sate the Hunger, rather than a necessity.
    Any help is appreciated in refining this. Thanks!
    *edit* Revision 2


    Ok, so after much hemming and hawing, I think I am going to try to run with this. I've made some adjustments to the scaling to ease the ramp up a bit, making it easier and more flexible at the lower levels, and increasingly more demanding to sate the Hunger as BP goes up (they can feed down to Hunger 0, for example, up until BP 3.)

    As for BP 6, this is a personal preference for me. Anyone else would be welcome to continue to scale this up as they like, but I prefer a scale of 5, with 6 being the special "Further Beyond" tier.

    I won't be using Messy Crits or Bestial Failures per se. This part is less nailed down right now, but I'll be looking to Atrocity Dice penalties as a guide as suggested by @Draconis.

    I'm pretty happy with how it turned out. ^_^ Hope it's fun for people!

          V5's hunger system definitely is interesting! I liked how visceral and unpredictable it made the moment-to-moment existence of a vampire seem. You could be riding high, totally fine, and then five minutes and a few bad rolls later, you're starving and having to decide between whatever your goal was and tamping down the Beast.

If you're on the ball, there's little difference, but get distracted and it's suddenly a..."Do I feel lucky, punk?" situation.

                              Hello.
                              We use V5´s Hunger System successfully now since almost two years in a weekly game.
                              I would not limit rouse checks per turn. Just the action economy will limit your players enough.
                              I did this too in the beginning and it´s not very intuitive either for players.
                              Also - if they want to burn up their hunger in one turn: Let them! It feels good for players to maybe go "All out".

                              Furthermore, let them record their own Hunger, it will still feel visceral and unpredictable to them. Trust me. All my players get really itchy when their vamps reach Hunger 4.


                              We use the Mending and Rolling Rousecheck with Advantage for disciplines as dictated by Blood Potency in V5.

                              Here is my oldcomment in a tread of using hunger:
                              Hunger pool instead of hunger dice - Onyx Path Forums
                              http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/the-classic-world-of-darkness/vampire-the-masquerade/1256266-hunger-pool-instead-of-hunger-dice
                              I don´t like Hunger Dice Mechanic for me They aren´t giving us nothing new (Hunger frenzy was always a thing and the mechanic was better.) They are also a


                              "I´m telling a story for 4 freshly embraced licks in a Requiem game WITH V5 Hunger mechanics. (When it says in Requiem to use a point of Vitae, then it´s a Rouse Check. It´s an easy hack and worked fine.)
                              After roughly about 100+ hours of gaming it I came to certain conclusions regarding V5 Hunger.

                              - It makes both as a player and ST the game a lot more random(fun! ^__^): The freshly embraced Ventrue clinged hard to his Humanity and declined drinking ANY blood, but was successful with EVERY rouse check after each day - which fed storywise wonderfully into his denial of his new existence! Meanwhile the Gangrel who used heavily Protean killed already a human in Hungerfrenzy and was almost always on the Edge due to almost always failing her rouse checks.
                              - Hungerdice is both heaven and hell as a mechanic in the dicepool, especially for me as a ST.
                              - Hunting for blood becomes way more prominent in game with V5 hunger mechanics. A vampire with Hunger 2 can quickly reach Hunger 5 by only 3 times of rousing the blood. My players get quite anxious to hunt as quickly before they reach hunger 5. Also reaching Hunger 0 is a sweet spot. Not only widens it the possible use of Blood by 20% percent(Which is a lot!) But I also homeruled that a Vampire with hunger 0 get´s +2 to resist Frenzies and +1 to rouse-the-blood-checks, also social dice penalitiey vs. mortals are reduced by 1.
                              2 of my players like that the other 2 don´t. - in the future I plan to use Geraukis "Between the Scenes" game hack to roll Hunting more into a specialized scene and into between-sessions-time.
                              - Hunger is huge game changer regarding the power levels between younger and older vampires, just because if an elder is unlucky with it´s rouse checks, it will be on the bringe of frenzy latest after 5 rousings of the blood. (In my games I assume that most my NPC´s have about Hunger 1 or 2). On the other hand I had our Daeva in the group heal 5 levels of lethal damage without getting hungrier.
                              - Rolling for Rousing of the blood are additional dice rools - that is a thing I don´t like.
                              - I personally like that even a Vampire with Hunger 0 has this uncertainity that they don´t know how long they will last before the Beast want´s to claim them. Will it be 5 or 15 rolls? I observed it nicely in my players - which is complety on theme and mood for Vampire the Requiem with me - that it keeps Players anxious and on the edge, very much likes vampires perceive it."





                              Gamelines: Requiem, Lost, Awakening, Trinity Continuum: Aeon, some V5.
                              Likes cheesecake (quark-based)

                                          Originally posted by nicodemus View Post
                                          maekkel thanks for sharing!

                                          did you not run into any issues mixing the healing of V5 with the damage system of VtR? or with the target number for success difference when Rousing?
                                          Grateful to serve!

                                          No, actually not but we use also a slightly changed system. Also we rarely have combat, as most of my players avoid it.
                                          Also Adversaries will normally not attack with heavy weaponry like swords or chainsaws or assault rifles.
                                          Thus a normal attack does mostly suxx+weapon mod in damage per attack, which means 1 to 7+ damage per hit. and afterwards the damage is halved.
                                          As the average vampire has 7-8 healthlevels, this hardly kills them.

                                          We use 3 damage types plus eventual tilts:
                                          Combat is Dicepool VS Dicepool - who makes more successes, hits.

                                          light damage is always halved, rounded down = sources: brawl, melee, firearms :
                                          Mending like in V5 , but each rouse check heals at least 2 levels
                                          heavy damage is always halved except for banes = sources: some supernatural weaponry, some banes, a vampire receiving more light damage than she got health levels :
                                          Mending like in V5 , for agravated = 1 Level is healed for 1 rouse check
                                          supernatural/aggravated damage (never halved) = sources: banes, extreme damage
                                          Mending: only once a night possible and 1 level costs +1 Hunger + 1 rouse check and 1 WP.
