            Originally posted by K9ine View Post
            On breaking points, nothing else as far as I have read.
            It also gives a bonus to Abjuration rolls (see page 140), and gives the storyteller a smaller dice pool to use against you with the Madness Condition (see page 289). And I guess you've got a better resistance against Lunacy, since Lunacy is a special Breaking Point roll. But yeah, most of what high Integrity is good for is keeping you from losing more Integrity. Just like high Sanity in Call of Cthulhu and other games that borrow the mechanic is mostly just good for keeping you from losing more Sanity. That's why I made that comparison in the first place. Think about it like a sort of health track, having high health is its own reward, because it makes it harder for you to run out of health. You don't need bonuses for having high HP or rewards for successful dodge rolls (beyond, you know, not losing HP), but you don't call health an "unbalanced mechanic".

            Originally posted by K9ine View Post
            I wouldn't really have a problem so much with this if a success on a breaking point gave you good conditions instead of bad, or simply gave you no condition but and extra beat.
            Why would you get a beneficial Condition for that. You were still just exposed to something horrific, and a success only means you're able to cope with what you experienced without suffering long-term psychological harm. The benefit from succeeding a Breaking Point roll is not losing Integrity. Actually coming out of such an experience more resolved is an exceptional thing, which means it requires exceptional success.

            Originally posted by K9ine View Post
            Do know if Willpower can boost breaking points? I would have thought no, because it seems like a passive thing.
            I don't know of any reason you wouldn't be able to spend Willpower on them.

                High Integrity makes you one badass cabrón when it comes to exorcisms and the like.

                Someone with high Integrity (let's say Integrity 8) and a decent Resolve/Composure (we're talking 3 dots each) who has a Virtue of "Thorough" can kill someone in cold blood without suffering Integrity loss. We're talking "I'm rolling 4 dice. Yes, that's after the -5 penalty." Heck, if you have the right Conditions (Inspired, maybe?), that murder can reinforce your sense of self.

                Same guy could run a good Abjuration - 9 dice vs. the Ephemeral Being's Power + Resistance is pretty solid against low-grade Ephemeral Being. Checking 2nd Edition Core, they'd have a pretty even chance against pretty much all of the Rank 1 or 2 Angels. Toss on Hardened Exorcist, and you've got a much better dice pool than most of them, and probably a decent dicepool for Warding/Binding as well.

                Hell

                =---=

                I like the Integrity replacement, WHW - it got the first Like out of me of this year.

                =---=

                As for why people don't want it to be a positive Condition, K9ine... some people (silly people!) pretend these are horror games ;p. More seriously, the Conditions it gives you currently pushes you towards making more Breaking Point rolls, which gives you more delicious XP. And despite the awesome benefits of high Integrity, it's also something that's optional to care about.

                1) Taking Breaking Point rolls is optional, as long as you have a justification ("My character works at a slaughterhouse - screams stopped bothering me after the first month").
                2) The Tolerance for Biology merit not only stops you from throwing up at the still-living person turned into Modern Art, it also technically waives any Breaking Point roll involved. Same thing for Raised by Wolves (if you're Wolf-Blooded), except that one also waives Breaking Point rolls for observing the supernatural.
                3) Losing Integrity is actually... not all that bad. Someone at Integrity 1 has a -2 penalty to Breaking Point rolls... if you pick up a meditation habit (Meditative Mind ••), you've counteracted that penalty entirely. So if Example Guy lost 7 dots of Integrity somehow, he'd still have the same dice-pool for murder (he'd lose a die if he does it more than once per day, but still.)
                4) On a meta level, Spooked and Shaken are good Conditions - they give you spotlight time. Guilty is better - it's only really a penalty if your ST calls for a lot of very specific opposed rolls - too bad the example dice pools for those actions are resisted, not opposed. And it lets you ham it up with confessions of guilt - no downsides.

                (On a side note, I kinda want to scrape together a little "handbook" for people who want to play mortal exorcists who punch above their pay grade.)
