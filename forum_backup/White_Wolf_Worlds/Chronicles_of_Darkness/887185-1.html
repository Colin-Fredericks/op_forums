                Minor realization I felt should be added.
                Hogwarts is a pocket dimension cast at indefinite duration and max advanced scale. The dark forest students are forbidden to go in mark the border (it does have depth).

                                        Honestly, I'd be tempted to just call Hogwarts and other such locations as being a form of verge in this universe, inherently magical locations that mages may have added effects to, but not necessarily created themselves. These hidden/obscured/extra-dimensional/ect. spaces would then serve as inherent mysteries for generation after generation of mages and reasons why they have become inhabited hotspots of the wizarding world. Unlike CoD verges, CoWW (Chronicles of Wizarding World) verges wouldn't need to be tied to a specific Path, but could be.

                                        If you say Hogwarts is inherently veiled, then perhaps studing this mystery is where much of veiling magic comes from, same if you say Hogwarts is hidden in a pocket realm, studying it has lead to mages being able to enchant tents with bigger insides and houses hidden between addresses.

                                            At some point I began imagining a Hogwarts like school of magic based on the 5 Watchtowers. I didn't refine the traits and aesthetics of these Watchtower based houses though, they were mainly just passing thoughts and ideas. The one I mainly thought about was Stygia/Moros/The Tower of the Lead Coin.

                                            For example, Moros of the House of The Lead Coin would be associated with determination, hard work, pragmatism, ingenuity, invention and science. Members of this house are usually very open-minded and accepting of new ideas and the concepts of death and the afterlife. The aesthetics would have involved lots of carved stone, wood and marble, dour looking gardens and crypt-like surroundings with symbols of the afterlife and skeletal themed statuettes, finely crafted, sturdy wood and marble bookcases and fine decoration and furnitures, with a large number of portraits, statues and busts of deceased great people, with ample ghosts present. Experimentation with machinery and alchemy and communication with the dead are common to this House. House Robes and general color schemes, for heraldry and decoration, are grey, purple and black.
