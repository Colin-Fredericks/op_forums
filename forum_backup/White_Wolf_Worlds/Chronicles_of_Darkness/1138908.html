              It is a versatile system in my opinion. As long as you set the tone and expectations I dont see why you shouldn't do it. If you still worried let me put this in perspective. On april 1st 2010 They released a supplement called Dudes Of Legend to celebrate April fools. This is all about making your game exactly as absurd or ridiculous as you want it. It included merits on: being a ninja, gaining armor just because your manly (or bountiful) chest is exposed, unicorns, and even went a low brow as to include merits for exceptional genitalia. They let Eddy Webb release a supplement where one can use their penis as a weapon or vagina as a storage cache. Im not joking. Please don't sick the moderators on me. They actually did that. The chronicles of darkness system should be able to accommodate your clown cult just fine.
