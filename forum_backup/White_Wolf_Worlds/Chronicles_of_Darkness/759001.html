    The Father's Form

    11-06-2015, 01:41 PM
    Been reading through the Werewolf book over the last while. Really enjoying it and most of the Gifts are pretty awesome. Except this one. I just can't understand what purpose it serves. At first I thought it was a way of assuming a Combat form not quite on par with Gauru that you get more time with, but pretty much as soon as combat begins, you're going to end up following the regular Gauru rules anyway. Then I thought it was a way of Cheesing a few extra rounds of Gauru, by forcing the transformation a second time, but it doesn't say that anything resets, so you probably just end up entering Basu-Im as soon as you get into combat.

    Then I thought that it was a utility gift, but your Stamina is on par with Urshul and the Urshul is actually faster. I suppose it can be used when you need that extra dot of strength, but there are other gifts to grant you strength anyway, and if there is that much disparity between yourself and the object, you're probably not going to move it anyway.

    Any thoughts and suggestions? Stuff I might have missed?

                      Originally posted by BigDamnHero View Post
                      I guess it's a way to get a bonus to Strength, Size, and Dexterity and keep opposable (albeit big and unwieldy) thumbs?
                      Don't forget the +1 Perception as well. Gauru form has the biggest attribute bonuses of any form, plus opposable thumbs, which would make it the ideal form for performing Rites if not for the built-in countdown timer to Death Rage. Father's form fixes that problem.

                      It's also a great intimidation tactic. Gauru is called the Killing Form for a reason, and taking it shows that you are willing to use deadly force, possibly even to risk Kuruth. Father's Form allows you to pose as the guy who's willing to kill you to win an argument, without actually risking Death Rage unless a fight breaks out.

                      And, as Lyrics of War pointed out, there's huge cultural significance to being able to enter was believed to be Father Wolf's true form at any time, and sustain it without losing control.

                      Still, it's not to everyone's taste, so I homebrewed an alternative Facet for those who don't care for Father's Form, inspired by 1e's Primal Form Gift. In my games, it's actually a Fetish that allows you to use Father's Form in an alternate way, but it works as a replacement as well if you don't like Father's Form.

                      Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.
