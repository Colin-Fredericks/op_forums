                                        Originally posted by DeimosMasque View Post
                                        Currently designing an Artifact and need a little help.

                                        The artifact is a sword that is basically unbreakable and can be made to cut through anything.

                                        What would the merit rating and stats be?
                                        Strictly speaking, "Artifacts" cast spells, rather than have persistent magical qualities, so an Artifact sword that can make itself indestructible could theoretically cast the spell to make any target temporarily indestructible. In mechanical terms, it sounds like you want an Enhanced Item with the dots allocated to improve Durability combined with two-dot Perfected Item merit from Signs of Sorcery to make it out of Siderite. Normal iron has 3 Durability, so I'd assume Siderite would be at least 4. If that isn't indestructible enough, you can add a point of Durability per merit point, up to 5. You can also always refluff it so that it's just magical rather than being made of Perfected Iron.

                                        Long story short, for seven dots, you get a sword that treats diamonds like tissue paper (ignores Durability) and would take some hefty magic to dent (Durability 7).

                                            From a narrative perspective, are your dirty socks important? Does their being there say something about the world? Are you looking for the sort of Essence that dirty socks could be expected to have?

                                            "Everything has a spirit" should not be interpreted too specifically — tightly-defined invisible entities are the domain of goetia, whereas spirits tend to diffuse into broad categories that result in particularly bizarre hybrids if they feed on Essence whose ties to their concept are too abstract. The spirit of your dirty socks, if it rates attention at all, is more probably a spirit of clutter or filth or fashion rather than a Dirty Sock Spirit, and the Essence your dirty socks give off can support spirits that aren't the spirit of your dirty socks — it can, in a pinch, kind of support any spirit.

                                            Essence scarcity for spirits in the Shadow is a matter of feeding grounds and the need to spend it to effect change; werewolves have a different relation to it on account of their own needs and limitations, and mages largely operate outside of these constraints because Essence isn't a thing they need and making half a dozen magath because you fed house-spirits horse-Essence to see what would happen is part of the game.
