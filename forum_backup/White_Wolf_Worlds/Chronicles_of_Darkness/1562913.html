    Warlocks of Arcadia, 2e

    08-09-2026, 10:29 PM
    The following was inspired by the Warlocks of Arcadia option found in Mage Chronicler's Guide, but revised and expanded. I borrowed heavily from Papa Bear's Realms and Kingdoms; but I have also heavily revised it. Without further ado:

    Introduction

    At the core of Supernal magic lies the interplay between human identity, cosmic origin, and spiritual resonance. A mage does not merely study spellcraft; they experience a fundamental realignment of their soul through the awakening of their Genius. This awakening is dictated by the intrinsic nature of their spirit, the Supernal Realm that calls out to them across the Abyss, and the path they forge to bridge the divide. Understanding a mage’s art requires viewing it through these interwoven lenses: the internal drive of their Archetype, the cosmic foundation of their Supernal Realm, and the specific Path that connects the two.


    The Archetypes

    Every human soul possesses a "higher self"—a divine, awakened reflection known as the Genius. While the sleeping mind remains bound to mundane perception, the Genius inherently reaches upward toward the Supernal Realm, seeking to pierce the Lie and reclaim its true cosmic heritage. Every Genius manifests through one of five fundamental Archetypes, which dictate the essential lens, primal drive, and spiritual disposition through which a mage confronts reality and channels magical force into the fallen world.

    The Thaumaturge (Forces)
    The Thaumaturge embodies raw power, environmental mastery, and formal, authoritative command over the energetic tides of the universe. Driven by an innate drive to direct kinetic, thermal, and elemental fluxes, this archetype views magic as a tempest to be harnessed through discipline, willpower, and divine ritual. Whether channeling radiant lightning or dampening wild oscillations, the Thaumaturge approaches reality as a dynamic sphere of opposing energies waiting for a commanding voice to dictate its vector.

    Associated Paths: Authority (Primal Wild), Determination (Pandemonium), Might (Aether), Strife (Stygia), Thunder (Arcadia).

    The Shaman (Life)
    The Shaman serves as a conduit for biological vitality, physical evolution, and the instinctual pulses of living nature. Operating through the rhythms of flesh, blood, growth, and instinct, this archetype views magic not as an abstract science but as a living, breathing communion with bodily existence. They walk with an innate sensitivity to organic harmony and decay, manipulating cellular form, physical vigor, and primal drives to heal, mutate, or unleash the untamed essence of life.

    Associated Paths: Destiny (Arcadia), Ecstasy (Primal Wild), Introspection (Pandemonium), Sacrifice (Aether), Tears (Stygia).

    The Alchemist (Matter)
    The Alchemist is a master of physical substance, structure, and material transformation, treating the corporeal world as a forge of infinite possibility. Rooted in the tangible reality of elements, metals, and physical inertia, this archetype seeks perfection through the alteration and stabilization of form. They view magic through the lens of crafting, transmutation, and structural permanence, turning raw components into enduring relics or reshaping the physical medium to reflect internal intent.

    Associated Paths: Artifacts (Arcadia), Doom (Stygia), Forging (Pandemonium), Immortality (Aether), Sculpting (Primal Wild).

    The Warlock (Space)
    The Warlock navigates the intricate geometry of distance, co-location, and spatial connection, treating reality as an interconnected web of coordinates. Moving fluidly through barriers and manipulating the gaps between locations, this archetype perceives the universe as a malleable blueprint where isolation and proximity are merely matters of perspective. Their magic centers on spatial dominance, weaving pathways across vast expanses, and confronting the profound isolation of untethered void.

    Associated Paths: Discovery (Arcadia), Oblivion (Stygia), Predators (Primal Wild), Scourging (Pandemonium), Unity (Aether).

    The Witch (Time)
    The Witch moves in rhythm with the temporal currents, mastering momentum, historical cycles, foresight, and the inevitable flow of cause and effect. Grounded in the perception of past, present, and future as a unified tapestry, this archetype manipulates the velocity of events and reads the echoes of destiny before they unfold. Their magic is one of patience, memory, and temporal manipulation, harvesting grace from chaos while riding the inescapable tides of time.

    Associated Paths: Eternity (Aether), Inevitability (Stygia), Observance (Pandemonium), Servitude (Primal Wild), Thistle (Arcadia).


    The Realms

    Beyond the Abyss lie the five Supernal Realms, the true foundations of existence before the cosmos was severed into Fallen reality. Looming at the edge of each Supernal Realm stands a sacred Watchtower—a cosmic beacon anchored by ancient myth to bridge the divide across the Abyss. When a seeker's soul stirs, it is the ringing clarion call of a specific Watchtower that echoes down to resonate with their Genius, piercing the sleep of the Lie to ignite an Awakening. By tracing their name onto a Watchtower's spiritual architecture, a mage permanently binds their soul to that Supernal Realm, determining the governing laws and Ruling Arcana of their magical legacy.

    The Aether (Prime)
    Aether is the Supernal Realm of celestial light, immaculate resonance, and pure, unfiltered truth where reality exists in its most ideal form. Illuminated by divine radiance, it is a realm where magic flows without friction, laying bare the underlying spiritual architecture of existence. Mages who align with Aether strive for absolute perfection, celestial authority, and spiritual clarity, channeling raw magical potency to bring ideal forms into the fallen world.

    Associated Paths: Eternity (The Witch), Immortality (The Alchemist), Might (The Thaumaturge), Sacrifice (The Shaman), Unity (The Warlock).

    Arcadia (Fate)
    Arcadia is a realm of enchantment, binding oaths, winding destinies, and capricious fortune where every action echoes with narrative weight. Built upon the subtle threads of luck, curse, and promise, its landscapes shift according to tragic and heroic archetypes. Mages linked to Arcadia navigate a world of grand opportunities and treacherous bargains, learning to manipulate the hidden web of probability and destiny to weave their own triumphant outcomes.

    Associated Paths: Artifacts (The Alchemist), Destiny (The Shaman), Discovery (The Warlock), Thistle (The Witch), Thunder (The Thaumaturge).

    Pandemonium (Mind)
    Pandemonium is a nightmare landscape of psychological ordeals, inner labyrinths, and terrifying trials designed to strip away delusion and forge mental clarity. In this realm, thought takes physical shape, reflecting an individual's deepest anxieties, hidden guilt, and unyielding focus. Mages who venture through Pandemonium undergo intense mental tempering, learning to command psychological forces, shatter mental illusions, and master the mind under absolute pressure.

    Associated Paths: Determination (The Thaumaturge), Forging (The Alchemist), Introspection (The Shaman), Observance (The Witch), Scourging (The Warlock).

    The Primal Wild (Spirit)
    The Primal Wild is an untamed, animistic wilderness brimming with raw spirit resonance, territorial instincts, and vibrant ecological power. Here, every rock, river, and beast possesses an active spiritual presence, locked in a continuous dance of dominance, survival, and communion. Mages connected to the Primal Wild immerse themselves in the hidden spirit world, forging pacts with unseen entities and tapping into the visceral, unbridled energy of nature.

    Associated Paths: Authority (The Thaumaturge), Ecstasy (The Shaman), Predators (The Warlock), Sculpting (The Alchemist), Servitude (The Witch).

    Stygia (Death)
    Stygia is the quiet realm of leaden skies, underground rivers, decaying ruins, and eternal silence, governing transition and the ultimate end of all things. It is a domain where momentum fades, physical forms slowly erode, and the ghosts of the past linger in solemn contemplation. Mages tied to Stygia understand the profound beauty and necessity of decay, utilizing its quiet stillness to command ghost-light, navigate endings, and accept the inescapable march toward oblivion.

    Associated Paths: Doom (The Alchemist), Inevitability (The Witch), Oblivion (The Warlock), Strife (The Thaumaturge), Tears (The Shaman).


    The Paths

    When a Watchtower rings out across the Abyss to ignite the sleeping Genius within a human soul, its call forms an eternal spiritual conduit between the mage's awakening consciousness and the Supernal Realm—a unique spiritual journey known as a Path. This bridge dictates the fundamental alignment of the mage's art, granting them command over its Ruling Arcana. Yet, crossing the Abyss is never without trial; spiritual obstacles, cosmic dissonance, and the fundamental limitations of the fallen human ego inevitably obscure a portion of the Supernal truth along that journey. As a result, every Path suffers from a blind spot—an Inferior Arcanum that inherently conflicts with the mage's magical mindset, creating a fundamental vulnerability in their cosmic perception.

    Path of Might (Thaumaturges of Aether)
    The Path of Might combines divine resonance with raw elemental tempests, creating mages who embody righteous power and authoritative command. Walking under celestial light, these seekers wield forces with immaculate precision, using celestial faith to command atmospheric and energetic power. They stand as radiant bastions of strength, channeling the raw momentum of the heavens into tangible, worldly force.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Death. Fixated entirely on triumphant energy and glorious, eternal vitality under the Aether’s light, these mages find the quiet stagnation, decay, and finality of end-states completely antithetical to their explosive worldview. They struggle to grasp magic that accepts or commands the silent, motionless transition into oblivion.

    Path of Sacrifice (Shamans of Aether)
    The Path of Sacrifice channels divine light into selfless physical service, offering biological vitality to uphold celestial order. These healers and protectors devote their own bodily essence to nourish others, operating with pure, altruistic intent. They view their own living form as an altar through which celestial purity and life flow into the world.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Space. Because their magic relies on intimate, direct bodily contact and immediate physical offering, they struggle with the cold, distant geometric abstractions of spatial manipulation. The idea of isolating, separating, or warping distance feels entirely unnatural to a practitioner whose entire philosophy is based on immediate, selfless communion.

    Path of Immortality (Alchemists of Aether)
    The Path of Immortality seeks the immaculate refinement of physical substance to achieve untarnished, eternal physical perfection. By combining divine ideal form with material stability, these mages craft bodies and relics that resist all wear and degradation. They view physical reality as something to be elevated into an indestructible, radiant state.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Mind. In their hyper-fixation on objective, tangible perfection and physical permanence, these mages dismiss the fluid, subjective, and prone-to-illusion nature of human thoughts and emotions. They view the psyche as a fleeting, unreliable distraction compared to the eternal, physical truth of refined substance.

    Path of Unity (Warlocks of Aether)
    The Path of Unity weaves sacred celestial threads across spatial coordinates to forge absolute communion across vast distances. Mages on this path dissolve spatial isolation, connecting disparate places and people into a harmonious, divine whole. They experience the cosmos as an instantaneous web of divine presence where no true separation exists.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Time. Because their magic demands immediate, omnipresent spatial connection and total co-location, they struggle with the linear, patient progression required by temporal flow. Waiting for events to unfold sequentially or dealing with historical momentum frustrates a mind built to span all locations simultaneously in a singular, divine moment.

    Path of Eternity (Witches of Aether)
    The Path of Eternity anchors temporal foresight within the everlasting present, perceiving time as a grand, divine cycle of existence. These mages ride the immutable currents of history and prophecy with serene grace under celestial light. They view all events as preordained, harmonic notes in an endless, celestial symphony.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Forces. Immersed in the tranquil, grand picture of cosmic cycles and eternal balance, they are uniquely ill-equipped to handle sudden, chaotic, and destructive physical energy shifts. The violent, erratic impact of immediate elemental tempests disrupts the serene, timeless balance they rely upon.

    Path of Thunder (Thaumaturges of Arcadia)
    The Path of Thunder fuses audacious bravery with wild, tempestuous energy, commanding storms woven directly into the fabric of destiny. These dynamic figures strike with sudden, heroic impact, manipulating fate through dramatic displays of power. They treat life as a theater of heroic trials where elemental forces accentuate crucial narrative moments.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Life. Fixated on high-stakes dramatic momentum and grand, sweeping displays of energetic power, they overlook the slow, subtle, and messy biological processes of living organisms. The quiet, gradual work of cellular healing or biological evolution lacks the immediate, thunderous impact required by their dramatic nature.

    Path of Destiny (Shamans of Arcadia)
    The Path of Destiny traces and manipulates the biological threads of mortal lives, guiding growth, health, and hereditary lineages through the web of chance. Mages of this path shape the physical futures of living beings, weaving vitality into heroic narratives. They perceive every pulse of blood as a thread in a legendary tapestry.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Death. Because their entire identity is built on nurturing living potential and weaving vibrant futures into the web of fate, the absolute finality of decay represents a fundamental failure of their art. They naturally resist and struggle to comprehend magic that accepts the terminal end of a thread rather than its ongoing woven journey.

    Path of Artifacts (Alchemists of Arcadia)
    The Path of Artifacts focuses on crafting enchanted relics and legendary tools imbued with weight, purpose, and fateful significance. These master artisans forge material objects that carry heroic destinies and alter the course of history. They believe that true destiny manifests through physical, enduring objects that can be passed down through generations.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Spirit. Relying so heavily on dense, physical matter and tangible craftsmanship to anchor their magical oaths, they find the ephemeral, formless, and disembodied nature of spirits difficult to comprehend. To an artisan who demands concrete form for their destinies, an intangible spirit feels elusive and untrustworthy.

    Path of Discovery (Warlocks of Arcadia)
    The Path of Discovery maps hidden pathways, fortunate coincidences, and spatial opportunities through the capricious web of luck. Seekers on this path find secret doors, shortcut routes, and auspicious locations where destiny naturally unfolds. They perceive space not as a rigid grid, but as a labyrinth of lucky turns.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Prime. Their magic relies almost entirely on the external, circumstantial luck of finding the right place at the right time, making it difficult for them to generate or manipulate pure, raw, unshaped magical energy from within. Abstract magical resonance feels alien when compared to the practical reality of finding a physical path forward.

    Path of Thistle (Witches of Arcadia)
    The Path of Thistle navigates the unpredictable tides of temporal turning points, harvesting grace and fortune from chaotic circumstances. Mages on this path manipulate timing and luck to turn tragic outcomes into sudden triumphs. They move through life like gamblers, betting on the precise moment when chance will favor them.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Forces. Because they achieve their goals through subtle nudges, fortunate timing, and riding the path of least resistance, they struggle when forced to meet raw, violent physical force head-on. They lack the brute energetic framework necessary to command or withstand direct elemental destruction.

    Path of Determination (Thaumaturges of Pandemonium)
    The Path of Determination channels inner psychological turmoil into disciplined, focused energy, using mental resolve to master elemental power. These mages conquer personal nightmares by unleashing controlled force against internal and external opposition. They view the mind as an engine powered by the flames of ordeal.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Fate. Relying entirely on absolute, unyielding self-determination and brute mental willpower to force their reality onto the world, they deeply distrust concepts like luck, destiny, or surrender to chance. The idea of yielding to fateful currents or trusting in luck directly contradicts their ethos of self-forged control.

    Path of Introspection (Shamans of Pandemonium)
    The Path of Introspection turns the lens of consciousness inward, mastering raw biological instincts through rigorous mental discipline. Seekers on this path conquer physical desires and sensory distractions to achieve absolute self-awareness. They use the nightmares of the mind to temper the wild urges of the body.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Forces. By withdrawing their attention deep into the internal landscape of self-mastery and psychological control, they lose connection with the external, outward movement of environmental energy. The projection of kinetic or elemental power into the world feels like an unwelcome distraction from their internal crucible.

    Path of Forging (Alchemists of Pandemonium)
    The Path of Forging subjects both mind and physical substance to intense psychological pressure, hammering them into an unbreakable vessel. These mages refine corporeal forms through mental endurance, creating impenetrable physical and psychological defenses. They treat both body and mind as raw iron to be hardened against the horrors of existence.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Time. In their drive to construct an unyielding, static defense that can withstand any ordeal, they build structures meant to resist all change. Consequently, they struggle with the fluid, shifting nature of time, finding it difficult to adapt to temporal changes or manipulate a current that constantly erodes static permanence.

    Path of Scourging (Warlocks of Pandemonium)
    The Path of Scourging strips away spatial illusions and mental defenses, forcing individuals to confront terrifying inner labyrinths of isolation. Mages on this path manipulate mental landscapes and spatial entrapment to break down ego and illusion. They trap targets in mental and spatial voids to purge them of falsehood.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Matter. Operating entirely within the realms of psychological terror, spatial geometry, and subjective perception, they lack any meaningful connection to solid, physical substance. Tangible matter feels too rigid and earthly for a mage whose craft exists entirely in the void of the mind and isolated space.

    Path of Observance (Witches of Pandemonium)
    The Path of Observance endures internal mental disorientation through quiet patience, observing psychological trials without reacting. These mages master temporal perception within the mind, analyzing thoughts across subjective time. They survive mental hellscapes by simply waiting out the storm with absolute stillness.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Spirit. Their method of enduring psychological trials is entirely internal, passive, and analytical, leaving them disconnected from the active, external, and communicative nature of spirit entities. They view reality through a lens of isolated internal observation, making outward animistic communion feel jarring and unnatural.

    Path of Authority (Thaumaturges of the Primal Wild)
    The Path of Authority asserts natural dominion over the wild elemental forces of nature, commanding storms and primal energy through instinctual right. Mages of this path lead with fierce vigor, directing natural phenomena through spiritual presence. They view elemental power as an extension of the wild, untamed hierarchy of nature.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Matter. Driven by active, fluid, and dynamic natural forces, they struggle with inert, stagnant, and heavily processed matter. The rigid, artificial stability of manufactured structures or heavy, unyielding stone feels suffocating to a mage accustomed to the free-flowing energy of the wild.

    Path of Ecstasy (Shamans of the Primal Wild)
    The Path of Ecstasy embraces unbridled vitality and raw physical sensation, merging organic life with the wild spirits of nature. These mages celebrate bodily experience, wild instincts, and primordial health in its purest form. They experience the magic of reality through visceral, uninhibited physical immersion.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Mind. Because their entire path is dedicated to surrendering to raw instinct, visceral emotion, and bodily sensation, they reject cold, detached intellectualizing. They view overthinking and cerebral analysis as barriers to true instinctual communion, making abstract mental magic their greatest weakness.

    Path of Sculpting (Alchemists of the Primal Wild)
    The Path of Sculpting molds the physical world like soft clay, imbuing raw material form with living spirit and natural vitality. Artisans of this path shape stone, wood, and metal as if they were organic, living media. They see physical matter as a living canvas waiting to express the spirit within it.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Fate. Fixated on direct, physical manipulation and hands-on creation, they struggle to interact with abstract, invisible webs of probability or preordained destiny. They believe reality is shaped by physical action and tangible effort, not by subtle oaths or coincidental luck.

    Path of Predators (Warlocks of the Primal Wild)
    The Path of Predators embodies the instinctual thrill of territorial dominance, tracking prey and commanding wild terrain through spatial awareness. Mages on this path master the geometry of the hunt, pursuing targets across open wilderness with spirit-guided precision. They perceive space as a territory to be claimed and traversed.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Death. Defined by the active, energetic drive of the hunt, territorial expansion, and the fierce assertion of living dominance, they naturally recoil from the passive stillness of death and decay. The quiet, stagnant aftermath of the hunt holds no interest for a practitioner whose entire focus is the active pursuit.

    Path of Servitude (Witches of the Primal Wild)
    The Path of Servitude immerses itself in ancestral memory and the eternal, repeating cycles of the natural spirit world. These guardians act as humble servants to ancient nature spirits, maintaining sacred temporal rhythms over generations. They view themselves as small, temporary stewards in an ancient, living cycle.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Prime. Deeply bound to specific ancestral spirits, natural rhythms, and localized traditions, they struggle to manipulate raw, unformed, and abstract magical energy. Their magic is fundamentally relational and historical, making the manipulation of pure, ungrounded mana feel alien and detached from nature.

    Path of Strife (Thaumaturges of Stygia)
    The Path of Strife maintains nobility and resilience amidst inevitable decay, commanding volatile energy in a fading, shadowed world. Mages on this path fight against entropy with fierce energetic displays, holding back the dark through sheer force of will. They are the blazing torches fighting against an encroaching winter.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Mind. In their desperate, outward struggle to project elemental force against physical entropy and decay, they neglect the quiet, internal landscape of psychological awareness. They treat conflict as an objective war against decay, leaving them blind to subtle mental manipulation and emotional nuances.

    Path of Tears (Shamans of Stygia)
    The Path of Tears walks the bittersweet line between mortality and decay, guiding living essence through its final, mournful harvest. These mages offer comfort in loss, understanding how physical vitality eventually surrenders to quiet darkness. They honor the tragic beauty of a life reaching its natural conclusion.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Space. Bound intimately to the direct, personal experience of bodily decay and local mourning, they struggle with abstract, distant spatial geometry. The act of reaching across vast distances or warping spatial bounds feels hollow and detached to a practitioner focused on immediate, localized grief and personal transition.

    Path of Doom (Alchemists of Stygia)
    The Path of Doom accepts the stoic reality of physical degradation, material decay, and inert, unchanging stillness. Mages of this path study rust, erosion, and the eventual breakdown of all constructed forms into silence. They find peace in the inevitable return of all physical things to dust.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Spirit. Immersed in the heavy, inert, and inanimate reality of decaying matter, they are completely out of touch with the active, passionate world of spirits. The dynamic, ethereal energy of animistic spirits feels entirely incompatible with their devotion to silent, motionless, and permanent material breakdown.

    Path of Oblivion (Warlocks of Stygia)
    The Path of Oblivion navigates empty voids, vast expanses of nothingness, and final end-states in silent detachment. Mages on this path observe the collapse of distance and the fading of spatial connections into a quiet abyss. They inhabit the desolate spaces where reality has unravelled.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Life. Fixated on empty expanses, total distance, and the absolute dissolution of connection into silence, they naturally reject the messy, vibrant, and warm energy of biological growth. The pulsing, chaotic vitality of living organisms represents an unwelcome noise in their quiet, empty void.

    Path of Inevitability (Witches of Stygia)
    The Path of Inevitability surrenders to the relentless erosion of time, mastering the silent, undeniable march of all things toward their final end. Mages on this path wield the temporal weight of age and ending, watching empires and lives crumble into history. They embody the inescapable truth that everything eventually ends.

    Its Inferior Arcanum is Matter. Focused entirely on the relentless temporal erosion that grinds all physical things into nothingness, they find no value in attempting to build, preserve, or manipulate solid matter. Physical substance is merely a temporary, fleeting target for the slow, inevitable grinding wheel of time.
