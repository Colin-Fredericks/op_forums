                    The Emerald Crucible: Amulet of Eternity (0-00000)
                    Unlike other alchemists, the Crucible are not a selfish group- their goal is helping humanity as whole to transcend the limitations of their physical body and stave off death. While alchemists could gain such gift through their research of the Fire and harvesting the humors of prometheans, most of mankind are not alchemists- and those who help them in their hunt gain some of their gifts. The Amulet of Eternity is a single, 1-5 merit which takes the form of a physical object, usually made out of emerald. The Amulet itself acts as a container of supernatural energy, requiring the user to "charge it up" to make it useful. In order to do so, there are two ways- the first is investing Willpower points into the Amulet (it can't hold more Willpower than its rating). The second is by using the humors of a promethean, which usually requires the help of an alchemist at dissecting the monster and harvesting the Fire from it. Doing so replenish the Amulet's energy ton its maximal amount.

                    Once the Amulet was charged, it has the following effects- first, as long as it holds at least one point of energy in it and is held by the hunter, she would not age. Removing the Amulet would make her rapidly age to her chronological age. Second, the effective amount of energy invested in the Amulet is treated as a Supernatural Tolerance trait to help the hunter fight supernatural effects. Third, and most important, the energy of the Amulet could be used in order to enhance the hunter's body and transcend her usual limitation. By spending a single energy dot from the Amulet, she can either enhance one of her Attributes by one for the next Scene, heal one level of Lethal Damage, remove a single personal Tilt which is caused by physical or mental harm or suppress a Persistent Condition for a Scene. The Amulet can by charged at any moment by spending the Willpower cost. All such effects require a physical contact between the hunter and her Amulet.

                    Order of the Four Cornered Seal: Gu (0--00000)
                    after years of investigating poisons, sickness and madness, the alchemists of the cursed Order learned how to disrupt their own, natural harmony into creating an artificial imbalance by refining special poisons out of vermin- poisons which grant their hunters both power and curse. The practice of Gu requires an alchemist to preform the traditional ritual (trapping venomous vermin together and let them kill each other until only one is left) and extract the refined poison. The hunter then consume the poison and roll for Stamina+Gu to resist the effects. In case of failure, he suffer from one level of Lethal Damage (Dramatic Failure turns it aggravated, for not only the poison wasn't refined but also charged with dangerous occult energy). In case of Success the hunter may choose any Dread Power whose rating is equal or lesser then her Gu rating. Mixing the poison with promethean humors grants +2 modifier for the roll. Once manifesting the same power 4 times in that way, the character may choose to turn it into a permanent power, which takes the form of a black tattoo shaped like the animal which was used for the ritual. The effective rating of all permanent Dread Powers can not be greater than her Gu dots, although she may switch such Powers with new ones after following the conditions, which creates new tattoos but won't remove the old ones. Temporary Dread Powers last for 10*[6- Dread Power] minutes once the poison is taken, and use Bashing Damage is fuel for their effects. Each Dread Power, both permanent and temporary, force a negative Condition or Personal Tilt upon the character as long as it is in effect.

                        Titgwai Gang- Gisei [0-00000]
                        The gang worship the ghost goddess of the train stations, giving her sacrifices in exchange for future gain. Those sacrifices allowed the Tek tek to grow in power, and she fulfilled her part of the deal. Gisei is a single, 1-5 merit, symbolizing the strength of connection the hunter has with the sacrifices she gives to herself. Once sacrificing a desired object through burning (as the traditional sacrfices for the dead) the cultist may then roll for Gisei+Occult+Manipulation. In case of Dramatic Failure, the Tek tek shows her displeasure, blocking the use of the Merit for a number of hours equal to 6-MCI. In case of Success, the cultist may spend one Willpower point in order to call upon the object for a number of days equal to her Gisei. In case it was a weapon, the summoned object deals Aggravated damage. Those items may interact with the Twilight. The cultist may also decide that instead of gaining the item itself he prefers to gain a more abstract benefit. In case of Success, you get the benefit of a single Merit for the duration of the Endowmet, with a rating equal or less than your Gisei. You may not have two such Merits active in the same time. Sacrificing a kishamono gives you +2 for the rolls. Exceptional Success would give you one such temporary Merit dot. Supernatural items destroyed through Gisei could be accessed even after the duration has ended, considering the roll was successful.

                        Precinct 44- Auctoritas [0-00000]
                        Authority, not truth, makes the law, and the bad cops of Precinct 44 know to to use theirs. Whenever preforming a Social Maneuver, you may spend a Willpower point in order to lower the amount of needed Doors by [Endowment]. In an addition, the cop may roll for Endowment+Manipulation and expand a Willpower point. In case of Success, the target treats fearing the cop as a Vice and obeying him as an Aspiration. Exceptional Success gives him a point of Willpower. Dramatic Failure makes the character immune to the influence of Auctoritas. Finally, when forcing a door, the cop may use half of his Endowment (rounded up) as a modifier for the roll.

                            so after a long time of no updates, I present potential Endowments for hunters who work with the different Sekhem Sorcerers, as well as the Tomb Raiders from Mortal Remains

                            Order of the Bull (a.k.a Taurans)- Calls of Earth (0-00000, special)
                            The priests of Sarruma the Mountain King know the ways of the earth and can evoke its power to their help. Each of the Calls is a separate, 1-5 merit which calls upon the aspects of the ground beneath their feet. A sorcerer may not try to activate one of the Calls in case their body does not directly touch the ground, and suffer -2 modifier in case they stand upon a "stone conduct" (such as activating it in a building while standing above ground floor), and +2 in case they are beneath the ground (like in a cave, or in a sewer). The Calls themselves are represented in the form of Influences, with all of them being tied either to earth (like sand, stone, mud, etc) or things are are connected to earth (plants, graves, fortitude, etc). The ST is the one to decide whether a certain Call is appropriate. In order to activate a Call, the sorcerer has to roll for [Call]+[Manipulation] and spend a special resource known as Chthonic Force. A sorcerer may only spend 1 Chthonic Force per turn, yet in order to activate a Call they must pay a number of point equal to its rating. Dramatic Failure would cause a Call effect equal to the one the sorcerer wished to preform, but it would turned against the sorcerer instead. Exceptional Success gives the sorcerer the Inspired Condition, as the might of the world's bones fill their body. The sorcerer has an a Chthonic Force pool equal to their Stamina+Resolve, and may only spend one point per turn. Regaining Chthonic Force requires participating in one of the harsh rituals of the cult (full refill), by meditating upon earth (one dot per Success) or claim it from another sorcerer which is bound to you as Chattel.

                            Adelfoi Aimatos- Blood Curses (0-00000, special)
                            The blood assassins are well known for their deadly arts, and their combination of physical harsh training and strange sorceries, they can bring death to their enemies with a blink of an eye. Each Blood Curse is a 1-5 merit, based around a single Condition or Tilt, including supernatural ones. In order to activate a new Blood Curse, the sorcerer needs to have access to two things- a drop of blood from the target and know their true name (not True Name). Once the ingredients are ready, the sorcerer may activate the Endowment by rolling [Curse]+[Occult]. The roll suffers from -1 modifier per 1 km which separates between the sorcerer and the target, and costs one point of Lethal Damage from the caster. In case of Success, the Condition/Tilt is immediately enforced upon the target for one hour per dot in the Curse. Exceptional Success also steals life from the target, dealing them Lethal Damage while healing the cot the sorcerer paid for the act. Dramatic Failure, however, creates the opposite scenario, with the sorcerer suffering another Lethal Damage while the target heals it (or gains a Willpower point, if they are already fully healed).

                            ​Loyalists of the Ptolemaic Dynasty- Entombment (0-00000)
                            The heirs of the Osiris Reborn cult seek to cross the boundary between the living and the dead in order to become like the undead gods they serve, and by spending a night among the dead they can mimic that divine glory. Entombment is a single, 1-5 merit which represents the ability of the sorcerer to cross the line between which separates life and death and transcend them both. In order to activate that power, the sorcerer has to spend a day and a night in a tomb next to a corpse and roll for [Resolve]+[Composure] by the end of the night. In case of a Dramatic Failure, the sorcerer's mind has managed to pierce the veil, yet it was maddened by the horrors to which it was experienced. Gain the Broken Condition. In case of Success, the sorcerer's body is transformed by the experience, as Sekhem rush into their body and infuse it with unnatural gifts. The sorcerer may gain any Affinity which its Pillar prerequisites is equal or lower the Endowment's rating, when the total rating of all Pillars needed for the Affinities is equal to the sorcerer's Entombment. The sorcerer may access those Affinities for a week before they require preforming the ritual once more, even though they may try again if they wish to change their Affinities. Failure in such attempts would remove the access the sorcerer previously had. Exceptional Success also gives the sorcerer a single Clue or a Willpower point. While the sorcerer may try once more using the Entombment ritual, using it too early damage the mind of the user- doing it more than once a week is considered a Breaking Point with a negative modifier which is equal to the number of the days which are still needed to use the ritual "safely" divided by 2, rounded up. Sorcerers may only access Soul Affinities using Entombment.

                            Parangelia Seth- Isfet (0-00000)
                            The time the cult has spent with the Lifeless and the prophesies they gained from their patron has taught them all kinds of dark secrets unknown to the common men. By invoking their patron's Name and Will, they can cause awaken the wild powers of Sekhem into life, and trap those wild energies into physical form. Isfet is a single, 1-5 merit which can be used in order to create temporary relics. By rolling [Isfet]+[Craft] and paying a Willpower point, the can cause the local Sekhem to crystallize and solidify into an item. The created relic can mimic any existing mimic or have new powers of its own, yet it does not carry a curse. In order to activate a certain relic, the sorcerer must use a command word which was decided during the item's creation. The effect lasts for a single Scene. The relic may be used for a number of times equal to the sorcerer's Endowment before the occult power fades away. Gaining an Exceptional Success while creating the relic allows the sorcerer to use the relic twice as much, while Dramatic Failure enforce a Curse upon the sorcerer which lasts for one day per [Isfet]. At any case but Failure, however, the Curse is activated upon anyone who enters the place the relic was created for as long as the relic still exist, for the act defiles the world around it.

                            Circle of the Stars (a,k.a the Strangers)-Golden Apple (0-00000, special)
                            The ancient order of the Strangers has learned to movement of the heavenly bodies and their influence upon the River of creations and destruction, and by invoking the right stars, names and gods, they may tap upon the energies which flow in the world and use them to enhance their chosen Hero. Golden Apple is a single merit, rated 1-5. Each Endowment represent a certain recipe, and which its finale outcome is a potion which could enhance any fruit with a supernatural power. When designing the recipe, choose both the needed ingredients and the powers which are contained in the ritual- which is a combination of Affinities and Dread Powers which their total rating and Pillar prerequisites may not be higher than the Endowment's rating. The ingredients needed for a certain recipe always require a Resources rating equal to that of the Endowment (with one of them always being a small piece of an mummy's flesh of a shard of a relic), while the process of making the potion requires rolling [Occult]+[Intelligence]. Dramatic Failure would make the potion into poison, causing one point of Bashing Damage per dot of the Endowment, while Exceptional Success would also give the one to eat the Apple a Willpower point. Once eating the Apple, the user gains access for all the powers contained in the fruit for the rest of the Scene, even though they need to pay any Willpower cost needed for them.

                            and now, for hunters-

                            Tomb Raiders- Dream Gate (0-00000)
                            While the Tomb Raiders are still small, many great things wait for them in the future thanks for the secret they managed to uncover- the ability to open Dream Gates to the Lairs of a Beast. In order to activate a Dream Gate, the hunter in question must at least know to where they plan to go. In case of a random Lair, they suffer from a -3 modifier for using the Endowment. In case they only know the Beast's general appearance, they suffer from a -2. In case they know their name, they suffer from -1. In case they are in close proximity to the Beast, they suffer no negative modifier. The place where the Gate is opened must be of the same nature as the target's Lair. The act itself requires rolling [Endowment]+[Manipulation]-[Target's Lair]. In case of a Failure, the Beast immediately knows that someone tried to enter their Lair, and in case of Dramatic Failure they also know where they are. Success would allow entering the Beast's Lair, but the Beast is still aware of the break. Exceptional Success means that not even the Beast knows their defense was compromised. Once in the Lair, the hunter may collect any material or artifact from the Lair, even though they may not carry out more than one item per Intelligence dot. Those items (or the items made from the stolen resources) hold each a single Dread Power based upon the Beast from which they were stolen, and may be incarnated in the real world. The hunter may open the Gate in order to leave the Lair without any roll, but it has to be opened from the inside just from the same place it was open from the outside- meaning going back from where they first entered the Beast's inner world- and must be opened by the same person which created the original portal. A Gate may be opened by another person or in another location, but it requires another roll and it suffers from a -2 modifier, for the Begotten has much greater control on their Lair from inside of it.
