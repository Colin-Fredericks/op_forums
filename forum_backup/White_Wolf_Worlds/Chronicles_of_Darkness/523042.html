    Feed limitation legally imposed

    05-21-2015, 05:31 AM
    I was working on using my city, Triest (Italy) as the setting of a vampire campaign and I'd like to hear some advice.

    One of the concepts I came out is a feed limitation: by decree of the Invictus the Kindreds are divided in two parts: the full citizens and the Young Bloods who are forbidden to drink human blood and have minor civil and political rights.

    This disctintion was imposed to defend the Masquerade, due also to the high numbers of vampires in the city and the spreading of videorecording tools. It forces the Young Bloods to feed from animals like boars and dears which they hunt at the limit of the city [in the real Triest there are many of them nearby the city].

    Nowadays the maximum number of full citizens is set and won't go higher. This leads to a harsh competions between YBs looking for a promotion; a battle of the have-nots which makes them willing to do anything in order to gain their space in society. Quite often YBs are used by the elders because the formers hope to gain the favours of the latters.

    This really helps the mantaining of the status quo. In fact Young Bloods due to low BP have very small access to vampiric powers and are easily terrified by the sight of the more powerful ones.
