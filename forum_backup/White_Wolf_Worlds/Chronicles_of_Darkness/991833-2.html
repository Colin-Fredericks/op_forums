            There are many who thinks casting sympathetically is incredibly useful, but there are some drawbacks I want to list just in case someone is confused by this thread:
            The first is the automatic Mana cost.
            The second is having to use a sympathetic yantra. This will eat one yantra slot and gives you no extra dice.
            The third is that you have to have a sympathetic connection. This means if you want to cast offensively you need to get something that's connected to your enemy, cast Borrow Threads on that object (which eats one of your active spell slots and often requires more than one Reach, making it a Paradox risk for mages who only invest to Space 2 for the sympathetic attainment) in preparation to your spell.

            Space is absolutely one of the most useful Arcana, but casting sympathetically is not as potent or useful for someone with Space 2 compared to someone with Space 4 or 5 who are much better equiped to gain the connections required for the casting and can bolster them to lower Withstand.

            If you instead manage to stalk an enemy you could instead cast via sensory range to gain more potency (due to lower Withstand) and don't have to worry about Ward.


            The best use of sympathetic range my Mastigos has had was breaking out of a Pylon's hideout together with two prisoners and some sweet magical loot. By preemptively setting up a safe escape location with Borrow Threads I managed to Co-locate that location with the two prison cells, the two loot rooms and our own location. (The Outward and Inward Eye was helpful to see all those locations at the same time so I could include more than one or two of them.) Then I just dragged people and loot through while the Cabal's eloquent Obrimos kept the Seers busy.

                                                        You might only see the Nimbus with Active Sight but seeing it isn't really the point. Peripheral Sight triggers at the activation of any magical effect. If a Mage's Nimbus radiates around the target of a building spell, that would trigger Peripheral Sight. Given that the book describes a Mage's Nimbus leaving wisps of magic any time they cast a spell, it doesn't seem outlandish to assume this means it builds when the spell does.

                                                        (While I'm at it, it doesn't say a Signature Nimbus can only be detected with Focused Sight; it says that a Mage may recognize any they've seen before using Focused Sight. Big difference.)

                                                        As for what you can do about it... A great deal, really. Raise your magical defenses, for one. Cast Ward on yourself, attempt to weaken or sever the sympathetic thread that binds you to the caster, raise your defenses with Wards and Signs, cast right back at them... The latter in particular is quite important, since it acts as a useful deterrent against being targeted with this sort of magic.
