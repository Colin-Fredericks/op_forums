                                                            I've come up with some preliminary ideas for merits. Feel free to modify or disregard them as you see fit.

                                                            * Anthropsychic Some say that mortals were created in the image of the gods, others that humanity creates its gods in its own image. In either case, many Demigods remain closer to human nature than most other supernaturals can match. A Demigod with this merit is treated as having a Virtue and Vice in any situation in which it is relevant, including as a prerequisite for additional merits (usually Myth is treated as Virtue and Impulse as Vice, but it could be the reverse under certain circumstances).

                                                            ** Secondhand Glory Part of the Demigod's divine presence rubs off on his closest allies. At the cost of a point of Faith (or Willpower?), the Demigod can designate one person as a boon companion, worthy of respect through his or her closeness to the divine. For the remainder of the scene, anyone effected by the Demigod's halysis will view this person in the same favorable, if inaccurate light as they do the Demigod her/himself.

                                                            ***** Halo Effect Those who follow the Demigod do not question each other's integrity, for all are touched by the same glory. All those effected by the Demigod's halysis treat each other as if under the influence of Secondhand Glory. They continue to pursue their own goals, but assume that the others are somehow working towards the same objectives, even if their actions show the exact opposite. This makes it possible for them to belong to a single organization, although it will behave so erratically that it almost might as well be several different groups.

                                                            0 - **** Religious Affiliation The Demigod has accepted a particular faith in her or his very essence. The Demigod no longer risks receiving "tainted" faith on a failed roll to draw faith from a person or Temple associated with this particular faith. However, this comes at the cost of being further from other religions. Attempts to draw faith from a person or Temple associated with other religions are reduced to a chance die. The cost of this merit is based on how narrowly the Demigod defines his or her religious tradition, and how prevalent it is.

                                                            0 - A tiny sect: there might be two congregations in the immediate area. The Demigod is unlikely ever to find any others without personally recruiting new adherents. At this point drawbacks balance benefits, so this does not cost any merit dots.
                                                            * - A small niche religion, small denomination, or sub-group of a larger denomination (e.g. Conservative Southern Baptists)
                                                            ** - A large denomination or equivalently large religion (e.g. Catholics)
                                                            *** - A major world religion (e.g. Judaism, Islam, Hinduism)
                                                            **** - An entire family of religions (e.g. Abrahamic religions)

                                                            Not the daimon, just a namesake.
