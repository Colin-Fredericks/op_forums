    Establishing the Standard Model [Cosmology Stuff]

    01-31-2025, 05:04 PM
    So I've tackled the subject of cosmology numerous times throughout my time as CofD fan, to certain amount of success. The thing is that, essentially, I tend to take the pluralist attitude towards the game's cosmology- basically, that there are numerous forms of "truth" that coexist without a true, unifying factor. However, I believe that in general, my attitude may be too pluralist, and as such I may need to take it down a notch if I wish to ever have a complete description of the occult physics that governs the CofDverse. As such, while I still support a pluralist attitude, I think that having a more focused concept may prove itself beneficial to us all in the long run.

    Now, in here I will start by including only canon and some canon-adjacent material (including my own head canon here and there), and then move to fanon and even integration of WoD to the setting in the long run, and I plan to develop this project going gameline by gameline. However, before we start doing so, we need to have a few assumptions (like any model requires). Those assumptions are essentially empirical- that is, they are derived from general trends that the setting suggest about how the cosmology is meant to function, and by defining them we can both test them and see what we "predict" that certain forces in the setting should have, even if not stated otherwise.

    Got it? Great. Let's start from the assumptions-

    1) The World is Pluralist- there is an assumption that there is no singular "truth" to the world. We derive it from the existence of the Pax Arcana and how symbolic entities do not interfere with one another, how the Arcana themselves in Mage lack a "unified Arcana" hinting on the existence of 10 Truths + Paradox (and potentially others), and that numerous monsters have limitations and immunities for affecting and being affected by other forces.

    2) The Soul is the Source- in almost all cases where supernatural power is added to a mortal entity, or modifications happen to a supernatural entity, it is represented by a changes to the soul or have a soul-replacement. The more severe the transformation, the more obvious and deep the supernatural power becomes. Many times, soul replacements are sources of supernatural power by themselves, like Geists, Horrors or Pyros.

    Conclusion 1- the Soul is Pluralist
    The Soul is composed of five supernatural aspects, be them the Subtle Arcana or the Pillars,. Held together, they form a soul, and any modifications to them could affect the operation of the soul. One could claim, however, that the soul is fundamental, that is, that it is a deeper, unified truth which can not be described through a singular Arcana- that's the Tremere's claim, at least. However, taking into account how the Arcana generally work, it seems to be counter-intuitive. Through most of how Awakened Magic work, combination of Arcana is required to manipulate numerous phenomena at the same time, and as such the idea of the soul as a fundamental entity seems to be the only example for going against that rule, making it less likely to be true. Instead, the Fivefold Soul Theory of the Arisen seems to be the more correct one, especially considering that Mummies deal with souls regularly.

    3) Templates are Important- this assumption is made to support the claim of the assumption (2), and could in fact be considered as a derived implication to it, yet it relies on different empirical evidence. Essentially, we can see that not all Templates are made equal- some entities can turn to some Templates, and while some Templates are permanent others can evolve, change or be discarded. For example, a mortal can become either a vampire or a mage, yet a vampire can't become a mage- though the later can become an Enraptured or a Tremere, yet not both. As such, it is safe to assume that there is a fundamental difference between Templates, and that they are mutually exclusive.

    Conclusion 2- Templates are Soul Products
    As we see that empirically, the more permanent the Template the more profound is the transformation, and we assume that all transmutations are expressions of soul modifications, it is likely that Greater Templates are related to cases where the soul significantly transforms or is outright replaced

    Conclusion 3- The Supernatural is Thermodynamically Stable
    And almost all instances of templates, there is no reversibility- the only case it could be claimed otherwise is for Prometheans, but even then a huge amount of energy, or Work, is needed to be invested in order to turn something which is already supernatural into mundane. However, it is comparatively very easy for a mundane entity to become supernatural. The fact that there are different paths for supernatural power which do not intermingle easily is a result of assumption (1), yet in general, the more su0pernatural the entity the easier it is to become more supernatural. Turning from a mortal to a vampire is relatively easy, and for the vampire gaining the Bloodline it is easier compared to the mortal becoming a vampire. The world is drawn towards supernaturality.

    3) Resources Represent Forces- in the real life Standard Model, the fundamental forces are mediated through a set of quantized particles. In the CoDverse, we can see that different supernatural phenomena are mediated in the same way- vampirism moves through Vitae, spiritual phenomena are represented through Essence, Glamour is used to mediate the Wyrd to the world, etc. Those entities are also quantized, as we know that there is a quanta of Vitae in game (a point of Vitae). As such, it is possible to claim that the fundamental forces must have a certain supernatural resource corresponding to them in order to activate

    Conclusion 4- Forces Hold the Soul
    We concluded that the soul is a composite entity, and that supernatural power comes from interactions with the soul. Also, it is well known that most supernatural resources could be harvested from mortals, generally with higher yield. As such, the different supernatural resource units not only interact with the soul, but are likely composing parts of the soul itself to a certain degree.

    4) Ephemera Mediates Forces- supernatural forces, at large, seems to be foreign to the world. Most of them seem to emanate from spiritual realities, which host their own species of supernatural entities. As such, it is likely that each type of ephemeral entity correlates to a certain realm, and each realm correlates to a certain force. While some realms may be a result of composite interactions, there should be fundamental realms of existence which represent the fundamental forces, and which are mediated through ephemeral beings

    Conclusion 5- Forces are Aware
    If ephemeral entities are assumed to be derived from the forces, then one should take into account the existence of their Rank- lower entities are subjected to greater entities, which generally implies the existence of a potential divinity who rules over all forms of ephemera of the type. Those entities are often associated with the realms in which they dwell, and as such they represent a coherent existence which rules over lower phenomena- the God Machine rules over Angels, the Principle manifest Qashmalim, Gaia may be the Shadow itself. If beings of ephemera mediate the forces and supernatural resources actualize it, then it is safe to assume that the will which mediate those entities and acts through the resources is that of the divine beings. As such, the forces are literal will of the gods, and which manipulate the world through their corresponding resource and derived entities

    So I think those are enough base assumptions for us to work with, as well as initial conclusions to help us guide in the correct direction. Basically, the model suggest that all supernatural phenomena could be traced to some kind of a fundamental force, which could break up to less fundamental forces the further they are from reality, as well as the potential creation of composite interactions. With that in mind, our role would be to identify the fundamental forces, and we are going to start in the next post- with Vampire.

          So, it has taken me a while, but I finished my Requiem survey- and with that, we can finally start analyzing the place of Vampire in the setting.

          Following our assumptions, the crux of vampirism should be how it affects the soul. While there has been some discussion about whether or not vampires have a soul or that the Beast replaces it in a manner similar to a Geist or a Horror, I think that between the numerous call out for the vampire souls across the gamelines (Diablerie being a case in point), how the Tremere section in Nameless and Accursed puts vampires in the section of "entities with souls", and more importantly, the Contagion Player Guide outright stating it, I believe it is safe to say that vampires, even though they are dead, do have a soul. That is a curiosity in the grand scheme of things as most dead things lack souls, which might be the reason for why vampires require Vitae- for when a soul is trapped in a dead flesh, there will be consequences, and as it seems, vampirism is the consequences.

          However, the vampire soul is not a cohesive entity- in most instances, it is presented in a dualistic manner. You have the Man, the source of Humanity which is closely related to Theban Sorcery, and you have the Beast, a predatory thing which drives the vampire into madness and is the source of their supernatural Potency and Cruac. There is a lot of discussion about whether or not the Beast is a corruption of the vampire's own soul/self or if it is a parasitic entity which crawled from some Lower Depth to grasp at the a corpse as a vessel. Some entities, such as the Strix, even claim to be the Beast, and their capacity for Synthesis support that claim- though there is a question about whether or not they are THE Beast or if they are A Beast. This is the funny thing about vampirism- in a way, it is the equivalent of supernatural, cosmological entropy. All forces can interact with vampirism by canon (see Imperial Mysteries), though they are forbidden to reverse it. This is going to be very important in this essay, but it is important to keep in mind that vampirism is likely an entropic force- like rubber, or heat. Not a real force, but something that could behave like it, and is found as the product of all forms of work, like a parasite.

          Now, that means that if we are to understand the nature of the vampire soul, we need to understand the Beast and its affects on the vampire existence. In order to do so, we should probably turn to the experts- which are (unsurprisingly) the Ordo Dracul and (more surprisingly) the Belial's Brood (the 1e editions, we will talk about 2e later). Starting from Old!Brood, their philosophy divides the soul into 3 main pieces- the Penuma, which is strongly related to the soul and Humanity, the Soma, which is strongly related to the Beast, and the Sarx, which is the flesh. That puts an interesting emphasis- because even though Penuma and Soma are intuitive, Sarx being a crucial part of the vampire existence is something to note. For the Ordo Dracul (which have experimented on vampires more than any other covenant), things become even more complicated- while their Coils have changed properties and naming conventions between editions, essentially, they seem to describe the same truth, that the Coils work by manipulating the different pieces of the vampire soul. And how they express it? Ziva/Soul/Anoushka are all basically the Humanity Coil with different perspective, Wyrm/Beast manipulate the Beast, Banes/Ascendant work around the Curse, Voivode/Blood/Upadana all work around different aspects of the Blood, and then you have the Flesh from 1e, Slumber which deals with Torpor, Locust which deals with Diablerie, Zirintia which claims supernatural powers and Quintessence which deals with the physical Domain. That shows that in their 1e incarnation at least, the Flesh was recognized as a important aspect of the vampire existence that needs to be controlled and studied apart from the Beast, and that the Beast is a triadic entity- the Beast proper, the Blood, and the Curse. That works with the suggested idea that the Curse is a mechanism which the Beast uses to protect itself from worse consequences of interacting with the Sun or fire. This is interesting to note, because that gives some very important context for how vampirism works- not through a singular predatory instinct, but through the Beast claiming the Soul, infusing the Flesh with Blood and binding it with the Curse. Those are the divergent forces of vampirism, with the other Coils governing other important aspects of vampirism even if secondary to those, as we are goin to discuss further.

          But before that, something important to note- within this discussion, we have already made an important observation, that is that both the Soul and the Beast seem to interact through a special medium of Blood Sorcery. However, here we have established that there are numerous other aspects of vampirism, and more importantly, we know that there are numerous other forms of Blood Sorcery. This leads to the question- if the Soul and the Beast have their own Blood Sorceries, does each Blood Sorcery correspond to a different aspect of vampire soul, and does every aspect of the vampire soul connects to a different Blood Sorcery?

          The answer- no, but actually yes.

          Numerous forms of Blood Sorcery clearly connect the vampire to some other forces- and in truth, it is not surprising. We have already established that vampirism is an highly interactive force, as expressed through how vampires are able to adopt the Coil of Zirintia or interact with Wyrm Nests through strange rituals. As such, not every Blood Sorcery relates to an aspect of the vampire existence, but it is not to say that every aspect of the vampire soul does not have its own, independent interaction. After all, as far as we can deduce, Theban Sorcery and Cruac are native to the vampire existence- while they need to be learned, they are quite natural for them and seem to interact directly with aspects of their existence. We even know that under some strong astral manipulation, such in the Golden Age of Islam Era, Cruac have manifested spontaneously within vampires whenever the Beast became too dominant (put a pin on that). As such, let's go quickly through the different Blood Sorceries and get out those we can probably relate to external forces-

          Ahranite Sorcery- probably a Fiend mediated force (Shaddad, by all likelihood, is a Duatic Fiend due to it being a "demon from Irem")
          Lithopedia- probably a Mysterious Places mediated force (remember that Mysterious Places, specifically Sentients, are Ephemeral Entities)
          Vodoun- probably a Ghost mediated force (the Loa seem to be related to the Underworld)
          Kigan- definitely Spirit mediated force
          Therion- likely a God Machine mediated force (while it affects any kind of "angel" or "demon", the relations to the God Machine are pretty obvious)

          That leaves us with Theban Sorcery, Cruac, Gilded Cage, Veneficia, Merges Sorcery, Kimiya, Triadic Evolution. Also, it is important to note that Cruac is further broken up into Styles, as it is basically a mixture of numerous different Blood Sorcery systems, with the main ones being Chaos Cruac, Creation Cruac and Void Cruac. Looking on those, we can easily move Chaos Cruac into the previous list- as it is very likely a result of a Sutekh/Will mediated force. Seeing the remaining Disciplines, we can make the following corresponding connections-

          Theban Sorcery- the Soul, easy peasy lemon squeezy
          Creation Cruac- the Beast, we established that
          Void Cruac- well, it is connected to the Strix, so... a different interpretation for the Beast?
          Gilded Cage- well, theoretically there seems to be a connection between a vampire and their domain, so... Domain?
          Veneficia- some variant of the Beast, I guess
          Merges Sorcery- Oh! Curses and poison and affliction- that's probably the Curse mediated force!
          Kimiya- clearly the Blood mediated force
          Triadic Evolution- well, that's clearly the Beas- wait, but isn't it Cruac?

          So, you see that while the Soul, the Blood and the Curse are pretty obvious, we have way too many claims for the Beast. You could ignore the divisions of Cruac styles and fold Veneficia into it, but even then Triadic Evolution is clearly the Beast's sorcery, though it can only be accessed by the Claimed of the New!Brood. Cruac, however, while limiting Humanity, does not need a compete degradation of the Soul and then a the act of Claiming in order to work. That means... something. And where is the Flesh Discipline? And why the hell does the Domain gets its own system as if it was a soul piece? What is going on?

          Let's take a step back and think about it- we know that at least one subset of Cruac is associated closely with the Strix, who claim mastery over the Discipline. We also know that Cruac can manifest due to astral shenanigans, like the Refrain. We also know that vampires have a strong attachment to the Astral- four out of five of their main Unique Disciplines are mental in origin, with the remaining focusing on shapeshifting (here is the Flesh again, and among the Gangrel of all Clans. Put a pin on it). We also have the whole fun stuff with the Torpor, where while it is unlikely that vampire souls actively go to the real Underworld in this state, it might be that their roam their own Astral reality- a decaying part of the collective human soul, which leads to wherever the Beast have crawled from. As such, it helps us to cement that the Beast has a astral origin, where the Strix lack it. They are Embodied Entities, creatures of smoke and shadows, not of some delicate, astral ephemera. This helps us to answer the previous question- the Strix are a Beast, but not the Beast, yet both have connection to Cruac. So Cruac comes from something older and more primordial which, when interacting with the human soul, created the Beast, one which the Strix can subsume to a degree. That, in turn, leads to a question- if the Beast is the "evolved" form of the Strix which have anchored themselves into humanity's soul, slowly evolving to become more "human" and successful predators, what exactly are teh Claimed, and how it is related to the even more complicated state of being in the Hollows?

          Here is the thing- the Claimed are soulless beings. Their Humanity is dead, and in a state similar to slasherhood, the Beast either devours the soul or that the soul simply decays and dies. However, something happens to the Claimed- is it the Beast managing to evolve and mimic Humanity, like demons in Frieren? Or is it something more complicated? Let's look on the Hollows as reference- the Soul is clearly separated from the Body in the form of the Reflection, or the Ka. However, the Hollow vampire is still a thinking being, and is independent from the Soul. That implies that the Mind of the vampire is separated from the Soul proper, in the same manner as Soulless people maintain their identity even though it quickly degrades without a soul. There is some aspect of Humanity, a Shell, that the Beast is able to grasp upon in order to create a functional identity. This Shell is what the Beast is Claiming, and what allows it to evolve, becoming a new entity. This Shell, of something dead and broken which can not evolve yet holds to the pieces of its past. This Shell is the vampire's own ghost.

          Yeah, vampires are dead stuff. Obviously they have ghosts.

          This explains why Triadic Evolution can't work until the soul decays- ghosts can only take priority after the soul leaves, that is after the vampire goes through true "death". That's when the Beast generally takes over, and given enough time and motivation it could find the ghost and devour it, Claiming the body's own memories and evolving. Finally, it manages to gain hegemony over the body, becoming the true predator it is meant to be.

          Ok, that solves Triadic Evolution, but what about the rest of the of the oddities? Well, Cruac is divided into two aspects, and due to the fertility and procreative aspects of Creation Cruac, I tend to connect it to the Flesh, while Void Cruac is the Beast's primordial magic. The connection between Creation Cruac and the Flesh is stronger due to the Gangrel- they are the Clan the closest to the Beast, and they hold strong flesh manipulation abilities, implying a connection between the Flesh and the Beast through Cruac. Gilded Cage and Veneficia are the oddities- we can't say for sure that they come from the vampire, but we can't disregard it either. Veneficia seems to be connected to the gods, invoking their Boons in a manner different from Cruac- while using Cruac, the vampire becomes a god, while in the case of Veneficia, the vampire serves a god. However, we also know that Roman Religion was pretty parasitic, taking over other mythologies and devouring them whole, so whatever the "Lares" might be, Veneficia is pretty vampiric. It could very well be that, just like how the Strix and the Beast diverge from the same "tree", the Lares are also up there, a different form of Beast... Though, the Lars seem to be deeply intertwined with the household and family, which might mean some connection to the astral or the ghost within the vampire? Still, not enough proof to support it, only that we can assume that (a) they exist (b) they diverge from the Beast. Gilded Cage is an even more complex issue- in a vacuum, it is very tempting to simply fold it as Sekhem mediated force, yet the fact that the Ordo Dracul and numerous other Disciplines allow vampires to interact with their Domain. So both Veneficia and the Gilded Cage seem to interact with forces who are external to the vampire existence yet related to the principle of vampirism. With that in mind, we are likely to be able to build the following "force diagram" for vampirism-

          Vampirism --> Soul [Theban Sorcery]
          --> Monster --> External --> Bonds [Veneficia]
          --> Domain [Gilded Cage]
          --> Internal --> Flesh [Creation Cruac]
          --> Blood [Kimiya]
          Penuma --> Curse [Merges Sorcery]
          --> Beast --> Shadow [Void Cruac]
          --> Ghost [Triadic Evolution]

          So, assuming that this is the force hierarchy within vampirism, we know that (a) each of those forms of vampirism should be related to an ephemeral entity (b) each should be related to Template. While I plan on making claims on independent Templates in the next post (this one is growing long enough without us bringing other monsters into question), we could discuss how they are represented in the form of Evolved Templates, such as the Claimed. It is pretty clear, however, that Evolved Templates" shouldn't include the External Expressions, due to their less essential connection to vampirism, but the others are fair game. So- what do we have here?

          Aspect -- Blood Sorcery -- Entity -- Evolved Template

          Soul -- Theban Sorcery -- Blood Angels (following Longinian Canon) -- Daywalking (a better name for Gloconda, think Raguel from Mythologies. Yeah, looks like a very bad consequence)
          Curse -- Merges Sorcery -- Edimmu (they were summoned through it) -- Whatever Dracula has become
          Beast -- Void Cruac -- Strix -- Claimed
          Ghost -- Triadic Evolution -- Black Dogs (have been theorized to be Beasts incarnated in Mythologies) -- Hollow
          Flesh -- Creation Cruac -- [???] -- Nictuku (specifically the form presented in the Nosferatu update)
          Blood -- Kimiya -- [???] -- Methuselah (by all definitions those are vampires who embraced the Blood)

          So who are the missing entities? What about other mutations of the Blood, like revenants and dhampirs? Where are the non-Kindred vampires? How is Malkavia connected to all of this? I would have loved to answer this, but the hour is late and the post is long, so this will wait for the next time. But before we go, let me tell you one word.

          Worms.

          Worms are important.

          See you soon!

                  Mummies seem to have some pretty decent, broad understanding of the cosmos. Mages have decent theories but are a biased source, both mundane bias and their ability to impose that bias onto the world around them. The Mummies, after all, seem to recognize the existence of Helios, the Dark Mother, the God Machine, Gaia, and Father Wolf (Re, Iset, Ptah, Azar, and Anpu) as well as acknowledge some secrets that they shouldn't know (The existence of the Supernal, Father Wolf's fall, the corruption/death of Gaia).

                  And their conception of the highest powers is dualistic. At the top of the food chain, it comes down to two "entities"--Nothing and Everything (Nu amd Khephera). But both of these abstracts are entirely useless concepts, even by their metaphor and mystical jargon. Thus, more notable, the two most important beings in the cosmos/tellurian/multiverse are "The Ability, active or potential, for Something That Exists to Stop Existing" (The Jaws and Hunger of Ammut) and "The Ability, active or potential, for Something to Start Existing" (Atum). So, in theory, "the One" would be that--the god-being that is, itself, everything. But such a being would be vast beyond conception. But could, theoretically, be broken down
                  into subsouls and subaspects much like a human. The ur-soul could be the Supernal Realms. Its Spirit would be the cosmic overcelestine bigger than individual worlds, stars, and moons. Its ghostly shadow is the entire Underworld leaking down into Ammut's gullet down to Oblivion. Its body is the material world and its complex Phenomena, and the God-Machine that is emergent from its thermodynamic balance. Its Dreams are the vastness of the Sea Ouroboros and individual Animas Mundi. Etc.

                  The Wyrd/Ma'at and the like do, as theorized, represent a form of thermodynamic homeostasis of an enclosed system. Shu and Tefnut, Yin and Yang, Stasis and Principle, etc, can all represent whether the forces are currently at rest/stable or in motion/flux, and the Resources quantify that.
