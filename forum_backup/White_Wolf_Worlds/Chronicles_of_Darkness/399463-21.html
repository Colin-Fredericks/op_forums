                        The existing points of comparison for 2e:

                        Humanity costs experience to raise; it exist as a trait largely for the purposes of losing it.

                        Harmony is costless and can break up or down, though the extreme ends of the scale are harder to get out of than the midpoint; the nature of Harmony 0 in particular makes it very difficult to rebalance without external aid.

                        Wisdom, like Humanity, costs experience to raise and exists as a trait primarily to make "high-Integrity monster" something you have to work at; it also uses special experience and has a specific prerequisite.

                        Pilgrimage, like Wisdom, can only be raised in certain circumstances with a particular type of experience; furthermore, it's actually impossible to lose your last dot in it.

                        Clarity is a little closer to Harmony, in that it costs no experience to raise and can vacillate wildly across a chronicle; unlike Harmony, you can't get back from a rating of zero at all and you have to put time into getting a higher rating (though the window for doing so gets smaller as your Clarity drops and larger as it gets higher) or else engage with a limited-use shortcut that almost inevitably complicates your life.
