    Socouyant: The Hollowing (a fangame in the works)

    12-08-2016, 05:11 PM
    This topic has been one that's been brewing on my mind, and after seeing the abundance of CoD fanworks, I decided "why not?" Anyways, I decided to base it on the Caribbean Soucouyant, a Vampiric witch that traditionally appears in the guise of an old lady, but sheds its skin at night to reveal its true monstrous form (a giant winged fireball). Of course, the details for the Soucouyant here will be different so as to be adapted to suit a variety of characters. Alrighty, here's what ideas I have so far for this custom splat, all suggestions are welcome.

    Themes

    Be careful what you wish for: As is the case of most Soucouyants, as they awaken within the skin of the person who birthed their envy and discontent; Soucouyants, the ones who try to keep themselves as decent human beings at least, realise the price of fulfilling their own desires is to ultimately become a skin-stealing, life-drinking monster.

    The Void that can never be filled: Once they open their eyes upon the first day of their new existence, the Soucouyant feels a constant emptiness within them that only feeding upon the Essence of other sapient beings can relieve...if only for a time. Failure to even attempt to fill it has its consequences, some of which are worse than death.

    The Storm Within: For every person that a Soucouyant fully Hollows out, the memories and personality of that individual stick with their consumer until death or other fates take them. In due time, a Soucouyant may acquire so many personalities and memories, that their own identity becomes threatened. Such an individual could find themselves falling into two fates, one that is worse than death, the other becoming an empty monster that is truly no one.





    What are Socouyants?
    A pertinent question indeed, for many are the questions that have stemmed from the Caribbean where these creatures were reported? The locals say they are witches, adorned in the skins of old women, that sell the blood of their victims to a Demon from Hell for more power. More nuanced scholars of the occult suggest that there is more to the tales than what appears, and they would be right...if only they knew full extent of the nature of what they studied. To begin with, a Soucouyant (also known as a Hollow) comes into existence as a result of many desires emphasised in the World of Darkness: the desire to be someone else, the desire to be someone better and the desire to actually be a completely different person. "But how can this result in a person becoming such a monster?" you ask, well, for some humans, the aforementioned desire becomes so strong, that it is said that their souls emanate a certain call...a call that is answered by something beyond the realms of men. A being known only as the Maw to occultists, it is known in folklore by the name of Bazil. It is a hungry entity who's appetite can never be sated, for the Maw hungers for the Essence that lies within all beings...yet its reach is limited within the physical world. To acquire its never-ending fill, the entity marks those who absolutely radiate overwhelming envy, ambition and desire (or a twisted combination of all three) or those who actually petition it for power. Once the Maw bestows its mark upon an individual, the change occurs in their sleep; whilst it is occurring, the person is beholden to a vivid dream, where an entity wrought of flame communes with them in a tongue that they cannot understand, yet strangely alluring. This figure then begins to expand, threatening to the engulf the dreamer...and when they are engulfed, their eyes snap awake with a rapid breath. Upon their awakening, they will begin to notice something strange; they awaken in clothes that are not theirs. When they get to their feet, they notice something odd about their height, they're taller than they previously were. As they go over to their wardrobe, that's when they notice something horrifying; there, hanging before them like a casual item of clothing, is their now-empty skin. Recoiling in horror, they race to the mirror, to see for themselves the horror that has befallen them; their before them, stands the target of their darker emotions...yet they are looking through their eyes. Within moments, a sensation of emptiness manifests within the person, gnawing at their very being as their target's memories flash rapidly within their minds whilst fighting down personality quirks that were never theirs. The person is now a Soucouyant, a servant of the Maw and cursed to bear an emptiness that can never be filled, only relieved by the lives of other beings. Upon removing the stolen skin, the Hollow sees their true form; a being of otherworldly flame, with powers given to better serve the Maw's appetites. It cares not for how the servants handle their curse, only that they give it their due.

    Now then, as well as the physiological changes, the Hollow undergoes several psychological changes as well; the emotions that drew the Maw's attention are greatly heightened and twisted into a parody of what they originally were, whilst the rest of their emotions remain relatively unaffected...if they had them to begin with. For example, a female Soucouyant could easily find herself being attracted to a handsome model; therefore, her twisted sense of desire could potentially result in an equally twisted result. In this case, the Hollow could end up desiring the object of her affection to be apart of her for eternity, thus resulting in her completely Hollowing the unfortunate man and her wearing him in a perverse emulation of romance and togetherness. However, this pales in comparison to the two potential fates that can befall the Maw's creations; it is well known that the Hollow absorb the memories and personalities of their victims, but there comes a point when those absorbed aspects begin to overwhelm them. As a result, they find themselves having to fight to keep their Individuality intact, lest those two fates come to the fore. The first of these fates amounts to what is multiple personalities; something ultimate snaps within the Soucouyant, thus allowing the personalities they've absorbed to take over their bodies. Indeed, when one of them is in control, it would ultimately prefer the skin that originally belonged to it in life and can easily go unnoticed by more mentally-intact Hollows; its easy to mistake it for the individual really 'getting into character'. As such, this state is referred to as Manybound. The last of the two is inherently considered inherently worse; for some individuals, the strain of so many personalities inhabiting a single entity ultimately causes a psychological implosion of sorts. They ultimately begin to see themselves as no one, their stolen identities becoming a means to an end. Such creatures live only to feed, and are considered among the Maw's favourites, since they are clearly not fussy in who they hollow. These are the Husks, and most Soucouyants slay them on sight...most anyway.

    The Hollowing and the Void
    When a Soucouyant thinks of the Hollowing, they think of two things; the way in which they feast upon the Essence of other beings, and Void that this the result of the ravages of their maker's hunger upon them. To examine the first of the two, the Hollowing as it is for feeding extracts the necessary nourishment for the Soucouyant's Void; in order to carry it out, the individual simply grasps the subject, and with mental effort, begins to extract the Essence from their victim. A sense of euphoria rushes into the mind of the feeder as their victim's memories flare into their minds; for the victim in question, they ultimately begin to lose pieces of their memories. Now, most Hollow generally only take as much to ease the pain of their Void before moving onto another person whilst their most recent meal gradually regains their memories Some Socouyants with a vengeful or righteous streak my continue to feed until there are no memories or personality left. However, sometimes euphoria overwhelms the individual, or they are feeding with the intent of completely filling themselves of their victim's Essence for something more. Regardless of the reason, the result is always the same; as a side effect of a full Hollowing, the victim is reduced to nothing more than an empty skin.

    Ultimately though, a similar thing happens to the Soucouyant as well, a Hollowing of their own; the mark upon them result in their own lifeforce being siphoned off to further feed the Maw. This gives birth to the Void that is present within all their kind. The Void is a sort of draining emptiness that siphons at the very Essence of the afflicted; to relieve it, the Hollowing eases the pain of the Void, temporarily filling it until their master claim its due. Those who fail to feed their master find themselves suffering the same fate as their victims; memories slip away as agony creeps in. The Soucouyant's fiery form becomes like ice, brittle and easy to shatter as their powers fade. Eventually, the Maw claim its final due and thus drains the last of the Soucouyant's essence. Now, as the Hollow's existence continues, their Void becomes ever greater, requiring more to fill whilst fully Hollowing other Soucouyants tends to meld their Voids together (savvy individuals consider it a way of picking up the consumed Hollow's tab). However, the individual is capable of drawing and storing more essence, and essentially grows more powerful as the Void becomes ever greater.

    The Powers of the Hollow
    As a result of the mark bestowed upon them, the Soucuouyants are imbued with all manners of gifts to better aid in the feeding of the Maw. To begin with, as creatures of fire, they are innately capable of manipulating it as thought it were apart and are obviously immune to the effects of extreme heat. As such, they ultimately lack a physically shape, ultimately allowing them to fit within any crevice...and their skins regardless of size and shape. Indeed, due to the peculiar side-effects of the Hollowing process, the skin is preserved indefinitely and is still seemingly alive; it still feels warm to the touch and there is also a pulse within its empty veins. To adorn a skin, the Hollow pours itself within gradually so as to avoid the need to adjust it as little as possible; indeed, a common sign of a Soucouyant being present is a person seemingly adjusting themselves as another enters the room. Should the process be rushed, it could result in a very misshapen skin that not only is a dead giveaway that something is very wrong, not to mention that it can also impede the Soucouyant's movement as they try to adjust the skin to a normal fit. Given their nature, they also cannot die of old age...but can still be killed; bullets, unless properly imbued, would melt but still deal damage, whilst swords must be used carefully. The Hollow's are mostly weak against certain types of rock salt blessed by Vodou Houngans and Obeah practitioners; it burns them as though it were fire, it strips them of their powers and makes a skin unwearable when smeared with it.

    However, these aren't the only abilities that Soucouyants develop; depending on the approach they take towards the Maw's dark blessings, all manner of powers are available according to each Ideology (more on them later.) However there numerous Paths that the Hollows can take; among these are the Bokor, which revolves around using the malign powers that permeate their Voids, the Houngan, which draws upon the powers of the Essences collected throughout a Hollow's life (with different abilities manifesting from combinations of memories and personalities) and the Obeah, which draws upon the parts of the Soucouyant that are still Human, allowing them to blend in better and mask their natures.

    The Ideologies
    With so many attitudes abundant amongst the Hollow, it is inevitable that their population is divided between several groups; with the exception of one, it would be rather inaccurate to call four of the Ideologies factions...they are merely groups of like-minded individuals, though they aren't above working with one another should the situation demand it. Indeed, they are divided into the Children of the Maw, the Penitents, the Society of the Flesh and the Plebeians.

    Children of the Maw
    Knowing that an entity such as the Maw exists, it is inevitable that some of its creations would worship it as a god; these are the Children of the Maw, fanatical zealots obsessed with feeding their foul maker. Whether it is a desire for power or a genuine devotion, all Children serve the Maw, and it is their duty to ensure that the others fall into line with the cult's tenants. Indeed, it is common for infidels to be consumed if they cannot be converted; for them not to honour the Maw is considered the greatest blasphemy and sacrilege in their minds. They embrace the powers that their maker has given them, and the dark appetite that has been bestowed unto them; indeed, many of their rites of worship involve humans being completely hollowed out, and their skins either being gifted unto promising acolytes or ritually hung in a display of devotion to their hungry god. As such, many walks of life can be seen on display in their shrines; Lawyers, Policemen, Army Officers, Homeless and even children can be seen with their skins flapping in some otherworldly breeze. Among the Children, the Husks are greatly revered; from their perspective, such individuals are pure of purpose, completely unfettered by the human emotions that held them back. Whether they adhere to the cult's tenants remain unknown, and it is highly unlikely that the Children don't care. As a result of their heavy amount of hollowing, they have attracted much attention from the Maw, and for that, they have acquired a Path of their own known as the Mawgift; those who use this power are said draw upon the very powers of the Maw itself, even capable of passing on the mark, thus creating more Soucouyants.

    Paths: Mawgift, Bokor and Houngan

    The Penitents
    The other extreme amongst the Hollow, the Penitents are those who ultimately reject the natures bestowed on them by the Maw; upon their first day, something snaps within them as they look upon their first stolen skin. As a result of this, the Penitent ultimately finds themselves vowing to never hollow another person, only doing so to other Soucouyants with a predatory appetite for Essence. As a result of this, members of this Ideology tend to feed rather minimally, just enough so that the Maw does not take them. Whenever they accidentally hollow someone, they take on the skins as a form of atonement; as such, the "wardrobe" of such beings tends to be considered rather bare, with them containing their birth skin, a few atonement skins and those taken from consumed Soucouyants. It is a notorious stereotype that these Hollow encourage their fellows to seek becoming Manybound, though this attitude actually varies. Some Penitents think that becoming Manybound is the perfect compensation for the lives they have stolen; after all, the personalities within completely take over when it is their turn. However, some think differently; they know that this 'compensation' is a mere imitation of the people that they have devoured, and as such, consider it a greater act of disrespect. Soucouyants of this Ideology have ultimately found themselves developing the Atonement Path, a set of powers that gives them advantages against other Hollows as well as extracting Essence from sources other than Humans.

    Paths: Atonement, Obeah and Houngan

    The Society of the Flesh
    Ambition, envy, desire...all Soucouyants carry these personality traits within them; most however try to suppress them, knowing far too well that it was these things that got them in their predicament in the first place. Not so for the Society of the Flesh who let these traits rule them. The Hollows of this Ideology embrace the Hollowing for it grants them their desire to be the top dog. One would think that they would hollow out humans indiscriminately, but this is not so. In fact, they are extremely picky in who they do this to, reserving it for any unfortunate human they see as threatening their position. If a Soucouyant lawyer suspects a human lawyer suspects another is getting the top cases, then their skin will be next in the wardrobe. Same could be said of Soldiers, Models, Actors, Politicians...anywhere that results in holding a high position in society will certain have an abundance of hollowing behind the scenes. Because of their pride, members of the Society of the Flesh rarely leave their skins unless they need to or are changing it for a better one. They are also sentimental, considering previously acquired skins as a sort of macabre trophy; indeed, at Society gatherings, the Hollows actually show off their collections to one another. One may brag about his Mayor piece, while another will be showing off the latest Writer in vogue. Because of their outlook and practices, Society members acquire the Ascension Path; its power grants them the ability to sense the ambitions of a nearby human and to essentially, adopt their personality immediately upon donning their skin to the point of complete flawlessness.

    Paths: Ascension, Bokor and Obeah

    The Plebeians
    Considered the Average Joes among the Hollows, the Plebeians are individuals who try their live a normal life as much as their state allows. Indeed, they see the other Ideologies each as a bunch of deranged lunatics at the very best and monsters at the very worst. As a result, the Plebeians are determined not to fall into any of these, but alas, nobody's perfect; each may feel tempted to join one of the other Ideologies, whilst others completely reject them. Each Plebeian acts according to their own conscious; some actually replace the person they've accidentally hollowed, especially if they had a family, while others may not actually give a damn. They are a varied bunch, embodying the extremes being humane and being a monster.

    Paths: Bokor, Houngan and Obeah
    In another's Shoes
    It had been another shitty day at work for Neil; too much damn paperwork for one thing, someone kept stealing his pens and someone kept eating his yoghurt . Worst of all however, and the very thought of it made his blood boil, was that bastard Peter. Peter, the son of Neil's boss; Peter, who had been made manager at age 25 whilst Neil had been working at getting the job for nearly a decade. A spoiled, trust-fund brat who deserved none of the things he had, for he had poured none of his sweat and blood into attaining his position. He was also a hit with the ladies at the office; with his slicked-back blonde hair, green eyes and finely sculpted body wrapped in the finest Armani suits that daddy's money could buy. Neil liked to think that the ladies chased Peter for his money, but alas, he knew that it was a lie. As he got home to his apartment, he swiftly grabbed a bottle of beer and settled down onto his couch. He drank, and he drank and he drank, until it was empty. Before long, he was onto his fifth bottle until he had finally passed out from all of the alcohol in his system.

    Once more, Neil opened his eyes, only to find himself completely shrouded in darkness...save for a figure of fire standing in the difference, it seemed human in shape, but his gut told him that it was far from being a man. The thing began to speak, but in a tongue that the office worker could not understand; "Sahi'nathrash, uzuhl no kezid...uzuhl no kezid." Though Neil could not understand a single word the thing spoke, yet each syllable breathed forth seemed to enrapture him; the creature had his full attention, its words ensuring that he could not tear his eyes away from it. Before he could begin to walk towards it, he noticed something flicker within the figure's form. A spark flicked from its body...and it started to expand, eventually becoming an amorphous wall of otherworldly flame. Enthralment became terror as the fire approached Neil; he tried to run, but found that his legs would not heed his commands. As the fire drew closer, he could feel its heat upon his skin; sweat welled up as he began to slowly burn, even at such a distance. Discomfort became pain, and pain became agony as the flames finally consumed him. Neil tried to scream, to give voice to the torment that ravaged his flesh, but alas, even his mouth would betray him in the end. The smell of his own burning flesh made him nauseous, his gag reflex being triggered inwardly. Desperate to escape the suffering that plagued him, the office worker drew upon every ounce of his will and with a burst of effort, he finally screamed.

    In the light of the morning, Neil would finally rise from the bed with a brief scream; to his relief, there was no fire to torment him and no voice to enthral him. As he looked around, he immediately began to notice something strange. Now, he could easily assume he dragged himself to his bedroom and easily blacked out there...but he knew very well he wasn't wearing a cuff-linked shirt and an Armani waistjacket and trousers. The closest he had come to something so extravagant was his brother's wedding five years ago; alas, he normally stuck to rather plain suits, shirts and ties. Pondering this strange turn of events, the office worker headed over to his wardrobe, determined to dress in something more practical. As he slid the door open, horror compelled him to leap back onto his bed with a yelp. There before him, hung an empty skin with black hair, a slight stubble, dressed in the purple shirt and tie he had worn for work yesterday. Instinctively, his own hands reached for his face; it was very smooth, as though the hair had been pulled from the roots whilst smelling of expensive cologne. With his horror compelling him, Neil raced for his bathroom; turning on the light, he saw for himself a face that wasn't his. Looking back at him was a face that was aesthetically pleasing, with green eyes and slicked-back blonde hair...this wasn't Neil's face, it was Peter's. He was also taller to boot, feeling awkward every time he made a step. He tried pinching himself, but alas, the pain was too real...this nightmare was all too real. Images then began to swarm his mind; names such as Kelly, Maria and Anna, women who Neil did not know, but knew now; he felt their cases upon him, even though he had never been kissed by them. The images flashed again, revealing the last sights that Peter had witnessed; he had been heading home from a nightclub, only to notice his subordinate approaching him in a trance-like manner. With a single touch, all things became blackness. In that instant, Neil finally understand what his predicament now was; he had stolen Peter's skin, his memories, his very life. Yes, he had fantasised about it, but he never actually wanted it to come to this...did he? Ultimately, Neil began to wonder about that strange dream he had...did it have something to do with why he was wearing another man's flesh? That would explain it, if only it didn't seem so insane...then again, that he was looking through Peter's eyes proved that he was perfectly sane. It was then that a phone began to ring, it was a ringtone that he knew couldn't have come from his own cellphone. He raced to the living room to find an Armani jacket, and so was compelled to draw the phone that he knew was in the inner pocket. Looking at the screen, he saw the word "Dad" and so proceeded to answer back out of compulsion. "Hello" he said in Peter's voice, cementing all that Neil was now enduring that day...and for the rest of them if fate was cruel enough.

                      Namely, a character gets dots in all Mental skills equal to the number of a fully hollowed victim, to represent the knowledge accumulated from a victim's memories. They also get bonuses to dice rolls involving social and mental attributes as well as social skills regarding that host (as well as extra for other circumstances).

                      As for antagonists, well, given their habits, it will certainly attract the attention of the Uratha. However, a Soucouyant's main antagonists are the servants of the Loa, who see it as their duty to banish unwanted spirits back to the other side. On the other hand, they also regularly find themselves in turf wars with non-Maw malevolent spirits, who see the Hollows as muscling in on their feeding grounds. Needless to say, Soucouyants from within and without the Children of the Maw hate each other mutually...albeit outsiders have varying reasons.
