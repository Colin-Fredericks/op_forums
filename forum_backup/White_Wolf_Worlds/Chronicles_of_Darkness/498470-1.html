        Generally, in any 2e gameline, the high numbers represent the more human-like aspects in the games, with the low numbers making you more monstrous. The higher your Humanity, the more like a human, and less like a vampire you are. A high Cover cements you more and more in reality as a real person, as opposed to someone wearing a skin. In Werewolf, Harmony may idealize an average of 5, but high numbers still represent as being more human, and low numbers more monster (wolf-spirit in this case). Even in Beast, a high Satiety makes you more human as you rely less on your Lair and Soul/Horror/Atavisms, and more on Nightmares (though you eventually lose all powers at Satiety 10), while at low Satiety you leave behind the subtle psychic powers for the brute force of your Horror-Soul manifesting strongly in Atavism, eventually culminating in a ravaging beast.


        So, given that thought, I think its safe to say that Pilgrimage will follow the same pattern. They could mess with things, but I don't feel that's going to be one. Starting low and working high feels right. Working high, suffering breaking points that set you back.. the part I'm most curious about, though, is what the new twist on it is going to be. Every game has a twist on the mechanic, and I don't think that just starting low is going to cover it. Humanity ties into all the banes, Harmony idealized 5, Cover can have multiple different instances, and Satiety doubles as the fuel stat. What special thing will Pilgrimage do?

        Pilgrimage measures your journey to the New Dawn. Likely has to do with vitrol and milestones somehow. Maybe the effects of the new disquiet/wasteland, though I suspect that's going to be tied into Azoth, not Pilgrimage. Hmmmm.......

            What is the Pilgrimage going to do? I'm guessing it's going to tell you when you've done "enough" to attempt the New Dawn.You start at three or or lower, and once you hit 7 or even higher, you've got enough of a potential accumulated that you're cleared to try. You say your goodbyes, climb to the mountaintop, and call an ArchQashmal a dick (details subject to change). This is where you (might) roll your Azoth, like you did in the old game. If you're lucky, you climb back own the mountain with some frostbite and amnesia, or you take the shortcut and throw yourself off the peak because you can still survive it.

            I have an idea that when you make the roll, you "bet" Pilgrimage and/or Azoth dots. If you blow it, you lose those dots. The Azoth consumes too much fuel, or those Milestones really taught you nothing. Or the scraps of a real soul you had sewn together just burn in the fire.

            There may be other benefits, naturally. Pilgrimage seems like it could be actively ameliorative for Disquiet and Torment. Since Refinements and Transmutations have had some structural changes, it could tie into those, although the latter is traditionally Azothic. Maybe it could be used to hide from Pandorans and Alchemists (fun fact, it's been 6 months since we actually have gotten an open dev preview).
