                                            Originally posted by Errol216 View Post
                                            It's been forever since I read P:tH and I've never been a huge fan of the magical girl genre, so I don't know much about standard tropes and well-liked subversions, but:

                                            I feel obligated to recommend the webcomic Sleepless Domain. The protagonists so far haven't tipped into monster territory, but a lot of the questions that are coming up about the nature of what they are and where their powers come from is inching its way towards turning it into a Deviant chronicle's backstory.
                                            I love the magical girl genre and I will say that Sleepless Domain is a great webcomic to use as inspiration for P:tH. Its not too dark and edgy all the time but has enough mystery and darkness to really make you question if the characters are actually doing more harm than good.
                                            (I wish there was magical boys too in Sleepless Domain but alas magical boys in almost all media are either not present, not able to become magical boys, are a joke, or are not real magical boys. I've come to accept this lack of magical masculinity by creating it in my own works.)

                                            In all honesty P:tH is a great standalone game, I adore it. But as a crossover game its not very good because P:tH either will completely overshadow everything else which is fine just be straight up about it, and it has no reason why they should not be using charms all over the place in big flashy ways.
                                            My other observation is that while it fits the classic magical girl trope the Inner Light does work but I feel to really capture how even magical girls can actually be pretty crazy ("I beat you within an inch of your life, you're my friend now and must renounce all your personality traits and goals that I dont think are good... or else!" >*v*< ) I, personally, feel a Noble should derive their inner power not from the objective good vs evil but from how much they believe in their cause. This would give some much needed human conflict that isnt just Radiant vs Twilight, instead creating Nobles who are unique people with different beliefs who are doing what they believe is the best they can. Instead of being good vs evil make it about conviction, a creed, dogma, and certainty. Nobles /believe/ their way is the best way and they draw their inner strength from that.
                                            At least thats how me and my husband play it.

                                                    Guest Thanks! I'd like to believe it helps give some much needed ambiguity to the Nobles. Im not a huge fan of moral absolutism if it wasn't obvious. :P
                                                    (It might be a little obvious that Tears is one of my favorite courts. Diamonds, Cups, Wands, and Pentacles following.)
                                                    It takes a little work to adjust some factors while still preserving the intent and feel of P:tH and the theme of hope, but also giving darkness some merit and creating the human state of believing something can be good while others think it bad, and still being a good person. It felt kinda hard to really make characters that were unique that were able to be strong without being penalized for not subscribing to a very specific set of morals, so I changed it to create a more real feel of good vs evil. (At least in my eyes.)
