                            Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
                            Pierce Deception. Does it find absolute truths or just what the liar knows to be false?
                            The entire second paragraph of the spell description is quite clear that what the spell does is identify when the subject of the spell is the recipient of an active untruth or deception, providing the specific example that it can't tell that someone is committing tax fraud just by looking at them but would instead need to see the falsified tax returns.

                            It's notable that Words of Truth has that stipulation specifically to prevent people from fishing for absolute truth by seeing which of their statements benefit from it; Pierce Deception doesn't need it because the closest thing you can get out of a lie-detector/illusion-bypassing spell to that is abstract visions of the disguised truth with the Reach effect.

                                                            Originally posted by quelsec View Post
                                                            Whats the exact condition for needing another arcanum in a spell? when reach is not enough?
                                                            A major reason is when more precision with the Arcanum is not enough. I interpret Serendipity, for example, as using it's Reach effect to parse through the threads of Fate to help you find one that allows you the opportunity to use the skills you want.

                                                            In the case of a Golem, it can only be machine like by itself. You need to create or bind a fully formed mind to give it depth of thought, which is beyond the scope of Matter.

                                                            With spells like Ban, it allows you to recognize the kind of phenomena to permit or exclude. Lastly, additional Arcana can be used to extend the functionality of the spell beyond the Arcanum's purview, such as adding Spirit to Word of Command to activate a werewolf fetish.
