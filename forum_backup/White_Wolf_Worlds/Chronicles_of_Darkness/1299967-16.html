                                        Originally posted by Korogra View Post

                                        I think it is intended to give the Beast Satiety so that they can Expend it on their Atavisms and Lair traits more readily, without being to worries about Starving

                                        I get what the intent is. It's to replenish resources for spenditures, as you say. But Beasts have Satiety Conditions that adjust based on what value the Beast is currently at with their Satiety. And when a Beast hits Satiety 10, they get the Slumbering Condition immediately. This means that the Beast loses access to all of their Atavisms and Nightmares. The Beast is also explicitly restricted from spending Satiety for any reason.

                                        So, it's very bad news for a Beast. Especially for a Beast that would associate with the False.

                                        I don't think this power is meant to be something that screws over the wielder as hard as it does for Beasts.

                                        Edit: Basically, it looks like the writer just knew that Beasts spend Satiety as their resource and went "Well, they get fill up with that then!" without knowing what that actually means for the mechanics of the game. And okay, it's hard to know all the details about all the different gamelines. But The Gentleman Gamer might want to double-check this as to whether it's appropriate as this is a great time to do corrections if they feel something needs to be adjusted, which I think is in their discretion.
