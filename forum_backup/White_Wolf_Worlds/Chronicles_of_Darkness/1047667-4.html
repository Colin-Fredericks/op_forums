    So... I have to just wonder what you'd be taking in place of Sacred Hunt? What's so critical at two dots here? Bottle spirit is pretty kick ass, admittedly, but that's Bone Shadow only. Chain Rage, Totemic Empowerment and Banish are pretty cool, but those are basically where you only need one person in the pack to take, and they're one dot each.

I've never felt that impressed by them before. Handy, I suppose, but they're other "bite the bullet for the pack" type of rituals. Harness the Cycle, a ritual that can be used four times in an entire year?

    And that's not counting Wolfblooded ritualist NPCs that you can add to the pack at any time and can cast a few of the Pack Rites for the pack.

                                                Plus there's difference between 'hunting' and the Sacred Hunt.

                                                Hunting is a natural inclination for every werewolf, it's part of their very being. And it's natural for a werewolf to, upon realizing what they've become, start hunting one way or another.

                                                But they don't belong here. And that hunt doesn't fulfill their spiritual needs for some reason. And they can get angry or try and resist but they'll still feel that pull and maybe they'll become mad butchers or think it's some horrible insanity and lock themselves up.

                                                The Sacred Hunt ameliorates that, bridging the gap between 'the hunt' and 'the Hunt' and allowing the Uratha to fit in, however temporarily.

                                                But it's not something every werewolf need know, because as social people, they tend to share one another's abilities. Wyrdhamster is in a position where, for whatever reason, the ritemaster is out of commission. Depending on their Primal Urge the other members of the pack have an option, where they can just bear through it until the ritemaster is healed, they can seek out any other pack, they can seek out a spirit or deal with their totem, or even just look for ancient werewolf lore and find where the rite has been recorded somewhere. This is a story entirely within the realms of Forsaken's setting. If he wants to have the Firstborn come down and grant every member of the party with free rites, that's fine too, but there's no reason the game should change because one group of player characters are encountering a setback.

                                                It's not a tax, it's not absolutely necessary, though it is obviously recommended for at least one person in a pack to have. It's not a requirement of half the gifts, it's not something every werewolf would know or would even want to know.
