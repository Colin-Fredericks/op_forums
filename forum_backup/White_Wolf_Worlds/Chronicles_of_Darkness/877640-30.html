                    I have been playing Mage the Awakening 2e for about 3 week (played 1e demo twice) and I thought that I would give the spell development a try. i am sure it needs some work but I could use some constructive criticism.


                    Splice Soul (Death •••)
                    Practice: Weaving
                    Primary Factor: Duration
                    Withstanding: Resolve
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Intimidate, Medicine, Occult.
                    This spell can only be cast as a ritual and takes 1 additional casting interval per level of potency (gaining no additional dice from doing so). The mage splices a small sliver of his soul to the soul of a sleeper, creating something very similar to a Soul Stone (pg. 98) with all the benefits and penalties that accompany it, except they cannot be used to create a Demenes, including the fact that this is an Act of Hubris against the Enlightened and Understanding Wisdom. He is a hitchhiker in the others body and is just along for the ride. For the duration of the spell the subject is considered a very loyal sleepwalker. Also as long as they are in the same room all magic cast by the mage with a target of self also affects the sleepwalker at no extra cost. This also creates a strong Sympathetic Connection between the two.
                    The sleepwalker is unaware they have this hitchhiker and the mage has no particular control over the sleepwalker. However since there is a sliver of awakened soul attached to the sleepwalker magic can be cast around him as with any other person that is aware on the truth. The individual does register as a sleepwalker to the various types of detection and if scrutinizes by Death or Spirit magic the sliver can be detected.
                    The mage can temporarily transfer the rest of his soul to the sleepwalker, taking control. For 1 point of mana the mage’s soul leaves his body and travels to the sleepwalker, this travel is not instantaneous unless the mage uses Space magic to make it so. His body gains the Thrall condition while he rides along with the host. The sleepwalker registers as fully awakened for as long as the mage’s soul is in control. The mage is fully capable of casting magic as if this was his true body. This transfer lasts for 1 hour or a scene at best then the mage must travel back to his body.
                    There is a danger in this as the mage’s soul uses the twilight to travel to the host’s body. While in the twilight he is just as susceptible to the hazards therein as any ghost would be, in fact he is the same as any other wandering soul in that realm. The two bodies act as anchors.
                    Lastly if the mage dies while a sliver of his soul is in the sleepwalker he has a chance to make the journey to reunite the sliver with his soul. Unfortunately for the sleepwalker his soul is consumed in the process and this triggers another Act of Hubris. If the sleepwalker dies the sliver returns to the mage reuniting the two, however the soul of the sleepwalker becomes a very angry rank 2 ghost with the mage as an anchor.
                    Even when this spell ends there is a lasting effects as the subject can never truly return to his former lie riddled life, the subject is a sleepwalker with a strong tie to the mage but has none of the benefits of this spell unless this spell is cast again.
                    The Additional Arcana listed below have added benefits and extends the usefulness of this spell and you may incorporate one per potency of the spell. The adders share the casting statistics of the spell when added.
                    Add Life ••: By adding life to the mix the mags may now use the sleepwalker’s senses. He may enter a trance and pay 1 Mana at any time to take control of the sleepwalker’s body. The mage also strengthens his host body as if the host was under the effects of a Body Control spell. This may be used in conjunction with the Mind adder below.
                    Add Mind ••: By adding mind he can communicate with the sleepwalker making his thoughts be known. He may enter a trance and pay 1 Mana at any time to take control of the sleepwalker’s mind. The mage also defends his host mind as if the host was under the effects of a Mental Shield spell. This may be used in conjunction with the Life adder above.
                    Add Space •••: By adding Space he can use the sleepwalker as a Sympathetic Connection. Also the mage and sleepwalker no longer have to be in the same room to share spells. This connection forms a conduit by which he can use his magic through.
                    +1 Reach: The mage may mask his soul from detection by other mages. Any attempt to detect the sliver of the mages soul will cause a Clash of Will.
                    +1 Reach: The mage may make the duration indefinite at the additional cost of 1 mana and a -10 to the dice pool.
                    +1 Reach: If the duration is longer than a week the mage may renew this connection at the cost of one Mana each time that he renews the spell. Renewing the spell in this way resets the duration counter extending the spell.

                    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                    Arcanum Leach (Death, Spirit, or Mind •)
                    Practice: Knowing
                    Primary Factor: Duration
                    Suggested Rote Skills: Academics, Occult, Science.
                    The mage names an Arcanum and casts the spell at a target. If the target possesses the named Arcanum the leach will attach to their soul. Once attached it will leach a small amount of arcane energy every time a spell is cast of the type named. This does not hinder the casting in any way and the drain is so slight that the only effect felt by the mage is a slight nausea. Once 10 spells have been siphoned the leach is full and drops off. Detached it sprouts wings and enters twilight flying back to the caster. If the duration of the spell ends before it siphons enough energy then falls to the ground and dissipates. The caster is aware that the leach is dead.
                    The caster can devour the leach and gain knowledge of the Arcanum siphoned. (In game terms the mage may spend experience in the named Arcanum without a tutor, the spell needs a potency equal to the level of knowledge learned.)
                    +1 Reach: You can use the stolen energy to turn a turn an untutored legacy attainment into a tutored legacy attainment.
                    +1 Reach: The leach can be set to siphon of skills in which case it takes 10 days to collect the information desired before returning to the mage.

                        Invest Soul (Death •••)
                        Practice: Weaving
                        Primary Factor: Duration
                        Suggested Rote Skills: Empathy, Medicine, Occult
                        The mage primes a living target to serve as a vessel of their Gnosis. The target, who must be a Sleepwalker with a Strong connection to a mage included in the Scale factor of this spell, may be made into a soul stone by any mage under the effects of the same spell (the caster does not require extra spell factors to be included).

                        For the Duration factor of this spell, a number of mages up to the Potency of this spell may imprint their Signature Nimbus on the target and spend a Willpower dot, ignoring the normal Size 2 limitation for soul stones. While the spell lasts, the subject counts as a soul stone for all purposes and gains a number of dots in the Banner-Bearer Merit equal to the number of mages using them as a soul stone (this may take them above the Merit's normal maximum rating of three dots, though dots above the fifth "fall off" unless the caster spends a point of Mana at the time of casting).

                        If the subject is used as the basis for a Demesne, the Demesne persists only as long as they are alive and within the bounds of the location the Demesne was made in (e.g. a Demesne forged from a Sleepwalker acting as a soul stone for three members of a cabal will be disrupted if they leave their church Sanctum).

                        While serving as a soul stone for a mage, the target is subject to the Nimbus Tilt of that mage for the duration of the spell.

                        +1 Reach: The target may be a Sleepwalker with a Medium connection to a mage included in the Scale factor of this spell.
                        +1 Reach: The target is not subject to the Nimbus Tilt of a mage whose soul has been invested in them.
                        +1 Reach: The target may treat the number of soul stones they serve as as a Supernatural Tolerance Trait, adding to relevant contested rolls and allowing them to store up to the spell's Potency in Mana.
                        +1 Reach: The target and any mage whose soul stone they serve as may share their senses as a reflexive action, much like a mage's familiar.
                        +2 Reach: As above, but the target may serve as a relay for two or more mages to share their senses.
                        +2 Reach: The target may remain a soul stone even after their death; as long as the spell's Duration persists, a portion of the dead subject up to Size 2 remains a soul stone.

                            #1) This is, to be frank, a completely overdesigned mess.

                            This is why I posted it for comments and criticism, as I started I am new to this entire way of thinking about mages and magic. If I do not get a grasp of this system then I will not be able to convince my face to face group to try it. So I joined a game on a PBP site to try and learn the rules of this system, this is my next step in attempting to bring the system into focus.

                            I have not submitted the two spells posted to the Storyteller on the PBP site because I do not think he is allowing Creative Thaumaturgy, I can see where it would slow the game down even more while they have a discussion about a new spell, stopping the game progression while it is going on.

                            So on to answering your question as best as I can. I do appreciate your critiques, but I can tell in your writing style that you are somewhat frustrated with this bumbling mess so I will try to be more concise with my explanations so as to explain my way of thinking as an outsider to this system.

                            #2) Why?

                            In my mind this isn’t something that you would do out in the open, slicing off a piece of your soul and splicing it to another person shouldn’t be taken lightly and as a whim. I see instantaneous magic as impulsive and somewhat haphazard, and I do not see this spell as either. Therefore I feel that limiting it to being only “Behind closed doors” is just.

                            As for the second part of that sentence, I was using the potency to limit the number of adders you could put on the spell because it is making the spell more potent. I see all these spells with adders and there is no associated cost for them. In my way of thinking each adder requires another casting interval. Additional casting intervals usually add dice to the spell, this is just stating that the benefit of the extra time is already being used to cast the additional spells.

                            #3) Alright, this seems like a decent simple effect — "invest your Gnosis in somebody through the same channels as making a soul stone" is workable on its face.

                            Ok, not much to comment on here.

                            #4) Alright, now we've lumped in at least one extra effect.

                            In my thinking this is a surreptitious and lasting way of turning a sleeper into a sleepwalker. The soul of an awakened being has been attached to the soul, no spliced to the soul, of a sleeper fundamentally changing the sleeper’s soul, the core and center of who he is. Once again why it shouldn’t be done on the fly. As for the loyal to you part well your soul attached to his soul should make him loyal.

                            #5) Why is this a consequence of the spell and not a prerequisite for its use?

                            May be it should have been worded a different way. The basic gist behind the statement is that with both pieces of the mages soul in close proximity to each other they receive the benefits of the spell cast by the mage. The sympathetic connection is there to be capitalized upon if you have Space. As for being a consequence of the spell and not a prerequisite, it is because of the spell that allows you to share this without paying for extra scale.

                            #6) Is there any meaningful reason that "the target is a Sleepwalker for the duration" is the outcome rather than "this spell targets a Sleepwalker"?

                            Because like Stealing Fire it is targeting a sleeper, but unlike Stealing Fire it is targeting a specific sleeper to transform him into a sleepwalker with the predilection for following you. This allows you to handpick a sleeper to groom as a sleepwalker instead of letting random events determine who is and isn’t a sleepwalker. So even if this spell wasn’t already an Act of Hubris, changing the fundamental beliefs of a sleeper would be.

                            #7) This is another effect entirely and also raises the question of what is actually stopping the mage from terminating their spell to return to their own body.

                            There is nothing that stops a mage from terminating the spell and returning to his own body. It is still not an instantaneous trip. Yes I know that a soul returning to his body after it left his body is supposed to be instantaneous. This spell makes it different, once again a limiting factor and adds a hint of danger. The mages body will remain in a thrall condition until the soul returns, so there is danger to both the soul and the body. Benefit: being able to take control of another, Price: danger to your soul and body. Every benefit should have a price, now if you are saying that the price isn’t high enough then I am open to suggesting as to how to increase the price.

                            #8) Understandable drawback, but this is yet another effect.

                            This is just to state that if the mage dies he does not move on instead his soul seeks out the piece of his soul that is still in a living body. The travel through the twilight to get to his remaining soul is fraught with perils but he has a chance to live again if he makes it to the other piece of his soul, of course the destruction of the soul already inhabiting that body is an Act of Hubris. If the sleepwalker dies his ghost follows the sliver of soul back to the mage and haunts him for as long as he lives. The mage is his anchor. He is pissed and he is powerful once again a cost for messing with someone’s soul. If I was the ST and this happened I would also find a way to make the ghost super resistant to the powers of the mage. But the ghost wouldn’t kill the mage because then he would move on and his revenge wouldn’t be complete.

                            #9) "The subject is a Sleepwalker when the spell ends, but with none of the benefits of the spell, the only subject-facing one of which is 'the subject is a Sleepwalker for the duration.'"

                            Poorly worded, all it is stating is that he is a sleepwalker with no connection between him and the mage. This is a way for the mage to say that this guy isn’t who he thought he was so he will drop him. But just because he was dropped doesn’t mean that he would return to the sleeping world as his soul has been changed.

                            #10) You've made this thing a lower-dot Stealing Fire without any of the associated drawbacks along with tacking on about three more effects.

                            First it is the same cost as Stealing Fire, three dots. Second it isn’t meant to do the same thing as Stealing Fire it has a different purpose. And as it states in the side bar “Many Roads” on page 124 spells with the same effect can cost different prices depending on how they are used and or implemented. It has its own drawbacks, for one a continuous strong sympathetic connection between the two making the mage very vulnerable to anyone with Space magic, which I am finding out should be everyone and my character in the PBP game doesn’t have but that is an entirely different can of worms.
