            dhik: thorny weeds or invasive plants; an infestation of poisonous, dangerous or invasive plants
            thilim: an oven used for cooking food
            dhilim: a spoon; a balance pan or shallow bowl
            dhi'im: to check; to approach; to come forward or bring forth cautiously
            thi'im: corpse-scent, or the smell of cadavers whether fresh or old
            dimm: a dead plant
            di'im: the poles that hold up fencing
            dhimhu: the actual plant that legumes are harvested from, not the edible legumes themselves
            di'nih: stubbornness, obstinateness; to have the stubbornness of a mule or billygoat; to be unshakeable or unswaying in one's beliefs
            dinigh: potash
            thirig: to be great or exceeding; lofty, high-up; excess, surplus, abundance or bounty
            thi'rih: to tear or cleave out a feature in the landscape, often said of the feats of certain mythological features; a gorge or canyon

                            haful: "war-one" = man of war = a brave person
                            hiirga'ar: "slip-escape" = scamp = a weak person
                            nuzahudu: "slow-gifting" = gift tardy = a greedy person
                            zagga-su: "glitter-bodied" = glittering = a fashionable person
                            rogea-su: "shark-bodied" = sharkskin = a noisy or loud person
                            gulukikul: "hateful one" = hater = an enemy
                            kukdu-mirska: "biting harmful snake" = biting snake = a sword or piercing weapon
