                    Originally posted by Felipe View Post
                    What do you guys think is going on with the Sin-Eaters and Changelings at the time?

                    - When food grows scarce and there are too many mouths to feed, the sacrifice of children to the gods for remittance is not unheard of. Children born with disabilities are especially likely to be given up to the elements. Sometimes those given up return as messengers of the gods, giving prophesy, or enforcing forgotten ancestral promises.

                    The fact that the Contract of Iron remains unbroken (the first great faltering of the "god's" power, in a thousand + years time), combined with the patrols of Father Wolf in the border between worlds, means that rebellious escapees from the Land of the Gods are not really a thing with a lot of precedent yet.

                    - Again, the patrols of Father Wolf and his brood in the Bordermarches (I figures the division between Hisil and other realms are very vague pre-Sundering) means that returnees from the Land of the Dead are rare. Those who do make that journey are known to bring death in their wake and are unwelcome with their old tribes. Often (relative to their rarity) they live alone at the edge of their former community, visited in secret by those who would make dealings with death.
