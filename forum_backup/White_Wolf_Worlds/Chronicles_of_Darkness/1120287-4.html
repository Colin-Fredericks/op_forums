                    I am particularly interested in this era as i am actually currently running a Mage game in a semi-realistic version of Celtic Britain based on a combination of the Runequest supplement Mythic Britain and Keltia a game from Cubicle & (although i think they may just have lost the licence from the original French company that owns the game). Both these games set Arthur and Merlin in a historical setting of dark ages Britain after the retreat of Rome from Britain with Arthur as one of many vying for the rule of the fractious British Celtic tribes and against the background of Saxon invasion and conflicts between the old druid way and the new faith of Christianity. Lots of great politics and intrigue as well as the supernatural backdrop of mythic Celtic Britain.

                    So In my setting I have the players as mages working with Merlin (of course a mage!) to support Arthur's claim to rule Britain so i would obviously like to see mage as one of the game lines treated here. I think with this being Celtic Britain the other game line should be (perhaps has to be) Changeling, weaving all the Fae themes into the fight between the old and new faith with teh Celts of the old faith connected strongly to the fae and Changelings. I could also see Werewolf having a lot of relevance with old celtic faith being very ties to connection with the spirit world and spirits of Britain. I could see vampires in there somewhere (maybe Morgana becomes a vampire!) but not as a main line for the setting.

                        Yeah; I don't have a strong opinion on what the second gameline should be (my preference for Changeling isn't a particularly strong one); but the first gameline really ought to be Mage. And if it is, Merlin should not appear — not directly.

                        Merlin should be an Ascended Being, having already altered reality to cement the essence of Camelot into reality. The sudden explosion of potential Arthurs that Arcanist refers to is the result of Arthur-flavored Supernal Symbology getting woven into the fabric of reality around this time. And the Camelot that Merlin helped Arthur build as part of Merlin's road to Ascension was considerably closer to the version of Camelot that wyrdhamster was asking about — meaning that there should be anachronistic Ruins resembling the Romantic Arthur that the “real” Arthur and his men can stumble across.

                        Merlin might send an Ochemata (sp?) to aid the Arthur-counterpart of this iteration of reality; but that's as close as you get to the actual Merlin showing up.
