    Sealed Evil in a Can

    05-15-2020, 03:00 PM
    There's a nice trope of "sealed evil in a can," where there is some Big Bad that must not be released or might just get released. In my DtD chronicle there is a good chance that one of the GodMachine's Occult Matrices (or the players' interference in it) might release such a sealed evil. Whatever it is, it should be very, very bad. To put it in perspective, if the choice is between releasing this SEC or causing a volcano to open up in Chicago, the volcano is the better option. I'd like the game to be still playable if the SEC gets out, and humanity might not even know that anything has happened (yet). However stopping the SEC and putting it back in the can might require Demons and Angels teaming up, setting off an entirely new Chronicle.

    The question is: what is the SEC? Maybe an idigam? If so which one? Maybe an elder new variant of Vampire? A Gentry that thrives on this side of the Hedge? I'd be grateful for interesting suggestions.

              Define your SEC's scope beforehand. How dangerous is it:
              • Physically Powerful - Are we talking Godzilla level threat or Terminator level threat? A Godzilla will likely involve the God-Machine sending a couple of high Rank Angels to do clean up. A Godzilla-esque entity would require some way to keep its destruction localized. If you talking about Terminator you have a localized threat that is killing off a small amount of people, maybe it is an Angel killer, but its not going to end the world.
              • Politically Powerful - Perhaps the entity is a would-be God similar to Mummies or the God-Machine itself with its own cult. The threat is less about how much damage it can cause physically, but how much its plans butt heads with those of the God-Machine. This is a being that is trying to conquer the world, not destroy it.
              • Existential Danger - Perhaps the entity's existence is a breach of the natural law, causing ripples to effect localized areas. Maybe its a Rain Deity or Water Elemental that brings flooding to its surroundings. Maybe it doesn't know of the God-Machine, but is a threat to it none-the-less.
              • World Ending Danger - The most dangerous thing to bring into a Game. World Ending beings would be things like Galactus from the MCU which eats planets. Bringing such a threat to the table is a danger to your game. The next step the God-Machine would do is likely try to create a Splinter timeline where that entity is stuck in a loop of destroying Earth.
              The Apocalypse Vault is a piece of Infrastructure where the God-Machine stores time lines where the Earth is destroyed. Check it out in the Splintered City Seattle book.

              Additionally the God-Machine will try to create Infrastructure to contain the issue calling upon Archangels and other assets to take care of this threat. The God-Machine applies enough resources as possible to deal with this threat and could theoretically deem using Demons as an efficient way to fix the issue, but the God-Machine usually doesn't know where Demons reside or who they are (If it did it would unravel the entire purpose of Cover).

              Originally posted by KieranMullen View Post
              The question is: what is the SEC? Maybe an idigam? If so which one? Maybe an elder new variant of Vampire? A Gentry that thrives on this side of the Hedge? I'd be grateful for interesting suggestions.
              My suggestions:
              • Unknown Creature - The being has no name and possibly existed before the Universe created. It can take on the guise of person and performs random acts of murder. The entity doesn't understand how reality works and behaves like a child discovering a new toy, if that would be dissecting humans and other supernatural creatures to see how they tick. This creature was contained by the God-Machine because it couldn't be predicted or accounted for in its occult matrices and was impossible to predict (Any future sight cannot view this creature).
                • I believe that unknown creatures and entitities without lore are more scary than anything for players because it means that they have to do a lot of leg work to figure out what this is. And not giving it a name humans know it by makes it even more scary to deal with.
                • Make sure it has a weakness that the players maybe can figure out by looking at its behavior. Perhaps it killed a bunch of people, but left a priest alone, because its damaged by people with True Faith. Or that the creature was killing people but stopped when it got to a bridge over water, suggesting that water can kill it. Perhaps a bystander managed to fend it off with a unique weapon (i.e. Bane like thing). These are breadcrumbs to help the players in their fight.
              • True Fae - This being may have a grudge against the God-Machine after being set free. Returning to the Hedge and sending agents to attack Angels and Demons alike.
              • Antediluvian - I suggest looking at the Vampire antediluvians that might be an interesting Antagonist to throw around.
              • Cryptid - High Ranked Cryptids can be the perfect creatures to throw at Demons. Check out how an angel dies in the core rules.
              • Exiled Angel - Perhaps an Angel that is rogue and has been siphoning off Essense from the God-Machine's Infrastructure. This entity could be a high rank or not, but if the God-Machine had a tough time trying to reintegrate it then its possible that he is a danger to the God-Machine's Occult Matrices.
              My suggestion is to make sure whatever the creature is that it's not God-Tier and is more a threat to the God-Machine's Occult Matrices than to the God-Machine itself. Also keep in mind how the God-Machine might clean up after this mess:
              • Send the entity into a Splinter timeline, possibly throwing the Demons and Mortals in the vicinity in with it.
              • Send Archangels which are powerful enough to defeat or recapture the entity. This will take time and likely result in the Demons allowing the God-Machine to handle this. Possibly leaving the area because the Angels are swarming. The God-Machine sends enough Angels to equal the problem, its about efficiency, and Demons don't require a lot of Angels to take care of, this however does.
              • The Angel Numina Witness Protection is likely going to be used a lot to keep Mortals from realizing whats going on.
              Feel free to ask me more questions on this subject. The more directed the better.

                          The session summaries aren't helping much, unfortunately, and the fact that you're not using the Cipher early on removes a major set of hooks from the table.

                          You seem to have at least two pieces of Infrastructure in play, looking at the Background History and Mission Start pages on the campaign site, along with the coverage provided by the 1999 Oklahoma tornado outbreak; do you foresee the CAPS, the outbreak, and/or the ring's current focal curiosity of disappearances and cattle mutilation having anything to do with the SEC, or are you angling to have that be an outside concern on the approach?

                          Consider the nature of the SEC's containment. Did the GM create its prison, or is it breaking into a separate metaphysical frame's handiwork? Is the Machine deliberately attempting to unseal it? If so, has it evaluated the most efficient means of doing so (correctly or otherwise) as force or finesse? If not, what is it trying to do instead and how has this gotten caught on the SEC? Is it a prosaic Monster In A Physical Container (possibly folded into esoteric spaces), or are we dealing with more obscure evils like a virus locked in something's DNA that needs the right trigger criteria to emerge? Is this thing held beneath the earth, within the clouds, packed into another dimension or more abstract containment, simply out of phase with anything it could threaten? Is its emergence supposed to be a direct and immediate threat to anything locally, or is it the equivalent of starting a new grand-scale occult matrix from scratch, where if nothing is done to stop it it'll be a disaster in four years' time?
