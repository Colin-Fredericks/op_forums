Righteous must be tested with hardship so they prove their faith is genuine) created his first set of servitors as ephemerals/spirits representing given Vice (or Virtue) so they could make humans succumb to them so he could judge them (let's face it, he's gonzo) or inspire humans to pursue given virtue. He also made mistake by giving them free will so thye could mingle with the flock and do their job.

            Those servitors discovered that they could materialize borrowing on spiritual resonance created by humans who pursued given Vice or Virtue for which they have affinity, so they strayed from the 'right' part and got seduced by the world refusing to go back to Shadow. Fallen are naturally paranoid that 'God' is walking Earth and must tread carefully to not get into the way.

                Honestly one thing I like with both Fallen and Descent is the BYO Battle form aspect.                                                 Especially as some of them don't even need changing - Lore of Portals (one of my favourites) for example can be used almost verbatim, just change the dice mechanic from cWoD to nWoD in a generic block of text prior to the powers.                                                 Some of them do - the item creation Lore, the Lores for manipulating and controlling mortals should probably get some mention of hook ups to Social Maneuvering etc, some Conditions and Tilts should probably be thrown in and pointers of when a Lore should apply one etc.

                                                The things that really need the time are Torment, Faith, Cover, Primum, etc. and a toolkit with a healthy spread of examples for converting powers back and forth. And, if there's space, some discussion on using DtD Demons in a DtF setting and visa-versa.

                                                        A few things.
                                                        1.) Rose already stated a while ago that the conversion guide has been pushed back and other things have a higher priority, thus it has no fixed release date.

                                                        2.) For the powers: Here it was never stated how they will be made. The past translation guides can't be taken as precedence here as the powers there were in essence mostly the same powers (except those that disappeared like the tzimisces). Thus as the powers in both demon forms are completely different it will probably get handled differently in this guide. I think only
                                                        rose can answer how it will be handled if you get conversion rules or complete conversions (from what I remember of dtf some powers were problematic for owod but less so in nwod 2.0
                                                        as the general power level rose there. what will be interesting also here will be if the tormented versions of the powers will instead become exploits or how things are done).
                                                        Also a few lores have an aequivalent already in embeds and exploits (raising the dead powers for example or healing powers).

                                                        3.) Also don't forget form powers. with the storytellers guide (think it was taht one) in dtf forms gained a few abilities that dont exist in any form in dtd. It will be interesting how that will work out.
                                                        Thus if there will be something about converting them or not.

                                                        4.) What also will be interesting is how earthbound will be handled if at all. As in the past there were even rules how someone could become an earthbound. dtf earthbounds were powerful enough to challenge a whole group of characters AND had some unique lores (others could learn them but using them proved to be quite tormenting). will be interesting if earthbound get conversion rules and also if their special lores get one.
