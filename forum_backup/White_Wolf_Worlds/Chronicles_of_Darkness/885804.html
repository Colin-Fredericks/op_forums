              Anything that helps explain (or make it look like you're the treatment, not the cause) occasional illness and/or whatever weird behaviors tend to crop up in ghouls, given feeding Conditions, how unhealthy their relationships often are, and the adjustments of becoming a minor template. Doctor, psychiatrist, priest, AA sponsor, rehab, or therapist fit the bill, and as a bonus most of 'em are authority figures.

              Remember that Ghouls are bound and probably addicted, so you can more or less dictate how much or little contact you have with them (within reason). Masseuse, tutor, home charity, handyman, on-call vet, any kind of trainer or instructor on a budget makes a good cover, for either party. Any activity requiring a modicum of privacy and casual interaction, that can be stepped up, wound down, or abandoned, without suspicion.

              Of course, an easier solution is just to ghoul an antisocial someone and use their money and home. Just make sure you save up Experiences to buy the Merit before your ST starts messing with your freely-acquired stuff.

                                  You should be okay as long as you keep them from going all Renfield. If you're going to have them take Breaking Points for you, make sure it's someone who can handle it. Though there's always a chance that you can be their next Breaking Point so perhaps don't.

                                  Vampires aren't even up all the day, so their ghouls certainly wouldn't be with them that whole time, unless they were a particularly servile one. The vampire is in the position of power, and can arrange when the meetings occur for the most part.

                                  As for police, it really depends on your goal. 'Subverting' an entire police force would probably be best done by controlling people high up in the ranks, commissioners and majors and councilmen. Look at something like Hamsterdam, it's not something you could pull off without someone high up allowing it. If you just have patrol cops under control, if numbers get bad in one area, someone's going to go above their heads and your ghouls are going to find themselves under suspicion.

                                  But if you just want someone to be a cop who does stuff for you like roust people out of certain areas a regular officer could do it. A detective would have more rank and probably be able to look further into certain crimes. Someone working in admin could just keep you informed of activities. I forget which of the clanbooks, I want to say Nosferatu, has a vampire called the Dead Lieutenant, who still works for the police and obviously has a lot of sway with them. If you want more specific information about what positions do what, you could check out Tales from the 13th Precinct. It's pretty heavy reading but does go into a lot of depth about the local law enforcement system.
