    A Mage the Awakening Chronicle in Los Angeles (Actual Play)

    06-30-2023, 12:58 AM
    Hello everybody. Last night my group started to play MtAw and I am so excited to finally get to run this game. I did precovid and while we had fun I didn't think I was doing the game justice back then. I've got to be a player in several games and have spent a lot more time in the community since then, not to mention seeing some actual plays on Youtube so I'm excited this time around as I think the game will be much closer to how I feel mage can be than last time around. Anyway, being the game just started I wanted to find a place to commerate the game and keep track of what is going on in it and this seemed like a good place to do it, so here it is.

    First a little background for anyone reading this that isn't me, the game is taking place in Los Angeles in March 2019. I'm using the LA setting from the 2e core and then of course adding on top of it. I did a little setting information google doc that you can look at here if you are interested in that sort of thing. The game itself is biweekly, alternating with one of the players for a VtR game we are also playing set in Manhattan.

    There are five PCs who are not offically in a cabal at the start of the chronicle:
    1. Eye of God - Mastigos Mysterium, at character gen Space 3/Prime 2/Mind 1 Obsession is "Disocver Hallows"
    2. Giger - Acanthus Mysterium, at character gen Time 3/Fate 2/Matter 1 Obsession is "Hoard Magic"
    3. Maxwell - Obrimos Mysterium, at character gen Froces 3/Matter 1/Prime 1/Time 1 Obsession is "Maintain Order"
    4. Ragnar - Moros Adamantine Arrow, at character gen Matter 3/Death 2/Forces 1 Obsession is "The Glory of Valhalla"
    5. Saike - Mastigos Adamantine Arrow, at character gen Space 3/Time 2/Mind 1 Obsession is "The Demon Wind"
    Editing this on 2/24/2025 to add a link to the new Chronicle I started this week, Trenches & Pilgrims, a Promethan the Created Chronicle set in World War One.

    Chapter One
    6/28/2023

    <Saike wasn't present for this session>

    “Los Angeles is the second largest city in the United States, second only to New York. LA county is home to 10,000,000 people, a quarter of all of California's population, and that doesn’t speak to the rest of the urban sprawl. Despite so many people living so close to each other it is easy to feel isolated and alone. The people you walk by on the sidewalk are people you’ll likely never see again. No one here knows your name. You are a face in a mass of faces. One young woman is experiencing this more than anyone else ever will. She runs through Little Tokyo, sobbing, bleeding, screaming for help, but no one looks at her. She grasps at a stranger's shirt, begging him to notice her, but he walks away, none the wiser to her presence. She sobs as she turns around, the only person who can see her walking towards her through the crowd, bloody knife in hand, but even he goes unnoticed by the crowd. No one notices the screams of her death, no one notices the man, covered in blood, leave the alley and disappears into the crowd. When her body is finally found, detectives and reporters alike ask how no one could have noticed such a gruesome murder so close to the busy streets of LA, so close to City Hall.


    Of all the people who feel invisible in LA, perhaps the homeless feel this the most. People go out of their way to pretend not to see they are there, to cross the street to avoid them, to see themselves as better than them. One such homeless man wishes he hadn’t been noticed, hadn’t been picked, stripped naked, cleaned and brought to this room full of chanted, hooded strangers. He wishes he wasn’t the one being forced into the golden sarcophagus they surrounded. He wished he couldn’t hear their prayers as the lid was closed on him.


    With so many people in this urban sprawl the city never truly sleeps. There are always vehicles on the roads, people walking the streets, going about their business, going to or returning from work, but in the evenings it does slow down. One man rides furiously, on his pedal bike. A moment before he was fine, riding to work for the night shift at the Good Samaritan Hospital, but the delusion had taken over in a moment, a psychotic break forcing him to ride and then stop, opening his arms welcomingly as the semi crashed into him, bringing his final moment.


    And there is a girl in her apartment, waking from a nightmare, a nightmare where she was a goddess of death, seeing over the underworld, and she begins to write, writing as an unseen, veiled woman, half alive and half dead, watches over her.”     <This is the intro to the game I read off to the players>

    The session began with with the three Mysterium mages recieving a text from Architect, the curator of the local athenaeum. Something strange had happened last night and the Guardians of the Veil found out about it through one of their agents who works in the LA General Medical Center's ER. The Guardians are too short on people to send themselves and reached out to the Mysterium to investigate it. The Guardians didn't give many details so Architect had decided to send these three to cast a wide net of knowledge in the arcana, and he also reached out to the Adamantine Arrow for protection, just in case. Due to this Ragnar recieved a text from the mage who had mentored him when he first awakened, Vulcan, telling him what the Mysterium had requested and letting him know to expect a second Talon. <This is of course Saike, who won't be making it to the hospital in time due to not making it for this session> Eye of God, being up late the night before, would have slept past the meeting time. When he didn't respond to his text Architect sent a sleepwalker to his appartment to wake him up, he was late to the meeting, but not extremely late as a result. The other three mages who did arrive quickly identified eachother, introduced themselves, and entered the hospital. They knew the name of their contact, Dr. Choi, and the receptionist got him on the phone and sent them to the room he wanted to meet at. When they arrived outside the room they detected some sort of magic, but as Dr. Choi rounded the corner he produced a small metal cylinder with a rod attatched to it with a chain. When he put the rod in the cylinder the magic faded. Dr. Choi introduced himself, told them he had something extremely weird happen in the ER the night before and reached out to the mage he knew, assuming they would want to know about it, he then took them into the hospital room. On the T.V. there was a new report playing. Dr. Choi turned off the T.V. but not before the reporter was able to say, "The authorities are still unsure of the identity of 'The Shadow that Walks', who are they, and what do they want?"

    There was a man under a blanket that completely covered him, hooked up to all sorts of machines, one to help him breathe, one showing his extremely slow heart rate, one checking for brain activity, etc. Dr. Choi explained that the man had been hit by a semitruck the night before, all his ribs were broken, his skull was caved in, his internal organs were ruptured, he had internal and external bleeding. When the paramedics arrived one the scene he should have been dead, he should have been dead now, but he was still breathing, his heart still beating, he even had some brain activity, despite most of it having been crushed by the truck. He showed them the man to allow them to look over the damage. Giger used Postcognition to watch the accident himself while the others discussed the identity of the man, Todd Williams, he had an employee badge for the Good Samaritan Hospital in LA with him when the accident happened. Giger watched the man biking to work the previous night. Everything was normal until out of the blue he seemed to start suffering some sort of psychotic break and raced through traffic, weaving in and out despite the danger. This went on for about five minutes before he ran a red light, stopped his bike in the middle of the intersection, and opened his arms, smiling, to be hit by the semitruck. Two times during this scene Giger's peripheral mage sight pinged a supernatural effect, once when the psychotic break started, and once at the moment of impact. He went back to the first ping and say a monter <I'll provide the art for it below> the size of a small dog flying in the air. Throughout the five minute scene it followed through the sky, and was present still at the scene of the accident, at which point it flew off.

    When Giger returned from his Postcognition Eye of God had just arrived. After introducing himself at the door to Dr. Choi as his shadowname the Dr. let him into the room, saying "No one who introduces themself like that isn't supposed to be here I'm guessing." Eye of God introduced himself to the rest of the mages, recieved a recap of the information they had gotten, and then Giger informed everyone of what he had seen through his magic. The group discussed plans of action, Dr. Choi asked if he should reward the room or if they would be able to get back in, Eye of God snapped some pictures and assured him they would, and they left the hospital, with Dr. Choi taking back out the steel cylinder and putting them ward back up. The mages all got into the limo that Giger had ridden in to the hospital, met his driver/butler Leopold, and were off to the scene of the accident.

    Not much happened at the scene other than some spells and mage sight work. Maxwell noticed with his sight that the flow of mana in this area was recovering from some sort of disruption, like the mana had been torn away, removed, somehow. Eye of God knew of a few ways that could happened, either a Prime unmaking spell destroying the mana, something Abyssal happened, or a Lower Depths sucking it down. Giger through up a Fate spell, Serendipity, to figure out the best course of action to finding the "Fly", to which he got some answers: "Find its nest" and "Find its prey". Giger also through up his sight and saw strings of destiny wrapped around Eye of God, unsure what the destiny was though, he never informed anyone about this. <In character Eye of God does not know about his destiny> In order to find its nest the the mages decided to return to Todd's hospital room and Eye of God would try to track the "Fly" back to its nest using sympathy. They got back into the limo and Co-Located back. There Eye of God did some big Space magic and ended up releasing some paradox into the spell causing the target of the spell to shift, <"One paradox dice isn't gonna happen I'll release it" and then it rolled a 10 and an 8 for two success LMFAO> but after a second attempt he managed to jump along Todd's sympathy to find the "Fly". The group got some magic ready <Acceleration for Giger, Maxwell cast Gravatic Supremacy on everyone, and Ragnar put up a Soul Armor spell on everyone> And they Co-Located again to the location of the fly using a borrowed sympathy from Todd.

    They found themselves in an abaonded warehouse, the "Fly" hiding up on one of the rafters above. The place was filled with animals that were not in great condition. Some mice and rats that were half eaten, still squeaking in pain and draging themselves along and a pigeon with no head, flapping around and running into thing. The "fly" wasn't aggressive towards the mages but they decided they would fight it. Ragnar did some powerful Matter magic in the form of Shrink and Grow, making the entire warehouse the size of a house, which frightened the "Fly" and the battle begun.

      Chapter Two
      7/12/2023

      Two men kneeled in meditation together, swords on the floor at their sides. This was Saike and Rasuto Katto. When their meditation had finished and Saike had confirmed that his meditation was focused on his swordsmenship skills Rasuto Katto confirmed this was indeed a good thing to meditate on for a young talon, as his sword would be needed by the Arrow. Then Rasuto Katto checked his phone, seeing the message that the Mysterium had requested the help of two talons and Saike was one of the two to be sent. Saike got the information on the assignment and used a Co-Location spell to arrive at the hospital, a Postcognition spell to learn who he was looking for, and then almost entered the room that Todd Williams was in before Dr. Choi stopped him. After he learned he was with the Arrow, Dr. Choi once again lowered the ward and allowed him to enter.

      From here the battle with the "Fly" was quite easy as the mages outnumbered it. Saike was the only to not use any magic, relying on his sword, Ragnar created a sword using Matter magic and the Shaping spell, and the Mysterium mages all used various attack and control spells to harm the creature and slow it down. <It is perhaps important to note that Giger released a paradox that slow Ragnar down during this fight, instead of slowing the fly down, this will be important in a moment.> Ragnar also revealed       his wyvrn ghost familiar in this fight, calling for it to manifest and attack the fly, which is gladly did. Once defeated though the mages quickly realized that the "Fly" could not be killed, Maxwell even tried to use the sword that Ragnar had shaped to behead it, which did not kill it. So information was to be gathered.

      Ragnar opened his sight and saw a swirl of ash in the center of the room, the source of some active magic that was irritating everyone's peripheral. He scrutinized it and revealed the following information, sharing it with the other mages:

      >>> Surface Information
      1. Primary arcanum: Space
      2. Resonance: The feeling of the end being just out of reach
      3. Age: Days
      4. Practice: Fraying
      >>> Deep Information
      1. The mystery is not related to the Supernal or to the Abyss
      2. Death, Prime and Space all have information about this mystery
      3. Death - The other side of this portal is missing the final moment of life, the moment that a life ends, death. Creatures native to this plane cannot die and feed off of the deaths of life forms that can.
      Several things happened at this point, one, Maxwell activated his sight and saw the lingering effect of paradox on Giger, scollding him for it quite hard. While this distraction was happened Eye of God, who saw an open doorway where Ragnar saw the swirl of ash, decided to walk through the door. He was teleported to a place filled with skeletons, their minds still trapped within their corpses. <Of course had he been able to use Life magic or Life sight, he would have also seen that the skeletons were still alive, despite their state of decay.> In a pool of murky water among the piles of skeletons he saw a massive green creature that slept, dozens of minds trapped within its skin. His Prime sight revealed to him that he was ina place that was lower in reality than the Fallen World, and with the use of Mind magic to recall his studies, he realized it was a Lower Depth. While in this realm a paradox was released by him, despite his attempt to contain it, and he noted an alien intelligence took note of him <Abyssal Backlash condition.> Upon his return to the Fallen World many questions were asked about what he saw, which he reported. Giger's Fate sight also revealed that the strands of destiny that had been around Eye of God when he first saw them were now twisted and flapping violently, like his trip to this realm had violently altered his destiny in some way. The group decided the best thing to do was the close the portal. Eye of God discovered the key, which was something rotting but alive in the vicinity. The mages gathered up all the animals that were in the warehouse and the "Fly" and put them in a box. Maxwell then took the box to the roof and using his Gravitic Supremacy rote, shot it into space. <All three of the Mysterium mages are either astrophysics PhDs or work on engineering projects that involve leaving the Earth's atmosphere, so they had a lot of fun calculating out how long he'd have to maintain this spell before the box wouldn't return to the planet.> The portal was still open, so the mages returned to the hospital, which Eye of God's Co-location spell was still maintaining, leaving behind Giger and Saike to see if thee portal closed once Todd was no longer in the pressence of the portal and then to get a ride back with Leopold. The portal did close, so now the mages must decide what to do with Todd and the portal.

              Chapter 4
              8/2/23

              Giger, in his sleeper persona, Ted Turner, attended a sort of party, a high class one, filled with the highest of the high class. Most of those there were incredibley boring but one man, Mark Slim caught his attention. They visted for a bit before Mark told Ted about a historic artifact, a vase from the Warring Kingdoms period of Chinese history. Ted convinced him that his father would have loved to see it, and he got an invitation to set up a time to come visit Mark's mansion and see the vase. <This happened during the day of the last session, so his character could make some progress while the other characters would have been.>

Ragnar had been informed by Vulcan that there would be a talon briefing mission that afternoon that he was expected to attend, so while he did the Mysterium mages took the time to do research in the Lapis Vivus Templum. Giger spent the time catching up, both on information that he had gotten from the day he had missed, and also on his own when it came to The Garden of Eternal Rot. Eye of God spent time researching demons in the Iron Maze, a section of the athenaeum dedicated to the realm of Mastigos. There he found records of The Worms of the Tapestry, an imp that takes the form of a fanged mass of wormed that feed on both the blood and the sympathy of the summoner, and then use that to repair parts of the world that have broken down, specifically, they close portals that exist between the fallen world and Lower Depths, the Abyss, or the Suprunal.

Three new threats had been indentified; first, three murders had happened in the past several months in broad daylight but with no witnessess, despite being in crowded areas, like Little Tokyo, near a crowded hiking area, and the on one of the beaches. No ghosts had been left by any of the murder victims, but the Guardians and Arrow are certian the supernatural is involved. Second, a pack of Werewolves has been sighted in the San Gabriel Mountains. Their alligence, and territory, are unknown but they have killed both human and spirit alike, so are the be considered a threat. Finally, The Shadow That Walks, a theif who has been stealing priceless sleeper artifacts, has remained unidenitified by mage society, even with the use of magic. Their sympathy is jumbled in a way that makes the identity hard to track. It is unknown if they are a part of the supernatural world, or if they are aware of it, but they may one day steal something of the mage's world, and there fore they are a threat. Any information gathered on any of these threats is to be reported to the first talons. During the meeting a mage spoke up, asking about the murderers, but no more information was given. <While Ragnar didn't know who the mage was by name, he does now know what he looks like.>



              After the meeting was over Ragnar approached Valkyrie, who was obvious to him due to the golden spear she carried. They discussed their shared symbolism briefly and then voiced interest in finding more information on the Werewolves, they decided they would find a Thyrsus and investigate.



              Finally, once the cabal was together again, they returned to Long Beach to investigate the past of the portal with Giger's more advanced study for the Time aracnum. Giger, with the help of some alcohol he had picked up,               <luckily it was the occupants favorite drink> they managed to get some alone time in his living room, in which Giger used his Time magic to send Maxwell back in time further than he had gone before, to see the opening of the portal. They learned the building was decaying at an excellerated rate, too fast to be natural but he wasn't able to detect any magic active in the area until the portal opened, and that there use to be a small hallow here, but it was consumed slowly as the schism was born.
