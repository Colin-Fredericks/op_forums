            Session two. Preparation and meeting the passengers.

            Session II

            Marek’s Thoughts

            1 May 1920

            Today was a productive and informative day. I learned that Wolfram had no experience with bladed weapons of any kind. Dacian has some, but not a lot. He endeavored to teach our German companion how to use a sword. I resisted adding my own comments to their efforts at training. Dacian was having Wolfram do some simple exercises first before doing anything actually combat related with the blades. You have to get used to the weight first before you try to use it. I am not schooled in a formal fighting style. I know how to use a bayonet to good effect. It is not pretty but it works. Before I was transferred to working as a sniper I had several opportunities to use my bayonet.

            We also learned today that Alla can fight, and rather well at that. I was not expecting a Russian noblewoman to be proficient in combat, but I am not one to judge. The surprise was a welcome one. Tatiana of course can fight, and the two of us sparred, changing forms as we went. I will admit that I missed that kind of fighting. My prey lately have been men or troublesome minor spirits. Fighting with Tatiana was a chance to go all out. I rather enjoyed myself. After a little while Alla asked if she could join us. Tatiana and I looked at each other, skeptical, but then Alla informed us that she has her own powers for combat and assured us that she would be fine. We took her at her word and included her in the fight. She was right. She could keep up, and did. I have to wonder if all Kindred are as martially proficient as she is, or if she is the exception rather than the rule.

            Dervala was also a bit of a surprise. She is familiar with using knives. She is pretty good with them, and joined Wolfram and Dacian in their practice. I think she said something about having older brothers, which explains some things. If they are protective and practical they would have taught her to use weapons suited to her physical capabilities. The knives suited her. I wish I could have seen more of their practice, but I was busy with my own and I wouldn’t have traded it.

            While some of us were sparring Lizette was apparently working on a duplicate Golden Eagle to act as a decoy when or if we found the original. I thought it looked rather impressive. She also had worked on a bomb that could be hidden and brought onto the train. Some people objected to this but I saw the practicality of it. If things should go horribly wrong, then it would be a good idea to have something like that with us. She assured us that it was something to be used only in an emergency. I was ready to move on after that, but some people still argued. Eventually arguments wound down and Lizette produced a fairly large stuffed bear to hide the explosives in. I thought it was clever. I’m sure with her height Lizette could pretend to be a young girl, and no one is going to take a toy away from a young girl.

            Lizette also demonstrated for us her skill with a chainsaw. I was a little surprised at first by the unconventional weapon, but it seemed to fit the petite builder well. It certainly looked like it could cut down a tree with ease, which meant that it would be more than effective against physical beings. She went on a bit about wanting to be able to set it ablaze so it would be even more terrifying. I brought up the concern that the fuel the machine ran on might ignite if she were to light it on fire, but she did not seem to share my worry. I will trust her in this for now. She certainly seems to know what she is doing around machines.

            Later, after the practice and chainsaw demonstrations I sought out Wolfram to talk to him about his spirit companion. I was curious exactly how he came to be Ridden by a spirit in the first place. Turns out it was something of a bargain. Wolfram lay dying and his Geist offered to bring him back to health if he would play host to the spirit. There is apparently a gain for the host. They acquire some supernatural abilities and are rather hard to actually kill. Wolfram said that one of his abilities was to be able to hide objects within himself. I’m not sure how that works exactly. It cannot be literal. It must be some function of the spirit that hides the item.

            Wolfram assured me that the Geist does not control him in anyway. He said that it was a mutual arrangement. The spirit gets to live vicariously through their host, and the host gains effective immortality and powers. I have seen bargains struck with spirits before and this deal with the Geist seems a little unbalanced. The host seems to gain more than the Geist does. I wonder if there is something that Wolfram is not saying or if there is something that he doesn’t know. I will keep an eye on him to make sure that he is not being unduly affected by the Geist or controlled in any way. I like Wolfram, but if he becomes a threat to the group I will put him down.

            ********

            3 May 1920

            We are on the train now. We have paired up and picked our cabins. I am rooming with Wolfram. We smuggled Alla onto the train by putting her in a trunk and then Tatiana carried it on board. I’m not sure how Alla managed to fit herself into the trunk, but she did. It seemed the easiest way to get her onto the train and still avoid the sunlight. This will be something to keep in mind for future ventures. She is limited to operating at night which means that we will have to plan around that. I am fine with working at night. A good number of my jobs are carried out in the dark. It better hides me and my activities. There will however likely be some things that must take place during the day. Perhaps we can keep the trunk for just such an eventuality. I wonder if a heavy coat or cloak would be suitable to keeping our Lady Kindred from burning? I will have to ask her next time I get a chance.

            The question of Alla’s sensitivity to sunlight reminds me that I should speak to the rest of the group and see if they possess any weaknesses that I should be aware of. Stories of Fae folk mention weaknesses to cold iron or church bells and such things. I do not know if our contingent of Fae have any such prohibitions or not. It would not be good to find out in the middle of a fight, or some important task that they have a weakness and are incapacitated. The same goes for Wolfram. Can he be bound like spirits can, or can something be done to the Geist to hurt the host? He is not as of yet a front line combatant, but he may be called into that capacity and this would be good to know.

            Tatiana and I should be up front about our susceptibility to silver as well. It is not a very common thing to make weapons out of pure silver, but I will not dismiss such a thing out of hand. I was given the opportunity to hold a sliver knife so I would know what it felt like. I will admit that it hurt quite a bit. I can imagine what it would feel like to be wounded by such a weapon. The silver plated knife hurt as well, but I could tell the difference. It was a matter of intensity. Dietrich always says that we should be prepared for any eventuality. It is a very practical mindset. I agree.

            ********

            This evening we walked through the train cars trying to find the Golden Eagle or some clue to its whereabouts. I suggested that we split up and go individually or in pairs to talk to people or search cars. It did not make sense to be seen entering as such a large and varied group. It would raise suspicions. Why would average people be traveling with obvious nobility? Plus our group is rather diverse. Like tends to call to like, and we defy that.

            Tatiana stayed to the side and watched people. I believe she was mainly paying attention to a man that was doing the same thing. He piqued my interest as well, but it would be too suspicious if we both kept an eye on him. The others split up and talked to various people or groups of people. I believe Alla wanted to talk to the mother/daughter pair. Dervala was going to talk to the other pair of young ladies that were talking quietly to themselves. I’m not sure who the others were planning on conversing with. I figured that all of us in the same car might get crowded, so I took the opportunity to look around in the other cars on the train. I did not break into anyone else’s sleeping cars, but may do that next.

            I did not find anything of note while looking by myself. Later Dacian joined me and we went to look in the baggage car. I am not familiar with how to pick a lock, but the dark man is more than capable. I believe the others had been drawn into a game of Bridge with the other passengers so we left them to it.

            Dacian and I searched everyone’s luggage and found some interesting things. The mother/daughter pair are supposedly on vacation yet they have no bags in the luggage car. It is possible that they are keeping them in their sleeper car, but there is not a lot of room for any good sized bags in them. William (the British man who likes card games and talking) has a shotgun, a revolver and ammunition in his bags. The telling thing is that some of the ammunition in his bag is for a weapon that is not present. This means that he is likely carrying it on his person or it is in his sleeping car. We just keep a close eye on him. Guns are dangerous.

            Ewan (the Scottish stand alone man) has a couple of bags, but no Eagle within them. It might be in his sleeper car. We will check there next. Christina’s (the woman traveling to collect important family paper work) luggage had a special lock on it, that Dacian picked with ease, and inside she has expensive jewelry and a functional dagger. It seems the lock is just to protect her valuables. Suzie (the young lady from the United States) has what looks like costumes, make-up and wigs to be used in disguises packed in her bags. It makes me wonder if the appearance she wears now is her real one or not and why she feels the need to hide her identity. Vera and Gloria (two young ladies from Austria) have occult materials such as carved bones, divination equipment and what looks like a ritual staff.

            After the Bridge game we exchanged information. Apparently Dervala can sense what a person’s utmost desire is and found that the two occult ladies want very much to find out who has the Eagle. Also, Tatiana sensed something magical being cast at Lizette during the Bridge game. It did not appear to be harmful to the young Fae lady, so we are guessing that it was something used to cheat in the game. This power seemed to have come from our two occult ladies. It would be interesting to know what other abilities they have, but to ask them would reveal too much about ourselves and our abilities. I find that it is best to be underestimated.

            There is likely additional information that can be gathered by looking in people’s sleeping cars as well as further conversations with pretty much everyone. Each person that I listed seems to have something of note going on. Whether or not it’s connected to the Golden Eagle is another story. We do not have enough information as of yet. We will attempt to gather more and I will keep track of our progress.

            ********


            Tatiana’s Conversations

            So, you can run as fast as a train, hm, volchonok?

            What? No, the tall woman is not my mate. We are sharing dens for safety. She is a very good choice to guard the approach with me - she fights well.

            No, not with her teeth. Her fists merely, but her powers as a Kindred fortify her. The others... well, they were attempting augment their lack of proper weaponry with the sword, and that was less than impressive.

            No, volchonok, they are not pups. Wolfram claims to have some skill with firearms - yes, yes, human things - and Dearbhail is not unskilled with a knife. Lizette seems similarly comfortable, but her choice to wield the chainsaw is odd.

            Chainsaws are another human thing, very loud. Very destructive too, but it requires gasoline to be anything other than an unwieldy club however.

            Bah, I should know better than to say ‘human’ and expect you to listen. We have found our prey, yes, but it is not so simple.

            Hush, it is not. He has a thing, a powerful thing, that is our true goal. And there are other hunters that we must discover.

            No, not Uratha. At least, none that we have seen so far. I do not know what the others have found, but one of the young women, at least, has some magical powers. She used them to cheat at cards, so either she is quite foolish, or it may simply indicate she has resources to spend trivially.

            There are many options, volchonok, that do not involve what you suggest. I myself shall do nothing as of yet however. Do not growl at me so. This path is much more well known by my fellows in this. They lead the hunt for now, with words and politeness and sneaking. Marek and Dacian spent much of the evening away. I presume they were breaking into the baggage car, and possibly others.

            Because that is the most obvious place to hide this Golden Eagle. We would be remiss in not making some effort to exclude that obvious answers.

            You are still not to chase our songbird. She and the others spoke with our fellow passengers, and played cards. They did not speak with our prey directly - much of the evening had passed before he was identified.

            Silly volchonok. I had no knowledge of his scent. Clearly. The others did not speak with him as he was playing it as I did, standing aloof, watching. He could not resist the English spy’s persistent entreaties to play cards however. That one is another to be after the Eagle, likely. Definitely some sort of spy. Dacian will speak with him more, in Bucharest.

            Yes, speak with.

            No, volchonok, we cannot simply kill them all and run off into the darkness. Not tonight.


            Dacian’s Book of Shadows

            1 May 1920 - Morning

            No one entered my dreams last night. Well at least not of their own accord. Pity.

            Today we prepare for our great adventure. I do not know what to think of this.

            1 May 1920 - Night

            Marek and Tatiana spared today. Tatiana was in wolf form for most of the fights. Alla joined in, but I’m not sure if it was to practice or attempt to put on a show. She is stronger than she looks. Lizette built a sizable bomb, just in case as she says. I assisted her in hiding it in a large bear doll. I believe they call them Teddy Bears now. Something about an American hunter, but it makes no sense to me.

            I assisted Wolfram after he mentioned the need to learn the arts of swordsmanship. I tutored him with my own rudimentary skills until Dervala come upon us. She is perhaps the most skilled with a blade of anyone. Lizette mentioned using a flaming chainsaw, which is simply horrifying to consider, but otherwise, Dervala is the most competent.

            Afterward, there was discussion of a more specific nature as to what we could do. I admitted that I could move without leaving tracks or by changing my tracks and also make it easier to move about unseen. Dervala sings, though I suspect she is capable of more. No one pressed the issue so we let it go. Wolfram and Marek track and/or hunt ghosts and spirits respectively. Tatiana hunts humans. She did not elaborate and as I believe I still fall into that category, I felt no need to ask for clarification. Fenric is apparently there solely to make a spectacle, while Alla seems well versed in traversing the social gauntlet, as it were. Lizette is an actual inventor and seems incredibly well versed in the newest advances (and possibly some which are still undiscovered) which science has to offer. We are quite a collection, so to speak, indeed.

            2 May 1920 - Night

            The trip into Vienna was uneventful, though the destruction left by the war was shocking. It seems that mine was not the only village utterly erased during the conflict. I grew morose and said little as we made our way into the larger city. I can only imagine how the desolation left by the Armies of the Sable Scales would look. Sleep will be a welcome escape from the constant reminders of the devastation war machines leave in their wake.

            3 May 1920 - Afternoon

            I have not had the chance to update earlier, though the stationary provided on the train is rather lovely. We boarded just after brunch and found our sleeping compartments. Fenric seemed completely boggled by my desire to spend time in the dark without shoes. Separately, we mingled as gently as possible so as not to draw undue suspicions. I sat just beyond a couple speaking of travel, but soon I allowed myself to be drawn into a conversation with William Forsyth and his American acquaintance, Suzy. William seemed to be on assignment from Britain while Suzy was traveling the world while she could. Though my curiosity was piqued, I decided to withdraw when William began trying to casually pull everyone into a game of Bridge.

            Marek and I used the opportunity to enter a fortuitously unsecured baggage car and search the bounty of readily available information about our traveling companions. Or in the case of the conservative mother and daughter, lack of information. We overheard Alla speaking with the pair as they discussed traveling on vacation. Unless they are keeping all their belongings in their sleeping car, I question their presence. However they were not so easily painted as our targets. When we entered, I used my Warlock’s Gaze but found the car bare of any magical items. So we moved to search the old fashioned way. Marek is very helpful in assisting in such efforts. I continue to wonder what kind of courier he is. William’s belongings included a polished shotgun, a well maintained revolver, and ammunition. While I am no expert in firearms, the cartridges for the shotgun and pistol were easily matched, though the rounds for a third undiscovered weapon was concerning. None of us noticed any signs that William was armed in the social car, so hopefully that means his sleeper is well appointed. Ewan Brandon, our intended mark, is a Scotsman who keeps to himself. This fact alone made him suspicious. But his lack of anything interesting, not least the Golden Eagle, in his bags after our previous findings were a stark contrast. Lizette and Wolfram spoke with a Christina, who was travelling to Turkey to finalize some sort of family paperwork. That charade crumbled when I found a specially crafted lock to secure her baggage. I could hear Marek blink in surprise as I simply opened that lock as if I had the key. And were I a common thief, or perhaps on less important business, I would have been thrilled at what I found. Christina had a treasure trove of jewelry as well as a quite functional dagger. Suzy the supposed American, had multiple costumes as well as wigs and disguise kits, which led us to wonder what she really looks like? Lastly we found various crafted bones, divination tools, and even what may be a ritual staff in the Austrian pair’s baggage.

            The Austrian women were easily identified later as Vera and Gloria. They had some magic about them which Tatiana observed was cast on Lizette during the Bridge game. I could not tell that anything had changed by looking, but then we believe it may have been simply to cheat at cards. We reconvened after the Bridge matches to compare notes. Dervala has some Spring Contracts and used them to learn that Vera and Gloria were absolutely trying to find the Golden Eagle. Marek and I were planning to examine the sleeping cars next anyway, but after these revelations, it seems like a requirement.

            Also a trip to the dining car soon would not be unwelcome.


            Lizette’s Plans

            It is May Day. We spent the day at Jamal’s residence. I have prepared several items for our journey.

            -A replica of the Golden Eagle, which we can replace the original with. It is based on sketches and a photo, but I am hopeful it will work. Even if we don’t use it now, it may be useful in the future.

            -A bomb powerful enough to bisect a train car and devastate much of it. With a timer. For Mayhem.

            -A teddy bear I can fit the bomb inside of (With help from Dacian).

            The group had little to say about the replica. Dearbháil and Wolfram had concerns about the bomb. They just don’t appreciate mayhem like I do. Of course I am not planning to use it. I mean, you load a pistol when you step outside…you are not hoping to use the pistol, but you are prepared to. Similar, then, with a timed high explosive device. You don’t step on the train expecting to use a timed high explosive device (for mayhem) or hoping to (for purposes of this example) but as with our hypothetical pistoleer, one needs be prepared to. For mayhem.

            I also showed off my chainsaw. Such potential these things have. Excellent tools, excellent weapons. I discussed some ideas about igniting the blade, possibly spraying a target with gasoline then igniting the blade so the target bursts into flames while being sawed. It should be noted this is an excellent deterrent. Few really want to charge a person with a chainsaw, fewer if it is on fire, and fewer still if you just sawed someone in half in front of them. I am in the Leaden Mirror after all; fear is my chief weapon. Then surprise I suppose. But eh.

            There was also discussion of using the Hedge to escape if the bomb was set. Mayhem!

            While I was exercising my genius, most of the others were sparring. Tatiana was quite keen on it. Probably a Werewolf thing. Alla apparently joined later. I think I heard something about Dearbháil and a knife, but that can’t be right. I think Dacian perhaps wisely decided to let, as they say, Werewolves be Werewolves, but I am sure Wolfram sparred some. Or at least I assume. Actually I sort of hate I missed that. German steel vs. a Werewolf is probably something to see.

            Did not flirt with Dearbháil, too nervous. Not sure why. It’s not like women are scarier than men, right? I am not afraid of men especially hot ones. I mean she is Spring Court for crying out loud. Prudes don’t last long among the Antler Crowned. Oh well.

            It’s been a good workday. Hopefully the boys enjoyed beating each other up.


            3rd May 1920

            It would probably help to date these damn things. Last one was 1st May, one before that was 30th April.

            We are on the train. No mayhem yet. Dearbháil and I settled into our room. She felt a little out of place with the crew. I tried to reassure her best I could. She is one of the most charming women I have ever met. Surely we can find something for her to do. Also a talented singer, and possibly with Spring Contracts healer. I am not of the Emerald Court, but I hear they have some useful desire detection and manipulation abilities. We do. With fear I mean.

            I also asked if she fancied anyone. She did not say so. I am surprised; I mean we have several rather handsome fellows among us. And me of course. And the vampire, but she is scary. Also, as mentioned, Spring Court.

            We met a number of other passengers. I met the most charming Austrian woman named Christina Esterházy. She has the sort of eyes you dive into and swim around in until the whirlpool draws you so deep you are lost. I was, once or twice. She is traveling to Istanbul to get some items related to her family’s holdings. Documents or something. Noble stuff. Wolfram, being noble, talked to her too.

            She seemed really nice, though Dacian said everyone is up to something. You know, after looking through their luggage.

            This Brit named William Forsyth drew everyone into a big game of bridge. Bridge is something I learned after traveling to Paris. It is not my favorite game, but a good social lubricant. He seemed especially keen to draw in our target, Ewan Brandon, who was sitting in the common car being antisocial. I counted to twenty in my head and when Wolfram had not asked Christina to be his partner I asked her. She seemed charmed. Unfortunately, I think she merely finds me amusing. Wolfram paired with Dearbháil, and a pair of Theosophists present stuck together. Christina and I got the Theosophists first and we were trounced. Tatiana told me they used some kind of power on Christina and I, so maybe psychic stuff, Theosophists are always going on about that. Then we switched and barely won against the Brits while the Theosophists clobbered our teammates.

            I learned nothing of Ewan, despite William’s apparent desire to get more from him. Dearbháil confirmed the Theosophical girls are seeking the Eagle. A mother and daughter pair did not remain for bridge so I really know nothing of them, but Alla spoke to them. William was chumming with a girl named Suzy from America, though according to Dacian she had disguise stuff in her trunk in the back and William had weapons among other things.

            Fun trip so far! Mayhem!

            End Session II

            I don’t have a great deal of additional information to add to their accounts. Amusedly, it was only Wolfram’s player who had a more than minor knowledge of Bridge. I had some knowledge but had to get him to confirm a few things. We used the game playing system in Winter Masques, with some hijinx.

            There is little else to add. Warlock’s Gaze used by Dacian is from Contracts of Spellbound Autumn in Lords of Summer.

            Some (hopefully) intriguing personalities. Let’s see what happens. Comments and Questions welcome.

                                        Time for the climax of our first story. Our heroes battle Angels, Demons, and Psychics but come up one Golden Eagle stronger

                                        Session IV

                                        Marek’s Thoughts

                                        5 May 1920

                                        It is done. We retrieved the Eagle and defeated the monster. It was of course not as simple as that, but it is accurate. The trap we laid for the monster worked for the most part. The explosive created an exit for Tatiana, but as she was getting out the monster stabbed my fellow Uratha through the side with silver blades. Tatiana made it out but within moments she jumped from the train. I could tell from the look on her face that she was in a good deal of pain and she was starting to frenzy. Having had that happen myself I understood why she flung herself from the moving train. She would be a danger to the rest of us if she stayed, so she removed herself from the equation.

                                        The second explosive went off like it should and flung the bladed monster a good distance away. I took note of approximately where it landed then jumped off the train myself to go after Tatiana. I figured that she would want to personally deal with Gerta, so I hopped off and waited the fifteen or so minutes it would take her to calm down before tracking down my friend.

                                        I found Tatiana easily. Her scent is distinctive, but there was also a lot of blood and that is one of the easiest things to track. I followed the scent right to her and helped her bandage her wounds using my shirt. That bitch got her good and I did not bother to hide the growl that came from my throat. I stayed composed though and offered to lead Tatiana to the monster. She smiled and we headed out.

                                        It was easy to find the monster. It was unconscious, which while less sporting, was better for us. I stood back and let Tatiana step forward and rip the monsters throat out. Apparently instead of blood this thing had motor oil in it and Tatiana spat it out with disgust written on her face. That could not have tasted pleasant.

                                        After the monster was dealt with we headed back to the train, which had stopped in light of the explosion. We integrated ourselves with the milling people and compared notes quickly. Dervala asked Tatiana and I if we were okay, and we assured her that we’d be fine. I do not know if she was completely satisfied with that answer, but we do heal quickly. Silver inflicted wounds take longer to heal, so Tatiana will be at less than full strength for a little while, but she will be okay. Tatiana and I noticed pretty quickly that Lizette was not among us and we, along with Dervala, decided to head back on the train and look for our wayward comrade. I believe Dacian may have come along too, but I lost track of him.

                                        We found Lizette engaged in combat with a now electrified monster version of Suzie. We knew that she was supernatural in some way and now we know what. Or at least we know what form we takes, but we are still not sure what exactly she was.

                                        Lizette was attacking Suzie with a hammer, and Suzie was doing her level best to hurt Lizette by shocking with electricity. Suzie had the Eagle and was trying with some amount of success to kill Lizette. Our slight friend was not doing well when we arrived. Tatiana and I rushed forward and attacked the electric creature. Tatiana went for her legs, trying to sweep them. She got a decent shock and was knocked out. I attacked, also going for her legs, and was also wounded for my trouble. The monster then attacked me in return and I felt the same sharp burn of electricity as before. It was not a pleasant experience.

                                        Lizette used our distraction to grab the Eagle and drop the hammer that she’d been using as a weapon. I grabbed the hammer, thinking it might make a good insulator from the electricity.

                                        Though sheer force of will Tatiana came around and got back up again. Though she knew she would only end up hurting herself, she attacked the creature one more time. The attack was successful and the monster that was Suzie fell apart. Literally. Into many pieces of junk. I’m not sure if that is what is supposed to have happened, but it makes cleaning up the “body” much easier.

                                        Tatiana was knocked out again from the electricity and I carried her back to her room and laid her on her bunk. I figured it would only be a short time until she woke up again, but figured out of sight was better, and the bunk would be more comfortable than the floor.

                                        Dervala fussed over Lizette, who was not looking very good, but seemed to be in a considerably better mood than she had been. We gathered the group together and Wolfram hid the Eagle within himself so it would be secure. We then agreed to hide the duplicate Eagle among the Theosophists things. They may not know it is a fake and stop looking for it. Gloria at least will have a hazy memory of what happened. She was also in the room that Lizette and the monster (what Lizette called a golem) had been in, but she was already unconscious on the floor when we arrived. It looked like she’d been knocked out by a blunt object. We assumed correctly that it was Lizette and her hammer that did that.

                                        We agreed to continue traveling on the train until we get to Bucharest. From there we will find alternative transportation to Malta and meet up with Jamal. It had been suggested that we just walk, but some of the others did not seem too keen on the idea. Tatiana was all for walking, but I am glad we decided to stay on the train. She is still badly hurt and the rest will do her some good.


                                        7 May 1920

                                        We met up with Jamal. Dervala initially confronted Jamal about the fact that acquiring the Eagle had been a test and she admitted that she wasn’t sure if it had been his doing or not. He defended himself by saying that if it had been a test on his part then the Fae members of our party would have felt the promise break. But it had not broken, so that goes a long way to proving that Jamal did not have anything to do with the test.

                                        We described the monsters that were Gerta and Suzie and he seemed to know about them, or things like them. He said that he had heard of a powerful entity that strives to change history, and that this test and the monsters was their handiwork. He called Suzie a rogue caretaker of reality and he warned us that our plans and those of the caretakers might clash. I am not sure what these “caretakers” are, but I do know that they are dangerous. They appear to have different powers between them, at least the two that we came across did. I believe that Ewan is one of these caretakers, though whether or not he is “rogue” is another question entirely. I saw him turn into a swarm of small mechanical wasps, so I have some small insight into his powers. I am fairly certain that the small size of the wasps would do nothing to dampen their effectiveness in a fight.

                                        Jamal also showed us how to use the Eagle. We each needed to put a drop or two of blood and a few hairs in a compartment. We are now bound to the Eagle and it to us. I think it will be a useful thing to have, but I will reserve final judgement until more time has passed.


                                        8 May 1920

                                        We are a pack. When Tatiana brought up the idea of becoming a pack with our traveling companions I nodded and she mentioned the idea to the others. They all seemed interested in the idea, which I’ll admit was good to hear. I have grown to enjoy the company of this motley group. I did mention to Tatiana and will have to bring up with the group the idea of bargaining with a spirit to gain a totem for our pack. I shall have to ask the others what kind of spirit would best represent all of us, then go about finding it. I am confident we will be successful.

                                        Because, silly volchonok, the metal monster had silver blades, remember?

                                        I did hunt without you, yes, but do not pretend you did not keep your distance on purpose when you smelt the Kuruth.

It was child’s play to track her down, and finish the kill, for all of her silvered flesh and oil for blood. The Eagle...

                                        Yes, impatient volchonok, I also smell of burning. I was about to say, the small one, LIzette, found the Eagle, and tussled with another metal monster, the not-American Suzie. We were lucky to find her when we did, as she had some power over electricity. It was like biting fire, but Marek and I finished her as well.

                                        I will introduce you to that human thing next time we come across an electric fence. You may mark our territory there, and see how you like it.

                                        I agree. Bah. It would serve us well, the moonlight above and the earth beneath our paws and space to be free. Even humans such as these are foolish.

It was not Jamal who set this test however - his promise would have been broken as soon as Gerta’s silver pierced my flesh, and the fairy creatures would know.

                                        Well, we will not. It will be a castle not in a city. Perhaps near a town. I could bring Elena there. She would be safer.

                                        No, you still may not chase the songbird.

                                        Dacian’s Book of Shadows

                                        5 May 1920 - Early Morning

                                        The night proved far more exciting than we could have imagined. Hopefully Tatiana and Lizette will recover quickly for us to speak of it fondly.

                                        After our planning session, the others looked to me to distract the guard outside Christina’s door. I had no idea of the man’s ability but I knew of a way to remove him from the area. I left Dervala in my room with the door open and the lights out then slipped down the hall. The guard had no idea why the lights in the car suddenly went dark. That the darkness then seemed to close in and surround him did nothing to reassure the man. Once I was confident he could not identify me through the darkness, I moved from my hidden corner and slowly crawled along the floor of the car as though I were climbing a steep wall. My intentions were to drive the man into a dark room with Dervala, a not unpleasant event. However the guard was more dedicated than I expected. Being a gentleman, he knocked on Christina’s door before slipping inside and closing the door behind him. I joined Dervala and closed my door.

                                        I peered out my door after we heard what sounded like a large dog and a suit of armor running down the hall. Unfortunately I did my job too well and I could only see my darkness. Just after I closed my door, I heard a strange metallic buzzing move by. I looked out again quickly to see a swarm of what seemed to be metal hornets following the others. Lizette came running by then so I assumed all was well.

                                        Moments later, the train shook from the explosion. I went back to the doorway and noticed Lizette knocking on Christina’s door. They spoke as the train began an emergency stop. I realized that this would be the perfect time to rob the train due to the chaos which would ensure. However, I was attempting to entertain Dervala and did not want the random things the others brought. We exited the train while the conductor, engineer, and several carmen stared in disbelief at the detached and mangled car ripped free from the train as a whole. William was outside as well and asked if I had seen or spoken with Suzy. No one had since before the explosion. Lizette was also missing given William and Christina were together as were Mr. Brandon and one of the Theosophists, Marek and Tatiana, and my Emerald Court companion. Though Tatiana looked as though she was in pain, she and Marek moved to find Lizette.

                                        I remained behind, in theory I told myself to waylay Ewan in case he decided to investigate the missing guests. But if I am honest with myself, I doubt I would have been any help with any sort of conflict. And given the state Lizette, Marek, and Tatiana were in when they I felt rather guilty for not at least making things harder for our targets. Collectively, we gathered our belongings and left the train while still in range of Bucharest. We leave for Trίpolis on the next train on our way back to Malta. I believe Dervala will be fully prepared to face Jamal about our “test” by then.


                                        7 May 1920 - Afternoon

                                        My dreams remain sorely uninterrupted as we found ourselves back safely in Malta. The smell of the spices in the market is incredible. I may never tire of it.

                                        I am however concerned as Dervala seems to be building her case against Jamal and these is feeding an unhealthy aggression in Tatiana. I have given Marek several concerned glances, but he has been looking deeply into nothing, so I assume he is dealing with spirits. Hopefully he is not simply ignoring me.


                                        7 May 1920 - Night

                                        Dervala did not unleash her wrath upon Jamal, though she did lay out our situation and made it very clear she did not appreciate “tests.” For his part, Jamal was very understanding and assured us that he was not involved, as the Lost among us would have felt the oath break. As this didn’t happen, the Irish rage washed away. Jamal is no fool, but neither is he unprepared. The creatures Lizette refers to as golems are the servants of some controlling entity which, like our “test,” changes the flow of events or sometimes history. Something about caretakers of reality. Suzy was a rogue agent among these caretakers but to what end, we could not be sure.

                                        Once trust had been reestablished, Jamal showed us how to use the Golden Eagle. Each of us placed a few drops of blood and a small clipping of hair into a compartment within the piece. Once it was done, the Eagle closed. We all waited a moment, sharing confused glances, before I asked if that was it. Jamal approached and confirmed that we were finished.

                                        We are apparently now bound to the Golden Eagle. Disappointingly, I feel no different.


                                        8 May 1920 - Evening

                                        We are a strange motley, but it works for us. The werewolves apparently felt the same as Tatiana gathered us together and requested that we form a pack. It was an honor to be considered as family among our strong and proud Uratha. And truth be told, it was good to have a family again. There is some talk of binding a spirit to us as a totem guide. I am unsure as to how this works, but I will stand with my brothers and sisters as we learn together.

                                        There is also talk, from Alla and Fenric, of purchasing a castle to hold as our own. Marek and Tatiana speak of territory, but the safety of such a structure, both within and without, is incredible. My father would be so proud and so sickly envious. It is good to have a family again.

                                            It’s the off week for Golden Eagle, but seems a good time to put up some journals covering what people are getting up to during the downtime. We more or less wrapped the last story at May 7th, 1920 and start the new one June 21st, 1920, so a month and a couple weeks past.                                             Downtime I

                                            Marek’s Thoughts

                                            15 May 1920

                                            Reznik got another job the other day. I completed it last night. The man was not a good person. During the war he profited by conning people out of hard earned money and left families homeless or destitute for no other reason other than greed. He tried to run. They all try. He did not succeed. I tracked him as a wolf through the woods. He thought that by hiding in the hills I would be unable to find him. I will give him credit, he knew the woods well. He may have eluded someone else, but not me. My spirit companion urged me to just tear the man’s throat out. I won’t say I wasn’t tempted, but Reznik’s kills have to be distinguishable from Marek’s. If my employer should see the state of the corpse, as is sometimes insisted, then he’d wonder at the wounds. I did want this kill to be a little more personal, so I used my knife to cut his throat. Not as satisfying, but effective nonetheless.

                                            Sometimes I wonder at my choice of tribe. You would think I should be one of the Iron Masters since they hunt man, but that is Reznik’s favored prey and I try to keep him separate from myself. I created Reznik as a way to keep my more illicit activities separate from myself so they cannot be traced back to me or mine. This was all before I became Uratha though. So now my favored prey are troublesome spirits. I have not had as much opportunity of late to pursue that but I will make time. I did come across a spirit of death while searching for an appropriate totem spirit. The death spirit was haunting, for lack of a better word, a small town in Russia. I’m assuming the spirit was created during the war and followed someone home. There had been a rash of people getting angry and giving into their rage over small quarrels. I will admit it was satisfying to see the look on its face when it went insubstantial and I hit him anyway. He tried to run too, but I made my next attack count and he didn’t get far.


                                            30 May 1920

                                            I found a spirit willing to be our totem. She is a spirit of protection which boded well. She seemed interested in my offer and was curious to meet the others. I could not introduce her to everyone, but she at least insisted on meeting Tatiana. I left a message for Tatiana at our territory in France and she met up with us shortly after. I believe the spirit was suitably impressed with her. Ochranńy, as I’ve taken to calling the spirit, can see to the heart of a person and deemed the both of us worthy. Tatiana and I both have family that we keep safe, no matter the means. I think Ochranńy appreciated that. So she agreed to be our totem on a provisional basis until she meets the others. I think she’ll stay though. She seemed quite amused by Tatiana and my spirit companions, and by my naming of her. She laughed at me when I asked if I could call her Ochranńy, since it is the Czech word for protective, but she agreed since the non-Uratha members of our pack cannot pronounce First Tongue. As for Tatiana’s spirit companion, she calls hers volchonok (wolf cub) which is apt. I call mine Dĕdeček (grandfather). Dĕdeček has seen Volchonok and seems amused by his antics as well. But then wolf pups are playful by nature.

                                            The next time we get together as a pack we will do the ritual to bind Ochranńy to us. I’ve never done this myself, but I learned how to do it from Alexej. I just need to make sure that the bargain is fair to the spirit and to us and then actually bind it. I hope none of the others have a problem with blood.


                                            5 June 1920

                                            Got another job for Reznik. This one is for a couple. A man and his wife have been seemingly generous in their community and have taken in several war orphans to live on their farm. It was suspected that not all was right there, and it was confirmed not long ago that they beat the children and force them to work the farm. There are about a dozen children ranging in age from 5 years to 15 years. I’m not sure what made them think that this was a good idea. Maybe they wanted free labor to work the farm and figured kids would be the way to go. Most of the children are older, but there are a couple young ones too. The couple, the Deuderman’s fled once it was discovered by the authorities what was going on at the farm. I was in the town making a delivery, which was my cover so I could investigate. Early in the day I arranged to go near the farm so I could see the children. I saw firsthand their injuries and living conditions.

                                            Late in the evening I went back by the farm house, but this time to get a scent to track the famer and his wife by. I thought everyone was asleep as all the lights were off, so I went in Dalu form. One little boy was awake and saw me. He was quiet for a moment but did not seem frightened. He had a black eye and some other bruises showing on his arms. He asked if I was going to find the Deuderman’s. I said yes. He then asked if I was going to kill them. I saw no reason to lie to the child, so I said yes. He said, “good.” I smiled. He asked who I was and I told that I was a spirit who protected little children from evil people. He asked why I did not help him when his parents were killed. That was harder to answer. I told him that because of the war there were so many children to help that I can’t get to everyone. He seemed to accept that answer. He then asked if our meeting should be kept secret. I told him that some people were afraid of my kind, so it would be best to not mention anything. He smiled and said that he understood. He wished me good luck and I left.

                                            Throughout the whole conversation with the boy something was trying to make itself known. I figured it out right before I left. He kid is wolf-blooded. I do not know if he’ll become Uratha one day, but he is a child of the wolf or moon.


                                            7 June 1920

                                            I found the Deuderman’s. They were not hard to track. The husband sweat like a pig and didn’t smell much better. The woman was sick I think, and had a very distinct scent. Easy to track. When I found them they begged and pleaded. They even offered me money. I had to laugh at that. All of my contracts as Reznik are paid, but I would have done this one for free. My laugh was perhaps not the most comforting sound they heard so the husband pulled out a gun. I shifted form and attacked. Before I could finish him off the husband got a couple good shots in, but by that point it didn’t matter. I used the extra energy to heal the wounds faster, which again, was not a comforting sight. At least not for the wife. She ran. I found her. Turns out she had a gun too. She managed to get a shot off. It was a good one, but not good enough. The bullet grazed my forehead. Things were blurred for a moment, but I recovered fast enough. She didn’t make it two steps after that. When I went back to collect my payment for the job I told Hans that I left the bodies in the woods for the wolves. He seemed satisfied. I know I was.


                                            10 June 1920

                                            I have been using my connections as a courier to find homes for the children that I orphaned once again. Not that they minded their keepers suddenly disappearing, but they did need homes. I told the authorities in the town that I heard what happened and wanted to do my civic duty to help. Not a complete lie, but I’m not doing this for any civic duty. I’m doing this because they are children and while not necessarily helpless, they nonetheless need help. I am in a position to do so.

                                            I also plan on heading home to visit father and Andel. I can see if anyone at home would like to adopt a child. There are one or two Czech children, a few German, a couple Russian. Quite the mix. I didn’t ask how the farmer and his wife gathered children from all over, but I suppose if they were sent away from home to get away from the war that might account for some of it.

There also happens to be something big going on very soon involving both tribes of Pure in the area, the Ivory Claws and the Fire Touched. We do not have much information on the Fire Touched as of yet, but we do know who the leader of the Ivory Claws is, and to avoid him if at all possible. I will relate more as soon as I know more.


                                                        Tatiana’s Conversations

                                                        Ah, volchonok. It is good to see you survived the journey. Have the Pure bothered you yet?

                                                        No, I have not changed my human fur to hide from the Pure. The humans would not care for my Russian uniform, here in Vienna.

                                                        Since when do you care so about human things? It is because many people lost many things in the war, and people wearing a Russian uniform did some of the taking from these here.

                                                        Yes, it is foolish. Are you nervous, volchonok, that with a pack of many humans and my Elena soon to be nearby, that I will forget how foolish human things can be? Do you search for reasons for me to turn from them?

                                                        A change of subject, eh? Very well volchonok. Marek and I have met with the Hunter, Krieger, it is true. We have not encountered any of the Pure. This is good. Marek has forbidden me from pissing on their shoes.

                                                        Well, would you like it is I marked your paws as my territory?

                                                        I didn’t think so. The others worry too much over the Pure. So long as we are not stupid, we will be fine and they will be no danger. Even if we are a little stupid, they will be distracted, Krieger says. Something about a museum.

                                                        A museum is a human thing, a building full of old things, and pretty things. Boring.

                                                        No, not pretty things like our songbird. Pretty like pictures, or statues.

                                                        I did say it was boring. Listen, you volchonok - stay away from the Pure Eggenberg. That will be a glorious fight, but Marek is likely correct, a short one should it happen now. He is a powerful creature, and I do not want him making a pet of you.

                                                        Oh, there will be fighting. Krieger perhaps has some task we could assist with and whoever took the necklace is unlikely to give it over willingly.

                                                        The reason we are here, silly volchonok. The woman from the train that caught Lizette and Wolfram’s fancy, she obtained an amulet with dark magic on it. It was supposed to restore her family’s fortune.

                                                        By being sold, I suppose. Not that she is inclined to, even should we find it. She and her maid are both obsessed with the thing.

                                                        If we knew who took it, I would right now be threatening them until they gave it back. I do have the thief’s scent, so I know who it is not - the servants, the lady, a werewolf. They are quite good at picking locks, Dacian says, with either magic or much much money. Not his magic, he says, or else I would not have been able to follow the scent back through the maid’s room, then the lady’s room, then to where they came into the hall.

                                                        Come now volchonok. No, he is not my mate. Not everyone I den with is my mate. It is to be less conspicuous that we have such quarters. Now, Marek and our songbird might yet mate. She does enjoy looking at him.

                                                        No, you are still not allowed to chase her.

                                                        Because he is not a silly volchonok.

                                                        Yes, it is also because he leads the pack and because a mate who is also in my pack would come between me and Elena and that will not happen.

                                                        No, she will not be in my pack.

                                                        Because I have said so.

                                                        You think so, eh? Leave me be, if that is how silly a volchonok you are, there will be no running tonight.


                                                        Dacian’s Book of Shadows

                                                        21 June 1920 - Afternoon

                                                        We arrived in Vienna at different times due several concerns. First, a rival coalition of werewolves which collectively call themselves the Pure, are in control of the city. The Uratha are not only unwelcome but are actively hunted if exposed. What sort of egotistical dictator would lead such a pogrom against another group of their own is beyond me. However the Uratha are not ones to simply bare their throats, so to speak, and are waging a series of calculated strikes to weaken the stranglehold the Pure have over Vienna. Second and ironically, Alla’s Invictus vampires are experiencing the same incursion into their domain as the Pure. A group known as the Carthians are mirroring the Uratha’s tactics for what seems to be similar reasons. Both the Pure and Invictus supported and backed the nobility, which suffered significant losses in power after the war. Each were apparently surprised when the “peasants” refused to return to their position of stepping stones.

                                                        I admit I enjoy my expensive tastes, however I also do not burden those which begin with nothing. Coming from a minor noble family, I can say that most nobility would be hard pressed to truthfully say the same. But I am not here to judge.

                                                        Finally, I brought a third concern to the attention of our cabal. If we were identified as a group by these caretakers of reality, it would perhaps be best if we were not so easily recognized in the future. The same concern was shared with the Theosophists from the train, which were conveniently based in Vienna. Dodging political infighting between both werewolves and vampires was one thing; avoiding reality golems and unseen psychics was quite another. As such, I recommended that we move in multiple smaller groups. The cabal was broken into couples so that no one was left alone and to appear as uninteresting as possible. Tatiana and I shared a room as did Alla and Fenric. Wolfram and Lizette, who disguised herself as a child, shared a room. And finally Dearbháil and Marek were left staring at each other with some very interesting tension between them. I was disappointed that I needed no Glamour. I suspect that if this boarding strategy holds, I will not be at a loss for some time. I was actually surprised as Tatiana chose to stay with me. It was assumed by all that she and Marek would share lodging, but she would hear nothing of it.

                                                        I spent an hour out of the sunlight, without my shoes, and relaxed while Tatiana stared at me strangely. Dearbháil and I were made aware of a burlesque house called Gemütlichkeit maintained by the local Spring Court, but I decided it would be best not to learn of such delights this early in the day. Especially not when there was another meeting in the evening.

                                                        21 June 1920 - Evening

                                                        We are about to call upon Lady Christina Esterházy for dinner and more information on the assistance she requested. Dearbháil learned that the Summer Court will assume leadership on 23 June and there will be a party of some form that evening. Marek determined that there would be a pack rite that evening as well. The conclusion of which should strengthen our personal resolve and allow us to heal wounds more quickly. To say I am excited is an understatement. I will get to witness magics of the Uratha firsthand before experiencing the effects before or possibly after enjoying the hospitality of the Summer Court. But now, we must away least Tatiana grow angry with my “wasting time.”

                                                        21 June 1920 - Late Night

                                                        Wolfram suspected the necklace involved dark magics and based on the two individuals which have been in contact with it, so do I. But I am getting ahead of myself.

                                                        Right away Marlena, the Lady’s Maid, seemed to know something about Lizette. I was unsure of the connection, but it was definitely there. After a bit of pleasantries, Christina lost a bit of her happy demeanor and explained why we were invited. Her uncle served as an Austrian commander in the Great War. Because of the treaties and restitutions imposed, the family coffers were empty which had left them low staffed. To attempt to restore a portion of the family fortune, her brother stole a valuable piece of jewelry to sell. It was this necklace which Wolfram spotted while we were travelling together. It was quickly obvious that Christina, though sound in mind in other matters, was completely obsessed with this piece. She has no desire or intention of selling it and wants it returned before Gunter, her brother, arrives home. The situation was very simple on the surface as Marlena was the obvious thief, given the necklace was left in her care while Christina was not admiring it. But as I mentioned, it was only simple on the surface. Both the jewelry box and the key were still present and only a man named Gunnar Hohenburg had seen the necklace other than the remaining staff. Marlena openly disliked the werewolf, as he was one of two Marek had warned us about. Tatiana spoke up to voice her disapproval of him as well, a touch I believe was best left in her mind. That a member of the Pure had been within the Esterházy estate and seen the relic was troubling. That Gunter seemed to enjoy the idea of marrying his sister to the man was more so. As we pieced information together, I noticed that Marlena’s eyes shared her Lady’s obsessive gleam in reference to the necklace.

                                                        I requested to observe the jewelry box and investigate with Marek and Tatiana. I found myself looking through the halls as we walked, planning how I would enter to steal such piece. Getting into the estate would be of no consequence for me. The room in which the necklace was kept however had no windows and the door locked. Secure for not having been built for such things. The jewelry box was locked when we arrived but Marlena turned to retrieve the key. With but a moment’s flinch, I excused “my mistake” as the box was actually open. There was no sign of tampering of any kind. The thief either had the key, used some form of magic, was a Lurker with helpful fingers for such things, or had incredibly expensive and functional lock picks. I used my Goblin Contract while interviewing everyone and found that only Marlena lied, and then only when asked if she had ever worn the necklace. Oddly, when we mentioned something of the occult in relation to Christina, Marlena forgot herself and openly mocked the idea. She believed her Lady to be ignorant of such things.The necklace had been gone for less than a week so it couldn’t have gotten far, especially given the strange addiction it caused. We excused ourselves into the garden to discuss the situation.

                                                        After I confirmed with our Uratha pack mates that we were unobserved, I listed the common powers for a relic of this sort to the others. Often the higher self was penalized for following the path of virtue while acts of vice richly rewarded user. Truly monstrous acts could even help the user gain greater understanding in some ability or knowledge. Physical contact seemed very unwise and the description we received from Marlena was concerning. The pendant was a smooth, black metal of unknown origin holding a large crimson gem in the center. The necklace itself was a chain made of silver, though if the pendant is of a strange metal, the chain may also be more exotic. Not that our Uratha pack mates would enjoy the more “mundane” effects, but it seems strange to mix the two, unless there is a magical reasoning with which I am presently unfamiliar. It is my professional opinion that such relics should be hidden away from the world until such time as I am paid to retrieve it or destroyed where it can harm no one. I believe the cabal is in agreement with at least one of those options.

                                                            It’s the off week, but I wanted to post some information for Golden Eagle

                                                            The Below is a general breakdown of how it works. The Players have seen this information but the characters, of course, have only a vague idea.

                                                            The Golden Eagle


                                                            The Golden Eagle has three major effects, slowed aging, limited regeneration and resurrection.

                                                            Most Characters age 1 year for every 5 while linked to the Golden Eagle. Werewolves and Changelings, who age slower anyway, only age 1 year per 10, before taking into account any other slowing aging effects. This has no effect on Vampires or Ghouls. These in frequent intimate physical contact (particularly lovers) age slightly slower, but not to the same extent.

                                                            A character that loses a limb or other part such as an eye can hold it on and have it reattach, even if it normally would not be able to do so, so long as they can find whatever limb they lost. If they limb or part cannot be recovered, they can use the Golden Eagle adding to the blood sample (and spending a permanent Willpower) and grow a replacement that is human looking to normal vision, but to Angels, Demons, or those with Unseen Sense: God Machine looks to be golden and mechanical. It may also be under the control of the God-Machine….

                                                            Vampires regenerate limbs anyway, but this effect does extend to their ghouls. Changelings enjoy the basic regenerative features, but the golden limbs don’t seem to mesh well with them; they only remain attached for 10-Wyrd years, after which the whole process (and another Willpower) must be done again. Natural regeneration or healing powers are not stymied by the above, and function normally and can forestall the need for either effect.

                                                            The Golden Eagle has the following effects if a character protected by it is killed. This does not interfere with a Sin-Eaters restorative powers in any way; a Sin-Eater can choose to raise themselves early, bypassing any negative effects from the Eagle but suffering those tied to being a Sin-Eater.

                                                            The Golden Eagle can raise the dead, if already tied to it. The body remains dead for a time equal to 11-Integrity/Humanity/Harmony/Charity/Synergy days, then resurrects at the end of that time. The body is restored at the Golden Eagle’s location or the body’s, as determined (in world) by the Eagle and (out of world) by the player. It can only raise a body at it if it is currently in the world, not inside a Sin-Eater or a Demon’s temporal space.

                                                            Bodies raised at their current location have as much fuel points as it had when it died, and zero willpower. Bodies reformed at the eagle have 0 fuel stats and 0 willpower. Sin-Eaters raised by the Eagle who died in a way resonating with their threshold immediately gain 5 Plasm, otherwise it stays the same.

                                                            A character with 8+ in their integrity stat does raise with some inspired feeling, and may roll Resolve + Composure (+Psyche, if a Sin-Eater) and gains temporary willpower equal to the result (added to 0, as they always come back with 0). This applies in Reverse to Werewolves, who only get this effect if they rise with 3 or less Harmony, not 8 or more.

                                                            Characters with 6- in their integrity stat wake with horrified feelings or terror. They must roll Resolve + Composure (+Psyche, if a Sin-Eater) or lose a permanent Willpower point. Changelings must make a Clarity check and Vampires a Humanity check or risk losing a point of that as well, while Werewolves make a Harmony check against physical. Sin-Eaters do not make an Integrity check. Note for Werewolves, it is 5 or higher Harmony, not 6 or less.

                                                            If Integrity is 7 (4 for Werewolves) nothing good or bad happens beyond the fuel stat loss.

                                                            A given individual can remain in connection with the Golden Eagle for 33 years (total, regardless of if consecutive) with no ill effects, minus 1 year per death (not counting self-resurrections for Sin-Eaters). After that an attached a person does not regain Willpower from sleeping due to nightmares involving the impending dissolution of their soul, and Sin-Eaters gain a penalty to Vanitas use equal to the number of years (rounded up) since their protection wore off. The slowed aging and regeneration still apply, but if they die during this time a loud shriek can be heard and they do not return. After a number of years equal to their integrity stat after protection is lifted, they simply die in a large flash of light when a loud shriek. A person can disconnect themselves from the Golden Eagle at any time, even after protection has worn off.

                                                            As a final note, Angels can sense the location of the Golden Eagle if nearby, and the God Machine itself is aware of it’s location. So long as anyone is attached to it, Demons can sense its resonance. Note neither Angels nor Demons automatically know what they are sensing.


                                                            Overall, this was to facilitate a game going over the course of almost thirty years, and putting PCs in somewhat more action oriented situations than is typical for my games. I wanted to give some reason to shy away less from danger than is necessarily sane. None of this has come up, but you have seen the system.
