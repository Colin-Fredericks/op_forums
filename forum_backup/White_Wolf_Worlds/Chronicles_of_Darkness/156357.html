    06-02-2014, 06:44 AM
    Each gameline has on forum one this kind of topic to put all needed info, so here's a Werewolf one. My first questions are those:

    Could Pack buy new Totem Influences dots? Even if not by the books, I would make it as house rule - but how much should it cost in Totem Points?

      I just answered this question in your Totem creation thread. :P

      Originally posted by Penta View Post
      There is no houserule necessary to do this. Under the original rules, spirits can buy the Greater Influence Numen (Book of Spirits, p. 142), which grants the spirit one extra dot in influences, as long as the influence does not exceed the spirits rank. Under GMC rules, spirits can exchange Numina for extra dots in influences, which is essentially the same. An extra numen costs three Totem Points, so a dot in an influence also costs this much.

      If you want to add that many influences or Numina to the Totem, you could think about granting it the Ban of Power Numen (Book of Spirits, p. 138). This lets the Totem take on an additional ban (or possibly bane, under GMC rules) that is more punitive than the spirit's default ban and gains the Totem two extra Numina, for a net increase of one.

                                                          Pregnancy is often poorly portrayed in role-playing games, and Unihar heavily played into pregnancy=horror! tropes that we could do without. It plays into sex=bad tropes as well, while also seriously skewing the role that Wolf-Blooded played in the game (do we really need to have Wolf-Blooded = breeding stock dominating their presence as a game element?). Unihar impacted on female characters more than male ones, and turned rape into a truly vile weapon of war (we really didn't need The Pure to go there).

                                                          Unihar just blundered through a whole load of cliches that roleplaying games have traditionally been terrible about. They existed in order to help tie Uratha to humanity, but the intention is to resolve that conundrum through other methods rather than the Black Birth.
