                                Ahahah, oh man, I'd totally planned to come back to this thread and answer that one myself if it looked like it was holding up the thread, I'd just forgotten. I need to perform penance for dropping a thick song like that and stalling the thread out now.

                                Mantis's Hymn, divorced from the context of a flying bald man who reads your memory card, is a lament. Not only is the song itself dirgelike, soft, low and deep, but it rings hollow. The choral accompaniment (and it sounds like an accompaniment; no one set of instruments really takes center stage in the hymn) is unreal, imitative. It's just a synthesizer, as is everything else about the hymn. Ghostly, sad and strange, colorless and tired. This is the caliber of music you will hear in the majority of the Autochthonous Depths when outsiders have not brought life to intrude upon that dark place.
