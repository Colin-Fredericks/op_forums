    Originally posted by MCN View Post
    "The Beast can blast individual targets in range (5/10/15) with gouts of fire. This counts as a ranged attack with a thrown weapon and requires a roll of Dexterity + Athletics – the opponent’s Defense. Damage is lethal and adds the Beast’s Lair dots as a weapon modifier." BtP Chapter 3 p78 (emphasis mine).

    All attacks are instant actions.
    Actually, at no point in Beast: the Primordial are attacks ever defined as being instant actions. What the rules presented in Beast actually say are: "On your turn, your character can attack using one of the following dice pools..." "...Resolve the attack roll like any other action. Determine damage by adding the successes rolled to any weapon bonus...” This is another thing that should be corrected at some point. If the Beast book is supposed to include all the rules necessary to play the game, then a statement that making an attack is an instant action should be included in it.

    I think the intent here is that the Reflexive is to gain the ability to breath fire, then an Instant action to make a ranged attack. Perfectly valid way to read the power in question, it makes reasonable sense to me, and it is consistant with how Basilisk's Touch works. So, I'm going with this.
    This is not what the power says. At no point in the actual text of the power is there any suggestion that the power is divided into the steps you describe (step 1, reflexively gain the ability, step 2, use an instant action to make a ranged attack). These two steps ARE described in the text of Basilisk's Touch as a means of spelling out the fact that the Atavism only makes your touch poisonous, but Dragonfire is clearly not of the same kind of atavism as Basilisk's touch. Basilisk's Touch enhances attacks you are already making. It makes your touch poisonous. It is of the same kind as Limb from Limb and Cyclopean Strength, which also enhance actions you are already making.

    Dragonfire, on the other hand, is the ability to breathe fire. Breathing fire counts as a ranged attack. It is of the same kind as Titanic Blow, which is the ability to make titanic blows that destroy objects (which requires a Strength + Brawl roll to hit, and is identified as an attack in the following text: "her attack ignores an amount of object durability..." There is no need for superfluous rules text here of the type you have described (and which are absent in the actual text), which, as near as I can tell, translate to, "this Atavism reflexively gives you the ability to use this Atavism."

    If you want to insist that the only way to read the power is as a Reflexive attack...
    I do not want to insist upon this, but it is, in fact, what the power says. It is obviously not what was intended, should never be allowed to work like that, and should be changed immediately, but it IS what the writers actually wrote. Why I suspect this happened is that the Dragonfire Atavism also includes an immunity to fire, heat, and smoke, and that part of the power is and should be reflexive.

    Storm-Lashed also doesn't talk about making ranged attacks, which is a rather important thing.
    Actually, it does. Calling down lightning is defined as being a ranged attack, and Sand in the Eyes says that, "This ability causes no damage, but a successful attack applies the Blinded Tilt..." Knock Back should probably be defined in its rules text as a ranged attack as well, but isn't explicitly labelled one.

    Basilisk's Touch requires touch attack, Dragonfire requires ranged attack,
    'COUNTS AS' a ranged attack. Not 'requires.' There is an important distinction.
