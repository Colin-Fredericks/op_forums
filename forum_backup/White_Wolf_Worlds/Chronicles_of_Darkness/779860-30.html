    My basic idea is a corporation named Poseidon International, a multi-national corporation founded by a Norwegian oceanographer named Roy Petterson. The corporation is based in Oslo, Norway and claims to be researching the ocean to figure out how it's untapped resources can benefit humanity. The general public is aware of two of their projects, both of which seem to be benevolent. The first being a study of sea sponges to see how they are resistant to cancer and a way to possibly transplant that trait to humans. and the second being a search for a cheaper method of desalination. However, the general public is unaware of a third, more insidious experiment. This experiment involves the capturing of Sirens and the harvesting of their DNA in a attempt to make humans amphibious so it could make colonizing the bottom of the ocean possible.

    Although the sirens consider Poseidon to be ignorant Flensers unaware of the secret potency of their flesh, if you were to call one of their scientists one you would be barraged with insults and a possible punch to the face. Even though Poseidon and the Flensers have some similarities, the differences out measure the similarities. In fact, Fesnsers and Poseidon hate each other with a burning passion. The Flensers accuse Poseidon of stealing their lawful prey while Poseidon accuses them of killing creature that could possibly benefit humanity. This often results in Poseidon research ships and Flenser vessels fighting each other in gun fights.

    How Poseidon figured out about the existence of Sirens is unknown. Theories range from one of their research vessels stumbling upon a wounded one all the way to an Abyssal Siren being the current chairman of the company. The most horryfing theory however, is the idea that the entire corporation is serving Echidna. This theory states that the comapny is trying to aid Echidna by capturing sirens and crossing their DNA with humans. Whatever theory is true the sirens know one thing, Poseidon will continue to be a constant threat to their existence.

Alchemists can drain the Vitriol (i.e. specialized Experience from the Pilgrimage) from Prometheans and have a Magnitude trait to mimic Promethean abilities and store Pyros. They gather in guilds interested in a certain cause. Much of the critique, mostly from others on this site, is the notion that to have Alchemists be villains they would have to disregard their humanity to prey upon the Created. An Alchemist off focused on something else wouldn't be a threat and then wouldn't appear in (the majority of) the Chronicle.

                                            Flensers have been given their own rules and abilities derived from centuries of science and eating Siren flesh. In my viewpoint I like it how there are quite the fraction of Flensers who do realize their transgression when eating Siren flesh and it drives them to find a way where such a practice would not be needed. Yet until then they still need their fix...

                                            I've spent the evening drafting up new Alterations and Merits for consideration and critique. I don't think they overlap too greatly with the current list. If anything sounds too complex it could be split into separate criteria.

                                            These first two I would have listed as Alterations that could effect the Siren's human form, although given only Malformations do that and there are no drawbacks using it turning them into Merits gave it better universal coverage. (Yet a Malformation similarity is listed far below)

                                            Carnivorous (••)
                                            Once per scene, the Siren may gain 1 Pneuma from eating the flesh of an animal native to her Water, minimum of Size 1. She gains an additional Pneuma if she hunted the creature herself. Much like the regular way of regaining Pneuma from her Water, multiple Waters may overlap in regard to the species eaten for this benefit.

                                            Herbivorous (••)
                                            Once per scene, the Siren may gain 1 Pneuma from eating vegetation or fungus native to her Water, minimum of Size 1. She gains an additional Pneuma if this vegetation was raised and harvested by herself. Much like the regular way of regaining Pneuma from her Water, multiple Waters may overlap in regard to the species eaten for this benefit.

                                            The following Alterations I will list with ideas for category.

                                            Barbed Quills
                                            (General or Discordia)
                                            The Siren's Diluvian form may have rough tiny scales or fine slender spines. This augments her unarmed strikes as 0L damage. She may fire these quills as a Short Burst (up to 5 shots) with a range of 5/10/20 both above and under the water, rolling Dexterity + Firearms. It costs 1 Pneuma to replenish these quills. Additionally, whenever the Siren is in a grapple they may spend 1 Pneuma to leave these quills in her opponent as a 1 Lethal attack. This requires another Pneuma to replenish the quills, as above.
                                            Inspiration: Sea urchins, pufferfish, lionfish

                                            Mollusk's Beak
                                            (General, Tropicana or Discordia)
                                            The Siren has a sharp beak for a mouth that can dislocate or perhaps sharp pincers elsewhere on her body. As a full round action she can bypass the Durability of an object to harm it. She subtracts the greater of her Strength or half Akashic Record from the Durability of the object, leftover value being inflicted as Structure damage. The Siren is denied her Defense when piercing Durability.
                                            Inspiration: Octopi, squids

                                            Bioluminescence
                                            (Tropicana or Polaria)
                                            Faint light glows from the Siren, which can be activated or dampened reflexively. When active she has a soft illumination no greater than a flashlight. This applies as a penalty of half Akashic Record to avoid being noticed, however the dexterous Siren may distract with her light to receive a bonus of half Akashic Record to her Defense for the first round of combat. For 1 Pneuma the Siren may inflict the Blinded Tilt on a single opponent for half Akashic Record turns.
                                            Inspiration: anglerfish, many deep sea creatures

                                            Blowhole
                                            (General or Polaria)
                                            A secondary breathing orifice exists along the Siren's back. She gains the benefit of the 9-again rule when using her Stamina to manage breathing in Diluvian form. She may spend 1 Pneuma to expel a poisonous substance from her system, reducing the remaining Toxicity by Akashic Record. If removing a major pollutant like Biotoxin, the rejection of the substance also inflicts 1 Lethal damage.
                                            Inspiration: whales, porpoises and other cetaceans

                                            Spectral Vision
                                            (Gardenia or Tropicana)
                                            The Siren's eyes are enhanced to see across a wider spectrum. She ignores any penalty to perception that is -1. She can recognize unusual features like bloodstains, body fluids and other residues or trace evidence, unless they were rigorously cleaned. This grants a bonus of half Akashic Record to such a search or investigation. The Siren receives this bonus a number of times per scene up to her Wits.
                                            Inspiration: Shrimp

                                            Horrendous Consumption (Malformation)
                                            How wil humanity (mainly Echidna's agents) survive the blighted aftermath of the apocalypse? This requires the means to find nutrition from the most dubious of sources. The Vodinaoi can eat their chosen food to avoid deprivation regardless of its state, suffering no drawbacks or damage from spoiled and rotten food. Because of the body odor produced from their altered digestive tract, the stench applies a -2 penalty against the Vodinaoi, such as for some Social rolls or when tracking them by scent. Once a scene, when feasting on the selected food the Vodinaoi can gain 1 Willpower for every Size category eaten. Sirens may chose to gain Pneuma instead.

                                            This Malformation can only be taken once. Select the category of food for the Vodinaoi to consume:
                                            • Carnivore's Diet: you are sustained by meat and flesh, however fresh or processed, including other animal byproducts (like eggs and milk). If you attack a creature with the intent to eat it your successful attack gains an additional point of damage (only Bashing or Lethal). Treat any vegetation or fungal matter as a Toxicity 2 substance if ingested.
                                            • Herbivore's Diet: you are sustained by the vegetation and fungal matter of nature. Instead of gaining Willpower you may instead gain temporary dice used for being attuned to your environment for the rest of the scene. This bonus can be divided amongst rolls pertaining to nature such as Science, Survival or Animal Ken. Treat any meat or animal byproduct as a Toxicity 2 substance when ingested.

                                                    Being upfront, these ideas were inspired by elements from the partially complete Undine: the Emergence document. Not word for word, mind, but curious as for how that separately developed idea formed around the time Siren was first being implemented. Curiosity being that game's version of Alterations, and Sirens being one of the Splats on top of it!

                                                    Carnivorous and Herbivorous
                                                    More of the idea with these is that the Penuma gain is less from digestion and more the symbolic nature of it. The Siren gains a bit of power from her place juxtaposed in the circle of life as befitting her Water and the food chain of the ecosystem. There's no true 'soul' to feed from, true, unless it can be ruled that the Pneuma is granted from the Water bridging to the Song like you say.

                                                    If eating the animal is seen as harsh, you could switch it so that the base Pneuma is gained from play with the animal and the second from being killed and eaten. I'll note that in my original draft it didn't have to imply the Siren killed every animal to feed from personally. A Siren with the merit could easily gain the Pneuma from having lobster bisque out with friends one night. They may sit on the spectrum of being friends to all of nature but to eat is a very natural thing. The animals may have fun musicals in the Lion King but the lions still eat the antelope.

                                                    Blowhole
                                                    The main effect of the Alteration is the poison/Toxicity removal system, although as not every Alteration is Pneuma dependent I didn't want it to be the only thing the Alteration did. I had forgotten Anuran Breath had the rote quality with it, kind of the main point if its the main 'breathe out of water' alteration. One possible fix is to reduce Anuran Breath to 8-agin and leave Blowhole at 9, the two then stacking to 8. Still, besides the use of 'sneezing' out of ones Blowhole it would be interesting if there was a different breath related effect that could be kept. As mammals cetaceans use their Blowholes to breathe and can stay submerged for quite the length of time...yet Sirens don't have that issue underwater and Anuran Breath does enough prolonging out in the air.

                                                    Spectral Vision
                                                    What I found most interesting from the ability suggested was that some animals, many smaller sea critters, see in an enhanced vision spectrum beyond the visible spectrum we know and can thus see things that are there but aren't always noticed. Like having UV lights on all the time (err, might make your next motel visit all the more uncomfortable). It's true sensory Alterations can be tricky to not overlap, as there are already several senses derived from the different mechanics sea life use (and the Killer Whale's/Orca's Instinct boosting Investigation already).

                                                    What could be useful is tying this Alteration to another newer subsystem in 2E, the Investigative routine (not the skill, the finding Clues and whatnot) much like the Investigative Prodigy merit but without the rating. Since such searches can use a variety of skills, it could be that the Siren's efforts always allow them to find at least 1 Clue even if failing (Dramatic Failure as usual) and their uncovered Clues gain an extra Element (free dice) as well.

                                                    Another way of taking it would be the Aura Sight/Twilight viewing ability, although that's less important for Sirens and the Sense Conclusion already does that job.

                                                    Horrendous Consumption
                                                    This was always going to be the harsher depiction of the dietary Merits above, fitting Echidna's theme that for every adaption she makes there's an evolutionary advantage lost.
