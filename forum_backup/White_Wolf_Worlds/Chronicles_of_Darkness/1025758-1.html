        Unlike Spirit mages, who get left behind without enough spirits to go around, Death mages have plenty of other things to do even without ghosts. And even if most ghosts are Rank 1 and of limited use to mages, Death mages can still boost their Rank to make them useful.

        And for the purposes of determining the ghost population, let's assume the combined number of Rank 1 and 2 ghosts are stable (since Integrity 10 people are indeed going to be rare). Then, the variable factor that decides whether Rank 1 ghosts are more populous or Rank 2 ones are is the average Integrity of humans in the setting.

        You assume Rank 1 ghosts will be the majority with the paramters GimpInBlack put up. This means that you are assuming most people who do leave ghosts do so at Integrity 1-6. This, in turn, means that you are assuming people who suffer Integrity loss do not put extra effort at all into reconstructing their shaken sense of perspective with introspection and therapy (represented in-game by spending Experiences to buy back Integrity)

        Between the large number of people who die at Integrity 1-6, and a sizeable number of people among them who do take effort to build up their Integrity back to 7-9, I don't think the ratio of Rank 1 and 2 ghosts will be that skewed.

                    Now, let's look on GimpInBlack guidelines compared to CoD universe population:

                    Integrity 7 - About 80% of mortals population -> Instant Rank 2 ghosts ( if any will be ).
                    Integrity 6 and less - About 19% of mortals population -> Instant Rank 1 ghosts ( if any will be ).
                    Integrity 8-9 - Less than 1% of mortal population -> Instant Rank 2 ghosts ( if any will be )

                    It suddenly make Rank 2 ghosts most populate supernatural entirety in CoD universe, four times more populous thatn Rank 1 ghosts. But books to this day states it's Rank 1 rather. ( Also Rank 2 ghosts are harder to run, as they need to be much more fleshed out. )
