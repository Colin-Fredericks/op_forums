                    How about making Daniel stuck in a vicious cycle? When loneliness becomes unbearable he goes forth to buy love from others. He showers gifts and help onto whoever catches his eye. However, whether through his infernal powers or corrupting behavior, the outcome is always tainted. Inevitably one of two things happen.                             This is a good observation. It was actually a deliberate choice to avoid filling in certain details, like the "resurrection clause" in the Immortal Dread Power. This is primarily to leave such a thing up to the individual Storyteller that might use the Horror. It is always my intent that any of the things I create can and should be modified to taste.                                 Rasputin was built to be a non-combat threat designed to fill a whole Chronicle, which is highly resistant to brute force solutions. He can enter or exit scenes as needed, and is in no danger from the PCs until they are potent enough to be world shakers. Even then, they'd need a lot of prep or out of the box thinking to be able to enact his Bane, which causes a whole other set of issues since it releases "His Elder God Master" into the material world.
