                                These races have been subjected to unspoken eons of evolution, changed into creatures and beings so utterly different from their roots, even attaining sentience.


                                Gitserai The first signs of these savage creatures are the burning luminescent “clouds” over head, blending in with other glowing lights. The second sign is usually a sharpened blade against your sternum.
                                The evolutionary descendants of raptors from the Mesolithic, this Dwellers race lurks in some of the darkest reaches of the Beneath. Biologically, they resemble winged raptors with their claws having evolved into hands. When unfoldered, these wings tend to glow in a dizzying array of colors, males typically more vibrant. In terms of technology, they are typically limited to hunter-gatherers.
                                They possess psionic abilities, communicating through telepathic-empathy links and empower their claws and teeth to shred many things they ought not be able to otherwise.

                                Fiercely territorial, most gitserai attack or ward away intruders and do not maintain long term connections with Monarchs. Still, they make a worthwhile investment for an armed escort.


                                Modkan This short stock race is covered in hair from head to toe, but often those that see the Modkan do not see them in their natural forms. Gifted with a propensity for crude, yet functional machinery and metalworking, yet a vulnerability to their own industries’s exhausts underground, the Modkan hide their short snouts behind hardened gas masks.
                                Barely standing up to a human’s knee, these industrialists arm themselves with energy weapons to make up for their physical frailty. And should a limb be lost, it can be replaced with the right metallurgy.
                                They do not trust Monarchs for their warping flesh and greater size and will over, but fresh resources and material are highly prized, enough that they might be willing to exchange goods. Light in particular, electrical lights holds a special place in their hearts.



                                Kessen This race is not flesh in blood or even could recognizably be called a “self”. In its dormant state, this nanite machine based… sludge gathers in pools where it busies itself devouring and converting nearby rock and any creatures that spend far too long within its grasp, breaking them down for raw resources. This is called a Spawning Pool
                                But in times of plenty or danger, it forms scouts, converting some of its mass into separate, mobile forms. These creatures replicate organic shapes and structure, forming limbs, exoskeletons, even artificial organs or even brains. Then then go out and find more things to dissolve into the Spawning Pool that made them… or form new ones.
                                An odd blend of biological and mechanical state of beings, these entities are all still machines and are relatively eager to serve.
                                It has been hypothesized that the original Kessen were nanomachines that were lost in the Beneath not too long ago, but evolved rapidly into a new, independent life form over subjective millenia.

                                The Boundless
                                Evolution is not pretty.
                                A horrible implication of the Monarch condition is that ones flesh is not a constant, static form. It writhes, twistes, evolves as the Monarch slowly adapts and unlocks the Principalities she has been given. One might have started with a somewhat humanoid or even a mostly human like appearance, but as the cycles of genetics and adaptation play out, such frailties are shifted for a more beastial, hardened appearance. She does however always retain the ability to human form, to choose not to change, if only for a time.
                                But Monarchs that have spent far too long in the Beneath have a different set of priorities. Trapped in the dark Depths for too long, where time is malleable, survival is the only goal. And evolution, adaptation is the key to survival. In here, maintaining the human identity is nothing but a tax, a limitation, a burden on the more fit, more apt genetics the Kingmakers gifted her with.
                                When pushed too far in these places, the Monarch sheds aside the last vestiges of who they were originally, becoming one and all the embodiment of the every changing, Endless Swarm
                                She removes from herself any pretenses of nobility, of restraint, of social order, replacing them with the need to constantly warp and change, to guide her legions up to ascension. She excises the final gene sequences that represented her original, weakened, stable state and devotes herself entirely to evolve, to grow, heedless of the cost.
                                By the time her former friends notice she has been missing and finally mounted a rescue party to save her, they find a bloated abberation that has shed aside weakness and affection a century ago.

                                    Nukukari: One of the players in the Beneath, if only barely so due to their ability to form Surface Access points. Generally despised by the other factions, who see them as conceptual, technological, and literal thieves. There is some merit to the view - though the Nukukari are tremendously clever, industrious, and curious, they are very unimaginative. Never first, they simply copy or take what they see works for other races. The greatest extent of their originality is "lets combine this thing with that other thing", which has led to unfortunate incidents such as the creation of the Tranquil.

                                    If you need something analyzed and replicated, however, they are a Monarch's most reliable bet. As long as they don't mind seeing knock-offs flooding the nearby Provinces before too long.

                                    Tranquil: A "recent" (considering the somewhat subjective time portions of the Beneath experience) development, thought to be the result of the Nukukari merging a hive mind with abducted specimens exhibiting "the Plain" phenomenon (see Hurt Locker). These generally uniform beings often flood into an conflict zone without warning and bring all combat to a grinding halt. Often even resource harvesting is inhibited, forcing one side or the other to come up with creative methods for removing the nuisances non-violently.

                                    They can be lured into certain Provinces by resourceful Monarchs, creating an enforced "safe" zone on their borders where hostility cannot happen, or to "protect" a more benign asset for a time.

                                                    First one is an "intro" to Vassals.

                                                    Second one is an example template



                                                    Both are very rough and are going to require some serious rework to accomodate alien Servants in the revised version.


                                                    Vassals are more advanced creatures created by the Monarch to act as her lieutenants. Each is more free willed and intelligent than a basic drone and is able to act as a driver, manager, coordinator, or often, commander in the Monarch’s stead.
                                                    For the most part, Vassals are created via a premade genetic template from the list of templates the Monarch possesses and paying a relevant resource cost based on the template. Once the relevant prices are paid, the new Vassal would be incubated, hatched and trained during a time span of about a week. Once the week has elapsed, the new Vassal is fit for active duty.
                                                    Pre-existing and soon to be hatched Vassals may be modified from the norm. Most templates possesses a number of “Mutation” representing packages of genetic modifications. Each, for the cost for further resources, provides new abilities, augments existing ones, or changes the Vassal’s equipment from the norm.
                                                    In order to apply a Mutation, the must have an open Mutation slot able to accept the Mutation. Vassals possess a predetermined number of slots inherent to their Template.
                                                    In the description of each Mutation, it lists which Vassals it may be applied to. In the case of some mutations, such as weaponry or armor morphs, they may remove existing features or come with statistics penalties, also specified in the Mutation’s description.
                                                    Each specific Mutations may only be applied once to a Vassal once and obviously, if a Mutation replaces an existing feature from the template, such as a weapon, other Mutations that replace that feature may not be applied.
                                                    Mutations take 1 hour to apply, during which the Vassal is disabled and inside a Cocoon. Additionally, some templates may receive multiple slots within the same category.
                                                    Removing or changing mutations from an existing Vassal or outright disposing of a Vassal does not refund the costs made to produce either.

                                                    As Vassals are part of the Monarch’s Colony, any metanormal effects that involve Authority use the Monarch’s Authority when determining the Vassal’s.

                                                        Updated Geodics.

                                                        Sooner or later, I realize I'm going to have to work on Principalities again, but I think I can have a workable draft for Servants real soon.



                                                        The Endless are not the first race to land upon our Earth. There are a multitude of creatures that traveled the stars and set foot upon our lands. The Endless and their Monarchs are but the latest in a long line of travelers, but there is without a doubt a first.
                                                        Long ago, in a distant time before memory, when the land was still being formed when even the ancient Celestines we all know were just newborns, the earth was struck visited by a meteor, more a seed… a ship that lodged itself all the way down to the Earth’s core.
                                                        Its inhabitants were shattered, broken divided everywhere along the newborn world, sinking beneath the molten seas. They survived, becoming a broken, fracture people: a race totally unlike anything in either Terran understanding or even Physics, the Xan-Qui, the Geodics.
                                                        Unfortunately for us, they are not a race that seems at all willing to make friends. They are a race that is scientifically impossible by our understanding, the very nature of our physics is just not compatable. Though the Geodics survived the fall to Earth, they are trapped within it, reduced to a mere fragment of its former self, one that should not exist as we understand it.
                                                        In order to survive, over the eons, they created a vast labyrinth of underground tunnels and passage ways to facilitate their existence, a process made difficult due to the lack of coordination, massive technology regression, and long stretches of hibernation; the surviving machines the Geodics held were put to the task, burrowing tunnels, harvesting resources, while the ancients slept. They ultimately failed this task as carbon life’s existence stole control corrupted the machines, forcing them to allow the new reality to also account of them. When they finally awoke, the Geodics could bring their Coreminds to the surface, their underground paradise becoming their prison.
                                                        They had no choice but to ensure that whatever intruders in their realm were removed, by any means necessary.


                                                        Physiology
                                                        The Geodics are named such for their appearance, crystalline yet shifting forms that look like ordinary earth, but despite their appearances they are not inert, floating by some electromagnetic means and bending in forming unusual shapes. They employ technology of a sort, far more advanced than anything we own, controlling these devices through electromagnetic manipulation and seeming telepathy. These same electronic field generates natural force fields and beam weaponry comparable to firearms or in some cases artillery cannons.
                                                        Though there may be thousands upon thousands of floating crystals in a Nexus, there is in truth only very few Geodics in a given Nexus, as in actually, a Geodic is a single, fragmented mind in a thousand bodies. In every Nexus, there is atleast one immobile monolith the size of a skyscrapper, seemingly made of stained glass. This is a Coremind, a vast and powerful entity of great and immeasurable will. Yet this power is ultimately difficult to move… and is much more susceptible to changes in physics than other lifeforms; it cannot leave the Nexus without dying or some sort of stabilizing Pillar.
                                                        To make up for this, Coreminds have the ability to Fragment, forming lesser intelligences that are copies of one of the dozens of personalities and inclinations of the Coremind’s congealed into a coherent, stable form. Once the Fragment has completed its duty, it disassembles itself, reforming back into itself.
                                                        Coreminds can Fragment a variety of ways and generally built their Fragments to suit a variety of tasks, either by creating one powerful and complicated agent to observe things in the surface world or a hundred smaller, dumber, more disposable units that matters little.


                                                        Technology
                                                        Geodics possess a great deal of technology up to and including advanced funraces, electronic equivalents, communications technology, and most prevalent of all, suits.
                                                        Geodics often equip smarter fragments with suits to allow them better physical abilities when it would be cheaper to do so than say make a more advanced fragment.
                                                        The most common type of suit unit that a geodic will possess is a Battlesuit, often very similar to the Devics used by HCP.



                                                        Rules
                                                        Geodic Fragments are treated as always Manifested Ephemeral Beings, using many of the same rules as Angels and Spirits do, except Banes and Bans.

                                                        They always possess an Influence (Crystals) equal to their Rank, representing their innate powers of manipulating other crystaline structures, the Blast Numina, and Telekinesis, plus other relevant Numina.
                                                        Coreminds are above stats.
