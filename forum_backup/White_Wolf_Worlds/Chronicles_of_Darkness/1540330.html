                      I think, like most people around here, I've never put down solid numbers like this.

                      Among other issues, it goes way too hard in the opposite direction where instead of the supernatural being far too rare for what the books describe, the supernatural and supernaturally aware humans are over half of the world's population. The setting doesn't really work as intended if half of everyone walking down the street is some sort of supernaturally connected individual.

                      One of the really good things the CofD did, is it made "global" numbers inconsequential. For every city with a few thousand vampires living in it, there's a city of equal size with zero vampires in it in favor of other things (or some mystery of why there's no supernatural presence at all!); and most of the splats have reason to gather that override things like relative populations to human residents (ex: Mages congregate around persistent Mysteries).

                      Even using the Contagion Chronicle you're not doing a worldwide survey where these sorts of numbers are ever going to come up. If you want 5K werewolves operating in an area, and you want to stick to the broad strokes of the books, just give a reason for so many in such a small place (the books offer plenty of options). The Uratha are not in fact evenly distributed across the globe.

And the realm have a curious trait in that, the Contagion can't spread here, it can't infect the realm, those who are infected with the Contagion can't enter the realm. And the law of this realm is very different from our own, you can breath in space but the air is kind of thin, and in certain section of space dangerous or cause strange effect, the star doesn't cause vampire to burn, while they're outside of a celestial body, but when on one, they start to burn, but slower and less intense that our sun. Now the Sworn and False battle both on Earth and in the Celestial Realm, for survival on Earth, and for glory in Celestial Realm. The Sworn is now an alliance between 5 Celestial Empire, which inculde the Cryptocracy Republic, which is a Republic and the most 'safe' and heavy guarded out of the 5 Empire. The Jeremiad Empire, which is a Theocracy with 10,000 religions all struggling for power and control over the Empire, but each give powerful blessing. The Ship of Theseus Collective which is a Oligarchy, they're the most technologically advance out of the Sworn, but also have the least amount of population, military, cultural identity, and goverment stability. The False are lesser in number and have no alliance to each other, but thrive from the vastness of the Celestial Realm to hide from the Sworn Federation. The Crucible Ark, is a Democracy, they live on their giant space ships call Ark, plundering countless world of it natural resource for it Arks. The Machiavelli Coporation, is a Megacorp that exploit the various native alien nation for it own benefit, and pitting them against the Sworn Federation and each other.                                   A quarter of the world's population is not older than the age of 14. You've here given global Promethean numbers that are several dozen times the rough estimate put forth in 2e, not to mention put per-subsplat numbers for multiple monster-types high enough to outnumber the tenth most populous city in the most populous country in the world not named India or China. You've got more of the rumored Nuclear Prometheans than people possessed by Infernal demons despite Prometheans being rare and Zeky being rare enough that most Prometheans haven't encountered one, more Beasts than mages when Beasts are explicitly outnumbered by mages, and numbers for rare variant werewolf auspices but no numbers for the primary antagonistic werewolf faction. Even if one treats "being a supernatural entity" and "being aware of the supernatural" as equivalent despite the bluebook Integrity question establishing basically anyone who's a player character has had some exposure to the supernatural that they try not to think about, these numbers are still kinda weird.

                                  I tend to take the view that playable monsters being accorded the same level of narrative importance as named sub-leaders in tokusatsu-archetypical evil organizations entails a similar degree of scarcity — there aren't going to be enough in an area to credibly treat any of them as faceless background characters without circumstances dramatically ratcheting up their density on the ground, like the mass gatherings of Prometheans in New Orleans and Boston in the 1e's Water of Life chronicle. My own Changeling chronicle plays slightly fast and loose with its local numbers by virtue of the freehold being sponsored by a couple Agencies of demons experimenting with fae Covers while observing a metaphysical gas leak, but I'd still be hard-pressed to put its total non-Integrity-bearing headcount in the triple digits altogether.
