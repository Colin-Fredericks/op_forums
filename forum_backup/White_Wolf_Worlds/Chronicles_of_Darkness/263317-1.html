    To the question - no, nothing happens to the physical deer, except the end of any Influence-based effects that the spirit might have been creating.Without anything feeding on the deer's resonance, it will probably attract a new Rank 0 spirit within a day or two.

    As for the Hisil in second edition...

    * I describe the inside of the Gauntlet. In 1e you stood in one world for the time it took to cross, then crossed. Now, you vanish from one world, hang inside the Gauntlet for a few Turns and then appear on the other side. Sometimes, you see things in there.

    * We've given Helios a purpose and motivation other than just "Luna's twin brother," which doesn't alter how Helions act toward werewolves but reveals much about the Hisil's "physics"

    * A lot of attention is placed on describing what the Shadow looks, feels, and tastes like - it's a sensory experience, especially for an Uratha.

    * Werewolve's Essence-senses are explicitly perceived as their sense of smell/taste. You don't *see* dormant spirits, you taste them in the not-air.

    * One of the example spirits is a big-box retailer; they act like corpulent slum-lords of the Shadow, enslaving the lesser spirits linked to the goods and consumers.

    Dave Brookshaw

                                    I dunno how Dave B might have the spirit of Wal-Mart look, but were I to do the spirit of a big supermarket retailer, the two things that spring to mind are:

                                    1) The spirit of a specific store. A vast monstrosity of steel and concrete, cables and lights, that clatters and skitters along the ceiling of the superstore's Shadow impression. To a large extent, the whole building is an extension of it, like a spider's web within which it can sense the presence of prey and its spirit-vassals. Think an immense, possibly-arachnid building-spirit-thing that bubbles and drips from strange, organic tumours of greed, hunger and other emotions, with too many limbs and too many eyes - some of which may look like immense human eyes, others may have the appearance of CCTV cameras or television screens, warped and twisted extrusions from the spirit's bulk. It's big, the size of an elephant, yet somehow hurries along the ceiling with disturbing speed, diving down on threads of thick cabling and ropy, muscly sinew to confront intruders or snatch up prey.

                                    2) The spirit at the heart of the whole business. The interior of the headquarters building is a hive - a complex pattern of cells and corridors that don't really match the building's real layout. Drone-spirits of work and fear rush from place to place, or squat in their little cells and stare across the Gauntlet at the office worker they are bonded to. Greedy things lurch along, lapping with their slithery tongues at the Essence that seethes across into the Shadow from the constant efforts of the human workforce. Go deep enough into the hive and you'll find the heart, the queen bee, an array of whirling limbs, spheres of flesh and machinery, threads of gristle reaching between its parts as they dance in the patterns of a great, vile orrery. It has so many faces, flipping from the face of a mascot or a logo, the face of a CEO or an advertisement's actor; talking to it is disorientating as its personas constantly dance and switch, and its voice is an entire choir of chanting throats in unison. Its tendrils plunge deep into the building around it, oozing roots of spirit-meat. Each limb, each spinning sphere, is bound with long stretches of papers and silks, marked with sigils of First Tongue and of greed, of corporate organisation and of profit reports. It can, temporarily, force its shape down into a humanoid form, a grey-skinned and featureless person in a suit, but this facade cannot be contained for long; it will soon rupture and burst out again to reveal the alien, inhuman force behind the human front.

                                    - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

                                                        Originally posted by cmwatford View Post
                                                        I was of the understanding that if you killed the spirit of a deer the deer that "birthed" it would sort of wither and die. Am I wrong in this assumption?
                                                        Yes, you are wrong. The Shadow and the Flesh are related, but not on a 1:1 basis. Things in one can affect the other, but it's not a perfect mirror. A deer in the Flesh is as hearty and deer-like as any other deer. While it does Deer Things, some Deer Essence will appear in the Shadow in that area. Some Spirits will be attracted to this Essence, and come to nom on it. If the spirits are strong enough to do so, they will attempt to protect the Deer by Strengthening it, or by messing with things that might try to harm the deer.

                                                        When those spirits are killed, the deer merely loses its supernatural protectors.

                                                        "Nihhina kalekal-zidu kal masun, kal manudanadu. Nihhina kalekal-zidu nukal shaghu-desasudu — nihhina kalekal-zidu kal innu-desasudu udhkal samm." Arthur Ashe
                                                        She/her, contributing writer for Scion: Pride 2021

                                                            To add to that though, Spirits have their own Influences and are linked to something that has a specific Resonance... So whilst the Shadow and Flesh aren't related at a 1:1 basis, doing something like killing the flesh deer would mess the Spirits over if the Resonance they took Essence from was the deer itself. The weakened Spirits may then be replaced by other Spirits biding their time in the area, like Death or Slaughter spirits... It all depends on how the deer dies and what Spirits gain Essence from how the area has been affected...

                                                            The main point is an area Resonant with something attracts the spirits that can gain Essence from such an area. When the Resonance is removed or changed to no longer feed those spirits, new ones will come in or the Spirits themselves may change by feeding off each other to survive to become something else entirely... At least thats how I see it. YMMV and I haven't played W:tF for some time now...
