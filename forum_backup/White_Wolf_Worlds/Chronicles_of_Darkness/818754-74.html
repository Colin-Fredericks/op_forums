        Originally posted by Arduras View Post
        Also, another question. What order do reflexive actions resolve? ie: dude with a magic ring reflexively destroys X vitae. I reflexively (parkour athletic action) throw a knife potentially prompting a check and rendering him unconscious from being full of lethal.
        There's really no "order of resolution" in CofD. The mechanics don't go into that much detail, mostly because its generally not needed. If something is reflexive, then its generally a faster-than-thought, takes little to no time type situation. In your above scenario, there's no reason why someone would be able to notice the ring being activated, pull a knife, throw it, and knock the person unconcious. And that's assuming that knocking a person unconcious stops whatever action they were taking, and Stamina rolls, and all that. If you're reacting to something reflexive, its generally going to be over before your reaction happens.

        There's no "stack" of actions. Everything, when its declared, is resolved pretty much right away. The only guideline is "what makes the most sense in this story?"
