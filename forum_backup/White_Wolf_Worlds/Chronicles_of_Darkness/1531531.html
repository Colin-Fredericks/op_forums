    Resonant Condition and the Spirit Arcanum

    02-26-2024, 03:09 PM
    Hey everyone.
    I'm playing a Thyrsus with Spirit 2, but until recently I've been too intimidated by the inaccessibility of the Spirit Arcanum to really delve into it. Now that I'm trying to understand how to proactively use it in-game, I came across a few things I'm having a hard time wrapping my head around and would love to hear your thoughts about them:

    1. Channel Essence seems to suggest some elements of the Fallen World have the Resonant condition and some don't, but isn't everything around us Resonant to some Spirit or other? The condition seems to be tagged to a specific Spirit, but Channel Essence seems to regard it as an attribute of the elements themselves.

    2. Once a Mage detects a Resonant condition in the Fallen World, could he Channel Essence freely, or would it be Withstood by the Gauntlet Strength? Is Essence accessible to Mages from this side of the Gauntlet?

    3. The Spirit Sight description states it "detects the presence and nature of the Resonance Condition and other sources of Essence". What other sources of Essence are there except for the Resonant condition?

    4. Channel Essence mentions it can be used to transfer Essence into a suitable receptacle. What's considered a "suitable receptacle"?

    5. How much Essence can be drawn from a Resonant condition? and does the drawn Essence keeps its original Resonance?

    6. Could a Spirit Knowing spell be used to track down the name and/or location of a Spirit with a specific Influence, Manifestation or Numen from afar? Would you limit it to just to the nearest Spirit that fulfills this requirement or just to Spirits on the same side of the Gauntlet as the Mage?

              Originally posted by Hubhub View Post
              1. Channel Essence seems to suggest some elements of the Fallen World have the Resonant condition and some don't, but isn't everything around us Resonant to some Spirit or other? The condition seems to be tagged to a specific Spirit, but Channel Essence seems to regard it as an attribute of the elements themselves.
              In game and story terms, the Resonant Condition means "spirits related to this phenomenon can sense and draw sustenance from its presence." I discussed it a bit here, if that helps.

              2. Once a Mage detects a Resonant condition in the Fallen World, could he Channel Essence freely, or would it be Withstood by the Gauntlet Strength? Is Essence accessible to Mages from this side of the Gauntlet?
              Essence comes from this side of the Gauntlet; Gauntlet Strength penalizes attempts by spirits in the Shadow to draw Essence. Taking Essence from across the Gauntlet as a mage would in fact be Withstood.

              3. The Spirit Sight description states it "detects the presence and nature of the Resonance Condition and other sources of Essence". What other sources of Essence are there except for the Resonant condition?
              Manifested ghosts and goetia. Werewolves. Fetishes. Things that have Essence in them.

              4. Channel Essence mentions it can be used to transfer Essence into a suitable receptacle. What's considered a "suitable receptacle"?
              Something with the capacity to hold essence, such as a spirit, werewolf, mage, or the products of the Craft Fetish spell.

              5. How much Essence can be drawn from a Resonant condition? and does the drawn Essence keeps its original Resonance?
              Without having Channel Essence as a rote or Praxis nor Spirit as a Ruling Arcanum, you will be limited by the fact that the spell costs 1 Mana to cast each time and only allows you to move as much Essence as your Mana/turn rating while the spell lasts, as well as the fact that you have to move the Essence into something that can hold it and your own Pattern splits that space with your Mana capacity.

              6. Could a Spirit Knowing spell be used to track down the name and/or location of a Spirit with a specific Influence, Manifestation or Numen from afar?
              That would entail legwork better suited to the Fate Arcanum a la Serendipity; Know Spirit and Spirit Summons are the primary published toolkit for this.

                      Originally posted by Hubhub View Post
                      But isn't everything in the Fallen world Resonant by that definition? You seem to suggest only some things are Resonant, but I'm having a hard time understanding what distinguishes those things from everything else- is it a matter of intensity (e.g. candleflame isn't Resonant, but an inferno is), one of emotional investment (e.g. a regular knife isn't Resonant, but a chef's favorite knife is) or something else?
                      If this were a prosaic game for simulating an alternate reality and not a storytelling game where emotion and memory are magically potent factors, yes, everything would have more or less the same baseline level of resonance. In practice, Resonant things are things important enough to draw attention to their possessing the qualities of a given type of Resonance — Inferno describes spirits as "the flesh that feeling wears," and Mummy describes the Essence of a thing as the quality that you couldn't remove and still have that that (e.g. the Essence of ice is cold).

                      Think of it in terms of symbolism: if the thing doesn't stand out enough to communicate something about itself or its environment, it's probably not Resonant enough to register for storytelling purposes without further activity. A candleflame in the right context could be fully Resonant, especially if it's given the trappings of ritual or even serendipitous framing, but an anonymous patch of dirt among many other patches of dirt in a field isn't likely to sustain an earth elemental of any notable quality nor put the local plant spirits any closer to awaking from dormancy on its own.

                      Analogy to Yantras is probably the best way to think about it in a Mage-only context; you need to be able to recognize Resonance sufficient for spirits to harvest Essence from, albeit this is from the perspective of the wider world moreso than an individual magician with occult training.

                      So barring Fate magic and Spirit 3, the Mage would just have to wander the Shadow or the Fallen World (and peek into the Shadow), casting Know Spirit en masse until he finds a Spirit with the specific combination of traits he seeks? Of course, I guess he could instead bargain for this information with other Spirits and Mages or maybe just research it in some old tome, but I'm wondering specifically how the Spirit Arcanum would help in this regard.
                      Spirit Summons lets you call a spirit within your sensory range by a specified type of Resonance, and Know Spirit can help you get more information from there.

                      Having a spirit Familiar is useful for this scouting process by virtue of camouflage and the ability to cast spells through it.

                                  I think it's hard to make an argument that the Resonant Condition only counts for things that are outstanding in some way when it's given the definition of "thing that falls under a spirit's Influence" and "if it matches the purview in some way, it has this Condition" or "anything matching the description of an Influence counts as tagged to that entity", with an explicit note that it's common.

                                  It almost makes me wonder if whomever was writing the relevant Spirit spells didn't quite recognise that, although other spells seem to not have such a concern so it's hard to say. It may also be possible that it really just amounts to a way of maintaining consistency in terminology towards the purpose of saying that, yes, mages can draw Essence from basically anything.

                                  In some ways that does seem like it would make them better at channelling Essence than spiritual entities tend to be, but I can see a few things that would work against that. One is that I read Channel Essence as being limited in total to moving a quantity of Essence equal to its Potency. So you can have the spell active for a week at, say, Potency 5, but across that week you'll only be able to move five points, in whichever direction. If you take in five, you have to end the spell and cast it again to actually give any of it back out again later on. It's a hassle, even before considering the fact that Gnosis determines the actual rate of transfer.

                                  The other is that Essence is an energy that mages can't do a whole lot with innately, but is still crowding out their Mana. So maybe you can pull more Essence from any random fire than a fire spirit can, but you're not really getting the benefit from it that the spirit would, and it's at the cost of space needed for the more useful energy. I think that trade off mostly doesn't favour the mage.

                                  You can potentially end up creating a scenario where spirits have access to more free flowing Essence than they would without intervention, but that might not be the best position to be in. It's like the movie Nope; you can reliably ensure that the abomination will be around if you provide food in an easier fashion than it gets by hunting, but it turns out that it's a super bad idea to make a wild animal associate you being around with food, just in case your side of the routine gets disrupted.

                                  Some werewolves might be tempted by the prospect of such a font of Essence, but I think plenty will perceive greater Renown in dealing with something that is messing up the natural ecology so much.

                                      The bar for being "outstanding" enough to be Resonant is quite low in this reading — literally just "if it's important enough to have the camera focused on it or otherwise be the subject of a description, it's Resonant with the things it communicates."
