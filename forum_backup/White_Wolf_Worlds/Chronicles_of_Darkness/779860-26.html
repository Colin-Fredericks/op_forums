So to counteract this, I was thinking that Conclusions could perhaps be 1-5 Dot rated powers, with new powers or new levels of effectiveness being able to be drawn.

                        For example, let's use the Protect Conclusion. When used as a verse, it'll automatically give it's dot level in Armor rating to the person, object or place it's used on to protect from damages. But there's a catch. The Opuses invoked in the Conclusion must be present, but the need for presence can lower the higher the dot level is. So if a Siren used Target-Water-Protect, the target was in the water and she has Protect 1, then the Water would slightly move to protect the target. Protect 2 might require the target to be right next to the water, Protect 3 may require the target to be in X Yards of Water. Protect 4 would rip water from any surrounding source to come out and protect the target, whereas Protect 5 could literally make pipes burst or take all of the moisture out of thin air to protect the target.

                        If we go this way, I fear we may have to lose the Attribute attachment, but it also brings in the idea of Conclusions having lower Exp costs. What do you think? D:

Or at least, something that expands upon that.

                                                            I've also been thinking on the nature of the Celestial Ocean, more things that could be a part of it or something. I was looking back onto our old discussions on "if it should be a bad end future timeline", "if it should be a part of the Astral Realms or be how the Sirens picture it", etc etc.

                                                            I was thinking, maybe we could write it as if it were like the Astral/Dreamlands, mostly the Anima Mundi? It's stated to be the Soul of the Universe and containing the ideals of the natural world and mankind's impact on it. It's also stated that when a person enters it, they get a sensation of vast size that makes a person feel small by comparison. TL;DR up ahead with bolded text before each idea so it's less of a headache to go through.

                                                            How I feel this fits into Siren is in a couple of ways. For one, it would feel fitting that the Soul of the Universe would seek out and create beings to uphold it. In fact, an example I have of this is in the Nasuverse (I think it includes Melty Blood, Fate/Stay Night and Boundary of the Void), there's a being called Alaya, who is the collective will of humanity to survive. It's modus operandi is to create Counter Guardians. In short, it gives the contracted one miracle in exchange for serving as a Counter Guardian after death and fix problems that would lead to humanity dying as a whole. It feels like we can do that here, except with the soul of the Universe doing it instead.

                                                            HOW IT CAN FIT WITH LORE/MECHANICS ON THE ANIMA MUNDI
                                                            There's still more than a few things that are fitting. Given it's description of "Warping the minds of those who enter it and making their very being into a part of the realm", a magical effect that makes people lose their individuality and become one with everything. Mages call this "The Ecstatic Wind", and Princesses know this as "The Gales". My idea is that Sirens know them by a much, much, MUCH different name. "The Song". To explain this, one idea for the ideal of Universal Harmony is that it can be dangerous or even a bad thing. The idea was that the Song is an ideal that obliterates distinctions (between Land and Sea for example), but like most ideals that would promise harmony, it would instead lead to the downfall of both sides (aka Causing the Deluge). The Song in a sense can be a danger of the ideal, since it enchants humans to walk into the sea as if it were land in search of beauty.

                                                            To give mechanical ideas on how this can fit in already made material, let's say that the Song is why the Waters are capable of migrating and shifting in the Celestial Ocean. It's why it can shift according to changes in reality altogether, and also why Sirens are capable of changing their Alterations by focusing on one of the Waters too. It also makes their Supernatural Advantage fitting in name, since "Akashic Record" covers "the records of the world".

                                                            It also gives a small reference to Werewolf the Apocalypse. In nWoD terms, the Shadow and Astral were both the same place in the oWoD, and Werecreatures could enter the Umbra (what it was called in the oWoD) via entering a reflective surface and interact with everything like it were physical. Sirens can do the same thing here, but with water.


                                                            HOW IT STILL CAN DEAL WITH TIME THEMES
                                                            But there's still one problem. We did agree that the Celestial Ocean would have time themes, acting like a bad end future. We also agreed on History themes too, such as "what has happened may happen again". I have an idea on this. Simply put, Mages have the Dream Merit, and Princesses have the White Rabbits merit. Both merits are basically the Astral/Dreamlands telling the Mage/Princess of things to come, or hints of things to come. Given this, what if the Celestial Ocean shows that to a Siren? Things to come in the future if they continue as they are now? This way, it still fits in with Time themes.


                                                            THEN WHAT ARE CETO AND ECHIDNA
                                                            There's one problem. All beings in the Astral/Dreamlands are ultimately ephemeral beings. How can they affect anything on earth? I say, we take the Luna Route. She took a form to mate with Father Wolf, and thus had half spirit, half man kids. Ceto, or the being behind her (if we still use Leviathan, I feel that revamping the Celestial Ocean can help us make a coherent backstory) were able to deal with humanity and raise them to a higher level where they became Sirens. It also helps explain why to the Sirens, the Astral/Dreamlands is a physical place. What are these beings exactly? We could say that they're basically Gods of the Anima Mundi. In particular, Echidna and Ceto are closer to humanity, hence how they're capable of taking a physical form and why Sirens have a physical form too.


                                                            FURTHER IDEAS ON HOW THINGS CAN FIT TOGETHER
                                                            As well, another idea was how the Anima Mundi makes everyone who walked into it get a sensation of vast size that makes the individual feel small by comparison. What better way to symbolize that than to make it's imagery the same as an ocean? Furthermore, this also gives some more meaning to the Furies, Survivors, and Therons (I thought it would be a good idea to add in sea creatures a siren can interact with in the Celestial Ocean), as they could be signs of people (Survivors) or nature (Furies) suffering.

                                                            Finally, how would the Tenemos and Oneiros be represented? I had earlier ideas that, given the Celestial Ocean shows more of the Anima Mundi than anything else, there would be different levels to the Celestial Ocean. I had the idea that there were the Reefs (Tenemos), which would be the most populated by the Survivors (representation of human individuals). I also imagine that there's the Surface (Oneiroi), which would have tons of different debris floating like trash on the water. Sirens could potentially pick these up and use Trace to see through the object to whatever human it belonged to. In truth, each of these objects are representative of a person. There's also the Skies, but I feel that as a whole, no Siren is able to access them. It's thematically fitting that they're unable to get too close to an ideal. In this case, getting closer to humanity. However, an Ascended Siren (I think we still need a name for these) are capable of going up to the Skies. I also had the idea of a more dangerous zone known as "The Depths", which would be where more dangerous things lurk and where Echidna herself is known to live. In a sense, going to the Depths means going deeper into the Anima Mundi.


                                                            HOW THIS DOESN'T INTRUDE ON OTHER GAMELINES
                                                            Yeah, so they're another supernatural that can enter the Astral. Mage, Princess, Changeling (though they can only go through Oneiroi), Beasts, and to list off two other fansplats, Demigod and Dragons. Dragons are possibly the most similar, given that they call it the "Dreamtide" and in a smaller sense, their fuel stat is Breath, which is what Siren's used to be before renaming to Pneuma.

                                                            Here's what I think that separates Sirens from the rest. First off, they aren't capable of going towards another's "Oneiros" section. The Skies are just out of reach for a Siren. Second, the Song doesn't affect them like it does other people. Mages and Princesses can be trapped by the Gales. To solve this, the Song simply passes through Sirens like a wind going through a hollow cave. They can't be endangered by the Song, but it's possible for other people to become enthralled. Finally, I imagine that unlike Mages (though like Princesses), there's simply no difference between the Tenemos and the Anima Mundi for Sirens. But unlike Princesses, the Tenemos is much more saturated with the Anima Mundi and thus much more heavily influenced. Finally, unlike how Mages and Princesses enter via their Oneiros/Crawlspace, and Beasts enter from their Lairs (which if I read correctly, are within the Tenemos), Sirens are mainly found in the Anima Mundi instead.

                                                            I think this can actually bring in some personal horror to a Siren. Since they can enter in anywhere to the Celestial Ocean, but don't use an Oneiros to enter it, what does it say about the Siren? I'm not willing to slap on mechanics, lore or anything on this. If a person or player asks this, I simply want the answer to be "use your imagination and let it make the result". C:
