                  Copyright law is dumb.

                  Basically, if the copyright holder for World of Darkness (Paradox Entertainment, I believe?) knows about something like Subnet and doesn't do anything about it...

                  Well, then someone can use that against them in a court of law.

                  No, really - if I were to, say, reprint the rules for Mage: The Awakening as Wizard: The Waking-Up, and I was taken for court about it, I could point to Subnet as a legal defense.

                  Now, normally, the lawyers on the other side could just shrug their shoulders and say "well, we didn't know about that". That would be a sufficient justification to throw out my defense. However, I could then reference posts on their company forums, which are moderated by the company, that reference Subnet.

                  In other words, they would have to deal with it for the purpose of covering their asses.

                  Basically, if it comes back up... just don't talk about it on the forums, OK? If it has been taken down by legal action, it's because someone brought their attention to it.

                  I personally have no issues with it (I used it a lot)... it's just that not talking about it here makes everyone, including copyright lawyers, happy.
