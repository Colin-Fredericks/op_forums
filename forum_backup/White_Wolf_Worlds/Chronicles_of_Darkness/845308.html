          Nah, Monster is the perfect theme song for Werewolf.

          For Beast, you have Sister Sledge's "We Are Family" (from their perspective) and Creature Feature's "Mommy's Little Monsters" (from the perspective of everyone else). Creature Feature is Beastly as all get-out.

          The wisdom through fear aspect has too many angles to properly cover with one song. I'm fond of the Hunger For Punishment, so I suggest the following: Abney Park's "Victorian Vigilante", Arthur Brown's "Fire", Johnny Cash's "God's Gonna Cut You Down", Miracle Of Sound's "I Am The Night", Queen's "Another One Bites The Dust", Ben Moody & Anastacia's "Everything Burns", Nico Vega's "Fury Oh Fury" and Lordi's "Rock Police". I'll stop here because there are far too many awesome songs about revenge out there.

          And if you ever feel like just enjoying your monstrosity for once, you might like Lord Dominator's "I'm The Bad Guy" (Wander Over Yonder), The Joker's "Where's The Fun In That" (Batman: The Brave And The Bold), Voltaire's "When You're Evil", Lordi's "Monster Is My Name" and the Boogie Song from The Nightmare Before Christmas.

          EDIT: By the way, Lordi is just as Beastly as Creature Feature. I heartily recommend them. A lot of their songs are about something mundane turned monstrous, from grannies to presidents.
