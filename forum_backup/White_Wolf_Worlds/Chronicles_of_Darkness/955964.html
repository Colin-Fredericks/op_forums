    The Known World [CofD Fantasy Shard, Brainstorming]

    08-22-2016, 07:24 PM
    every once and awhile, I toy with the idea of making a fantasy setting for the CofD- and now I do have some free time so why not? While I don't have any problem at using "fantasy like" names for countries, peoples, organizations, etc- I do think that there is some kind of magic at using generic terms and Capital Letters as names and terms for things, so this the path I'm going with. Anyway, feel free to add your own suggestions and thoughts about the subject!

    The World, by itself, is a mystery. Only few lands are explored, and few roads are traveled. The edges of the map are blank, waiting to be filled by courageous souls who dare to go beyond the safe and the known and travel to new places, both terrible and wonderful. The World is wider and deeper than what anyone can even imagine, and most of the people prefer to contain themselves to what is called as the Known World- those few territories which are, more or less, understood by the common people. Traveling beyond those boundaries is truly a dangerous act- but even inside the Known World there are many terrors at the night, with monsters, magic, gods and demons waiting in the shadows, bringing with them darkness and despair.

    In the center of the Known World stands the Republic- the only point of light in the vast darkness of the world. Considered to be the only country free from supernatural manipulation, it stands as both a neutral ground for other territories and as a trading center where all kinds of goods- both legal and otherwise- could be traded through the World. North to the Republic stands the ancient Empire of Blood, which survived over a millennia. Controlled by strong, noble families, the fact that it is truly managed by vampires is practically an open secret, as every commoner is forced to donate blood and is controlled through mystical blood bonds. On the east, one could find the Spirit Plains, the homeland of barbarian tribes who follow spirituals totems. The tribes are led by powerful, shapeshifting monsters, half men half wolves who supervise both flesh and spirit into creating a "perfect harmony". Laying west to the great Empire, the Academy stands divided. It is a place of magic, both wonderful and horrible, and those who hold the power fight whether it should be free to roam or limited to the selected few. Between the Empire and the Plains the Faerie Woods dwell, where most mortal people live in villages at the edge of the forest. Very few dare to enter the woods themselves- shining lights and laughing songs lure the unwise, trapping them into a living nightmare from which only few are able to escape. The area west to the Republic is simply called the Wasteland. Only the desperate and the exile would live there, as the place is more prone for natural disasters and the lands itself produce almost nothing. This is the land of outcasts and madmen- and the few monsters who search for home. Spread through the Known World are the Free Cities- a loose alliance of kingdoms and democracies who stand side by side as they both investigate the unknown and fight against it. Each has its own unique history and culture, and all are committed to fight the unknown and the monsters who hide in the shadows. The dark Necropolis is a great graveyard found southwest to the Republic. Filled with necromancers, ghost lords and death cults, only the richest people may afford being buried in its ground, and many seek after the guidance of their ancestors in it. The only country on in the southern desert is the Kingdom of Law- an ancient country prospering upon slavery and the ruins of the past. Their harsh priest kings worship the bodies of the mummified ancients as gods- something considered to be superstition for many years, until one day, the gods woke up. The closets country to the Republic in terms of culture and trade is the great Theocracy. Found between the desert and the Plains, it is both advanced in terms of science and magic and both dedicated in the worship of its god. Its messengers are hidden in plain sight in search for treason- and those who betray their god and survive hide even deeper. Finally, on the edge of the Known World, the Great Wall in a desperate attempt to separate the "civilized" from the "none civilized". Beyond it, one could find the Primordial Wilderness- place untouched by men where only the bravest dare to live. This is a land of fear and terror, where monsters feed upon nightmares and common people live in terror, until an hero rise among their ranks to slay the monsters in search for personal glory.

    Enter the Known World- a realm of magic and corruption, of wonder and terror. Explore unknown lands and seek for new adventures. Be a traveler, a savior, a destroyer, a king or a commoner. This is the world where every dream can be fulfilled...

    That is, if you survive.

      The Republic

      Also called as the Free Republic, it was founded long time ago upon a former territory of the Theocracy. Its independence came after a great rebellion, in which the people of the Theocracy defied their own god and church while declaring their own state, one which is free from the manipulations of gods, demons and monsters. While there have been a number of times through the history of the World to conquer the Republic (both by the Theocracy and the other countries), the Republic survived, creating a stable government which is based around freedom, innovation and justice. No god watch over this country, and no demon whispers in its ruler's ears. This is a country made by men, for men- a safe place in the darkness of the World.

      Thanks to its constant policy of neutrality and geographic location, the Republic has eventually turned into a prospering trading hub, where goods from all the Known World are being bought and sold. From rare herbs and medicines from the Spirit Plains and up to exotic spices from the southern Kingdom, the Republic is a great market where the common citizen can find almost anything. Nobles from the Empire come to there to find new slaves for their household, students from the Academy search for new materials for their research and even accursed alchemists from the Wasteland search after new test subjects. Merchandise is not the only thing to be sold and bought, but services too- undertakers from the Necropolis, mercenaries from the Spirit Plains, explorers from the Free Cities. While there is no official religion to the Republic, many priests from the other countries seek new converts and build their own temples for nomad travelers of faith, making the only place where shrines for the Crone and the Night God could be found side by side, just next to ones dedicated to the Machine God, the Wolf God and the Principle among others, each is supported by its own Mystery Cult. Mortal citizens through the whole World are welcomed in the Republic, allowing them a small amount of solace in their harsh, scary life.

      While freedom is valued in the Republic, there are laws meant to maintain that freedom. The country is governed by the Elected Council, a political body chosen by the citizen of the Republic for every four years. Anyone can put her name to the election, although very rarely someone who isn't already a powerful figure (merchant usually) would manage to get elected to the Council. While there many rules were made through the ages, there is one law which was established as soon as the Republic was declared- no magic is allowed among the Republic's walls. While at first it also meant that anyone with supernatural powers may not even enter the land, since then the rules were bent a little, making it so those touched by the occult are able to enter the country- even though they have to register beforehand. Still, magical merchandise is forbidden by law, and individuals with magical abilities may not become citizens of the Republic. However, like in any country, laws are meant to be broken- the Black Market is the strongest criminal organization in the city, where illicit goods and being traded, together with human (or not so human) traffic and illegal immigration. On the other hand, the Council employs the Exorcist Order, made up of individuals trained at sealing, warding and defeating the supernatural, and are the only ones with a permit to use supernatural abilities. Anyone who is corrupted by the supernatural has only three choice- leave, join the Order or go seek refuge in the Black Market. While many individuals try to hide unwanted abilities, hiding such talents is against the law- and the Order is specialize at tracking such people down.

      While finding illegal talented people or undercover monsters and fighting against the Black Market are important mission, the Order's top priorities are fighting and destroying any kind of dangerous supernatural influence- they root out hidden Mystery Cults, banish the ghosts or the dead, ward against spirits and burn Infrastructures and similar mysterious place to the ground if possible, or maintain a watch around them if not. The most problematic enemies are those horrors which spontaneously rise as a result of the corrupting influence of the supernatural. When the dead rise from their grave, humans are transformed into beasts or alien creatures who were never human creep into the city, the Order come to banish those horrors away- or take them into custody and further research. Those people who lack supernatural talent yet still wish to fight for their country usually join the Border Guard, who fight constantly against a potential invasion and patrol the borders, standing at the gates of the cities and watch the walls. It is thanks to the Guard that the Republic has managed to survive through the ages, and the name of every fallen solider is written on the Wall of Honor. Of course, not everyone are able to withstand the horror of the darkness, and those broken people are sent to the Institute of Lost Souls where their trauma is, if not treated, at least contained.

      While the Republic works hard to banish supernatural effects, magic creeps in everywhere it can, corruption the land and the people as one. Among the greatest weapons at the hands of the Black Market are the Dreamers- using a combination of psychological conditioning and occult manipulation, any person can become an agent of the Black Market and serve its cause. The use of Dreamers is outlawed by the Council and punished harshly, although some whisper that it wasn't the Black Market to invent the process but steal it from the Council itself. Supernatural diseases is another concern for the Republic and its Order, and many of those Infected by them are contained by the Order or treated in the Institute. The Guard, of course, has its own secrets- those who join its ranks swear powerful oaths upon the Wall of Honor, and those who fail become Cursed by it. Among the villages of the Republic, a new form of heresy takes hold, one who objects any use of violence and war called the Plain Brotherhood, and those who accept it are transformed by it. Perhaps it is because the lack of direct divine manipulation, but certain in the Republic just seem to born lucky, making them among the happiest people in the Known World. Through the Republic, anyone in a moment of crises may have they soul broken, creating a certain form of vamprisim which devours the vitality of others through mind and touch- and such individuals almost always ends up in the ranks of the Order or being treated in the Institute. Finally, lately certain individuals awake in the middle of the night after being treated by some sort of procedure which gave them enhanced abilities. Controlled through some form of potion or serum, the Lost are forced to preform certain mission for the Republic, yet without its protection. Many among the Order, who don't see a sign of supernatural influence in those people, believe it to be some sort of experiment done by the Guard using stolen technology, but even if it is true they have no proofs about the subject.

      While the Republic may not be the perfect country and has its own problems, it is the safest place for the regular people to live their life. There, they can sell and buy their goods, there they can work in their daily job and watch their children grow. They can find friends, loved ones, family. There, they aren't forced to give their blood to hungry nobility, or follow a shapeshifting warlord to war or suffer from harsh punishments under the name of harsh and divine laws. There, they can look at stage magicians or crazy preachers and simply laugh, knowing that they are safe, knowing that they are free...

      Until they peel the outer layer. Until they see what hides beneath the shiny and attractive skin.

      In the country of free trade, everything has a price- especially the freedom.

      Regional Sum-up
      Full name- the Free Republic

      Government- Democracy

      Governing Body- the Elected Council

      Official Religion- none

      Important Bodies- the Exorcist Order (paranormal investigation agency), Black Market (criminal underworld organization), Border Guard (military organization), Institue of Lost Souls (trauma support organization)

      Important Places- Wall of Honor

      Defining Event- the Great Rebellion

      Covering- Mortals, Supernatural Mortals, Mystery Cults, Dreamers, Infected, Cursed of Hachiko, Plain, Atariya, Psychic Vampires, Lost Boys, Horrors, Antagonist's Revenant

                          The Empire

                          No nation is older than the Empire of Blood. It was there to see the Republic waging war toward its own independence and freedom from supernatural manipulation. It was there to see the Academy being established as a safe place to learn occult magics and discover hidden secrets. It was there to see the Kingdom of Law rising upon the ruins of a far older realm, even though it wasn't there to witness its destruction. It was there before the Necropolis grew like mushrooms upon the grave, before villages were settled near the Faerie Woods, before the Theocracy rose to greatness under the watchful eyes of their god. Perhaps the barbarians tribes of the Spirit Plains and the Primordial Wilderness have been there before the Empire, but it doesn't even consider such primitive cultures as something to take note of. Among all civilized, surviving countries, the Empire was the first- always watching, always hungering.

                          And how low has it fallen.

                          The Empire is crumbling. Sure, it may look stable- but it just a mask, an illusion it use to maintain its own power and influence. Once, it was truly a spectacular sight- one nation united under one ruler, with an hierarchy of blood controlling everything through a perfect, feudal system. But now, it is torn apart, after ages of internal struggles and political maneuvers. The Five Noble Families may share drinks and follow the Emperor of Blood, but in truth the Emperor is practically powerless, bound to the wills of the Court of High Clans. Each of the Five Families may seem loyal to the crown, but in the shadows each plans for a revolutions, replacing the current ruler with its own puppet. Still, the Emperor is not a fool, and survived over an hundred years of manipulations, trickery and assassination attempts- yet no one can rule forever, and lately madness starts creeping to his mind. It won't be long before the Emperor would be needed to be replaced, whether they like it or not, however all know that once that moment would come a civil war would erupt through the Empire, and more blood would be spilled then all of its nobles could drink.

                          That was not always the case. Years ago, long before anyone can even imagine, the Empire wasn't ruled by a group of power hungry nobles and a weak Emperor. It was united under the leadership of a single family, the High Imperial Clan. Supported by its own, undead ancestors from the shadows, the Imperial Clan has conquered new territories and absorbed other realms of the north. Through the conquest, five local, lesser families of the undead supported the Imperial Clan, and as a sign of gratitude, the first Emperor of blood named each line of living and dead as noble and divided the empire into five Provinces around the Imperial city, like in a form of flower- the Province of Shadows is a great jungle near the Faerie Woods where its people are obsessed with sorcery and superstition. The Province of Savages is a found near the Spirit Plains, a vast grasslands and hills where powerful warriors dwell. The Province of Serpents borders the Republic, a land of traders, drugs and prostitutes where every lust may be fulfilled. The Province of Haunts is a desolated place, a maze of caves inside an hollow mountain where miners and low status individuals work and prisoners are held. The closest family to the Imperial Clan got the far north, in a land called the Province of Lords- a place of magnificent architecture and rigid laws bound strongly to the wills of the nobility. And in the middle- the Imperial City, the most wondrous place in the Empire, built as layers upon layers where graves are founds alongside the living and the undying ancestors guide the fate of their nation.

                          Yes, it was truly a magnificent time- until the Night of Smoke happened.

                          There was no fire, yet there was light- the shiny yellow glow, like golden coins, like owl eyes. The smoke filled the Imperial Palace, and screams filled the air. All the living descendants of the Imperial Clan jumped to their death or cut their own throats in some form of terrible ecstasy, and all the respected ancestors of the cities set themselves on fire or staked their heart and waited to be greeted by the sun. By the end of the end of the Night, there was no survivor from the Imperial Clan, neither living nor dead- and the fate of the Emperor was the worst. Its organs were scattered through a locked room in some form of terrible form of art, his crown is still upon his head as his violated body sits upon the throne, holding a sword stick with blood. And above his head, was written in crimson letters "So Will Be Done For Those Who Don't Pay The Price For Our Gifts. Remember The Crime of the Sixth, Beware From the Wrath Of The Seventh"

                          Following the Night, a Civil War erupted through the Empire- territories rebelled against their masters, nobles declared themselves free from the crown. It took 300 years, but eventually the Five Noble Families has managed to get to a an agreement, choosing a new Emperor of Blood and uniting the Empire back. The Imperial Palace was rebuilt, even if less magnificent then before. The Court of High Clans was installed as a tool to give each of the Families an hold of power. The Church of Night was established as the state religion, educating both the living and the dead about their place in the Empire and their duty to serve, making the Night Pope into one of the most influential people in the Empire with the authority to crown the next Emperor. Sure, it wasn't as great as it was once- but it survived, and functioned, and that was enough.

                          At least for awhile- until the other Five Families had enough Emperors from the line of the Lords. Until one too many such Emperors lost his mind during his rule, with great consequences. Until the Night Pope has started to make plans toward taking the crown for himself and creating an "Holy Blood Empire". Until the Cathartic Movement came from the Republic, bringing new ideals to the rotten corpse of the Empire.

                          While everyone knows that vampires rule the Empire, it is never done officially- following the tradition of the High Imperial Clan, the Noble Families rule behind their living descendants, ghouling them in order to prolong their life until they show themselves fit for the Embrace. The Emperor himself change his identity every ten years, with the number near his title shows how many such cycles he has ruled. Most commoners, however, live pretty miserable life- they don't only pay with money for noble protection, but also with blood. Every noble has the right to use them to feed, and they use a complicate network of blood bonds and the effects of the Kiss in order to bind the masses to their will. While killing a commoner with no reason while feeding is illegal, many nobles could easily find a "reason" to do so. The only way to rise from that status is by becoming a ghoul and turn into a personal servant of one of the noble vampires, and even then there are limitations to status- only those of noble stock may be Embraced by law, and the rest are doomed into eternal slavery.

                          Still, exceptions do happen from time to time- with so much blood magic going through their system, it is not unknown for mortals to rise from their grave as Revenants. For that reason, the faith of the Night God teaches that only nobles have the right o be buried, so the wisdom of their ancestors would be preserved, where commoners are destined to be burned upon death. However, all laws are meant to be broken, and from time to time the bodies of the dead, accidentally or not, aren't purified and return to unlife. Most Revenant are considered to be bastards, rarely treated well and are uplifted only after showing themselves worthy by using the blood and soul of a criminal (although some Revenants manage to gain that status by themselves, becoming outlaws in the eyes of both Church and State). For that discrimination, most of the Revenants join the Cathartic Movement, which seek to purify the Empire with new ideas of freedom and equality.

                          While the most of the affairs in the Empire are dealt either by the Court or the Church, years ago the Emperor recognized the needs at advanced occult research as a part of competition with the Theocracy and the Academy. That lead to the establishment of the Dragon Order- originally a personality cult dedicated to the Dragon, a semi mythological vampire which transcendent beyond the Curse, their research of the vampire condition and other supernatural subjects has drawn the Emperor's attention and made them into a legal construct with a voice at the Inner Circle. While the Night Pope would gladly brand them as heretics, as long as the Emperor rules he can't touch them, making them into the greatest supports of the Emperor even as he lose his mind. The Circle of the Crone, however, does not enjoy that protection- being pagan worshipers of a primordial goddess, the followers of the Night God hunts them everywhere they can, shattering their idols and burn their groves.

                          There are also other, weirder and smaller mystery cults and political orders through the Empire's rich cultures- the Knighthood of Blood is an order of warriors once dedicated to the protection of the Emperor of Blood and the Imperial High Clan, yet since its fall they lost much of their honor, becoming swords for hire in the hands of the nobles in their inner struggles. The Gallows Post control the many paths inside the different Provinces and are the best guides in the Known World and beyond it, and no one may travel in the Empire without paying their due. Hell's Brood runs through the Empire as mad dogs who kill everyone in their path, a former heresy inside the Church who went out of control. The Imperial Cult of Prophets shows up from time to time, claiming that the current Emperor is a fraud and the soon the rightful blood would rule again. While they were usually seen as the mumbling of madmen, as the Emperor grows weaker and the war comes their numbers seem to grow at every passing day. The criminal world is controlled by the Sewer Guild, a place every outlaw or rebel against the crown can find safe escape. The Wood Witches hide in the villages and outside the civilized lands, making pacts with weird beings and outsiders in exchange for power- no more than a nuisance usually, but their hunger for power has drawn them from the forests and made them lay their eyes at the throne. The Faceless Brethren serves as an intelligence network, unknown by anyone outside the Inner Circle. The Wild Riders are more of a trend then a true structure, dedicated to exploring unlife to the edge and are popular especially among the younger vampires. Lately, vampires who traveled to the Faerie Woods has returned with strange behavior and powers, creating a cult called as the Children of Thorn. The Harbingers are a small death cult, growing among those researchers who didn't joined the Dragon Order. Similarly to the Movement, the Holy Engineers come with foreign ideology, as they bring the words of the Machine God to the lands of the Night God. The Priesthood of the Grove are similar to the Circle from the outside- but instead of seeking after true enlightenment they use their influence for earthly power. The Society of the Accord is an elite club among the nobles where they secretly indulge their bestial nature outside of the Church's watchful eye. The Sun Walkers are made from those few rare commoners who somehow rose for greatness and which seek to bring more equality between the commoners and the nobles- something which sets them apart from the Movement. The Imperial City Watch guard each city at the empire and quench and potential rebellion. The economy of the vast Empire requires special management, and the artistic Masters of Coin deal with it.

                          Even more exotic organizations exist at the furthest reaches of the Empire- the Spirit Dancers who are possessed by their gods, the monastic Children of the Snake who practice self control as a path for enlightenment, the toxic Golden Scorpion and their powerful poisons, the Architects of Cities who work as mystery cult of masons, the Speakers of Earth who become one with their land, the mystical House of Mysteries maintain the Empire's ancient history while recording the old magic, the Followers of Storm who hail from the Province of Shadows and follow their original chaos god, the prophetic Dreamseers who whisper terrible secrets, the secretive Watchers in the Dark who collect information while following the common men and the Lawgivers of Old who know the ways of the Underworld and necromatic secrets kept by the Church

                          And all of those are before including the rumors- the Children's Crusade who are whispered to hunt through the land, the Choir of Heaven who seek to become into angels and gods, the mystery cult of bull slayers which name itself as the Faithful of the Sun, the mysterious Seventh who are claimed to be some sort of terrorist organization without a known agenda.

                          While most of the vampires belong to the Five Noble Families, there are whispers about other, stranger beings in the lands of the Empire- some are more familiar, even if weird in some way, like the Ice Clan which exists in the cold tundras of the north, or the Demon Princes who still rebel against the imperial control in the Province of Serpents, creating some sort of independent society known as the Seven Cities. Others are weirder- the Clan of Worms are birth comes from bloody mess. Reflections who took life of their own populate the more barbarian lands of the Province of Shadows. Gargoyles made out of stone and fed with blood guard each of the Royal Palaces, with the Imperial Palace being actually made of them. The Church warns its faithful from following the ghosts of Former Lovers, saying that they are destined to lead them to doom. Weird Parasites sometimes infect the population, and one of the Order's experiments which went out of control created the Shadow Infection which makes ghouls from slaves to masters. In the woods of the Province of Savages, a race of shapeshifting beasts moves among the people, turning into blood drinking monsters under the moon. The Dragon Order's most funded research deals with ghosts, especially since it is not unknown for the spirits of the dead to claim the living in failed attempt to return to life. Deep in the Province of Shadows, one could find the Obsidian Lands where the Jaguar Witches practice a form of blood magic. Originally from the Province of Serpents, many among the Eaters of the Dead leave to the Province of Haunts in search for solace, while the Beautiful Ones worship the Mountain Mother- the original goddess of the Serpents- and literally grow fat upon their follower's admiration. Horror stories tell about the Memory Eaters who drive both mortals and undead mad, and the Devil Singers infiltrate their way from the land of Shadows in search of power, perfectly human until their head leaves their body. The Sons of Terror steals both hearts and blood for their heretic experiments as they are hunted down through the Empire. The Phantoms are the most culturally accepted among the many weird monsters, and some have even managed to be accepted to the Court and Church.

                          And those monsters are nothing compared to the ones the vampires make by themselves- the mad products of botch Embrace, or the Half Damn products of forbidden liaison between the living and the dead, a practice common especially among the followers of the Crone and despised in the ranks of the Church. And there are other terrors in the night who wait for the unfortunate soul to stumble upon them- like the Hunter who murders nobles in their sleep, the Fallen Stars who hide in the body of other vampires and devour their body and soul, the Stormwind Hunt who ride from the Faerie Forests in search for ghouls and vampires, a weird sickness which devours the minds of the wise to bring madness, people who never was are created from botch blood magics and contain secret rituals in their fake souls, fallen blood magician escape their death by becoming into ash the devours the living and the Black Hounds who serve the Herald of the Night and devour sinners.

                          And more then all- more than the dangerous politics, the strange monsters and the bound fate to which every person in the Empire is born to- none is scarier then the Owls, the demonic spirits of the underworld who once destroyed the Imperial High Clan and brought chaos to the land. For years they weren't seen through the Empire but in the most distant places and in the most terrifying rumors- but lately, their numbers are growing. Their wings cover the moonlight as they fly, and the smell of ash fills the air. The heresies of the Night Lady seem to grow once more, and the common people lock their doors with fear and whisper desperate prayers to the Night God.

                          But it's not going to help them. The change is coming, if they like it or not. Last time the Owls showed, the Emperor has fallen and the Empire almost crumbled.

                          This time, they won't mess up.
