                            Okay, so I'm looking this over again to give a more detailed assessment, CatDoom.

                            Man. You tightened it up nicely, and I really have no complaints about what you have so far. I like their Bane, I like their Gift, I really like your new version of Mortify as a Devotion, and think it works perfectly for a converted Kingjan.

                            I'm gonna talk about Kingjan now and converting each of its levels of ability.
                            • So Degradation on its surface feels like a similar power to Mortify, but I'm thinking that there should be a subtle difference, namely that it should bolster the victim in the Xiao's presence while weakening them when they're away from it. Before the Xiao can learn to drain his victims, he must perfect the practice of breaking them. When activating this power, the Xiao speaks calmly to the target, flattering her or otherwise expressing approval in an apparent attempt to bolster her confidence. In fact, the power is a subtle attack, designed to weaken the victim's ability to function without the Xiao. The vampire's words seem harmless enough, but the mystic undertone delivers a deeper, more insidious message: "You are only at your best when you are with me." So this is from Degradation. I'm thinking that you make the roll, and it also afflicts them with a Condition, where they either gain a dice-trick or a bonus in the presence of the Xiao, and they suffer that bonus as a penalty when outside of it. Maybe they gain a +2 to actions in the Xiao's presence and a -2 when they're outside of it. As a direct bonus to the Xiao, they maybe gain +2 or 9-again to either encourage the target to act or to stop them from acting, as the Xiao's approval and presence are the source of their confidence and ability. I think this makes sense as requiring Majesty as its prerequisite.
                            • You've converted Mortify, so it's all good. Great job on that.
                            • Similarly, you've converted Oblivion's Kiss, and it's excellent.
                            • Wave of Apathy is okay, I guess. It reminds me of Dread Presence in how it suspends Willpower usage, but unlike Dread Presence, it's all willpower usage instead of just offensive. I'd probably keep the effect, but give it some additional oomph. Maybe it just enhances Dread Presence by offering an alternate version which instead of granting a bonus to your character, makes it so the victims can't spend willpower at all and suffer a penalty to actions equal to Nightmare due to their apathy and fear of failure. That could work.
                            • Blessed Detachment sounds like some kind of inverse Blood Bond effect, but I'm not sure how to represent it mechanically. Hmm.
                            You've done a great job thus far, nice work!

                                                            This is actually some of the best feedback I've gotten on them. I made them Kindred because originally they were a bit of a joke (catgirl gangrels). Eventually I expanded on that, made them Mekhet (the other clan in the running was Nosferatu). I could definitely do with a bit of expansion. And I'll admit I'm not actually familiar with most of the Bloodlines you named. Much as I love the way Bloodlines work, there are just a crapton of them.
