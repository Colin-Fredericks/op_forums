    [WiP] Legacies the Unspoken, a collection original Legacies from the forums

    01-03-2026, 12:59 PM
    Hello and a late happy new year! It's been a while.

    This document is something I've originally thought would be finished and shared in it's complete form by either christmas or New Year's, but I ended up being super busy all November and then getting sick for most of December and all thoughout the holidays. Ultimately I decided to share this in it's incomplete form both as a late gift to the forum community and the gauge reaction to it by the authors of the works I'm compiling.

    Without further ado, here you go: https://drive.google.com/drive/folde...usp=drive_link

    As the thread titles mentions it's still a work in progress, and by my count I still have 20 odd legacies that fit the criteria I set for this compendium, but if you like the idea of this doc and know of a Legacy I missed feel free to point me towards them. I relied entirely on the forum's search function to collect the materials so it's possible I missed things.

                          Awesome work collecting some awesome works! I have one for you, a 1E Legacy I never updated to 2E. It's a ... familiar face reimagined slightly for Mage: the Awakening. What can I say? I was being a bit cheeky when I made this!

                          The Sidereal Fellowship
                          The life of a mage is fraught with dangers unimaginable to blissfully ignorant Sleepers. There are things out there that no gun can kill, hands no faith can stay, weapons no Sleeper can wield. There are fates far worse than death.

                          Many such fates are inflicted by creatures and forces that have no right existing, things that exist outside of Fate as mages know it. When a man who died three years ago yet walks another man, that is a subversion of the natural flow of interconnections. When the alien Fae steal a human and replace it with a doppelganger made of bits of wood and string and shadows, that not only alters the stolen Sleeper's fate, it leaves behind something that by rights should not even be. It even steals one more Awakening from the infinite potential of mankind, a crime for which no punishment is great enough. And when the idigam come from their bizarre homes deep in the spirit wilds, they warp and twist reality to the point that history as it should have been becomes something that should not be.

                          The Sidereal Fellowship exists to battle such forces, to ensure that Fate progresses as it was meant to progress. They appear often out of nowhere and vanish just as quickly, remembered by none, thanked by none.

                          They wouldn't have it any other way.

                          It is not known exactly when the Sidereal Fellowship was born, although it is known as one of the most ancient Legacies. There are scant few records of its presence throughout history, however, a sign that they have performed their duties admirably. One fact that the Sidereals sometimes divulge, when it benefits them, is that the Legacy is the child of a survivor of Atlantis' fall, an Acanthus called Kejak. In the aftermath of the Ladder's destruction and the return of mages to this world of darkness, Kejak saw the monsters, indescribable and inhuman, which preyed upon defenseless mortals. In their formidable city-state, the Awakened had long forgotten this truth.

                          Many of these creatures quite literally altered the Fates of those they victimized, twisting the strands of destiny or cutting them entirely. Some of these forces were Abyssal in nature, and some were from planes even the magi knew not. Kejak and his followers strove with these beings to prevent the unraveling of the whole Tapestry with the fraying of enough threads. It was an uphill battle. They were few, and they still lived mortal lifespans. Sleepers feared them for their powers, rather than thanked them for their struggles, and some mages died at the hands of those they'd fought to protect.

                          Kejak then retreated to the highest mountain he could find to seek the answers to their plight. In meditation, he forged his own soul, simultaneously giving it great power to fight the threats to Fate as well as hide that power from Sleepers. Better they knew not their benefactors and be free to live as they would, Awakening and realizing their own potential in due time. He became Kejak the Sidereal, his power governed by the Fate written in the stars. And he taught his disciples how to walk the Sidereal path, becoming hidden within Fate and yet able to reinforce it in the Tapestry.
                          The newborn Sidereal Fellowship carried out their chosen duties with pride and the knowledge that they alone kept the greatest threats to Sleepers at bay. They sought out the Fae and destroyed or drove them back to their insane realm. They battled the undead and sent them to their proper Fates. When one of the godlike alien spirits emerged from the Shadow to satisfy its unknowable urges on the mortal world, the Fellowship vanquished it.

                          To this end, Kejak also developed a style of martial arts that perfectly blended Eastern esoterica with soundness of mind and body, allowing its practitioners to move in perfect harmony with Fate. This magical fighting style has had many names throughout the centuries, but is known most simply as "Sidereal Martial Arts", and it gives a Sidereal the edge in her struggles with creatures outside Fate. No Sleeper fighting style can match the awesome power of Sidereal Martial Arts.

                          In modern nights, the Sidereal Fellowship continues that ancient campaign. Its members are not often found among cabals of non-Sidereal mages, due to the difficulties in their fellows even remembering the Sidereal. Sidereals prefer to run with their own kind, or sometimes go alone, although their lifestyles are dangerous even for mages, and their numbers are too few to make many Sidereal cabals.

                          Sleepers do not remember the Sidereal herself, when they are lucky enough to meet her. Rather, they most likely met the Sidereal under the guise of a resplendent destiny, a semi-real persona woven from the threads of Fate. Using these resplendent destinies, a Sidereal can interact normally with Sleepers and mages, building up a presence in the community or garnering long-term allies and even enemies. Unfortunately, the resplendent destiny is not truly real, and can vanish due to vulgar magic or the Sidereal herself discarding it forever. In some cases, this is as easy as a neighbor vanishing or moving away. In others, the resplendent persona has a wife or husband, friends and coworkers, all of whom have no idea that the person so central to their lives is simply a figment of another's imagination.

                          It is a sad fact of life for Sidereals, then, that they cannot grow close to others outside their own arcane order without the inevitable difficulties. Some have tried, and even successfully maintained, relationships to Sleepers and non-Sidereal mages for their whole lives. Most do more harm than good to themselves and others for trying, and the pain of failure sticks with them.
                          It is no wonder that relationships within the Legacy, romantic and familial, are most common for Sidereals. Life among Sleepers is simply too difficult, given their unique nature and duties. Ever must they walk apart from mortal men and women to protect them from the mad hungers that stalk the shadows.

                          Parent Path or Order: Acanthus, Adamantine Arrows or Guardians of the Veil
                          Nickname: Ninja
                          Appearance:
                          Sidereals can truly exhibit any appearance, even in their natural guises. They all tend to be healthy in their true guise, as physical prowess is necessary to their survival. They have few other commonalities. Since experienced Sidereals can craft resplendent destinies for themselves that can appear to be anyone, there are no truly unifying characteristics among the Fellowship. Young or old, sickly or sturdy, they can be of any ethnicity and any gender. These features may even vary from day to day with resplendent destinies.

                          Background:
                          All Fates can draw Sidereals together, but the Legacy most often calls to those who protected others as Sleepers: cops, defense attorneys, bodyguards, even doctors, teachers, protective parents, and siblings. They come from a natural inclination to safeguard the lives and happiness of others, or from those who seek to uphold whatever laws or codes they value.

                          Organization:
                          Sidereals are rare enough that one cabal may never meet another group of Sidereals. They tend to form cabals with their fellows and stake out a particular territory, where they govern themselves as they see fit. Sometimes, this is a total democracy within the group, and sometimes the wisest or most powerful Sidereal will lead the group. Some cabals wander a region, smoothing out snags in the loom of Fate as they encounter them, borne aloft by winds of chance and destiny.

                          Some groups simply allow Fate to steer them, while some take a more proactive approach: they search the stars and games of chance for answers, they bind oaths, and look into dreams to see where they are most needed. Just as with other aspects of the Legacy, Sidereal organization can vary wildly from one group to the next. There are few overarching features to define them.

                          Suggested Oblations:
                          Rigorous martial arts practice, including sparring with other Sidereal mages; meditation upon high places or clear nights; games of fortune; protecting a Sleeper in some indirect fashion; teaching another and so subtly guiding her Fate; surrendering control over one's destiny and allowing the universe to take her where it will for an hour

                          Concepts: Cops, doctors, athletes, bodyguards, teachers

                          Attainments

                          1st: Arcane Fate
                          Prerequisites: Gnosis 3, Mind 2 (primary), Fate 1, Subterfuge 2, Occultation 1
                          Their Patterns hidden in the eddies of Fate, Sidereals become difficult to notice for Sleepers and even harder to remember. This functions similarly to the Mind 2 spell "Incognito Presence," except that it is always active and functions as though the mage had rolled successes equal to her Mind dots. Mages and other supernatural beings are subject to this Arcane Fate effect, unless they have some kind of mental shield, in which case they are allowed a reflexive Gnosis + Mind (or their relevant supernatural advantage + the rank of the shielding power) roll against the Sidereal's automatic successes. If the witness succeeds on the roll, he can remember the Sidereal normally for that encounter.

                          The Sidereal can also easily read the connections between those governed by Fate, allowing her a permanent Duration Fate 1 "Interconnections" effect. By concentrating, she can view the threads of Fate that bind all things together and discern the sympathetic connections between people, places, and things. Even as she removes herself from the normal threads of Fate and governs their weave, she grows in her ability to perceive them.

                          2nd: Resplendent Destiny
                          Prerequisites: Gnosis 5, Mind 3, Fate 3, Subterfuge 3, Occultation 2
                          Skilled Sidereals can weave "costumes" out of Fate, whole personalities and appearances, each one unique as devised by the mage. It is not a true bodily transformation, but more like an illusion that clouds the minds of those who see her. This works like the Mind 3 spell "Imposter," counting the mage's Mind dots as automatic successes (and thus fooling sight, sound, and touch by default), with a few important differences. First, the mage must create a fictional persona, and cannot mimic an existing person with her resplendent destiny.

                          Second, the power of this attainment has a default Duration of permanent. She may wear the resplendent destiny for as long as she wants, and may even "schuck" it to return to her normal guise at will as a reflexive action. She can then don it again at a later time. Supernatural witnesses are subject to the same resistance roll as outlined for Arcane Fate, above. Mages, however, see right through the resplendent destiny.

                          The Sidereal may hold a number of destinies equal to her Gnosis dots. While under the guise of a resplendent destiny, the mage may not use the "Imposter" spell. Using vulgar magic while disguised can fray the destiny and possibly destroy it. Once a resplendent destiny has been burned away in this manner, it can never be recreated. Consider the destiny to have a "Durability" equal to the mage's Gnosis. If more successes are ever rolled for Paradox in a single scene than that Durability, whether or not they're taken as backlash damage, the resplendent destiny burns away and is lost forever.

                          Optional Arcanum: Fate 3
                          Even as she learns how to weave a new destiny for herself out of the threads of Fate, a Sidereal may also learn how to alter the destinies of others. She can grant a temporary turn in fortune, a blessing that lasts only for so long but is quite powerful and can even avert death in some cases. Regardless, the Sidereal knows that, in the World of Darkness, even a single blessed day can make a lifetime of struggle and suffering all worth it, renewing the soul's vigor and enabling Sleepers to fight through one more day of the Lie.

                          This is the Fate 3 spell "Blessing." The mage rolls Manipulation + Subterfuge + Fate as an instant action, and the effect only applies to Sleepers. Though the default Blessing as granted by Sidereals is an instant action, the Chosen may choose to cast it as an extended spell to grant a more potent blessing.

                          3rd: Duck Fate
                          Prerequisites: Gnosis 7, Time 4, Mind 4, Subterfuge 4, Occultation 3
                          Near the pinnacle of her power, a Sidereal at this level of enlightenment may dramatically, if temporarily, alter her own Fate. She can literally dodge the deleterious Fate she might otherwise suffer, whether it is a result of external factors or her own actions. This ability may be used in place of a standard dodge maneuver in combat or when afflicted by harmful environmental effects. The spell is not a perfect defense, because the mage may still be in harm's way once the magic ends, but it can give her time to escape an otherwise certain doom.

                          Effectively, this is the Time 4 spell "Temporal Stutter," but it only applies to the mage herself and uses her Time dots as successes. Thus, at Time 4, the Sidereal disappears from the point in Time at which she previously existed and reappears twelve seconds later – possibly giving her time to escape or causing enemies to overlook or run past her. This follows the same rules for reappearing in the same location as an object as Temporal Stutter does.

                          Unlike the normal version of Temporal Stutter, the Sidereal using this effect can be considered to have moved as far as is possible for her during the intervening time. This movement does not take into account vehicles, because the attainment works only on the mage herself. Thus, as far as she can travel in 12-15 seconds (possibly aided by magic, if she had such a spell cast previously, like "Acceleration") on foot or maybe by bike is as far as she can get with Duck Fate.

                          Optional Arcanum: Fate 4
                          Having mastered the ability to twist and weave her own Fate, the Sidereal can also ensure that another person's Fate progresses naturally by destroying supernatural forces preventing that flow. She can use the "Destroy Bindings" Fate 4 spell as an instant action, but may not use the ability against a mage-familiar bond. That is not a forced indenture, nor does it unduly alter the destinies of either participant. Certainly not like existence as a ghost or having one's soul trapped in the wrong body or a soul jar, to be used by some unscrupulous mage. Naturally, then, the Sidereals are perhaps the worst enemies of the Tremere Liches, whose heinous acts constitute the worst offenses against Wisdom and Fate possible in the Fallen World.

                          I don't think I ever finished the Sidereal Martial Arts Fighting Style, but here's what I had. It's not balanced in its final form or anything yet, but hey, it is a start. Very much a WIP, but I don't know if I'll ever get around to finishing it now, so here ya go.


                          Magical Fighting Style: Sidereal Martial Arts
                          Prerequisites: Five dots of Fighting Style Merits (at least one of which must be rated 3+), Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 2, Brawl 2, Time 2, Fate 2, Sidereal Legacy
                          Effect:
                          Your character is trained in the unique and powerful martial arts practiced by the Sidereal Fellowship, able to weave Fate and Time into her movements to strike with surprising force or from seemingly impossible angles. She has been training since her induction into the Sidereal Fellowship and will continue to train throughout her life. Even masters of the art are always pupils, constantly learning from their fellows, even rank amateurs.

                          Sidereal Martial Arts consists of a series of forms, each guided by Fate and Time, that provide beneficial effects to their users. The forms may be used together, but each requires an instant action to activate. Each lasts a number of turns equal to the user's Gnosis rating.

                          Dots purchased in this Merit allow access to special combat maneuvers. Each maneuver is a prerequisite for the next. So, your character can't have "Soulfire Shaper Form" until he has "Four Magical Materials Form." The maneuvers and their effects are listed below, most of which are based on the Brawl Skill.

                          Maneuver 1: Four Magical Materials Form
                          -By tapping into the primal flows of Essence and Mana that infuse the Fallen World, the character may harden herself to attack and remove imperfection in her body. She gains +1 Stamina while this form is in effect.

                          Maneuver 2: Soul Fire Shaper Form
                          -Gnosis is considered 1 higher for purposes of maximum Mana pool, Mana expenditure rates and for supernatural tolerance. This can take the effective Gnosis rating for these purposes above 10.

                          Maneuver 3: Games of Divinity Form
                          -Mortals and supernaturals whose potency Advantages are less than the Sidereal's must spend 1 point of Willpower to attack her. Costs 1 Mana.

                          Maneuver 4: Demense and Manse Form (I was working on subbing this with Spell-Shattering Palm)
                          Maneuver 4: Spell-Shattering Palm
                          -May make a counterspell action as "Counterspell Prime," as a reflexive action, using Dexterity + Brawl (with an appropriate martial arts specialty). This costs one extra Mana.

                          Maneuver 5: Prismatic Arrangement of Creation
                          -May not be surprise attacked, double Initiative rating, immune to sensory penalties. Unseen Sense allows her to sense every supernatural effect, object and creature within (Gnosis x 10) yards. Noticing something hidden requires a reflexive perception roll versus the concealing effect, adding the mage's Gnosis in automatic successes.
