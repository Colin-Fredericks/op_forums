            Sorry to drop off for a bit but it looks like there's been a lot happening (and belated b-day cake for the anniversary). And there's even a TvTropes page too, which is cool.

            I had some more refinements for the Chorus model. Passive and Active are as they are, stackable depending on where a Siren puts her focus. Invested, which requires a lot of Pneuma to upkeep a new permanent effect, has its own tier from the effects being scalable for Pneuma price.

            Comparing the Akashic Record table to possible costs, I thought of having Invested be tiered for 30, 50 and 75 Pneuma. Or for better modulation, perhaps, the basic Investment is 20 Pneuma per x effect. This doesn't mean that the Siren needs to have a Pneuma pool big enough to Invest all in one go, although high Akashic Record Sirens would have an easier time of achieving a certain level of Investment. For lesser Sirens, they achieve the Invested effect once they are able to encode enough Pneuma into the song, the duration starting once that has been achieved. A partial Investment can be kept indefinitely if the Siren is part of the Chorus, although doesn't take effect until effectively powered of course.

            So let's try applying this to some Chorus models.

            The Chorus of the Black Huntsman

            Invested: the Faustian Bargain
            The Siren can grant a robust supernatural deal with a mortal, encoded into the Song that binds the Siren and their target into an inescapable declaration. A deal is tracked by its Boons, which are usually weighted for the mortal. However, the Siren can force her partner to upkeep their end of the deal by receiving their own Boon representing the mortal's efforts, which is free but cannot be greater than the mortal's Boon. However the mortal agrees to more terms (Siren Boons) to increase the effectiveness of their Boon by one level.

            [30 or 20x Pneuma] Small Boon: a permanent minor effect, like a Conclusion 1-2 Verse or new Social/Mental Merits
            [50 or 40x Pneuma] Medium Boon: a permanent effect like a Conclusion 3 Verse, new Physical Merits, new Skill dots, or 2 Small Boon effects
            [75 or 60x Pneuma] Large Boon: a permanent effect like a Conclusion 4-5 Verse, new Attribute dots, 2 Medium Boon effects or 3-6 Small Boon effects

            -------------------------

            Commentary: this is an interesting Chorus as much like the Embassy to Stories for Princess it grants the Siren in this case one of the main systems of another game line, the Changeling ability to craft and bind others to Pledges. Yet it isn't dependent on having Changeling present in the game for it to be used, unlike the Embassy to Stories privileges that grant Unseen Sense: Wyrd and use the talecrafting rules. Thematically, for the scope of your Chronicle, it could be the Wyrd upholding the bargain if you ran a crossover game, although it works on its own merits for being a pledge 'cast out into the Song' that will then recur to the benefit of both sides if the terms are upheld. Like an omega level Verse. There's a lot of shared terminology with Changeling, although that is only because both the game and the Chorus are drawing from similar folklore, and again a point for the Chorus side for not needing another venue to become required reading.

            This is really making me want to blend Sirens and Changelings together more. I am having plenty of plot ideas involving Siren flesh entering a Goblin Market.

                        Investment Costs
                        One of the main ideas for the Investment affect, and how it ties into Choruses in general, is that it's of a lot of upfront effort and commitment to the Siren's part for the cause of her Chorus. Once Invested, it is a permanent effect available as is any other Chorus benefit. It needs no other costs by this point, at most an activation condition. If a small bit of Pneuma and a Verse can cause a change in the world, a great expenditure would cause an equally monumental change. And the Invested benefit should be scalable in that regard, better done with the 'X Pneuma per instance' ratio.

                        It also serves as an alternative scale of the Chorus benefits. A Siren's advancement in a Chorus is not tied to her Akashic Record but by how much Pneuma she puts into it, investing herself. Which, when taking the upper bounds of Investment into account, would have no theoretical limit. Yet there's limits as to how this would realistically enter play, such as how you would get that much Pneuma to begin with.

                        Another thought is that, for the purposes of becoming Invested in her Chorus, a Siren could take 1 resistant Aggravated damage to get their Akashic Record in Pneuma for the Investment only. Choruses would likely warn of Sirens so motivated to cause a grand scale effect that they undid themselves in the process.

                        The current unit for determining tiers of Investment, as I presented above, is a block of Pneuma for each layer of the effect. 20 is closer to an ideal number, the lowest base unit I would suggest under this model is 15 Pneuma per instance of the effect. That is the max Pneuma pool for Akashic Record 6, creating an iconic Siren symmetry of the '3's compared to the Akashic Record prerequisite of 3 for any Chorus. Having a universal ratio for Chorus Investment is important because regardless of what the support, benefit or thematics of the Chorus is it will make the OOC budgeting of Pneuma very straightforward. It is also used as the cost to switch Active benefits if the Siren focuses on a different Chorus before the month concludes.

                        A Siren Investing in her Chorus is not putting that Pneuma towards other purposes. Investment, in practice, is a 'Pneuma sink.' Is she finding a regular tithe of Pneuma leaves her drained, or that the great investment leaves her without the Breath to power other abilities unrelated to the Chorus once she had that set up?

                        This could also be represented with the 'binding Pneuma' you suggested, as it was in Genius or the Demigod venue being developed elsewhere on the site. In that model the Siren's max Pneuma pool is shrinking, she has already dedicated the Pneuma for a specific purpose in the Chorus so other use and storage becomes limited. If that where the case I would drop the ratio to 10 Pneuma per. Although this changes the mechanics and application of the Chorus benefits.

                        Firstly, your rank of Akashic Record regains emphasis. While this creates a better defined cap a Siren literally could not impart certain tiers of Investment until she gained Akashic Record, gaining more of her Pneuma potential to dedicate. And while ending an Investment is easy (she removes the restriction on max Pneuma pool) it creates further punishment for changing Choruses. Pneuma would have to be dedicated for a cycle to get the Active benefit early before the restriction is removed, and the same amount further bound to become Invested at such a time.


                        In short: Invested as a 'Pneuma sink' or 'Pneuma pool restriction' will have to be determined for the bounds of the top Chorus benefit are implemented.

                        ----------------------

                        Also going to comment on the other Choruses for the points raised above.

                        The Chorus of Gloriana's Satirists

                        Invested: Sweet Nothings Will Get You Anywhere
                        By becoming a Critic the Siren finds that the world of politics grants leeway only one way. Privileges unavailable to most come who you know. At any time, once a Session, the Siren may exchange her Politics dots of Social Merits into other Social Merits regarding political or business dealings for the rest of the scene.

                        Drawback: while this will adhere a new group to the Siren's beck and call, it does not help her with the group she left behind with the temporary exchange of Social Merit(s). Let alone how some in the new organization may react unkindly to sudden upstarts. Depending on how word of her affiliation change gets out or her abuse of this benefit affects a group, the Siren risks gaining the Notoriety Condition.

                        [20 or 15 Pneuma] The Siren gains the benefit and drawback outlined above. For every additional [20 or 15] the Siren invests she may choose to increase the number of times a Session she may use this benefit, increase her effective Politics dots by half its original rating (round down) for the purposes of this benefit, or negate 1 incursion of the Notoriety Condition from the use of this benefit.

                        Commentary: I always like seeing more uses politically minded character perks, because at least in the games I've played they're ones overlooked. I'm reminded of the Embassy to the Economy in Princess to some extent, as they both cover similar spheres of influence but the Chorus is exclusively as to how the Siren positions herself with the organization in question, while the Embassy is more focused on the finance.

                        This Chorus is also an example of a stackable Invested Benefit, as more cost increases the level of benefit. This is contrasted to the Huntsmens dealmaking, in which the tiers of possible Boons (normally) have separate costs to set up, although the Siren would only be limited by her Pneuma in the number of such deals she could keep active.

                        The Chorus of Turnadot

                        Active: Gaze of True Love
                        As a Matchmaker the Siren is premier on realizing the extent of others relationships. By touching a target, the Siren may ask the ST a question about the target's relations, which must be answered to the best of the ST's ability. For each target the Siren can only ask a number of these questions up to her Empathy dots. Once touching the target, she can ask any number of questions she is allowed.

                        {example questions remain the same}

                        Invested: Love Conquers All
                        The Matchmaker is able to prompt and shape her targets into new relationships, or bring nuances into already present ones. [Basic price is the Inspired Condition, while further investment likely gets into crafting Relationships between subjects]

                        Commentary: this expands on mechanics introduced in Siren, making it a decent vanilla type of Chorus. Although it is half complete on the draft, any further comments I have are for the use of Relationships in the system. I would suggest removing Relationships from being a 'merit' as they have more particulars and variation than other merits, letting it be an independent system.

                                                    Alrighties, I've finally edited the Choruses so that they don't rely on Pneuma expendature to activate Arias and I've created the Invested Aria for the Chorus of Turandot.

                                                    The Edit on Arias and explaining Cycles

                                                    There are usually three benefits associated to Choruses, known as Arias. Arias are gained by a Siren spending a prerequisite amount of Pneuma while she’s within a Chorus within a Cycle, which covers a lunar month. When a Cycle is over, a Siren must repay a certain amount of Pneuma to regain the Arias she desires. Choruses are special however that a Siren may belong to any number of them so long as she has the Pneuma to pay within a Cycle. There is a limit to how many Arias she may own at once however. She may have as many Passive Arias as she can pay for, a number of Active Arias equal to her Akashic Record and a fiinally Siren may only ever have one Invested Aria at any Cycle in time. If a Siren cannot renew the Pneuma cost for any Aria she was within at the beginning of a new Cycle, then she isn’t considered a part of the Chorus the Aria(s) belonged to. Passive Arias require around 10 Pneuma, Active Arias cost 25 Pneuma and Invested Arias cost 35 Pneuma to unlock respectively. Dice rolls are not required to activate an Aria. Most Choruses do have a Prerequisite skill and Akashic Record rating before one can gain an Aria or make use of it, which effectively means a Siren who wishes to first join a Chorus must have the bare minimum for the first Aria. To gain the Passive Aria, the Siren must have Akashic Record 3, the required Skill to be at 3 dots. The Active Aria requires Akashic Record 5 and the prerequisite Skill at 4 dots to gain. The Invested Aria demands the Siren to have Akashic Record 7 and the prerequisite Skill at five dots.

                                                    Invested Aria: Love Conquers All
                                                    If love conquers all, then perfect love perfectly conquers all. At this level, the Matchmakers and Homewreckers can truly live up to their name, creating relationships where there is none or breaking them apart. The Siren may at any point choose two people in her line of sight and divide her Empathy dots to grant them the Relationship merit or a Social Merit that represents a connection between them (Allies, Contacts, Mentor, Retainer, Staff and True Friend is applicable) at an equivalent number of dots for a scene. The Siren may inversely use the same dots to deny a person the use of the same merits above, to which they remain supressed for a scene. If a person’s Relationship or merit dots are destroyed, then Sanctity of Merits applies.
                                                    A Matchmaker may also edit already existing Relationships, as well. She may use her unspent Empathy dots to add or subtract Kinks and Comforts at a rate of 1:1. A Matchmaker may take out all the Kinks from a Relationship or a Homewrecker may deprive a relationship of it’s Comforts in this fashion.
