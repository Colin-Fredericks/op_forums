                                                CADMUS' CROP

                                                The dragon can create life from nothing, summoning the aid of a personal warrior.

                                                Spartoi have 3 in every Physical Attribute, 1 in every other Attribute, and 1 dot in Athletics, Brawl, Weaponry and Firearms.

                                                The effect can also be delayed; the dragon places the tooth into the ground after breathing on it, and the Spartolos will rise and carry out the Dragon's will only when the trigger that the Dragon set is met (for example, an intruder entering the Lair).

                                                Spartolos
                                                Mental Attribute s: Intelligence 1; Wits 1; Resolve 1
                                                Physical Attributes: Strength 3; Dexterity 3; Stamina 3
                                                Social Attributes: Presence 1; Manipulation 1; Composure 1
                                                Skills: Firearms 1; Brawl 1; Weaponry 1; Athletics 1
                                                Willpower: 2
                                                Initiative: 4
                                                Defense: 1 (reduced to 0); 2/1 Armor
                                                Speed: 11 (species factor 5)
                                                Size: 5
                                                Weapon: Sword - Damage: 2 Lethal; Dice Pool: 6
                                                Health: 8

                                                (Spartoi possess no Virtue or Vice.
                                                Instead, a Spartolos receives a point of Willpower if it bests an opponent in combat or contest.)

                                                ---
                                                Healing:
                                                This is a sketch for another possible system - still working on some of this.

                                                It might look a bit messy, because I balanced it for 1 Breath = 1 Bashing damage,
                                                Maybe this is, yknow, wrong, maybe it is better to go back to a more Vampire-like price.

                                                As written here, Dragons may lose more Breath to healing themselves than say, Vampires, but counterbalance it somewhat by a greater degree of flexibility. And their defense is definitely on par (the legendary "dragon scales").

                                                USES OF BREATH

                                                PROTECTIVE SKIN:

                                                First of all, the Oroboros can cancel damage as it happens (as a reflexive action). For 1 point of Breath, the dragon can cancel out one point of Bashing or Lethal damage.

                                                Cancelling Bashing damage is covert; the dragon's body simply appears to be more sturdy, more resistant to harm. Cancelling Lethal wounds however is clearly supernatural, as bullets bounce off and weapons slide away from the dragon's skin. (Fortunately a bullet is too fast for the naked eye so the dragon can usually simply deny that she got hit in the first place.)

                                                (This power can protect the dragon from Exceptional Successes or Conditions like Leg Wrack or Stun, reducing the number of successes on the opponent's roll below what would be required. It can also protect her from Resistant wounds, which would be impossible to recover later with supernatural healing).

                                                Aggravated damage can not be downgraded in this manner.

                                                HEALING WOUNDS:

                                                A point of Breath recovers a point of Bashing damage.
                                                Two points of Breath recover a point of Lethal damage
                                                Five points of Breath, plus a point of Willpower can restore a point of Aggravated damage. Only one Aggravated wound can be recovered this way per day, and this includes uses of the Mend Edict she received from others), she can not do it again until the next day.

                                                REGROWING LOST BODY PARTS:

                                                Finally, the dragon can channel the same energy to mend wounds that would normally be beyond human regenerative capacity.

                                                1 Breath can heal small, cosmetic damage: a lost tooth, a permanent scar.

                                                2 Breath (equal to 1 Lethal damage) can regrow more significant damage such as a finger, an eye, ear or nose.

                                                5 Breath+1 WP (equal to 1 Aggravated damage) can regrow a hand, and most internal organs (the heart is the only exception).

                                                10 Breath+2WP (equal to 2 Aggravated damage) can regrow a whole limb. It can also regrow a head, a power only used by Hydrae (a person with only one head dies like anyone else, obviously).

                                                Should a dragon lose her body, regrowing an entire body from scratch means an expenditure of 25 Breath+5 WP (equal to 5 Aggravated damage), AND then the dragon has to restore her Health track separately, as a freshly returned body has Aggravated damage in ever Health box. This means being out of commission for about two weeks, provided that the dragon has a steady income of Breath. Dragons who have lost other sources of Breath while dead can only rely on the internal energy source of the Heart (1 Breath/day) and so the same process for them can take months.

                                                Reattachments: It takes 1 Breath to reattach a cleanly-cut body part. Larger body parts or gorier wounds that have destroyed a lot of flesh may take 2 Breath. Anything bigger requires complete regeneration as above.

                                                Cauterization:

                                                Should the tissue on a wound get burned thoroughly (cautered) for any reason, that wound can not regenerate itself for the duration of the scene (essentially becoming a Resistant wound temporarily).

                                                Lastly, features that only manifest at higher levels of divinity heal differently. A lost horn may turn into a head scar when the dragon returns to human form. These wounds in Divine form heal once the other, smaller wound heals in human form.

Those belonging to the North are knowledgeable and secretive, they seem to know every secret there is (really they don't but there close) and they know how to put those into good use,they are masters of propaganda and blackmail,with a little whisper here and a little whisper there, they can bring whole kingdoms to there feet. The ones who belong to the East are prideful and narcissistic, they think that all should bask in their glory and will do anything to get that,they are master persuaders, no one can seem to say no in their presence,for good or ill(mostly ill). Those who claim South are wrathful and tyrannical,they see world as theirs to take and they take it forcibly, who ever stands in there way they see it forfeits the right to live,which they are quite happy to take. The ones who are West are cruel and apathetic,they all have a goal and they will do anything to reach it,they are master planners,always seeming to be ten steps ahead of you(think James Moriarty). The Zhen are mysterious and insightful,they are the guardian angels of the Oroboroi, when ever you hit a crescendo,they are there watching,evaluating. Next is resurrection, the Eternal can't die,period. The process is actually much like the dragons, when ever there form is destroyed,they appear in the Treasury. From there all they have to is find a way back to their Shrines. The directional Eternal don't have much of a foothold on the Daylight, what this means that if they what to stay in Daylight they must first do something,create a Shrine. A Djinn's Shrine is its resting place,it is where they normally replenish on there Breath( e.i. At dawn everyday if the Eternal is at its Shrine it will gain a point of Breath), it is also their gateway into 2nd level Treasury(other than death). All phoenixes have to have a Shrine, without one they would dissipate into the Treasury in a matter of hours. This is law for all but the Zhen,they don't have no need for it since they are not as unbalanced as the rest. Why would a Djinn want to be in Daylight you may ask,well much of the same reasons as an Oroborus actually. You see the Eternal were created to be servants,that was their purpose. Now that most of them don't have that purpose,they seek out what they most envy,rulership. "Why can't we rule?" They ask,"we were created by the Longs just the Oroboroi,we have as much right as them." The thing the Eternal want is to usurp the usurpers,but the Eternal is not of flesh and blood,so they have to do their ruling subtlety,Influencing,Urging,Possessing,and Claiming the people around them. The Eternal can have Assets, the number of Assets they can have is locked by their Rank,also they have to spend a number of Breath determined by their Rank,if they can not pay they dissipate into Treasury and they lose their Roost, as there is far more Eternal than Oroboroi, the phoenixes normally act as the dragons rivals and competitors in their chosen domain. This is normally the reason for the Cardinal Strata,not so for the Zhen. While the other Strata may seem more dangerous than the Zhen,it couldn't be more wrong, the source of this is a simple thing, their Tests. While a Zhens test can be beneficial and a source of power, they all come with a price. The Zhen believe it is their duty to make the Oroboroi perfect rulers, and they will take out any distractions it sees that will get in the way of that. One example, if the Zhen notices that the Oroboroi is spending to much time being with its family than running and maintaining its Province, it will use its Influences to take the family out of the equation,permanently. If the Oroborus survives the tragedy it will be rewarded(if it did exceptionally well,the Zhen might even resurrect its family,might.) if it doesn't, than the Zhen will make it disappear. For this reason when a dragon learns that a Zhen is in town, the dragon will be experiencing more sleepless nights than not. It's also important to note that if a Zhen thinks a mortal is worthy of a Heart, it will arrange circumstances for the mortal to become the owner and become one with the Oroboroi. If there is no Heart possibly available or it thinks it surpasses the average, it will set up a test to see exactly how worthy it truly is, if the mortal passes it will do the impossible, it will become a Heart. It will sacrifice its Eternal life for the mortal to become a dragon, creating a new Heart into the world.( if it does this,treat the Method as Inheritor.)

                                                            The appearance of the Eternal varies, the can take on any form. The most common of those is.bird and a person but it can be anything,from squirrels to horrific monsters. The only common fact is that it is made of smokeless fire. The color of the flames depend on the Strata, South is red, North is black, East is white, West is Blue, and the Zhen is yellow.
