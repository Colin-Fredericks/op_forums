    Let’s be a little more precise than “the outskirts of Boston.” Rhea, Cano and Rene arrive in a graveyard, late at night. They’re not the only ones there. We see Spooky, but something about him immediately sets us on edge, and with Mage Sight and Supernal Visions, Rhea discovers that he’s… a vampire. Vampires are real? She did not know this 30 seconds ago. Damn. She throws up a ward and demands to know what the hell is going on.

    Turns out that Spooky and Shard died. Except… not completely. Shard is in the service of a death goddess, see, and for unknown reasons, she decided that it wasn’t their time yet and sent them back. Spooky is a vampire. He has very little idea what exactly that entails. Shard has been reduced even further – he’s now an extremely weak spirit/ghost/weirder thing on the cusp of dissolution, unable to manifest.

    Well. Rhea is super spooked and threatens Spooky with immolation (she is an Adept of Forces, in addition to Master of Prime) should he try to drink her blood.

    Shard reports that there are now several versions of himself running around the world, and would we please pretty please help him gather than and put him back together into a real person. He can point us in the direction of his various shards, but nothing more than that.

    To cut a bit of exploration short, we eventually locate a tiny, extremely cute Changeling whom Rhea dubs Chibinagi (Chibi-Kusunagi). Chibinagi is living in the tunnels underneath Boston, eating rats and hiding from everyone. We convince him we mean no harm by offering him food, and explaining that we’re his friends. Shard (the ghost/spirit thing) fuses with him. Cano thinks he’s the cutest thing ever, and begs Rhea to let him stay with them. Her studio apartment is starting to get rather crowded, but she sighs and agrees.


    Spooky, being an extremely inexperienced vampire, has an accident while hunting, and kills the woman he was feeding from. He doesn’t tell the cabal about this.


    An hour north of the city lies the home Rhea grew up in. The home where she killed her father. The home where her mother still lives.

    When goblins finally return to report to her on the state of the house – one female occupant, the whole place under some sort of illusion, gives them the creeps – Rhea is unnerved. She hasn’t been home in two years, not since the accident. But things are different now. She has friends. She’s a far, far more powerful mage. She’s ready to face her past. It’s time for a visit.

        Rhea's childhood home is an unremarkable suburban house in a quiet little neighborhood. Rhea, as the only member of the cabal that knows how to drive, takes everyone there in her car. She's visibly nervous as they pull up. She can't see any evidence of the illusion that the goblins reported. They knock on the door. Skeleton Key and Cano, deciding that something is off about the whole situation, decide to sneak around the back and drop through a window into the basement rather than enter with everyone else.

        Her mother answers. Like Rhea, she's a small hispanic woman. She looks at Rhea, looks at her friends, and invites them all in. Sit down, she tells Rhea, which Rhea does. There's a bit of awkward discussion, in which Rhea is shamed for not calling or coming home in the two years since her father died. Rhea is quiet and obedient, defending her mother when Chibinagi calls her out on berating daughter. The mother gets up to make tea for everyone, telling them all to stay put. Rhea sits on her hands. Chibinagi is less obedient, and sneaks after her into the kitchen. He notices something strange - Rhea's mother seems less... real, less well-defined while she's puttering about in the kitchen. Smudged a little.

        In the basement, the illusion over the house is poorly maintained. There's water on the floor, leaves and detrius scattered on the floor, broken windows. No one's been down here in months or years. Spiders everywhere. And, in one corner stuffed in a cabinet, is a dead goblin. Skeleton Key attempts to look into the past, but looses a clash against a powerful ward. He tries again and gets through. Several days ago, the goblin was killed up on the main floor, then brought down here by a barely human figure - but even as he watches the scene, the past begins rewriting itself, filling in details of Rhea's mother, making her look more human, more real. He looks deeper into the past. Two years ago, the house was empty. Then, one day, Rhea's mother simply appeared, and started keeping house as though she'd always been there. Skeleton Key freaks out, bumps into something, makes a crash.

        Rhea's mother is not amused by the crash in the basement. "More of your friends?" she scowls. Rhea mutely nods. "I'll go bring them up, I suppose. No, stay," she orders as Rhea rises to follow. Rhea sits back down. Chibinagi is not so obedient, and sneaks down behind her.

        The postman delivers a package. It's addressed to Mathias, but Rhea signs for it. This is intensely concerning.

        In the basement, Rhea's mother is... less human. Like in the past, she's blurry around the edges, more obviously supernatural. Skeleton Key hides behind a couch, and fails his clashes to learn more. As she stalks towards his hiding place, he panics and shoves the couch over on her. His Fate magic announces that it's very full of venomous spiders, and Skeleton Key and Cano flee upstairs as she screams.


        As her mother staggers up from the basement, covered in rapidly swelling bug bites, Rhea is nearly hysterical, screaming at her cabal for attacking her mother and rushing to her side. The woman is back to looking human now that she's on the main floor. Rhea rushes her out to the car, intent on getting her to the hospital *right now*. Chibinagi manages to get her to take a few deep breaths before she starts the car, severely reducing her likelihood of crashing on the way there. She leaves the other Mages behind, only taking only her mother and Cano.

        At the hospital, they check her in to the emergency room. Rhea's not crying - she's not really the sort. Mostly she's confused and distraught at why her companions would attack her family. Cano's attempts to comfort her are interrupted by a very shaken nurse, who announces that they lost her. Not dead, but... Rhea rushes in to find a pile of leaves on the ground. Apparently her mother just up and dissolved a few minutes after she left Rhea's side. Cano, from the pile of leaves, extracts a pair of crossed sticks tied together with twine. Confronted with this evidence, she finally has to believe what her friends have been trying to convince her of - something is very, very wrong here, even though she herself can find nothing unusual with Mage Sight or the arcana at her disposal. They head back to her mother's house.


        Remember Rhea's Flame spirit, given her by the Priestess, to help her discover her true name? It didn't leave the house when Rhea did, and has been helping them discover the truth about things by burning away lies. The other Mages have discovered that the entire structure is decayed, the same way as the basement was - it's only an illusion presenting the whole thing as intact. It's also located some interesting bundles of sticks, similar to the one her mother dissolved into at the hospital scattered around, and keeps trying to burn them.

        Cano walks in to see the other Mages holding a bundle of sticks over the stove, burning them. She asks what's that. Chibinagi replies that it's a Fetch.

        Rhea walks in to see the other Mages holding her mother over the stove, burning her.

        She screams at them to get out, what the hell are they doing. As soon as she enters the room, the others begin to see what she sees, her mother beginning to reform, first as a blur, and then into a burned woman Rhea is cradling and rocking in between screaming at them. They manage to get her out of the house though, because the flame spirit has moved from trying to burn the Fetches to trying to burn down the whole house. It's succeeding.

        Rhea *knows* this isn't her mother. She can feel the magic now. She's torn between knowledge of the Truth - that this is a fey construct - and the truth, which is that her friends are asking her to let her mother go, to let them burn her alive. She cries, and cradles her, and cries more, and, finally, lets them take the bundle of sticks from her arms and toss it into the flames. As she lets go, the illusion finally gives way completely. The house, now aflame, is more ruin than house. It's just a bundle of sticks covered with Fey magic.

        In a torrent of power the likes of which the others have never felt from her before, Rhea turns the burning house into an inferno - Transmute energy, turning heat into flames, which produce more heat, which is transformed into more flames, which... the inferno consumes everything as she cries out her connection to her past. She never lived here. Her mother isn't real. Mathias knows and knew. What else about her life is a lie?


        So as a player, I figured out what was going on fairly early on. Whenever the other players tried to look at things magically, the ST would ask Rhea to clash, which she inevitably won (good luck + having lots of dice), which resulted in no one being able to learn anything. She never had to clash when she tried to examine things, merely failed without a roll.

        The final spell also gave Rhea her first ever paradox condition. Very appropriate. I loved the whole session.

            This session was the darkest in the game. Tl;dr - Mathias forges a Lasting, Connected sympathy with Rhea against her will. It is not graphic, either in this post nor in-game, but consider this a trigger warning.


            It's time for a long talk with Mathias. Why, exactly, was a package with his name on it delivered to her mother's house? Why was her mother a Fetch, and why has the house not been lived in for years? They sleep on it, but in the morning, discover the package addressed to Mathias has disappeared. Some Time investigation shows that a hobgoblin snuck in in the middle of the night and stole it. Violating her sanctum like that would subject Mathias to political repercussions... but if it's just the fey, well, nothing much she can do about that.

            Now Rhea's nervous. The Guardian of the Veil knows more than he let on last time he saw her, and she wants answers. They arrange a meeting in a neutral place of his suggestion - one of Harvard's lecture halls. He wants to see her alone, but she refuses, insisting that her cabal be present for the meeting, since at the moment she feels decidedly unsafe.

            They meet two days later, and as promised, Mathias is alone. He has a runic circle drawn up on the floor, warded and veiled so, according to him, they can speak privately. He steps in and gestures her to join. The rest of the cabal sits down to watch over them, to make sure nothing funny happens while they speak.

            Rhea steps into the circle. A complex, prepared spell triggers, stripping her wards. She and Mathias both vanish. She's been kidnapped.


            Rhea finds herself in the middle of a vast, sandy desert, still standing in the warded circle, while Mathias is now outside. The wards have come to life now, and while the recognizes several of the arcana used, there are plenty that she doesn't understand. She's scared.

            "Do you have any questions?"

            Rhea shakes her head mutely. She had questions, but they've all been silenced by the violation of the truce she'd thought protected her. Though most of her protections were stripped during the teleport, she managed to replace her Supernal Veil without him noticing. She feels a clash, but veiling is her speciality, and she has three dots of Occultation - again, she's covered her true abilities, and is posing as a mere Disciple of Prime with Gnosis 2.

            "Hm, strange. You, of all people, I expected to grow more quickly. Well then, shall we get started?"

            She considers revealing her power, tearing aside the wards and going for his throat, but she's in the middle of a desert. He's prepared his ground, and she's alone, and he's a more experienced Mage by far. It reminds her of... of another time. Rhea has no idea what he wants from her, but she feels helpless to even protest. Her throat is frozen, so she simply nods.

            Reading from a tome, a prepared Rote, he draws forth both of their numbuses. They mingle, forced together, forging a connection, even merge to a small extent. This is her innermost soul he's manipulating. Rhea feels vastly inferior, but more than that, violated. She's left shaking as the magic dissipates - but not the connection he's forced open.

            "I'm disappointed, I'd have expected you to have figured out more by now. Would you like me to take you back to Harvard?"

            She looks around at the desert. Rhea has no idea where she is or how to get home. Though she hates the thought of letting him work more magic on her, she mutely nods. He touches her arm, and flinching, finds herself back in the room with the rest of her cabal. They ask her what happened, but pale and shaking, she refuses to answer.

            She keeps it together long enough to get back to her apartment and lock herself in the bathroom before throwing up.


            Mathias kidnapped her from neutral ground, implied that he knew more about her than she herself did, and forged a Connected sympathetic bond between them. Stronger than a lifelong partner, stronger than parents, children, her own blood.

            It's also Lasting. She's literally incapable of emotionally letting go. So yeah. Soul-rape.

            And she has no idea why.

                We're still about 6 months in the past here, so don't take my account of anything, especially not fights, as a blow-by-blow. Everyone was doing things and helping, but I only remember - and am putting on the page - the pivotal details that shaped the outcome.


                Taking a break from Rhea's issues for a bit - she's not ready to talk about what happened, nor is she able to take immediate action - Chibinagi assumes center stage. His project at the moment is to find and merge with the other shards of Kusunagi's soul, in an attempt to reforge the blade Kusunagi and return himself to being a Mage.

                It's an easier problem than it could be, because he can always sense the pull of his other selves, and can follow it like a compass. Late at night so that Spooky can accompany us (remember we have a Vampire who hangs out with us?), we follow Chibinagi's instincts to Boston's docks, and from there to a specific warehouse. Locks are not a problem for Mages.

                Skeleton Key, Chibinagi, Rhea, Spooky, Garrett and Cano enter. It's dark, so while the others discuss the best way to get darkvision, Rhea goes over and turns on the lights. It's the sort of person she is. The place is full of construction machinery, some of which looks like it's been in a fire.

                Exploring further results in an ambush. Naturally. We were expecting that, but the combination of fire elemental and animated backhoe-golem has most of the party backing away carefully. Garrett doesn't want to try wrestling with heavy machinery, Chibinagi and Cano are useless in a fight, and Skeleton Key likes to avoid personal danger. That leaves Spooky - a relatively new vampire - and Rhea.

                Rhea is an Adept for Forces and Matter. She creates a bubble of altered reality around herself. Fire crossing the barrier is suppressed. Metal crossing the barrier is turned to gas. Is the problem solved? No, not quite. While the fire elemental hisses and withdraws, the golem works by non-material rules, and turning gaseous simply means that rather than bludgeon her to death, it's going to try forcing its way down her throat and suffocating her.

                She could probably deal with this, given a few seconds. She doesn't get a chance. Spooky's urge is fear. As she starts coughing, he embraces it. He comes up behind her and clamps a hand over her mouth, pinches her nose shut. To protect her from the thing trying to get inside, you see. Whispers in her ear that it'll be easier if she doesn't struggle.

                Rhea is a young woman, entirely human. She panics. She's also a Master of Prime. The area explodes in Supernal Flame. Spooky and the Golemn take 6 Aggravated damage. So does Cano, who happens to be a bit too close. Everyone screams, and Spooky goes into frenzy. He's not dead, but either he or Rhea will be next round.

                Skeleton Key releases one of his hung spells. Rewind.


                Skeleton Key, Chibinagi, Rhea, Spooky, Garrett and Cano enter the warehouse. It's dark, so while the others discuss the best way to get darkvision, Rhea goes over and turns on the lights. It's the sort of person she is. The place is full of construction machinery, some of which looks like it's been in a fire.

                Exploring further results in... wait, we've been here before. Cano drops to the floor screaming, covered in horrible burns. Rhea looks around, eyes widening in horror, and collapses in a sobbing mess. Skeleton Key grabs a fire extinguisher and sprays it full in the face of the emerging fire elemental, stunning it. Garrett grabs the women and runs, dragging Rhea a bit until she finds her feet and stumbles after him.

                Spooky and Chibinagi look at the sudden chaos in confusion. They weren't included. They have no idea what might have just happened, and follow the rest of us outside. Garrett heals Cano of her horrific wounds, while Rhea cries on the ground.

                The physical damage is quickly remedied with Life magic. The psychological is not so fleeting.


                Rhea could have thrown more damage with her spell than she did, but... well... I, as a player, didn't actually want to kill another PC, nor end her story with Cano.

                                    So, brief recap of the situation as a whole.
                                    A) The cabal knows very little about Rhea. They don't know where she comes from, what she wants, the limits of her power... nothing. She's an enigma wrapped in a mystery wrapped in a small Hispanic woman.
                                    B) She doesn't know her True name, and her mother was a Fetch.
                                    C) She has a rival Mathias, who recently kidnapped her. She hasn't told anyone what happened, but it clearly shook her.
                                    D) She has a fire spirit following her around, who's bound to help her uncover her True Name.

                                    With that laid out, there's only one way to move forward - outside help. Rhea reaches out to one of their Mage friends, Vagabond, who introduced her to the cabal and has generally been nice to her. In return for promising not to do anything rash for a few days, Vagabond helped her find a Mind mage to guide her through dealing with some of this stuff.

                                    They make their way to an office building, step through some wards designed to keep Sleepers away, and introduce themselves to Witch Doctor. After an adorable exchange involving macaroni and cheese, Rhea eating it out of the pot, and the classy, slightly snooty Cano looking on with horror, Witch Doctor explains some basics about the Onieros. None of the cabal - currently present Rhea, Garrett, Cano and Skeleton Key - know anything about it. As a favor to Vagabond, he'll accompany the cabal into her mind. We all lay down in on pillows and take sleeping pills.


                                    The first layer of Rhea's mind is her conscious desires and impulses. It's an orderly library, suffused with her nimbus, a gentle golden light forming into letters and annotations on the surroundings, labelling the shelves and the books on them. Witch Doctor is just a little creeped out. Her mind is way too orderly for his taste.

                                    It's at this point that Rhea's spirit-buddy, unnamed, showed up. He's a fire/truth spirit, who's purpose is to help her discover her real name. It mostly does this by setting fire to anything it finds that's a lie related to her past/nature/identity. Now at this point, she's slightly nervous. This spirit has been helpful in the past, but also fairly traumatic. Setting fire to lies is all well and good, but when one of those lies is an illusion of your mother, well... Rhea came here because possibly her entire life has been a lie. What's even going to be left of her memories after it's done? Flame spirit looks around and darts off. Oh shit. The cabal + Witch Doctor give chase.

                                    We find a secret passage out from the library into the next layer, her Virtue. Courageous. In her best moments, Rhea steps up to impossible challenges, or, despite the fear, unveils her full power. This is represented by her workshop - filled with all sorts of machinery and tools, plus various objects representing times and people embodying said virtue. We wander around a bit, finally ending up in front of a rug she brought home from Japan.

                                    Remember the High Priestess who summoned the flame spirit for her? The priestess died shortly thereafter, and the killers salted the earth, destroyed her hollow, killed everyone in the area, and desecrated her corpse for reagents. After all that, Rhea returned to the site of the devastation and undid most of their work, or at least as much as she could. It was a powerful scene, emotionally and magically, as she lifted the curse on the land, laid the priestess to rest and generally revealed her real strength to the rest of her cabal for the first time.

                                    Anyway, back to the Onieros. We all enter the tapestry/memory, and see the valley where all this occurred regrowing. Peaceful landscape, plants, even a few animals, and... a little girl, who waves to us. Rhea has been hoping to meet the High Priestess again, but, well, she'll take the little girl instead. It's the ghost of someone involved in Spooky's backstory and plot (which I won't detail here). Suffice it to say, they had a nice conversation and Rhea says she'll try to return to Japan and help said ghost if she ever gets a chance. The little girl gives Rhea a piece of gum (never turn down an NPC quest item) and points to the sky - a shooting star. Her flame spirit had been through here, and apparently decided nothing here was a lie worth burning.


                                    With a glance at Witch Doctor for confirmation of what they should be doing, they give chase again. Straight up. Reverse Gravity on the group. Upwards we fall, air rushing past. It gets colder - Rhea's very scientifically minded, and as mentioned, way too organized in her head, so they get accurate physics simulations even in her dream realm. Eventually we fall up into a cloud. Most of us manage to stick together, but Skeleton Key gets separated.

                                    He hits the face of the moon. Crack. Unhurt, he stands up, just in time for the surface to crumble away beneath him. He's in the Realm of Rhea's vice - Deceptive. Beneath the surface of the moon is a military-looking complex, an underground bunker, with every door locked, every corner watched, every section cut off from the rest. Each door is labelled with a series of numbers carved into the concrete, which he can't decipher. He wanders a bit, then breaks into one of the locked rooms.
