    Secret identities and true names

    02-09-2016, 03:30 AM
    How do you separate "sleeper" and mage? I know mages can make up new names, but what about going to meet with your cabal mates and order? Given today's tech, and a mage's abilities, I find it hard to believe that one could keep their identities separate.

    Mages wouldn't have to use the info to hurt you, they could sell/trade it for resources and services. I also find it hard to believe that many mages would dress elaborately and have almost serial killer-like movement patterns for when and where they practice magic or interact with other mages (aka not doing it too close to home).

    A Forces mage could just see if you have a phone, and unless you are very paranoid, or taken an identity seminar ingame, they could probably know you instantly. Track records, addresses, plot a web of your contacts, scan them for info and home in on you. This isnt even taking into consideration the wealth of things information gatherers could do with the other 9 arcana, especially conjunctionally.

    Is it easy for your name to end up in the wrong hands in mage society? Does it usually take beginner mages a while to build solid defensive behaviors and practices? I know this is a game about the unknown and the paranoia of consequence, but I sometimes struggle to envision how information flows in this world.

    It seems to me that, logically, it would be very hard to keep your true name a secret unless you dedicated every aspect of yourself to it. You would need to be the kind of person that can throw away an identity and lifestyle easily. Otherwise, you need to work defense and damage control with arcana to constantly fudge your sympathy and screw around with your identity and how others perceive you. Finally, I think some people don't care if their true name is known. They either know too many important people that anything done to them would be brought into the spotlight. Additionally, they set out to know plenty of names and be known by many, so they become part of a network that almost functions like a cold war state. Nobody wants to shoot first.

    This still brings to mind Seers and other enemies who would either destroy you or ceaselessly tempt you with this info, but I would assume info like this isn't readily available to these enemies without due effort.

    Anyway, am I on the right track? Do you guys have clever methods of keeping your name hidden while still being physically present in the mage community and leading your normal life?

                              Originally posted by kryptonitekrusher View Post
                              have almost serial killer-like movement patterns for when and where they practice magic or interact with other mages (aka not doing it too close to home).
                              Actually, the overwhelming majority of serial killers are actually highly territorial and do kill fairly close to their home. They tend to be local rather than mobile; in fact, most have jobs that provide access to potential victims. Opportunity is everything to a serial killer.



                              Okay, now on to the actual discussion.


                              First, I'd like to point out that sympathetic names, IIRC, are only bad (magically) if someone uses sympathetic magic (ie Space) to target you. There are spells and techniques to deal with someone targetting you with Space. At least there was in 1e, and I'm assuming a number of them are going to exist in 2e. Other uses of a sympathetic name could mean targetting your mortal family and friends.

                              Much like Demon, Mage is a game that evokes Cold War-era paranoia. Much like the Cold War, however, there tends to be a certain polite code of conduct. The three factions of Awakened society (Assembly, Pyramid, Diamond) don't want to go to actual war, because, much like the threat of nuclear winter, that's a situation where Mutually Assured Destruction comes into play. No one wants a mage war. Very few want to escalate to harming on someone else's family, because then the same favor is payed back.

                              So, why use Shadow Names and fake IDs at all, then? Because its always best to just remove as much temptation as possible. Its like going jogging at night in the park. Most people realize that doing that is putting yourself at risk of being attacked. Why present yourself as an easy target? Sure, if someone REALLY wanted to, that mage could find out all your names, sympathetic, fake, shadow, etc. That's not the point. The point is to make yourself less of an obvious target with a smaller window of opportunity. Casually using your sympathetic name is like waving a red flag or painting a bull's eye target on your back. Most criminals and villains are opportunistic. If they think that you're leaving them an opportunity, they'll take it. If they have to put in work, chances are they'll simply pass you by in favor of following their personal Obsessions instead. Why go around killing each other when there's so many juicy mysteries running around to study and gain power from?
