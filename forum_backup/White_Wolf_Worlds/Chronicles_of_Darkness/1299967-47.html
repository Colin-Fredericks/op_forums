                                        Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                                        As far as I can tell, the only game systems associated with Sekhem, Heka, or Dedwen is that Sekhem is Mummy's power stat; and I'm loathe to have anything less than an Imperial Arcanum mess with a power stat. Heka and Dedwen inform us about how the Arisen see the world; but they don't have any game systems (that I know of) associated with them, so I don't know how you'd go about bringing Mage's game systems to bear on them directly. I could see, for instance, a mage using Prime to manipulate that thing that mummies call “dedwen”; though in Mage terms, it would be a matter of manipulating the abstract concept of “value” or “utility” — which is very much not the typical view of Prime and might need an Attainment to do. Or I could see a mage using Space to manipulate the mystical connections that the Arisen call “heka”.
                                        You are correct in that Mummy does not have many game systems related to Heka, Dedwen and free Sekhem (as in, not the power stat of a Mummy), and that much of their treatment is more philosophical. According to alchemists, it is possible to transmute Dedwen into Sekhem, but that is more of a view as to how Sekhem comes to be. Heka is related to the flow of Sekhem in the world, specifically Geomancy, Ley Lines (Sekhem flows) and the flow of life energy within the body (which sounds like the meridians from Qigong). This also wouldn't necessarily need rules, as long as you would have rules for Sekhem itself.

                                        In addition to Sekhem as a power stat, Mummy treats Sekhem as a property of objects and the world around us, that flows and can be focussed in objects or corpses to create Vessels or Amkhata. The main description from the text is "Sekhem is the mystical energy emanating from all living things, allowing for the free flow of feelings and recollection in the universe. Revealing itself through passionate moments and intense memories, it subtly alters the environment as it coalesces. It brightens the light during a joyful birth. It thickens the air after a painful argument." Also, according to the text about vessels it causes curses when concentrated.

                                        This sounds like something Mages should be able to interact with, possibly with some combination of Prime, Time and Fate, as Sekhem carries Resonance, history and curses.
