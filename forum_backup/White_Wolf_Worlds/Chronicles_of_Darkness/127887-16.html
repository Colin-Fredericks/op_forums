                ArcaneArts:
                "Har kal numu — har mu annakal nu.": The world is not beautiful, therefore it is. (lit. "World be not-beautiful — world beautiful from being not.")


                Seraph Kitty:
                nusu-hith: A spirit without form or shape; the term is sometimes applied to spirits who are unable to form outside of the Shadow. Plural is "nusu-hithim".
                Visktha-Su: An older name for the Idigam, it means "The Dream-Bodied".


                Satchel:
                ihifara: scout
                thima-luhal: genius (lit. "master of thoughts")


                Dessembrae:
                "Don't even bother killing him, he isn't worth the effort.": Nu thala-nirihdu anna nu usdu numunul; nu kal agthu-nihsam. (lit. "No problem-fearing from not killing this male; [he] be not effort-worth.


                And a bonus, Ephsy's signature:
                "Curiosity killed the Mage.": Namzu-desasu kal Umma-us. (lit. "Wisdom-lust be Mage-death.")


                thala-nirih: worry (lit. "problem-fear")
                agthu: effort, action
                nihsam: worth, value
                namzu-desasu: curiosity (lit. "wisdom-lust")
                annakal: because; to be from being
