    The Doctor As An Unchained + Cipher

    08-10-2015, 02:50 AM
    Virtue: Brave/Creative
    Vice: Fickle

    Incarnation: Psychopomp
    Agenda: Inquisitor/Tempter
    Catalyst: Freedom Of Movement

    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 6, Wits 4, Resolve 5
    Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 4, Stamina 4
    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 5, Composure 4

    Mental Skills: Academics (History) 4, Crafts (Machines) 6, Computer 4, Medicine 3, Occult 4, Politics 2, Investigation 4, Science (Time Travel) 6
    Physical Skills: Athletics (Running) 3, Brawl 1, Drive (TARDIS) 3, Firearms 2, Larceny (Sonic Screwdriver) 3, Stealth 2, Survival 2, Weaponry (Swords) 3
    Social Skills: Animal Ken 2, Empathy 3, Expression (Humor) 4, Intimidation (Grandiose Threats) 3, Persuasion (Leadership, Diplomacy) 4, Socialize 2, Streetwise 2, Subterfuge (Technobabble, Bluff) 3

    Merits: Area Of Expertise (Time Travel, Sonic Screwdriver), Common Sense, Danger Sense, Encyclopedic Knowledge (Academics, Crafts, Science), Eye For The Strange, Fast Reflexes 2, Good Time Management, Indomitable (Epic), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Time Travel, Sonic Screwdriver), Investigative Aide (Academics, Crafts, Science), Patient, Professional Training 5 (Time Lord: Science, Expression, Persuasion), Tolerance For Biology, Trained Observer 3, Virtuous (Advanced 3), Fleet Of Foot 3, Hardy 3, Greyhound, Iron Stamina 3, Parkour 4, Quick Draw (Sonic Screwdriver), Seizing The Edge, Sleight Of Hand, Closed Book 3, Contacts (Inquisitors, Tempters), Fast-Talking 4, Inspiring (Epic), Iron Will, Pusher, Sympathetic, Table Turner, True Friend (Companions), Untouchable, Untouchable Style 4, Advance Form, Consummate Professional (Inquisitor, Tempter), Gadget (Sonic Screwdriver, Psychic Paper), Living The Lie, Multiple Agendas, Resonance Sensitive, Resonance Aware 3, TARDIS*, Terrible Form 4, Versatile Transformation

    Sonic Screwdriver's Embeds (Lambda): Open Sesame, Right Tools Right Job, Like I Built It and Cause And Effect.

    Psychic Paper's Embed (Precision Engineered): Authorized.

    *The TARDIS is considered to be a Lare (see Demon's ST Guide Page 79) of Rank 5 with a special Incept, Influence or Numen that allows it to travel through space-time. It cannot travel to alternate planes of existence (use Rip The Gates for that). Its other traits are up to the ST.

    Embeds

    Vocal: Across A Crowded Room, Animal Communication, Borrowed Expertise, Eavesdrop, Everybody Knows, Imagine, Muse, Remote Link-Up, Voice Of The Machine

    Instrumental: Data Retrieval, Efficiency, Functional Identity, Like I Built It, Open Sesame, Right Tools Right Job, Shift Consequence, Synthesis, Turn Blade

    Cacophony: Bystander Effect, Cause And Effect, Cool Heads Prevail, Hesitation, Just Bruised, On The Mend, Raw Materials, Shatter, Special Someone

    Mundane: Associate And Integrate, Authorized, Deep Cover, Going Native, Homogeneous Memory, In My Pocket, Interference, Never Here, Occam's Razor, Persistent Legend, Quick Change, Without A Trace

    Exploits

    Allies Into Gold, Context Matters, Deep Pockets, Force Relationship, Four Minutes Ago, Hellhounds*, Inflict Stigmata, Rip The Gates, Stop

    *Uses Crafts instead of Animal Ken, since the Doctor builds his Cryptids from scratch as machines rather than transforming animals.

    Demonic Form

    Modifications: Blade Hand, Component Indicators, Sense The Angelic, Tough As Stone

    Technologies: Aura Sight, Environmental Resistance, Mind Reading, Multiversal Antennae

    Propulsions: Long Limbs, Plasma Drive

    Processes: Amorphous, Memory Theft, Nanobot Composition

    Defense: 7
    Initiative: 10
    Speed: 15
    Willpower: 9
    Health: 9
    Primum: 7
    Glitches: The Doctor possesses two hearts that beat at an average of 170 times per minute, which is much faster than the 60 to 100 beats per minute of a human. This may be noticed through x-rays, stethoscopes or just by hand. Supernaturals with keen senses, such as vampires and werewolves, do not notice this from a distance like they normally would. This Glitch is a Major Brand.

    Cipher

    Wibbly Wobbly, Timey Wimey

    Not even the Doctor knows every single quantum branch and cosmic permutation of all timelines, but he’s been through enough to know the most important patterns to look out for in case he needs to make emergency alterations.

    Prerequisites: Cool Heads Prevail and Synthesis

    Dice Pool: Wits + Academics + History Specialty + Time Travel Specialty With Area Of Expertise

    Cost: 1 Aether

    Dramatic Failure: The Doctor suffers a -3 penalty to all Mental rolls for the rest of the scene from the overload of information from all the timelines coursing through his mind.

    Failure: No effect. The Doctor may try again, suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt.

    Success: The Doctor gains the Informed Condition, which he may apply to any roll that benefits from his Time Travel Specialty.

    Exceptional Success: The Doctor figures out how to piece it all together in the most efficient manner possible. He may designate a number of rolls equal to Primum to receive the Rote Quality, as long as they would benefit from his Time Travel Specialty. If any of these rolls would already have Rote Quality, he gains the Steadfast Condition instead.

    Reverse The Polarity Of Neutron Flow

    The Doctor possesses such vast knowledge of occult physics that it often sounds like gibberish to most, but he use that knowledge to practical effect at a moment’s notice, especially when lives are at stake.

    Prerequisites: Synthesis and Cause And Effect

    Dice Pool: Intelligence + Science + Time Travel Specialty With Area Of Expertise

    Cost: 1 Aether

    Dramatic Failure: The Doctor Resolves the Informed Condition, but his Plan suffers from the Volatile Condition and offers half of its normal bonus round down. The Gadgets also suffer from the Volatile Condition.

    Failure: No effect. The Doctor may try again, suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt.

    Success: The Doctor can Resolve the Informed Condition to craft a Plan as an instant action. Add his Primum to the Wits + Composure dice pool for creating this Plan, and add the benefit of Resolving the Informed Condition to the roll. Alternatively, he may Resolve the Informed Condition to create a number of Gadgets up to Primum, which last for the scene.

    Exceptional Success: The Doctor prepares an additional number of Plans equal to his Primum/2 round up, and may select one Gadget to last for a full chapter.

    Time Lord Victorious

    The Doctor may hesitate to use the full extent of his power for fear of the damage it may cause, but if the need is dire, he can put that hesitation aside and work both wonders and terrors.

    Prerequisites: Cause And Effect and Shift Consequence

    Dice Pool: Manipulation + Occult + Time Travel Specialty With Area Of Expertise - Composure

    Cost: X Aether + 1 Willpower

    Dramatic Failure: The Doctor must roll for Compromise at a -4 penalty.

    Failure: No effect. The Doctor may try again, suffering a cumulative -1 for each new attempt.

    Success: Once per chapter, by completing a Plan created with the previous Interlock, the Doctor may change a number up to Primum of targets of Conditions and/or Tilts affecting persons or locations encountered within the same Chapter. Each change costs 1 Aether.

    Exceptional Success: The Doctor gains the Steadfast Condition.

              I decided to change the fourth Key from Fractal Reality to Imagine. The former is more about repetition, while the latter is about innovation. Besides, giving a Cipher with only Cacophony Embeds to the Doctor sounded weird to me.

              In the show, the Sonic Screwdriver has done dozens of things, but the Gadget version is more limited. The Unchained Doctor has the Efficient Hardware Merit at 4 dots, which allowed him to make a Sonic Screwdriver with Right Tools Right Job, Open Sesame, Data Retrieval and Shatter. He got an Exceptional Success on the Installation, so those 4 Embeds can be more callibrated and precise.

              The Gadget version of the Psychic Paper only has Authorized, but the Doctor got an Exceptional Success on that Installation as well. That lets the Psychic Paper fool both people and scanning technology.

              EDIT: I found 3 very good images that give an accurate idea of what the Doctor's Demonic Form looks like. Here are the first, the second, and the third. The major difference is that his head is not featureless. It is still mechanical, but very human-like and expressive. Gallifreyan symbols appear on his hands. His eyes just barely hold back the roaring storm of maniacal possibility and fractal blueprints just behind them, like a Genius with very high Inspiration.

                              I added the Sympathetic, Pusher, Taste and Patient Merits. I also added the Machines Specialty to his Areas Of Expertise. I added the two hearts of Time Lords as one of the Doctor's permanent Glitches caused by high Primum. Added the Eavesdrop, Muse, Across A Crowded Room, Animal Communication and Turn Blade Embeds. I changed the Data Retrieval Embed inside the Sonic Screwdriver into Like I Built It, and altered several Demonic Form powers. Added the Allies Into Gold and Demon House Exploits, with the latter applying to the TARDIS.

                              As for K9, I'd say he is an intelligent Gadget, rather than a preexisting animal mutated through the Hellhound Exploit. The Doctor uses Embeds like Occam's Razor to make people think K9 is a hi-tech toy, science project and the like. K9 has been Embedded with Read Hostility, Right Tools Right Job, Remote Link-Up and Hesitation with the use of the Doctor's Efficient Hardware Merit. The first three Embeds allow him to sense incoming attacks to warn others, make the most of his built-in tools and establish remote communication with his friends, respectively. The last Embed fires laser beams, which can inflict the Stunned Tilt. Also, I like to imagine this version of K9 as looking like this, and having legs and retractable mini-arms for handling objects like a human. I suspect K9 didn't have legs in the show because of budget and special effects restrictions.
