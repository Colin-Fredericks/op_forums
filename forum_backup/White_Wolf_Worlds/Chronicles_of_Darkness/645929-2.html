        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
        Too many and it starts looking a bit like Promethean though.

        Actually that might make for a decent crossover.
        Easy solution is simply not to use Promethean's system then.

        Promethean's system is about a half formed person attempting to forge their own path into a world that hates them merely by existing and succeeds by eventually gathering enough life exp to become the person that they aim to be.

        Vampire's doesn't support such a system from the get-go. Vampire assumes that you've had a mortal (and provided you stick around long enough, Kindred) history and holding on to the past is the thing keeping you from running off into the deep end.

        If Promethean's system involves going forward (gaining Milestones and the like), I can see Vampire's system going backwards. Spending time looking up various past stories, documents of days long past, wrangling occult secrets from insane gurus. All in a effort to tie touchstones together.

        That being said, said person's lifespan is going to be short. Just because your human now doesn't mean that the Requiem is done with you. You lost all your "powers", lost your status as a Kindred in the dance of the dead, and now you have the attention of Kindred elders who would just love to have you as a trophy to hang in a closet for all their friends to gawk at.

        You may be done with the vamp life but the vamp life ain't done with you.

                            Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                            Too many and it starts looking a bit like Promethean though.

                            Actually that might make for a decent crossover.
                            Promethean, from the start, has been a glorified Golconda chronicle using a different supernatural as a base. There are several supernaturals out there that effectively have paths to becoming human, so, by your logic, they all become a bit like Promethean? Does that mean a highly social Mage game is like Vampire? Or a magic-focused CtL game is like Mage? Speaking of, the story and legend focus of CtL and BtP is worth noting. Overlap happens by necessity - otherwise, we would only have the Big Three as game lines.

                            Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                            Curious how would the ordo dracul feel about a cure if it was found? I think even those who thought the desire for one was folly, would have a scientific interest in the existence of such a thing.
                            It depends on the faction. The Sworn of the Ladder would be overjoyed. The Sworn of the Locus would be horrified. The Sworn of the Mysteries might ban it as athanama to the goals of the Ordo as a whole. The Sworn of the Dying Light would drool over studying it. The Sworn of the Axe would probably roll their eyes, think it dumb, and likely won't care unless it offers an opertunity to line their pockets through the Right of Dibs Dracula gave them.

                            Remember, the Ordo Dracul isn't some monotheistic entity like the Lancea et Sanctum. Its a disparate group of vampires with different approaches to Transcendence, and can't agree even on what that Transcendence is. Just "its something past vampirism." Not all of them are cultish, not all of them are scientists (actually, only one third are sciency, the rest are warriors and socialites).

                            Originally posted by Maina View Post
                            If I recall correctly, and has been mentioned earlier, the Coil of Ziva handles becoming more human. It succeeds, though not permanently, at the highest level. It's considered basically heresy. I think the Ordo would despise it. They want to remove the weaknesses of their condition, not the strengths. Becoming human again is the opposite of their goals. It's a devolution. Their goal is evolution. It's anathema to the Ordo Dracul. But that's my interpretation of the information we have thus far in 2e.
                            The Coil of Ziva is a mix of the Coil of the Ladder and Coil of the Locus mixed into one. So, yeah, its kind of like a two way street there.

                            I'd like to point out that its not so much opposite of their goals - remember, the second Bride agreed to join Dracula's order for the very reason of achieving humanity again. There's a reason why its a heresy - you don't bother to declare something a heresy because it doesn't happen. You make something a heresy because, somewhere, people frowned at it but it still happens anyways. There are lots of Ordo Dracul vamps who seek humanity through the Coils. Officially, its banned. Unofficially? One rule that the Ordo really takes seriously is the right of each member to decide their own path to Transcendence. Banning becoming human is like making suicide illegal. If you're successful, what are they going to do to you?
