                    My biggest hope is that they don't fuck up the theme.

                    Everygame after the big three has something, mechanical or thematic, that makes it a bit distinct from the big three.

                    Promeathean is a lot suckier than most splats, but you can get your happy ending, the rules for it are right in the book, for example. Vampire, Mage, and Werewolf don't have built in happy endings.

                    Geist's theme was Accepting Death and that the world is dark, but that there was genuinely good stuff too and that Life was worth celebrating. It is upbeat, it is happy. Likewise, being a Sin-Eater usually doesn't suck. The Sin-Eater Template has no major drawbacks. You were dead, now you're not and as an added bonus you have powerful psychic abilities and a head guest that has it in their best interests to keep you alive and happy. That's awesome.

                    Yeah, there are cases of a a particularly weak willed Sin-Eater being bullied into compliance by their geist, or particularly inhuman geists driving hosts insane, but that's always presented as an NPC. It's not the default, it's either an NPC or it's a Player opting into it with their PC because they think it will be fun or interesting. Also, yeah, if you died too many times you were either driven insane, became a meat puppet, or just stayed dead, but you're not starting there, you get there by screwing up a lot. The default assumption is that either the Sin-Eater is mostly in control or that both host and geist are equal partners.

                    Being a Sin-Eater does not suck. Please keep it that way, because that makes Geist distinct from both Wraith and from the rest of the CroD, because no inherent drawbacks Every other splat has at least 1(except Hunters, but they hardly count).

                                                            I think the biggest downside of being a Sin Eater is the Geist themselves; one of the sample adventures, Dem Bones, had the titular Geist repeat the same bar of a particular song over and over again, to the point his Sin Eater was using suicide just to get any sleep.
