                Do people really not understand Mummy and the French Revolution? Besides the obvious theme of the laboring class revolting against tyrannical overlords there's the fact that it's the only occidental Dark Era for Mummy.

You can argue Ruins of Empire but it's split between Egypt, Turkey, and Britain, with French territories only added from backer expansion. French Revolution, as trite as maybe some people think a European Dark Era is, is Mummy's first Era of such.
