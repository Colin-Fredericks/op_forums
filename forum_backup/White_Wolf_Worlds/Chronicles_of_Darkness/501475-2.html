                                                        Sleepy Hollow's vibe is a little... schlocky, but it's got tons of Hunter material, and at times some really extraordinary visuals. Also if Fox ever gets around to releasing the music as an album, I'll be using a bunch of it as neutral background music.

                                                        The Sinister movies do a really amazing job at showing just how alien ghosts can be, visually and behaviorally.

                                                        Gravity Falls is a fun example of a non-Splat-dominated WoD town with a weirdness-distilling agency. Also, the characters are generally very good people, but have some understated but serious disfunctional issues with each other that do a great job of showing that just because you're part of an (actually) loving family doesn't mean you can't exhibit seriously divisive and toxic behavior on the down-low. I've personally started playing with it as part of a setting, as a kind of supernatural demilitarized zone with a reputation for quietly and repeatedly chewing up and spitting out enough vampires, werewolves, and changelings that the only ones who go are assumed to be crazy or leveraged.
