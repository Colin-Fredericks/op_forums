                                        Honestly, I think these are terrible. How exactly is Invictus uniquely positioned to prevent the Acolytes from summoning Cthulu and turning the place into Hell on Earth or ensuring kindred society is not overthrown by a Dragon with a god-complex, a few scales and coils, and a couple of ideas on how to make the local kindred more useful to stroking his ego, or even keeping the Sanctified from marching out on a genocidal purge? Acolytes get to construct frankenstein monsters or summon monsters, Dragons get loads of additional psychic powers otherwise closed to vampires and resistances to disciplines, and Carthians (just like Sanctified and Acolyte spellcasters) can mystically deliver unavoidable and unresistable crippling strikes or death blows but the best thing you come up with for Invictus is the ability to skip eating for a few nights if they already have lots of willpower and vitae to blow already.

                                        I don't care for the oath system because much of it literally rewards oathbreakers and punishes individuals who act in good faith. This is only intensified when you flushed the few restrictions that were the only pretense anyone could claim Oaths are an Invictus signatory ability, as well as the only real deterrence against abuse, down the toilet.

                                        Oath of Abstinence
                                        Because Elders (Invictus and otherwise) don't already have multiple methods of circumventing the vinculum dangers attendant with consuming kindred blood. But don't even think of using your disciplines.

                                        Oath of the Hard Motherfucker
                                        That's a name I expect the Carthians to use in reference to this oath especially due to it's purpose. Why doesn't the Invictus use a name for it that sounds a tad bit more professional and not the ghetto fabulous pejorative currently being used?

                                        Oath of the Safe Word
                                        This strikes me as something that would be used as a punitive judgement for a minor offense.

                                        Oath of the Handshake Deal
                                        Can covenant status be offered as collateral? Because if so, this is the "I steal ur powrz" power like everyone else has gotten.

                                        Oath of the Refugee
                                        You have status equal to zero when you flee a city so you cancel out zero successes to any efforts to pursue or attack you. Also, why wouldn't the Sanctity of Merits apply when you lost the attendant city and covenant status?

                                        Oath of the Model Prisoner
                                        This one makes no sense to me. In my experience kindred taken prisoner (and if it's known they are kindred) are kept staked when they aren't being actively questioned or needed conscious for an experiment until or unless they are released. It's easier to hide or transport them when you can stuff them in a duffel bag or throw them in a shallow grave or freezer and not actually worry about killing them permanently. Keeping them in torpor also means (most of the time) the captive can't spend idle time plotting to use Protean, Obfuscate, Dominate, Majesty, Animalism, Nightmare etc. to overpower their captors and flee. Since this oath explicitly doesn't require a notary and does direct Aggravated or Lethal damage it has the greatest potential for abuse. But we'll pretend for a moment that vampires are not pragmatic and that they follow the Geneva Conventions. Creative applications can have this oath apply to being kidnapped or otherwise 'unjustly' detained. Even overthrow a prince. Captors can get around the restrictions by subcontracting interrogation or imprisonment to individuals or groups who are not covenant members.

                                        Oath of the True Knight
                                        Does this oath require you to end previously existing oaths and vinculums and forswear other covenant and city (and possibly clan) affiliations first, or does it dissolve them when the oath is taken. Everyone else so far has gotten the ability to become immune to vinculums or break the blood bond prematurely, so I see no particular reason why it wouldn't dissolve pre-existing obligations. But it's unclear on that point as well as whether or not its the knight or the one attempting to force a vinculum as the one who takes Aggravated damage.

                                        Invested
                                        As has been stated previously, another merit already exists with the same name. This is a cut and paste of the same merit every other covenant has gotten to give PCs a jump on status accumulation into a covenant they don't currently belong to formally.
                                        I don't like these merits because if you have a non-hostile relationship with someone in a given covenant you should already be able to trade minor favors in the form of a single dot of a social merit already. If you're referring to the limited access to their covenant signature abilities like Coils and Cruac, well, Invictus doesn't have any signature abilities to share and they don't have any useful merits requiring more than one covenant status to obtain, so why bother?

                                        Courtoisie
                                        Considering some of the comments in this thread, it's obvious that it's not being taken seriously as a fighting style. Then again, not only does it use the same name as the bloodline signature discipline of the foppish Spina bloodline, it's a subpar fighting style currently. While the other covenants actually has abilities to improve their survivability or lethal ability in combat, the Invictus opts to becomes Spider-man, trading quips during a fight for actual lethality. The only really useful ability is Establish the Duel and that's only IF it works automatically even if another character is attempting to sneak up on you to stab you in the back using Obfuscate and breaks the discipline's effects. It's not even particularly useful against multiple attackers because switching to the more dangerous foe or not persisting in beating on someone who's fleeing, torpid, or dead, ends the benefit.

                                        Crowdsourcing
                                        This looks like a variant of Carthian Pull and Attache applied to PCs instead of a Retainer. This is sort of something I could see a Commissioner in Invictus having but since they officially don't have any titles beyond Notary, and that was rendered obsolete with the proposal regarding oaths here. Why should anyone bother with more than one dot? Why do you need Academics, it has nothing to do with gaining or applying a formal education? Unless Carthian Pull is hard capped at 5, it's superior to Crowdsourcing.

                                        Moderator
                                        Another one where nobody should actually bother with more than one dot. Someone with 3 ranks in Computers should already be able to use the Internet as a 1 dot Library, Allies, or Contacts with limited value and functionality.

                                        Information Network
                                        Unless the specialty is interdisciplinary or considered an area of expertise, this merit isn't worth purchasing.

                                        Prestigious Sire
                                        Another merit mash up. Mentor with rank 1 of Dynasty Membership. A 4 dot Mentor can already use his (presumably) superior social skills and status to speak on your behalf. If your social interaction ability is so questionable, you're better off investing in that as well as Dynasty Membership and a rank or two in Etiquette rather than this merit that only lets you situationally leech off another character's status ratings.

                                        Tech Savvy
                                        While there is an actual mechanical purpose to the tiers here, it's unnecessary. You can't jury rig something together that's functional with all those prerequisites already? And since when could you no longer obtain equipment with improved Durability, functionality etc. than the default versions if you had the Resources score? Is this merit supposed to enable Invictus members to obtain equipment type Hunter Endowments?

                                        Noblesse Oblige
                                        This merit may not be advisable for someone who is upwardly mobile since they would likely lose their official position for another one if they got promoted. But I can also see it as a mechanic who's purpose is to actually kill any desire for upward mobility.

                                        Social Engineering
                                        Interesting idea, kind of lackluster in some of the effects for a four dot merit with some extensive prerequisites. There are no examples for using social engineering to prop someone up (in other words, moving a mortal associate up within an organization to where he's a more useful retainer or ally, or even a kindred protege.)

                                        A +1 bonus to a Social roll against the victim, or -1 on one of their Social rolls. Another character can access the bonus or take advantage of the penalty if the information is shared.

                                        A mere +1 bonus to a single Social roll against the target? I can get more using the Willpower point straightforward to apply to that roll.

                                        A damning piece of information that, when used, removes a dot from the character’s Allies, Contacts, Feeding Grounds, Herd, or Status. The Merit is eligible for Sanctity of Merits.

                                        If they're capable if instantly buying the lost dot back with a convincing display of crocodile tears or application of a discipline (that probably helped them get said merit in the first place.) why bother? Even blowing a Prestigious Sire roll has restrictions on when you can buy it back.

                                                Noblesse Oblige
                                                This merit may not be advisable for someone who is upwardly mobile since they would likely lose their official position for another one if they got promoted. But I can also see it as a mechanic who's purpose is to actually kill any desire for upward mobility.

                                                Social Engineering
                                                Interesting idea, kind of lackluster in some of the effects for a four dot merit with some extensive prerequisites. There are no examples for using social engineering to prop someone up (in other words, moving a mortal associate up within an organization to where he's a more useful retainer or ally, or even a kindred protege.)
                                                A ten minute window of free access to someone's Haven? How is that not HUGE?

You can't instantly buy something back with Sanctity of Merits - you kinda do need to justify the new purchase, after all. The idea is that you don't lose experience points for something that can be destroyed, not that your merits can't be destroyed.

                                                The +1 bonus is designed to be shared with everyone as possible. That means that the entire city is getting an edge at heckling the target. Its blood in the water, causing sharks to circle.
