                                      Sure, it's pretty simple.

                                      Roughly twenty percent of human structures have some sort of recognizably 'God Machine' infrastructure I've proposed, which implies a kind of weird nWoD which we're comfortable with. Only a quarter of these are 'active'. Active does not imply they are inhabited - they may be being used for storage, or simply currently empty. A quarter of the active infrastructure is in current use.

                                      So, 20% of human structures house some sort of infrastructure. ( IE : A broken down factory with huge water damage, broken windows, and rats )
                                      Of that, 25% is 'active' - considered to be a current, usable resource, rather than abandoned. ( IE : An empty garage, a safehouse with nobody in it )
                                      Of that, 25% is being used RIGHT NOW - implying personnel, additional security, and story seeds. ( IE : People. Possibly angels. Who knows? )

                                      We like to use this in tandem with Google Earth. So, when we're trying to determine where infrastructure is on our street we can zoom in with the nice 3d buildings and mark them. We just point to a building and roll a few percentiles.

                                                          Originally posted by Phaolan View Post
                                                          Yeah, but...
                                                          The following answers are not canonical, but I have used them functionally, so maybe they'll be useful to you!

                                                          Does Infrastructure specifically stand out to the Fallen?

                                                          Yes. Fallen can detect any infrastructure at a glance. However, because nearly anything you can imagine is in some way infrastructure, the practical utility of this ability is not particularly important.

                                                          Or do they need to actively look for it?

                                                          No. The ability to see it is passive, like another spectrum of light.

                                                          Will Infrastructure always be clearly visible to the Demons wherever it is?

                                                          No. Demons must 'see' something to identify it as Infrastructure UNLESS it is being used in some way ; active use / activation can cause 'vibrations' and 'signals' which can carry through solid objects and over long distances on a case by case basis. The bigger the matrix, usually, the more profound the disturbance.

                                                          Is it possible that they're ever mistaken about seeing a piece of Infrastructure?

                                                          No. You might suggest that some device could mimic infrastructure as a God Machine trap, but that device itself would by definition be infrastructure oddly enough so it's not really a trick.

                                                          Would it work like how an artist always sees the architectural elements of buildings downtown?

                                                          Not exactly ; it's more just another 'sense', another organ along with eyes and nose.

                                                          Or is it more like seeing an English word in the neon signs lighting a Japanese city?

                                                          Not sure what's meant here, sorry!

                                                          Would a mundane wall or slab of concrete suffice to hide Infrastructure from the eyes of a Fallen Angel of the God-Machine?

                                                          Yes, unless the Infrastructure was active. If it was active, it might produce waste heat (Aether), vibrations or signals which might be noticed. The ease with which one might notice these things is dependent on the context of the infrastructure (and matrix) in question.

                                                          The idea of my players' character wandering around the city and just pointing out Infrastructure the way I point out faux-Victorian columns doesn't bode well for me keeping much mystery...

                                                          Understandable. Infrastructure is built and maintained by humans as well as the God Machine, so you're not going to bother to describe most of it as it would be reduntant. Most of it remains unused in potentia until the God Machine stirs and makes use of it in some project or another, like an orchard with many unpicked peaches. The modern world is a garden of plenty for the industrious matrix-monster.

                                                          That said, Players will end up paying much more notice to active infrastructure, which is when infrastructure being used to currently produce a matrix of some sort. Because demons can intuitively sense this 'charge' and how it 'builds' (depending on the unique context of the situation), this is how infrastructure sense is useful in a practical way. Identifying a door is infrastructure isn't particularly useful because -every- door is infrastructure. However, identifying that it is slowly charging up and at 12:05 is going to discharge something... which implies the God Machine is using it in some way... that's going to merit significantly more attention.
