    Given that Embeds are physical laws that exist in nature, can anyone other than Demons make use of them? Can a Demon teach a human (or other supernatural being) how to use an Embed? If not, why?

    I mean, if a Demon tried to teach a human a given Embed, which would it be like?
    a) Teaching a child to ride a bike, where, after some some practice, handholding, and falls, the human would be able to pull it off pretty well?
    b) Trying to explain how to ride a bike over the phone, in Russian, to someone who only speaks English, so it's just a bunch of meaningless noises?
    c) Trying to teach a dolphin to ride a bike? I.e. regardless of how intelligent and capable the dolphin may or may not be at understanding your explaination of how riding a bike works, they're simply not physically capable of manipulating the bike in the first place, so the attempt is doomed to fail.
    d) Trying to teach a snail to ride a bike, where the whole idea is just pointless?

My solution will probably be to allow the demon to keep the description, but lose the associated mechanical benefits until they buy the appropriate merit, or else let the player go into XP debt for it.

                                        i would imagine that the appearance wouldn't change, but the mechanical benefits wouldn't transfer. a man who wanted to be huge would still be huge, but the hollowed out soul doesn't provide extra health. the person is still beautiful, but they can't carry the body or attitude to make them Striking enough for bonuses. you may have a great job, but you can't yet access the wealth. the "code" that determines what capabilities the mortal had (ie his merits) was destroyed with the soul (or, it WAS the soul), and the demon poured his own codes inside. they don't always connect logically (ie why a giant guy doesnt get the merit benefits of giant), but thats part of the strange horror of demons living in a world that is basically the Matrix--the god machine's rules control the world, and hacking said world doesn't always work completely or even logically.

                                                    External Merits are tied to Cover. The idea that they will just go away without Legend is idiotic. When you are a multimillionaire, it's going to take a lot more to prevent you from accessing your money than a missing PIN (which you should know with a Soul Pact because you BECOME them; otherwise the Cover is useless as you won't be able to maintain it at all). The drawback is that Cover is fragile, subject to being blown, traded, lost or burned. Yeah, I'm rich and influential...until I have to burn my Cover. Then it all goes away in noise and fire.

                                                    There is no good in game reason for it. So hang it. You make someone a millionaire and take their soul, which they have to agree to by the way, then yes, you are now rich, until your Cover fails apart.

                                                    Legend is for things like Fighting Style Merits.
