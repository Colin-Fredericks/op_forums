        Sin-eaters have human souls, which beasts don't seem to have, werewolves have this synergistic hybrid vigor thing going on, which beasts, with their completely human bodies, don't really share.

        So I was thinking more along the lines of where the advantage comes in.
        ,
        Beasts are smarter than goetia, but that leads to 'Why does the Dark Mother want intelligent children?" While I quite value intelligence, the loss doesn't really seem to hamper ephemeral beings like goetia.

        Another advantage is the power trait, lair which goes up to 10, which can lead to goetia with rankings over 5. So perhaps like the green sun princes of exalted, beasts are embryonic gods, the next generation of dark mothers and fathers.

        Of course that leads to the question, what are all these dark gods of fear and hunger up to, surely beasts must have resulted in some unfettered of rank above 5 by now?
        Human forms do grant a modicum of social camouflage, even if that's hampered by certain Atavisms (Alien Allure, and Looming Presence in particular). Granted the Forsaken have more than a few tricks that have more finesse when dealing with mundane humans, Beasts aren't subject to having people immediately lose their shit when they utilize the more monstrous aspects of their power set.

        As to your question, even if one of the Begotten held on in the face of despair that long, odds are that they'd necessarily spend more time haunting the Anima Mundi and Temenos than any other Realm of existence, as those are easier to access and don't require a link to the Primordial Dream to support the Beast Soul's presence. Generally speaking, they'd stay where the food is abundant, competition is minor, and enemies are few to nonexistent.
