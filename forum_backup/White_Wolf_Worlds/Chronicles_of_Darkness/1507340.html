    Neither Left Behind Nor Forgotten-What Do You Want from Kinship?

    04-03-2023, 08:57 PM
    Yeah, slot this in with the Dreaming thread I still need to get around to, but in some ways, I should probably knock out a thread on my thoughts on expanding Kinship given that's a lot more set. But anyways, Kinship is generally regarded as one of the better parts of Beast, where it's most arguable heart is thematically at and is generally mechanically well supported, but I think it'd be fair to suggest that there's way for the mechanics to be improved upon or otherwise expanded, so to kick some gears into motion, let's have it as a chat-

    What do you like about what Kinship already does, and what more do you think it could do or would like to see it explore?

          I like Kinship's concept overall, it makes sense that a Temenos/Anima Mundi Astral Entity of "Monster" that has a connection to the greater symbol of "monster" also has a connection to things thought of as monsters. I do have some nitpicks of the mechanics though.

          Classifications: No complaints here. I speculate Demon's exclusion is due to the Astral not actually having the specifics of the Unchained at all. Maybe it's because the God Machine covered its tracks too well, and Demons are included for securities sake.

          Thicker than Water: I'm not a fan of Thicker than Water giving a good impression by default. It might be my dislike of auto-successes biasing me, but I feel that while the familiarity of Kinship may soothe things, nothing should be taken for granted when social maneuvering with other supernatural creatures. I can easily see say a vampire initially regarding a Beast as potential competition, rather than family, from the familiarity that would supposedly help smooth things over. I guess I'd have the first impression have a bonus or penalty based on context, and afterward have it as a smaller general bonus to impression. I do like the bit where it helps with feral monsters, that make sense to me.

          Family Resemblance:​ Mostly, I would just like a clarification that a Beast can't spot anyone unless they are specifically looking for supernatural qualities in people. The text (which I may be misreading) gives off the impression that they automatically can tell when someone is supernatural and then describe the roll as if it's an action they have to take instead.

          Mother's Kiss: It's fine, I guess? I would prefer for it to be limited to someone with the Family Ties condition. I honestly can't say why, it just makes more sense to me that way. That said, it would be cool to see this concept explored more, with Kinship merits giving it more specific benefits towards a certain type of supernatural creature or something.

          Passing Resemblance: One of my favorites, actually. I like that the Horror is mutable, and I like that a Beast that hangs out with Vampires starts acting and looking like a Vampire.

          Family Dinner: The one I expect to be the most controversial bits of Kinship. My feelings on it are mixed. I like the dichotomy of this being the best way of feeding at High Satiety, and still being useful at low Satiety, but I feel like it very easily can make normal feeding feel irrelevant, even when it's a big enough part of Beast it acts as a classification for them. I think this really needs to be more emphasized as something Beasts do, right there with Normal feeding, but lowered in effectiveness somehow. I don't know, maybe it should be a roll with a set number of times a Beast can benefit from it over a period. Definitely, an area to explore more.

          Family Ties: Ok, this might be weird, but I'd like to see either being able to give this or an equivalent to mortals. Just hear me out: A Beast has plenty of mechanics Tying them to their Legend, but very little tying them to their Life. A Beast may be a Monster, but they also are a little bit Human, and that side of their Family is important too. I wouldn't have this grant any power to the Beast or whatever, instead, it's a protective ward against any who would harm the weaker of their family. I'd also have it be completely separate from Cults, though one could mix both if they wanted.​

              On a narrative level:

              I wouldn't mind something like how Lunars in Exalted 3e have lots of ways to get someone's heart-blood besides killing them that resonate with different themes (in that case, they're Attribute Exalted so they have an alternative for each Attribute.). Giving each Family (or Hunger) a unique way of establishing Kinship that biases them towards certain groups could give some interesting dynamics to thing. I could see going the other way where as a Beast's Legend grows, they discover ways to gain Kinship in ways that resonate with their Legend instead of something more splat-focused.

              On a mechanical level:

              I would love some ST-level advice on how far to take things like Kinship Merits. It doesn't have to have one set mode. A dial could be good too. But it can be hard for groups (esp. ones not super mechanically inclined or with deep knowledge of the other games) to know exactly what a Beast can learn from their bonds with other supernaturals. Can that directly copy splat-specific Merits or only get similar Merits that can overlap in function? Those sorts of questions don't necessarily have a single right answer, but it could use some guidelines since it's very "whatever the ST feels" at the moment.

                      I've said it elsewhere, but the big one is this: now that the way the Dream works is pinned down a little more, the Begotten's internal mythological framework for why other monsters resonate with them shouldn't be that they came from the Primordial Dream, it should be that they came from the Mother's Land.

                      The world-soul is sufficiently terra incognita that you can draw lines between the hungry cousins of the Children of the Dark Mother and the place she supposedly makes her Lair without having to speculate on why so few of them have any significant magical ties to human culture.

                              Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post
                              Was gonna write something, but then I realized that I forgot most of Kinship mechanics, so, uh…

                              Oh, did we have anything that works alone in a Beast context, other than just crossover hooks? Something like how Hunter 2e looks less at the other gamelines for their hunts.
                              I feel like I need to clarify on a point in the interest of the question: Beast (should) operate(s) on an assumption that there's at least always some other kind of monster at play in the world other than themselves, even if at a minimum you're talking Goetia, but more realistically at least Horrors from the Chronicles core Horror Creation System-The Beast can have Kinship and gain advantages from that Kinship (and offer advantages) with the Black-Eyed Kids as much as they can the Kindred. It does it pretty well so long as you have the tools to make your own monsters....with the obvious caveat that none of the Beast books have the Horror Creation System. This is an oversight that needs correction moving forward.

                              As a basic rundown of my thoughts for Kinship (mostly as it exists now, not going into ideas for Beast, Family, or Human Kinship):

                              1*) Family Ties should be a scaleable tiered Condition from 1 to Five, for making more meaningful differences between relationships of varying depths.

                              1.5*) and honestly, a set of paired conditions representing each side of the Kinship, one for the Beast and one for the Kin, to better represent the way feelings can differ and grow and make it work to build up one side and another, and also build into point 2.

                              2) Attach different advantages that a Beast can get from a relationship to those different tiers to again incentivize building those relationships, and also emphasize the way sympathy is also "contagious" of each other's nature for people in relationships to Beasts.

                              2.5) In particular, it also means the Kin can gain more advantages and become more like the Beast, possibly by getting access to Birthrights, Normal Effects of an Atavism/Nightmare, provide alternate means of mitigating their own Integrity threats in a way that still inflicts a more monstrous nature on them (akin to Inuring spells or trading breaking points for Banes in Requiem, but unique in it's predication on the relationship to the Beast), and access to Kinship Merits from other associated monsters, because I think a vampire and a changeling who are Kin with a Beast but never meet each other should still have the chance to look more alike than not. Being Kin with a Beast should allow them warp and become weirder.

                              3) In the spirit of Kinship being the actual Integrity-alike of Beast in a sort of operational space shared with the Conviction and Loyalty Touchstones of Renegades, and in support of the idea that you are the company you keep, maybe an adjacent Condition set that reflects whether you don't really have connection, if the majority of your connections are trying to maintain their humanity, or if the majority of them don't give a shit, and a soft presence effect for it.

                              *Yes, I'm ripping off Golden Sky Stories, why wouldn't I?

                                                  I gave your post a deserved upvote. It covers all fragmentary ideas I had to improve Kinship and goes well beyond them.

                                                  I do want to tackle an unmentioned matter. Based on what I saw on the Forums, people seem to be warmly predisposed to the idea of Kinship with Mortals. The homebrew Obcasus Rite that allows that is massively liked. I also think that it's a conceptual gold mine for the game, even if Kinships to Mortals and Monsters don't get equal metaphysical power. They could organically connect Beasts to their Cultists and human family and friends, which would underscore the "No Neat Boxes" principle. It could evoke the subject of monstrosity within humanity and humanity within monstrosity.

                                                  Even if by default Beasts consider Mortal Kinships impossible, there could be an in-universe way to bridge the gap, because having options is more fun than a hard "no" (the same could apply to the Unchained.). It could also be neat to consider if Kinships could be formed to the local vindictive tiger that villagers take pains not to upset, or the old catfish that snapped hundreds of fishing lines and swallowed dozens of pets whole. They're not monsters in the common understanding of the term, but they're the kinds of things that make their mark in the common consciousness.

                                                  That would likely require ironing out what allows for Kinship. Can it only be formed to beings that the bulk of humanity would consider monsters? Only beings approved by the Dark Mother? Beings who cause Integrity loss, leaving their mark on he Primordial Dream? Whichever is most thematically appropriate, and allows for most versimilitude.
