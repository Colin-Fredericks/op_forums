            From the extremes of a millionare with Resources 0 to a humble worker with Resources 5 there is a space that I see it has to be negotiated between narrator and players, basically to make things coherent and avoid abuse.

            As Narrator I want to give the players what they want to play, I want them happy, but I want some reciprocity and flexibility from them to adapt their characters a bit for the sake of the story (and for the sake of the mental sanity of the narrator).

            So, if a player wants a Millionare but invest no merit point in Resources, I will have him invest merit points in things coherent with the social status he wants to have. I also will make him understand that I will not allow abuses, like telling me that his character has 5 Ferraris and then in game selling one and asking me to give him 3 cash equipment of 5 each one, or sell his mansion to rise to a Resources 3 merit for free.

            Narrative flavour, yes. Rules cheating, nope.

                                                    I want to make sure I have all of this right, based on what I've read here:
                                                    -Characters can craft or acquire Equipment, and said Equipment provides its Equipment Bonus.
                                                    -Merits can be involved in buying or finding Equipment when appropriate. Resources is the go-to in most cases, although some Social Merits may be used in some cases as per Availability and Procurement (CofD 100).
                                                    -Generally, Characters can't create/purchase/find Equipment that confers a Merit, either permanently or temporarily. Any exceptions have to be explicit, such as using a Disguise to emulate one dot of a Social Merit.
                                                    -Merits with physical representations (such as Object Fetishism) generally don't require said physical representation to be paid for/found/etc. The Storyteller may say that the item should be crafted/bought/found to justify purchasing the Merit, but that's something that the rules don't require and that should be discussed within the group.
                                                    -Objects that are physically representative of a Merit are not Equipment; that is to say, they generally don't carry their own Equipment Bonus separate from the Merit. Any exceptions would have to be discussed in-group.
                                                    -Trading Dots from one Merit to another (e.g. Sacrificing Status to gain Resources or vice versa) is technically possible under Sanctity of Merits, but it requires ST acceptance. Sanctity of Merits is generally there to allow characters to change appropriately given proper circumstances, not to turn your Merit Dots into a floating pool you can re-allocate willy nilly. (Easy Come, Easy Go [HL 81] being the only in-rule exception I've found so far.)
