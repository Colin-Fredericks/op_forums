                                                        Can you folks please help me remember how to correctly apply the rules for legacy attainment dice pools? I'm getting back into Mage and having to reparse these rules.

                                                        I guess please just check and correct my examples; hopefully these are comprehensive.

                                                        Example 1: Telekinetic Strike (Forces) as a third-tier attainment requiring Forces 3
                                                        1. Assume the casting mage has Forces 4.
                                                        2. The free reach goes to instant casting.
                                                        3. Range
                                                          1. Casting the spell at touch range uses the normal Dexterity + Brawl or Weaponry; no special alternative roll just because it's a legacy attainment.
                                                          2. It's possible to activate the attainment as an Aimed Spell and the attack roll is the normal Gnosis + MAX(Athletics, Firearms) - Defense -- it doesn't have a special Aimed Spell roll just because it's a legacy attainment.
                                                          3. Whether the attainment must be cast via touch or aiming is not built into the definition of the attainment; the mage can choose which method to use at the time of casting.
                                                          4. Casting the spell automatically succeeds if the above rolls succeed.
                                                        4. Spell factors (Note of confusion: I can't remember if "additional spells factors" uses the mage's dots in the relevant arcanum or the attainment's prerequisite dots in the arcanum)
                                                          1. Duration is inapplicable.
                                                          2. The maximum possible effective Potency of the spell is 1+2+3 = 6.
                                                            1. One because the casting succeeds automatically (every successful spell gets one free Potency rating)
                                                            2. Two from "additional spell factors that would incur a penalty..." The mage has Forces 4. Each additional level of Potency applies a -2 penalty to the die roll.
                                                            3. Three from the the mage's Forces rating of 4, minus 1, since Potency is the primary spell factor.
                                                          3. Alternatively, the attainment could have Potency 4 and Scale of a large room by applying the permitted additional spell factors to Scale rather than Potency. Or it could have Potency 5 and Scale of a small room. These Scale factors only matter for Aimed Casting.
                                                          4. The allocation of additional spell factors is not part of the definition of the attainment and can be varied between castings according the mage's choice.
                                                        Example 2: Mental Shield (Mind) as a third-tier attainment requiring Mind 3
                                                        1. Assume the casting mage has Mind 3 and Gnosis 4.
                                                        2. Rather than instant casting, use the free reach for Advanced Duration. This spell takes one scene to cast.
                                                        3. Since this is a third-tier attainment mimicking a Mind 2 spell, it gets another reach. Say it's spent to make the Duration as Indefinite.
                                                        4. Touch/Aimed range
                                                        5. Spell factors
                                                          1. Potency is meaningless for this spell.
                                                          2. Duration is Indefinite via reach.
                                                          3. Scale can be 1 + 1 = 2 ranks to affect via Aimed Casting everyone within a small room, which could include the casting mage.
                                                            1. One Scale rank is a freebie for the (automatically) successful casting of the spell.
                                                            2. One additional spell rank that would incur a -2 penalty since casting mage has 3 dots of Mind.
                                                        6. Clash of Wills: Affected subjects get to roll 4 + 3 + 4 = 11 dice
                                                          1. Four for casting mage's Gnosis.
                                                          2. Three for the casting mage's Mind dots.
                                                          3. Four because the spell's Duration is Indefinite.
                                                          4. Subjects still have the option to spend a Willpower point to further bolster the roll.
                                                          5. Or maybe this is wrong and subjects simply get an automatic 3 successes on the clash roll per the "If the Attainment would require a measurement of its successes" bit. Not sure whether they can use Willpower in that case.
                                                        7. The mage can have multiple instances of this spell in effect simultaneously, each effecting different subjects. Each casting counts against his maximum number of active spells (in this case 4 max).
                                                        Finally, a mage cannot take advantage of Teamwork when casting legacy attainments

                                                        Please point out where I got something wrong or left something out. Thanks to anyone who puts the effort into checking me on this stuff.
