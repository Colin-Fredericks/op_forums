    03-17-2015, 09:48 PM
    Baron Ornskjolf is one of the Strix mentioned in the 2nd edition chapter of them. In a sidebar at the end of his section, it mentions that the Baron has managed to sire several childer, who are strange Mekhet as a result of his being Strix. I was wondering, what kind of stuff would you assign to one of these weird Strix-made Kindred? What do you think is appropriate? A bloodline? Some kind of smaller benefit?

                  Make them a new bloodline. Hells, this is exactly the situation that bloodlines are meant to model! A lineage of vampires with a little something extra to their Blood. Remember - new bloodlines are much more flexible than the old ones. Not everyone has a new discipline; the very first new bloodline, the Neglatu, don't! And, even when we do get four disciplines, there's been an emphasis on using either devotions, a merit, or something else, as the main thrust of the bloodline.

                  So, the "Strix bloodline" would involve a Gift that let them purchase specific strix traits as if they were devotions. Simple and straight forwards.

                          Bloodlines aren't hardcoded to arise only when deliberately desired by Kindred at Blood Potency 6. With sufficient narrative justification, Bloodlines can manifest in a variety of conditions, both as things people make and things that happen to them. "The Baron has an unusually strong bond with his host body, and can sire Kindred." definitely sounds like the kind of territory where a Bloodline can manifest. Obviously the Baron himself doesn't manifest a Bloodline, because he's Strix, not Kindred. His childer seem to be fertile ground for that sort of thing.

                          I'm still fiddling with a writeup, but I'm definitely thinking the Bloodline weakness might be manifesting the eye glow when they use Disciplines, or maybe when they Frenzy. Definitely the sort of thing that can give people a false flag when they see those Kindred using disciplines. I could even imagine some Strix creating or finding some way to "infect" Kindred with this kind of bloodline as a way to sow that sort of chaos, though they also have an ability that can do that effect directly.

                                                  The interesting thing about Strix is that many of their banes can potentially be shared by vampires already. Crossroads, holy symbols, hated by beasts, can only enter when invited... Strix and vampires have more in common than either species likes to admit. If you were looking to create a bloodline-like template that isn't instantly recognized as something Strix created, you could try making them a Mekhet bloodline that has an additional bane that doesn't count toward your humanity banes, and must be a bane shared by Kindred and Strix.Alternatively, you could give them a variant of the Repulsion bane (B&S 109) and choose fire or sunlight; not only will fire harm them, they have great difficulty even coming near it, to mimic the Strix's banes.

                                                  In terms of special abilities, if you wanted them to be a bloodline the simplest solution would be the allow them to buy certain Dread Powers that are rules compatible with vampires, say for 3 xp each, the same cost as an in-clan discipline. You could also try developing some unique powers for them based off the Strix, but that would be more work.
