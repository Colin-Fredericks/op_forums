        Originally posted by Leliel View Post
        Yeah, I hear there was a bit of a scheduling kerfuffle; they wanted Morticia's arc to be before theirs, for whatever reason.
        I mean, a pretty good reason immediately presents itself in the form of "Morticia's arcs deal with stuff that touches more of the bigger picture, whereas the Candlelight Brigade always has too many rabbits to chase and wasn't really left wanting for screentime back in Season 2."

        Even if we never met the Wardens of Woe, getting a better look at where Umbra came from shifts her whole placement in the story, especially when you compare it with the shit Chris's arcs have her dealing with.

        Also, the Tsarina Pestis arc and its...rather mind-screwy antagonists. Nice touch that they called out Sterliza for being just as much of a monster as the Psychos for her blind hatred of Pestis leading her to thoughts like "it's not really evil if I bring down this building to kill Vizier Fever..."
        There's a lot of little tweaks and nudges to Perdition's background that came up in this arc, but I think the biggest one is just the pointed reminder that (just as the Clockwork Device views Perdition's defection as stemming from a defect) it is a mistake to assume uncritically that the Device — a distributed entity that we know values its own self-preservation over most any other goal it has — is little more than a malfunctioning cosmic supercomputer.

        It's easy to forget that the Demiurge is basically the Gnostic Devil. Sometimes it takes a stranger ranting at an empty room to piece that together, and that's delightfully spooky.

                                                "The HSQ of LLC" is "The Holy Shit Quotient of Long Lost Cousins." Long Lost Cousins is a nonspecific crossover event that, in the fiction of this series, comes in somewhere around the introduction of Ranger Nightmare. It involves the Strix in some capacity (leaning on the "Kindred" angle) and features Steve acting as the mouthpiece for the larger phenomenon through which "vampires is am liars alot" (and, more prosaically, Pancryptia) can be observed as an example.

                                                Going South is a nonspecific crossover event whose title is riffing on the Winter Court through Summer's involvement and an implied bad time with the Stepmother. The creepy ravens are Strix or hobgoblins of some stripe, drawing more particularly from the imagery of the Unholy and her durance-esque origin story.

                                                The Phobos Defender and Lilitu Xangmu are "The Begotten and the Dark Mother, but the post was made before the Kickstarter was out."

                                                I'm pretty sure a lot of the other stuff on the Uncertain list is just down to Sikla throwing out a bunch of names.
