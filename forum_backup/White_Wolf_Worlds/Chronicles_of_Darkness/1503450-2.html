    Yes. Although it would be more than a little bit rapey. One thing that you have to remember with Ecstatics is that a big part of what they do is deliberately push beyond social boundaries.

    Hell, given the cults association with the underworld and the lands of the dead the sex party might not even come before the killing part.

    Historical factions pushed past social boundaries that would already have seemed extreme to modern sensibilities. The cults of Inanna/Ishtar and Astarte live in a world where lives were brutally short and might made right.

    The practices of historical ecstatic factions in particular (and ecstatic factions in general) probably shouldn't be comfortable or even palatable.

    The only thing keeping the kiss of Astarte from being considered left handed is probably the fact that they fight on behalf of local consilium.

    The next group are probably just as uncomfortable in many ways - Maenads are a bit like a (mostly female, again - I'm starting to see a trend here) sociopathically murderous version of the fellowship of Pan. Good times.

    I doubt modern legacy holders from these historical factions would be especially common or widespread in the modern world - more like little pockets of cultural preservation.

        A quick bit of feedback on the formatting, including the name of the spell that the Attainment replicates makes it much easier to read. In the second optional Attainment, for example, you could have dedicated the first paragraph explain the flavor and how to activate it, the on the next one write something along the lines of "This replicates Ghost Shield, with Reach assigned to instant use", which makes it both concise and easier for players to reference during sessions. In case of custom spells, I recommend the format "This is a [Practice] of [Arcana] utilizing the purview of [chosen Arcana's purview] to [explanation on how its possible], with Reach assigned to [chosen factors]".

        Regarding the Attainments themselves, the beauty and seduction angle do not seem to flow in a way that would be practical in and out of the battlefield. An example would be the last Life Attainment, utilizing Control Instincts with Reach to instant use, sensory range, advanced duration and affecting supernatural beings would make it have a powerful effect both in conversation and battle. Honing The Form, in contrast, could be cast as a ritual as the 4th Attainment, utilizing the Reach for advanced duration and going beyond trait limits.

        While Ghost Gate is useful for a quick getaway, but it doesn't have the same offensive power as for example, a spell that makes emits ectoplasm that is then transformed into a Safe Place. This would use a Patterning of Life and Death to replicate Spirit Manse, allowing it to take the form of an idyllic natural home (aspect of seduction) or a dense jungle that aids them in their hunt (aspect of war).

        Your explanation invokes a sense of utilitarian, primal beauty. But how they view combat is not very clear when looking at the Attainments.

                Maenads


                Origins

                Parenting: Thyrsus,

                Background:
                The Maenads have long been associated with violence, ferocity, and Madness. They worship Dionysus through bloody rites in which women gather under the full moon to take drugs and engage in wild dancing that becomes more frenzied and out of control as the night goes on, inevitably culminating in bloody death as their prey is torn apart and offered as sacrifice.
                Even more concerning is that this madness is not contained within the legacy itself, often spreading to others nearby and on occasion tainting whole communities.
                Modern maenads operate with more subtlety than their ancestral sisters, often finding much common ground with their fellow worshipers of Dionysus in the fellowship of Pan. Their savagery remains however and the only thing stopping the entire legacy from being declared left handed is that where possible they direct their murderous passions towards enemies of the local consilium, and where not they hunt animals or spirits. Nevertheless, their practices take an inevitable toll on their wisdom.


                Appearances:
                Maenads are violent, wild, and unpredictable. Sometimes they manage to maintain a facade of normalcy, but more often they find themselves drifting to the fringes of society - especially those fringes where violence is considered acceptable.

                Doctrine
                Despite their (deserved) reputation for violence, the Maenads focus is on finding ecstacy through wild uninhibited abandon. Their rites involve as much drinking, drug taking, and sex as they do violence - violence is just such a depressingly inevitable end point that it quickly became so synonymous with the cult that it shaped their own identity.



                Prerequisites:
                Brawl, athletics, (feels like there should be a merit representing status in a spirit court) spirit allies would not go amiss, secret society status

                Initiation:
                A prospective Maenad is invited to participate in a full moon party where she experiences possession by a spirit and learns to completely let go of her inhibitions and sense of self.

                Organisation:
                The Maenads meet every full moon to enact their rites although these are not always the violent spectacle their reputation paints in people's minds and sometimes they are little more than wild parties.
                Sleeper cults are surprisingly common amongst the legacy, with sleeper worshippers often becoming possessed by the spirits that flock to surround the cults activities.

                Theory:
                Inhibitions, expectations, and restraint are products of the lie. By deliberately letting go of those restrictions and acting out their wildest impulses they free themselves from the hold of the fallen world and embody the higher symbolism of the primal wilds.
                Over time the legacy have gained allies among spirits of ecstacy, inhibition, and savagery who they work alongside to shatter the false restraints of the fallen world.

                Magic

                Ruling Arcana: Spirit

                Yantras:
                Wild dancing (+2 but only used in rituals)
                Full moon (+1)
                Breaking social taboos (+1)

                Oblations:
                Acting without inhibition in a dangerous or antisocial fashion, encouraging wild behaviour in others,


                First attainment: Sight of the Wild
                Prerequisites: Spirit 1, initiation
                Primary factor: Duration
                Reach: Instant

                By releasing her inhibitions and opening herself to the instincts of the primal wild, a Maenad is able to open her senses to the spiritual realities of the world around her.
                This functions identically to Exorcists eye.

                Optional: Life 1
                Primary: duration
                Reach: instant

                The primal wild resonates with life within the fallen world and by watching it's pulse as the lie falls away the Maenad can sense living beings nearby.
                This functions identically to Web of Life


                Second attainment: Primitive Liberation
                Prerequisites: Spirit 2
                Primary factor: Duration
                Reach: instant

                Spirits flock to the resonance generated by Maenad rites and Maenads quickly learn how to open the way for them to manifest or hold them from the material world. Generally this is used to make manifestation easier, allowing the spirits a foothold in the material world, thereby spreading the madness of their rites and damaging the lie.
                This functions identically to opener of the way.

                Optional: Life 2
                Primary: Potency
                Reach: instant
                Maenads use drink, drugs, and other intoxicants to help lower inhibitions and ease themselves into States of ecstatic savagery. When needed however they quickly sharpen up, denying intoxicants a good of them just as easily as they can deny spirits. Dangerously wild but without a hint of the intoxication that brought them to this point.
                This functions identically to cleanse the body.



                Third attainment: Abandonment of Fetters
                Prerequisites: Spirit 3
                Primary factor: Potency
                Reach: Duration

                By spending a scene leading wild ecstatic rites the Maenad can erode the lies and inhibitions that separate the fallen world from the shadow, and by doing so bring the world closer to the primal wild itself.
                This functions identically to place of power

                Optional: Life 3
                Primary: Potency
                Reach: Instant
                Maenads not only erode the lies that separate the shadow, but also those that restrict her with minor injuries, leaving her wound behind along with her inhibitions.
                This functions identically to Knit


                Fourth attainment: Inhibitions Unbound
                Prerequisites: Spirit 4
                Primary factor: Duration
                Reach: instant , advanced duration, sensory casting
                Withstand: Rank
                Through surrendering to the savagery of her primal instincts a Maenad eventually gains some measure of control over the wild rites she oversees. In doing so she also gains a measure of control over the spirits drawn to those rites.
                This functions identically to command spirit

                Optional: Life 4
                Primary: Duration
                Reach: Instant, advanced duration, sensory casting.
                Withstand: Composure
                Maenads gain control not only over spirits, but also the instinctual acts of those caught up in her rites.
                This functions identically to control instincts.


                Fifth attainment: Queen of Shadows
                Prerequisites: Spirit 5
                Primary factor: duration
                Reach: advanced duration
                Practice: Making

                At the pinnacle of her art the Maenad takes a position of rulership amongst the spirits flocking to her rites,
                giving her a rank amongst spirits no higher than her spirit arcana.
                Spirits of lesser rank may seek (or be persuaded) to attach themselves to the Maenads court and she is treated with the honour and dignity due to get station (and expected to uphold the etiquette of the courts).
                When calling, controlling, binding, or otherwise using sympathetic magic on members of her court the mage may cast spells as if the spirits were in intimate sympathetic contact with her without need of the side Arcanum.
                Any spiritual alliances the Maenad makes are rendered null and void when the spell expires (but she will be expected to uphold her obligations). It is common for Maenads to enact the rites to renew their court regularly.
                (This functions in the same manner as the 1ed spell spirit court).



                Optional: Life 5
                Primary: Duration
                Reach: Instant, advanced duration, raise attribute above max

                Drawing on the power of her court, the Maenad becomes faster, stronger, and tougher.

                This functions identically to honing the form

                            Originally posted by Tamuz View Post
                            One thing that you have to remember with Ecstatics is that a big part of what they do is deliberately push beyond social boundaries.
                            By the by, that aspect is a perfect fit for the Mastigos as a parent path. They focus on deliberate violations of taboos and such as part of their path.

                            The experiential aspect is very Thyrsus, so I'm not saying these don't fit, but you can actually mix and match with any of the paths:
