    Angelic Hierarchy

    12-05-2014, 11:21 AM
    I made this for a shard where the God-Machine is akin to the Demiurge or a God of Order rather than the penultimate creator. Sort of like Mechinus from Planescape. Being a God of logic and cosmically arcane laws, it would have a hiearchy with which to better prioritize its servitors under. This is a guideline, then, for anyone who wants to try for that, while making it easy to compact or expand depending on the game's Gonzo factor. The names can also be altered depending on region, since what is a name? A robot rose by any other would look just as fake. Even had an idea where the GM has 3 Aspects (like how Hindi religion has Brahman, Vishnu, and Shiva, only different), but that's another story. Without further adieu, here it is:

    Angelic Hierarchy


    In service to the G.O.D. (an acronym for General Overseer Dynamic), Angels were born as agents meant to carry out its will across time and space. While they follow arcane laws that seem almost contradictory, each is made with form and function in mind be they flesh and bone or glass and steel. Over the fractal centuries, the Unchained have noticed through shared experiences and deductive reasoning that their heavenly kin fall under a system of mechanics and ritualized governance. Whether this is true, or a ploy to deceive the damned, none can say with certainty--for why else would an omnipotent deity need to forge a chain of command when all are bound to its will alone? Regardless, the following is the general system used by the Angelic Host:

    Minimum Lvl Primum--Rank
    1--Petitioner
    2--Archon
    3--Principality
    4--Power
    5--Virtue
    6--Dominion
    7--Throne
    8--Cherubim
    9--Seraphim
    10--Demiurge, God of the Fallen

    Beneath the G.O.D., angels are divided into three tiers of importance, then further divided into 3 ranks. Called Spheres, the collective groupings often form around Infrastructure, with the S-1 (First Sphere) involving basic units and/or simple tasks. In time, the S-1 may either advance to S-2 or become recognized under the jurisdiction of a nearby S-2 or S-3 as infrastructure is improved upon for future use.

    First Sphere
    The basic outpost, often a budding branch of a bigger occult-matrix or the start of one within a new territory. Theirs is a street-level push for the God-Machine, and are the most common for demons to deal with. Petitioners, Archons, and Principalities comprise the S-1.

    Petitioners -- Also known as malakim, petitioners are the infantry of the Host. They perform basic functions for the infrastructure that can't be trusted to mortals or even sworn-supernaturals. Often they're found in cells of three or more, working under an Archon superior. Depending of the area of influence, Guardians may handle security and defense of a building or neighborhood; protect a small organization (such as a cult or charity) or singular VIP. Destroyers hunt down potential threats as thugs or assassins, removing local threats to the infrastructure. Analysts scout the necessary assets, marking key targets or places of note for study. Psychopomps ensure pieces of the said-infrastructure are made and assembled, leaving Messengers to handle PR with the local populace.

    Archons -- Lieutenants and adjutants of the Principalities, each Archon commands a least one cell of Petitioners, leading them towards a particular goal necessary to the GM's plan. A Guardian Archon may become head of a security firm or neighborhood watch, while his Destroyer counterpart leads a gang of stigmatic malcontents. Messengers aim at the media and similar institutions as they affect the dispersal of information, while Psychopomps manage small-time businesses that fund Analyst-controlled research labs.

    Principalities -- As their name suggests, Principalities govern all Elohim under their sphere of influence, rarely leaving the confines of their designated facility. Most become one with the environment, be it a building or collection thereof. This can leave them vulnerable, which is why they are served by at least two Archons; one to protect their vessel, and another to handle the needed resources for the occult-matrix. Beyond that, the Principality's main objective is the protection of the linchpin with rare exception. Failure means demotion, or worse, erasure. Often, the type of infrastructure in question defines what Incarnation the Principality is. Destroyers frequent Elimination InF, Guardians with Defense, Messengers are best with Concealment, and Psychopomps handle Logistics. What the Analysts do with Control & Command InF generally remains hidden even from Archons in the know.

    Second Sphere
    Influencing either a major city or small region of a continent, S-2s centralize multiple S-1s to handle major projects; ones that require greater amounts of time, resources, and conjunctive infrastructure to complete greater occult-matrices. Optimal S-2s command at least four or five branching S-1s, depending on the scenario.

    Powers -- The Erelim are usually what lesser ranking angels refer to when they speak of "the Authorities". Each Power is a regional coordinator of Angelic activity, and often work in tandem with each other when commanded by the Virtue they serve under. This includes those within splinter-timelines deemed of interest to the G.O.D. Each commands a number of Principalities as efficiently as possible. Destroyers are often interested in developing new weapons or utilizing military or some other well-armed asset to complete a project. Guardians focus around enhancing defensible InF or protecting key organizations. Analyst Erelim prefer information control, keeping tabs on suspicious news reports and potential demon sightings. Like spy-masters they disseminate possible leads among relevant agents to mark suspects for retrieval and processing. Meanwhile, Psychopomp Erelim ensure logistical contingencies should infrastructure be compromised or need be repurposed. Messengers devise elaborate webs of social connectivity, helping best decide who to lean on, and who to cut away from.

    Virtues -- Like Principalities, the Gibborim are generally given a location to become part of. These strongholds become meeting points for Erelim, who average around four per Virtue. The Virtue then directs the Powers to coordinate about the relevant territory, planning for any and all eventualities therein. Demons liken these groupings to a ruling thumb and four fingers, forming one of the many hands of the G.O.D.. More often than not, each of the five represents one Incarnation, with the master Virtue in question reflecting the purpose of the whole. Destroyer Virtues are intent on war and threat removal, having its Powers center themselves controlled chaos within a region. Guardians amplify Orwellian surveillance programs and secret police tactics against demons, supernaturals, and other possible threats. Messengers encourage one form of propaganda or another to augment popular belief to satisfy their objective. Psychopomps invest in acquiring private land and territory for remote projects and trade. Analysts begin massive research divisions to better the results of relevant InF, as well as catalog new technology and arcane techniques.

    Dominions -- Similar only in name to servants of the Promethean Principle, the Hashmallim are what connect the Virtues together. They range in commanding metropoleis like Tokyo and New York, to countries like France and Turkey. Theirs is a force to be reckoned with, and never taken lightly. To do so without aide is to suffer the cold wrath of their unfeeling Lord Eternal, and know the grinding of its cosmic gears. At this level of influence, Incarnations become less restrictive in what the angel is tasked with, as the God-Machine elevates its progeny above the rank and file.

    Third Sphere
    On a global scale, S-3s are the rarest and theoretically most powerful of the G.O.D.'s operations. Few are those who have lived to see or even hear of angels at this level of strength and power. They are the elite, and the most deadly of the Angelic Host for any man or beast to do battle against.

    Thrones -- Ophanim are thus named for the idea of stewards overseeing the seat of power that is the World itself. Each manages the lesser Dominions of an entire continent, never leaving the mighty strongholds that they or those that came before forged so long ago.

    Cherubim -- The chancellors of Heaven, these angels settle disputes and interactions between the Thrones of G.O.D.. No more than one is represented per connection among the seven continents, and each is as a mobile army of one when called to war.

    Seraphim -- If the cherubim are akin to heralds, than the Seraphim are avatars to the God-Machine's eternal glory. Each may be a millennium old or greater, for none can hope to slay beings of such raw power. If such a mythic force exists, they may range between five or seven in number, for anything higher would surely douse all hope of rebellion against the G.O.D.

                              Originally posted by DesolateDolly View Post
                              Was there a particular thought process between reversing the sphere numerics and re-naming the Archangels to Archons? I'm curious.
                              Yes, yes there was. First, I thought it would make sense given that the larger the sphere, the more complexity is involved to act as a node between the lesser spheres. As an added bonus, it confuses the hell out of people who think they know how the system works. As for Archangels, it felt wrong to have them so low on the food-chain since some of the most powerful angels were noted as being Archs. Thus, it felt better to replace that name with something less potent a word, to avoid confusion and disappointment if demons came across an archangel called Gabriel, only to find out he's a total wimp. Unless you plan on archangels being as powerful as noted--but that's basically overkill, since every angel there after said-rank would need to be more powerful than that. In short, you'd end up with some Dragonball Z levels of goofiness at that point.
