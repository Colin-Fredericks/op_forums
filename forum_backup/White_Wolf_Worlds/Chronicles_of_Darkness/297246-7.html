                Originally posted by nofather View Post

                Based on your personal opinion, should that include Moon Gifts, or are the Lunes and Luna more picky? First edition had a lot of gifts, but many were limited to auspice or tribe or lodge, which cut down on the amount an individual werewolf could find. I actually agree with your opinion, but I think for simplicity's sake it wouldn't be unreasonable to have Gift write-ups in Lodge entries, appreciating that, 'Anyone can get this gift from this totem spirit, but this lodge tends to have it more than others due to their closeness to it.' Of course, that depends on how Gifts are obtained by Werewolves, Inflicted suggests gaining gifts isn't solely a blessing.
                I'm not yet sure on Moon Gifts; I can see some argument for more restricted access to specific Moon Gifts that specific Lunes are caretakers of. In theory, any Moon Gift is an extension of the quicksilver communion with the Warden Moon itself during the First Change, and require no more outside influence than already exists in Luna's link to the Uratha's spirit, but it could be that Lunes guard particularly esoteric symbols linked to the Auspice that they can mark a werewolf with.

                I think I'd tend more towards such Moon Gifts being accessible by undertaking a spirit quest to track down the specific Lune with the Gift in question, but I can see situations where a given Lune with an agenda might decide that it wants a Lodge of werewolves tasked with a particular purpose to which the Gift is well-suited (or for which it can dangle the Gift as a carrot/reward for service).

                Gifts are always beneficial to the recipient, but receiving them may not be the most pleasant experience - take a look at my story in the fiction Anthology for hints at that That said, I could see mileage for 'cursed' Gifts or Gifts that come with significant drawbacks as well as powers, because of what happens in how a Gift is gained. Interesting notion to mull over.

                I know that when I used to work for another company and did some art direction there, we had a turnaround of 2 weeks to a month for artists to get work in, but that was working to a particularly frantic timetable and I don't know whether OP run a more relaxed schedule
