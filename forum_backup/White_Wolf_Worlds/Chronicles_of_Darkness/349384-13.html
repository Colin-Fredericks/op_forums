                    Frankenstein's Monster

                    Frankenstein’s Monster is a fictional creature that has seen as many adaptions, re-interpretations, and re-telling’s as Dracula (possibly more). He has been depicted as everything from a remorseless monster to a sympathetic creature longing to belong. For this write up, I am presenting The Monster as I believe he would appear in Promethean the Created.
                    The version of the Monster is the progenitor of the Frankenstein lineage, last seen in the opening fiction under the alias “Mr. Verney”. After a climactic encounter with the Pandoran known as “The Bride”, the Monster had finally destroyed the creature, but was left shaken by the battle. After retreating to the arctic for several years, he has returned to the place of his creation, to the ruins of a manor house only a few miles from Castle Frankenstein in Hesse, Germany. Reclaiming the name “Adam”, it is here he has chosen to begin his Pilgrimage anew, and complete the journey started over 200 years ago.

                    Adam– Frankenstein’s Monster
                    Virtue: Love; Vice: Rage
                    Lineage: Frankenstein; Refinement: Plumbum (Lead)

                    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 4, Resolve 5(6)
                    Physical Attributes: Strength 7, Dexterity 4, Stamina 7
                    Social Attributes: Presence 4, Manipulation 3, Composure 4
                    Skills: Academics 4 (Music, Philosophy), Athletics 5, Brawl 5, Crafts 3, Empathy 3(Detect Lies), Expression 3 (Eloquence), Intimidation 5(Threats), Larceny 2, Medicine 2 (Anatomy), Occult 4 (Alchemy), Persuasion 2 (Pathos),Science 3 (Chemistry, Electricity), Stealth 4(Stalking), Subterfuge 3, Survival 5 (Arctic), Streetwise 2, Weaponry 2 (Improvised)
                    Merits: Air of Menace*, Alternate Identity 2, Area of Expertise (Alchemy, Music), At Any Cost*, Choke Hold, Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Elpis 2, Fame 3, Fast Reflexes 2, Fleet of Foot 2, Giant, Indomitable, Iron Will, Language (German, many others), Repute 3, Style: Improvised Weaponry 3, Iron Skin 4, Style: Relentless Assault 5, Style: Street Fighting 3, Striking Looks 2 (Frightening)
                    Merits marked with an asterisk (*) are from the Hurt Locker playtest document.
                    Bestowment: Unholy Stamina, Unholy Strength
                    Transmutations: Corporeum (Autonomic Control, Hard Body, Human Flesh, Regeneration, Swift Feet), Deception (Incognito, Leave no Trace, Silent Steps), Disquietism (Alembic 3, Nameless Dread, Quell Disquiet, Soothe Disquiet 5), Electrification (Black Out, Divine Lightning, Feel the Spark, Generator, Jolt, Shock), Mesmerism (Atavistic Instinct, Firebringer, Fixed Stare, Flight, Logos), Sensorium (Translators Eye, Night Sight), Saturninus (Cleansing Ritual, Refined Masquerade), Vitality (Battering Ram, Bludgeon, Defenestrating Blow, Fist of Talos, Hyperion’s Flight, Might 5, Pulverizing Blow, Shoulders of Atlas, Stupefying Blow, Thunderclap Blow, Titanic Vigor, Titan’s Throw, Vault), Vulcanus (Electroshock Recharge, Sense Flux, Sense Pyros)

                    Azoth: 5 (Reduced by going to the Wastes)

                        Dr.Jekyll & Mr.Hyde

                        For this write up, I am presenting Dr. Henry Jekyll (and his alter-ego, Edward Hyde), the titular character of Robert Louis Stevenson’s 1886 novel The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, as a member of the Cheiron Group conspiracy from Hunter: The Vigil.
                        This version of Dr. Henry Jekyll is a chemist employed by the Cheiron group, developing new, cutting edge combat and performance enhancing drugs. While presenting the image of a clean cut and morally upright person, Jekyll’s darker impulses led him to create an implant that stores an experimental steroid that increases his physical strength and prowess. While the steroid does grant a boost to such traits, it also led to a psychotic break. Now whenever he triggers the implant, his alternate persona “Mr Hyde” takes over. Hyde is an amoral sociopath who acts out the impulses that Jekyll represses, and utilizes all the physical benefits of Jekyll’s steroid. Even more disturbing is that the implant has triggered by its self on more than a few occasions. Dr. Jekyll is currently working on a way to revise the formula that lets him enjoy its benefits without unleashing the “Hyde” persona. Likewise, Hyde is seeking a way to rid himself of Jekyll’s moralizing and hypocrisy permanently.

                        Henry Jekyll/Edward Hyde
                        Virtue (Jekyll): Prudent; Vice (Jekyll): Hypocrite / Virtue(Hyde): Determined; Vice (Hyde): Amoral ; Vice-Ridden (Hyde): Violent
                        Conspiracy: Cheiron Group

                        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4 (Hyde 3), Wits 3, Resolve 3 (Hyde 4)
                        Physical Attributes: Strength 3(Hyde 5), Dexterity 3, Stamina 3(Hyde 5)
                        Social Attributes: Presence 3(Hyde 4), Manipulation 3, Composure 4(Hyde 3)
                        Skills: Academics 3/2(Psychology), Athletics 2/3 (Running), Brawl 2/4, Computers 2/0, Crafts 2, Intimidation 1/3, Larceny 1/3, Medicine 3/0, Politics 2/0 (Corporate), Science 4 (Bio-Chemistry), Socialize 2(Etiquette), Stealth 2, Streetwise 1/3(Vice), Subterfuge 3, Weaponry 1/2 (Clubs)
                        *The ratings after the slash are used while in the “Hyde” persona.
                        Merits (Jekyll): Allies 3 (Lawyer Gabriel Utterson), Alternate Identity 2 (Hyde), Area of Expertise (Etiquette, Psychology, Bio-Chemistry), Interdisciplinary Specialty (Bio-Chemistry), Professional Training 4 (Academics, Medicine, Science), Resources 4, Status 3 (Cheiron Group), Striking Looks 1(Stylish)
                        Merits (Hyde): Area of Expertise (Vice), Danger Sense, Defensive Combat (Brawl), Fast Reflexes 2, Hardy 2, Iron Skin 2, Style: Improvised Weaponry 1, Style: Parkour 2, Style: Street Fighting 3, Tolerance for Biology, Vice-Ridden (Violent), Striking Looks 1(Cruel)
                        Endowment: Thaumatechnology (Strange Form 5; Jekyll/Hyde have the Strange Form Dread power as an Endowment. When activated, it triggers the “Hyde” persona and grants +2 to Strength, +2 Stamina, +1 Armor, and alters Intelligence, Presence, Resolve and Composure)
                        Integrity: 5
                        Willpower: 7
                        Size: 5
                        Initiative: Jekyll 7, Hyde 8
                        Defense: Jekyll 5, Hyde 7
                        Armor: Jekyll 0, Hyde 1
                        Speed: Jekyll 11, Hyde 13
                        Health: Jekyll 8, Hyde 10
                        Condition: Dissociative Identity Disorder, Malefactor



                        Dissociative Identity Disorder [Persistent]
                        You have suffered a trauma severe enough that it fractures your character personality into one or more additional personas, allowing you to deny your trauma or any actions the trauma causes by placing the blame on “someone else.” An abused person might develop a tough-as-nails survivor personality, create a “protector” or even become a murderer to deny the abuse she suffers. The personalities may or may not be aware of each other, and they come and go through your character’s mind in response to specific situations or conditions.
                        The character selects a specific breaking point. If a Breaking Point roll involving this specific breaking point is failed, the alternate persona takes over for the rest of the scene. On a dramatic failure the alternate personality remains in control until another roll for a specific breaking point is failed (this could be the same breaking point or a different one). This alternate personality usually has its own virtue, vice, aspirations and Breaking Points. The persona may also manifest different Skills (all skills are purchased normally, but certain personalities might not be aware they have such skills or may use them to different degrees of proficiency).
                        Possible Sources: Neurological conditions, Psychological trauma, encountering a breaking point
                        Resolution: Regain a dot of Integrity, or achieve an exceptional success on a breaking point. Long term psychological treatment can also re-integrate the personalities.
                        Beat: When this the actions of one of the characters alternate persona causes severe complications in the life of the primary personality.

                        Malefactor [Persistent]
                        Your darkest nature and most venial impulses are forced to the surface. While this condition is active, you no longer gain willpower from your Virtue. You continue to gain willpower from your vice as normal, but you may also fully recover all of your willpower by acting out for vice in a more extreme manner.
                        Resolution: Increasing your Integrity; suffering a breaking point when acting out of Virtue.
                        Beat: Whenever acting out your Vice damages a relationship or causes long term trouble.

                            I'm not stopping to analyse completely each sheet right now, but I would make the Frankenstein Monster even more bright. Maybe he isn't the most successful promethean around in his Pilgrimage, but he is described in the novel as not just a mockery of human life, but actually of its highest state. He's as much a genius as the doctor himself, if not even smarter.

                            For Jeckyll and Hyde I would go far from this, assuming I'm trying to be honest with the original character, and not with the great number of bad adaptations of it. The concoction created by Dr Jekyll could be meant as a mental weapon to control the creatures, something to subsume their inhuman urges and give the imbiber more clarity of mind. Imagine a potion able to convert all the Alternate Integrities back into just Integrity, and put some common Breaking-Points, like killing, back into place? It could cure even Slashers! For the sound mind, maybe a greater capacity to resist loss of Integrity and to comprehend those who don't have it.

                            But something went wrong, as you could expect, and Hyde is devoid of Integrity instead. Maybe it is a state similar to terminal Soulessness, but more stable, or maybe it is something else. But as evil as he is, Hyde never was more physically able or less smart than Jekyll.

                                Re: Frankenstein's Monster

                                You mage a good point for the Creature's Intelligence. I re-read a bit of the novel and agree that his intelligence is much greater than much of pop culture gives him credit for (seriously, He learns a sophisticated degree of language and reading after several months of pure observation). I updated his intelligence to a 4, learning at least some room for even more improvement.

                                Re: Jekyll/ Hyde

                                I mulled over a few different ways to present a modern World of Darkness Jekyll/Hyde. From my reading of the novel. Jekyll played the part of a upstanding citizen, but had urges that were contrary to his self image. His formula was conceived as a way to exorcise these urges, but instead gave him a way to indulge them without tarnishing his reputation. Jekyll enjoyed being Hyde..until he lost control of him.

                                As far as the actual mechanics, I almost went with making the Hyde personae a slasher but with no real change of capabilities from Jekyll, but decided that the pop-culture spin where Hyde is more physically powerful was more interesting (as always, your mileage may vary).

                                        The Invisible Man

                                        For this write up, I am presenting the Invisible Man, combining elements from the 1997 novel by HG Wells, and the 1933 film, as a Genius from World of Darkness: Slasher (further augmented by a Dread power to represent his unique condition).
                                        This version of the Invisible Man is Dr. Jack Griffin, and independent chemist who spent years attempting to devise a compound that could render materials invisible. After having squandered his resources, alienated his associates and been laughed out of academia, Dr. Griffin was reduced to living on the streets while he still worked desperately to perfect the formula that would give him the wealth and fame he desperately desired. He eventually created a formula and experimented upon himself. The formula was successful, but unstable, making Dr.Griffin permanently transparent and unhinging his mind. Now he indulges in theft, arson, and occasionally murder while his delusions become increasingly grandiose and megalomaniacal.

                                        Dr. Jack Griffin – The Invisible Man
                                        Virtue: Ambition; Vice: Reckless Vice-Ridden: Greed
                                        Undertaking: Genius

                                        Mental Attributes: Intelligence 4, Wits 4, Resolve 3
                                        Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 4, Stamina 2
                                        Social Attributes: Presence 2 Manipulation 3, Composure 3
                                        Skills: Academics 2(Research), Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Computer 2, Crafts 3(Jury Rig, Traps), Intimidation 2(Coercion), Investigation 2 (Scrounging), Larceny 4(Arson, Burglary), Medicine 3, Science 4(Chemistry, Optics), Stealth 4(Invisibility), Streetwise 2(Homeless), Subterfuge 2(Disguise), Survival 2(Urban)
                                        Merits: Anonymity 3, Area of Expertise (Chemistry), Eidetic Memory, Fast Reflexes 1, Fleet of Foot 2, Interdisciplinary Specialty (Chemistry), Murder Expert, Professional Training (Scientist; Academics, Medicine, Science), Tolerance for Biology, Vice Ridden
                                        Talent: Profiling Frailty: Intolerance for Chaos
                                        Integrity: 4
                                        Willpower: 6
                                        Size: 5
                                        Initiative: 8
                                        Defense: 6
                                        Speed: 14
                                        Health: 7
                                        Condition: Madness

                                        Dread Powers

                                        Invisibility: Griffin’s flesh and blood do not register to sight, even under infrared or ultraviolet light. This ability does not extend to items worn or carried and even items swallowed will be visible for several minutes. Once Griffin sheds his clothing he becomes nearly imperceptible to the naked eye. Perception rolls to spot him suffer a –5 penalty, except those based strictly on smell, sound or motion. Opponents fight blindly and must rely on cunning and their other senses to hit.                                             Dorian Gray

                                            For this write up, I am presenting Dorian Gray, the titular character of Oscar Wilde’s 1890 novel The Picture of Dorian Gray, as an Eternal from World of Darkness: Immortals.
                                            This version of Dorian did not die as the original tale would have people believe. Instead, he faked his demise will leaving a will bequeathing his considerable fortune, to previously-unknown illegitimate son, legitimized posthumously in the will. Of course, this was Dorian posing as his own descendant. He has repeated this ruse several times. Most recently he has returned to London after spending several decades abroad. He is currently posing as his latest descendant and operating as a club promoter, patron of the arts, and information broker.

                                            Dorian Gray
                                            Virtue: Repentant; Vice: Hedonist Vice-Ridden: Romantic

                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 3, Wits 4, Resolve 3
                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3
                                            Social Attributes: Presence 4 Manipulation 4, Composure 4
                                            Skills: Academics 3 (Art, Literature), Athletics 1, Brawl 2, Crafts 2 (Art), Empathy 4, Expression 2(Eloquence), Firearms 2, Intimidation 2, Medicine 1, Investigation 3(Blackmail Material), Larceny 2, Occult 1, Persuasion 5 (Seduction), Politics 2(British), Socialize 5 (Aristocracy, Carousing), Stealth 2, Streetwise 4 (Vice), Subterfuge 4, Weaponry 2(Clubs, Knives)
                                            Merits: Alternate Identity 3, Area of Expertise (Art, Seduction, Vice), Barfly, Contacts 4 (Artists, Aristocracy, Drug Dealers, Prostitutes), Etiquette 4, Fixer, Inspiring, Language (Many), Professional Training 5 (Socialite; Academics, Politics, Socialize), Pusher, Resources 5, Staff 3, Striking Looks 2, Taste, Vice Ridden
                                            Integrity: 6
                                            Willpower: 7
                                            Size: 5
                                            Initiative: 7
                                            Defense: 4
                                            Speed: 11
                                            Health: 8

                                            Dread Powers

                                            Anchor: Dorian’s immortality is anchored to his Portrait. Any damage inflicted to it is also inflicted to Dorian. This damage cannot be healed unless the Portrait is likewise repaired. Destroying the Portrait will destroy Dorian Gray. The Portrait has a Size of 4 and a Durability of 2.

                                            Immortality: Dorian does not age as is immune to natural poisons and diseases (but can still indulge in drug use with no risk of overdose). He regenerates damage at a rate of 1 bashing every 2 minutes, 1 lethal every 8 hours and 1 aggravated each day. He can also regrow lost limbs within a month and damaged organs within a few weeks. Even if killed He will return to life within a day.

                                                            Santa Claus

                                                            For this write up, I am using the Horror rules from Chronicles of Darkness to present a version of Santa Claus.

                                                            No one is really certain who or what Mr. Christopher Nicholas is. While it seems unlikely that he is the true mythological Santa Claus, he wields a large degree of otherworldly power that even master occultists are at a loss to explain. He even looks the part of a distinguished older gentleman a stocky build, snowy white beard, warm smile and twinkling eyes. To mortal society, he is a reclusive philanthropist who gives vast sums of money to a large number of humanitarian causes each year. To others he is a mysterious figure that is most active around the time of the solstice. For those that are charitable, honest, and kind; he leaves gifts and offers encouragement. For those that are selfish, wicked and naughty; he leaves a gentle warning, typically a lump of coal. The worst offenders he leaves for something else to deal with..

                                                            Mr. Nicholas
                                                            Virtuous: Jovial Virtue: Generous; Vice: Gluttony
                                                            Aspiration: Reward the Virtuous, Spread Cheer, Inspire Hope

                                                            Mental Attributes: Intelligence 6, Wits 5, Resolve 5
                                                            Physical Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 4, Stamina 5
                                                            Social Attributes: Presence 7, Manipulation 4, Composure 5
                                                            Skills: Academics 4(Religion), Animal Ken 4, Athletics 2, Crafts 5(Toys), Drive 3(Sleigh), Empathy 5(Children), Expression 3, Occult 4(Folklore), Persuasion 5, Survival 3, Stealth 5, Weaponry 2
                                                            Merits: Area of Expertise (Children, Religion, Toys), Danger Sense, Eidetic Memory, Indomitable, Inspiring, Language (All), Resources 5, Safe Place 3 (Workshop), Staff 3, Striking Looks 1, Virtuous
                                                            Integrity: 10
                                                            Potency: 8
                                                            Willpower: 18
                                                            Size: 5
                                                            Initiative: 9
                                                            Defense: 6
                                                            Speed: 14 (Flight 19)
                                                            Health: 10
                                                            Powers of Santa: Eye Spy, Holiday Miracle*, Immortal, Influence 1 (Hope), Know Soul, Surprise Entrance, Vanish*

                                                            Holiday Miracle: This functions as the Miracle dread power, but Mr. Nicolas may use it freely as long as it benefits others. This ability has no effect on the subjects Aspirations, but anytime they would regain Willpower from their vice they also gain the Guilty condition.

                                                            Vanish: When Mr. Nicholas is in a situation that might turn violent, or if he simply wants to leave, he may disappear into thin air. He will reappear sometime later (as little as a few minutes, or as long as several months) at one of his Workshops or any place with a high concentration of cheer and goodwill.
