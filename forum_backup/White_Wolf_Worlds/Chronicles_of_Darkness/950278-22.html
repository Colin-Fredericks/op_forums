            I think I did get a lot of this thread. As an example of how I'm deliberately using the criticisms people have brought up, the comments about sticking to a theme helped me do something I'd been struggling with for a while: incorporating the Atlantis/Golden Age mythology from Awakening into the backstory. I knew I wanted Atlantis to be a significant Bardo, as well as Lemuria, but the idea of sticking with the concept of transgression gave me a burst of inspiration.

            Also, I'm going to just slightly tweak my description of the Abyss for that theme. The new Abyss has a strong theme of "breaking things" or "corrosion", and I'm going to emphasize that what the Abyss does is essentially transgression stripped down: it's ultimately the primordial root of what Mages do. It breaks things not because of some higher purpose or desire, but because they're there.
