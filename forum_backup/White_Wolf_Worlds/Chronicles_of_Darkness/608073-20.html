                Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
                If you mean it's about otherwise human characters with weird abilities, then yes I think you're right. But I don't think it's narratively "superhero stories" in the sense of "fighting supercriminals to protect law and order" which is kind of thematically central for the genre (there are outlier cases, but those tend to be intentionally deconstructing the genre).
                I'm sure you *could* do a darker Justice League or Avengers with Deviant, but that's not the genre.

                X-Men, protecting a world that hates and fears them (while rescuing their own from killer robots, racist conspiracies, and fellow mutants who experiment on their own). Doom Patrol, freaks united to turn their curses into the strength to make a difference. Inhumans, largely fighting to live their own lives while avoiding the humans who would exploit them (they were secret for most of human history) and dealing with their own when the transformed go mad. There's a rich vein of "outsider heroes" in superheroic lore given how many comic fans are weirdos ourselves. Frack, DC has a team called the Outsiders. Until legal crap turned the franchise into a mess, X-Men was *the* superhero juggernaut for over 20 years. And that's just Big Two US comics. I'm not even going to try tapping the potential manga/anime vein here. There are, hopefully obviously, a lot of other things that gamers can do with Deviant, but this is a superhero subgenre that's just begging to be exploited by folks who want to light a candle rather than curse the World of Darkness.

                Heroes die. Hope is a phoenix.

IMO, keeping Integrity reinforces this basic thesis for Deviant in important ways.
