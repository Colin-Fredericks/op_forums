                                                        The bits of info we've got so far make it seem like Deviant creation is super flexible, and I really hope that's the case. With the wide range of origins they've mentioned, and how it seems they can be established canon groups (Cheiron) or originals (new orgs that the book will mention, cults, "my character accidentally became the brundlefly while trying to cure cancer in his garage"), I'm guessing that character creation isn't so much splat/mechanics based as coming up with your backstory and origin and using a more toolbox-y set of mechanics to represent it.

                                                        So I could see there being X-Splats that represent archetypes, but with multiple possible origins. So there's a "cyborg" splat, but that could the the result of mad science, God-Machine cultists, etc.

                                                        With the flexibility it looks like we'll have, I can't wait to tinker with it and come up with Deviants created by existing splats and organizations (like how Promethean had Vamp/Werewolf/Mage-made prometheans, and how fans have made more for other splats). So far, I have:

                                                        Vampire: Ordo Dracul-developed "superghoul". Heavy on gothic pseudoscience.
                                                        Mage: Honestly, each individual Path has multiple ways they'd go about this. Maybe it just started as an experiment in creating sleepwalkers or trying to isolate a "paradox witness" gene?
                                                        Hunter: Came up earlier in the thread- essentially, a hunter becomes a deviant when they go a little overboard with their endowment. Or, as someone put it, "when thaumatech implants take a little too well".
                                                        Promethean: Most Prometheans try to deal with the curses of their condition by becoming more like humans. What happens when one decides to see if he can make humans more like Prometheans?
                                                        Changeling: Blurs the lines a bit, since changelings and deviants are already quite alike. I could see changelings trying to create their own changelings, and getting a deviant out of it. Or, some sort of cold-iron Fae-hunter cyborg.
                                                        Demon: "make humans more like demons" is already listed as a possible goal for a demon, soooo...

                                                        And so on.
