    Originally posted by E11even-E11even View Post
    Well, I really hope the last three Kith and Kin stretch goals are soon.
    Agreed, I do hope those will come sooner rather than later.

    But I also want to agree with the Eldritch Minister in saying that, outside of my last post's points, I really adore this version. It is amazing. Clarity really feels like the underhill's traumatic feel, but clarified (heh) it somewhat. Each of the courts now feels both evocative AND useful. Mantle isn't just balanced, it's awesome. Five dots in mantle almost feels more epic than the crown itself, which rightly feels more like a sixth dot of mantle.

    And the Hedge. Omg, the Hedge. I can't even put into words how amazing that is. The Skein becoming the Dreaming Roads.. The Mirror World... Hedge Ghosts.. the streamlining of hedge navigation into an epic chase against the Hedge's attention. Just.. everything. The way it feels Narnia, Wonderland, Fantasia etc isn't your arcadian memories but rather the Hedge.. just wow. That alone makes it feel so much more fairytale.

    And yet, they introduced many elements of owod changeling. There is more childlikeness and childishness. There is more make believe and fantasy, alongside of gameplaying and system gaming. Making bridgeburners more obviously Dauntain/autumn people was a BRILLIANT move.
    killing dreams and creativity... seriously, kudos to whoever made that change, it made bridgeburners vastly more interesting to me. I never used them before, and I can't wait to use them.

    I'm on the same page as Sean. The nostalgia reminds me of the days (when we called him Arc XD) when the game first came out and we were all wild eyed and inspired. When it inspired so many creative juices. It's a great sign that almost all of everyone's issues with it so far boil down to word count. People's doubts and fears about the changes are mostly gone, and almost every issue will be solved by the companion book, because we all want MORE. We love it, and each of us has different ideas about what was left out that shouldn't have been, but.. that's an amazing sign. While some have some gripes about certain things, it looked like most of them knew the solution already. Most of us grok everything, and like a glamour addict, need a bigger fix XD

    Seriously, huge kudos and mad respect to the team. It's bloody BRILLIANT and beautifully mad. I can't wait to solve my billing and bump up my pledge. I can't wait to get the colorful and prestige changeling book we all wanted (comparing green and white 1e versus dreaming 20 almost hurts sometimes)...I can't wait for the Durance to be over.

    Ps. Please tell me some of the extra art budget was/will be spent on Stephanie Pui-Mun Law or Melissa Uran. Id give up an Icon to get more of them.

                    I may have already missed this part of the discussion, but do some of the changes to the kiths previewed seem strikingly different from anything in other games and in some cases needing a bit more information (looking at you Gristlegrinder). I hope that they will provide more information to clarify or maybe some tweeks to this before people just run around gulping down everything in sight.

                    Also, some of the concepts seem muddled to me.

                    Example: Ogres are creatures of violence. They escaped the hedge by forcing themselves on other things or other beings. They have been shaped by the violence inflicted on them sure, but they are typified by the fact that they used violence (and probably still use it) and it marked them. So why then is their Affinity Contract the Shield? I mean sure Ogres are probably tough but it is by inflicting their will on the resistant or the unwilling that they are defined, so wouldn't Sword make more sense?

                    Now I am still super excited about this project and I am backing it, but there are parts of this I wish I understood more.

                                                    • Changeling the Lost is a game about having something terrible and horrible happening to you and you are trying to recover from and move on from those horrible events that scarred you.
                                                    • Changeling the Dreaming is a game about having something wonderful and amazing happen to you...and you have to watch as that wonderful thing gets taken from you, not just Banality, but from time itself.
