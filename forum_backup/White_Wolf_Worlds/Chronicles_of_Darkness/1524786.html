    Reality Stalkers 2e update - help??

    10-30-2023, 11:57 PM
    So,

    My Acanthus player wants to become a Reality Stalker, which is sick, they are a cool legacy. However I am having some problems building them into 2e, so I'm here for some potential insights. Here is what I have so far:



    Reality Stalkers
    Sneaks


    Pages 173-176 of The Mysterium and 77 of Nameless and Accursed for Lore


    Parent Path: Mastigos
    Parent Order: The Mysterium
    Prerequisites: Space 2, Mind 1, Larceny 2, one of the following additional skills at 2 dots or higher: Athletics, Streetwise Subterfuge, Survival
    Initiation: Travel through a crack after having accepted the philosophy and teachings of the legacy.
    Ruling Arcana: Space
    Yantras: Cracked glass (+1); A Key (+1); Need one more here
    Oblations: Discovering a secret; Breaking into a building without magic; Traveling long distance without using magic
    ​
    So, let me start out by saying, I hate the attainment in N&A and will not be touching that, no offense to whoever wrote it and I know a lot of people like it, but it doesn't do it for me.

    Anyway, my plan is to make some tweeks to their themes based on how I envision them. The vibe they give in the Mysterium book isn't one of these sneaky people who are using the cracks as spies or thieves, they aren't GotV. Instead I like them as potentially that, but primarily I see them as explorers, going through the fractures of the world to see what is on the other side. Sure, you can use these attainments to steal or to spy, but I enjoy that being secondary to the explorer theme, and my player seemed to be okay with this.

    So, I want to make them Iris mages basically, a legacy of mages that use Space to understand and use all Irises, or at least being able to. A sneak with 0 Death will probably not go through a Iris to the UInderworld, but they should be able to if they want to. Basically, the fractures they see and use is any Iris, I like this idea a lot. The current plan is to use the Space attainments they gain to allow them to see and then interact with Irises, and eventually create their own (this will probably be more limited, you probably will need some Death if you wanna make a fracture to the Underworld, to keep with this example). For the Space attainments I think this is fine, and already have an attainment in mind that will have a double effect, instead of having a secondary arcana avalible, it'll be two Space effects, the ability to notice all Irises, even closed ones, with PMS, and then the ability to learn the key of any Iris they can touch, or could touch if it was physical. After that the attainments will diverge, with Space attainments continuing to mess with Irises, while the optional attainments will become Mind, after all, some Irises take your concious into the Astral, so the Mind optional attainments will grant protection and utility in the Astral, so that the magic can be used there.

    This does leave Death/Underworld and Spirit/Shadow a weak point for them, even though they can access it they can't really do as much there but I don't see a huge problem with that, they are explorers, they will be preping before they go in (hopefully).

    What do you think, does this work? Or does it only sound good in my head and I should get back to the basics before I spend time developing the attainments?

    -TY

                                      Space is primary, Mind is optional. So you should be able to remove it from the prerequisites. The Piercing Glance merit sounds appropriate (Signs of Sorcery page 26), giving them a way to detect concealed Irises and magically concealed areas.

                                      I would like to suggest a second option: deviate entirely and make them masters of perception, focusing on Forces as their secondary Arcana. Here is a rough draft of the Attainments:

                                      Space 1

                                      The Outward & Inward Eye, with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

                                      Forces 1

                                      Receiver with Reach assigned to advanced duration.

                                      Space 2

                                      Intrusion Warning (Guardians of the Veil Order book page 198) with Reach assigned to instant use and advanced duration.

                                      Forces 2

                                      Nightvision with Reach assigned to advanced duration and auto adjusting to extreme stimuli.

                                      Space 3

                                      Locate Object, with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and advanced scale.

                                      Forces 3

                                      Tune In with Reach assigned to advanced duration and adding [Potency] to rolls to parse through the data (custom Reach option).

                                      Space 4

                                      Scrying with Reach assigned to instant use, sensory range and advanced duration.

                                      Forces 4

                                      Zoom In with Reach assigned to advanced duration, seeing for miles and perceiving dust sized particles.

                                      Space 5

                                      Teleport with Reach assigned to instant use and sensory range.

                                      Forces 5

                                      Transmission with Reach assigned to instant use, remote range and encrypting a message.

                                      When examined holistically, this builds up to a very useful toolkit, especially if you give the option to spend a mana for sympathetic range on the ones that are cast at sensory range.

                                      They are capable of seeing for miles, are aware of minute details that others cannot see, perceive spectrums of sound and energy others can't, can see in the dark, are nearly impossible to surprise, can make custom alarms and can set up a scrying network. They can scout out places before a heist, locate anything quickly, setup warnings against guards, move anything and anyone within their miles wide perception, download or upload data remotely and utilize a much greater sensory range in spellcasting.

                                      All of that without hiding anything and focusing on the pursuit of information, all in line with the Mysterium's creed. But their methods are under constant suspicion of being too in line with the Panopticon, admonishing them to think carefully how they use their Attainments. But its a small price to pay, because if there is an Iris, they are primed to find it and, once inside, they can examine it in much richer detail than anyone else, instantly contacting any relevant specialists and securely documenting any key details using their Scrying network and mastery of data transmissions.
