    Thinking about how to update Inferno

    09-19-2016, 08:10 AM
    I've been reading that book again, and I remembered how much I liked it. Yes, the book could have stood to focus on the Inferno itself a little more (just because Hell is ultimately humanity's conspirator doesn't mean it isn't that interesting-Ars Magica's take on the Infernal comes to mind), but Indebted were awesome, Possessed almost as much (because derangement enhancers were always a little too specific to be good).

    So I was thinking-how do I bring the book into 2E, beyond the projects I've already seen?

    The fluff comes first-to go with 2E's more amoral world, I was thinking the Inferno isn't really just evil because they are (driven by Vice, but to-ma-to, to-mah-to), but to make the fact that their Hell is part of, or a function of, the Lower Depths-demons are always lacking something, and they are driven by their desperate hunger for whatever it is they feel they need. Their Testaments are expressions of this hunger-they literally devour and absorb the lore about them they find palatable, changing and evolving to fit the form they were given. This is also about the only way you can deal with them and come out even where you started-you have to keep the deal squarely in the area of "if you change this, you will lose access to what you want" for demons; otherwise, yes, they do betray the spirit on a whim. They can't help but take as much as they possibly can, including power in a working relationship.

    Also, Whisperers-the core idea is good, but they need easier ways of Minor Shaping (waiting for an Integrity roll before it can do anything to communicate in a way other than schizophrenia-voice is boring) and rules for what they do when not attached to someone. Allows greater flexibility and more paranoia. And their relationship with the playable kind of demon (I like the idea that God-Machine angels can't become Diaboli, as they have free will, just obscured...but the Inferno likes to claim Unchained are actually the loyalists' corrupted version, partially out of actual belief, mostly because it lulls Unchained into a false sense of security the Unclean can exploit).

                  "So I was thinking-how do I bring the book into 2E, beyond the projects I've already seen?"

                  So, we start with the standard questions for this topic.
                  1. Are you intending on keeping the 7 Deadly Sins theme? That's no longer a thing in the CofD by nature, so you'd need to build that from the ground up unique to the Inferno.

                  If I am, it's only as Possessed power classifications, and I'll imply it's because the 7 Deadlies are so ingrained to modern consciencess that it's influenced the ever-malleable Inferno. I'm more aiming for the theme being one of desire and hunger-how they both drive and destroy.

                  2. How are you going to define "evil" with the changes to Integrity from Morality? The Inferno needs to feed on something like sin - what metric are you using? How do you intend on avoiding the whole association of mental imbalance = evil theme of the old game?

                  Here's my big twist-embracing the Inferno actually cements Integrity, if you aren't killed or driven nuts by diabolic mind rape before you get experienced. Real evil requires intent and will, and that's a function of a stable mind and strength of will. Thus, the deeper a true diabolist sinks, her patrons guarantee that she will be cold enough to ignore the consequences that might cause a Guilty condition in the future, and if so, she resolves it by denying she ever felt bad in the first place. The idea is that an act of true malice gets a bonus to the Integrity roll, as the ego is actually confirmed by the will to do this thing and not feel regret. Succeeding in the roll grants something like a Hubristic or Zealous condition, as the person either revels in invulnerability or the self-righteousness of the action, and the devils (thank you, Omega) egg her on. This also means diabolists come off as being very coldly rational-just as the deepest level of Hell is ice, so to is the fully initiated malificar's heart, chilled and relentless.

                  3. Whisperers basically act like goetics. Are you going to establish a difference? Because, as things stand, there's no meaningful difference. While on the matter, what would these creatures be "lacking" that makes them different from existing ephemerals? The answer for strix and the Lifeless is obviously life essence.

                  Form. Identity. Really, the self-ironic, given how selfish they are. They exist largely in relationship to what they can grant, what they know, and what they want-nothing about what they are. And they hate it. Hence, Testaments-by describing the devil, you define the devil, and in the process, give him an answer to the question "Who am I?" Whisperers can be considered a devil's truest state, in this case-once in that state, they become little more than their own actions on their host and whatever area they've nested in, living coincidences and inspiration with a thought process. They're different from goetics in that goetics actually do have a true form-an intangible one made of astral energy, but its theirs. Devils don't even have that, naturally.

                  4. Relationship to the Lucifuge? Belial's Brood and Maeljin? Beasts?

                  Keeping it vague, just as in the book. Beasts probably do have a kinship, though-but not to Whisperers, as once again, a Whisperer is more the quality that makes a being Kin rather than Kin itself. Results in some interesting effects when the Begotten discover a Whisperer-possessed.

                  5. You already mentioned angels, but you haven't discussed the Exiled (DtD's equivalent to fallen's Earthbound demons). How about things like strix, fae (and Fae) and qashmal?

                  Like in the original, True Fae and qashmal are too important to reality to be possessed-but I'm thinking about giving them to Diabolize particularly wicked Hedge Beasts and oneiroi (in fact, it could be said that the Succubi changelings are familiar with are Diaboli fae). Strix are explicitly from the Lower Depths already, so they can't be Diabolized for the same reason you can't fall off the floor. Exiles, though...Exiles are something special. After all, they're angels too-but with Abandonment comes wanting things like a new mission, or to return to God. And the devils hear that...

                  6. How will you integrate Social Maneuvering and Investigation subsystems? Specifically, in relationship to the Possessed dealing with their inner demon (ha!) and summoning rituals?
                  Haven't decided. Probably the internal debate between Possessed and soul-buddy deserves a subset of that for coaxing extra power in return for favors out, and summoning rituals...can be ported to Investigation as is, with special Botch mechanics for being swindled by cults looking to sacrifice stupid diabolists.
