              Lately I've been constructing a court whose patron entity is a bullet-train hydra (appropriately, the Court of the Hydra-Line), a very recent court started by a Changeling who escaped from Arcadia when their Keeper tried to forcibly incorporate said bullet-train hydra into its realm. It failed, rather miserably, and the Changeling managed to form a rapport with the hydra. Eventually, he managed to convince it to share its power in exchange for its own form of worship, leading it to growing and the court itself solidifying.

              As for what the hydra is, I'm pondering on it either being a powerful Hob, a Horror that lives in the Hedge, or perhaps something else that simply was picked up by the Gentry and it adapted to the Hedge. What I'm working off of is the idea that the court doesn't just gain power from the Patron itself, but their actions also work to empower it too. Worship, glamour and general actions that enforce its power, which in turn empowers its followers into worshiping it easier.

              I'm still working on the Bargain itself, but I've got a few contracts and the mantle already written up.

              This whole court was inspired by this: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AKSz-BarF2Y

                                  I would say no to a Mummy, for the same reason a Demon, an Archmage or even a Beast Incarnate wouldn't work; they're not only too small of a source, but innevitably they're not going to stick around to freely offer up that level of protection, especially if the Court is to catch on in other Freeholds. The entities that grant protection are grant, inhuman and connected to power in a way that the playale splats simply aren't. In a sense, they are storytelling devices. A Mummy, a Beast Incarnate, an Archmage? They are all singular units with their own statistics, a limited pool of disparate powers and, innevitably, will have a fall from grace and into death.

                                  Now, if you were to make a Bargain with the forces that allow Mummies to come back, or the very embodiment of Demonic Primum, or the Watchtowers (even if that is highly unlikely) or even the Dark Mother herself? That's a source.

                                  But this all does rely on ST fiat, so if you want to do it that way, then go for it. Just remember, Mummies do slumber and I doubt the court would have its power when it is doing so.

                                              Worth remembering: fae little-b "bargains" operate on similar constraints, and the workarounds for changelings are equally applicable to the patron of a Bargain as a means of upholding their end of the deal — the Apex of a Begotten hive very likely has the social capital to support a Court by mere virtue of knowing the right monsters, and the Unchained can make a Pactbound the core of a cult through simple magical documentation, to say nothing of the occult backing Seekers and Arisen have to offer.

                                              Singular Prometheans and lone werewolves are more likely out of the running, but if you have the reach to keep your word then you have the power to make a Bargain. It might not necessarily be a very potent Bargain in terms of what it offers to changelings vis-a-vis keeping the Fae and their agents from hassling you, but as a narrative basis for Contracts and Mantles it's entirely in keeping with the metaphysics that a pact with Merlin Hookhand Imhotep gives a group of changelings more options for their magic.
