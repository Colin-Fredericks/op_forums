                Yes! We finally can talk about it!

                So the era I'm not sure about how people will react to is Wild West. I was very surprised that it got paired with Mage and Changeling, but the more I think about it, the more I think those are nice fits to the themes of the West in those eras. I also hope we see a bit of the Canadian West too, specifically the Red River Rebellion. Also, this era matches the last decades of the Nameless War, which could be interesting. I think that's the same one nofather is talking about, but I'm not sure.

                I am very pumped for Werewolf/Deviant Golden Age of Sci-Fi. I was half-hoping that my suggestion of Mummy would get used, but Werewolf was my second choice for that era and Deviant is the obvious choice to go with it.

                I am beyond happy that Mali made it in and I've paired it with Demon multiple times when suggesting it all the way to DE1's Kickstarer, so the Hunter/Demon pairing is fantastic.

                I'm not going to be the one to spill the beans on Scandinavian Witch Trials, but it is going to be awesome. I was kinda lukewarm on the idea since we already have a lot of Europe and we've covered it in both Dark Eras books (Doubting Souls and likely Light of the Sun), but then the backer paired it with Mummy, which was totally unexpected and awesome. The fact that Monica, Matthew, Meghan then tossed us Mummy fans a bone by pairing it with Geist is just icing on the cake.

                Also, since it was asked for, here is the original era pitch that was NOT used. Once Deviant comes out, I'd love to do a write-up of this one. I've done some minor edits for clarity and grammar.

                The Norwegian Crusade(1107-1113)
                This era focuses on the crusade undertaken by King Sigurd Jorsalfar the Crusader, who took 5000 men and traveled down from Norway to England, Galicia, Al-Andalus, the Balearic Islands, Sicily before finally reaching Outremere. So not only can we look at the societal impact of the crusades in Europe, while also studying this specific campaign, we can also look at the culture of Al-Andalus and the Crusader states. It gives us a wide cultural and historic basis to press into the chapter, but not so big that we are trying to examine one of the biggest events in Medieval history as a whole. We can also look at the Byzantine Empire before it fell and the crusaders journey home through the mainland of Europe. All in all, this gives us an equally exciting and varied plate to study as in the previous pitch.
                As for the gameline i would like to suggest Deviant the Renegades, as i seem to recall that they at least for now do not have any era prior to the early modern one. So through this we can examine the Renegades in a pre modern era, and through the lens of several cultures and perspectives.
                Hawara was unexpected for me and it was one of the eras that I thought people would be pleasantly surprised by. Promethean/Beast was the original combo, then one of the expansion backers tossed Werewolf into the fray.

                Arthur is the other one that I could see putting people's noses a bit out of joint due to the absence of Mage, but I think that means Merlin will be out of focus, which is fine by me. This was originally Changeling/Hunter, then Vampire got added into the fray. It is going to be balling!

                Adding Demon to Light of the Sun was another expansion that was on my shortlist and I really hope we get to find out what Demons are like under Mage Sight.

                French Revolution Mummy speaks for itself. Infrastructure vs Relic Arcitecture. Let's do this!

                Originally posted by Daigotsu Max View Post
                I fist pumped when I saw Promethean as part of the Great War. So happy.

                But also Beast gets a second era so I'm also happy.
                Its a bit bittersweet for me. I REALLY wanted to add Mage to 1001 Nights, but I also really wanted to add Promethean to Great War, so it works out in the end. Ironically, the only era that I didn't correctly predict before Monica announced the TofC to the expansion backers was my own (I didn't try to predict line for Wild West or Mali though). Also, everything except adding Vampire to Arthur was on my shortlist of expansion when the kickstarter ended, so I really have zero grounds to complain about and am overwhelmingly happy with how things have turned out as a result. I think we are going to get some really cool stuff out of Great War too. I specifically wrote in some major crossover points for Ulgan/Forsaken stories and an exploration of the Rivers of Death.

                Also, if anyone is interested, I did some breakdowns, and this is the most even spread of eras it could possibly be. Each gameline got three eras, barring Beast and Deviant, who each got two. Both are relatively new games and Beast has the most upcoming products of any game, so that makes sense. Each game also gets a fairly even number of crossovers. Vampire and Demon get five each, Promethean and Werewolf get four plus an extra crossover with each other, Beast and Deviant gets three, and everyone else gets four. I'm really amazed at how equitable the Devs managed to be with this book.

                                    Originally posted by Suleri Drals View Post
                                    Without wanting to troll or being offensive, I don't see any logic in the choice of these combos. (Non-mage Camelot is especially hurting me but it's not the weirdest one).
                                    Has anyone here any clues on the reasons behind these splats - eras choices? I'm just curious.
                                    I can touch on some of it since I've know about the final contents for the last three weeks, so I've had some time to process. One thing to keep in mind is that some of the choices were dictated by what the backers who added something to the book (like your's truly) wanted to add.

                                    For Arthurian Britannia, there is actually a seven page discussion thread on this very forum where people discuss the merits of each of the three chosen eras, as well as Arthurian Mage. I will let nofather speak to Vampire in this era, since that was his expansion, but I can speak to Hunter and Changeling.

                                    Hunter: Knights of the Round Table is probably all I have to say. Yes, it is anachronistic for the time period, but if you are going to reconstruct the Round Table in a post-Roman Britain, Hunter is definitely the way to go. That would also allow us to see what the Compacts and Conspiracies of late Rome were like, which I for one and very excited for.

                                    Changeling: Changeling has always drawn on fables and I don't know a fable more foundational to a nation than the legend of King Arthur. Camelot itself can work as a strong analogue for a Freehold and Arthur, the city, and Merlin have been previously mentioned in Changeling's 1e books.
