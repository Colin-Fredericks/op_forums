    Think of fictional characters and say what they would be in CofD

    12-24-2014, 07:31 PM
    As the title says. You may include an explanation behind your choice and some stats if you want. It does not have to be a perfect match.

    Ghost Rider (Marvel Comics): a Sin-Eater. His Threshold is Torn and his Archetype is Reaper all the way. He has Rage 5, Shroud 5 and Hearse 5. He has the Pyre-Flame and Industrial Keys. He has a Memento motorcycle. He has a Reaper Merit that gives him +1 on attacks for each 2 points of Morality stat the target has below 7. He has a Flaw that only lets him use his powers when innocent blood is spilled, and once that happens, it's hard for him to stop. He can't harm anyone who he perceives as innocent (usually someone with Morality stat at 8 and higher, or who fulfills their Virtue often). Psyche is 7. Strength, Dexterity, Presence, Firearms, Weaponry and Drive are all at 5 or more. The Penance Stare is a quick Ceremony that requires a grapple and the target to be truly wicked. Its dice pool is Psyche + Presence vs Composure + Powerstat and it temporarily removes all willpower points, the ability to regain willpower and inflicts the Broken Condition.

    The Doctor (Doctor Who): either a True Fae or a Genius. If he is the former, he is a Fairest (Telluric/Oracle/Artist). If he is the later, he is a Staunen and a member of the Navigators. Explanation for the former can be found here: http://www.giantitp.com/forums/showt...-is-a-True-Fae.

    Aang (Avatar: The Last Air Bender): a Mage. While his powers would suggest aptitude for Forces and thus the Obrimos Path, his connection with major Fate related events and his carefree personality suggest Acanthus. He would be a member of the Adamantine Arrow. If you are using Papa Bear's homebrew 25 Paths, Aang is a Magi (http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...1818#post41818).

    Finn (Adventure Time): a Changeling. He would be a Fairest and his kiths would be Apollonian/Soldier (the Apollonian kith can be found here: http://1d4chan.org/wiki/Changeling_H...ths#Apollonian). The Land Of Ooo was where he spent his Durance, and his Court is Summer. He does have Court Goodwill with Autumn due to his fearlessness, as well as his penchant for investigating mysteries and uncovering secrets.

    The Joker (DC Comics): a Slasher, natch. His Undertaking is Maniac. Alternatively, he is a Changeling or even a True Fae that really likes spreading chaos on the Ironside. His Seeming is Darkling and his Kiths are Razorhand/Moonborn/Lurker. If he is a True Fae, his Titles would be himself (Actor), a special gun (Prop) and a group of ogrish thugs (Wisp). Either way, his Morality stat is zero or absent altogether. I suggest using my reworked version of the Lurker kith: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...431#post349431
    Last edited by GibberingEloquence; 12-24-2014, 09:11 PM.

Put dots in Life and Matter for physical prowess, super endurance, regeneration and improving/breaking objects. Also put dots in Forces, Death and Prime for imitating his magical abilities from the games.

                                              The Slender Man: he shows up in woods, his mind is alien to us, shows up using different forms in the stories of different cultures spanning wide temporal gaps, he kidnaps people (often children) and has strange powers. Sounds like a True Fae to me. He would have the following stats:

                                              Virtue and Vice: See below, under Fae Aspects
                                              Concept: Mysterious Boogey Man

                                              Seeming: Darkling
                                              Kith: Mirrorskin

                                              Mental Attributes: Intelligence 5, Wits 4, Resolve 4
                                              Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 3, Stamina 4
                                              Social Attributes: Presence 6, Manipulation 4, Composure 5

                                              If you are using Second Edition rules: Power 7, Finesse 8, Resistance 6

                                              Mental Skills: Academics 2, Computer 1, Crafts 1, Investigation 3, Medicine 1, Occult 3, Science 1
                                              Physical Skills: Athletics (Using Tentacles) 3, Brawl (Claws, Tentacles) 3, Larceny 3, Stealth (Forests) 6, Survival (Hedge) 2
                                              Social Skills: Animal Ken 1, Empathy (Children) 1, Expression 1, Intimidation (Appearing Out Of Nowhere) 5, Persuasion 3, Streetwise 1, Subterfuge 4

                                              Merits: Encyclopedic Knowledge, Eidetic Memory, Disarm (done via tentacles and with Brawl instead of Weaponry), Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Ambidextrous, Fast Reflexes 2, Iron Stamina 3, Harvest (Emotions 5, Dreams 5), Dream Combat 5, Rigid Mask 2, Heavy Weapons (Tentacles) 5, Lethal Mien, Charmed Life

                                              Health: 11 (Size 7)
                                              Willpower: 9
                                              Defense: 4 (7 if you're using GMC)
                                              Initiative Mod: 10
                                              Speed: 13 (Species Factor 6)
                                              Wyrd: 8

                                              Contracts: Forge 5, Mirror 6, Smoke 5, Darkness 6, Moon 6, Illusion 5, Stone 3, Vainglory 3, Control The Elements (Fire) 4, Dream 4, Wild 2, Fleeting Autumn 1, Goblin (Fair Entrance, Fool's Gold, Delayed Harm, The Blessing Of Forgetfulness, Mirror Mirror, Diviner's Madness)

                                              Maximum Glamour/per Turn: 30/8

                                              Frailties: cannot activate Contracts or Slender Walk while within someone's direct line of sight (major taboo), cannot speak (major taboo), must frighten anyone who catches him on a picture or video (minor taboo), if meeting a child he wants to kidnap, must present himself as friendly (minor taboo)

                                              Mien Blessings: The Mercurial Visage (Mirrorskin), Spurious Stature (Gargantuan), Fade Into The Foliage (Woodblood)

                                              Fae Aspects:

                                              Immortal Flesh: True Fae are fantastic beings made of the fabric of dreams and madness. When manifested in the mortal world, they can shrug off the attempts of the mundane to damage them, and even the most destructive of harms will not be as severe to them. Any physical damage that does not come from Cold Iron will deal only bashing to the True Fae. Certain supernatural sources of damage can deal lethal to the Gentry, but only Hand-Forged Iron will deliver aggravated damage. Additionally, they are not affected by Combat Tilts unless they were caused by cold iron, hand-forged iron, or supernatural powers. Their flesh is also immune to natural infirmities. They do suffer wound penalties, but they can't be knocked unconscious by Bashing damage.

                                              Alien Mind: Slender Man regains one point of Willpower during every scene in which he stalks and/or frightens someone. He regains all Willpower points when his actions drive someone mad (using Contracts does not count). He also regains one willpower point every time midnight strikes.

                                              Tentacles: spend 2 Glamour + 1 Willpower point. Gain +2 automatic lethal damage to Brawl, +2 to Defense, +2 to Athletics and +2 to initiate/maintain a grapple. May carry twice as much weight. Once per turn, he may attack with his claws and then spend 1 glamour to also attack with tentacles. Mundane Stealth rolls are halved round down and can't be boosted with willpower. May end this effect at will.

                                              Sickness Aura: remaining within a varying radius of the Slender Man for more than a period decided by the ST requires a reflexive Stamina + Resolve roll. If the roll fails, you take one lethal damage immediately, plus one bashing damage every following day unless you receive medical attention. Other symptoms may include coughing with blood, fever, hallucinations and compulsive behavior. This aura also makes electronic equipment malfunction according to the ST.

                                              Slender Walking: the Slender Man may reflexively teleport to any location within Wyrd x 25 yards by spending 1 glamour and rolling Wyrd + Stealth. To teleport toward a victim he can't perceive, he must spend 2 Glamour and sense the target’s fear by rolling Investigation + Wyrd vs Resolve + Composure + equipment (maximum distance is determined by the ST). If the victim gets as many successes as Slender Man or more, he cannot Slender Walk toward that person for a period decided by the ST. Doing research on the Slender Man or thinking too much about him imposes penalties on this roll. If using either type of teleportation for a surprise attack, a -1 penalty is imposed to Perception rolls to detect him, and an Exceptional Success increases the penalty to -3.

                                              Titles: Slender Man (Actor), Garden Of Shadows (Realm) and Master Proxies (Wisp)
