          I'd say it depends on the number of vampires involved, their willingness to involve innocents and/or keep a low profile so that hunters don't start popping up, and their power obviously.

          It could very well look like your average gang wars, except with fewer corpses and more pieces of clothe full of of ash. Well, fewer corpses that you would expect, cause you might find some other corpses in seemingly unrelated places, most of them lacking a lot of blood for some reason. Blood becomes an important resource and when you need a lot to heal and fight, you can't be as careful. There would probably be more feeding accidents : dead people, people reporting being assaulted and bitten, maybe getting sick left and right (from vampires who don't take time anymore to check on their victim's health history and become carriers). I'd imagine that's if we're talking about generally low level vampires who are somewhat trying to preserve their humanity.

          Some vampires could be willing to start enslaving mortals left and right to build themselves a small army and this is where things could start getting really nasty. The gang war involving unknown groups of like 50 people now involves whole known gangs. Then, someone has the smart idea of putting a blood bond on the chief of police, the mayor and so on. You suddenly have a huge number of random police brutality and a big search for a number of drug stashes (rival vampire havens) during the day. That doesn't seem like a good recipe for keeping one's humanity, though, so hard to say how long that would last until the vampires responsible for this to become a draugr, which, if it is discovered (which is usually not too hard), might lead their allies to turn on them.

          Also, a war is the best occasion to slip in the occasional diablerie, there's another downward spiral.

          Lastly, the war being visible might be a good thing. Pushing the rival side to more and more extreme actions is a good way to get hunters to target them. Let them fight it out, lay low for a month or two and become the default new ruling faction.

                          Originally posted by Basic View Post
                          Its more of a shadow war with some hot spots. Right now the Invictus vs the circle of crone and they both are strong.
                          Economic downturn as small businesses collapse, higher rates of murder (or at least death as murders can be disguised as natural causes / suicide / missing / etc.), sporadic vandalism and property damage. Basically all the symptoms of vampires sabotaging each others assets whether they be financial, property or people. General instability as people fight over control of tactical assets too - scandals deposing chief of police in favour of a new one every other month. Also people trying to build up new assets - a particularly cultish martial arts school for example (the sensai is a ghoul), a new firm of bouncers muscling in. A Cold War is about out-threatening your enemy. So look for instability in society, rapid transitions and a general lack of co-operation (e.g. you'd expect politician X and politician Y to find common ground and share information, but they don't because they're controlled by rival vampires).

                          When the war gets hot, you can turn the above up a few degrees as power shifts are faster, businesses implode in scandals and the common people will probably get a general feeling that the entire society is hopelessly corrupt and messed up. You list Shadow War as the counter-part to Hot War. I don't think that's the right axis. I think it goes: Cold War - Hot War (i.e. Cold War is maneouvering and cats paw whilst avoiding provoking an all-out response, a Hot War is where real force is deployed to try and depose your enemy), and Shadow War - Open War (i.e. how much we try to conceal our actions vs. how much we no longer care). You can have a Cold Shadow War or a Hot Shadow War, or a Hot Open War. It's a 2x2 matrix.

                          Vampires are obviously going to want their war to be a Shadow war as much as possible. But the more desperate they become, the more the temptation will be to discard secrecy where it's holding them back. It takes time to set up all the shell companies to hide your secret ownership of a dozen local businesses, it's not easy to recruit muscle when you're a faceless unknown in the shadows, infiltrating someone's secure haven in the day without attracting attention is more tricky than driving a JCB through their bedroom wall. A war can threaten the masquerade because going Open can give you powerful but dangerous advantages. It's a little like a game of brinkmanship - who dares drive closest to the edge? If you've ever seen the Netflix series Daredevil, the first season, recall Wilson Fisk's slow and steady manipulation in the shadows. And then (minor spoiler)

                          how he out-maneouveres them with Vanessa's support and faith that others will see him as she does by suddenly going public and changing the game completely.


                          Imagine the way your players will reel if a vampire opponent, especially an elder, pulled that stunt on them half-way through a campaign just as they thought they were about to ferret said elder out into the sunlight. It wouldn't necessarily have to be a break in the Masquerade to be clear, anything along that theme.

                          I hope some of this helps.

                              You also mention that it's Invictus vs. Circle of the Crone. These have quite different operating environments if we're going to be stereotypical. So they can target each other's "habitats" for want of a better term. For example, in the UK in the late Eighties, rumours of Satanic Ritual Abuse flew around the UK leading to children being separated from their families, paranoia, very dubious psychological assessments (for example, subjecting young children to leading questions inducing false memory syndrome). It was a Daily Mail style witchhunt. With influence in the media and police, Invictus could relatively easily stoke up such rumours in their area. The consequence would be the whole community on edge and keeping watch for strange meetings in the woods, children going missing, etc. Even people buying too many candles will attract attention if the community is scared enough. Invictus can hit the mattresses (Mafia term) whilst the Circle are forced to tip-toe around on edge the whole time because the city paper is running stories about peadophile satanists dancing naked in the woods. Just for example.
