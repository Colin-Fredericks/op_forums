                Life in Yeomal Seoncho Era. Part 1
                This was a time of rapid transition. End of Goryeo was filled with invasion, corruption and starvation, while Beginning of Joseon indeed brought many reforms, including redistribution of lands for the peasants, powerful armies and cleansed politics. Of course, this changes did not happen at once. It took nearly 100 years. So in this section, I will divide every part with things they have in common, then Goryeo and Joseon. I will not question basic systems of this era. The ideal of nation as a centralized collection of self-sustained small communities has its flaws, some of them fatal,
                but this is not a history criticism.

                Local life and rulers
                Medieval Korea is an agricultural nation. Vast majority of population are farmers who grows rice, millet, and beans. Crops like potatoes, red pepper, and tobacco has not yet been imported to Korea. Both men and women contribute to farming, although men do more hard work, and women typically engage in secondary business like weaving. Trade is very primitive, and despite many attempts to make an official metal currency, Korea's de facto currency is rice. There are one or two markets in each city, but most of them don't import or export goods to other cities, so many regions are self-sustained. Most of the trading in rural towns are barters. Local nobles fulfill their exotic needs by presents, sometimes working also as bribes. Trading presents will be the main part of nobility's economic system until 17th Century, when advanced markets will emerge.

                End of Goryeo
                Although the government sends governors to each provinces, their powers are limited and only acts as military leaders. The local aristocrat families own vast lands acquired by both legal and illegal means, essentially making their own fiefdoms. Many farmers farm by borrowing their lands, in return, they have to pay a percentage of their earnings to the landlord. This semi-taxation rate is insanely high, and many peasants willingly turn themselves into slaves of the nobles. Others leave their homes, becoming wanderers, beggars, laborers and even bandits.
                Eventually, local landlords will even make private armies.

                Start of Joseon
                Because of the reforms lands will be granted to commoners, many becoming self-employed farmers. The government sends magistrates to each town, and their powers are indeed absolute except superiors, Ombundsmans, and censors which are all from central government. Local nobles will become little more than servitors of the magistrates, although some successfully participates in central politics and retains its local powers. It would be a lie to say all of these magistrates were full of integrity. Since Joseon exempted its ruling class from taxes, in return they were paid with very little wages, barely enough to survive, let alone to maintain dignity. So many of them did took some "gifts" from local citizens. However the corruption was bearable, and there were moral officers who would not touch the peasants even when their family was starving.

                Central Govenment
                As East Asian political system abandoned feudalism long time ago, there are no such things as barons and counts. Only the central government, which is ruled by the king, has absolute power. There are two ways to get a post at the government, one is passing the national examination, the other is called Eumseo(음서), which is by having a high raking officer as your brother, father, uncle, grandfather, or father-in-law, although in this way, your maximum position is limited. There are few things learned men can do beside applying for civil service, so many people study hard for exams. This is a tradition that still defines Korea today.

                End of Goryeo. Capital City: Gaegyung(Now Gaeseong, North Korea)
                After being defeated by the Mongols, there appeared two another ways to get a post: Flattery to the Yuan dynasty, and bribing your way to it. King Gongmin's reform somewhat made it impossible, but still their are many unsuited officials in this era. As chaos reigns, King Woo, Chang, and Gongyang was powerless, and government was ruled by few powerful men, the first being Yi Inim, the second being Yi Sunggye. Governmental system are a mess, filled with temporary offices made permenant.

                Start of Joseon. Capital City: Hanseong(Now Seoul, South Korea)
                National examination is highly praised, and Eumseo is limited to father and grandfather and is disdained among officials. Systems are redefined elegantly. The government is split into three major bodies, Uijeongbu(의정부, Discussing and Deciding Department), where three chancellors decide the general policy of the nation, and Yukjo(육조, Six Ministries), where public servants execute those policies, and Samsa(삼사, Three Offices) where young censors, inspectors, and advisers freely criticize those policies and officials. The king oversees everything, and tell his subjects his thoughts as final say resides with him, but cannot force his opinion since that would result in criticism and complaints. A national historian records everything, and once a king dies, those memos are made into Veritable Records, which the king is absolutely forbidden to look, and only his subjects can search for it on special occasions.

                Academics, Religion, and Philosophy.
                Academics is highly encouraged in Korea, as Confucius said, it is learning that makes other virtues worthwhile. Poetry and painting, writing essays(but never novels) and enjoying music and nature are considered basic accomplishments of the nobility. Science is advanced, making very accurate calendars and calculating eclipses bypassing medieval Europe. But lack theories and experiments, making it just tools of trade. Buddhism in Korea is Mahayana Buddhism, a sect that focuses on working towards benefits of everyone, not only people who are trying to become Buddha. Neo Confucianism starts to spread. What is Neo Confucianism exactly? Well, Confucianism is just basic ethics. Be loyal for your lord, be kind to people, study and practice, etc. Neo Confucianism adds metaphysics to it, explaining how principle of the universe becomes mandate of heaven. Korean mythology, although lacking central belief system, is widely spread. Buddhist temples accepting some of its deities.

                End of Goryeo.
                Buddhism is very prominent, Gaegyung has more than 400 temples in its area. However, temples are as corrupt as local landlords, owning mountains and rivers, lending food by extremely high interests to poor farmers. Neo Confucianism is studied, but is not yet state religion. The nation accepts pagan religion too, as governors duty was to visit holy sites of Korean mythology and perform rituals.

                Start of Joseon.
                Buddhism and Korean Mythology is persecuted, calling it "mere superstition". Jung Dojeon wrote on his book 'Ravings of Buddhism(불씨잡변)': [A foolish monk says "If there are no hell, how can we stop people from being evil?". I answer "A gentlemen does not do good on hope of rewards, he does not refrain from committing evil on fear of punishment, he only does what is right, because it is right."] You could say this predates state atheism by 500 years. Neo Confucianism becomes state religion/philosophy, and other thoughts are considered heretic. While studying those were not illegal, they could cause social problems and political criticisms.

                    Life in Yeomal Seoncho Era, Part 2

                    Classes
                    Legally, there were only two classes in both Goryeo and Joseon. Freemen and Slaves: freemen are people, slaves are treated as half-human, half-thing. Although slave's life was filled with cruelty and injustice, it wasn't atrocious as there were few heavy labor besides farming. Many peasant chose to live as slaves than suffering exploits and burdening military service. Freemen are divided into three classes: Nobles, Petty nobles/Middlemen, Commoners. Although this isn't legal division, it is cultural and influence daily lives.

                    End of Goryeo
                    Classes are separated firmly. Although there are some cases of exception, a noble's son will be treated as a noble whenever they go, a petty noble's power only works in his region, a commoner cannot dream of entering aristocracy. Furthermore, there are villages called Hyang, Bugok, or So(향, 부곡, 소) that its villagers are treated as slaves from birth.

                    Start of Joseon
                    Although class exists, movement between classes is more fluid. Many prosperous commoners pass the National Examination final round, making their direct family noble(in Joseon Era, it is called Yangban(양반)) for four generations. If noble's great-grandfather, grandfather, father, and himself do not pass the National Examination first round, they are treated as commoners. Petty nobles are stripped of their privileges and become just wealthy commoners. A new class called Middlemen emerges, they serve as experts in fields like mathematics, medicine, law, and translation. Hyang, Bugok, and So are repelled, but monks, performers, shamans, butchers are treated as slaves legally, though they have freedom.

                    Names
                    All nobility have their names that consists of a surname with one or two(usually one) Chinese letter and a personal name consisting one or two letters, both men and women. However, a women's name is seldom mentioned out side her house. It is considered extremely rude to call someone by name. It is used only officially or by someone superior than you, like parents or king. People use Ja, an official nickname given by parents, elder relatives or teachers and Ho, an unofficial nickname given by oneself or friends.
                    Commoners do not have surname and typically called by where they live+sequence of birth, like 'Third son of shoe seller", or they have pure Korean names, usually vulgar.
                    A king's name is treated heavily, even using its Chinese letter is illegal, referring to king by his name is mutiny(Though it is common for kings to change their name to one exotic letter, in order to prevent inconveniences). A ruling king is called Jusang(主上, high lord) or Geumsang(今上, current highness), or simply his majesty. After king dies, his subject and his successor discuss about Siho(시호, 諡號, Posthumous Name), and after that they are called by their posthumous name.

                    End of Goryeo.
                    Yuan gives Goryeo posthumous name, and forbids use of Jo and Jong, since they can be only used by independent countries. Gongmin is given by Yuan, and Gongyang is given by Joseon. Woo and Chang are their own names, since they are treated as illegitimate.

                    Start of Joseon
                    Taejo, Taejong, and Sejong are all names according to the siho rules.

                    Languages
                    Both Goryeo and Joseon use Chinese character Hanja(한자, 漢子, hanzi) since Hangeul is not yet invented(although it is soon after Yeomal Seoncho). There are two ways to write. The first is called Idu(이두), which borrows only sound(sometimes meaning) from Chinese characters and follows Korean grammar. Commonly used by somewhat learned commoners and Petty nobles, it is treated as a frivolity to most high nobles. The second is Hanmun(한문, 漢文), which writes Chinese letters according to archaic Chinese literary grammar. Most commoners and slaves are illiterate, unable to read but most simple Chinese letters.
                    Mechanically, anyone with one dot in academics could read and write Idu fluently, and can roll for understanding approximate meaning of Hanmun. Two dots in academics grants a character ability to read and write Hanmun with ease, and can roll to discover a text's authenticity, era, nationality, and style by carefully analyzing its usage of words, phrases and grammars.

                    Foreign Relations
                    Medieval Korea's diplomatic policy is Sadaegyorin(사대교린, 事大交隣), serving the powerful, befriending neighbors. Powerful means Han Chinese, although in dire situations, other "barbarians" like Cathay, Manchu, and Mongol could be served. One thing to note is serving does not mean Korea is China's colony. Korea, Tibet, Uighur, and Vietnam are independent countries, only accepting China's superiority(despite everything told by the current Chinese Communist Party). Neighbors mean Manchurian, Ryukyu(currently Okinawa of Japan) and Japan. Because of Yuan empire's multicultural aspect, Korea in this era has lots of contacts with places that never will be again mentioned until 19th Century. Both Goryeo and Joseon government accepts all foreigners and tries to use their abilities, but often they are faced with discrimination among the public.

                    Foreigners that could be found in Korea without difficulty: Chinese, Japanese, Manchus, Mongolian(Until Goryeo)
                    Foreigners that could be rarely found in Korea: Mongolian(After Joseon), Tibet, Uighur, Ryukyu.
                    Foreigners that could or could not be existing in Korea: Turks, Ainu(now Hokkaido of Japan), Thais, Vietnamese, Champas and other Southeast Asian nationals, Arabians, Persians.
                    Of course, European or African nationals could theoretically exist, since Marco Polo visited Yuan in 13th Century, but no known records talk about it. The first official westerner to visit Korea is Portuguese mercenaries hired by Ming in Imjin wars in 1592. And the first official westerner to live in Korea is a Dutch Jan Janesz Weltevree in 1627.

                    Life of Women
                    Patriarchy and misogyny rules medieval days, as women are treated as second to men, and are told that their life exists to marry and nurture, and help your husband. Still, life of women is surprisingly free in this era, compared to the puritan society of late Joseon. Although women cannot become officials and are treated unequally, they could own their private property and move wherever they want. Many women expressed deep piety in Buddhism, if not outright becoming monks. They often traveling to many famous temples all by their own, something that even Buddhism-hating Joseon dynasty did not touch. Women typically have less education than men, but they still could write poems that is accepted better than her husband's, study obscure Neo Confucian texts, and debate with men equally. Many women payed much attention towards education of her young, something that has became a Korean tradition. Many books praise only mothers, not fathers for raising her sons to become loyal officials and daughters to paragon of filial piety, boasting "and who fools dare to say only men contributes to the country!"

                    End of Goryeo
                    Sexual scandals are common, although frowned upon, most of them are not punished. Divorce and remarriage is even more common than modern South Korea, noble women marrying even three or four times. A Chinese diplomat chided "People of Goryeo look at marriage as if were nothing more than contracts, how pathetic." While modern perspectives view this freedom as a virtue, it has its downsides. Many women from humble families get divorced so the husband can remarry with a woman of high status. Fathers do not intervene in education and usually its up for the mother to nurture her siblings. Concubines are rare, according to Chinese texts: "In Goryeo, one official tried to make concubines legal, as the word got spread, women came marching down the roads. An old hag, their leader, pointed at him and said 'there goes the old impotent who begged the king to get concubines!' The other officers were so afraid of their wives, so no more words came out on that matter."

                    Start of Joseon
                    Because of Neo Confucian virtues, women's freedom is restrained, although some could argue that they are more protected in return. Divorcing your wife who endured through hardship, to remarry some noble women is basically saying: "Hey! Censors! Free criticism day! I don't care about my political career anymore!" Early Joseon discouraged divorce without reasons among nobility. Women are free to remarry once her husband dies, and commoners do not have such restrictions. As father's, teacher's and nation's role in education is emphasized, mothers do not have the sole glory of educating their children well, but still they are praised. Concubines are treated as normal, although many nobles cannot afford one.

                    Be creative when creating female characters. Player characters are exceptional, and there are indeed a lot of great women in this era of chaos, both traditionally and untraditionally. Supernatural society is more equal than its normal counterparts, albeit some discrimination still exists. Even when it comes to stereotypical "helper of the husband", this two queens make a sharp contrast.

                    Exceptional People -Queen Wonkyung and Queen Soheon
                    Wonkyung is the wife of Taejong Yi Bangwon. When Taejong attempts the First strife of Princes, she ordered her family to support him, preparing weaponry and manpower. By hearing false rumor that her husband was lost, she grabbed a spear and said "Rather than wait for the execution I will die fighting." It took nearly all her servants to hold her down. Once Yi Bangwon became king, she became jealous of other concubines and had a lot of fight with her husband. This only worsened after Taejong mercilessly executed her brothers, fearing they would play with the crown prince. Taejong considered divorce, but stopped by his brothers and his sons, telling him he broke her heart enough.

                    Soheon is the wife of Sejong Yi Do. After succeeding his throne to Sejong, Taejong again purged Soheon's family. The pregnant queen was forced to watch in tears as her father was killed, and her mother made slave. Nevertheless, she helped Sejong with his many works, often acting as co-contributors, especially in Hangeul. She managed cocubines and daughters-in-law flawlessly, herself having eight sons and two daughters. Although thought by many people as passive, she commanded entire fire fighting efforts when Sejong was away, while being 9 month pregnant with her sixth son.

                        So, I wrote my example Leviathan character(sad that no one replied). I am planning to make four Leviathan characters(two pro-Goryeo, one neutral, one pro-Joseon) and four Awakened characters(one pro-Goryeo, one neutral, one pro-Joseon), and two other supernaturals. This is not a final draft, and could be changed with your ideas.

                        Hwahwa

                        "We gisaengs aren't called talking flowers for no reason, Chancellor. If you wished your secrets to be secrets, why didn't you play with normal flowers instead?"

                        Background: Hwahwa does not remember her original name, nor her childhood. Only thing she remembers is she was grasping on to the empty stomach when entering this Gibang. The person who brought her said she had "demon-like beauty" and "will profit her master tenfold" once she was bought. She was raised there, her master and senior Gisaengs forcing her to learn various entertainment skills, such as poetry, music, songs, dancing, and some Confucian texts by starvation and beating. Quickly enough, she showed exceptional beauty and skills, making her one of the most famous entertainers. She named herself Hwahwa, which could be interpreted as many ways, ince 'Hwa' could mean sixteen different Chinese characters.

                        At one banquet, she had the most peculiar experience as she half-joked to one of her clients "Such bravado, if you truly think I am beautiful, maybe you should buy me and make me your wife?". The client stormed out, while others, including Hwahwa herself, thought he was offended, thinking she was taunting him. But one day later, words got spread he was selling his fortunes to pay her ransom. He was stopped by his friends and relatives, but it didn't end there. Blowholes started appearing on her neck, her skin turning gray and smooth, she could hear sounds that could not be heard before. Terrified by her change, Hwahwa faked illness and refused to serve as gisaeng.

                        But that was only the beginning. While sleeping, she was abducted by royal agents. When she woke up, she was middle of a room deep inside the palace, there she was told by invisible voices the history of Leviathans, who she is, and what she could be. And that she will marry a royal family, and will bear many fruitful Leviathan children for the dynasty. It was for her own good, they said. Would you waste your life living as some entertainer, who will likely become some whore or concubine, or become part of the noble family? Such an easy decision. To their surprise, Hwahwa managed to escape the palace after spending three nights with the hideous husband, then made her way to gibang again, this time willing.

                        From time on, many people witnessed strange things. Her master succumbed to madness, and left Hwahwa in charge of the gibang. Many officials who visited their disappeared every few days, returning with always gleaming look on their face. Gisaengs working on her gibang asked questions lot more, a harmless curiosity appearing as innocent. Leviathans and a few others know the truth, Hwahwa is controlling entire politics. She had made many important people her beloved, and uses cultists of her to gather information that only she could wield it, which she uses to make her cult even greater. She is now meeting with Yi Sunggye and his people often, even agreeing to provide "special entertainments" to them. Some say she is just trying to be friendly with new powers. Others think it is not the case, and say that she hates Goryeo for everything they done to her, and will stop at nothing to its downfall.

                        Description: Hwahwa is at her early twenties at the year 1388. She is strikingly beautiful, having long black hair, a curved body line, white skin. small nose and red lips. She does not make rash movements nor does she shows her emotion directly. She is both capable for acting as a coy girl unaware of her beauty, or as flirting seducer who toys men's minds. She is extremely well educated, quoting old poems and proverbs when speaking to scholars. She only attends to important banquets, but even then only provides entertainment, and will not agree to any sexual acts. To those who know her as Leviathan and master manipulator, she maintains the same attitude. Only failing her uttermost valuable plans, or confronting her with dire physical force will she show her true nature as a megalomaniac and a broken girl who seeks for revenge. Her apotheosis form is a giant dolphin. She slowly transforms from human, to somewhat slithery and gray skinned human, to a hybrid of woman and dolphin.

                        Storytelling hints: Hwahwa is an outcast among other Leviathans, not that she cares. To the cohort that is loyal to Goryeo, she is the key person that stops united front of the Tribe. Strike her down, and anti-Goryeo Leviathans will lose their leader. Leave her alive, then she will sow discord into the Tribe, handing the throne to her new allies. To the cohort that tries to found Joseon, she is an enigmatic supporter who has her own thoughts. In truth, she is neither loyal to either of them, as she views every politician that failed to save Goryeo's people from suffering as unworthy. She plans a future that none of the pain that happen to her could happen to any other person in Korea, and to do that, she must be in control over the king. Yi Sunggye's advisors suspects this, and does not let Yi Sunggye or his sons near her. If someone crosses her path, she will first spread rumors and stories to make their social life hell, then using her cult to hunt and bring them to her, finally destroys their minds with her power, submitting them to her will.

                        There are some things that even her iron will and master schemes do not protect her. She is indeed afraid of invisible voices from walls that once kidnapped her, leaving one door open whenever she sleeps. She feels deep connection to her gisaengs, and will act emotionally if one of them is involved. Finally, she is a poor fighter, if someone could penetrate her social defenses and henchmen, she could be easily slain in battle.

                        Story hooks:
                        1. A rumor is that Hwahwa is impregnated by one of Yi Sunggye's son. Perhaps it is the opposite, that Hwahwa impregnanted him, using her power as Leviathan. Fearing what this could mean, Jung Dojeon personally sent assassins, which all turned up dead. Now he turns to player characters, asking them to find out whether this rumor is true or false. And if possible, exterminate her.
                        2. One men causes a lot of commotion in front of the gibang, saying that he is heartsick of Hwahwa, asking her to meet him. Knowing this is because of the wake, Hwahwa lets him in and haves him as her cultist. But even for beloved, he is acting unusual indeed. He turns up every day, sending flowers to her(which is very rude thing to do to gisaeng, given the fact that they are called talking flowers), insulting other gisaengs for sharing her attention. Why is this happening? Hwahwa asks player characters to investigate. The answer is he is a demon who is hired by Yi Bangwon in disguise, and faking his submission to gather information.
                        3. When having drinks with scholars, a young men beats Hwahwa at a poetry duel, and he quickly becomes a notable person in Gaegyeong. Amused, Hwahwa tries to make him as her own beloved, but quickly discovers that he is already a beloved of a Leviathan, who is a Goryeo princess. Angered, Hwahwa plans a devious plot to snatch him from her, which is something that violates the rules of the Tribe of Korea.

                                OK. This is an another sample character, middleage mutant samurai turtle.

                                Gwigok Gamyeong(龜谷 家明, Kameya Ieaki)
                                "Ilsohyeonmyung(一所懸命), to dedicate one's life to one obligation. I prefer reciting it with my native tongue, isshokenmei."

                                Background: Gwi-gok Ga-myeong, original name Ka-me-ya Ie-aki, was born in Sakaido(now Kyushu), Japan 1351. From outside, House Kameya was known as an ordinary warrior family like any other, but in truth, they were carriers of the Bahamutan Strain, forming secluded cults. As Iekai was born, he was surrounded with worships and adoration, his family spent countless fortunes and efforts to educate and nurture him, granting him whatever the young Leviathan wants. To some, to much attention spoils the child, not for Iekai, he understood privileges comes with a heavy duty, learned literary education tirelessly, practiced hard on martial arts, and became a humble and likable person.

                                As he grew up, Japan was getting through a Nanboku-cho Era, as there were two Tennos, each claiming that they were rightful ruler of one lineage of ten thousand years(万世一系). Although nobles of Kyushu supported Southern dynasty, they did not waste the opportunity that Bakufu of Japan lost control of Sakaido, and many local rulers turned into pirates, pillaging Japan and other countries. Seeing no virtue in piracy, Iekai persuaded his family to refuse the order of pirating Kyoto. But they could not resist the flow of history, as Northern court attacked Kyushu and drove out Southern court sympathizers. He was forced to flee with only his body, leaving his beloved and family to die, and sailed to Tsushima(Korean name Daemado).

                                The remnants of the Southern Courts attacked Goryeo, and Ieaki, seeing no choice but to conquer Goryeo and make it their new home, accepted reality. The Goryeo's military was weak and underfunded, its captains being corrupt and incompetent. He easily won battle after battle, and he thought their victory was near, until he met Choi Yeong. This white haired, battle-hardened general of sixties defeated the young Kameya Ieaki, and he was held captive. As Ieakai prepared for death, a women in black hood knocked his prison door, asking if he want to live. She told him a great crisis is approaching to Goryeo's Leviathans, and the Tribe could use some extra hand. Grateful that they are offering their enemy and captive an opportunity, also seeing that he had nowhere else to go, Kameya Ieaki accepted it. He is now known as Gwigok Gamyeong.

                                There are now strange tales going on in Gaegyeong, as people are turning up dead, wounds showing they were attacked by a giant katana. The government says those are nonsense, but many people swear they saw a giant turtle leaping walls, followed by shadowy men who wield Japanese weaponry. Mages and other supernaturals who is known to be pro-Yi Sunggye, or those who knows to oppose the regime will be visited by Gamyeong and his cult. He has failed to save one dynasty, and he will not fail to save another.

                                Description: Gwigok Gamyeong dresses like ordinary Korean to prevent hassle, and it is difficult to distinguish him as Japanese without a detailed look(only for Koreans, as they will say "I dunno, but something about him doesn't look Korean"). A decent looking man in his late thirties, he is a quiet person who does not show his abilities nor his knowledge without being asked. His answers are often polite but short. His Korean is fluent enough, but he has an undeniable Japanese accent(a Miami accent could be fitting for English users). When questioned he does not deny he is Japanese, saying he is deeply moved by the nation's generosity to him, and expresses his deep loyalty to Goryeo. He lives in a mansion, which belongs to the royal family just outside Gaegyung. He practices calligraphy and swordsmanship there, and some of his beloved living there as well. When carrying out a mission from the Tribe, he dresses in black Korean clothes, but wields his heirloom katana. His apotheosis is a giant leatherback sea turtle, his hybrid form looking like the protagonists of 'Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles".

                                Storytelling hints: Gwigok Gamyeong is the fist of the Tribe. When there is order for execution, he carries out the mission. When dire threat approaches, he is sent to slay it. A combat pragmatist and trained tactician before the time of Bushido, he uses various methods to get victory, using traps, deception and surprise attacks. Although he will refrain from killing the surrendered, children, Buddhist monks, and members of the Tribe, he will do so if it seems necessary. He complements his mediocre talents by his rigorous training and almost inhuman feats.
                                To the pro-Goryeo Player Characters, he is a valuable ally and a key asset, who is devoted to Goryeo dynasty to the almost point of selflessness. To the pro-Joseon Player Characters, he is the last supernatural bodyguard of the Goryeo dynasty, eliminating him will mean they could use violence to get what they want. If Gamyeong thinks someone could threaten the Goryeo dynasty, he first visits them and sends out his warning in well-mannered way. Second time, he uses his cult to cause the mood of fear and anxiety over them. If they still do not back down, he will attack them personally.

                                There are few ways to put Gwigok in corner. First, his Japanese ethnicity arouses suspicion and fear among Koreans, even to the Tribe. It is only magnified to the fact his cult contains of many surrendered Waegus. Using it could put him in dangerous situations, fabricating or finding some evidence could launch an official execution order. Second, Japanese pirates are still out there, and many consider Gamyeong as a traitor to their own kind, and it could cause serious trouble in his cult. Finally, defeating in battle could count, but he is a fearful foe even when attacked in surprise, and can endure damage by his power as a Bahamutan. When cornered in his own house, he will wear full Japanese battle armor and will wield multiple weapons, fighting until death.

But if Gamyeong learns about this, the betrayal will surely make him reconsider his allegiance. What do the Cohort do?
                                2. Gamyeong is very proud of his katana Gyakuchou(逆潮, 역조, Yeokjo, Riptide), which is a heirloom of House Kameya.                                             This is my third example Leviathan, who is the current leader of the Korean Tribe.

                                            Princess Hyonyeong
                                            "Goryeo is my home. It never had been a good home to me, and you will not see me admitting I have many happy memories of it. But still, Goryeo is my one and only home."

                                            Background: Princess Hyo-nyeong(which is a title, not a name.) was born in as a daughter of king Chunghye. Her father was a reveler whose hobby was to rape relatives and subjects' wives. His lascivious acts continued to the point his subjects asked the Yuan Emperor for permission to change the king. After her father was dethroned, young Hyonyeong watched her sister Jangnyeong sent to Yuan to marry their prince, her two elder brothers take the throne but both surviving less than five years. Although the successor Gongmin ruled fair and square, Hyonyeong always had a piece of fear whenever she saw him. In the small room at the deepest palace she submitted herself to books, reading history and scripture fervently.

                                            One day, she found a ragged text which contained the history of Goryeo's foundation. It was marked [restricted] by the seal of Yuan inspectors. Out of curiosity, she ripped the seal and looked at the contents. What wonder she found! Story of Wang Gun, who was a son of Wang Ryung the master merchant of the west sea, and a grandson of a great pirate Jak Jegeon who brought fear in the entire ocean. Legacy of the Founder, who sailed behind his enemies lines and surprise attacked them from behind, creating a secondary battlefront. Best of all, they could do that because they were more than human. They were Leviathans.

                                            As Hyonyeong learned about this, her heart surged with great pride. It was only weeks after she entered the metamorphosis. She quickly learned that Gongmin was a Leviathan too, and her feeling of fear is just a mask for territory instinct. She experimented with her powers and tried to improve her cult. It is unsurprising that she gained great respect of the Tribe, both by her heritage and her skills. Hyonyeong was the first to warn the Tribe of Gongmin's madness, the first to notice dark clouds spreading over the Tribe, and the first to take action about it. She gathered Leviathan, Lahmasu, and mortal allies by persuasion, lies, and force. Gongmin's death shook her, and as she inherited the role of the sole leader of the Tribe from Gongmin, the more and more brutal and manipulative she became.

                                            Now, the Tribe is beginning to worry she might turn into Ophion or Typhon, but none addresses such fears out loud. Hyonyeong knows that Yi Sunggye is the would-be usurper, and she will destroy anyone, including the Leviathans, who dares to slow her attacks. At one gathering, she cried out loud there is no such thing as neutrality. You are either with her, the Tribe, the Goryeo dynasty, the true Confucian way, or you are with the heretical enemy.

                                            Description: Princess Hyonyeong is a woman of late forties. She is unmarried and has no plans for doing so. Even by just her looks, she spread the aura of majesty and charisma. In mortal society, she resides in her mansion, but frequently gets visits from nobles, scholars, and monks. She appears to be generous and tolerant, giving large gifts to all her visitors and listening to thoughts that do not match with her ideas. But she views them nothing more as chess pieces to move and will not hide it to fellow Leviathans. To the Tribe, she acts like a dictator who thinks herself as a necessary evil. She will not accept disobeys from fellow Leviathans, and will demonstrate her martial and social prowess to her opposition. She is possesses almost absolute knowledge thanks to her channels, cults and contacts. Her apotheosis is a giant sea serpent.

                                            Storytelling hints: There are few, if any things that Hyonyeong will hesitate to sacrifice for Goryeo. As a confucian, she understands that Goryeo needs reforms, but also is aware that those reforms could be used by Yi Sunggye faction to gain influence. Being the matriarch of the whole(well, at least nearly whole, since some Leviathans like hwahwa do not accept her as their leader) she wields immense power both in mortal and supernatural societies. Those who oppose her will have to prepare for countless attacks from famed scholars, ambushes by assassins, and direct attacks by veteran members of the Tribe. To the pro-Goryeo player characters, she is a dictator that needs to survive, since there are no members of the Tribe that could handle such gargantuan task. She could act as a unreliable mentor or helper to them, although asking her help for poor reasons(ex. We are too weak for them!) will only infuriate her. To the pro-Joseon players, she could act as a 'final boss' to the chronicle if you are going to finish your story by the creation of Joseon.

                                            She does look invincible, but there are ways. Many Leviathans have discontent with her rule, and a skilled diplomat could persuade them for open treason. Her position as a princess also restricts her somewhat, so she mostly relies on her mortal acquaintances for social influence. Making them convert or threatening them will make her lose core assets in protecting Goryeo. Finally, her cult is a secret society, so exposing them could land her(and the entire Goryeo royal family) in a very bad position. As for comabt, she is a skilled fighter, and defeating her in battle will be difficult.

                                            1. Hyonyeong makes a renowned scholar into her beloved. His pupils became suspicious of their master's behavior and begins to investigate. They find out about their master is meeting with the princess regularly, and openly accuses them of adultery. Yi Sunggye faction asks for investigation to the scholar. Although they did not yet turn the arrow to Hyonyeong, it is only matter of time. Hyonyeong is in surveillance, so the Tribe cannot contact her. What should they do?
                                            2. Although never had been married, Hyonyeong has some secret children, both born or sired. One of those son is a Lahmasu who killed a civilian by accident. Hyonyeong herself executes a kill order to the Player Characters. When confronted, he pleads to the PCs, asking them to let him see his mother for once and for all. Should they accept it? If they agree, he will be killed by Hyonyeong herself the second he enters the palace, if they let him hide, Gwigok Gamyeong will be sent to hunt them.
                                            3. A masked men turns dead at a riverside. The players character investigates this, and learns that he belongs to Hyonyeong's cult Muyeong-Gongheo(None zero, Null void). Luckily, he had some secret codes and the date of the next meeting. If they disguise as cultists and infiltrate the gathering, they could earn hidden information about Hyonyeong.

                                                This is my final example Leviathan. He roughly has the same power level as the starting Player Characters, and can be played as a premade Player Character when playing 1 on 1. Sorry for the long delay.

                                                Kim Cheongsu
                                                "OK... I get that I'm a half-god, half-monster from ancient sea that fucks with people's minds. So... what now?"

                                                Background: As a landlord's son born in Andong, Kim Cheongsu had little worries. Sure, he didn't have much interest in academics so fame and honor were something he could never have, he had a lot of brothers and sisters which could cause(and has caused in his upper generation) inheritance disputes, but he didn't want much. He liked going out and enjoying mountains and river, resting at a tree near his family's mansion, hearing strange tales and interesting news told by monks, elders and wanderers. His family didn't expect much from him either, and was even pleased that he will not participate in infighting of the sibilings. To Cheongsu, everything was completely fine until the metamorphosis began.

                                                His eyes occasionally bulged out like an angler fish, which was also resembled by sprouting lantern on his forehead whenever he woke up. Deformed squirrels and rabbits began to following him, and his slaves would instinctively react to his very thoughts. Panicked by this transformation, he gathered every bits of information he could find, until he heard from a village elder that long time ago, a women from the seacoast married his great-great grandfather, who had the exactly the same condition as Cheongsu. And that his favorite tree was planted only a year after their wedding. Shocked at this information, he shoveled the tree that night.

                                                What he found was something that he still has difficulty expressing it. Hundreds of infant skeletons, each having deformed skulls or bones, were buried near the tree. While he stood still, stunned that his family had so many deformed stillbirths, he was surrounded by his relatives. They claimed this bloodline was a curse which should be kept secret from the outsiders, and must be exterminated, and said "We're sorry, but you bear that curse more than any of us. Goodbye." As he was brutally assaulted, Cheongsu managed to transform and crept to the river, blacking out almost he touched the water, letting the flow consume him.

                                                When he woke up, he found himself bandaged and cured by a woman with brown skin and exotic face. She introduced herself as Rania, a hermit from Arabia who dwells in a cave near the river. She refused to tell further, but her care and medicine was enough to make him regain his strength. Not knowing what to do next, he spent some time with Rania in her den, and found that he enjoyed her company with her surprisingly vast and insightful knowledge, caring and humble character. But that did not last long as his relatives discovered their hiding place, forcing them to flee. Now Cheongsu wanders the whole country with Rania as his company, his destination Gaegyung. Although that does not stop him from detouring to famous scenic spots.

                                                Description: Kim Cheongsu is only a teenager, while he is accepted as a legal adult, culturally he is still a minor. He has more than average looks and physique, but anyone looking at him will instantly now he is far from a trained body. He talks with a heavy Yeongnam-reigion accent and is not familiar with the aristocratic customs of the central nobility. He is often clumsy and distracted, but make this up by his sense of humor and enthusiasm. He learned from Rania the basic history and abilities of the Leviathan and is keen to explore it. But he has almost no information of the current Tribe, and the Player Characters will most likely be the first other Leviathans he will meet. He is fairly attracted to Rania, but will not admit it out loud, thinking she is so better than him.

                                                Storytelling hints: Rania is a powerful Promethean who entered Goryeo four hundred years ago, and Cheongsu does not know this. Learning her true nature will shake him hard. Except from this secret, he is an ideal friend to both pro-Goryeo and pro-Joseon Cohort. Just following him and Rania to Gaegyung, and learning about the problems and issues of current Korea could be good first phase of the Chronicle. Otherwise, trying to recruit him for PC's faction could be a good mission to a newly fledged Cohort. Cheongsu understands that big houses like his family in Andong are the plague that corrode Goryeo, but is reluctant at the idea of founding a new nation. He will be fairly moderate even when after joining a faction.

                                                It is hard to turn him as an enemy, but even if you did, he himself is nothing to fear. He has little fighting skills, have few contacts outside his hometown and his cult is disorganized. He has roughly the power of one starting character, and cannot stand the might of the entire cohort on his own. Kim Cheongsu knows this too, and he will try to flee, but if things get dangerous he will try to save Rania because of his debt to her and his chivalry. Of course, whether the Tribe can take on the 400-year-old Promethean, that is an entirely different matter.

                                                Story hooks:
                                                1. It is well known that Divine Fire and Primordial Waters do not mix well. As Cheongsu develops his powers, those two opposing forces clash with all their might. Almost everyone around him, including his Beloved, go insane. Rania quickly realizes this is appearing because she broke her role when coming out from that den. She disappears without leaving a message. Cheongsu asks the Player Characters to aid in his search for her.
                                                2. The Kim Clan of Andong is a traditional and powerful family. Although Cheongsu's family was only their small branch, the head of the eldest branch learns about his escape, and sends a message to him saying he will arrange a "make-up meeting" with his family. Because this is an offer from the Clan's leader, refusing it will cause a major commotion. Cheongsu accepts this, but asks the Tribe some reliable bodyguards for the rendezvous.
                                                3. Before arriving at Gaegyung, Cheongsu wants to go for the Geumgang mountain, which is the most famous tourism site in the Goryeo dynasty(and still are, at least in the North Korea). The problem is it is the territory of the elder Tannim turned into Typhon, who dwells in the nine-dragon falls. She will lash out at the party if they approach near her.
