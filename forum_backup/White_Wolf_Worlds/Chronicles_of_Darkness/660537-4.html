                                Originally posted by Omegaphallic View Post

                                Not really, Larcenist is more of a con artist kith, they have some ability to steal, but they do so by conning people, they're a social kith at heart. Lurker is more stealthy, pick pocket, B&E type.
                                They don't really make very good con men. A con man wouldn't need Larceny, since they're not really burglars or pickpockets, as much as they would need Empathy (to pick out good marks), Persuasion (to fast talk), and Socialize (to ingratiate oneself with the mark), in addition to Subterfuge. So, one could make a Larcenist con man, sure, but it's not ideal, and they won't gain much use from the Kith's "always on" Larceny bonus (unless they're really bad con men who have to resort to just outright stealing what they want). One of the more social Kiths would probably work much better for a con man.

                                Besides, the write up focuses on them being pickpockets more than anything else. Their quote is about being pickpockets, They're described as being "quick and nimble fingered." The Fairest example uses their looks to "distract from what her hands are doing." It even says that assuming they're dishonest because of their penchant for thievery is "often furthest from the truth" - which would be a hard sentence to justify if they were natural con men, since dishonesty is pretty much the defining virtue of con men.

                                So, they're primary function seems to be as pickpockets (some of whom use lies to distract or trick their targets). The Lurker gets Sleight of Hand for free, so they are also good pickpockets. Simply add a line or two to the description about how some Keepers give them strange rules to abide by and how some Lurkers use social means to facilitate their thievery, then add Subterfuge to the list of Skills that can be boosted by the Lurkers blessing and you have one Kith that serves both roles perfectly well. Even the example Seeming descriptions all seem like they would work equally well for either Kith.
