    Notes to hit on a first campaign

    12-15-2014, 05:18 PM
    My group has always wanted to try out C:tL, and I've been elected storyteller. A couple of the players have been in a one-shot Changeling game that wasn't well run, but aside from that we're pretty new, and overall most of our experience has been oWoD Werewolf...most of us have very minimal nWoD experience, and one has never done WoD at all. I've been pawing through the book on and off for a few months but haven't played, and I want to make sure I get the themes right.

    What I've got planned so far for the first session or two is waving an NPC's keeper in their faces, as well as all the PCs' fetches. When starting brand new in the setting, what are the most important thematic elements for me to hit right away? Of course I need to get the players terrified of their old keepers and instill a healthy uncertainty about just how safe they are, but I'm not sure whether the most important foundation is "you should be terrified" or "you should realize how broken you are from your durance".

    I'm also pretty uncertain where I should go digging for everyday adventure seeds. What _should_ drive the characters, what directions should I encourage them to move in? Where should they find their goals, in a smaller scale? Yes, they need to accept and start to heal from their abuse at the hands of the fae, but where does that start turning into story seeds? Where can I find goals that work for three PCs together, as different as they might be? Again, I'm coming from a W:tA background, where motivation is very easy to create. Where am I most likely to find / manufacture duty for the Changeling PCs, when there's not currently the Gentry coming after them or someone else, and when there's not major drama going on with their old family...or should that be all the time?


    Oh yeah... Vows. They're powerful, and there's good mechanical incentives to be pretty loaded up on them all the time. Should I encourage this? Should I try and keep players from committing very much? As an ST could anyone give advice on how to use them as story driving tools?

    Any other thematic tips or things to avoid for new STs?

    Thanks!

      Vows are good. Encourage as many Vows as possible.

      Then use them to generate drama. Oodles upon oodles of delicious, delicious drama.

      A player has an Alliance Vow with two NPCs? Have them feud, and ask the player for help...

      Heck, they can be your adventure seeds!

      Are you going to start them off fresh out of their Durance, or are you going to set it a bit after that? If you're going the first route, emphasize that they can't trust anybody unless they have a Pledge on it. If later on, you can start everyone off with a few Vows as a way to help define the setting a bit.

      I would also suggest that you shouldn't waive everyone's Fetches in their faces within the first few sessions. Oh, sure, do it if they specifically try to get back to their old life, but some Durances take centuries, and the Fetch came and went. Maybe show off one of the Fetches, and hold on to the rest for dramatic revelations later on?

          Yes, fresh out of their Durance, which has the advantage that it doesn't assume a ton of player experience with the game world (and the disadvantage that I have an awful lot to get right).

              Okay, few questions:

              1) Do you plan a more Sandbox style game (Players make their own plots or find plots around) or Story Driven (An old wizard with a map meets you in a bar). Both can work fine for Changeling, but my advice would be affected by the group preference.

              2) What themes in Changeling is your group interested in? Reclaiming old lives? Romance? Politics? Hedge Dungeon Crawls? Exploration of mysterious Hedge realms? Battling loyalists? Changeling is a broad game, full of options.

              Changeling, compared to many games especially but not only Classic WoD games, does not have an obvious conflict. I mean there is the Gentry, but for the most part They are There and We are Here, and going back There is almost unthinkable till near the End. So you want to determine what people's view is of what Changelings do and discuss it.

              Of course, My thread Malibu Dream House is there for folks to pillage for plot ideas, location ideas, NPCs, or really whatever you need. If scenarios or characters seem cool, feel free to take them. It's a sandbox, but you have options.

                  By way of setting and description, I advise toying with the characters' perceptions - describe contradicting or impossible surroundings. Then, depending on the Clarity level of the characters, either note that they recognize that these are just tricks of perception or note that they believe fully in what they've perceived.
