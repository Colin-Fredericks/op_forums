            I'd say space is more scary because the murder artist can be watching you at any or every moment and toss a spell from anywhere in creation at any time he feels like it.

                                                            Originally posted by Malus View Post

                                                            It's one step closer, mind. The moment where you realize on a fundamental, concrete and objective fashion of how frail life is next to your understanding of the universe, it makes taking that step all the more easier.
                                                            Or the more easily they can snuff out a life, the more likely they are to take safeguards to prevent wanton destruction. This I think is the crux of the wisdom mechanic.

                                                            Just because you CAN do something, doesn't mean that you should. Being prepared for violence does not turn a mage into a murder hobo, degenerating down into the abyss.

                                                            It is the tension of being ready to do what sometimes must be done to prevent greater horrors, while making every effort to avoid that necessity that brings out the best of this game line.

                                                            But if sliding down into the darkness and reveling in carefree mayhem and death is your thing, more power to you.

                                                            It takes all kinds, and there is no one right way to go about things in a game.
