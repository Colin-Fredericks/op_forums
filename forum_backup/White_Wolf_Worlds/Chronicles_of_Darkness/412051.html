    New York by Night

    02-19-2015, 05:16 PM
    Here's a rough outline of what I have so far (all comments are welcome):


    The Basics:

    New York is a multi-domain region predominantly run by the Prince (or as he is currently titled the Prince-Archbishop) of Manhattan. The Prince of Manhattan, however is bound by something called "the Old Rule" which essentially means the Prince of Manhattan can expect loyalty from the other domains and they are bound by his Laws, but he can't actually revoke the domains of those Princes (none of who claim the title of ‘Prince’). To further complicate things, the Old Rule is actually based off an older treaty between the Vampire Domain in Brooklyn/Eastern Queens with the Thing (or Things) known as "The Old" that claim everything on Long Island east of Lake Hempstead and have since 1700.

    A series of revolts over the past 350 years have led to significant rising and falling of the population (all but one have been by the Carthians). This has left many Elders to withdraw from the Danse Macabre, and there are a number of smaller areas (mostly in the outer Boroughs) controlled by solitary elders who have become unbound over time. The large number of sedentary but highly territorial Elders has led them to become the rare pillars of stability, and most Covenants (largely run by Ancillae) seek their support in their various bickerings for control.


    The Laws (including the Old Rule) which apply to all of the Domains, but generally are most important in Manhattan:

    1. The Old Rule Shall Not Be Broken
    2. The Prince Alone Addresses the Old [One[s]] (every Prince since 1700 has had a variation of this, traditionally one of the Brooklyn Dynasties speaks for the Prince, but this practice stopped 50 years ago when two of the Houses started suddenly following the Ghost Wolf religion of the Munsee people who the Old [Ones] used to protect and practicing Cruac)
    3. None shall interfere with Mortal Politics (a huge Masquerade breach happened with Tammany Hall sometime in the past but this rule is highly open to interpretation)
    4. No Feeding Above the 13th Floor of any Building (this one is a mystery but none of the last three Princes removed it)
    5. No Carthians may remain in Manhattan after Sunrise (Carthians led a revolt against the Prince-Archbishop, ruled for quite some time, but were eventually driven out by the L+S and Invictus)

    Other Traditions of the Area:
    1. Due to the heavy influence of the Lancea et Sanctum, the embrace is traditionally only given to those who are considered “irredeemable” and the vetting process must be approved by the Lord of the specific Domain (or more commonly the Primogeon of that Domain is given the authority). However, the Prince of Manhattan is typically notified of this, as he is ultimately tasked with maintaining the Masquerade and upkeeping the Traditions. Overpopulation is considered a violation of the Traditions, and the Prince-Archbishop will generally thin the population starting at the top and working his way down.
    2. Quite a bit of import is attached to the use of titles. Generally a “Lord” is one who can claim the protection of the Old Rule, and no leader outside of Manhattan would EVER use the title of Prince to refer to themselves, even though the King of Queens has taken a bold step in calling himself King.
    3. Crime has always been a temptation amongst many of the Kindred in the All-Night City. Most Kindred refer to their Coterie’s as “Gangs”, “Packs”, “Ciphers” or “Crews.” Drugs, especially cocaine and heroin, are a fascination amongst most of the younger Kindred. Controlling the major drug distribution networks is a point of pride amongst the Neonates and some Ancillae, a fact which strains the Masquerade, and means the most established groups of vampire hunters are generally well armed criminals.
    4. Due to the spread out nature of the Domains, and the importance of holding territory, the Covenants operate largely independent of each other Domain to Domain. While the Prince-Archbishop has Bishop’s in Queens, the Bronx and Brooklyn (for example), he doesn’t have direct control over those Bishops or their layity. The Invictus is hypothetically this organized via “The Boardroom” however, in practice the elders from Brooklyn, Queens and the Bronx tend not to find the Manhattan-centric nature of things off putting.

    Manhattan at a Glance:
    • Population: About 50 Kindred, roughly equally split between the clans. Covenant wise the Invictus and Lancea Sanctum make up about 25 of these combined. Another 8 are part of the Dragons, which is relatively close knit group. The remaining 7 or so are part of the Manhattan Crones. The remainder of the Kindred are unaffiliated and tend to stay far uptown. The Population tends to rise slightly over the weekend, as the other Kindred domains move among the herd into the bars and clubs in across the Island.
    • What Makes Manhattan Unique:
      • If you can make it here you can make it anywhere: Manhattan’s kindred thrive on conflict. The large parts of the City which aren’t considered “traditional” are filled with an endless succession of coteries battling over influence. This constant struggle has led coteries to be the primary form of organization in Manhattan, if you’re not in one, you probably stay with the unbound way Uptown or you stay within your covenant’s territory. Killing another Kindred is considered a violation of the Traditions, but if two powerful coteries end up getting into open conflict most people will look the other way if a neonate or two ends up caught in the conflagration.
      • Weekends here are kinda Bridge and Tunnel: Manhattan Kindred tend not to travel out of Manhattan too often, but Kindred from the other Domains tend to come into Manhattan. This has led to a general belief among the Manhattan Kindred that they are superior and has caused quite a bit of bitterness between the Manhattan kindred and their "bridge and tunnel" brethren.
    • The Prince-Archbishop:
      • The Title is intentional, the jobs of Prince and Archbishop represent the Secular State and the Unholy State in the Prince-Archbishop’s mind. He considers enforcing the Traditions and the Law against the Old to be sacred and part of his duty as Archbishop, and generally enforces those Laws under that title. He considers violation of the other Laws and sedition to be within his role as the Prince, and will refer to himself by that secular title. He generally considers his position as Archbishop to be superior, and generally dresses in vestaments for that role. When he’s operating solely in a secular capacity, or interacting with mortals he almost exclusively wears a suit and tie.
      • Nosferatu, embraced sometime in the early late 1700s by the former Prince of Albany. His pale eyes, and gnarled hands add an aura of menace to his appearance.
      • Originally Prince of Manhattan during the late 1800s before being forced into exile after the 3rd Carthian Revolt dislodged him (a “Shadow Bishop” was elected to replace him, as the Carthians “enlightened” that Lancea Sanctum about the importance of elected leadership in their Covenant).
      • Spent the succeeding century serving as Senschal to the Lord of Queens, the Lord of the Bronx and (for a short while) serving under the Bishop of Newark in the 1930s, when the Bishop of Newark was briefly able to claim the title of Cardinal of Northern New Jersey before the domain splintered.
    • The Officers of the City:
      • Primogeon (Elder from each Clan):
        • Olga Silver (Ventrue)
        • The Prince-Archbishop (Nosferatu)
        • Steven Loren (Mekhet)
        • Peter Harlan (Gangrel)
        • Alex Bloom (Daeva)
      • Senschal: Father Torricelli
      • Sheriff:[TBD]
      • Herald:[TBD]
      • Hounds:[TBD]
      • Master of Elysium: Lady Jacinta Munso
    • Domains: Largely split "by tradition" among the various factions. This tradition means that, regardless of the changes in leadership the head of each of the 4 Covenants is allowed to claim praxis over a predetermined Domain. These borders are general considered sacrosanct, and the leader generally uses the title “Regent” to describe the area they hold. Most neonates are granted territory within their factions region.
      • The Ordo Dracul control Harlem (Everything North of Central Park to about 150th Street) by tradition, though their small numbers mean an unknown number of unbound claim parts of Washington Heights and most of Inwood (generally this is where new arrivals to the City, who lack a reputation are told to remain).
        • Leader: Steven Loren, Kogaion of the Ordo Dracul. Though no one ever talks about it, Alex Bloom (the leader of the Manhattan Crones) and Steven Loren both used to be close friends and both moved down from Boston together. They both took their background in Theosophy in different directions. Steven is a very solitary man, and generally operates through his agents.
        • Covenant Meeting Spot: Low Library at Columbia University. The Ordo have always been attracted to Low Library, it could be because it is near the nexus of several ley lines, or it could just be the exceptional architecture. The covenant holds a meeting here once a month. Once every few years they also host a conference for all Dragons across the Night Cities where anyone is allowed to present their research for review.
        • The Apollo’s Sirens: The Apollo Theatre is one of the later Wyrm’s Nest’s that the Dragons noticed that formed as an important Nexus in Harlem’s ley lines. Over time it has spawned a group of two Daeva and one Invictus (Dominique, Simone and Madison) who have taken to studying the power of their voices and it’s ability (especially through song) to effect the human mind. In addition to their supernatural experiments, they are known for their generally androgynous appearance and exceptional singing and songwriting ability. Their voice lessons are known to enhance some of the effects of the use of the Majesty and Dominate disciplines, and having them sing at a private Kindred event is a sure sign of class.
      • The Lancea Sanctum controls the Upper West Side and most of the Upper East Side essentially tracking Central Park on both sides (though Central Park is considered poor feeding grounds). They generally claim Redeemer Presbyterian Church (UWS) as their primary meeting spot for Covenant wide events that they generally don’t want others to attend to.
        • Leader: the Prince-Archbishop, though in practice he delegates a good amount of authority for spiritual matters to Father Torricelli. Father Torricelli is an extremely orthodox elder from the Ventrue. He generally operates as something of an inquisitor and is the most skilled user of Theban Sorcery in Manhattan. While the Prince-Archbishop rarely uses force to back up his edicts, Father Torricelli looks for excuses to be forceful.
        • Covenant Meeting Spot: Redeemer Presbyterian Church on 83rd Street and Amsterdam Avenue as their primary meeting spot. They generally hold a “Midnight Mass” here for covenant members only, during the weeknights. This service is significantly less traditional compared to the one the Prince-Archbishop runs on Sundays. There’s significantly less ritual, and most of the sessions are conducted more like 30 Minute sessions of study of the Testament and/or quiet group dirges.
        • The Vigil: Just because you’re dead doesn’t mean your old life is dead. The Vigil specializes in helping people cut connections with their old life, in order to become the predators they were meant to be. The Vigil is run by a Mekhet Sanctified Priest named Carlo Provenzano, who still manages several funeral homes, along with a group of four other Kindred. The Vigil provides funerals, official records, and makes careful use of manipulation to ensure that Kindred are able to pass into their new existence and leave their mortal life behind. The Vigil, though nominally providing a benevolent service, often finds itself consulting for Kindred who have already breached the Masquerade by getting too close to their old lives. This has led them to become very adept at covering breaches of the Masquerade as well, and they are sometimes called in to handle these problems as well.
      • The Crones of Manhattan (who follow a Neo-Egyptian/Order of the Golden Dawn based system) have domain over "The Museums of Manhattan"
        • Leader: Alex Bloom.
        • Covenant Meeting Spot: The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Notable for being right in the heart of Lancea Sanctum territory, the Met is the most important meeting spot the Followers of Apophis have. In a forgotten storage room painted in hieroglyphics and imagery showing (among other things) Alex feeding the Sun Disc of Ra to Apophis, the Followers practice weekly group rituals and initiate mortals into the Mysteries.
        • The Followers of Apophis: At some time in the late 1870s Alex Bloom, a Daeva Theosophist from Boston, arrived in New York. He was fascinated by Egyptology and become heavily involved in the Metropolitan Museum of Art. At some point in the 1890s, he had an extreme revelatory experience and began to develop a form of blood magic which he ended up teaching to several other members of the Carthian Movement and the Ordo Dracul. By 1920 this group has swelled to eight members, controlling most of the Museums in Manhattan. Unlike the Ordo Dracul, which tends to keep much of their occult knowledge to themselves, the Followers of Apophis are more than willing to contract out their use of blood magic. Each of the three senior members maintains a personal “Mystery,” a term they use to describe the mortal secret societies they use. Their philosophy revolves largely around controlled moments of excess, and as such they are also a popular group to host parties for the Kindred.
      • The Invictus control most of Manhattan below Canal Street, but largely operate around Wall Street and the Financial District. The Covenant forbids anyone to walk on the block surrounding City Hall, and in general people steer clear of the Court’s at Foley Square in order to not break the rule on interfering with politics.
        • Leaders: Olga Silver and Cathy Ireland are two out of the three Viscounts of Lower Manhattan, the third currently spending time in torpor is Avia Cohen (only two of the three are active at one time), have run lower Manhattan since at least 1830. Generally of the two active members the one next in line to enter Torpor serves on the Primogeon. Currently Olga is a year away from entering into Torpor and serves as the Ventrue Primgeon.
        • Meeting Spot: Depending on who you’re in with, you meet in a private room at the back of either Cipriani or Delmonicos. However, if you’re a neonate looking to make a name for himself, or an elder looking for a hand doing something lucrative, you meet at Bowling Green (either by the Bull or by the Stairs to the Courthouse).
        • The Exchange: The Exchange is one of several coteries of Invictus members who have been trading favors and stabbing each other in the back since the early days of Wall Street. What separates the Exchange from more established coteries like Merchant Services and the Gold Street Pact (aka “The Pack”), is they tend to stay away from the manipulating the banks and financial instiutions of FiDi directly. The Exchange are all former members of the financial services industry (or those who are quick to learn), and their immortality just makes working all night that much easier. While the leadership of the Exchange is done largely around meriotocratic lines, they are essentially divided into three divisions, each overseen by an elder. Traditional Investments (anything that a normal investment bank would handle) is run by Peter Harlan a 200 year old Gangrel, Real Estate is run by Ian Underhill a 150 year old Daeva, and Kindred Investments (ghoul trading, formalized ledgers of favors, blood of high vintage, and nearly any other type of supernatural item of tradable value) is run by Michelle Dickerson a Daeva who survived the Great New York Fire of 1776. The Exchange maintains a small set of largely internal offices in the World Financial Center and in most ways operate like a mortal business, most of the members work normal work hours, they draw formal salaries (with generous bonuses) and the work environment can be (literally cutthroat). Any Kindred looking for money (whether as a loan or to invest), to buy real-estate (especially to buy it from under an enemy) or to have something valuable held for safe keeping will use the Exchange.
      • The rest of Manhattan is largely unclaimed. Midtown used to be the domain of the Carthians, and since has turned into a constantly shifting region largely claimed by two rival coteries of Ancillae (The Triple Six and Invite-Only). The Prince-Archibishop has declared Times Square to be a Rack (and not claimable) but most of Manhattan south of 34th Street to Canal Street is nominally open to anyone ambitious enough to claim it.


    The Remaining 4 Domains:
    -Gold Coast Republic (along the NJ Gold Coast, essentially everything between the Goethals Bridge and the George Washington Bridge) - The Carthians were "gifted" this territory after they were exiled from Manhattan. They largely are a buffer against most of the scattered vampire domains which rise and fall rather rapidly across Northern NJ. They operate under normal electoral lines.
    -The Regency of the Crossroads (The Bronx) - A 7 member Coterie of Santeria practicing Crones run this domain under the motto "Only with absolute loyalty to each other can the few control the many." Their leader Mambo Racine-the-Pure used blood sorcery to whipe out the former Carthian leader who insulted her, but she had no desire to be a leader in anything but name. The former 2nd in command Carthian in the Bronx, a well regarded Invictus member from Manhattan and Racine's childe form the three member "Board" that runs the day to day business. Racine hypothetically has veto power, but rarely gets involved.
    -The Kingdom of Queens (Queens, except the section which borders Long Island, which is largely under the control of the Brooklyn Dynasties) - Imagine the sample Prince "The King" from Damnation City. He rarely talks about it, but King Angel is actually a member of one of the Brooklyn Dynasties (which might become a problem next time his particular Dynasty is in charge of Queens in two years).
    -The Brooklyn Dynastic Court (Brooklyn, Eastern Queens, including the Rockaways): This the region I'm most interested in. Essentially the first 5 vampires in New York were Revanents who were created, as legend has it, by the Old Ones. They kept together and fed largely on other farmers. It wasn't until after they survived in this State for 50 long years, that a group of recently arrived European Kindred properly embraced them. They went on to become the first powerful faction in New York and secured the original agreement with the Old Ones (that eventually morphed into the Old Rule) which permits no Western Vampires to go East of Lake Hempstead (and to provide a 15 year old male redhead on the shores of the lake every 13 years). The first Prince of New York was from one of these families and tried to break the agreement with the Old Ones, he and his Childe were destroyed the day they passed beyond Lake Hempstead. The four remaining dynasties (Dudley, Moore, Tarbox and Dall) have since adopted a system where every 13 years they rotate which House rules the domain/brings the offering to the Old Ones. The Second Law of the Domain was introduced by them for this reason (back when the most powerful domain was Brooklyn), however traditionally the Prince would just designate them as his spokespersons and they'd continue their sacrifice ritual. However, the last two times this happened House Dudley and House Moore (13 years apart) adopted started following a strange religion they called "The Way of the Ghost Wolf" and practicing Cruac. Since them the Prince sends an unlucky neonate to handle it.
    -Notably absent is Staten Island. For whatever reason the island makes Vampires uneasy, and it has ever since the group of Ordo vampires who once claimed it as their domain all mysteriously vanished. The Prince doesn't even bother to make a claim on it, and generally uses it as a place to put troublesome vampires (who never return).

              Some Sample Characters and Coteries (a work in progress):

              Manhattan

              Solitary Elders

              Tombs               (Nosferatu, Unbound, 'Lord of the Tombs'): Tombs has been Lord of the Tombs (aka the Manhattan Detention Center) for so long that among the Kindred the building and the vampire are fundamentally indistinguishable. Like many elders across the various NY Domains, he was highly involved in politics in the past, only to find himself one of the few survivors of a revolt or other tragedy that saw many other Kindred perish. Similarly, like many of those elders, seeing all of his allies in the Danse Macabre disappear led him to withdraw. According to reports, most prisoners have a hard time sleeping in the Tombs and speak of a mysterious force that snatchs people out of their beds at night or fills their mind with terrifying dreams, a legend which has grown so intense that no prisoner wants to be stuck without a cellmate. It's no secret that many of the corrections officers are in his thrall, though it seems that he's been fighting a protracted battle of influence with the coterie that runs Rikers Island, along with elders who run the other Borough's detention centers, for control of the entire Department of Corrections. Tomb's rarely leaves the Manhattan Detention Center, he prefers to speak to people in parked cars along the parking lot on White Street that runs through the center of the prison. Despite being largely sedentary, Tombs is a very well respected information broker and despite the rules against interfering with politics, he is adept at navigating certain people into his prison. He's an excellent source for finding potential ghouls of the more physical variety, in addition he is often willing to grant feeding rights in his prison to those that provide him with select mortals (that he wants in prison for some arcane but likely nefarious reason).

              It is also no secret that Tombs is often requested to make certain troublesome Kindred disappear for some time in the lightless cells underground.

              Michael “Baixing” Lee (Mekhet, Regent of Chinatown): Baixing is one of the oldest Kindred in the Night Cities (though you wouldn’t guess by looking at him because he looks in his mid 20s), and yet has never fully been a part of the Night Cities and has never in his heart accepted Western Culture. Baixing (meaning “Commoners”) is the ancestor of one of the first Chinese families to move to New York City and has been the protector of them and their descendents (who are major property developers) throughout Manhattan’s Chinatown. His enforcement is sporadic, but generally it is considered unwise to feed on people in Chinatown because occasionally Baixing takes offense, and goes out of his way to make violators next few nights uncomfortable. Some stories speak of people feeding in Chinatown only to have their Haven be occupied by the police at 3am, or to find their ghouls imprisoned, or any other of a series of unfortunate events that are never quite traced back to Baixing. Despite his philosophical opposition to Western customs, Baixing always shows up whenever the Prince Hold’s Court, he has an Ex Parte position on the Primogeon, speaks flawless English, dresses in casual yet fashionable Western clothes and goes by the name Michael Lee (you NEVER call him Baixing to his face). He’s remained relevant for another reason, Baixing has a significant amount of influence among practioners of Chinese traditional medicine, some of those who are rumored to be part of a very powerful group of sorcerers. He’s sired twice, both times Westerners. The first one, a man, went on to be destroyed for trying to set up control in the area of the Old Ones (he claimed he wanted to get away from his Sire). The second one, a woman (embraced 1900) named Caroline Miller went on to become the second in Command to the leader of Brooklyn’s Ordo’s (from House Tarbox). She also hates her sire with a passion, and refuses to attend Court in Manhattan for fear of seeing him. He wants nothing more than to mend things with her.

              Downtown Coteries

              The Archivists               (Mixed Coterie, mostly Nosferatu and Mekhet, Claim Washington Square Park and surrounding parts of NYU): The Dragons may have their tail wrapped around Columbia University, but the Archivists a group of two Ancillae, their childer, and two others (a great Coterie for novice PCs to be a part of!) are the primary Kindred controlling the administration of NYU (some of the more distant dorms are claimed by other Kindred). Taking advantage of the Carthian Exodus (in the late 80s), a Mekhet of the Invictus (Michael Missioni) and a Nosferatu Dragon (The Bookworm), came together to establish control over Washington Square Park as their own hunting ground (the Park itself is a terrible hunting ground but it has a prestige value among Manhattan Kindred). They quickly expanded their influence in the surrounding area, eventually working their agents up into the administration of the school. While the Village, be it the East, West or Greenwich, is largely considered the Rack everyone knows not to feed in Washington Square Park or in any NYU building directly surrounding the Park. While the Archivists don't have access to the same amount of Kindred History and Lore that the Dragons or the Sanctified control in their massive private libraries, they make up for this by keeping their finger on the pulse of the latest information and offering it to the public. Claiming territory that's in the middle of one of the best hunting grounds in the country has it's advantages, and the Archivists are quick to grant "weekend hunting rights" to those Kindred who have information to trade (and its shocking the banality of the information they'll accept). This also means that the Archivists are great to visit if you need information on any Kindred in the region, they thrive off of trading rumors and their only price is more information on anything Kindred.

              The Cacophony around Washington Square Park points any interested Kindred to Bobst Library on the South Side of the Park. Those in the know recall that you go to the Help Desk in front and ask for a tour of the Basement Rare Book Room. An agent of the Archivists will take your request downstairs, research it and get back to you within three nights. They demand recompense in "information of equal value" (they've developed a system for this that no one but them understands).

              Haute Couture(Mixed coterie, claim Broadway from Houston Street down to Canal Street): From the 60s to the early 80s Haute Couture were at the top of the game. Most of the City’s up-and-coming Carthians and Invictus worked together to control most of the Art and Fashion scene throughout the City. They controlled who was on the “Vampire VIP” lists at all the hottest clubs, owned the client lists for the celebrities with the biggest cocaine habits and ran nearly every major downtown club. It didn’t matter the scene, they moved like chameleons, punks at Pyramid Club one night and stock brokers at Boom Boom Room the next night. They started to believe their own legend, and tried to sit out of the Carthian revolt. Six of the Eight members ended up caught in the purge, along with the12 Ghouls they used to keep their fiefdom running, with only two neonate’s remaining they carefully pulled a few disparate Kindred from across the region to join them and are slowly working to regain their former glory. While they used to claim significant territory across SoHo, the West Village and Chelsea, now they only lay claim to Broadway in SoHo, and many of the surrounding side streets. This area is generally considered very sparse for hunting at night (as most of the stores close around 9 or 10pm), and reflects well the fall they’ve undergone.

              The Overlords (assumed to nest somewhere in Midtown, no clear claims): It’s relatively common to make one’s haven in the various tunnels that run under the City, but travelling through the tunnels is a completely different issue. The Overlords are the people you go to see if you need to get from point A to point B without traversing the surface streets. While the Overlords maintain knowledge of nearly every underground tunnel in the City, they are only 6 in number. Their leader is a surprisingly charismatic Gangrel going by “Gator” who has seen the entire infrastructure develop. The Overlords are highly secretive, have a shocking level of knowledge of the underground, and while they don’t attempt to claim all the subway and sewer tunnels as their own they are well aware of everyone who lairs underneath the street. For a price they can get anyone across the City in secret, though it tends to cost a bit more to points outside of Manhattan (where their influence is significantly less).

                                      Hey guys, a bit of a delay in the next few parts. I must confess that while writing this, I started to realize how little of New York City I was actually familiar with (it's really fricking huge!). If anyone has knowledge about the Bronx, Queens and Brooklyn I'd love to have a brainstorming session about sufficiently dramatic locations for Vampires to use and operate around. Below is the "Kingdom of Queens" which operates largely under the rule of King Angel and his gang "Los Angeles Matones." I've also drafted the section on Brooklyn, but because it ties so largely into the Cities history I want to nail down a set timeline for certain events before I post that (so look out for a section on either the Bronx or New Jersey next).

                                      Queens [Kingdom of Queens] at a Glance:
                                      • Population: About 40 Kindred, with a slight bias towards Ventrue and Gangrel. Covenant wise the Invictus make up about 20 of these (generally following the lesser formality of the King). Another 10 work with the Lancea Sanctum. Another 3 are part of the Dragons (mostly sticking to an abandoned Mansion in Jamaica Estates). There is also a 7 member coterie of Carthians who control Rikers Island. They are loyal if not distant. The remainder of the Kindred are unaffiliated elders who make up the secretive “Club for the Promotion of Stability Amonst the Kindred” or “The Elder Club” for short. Very few independent Kindred stay in Queens for long as the Coteries are extremely territorial and (though killing is not allowed) intimidation and pain are a common remedy.
                                      • What Makes Queens Unique:
                                        • Family First: Your coterie (more commonly your “gang”)is your family, and family always comes first. The Kindred of Queens are highly territorial, and each coterie knows the other coteries signs and symbols. Being outside of a gang is similar to not existing, the elders won’t respect you, and the coteries will torment you. There is no common feeding area, though there is unclaimed territory. People know not to claim what they can’t hold, and the King treats those harshly who can bite off more than they can chew.
                                        • Respect for the OGs: Despite the relatively young King of Queens, and the extreme form of iconoclasm he’s brought to the Invictus, he is well aware that his reign is at the pleasure of the elders. Behind every major political action in Queens is the approval of the Club for the Promotion of Stability, a 150 year old political organization that was founded in response to agitations from the Night of Rage in 1827 (the Night Cities first major Carthian Revolt). These Elders collectively administer Queens in Four Sections, but claim far smaller personal territories in the areas they administer. Each coterie operating in their area needs to maintain the approval of their regions Elder. The practical effect of this being most of the neonates and ancillae end up fighting each other most of the time and spend less time concerned with the Elders.
                                      • King Angel Bautista (Ventrue/Invictus):
                                        • Angel was embraced by Kees Vandergraff-Dall of House Dall in Brooklyn in 1975. This makes him one of the rare 4th generation members of one of the Brooklyn Houses. His dark skin and difficulty speaking English originally, combined with his insistence of staying involved with the drug trade and his general scoffing at the more formalized Invictus traditons led him to denounce his House and move to Queens in the late 70s. He’s mostly renounced the ties to them, as stilted and has dwelled in Queens since.
                                        • Angel is the founder of the 5 Kindred Coterie, the Angels of Death (or in Spanish Los Angeles Matones which translates slightly differently). They’ve largely been able to control most of the drug trade in Queens by being quick to infiltrate a number of drug delivery services and then following the suppliers up the chain. This has given them a significant amount of money, and the operational ability to hold sway over many other Kindred gangs (many who were previously involved in several drug mills scattered throughout the Borough).
                                        • Angel took over in 2005 when the former Invictus ruler of Queens (an Gangrel Ancillae named Michaela Yin) attempted to reduce the power of the Elder Club by claiming a spot for herself in the Elder Club. The Elder Club rejected this, declared Michaela Yin and her kin “out of favor” and gave their support to Angel and his coterie. Angel has been in charge ever since, and has wisely not questioned the Elders.
                                        • Angel is certainly not the oldest coterie leader, but he more than makes up for this in his actions. He’s a savvy leader who has been able to keep most of the more powerful coteries fighting each other more than anyone else and he’s been wise in selecting allies. For example, he backed the Ghost Wardens of Rikers in their attempt to gain complete control of Rikers Island from the childer of Tombs (see the Manhattan section) and his coterie. This has largely kept the Carthians pacified (and ironically willingly imprisoned).
                                        • Angel has started to develop an inferiority complex. He’s been granted a spot in the Boardroom of the Manhattan Invictus but he’s also the youngest member and directly at odds with Brooklyn on most issues. This has led him into dealings with the Carthians more and more, a development which the Three Sisters have found very concerning.
                                      • The Officers of the City:
                                        • The Elder Club (see below for description of the members): The ruling elders of Queens are essentially it’s ruling council. They largely operate and meet in secret and their edicts are vague and on the surface appear to be lacking in force. Unlike the Three Sisters who control Manhattan however, the Elder Club is truly not interested in being in charge and has very little interest in micromanagement. Let the coteries fight amongst each other for supremacy, the elders will stay in charge regardless.
                                        • Seneschal/Herald (the “Bookie”): Hayden “HNIC” Wright is about 6’2’’, muscular, black and covered in gang tattoos. When it comes to keeping the mortal gangs in line, and making sure the count is right, it falls to Wright. In addition, when the other coteries need to be made aware of something Hayden is who gets sent. Ventrue/Invictus, member of the Angels of Death.
                                        • Sheriff (the “Enforcer”): Raul “Mano Negra” Mercado is on the shorter side, about 5’8’’. He tends towards dressing like a typical Astoria hipster of the Latin persuasion, beanie hat with reservoir tip, ironic t-shirts referencing old alternative latin bands (hence his nickname “Mano Negra”), and a pair of combat boots over his pair of torn up selvedge denim. This is all an act though, he’s got the image of the Arch-Angel Gabriel tattooed (all in black) on the back of his right hand (also part of the reason for his nickname). If you see that tattoo, chances are you already fucked up. His preferred method of dealing with those who disturb the order of the King (and by extension the order that the Elder Club seeks to maintain) is kidnapping followed by sending the kindapped crew a staked and beaten up body with a note along the lines of “I got him back, pay me what you think is appropriate for another member of your crew not to suffer the same fate.” Mekhet/Invictus, member of the Angels of Death.
                                        • Master of Elysium (the “Club Queen”): Grace “Club Queen” Qin is a woman who could have been a model (according to some she was). She’s 5’11’’, has sharp features, and never wears the same outfit twice. Half the clubs in Queens have the owners eating out of the palm of her hand (or perhaps elsewhere). She generally helps maintain the image of the Angels of Death as being rulers of the social scene, and is quick to hold Elysium at all of the hottest clubs in Queens. Daeva, Invictus.
                                        • Hounds (the “Knives”): Hector Alvarez and LaMonty Myers round out the members of the Angels of Death. They’re both Gangrel, dress equally effenimnately (but never quite in drag) and have been together (some say romantically) since before the Angels of Death formed (possibly even in their mortal days). Generally, when a major point needs to be made these two are the ones you bring along, as they are fountains of rage and truly terrifying when using a blade. Gangrel/Invictus, members of the Angels of Death.
                                        • The Bishop: Amanda “Priestess” Long is a Nosferatu of some regard, being that she was sired “accidentally” by the Prince-Archbishop. She’s held the title of Bishop of Queens for at least 50 years, when she took over from the former Bishop Anne who was found to have spoken with the Old Ones (a crime the Lancea et Sanctum takes very seriously). Nosferatu, Lancea et Sanctum, leader of the Sanctified of Queens.
                                      • ….But Manhattan is the City…. (why get stuck in Queens)
                                        • Manhattan’s Kindred are squeezed onto a narrow Island with a population of 1.6 million people. You can’t make a ruckus without other people noticing, and you probably have to take the subway everywhere. There’s a general sense of claustrophobia and competition between the Manhattan Kindred. Queens is a City of nearly 2.3 million people spread out over a significantly larger space. If you want to live in a big house in Jamaica Estates (see DMX and Nas in Belly) or live in a high rise overlooking the East River in Long Island City, Queens is the place to be.
                                        • In Queens a neonate can make a name for themselves. Though nominally Invictus, unlike Manhattan, the local politics aren’t dictated by what covenant you’re in, rather the crew you’re in matters. The King is quick to show favor to those Kindred who deserve it, age means little to him.
                                        • Queens is the place you go if you want to play the Danse Macabre with a more mortal focus. Working amongst the criminals in Queens is how you make a name for yourself in court, and most of the Kindred can’t help getting personally involved in all the money making endeavors.
                                      • Domains: Queens as a domain does not actually encompass all of the areas that make up Queens. Generally it covers every part of the Borough North of the Jackie Robinson Parkway/Grand Central Parkway though parts of Jamaica Estates and Hollis are in dispute. Queens is divided into Four Administrative Zones, each overseen by a member of the Elder Club. They rarely act directly to control the zones, but everyone claiming territory in the zones is very familiar with the elder in charge of the region.
                                        • Western District (Astoria, Long Island City, Sunnyside): The western district is also the most populated due to its proximity to Manhattan. The Angels of Death mostly operate here, along with two other loyal coteries.
                                          • Elder: Lawrence Maniscalco is a 300 year old Gangrel or perhaps older. He spent most of his mortal days as a trapper in upstate New York and was either very familiar with the Algonquin tribes there or perhaps was even half-Algonquin (no one knows). He likes the Angel quite a bit (but refuses to call him anything other than Angel) and has taken him on as his protégé. This has caused some friction with him and the other three members of the club.
                                        • LaGuardia District (Laguardia Airport, Corona): Laguardia airport is the crown jewel of this district. The King put an end to the bickering over the airport, and has given control of this area to Caballos Reales. The other two coteries in the area mostly bicker with each other over distribution rights, as the Caballos Reales are mostly concerned with getting contraband in through the airport.
                                          • Elder: Vincent Josephs is the youngest member of the Elder Club, having been embraced during the turmoil of the 1810s by an eccentric Daeva. He maintains a small hunting ground in Corona, but is also known for placing feeding restriction on a certain family of German immigrants in the area. This rule has generally been interpreted by many of the coteries to “feed on white people” at your peril as only he seems to know who counts.
                                          • Caballos Reales: The Royal Knights (or the Real Knights, depending on who you ask) are a 5 member Invictus coterie that functions along the lines that a more traditional court would use a knightly order for. The Knights have formally sworn an oath of fealty to King Angel, and operate as his agents in the area surrounding the airport. Their leader Dick Nolan (a Nosferatu Invictus) was originally a tough-guy leading yet another coterie of Kindred (The Blood Hawks) in their struggle for territory. When Angel took over, he offered him a reinvention of themselves, offering to consider them Knights of the Invictus and letting them take over his connections amongst the baggage handlers in LaGuardia. The Knights have been loyal ever since.
                                        • The Meadows District (Ridgewood, Flushing and Riker’s Island): The Meadows district is generally the domain of the Sanctified which the Bishop of Queens rules over. The Sanctified tend to all operate largely independently within the district but remain in close contact. The Bishop maintains a very good working relationship with the Elder Club member who controls the area, but of late she’s been having some trouble with a group of independents that Eric Eisenweig (the Elder) has allowed to start operating in the area. In addition, Eisenweig is hypothetically also the administrator of Riker’s but he has little interest in it or the Ghost Wardens.
                                          • Elder: Eric Eisenweig was embraced in the early 1800s and still speaks in a heavy German accent. He’s a Mekhet who lives up to the ideals of his clan, and is a master manipulator. He maintains good relationship with the Bishop but he’s become offended due to her repeated attempts to tell him about Longinus (he follows his own personal brand of Christianity). He’s recently allowed a difficult to control group of independents (Carnage Incorporated).
                                          • Carnage Incorporated: Is a mixed coterie of mostly Gangrel and Nosferatu, who have a penchant for causing mortals to fear for their lives (generally while dressed like clowns from ICP). They specialize in home invasions, muggings, and other type of fuckery that have made tormenting the kine into an art that the Sanctified could only dream of. The group was brought in by Eisenweig in the hopes of breaking the unity of the Sanctified in the area and this has largely worked. Many of the solitary Sanctified have found themselves on the receiving end of Carnage Incorporated, and have started to withdraw and form coteries with each other for mutual protection of their territory.
                                          • Ghost Wardens of Rikers: A mixed Mekhet and Nosferatu coterie controls Riker’s, and are the only remaining Carthians in Queens. Riker’s Island used to be under split control by the childe of Tombs and the Ghost Wardens during its early days, it is now controlled entirely by the Ghost Wardens since at least 1975. The Ghost Wardens are highly secretive, their leader goes by the name Ghost and tends to represent the group. Anything else about them is unknown, except that other Kindred except Eisenweig (who travels to Riker’s often to meet with them) are not allowed on the Island.
                                        • Eastern District (Everything East of Flushing into Long Island by “The Line”): The Eastern district extends to “The Line” one of the most interesting examples of the Kindred Cacophony in existence. The Line is a point the Brooklyn Families established initially with red stones spaced out evenly North of Lake Hempstead. They laid these down when they originally entered into their Covenant with the Old. Most of the rocks have since been removed, so the Brooklyn Families came up with a set of symbolic warnings along all of the major roads leading to the line, warning that crossing that line would mean certain death. Over the years the borders of the domains switched, and a Gangrel named Abraxas was given the territory in exchange for his promise to maintain the sanctity of the Line something he’s done for the past 100 years.
                                          • Elder: Abraxas, a Gangrel embraced during the American Revolution, has controlled the Eastern District since it was fully granted to the Queens domain by the Brooklyn Families 100 years ago. He’s a small skinny man, who tends to dress like a bum (with all the olfactory sting that entails). He is however considered to bed one of the sharpest members of the Elder Club. He’s become concerned with the activity of the Dragons recently, but also thinks they are an important counterweight to the power of King Angel (who he has publicly expressed reservations with).
                                          • The Dragons of Queens: The Dragons of Queens are led by an occult investigator named Clarence Gladewell. They are mostly based around a mansion in Jamaica Estates where a cult murder happened. They’ve recently tried to recreate the events in two locations over the past 15 years (two mass cult suicides). Whatever they’ve done it seems to have achieved their goals, Abraxas is concerned that this might lead to a Masquerade breach. The King considers them too far out to be much concerned.
                                      • Elysiums:
                                        • Club Nights the King receives visitors on Friday and Saturday night at any of the various clubs around Western Queens. He usually is flanked by mortal guards, and another member of the Angeles of Death. If you really want to make a name for yourself, this is where you meet him;
                                        • The Schleswig Social Club: an old and traditional German Beerhall/Meeting Room is where the King holds his monthly Elysiums. Everyone is expected to dress formally to this event and this is one of the rare times where it is obvious that the King is still very much a product of the Invictus.
