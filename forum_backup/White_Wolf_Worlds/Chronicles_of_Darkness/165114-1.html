            I think that's mistakenly conflating a metaphysical significance with a social role. Perhaps among the ancient Greek Kindred, there was some sort of trial by fire involving the Predatory Aura that had to be passed before you could sire. The Kerberos might insist that their relationship with the Beast accords them a greater ability to test the applicants because they provide a better challenge, therefore making them the best at choosing who sires and who doesn't.

            I don't really think it has anything to do with a magical clan sorting hat ability or anything like that. They fit that social role within the Greek Kindred hierarchy. They facilitated their political savvy through the application of their auras/their facility for Majesty. Hence why this bloodline rose to prominence as opposed to another lineage in that social position.

            When they weren't among the Greeks or the Greeks decided they preferred less rigid rules for siring, the Kerberos supposedly adapted, finding other political rolls to function in. Hence that part of the write up. I don't get the impression they failed, things changed, so they changed.
