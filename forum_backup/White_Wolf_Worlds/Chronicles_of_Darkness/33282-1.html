    26 Mage/Prometheans : A young Sleepwalker was found death in his appartement. The scene registers as strange to mages eyes but the can't pinpoint why exactly The only things they are sure is that the corpse was torn apart by spirits. Some days after another Sleepwalker disappears, this time no traces of the body are left. Mages begin to panic as they search for an unseen ennemy as the rare in the know ask themselves why a Created would prey on the Half Awakened.

    27 Mage/Demon : A group of demons allied with local mages become fascinated with the worldview of the Awakened and try to emulate some entities of the mage cosmology. Some are fascinated by the Arcana and claim they could be the key to the God-Machine destruction or salvation, others turn their look of abyssal entities whose nature is so like their own.

        28. Werewolf/Geist: The year is 1714. Defending an entire Caribbean island as your territory is a tall order, but it helps that your pack's totem is the spirit of your small ship. And you'll need all the help you can get. Queen Anne's War, as it's known in the New World, has brought unforeseen consequences. All those loyal privateers have been cast aside by the English Crown and now work their bloody trade on anyone they can find. The Shadow was already being overrun by spirits of violence and greed; now the unquiet dead of both the war and the piracy epidemic are causing problems. Luckily, new allies are offering to join your crew. They're experienced sailors who died during the war--and they're uniquely qualified to deal with those unquiet dead.

            29. Hunter and Vampire: The Lancea Ex Sanctum has always been particularly strong in the city. But then the Malleus Maleficarum starts doing a major push into the domain. The Hunters turn their Benedictions upon the vampires, while kindred respond with Theban sorcery. An old time religious war is breaking out in the city. Participate, or get caught in the cross-fire?
            +Promethean: Added to the fire are other inhuman beings, seemingly watched over by angels of living flame.
            +Mummy: A cult awakens their own prophet/messiah, who unleashed powers straight from the Old Testament upon those who oppose them.
            +Demon: Inside one of the cathedrals on the outskirts of town, a congregation has taken to summoning their own deliverance through strange clockwork angels sent by God.
            +Geist: Rolling into town is a Krewe that believes Christ was the first Sin-Eater, and is looking to expand their following. It helps that they too can turn water to wine.

                    31.) [Werewolf and Mummy] An rare spiritual event is about to occur, Kamduis-Ur and Anpu are... going on a date! Turns out the two canine death deities have been secretly seeing each other for much of recorded history, but their upcoming date is special for some reason. So mummies and Bone Shadows are being teamed up and sent to acquire various items, spirits, creatures and even people that are somehow important for this romantic encounter and bring them to the location in the physical world that has resonance with both romance and death (such as Savannah GA, Paris France or whichever city of love built on it's dead is convenient for the campaign), where the date is set. However, the Deceived are putting it all on the line to ruin this date, as are the Pure, and the Storm Lords have all been offered a great boon by an overprotective Skolis-Ur if they somehow ruin the date enough to break his little sister and that "Jackal-ass" up. And just why is THIS date so special anyway?

                        32.) [Genius, Princess, and Leviathan] They've said Walt Disney was a genius. They were right, in a way, he was a Genius. And one who'd secretly inserted Wonders beneath the rides and key locations of Disney World. Their true purpose is unknown, but is suspected to be involved with monitoring children's dreams and cultivating a certain sense of wonder. Baring the occasional Inspired visiting the area to study Walt's work (or for personal reasons), the area is pretty quiet, supernaturally speaking. Some of that may have to do with the Disney Princesses Nakama that set up shop in the Magic Kingdom. But a storm is brewing - a Cohort of Leviathans is planning on conducting a little ritual during the height of the tourist season. One that looks to inflict the full force of their Wake upon all the malleable minds present.

                            33.) [Demon & Beast, though can be mixed with any two other splats] Suddenly, every Hero in the city dropped their quest. Well, maybe not exacly the quest, but all of them, simultaneously, lost their interest in the Begotten, some of them even in the middle of a fight. Similar events happen on the Unchained side of the fence, except instead of Heroes, we have Hunter Angels. A bit after that, they disappear. All of them are gone. With a poof. Then, in terms of the pro-active anti-supernatural instances(Except mundane Hunters), there's a long period of silence. Neither Demons nor Beasts are not bothered by anything for months. And then, after that, they return. But not in the way you expect them. Heroes when confronted with a Beast react with little more than to a normal citizen, but instead, they obsess over another prey. A mechanical one. Similarily all the relentlessness, mechanical precision and others of the Angels is suddenly turned towards the Children. Just what the heck happened here? How can either of the sides survive the meeting with the new threats? Will they reach their arm to the others, even if simply for mutual survival?

                                34): [Changeling/Demon]: Visit the underpass at midnight on a full moon and spill your blood on an antique florin and it appears to you, an angel straight out of Da Vinci's blueprints - it has wings of stretched fabric and wood, and gears clunk behind a gilded Carnivale mask. They call it The Venetian, and rumors swirl around the freehold as to what exactly it is; an impossibly old changeling, a very odd hob, one of the exiled Gentry, or something even stranger. The Venetian takes parts of people's lives and trades them like trinkets - your face for that of another, your relationship with your daughter for a new ex-wife - in order to let them start over, in order to let them better hide from the Fair Folk. All it costs is your soul.

                                35) [Hunter/Mummy]: You know the monster causes madness in the normal people around it, but you hope your willpower is enough to carry you through to the job's end. As you bury the ancient dagger into the undying thing's flesh, visions flood your mind of people and places long past, speaking tongues long dead... and you realize that you've hunted this creature before, in a life an age ago. It escapes while you're dazed from the flood of memories, but you're filled with new conviction; you /will/ get revenge for the time it killed you in Babylon.

                                36) [Mage/Werewolf]: The wolves are usually seen as a boon to any consilia, provided they don't get in the way too often; the Thyrsus appreciate how they clean up the messes in the spirit realm, and some of them even answer questions when you ask nicely. Which is why it's strange that a young cabal is dead at their claws, stranger than the average murder of the Awakened. The local Forsaken claim ignorance - it was not the work of any of their Tribes, they say - but the spirits whisper about the return of Eater of Names, a totem unknown to the werewolves and the mages alike.

                                    [Beast/All]The city is plagued with disaster after disaster. The Birds of Dis haunt the night, a pair of Huntsmen scour the city for victims, a grand host of Bat Shartha lurk under the city, waiting for the next moonless night that they might fly forth and devour, and countless other calamities. All seems hopeless.

                                    And so the city's Apex, a powerful Beast, has sent missives to the various supernatural factions of the city, calling upon the shadow people to abandon the secrecy and lies so that they might come together and survive. This grand conclave will occur in just a short while, and the players are representatives of their groups called to help ensure it happens. But even discounting those who darkly murmur of traps and cautiousness, getting people to get along is difficult when their ideology is similar; getting, say, the Hunters in Darkness and the Predator Kings to sit down at the same table, much less work together, may be a herculean task. And that's assuming the topics of discussion don't try to crash the party...

                                        38) [Vampire/Mummy]: The Khaibit are a secretive bloodline, largely known for their roles as servants to the powerful; such has been their role since time well beyond the memory of even the most ancient elder. It's because of this that many in the domain grow concerned when a large flock of them appear and beging ruthlessly carving out a niche, destroying local mortal powers in the occult underground and fortifying a new Shadow Cult around them. What has brought them here, and why do they seem so harried? And just what is in that sarcophagus they've brought with them and buried deep beneath that skyscraper downtown?

                                        39) [Geist/Mummy]: It isn't just people that leave behind ghosts. What does it look like when a whole civilization is dragged down into the Underworld... and what happens when it tries to come back? The new museum exhibit has powerful and ancient ghosts anchored to the artifacts, mute and devoted utterly to vigilance; the freshly dead are being enslaved in greater numbers than ever before. Every Sin-Eater knows what it's like to come back from the grave, but do they stand a chance against those who can never die?

                                        40) [Demon/Mage]: The Ministry of Panopticon has driven all other Seer factions out of the city, and have turned their sights on the local Pentacle mages. Cursory attempts at espionage reveal claims from these Seers that The Eye has blessed them by descending to the Fallen World physically, has sent forth angels to act as weapons and patrons for their pylons. From the shadows come forth unlikely allies; the Unchained, who claim the situation is not what it seems...

                                        41) [Werewolf/Mummy, others included]: A bloody turf war has consumed the area, with the chaos spilling out into the daylight streets that threatens to cause mass hysteria. The media spins it as heightened gang activity, but those in the know have caught glimpses of the truth; a splinter group of Bone Shadows who claim allegiance to Kamduis-Ur in the guise of Anubis and a cult dedicated to a Mummy that call themselves the Argent Hand of Osiris. Will you try and broker peace between the two ancient foes, or back one of the factions and hope peace will come with victory?

                                            42) [Beast/All] Tensions rise among the city's supernatural communities when a new group of hunters begin to make their presence known. Before long, a series of attacks have been made. A vampire's elder's Haven is set aflame by a summoned brood of fire spirits, who claim to have been compelled to do so by a potent enchantment. A pack of werewolves are found murdered by the docks, the hunters who killed them easily matching their savagery with ruthless skill. Even the local Consilium was not spared, with many Mages cut down in a surprise attack while they were gathered together. The only lead any of the affected groups have to link the groups together is a Sign marked on many of the victims by these hunters.

                                                            47: (Hunter, Genius) So it turns out Tesla was some kind of evil mad scientist, go figure. What's really shocking is that he was hounded at every turn by none other than Thomas Edison. A secret journal of Edison's is rumored to be somewhere in New Jersey, hidden through the decades to keep it away from the wrong hands and eyes. The problem is that there's been activity at the remains of Wardenclyffe Tower, and if something does happen so close to New York City there's no chance of a cover up. Will hunter and genius be able to work together? Will Edison's journal and Tesla's secrets be too valuable to let into the hands of the other? And what would it mean for either group if Edison's journal spread through either community?
                                                            * Promethean: More importantly, what does it mean to have more information on the men who were able to harness lightning on an industrial scale? Could this help on the path to the New Dawn? Or be a further detour away from humanity?

                                                            48: (Hunter: Barrett Commission/TFV, Mage: Adamantine Arrow/Silver Ladder) Recent intelligence has come to our attention that the Marine Corps has been infiltrated, and needs a thorough sweep to remove all dangerous elements. Unfortunately, we can't handle this one on our own. Due to the nature of what's happening, we need to work with another force that knows. Work with them, clear house, and get back any information you have on who these people are and what they're capable of.
