                                                So, some thoughts about "primitive vampires" in the Era-

                                                As it looks like each template has 2-3 versions for a proto-version of it, it makes sense that we should try and spin a few more ideas for our dear old bloodsuckers. The first would be the one we have already pitched- that is, vampires who get a strix trapped inside of them. As we know, evolution doesn't act only upon vampires, but upon the Owls too, and the Birds of Dis are hinted to be far from a unified breed. As such, let's assume that while the modern strix do have the ability to embody themselves in the dead without any side effects, it may not always been that way. Perhaps, long before, there were strix who had some troubles as using that ability- while it worked fine for animals and "soulless humanoids", those strix couldn't handle well the removal of the residues of the soul from the dead corpse, and when they tried to possess the dead they discovered they can't get out, and that the Vitae has animated the remains of the lost soul back to life. The way I see it, those vampires are much more "dead" than the modern kind- no blush of life or any trick like that. They also have Shadow Potency instead of Blood Potency, and they are required to drink the breath of other humans in order to survive. On the up side, they are not vulnerable to the regular banes of fire and sunlight, and they also won't be paralyzed from being staked (as their heart is not the center of their "vital system").

                                                Another version could rely of the Pijavica as "proto vampires"- corpses who, after not being buried and being exposed to occult powers, spontaneously decompose into masses of blood which move through the the wilderness and sleep in the earth during the day. When they find a suitable host, they swarm into their throats and infuse themselves with the person to create a some sort of symbiosis. turning them into a mutated, monstrous beings who are fluid of form and require blood in order to survive. You could replace the "unburied corpses" origin with them being the spilled blood of some mythical beast slaughtered by the rising homo sapiens long ago, some sort of making them into the "blood of Echidna". Whatever the case, the outcome of those "embraces" ends up with some much closer to the modern Kindred, even if more powerful and dreadfully unstable. They are also yet to be able to reproduce, and when killed they would quickly rot away into a blood pool, which would then search for their new victim/host.

                                                Third idea is perhaps using the Mnemovores from Wicked Dead, only with a bit more.. mysterious way. Ancient beings who lurk in the shadows of time which devour the minds on unaware humans, feeding upon their memories and leaving hollowed brains behind. I imagine them rising from mists and fogs, lurking unseen and silent. While most of their victims ends up dead and gone, some of those brought into a blank state start devouring memories by their own, becoming some sort of "psychic vampires"- still alive, but ever hungry for the minds of others in order to maintain their own. They whisper tales about the "people in the mist" and the fear they spread. Why, how and if those creatures were extinct are yet unknown.

                                                Finally, while not an actual idea and more of a concept, I think about using the Hollows as a separate form of primitive vampirism, one descended from pre-human races, tentacled monsters which are frightened by the "Tall Ones" and yet prey upon their life and soul. They are an ancient race, which has started to decline, and in order to avoid extinction they have turned to devour parts of the human souls and replace them with certain parts of themselves, creating occult abominations with no shadow or echo. They may also be used as some sort of crossover with Mummy, sharing few cosmological principles together such as the ancient non human civilizations and the five folded soul.

                                                EDIT- so, considering the "Sanctity of 5" rule, it makes sense that I'll add one more idea to complete the set! Very well, then. Perhaps we could play with the "aswang" angle to form "living vampires"- humans who are transformed by a supernatural plague which usually kills them, but those who survive find themselves transformed into predators who thirst for human blood. They have some limited shapeshifting ability, turning from their "mortal" form to their "hunter form", and they gather together in some form of ancient tribes which ambush for lone travelers and sneak into human communities.

                                                Well, that's what I have for now. Feel free to share your thoughts about the subject.

                                                        And now, some more thought about Werewolf Primitives-

                                                        The first would be, of course, the "idigam infused humans". I think about them as early experiments done by the Shapeless in human Essence, creating mercurial and unstable shapeshifters who can Essence Shape themselves and, at higher levels, others and the world around them. With the idigam being so alien, I think about those creatures as unintentional creations, those who are formed when the idigam "partially coalesce" inside of their souls, carrying a piece of an ancient and alien deity inside of them. They could be both the "high priests" of the Formless or their greatest enemies who use their powers against them.

                                                        The second idea is for humans and beasts who are lost in the Border Marches, and have their spirit meld into flesh and animal melds into man. By the end of the process, the humans become much more animalistic, and the animals much more human like. Their soul becomes incarnated, being too big to be hosted inside of their body, and takes the form of what would be later known as an Omphalus Stone. They share many properties with the Pangaeans, resonating with the Pillars of Creation as they wear their skins and channel their primal powers as demigods of both beasts and men. I think on partially base them around the Purified, making them a "proto template" of spiritual immortals.

                                                        The next idea is using the Old Ones which I once worked upon, only that this time they are still alive. Those are the ancient shapeshifters, who have shifted between predators and prey since the ancient times when the Lizard Kings ruled the world. They slowly creep into the skins of their prey, learning them and adapting to their ways so they could walk among them unseen. Being creatures of flesh, they are constantly evolving and improving to become better and better predators- and with the rise of humanity, the ancient scaled monsters has also turned to learn what they can from humanity and eat it from the inside. However, the naked monkeys has proved themselves more problematic than they thought. Souls are not easy to be digested by those terrors, and humans have discovered that by eating the apex predators, they can harness their memories- and take their place at the top of the food chain.

                                                        With the change in the Shadow's structure and the rise of the new breed of spirits, there is surely a lot of chaos during that Era- many sources of Essence are probably dried out or taken over by the idigam, and many are not aware of the dangers of Essence mixing. From those Essence wars, Magaths rise as they face the choice between hibernation (and possibly death), and mad life. While that's bad on itself, things become even worse as Magath add souls to the mix, and in the flux of concepts they gain human like properties, invading the world of Flesh and shaping themselves a body out of the elements of the world, one which is shifting and unstable, but is good enough for them to hide among humanity in the fear of Hisil's royalty. I think on using my Intruder splat as a concept for them, which is in turn based around Monte Cook's Demons.

                                                        Finally, we must ask ourselves what about the so well desired Void Leviathans and their terrible horrors from Outer Space? For now, I think about the visitation of those alien beasts from the Beyond, who burn all they can see with their cold, starry light. However, while consumed by the terrors of the horrors of the void, some find themselves reborn in the frozen flames- or, perhaps, changed. People who carry inside of them the sparks of visiting monstrosities, and when cloaked by the light of the stars they can channel the miracles of the Beyond into form (I lean toward using the long dead Phoenix: the Risen as an inspiration, giving it, how appropriate, a rebirth :P)

                                                            And as I haven't commented yesterday, here is my version for Mage's Primitives-

                                                            Like I talked about before, the first template I wish to suggest is that of Invokers- that is, people who are "Awakened to the Bound". Unlike the conventional Awakening, the Invokers don't go through a Mystery Play guided by the symbols of the Supernal. Instead, we have the Old Gods which were banished from Heaven, creatures of wild and primal magic who are bound to the Fallen after being exiled by the Kings of the Lie. Feeding upon magic and souls, the Bound cut away pieces of their chosen vessels and instead transplant into them shards of their power, allowing them a direct connection to Supernal through the Bound, and as such giving them a measure of protection from the Abyss's corruption and paradox. However, it come at a terrible cost- for the prophets of the Old Gods are also their slaves, and suffer from their torment and twisted desires (think 2e's Infernal Exalts, in the CofD setting).

                                                            Now, as Khanwulf has mentioned, the Abyss is, of course, already around- and many things lurk in that empty, primordial void. Beings of ancient order, of time which never was, lurk in the dark corners of that vast emptiness, and in certain places where those fictional worlds are stable enough, enlightenment may be reached, even if not as you expect it to be. Those ancient terrors, things which should never exist, bind themselves to the mind and souls of those who stumble upon those empty places, and slowly corrupt their patterns and drive them into madness. Some, however, manage to tap into the power of those alien beings, and evade insanity by accepting their fate in a shattered world- and are able to conjure both the unholy spirit and their home worlds into existence.

                                                            While Atlantis is fallen, the many civilizations which once build it linger still, exist in the blood of their descendants. Magical lineages of dragons are spread upon the world, as the atlanteans call upon the magic in their blood to form miracles and wonders. In later time, many of those proud legacies would slowly degrade into Proximi dynasties, as the Quiescence corrupts their magic- but at this Era, the Dragon People wear their brands in pride, a p[roof that their are not fully human, and that they carry in them the blood of the true kings and queen of the fallen worlds. Mechanically, they are going to be somewhere between Proximi and Mages- lacking certain the broad capabilities of the Awakened but with much greater potency for their spell power.

                                                            As for the fourth primitive, we are going to look upon the oldest of Reapers- beings which have never been humans, yet are quickly to start preying upon the tasty, soul bearing humans as a source of nourishment. Oh, those beings are far from being at the top of the food chain, nothing like the powerful Bound, the alien abyssal horrors or the majestic dragons of Atlantis. They are parasites, creatures who cling to others and slowly grasp their souls with their branches and root. Those are soul eating trees and plants, which bare tasty fruits born from the souls which they ate. Those fruits are rumored to grant great wisdom to those who manage to grasp them- and perhaps may even be how humans got souls in the first place. But most who try to get them die slowly, their souls are being sucked away and their flesh decomposed to feed the plant. Sometimes, however, someone manage to go through the ordeal, having their soul partially eaten- but they manage to capture the fruit before their destined death. They survive the experience- but at a terrible price. A seed of the plants keeps eating at their souls, and as such they require other souls to nourish them, and live upon their expense.

                                                            Finally, as it was mentioned by Seraph Kitty, as we are talking about a post-Fall Era, we should have "regular mages"- that is, mages who are Awakened by the Watchtowers and have their souls called by higher realms. However, while the light of the Supernal does reach the Fallen in this Era, it is still not well defined- the Lie is not as solid, and the Paths still mix and mingle. I think on a much more fluid version of the modern Awakened, something which generates as the "Truth" is yet to collapse, and many worlds exist in superposition. Those who Awake in this Era discover the desolated Sea of Stars (the Astral) and the flexible soul of humanity, and have their powers manifest themselves upon their flesh and spirit. Paradox is still a problem, but magic flows much more potently, allowing the mages to live in their world and dance with the primal energies of the world before.
