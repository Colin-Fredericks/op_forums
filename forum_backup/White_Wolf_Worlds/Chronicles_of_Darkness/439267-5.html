        Overall I am really excited for this book. The merits presented thus far are largely excellent; most of them feel useful without overshadowing existing merits. I'm also impressed by the variety and flavor. Avoidance is a great style that could work equally well for pacifists, cowards, or tricksters. Fair Fighter is a delightful incongruity. Animal Ken gets not one but three styles to work with.

        Now to specific questions/concerns. Body as weapon (3) seems a bit underwhelming. For one more dot in Martial Arts I can get the same benefit, along with other techniques, and without any of the drawbacks. Especially if mix and match style progression is going to be a thing I'm hard pressed to think of a situation where I wouldn't take the 4th dot that allows me to always deal lethal without spending willpower or suffering penalties later.

        Speaking of style progression, I'm also having difficulty interpreting that passage. Penta's explanation in post 66 makes the most sense to me but I await further clarification.

        Uke Nagashi (Iaijutsu 4th dot) I'm not sure why this merit increases your defense, given that it negates an incoming attack. Does it only counter one attack per turn? Also, raising defense probably lowers the opponent's successes, and if I understand the merit correctly that in turn lowers your counter damage. I like most of this merit but Armed Defense does this particular idea better. Then again Armed Defense is the most abusable fighting style in core 2e.
        Also the fifth dot should probably be a contested roll rather than a resisted one since there's no degree of success.

        Strike and Develop (Spear and Bayonet 3rd dot) This is a 3rd dot merit that effectively doubles your successes for weapons that already deal +1 or +2 lethal. Boxing's 4th dot "roll dex bonus damage" is potentially op if paired with fighting finesse (allowing players to double dip dex) but that requires more set up and investment than this. At minimum this is a bonus +1 lethal, and while the damage over time factor is unique I think a inflicting a penalty might be more appropriate.

        Double Strike (Two Weapon Fighting 4th dot) I'm really unclear on how this works. Do you split your pool before defense, or after? What if the opponents have different defense scores? Even assuming both opponents have a modest defense of four and my character is a master with ten dice, splitting my remaining six evenly between them and taking a -2 on each leaves me with... 1 die to attack each opponent. This is in no way a good choice and I would be much better served with another merit.

        Kiriage I'm assuming that the +2 targeted strike is after the -2/-3 for specified targets, instead of replacing those modifiers? That is, it effectively reduces the called shot penalty by two rather than eliminating the penalty *and* providing a bonus. So a called arm strike is a 0 and hand is -1, assuming no further modifiers are in place from other styles. I'm not sure it should apply to hands at all, since attacking the hand is already a very minor additional risk for a much higher chance of inflicting arm wrack, so much so that it's only really worth trying for the arms if hands would drop you all the way to chance die.

        Similarly, is Boot Party taking into account the usual +2 to attack a prone opponent (who doesn't have Rolling or Ground Fighting)? I would be interested to combine it with ground and pound for a high risk/high reward move. Take a penalty to your attack and go prone for the chance to inflict massive damage? I'd try it.

                Air of Menace: It's a merit that makes a statement about a character. I like merits that makes statements about characters. However, NPCs/Mooks don't have willpower to spend, which means that as long as the character with this merit doesn't throw the first punch, they have a /lot/ of leeway in what they can get away with.

                Cohesive Unit: This is a grab-bag of ideas that feels really rough. The pool mechanic is a smidge "eh" in the sense that it's another thing to keep track of. I think the intent, judging from the drawback, is that a group can only benefit from one leader with this merit. That isn't explicit enough. "Actions to help the team" is about as vague as you can get. Drawing on a pool of dice, etc etc are all mechanics and phrasing that are not commonly found in the Storyteller System. Id recommend something more familiar.

                Support Network (first time): I like this conceptually. It has no actual mechanics, though. I'd also almost want it to be cheaper tied to other social merits... Hrm. Maybe, something to bat around.

                Support Network (one dot version): This is listed twice for some reason. "One negative condition" is really vague and will be abused horribly. Do you get a beat for resolving conditions this way?

                Professional Therapist: Feels boring, somehow. Also between this and Meditative Mind, breaking points become pretty trivial pretty quickly. I mean that's also five merit dots so...?

                Support Group: Don't use the phrase "mind rape." I don't mind it personally but I've gotten in to several hour long discussions with people who are quite passionate about that phrase not being used. Strangely less boring in my mind than Professional Therapist, largely because it represents a certain amount of personal development on the part of the character. My gut says to limit it to 1/story and talk about something that made you lose integrity, to prevent it from becoming routine and boring.

                Torturer: Sure.

                At Any Cost: Complicated and hard to follow. It isn't when you stop to think about it, but you also have to stop to think about it. With two variables (Resolve and Composure) its actual value will vary widely. I also feel like there's going to be some tilt somewhere that's just plain silly when combined with this but... eh.

                Defender: The shounen anime merit! I like the concept. Cost may need to be adjusted slightly. I'd throw in rules for refilling this extra pool (possibly by having a touching or meaningful scene with someone who is your friends / family). More stringent criteria than "friends or family" may be a good idea too, not sure.

                Dominating the Center Line: Epic name for a meh effect. Wording is off, needs to clarify that it triggers on the user's miss and what "similarly armed" means. Or just give it a simpler mechanic.

                Know the Territory: I do not like effects with two variables in them (Wits and Composure) in this case. It looks small, but it can add up. Something in my gut is flagging this merit in general though. The trap option definately needs looking at.

                Living With Death: This is going to stack horribly with other things that affect All Out Attacks. The one Garou fighting style is the first thing that comes to mind (use Totem benefit to pick this up ignoring the prerequisits). I'm sure there's other stuff, too.

                Mental Scarring: "Mental Conditions" is not a game term. The drawback makes for a lot of book-keeping. Like the concept.

                Power Object: I really like the concept. I'd almost say have this trade effects with Professional Therapist.

                Master of the Mask: I feel like this is just Striking Looks with a fancier name.

                Aggressive Driving: ngl, never really look at driving rules, nor do my players.

                Body As Weapon: Feels weak, honestly.

                Drone Control Style: Feels like something yo need just to use drones ata ll? Hard to look at this merit in a vacuum and understand it.

                            Armored Combat
                            O - The "wear for a chapter" thing feels like tedium that eats wordcount without much benefit.
                            OO - Feels weakish. Maybe up it to a -2 penalty?
                            OOO - Wording is clunky, but it's good.
                            Overall: I find it interesting that this is a style you take to increase offense, not defense. 3 EXP for +2L damage to your brawl and weapon attacks is nice. This isn't a bad thing, but the name and implications of a combat style centered around armor implies that you take it to be tankier.

                            Avoidance
                            O - I love this. For one dot you can make a non-combat character that won't easily get murdered in combat, opening up more concepts in more games.
                            OO - Eh. Meh? This feels like a sucker punch to your allies. I'd put the choice in the defending player's hands, not the merit-holder's. I mean it technically is, but not in a healthy way "Well I'm not your ally!"
                            OOO - Hrm... Like the concept.
                            OOOO - It's definitely a power that should be here. Feels underwhelming for a four-dot maneuver, though.
                            Overall: I love this concept and definitely want to see it in play. It's something I feel the game needs. The later dots could use some work, in my opinion. If you want to take this style from "really good" to "great" then you need to give someone with avoidance tactical decisions during combat without breaking theme. A tall order, I know.

                            Berserker Style
                            O - Cool and thematic.
                            OO - This is pretty meh.
                            OOO - This is going to be a pain to keep track of. Really it's use is to make Brass Knuckles and Knives with 3L damage, practically speaking.
                            Overall: Feels a bit all over the place. I'd like a tighter mechanical thematic. It doesn't feel very berserker-rage-y, if that makes sense. If Warcry was buffed up, maybe moved to dot 3 (affects all opponents, maybe impacts Defense and/or Initiative) it'd be cool.

                            Bowmanship
                            O - Nice basic ability. You should so make it +(Merit Dots) instead of +2.
                            OO - Wait, what? This is referencing what I assume are basic mechanics I'm unfamiliar with. Bows have variable damage now?
                            OOO - Feels complicated for what it does. I'd just apply Shaken against unaware opponents.
                            OOOO - Neat. I like it.
                            Overall: Why the heck does this use firearms when bows normally use Athletics? Or am I wrong there? Otherwise the style is pretty cool and bow users should love it!

                            Boxing
                            O - Solid.
                            OO - I like it, and it creates a clear picture in my head.
                            OOO - I would just grant brawl attacks the Stun weapon quality and make this the four-dot, personally. That said, it's pretty nice. Is a strike against the head -3 total (-2 then -1) or -2 total?
                            OOOO - I don't like added dice rolls, personally. This also feels like something that should cost willpower or some such. I'd almost say 8-again is a good mechanic to represent combinations.
                            OOOOO - This is obscenely overpowered, especially when stacked with other things that add to brawl damage. Armored Combat 3, Boxing 4, Martial Arts 5, Street Fighting 4, etc etc.
                            Overall: A really solid style with a strong thematic. Needs some tweaks but is definitely on the right track.

                            Chain Weapons
                            O - Hrm. I like this. I don't like it as a one-dot power, but I do like it. Feels like too much of a change in the fighting dynamic for one exp. Maybe as a capstone power it's great. This is going to stack in horrible ways with Vigor.
                            OO - I really like this, and would make it the one-dot power. It feels foundational. I'd make it a flat bonus to grapple rolls if anything, otherwise you'll have magic chains with a +10 bonus.
                            OOO - Hah, this is pretty fun.
                            Overall: This style could benefit from going to 4 or 5 dots and a bunch of polish, but it has some cool thematics going on.

                            ALTERNATE Chain Weapons
                            O - I like the thematic, but there's a little too much going on and it's a huge wordcount sink. I know it's rough, but. "Targets that attempt to attack you in melee take 1L damage unless they take a -2 penalty to their attack roll" is fine.
                            OO - Feels boring and speedbump-y. If it had something that built off of it later, it might be cool.
                            OOO - This is hugely overpowered with allies. An aside, I think light weapon style has something like this, and you can use light weapon style with chains.
                            OOOO - So... you get a +1, maybe +2 bonus to called shots? Meh.
                            OOOOO - Chains are melee weapons. There is no "ranged grapple" possible. This is just weird and trying too hard to be epic.
                            Overall: Not a fan of this aternative version. I could see playing with the ranged attack and aiming mechanic a bit more.

                            Combat Archery
                            O - References mechanics I don't have. If bows effectively take a turn to reload in between shots then this merit is mandatory to make them really viable. That's not necessarily a bad thing, and one could argue it's an EXP towards fidelity, but it is something to be aware of when making mutually exclusive styles.
                            OO - Meh, I'd just negate the penalties all the time.
                            OOO - This can potentially allow two attacks per turn, which is generally considered bad juju.
                            OOOO - I like this one. It's cinematic and uses existing rules and etc. Very nice.
                            OOOOO - This is horrendously overpowered. It allows you to move a great distance (or do other things) to kite opponents /and/ it negates defense. I think the core concept is usable--maybe even worth a style unto itself--but it needs work.
                            Overall: This one is really rough and I look forward to seeing the next draft. I'm really not sure why this one uses athletics while the other one uses firearms? If you want to put in dice-swapping mechanics I'd make it the one-dot or two-dot power.

                            Falconry
                            O - I feel like any small animal you'll actually be concerned about will be supernatural in nature and probably not subject to shaken. Neat idea, but needs different mechanics.
                            OO - Does the bird do this separately and in addition to your action, or do you sacrifice your action for the bird?
                            OOO - If you use a falcon's traits for this, then it will always be a chance die.
                            OOOO - I don't actually understand what this does or how it's suppossed to work.
                            Overall: I think the animal styles need to be thought out more, and they should work without multiplying the user's actions if at all possible.

                            Grappling
                            O - Pretty sure you can already inflict the knocked down tilt with a grapple?
                            OO - I would just make this a Move and incompatible with the Damage Move. The way it's written, this can be used outside a grapple (-2 attack roll) and you can't get your extra move on an exceptional success.
                            OOO - I like it! Makes that not-frequently-used maneuver much more interesting.
                            OOOO - (Dynamic Guard). Weirdly named, but okay for the mechanic side of things. Kinda oddly worded since it isn't really a maneuver?
                            OOOO - (Lock Flow). Sure. I'd make it apply to Restraining too.
                            OOOOO - (Tap or Snap). I like it a lot!
                            OOOOO - (Positional Dominance). This is also pretty great. I fear it /might/ be overpowered, but I'm not sure?
                            Overall: I really like the work done here. Sure it needs a bit of polish, but it's pretty close.

                            Iaijutsu
                            O - This is a pretty huge benefit that isn't really supported by the fluff in an way. You just randomly get a bonus to avoid surprise because you're holding a sword?.Some dissonance there.
                            OO - I would just make it ignore the penalty in general. Why put the turn tracker in? I'd also make this the one-dot and make it lower the penalty by your dots in this merit (max +0).
                            OOO - This feels like a four-dot or five-dot maneuver and it needs to sacrifice defense or something. Otherwise it's really cool.
                            OOOO - This does too much. I'd split it up into two different maneuvers. One for the +Defense, one for the counter-attack. The counter attack is probably too powerful.
                            OOOOO - This is pretty overpowered.
                            Overall: Nix the five-dot, swap 2 and 1, shift 3 and 4 both up and find a new 3-dot. This is a really solid style for the most part, but it needs a lot of fine-tuning.

                            K-9 Style
                            Effect: Wait so all that falconry stuff requires animal ken rolls?
                            O - I feel like this isn't really a maneuver so much as a stand-alone merit.
                            OO - Sure
                            OOO - I like this one.
                            OOOO - I like this one too.
                            Overall: Has all my comments with regards to using animals in fights, but! I like this style a lot more. I could see a polished up version of this making playing with a police dog fun.

                                Combat Archery is a bit too powerful for my liking. Well, except the first dot, which I would keep around as a merit. But over the course of the style, bows start to become better than guns, capable of doing high damage (going as far as burst fire), without the noise and also ignoring defense. It even gets rid of the disadvantage of a target trying to close the distance. If this was a supplement for over the top action, it would be great. But it feels a little too power fantasy to tap into the more horrorfying nature of real violence. It also goes back to athletics for the roll, which I am not a fan of. In GMC, with athletics adding to defense, archers get even more benefit from the stat they use for combat, whereas other fighters have to invest in a merit (which is also only useful when using the skill). I suppose the same is true of thrown weapon fighters, but thrown weapons aren't capable of causing as much harm, generally. In several games I have played and ran, archery running with athletics always came across as overpowered.

                                The other archery style is nice. Useful, but without being too much. The Out of Nowhere part is incredibly thematic. As I said before though, the first level of Combat Archery would be a good merit.

                                I am absolutely loving some of the more niche styles, like falconry. It really opens up some interesting character concepts, which is always a good thing.

                                            Originally posted by Rani Neferet View Post


                                            So your saying each maneuver is going to still be 1xp regardless of rating so long as you have the prerequisite ratings worth of maneuvers?

                                            You count each maneuver according to dots, so if you can figure out a path, you could buy a 1-dot, 2-dot, 3-dot, and 4-dot maneuver with the same tag spending 4 experience to do so. You would have to meet prerequisites so it could easily require more experience. You could also spend 10 experience on 1-dot maneuvers with the same tag to prepare you for a 5-dot instead. This means you could spend 5 experience to get a 5-dot maneuver or up to 11 experience, not including other prerequisites.

                                            Note: last sentence is the sum of experience spent on the last maneuver and all maneuvers required to meet style prerequisites.
