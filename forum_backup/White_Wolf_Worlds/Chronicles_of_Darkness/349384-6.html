    Today's Submission

    Pinhead
    The entity known as “Pinhead” is a high ranking Cenobite. Cenobites are a caste of Demonic beings that hail from a realm known only as the Labyrinth and do the bidding of a vast alien entity called Leviathan. Pinheads many titles include “The Black Pope of Hell”, “The Angel of Suffering” or simply “The Priest”. As befitting his station, Pinhead is vastly powerful and is largely immune to anything as vulgar as physical violence or the demands of petty conjurers. So great is his power it is said that Pinhead only answers to Leviathan itself. Despite his transformation into a Cenobite, Pinhead retains a significant amount of his former human perspective and retains his Virtue and Vice.

    Virtue: Contemplative – Pinhead is measured and thoughtful, never taking any action (either merciful or cruel) without due consideration.
    Vice: Ennui – Pinhead is prone to boredom and disenchantment, eventually questioning the purpose of existence entirely.

    Rank: 5 (Dominion)
    Malapraxis: Pinhead is linked to the vices exercised in pursuit of extreme forms of pleasure and pain. He also has some power over those with an obsession for solving riddles and puzzles.
    Attributes: Power: 14, Finesse: 14, Resistance: 14
    Willpower: 10
    Essence: 50
    Initiative: 28
    Defense: 14
    Speed: 33
    Size: 5
    Corpus: 19
    Influences: Desire 3, Suffering 3
    Ban: Pinhead can only use manifestations in the presence of a solved LeMarchand puzzle box. Anyone holding a puzzle box can make an offer or negotiation that Pinhead must hear out before taking any other action.
    Bane: LeMarchand’s Puzzle Boxes
    Demonic Powers: Corruption, Dragon’s Tongue, Lord of Lies, Keeper of Secrets, Soul of Sin
    Manifestations: Twilight Form*; Avernian Gateway, Discorporate, Image, Materialize, Shadow Gateway
    Numina: Awe, Babel (Inferno.pg. 52), Blast, Dement, Ensnare (Book of Spirits, pg. 140), Hallucination, Rapture, Regenerate, Seek, Telekinesis

    The Lament Configuration
    It is believed that Phillip LeMarchand (a French architect, artisan, designer, and possibly mass-murderer) constructed more than 270 of his unique puzzle boxes before he vanished. The rumors surrounding the devices suggest that they can provide “the ultimate pleasure” once solved. Details about what that “pleasure” might be remain vague. This particular puzzle “The Lament Configuration” is LeMarchand's first and arguably most classic, puzzle construct. When operated properly, a pleasing melody issues from within which belies its true nature.
    Size: 1 Durability: 4 Availability: 4

    Powers
    Bad Penny: The Lament Configuration is drawn to those that will use the device to its full potential (most commonly jaded thrill seekers or the insatiably curious).

    Puzzle Box: Wits/Intelligence + Academics/Investigation (extended; 10 minutes/10 successes). A success opens the box and plays a pleasant melody. If willpower were spent for any of the extended rolls, the puzzle box instead summons Pinhead and a cadre of attendant Cenobites. This effect is considered an Invocation (Inferno, pg. 74).

    Reward Temptation: Manipulation +Subterfuge; Success/Days; Regain Willpower when acting in accordance to Vice. While under the influence of this ability, the user cannot regain willpower from Virtue, suffers a -1 to Breaking Point checks and gains the Obsession [Solving the Puzzlebox] Condition

    Unbreakable: Immune to Mundane Damage

    Notes: The depiction of Pinhead has varied widely over the series of films, novels, and comics adaptations. The version presented draws inspiration from the first 3 films and the comic series (both the original from Epic and the recent series from BOOM!)

    The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
    Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
    The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
    Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
    Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

        I'm not very good at crunch, but I do like me some Hotline Miami:

        The Fans:

        Ashwood Abbey cell. After discovering an almost godlike killer had effectively killed every member of the Russian Mafia in Miami, these five people fell in love, and decided that an escape from their hated, shallow lives would be to cosplay him. Accurately. They're only starting up, but already the changelings of the Court of Eternal Summer quietly trade warnings and legends about a colorful van that disgorges masked killers who do not negotiate, do not speak, only kill, leaving no survivors among their intended targets. All stories of them come from the people they spare, and to their credit they don't hurt innocents, only attacking people they've heard bad stories about. What they don't do, noticeably, is question whether or not the targets deserve mercy...and most of said targets are mortal.

        Tony:

        Virtue: Loyal. He's rude, abrasive, and more than a tad cruel, but Tony's the best friend you could ever have. He helps you hide the bodies, after all.

        Vice: Sadistic. You don't develop a killing style based around literally beating the life out of people with nearly-bare fists without the capacity to enjoy it.

        The tiger-masked effective leader of the Fans, Tony is a self-admitted thug with a pair of enchanted brass knuckles that enhance his already-gifted strength, allowing him to kill or incapacitate with a single punch. Of the Fans, he's the most bloodthirsty and least altruistic, seeing the Vigil as mostly a way to indulge his bloodthirst in a borderline-acceptable way.

        Corey

        Virtue:         Dedicated. Corey spent years working on her martial arts and reflexes, and she applies that focus to everything in her life.

        Vice: Submissive. She doesn't really like thinking for herself, she finds it a lot easier to let her friends do the planning. If that involves mass murder, so be it.

        The lithe, zebra-masked stealth specialist, Corey is a quiet, meek woman who mostly signed up to sharpen her already masterful skills with a katana. Corey possesses almost superhuman reflexes; if she's anticipating violence, she can actually roll out of the way of gunfire, so long as she knows who's shooting and where from.

        The Swan Twins (Alex and Ash Davis)

        Virtue        : Loyal. The Swans define loyalty, to each other and to their friends. It even reflects their hunting style, the twins are always working as a team to counterbalance their weaknesses.

        Vice: Cynical. Alex, and to a lesser extent Ash, are so pessimistic about human nature that killing people honestly doesn't strike hem as an inherently bad thing.

        Alex and Ash Davis are fraternal twins, and revel in the fact that if you can't see their faces it's a bit hard to tell either of the muscular siblings apart (they both wear motorcycle helmets decked out to resemble identical swan heads, with the only clue to who's who being the number on the forehead). Ash is the team's planner and mechanic, while Alex is the muscle-not that it matters much, because the Twins never stray far from each other during a mission. Alex is capable of wielding a chainsaw as a sword, while Ash provides gun support from safely behind her.

        Mark

        Virtue        : Kind. Mark's the only Fan for whom altruism is the primary motivation; he honestly thinks they're doing something to help.

        Vice: Indulgent. Mark's a hedonist, born and bred. Food, adrenaline, drugs, you name is, he's done it, and is doing it.

        Tall and wide, bear-masked Mark doesn't really have a role on the team, but given how he can wield a machine gun in each hand without hurting himself, nobody minds. He's about the closest thing to a "true Hunter" of the Fans, being here just as much for the sake of saving people as getting his heroic bloodshed kicks, but he's still an Abbey man; he assigns as much moral weight to his bloodthirst as he does buying pizzas.

                            Oh cool, this is still going strong. I posted earlier in this about doing up the Originals as part of Vampire the Requiem, basically making them another origin story for the five main clans and VII, even while pointing out no one really believes it because of the obvious contradictions in known vampire history. Decided to try an alternate take, of the "TVD" style vampires as their own clan, based off them.

                            Basically, Clan Mikaelson, though they'd probably have a cooler, more ancient sounding name, like the others. Also would allow me to use it, without those pesky copyright issues.

                            So basically, this particular clan got all the luck. Many of their weaknesses are easily mitigated, except for one. OR, you can skip the Clan business and make them replace the normal vampire template, and treat the Main Five Clans as bloodlines spun off from the five siblings. Can make it work either way.

                            Clan Mikaelson

                            Favored Attributes: Strength, and Charisma

                            Clan Disciplines: Celerity, Dominate, and Vigor

                            Clan Merits: In a rare display, this clan also has a few Merits all shared by their members. This does not cost extra, and is considered covered by their enhanced Clan Weakness. All Clan members have the Blush of Life and Eat Food merits, always active without spending Blood to activate. They also all possess Heightened Senses.

                            For some strange reason, this clan takes less damage from sunlight and fire. While not immune, these are never aggravated damage for them, letting them survive exposure a few precious seconds longer, and allows them to recover from its burns like any other form of damage. Some have even found mages willing to craft them rings that let them avoid sunlight damage completely. Fortunately, these rings are useless to other Clans, and each ring is bound to the vampire it's made for. This comes with a trade-off however.

                            Clan Weakness:
                            Flipping the Switch: They have a more intense Beast, due to having ALL their emotions heightened, but they don't seem to suffer from Degeneration in their natural state. Vampires of this Clan have to resist a frenzy check at a -2 penalty, not just for rage or fear, but sadness and grief. But rather than degenerate, when they fail a roll, they have the option of either taking the frenzy OR "flipping the switch".

                            This sets their Humanity to 1, basically becoming either a psychopath or a sociopath, depending on their normal psychological makeup. Rather than going on a rage or fear induced bender on the spot, they're more likely to walk away, and then go on a several weeks-long (or month or even YEARS) murder spree as they do what they want, when they want, how they want, and remove the hearts of anyone who tries to tell them otherwise.

                            Normally, the vampire can flip the switch back any time, but... they're usually having too good a time to do so, without some kind of outside stimulus. Usually this involves encountering someone or something that actually manages to make them feel a positive emotion again, which flips their Humanity back to normal, at which point, they have to make a massive Humanity check based on the worst things they did while "off".

                            Wooden Doom: In trade for their gentler treatment of sunlight, this Clan finds wood to be much more deadly. A wooden weapon of any kind impedes their ability heal, preventing them from spending blood to heal the damage until the weapon is removed. In addition, a successful Stake attack is an instant death sentence, though the vampire does get to attempt to soak the damage normally. If successful, the heart was just missed, or the vampire is managing to hold the weapon back from fully penetrating.

                            RSVP: Clan Mikaelson cannot enter a private residence uninvited. The threshold acts like a kind of invisible barrier preventing entry. Only someone in residence can invite them in, though this includes OTHER invited guests. A total stranger cannot invite someone in. Regardless, their powers of Dominate only make this a bothersome effect if no one is home.

                            Supernatural Merit
                            Daylight Ring (5 pts) - Requires an Awakened contact, or a Mentor or Ally with said contact. Grants a ring that prevents all damage from sunlight, and even allows the wearer to function normally during the day with no ill effects. Can only be used by vampires of Clan Mikaelson. Each ring is crafted for a specific vampire and cannot be used by any other.

                            The Originals
                            Supposedly there are six people who founded this Clan, a Nordic (or possibly Celtic) warrior and his five children. They were transformed into vampires by their mother, a powerful witch (Awakened Thrysus Mage) to fight off a tribe of Pure werewolves that were harassing their settlement. Since then, their parents became disgusted by what they had created, and supposedly to this day, the father hunts his children, seeking to destroy their entire bloodline. A boogeyman of vampire lore, he's referred to a "Mikael the Destroyer", and anytime there's a large number of vampiric deaths that go unexplained, they're attributed to him.

                            The "Original" members of this clan are Methuselahs, being over a thousand years old, and have a few unique traits attributed to them.

                            Invulnerability: the Originals cannot be destroyed. They are still vulnerable to the weaknesses of a vampire, but this does not kill them, but instead drives them into torpor. While in torpor, their bodies become indestructible. They cannot be burned, mutilated, or harmed in any fashion. Best one can do is lock them up in a box and dump it into the river, and hope you're dead before someone finds them and accidentally lets them out. How? Supposedly some consequence of the spell used to make them what they are.

                            Still, no spell is perfect, and there was one weakness: the wood of the venerable white oak trees of the forest used in the ritual. Once they learned of it's effect, the family rightly burned it down, and saw to it that over the centuries, that the forest was eradicated. Only two known weapons are known to exist, a vial of ash, made of the burned remnants of those trees, which when coating a weapon and plunged into the heart, locks the Original in torpor until the item is removed. Vials of this have been passed around fo centuries, and no one is sure how many still exist. A few buy a vial, only to find out to their short-lived surprised, that they bought a fake.

                            The other is a white oak stake, enchanted by their now-deceased mother, and given to Mikael the Destroyer. It can not only kill their children instantly, but it can bring the Final Death to ANY vampire staked by it. If true, it would explain the man's legendary death rate. Still, occassionally rumors surface that another object made from a tree of that long-lost forest has been found, and those who believe in these figures scramble over each other to possess it.

                            Superior Dominate: the Originals powers of compulsion are far more potent than others of their kind. Basically, an Original, when using Dominate, prevents a character from applying their Supernatural Resistance attribute (Blood Potency for vampires, Gnosis for Mages, etc). to the roll, meaning other supernaturals are no better than your average humans when resisting them.
