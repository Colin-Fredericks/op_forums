    Crossover/Conversion Question: Would Beasts Register on "Sense Wyrm"

    12-10-2015, 03:12 PM
    Dear Onyx
    Since *official* "Conversion Manuals" that allow you to incorporate/switch/adapt elements of Vampire/Werewolf/Changeling ACROSS the Worlds Of Darkness have been released, it stands to reason that this situation would eventually pop up on some gaming table in the future.

    The Garou Gift "Sense Wyrm" is a radar that "pings" (so to speak) when a Person/Creature/Spirit "Stinks of the Wyrm"

    Now, a Wyrm Spirit LITERALLY feeds on the Enrgies of Pain, Fear and Suffering.
    Much like a Beast Does, coincidentally

    Hence, should a Garou use "Sense Wyrm" when in the presence of Beasts, would they be marked as "Of The Wyrm" much like Vampires of Humanity 6 or Lower?

    Insomnically
    Lin Liren.
