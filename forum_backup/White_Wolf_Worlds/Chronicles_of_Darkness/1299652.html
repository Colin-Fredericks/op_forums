    Open letter about the Chronicles of Darkness, Onyx Path, Beast and the future

    03-25-2019, 01:48 PM
    Ok, let's do this thing.

    I feel like it's time for me to be open about some things and try get something good out a bad situation.

    Turns out there was a damn valid reason I felt awful in last bunch of months. Now I'm fighting the thing, but there's a chance I won't win. A minor one, admittedly, but still big enough for the doctors to be frank about it. Still enough to be scared.

    To be honest, it's not something I wanted to discuss. I told only a few people and I'm afraid sharing it here might not be the best idea. I'm afraid it would look like attention-seeking, I'm afraid it might be inappropriate and hurt my freelancing future, and I'm afraid people will take it the wrong way. But I also lost 15 kilograms in 30 days, so I have plenty to be afraid of right now. So I hope it will still be worth it.

    Now I'll talk a bit about Onyx Path.

    I know loyalty to brands is rarely smart in the modern world, but Onyx Path shaped half of my life, so here we are. Onyx Path is not perfect, nothing is, but it still earned our love. I've wanted to write for it for 14 years and think its products and history are among the best of the industry

    Now, I can't talk about the company when I did not work for it, can't talk about experiences that aren't mine. But the Onyx Path I saw? That one is awesome. And I'm not saying it because they gave me a chance (though, hey, that felt good), but rather because I honestly believe the people that work within it right now are among the kindest, most talented people I've ever seen. Things are done the right way by the right people and each project is filled to the brim by passionate folk who wish nothing but the best for those they work with, their fans and the games they're writing.

    Naive? Maybe, but that's what I think.

    The Onyx Path I got to know has a great future in front of it, where it will keep improving. Believe in it.

    Now let's talk about Beast. I carefully cultivated to reputation of “Beast guy” around here, with its pros and cons, so I feel like I need to cover the topic.

    Beast is not perfect. Beast is a mess. Beast has been criticized by cool people who talk because they know what they're saying. People better than me.

    I still love that game. I see what it is, what it can be and I see what it says to me and others, no matter its flaws.

    Now, and here's some unusual pride, I think I get how Beast should be written, what it should cover and the right tone to be honest about its protagonists while still providing a good game with conflicts and depth. Just as I can understand it, so do others. Talented people who write better than me and have a better idea of how to make a game great.

    Beast will become the game I see when I look at it, eventually, because of Onyx Path and the people who run it or write for it

    This company, this community and many of those who might read this, you're good people and part of a promising future. I've been around here for almost 15 years and being here, dreaming of becoming a Onyx Path writer and interacting with all of you has made me a better person and artist.

    The idea I still have the opportunity to work on more books and maybe, someday, get good and respected enough to become a developer and help the company I love improve further is pretty much the goal that's helping me fight through all of this

    Hey, it might not happen. I might not get more assignments (with this thread possibly being what screws me), I might not be a good enough writer. Hell, I might not be there at all. But I'll still love these books and the good people who surround them as much as I'm allowed.

    The Chronicles of Darkness are good. The World of Darkness is good. Scion is good. They Came from Beneath the Sea! is a thing. Bloodlines 2 is happening. Signs of Sorcery and Shunned by the Moon are close. Not to mention Critical Role just made all the money and L.A. by Night is doing great: our hobby is going through a Renaissance.

    I'm thankful for many of you and the time spent here. I hope this was not as pathetic as a side of me insists it is.

    I hope it helps you to see the future I see when I think of Onyx Path.

    And if in the future I show you a tattoo of the Beast skull on my arm, it will mean I won my humble battle.

    Now, time to tag some people:

    Second Chances , White Oak Dragon, you're the best colleagues I could ask for and I'm glad to call both of you friends.

    ArcaneArts , Yossarian , ajf115 , Heavy Arms , Guest, Arcanist , Vent0 , Caladriu , Master Aquatosic , Charlaquin , AzraelFirestorm , Thorbes Papa Bear , tasti man LH , Satchel , reseru , LostLight , Prometheus878, Watcher , glamourweaver nofather , GibberingEloquence espritdecalmar ElvesofZion r , TheCountAlucard , Gellydog you're awesome, smart people and make this place better. Yeah, I know some of you did not interact much with me, but I'm a lurker at heart and carefully look to what happens around me. I know what I'm saying.

    I'm sure I missed some of you: I'll add more as they come to my mind.

    (Not tagging developers and other "big" writers, it's a bit too shameless for me, but the same respect and thankfulness goes to you. If you're reading this, thank you and good luck for what will come, whether I’m part of it or not. If this thread was a big mistake, don't hesitate to tell me)
    Last edited by Cinder; 03-28-2019, 03:26 AM.
