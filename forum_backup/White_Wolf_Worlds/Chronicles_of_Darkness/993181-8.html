            Sort of a golem ghost:
            ]Necroamalgams
            Wise adherents of the Moros Watchtower warn of necroamalgams that spawn in the crush of unhallowed bodies in a communal grave. These monsters may have half a dozen heads and arms that sprout from a twisted collection of bones and withered organs. Denied identity in death, the necroamalgams seek revenge on the living. They cow mages with bellowing cries or (especially in the case of amalgams of children) an eerie, singsong chorus.
            Rumor has it that a mage can destroy a necroamalgam by confronting it with the names of its component ghosts — although she must first discover those names. The portion or body part associated with each name supposedly turns to dust upon hearing its name, weakening the remaining whole. If all of its names (anywhere from three to 10, depending on its size) are spoken, the creature is totally destroyed.
            Although the necroamalgams are a small minority in Copp’s Hill Burial Ground, they are common enough to represent a serious hazard to the unprepared. These beings actually exist around the world in mass, unhallowed graves. It’s rumored that dark Moros (such as the Tremere) nurture necroamalgams by engineering the creation of such graves.
            Statistics for a five-ghost necroamalgam follow. Rules for larger or smaller versions appear in parenthesis. Attributes: Power 7 (2 + 1 per component ghost),
            Finesse 2, Resistance 3
            Willpower: 3
            Max Essence: 10 (2 per component ghost)
            Morality: 3
            Virtue: Fortitude
            Vice: Envy
            Defense: 7 (equal to Power; necroamalgams always
            have a higher Power than Finesse)
            Initiative: 5
            Speed: 19 (Power + Finesse + 10)
            Size: 10 (5 +1 per component ghost)
            Corpus: 13 (Resistance + Size)
            Special: Two physical actions/turn. (Necroamalgams
            can take one additional instant physical action per turn for every three of its component ghosts, rounded up.

                    Try this!

                    Necroamalgam
                    A rare type of Ghost, the Necroamalgam is a bizarre composite of multiple entities merged into a single being. They most frequently arise from unmarked mass graves that lay near an open Avernian Gate. They speak in a chorus of shrieking voices. The entities are typically comprised of between three to a dozen ghosts, and manifest as a twisted mass of bones, skulls, and withered flesh, the more entities that comprise its form, the greater its power.
                    The circumstances of their burial have denied the individual ghosts that make up the Necroamalgam their identities, which leaves the gestalt with little but jealousy and contempt for the living.
                    The example below is for a Necroamalgam comprised of roughly 3-6 entities.

                    Rank 2[Ghost]
                    Power 7 Finesse 2, Resistance 5, Willpower 7, Essence 15
                    Corpus 15, Size 10, Initiative 7, Defense 5, Speed 19
                    Virtue Coordinated, Vice Enraged
                    Aspiration: to lash out at the living, to be remembered
                    Influence: Anchor (Mass Grave) 2, Jealousy 1
                    Ban: Being confronted with the name of one of its component ghosts weakens it, speaking all the names destroys it
                    Bane: A grave marker dedicated to one of its component ghosts
                    Manifestation: Avernian Gateway, Image, Materialize, Twilight Form
                    Numina: Awe, Banshee Wail*, Phantom Arsenal*, Regenerate, Stalwart

                    Numina – Banshee Wail: The entity creates an earsplitting scream that deafens anyone nearby. Anyone within 20 yards of the entity must succeed on a Resolve + Stamina roll – the number of successes gained or suffer the Stunned and Deafened Tilt. The Numen costs one Essence to activate.

                    Numina – Phantom Arsenal: The entity spends 1 Essence point to manifest blades, claws, fangs, hooks or other appropriate weaponry. The modification acts as a +1L weapon. By spending an additional point of essence, this weapon gains the “9-Again” quality.
