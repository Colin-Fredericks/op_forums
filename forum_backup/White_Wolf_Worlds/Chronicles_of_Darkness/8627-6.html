        The recent update to the Khabit got me in Mekhet mode, so I decided to update the Hollow Mekhet as a Clan.


        Proto-Mekhet
        In the nights before the Mekhet joined the all-night society, there was a time when they did not so closely resemble the Kindred Clans. These Proto-Mekhet could not embrace as the Kindred do. They could only be created through the posthumous embrace, and it left them empty. Soulless. Hollow. Since joining the other Kindred in the Danse Macabre, the Mekhet have grown past this limitation, and tonight there are no more hollow Shadows skulking around, running and hiding from their own reflections. But there are some who believe they could still be created, if a modern Mekhet performed the proper funerary rites.
        Nickname: Hollows
        Bane (The Hollow Curse): Poto-Mekhet do not leave any residual impressions of their presence. They have no shadow, no echo, no reflection, no footprints, and are not picked up on recording equipment of any kind. Any character who spends time in one’s presence can make a reflexive Resolve + Composure roll contested by the Hollow's Humanity to notice the absence once per scene.
        Favored Attributes: Intelligence or Wits
        Disciplines: Auspex, Celerity, Obfuscate
        Proto-Mekhet count as Mekhet for the purposes of qualifying for prerequisites.

        The Ka
        When a Proto-Mekhet is embraced, its soul, or Ka, leaves its body, or Ba. This creates a Rank 2 ghost anchored to the vampire. This entity follows the normal rules for ghosts. Like all ephemeral entities, Ka use the simplified attributes of Power, Finesse and Resistance. A Ka’s Power is equal to the highest of its anchor’s Intelligence, Strength, or Presence, its Finesse is equal to the highest of its anchor’s Wits, Dexterity, or Manipulation, and its Resistance is equal to the highest of its anchor’s Resolve, Stamina, or Composure. A Ka’s Virtue, Vice, and Integrity are those its anchor had in life.

        All Ka share the same ban. Any meal prepared specifically for a Ka and appropriately set becomes an Open Condition for it. If the person who prepared the meal then invites it, by name, to dine with them, it must immediately materialize at the table and cannot leave until it has eaten all of the food that was prepared for it. Doing so fully replenishes the Ka’s essence pool. Additionally, a Ka shares all of its anchor’s banes except for sunlight and The Hollow Curse. For banes that refer to Humanity, use the Ka’s Integrity instead.

        Manifestations:
        All Ka have the Twilight Form, Image, and Materialize Manifestations. When using the Image Manifestation, a Ka can only appear in reflections, recordings, or other visual mediums, and has the same appearance as its anchor’s image or reflection would. When Materialized, a Ka appears as a living shadow, disturbingly similar to a Strix, but in the exact size and shape of the shadow its anchor would cast, unless it uses the Mortal Mask Numen, in which case its appearance is identical to that of its anchor.

        Numina:
        All Ka have the Numina Dement, Mortal Mask, and Sign. In addition, they have two unique Numina: Snatch.

        Consume Flesh:
        The Ka must be materialized to use this Numen. This Numen allows the Ka to regain Essence by consuming dead flesh. Each size category worth of flesh is worth three Essence.

        Snatch:
        The Ka must be using the Image manifestation to activate this Numen. It spends three Essence and rolls Power + Finesse – the target’s Resolve + Supernatural Tolerance. On a successful roll, it reaches out from the mirror, picture, or screen, and pulls the target into Twilight. The target is then forced to manifest in the same medium as the Ka, and is trapped in that image for a number of hours equal to the Ka’s successes. If the victim is killed while in twilight, the remains disappear. Otherwise, the victim is returned to the material world in the same spot he was snatched from when the duration ends.

Continuing with the theme of updating mechanics from the Clan books (and my ongoing Masquiem project), I humbly present Malkavia, Malkavians, and Dementation. <--- link to google drive


                                                    Malkav’s Disease

                                                    Named after the first patient known to have contract it, Malkav’s Disease, or Malkavia, is a contagious illness that afflicts Kindred and Kine alike. Malkav’s Disease affects the mind of the patient, eroding their sense of reality and causing severe mental and emotional distress. Once each day, an infected character can make a reflexive Resolve + Stamina roll to resist its effects, as normal. The number of successful rolls required to beat Malkav’s Disease is equal to the Blood Potency of the vampire it was contracted from (or, if it was contracted from a mortal, the Blood Potency of the vampire he contracted it from -2). Unlike ordinary diseases, Malkavia does not deal damage on a failed resistance roll. Instead, the character gains both the Afflicted and Delusional Conditions (the Storyteller decides the nature of your character’s delusion). Once these Conditions are inflicted, the disease has run its course and the character need not continue making resistance rolls.

                                                    Condition: Afflicted
                                                    Any living creature or vampire who drinks your character’s vitae contracts Malkav’s Disease. If your character is a vampire, any living creature she feeds on contracts it as well. This Condition expires normally (without resolving) after a number of nights equal to the number of successes you were short of beating the disease. If your character is a vampire, it expires after the same number of months instead.
                                                    Possible Sources: Malkav’s Disease
                                                    Resolution: Your character empties every point of vitae from her system.
                                                    Beat: n/a

                                                    Malkavians

                                                    Background: The origins of Malkavia, and subsequently of those afflicted by it, is a matter of much contention among Kindred scientists and historians. The most commonly accepted theories are attributed to the Defiant scholar Nicholas Polaski, who performed some of the earliest medical studies on the disease. He coined the name “Malkavia” after his presumed Patient Zero, a Ventrue named Grigori Malkav. Polaski attributed Malkav’s affliction to an evil agent in the patient’s thoughts that he believed was be spread by a psychological process he called “Dementation”. Modern medical science has disproved many of these ideas, but it was Polaski’s findings that lead to the understanding of Malkavia as a disease rather than a curse. Tonight it is understood that the disease spreads through vitae, although infection through Dementation is still a commonly held misconception among the lay Kindred.

                                                    The Becoming: It is not well understood what causes certain patients’ vitae to be permanently altered by Malkav’s Disease while others recover in relatively short order. However, some Malkavia experts are beginning to theorize that the infected Vitae may act similarly to that of an Avus. In this model, Malkavia may not be a disease at all, but some form of infectious Bloodline.

                                                    Nickname: Demented

                                                    Requirements: Any Kindred infected with Malkav’s Disease, regardless of Clan, can join the bloodline as though her sire was a Malkavian. Although it is always the player’s choice whether or not her character joins the bloodline, the character herself need not desire, or even be aware of the change in her blood brought on by the disease.

                                                    In the Danse Macabre: Among the ignorant, Malkavians are hated and feared as lunatics and plague-bearers. Many domains exile known Malkavians, and some even have them executed. Among the informed, the Demented present an opportunity for further study of their condition. Most ambitious Kindred scientists would consider unethical experimentation a small price to pay to get their names in the Ordo Dracul’s medical books, right alongside Nicholas Polaski. As a result, only the most far-gone Malkavians are mad enough to reveal their nature. But, in a rare few domains, the hallucinations their madness causes them are regarded as nigh prophetic visions. In such courts, the Demented are often made trusted advisors to the Prince and respected above mundane Kindred, their affliction seen as a true gift.

                                                    Bloodline Bane (The Terminal Curse): The Demented can never be cured of Malkav’s Disease. She must continue to make resistance rolls each night no matter how many she succeeds at in a row. When a Malkavian fails a resistance roll and gains the Delusional Condition, the disease merely goes into remission until she resolves it. The Afflicted Condition does not expire normally for a Malkavian.

                                                    Bloodline Disciplines: A Malkavian retains the disciplines of her parent Clan.

                                                    Dementation

                                                    The “Gift” of those tainted by Malkav’s infectious blood is Dementation – though most would be more inclined to call it a curse. The powers of Dementation are drawn from the madness brought on by Malkav’s Disease, and a Malkavian can therefore only enjoy their benefits while suffering the Delusional Condition. Just as all vampires can sense their Kindred through the shared vitae of their sires, the Malkavians can sense their fellow Demented through a kind of shared delirium known as the Madness Network. All Malkavians with the Delusional Condition treat each other as sharing a twice-removed blood bond for the purposes of Blood Sympathy. Additionally, the Malkavians can draw twisted insight from their shared visions. When a character joins the bloodline, she gains the Omen Sensitivity Merit. However, she can only use this Merit when she has the Delusional Conditon. Finally, if another character is foolish enough to actually listen to a Malkavian’s ravings, the Demented can pass her delusions on to anyone who lets her in. A Malkavian with the Delusional Condition can use Social Maneuvering to attempt to make her mark understand what she sees. If the attempt is successful, the Malkavian can resolve her Delusional Condition and give it to her mark instead.
