    Oh I've faith that there will be some reasons to join the loyalists. At the very least, we've been told that Deviants can be a danger to people around them. I imagine there will be a sliding scale in the organizations, with some goals being more morally justified, even if not something the entire world would get behind. And even among loyalists you could probably have one guy who's willing to destroy everything in their way to the target, and another who doesn't want any collateral.

    Though the emphasis is still probably going to be on Renegades at first. I think we've been told that loyalists won't be expanded upon in the core book, but it's been a while. There'll even probably be ways to go from one side to another, with Loyalists going Renegade, and Renegades going Loyalist.

            The Disaster of Project Yoon-Sun

            The United States Special Interests Research and Training unit (SPIRTAI) was a joint division between the United States Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM), United States Army recruiting command (USAREC), United States Army Test and Evaluation Command (ATEC), United States Military Academy (USMA), The Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL), and overseen by the Marine Special Operations command (USSOCOM). SPIRTAI's main function is to promote bright students and young military personnel into special groups that function as "Think Tanks" to solve many problems in the military either as a whole or specific cases. The groups created were not at all dissimilar to several Gifted Educational programs in elementary and post elementary schools.

            While the division focused on the creation of fresh innovation across the broad spectrum military Academics and special developments, by 1973 the need for the inclusion of private military contractors due to a steady decline in budget for this area of the military. With the inclusion of private contractors, the division suffered a confusion due to several reorganizations and HR complaints on some of the research and tasks requested of the Think Tanks involved resulted in the division being quietly and largely disbanded with the only remnant being the remaining Think Tank group and it's associate project (would come to be known as Yoon-Sun after one of the members of the group) which continued it's study under the watch of Three generals that composed the decision making party of SPIRTAI, the funding of the Federal Special Interests Group (FESIG), and personnel/resources of Limited Ltd. (a business with several companies but prides itself in training and scouting programs for schools, other companies, the military, and governmental positions).

            Project Yoon-Sun revolved around the idea of helping Air-Force pilots withstand greater g-force pressures and other related hazards to protect the pilots and soldiers in duty. The group found many possibilities in certain types of people if born in certain kinds of hypothetical environments or stresses. All of it theoretical to them but their research was in how technology could be created to emulate the types of protections that pilots would need. Over the course of their research they concluded that there could be similar technologies that might help soldiers in other divisions. Not told to the Think Tank, an illegal Human Research group created by FESIG and Limited LTD under the command of the corrupt Generals of SPIRTAI had been carrying out illegal human experimentation using the findings, research, and ideas of the Think Tanks for its entire run. The Most successful became Project Yoon-Sun which genetically engineered what the differring parties hoped would be the ideal test subject for use in enhancing various military personnel.

            After several failed attempts, a child of Project Yoon-Sun survived the engineering and mechanical process. The Child grew in the care of the personnel of the engineers. The child did not meet anyone of their age and mostly consisted of tests after test. The child did not age the same as would be expected. Over time, a well meaning but ultimately extreme group calling themselves The Unification Corps heard of Project Yoon-Sun and attempted to embed themselves in the organization that studied the Child in an attempt to free them. On the Child's technical 18th birthday, the Corps attempted to break the child out. While they were successful in that the Child escaped, everyone present save for a few, died. Survivors report that the Child became unstable during this time.

            With the Child on the run and attempting to find out more about themselves as well as the outside world, several groups are now both on a hunt and a race. The three generals want to recover the Child for their climb to the tops of the military political ladder as well as the Child's obvious application on the soldiers of the battlefield. FESIG wants the child back as they consider the Child to be technically fully funded by them and the escape created a possible loss of monetary assets if seized by anyone else. Limited Ltd. cannot allow the Child to be caught or seen outside of a laboratory for the research they conducted and how it may help many of their goals, chief among them the Recruitment program. And finally the The Unification Corps, while well meaning, see the Child as the ultimate weapon to be hurled at their enemies, specifically the military.

            tl;dr: I def see a Renegade with more than one Org... and I think I may have just created my character for when it comes out
