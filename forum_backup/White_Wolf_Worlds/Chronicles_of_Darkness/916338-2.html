                                    Well I didn't have a graph or anything, but as a rule of thumb-

                                    If you go for the flaw route, how specific it is really doesn't matter, since flaws aren't rated, if say a lust witch gets handicapped in the story because they couldn't manipulate another emotion or even another sin, or even manipulate another mental process, they'd get a beat.

                                    If one goes this route, one might allow an influence mage to broaden their powers, using the lust witch as an example, perhaps after some studying they could replace that influence- lust, with influence-sin, or influence- emotion, possibly even influence-minds if that's on the table.

                                    The other option is to give specialization dice, I can see two routes here.

                                    One, give a number of bonus dice based on how specialized the influence is, in this case mages can trade versatility for raw power.

                                    If that seems like too much, perhaps make a merit available to those with more specialized influences, perhaps it gives specialty dice, or perhaps it lowers essence costs, I don't know.
