                        Here's what I said via PM on the diablerie topic:

                        Powers that are based on knowledge and experience can be gained via diablerie. That covers Cruac and Theban Sorcery. I'd usually say it doesn't include the Coils of the Dragon, because they involve things like experimenting on yourself in addition to learning. But if a character achieved their rank in a Coil primarily by meditation, it might transfer via diablerie. Oaths and Law can't be transferred via diablerie, because they require things outside the character (like agreement to an oath to a specific person) in order to work.

                                    I always imagined the humanity part of ritual disciplines represent spiritual investment in the discipline, not just numbers requirements

                                    Cruac doesn't cap your humanity just because you know a ritual (not even some truly horrible scales or diablerie, that is the ultimate wickedness, can cap your humanity, why just knowing some procedure would?), it caps your humanity because you have to get THAT close to your beast to commune with it and harvest the power of its blood. To really know the ritual, just learning the procedure isn't enough, you have to craft that close connection for yourself, and it's a thing diablerie can't steal.

                                    Theban Sorcery requires you to have a minimum humanity because it works with your rational human mind, the part of you that dreams, that makes plan, that cares and, most of all, has the power to believe (whereas the beast cares only about safety and blood), the farther away you get from that enlightened state of mind the harder it is for the power of your beliefs to call that higher power that gives you miracles and that pure state of mind of true belief is also something you can't steal with diablerie.

                                    Dominate is a power ventrue develop naturally, without investment sometimes, just by the process of becoming vampires. Learning blood sorcery feels a lot more deep than that.

                                    At least, that's how i picture them, i feel that being able to get blood sorcery dots from diablerie kind of detracts from the deep spiritual themes i like to evoke with it.
