        Originally posted by nofather View Post
        The Werewolf myth has always been interesting to me as a werewolf fan, but even with the few similarities I don't really see it coinciding with the Mage origin story.

        But looking into what we do know about the various myths could suggest other things. For instance Dave Brookshaw pointed out that the Gauntlet may be a 'gaping wound left in reality' after the fall of Father Wolf, providing that story is true, and the fall of the Border Marches, similarly. But if you look around a bit more, tied in with the Maeljin who are currently considered entities of the Inferno, you'll see the myth of the First Wound. According to Werewolf legend, the First Wound is where Father Wolf was killed, and from that act was born the Maeljin of Violence, Thurifuge, who built a palace on or from Father Wolf's bones. Thurifuge is now considered a high-ranking demon of the Inferno, I think Maeljin are around rank 8. So if you wanted you could have a story in your game connecting the Gauntlet and the death of the Border Marches to the Inferno or, from a Mage point of view, the Lower Depths.
        Which reminds me: there seems to be some possible correlation between the Maeljin and/or Demon Princes and the Angels who cursed humanity in the Testament of Marco Singe. With the usual caveats that Marco is an unreliable narrator repeating information from an untrustworthy source, the Demon Princes and Maeljin aren't exactly things that anyone has done a comprehensive empirical study on, etc.

        Nevertheless, we have one of the Angels (I think it was Strife) being imprisoned at the "bottom of the world" and a pack of Werewolves in one of the books that found a Maeljin in Antarctica and decided to worship it.
