    Colleges

    04-26-2017, 12:52 PM
    Derived from this thread, and linked conceptually to this thread.

    The idea is for a Beast Z-splat (or actual Y-Splat? A social Splat, regardless) that leverages the whole "teaching culture". These would be philosophies and social convention based around teaching certain Lessons. Mechanically, member Beast would gain Kinship (and access to Kinship Nightmares) with the concept behind the College itself, in addition to the inherent advantages being part of a club and shared community brings.

    So, thoughts on this topic? Would would be some good Lessons to base Colleges around? How would this influence the social group? What Nightmares would derived from such philosophies? Or any other comments?

          I wonder, might it be better to just call them 'schools'? Both for the teaching connotations, but also for the connotations of groups moving together. Besides, the Dark Dream is often likened to an ocean.

          As for an initial list of ideas for lessons:
          • Humility - You cannot do everything or anything. Hubris is a road to ruin.
          • Cost - Everything has a price, and often that price is steep.
          • Community - Humanity's strength is its unity. Alone, you are weak.
          • Caution - The world is dark and full of terrors. You can avoid them if you are careful, though.
          • Will - What is humanity without the will to carry on, to persevere, to become better, to survive? Dead. That's what.
          I'll admit that those are based on the ones in the 'lessons' section of the corebook, but hey. I think they're pretty good.

                              Originally posted by Vent0 View Post

                              Like? What would that be called and how would they be conceptually grouped?
                              It strikes me that these splats would probably push at the boundaries of 'monsterhood'. Kind of like Life. A counterpart to Family-as-Legend. Maybe...
                              • The Cunning - Riddlers, deceivers and tricksters. Zahhak and the Serpent in Eden. They teach humility through exposing flaws and weaknesses.
                              • The Wise - Monsters who nevertheless masters in their field. Wayland, the Witch of Endor and Andvari. They teach costs, as all their gifts come at a price.
                              • The Mighty - Monsters who are masters of combat and martial endeavour. Grendel and Ravana. They teach the importance of will, for to face them without it is to be defeated.
                              • The Mothers - Monsters who are a reflection of parenthood, birthing and presiding over litters of their own. Tiamat and Echidna. They teach community by holding up a mirror to humanity's and showing them that strength comes from the many.
                              • The Haunters - Monsters who are, and that is all they need be. Wendigo and possessing spirits. They teach caution, for they can be anywhere and everywhere. Only vigilance can keep them at bay.
                              It's kind of similar to Hunger, but these are, after all, artificial divisions created by the Beasts themselves.

                                  College of the Joker
                                  [Desc]
                                  Lessons
                                  Laughter is life - [Don't take things so seriously, because you might not be around tomorrow to enjoy them.]
                                  Rules hold you back - [Does the world make sense, or obey the rules society has constructed around it? No! "The only sensible way to live in this world is without rules."]
                                  No one is different - [Despite airs and pretension, we're only separated by our circumstances. All it takes is one bad day to turn a hero into a villain.]
                                  Kinship
                                  [text]
                                  Sample Kinship Nightmares:
                                  • They're All Laughing At You - [Target thinks everyone is mocking them. Makes them distrustful or hostile to everyone, or maybe flee in shame? Other Mental Conditions?]
                                  • You're Just Like Me - [Target is convinced they aren't so different from you, reducing animosity. Social perk? End combat? Prevent Willpower spent to hurt you, since their heart isn't in it?]
                                  • You're Holding Back - [Target is convinced rules are preventing them form completing their goals. Act uninhibited by morals, ethics, or personal code?]


                                  College of the Butterfly
                                  [Desc]
                                  Lessons
                                  Small efforts can lead to great change - [The merest wing-flap can lead to a hurricane.]
                                  Repercussions can't always be seen - [If a wing flap can spark a wind storm, what makes you think you an predict the effects of your own actions?]
                                  Change can be transformative - [Like the difference between a caterpillar and a butterfly, who we were doesn't have to dictate who we will be.]
                                  Kinship
                                  [text]
                                  Sample Kinship Nightmares:
                                  • Just As I Planned - [Target is convinced that even your minor involvement led to this inevitable outcome. Beaten Down Tilt, since opposition is pointless? Paranoia Condition?]
                                  • Look What You Have Done - [Target is overwhelmed at the unintended consequences of their actions. May cause choice paralysis? Force them into odd behavior in the belief it will get what they want?]
                                  • This Isn't Even My Final Form - [Target is convinced that what they see is not the end of things. May confuse another target with you?]
