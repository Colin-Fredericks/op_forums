                                              The Sin-Eaters' stereotypes on other templates contain some of my favourites.

                                              The Forgotten stereotype on mortals is quite good: "Don't risk it. It's a mug's game, man."
                                              The Torn have another good take on mortals: "Just stay behind me. Stay behind me, and don't look too hard. And please, shut the #*%@ up."
                                              However, the crown jewel of the set, and possibly the entire World of Darkness, is the Prey stereotype on Mages: "That's cheating."

                                              Half of the stereotypes from the Court of Mirrors in Princess: The Hopeful are really good (I echo the above sentiment that PtH has a lot of good stereotypes), but their stereotype on themselves deserves an honourable mention: "No, you're not the True Heir! I am!"
