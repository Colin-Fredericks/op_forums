                    05-22-2016, 12:44 PM
                    Understood. I was just afraid that your feelings on the matter were making you miss that this was just an example, not an absolute. I see that happen a lot.

                    Touchstones generally are living people. I suspect that holds true because people make better NPCs, and can lead to better stories, than just an unfeeling material. Touchstones are supposed to bring in conflict for your characters - how you can lose it, how you can protect it, how you react to it being threatened. These are things you go to great lengths to protect. So, in a real way, these should have a measure of fragility to them, but as the result of outside threats rather than simple age. Out of the fifteen example touchstones, two are places rather than people, but they still have troubles associated with them.

                                                        But, lets say you do make Mr "I am an Island." Vitalis covered how to make Touchstones without connection to your mortal life.
