                            Anyone under the influence of a vinculum or application of a discipline is not considered “willing”.

                            Swooning vs Scarred is applied based on how the vampire goes about feeding, not whether or not the individual is “willing”. Does the vampire attempt to feed off the person under false pretenses and without resorting to physical assault? If yes, that’s usually Swooning. Does the vampire simply maneuver to sink their teeth into the victim and feed? If that’s the methodology, then that’s Scarred.

                            The person ordered to submit to being fed upon via Dominate isn’t going to remember being fed upon. They’re mesmerized and ordered to submit. Mesmerize breaks with the attendant Lethal damage from the feeding but they’re usually under the effects of Swooning.
