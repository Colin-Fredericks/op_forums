    Homebrew "Coil of the Devourer" - Request Assistance

    10-07-2016, 02:43 PM
    This is a Coil I've been working on while waiting for Secrets of the Covenants to come out. Each of the 'main' Coils is focused a single primal aspect of being a vampire, and the concept of the Coils is that they are attempts by the Dragons to become more than merely Kindred. This Coil is about feeding - improving how the Kindred can feed, what they can feed on, and how they can use what they consume. Several of the Scales have to do with trying to avoid Blood Bonds, as my character has a PARTICULARLY STRONG INTEREST in that in the campaign at the moment. Unfortunately, I'm still trying to come up with a good 4th step for the Coil, so one of the folks in my group suggested I toss the whole thing up here for comments and suggestions. I'm also still working on the details of the different power sets in the 5th Coil, so they're still kinda preliminary, especially the Aether-based ones.

    Thanks for reading it - let me know what you think!


    Coils of the Devourer

    Tending the Flocks •
    In order to study the Blood, the Dragon must first have a sufficient supply. Hunting at random, however thrilling, is also too inefficient to produce the necessary quantities of Vitae. Gathering a suitable herd of mortals, however, is often difficult and time-consuming; gathering a herd of supernaturals is exceedingly dangerous. Thus, the first course taken by the prudent Devourer is to improve his ability to both hunt and herd effectively. With this Coil, the vampire's bite begins to plunge deeply into the minds and souls of his victims as well as their bodies. When feeding violently, the Dragon can choose to apply the Amnesiac Condition (covering the immediate memories of the hunt and feeding process) instead of the Scarred Condition, draining away his victim's most recent memories along with the blood. When feeding non-violently, he can inflict the Addicted Condition (relating to being fed upon) in place of the normal Swooning Condition.

    Strength in the Blood ••
    As the Dragon's understanding of the nature of Vitae grows, he can begin to make changes to that nature for his own benefit. The second Coil of the Devourer concentrates the blood, forcing more of the essence of Life into it and allowing the Dragon to gather more strength from the herd. Increase Vitae storage by 2 per level of the Coil.

    Taste of Ambrosia •••
    Diablerie is as forbidden by the Ordo Dracul as by any other Covenant, but the Dragons study it nonetheless - the process itself is a fascinating one, and the Dragons are founded on the principle that limits are meant to be exceeded. The true Amaranth is an abomination, of course, but only because it involves the destruction of the soul. With this Coil, the Kindred is able to lap from the surface of the soul without consuming it, just as another Kindred might drink from the veins without killing their prey. While feeding, the vampire can devour his victim's spirit to restore his own Willpower pool. Each action spent feeding allows the vampire to steal a single Willpower point from the victim's pool. At least one point of blood must be consumed. The first point each night is relatively safe to take; taking more requires a Resolve + Composure - (WP points stolen that night) roll each turn to avoid the Tempted Condition, as the vampire's beast is inflamed with desire.

    A Hunter Of Greater Prey •••••
    Ultimately, the Devourer comes to understand that Vitae is shaped by the essence of Life concetrated into it. It is this force of Life that allows the Kindred to exist, living the Life stolen from others each night. Accordingly, if Vitae can carry this fundamental force and bestow it upon another, why can it not do the same for other sources of strength? By enacting this Coil, the Dragon becomes able to create Vitae in a host of new forms, each one carrying the strength of a different Supernatural. When feeding on a supernatural, the vampire can inflict 1 Lethal and drain 1 point from their Power Pool. Instead of gaining normal Vitae, the blood he takes is infused with that power, able to unlock new abilities and strengths from the vampire's form. These alternative Vitae are still stored as part of the vampire's normal Vitae pool and can be spent to accomplish many of the same functions as usual, but each point must be marked in some way on the sheet so as to track them as distinct types of Vitae. They cannot be used to maintain the vampire and stave off torpor from night to night, however - that requires the essence of Life carried in the normal vitae. The particular strength of each new type of Vitae are listed below. The listed Physical Intensity attributes and skills replace Strength, Dexterity, and Stamina in determining which dice pools can be augmented by the Vitae.

    Changelings (Glamour)
    Physical Intensity: Manipulation, Perception Rolls, Rolls to decipher riddles or discern truth

    Discipline: Auspex – Spend 1 Glamour Vitae to activate Auspex powers (even if the Discipline would not normally cost Vitae) to achieve the following effects. Auspex 1-3: In addition to the normal list of possible questions, the player can also ask questions about Supernatural powers used in the immediate vicinity. Such questions cost two successes each, not one. Auspex 4: The Kindred can access the target’s dreams (or, in the case of Kindred, their Torpor dreams, including during the hours of daylight) and either read those dreams or modify them as they normally would read or modify thoughts. Auspex 5: The Kindred’s astral body travels through the corresponding regions of the Hedge rather than the mundane world.

    Special: Blush of Life - Changelings naturally shroud themselves with Glamour to conceal their nature behind a flawless illusion of mortality, and a Kindred drawing upon blood infused with that Glamour can accomplish the same. When the Blush of Life is activated using Glamour-infused Vitae, it seamlessly masks the Predatory Aura. Kindred can no longer automatically recognize the Dragon as a fellow vampire, and anyone using supernatural powers to identify him as Kindred must engage in a Clash of Wills against his Blood Potency + Composure.




    Demons (Aether)
    Physical Intensity: Intelligence, rolls to lie/deceive, rolls involving languages or memory

    Discipline: Obfuscate - If an Obfuscate power is activated using Demon blood, the player adds his Resolve to his dice pools during any Clash of Wills involving that power.

    Special: Blush of Life - In a world filled with lies, Demons are the masters of deception. With perfect control of their expressions and behaviors, they can lie with impunity and present any impression they wish to the world. Kindred find it difficult to maintain human behavior as their Humanity drops, but one who partakes of Demon blood can also partake of their skill at acting to compensate. When activating the Blush of Life using Aether-infused Vitae, the vampire is not subject to Humanity-based penalties to social rolls with mortals for the duration of the Blush.

    Werewolves (Essence)
    Physical Intensity: Resolve, rolls dealing with spirits, hunting/tracking rolls

    Discipline: Protean – When Protean is activated using Werewolf blood, the Kindred receives one of the special bonuses of the five werewolf forms depending on the level of the Discipline. Protean 1: Sheep’s Clothing - any efforts to pinpoint the Kindred while concealed in the ground suffer her Blood Potency as a penalty. Protean 2-3: Either Chase Down, using Vitae, or Weaken the Prey, as appropriate to the form taken. Protean 4: Primal Fear. Protean 5: Badass Motherfucker.

    Special: Predatory Aura – By infusing the Beast with a portion of the Werewolf’s Essence before Lashing out with it, the Dragon can batter his target with an aspect of Luna's curse instead of his own. If the Dragon wins the contested roll, he inflicts a Hunter’s Aspect in place of the normal Condition. The Bestial Condition is replaced with Resigned, Competitive is replaced with Submissive, and Wanton with Unaware. The Condition still fades naturally after a number of nights equal to the Dragon's Blood Potency.
    ---------------------------------------
    Resigned
    Your character faced down his hunter, and the frightening beast has shown him the essence of doom. While he may still fight or flee, there’s a part of him inside that welcomes the release of death. Your character may not spend Willpower to add dice or resistance for any action to defend himself from a deadly threat. He can fuel Gifts or use other actions, but his self-defense becomes lackluster at best.
    Resolution: Suffer a wound penalty from lethal or aggravated damage, or suffer a lethal wound in your character’s last Health box.
    Beat: n/a

    Submissive
    Your character faced the full bore of a Dragon’s essence. He’s cowed, and cannot bear to face the Dragon's fury. He cannot directly attack, insult, or defy the Dragon without first spending a Willpower point and making an extended, reflexive Resolve + Composure roll. You may make one roll per turn, but each roll requires an additional point of Willpower. The target number of successes is equal to the Dragon's Blood Potency. You don’t need to spend Willpower or roll if the Dragon has attacked your character this turn.
    Resolution: Suffer a wound penalty from lethal or aggravated wounds, or suffer a lethal wound in your character’s last Health box.
    Beat: n/a

    Unaware
    Your character has been dazed and confused, distracted, and internalized. He’s so withdrawn inward, that he cannot notice the proverbial (or literal) wolf at his door. Reduce his Composure by (the Dragon's Presence – 1), with a minimum of –1 — if the Dragon has Presence 4, it reduces Composure by –3.
    Resolution: Suffer a wound penalty from lethal or aggravated wounds, or suffer a lethal wound in your character’s last Health box.
    Beat: n/a
    ----------------------------------------

    Scales

    Poisonous Prophylactic
    Prerequisite: Tending the Flocks •
    The Dragon mixes hemotoxins, purifying salts, and binding agents into a thick paste that is then dried in an oven into a gritty tablet approximately the size of a triple-thick quarter. Once prepared, the tablet will remain potent for up to 12 hours before use. The mixture acts as a denaturing agent to Vitae, breaking it down and releasing the essential Life uselessly. If held in the mouth, the tablet will reduce any blood or Vitae drunk into a form of devalued blood, unable to provide Vitae, but also unable to create blood bonds or cause addiction in the drinker. The tablet tastes foul, and as it slowly dissolves in the mouth it will also destroy a single Vitae from the vampire's pool every 15 minutes until spat out (if swallowed, the tablet causes the vampire to immediately vomit it back up in a shower of gore, losing 3 Vitae on the spot). The tablet also inflames and frightens the vampire's beast; once it is disposed of, the vampire may need to roll for a Hunger frenzy at the ST's discretion (particularly if his pool has been reduced below 1/2 capacity). If given to a mortal, the tablet will still protect them from Vitae; however, they will take a level of lethal damage every 15 minutes as the mixture renders their lifeblood inert. Ghouls will respond to the table the same as a vampire would until their stored Vitae runs out, then as a mortal thereafter. The mixture can also be injected into a body, in which case the injector can set the dosage to affect a certain number of Vitae. The mixture will continue to be active until that many Vitae have been neutralized, whether through the damage done every 15 minutes or eliminating freshly injected blood. The potential remains for Hunger Frenzy when the mixture wears off.

    Galvanic Amplification
    Prerequisite: Strength in the Blood ••
    The connection between life and galvanic energy (an aspect of electricity poorly understood and long-since dismissed by mortal scientists) has long been acknowledged by the more scientifically-minded of the supernatural communities. The Dragon can use this to his advantage. By having a subject Kindred drink to repletion (full Vitae pool), then applying carefully calibrated and placed current to their body, the Dragon can 'enliven' their blood, making it more vital and resistant to outside influences. As a mortal body's blood produces antibodies to attack and destroy foreign organisms, the enlivened Kindred's blood will begin breaking down and consuming foreign Vitae with extraordinary speed and effectiveness.

    For the next 24 hours, if the subject vampire drinks Vitae, he will automatically resist forming blood bonds without the expenditure of Willpower. He may add half the Dragon's points in the Coil of the Devourer, rounded up, to his Blood Potency to resist the blood bond, and any such resistance attempts during this time will not add penalties to future efforts to resist the same vampire. Vitae addiction is unaffected by this Scale.

    Sanguinary Incubator
    Prerequisite: Strength in the Blood ••
    The difference between blood and Vitae is obvious but not well-understood, even by the Kindred. Similarly, the precise reason why artificially-preserved blood is so much less potent for the Kindred remains something of a mystery. What the Order does know is that there is some essential aspect of Life held in blood and concentrated in Vitae which somehow escapes or breaks down after the blood is extracted. The Dragons have failed to find a workable means of preserving the fluids' vitality outside its host; however, they have discovered means to preserve the integrity of the blood or Vitae when placed in a living or Kindred body. By encouraging the Vitae's tendency to hold together and maintain its identity (and provoking that same trait in mortal or supernatural blood), they can also allow it to hold together in a mass and be extracted as a discrete sample at the desired time.

    By treating the essential fluid with an alchemical serum and returning it to a Kindred or mortal body within minutes of extraction (usually via an injection), the Dragon can preserve it and its nature for future use. Mortals can carry a single point; ghouls and Kindred reduce their Vitae Pool Maximum by 1 for each point being carried. Once withdrawn again, the blood or Vitae will have the exact same properties as when it was first withdrawn from its original owner, as if no time had passed. Due to the treatment to make it cohesive, blood or Vitae stored this way is attracted to the strength of a Kindred's own Vitae and will 'rise to the surface' in response to it. Consequently, a Kindred feeding on someone hosting such blood will automatically consume the stored fluids before beginning on the host's own blood. Dragons using this Scale are advised to monitor and protect their subjects diligently lest their precious samples be slurped down as a night's snack by some Carthian yokel.
