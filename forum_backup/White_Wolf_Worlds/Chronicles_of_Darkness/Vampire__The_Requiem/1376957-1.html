                    Originally posted by Tessie View Post

                    By RAW, you use the rules written under Swarm Form when you use Swarm Form.
                    Indeed and the very first sentence for Swarm Form is: "When taking the Beast's Skin..." Later on when they reference "Roll Strength + Brawl" they are referring to the stats of the swarm because that is the baseline of this power. There actually is one place where they specifically note to use the Vampire's own stats, and that is in reference to movement speed. Why would they specifically call out that one specific part unless the intent was for the rest of the power to behave in the same way as Beast's Skin as stated in the very first sentence?

                    Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                    What strenght+Brawl are they talking abou if there is no swarm stat defined in the book? .
                    Where do they have stats for other animal forms? There are some in a word file here on the forum but also the ChroD base book which also includes a templates for various animals and antagonists, one of which is "swarm of vermin". I personally think that template is on the strong side but you as the ST get to make of it what you will.

                    Perhaps if you decided to stat it out yourself, a starting point could be to take the base animal's stats and bump strength and stamina by a dot or two for the extra muscle and biomass, then bump brawl by maybe a couple dots to signify swarm tactic coordination or something. Most size zero creatures are going to be quite low in all of those stats so you're looking at 2-3 dots each.

                    Again I think using the Vampire's stats is a just peachy homebrew rule if you like that, but a thorough read of the actual wording makes it clear that by RAW you use the "Physical Attributes and Skills" of the swarm.

                    Edit: It is also clear why they would word it in this way to prevent it from becoming overpowered. If it used the Vampire's own stats then they could easily break the power by spending xp on physical attributes and skills to steadily increase the combat effectiveness of the swarm. By making it use the animal's stats, they are forever fixed in place and even physical Disciplines are hardcapped at 5 dots whereas attributes can go all the way up to 10 with higher Blood Potency.

                                    Double means that you can use the trained guard dog stats to represent a swarm of vermin.

                                    Regardless, that was published a long time after Swarm Form was published, not to mention that each game is supposed to be fully playable without additional supplements. That list of animals was specifically made to complement Vampire.

                                    Neither can we use the animal's own stat in many cases because most animals with Size 0 would never be represented through stats. An ant does not have any Attribute or Skill dots, or any derived Traits. It's simply a feature of the environment.

                                    By process of elimination, it's only the vampire's own stats that can be used in those cases. For the sake of consistency they should be used in all cases.

                                    Then there's also the matter of "does this make sense?" Does it make sense that several cubic meters of vermin all tearing at a victim would be just as effective as a single individual in terms of the dice pool? While also dealing lethal damage even if the species (like toads, or most insects) naturally deals bashing?
                                    Does it make more sense that the swarm is a physical representation of the vampire and uses the vampire's stats, rather than those of a single individual creature within the swarm with no scaling to account for the fact that it's a swarm?
                                    I think the latter makes much more sense.

                                                            Cross-posted from the "Vampires vs. Werewolves!" thread:

                                                            On swarms: as much as I like the VtR:2nd corebook, I'd suggest that Onyx Path really nailed the mechanics of swarms more effectively in Demon (pg. 179) and in Beast (pg. 126). If I were running another Vampire game, I'd probably adapt Swarm Form to the following:

                                                            - Radius is two yards per dot of Blood Potency
                                                            - Speed is double normal; move in three dimensions; fit through any opening
                                                            - Inflict one bashing damage per turn to everything in the swarm's radius OR condense on one target & inflict lethal damage/drain Vitae equal to Blood Potency
                                                            - Armor effective against swarm damage only if covering the whole body, and even then only at half normal rating
                                                            - Everyone else in the swarm's radius takes -2 penalty to all actions; may trigger Breaking Points for the entophobic
                                                            - Taking damage reduces radius proportionally (take half damage, shrink to half size)
                                                            - Non-area damage capped at 1 (normal) or 2 (Exceptional Success)
                                                            - On any turn in which the swarm condenses, these caps double (max. 2 normal, 4 Exceptional); easier to squish a lot of tiny things when they're all tightly grouped together
                                                            - Full damage from fire, sunlight, area attacks
                                                            - Celerity & Resilience work as normal
                                                            - Vigor passive adds one yard of extra radius per dot; Vigor active adds to effective Blood Potency for that turn when condensing
                                                            - Can't use Claws of the Unholy
                                                            - Only one physical "active" effect per turn (e.g. if using Vigor active, can't use Celerity active)*

                                                            Edit:
                                                            *Watsonian explanation: each of those tiny, gnashing bodies can't channel the power of the Curse as effectively as the vampire's "real" form, and/or the vampire's power is already taxed by independently coordinating so many moving parts at once.

                                                            Doylist explanation: being a swarm already makes it very tough for most foes to cause you harm. It should be an overall buff, to reflect the XP spent on the Merit, but the combination of automatic lethal damage (Vigor active) + swarm immunity + preemption in case someone brought a grenade or a flamethrower (Celerity active) seems too much; pick two.
