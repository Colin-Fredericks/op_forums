                                                    Having touchstones does make Detachment rolls significantly less painful, but remember that just being forced to make the roll causes a condition unless you get an exceptional success; those conditions can really hurt under the right circumstances. A Humanity 7 vampire with two touchstones isn't likely to lose a Humanity for taking a bullet in the shoulder (Detachment roll at 7 dice for suffering an injury that would hospitalize a human), but if they get less than 5 successes they might suffer Bestial condition, making Frenzy all the more likely if the fighting continues.

                                                    I think it's also important emphasize that a vampire has to work to hold on to his or her Touchstones. They represent relationships that require time and energy to maintain and secret vulnerabilities the character's enemies would love to exploit.

                                                    You shouldn't get extra dice on Detachment rolls just because you know that you have a daughter out there somewhere; you have to sneak into her room at night every so often to read her a bedtime story, or dedicate time to patrolling her neighborhood and scaring the monsters and burglars away, or something similar. Likewise, you shouldn't be safe from humanity loss just because your childhood home is still standing. You have to take time to walk in the yard, look in the windows, maybe even sit by the spot where your bed used to be and have a bit of a cry. These are things that aren't necessarily easy to conceal, and which could lead to all sorts of complications, some of which might even trigger breaking points.
