        Originally posted by Scutarii View Post
        Could you give an example of how to use these 'softer' targets to drive the Werewolves back, prevent them launching or succeeding at a Hunt targetingthe Vampires, etc.? These Vampires don't want these Werewolves next door and want them to leave, but will settle for some form of deal/oath between the groups that will keep the Werewolves away from Vampiric interests.

        In the second scenario (Werewolves have killed touchstones and Vampires want them dead) could you give an example of how to use the manipulated 'softer' targets to cause long term harm or death to the Werewolf?

        'Target them through the less supernaturally potent members of the pack' is a starting point, but what is the scheme that gets to pushing the Werewolves away?
        Suicide bombers, getting information, poisoning the pack, selling out to rival packs for advatnage. There's several ways you can leverage this.


        Cool, biting = bad, clawing = simpler to deal with..but still bad. Does mean not getting caught up in grapples is important. What tools do Werewolves have for dealing with something in a non-solid form like Protean 5? I don't mean ghosts/spirits in Twilight (there's a gift I've seen for that as well as a Rite to force them to manifest).
        Protean swarm form and mist forms are actually pretty powerful advatnages on the vampire side. However, I think Gifts of the Elements or some other nature-power might work against the vampire in this case.


        What could the Kindred do to ensure they aren't being found - especially considering their daytime issues? Same for Vampire ghouls and key mortal pawns, how to keep them safe from determined Wolves trying to force a face to face encounter?
        Not much can escape a werewolf hunting. Wolves literally have a power to track you by where your shadow has been. The best thing that vampires can do is just get in a car, get distance, and plan an encounter somewhere they have the advantage. Tracking on two/four feet takes time. Harrass the wolves through police, so that they can regain initiative.


        Any suggestions on how a Vampire could deal with a determined Rite master with Spirit allies spying, attacking from the Shadow and Twilight, etc.? An allied Fire spirit in Twilight hanging around the Kindred for example.
        Spirits need essence, so there's limited number of abilities they can use, and won't go somewhere too far from their fetters. Reaching is very costly, and having powerful abilities is reserved for more powerful spirits, who demand much in return for their services. The best defense is making sure the ST plays the spirits intellegently. Also, perform an abjuration.

        What are those key resources the Werewolves have in the world that they really need that the Kindred can attack and take from them?
        Their pack. Werewolves aren't psychopaths, they have friends and family. Many Iron Masters end up with lots of contacts and allies, or even join the poilice department. The wolves are very territorial, so wll have buildings and neighborhoods they want to maintain and control, which includes people.

        And then, there's the locus. Wreewolves need essence, and maintaining a locus is critically important. Not only for their own reserves, but for their spirit allies that this pack is theoretically hiring out as spies and attackers. Abandoning a locus is a huge blow to the Uratha, larger than forcing a vampire out of its feeding grounds. Vampires can feed anywhere. Werewolves need that essence to maintain spirit allies. Otherwise, they have to spend lots of time hunting spirits in the Hisil, and who has time for that? The wolves could also eat people, but that would eventually trap them in the Hisil as well, making it a losing proposition.

        After speed reading the W:tF2e book I never really came away with the feeling that the Werewolves actually care that much about mortal society. They can fairly effectively live without it and can adopt an almost 'scorched earth' policy, losing homes, wealth, contacts and the like in the pursuit of the Hunt and rebuild after. Afterall, if they Hunt down and kill the Vampires they can rebuild their homes, wealth and contacts. I think there was even a tribe or lodge that was sort of about knowing when to discard resources for the Hunt.
        Scorched earth policies are very good ways of creating Wounds. Forsaken want to maintain the Hisil/Flesh balance, and the Pure have spirit gods they want to follow, forcing certain resonance. Unless your werewolves are demented, no one wants that much bad spiritual resonance in the area. Spirits of destruction are not easy to get rid of.

        And there's very good chance that you can't "simply rebuild" after scorching it. Losing your Contacts can lead to them refusing to talk to you ever again, which means that long term hunting is difficult. Losing your home can raise the eyebrows of insurance companies, who mark the wolves for arson investigations.

        And, of course, there's the fear of hunters as well. Going Garau in front of witnesses leads to wolf bloods. Wolf blood can become hunters immune to lunacy. Werewolves need to hide from humans as much as vampires do.

        I almost don't see how you can avoid the Death Spiral. If the Vampires are bing successful at the shutting down of the Pack through social means that's likely to start causing frustrations and tension and aggression amongst the group of almost hair trigger death rage monsters who have specific rites and culture about Sacred Hunts.
        First, its a false cliche that vampires only work through Allies and other social contacts. Yes, the Invictus and Movement specialize in it, and their Masquerade counterparts encourage this cliche, but that's only two of five covenants in Requiem. An Ordo Dracul with Coil of the Vovoid, for instance, would rely on bloodbonds instead, inflicting a full Vinculum when the wolf bites him in combat. The Lance and Circle would use their rituals to mess with the wolves instead of social groups.

        Meanwhile, a Daeva that approaches the wolves in a crowded space can Majesty the entire group, assuming they're not willing to Garou out in public. Even if one of the wolves had Fearless Hunter, it won't protect the pack as a whole. Fearless Hunter protects against the whole pack area-based influnces; Awe is a wide area, but its uncontested. Confidant, Green Eyes, and Loyalty are all single targets. So, if we assume that a wolf has Fearless Hunter (doubtful, if this group is as honorless as you imply; FH is an Honor based Gift) and does succeed against Confidant (again, its doubtful, since the vampire will still likely ahve a higher dice pool than the wolf), that's still a bunch of other wolves that don't stand a chance. Confidant has no cost, so it can be used repeatedly in rapid fire succession. Once the wolves have been Charmed, they won't want to attack the vampire. However, the vampire can use Green Eyes to send them on suicidal missions. Or a group orgy that ends up with all their blood drained, or under full vinculum plus addicted to the Kiss. A single vampire, in the right situation, can shut down an entire pack, and that's as a starting character. Imagine if we had Loyalty and Idol as well? Backup ghouls and cotorie mates?


        The problem here is that your theoretical vampires are letting the wolves play to their strength - predators hunting their prey. The vampires need to shift the confrontation to some arena where they have the advantage. Werewolves are built to hunt down their prey, slowly wearing down their strength. Vampires are built for shocks of speed and direct confrontation and social maneuvering; even powerful Xanatos Gambit elders manipulate through moving their empowered pawns, who use their rather direct abilities. This picking at each other is just something that wolves specialize in. That's not how vampires hunt at all. Death spiraling is playing to werewolf strength, not vampire strength.

                                                In general, werewolves hunt. Everything that werewolves do revolves around hunting, its their main strength. They need to maintain hunting grounds, they care about their pack, they are master shapeshifters. If it revolves around one of those four things, chances are that a werewolf can do it, and be better than any other supernatural at it. Hunting, territory, pack, shapeshifting.

                                                Rahu are the warriors - they hunt on battlefields. Irraka are the stealthy types - stalkers and assassins. Itheur are the shamans, who hunt down and bind spirits. Elodoth are the social face of the pack, the envoy, the judge, the one who plays politics and knows the red tape and connections to get things done. The Cahalith are... well, they're glorified bards - they're part socialite, part pack caretender, who coordinates and strengthens the pack on the hunt; jacks of all trades. Much like the D&D party that the five Auspice kinda reflect, these five play off each other, each filling a different need and tactic in the hunt for their prey in different ways. Packs are designed to work with each other, and play off each other's strengths. Each has a role that they are called to fulfill, and are encouraged to act within it.

                                                            I agree with the cops thing. The most important thing is to never meet them face to face if you can avoid it.

                                                            Because they can track by scent, Hunters in Darkness can locate all your safe places just by looking at you, an Uratha with Know Thy Prey can identify all your allies, contacts and other social merits just by looking at you, and an Elodoth can identify any lie you tell them face to face

                                                            As far as obfuscate, they can track you via Impossible Spoor (triggering a clash of will at least), and Boundary Ward can prevent you ever getting the drop on them in their den. Lore of the Land will outright reveal you if you are nearby. If they have Pack Awareness it makes it even harder to surprise one without alerting all the others. Similarly, if you fail to surprise one, you fail to surprise all the one with Pack Awareness. Fearless Predator means that if you fail to Dominate or Majesty one, they all resist. Together they are strong.

                                                            The best options are to split them up. Like Ashenrogue said, get one of them detained by Police for questioning. Frame them for a crime and have the police keep them for questioning or in Jail overnight (a Saturday night arrest sometimes means you spend the weekend in Jail in some small towns until court opens up on Monday to process you). The important thing again, is to never have it link back to your coterie. While there, you can give them water with one of your vampire's blood in it - get some viniculum going.

                                                            Turn them against each other. Let the police come asking for one of the others, as though the detained one broke under pressure. Make up lies. Find out where they spend time separately and force them to spend more time there alone. You have to take them down one by one. The thing is, killing one is going to get the rest banding together to find the threat, so the best thing to do is to let there appear to be no threat for as long as you can. Take out one or two in separate coordinated strikes, get one or two under viniculum. Either way they are much weaker as a pack without one of their auspices. Of course with Read the World's Loom they might be able to divine vampire involvement, it's still helpful.

                                                            You can also just turn the city on them, calling a citywide manhunt. Make the territory too hot for them. Turn the gangs against them, get their faces on the news, turn the people against them. Force them underground then find where they are underground and get that info out too. They can't fight the territory. Taking their territory from them is definitely a victory against werewolves.

                                                            If you're eventually gonna fight, then taking one or two down first is your best option.
