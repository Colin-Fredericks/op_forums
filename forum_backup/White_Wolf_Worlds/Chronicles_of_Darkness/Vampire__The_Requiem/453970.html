    Fun with Bane Ideas

    03-23-2015, 04:43 AM
    The Bane Idea thread! Let's see what kinds of Banes people can think up!

    Just to get the ball rolling...

    Blood Bed: Your character is bound to blood in a way that other vampires are not. If your character doesn't sleep in an area that's had at least two points of blood spilled onto it, all rolls to resist frenzy are capped by her humanity dots for the rest of the night.

    Hallowed Ground: Your character cannot pass over sacred or otherwise spiritually significant grounds. Should he do so, he immediately suffers bashing damage equal to (10-humanity). He cannot heal the damage until he leaves the site.

                  Running Water: Your character finds crossing running water to be extremely troublesome. Note, this bane refers to naturally occurring flowing water--rivers, streams, rain, and the ocean count as running water while showers, sewer lines, pools, and ponds do not. In order to pass through or over running water your character must spend a point of willpower. Also, as long as the water touches her, all of her dice pools are limited by her humanity dots. However, despite not being able to cross by her own volition, this bane can be avoided if she finds a way to be carried over or through such barriers by someone else such as by boat or by car. Furthermore she may avoid the bane if she can fly over it by at least (10-Humanity) hundred feet.

                                      Eastern european folklore often depicts vampires with Arithmomania, also known as complusive counting. In the folklore the counting is usally of beans, seeds and grains of rice. Supposedly, people in eastern europe would scatter these items on top of a vampire's grave in order to distract them and keep them from hunting at night.

                                      So I think that a vampire with this bane would have to roll humanity when confronted by a large amount of small objects of the same type. If they fail the roll, they have to count all of them or lose a willpower point.

                                          Moral Blood: Your character finds that one's morality impacts how nourishing their blood is. Perhaps she favors the blood of the innocent (morality stat 6 or more) or contrariwise, she may favor the taste of a darker life (morality stat 5 or less). When drinking from her preference, she may reap blood as normal. But, when drinking from a vessel of an opposing morality to her preference, the first (10-Humanity) Vitae she drains carries no nourishment.

                                          Blood Engorgement: Your character finds that when well-fed, blood seems to super-saturate his body in an inconvenient way. The effect gives him a dark, ruddy complexion and a bloated countenance; some might even claim his flesh is pumped drum-tight with blood. Whenever he feeds to within (10-Humanity) points of his Max Vitae limit, his flesh expands with blood, thus capping all his social roles by his humanity even when invoking the blush of life. The effect persists even through the day sleep, and won't abate until the excess Vitae is spent.

                                              Compulsion: Your character is hounded by strange OCD-like urges. The subject of the compulsion should be chosen when taking the bane, and folkloric myths are a good reference. Counting seeds and grains of rice, untying knots, or organizing silverware are all acceptable compulsions. When faced with such a subject your character must succeed in a humanity roll to resist indulging in the compulsion. If he fails, he must enact the urge, or he may spend a point of willpower to avoid it. If the subject remains in his presence for more than (Humanity) minutes he must re-roll to resist the urge once again, unless he leaves the area. Conversely, if he fulfills the urge, the subject which caused the urge may be ignored for the rest of the night.
