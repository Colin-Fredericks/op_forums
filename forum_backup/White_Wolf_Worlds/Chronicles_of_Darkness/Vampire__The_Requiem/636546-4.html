        Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post
        Maybe the blood bond got a revamp (heh) in 2nd edition, but in 1st the bond worked like this

        1st stage - No compulsion is manifested yet, although the subject does feel subconsciously drawn to follow the vampire around. They just keep appearing in the same place by coincidence.

        2nd stage - The subject begins to perceive the vampire as a friend, although is still not outright compelled to follow orders.

        3rd stage - The subject becomes a slave to the vampire's will.


        And in regards to severing the bond, the book clearly stated that death of the master would sever the bond, but said there were accounts of fellow vampires continuing to fulfill their master's will after they were destroyed. The length of time for doing so varied from one individual to the next.
        The bond did got revamped in 2nd ed.

        Now, though here are still fluff indicating the same degrees of obsession, the mechanics become clear in the effects, relating to the Social Maneuvering system.

        Each drop causes an improvement of +X in Impression and a reduce of -X in number of Doors, plus +X bonus dice in social rolls against the target (no powers covered, though). Just toss the number of stages in those Xs and you got most of it. How you interpret the fluff can actually change how the Social Maneuvering works, since you can change the actual interests of the target, but it is completely at ST or Player discretion. No Soc-Man rule is really changed.

        And the bond ends in 1 year, give or take, all the stages vanishing at once. You need blood to restart the time countdown.
