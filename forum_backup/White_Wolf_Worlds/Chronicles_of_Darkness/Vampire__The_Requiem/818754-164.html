            You roll the contested roll every roll of the ritual.

            I agree that the Potency system is completely whack. It's extremely random whether you'll get good Potency or not. Higher dice pools increase the plausible range of outcomes, but 0 extra successes will always be part of that range, and never less plausible than any other outcome.
            Unfortunately I don't know how to fix it. Your suggestion of being able to keep rolling is interesting, but that would make it very easy to reach outrageous amounts of Potency. Personally I just avoid any rituals using Potency or, if my ST allows it, rewrite them to not use Potency at all.

            How to determine Target Number can be found the first edition book Blood Sorceries. The writers use that book to design rituals.

                                    Entombed Command allows you to set a trigger by which somebody becomes Mesmerized and susceptible to orders and other depended uses of Dominate from you, which Chain of Command can piggyback off of to allow you to use a proxy to apply your specified uses of Dominate, thereby circumventing the stated limitation of Entombed Mesmerize ("If the vampire isn't present to capitalize…"), is my read.                                                 11-12-2018, 05:36 AM
                                                Originally posted by Enokh View Post
                                                I KNOW this has been answered before, but fuck if I can find it.

                                                How/when does Auspex force a Clash of Wills for Obfuscate 1-3? Is it when you use Auspex 1 in an area with Obfuscate at all, or would it be when you use Auspex 1 and the answer is someone who is Obfuscated? For instance, you don't Clash at the invisible man waiting to kill you if you use Auspex 1 to find the person most likely to give you what you want, but WILL if you ask who is most likely to hurt you.
                                                It's when you ask a question with Auspex to which the answer is covered by Obfuscate.

                                                Examples:
                                                Auspex 1: "Who here most wants to harm me" would Clash if that person is Obfuscated.
                                                Auspex 2: "Is this person a supernatural" would Clash if that person uses Obfuscate to masquerade as a mortal.

                                                It's not a specific rule that Auspex and Obfuscate will lead to Clashes. It's just an example since that's one of the more common situations where a Clash might occur.
                                                When the question stated with Auspex would reveal something that Obfuscate has hidden, then there will be a Clash because both powers can't be resolved at the same time.
