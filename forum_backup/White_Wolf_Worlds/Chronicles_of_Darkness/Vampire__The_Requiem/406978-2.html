                    I hate to rain on the parade, but the psychokinesis merit is described as the manipulation of a force. The examples include manifestations of force so I guess those are okay too, not much practical difference really. Known forces include electricity, magnetism, gravity, nuclear, and friction. Common manifestations of forces would be heat, cold, light, radiation, sound, wind, weather, and earthquakes. Wind for air, magnetism for metal, cold for ice (cold + water = ice), heat for fire (heat + combustibles = fire) would all make sense. A weaker argument could be made to include fire and ice as they are also manifestations of force. It just so happens the merit includes fire so go crazy. Fire is not however matter. Things like metal, blood and wood are out. A force might manipulate one of those things, but you would not create them.

                                    I would agree that not having the Scale to begin with would help, but yes.

                                    Seriously, though, it feels... out of place? Alright, hear me out on this one:

                                    The Coil is about reclaiming former powers of the mind, fluffwise. The other scale is about shielding people from mind control by lobotomizing them (which is cool and thematic.) Grafting body parts from other creatures just feels like an attempt to shoe-horn Vicissitude in; I'd much prefer to see Scales dealing with twisted psychological experiments, or "experimental" brain surgeries. Grafts should be specific and... messy, with stuff like opening up "third eyes" or engorging the pituary gland so that you can speak to Eris.

                                    As it is, the Scale is either too niche, or too strong to pass up on if you're going Ordo. I mean, it's missing any information on how you would deal with traits you don't have (like Rank or Renown) and it's incredibly vague on what you can steal.

                                    Can I steal a Werewolf's ability to shapeshift if I come up with the right bullshit? Can I take a Demon's ability to form covers, lie perfectly, or spoof?
