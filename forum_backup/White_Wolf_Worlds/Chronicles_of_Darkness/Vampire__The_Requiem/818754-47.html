                                                            Originally posted by FutureBondVillain View Post

                                                            Generally yes. You can lash out at someone using Obfuscate 4 though, and if someone is invisible per Obfuscate 3 and attacks you you are able to perceive them and lash out to break Obfuscate.

                                                            I was thinking about being alerted to their presence by their "passing signs", like creaking floor boards, etc. (as per Obfuscate 3 description). Or having some other reason to think there was a vampire there.
