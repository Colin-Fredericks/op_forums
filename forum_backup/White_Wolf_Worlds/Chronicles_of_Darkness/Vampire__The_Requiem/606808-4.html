        Well, given claims that Obfuscate is potentially overpowered, I re-read the disciplines. A few points I noticed:

        Fifth dot is entirely irrelevant to this discussion.

It is a really nice Gift, but it certainly isn't a bigger advantage than, say, Garou form.
