        This is what I use for my Homebrew


        Originally posted by The Dark
        The Dark is the native otherworldly place of Strix and Tengu. It is where they can vanish to and come forth from if they are so inclined and empowered. Defining the Dark as a place or world is skirting several implications made by those words because the Dark, while it is usually spoken of as a location when it is described by those that know of it, lacks many qualities of anything else labeled a place, location, or world.
        Nothing outside nor inside the Dark can sense anything within the Dark. Within the Dark there is no sense of space, time, matter, other beings within the Dark, or even one’s own body. Without these it is hard to say movement or travel happens but while in the Dark a being is just a consciousness experiencing a profoundly deep blackness. Beings in the Dark with the ability to sense things without light can shift their perceptions to what seem to be windows of sensory input from the places of absolute darkness which are Resonant with being enervated of life force, such as the corpses left from vampire feeding sealed in a shallow grave, the husks left by Reaper Mages locked in a lightless cell, the area between the walls that affected by a miasma of siphoned of vitality caused by unspeakable life-draining horrors, ect... These area are where beings that have the Gate Manifestation as it relates to the Dark, which is built upon an Opening the aforementioned Resonant Condition, can cross over. Beings native to the Dark naturally are in Twilight in the Material world and are visible but incorporeal to vampires, being on the same metaphysical frequency but not physical beings themselves.

        I strongly agree with this, and is why I've made the Dark in the way I have and am currently Homebrewing the flattening of the power stats to instead be being pulled out of the material world as high ranking Ephemeral beings are, making Methuselah vampires caught in cycles of blood-thinning torpor that if they break leads them to being drawn into Dark, only able to escape based on Conditions, like Ephemeral beings. (Doing the same with the other splats at power stat 6 or above for their particular otherworlds)

        I also use the various power sources Vitae, Mana, Essense, Plasm, Glamour, ect... for the appropriate Ephemeral being and to determine what physical beings are on the same "frequency" to see them in Twilight.
