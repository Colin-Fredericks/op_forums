    Mystery Cults in Requiem

    04-01-2016, 02:08 PM
    So, I wrote this mystery cult for a character of mine:

    Mystery Cult (Sworn of the Axe •••••)
    •: Sworn (•) Merit or Spec in a weapon of choice
    ••: The Atrocious, Cutthroat or Enticing Merit
    •••: Fighting Finesse (••) or a dot of Weaponry
    ••••: Kindred Dueling (•••) or Twilight Judge (•••)
    •••••: Cruscible (•••) or the character can select a new Mystery after one is mastered.

    Issue is, ST thinks mystery cults are only strictly human organization, to which I disagree wholeheartedly. Thoughts?

    PD: I'm in the process of making mystery cults for the Dying Light and the Sworn of the Mysteries.

              A couple of things to add some clarity here. I did not say that Mystery cult could not be taken by vampires. The issue that I had was that I think the cult itself needed to be a human thing. Something open to mortals as well as vampires as it is not a vampire merit. Like stated above, it is a mortal merit.

              "One dot is an
              initiate, two a respected member, three a priest or organizer,
              four a decision-making leader, five a high priest or founder.               If
              you wish your character to begin play in a cult, work with your
              Storyteller to develop the details."

              ^this is what We are trying to do.

              "Designing a Mystery Cult requires three things, at bare
              minimum. First is a Purpose. This is the defining reason the
              cult exists. Usually, it’s tied in with the cult’s history and recent
              background. Second is a Relic. This is an item that grounds its
              members’ faith. For example, a piece of the God-Machine, an
              ancient text bound in human flesh, or the mummified flesh
              of a saint. The last is a Doctrine. Every cult is defined by its
              rules and traditions."

              ^ From what We had been discussing. I did not feel that this cult had met those 3 things. His cult as I had understood it was the Sworn, part of the Ordo. I did not feel that the ordo is a cult that has a relic that they worship and routed in some sort of faith. Also I do not think they have a doctrine of rules and traditions other than .. dont share our secrets. They do have a purpose, but it's an individual purpose, not a group purpose.


              And only to further my point this is a section of secret society junky which indicates that the Mystery cult initiation is for non kindred organizations.

              "Secret Society Junkie (•)
              When the vampire takes Status or Mystery
              Cult Initiation (see p. 121) reflecting non-Kindred organizations,
              she also gains Herd dots equal to the Merit"



              I did suggest that he bring this here and get some outside opinions about what the intended purpose of Mystery cult merit was intended for. So that is the purpose of this thread. I do believe.

                  I think the doctrine of rules and traditions is laid out mostly in the Rites of the Dragon, which probably acts as a relic as well. But it's much more clear in the Ordo Dracul book. The person would be Mara.

                  Also, the Secret Society Junkie merit referring to non-Kindred organizations is to apply the Herd dots to it, something that wouldn't fit with Kindred organizations, as Kindred aren't herd.

                  A brief section about the Sworn of the Axe, from p59 in the Ordo Dracul covenant book.

                  All the efforts of the Ordo Dracul are for naught if it cannot protect itself from those who would do it harm and steal its secrets. Those Dragons who swear the Oath of the Axe are the covenant’s most honored members, the first among equals according to the Rites. To them go the first spoils of battle: first choice of victims and first claim to properties seized. In return, the Sworn of the Axe are expected to place their unlives on the line for the Academy. If vampire-hunters are on the trail of a fellow Dragon, the Axe is Oathbound to intercede and defend their fellow Dragon no matter the cost to their own number. In times of violent conflict, the word of these martial vampires is supreme; a Dragon who disobeys them in matters of war and defense can expect to suffer immediate destruction. If exceptional mercy is shown, the miscreant can still look forward to an unusual amount of pain and suffering that will forever burn the memory of her insubordination into her flesh and soul.

                                          Throw my hat into the pile that reads Secret Society Junkie as only granting a herd bonus for Cults that have mortals, not that Mystery Cults can only be mortal institutions.

                                          That being said, the thing you have to be careful with in Cults is making sure there is something of substance there, and not just a way to spend 5 Dots to get 10 Dots worth of Merits. I would want to rework some thematic levels in there beyond just combat/status bonuses. I especially don't like Level 2 which is just a generic buff, and seems more associated with the practitioners of the Coil of Wyrm than members of the Axe.
