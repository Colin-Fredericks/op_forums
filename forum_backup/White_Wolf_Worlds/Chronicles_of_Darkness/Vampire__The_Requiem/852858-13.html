        Just wanted to say this AP inspired me to run a similar game with my group.
        Just a few changes on my end - the Prince is Carthian and the Anarchs are Invictus (because let's face it, it makes perfect sense); and Tourette isn't just a Malkovian, she's a Hollow (see Mekhet clanbook) who forgot which one is supposed to be reflection.


        As such, a couple of things I want to share:

        That Bloody Axe

        It used to be a regular fire axe, until someone used it to chop up a family like firewood. To this day, the blade is still stained by old blood, and the wooden handle almost seems to pulse when handled. It is thirsty, and needs to drink deep.
        System: Whenever the axe kills something, the blood seeps through the handle to the wielder, who gains 1 Vitae, as if feeding. Also, the axe feels like a kindred spirit to the wielder's Beast, who can spend 1 Vitae to add a +3 to a Predatory Aura roll. The Beast also doesn't want to let go of it, and forcingly removing the Axe from its wielder is grounds for a Frenzy check.

        The Heart Full of Flies (AKA: Where the hell did Pisha get that?)

        It's a glass replica of an anatomically correct heart, like one might see in a medical school. Except the inside seems to be filled with horseflies - living ones, at that.
        This one made a buzz - it also popped up as a major Artifact in a Mage game I ran for my group. If you want to see where it's from, check Mage's Reign of the Exarchs.

        System: If a Kindred replaces his heart with this one (specifics for that still TBD), he can then use the highest of his Humanity or Blood Potency to determine his Max Vitae and Vitae per Turn. However, he must also use the lowest Humanity or Blood Potency to determine the effect of all Banes.
