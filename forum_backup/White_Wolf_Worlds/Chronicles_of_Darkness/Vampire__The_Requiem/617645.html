    Elysium

    08-06-2015, 08:57 AM
    So, I'm currently writing a 2nd edition chronicle based in New Orleans, and I'm pondering how to run Elysium in this particular campaign.
    Currently my plan is to have several Elysium in the city, each sponsored by different clans. They will, of course, be open for any and all who wishes to go there, but each will have it's own style (matching the clan) and focus. While one may focus more on the business side of Elysium, another may simply be a hangout, and another something else entirely.
    I have an idea of what I want from a Gangrel Elysium and from a Ventrue Elysium - descriptions below - but I'm not so sure of what I would want from other clans.

    And so, I hope you guys have some suggestions? They will be much appreciated.

    Gangrel:
    An underground fighting pit with several arenas and lots of seatings. There are two sections; "The Pit" and "The V.I.P. Section",
    The Pit is a large open space with visible conrete floors. There are three arenas, which are constantly active, spread throughout the room. These "arenas" are merely floorspace surrounded by wooden walls, approximately 10 meters wide and 1 meter high. No seating. Illegal underground fighting at its worst.
    Anyone here can bet on any fight, cash only (no credit!). And any mortal can fight - which means kindred can supply their own fighters, if they so desire, and see how they fare against opponents of equal breeding, or against the house fighters. But fihters enter at the own risk; and though this is strictly hand-to-hand combat, no weapons allowed, there are no other rules - so, occassionally someone dies.
    The V.I.P section if for kindred only. Black carpets on the floor, wall to wall. In the middle of the room is an actual arena. A "cage" of hardened glass walls with red metal edges. Around the arena are tables and seatings. Blood dolls cater to your every need, save blood only. You are hunters after all, act like it! The master of Elysium, an ancillae in the employ of a Gangrel elder. Here kindred come to wager in the hopes of gaining the elders favor. They wager secrets, informations, boons - or whatever else the Elysium's benefactor deems appropriate, and fights the house's champion. If they win, the elder will grant them a boon. If they lose, their wager is forfeit. But these bets are often bets of desperation - cause as they say "The house always wins".
    At other times kindred elect to fight each other in the arena. settling rivalries or evening scores over slights given.

    Ventrue:
    A fancy cigar club riddled with the cities mortal elite. Only members are welcome. Fine scotch, brandy, rum and high end cigars are enjoyed thoughout the premisis. At the tables people are conducting business, having conversations or playing backgammon/chess. The walls are filled end to end with hunting trophies; lions, bears, deer - you name it.
    The back room is for kindred only. This room resembles the others, yet here there are mortal retainers that are "in the know". They cater to the kindred, wine and dine them with their blood. After all, power means nothing without the luxury it affords you. Let others hunt if they must, but the civilized are above such things.
    Politics is the name of the game in this Elysium. Kindred often have only brief visits here as they come merely to conduct their business and leave soon thereafter. Especially if said business is with kindred they barely know or don't trust. It's usually here Ancillae announce tasks they can't be bothered to do something themselves (or simply just can't do themselves), and seek to employ others to do so.

    These ideas are in the "processing" stage, but they shoudl give you an idea of my frame of thought. I look forward to reading your thoughts on the matter.

                                  See, I dislike the Gangrel idea, because it doesn't have that bestial feel of the clan. I would start by adjusting the location, by moving it just outside of town. Next, I would put the Pit in the swampland, with gators and wildlife free to come and go. This would mean fighters have the murk, the muck and an easy way of disposing of "accidents". I would play up savagery of the setting by letting it be a local rumor that one or two of the local beasts have developed a taste for Vitae. The seating is in wooden platforms, on stilts, so onlookers are safe from the creatures and the fighters.

                                  Mekhet should have an old, rambling family home, in the French Quarter. It should be complete with balconies, twisted trees, and no electricity. Locally it is rumored to be haunted, and the current owner, an old woman, rumored to be a witch, only makes the rumor stronger. The clan loves how old the house is, and there's the fact that anyone coming in is at a disadvantage due to the candlelight and the moving shadows. Of course there should be books on top of books, ritual tools and lightning fast internet, all available at the low, low, cost of 1 Vitae a month to the old Lady.

                                  Nosferatu should be someplace very public. The city loves the weird and the terrifying, so they can pass with a little effort and I love the subversion of the hidden hideous. Let's not put them in a cemetery, its too easy. Bars are too much of a cliche. The Old Ursuline Convent mixes insult and humor, the Haunts haunting an old Convent. Maybe it has always been theirs, maybe its only been owned for a hundred years, but the blasphemy is too perfect to pass up.

                                                          Okay guys, thanks for your advise. I appreciate it

                                                          Now, here's the thing. The chronicle itself will focus largely on the covenant politics and rivalries in the city, and I want to have the clans having something tangible for the players to relate to. Elysium just seems like a good way to do this, without taking too much focus from the covenant politics. And it could be used to add to the political drama; perhaps the Ventrue Elysium sometimes has a meeting for the clan where they discuss schemes and machinations that could help the clan rise within the covenants, thus giving the clan a greater foothold in the city and yet not depriving the Ventrue of different covenants their personal political lifestyles (That idea is purely brainstorm though).
                                                          Now not all clans need to have their own agenda akin to the example above, and indeed not all clans need to sponsor an Elysium. I just like the idea of introducing clan-related subplots through Elysium, and I think an Elysium having the feel of a certain clan (although it would seem that the Gangrel Elysium described above may not be a great fit for that ) would add to the drama of the place, instead of it "merely" being yet another Elysium.

                                                          It should be noted that this is my first time running a VtR chronicle, and my players first time experiencing this world. Although we each have oWoD backgrounds, having played both DtF and VtM. And I suspect that my players would like having an "easy" way to deal with their clans while experiencing and getting to know their covenants, which will take the front seat of the chronicle.

                                                          What I was going for with the Gangrel Elysium was an "unleashing the Beast" like quality while asserting some kind of dominance/Alpha-malle presence among the kindred. And my thought was that this kind of structured physical savagery could do that, while also serving as a political use for the competative beast - should the players find themselves in an hour of deperation and need a favor from an elder whilst not having enough leverage to actually negotiate one (but there having the possibility to win the favor).
