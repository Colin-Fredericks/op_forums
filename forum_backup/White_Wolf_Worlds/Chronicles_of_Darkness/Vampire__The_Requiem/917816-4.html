        Read a nice story that dealt, briefly, with some of it called the Night Cyclist. But really it's something most books about vampires get into. I don't believe you lose your soul in in-game terms, and in real life that's not a problem, but you are for sure losing some of your Humanity, and you're going to spend most of your time around other beings that are somewhere between Humanity and Beast and never really at either extreme.

        They're all going to be dealing with it differently, and people have a hard time with being around people who are different, especially if it flies in the face of your own beliefs. It's hard, doing that social stuff with other vampires, but it's all you really have.

        And of course the blood. I imagine you get over that quick, but that only serves to make clear the difference and distance between you and humans. They're food, now.

                                                            It's a matter of interpretation I think, although yes, it says "soul" in the text. My interpretation is that it is the vampiric essence, the curse that animates the body, that is eaten. I guess you could call it a soul.
