              The Missing Children (Daeva Bloodline): A nomadic bloodline who is constantly traveling around the world in an effort to escape not only the pursuit of mortal authorities, but also the decrees of Princes and Barons who refuse to tolerate their presence in the city. The Missing Children are sired from underage mortals between the ages of 10 and 15 years old, and they were inadvertently founded by a Daeva who belonged to the Lancea Sanctum, his name was Julius Beckman. According to the stories, he posed as a priest and preached to a mortal congregation, while secretly feeding on the blood of the altar boys. Their founder was a pedophile, who found the blood of children to be his preferred meal. One day there was an accident, he went too far and without thinking clearly, he embraced one of the altar boys in an attempt to save his life. The boy's name was Mathew Pensin, and he was the first "official" member of the bloodline, according to its own members.

              Although Julius was ultimately hunted down and destroyed when his activities were discovered by the Prince, Mathew survived and went into a decade long torpor, hoping to avoid pursuit. When he emerged again, he was lonely, and his mind was still much like that of a child. He longed for friends, and so he embraced some more children, telling them they could be friends forever. He targeted runaways, the victims of abusive households, and occasionally those who never wanted to grow up.


              Because the bloodline travels around so frequently, it is hard to gauge just how many of them exist in the world. Not all of them choose to stay with the group, and splinter off to form their own clubs across the US. However, Mathew is still around and is usually seen traveling with a company of 8 Missing Children, so at the very least there are 9 of them in the world. They operate much like a special club that would be expected of children. They all share the same Haven (referred to them as the Club House), and they have a strict No Entry policy for anyone they feel does not meet the club house requirements.


              As with their parent clan, The Missing Children are ruled by their vices, but this is more likely due to the fact that as children, they already lacked self control prior to the Embrace. However, their additional weakness is that because they are trapped in the bodies of children, a great deal of the city remains off limits to them, and they have to constantly be on alert for signs of mortals wishing to bring them back to their families (especially the cops).Night clubs and other adult themed Racks bar entrance to a group of children looking to get in. They can never own a business for obvious reasons, nor can they work in one due to Child Labor Laws, and they cause far too much problems to be trusted with any kind of authority in the various Covenants. As a result, it is impossiblefor The Missing Children to acquire the Status, Herd, or Covenant Merits.


              In addition to the powers of Majesty, Vigor and Celerity afforded to them by their parent clan, The Missing Children have also adopted the power of Auspex, which they use to keep a constant watch on their surroundings, so as to not be caught off guard.

                          I created this bloodline for a game set in Japan that I never got a chance to run. I have updated to B&S format.

                          Background: It is said this bloodline originated in the 17th century during the Tokugawa shogunate. The founder of the line was a Nosferatu who had been a respected actor in his living days. Having grown bored with the traditional Noh style, he quickly become enamored the new (at the time) style of theatre known as Kabuki. Adopting a wild, flamboyant persona, he gathered a following of like-minded kindred, and mortal followers; embracing those with the most potential. Calling themselves “Mikaboshi”, after the mythological spirit of dark passions and wickedness, they were dedicated to pushing boundaries. At first their pursuits were purely artistic and theatrical, but over time, this extended into political and social causes. Despite the popularity of their shows, their insistence on undermining the values of the notoriously traditional Japanese courts earned them a number of enemies.
                          This came to a head in 1844 in Edo (now known as Tokyo). The shoganate at the time disliked the lawlessness and mixing of classes common to Kabuki performances. This, coupled with an epidemic of fires sweeping through the city, allowed rival to put the theater used by the Mikaboshi to the torch. Few of the Mikaboshi survived the fire. Among the lost was the founder of the line himself. Since that time, remaining members of the bloodline have retained close ties to the theater (as well as the criminal elements associated with it).

                          The Becoming: Traditionally, the Mikaboshi were chosen from artists and entertainers, but anyone who displayed an irreverent, adventurous, or flamboyant personality were often inducted into the bloodline as well. While many modern Mikaboshi consider themselves artist of one form or another, just as many are self-styled intellectuals, students of the macabre, would be revolutionaries, or text-book case sociopaths. The Mikaboshi are also known for their ties to crime, and a fair number of Mikaboshi are recruited from such backgrounds as well.

                          In the Danse Macabre: The Mikaboshi bloodline retains a strong tradition of performance. They are often sought out as entertainers for events both within and outside of Vampire society. However, the bloodline seems to have a knack with working with the seedier elements of society reaching back to its inception. This allows them to use their reputation as mere showmen to cover for other activities common to the bloodline, such as smuggling, spying, sabotage, propaganda, and occasionally murder.
                          They tend to find themselves in revolutionary minded – covenants (such as the Carthians), but are just as easily drawn to the transgressive occultism of the Circle of the Crone or Ordo Dracul.
                          Some few find themselves within more tradition minded covenants, but even there they often find themselves among the least traditional members.

                          Nickname: August Stars (respectful), Freaks (disrespectful)
                          Clan: Nosferatu
                          Bloodline Bane: It was said the founder of the Mikaboshi (despite being regarded as a handsome man) despised how he looked; and often used his exaggerated showmanship to compensate. This quirk has filtered into the blood of his descendants. Mikaboshi cannot abide their reflections. While fleeting glances and incidental reflective surfaces may irritate them, being forced to look fully upon their reflection in a mirror compels them to flee the image or smash the offending surface to pieces. When confronted with their reflection, the Mikaboshi must make a Resolve + Composure roll or suffers the Frightened Condition for the remainder of the scene. This effect can be mitigated if the Mikaboshi’s features are obscured or disguised (such as by wearing a mask or heavy makeup).
                          Bloodline Disciplines: Majesty, Nightmare, Obfuscate, Vigor

                          Mikaboshi Devotions
                          The Mikaboshi are known for their talent at combining Majesty and Nightmare to warp the emotions and perceptions of others, mastering the tension between attraction and repulsion.
                          Even if they do not possess dots in the requisite Disciplines, upon joining the Bloodline, Mikaboshi lean the Cross-Contamination Devotion (locally referred to as Akuma no Miryoku).

                                  Name: Melvins



                                  Quote: You won't lay a hand on me EVER again!



                                  Bloodline Information: Sometimes the embrace into the Nosferatu is actually a step up from one's mortal life, and for the young Patrick Melvin, it was the greatest gift he could ever ask for. At least from his perspective, others might disagree with that assessment. Patrick Melvin was a 90 lbs weakling, a geek who was easily picked on and abused by others. Even at the age of 18, he still did not show any signs of a testosterone induced growth spurt or masculine aggression. Not a day went by that he wasn't stuffed into a locker, mugged for his lunch money, pushed into a mud puddle, or any other manner of degrading and humiliating incidents. He probably would have remained a scrawny weakling for his whole life, had his last bullying not gone too far.

                                  One night, Patrick finally worked up the nerve to fight back, and dropped a lit match into the gas can of the the new car one of his bullies had received for their birthday, blowing the vehicle into scrap in the process. He was promptly chased down the streets and into an alleyway where his pursuer beat him to within an inch of his life. He probably would have killed the poor boy, had it not been for the intervention of his soon to be sire who tore the bully apart like tissue paper. As he stood over Patrick's bleeding and half conscious form, the Nosferatu chuckled and promised that his suffering was over. He had been watching him for some time, and now that Patrick finally had the will to stand up for himself, he deserved a reward. That night, Patrick was embraced into the clan.

                                  After the initial shock of becoming a vampire had run its course, Melvin's sire was surprised to find just how thankful the boy was to join the clan. Even Nosferatu who came to accept their condition never expressed such mirth about the embrace. Melvin couldn't be happier, and he especially took to Vigor and Nightmare as his disciplines of choice, almost completely forsaking Obfuscate when his sire tried to instruct him in its use. As time went on, Melvin became stronger than all of his bullies combined, and was able to send the biggest, strongest jocks screaming in terror with a mere glance. It was every nerd's fantasy, after a life time of being abused and humiliated, he was powerful and he was feared.

                                  Melvin developed a reputation among his clan for being a more hands on approach to problem solving. While others of the clan chose to hide in the shadows and pull strings, Melvin followed the path of the warrior, going head to head against his enemies. Occasionally he got sloppy and left behind some evidence, but under the guidance of his sire, he learned how to cover his tracks.


                                  Eventually Melvin began to consider passing on his "gift" to others. He was by no means the only youth in the world who suffered under such humiliation. And why should he be the only one granted freedom from such torment? When Patrick Melvin learned all he could from his sire, he left in search of his own progeny. This began the start of the Melvin bloodline, Nosferatu liberators who sought to grant power and respect to those who were abused in life.



                                  Parent Clan: Nosferatu



                                  Nickname: Avengers (among outsiders), The Empowered (among each other)



                                  Covenant: Ironically for a bloodline who's all about standing up against oppression, there are quite a few Melvins who join the ranks of the Invictus, climbing up the ranks of the corporate ladder in order to make a name for themselves. It's almost as if they are making a statement against those who abused them by rising to the heights of social and political power. Others seek to join the Carthian movement where they can fight for equal rights alongside others who share their views.

                                  Some members of the bloodline turned to the occult including voodoo, witchcraft, and other pagan arts when they were mortal in order to provide some comfort to their tormented lives. Upon joining the ranks of the undead, they flocked to the Circle of the Crone in hopes of learning how to create real curses. The covenant is also popular among those who were simply fascinated with the supernatural, including comic book geeks, horror genre fans, and novice writers.

                                  The Ordo Dracul has attracted more of the intellectual minded Melvins who are fascinated by the idea of studying the teachings of Dracula himself, learning how to become a more effective avenger without devolving into a ravening beast.

                                  The Lancea Sanctum probably has the least attraction to the bloodline, mostly because it doesn't seem to have anything that resonates with their beliefs. Occasionally there will be a Melvin who gets it into their head that they have been chosen by God to be an divine crusader against the corrupted regimes, but for the most part, the young Melvins are too attracted to the power that the other covenants can offer them to worry about the concept of a divine monster.



                                  Appearance: The standard Melvin looks like a stereotypical geek, albeit with slightly exaggerated features. Excessive acne, buck teeth, large ears, thick rimmed glasses, obnoxious laughter and an almost complete lack of muscle definition. However, this is not their only appearance as others have been known to display permanent bruises or scars across their body. Signs that they have been physically beaten or tortured during their lives. Melvins who suffer from mental impairment have also been said to manifest hideous deformities resembling figures such as Leatherface or Jason Vorheese.

                                  However they might appear, it is always suggested that they are easy prey who would not put up much of a struggle, only for their attackers to find out too late that this is not the case.



                                  Haven: The haven of a Melvin is typically one of comfort and security. They have a tendency to decorate their lairs with memorabilia of what they are interested in when they were alive, as well as after death. Their favorite bands, novels and TV series, model airplanes, and other such hobbies clutter their living area, with the collection steadily growing over the course of their unnatural lifespans. Some of them keep a large collection of pets including insects, spiders, and reptiles. Should anyone attempt to invade their Haven, a quick flare of Animalism can turn their collection into a private army of deadly animals ready to pounce on the invader. Melvins typically choose to live underground including basements and cellars where protection from the sun is an easy modification to make. Anything upstairs is typically reserved for their Ghouls if they have any, in order to keep up appearances from prying eyes.



                                  Background: The Melvins choose to sire people who were abused during their lives and include the ranks of geeks, the homeless, the mentally challenged, and even children in some cases. However before they will embrace them, the mortal has to show that they have the will to stand up for themselves. They have to fight back against the ones who abused them. Once they have proven that they are sick of being pushed around, the bloodline will grant them the power to strike back. Sadly, many who are abused for so long can not muster up the courage to fight, and so the embrace is offered to only a rare few.

                                  Melvins are typically warriors, choosing to tackle their problems head on. They are sick of keeping their heads down and hoping their enemies will get bored and move on. It's time to start fighting back, and to this end, they specialize in physical talents. If they didn't know how to fight before their embrace (and almost none of them did), they soon begin to learn. Either picking up a weapon, or going in with their bare hands. However, many of them used to be nerds in life and so they also have a focus on Mental talents, combining brains with brawn to become more efficient. Social talents are often the last thing on their mind, as they didn't have much luck in that department when they were alive, and have even less luck with it after becoming Nosferatu.



                                  Bloodline Disciplines: Nightmare, Vigor, Animalism, Resilience

                                  The Abused have chosen their specialties based on both before and after their embrace. Sick of having been pushed around by those with a stronger physique, they have studied the powers of strength and fortitude that comes from Vigor and Resilience, giving them Olympic levels of strength and endurance. Many of them found that they associated better with animals than they did people, which has lead to them having a gifted talent for Animalism. Finally, Nightmare was a natural calling for two possible reasons. Many of them were already creepy and disturbing to their mortal peers, driving others away in revulsion. Others crave the power and respect that they felt their bullies commanded, and would love to see others flinch in their presence instead of the other way around.



                                  Weakness: Melvins have a predisposition to fight against what they view as cruel authority and dominance. Such feelings are the driving force behind their Requiem and it is what motivates them to keep rising each night. When a member of this bloodline sees the weak and underprivileged being taken advantage of by a stronger and more successful character, they must use an act of Willpower in order to avoid rushing to their aid immediately. If they fail to do so, the Melvin will take it upon themselves to fight back against the bully figure until they have been driven from the area or disposed of, whichever comes first. Depending upon outcome of the situation, this can endanger the Masquerade!



                                  Organization: The Melvins are a close knit community who love to socialize with their own kind. For the fledglings, these meetings are healing groups and therapy sessions designed to help them overcome the trauma they suffered in their lives. For the older members, it's the perfect place to share stories and experiences. Particularly the ways in which they got revenge on some self entitled pricks who decided to push others around. The Abused whish to make it very clear that they are through being pushed around for the amusement of others. Those who mess with a Melvin unprovoked and manage to walk away the victor may soon find the rest of the local bloodline descending upon them in search of avenging their injured (or slain) comrade.

                                  Despite their desire to avenge the weak, the Melvins know that there is a line they can not cross. If a Melvin makes the first move and it backfires against them, the bloodline can not rush to their aid without drawing the attention of the offended party who could mistake the act to be a declaration of war. As such, the bloodline warns their members that if they decide to go stomping around the enemy's territory uninvited, they do so at their own risk. They will only get involved if that enemy decides to target them without reason, such as base prejudice.


                                  Many of the bloodline are embraced at a young age, typically the mid to late teens who are at their most unstable from a life of bullying. In order to throw people off their trail, it is not uncommon for the Melvins to own at least one Ghoul who takes on the disguise of being their parental figure. Supplying them with shelter, clothing, and education when dealing with the public. When in private, their Ghouls are used for more sinister purposes such as procuring food, acting as security, and establishing contacts.



                                  Concepts: Dark Avenger, Masked Vigilante, Nerd Rage Berserker, Kindred Freedom Fighter, Reclusive Animal Lover.
