    Name: Cainites



    Quote: As Cain murdered his brother, so too do we sustain ourselves on the blood of our kin.



    Bloodline Information: As the members of this bloodline will tell you, they are descended from Cain, the biblical brother killer. Whether or not this is merely Ventrue boasting, or if they truly have some relation to the infamous murderer has been lost to the ages. The Cainites are called Kin Slayers and with good reason, for they sustain themselves on the blood of their own family tree. Brothers, sisters, parents, offspring, cousins, and other distant relatives are the means for which they feed.

    As an ancient bloodline of Ventrue, the Cainites had no shortage of subjects to embrace. The noble families and even royalty had been riddled with jealous relatives seeking to claim the inheritance for themselves. If they felt they weren't getting their fair share, it was easy enough to orchestrate the demise of their family rivals. Of course most of these murderers hired somebody else to do the job, while only the most spiteful would stain their hands with blood. These extremists were the ones the Cainites targeted.

    As the ages have progressed and civilization had changed, it is becoming harder to find high standing individuals who are willing to murder their kin, and so the Cainites have had to branch out their recruitment process to middle class families as well, though they still retain enough pride to shun the low class mongrels. If they started to embrace lowlifes for murdering their family, the bloodline would outnumber all other clans, or so they claim.


    Members of the Cainite bloodline often spend their unlives constructing genealogies of their family tree, following its progression throughout the ages while keeping a list of vessels they can feed upon. Because of their special diet, members of this bloodline never stray too far from their family, which is potentially dangerous if their family recognizes them.



    Parent Clan: Ventrue



    Nickname: Kin Slayers



    Covenant: Unsurprisingly, Cainites are popular among the Lancea Sanctum, seeing as how they both claim to be of biblical origins. Those who become Sanctified are especially fond of terrorizing kin slayers, sometimes even choosing to embrace them as punishment, reminding them forever of their sin. Some of the more mystically drawn Cainites join the Circle of the Crone, performing dark rituals that require one to make a sacrifice of their own flesh and blood, more accurately translated to a family relative. Those who join the Invictus are downright ruthless in their pursuit of wealth and power, it is not uncommon for them to have slain a close relative in order to become next in line for inheriting the family business. The Carthian Movement and the Ordo Dracul do not hold much appeal to the Kin Slayers, however.



    Appearance: The Cainites dress to impress whenever possible. Much like Cain strove to earn God's favor over his brother, they are unsatisfied unless they appear to be the most successful member of their family. They own the most land, they wear the finest garments, have the most expensive cars, display the fanciest hair cuts, and so on. Often at times, this obsession with being the best is subconscious in nature. They may not even be aware they're trying to outshine the rest of their family until somebody brings it to their attention. They often display a subtle air of dominance and authority, as if they refuse to allow a relative to get the better of them.



    Haven: Cainites prefer to make their haven in a place that is located near their relatives, but not so close as to draw unwanted attention. By calling upon Celerity, they can quickly travel between their Haven and their family residence to feed before returning back to safety before anyone notices their presence. Early Cainites may choose a nearby suite that overlooks their former household, but as the ages pass and their immediate family members start to die off, they are gradually able to upgrade into more elaborate dwellings as their descendants have more trouble recognizes them than their ancestors would.

    Most havens can also be expected to hold a library where they keep records of their family tree. Copies of birth certificates, old photo albums, genealogies, family crests, birth mark records, and other things used to track the progress of lineage. The more ancient Cainites often have rows upon rows of books stacked upon the shelf, tracing centuries worth of lineage.



    Background: All Cainites have one thing in common, they all murdered a family member. It doesn't matter who, as long as they were related by blood. Those who join this bloodline either voluntarily or being forced into it undergo a rite of passage whether they know it or not. Before the Cainite is inducted into the bloodline, they have to kill a member of their own family. Because of this extreme requirement, many within the bloodline once served as Ghouls until their sires judged them worthy of taking the final plunge. Others are simply murderers who the bloodline came across in their travels.

    As murderers, physical skills are more prominent for this bloodline than other Lords, as are Intelligence needed to cover up their misdeeds. Social skills would have been important for most killers, but as their victims are family members, they usually already have the trust necessary to get close to their prey.



    Bloodline Disciplines: Dominate, Animalism, Resilience, Celerity



    Weakness: The Cainites are cursed with a unique appetite, they can only feed on blood relatives. It doesn't matter who the relative is, as long as they are descended from the family tree. Additionally, they can only embrace those who have slain a member of their own family.



    Organization: Cainites use one another like an investigative network, using their contacts and connections to trace lineages. If two Cainite families marry one another, this can allow them to share vessels and make feeding easier to accomplish. Some Cainites actively try to orchestrate such a union, sealing their alliances with blood ties, instead of handshakes and contracts. Others fear that such a union would provide further competition and strive to break up the marriage rather than encourage it.



    Concepts: Least favorite child, Second heir to the throne, Got cheated during the will reading, Abusive parent who lost control, Abused child pushed too far, Values family reputation and status over love and tolerance.

                        Name: Gourmands



                        Quote: No, I'm sorry, you still have to feed on blood. But I can make your next meal taste like your last one!



                        Bloodline Information: This bloodline was founded in the early 1900's by a Mr. Eric Barnes. In his life, Eric was a man who loved his food, and was always trying out new recipes. He even went so far as to purchase a number of unbranded restaurants to draw more inspiration from. Realizing that a kindred who had control over the mortal's food supply was a big asset, the Ventrue embraced him, only to find that undeath had done nothing to curve his appetite. Eric loved food with a passion, and the thought of being forced to subsist exclusively on the same meal for the rest of eternity was maddening for him. And so he devoted the early nights of his life to overcoming this weakness. He worked feverishly on brand new recipes, mixing blood into the sauces and drinks for his meals, dying the spices and powders with his vitae. The results varied, and he usually ended up vomiting the meals back up, but as he refined his talent, his tolerance for mortal foods began to increase.

                        Eventually, the dedicated chef unlocked the secret to consuming mortal foods once again, although they had to be mixed with blood before his undead body would be able to stomach them. It may have been disturbing to others, but as long as the young lord was able to enjoy his feast, that's all he cared about.

                        Eventually other Ventrue caught wind of Eric's new powers and they sought him out to learn the art. All liquid diets were for the homeless and paupers who couldn't afford real cuisine, and as the lords of Kindred domain, it would not do to be seen feeding on the same slop as their lessers. Those who learned the art became known as the Gourmands, and thus the bloodline was born!

                        Over the last century, the Gourmands have become famous among Kindred society. They hire out their services to vampires who can afford them, preparing grand banquets and feasts that give the diners a reminder of what it was like to be human for the night. But as members of the Ventrue, they would be insulted to prepare "junk" food and microwave burritos, and so they demand steep prices for the meals they prepare. Only the higher ranks of kindred society can afford them, which makes dining on their cuisine a much envied luxury by the peasants of kindred society.

                        It should be known that the Gourmands have not completely shirked off their vampiric nature. They have not evolved beyond consuming the blood of others, they have simply found a new way in which to do so. A new way which brings them a veritable fortune and great esteem among the kindred population.


                        Parent Clan: Ventrue



                        Nickname: Master Chefs



                        Covenant: The Gourmands are welcome everywhere with the exception of the Lancea Sanctum. The Sanctified beliefs that they exist to prey upon and punish mortals for their lack of faith does not blend well the Gourmand's adherence to finding alternate food supplies. On the other hand, the Invictus are practically throwing cash at them for the chance to prepare grand banquets and elaborate feasts at their exclusive parties and clubs. The Ordo Dracul also favor them, for it was through hard work and dedication that Eric discovered alternative ways to feed, and hope to learn from their teachings in the process.

                        The Carthians also like to hold parties and get-togethers, hoping that the food they prepare will encourage others to attend their meetings, and maybe shmooze up to the power mongers, convincing them to me more lenient and forgiving. As for their relation to the Circle of the Crone... well infusing blood into food before you consume it is probably right on their list of socially acceptable policies.



                        Appearance: A lot of Gourmands are overweight in physique, a draw back to dedicating one's life to indulging their appetite. Not all of them are fat, however, such as those who maintained regular exercise to burn off the weight they gained. It is also a common stereotype that they are of French descent, given their nature. Again, this is not always the case, but it is common enough that many come to expect it of them. In all cases though, the Gourmands are exceptionally clean and refined. They always make sure their hair is tucked out of the way and their hands are thoroughly washed, to prevent contaminating their food, and they behave in a manner that suggests they are above the common rabble that are their peers.



                        Haven: Gourmands always make their havens near places where they have easy access to all the best ingredients available. Perhaps they live down by the docks, importing fruits, vegetables, and meat that can not be obtained on the continent. Perhaps they own farm land, using a Ghoul work force to farm the crops, and raise the animals until they're ready to be slaughtered. Maybe they live on top of a 5 star restaurant, savoring the delicious aromas that drift through the halls as the chefs prepare the exotic meals and fine cuisine for the mortal customers. Wherever they happen to live, it is typically devoted to the culinary arts.



                        Background: During their mortal years, most Gourmands were drawn to the culinary arts. Not all of them were chefs, though many of them were. Others worked as food critics, wine tasters, waiters, and restaurant managers. After their embrace, they realize the pleasure that has been denied to them, and quickly seek to remedy the situation. Their sires quickly begin to instruct them in all the cooking experience they possess, but also encourages them to discover new secrets on their own. It is imperative for members of this bloodline to have the Crafts skill, with food being their specialty. If they don't know how to cook, they're quickly going to find life as a vampire to be exceptionally hard. Mental and Social skills are their primary choice, rarely falling onto Physical skills. Why would they need them, anyways? Who in their right mind would try to slay this bloodline and destroy what they have to offer?



                        Bloodline Disciplines: Dominate, Animalism, Resilience, Sanguine Cuisine


                        Sanguine Cuisine: is the unique Discipline invented by the Gourmands. Although it has practically no use in combat, it's applications for social interaction among the kindred community is tremendous. Sanguine Cuisine is the ability to experience the pleasures of mortal food, while still adhering to the vampire feeding habits. In order to prepare the food, it must be infused with blood either from the kindred using it, or from a donation. Traditionally, the blood is infused into the food while it is raw, and then cooked to perfection before eating it. The amount of blood put into the food is the amount of blood it contains, so the cost for using Sanguine Cuisine varies by individual meal!


                        Level 1 - Mystery Flavor:

                        Cost: 0
                        Dice Pool: Perception
                        Action: Instant
                        Duration: Permanent

                        The first level of Sanguine Cuisine allows the vampire to alter the taste of blood. Since they can only change the flavor to ones that they have experienced, the initial use of this power is restricted to foods they ate when they were mortal. As their mastery increases, however, they can acquire new flavors. Mystery Flavor wears off at sunrise, however, so the blood must be consumed within the same night to enjoy its benefits.


                        Level 2 - Red Wine:

                        Cost: Varies
                        Dice Pool: Crafts
                        Action: Instant
                        Duration: until Sunrise

                        The first step to dining like a mortal, the vampire can infuse blood with other liquids. If the blood used is kindred vitae, drinking the beverage has the same effect as drinking said vitae, even being able to create Ghouls or form Blood Bonds! The effects of Red Wine fade at sunrise, where the drink shall return to its normal state.



                        Level 3 - Your Last Meal:

                        Cost: Varies
                        Dice Pool: Crafts + Intelligence
                        Action: Instant
                        Duration: until Sunrise

                        The second step to dining like a mortal. Your Last Meal allows the kindred to infuse blood with solid foods. As with Red Wine, if the blood used is vitae, consuming it has the same effect as drinking the vitae itself, and the effects fade away at sunrise. If the blood used in the recipe is kindred vitae, it's natural powers are enhanced beyond the norm. Ghoulification and Blood Bonds have double their usual length. Ghouls only have to consume the vitae once every 2 months, and blood bonds only need to be renewed once every 2 years. Ghouls are also able to learn physical disciplines twice as easily (half experience cost), as if the food is providing them with a rush of vitality.


                        Level 4 - Spice of Life:

                        Cost: Varies
                        Dice Pool: Crafts + Intelligence
                        Action: Instant
                        Duration: until Sunrise

                        At this stage of mastery, the vampire is now capable of infusing blood into sauces, spices, and seasonings. By combining this power with level 2 or 3, the vampire can provide some additional flavor to their meals! The effects of Spice of Life fade at sunrise, where the ingredients return to their normal state. If kindred vitae is used for the ingredients, the aroma becomes mouth watering to those with a sense of smell. Those subjected to such a powerful aroma temporarily change their vice to Gluttony, and must use an act of willpower to resist the urge to consume the food that has been seasoned.



                        Level 5 - Food Storage:

                        Cost: 1 Extra Vitae
                        Dice Pool: Intelligence + Science
                        Action: Reflexive
                        Duration: Weeks = to Successes

                        Expending blood to prepare a meal every night grows exhausting rather quickly. With the final level of Sanguine Cuisine, the vampire is able to preserve the blood stored inside their drinks, foods, and ingredients so that they can be stored away for later use. Food Storage is able to preserve the vitae inside for a number of weeks equal to the successes rolled during the infusion process.







                        Weakness: Their dedication to the culinary arts has cursed the Gourmands with a very sensitive appetite. They are revolted by the idea of feeding on regular blood, which to them is on par with consuming raw meat. A member of this bloodline must make a successful Composure roll in order to feed in the normal method, or they will begin to vomit the blood up, retching in disgust.

                        They gain a bonus of +2 on the roll to feed on blood which is under the effects of Mystery Flavor.



                        Organization: The Gourmands are a close knit community, for they share recipes and ingredients they have collected over their extensive life times. Some Gourmands choose to live alone, traveling the world in search of new flavors and meals to practice. Others choose to live together, operating under the disguise of an exclusive restaurant who's entire cooking staff are actually Gourmands, and their customers are all vampires.

                        They are also one of the few bloodlines who publicly shares their signature discipline with the rest of the community. They take pride in their accomplishment, and will sell their meals to those who can meet their prices, which are often exorbitant in order to prevent the fast food junkies from disgracing their reputation. It's obvious from this display that they have the ability to create such foods, but they consider it beneath their station to do so.



                        Concepts: Strict Food Critic, Former Iron Chef Champion, Overly Snooty Sue-chef, Cultured Cannibal, Late Night Cooking Show Host.

                                        Yes, Blush of Life allows you to eat food, but as I understand it, you end up puking it back up after the scene and you don't get any real sustenance out of it. Otherwise, you could just keep Blush of Life up all the time and never feed on humans again. As for Web, Ralab, or Blue Alchemie, I don't even know what those are, since they're not mentioned in my book.


                                        Sanguine Cuisine is a vanity power, not unlike Majesty. It's something to wave over your fellow kindred and say "Look how much better we are than you!" And considering it's a Ventrue bloodline, I felt like it was perfectly fitting for them. They call themselves lords, the social elites of both mortal and kindred society. Why should they be lowered to the same dietary habits of the lesser clans?

                                        As for turning blood into gold, I don't believe that kindred would be particularly interested in transforming their only available food source into worthless (to them) metal. When it comes to social groups, once again I draw attention to how many vampires would be willing to pay to sample the benefits of this power. All it takes is a well cooked buffet for the city's Prince and Primogen to escalate them up the ranks of society, especially if they're the only ones in the city capable of using this discipline. Conversely, they publicly denounce a fellow kindred from dining on their creations, and they would likely suffer a drop in popularity while their peers are dining at the fanciest kindred owned restaurants in town.



                                        To sum it all up, Sanguine Cuisine is a discipline that is more focused on role playing, than working through game mechanics. It's not so much what you can do with the power, but how creative you are in exploiting it. Which I feel is a lot more fun, and puts emphasis on thinking how the characters would react in this situation, instead of just some new power to throw around.
