                            People seem to forget that there are two forms of Obfuscate.

                            One is mental invisibility. If cameras work on a form of Obfuscate, it works on this level. There's no indication that indirect methods like flour do work on this level. You have to cause a ruckus to be seen.

                            The other is literal invisibility. You are invisible, and it isn't all in everyone's head. Indirect methods like the flour work on this form. It isn't broken by causing a ruckus, but people can perceive you if you stab them. Or if you're kicking around flour, I guess.

                                All forms of Obfuscate are mental invisibility. One works by making observers percieve you as nobody important. The other works by making observers completely ignore sensory information about you. But no form of obfuscate works by bending light. You're never "actually invisible", you just may as well be for all intents and purposes because people who percieve you ignore what they percieve about you.

                                As for cameras, I personally rule that both forms work on people viewing you through cameras. With Face in the Crowd, people viewing the camera feed see "some guy" on the feed. Depending on where you are and what you do while on camera, "some guy" being in a place no one is supposed to be or doing something illegal may still raise alarms. With Cloak of Night, you're there, the camera records you, anyone watching the feed sees you, but they ignore that sensory information. They "missed that detail".
