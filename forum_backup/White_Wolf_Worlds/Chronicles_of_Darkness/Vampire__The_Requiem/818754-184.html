    Remember that Dominate 2 requires sustained eye contact, and at least three turns plus one Vitae per use—plenty of time for the other agents to react. Awe doesn't stop people from being hostile toward you, it just removes penalties from social rolls, so it won't help you Social Maneuver against someone whose impression is already Hostile. Confidant similarly has to be used on each person one by one, leaving time for the others to react, and it resolves if the target makes a significant sacrifice for the vampire—at which point the target, according to the book, feels tricked or ripped off. And because of Awe, they most definitely remember the vampire's face, and can radio in an APB.

    Dominate and Majesty are powerful, but they're not magic bullets.

        Originally posted by Marcus View Post
        It's sooo difficult to create a threat to even just one or two kindred with dominate 2 or Majesty.
        I.e. a driver of one of their was dominated 3 to crash against a police patrol as soon as they would have met one.
        It resulted in both of them spamming dominate and Majesty, not even on in a smart way, letting every agent (6) powerless, just spamming it every turn.
        Any suggestion?
        For Dominate: Have them encounter more than one person at a time. It generally takes two instant actions to Dominate someone into not attacking you (one to inflict Mesmerized, one to issue the command) which is great when it's a one-on-one fight but extremely costly if you face multiple opponents. In a two-on-one fight it means you've taken 3-4 attacks before you've decommissioned the first of them.
        Also remember that Mesmerized resolves on any damage taken and if you take a breaking point as a part of the command. A breaking point for a mortal could be to stand idly by while their friend gets punched by a monster. In fact, experiencing anything overtly supernatural (being dominated is implicitly excepted) like the Gangrel coterie mate using Protean or anyone using inhuman amounts of strength is a breaking point for almost all mortals. Once Mesmerized is resolved they gain +3 to contest further Mesmerize attempts that scene meaning it's extremely difficult to spam it on the same person.

        For Majesty: Start the fight before any Majesty Conditions have been applied. Awe by itself is not great when the threat of violence is already present. It definitely helps the vampire to talk themselves out of a tight spot, but only if that options was already present. Just activating Awe doesn't create new opportunities. Charmed does present new options, but when already in a conflict they're limited, and in a violent conflict almost nonexistent (provided you've already applied Charmed as applying it requires a social interaction that I would disallow in a violent conflict). In your example a Charmed police officer might be persuaded to look the other way (and thus resolve Charmed one that's done) but that's something that still requires social manoeuvering. If there's six officers on the scene it'll be much more difficult as you need to persuade the whole group. It also takes a bunch of time to set it up as you need one instant action for Awe and then one instant action per officer to be Charmed. With Green Eyes you can basically skip the social manoeuvering, but that instead costs one Vitae per target (provided whatever you want them to do isn't a breaking point or they risk taking damage).

        Basically, ramp up how fast the conflict escalates to a fight. Dominate takes a bunch of time to apply and is sometimes only temporary (if they take a breaking point) while Majesty can basically not end a fight at all once it's broken out. Also, increase the number of opponents. It takes time per opponent before Dominate and Majesty (except Awe) has any effect.

                    Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                    Why you say 3 turns? It just need 1/2 turns.
                    It takes three turns to actually use Iron Edict (which is what Draconis specifically talked about). One turn to apply Mesmerized, a second turn to activate Iron Edict, and at least a third turn to issue the command. The Exceptional Success bonus for Mesmerize only applies on the same turn which is before you've activated Iron Edict. If you do get an Exceptional on Mesmerize you could still issue a basic command without spending an extra turn.
                    Also, Iron Edict must be reactivated for additional commands unless you bake everything into a single command that takes many turns to issue.


                    Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                    Thanks Tessie, they are good advices but the point was a bit different since the agents wouldn't open fire so easily.
                    Imagine the scene: The car crash against their, they would be surely on guard but there could have been several reason for the crash: Sickness, losing the control, drugged driver and so on.
                    I got it. My advice was for future encounters. Dominate and Majesty has limits so if you don't want them to be an auto-solve button you'll have to have these limits in mind when designing encounters.

                    Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                    I was dreaming about corruption maybe, or a funny chases, but nothing has happened, just a boring Dominate/Majesty spam
                    You could've had the driver cause additional complications. From their perspective they've just blacked out a little between the moment they saw the cop car and they crashed into said car. They should be confused and can act in ways that appear irrational or influenced by the police, if they don't outright panic and try to drive away. Whatever the driver does the PCs wouldn't have time to influence them with Disciplines, and if the driver drives away with the PCs, they become accomplishes in the police's eyes, causing complications for both Dominate (as it's difficult to get eye contact with someone who's about to cuff you) and Majesty (that just isn't particularly effective in this situation).

                    And remember to also include social manoeuvering when the PCs use Majesty. Charmed would only make it easier to persuade the officers. A high social character can often do the same things that a character with Majesty 1+2 can do. Would you have been as disappointed if it was a high social character who defused the situation through mundane means?

                                                    Michael - Credit to Vasilios Baggeas

                                                    Bloods Groomed Scion
                                                    Prerequisite: The Vast Family
                                                    Procedure: First the Dragon must ensure that the subject is suffering from either the Enslaved, Enthralled or similar such persistent Condition although the Dragon need not be the one to inflict said Condition. The subject of this procedure must then be subjected to a third level blood bond to the Dragon with the third taste of Vitae being laced with a socially prepared alchemical mixture. After all of the above criteria have been meet the Dragon may drain the subject of their Blood.
                                                    Outcome: the Dragon spends a point of Willpower and the subject will arise as a newly Embraced Vampire.
