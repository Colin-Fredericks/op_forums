    Baltimore: The Kiddie Pool Saga (Soliciting Ideas)

    08-15-2016, 11:37 AM
    My ST has asked me to write a post asking for input into the game he is running and I am playing. Specifically, he has asked me to solicit ideas for Carthian npc's.

    ST: "Ask them to create a starting Carthian PC and imagine what they would do if they arrived in Baltimore. What would be their in character opinion of the situation? What would be their goal and how would they go about trying to achieve it?"

    So, to anyone who is familiar and interested our AP (Baltimore: the Kiddie Pool Saga) we would very much appreciate it if you would take the time to post your ideas for newly arrived Carthian PC's along with their probably goals/actions and methods for achieving such. If you put your ideas in spoiler tags, I promise not to look.

    Thanks in advance

                      As i see it, news from the bloody Baltimore’s status quo change travel far by night, and amongst those who answer this call of change i’d say there are four broad types of vampires:
                      1. The “innocents”. The ones arrived by seer (bad) luck, or through a situation not of their choosing.
                      2. The “fanatics”. Crusaders for whatever cause, who want to see their truth (whatever it is) ruling this princeless domain.
                      3. The “monsters”. They crave what usually they cannot achieve, be it fast power, be it the heart blood of others, consequences be damned. These are the more driven, as they know that in these changing nights anything is fair game as long as you don’t get caught.
                      So… i’ll try my hand at a
                      monster


                      Holloway – Nosferatu, Carthian.
                      He is young, intense, and very familiar with murder. They say the quiet ones are the ones you have to take care about, and Holloway follows definitely that description. Sweet talking, smiling and sadistic to the core, Buck was clever enough to limit his murders to foreign travels to shady underdeveloped asian countries when he was mortal. His embrace was not so much damnation as the next logical step. But he was not ment to be one of the powerful lords of the night; no. Buck was Embraced in (insert city here) as cannon fodder, alongside some other 6 poor basterds.

                      [My idea was that he would have been created as a Draugr (as they are as expendable as they get) but don’t want to make too much of a special snowflake. Your call.]
                      More precisely, Crone cannon fodder, in his war against the Lancea Sanctum. The guerrilla tactics his sire had in mind would work best with creatures best trained in Obfuscate and Vigor, and so those are the Disciplines in which Holloway excels. But Holloway was intelligent and imaginative, and that is a problem when you’re designed to be expendable.
                      One night, three monts into the war already, Holloway felt his blood bond (who he didn’t even know he had or how it worked) shatter. They were in the middle of a mission, deep into the bowels of the city, and the frenzy was almost inmediate.

                      Only one vampire got out of the sewers.

                      Since then, Buck was on the run. He knew he could not stay in the city anymore, so he got up in a train, and vanished in the night. On that train he met Holloway, a homeless man who would end dead four hours later. About what they talked, Buck will never tell, but when he leapt down the train he had picked not only his blood, but also his name.
                      Holloway has a very clear perception about himself: he knows that he is the second lower level in the night food chain, and he knows that only the ruthless and the witty survive to climb.
                      Eventually he made contacts along the road, and they told them about Baltimore. There was the opportunity he had been waiting for: a city in chaos. Power and position ready to be taken, if he had the courage and intelligence to do it. And so he waited patiently, until one new player shook the city in the right way. That player is of course, Broom.

                      Objectives:

                      1) Risk only for reward. Holloway won’t be expendable again. He is willing to take risks, of course, but only if they will lead to position and boons.

                      2) Power. He would be most interested in being one of the law enforcers of the new Baltimore, as it will give him ample opportunity to exert his violent inclinations.

                      3) Kill the Mekhets. Holloway is adamant about one conviction: the reason Baltimore has not surrendered yet to Broom claims are the Mekhet. Holloway is definitely afraid of the things they’ve told him about the Mekhet in the city, so he knows well enough what effect they have in the others. This invisible menace is what maintains still that strange, somewhat indolent, atmosphere in the City. And so, if Broom takes him, he will constantly advocate for attacks unto them… guerrilla style.

                              Hector Long, Daeva Carthian

                              "Look, man. A lot of it's fucked. A quarantine zone where new fangs are tossed in to prove themselves or get removed from the gene pool. If we did something like this, people would be yelling about Nazis, gestapos, and all the usual jackboot slander.

                              "But hey, if there's one already sitting here giftwrapped...

                              "So here's my pitch. Democracy fails. At least what people think is Democracy nowadays. It's pretty much just an oligarchy, right? 'Representative Democracy' - yeah, yeah. New spin on an elite political class. And that's just on the mortal side. Add us licks and everything spirals out even quicker.

                              "Sortition.

                              "Don't laugh, let me finish. I'm serious. We can't just embrace anarchy, right? There needs to be some system in place for deciding who leads what where. There's gonna be a power vacuum in the zone while all the upper-ups are fighting for the good stuff. That leaves an opening.

                              "We step in, get people together as a group in the zone. We start a discussion on taking care of ourselves. We'll call them different names, but we add our own Sheriff, our own Scourge, our own Harpy. The adults can all laugh at us play House or whatever, but we do it.

                              "And we don't vote. We draw lots. Anyone who wants a job can put their name in that hat. The drawn lot gets the job for the next three months. That's it. I'm sure there's nuance on stuff I'm not thinking of right now - can people throw their name in multiple hats? Once you have a job, can you go for it again next turn? Those are details we can work out, but you get the picture.

                              "It's time for an experiment. And I want to know how close random chance comes to setting up a system just as good as the cronyism, nepotism, and corruption B'more is known for.

                              "My bet is it makes a good run."

                              Objective: Run an Experiment in the Quarantine Zone / Kiddie Pool

                              Tools: A socializer and talker, Hector has a talent for knowing when to use Majesty to put spin on a conversation. Leave you feeling a little better about a plan of action. Draw you into an idea, charge up some passion, then leave you wanting more. He does outreach to other groups, even the Invictus - anyone who wants to be involved - why not? In the end, he just wants the results. And while it would be great if the system ended up a giant middle finger to the powers the be, for him it's a section on his resume. Not many, especially as a neonate, get to lead significant political experiments on this kind of scale, and that makes it a chance not worth passing up.

                                  I've been musing over it a few days, and i have another one. Her name is Carmen Diaz Cortés (with “s”, pretty please ^^). These days, she goes by her surname, “Cortés”, as a way of reminding herself of her mother and where she comes from. "Altivo", her horse ghoul, is another way of doing precisely that. She’s a Carthian Gangrel,
                                  and a fanatic.

                                  Carmen was once a young spanish girl. She was 17 years old, born and raised in Córdoba, Spain. She was smart, considerate, well spoken and fairly beautiful in a healthy way, but too naive for this world. Carmen had a sheltered and comfortable life, as her family has quite a lot of money (they produce olive oil). Carmen absolutely love horses, having ridden them since she was 5 years old, and having just finished high school, took a free sabbatical year (not common at all in Spain, but she could afford it) and travelled to America, in order to visit her best friend, Claudia, which she first knew in one such competition.

                                  The had together one hell of a time, enjoying the world and the New York friends and parties like the privileged kids they were.

                                  But things change, and so one night things went south too fast, surrounded with the wrong people and the wrong substances. The next days (or weeks, for all she knows) were just a blur. When she came fully into her senses, Claudia was nowhere to be found, she was in an unknown rural location, she had grown a par of fangs and was a very literal slave to her terrible new thirst, to her instincts and lasty to her sire, “Windhund”. Carmen had died already, and her naivete followed quickly after: she had been turned into a monster in order to “guard the territory and be a loyal mutt” to the “pack”. The pack was extense, as she would learn over the span of several months and travels, and had a extremely rigid hierarchy, based upon personal strenght, territories, war trophies and dead -and still breathing- liegemen. They boasted as being invictus, and wherever she went he saw chains – in the form of blood bonds, debts and blackmail most often han not, but literal chains too every now and then- and servitude. About her family or friends, she of course never heard again. She didn’t even know where she was, or where she travelled with her sire: the names of the locations were always secret.

                                  She spent two years being abused in every way her sire saw fit, until one night one band of “those unreasonable vermin” –her sire’s words- assaulted their land. They were many, and they were every bit as mad with rage as she was by then. She volunteered to tear her sire’s head appart, and they laughed while she was doing it.

                                  She was with them ever since, forgetting her past and oblivious to her future, with only her name as a link to her previous reailty. And so it was, until they converged with another Firebrand group who were waiting for new recruits. They had words about a city who had recently lost her dictatorial Prince, and was easy to take. Baltimore was the promised land. Hands were sake non it, and so they feasted, as they waited for more revolutionaries to join them.

                                  But what arrived was not reinforcements, but the wrath of the Invictus. They exploded from the dark, attacking them with pawns, mortals and literal fucking kinghts and war horses. That last detail was her salvation; she had fighted as hard and wildly as she could, but in the end she was able to took one of the horses and fled the battle.

                                  Carmen wandered for weeks before she was able to pinpoint her location and how to arrive to Baltimore. And somewhere along those nights, under the solitude and the familiar, deep horse’s eyes, she started to remember herself. Her previous self, before the blood and the endless nights. She was no longer that little girl, but she was no longer the beast she had been in those last years either. She started to think about the Carthian Movement in a whole new light, reflecting about those years on the leash and on the run. And she started rebuilding herself anew.
                                  It was then when she took “Cortés” as her name, and named her horse –and ghoul- “Altivo”, after her favourite one, back in Spain, named itself after a Disney character.

                                  Now she’s arrived in Baltimore, and though she sees promise and opportunities all around, she also sees the same mistakes that doom every new revolution. For instance, she absolutely disapproves of any killing within Carthian ranks, as they only widen the cracks hidden behind every kindred partnership; and thusly disapproves of Broom act killing Seville as he would have made a very powerful ally in the Carthian cause.

                                  Objectives:

                                  1) To further the Carthian Cause. – Cortés will talk and bargain with everyone she feels is not hostile towards the Carthian Movement in order to improve the Movement allies. She’s smart and her dialectic is intense but level-headed when needed to be. She will try to make contacts as much as she can between the neonates –even future Invictus, if she feels she can reach them- in order to establish herself as a potential ally and/or confident.

                                  2) Murder, never between kin. – Cortés is not oblivious to killing and the resolution it brings to problems, but will never stand for it when happens between two Carthian brothers or sisters. That is set in Stone for her, and though she knows it will keep happening (her naive days are way, way behind her) she’ll always advocate for any other way.

                                  3) Never again a slave. – Cortés will never again fully bend the knee (she's smart enough to do it if in an un-life threatening situation as long as she has the option to do something about it in the future). She sees blood bonds as just another chain, and that is one of the few conditions that would make her try to kill another Carthian if inflicted upon her. (She does not consider ghouls to be slaves, though, as the benefits from the Blood outweights the downsides in her eyes).
