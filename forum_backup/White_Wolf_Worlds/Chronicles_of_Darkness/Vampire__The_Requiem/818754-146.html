                                        Questions about touchstones:

                                        1. What does getting detached from a touchstone mean? Does your character stop caring about that touchstone altogether, or does she still care but simply not gain the benefits from the touchstone anymore?

                                        Say for example my character has his human ex-girlfriend as a touchstone attached to humanity 6, and my character drops to humanity 5, what happens from a narrative point of view? Does he stop caring about his ex-girlfriend altogether? Or does he still care about her but to a lesser degree? Or does his relationship with his ex-girlfriend become more toxic? What happens?

                                        2. What happens if a touchstone starts acting in a way that isn't in line with what you need from that touchstone?

                                        To use the ex-girlfriend as an example again: Say my character has his ex-girlfriend as his touchstone because she was always the rock in the ocean to him. She had a solid head on her shoulders and knew that was right not just for herself but for my character as well. She kept him straight. After the embrace, she kept reminding him why he should care about his humanity, and humanity as a whole. But now this ex-girlfriend has changed. She became a reckless junky who sells her body to the highest bidder just so she can get enough money for her next hit. These days she isn't above pick-pocketing and thieving to get what she needs. She's no longer the ex-girlfriend my character fell in love with, she is no longer the person he wanted or needed. He can't stand what she has become. Her current behavior just makes him hate her, it makes him hate himself and it makes him just not care anymore. What consequences does this have from a mechanical point of view? Does my character lose his touchstone? Does she stop being a touchstone for him? What happens?

                                            1. It could be narrated any number of ways short of death or destruction of the touchstone to explain why they don't interact. The ex moved out of state and acquired a new significant other. The vampire got a status increase/decrease and is spending time to prove their worth. The great grandmother's wedding ring is stored in a safety deposit box and the bank employee who was letting in the vampire at night doesn't work there anymore and the vampire hasn't gotten around to retrieving it or ensnaring another bank employee to let them inside at night.

                                            2. That's really not possible for inanimate touchstones (unless the vampire's grip on reality is tenuous) but it could be cause for detachment in cases of people touchstones.

                                            Per your example, that sounds like your ST screwing around with your touchstone to stir up drama appropriate to touchstones. Mechanically as long as the vampire's Humanity rating stays at the correct level, and the character interacts with her (maybe to enable her behavior, maybe to try and straighten her out) it counts as attached. Ceasing to interact with her counts as detached.
