                                        Isn't it an option to just pretend you had nothing to do with the sire's disappearance ? She could be dead but the culprit is never found, in torpor somewhere, on the run and left the city... I'd try not getting caught in the first place, honestly, rather than pretend it was "only" a murder and no diablerie happened (if important people are concerned, they'll just ask some mekhet to check you out and you'll be found out). In a way, the blood bond can help throw suspicions off since it's pretty unlikely (unless other vampires are aware of your hatred).

                                        Aside from that, well sounds like you're up for some hefty humanity loss. Trying to hurt your regnant at blood bond 3 is a pretty difficult check and the diablerie itself... yeah. Plus, while it's possible continued abuse drove your character to the edge, this is probably something that'll scar him mentally. You'll be going against every artificial instinct of the blood bond to commit the worst kind of murder possible for a vampire. Plus don't forget about blood sympathy. Having flashes of what your sire is feeling while being diablerized does not sound fun.
