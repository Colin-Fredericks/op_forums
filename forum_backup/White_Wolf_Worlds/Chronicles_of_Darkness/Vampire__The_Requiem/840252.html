    Dominate

    03-01-2016, 07:52 PM
    I started a new Vampire chronicle. Unlike in the last one, one of the PCs is a Ventrue. He took Dominate at 3 dots on chargen, and is rather fond of solving problems with it. The problem with it is that it works... and a little too well, at that. Some things he was able to do with it include:
    • Force someone to open a door to their house in the middle of the night and then stand still and silently afterwards (he caught their eye through a peeping hole).
    • Make someone recall memories another vampire had made them forget. This one actually pushed the plot forward, so I'm not entirely complaining.
    • Have a random person off the street watch a building for him all night.
    ​His dice pool on the first-dot power is so big that no one can really resist it. Am I being too lenient here, and it shouldn't be so easy?

                          If you know how Dominate works, it's super easy to dodge it. You have at least one opportunity to act between Vampire Dominating you and declaring their command. Taking any amount of damage from any source removes Mesmerized, so if your target is hurt in the space between you completing the power and commanding them, the spell is broken.
                          Using combat turns, it looks like this:
                          Round 1:
                          Dominator: Dominate as a Instant Action
                          Everyone else: Still have their actions
                          Round 2:
                          Dominator: Issue the Command as a Instant Action
                          Dominated person: OBEY

                          This means that particularly determined vampire, or even mortal, can make your players go O__O by using his action to punch or stab himself to break the spell.

                                              It's doubly tricky to remember how being mesmerized feels, because you don't remember it after you stop being mesmerized! But a high ranking vampire probably asked someone to Dominate him, and then order him to remember how mesmerize feels.

                                                      03-01-2016, 09:38 PM
                                                      Sure. The intent is definitely so that you can't just mesmerize people in middle of combat and deny turns to them. In practice, all mesmerize does is making target character vulnerable to your next command. Due to the fact that a) you have a space of time between being mesmerized and receiving the command, and b) damage breaking it, it isn't a very practical combat power. Which is working as intended, because I remember that whenever Dominate popped up in previous incarnations of Vampire, people complained it was way too powerful and undefendable-from.
                                                      Accidentally, this balancing feature creates a situation where someone can punch themselves to break free before receiving an order - which I find cool. Again, it's not something to abuse to deny players the power - it's more something to pull out when the target is a experienced individual; one of Ventrue themselves or a jaded, veteran hunter, for example. Note that not many people will *know* that they are mesmerized, and even fewer will know that grabbing that pen and slamming it into your knee will snap you out of vampire's mind control
                                                      As for being totally hypnotized - dunno, IMHO mesmerized is simply *potential* to being controlled (which is exploited by issuing the command, and thus completing the spell). Full hypnosis is more like Dominated, which is escalated version of Mesmerized - thing that you can turn Mesmerized into if you have more dots in Dominate and even more time on your hands to play with the victim.
