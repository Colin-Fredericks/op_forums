                                                    12-18-2014, 03:41 PM
                                                    If you train in acrobatics then you would represent that by dots in athletics and dexterity, possibly other skills and attributes. Unless you were naturally talented to the point that the improvement was minimal, you would increase attributes to represent the effect of the training. If you stole the knowledge from someone that went through the training then you could get the skill increase, but you would not get an increase in dexterity. That is an approximation of learning a Coil.

                                                    After researching, meditating, experimenting and everything else you learned part of a Coil so you get a dot. The mechanics represent the "enlightenment." If you steal knowledge of it then you know how to get there, but you didn't go there yourself. Your body was not trained to behave as it should so you don't actually have the power. You only know how to get it. A player could argue they should be able to buy it at that point. That is house rule territory.

                                                    Clan specific disciplines can be learned by anyone. Bloodlines not tied to the specific clan can learn them as their bloodline discipline. All it takes is the "insight" from the blood of someone that knows what you want to learn. Diablerie would include the blood needed to learn and the act gives you the experience so you get a dot.

                                                    It costs the same experience to learn a Coil as any other discipline, but it also takes time to learn a Coil. Coils are more difficult to learn than other disciplines. Unique bloodline disciplines require joining the bloodline to learn. Coils are closer to that than any of the other disciplines.

                                                    It would be like a player saying they could change into an animal after learning Protean 3 despite their character never consuming an animal before. They only did part of the required activities. There is no justification to say if you diablerized to get the Protean that you could change into the animals the one drained could change into. You could allow it to work that way, but that would simply be relaxation of rules to get a desired result. That is definitely house rule territory.

                                                    You could say that you can diablerize and get a Coil dot if you practice if for a month, but that is house rule territory. A Scale is more like a traditional discipline dot. You can justify gaining that with diablerie. A blood ritual can be justified. Sorcery would be a stretch, but you wouldn't be completely out there. Getting a Coil without putting in the time to train pushes into the realm of credulity. If you can learn Coils from diablerie then you should be able to learn unique bloodline disciplines from diablerie. You should be able to get merits from diablerie - Claws of the Unholy. It gets out of hand quickly.

                                                    Edit: In case it was lost. You don't need justification. It is a game. If it makes things fun then house rule away.

                                                        Originally posted by TittoPaolo210 View Post
                                                        I didn't considered this, but then, why can't you get coils the same way, since they too are a pretty relevant part of the victim?
                                                        Well, to me, Coils potentially involve doing weird stuff to your body, which doesn't seem like something diablerie should transfer. A diablerizing vampire doesn't get her victim's scars (although that would potentially be cool). As I said originally, if a vampire achieved Coil dots purely through meditation or some other sort of internal change, it makes sense that they'd transfer.
