                        Rules 2 and 3 imply a quarantine to me: don't bunch together, and don't leave the city. Rules 1 and 5 keep chaos down; no murdering each other, and no stirring up panic by impersonating the Nameless. These four rules, along with the removal of Theban Sorcery, largely hamstring Kindred society; no large gatherings, no threats of execution to keep the masses following a leader, and half the magic is gone. All this paints the Nameless as working some sort of weird social experiment with the Kindred of Montreal, one aimed at (to use some meta terminology) ensuring nobody goes beyond Tier 1 and instead has to rely ln themself and a small coterie at best. It empowers the individual and the small group by making anything that enforces a social hierarchy nearly impossible. Rules 4 and 6 don't fit into my theory at all, of course, and neither does the lack of a ban on Cruác or whatever the Tsihsketeri know. Rule 6 confuses me more than anything; what does a church cross have to do with any readily-apparent theory? No ready tie to the Strix, the dismembering of the social establishment, or any sort of ancient native prophecy pop out to me at all. As for what the hell the Nameless is, I'm just as lost as anyone else and clearly must write it off as the demon from Montreal By Night hopping from oWoD to nWoD somehow.

                            well, from what I can gather from the Rules, I think that there are a number of conclusions:

                            a) The Nameless need vampires. Sure, it doesn't allow them to do big gatherings and kills them every time they break the Rules, but it needs them. Rules I to III show it. So what ever the Nameless is, and how powerful it may be, it needs the kindred, and it invest so much energy and resources in order to keep them in line because it is THAT important.

                            b) Rule IV seems to be kinda obvious- but this IS rule IV, and 4 is the magic number, so I believe that there is something beneath the surface.. I believe that that Rule is the most important one, and that it exist for the same reason Rule V exist- in order to keep the Nameless nameless. For some reason, like it needs vampires, it also needs to keep itself as a mystery. That make me think that maybe the Nameless gains something from being the only terror in the night- and if someone tries to impersonate it, he may hurt the complicate plan of the Nameless.

                            c) The last Rule seems to be the weirdest of all the others- all of the Rules exist, more or less, on the personal level. Suddenly, there is a Rule that targets a specific thing- the Cross. The reason seems to be connected to the fact that the Cross is the trigger to this all plan- this is where the first Prince was killed, and by doing so Theban Sorcery stopped working. To me, it sounds like it was some of a ceremony- killing a "Prince" upon a "Royal Cross". In short, if one wants to allow Theban Sorcery to work again, or to destroy the complicate plan of the Nameless, it should involve the Mount Royal Cross.

                            Now, if we add that to the fact that those who know the most are a line of vampires which is named after Owls (which we don't need the native American mythology from Wikipedia to tell us that they are a bad sign) and that they claim that the Nameless is the least among their problems and that there are worse things in the way... well, I don't have a clue about what comes out, but it reeks I tell you!

                            Edit- yeah, I've had a sudden relaization that the "Cross" may not actually mean the Church, but the Royal Mount Cross, for this was were everything has started.

                                (Apologies for the double post: these forums do not get along well with an iPad.) I've found a minor break in the case! The Rue Saint Paul (a street in the city) is said to be haunted, and Kindred who lurk there meet ill fates. According to the Great and All-Knowing Wikipedia, Rue Saint Paul is the oldest street in Montreal, and was originally laid out by François Dollier de Casson, a chaplain who served the French soldiers who fought against the local Mohawk in an action that may well be the one that led to the French Kindred's "ghoul farm" of natives.

                                Given that the natives don't fear the Nameless, that it made its first attack at the Cross, has a Rule against approaching the Cross, removed the magic practiced by Christian Kindred from Montreal, and haunts a street plotted by a chaplain involved in an atrocity against the natives, you start to get a picture of the Nameless as something somewhat on the side of the Tsihsketeri and violently opposed to Christianity. Not sure how this ties into Kindred at large, but nobody else mentioned it yet.

                                                            I must say that I don't think that the Nameless is a Tsihsketeri seek for vengeance. The reason is that the Tsihsketeri don't believe they shouldn't fear it because it is their friend, but because its existence means that there are worse things in the horizon.

                                                            Also, it occurred to me that whether the Nameless is a vampire, a strixs or something else, it doesn't really understand the modern Kindred. After all, it didn't gave them the rules right at the beginning, but too its time, spreading terror until it finally gave out the rules. While spreading terror may be its purpose, I'm not sure it is- after all, at the beginning it killed a prince after a prince until he gave a specific message for "no more princes", so I think that there is a certain ignorance from its side about how vampire works.

                                                            so it made me think about two different possibilities:

                                                            a) the Nameless seek chaos. Everything it does is done in order to make things more and more crazy. It needs the vampires for some reason (like the fact that it killed the prince on the Cross in order to stop Theban Sorcery), but it doesn't want them to interfere with its project. Its greatest secret is that there is no real higher meaning for the Rules- they are simply made in order to draw of attention from its project, which involves Theban Sorcery and vampires. In a way, it was like giving the Kindred a riddle to solve which had no solution so they would leave it alone.

                                                            b) the Nameless is a defense mechanism against something bad- really bad. It has existed since the times the Tsihsketeri ruled the land, and so they know what it truly means. All of those weird Rules are a some kind of a ritual meant to protect the vampires of Montreal, and if it would fail what ever the Nameless tries to stop will be free to kill everything. The thing may be based around the vampiric Beast (so the chances are that it is connected to the strixs), which is why it drain the very energy of Theban Sorcery in order to create some kind of a barrier or a seal upon it. The very presence of vampires is needed in order to work the ritual because of the Vitae in their vines which carries mystical energy

                                                            c) same as before- only that instead of stopping some great evil, it tried to summon it. Maybe it even tries to awake God himself- and from what I remember from Danse Macabre, it truly isn't a good thing
