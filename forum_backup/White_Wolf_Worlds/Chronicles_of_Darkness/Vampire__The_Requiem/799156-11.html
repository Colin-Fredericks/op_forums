                            Originally posted by saibot View Post
                            In a Chronicle in which werewolves can sense the Predatory Aura without the aid of physical signs, on an entirely mystic level, you could make an argument they would feel it, but probably have no way of interpreting exactly what is happening or what they should do.
                            Interestingly, this is an area where, by default, vampires hold an advantage over the werewolves. The wolves have no way to detect vampires without specific Gifts (Scent the Unnatural and Boundary Ward, both with sharp restrictions), while vampires can tell a werewolf instantly from their aura. Vampires always know when another predator is around, while werewolves senses tend to be focused on the Hisil more than fleshy beings.

                            That said, there IS a proto-Tribe in the Werewolf book who thinks that they should have vampires as their Sacred Prey, so they might have some tricks up their furry arms. But that is firmly up in homebrew territory, so not much we can say here will help if that's the case.

                                            Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                            I never got the impression that vampires could auto-sense werewolves. In fact, I think this sort of flies in the face of the entire point of Hishu form.
                                            Others and the Predatory Aura sidebar, p92, specifically states that when a Kindred interacts with a werewolf, they get the feelings they normally wouldn't experience. Vampires can theoretically detect others as well, though that's up to ST discretion.

                                            Hishu, as per the rules on WtF p96, just inflicts a penalty to pursuit or trying to pinpoint, not general detection. The point is to evade pursuit, or avoid being picked out of crowd. Predatory Aura works once you've already spotted the werewolf, and you recognize him as a fellow predator when face to face. The two abilities operate on different wavelengths. Even then, hishu doesn't negate any attempt to sense them, its only a penalty, which is an odd thing to reconcile with a power that auto-succeeds. Even if you want to argue that the innate ability of one supernatural is clashing with the innate ability of another, that'd be a Clash of Wills roll that the wolf remains unaware of if he loses. It is still a situation that favors the vampire.

                                                        Let me give an exmaple of how I see the two of them (hishu form and pred aura) working.

                                                        Imagine vampire A, B, C, D, and E are all from different clans, and are at a bar discussing the latest moves by their rivals from other covenants. Five werewolves, one of the so-called twice-blessed pack, walk into the bar, all in Hishu form. The vampires would suffer a penalty (-Primal Urge) to actually NOTICE the wolves coming in. The vamps wouldn't really pay the wolves any mind. And, if you don't notice someone, then Pred Aura doesn't trigger. The two groups, oblivious to each other, go on their night, both blending in to humanity in general.

                                                        The wolves, however, came here to hunt down one of their prey - the Storm Lord of the group tracked one of the Claimed to this bar. And, once they found him, the werewolves confront him. Start drawing attention to themselves. At this point, all eyes in the bar are shiting to the wolves and the Claimed. You can't really not notice and pinpoint the wolves, even with a penalty. They're the center of attention. At this point, the vamps take a good look at the wolves, and -now- Predatory Aura triggers. As a group, the vamps stiffen, and quietly exit the bar.

                                                        The next night, ABCD and E go back to the same bar, and try to glance around for the werewolves again. Vamprie E, a Mehket, even uses Auspex. Each vampire suffers -PU to detect the pack quietly sitting in the corner, they just blend in. Three of the vamps fail to notice. E's Auspex dice pool is penalized, and bad luck causes her to fail as well. Vamp A gets lucky and rolls a 10 on a chance die, spotting the pack and immediately feeling the auras again once she does. The cotorie leaves the bar again.

                                                        Werewolves, having noticed that group walk out both times they spotted the pack, decide to investigate. They get up, and as a group in Hishu, follow. The vampires suffer PU as a penalty to notice they're being followed through a crowd. A few blocks, the crowd thins, and the pack stands out. Predatory Aura triggers again once they're spotted.
