    Mystery Enlightenment: A fan writeup

    04-28-2016, 02:19 AM
    Mystery Enlightenment

    Sometimes the coils aren't enough for one dragon or another. Sometimes they're tired of frenzying at all, even if they are more powerful for mastering the coil of the wyrm. Sometimes they simply fail to see why they shouldn't be able to replicate Dracula's defeat of the sun, after all Dracula for all his exceptional nature was still fundamentally kindred. When a Dragon pushes the coils too far, when their want becomes need and need becomes obsession... Most of the time, bad things happen. Spectacular explosions, dark creatures flocking to the individual and devouring them. An infectious susceptibility to frenzy that induces rage in humans. But sometimes, in the depths of their obsession they realize a fundamental and intuitive truth about the curse of vampirism and become enlightened.

    Termed, 'Enlightenment' by those who do not possess it themselves, this is a special state, a sort of partial transcendence that some kindred of exceptional talent and ability may achieve with a lot of work, or else phenomenal luck and a touch of madness. This is an understanding on a level without words, without formal ideas, about what makes a particular aspect of the curse tick. Internalization isn't something you can teach, only something you can learn and be guided towards. Hence, this is an extremely rare state and should be accordingly difficult to achieve.

    The journey of a thousand miles

    So how does one achieve enlightenment in a particular mystery? What circumstances make conditions particularly favorable, or unfavorable, what signs of approaching it are present? Firstly, though it can equally likely herald disaster, obsession is almost always a component. Pursuing enlightenment often means not caring as much about the great work as a whole, and more about one's individual priorities. Withdrawing from Order gatherings, spending all time not spent feeding or in daysleep performing research and experimentation, when someone on the verge of great disaster or great understanding begins their journey the Order has learned to recognize the signs. Due to their possession of many dark secrets, Order kogaions are the most likely to achieve enlightenment of their mystery. It's that sort of touch of madness, it's that sort of extensive and weighty practice of abandoning so many human ethics and striving for the next stage of existence that kogaions often have that can be found in those on the road to enlightenment as well. Just as often however, the disaster that follows has led the Order to snuff out these potential lights, lest the chance of them becoming raging wildfires manifest.

    Next, one must have reached the commonly accepted limits of a given mystery. In mechanical terms, no Dragon has ever achieved enlightenment without possessing the fifth dot of the mystery for which they are attempting to perfect. Attempts to do so otherwise and surprise their peers invariably and without exception result in dismal failure. However, the fact remains that while the fifth dot is needed to perfect a mystery this is very much not a sixth dot of the coils. A sixth dot of the coils implies that if only one were to spend enough time in routine study, in contemplation and the attempt of chrysalis, that they would find enlightenment. Enlightenment is not a coil, but the destination to which the coils are the journey.

    Finally, the achievement of enlightenment is personal. This is a portion that few individuals realize, even those who have done so. You cannot teach enlightenment, and enlightenment feels different for every kindred who achieves it. It's the same truth, it's just colored by the experiences and existences of whoever grasps it. This component in its rarity and lack of proliferation is often the downfall of kindred who deliberately seek the mysterious transfiguration that has been witnessed on rare occasions. Strix and other dark spirits aware of this pursuit will often bargain with the kindred, offering to teach them secret knowledge or simply hand them enlightenment on a silver platter. Should the kindred accept, disaster is not far behind.

    Yes, but how do you get there?

    As mentioned before, the coils are a journey to enlightenment. Not everyone gets there, but the coils fundamentally exist as stepping stones and preparations to shed an aspect of the vampiric curse. In clearer terms, each of the enlightenment bonuses is a permanent and irrevocable change that comes over the dragon who approaches and grasps it. The storyteller is recommended to by and large reserve this process for either the capstone of a single character's personal story, or else make it a plot point for non-player characters to pursue themselves. After all, a particularly prominent kogaion making a double or nothing gamble for enlightenment is very much a potent driving force for a story.

    However, what goes on should be divided into three steps. First, the impetus. The impetus should cover something kicking off the dragon's search for enlightenment. Maybe sunlight denies them a critical ability to function, to intervene in a beloved mortal's life when they could have otherwise saved them. Perhaps frenzy and personal grudges combine to bring them to assault the Prince, and in a moment of weakness simply refuse to ride the wave. Maybe even a lack of control, a betrayal by a blood bound individual drives them to desire even further power over their thralls. In these things and more, impetus can be found and in a best case scenario should be created organically. As a product of natural roleplay, the impetus should arise and be discussed between the storyteller and a given character's player during downtime. Is it a great enough impetus to drive the player's character to obsession? Is it enough to drive them to discard certain basic assumptions and spark that little bit of madness that might let them accept an idea slightly divergent from what most think about reality? If the answer is, "Yes" to either or both questions then it is possible or even likely that the impetus has been given.

    Second, the research. Not everyone gets this far, some become frustrated with the coils of the dragon and the mysteries they've been presented with. Some look to the magics of the Circle as they trade favors with those similarly disenchanted with their fellow Acolytes, to learn what they may need to synthesize some new change in themselves. Others may look deeper into devotions and bloodlines, searching for one that might have been possessed of the power to alleviate their condition. But for those that still stick with the coils, they have to hit a dead end in their research, a sort of desperation. This produces even more and more extreme methods, until they are forced to confront a truth about themselves and the curse that they have never perceived as possible. What is this truth? Well, that's left to the storyteller to decide.

    Third, the enlightenment proper. The process should delve into the abstract, into a metaphorical representation of what exactly is occurring. Perhaps the scene may shift to a student of the wyrm fighting their beast inside their own soul, in a battle to dominate the other once and for all. Perhaps a student of the ascendant may be tied to a post, lashed by whips of sunlight as a judge calls out their sins before each burning strike and enlightenment comes when they realize the purity of the sun doesn't burn them as their selfhood is more than their misdeeds and the profane nature of the curse. Regardless of the imagery and test involved, the process should be intense enough and difficult enough that the majority of kindred including elders should fail even as there's a very real chance of success.

    Onwards and upwards

    Now for the mechanical effects of enlightenment proper. These are expressions of the fundamental and soul-baring truth the vampire has internalized, and ultimately embodied. Each of the three mysteries in the core book are represented here, though the mysteries in Secrets of the Covenants will have suggestions as well.

    Ascendant: By spending ten additional points of vitae when activating the blush of life, not subject to normal spending limits, the vampire's blood potency for purposes of sunlight damage may well and finally, drop to zero. This renders the individual as able to walk in the sun without fear of damage for the next twenty-four hours, though this feat is notably taxing on all but the most powerful of kindred.

    Wyrm: The nature of frenzy changes for the vampire. Frenzy becomes a state of mind more than a curse, for the beast is eternally leashed to the vampire afterwards. Mechanically, the vampire gains access to an improved version of riding the wave that offers no mental detriments. However, while they may leash their beast the beast is still a separate entity ever wishing to take control. Corruption is not far behind for the incautious wyrm enlightened, and all degeneration checks made while using this form of riding the wave are made at a -2 penalty. Finally, the vampire is immune to involuntary frenzy as the beast cannot exert its will over the vampire.

    Voivode: The vampire gains access to a fourth step of the blood bond, which causes the thrall to become completely incapable of causing harm to their regnant. While it may be purged through the normal means, betrayal is an alien concept to thralls so bound.

    Ziva: A return to humanity.

    Zirnitra: No longer requiring willpower to activate one's psychic merits, as they become simple extensions of the vampire's being which never produce drawbacks even under normal circumstances.
