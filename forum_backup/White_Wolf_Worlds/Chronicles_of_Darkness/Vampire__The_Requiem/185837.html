    What Kindred Would You Be?

    07-01-2014, 07:41 PM
    So I made a post on Changeling asking what kind of Kiths everyone would be, but now I'm curious as to what kind of vampire you would be if the World of Darkness were a real thing.

    What to List:

    Clan

    Covenant

    Reasons:


    Optional Listing:


    Bloodline

    Disciplines



    ---------------------------------------

    So here's me! ^_^


    Clan: Nosferatu

    Covenant: Circle of the Crone

    Reasons: I make no attempt to hide the fact that the downgrading of vampires in the recent decades infuriates me. I am a horror genre fan, and I love it when vampires are portrayed as monsters as opposed to sex symbols. If I had the choice of clan, I would definitely pick Nosferatu, for they are the very personification of monsters. Gangrel would be a close runner up, however. I'd be one of the Nosferatu who embraces their nature and takes delight in flaunting it around the pretty folk. In some poetic way, I actually find the Nosferatu to be more beautiful than the bustiest of Daeva.

    As for the Covenant, as I mentioned on my Kiths topic, I like the idea of magic and sorcery, so a covenant who practices blood magic would certainly appeal to me. I'd just need to build up a pain tolerance, I suppose. A runner up choice would be the Carthian Movement, as I feel that the feudal era system of vampire society is ridiculous and needs to wake up to the fact that the class system is dead and gone. The Invictus and the Lancea Sanctum can go fuck themselves, and I don't like the goals of the Ordo Dracul (like I said, I like vampires being monstrous). Being Unaligned may sound like a good way to go, but in a society that thrives on betrayal, I'd rather have some kind of back up than face an army alone.


    Bloodline: Malkavian Afflicted or the Noctuku

    Even as a human I'm not completely all there, and have had a string of mental conditions throughout my life. So what better way to join the undead than as a raving lunatic and "spread the love" with Dementation? Alternatively, if you're going to be a predator, why not be an apex predator? Sure, as a Noctuku I'd be terrifying to the rest of the community, but as I've already said that's exactly how I like my vampires to be. Besides, I don't think locating food would be as difficult as most people think. Vampires can regenerate, so just lock one up in your basement and you'd have a renewable food supply!

    Disciplines: Nightmare, Vigor, Resilience, Protean, Cruac, Dementation or Phagia (depending on bloodline), Animalism


    As a vampire, I would train in Vigor and Resilience for the sweet, sweet pleasure of beating the crap out of my enemies in close quarters. I would train in Nightmare to appear more terrifying and appeal to my interpretation of vampires. I would of course study Cruac as a member of the Circle, and I would put in the effort to learn the power of Protean for the added benefit of having a set of retractable claws in battle, or the awesomeness of being able to turn into a variety of different animals. Speaking of animals, I like them more than I like people. Especially cats, I'm a cat lover, I want a haven guarded by Ghouled house cats!

    As for the bloodline Disciplines, either I could drive people insane as a Malkavian afflicted, or I could tear their skin off and wear it like a suit as a Noctuku. Either one sounds enticing to me.

              Ooh! I love these kinds of things!

              Clan: Mekhet

              As an INTP, Mekhet seems like a nearly perfect fit for me. My greatest skill has always been my ability to intuitively understand people's thoughts and emotions. Even as a young child, I just understood people's thoughts and feelings, which made me an excellent listener. As I grew older I studied psychology and developed an understanding of those processes from an intellectual level as well. In short, I have IRL Auspex. I almost went with Daeva, because I am also very charismatic. But ultimately I think Mekhet is still more appropriate because my social skills come not from raw magnetism, but subtle application of psychology and acting. My aforementioned knack for knowing what and how people think, coupled with my ability to outwardly display what ever emotions I want, allows me to manipulate social situations with ease, but in a way that more closely resembles clever use of Auspex and Obfuscate to garner bonuses on mundane social rolls than outright control via Majesty. Not to mention despite being very socially skilled, I'm actually extremely introverted, which doesn't mean too much on its own, but alongside all of these other points in favor of Mekhet over Daeva, it adds up. Additionally, I'm very intellectually driven. No desire is stronger for me than my curiosity and drive to explore and understand the mysterious and unknown, and I think that more than anything is what the Mekhet are all about.

              Covenant: Ordo Dracul

              From the very first time I read Vampre: the Requeim, I knew this was the Covenant for me. Something about them struck a chord on a level I can't explain in words. Their goals, their ideology, their methodology, their reverence for the coolest vampire character ever written. Everything about them just resonates with me on a deep personal level. I find their goals to be the most noble out of all of the Covenants. The Invictus and the Carthians are just two sides of the same old political coin, and I honestly find both equally petty. The Sanctified scare the hell out of me, seriously, what the fuck. They combine all the worst parts of organized religion and twist them into something far more sinister and, honestly, dangerous. The Crone I could probably get along with fine; even if I don't agree with their religious views, they seem pretty content to let me believe what I want if I let them believe what they want, and I can respect that. But the Order really has the right idea - the pursuit of personal enlightenment and self-improvement, that takes a different form for each individual. What more respectable goal could anyone ask for than that? I could go on, but I'll leave it at that for now.

              Bloodline: None

              I freaking love the Morbus and would really love to be able to say I would be one, but if I'm being honest with myself, I would not join a bloodline. I wouldn't want to get myself involved with any group as insular as a bloodline, and would be much happier finding my own path. Maybe after I had followed my path along the Coils to a point where I felt I had made a breakthrough truly worth sharing I might start my own bloodline (if my blood was potent enough) and let my insight guide them down their own paths. But I would not join any existing line.

              Disciplines: Auspex, Majesty, Obfuscate, and as many Coils and Scales as I could learn (Voivode primary)

              Pretty much the reasons above explain my Discipline choices as well. I know Majesty is out-of-clan, but I'm sure I could find a Daeva to teach me at least a little of it. Also, I'd probably join the Moirai Shadow Cult if I could, and one of their benefits is in-clan Majesty for Mekhet members and in-clan Auspex for Daeva members.

                                      Clan: Gangrel

                                      Covenant: Carthian Movement.

                                      Reasons: My reasoning for Gangrel is rather simple: I love the idea of a ever shifting body and the the changes such a mutable form would bring each night, for either peaceful utility, hunting, or defending myself from kindred rivals. It doesn't hurt that I have a naturally rough and aggressive attitude to begin with, and the predatory and gruff nature of many Gangrel would fit quite well with me. For a much less vampiric reason, I would find the ability to become a number of different animals to be truly enlightening. Flying, swimming, leaping from tree branch to tree branch or digging beneath the ground- these are all perspectives that could truly help one think and inspect their own natures. Compounding that insight is the amount of understanding that Animalism provides- sure it may be primarily commanding the beasts around them, but it still provides clearer methods of communication between vampire and animal. As for Covenant, the New Blood and Smoke Carthians have a certain appeal to them that the older Carthian Movement worked too hard for. I for one like cooperation, not in a socialist sense like some people incorrectly peg the Movement as, but cooperation in a predatory way that just happens to appeal to human concepts of innovation and change.

Nope. The Brood offers more, just by heeding The Call. If I didn't do such early on, I sure as hell would have later after I got fed up with a vampire society that was just as much a farce as the human one I cam from.

                                                  Bloodline: Sta-Au: the Wicked and Cruel Ones. Go figure, while odd bedfellows they seem on the mark about quite a lot of things and they specifically survive in hostile areas. Besides which, they're one of the few endemic North American Bloodlines that may well exist where I live. Funny how that works out! Sure, you don't regain Humanity but... at that point it probably doesn't matter, and you sure as hell don't want to do that among the Brood as currently written anyhow.

                                                  Disciplines: Auspex, Obfuscate, Protean, Resilience as per the Bloodline. Initial three dots will definitely end up Auspex 1, Obfuscate 1, Protean 1 and progress from there. possibly a touch of leftover Celerity from being Mekhet before getting tired of it all and wandering to a Land of Worms. Probably a bit of Animalism eventually later in unlife, possibly the earlier stages of a Coil (iffy, I doubt I'd be in the Ordo for the long haul) but certainly the Bloodline Devotions and Investments. So many directions, so many potentials over time and using Ghost Walk lets say hello to certain Numina and possibly Influences as well. Crazy, eh? Never know what I might pull out of undead sleeve.

                                                  Walking by day? Oh woe... the angst of being denied such, I think I'll just... walk as a ghost! Or not worry about it when I'm back home among the Worms dragging your cherished mortals behind anyhow; did you know that sunlight is far less of an issue over there AND they're usually out of the way enough to be -not- worth others coming in? What are you going to do, send a band of nomads after me and my Convey on their home turf? Nah. Mortals? Feel free. Come in, take what I want, go back out while the others cower in gilded cages. Sounds great!

                                                  Actually... please send some mortals after us! When they die they will be anchored there and doubly useful as feeding stock after death.

                                                  Yep. I'd be a vicious, vicious monster, thrice over by Bloodline and Covenant. Odd how both appeal to me more in the long-term than the rest, but why fuck around? If it didn't start happening early, it sure would later. Vampiric society is like mortal society; full of near sociopathic assholes. Why not be the bigger one?

They ape what a lot of the Brood has already achieved, much like the Crones. Or so I'd think if I were Kindred. Measured approaches to something immeasurable. Bread and circuses to distract oneself. Hell, I might even have "dabbled" in the Ordo initially, developed a Coil or some scale before it got insufferable.

                                                      Hell, even the territorial, lone predator shtick gets old: with the Brood you have a legitimate community with your Conveys that aren't just predicated on blood relations set in stone. Nope. The Brood offers more, just by heeding The Call. If I didn't do such early on, I sure as hell would have later after I got fed up with a vampire society that was just as much a farce as the human one I cam from.

Go figure, while odd bedfellows they seem on the mark about quite a lot of things and they specifically survive in hostile areas. Besides which, they're one of the few endemic North American Bloodlines that may well exist where I live. Funny how that works out! Sure, you don't regain Humanity but... at that point it probably doesn't matter, and you sure as hell don't want to do that among the Brood as currently written anyhow.

Initial three dots will definitely end up Auspex 1, Obfuscate 1, Protean 1 and progress from there. possibly a touch of leftover Celerity from being Mekhet before getting tired of it all and wandering to a Land of Worms. Probably a bit of Animalism eventually later in unlife, possibly the earlier stages of a Coil (iffy, I doubt I'd be in the Ordo for the long haul) but certainly the Bloodline Devotions and Investments. So many directions, so many potentials over time and using Ghost Walk lets say hello to certain Numina and possibly Influences as well. Crazy, eh? Never know what I might pull out of undead sleeve.

                                                      Walking by day? Oh woe... the angst of being denied such, I think I'll just... walk as a ghost! Or not worry about it when I'm back home among the Worms dragging your cherished mortals behind anyhow; did you know that sunlight is far less of an issue over there AND they're usually out of the way enough to be -not- worth others coming in? What are you going to do, send a band of nomads after me and my Convey on their home turf? Nah. Mortals? Feel free. Come in, take what I want, go back out while the others cower in gilded cages. Sounds great!

                                                      Actually... please send some mortals after us! When they die they will be anchored there and doubly useful as feeding stock after death.

Odd how both appeal to me more in the long-term than the rest, but why fuck around? If it didn't start happening early, it sure would later. Vampiric society is like mortal society; full of near sociopathic assholes. Why not be the bigger one?
