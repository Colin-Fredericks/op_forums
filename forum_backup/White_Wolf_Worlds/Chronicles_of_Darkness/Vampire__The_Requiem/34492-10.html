    133.

    1:7 And the LORD said unto Satan, Whence comest thou? Then Satan answered the LORD, and said, From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.
    1:8 And the LORD said unto Satan, Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth God, and escheweth evil?
    1:9 Then Satan answered the LORD, and said, Doth Job fear God for nought?
    1:10 Hast not thou made an hedge about him, and about his house, and about all that he hath on every side? thou hast blessed the work of his hands, and his substance is increased in the land.
    1:11 But put forth thine hand now, and touch all that he hath, and he will curse thee to thy face.
    1:12 And the LORD said unto Satan, Behold, all that he hath is in thy power; only upon himself put not forth thine hand. So Satan went forth from the presence of the LORD.

    A domain ruled by the Sanctified is plagued by series of brutal murders. Someone, clearly not done Kindred as some of these murders are committed in the daytime, plagues the community, plagues the truly righteous mortals trying to ruin their lives and make them denounce the God through taking away those who are close to them [But not taking their lifes, at least not before they curse the God]. And when kills, he clearly does it in Biblical fashion. The Slasher did not resist temptation of announcing himself, leaving clues and messages in which he styles himself the agent of the Lord, testing the righteous. What is worse, hunters followed the trail of bodies, trying to put stop to the tide of murders.

    How will local Sanctified react to someone usurping the mantle of God's chosen? Is this madman delusional or really is there something to his claims, as he displays clearly supernatural abilities [A scourge]? What to do with hunters who might uncover local Kindred by accident?

    PS. Personally I envision the Slasher as a Legend or quasi-Legend with appropiate Frailty.

    “I am absolute, I am perfect, I am supreme. I shall be eternal. My tragedy, is that there is no other fate for me. My powerlessness was that I couldn’t subjugate my journey to the gods, while dreaming of rebirth at the end of distant time, like other pharaohs.” Ramesses II, Fate/Prototype: Argent Fragments.

        134) Ah the 50's. A time when organized crime was just starting to become a major threat in America. When Al Capone is crowned the Invictus Prince of the city, he's looking to expand his operations. Will the players earn their way into the legendary crime lord's inner circle, or will they meet final death at the hands of gang warfare?

        Vampire Mobster story.



        135) When the head priestess for the Circle of the Crone is found crucified in her own Haven, it ignites a full blown war with the Lancea Sanctum. The Pagans are so pissed off, they're attacking the Sanctified on the streets, not even trying to be political or stealthy about it. Was this actually the work of the Sanctified, or is one of the other factions trying to ignite a war between the two and swoop in when they're too weak to fight back?



        136) The Carthian movement has finally done it, they've convinced vampire society to become democratic in its government. All decisions from now on will be decided by popularity of vote. But when the new government uses too much deliberation, their problems begin to pile up. And various sub-factions are now squabbling over who's ideas are right and wrong. The Cathian Movement is beginning to crumble to pieces, and those who counted themselves among the ranks of the Invictus can do little to hide their smiles as they watch it all come tumbling down around them. Have they truly given into the Carthian way of thinking, or have they decided to let their arch rival wipe itself out? Either way, somebody needs to bring stability to the situation, before full blown anarchy comes to the city's kindred.

            137) It started with them breaking the mold from clan stereotypes, then it moved onto other things. Eating mortal food, walking in the sunlight, and now there are rumors that one kindred may have actually gotten pregnant after her Embrace! Something is causing a fraction of the newest vampires of the city to develop... wrong. Their clan markings are weaker, they seem more human than they should be, biologically as well as mentally. The Prince finally decides to call down the hammer when the newest Fledgling spontaneously manifests Dominate, Nightmare, and Majesty without any formal training and no apparent clan lineage. These "thin bloods" as they are being called, need to be driven out, and the cause behind their creation discovered!



            138) A recent census taken shows that 5% of the city's population are vampires. That is far, far too many vampires! In order to regulate the population control, the Prince decides to hold a competition. The winners will be allowed to hold onto their domains and maybe even granted new ones. The losers (who aren't destroyed in the process) will be exiled to the suburbs.

                            142) Our vampire player has themselves a secret admirer. Well more like a crazy stalker. It turns out she's mortal and fell madly in love with him after witnessing his true nature. The World of Darkness is more terrifying than she imagined, but she's just crazy enough to stick around for her "true love" and see it through.                                         145) For as long as anyone can remember members of 4 vampire lineages would take turns bringing a 15 year old red-headed child to the edge of the Lake at the edge of the domain every 13 years. They said it would keep the Old Thing away. The last two times one of the lineages went, they came back changed: speaking an Indian language that hadn't been spoken in 300 years and practicing a bizarre new form of blood magic with what seems more like fear than devotion. The PCs have pissed off the Prince, and he sends them to deliver the next 13th year sacrifice to the edge of the Lake. What will happen?

                                                    148) There's a turf war between the local Ventrue and Daeva clans, each wanting to prove that they are the true echelons of society by establishing their clan as the majority rulers of the city.                                                         149.) It's Vegas Baby. Anything goes... including the toppling of Princes.

                                                        Las Vegas is a city of illusions and sin. Everything is different there: Wolves wander the Desert, the Vampires rule the Casinos, and there is rumor of a Mage enslaved to the local Prince that can strike fear into the local creatures with her powers. The saying 'What happens in Vegas, stays in Vegas' is notoriously true with the creatures of night.

                                                        The previous prince was murdered two years ago, but the internal investigation to what happened to him has gone cold with no results only that the killer went by the alias "The Voice". The clans have specifically selected the players to investigate what is happening. Delve into the twisted politics of keeping power over not only blood, but money in Sin City. Solve the mystery behind the last prince's death, choose to friend some of the local power brokers, or the abused, and find the Voice before they strike again.

                                                        Your first night in Vegas? The Voice has struck: exploding a wing of Ceasar's Palace... Good luck.

                                                        In this story, after investigation and choices of the players, they find that the Voice, all this time, had been the enslaved Mage at the Prince's and party's side. Add as many twists and turns as you like, it took my players 2 months to figure out the Voice was the Mage. Our conclusion came with the Prince becoming paranoid of being next and destroyed a club with a bomb that was a hot bed of Mage activity. In return, the Mage murdered him, without knowing just how close to home the threat was.

                                                            150) Self-Made... Man?

                                                            The city is in an uproar after a secret message to the Prince somehow became common knowledge- that a kine somehow forced the Embrace from a neonate.

                                                            The power structure is paralyzed as the Elders, the Primogen, and the Prince all hurl responses and motives and consequences and half-formed plans back and forth among one another in a near-literal Frenzy. Worse, the metaphysical and political consequences of any given opinion could be catastrophic, so everybody's deployed their own under-table agents to get the job done.

                                                            Is the story immediately proven or disproven as a hoax? Is this only a momentary hiccup as a plan rattles together after a couple of days, a consent is formed, and the mystery is dealt with? Does the mystery disappear entirely, with no further evidence or update, after that first baffling revelation? Is it a protracted cold war, with the unthinkable, an unknown vampire traitor among them? Is it the start of a dreadfully successful hunting campaign against local vampires? Is it ultimately of little meaning, but responsible for tipping the power in one direction or another, enough for a coup, enough that it comes forward that the story was misinformation? Dear God, did the human know this would happen, set the stage for him to attack or seize power or run? That DICK!!

                                                            ...is the new vampire... accepted?? As someone's pawn, or protege, or genuine attempt at altruism?

                                                            How long do they survive the All Night Society?
