                                                No the prohibition is against willfully ceding control of soul (such as it is) to other entities for power or esoteric knowledge, which is exactly what you are doing when you permit a ghost, demon, angel etc. to use your body to scrawl messages.
                                                Involuntarily losing control would be problematic and quickly reduce the covenant to non existence if there was any attempt at enforcement since there's always been Dominate, and, if you're going by several of the noisier forumites' interpretation, Majesty is both unresistable and undetectable.

                                                As for why much of the Ordo Dracul covenant book doesn't apply anymore. How about by pointing out that their purpose isn't transcending the vampiric curse, but spitting on God and that Dracula is not their founder. Then there's a bunch of other little things like how knowledge of the Coils or belonging to a Greater Oath does not denote covenant rank any longer. Or how Kogaion went from being a singular individual who knew of all the wyrm's nest in a domain and advisor to the Oath Sworn to a singular undisputed leader of the covenant, not the Oath Sworn. While I never particularly cared overmuch for the concept of the Mysteries Sworn, (I find it unlikely that more than one or two would actually exist in most domains given their stringent requirements.) with the emphasis on drinking Vitae to accomplish anything, they're even less relevant now.

                                                        Dominate and Majesty have nothing to do with this issue, I'm talking about being overtaken by otherworldly spirits, demons, etc. There's an entire bloodline that revolves around controlling, utilizing, and eating ghosts.                                                         There are a lot of strange assertions floating around in this thread that are perplexing to me. There was talk earlier about the Impaled being part of the Axe Sworn. That's just not the case. There's some overlap because the Rites of the Impaled are very martial, but there's no requirement for an Impaled to be Sworn of the Axe. They're kind of a cult within a cult.
