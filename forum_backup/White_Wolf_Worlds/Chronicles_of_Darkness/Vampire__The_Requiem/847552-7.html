    Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
    So, how is doing something to make yourself feel better meta?
    Like calling/ hanging out with your bestie (touchstone)--- (Authoritarian) Proving yourself the best? (Junkie) Getting your fix?
    I think it comes down to Masks and Dirges being too much like caricatures of how people act, rather than reflecting real people. I don't think there are two specific activities that make people feel good, I think they feel good from a ridiculously wide range of things. So, saying, only these two things make you feel good, is offputting and weird. It becomes meta, because the character should feel good from doing more than that. Like I said, a real person who feels self-affirmed cracking jokes doesn't feel self-affirmed for cracking a joke at a funeral (or other inappropriate situation, they just feel awkward and weird). It's not realistic. It's distiling a character into digestible chunks. It's highlighting their strongest quirks. It's something that's important in short duration media, like, say, TV shows, or even stand-alone movies, so you can quickly identify what broad archetype the character falls into and process it as such. It's a shortcut that purposefully limits the depth of the character to make it easier to understand and follow.

    Now, that kind of thing works great in, like I said, TV or movies. It works for people who want to play a story game, where the group of players gets together to tell the best, most interesting, most dramatic story possible, because you are not worried about verisimilitude, just drama. It doesn't work for people who play for other reasons.

                                        I feel like part of the problem is revealed in you claiming mask and dirge are just two actions which regain willpower. They aren't. They are far broader than that. A jester doesn't just get WP from cracking jokes. Though, for the record, I see absolutely nothing wrong or forced about a vampire cracking a joke during a funeral if that's their coping mechanism. Might be socially crass, but there are people that handle stress with humor, and this isn't out of place or unrealistic. But a Jester gets WP for pointing out absurdities and flaws and not taking things seriously. That's more than just jokes. They also gain willpower for wit and cunning.

                                        These only seem like caricatures because you reduce them to a single action and assume that players will or are expected to do this one action any time they use WP. They are broad character archetypes, and any action that fits them earns WP. And any action that falls in line with them can be justified instead of forced by a decent roleplayer. Ideally, these actions should just happen in the course of play because Mask/Dirge are core parts of the PC's personalities and the WP regeneration is less a carrot and more just a simulation of what such self-affirming actions do for the psyche. But even if your players are the sort to only RP for the sake of regaining WP, the actions they take should and will be perfectly IC if you use Anchors according to RAW.
