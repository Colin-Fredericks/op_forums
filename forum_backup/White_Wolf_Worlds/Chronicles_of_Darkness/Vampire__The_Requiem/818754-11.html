    Originally posted by MCN View Post
    Actually, here's a question from me. If Majesty, Nightmare, and Dominate are "pushing out" with your Aura, like upgraded versions of Predator Auras... Auspex is an enhancement to Kindred Senses, Obfuscate is twisting your aura inwards and hiding it, and the physicals are like enhancements to Physical Intensity/healing... what's Animalism and Protean? How would you describe them? They don't feel like Aura manipulations, and I don't think there's a base vampire ability they corresond to?
    I'd say that Animalism is Aura manipulation. You're using your aura to communicate with animals, essentially using your Beast to act as an interpreter between yourself and other beasts. You could also look at it as an enhancement to the properties of your Vitae, imbuing it with a small amount of your Predatory Aura to allow it to communicate your commands to the animals that smell or taste it.

    Protean is a bit more of a stretch, but vampires have some minor shapeshifting abilities in the base template. The fangs are an obvious example, but also arguably The Cleansing - returning the vampire's body to the form it was in at the time of embrace. Protean enhances this inherent shapeshifting ability and uses it to make the vampire's body more closely resemble her Beast.

    Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.

The Murmure, meanwhile, are a Gangrel bloodline that's been confined to the foothills beyond the city's limits, a lifestyle that grew increasingly less viable with the rise of urbanization.

                                            In the 1860s, at the peak of the Industrial Revolution, amid rising urbanization, one of the Marinaio by the name of Ghyslain believed he'd been chosen by the gods of oceans and rivers to take the role of Prince, a belief confirmed by the Murmure's matriarch, Grandmother Whispers. With the Syrah already indulging in the heresies of the Ordo Dracul, and a coven of Stylo scribes willing to break faith, a coup was enacted and the Mad Prince Ghyslain ascended the throne.

                                            Since then, Ghyslain's basically turned the world upside down by giving his Sheriff extreme leeway to punish any members of the Lancea, while offering extreme leniency to anyone who supports him.

                                            What's also helped is that Ghyslain might very well be actually touched by the gods. The man has a compulsion forcing him to obey prophecies, omens, ritual taboos, etc., and yet it seems to help him. By accident or design, he's managed to avoid most every assassin thus far.
