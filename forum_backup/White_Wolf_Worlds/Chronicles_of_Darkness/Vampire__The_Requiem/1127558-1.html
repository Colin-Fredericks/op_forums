                                        ... Um, I said directly that VtR 2e addresses Tilts. What I said is that VtR 2e doesn't use the Tilt system like the other games do. The rest of the 2e supernaturals have powers (either innate or bought) that interact with Tilts; either inflicting them, protecting you from them, or removing them. That's why anything that imports the full Tilt system to VtR 2e is inherently a house rule. It's not designed to deal with them.
