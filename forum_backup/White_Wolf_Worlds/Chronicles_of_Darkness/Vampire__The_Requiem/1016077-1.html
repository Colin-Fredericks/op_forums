        I agree with Tsusasi, both the vampires roll their Power stat+ BP. Though instead of the vampire being affected, the power is affected with the result.
        Again I reiterate, the book literally says you attack the power. If you win the lashing out rolls as per page 92, the power stops working on you, Not the vampire is affected by the conditions, but the power stops working on you.

        Say the aura is a car, and the vampire is the driver. You can smash (lashing out) up the car to stop the vampire, The vampire rolls his drive to control the car, but once the car is stopped, the vampire is still ok and able to do what he needs to do.
        The car doesnt roll it's own drive.. same with the aura, the vampire rolls for both. It's a test of power and control.

        EDIT: It's not a huge deal to play it either way. Whatever works, the results are exactly the same. The power is stopped or not stopped. It's just how you want to picture it.

                                                Originally posted by Maina View Post
                                                Lashing Out is basically a contest of wills between Beasts. It's vying for dominance. The power stops because your Beast has proven itself dominant to the other Vampire's Beast. Awe is a modified Predatory Aura. This has been confirmed elsewhere in the forums. When someone Lashes Out at you, you don't counter by Lashing Out at their Predatory Aura. You counter by Lashing Out at them directly.
                                                Yet you can lash out at things that do not have Beasts, so it is not simply that. It is an intensification of the Predatory Aura, that may or may not trigger a direct clash with another vampire's Predatory Aura, another supernatural's "some sort" of aura, or even a mortal's strength of will. Or in this case simply shake off a supernatural effect by letting that aspect of herself a little loose.

                                                So while I understand how people can choose to interpret it the way you do, it is not a clear cut or set in stone interpretation.

                                                I haven't found anything to suggest it is in fact even a more sensible interpretation than mine (or Vitali's), so I won't be changing that (sans a new argument that actually does change my mind ).

                                                        Originally posted by Howalt View Post
                                                        So, does anyone have a cheat-list with the powers you get to lash out at?
                                                        All of Majesty.

                                                        Nightmare 1.

                                                        Obfuscate 3 (and once Cloak of Night is shattered, all uses of Obfuscate are simultaneously shattered).

                                                        You can Lash Out against powers that rely on Predator's Auras that you can see / identify. Majesty, Nightmare and Obfuscate are all powers that can be Lashed Out against from this criteria. Obfuscate 3 is only lashed out against once Auspex spots them. Nightmare powers can be used either subtly (and thus unidentified) or obviously (which involves using Nightmare 1) for more power. And Nightmare 1 can be Lashed Out against, ending the ability.

                                                        In theory, you could Lash Out against Dominate to break it, as that also uses Aura, but every Dominate power makes you passive, and Lashing Out is definietly an agressive move. So, its not quite a valid method in the majority of cases. I suppose you could test it out by ordering the vampire to lash out against your control, but that is a bit of a niche case, unlikely to come up outside of an Ordo experiement.
