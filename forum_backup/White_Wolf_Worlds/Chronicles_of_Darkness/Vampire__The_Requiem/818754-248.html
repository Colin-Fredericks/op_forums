                    Originally posted by Maina View Post
                    Beaten Down and Surrender is optional. Intent is not. Intent should not always include murder, and making Intent clear on both sides can help encourage surrender/fleeing over fights to the death.
                    That.

                    Intent should be the first consideration at the beginning of combat in any RPG, because the idea that every combat is to the death just because the opponent must stop moving is dumb. Defeat isn't that.

                    Any vampire who start their turn having to heal to avoid torpor knows that things aren't ok. Why burn more resources in a fight you already knows you're likely to loose and risk death? No rule is needed for the character to accept this is a good time for surrender, and other gracefully accept it.

                    Even more if you play the social aspect of it. People who push the envelope on violence get a reputation for it, nut this is frequently just a reputation as a dangerous asshole. Even in ancient warfare this was actually an attitude to avoid, not to indulge, and the few occasions of indulgence are historically relevant precisely because of that.

                                                            There's nothing at all barring it from Animalism alone, and it is the whole effect of a level two Discipline, why does you want to ask for more?

                                                            As it stand, the Discipline upkeep alone is more than enough to cover your bases, it already cost as much as a level 5 Merit, IIRC. That will make those dogs far more reliable without being far more powerful, and at the very least protect them with the Sanctity.
