                As the person who made the original comment about the seventh rule, let me post my thoughts - it was originally nothing more than a joking reference to the covenant VII. The original write up has six rules, listed with roman numerals. So, I immediately thought about VII as a result. Nothing more than a guess.

                Could be a clue, could be nothing more than Machine joking back. My thought was that the Namless is an example city where the VII were in power - as in, Ruled by VII. Certainly, I think that VII has the possibility of breaking Theban and effectively hunting down other vampires like the Nameless is. Sure, they're not leaving behind the big VII like usual, but its lack seemed just as suspicious and telling to me. VII now has the possibility of being tied to the God-Machine, so that could be a whole thing as well, explaining the religious references as well.

                As for the string for characters, it could just be gibberish instead of a cypher - how "rule 7" would look if translated to text.

                Honestly, its still nothing more than a half-baked theory. There's something about "divine" magic and crosses that need to be avoided for some reason. There's something about the Rue Saint Paul that I don't understand that either. Why only 4 vampires? That's about the standard party size, but that means that a group of PCs can't meet with even lone NPC vamps when together, making me wonder if there's more behind that number (more god-machine references come to mind, but it could just be me reaching).
