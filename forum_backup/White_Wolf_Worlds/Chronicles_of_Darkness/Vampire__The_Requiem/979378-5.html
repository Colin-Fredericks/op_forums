                                        So to elaborate on why I don't want to connect the Tremere covenant (or subgroup within Ordo Dracul if I go that way) is basically that embraced mages die. I know something different happened with the Tremere vampires in Mage but I feel like the whole thing opens cans of worms I don't really want to deal with in a predominantly vampire game. So, the Tremere from Mage won't exist, but their existence wouldn't be relevant to the game I want to run anyway. If I decide I want to do liches I'll probably call them something else.

                                        I am totally going to crossover, though, although to what extent I'm not sure. I love the idea of Ordo Dracul investigating the splinters (Demon) in Seattle, so that's probably going to be a thing. I also have some ideas for Werewolf and Mage. I'm going to pick up other 2e corebooks as I can and incorporate what I can when I can. But the primary focus will be on vampires and I don't really want any vampire stuff to directly depend on other game lines.
