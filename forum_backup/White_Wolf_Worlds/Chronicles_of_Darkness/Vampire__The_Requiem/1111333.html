    Ordo Dracul Chronicle

    07-04-2017, 01:28 PM
    I'm going to be running a single covenant game within the near future, and most of my players are leaning towards the Ordo Dracul at the moment. We will be using second edition vampire rules, and I was wondering what books from 1e I should make use of (if any).

    Also, should I allow the common coils only (Wyrm, Voivode, and Ascendant) when it comes to the begunning of the Chronicle, and let them learn the more esoteric coils (Zirnitra, Ziva, and Quintessence) later on in the game.

    Also, can people help me with some ideas on how to design Elder coils and scales for each of the mysteries, like how Quintessence is part of the Voivode mystery?

      I think the Ordo Dracul book still has a lot of good setting material, even if the crunch is dated (though the...alchemy Discipline whose name I can't recall might be a good place to mine for Scale inspiration).

      If you want the chronicle to have a higher weirdness quotient, let them take whatever Coils they like; if you want it to be more mysterious, limit the Coils to those in the core at first. Alternatively, if you want to inject a little paranoia between Dragon characters, let players take their Coils privately (whatever they want), and then let them lie about it in-character (if they want).

      As for Elder Coils, I feel like the design principle is that it should expand the scope of the theme...so, an Elder Coil of the Wyrm might let the vampire extend her will over the Beast into a physical manifestation? An Elder Coil of the Ascendant probably doesn't let you walk in sunlight, but maybe it obviates things like Torpor.

                  Some thoughts on the Ordo only game:

                  ~ Nail down how Wyrm Nests work. This is going to be a huge part of your game. You'll want scenes that revolve around using them.

                  ~ Figure out how much of a social challenge you want the Ordo to have. In a regular game, the Ordo Dracul serve as a kind of wild-card in the standard Invictus v. Carthian, Lance v. Circle standoff. In a pure Ordo game, the socialization tends to take a back seat to being vampire-mage types. No, they don't get the magic, but they do have the curiosity. Most of the different social challenges the Ordo Dracul face tend to revolve around getting access to a Wyrm's Nest, be they a social challenge, or a physical one.

                  ~ Make sure to leave lots of room for Sworn of the Axe types and Sworn of the Mystery types. The stereotypical Dragon is going to be an investigative Dying Light type. Try and branch out to make sure you've got lots of things for everyone. Its very easy to do an underground fighting ring in a Wyrm's Nest for the sworn of the axe. That's part of what they do. Nailing down some Sworn of the Mysteries plots for PCs are going to be a bit trickier.

                  ~ The Coil of the Quintessence admits to being part of the Mystery of the Voivode. Think about possibly tying Zirnitra, Ziva to the other Mysteries as well as if they were "advanced" coils. This way, you can also keep a bit of the themes of turning Beast, Stasis and Blood on each other to grow (as per Rites of the Dragon fiction). This also makes using the Ordo Dracul titles slightly easier to use as well.

                  ~ There's lots of rumors about Dracula, two of his missing brides, the Unholy, and more. Think about bringing in some of these rumors and cult-of-personality worship.

                  ~ Keep a close eye on what psychic powers you bring in. Its very easy for Zirnitra to become the uber-coil, and have psychic powers replace disciplines and do everything that way. I know of a number of games that just flat out banned Zirnitra because of how quickly it went off the rails.

                  ~ Potentially think about devising some Coils or Scales or abilities for dealing with ghosts, or how regular disciplines interact. The Ordo v. haunted house is a pretty common plot.

                  ~ Other potential plot include investigating the Jaing Shi or other Night Horror: Wicked Dead alternate vampires. There's hints of the Jiang Shi in core book, and we're getting some more on dhampir in the upcoming ghoul book. Have fun with non-Kindred vampire types popping up to investigate. Think about awarding blood beats for finding them and using them as part of the Ordo Dracul's attempts to learn coils / scales (see core book for more info on blood beats).

                  ~ Strix. Many don't like them, but the Ordo Dracul is ripe for bringing them in as "demons." By demons, I mean to make deals for information / mystic ability, for temptation, and more. The Ordo Dracul have a proscritption against becoming the servant of an angel or demon, and the strix are perfect to fit that place. Its also possible to bring in hints of the Belial's Brood in with the strix, perhaps making up Vestments that could be mistaken for Coils as part of the slippery slide.

                              Originally posted by Ever Professional View Post

                              I am trying to figure out how Wyrm's Nests work in general, so any help there is appreciated.
                              Sure. At the most basic, "wyrm nest" is just a fancy phrase for generic "place of power." Most commonly enough, people tend to use a bit of crossover - ley line nodes/hallows, spirit locus or haunted houses.

                              Once you have the location, something about it somehow makes it easier to develop a coil when you perform a certain kind of "ritual." Back in 1e, when we had Buddhist and mesmerism as methods of learning the Coils, I just used whatever resonance was appropriate and said it happened. Now? The Ordo Dracul is absolutely more blood alchemy / pseudo-science-medice based as a whole instead. So, I tend to propose using the equivalent of tass in each location. I don't know if you're familiar with the idea, but tass is a concept in Mage that, somehow, mana takes on physical form. This can be as a sacred spring that, when drunk, gives you mana, or perhaps an aloe plant you use to rub on your skin, etc.

                              What this means for the Ordo Dracul? I personally run things such that the Wyrm Nests always have some kind of physical manifestation of the place's energy, and the Defiant take said manifestation, crush it up with blood, and ... apply it somehow. Bath, drink, etc. Drug treatments, in short.

                                                  Originally posted by Ever Professional View Post
                                                  I've played a bit of mage, so I know about Tass a little bit. That's probably a good way to work with it; most of the answers I've gotten about it in the past haven't really helped.
                                                  Well, its always been extremely vague and hand waved. Details sorely lacking. There's really no answer, because its always been kind of open ended. 1e did have the Wyrm Nest rituals, but that was basically stealing mage-like rituals, adapting them, shoving the energy into a human, then eating the human. You could probably make a Scale to do the same thing.

                                                  So, should I check the Ordo Dracul book put for the alchemy part, because I don't remember it being described all that much in the we core book.
                                                  Not really there either. When I say alchemy, I'm just talking about how scales work, and the focus on pseudo-scientific processes that the Ordo use. When using a scale, you create a mixture of blood and other chemicals and inject it, or bathe in it, etc. Or you use sleep deprivation with sun lamps. Etc. Its all coated with a veneer of mystical-science mix. Alchemy is the most familiar term I use for that kind of theme.

                                                  Back in 1e, blood alchemy was actually just a bunch of devotions for making what amounts to witchy potions that stored disciplines. You had to buy up a discipline that was a glorified curse power, then buy devotions that mixed the two. In 2e, you're better off just developing a scale.

                                                  Scales were really great inventions to cover all the miscelanous abilities of the Ordo Dracul that were back in 1e.
