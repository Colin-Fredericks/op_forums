                                            One Vitae needs to be spent as regular, while the remaining Vitae cost is spilt by either the ritualist or supporters. That means ghouls can perform single dot rituals without help (as they only cost 1 Vitae), and all rituals regardless of dot rating if they have a vampire assistant to spill the remaining Vitae.

                                            Anyone else is shit out of luck, though, since they can't spend that initial Vitae to power the ritual. Nothing bars them from learning Crúac, though, if they wish to teamwork their resident Acolyte's rituals with the Altar Merit.
