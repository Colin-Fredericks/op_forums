    [2E] Frenzy Question: Insults?

    06-04-2015, 09:22 AM
    So I didn't get into Vampire until Blood & Smoke/2E. Like, at all, except for V:TM - Bloodlines back in the day (and every couple of years when I want to play a great game). So when I started running a 2E game, I read through the Frenzy rules and thought I had them down.

    However, some more vampire-savvy friends were asking me about why, when they called a bunch of vampires in a room vermin, nobody frenzied. I stared at them like they were asking why it rains sometimes and said, "Because vampires aren't going to Frenzy just because you're a dick?"

    Except when I read through the book again, and noticed a couple of lines "Insulted by a superior" and "Insulted by an inferior". Which seems to back up his assertion that insults provoke Frenzy. If so, then I've been running my games wrong*. In my defense, the book is very shallow about what provokes Frenzy. It's: "When the vampire faces danger, hunger, or threat". And then later for situational modifiers "if the vampire's rival has made a point of goading her every time they meet, it may impose an additional -1 die penalty to the roll."

    *: Just kidding, there's no such thing as running a game wrong. But I'll still feel like I've got egg on my face.

      I think it would depend on the insult. Personally, I wouldn't call for a frenzy check for simple namecalling (maybe if it's a deeply offensive slur). It's got to be something that seriously degrades, humiliates, or enrages the character to count for a Fenzy check in my opinion, and what kind of insult triggers that is going to be different for each character.

                              With a few exceptions the frenzy triggers are left up to the discretion of the ST, with input from the player. Being insulted or embarrassed isn't a hard and fast thing. It varies depending on individuals and circumstances.

                              If you want to model goading someone into frenzy mechanically, you could use extended social actions (Charisma+Intimidation/Persuasion, minus target's Composure). Each roll is a turn of hurling insults. When you accumulate the target's Resolve (maybe Willpower if you want it to be more difficult to accomplish) in successes, they roll to resist frenzy.

It could work with some modification, but I like an extended action as a model of what's happening in absence of a more complex tweak of SM.
