    Somatics: A single physical discipline

    08-27-2016, 03:20 PM
    So, I know some (at least I am) are unimpressed by the new physical disciplines. Sure, they're better than they were in 1st edition, but still require improving in a linear fashion by spending dots one after another to improve the singular effect (well, several different effects, but always the same ones). It's still less impressive than most other options you could spend your Experience on.

    I decided, as such, to create Somatics, a single physical discipline which provides all the benefits of the three physical disciplines and then some, making Kindred that buy it more physically powerful than they'd otherwise be. It's a little potent, as even the individual powers get a slight boost in some cases, to last longer, but the idea is that it makes Kindred more of a physical force by default. I also snuck in a flight type ability, too, but made it more limited than, say, using Protean to fly long-term as a bat.

    That being said, I'm open to input, keeping in mind it's MEANT to be pretty potent. Still, I'm considering if maybe I want to switch Aviation and Celerity around, depending how potent people think the ability to fly and a Dex increase is, compared to Celerity's fairly large improvements.

    In case it need be said, if you don't think the physical disciplines are underpowered, then that's your right to think, but this thread isn't meant for you, just don't use the discipline.
