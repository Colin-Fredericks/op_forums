            Originally posted by Newb95 View Post
            I think I will use the rules in reap the whirlwind at this point
            The rules in Reap the Whirlwind are the same in the revised version as in the original. It says that you can use any object you can lift as an improvised melee or throwing weapon, and the book's section on Attributes describes Strength as "How much your character can lift, and how hard he hits."

            Given that the text did not change at all between publishings and "getting hit by a truck" is specifically given as an example of the sort of massive bodily destruction that vampires still suffer lethal damage from, we can safely conclude that they did not put a stealth erratum in the second publishing of the quickstart book for VtR2e just because the wording was less precise.

            This is a "book was designed before they were allowed to do a formalized Second Edition" artifact, kind of like how the rules for Fatigue weren't in every book and a couple of slightly different Conditions filled the role instead. Vampires throwing cars is still an impressive feat rather than something any schmuck with Vigor 1 can pull off.

                                                        Originally posted by espritdecalmar View Post
                                                        Can a victim be reduced to zero Composure through Mortal Terror? If so, what happens if that threshold is hit?
                                                        I vaguely recall reading mechanics somewhere in 1e that an Attribute reduced to 0 prevented you from performing any tasks that required that Attribute, but I don't remember where I saw that, I wouldn't know where to begin looking, and it might have been unofficial content.

                                                        In the context of 2e, since no mechanics are provided for Attribute ratings below 1 besides the Strix's rules for decaying corpse hosts, a rating of 1 represents below-average talent in an Attribute, and Mortal Terror already applies a Condition that also penalizes Resolve, I think it's safe to run the Dread bonus as capped at reducing the victim to Composure 1.
