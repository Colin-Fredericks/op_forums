          There's also the new Pack Alpha Merit, which seems to have largely superseded the old Pack-Minded Merit (enough so that I did red-text Pack-Minded in my directory).

          Mind, the most difficult part of getting assorted splatbook Merits, Devotions, etc to work properly with Blood & Smoke will be those that revolve around Virtue and/or Vice. After all, the themes of the assorted Disciplines are still the same.

          That said, the Of Rose and Thorn Merit is a very specific exception, since it modifies each level of a Discipline. The first dot, Feral Whispers, could still work as advertised. The second dot, Obedience, has been merged into Feral Whispers; one route that might work for this would be to simply keep it the same as old for the Merit, working from the theory that it's just that much harder to get something as alien as a plant to hew to a command; another option would be to go ahead and use the new Raise the Familiar power to, in essence, fortify a dead tree with which the vampire can meld (via Protean 1) knowing that the thing is going to be particularly difficult to harm, and it may gain some nasty thorns (in semblance to the fangs that animals can get from the power). Call of the Wild is similar enough to Summon the Hunt to still work as advertised. The real problem comes at the fourth dot; Feral Infection is more or less akin to the old Leashing the Beast, which simply wasn't represented in Of Rose and Thorn. The best I could recommend there is for the fourth dot to allow melding with the tree via Protean 1 without a vitae cost, as can now be done with soil but not other material; since the vitae cost now is the Durability of the material, this would a pretty significant benefit, potentially worthy of that fourth dot. And there's no fifth dot to the Merit, which neatly saves trying to find a way to work Lord of the Land into it.

          Mind, this is best-guess territory.

                              Oh crap, I forgot I was going to do that. I'll get it posted asap.

                              EDIT: Here we go. I made it a series of single-Discipline Devotions instead of a Merit.

                              Of Rose and Thorn (Animalism 1)
                              The vampire with this Devotion can use Animalism to “talk to” and command plants, as well as animals. Such interaction is difficult because plants do not have conscious thought, nor do they communicate in the same way animals do, but the complex combinations of pheromones they give off, and their meanings, can be learned just like an animal’s body language.
                              Cost: None
                              Dice Pool: Wits + Science + Animalism
                              Action: Instant
                              Duration: Scene, command persists until carried out.

                              Roll Results

                              Dramatic Failure: The plant misunderstands the vampire’s command, growing contrary to the vampire’s instruction.
                              Failure: The vampire is unable to communicate with the plant.
                              Success: The vampire can communicate with a plant, just as she would an animal with Feral Whispers. She can also use commands to influence the plant’s growth, though such commands are limited by the nature of the plant. Roses can be made to bloom in a variety of different colors and vines can be directed to grow in strange patterns, but an apple tree will never bear oranges. With the Beast’s guidance, however, plants do grow at three times their normal rate until they have grown into the commanded state.
                              Exceptional Success: As with a success, but the plant is able to grow at six times its normal rate instead.
                              This Devotion costs 1 experience to learn.

                              Mandrake Familiar (Animalism 2)
                              By applying her knowledge of Rose and Thorn to the process of raising animal familiars, the vampire can cultivate undead plants that live on Vitae.
                              Cost: 1 Vitae
                              Requirement: “Water” the plant with 1 Vitae (included in cost).
                              Dice Pool: None
                              Action: Instant
                              Duration: (Blood Potency * plant’s size) nights
                              The vampire tends the plant, “watering” it with her own blood. Enhanced with the properties of the vampire’s Vitae, the plant gains something resembling a vampire’s immortality. It no longer needs sunlight, water, fertilizer, or anything but the vampire’s Vitae to survive. However, if the vampire does not renew this power regularly, the plant will wilt and die.
                              If she has Of Rose and Thorn, the vampire can use it to communicate with and command the plant across any distance. Additionally, she can command it to grow in strange ways that natural plants cannot. It is still restricted to growing actual plant matter, however; it cannot bear human heads instead of fruit, but it can bloom strange and exotic flowers, or flow with poisonous or hallucinogenic sap. The mandrake also gains a limited capacity for autonomous locomotion. Left to its own devices, it will behave much like a carnivorous plant, remaining motionless until potential prey approaches and then attempting to injure it and absorb its blood through its roots, but the vampire can issue commands for it to move in specific ways if she so desires.
                              This Devotion costs 1 experience to learn.

                              Sow the Fields (Animalism 3)
                              By the same process that allows a vampire to carry a command to many animals over great distances, a master of Rose and Thorn can compel large numbers of plants to grow in an area.
                              Cost: 2 Vitae
                              Requirement: Spill 1 Vitae upon the soil (included in cost).
                              Dice Pool: Intelligence + Science + Animalism
                              Action: Instant

                              Roll Results

                              Dramatic Failure: The ground becomes temporarily infertile. Any plant life already growing there will wilt and die, and it will not grow new plants for a full month.
                              Failure: The plants fail to grow
                              Success: The vampire can chose if she wants to grow a specific kind of plant, otherwise the affected area will begin to grow with various kinds of carnivorous plants. The Vitae she spills carries through the soil for up to (successes * 100) yards (successes * 90 meters) and seeds it with her will. The chosen type of plant will begin to grow throughout the affected area at triple their normal growth rate until it is overrun. If the vampire has Of Rose and Thorn, she can also include a single command, which all of the plants grown with this power will carry out.
                              Exceptional Success: As with a success, but the plants are able to grow at six times their normal rate instead.
                              This Devotion costs 1 experience to learn.

                                      I once merged the Nburu discipline with Of Rose and Thorn for an Adroanzi NPC in my campaign, as I thought they occupied about the same design space. These rules assume that the rules for creating Mandragora from the Ghouls book are also used, and are meant to synergize with them. I am not yet totally happy with the discipline, as it is a bit bloated, so comments are welcome.


                                      Nburu

                                      The Adroanzi have always seen themselves as gods of nature. While ancient history holds the secrets of how and why the bloodline came into being, their defining power has survived throughout time. Nburu is the discipline that allows their control over nature. This discipline not only has a specific set of powers, but a Gardener’s understanding of it also improves his chances of successfully cultivating mandragora.

                                      If character is brought into the Bloodline, all his Animalism Discipline dots turns into Nburu. A character cannot have both the Animalism and Nburu Discipline at the same time. It is possible though, to buy Animalism powers as Nburu devotions (or vice versa) after tasting the blood of someone who has the Discipline. These devotions require a Nburu rating of one higher than the Animalism power (or vice versa) and cost 2 XP per devotion.


                                      • Cultivate

                                      Normal gardeners talk to plants to make them grow. But when Adroanzi talk to plants, they do not only respond, but grow as commanded. This power does not normally work on Mandragora.

                                      Cost: None
                                      Dice Pool: Manipulation + Survival + Nburu
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Duration: Until the task is complete

                                      Dramatic Failure: The plant misunderstands the vampire, giving false information when the vampire asks, or growing in a different way than the vampire wanted.
                                      Failure: The plant does not respond to the questions or commands of the Lacrimist.
                                      Success: The Adroanzi is able to speak to a plant. This is no easy conversation. Plants “think” in alien, inscrutable ways – sometimes simple, other times woefully complex. A Lacrimist might be able to learn who was in her garden, what the plant hungers for or if it is suitable to become a Mandragora, but will have no luck discerning elements of time from flora. The Adroanzi can also command a plant to grow in a certain way, and somewhat quickly. He can demand that it bloom. He can force it to produce nectar. He can stir a vine to climb a wall, slippery moss to spread across a stone path, or the branches of a tree to grow together so that visibility is limited to nearly nothing. Given the commands thusly, foliage does grow at thrice its normal “growth rate” until its task is complete.
                                      Exceptional Success: The plant delivers exceptional information, or the growth rate of the plant is five times its normal rate.


                                      •• Unnatural Graft

                                      With this power, the Adroanzi can graft two plants together, adding part of one plant to another other. Most Gardeners use this to add branches with vicious thorns to plants they want to turn into Mandragora to make it easier for them to catch unsuspecting prey, or to add a reservoir for Lacrima to accumulate. This power does not normally work on Mandragora.

                                      Cost: None
                                      Dice Pool: Dexterity + Survival + Nburu
                                      Action: Instant

                                      Dramatic Failure: The plant dies within hours.
                                      Failure: The graft does not "take" automatically and the plant is weakened. Both plants will need to be kept alive for a few weeks for the graft to take. If the graft is unnatural, the plant will probably die.
                                      Success: In case of of a natural graft, it takes instantly. In case of an unnatural graft, both plants will need to be kept alive for a few weeks, for the graft to take.
                                      Exceptional Success: The graft takes automatically, even if it is unnatural.


                                      ••• Synthesis

                                      Adroanzi developed this ability to not only meld two plants together perfectly to benefit their garden, but also to meld themselves with the largest of plants to stalk prey, watch their mortal charges and hide from superior enemies. Typically, trees or larger cacti are the most appropriate candidates for this application of the power, which bears some resemblance to the Protean power Unmarked Grave.

                                      Cost: 1 Vitae (or more)
                                      Dice Pool: None
                                      Action: Instant

                                      At its most basic, the Adroanzi can now use Cultivate and Unnatural Graft on Mandragora at no additional cost.
                                      By spending an extra point of Vitae while activating Cultivate or Unnatural Graft she can now completely meld two plants together, resulting in an unnatural new kind of plant that is unable to reproduce:
                                      • When used with Cultivate, the vampire can merge a seed of one plant with an existing plant and then command the plant to manifest new elements, depending on the seed merged: a blood-red maple tree may bloom roses, the grass beneath one’s feet may manifest thorns, a thick hanging vine may be infused with medicinal or hallucinogenic properties to humans (or to Damned who drink the blood of those humans). Once again, growing such elements occurs at a growth rate of thrice its expected speed.
                                      • When used with Unnatural Graft, the vampire can take a large part of one plant, and graft it perfectly onto another, literally merging the plants. This obviates the need for waiting until the new elements grow and also enables to vampire to create otherwise impossible grafts. It is also possible to first use Cultivate normally to make plants intertwine before merging them, but this obviously eliminates the speed advantage.
                                      • As an added benefit, the vampire can now create hardier plants, to increase the chance of successfully turning them into Mandragora to almost hundred percent.

                                      Alternatively, the Adroanzi can use spend one Vitae to meld into a plant that is at least the same size as he is. This necessitates no roll. The plant may be either dead, alive or a Mandragora. While he is melded with the plant, the character’s senses are extended from the plant, providing a full 360 degree range of sight, hearing, touch, etc. These senses extend throughout the entire plant. For example, if he melds with a tree that is taller than a nearby wall, he would be able to see over the wall from the vantage point of the tree’s taller branches. As his sense of touch extends throughout the tree, he could feel people climbing or damaging it. His Kindred senses also continue to function, and he remains capable to sense the predatory aura, even though another vampire's predatory aura can't force her into a fight-or-flight reaction. If the plant is a Mandragora, the Vampire can even absorb blood or Vitae that is spilled on the plant, or the ground around it. The plant dilutes the Vitae enough that it can't result in a blood bond.
                                      The only way to harm the vampire is to damage or destroy the plant he resides in; he takes one point of bashing damage for each point of Structure that the plant loses. When he takes his first point of Lethal damage, he must emerge. At the end of the scene, the character is immediately expelled from the plant. He may end the power at any time, exiting the plant as an instant action. Alternately, he may quickly exit the plant as an element of surprise or to be able to quickly escape. Exiting from the plant in this fashion is a reflexive action and costs one additional Vitae.


                                      •••• Dark Spirit of the Forest

                                      The Adroanzi terrified the tribes they commanded, as well as their enemies, by forming a reputation for springing seemingly from nowhere. No matter where their targets would hide and no matter how fast they could travel, they were vulnerable to these dark gods of the land. Without any warning, the Adroanzi could step out from behind a tree next to a terrified victim, convincing even the most seasoned chieftain that there simply was no escape from these lords of the night.

                                      Cost: 1 Willpower
                                      Dice Pool: None
                                      Action: Instant

                                      The Adroanzi can now use Synthesis and stay melded with a plant indefinitely, enabling him to sleep within a plant and stay within his garden during the day without problems.
                                      Furthermore, by spending a Willpower point he may instantly transport himself from the plant he is inside of to any other plant he can clearly and directly see that would be a suitable candidate for the Synthesis power. It is also possible to transport himself to one Mandragora he created for this express purpose that is possibly outside of visual range (often the central tree of his garden) .
                                      Finally, if the plant the vampire melded with is a Mandragora, he can also animate the plant for a scene by spending a Willpower point. The plant may only move slowly, but this can still be used as an attack using the Strength of the plant, if the target is close. For example he can use the roots of a tree to trip someone, the branches to entangle someone, or thorns to cut someone. A patch of ivy has the equivalent of Strength 2, while an old oak might have a Strength of 10 or more. If the target is aware, he may apply his Defense against this attack. If the plant has thorns, the Vampire can also drink the blood of the target via the plant after a successful grapple. Attacks made through the plant always go last in the initiative order.


                                      ••••• Vengeance of the Garden

                                      The vampire sprinkles the ground of her garden with Vitae, much in the same way in which she would normally create Mandragora. Once she has established her garden this way, the vampire gains greater control over the plants and is privy to everything that happens within it.

                                      Cost: 3-9 Vitae and 1 Willpower
                                      Requirement: The vampire must sprinkle Vitae all through his garden. Three Vitae is enough to
                                      secure a small garden, six can secure a small park the size of two football fields, while
                                      nine is enough to claim a large park.
                                      Dice Pool: None
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Duration: One week per dot of Blood Potency, longer if the vampire remains in his territory.

                                      The vampire marks her garden. It remains empowered for at least one week per dot of Blood Potency, though the effects of this Discipline only end when the vampire leaves her territory after the duration.
                                      When the vampire is within her garden, she can feel when a person or animal crosses into her territory; if an invader is a vampire, she knows his general Blood Potency. She always has a rough idea of where everyone is within her domain, and only supernatural means such as Disciplines can cut through her awareness — triggering a Clash of Wills.
                                      As the undisputed master of her garden, the vampire's use of Cultivate on the plants in her garden is always successful. She can also use Cultivation on all plants in the garden, or merge intertwined plants with Synthesis, from anywhere in the garden. Finally, Dark Spirit of the Forest can now be used to transport herself to any other plant in his garden that meets the requirements, and also to animate all the Mandragora in his garden at the same time, even while he is not melded with any.
                                      When the garden is animated, each victim might suffer multiple penalties, like a –2 reduction to Speed due to grasses and weeds pulling at its feet or –1 penalty to other rolls while being accosted, depending on the action and how much vegetation is present. Furthermore, each victim may be the subject of one attack, grapple or trip from nearby vegetation per turn. If a grapple is successfully initiated and the target immobilized, the plant will feed, inflicting 1 Lethal per turn (or making him lose one Vitae, in case of a vampire). Alternatively, anywhere from one to three Bashing damage (as determined by the Storyteller) may be inflicted automatically, based on how dense the local foliage is. This damage ignores armor and a subject’s Defense.


                                      Devotions

                                      Silent Passage (Nburu •, Obfuscate •)

                                      Adroanzi, being self-proclaimed masters of nature, reinforce their mystery by use of this power. They can move through the wilderness without fear of being heard or discovered. Even animals have trouble finding evidence of a Gardener’s presence.

                                      Cost: 1 Vitae
                                      Dice Pool: None, enhances Face in the Crowd
                                      Action: None
                                      Duration: None

                                      Silent Passage enhances Face in the Crowd. To have effect, the vampire must be walking through grass, on soil, through water, or across a naturally occurring surface. The power does not function if the character is walking across concrete, wading in a constructed fiberglass-bottom pool, or traveling on or over any sort of similarly worked or artificial surface. The power lasts for the duration of the scene.
                                      First, he is virtually silent when walking or even running. Observers using Heightened Sense potentially hear the character, but this requires a successful Clash of Wills (Blood and Smoke, p. 125). Users of this power also mask evidence in regards to scents. This can be subject to a Clash of Wills as well. Finally, Silent Passage also hides evidence of the vampire’s passing. He does not leave tracks in the sand, break the grass as he walks through, or disturb the surface of water as he is walking or swimming. In the case of traveling through water, the character may move no faster than his normal walking speed. Moving any faster will disrupt this power and cause it to fail. Tracks and other evidence are not completely hidden, but any roll made to track or investigate the area receives a -5 modifier. Each roll takes one round, and the observer must roll more successes than the vampire’s Wits + Stealth + Obfuscate or Nburu, whichever is higher.

                                      This Devotion costs 1 Experience to learn.


                                      Defiled Presence (Nburu ••, Vigor •)

                                      Centuries ago, when the Adroanzi were lording over the tribes of the Lugbara, they used a number of tactics to keep their mortal followers obedient. From time to time, the chieftains and their people needed to be punished. The most despicable of Adroanzi’ punishments struck the people indirectly. Without the land and all that grew from it, the people likely starved and were deprived of materials for building their homes. With this power, the Adroanzi could destroy swaths of smaller plants, kill trees and insects, or even render tools useless.

                                      Cost: 1 Vitae
                                      Dice Pool: Strength + Survival + Nburu
                                      Action: Instant

                                      Success: If the character was attempting to destroy larger amounts of insects and/or smaller plants such as grasses, weeds, flowers, small shrubs, and the like, he kills all of the plant life and insects in a radius of one yard per success. Larger plants, such as trees, that are within the affected area are not harmed (though they may show signs of disease or rot). If the character was attempting to destroy a single larger object, the object loses one point of Structure per success rolled. He must touch this object. This bypasses the object’s Durability.
                                      Exceptional Success: No additional damage is inflicted, but anything destroyed is utterly gone. The plants or object becomes little more than ashes, as if it was completely burned or decomposed.
                                      .
                                      Modifier Situation
                                      -1 Per point of an object’s Size that exceeds the user’s Nburu rating.
                                      -2 The target object has been worked into its form (baseball bat, door).
                                      -2 The character is unable to directly touch the object (wearing shoes or gloves).
                                      -4 The target object has been manufactured (cotton shirt, leather jacket).
                                      .
                                      This Devotion costs 2 Experiences to learn.


                                      Hungry Swarms (Nburu •••)

                                      One of the most terrifying abilities of the Adroanzi, they can summon nearby insects and imbue them with a thirst for blood capable of killing small animals as well as placing human sized creatures in grave danger. With the first taste of the vampire’s blood, swarms of ants, spiders, mosquitoes, or anything capable of hunting for prey swarms their commanded target, eager to consume it whole.

                                      Cost: 2 Vitae
                                      Dice Pool: Presence + Survival + Nburu
                                      Action: Instant
                                      Duration: Successes in turns

                                      Success: The insects follow the user’s command and swarm the victim. See below for details.
                                      Exceptional Success: As with a success, but the insects can be directed to attack multiple targets.

                                      The Adroanzi initiates this power by shedding his own blood (usually through cutting his hand) and flinging it on the ground or in the air around him. This immediately summons any available insects to him, where they can then be directed by the user of this power. Once focused onto a specific target, they cannot be redirected (though the vampire can use the power again on another target). They simply continue to swarm the victim until the effect is ended.

                                      Once Hungry Swarms is activated, the target takes one point of lethal damage (if living) or loses one Vitae (in the case of a vampire) each turn. Additionally, the swarms obscure the victim’s senses enough to reduce his effectiveness in anything he attempts. All of the victim’s dice pools are reduced by two as long as the power is in effect.

                                      If the vampire marks the target using his Vitae, instead of flinging it, the swarm will continue to attack until the mark is removed or the night passes, at which point the Vitae burns away as if it was never present.
                                      .
                                      Modifier Situation
                                      +2 Character uses the power within his garden.
                                      -3 Character uses the power in an urban setting.
                                      -5 Character uses the power within a maintained building.
                                      .
                                      This Devotion costs 2 Experiences to learn.
