                        Clan: Nosferatu

                        So I've mentioned in the Werewolf and Changeling threads that I am most likely a Cahalith and would most likely be taken and shifted into a Darkling. Both are true, and Vampire oddly illustrates it best with the fact I'd be a Nosferatu. I wouldn't say that of the x-splats the Nossies represent me best-if anything a Fairest Shadowsoul might do that best, and the only reason I don't put more in that is the way I'd react in Fairest conditions, off the side tangent now Arc- but they are surprisingly good in the mix of noise and silence. When I want your attention, I'll get it. When I want to fade into the background, it may be a little more work but I can do that work easily enough. I also have a macabre streak, and an emphasis on never neglecting, denying the ugly parts of life-on a positive spin I make a point of looking at the ugly to find something beautiful and uplifting about it, which doesn't hurt the Nossies. Point is that the creepy, the dark, and the horrific have a place in my hands and in my life. Unsurprisingly, that does make me a bit of an outsider, and I have lost track of the number of times I felt like the outsider looking in.

                        Covenant: The Carthian Movement

                        Truth be told, while I enjoy several covenants as a player, I'm less thrilled with the prospect of personally joining them. Still, it's hard to deny the benefits they offer, and employment beats being unaligned on a lot of fronts-at least someone should theoretically care if the hunters smear my face on the wall. Still, why the Carthians? I'm not politically minded, and what little I am leans towards moderation between two sides. I'm not sure that vampire society needs the sort of revolution they talk about. So why them? Well, first off, change as a principle. I'm not sure if the popular modus operandi of vampires is the right way for it to work, but I can definitely say that even if it largely works there are details that definitely need work. Even if it doesn't, someone needs to be asking the questions, re-evaluating what works and what doesn't. I'm a natural devil's advocate, and even if I don't want to overthrow the current model, I'm definitely willing to challenge it and see if it deserves to stand. Second, Negotiation. The Carthians occupy a neat niche where they can actually mediate for all the other covenant-they aren't exactly a neutral party, but they can certainly have members who can be, and I can very easily be one of those people-hell, I am. My willingness to question and work for a better answer mixed in with this aspect of the Carthians means I could live quite happily trying to work the angles of society to get them to work better. I might be a softcore revolutionary, but simply put their need to challenge and improve society appeals to me.

                        Bloodline: None

                        Disciplines: Majesty 3, Nightmare 4, Auspex, Vigor 5, Celerity 5, Obfuscate 3

                        Before I even touch on the Nosferatu powers, I'd have to learn at least some of Majesty. I'm sorry, I'm vain. I'm selfish. To be able to live in the spotlight? To get attention? Sign me up. I'm not sure how far I'd go with it though. Getting your foot in the door is one thing, and creating inclinations to like you is okay, but how far do I go before I feel like I'm really just violating people? I want a hand up, not a hand holding everyone's heart.

                        Of the Nossie powerset I'd probably go with Vigor...maybe not first, but definitely fullest. I'm not very strong in real life, and that has always bothered me a little bit. I don't build muscle easily, either, so to magically become more potent is appealing. The vast amount I could do with that is nifty, plus it's the least ethically challenging. I think for that reason Nightmare would also grow a lot-truth be told, I don't really want to hurt people, and if I could just get people to back down, that would be lovely. Of course, there's a line to be drawn-how far do you scare a person before you're being unfair, before it becomes cruel? Still, Nightmare would probably grow a lot, even if I felt guilty about some of the higher power. Obfuscate is the first one I thought I'd raise high as possible, and the first one I immediately back down. Just as I want the ability to grab people's attention, positively or negatively, I also want the ability to just disappear. But do I want to really be invisible? Do I need the temptation of invisible knives and guns? I have no problem lying to people, but words are different from looking like the person others want to see most-could I really trust myself with that power. I couldn't resist building it up, but I'd be five kinds of worried about doing wrong with it.

                        So onto something a little lighter, I love speed. I love velocity, I love going fast. I get ecstatic at high speeds. Add in Vigor's addition to distance to throw in freefall, and good lord I might get shot for breaching the masquerade just by freerunning. Still, how could I resist?!

                        Auspex has some clearer lines of use for me than the other morally ambiguous powers. I debate Majesty, Nightmare, and Obfuscate at the third dot(maybe even earlier), but Auspex clearly cuts off after that. I'm fairly good at reading people and scenarios, but it never hurts to know more. Still, of the six I'd seek after, Auspex is probably the one I'd go after the least-while I as a player love it, I wouldn't be as crazy about it in life, and quite frankly the entire thing would probably just make me uber paranoid, and I don't want to be that guy.

                                                Clan: Nosferatu

                                                This is an interesting one for me. Because, to be blunt about myself; while on the surface I am all smiles and joy, under my skin I am fully aware of how manipulative I can be. I know what words to say and what I can do to get exactly what I want - and more than that, I've gotten it down to a very fine art and I know that it works. I am often the one people come to if they need support and I am the one most often told secrets to because of this. I am not so quiet and withdrawn as to be a Mekhet, however - and the likes of ancient history and forbidden knowledge would have little interest to me. Certainly not enough to make a Requiem out of. Which then turns me towards The Nosferatu.

                                                While yes I am social, I am only social to the point I wish to be. Neither am I squeamish about gore (except eyes and nails >>) or the darker parts of existence. If people knew more of me I would likely be a social outcast rather than the somewhat-butterfly I truly am (due to hobbies and the like). And yet the idea of knowing things and having leverage is deeply appealing - but again, not in the same way it is for a Mekhet. If I had the choice I would be unchanged from how I am now - my Curse would be an aura of weird less than any deformity. To be frank, for all I am not exactly a beautiful person I am currently capable of acting in society. I'd like that to continue, even if I make people uneasy.

                                                Covenant: Unaligned

                                                For all I play many characters whom are fine with the Covenants, none of them truly stand out as something I would wish to join. I am at heart a survivor - enough not to put myself in the firing line by attacking the system with the Carthians or being the pawn of some Invictus Elder whom does not care for my life. Neither am I religious - on either end - and I would not wish to meddle too much in The Dragons less I mess something up for myself.

                                                Bloodline: None

                                                Disciplines: Animalism 3, Vigor 4, Nightmare 4, Obfuscate 3, Celerity 2

                                                So these are fairly straight forward for me; Animalism is because I love animals. I'd probably not go too far into the tree if only because talking to them, being able to command them if needs must and summon them to my aid. That and, well, it's always good to have pets!

                                                Vigor because, unfortunately, my muscles in real life are poor. I suffer from some conditions which weaken my joints and have messed my muscles up. The idea of having the strength to rip a man in two is rather intoxicating. It would let me do things I could never do in life - and, if I have to fight, beat the other guy to the point he doesn't try again!

                                                Nightmare... well, it's useful. As I have said, I can manipulate to get my own way but I have never been one to manipulate someone in that way. I hope I never would - this would, again, be nothing more than insurance. That if you come after me, I can break your mind in two - it is either me or you and I will never chose you over my own life. Just as I'd quickly learn not to do with other Kindred.

                                                What better way of gathering information and getting around than simply being invisible? Being able to hide things is a wonderful gift - or stalk people and break their necks, if I had to. I'd prefer not to, but again everything has a reason.

                                                And finally, celerity. To put it simply; I can get the hell out of dodge quickly. Or close in on someone. It'd vary, I imagine!
