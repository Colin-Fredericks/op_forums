    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post

    Okay, but if Mother of Archbishop Prince is one of this 'Dark Succubus' Deava, what does it mean? I would suspect she would be much quicker on Final Death, as she:
    1. Was much older to begin with ( probably Embraced in late Middle Ages ).
    2. As 'Dark Succubus' she travels - a lot. And travel means danger for Kindred. When Archbishop Prince is more or less in the same domain all the time
    The character concept you just described wouldn't need to move around, the second point is a non sequitur. Being a dark succbuss does not require much moving in that era of France"s history, and is indeed quantifying the dark succubus must also be a traveler is inappropriate for such a concept during that time, as travel was uncommon for vampires at that period.

    Lets assume she is competent, politically adept, far from stupid, and thus not dead for simple recklessness. She did embraced a historic figure of some note after all, and that wouldn't be an easy feat to claim such a childer. She'd do quite well to willingly blood bind herself to stable Invictus regimes, who'd be blood bound in turn to his sire or liege. Her not holding blood bonds over those near her is wise as that leads to less resentment over continual control, which would lead some to want to kill her for her interference. Thus keeping her childer far away protects her and them,. While being bound herself is the ultimate seduction, someone willing to be at your mercy, willing to devote themselves to you, can be powerfully motivating for holding the person in great value and prizing them, especially if they are not jealous and don't require you to be exclusive to them.

    Now add daeva level wanton sexy times, powerful political backing, and raw power of an elder vampire, and I'd expect your hypothetical archbishop prince's sire to be doing quite well for herself.

    Invictus have the code for beating the need for blood as Elders, willing blood bonds through fealty chains. Non-Invictus can tap into that, and those bound to those which are have the same benefits but a step removed in the web of loyalty, with Lance sire-childer blood bonds making the ancients able to accepr blood tithes from any member of the Lance et Sanctum.

    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
    What Vassel to Invictus? Archbishop is highest Lancea et Sanctum in region. It's more like Invictus need to answer to him - or rather try to work with him and not make him angry.
    I was not talking about the arch bishop, the question that I said was raised was about the arch bishops sire... as above^


    I realize this is not a simple a to b solution but rather a complex web of interconnected relationships. But it is more plausible than simple answers because it mirrors real life politics and history in complexity.

    Remember: Blood Bonds make you immune to the Blood Bonds of others, they are not something to avoid but to be maintained. Why not be loyal to the person who gave you eternity and plenty of personal space by sending you out as a missionary? Sounds like a sweet deal. Elders are lucky to have blood bond to those they can trust. Who better to trust thank someone who isn't around?

    The old world (in the classic Europe vs Americas sense, not the game lines sense) should have ancient vampires at the top, with only major cleansing events causing renewal (massacres by hunters, infighting, or strix).

    Originally posted by SunlessNick View Post
    Disapproval of slavery is older than 400 years. That said, it's an option.

    But the blood bond instills devotion. It doesn't have to determine which way the power flows. What if the vampire the Bishop is bound to is mad and broken, confined to a cell because he can't survive in the world outside, but protected and cared for by the Bishop because of the bond?
    It isn't slavery if you blood bond them and then send them out for missionary work they'd agree to before the embrace and blood bond anyway, in that case the blood bond is a favor protecting them from those that'd use the blood bond to sway them from the cause of the Lance, which given this guy's history he'd be a major advocate for. Also feelings of devotion to someone who only rarely makes a reasonable request and is far away wouldn't be the greatest restriction.

    In your example though, which is valid, I could see the arch bishop prince bound to a vampire that needed restrained for their own well-being taking extreme steps to ease that beings suffering. Keeping them well fed is obvious, if messy for the amount of blood an insane vampire can spend, but also perhaps employing Ordo Dracu in a project in how to cure vampire madness. I see Malkovian Madness plot lines dangling from this premise and perhaps even the root of that plot in the particular world that Chronicle of Darkness takes place. Trying to split separate the madness from a vampire could have all sorts of metaphysical consequences.
