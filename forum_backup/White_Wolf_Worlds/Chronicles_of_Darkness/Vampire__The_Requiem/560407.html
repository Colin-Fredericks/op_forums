                                          Originally posted by Krzyzstof View Post

Not quite sure where the flesh crafting would come in though. Maybe some mad scientist discovered it with all of his 'subjects'. Crossing animals with humans and what not.
                                          I based my Tzimisce around Ahzi Dahaka i.e. the aspiration to make the world part of oneself. In Blood and Smoke, Seeker Parson describes ghouls as extensions of their regnant like extra limbs. My idea was that Tzimisce are able to extend their protean abilities to their ghouls to create szlachta and then leave them in that state. No living chaise longues or wallskin, but "hellhounds" are fine and they don't necessarily have to be born as dogs or wolves.

                                          So for these guys, "Your body is mine to shape as I see fit", whereas for those who can use dominate "Your mind is mine to shape as I see fit". Auspex is good for that sort thing too. The reason they're known for sleeping near their native soil is connected to their need to sleep in the soil in order to reshape ghouls in different ways. And then Lord of the Land would be the main reason for their hospitality traditions. Aside from that, it doesn't take much to tick off transhumanist Gangrel at the best of times. Etiquette a l'Invictus would be essential to prevent visiting elders from ripping each other to shreds.
