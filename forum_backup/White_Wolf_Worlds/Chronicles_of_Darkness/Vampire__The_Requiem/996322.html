      That's a complicated question. I would say it's not immediately obvious until the power is turned directly against you.

      Yes awe technically effects everyone in range. I would say if there was a reason to roll wits composure - their majesty dots for anything revolving around your own safety you could choose to lash out to break their hold on your attention.

      Of course any lash out attempt would still require a willpower.

          FAQ:
          Does a vampire affected by Majesty 1, Awe, know that they’re being affected by a supernatural power?
          Not automatically. They just feel what Awe wants them to feel. However, they retain their faculties enough that if a Daeva seems too amazing to be true, they might be able to turn around and defy it with the predatory aura.

          Majesty has always been super subtle. You would have to have a good reason to think they are using the power because if you are wrong, you just attacked them - they know it.

          Lashing out is noticeable. And it is something a vampire needs to choose to do. It is not something that goes without repercussions.

          Lashing out requires you to roll 1 of 3 attributes + BP against theirs. So if they are using awe and you lash out and lose, you are now affected by awe and a condition.
