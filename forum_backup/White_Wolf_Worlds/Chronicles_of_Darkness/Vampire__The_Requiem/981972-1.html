                        1) Obtenebration is no longer a discipline. Its a series of devotions. Different rules apply. There has never been a rule that says bloodline merits, rituals, or devotions are off limits, even in 1e. Furthermore, this is a crown. A completely new type of mechanic that is not in the core book. Things are clearly different.

                        2) We've had David Hill, the man who wrote up the new Khaibit as well as a major Vampire writer, say that non-Khaibit can buy the Obtenebration disciplines. Death of the author and all, though, so its not like there's police to force you to do anything. But it does lend some weight.

                        3) Even if you want to forbid the devotions... If you're making a custom power anyways, what difference does it make if you have to make a devotion that's slightly tweaked than a narrowly used ritual? Why reinvent the wheel when the solution is right there?
