                                No one has brought up Buffy yet? For shame.

                                Buffy vampires are in my opinion the perfect examples of Daeva. Most people go to Anne Rice, but they're much more like Toreador from Masquerade, with their vaguely psychic powers and their obsession with beautiful things (mostly beautiful people). While that is an aspect of Daeva, there's a lot more to them that I think Buffy captures exceptionally well. They're strong, they're fast, they're usually very pretty, and often use that to manipulate people, so they've got the Discipline spread down. Theyre all debauched as hell and love being evil just because it's fun, so that covers their 1e Clan weakness. Both of the major male vampire characters eventually fall in love with Buffy, and there are multiple other examples of vampires developing unhealthy obsessions with human characters, so there's the 2e Clan Bane.

                                Most importantly to me though, they're all extremely passionate, and that passion expresses itself in a variety of ways for different characters. Angel is your classic broody heartthrob. Drusilla gets the sexy-but-crazy angle. Spike embodies aggression, and thrill-seeking, with a healthy dose of massochism. Though he ultimately is, in his own words, "love's bitch, but at least I'm man enough to admit it". Even the minor vampire characters, on the occasions that we get the chance to delve a bit into their motivations, are entirely driven by passion, and all have different takes on it.

                                The Master and the Turok-Han (and I've always kind of thought The Master was one of them) are the exception, but they're clearly Nosferatu - they've all got the classic Count Orloch face, they all live underground (technically inside the hellmouth, but come on), they're super strong, their presence has a terrifying and divisive influence on Buffy and the potentials... We don't see any examples of them using Obfuscate, but then, that's exactly how Obfuscate works, and they did pretty much show up out of nowhere near the end of the final season (unless you count The Master, and he was only in the first season and that one random alternate timeline episode.)

                                ...I really like Buffy.

                                                    I suppose Nick and Cym from The Forsaken would be Ventrue nomads with a high tendency to create revenants. The Night Flyer a Nosferatu, though I'm basing this on the movie. Richard Laymon makes some of my favorite vampires, if they're vampires at all. The Traveling Vampire Show probably shows Gangrel, while Bite depicts a Daeva, and The Stake a Mekhet, In The Dark depicts a particularly ugly Mekhet or Nosferatu. Edward Lee's Brides of the Impaler some Lancea et Sanctum Nosferatu, likely part of a heretic caste. I try to imagine Edimmu like the vampires from Lifeforce. Charlee Jacob's vampires are probably Nosferatu sired by some unimaginably powerful elder with a vicious curse and some spirit guides.

                                                    Innocent Blood might fit the more vice-oriented first edition Daeva. The Vampire Boys series and Vampire Killers movies depict Daeva, though with the former I think Demetrius might have been a Gangrel. With the latter it's possible they were Mekhet. They certainly belonged to the Circle of the Crone.
