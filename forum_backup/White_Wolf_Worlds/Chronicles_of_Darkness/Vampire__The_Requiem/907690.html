          Yeah, clans don't dictate your behaviour, they're just about a weakness and disciplines. They have a reputation, but being a Daeva doesn't make you into a sex machine. A vampire was, on the other hand, chosen by a sire for reasons, and those might be that the sire thought they would make a good daeva (in that case), but then, what does the sire think it takes to make a good daeva ? Plus, aside from a few cases, nobody gets a say what clan they'll be before some shady jerk comes from nowhere and embraces them.

          Anyway, in Night Horrors : Immortal Sinners, you got Jacob Skinner, a daeva Ordo Dracul who kidnaps kindred to develop a new coil. He's got none of the prototypical sexy Daeva attitude and is a relatively good base concept for an NPC.

          There's basically no limit on how a vampire character can go opposite to their clan stereotype. For example in the game I'm running, we got a ventrue who's (after more than ten years of being a vampire and in the Lancea) kind of spooked by what vampires are and their powers and. He's really the opposite of the assertive self important type most people see ventrue as. Until recently, he wouldn't even use dominate outside of self defense (combat) situation. He has been recently bummed out by the notion that the Lancea Cardinal he's worked for a long time for in the past would actually order his bodyguards to kill an inquisitor that seems to be on the verge of defection to the Circle of the Crone (that's the version of the events they have anyway).

                  This is kind of what I miss in 2nd ed Daeva. They became much more narrowly focused. In 2nd ed they lean more heavily towards the sensual side than 1st edition, where they were basically defined by their vice, which could be gold or knowledge or sex or praise or just beating people down. Its also where I think the Daeva clan book fell down.

                  That being said, its real easy to do a 2nd ed Daeva that is scientifically minded. I see no reason that this Kindred doesn't set up shop in a college campus, a laboratory or even a monastery (all locations where people are up late doing things). Even with little social skills, a dot or two in Majesty makes the Daeva immediately become very, very popular. He could feed off of lab animals or people in these areas, only leaving to gather items for their research. What might be neat is that the Davea is not use to people going out of their way to help them or feel comfortable about it. He might even become very jealous and possessive of his lab and people, viewing minor, normal acts as treason. I think the hardest part of playing his character is creating reasons for them to leave their home base. A campus might be better since people come on and off the campus and he can't control all of them which leads to more opportunity to interact.

                  I always view clan from a meta-angle. In game, its what your sire was. From a meta perspective its what vampire archetype you want the character to be (and playing against type can be just as fun as playing to it). Daeva are meant to be the cool, Anne Rice or True Blood vampires (mostly). All Daeva means in this context is "cool, sexy scholar." There are tons of examples of those in fiction all over the place, although most of the science part is off screen/off page or montaged.

                      Its really ironic that the 2e Daeva is more like a succubus than the 1e, but lost the nick name. The new nickname kind of works too - they're not just the old Bruja mixed with the Toreador; they've taken on a Follower of Set style attitude as well, though without the snake-god worship. But the temptation, drug, corruption and deceit angles are entirely there. I also imagine that we're going to get a Protean-based bloodline for the Daeva eventually with a snake theme to it, complete with venoms and other kinds of drugs.

                      I bring this up because the drugs and perfumes that the Daeva are known for using are entirely thematically appropriate as a kind of blood alchemy. The sterotypical Daeva isn't really "scientific" because of the skillset the clan's disciplines tend to focus on. In other words, physical strength and agility, as well as the ability to manipulate emotions. Scientists rarely focus on those. However, the Empathy and Subterfuge skills are all that you really need out of a Majesty user, and you can let all other social skills rot. And the former is useful for a scientist type, while the latter is a key skill for almost any vampire. Meanwhile, as for the physical Disciplies? The Ordo are known for getting into fights over Wyrm Nests, so I honestly recommend accompanying physical skills anyways so you don't die. This set up lets you go with Mental/Physical/Social for your 11/7/4 skills, and retain full use of your clan Disciplines.

                          Experimentation isn't always using chemicals or physical objects. Plenty of Scales and Chrysalises use experimentation on people as launching points. A Daeva would find a niche here, able to better wrangle the subjects brought in for such tests. Collecting background information, keeping them interested and excited about the project, being the friendly or attractive face that the subjects can 'trust.' As the subjects will be mortal, even a tertiary build of social talents with a little Majesty should go a long way in keeping the subjects wrangled and invested.

                          An easy mode of hunting would be to have a secondary experimentation in the field of blood testing or donation that the Daeva oversees. Or make blood testing a part of the normal process for other experiments. "We're trying new methods of extraction that ease the pain and maybe even make the feeling of giving blood enjoyable." The extractions are blind for the subject, either blindfolded or arms through a wall/curtain. Questions afterward cement the Masquerade - "Was the event painful?" "Would you be comfortable giving blood like this again?" Most subjects should be one-shot, helping the Daeva to avoid becoming obsessed with a single target who would take away from their focus on the science.

                                  There was the something in 1ed called following the dragon's tail or something (I believe it was usually kind of like an assignment, like a science or social studies project given by the teacher). Basically, an Ordo Dracul did something important like killing a person or something and had to watch closely the long term consequence of that action (did it send a loved one of that person into depression, did they get out of that depression with a new purpose or kill themselves and what consequences did that have, etc). A social Daeva could his his majesty and other skills to cause that event (like seducing someone married and causing their divorce) or to fit in in a circle of acquaintances of the person they're observing so they'll be able to gather more data.

                                  An players from my previous vampire game (1ed) played a Daeva biker, mostly physical stats and disciplines (not sure he even had majesty and if he did, not above 1). He could handle himself in basic social situations (and went in bars to hit on women to feed), but he really wasn't the type of guy who could fit everywhere and maneuver in delicate court situations (he usually let the nosferatu do most of the complicated talking, which she was pretty bad at anyway). It's good to remember that besides their reputation as "serpents" and deceivers and sexy vampires, they are the only clan with two physical disciplines (unless you count protean as one) with direct combat applications. The nosferatu were presented as potential shock troops in 1ed (probably cause they have vigor and a bonus in strength), but a fight oriented Deva can be a hell of a wrecking machine.

                                  And yeah, I think I liked the old daeva weakness better, but that stuff can be houseruled. Their new weakness (as well as the nosferatu's) is completely inapplicable in my current game, so I had to tweak them a bit. Applying the old weakness to the new mask and dirge can be an idea. Lose willpower if you pass an opportunity to defend your mask makes for very image centered vampires who can't stand it when people see through their game, while doing it for the dirge means that the mask is a heavy burden and that daevas are often tempted to let their deeper personnality come up, however good or bad that personnality is. The system is relatively easy to tweak without major repercusions, and I like it for that.

                                      Originally posted by Icon787 View Post
                                      Would like some ideas for a character build. An intelligent Daeva heavily focused on scholarship and research, somewhat to the detriment of her social skills. Ordo Dracul, possibly Sworn of the Dying Light.
                                      Do you have any suggestions for combining the sensuality and obsession of the Daeva with spending most of the night in a lab or library? What does this Daeva look like while hunting?
                                      Two ideas:

                                      This Daeva believes in applied sciences. The best theory isn't worth a damn if she can't put it to good use, so she spends lots of time outside her lab conducting field tests on herself and others. Outside of her core academic competencies, she's something of a jack of all trades, because you never know what skills you'll need for your experiments. The way this gal thinks, she's only ever wasting time if whatever she did didn't yield data. Failure isn't really a word in her vocabulary, because science doesn't fail, even if a particular theory might. She makes good use of all the Daeva Disciplines - the physical stuff gives her more survivability when an experiment goes south, and Majesty helps her keep a lid on things and proves useful in "recruiting" impromptu test subjects.

                                      This Daeva was always a brilliant researcher held back by his lack of social graces. Maybe he registers on the autistic spectrum, maybe he's just a bit too sure of himself or has a particularly foul mouth when agitated - net effect being, he was never liked by his colleagues and superiors and all too often ended up passed over when it came to funding, grants and lucrative positions. Too absorbed in his work to ever be bothered with the ongoing ostracism, what he couldn't stand is the negative effect his social standing had on his research. Embraced on a whim by a Daeva, he quickly found out his scientific talents will be put to good use in Ordo Dracul. More importantly, among his newfound vampiric abilities, he saw Majesty as a perfect tool to compensate for his social ineptitude without putting in a real effort to better himself. A circle of assistants and sycophants surrounds him, and his sapient test subjects are unnervingly compliant and eager to please. Sometimes he's annoyed with his assistants sucking up to him or squabbling to get his attention, but for the most part, he's all too happy that people actually listen to him and obey commands, which saves him time. Truth is, he's grown way more dependent on his vampiric charm than he should, and even the other Defiant watch this celebrity cult he's built for himself with growing unease.
