    Soliciting Ideas for Carthian NPCs and Motivations

    03-30-2015, 09:19 PM
    Hey all. My ST has asked me to poll the forum for Carthian ideas. In the game he runs and in which I play there is an ongoing influx of Carthians and Carthian Coteries into the city of Baltimore. My ST wants these coteries to be more diverse than they would otherwise be if they were created exclusively by him. To that end he seeks the help of the forum as a randomizing factor.

    So, please post your ideas as to what a Carthian Vampire might look like and think like. What are their ideals and goals. How might they go about achieving them?

    Some of your ideas will be used by the ST in our game. (links to which can be found in my sig.)

    Thanks in advance for your help and ideas

      A conspiracy of Night Doctors scattered among the Carthian Coteries that have come to the city. They're obsessed with cataloguing and studying different lines of Kindred lineage, looking for any kinds of similarities between Kindred of similar lines that can be discerned through their Thanatology. They may also play around with engineering some kind of parasite or bane that targets specific lineages in order to remove those Kindred lines which they think form too great a burden on the All-Night Society. They may favor a technocratic form of government wherein experts might organize Kindred according to complementary quirks of behavior, lineage and clan. They're most likely lead by an Ancilla or an Elder who was intrigued by the discussion of eugenics throughout the last several centuries, wondering what might be observed in the study and possible implementation of Kindred genetics.

      Their membership should seem very mild in their normal lives. That is, they behave like moderate Carthians with a slight leaning towards technocratic ideas being promoted among the members of the Movement. They don't draw attention to themselves in these guises, preferring to work to accomplish their goals in their Doctor alter-egos. Distressingly, some of the allies of this 'conspiracy' might not even understand the ideas they're following totally, they may just buy into an implication of ubermensh Kindred.

          A powerful elder from a distant city is stripped of his resources and left vulnerable after being outmaneuvered by a similarly potent rival of a century or more. Faced with the daunting task of rebuilding his empire from scratch, he flees to Baltimore. Shedding his former identity like a snake sheds its skin, he falls in with the city's growing Carthian community. An experienced manipulator and deal-maker, he becomes a rising star of the movement, and one of the loudest voices condemning the status-quo in the city. Of course, if his past history were to come to light it would call his dedication to the movement into question, and his new allies might well turn on him just as quickly as they rallied to his side.

              A coterie motivated by the ideals of Ayn Rand. Very much Bioshock influenced. Their ideal form of government would be a city where power and prestige is visited on those who best serve the needs of the city and allow its inhabitants almost unrestricted freedom. The needs of the city are not clearly defined. On the surface this government would allow a lot more individual freedom to most members of the city but, it is likely that such a set up would favour powerful individuals (elders). Boons could also become an official currency and embraces, territory petitions and maybe even indulgences would have fixed prices.

              I picture the coterie as being led by a Ventrue or possibly a charismatic gangrel. They'd be an intellectual and an orator. Polite, courteous and utterly commited to their own point of view. Someone who tries to persude by questioning rather than browbeating. They'd also be pretty hard to put down, a lot of resilence.
              The other members would complement the leader, one would be a decent fighter. The other would probably be more focused on details and pragmatism than the leader. This coterie would act as a unit and be very hard to seperate. Probably at least one would be an Ancilla and the other two would be experienced neonates at least (on a par with Felix and Alice maybe). I'd see them as a a group that could rally people behind them but also be dangerous because they act, as opposed to Dorris Beatte (who seems to mainly just preach and rabble-raise).

              Another idea, Anonymous style hackers. A group of neonates that use the technology of their era to great affect. They don't fight people directly, they troll and in extreme cases call the police on enemies and in so doing, endanger the masquerade. Their danger is in using human technology against vampires in a way that most aren't expecting. Perhaps a ventrue is amongst them to scramble their memories and make it difficult for the Mehket to find them. Their long term goals would be to allow neonate vampires more freedom. They could be a classic, rebelling against the elders group, but using tactics and methods that Baltimore is not quite ready for.

                      For your consideration, several of the Carthians from my own game. The important context points being that the game takes place in Chicago, in 1983, and the Invictus currently hold power.


                      Alan Oleny is a Neonate Gangrel and a member of the Carthians. Alan has been active in Chicago since the early 1950’s. He’s the leader of a coterie called the Homefront, a group of Carthians dedicated to preparing for survival against worst case scenarios (read : doomsday prepper). Most of the city considers him to be paranoid, which says a lot considering he is a part of Kindred society.

                      Alan's Mission : To fortify, train, and prepare for eventual discovery of the Kindred by the Kine.

                      Alan’s Position : With the continual progress of science, humanity will inevitably find us. If we want to live forever, we need to be prepared to live forever.

                      Alan's mission makes a lot of sense to the newest recruits of the Carthians and they are often swayed by his points. This often fades after a few years of undeath though. Carthian leadership sees the Homefront coterie as a liability to the cause. In his opinion, they are too self centered, not willing to aggressively pursue the the goals of their fellow Carthians. Likely the reason they are still part of the Covenent is because of the difficulty of extracting them and the possible fallout of doing so. The inner circle of the coterie aren't first years.



                      Donald Soren Foster is a member of the Carthian Movement and one of the Harpies of the City of Chicago. He has been active in the city since the mid 1960’s. While he reflects an Elder’s refinement and dignity, he makes up for it with his panache and dramatism. He’s as good a source of gossip as any of the other Harpies and he is the source of choice for those who are in a good mood or are looking for an entertaining update.

                      Donald joined the Carthian Movement in 1970 following abandonment by his sire, Marica Holley. She moved out to Hollywood in order to get closer to the stars there, but announced to David that he had grown enough to move out of the house, so she was leaving him behind. With the blood bond vanishing and no community of his own yet, he found himself in need of friends and the Carthians welcomed him with open arms. In just a few short years, his charming demeanour and talent for storytelling won him a lot of favour amongst the Covenent and pushed him to be their face.


                      Donald's Position : The best way to teach and to learn is through a story. People engage in a story in a way that classroom teaching can never achieve and allows for that same education without the savage consequences of misstep in the real world.

                      Donald's Mission : Tell the story of how strong the will of the people is and use it to inspire change.

                      It is well known among the Covenent that Donald isn't very fond of Silvio Scarano, the leader of the Wrecking Crew. For his part, Silvio doesn't like Donald either. Both of them claim that the other is a secondary asset, that what they provide to the Covenent is of true value and that the other exists just to give them the freedom to work.



                      Henry Saine

                      Henry Saine has been a known member of the Kindred community of Chicago for six years now. He and his sire, Simone Verlaine, seem to have a very on again off again sort of relationship. When they are on, she keeps Henry by her side and does what she can to teach and encourage him. When they are off, they act like they don't even know each other. Henry's favourite method of hunting is to dress up nice and head on down to Boystown, where he can easily find and pick up a man for a quick bite. He is pleased with how easy it is, and how his prey is not likely to talk about their encounters due to homosexuality being such a strong taboo. However, one of his outings did involve him getting stalked by a bunch of red neck gay bashers, who beat him severely out back of Afterlife (the PC's club).


                      Henry’s Mission : Promote charities within the city and earn as much funding for them as possible from wealthy donors and government.

                      Henry’s Position : Share the wealth, nobody should ever have more than they can use. Anything they can’t use should be redistributed.

                      Henry's favourite charity at the moment is homelessness. He's working as fundraising advocate for the "Chicago Coalition for the Homeless", a new charity that caught his eye. Despite Henry's chosen method of hunting, his sexual orientation is still heterosexual. He has an apparent sweet spot for female victims and could possibly be bribed with a chance at some.


                      Silvio Scarano

                      Silvio Scarano is a neonate and an entrenched member of the Carthians. He leads a coterie of Carthian kindred known as the Wrecking Crew. The Wrecking Crew represent the strong arm of the Carthian movement. While not the Covenent's only able fighters, when the leadership decides they want to send a message, the Wrecking Crew is who will be engraving that message into the recipient's face. Silvio has led the coterie since it's initial formation in 1957. The Wrecking Crew favours sledge hammers, pick axes, and fire axes for their armaments. They dress in hard hats and city worker's overalls, typically with the upper 'shirt' portion undone and tied at the waist to let the crew show off their own individual band or attitude T-shirts.



                      Mission : To find vulnerable parts of the city's existing power structure and hammer out those parts, one at a time.

                      Position : To make an omelet, you need to break a few eggs. The Carthians need to make an omelet.

                          I'll give it a shot at how the Randians might go about interacting with the Baltimore Carthians and citizens. I've kept it in broad-strokes so your ST can pick what ideas to use or discard and I have not charactised them, as I'm sure your ST will do a great job of that. If you want more ideas for them, or to I missed the point and charactisation is what you wanted, let me know!

                          Firstly I see the Randians as being quite subtle in their approach, at least initially. They value individual freedom a great deal which would automatically set them at odds with the secret police style set up of Baltimore. They would be pragmatic and smart about it though. They wouldn't flaunt laws immediately. I could see them gaining allies, especially amongst the kiddie poolers and the "have nots" of Baltimore by helping them gain small freedoms and bucking the more repressive aspects of the system. A little like Alice and Felix have done so up to this point. A hound might be encouraged to not repress a hungry Kindred, as it is infringing on their freedom to feed themselves. In return for their help, they would rack up boons. They could be quite focused on Elysiums, particularly the more artistic side, as a means of entertaining and indoctrinating the masses. A bread and circus type approach to the worse offs.

                          For those more powerful, they would probably seek to petition and be useful for. They'd probably want to feel out Seville and see how closely his views align with theirs. The leader would probably enjoy debating him and would defintely want to keep on his good side and ideally sway him over to their view point if possible. I think they'd be willing to compromise with the right person if it resulted in some of their ideas being put into practice. They might really dislike the clan based lines Baltimore has been drawn up on, and would promote a less "racist" society, where one clan is not notably more powerful than any other. They'd probably espouse less laws, and impose some kind of fixed boon economy if they could. In the long term they'd want to gain some territory as regents to implement their ideas into practice. These ideas might even work on a small group basis but would likely collapse across a city. The masquerade would be a potential problem if they offered too much freedom to neonates I would expect, though they themselves would see the importance of the masquerade. They'd want to sway people to their side by showing a working concept in how they'd govern their territory and by persuading them that their ideas are best. Any failures could be blamed on "entrenched ideas about the monarcy", if they had a fresh batch of neonates to work with, next time would be more successful!

                          Their ideas could be attractive to some elders as their stance on individual freedom actually keeps the power dynamic with entrenched individuals (i.e Elders) although on the surface it would make life easier for neonates as they wouldn't have to worry about being murdered for questioning the Prince and so forth. I think they'd hate unconsenual blood-bounds on kindred, mortals are fair game, as an infringement on personal individualism. They could be an interesting counter-point to Felix and Alice by being quite active in their attempts to gain influence and territory. They would claim to value reason above all but wouldn't afraid to get their hands dirty for their ideals. How hypocritical they are is also up for debate.

                                  Randian opinion on Dorris Bettes and Michael Seville.

                                  On Michael Seville. Its hard for me to say because it would largely depend on what Seville's political views for the city are. They would respect him as an individual, no doubt and try to discern what his ideal Kindred society in Baltimore would look like. If his politics are broadly similar (it does seem that Seville would offer more individual freedoms to Baltimore's citizens) then they might throw their support behind him as a force for change, even if that change is not as radical as they would like. In this scenario, they'd try to become trusted lieutenants and push for more radical measures to be taken and request a territory for services rendered. I could see them working as debt collectors and advocates for Seville in this scenario.

                                  On the other hand, if his political views are incompatible with their own. Say his ideal society is just as repressive as currently, then they would probably try very hard not to piss him off and would work for their goals independently of his. They'd probably be a lot less productive without the backing of an Elder and Seville is the one who is best positioned to help them. In either scenario they would respect Seville on an individual basis, particularly his willingness to meet vampires of all ages. In both scenarios they would clash with him over the presence of the "Kiddie pools." They'd want this custom abolished in its current form. Individuals should be taught how to behave by "enlightened" individuals. They would also want to use Elysium for this "educational purpose" (indoctrination).

                                  On Dorris Bettes. They would think her heart is in the right place but her methods are ineffective. They favour actions that have results rather than sabre-rattling. They'd view her attempts at bringing in outside vampires as misguided. Those vampires were largely killed for not understanding Baltimore's laws. A better solution would have been to educate the vampires on how the Kiddie Pool worked so that a group could survive the pool on mass. These wouldn't be radicalised so they could survive the Mekhet thought police. They would be vampires favourable to the Carthians with the goal of changing the balance of covenants. On a personal level though, they'd probably quite like Dorris Bettes and might consider recruiting her into their coterie, as a way of gaining a quick power base amongst the Baltimore Carthians. They'd want to make sure she didn't sabotage their plans long term though. (Maybe Bettes would be viewed by them how Terry is to Alice and Felix. An amusing side-kick with potential)

                                  I'd say how the Randians approach Bettes would largely be determined on how you want to present them and their ideology. If she does join them because they persuaded her or was seduced then it would show them as practising what they preach. If you want to play them as being hypocritical and their ideology is just a mask for ambition, they might even blood-bound Bettes, though of course they'd claim it was a consensual choice she made.

                                  If Bettes did join their coterie whether willingly or not, it would give them instant access to the territory they desire, even if they have to work to make it viable and it would give Bettes some useful allies to shore up her own position. How this would play against Seville could create some fun conflict. This coterie (including Bettes) could be a bridge that would unite the Carthians fully behind Seville, if they back him, or fracture them fully into squabbling groups if they don't. Or for fun, unite the Carthians in the short term only for it all to fall apart at a later date as desired. They could also be a fun reflection and rival group to Alice and Felix.
