        Originally posted by Malus View Post
        Banes in vampire are curses. Plain and simple. That's all the symbolism there's to them, and curses are damned specific (pun intended).
        Both the 'great curses' are explained in terms of symbolism. P101-102.

        'Sunlight represents truth and honesty. It stands for visibility, where Kindred exist in deceptive darkness. While it isn't always immediately deadly, it burns Kindred flesh.'

        'Fire represents humankind's dominance over the natural world, and ability to manipulate its environment as a tool. This symbolically human tool destroys Kindred flesh like nothing else.'

        Like I said when originally asking, I think storytellers could go either way depending on where they leaned. But the book clearly embraces the symbolism of the great banes. Two of them, at least.
