            I feel the disciplines have a thematic progression that naturally stops at the fifth dot. Let's say Auspex, which is about insight/empathy. First you have insight into/empathy with people, then empathy with objects, which is naturally more difficult. At the fourth dot you have near perfect empathy with another living being, and at the fifth your insight is no longer bound to your physical body. Going further than this would be transcendence or Buddha-like enlightenment, which I don't think would suit Vampire, the theme has gone as far as possible while still feeling Vampiric.

            Progression now, would involve mixing themes or focusing aspects of them, which is the ground devotions cover.
