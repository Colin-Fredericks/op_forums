                  I strongly recommend that you set yourself at least about the rules for multiple actions. Those are by far the most game-breaking rules of WoD, and if one side have them and the other not, your PCs aren't going to last long. Ideally, though, you should convert one game to the other. I would keep the Requiem rules, in this case.

                  Even converting the games, it doesn't mean you can't keep the Disciplines. The most interesting thing in such a game would be for supernatural powers to work mostly as they should in each universe. Only pay attention to the multi-action thing.

                  For Requiem vampires diablerizing Masquerade ones, I see no need to change the Requiem's rules. At most, you could think about they keeping the new Disciplines they find on Masquerade. Requiem already presumes that the vampires are a multitude of undead species that evolved to be alike, so it isn't a big deal for them to not be the same creatures as those new vampires. From a Masquerade standpoint it isn't so simple, and you could use some more weirdness happening. Maybe a Masquerade vampire who diablerizes a Requiem one just got the same benefits as ever, coupled with a new Discipline. Or, maybe, it changes in weird ways how the Curse of Caine works.

                  What about a Masquerade vampire who manages to diablerize a Requiem one and develop Blood Potence, being effectively able to "low generation" just with time? How much it would impact the setting?

                      RE: Vampire Crossover

                      Being familiar with both rules sets, I will offer the following advice. My suggestions are intended to both follow the easiest mechanical path, as well as generate interesting story elements.

                      Use one ruleset as a base - While the WoD and CofD base mechanics are closely related, they are different enough that attempting to replicate design elements is problematic enough to not be worth the effort. Either run CofD or WoD, it will save time, energy and frustration.

                      Cainites as Wicked Dead - The Requiem setting and mechanics already allow for creatures that are Vampires, but not necessarily Kindred. For the most part, the most of the core template mechanics would remain the same. At a glance, a casual observer would be unable to tell the difference between a Cainite and a Requiem Kindred. However, the Powers of the Blood might be a great place to stress differences between the types of Vampires. Requiem Vampires have a Predatory Aura, but Cainites might not, or perhaps Requiem Kindred cannot participate in Vaulderie (or if they can, what does it mean?).

                      Disciplines - Likewise, most common disciplines are not dissimilar enough that it would be worth it to use different mechanics. Vigor is just Potence with a different name. And don't get hung up with the multiple actions thing - multiple actions is a meta-mechanics (and problematic one at that) the World of Darkness uses to represent superhuman speed and agility. CofD opted to express that in a different way. Requiem Celerity isn't different because of some fundamental metaphysical setting aspect, it is different because the game developers killed multiple actions with fire and then beat its corpse with a shovel.
                      Variations in the common disciplines could be represented by alternate abilities or Devotions - more differences for observant vampires to pick up on.
                      As far as the more unique Disciplines, I would reference the (oft mentioned) Translation Guide. This is a time saver more than anything else. A few 2nd Edition tweaks might be in order, but no sense in doing the work to convert Disciplines when an official source has already done the work for you. And for the sake of simplicity, each vampire types can learn the disciplines ofhte other with proper instruction (always out of clan - even if they share a clan name).

                      Generation vs.Blood Potency - Again, this is addressed in the Translation Guide (pg. 38-39). I would use the same over all mechanics for Generation as for Blood Potency, but Cainites can only raise Potency using Merit dots at character creation. After that, Diablerie is the only way to increase power. To compensate for this, I would grant Cainites immunity to Dominate attempts from either Cainites or Requiem Kindred of lesser Blood Potency. High Generation Vampires of the 14/15th generation would be mechanically Revenants. Low Generation/High Potency Vampires in Masquerade are seemingly more powerful than their Requiem equivalents, perhaps this is an element of the blood, or it could be an element of the world, and High Potency Reqiuem Vampires find their power and abilities radically enhanced.

                      Diablerie - Use the same basic Requiem rules, with a few distinctive permutations. If a Cainite diablerises a Requiem Vampire of higher potency, then in addition to the usual benefits, they become effectively Unshackled - their Blood Potency becomes that of a Requiem Kindred - Rising with time and will, but decreasing in Torpor. Whether or not this is a temporary or permanent alteration is up to the Storyteller. Likewise, a Requiem vampire to diablerises a more potent Cainite gains the usual benefits, but then their potency freezes as if they were a Cainite of the appropriate Generation.

                      Paths of Enlightenment - II would run the paths as a meritwith a requirement of Humanity 5 or less. Having dots in the Merit allows the Vampire to swap out Humanity breaking points from Humanity 5 or less for more Path-Appropriate ones and the Path itself would count as a Touchstone that would become detached if the Vampire raises his Humanity. To make a Path work in the Reqiuem context, it would need to keep the Vampire from sliding further into depravity, essentially cutting off part of their remaining humanity and offering it to the Beast to keep it sated.

                      Storytelling - A Requiem Vampire that somehow get shuttled to the (one) World of Darkness, will be trapped in a eerily familiar world, but with the volume turned up on everything. The War of Ages makes the occasional classes of Covenants look like a tea party and their are ~literally~ Apocalyptic forces at play. The supernatural is more overt and has greater influence over mortal society, yet somehow the majority of mortals are seemingly oblivious to everything and everywhere you look there are seemingly signs that The End Is Nigh. But Vampires are Vampires and Reqiuem Vampires aren't at so much of a disadvantage that they cannot make their mark. They will have tricks and talents that Cainites won't be expecting, including various forms of unknown Blood Magic, Disciplines and Devotions. They may even carve their own niche (an Invictus may see the Camarilla as everything they ever wanted, a Crone might find a home and a cause in the Sabbat). And their are entire new realms of new Disciplines and metaphysical exploration available to an enterprising Requiem Vampire.

                      For the Cainites,the presence of the Requiem Vampires opens a large number of possibilities. Their freedom from Generation would be coveted, especially among the Sabbat (the ability to rival the power of the ancients with only time and will seem like a dark miracle). The Tremere and other mystically minded factions would be intrigued by new Blood Magic (especially the Coils of the Dragon). And others would seek was to leave the World of Darkness for Chronicles of Darkness (freedom from the Jihad and impending Gehenna, a world of nothing but blood and endless nights stretching into eternity - hell, the Lupines there will mostly ignore you if you stay out of their business!)

                      The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
                      Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
                      The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
                      Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
                      Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

                          That's mostly what I would do, so I endorse this take.

                          I would only change the part on Paths of Enlightenment. It doesn't matter how much different Humanity is in Requiem, those aren't your Requiem vampires. I would make the distinction between Humanity and Paths as another way to stress the metaphysical distinctions between Cainites and Requiem Kindred. So the simplest route would be to use the Paths as is, but with the rolls and rules for Breaking Points of Requiem. A Path is mostly just another set of Breaking Points.

                          Can a Requiem Kindred learn a Path? And a Masquerade Cainite would have the same Beast, countered by the same Humanity? The answer is up to the ST, and maybe Humanity isn't even the same thing for both groups.
