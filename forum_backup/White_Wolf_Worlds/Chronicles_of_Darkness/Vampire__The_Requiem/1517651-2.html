                I miss virtue and vice, honestly, I do like the 2.e got rid of the whole imitation of emotions, old humanity system, and fog of ages. These changes all made vampires more human so the mask and dirge aspect that made vampires only pretend humans with their masks kind of seems like a sidestep away from that. It also does not really play well in a mortals game where the players are turned into vampires since humans still use virtue and vice.

                I just never really liked the concept of masks and who you are in the dark because realistically everyone wears many masks not just one who you are among friends may be different than who you are among coworkers, or strangers for some examples. But a virtue is something you always have; it is a concept you idolize and attempt to emulate among all people whereas a vice is a negative trait that is also always a part of you.

                I just feel they represent vastly different things. Those things in the sense of virtue and vice are also things I prefer. They made an excellent system for it in the chronicles core book.

                            Say for instance you had virtue loyal, to gain 1 willpower you would have to stand up for your group to your own detriment or minor risk, however you could refresh all your willpower if you let your loyalty put you in extreme personal danger such as taking a severe blow for an ally or suffering a breaking point to defend your allies.

                            If you had the vice spiteful, you would gain 1 willpower by getting back at someone who had rightly wronged you, this would have to be petty or cruel to gain thepoint. You would gain all willpower if you "cut off your nose to spite your face" i.e as a result of your spite you ended up just as bad off as the one you went against and were happy with that.
