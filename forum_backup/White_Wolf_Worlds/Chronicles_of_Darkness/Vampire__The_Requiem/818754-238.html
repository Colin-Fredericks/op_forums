                                Originally posted by Vintervalpen View Post
                                What's considered a magical effect as per the Sorcerous Eunuch merit? Are disciplines magical effects? Werewolf gifts/rites? Supernal magic? Depending on interpretation, this merit is either really very extra good, or just decent, and I'm not entirely sure what was intended with it.
                                This has been discussed a few times. The consensus is that among vampiric powers it's definitely only Blood Sorcery that is affected, hence why Blood Sorcery specifically can't be used by the character.

                                It's less clear about other games since each game is designed primarily for itself with mostly just the system allowing for crossovers. Anyway, here's my unofficial rule of thumb:
                                "Any category of powers that generally takes more than a turn to activate should be considered sorcery in this context. That means Mage spells, Ceremonies, Pack/Wolf Rites, and Obcasus Rites, but not Haunts, Gifts, or Atavisms/Nightmares."
