    Originally posted by Elderand View Post
    Ventrue were prone to insanity, now they just have slightly more trouble with touchstones (it seems to be the conscencus that their clan weakness is a joke)
    Actually, looking at it from an abstract mechanical point of view, I can see how it looks like the "nicest" bane to have, but in actual play I have found it as punishing as the others. When a Ventrue begins to lose humanity they really plummet down the ladder, possibly reaching Humanity 3 quite fast, only stabilising there because the acts you have to commit to go further are willfully inhuman, rather than something you might typically encounter as a vampire.

    Of course one can "buy" an extra touchstone with the merit, but as with all merits this has to be justified in the narrative and it's reasonably difficult for you to really develop a relationship with somebody that makes you reconnect to your humanity.

    If I'm a Ventrue, let's say my touchstone was my nephew who was only eight or nine when I was sired. I see him grow up, watch over him as he goes to college, has a family of his own. Then me and my Daeva associate have to break somebody's mind for information. We're both changed by the experience, more animalistic, amoral. However the Daeva still goes to see her sister at the weekends. However after breaking that guys mind, I literally don't give two fucks about my nephew any more (mechanically: touchstone detached). That is going to be very hard to recover, to either rebuild that connection or forge another as meaningful. In the mean time I have become much more prone to losing my human nature and if I do, I'm going to have to work even harder to regain what I lost. In the new edition the Ventrue are lords of emotional apathy and distance. I think it's a much more interesting weakness that the first edition's one.

            Depends how you interpret Detatched vs. Lost. In my games, you lose a Touchstone if they die or you burn bridges with them. I'f you're still in contact with them but the relationship doesn't hold the same meaning to you that it used to, they're detatched.

            I would consider my friend Alex a Detatched Touchstone - we haven't so much as talked to each other in years, we're both just different people than we were when we became friends and if I met him for the first time today, we would not become friends, we probably wouldn't even like each other. But even now, if he needed me for any reason at all, he would only have to ask and I would do it without a second thought and I know he would do the same for me. We're Detatched, but still each other's Touchstones. One of us would have to die, or to do something completely unforgivable to lose that connection.
