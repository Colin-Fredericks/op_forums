                                        It's kind of indicated in the text, though not stated directly that I know of, that each strain of vampires started out very different from the others. Over time, with the intermingling of blood, they became as similar as they are now. The prime example of this from wherever I first heard this theory is the bonus clan presented in the Tokyo setting in 2e.

                                        Every Discipline except the Clan ones are common enough that any given vampire's blood might manifest the ability naturally (or, if you like teaching, by trying to force the matter with a teacher).

                                        Of those, certain Clans manifest certain Disciplines more often than others, perhaps as a reflection of their origins. These are in-clan Disciplines. They're just more common.

                                        Of these, each Clan has a unique Discipline that originated from their bloodline (in the general sense, not the specific Bloodline sense) which only that Clan manifests naturally. For a vampire of another Clan to manifest this ability, they need some of blood that has already manifested that ability to intermingle with their own. This is because it isn't an ability found in the blood and beast of other clans.

                                        A Bloodline is what happens when a vampire has blood potent enough that it can start passing on its traits almost like Clan blood can, whatever those traits were be. Based, as one might imagine, upon its founder.

                                        It's basically magic genetics. Magic because Vampire genetics are passed down by blood, not actual genes, and you can integrate the blood of other vampires into your own magic-blood-genes by ingesting it. Some things (Clan disciplines that are not your own) do not pass down to your Childer unless you create a new bloodline to make your own magic-blood-genes more potent.

                                        Granted, there's a lot of leaps of logic from the book in that, but it's my interpretation, and it's a good enough way to explain it for those who wanted an explanation.

                                        Edit: Though if you're feeding Vitae to your new Childer to Embrace them, wouldn't they have access to any Out-of-Clan Disciplines in your blood? Maybe! I don't think that's clarified anywhere.
