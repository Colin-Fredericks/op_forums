        Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
        I believe there was a thread about being blind / no eyes and dominate still working. I am terrible at finding old threads, but I want to say that it's there and it works. I could be wrong.

        If you forget the information, what good is it to you anymore? How do you dominate yourself? Would you need a mirror to see yourself? Could you even give an order once you are mesmerized because you are hypnotized and waiting for orders? Personally. I dont think you should be able to mind roll yourself.

        You could dominate someone and command "Speak only the truth" and then everything after that should not be subject to skepticism. Every witness would know it to be truth.

        Dominated to forget and dominated to remember, if they were the same level dominate I would suggest a clash of wills.
        Yeah, Vent0 covered this pretty well. You can command yourself to forget whatever information you want yourself to forget until you encounter such and such trigger you arrange for yourself to have. For example, you know you're meeting someone who also possesses a high level of Domination. Before you go to meet them you Dominate a servant into saying a certain obscure phrase to you when you return. After that you Dominate yourself into forgetting said info until you hear that phrase, so even if someone does try to extract the info from you during that meeting you don't actually know anything at the time, and it's only when you return and your servant speaks the triggering phrase will your full memory return.

        Now yes, this can potentially backfire pretty disastrously depending on circumstances, but it's a pretty solid strategy.

        And commanding someone to speak the truth only will only get them to answer what they believe is the truth, not necessarily what is objectively true. If they've been mind controlled (or even just convinced) into honestly thinking that something is true even when in reality it is false, ordering them to tell the truth is only going to get them to tell you the exact same thing that they would have told you anyways even if you didn't specify that they tell the truth, since they tell what they believe to be true anyways. You can't really order most people to tell the objective truth because not many people have the ability to know what is objectively true, and Dominate doesn't give that power to people who don't naturally have it.
