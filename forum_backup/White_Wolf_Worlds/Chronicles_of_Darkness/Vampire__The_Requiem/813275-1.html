    Originally posted by Khanwulf View Post
    Good suggestion Live Bait. To be clear, I don't think the humanity roll is a bad thing mechanically, it just seems a tad odd to have something that represents how well the vampire can disguise their monstrous hunger as a bane for the motherly bloodline.

    ​

    The Qedeshah make very interesting candidates for a "Lilith" bloodline. Their origins point to such a connection, but they are not the only bloodline to make that kind of assertion. The Asam, for example, look like excellent candidates for a connection back to some goddess-monster figure.

    So I thought a bit... what if there were a bloodline under each clan that claimed connection back to the Lilith-ish figure of mythology? What if... they were all correct? And in some way the vampiric progeny of Lilith start/started clanless (revenants?) and when adopted acquired the bloodline appropriate to their ancestry?

    Something like that... have cake... eat too, something.
    It's certainly possible indeed. But then i guess my old chronicle in Rio de Janeiro had factions & sub-factions aplenty without bringing more bloodlines with "lilithian" claims of origin into the mix.
