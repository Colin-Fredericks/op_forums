        I think people may be getting too focused on the specific exemple, so let's broaden things up.

        The point was that mekhets would keep secret how easily they can, well, find out secrets. And it seems on the surface to be reasonable, but if you dig just a little deeper it start straining credulity.

        For this to be true you have to make an astounding number of assumptions. First Mehkets never tell anyone, seems the most reasonable, except they are a clan, which are far less controlled than covenants are. Even covenant secrets gets out from time to time. Leaks happen just because of basic human nature.
        Second. Mekhet never have shared, willingly or not, their gift with anyone else.

        Over the entire history of vampire kind, never has a mekhet spilled the bean, cracked under torture, been dominated or charmed into speaking, been diablerized by a blabber mouth, shared secrets with a covennats or coterie member. Spontaneous (real or strix induced) embrace of mekhets never was lacking in judgement, never has a strix just dropped the bomb of what mekhet can do just for shit and giggle to instigate distrust.

        It just take one good exemple of one of these things to spread the knowledge that mekhets can just look at you and play twenty questions in their head and suddenly they know all your dirty little secrets.
        The alternative is that Mekhets are super organized and policed clan of unbreakable badasses that never show any weakness, get possessed or diablerize or who manage to eliminate every trace of the knowledge when it's spilled.

        All of this has huge implications for Mekhets that the book just doesn't explore. And it can be fun to explore them. It doesn't have to end up in Mekhet being exterminated but that's a possibility. There are countless others. But applying the previous relationship and position of the status quo of the previous edition to this one when things have changed so dramaticly ? Well, you can if you want, but personaly I find it hard to swallow.

        And it is a problem that is highly exacerbated in the case of auspex. Pretty much everything else has a countermeasure somewhere, be it opposed rolls, lashing out, merits blocking or what have you. Auspex just work, the countermesure seems to be that you can get false information but any Mekhet worth his salt will test several time and corroborate through other means once he become awares false information is a possibility. And since the false information is matter for the ST and not for the person being observed, you can't even really plant false trails in the hope a mekhet will pick up on that false information. The false info he get could be entirely different to the false trail you prepared. So for all intent and purpose there is no real defense against a mekhet finding out your secrets.

        Unless you have access to blood sorcery and have protection 4 as a theme. Then you may be protected. And if you don't have access to blood sorcery, you need to find someone who does and convince them to help you and then you have to trust them to actually protect you and not lay a curse or use this opportunity to find out the very secrets you wanted protected.

        Mind you my point is not that it was wrong to do auspex the way it was done, but that the implications of it could have been explored more. Which for now is up to indicidual ST. I do hope the subject get explored more deeply in future books. It would be a shame to ignore the potential there for the sake of maintaining some sort of status quo.
