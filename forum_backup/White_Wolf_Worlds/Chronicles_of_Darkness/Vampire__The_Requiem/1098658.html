    City Concept - ruled by Ordo Dracul

    05-31-2017, 12:47 AM
    Just wanted some thoughts about this. I was thinking of having an Ordo Dracul prince and several of them occupying key positions. I was thinking the Geheim (Ancient Bloodlines) because they have access to Blut Alchemie and some of the characters are Prometheans in this crossover. I was thinking the Sheriff could be an Azerkatil or Moroi.

    I looked in Damnation City for an Ordo Dracul prince who just turns out to be a charlatan. I was thinking of a cold, calculating prince who prizes knowledge, occult and otherwise, among all things. Other vampires could be alchemists, pseudo-scientists, ley-line hunters, and parapsychologts.

    I think the Invictus and Lancea Sanctum had a strong presence, but they were driven or hunted out by the Ordo Dracul. The Ordo Dracul was able to create 10 Blood Prometheans (Extempores) which gave them a huge military advantage.

    The Carthians and Crone never had a large presence, but have mostly been left alone.

    Any thoughts or ideas on this "premise"?

      I think instead of choosing specific Bloodlines for all the minute titles of the domain, you'd be better served with the broad strokes. The Ordo is usually more concerned with their research than political sway, and they tend towards the smallest membership of the major Western covenants; why are so many of them here (a good place for a sexy occult mystery), how did they take power, and why haven't the other power blocs slapped them down? If this is going to be a Promethean crossover, I think it might be more useful to have some mystery attracting the Ordo and scaring away the others, rather than trying to involved the Created in the intricacies of Kindred factional warfare.

          Maybe aim for something between utopian intentional communities and states where a fringe philosophy or religion is mandated by the authorities. I imagine living in an Ordo domain as a deeply strange and confusing experience where official titles are used in place of names, no one speaks plainly, and historic havens are annexed and repurposed as laboratories and meditation chambers. Loudspeakers play faded tapes of meditative chanting and white noise at Elysium, just loud enough that you can't shut it out. Stylized poster portraits of the Prince with seductive hypnotist eyes go up everywhere, and people whisper that the Kogaion can see through them remotely. Token titles ("Reverential Watchdog of the Needle's Eye", "Eleventh Elder") are awarded to loyal outsiders to use at social functions, with restrictions. Esoteric academic arguments become deadly serious when the scholars also hold positions of real power and pawns to set against each other. Play up the feeling of "what the hell is happening to this town?" and position the Ordo as the only path to the truth.

              Hi Lex and Caladriu, thanks for the input.

              My thought was that originally the city (let's call it "Providence") was traditionally controlled by both the Invictus and the Lancea Sanctum that had a "normal" city in that regard. The Circle of the Crone and the Carthians just received scraps from the table. And the Ordo Dracul had almost no political influence.

              However the Ordo Dracul created Blood Prometheans, around 10 - 15 of them. This gave them the military advantage they needed to take control. The Invictus and Lancea Sanctum were crushed or driven out of the city. This new power vacuum allowed the Carthians and Circle more leeway, but still the Ordo Dracul remained supreme as the conquerers. This all happened 25 years ago.

              Yes, the Ordo Dracul would prefer to be alone in their studies, but they find that by dominating the city they have unfettered access for research, experimentation, what have you, etc. Nothing is withhold in terms of their studies. The alchemists, pseudo-scientists, ley-line hunters, and parapsychologists all come across things that could result in better defenses for the city or for attacking their enemies.

              Numbers-wise there are probably 20 Ordo Dracul vampires with 3 times that number of ghouls.

              There are probably eight Circle vampires with 12 ghouls and six Carthians with 10 ghouls. The Circle sometimes does favors with Cruac for the Ordo Dracul thus they are allowed a greater number of ghouls and embraced vampires. The Carthians have very little to offer the Ordo Dracul at this time, but they still try to curry favor since they are not represented among the primogen.

              The Invictus and Lancea Sanctum are not welcome in the city and if they arrive they are typically killed on the spot. The Invictus and Lancea Sanctum would have to marshal a large force to take Providence back and dislodge the Ordo Dracul. With how powerful they are, it would seem almost impossible to do so at this point. However the Blood Prometheans still cause wasteland, disquiet, torment, etc. So that could be used against them if the Invictus / Lancea Sanctum bloc somehow figured it out.

              I picked the Geheim because their parent clan is Ventrue and they have access to Blut Alchemie which makes sense given the Prometheans. Having a Moroi or Azerkatil Sheriff just seemed to make sense, but I do take your meaning by having everyone with a bloodline.

              The prince's name is Elliot Geheim and his stats are kind of high. Let's just say has he as Blood Potency of 8 and must find creative ways to feed. He may be close to torpor soon, but still believes he has a decade or so left in him. Elliot is still attempting to groom an heir so that they change of power experiences little hiccups.

              Does that give you a better idea? Anything I am missing?

                  Hey there, quite coincidently I've been reading through Damnation City recently and I have a couple of suggestions to add to the already excellent advice that's been given.

                  First off, honestly I wouldn't put any hard numbers to anything. In a game like Requiem I've found that hard stats often end up limiting one's options rather than aiding them. (For instance: how many Blood Homoculi does the Order have? Who knows really. But break one of the Prince's edicts and one will break down your Haven door and drag you kicking and screaming into the sun.)

                  Second, if you're building an Ordo Dracul city then borrowing some ST advice from Mage the Awakening 2nd Edition might be a good idea. Don't just make the region rife with mysteries for the Dragon's to uncover, make the Princedom itself a mystery to the Defiant. (Example: How did the Ordo Dracul take over? Well, the Conspiracy of Silence and the Chapel & Spear were firm allies for decades. Then, something happened and they started fighting over something. Then, silence for a long time. Finally, an individual starts sending voice recording around to all the prominent Elders of the city introducing themselves as the new Prince and politely informing them of the new rules of the city. Is that true? It's what the players are told by their Elders and superiors. Perhaps they'll discover the real truth someday.)

                      I'll agree with the other esteemed posters, in particular treating the setting much like a bit of Mage. You have the Who, What, How and Why to consider. At the moment you've focused on the Who and How, a bit of What, by you're mostly handwaving the Why. This is gentle criticism, as the base assumption of all Vampire domains is "we want safety+blood, and thus need control."

                      But as pointed out, the Ordo doesn't control many cities. Why? Because for what they usually care about they don't need to. Why is Providence different in that regard? Surely the movers behind the Ordo there could have just picked someplace of comparable population and less established hierarchy and made it their own with less fuss, no? (I'm looking at Rhode Island, here.) If academia are needed there are sleepy little midwestern college towns in relative abundance.

                      So what makes Providence worth the fight? And then, yes, from the player's perspective the How should be (haha) obfuscated enough to make that a question for investigation as well. You as ST may have exactly How in mind, but hold things loosely and you may find the players pushing you toward new and even cooler ideas.

                      --Khanwulf

                              Originally posted by mikerand View Post
                              The Invictus and Lancea Sanctum are not welcome in the city and if they arrive they are typically killed on the spot.
                              I would carefully think about this policy. Invictus and Lancea Sanctum vampires from outside Providence likely don't care who holds "the sewer of New England", but if their childer, sires, allies, friends, and faithful supporters keep getting killed every time they pass through on 95... well, whatever force the Ordo Dracul has is unlikely to stand up to a concentrated effort by dozens of aggrieved kindred. Plus, baldly stating that those vampires are killed reduces the mysterious atmosphere.

                              Maybe instead any "foreign" vampire who passes through the city finds they are missing a few hours of their memory. The last thing they remember is driving northbound past the PVD airport exit, and the next thing they knew, it was three hours later and they were crossing into Massachusetts, a trip that shouldn't take more than 45 minutes. They aren't injured or anything, but it's clear that "stuff happened" while in this fugue. The seating arrangement in vehicles may have changed, maybe the gas tank (or blood pool) had been running low and is now full, or they are missing a small personal item, like a paperback novel they were carrying or one sock has vanished off their leg. Maybe the radio is tuned to a station they would never listen to. Or maybe there are additional items in the car the passengers don't recognize, like a movie ticket stub, a half empty Del's Lemonade, or a used latex glove sticky with some unidentifiable goo....

                              Just the feeling that someone in Providence was able to mess with you like that... it's such a personal violation... It would probably keep more vampires away than simply killing intruders.

                              Example: "Chervyl, did I hear you wuh driving down to New London next week? Well, take 495 to Woostah, then 395 south. I know it's out of the way, but trust me, something is just, uhh, wicked wrong in Providence. I dunno what, but every time I go through theh it creeps me the hell out. Weird, weird shit just stahts happening anytime I'm between Pawtucket and War'ick."

                              But just "bang! the other guys are dead!", is a little heavy handed for the Ordo, and is likely to draw others' attention.

                              As for any who try to relocate there, well, gaslight 'em. Use lost time, missing personal items, and haven doors mysteriously getting their locks changed (while the haven's owner was there!). Maybe the arriviste goes to sleep in their Cranston haven at dawn, but awakens at sunset in their backup haven in Bristol. Maybe they suddenly gain a dot in one of their disciplines for a night or two, or a bane they have had for years simply vanishes for a few nights. Maybe every human they feed on winds up dead a few days later, with no pattern: muggings gone wrong, suicides, traffic crashes, carbon monoxide leaks, whatever, the interloper's herd is doomed. Maybe they lose an hour or so of memory, and find a tissue sample has been taken. Or maybe they awaken with a stake in their bed, or a bloody stake in their hand.

                              The Ordo probably can't force them to leave, it's more effective using horror to make the interlopers desperately pray they find a way to leave.

                              Think horrific mystery, not open threats.
