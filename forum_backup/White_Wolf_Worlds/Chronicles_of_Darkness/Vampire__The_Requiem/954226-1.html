    I agree that the RAW seems to be as Vitalis and others have said. I am sympathetic, having thought that exploit was cannon but rules are rules.

    That said, one nitpick:


    Size here has a capital S and therefore indicates the variable, not a general reference.

    Page 182 of Blood and Smoke, while talking about objects, indicates that a door is Size 5. So it would appear to me that the 'wall-thickness' argument is a presumption not explicit in the text, as a Door is certainly only as thick as the wall.

    The intent of this requirement is unknown to me, but it seems plausible that it was to prevent Gangrel melding with small objects ('Elder in a coin' would be a funny devotion).

    That said, it is also likely that the intent of the requirement matches the presumption of the 'wall-thickness' argument and this is an innocent inconsistency between RAI and RAW. The 'ground-argument' makes this more plausible than not.

                                            I don't think interpreting rules literally should come at the expense of a good story. The inclusion of concrete was a nod to foundations which are not necessarily at ground level. The key points seem to be the structure has one clear point to meld in and out, the structure is at least as large as the vampire, and the structure can't be moved. A wall, or ceiling, should be fine as long as it fits the same conditions. There are inherent disadvantages to not sticking with the floor, usually the last thing ever touched.

                                            I would not allow for melding through anything with unmarked grave because the intent is to create a hiding place. Primeval Miasma, or a variant, should be used to go through something. That would be the power with the proper intent.

                                            For transport, I would suggest a Protean/Vigor devotion that allows for melding into the floors of ships, train cars, or airplanes. I would stick to more or less the same spot would be used to exit as enter. The added boost of power makes up for the fact that the structure moves. The idea is too good let technicalities get in the way. Protean 1 / Vigor / Obfusctate 5 for the vampire pirate's ghost ship is what I would be thinking.
