    Bloodline: The Orsini

    12-18-2015, 02:06 PM
    Existence itself is a contract. We may choose its form, perhaps faith, perhaps law, but we - more than any - are judged on how we keep it.

    Inspiration: Clarice Orsini: Da Vinci's Demons, Cersei Lannister: Game of Thrones.

    Background: The dynastic policies of the middle and renaissance ages became critical to the mortal power structures of Europe. Lineages and families of note would trade their scions between themselves, seeing shifts of land and power along with these arranged marriages. The mortal line of the Orsini entrenched themselves into (or under) the Italian nobility, seizing upon the connections of the Roman Catholic Papacy to further their own reach across Europe.

    When the power brokers of this extensive family were brought into the Kindred fold, the byzantine alliances and hierarchies also made the natural jump into Kindred politics. The First and Second Estates benefited the most from these kindred attuned to the royal and/or divine contracts of Europe, though with their Roman Catholic beliefs converting almost entirely to the tenets of the Lancea et Sanctum.

    In the modern nights, some of the Orsini oaths and contracts seem positively antiquated. However, others made the transition from feudalism, to guild structures, to mercantilism, to modern capitalism quite easily.

    The Becoming: Lineage is very important to the Bloodline, oftentimes rivaling the extensive histories of the Ventrue in their record-keeping. Any Mekhet Childer created by an Orsini while under an active Oath of Bloody Matrimony are considered legitimate and will be approached by the greater dynasty as a natural part of the Requiem.

    For Mekhet not blessed by Sires in such arrangements, there are two other ways for Kindred to join the line:
    • Enter into an Oath of Bloody Matrimony with an Orsini of good standing (without the Tainted condition).
    • Perform an Oath of the Sanguine Verdict on an Orsini of poor standing (with the Tainted condition).
    When choosing Orsini Childer, Sires will commonly look for mortals in the intellectual pursuits. Lawyers and barristers, writers and librarians, people familiar with the power of language and its many nuances. Lineages of multiple generations might see elder benedictine monks side-by-side with neonate InBoard moderators comparing calligraphic texts with social media trends.

    Interactions with the Danse: Historically, the majority of the Orsini line has been involved within the Invictus or the Lancea et Sanctum. This very much mirrored their mortal connections to the dynasties of Europe and the powered halls of the papacy. Their codified upbringing also finds an easy place among the Ordo Dracul, and even crafting more intellectual Carthian Experiments.

    Orsini less often lead a city or Covenant themselves, preferring instead to act as seconds and assistants to a more direct power. As talented Notaries, their services lend themselves to Seneschals, Advisers and Deputies of all stripes.

    Cities with a strong Orsini presence will tend to develop very structured rituals, habits, and laws - especially if members find themselves on both sides of any opposing factions.

    Clan: Mekhet

    Nickname: Oathbound, Scribblers

    Bloodline Bane: The Oathbound Curse: Orsini blood runs most hot on the page. Members of the Bloodline must be under the active effects of a major Blood-Oath at all times. If they are not under an active (in good standing) Blood-Oath, they suffer the Languid condition. In addition to falling to torpor, this condition may be resolved by swearing, reactivating or repairing a major Blood-Oath.

    The following are considered Major Blood-Oaths:
    • Oath of Action (Requiem 2nd)
    • Oath of Abstinence (Secrets of the Covenants)
    • Oath of Bloody Matrimony (Custom)
    • Oath of the Clear Accounting (Custom)
    • Oath of the Faithful Supplicant (Custom)
    • Oath of Office (Secrets of the Covenants)
    • Oath of the True Knight (Secrets of the Covenants)
    In addition, any time an Orsini knowingly violates a Blood-Oath, their spirit appears as the worst type of criminal to any who look upon it. Gain the Tainted condition. Rather than a soul of any departed, use the Blood Potency and personality of the Notary who penned the broken Oath to drive the condition's effects. Resolve the condition normally.

    Bloodline Disciplines: Auspex, Dominate, Celerity, Obfuscate

    Bloodline Gift: An Oath Above: Orsini may add the rating of any Dynasty Membership (Orsini) merit to their Status (Covenant) merit when determining:
    • If they meet the Covenant Status pre-requisite of a Notary or Oath Merit.
    • Any variable effects in an Oath tied to Covenant Status.
    Orsini also have a number of custom Oaths available to their line. New(ish) Oath terms:
    • Administered: This is an Oath a Notary learns and can subsequently administer to others. The Notary purchases the Oath, not the participants.
    • Communal: This is an Oath that affects more than one Kindred. Any involved Kindred may contribute to the Oath, but the full cost must be paid by the pair or grouping.
    • Personal: This Oath is sworn by the person directly. They must swear or affirm the Oath in the presence of a character with the Notary merit. The Oath-taker pays for the merit themselves.
    Oath of Bloody Matrimony (•••) [Communal]

    This Oath has seen perhaps the largest evolution among the Orsini. Originally, it was a Pact between two families, based around the political and dynastic marriages of the middle ages. As Childer-as-property fell out of favor, it evolved into a direct Oath between a Kindred husband and wife. More modernly, it has changed form to represent common law arrangements, domestic partnerships, same-sex and asexual marriages.

    Each partner composes and recites personal vows of matrimony to each other (each partner may choose different vows if they wish). They then mix their blood into a suitable vessel (many still use traditional Sanctified chalices) and share it, sealing the Oath between them.

    Effect: Violating any of their marriage vows becomes a level two breaking point for the Kindred. Regardless on the results of the breaking point, the offended partner intuitively knows which specific vow has been broken.

    Additionally, while the Oath remains in effect and in good standing, each partner may feed from the other without consequence. This feeding within the bounds of marriage avoids the creation of any blood bond, vitae addictions, or the negative aspects of the Kiss. The Invictus and Sanctified covenants do not see this form of mutual feeding as perversion, but instead a blessed union.

    Both members gain blood sympathy toward each other at one step while the Oath is active. Mekhet in Bloody Matrimony with an Orsini of good standing (not possessed of the Tainted condition) may manifest the Orsini Bloodline if they meet Blood Potency requirements. Any Mekhet Childer of such a marriage may automatically join the Bloodline upon meeting the Blood Potency requirements as well.

    Oath of the Clear Accounting (•••) [Communal]

    This Oath sets the foundation between Orsini Sires and their Childer. It guarantees a (somewhat) safe place to transition from the living to the dead. The Sire swears to allow the Childe freedom from their supernatural manipulation during this training process, and in exchange the Childe pledges dedicated service to the Sire for that same duration. Most Oaths end once the Accounting has been successful, though some Childer/Sire pairings continue it long after, some out of mutual respect, some out of mutual distrust.

    Effect: All attempts by the Sire to influence the Childe via Auspex, Dominate, Theban Sorcery, Predatory Aura or the Blood Bond automatically fail. In exchange, the Sire may inflict the Tasked Condition on the Childe at their leisure, regardless of either character possessing the Dynasty Membership merit. New tasks replace any unresolved ones (they do not stack).

    (TASKED - Your character’s clan, covenant, or family tasked her with a duty, and the responsibility carries weight. Take the 8-again quality on all rolls relating to the task. Any rolls not pertaining to the task lose the 10-again quality. Possible Sources: Dynasty Membership Merit. Resolution: Complete the task; fail the task. Beat: n/a)

    Oath of the Collusive Shade (••••) [Personal]

    A rare Oath even amongst the Orsini, it is sworn as a last ditch effort to protect themselves against the ultimate loss of control - possession by the Strix.

    Effect: Orsini may swear this Oath as a Reflexive Action instead of making a resistance roll against the Possess Kindred (2nd Ed. p205) action. First, as long as the Strix gained any successes on the possession roll, count it as an Exceptional Success - The Strix suffers no penalties to using the host's skills and disciplines.

    Additionally:
    • The host suffers no breaking points for any atrocities or crimes committed by the possessing Strix. This includes the benefits and drawbacks for Diablerie - it does not taint the host.
    • The host is immune to any uses of Synthesis by the Strix.
    • The host may spend a point of willpower to inflict the drawbacks of the Tainted condition on the Strix. The dark veins are already there, but the host may apply their Blood Potency as a penalty to any one die-pool of the Strix during the chapter, or as a bonus to any one die-pool opposing the Strix during the chapter.
    Oath of the Faithful Supplicant (•••) [Personal]

    Traditionally this Oath is sworn by the Sanctified members of the Orsini, but its base premise can be expanded to other creeds or faiths. The supplicant physically embodies a tenet of their faith in a room or a building. It could be a prayer room, a full church, a confessional booth, anything appropriate to the specific creed or ideology. While this location remains untainted, it acts as a direct bulwark to the Orsini's conviction.

    Effect: Choose a Humanity Sin and the Holy Day bane. The Humanity Sin must be something feasibly permissible by your Creed or Ideology, and the Holy Day chosen must be something related to that Creed or Ideology. Do not check for degeneration when committing the chosen sin so long as it does not violate the tenets of your creed or ideology. This does not count against the limit of banes a Kindred may normally express.

    Additionally, interacting with a fellow believer within the location on the Holy Day allows the Supplicant to refresh one Willpower point. If your chosen location is destroyed, the above effects end, and check for detachment as if it were a three-dot Haven.

    Oath of the Sanguine Verdict (•) [Personal]

    This Oath is used by a bastard Orsini or Mekhet entirely unrelated to the Orsini, to enter the bloodline without going through the process of Bloody Matrimony. The oath-taker must capture (or be given) an Orsini suffering under the Tainted condition gained through Diablerie or the Oathbound curse.

    Effect: The Oath-taker drains the tainted Orsini of all blood, then starts the very first step of Diablerie. This destroys the victim. If the Oath-Taker gets at least one success, the Oath is triggered and the Orsini Bloodline manifests in the Oath-Taker (They must meet the Blood Potency requirements.)

    If the new Orsini stops the process at this point, the Diablerie is not fully committed - the soul is tasted but allowed to go on to its next destination. The new Orsini gains no other advantages from the attempted Diablerie, but must only check against premeditated murder in terms of breaking points. Once completed, replace this merit with the first dot of Dynasty Membership (Orsini).

    The Oath-Taker can fully continue the Diablerie if they wish, using all the standard rules.

    Oath of the Serpent's Tongue (••) [Communal]

    This Oath of Secrecy is often utilized for covert operations or missions of a sensitive nature. Used by a handler to control a coterie or team on a specific mission or task, the agents all swear or affirm against revealing the mission to anyone not a part of the same Oath.

    Effect: If an agent reveals critical or sensitive information about the mission to anyone not a part of the Oath, their tongue transforms into a serpent and slithers free of their throat and mouth. By the end of the current night, a similar serpent will approach the Notary. By comparing the pattern of the serpent's scales to the agents' signatures, they can divine who let spill a secret. Killing either snake has no effect on the Oath. Restoring one's tongue so that one can speak again is the equivalent to healing two lethal damage.

    Oath of the Soothed Heart (•) [Administered]

    The Notary writes out a list of conditions as part of the Oath. These conditions describe very specific instructions for a duration of voluntary torpor. The Oath-taker swears or affirms the conditions and swallows a handcrafted splinter which burrows directly into their heart. Once the oath's instructions or conditions are met (or the chapter ends), the splinter dissolves, the Oath-taker emerging from torpor at that point.

    Effect: While the Oath is active, the Kindred is considered staked. Dreams experienced by the Oath-taker while in this torpor are either refreshing or prophetic. Upon emerging from torpor the Kindred may either regain one willpower point, or may utilize one instance of the "Dream Visions" merit as if they were a Mekhet. If the Oath-taker already possesses the Dream Visions merit, they may use it an additional time during one scene (for example, if they meet two new Kindred at the same Elysium event).

    Oath of the Vestal Witness (•••) [Administered]

    This Oath must be sworn in the presence of recognized Kindred officer, nominally in pursuit of official business. The Notary mixes a drop of the Witness' blood with specially prepared ink. After the Oath is taken, the Notary transcribes every question made by the officer and every answer given by the witness into an official testimony. After the testimony is complete, both the officer and witness affix their names.

    Effect: The witness has a strong desire to avoid falsehoods while giving testimony. Any time the witness attempts to deliberately lie, they feel a sense of guilt. While this doesn't force a change of behavior, it gives the witness a second chance to tell the truth if they wish. If another effect or ability would force the witness to lie, they may immediately attempt to resist that ability even though they failed before. This time, add the Kindred Officer's appropriate Status merit to the resistance roll. Success allows the witness to ignore the power and tell the truth (if they wish - though nothing forces them to). All other aspects of the power or Condition remain in effect. After the complete testimony is given, the Notary leaves each page out in the sun for a full day. Any testimony the witness knew to be false is burned away at the edges, leaving only the true statements pure on the parchment.
