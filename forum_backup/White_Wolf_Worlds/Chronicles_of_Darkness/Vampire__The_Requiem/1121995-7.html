                        Hmm... me as a vampire?

                        Clan: Mekhet, without a doubt. As it already stands, I'm the person who can walk into and out of a room without people noticing, pipe up in a conversation I was part of all along and have people who arrived after me think I was eavesdropping, and know things other people don't think I should know just because I can listen and remember. They're difficult skills, apparently, but I don't find them difficult to master. Perhaps most importantly is that people find that somewhat unnerving, and I sometimes enjoy the satisfaction of putting someone exceptionally arrogant in their place by virtue of simply knowing or remembering more a little too much to be healthy.

                        Covenant: That's difficult to say. Not sure any of them would be particularly appealing to me, but the Invictus offer some well-needed stability, the Carthians at least have an appealing party line, and mastery of Theban Sorcery could, barring the abhorrent philosophy and theology of the Lancea et Sanctum, be a well-needed anchor to my Humanity. If I was to pick my most likely option, it would probably be Carthian liaison to other Covenants, the kind who is very, very careful to toe the line and not cross anyone.

                        Nightlife: I, completely by accident, and due to my overdeveloped sense of loyalty and duty to my associates, would likely become rather popular in the local Domain. I'm someone you can talk to, I'm someone you can trust. I'm a flawless confidante with spotless honour and far too much integrity for the average vampire, but just because I wouldn't betray a confidence doesn't mean that I don't know things and would be able to, quietly, delicately, find a way to nudge things. And just because I'm the kind of person who tries to solve his friends' problems with hugs and unconditional support doesn't make me harmless. You see, one thing I've learned about myself, something that would be horribly terrifying if I ever became a vampire, is that when I get angry I don't run hot, I run cold. The more pissed off I get the more distant from the situation I become, until I become a brutal, clinical machine capable of using information and insight as scalpel, pruning sheers, or sledgehammer. There was an incident at my high school involving someone who became unhealthily obsessed with me and they'd threatened to kill themselves if I didn't spend a certain amount of time with them, and used that same threat to try and blackmail me into sleeping with them. When I still preferred my actual friends they went a step further, and they went into excruciating, graphic, uncomfortable detail of hypothetical sexual exploits between her and I in the school common area. Most of the high school couldn't look at my face for almost a week. I eventually reached the point where I verbally, publicly, tore her apart until she actually assaulted me to get me to stop talking and I absolutely did not give a damn. She ended up expelled for assaulting and threatening another student.

                        If my vampiric self in Frenzy is anything even remotely like that? Run. Run as fast and as far as you can when I tell you to because you will not be able to tell when my switch is flipped on your own until you're already bleeding out.

                        It probably also wouldn't help that, regardless of how distasteful and uncomfortable I find violence, I am very, very good at it. I've been practicing martial arts for twelve years now, and my master has mentioned a couple of times that despite my unwillingness to hurt people and my reluctance to utilize pain in medium-force sparring, I'm one of the quickest to pick up exactly how to use a self-defence technique to drop someone to the floor or get them to submit. I can see gaps in other fighters' guards that I don't take advantage of because of the brutality involved in the potential attack. But once I was in a situation where I didn't particularly care what happened to the other person. I was at a bar, had lost my friend, and when I found her some guy was kissing her. This wasn't an unusual scenario for her so I was going to leave and text her when I got home to let her know I was home safe (this was an established pattern for us). Then I noticed a couple things in the next second or two. He'd pinned her hands, and she wasn't kissing back. By the time I realized that, she'd kneed him in the groin and bolted past me, then the guy turned to lash out with a fist at the closest thing nearby. Me. A switch I wasn't aware I had flipped to the "off" position. I countered, caught the guy's arm, and using his momentum I front flipped him, pinning him to the floor with an arm bar braced against my knee and a heel planted on his collarbone, and kept him there until security came to throw him out. I was still riding that adrenaline buzz when I drove my friend home, then drove myself home, but when I got home I was a shaking, sobbing mess. I knew, with absolute certainty, that if I had just applied a little more force in a slightly different way I could have dislocated his shoulder and snapped his collarbone. But I hadn't. The fact I know I could, that I was capable and aware, still scares the ever-loving daylights out of me. I seriously doubt that me as a vampire would have that kind of control.

                        Elder: If I survived to the point of being an Elder, I'd probably be an utter terror of a Shadow. The loyalty would probably fall away, the inhibitions too. I would have had time to hone my memory beyond simply quick into a precise information engine. I'm the one you come to if you want to borrow a library, I'm the person you broker information with, I'm the one with the strange rules and strange code of honour. My loyalty would have transferred from people to more esoteric Touchstones, and that would make me unpredictable. You could bind me with a Ban and a Bane, but if you pushed me hard enough I doubt the backlash of breaking a Ward would hold me back. My Frenzy burns cold, so you don't know it's happened until you're already dead. Somewhere along the path from Neonate to Ancilla to Elder I would grow to be more accepting of violence, and actively hone my own skills. The Khaibit actually fit me quite nicely. Diligence and loyalty paired with brutal efficiency. The might of Disciplines adding onto centuries of time to hone my martial arts skills from hobby and art form into something that makes me more than capable of protecting myself despite the "Friendly Neighbourhood Vampire" exterior. I would fade into darkness, into obscurity, from all except those who would seek me out. But that doesn't mean that I'm not watching. Maybe I'll amuse myself by walking unnoticed and unseen with Obfuscate. The rush of surprise from my seemingly harmless Mask concealing a brutal machine of a monster will have faded now that people know what I look like. Ah well, simple pleasures. It's the little things that stave off ennui after all.
