    Post your Vampire NPCs!

    08-02-2016, 10:48 AM
    For posting useful and/or interesting active or historical Vampire NPCs for others to use in their games.

    Starting off with:

    Animander Khajag
    The Uncarnate, Whispers in the Blood, Crimson Sorcerer, the Heretic, Blood God, the Eternally Surprised

    Animander Khajag is a myth to most of those who've even heard of him - attributed to all manner of fanciful deeds, powers, and origins. Some Circles of the Crone trace their traditions back to those Khajag was said to be a part of. Rare, restricted documents in the Lancea Et Sanctum accuse someone of that name of heresy and stealing Covenant Secrets. A few elder Dragons have occasionally admitted to knowing of him, but say nothing more. What the few scholars who study the figure have posited is this: he was an old blood sorcerer, probably a Mekhet, who stole some of the secrets of other Covenants in a mad thirst for arcane knowledge and power. What separates him from countless others is that he seems to have partially succeeded in his ambitions. Accurate accounts are sketchy, but some time in the 16th century, he conducted a massive ritual that either incarnated him as a god, or scattered his essence throughout his bloodline and the world. His "adherents" claim he manipulated the ancient dead (Arisen?) into constructing a grand web of Geomancy, co-opted a work of "the Gears of the World" (an "occult matrix"?), and used a synthesis of proto-Cruac, Theban Sorcery, and the nascent Ordo Dracul's transformative philosophies to transcend his Requiem. His "Eternally Surprised" moniker originates from his expression moments before his ascension - one of surprise and wonder at the nature of the divine, his more optimistic followers claim (his more cynical ones liken it more to an expression of "Oh, SHI---!"). It is only known that he didn't utterly destroy himself by various manifestations afterward that indicate he still exists in some fashion.

    Useful for:
    • If you want some cross-Covenant secret magic.
    • If you want a kind of Malkovian hive-mind with an actual guiding intelligence behind it.
    • If you need to explain various "directed circumstances" behind some vampiric event.
    • If you need a ready Archmaster-tier vampire character for PCs to interact with.
    • Any other vampire-equivalent "A Wizard Did It" explanations.
    • The "Ops" bit at the end lets you be a vague or contradictory regarding his powers, goals, or coherency as needed - he might not "be all there" after all.

    Sample Devotions:

    Body Double (Dominate 4, Obsfucate 4)
    Cost: 5 Vitae, 1 Willpower point
    Effect: By means of the Blood, you reshape a recently dead corpse into a physical and mental likeness of yourself. You animate the target as a Revenant, with an appearance matching yours, and a mind with memories like your own (though the vampire may opt to "withhold" or alter some memories prior). The new Revenant may possess Discipline dots up to your Blood Potency, with individual Discipline dots not exceeding your ratings in them. After raising the target, they are an independent actor (though since they think they are you, you can usually predict their immediate future actions).

    Shroud of Might (Resilience 5, Majesty 1)
    Cost: 1+ Vitae, 1 Willpower point
    Effect: By giving the target your Vitae, you can supernaturally enhance their protection and presence. Feed the target up to your Blood Potency in Vitae - for the remainder of the night, they are treated as having your Blood Potency (if higher) for resistance rolls and for Taint of the Predator.

    Sample Plot Hooks:
    • For over 60 years, a heresy worshiping some kindred as a "Blood God" crops up and gets brutally squashed, with only a single survivor remaining to spread the forbidden lore and start anew. The problem is, despite every precaution, it seems there is always at least one who survives the purge, as if some malign intelligence is influencing circumstances in their favor. Now the heretics are threatening to upload their blasphemies to the internet, where any Neonate can find them. They'll be impossible to quash at that point and must be stopped.
    • Something strange has happened to the people of the domain. Some are spontaneously manifesting Disciplines, and this is not only threatening the Masquerade, but making feed more difficult.
      • Worse, they eventually seem to be growing in power. Those fed on repeatedly grow in might faster (Each Vitae taken gives 1 Blood Beat?). Killing them with a fatal feeding not only has its own inherent risks, but are more likely to result in a Revenant than not.
      • Ghouls fed Vitae that was taken by the empowered mortals seem to undergo a progressive affliction, hearing whispers, and eventually engaging in suspicious sleepwalking like behavior. Worryingly, Shadows are reporting similar stains upon them that Diablerists are said to possess (Tainted Condition, but with dice towards actions that the "whispers" want, or more generally the downfall of the vampire?).
      • Kindred rarely travel, but the few who do find their plans "cancelled" and contacts outside the domain growing cold and distant. It eventually becomes clear - your domain is getting quarantined.
    • Some clueless neonate is going around trying to recruit Coteries for some crazy plan. Odd thing is, he got ashed, then a few days later he was back, asking again. No memory of the previous "him". That one got ashed too. The very next day, he's back at it. It would be funny, if it weren't for stories that now there are two of them doing it. Elders are worried if this continues, the Domain will be flooded with the same idiot.

                  I've got a few that I've written up in the past few weeks. I'll start posting them here: thanks for starting this thread.

                  The Lonely Vampire
                  Susanna Myska lives in a small town*. She’s one of very few vampires, if not the only one. Her problem is that she’s on a definite downward spiral: her Humanity is hemorrhaging as she attempts to fight her various addictions. Before being Embraced, she was a smackhead. Now she’s also a vampire. She’s hardcore in the denial stage of her transformation: she spends way too much Vitae on the Blush of Life in order to eat food or drink, then is forced to hunt new Vitae very quickly. Not to mention the risk of frequent Frenzy. In such a small town, this is a problem. She uses her Disciplines to keep herself from being noticed, and tries to stick to large game animals like deer, or occasionally targeting livestock when desperate.

                  Her Sire and mentor tried to get her on the straight and narrow: unfortunately, when they explained that she could get the kind of high she used to get from the blood of those who were intoxicated, all that meant is that she started shooting up her prey with drugs. In a drug-fueled Frenzy she managed to incapacitate her Sire and drain them dry. Now she’s addicted to Vitae as well.

                  Using Her in Vampire Games
                  She’s mostly an antagonist, and a potential Masquerade breach (or at least a likely first suspect to investigate). She can also be manipulated by leveraging her Vitae addiction: she doesn’t have the Resolve to turn down the opportunity to take a drink, and her Sire never told her about the Blood Bond anyway. That being said, what are the chances she doesn’t already have a Level 3 Bond to another Kindred in the city?

                  Also, while not a Diablerist (yet), she still wants at the delicious ruby liquid running through the player characters’ veins. Can you trust that?

                  Of course, as a Little Lost Lamb, maybe the PCs could break her dependency? Get her fixed up, make her a Fair Lady. She’d be a powerful ally, but can she shed the only remnant of her human life?**

                  Finally, as a crazed hermit who’s only an unlucky Frenzy check away from pissing off the wrong creature or breaking the Masquerade, she’s unlikely to have anybody who will look for her if she disappears. A desperate member of the Ordo could always use a test subject for their experiments. Luring her with promises of a regular fix and a sanitary living environment might be just what she needs though.

                  Using Her in Other Games
                  She doesn’t know much about vampire lore, but she knows good places to find the vulnerable members of human society, the drug dealers, prostitutes, and other lowlifes (great as a supernatural Streetwise contact). She knows the territories of other vampires because she either avoids them or tries to find out when they’re vulnerable.
                  In this scenario, she’s a wild card, but not quite as dangerous as she is to other vampires: other supernaturals don’t have Vitae, after all. Of course, if she ever obtains Unnatural Affinity, she may become addicted to a new source of blood.

                  * Author's Note: I came up with Susanna while driving through small towns around San Antonio, Texas. Texas has a bunch of European immigrant families in that area, Susanna herself is Czech Texan.
                  **: AN: I had the idea that maybe Susanna's Touchstone is her drug addiction. It seemed weirdly appropriate, and I don't see vampires getting made very often who are so far down the rung of human society.

                              What's that? Another post already? I had these ready to go. Apologies for not following the same format. This one was created as a potential PC. I had to pare some stuff out and edit to make sure I didn't include a lot of useless info.

                              Conrad Abney, The (Un)Living Trophy
                              Concept: Son of a congresswoman and CEO, on the verge of getting out of his childhood home and embracing a new independent life. Taken by a Kindred with a Point to Prove to the establishment; Sire goes up in flames, so the kid’s left to piece together his Requiem on his own.

                              Mask and Dirge: His Mask is Follower, because to follow is familiar. He responds very positively to a strong central authority figure, and tends to think of even his own goals through the lens of how he can gain approval from others. His Dirge is Competitor; he desperately wants to make his own way, and if he isn’t seduced by a charming tongue he lashes out against anybody he thinks is trying to take advantage of his “weakness.”

                              Touchstone: His parents. They made him. They used him. They loved him. He was going to get away from them. But he didn’t get away from them on his own terms. He was violated and Embraced and now he worries that he’ll never get the closure he needs, the satisfaction of telling his parents “No, I am my own man.” He stays near them, trying desperately to find the opportunity to reveal himself. Perhaps one day he’ll work up the strength to challenge the Masquerade, but for now he watches his parents live their lives without him.

                              Joining the Covenants

                              Carthians
                              His sire was an Invictus, desperate to recruit only the most promising new blood to the Covenant, and also to strike a blow against the reigning Lancea et Sanctum establishment. There was no way Conrad was going to join the Invictus, since they used him to the point of violation. And the Lancea smelled too much like his old life; it was at once familiar and perverse, and he needed to put his old life behind him.
                              Enter Ilga, his Carthian investor. Ilga sold him on the local movement’s focus on laissez-faire capitalism: he would be a Partner, free to advance as quickly or as slowly as was necessary. Deals were the most important aspect of their society, anybody that was part of a deal was treated as an equal, and once a deal was complete there were no more expectations of either Partner.
                              Naturally, this was an unrealistically positive spin on the movement, and the reality of the city, but Conrad was seduced all the same. He accepted Ilga as his Investor, with clear terms as to what was expected of him. He’s not a slave, he’s a Partner, and ready to climb the ranks. Of course, now he’s no longer allowed near his home -- that territory belongs to the Padré -- but whenever he gets a chance he visits home, just to watch.

                              Invictus
                              As a scion of a wealthy, political family, Conrad had been groomed from birth to dominate in the political arena. The Invictus would not let such an asset go: while Conrad’s Sire was a pathetic stooge who would never have made it, Conrad himself was embraced by the Covenant as a promising son. At first he buckled against the Invictus’s rules, and the high-society aspects put him off, and the higher-ranked members wanted to use him just as much as his own family, but the lower-ranked members of the Covenant couldn’t handle him. He quickly outmaneuvered them, and realized that he was a natural. He dominated lesser members of the other Covenants, including rival Dragons, proving Invictus superiority to the reigning Judges.

                              He still buckles under the weight of the Traditions, but he’s a shark in the pond: soon he’ll get to start dictating Tradition.

                              Circle of the Crone
                              Being a vampire is the best thing that could have happened, they say. Or at least it’s not bad. No, being a vampire is natural. He’s not undead, he’s still alive. The Circle removed Conrad’s trepidation about his new condition, and he’s eager to start pushing the limits of his new body. He’s hungry, and he no longer thinks that’s a problem.

                              Ordo Dracul
                              His sire didn’t embrace him out of spite or as a political statement, but as a means to understand himself a little better. Sire’s death meant that Conrad “inherited” the Ordo materials in sire’s possession. He wants to know more about himself.

                                  The Sun Chasers
                                  This coterie of dragons has three shared interests the Mystery of the Ascendant, keeping their Humanity high and bikes. By day the true members of the coterie can be found (or could if anyone was up to the task) checking out nests and trespassing on other kindreds tertiary, by night their activitys are no stranger than the rest of their covenant. Few think to question why they all need tinted visors (so no one can see their face makes much more sense than going out at day) and dragons knowing stuff they shouldn't is par for the course even when their leader isn't a Mekhet.
                                  The membership of the Sun Chasers can loosely be divided between the full members who can go out by day and are seen as useful and the pledges (or whatever else the full members feel like calling them) who are expected to make themselves as useful as they can and learn the coils.
                                  Of corse the Sun Chasers know that all work and no play makes for a dull dragon and so they also know all the best indoor activities and how long they're likely to be exposed to the sun getting in (if 20 minutes takes you more than one Vitae to fix you need to work on that).
                                  A full member is expected to:
                                  Spend a full day outside
                                  Feed twice in one day
                                  Feed in direct sunlight facing the sun
                                  Spend at least half an hour outside in normal clothes
                                  All monthly, also as required they are expected to
                                  Defend the clubs honour
                                  Investigate
                                  A understanding of the first two coils is also required, three is preferred.
                                  The leadership and founding members are a Mekhet by the name of Heather and a Gangrel called Emma, both joined the Ordo before dropping below humanity 7 and put together the clubs structure when others came to them for instruction in the Mystery of the Ascendant.
                                  Heather is the de facto leader of the two and is the more active in searching out new things to investigate, at present she has kept her Humanity high and is thinking of applying for permission to study Ziva, she has a Twilight Judge in her family if she goes up 2 or 3 generations and then down 1 or 2 and thinks she can get him on board by arguing that the Ordo should have someone who has practical knowledge of every aspect of the vampire condition, even coils that don't really make them stronger.
                                  Emma is the better mechanic of the two and has recently overtaken her friend in her understanding of the coils, she has a better grasp of how the club is running and who needs kicked out of their comfort zone (Heather can always be counted on to find such a task).

                                  Story Hooks
                                  One of the players wants to learn a new coil, his new mentor calls him "pledge" and expects him to help fix her bike, hope you have dots in Craft and Ride (if not she teaches them first).
                                  Emma's had enough and is starting her own chapter, while Heather try's to get to grips with the hands on side of things two day-riding vampire biker gangs that aren't sure how they feel about each other have awkward encounters at local nest sights.
                                  A Helion has taken Issue with these vampires who don't respect the sun. The club don't know what's happening but any test's a path to improvement. Two questions form in one members mind, can this be used to reliably summon sunlight at night and is their any way to do a drive by of an Elysium playing "The Sun Has Got His Hat On" without being subject to a Blood Hunt?
                                  Heather has recently formed a new touch stone with a group of mages she's found and taken to hanging with by day, the curiosity goes both ways and any enemy's of the Ordo may be subject to scryings and "pranks" while those who her new friends disagree with find they face an unseen hunter who feeds them blood before they are fed on in turn.

                                          Andrea Meadows
                                          Mekhet

                                          Andrea awoke to total darkness. Total darkness was where the Mekhet felt her best, after all. The blackness outside spoke to the blackness within. She tugged the blanket from over her head and rose to a crouch in the close confines of the closet. She listened at the door for a full minute. Satisfied she was alone, she opened it and entered the cramped bedroom. Like a ghost, Andrea slipped through the cozy house to the living room, weaving through piles of books to the flashing red light on the side table. She tapped the button.

                                          “Andrea, it’s Mark. I was wondering if you were open for tutoring next Wednesday. We’ve got a World History midterm coming up. Leslie might want to sit in, too. Let me know.”

                                          Tutoring – high school or college, in a range of subjects -- was how she earned spending money. Plus, she got to interact with people. She always thought of them as people, except when she didn’t. Andrea looked around the room, currently lit streetlights shining through bar-warded windows. She hadn’t bothered taking down the Kinkaid knockoff or the photos of people she never knew. It was Matilda Simpson’s house, after all – and still was, as far as the city and the bank were concerned.

                                          Andrea hadn’t intended to drain the familyless pensioner. But rather than cry over the accident, she used it to her advantage. The lady’s check was direct deposited, and her pin numbers and other relevant information were conveniently stashed in a drawer. A computer and some phone calls later, and Mrs Simpson had internet. Between that and the local bookstore, she was set for reading material – the second-most important thing in her existence. The first, of course, was blood. First things first.

                                          She loved to learn things, so she took night classes, went to evening readings and read voraciously. She liked people okay, and was good at reading them too. But every few nights, Andrea stopped seeing them as people – they were kine, the herd, and she was the wolf among them.


                                          A clerk at an Ohio B. Daltons, Andrea was unprepared for the attack in the back corridors of the mall after closing. Her Sire brought her back but told her little, intending her to be a distraction for some Hounds. But Andrea figured out pretty quickly what she was, and left town before there were bodies to find. in St. Louis she found a lick to explain the basics, as well as how to keep under the radar, but was politely shown the door after that. Over the next few years, she developed her pattern of feeding once or twice a week, stashing the too-frequent bodies. When she developed her ability to cloak herself and her victim, her feedings were less hurried and more careful. She stayed in a local until she discovered other vampires, and then headed for another part of town (or another town). In 2009, she arrived in Vancouver, and thus far she's seen no undead. She currently lives in the house of a pensioner she accidently killed. The old lady had direct deposit, and the power company drew from her account, so she has a small, free house with a well-secured closet in the master bedroom. Having come to terms with being undead, she spends her early evenings reading or taking classes. She can be amiable if not overly friendly, and sometimes talks to people at bus stops or cafes. When she's ready to hunt, she's all business, and when she picks out her prey, she's remorseless. Invisible, she grabs and drinks, and both are unseen. Then she's gone, and the victim staggers with spinning head.

                                          Details
                                          Mask: Scholar
                                          Dirge: Survivor
                                          Banes: Hated by Animals, Face of Hunger*
                                          *When the Beast decides it is time to feed, the compassionate part of Andrea shuts down. She isn't unduly savage, she may make some effort to not kill, but people become prey, and she a cunning predator. Once sated, she considers the consequences of her actions with no thought to the morality of them. If she kills in a frenzy of anger, or feeds when she isn't "mentally prepared," the weight of her action will take its toll.


                                          Plot Hook
                                          -In our game, Andrea was completely off the radar, living in a boring suburban part of town. Then one of her kills woke up in a dumpster and caused a stir with his prodigious feeding rate. The Sheriff found the revenant, and traced him to the accidental Sire. As punishment, she made Andrea kill the revenant herself.

                                          -The PCs come across her. How they react to her depend on their station in the Danse Macabre, and how much or little they know. How she reacts may depend on the first impression -- she's a survivor, and while she might fight for her digs and haunts, they aren't worth her unlife. If handled with care, she could be a font of information, both academic and local gossip.
