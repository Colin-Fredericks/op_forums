        Originally posted by Yossarian View Post

        Something that's always irked me (mildly) is that that videogame's subtitle is basically meaningless. We decided we should change that...!
        Ah... but is it, though?

        I wonder if it is a very subtle reference and meaning, and a potential clue about the overall story. You have the clans, which are major bloodlines, and you have some bloodlines in the game. All connect to one original vampire through blood. I was never satisfied with the idea of an Antediluvian being in the Sarcophagus. Why? Because all blood lineages were feeling the emergence of the relic! That means that you need a common ancestor for the whole Kindred population to be affected, regardless of Clan. Which leaves only one option...

        What if Caine ruled for centuries, using his trusted Ghoul as a frontpiece? The king could operate in the day, and fulfil his master's wishes. At some point something happened, and the king was buried with Caine.Hence the mummy in the coffin. In addition, if you read the ship logs, you'll find that something was eating the crew along the way, including a superstitious Turk who had been speaking of some form of legend of doom surrounding the sarcophagus. Also, it was opened up from the inside, meaning it was deliberately built to be opened up if you are inside. The had to be an internal unlocking mechanism.

        Considering all of that, It might be that Jack was used by Caine to get him into the world, using the link of Blood to commune with him. Someone told Professor Johannsen about the location, and it would imply Kindred involvement. From there, he used the empty stone box to cause mayhem in the city, to have the enemies of the Anarch Movement tear themselves apart, thus getting rid of them without the need for a war being fought by the Anarchs.

        And the Cabbie? Maybe he was a herald, of sorts, for Caine? An observer and mouthpiece for him, when needed. Someone deeply ancient. Given the Malkavian PC's reaction, could it be Malkav himself? A possibility.


        All speculation from myself, but it makes you wonder why Troika had that title. Given just how much of the game has a huge attention to detail, maybe it is a subtle nuance to be understood?
