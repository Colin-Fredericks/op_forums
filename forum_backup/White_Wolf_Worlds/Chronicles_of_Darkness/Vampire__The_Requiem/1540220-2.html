        The covenants aren't concordats writ large; it's more like concordats are covenants writ small, if that makes any sense. We're mostly saying that the covenants are more than social organizations; there's no exclusivity, just supernatural support, whatever you want that to mean; this is very briefly discussed in Dark Eras 2 in a blink-and-you-miss-it sidebar. Like, a coterie of Kindred can't spontaneously create a covenant-level power, but former members might carry old trade secrets with them if they don't get whacked by their former comrades (Laws are kind of exception to this; they all have an implied Status prerequisite unless otherwise specified, so you actually can't steal them by the strictest reading of the rules. And Oaths just work differently than all the other powers in general because you technically don't buy them).

        Also, since this is a book of all the Covenants...will VII be having one?
        Maybe, but I'd be more inclined to do them in a bigger book of horrors, if at all. VII is more interesting as a multiple choice antagonist, and we'd have to be really sure of the idea (or ideas) we were presenting.

        But Belial's Brood is definitely on the table.
