              Lots of cool stuff, but a few things that made me strongly scratch my head (as I described in my comments in the actual blog).

              Pledge To The Worthless one is very fitting as an ultimately damning and unreversible sacrifice. And it says nasty nasty things about Theban Sorcery, which I love. (Interesting question in the context of it though, Charlaquin.) Apocalypse and the Judgement Fast are suitably terrifying. But again, the ratings for some of these rituals are all over the place. With all its benefits Apple Of Eden has to be at least a 2 if not a 3 dot ritual, and Miracle Of The Dead Sun should outright be a 5 dot ritual, given it blots out the sun and effectively allows Kindred to act as though it was fully nighttime for the duration. To mention a few.

              Already spoke about my gripe with Crusade in the blog (the second and third dots are fine, but that first is just weird and completely turns me off, which is a shame because I really wanted to like it), but there are very cool merits in this otherwise.
