    Unless I'm wrong about how Unnatural Affinity works, it only provides another type of creature the vampire can feed on that counts as Kindred for feeding restrictions. It does not remove the ability to feed on Kindred.

    In the case of the Strix, given the dangers involved in this type of prey I am inclined to be generous with how to feed on them. At the very least, (attempting) feeding on anyone possessed by Strix would work, corpse, revenant or human.
    Given the fluff, namely how the Strix are physical shadows and have vitae distributed through out their bodies, that they feed on breath and they can tear each other apart to devour one another...I'm also willing to take it a step further. I'd say that Unnatural Affinity: Strix let's you consume the Birds of Dis in the normal Shadow Form, provided you can keep it from running away. You might not need to breath or grasp the darkness, but you can inhale smoke* if you choose too.

    Just beware; I'd rule the Kiss doesn't apply so there is nothing stopping the Owl from trying to take control of your body.


    *yes the strix aren't literal smoke, it's poetic license.

            Originally posted by Aron Times View Post
            Edit: What the hell? Why do I have only 1 post, and no avatar? I've been posting on the White Wolf forums for a long time. I'm pretty sure I've been posting here for a while. Was there a recent forum purge?

            Hello, I'm the player of the Strix-embraced vampire. I originally wanted to play a Ventrue Carthian or Invictus, but the DM talked me into playing something more... unique. When he asked me if I was okay with being Embraced by the Strix, I thought that he wanted me to play a Strix-possessed vampire with the memories and personality of my character. He clarified that I would be a normal vampire of the clan of the last vampire the Strix fed from, in this case, Mekhet.

            What happened was that I'm starting play with a BP 6, Humanity 2 Mekhet with no social merits. He was a disenfranchised outcast in life, and he entered death as a super-powered monster who is ignorant of the Traditions or even of his own undead state. First thing he did upon waking up was to contact the police. He just found out that he was supposed to have been dead for two weeks. He doesn't buy it, and believes that he was attacked by a psycho "last night," and the cops he's talking to are bullshitting him because he's a foreigner (a Korean who moved to the USA to study in college) and they can't be bothered to help some stupid squinty-eyed immigrant.

            Yes, my toon is tearing holes into the Masquerade out of ignorance. He has noticed that he doesn't need to breathe, but with Humanity 2, it doesn't really bother him that much.

            We had a brainstorming session last night with the DM about how the heck I'm going to survive for any length of time in this state. I told him that if my character were completely rational and knowledgeable about the vampiric state, he's go into torpor for 25+ years so he wouldn't have to deal with having to feed on vampires and getting enslaved by the blood bond. We then talked about Unnatural Affinity, and he told me to choose wisely. Possible contenders were: ghosts, werewolves, strix.

            1. I have no way of interacting with ghosts, so it would be quite tricky to feed from them.
            2. Werewolves, well, I have no problem rerolling, but not so soon after character creation.
            3. Strix? He told me that he'd ask Onyx Path, and here we are.

            As for the Strix, I was wondering if it would be a way for my character to enter the All-Night Society as a Strix Detector/Hunter of Owls. My character can taste someone's blood, and if it nourishes him, it must be Strix or vampire blood. We weren't sure if he had a way of telling apart the blood of a normal vampire vs. a Strix-possessed vampire. Presumably, he'd be able to feed on a Strix-possessed mortal.

            Unless you're trying to handicap yourself and push the rules to the breaking limit, I'd question the decision making here. The high BP, low humanity, and feeding restriction on their own would make playing a starting level character difficult, but combine those together and it's going to be a mess.

            He's two steps away from being a mindless monster, and even passing as human is going to be difficult given that restriction. That means sinking vitae, which is already a valuable commodity for your guy, into any attempts at social interaction. As a neonate, he doesn't really have the political leverage to arrange for feeding off other kindred, and unless he sets up some sort of symbiotic relationship with a werewolf, he's going to die. quickly. At humanity 2 with no built-in social network, even interacting with other kindred is going to be a hot mess.That combination of traits means he's going to be isolated both from humanity and other kindred. Even if he doesn't get ripped to shreds trying to feed off a lupine, he's going to lose his humanity pretty damn quickly.

            Good luck! I'm curious to see how it plays out.

                                "The Call of the Void, Mr Murdock." The therapist clicked his lips as the words flowed out. "Tell me more about this concept."

                                Murdock rose to speak but as his voice started to form, words of various sinister construction fell out from him. He retched over, blood falling freely out of his mouth as vomit hit the floor. His mouth continued it's motion. Speaking a thousand harsh concepts.

                                The Therapist flashed his optics. His face unmoved. "Ah. Most intriguing I must say. Seeing as you are currently incapacitated in this manner, I found it prudent to do such research on my own behalf on the subject. I dearly wish to hear your side of the concept, giving your present condition as a case study, but it stands to reason that is such a thing as sticking your hand too far into the fire."

                                Murdock fell on all fours, the voices coursing and pulsing through his brain like writhing larva of some insidious parasite. He struggled to remain lucid. He grasped the islands of the mind.

                                "Call of the void is the social phenomenon of which one has the thought of engaging in harmful actions against another or even one's self. The thought of holding a knife and the fleeting thought of stabbing a friend. The impulse of driving a car and slamming it head on onto another while driving. Such a puzzling thing to be included into human nature." The Therapist folded his hands together. "Don't you agree Mr Murdock?"

                                Murdock continued to vomit, his vision swarmed. Shadows danced around his eyes and inner demons laughed in his ears.

                                "I must say, given your present condition, could you perhaps enlighten me on how this... situation came to be?"

                                Murdock's body went limp as his mind called forth events. With raspy voices composed of talons and blood, they spoke. They spoke of the glee of which they killed, murdered and allowed themselves to be murdered. They spoke of the toy-men which they broke and delighted in their fear of the hunt. They spoke of the families shattered, and parliaments dissolved. They spoke of structures long dead and buried awakened only for the pure amusement of being slain again. They spoke with laughter of the never dead.

                                The Therapist tapped his pen, his notes long compiled. For a long while, there was silence.

                                "Thank you Mr Murdock. That was quite enlightening." He slowly turned away, leaving Murdock to arise in a quite unfamiliar place. A place with no doors and only a slight heavily barred window. Red horizon covered the sky as leering creatures walked past and occasionally tapped on the glass. Panic within him rose and for once, all the voices shared the same fear.

                                "You see, Mr Murdock, You are quite the case study. We can't have you running wild to... degenerate nor shall we need you to self repair. you shall be kept as an example as your kind. A lesson for the Analysts to practice on. We are quite thankful for your kind's ability for stasis. Quite wonderful to work with a blank slate."

                                Murdock panicked. He tore at the walls. He screamed at the window. He followed every suggestion that his insane mind and soul could come up with. Finally, he wept.

                                "Until next time, Mr Murdock."
