You don't need to know if Ghouls can learn Devotions or not to play Requiem. It wasn't in the core book of either edition. The thing is, books have limited space and can't possibly cover every possible situation. If you encounter a situation not covered by the book, like a Ghoul trying to learn a Devotion, it is on the Storyteller to make a call. For the purposes of making such a call, I see nothing wrong with referring to a rule from a previous edition when one exists. I mean, I also see nothing wrong with ignoring a rule from a previous edition when one exists. In neither case is the previous-edition book that the rule is found in to be "needed" to play the current edition. It's just a tool the Storyteller can use or not use as they see fit.

                                    "Learning Disciplines: Ghouls may learn Disciplines under

                                    "Many vampires develop the abilities listed here for each
                                    Discipline, but some manifest new powers of the Blood. These
                                    new Discipline techniques, called Devotions, can manifest
                                    spontaneously in some Kindred, or can be developed by
                                    careful honing of Discipline abilities. Once a vampire knows
                                    a Devotion, she can teach it to another Kindred in much the
                                    same way as the standard Discipline techniques. Any vampire
                                    with the appropriate prerequisite Discipline dots can develop
                                    any Devotion for which she has the dots — as long as she has a
                                    teacher, or has plenty of time to practice."
