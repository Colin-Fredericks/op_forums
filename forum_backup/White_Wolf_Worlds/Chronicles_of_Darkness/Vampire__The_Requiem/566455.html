    Vampire 'Evolution'

    06-24-2015, 11:50 AM
    Hi everyone! I have some brain food for you all!

    Along with some of my other questions, this one is potentially also scrubbish, if at least not weird.

    Awhile ago I had a character, originally started in Masquerade and transferred to Reqiuem (did not play 2E with him though), who was utterly power hungry. Not politically, but physically. He want power over everyone and everything. So, he diablerized as many enemies as he could (I always joked that he thought of Disciplines like they were Pokemon. Gotta catch 'em all!). There was a devotion he took (I don't remember if it was house ruled or if it was there normally) so that his aura did not give this away (I think normally it was black,veiny lines in the aura). It did take a huge toll on his psyche, would be very paranoid at times/act irrationally as some of the souls he devoured would linger. Sadly, the game was ended as I had to move away but I was in the process of trying to create him into something more. (OOC, him also turning into a demon/werewolf/mage powers/etc etc. Anything that he could). Now, originally what my plan was, was to somehow get into the Lucifuge. It never states that only humans may join them (actually states that the leader is most likely not human), and it gives him access to demons. He would go through demon after demon until he found what he was after. Either someway to 'evolve' into one, or find a way to infiltrate a mage tower. I hadn't worked out a way for the whole werewolf thing yet, and I have no experience with any of the others (very little in anything but vampire and hunter really). If he was able to obtain what he wanted, he would have the benefits of all, as well as their weaknesses. This is assuming, of course, that everyone/thing he has/had pissed off doesn't manage to kill him first.

    So, I guess my question is, is there a way for a vampire to 'evolve/transform' into a demon/werewolf and somehow make his way to a mage tower (or anything else for that matter.)? It just happened to come across my mind again and figured I would ask those a lot more knowledgable than myself

                      There is a vampire, Eddie Vines, who is also possessed by a demon. I don't think I'd want a vampire to become a demon, since then they'd be unplayable. But a vampire can learn the standard occult workings that allow people to theoretically control demons, anyone can. If you want a tie in to the Lucifuge you can give them a +2 bonus to dice pools to dealing with demons in a demonologist fashion but I wouldn't allow them to get the endowments, normally, as the vampirism sort of wipes out the demonic vestiges in them.

                      A werewolf who is embraced before their First Change is a vampire. They might have access to the dead wolves bloodline but they're never going to be a werewolf again.
