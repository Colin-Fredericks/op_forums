                          The Unholy is another example of a 1e vampire of incredible age... as is Zagreus--both from Night Horrors: Immortal Sinners. Of course The Unholy survived by being a diabolist terror. That aside, the reason elders in Requiem are scary is not because they have 6+ dots in disciplines (though they could have 6+ in skills!), but because they have had time to accumulate resources and develop unknown powers during periods of higher Blood Potency. Note that you don't lose access to your purchased abilities once BP drops from Torpor--you only lose the ability to buy more of them until it goes up again!

                          On resources: we're talking about favors, contacts, knowledge and money. Money is a proxy for time and for domain; time is of course a precious thing each night, to be spent in ways that assure safety and security and power (which leads to the previous two). Domain equals blood, safety and security. An elder who doesn't recognize the value of money in managing her unlife is going to have a harder, much more feral existence.

                          Now, you can have an elder who is NOT scary by simply embracing the Torpor angle, or assuming that the poor fellow has only just gotten by over the centuries. Not everyone is clever enough to thrive. Some can only tread water until they forget everything else except a low cunning that sends them fleeing danger.

                          --Khanwulf

                          Edit: Ok, after re-reading, The Unholy isn't actually that old, but she is a big, scary elder! Zagreus is ancient, however...

                                  I've established that the oldest clan in my world are the Mekhet, and the oldest vampire that has been onscreen in my games was Embraced in 2290 BC, followed by a Gangrel from the second century AD. The Mekhet keeps a low profile, the city being her sanctuary while she looks back towards Egypt, but the Kindred who know her rightly fear her.

                                  In my setting, the first vampire was a Darkness-Claimed human who, at some point, managed to pass on a sort of spiritual infection through the blood to three different mortals. In turn, they each spread their power to three others. That makes 9 clans, 5 of which are extant and large enough to be recognized. The Mekhet, the eldest, were created c. 3600 BC. The youngest clan, Julii, formed in 250 BC.
