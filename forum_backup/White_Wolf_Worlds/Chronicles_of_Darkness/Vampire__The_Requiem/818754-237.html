            He was supposedly John Wilkes Booth. His dark secret was that, despite appearances, he was really not a devout adherent to Vidal's perverse interpretation of the Sanctified as Roman Catholicism for undead bloodsuckers and STs should probably manufacture specifics.             It's rather easy to hide any "deepest secret" from Auspex mind rapists. Simply say Dominate was used to remove or alter the specifics from his memory with a big middle finger to Auspex users that Donovan "knows" it was removed or changed and doesn't care.
