    Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post
    You could just use a mirror.
    Dominate requires direct eye contact, it won't work through things like reflections and recordings.

    At the risk of reviving an old, at times heated argument, the reason I think it would be ill-advised is that in order to use Dominate on someone, you must Mezmerize them. That is to say, you must put them in a state of mind where their autonomy is suppressed and receptive to suggestion from an outside source; a state which clouds the subject's faculties in such a way that they don't clearly remember the events that transpired while they were in that state..
    I will point out that the Mesmerised condition only blanks out memories if it is resolved, not if you allow it to fade naturally at the end of a scene.
