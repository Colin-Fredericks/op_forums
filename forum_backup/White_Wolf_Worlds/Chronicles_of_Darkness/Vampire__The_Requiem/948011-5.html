        Vitalis is being pretty generous in suggesting 'only' protean 2 and vigor 1.

        Frankly with what you're describing in that merit, I would push for Protean 4, and Vigor 3 as a START. To me, you're not describing something that is starting-level. You're describing something wholly unique and terrible that would bring out horrors and legends across kindred society and beyond.

        That is simply beyond starting vampire power-base. Night Horrors: Immortal Sinners covers characters and powers like what you're describing and these are the things that Sires tell their Childer about to keep them in line for centuries.

        I'm not saying I don't like your idea. I'm saying it's something to aspire to, not start with.

        As you develop this further, I would say the higher level functions DO require Nightmare (probably 2 plus), yes, two SEPARATE clan-Unique disciplines and the story by which you develop something like that should not be skimmed over, glossed quickly and should take decades if not centuries to develop.

        That still doesn't make me okay with it by any scope as again, this is breaching.

        If you want to see the scope of which I'm talking just take a look at Holger, and really let everything about him from his background to his actual powers sink in a little bit.


        Edit: Frankly, the more I think about it, a devotion/merit like this also probably should require at LEAST Blood Potency 3 to learn/develop.

            Jumping off of CrimsonOwl's point about scope, and acknowledging the talk of bloodlines and blood potency, might I suggest considering raising the power-level the game starts at.
