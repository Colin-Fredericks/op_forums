    Yeah, it's definitely possible to develop Coils (or, as the book points out, alternate coil dots in already established Coils) on your own but it should be rare.
    The first Coil dot that anyone develops should always require some sort of foundation, imo, but it could potentially be notes about the Mystery and the internalisation of Coils rather than full mentorship. Any coil dots after that (even in other Mysteries) is "merely" applying the principles in new ways so theoretically possible without any external support at all. But that would be rare and not something that I would allow without good reason. Especially not for, say, a Carthian who learned a single Coil dot through espionage and then try to develop additional dots on their own without having the Ordo mindset or putting in effort and resources into mystical research and studies.

                                It has to be an animal corpse and a skeleton is not a corpse. That’s up to your ST if some bones or a taxidermist product in a zoo constitutes a corpse.                                                             I'd say it applies to anything that raises your Attributes stats passively. Someone with a high amount of Strength through Vigor is no different from someone with a high amount of Strength through Blood Potency 6+, in my opinion that is.

The "When he can barely walk through a crowd without accidentally hurting someone," -part doesn't apply to Physical Intensity, is what I'm saying.
