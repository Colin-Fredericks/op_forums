            Originally posted by Aspel View Post
            This is actually some of the best feedback I've gotten on them. I made them Kindred because originally they were a bit of a joke (catgirl gangrels). Eventually I expanded on that, made them Mekhet (the other clan in the running was Nosferatu). I could definitely do with a bit of expansion. And I'll admit I'm not actually familiar with most of the Bloodlines you named. Much as I love the way Bloodlines work, there are just a crapton of them.
            Glad to help. You can look most of them up here or there, they're all Bloodlines that deal with the dead and with ghosts, so it might be valuable to track them down and read up on them, because they give a good sense of different ways to go with it. For what it's worth, I think there's a lot going on here, and narrowing down what the essential elements of the bloodline are would be a good decision for picking one.

            For instance, I think the Protean bit doesn't really seem appropriate. Their curse and the unearthly nature of it sounds more appropriate for the Nosferatu, I think. As an additional reason, the Nosferatu Clanbook discusses how the Haunt's cthonic origins and associations give them a strong connection to ghosts and whatnot. It even includes a few merits to model that relationship. Essentially, there's a lot to work with there for a bloodline which is weird, unearthly looking and has a relationship with graveyards and ghosts. Since this element seems to be the most prevalent, there's a lot more to work with there than I think there is for a Mekhet or for a Gangrel, so my vote would be for them to be Nosferatu.

            Now, there's another thing which is important to discuss, namely their mythological origin and that association. I think there's two things to address here: First whether the total mythology of the creature is appropriate for the Nekomata bloodline. I personally feel that it isn't, and that while they might share a name and associations, that they don't need to necessarily have cat ears or cat tails. These elements and the over-emphasis of their animalistic nature feel like extraneous elements which don't necessarily contribute to the theme. I can't make you do anything, but I'll say I don't think they need to be kept, or if they are, they should be revised to be more subtle and less overtly masquerade breaching. If you go along with their being Nosferatu, than I suggest things like their ears getting a bit unusual or maybe some kind of small, vestigial tailgrowth. Evoke the mythology a little bit, hew to the loneliness curse of the Nosferatu, and don't overdo the aesthetic and I think you'll get a stronger presentation.

            Incidentally, I'm reading the wikipedia entry for the Nekomata, which talks about them eating people and bewildering/scaring them. So I think that's even more to work with insofar as their being Nosferatu is concerned.

            Originally posted by Aspel View Post
            Essentially they're useful simply because they can talk to the dead. They're spymasters, and partly inspired by an idea for a character I had that no ST would ever want to deal with: A vampire with Blood Tenebrous, Kindred Medium, Animalism, and a ton of Allies and Contacts that are ghosts, spirits, and animals. Just essentially a person sitting at the center of a network of unseen spies and servitors.
            I think this is sort of the issue. Between the mythology and how it suggests more strongly for their being Nosferatu, it doesn't really feel like there's anything there for making them spy masters with ghosts. I'd actually go with another Bloodline for that, rather than try to insert it into the Nekomata write-up. Now, apparently in the mythology, they have a thing for siccing ghosts on people that wronged them. Not only does that sound pretty scary and sort of Nosferatu-like, it sounds fairly thematically unified with these other elements. They're Kindred who speak to ghosts. That's weird for the Kindred. Maybe, rather than the typical usage for ghosts being about spying on people, they use them against others to seek retribution or to aid them in hurting them?

            Actually, thinking about it, I think a concept for these guys COULD be a ghost spymaster, but that'd be more of an individual character's thing than the Bloodline's concept. Or at least, that makes more sense to me.

            Originally posted by Aspel View Post
            I could do with tweaking them (maybe even giving them a full rewrite) with that concept in mind. The Kitsune-bi also... as you said doesn't really DO anything other than make spooky light. I should probably tweak that a bit as well, maybe make it draw in ghosts or something.
            I'll stress that I think the ghost spymaster makes sense for an individual character concept rather than the bloodline's concept.

            For that Devotion, I'd make it something like burning Vitae (not literally, obviously) in order to attract ghosts. Maybe it marks the Nekomata as an Anchor for the nearest Ghost and draws them out. Maybe Ghosts can come to that location and gain Essence. The last one might be stepping on the Dragolescu's toes, though.


            Originally posted by Aspel View Post
            And the last power is definitely in need of a rewrite. I was originally trying to save space, but that just makes the mechanics bad. I was mostly trying to crib from the Quicken Dead ceremony from Geist. Their "theme" as it goes is just sort of being these awkward, weird cat people who talk to ghosts and do weird unsettling cat things. But I feel like that might not entirely come through as it is now.
            That concept once again sounds more Nosferatu than anything else. I'd suggest looking at the Burakumin or the Sangiovanni first, since they both have different takes on raising zombies, and would be a good place to start.


            Originally posted by Aspel View Post
            I felt it was wrong to give them weird features (there also are intentionally no mechanics for it; I was tempted to give them the Nosferatu drawback, but felt that having them be walking Masquerade violations was already bad enough) that they can push away with Protean but not give them Protean. It felt wrong to have an animalistic clan without it.
            I don't think Protean really does much for their concept as it is. I think giving them Animalism as Nosferatu might be appropriate if they have powers over other cats and whatnot. Maybe as part of a Gift, or as a Devotion, they can use Animalism powers on Ghosts? That might actually be a more unique way for them to deal with Ghosts, since the Apollinaire already have the whole missing an eye, speak to/see ghosts thing going on, and it'd make them more unique. Maybe their Gift could focus around how Ghosts or things which seem to be like Ghosts interact with the different levels of Animalism.

            Still think some mechanics would be good for that Bane. Consider what you do with their Gift and their reason for being which can tie into it.

            I hope all that helps.

                    For what it's worth, I get the association of seeing ghosts with knowing secrets/secrets beyond the grave and all that, but I really don't get that vibe from the Nekomata folklore, hence my suggestion to pursue the Animalism avenue.

                    I also think that those Bloodlines which have involved multiple Clans (The Licinii, The Moroi? I think it's just those two) had that two-clan nature strongly integrated into their write-up, so if you were going that route, I'd suggest not doing it haphazardly, and putting a strong thematic emphasis on it. Personally, I don't think it's there if you want to do the Nekomata thing.

                    Bloodlines which are just a change to the Kindred state are sorta different. These are the Sta-Au, the Therion and the Neglatu, and they're all written very specifically about it. Therion and Sta-Au are irreversible alterations to the Kindred state, and the Neglatu are pseudo-revenants, so unless that's integrated into the write-up and thematically jives, I don't think it's a good route to pursue.

                    I'm speaking with an eye towards trying to keep the bloodline sleek, in the Blood & Smoke style. It's good to keep it simple and not totally reinvent the wheel. Too many moving parts in a bloodline and you risk losing that.
