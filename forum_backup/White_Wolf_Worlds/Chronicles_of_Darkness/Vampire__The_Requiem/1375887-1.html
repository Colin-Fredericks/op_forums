        What about updating the Sangiovanni bloodline from 1st ed.?
        I intend to make them a ghoul family that allows anyone from it who gets embarrassed to join the bloodline. Weakness is they’re closer death and have one less health than normal, strength is once a ghoul they can take one aggravated damage and age a week to gain one Vitae and have the ability to learn the Necromancy discipline even if their regent doesn’t know it.                                     They’re a ghoul family that can join the bloodline when embraced even if they’re sire isn’t from the bloodline, the bloodline also passes sire to childe as normal so they can be any clan but I assume the history given in B:tC is more or less true and they have a large Mekhet core (about 40% of members).

                                    So mostly Mekhet but players or STs can do something different if they want and an all Sangiovanni game allows all clans and ghouls to get in on the fun.

                                            Why you want to be us. We don’t come into the Requiem blind, imagine if your family had been able to be there for you at every step of your embrace, not needing to lie or hide your nature, knowing what you were getting yourself into whether Kindred or Ghoul.
                                            Why you should fear us. The dead don’t keep their secrets from us, three can keep a secret until one of them is dead, you haven’t been killing people who know stuff you don’t want others to learn have you?
                                            Why we should fear ourselves. Family is the most sacred institution and it’s where betrayal hurts the most but is it really betrayal if it’s for your own good? The favour of a powerful family member is a valuable thing and there are a lot of people competing for it and not everyone can become immortal, getting noticed just makes someone more able to... oh, too real for you? Just pretend I said something about some things best left unknown and go back to whatever it was you were doing and pretend you’re any different, that you wouldn’t make the same calculations in our situation.

                                            Obviously they’re not all that bad but not everyone can live forever and their entry mentioned stuff so go as dark and edgy or comfy and wholesome as you want.
                                            For balance reasons I recommend having Nosferatu reduce the extra dice they get from the status merit by one to offset being able to neutralise their clan bane for one Vitae and having their boon only work for the scene it’s activated in to prevent the Ordo having it too easy.
                                            And have fun, or engage in emotional masochism if that’s your objective.

                                                        I’m not completely happy with my attempt to design a Mystery Cult to represent the Black Ring but I have two merits for those who take Status in the bloodline.
                                                        The first is for kindred only and represents taking up a role in the maintaining of the ghoulhood of mortal family members giving you Status number of dots in Staff, Herd, Library and Retainer, if you go more than Status nights without making time to oversee your charges lose a dot of status.
                                                        The second is for getting access to the families resources and gives Resources, Feeding Grounds, Safe Place and Contacts, you are expected to use these assets to aid the family fail to do so and they can be taken off you.
                                                        The Mystery Cult is speciality in Occult relating to necromancy (of course), the Patient merit, Hobbyist Clique, then I haven’t found anything for the last two dots I’m happy with. I don’t want to just say these supernatural merits are ok I’ll use them, I want something that really fits.
                                                        Most of my bloodline ideas are not this extensive but the Sangiovese just seem to fit with expanding to this level of detail. It’s nice to be able to share it.
