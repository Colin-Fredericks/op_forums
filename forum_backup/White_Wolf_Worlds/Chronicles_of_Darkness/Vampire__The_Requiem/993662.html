                                      I'll take a look. And MCN, I wasn't talking about just influencing or viewing, but actually entering dreams the way Changelings and Beasts can. Maybe entering the capital d Dream as well.

                                      The problem with Auspex 5 is it's kind of on the high side, but other than that it seems easy enough. And I'm generally sticking to B+S philosophy of using Devotions instead of new Disciplines, especially since dream powers are the sort of thing that would be very easy to handle as adjustments of the core Disciplines.

                                      Here's how I'd handle it: Seeing into dreams is already handled by Auspex, so I'd make the following a devotion set, probably learnable by any vampire (since if I actually did this in a chronicle, I wouldn't want to force any of the players into playing the only bloodline with dream powers).

                                      Auspex+Majesty: Enter dreams.

                                      Auspex+Nightmare: Cause Tilts/Conditions in sleeping people by attacking their dreams. More effective if the vampire is actually inside the dream. Can also be used to drive out other entities, since the illusions created by Nightmare are "real" within the dreamworld.

                                      Auspex+Obfuscate: Shape dreams in other ways. Hiding or changing information, creating barriers, and so on. Similarly to Nightmare, Obfuscate illusions are more "real" when summoned inside dreams.

                                      Auspex 5: Your astral projection can enter dreams as well.

                                      I'm probably going to wait for Changeling 2e and the Beast Player's Guide, since both are probably going to have info on doing stuff in dreams, but that's a preliminary outline. I definitely want to get in that illusionary Disciplines allow for limited reality warping when used in dreams. So a monster summoned with Nightmare is as "solid" as anything else in a dream.

                                          Originally posted by Geckopirateship View Post
                                          I'll take a look. And MCN, I wasn't talking about just influencing or viewing, but actually entering dreams the way Changelings and Beasts can. Maybe entering the capital d Dream as well.
                                          The closest thing to a capital D-dream is the so-called Primordial Dream, which actually has nothing to do with real dreams. Its part of the Astral and its three levels, a dark reflection of the collective unconciousness of humanity and their relationship to the natural world. But to call it a Dream is misleading. Actual dreams exist outside of the Astral, as per David Brookshaw. At best, dreams touch on the Astral and Hedge and serve as a bridge before fading away into their own thing. And, curiously enough, Beasts actually have very little to do with regular dreams. The Begotten can use a sleeper as a gateway to the Astral, and upset a person's dreams, but they don't actually stay in the dreams. You just pass through it momentarily before being shunted entirely in the Astral. Horrors can act independantly and slip into a dream to feed on it, but that's not something the PC actually does; its outside of their control. At best, someone can argue that you -might- be able to find some kind of pathway from someone's Onerios into their dreams, but I've never seen any rules or guidelines for it.

                                          Meanwhile, Changelings physically -enter- the dream to the point their body disappears, and experience dreams as an extension of Hedge-walking. That is nothing like a Beast and their pseudo-relationship to actual dreams. Plus, I somehow doubt that you're talking about having a vampire make body vanish from reality and turn into dream-material. So, when you say "like Beasts and Changelings," that is a bit misleading, since that's rather something I don't think would make sense.

                                          Oh, and for the record, entering the Astral through use of Auspex 4 is cannon, as per the Astral Realms book.

                                              Auspex 4 in 1E (that the Astral Realms is written for) and 2E are extremely similar, but functions somewhat different. Telepathy works by projecting your consciousness into your subject. From there it's just a matter of going from the consciousness and memories to dreams. Lay Open the Mind on the other hand is "just" connecting two minds. To be able to "physically" visit the subject's dreams you'd have to create a projection of yourself inside the subject's mind or dreams. That's my rationale as for why Auspex 5 would be required to visit a subject's dreams, but since Auspex in 1E already could do that I'd keep the devotion to just Auspex 5 and not include Nightmare, as I previously suggested.

                                              Other than that I second that some clarification is needed about the Dream with a capital D. I've written with the assumption it's only about the personal dream plane. The Oneiros is more difficult, and Temenos likely impossible for a vampire to enter.
