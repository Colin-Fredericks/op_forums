    Converting Bloodlines from the F.E.A.R. Supplements into 2E

    11-05-2015, 11:23 PM
    For those who've downloaded and read the F.E.A.R supplements from Jesuncolo's thread, you'll recognize these bloodlines.
    http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...lement-revised

    Cherubim
    Parent Clan: Ventrue
    Nickname: Immortal Children, Lost Boys
    Disciplines: Celerity, Dominate, Majesty, Resilience

    Bloodline Gift: Angel's Voice
    A Cherub's voice can lull and influence whoever hears it. Used in conjunction with Dominate and Majesty, the Immortal Children can easily subdue their prey before draining them dry of all blood. A Cherub gains a bonus equal to half his Blood Potency (rounded up) on all Expression, Persuasion, and Subterfuge rolls. Whenever he sings, those who hear him must roll their Resolve + Composure (Kindred and other supernaturals can add their Advantage Trait to the dice pool). On a failure, they suffer the effects of the Insensate Tilt for the duration of the song.

    Bloodline Bane: Demon Child
    Cherubim indulge an appetite for the blood of other vampires, craving it sooner than other Kindred. Members of the bloodline treat their effective Blood Potency as two dots higher for determining what prey they must feed on. This means that members of the bloodline begin hunting Kindred sooner or later as their thirst demands for more potent blood to be drunk.

      Nostradamians
      Parent Clan: Mekhet
      Nickname: Soothsayers (Old), Doomsayers, Nostradami
      Disciplines: Auspex, Celerity, Dominate, Obfuscate

      Bloodline Gift: Vaticinia Nostradami
      The Nostradami are feared (and, once upon a time, revered) for the oracular powers they have at their disposal. This Gift allowed them to divine the fates of entire domains through dream and vision. All members of the bloodline develop the Dream Visions Merit, and enjoy the 8-again rule on rolls made to invoke it. Additionally, he may purchase the Omen Sensitivity Merit, and can spend Vitae in place of Willpower to activate it. If the vampire already had Dream Visions upon joining, he instead gains the Dream Merit (Mage: The Awakening, p. 82) at one dot.

      Bloodline Bane: Moon Blinked
      While as much at home in the dark as all Mekhet outside of their bloodline, the Doomsayers are influenced adversely by the light of the moon. Most seldom travel under moonlight without some means of concealing themselves from its rays, and they suffer the full force of this curse on the nights of the full moon. A Nostradamus suffers from the Distracted Condition (Beast: The Primordial, p. 323) whenever he is exposed to moonlight. In the light of a full moon, however, he must also roll to resist Frenzy. This bane is not active on nights of the new moon.

      -=-

      Hand of Fate (Auspex oooo, Dominate oooo)
      This is the dreaded power that caused the Nostradamians to become hunted in the first place. By combining the mental compulsion of Dominate with the otherworldly insights of Auspex, the Doomsayer is able to literally place a post-hypnotic command right into the brain of the target that triggers after a set amount of time, Doomsayers will typically give conditions such as upon the next rise of the moon, or on the 8th day of the 8th month and other similar events as the trigger point. The vampire must have first inflicted the Mesmerized Condition on his victim.

      Requirement: Nostradamian Bloodline
      Cost: 2 Vitae
      Dice Pool: Manipulation + Expression + Dominate vs. Willpower + Blood Potency
      Action: Contested; Resistance is reflexive.

      If the contested roll is won, the target will subconsciously carry out the prophecy to the letter it states after the trigger point occurs. If the vampire fails or ties on the contested roll, he may not use the Devotion again on the target for a year. A dramatic failure is worse, as the prophecy is turned upon him instead in a twist of Fate.

      This Devotion costs 4 Experience to learn.

          P.S: I am going to be save the Angels bloodline for last. Maybe even reimagine them with a different historical background. In the meantime, here are the Flayed.

          The Flayed
          Parent Clan:           Nosferatu
          Nickname: Skinners
          Disciplines: Celerity, Nightmare, Obfuscate, Vigor

          Bloodline Gift: The Arbiter of Xipe-Totec
          As priests to a god of life, death, and rebirth, the Flayed claim as their Gift authority over a part of that cycle. Upon being initiated into the bloodline, the Kindred gains an Unseen Sense related to ghosts, and can purchase the Haunted Channel Merit (taken from Nosferatu: The Beast That Haunts The Blood, p. 109)

          Bloodline Bane: Flayed One
          Like all Nosferatu they have an eerie presence to them. The nature of their Becoming, though, further exacerbates the effects of their Clan Bane. Their skin is flayed off and sacrificed to Xipe Totec prior to the Embrace. And the static nature of the Kindred preserves this appearance for eternity. A Skinner treats his effective Humanity as four dots lower for the purpose of social penalties. Furthermore, all Presence and Manipulation rolls which do not use the Intimidation Skill do not benefit from the 10-again rule, and subtract 1s from successes.

          -=-

          Haunted Channel (o to ooooo)
          Effect: When a ghost endeavors to communicate with the Nosferatu or manifest near the character, he finds it has a much easier time. All rolls made to attempt a Manifestation or use one of Numina such as Clairvoyance, Ghost Sign or Ghost Speech gain a bonus equal to the dots in this Merit.

                  Wait .. why change the Angels??? I liked them ... kinda sorta. lol. Ok so intrigued by this I pulled out my FEAR Corp stuff and looked at the bloodlines in there. Still to come other than the Angels are the Wind Walkers, the Pygmalions, the Chupacabra, and the three Chinese ones from the FLHS book. I did a little work and came up with the following to help out.

                  Angels
                  Parent Clan: Nosferatu
                  Nicknames: Erinyes
                  Disciplines: Nightmare, Obfuscate, Resilience, Vigor

                  Bloodline Gift: Wings of Night
                  This bloodline is blessed with the ability to glide for distances, using thermals and updrafts to keep them aloft.

                  Bloodline Bane: Soul of a Bird
                  The Angel’s wings are large, cumbersome. They are hard to hide and all it takes is one look at winged kindred to start the blood hunts, rumors and other raucous that will more than likely lead the Angel’s destruction. All Angels suffer from a permanent -2 to all social rolls.



                  Pygmalion
                  Parent Clan: Daeva
                  Nickname: Crafters, Twisters
                  Disciplines: Celerity, Majesty, Vigor, Dominate

                  Bloodline Gift: The Grasp
                  Those of Pygmalion’s brood are able to twist and shape the very flesh of what they touch. Sadly most, if not all victims of there power, go completely mad if not die, when subjected to the Grasp. Examples of the discipline have ranged from general physical changes, making one more beautiful, to warpings of the very body itself, as documented in the infamous Grecian Doll incident. The exact nature of the changes wrought by this gift are left to Storyteller.

                  Bloodline Bane: To Be Perfect
                  Like their parent clan, the Pygmalions suffer from the inability to control their vices, but for them the curse is a double curse, as when the Pygmalion is confronted with someone they can “perfect” they must resist the temptation to “perfect them” or lose suffer from the Obsession condition, meaning that once they succumb they will not stop till the perfection is done. Sadly this can instantly lead to their destruction as they literally attack someone to “perfect them”. Many stalk their prey for days before striking though, letting the madness of the urge to prefect the target drive them to take their target at the most opportune time, so they can squirrel them away somewhere isolated and quietly to work on.


                  Wind Walkers
                  Parent Clan: Nosteratu
                  Nickname: Wendigo, Ithaqua, Walkers in the Cold
                  Disciplines: Nightmare, Protean, Obfuscate, Celerity

                  Bloodline Gift: Predator’s Guile
                  This things are supreme hunters, moving like the wind, silently shifting from place to place before striking at their prey. Wind Walkers gain a bonus equal to the blood potency in all hunting type rolls, from stalking their prey and hiding from their prey to actual ambush of their prey.

                  Bloodline Bane: Feral Nightmare
                  These kindred are almost bestial in their nature, near mindless hunters that seek to feed and little else. The kindred suffers from a permanent Bestial condition.


                  Chupacabra
                  Parent Clan: Gangrel
                  Nicknames: Stalkers
                  Disciplines: Vigor, Resilience, Protean, Celerity

                  Bloodline Gift: From Nowhere
                  These nightmarish things have the ability to blend into literally anywhere, melding with the jungles of either wild or the city in order to hunt. These kindred are able to add their blood potency to their stealth and athletics rolls.

                  Bloodline Bane: The Thing
                  This bloodline suffers from a physical degenerative disorder, as if the blood itself is warping them into the nightmares that their minds become. As time passes (re: Blood Potency goes up), the Chupacabra slowly “devolves”. Its jaws elongate, the eye teeth becoming more pronounced. In high Blood Potency members, the jaws never close properly remaining open, saliva dripping from them. The eyes slowly color to red with black pupils, the finger and toe nails strengthen and grow, becoming jagged claws (+1 die to all hand to hand tests), and their backs slowly grow crooked, so that the creature has a slight hunch. As well, their skin becomes rough and almost scaly. This of course causes them to lose any type of “hi I’m friendly” type of interaction. Then again, 99.9% of Chupacabra would rather just bite your face off then be friendly.


                  Pao Yu
                  Parent Clan: Ventrue
                  Nicknames: Speakers, the Cranes
                  Disciplines: Animalism, Dominate, Resilience, Auspex

                  Bloodline Gift: Crane’s Elegance
                  The Pao Yu are masters of negotiation and political maneuvering. They add the relevant stat twice to their dice pools when doing all negotiation and political rolls

                  Bloodline Bane: Inner Nightmares
                  These kindred suffer from a rapid form monophobia, wanting to be around people at all times, the more the better. Starting at Blood Potency 4, a Pao Yu must have at least 2 people around him at all times, and will suffer -1 die to all social tests unless this is so. For here on in, the Pao Yu’s need to be surrounded by people increases as their blood potency does, with the number of people needed being equal to the blood potency divided in half, round down.


                  Jian
                  Parent Clan:                   Ventrue
                  Nicknames: Tigers
                  Disciplines: Animalism, Dominate, Resilience, Celerity

                  Bloodline Gift: Tiger’s Fury
                  The Jian are peerless fighters, excelling in hand to hand combat as well as other arts of war and combat. They are able to add the blood potency level to combat rolls starting at blood potency 3 and higher.

                  Bloodline Bane: Knives Everywhere
                  The Jian seem to slowly become more and more paranoid about conspiracies. Older Jian see suspicion and threats everywhere which drives them to train harder and harder to better their skills. As such Jian of blood potency 4 pick up the Suspicion Derangement as per Page 98 of the World of Darkness Core Book. At Blood Potency 6 they upgrade to full Paranoia.


                  Lung
                  Parent Clan: Nosferatu
                  Nicknames: Dragons or T’angs
                  Disciplines: Animalism, Dominate, Majesty, Resilience

                  Bloodline Gift: Will of the Gods
                  Those of this bloodline often masquerade as divine beings and as such they have developed a way to literally feed on the power of faith that their followers generate towards them. Known by the word Inori, this complex power was developed by some of the first bloodline members and allows the faith of the followers to generate a pool of power for the kindred to tap into and use for his or her benefit. This can range from simple increased healing to full on soul eating to restore wounds and willpower. This power can often be fatal to the target, but sadly most of the victims of this power are willing, followers who have been enslaved to the will of the Kindred.

                  Bloodline Bane: Aspect of the Gods
                  The Lung blood further modifies the Nosferatu curse to make it so that the strangeness of the appearance tends to lean toward manifestation of looks that seem to make the kindred more like the classical images of divine beings. A third eye or multiple arms are common. As a Lung increases their skill in their signature discipline Inori, they slowly start manifesting the changes. These changes are visible and sometime difficult to hide, which leads many Lung to become trapped within their own temples, leery of going outside because even at night their inhuman form will arouse suspicion and call down possible harm on them from other sources.
                  At blood potency 4 or Inori 3, which ever comes 1st, the Lung manifests its first change. On change occurs each level of Inori or Blood Potency gain from their on in. Rumors run wild of a Lung who resembles Kali hidden away in a temple deep in Asia, tended to by a long thought dead cult of mortal and supernatural assassins. Lung with these changes suffer -1 die in social roles unless the people they are dealing with are “used” to them (such as followers and worshippers) or they are using their looks and changes to intimidate someone.

                          Aside from revising the Nostradami somewhat with regards to their Gift, here is a little expansion on the Chupacabra bloodline.

                          Chupacabra
                          Parent Clan: Gangrel
                          Nicknames: Stalkers
                          Disciplines: Vigor, Resilience, Protean, Celerity

                          Bloodline Gift: From Nowhere
                          These nightmarish things have the ability to blend into literally anywhere, melding with the jungles of either the wild or the city in order to hunt. A Chupacabra may add his dots in Blood Potency to their Stealth and Athletics rolls. These gain the 8-again rule in areas claimed as his territory, reflected in the Feeding Ground and Safe Place Merits.

                          Bloodline Bane: The Thing
                          This bloodline suffers from a physical degenerative disorder, as if the blood itself is warping them into the nightmares that their minds become. As time passes and their Blood Potency grows, the Chupacabra slowly “devolves”. Its jaws elongate, the eye teeth becoming more pronounced. At its worst, the jaws never close properly, remaining open with saliva dripping out. The eyes slowly color to red with black pupils, the finger and toe nails strengthen and grow, becoming jagged claws (treat as 1L natural weapons), and their backs slowly grow crooked, so that the creature has a slight hunch. Their skin also becomes rough and almost scaly.

                          A Chupacabra's Humanity now serves as a cap on all social rolls made with mortals. Furthermore, for every two dots of Blood Potency they possess, their effective Humanity is reduced for the purpose of determining penalties to any actions related to humans.

                                      How about I just drop in the quote from the Intro section for you

                                      "
                                      When this first started to work its way around the forums, many were of the opinion that it was. This led me to think that if there are weird powerful beings running around Arcadia stealing people for their slaves then maybe the same thing was going on with the other realms. Now at this point the general hub-bub was that they were supernal realms. So I looked up my trusty copy of Mage and read on the descriptions of the other four supernal realms and then thought, what about the hedges. If Arcadia has one, then it stands to reason the other four do as well. I envisioned the "hedge" of the Aether as a vast series of islands floating in the air surrounded by eternal light, the Primal Wild as savannah grassland inhabited by all the animals across time. A hellish, apocalyptic wasteland proceeded Pandemonium and a vast marsh and fen filled graveyard full of mausoleums and tombs led to Stygia. When Geist came out I initially dropped my idea for Stygia and instead opted that the upper mysteries were that hedge, with the lower depths being links outwards to Stygia itself, I have since scrapped that idea and instead continued with marshy graveyards of the dead for Stygia.
                                      So then came the notations that the supernal realms were more of a contemplating your navel, inner being type of thing, than an actual location like Arcadia is in Changeling. So I had to rework what I had a bit. Using Geist’s Underworld as the idea, I made each of them an “outer realm”. Like the Underworld these realms sit next to ours, brushing up against ours at times. Some of these Realms, like the Underworld, Arcadia, Stygia, Pandemonium, the Primal Wild and the Aether are more “permanent” with a consistent connections, and borderlands between them and use. In the case of the Arcadia, this is the Hedge. If one thinks about it the Upper Mysteries are the borderland to the Lower Mysteries of the Underworld. For the other four I did the apocalyptic hellish Wasteland for Pandemonium. The lush natural world of the Savannah for the Primal Wild. The dead and ruined world of cities and tombs known as the Cemetery for Stygia. And the vast bright floating islands of the Archipelago for the Aether. Each of these borderlands linked the Fallen World to their respective outer realm and in each realm there was a group of entities that did like the True Fae of Arcadia did, namely take mortals to be their slaves. For Pandemonium I have the Daemons, hellish entities that seek the souls of those steeping sin to use and mold. Those that escape are the hellish Taken, forever warped by their blight and torment. For the Primal Wild the Totemics, staggeringly powerful spirits, rule its vast primal beauty, warring on each other in savage games of predator and prey. Those that they take become primal beings linked with elemental animal natures. These Primals are creatures of the hunt, evolved and skirting that delicate edge between man and animal. For the Aether I have the entities known as the Seraphim (but they aren’t the only ones out there, see the chapter on the Aether for more details), who soak and feed of the faith of mortals that seeps into the realm. There servants were the grigori, the watchers, and unlike the rest of those taken by these outer realm entities, wanted to be where they were and those that are now no longer grigori are Exiles, forever bound in the conviction of their goals and the faith of their powers to put them back with the Seraphim. For the dead world of Stygia there exists the Reapers. Once the Reapers had servants, people known as Inquisitors, but now they are gone. No one knows what happened, just that one day they vanished, their Tomb Towns and Boltholes empty and the dead world of the Cemetery naught but a place of the dead.

                                      "

                                                      And here are the Angels....

                                                      Angels
                                                      Parent Clan: Nosferatu
                                                      Nicknames: Erinyes
                                                      Disciplines: Nightmare, Protean, Resilience, Vigor

                                                      Bloodline Gift: Wings of Night
                                                      This bloodline is blessed with wings which allow them to glide for short distances, using thermals and updrafts to keep them aloft. This functions mechanically as a permanent manifestation of the Patagia adaptation provided by the two-dot Protean power, Predatory Aspect. If an Angel chooses Patagia as one of her three adaptations from Predatory Aspect, she doubles the distance she can travel via gliding. If she also develops the Wings adaptation with Unnatural Aspect, she adds her Blood Potency to her flying Speed. If she has both the Patagia and Wings adaptations, she gains 8-again to all rolls for maneuvering while in flight.

                                                      Bloodline Bane: Soul of a Bird
                                                      The Angel’s wings are large, cumbersome. They are hard to hide, and persist even for those who take animal form via Beast's Skin. All it takes is one look to start the blood hunts, rumors, and other sorts of raucous that will more than likely lead to the Angel’s destruction, (or, less fatally, her hasty departure). This is only made more complicated by the fact that an Angel who stays confined to the ground for too long grows more restless, and subsequently more violent. An Angel who fails to spend enough time to fly within a number of hours equal to his Humanity suffers from the Obsession Condition related to this instinct. If he has yet to take flight when either another interval passes or he fails a detachment roll, he must roll to avoid falling into frenzy and attempt to fulfill her Obsession. Multiple occurrences impose a cumulative -1 penalty that maxes out at -5, which only resets once the Angel takes flight.

                                                      Additionally, the bloodline suffers from a permanent -2 to all social rolls, and treat any first impressions as one step lower for the purpose of social maneuvering. This social stigma, though, does not apply to Touchstones and (at Storyteller discretion) other characters.
