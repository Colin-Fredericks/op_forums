    Well, if memory serves from the Demon line, Strix are known to nest and corrupt G-M infrastructure, and Angels have a kill on sight program when it comes to the Strix.

    Now, looking between the Strix and Kindred relations, we know that Strix on some inherent level don't like kindred for a myriad of reasons, and according to Demon the Strix can provide some strange insights on the God Machine, despite claiming to have no memory or reason as to why they would know these things.

    Now, as we all know, Infrastructure is meant to do whatever the G-M needs to keep the status quo. Strix originally might stem from some dark shadowy place from before time, but most form here and now. One thing all Strix ro agree on is that their is some inherent connection between Kindred and Strix.

    So, maybe looking at the Kindred as some great cosmic tool might not be the only approach. An originally antagonistic force on reality might be a fun approach to consider.

        What if... things are backward: the vampiric curse virus was originally a kind of GM infrastructure that was subsequently corrupted by the Strix. Only they were unable to fully accomplish what they intended to do, and so despise vampires who cling to some form of "original programming".

        You could tie this to accounts of early humans living for enormous spans of time (ok, centuries)--they benefited from GM stabilizing protocols that were subsequently corrupted. The GM cannot simply go in and rip out the core human infrastructure that underpins this, as it would destroy humanity. Perhaps relate this to theories of mitochondria as cellular elements descended from specialized bacteria? (Yes, Virginia, the human organism is already a hybrid creature, and you personal DNA changes frequently!)

        If you stop and think, immortal humans would contribute to the objective of reality stasis with humanity at its center. It's more manageable than a constant churn of ensouled individuals who are aging, dying and being replaced--a logistically messy situation with significant and uncapture-able risks.

        So what might be the original objectives of the Strix in this? If the theory is correct that vampires can evolve into Strix-form, then they are interested in propagation. They hate humanity because humans are both food and unrealized Strix kept from becoming "them" because of the GM (among other things).

        The link between Strix and GM could explain their early origins, in the sense that the GM itself plays fast-and-loose with chronological ordering. So the Strix may have inserted themselves into prehistory using similar protocols in their attempt to affect the original corruption of human cellular infrastructure. You could even run a campaign in which one or several Strix are attempting to gain access to infrastructure that will enable them to fold time and accomplish this. You could even explain the scatter-minded nature of individual Strix through their appearance recursively through time as they fought/fight a battle with the GM to locate and rewrite the nature of humanity.


        --Khanwulf

            In mereology (the philisophical study of how parts create wholes), there is one theory called Universalism, which states that any two objects compose a third.

            In the philosophy of mind, there is a problem with functionalism. Functionalism is the theory that mental states are defined by their causal roles - both in how you enter that state, and which behaviors and mental states it might lead to. The problem is that any thing with complex enough states that are causully related would be judged by functionalism to have a mind. An economy might have a mind.

            Put the two together. My theory is that the GM came up with civilization. Civilization literally is infrastructure. Maybe the GM existed elsewhere before civilization, it would be composed of slow, ultimately basic components. But the development of civilization would be a massive upgrade. Without civilization, the GM would effectively become braindead. In some sense, civilization *is* the GM.

            In parallell, all vampires are a kind of social parasite. They require large populations to sustain themselves, laws and customs to exploit and subvert for protection.

            In the civilization-as-GM-brain theory, vampires are quite literally a disease. A cancer or virus. The interaction of strix and infrastructure may be the most exotic examples of an interaction that is already writ-small in the kindred and human society.

                The Holy Engineers should be of interest to this thread. Written up in the 1e book Danse Macabre and referenced in 2e's mention of the Julii, they are a covenant of vampires who claim to use the God-Machine as a medium to commune with their master, Death. This communication takes the form of the Radio Sickness, electromagnetic prophesies that answer questions the god talkers have yet to ask, or else destroy them, if they fail to provide the question to the answer and close the time loop.

                Their lore relies a lot on the writeup of the God-Machine from the 1e corebook, and so precedes ideas like Infrastructure and the Unchained, but it presents a unique relationship between vampire and immaculate Machine – not as slave and master or as enemies, but as user and tool.

                                                Angels are agents of the God-Machine, built to purpose often for the specific end of accomplishing a goal beyond the reach of cultists and other dupes.                                                 Demons are rogue angels and therefore are cut off from all the things angels commonly benefit from through their connection to the God-Machine, chief among them being access to their own layer of Twilight and provision of maintenance that prevents their long-term cover identities from degrading in convincingness to the point where their forename falls off and their wardrobe is literally identical copies of the same outfit.

                                                Demons are much easier to find on purpose than angels — the Unchained can fall victim to individual slip-ups, but angels reveal themselves on purpose from a position of power when ordered to, and it's only the fact that the thing giving them their orders is big, busy, and fallible that makes these occasions not advance Step Forty-Seven of an intricate and ever-unfolding Plan to the appropriate drop point.

                                                An angel only needs to engineer stigmata in a population of vampires to make them dramatically better-equipped to locate any major work of the God-Machine that isn't deliberately hidden, and in an area where such a confidence scheme is in place, the God-Machine absolutely throws a sheet over everything it hasn't already slated for demolition in the first place.
