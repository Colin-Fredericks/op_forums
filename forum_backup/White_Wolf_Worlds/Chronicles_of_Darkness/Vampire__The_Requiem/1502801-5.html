    I was reassessing Majesty 3 applied to Confident and I had the feeling it is a bit too weak compared to even just Dominate 1.
    The book mention that any action that WOULD lead to a breaking point shatters away the charmed condition (so, in my understanding, before the breaking point occurs so before the action is taken, opposed to dominate where prior you have to hit the breaking point to be awakened, which make it far more useful). So, since almost everything, for a normal person, is a breaking point, does it seems fair to you? Unless you do not charm a professional assassin or a street thug, there are very few situation where a 3rd dot if this discipline can really be effective.

    Any thought?

        You are missing a good number of things, I think. First, Majesty 3 allows you to rewrite your victim's emotional state entirely for the cost of a viate. Clever use of that ability can often get you the effect you desire without you having to do more direct means.

        Second, the ability to make requests comes without a roll. Once a victim is charmed, Majesty 3 enables you to play with them safely, without concern for the whims of dice. Yes, they won't take a breaking point for you if they are charmed, but if that's a major limitation for you it seems like you're trying to fit a square leg in a round hole then complaining the round hole is too weak.

        But then again, the power doesn't stop there, either. If your victim is enthralled, Majesty 3 will have them blow through breaking points for you if you wish.

        All that said, I do believe the first level of most disciples is fairly pushed, but honestly I think that was an intelligent decision during Requiem's design, it enables multiple builds to be powerful and flexible rather than almost forcing players to rush level 5 of a chosen discipline.
