                Abjuration explicitly calls out using the power of the soul. Pretty much every supernatural has its soul transmuted or maimed in some way. I don't allow it, because anything whose soul has been changed enough to no longer have Integrity no longer has the metaphysical power to abjure any longer.

                Summoning and exorcism just make use of manipulating Conditions in a way that, while ritualized, don't really do anything magical, and so those are fine. I mean, lighting a fight to make resonance for a Fire Spirit isn't rocket science (per the in-book example). But you need Integrity to Abjure.

                        When it comes down to it, I don't think house rules belong in this thread unless someone is explicitly asking for them (and in that case, it's not really so simple a question anymore).

                        As such, I don't think it's appropriate to answer with "the answer is that anyone can use abjuration, human or not" because this is not supported by the RAW. Unless a book or writer answers (or has answered) differently, abjuration and binding and warding require Integrity, though it is ultimately up to the GM and how they want to handle it.

                        Though I suppose you could also interpret it that someone without Integrity doesn't get the modifiers based on Integrity, I find that to be further of a stretch.

                        The two posts prior to mine stated "I do it this way," so I tried to provide an answer using the book rather than how I personally run it (which I find to be entirely irrelevant to the "Simple Answer" thread).

                            "This system replaces the Integrity system in in the World of

                            characters who are Embraced replace Integrity with Humanity,
                            and start with seven dots (regardless of their Integrity scores).
                            These ratings are compatible. For example, if a Discipline affects
                            a vampire character based on her Humanity rating, Integrity will
                            work similarly on her mortal counterpart."

                            One can argue that Humanity is compatible for the sake of Abjuration, per that text.
