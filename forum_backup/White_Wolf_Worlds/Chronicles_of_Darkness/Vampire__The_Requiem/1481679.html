    Belial's Brood 2e Tweaks and Houserules

    05-22-2022, 03:21 PM
    Hey everyone! The Risen Beast rules in [the fantastic] Wild Hunt inspired me to take another, deeper look at the version of Belial’s Brood in Spilled Blood this week. I found them a little… messy. Because I apparently have little better to do, what follows are some houserules for the covenant I intend to use in my games going forward [assuming I use the Brood]. My goal was to make them less player-facing, reduce Triadic Evolution's cognitive load, and make them a little more dangerous over all. Let me know what you think!

    The Claiming
    The effects of the Claiming are much the same as those laid out in Spilled Blood, with a few notable changes, summarized below:
    • The Brood still lose their Mask and Touchstones. These are replaced with two new ways of replenishing Willpower. First, every time a Claimed successfully inflicts a Condition through Lashing Out, they regain a single point of Willpower. Second, the first time in a scene a Brood drains a mortal to death or diablerizes another vampire, they regain all spent Willpower points.
    • The Brood technically exist in a perpetual state of frenzy. They gain all the benefits of frenzy, but are still capable of using tools and performing complex actions, much like a Passenger as described in Wild Hunt. Since they are their Beasts, any goals they may have are considered the same as a frenzy’s goal for any and all purposes.
    • Claimed still gain a persistent Condition as described in Spilled Blood. Additionally, they automatically gain a dot of Triadic Evolution upon their Claiming.

    The Evolved Beast
    The most dramatic differences between these rules and those in Spilled Blood are the nature and mechanics of the Brood’s blood sorcery, Triadic Evolution. Under these rules, it is replaced by a unique system somewhere between one of the physical Disciplines and a blood sorcery Discipline. It is rated from one to five dots, and has passive and active effects similar to a physical Discipline, but the bulk of its benefits come from a diverse collection of minor powers, like blood sorcery rituals. This is both to cut back on the amount of narrative calculations and arbitrary rolling, and because it feels more natural for evolved draugr to me. Since these rules are not intended for use by players, it is unnecessary for the Claimed to use player-facing systems. Consequently, the number of dots an individual Claimed has in their Triadic Evolution is assigned based on what feels right by the Storyteller, and it does not necessarily “count” as a proper Discipline by other mechanics.

    The updated rules for Triadic Evolution are presented below:

    Triadic Evolution
    When the Man submits to the Beast, allowing itself to be consumed, the Predatory Aura is changed forever. The Claimed are Beasts, but through their hungry apotheosis, they retain their ability to reason, and thus become fiercer and stranger than their former Kindred.
    Cost: None or 1 Willpower per active effect
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: None [for persistent effects] or Reflexive [for active effects]
    Duration: Permanent [for persistent effects] or as described [for active effects]
    Triadic evolution has two kinds of effects: persistent and active. Persistent effects are always on, and have no cost. Active effects are Reflexive, and cost one Willpower per effect. This does not change a Brood’s per turn limits on spending Willpower, so only one active effect may be activated per turn.
    Persistent: The Claimed gains the benefits of a number of Manifestations equal to her dots in Triadic Evolution. She must know a Manifestation to gain its benefits, and automatically learns a new Manifestation each time she gains a dot of Triadic Evolution. She may learn additional Manifestations as a part of diablerie, at the culmination of a period of intense conflict, or whenever the Storyteller deems it appropriate. There is no upper limit to the number of Manifestations a Claimed can know.
    Active: By spending Willpower, a Brood can evolve his Predatory Aura further. When spending Willpower, choose one effect from the following list. As previously stated, only one active effect of Triadic Evolution can be used each turn.
    • Gain the benefits of an additional Manifestation for the rest of the scene. The Claimed must know the Manifestation and not currently benefit from it to use this effect.
    • Grant the benefits of one of her Manifestations to another vampire for the rest of the scene. That vampire must not currently benefit from the chosen Manifestation, but does not need to be Claimed. A non-Brood vampire using any number of Manifestations suffers a breaking point at Humanity 1 when the scene ends.
    • Swap out one of her persistent Manifestations with another Manifestation she knows. This change lasts until she decides to swap it again.

    Manifestations
    Manifestations are a little different under these rules by necessity. Rather than being specialized rituals with variable durations, all Manifestations are permanent enhancements to a Brood’s vampiric nature. They also lack dot ratings, so a Claimed at any level of Evolution may develop any Manifestation.

    A Manifestation can be a persistent improvement to the Claimed’s abilities, or more rarely, an ability he directs at others. If a Manifestation targets another character, then it typically costs a point of Willpower and requires the Brood to successfully Lash Out against his victim [waive any Willpower costs due to differences in Blood Potency]. However, some Manifestations function more like rituals, and may be resisted as such.

    Make the following changes to the Manifestations in Spilled Blood:
    • Acidic Blood: The Brood spends 1 Willpower to gain the benefits of the Manifestation for the rest of the night, as written.
    • Animal Magnetism: Remove duration limit.
    • Camouflage: Remove duration limit.
    • Cover Your Tracks: Remove duration limit.
    • Don’t Look it in the Eye: Remove duration limit.
    • Enter the Zone: The Claimed can send another vampire with the Tempted Condition into a frenzy. She spends 1 Willpower and Lashes Out against her target. If she succeeds her victims falls into a frenzy instead of taking a Condition. This frenzy is focused on something that aligns with the Brood’s persistent Triad Condition, but cannot focus on harming her in any way. This cannot be used against other Brood, nor does it affect Kindred with Passengers.
    • Fight or Flight: Remove duration limit.
    • Hounds to the Hunters: The Claimed spends 1 Willpower and rolls Wits + Occult + Triadic Evolution to track the scent of something that was present in the area within the past week. Characters defend against this Manifestation as if it were blood sorcery by rolling Composure + Blood Potency. Once the Claimed has his target’s scent, he may track it regardless of the trail’s condition or any defensive countermeasures they have. This sense does not trigger a Clash of Wills, even if the target is hiding through supernatural means, such as Obfuscate.
    • Instinctual Conversation: As written, though characters that wish to resist the Claimed’s telepathic intrusion roll Composure + Blood Potency vs the Brood’s Presence + Blood Potency. Remove duration limit.
    • Merely a Flesh Wound: The Brood ignores the effects of Wrack Tilts unless both arms or legs are Wracked, in which case they suffer the corresponding Tilt’s full effects. Remove duration limit.
    • Perfect Ambush: Remove duration limit.
    • Riding the Man: As an instant action, the Claimed may suppress their Bestial nature, drawing on what remains of their Man. They lose the benefits of their perpetual frenzy, but add the higher of their Triadic Evolution or Blood Potency to all Intelligence, Manipulation, and Wits rolls, and do not suffer any penalties when relating to or socializing with mortals. They may cease Riding the Man at any time as a reflexive action.
    • Sprint: Adds Triadic Evolution dots to Speed, and the Claimed may spend 1 Willpower to add her dots in Triadic Evolution to Athletics rolls for the rest of the scene, including Dodge rolls, but not attacks with thrown weapons. Remove duration limit.
    • Survive: The Claimed may spend 1 Willpower to add her Triadic Evolution to her Defense for the rest of the night, or until she takes a violent or offensive action. This bonus does not apply to Dodge rolls.
    • Trophy: When the Claimed kills another vampire, he may spend 1 Willpower to keep their body from decaying. The body remains preserved for the rest of the night, during which time it may be harvested for any remaining Vitae or small trophies. At sunrise, the body decays as normal, though anything harvested from it remains preserved until the activator of Trophy meets Final Death.
