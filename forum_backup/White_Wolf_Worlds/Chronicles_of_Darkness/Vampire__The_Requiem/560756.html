    Dominate yourself

    06-19-2015, 09:47 AM
    Hi everyone! (I have so many inquiries lately!)

    I was talking to a friend who plays/runs a LARP and I am questioning on of the things that he said goes on there. I'm simply curious as to if it would be possible without house rules. They still run with 1E but I do know that they have altered some of the rules (main on I remember is that Nos no longer have that social stigma of not re-rolling 10's and 1's subtract successes. Can't remember what it was replaced with though)

    It's a fairly large group and has been running for the better part of a decade if not more (he's only been apart of it for the past 1-2 years). He mentioned that they have been using a bit of a work-around protection against Dominate. Which is that you, or someone you trust, Dominates you against being Dominated. I can't remember the exact details if it was either you can't be Dominated or you simply attack any vampire, other than initial user/yourself, who tries to use Dominate on you. My question is, can that actually work? Would you know if someone is trying to use it on you? Would you have enough to to react if it was simply to attack the caster, or could it be blocked out all together? (Usually eye contact is needed, so as soon as someone tries it, they break eye contact and leave the room or something)

    TL;DR Question: Can you use Dominate in some way to protect yourself against Dominate? Or any other mind altering Discipline?

              Well, it wouldn't be as simple of just using dominate whilst looking in a mirror.                               1e Conditioning gives the victim a penalty to resist the user's Dominate and a bonus to resist anyone else's. At maximum it can get up to +5/-5. You can't do it to yourself, but in theory you could get someone else you trust to to do it to you. The problem is you're then willingly letting someone turn you into their mind slave and just kind of hoping they won't abuse that power. Also by the fluff your character basically becomes a vegetable with no personality under the effects of heavy Conditioning. I remember the MET rules had a sidebar about doing more of a manturan candidate thing, since roleplaying a zombie would obviously not work very well in LARP. But even if your personality is apparently left intact, a character under the full effects of Conditioning is barely sentient, their free will has been all but completely supplanted by their master's commands.

                                          1st edition you could only be subject to one Mesmerize effect at a time. The trick is to get someone to use Mesmerize to place a benign command on you that takes effect with a predetermined trigger. (ie. Call me 15 minutes before the next sunrise.) So, If anyone else tries to Mesmerize you while the first effect is still in place, the second Mesmerize command doesn't take hold. It does rely on you trusting someone else to use Dominate on you and not insert other instructions or alter your memory further.

                                                      If you could dominate yourself, there are so many other tricks you could pull as well (let's say that even if you can't just look in a mirror and do it, you've got a devotion that lets you dominate yourself or something. Thought exercise time)

                                                      *Establish trustworthiness. Before a business deal, diplomatic meeting or other situation where you want to form an honest alliance and want to be trusted, just publicly Dominate yourself "Deal honestly and tell anyone if you plan to betray them". Suddenly, people can trust you a lot easier and you can achieve your goal without worrying about other people mistrusting you
                                                      *Willpower replacement (concept, not mechanical benefit). "Refuse all bribes", "Quit smoking" (not useful for a vampire, but just an example), "Do everything in your power to kill the Prince, even what you would have considered unthinkable"
                                                      *Imprint reflexes. "Draw your weapon the moment you think you're in danger" "Attack Josephine whenever you see her"
                                                      *Time-delayed knowledge. I forget, can you make someone forget something with dominate? If so, write a message to yourself and hide it on your person (say a little used pocket). Then, implant a command in yourself to pull the paper out of that pocket in X time and read it. Finally, remove the knowledge of the paper from your mind. With email, this gets even easier. This means that you can do tricky plans against people who can read minds or detect lies
                                                      *Related to the first point, but it can be used to make contracts binding. Make it known that whenever you sign a contract, you will willingly Dominate yourself to carry it out faithfully

                                                      Sure, none of these are going to provide you with mechanical benefits (except maybe the reflexes one. Depends on the ST there) but it's some cool story tools to play around with
