                                    In Requiem for Rome, it's even said that a lot of vampires don't even think of those powers as Disciplines, and believe Daeva are just that attractive and alluring because they are, while for the Daeva themselves the use of Majesty is almost reflexive and not something they consciously do necessary.                                     Making other Vampires always notice when you turn on Majesty can easily make it useless, so I prefer to come down on punishing it only when it's being used too egregiously. Why do you care if Daeva girl is being popular across the room? She can knock herself out, and social rolls aren't magical mind control, so even with Awe she won't be able to make anyone do anything that just isn't in their nature (unless she uses the advanced dots, in which case she's suicidal in the face of other vamps), so who cares again, exactly?
