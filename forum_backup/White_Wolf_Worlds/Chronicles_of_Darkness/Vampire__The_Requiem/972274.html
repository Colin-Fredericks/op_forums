    Odd frenzy question

    09-18-2016, 10:11 PM
    In the Blood and Smoke book, one of the ghoul merits is Beast Whispers. Allowing the ghoul to act as a Touchstone for Kindred.
    The Rules allow a touchstone to attempt to talk down a frenzied vampire with an extended social action.
    If a ghoul had majesty 2 thus would get 2 extra dice to presence rolls would this help in calming down a frenzied vampire?

    What about dominate? Would dominate be allowed as part of the Social roll to stop a frenzy?

    Normally social and discipline rolls fail to stop a frenzied vampire, but this is an odd exception.
    How would this work?

          Normally only someone who is a touchstone to a specific kindred may talk that kindred (and only that kindred) out of frenzy on an extended social roll which takes into account the kindred's blood potency. With the merit they can potentially talk any kindred from frenzy, not just the one for whom they're a touchstone. The merit doesn't let ghoul touchstones add their discipline rating to use powers against kindred and attempts to Dominate the kindred out of frenzy would still auto-fail. (You aren't talking them out of frenzy you're trying to instantly force them to obey you.)

                                      This isn't really covered in the rules, so I'd say you have to decide what 'Talking Down' is in a thematic sense.

                                      For me - Touchstones are a deliberate connection of a character's Man side of the Man/Beast pair to Humanity. Touchstones are a tangible reminder/example of what is human. This gives touchstones a special ability to reach through the ravings of the Beast and allow the Man to come back into control as a guide away from the beast.

                                      Going with that theme, I would consider Disciplines in ghouls a manifestation of the Beast, not a manifestation of what makes Touchstones human. Disciplines would actually detract from the essence of humanity. I would say that the frenzying Beast would process another Beast in that and actually respond like a fellow predator - sticking with the Feed/Fight/Flee trio. If the discipline is something that adds a passive effect to the Touchstone's stats - Applicable to a talking-down Social Roll - I would let that fly. But if the effect attempts to alter/control the frenzying Kindred, I'd auto-fail it.

                                      If, however, you see Touchstones gaining their special connection with Kindred as something inherently 'them' - a personal relationship where they have a back door during frenzy - I could see it making sense that Disciplines can go through that back door.

                                      I personally think RAW lends itself more to the former, but the example isn't specifically laid out in the books as far as I know.

                                              I think that it is pretty well covered in the Book.

                                              Awe gives +Majesty to presence rolls. Not "in some cases", but in ALL cases that Awe is active. therefore, if it is active ( and not shut down with a lashing out ), you get the bonus.

                                              Touchstones are allowed to make an extended social roll to talk the vampire down from a frenzy. Social roll would likely be Presence + Persuasion ( Awe, striking looks and skill specialties all apply )


                                              A direct attempt on ending the frenzy through a discipline auto fails. A direct attempt to do something to a vampire when you are a ghoul autofails, Such as Dominate or Charm. Awe does not directly influence anything external. It Internally changes you. It indirectly does because it only allows the user a dice bonus to their direct attempts, Striking looks or a skill specialty would.

                                              A frenzied vampire does not flat out ignore the beasts. No where does it say that a frenzied vampire is immune to disciplines. It actually says you can use them on a vampire who is frenzied. You can dominate a frenzied vampire, you cant end their frenzy, but you can change their targets. You can charm a frenzied vampire to perhaps not eat you, but someone else.

                                              Awe and majesty is so very subtle that no one knows they were ever effected by it. It even says that vampires dont know you are using it and dont ever feel cheated after.

                                                  Worth noting that Ghouls using Disciplines on Vampires do not get to use their Discipline rating if it is contested or resisted. Auto-fail only happens if they have any stage of Blood-Bonding to the target.

                                                  Talking someone down from Frenzy also seems more like Manipulation than Presence (I'm not sure how much charisma and authority one can conjure up as a Ghoul or Human against a Frenzying Vampire), but I admit that strongly depends on both your Storyteller and your attempted method.

                                                  Personally, I see the exception being made for Social rolls. I see nothing in the text that indicates an exception for Disciplines. Using Disciplines to coerce a vampire into ending their Frenzy fails. A Frenzying Beast can't be swayed by another Beast. Only the Man, which is what a Touchstone petitions with their social roll. That's how I'd justify it in-character.

                                                  Though I don't really think allowing the bonus from Awe (and not any other Disciplines or Majesty abilities) would break things or be too much of a stretch. But I definitely wouldn't allow anything else to work.
