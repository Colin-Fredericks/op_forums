    In situations like this, I tend to follow the 'Protagonists rule'. Briefly, rule in favour of the protagonist. In a Vampire game, it's more likely that the vamps will notice the werewolves and be able to use their Disciplines to hide/escape/defeat them.

    In a werewolf game, the wolves are more likely to notice/track/hunt the vampires.

    Use all the same rules, just give slightly more weight to the protagonists. (This being a Vampire thread, I'd suggest the bloodsuckers have the edge.)
