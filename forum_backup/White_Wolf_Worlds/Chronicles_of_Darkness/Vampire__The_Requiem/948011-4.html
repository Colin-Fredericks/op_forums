                                                    Originally posted by K9ine View Post

                                                    Graveworm (OOO, or OOOOO)

                                                    Prerequisites: Must be Nosferatu.

                                                    Effect: For some Haunts, the curse manifest as a physical thing by warping their body in such a way that is visibly grotesque, but for others it's much worse.
                                                    Your Beast is not content to simply writhe within your soul, it emerges in physical form. You have one or more orifices on your body, that nest a round, sucking, maw. By spending a Vitae, the maw emerges, revealing a long, leech-like parasite, often in a gout of blood. The worm (or worms) can be used as another pair arms or legs, giving you a better reach for climbing, and the ability to grapple at a distance.
                                                    I dont know if this has been said or not, but this is Protean. Transforming and twisting your body to give you animal like abilities. So for 3-5 experience you are doing what Protean 2 could do - that's 6-8 experience. Based on just that I dont think this = Merit = is fair.

                                                    I try to shy away from making merits to do things Disciplines can already do and I never make merits that alter disciplines. If you want to Alter a discipline to do more or make it better. You make a devotion(page 142) I would probably make this Protean 2 and Vigor 1 1 dot devotion 1 experience. ( for the 3 dot version of this merit )
                                                    For the 5 dot version of this merit I would say 4 protean and 1 vigor devotion ( for 2 exp )

                                                    Edit: there is one merit that alters protean Claws of the unholy. prereq. Protean 4

                                                    Originally posted by K9ine View Post
                                                    Terrible Reach (OOO) The worm (or worms) are 10 meters long, and extends your reach to accomplish any task that doesn't require much manual dexterity. For example: Slipping it through prison bars to grab a prison guards keys would be appropriate, while operating a keyboard or firing a gun would not. This appendage is strong enough to bear your weight, and can be used to help you climb 10 meters as a reflexive action (Storyteller wants this to be a move action at half your speed instead, seems reasonable). The worm can also be used to used for an unarmed attack, or grapple up to 10 meters away. When grappling your victim may only attempt to use the Break Free, or Damage moves while grappled. If you take damage in any of your last three hit boxes (Bashing, Lethal, or Agitated) the grapple ends, and you get the Arm Wrack Tilt, giving you a -2 penalty to using these appendages for all physical actions.
                                                    As for what it does Mechanically. 10 meters is about 30 feet. Which is about a 2 story building.. So you could grab someone off a roof and pull them down? That to me seems a little over powered. 10 feet is more acceptable.

                                                    Climbing 10 meters as a reflexive action. is Celerity. you would have to be moving so fast... I agree with storyteller, make it a Move action as you are moving. seems appropriate.

                                                    Using the appendage as a fist instead seems fine, but again I am not ok with grappling someone so very far away. Though the additional grapple mechanics seem acceptable.

                                                    Originally posted by K9ine View Post
                                                    Hungry Maw (OOOOO) The worm is not just a sucking mouth, but has grown a ring of hooked fangs. This Maw is always hungry, and is visible on your body unless covered by clothing. If touched it may attempt to bite, and cause a dramatic failure on affected social rolls related to physical intimacy. This maw can be used to feed during a grapple up to 10 meters away.
                                                    Again I am not ok with feeding off someone who is on the ground while you are on the roof. It is too far. I would say 10 FEET at most, but to be able to feed at a distance like the Strain vampires. Seems worth a devotion and kind of cool.


                                                    That is my opinion.

                                                    Second Edit: I made something to feed from a distance for my game, but it was a 5th dot Discipline so it took 15 exp to get...
