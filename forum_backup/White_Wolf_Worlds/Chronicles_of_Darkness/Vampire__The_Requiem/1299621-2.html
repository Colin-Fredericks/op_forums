                                                        I'm pointing out that the 'lack real motives' critique is bullshit. You can love them, hate them, or be completely ambivalent towards the Strix without misrepresenting them.

                                                        People still here for the point of the thread to think about if they want to consider VtR 2e deserve a real discussion of things, not just mud flinging.

                                                        There's actually some decent critiques of how the Strix motivations are structured. Their general motivations as a group could use a paragraph or so to give them a bit more teeth. The "jealous of living things since they'll never be a living thing," aspect is a good motivation for why they're so active in attacking people and causing trouble compared to other ephemeral entities (and even why they care so much about the undead as the closest they can get to being alive), but there's a bit of a lacking in a why they care. Though they're NPC only baddies and STs don't really need more depth to be able to portray them well.

                                                        Also, Strix are highly individualistic and hugely defined by their Vice (since they lack the diversity of character traits and anchors like most characters). The areas of vagueness in their general motivations as antagonists are largely there because they're not all going to approach it the same way. Overly defining the why of their envy of the living reduces the variations on individual Strix.

                                                        To relate this back to that werewolf thing. Apocalypse werewolves really only worked as antagonists for vampires in two arenas: direct violent enemies, or cagey allies of another faction working with some vampires against a greater good and helping eliminate vampires not on board. Forsaken werewolves have nearly countless ways they might come into conflict with vampires, as there's nothing predetermined in them coming into conflict at all. Each instance is going to be somewhat unique even if we could create some basic categories of them.

                                                        The Strix are far more like Forskaen than Apocalypse; even if a bit in the middle. While they have innate reasons to be in conflict with vampires, each Strix has their own interpretations on why, and how they want to handle it.
