                                                  My Boy Builds Coffins by Florence and the Machine: Used it as a song sang by a Daeva musician during a meeting of Elysium.

                                                          I'm actually kind of surprised by all the songs with lyrics here. Maybe it's just my groups that can't handle songs with lyrics (either they start complaining about the song if they recognize it or they start critiquing the lyrics meaning, which can turn into really interesting two hour discussions in and of themselves).

                                                          That being said, I've a number of soundtracks I've made use of over the years: Mass Effects 1-3, Silent Hills 2-4, Broken Notes (A Silent Hill remix album), pretty much any and all of the Castlevanias (I lean heavily on the Castlevanias). I've got a few background songs I pulled from Ghost of the Shell to get an appropriately punkish sounding noise for the Carthian Movement.
