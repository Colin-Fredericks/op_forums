                                        Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post

                                        The issue as I see it is whether the ‘volume’ of Vitae corresponds one-to-one with volume of blood, not whether vampires have blood in their bodies in the first place.

                                        Well, in that case, I'll just say here what I wanted to edit into my previous post:

                                        I'd say the ratio of vitae to blood varies on a lot of factors and I don't think it's ever an exact number or ratio. A large cow will only give you 3 or so Vitae, even if you drain it dry it won't give you more than that. Yet even a petite (size 4) human gives you 5 Vitae at the very least, assuming you drain her all the way until she drops into a coma or dies. Yet an elder vampire of normal human size can hold 75 vitae. Yet it also states that when a vampire uses Vitae, he doesn't lose blood, the blood merely loses Vitae. Not to mention when a vampire goes in torpor to lower his Blood Potency, one speaks of "thinning the blood", meaning the amount of vitae the vampire's blood can hold becomes less, even though the vampire in torpor doesn't literally lose or leak blood.

                                        So in short: it varies and there is no single correct answer to the OP's question.
