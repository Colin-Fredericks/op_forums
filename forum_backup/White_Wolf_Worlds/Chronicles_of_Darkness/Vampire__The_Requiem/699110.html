              How long they take to show up depends entirely on how far away they are at the time. Using it in the middle of a building will mean waiting longer than if you used it in the woods.

              Commanding said creatures requires Feral Whispers, so it will last as long as normal. You don't need to command them to attack anything that you spilled the blood on. They'll do that simply to get at the blood.

              The effects of being attacked by a swarm of crows is probably best modelled as if the vampire was using the Swarm Form merit on page 114. In other words, the victim gets the distracted condition and the swarm makes an appropriate strength + brawl roll each turn, ignoring defense. So probably 5, 6 dice? I dunno.

                  There's no set time they could take to reach your destination, but a good rule of thumb is 6 turns-Successes (min 1 turn). For flocks of ravens and other swarms use the rules for swarms in the book as per the Swarm Form Merit (p.114). I would say that a swarm summoned by Call the Hunt would have a size equal to a vampire with Blood Potency equal to your successes in the activation roll. There's some stats for several animals in the Onyx Path Blog so you can check the Defense, Speed and attack pool of the swarms.

                  A flock of crows or ravens, for example, would have a Speed 14 (fligth only) and attack rolling 2 dice (Strength 1+ Brawl 1), for Health you may use 4 health levels (those of a crow) for each point of effective Blood Potency (see above).
