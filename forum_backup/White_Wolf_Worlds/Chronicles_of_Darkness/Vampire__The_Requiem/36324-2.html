                One of the great things about the World of Darkness is the unstated danger of knowledge. We feel at our most vulnerable when we are ignorant, and thus we seek to grow in knowledge, boxing and categorizing everything into concepts and conceits we understand and can deal with. And because we feel we know these things, we try to make as much of the world fit under those concepts until we gain enough knowledge to break it into another category.

                The danger, then, is that we are prone to thinking of any unknown as one thing when it is very probably an other.

                    Maybe the Nameless is just a (or many) clever vampire.

                    - The name of the Ventrue Prince, Thomas Sheridan, is shared with an irish actor who is known for his work on elocution (how you behave on stage). That guy specialized on how to deliver messages and convince an audience of what you wanted them to believe.

                    - The date of the Ventrue Prince's death, November 5th. And nobody went "Remember, remember... the 5th of November..."? Someone kills the figure of authority on the anniversary of an failed plot to topple a government. Someone has a sense for theatrics...

                    - A young unnamed Carthian was the only witness to the Prince's demise which, honestly, reads like a well rehearsed play.

                    - That night was named "The Martyrdom". The name irks me. Martyr implies "somebody who suffers persecution and death for advocating, refusing to renounce, and/or refusing to advocate a belief or cause, usually a religious one". The Prince was Invictus. Maybe he believed in some religion, but the term would have been better used for the Circle or the Lancea, The again, Thomas Sheridan's father, also named Thomas Sheridan, was an Anglican divine. I don't know...

                    - The second execution has the same vibe as the first one. An unnamed neonate walks in by chance to be the only witness to the Seneschal's execution. I think a repeat of the Ventrue Prince's death was only to make a point.

                    - Then, the Carthian Movement had a big party who was crashed by our Nameless. Everyone died with but one witness. There's always one witness to spread the word...

                    - The only surviving Kindred had her eyes taken and won't regrow them. Maybe she was dominated... maybe she sincerely fears the Nameless... maybe she has glowing eyes... or maybe she's the damn Nameless... who knows!

                    - The rules... the Nameless seems to have a keen awareness of every infraction. I call either Carthian Law, powerful blood sorcery or, like it was suggested, a threnody with either something like Lord of the Land (which coincidentally increases dread on its domain) or Auspex and the sacrifice may have been the Ventrue Prince to make the effects encompass the entirety of its domain (the whole island). With the last option, maybe the lost of theban sorcery is merely a side effect of the Beast's manifestation in the threnody.

                    Maybe the Nameless is an icon. It never speaks (it is only rumored to). The only description is "a black figure with shining eyes". The only one who saw the Nameless are outsiders and "witnesses" of those three nights. Very reliable sources...

                    The state of the city in the Movement's paragraph: "city without Elysiums, without formal feeding grounds, without proper law enforcement, and without all the trappings of a typical city". The rules stuck in stasis every Kindred take for granted don't apply. If this isn't a revolution where the established order and its traditions were toppled...

                    Maybe the whole thing was a Carthian scheme to take the city. The goal of that Carthian reunion was to establish dominion. That night is called "The End and the Beginning". Why? Technically, the Nameless has taken over since the death of the Invictus Prince. Isn't that the moment "the End" of the domain as they knew it? Or the Carthian reunion was the end of the construction of the icon that is the Nameless and the official beginning of their dominion? In a city where everyone operates in little cells, communication is everything and the Movement seems to control it with its communication network, phone trees and PO5170.

                    Maybe the Nameless is simply a myth the Movement constructed to establish and maintain their leadership, one that the Kindred population is now too afraid of to even question or contest.

                    Maybe the only reason the Tsihstekeri are exempt is because they are neutral, make an effort to stay on the sideline and have a truce with the Nameless. And that's why they know and the Ventrue who are told the secret don't fear the Nameless anymore because he isn't some boogeyman, but just another vampire.

                    Why do those in the know are afraid of something else entirely? What is worse than a vampire is a vampire's predator. If you think the Nameless' rule is harsh, wait until the Strixes are unleashed! A whole population of encroached French Kindred were destroyed overnight by the natives after decades of being miserably on the losing end of the war. You need something really powerful the French never saw coming and it must have been an unconventional tactics as most conventional ones would have been used during those five decades and failed. Maybe Rue St-Paul is "haunted" because it is one of the oldest road of the city where one of the blood farms may have been and it still suffers from the fallout from those owls.

                    One of the many possibilities. Maybe the Nameless is instead an old Nosferatu delighted by the fear induced by his rule while recreating the blood farms of the French, but with a vampire population so he can feed.

                    When in doubt... ... ... Everyone's grudge!
