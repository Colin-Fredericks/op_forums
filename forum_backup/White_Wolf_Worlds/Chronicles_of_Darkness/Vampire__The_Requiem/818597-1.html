        It can be fun being the demon that people sell their souls to just like some people like playing the people willing to sell their souls.

        Plus you buy this once, and you can have a lot of people who are effectively Retainers without actually having to buy Retainers.

        Plus, if you're smart, you can find someone who wants to use those three days for something that you also want. Maybe a rival's favorite Ghoul slept with this guy's girlfriend. He wants some vengeance. He's not thinking clearly, which isn't your problem, but when he's done? You have a weakened rival, a new thrall, and none of it can be traced back to you.

        It's definitely situational. Even more so than other Devotions which tend to be more situational than Disciplines in turn. But once you're thinking like a proper vampire, doing things the indirect way rather than the obvious way, it seems like it could be a really fun tool.
