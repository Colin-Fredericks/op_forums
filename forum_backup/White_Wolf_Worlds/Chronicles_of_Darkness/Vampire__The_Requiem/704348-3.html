        Way i see it, the greatest complication with maitaining such an operation would be:

        1) Correctly gauging a vampire's individual power level is not always simple even for kindred, that have something of a sensitivity to it, much more so for a ghoul or mortal, not to mention one might get unlucky and come across a paranoid and inhumanly observant one (Mekhets being particularly prone to this combo, in fact) that may catch on to someone stalking them (and prepare itself accordingly) before one is even sure of wanting to taking them down, what can complicate somewhat things.

        2) Blood Sympathy connects a vampire to their "blood relations", read, their sires, grandsires, childer and grandchilder, all of whom have a chance of catching glimpses of any emotionally-charged situations a kindred goes through - like being attacked, staked and tortured into compulsory embrace and becoming someone's vitae factory. It ain't anywhere close to reliable, but the longer one messes with a captured vampire, the greater are chances of some "relative" trying to track them down, or getting help of those with an interest in that kind of disturbance, like hounds and sheriffs.

        And for the sake of reference, it doesn't take an ancilla or elder to have grandchilder: neonate A embraces neonate B because it was feeling really miserable and needed the company of a friend/lover. A few years, months, damn, even as little as a few weeks later, neonate B goes on and embraces neonate C, one's best bud since forever who was about to have a miserable, painful end due to a drive-by, car accident or disease on terminal stage and B decided to give fate the finger. So, depending on circunstances, there could be between 0-4+ kindred of the same city who occasionally get glimpses of weird crap going on and might be looking for them, per kindred one captured straight from the streets.

        Damn, the ghoul's system you have given so far sort of digs itself an even bigger hole in a way, as every hobo/hooker/drifter/illegal immigrant/etc embraced is one more undead prisioner under regular imprisonment and torture with a blood tie to another undead prisioner. What means your prisioners will be at least aware of each other's existence and might try to find ways to help each other or complicate their wardens lives, not to mention the whole emotional hoopla involved in their whole situation might lead them in who knows what weird mental paths and things blowing up into crap like the Barjot's or even worse, Melissidae's brand of hive mind would not be out of the realm of possibility. One could end up with some sort of weird brand new bloodline being born or even worse surprises into one's hands without wanting to.

        3) Zoo maintenance - simply put, serial killers usually have being killers in their favor - they kill people, throw the corpse somewhere to rot and move on, rinse and repeat. Meanwhile the ghoul can't do the same with the kindred, because it's milking them out of the all too important vitae they produce. So, not only does one never rid oneself of one's corpses (because they are one's cash cow so to speak), but one needs a whole operation to keep them imprisoned and reliably producing vitae and will look for every chance of escape they can.

        And because one needs a variety of vitae sources to try to forestall any vinculum formation, obtaining ever more animate corpses - through straight capture or coerced embrace of random people - becomes a necessity and the more kindred you have, the greater becomes one's requirements for space arrangements to keep them caged (not to mention the cells, restraints & etc), access & expenditures into sources of animal blood, boxes or cellars for torpid ones, "jailors" that must be paid of for their service, in vitae possibly (as i have difficulty of thinking of much beside potential immortality that would be worth such an utterly atrocious job), driving upkeep costs even higher and so on, ever harder to hide effectively as one goes...serious snowball effect there.

        I can see one possible workaround on the last problem, that would be for one to keep one, at most two vampires imprisoned and deal with the "vinculum avoidance" issue by regular trade with other ghouls who are similarly taking care of only one or two young kindred. That way one only needs to dedicate the time and resources for one or two prisioners - much more manageable, as real life shows us all too awfully well - and keeping one's contact list safe and up to date. Networking things considerably disperses the management costs of the whole operation and might considerably reduce the need for periodic additional embraces, much diminishing most of the other risks, i guess.

                                                About what I would do to capture a vampire with minimizing risks, assuming I'm an Hunter, a weak Ghoul or simply a mortal character that wants one to study and experiment with...
                                                well, it depends on how much information I got about them.

                                                To localizate a vampire from a safe distance, I would use an high definition camera and, from an elevate position at the maximum distance ( which is several miles with an electronic camera ) I would record night clubs, discoteques and other noctural recreative places. Every five minutes I would control the recording, searching for people that would appear strangely obfuscate despite the others being recorded clearly. Then I would have to do a drawing of the subject or to memorize his face or anatomy ( that would require some training in visual memory techniques but it's not really hard to do ).

                                                That would require much time but is a safe method. If I had some more economical resorces, a termic camera ( like those used by zoologists ) would speed up the process a lot allowing me to detect people with unusually low body heat.

                                                I would try to understand roughly how that vampire moves and if there are others like him which he abitually hang out with, always from a long distance. When I found one that I'm reasonably sure routinely moves alone, I would choose a location that allows me a clear sight, arm myself with an high quality hunting crossbow ( +2 dices for quality equipment ), with a binocular gunsight ( +1 ) and a laser pointer ( +2 ); I would assume diazepam to calm nerves and have a firmer hand ( +2 ); then I would take my time to aim ( +3 ) and to concentrate ( 1 point of Willpower for an extra +3 ), and, finallly, place a wooden quadrell between his shoulders right in his heart from 120 yards.

                                                Another thing to note is which kind of vampire will you be hunting for in order to erect the battery farm and how will you deal with the consequences?
                                                The best way to deal with the consequences of capturing a vampire, if the hunter know nothing about him, would be to immediately left the city, travel a long way and having the alread-prepared prison far from any big ( and even small) tow, in a barren location. Beside Auspex 6+ or strange devotions, I guess it would be impossible to track the kidnapper thousand of miles away, in a bunker lost in the desert.
