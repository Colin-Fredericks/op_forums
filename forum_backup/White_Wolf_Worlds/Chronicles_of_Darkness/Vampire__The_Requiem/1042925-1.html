                    You can get burned by ice/extreme cold tempatures, from chemicals, and boiling water as well as from electricity and fire. I'm really not convinced that "getting burns" is a good argument for lethal damage in this case. As another said, extreme heat is not a bane. Now, if clothes caught fire, that'd be one thing, but that's not what we're discussing here, and I don't think I'd allow that with electrokinesis from a vampire, since I'm also opposed to vampires with pyrokinesis. No evoking anything approaching banes as powers.

                    As for the argument that the player sunk XP into a power? Perhaps that would be true if there wasn't more to the ability than shooting electricity in combat. But you can power electronics, arguably control computers and machines to an extent, shape electricity in artistic ways, increase or decrease the intensity of it, manifest it from nothing, and more. Attacking is only one of sixth of the abilities you get with psychokinesis at best. In theory, I think you can even use electricity to put out fires by ionizing the oxygen in the air, though I'd probably require an exceptional success for that.

                    Now, if it was an ability where dealing combat damage was its -only- ability, then yeah, I could see that argument. Or even half of the things you can do. If violence is somehow the purpose of the ability, then I would agree with "spent xp = lethal." But I can't agree with that argument when psychokinesis does so much. If "spent xp" was the deciding factor, then Protean 2 claws would be dealing lethal as well. They don't. Should someone who spent XP to buy Martial Arts 4 be able to deal lethal to vampires? I'm not convinced that should either, despite being an ability that primarily revolves around dealing damage.

                    There's some flexibility in here depending on the game, and I'm not a fan of allowing it. It just doesn't feel thematically appropriate to me, and that's not the kind of story I would want to tell.

                    Now, if the OP bought the power with the expectation of dealing lethal, and only intends on doing lethal, and there was no discussion on the matter beforehand, I would allow, as an ST, for the player to refund the ability for something more relevant.

                                    Originally posted by Maina View Post
                                    Blood Sorcery explicitly can't create Banes.
                                    Bane manipulation is the purview of the Coils, not Blood Sorcery. And the Coil of Zirnitra does not directly manipulate fire: it unlocks access to a psychic power that allows to create and control fire, electricity, or cold (or even two or three elements at once if you buy the Psychokinesis Merit multiple times).

                                    The Mystery of the Ascendant, which is an entire Mystery dealing specifically with the Banes of Fire and Sunlight, cannot grant power over either one.
                                    The Coil of the Ascendant does something different: it provides increased resistance to fire and sunlight, and makes the vampire more lifelike. Pyrokinesis as bestowed by the Coil of Zirnitra and the Psychokinesis Merit allows the vampire to push fire away from oneself or snuff it out (among other things). One is similar to wearing fire-resistant body armor all the time. The other is akin to carrying a fire extinguisher (with a charge equivalent to one's Willpower) all the time. They are different and complementary enough in their approach they can easily co-exist.

                                    Plus it just seems really against theme for a vampire to be slinging around and controlling a substance that is supposed to symbolically and literally destroy them.
                                    Vampires are entirely able to sling around and control fire by mundane means (barring frenzy issues) - grab a flamethrower and/or an extinguisher - so I'm unable to see any theme issues here. Besides, the main theme of the Coils includes overcoming Kindred limitations and allowing vampires to 'cheat' with banes, frenzy, humanity, and other foibles, so it overrides any such concerns.

                                    And I'm not even sure how you'd learn it with the whole Frenzy issue.
                                    The Coil of the Ascendant can help a lot here, and so do high Willpower and/or the Coil of the Wyrm. Pretty much any trick that helps vampires control frenzies may be the solution. I expect most vampires that are interested in getting pyrokinesis develop high Resolve + Composure, the Coil of the Ascendant, and/or the Coil of the Wyrm in parallel to the Coil of Zirnitra and Psychokinesis.
