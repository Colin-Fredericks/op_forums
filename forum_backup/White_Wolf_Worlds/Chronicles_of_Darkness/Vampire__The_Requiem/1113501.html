    Daywalking

    07-08-2017, 10:38 PM
    So I've been debating removing the sunlight bane, or at least altering it sufficiently that Kindred can survive in it.

    Humanity should limit rolls during the day, although I hesitate to limit die rolls to humanity dice. Maybe a penalty based on humanity? In some cases that could be harsher, as dice pools smaller than one's humanity could be reduced. It'd be less of a limitation for stronger Kindred, though.

    I am thinking 0 or 1 vitae per turn for all BP, or perhaps scaling like 1 vitae for 1-7, 2 for 8-9, and 3 for 0. Or some other scaling that is still restrictive even for the most powerful.

    For Disciplines, I am again thinking of 0 or 1 dot limits for everyone, or maybe allowing it to scale similarly to vitae above. So powerful elders will have a few additional tricks.

    Also, considering keeping things so that higher BP is penalized more than low BP by daytime.

    I'm not sure I am actually going to do this, but it's been on my mind as a possibility.

                                                          Originally posted by Malus View Post
                                                          Isn't sunlight considerably less harmful the lower your blood potency and higher your humanity is in 2e? Something like just created vampires taking 1 bashing every 4 or so hours if I recall correctly. Or is this hack supposed to be for 1e?
                                                          True of 2e as well. But it's lethal damage minimum, even with 7-10 Humanity.

                                                          Specifically: 1 lethal at Humanity 7-10, 2L at Humanity 6, 3L at Humanity 5, 1A at Humanity 4, and increases until you get 5A at Humanity 0.
                                                          Meanwhile, Blood Potency 1-2 gives you a ten-minute interval. BP 3 gives you one minute, and BP 4-5 gives you a turn. At BP 6-7 a single turn counts as two intervals, at BP 8-9 a single turn is three intervals, and finally at BP 10 a single turn is five intervals.

                                                          So a Humanity 10, BP 1 vampire would suffer one level of lethal damage every 10 minutes.
