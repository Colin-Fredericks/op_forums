    Noctuku Update for Second Edition

    03-11-2015, 08:29 PM
    EDIT: This is still incomplete, but I decided to update what I had so people can give feedback. I plan to make two more dots in Phagia.

    The Noctuku have always been one of my favorite Bloodlines, so I decided to try and update it. I hope to comeback to this and add Devotions that bring back some of the powers that Phagia granted, but right now I don't think I'm up to that challenge. I'm sure I've made some serious mistakes, so please point them out so I can fix them.

    Noctuku
    The Noctuku are considered monsters among monsters by other Kindred because their preferred prey is other vampires. Because of this, Noctuku usually live a solitary existence to protect themselves from other vampires and other vampires from them. Noctuku who abhor a solitary existence usually group together into tight-knit clutches. These clutches are surprisingly stable do to the precautions and ritualistic behavior taken on to reduce the chances of a self-destructive feeding frenzy.
    Clan: Nosferatu
    Nickname: Bogeymen, Cannibals
    Disciplines: Nightmare, Obfuscate, Resilience, Vigor
    Bloodline Bane: Those who join the Noctuku bloodline soon find that their hunger has twisted and grown. All Noctuku suffer from Vitae Addiction and can never rid themselves of it. In addition, Noctuku find that they hunger for the flesh of their prey. If a Noctuku fails to deal at least 1 lethal damage by eating the flesh of his victims for a week, he gains the Bestial Condition. A Noctuku who has gained the Bestial Condition and has not eaten for a week must drink the blood of a victim and eat at least 3 lethal damage worth of their flesh before he can resolve it. Even if he has had the Bestial Condition within the last month, he can still gain it from not eating the flesh of his prey. Despite their need to eat flesh, they cannot actually digest it and will throw it up like anything else they eat.

    Bloodline Gift: Apex Predator
    As a predator of predators, the Noctuku often can't help viewing Kindred like other vampires view mortals. The Beast of a Noctuku is an all-devouring thing that is difficult to deny, but it does provide the tools needed to hunt its favored prey. All Noctuku have the Vitaevore Advantage and may purchase the Phagia Merit to increase their ability to feed.

    Vitaevore
    Do to the particularity of their diet, Noctuku are better at tracking other vampires and are somewhat more resistant to the Vinculum. All Noctuku gain the benefits of the Weakened Bond Merit and can track other Kindred by tasting their blood as if they were mortal.

    Merit: Phagia (• to •••••; Style)
    Prerequisite: Noctuku
    Your Noctuku character has begun to heed the secrets his Beast whispers to him. Known to the Noctuku as Phagia, these techniques assist in hunting the forbidden prey: Kindred. Phagia transforms the Vinculum from a means of control into a tool for feeding. While a Noctuku still feels all the love and slavish devotion anyone under the Vinculum does, he also feels a terrible hunger for the one who has him bound.

    Devouring Maw (•): Whenever your character succeeds on a Feed move, he may deal an extra point of lethal damage to his victims as he tears and swallows their flesh along with their blood. Anybody who has had damage dealt to them by Devouring Maw while being fed upon gains the Scarred Condition as if they were mortal.

    Just a Taste (••): If your character is under the Vinculum, he develops blood sympathy towards anyone who has bonded him. A stage-one blood bond is treated as three times removed, stage-two as two times removes, and stage-three as one removed. If your character already has a higher level of blood sympathy to someone than this Merit would grant, use the higher one instead.

    Dark Hunger (•••): Your character gains a bonus to tracking and detect blood sympathy rolls against whoever has him under the Vinculum equal to what stage he's at. He also gains a bonus to his Humanity roll for harming someone who has bound him equal to the stage of the Vinculum he's under as long as he's frenzying.
    Drawback: Your character also gains a penalty to resisting frenzy when hungry or starving equal to the stage of the Vinculum he is at as long as he is near someone who has bonded him. Use the largest penalty available if your character is near multiple people who have bonded him. If your character frenzies do to hunger he will try to feed from the person who he has the highest bond with first if possible.
