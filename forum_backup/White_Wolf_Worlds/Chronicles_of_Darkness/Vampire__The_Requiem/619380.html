    Domain: Panem et Circenses

    08-07-2015, 05:01 PM
    Designing a domain set in a fictional city to avoid geographical realism. The city itself is still a work in progress, but I wanted to come up with a premise that would make the domain itself interesting but not so far out of the norm that it's intimidating for new players.

    My first point, which is the most critical to the plot, is that this is a domain that only recently was brought back from the brink of chaos. Elder feuds spiraled out of control, people died. The major players met their final death or fled in the last year. But their pawns still hold grudges.

    Point two: the new Prince is a puppet...sort of. Cheshire, as she calls herself, is a Daeva only a few decades into undeath, and was appointed by a circle of Kindred who despise each other. She's not the strongest, smartest, or best connected Kindred in the city. She was the compromise that left everyone unhappy. She knows all this, and doesn't give a rat's ass.

    She's just enjoying the hell out of it. She doesn't care about the feud between her Primogen, but is smart enough to maintain the balance of power between them, since any one of them could probably take her down if they could survive the wrath of the other four.

    Basically I designed her as a bored, uninvolved, hedonistic Prince who is unliving on borrowed time and can be deposed in play.

    Which leads us to point three...and where Panem et Circenses comes in.

    The Prince doesn't want to deal with matters of state that don't interest her. She's bored easily and concerned with the luxury that power affords her, not power itself. So you want her to oversee a matter? Well, make it worth her time.

    Each Covenent, as well as each Primogen, and some independent coteries, have a specific place where they can host Elysium. Any host can call for the Prince to settle a dispute or hear a plea at their Elysium...but you provide entertainment.

    The Sanctified hold their Elysium in a graveyard chapel where they put on Bloody Passion Plays, sufficiently altered to make them palatable for unbelievers. The Mekhet Primogen hosts in the VIP room of the hottest S&M club in the city, where Kine perform live extreme fetish shows. One Coterie of Drakes makes their shared Haven into a museum of the uncanny and abnormal.

    It doesn't matter what kind of entertainment...but if your show isn't sufficiently satisfying, good luck getting your matter across. The Prince may even leave before it's over if she's bored. And if she leaves before the entertainment is over, it's her unspoken decree that for the rest of the night, Elysium--and the protections it affords--are nullified.

    Thus, even the most reserved Kindred need to be gracious hosts and entertainers aside for any political leverage. Have fun!

                                      If you want her to be incompetent as Prince, but able to survive the NPCs, so she needs true back-up from the Elders. They need to see her as more than just a little compromise between them, but truly an asset. Of course, that depends on their long term plans.

                                      Another option is for her to have the back-up of something more powerful than the Elders, but disinterested from their politics. Maybe her sire is a true Methuselah with far reaching power, but little concern for the city itself. She won't intervene in their decisions and fights, but also won't let someone hurt the girl. In that case, she can be just spoiled, and her status as compromise between elders don't need to have a straight correlation with the protection she receives.

                                      On those cases, the ways for the players to change things is to work on he Elders or the Methuselah, but mostly the Elders. Either upsetting their power balance or just offering a better option will do. Maybe they get rid of her, maybe they just strip her of power and send her to a place better fit for her. The Methuselah should be convinced to take maters on her own hands for her sake or against her, maybe forbidding the Elders from using her, or she can be convinced to remove any protection over the Prince, letting her to be eaten by the wolves.

                                      You could also play the deception route. This is the perceived state of the city, and most Kindred believe it. But when someone go against her wills, bad things happen, and the Elders do nothing about it. What's going on? She really isn't powerful, but maybe the Elders aren't on the game most people think they are. They can be Dragons doing an experiment on the city about the ability of Kindred to survive, organize and prosper in such conditions. Maybe they decided on a twist for their usual schemes, each one took a couple of pawns to fight for them by proxy and the city is truly a giant living board game for them, so they don't have to risk their necks and can yet take some fun of the process.

                                      Maybe she isn't so powerful, but she seems to be. She's a less-than-perfect compromise, but with perceived power she can at least keep the city from falling apart. People aren't happy, but they fear her enough to try a coupe. Under the disguise of seemly futility she is truly giving attention to some maters as needed to appease the Elders. And with the entire circus, the vampiric pop of the city is too busy to figure things or organize by themselves. The Elders just have to keep her perceived power. It doesn't solves their enmity, but until they find a way to solve it, she keeps the city running, poorly but without bloodshed. She isn't a happy pawn. She knows she's as good as dead, and the only thing she can do to buy more time is to put a smile on the face and obey.

                                      Those other scenarios require the players to peel the layers of deceit. Things are still similar to the original idea, but not quite. The distinction is that people thinks it is precisely the original idea, with the add perceived power from her, when she truly has none. At most she have the power to fake real control of the situation.

                                      For a twist, she can have actual leverage on the Elders. Be it a secret, a weapon or an ally, it isn't something she uses to fool the people, but something she uses to keep an upper hand against her real abusers. She can't use it to full extend, or to actually achieve control of the city, because if she becomes an actual danger she will be targeted for destruction. But once she became the compromise on their petty internal war, she can use it to prevent herself from becoming disposable. The people isn't happy with her rule, but her life actually depends on her being a crappy ruler. She can use said leverage also against the Keepers of Elysium, maybe they're even the Elders or their childes, so she can lift Elysium protection. She's hated by the people, but as long as she plays her cards right, the Elders will protect her. She desperately needs help to actually leave this situation, for even being able to keep it a long time, as soon as she looses leverage against one Elder she will be destroyed.

                                      With this option you give her one thing to guarantee the Elders protection, and even some measure of power over some other Kindred, but at a cost of being a liability to everyone. The players need to find the leverage and decide what to do with it, from seizing it themselves to destroying it or any other decision. She doesn't need to know the best use for this leverage, she's under so much pressure that she'll just keep running the first plan she could conceive and pray for it to work one more day.
