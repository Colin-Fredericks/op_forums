    Strix, Shadow Form and Damage.

    12-04-2015, 06:27 AM
    Hey all, I've been through every possible resource so I'm looking for a hand with Strix in my VTR2 game. Questions as follows:

    1) Strix in Shadow Form (assume SP1). Can they actually hurt a vampire? I've assumed they are effectively just animate, sentient shadows; unable to actually do any direct harm (short of breath drinking).

    2) Harming Strix in shadow form. Strix ignore all damage. Unless it's magic, or is a special power that does agg in which case it is reduced to bashing. Consider though, Claws of the Unholy, or Feeding the Crone. Both do aggravated wounds, but they're still physical attacks that can't actually touch the Strix to inflict those wounds to begin with.

    Any ideas or clarification would be much appreciated!

      I'm not a developer, so take what I say with a pinch of salt thrown over the shoulder:

      1) Strix cannot use Breath Drinking on vampires. In Shadow Form they don't seem able to inflict physical injury, but they're largely immune to Kindred attack. This is great for terrifying scenes as the vampire is haunted by some dark thing of smoke and shadow. Their Dread Powers still affect vampires normally, and they can presumably harass the vampires with other Skills.

      2) This one is going to be a judgment call for your group. Claws of the Unholy is a power born of Vitae and Kindred nature, so maybe it has the power to rend the shadow-owls—only to horrify the vampire as the Strix in question seems unfazed. Or maybe they simply don't work on a shadow-form Strix and the vampire needs to pursue other methods.

                      The whole "only deal bashing" bit is nothing more than just extending one's health track, as far as I'm concerned. Yes, you can kill it, but you must have a lot of damage to put them down. The introduction of the Khaibit into the game changes everything, though, when dealing with the strix. Dealing automatic lethal damage on top of being able to hit the thing, and passing out the weapons, frankly, makes it far less of a threat when it comes to hurting them. Suddenly, we went from nigh unvulnerable, to managable. So, I opened the door a bit more. They're still an issue, but act differently enough that they're still a threat, but not one that's not as much a physical threat in shadow form. Which is something I'm okay with as well.

                                                          Everyone can deal with the strix by trapping them in a physical body or circle and setting it on fire. Strix also often come with individual banes that anyone can discover and exploit. Mind control is also effective, unless it happens to have a specific power. Shadow manipulators can do some things (including Obtenebration, psychic powers, and death magic) but the exact details have never been really explained, so that's up to ST fiat at this point.

                                                          By the same token, however, everyone can deal with ghosts, spirits, angels, and goetics by their weaknesses or mind control. So its a moot point, all is said and done.

                                                          But, that said, lets not get into a power measuring contest. Do we need to go into Obtenebration v. spirits and the like? Its just unnecessary to start saying that this or that power is required. Not to mention a large assumption - not every werewolf out there carries around fire elemental magic, so assuming that they'll have it in case they happen to run across one of the Owls of Dis is a bit of a stretch. Just like we can't assume every mage will have sufficent Death magic, every psychic can control shadows or flame.
