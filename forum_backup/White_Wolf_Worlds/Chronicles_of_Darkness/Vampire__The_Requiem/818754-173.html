    Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
    Whats a Ventru probably going to do with a Revenant who he thinks is a worthy Kindred, but not a worthy Ventru?

    Send him packing?

    Take him to Elysium a few times Hoping he comes to someones notice?

    or try introducing him to a prospective sire?

    If hes worked for you faithfully and you feel the need to reward him but he doesn't have the poise of a Ventru?

    As a Ventrue, all of these questions can be answered very easily. Just put yourself in the Ventrue's shoes and ask yourself: How is this going to benefit me? What course of action would benefit me the most? How can I improve my position using this?

    If you're a proper Ventrue, you probably have at least 10 Kindred on call, all owing you a favor. Asking one of them to embrace this Revenant could be that favor. But again, ask yourself this: how would this benefit the Ventrue?

                                            So, here's a short little excerpt from the Humbled Condition, which is the source of my question:

                                            "Your character has felt the touch of the divine and trembled.
                                            She feels unworthy and wretched. For as long as the Condition
                                            lasts, she suffers a –2 die penalty to Resolve rolls and may not
                                            regain Willpower from her Requiem."

                                            The Condition refers to your requiem, which is described at the start of the book as:

                                            "What are you going to do to make it through tonight? What
                                            about tomorrow night? And after the deeds are done and your
                                            belly’s full, how are you going to live with yourself? What are
                                            you going to do with your damnation that makes it worth all
                                            the sins along the way?"

                                            But what does this mean in game terms? I often see mechanics refer to "your requiem", but it's never clearly defined what that means from a mechanical point of view. Is your requiem simply your Dirge? Is it your Dirge and Touchstones? Or your Mask, Dirge and Touchstones?

                                            I also saw some mechanics refer to "your masquerade", so I suppose the same question applies there as well.

                                            My initial gut feeling tell me that "your masquerade" = your Mask, and "your requiem" = your Dirge, perhaps your Touchstones as well.

                                            Anyone has a clear answer for me?
