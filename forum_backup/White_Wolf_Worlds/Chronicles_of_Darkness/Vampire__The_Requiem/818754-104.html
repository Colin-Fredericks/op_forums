                Arrows are made of carbon or graphite and plexiglass. Crossbow bolts are made of carbon, graphite, and aluminum. They're also capped with steel tips if you're doing more than shooting through burlap bags, no wood. If you're jury rigging crossbows and compound recurve bows to fire chunks of sharpened wood to "stake" a vampire, you're right back to square one of using improvised weapons. So no, it's really not ridiculously easy to impale a vampire with a stake using bows and crossbows. But if a player wants to spend the XP to hyperspecialize like that, I don't care. It's easy to counter if they get stupid and arrogant about it.

                            Originally posted by Gellydog View Post
                            ​How organized to people typically play the clans?

                            Clan Status is something you can buy dots in, but most of the fluff I've read seems to focus much more on the Covenants as sources of organization for Kindred society. Is that an accurate reading?
                            On the one hand, the only consistent thing tying a clan together is commonality of themes and mechanics plus the fact that solitary Kindred still come from somewhere.

                            On the other hand, a childe represents an investment of Humanity and a greater point of influence for their sire than most other vampires without the Vinculum, your sire is the vampire with the most notional care for your well-being and interest in seeing you integrated with the All Night Society, Dynasty Membership is a covenant-agnostic Merit, and even vampires with no direct relation to you can find their way to you through sympathy if they're nearby and share your clan.

                            Clan Status is generally the vector by which you get characters who play to and reshape their own clan's archetypes being better at influencing their fellows through mundane channels, and it serves as one of the ways for an out-of-town vampire to shake things up in a city without pulling on more discrete connections or mystical force, which is useful considering how much investment Disciplines and Blood Potency take and how much politicking City and Covenant Status are gated behind by comparison.

                            There's relatively little inherent and deliberate organization to the clans, but vampires are people and their lineages tell you things about them and the world they inhabit that their less inborn mystical/social ties can't as readily establish.

                            Resident Lore-Hound
