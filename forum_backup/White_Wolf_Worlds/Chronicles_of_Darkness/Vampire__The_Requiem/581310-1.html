                                                    Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                                    Where are described Shadow Cults of Requiem? In 2ED corebook? They sound interesting.

                                                    They are interesting They were introduced in the Mekhet clan book, and seem to be the inspiration for the Conspiracies in The Danse Macabre and Hunter: The Vigil. They probably also inspired the Mystery Cult Initiation merit in GM and B&S.

                                                    The core concept was based on the idea of Mekhet building secret societies as a means of bringing mortals under their influence despite lacking mind bending powers like Dominate or Majesty. Initiation into the cult was racked via an escalating merit, which brought bonuses based on the themes the cult was built around. Shadow Cults have three levels of initiation. Humans could be brought into the first level of the cult, from which they would serve as food or minions. The Mekhet clan book has a bunch of additional rules for having a character go through the process of building such a cult for themselves to lead.

                                                    Rendered more like a Mystery Cult, the Tremere might be...

                                                    A story is told among the Magi of the Atlantean traditions. It is story of eager Mages seduced and betrayed by the cursed undead. A story of vampires who drank souls and who destroyed themselves by attempting to consume Mages. Magi who were then left corrupted by the treacherous assault. Needless to say, the Kindred heirs of House Tremere tell a very different version of the tale.

                                                    In the vampire's account it was they who were betrayed. A wise kindred mystic named Tremere and his students had spent centuries studying the mystic arts to find a means of alleviating their cursed condition. They were approached by a group of Mages who promised to share their mystical knowledge and aid in the search for a cure for vampirism. Though the mages shared many secrets, their altruism was a mask hiding a sinister agenda. The magi hoped to use their arcane arts to steal the immortality of the Kindred, and once the vampires dropped their guard, the Mages struck.

                                                    Tremere was laid low, cast into permanent Torpor by mystic means. Most of his followers were tortured to death in the Mages' experiments, as the Magi used the power of the Kindred curse to transmute themselves into immortals. All was not lost, however, for a few of Tremere's apprentices survived. The Magi had taught the Kindred too many of their secrets, and the followers of Tremere were able to summon powerful elemental spirits to their aid. In a single night of sorcerous fury the power of Kindred blood joined with the essence of the shadow realm to overcome Atlantean arcana. The magi were driven from house Tremere, and the Torpid form of the house's founded was reclaimed.

                                                    Tonight, most of the followers of Tremere have given up the quest for a cure. Instead they hold an endless vigil over his body, and constantly study the occult in a ceaseless mission to find a way to revive him. Their ancient pacts with the great elemental lords still hold; granting them both power and protection. The grounds of the Chapter houses of the Cult of Tremere are guarded by earth elementals and golems, who can crush most intruders with ease. While the halls within are patrolled by invisible spirits of air and shadow, who lead unwelcome visitors astray. In battle the Tremere wield powerful amulets which direct the power of the spirits against their enemies. The mightiest of the Tremere even summon and bind beings of living flame, to unleash upon Kindred foes.

                                                    * Tremere initiates are schooled in the art of "Hermetic Theurgy", the evoking of spirits for knowledge and power. They gain a free "Spirits" specialty in Occult.

                                                    ** Tremere Apprentices who have proven themselves are introduced to the cults network of occult scholars. They gain the Contacts Merit (Occultists) free of charge.

                                                    *** As a Tremere experiments with Hermetic rituals and undergoes further cult initiations they become attuned to the spirit world. They gain the Unseen Senses Merit pertaining to Spirits for free.

                                                    **** Highly proficient in handling spirits, high ranked Tremere manifest the ability to converse with spirits and perform summon rituals with little preparation. They gain the Medium Merit pertaining to Spirits for free.

                                                    ***** Just as powerful spirits become renown among occultists, so to do power full occultists become renowned among spirits. The Archons of the Tremere have the equivalent of a three dot Fame Merit among spirits, granting them a +3 bonus when attempting to communicate with and influence spirit entities.

                                                    (see The God Machine Chronicle for references on spirits and supernatural merits)
