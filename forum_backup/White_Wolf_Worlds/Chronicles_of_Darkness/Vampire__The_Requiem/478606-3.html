                                            Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                            Personally I like the image of a vampire paladin chopping spells in half, but that's just how I roll.

                                            I believe that many would agree with you, including myself, and would welcome some mechanism for doing as you suggest (within reanson). The criticism of Crusade is primarily that just breaks verisimilitude and is overpowered within the context of 2e mechanics and comparable merits.

                                            I think that one of the problems is that much of Crusade might be trying too hard to fit into the niche of a significant combat ability for LeS characters that chose not to invest any merit dots in Theban Sorcery.

                                            After reading the constructive criticism of many here, I would suggest tweaking Crusade as follows:

                                            1. Increasing the Occult prerequisite (and/or allow TS as an alternative prerequisite).

                                            2. Focusing the merit on a particular designated weapon more than the vampire. In order to gain the advantages of the merit, require some moderately difficult ritual such as to anointing a weapon with Vitae, the blood of a large predator and blessed holy water under a full moon. The magic in the weapon will store for one month per blood potency of the vampire. When facing a magical opponent, the vampire who conducted the ritual can then invoke the magical protection in the weapon (up to Crusade dots) for up to an entire scene.

                                            If you want to keep with a strict LeS and Longinus theme, possibly only permit Crusade to use a spear as the magic weapon and require a Weaponry specialty with Spears as a prerequisite.

                                            3. Make Silence the one dot power and/or alter it to reflect training in removing more targeting penalties besides the throat to disrupt a target's use of magic.. In addition to targeting the throat to affect someone's voice, it could be valuable to target to eyes to induce blindness, wrists and elbows to impede gestures and targeting, etc. The attack also need not be a typical strike, and could include feats such as rustles dusts, leaves or other objects at a target. These special attacks should result in Tilts without damage consistent with 2e combat.

                                            4. Add a two dot power similar to the Indomitable or Iron Will merits to resist magical mental coercion or illusion.

                                            5. Shield can only deflect direct physical and noticeable attacks like fireballs. No deflecting mind control. A willpower point expenditure might be considered and it should be a minimum 3+ dot power.

                                            6. Eliminate or reduce the additional damage of sorcery or discipline level for Hammer or possible cap it at the weapons natural damage modifier.

                                                    Perhaps also make the dice pool modifier for the rank 1 Crusade equal to the dots in the merit rather than equal to Weaponry? Capping the penalty at 3 (i.e.: opponent must spend Willpower) rather than at 5 (leaving some of those other splats that use only 2 stats for their power dice pools in a lot of trouble) and encouraging purchase of the other dots of the style because from my point of view rank 1 is the bit that I want and the other two dots are really not very attractive. Nice to haves but fundamentally if I am hitting a sorceror with a weapon I am probably doing fairly meaningful damage anyway and there are plenty of ways of buffing that damage (blood for more strength, willpower for more dice, all out attack all give more dice for more potential successes), or Vigour. Or called shots as often Rituals or Extended Castings leave their user without a defence.

                                                    A starting character with a single merit dot and 4 dots in Weaponry (the kind of player who wanted to have weaponry 5 at the start but couldn't justify more than a 4 right now) is going to be close to immune to any incidental (i.e.: ones they have but aren't a focus of the character so their attributes and skills aren't gamed a little to help the dice pools on them) Disciplines people have and maybe also some of the more focused ones too.

                                                    Assuming resisted with blood potency+attribute and average values for attributes is 2 this character reduces the dicepools of all (?) Disciplines by 7 dice at character creation.

                                                    If they also take Indomitable they are causing a -9 dice penalty to any of the mental Disciplines like Dominate and Majesty and Nightmare.

                                                    If it's a power that can be defended against then it could be even more of an issue. Athletics 4, Weaponry 4, Dex 2, Wits 2 and a Shield (giving +2 or 3 to the defence, including against ranged attacks) is causing this magical attack to suffer a -12 dice penalty. And that attribute block (Dex 2 Wits 2) is...maybe not the most common on someone focused on being a 'knight' with a sword and shield.

                                                    Note also that against an enemy whose primary form of attack is magical or magical ranged there is no reason not to be in all out attack against them because you're not resisting with your defence anyway.

                                                    I'd be really damned happy spending 3 of my 10 merit dots at creation and 4 of my 11/7/4 skill dots for that. And most of that I'd likely have taken anyway if I were making a Lancea 'knight' so it's really not a hardship or meaningful additional cost.

                                                    Now that may well be the intended result (it makes a Lancea 'knight' really, really, really hard to hurt with magic) but this same character is also really very good at physical violence and has not spent that many resources achieving this state so could happily spend a few merit dots for some allies and connections and having a solid set of social attributes so there is no reason this character needs to be weak socially so the argument of solving all your problems with violence need not come up for this character.

                                                    Basically, if I were playing a member of the Lancea et Sanctum I can't see a reason why I wouldn't take this merit at one dot (and ignore the rest of the powers because those require other supporting expenditures to be worthwhile). Even a single dot of Weaponry on the character makes the merit worth it. So long as taking the merit is not directly deleterious to my concept of course. But Lancea Priest who dabbled in their 'youth' before finding their true calling? Gangrel with Frenzy problems who finds solace in the teachings and disciplined life of the Lancea? Anyone the Lancea are just trying to tempt into the Covenant with some small enticement?

                                                    This would be a merit I would be looking to steal through hook or by crook with merits like the Carthian one that lets you access other Covenant's unique stuff.

                                                    Also, what if your favoured weapon is a stick? Like a jo-staff or other short stick like a...walking stick? You could avoid the most obvious issue of requiring you to be holding a weapon if your weapon is something you could potentially be carrying with you frequently.

                                                    I have nothing against this in concept - I just feel it's far too powerful as it sits right now and it's the kind of thing that would frustrate me as the GM a lot. Because 'and you chop down the enemy wizard/sorceror/ventrue/etc. with little threat to yourself' gets a bit repetitive after a while.
