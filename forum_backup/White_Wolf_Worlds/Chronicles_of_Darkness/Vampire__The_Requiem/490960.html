    Nosferatu: Clan Curse Ideas

    04-24-2015, 05:54 PM
    Back in Vt:M, the Nos were all universally hideous and deformed, so they just failed at any interaction requiring physical appearance.

    I've actually been really happy with the way the new Nosferatu clan weakness has manifested. I like the idea that you don't have to be terrifyingly ugly as a Haunt, but that something else is just . . . wrong, about you. I love that with a high Presence, or even Majesty or Dominate, you can surmount some of your Curse . . . but if you fail, you fail hard no matter what.

    To that end, I'm curious what other people have come up with to describe their character's bane and the way it manifests (if at all; obviously even if you cover up whatever's physically not right, there's still that aura of strangeness that still unsettles mortals, but that's beside the point).

    I'll offer a few starters:

    Elena Kerensky: A woman in her thirties with a patrician air to her, she's fairly average-to-attractive, but she's a touch pale—almost with a faint blue-green undertone in the right light. This, along with the slight dampness to her skin and the smell of salt and water that constantly clings to her, almost gives her the air of a drowning victim.

    Violet Madrone: This girl is incredibly beautiful. She's perfect. Too perfect. In fact, she's one-hundred-percent symmetrical, head to toe. From the arc of her hairline to the porcelain skin of her brow to her sapphire-toned eyes to her golden-ratio perfect nose to her full cupid's-bow lips to her gently pointed cheekbones and chin, either side of her face is a perfectly symmetrical match to the other. She's like a living doll, a creature that just climbed out of the depths of the Uncanny Valley.

    Phillip Karlsson: A tall, slender, athletic man, with a receding hairline and slightly elongated, rodentine features. He's calm, composed, unobtrusive. In fact, unless he actively speaks or makes effort to engage with you, he almost seems to immediately fade into the background. Almost, but not quite. His presence kind of wavers right on that border, just like that feeling you get when you twitch awake, not having noticed you were about to fall asleep.

    "There is the world of light, and the world of darkness. And some in the light prefer the darkness." — Loren Eiseley, The Night Country

      BBQ Girl: A wide-faced, friendly young woman, with mildly frazzled hair and a sturdy neck. Despite her job as a grill cook and her namelessness and her minimum-wage aura, she's got a confident face. A stern face. A stiff face, in fact. It's like she's done something surgical to freeze her expression, only there's something so... painted on about her freckles, her eyes, her lips, as if they're illustrations on an external paper stretched over a hard rim. Occasionally, her skin shifts alignment on her head, so she looks like her face is slightly detached from the axis of symmetry. There's a patch of skin missing on her brow, under her hairline; underneath is warm, slightly rusty metal, two visible rivets and a hole that seems to open into a dark red nothing. She says it's from surgery. She always smells like grease and fat and blood, for more than one reason.

          Slink: She's damn graceful. When she walks it's like tall grass blowing in the breeze. Flowing. Effortless. When she dances... man. I saw her frenzy once, she chased a guy down and when she was running, she just... she just like rolled over everything in the alley until she wrapped him up. I try not to think about it anymore.

                          Bacon Strips: A physically in shape man who appears to be in his early 30's. The muscles on him indicates someone who routinely works out either as a hobby or because his job requires an above average physique to perform. He is clean shaven and bald, with a friendly disposition and a square jawline. Unfortunately he suffers a terrible skin condition from which he gets his name; across his body are streaks of exposed muscle tissue from where his skin has been peeled or flayed off his body. The pattern resembles tiger stripes along his head, arms, legs and back. It's easy enough to hide the rest of his wounds, but the head is the more difficult feature, generally requiring him to wear shadowy hoods or a mask of some sort when in public,



                          Grandpa Harold: To the casual observer, Grandpa (who he insists on being referred to by) appears to be an ordinary man in the state of advanced age. Like, really advanced age... like, why isn't this guy hooked up to a life support machine yet? Grandpa's body is withered to the point of near mummification. His hair is ghost white and is so thin it seems like cobwebs floating about his head. His face is sunken and his eyes seem hollow as they never open wider than a half squint when he's feeling a surge of emotion. When in public, Grandpa Harold is confined to his wheel chair, holding a walking cane over his lap, and with a rather attractive nurse pushing him where he plans to go. Grandpa Harold loves to tell stories of his youth both as a human and as a vampire, and many kindred respect him for the experience he has to offer. Those who lack proper respect, however, receive a swift cane across their backsides!



                          Katie, the Slob: One of the Nosferatu who was lucky enough to retain her mortal visage, Katie is easily able to disguise herself as a human. Unfortunately it takes more than physical appearance to make her popular around the community, and Katie is almost universally despised because of her crude, vulgar and downright unseemly mannerisms. More than once she has been caught picking her nose in public and then wiping it on her clothes or even the environment around her. Sometimes on other people if she thinks she isn't being watched! She belches loudly and without covering her mouth and swears like a sailor during conversation. She appears to have no shame when discussing sensitive matters or secrets and no matter how high in the status quo she is associating with, she speaks with them in a casual, flippant manner. She wears the same clothing for days at a time, accumulating various stains throughout the weeks and appears to do her laundry once every 6 months. She forgoes bathing for 3 or 4 days on average, and her hair is left wild and unkempt. Katie, the Slob offends everyone within sight and sound of her location, and unless they have business with her, most try to avoid getting close enough to strike up a conversation.

                              (In the last game I was running, I definitely played up The Contaminant Worm aspect of the Kindred condition; for example, a well-to-do high-rise apartment building that ended up inhabited and owned by Violet Madrone became this lovely, secure, spotlessly clean, but sterile, cold place that looked like it was inhabited by the likes of Patrick Bateman from American Psycho and Zeke Hawkins from Sliver. The elevator hummed smoothly and a little too quietly, the shadows thrown by fluorescent hall lights—which buzzed just the tiniest bit—were just a shade too sharp, and as for the security cameras . . . anyway, the non-mechanical aspects are a big part of storytelling for me.)

                              Holy's idea is a really impressive expression of both of Curse and Contamination. It's likely no one could ever put it together in concrete words, but they'd still be a little off-put by that character's presence, although not necessarily consciously, even if the character was otherwise totally normal. (To bastardize the line from Fight Club, after Tyler splices a single frame of porn into a family movie: "They don't know that they saw it, but they did.")

                              Hate and bad arguments would just seem to follow him, even if he's the nicest guy in the room. This actually stands out to me even more because of that particular psychological phenomenon everyone has, where part of your mind can sometimes just come up with the most horrible thoughts, even if you aren't necessarily racist, sexist, ableist, ageist, or any other -ist . . .

                                          Stumps Mcgee: Something went wrong with his embrace. No, he didn't become a larvae or some other abomination, it's just a unique condition for which nobody seems to have an explanation for. Mcgee's body seems to have a defective preservation ability. Every night he wakes up, a random body part has rotted and festered, sometimes even falling off depending on where it is located. The rot apparently lasts for 24 hours because the following night, it has been reconstituted, only for a different piece to take on the affliction!


                                          Cupcake: She's the sweetest girl in the city, and that's the problem. The girl who is affectionately referred to as "Cupcake" is just too energetic, too bubbly, too friendly, and too aggravating for other people's taste. Staying in the same room as her for longer than 5 minutes quickly becomes grating on one's nerves. She often wears pink and bedazzles her clothes with fake gems and glitter. She even still wears her hair in pigtails and has key chains with her favorite characters attached to them. Her Haven is littered with stuffed animals, silly wallpaper and kitten posters, and she will not hesitate to sing the jingles of infomercials and children's broadcasts when they air on TV. In Cupcake's attempt to be cute and friendly, she comes off as childish and obnoxious!

                                              Maximilian Dean: He's always felt like an outsider because his curse has manifested in one of the most literal ways possible. Maximilian is drawn to ideas, organizations, and occupations that separate him from society by their very natures. As a member of the Ordo Dracul, he is a natural scientist. His curse is his refusal to let go of provably wrong theories, and indeed, his willingness to adopt the most bizarre ideas and methodologies. He has been, at various times in his existence, a phrenologist, a lobotomist, a member of the Ordo Templi Orientis. All occupations completely out of sync with modern science. His current Mask is the behind-the-scenes leader of a Church of Scientology. These attributes would be bad enough, except he is also the primogen for the Nosferatu in his city. All of the other Kindred in the city must eventually deal with him and he is about to make his move to replace the current prince. For those in the know, they can only imagine the chaos he'll plunge the city into should he ever achieve that goal.
