                                I think there's a misperception here that Majesty creates sexual feelings. It doesn't, not necessarily. It does what it says on the page: makes a vampire seem like the coolest person in the room, gets someone to lend her a car, encourages him to do immensely stupid things for her. It doesn't necessarily make him feel anything sexual -- that's going to depend on the social interactions between the vampire and the victim. Most of us like and trust and use poor judgment around people we'd never sleep with, and this is just as true with Majesty as without.

                                Similarly, while the Seductive Beast skews more easily towards sex, it's really about desire and indulgence, and that can take a lot of different forms.

                                How this works in play depends on two things: the thoughts/feelings of the characters (whether player or ST), and the comfort zone of the group.

                                For the characters, not everyone is open to being seduced, and not every vampire is everyone's type, no matter how awesome she seems.

                                For comfort zones, I'll be blunt: a lot of people who are cool with a vampire charming a Storyteller character into providing a point of Vitae (with or without makeouts) are going to be skeeved by the pickup artist homewrecker vampire you describe. That's because most of us have very limited experience with blood loss,* but many have somewhat more experience with being mistreated sexually.

                                Vampire, and Majesty specifically, can be very evocative because of how they lend a supernatural tint to things we as real people can identify with... but for the same reason, one needs to be careful with what exactly we evoke.


                                * I once lost more than a pint of blood due to a medical mistake. If I felt the way I did then after a hookup, I would not be friending the vampire on Facebook.
