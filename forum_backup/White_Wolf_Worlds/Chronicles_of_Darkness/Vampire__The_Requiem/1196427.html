    Ordo Dracul plot hooks needed

    03-17-2018, 06:23 AM
    Hi guys,
    I need a little bit of help: I want to make the Ordo the Focus of a few plot lines ( some crossover, some vampire-only) and I would love some ideas, as I am stumped as to how to flesh out rudimentary ideas I had.
    1. Something for the Ordo players to do. What could be typical Ordo Business (in a cross-over-friendly gameline)? Investigate ghost-ridden houses? Search for Dragon's nest? Kidnap a Supernatural for study? Sample different types of blood? What would be the Focus of the night for a Sworn of the Axe? Dying Light? Mysteries?
    2. For non-Ordo kindred, what would interaction be like? What is the Ordo up to in their spare time? Why do there covenants respect /tolerate/fear them?(Details/examples for Ordo Experiments and Research welcome!)
    3. What are typical skeletons in the closer for the ordo, that either kindred or other supernaturals (e.g. Beast's Whispers) could use against them? Experimental diablerie?


    Thankful for any hooks, ideas, concepts you throw my way!

He's approached the Ordo about help making some kind of Coil of the Embrace, making reproduction easier. Some of the Ordo want to tell him to take a hike (whether this is because they respect the Tradition, think too many new Embraces would be dangerous, or because they fear the other covenants raining hell on them if they help). Others want to help (whether this is to spite the Lancea, or because they like the idea, or because it might be worth studying even if they don't want to use it).

          Another faction of another species claims Vlad as one of theirs, and has evidence supporting the claim.

                      1. A. Something an Axe Sworn may be dispatched to do...
                      An elder Dragon recently met Final Death. She was working on a project to transfer the full vampiric curse to animals. Get rid of her test subjects before outsiders learn what she was doing.
                      1. B. Something a Dying Light may be dispatched to do. An elder Dragon recently met Final Death. She was working on a project to transfer the full vampiric curse to animals. Gather up her documentation and provide a peer review authenticating or repudiating her findings.
                      1. C. Something a Mysteries Sworn may be dispatched to do. An elder Dragon recently met Final Death. She was working on a project to transfer the full vampiric curse to animals. The sheriff, herald, elysium master (a covenant outsider of mid level influence and some high level connections) learned of the Axe Sworn’s mission and bailed them out when they underestimated one of the creatures. Get them to agree to keep the potential Masquerade breaching incident a secret or take preemptive steps to diffuse the GPA about Dragon activity to avoid the fallout (which could be spin doctored any number of ways to portray the Ordo Dracul as an intolerable threat to stability.)

                      2. Honestly that depends on the setting. In one setting I play in, the previous prince was a Dragon who was deposed in large part because the remaining Dragons formed a power bloc and allowed him to be ousted by the current prince for brazenly abusing his former position as provost and effectively decimated the covenant sending them to their deaths or indefinite torpor and destroying wyrms nests and libraries in order to secure his praxis and spite rivals within the covenant.

                      3. The same kinds of skeletons any other kindred may have. Diablerie. Secretly murdering a beloved childe, sire, ghoul, living family member or friend of someone very influential or even of a kindred who is supposedly their best friend. Or maybe they killed their own living child or spouse in order to learn a scale. Or they’re the ‘at large’ serial rapist or murderer. If the domain is highly regulated or “picky” about not allowing revenant or ghoul creation, that is something they may have and are keeping secret. (They’re keeping Uncle Morris alive until there’s a cure for emphysema meanwhile the Dragon does medical experiments that involve an illegal organ harvesting ring and stem cell collection.)

                          The Sworn of the Axe have been instructed to provide support to the enforcers of the Carthian Committee that rules the city. The Carthians are sympathetic to the Dragons' scientific mindset in a way neither of the religious covenants are, nor the orderly Invictus, and on a political level the Ordo wants them to remain in charge.
                          Some of the Carthians are skeptical of the Ordo's motives in this, suspecting they are more interested in taking power for themselves. This may be so, or the Ordo might just care about a safe environment to experiment in (of course what environment could be safer than one they rule?).

                          A local Acolyte cult has had a lot of success in the creation of Mandragora. Several members of the Sworn of the Mysteries have been tasked with investigating whether their claims are true, and the effects and capabilities of the plant ghouls. Discovering and replicating the method of their creation would be ideal, but not at the cost of an inter-covenant war. Failing that, just learn what the Mandragora have to teach about the processes of vitae.
                          Some of the Acolytes mean to welcome them with open arms, in the hopes of converting them to the Mother's cause.

                          The local Dragons are paying Storm Lord werewolves to capture and bring them the spirit-ridden rather than killing them, in the hopes of studying the effects of different types of possessing spirit on the blood of the host.
                          Apart from any complications among vampires, the other Storm Lords really don't want the rest of their tribe finding out about this.

                              The Ordo receives a letter with instructions to pick up a shipping container at the local port/train yard and secure it so that it may be secretly opened. The letter is from the Chairman of the Council Ordo which rules Seattle. In exchange they are offered arranged safe transport and a prestigious haven large enough to share, if so inclined, in Seattle, redeemable as long as the Ordo rule Seattle and the Ordo has ALWAYS ruled Seattle making it a handy safety net should things turn sideways locally. The container is to not be opened until a representative arrives to examine the contents, however whatever is inside moves around, and is clearly some type of being, though it doesn’t speak or attempt to escape. The container is from Central America in origin. Is this a test? Is there something amazing inside that could lead to the discovery of a new Coil? Is this a prank or trap? What is within that shipping container?

                              Also as ST you should ask the Ordo what they want to research, because knowing what questions they want to answer will help drive plot based on PC desire instead of merely reacting to the environment. The way I imagine the Ordo is that almost every single one has a couple pet theories and arcane science experiments they want to investigate and reap the benefit from. Think about what other Ordo (NPC) are researching, have them discuss their pet theories, and as the PC about theirs, if the PC doesn’t have any have the other Ordo treat them like they are boring and not useful, a low level of contempt for someone not pursuing the Ordo’s purpose, like a child who doesn’t have a job, suitable only for dull errands. That realistic reaction would also act as social engineering to get them to brain storm projects they want thus, letting you know which metaphysics and game mechanics might be applicable for what they will explore ahead of time. Be sure to sow the NPC’s plans with both things that will pan out, things that won’t, and many things with weird unintended consequences of varying reliability, usefulness, and danger. This type of things is where Scales come from after all.

                                              I used an Ordo Dracul plot hook in my campaign for one of the many sub plots I'm weaving into the main plot.


                                              The plot hook goes like this:

                                              The Ordo Dracul found a Mekhet girl capable of going out during the day with no sunlight damage whatsoever, the sun merely annoys her, but that's it. The Ordo Dracul took the Mekhet girl in and she became one of their top scientists, but also one of their top subjects for experiments. The Ordo wants to know how she can walk during the day without using any Coils or Scales.

                                              This research will eventually lead them to her Sire; Baron Örnsköld, also simply known as the Baron.

                                              For those who have read the entire V;TR 2e book will probably already see where this is going. As it turns out, this girl is no ordinary Mekhet and her Sire is no ordinary Sire. The Baron is in fact half-Strix. The result of a Strix fusing with the vampire during (or shortly after) his embrace resulted in the Baron becoming a bit of an anomaly. He's not quite Kindred, but he's not quite Strix anymore either. He's something inbetween. Kindred enough to be able to embrace normally, yet also Strix enough to have his Childer being something... more. In this case, his Childe is a Mekhet who can walk outside during the day.

                                              All of this is of course incredibly fascinating to the Ordo Dracul, but also incredibly dangerous. How far is the Ordo Dracul willing to go in their pursuit of their ascension? What will they do when they find out the Baron is partly Strix? And what will the Baron do when he knows his secret is no longer a secret? I'll leave that up to you and your players to decide if you decided to use this hook.
