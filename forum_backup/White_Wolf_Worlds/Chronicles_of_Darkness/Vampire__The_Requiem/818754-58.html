            It's a Clash of Wills. Technically when you come into contact with something under the effects of the illusion, the illusion temporarily dissipates and you don't need Auspex to notice. (It's in the entry for Oubliette.) There's an example of how someone with Auspex would go against Oubliette would play out as a Clash of Wills on page 126 in the book. The presence or absence of the illusion generator is irrelevant.
            If I'm going to allow them to tear down the entire illusion (as opposed to simply not being affected by it), just have the Auspex user lash out and if Oubliette user is another PC, have them roll if they want to spend the willpower. If it's an NPC using Oubliette I'd probably rule it's an automatic success for the Auspex user.

                                                    Without a graphical aid, here's what I would do.

                                                    1. Is this character a ghoul? If yes, go to 2. If no, go to 3.
                                                    2. The character's regnant rolls Blood Potency. If the roll is successful, go to 8. If it fails, go to 3.
                                                    3. Did this character die by exsanguination? If yes, go to 4. If no, go to 5.
                                                    4. The vampire who exsanguinated the character rolls Humanity +2. If the roll is successful, go to 9. If the roll fails, go to 10.
                                                    5. Has this character consumed vampiric Vitae in their lifetime? If yes, go to 6. If no, go to 9.
                                                    6. Has this character been exposed to vampiric Vitae within one week of death? If yes, go to 7. If no, go to 9.
                                                    7. The vampire who's Vitae this character was exposed to after death rolls Blood Potency. If the roll is successful, go to 11. If the roll fails, go to 9.
                                                    8. The character rises as a Revenant in one week, minus a day for each success rolled.
                                                    9. The character rests in peace.
                                                    10. The character rises as a Revenant at an unpredictable time during the next week.
                                                    11. The character rises as a vampire in one week, minus a day for each success rolled.
