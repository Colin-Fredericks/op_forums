          Majesty as a whole is pretty weak against those with high Blood Potency. Basic Awe can be shrugged of with Predator's Aura. Confidant and Loyalty are already contested with Blood Potency (plus the Indomitable merit).

          Majesty isn't direct Dominate. Its meant to be a subtle crowd-control power, spreading out through a large number of people, used like a scapal. Trying to use it as a blunt force instrument is meant to be a problem. If you want to use it like a hammer, that's what the Cult of Personality devotion is for. And, even then, high BP are resistant to the effect. This is a constant theme throughout the Discipline. I mean, look at the name. Idol. You use your aura to make yourself a figure of adoration that the weak willed are swayed by. Stronger, older figures that know better shrug it off. There's a reason its not called Sovereignty anymore.

          With Majesty, you go through the steps. You need to put in actual effort to use it to the full potential. You walk into the room, you activate Awe. Everyone who sees you catches your eye. Then, you spend time mingling. Go up to everyone, smile. They welcome you into the circle for a bit (because Awe). You spread Confidant around. It doesn't cost vitae. You seed the area, making the ground fertile. For those people you -really- need to impress, you need to spend the time and attention to flatter them with Loyalty. You don't insta-snap the power and enthrall ancients to your whim. That would be too much. That's also the domain of Dominate, and I like the powers not overlapping.

          That said, I don't recommend elders facing down someone with Majesty anyways. Enfeebling Aura means that Daeva are probably going to out muscle even some elders once the rival's own Vigor is stripped away. Someone with Majesty 5, Vigor 5 and Enfeebling Aura can potentially out grapple even an elder; though, much more likely, they'd just stake them - 5 damage is nothing when Enfeebling just ripped away your Resilience; you're dealing 6 damage at minimum at this point.

          In a way, I like comparing Dominate and Majesty to the Invictus and Movement. One's all about direct loyalty and unassailable elders, and the other is spreading out over a large crowd of younger folk.

                              But that's just it. They're all pretty limited in some way. Dominate needs eye contact. Majesty has strength-of-will issues. Obfuscate works pretty well at hiding you from anyone, no roll needed of any kind, but you're FUBAR when it comes to technology. Auspex's telepathy can project thoughts, but not really control. Animalism 4 Feral Infection isn't eactly difficult to resist either - may not be Blood Potency, but still less likely as the vamp ages - and even then, its undirected frenzy. Nightmare causes hallucinations - its a glorified illusion ability now, mixed with Dementation. You can shrug it off most of it with willpower, Lashing out against offensive images, or just Riding the Wave.

                              Of the six "mental" disciplines, Dominate and Obfuscate are the only one that's most likely to succeed on an elder. You succeed on that first Mesmerize power, everything else is cake walk. Obfuscate, much like Animalism 4, has a few rolls that you subtract your Discipline rating from. Everything else relies on being tricky and smart with your powers.

                              Honestly, the only difference between Idol's roll and Feral Infection or Touch of Shadow's rolls is that Blood Potency can rise beyond 5, while even an elder is unlikely raises their attributes that high even with BP 6+. And if you use a power on an elder, you can expect a large dice pool, no matter if we talk Resolve+Composure or Wits+Composure. And likely some merits as well to boost those rolls higher. I think we're pretty consistant here.
