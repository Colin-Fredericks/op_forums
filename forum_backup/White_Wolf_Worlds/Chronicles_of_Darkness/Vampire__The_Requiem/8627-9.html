                                            I modified my version of the Toreador Bloodline, dropping the Devotions because they weren't mechanically sound, and I didn't have much feedback on them. I've made the Bloodline Gift into the standard rather than optional, but included something to help set them apart: The Toreador can pick either Dominate or Auspex as their 4th in-clan dot. This is to reflect their dual nature as both connoisseurs and patrons. The connoisseurs can appreciate qualities of art and people that are invisible to the eye, while the patrons are capable of ensuring their artists remain loyal and productive.

                                            The way I figure it, it's just a quirk of the Blood. Some inherited his facility for Auspex, while others, were embraced with an inclination towards Dominate.

                                            I am no longer participating in the community. Please do not contact me about my previous work.
