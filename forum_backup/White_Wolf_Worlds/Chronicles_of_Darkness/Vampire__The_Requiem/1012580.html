    Zulo/Chiropteran Marauder/Gangrel Warform

    12-05-2016, 11:00 AM
    I really like the monstrous forms of the Tzimisce and Gangrel in Classic WoD, partly because being able to turn into a great beast can make a single vampire more of a threat to werewolves, and because it reminds me of Dracula's demonic second form ability in the Castlevania games.

    I don't know as much about Requiem, and haven't been able to find any discipline like this in the core rulebook, so I was wondering if anyone knows about any discipline which might be similar to these from the classic WoD.
