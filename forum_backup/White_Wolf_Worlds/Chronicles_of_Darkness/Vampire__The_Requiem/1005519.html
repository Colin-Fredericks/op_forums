          Originally posted by Caladriu View Post
          I don't see why not. You'd just need a different set of breaking points, and maybe alter your Touchstones.
          You'd also need to work out how all the various things that key off Humanity would be affected - Banes, Clan weaknesses, sunlight damage, I think a couple of Conditions...

          The thing is, Humanity is actually super central to Requiem. The whole premise of the game is playing out the descent into monstrosity. How human a vampire's worldview and behavior is has a real, measurable effect on them in Requiem, making them, in a very literal sense, less human the lower it falls. So, a Requiem vampire that abandoned Humanity for a Road would pretty much just end up a Draugur.

          If you want to bring Roads into Requiem, I would recommend making them behavior codes that protect Humanity, kind of like Touchstones, rather than Humanity replacements. In fact, they would probably work just fine as actual Touchstones.

                                  Charlaquin has provided [her] usual excellent advice. The main answer to your original question is "yes, it's possible"--you can rip out humanity and flat-out replace it with a path if you want and your storyteller agrees. There are some things you'll want to be aware of:

                                  1. Please keep in mind that in VtM roads/paths were expressly monstrous in nature. Vampires taking them as their way of handling the mental+moral strain of unlife had to run down their humanity and then carefully adopt the new Path and build it up. The reward was freeing themselves of the shackles of moral concerns VtM was tied to, however the consequences of a Path remained that the vampire had an effective Humanity of 1 as far as system interactions went--she was a monster, just wasn't in danger of losing her mind.

                                  2. VtR 2e assumes that you're playing against the trope of "I don't want to become a complete monster because then I lose my mind". If your character can cut a deal with his beast such that it's happy with you behaving in a different way, and you're happy not being human, then cool.

                                  There's several ways you could accomplish this. The easiest is just to scribble out "Humanity" print off a list of your path's replacement sins, and assume you have Humanity 1 for everything except Degeneration rolls. This will bork you for Theben Sorcery, but Road of Heaven could flip the logic and treat your Path level as Humanity (treating it as 10 for Cruic?). You can do this, handwave the rest on the fly, and probably have a fulfilling vampire experience reveling in the night so long as everyone at the table agrees.

                                  Another way others have suggested is to treat your Path/Road as a merit that acts as a Touchstone. Now Touchstones are meant to represent things your vampire spends attention on, and have a risk associated with--they stabilize the character and remind her of her purpose and what it means to be her. The default assumption is that "to be her" refers to humanity and human connection, but not necessarily (or, not so directly). You could run the Path Merit from 1-5, adding a Touchstone at each rank along with two of the Path's "sins" in ascending order. Road of Kings would then include at Merit rank 1:
                                  2 Breaking your word to your superiors
                                  1 Breaking a sworn oath
                                  Merit rank 2 would add sins for Road level 3 and 4, and etc. Violating any of these sins would force a roll of the Merit level, to avoid detaching the highest attached Path-merit touchstone. Subtract the path level from your Humanity score to determine social penalties for interacting with mortals.

                                  A third approach is similar to the second, only instead of adding touchstones, the Path-merit removes a breaking point per level as if it were a bane taken, substituting the Path's sins for the bane. Breaking points removed cannot be actions otherwise forbidden by the Path.

                                  Personally? I'm still not sure any of this is really needed; it adds complexity and muddles some of the charming clarity of VtR vampiric existence, in which Humanity is not that expensive to build up and you can take Touchstones for virtually whatever you could think of. --I could build a 7,000-year old NPC (let's call her "Morrigu") from Doggerland whose touchstone is "Celtic standing stones" (or, a particular one) representing vestiges of ancient worship practices, and it'd work fine. She'd hover around 2-4 Humanity for millennia.
