    Is "My Clan Discipline for Yours" as common as PCs seem to expect?

    07-16-2016, 07:09 AM
    There is an undercurrent of expectation that trading one clan discipline for another is a fair trade and should happen among PCs, particularly in community-based games. I'm not so sure this makes sense from an in-universe perspective, but I can't put my finger on why. It just feels wrong. What are your guy's thoughts on the subject?

      Well, why does it feel wrong? Is it possible that this is an expectation from Masquerade that slipped over, where clan disciplines were considered sacred secrets to not be shared? Remember, in 2e you don't even need the blood of a Nosferatu to learn Nightmare - if your sire knows it, then you can learn it from them. Even the vitae you need to drink can come from anyone that knows the Signature Discipline. And that's not touching on the number of bloodlines with signature disciplines for a fourth. The big "secret disciplines" in Requiem are the covenant magics. Sharing Oaths, Laws, Coils, Cruac or Theban with outsiders carries still penalties and taboos. But Requiem has never restricted signature disciplines in any way beyond what's in the 2e core.

      Another possibility is that you might have been feeling some aversion to the idea of casually swapping vitae among players - willing blood bonds and addiction seems crazy to a number of players. Could this be it?

      As for me and my games? I have never once had an time with it being heavily restricted. Clans, even when organized, simply don't have the power to regulate Disciplines in that manner.

          The way I run things, Disciplines are a meta-game concept. The characters don't conceive of ten discrete powers each with five levels of mastery, and the idea of "swapping signature disciplines" wouldn't really occur to most vampires in my games. There is, however, a certain awareness that Vitae is power, and the Vitae of different Clans makes them powerful in different ways. The wild blood of the Gangrel makes them close to their Beasts and allows them to commune with and even take the form of animals. The powerful blood of the Ventrue makes their gaze hypnotic and forces man and beast alike to obey their commands. The horrid blood of the Nosferatu makes them easy to miss and terrible to behold. Etc. A vampire of one Clan may desire the power of another Clan's blood, but they may or may not consider the risk of blood bond worth the power. The kind of mutual trust required to knowingly and willingly exchange Vitae is rare among vampires. More often, powers of the blood might be discovered accidentally after being unknowingly or unwillingly blood bound, stolen through Diablerie, or taken as payment for a service rendered to a lesser vampire who's blood you don't believe potent enough to bind you. But I don't think blood sharing within a coterie is all that unheard of. Certainly something I would allow PCs to do.

                  It isn't very common in my games because the players tended to view it as rather pointless to even bother distinguishing between clans if everyone's power spread is uniform and clans leadership are just fine with clan mates freely trading clan signatures as minor favors. That doesn't mean you didn't encounter a Nosferatu with a rank or two in Auspex or a Gangrel with Majesty but it would be extremely rare to see a Mekhet with Dominate at level 5 or a Daeva turning into mist. Personally I don't see clans particularly wanting their most powerful abilities to be in the hands of outsiders so they would discourage the creation of or outright eliminate such aberrations publicly advertising their repertoire frequently based on trumped up accusations like diablerie.
