    Ancient Clans Beyond The Roman Camarilla (Also Homebrew)

    02-24-2024, 02:02 AM
    Besides the Grettir, Akhud and Baali in Requiem For Rome 2e. Are there other clans beyond the five that once existed until they became seemingly extinct in other parts of the globe? Was wondering if there was once clans in Asia that were the Ventrue/Julii analogues but I guess the Daeva may already fill the niche even without a clan that fills the same purpose.

    My idea so far is that there was once a clan in India who are called the Asuras. They were like rivals to the Daeva who are also native to the area (made them rivals due to mythological reasons). Due to their seemingly demonic nature since I imagine they have protean amalgams that are similar to demon shintai from KOTE, the daeva may have rallied other clans such as nosferatu, mekhet and gangrel into a holy war against the clan. The asuras may have been driven extinct, but some kindred may have diablerized them resulting in bloodlines such as Amara Havana and Rakshasa (For the Amara Havana, one of their origin stories tell that they were the result of the mixing of Kali and the demon Raktabija could be interpreted that they were from Daeva who diablerized Asura during the holy war). Some of the Asuras may have survived and even joined the VII out of vengeance. The Asuras may also be related to the onis and may have even resulted in myths about them, but they were onis in one of the Dark Eras books but they can be explained as a non-embracing offshoot that separated from the Asuras before they evolved into kindred.

    So far my ideas for the Asuras is this.

    Nicknames: Demon Kings

    Disciplines: Protean, Animalism, Vigor. (Although may probably make it Protean, Majesty and Vigor to imply they are related to the Daeva thanks to a common ancestor)

    Bane: Maybe something similar to a Wraith The Oblivion shadow? I'm new to Requiem so I'm not good at the mechanics yet.

    I also have ideas for a Julii/Ventrue analogue in ancient China that may be the result of an apocryphal Taoist immortal cursed by the other immortals and the gods. But the Daeva could still fill the niche as they might use the myth of Nuwa to legitimize their role as rulers.

                                          Minor aside, but also bringing up some bloodlines of questionable origins:

                                          - Rakshasa, who in a certain version of their origins, began as hungry corpses uplifted into kindred condition by the civilizing/taming efforts of an undead brahmin.
                                          - Vedma, a bloodline of eastern european witches among whom some members claim to predate the Gangrel in lineage.
                                          - Annunaku, while better known as Gangrel landlords, according to some rumors, cross the lines into Ventrue and may predate not only the Invictus, but the Camarilla itself.
                                          - Gorgons, though more associated with the Mediterranean than Mesopotamia or the Middle East, also tell a similar tale of links to lost gods & monsters and predating covenants present and past.


                                          There are probably a number of others i forget, but 4 is a good start i guess. Will add some more if i remember anything.

                                              Had added the Cihuateteo to the list, but then while reading the wiki text realized that official materials never really adressed any possible relation between either bloodline or the witches from Wicked Dead.

                                              A link through service to their gods, with dead Cihuateteo spontaneously on the form closer to the deity they were more beholden to, the Jaguar (Gangrel) & the Whore (Daeva), blurring the lines between clan, bloodline and other, while based on a common mythology & fluff, was 100% me confusing homebrew from chronicles long past with actual canon, my bad.

                                              For an extra bonus, the chapter on Ars Speculorum in Mythologies would also mention in passing the possibility of Red Jack and Bloody Mary being newer forms of both deities, before either version of the Cihuateteo was a thing.


                                              Beside this "alternative" take on the Cihuateteo, i did sketch out a few other clans from mixing and matching from book bits and other stuff.

                                              An east asian clan, where i mixed Formosae, Galloi & Aswang (though more precisely an all-female "early edition" variant from the Antagonists bluebook) under a "beauty hiding atrocity" theme, based on Taiwan (because Formosa, duh ) and worshipping a cthonic version of Doumu in place of Cybele. Though i'll be the first to admit i was just improvising from Izanami more than anything.


                                              Another early play at making a clan was something of a weird revenge of mine over a player's insistence of having his Khabit's sire be an ancient harkening to the Roman Empire when my original intent was to keep the scale and timespan mostly within the low hundreds/age of sail, at least to start.

                                              "So he wants a sire from Rome? Good for him, because i'll make a Khaibit Victrix, betrayer of the Senex, doom of the Julii, out of her! "

                                              And well, from me playing with the Khaibit's 1e discipline set (as close to Daeva as Mekhet), redoing Victrix's backstory with some decidedly iberian twists and themes and some extras and deviations for the intervening centuries, would come to be the Numantine clan, as a sort of shadowy undercurrent moving between Mekhet, Daeva, Khaibit, Circle of Crone, Belial's Brood and VII perhaps, but not quite the face of either or how one would expect them.
