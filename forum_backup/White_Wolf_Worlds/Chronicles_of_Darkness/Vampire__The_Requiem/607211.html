    An Irish Tale

    07-29-2015, 07:23 PM
    I was telling the story of what my girlfriend would do if she actually got embraced, and her objections and affirmations grew into a proper discussion and desire to play that story. So, I'm indulging in a bad idea: The NWoDing of my life. :P

    I'm reaching out for some help though, as the whole idea is rather serendipitous and therefore, I'd rather not go off and make a whole world. Plus, the conversation was more of a 'what if' so even as storyteller, I don't want to control all the details. As such, I wish for the community to help out.

    Create characters, as fleshed out as you can. Favor the young over the old and if possible, link it to other characters in the trend itself. Oh and they don't need to be vampires. Humans are far more interesting, as she will be playing yourself and thus wants to see her powers trump human advantage. A mage or two would be extra fun. By way of setting, I propose the following: Given that tired stat of 50k:1, and the population being what it is, Dublin should be where all the vamps crowd around (14 or so), with Cork and such getting maybe a few, and every other county just the one. I like this, as we can play Ireland as very Carthian in Dublin (reflecting Republican Ireland) with some Ordo around the universities but have anywhere beyond the Pale owned by the Invictus (landlords), Lancea (Catholic Ireland) or Circle (Pagan). Lastly, weight in on what should happen to me. I know, 'conceited much' but she wants me in the story and I don't want to be her touchstone forever. She is a Daeva, so vamping and ghouling me (if she figures out the latter) are options but I could also be a mage or changeling. Ideas that result in me being cool but not Mary Sueing are best.

    Questions? Concerns?

    Oh and my story, so my judgement is final, just in case we have any disagreements that need ironing out or judgements that need to be made.

              Oh, well, that's one of the challenges with a character where the concept is "yourself' - what attributes above 2, if any, would one have? Any 1s?
              What are one's strong or weak suites in relation to skills? Not always simple to work outside a familiar model, but entertaining nonetheless.

              As you mentioned she'll be a Daeva, i'm guessing social or physical attributes are - or will become - strong spots for her soon enough.
              How does the embrace shapes her? Does the Favored Attribute dot go in Dexterity or Manipulation? What about her 3 discipline dots?

              A neonate with all clan disciplines at 1 can feel very different from one with Majesty 3 or another with Celerity 1 & Vigor 2 or vice-versa, what can have a strong effect in how she experiences and feels the first nights of her Requiem, Not even going into out-of-clan disciplines territory (improbable, but possible, specially if her sire was a diablerist, for example) yet, but think of Lestat almost entranced with his enhanced senses after becoming a vampire. The favored attribute, depending on how well it "sinergizes" with her disciplines and previous stats can or not further enhance this effect.

              No matter the distribution, accidental optimization or not, this kind of detail can be of considerable use in the story, as "just" going from 1-2 to 2-3 after the bite is a considerable degree of change. one's combinations (or lack of them) just make it starker. A matter of applying multiple changes to the person or one strict but incredibly dramatic change (and let's be honest any discipline at 3 is quite a ridiculously impressive thing on itself).

              Also, because not everything is flowers, there's the matter of adaptation to her new condition as a nocturnal undead predator.
              - Does she live by herself or with friends, lovers or relatives, people who might note (and look askance at) a sudden change of habits (like not breathing - that can be a very bothersome tell if one shares the room or bed with another person)?

              - The first draught of blood probably comes from her sire at the embrace (supposing it doesn't have a handy unconscious victim close by to "properly wean her out") but what about after that? Unless she finds a convenient nearby slaughterhouse or develops Animalism quickly, animal blood will be mostly inacessible to her due to kindred nature scaring most of them, making it a "too much work for too little good" situation when compared to the (comparatively) easier and far more satisfying temptation of engorging oneself into mortal blood. The longer it takes her to feed again, the greater the chance of losing control due to starvation at a wrong moment and attacking someone one would prefer not to.

              - How many nights before her first lone hunt?Will her first hunt be brutal & savage or deliberate & seductive? Will hunger or inexperience interfere? What if she gets sloppy, or accidentally comes across the hunter, another kindred - or just some random creep or weirdo in the process? Note this may serve to set up an accidental encounter with either of the two kindred you previously mentioned or the Sheriff or his agents (hounds, his ghouls, the hounds own ghouls, etc) and maybe using her as a tracker to catch her sire (what she can be very effective at, with Blood Simpathy & all).

              - Does she have a job or source of income? How does her nocturnal condition impact (or not) in lifestyle, study or work? Can her new capacities help in solving any issues, or not? Would she care to continue studying or working after becoming a vampire?

              Overall i would say, explore the basics - learning what powers works for her and what needs, urges & weakness her vampiric self possesses (does she have a normal reflection, blurry or none?Vampires are generated only by embrace or accidental making of revenants is possible?) can be used as the basis for a number of situations, tense, tragic or humorous, with what fits each definition depending on circunstances...

              An armed thug trying to rob you when a loved one is nearby can be tense or tragic but one trying to rob a hungry vampire just out her (failed) first hunt would be quite (darkly) humorous indeed. One memorable example with my players was when one of them, just out of a popular nightclub, was badly run over by a drunk driver in a busy street in front of the place and the poor PC, to avoid breaching the masquerade pretty badly, had to play dead until the ambulance came up and people put it in a black bag, (from where it called by cell for help in getting out of the hospital's morgue).
              The incident made the rounds amidst kindred society and while the coterie got out ok, thanks to exemplary discretion, some more important kindred had to hear some pretty harsh words about "bad location and sloppy placement of exits", with rumours, politicking, threats and much gnashing of teeth being done for nothing.
