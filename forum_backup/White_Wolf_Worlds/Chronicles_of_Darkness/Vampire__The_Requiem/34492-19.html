    287) Some strange blood-born parasite has been spotted in the domain. It takes over a human host, slowly beginning to control their actions as starts replacing more of their blood with itself. Eventually, a human is nothing more than a puppet of the parasite, completing its strange goals until its host is killed. Then it vacates its old body and hunts for a new home, leaving an exsanguinated corpse - and threatening the Masquerade. And now, some fool Ancilla had the great idea to try and drink from one of parasite's hosts. And then share their corrupted Vitae with their Ghouls.

    Malkydel: "And the Machine dictated; let there be adequate illumination."

        288) Well, shit's fucked! Belial's Brood decided to go full blown public with their activities. I guess when you perceive yourself as a literal demon, you don't care about mass panic and wanton destruction. Anyways, the Princes are unable to convincingly spin this off as a mundane incident. The masquerade is rapidly deteriorating and, in less than a month, the whole country will know that vampires exist. After that, the rest of the world is sure to follow. That's when the Kindred receive contact from the most unlikely allies; the government itself. Turns out they know all about vampire society, they have numerous organizations dedicated to keeping them in check. And now they're 'offering' a chance to work together and stamp out the Brood while there's still time.

        Now, vampire coteries and hunter cells must put aside their differences in order to stop the world from plunging into darkness. Assuming the national leaders don't nuke everybody back to the ice age!

                                            298) So the Ordo Dracul, you know those guys, right? Bunch of mad scientist vampires seeking to become the perfect being? Well, it turns out they're really big fans of Jojo's Bizarre Adventure and have used the energy contained in the ley lines to create a stone capable of not only eliminating a vampire's weaknesses completely, but also dramatically amplifying their supernatural powers by hundreds of times their normal capacity. Any vampire who utilizes the stone would become a perfect being with no discernible limits to their abilities. There's just one problem in that the stone was stolen before its power could be unleashed and nobody knows exactly who's responsible. The most likely culprits are the Mage cabals and some incredibly ballsy Hunter cells. But what if there's more at play here than the usual suspects? Investigations into the scene of the crime have turned up a strange piece of evidence left behind. A small discarded cog smeared in what seems to be human blood was found half buried in the center of the ley line's convergence point and it's giving off more energy than the Ordo Dracul have ever encountered before!

                                                299) Mute with Horror

                                                A reclusive Nosferatu ancilla contacts the coterie about setting up a network of clandestine media contacts. The vampire, a mute, has decided after many years to embrace their Curse (their unnerving taint is carried not through voice or aspect, but through writing) and wants to branch out a home horror ghost writing hobby into a respectable commercial venture.

                                                Characters will be responsible for introductions/establishing secure lines of communication to writers, publishers, and/or printers, and making sure there's no Masquerade leaks (or preparing an argument against an establishment worried about potential Masquerade leaks). They also have to deal with the fact that all communication (even sign language) is phrased in an extremely ominous and threatening manner, regardless of their employer's intent, so that sheepishly cheerful grin continues to come off as slasher-ey.

                                                            302: A Vintage Fit for Lords

                                                            A Crone ancilla, a local vintner of some renown, has not been seen in some time; their last sighting was on their mandragora plantation. The only clue to their disappearance so far are 1) any mortal sleeping on-site has weirdly vivid dreams, and 2) that many of the thorns on the rose trellis orchard-maze have begun to turn a tarnished silver.
