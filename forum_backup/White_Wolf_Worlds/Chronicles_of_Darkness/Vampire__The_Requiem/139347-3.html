    Originally posted by Charlaquin View Post

    Sure they do. A human's Vice only gives him one Willpower and his Virtue only gives him all Willpower. A vampire's Mask and Dirge each give her both. Each is like a Virtue/Vice set, and vampires get two of them. On top of that, vampires get Willpower for interacting with their Touchstones. Humans can get Willpower for interacting with True Friends, but that's a 3-dot Merit approximating a benefit every vampire gets for free, and can get more of for 1 Merit dot a pop.
    I was comparing more to other supernaturals, to be clear. Werewolves have the same allotment of double-acting Blood/Bone anchors and touchstones (they have more of them, even), and Mages, while they use Virtue/Vice, have so many different ways to regain willpower through magic it doesn't matter that they lack touchstones. Both still regain willpower from sleeping.

                    The whole "6 months of night and 6 months of day" thing is pretty overstated anyway. Granted, north of the Arctic Circle or south of the Antarctic Circle you'll get periods of 24 hours or more without the sun rising near the winter solstice and 24 hours or more without the sun setting near the summer solstice. But it's only in very close proximity to the poles that you get true 6 month days and 6 month nights. And there really arent any humans living that far north or south to feed on. Like, granted, you won't have to spend Vitae to wake up so the only thing you'll be spending it on during those 6 months is healing, Physical Intensity, and Disciplines. But what are you even doing there that whole time? 6 months active with no one but penguins to talk to? You'd go Draughur from the isolation. I could maybe see one ancient, deranged Gangrel shutting herself off from the world to go live as a polar bear for eternity, but talk about a boring game of Vampire the Requiem.
