                        Originally posted by Master Aquatosic View Post
                        How do vampires hunt in places and times with unlit nights? Do they sniff for blood or do they have low-light vision?
                        Both. Mechanically speaking, vampires suffer no penalties from lack of lighting short of complete darkness. In complete darkness, they take only -2 to rolls based on vision. This is described as their vision in dark conditions being superior to humans, as well as their other senses compensating for the reduced visual acuity.
