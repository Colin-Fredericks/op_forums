                    The reason to use word Flesh over Body or Person is very simple and has nothing to do with intention of destroying clothes. It's the fact that word "flesh" is very fleshy, very visceral, full of blood and meat and bone, while body, person are relatively neutral and lack the wet, twitching and pulsing aroma of "twisting flesh".

                                                The first power of Protean melds a vampire with material. The vampire no longer bodily exists which is why damage is caused not by digging out the vampire but damaging the structure of the patch of dirt or concrete foundation, etc.

                                                Mechanically, Protean works like Captain Marvel. Billy Batson says "SHAZAM!" and he disappears to be replaced with Captain Marvel, minus the bolt of lightning. Thematically, the change is more like a physical morphing from one form to another. This leads to two reasonable explanations regarding personal possessions in my opinion.

                                                Protean could not directly affect personal possessions so hand held objects may be dropped or clothing damaged. This would be annoying. I doubt it is the assumed position for this reason.

                                                The alternative is that Protean does affect objects worn that are meant to be worn and hand held objects held in the hand while the vampire is affected by Protean. When the power ends the affect of the personal possessions does as well and they too revert back. Sometimes they might temporarily disappear or clothing might rip but all is as it was when the power is over. It is magic.

                                                            Semantics is super important in these games. Like WHW said. it states flesh changes. Like I stated and Rathamus backed up it creates a slippery slope if we look at it as a vampire controlling materials. Protean is a very bestial power, natural and down to earth. Back in 2000 something when 1E was written protean specifically stated clothes. Now, with the current book protean has been completely revamped and 3/5 of them specifically state flesh. The 5th one says you become nothing more than smoke and hunger ... no longer does it state clothes. Besides what happens to your cellphone once it dematerializes into smoke??

                                                            Anyway that said....

                                                            What this thread has come up with is: Half think that clothes do not shift and gave actual evidence from the book to support their reasons. The other half seems to take on the stance, It is too inconvenient or DMmagic: Clothes shift.

                                                            So let's say this Question is answered with: It is entirely up to the Story Teller and how they interpret the power.
