                                                      Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                                                      I'm not saying its not better, just I'm not sure it is worth of 10 vitae while with 5 vitae you can hipotetically survive a nike bomb (Juggernaut), with 8 you can have all the stuff from force of nature and so on.
                                                      Ten Vitae for an explosive effect covering a fairly large "small" area for a scene seems about on-par with a more singular effect than Lord of the Land or Oubliette, either of which has a lower range of Vitae costs but comes with a fairly definite use condition besides "smear blood everywhere."

                                                      Also, the power doesn't simply generate the Swarm Tilt — individual animals don't all meet up together before arriving on the scene, nor do they necessarily glom into one big mass; a cat on its way to the alley full of blood is going to hit harder and stutter sideways from anyone trying to stop it. The pristine killbox is absolutely the main effect, but "very little is going to actually stop the animals from arriving" is worth remembering.

                                                      Also also, Vermin Flood's prerequisites are intermediate competence in three common Disciplines. Juggernaut's Gait takes five dots in Resilience and is functionally useless without Blood Potency on par with an elder, while Force of Nature takes four dots in two different Disciplines, one of which is clan-specific, and gives you comparatively little that you don't already have from the prerequisites besides the ability to change them faster. Considering this is explicitly a more potent version of a power that costs two Vitae and can theoretically take as much as a full night to kick in, "It happens pretty much immediately" would justify a good deal of the extra cost.
