    B&S 'Lashing Out' against Disciplines.

    12-23-2013, 11:03 PM
    Majesty1 (Awe) and Nightmare1 (Dread Presence) both have no activation roll, no cost, and state that the only way for Kindred to resist is by 'Lashing Out' and reference the Lashing Out page. However, that section does not at all comment upon using 'Lashing Out' in this fashion.

    So how precisely does this work? Do other Vampires have to spend a Willpower point (which you normally do to 'Lash Out') just to try and resist these Disciplines? And what are the mechanics for the Lashing Out in this situation. Is it a contested Lashing Out as normal, or do I just roll PowerAttribute+BP and if I get a success I resist the power?

                                          The point is that when you lash out, you also end Majesty. That doesn't automatically bring with it that Majesty is immediately obvious. If he's cooler than you under the effects of Majesty, then it'll feel right and make sense for him to be, too. That's part of it. Nothing Majesty does at the lower levels is impossible. It makes someone incredibly alluring, but it's possible for someone to be. It makes your words seem very sensable and poignant, but it's possible that they are. Any Vampire who suspects they are under Majesty may lash out, but it's just that: a suspicion. They might be wrong, and if they are, they've just lashed out with no reason whatsoever.

                                          Anyone who wants to last in the Danse better make sure that they realize possible consequences for lashing out at anyone they suspect of Discipline use may very well be dire. Any game I'm Storyteller has the player guessing if that Kindred I'm describing as a magnet of personality is using Majesty... or just a strong, alluring personality. They'd do well to make sure which it is before taking rash actions, and they know this.

                                          I can't claim 100% that this is the intended writing, but nothing I've said is contradicted by the rules, it makes sense from a lore perspective (as the Daeva really have never been described as being super-obvious and easy to throw out of Elysium whenever they use a little Awe) and it's a lot more interesting from a gameplay perspective too.

                                              Keep in mind, Awe just makes all of your actions cast in the best possible light. So even if you become skittish or bloodthirsty or whatever from losing the Predatory Aura contest, it has no impact on how everyone who WAS affected will perceive you. They'll find you charming and likable no matter what you do. Also, I would say Lashing Out when affected by Majesty is a player choice (or ST choice for NPCS), not a character choice. The character doesn't notice that they are being affected by the discipline because that's just how Majesty works. It affects the minds of everyone who sees you, and they just think it's totally natural to be so into you, cause you're just that awesome. The Beast, on the other hand, doesn't always appreciate being manipulated, and will sometimes fight back of its own accord. It doesn't mean the person is consciously aware, just that their inner monster is rejecting the mind-affecting power.
