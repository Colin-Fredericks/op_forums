                Ok, let's accept that they aren't human.

                However, they are less not human than Demons or Prometheans, in that they THOUGHT they were human for a long time. Same deal as far as I can tell with Beasts when they come out: they THOUGHT they were human, mostly (and couldn't put their finger quite on what was wrong with that idea) until they changed, and now they realize they were never human.

                BUT, and here's the important bit, up to that, their EXPERIENCE was as a HUMAN. They were experientally human, even if they were not technically, spiritually, scientifically, whatever human. That is what differs between Werewolves and Demons/Prometheans.
                Both are quite certainly Not Human.
                But Demons and Prometheans have also never been Experientially Human, while Werewolves have. Werewolves have gone through a period living as a human, in which for all intents and purposes they could have been human, but for the werewolf truth lying dormant in them. And that's what we're driving at here, is Experiental Humanity.

                Mummies are an interesting point here; they were definitely Human once, but they may not be Experientally Human anymore, as they may not have access to those experiences anymore.

                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                        Their experience don't matter squat. Right now they are metaphysically as qualified for embrace as either of the two never-human splats. You'd be as fruitful trying to embrace a potato.
                        They qualify just as much as the mage, changeling, and sin-eater do. Technically, none of them are considered human enough to be Embraced, due to the no-cross-template-splatting rule. However, there's no reason for werewolves to be grouped with the never-humans, since, mechanically, they were human right up until the First Change.

                        White room theorycraft time! Imagine a Mehket and a soon-to-be werewolf was sitting in a bar. The Mehket can get prophesy-like powers that could reveal that before the end of the night, the soon-to-be-wolf will become a full wolf and death rage, killing everyone in the room. The vampire decides to immediately embrace the werewolf potential before (s)he can turn.

                        Now, ignoring the entire cheesiness of the situation, this would theoretically work. There's nothing about a soon-to-be-changed werewolf that would prevent them from being Embraced. So, at this point, they pretty much are humans. I don't think you can really get around that. The potential to become a werewolf doesn't make you not a human in terms of the Embrace. At no point does a Promethean (pre-New Dawn anyways) or Demon qualify.



                        Moving forwards - if we have a ST that throws that no-turning-into-another-tempalte rule out the window (which this entire discussion hinges on), then there's nothing about a werewolf that would make him disqualify from being Embraced. Especially in light of all the fiction out there that touches on the subject of hybrids, or the vamp curse destroying the ability to shapeshift. Demons and Prometheans wouldn't work, demons wouldn't work. There's nothing about a wolf that suggests they should be grouped with the alien minded machines. No more than any other supernatural.
