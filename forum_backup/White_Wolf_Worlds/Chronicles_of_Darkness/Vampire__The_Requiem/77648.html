    Killing Kindred & Breaking Points

    02-23-2014, 10:47 AM
    Does killing Kindred or other supernatural creatures (werewolves, Geister, Prometheans, etc) count as "murder" for the purposes of breaking points on the Humanity chart, or does Humanity only apply to relating to humans? What about Kindred who are so degenerate that they bear at most a passing resemblance to humanity: Draugr, Stryx-possesed, etc?

      Killing kindred when you ARE a kindred is certainly something that would shake your sense of humanity. Killing something you might one day become would be as well.                       I don't see degeneracy as really having anything to do with it, anymore than killing a degenerate human. I'm pretty much with Saibot on this; otherwise whacking other Kindred should most certainly be a breaking point. These are the only people who can empathise and understand you and your state fully. They are what you could be, or what you could become. Going out of your way to whack your social circle who are in an extended way a mirror of yourself because you find them -horrible-... that should most certainly distance you from Humanity. Obviously it rocks your boat enough to take an extreme risky action.

                      Them being good or bad individuals may alter what level of Humanity the Breaking Point is, but I think it should remain one nonetheless. Kindred are individuals. People are individuals. Few in the world are saints, they're exceptions for a reason (and even some of those saints may not be all that great of folks...) going off and whacking them and validating it as not being murder is semantics bordering on sociopathy. It's not what a sane human consistently does, and even then it is a slippery slope. It's not considered acceptable in most societies without high levels of social ritual (and even then it's hotly contested) barring self-defense, and even then that runs into issues. How is doing that do your own kind and peers somehow less worse?
