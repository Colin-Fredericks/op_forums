    Originally posted by Ventrue Life View Post
    So from what I gathered, Resilience adds to your Health levels. But how about Vigor and Speed? The book doesn't say that Vigor should be used to calculate Speed, so I guess Speed isn't affected by Vigor?

    How about Merits that have a certain amount of Stamina as a prerequisite (for example the Relentless Merit, which requires 3 dots of Stamina)? Does Resilience count when checking to see if you meet the prerequisites for the Merit?
    Attribute dots gained through Disciplines counts as actual dots unless stated otherwise. Vigor increases Strength which increases Speed. Both Vigor and Resilience counts towards Strength and Stamina prerequisites on Merits.
    An example of when they don't apply is when Lashing Out with the Monstrous Aspect, which specifically states that Vigor does not apply to the Strength+Blood Potency dice pool.

                    Originally posted by Ventrue Life View Post
                    If a vampire diablerizes another vampire... who happens to be the host of a strix... what happens?
                    Simply put, that doesn't happen.

                    Your target needs to be torpid to be subjected to diablerie and the Strix a) aren't subject to torpor, b) can't possess a vampire who isn't already torpid themselves and c) aren't ejected from a body unless it's taken enough damage to destroy the host, which happens to be the threshold for diablerie to begin in the first place.

                    In the event that it somehow does happen, the endless variety of weird shit the Owls get up to may change the answer, but in general the process suggests that your last guess is the closest probable outcome.
