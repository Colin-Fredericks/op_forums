                                    Originally posted by Father Enoch View Post
                                    The Devotion "Conditioning" (2E Requiem, Pg 143) is written to only use one Discipline Dominate. Aren't Devotions supposed to be a combination of Disciplines?

                                    Is there a required Discipline not mentioned? And if there isn't, why is Conditioning listed as a Devotion?
                                    Not really. Disciplines are the cemented (or metaphysically more mainstream at the moment) forms of the expressions of the Beast's(/Curse's/Blood's/etc.) power, practiced and cultivated enough that it becomes a norm. Devotions are mutagens and strange iterations of viral curses, cancerous memetics, and fractally warped legacies-deviations from that now established strain of power which denote things that were, things to come, or things sideways to....well, not the sun.

                                    The most common way for such to develop and otherwise express is "This power is the unholy love child of this Discipline and that Discipline", but any given growing/rotting tendril of the Beast's/Curse's/Blood's power has enough muscle and sinew to writhe and wriggle in different ways. All it takes is someone looking at their inner eye* expressed by Auspex and wondering "Okay, but what if I rip it out and leave it on the bannister, can I still see what's happening underneath the skin of reality at that point?" to realize what might be done.

                                    As an idea, it's a personal microcosm of the dread barbarians at the gate leave Requiem Kindred-not so much that they invade, so much as they infect, change, and become part of the family/self.

                                                            Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                                                            By the way by boosting your strength with blood you can easy use a car as a weapon. Let's consider strength 4 Vigor 3, with 2 vitae it's totally feasible.
                                                            Physical Intensity only adds to rolls, not Traits. More concretely, vampires whose Blood Potency used to be high enough to support baseline Traits above 5 still benefit from those Traits.
