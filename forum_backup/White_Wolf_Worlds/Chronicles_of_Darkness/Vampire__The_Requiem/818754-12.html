                                                            Originally posted by Gorin View Post
                                                            Hello VtR community!
                                                            I've got a simple question, sorry if it was already answered in some other topic (i've googled hard before asking )
                                                            The question is regarding the Charmed Condition wording:
                                                            It's description consists of two paragraphs. The first one is pretty clear and states that vampire can add his Majesty dots to any Manipulation rolls against the victim.
                                                            The second one explains how the victim should act when suffering the Charmed Condition effects. The question is does the second paragraph have anything to do with game mechanics? Does the vampire require to roll the dice or it is assumed that the victim acts literally like "If he asks, you’ll do favors for him like he was one of your best friends" with no game mechanics like social skills or social maneuvering rolls needed?
                                                            It helps to keep in mind that the majority of the developers for the CofD/nWoD game lines fall into the "there is no fluff/crunch division" camp of game design. The description of how a character under the Charmed Condition should act is just as much a rule as the part about adding Majesty dots to Manipulation rolls. It doesn't directly interact with any subsystems, but it is a game rule, just one that pertains to roleplaying.The player (or the storyteller if it's an NPC) should roleplay as though the vampire was the character's close friend, for whom they wouldn't be likely to refuse requests for minor favors.
