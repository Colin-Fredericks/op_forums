    [Bloodline] Avakyr: The Muses

    03-20-2016, 07:39 PM
    I had cried my last bloody tear. Shed my last drop of the Blood, accepted my fate. I had tried, and failed. Transcendence. Rebirth. Golconda. Whatever you want to call it. I faced the test and failed, and in my despair awaited the sun's rise upon the rooftop. Let it come. I would see the sunrise one last time. I would have my transformation, oh yes. Chemical and spiritual. Damnation reaching into my breast and cracking me apart, scattering me into ashes and flame. A fitting end at the dawn of my two hundredth birthday.

    Some power yet stayed the sun's hand over the horizon. Dark clouds gathered overhead. I knew better than to even expect a simple suicide by sun. What more could I have done? As I cursed my lack of matches to kickstart the pyre myself, red-stained tears began to fall around me. I felt a presence then, powerful, terrifying, at once cold and yet sending a white-hot sensation through my chest. Had the sun finally risen? Had I finally met my Final Death? The taste of cold blood graced my lips. I lifted my head to the sky to scream, to rip my own throat out in defiance of a world that would not let me reject this curse, and I saw her. Descending from what seemed to be the heart of the black roil on great wings of black. Even now I struggle to describe her, though that sight has forever burned into my mind. Her skin of purest alabaster, her hair as pale blonde as the cold dawn sky, her lips the perfect red of an unbeating heart. Clad in the raiment of the storm itself, she was perfection, beauty incarnate. An angel without a pulse.

    As I looked upon this visage and trembled, I wondered if the dawn had finally come for me after all. Had it sent an angel to claim me, shown mercy upon me for all my penance? A vengeful God sending down an angel of death to tear my broken soul asunder? I could not long meet her pearl-eyed gaze, nor could my throat, parched of blood, form the words to the questions I needed to ask. When she spoke to me, I listened, and never have forgotten her words.

    "A curse that blinds us
    A curse that breaks us
    A curse that binds us
    A curse that wakes us
    To hunger, to flood
    To Beast and Blood"

    These are the words once spoken to me as I awaited the dawn. The Suspire ran red with my tears. Failure left me without will to endure. And in my darkest hour, she finished her words:

    "Upon whose cheeks red streaks run
    In whose soul would curse be undone
    'Tis not your time to meet the Sun
    Long you toil before break of dawn
    A ray of hope when all is gone

    You who have failed must guide others. It is not your place to cast yourself into ash and exile. For I will show you the path that you must tread, where angels fear to go, where the Beast quavers. Realms of blood and smoke where the darkness is older than our sun. You will know pain. You will burn with hunger. Yet you will persevere. You know what terrible toll damnation demands of the soul. You, who have felt so keenly this pain, would you now leave others to suffer it without guidance?"

    "No," I whispered, because I knew that if there was to remain any hope for me, for any of us, I must foster it in others. A muse must remain true.

    "Then I fly to meet the sun, as my own angel did before me. I cannot tell you how many years you will spend in the cold and dark. It will be a time of pain and anger, but with it comes a thing so often stolen in dead of night: hope."

    And she lifted her raven wings to the sky, even as the sun broke through the clouds. A single searing shaft of golden light fell upon me, pierced me then, and I knew no more until I awoke just as dusk began to fall. Alone, in my haven. The pain of that one ray of light still burned within my breast, terrible but oh so freeing. It was deliverance by the thrust of a red-hot sword, truth and freedom passing as a stake into my heart. A scar no Blood can heal, one I bore proudly that I might remind myself of who I am when I needed it most.

    Now it is your turn to bear this scar, and for me to join my sisters in the Sun. One day you will do the same, but not before your trials by blood and fire. You know what it's like to go through this alone. Now you will show others they don't have to. The night balances the day; you are the balance between Humanity and the inhuman.

    Background: The Avakyr record no verifiable history of their bloodline. They seem to be ancient, but their stories are shrouded in a personal origin, the battle with Damnation. For perhaps as long as some vampires have been Damned, they have sought ways to free themselves of that burden. Some wish to reclaim their mortality, or transcend the limitations of the Blood to become truly godlike. Others would quell the Beast forever and live on eternally free of its shackles. No vampire has mastered the Beast, not completely—or at least not in any way that survive as more than apocryphal tales and misguided myths. This doesn't stop some from trying. And it doesn't explain the angels.

    If legend is true, they came to the first vampire who failed her Suspire, a mythical quest on the road to Golconda—a fabled state of being seldom mentioned in modern nights. It is this state in which a vampire is said to master the Beast forever and never fear its debased urges. Most Kindred think the idea a mere fairy tale, at best, a useless salve for the souls of those too weak to endure the Danse. Others find it intriguing, if only in its persistence. Some covenants, like the Ordo Dracul and the Lancea et Sanctum, find the very idea repugnant, a slap in the face to those who view themselves as the ultimate predators. For young Kindred, and the jaded elders who might desperately seek a new hope as they cling to the last bloody shreds of their Humanity, the idea of "something better" finds a certain timeless appeal.

    The Avakyr first appeared to a vampire that had failed the Suspire. This seems to be the way they pass on the bloodline. Any clan, it seems, can join the Avakyr, but the requirement is a world-shattering breaking point in their unlives. Upon wings of marble white or raven black the angels appear to offer a choice. Stave off despair and light way for another soul in another life, or give up and let Damnation win. Those who succeed in passing on their mission are said to join the others in the Sun, vanishing without a trace into the dawn. A few more cynical vampires think that perhaps the Avakyr has duped another disciple into carrying the burden for a while, and go to enjoy a centuries-long vacation. Either way, the Avus does not stay to show her ward the proper path. Beyond the initial guidance, this is something every Avakyr must figure out for herself.

    As a result of their unusual genesis, Avakyr can come from any clan and any background. Their sole unifying factor is a spiritual low point that sends them into the most soul-crushing depths. Failing the Suspire, killing or Embracing a mortal loved one in the throes of hunger, or suffering the sanity-eroding terrors of Strix possession might all count. Then, as the vampire lies broken and bleeding from her wounded soul, the Muse appears. The Muse, an angelic vampire, appears only when the suffering Kindred has reached her utter end. She offers the choice that will define the rest of a vampire's unlife. Those who choose to rise again become Avakyr, inducted on the spot by the Muse.

    The Becoming: The Avakyr are born of those strong enough to overcome their own moment of greatest weakness. Muses seem able to discern between those who will fail and the ones who can piece themselves back together. Recruits all have a keen eye for qualities in others and an introspective mind. Most have a high Presence rating and many dots of Social Skills, although some take a more personal, understated approach to guiding others. Bloodline members rarely hold significant stations in a domain, though some serve as spiritual advisers to the Prince and other Kindred. In such domains, few vampires openly cross the Muse, for her guidance in spiritual matters affords her a sort of monk-like status among the Damned. Others may seek her out for her wisdom, or to combat the darkest of threats to Humanity.

    Domains with a Muse (or, rarer still, several) tend to cling more tightly to certain Traditions. Even the most jaded elder thinks twice about breaking certain taboos. Beware the angels who cry bloody tears, they say, for they are ever vigilant, and if they should turn their gaze from you, then truly you have no hope left. And if a Muse should ever abandon her domain without inducting a replacement, depravity and dissolution can only follow.

    Interactions with the Danse: A Muse is always seeking to better herself by providing a beacon for those around her. They do not crave much temporal power, but instead seek the sort of detachment from such that only comes from staring at one's own soul laid bare. Her voice in the Danse Macabre is always one of reason, preaching restraint, balance, in order that the herd and the hunters grow stronger—and neither becomes too Damned to rise up. They tend to eschew membership in any one covenant, maintaining relations with multiple factions. When they do join a covenant, it is because the Muse feels their gifts are most needed therein, or the region is too destabilized to foster spiritual growth, so she must help bring chaos to heel. The Ordo Dracul rarely, if ever, invite an Avakyr into their ranks, while certain sects of the Lancea et Sanctum happily do so ... and others find their very existence a blasphemy upon the covenant's dogma. Carthians are perhaps most likely to welcome a Muse outside of the Circle of the Crone, who see the winged vampires as yet another facet of the Goddess.

    Nickname: Muses

    Clan: Any (most often Daeva)

    Bloodline Bane: Avakyr must always tread a careful line between Blood and balance. They have met the gaze of the abyss and chose not to look away, but instead show others the path. She suffers a -1 penalty per dot of Humanity below 8 on all Social rolls made with vampires and mortals, as well as any rolls to activate Avakyr Devotions. Additionally, if her Humanity ever dips below 7, she suffers both her clan bane and a general bane for each dot below 7. She loses access to her Devotions and benefits from her Touchstones until she restores her Humanity. Note that, unlike most Kindred, Avakyr can attempt to atone for their sins and overcome banes gained from failing Humanity.

    Bloodline Disciplines: Majesty, Protean

    Sanitatum
    The signature powers of the Avakyr are strange, healing the physical wounds of others to soothing the damaged soul. Perhaps their most famous power is the ability to spread great wings from their shoulders and fly through the night. These powers have as many names as the feathers in an angel's wing, but the most common (if such a thing can ever truly be common, even for vampires) are usually referred to as Sanitatum. A few examples of such powers are listed below.

    Mend the Broken Flesh
    (Majesty •, Protean •, Resilience ••)

    This power, seemingly benevolent, allows the Muse to heal with a touch. She infuses the healing properties of the Blood into her target, restoring their wounded flesh as easily as she does her own. This does carry risks, however, for consuming the Blood is never without its price.
    Cost: 1 Vitae (or more)
    Dice Pool: Presence + Medicine + Resilience
    Action: Instant
    The character touches a target and spends her own Vitae to heal the target's wounds. She spends 1 Vitae and rolls Presence + Medicine + Resilience, healing 2 bashing per success or 1 lethal damage per success. This follows all the normal rules for healing, as well as the risks of blood bond as though the target had drunk the vampire's blood. This power may revive torpid vampires. Aggravated damage may be healed, as well, but requires 5 Vitae and 1 Willpower per point healed.

    This Devotion costs 2 Experiences to learn.

    Purify the Tainted Blood
    (Majesty •, Resilience ••)

    A vampire may purge herself of illness with ease. With effort, she may apply this ability to others, her Vitae consuming and healing the target of disease or poison. Certain blood-destroying toxins may be immune to this effect, at the Storyteller's discretion. 1 Vitae is usually enough, though this counts as the victim consuming it, with all the risks and benefits.
    Cost: 1 Vitae
    Dice Pool: none
    Action: none

    This Devotion costs 1 Experience to learn.

    Salve the Wounded Soul
    (Majesty •••••, Resilience •••)

    One of the most mysterious powers of these angelic Kindred is the ability to soothe the wounds within. This power takes two forms: the Muse may provide inspiration to help a vampire in her struggle against the Beast. By spending a scene in contemplation and reflection with the Avakyr, a character gains a Beat, which may only be used toward Experience costs to increase Humanity. Second, establishing a relationship with the healing presence of the Avakyr provides a buffer against the worst losses of the Danse. Anyone suffering a second-stage Vinculum toward the Muse benefits as though she was a Touchstone, for as long as the blood bond persists. Anyone under the spell of a third-stage Vinculum acts as though she has multiple Touchstones every time she must make a detachment roll. This benefit lasts as long as the vampire drinks from the Muse at least once a month.

    Avakyr must beware this use of their power, as abusing such bonds is both risky to Humanity and cause for other Kindred to fear her. Few partake in the full benefits offered, even if they study with the Avakyr, but those who find their souls too damaged by the actions of the Beast may see little option.

    This Devotion costs 5 Experiences to learn.

    Spread the Raven Wings
    (Majesty ••••, Protean ••)

    It is from this power that the angels earn their name. They may manifest great feathered wings (in some cases, more leathery, bat-like wings) or white, red, or black, and soar aloft. This power is awe-inspiring but also threatening to the Masquerade, so most Avakyr reveal their wings only among the Kindred. By activating the power, she may fly at up to her full Speed (which may be further enhanced by Celerity or Vigor). While this power is active, Awe is in its full effect. If the character possesses Majesty 5, she may activate Idol for 1 Vitae instead of its usual cost while this power is active. Additionally, she adds any bonus to Social rolls from high Humanity to all Social Skill rolls, not just those made with humans, while any vampires contesting her awesome presence subtract their penalties for low Humanity, if applicable.

    Cost: 1 Vitae
    Action: Instant
    Dice Pool: None

    This Devotion costs 4 Experiences to learn.

                          I absolutely love this bloodline. It's just so goddamn perfect.

                          Originally posted by Claire Redfield View Post
                          I definitely need to give Raven Wings some more oomph. I'm thinking on what. One idea I originally had was just adding Protean to Majesty rolls, but that seems too strong/boring anyway. Something that really screams "alabaster angel vampire."
                          It doesn't break the Masquerade.

                          For vampires, it doesn't need much, it already gains the social bonus from High Humanity, as well as Awe and Idol. I say give it something that breaks the rules of the setting, a little bit.

                          So it doesn't break the Masquerade. Observers whose Resolve is lower than the Avakyr's Presence automatically ignore her. To them, she isn't there. Those that match or have a higher Resolve than the Avakyr's Presence must still suceed at a Resolve + Composure roll in order to actually see her for what she is. That said, mortals who have ignored the Avakyr but subconsciously witnessed her are guaranteed to dream about her within the following week. For the next month, whenever one of those mortals passes by a statue or depiction of an angel, they will stop and stare, feeling a vague sense of wistful awe.

                          This allows the Avakyr to reach those that need her regardless of where they are.

                          I'd also suggest a Rain of Blood Devotion that acts as a sort of "calm emotions" effect to those touched by the blood.
