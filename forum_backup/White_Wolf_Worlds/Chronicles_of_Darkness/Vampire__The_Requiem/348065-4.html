        Originally posted by Unahim View Post
        Pretty much all of the worry about a Mekhet finding out everything about everyone is solved by the fact that the visions are cryptic in nature.
        Another irreconcilable difference in our interpretations, I suppose. The visions are cryptic because that makes it cooler and more flavorful, not because the player shouldn't be able to tell what the answer actually is. That's why it says the answer should include "the imagery conjured by the Beast", not that it should only be imagery. You give misleading information (like the knife fighting of friendship) for dramatic failures, not because you felt like messing with your players.

        Originally posted by Unahim View Post
        What exactly do you think "Does this person want to betray the Prince?" is going to look like in a cryptic vision?
        A sinister smile for yes? A vision of the target kneeling in submission for no? A dagger in their hand for yes? A feeling of fear and a whimper for no? Should I go on?

        Originally posted by Unahim View Post
        If you're trying to infiltrate a place and some guy asks you "Password?" picking the password from his mind will work, asking with Auspex 2 will not.
        Why not? It's a secret. Use Uncanny Perception, ask "what's the password?" and the ST gets to choose between a creepy voice whispering the answer in your ear or bloody letters spelling it out on the wall or whatever else they can imagine.

        Originally posted by Unahim View Post

        So what? You can help them or sabotage them or play 20 questions until you find out about some sort of proof. Or just bash their head in because you're the Sheriff and you don't answer to anyone except the Prince. Or develop a reputation for being always right, until you saying something like that publicly will instill enough doubt in everyone's mind that you've still hurt them politically. Or use any other of the infinite possibilities created by the fact that you know everything about everyone's schemes.

        On top of that, you can inflict conditions on people.
        Why, yes. I did say it made half of Auspex 4 almost pointless. Remember: from my point of view, Auspex 2 has some important limitations, and the memory viewing part of Auspex 4 is the moment you do away with those limitations. Compared to that, Conditions are less impressive.

        Originally posted by Unahim View Post
        I give a very specific reason why they could be that way, which is not dependant on chance or randomness at all, but ease of writing and word count.
        You... didn't? You said "the examples there are a far smaller grasp of what is possible than is the case for Beast's Hackles" and "its examples are thus not representative of the full scope of the ability", but unless I've recently become blind, you didn't talk about why. Also, really? Ease of writing? Word count? They could have removed either the "right now" in the question about fear or the "here" in the question about hate. That way, there would have been one question concerned with who is immediately present, and one question with no such limit. There, done. People like me wouldn't be under the impression that this is meant to be a limitation on the whole dot of Auspex.
