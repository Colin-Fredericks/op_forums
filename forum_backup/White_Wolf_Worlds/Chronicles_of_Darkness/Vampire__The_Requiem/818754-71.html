You’re not obviously hypnotized — you’re a bit quiet and reserved compared to normal, but nothing out of the ordinary. Her control isn’t obvious; she just asks him to do something and her victim acquiesces. He could unlock a door, pass her a gun — or forget ever meeting her. Some cruel vampires mesmerize passers-by just to take the rap for their crimes.

                                                So I've been curious about the "Extra Sense" of Protean. 360 degree vision with whatever animal sense you can justify. Unlike every other aspect written, this one doesn't have any discrete mechanics written for it. It's been suggested that you can't be surprised, or that you can see invisible entities if they're touching the ground.
                                                It's weird but also cool, problem is without any irrefutable mechanics written it falls on the storyteller's interpretation (which may vary a lot from ST to ST) what mechanical benefits this provides.

                                                So what would be reasonable advantages to expect when using this aspect? If we amp it to Protean 4 and essentially "use all the senses" what then?

                                                    My opinion: The most important mechanic in Chronicles of Darkness (and any traditional RPG for that matter) is that every action must be processed through a human brain, which determines if the action is possible, and if it has reasonable enough chances of both success and failure and sufficient consequences for failure as to require a random number generator (read: dice) to determine the outcome, and what system is most appropriate for utilizing the random number generator. Therefore, in my opinion, "you have 360 degree vision" is a mechanical advantage all by itself, just like "you have a prehensile tail" or "you can see in the dark." It affects the decision making process of the brain that is running the system, and will affect what outcomes that brain will determine are possible or impossible and how best to resolve uncertain outcomes.
