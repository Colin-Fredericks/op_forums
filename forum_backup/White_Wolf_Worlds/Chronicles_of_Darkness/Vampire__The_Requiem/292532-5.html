                                                Any mechanic that rapidly expands the scope of what someone can do in relation to "normal" options with little to no cost will almost always end poorly, unless the game is designed to handle that. You can say that Vampire Disciplines are by and large more powerful than anything they can buy through this coil, but that's exactly the reasoning that created the Eclipse problem. You'll run into hedge cases where the merits are better and/or cheaper than the discipline alternatives. Yes an ST can exorcise "common sense," but that assumes the ST is more familiar with the game mechanics than their player and has that kind of foresight. Not every ST will catch every hedge case, nor should they be expected to.

                                                Narratively there's a bit of dissonance as well. The mechanics encourage the vampire to expand different psychic abilities rather than focus on "reclaiming" one or two and how that impacts their Requiem. This works for a hedge mage, but less so for a psychic. Admittedly, this can be fixed with a slight adjustment to the fluff.

                                                That said, the coil isn't really that bad. Vampire is a game framed in a way where this sort of thing can be kept under control. In contrast, Grafting the Unholy Flesh is just a really, really bad idea. It allows a rapid expansion of power with little to no oversight or cost. Yes, there is the fluff cost of having to actually go about the grafting process but even taking that into consideration, it still drastically the rate of power growth. Its also somewhat vague on what counts as "one power." If you take a Vampire's Eyes to get their Auspex, and they have Auspex 5... do you get all 5 dots (with "Auspex" being the power) or do you get only a single level?

                                                What's a shame is I do actually like the fluff and the potential stories opened up by Grafting the Unholy Flesh being used responsibly. I just know it won't be used responsibly.
