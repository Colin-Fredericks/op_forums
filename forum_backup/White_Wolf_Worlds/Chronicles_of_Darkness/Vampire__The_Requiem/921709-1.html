                My suggestion for you would be to try and visualize how you would teach a power if you knew it. That's the method I used and was emulated by others in my group. Occasionally one could spike and develop a power out of sequence but you couldn't ever rely on it until you purchased it and any of the attendant ranks.

                One of my characters wanted to learn Protean (first edition version) after witnessing a kindred transform into a veritable nest of fleas and escape. After some horse trading, her sire managed to get a Gangrel Dragon teacher with a reputation for "unorthodox" teaching methods, agree to teach her Protean to mastery level. (Which is in character terminology for 5 ranks in a discipline.) At their first meeting, my character learned to channel her Beast's reaction as bluffing aggression to hide fear and repress the flight response. (Easy enough since they had never met before and the power had been paid for in xp.) In their first formal lesson she had my character dig a hole 6 ft. deep, then about an hour before sunrise, she shoved my character in the hole and told her to find a way out before sunrise by merging with the dirt or be stuck outside during the day under loosely packed dirt then proceeded to bury my character. My character temporarily manifested the protean claws that evening trying to dig herself out but didn't meld in the soil. Another session, she encased my character in a box with quick drying cement. Then threw the box into the lake. She triggered the animal transformation by setting a pack of hunting dogs on her and informing my character she had to escape them using no powers except protean before they tree'd her and melding into something counted as being tree'd.
                Mist form just had her locked in a steam room that kept increasing in temperature and she had to escape through grated vents.

                                The two stances aren't quite opposites.

                                You do need to have an inherant ability. Thankfully, though, all Disciplines are growths of inherant vampire abilities: Majesty, Nightmare, Dominate and Obfuscate are all variations of controlling one's Predator's Aura (Dominating, Beastly, Seductive, and negating). The four physicals relate to a healing and blood buffing to push your body further. Auspex reflects hightened vampire senses taken to an extreme, and Animalism reflects the corruptive nature of the Curse to spread. The five signature Disciplines do need a small boost as well to learn them in the form of taking a bit of vitae from someone that knows it.

                                However, like just about any other talent you might have, you need to train it. Natural ability only goes so far without practice and training. Sure, you have to practice with mastering your Beast and Lashing Out and similar, but that's still just practice using a mystical force rather than mundane talent.

                                    A simple way to avoid entire sessions devolving into a bad remake of Rocky is to not run the training sessions during game time for the main storyline. They can be done as solo sessions or side quests. Or simply narrate something to that effect occurs in down time. One of the advantages of using NPC teachers is that the ST can set their availability and decide when they have other obligations to address instead of teaching. (So players get back to the main story.) If they're training in a given discipline but haven't yet paid for it, you're free to situationally allow or disallow the player to use the power in question according to the needs of the story.
