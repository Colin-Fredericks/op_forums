                                                Originally posted by The Storyteller View Post
                                                In real life, the disgruntled underling who was looked over for promotion can't mind control the incompetent guy above him, or rip his face off, or invisibly ruin his life, or tons of other things supernatural powers can do.
                                                They can't even do it in Vampire. Not every vampire is a cunning mastermind. Mind control isn't always guaranteed, and ripping faces off certainly isn't. And, more importantly, not everyone wants a title.

                                                They totally exist. But in the real world, the lower orders of talented people have no outlet. Being really smart in the real world doesn't give you mind control or invisibility. Being a great salesman doesn't let you read minds or create illusions.
                                                And being a master mind controller doesn't mean you would be a good Prince, or even want to be one. A great salesman in life might want to continue being a great salesman in undeath, and not use their abilities to promote the Prince as a harpy. Because, again, they might be diametrically opposed to one another.

                                                Super powers change everything.
                                                Some of the more in-depth posts responding to atamajakki's question have pointed out that super powers don't change everything.

                                                You have more tools at your disposal, but that doesn't mean you'll be a great ruler. One vampire might be able to kill a dozen others without taking a shot, but he might be a horrible investigator, or simply not want the job. Then there's the subfactions, who might have to take what they can get. And blood bonding, which a superior can use to keep people beneath them in line.

                                                One of the best examples of this in Requiem's vast setting, to me, is Billy Vines. He made a deal with a demon and can, without his vampiric might dimmed at all, walk freely in the sun. He has ~12 hours more to work with than every other vampire around, can use his powers to walk into the home of an enemy, or someone who outranks him, and kill them. But he doesn't have the ability to leverage that into power. If he acts too much during the day, other Kindred will catch on, and he'll find himself destroyed for the danger he risks. And where would he start? There's no guarantee killing anyone would get him anything. He's still no more charismatic than he was, no more intelligent or witty. He has another tool at his disposal and can see no way to use it.

                                                Yes, we as people who have the benefit of knowing the rulebooks and how things work can. But Billy is not an out-of-world character who has the benefit of being able to look at it from our angle.
