            It's an Extended action so the tracker only gets a number of rolls equal to their Wits + Resolve total (unless they have the Patient merit then its +2 to that total). So no the tracker doesn't get an infinite number of rolls.

            That depends on how you want to run tracking. If the Obfuscate user keeps moving, the tracker keeps rolling since the Obfuscate user keeps moving the goal post and resetting the tracking attempt environment changes.
            I normally run tracking with the one being tracked stating when they stop moving then inform the tracker of relevant modifiers and having them roll. The one being tracked doesn't get to move last second to avoid being found. The tracker doesn't get to find a fresh new trail to follow and keep rolling.

            The tracker can technically do either or. They can attempt a penalized physical attack if they want but if they were smart they'd have brought a can of paint or bucket of confetti to throw in the area without penalty instead. If he has Auspex he can ask a relevant question and provoke a Clash of Wills. Or he can Lash out with the Obfuscate penalty (and bonuses, if any) and try to shatter all the Obfuscate effects they have running currently.
