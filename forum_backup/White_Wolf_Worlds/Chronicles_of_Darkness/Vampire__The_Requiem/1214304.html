    Fan Bloodline: Penanggalan

    05-02-2018, 04:46 PM
    So, I'm a huge fan of the Penanggalan, Mananggalan, Rokurokubi and other Asian vampires and while the write up the Penanggalan got in Night Horrors: Wicked Dead was pretty good, I wanted to try my hand at coming up with a playable bloodline for playing flesh-warping Asian vampires with a pleathora of freaky abilities. This is my first attempt at writing up my own bloodline and I'm interested in hearing critique on what I've written up. I'm a little concerned about the bloodline bane and if the bloodline gift is balanced. Let me know what you think!

    Penanggalan
    Nicknames: Menanggalan (Philippines), Krasue (Thailand), Rokurokubi (Japan), Freaks (Derogatory)
    Parent Clan: Nosferatu
    Disciplines: Obfuscate, Nightmare, Protean, Vigor
    The Penangalan are one of the most ancient bloodlines of Asia, tracing their lineage back to the ancient Kingdom of Khemer. They claim descent from a vain noblewoman who struck a deal with a spirit in the form of a black owl for eternal beauty and power over her flesh. They say once she was discovered for her cursed nature, she was sentenced to death by burning, but as the flames began to consume her body, her head detached from her body and flew into the night. From their ancient homeland on the Malay Peninsula, the bloodline has spread throughout Asia, forming blocs of influence in Thailand, the Philippines, and Japan in particular.
    In modern nights, the Penangalan stalk the edges of Kindred society, feared even by other Kindred for their terrifying and unnatural gifts to warp dead flesh. Their Requiems are sticky, bloody things, caked in viscera. The Penangalan embrace their monstrous nature and delight in the hunt and the terror they inflict in mortals. In a Kindred society ever more concerned with their ability to maintain the Masquerade in the face of ever-more attentive mortals, the Penangalan are often viewed as liabilities, albeit liabilities no one is particularly sure what to do with.
    The Becoming: The Penangalan select their potential recruits carefully. For the most part, they select from people who are already obsessed with flesh in some way or another: models, plastic surgeons, and tattoo artists are all common targets in modern nights. The Penangalan do not necessarily embrace only the beautiful, but their prospects must be striking looking in some way. Physical and Social attributes are often primary, with Stamina and Presence typically being the highest. Medicine, Expression, Brawl, and Intimidation are all valued skills. Common merits include Striking Looks and Mentor, with Herd and Retainer being less common. Penangalan almost always embrace their targets directly into the bloodline, rarely taking on prospects from outside the bloodline.
    In the Danse Macabre: Like most of their parent clan, the Penangalan occupy the outskirts of Kindred society, though the consciously monstrous Penangalan are rarely overly angst ridden about that fact. Many Penangallan do not even inhabit the cities that most Kindred make their homes in, preferring the unlives as solitary predators in rural areas of their home countries. However, as their traditional hunting grounds dwindle with the rise of global urbanization, more and more Penangalan are moving into the cities and becoming part of broader Kindred society for the first time.
    Those Penangalan who do join covenants typically gravitate towards the Ordo Dracul or the Circle of the Crone (or whatever their local equivalents are). For beings to whom changing their forms comes naturally, the Ordo Dracul’s message of evolution and change resonate strongly, and the Circle’s embrace of bloody handed passions suits the Penangalan just fine.
    A few Penangalan join the Carthian movement, though it is usually out of a desire to rabble-rouse and cause chaos than it is out of genuine political animus. The Invictus, to date, have never allowed a member of the Penangalan to join their ranks, finding the flesh shapers disgusting and walking threats to the Masquerade. The Lancea Sanctum rarely holds much power in the traditional lands of the Penangalan and no member of the bloodline has ever sought membership in that covenant.
    Bloodline Bane: The Life-Hating Curse, on top of the Nosferatu clan bane, the Penangalan also suffer from an irrational hatred towards pregnant women and a desire to consume their blood. When presented with the opportunity to feed on a pregnant woman, a Penangalan must roll to resist immediately falling to hunger frenzy. Upon feeding on the pregnant woman once, the vampire gains the Persistent Obsession condition with the victim that can only be ended by killing the subject or the subject’s pregnancy ending (either by the death or birth of the fetus).
    The Nosferatu clan curse typically manifests itself as a vague scent of vinegar that follows the Penangallan around wherever she goes.
    Bepusing
    Berpusing (lit. “Twisting” in Malay) is the gift of the Penangalan, giving them astounding power to shape their dead flesh in shocking and frightening ways. Using Berpusing, the Penangalan can stretch or detach their limbs, change their sex or age, or take on a horrifying war form.
    All Penangalan gain the first devotion of Berpusing upon officially joining the bloodline. All subsequent Devotions cost two experience.
    Membentuk
    The basic gift of the Penangalan allows them to warp their flesh in dramatic, though not explicitly monstrous, ways. For a single point of Vitae, small features of the Vampire’s shape (eye color, hair color or texture, superficial facial features) can be altered. For two points, major physical features can be changed (height, weight, skin color). For three points, the Penangalan can go so far as to change his sex. The Penangalan cannot make himself noticeably inhuman with this gift and outside of changing sex, they cannot alter their organ structure with this gift. As with other changes to the Vampire’s form, these changes fade during daysleep as the body returns to the state of their embrace but can be made permanent by spending a point of willpower.
    Lehar Panjang
    Requires: Protean 2
    The signature ability of the Japanese Penangalan, Long-Limb allows the vampire to stretch their limbs in obscene and frightening ways. The vampire can stretch their neck or limbs five feet per dot of Stamina they possess (Resilience does not count towards this total). This stretching allows the vampire to attack, feed, or grapple at range.
    Menanggalkan
    Requires: Protean 2, Nightmare 1
    Perhaps the most famous ability of the Penangalan, this ability allows them to detach their head and viscera from the rest of their body. Upon activating this gift and the vampire spends a point of Vitae and concentrates for a turn, the vampire’s head mass (consisting of the vampire’s head, lungs, stomach, and intestines) detaches cleanly from the rest of the vampire’s body. In this form, the Penangalan is a size 4 target, though she maintains the same number of health levels as her normal size. The head mass floats above the ground, flying with a species factor of 10. She automatically fails any rolls which would require fine manipulation, but she can use her viscera as a tentacle to perform basic tasks; she also fails all social rolls except for Intimidation. The Penangallan can constrict prey easily with its viscera tail, which is preternaturally strong; she gains the 9-again rule on rolls to grapple. She no longer needs to grapple to make bite attacks.
    To reattach the head-mass to its body, the Penangalan must spend three turns forcing its viscera back into its neck stump. If the Penangalan’s body is destroyed while she is detached, she can continue to live indefinitely in this form, though doing so is difficult to say the least.
    Using this gift, the Penangalan can also detach individual limbs, typically hands. A detached hand can grapple or grasp items at a distance, though if the vampire target is out of his line of sight, he must rely on feel to navigate unless he possesses some form of clairvoyant senses. If a sensory organ (such as an eye or ear) is detached, the vampire continues to receive sensory information from it. Other limbs can be detached in the same manner but doing so is rarely useful and often comical. If a detached limb is destroyed, the vampire is considered to have taken a point of aggravated damage; the limb regenerates once the point heals.
    Raksasa
    Requires: Protean 4, Nightmare 2
    The ultimate gift of Berpusing allows the Penangalan to take on a frightening war form. By spending 2 points of Vitae, the Penangalan can manifest four Beastly Aspects from the Protean 2 discipline power and two Unnatural Aspects from the Protean 4 discipline power. The form that the Penangalan takes when using this gift is always horrifying to behold, she fails all social rolls except for Intimidation, which she receives the 8-again rule for. She can also use the Nightmare 2 power, Face of the Beast, for no cost in this form.
    As with the Protean 2 power, the Aspects the Penangalan chooses are set once she takes this power and she must spend a full day sleeping in her Unmarked Grave and spend a point of Willpower to change them.
