    Make the Haven Merit Great Again

    07-18-2017, 05:27 PM
    So Safe place and Haven is kinda like the middle child of merits in most of our games. It's usually bought by at least on player but beyond a comfortable place to stay it 's just not worth it. It gives you extra dice to safety and waking when attacked and blood smells extra good inside your comfort zone.
    And for this you’ll have to pay for 2 merits, sure you only need 1 dot in Safe Place, but the first dot in haven is twice as expensive as other things you want.

    This is really good, and i give them that. It is just that this is almost never good in our chronicles, attacking the PCs during the day in their home is just a booring dick move. And beyond, our games are usually never really violent.

    So for our group, Haven is really subpar with other merits but it fills themes and gives mood and personality to the characters. So I’m looking for other things the Merit could do either instead of what it does or add to it to make it worthwhile.


    PS. I don’t need to hear ”how good it is”, because we all know that, it’s just not useful in our playstyle DS.

                                          Rather than come up with extra effects, our table just made the Merit cheaper. We made Haven a one-dot Merit. You attach the Haven Merit to a Single Safe Place. The Haven Rating is equal to the Rating of the Safe Place it is attached to. If you buy multiple Safe Places, you can can buy additional Haven merits to attach to those. Oh, and for Communal Havens, multiple Kindred could attach their Haven merit to the same Safe Place.

                                          People were willing to spend an extra dot on haven, but an extra dot per safe place pip was asking a lot.

                                                  I was going to just post how delighted I was to see such an elegant and effective solution to two problems at once (haven merit value and the daily Willpower issue). But, there are some structured contributions I can throw out as framework suggestions....

                                                  Haven (• to •••••)
                                                  Prerequisite: Safe Place •
                                                  Effect: A good haven is not only safe from the sun, but also familiar and comforting. The dot rating reflects your character’s affinity for his home and its defenses against the sun’s intrusion.
                                                  A low rating might be poorly sealed, undecorated and subject to distractions whenever the neighbors wake. A high rating might imply an ancestral home with subterranean vaults furnished through the work of generations to considerable ambiance.
                                                  Haven dots provide several benefits:
                                                  * Add your Haven dots to any Humanity rolls to notice danger while sleeping;
                                                  * Add Haven dots to any rolls to remain awake (Stamina + Resolve) .
                                                  * Similarly, add it to any Kindred senses (see p. 90) rolls within.
                                                  * Further, you may roll your merit dots in dice to regain 1 Willpower whenever you sleep in your Haven.
                                                  * Select one skill specialty for each dot of Haven. Exercise of a designated specialty within your haven benefits from the 9-again quality, or if already possessed, the 8-again quality. If the 8-again quality is already possessed the specialty may be exercised as a rote action.
                                                  Drawback: Losing a Haven is a breaking point at Humanity 8, minus its dot rating. A Haven must be tied to a Safe Place Merit (see p. 123). Like a Safe Place, a coterie may share a Haven Merit. Each member that wishes to benefit must invest Merit dots in both the Safe Place and the Haven. Should you roll a critical failure while attempting to regain Willpower sleeping in your Haven, reduce your Haven rating by 1 dot (subject to Sanctity of Merits)--something feels off and will require re-arrangement and investment of your time to correct.


                                                  Haven of Clay (• to •••)
                                                  "Let the other clans wrestle and strain with esoteric riddles of what it is to be a monster. They need havens, an audience, solitude, mortal institutions, or secrets. The Gangrel take what they want. All that they need is locked away in the endless potential of their horrific clay." (VtR 2e p. 17)
                                                  Effect: You have embraced the independence of the Gangrel clan and your haven is the dirt or other substance you sleep in, your Unmarked Grave. While you slumber, the flesh of the earth comforts you the same way the gentle embrace of a mother's womb comforts a child waiting to burst forth into the world. You may roll your merit dots in dice to regain 1 Willpower whenever you sleep in your Unmarked Grave.
                                                  Drawback: However on a critical failure you lose 1 Willpower to nightmares and the lingering desire to remain much longer.



                                                  --Khanwulf
