                                        Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post


                                        The Antitribu mod is also a good one to check out, as it adds a bunch of content, including the Companion mod and a Sabbat ending.

                                        They also added a bunch of different NPC models, so you don't have clones walking down the streets, and enemy vamps will actually use disciplines against you. Their Samedi model also looks noticeably more disturbing than the Final Nights version.
                                        Thank you for the tips. Much appreciated.

                                        Just so you know, I'm already aware of the Antitribu Mod, and TFN. I've tried to watch playthroughs of Antitribu, and they have some interesting ideas, from what I can see. However, I'm not fully sold on it.

                                        What is likely to now happen is that VTMB is going to be the original painting myself and a friend will be copying onto a new canvas. What I mean is that the city won't be LA, but a fictional city on the coast of California (at present!). It will be using the game hubs as the start point for the geography, then added on to. When you start looking at the hubs, they make sense as a game, but not as a real life city. So, there needs to be reworkings, and alterations. Also, if you have the Plus Patch, you gain more map areas, because some places - like the museum - have additional areas added on, because of content restoration.

                                        What is amazing, however, is the fact that you can back engineer a ruleset for this game, based on what Troika has given out. Read the text files included in the game, and they tell you things like how combat is calculated. Add to that the Character Sheet, and some other elements, and you have something you can work with. Moreover, the basic test rules play things a little differently. Quite fascinating to see in action.

                                        What Troika created is a majestic - if flawed - masterpiece, and is what got me interested in the World of Darkness in the first place. A shame they went under...
