                                                If you're really set on him having Protean out of the gate, then I'd have to agree that you just remake him as a Gangrel.                                                             I'm pretty dead set on playing a Nosferatu. And I really don't want Protean anyway (rather have nightmare), but I still like the tenticle idea, so I homebrewed a merit that adds to the physical deformities that Nosferatu can manifest. My Storyteller likes the merit, although he said he might make some changes.
                                                            Graveworm (OOO, or OOOOO)
                                                            Prerequisites: Must be Nosferatu.
                                                            Effect: For some Haunts, the curse manifest as a physical thing by warping their body in such a way that is visibly grotesque, but for others it's much worse. Your Beast is not content to simply writhe within your soul, it emerges in physical form. You have one or more orifices on your body, that nest a round, sucking, maw. By spending a Vitae, the maw emerges, revealing a long, leech-like parasite, often in a gout of blood. The worm (or worms) can be used as another pair arms or legs, giving you a better reach for climbing, and the ability to grapple at a distance.
                                                            Terrible Reach (OOO) The worm (or worms) are 10 meters long, and extends your reach to accomplish any task that doesn't require much manual dexterity. For example: Slipping it through prison bars to grab a prison guards keys would be appropriate, while operating a keyboard or firing a gun would not. This appendage is strong enough to bear your weight, and can be used to help you climb 10 meters as a reflexive action (Storyteller wants this to be a move action at half your speed instead, seems reasonable). The worm can also be used to used for an unarmed attack, or grapple up to 10 meters away. When grappling your victim may only attempt to use the Break Free, or Damage moves while grappled. If you take damage in any of your last three hit boxes (Bashing, Lethal, or Agitated) the grapple ends, and you get the Arm Wrack Tilt, giving you a -2 penalty to using these appendages for all physical actions.
                                                            Hungry Maw (OOOOO) The worm is not just a sucking mouth, but has grown a ring of hooked fangs. This Maw is always hungry, and is visible on your body unless covered by clothing. If touched it may attempt to bite, and cause a dramatic failure on affected social rolls related to physical intimacy. This maw can be used to feed during a grapple up to 10 meters away.
