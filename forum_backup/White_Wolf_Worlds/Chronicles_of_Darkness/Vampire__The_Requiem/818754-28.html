                Yes. And as a matter of fact, OPP is working on a book right now that offers more tools for games about elder vampires.

                The game includes rules for torpor, which more or less necessitate vampires creeping into their coffins and sleeping for a few decades everyone once in a while. That creates a good device for pushing the timeline along. And Damnation City includes new systems that accommodate high level political play. It's designed for the modern nights, but it could be adapted pretty easily to accommodate a game a few hundred years in the past.

                                                            There is the devotion Conditioning (Dominate ••••) that allows the vampire to apply the Subservient condition followed by the Enslaved condition. Someone who has the Enslaved condition counts as always having Mesmerized for the purposes of Dominate, but no amount of damage resolves the condition and it doesn't fade with time.

                                                            Originally posted by tsusasi View Post
                                                            You are not going to be able to dominate someone into wanting to destroy themself without a homebrewed devotion[..]
                                                            In 2E I find nothing about suicide being beyond the powers Dominate. Even Mesmerize should be able to do it since the condition only resolves after the action has been performed.
                                                            My group has house ruled that it requires more advanced supernatural powers (I think Conditioning might be enough) to mind control someone into trying to destroy themselves. Pure Dominate would instantly fail before the action was even attempted.
