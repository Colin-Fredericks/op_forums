                            I and other were in favour of the idea that almost everything is a Frenzy threat for a vampirebut not everything requires a roll to resist to it if you want to.
                            Let's make an example: your prey is escaping? That's for sure something that make you frustrated or hungry, not enough to threat your ability to maintain the control but enough to be able to free your beast if you wish.
                            Same if you are fighting but still not hurted.

                            Beast is always lurking from the very deep of the vampire shadows waiting for anything to be able to free itself. The Frenzy rolls are just something occurring when the stimulus is actually threatening the vampire capability to mantain the control.


                            On the other side I wouldn't allow to free the beast (and ride it) if you are in a quite place, not angry and not hungry at all.

                                                            Yes. In the first edition covenant and bloodline books there are several factions across the various covenant books and individual bloodlines that engage in extensive body modification or stunt showmanship to highlight the fact that they're capable of doing things beyond human limitations with willpower and vitae expenditure.

                                                            As far as second edition is concerned, the mechanics for Scales and Night Doctor Surgery does infer that there are limits to organ removals, blood transfusions, exsanguinations etc.

                                                            As for inserting armor underneath their skin or becoming high end prison wallets, that's ST discretion. Do keep in mind however, that when a vampire uses the Blush of Life, a vampire is effectively alive and needs to eat, drink, excrete waste, can grow hair etc. and shows up as alive under medical examination. And also, by default, a vampire's body reverts itself to their physical state at their time of death every day so you may want to take those factors into consideration.
