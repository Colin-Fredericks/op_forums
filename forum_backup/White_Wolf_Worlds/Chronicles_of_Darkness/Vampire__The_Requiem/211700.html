    Pseshkf Question

    07-28-2014, 06:22 PM
    "Weapons so coated cause 1 additional lethal damage to physical targets (including Kindred)"

    Does this mean that the rest of the damage for a normal weapon is also converted to lethal against kindred? If I used a knife and coated it, would all the damage I do with that knife then be considered supernatural and be lethal to Kindred? My Storyteller and I are having a bit of a disagreement about it at the moment, since he thinks it would only do that 1 lethal to kindred and the rest would be bashing.
