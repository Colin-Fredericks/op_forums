                              Well I have some postulation regarding the origin of Void Creatures, but as to the rest all I can do is think back to the description of Blood Sorcery from Sacraments & Blasphemies stating that Blood Sorcery cannot create or manipulate fire or sunlight, and that most things created by Blood Sorcery are destroyed when they come into contact with daylight or fire.

                              I believe Void Creatures are another tie to the strange abyssal realm that the Strix originally hailed from before settling in the delicious life filled world of Chronicles of Darkness, and that Void Familiars are just other strange and life hungry creatures from that otherworldly realm. That said I believe the description was vague enough that STs should have the final say in where they originate from, if they want to delve into that at all, but that it stands to reason that they have some sort of connection to the Strix in both appearance and otherworldly mentality.
