                    Oh, I know this. But three counterpoints.

                    Yes, it doesn't condemn you to a life of wanton hedonism, but that is the fantasy of the Daeva. If not hedonism, then at least aestheticism. As a creator why create a Daeva that doesn't embrace the fantasy? Unless, as you said you are specifically trying to subvert that fantasy as part of your character idea. Which he very well could be.

                    And in game, you are less likely to be turned by a Daeva if you yourself aren't the kind of person a Daeva would be inclined to turn. Not that it's likely for any clan, but it seems especially unlikely that a Daeva would turn this charity worker for no reason. And consider that being made a Daeva does influence both your Beast and your Curse. It's not just a club you get sworn into. Daeva's tend toward hedonism for a reason.

                    Further, the letter less florid/focused on the beauty of vampirism than I'd expect from a Daeva. Yes, I'm stereotyping but that helps when deciding where a character best fits, and there's nothing that suggests Daeva to me. Lust is their defining trait.

                    The only real angle I see is as you said, a Daeva in denial, trying to square his lust with his old morals. Horrified at how much he loves to feed and staunchly refusing to, except he can't stop himself. So he finds a way by perverting it as some way to mete out justice. The whole "I can't do it to animals" thing is an obvious lie, he just can't stomach ground chuck when he's had a taste of filet mignon.

                    Nosferatu is a better fit (or Gangrel), as is, in my eyes, any clan. Daeva would be the last choice for this character except where clan disciplines are concerned. I was just brainstorming like the OP asked. My opinion is another grain of salt to take as you please. I was, like many others, more confused by the not feeding on animals thing than clan.

                        Social workers often have a position of advantage over the people they work with. Daeva often have signiciant power over the mortals they interact with. The fantasy of a Daeva is a social worker's worst nightmare and intensely traumatizing, and it's a remarkably easy power to use to make your Requiem easier. Nevermind the idea of subverting the archetype, there's a rich playground of power differentials and coerced behavior and misguided 'help' to be had in playing a Daeva. They disrupt the lives around them. That's a lot of material to work with.

                        Originally posted by Amravanti View Post
                        And in game, you are less likely to be turned by a Daeva if you yourself aren't the kind of person a Daeva would be inclined to turn. Not that it's likely for any clan, but it seems especially unlikely that a Daeva would turn this charity worker for no reason.                         If we want to really take a look at Daeva in 2e, they're arguably the least likely clan to have a "type" precisely because their Curse is not picky about who they're obsessed with, and that obsession seems like one of the ways that Daeva might Embrace. (If you look at the fiction that inspires Daeva, you'll see that behavior of obsession and possession replay itself multiple times.

                        If you look at the way the mechanics reinforce the nature of the Daeva, it's that it gives them a tool that's easy to use to help solve problems (Majesty and Awe), it can make you innately better at manipulating people or moving fast, and it lends itself to becoming obsessed with the mortals you feed on.

                        The idea of their hedonism is really more of a 1e thing. They're not "the ones who host debauched parties" they're "the ones you love" and you might observe that all the talk of sex goddesses isn't the talk of sex seeking for self pleasure. It's talking about it through the medium of divinity and worship. The thematics of the Daeva in 2nd edition aren't of hedonism, they're about worship, power over others, love and obsession, and pleasure derived through those things.

                        I think if you're looking specifically for hedonism within this set, I'd either make it a part of the character's concept, their immediate Kindred lineage, or part of their Bloodline identity.
