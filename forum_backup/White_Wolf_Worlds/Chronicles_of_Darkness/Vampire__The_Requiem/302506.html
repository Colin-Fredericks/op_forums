    Rules Questions: Iron Skin, Multiple Styles

    11-04-2014, 03:10 PM
    Does Iron Skin (the merit) stack with other forms of armor on a Vampire? The merit only applies to bashing attacks, but swords and bullets both deal bashing to vampires now. It's specifically noted that characters with supernatural armor - with a werewolf's hide being used as an example - can stack that armor with normal armor... except Iron skin isn't supernatural armor inherently, though it might be on a vampire. It's "natural" armor that might "become" supernatural with the Embrace, so I'm not sure what to do with it.


    Can characters benefit from multiple Styles simultaneously?

    The easiest example is physical conflict: If I've got Martial Arts 5 and Street Fighting 2, can my successful unarmed attack with hands as +2L weapons, per Martial Arts 5, also cause my opponent to take a -1 penalty on his next action, per Street Fighting 2?

    Obviously there are some things that clearly can't be used together - can't spend WP on multiple things or sacrificing defense multiple times, etc. But there is a LOT of room where mechanically, I can see multiple styles applying. Some make quite fine thematic sense, too. (Kindred Dueling and Light Weapons, Ride the Wave and Martial Arts / Street Fighting, etc).

    With the social styles (Etiquette and Fast-Talking) there's lots room for combination as well.

                                              Thanks for the input, folks. Hope we can get an "official" response soon, maybe even get this added to the FAQ!

                                              I could see the "stacking styles" even for passive bonuses being a balance problem in some cases, but I think it's more of a danger in a mortal-focused game where the primary combat skill has been maxed out and buying merits becomes more xp-efficient than putting more dots into attributes. That's typically no longer the case once you've opened up the supernatural powers menu.

                                              If it became a real problem, I'd suggest creating a "hybrid style" merit or something, where each dot in the merit allowed you to combine stuff from a single other style. So 2-dots of Hybrid style would be needed to get the passive bonuses from 2 styles at the same time, etc. Unless there's some blatantly unbalanced combination of passive abilities I'm missing (entirely possible), that'd at least move the xp-efficiency way down compared to alternatives - which seems to be the main complaint I've seen so far.

                                              I've yet to see a Style+Style combination that's as powerful as Style + PT5, anyway. (But PT5 is le broken.)
