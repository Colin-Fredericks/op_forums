    There's risk getting into 2,000 lbs of steel that races along at 75 mph.

    Taking blood from people to prolong your (arguably) unnatural, parasitic existence doesn't exactly fit the same mold.

    Yes, if you feed from someone, and that damage you dealt to them ends up causing their death, you are responsible.

    EDIT: This is getting into a silly philosophical area. My point is that vampires are not doing their vessels any favors, no matter how you slice it. At the base they are draining life from people to sustain themselves, and that is a bad thing in the grand scheme. There's wiggle room as to how bad it is, or how bad it can become, but it's not comparable to mortals consuming food.

                                                    People consent to harm all the time. Masochists, tattoos, piercings, body modification. If you take the lethal damage to mean 'harm' instead of being a mechanical representation of blood loss, there is still nothing immoral about harm done with consent. And addiction does not invalidate consent. That is, if an alcoholic orders wine at a restaurant, the waiter has consent to provide that wine even though the alcoholic is an addict. As the vampire is ideally controlling the space between 'doses' to protect his or her Blood Doll, I'd argue feeding is less immoral than giving an alcoholic booze.

                                                    I doubt anyone will say that feeding is doing anyone a favor except the vampire, but done willingly it is a very pleasant experience for the mortal and the harm caused is not really any worse than anything listed at the beginning of this post. Due to this, I imagine you could get a herd without using any mind-control whatsoever. You just need to find the right type of individual.

                                                    So I maintain that you can ethically feed as a vampire. It's just almost never going to happen. It's the providence of a Humanity 10 vampire to be that strict with one's feeding.
