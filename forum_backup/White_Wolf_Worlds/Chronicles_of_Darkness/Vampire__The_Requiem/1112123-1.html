            Alright so - as a person who started off as a Masquerade player and moved to Requiem, this is my perspective on things - it depends on your creativity as a person. Well, not exactly - it depends on your -need- for creativity, versus your need for structure.

            In my experience, there has never been a concept in Masquerade that I could not replicate in Requiem, or improve upon. The world is, in my opinion, far more dynamic in nature. This could be due to the people that I had the experience of playing with, but I've found my creativity a lot less strained trying to make a concept for a requiem character.

            That said, the benefits of Masquerade are structure. You can reasonably expect that a Ventrue in Masquerade is going to be lordly, and not a bloodline who has no interest in the throne because they are more interested in finding the holy grail due to some curse placed on them. This can make the games easier for an informed player and an informed ST.

            In the end, however - my personal experience is that I had a lot less fun with Masquerade due to the banality of the setting. It was kind of like playing in a fanfiction of someone's story, versus making my own story - which, as I understand, is a comparison you might hear quite often. I still -play- Masquerade and I still play requiem, but I play Masquerade more for the company I keep than the game itself.
