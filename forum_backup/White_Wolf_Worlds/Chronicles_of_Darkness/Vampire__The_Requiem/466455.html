                                  Boooo to another addition to the theory of "all powerful / mysterious vampires in the past were the strix." Much like boo to the theory of all great historical figures in humanity being supernaturals of various ilk... it washes out the mystery and the horror as well as diluting the centrality of human themes.

                                  That's just me and my one-man crusade against the Strix as central antagonists talking though. Pay no mind the the madman on the sidewalk.

                                  As for the original topic of the thread itself, page 80 of Left-Handed path describes the Theban as an "undead sorcerer" who used "blood sorcery" to change his nature. Given that in that same bit of lore it says that the Theban found them even before they set out to seek him, the combination of those two point to Divination and Transmutation, the two favored Themes of Theban Blood Sorcery in Sacraments and Blasphemies.

                                  My guess / personal canon is that the Theban was either the vampiric equivalent of an archmage, with 6+ dots in Theban Sorcery and Transmutation, or he was simply a master of Transmutation with the power of plot allowing him to take advantage of the line of Transmutation 5 which states "The fifth dot of Transmutation allows the ritualist to transform any object or creature into any other, transferring and changing characteristics as she sees fit."

                                                      The usual Lancea Sanctum reasons.
