    Very well done, Ophion, Pendragon, and Laraqua. I fancy your work a lot and I'm going to adopt Ophion's version of the Coil and Mystery of Hunger as the B&S replacement for Coil of Blood until and unless an official version is adopted. Frankly I find this a much, much better fit for the third basic Coil of the Dragon than Coil of the Voivode.

    To minimize the need for blood is central to the trans-vampiric Great Work of overcoming the flaws and limitations of the 'Curse' just like to overcome the banes, undead nature, and the Beast. To counter the effects of Blood Potency on feeding looks of great usefulness to all Dragons that expect to become elders. On the contrary, Coil of the Voivode only looks useful to a subset of elders that plan to develop a small army of thralls.

    Slagheap, your more direct conversion has merit, too, but I think the other version fits B&S as a whole somewhat better.

                                    Originally posted by AlucardKnight View Post
                                    To pendragon, I always just allowed "the coil of the blood" as just another coil, players could take. Not changing it a bit, I mean 2nd edition does say "these aren't all the coils" and people are encouraged to create their own. I just added the coils in ver. 1 to 2.

                                    The problem with just adding Coils from 1e is that you will lack 1-2 tiers (depending on which Coil and which books you have access to), are often unbalanced (sometimes even in first edition), often clashes with second edition design philosophies or themes, and might not even work properly if they interact with rules that has changed between editions.
                                    For example, Blood of Beasts (second dot of Coil of Blood) is 1.) wholly passive when 2e Coils are supposed to be interacted with as to make them more interesting and 2.) ridiculously good for its level considering how much of a problem feeding at BP6+ is supposed to be in second edition. Another example is Sun's Forgotten Kiss (third dot of Coil of Banes) that just straight up doesn't work in second edition because the rules for how vampires take damage from sunlight has changed (as well as being extremely underwhelming for what used to be the capstone dot for that Coil, even within the context of first edition).
                                    There are reasons why there have been so many attempts to update the 1e Coils to fit with VtR 2e.
