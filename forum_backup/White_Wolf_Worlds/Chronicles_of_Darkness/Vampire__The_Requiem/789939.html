    Mystery Cult Initiation for the Cult of Set (Khaibit)

    12-20-2015, 06:05 PM
    Hey, I'm looking to make a Khaibit Ancillae who runs a cult of mortals / Ghouls to watch for "Creatures of Shadow" (Largely Strix, but also "other creatures that don't need humanity to survive"). I am hoping for commentary / criticism and help fleshing it out. The Cult is largely meant to provide a support role, though the evil Kindred Overlords are not above sending in shock troops if the situation requires. The Cult would have heavy associations with the Circle of Crone, but would be short of being a cult of the great mother in disguise. I imagine there is some pairing between Set and the Crone.

    I am thinking the relic should be an alleged section of Apep, mummified meat and shadow with an ancient spear piercing it. Clearly otherworldly. It may be a dead stryx or a dead ... one of those retainers that one Cruac Style can make.

    For benefits I have...
    * Language [Arabic]
    Initiates at this level are indoctrinated into the book of lies (written in arabic). Initiates are judged based on what they are willing to believe and how they are able to grasp the truth from lies. Some never progress further, just believing that the the Cult is a fellowship or secret society or old money club. Others are categorized based on how they take to the information.

    ** Language [Ancient Egyptian]
    Those initiates who show the correct mix of being able to divine the truth and be controlled progress. They are given access to ancient shards of text and told that it will she'd light on the book of lies. It is legitimately reclaimed but undeciphered text. Here little slips of the supernatural happen, a slow boil to see how they acclimate.

    *** Eye for the Strange
    This stage is defined by those who have seen the husk and know what is out there. These people know the cults true mission and are put on watch for the supernatural. This is where vampires take an active interest in the cultists life. Often, these are made into Ghouls and set to investigate other supernatural and/or rivals.

    **** Three dots to spend on Retainers
    These are the high priests of the cult, in charge of their fellows. They indoctrinate others. They wash money. They assign missions. You gain retainer dots to represent other cultists you are in charge of. Even if they die or are driven insane, new ones can be gained quickly.

    ***** ?????
    No idea for this level. Presumably you are at this point a shadowy vampire overlord, or you are one of their most trusted Ghouls. Cultists at this level know about the vampire conspiracy controlling them.

                  One of my players is running a Khaibit in my active game. so I ended up splitting the Cult of Seth into three different castes to flesh them out a bit more and offer options. For the leader/handler caste I made the level five a version of Blood Oaths called the Contracts of Hepzefi. Then reskinned any Invictus Blood Oaths that could've feasibly had a version active during ancient/old Egypt, and let them have access.

                  If curious or helpful, here are the levels I made for each of the castes. I had made it more Kindred focused, however, so each caste is fully behind the Masquerade. I've been considering if it would be better to switch some of these to style merit instead, but haven't yet revisited.
                  Cult of Seth: Warrior Caste (Asps)
                  • Quick Hollow ●
                    • Every Asp needs a base of operations, but the Asps rarely have time to invest in a permanent home. Quick Hollow allows an Asp to find an abandoned location to use as a haven, or allows them to create a makeshift haven on the go. This is done with as an extended test with a target of (6) successes, each roll taking 30 minutes of preparation.
                      • Finding: Intelligence + Survival
                      • Creating: Intelligence + Crafts
                    • At the end of the extended test, success allows the Asp to count the location as a Safe Place (but with no Mechanics) and gain the Haven ● effect.
                    • This cannot be stacked/shared with normal Haven traits, however if multiple Asps undertake this action at the same location and each succeeds on the test, they are allowed to stack like a joint haven.
                  • Ironscale ● ●
                    • At this stage the Asp has been trained in the defensive arts primarily. She gains the Defensive Combat ● Merit with a focus in Weaponry.
                  • Graceful Strike ● ● ●
                    • The Asp continues to train in the weaponized arts, this time gaining mastery among the weapons found in the ethnic roots of the Cult. Choose a weapon of Middle/Near-Eastern origin. Gain Fighting Finesse ● ● with your chosen weapon.
                  • Mongoose Hunt ● ● ● ●
                    • The Asp is now specifically trained to target the most dangerous abodes of the enemy - the Kindred body itself. The Asp receives three dots of training in the Kindred Dueling Fighting Style. If she already has dots in this Merit, they stack proportionally. Refund any dots which would push the Merit past rating five.
                  • Serpent Sight ● ● ● ● ●
                    • This tier separates depending on if the Asp is actually a member of the Khaibit bloodline or another Kindred ally of the Cult.
                      • For non-Khaibit: You gain the Udjat Devotion that all Khaibit receive, allowing the Asp to see in absolute darkness, and gain immunity from blindness.
                      • For Khaibit: You gain the Shatter the Shroud devotion as soon as you qualify by having Auspex ● ● and Vigor ●.
                  Cult of Seth: Priest Caste (Vipers)
                  • Serpent's Tongue ●
                    • The Viper first learns to communicate the internal language of the Cult. Despite the name, the language is completely silent. Simple Pictographs make up the written aspect, while direct communication is done comepletely through hand signals. The Language isn't designed for long and complicated conversations, more utilized for notations and quick communication in the field.
                  • Wait of Destiny ● ●
                    • The Viper begins to taste the teachings of the self-control needed for the deeper mysteries of the cult's sorcery. The training is first applied to more mundane and traditional tasks of great length. She gains the Patient ● Merit.
                  • Sacrifice to Typhon ● ● ●
                    • Now the Viper is brought into the supernatural lores of the cult. She gains the ability to learn the discipline of Cruac. The rituals of the Sethites are limited, however, to only a certain subset of those mastered by the Circle: Pangs of Proserpina, Rigor Mortis, Cheval, the Hydra’s Vitae, Touch of the Morrigan, and Blood Blight. The cult has a few unique rituals known only to them as well: Genius Loci, Amamet's Pursuit, The Hand of Seth, the Thrashing of Apep's Coils, and the Rite of Going Forth by Day.
                    • Drawback: Sethite rituals often involve complicated symbolic formulae, vestments, masks, braziers issuing odd-colored smoke, altars and the like, and imprecations to the powers of Ancient Egypt, particularly to the demon Amemet the Devourer and, of course, to the Lord of Entropy himself, Typhon Seth.
                    • Although the paraphernalia helps (it offers a +2 equipment bonus to the dice pool), in addition to the Rites normal sacrifice, Sethite Cruac requires an additional blood sacrifice, using it in some way as part of the ritual.
                      • One to three-dot rituals require the death of a mouse or similar small animal.
                      • Four-dot rituals require a more substantial sacrifice, such as a dog, a cat, a sheep, a bull or a goat.
                      • Five-dot rituals require a human sacrifice and the attending Breaking Point roll.
                  • Thoth's Plunder ● ● ● ●
                    • With the Supernatural skills now in their hands, Vipers are ready to go into the field to join the Asps and Cobras in close knit teams. The ability to communicate in the local environs is paramount. The Viper receives one dot of the Multilingual Merit (with two starting languages of their choice.) From then on, as an extended Intelligence + Expression action with a target of 6 Successes, the Viper can swap out those chosen languages for new ones. The extended test must take place in within hearing range of speakers of the language, though the Viper need not engage the speakers themselves.
                    • Drawback: The speaking must be done by a living human. Kindred do not count, nor do recordings of speakers or broadcasts.
                  • Apep Swims ● ● ● ● ●
                    • The Viper learns to focus themselves on the scales of Apep as it swims through the river of death. The Viper received the Meditative Mind ● ● ● ● trait.
                  Cult of Seth: Master Caste (Cobras)
                  • Hungry Minds ●
                    • The first stage of leadership for the Cobra comes in managing the extensive research and writings of the cult. The Cobra gains the Library ● Merit, with a skill focus of either Academics or Occult.
                  • Sweet Whispers ● ●
                    • At this stage, the Cobra is first tasked with coordinating informants to the Sethites. The Cobra gains the Contacts ● Merit and my choose either the Sethite Informants focus, or may choose a different focus relevant to the Cobra's particular methods.
                  • Shrouded Skins ● ● ●
                    • The Cobra is now embedded in the field, either alone or with a strike group. Papers and reputations are planted in both the mortal and Kindred worlds to give a functioning cover to the Cobra's missions. They gain the Alternate Identity ● ● Merit.
                  • Expanded Assets ● ● ● ●
                    • As a proven and well-traveled member of the Cult, the cobra now has a number of assets at their beck and call. The Cobra may distribute three Merit points among Allies, Contacs and Resources. This breakdown can be redistributed once per story, or when the Cobra establishes a base in a new city.
                  • The Contracts of Hepzefi ● ● ● ● ●
                    • The Cobra has now achieved the pinnacle of Mystery Leadership. They now have access to the Contracts of Hepzefi, mystical contracts similar to Invictus oaths, and may buy those advantages as if they had the Notary merit. They automatically gain the Contract of Nunet (Same as Oath of Fealty, only use the oathbound's Mystery Cult Initiation rating rather than Invictus Status for determining the amount of blood which may be taken.)

                      Just read them in the writeup. There are a couple of things going on with the Followers of Set. They stand as watchdogs vs. spirits and other supernatural things. They have Cruac. And they do some Fight Club esque terrorism.

                      The Followers of Set [Cult of Typhon Seth]
                      The Followers of Set band together to loosen the grip that the spiritual world has over the world. Those powers would bind you in oaths and chain you to complacency. They are the possessor of bodies and consumers of souls. It is only through the disruption of the status quo and reduction ("corruption," as I am certain their propaganda will tell you) that the terrible things leaking out of the eye of hell can be pushed back.

                      Cultists: The Sethites are quite a large conspiracy, but they prefer to recruit their human members from those who are less in the right position, and more in the right place: technicians, postal workers, mechanics, drivers, and so on.

                      Initiation Benefits
                      • New recruits are kept from the true secrets of the cult, but every pair of eyes that is at least aware of Apep's grasp is useful. Gain an Occult Specialty in [Immaterial Beings].
                      •• Those who prove themselves capable are often tasks with objectives that they may carry out through other members in the cell. Gain Allies [Followers of Set] 1, which generally applies to people in entry level, support, or low-status positions such as cooks, drivers, groundskeepers, mechanics, and so on.
                      ••• Respected initiates that have proven they’re likely to handle direct contact with the supernatural well are taught to see the supernatural taint that influences the world. They gain the Eye for the Strange merit.
                      •••• At higher ranks within the organization, members are assigned wards and students. Take three dots in Retainers, allocated as the player sees fit (that is, one three-dot Retainer, three one-dot Retainers, etc.).
                      ••••• The highest ranking Followers are high priests whose rituals keep the darkness at bay by sowing chaos in the mortal realm. You gain a single dot in Cruac and an Occult Specialty in Cruac, but you do not learn a ritual for free. You may learn rituals you qualify for at their normal experience point cost. Those who are not members of the Circle of the Crone should show caution with whom they allow to see this gift, lest their existence be cut very short. Mortal practitioners may pay the Vitae Cost in lethal damage so long as they are leading the rite.

                      -----

                      The idea behind the character is that he started as a Not!Lovecraftian Investigator with enough wherewithal to survive touches with the supernatural. The Cult of Set caught wind and pressganged him into the cult. His job was ostensibly to look into and investigate other supernaturals for Vampires (and on the downlow their political rivals for blackmail). He rose through the ranks, got Ghouled, and died horribly against a Stryx and was post-mortum embraced. Time went on, he continued looking for outside supernatural influence upon vampires (particularly Stryx) as his "official" job. A sudden and unusual surge of Stryx activity found him outting several people as possessed. Khaibit did what Khaibit do, but they were sloppy. Turns out all the possessed were of one family line, whose ennui-addled Elder was very not happy with them. Cue purge and mass staking. Character forced into indentured servitude, ostensibly to pay back the "debt" his family garnered by destroying the elder's resources. Character helped with the ennui and got the Elder into religion (CoC and the Cult), which s/he doesn't take seriously but it is a quality time thing they can share. Character is trying to build up a hidden powerbase in plain sight and earn freedom for himself and the rest of the Khaibit... although lately he has been considering just indoctrinating new Khaibit instead.
