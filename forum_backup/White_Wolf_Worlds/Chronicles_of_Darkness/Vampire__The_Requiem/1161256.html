    New Bloodline: The Children of Lilith

    11-21-2017, 04:03 PM
    Hey everybody! Haberdasher here. I've temporarily risen from the torpor that is the semester to touch base and give you guys a little something: a new bloodline!

    Since Rose and company are now giving us conversions of 1e bloodlines, I have been a little hesitant to continue my project of updating of 1e bloodlines, since I'm kind of a purist when it comes to content and generally prefer official content to home-brew, even when its my own stuff. I am, however, enthusiastic about doing original bloodlines (read: take inspiration from Masquerade clans and bloodlines, and come up with 2e bloodlines that follow the spirit of them).

    Today's bloodline is one I've been sitting on for a while, but since it's break for Thanksgiving, I figured I would take the time to share them. The Children of Lilith are inspired by the Followers of Set in aesthetics and powers, but I wanted to take them in a slightly different direction. Just like the Khaibit are implied to be the original Mekhet, I present the Vipers as the potential "progenitor" bloodline for the Daeva. They're Daeva who play the whole "manipulative social Serpent" stereotype to the hilt, but with Protean and Obfuscate thrown in for extra lols and unpredictability. Making their founder the biblical Lilith who then embraced the biblical Abel was a shout-out to Masquerade's Caine mythology. Letting them shape-shift into a massive smoke snake was a very intentional shout-out to the Setites, but the fact that I say it's explicitly compared to the smoke form of a Strix is meant to show that the Vipers' account may not be entirely true, and that there may be more in-universe truth to the core book story about Inanna's deal with the Strix than the Vipers think...

    Let me know if you are still interested in seeing me update 1e bloodlines for 2e, or if you just want to see me do original bloodlines. And as always, leave comments and advice!


    New Bloodline: The Children of Lilith
    You know what they say about temptations: the only way to get rid of them is to indulge them. Now have a bite.

    Stories about how the clans started are as numerous as vampires themselves. Many Daeva are familiar with the story of Inanna, who made a pact with the owls of her sister Ereshkigal for the return of her beloved Lilith, who came back to the lands of the living as the first Daeva. But there is another story, one that gets at the heart of their nickname: the Serpents. One bloodline has taken this story to heart. They see themselves as the original Daeva, children of another Lilith: the Viper of Eden.

    Background: The Children of Lilith are immeasurably ancient; so long has there have been stories of Daeva using temptation and seducation to manipulate mortals, there have probably been Heads. The Children of Lilith make no secret that they believe themselves to be the original Daeva, crafted by the hand of a rebellious goddess against a despotic ruler.

    Borrowing from certain Jewish and Christian traditions, the Vipers say the first woman was not Eve, but Lilith. God made Adam and Lilith, physically seperated them from each other, and named them. Together, the pair named the animals and established dominion over them. But Lilith objected to the way Adam took her, saying that he had not named her, so he had no right to command her. This angered Adam, so he asked God to remove Lilith from Eden and give him a new woman to have as his mate. So God cast Lilith out of Eden, into the wilderness that surrounded it. She cursed Adam's name, and swore to never forgive him.

    The Children of Lilith say that she was found by Lucifer and his angels, roaming the world after their Fall. The rebel angels adopted her into their tribe, and Lilith fell in love with Lucifer. Lucifer gave Lilith many gifts, one of which was the power to change her form so that she could visit the paradise she had been banished from. Lilith took the form of a serpent, and slithered into the Garden. She saw that Adam with his new mate: Eve, a woman that God had fashioned from Adam's own body, given to him to name and rule over. Lilith was filled with envy, and decided to poison their bliss. She befriended Eve, and began whispering to her, telling her hidden truths about the universe that Lucifer had shared with her at night and cunning half-lies. And Eve repeated these words to Adam, and so enmity began to grow in their hearts for each other, and for God. God was forced to expell Adam and Eve from the Garden for their rebellion, and he cursed Eve- not that she would be forced to crawl on her belly for the rest of her days, but that she may never bear children.

    Lilith was heartbroken by this news, but Lucifer gave her one final gift: undeath. Lilith arose with an insatiable hunger in her spirit. She observed Adam and Eve from a distance, gloating as they struggled to eke out a miserable existence. She saw the jealousy grow between their sons, and when Cain murdered Abel, she raised him to undeath, the first of her unholy children in defiance of God's curse. With Abel's death, vice and sin truly came into the hearts of humanity, and Lilith and her children have been there every step of the way, breathing sweet poison into their ears and giving them their hearts' content, that their mother's revenge may be carried out, one damning fantasy at a time.

    The Becoming: Most Children of Lilith join the bloodline as mortals or occassionally vampires who were members of Viper mystery cults. Prospective Vipers are usually selected for either their intellect or charisma. Many are chosen because they have social connections that the cult can exploit, but just as many were socially marginalized in life. In past centuries, most Vipers were Middle-Eastern or Mediterranean in their looks, but as the Vipers have spread across the globe, particularly to cultures that have significant serpentine figures in their folk lore, they have diversified their ranks. Mental or Social Attributes are always primary, while Mental Skills and Social Merits are usually favored.

    In the Danse Macabre: Vipers are always high-ranking members of Viper-led mystery cults. The exact character of these cults may differ from region to region- one in Oslo might disguise itself as a Norse neo-pagan group that focuses on Loki and his children, particularly Jormungandr; another in Mesoamerica may ape an indigenous faith movment; one in San Francisco might masquerade as a New Age ecstatic movement that purposely adopts the Serpent of Eden as a totemic figure- but the goal is always the same: cultivate networks of social contacts to benefit the cult, and corrupt more humans to fulfill Lilith's mission. In Elysium, they usually hang back on the margins, uninterested in drawing attention to themselves or making bids for Praxis; Vipers prefer a more subtle style, prefering to build up Boons. They excel in procuring items for other Kindred, usually things that they wish to keep secret, often boiling down to a steady stream of mortals or vampires for a client to feed upon, often drawn from among the ranks of their cult's lower-ranking members; though sometimes, it might be as mundane as information, or access to an influential mortal, or an outlet to indulge some depravity- for a Viper, the chance to gain a Boon means that no appetite is too despicable to facilitate. Vipers are never Princes, but they are always safe, for addicts never kill a dealer who keeps providing, and the blackmail that Vipers have on their clients can make even the most determined elder balk at the thought of betraying them.

    Politically, most Vipers join the Circle of the Crone thanks to its “do what thou wilt” attitude, which dovetails nicely with their goals for humanity. Some find great success using the combination of secretive favors and reputation-destroying blackmail to create a subtle carrot-and-stick routine in the Invictus. While the Lancea et Sanctum may seem an odd choice, some Vipers find that the opportunity to tempt mortals into iniquity and then punish them for it to be too perfect an opportunity to pass up.

    Clan: Daeva

    Nickname: Vipers

    Bloodline Weakness: A Child of Lilith is dedicated to tempting mortals, so much so that it can be literally traumatic for them to pass up an opportunity to do so. Whenever a Child of Lilith fails to encourage a mortal to indulge her Vice, he loses a point of Willpower.

    Bloodline Disciplines: Celerity, Majesty, Obfuscate, Protean

    Bloodline Gift: Serpentis
    Serpentis, informally referred to as the Blessings of Lilith, allows a Viper to blend his disciplines in ways that are as alluring as they are outwardly grotesque.


    Fangs of the Asp (Protean @@)
    When a Viper uses Predatory Aspect, he can choose to have one of his adaptations be hollow fangs that he can use to inject targets with a target with a poison with a Toxicity equal to his Blood Potency, and the target suffers lethal damage equal to the user's Blood Potency rounded up every hour until the venom runs its course, the target is dead, or the target finds and uses antivenom. When he successfully bites a target, he can either feed from them or envenomate them, but not both, and he cannot feed from a target he has envenomated until the venom runs its course or the target receives antivenom.

    This Devotion costs 2 Experiences to learn.


    The Ecstatic Sting (Majesty @@, Protean @@)
    With this Devotion, a Viper can transform his tongue into a massive forked tongue, with which he can lash out at enemies and potentially feed, filling victims with euphoria even in the heat of battle.

    When a Viper uses Predatory Aspect (Protean @@), he can select as one of his adaptations a forked tongue that extends up to one yard or meter. The tongue can not be used to pick things up, but he can use it to attack targets with a Dexterity + Brawl roll, and attacks with the tongue deal 0L damage. He can also attempt to use the tongue to grapple enemies with the tongue for the purpose of feeding. A successful grapple and feeding roll inflicts the Swooning Condition on the target, even if the Viper used the attack in combat.

    This Devotion Costs 2 Experiences to learn.


    Blasphemous Visage (Majesty @@@@@, Obfuscate @@@@, Protean @@@@)
    Children of Lilith claim that they developed this Devotion after seeing the images of animal-headed gods in Egypt, or even inspired the depictions of the Egyptian gods. Whatever the case, this Devotion allows Children of Lilith to shift their appearance into that of an animal-headed human. The animal in question is usually serpentine or reptilian, but can also reflect the nature of the Viper's Beast. Children of Lilith often use this Devotion when interacting with their cult members, maintaining a strong level of anonymity

    Cost: 3 Vitae
    Dice Pool: None
    Action: Reflexive
    Duration: Scene

    When a Viper uses Blasphemous Visage, his appearance shifts into something resembling an Egyptian god. While in this form, it is as if he is using Idol (Majesty @@@@@), but this Devotion makes it so that targets do not need to be under the effects of Awe (Majesty @) or be Charmed to be effected, and it carries the benefit that those who gain the Enthralled Condition do not realize that the Viper and the “god” are one and the same: they will know that the Viper was in the room, but lost track of them when the “god” appeared, and will only remember the Viper is supposed to be present when they see him next in his normal form. A vampire who succeeds on the roll to resist becoming Enthralled can attempt to use Auspex in a Clash of Wills to discern the identity of the Viper, and he remembers it after Blasphemous Visage ends.

    This Devotion costs 3 Experiences to learn.


    Shadow of the Dark Mother (Obfuscate @@@@@, Protean @@@@@)
    The ultimate expression of Viper ability, Shadow of the Dark Mother allows the user to take on the form of a giant, shadowy serpent. Astute Kindred who witness this power note a disturbing similarity between this form and the shadow-forms of the Strix, a likeness that Vipers shrug off: after all, they are snakes, not owls.

    When a Viper uses Primeval Miasma (Protean @@@@@), he can instead choose to take on the form of a snake that uses the rules for Strix: his Blood Potency is treated as his Shadow Potency; his size stays the same; his Attributes switch to Power, Finesse, and Resistance and is calculated as the sum of his relevant Attributes capped by Shadow Potency; retains his Masque, Dirge, Humanity, and Aspirations; does not have any common powers, Host Powers, or Dread Powers except for Shadow Form; has Speed 12, but cannot fly; can use any Discipline he knows; and retains his Banes. If a Viper loses all of his Corpus while in snake form, any damage left over is applied to his physical form, and is downgraded as appropriate. He can attempt to feed from a target using his Power dots for the grapple roll.

    This Devotion costs 5 Experiences to learn.
