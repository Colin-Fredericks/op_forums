    Secrets of the Covenants - Grafting Unholy Flesh

    12-14-2016, 09:00 AM
    Hi guys. I've been reading the book and came across this Scale of the Dragon:

    Grafting Unholy Flesh
    Prerequisite Coil: The Dragon’s Breath
    Procedure: The Dragon must take a body part from another Kindred, psychic, or other supernatural creature. He must work quickly, and graft the body part onto his own form, letting his Vitae vitalize the tissue before it completely decomposes. The Dragon must know of a supernatural ability possessed by the subject, and choose a body part symbolic of that skill. This procedure may only take place on a Wyrm’s Nest.
    Outcome: The Dragon must spend a point of Vitae in order to fuse the body part with his own form. So long as the Dragon keeps the body part animate as part of his body, he gains access to the ability. Any costs aside from Vitae and Willpower costs must be paid with both one Vitae and Willpower per point of its normal cost. If the victim survives the surgery, she loses access to the ability so long as the body part remains detached. She similarly loses any ability to regenerate that part. The body part has Health Levels equal to its Size, separate the vampire’s. If it loses all its Health Levels, it becomes worthless to the vampire. He may use this to affix a body part to another Kindred or ghoul.
    How would you apply this supernatural ability gain, when the supernatural creature is a Mage? Do you get one point of a sphere, one sphere with the same proficiency that the mage has, like, forces 3, one specific use of a sphere...?

    And, there's another part here as well, which says "Some use it to recover the magics tied to their mortal bloodlines". Does it mean a Proxy can use this to regain access to his full magic power?

    What do you think?

          The recipient will get access to that Arcanum. It's up to the ST to decide whether all dots or just one dot is transferred. The problem, though, is that the Vampire/ghoul has no Gnosis or Wisdom. Without Gnosis they lose ~half the Mage's base dice pool, can't use any Yantras to boost their dice pool (since the number of Yantras are determined by Gnosis), and they've already used one Willpower point in order to cast the spell so they can't boost the dice pool through Willpower either. And without Wisdom they can't contain Paradox at all.

          Edit: Do you mean steal a Rote/Praxes, or stealing the active effect of a spell? In the first case you wouldn't ever be able to cast the spell since you don't have a dice pool at all, and in the second case you're stealing a magical effect, not an ability.
