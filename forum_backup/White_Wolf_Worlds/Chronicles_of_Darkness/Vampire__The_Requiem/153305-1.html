        Here's one I came up with, I suspect others have toyed with similar ideas but here it is anyway.

        The Chevalier

        Parent Clan: Ventrue

        Bloodline Concept: Lineage of Kindred knights that populated the Old World

        No one knows where exactly in Europe the Chevalier originate or just how early the bloodline formed, but many Chevalier trace their lineages back proudly to various founders from mortal knight orders, often considering these 'founders' as those that branched off to spread the bloodline's legacy in more distant regions of the Old World (each branch with its own coat of arms of course, though all seem to feature wolves). Members of the bloodline are known to be very proud of their lineage and their status as Chevalier and have been known to get their hands dirty when necessary to prove their worth to rule. While much fewer in numbers in the New World the Chevalier have begun to spread never the less, the Invictus often welcoming the bloodline with open arms (whether they actually like the bloodline members or not).

        The Chevalier normally hold strongly to a twisted mockery of the chivalric ideals of the mortal knights of old. The exact nature of these ideals vary with the specific branch of the bloodline (as well as the ideals their sires feel are important).

        Covenants: The Invictus is by far the most common covenant the Chevalier belong in as the 'knightly' ideals that the Chevalier hold to so proudly often fit best with the First Estate. However the Lancea Sanctum does boast a noticeable number of Chevalier members if not Invictus Chevalier sympathetic to the Second Estate. Occasionally a few of their number have been found in the Ordo Dracul as well, its rigid structure and desire to advance further as a vampire appealing well to some members of the bloodline. Chevalier members of the Carthian Movement and Circle of the Crone however are all but unheard of.

        Disciplines: Animalism, Dominate, Resilience, Vigor

        Weakness: Everyone has their place in the world. The peasants serve their lords, their lords serve their dukes, and their dukes serve their king. The Chevalier's beast believes this more strongly than nearly any vampire's. In addition to the normal Ventrue clan weakness all Chevalier suffer a -2 penalty to all Predator's Taint checks. (Alternate idea was requiring 3 successes for all Predator's Taint checks, feedback and ideas on the weakness and the Bloodline are appreciated)

            Name: Windego



            Quote: It started as an act of desperation, but now I do it out of necessity.



            Bloodline Information: This bloodline traces its roots back to Native American folk lore, and appeared during times of great famine. The first European Windego were created when the Pilgrims came to the continent hoping to mine riches, only to begin starving to death when the continent bore none. The Windego devour the flesh of their prey to survive, but unlike the Nocktuku who prefer their fellow vampires, the Windego consume the flesh of humans. Whether the Nocktuku have any relation to the Windego remains unknown, for the opportunity to question either bloodline is scarce, as are those willing to sit down and talk with such creatures.

            According to the legend, those who consume human flesh will become a Windego, driven by an insatiable hunger to continue doing so until they are destroyed. In the past, Windego sightings were reported during times of famine and droubt, when the starving mortal population was desperate to maintain food supplies. Some broke under the pressure and began to eat their fellow humans to survive. The bloodline would seek out those who had performed this tabboo, and force the embrace upon them, thus reinforcing belief in these legends.

            As time passed, and the science of agriculture and food storage increased, resorting to cannibalism became less of an issue until finally the cravings were believed to be stamped out entirely. The Windego who survive to the modern nights tend to live out in the wilderness, on the borders of Indian reservations, or backwoods trails. Rarely, the Windego have been able to take up residence in densely populated areas, feeding upon the population in secret. These Man Eaters typically become associated with urban legends centered around their favorite hunting grounds.

            Cannibalism has become less of a necessity to survive in times of crisis, and as a result, this bloodline has dwindled in size. Some even believe the Windego are a dying breed who's cravings for human flesh, and the rarity of potential Childer has made it difficult to replenish their numbers. In nights past, they could feed more openly, when the settlers already expected the natives of being savages, and the natives feared their bloodline enough to avoid conflict with them. But now, their ancestral lands are over populated, and technology has advanced considerably. The government tends to take notice when people go missing, replaced by half eaten corpses in the middle of the night.


            Some Windego have migrated to distant lands and developing nations, who's technology to fight off widespread hunger is scarce. Rumors suggest they might be making a return to power in Africa and Canada, but nobody has as of yet had the patience or courage to investigate. However, as they are of Native American descent, many others could not bring themselves to abandon their ancestral homelands, and chose to stay, despite the looming threat of modern civilization.


            Parent Clan: Nosferatu



            Nickname: Man Eaters



            Covenant: The Windego rarely travel into civilized territories long enough to join in any of their politics, but they do have an attraction to certain religious factions. Possibly due to their ancestry being steeped in religious folk lore. Most of them, however, are Unbound, choosing to operate independently, where they can focus on remaining undetected.

            Some Windego can be found among the Circle of the Crone, who's pagan rituals coincide nicely with their wilderness habitats, and their practice of blood magic grants them easy access to food. Others find themselves joined with the Lancea Sanctum, punishing those who take human lives for their own benefit, including murderers and cannibals.

            The strongest concentration of religious Windego, however, can be found among Belial's Brood. Already having performed the atrocity of eating their fellow man, these Windego feel they have no lower depths to sink to, and give themselves over completely to demonic activity. These Windego are truly nightmarish beings who's signature methods of killing are reflected by urban legends and myths. Slenderman impaling bodies on tree branches, Shriekers attacking anybody who shines light upon them, or even the famous Predator hanging bodies upside down with their skin peeled off!



            Appearance: The Nosferatu have always had a reputation for being unnerving, but the Windego are nothing short of abhorrent to look upon. The stories depict them as being emaciated, as if they have not eaten in months. Their skin clings tightly to faintly visible bone structures, but this is the more human of their characteristics. The stories also depict a Windego as being an amalgamation of man and beast, possessing the claws and teeth of a wolf, the head of a deer, the torso of a man, and the legs of a goat. Finally, it also describes their bodies as having rotted away, such as the case when their front torso has no flesh, exposing organs for all to see! While this may be a traditional interpretation of the bloodline, it is not the only monstrous appearance they can manifest upon their embrace.

            The Windego's appearance seems to mock them for their taboo of consuming human flesh, almost nothing remains of their original human identities, a permanent reminder that they are no longer worthy to be thought of as humans.


            Haven: Only the most cunning Windego are free to operate in human society, most choose to live out in the wilds. Here, there is plenty of locations to dispose of their left overs, and tracking them down is a nightmare when they have so much territory to hide in. Any number of caves or abandoned cabins are possibly locations to hide out in, and any Nosferatu worth his Vitae is going to have modified their Haven with all manner of traps and secret passageways. It can take years of thorough searching to find their whereabouts.

            Windego often choose to live in solitude, unless they are mentoring a Childer. Feeding one of their kind is already risky enough, so feeding an entire warren is pushing their luck with a cattle prod. Typically, their Havens are located in territories where mortals are likely to roam, but not enough to draw attention. Nature trails and walkways are common selections, where they can abduct late night hikers and party goers, while disappearing into the wilderness before anybody knows what's happened.



            Background: In the old nights, the Windego would seek out those who had already committed the taboo of cannibalism and induct them into their bloodline. But as starvation became less and less of a problem, they are finding natural cannibals harder to come across. Some Windego adhere to the old ways, choosing to embrace campers and explorers who become lost in the wilderness. Others choose to take matters into their own hands and trick the mortals into eating human flesh without realizing the truth until it is too late. When they are reduced to their most desperate measures, the Windego decides to forgo tradition entirely and just outright embrace normal people. Doing this has lead to stories of mortals being "possessed" but a Windego spirit, instead of voluntarily choosing to eat humans.



            Bloodline Disciplines: Vigor, Celerity, Nightmare, Protean Expansion


            Protean Expansion - The signature discipline of the Windego is a very specialized form of Protean that relates to altering one's physical size. Stories of the Windego portray them as creatures who never stop growing, standing as tall as trees and swallowing men whole. If the Windego goes into torpor or sleeps through the day, their body will return to normal size, as if regenerating back to the state of embrace. The increase in their size also provides temporary Health boxes.

            Each level of Protean Expansion increases the Windego's size by 3 levels. Upon obtaining the 5th rank of mastery, the Windego can grow by 15 size ratings!


            Weakness: Blood alone is not enough to satisfy the Windego's appetites, they must consume the flesh of their prey as well, which will be converted into Vitae upon swallowing it. An ounce of human flesh restores 1 point of Vitae when consumed by a Windego. Because of their eating habits, they kill their victims almost every time they feed, and therefor covering up the evidence of their attacks is much more difficult than it is for normal vampires. Mortals tend to take notice when people go missing, or when their remains turn up later having been torn to shreds!



            Organization: Simply put, the Windego have no organization, just a shared culture. Feeding even a single Windego is a complicated thing to hide from mortal attention, so having a group of them gather together would have the government scouring the woods with armed forces. Windego Childer will remain with their sires until they have learned enough to survive on their own, and then are usually abandoned if they're not officially released.

            As a result, Windego place great stock on being self sufficient, and frown upon those who need to be coddled just to make it through another night. The preferred method for punishing a weak or cowardly Childe who can not handle the Requiem is to diablerize them. The Sire taking back the Vitae they wasted embracing the fool, as well as consuming their essence in the process!



            Concepts: Lost Hiker, Shipwreck Survivor, Famine Victim, Serial Killer, Inbred-Hillbilly Turned Psycho, Black Magic Shaman

                The Children of Jumlin.

                Based on the native American legend. The bloodline premise could be translated to any clan I suppose but it was originally designed for a gangrel character.

                You can look up the story of Jumlin if you like but a rough summary is:

                A medicine man desperate to have a child makes a pact with a dark spirit. The spirit possesses him. Jumlin gains an insatiable hunger. Starts having kids with all the women in the village and is unkillable.

                The Warriors go to a neighboring village to get a blessing from the medicine man to kill Jumlin but when they return he had fled and taken his children with him.

                As he raided villages he would leave behind one of his children as an orphan.

                These children were the Decendants and the first of the gangrel in North America. The bloodline itself has a much stronger connection to Jumlin and can see the desires of others.

                Basically the discipline I was working on allowed you the ability to see a person's vice, then use it against them socially, and at the highest level steal the willpower they would have gained for their vice for yourself.

                I never fully fleshed it out.

                Yes it sounds more like a Deva bloodline and it would suit them well. I honestly saw it as working for any clan but the characters had to have native American Heritage.

                The clan weakness was that they could never gain willpower from their virtue.

                                                            Name: Hannya



                                                            Quote: They say if you're not careful, your jealousy will make you into a monster...



                                                            Bloodline Information: Originating in Japan, the Hannya are described as being women who turned into demons after falling prey to jealousy and obsession. Many Japanese actors use a Hannya mask in their plays to depict such women who bare small horns, metallic eyes, sharp fangs and a wide mouth. The bloodline has no original founder that they can remember, leading to speculations that these Nosferatu first appeared as the result of a spontaneous Embrace, and gradually spread out from there.

                                                            The Hannya bloodline is exclusively female, and their faces often resemble the masks for which they are named after. Driven by obsession and jealousy, Hannya often seek fame and admiration, so they put on plays and other performances for the supernatural community, using Ghouls as their cast and crew. Thus leading to their monicker of Actors. While they often crave the admiration of the mortals, they are refreshingly sane enough to know exposing themselves would only lead to problems.

                                                            When dealing with others, the Hannya tend to be fiery tempered and even violent as they lash out in bitterness. But when they feel they have some privacy, they turn sorrowful and tormented, revealing that their anger is just a mask to hide their true feelings of regret and loneliness.


                                                            Parent Clan: Nosferatu + Daeva



                                                            Nickname: Actors



                                                            Covenant: Being of Japanese descent, western religion holds very little interest for these Asian kindred. Most who are looking for spirituality join the Circle of the Crone, who's pagan beliefs match perfectly with the oriental belief in spirits, demons, and multiple deities. Those who do join with the Lancea Sanctum, however, are very fond of preying upon those who share the Vice of Envy. It is not uncommon for them to embrace women who possess this Vice, but they typically just spook or terrorize any males. Others hope that they can learn to overcome both their curse and the jealous impulses that drive them, and so they join the Ordo Dracul, taking up the quest for enlightenment and perfection.

                                                            Other Hannya feel at home in both the Invictus and the Carthian Movement. Those who join the Invictus strive to rise to the top of the organization, where flaunt their wealth, fame and status to other kindred to appease their ego. Those who join the Carthians take pride in their ability to sway the hearts and minds of others to willingly side with them, and consider it a badge of honor when their side of the argument is declared the victor.



                                                            Appearance: While the body of a Hannya can vary in its looks, the face of a Hannya tends to resemble the famous Noh masks that are commonly seen in Japanese plays. The masks come in many styles, but the most iconic appearance depicts a creature with a small pair of horns, a wide mouth, long fangs, and metallic colored eyes. Sometimes their fangs are straight, and other times they curve sideways. Because of the width of their mouths and the size of their teeth, they sometimes have trouble closing their mouths all the way, making them look as if they are pulled into a snarl, exposing their fangs in the process.
                                                            Name: Hannya




                                                            Haven: Theatres are a popular location for the Hannya to make their living arrangements. Because they work as actors, they love having a private stage readily available. In fact, many Hannya actually purchase the theatre, so they don't have to share the stage with other acting troupes, and can schedule their plays whenever they wish. It is not uncommon for performance stages to have secret compartments hidden underneath it, and these hide aways can be easily modified into a sunproof room for them to sleep through the day and emerge from at night. These havens are also easy to modify with various defense mechanisms including animatronics, trap doors, smoke and mirrors, and (for the especially brave Actor) pyrotechnics. What seems like harmless props during a late night show can become deadly traps to uninvited guests trying to force their way into the haven.



                                                            Background: The stereotypical Hannya is a woman who was obsessive and jealous in life and became a demon as a result. But this is not always the case. Sometimes the Hannya will embrace a woman who they feel envy towards, and consequently the new childe will become envious as well, when they struggle to maintain what was forcefully taken from them. Hannya are typically embraced from artists, dancers, and actors who win the hearts and minds of the crowd. Those who become embraced often meet such a fate after they have been driven to a crime of passion and jealousy. Perhaps a better artist comes along and people begin to think the Hannya's style has grown stale over the years.

                                                            Maybe they are delegated to acting as a stand in for the real star of the show, and orchestrate an accident. Heck, maybe the passing of time has caused their once youthful looks to fade and they are no longer the sex symbol they used to be; taking out their frustration on the younger and prettier actresses. Because of their past, they tend to place a higher focus on Social and Mental skills, using their talents to manipulate the crowds, and outwit their enemies. Physical talents are usually a last resort for these jealous women, to be used when everything turns to shit and they need to quickly put an end to the problem.

                                                            The vast majority of Hannya are of Asian ancestry, as Japan is the country of their origin. Western Hannya are extremely rare, and those who do join the bloodline are generally tourists, who had the misfortune of being targeted while on vacation.




                                                            Bloodline Disciplines: Nightmare, Celerity, Vigor, Majesty,



                                                            Weakness: The Hannya are consumed with jealousy and obsession either before or after their embrace, sometimes both. Their transformation into ugly demons only adds more fuel to their fire. Upon joining this bloodline, Nosferatu members also gain the Daeva weakness (spending 2 willpower to resist their Vices), and the Daeva members gain the Nosferatu weakness (10 again does not apply to Presence or Manipulation rolls and 1's subtract from successes. Excluding rolls that involve Intimidate or Composure).



                                                            Organization: Truth be told, the Hannya don't like to live in groups of their own kind. They prefer to be the star of the show as much as possible. When two or more Hannya operate in the same location, it results in competition between them as they battle for control and popularity. A Hannya will keep their childe around long enough to educate them on their ways, but generally dismisses them once they feel they have learned all they need to.

                                                            When a Hannya isn't traveling with a coterie, they prefer to keep a presence of Ghouls on hand. In addition to providing security for the Hannya, the Ghouls also work as their acting troupe and stage hands, and the blood bonds formed out of drinking their mistress' vitae causes them to worship and adore her, just the way the Hannya likes it.



                                                            Concepts: B-list Celebrity, Competitive Dance Instructor, Scorned Lover Turned Killer, Struggling Playwrite, Starving Artist, Vindictive Harpy, Accident Organizer
