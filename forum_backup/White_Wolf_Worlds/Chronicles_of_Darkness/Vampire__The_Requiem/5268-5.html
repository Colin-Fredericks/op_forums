                Alright, here we go. Assamites all over the place!

                Caveat: A lot of ink has been spilled over the years over the Assamite castes. The Viziers and Sorcerers are often conflated, and for good reason. Personally, I'd simplify those distinctions in a Translation Guide game, and just merge the intellectual castes into a single Sorcerer caste, and just call them Viziers as a nickname. The Viziers always seemed superfluous to me.

                Assamite Viziers

                Nicknames: Scholars, Artisans

                Disciplines: Auspex, Celerity, Quietus

                Clan Bane (The Pedagogic Curse): The Beasts of Viziers demand thoroughness in all things. Note the Scholar's highest Mental Skill. Whenever she gains an exceptional success using that Skill, including with disciplines, roll her Humanity. On failure, the Assamite gains the Persistent Obsession Condition in association with that Skill. For example, if the Skill were Academics, and the character were conducting research, she might become obsessed with finding every piece of information she can on the subject. If the character ever gains more dots in a different Mental Skill, the Obsession trigger shifts.

                Favored Attributes: Composure or Intelligence



                Assamite Sorcerers

                Nicknames: Magi, Witches

                Disciplines: Auspex, Obfuscate, Quietus

                Clan Bane (The Mystic Curse): Assamite sorcerers are beings bred of magic, and they cannot hide that. The Predatory Aura of a Magus is exceptionally obvious and occult. So blatant is her nature that whenever she engages in a Predatory Aura contest with a mortal, her dice pool is limited by her Humanity, as the opposition is easily able to size her up. If the mortal succeeds in contesting her, the Sorcerer gains the Condition she was attempting to impose. In addition, when attempting to track the character via indirect clues (Vampire: The Requiem, p. 138) mortals do not subtract the character's dots in Obfuscate from their dice pools. This bane does not apply to interactions with other Kindred.

                Favored Attributes: Intelligence or Resolve

                Special: Assamite Sorcerers have access to the blood magic known as Dur-An-Ki.

                So, I kinda split the difference with the Sorcerers. They've had Auspex or Obfuscate depending on the edition, but because Requiem's paradigm doesn't tend to give out blood sorcery as clan disciplines, I've just given them both. I'm not sure if I like the Bane I came up with, but the Classic version is so weird and circumstantial, and it made sense to tie it into Predatory Aura.

                        Time for some deep cuts.


                        Nagaraja

                        Nicknames: Serpent Kings, Flesh-Eaters

                        Disciplines: Auspex, Dominate, Protean

                        Clan Bane (The Cannibal Curse): The Beasts of Nagaraja are depraved and debased. In order to feed, the Serpent King must consume flesh along with blood. When feeding, she gains no nourishment from the first few Vitae taken, equal to 10 – Humanity, unless she first eats a piece of her victim's flesh, inflicting two points of lethal damage. The Kiss of a Nagaraja always causes the Scarred Condition in mortals. This bane does not apply to feeding from other Kindred until Blood Potency 6.

                        Favored Attributes: Resolve or Stamina

                        Special: Members of Clan Nagaraja have access to Necromancy as an out-of-clan discipline.


                        The mechanics for the bane here seemed pretty obvious to me, especially in the sense that they don't make them utterly unplayable. Originally I thought I might make it so they had to inflict 10 - Humanity points of lethal damage, but that means that even at Humanity 6, they're killing almost every victim, and breaching the Masquerade like it's going out of style.

                        I had a bit of trouble coming up with a third discipline, since if we're holding true to our pattern, Necromancy is out. I chose Protean because they have horrible serpent tooth-filled maws in cWoD, but I didn't want to pile onto their bane. Plus, I always wanted them to be able to do snake stuff, a la the Setites. If you don't like that particular twist on them, I'd give them Resilience instead.


                        True Brujah

                        Nicknames: Elois, Sages

                        Disciplines: Majesty, Praestantia, Vigor

                        Clan Bane (The Clinical Curse): Where the Brujah run hot, the True Brujah run cold. When interacting with others, treat the True Brujah's Humanity one dot lower than usual for determining Social penalties, and treat any Empathy failures as dramatic failures. This bane applies to all mortals, including Touchstones, and other vampires.

                        Favored Attributes: Presence or Strength

                        Not sure about this bane. It's modeled too much on Nosferatu, I think. I was tempted to just give them the new Ventrue bane, but that's not quite the archetype. A Ventrue feels like everyone around her is a pawn; a Trujah doesn't feel anything at all.

                        Suggestions?

                        Oh, and why not Temporis? Because ehhhhhhhh. It's complicated and tone breaking, even more so in Requiem than Masquerade. Plus, you could just rename Praestantia Temporis, if that suits you. PenDragon has a very good Second Edition conversion linked in the homebrew thread.
