    Not So Frequently Asked Questions!

    11-20-2014, 07:50 PM
    Okay, so Ghouls are counted as having a Blood Potency of 0. I am curious, does this allow them to "feed" on animals as a source of Vitae?

    Also, Nossie deformities, for those of us going at it old school. Do they only manifest when the Lonely Curse sets in, i.e. when the Haunt reaches Humanity 6? Or are they present from embrace, just without the mechanical effects until they degenerate accordingly?

    When a Kindred riding the wave bites a grappled opponent, can they opt not to take the automatic blood point since they are more in control of the Frenzy?

    I'll probably have more to add to this thread over time, but these will do for now. Thanks in advance, developers!

      1) Ghouls cannot feed, they require kindred to provide Vitae for them.

      2) Reasonably speaking yes, as the penalties thematically arise from the deformity. I would, however, give them a light version of it even at high humanity and never allow them to take the higher version of Striking Looks.

      3) A vampire riding the wave is still barely in control, they can provide direction but that level of restraint shouldn't be possible in frenzy.

                                  Personally, I would say if you want to go the "all nos are deformed" route, I would say more power to you, but that the mechanical effect of the clan bane is caused by something other than physical appearance. They may be disfigured and terrifying from the moment of embrace, but that alone doesn't cause them to count their Humanity lower for the sake of interactions with mortals. It may or may not add other penalties and bonuses to certain rolls, Nos clanbook style, at ST discretion, but that's purely the result of their physical appearance. When their humanity drops and their bane kicks in, that's something beyond just their looks; something altogether more supernatural, that causes them to make mortals uncomfortable in a way their deformities alone never could. Almost like they can't fully control their predatory aura and they're always giving off a low-level sign that "I'm not human and you should fear me".
