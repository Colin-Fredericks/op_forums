                        The Status Quo

                        So, when last we met the members of the Carthian uprising are feeling pretty good about themselves. They believe that their leader Broom (nosferatu, carthian, older ancilla) has succeeded in the destruction of both the Prince and Michael Seville (nosferatu priscus.)

                        Meanwhile, Pilgrim (gangrel priscus, elder, archbishop) has seized the inner harbor (kiddie pool) and locust point. This makes it harder for the carthians from across the harbor to operate in the city, but it also makes it harder for us.

                        Finally, Christina has accepted Broom’s offer to act as Master of Elysium. This is a dangerous game she is playing.


                        Canadian Romance

                        Myself, Alice and our entourage have been off the grid for awhile. We have been moving between temporary havens while picking off whatever Carthians we can find. We have even travelled all the way the far northeast side of the city; where the interlopers probably feel the most safe and destroyed several vampires there. We hope this has struck fear in their hearts.

                        So, one night we return to our temporary haven to find Tigger (ghoul of Terry the mekhet) waiting nearby. She tells me that Terry is being chased by Carthians and points us in the right direction. Alice and I get moving and when we catch up we see the following:

                        Terry the mekhet, Kat (gangrel, my vassal) and few of their ghouls are cornered near a self serve carwash by Jonathan Poole, and unknown vampire and several ghouls. I know exactly when they are up to:

                        Jonathan Poole, who has failed to get any girls to like him with his white knight routine, has been trying to force Kat into a blood bond. He already succeeded in giving her the first taste via trickery when he hired someone to shoot him in the back while he was talking to Kat and his blood splattered all over her face. Anyway, apparently he has given up on subtlety.

                        Alice and I attack from Obfuscate.


                        Jonathan Poole: “You!! Why must you always interfere!?!”

                        The fight was not one sided, but we did prevail. We are preparing to stake Poole so we can take him to a secondary location for destruction when two SUVs roll up and several vampires disembark. Alice and I have already begun moving into the carwash in preparation for our patented Obfuscate and get the fuck out of there maneuver when we hear the voice of Roy (ventrue, Baron of the NW side of Baltimore.) We decide not to rabbit out of there and wait to see what happens.

                        So, Roy wants to know wtf is going on. We were under the impression that this was the barrens between the territory of Roy and Baltimore proper. Apparently Roy has expanded his territory all the way to the borders of Baltimore.

                        Anyway, we tell Roy that Kat is our vassal and that Poole, who has sworn loyalty to a false prince has tried to capture her and force her into a blood bond.

                        Johnathan: “You need to be careful, all of you. I work for Broom, the Prince of Baltimore. He will not be happy to hear how you are treating me.”

                        Roy wants to know why he should bother saving Johnathan, and Johnathan offers a lifedebt to Roy. Well Roy looks like he is very happy to accrue a lifedebt. However, Katherine (one of Roy’s vassals) was present at the Elysium where Poole refused to repay boons he owed to the ghoul of Patricia Mason. So, with this information Roy is not inclined to accept Poole’s lifedebt.

                        Roy: “Your credit is no good.”

                        We take Johnathan Poole. He is never seen or heard from again.


                        Out from the Cold

                        Alice and I have had enough of the off grid life. We decide to return to the city and reclaim our haven and territory. We call in a few favors and have our haven thouroughly searched for explosives, listening devices, magic naughtiness, or traps of any kind. From there, we went to visit Michael Seville in the halls of the dead.

                        We travelled there in the usual way, and when we arrived Sam Bast (nosferatu, carthian, neonate) informed us that Seville has had no visitors and we should feel free to enter his office.

                        Sam Bast: “He will probably be glad to have company.”

                        So, we were quite surprised when we walked into the office of Seville to discover that he is not alone. Also present are Peter (mekhet, elder, invictus, Alice’s grandsire) Helen (mekhet, circle, archon) and Simon (mekhet, lance, archon.)

                        Peter: “I cam here personally to tell you this so that you would have the maximum amount of time to make your decision…. Felix, I’m glad you’re here. I want you to deliver a message to your family. It is entirely up to you which member or members of your family you tell, but I don’t think it will go over well if delivered personally.”

                        Peter: “I think the charade has gone on long enough. Neither Septimus, nor his poltical position are strong enough challenge me. If he does not give up his ambitions for the throne, I will kill him.”

                        Peter and his companions leave. We see the surprised face of Sam Bast before the door closes again. First, Seville wants to know if we are present here by coincidence or if it was planned ahead with Peter for us to arrive simultaneously.We assure him that we did not know Peter was going to be here.

                        I was a bit distracted during the conversation because of the threat made by Peter. What can I do if Alice’s grandsire and my uncle make war with each other. It would be ugly. Anyway, discuss the situation with Seville.He is certainly not interested in a Baltimore with Prince Septimus. Why? Because Seville hates the church, and Septimus will need to be propped up by Pilgrim to hold the throne. I doubt Pilgrim is doing this for wholly altruistic reasons.

                        I wound up giving this information to my aunt Octavia and grandsire Konrad VonBerg. Konrad forbade us from telling Septimus. Hopefully he and Octavia will know what to do.


                        A Warning

                        At the beginnig of Broom’s ‘rule’ Dorris Bettes approached me and asked for my help. She was planning to insinuate herself into Broom’s administration and feed information back to the loyalists, but she was worried that she would be compelled by enemy Ventrue to out herself. So, she asked me to protect her from such a fate. (We are using 1st ed rules. If I compell her to do something small on a daily basis then another Dominate user would have to beat my successes) So, I have made Dorris to count sheep in her head every morning as she goes to sleep. I will have to visit her periodically to refresh this compulsion. Hopefully she will be a good enough actress when and if some other ventrue tries to Dominate her.

                        So, one night Alice and I travel into Carthian territory to find Dorris. We have really scary levels of Obfuscate at our disposal which we use to snatch Dorris and take her to a secure location. During her window of full memory lucidity, she tells us that Broom and his allies have been constructing a highly secure communal haven and it is almost finished. If all the key members of his faction are so well guarded by day, it will be harder for us to retake the city…

                        We return Dorris to where we found her, both geographically and mentally.

                        There Are Four Lights!

                            So, after last session we as a group decided that it was time to wrap things up. We have been playing this game on and off for 5 years now. The Kiddie Pool Saga is coming to a close.


                            Wrapping Things Up

                            I apologize that I will not be going into as much detail as I usually do, but here is a sysnopsis of how the game ended.
                            • As we were preparing to sabotage Broom’s secure communal haven, the city was invaded by multiple coteries of invaders from Philadelphia.
                            • The Prince made the call that cannot be ignored. All the mekhet of the city (and one venture were gathered. Along with most of the combat capable kindred of the city, we traveled to Philadelphia. We were met there by coteries from Brooklyn led by my great-uncle Adhamar.
                            • The Prince wasn’t lying when in an earlier chapter he claimed to know the resting place of the Prince of Philadelphia. He personally led Alice and I along with the other mekhet to destroy the Prince of Philadelphia and his inner circle. My great-uncle Adhamar (brother to Konrad Von Berg) is the new Prince of Philadelphia. New York, Philadelphia, and Baltimore are all led by Invictus Princes and are now more closely linked than ever.
                            • No prisoners are taken. Any Philly vamps we catch are destroyed as well. Majesty is used to call back those who think they have escaped. I have no doubt there will be stragglers, not to mention the coteries still in Baltimore.
                            • Philadelphia is no longer a threat. Upon our return, Michael Seville comes out of hiding and destroys Broom. There is much wailing and gnashing of teeth as the Carthians of the Broom administration realize they have been duped. Neither the Prince, nor the Nosferatu Priscus were defeated.
                            • Alice and Felix, along with the rest of the mekhet and the city begin to purge those who have sworn their loyalty to a false Prince.
                            • During these chaotic nights, the ST forces me to make a difficult choice. Both Antony (adopted childe of Octavia) and Darren Soderberg (childe of Septimus) are under siege by enemy forces. We must choose who we will assist. We saved Darren. Blood is thicker than adoption papers.
                            • As things cool down, we discover that Reaper (daeva, former hound) has been taking advantage of Alice’s and my busy schedule to move into our territory. It is one thing to try to take what is ours, but to do it when the city is under siege is unforgivable. We hold Reaper in the highest contempt. Had the game continued, we would have made his unlife miserable.
                            • We did conclude the map quest. Final map clue lead to werewolf territory. We chose neither stealth nor combat to get the last piece of the puzzle. Instead, we went with diplomacy. We used our history with one of the werewolf packs to open dialogue and eventually get our hands on the final piece of the puzzle.
                            • Each map led us to a piece of a compass which when assembled did not point north. Instead, it lead us to some kind of magic item which allowed the holder to use auspex powers on other members of their blood lines from a great distance.
                            • Neither Konrad nor Octavia are able to convince Septimus that a seat on the Primogen council is good enough. He will not back down from his bid for the throne. Peter challenges him to a duel. Septimus accepts. Peter wins. Septimus dies.
                            • We had been grooming Lars Bjornson for Michael Seville for some time now. We decided the best way to prepare him for unlife in the city would be to drag him around with us on our super dangerous adventures and see if he lives. He did. So we reported to Seville that he was ready. All this time, we had assumed that he would be made nosferatu. Instead, he is embraced by Vicky (venture, childe of Adhamar she has appeared in the game way back in part 1) using the indulgence to sire which we had transported to New York for Seville so long ago. Adhamar now has a childe and a grand-childe in Baltimore, and Seville has seriously altered the family dynamic of House Von Berg.
                            • Having completed the map quest, Alice and I are able to have an audience with the Prince. For real. No obfuscate disguises. By completing the various trials required to put together the compass and retrieve the artifact we have demonstrated beyond a shadow of a doubt that we are worthy kindred. We have a conversation with the Prince in which many of the truths of the city are revealed.
                            • One of the big questions we wanted answered was why Michael Seville was allowed so much power and influence in the city. The Prince tells us essentially that he would not tolerate anyone else so close to his equal in the city. However, he and Seville have known each other since they were fledglings. They are true friends.
                            • Now that the city is secure, the Prince finally steps aside and prepares for a well deserved rest, but not before he makes his childe Peter the Prince of the city. Peter and his sire form a dynastic house and Peter gains the merit ‘speaker for the eclipsed.” Long may He reign.


                            We are members of the inner circle now, as much elevated over most of the citizens of the city as they are above the residents of the Quarantine Zone.

                            The End.

                            There Are Four Lights!

                                                    The Prince did not have a casting photo, but was described as being similar in appearance to Peter. The Prince was Peters father both in life, and in unlife. As the game's theme was 'family,' I feel this was appropriate.

                                                    One last thing I want to mention about the game before I put it to rest: A lot of people over the years have wondered why it is that Felix was allowed to be so naughty and get with so much misbehavior in the city. I asked the ST and he said basically "You drank the blood of the Prince's bloodline, Self identified as an unofficial mekhet of the city. You asked to be judged by their standards, submitted to regular telepathic surveillance, and fought against/killed the enemies of the Prince on a regular basis. So yeah... you had leeway."
