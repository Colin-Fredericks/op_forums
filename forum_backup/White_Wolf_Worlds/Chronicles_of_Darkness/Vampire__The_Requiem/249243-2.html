                    Aww, such praise. I'm blushing here.
                    Bring us a classy and aristocratic use of Subvolo merged with Resilience! The obvious choice is creating a shield, but that feel a lot like telekinesis...
                    How can I turn down a request like that?

                    Inviolate Air (Subvolo ••••, Resilience •••)
                    Cost: 1 Vitae
                    Dice Pool: Dexterity + Athletics + Subvolo
                    Action: Reflexive
                    Duration: [Successes] Actions

                    Slings and arrows cannot mar the sky, why so should they harm the crow? With a wave of his hand the vampire dismisses all ranged attacks. Any small projectile (size 0-3) that comes within a few yards of the vampire grinds to a halt, left to drift harmlessly in the air. Attacks performed in extreme close combat and projectiles with internal propulsion (e.g. rockets) are unaffected. All other ranged attacks fail completely, and will do no harm to those within the vampire's protective bubble.
                    This Devotion Costs 3 Experiences to Learn.

                    Also added to the doc.

                    Edit: I'm feeling inspired, have three more Devotions:
                    Silent Embrace (Subvolo ••••, Obfuscate •••)
                    Cost: None

                    Like the wings of an owl subtle forces steal away sound, silencing victims for their sojourn to the clouds. With this devotion a vampire can implement Obfuscate remotely, anyone she could nab with The Gentle Talon is a viable target. Similarly, assuming her blood potency allows it, she may use the Gentle Talon and Touch of Shadow as one combined action, silencing and snatching simultaneously.
                    This Devotion costs 3 Experiences to Learn.

                    Ascendant Familiar (Subvolo •••, Animalism ••)
                    Cost: +1 Vitae, Enhances Raise the Familiar

                    Logic would dictate that Subvolo masters would select similarly exalted companions, but that needn't be so. With the blessing of the crow any beast may stalk the sky. By spending an additional point of Vitae while creating a familiar the vampire blesses it with the gift of Subvolo. Hence forward the familiar can fly, constantly affected by Weightless Step and enhanced by On Wings of Shadow.
                    This Devotion costs 2 Experiences to Learn.

                    Firmament Reign (Subvolo •••••, Animalism ••••• or Obfuscate •••••)
                    Cost: None

                    It is said the first crows made their havens on mountain peaks and atop towering trees, removed from earthly concerns and furnished with stolen finery. The truth of these tales is suspect, but the possibility is there. With this devotion the vampire extends the powers of subvolo across his entire domain, anywhere he has claimed through Lord of the Land or Oubliette. Within his powers are enduring, lasting indefinitely so long as the territory is his. Moreover all powers of Subvolo come more easily, enjoying a 1 Vitae discount to activation (minimum 0).
                    This Devotion costs 5 Experiences to Learn.

                                Feedback like this is just so heartening. Have another devotion
                                The Silken Hand (Subvolo ••, Majesty ••)*
                                Cost: 1 Vitae
                                Dice Pool: Presence + Expression + Subvolo vs Composure + Supernatural Advantage
                                Action: Instant

                                The grip of Subvolo is one of terrible, stomach pitting helplessness. But mix in a Daeva's saccharine smile and that weightlessness becomes euphoric. Her attention gripped with a simple gesture, the victim is drawn in, sliding weightlessly (as though affected by Weightless Step) to the vampire's side. She gains the Charmed Condition, and a small degree of Subvolo's power. So long as his victim remains Charmed the vampire may carry her as though weightless, and share the effects of Weightless Step and On Wings of Shadow while in physical contact.
                                This Devotion Costs 2 Experiences to Learn
                                *A nearly identical version of this Devotion exists, named The Iron Hand. Adapted by Ventrue it requires one dot of Dominate instead of two Majesty, inflicts the Mesmerized Condition, and uses a dice pool of Intelligence + Intimidation + Subvolo.
                                As for a Bloodline, the fluff of Subvolo is that it belonged to a lost clan, the Kindred of the Sky, who may or may not have become strix. There's clearly demand for something, perhaps the Kindred of the Sky deserve a full write-up...
