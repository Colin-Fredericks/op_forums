    Originally posted by Marcus View Post
    Talking about just numbers I think the situation is: (BP 5 for vamp. and equivalent for Werewolf...at greater BP the balance hangs for the vampire I suppose)

    (I took it from an old topic)

    "Werewolf in Gauru attack pool: 5 Str + 3 (Gauru) + 2 (Merits that increase Strength) + 10 (for Crimson Spasm - I made a roll with 18 dice and dobled the successes) + 5 (Gift of Strength) + 5 Brawl -1 (Relentless Assault to gain +1 damage) = 29 dice, with 8-again from Killer Instinct (1st dot Rahu Auspice Gift)
    Werewolf in Gauru damage pool: 2 (teeth) + 1 (relentless assualt) + 1 (living weapon) + 5 (slaughterer) = + 9 lethal
    Werewolf Defense: 9 (Dex/Wits + Athletics)
    Werewolf Health: 5 (size) + 5 (Stamina) + 4 (Gauru) + 1 (Favored Form) + 5 (Warrior's Hide - 2nd dot Rahu Auspice Gift) = 20

    Vampire attack pool: 5 Str + 5 (Frenzy) + 5 (Passive Vigor) + 6 (3 vitae) + 5 Brawl = 26
    Vampire damage pool: +0 agg (claws of the unholy) + 5 (Active Vigor) = + 5 agg OR +2agg (feeding the Crone) + 5 (Active Vigor) = 7 agg
    Vampire defense: 9 (Gauru prevents you from adding any skill to defense so just Dex/Wits + Celerity) but with Coil of the Wyrm 4 add 5 for BP and rise defense to 14
    Vampire Health: 5 (size) + 5 (Stamina) + 5 (Resilience) + 5 BP (Coil of the wyrm 4) = 20

    I've ignored the Efficient Killer merit because I think the drawback ("This Merit only works on living targets with discernible weak points") rules out Vampires (if there's something to contradict this I'd love to know)."

    So the pools are 2-3 dice lower (the 8 again can be compensated from 5 more defense) and this not sound to me as an impossible match for the vamp.


    I have to say this REALLY undersells the Garou. First and Foremost, you ignored the All-out attack bonus for a Werewolf who has relentless assault, that is another +2 dice. The second thing is totem bonuses, but this isn't really a issue as the werewolf may have a non combat focused totem.

    To get to vigor 5 as a Gangrel, you would need to throw down 20 XP. Celerity 20 XP Resilience and Protean 18, assuming one of these starts at 3 dots. 1 for Unholy claws. Coil of the Wyrm 16. BP 25 in order to be at BP in order to make him more combat effective as it were.

    The above Werewolf we will say is a Blood talon Rahu. He starts with 2 purity, one glory. His starting gifts are Slaughterer and Primal Strength. To raise his glory and Purity to 5, the base for this build and how he unlocks most of the gifts we need to spend 21 XP. 15 on Merits for favored form and relentless assault Period. As far as supernatural talents goes, that means the vampire is over an astounding 39 XP. 49, if you drop the Primal Urge down by 2, as he does not need to be that high of a Primal Urge to be combat effective like the vampire in frenzy does. That gives us a lot of room to maneuver this Werewolf into an even more powerful position.

    So based off of your formula, I will leave the Werewolf at Primal Urge of 5, but leaves him with 39 XP to dole out.

    At Purity 5, This Werewolf has the following gifts:

    Full Moon: 5
    Primal Strength
    Slaughterer
    Hit and Run(5 XP)

    At Glory 5, the Werewolf has the following gifts:

    Berserkers Might: (Agg damage to your claws is reduced to your successes)
    Fearless Hunter
    Black Earth, Red Hunger
    Predators Unmatched Pursuit

    At Honor 5(15 XP), the Werewolf has the following gifts:
    Perfected Rage
    Crushing Blow
    Lay Low the Challenger
    Knotted Paths
    Deny Everything

    At Cunning 5 (15XP), The Werewolf has the following gifts
    Unchained
    Incite Fury
    Natures Lure
    Feet of Mist
    Primal Allure.


    Now you have a werewolf able to soak damage, dodge with Hit and Run(Similar to celerity but unlimited.) When he hits he activates crushing blow, slaughterer, and lay low the challenger. Your Kindred now sufers -5 defense, -2 to physical rolls versus the werewolf without the expenditure of willpower, and takes a significant more amount of weapon damage then you are doling out. And We will just throw your 5 successes from crimson Spasm onto lethal damage instead. The Dice pool is big enough to gaurantee a hit, a +5L to increase the existing dicepool to +14 makes it a wonderful little boost in damage.

                                                One thing that's always overlooked is teh "Feed" move. If a vamp gets a dramatic success in a grapple - not that hard with all the successes flying about from frenzy/blood boosting etc - he can choose both damage move and feed move. This adds an effective blood potency in successes damage to each successful grapple - and allows the vampire to recharge during combat - something not as easy for the wolf.

                                                It makes topping out in blood-boosting for a grapple really attractive; even at BP3, the vamp can add 6 dice (nine in frenzy) to any grapple pool; if any of that gives a dramatic success, the move become self-sustaining. Perhaps not enough to make sure the wolf is going down in death rage - but then again, if your ghouls/animalism summoned hoard/other minions are helping soften it up with bullets, it can help.

                                                Failing that, what is wrong with a silver bullet? Doesn't everyone know that's how to kill a werewolf? With celerity to keep out of range?
