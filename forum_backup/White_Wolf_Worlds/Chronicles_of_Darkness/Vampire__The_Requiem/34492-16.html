            234) Mostly cribbed from Inception
            A Bishop of the Lance has been having terrible dreams during his daysleep. These are not the normal torpor nightmares. He dreams of a series of twisting, sulfurous caverns, populated by creatures that could only be called demons. He seeks to escape but the path seems to be shaping itself as he travels, taking him to deeper and darker torments. He wakes at sundown, covered in blood sweat, and remembering the horrors in exacting detail. The Bishop knows his lore well and believes his nightmares are actually visions into one of the realms beyond this mortal one.
            He has prayed nightly, castigating himself with blades and fire, calling on God and Loginous to free him. It seems his prayers were answered last week. In a fit of inspiration (he would say divine inspiration) the Bishop discovered a magical rite to let a small number of Kindred open the door to the realm he sees.
            Of course he can’t turn to other members of the Lance, treasonous snakes that they are. They’re just as likely to find a way to end his unlife there and declare themselves bishops. An coterie outside of the church’s hierarchy is what he needs.
            And of course he has no idea what the coterie should do if this planewalking actually works.
            (Maybe bring a mortal along, find a tower in the middle of the realm and write their name on it?)

                    237) Over the last several weeks, you've noticed some disturbing patterns of late. The Gangrel seem to be appearing within the city limits with increasing frequency. Nosferatu have begun to set up shop in more publicly accessible areas. Daeva have reluctantly sworn vows of chastity, while Ventrue seem to be donating large tracts of territory and finances towards more charitable enterprises. Even the Mekhet seem to be actively avoiding the shadows of late. Someone, or something, has the clans of the city frightened away from their typical M.O's and nobody seems exactly clear on what's causing it. Scrounging together a coterie, you decide to do your own investigation into the matter. One can only imagine the level of prestige you'll gain by putting the fears of not one, but all five clans to rest!

                                        243) You hear about the Nosferatu primogen’s secret herd? They’re LARPERS! The Harpy’s childe’s roommate’s ghoul saw him at a D&D event “dressed up” like a rotting lich. Everyone apparently loves the effort he puts into his cosmetics.
                                        Of course, now every Nos and even some Kindred from other clans are starting their own LARP groups. I get it. You want to let the mask down and maybe even get some compliments on how ugly you are. Maybe even show your fangs and hear the Kine go “oooohhh” without running away in terror. How long do you think the Prince is going to sit on this before putting her foot down.

                                        244) Welp, the LARPERS are hunting us now. Apparently these nerds aren’t as blind as some of us would have liked. First it was just the people who were making them their herds. Good riddance. Saves the Hound the effort of hunting them down and bringing them to the Prince for violating the Masquerade.
                                        Then they starting hitting everyone else. You know that elder with the tattoos? Right, Rodrigo. He was out with his ghoul, Cynthia, doing whatever it is elders do, when these teenagers, fucking teenagers mind you, bust swords and axes out of their backpacks and start hacking at him. He had his own hidden in his cane, and Cynthia said he fought hard, and he was a swordsman back when that title meant something, but she saw him get his head cut off before running as fast as the blood would take her.
                                        I’ve heard some rumors here and there that other Kindred are ending up meeting the end. Apparently all that time playing with toy swords is pretty good training for the real thing.

                                                    It’s heinous. It’s evil. But there’s a nomad passing through who talks about Many, a Kindred living out in the wilderness. Many spoke with the nomad for several nights, regarding diablerie and how with the proper purity of the soul it can be a joining instead of consumption. Ascension instead of Dissolution.                                                         247) An unpopular, but canny, Nosferatu has managed to get the Invictus to grant him multiple room-sized territories in several hotels, motels, and ghetto apartments. Since none of the buildings themselves fall under specific Invictus control, everybody thought he was just setting up a series of saferooms and backup havens. In fact, he's started a business venture; vampire fast food, for the rich, the desperate, or the lazy.

                                                        All the rooms have been soundproofed, their floors and furniture are either wipedown-ready or instantly replacable, cellphone jammers have been installed, and all the entrances/exits and LOS are compromised or favor the intruder. The landlords are Dominated into placing the most disappear-able guests into those rooms. For a small(ish) fee, a vampire is given access for an hour. For a large fee, a vampire is given until sunrise.

                                                        The Invictus is split on whether to knock the venture down to discourage future smartasses from exploiting fealty boundaries, or to snatch up the idea and just franchise the little turd out of existence.

                                                        The Nosferatu has also been dropping hints that this is only the first custom-prey-oriented idea; his followup is using long-term Dominate, Nightmare, and Auspex to create groomed, tailored prey humans (for a very large fee, by commission only).
