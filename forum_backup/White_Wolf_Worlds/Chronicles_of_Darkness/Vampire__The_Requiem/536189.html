    Gangrel Insects?

    06-01-2015, 08:04 PM
    Hi everyone

    I had an incredibly random though the other day. If vampires have the ability to transform/take aspects from animals, what is stopping them from adapting that ability to other creatures? Instead of growing claws, giant mandibles come out of their mouth. Instead of transforming into mist, they disperse into a swarm of locusts. Now one issue I see is, if they are able to completely turn into an insect, would they have a giant version of it or would they shrink down to the normal size?

    Not sure if anything like this has been thought up of. I only play with the vanilla content for the most part. Haven't gone into any of the other bloodlines and whatnot.

                                                      Okay okay, so some technical issues with it.

                                                      I understand the problem with turning into insects without it being used for 'Swarm' Form. However, I was perusing about last night and came across some massive insects.

                                                      I'm excluding any instinct species such as the Meganisoptera; also called Griffinflies) which is a type of Dragon Fly with a wingspan of around 28 inches. (Unless you're character was around during the Carboniferous period, doesn't make sense for you to have the ability to transform into one of these monstrosities).

                                                      I'm talking about insects such as the Titanus Giganteus or; the Titan Beetle (the second largest known beetles species (you will see why I chose this one instead of the first!)
                                                      (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titan_beetle)
                                                      On Average, they grow up to around 6.5 inches and weight up to around 7 lbs. Only the Hercules beetle is known to grow larger, mostly due to the fact that it has a giant horn that makes up about half of it's length. The Titan Beetle also has incredible powerful mandible for it's size, being able to break easily through a pencil. It is also believed to be able to burrow with skin (kind of like the scarabs in The Mummy; however sadly, with research I have found out that Scarabs are unable to do so. Although apparently they are fantastic diggers)


                                                      Queen's Alexandria's Birdwing (yes, it's a butterfly, but no one ever suspects the butterfly!)
                                                      (http://www.mnn.com/earth-matters/ani...ndras-birdwing)
                                                      These beautiful creatures, mostly found in New Guinea, can have a wingspan of over 1 foot! That's insane for these gentle giants!


                                                      There was one other one, kind of terrifying looking, but can't remember what it was. If a vamp wanted to use these for their "animal" form, instead of themselves shrinking to that size, or a massive version (which if I GM'd a game, and the player had a good reason for it (Ie. They use to be a pharaoh and turns into a giant scarab beetle), I would allow it as that is bloody terrifying), they turn into x amount that would make up the size of the character. So for example with the Queen's Alexandria's, they would turn into 5-7 of these interesting-choice-for-a-vampire creatures. Of course they would follow under the swarm rules, the health/blood pool is split among them, they lose one and they lose that chunk of health/blood.

                                                      I do like the idea that any character who wanted to turn into, well, anything, has to have at least eaten to once before. That makes sense to me.

                                                      ​Thoughts?
