    I believe it is not intrinsically tied to the Covenant itself, as it was pointed out that it is very much liked by some players (myself included)
    My guess'd be that the Cronites weren't all that appealing because you have focused on presenting raw power they can give, without considring that every Covenant has something to offer in its place, often more powerful than blood sorcery costing precious XPs.

    It is very true that the Circle has to overcome its un-cool apeal of savages, diablerists and "old ladies in the forest". Who in our world has to do just that too?
    That's right, modern sects, particularly satanism. These groups don't entice people by saying that they will perform miracles, rather, they offer a sense of belonging, the feeling that someone has truly got your back. Of course, the Sanctified try to do that too, but if you are not particularly into having your undead butt kicked around by pretty much everyone or being constantly told that you are guilty of a mortal sin by the simple fact of your existence, then it is not the wisest choice. And the Ordo? Please, even themseves can barely understand all that mystical mumbo-jumbo and they confuse surpassing one's nature with defying it.

    The Cronites, however, can give you all the answers and learning environment you can take. No slaves or masters or stupid, millenia-old rules no one even cares to explain. And most importantly: you get to accept who you are now and really make sense of the Beast, the thing that bothers every Kindred in existence. And if you learn some cool rituals along the way? Great, tearing your enemies' throats with your teeth is so much fun!

    Moreover, you do not have to spend the rest of your unlife in some cave to get all that. No, you get to frequent the BEST parties, take the BEST drugs and have the BEST sexual experience you can imagine, because the Cronites know that sensuality is one of the most important parts of a sentient being. Forget all those boring Sanctified monks or stuck-up Ordo mentors - they keep you from it because they know that once someone tastes freedom, he will never again take their shit seriously.

    That way, you have the profit AND pleasure. If NPC the players care about are Cronites too, all the better. Believe me, portraying the Circle in my chronicle that way has worked wonders.

                    there is a reason why joining any convenant and gaining status in them is a breaking point. These aren't clubs for good people to join as a way to deal with their condition. All of them are clubs for monster who revel in some aspect of their monstrosity.
                    The humane vampire stays far away from them and is unaligned. Of course the humane vampire is a short lived vampire.
                    Not necessarily disagreeing with you, but that is not the game as intended. Covenants are a means of coping with your condition that are supposed to hold you to some level of Humanity. They are means of emotional support as well as a kind of occupational therapy. Anything is better than just sharing your four walls alone with the Beast. The Covenants are a primary way to do this. The reason the covenants are unpleasant to mortal sensibilities is not that they are somehow inherently evil, but that they are made up of vampires. Science is not bad, but a vampire scientist has less scruples than a mortal one. Faith is not bad, but the Beast twists everything. Progress is not bad, but you got to watch out where you lead that change when your main motivation is selfish predation. The establishment is not bad, but an established alpha predator has to be merciless. An Unaligned Kindred has the problem of having to find their own way for how to handle their condition that does not involve becoming a monster in short order. It is not impossible, but you got to work for it. As page 31 of the Core says:

                    Originally posted by Vampire: the Requiem Second Edition
                    But the problem is that the one thing we can always do is hunt. And the vampire who only hunts, who times his existence based upon how long it is until he can next feed, becomes a hollowed-out, snarling monster at a truly terrifying rate.
                    The Breaking Point for joining is not because you are joining an inherently inhuman or evil institution, but because you join a vampire institution. It is a kind of rite of passage from your former mortal life to your Requiem. The Unaligned simply ignores this step, possibly by fooling themselves a bit longer.

                    The Curse, and by extension your Clan, is what drives you to be one of various kinds of monsters.

                    Politeness is the lubricant of social intercourse.

                                I'd just like to add that its perfectly ok to change either the circle or sanctified to be more appealing to players. By the nature of the game itself, each city in the world can have surprising differences within the same covenant. Perhaps a player wants the sanctified within his city to be less religious and more scholarly. You can even alter the core doctrine by saying vampires originate from Caine and that sanctum doctrines are based on the ancient writings of Caine. If the end result is that the sanctum still functions the same but ends up more appealing to players then go for it. Personal chronicles among friends are not official canon and therefore have no reason to follow canon if that is unappealing to players.

                                All this said, if the vast majority of your players are interested in the Ordo then just make an Ordo focused chronicle. Theres nothing wrong with that.
