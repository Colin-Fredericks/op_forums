                                                        Originally posted by Primordial newcomer View Post
                                                        Can y'all sell me on the circle of the crone? […] I don't understand what connects them as a covenant that can keep itself from be taken out by the others
                                                        Crúac and few standards, to be pithy about it.

                                                        The Circle is one of the least populous major covenants and one of the most permissive as an organized group; its paradigm of the dead seldom takes a dim view of Kindred becoming magical monsters who do as they please, but what it does do is offer a great many marginalized vampires a shared community that enables them to both buffer the consequences of their inhumanity and have a set of things they care about to justify at least dragging their feet a little on the long slide down the ladder.

                                                        You'll find a lot of weird old elders who have started to mutate over time, but stave off draugrhood for the sake of their place in the local community. (Keeping their grip on the blood magic they've built up over the decades is another motivation — learning Crúac tilts a vampire heavily toward the more monstrous end of the scale, and being more monstrous quickly negates most of the drawbacks of learning Crúac.)
