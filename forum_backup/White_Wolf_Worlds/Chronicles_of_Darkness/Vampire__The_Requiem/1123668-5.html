                                    Originally posted by Leliel View Post

                                    What was said above. Dhampiri do not have Disciplines, they have the fact that the very nature of being life born from death means they all have Fate Itself as their godparent. They have Twists, somewhat conceptual control over destiny, and besides their Beast being a second Vice that only rears its head when the dhampir is stressed, they all have a third Anchor, Destiny. Which they get Willpower from pursuing or actively thwarting, with both achievement and utter denial of resulting in a full refresh.
                                    Let me expand on this response a bit:

                                    Though it's a bit hard to piece together (the dhampir section is all over the place) they not only do not have Disciplines, they cannot learn them--because they are immune to becoming ghouls (but not immune to bonds). They also cannot be embraced. Both of these are from throw-away lines in disparate sections, challenged by other text on mindset indicating that they can aspire to become immortals like their undead parent.

                                    They "like" to drink blood and vitae, but apparently gain absolutely no benefit from doing so, except bonds and addiction rolls. This is... essentially a stiff middle finger toward the player, making them harder splats to roleplay, IMO.

                                    It's unclear as well whether dhampir age and die of old age normally--I believe the intent is for them to do so, but other text implies that their vampire parent gains a valuable, long-term benefit for grooming their 20-year+ investment. If they cannot be ghouled and age normally, dhampiri have as much utility as a mortal with a short stack of supernatural merits--and for some of such merits out there, rather less utility.

                                    You do get an extra route to recover willpower (destiny), which is needed since you burn willpower to activate twists. In a somewhat cool fashion you can substitute a new destiny for one you fulfill, so if you are working with your ST on this you could lay out a clear line of breadcrumbs to follow with some mechanical benefits. If you don't work with your ST? Well you're not going to get much good out of destiny.

                                    Twists, mechanically, are structured to not exceed three dots in power level. And they're pretty mild, offering more utility than crunch. You will not get any kind of benefit on par with, say, the physical disciplines, but you could certainly surprise someone!

                                    There is no healing mechanism that I see, beyond normal mortal healing. Not even a twist. This is likely to be an issue during play, since their sorcery mechanism relies on damaging themselves. On the other hand, that's more meaningful if it takes you a week to recover....

                                    My quick evaluation? I'll have to houserule a few things to use them. Namely dispensing with the no-embrace and no-ghoul restrictions, which seem unnecessary.

                                    --Khanwulf
