    Requiem Resources

    06-20-2017, 12:53 PM
    Since A Thousand Years of Night comes out this week, I’m going to kickstart this resource. This document will (eventually) all of the CofD books either from the Vampire gameline, or directly applicable to the gameline. It’s aimed at giving neonate Storytellers and Players a place to start, but should be useful for anyone. I’m starting with just the core rulebooks and will add others in time. If you have any suggestions, that are not part of the Requiem gameline, let me know what they are and why they are useful so I can add them to the list.
