                            Abstinence - If there wasn't such a potentially high vitae cost, I could see using this as a temporary measure in a few situations. Living on someone's hunting grounds for a time without putting pressure on the herd - a further way to actually handle long-term imprisonment (You can keep me locked in this room for eternity, Prince). Also - knight-guardians at their same posts throughout the years just waiting for an enemy to come. Clarification on what "imbibe" means could help - see if you *can* stack this with mystical blood transfers of other Oaths, etc.

                            Handshake Deal - Awesome.

                            Hard Motherfucker - Not sure how I feel about this one. I like the deliberate conflict it causes between Inv/Car - but I wonder if the trade off should be a little worse. You're losing experiences, but maybe there should be some additional pressure to end a traitor. Or a traitor, once revenged upon, is freed/forgiven so as to not see a chain of new recruits kicking the shit out of the same guy but always letting them go. Probably just addressed by the table, though. I suspect there should also be a Sanctity of Merits comment for non-betrayal changes. Someone who's promoted up from the coterie would get to respend those Merit Dots, or redefine their role as a higher status Invictus member, rather than a coterie member.

                            Model Prisoner - Really like this one. "Oh - I'll surrender peacefully, but the Prince will know exactly how I'm treated." A little put off by the mystical damage bit, would almost rather the damage was Government Merit suppression instead. Like - the officer who ignores the mistreated prisoner loses City/Cov Status unless they punish the offenders or free the prisoner, etc, or maybe even becomes a Masquerade threat themselves because of the wounds (some sort of social penalties rather than wound penalties).

                            Office - Love it. I would totally agree that a meaningful service to their officer duties counts exactly like a meaningful interaction with a Touchstone, including frenzy talkdown attempts.

                            Refugee - Interesting, and a good balance to the Carthian 'city invasion' Merits.

                            Righteous Kill - I like the idea of ritualized pre-remorse for a sin a lot, though I could also see this being a specialized Sanctified-Invictus oath crossover rather than a purely specific Invictus Oath. And it looks like the best 'confessor' is a low humanity Kindred. For many sins they won't even need to test at all, nevermind the bonus die. I hope there a bit of specificity involved: "Sire, I need to kill these three individuals, they are the ones who witnessed the Primogen's frenzy." - And those specific killings are affected, rather than a blanket "I made the Oath, now I can kill anyone related to the case as long as I can justify it."

                            Safe Word - The other one I'm not sure on without some play examples. Feel like there could be some other interesting asymmetry without it being a primarily combat trump. I suppose you could use it just as someone was giving a speech before the court or the like, but most often I suspect it will be used as part of a combat roll.

                            True Knight - Like it a lot. Curious if there will be different interpretations regarding "neutrality" between the differences of Knightly Orders. Also, can you violate this Oath, and if so presumably Sanctity of Merits would not apply?

                                I think the point between Safe Word is that it's a way to get someone to trust you. It's giving someone power over you as a sign of goodwill.

                                It also makes coteries of Invictus terrifying; imagine, if you would, a group of, say, five Invictus who have sworn the Oath of the Safe Word to each-other. You have a group of five Vampires who pool their Willpower pools, and who each have the best social merit of each member.

                                Alternatively, imagine an Invictus sworn into the office of Sheriff who has an Oath of Office sworn for their position and an Oath of the Safe Word running with the Prince, who 'shares' their City Status ••••• with the Sheriff. That Sheriff has a big Willpower pool, and gets +5 dice when spending Willpower.

                                Combine that with swearing an Oath of the True Knight, and you've got an almost ideal enforcer.

                                Ooh, and Oath of the Handshake Deal can be used by Invictus rulers to help police criminals; the criminal has to make an oath to not commit a crime, the Invictus makes a token oath, and they shake on it. If the criminal commits a crime, the city immediately and permanently claims their wealth or their allies or their True Friend or whatever.
