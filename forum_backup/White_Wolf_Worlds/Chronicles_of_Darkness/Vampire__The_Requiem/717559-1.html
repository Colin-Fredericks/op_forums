        There's a bunch of great ideas here, guys. I'll sort through them shortly, but I think I've got a lot of material to draw from.

        Originally posted by Chattnos View Post
        What is your take on Atlanta's history?
        I been trying deal with whether strix would drawn to the battle for Atlanta and would some be destroyed in the burning.
        Was Sherman's March in part an attempt to deal with strix?
        Oh boy, don't get me started. Are you planning on running an Atlanta game? because my history and setting has gotten EXTENSIVE, probably in large part due to the fact that I've lived here my entire life. It's gotten ridiculous enough that I've considered formatting the bits and pieces out as a fan-made setting supplement, though I'm not sure if there'd be any interest in that.

        The details of Sherman's March is still foggy. It was either an unfortunate tragedy or an ambitious power move by an upstart kindred who would become Prince. Real estate, and particularly the unique real estate practices of segregation in Atlanta are one of the largest factors in the history and current power infrastructure of Atlanta. The throughline of modern power is a Prince who's rise to power dates back to Reconstruction (though he's currently in torpor while his childe leads), who has solidified his control by manipulating the value, both for mortals and immortals, of neighborhood housing markets. There was another big fire in 1917, not as extensive as Sherman's March, but targeting O4W, which was a predominantly black neighborhood at the time. There are rumors that both big fires were risky manipulations by this Prince, but there's a darker implication that a Strix is responsible, and that another such incident might be looming on the horizon. I'm leaving it ambiguous at the moment though to give me more flexibility as the game proceeds.

        Chances are, though, I'll probably be leaving Sherman's March as simply the result of wartime actions. I tend to lean more towards the philosophy that the lives (or unlives) of supernaturals are shaped by historical events rather than the other way around.

                    I'd probably have a vampire who lives down there, Obfuscated almost all the time. Probably a Morotrophian who gives lip service to the Invictus of the city but is actually a member of the Prima Invicta and occasionally makes withdrawals on their behalf when they need to wipe out or put a large population in danger.                     A small coterie of Morbus would arrive, attempting to spread a strange disease in order to lure members of the CDC into action so they can study them in order to prepare for their larger plan, which likely involves a more dangerous outbreak. Maybe tie it into the Plague Angel himself, or his childer. I'd also tie in the Fire Touched werewolves of the Pure, taking over a nearby church or opening a new one to incorporate it on their grounds, exploring things tentatively but carefully, as the place would have a lot of spirits that wouldn't like them and they'd quickly realize there was something else guarding the place, if they didn't know exactly what. in the meantime they'd try to use their influence to bring other Pure packs in the area aligned towards them, or wipe out or convert Forsaken.
                    I've strongly considered the idea of a vampire down there, either in the tunnels beneath or in some blocked off lab that's long forgotten, though I'd probably make them a Dragon. The Dragons rule the area pretty much unfettered by the Invictus Prince, who's seat of power is incredibly localized. The Dragons would have to weigh the threat of an outbreak or attack vs. asking for Invictus help, knowing the latter would weaken their own power and probably be used as pretext by ambitious Invictus to seize domain as their own.
