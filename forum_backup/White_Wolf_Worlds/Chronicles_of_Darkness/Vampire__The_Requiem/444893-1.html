    So, werewolves & mages aside, fire is the most obvious weapon, although the host is toast.
    Here is something that came up tonight - can a kindred use Domiate on a Strix? (I don't see why not, it has resistance and Shadow Potency).
    Do you dominate the Strix or the host? (I'd assume the Strix).
    Can you dominate a Strix to leave the host? (Any reason why not?).
    As it stands, with a PC Khaibit & a PC Ventrue who specialises in Dominate, is there any reason they can't spot & dominate the Strix?
