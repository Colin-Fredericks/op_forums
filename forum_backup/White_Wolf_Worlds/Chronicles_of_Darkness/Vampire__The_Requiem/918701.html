    Rules: Vampire the Requiem (Second Edition) Protean vs. Clothing

    06-21-2016, 02:01 PM
    I've scoured the book and can't find anything, not even a fluff piece describing the effects of Protean related to clothing. There's an old post from 2013 that predates 2.0 that doesn't really have a definitive answer. I've consulted with 3 different storytellers and had 3 different answers. One says Protean only relates to Flesh as it only mentions flesh. Another says "of course the simple clothes change with you", and a third says "depending on the theme of the game, I'd rule it either way, as the story needed it."

    I feel like without any definitive line relating to clothes being in the book. All of these answers are correct. There's no mention in the FAQ even related to it.


    Thoughts? Clarification? Official Ruling from on High?

                              I wanted to add my opinion to the mix of things. I understand that there are other interpretations to this power, but I will give you my reasoning.

                              The first dot of protean states that the earth opens up and you sink into it. You are cocooned inside the ground. You have to pay extra to sink into anything else based on it's durability. Concrete for example. So I am torn on that one. I think you could take your clothes because it seems more like the ground eats you...

                              dots 2,3,4 all specifically state that the vampire's flesh changes. Meaning Body, skin and bones. These are inward out changes. The beast inside of you trying to get out. Which seems to imply to me that should you turn into a kitten.. you would be in a pool of clothes or if you turn into a tiger, your clothes would shred. Because they are not part of your flesh. I believe that if it was intended to be otherwise they would have said "body" "person" or any other word that has a different connotation .

                              5th you burst into smoke. it even says that the pinnacle of protean you become "Nothing more than smoke and hunger" not "nothing more than smoke, hunger and your wallet" (ok being a little silly here )

                              I can see the other points that Wolves can now change with clothes, but that's only if their self control is on par. Their harmony is right. Protean isnt about control, its about giving in to the beast. The primal part of you


                              I dont want to take what Saibot said out of context, but I dont think " Protean 1 is already an ability to meld with materials " . I mean at least that is not what protean is intended for. It's not meant to be a power that lets you merge with materials. In saying so, to me, Creates a slippery slope. It makes me ask, why cant I merge into the wall of the bank vault and come out the other side? Why cant I merge into the second story floor and hide? Protean is an ability that brings you closer to the beast inside. The animal. Nature. It is very bestial. And beasts don't care about clothing or how inconvenient it is to have no clothes on hand.

                                                          So, is the takeaway from this discussion really that there isn't a hard and fast ruling on the field?

                                                          To me the feeling I get is that it's really up to the THEME of the game. And just because a house rule for one game in this case works, doesn't mean it would apply to the next game a group runs.

                                                          If you want to be the flashy swarm of bats that reforms as your awesome self clad in Roman armor, and it fits the theme of the game, go with it.

                                                          If you are playing a game that logistically speaking, you need to have a 'plan' (That's asking a lot some times), then play a game that makes your player use the space between their ears and come up with a solution together.

                                                          Alternatively, one thing Vitalis and I discussed on the side was a Merit/Devotion requiring perhaps Vigor or Resilience to end the discussion altogether. If that fits your game, do that!

                                                          For me, the initial choice was simple: The ST said I'm naked, so, I was naked. The scene continued and made for some certainly interesting storytelling. But it also sparked a curiosity to scour the book and do a lot of research to try and find it covered.

                                                          First Edition very clearly spelled out that Protean changes included your clothing, but I say that with the firm understanding that 1.0 and 2.0 are not the same game/powers.

                                                          I'd like to thank anyone who's contributed so far, and anyone who continues to chime in on the discussion, it's great.
