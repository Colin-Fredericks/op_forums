                                            Originally posted by Griautis View Post
                                            No, but those are examples on a totally different level. Sadly, Auspex 4, if allowed to do so, can directly duplicate the effects of Nightmare 2 and Majesty 2.
                                            Not really. Dread Presence and Confidant create very specific and very complex emotional states with a variety of effects. A charmed victim implicitly trusts her charmer and wants to do favors for him. A Frightened victim feels in mortal danger from the vampire and wants to get away from him at all costs. Someone whose bad memories have been brought to the surface feel bad about the time that thing happened. To me, the biggest difference is that Majesty and Nightmare affect how the victim feels about the user. Auspex 4 can only show the victim a scary or nice thing, it can't actually control how they react to it and it can't force an association between that image and the vampire.

                                            Originally posted by Griautis View Post
                                            And tell me, what's the difference between Auspex 4, pushing in memories of fear and making you run from me and with Nightmare 2? how exactly nightmare 2 works? By creating what's not real.
                                            The difference is, the former might leave the victim Shaken or Spooked, depending on how they feel about the memory - they remembered or were shown something horrifying and it may rattle them a bit, if it happens to be a vision of something that disturbs them. The later will directly alter the victim's emotional state and forcibly change it to a flight response, with the vampire as the source of danger.

                                            Originally posted by Griautis View Post
                                            The problem I have with such usage, is that thematically such usage does not fit Auspex whatsoever.
                                            I disagree. I think you are focusing too much on the results and not enough on the methods. In my opinion, using someone's horrible secret thoughts and memories directly as a weapon is much more thematically appropriate than what amounts to astral projection is, for example.
