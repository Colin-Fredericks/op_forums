                        Originally posted by Ventrue Life View Post
                        Hence my confusion, and question whether that means the strix is protected from all his banes or only his fire and sunlight banes.
                        For which see "vampiric hosts use the Strix's banes for the duration of the possession, nonvampiric hosts of Strix without Preservation decay over time or are more fragile and need the upkeep required of a living body, and the Strix being insulated from damage does not mean it doesn't still have the banes it has."

                        Possess Corpse specifies that banes break through the body's "everything deals bashing damage" durability, and Possess Revenant and Possess Kindred are subject to the standard copy-over; you might make a case for Possess Living being an exception if not for the fact that, as you point out, there's a core Strix bane that specifically applies across host bodies even if that Embodiment didn't have a natural point at which it immediately switches to Possess Corpse.

                        The context you brought up "the Strix does not suffer from those banes" in is pretty clearly about the Strix not suffering damage from those banes, given that the section you quoted from goes on is discussing Kindred hosts and frames the process that is usually carried out with fire as a generalized process of surrounding the host with the Strix's banes — it doesn't not suffer those banes just because it's insulated from direct contact with them any more than I stop being allergic to poison ivy just because I put on an NBC suit.

                        Your question is pretty much inherently answered by the Banes section: "Host bodies protect against the damage and repulsion of the two great banes" and "It is repulsed from the bane as though it were sunlight, and if the material is used as a weapon against the Strix — even in Shadow Form — it deals lethal damage" does not in any way imply that wearing another body stops the Strix from exhibiting its more behavioral quirks even if there weren't fiction in the books where a character specifically leverages the counting compulsion of an embodied Strix against it.

                                        Originally posted by Horodrigo View Post
                                        I'm having some difficulties with the effects of a homebrew devotion. I wish to create a Devotion that allows me to conserve my vitae potent after it leaves my body.
                                        It should be Resilience for the "remain potent" factor, or should it be Vigor, or should be both? And there is any way to understand this on the rules or it's just GM fiat?

                                        I'd actually somehow tie the Devotion in to Blood Potency. Have it be a Devotion that requires both Vigor and Resilience, and have the duration of your Vitae's potency depend on the vampire's Blood Potency, for example, 1 hour per dot in Blood Potency.

                                        If anything, this sounds like something that belongs in Blood Sorcery territory. Have it be a Theban miracle, Cruac ritual or a Scale for Coil of the Ascendant.

                                        For example:

                                        Preserve the Vitae
                                        Prerequisite Coil: The Warm Face
                                        Procedure: The subject, who needs to be a vampire who knows how to use the Warm Face (Coil of the Ascendant ••), has to use blush of life before the start of the procedure. The vampire then hooks himself up to a device that can draw blood and store it. The vampire uses physical intensity to pump his blood through into a container, hard and fast. The amount of Vitae that can be used for physical intensity is limited by the vampire's Vitae/turn limit, as determined by his Blood Potency. The Vitae spent using physical intensity gets stored in the container used for this procedure. The vampire can then bleed additional Vitae into the container if he so desires. When the procedure is finished, the Vitae inside of the container will be preserved for a prolonged period of time.
                                        Outcome: The Vitae inside the container will remain potent for a number of hours equal to the Vitae spent through physical intensity. During this time, the contents of the container can be used for Cruac rituals or other blood sorcery that requires Vitae, or it can be fed to humans or vampires, with the normal consequences of doing so. Even when the blood inside the container is spilled or mixed with another liquid, the Vitae will remain potent.


                                        There are no hard rules on this, so anything goes. That's just how I would do it.

                                                        I would make a Scale out of the whole process. Preserving Vitae through "mundane" transmutation into a beverage. Int+Crafts to perform the process.
