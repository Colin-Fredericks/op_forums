                                Originally posted by Marcus View Post
                                What about a grapple 1 vs. 2-3 or more? Does the successes of the group sum in order to surclass the successes of the single enemy? How can I count tge damages???
                                Can you clarify me this aspect?

                                I suspect this is getting expanded on in Hurt Locker, but in Requiem since everyone tags to the same initiative in a grapple, I let all involved choose if they are attempting to control the grapple, or if they want to support someone else's attempt. The supporters use teamwork rules as normal, rolling first and adding their successes as a bonus to another character's roll. All the non-supporters then make their rolls, and the highest wins.

                                It gets a little messy from there, but I let the winning roll choose who on their team (they, or someone who supported them) makes a maneuver using their successes against anyone else in the grapple.

                                So - for example - one of your supporters might have a shiv and be doing lethal, so you give them the Damage maneuver. Or - you're in a moshpit trying to rescue someone, so you let your boyfriend take the Break Grapple maneuver and GTFO. You have to think a little creatively when it comes to immobilization (sans cuffs) and going prone, though.

                                When someone new attempts to enter the group-grapple, I use the highest defense rating out of everyone in the mass to oppose.

                                            Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                                            This is really why I don't have any powers work over video or a camera.
                                            To be clear, I think that's a perfectly reasonable call to make, and a valid interpretation of the text, even if it's not the way I personally run it.

                                            Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                                            The Daeva charming someone over the phone.
                                            I wouldn't allow that. Charming someone with Confidant or Loyalty requires them to first be affected by Awe, and Awe relies so heavily on visuals that I wouldn't allow it to work over the phone. But, I would allow Awe to work over a camera feed because it affects everyone who sees the Daeva. You look at the vampire and he's too good to be true. You have trouble paying attention to anything else because he's just that gorgeous. You view everything he does in the best possible light because he's too perfect for you to see his flaws. Nothing about that seems to me like it would require the observer's physical presence to notice. That said, the Daeva would have to spend the Willpower to suppress The Lost Visage for this to work, otherwise the people watching on camera won't be able to get a good enough look at him to notice his beauty.

                                            Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                                            The Nosferatu terrifying someone over FaceTime.
                                            This one I'm iffy on. I would certainly allow the Nos to activate Dread Presence, and for someone seeing her over FaceTime to be affected by the global effect of Dread Presence - her Intimidate pools would be bolstered against them, and they wouldn't be able to spend Willpower to attack her (not they can really attack her from the other side of a FaceTime call anyway). Because they can clearly see that she's fucking scary. I would not allow her to specifically designate them as a target for her illusions, or to create illusions on their side of the screen. But, if she creates an illusion that anyone can see on her side, and it's in full view of the camera, they would be able to see it just fine. Again, this all assumes the Nos is dropping The Loat Visage.

                                            Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                                            Mekhet using Auspex to find out more someone they saw on on something they saw on TV.
                                            This is another case where I think it depends on what exactly the Mekhet is trying to learn using Auspex. Beast's Hackles only gives the user pertinent information about immediate potential danger, and people on TV can't hurt you. Spirit's Touch gives the user information about the thing they're touching, so you could find out more about the TV, but you can't actually touch the people on the screen. However, Uncanny Perception gives you information about people based on observations of subtle details about them. I could certainly see using Uncanny Perception to find out if an actor is a vampire by watching him on TV, or to figure out an actor's true emotional state at the time of recording.

                                            Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                                            It doesnt make sense it seems silly. I look at electronic devices as a filter...It dilutes to the power so it's not potent. Also if they work through cameras, they would have to be able to be recorded, ( as there is very little difference between live and recorded ) which I dont feel vampires record well...
                                            Yeah, I'm cool with the powers I mentioned above working through recordings. But I can also totally see why you wouldn't want to allow that. Personally, the most compelling argument for me is that these powers are all manipulations of the Predatory Aura, and how the heck is the Predatory Aura supposed to travel through a recording? I'll totally buy that that seems silly and doesn't make sense to people. For me though, I see The Lost Visage as an example of the Predatory Aura working through recordings, strange though it may seem. And I look at Rose's way of explaining the Predatory Aura as not a literal halo of vampiric energy, but as purely phenomenal. Vampires recognize each other by the palor of their skin, the hungry look in their eyes, their inhuman stillness, not by psychic impressions. The Lost Visage works by the Beast instinctively knowing just where to stand or just when to move, so that her face is always just out of frame, or obscured by glare, or movement blur, not by magically distorting the recorded image itself. Majesty works by the Beast instinctively knowing just how to move, when to smile, when to be assertive, and when to be winsome, to perfectly capture observers' attention, not by putting magical instigram filters in observers' brains. All those things, in my opinion, would be captured on video, whether live or recorded. But, that's just my take, and I've got no problem with other ST's interpreting it differently.

                                            Originally posted by Vitalis View Post
                                            That said... Dominate is the only one that says you can't so... RAW without considering Intent. have at it.
                                            I wouldn't say it's a direct application of RAW without considering intent, nor would I say it's indicative of Clan favoritism (Ventrue is my favorite Clan, so...) As I explained above, based on statements from Rose I believe the intent is that aura-based powers work on purely sensual phenomena, and therefore any medium that carries that sensual information carries the power as well. I look at The Lost Visage of evidence of a power working in a way that is consistent with my interpretation. If others find that interpretation silly, that's cool. I wouldn't try to argue if I was playing in one of their games.

                                            Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.
