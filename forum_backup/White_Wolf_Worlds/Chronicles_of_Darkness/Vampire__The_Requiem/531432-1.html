    Originally posted by Tabanese View Post
    My second example is how I made sense of this while thinking about it. For those who know the game, this will seem vague but hopefully, that just makes it approachable for those who don't. Suppose five League of Legend gamers are embraced to the different clans. As their preferred past-time is an online video game, they use it as an escape from the unpredictability of their new life. However, though the Beast wouldn't play League normally, it is chained to the whims of the Man as much as the Man to it. As such, the players notice they now play different. The Deava suddenly finds it an easy affair to chain deadly combos together and out-play opponents. What they intend to do and what they do now line up with frequent regularity. The Gangrel finds himself pulling back from an attack moments before an ambush, and knowing -just knowing- which path to take. His survivability shoots up. He also finds himself preferring tanks and melee. The Nosferatu starts to play with his opposition. He just knows which champion and items will give his opponent a hard time and how to play up this advantage. He also starts to use chat to infuriate the enemy team, unnerving daring advances and splitting defense against shadows. The Mekhet starts to notice tells and give aways. He always seems to be aware of cooldowns and mana reserves of the enemy, as well as deriving the location of others from small and subtle details. The water splashing through fog of war. The bush moving. The enemy suddenly having dragon buffs. It is as if the game is an open book. The Venture stops playing a personal game and develops a wider perspective. He never plays against opponents who defend properly anymore; there is always a weak spot. Additionally, he rarely goes a game without directing his team. Previously, he only spoke to them to badmouth their preformence but now, he instructs and his instructions pay off. After a few minutes, he has his team coordinated in a way usually reserved for the pros. And that's in solo qeue.
    Asa League of Legends player myself, that example came as a shock initially. Ihad to re-read the three first lines two times in order to make myself sure i wasn't just fooling myself over its meaning xD That being said, i've enjoyed your examples very much. Nice and weird, mixing these two hobbies in one message in OnyxPath.

    But let's not forget you're talking about vampires: you have gone and thought about the good things (well, not exactly good in that chat bothering Noseratu... let's say you've focused on the ways being a vampire would improve their playstyle). But now, you have to go back and think about all the wrongness they would inherit, too. They are lonely predators by nature, who weirdly need to stick together as a reminiscence of what they were as the Man - some dying part of themselves- whisper to them. But they have gone over the dark shadow of the world, and now some part of them crave pain, blood and violence... whatever form of violence it takes. So you have to confront things like the Gangrel increasingly going abruptly afk after rage burst; or the Mekhet getting herself lost in the symbolic pattern of those little animals that buzz and fly over some points in the map but no others, that suspicious three lane shape; the Daeva forgetting about teamplay and wandering alone because of the bloodlust and caring only about her feelings when achieving the kill, the Nosferatu's tactics going wrong -too hard, too good- and backlashing into permanent bans; the Ventrue refusing to acknowledge her faults and trying to control her team to the point of dictatorial play.

    Anyway, thank you for this amusing chat. Has been funny to think of Vampires in LoL for once, anyway.

    PS. Know what? Werewolves. With their pack instinct, now THOSE would be fine League of Legends players. xD

                                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                        I was assuming they could drink the fishes.
                                        There are some serious problems with that option. I'll try to number them here:

                                        First: Pressure. From Europe to America kindred would have to travel trough the Abyssopelagic Zone, from 4000 meters (13,100 feet) to 6000 meters (19,600 feet) deep, which means a pressure from 400 to 600 atmospheres (in the Hadalpelagic Zone, in deep water trenches and canyons is even worse, reaching higher and higher pressures until eight tons/square inch in the oceans deepest point: the Mariana Trench. But we'll forget about that). The water temperature is near freezing, and there is no light at all.
                                        Even in the surface, where a bipedal body can move with -somewhat- speed and accuracy, fishing is a daunting perspective. Moving and hunting at 500 atmospheres of pressure? Absolutely no way, but for the very, VERY old ones. But we'll talk about age later.

                                        Second: Blood. There is plenty of life at the bottom of the oceans, yes, but most of it is microscopical and/or invertebrates (starfish, tube worms), whose body fluids i wouldn't consider fit for a vampire (even young ones) without a specific devotion at least. Try to think about drinking insects... and even that comparison would be generous.

                                        Third: Time of travel. I was being naive, thinking of only three months. I was assuming it would be like a normal walk in the surface. Vampires don't need to rest, but the pressure would make each step a monumental challenge... if possible at all. And also what points Tsusasi: trenches? Deep currents? Add more and more time to the travel, even if they manage to surmount those obstacles. And time, in such an inhospitable environement, is crucial for survival: the less time you spend there, the likelier is you'll be coming out alive.

                                        Fourth: Age. MCN brings up that low-blooded potency vampires are more mobile and able to feed on animals than elder ones. He's right. But what they have in flexibility, they lack in raw power. Crussing pressure? No Vigor enough for moving, no Resilience enough to resist it. No specialized powers to travel. Too little blood supply to hang on when hunting goes badly... and that should be the rule, not the exception, in such an alien environment.

                                        All those things considered, i think walking the oceans bottom is out of the table for vampires trying to reach another continents. But we're not out of solutions here. Let's look to another perspective. Not walk, but swim: swimming all the trip, standing at just the right depth that allow the vampire to flee the light, but high enough to dodge the crushing pressure.

                                        We're talking then about traveling through the Mesopelagic / Bathypelagic Zones.

                                        The Mesopelagic Zone lays just below the Epipelagic Zone (where there is light from the sun), and extends from 200 meters (650 feet) to 1000 meters (3200 feet). It is called the twilight zone, because the light that penetrates to this depth is extremely faint. It is in this zone where the strange and bizarre bioluminiscent fish dwell. If their blood is able to sustain a vampire or not, i leave it at ST discretion. Keep in mind, we're still talking about pressures from 20 to 100 atmospheres. That's insane.

                                        The next zone, is the Bathypelagic. It is called “the midnight zone” or “the dark zone”. It goes from 1000 meters (3300 feet) down to 4000 meters (13,100 feet). As its names suggests, there's no light here, but which is produced by the creatures themselves. The water pressure at this depth start ressembling that of the Abysoplegic Zone, reaching 5,800 pounds per square inch. There are large number of animales here in spite of that (Sperm whales can dive down to this level in search of food, giant squids live here too). The problem then, comes not only from facing the crusing pressure, but also the occasional predator... much bigger, much meaner, much more agile than any bipedal body. The feed problem could be solved by Animalism. But not every vampire has it, and not everytime it would solve anything: there are ENORMOUS areas in the sea without any fish presence.

                                        [Speaking of disciplines and everything else: there's one clan that could attempt such travel swimming, and of course i'm talking about the Gangrel. But such as i see it, again only specialized ones and old (and weird) enough to know how to... and we were talking about all vampires, regardless of clan.]

                                        So, all in all, let's say we're travelling through the Twilight Zone,as is the only one suited more or less to hunt and avoid the sun. The chances of survival depend on two factors: luck and time. Luck to find prey (and to hunt them; they are adapted to the pressure and swimming; our average young vampire is neither) and time (before something goes awfully wrong – a current, a fight, most probably a loss of direction (everything is dark blue there without solid points of reference, you can easily swin in circles)-). Definitely not a good idea.

                                        TL,DR: If you're not a gangrel heavily specialized in marine adaptations in Protean, don't try to swin between continents.
