    Help! I love VtR2 and my players love VtM

    04-08-2015, 11:31 AM
    We all agree that we love the clans of VtM, but there's a huge gap in our love for cWoD. What do we do? I'd rather just use VtR2 with the Translation Guide.

    1. I really like Blood Potency (I really love Spirit from http://vampirerpg.free.fr/Rules/Anne-Rice.html) as opposed to the uber-stat that is Generation;
    2. I love the mystery and myth of no-single-creation myth of VtR2 as opposed to "we all come from Caine, and he really exists);
    3. I love the tuned WW v2 post-God-Machine mechanics;
    4. I love the weirdness of monsters and other splats (heck there are whole books of make your own creatures with bizarre dread powers);
    5. I love the new rules for Humanity and touchstones, particularly with banes hacked in. Really intensely dislike alternate paths of humanity, etc.;
    6. I love the no-metaplot, world not populated with figures who are way more canonically important than you.

    What do we do? If I've discovered one thing about myself as a GM is that I have to be REALLY INTO what I'm doing/running to be an effective GM? I CAN'T be the first GM who has run into this problem!

              First off, I highly suggest using Yossarian's 13 Licks update to the Vampire Translation Guide, the guide is optional when using 13 licks but not required. Use this to run a 'Masquiem' setting, as I plan to once I've sorted out a character sheet with Mask, Dirge, Generation, and the term Blood Pool instead of Vitae (personal preference), as well as a version with Blood Potency instead of Generation.

              I'd also like to point out that Blood Potency is more of an uber-stat than generation (it is rolled to resist ANYTHING supernatural), it's just priced accordingly, unlike generation. If using Masquerade I'd suggest increasing the cost of Generation to 2-3 background points, and possibly getting rid of the immunity to dominate that it gives (as I do, it makes it far more difficult to determine somebody's generation). I'd say make it 1 point for levels 1-3 (one blood point isn't that big of a deal), and 2 points for levels 4 and 5 (levels 6+ aren't available to PCs), without the immunity to dominate that it normally grants, although really I suggest using Requiem and either having Blood Potency or just treating Generation as being equal to 14-Blood Potency.

              Remember that you don't have to use any clans that you don't want to, I like to swap out the Settites for the Mekhet (with their 'role' being priests and mystics), and that how much history is known is up to you (my vampires know that vampires existed in the times of Ancient Rome, when the Camarilla was active, but not since then), along with any rules (I'm not fond of paths either).

                      Foot-dragging I've experienced in the past, and some shoe-horned ideas for address:

                      Only five clans?

                      Let players start out as bloodlines right off the bat. A lot of the bloodlines will have some thematic elements that a player can hook onto, and many of the old Clans make pseudo- or direct appearances (Lasombra / Khaibit; Malkavians / Malkovians; Torador / Toreador; Giovanni / Sangiovanni). Lots of people hated that there were no Tremere. I always point them to the Ordo. Big gap I can remember is no Tzmisce equivalent. And yes - there are bloodline and full clan conversions out there as well.

                      Covenants are weird.

                      If they really like the old structure of Cam / Anarch / Sabbat, those do still play out in reflections within the Covenants. Cam = Invictus, Anarchs = Carthians, Sabbat = Crone and/or Theban, and it shouldn't be difficult to play up those rivalries.

                      My Clan's Discipline spread was nerfed.

                      While I really enjoy the focusing on the five archetypes for Requiem Clans, I remember a lot of people were apprehensive during the shift. (Ventrue lose Presence and get Animalism? Lame!) Especially in Blood and Smoke, this is mitigated by the disciplines being a lot more powerful or a lot more story driven (usually both). Also, you can mitigate this by hand-waving out-of-clans. Just let people buy whatever basic disciplines they want without worrying about teachers, etc (if they have the XP). You could even just middle the XP cost - all discplines are Level*4XP (or 4 Experiences) to raise giving everyone a chance to spread out wherever.

                      Where did Generation go?

                      The switch from Gen to BP was one of my favorite mechanical changes. While in my games I ditch the merits-for-BP discount, players who really like to go low-gen can start at BP 3 if they ditch some starting merit dots. Also, you can just start people at 2 BP (allowing for Bloodlines) with a hand-wave. For people that liked the thematic element of Elders / Ancillae / Neonates based on the Generational scale, take a look at the Invictus Dynasties.
