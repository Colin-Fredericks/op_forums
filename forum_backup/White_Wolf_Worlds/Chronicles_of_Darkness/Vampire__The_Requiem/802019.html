    Preventing Frenzy?

    01-06-2016, 08:45 AM
    I'm playing a game of experienced ancillae/newly Elder characters. Mine is essentially built to be the Domain's Hound. Kindred Duelling is a good way to display she's meant to fight other vampires, as opposed to the various other things a vampire might fight, but I have one problem:

    There are many ways to force a Frenzy on someone. Cruac, Kindred Dueling, chucking a molotov, whatever.

    How do you keep the Elder Ordo Dracul who has mastered the Mystery of the Wyrm from frenzying and just wrecking everything and everyone? This is something a Hound would have to keep in mind, I assume.

    Less drastic examples are also welcome, but this particular one is an active concern. She's Crone, but any options are available just for my personal knowledge. Discipline, devotion, ritual, merit, core power, whatever.

      If you've mastered Wyrm... you don't. You get to set whatever your frenzy goal is with Wyrm. So, lets say someone forces you to frenzy. You set "Use Predator Aura to cow the offender" as your goal, you get huge bonuses to do it, and then you slip out of frenzy when you acomplish your goal.

      Mystery of the Wyrm is there to ride the wave at a moment's notice and abuse all the benefits while remaining in control of your actions. You don't worry about holding it in check. You ride it like a horse.

              New Devotion
              Leash the Beast
              (Animalism ••••, Vigor ••)

              Animalism allows one to incite bestial rage and vampiric frenzy. This Devotion turns it around, leashing another vampire’s Beast with the Predatory Aura, allowing the user to force them into docility whether the affected Kindred wants it or not. It either keeps them from frenzying at all or ends a frenzy they are in.

              Cost: 2 Vitae
              Dice Pool: Presence + Intimidation + Animalism vs. Resolve + Blood Potency (if resisted)
              Action: Instant
              Duration: Scene

              Roll Results
              Dramatic Failure: The vampire fails to leash the Beast and is incited herself. She immediately frenzies with the goal of subduing the original target or fleeing from him.

              Failure: The vampire fails to leash the Beast.

              Success: The target becomes unable to frenzy for the rest of the Scene. A frenzying vampire immediately exits Frenzy. Furthermore, the affected character takes a penalty equal to the user’s Vigor on rolls to use the Predatory Aura for the rest of the Scene. Any supernatural powers that force frenzy must first win a Clash of Wills against this power.

              Exceptional Success: As above and the target becomes unable to use the Predatory Aura for the Scene. Any attempts to lash out against him are uncontested.

              This Devotion costs 3 Experiences to learn.


              New Merit
              Beast Tamer (Style, • to •••?)
              Prerequisite: Animalism •, Wits •••, Composure •••

              The character is experienced or trained in dealing with vampires in the throes of Frenzy. She knows how to move to avoid harm or make them attack with less force. The best have learned how to reign in a rampaging vampire, allowing them to tame a frenzying vampire like an undead beast tamer.

              Know the Beast (•): The character has an instinctual understanding of the way a frenzying vampire moves and attacks. When a frenzying vampire attacks the character, reduce the bonus from Frenzy on the Attack roll by the character’s Animalism down to a minimum of 0.

              Survive the Onslaught (••): The character’s skill at staying unharmed against a frenzying opponent has grown keener. When he has a chance to focus he evades with surprising finesse. When spending Willpower to increase his Defense, increase his Defense by +4 instead of +2 against frenzying opponents. When Dodging, a Willpower point grants +4 and 8-again on the roll.

              Soothe the Beast (•••): Now that he knows how to survive, he can use his understanding to diffuse the Beast’s rage through psychological tricks. After a successful Dodge the character can attempt a reflexive Social Roll appropriate to the situation. Successes on this roll are treated as if they came from a Touchstone trying to talk down the frenzying character (see pg. 104, Vampire: the Requiem Second Edition).


              Aside from these, I could imagine similar Crúac and Theban Sorcery Rituals. Since you say your character is Ordo, how about developing a Scale of the Wyrm that deals with this?

              Edit: Thinking about it, you might want to trade the effects of the first and second dot. Also, you could add a third dot (pushing Soothe the Beast to fourth) that is about using Social Rolls to change the goal of the Frenzy mid-Frenzy.

                              Wait, so you're trying to prevent other vampires from Frenzying? The only means I'm aware of are preventative measures. The Reason's Salon Devotion gives everyone in the area bonuses to resist Frenzy. The Chapterhouse Merit in the Secrets of the Covenants Ordo preview does something similar. Once a vampire has started Frenzying though, the only things that will stop them are getting what they want, their Touchstone talking them down, a stake to the heart, torpor, or final death. That's why I always specialize my Sheriffs and Hounds for staking, so they have a non-lethal way to deal with a Frenzy situation.
