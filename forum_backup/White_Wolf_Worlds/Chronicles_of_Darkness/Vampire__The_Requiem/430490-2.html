                                        Crowdsourcing seems quite cool, but there's not (to my knowledge) been any guidance on what more than 5 dots of Resources (for example) would actually involve. At what dot rating can I initiate a takeover of a major company? Or is 8 dots of Resources private country money? I'd really like to see some guidelines on this.

                                        Also the downside of Crowdsourcing is unclear: "Then, those Merits are lost. The original owner loses access to them for the remainder of the story."

                                        Does that mean that the dot contributed is lost for the remainder of the story, or the Merit is lost? The idea of contributing a dot from my five to a crowdsourcing project only to see my entire wealth inaccessible for an extended period is totally unappealing, so I guess it must be the former - it might be worth explicitly saying that.

                                        Information Network is a really cool idea, but I don't think it's taken far enough. In fact, that's a criticism I'd level at a number of these merits (Moderator, Social Engineering and Tech Savvy all especially jump out).

                                        I think it might be better to reconstitute these merits as Styles of a kind, interplaying with the social manoeuvring (doors) mechanics and changing how other merits work (especially the workhorse Resources, Contacts and Allies) rather than just giving specific bonuses in specific scenarios. I'd love to see something more like (just hashed together quickly, obviously):
                                        Network(• to •••••, Style)

                                        Prerequisites: Manipulation •••, Wits •••. You must also possess the Contacts merit at least equal to the dot rating of this style you wish to buy.
                                        The Invictus capitalise on their associates more comprehensively and brutally than other covenants can manage, turning their casual contacts and sources of information into a fully fledged spy network as their control over their associates increases.
                                        Effect: Network can only be used with Contacts you have an established connection with (i.e. possess the merit pertaining to). Casual contacts and associates cannot be leveraged using this style. Your character gains access to the following abilities at each level:
                                        Information Network (•): Every Contact your character counts as a Skill Specialty related to the information he provides. However, she may only use that Specialty when utilizing information gained from the Contact. If she loses the Contact for whatever reason, she also loses the Specialty.
                                        Corroborating Evidence (••): Your character can use multiple contacts to get better information than would otherwise be available by combining their reports into a comprehensive source. When you roll to access information from a Contact and have a second Contact that might be able to provide additional information about that subject, however tangential, and succeed you also immediately get the Informed condition pertaining to the information you gathered.
                                        Vicious Rumours (•••): You can now use Contacts to dig up blackmail material about your enemies - worse, you can use the information gathered to viciously invent plausible rumours that they are going to have trouble disproving. A valid use of a contact is now to create the Leveraged condition on a target, even if they do not have anything obvious to be Leveraged. The storyteller can rule this is inappropriate in some situations, but should be lenient with its application.
                                        Sword of Damocles (••••): Many kindred have contacts, but few possess the kind of damning knowledge that you can glean from yours. If you are interacting with someone who possess the Leveraged condition (and they don't resolve it) created by Vicious Rumours or about whom you have the Informed condition from Corroborating Evidence, you are also considered to have hard leverage substantial enough to remove two doors when forcing them as part of a social maneuver.
                                        Spin the Web (•••••): Your character has gone beyond simply gathering information from his sources - now she can create it whole cloth. You can access your contacts to, instead of discovering information, provide it to them. Such information spreads like wildfire within their organisation and is seem to have come from a trusted source. This can quickly destabilise organisations, cause chaos on the markets, or disrupt whole cities if used carefully. The rumours die down after a while, or after definitive proof is produced that shows it as a lie, so the canny kindred uses it to spread vicious truth rather than lies if possible, and always times the introduction of such information carefully. Mechanically, anyone who is affected by the rumour is considered to have one less door when the character is trying to convince them of something related to it, but the narrative effects can go much further.
