    One thing I think people often forget with Predatory Aspect:

    While every Gangrel’s Beast is different, its
    manifestation usually takes inspiration from a predatory or
    scavenging animal of some form.
    supported by:

    When she changes adaptation,
    the player should work to maintain the theme of a specific

And it's just scavengers and predators, so it's more restrictive than Beast's Skin as to what animals are considered acceptable. Honestly, I don't think tardigrades qualify for either power.

    Protean does have a lot more options than it typically gets used for, but it does still have limitations.

                MCN

                I just want to check that I'm understanding what you're saying. What I'm hearing you say is that there is no reason to expand the palette of animals used for Protean to those not traditionally associated with vampires, because the newly introduced animals have no characteristics that provide a rationale for creating an ability with a substantially different mechanical effect from those already available. In short, don't fix what ain't broke.

                Thing is, though, I'm not suggesting an expansion of the animal palette for the purpose of creating new mechanical effects that don't already have an analogue in the powers of Protean. Rather, what I'm suggesting is that by introducing a new palette of predatory animals more in tune with the themes of the Pijavica, that clan can use the same mechanical effects of Protean as the fur-and-fang Gangrel just with new, parallel, animal traits justifying them. This removes the need to re-jigger Protean to fit the themes of the clan, and gives one possible answer to the original question posted.

                Maina

                The Tardigrade is a predatory animal.

                True, a micro-animal.

                True, not an obligate carnivore.

                However, the way Tardigrades feed is they have a retractable styler, which is a tooth-like mouth part in the shape of a spear, which they extend to breach the cells of algae, mosses and, yes, small invertebrates. They move around with their legs, guided by their senses, using eyes and bristles across their bodies that sense vibration. They move toward their prey, breach it with their styler, and then suck the internal fluids from the target.

                Extendable fangs, used to break the skin of prey and then using their mouth to suck out internal fluids. Sound familiar in a vampire game?

                Now, sponges, lacking motility, seem an odd fit for predatory animals, I admit. But, there are sponges that trap prey and then feed from them in place. They are killing another creature to feed from it's body. That's pretty much the definition of predation.

                If a vampire were, through some horrible mishap, encased up to their neck in concrete, unable to move, but used Dominate or Majesty to hold prey near them, then drank them dry, would that not count as predation? For that matter the ability of vampires using Protean 1 to feed on blood spilled over their internment mirrors the feeding habits of sessile predators.

                I freely admit getting outside of the traditional fur-and-fang angle of Protean gets really weird, really fast. The original topic here, though, was Pijavica and Protean. My point is that, if you consider animals not normally associated with vampires, but still predatory animals, Protean begins to make more sense as a Pijavica discipline.

                And, if you expand the palette of predatory animals available for Protean, the entire Pijavica life-cycle and body plan start to make a lot more sense.

                                                            On another note, finally finished that Devotion I mentioned a couple days ago; I hope you all find it interesting!

                                                            ______


                                                            Carpet of Corruption ( Animalism 2, Protean 4, Resilience 3)

                                                            The Pijavica are masses of blood and viscera that have gained sapience and terrible hunger due to the Embrace, and even though they have attained a human-ish form they never forget the uncertainty of survival as ravenous blobs - they remember what it was like to be less than Kine and Kindred both. But they also remember the strength of such a base form can offer, even if others cannot see the benefit, and the eldest of the Clan can shape the basest of living creatures into a semblance of themselves. With this Devotion the Pijavica have been known to coat their entire havens in thick carpets of ravenous unliving ooze; semi-vampiric colonies of lifeless bacteria, dead skin, or disgusting combinations of the two - perfect for acting as a security measure or a method of ( not so)subtly stealing blood from unsuspecting guests or intruders.

                                                            Cost: 2 Vitae

                                                            Requirement: Blood must be spread where the user wishes this Devotion to take effect ( included in the activation cost)

                                                            Roll: None.

                                                            Duration: A number of nights equal to the User's (Blood Potency * Dots in Animalism *)

                                                            The Pijavica spreads her blood upon whatever surface she wishes to use this Devotion upon, and within the scene colonies of undead micro-organisms and reanimated motes of dust ( which are in fact tiny flakes of dead skin) will begin coalescing upon whichever surface the user marked, smeared across walls and floors like foul smelling slime molds or dangling from the ceiling in streams like some sort of cave-dwelling bacterial colony feeding off of a stalactite. These colony masses behave similarly to more ordinary creatures raised by the second level of Animalism save for the inability to move ( well not quite true, these colonies can attain locomotion, albeit it is extremely slow going, oft to the point of near immobility.) and their lack of sensory apparatus that other, much larger animals possess. What they lack in mobility or traditional senses is mitigated for the purpose that they serve, which is to weaken and steal the blood of those who come in contact with them who are not the user, by manifesting "natural" weapons that emerge from the ooze such as fleshy, rot inducing spines, bone spikes, even beds of jagged fangs or blood sucking tendrils.

                                                            The Carpets of Corruption inflict a number of lethal points of damage ( even to vampires!) equal to the User's Blood Potency upon contact and absorb amount of blood ( or even Vitae) equal to the amount of damage inflicted - though this damage can be avoided by a Dex + Athletics roll - the User's Blood Potency. Like ordinary Familiars these Carpets lack the ability to convert blood into Vitae, should Vitae be absorbed by the colony it may use the stolen lifeforce to sustain itself as if the User had fed it herself, though the duration for which it remains "alive" is determined by the victim's Blood Potency rather than the User. In addition, if more than one point of Vitae has been absorbed, the Carpet may store the rest to be used to sustain the colony when needed unless the User commands otherwise. At any time, the User may coax any colonies who have absorbed any blood or Vitae to exude their cache, allowing her to drink the blood or Vitae as normal ( along with the risks of vinculum or vitae addiction as normal.)

                                                            These colonies are vulnerable to fire and can be easily destroyed with application of flame, and should they not be sustained by their master( or any unlucky vampiric victims) they will die and dissolve once a number of nights equal to the user's ( Blood Potency * Animalism) pass.

                                                            *This alteration is due to it being impossible to quantify Stamina scores for such minuscule creatures.
