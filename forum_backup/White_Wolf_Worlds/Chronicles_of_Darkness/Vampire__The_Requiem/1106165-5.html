        So mechanics.

        - Skills at 6+
        These are a welcome addition to the game. If anything, I wish it was a mechanic that was played with more.

        - Devotions
        Annuls of Death is good, fluffy and a cheap expansion to Auspex 3. The concepts that want it will love it.

        Bones of the Mountain - Follows the concept of "so long as a vamp jemmorages blood, they win combat." It's pretty powerful, and I like it. Personally I don't think this will ever be worth the exp as a PC (there are other uses of your exp that are more efficient out to 200-300xp), but its a neat out of context threat for your fighter to face.

        Celebrity - This is the first power that seems on a whole different tier of power. Use it on your Ghoul, and you suddenly have a soirce of nigh unlimited Allies, Fame, Herd, Retainer, Staff and Status, with the downside that you are popular. Use with Alternate Identity.

        Consumption - Pretty great, if niche devotion. Instantly enslaving mortals decinately has its uses, as does possessing people from over the phone.

        Crush of Years -

        Legion - Niche, but powerful. Has that whole master of the land field. I wish it didnt require Auspex, since its very Gangrel and Ventrue themed.

        Malignant Smog - Interesting thematically. Mechanically it's pretty powerful (hello Immobilize and Stun). We need to know the Potency of the poison you turn in to, if applicable. Sadly, I feel like most people will pass the roll to ignore the condition. Also! Good example of using Blood Potency in mechamics to make the devotion more appealing to elders. I don't know what happens if someone is affected every 0 turns, which is technically possible.

        Pass into Yesteryear - Epic, thematically and mechanically. Its a blessing, a "blessing" and a curse to pass out to people.

        Praternatural Instinct - They are *really* pushing Mekhet as being masters of defense. Its great, but requires so much Auspex.

        Spontanious Ignition - This is lulz, and likely to set everything on fire. Doing things with Fire is always tricky, but I feel this devotion does it as respectfully as possible. I do feel that this is a very "why you should fear elders" power. Surviving their own ignition, however...

        Unbridled Force - Epic, iconic, perfect. Vampire telekonesis is a go.

        Overall - These Devotions are pretty well written, but I'm not sure if they feel particularly like Elder exclusive things. It never feels like the Elder has transcended or broken through. Imo, Elders should be borderline out of context problems for those of lesser blood. This just feels like some nice Devotions.

        Exceptions are Spontanious Ignition, Unbridled Force, Legion, and maybe Bones of the Mountain.

                Blood Sorcery
                The fluff section is nice. Doubling the costs is fluffy!

                The rituals themselves are... meh.
                Manananggal's Working - Its nice, and fluffy, and useful... but is it really an elder power?

                Gwydion's Curse is obviously meant to be used with Madrigora Garden (which will hopefully get errata so it works). It works off Potency, which is a terrible mechanic, so you wont be animating anything of size. The dicepool is meh, but its "free" I guess. The plants dont seem to be under the ritualist's control, so apparently they are in danger too? If the plants were under the kindred's control and it affected a meaningful area it would be worth it. I want to lile this, but the mechanics don't back it up.

                Scapegoat feels expensive for what it does. I also don't like avoiding detatchments as being a thing Cruac can do, even like this.

                Curse of Isolation - This is interesting, if expensive. I wish it had more oomph for its exp cost, amd I wish the mechanics went into what stealing a touchstone actually meant.

                Orison of Voices - Epic and appropriate. This is an elder power.

                Sins of the Ancestors - This is a remarkably open-ended power with no guidance on the scope of questions that can be asked. I kind of like it as an elder power.

                                                Over all aside from the Elder Character "template" most things don't feel Elder Epic.

                                                I like the idea of using the MCI merit for groups/factions within covenants, like I could see using the MCI merit to create various Knightly Orders with different agendas and a Belial Cult within Lancea Sanctum.

                                                The fiction is great.

                                                Still fun book.

                                                I think to get the, most out if this book you'll want secrets of the covenants.
