    215) There's a new vampire on the scene who's garnered a mysterious reputation. Word around the domain is that this kindred appears to be honest and forthright in his dealings with others. He doesn't lie, doesn't cheat, and doesn't manipulate. If he sends you on a suicide mission, he makes sure you know the risks before doing so. He also seems to be genuinely happy and charismatic without having to utelize Disciplines to compel the minds of others. Which may explain why so many neonates and fledglings have taken a liking to the guy after only a few days associating with him.

    When these fledglings began to reject their Sires in favor of following this newcomer, many Elders were displeased. But the latest information to leak their way has them seeing red with anger. Mr. Popularity is one of the Brood, and those who flock around him are corrupted into the service of his demonic cult. Many young and inexperienced vampires have already fallen victim to demonic influences, and if the cult isn't stamped out immediately, the domain could see a full scale invasion on its hands!


    That's where you come in. He won't approach any of the elders, claiming they're too paranoid to have a civil discussion with. But he will approach you, thinking your coterie is still relatively young by vampire standards. Your mission is to infiltrate the Brood as double agents and destroy the cult from the inside out. But once you're behind enemy lines, you begin to hear the The Call, and it only becomes more incessant the longer it takes you to complete your mission. Can you hold onto your soul long enough to purge these demons from your land, or will you sell your soul for tainted power?

        216) Urban planning and expansion being what it it in about 3 years two cities will becom one. Where as this is great news for the local economy and what not with new homes being built and stuff the local kindred haven't got any agreements in place yet, in fact they don't know much for sure about the new city.
        Will our heroes volunteer to travel to meet the new neighbours, try and claim that new feeding ground or seek to sell their services to those of more ambition?
        Possible complications include.
        The new domain although smaller is near exclusively one covenant.
        20 years ago a settlement/recon force was sent out to this city, a message was delivered by crow telling the prince that if this wasn't intended as an act of aggression he had chosen his envoys poorly and any intrusions across the river would be seen as an act of war, the new city has already expanded past the river.
        Strix/VII/Brood etc.
        One city has been keeping its population down so every kindred has plentiful feeding territory and does not wish to share with it's "frivolous" neighbours, war is coming.
        The one kindred in town who came from this city has skipped town telling his friends that they too should dig in or get out.
        All envoys sent disappear, the domain seems empty and yet it clearly isn't safe.
        The other city is very ready, they know all about their new neighbours and are ready to hit them hard, there will be a prince of their choosing on the throne or blood on the streets.
        Feel free to invert any complications if you prefer.

            217) Teeth as long as steak knives, eyes solid white and big as a man's fist, an elongated jaw that stretches to the chest, claws like hooks, hair like tentacles and wings like a crow. That is the description a Ghoul managed to give about the monster that attacked and devoured his Nosferatu master in the dark alleyway they often frequented. According to his report, the creature just dropped from the sky, grabbed his master by the shoulders and chomped down on him without a word of explanation! When it had finished it's meal, it turned and screamed at the Ghoul before spreading its wings and soaring back into the sky.

            This is not the first instance of random attacks on kindred society, it's just the first time somebody managed to give a description of the attacker. Whatever this thing is, it targets vampires specifically, attacks them completely unprovoked, and flees immediately afterward. And with the ability to fly, it's almost impossible to predict where it will strike next. Which has the kindred of the city jumping at shadows as paranoia begins to set in. And that's when the court is approached by a strange group of teenagers who wish to offer their assistance in getting their wayward "sister" under control!

                218) Dirty Laundry
                Every night for the last four weeks, between 2am and 3am (Eastern Standard Time) there is a live web stream called Dirty Laundry. It can be found just by making a search of the title. Despite many, many attempts to remove the sites and the mirrors hosting this stream, another one and another one always seems to pop up. The Masquerade seems to be secure as any mortal, ghoul, mage, or lupine that is shown the stream only sees a black screen and only hears a faint static. As far as anyone can tell only Kindred can see the show.

                The stream itself shows two well-dressed news anchors, a nosferatu and daeva. They report on Kindred politics at a variety of locations. Most importantly, their information appears to be incredibly accurate. They’ve already exposed schemes that have left the Prince of Orlando deposed, a covenant in Santa Fe completely wiped out, several coteries along the west coast turning nomad, and a Lancea Bishop committing suicide.
                Some Kindred think this is the best thing since bottled blood. Others say that this news station needs to be destroyed for the good of all. Others are on the fence seeing the inherent risk, but enjoying the manipulative elders being exposed for all to see.
                So what do you want to do?


                219) Dirty Laundry 2: Even Dirtier
                After a few days of trying of trying to investigate the web cast you get an email. It’s from someone named Cicero says they are looking to expand coverage in your area and offers you a job as a reporter. All it requires is a keen eye, an open ear, and a quiet mouth. Take a few videos of minor meeting between Kindred, maybe write a couple paragraphs about the local covenants, maybe some petty theft or mind reading here and there. They also are willing to pay your heart's desire, whether money, power, blood, or something of your choice.

                    220) From the Depths Below. (Best suited at a coastal city)
                    The Mariners visited last Elysium. They were an odd bunch of Kindred, though individually they were different. Webbed hands and feet, black eyes, one of them even had long tail coming out of her back. All who have taken to living beneath the waves, and who have been changed through Protean or other means to adapt to unlife in the sea. They’re mostly Gangrel, but there is a Nosferatu and even a Daeva with them, and they speak of other clan members back in their undersea community, almost a city unto itself.
                    They come with an offer for any Kindred to join them. There are no sects, no politics in the ocean. Blood is plentiful once you learn to enjoy the taste of fish, though they say whale blood is nearly as sustaining as human. Go deep enough and there’s no sunlight. Havens are crude but they can be secure, and there is plenty of room for all. Any who join will be taught how to be a Mariner and guided on how to survive as sea-vampire until they are ready to make it on their own.
                    Some Kindred, especially the neonates, are looking at it as a way out of a twisted, incestuous system of abuse and power-mongering. Word spreads to a few of the exiles out in the barrens who think it might be a good place to lay low for a while. Things really come to a head when an elder agrees to join them.

                                                        229) The time approaches, and the Lord of the Harvest requires a blood sacrifice to ensure a bountiful crop for this year's herd. The Circle of the Crone is only too happy to feed this ancient pagan lord, for he infuses the land with his divine essence, making those who consume its bounty grow strong and healthy. Perhaps that wouldn't be so bad, just another missing persons case gone unsolved. But that's not all there is to it. And unfortunately for the rest of the city, none of the Circle are willing to volunteer themselves. And unfortunately for you personally, your coterie has been chosen as this year's sacrifice!

                                                            230) Well, the Prince is pregnant. She tried to hide it but a chance meeting with some gossipy harpies has spread the secret as far away as the suburbs. Almost everyone is surprised, as only a few elders and scholars are aware that Kindred pregnancy is even a possibility.
                                                            The factions are reacting stereotypically. Invictus and Carthians are planning on using the pregnancy, and the Prince’s subsequent physical weakening, to gain more power. The Lance wants the Prince sent to Final Death before the abomination can be born. The Ordo want to study the entire process and the creature that comes out. The Circle wants to either sacrifice it or make it their god or something (no one really knows with the Crones)

                                                            231) There’s a Kindred named Dr. Netchurch who has seen this phenomenon before and is rumored to have even assisted in the birth. He just happens to live on the other side of the country and rarely leaves his domain. He’s also rumored to be a Malkovian.
                                                            Optional twist: (He’s actually one of Belial’s Brood masquerading as a Malkovian, and thinks that a Dhampir child could be a keystone towards becoming the Anti-Man.)

                                                            232) Maury Show.
                                                            The Prince has a moderate derangement of Nymphomania and indulges in sex regularly. There a few, maybe even several, possibilities of who the father is. Find him, get him out of the city or in the Prince’s mansion before any of the other Kindred can figure out who it is. Or find him and sell him to the highest bidder. Or keep the father and make your own power play.
