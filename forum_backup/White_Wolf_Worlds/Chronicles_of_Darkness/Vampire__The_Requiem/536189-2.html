                                        Found this thread because I was looking up "insect Gangrel" because of an enemy NPC duo I had- a Melissidae and her Gangrel sister/bodyguard.

                                        Protean is super flexible in B+S, and it is largely a matter of fluff. Armor and Claws are options, and there's nothing stopping you from saying that when you use them you're turning into a lobsterman. And that's not even counting making up your own powers based on animal abilities. Sticky tongue and 360 degree vision? Chameleon. Biological arm cannon? Mantis shrimp. Ability to sense electricity? More than one fish can do it. Spraying spider-man-style sticky webs? Velvet worm. Animals are weird, man. So there's nothing stopping you from describing existing powers as manifesting in buggy ways or making your own.

                                        Now, Fluff is where things get interesting. You brought up that some people don't even think of insects as animals, and that's something to consider- even other vampires might think a vampire who's really into bugs is weird.
