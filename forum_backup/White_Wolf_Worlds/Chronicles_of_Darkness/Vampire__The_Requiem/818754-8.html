    Originally posted by Blakemikizuki View Post
    there is a Ordo Dracul coil that allows the Dragon to gain supernatural merits and minor template merits.
    There's a highly controversial playtest Coil that's had a lot of feedback and is going to be revised. The final product could change a lot.

    And you are heavily into crossover territory here, to the point this isn't even really a Vampire question anymore. Its a question about what happens when you mix a werewolf and a mortal psychic ability. And, with all things crossover, this is going to be entirely up to individual STs and how they run things. I personally wouldn't let you halve the time with Biokinesis. I always round up, so 1 Bashing / half round rounds up to 1 bashing / round. Others will say different things. And all of them will be right, because there is no way we'll ever have an official answer for this entirely niche section.

    So would the Dragon be able to reduce the damage by two steps. Example of being hit by a grenade would normally do 4 aggravated, so would the vampire take 4 lethal or 4 bashing?
    Vampires aren't Uratha. They're still vampires. If you are allowed to take "A Wolf's Meat," you only gain what is specifically in that Tell. You become vulnerable to silver, take agg, and break out into a burn/rash from touching it, and you regenerate 1 bashing/1 lethal per turn.

    At no point do you gain the ability to shrug off damage. Silver allergy and flesh regeneration is it. There is no Tell for changing what kind of damage you take besides gaining silver as a bane.

                                "Kill yourself with this" or "shoot your own head" both fit in the four word limit for Dominate 1. Though an argument could be made that attempted suicide should trigger a Breaking Point, which would resolve the Mesmerized Condition. With Dominate 2, the character has to succeed the Breaking Point roll to resolve Dominated. So my ruling would be that you can give the command with Dominate 1, but they'll come back to their senses before they can pull the trigger. With Dominate 2, they'll go through with it if they fail the Breaking Point roll, but if they succeed they'll snap out of it.
