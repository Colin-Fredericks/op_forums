    Ghost plot needed!

    02-29-2020, 12:38 AM
    So my coterie has a hospital in a low income neighborhood as their domain. They discovered they were being observed by an angry ghost and followed it to an abandoned chapel in the basement near the morgue which led them to a crypt that the hospital had been built on top of. They know that Italian vampires (the Sangiovanni bloodline) had been responsible for the hospital and that the ghost only speaks Italian (which none of them do). They went down into the crypt and found vampire skulls (obviously surprising) and a torpid Sangiovanni elder among the mortal bodies. There is another passage they haven’t explored yet.

    Here’s the catch, I was basically pulling one thing after another out of my butt and I have been wracking my brain to put a story behind all this. Anyone have an idea? I need a way to bring all these random bits into a coherent story.

    If it makes any difference, the coterie consists of a Gangrel con artist/seductress (no Covenant), a Nosferatu formerly abused wife/fighter (Carthian), and a Mekhet hematologist (Ordo Dracul). They have a Mekhet karaoke KJ (Carthian) with them as the Mekhet pc didn’t have Auspex so they needed someone who could see what was watching them.

      What was an Italian Sangiovanni doing nesting in a hospital in this presumably non-Italian domain? Have you established or hinted at any reason for that?

      What I am going to suggest: that's not a ghost. Or rather, it's very like a ghost, but the vampire skulls strongly suggest that this necromancer has been up to some cannibalistic rituals down in this crypt. The presence of skulls is abnormal; vampire skeletons normally lose their cohesion and fall to ash without the vampire's undead blood to sustain them (unless these are skulls of some other vampiric creature that one wouldn't call Kindred...) The fact that the ghost speaks the same out-of-place tongue as the Sangiovanni suggests a few things, all of them tying to the necromancer: that the ghost is a long-time thrall brought from the old country, that the ghost was selected from communities the necromancer feels comfortable with...

      ...or the ghost is the torpid necromancer, or one of the necromancer's brood watching over him in torpor. The beneficiary of a necromantic rite which uses the dead and preserved remains of something that was killed when already dead to raise a vampire's consciousness in a ghostly state. Are the skulls Anchors for the vampiric ghost? Necessary arrangements which could disrupt the effects of the rite if moved out of alignment? Necessary to conduct the rite itself and kept should the rite ever need repeated? Are they just ghoulish trophies, preserved over the course of the rite but no longer necessary except to set the proper milieu for the necromantic crypt?

      Has the coterie just disturbed the central haven of an already angered ghost-lord after taking over the territory wherein he still resides? Or did the lord of the land deliberately lure them to the heart of his power?

          Originally posted by Stupid Loserman View Post
          The presence of skulls is abnormal; vampire skeletons normally lose their cohesion and fall to ash without the vampire's undead blood to sustain them (unless these are skulls of some other vampiric creature that one wouldn't call Kindred...)
          Vampires decompose to their "true age" when they die, meaning skeletons are usually the only remains that doesn't turn to liquid or dust.

          I like the idea of the ghost being the torpid elder, but an elder should know better than to draw people (especially other vampires) towards his own torpid body. That's just asking to be killed or diablerised. So unless the players managed to surreptitiously follow the ghost, it would probably make more sense for the ghost to be a servant.

          I wonder how the PCs discovered the ghost in the first place. Generally, ghosts don't leave any traces of their presence unless they use powers or someone happens to be sensitive to ghosts. It seems to me that the ghost was doing more than just observing them (especially if they know it speaks Italian). It might be that the ghost was wilfully drawing their attention and leading them towards the hospital and torpid elder. If it's a servant, then maybe it managed to break free from whatever bindings the necromancer put on it, or maybe it found a loophole in its commands that allowed it to act maliciously towards the elder while still complying with its mission?

          As for the skulls, you could take some inspiration from Path of Bones from Vampire: The Masquerade. It's a Necromancy Path that allows the user to see through the eyes of a skull or to gain memories from the skull's original "owner". Since they're vampires, they might be the elder's enemies that they mine for information, or old teachers that hold mystical secrets they haven't yet mastered, Sith holocron-style.

Figure the coterie can communicate with the ghost because it's the 21st century in the NWoD, too. Getting hold of a speech-to-text app with Google Translate shouldn't be too hard for them.

              And now the PCs have to wake up their "guest" to figure out what's going on. They might end up getting a new patron/mentor out of it. Or another scary-ass enemy.

                      Well, the first thing to answer before anything else, imho, is - did the ghost bring the PCs to the abandoned chapel unwittingly or by intent? Did it know they were somehow following its tracks? Because defining that can make loads of difference on establishing what the heck it's doing, relation to the inhabitant(s) of the crypt and possible objectives in relation to them.

                      In fact, how did they track it down, is it unable to turn invisible or cross through walls? Because that could put some serious crimp & complications in a chase sequence. Or did they use Auspex or some other supernatural/magic trick to detect some sort of ectoplasmic spoor or whatever?

                      Try to remember that stuff as it can help with giving us a clearer idea of the pieces in the puzzle - and don't be affraid to say "I didn't think about it, just said whatever, did it the 'mundane/normal way' and made a roll or two", because if your group did not ask, is because they just went with the flow and didn't pay that much attention either.

                      What else can you tell us about the ghost - does it seem to be male or female, young or old, tall or short, portly or gangly, what kind of clothes does it seem to wear or is it unclear, the ghost a distorted, deformed, half-naked barely thing, just a vague translucent animated outline or something else altogether? How does it look?

                      You described it as an "angry italian that speaks no other language" - well, what does it do to make it seem angry in the first place? Is it the way it speaks annd gesticulates or does it forcefully shake or throw objects (or people) around poltergeist style.

                      Also, how did an italian that speaks no other language end up dead in the domain? Was it a tourist, immigrant (legal or illegal) or was brought as already a ghost from its homeland for whatever reason? Details, details, the more you remember, the more consistent you can make things seem to the players.
