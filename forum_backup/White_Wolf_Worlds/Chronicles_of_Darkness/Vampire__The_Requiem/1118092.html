    Ventrue gripe

    07-19-2017, 02:27 AM
    So something bothered me for some time now. We have Ventrue - lords and senators, generals, ceos. People who even as vampires crave worldly power over others. And we have Daeva - social predators who tend to get obsessed over people. Now why do Ventrue have Dominate instead of Majesty? Dominate is not effective at ordering around large groups of people or at projecting your power around. It is very much a "go with me to the toilet power". Meanwhile Majesty lets you control masses and flaunt your weight around. Why weren't those swapped outside of tradition?

              Dominate is about being in control. Majesty is about being a rock star.

              Dominate lets you tailor the behaviour of the people around you, no strings attached or jilted exes. Your slaves won't even know they're in your thrall if you don't want them to, whereas a Daeva -- with all his glamor -- is going to be remembered by his victims for a very long time. Yes, you have less control over crowds, but crowds are messy. A Ventrue finds the keystones in the room and waits to give them the ever so innocuous signal to start the riot. She always has plausible deniability. Or maybe she does rule openly. Dominate is a terrifying threat: above all, people fear loss of control. Of consent. We hate the idea that we could do something we don't want to do. A Ventrue doesn't even need to use her Dominate if her minions fear what she might do with it. She's dictator for unlife, and no one will question it.

              Majesty lets you be the superstar you always knew you were. What's the point in having worshipers if they don't know what they're doing? A Daeva's victims have that singularly human, singularly dangerous quality: obsession. A Ventrue -- for all her power -- can only manufacture loyalty, and if she ever slips, she's going to be in trouble. The Daeva's thralls can think and problem solve, and they do it all for love. Yes, you have less direct control over people, but slaves have a nasty habit of revolting. A Daeva convinces his followers that it was their idea to carry him in a palanquin, and he doesn't need to mess around with their memories to achieve that effect. Fear isn't the point, although he may be very frightening. The point is that the little people look up to you and think you're god. You're the man of the people, and you can almost believe it.

              A Ventrue doesn't care if people love her. A Daeva doesn't care who knows.

                  The Ventrue are not about worldly power so much as they are about control — there are Ventrue who rule rural trailer parks with great satisfaction. The Daeva, meanwhile, are creatures of particular passion — the nature of their obsessive mentality is rooted more in addiction and vulnerability than risk aversion and possessiveness.

                  Dominate is not effective at ordering around large groups of people or at projecting your power around.
                  Majesty, meanwhile, is not effective at getting people to do things outside the purview of regular and relatively immediate social influence; a plain Daeva can't erase your memories or seed trigger phrases or inhabit your body. A Daeva with middling prowess in Majesty can make you want to do things for them when they ask, but a Ventrue with middling prowess in Dominate can make you do things for them whether you want to or not, with or without being there to ask.

                  Meanwhile Majesty lets you control masses and flaunt your weight around. Why weren't those swapped outside of tradition?
                  The ability to turn people into undyingly loyal followers for a few nights at the height of your power has its appeal, certainly, but it is dreadfully imprecise and limited in its reach when compared to the ability to mold a population's behavior to your specifications and rewrite local history with marginally less investment.

                      Dominate is about control. Memory erasing, the creation of sleeper agents, general ordering around, and possessing people if you need to get the job done right. Dominate is a very good tool for control, and it is very hard to rival it. The Ventrue are all modern day kings, and thus they can use dominate to arrange things as they see fit.

                      Majesty is about love; no, not the happy go lucky love that is typically associated with the word. We're talking about a darker love, a more obssessive kind, the kind that people get hurt over. Majesty can turn anyone into an obsessed lover who will do anything for the person they love. Above all, Daeva want to be loved and worshipped.

                      Dominate is about control, and unless you slip then you can expect what your slaves are going to do. Majesty is about obsessive love, and while you can generally expect what your slaves are going to do they still have the free will to attack your other slaves over jealousy.

                      It also probably doesn't help that I've seen some STs treat Majesty as an area of effect Dominate, so that could lead to some confusion.
