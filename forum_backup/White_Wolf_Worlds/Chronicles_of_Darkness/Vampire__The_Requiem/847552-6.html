                                Have you considered the Will to Power Twist? Basically, Willpower is assigned to one of the Aspirations a player has and can only be spend to move towards that goal. They just can't bring themselves to invest in what isn't a goal. Throw in Willpower refresh only upon goal completion (or a milestone) and suddenly all Venture are driven by nature, being completely despondent in loss and exuberant in victory! (It would also model the idea that the Ven always win: It doesn't help them win but it does add a cost to pursuing hopeless goals. Better attach yourself to the winning horse than back the underdog unless you have a trump card.)
