    Although this topic is probably death, I'll put the Coil version I showed my players. It had some restructuring compared to the last, and some very minor changes to the scales because of it. Thank you very much for all your feedback, goodbye.

    The Dragon's Path (Coil of the Ascetic)

    Life Flow •
    At this basic level, the Dragon's trained to increase the flow and capacity of life, recreating its semblance to ingest normal food with willpower alone. The Dragon can hold one additional Vitae for each dot in the Coil of the Ascetic, and activate the Blush of Life with a Willpower point instead of one Vitae but only if her body has Vitae.

    Eliminating Desire ••
    A Dragon on this path is resistant to Vitae's echoing temptation, developing greater endurance against the Beast's hunger than the uninitiated. The Dragon is immune to Vitae Addiction, and ignores up to half his number of dots in the Coil of the Ascetic rounded up, in Hungry and Starving penalties to Frenzy.

    Escaping Dukkha •••
    Increasing the life flow, the Dragon can enter a state accepting of the life of lesser forms. Add the Dragon's dots in the Coils of the Ascetic to the limit of Vitae obtainable by performing the Feed move. At this level, while benefiting from the Blush of Life, the Dragon can garner substance from humans regardless of Blood Potency.

    Above The Blood Karma ••••
    All prey is equal, that is to say, beneath the Dragon, humans, animals and even those of unnatural origin. The Dragon may inflict The Assault or The Kiss on non-humans with a bite, or use Lashing Out against them, without the need to spend a Willpower point as if they were human. At this level, while benefiting from the Blush of Life, the Dragon can garner substance from animals regardless of Blood Potency.

    Unlife's Fulfillment •••••
    The Dragon is most satisfied with taking the life within a prey or the precious liquid that carried it. The Dragon gains an additional Vitae for every 2 obtained from cold blood. Furthermore, taking the last point of Vitae available in living prey provides 3 additional points of Vitae to the Dragon, and does 5 points of Aggravated Damage in addition to any other damage to the victim.
    At this level, while benefiting from the Blush of Life, the Dragon's Blood Potency is considered 1 for the purpose of drinking cold blood, and feeding awards the Sated Condition.

    Sample Scales of the Ascetic

    Acid Pills
    Prerequisite coil: Unlife's Fulfillment

    Procedure: The Dragon makes a few pills of exotic animal bones such as elephant tusks or tiger fangs and other arcane ingredients; then, proceeds to ingest them using the Blush of Life.
    Outcome: The pill combined with the revitalization of the vampire organs produce a rapid digestion and absorption of nutrients allowing the vampire to eat normal food and break it into their Vitae without the need to vomit later. The food ingested while on the pill tastes the same as when the vampire was alive and resolves the Wanton Condition. The pill deals 2 points of Bashing Damage If they're ingested and nothing else is consumed. Taking more than one pill per day causes severe acid burn dealing 4 points of Bashing Damage for each overdose pill. Each douse works correctly for roughly an hour or until around 2 Pounds of food are consumed. If the food is raw fresh meat, ingesting 2 Pounds of it produces enough substance for 2 Vitae points: one effectively human blood, the other animal blood.

    The Coal Heart
    Prerequisite coil: Above The Blood Karma

    Procedure: The Dragon prepares 8 black oily substances made of 8 different animals poisons. The concoctions must be used to coat 8 needles that are injected in nerve points in the body causing a total of 4 points of Bashing Damage that the Vampire must heal expelling all the needles in the right order.
    Outcome: The Vampire's heart compacts itself becoming a hard dark mass like coal. Even while immobilized or unconscious, attempts to strike the heart are at -5 instead of -3 and must surpass an additional general armor of 2; this boons last 24 hours. The vampire must spend a Willpower point to keep the benefits during daysleep avoiding the Cleansing. The heart may momentarily regain its medical standard size and color and loose this benefits using the Blush of Life. It'll shrink again regaining all the benefits within the 24 hours when the vampire whishes it.

    Ghoulish Mantra
    Prerequisite coil: Life Flow

    Procedure: The Dragon must recite this mantra while in deep meditation along with a Ghoul. The process requires to breathe the fumes from burning human remains no more than a week old with mystic herbs and paper drawn symbols, inducing a strong hypnosis which forces the Ghoul's body to link itself with the vampire and transform its very core. This process is very strenuous, demanding a Willpower point from the Dragon and the Ghoul to initiate the ritual; then, the Dragon starts an extended Intelligence + Occult roll with a target of 8 successes and half an hour intervals.
    Dramatic Failure: The Vampire gets the Dependent and Swooning Conditions towards the Ghoul and the Ghoul gets the Anchor Condition connected to a ghost related to the burned corpse as the process recoils and end. The Storyteller is encouraged to introduce a vengeful ghost.
    Failure: The Vampire loses a Vitae point and gets the Stumbled Condition.
    Success: The Ghoul can now hold a number of additional Vitae equal to the Dragon's dots in the Coils of the Ascetic plus her Blood Potency for an equal number of months. If the process is attempted multiple times on a single Ghoul, only the latest success remain. As a side effect, the Dragon inflicts the Mesmerized Condition on the Ghoul.
    Exceptional Success: All the normal benefits of an Exceptional Success on an extended roll, but nothing more.

            Yes, I'm using the coil of the wyrm but with the name 'The Way of the Dragon.' My setting is basically a Invictus regimen of Mekhet/ventrue with a different name and split into three branches, there is also the Lance, and the Academy; no Carthians or Crone. The Academy fills in for the Ordo Dracul, but only developed the coils of the Wyrm and the Ascetic as their base. Old Beijing Vampires had some issues with the Strix, so vampires walking in the sun was never a good notion, and so the coil of the ascendant was never developed nor the Voivode without a direct Ordo Dracul instruction.They do, however, have the coils of Ziva and Zirnitra as secret coils, and will try to get their hands on the coils my players will bring.

            But right now, the Coterie hasn't made it to the city yet, just a traveling session left. I'm developing the city with a returning player who will be a new character and their contact in Beijing. The coterie will be occupied just trying to hide/fit in at first, and beat the mistrust everyone have for them and not get killed because the locals think they brought Malkavia with them.
