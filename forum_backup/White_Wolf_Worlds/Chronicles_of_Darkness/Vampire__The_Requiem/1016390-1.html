                The other was a Carthian offering any member of the Movement in good standing tutelage in some of the Devotions she'd accumulated over the years. She listed her Twitter account, which was ericateachesnyc. She's Daeva; that's why they skew so heavily toward Majesty.

                                    Completely irrelevant media reference and language tirade ahead:

                                    Ironically, anyone that's seen Samurai Champloo might recall an episode where Fuu is trying to teach Mugen to read and write in Japanese. About half way through the episode, there's a bit where he's asked to read a block of text. He calls out the no kana (の), recognizing it. He notes it a few more times in the text.

                                    の is one of the single most common kana you'll see in a piece of Japanese writing, because it denotes possessives. It's essentially the same as adding apostrophe-s to the end of a word. So in this case, the bit I had in that description was 羽の飛翔. 羽 literally means feather, and 飛翔 means flight. So, it's basically a feather's flight. And if you're romanizing it, it's literally Whatever-No. Or in this case, hane-no-hisho (pronounced like hah nay no he show basically.)

                                    Mugen knew that particular kana because it's used pretty constantly. I was briefly tickled when that kana was called out there.

                                    So if anyone's interested in strangely obscure cross-language reference humor, there you go!
