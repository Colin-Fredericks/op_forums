                        Originally posted by falco1029 View Post
                        So... anyone else notice the Chorister merit is much much better than each of the other pre-status merits? Like it's almost to the point of ridiculous.
                        Strictly speaking, it's the Carthian and Invictus versions that are weaker than the orher three. Chorister, Laity, and Independent Study all allow the character to learn the Covenant's brand of Sorcery if they have a teacher. It's just that the Circle of the Crone is more liberal with teaching their Sorcery Discipline to preliminary members than the Lancea et Sanctum or the Ordo Dracul (and the Ordo is more strict about it than the Lance, if the presentation is any indication). Although, the Circle only allows Choristers to learn the first dot of Cruac. Technically Sanctified Laity can learn up to five dots of Theban if their teacher is willing to place that much trust in them, and Independent Scholars can learn any number of Coils and Scales that their mentors are willing to risk teaching them under the table. On the other hand, Choristers can learn Acolyte Merits, so that's a pretty significant perk. One Foot in the Door doesn't allow you to be a leige in Invictus Oaths, but it doesn't explicitly disallow it like Casual User does for Carthian Law. Although, Casual Users do get a vote, just like Status-carrying Carthians do.

                        This actually just further emphasizes why I like that they're separate merits. They tell us a lot about the cultures of the Covenants and how much each Covenant trusts it's preliminary members with its secrets, relative to the other four.
