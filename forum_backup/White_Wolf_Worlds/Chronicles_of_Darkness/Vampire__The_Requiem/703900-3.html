                                        Sapience is the ability to make decisions AND act based on common sense with a full understanding to where one will not act or behave in a manner the individual deems as wrong or harmful. It is not an 'either you have it or you don't' trait. And no vinculums and Dominate aren't the same but they still strip away one's sapience. Vinculums incrementally erode an individual's sapience to the the point of where your regnant can talk you into doing nearly anything in seconds. Dominate outright eliminates sapience by taking away any opportunity to make decisions and compels you to act as directed, but experiencing direct physical harm while carrying out the activity ends control immediately and self-destructive actions are not possible until the pinnacle level. And you don't remember doing whatever it was you were made to do. Majesty immediately strips away one's sapience by compelling certain behaviors even when they go against one's better judgement, are morally repulsive, or are harmful. You don't get to refuse, you just get to feel like the popular kid likes you for doing it. (The reality is they wouldn't give a warm bucket of spit for you.) It quickly erodes one's sapience to the point to where ordering self-destruction is possible. This entire thread is nothing but a confirmation of how much one can get away with forcing someone to do at a given level of Majesty.
