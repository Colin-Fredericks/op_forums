    1001 Vampire Story Hooks

    12-21-2013, 01:07 PM
    1) A recent assassination attempt on the prince orchestrated by the Belial's Brood has succeeded. What this means is that the city currently doesn't have anything resembling a government for vampires.

    The PCs are free to do anything they want without fear of reprisal - break the Masquerade, kill anyone they want, Embrace anyone they want.

    Of course, the same applies to the few other vampires who're still left.

                                      10) Autumn brings the early dark, and local Kindred have become aware of an Orphanage where children play a game called "Prince of the Playground" at dusk. They wear piecemeal costumes of castaway adult garments. They form cliques, politicking for control of the slide, the merry-go-round, the swings, the sandbox. They use words like "Venture," "Gangrel," and "the Masquerade." Several children hold one down, tickling him. Howling with laughter, he shrieks, "Better stop that, or the Beast will get you!" Where did they learn to act like this? Who taught them? It's eerie how their movements mimic the Danse Macabre...like a miniature mummers play.

                                              12) Life as a kindred in the city is great. Crime is high, industry going strong, lots of people move in and out of town, and the police have about as much of a clue as a blind, deaf and mute Sherlock Holmes. And then something changes. Over night the city newspapers begin reporting, with precision, on the people the kindred have made vanish. Every single one, and only them. They're all called "mysterious disappearances", and it only takes five days before they start calling it a conspiracy, and on the sixth open discussion begins in the city. "How are we to find and punish whoever is responsible?"
                                              The strange thing? Any attempts at stopping them from writing the stories, from dominating editors, charming reporters or even killing folks, fail to produce the intended result.

                                                      14) A Nomad shows up at Court claiming to have inside knowledge from and unimpeachable, but unverifiable, source.
