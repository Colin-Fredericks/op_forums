        It's pretty simple. Humanity isn't morality. It represents the relative strength of your Beast versus your Man. Theban draws its spiritual/magical strength from a vampire's Man. Cruac draws its spiritual/magical strength from a vampire's Beast. To do more potent Cruac rituals, you need a more potent Beast. To do more potent Theban rituals, you need a more potent Man. It's metaphysics, not morality.
