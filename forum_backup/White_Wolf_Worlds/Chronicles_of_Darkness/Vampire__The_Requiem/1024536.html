    Diablerizing a Beast (the Primordial)

    12-30-2016, 07:11 PM
    This unusual situation came up as a result of my the last session of my VtR game. The game I'm running primarily focuses on Kindred, but there was a singular Beast (as in, Beast the Primordial) who served as a secondary antagonist largely focused on keeping the vampire population hemmed into a fairly small downtown area. So in a previous session two of my player characters came across the Beast in a park and angered her by not showing her the fear and respect that she demands from the Kindred population. She decided to try and put some fear into them and began imposing her Lair on the world, prompting them to defend themselves. The Nosferatu character used his Nightmare discipline, prompting the Beast to become terrified and run away, a humiliating act that fostered a powerful need for revenge in her. So, naturally, being a vindictive, petty Beast, she decided to invade the Nosferatu's haven, attempt to murder his Touchstone, then arrange for him to come across it and feed off of his terror.

    Things didn't go as said Beast planned. She failed to completely kill the Touchstone, and the Nosferatu managed to get a hold of her and sink his fangs in. Knowing that the Beast would keep coming back and attack his loved ones again and again, the Nosferatu used everything he had to take her down, and he managed to kill her by draining every ounce of blood in her body. Seeing as how this is basically the same manner in which a Kindred commits Diablerie, I talked with my group and we all decided that it seems like it'd be great if the amount of supernatural blood the Nosferatu imbibed caused some kind of permanent change in him. Fueling this idea is how oddly similar these two characters are on a thematic level. Both are creatures that utilize Nightmare fear-based powers, both are fairly weak physically but seek to create the illusion of extreme danger to keep enemies at bay, and both utilize shadows as their aesthetic theme (the Beast sinks into and teleports through shadows, watching people from within them, the Nosferatu's curse takes the form of shifting, whispering shadows swirling around him).

    Currently I'm a bit paralyzed as to what to give him, simply because there are so many possibilities, so I thought I'd see if anyone had any cool ideas. I'd like the boon to be fairly substantial (the Beast was of a greatly higher power than the Nosferatu), but I'd like it to also have a significant downside as well, perhaps a new bane. The Beast killed was an Eshmaki Nemesis with Lair 3 and lair traits of Darkness, Silence, and Maze. She utilized the atavisms Eye of Heaven, Shadowed Soul, and From the Shadows and the nightmares You Deserve This and Behold My True Form.

                          It's been a very fun game. This is me and everyone else in the group's first experience with VtR, and it's really the furthest I've ever come to running a straight sandbox style game too.
                          That Khaibit bloodline looks really appropriate on the surface but I'm not sure it's a great fit. The first dot it grants isn't really that useful (vampire's can already see in very low light) and all the other dots require a lot of prerequisite disciplines that would take a very long time for him to develop. I do like the idea of a unique bloodline though. I was thinking of having the drawback be some kind of new bane involving an aversion to bright light. Like ordinary bright lighting (like you'd get in a Walmart or something) would be uncomfortable and maybe give some penalties to certain dicepools (perception, resolve + composure rolls, etc.) while extreme bright lights (like floodlights) would be capable of bashing damage. Ideally it would accompany some power that makes him more capable in darkness.

                          Has anyone else had something of this sort come up in a game before? Vampires drinking from other supernatural creatures and gaining some effect or condition?

                                                  One potential avenue would be let the nosferatu impose lair traits (NEVER traits that interact with banes like fire or sunlight). In this case, give the bloodline a custom discipline loosely based off of how a beast's Lair trait works. The bloodline weakness should be that you can only gain nourishment from people who have just experienced a breaking point or are inside the lair. Have The Beast (vampire's beast) be what lurks in the lair.

                                                  Offhand, for the discipline.
                                                  Dot 1 lets you collect up to 1+(Discipline) Chambers. First one is your Heart, the rest must be collected. Your Beast lurks here in perpetual frenzy, wuth a spirit form like a beast's (no bonus traits).
                                                  Dot 2 You may impose Lair Traits on the world like a beast. You may impose one, but hold up to (Discipline).
                                                  Dot 3 You may bring your Lair to the real world and trap people there like a Beast can. Merge with your Beast when in your lair.
                                                  Dot 4 Basically primordial pathways + hold the gate.
                                                  Dot 5 You can diablerize spaces.

                                                  +1 for a bonus Nightmare and a weakness. Attracting heroes may be too much.
