                So, bringing a weapon isn't an automatic failure in negotiations. It IS pretty common for diplomats to have some security, after all.

                If you avoid stereotypes a little, you can do a lot better with Daeva. Go for 1 dot of each Discipline, or just Majesty 1 and Celerity 2.

                With Majesty 1 you ignore social penalties. It seems little, but what it really does is PRECISELY let you go with silver weapon to face them.

                From there, your actual capacity for negotiating will rely on Attributes and skills. Werewolfs do have Wolf-Blooded and Mortals as pack members nowadays, so you don't have to impress them so much. Some of them can have little respect for weaklings, but believe, most will respect whatever means you got for the Hunt if they are effective.

                And when things get sour, don't think about taking a werewolf down alone. It isn't about vampires not being able to take down one, there are a lot of ways to do so. But first, to be prepared for combat against most werewolfs means to spend most of your resources on combat. Second, a werewolf almost NEVER walks alone.

                Context will play a much larger role on combat, since your reasons to fight, target and goal will be very specifics. If you're a good diplomat, by the way, you can end up hunting a werewolf WITH OTHER werewolfs, and them you will have a function for the pack precisely by not having the same habilities.

                Of course, if you don't manage this, go for fellow kindred to help you. To go alone against a pack in their territory is a suicidal proposition, and to fend off an attack you can surely count on other vampires. Or just run with Celerity.

                    Now consider the case werewolf attack you (maybe cause you won't use the active power of celerity): he make 21+2 for the claw - 14 (vampire defense)= 9 lethal damage.....- 5 using resilence = 4 lethal damage...not so much....Edit: or maybe bashing I'm not sure

                    All this to say that both the race have the chance for a fair "duel".
                    In the real case where you have to build a normal vampire you could chose different way to defeat a werewolf. Maybe using obfuscate and domination or majesty properly. Anyway all depends on the stats of your opponents.


                    EDIT: Anyway without aggravated damage the story is so far different cause if you don't make more lethal damage of the health of the Gauru in one turn, in the enxt he will completely heal himself for free and you have to start again. In this way at long time you will finish vitae and die :P Or you could use obfuscate to go away or make some kind of backstabb, or dominate to let him go away and save your life
                    Not really sure this is such a fair fight, I mean the vampire in question has 5 celerity, 5 resilience, 5 vigor and 4 protean right? That's 67 experiences worth of disciplines. The Werewolf only has Crimson spasm (15 experiences to get Purity 5) + Berserker's Might (2) + 15 to get Glory 5 = 32 Experiences - less than half what the Vampire has.

                    With the other 35 experiences the Wolf could buy; Primal Strength (2) to add + Purity Strength, Slaughterer (2) + Purity Damage, the Relentless Assualt (4) Merit allowing him to retain his defense when making all out attacks, trade 1 attack die for 1 extra damage, the Living Weapon merit (5) to increase his damage by 1 and ignore non-magical armour, the Favoured Form Merit (4) to gain +1 Strength, +1 Stamina in Gauru, Embodiment of the Firstborn (5) to gain +1 Strength, We'll throw in the Fearless Hunter gift (2) to add his Glory Renown (5) against all mind-influencing powers and since we still have 11 experiences unspent we'll pay 5 to get him to Primal Urge 6, pay another 1 to give him the fifth dot of Favoured Form which will let him apply the advanced action quality to brawl rolls in Gauru (roll your pool twice, take the best result) and the last 5 experiences to buy Tongue of Flame from Gift of the Elementals which will let him use fire against the Vampire if he wants.

                    The revised dice pools now stand at
                    Werewolf in Gauru attack pool: 5 Str + 3 (Gauru) + 2 (Merits that increase Strength) + 8 (your number for Crimson Spasm) + 2 for all-out attack + 5 (Gift of Strength) + 5 Brawl -1 (Relentless Assault to gain +1 damage) = 29 dice, with 8-again from Killer Instinct (1st dot Rahu Auspice Gift) and with the advanced action quality
                    Werewolf in Gauru damage pool: 2 (teeth) + 1 (relentless assualt) + 1 (living weapon) + 5 (slaughterer) = + 9 lethal (Predator's Jaws on p.98 specifies that a Werewolf's bite causes lethal to Vampires even in Hishu)
                    Werewolf Defense: 9 (Dex/Wits + Athletics)
                    Werewolf Health: 5 (size) + 5 (Stamina) + 4 (Gauru) + 1 (Favored Form) + 5 (Warrior's Hide - 2nd dot Rahu Auspice Gift) = 20

                    Vampire attack pool: 5 Str + 5 (Frenzy) + 5 (Passive Vigor) + 4 (2 vitae) + 5 Brawl = 24
                    Vampire damage pool: +0 agg (claws of the unholy) + 5 (Active Vigor) = + 5 agg
                    Vampire defense: 9 (Gauru prevents you from adding any skill to defense so just Dex/Wits + Celerity)
                    Vampire Health: 5 (size) + 5 (Stamina) + 5 (Resilience) = 15

                    I've ignored the Efficient Killer merit because I think the drawback ("This Merit only works on living targets with discernible weak points") rules out Vampires (if there's something to contradict this I'd love to know).

                    So the Vampire is going to be getting about 5 successes. That's 10 agg damage minus 5 from the Werewolf's Berserker's Might = 5 agg. The Wolf will be getting at least 6 successes (I've no idea how to account for 8-again + advanced action), which is roughly 15 lethal damage minus 5 from Resilience = 10 lethal. With their respective Health pools the Vampire will die after 3 rounds while the Wolf will still be alive (though the Vampire also runs out of Blood after 3 rounds, so he's kind of screwed either way). In my opinion the best option for the Vampire is to forgoe defense and throw everything into a hail mary attack roll (all-out attack + vigor + 4 vitae bonus) and hope he gets lucky.

                            Originally posted by PenDragon View Post
                            To answer that question: Yes, I'd say it could. With the addendum that your storyteller is (hopefully) aware of your intentions and won't deliberately pit you against Werewolves you cannot possibly handle.
                            This is probably the best answer for your actual question - your effectiveness is always going to be down to how your ST makes your enemies.

                            Though, I do suggest that, if you're going to be more of a mediator, I'd put some effort into being good at Social Maneuvering. Sympathetic, as a merit, is insanely good for that, as is Fast Talking and Pusher - five doors with the first meeting is nothing to sneer at, and its relatively inexpensive to buy. The first dot of Majesty to make sure you get off to a good impression (it eliminates social penalties, which helps prevent them from looking down, and helps with that initial Impression). You really only need the first dot.

                            Talk to your ST about maybe getting some alternative Protean 2 powers, like pheromones, or something, or a social boost. There's no need for all of it to be physical.

                            EDIT -
                            Originally posted by Regentwill View Post
                            The game in question is an online chat setting, I would most likely be dealing with other players of the same XP level as I would be.
                            Hmmm... in that case... forget what I said above about the ST and enemies. I recommend going with a grapple build - get Protean 4 then invest in Vigor. You get Protean + Vigor (vitae-enhanced) as automatic successes to grapple someone, which is difficult for even Werewolves to overcome. It makes a good lead in to feeding (so invest in Unnatural Affinity too), plus you'll potentially be dealing unhealable agg with damage moves.

                            Talk to the STs about how the Choke Hold merit actually works in that game - with the automatic successes from your claws and Vigor, you can easily knock them unconscious, though how long that time will be when Gauru has such a high Stamina is open to question. I don't think the Grapple FS will help in this situation, though it might be something for fighting non-wolves.

                            Have a high Wits+Composure score, maybe with some Trained Observer. I suspect that the wolves may end up using Efficient Killer when combined with Cheap Shot, making it a killing blow if their Dex+Subterfuge check beats your perception check.
