            Me personally? If I'm not sure, I would ask myself if a human or ghoul member can do something. If not, what is the reason and does that reason apply to revenants. So in answer to your question, if a revenant can find a notary willing to notarize their oath and a liege willing to accept their service, yes they can participate in an oath. (Given the social stigma of being a revenant or affiliated with a Masquerade breaching hunting screw up waiting to happen, that is going to be unlikely. But I can think of a few instances of an Invictus member agreeing as leverage or a boon to the revenant's creator also.)
            If they can join the Carthian Movement, they can use Carthian Law and count as a voting member.

            Personally, I have a revenant character in my setting and he knows a few Theban Sorcery rituals from his time as a ghoul and he can use them. His regnant was an inquisitor and the Sanctified bishop in the domain considers his state of revenancy to be a divine decree that he is intended for damnation and any powers he had innately are a gift from God. His regnant will uplift him once his murderers are brought to justice, one way or another.

                    I interpret the book on a very literal level. So this is only my interpretation of it. Others will likely have their own opinions.

                    Level one says, "This level of Auspex can only answer immediate questions about danger or weakness"
                    I take immediate as meaning right now. Will "she"( whoever your she is ) hurt me? Hurt John? > right now < Not did they, will they next week or whatever. So I don't see any of those questions being about an immediate strength or weakness. I would not allow them for level 1.
                    Unless she was trying to pick a fight with you and knew John was hiding in the next room waiting to ambush you, or something.

                    Level two says: "This level of Auspex focuses on questions concerning the secrets and weaknesses of a single character." And "The Beast sniffs out the victim’s dark secrets, things that she doesn’t want anyone else to know."

                    So your #1 Falls directly into that category. A bane is a major weakness as they often lead to your character getting hurt.
                    #2 is her knowing John some sort of terrible secret or weakness to her character? if not, then it doesn't fall into this category.
                    #3 is a little iffy. I suppose it is a secret that she knows, but it's John's secret and he is apparently not there. But I would probably let them both slide.
                    #4 Absolutely. I would say it's closely related to "psychological vulnerability." As you generally dont want secrets to get out because emotionally someone can get hurt ( or even physically )
                    #5 Again, is this some sort of secret or weakness on her part? If not, then it doesn't fall into this category.

                        By my standards... I don't allow Auspex omniscience or unlimited fishing expeditions. I also don't allow questions or commands at any level that use out of character terminology. So commands/requests to only hit someone until you do x levels of damage or questions that make direct reference to Humanity scores, Banes, Attribute ratings Health, Dirge, Mask etc. yield nothing. Find a way to phrase it using in world terminology.

                        Level 1 or 2 only. Higher levels of Auspex may have different results.

                        1a. Nothing.
                        1b. Something current or recent will be revealed.
                        2a. Unless there's a specific reason the person doesn't want others to know John, No result.
                        2b. See 2a.
                        3. Nothing. You should be directing the power at John to find his secrets, not a 3rd party.
                        4. The "deepest" secret is beyond the scope of level two, but some current and pressing concern would be revealed.
                        5. Usually falls in the realm of Auspex omniscience. Unless the last meeting was meant to be a secret, the question yields nothing.

                                        Who says the word "Bane" isn't in-world?

                                        Every single Vampire has banes. Keep in mind that Banes aren't just the three you can pick. Clan Banes qualify. Fire and sunlight are Banes. Bloodline weaknesses are also Banes.

                                        Bane is an in-world term. Fire and sunlight are "great banes" in specific.

                                        The real trick is how to word the Auspex question so that you don't get "sunlight" as your answer. That's pretty useless. And I wouldn't allow "what are all of Max's banes" as a question, since you don't get multiple answers for Auspex questions.
