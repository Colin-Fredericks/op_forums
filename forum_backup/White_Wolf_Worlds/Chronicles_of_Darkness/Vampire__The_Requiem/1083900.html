    I want to try and commit Diablerie for the first time. Looking for advice.

    04-26-2017, 05:25 AM
    So, as the title says, I'm looking for some help in pulling of a successful Diablerie. I've been playing VtM and VtR (mostly the latter these days.) for gods damned AGES, and I've literally never even seen a Diablerie attempted, let alone pulled off. It's just never come up in the various groups I've been in, although I suspect a large portion of that is due to extreme luck in avoiding "that guy" in my gaming group, at least for NWoD games. So, with that little disclaimer out of the way, I was wondering if you might be willing to help me try and commit and get away with my first Diablerie ever. I've spoken with my ST, and they're okay with it, and even went to the trouble of clarifying that, to their mind, the Tainted condition is what Auspex picks up, not just the act of Diablerie itself. So once that goes away, no more auspex outings at level 2. Level 4 might still do it, depending on the circumstances though.

    Enough of the preliminaries though. Here's the slightly more relevant details for the act itself. I want to diablerize my sire, a woman named Anabelle in the Ordo Dracul, a minority and out of power faction within the city of Paris, France in 1999. I am also a member of the Ordo. My sire is a Nosferatu, Mystery of the Ascendant, and I am a Nosferatu, Mystery of the Wyrm. I only have the first dot. I am currently blood-bonded to my sire at the third level, which is both illegal in the city, and the reason my character wants her deader than dead. This is the third time in his life this has happened. First when he was mortal, then when he was a ghoul, and finally, lastly, as a kindred. I do understand he has some intense and loving devotion towards her, but he's built up enough simultaneous hatred in his heart over the years I think it could be plausible. The ST seems inclined to agree at least. Other than that, I have Blood Potency 2, 2 dots in Brawl and 3 in Strength, and 2 in Vigor. My sire, Anabelle is very much a non-combatant, although I'm sure it'd be a hell of a mess if it came to straight fisticuffs just due to power disparities, but not unwinnable. She tends to harbor guests and students (me) in a small apartment/library and only has a single ghoul retainer on hand at most times. She has a single other student, another player, who is currently distracting or otherwise inhibiting the ghoul as part of their personal activities. The city itself is Carthian run, abhors the act of Diablerie, but has also declared any form of Viniculum to be illegal as it is tantamount to slavery. This only applies to Kindred though.

    That's all I can think of for relevant details, at the moment. Please, feel free to ask questions about the situation or my character or whatever you feel is pertinent and relevant. I hope that crowd-sourcing this question and idea to a larger audience, and a more experienced one with this particular subject might give me a greater chance of actually pulling off something that I've never really had the chance to see in game, and would very much like to explore. Thank you for any and all responses. I eagerly await them.

      Just to clarify - Did your ST say Auspex 2 only detects diablerie if your character has the tainted condition active? (That's how I run it personally.) If the answer to this is yes, my response is going to be based on that answer and what your "character" knows in regards to diablerie.
      Is your character actually aware of the tainted condition and the specifics. (If your ST allows, your character can potentally pawn off self experimentation with that to develop a scale that conceals the condition and advance in the Ordo as well. Or an Obfuscate only devotion to conceal specific details from being uncovered.)
      How legalistic and forgiving are the Carthian courts for the crime of diablerie? Patricide? Killing in "self defense"? Your character should be prepared for the possibility that they'll be discovered and that they'll have multiple infallible lie detection and truth extraction methods via Auspex, Invictus Oaths, Carthian Law, Cruac, and Theban Sorcery. So a credible explanation of extenuating circumstances may be needed. Your character may be able to mount a self defense case stemming from allegations that your character's sire forced your character under vinculum and experimented on him.
      Do they count crimes committed outside the domain as "prosecutable" or is it deemed outside their jurisdiction? (I played in an Invictus domain setting like that, where it was a favorite Carthian and Crone tactic to drag rivals outside the city borders and torpor or kill them. Until it started backfiring. )
      Does your character's sire have any other childer or family in the domain. What about other Nosferatu in the city? (Again. This sort of falls under information your character may or may not know about.)
      I would advise disposing of her ghoul. (He could be a possible witness against you via manufactured blood sympathy.) Or may seek revenge until the vinculum wears off.
      Another option is to skip town or go into torpor for a few months until the tainted condition is resolved and claim "educational pursuits"

          A few caveats. First, blood bonds expire when the bonding party dies. You don't need to diablerize your sire to destroy the blood bond. If you really feel the need to get the bond out of the way prior to her death, you could possibly owe someone within the Order of the voivode mystery a boon and have them use the blood cleansing ritual on you. It's a fairly basic scale that involves rolling your blood potency against that of the bonding party though it's possible to artificially boost it by spending more vitae on the scale. However, if your goal is diablerie because of a personal vendetta, I would advise caution due to some fairly obvious things like addiction to vitae, addiction to diablerie, etc.

          All that aside, you'll want to prepare a defense for your character. Best case scenario, this will be a wholly legitimate one that outlines your sire's crimes in excruciating detail. No well-informed individual ever said however, that kindred were honest creatures. Concocting, "Evidence" is always a way to go if you need to get off scot-free yourself. The middle-ground of course, is always maneuvering your sire into positions where she looks like she's doing something terrible but actually wasn't. Advice on doing that, make sure whatever you've manipulated her into is not technically punishable but seriously frowned on nevertheless. That would provide a false source of frustration and a motive for your character to diablerize her, and ultimately give you a more sympathetic outlook in the crime itself.

              Nothing in 2nd edition says blood bonds expire at the death of the regnant. That was first edition. 2nd edition, it's left up to the ST to decide if they break upon the regnant's death or if the thrall maintains a semblance of grief or desire for revenge or wishes to continue pursuing what they believe their regnant wanted them to do. The original poster already said their ST concurs that the regnant's mistreatment would constitute sufficent justification to override the vinculum for the PC to diablerize them so it's a needless and unnecessary distraction to go chasing someone down and selling themself into slavery to get the vinculum removed since it doesn't inhibit the act of diablerie in the slightest. The character only takes a hit to their Humanity roll which is likely to fail anyway. The point is that the player wants to act out a diablerie and the consequences.
              Also, scales aren't proprietary. "Anyone" can learn them. (Assuming they want to jeopardize their Humanity.) You just get a price break if you know the respective mystery. Of course if said character learned such a scale and offered to perform it as a city service, it could be another avenue to pursue which might also increase the covenant's standing in the city. But that's tangential to the original question.

                  Save up your Willpower, it was an embarrassing night when one of our diablerists failed the roll to commit it and couldn't go anymore because she used up all her Willpower in the fight to get to that point .                       I personally hate the "only detected as long as you have Tainted" interpretation. Here is why, and relevant to your question:

                      It goes away in months = Blood Potency, minus one month for each Aggravated damage you take.

                      Simply give yourself agg damage (Fire is probably the easiest to obtain, and you're Wyrm so you can probably handle the frenzy checks) equal to your Sire's Blood Potency, and you can't ever be detected.

                      Just be sure to do it somewhere hidden; aggravated damage is the only kind that actually leaves permanent scars on Kindred.

                      This all assumes you know you can "burn" out the taint, but given this seems premeditated and you're Ordo Dracul, this doesn't seem like information that would be hard to find.

                              Be aware that Diablerie is an extra special act of evil on top of murder.                               You can handle this by either going the extra mile to prep for a diablerie charge or you can try to prep for murder and conceal diablerie but you need to handle it somehow
