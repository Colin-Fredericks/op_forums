        Mmrrrr...... *glances through* Well, not bad, but one thing springs instantly to mind. I honestly think you're trying to equate vampire elders to archmages or Gentry here. Elders are equivalent to Master mages or the royalty of Changeling Courts. The Methusalah, vampires who surpass elders, are the ones who adapt new templates like Archmages and True Fae. Of course you'll be disappointed if you think that elder devotions are supposed to be the equivalent of archmastery. They're not.

        Not to say there's not demand for a Methusalah power set and template. There is. Some people get great use out of the Archmage book, though I personally never had the need for it. But you're misidentifying what being an elder means.

        --------------------------------------------

        Now, on to the actual abilities... honeslty, if this is supposed to be the equivalent of a vampire evolution, you are being far too tame. The overwhelming majority of these are reporducable as devotions, Coils, or merits; Celeritous Power has a low level equivalent in Hint of Fear, just expanded to other Disciplines, which other low-level Devotions could do as well. Or, like Permanency, some of these Transmutations don't really do anything of note. I'm sorry, but if your goal was to have advanced abilities for vampires, reproducing things that neonates could have access to is far too tame.

        There is only one worthwhile ability - the one that gives Clash of Wills rote quality, and I personally find the idea of switching to attributes to be a deliberate break from and added complexity to the rules for no reason other than more dice to be distasteful. While it does offset the powercreep of other gamelines casually gaining rote quality, its pretty much only useful on crossover; you're already going to win a Clash of Wills by virtue of simply having higher BP.

        ------------------------------------------


        Lastly, on to the template.
        - Free transmutation. Bleh.
        - no loss of BP in torpor. Pretty standard according to legends of the Methusalah. I wouldn' texpect such a template without at least this much.
        - Everything is now affinity cost in terms of disciplines. Just... why? Feels like you're trying to eliminate clan here. No reason for it; even Archmages don't eliminate Path affinities.
        - Cannot be diablarized except by another Methusalah. Again, why? Feels more like power creep than something that involves story. Power hungry vampire who wants to devour the soul of an ancient is a classic plot hook, and this gets in the way. If you've already reached the point where you could theoretically be diablarized? You're as good as dead. I fail to see the point of even having this as a thing.
        - No need to spend willpower to Lash Out. Okay, that's pretty nice effect, and thematic.
        - The anti-Auspex 5 ability. Sounds like you have a beef with Auspex that you wanted to plug, not something worth noting about ancient vampires.
        - Devotion creation - this is actually a possibility for neonates developing Devotions as it is. This does nothing.
        - Hard to kill. Part of the whole "can only be killed by another ancient diablarizing" theme running through the place, which I think gets in the way of stories more than not. I also think that Final Death by fire or sunlight shouldn't be excluded. YMMV.

        So, other than the torpor bits, costless Lashing Out, and "can only be killed by acients diablarizing each other" parts, I think the majority is rather pointless, reproducing low level things, or flat out unthematic. It doesn't even touch on Banes or the like, something I imagine would be rather critical for ancients.
