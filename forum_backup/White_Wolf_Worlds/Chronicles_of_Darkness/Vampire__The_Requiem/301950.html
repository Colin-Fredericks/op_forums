                  Oki, I've put it on my Google Drive thingy. Not sure if I did it right though, not really into this stuff..
                  If I'm correct, you can view and probably download it here: https://drive.google.com/folderview?...HM&usp=sharing

                  Here's a little explanation:

                  Lightning Bolt = Instant Action
                  Arrow pointing left and right = Contested Action
                  Arrow in half a circle = Reflexive Action
                  Arrow in full circle = Extended Action
                  V: = Vitae cost
                  W: = Willpower cost

                  I've let the Aura Perception card of Auspex fairly empty. The idea is the PC using it can jot down the colors he knows.

                  I know it doesn't look really cool on the computer, but if you print it, they'll fit perfectly into Magic card sleeves.
                  It ain't perfect, but it worked for our group.

                  Anyway, I'm guessing no-one has 2.0 cards?
