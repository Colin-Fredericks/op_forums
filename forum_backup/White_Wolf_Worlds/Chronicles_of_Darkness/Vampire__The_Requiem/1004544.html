          Are they that voluntary ? I seem to remember that in 1E you could negate it temporarily with a willpower point (or permanently on a given occasion, like for one photograph) with a willpower dot that you had to buy back, but the "Lost Visage" part in 2E doesn't really expand on any of that. I guess given the wording, you could say it's voluntary and can be turned off at will, but that seems pretty much open to interpretation. My impression was more that it was something the beast's presence cause alongside with Vampire's need to lie and hide to survive, and so it was a bit too engraved into kindred existence to be bypassed just like that, but yeah, it's not explicitly said.

          Seems secondary, though. It's not like it's a hugely major point of the setting so you could ask your GM to let you. If your character wants to take pictures without the vampires agreeing to it, then, yeah, I guess a devotion would work, and while I'm not a fan, a Carthian Law would seem thematically appropriate. Carthians could have a Law that lets a few authorized persons break through the lost visage and capture kindred's images. Kind of like they're journalists and they can take pictures of whoever they want (I'd see that as mostly used for blackmail by Carthians). An Invictus oath could be an idea too, the vampire who takes the oath becomes sort of a official portraitist for essentially the same effect.

          Don't really see a Cruac or Theban sorcery ritual for that, seems pretty mundane and silly. A scale could be something, though, a scale developed by a dragon with way too much time on their hands.

          Then, is it the vampire who takes the picture who goes past the lost visage or the camera that's been specially prepared ?
