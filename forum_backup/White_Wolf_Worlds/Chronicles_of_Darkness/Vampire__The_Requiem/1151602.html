                      The Bishop is the highest Lancea et Sanctum authority in a domain. If they are a Prince, they're an Archbishop. An Inquisitor is appointed 'to root out and destroy heresies within the parish.' But they're basically enforcers with a large variety of jobs. Priests serve as counselors to individual kindred and coteries. A Legate is someone who travels to other domains on behalf of their covenant. A Crusader, something not all domains will have, is more of an executioner or assassin.

                      There's a bunch of lay positions. You might check the Lancea et Sanctum book for specifics. That's where all this comes from.

                      Some other terms for Archbishop used by the Lancea et Sanctum: Episkopos, Exarch, Father/Mother Superior. Bishops have Presbyter, Monsignor, and Shepherd. Priests have Pastor, Father/Mother, and Predicant.

                                          Here's the entirety of their brief section. Covenants: the Lancea Sanctum, p72.

                                          'Similar in more secular parlance to a medieval knight, a Crusader is not a leader among the Sanctified, nor is he a mere soldier. The Crusaders are also not considered Inquisitors. More or less, a Crusader is a member of the 'elite' warriors of the Lancea Sanctum. Renowned for their martial ability, they are fierce combatants, feared assassins, and cold killers. Crusaders are perhaps overly indoctrinated into the covenant, set upon rigid schedules of prayer and ritae, nigh fanatically enthusiastic to do anything (including meet Final Death) for the covenant. Crusaders are also expected to keep up their martial training (and this can include any kind of fighting, be it with melee weapons, firearms, or abstract tactics). They are not viewed as individual Kindred. They are seen as predatory weapons meant to be wielded for the glory and grace of the Dark Prophet. Many Sanctified see this position as outdated and bullish, though other older Sanctified feel that when the time comes, they’d rather have the protection than be without it.'

                                                  In my New York City setting (the Night Cities), each of the Boroughs has a Prince and maintains their own court and laws. In theory they are all subservient to the Prince of Manhattan.

                                                  Since 2000, Manhattan has been controlled by a Sanctified who goes by the title "Prince-Archbishop."

                                                  Below him, he has a Primogeon made up of Kindred from each of the Covenants. He also has a Synod made up of the leaders of the Sanctified across all of the Night Cities: this includes parish priests and Bishops.

                                                  His Seneschal is from the Invictus.

                                                  His Sheriff is called the Grand Inquisitor, and her Hounds are called Inquisitors.

                                                  Otherwise most of the other positions are held by a variety of Invictus and Sanctified without the need for special titles.
