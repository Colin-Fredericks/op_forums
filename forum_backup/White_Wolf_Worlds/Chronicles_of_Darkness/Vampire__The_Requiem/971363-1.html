    Weak vampires that commit diablerie get dusted as they should. For those that are not weak, I think there is a view on diablerie that is slightly exaggerated. You can make something of a recent diablerie, but if it occurred long ago then problems can arise. Vampire society is hypocritical. Diablerie is the worst thing a vampire can do, worse than murder for mortals, but sometimes a Prince will order diablerie as a punishment, like some societies execute murderers. Would you really blackmail a hitman or serial killer? Do you tell the Prince a vampire is a diablerist but don't know the victim or how long ago it was? What if the Prince says, "I know"? Personally, if I was a Prince I was say, "Okay, since you know, go deal with them." Whether I gave the diablerist a call would depend on which I liked better.

    I view vampire society like a prison with the vampires like prisoners, but I don't think everyone can relate so if I knew a diablerist I would give them a wide berth and try to avoid being vulnerable to the diablerist, certainly, but otherwise would leave them alone because unless you're Willie McCoy I say:

    You don't tug on Superman's cape
    You don't spit into the wind
    You don't pull the mask off that old Lone Ranger
    And you don't mess around with a diablerist

                        Rape is outside my comfort zone. I distinguish between killing and murder. I think "are you a diablerist" and "have you ever committed diablerie" are practically the same question. It seems to me some people like to play dark superhero and Auspex 2 to find out if a person is a murderer or rapist would fit that well. The power does say it finds out secrets. If you prey on unsavory folk then you should have little trouble finding them. I can understand saying a vampire that last committed a murder a 100 years ago wouldn't register with Auspex 2 though.

                        I view Auspex 2 as the power to use to find out enough info about a person to figure out what kind of person they are, their nature. You need Auspex 4 to dig deeper into the mind. If something is a breaking point for Integrity or Humanity then I think it should tend to be available for a query at Auspex 2. I view this not about the act but how it affects the individual. I also view breaking points for Integrity analogues, not necessarily all replacements, as trauma so I think a person learns to live with it rather than gets over it. Probably not a view for everyone.

                        Vampires have paranoia as a defining characteristic. Mekhet are avoided because they have Auspex. What happens to those that are caught using Auspex regularly? Given that many a vampire would take precautions against other vampires so that a diablerist would not be seen as much of a personal threat, would vampires tend to view a diablerist or a nosy vampire that likes to use Auspex and tell everyone about all the juicy secrets found as the greater threat? I know the politically correct answer. Now how many vampires would act on their true feelings, I wonder.

                                Auspex 2 lets you know they're a Diablerist. Auspex 4 lets you know how they did it, who they did it to, and make them feel guilty or scared over it. It also lets you know if they did it more than once.

                                Relying on the Tainted Condition seems rough. A Vampire can burn the Tainted Condition out of themselves immediately after committing the act. If they only Diablerized a Blood Potency 1 Neonate, they only take a single point of damage, and the wound or scar (remember that aggravated damage causes scars for Vampires) can be hidden somewhere out of sight or explained some other way. It's just a matter of how much they're willing to hurt themselves to hide.

                                Asking if someone is a Diablerist and having it read as "No" when they did it earlier that same night seems like actively working against the point of the ability and kind of being a dick to Mekhet characters. It's their whole schtick, to know secrets.

                                Even if it's a Blood Potency 10 Ancient, the Tainted Condition doesn't even last a year.

                                When the mind control power can control minds without spending Vitae at the first dot, and the Social power can make you loved even as you beat someone's face in at the same, and the hiding power makes you impossible to notice so long as other people are around... it seems arbitrary to make the secret-gathering power be unable to gather secrets that happened so recently on the time scale of immortals.

                                Everyone is free to run their game how they like, but I think the "only detect it when recent" interpretation really goes against the grain both mechanically and thematically, and "only detect it when Tainted" is even worse for the reasons above.

                                            I feel like the confusion here is mostly due to the changes in presentation of Auspex in 2e. In 1e, Auspex 2 was called "Aura Perception", and it let you see an actual psychic halo of colors around someone, and through those colors you could discern all kinds of information like what their mood was, if they had any Derangements, the influence of possessing entities, if the person was a supernatural creature and what kind, etc., and yes, if they were a Diablerist. Theoretically, if everyone actually memorized the stupid color meanings chart, the ST could just describe the colors seen in someone's aura and the player would know what that meant. In practice, it usually ended up working by the player asking questions and the ST answering them, or at least that's how it worked in the games I played in. Either way, no one really questioned that even if you only commit Diablerie one time, 100 years ago, you would still read as a Diablerist to an Auspex 2 user, because it was described as black streaks in your aura that would be visible to anyone who could view your aura.

                                            10+ years later, 2e decides to make a cosmetic change to the whole Aura thing. Vampires viewing colored halos around their victims and reading psychic impressions off of objects they touch isn't considered thematically appropriate any more, so the "predatory aura" becomes less of a psychic halo and more of a convenient way to describe all the subtle little tells vampires have and can recognize each other by - the way they move, the way they look at humans, the pallor of their skin, the forced way their chests rise and fall. Predator's Taint becomes Lashing Out, not an automatic clash of auras but conscious and overt displays of aggression, seduction, or dominance. Auspex 2 gets cleaned up to work the way many groups had been running it anyway, and gets re-named to "Uncanny Perception". Instead of a new-age psychic reading, it's Lie To Me style observation of those subtle tells like microexpressions and nonverbal social cues (filtered through the perception of the Beast, of course). Now, because there's no actual halo of energy for Diablerie to stain, it's more of a strain on verisimilitude for some people that Diablerie would permanently change the Diablerist in such a way that would for ever be observable under this new, less psychic, more sensual version of Auspex 2.

                                            I suspect the intent was for the question "is this person a Diablerist?" to come up "yes" even if they had only done it once, 100 years ago (or if they are being possessed by a Strix). But I don't think tying it to something more conditional, like having Tainted or having Diablerie addiction is an unreasonable call to make. Ultimately it comes down to what you think will make for better game play.
