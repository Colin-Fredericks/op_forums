    Help with understanding certain interactions with the Owls of Dis

    02-26-2016, 01:00 PM
    Okay, so I'm playing a mixed Chronicles of Darkness game but I'm bringing in one of my favorite hold overs from Masquerade the Sabat. Vampires who believe that the Kindred belong ruling the world not playing dress up as humanity but as a Covenant not as a group of clans. The Sabat's local leader has a plan to seize control of the god machine and use it for what he believes it's true purpose is to make gods. He will become a god, and his people will rule the world.

    That said...the players...have no idea who he is other than a name that leads to a false ID that leads to a cat's paw, that bounces around a few more people and ends up nowhere. Just yet. His big reveal won't be till later but for now that have met his Mekhet spy "Alexander Bell", or was it "Ted Fitzgerald" or was it "Steven Crane". Now when they face off against Mr. Bell tonight they this mixed group of 2 werewolves, a Vampire, and a beast (LOOOOOOONG story, it makes sense because they are playing themselves and were friends before their changes, and the death of a close friend drove all of them different directions).

    I want them to face bell but the two Forsaken will rip him apart like he's made of tissue paper and the beast is an Eshimaki dragon so if you guessed they can breath fire, your very right. So I thought what if he could summon up some help that wouldn't be so easy to kill. My first thought was a small band of 2 or three Stryx thought I wonder does the Werewolf rule about being able to inflict lethal damage effect Stryx? Does dragon fire (a flame thrower attack) cause lethal or bashing damage? or does it only do Lethal when it would do aggravated damage?

    So why am I asking this here? Well I figure if anyone knows about the Owls of Dis it's the Requiem forum. So? Help?

      Originally posted by HaplessWithDice View Post
      I wonder does the Werewolf rule about being able to inflict lethal damage effect Stryx?
      No. They only take damage from special Aggravated damage attacks, which is then reduced to Bashing.

      Does dragon fire (a flame thrower attack) cause lethal or bashing damage? or does it only do Lethal when it would do aggravated damage?
      Neither. Strix don't take damage by fire unless they're completely surrounded by it, which the blasts of Dragonfire don't seem to do. As long as they can escape,they won't take damage. If you use a Satiety Expenditure, however, it ups to Aggravated damage which, then, may cause Bashing damage to the strix. Also it allows you to burn other things, which can set a fire around the strix and potentially trap them.

      This is referring to 'loose' Strix, who are not possessing anyone and exist as shadows and darkness.

          So, usual disclaimer: crossover always creates weird edge cases and at the end of the day the answer will always be the one that best suits your chronicle. Now that that's out of the way, here's my input on this situation.

          Strix are not spirits and don't have Rank. No matter how high a werewolf's Honorary Rank, it's not higher than the Strix's Rank, since the Strix doesn't have a Rank to be higher than. Shadow Potency works similarly to Rank, so you could argue that a werewolf's teeth count as a Bane to a Strix with lower Shadow Potency than the werewolf's Honorary Rank. However, Strix Banes don't work the same way as ephemeral entity Banes do. Strix Banes work like vampire Banes do, they're not substances that harm the Strix, they're behavioral restrictions mined from vampire folklore, and more akin to ephemeral entity Bans than anything else. So pretty much any way you slice it, I don't see werewolves' natural weapons doing agg to Strix.

          As for fire, it doesn't do damage to Strix at all. Instead, fire (and sunlight) is solid to Strix. It forms an impenetrable barrier to them, which is useful for herding them around the battlefield, but probably won't kill them. If the Strix is surrounded on all sides by fire with no means of escape, then it takes one point of lethal damage per minute until they die or they somehow manage to escape (like if the fire goes out). Trapping a Strix in this manner would be extremely difficult with a flamethrower-like fire breath attack for a couple reasons. For one thing, being hit by the jet of fire would simply push them back. For another, in shadow form they can fly and slip through any gap that isn't airtight, so unless you can somehow create a complete sphere of flames around it, and sustain it long enough to kill the Strix, it's probably going to escape. Your best bet is to get the Strix to embody itself in a corpse or a vampire, and torch the body.

          Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.

                                      Originally posted by nofather View Post
                                      This is referring to 'loose' Strix, who are not possessing anyone and exist as shadows and darkness.
                                      This has been mentioned a couple times but is worth underlining. A strix using its Embodiments to manipulate a body can suffer physical damage to the body, but that doesn't directly injure the strix. It only breaks down the vehicle it's currently riding. When the text describes the strix's natural form outside a stolen body as a shadow owl, it's being literal. Their native form is darkness. Not shadowy ephemera, but actual shadows. You can't bite darkness. You can't wrestle darkness. You can ward it away with light, or manipulate it directly with certain very specific powers, but that's the main reason strix are so untouchable outside exploiting their banes.

                                      Since you're using them as reinforcements for the Sabbat, you might want to use the Khaibit bloodline as the basis for a Lasombra-inspired cross-clan lineage, to use their influence over shadows and the Abyss as an explanation for why strix might ally with or serve a Sabbat vampire. Or you might play up a further layer of paranoia by implying some uncertain number of Sabbat might in fact be strix controlling Kindred bodies, stirring the pot. Or something between: perhaps the strix come to the Sabbat's aid unbidden, and the Sabbat don't really know what to make of it, or whether to trust their capricious new "allies."

                                                  Meanwhile.

                                                  While strix make great antagonists, I question their placement as extras to make a fight more dangerous. While they do have some direct combat abilities, they are pretty simple, causing others to lose a turn or changing their damage to Aggravated. Coupling this with the sheer difficulty of actually doing harm to them, often requiring research to take advantage of their bane, they're likely to survive the death of the 'boss,' and be really hard to get rid of afterward. You can change this by having them possess someone, but at that point you may as well be using a zombie, revenant, human or vampire to begin with.

                                                  Strix seem better suited for a more insidious or long-term threat, rather than extra meat shields to make combat more dramatic.
