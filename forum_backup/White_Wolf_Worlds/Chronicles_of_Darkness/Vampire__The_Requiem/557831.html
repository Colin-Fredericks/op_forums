    My variants for clan banes

    06-17-2015, 12:36 AM
    Now I'm thinking upon some new format for clan banes. What I've got in the 2nd ed, seems to me the move in the right direction, but the result is not what I quite like. So, what about, for example these:

    Ventrue. The Curse of the Ambition.
    The Beast of Ventrue is the embodiment of great Ambition. Ventrue always win. Ventrue always get what they want. Choose one of your Aspirations. It becomes the main goal to you. After the chapter in which you did nothing to fulfill it, roll Humanity. If you fail the roll, take the Deprived condition.
    On the other side, if you fulfill (or achieve great success on the way to fulfill) your Aspiration, roll Blood Potency. You gain [successes] Willpower points (but not more you’ve spent).

    Daeva. The Curse of the Passion.
    The Beast of Daeva is the Beast of Passion and desire. There’s never enough for Daeva, never too far. When you fulfill any of your Aspirations, roll Humanity. If you fail the roll, take the Hedonistic condition.
    On the other side, when you take a new Aspiration, roll Blood Potency. You gain [successes] Willpower points (but not more you’ve spent).
    ---
    [Hedonistic Condition]
    What you,ve got is not enough for you. You want more. You cannot resolve your Aspiration until you resolute this Condition.
    Possible sources: The Curse of the Passion.
    Beat: each time your Aspiration becomes breaking point for you, get a beat.
    Resolution: fail the breaking point for resolving your Aspiration.

    I've not done other clan banes yet, but the idea is to use Humanity, Blood potency and some other plot-oriented traits (aspirations, touchstones, mask and dirge). And also make it a source of Willpower and Beats.

      Interesting idea to have the clan banes be clan blessings half the time.

      That said, I wouldn't personally run anything off aspirations. Aspirations can be anything from "Go hunting" to "bloodbond the President," so it's really way too easy to manipulate your bane this way. This is mostly a concern for Ventrue, who get bonuses for fulfilling aspirations and no penalties. Daeva get both rewards and penalties, so there's less motivation to game it, probably.

                          Krzyzstof, yep, you've grasped the idea.

                          The problem as I see it is that while setting tells us about the clan psychology, dictated by the Beast, the system doesn't really encourage us to do it... or doesn't make us too. As for my opinion, it should encourage and make both. It pretty well represents the Beast - the Beast's urges that dictate some way of behavior or lure into it. And that's not even the choice of the character - and never should be! It's a Curse!

                          Ventrue are ambitious. Why? Because their Beast is! It wants to subdue, to dominate. It gives them the perfect instrument for it - Dominate discopline - and wants it used to fulfill its urges. And it doesn't care if the Kindred themselves want it or not. If they do so he rewards them. If they don't... it can turn their unlives into hell.

                          Daeva are passionate. That's also their Beast that drives them to the edge of sencitive experience. Never enough. Never too far.

                          Gangrel are feral. Their Beast is concerned hunt and survival, it doesn't care of anything else.

                          Nosferatu are monstrous. Their Beast wants to bring horror and fear into the world.

                          Mekhet... well, their Beast is the most complicated. A bit Lovecraftian, a bit God-Machine-like (remember frenzy described in "Shadow in the Dark"?).

                          The point IMO is to make Curse dictating behavior. But not too often, for the characters could do other things. But often enough to make them remember that they're not blessed. They're cursed.

                          By the way, it's a good idea to make 'blessing' side of Clan Banes be connected with breaking points - some kind of 'Devil's Contract'.

                                                  While it's interesting to think about in broad strokes, I wouldn't want to mechanically enforce this "clan psychology." People being less defined by their clan is something I really liked about VtR over VtM. Daeva don't have to be seductive, though they're more inclined towards it after Embrace. Mekhet might never get a dot of Obfuscate, so unseen feeding might never happen.

                                                  It's interesting to view how the average vampire of each Clan may differ in broad strokes, but I get really wary when it comes to pigeonholing people into certain roles or behaviors or personalities based on their Clan. Clan isn't really something anybody chooses, so anyone could be a member of any Clan. A former rock star could end up Mekhet instead of Daeva, which might be what you'd expect. A total shut-in computer nerd could be Embraced by a Gangrel rather than a Nosferatu. A social worker that spends all their time trying to help others above themselves could end up Ventrue, etc, etc, etc. These aren't common cases, but I wouldn't want the mechanics to force them to act against who they are. These sorts of contradictions can make for really interesting characters.

                                                  I also like the Beast being the one true unifying tribulation for Vampires, and what they build their Covenants around. That's more of a personal thing, though, and I won't fault anyone for having a different interpretation of the Beast.

                                                  The Clan psychology thing above is kind of how I feel about the initial OP, too, but I didn't want the first reply to be something that was shooting down someone's ideas.
