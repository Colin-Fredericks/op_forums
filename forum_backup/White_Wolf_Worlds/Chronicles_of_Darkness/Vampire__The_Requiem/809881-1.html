        I'm not done yet, but that's the first draft.

        New York general mood

        The basic idea is that the city is way too large to be claimed by a single kindred. NYC has five boroughs which loosely correspond to the various kindred influence. Each borough is under a different influence and the five leaders hold a conclave at least once a year to address potential threats and common issues.
        Anyway, even broken down into five boroughs no Prince can claim he controls everything that's going on in his own domain: a lot of districts remain unwatchable, whole parts of the city are occupied by nomads, unbound or even small covenants which practices and traditions totally degenerated.

        That's the general idea, specifics vary from one borough to another. I'll start with Staten Island.

        Staten Island:

        A rural part of NYC till the 60's, Staten Island is the less populated of the 5 boroughs (over 470 000 inhabitants though), after urbanization soared, concerns about preserving the old rural parts of the island rose as well, leading to a policy preserving the local ecology: parks, zoos etc. Close to 80% of the population is white and it is the only borough in NYC where the Republican Party generally beats the Democrats.

        The mood of the kindred side of Staten Island is an old Victorian mood, a part of NYC that seems being a remanent of the 19th century. Very conservative and quite isolationist the Island is under a double influence. First and foremost the Ordo Dracul whose Society for sciences is a front office for managing and funding their real experiments. Secondly the Lancea Sanctum rules over the Roman Catholic Church and warn their flock against these secular and blasphemous scientists whose soulless science is an outrage in the face of God. Needless to say the opposition between both covenants has been fierce since the start and has led to many conflicts a lot of them vicious and deadly.

        Staten Island's Lancea Sanctum is very traditional we might even risk the word "bigotry" to qualify it. Meaning gaining high status in the covenant is about being devout to the words of Longinus and therefore (they think) excelling at handling Theban Sorcery. The politicial and organizational aspects of the covenant are a secular necessity but brings little prestige. Quite ironically it's very similar to the way the Defiants consider their own politicians, including their Kogaion.

        Several decades ago that led to a surprising result: Sanctified and Defiant politicians came together to try to put an end to the constant and counterproductive infightings between the two covenants. At first the coterie they formed was met with scorn and mockery but the truth is they had two assets none of the two covenants could ignore. First they really had secular power: you might consider that only science or theology matters but you'll need infrastructure, funding and many secular/mundane concerns being taking care of in order to carry on. Second they really are politicians and thus they are able to add some water to their wine and, by giving some ground, actually achieving something. And achieving they did, the Council (the name of their coterie) has greatly eased the coexistence of the two covenants. It's not easy, they don't have the highest status in their respective covenants and have to be clever and subtle when they are pulling their strings, negotiations can be going for month for petty details considered highly symbolic for one or both (the worse case scenario) covenants and crucial but down to earth issues might be dealt with in one night by high status Sanctified and Defiant alike unconcerned by such mundane questions.

            Manhattan

            For most non New Yorker kindred, Manhattan seems to epitomize everything the Invictus crave for: money and power worldwide. And it did for those British Invictus who first ruled Long Island especially when it became the British center of operations during the independence war. The American Invictus love to remind everybody of the crucial part they played during those nights, rising against the British presence through the gangs and militia they controlled and being the cornerstone in the victory against the British vampires when George Washington freed the city in 1783. Some might say that patriotism had little to do with that, they'll add that younger Invictus saw an opportunity to seize power from their elders and they took it. Anyway in the meantime, the Circle of the Crone played a clever game. The Circle knew too well that even in the New World , even when fleeing persecution with other persecuted minorities, those former minorities would become the new orthodoxy and would in return persecute pagans and put them to the stake just as the Europeans did to them not that long ago. And it wouldn't concern only the kine: taking advantage of the bigotry of the time, the British Lancea Sanctum allied with the British Invictus would bully the Circle itself. And now what ? What they gonna do ? Wait to see if the American Invictus would be more charitable ? The acolytes knew they won't. So they took matters into their own hands, as the British influence natural and supernatural alike was fading, they used sorcery, shamanism and voodoo rituals to fill the empty space, to seize control of the economic and financial powers. Never ever again, the Circle will be oppressed because they learnt their lesson well: no matter who you are, no matter how you used to be persecuted in the past, if you control businesses, banks, politicians everybody and his dog would legitimate you. And that's what they did, let the Invictus upstarts and the American Firebrands win the vampiric side of the independence war, in the meantime the Acolytes would spread their grasp on finance and industry through magic and pacts.

            So here we are today: the Invictus is known as the Syndicate, ruling the gangs and especially organized crime. They thought they could take control of big money after the independence war but it was to late: when it was all set and done, the Circle of the Crone was the unmitigated ruler of what would become Wall Street. Now they call their organization the Firm. Forget about your neopagan hippies, we are talking suits, corporation boards, the banking system, the Federal Reserve, traders, raiders, banksters. You want to rise in the business ? You'll have to cheat your way to the top, you'll have to strike a deal with the Firm. Rituals are not cheap bacchanals conducted in some exclusive pub in the neighborhood it's been performed in back office, in secret corporate rooms with velvet curtains, marble columns and several millions worth occult paraphernalia.

            (Thanks Woland)

            Now the Executive Board rules the city. The Prince (known as the Chairman) is from the Firm (Circle of the Crone) and the board is a collegial representation of the Firm and the Syndicate. The Lancea Sanctum, the Movement and Ordo Dracul aren't represented. Nonetheless the Lancea Sanctum holds sway on the Syndicate (whose Godfather has a strong Italian legacy). The few Ordo Dracul just don't care ; as for the Firebrands they are just pissed off about having being played upon and discarded from the political arena although they also played a key role in the "battle of New York".

                The Bronx

                In the old days, the Bronx history mixed with Manhattan's but when talking about modern history you'll have to consider two eras: pre and post the 90's zero tolerance policy. Before the 90's the situation spiraled totally out of control. The place was such an urban jungle and the most infamous crime plagued place in the US that even the kindred counterpart of the borough was dangerous. We're talking ruthless nomads, diablerist coteries, lunatic kindred and forbidden covenants such as the Brood or any kind of degenerated self appointed guru kindred rising star. So when the kindred say the "Carthian Movement used to be charge" it has to be taken with a grain of salt to say the least. Sure they were the only traditional covenant around, but they were battling constantly and we are not talking about some political backstabbing subtleties, we are talking guns and fangs, we are talking torpor and final death, we are talking no rules at all.

                Then surely what had to happen happened: kine and kindred alike grew tired of the whole mess that threatened to spill over Manhattan itself. They decided it was time to step in and it backfired dramatically. The humans call it the zero tolerance policy and it was, indeed, the mortal counterpart of this war. For the kindred it was a full fledged war: as the police forces were taking the streets back by day, by night the Syndicate maimed, burnt, staked, tortured or otherwise introduced to the sunrise every kindred judged suspicious or simply poor suckers they didn't know. They probably killed many innocent leeches (if there is such a thing) but no mofo that crossed their path ever survived the experience. Okay I know what you're thinking: the Carthians allowed it ? They left the Invictus seize whole districts in the Bronx ? Actually they had little choices, they couldn't wage a two front war. Battles against the unbound (one might say the unleashed) had left a bitter taste and personal feuds blinded them all ; the Syndicate, at the time, looked like the lesser evil. Fools they were. When the unbound kindred were burn to the ground what do you think happened ? The Syndicate took credit for it all claiming the Bronx although they had only one third of it. Obviously they had the nicest parts, those parts in which gentrification began and guess what ? The Firm backed them: the Godfather was declared Capo of the Bronx and the Firebrands were discarded once again: they won't be represented in any Conclave because for most kindred from other boroughs, the Bronx is a natural (geographically speaking) extension of Manhattan. So of course, the Carthians do have a "Prince" (they call him Leader) but he's not recognized anywhere, he could as well being a tribal chief. Too much is too much. The Movement lost Manhattan in the 18th century and now the Bronx ; although they paid more than their share to win these battles, although they're still holding power on most of the Bronx, they're still being denied. Now this is war. The Firebrands are, indeed, branding fire. Don't go north Bronx today because the old days are back: violence, crime, ruthless gangs and crazy kindred, the Firebrands will open the very gates of hell to bring both the Syndicate and the Firm screaming and kicking to hell. That's a nasty war, if you're not a Carthian and you're strolling north the Bronx you might end up dead, if you're foolish enough to claim some Syndicate or Firm support you will be killed.

                    Brooklyn

                    In recent history Brooklyn has become the third business center in NYC after two Manhattan's districts. The theme of Brooklyn is new blood. An Invictus covenant, totally disconnected from the Syndicate did a lot of work to propel Brooklyn into the business competition. They didn't do that alone though, they're working with the Movement to smooth employers-employees relationships and it is working. What happened ? New blood happened, neonates and some ancillae are managing the place. Historically speaking places of power in NYC were about Long Island, the oldest vampires struggled there. Okay there's the exception of Staten Island that drew some old vampires too, but the Richmond County has always been about ideological feuds, science versus religion, the Lancea Sanctum against the Ordo Dracul, secular power never mattered that much for Staten Island's kindred. Now, in Brooklyn we're talking business. We are talking ambitious young vampires that left Manhattan for Brooklyn to start something there because they couldn't compete with Manhattan's elders. The most dynamic, daredevil and ingenious neonates started to emulate American pioneers: building a new world, fleeing the old world to start again. Okay that might sound a bit emphatic since no Brooklyn kindred ever crossed the Atlantic Ocean but in their mind it was the same thing, in their mind the Syndicate battling against the British is a big joke: most Syndicate leaders are actually Europeans, some them even British. In their mind they fled from the old world (Manhattan) where the game was rigged for neonates and they carved their own destiny like mortals did in 1776.

                    As a result Brooklyn "by night" can be quite a sight for a non New Yorker kindred: the borough is being held by young vampires. Ancillae are the eldest and they are few of them. Younger vampires also means closer to the Man and Brooklyn kindred are probably much more attuned to mortals than those from Manhattan. You'll have the stereotypes alright: Invictus is opposed to the Movement, Lancea Sanctum is close to the Invictus and the Circle's Acolytes are kinda blasphemous to the Sanctified. It's just that they won't take all these things to the extent oldest kindred do. They won't kill each other over this, they won't deny their rights to be represented over this. Don't kid yourself, they're still vampires and they can be vicious to one another but it's generally for personal matters. As a community though, it's working and the borough is a rising star, still way behind Manhattan so their Elders don't see them as a threat ... yet.

                    What's striking about the Brooklyn's kindred is their urge to belong. For instance: Brooklyn's Invictus don't call themselves the Syndicate, the Corporation or anything along those lines, they call themselves Invictus and they try to emulate them in any way: the titles, the courtesy, the way they dress. Some old fashioned old world leeches might call them wannabes but are they ? In some ways they are closer to the traditional Invictus than the Syndicate, it's just they are much younger. And this is their strength: they might appeal to gifted neonates feeling that they can achieve in Brooklyn what they would be denied in other cities. The same could be said about the the Movement: these Firebrands talk about socialism, equal standard of livings, about being the 99%. But they're still close to mortals and improving social daily conditions still matters to them and they're able to deal on regular basis with the Invictus to achieve what they want. To them the Bronx's Carthians are bogeymen lost to the cause, they won't have anything to do with them and are glad they are not represented in the Conclave. The same thing goes with the Lancea Sanctum: they're here to torment mortals but especially the evil ones and, amazingly enough they generally lean towards the Movement, explaining to the Invictus that Christian charity commands that they should be charitable with those less fortunate and lenient towards the poor. The result is that the Lancea Sanctum in Brooklyn is greatly smoothing things up between the Carthians and the Invictus.

                        And that's what I did for Queens

                        The Queens

                        The Queens is the most multi cultural borough in NYC, it used to be the movie capital of the nation before the industry moved to Hollywood, nonetheless it's still an artistic cluster: movie studios, theaters, museums, the Queens is vibrating with art.

                        So what's happening in the kindred world ? Which covenant claims the Queens as a domain ? The truth is: none. Sure you'll have companies, night clubs run by the Invictus, you'll have a light version (a more liberal version) of the Lancea Sanctum, the Movement will be outspoken about gay rights, pro choice and political correctness. The Circle of the Crone will fight for neopagan rights and spread the witchcraft culture and the Ordo Dracul, well, they would migrate to Staten Island unless they're dealing with plastic surgery. Everyone can belong in the Queens but none nearly fits like the Daeva do.

                        Yeah, you got that right: no covenant can claim the Queens, but a clan does. The sirens are everywhere: Invictus Daeva might run this night club or art gallery, Firebrand Daeva might be quite convincing about the new social political trends, Lancea Sanctum Daeva might urge revisiting the dogma to fit the modern world and Ordo Dracul Daeva might long for circumventing the curse to help her being more proficient at socializing. They might be everywhere but, at the end the night, they'll throw parties, talk about art and the hottest things, they'll decide about who's talented and who's not, which political thoughts are trendy and who's in and who's out in the kindred political game. They really run the show.

                                    I've actually been going through the published fiction and setting fluff for CoD and compiling a list of all the bits and pieces that I can pin down geographically.                                                         I know there's the temptation to make things darker, and it's also a sort of natural extension of adding supernatural creatures into the already complicated situation of New York City, but don't lose sight of the fact that it's safer than it's ever been.                                                         Like for example, Manhattan is just NOT the place to be getting into big fights in the streets. It's not that they're afraid of people thinking Vampires exist, but rather that they don't want people to realize how unsafe they are walking the streets, because they think the gangbangers and muggers are in other boroughs.
